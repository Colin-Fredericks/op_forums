    Muse Miracles


    Path of Creation


    *Instructions
    Description: Tells the user how to make something.
    System: Roll perception + craft vs 5 for a simple object (a meal), 7 for more complex stuff like gun powder or 8 for magical items. A botch gives the user the wrong information.


    **-Repair
    Description: Repairs a single object.
    System: Spend a Favor and roll Intelligence + Crafts vs 5 for simple objects (a bike), 6 for more complex ones (a car engine), 7 for big and complex machines (a tank) and 8 for magical items.
    Botch: Makes the condition of the object even worse.


    ***-Break
    Description:     The user hums out a long, continuous note that causes one object to break down.
    System: Spend 1 Favor and begins humming, roll Willpower vs 6 for simple objects (a bike), 7 for more complex ones (a car engine), 8 for big and complex machines (a tank) and 9 for magical items. At least 3 successes are needed for any roll over 7.
    Botch: User receives a point of damage.

    ****-Customize
    Description:     The user can alter the form on a non-living, mundane object such as turning it into vapor, modifying the density or separating the object into components.
    System: Spend two favors and roll Wits + Crafts vs 7 (8 for more complex objects and/or alterations). More successes equal a better job.
    Botch: Object melts into a hot puddle of goo.

    *****-Master of Flesh
    Description:     Now a master of creation, The Host has granted the user the power to alter flesh, animals and even living objects like an artist does to clay. Allow them to do something as simple as fixing a cleft-palate to turning a dog into a larger, stronger protector.
    System: The user must be touching the target, pend two favors and roll Wits + Crafts vs 7 (it will take a 1 to 3 rounds depending on the complexity). The number can be adjusted for more complex or simpler jobs (min 6, max 10). The more successes equal a better job while a botch infects the user's hands with severe arthritis for the rest of the scene. Returning the subject back to normal can be done in one round by spending a single Favor point.
    Note: Using this Miracle for nefarious purposes calls for a Morality check.

    Path of Objects

    *-Absorb Lore
    Description:     Learn about the history of an object or place.
    System: The user must touch the item and roll Intelligence + Investigation vs 7. The more successes equals a longer, clearer and more descriptive reading.
    Botch: The reading comes in a jumbled mess that is impossible to decode.

    **-Living History
    Description:     By tasting a few drops of the subject's blood or tears*, the user can learn about his/hers history, personality traits and abilities.
    System: After ingesting the tears/blood, spend a Favor, and roll Intelligence + Investigation vs 7. The more successes equals a longer, clearer and more descriptive reading.
    Botch: The user spend the next round vomiting up the blood/tears.

    *Other bodily liquids can be used, but these are the more preferred methods.

    ***-Divine Skill
    Description:     If the user is making a roll for an untrained skill, this miracle lowers the penalty by 1 for each Favor spent.
    System: Spend one Favor per point (max 3) on the next roll.

    ****-Telekinesis
    Description:     Cause objects to levitate.
    System: Spend a favor and roll Willpower vs 6 (increase the difficulty to bigger objects and/or more complex maneuvers.)

    *****-Breath of Life
    Description:     Briefly brings a mundane object to life under the user's command.
    System: Select one object, spend two Favors and roll Willpower vs 7 (8 for more complex objects); each success equals a round of activation. This does not stack.
    Botch: Object is immune to the Miracle for the rest of the battle/scene.

    Path of Support

    *-Inspire
    Description:     Give a stumped subject a burst of inspiration.
    System: Spend a Favor and roll Wits + Subterfuge vs the target's willpower (subtract one if the subject is knows of the gift). Each success equals a better and more descriptive plan (ST's discretion).
    Botch: The idea is as dumb as Hell and is useless at best.

    **-Lend Favor
    Description:     Grant a point of Favor to someone else.
    System: Touch the target and spend a Favor that will go to them. In the case of other Mystics, the Favor will turn into whatever power source they use.

    ***-Miracle Boost
    Description:     Grant an ally an extra die on the next Miracle (or special ability for other Mystics) they use.
    System: Touch the subject and grant them a Favor per die you want to grant them (max 3). It is automatically used on the next ability they use and if no ability is used by the end of the scene/battle, the Favors are wasted.

    ****-Bestow Turn
    Description:     By sacrificing his/her turn, an ally get an extra turn in the next round.
    System: Spend two favors and touch the subject or spend an extra favor if they are not in reach. The user loses their next turn in return for the subject to go again. If the battle ends before the Miracle can be used, it is wasted.

    *****-Mass Support
    Description:     The user is engulfed in a burst of light that put all that see them at ease, granting the five closest viewers an increase/degrease on the difficulty on their next roll.
    System: Spend three Favors, if the subject is an ally, they get a one point decrease on the difficulty of their next roll while enemies are given a one point increase. This lasts until a roll is done unless the game session ends and the Miracle is wasted.

        Path of the Umbra

        *-Gauntlet-Finger
        Description:         Locates portals to the Umbra.
        System: Roll Perception + Awareness vs the area's gauntlet rating (minus 1, min 1). The more successes equals a more accurate description while a botch tells the opposite.

        **-Come With Me
        Description:         Take a person who normally can't enter/leave the Umbra with you.
        System: Hold the hand of the person who you want to take with you and spend a Favor to leave/exit the Umbra. This Miracle can only be used on one person at a time and hand contact must be kept at all time or the other person will get lost in the Umbra.

        ***-Umbral Swarm
        Description:         Attack and damage a target with beam of cloud-like Umbral energy.
        System: Spend a Favor and roll Dexterity + Melee vs the target's Dexterity + Dodge. More successes equals another point of damage (which is soakable). A botch inflicts a point of damage on the user.
        NOTE: This Miracle only works in the Umbra.

        ****-Umbral Armor
        Description:         Creates a temporary see-though set of full-body armor for the user or someone else to get through the nastier parts of the Umbra.
        System: Spend two Favors to create the armor which lasts about ten minutes (and spend an addition Favor to keep it going for another five). This armor will provide protection against the elements of the Umbra though it will not protect against battle damage. While the user can still wear armor, the Miracle does not stacks.

        *****-Warp
        Description: Warps a target to another part of the Umbra.
        System: Spend two Favors and roll Wits + Awareness vs the target's willpower.

        Botch: Warps the target right next to the user.
        1 success: Warps the target ten feet away in a random direction.
        2 to 3 successes: One mile in a random direction.
        4 to 5 successes: 3 miles in a random direction.
        6 or more successes: Up to 10 miles in whatever direction the user desires.


        Path of Nature

        *-Nature's Plenty
        Description: Helps user to search natural areas for food and planets that can heal.
        System: Roll Wits + Survival vs 7 (if the user has any specialty or knowledge with nature-related stuff, target number can be lowered). The more successes equals a better find while a botch give you the wrong information.

        **-Bloom
        Description:         Can make a plant bloom and bear food out of season or make it wither.
        System: Spend a Favor and roll Wits + Survival vs 7. The more successes equals a better result while a botch does the opposite.

        ***-Ensnare
        Description:         Entrap a target with branches, vines, etc.
        System: Spend a Favor and roll Wits + Subterfuge while the target rolls their Dexterity + Dodge. The more successes equals a better result while a botch cause the branches, vines, etc to crumble to dust.

        ****-Earth Trap
        Description:         Turn patches of dirt and sand into a quicksand-like substance that makes a target sink knee deep in it.
        System: Spend two Favors and roll Wits + Subterfuge vs 8. This will effect a 10 by 10 foot area for a half hour.

        Botch: the earth becomes harder than before.
        1 Success: It's muddy enough to leave footprints and be a bit of an annoyance.
        2 to 3 Successes: Causes the target(s) to sink in up to their ankles.
        4 to 5 Successes: Causes the target(s) to sink up to the waist.
        6 or more Successes: Causes the target to sink up to their neck.

        NOTE: The effects of this Miracle can be shut off early. If used for malicious purposes, a Morality check must be made.

        *****-Pure Earth
        Description:         Heal natural, polluted areas.
        System: Spend two Favors and roll Perception + Survival vs the area's gauntlet rating, minus one. More successes equals a better result while botch makes things worse.

        Path of Beasts

        *-Doolittle's Tongue
        Description:         Talk to and understand mundane animals.
        System: Roll Charisma + Animal Kin vs 7. The more successes equals a better result while a botch comes out jumbled.

        **-Warp the Beast
        Description:         Calm mundane animals or rile them up.
        System: Spend a Favor and roll Manipulation + Animal Kin vs 7. The more successes equals a better result while a botch does the opposite.

        ***-Insect Plague
        Description:         Send a swarm of near-by, mundane insects to attack/distract a target or destroy things.
        System: Spend a Favor and roll Manipulation + Subterfuge vs 8. The more successes equals more insects and damage while a botch causes them to attack the user for one round.

        ****-Command Animal
        Description:         Makes an animal (even mystical ones) obey a non-suicidal command.
        System: Spend two Favors and roll Manipulation + Animal Kin vs 8. The more successes equals a better result while a botch protects them from ever being under control of the Miracle for the rest of the scene. In the case of mystical ones, they are allowed to roll willpower to avoid doing something that would be against their own agendas.
        NOTE: If used on a werecreature, they must be in their normal animal form for this Miracle to work.

        *****-Wild Form
        Description:         Temporarily transform into the mundane animal of the user's choice.
        System: Spend Two Favors to activate this Miracle for 20 minutes minutes (and spend an addition Favor to keep it going for another five). This can be shut off at anytime, but once the duration is over, the user automatically transforms back to their original form. The user keeps their abilities and Miracles.

            System: Spend a Favor to permanently purify it.

            Description:             Same as the Child of Goliath Miracle.

            *****-Sacrifice
            Description:             By sacrificing some health health, you can create a burst of holy energy burst
            System: Spend two Favors and a point of health to create a 5ft burst of holy energy that inflicts a point of damage per Sin (it's soakable) of any adversary caught in the blast. If the user is already down to one health level, they can't use it.

            Path of Redemption

            *-Second Chance
            Description:             Same as the Deadman Miracle, Scales of Justice.

            **-Saultie's Bane
            Description:             Temporarily bestow the user's curse on another to teach them a lesson.
            System: Spend a Favor and roll Manipulation + Empathy vs the target's Willpower.

            Botch: Makes the user's curse worse for the rest of the scene.
            1 Success: 5 minutes
            2 to 3 Successes: 1 hour
            4 to 5 Successes: 1 day
            6 or More: 1 week

            Note: If used for malicious purposes, a Morality check is needed.

            ***-Inner Good
            Description:             Same as the Aasimar Miracle.

            ****-Walking Target
            Description:             Same as the Child of Goliath Miracle.

            *****-Temporary Forgiveness
            Description:             Temporarily eases a Mystic's drawback or another target's curse. But not your own.
            System: Spend two Favors and roll Wits + Aura vs 8.

            Botch: Makes the user's curse worse for the rest of the scene.
            1 Success: 5 minutes
            2 to 3 Successes: 1 hour
            4 to 5 Successes: 1 day
            6 or More: 1 week


            Path of Healing

            *-Angelic Touch
            Description:             Same as the Aasimar Miracle.

            **-Remove Disease
            Description:             Removes any disease the target is suffering from.
            System: Spend a Favor and roll Perception + Medicine vs the number on the chart. Only two success are needed. If the disease is mystical in nature, then add a point to the target score.

            Common cold, the flu, etc: Target score 6
            Strep Throat, chicken pox, etc: Target score 7
            Deadly, but curable diseases: Target score 8
            Deadly and incurable diseases: Target score 9

            ***-Plastic Surgery
            Description:             Fixes a target's mundane deformities.
            System: Spend a Favor and roll Wits + Medicine vs the number on the chart. If the user has a crafts score of 4 or higher, the target number is reduced by one.

            Harelips, barely noticeable scars, etc: 7
            Moderate deformities: 8
            Severe deformities: 9

            ****-Lend Health
            Description:             Lends a point of health to the target.
            System: The user must touch the target and infuse one point of health to the other. (The user will lose one temporary health point when doing this.) This last until target loses the point or the scene/battle ends. If the user is already down to one health, they can't use this Miracle.

            *****-Reattach
            Description:             Reattach severed body parts.
            System: Spend two Favors and roll Wits + Medicine vs 7 (for fingers, ears, noses and toes) and 8 for limbs, breasts and private parts). Only one success is needed, but if the user botches the attached part rots and falls off.

                Path of The Mind

                *-Addle
                Description: Temporarily addles the target's mind.
                System: Spend a Favor and roll Manipulation + Subterfuge vs the target's willpower. Each success equal's a round of activation while a botch effects the user for one round. The target becomes dizzy. (Cannot be used in battle.)

                **-Memory Leech
                Description:                 Absorb and copy a memory from the target.
                System: Spend a Favor, touch the target's head and roll Perception + Awareness vs the target's Willpower. Each success results in a larger, more clear memory while a botch sickens the user for a round. The target will still have this memory.

                ***-Eye Beam
                Description:                 The Oracle's third eye shoots a beam of holy energy that damages the target (that shoots up to 40 feet). In case of Infused, it comes from their regular eyes into a single concentrated beam.
                System: Spend a Favor for each point of soakable damage you wish to inflict with the beam (max 3). The target can dodge this attack.

                ****-Dream Gaze
                Description:                 By making eye-contact, the target enters a harmless, zombie-like state until the threat of physical harm.
                System: Spend two Favors, make eye-contact and roll Manipulation + Subterfuge vs the target's Willpower. Each success equals a round of activation while a both inflicts this Miracle on the user for one round. This Miracle cannot be used in battle.

                NOTE: If this Miracle is used for nefarious purposes, a Morality check must be made.

                *****-Mind-Jolt
                Description:                 By making eye-contact, the user can temporarily enter a target's body and take control of it.
                System: Spend two Favors, make eye-contact and roll Manipulation + Subterfuge vs the target's Willpower. Each success equals a round of activation while a botch makes the target immune to this Miracle for the rest of the scene.

                NOTE: If this Miracle is used for nefarious purposes, a Morality check must be made.


                Path of Senses

                *-Sense Evil
                Description:                 Same as the Deadman Miracle.

                **-True Sight
                Description:                 See through illusions and disguises.
                System: Spend a Favor and roll Perception + Awareness. Each success equals five minutes of activation that only the user can see while a botch will make everything blurry for one round.

                ***-Warning
                Description:                 Sense when the user and/or allies are going to be attacked.
                System: Spend a Favor and roll Perception + Alertness vs 7. Each success equals five minutes of activation (at least three successes are needed to sense mystical tactics) while a botch gives the user a headache for one round.

                ****-Telepathy
                Description:                 Send a telepathic message/image to a target.
                System: Spend two Favors roll Perception + Awareness vs the Gauntlet rating the target is in (minus 1). Each success equals a longer and clearer message that can be given. A botch prevents the user from sending another message to the target for the rest of the scene. If the target is one of the Touched or an ally, than the target score is reduced by one.

                *****-Future Shock
                Description:                 Predicts possible futures.
                System: Spend two Favors and roll Perception + Awareness vs 8. Each success shows a longer, more vivid potential future while a botch gives a jumbled message.

Each success exposes more of a lie while a botch prevents the user from using the Miracle on the target for the rest of the scene.

                ***-X-Ray
                Description:                 See through walls, barriers and even clothing.
                System: Spend a Favor for five minutes of activation (this can stack).

                ****-Eyes of Another
                Description:                 See what another person is seeing.
                System: Spend two Favors and roll Perception + Awareness vs the target's willpower (if done willingly, reduce the number by 1). Each success equals a round of activation while a botch gives a blurry picture.

                *****-Past Shock
                Description:                 See the past of a person, object or place.
                System: Spend two Favors and roll Perception + Awareness vs 8. Each success shows a longer, more vivid potential future while a botch gives a jumbled message.

                                Path of Emotions

                                *-Calm Emotions
                                Description: Same as the Light-Bringer Miracle.

                                **-Empath
                                Description:                                 Know exactly what the target is feeling and thinking which will appear as a ghostly being by them that only the user can see. Example: A person who greatly regrets an action's "ghost" will be gray with a look of sadness with its hand on the target's shoulder. While someone who would like to kill the user, will have a "ghost" that is giving a sinister look at the user as if they can't wait to wrap their hands around her neck.
                                System: Spend a Favor and roll Perception + Empathy vs the target's willpower. Each successs gives a more detailed image while a botch makes it blurry and unreadable.

                                ***-Stir Emotions
                                Description:                                 Bring forth an emotion (fear, joy, anger, etc) upon your target.
                                System: Spend a Favor and roll Manipulation + Empathy vs the target's willpower. More successes equals a more powerful emmotion and 5 minutes of activation while a botch brings the opposite emotion for five minutes.

                                ****-Mob
                                Description:                                 Rally a group to feel a strong, powerful emotion and act upon it.
                                System: Spend two Favors and roll Charisma + Leadership vs the average willpower. It can be lowered by one if the majority of the group are allies or the Touched.

                                Botch: The group (up to five) feels the opposite and will turn against you for a round.
                                1 Success: About 3 targets could go either way with a little coaxing.
                                2 to 3 Successes: Up to 3 targets will rally to your cause as long as it doesn't go against their ideals.
                                4 to 5 Successes: Up to ten people have no problem rallying for you as long as they don't get too badly hurt.
                                6 or More: Up to 25 people will drop what they're doing, regardless of beliefs or danger to your cause.

                                NOTE: If used for nefarious purposes, a Morality check must be made.

                                    ITEMS OF FAITH

                                    These special Talents are akin to artifacts in Dungeons and Dragons. Thus they are something that the ST must not give away to the players at the start without a good reason.

                                    Armor of Judgement
                                    Description:                                     This golden breastplate weighs 20 lbs and held together with gold rings tied with blue ribbons. On the chest are seven various gems, each the size of a golf ball. The Talent can be worn under a jacket or a loose, baggy top.
                                    System: The Talent works just like a regular breastplate, but with a twist. Each time someone attacks the wearer, a gem shatters and the attacker receives a point of damage in return (it can be soaked). The gem can only be replaced by one of the same size (worth at least $500) and three Favors are used to activated.

                                    Claws of Fenris
                                    Description:                                     Resembling Freddy Kruger's hand glove, but with the knives being replaced with deadly wolf claws of the same length. When worn, it will automatically fit on the wear's hand no matter which one she has it on.
                                    System: The Talent, when used like a regular weapon does Strength +1 damage on a successful Dexterity + Brawl roll. When three favors are activated, the user transforms into a nine-foot tall, 800-pound Werewolf with dark gray fur for the rest of the battle or until it is shut off.
                                    Statistic Adjustments: Strength +4, Dexterity +1, Stamina +3
                                    NOTES: While the user has no access to a normal Werewolf's extra abilities, she can still use her Miracles, talk as normal, but still has the vulnerability to silver.

Any Demon/Vampire that comes in contact with it, is treated as if they've been hit with sunlight (Vampires) or holy energy (Demons) that does damage per Sin point (this can be soaked.)

                                    NOTE: Infernalists, Demons, Mortals with a Humanity score of 5 or lower or Mystics with a sin rating of 5 or higher try to access the crown's powers, the thorns will start to burrow into her head (a half inch her round); causing a point of aggravated damage until it is removed or the wearer dies.                                     System: This Talent allows the user to access the Flaming Sword Miracle and by spending two Favors, she can access a portal to the Umbral realm of Eden. As she found more excuses for Adam to join her in bed, a sinister and almost alien voice began suggesting darker ideas. Thanks to this voice, Carla was able to manipulate Adam into believing that it would "make him less of a man" if people found out, but what really convinced him was the fear that him and his little brother, Tom, would be separated. So she promised that as long as Adam fulfilled his mother's desires, she'd leave Tom alone.

                                                    Since it allowed him to be free from his mother, Adam dived head-first into every aspect of school he could get his hands on from honors society to the school newspaper. It was tiring work, but it was a worth while distraction. The rest of his time was spent with Tom, doting on and playing with him. Most of all, Adam wanted to protect his innocence.

                                                    This all worked until Adam was 16 and Tom was 9. With tears in his Tom’s eyes, Adam learned that their mother had broken her promise by "touching him". Enraged, Adam vowed to get him and his brother out of town by stealing their mom's car and hawking off the family heirlooms. Overhearing their plans, Carla was horrified and begged the voice to give her the power to stop them. Transformed into deformed creature, she locked all the doors into the house and attacked her sons just as Adam received his Revelation and easily killed his mother. When Officer Teller came to investigate a "disturbances" coming from the house, Adam dropped his mother's corpse at his feet and left.

                                                    Current Status: Adam always dreamed of traveling the country with his brother with nothing but a Triple-A guidebook to lead them. Now his greatest wish has been fulfilled as him and Tom see the wonders of the country and help those in need.

                                                    Appearance: In a mid-90's boy band kind of way, Adam is quite attractive. Even being on the road, he still has a clean-cut look in both clothing and his short, brown hair. His Aura gives off a fierce protectiveness with a touch of youthful hot-hotheadedness. As for Tom, he looks like a younger, skinny and blonde version of his brother.

                                                    Role-Playing Notes: Your Revelation has changed you for the better and for the worst. On the bright side, you are able to stand up for the injustices around you and have become more optimistic about your future than you ever been. Since you've now dived into your role as a warrior for The New God, it has brought out a hot-headed side of you that likes to come out when you believe you are in the right. For you would rather die than see anyone suffer like that.

                                                    Note: Despite what Adam thinks, Tom knows that Adam often cried himself to sleep before his Revelation and believes that Carla was probably "touching" him as well. He has yet to ask Adam about this, but is in no hurry to try. Tom is generally a sweet and curious child that treasures the extra time his brother can spend with him.

                                                    As a PC: If the ST is comfortable with role-playing Tom or having another player use the boy, Adam can be playable since he won't leave his brother for a long period of time. While Adam can be a team player, he might set some sparks in certain groups.

                                                    As an NPC: One obvious scenario is helping him if Tom gets kidnapped. Since Adam is wanderer, he can help the PCs with transportation or become an enigmatic helper in the party's time of need.

                                                        Penelope Maradox (Deadman)

                                                        History: Whenever someone waxes nostalgic for the morality of the past, it never fails to make Penelope burst into laughter. Spending her childhood in the 50's and her teenage years during the Civil Rights movement, Penelope knew these were far from happy times for someone of her race.

                                                        At the time, she encountered acts of racism and cruelty that most Black youth these days wouldn't believe. But the biggest struggle she had face was with her own misanthropy and faith. How could a loving god let such cruelty happen? How could he back up radio evangelists who praised the morality of segregation without sending a bolt of lightning down on them for blasphemy? Why did it let her father bleed to death when she was ten because it went against the doctor's moral code to treat a black person?

                                                        Sure the Civil Rights Movement helped restore a little of her faith in humanity and she was glad to help out, but it all feel down after the assassinations of Martin Luther King Jr. and Malcolm X. If the world was going to play dirty when true goodness tried to make an impact, then fuck it. Let the world go and destroy itself while she sat back, flipped it the bird and watched.

                                                        Despite her misanthropy, Penelope had always been a smart and confident woman, but it was her timing that would do her in. When the Satanism panic of the early 80's hit, her neighbor, Tessie Roberts, took it to heart. At first Penelope tried to ignore her paranoia during neighborhood meetings, but it only grew worse until she told Tessie off in front of the entire neighborhood for how she was scaring everyone and making a food of herself. With the rest of the neighbors taking Penelope's side, Tessie quietly stewed in her defeat until her paranoia over her neighbor would go into overdrive when a local boy went missing and was later found dead in a river. The police ruled it as an accidental drowning, but Tessie knew that it was all Penelope's doing and convinced her husband to help kidnap her and hang Penelope for her crimes.

                                                        While Tessie got out of serving time in jail by reasons of temporary insanity (her husband killed himself in prison a year later out of guilt) and was eventually released from the asylum she was sent to five years later little did she know that years later Penelope would come back.

                                                        Current Status: After visiting Tessie, who succumbed to an fatal heart attack (which she was powerless to stop), Penelope has begun her holy mission with a quiet zeal. After learning that Tessie was diagnosed with paranoid schizophrenia, Penelope has empathy for the "screwed-up twit." She is being helped out by member of the Angel-Watchers Society who is being considered for membership to the Ordeu of Angelous.

                                                        Appearance: Even after nearly two decades, Penelope doesn't look a day over thirty-five. She still has her milk chocolate colored skin, loose, wavy black hair and her eyes still radiate that fierce intelligence she had when she was still alive. The only things that have changed are her wardrobe and the rope marks on her neck. Penelope's sense of style is fairly casual with a slight skimpiness in her choice of tops. While she has no real embarrassment towards her Sign, she will wear either a choker, scarf, or use make-up to keep the Mortals from freaking out. Her Aura enhances the confidence she naturally radiated in life with a twist of there being something almost dangerous about her.

                                                        Role-Playing Notes: A true wildcard, you are not one who usually plays by polite society's rules. Not just because your methods are unorthodox, but that you see so-called "polite society" as laughably idiotic. It was nothing more than the need for scapegoats.

                                                        You take pride in your cynicism and your new mission in life that you take rather seriously. Some people think you are a bit of bitch, but you honestly have a caring side that strives for justice (it's just hard to see at first). You are definitely not the easiest person to get along with, though those that you do have a friend for life. You also never really liked your name and prefer to be called, Maradox.

                                                        As a PC: PC or NPC, Penelope is likely to start a few fires, especially with more sensitive or conservative characters. Since computers still ran on floppies the last time she was alive, she will need a bit of help on the technological aspects.

                                                        As an NPC: On one angle, Penelope can be played to represent the darker side of the Celestials, proving that they can be just as dark as any other Mystic. Play on her moral ambiguity to really throw the PCs for a loop.

                                                            Margie Bergstrum (Infused)

                                                            History: There are some people out there who might consider Margie a loser with her endless career of jumping from dead-end job to dead-end job, the highlight of her week being Full-Frontal Friday (where Margie and her friends rent a non-porno movie with full-frontal nudity) and a bad-luck streak a mile long.

                                                            Eventually Margie's life took an unexpected turn when her grouchy neighbor, Mr. Vanderholt, knocked on her door. Just as she opened it, the broken, bloody form of the old man collapsed in her arms and with his dieing breath, grabbed her wrist and told her, "Carry on for the sake of the New God." A surge of power entered Margie's body, transforming her into one of the Infused and leaving her alone in a part of the world that most Mortals never get to see.

                                                            The idea of becoming a superhero was tempting, but she decided against it. Besides, she has some more important things to worry about than designing a costume. Mr. Vanderholt had granted her the responsibility of reading and taking care of The Tome of Ancients. The haste fully written visions of the history of the Angels, First-Born and Demons could be a great accent to her fellow Celestials if a few pages weren't missing.

                                                            Current Status: With a new purpose in her life, Margie has been trying to be the best Infused she can be, but she has learned that looking for crimes to solve isn't as easy at the media portrays it as and has given up on that idea. The other problem is finding the rest of Mr. Vanderholt's notes that could either be with the police or the people who killed him.

                                                            Appearance: The best way to physically describe Margie is to picture a more toned-down version of Excel from the Excel Saga. Margie wears her heart on her sleeve so how she feels is shown upon her to the fullest degree. Her clothes tend to be very casual (due to a limited supply of money) and can be seen in her nightshirt well past noon if she has a day to herself.

                                                            Role-Playing Notes: You may come off as a bit of a goof, but there is more to you than many less caring and empathic people notice. You're a very brave, dedicated (if not somewhat hyper person when something excites you) who just wants to make the world a better place. Life has never been all that easy for you, but you never let it get you down even if it does make you depressed at times. (But that's nothing your tireless devotion can't fix and reality show marathons can't fix.) On the downside, you tend to have a bit of an inferiority complex that likes to pop up around those who are luckier and more competent than you.
