    In my games I've separated those who wanna protect and stand by mankind and/or the planet (Reconcilers) with those who wanna seek amendment from God and be let back into the warmth (Pentients, I'm so original with names!). There are also "Unaligned Demons" which I call "Renegades."
    As far as my Reconcilers and Pentients go;
    The former tend to get along decently with Pentients cuz most of them seek to do good to please God and Faustians because their short-term goals both tend to benefit humanity. They do not get along at all with Raveners who are on the opposite end of the spectrum while Luciferans and Cryptics are fairly neutral.

    The latter, aside from Reconcilers, tend to work well with Cryptics as they feel their knowledge-seeking might solve the mystery of where God went and how to get to him. Their main opposition are the Luciferans for obvious reasons and in the long-term they would be opposed to the Faustians given the whole 'make a weapon against God' thing; though in the short-term they tend to stick away from open conflicts. Raveners are also a big enemy as to the Pentients they are the epitome of evil and even those who don't see it as a sacred duty to fight them generally want them gone because of how they ruin everything.

    And Renegades well, anything runs with them much like any unaligned splat.
    What do you think?

                        Pardon the bad English and grammar, aint my first language

                        Apocalyptic Forms 101
                        Or How to become Nightmare Fuel.

                        Im starting to write down rules on how to create apocalyptic forms based on my homebrews.



                        What is an Apocalyptic form?.


                        Hell Is Other People

                        Its an extension of the Elohim's real nature which bleeds through the mortal carcass. It’s the pain and anger, the self mutilation and cannibalism of the instinct of the angel who fell from grace.



                        - I Love You. That’s why I must hate you.
                        “The Thing cannot be described - there is no language for such abysms of shrieking and immemorial lunacy, such eldritch contradictions of all matter, force, and cosmic order. A mountain walked or stumbled.”
                        • H.P Lovecraft. The Call of Cthulhu


                        Rule of thumb, the more torment the player has, the more nightmare fuelish it looks like..but should this always as a rule?. Personally, no. Its more, i think its more disturbing when it looks more sacred. Personally, i see that some of the manifestation's are related to the character's nature and demeanor. A Devil could easly have Wings that look torn and shredded...but for dramatic purpouse, if the character has a Nature/Demeanor of someone who (-in a way- brings hope, it would make sense that his wings are radiant and shining like the ones of angels. After all, one of the "Devil's Tricks." its that he doesnt looks like a monster but like a beautiful angel.

                        To me, angels are creatures so abstract and far from reach that they become Alien-Esque even.

                        https://themonstermaven.files.wordpress.com/2014/01/cherubim_11.jpg

                        https://s-media-cache-ak0.pinimg.com/236x/fe/0a/e0/fe0ae03960433f7acee0db6647d7fa51.jpg

                        http://41.media.tumblr.com/8877be9f81dd86efa946b45914222683/tumblr_n0rjwadbcQ1qz5q5lo1_400.jpg


                        http://40.media.tumblr.com/01c325ac8b50a1735595e18fe4d08a00/tumblr_n9ygrsO1NU1tu9py2o4_r2_500.jpg

                        https://s-media-cache-ak0.pinimg.com/736x/2c/52/05/2c5205f58f2f98fa4e60f69793ace8e4.jpg



                        Angels are horrying creatures made of nothing but loyalty and love. Asexual and wanting to do Her bidding.
                        “Love is the law, love under will.”
                        ― Aleister Crowley, The Book of the Law
                        Theres nothing more terryfing that the things we do for Love, for feel special. To be able to feel something and for someone to correspond it. They dress us, address us and makes us feel part of a bigger thing. We are Whole.

                        So, Whats the Anti-Love? How withered is the soul after not just being heart brokened but being tortured and shattered beyond comprehension? Will we still wear the same dresses we were given? Will we scream in rage, in blood, in putrid profanity against He/She/It who loves thy?.
                        The Fallen ones are not only Hatred against Her, but sadness, sorrow, fear and self loathing. Some obsessed, some down with the stockholm’s syndrome, some feeling themselves out of an abusive relation. To me, the demon’s apocalyptic form its not only a manifestation of the horror that leaks through the human carcass, but a cry and a whimper of “¿Why?”.
                        In my games I describe the act of turning inside out, of the angel ripping his own wings off and self mutilating in all his rage and hatred against himself, or against her. Like a little child who gets tossed away with out saying what or why. Like being abandoned by a loved one and never being mature enough to comprehend your condition.
                        Love is a horrible thing, which makes us do terrible things.


                        - 4 Good and 4 Bad.

                        Usually the player has a grand total of 8 apocalyptic forms. theres two ways to use these when it comes to character creation and roleplay. One, give the player 4 basic ones or let him choose em. And through RP/Gaining torment. the player unlocks slowly other 4 based on his action and perceptions, as metaphors and symbolisms of how "His Name Changed." through this experience

                        The other way, it’s the simple one. To let the player pick all 8 on his character creation and roll with it.

                        -Dominion
                        “Nearly all men can stand adversity, but if you want to test a man's character, give him power.”
                        ― Abraham Lincoln

                        In my games, i give something to the demons called or Dominion. To me, there’s an connection between what the Elohim created to its creation.
                        The dominion mainly depends only on the player and the angel’s backstory.
                        Days of Fire states that theres a place for every angel, and every angel for a place. And the book already states that angels were able to create everything that existed.
                        The best way I can use to translate this concept is the idea of Spheres on Mage the ascension but a little bit more limited. Example
                        Dominion one: you can sense things related to your original creation
                        Dominion two: Atmosfear: causing a small manifestation of your will. Example.
                        Liriel is a devil who collaborated in the creation of light as an electrical source. He may roll conscience at a difficulty of 7 to make lights turn off on a room, or a small electronic device. Nothing bigger than that. The difficulty is determinate by the GM based on how big or how much of a theatrical effect would it make as well the amount of witnesses and how much fear or creeped out are they
                        Dominion three :Altering States: By rolling Conscience, the player might use his dices as bonifiers or penalizers related to the objective.
                        Alastor, angel of vengeance is fighting a man who violently killed his fiancée. He rolls in order to call upon a Reckoning on the assailant. He gets 2 successes. He decides to use both successes to increasing his damage by 2 lethal damage if He connects. He could also use it to give himself a lower dodging difficulty since he has a vengeance oath against his target.
                        The player is only limited by how big and massive it is, the ST must keep in mind one sacred rule..Drama. im aware that combat wise this can be too op, so its up to the ST if he decides to use this sytem. I use it since some of my players are limited when it comes to Pacts due the difficulty of a demon using Light in order to create a pact with a mortal who’s needs don’t fit what the demon may offer.

                        Creating Apocalyptic forms.
                        If I can’t inspire love, I will inspire fear!.
                        Frankenstein’s Monster, Mary Shelley.

                        Creating apocalyptic forms is something that’s been discussed a lot between players and ST’s. so, im going to Try and make something more cohesive.
                        The problem about apocalyptic forms is that it’s a bit delicated when it comes to create them, since its pretty easy for them to be derailed by the players.
                        So. Lets start by some basics.
                        We will need the Player’s guide as some sort of Walkthrough. Or for a basis.
                        Can we use the core book? Sure, but I find it a bit limitated when it comes to options.

                        First things first. In order to create an apocalyptic form we will need to answer some questions, some might lead to more questions that I haven’t considered yet, this is good.
                        Is it a General Form or a House Exclusive.
                        Is it an apocalyptic form that Any demon may have or does it belong to a certain house?
                        If it’s a general or Common form. Does it exist already? Can be physically altered so it resembles what youre looking for?
                        Example: Joe’s character. Crowley is a Halaku heavily inspired by Spawn and Hellraiser. He wants Chains like tentacles coming from his body. So the ST suggest him to buy Extra limbs (3) and Claws/Teeths (1) so the chains have hooks in every end. System wise, it works as a Combo

                        If its something you want exclusive to your house. Remember that houses can (Somehow) be divided by their Aspects or Stereotypes.
                        Example: if we are dealing with a Slayer, he would usually see things related to death to pick as an apocalyptic form. Stealth, ghosts, shadows, silence or the grim reaper. James Character. Clarissiel wants his apocalyptic form to be something more intrusive. Having in mind the idea that the grim reaper has no obstacles, he talks it to the GM and creates the Apocalyptic Form “Death knows no Obstacles.” Which allows him to phase through objects and surfaces. Since it’s a pretty game breaking/changing apocalyptic form, the GM decides to add some weaknesses.
                        Such as fire still being able to hit him and that he cant phase if theres crosses around the house, he may phase through vehicles or bullets by taking stunning damage instead of lethal damage and spending one point of faith in order to do so. The gm decides that the cost of such form should be 4. And to move through objects he will have to do a roll of Stamina + Athletics.

                        To which skill is the apocalyptic form related to.
                        Is it social?, is it for combat, is it for stealth, is for an element or concept (such as fire, light, water, death), is it biological or is it supernatural. By biological I mean, poisons or toxins, spines and scales. By supernatural I mean voices of the dead or seeing how long has someone before dying.

                        With that being said, is it an area effect. Target effect, personal effect –external or personal effect internal.
                        By external is something that manifest out of the character (Wings for example). Internal is something that happends within the character and that human’s cant see.

                        Is it Humanoid, or Bestial like?
                        Mainly for showing off, will the demon keep a humanoid visage or he will looks less and less human.

                        is a torment related apocalyptic form or is neutral?
                        When using it, will people have nightmare fuel or will it slowly be degraded as the permanent torment of the player’s character increases.

                        Can it be done through Lores?
                        If you can, then why do you need the apocalyptic form? :P

                        Costs.
                        The smaller and more “subtle” it is the lesser the cost should be. The more spectacular and showing off it is, the highest. Area effects, shields, regeneration based on elements should cost 4 points. While things like fangs/claws, change eyes/hair colour, perception related, lashing tail or things should cost less (1).

                        Limits of the Apocalyptic forms.
                        They are only related to the demon the fallen mythos. So, no umbra, no arcadia, no mage or technocracy related.
                        They are not related to technology or modernity. These are ancient forms which happened to be useful in today’s modern world
                        Can apocalyptic forms reach dream’s of the mortal? Personally, it’s a big no no in my games.
                        Can a demon create apocalyptic forms related to the wyrm?. You could take some cues from Freak Legion, sure… though if you meet a werewolf, well…that’s your problem .


                        And now for something completely different.

                        The Taboos of the Fallen.

                        On my game theres “Taboos.” That demon’s don’t cross, no because they cant. But because they shoudnt. More than an strict line, see it as a guideline, these might be broken though.

                        One. Never mating in apocalyptic form or in true visage.


                        One word. Nephilims.
                        Two. Demons shall not intervene in the world of man.

                        Except for the most extremist ones, demons consider that mankind is already doomed by their own actions, so most of them try not to get involved with massive events such as wars, school shootings, serial killers etc..
                        Three. No summon angels. We are in this together now. We don’t need God in here anymore.
                        Fourth: We are Alone in this Fight. What ever god has created its now our enemy. For they will try to harvest our power for their own goals.
                        Fifth: All states, and countries should have at least one Fallen Reign leaded by its Tyrant, followed by an Infernal court.
                        Sixth: only a Tyrant may allow the Invocation of any fallen ones on the Abyssm
                        Seventh: Consuming some other brother’s essence is forbidden
