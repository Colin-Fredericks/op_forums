    Mage, Ronin, Revenent and Fomorus - Crossover and logic behind it

    03-25-2018, 11:09 AM
    Here's the idea. A rich and powerful Ventrue Elder wants control of a city. He feels like to get an edge on the competition he needs some forces which act as Unknowns. The other Elders know Kindred too well to be defeated through conventional means, so he decides to put out feelers for unusual supernatural creatures who he can employ and control.

    I feel like a Ronin, Revenent, Fomorus and Mage would be fitting choices.

    Revenent gets the protection of a powerful domitor, and can increase their Discipline levels above 1.

    Fomorus is addicted to a Pentex product which has been discontinued, and thanks to the Contacts and Resources of the Elder he can have as much as he wants, for a price. This serum keeps him young and strong, and is the source of his Powers.

    Ronin is disillusioned from the Garou, since the only Tribe they tried to join rejected them. They're young and carefree, and getting to do a job where their curse is valued, exalted, is amazing. Plus he can live in luxury in his early 20s, as long as he does the odd job for his employer.

    Mage... I'm drawing a blank. I can't see why a Mage would work for a Vampire, and how the power balance would work between them. What could a Mage really, really need? Money doesn't feel like a compelling answer for me. Mages can make money themselves after all, one way or another. Perhaps the Ventrue is in control of a powerful Mage-related item, and working for them allows them access to the book/relic/whatever.

    Questions for the forum:

    - How would you work power balance in such a case? Our Ronin is stuck with level 1 gifts, our Revenent has high EXP prices, and our Fomorus and Mage can spend their EXP quite efficiently to gain power.

    - What would strengthen their motivations to work for such an individual?

    - How would they work together? And what would a Kindred need them for? What do they bring to the table?

      If the Ventrue is careful and clever enough not to get backstabbed this could be the edge he needs to perform a coup well above his station.

An up and coming young mage wouldn't mind making friends with the new prince of a city after all. Few can counter the Technocracy bureaucratically like a Ventrue Prince.

                      Building on the relationship between the Ronin and the Ventrue...

                      Our Ronin grows up as a ward of the state. Mother in jail, father off the scene, and becomes homeless at 15. He learns to live with the fact that society has rejected him, and that "good" people will look the other way when it suits them. He's got a hunger, and he survives by begging and stealing.

                      At 16, he goes through the First Change. His newborn Rage alienates the few friends he has, and he is lost until Garou find him. Finally, he might belong. Glass Walkers try to induct him, but the Totem rejects him and he is cast out. This only deepens his sense of rejection. He is now a pariah among what are meant to be his kin.

                      Now 17, a hungry Ventrue with a taste for homeless blood attempts to chow down on what looks like a normal kine. Panicking, and mildly resistant to the Kiss, our Ronin takes wolf form and escapes. The shocked Ventrue reports the appearance of this homeless youth to his Sire...

                      The Elder, upon meeting the Ronin, takes a genuine liking to him. He can see a real hunger and alienation in this young man. Seducing him with Presence and tempting him with expensive gifts, the Elder essentially establishes an asexual "sugar dating" situation with the young Werewolf. Storytelling this I would make it deeply uncomfortable. Never play down the fact that this young man has been rejected by society and the Garou nation, and that for the first time in his life he is fulfilled and supported, and not only that his curse is celebrated and treated as something special with this Ventrue... but at the same time don't shy away from the fact that a 150+ year old undead and manipulative man is controlling a 17 year old with money and intimacy and emotional control, both mundane and supernatural. Ideally it should challenge people.

                      Our Ronin would be deeply defensive of the Elder, and have a total attachment complex. Terrified of rejection, terrified of failing the man he loves, terrified of being alone again. Generally scornful of society and "good" people, as he's been on the other side of things and seen all of society's ills, leaving him somewhat amoral. Easily stressed, and unfortunately prone to Frenzy. Being rejected by two societies and learning to cope with the curse alone has left him with a high Rage rating.

                                  Story Beats:

                                  Elder needs you to be incognito security (disguised as catering) at a classy business event. Keep his Ghoul alive, prevent interference from potential threats, protect the Masquerade.

                                  Elder needs the 4 team members to trawl through all of the articles seized from the compound where the Psychic was "acquired". Unfortunately there are still some dangerous living inventory within the storage, like a pack of Warwolves (Pentex experiment, they are like Garou but mindless and only have Crinos and Wolf forms). A Black Team sent by a Pentex branch arrives to claim inventory and destroy anything dangerous halfway through. The idea is to tie the Psychic's backstory into a session so people get to know them.

                                  Elder needs the team to deal with a problematic group of Garou who are interfering with the Camarilla's business, and his own. This pack includes some Garou who were present when the Ronin failed the rite to enter a tribe.

                                  The Dhampir/Revenent tries to discover their origin, as they don't know the identity of their mother (who is either a thinblood or a revenent). They cash in a favour the Elder owes them from a previous session to get the information they need, and the help of the others. The path to discovering what happened to their mother leads them through some dangerous scenarios, naturally.

                                  The Prince has placed a bounty on a pack of infernalist kindred, who include a member of the illustrious Red List. The Elder tries to handle the situation so he can claim some hefty boons. Among the infernalists is a Nephandus who the Mage has encountered before, who represents a toxic and subverted version of the Mage's paradigm.

                                  As a finale, the Elder is killed by a rival Kindred after being betrayed and outmaneuvered, leaving his coterie of "peculiars" high and dry. Do they just aim to escape with their lives? Do they stick together? Or do they try and take over all of the wealth and power their employer had collected, by force?
