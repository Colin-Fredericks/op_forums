    Car Chase Rules?

    03-19-2018, 11:18 AM
    I've poured through my books, and maybe I'm missing something, but are there rules for car chases? I'm thinking the player would be rolling Dex+Drive, but I can't find anything to back that up

    If it helps, the scene will be set in Brooklyn/Bronx, late at night (0200 or 0300). The players will have access to a SUV and are being pursued by a Sabbat pack driving a panel van

      M20 has a chapter on this, beginning on page 458. Vehicles have a lot of stats, most important for a car chase may be "Save Speed" (every 10mph above Safe Speed adds +1 to difficulty), "Max Speed" (guess what, that's how fast the car can get), "Maneuverability" (the maximum amount of dice the driver may use; especially important for heavy trucks, tanks, etc), "Durability" (health levels of the vehicle) and "Structure" (Soak).

      Stunt Driving get's described as a Dexterity + Drive action with no fixed difficulty. Personally, depending on the situation, Wits might also be a good stat if you want to react to anything happening on the streets.
      Ramming with a car deals the car's Durability in Bashing Damage + 1 for each 10mph the car was driving at the moment of the crash. If it's a crash and not just rolling over some poor guy, the crew inside the car also takes half the damage (if strapped in, if not all bets are off). Here, I'd personally take an approach where I halve the damage and make it lethal instead, depending on the situation.
      And if you want to shoot somebody from inside the car, assume a -1 to -3 dice penalty depending on speed. Characters in the car are protected (aka the car takes the damage from the attack) unless the attack is targeted at the window (+3 to +5 difficulty depending on speed or how big the window is) or scores 4 or more successes (Threshold rules, I'd guess).

      The book also states that a car explosion deals 12/Agg damage. Considering of how hard it is to blow up a car (unless you live in Hollywood Land), it is totally up to you if you need this info.

              Every set of rules has some drawbacks and bad simulation, you just have to devise and use house rules for those situations.

              Here's the Car Race rules I used in my last session, they worked out pretty enjoyable but still need a bit of work:
              Stages
              1. Across the Forth bridge - fast, light traffic
              2. A90 & Queensferry Road - fast, moderate traffic
              3. Telford Rd roundabout & ferry road - moderate traffic
              4. Leith - slow urban roads
              5. Docks - fast urban roads

              Each stage requires a Dex+Drive roll and position is determined by total successes. Difficulty is determined by traffic/road conditions:
              Condition Target
              Light traffic/A roads/motorway 5
              Moderate traffic/suburban roads 6
              Heavy traffic/urban roads 7
              Fast -1
              Slow +1

              Opportunity Deck

              Draw five cards before the race, draw a new one each time one is used. You may attempt as many opportunities as you like but must try at least one per stage.

              Hearts
              This is an opportunity to be brave. Describe what you are doing and make a Willpower check against the difficulty (see below) to succeed. If you succeed you get back any spent Willpower points.

              Diamonds
              This is an opportunity to be clever. Describe what you are doing and roll the highest of Wits/Int/Per plus Drive (or another skill) against the difficulty (see below) to succeed.

              Clubs
              This is an opportunity that requires toughness as you smash your vehicle roughly around. Describe what you are doing and roll Sta plus Drive against the difficulty (see below) minus three to succeed (reward difficulties are similarly lowered). This costs you a health level (no soak).

              Spades
              This is a dangerous opportunity where you attempt to take another driver out of the race. Describe what you are doing and make an opposed Dex+Drive roll against the difficulty (see below) to succeed. The winner takes five successes from the loser, but if you botch you have crashed, are out of the race, and possibly badly wounded (roll a number of dice equal to the Drive roll difficulty vs difficulty 6 and take that many health levels of unsoakable damage). You may not spend Willpower points to gain automatic successes.

              Number Cards
              If the card is a number card then the number is the target difficulty, however, if the opportunity succeeds, roll a number of dice equal to the number against difficulty 6 to determine how many extra successes are added to the race total.

              Face Cards
              Face cards allow you to swap places (i.e. total successes) with another racer. Target difficulty is seven.

              Aces
              Aces are wild - choose your own difficulty as for Number Cards above.

              Jokers
              Jokers are crazy. Describe something crazily dangerous and roll Dex+Drive to succeed, choose your own difficulty but add a number of successes to your total equal to the difficulty.
