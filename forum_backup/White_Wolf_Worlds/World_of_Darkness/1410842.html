    What about Secret Conventions that most of the Other Technocrats don't know exist?

    08-28-2020, 10:09 AM
    Just a thought how the NWO has a lot of control over thought and global psychology and well pretty much they seem to be the quintessential Technocrats. Control is a hyper secret and uber compromised Controlling Organ of the Technocracy. So what if there are secret facets of the Technocracy hidden from the vast majority of the Organization. What I am first thinking of is a sort of Time Cop Convention. Like I think back to Star Trek and the Department of Temporal Investigations who are in charge of keeping the Timeline clean, and others like Immortus and the Council of Kangs obsessed over control of the Timeline. An Adjudicators of Temporal Harmony Convention would be interesting, as it could explore the idea of the Technocracy managing a hidden front of the Ascension War normally only observed by Archmagi. They could have their handful with incidents similar to the Week of Nightmares and the normies among the Awakened would be none the wiser. It could also represent a weird notion that Control is aware of the wider Multiverse and have some level of communication with their counterparts in other Timelines/Universe.

    I can see Agents being recruited from the vetted newly awakened and essentially disappearing as they join the ranks of Time Barristers or Magistrates from the Past and Future. And if used as PCs then you could have justifiable cause to make characters with really out there backstories like folks from a collapsed Timeline that experienced the Time of Judgement or fell to Gehenna, or just an Enlightened Artisan who was just too revolutionary for the timetable and removed from Sorcerers Crusade Era.

    They could even be twin group with one assigned to Parallel Worlds while the Temporal group handles the actual timelines. I guess Correspondence would be a good one for the sister organization, even though Multiverse travel is kind of beyond normal levels of Correspondence, So maybe a Prime Convention to link the Three Conventions?

    What are your thoughts on the Idea of Secret Conventions? They could even be outside Control's well control and could be very carefully unknotting the mess that is the compromised Control.

                  A few years ago the Mage players became aware of a secret Convention called Section VII and, ultimately, its nefarious purpose. The Convention was very interested the power of belief and myth-making. Magic shouldn't happen, but it does. There were dragons, but the Consensus drove them away. Enlightened operatives and, if Ivory Tower records are any guide, Tradition mages seem guided by personified forms of inspiration that are known to reappear over successive generations. Some of these "avatars" (to use the Reality Deviant term) inspire their human associates to become really, really powerful (or at least very annoying and resistant to easy removal).

                  But maybe those avatars could go the way of dragons? Not exactly, but Section VII found that by influencing media, folklore, urban legend, and commercialization, they could inhibit the return of some avatars. It's hard to be inspired by a being that is completely different than your conceptualization of it. One of their greatest successes was with the Akashic Brother who called himself Po Banyan, whose shining blue ki-rin avatar, Bae, would physically manifest for Sleepers to see when the mage drew upon his strange abilities. His staff could knock down trees, though he put more effort into knocking out the loggers harvesting them.

                  Likewise, John Chapman, a mage following his own calling, produced cider that enhanced one's appreciation of the world around them, of togetherness and being at ease. He planted his apples across the country, and even (inadvertently) influenced presidential candidate William H Harrison into adopting the log cabin and hard cider as campaign themes. This was strongly at odds with the Technocratic view of human industry. The Progenitors quickly took care of President Harrison; Section VII dealt with Mr Chapman a few years later. This wasn't as successful, as his legend is deeply ingrained in the American psyche, but by manipulating what was known, they've manipulated what can be. They worked with Henrietta Lacks, too, but in a manner too convoluted to go into.

                  Because this line of inquiry required acceptance of so much esoteric, mystical nonsense, Section VII was kept quite secret from rest of the Technocracy. It was also, ultimately, a failure. The avatar, Bae, returned at the Chronicle's end after the PC's found the staff of Po Banyan, and is ready to be part of a new mage's Awakening. They also discovered the last two hidden barrels of John Chapman's apple jack and cider, the results of which are still rippling through a second chronicle (I had two concurrent games with different players; the 2nd of those games is ongoing). You just can't keep a good avatar down.

                                  In general, when a group in Mage gets “wiped out” but nonetheless manages to survive, it reappears shortly thereafter in some other guise. this is because in order to operate, you need to have some sort of front group. The most successful example of this are the Ksirafai, who disappeared from the Order of Reason only to have been revealed centuries later to have infiltrated the Order of Hermes as House Janissary. Other destroyed groups tended to re-emerge as Crafts, like the Children of Knowledge or the Templar, formerly Solificati and Cabal of Pure Thought, respectively.

                                  So if the Craftmasons survived, what's their front group?
