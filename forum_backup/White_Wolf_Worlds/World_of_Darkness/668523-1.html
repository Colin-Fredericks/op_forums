                Originally posted by Primal Flame View Post

                One thing that always bothered me is the need for different critters/cosmologies in Asia. The Thousand Hells idea looks nice, but why is it tied to Asia?
                The Yama Kings were powerful spirits who basically ripped huge chunks of the Umbra/Shadowlands apart, corrupted them, and then went out and captured wraiths and shoved them into their kingdoms. No other spirits managed to replicate that feat, which is why the Thousand Hells are unique to to that part of the world and can't be found everywhere else. So the simplest explanation is that most likely spirits in other parts of the world tried to do something similar, but presumably either they were incapable of replicating the feat or they were prevented from doing so (by werewolves, mages, etc).

                Of course, certain places like the Atrocity Realm, Scar or the Abyss seem to be a little similar in some ways to the Thousand Hells so possibly there may be a link.
