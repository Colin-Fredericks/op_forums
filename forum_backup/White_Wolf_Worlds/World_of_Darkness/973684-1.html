    Originally posted by Frozenfishy View Post
    Spirit can be an awfully broken Sphere if allowed to be, but it also comes with all kinds of inconvenient drawbacks, catches, and caveats.
    Very true. In theory you can replicate all the other spheres effects with Spirit simply by knowing who to talk to. The biggest downside is reputation. The more you rely on the spirits, the more they expect from you in return, and the moment your goals deviate from theirs, say goodbye to god-like powers and hello to a whole mess of problems. Also, spirits have their own internal rivalry going on, and striking a deal with one brood may make other broods look unfavourably upon you. The spirit sphere makes the most use out of those social attributes.

                                                    Originally posted by Caitiff Primogen View Post
                                                    I've always viewed the Syndicate as being pretty squarely in the middle of the Technocracy's scale-of-gray. This is an entirely personal perspective that I make no claim to being objectively true, but I usually rank the Conventions "ethically" from Void Engineers -> Progenitors -> Syndicate -> Iteration X -> NWO (most to least)
                                                    Really? I've generally placed it roughly in this order, Void Engineers -> Iteration X (Post Avatar Storm) -> NWO -> Progenitors -> Iteration X (Pre-Avatar Storm) -> Syndicate.

                                                    Originally posted by Michael View Post
                                                    It-X are kinda interesting. I think their convention book wants to portray them as the a-political scientists. There's a recurring theme that they're individually quite nice people, their flaw is that they don't question the authority of the NWO. 'Course, then the later books bring up the Computer again and we get back into the traditional dodgy territory.
                                                    I never really got that vibe at all. Rather I got the vibe that they were intensely, militantly political. The entire convention identifies itself by service to the Technocracy. While the Void Engineers and Syndicate and whatnot are all plotting and scheming and sort of acting in their conventions best interest. Iteration X puts the Technocracy first. They are, and have been for ages, THE foot soldiers of the ascension war. The NWO has traditionally focused on conversion rather than killing. And the Iterators were there for the opposite.

                                                    They are, from top to bottom, a convention that has built itself to pick up the slack that the other conventions have dropped because of their scheming. They build the equipment. They make the primium, they fight the wars. They neither have the time nor the inclination to be 'nice'. Because if they stop.... well the war falters.

                                                    And then the pogrom ends. And the computer disappears. You have a convention that has for so long, set aside personal ambitions in order to focus on getting things done.... no longer needing to get things done. A convention who's members include thousands upon thousands of soldiers, suddenly with no enemies to fight. So what do they do? They go looking for one. Iterators post avatar storm are both spoiling for a fight, and suddenly given much more time to think about the morality of what they're doing.

                                                    Though, depending on the Era, whether that makes them jackbooted thugs or power-armored paladins is anyones guess.
