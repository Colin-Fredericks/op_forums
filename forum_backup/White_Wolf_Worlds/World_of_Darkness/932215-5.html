                                V20 Fortitude does not give a passive bonus to Stamina, it only gives a passive bonus to Stamina for the purposes of soaking bashing and level damage. The difference may seem minor, but it is important for resisting certain combat maneuvers and supernatural powers. V20 Potence is a poor man's substitute for earlier form of Potence, as always having one extra die per level is much less impressive than always having one extra success per level. V20 Celerity does not even compare, I would gladly give up the passive bonus to Dexterity for only spending one blood pool per turn to gain (Celerity) extra actions rather than one blood pool per extra action (especially since you lose your passive Dexterity bonus when you take extra actions).

                                            Even if Fortitude is added to Stamina for physical feats, is it added to Stamina for supernatural feats or resistances? I do not see any evidence of that in V20. Anyone, Fortitude really did not change for the better, it is Potence and Celerity that changed for the worse.

At the very least, they could have made it competitive with the innate ability to boost Strength with blood. If the final damage is reduced to '0' health levels, the character also ignores any secondary effects of the attack.'. I think that version of Fortitude would have been more competitive than the current version of Fortitude.
