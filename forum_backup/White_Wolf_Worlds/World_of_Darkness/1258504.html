    Escape from Prison the supernatural way...

    10-02-2018, 01:58 AM
    Okay this clip reminds. Enid the low key supernatural way of escaping from Prison. As in you appear super human but not like fireball telekinesis superhuman.

    https://youtu.be/6UVSE1JYhWQ


    So it’s giving me ideas of the type of supernaturals like Werewolves who would go to prison, maybe death row, but not break out because they are fighting bands while in prison. Maybe with a fetish to appear as them in solitary confinement while they hunt, or maybe even a Bane is bound under the prison and they need to keep tabs up close. And they only escape last minute. Like I can see a high rank werewolf agreeing to do time or go on death row, the pack sneaking in to rescue him only to have him point out he needs to stay to guard the place, then a big omen happens and they escape using super durability, just taking a hanging/electrocution/lethal injection/firing squad and busting out last minute. Makes me think maybe some prisons kind of half know there is more out their the. Officially on paper.

    Other ideas are Mages using prison as a somewhat safe place to meditate/gonon seeking for a decade, or Vampires going their for th easy access to to food. Or hell a prison freehold that has changelings tending it from the inside.

    I remember the talk about Lasombra having their own submarine, so what about a secret black ops Prison half run by the technocracy to hide low key supernaturals like ghouls, werewolves and vampires till the actual technocrats step in.


    Just the video inspired me.

          Prison would be bad for most supernaturals. Just like most normals. It would be very difficult to go through the entire process of being put into prison without being forced into sunlight, making it a bad option for vampires even before you get into all of the surveillance. For a Tradition mage, prison would be exceedingly dangerous. Keep in mind that the Technocracy has a huge amount of control over the government. They could have corrupt guards help arrange your assassination even if they didn't have magick to help them. Plus, mages are usually physically human and vulnerable to attacks from humans, even mundane prisoners with no link to supernatural factions could pose a threat to many mages, or at least push them into using magick unnecessarily often. Plus, the Technocracy can probably monitor the security cameras in most places, including prison, which means that it is something that mages want to avoid.

          For a werewolf it would be bad too. The cameras mean that any use of supernatural power could be recorded. A prison would almost certain be associated with a very high Gauntlet and the Umbra near a prison would likely have all sorts of nasty banes. If Pentex learned that a Garou was in prison, they would be easy prey, especially with help of corrupt prison guards which are probably more common in WoD than real life.

          In the case of a mage or werewolf, prison would be more survivable with magic (Gifts or Sphere magick) that could manipulate technology. Even so, it would be like living in dangerous enemy occupied territory.

          Not that I'm saying that a prison game couldn't work, but I think that a supernatural would need some other reason to go to prison than it being a safe place to hide.
