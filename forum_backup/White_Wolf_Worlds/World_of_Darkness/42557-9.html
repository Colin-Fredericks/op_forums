                                        *snerk*

                                        Okay, how about introducing a lot of you to something new? What would the Undersiders, the main teenage supervillain(?) team of Worm be?

                                        For those of you intimidated by THREE NOVELS' WORTH OF CHAPTERS, here's the basic rundown:

                                        Skitter, aka Taylor Hebert: Lonely, slightly paranoid and depressed nerd empress of insects. Capable of controlling and hijacking the perceptions of every arthropod in a set area by thinking about it, has the multitasking abilities this requires, and is later revealed as a genius strategist.

                                        Grue, aka Brain Laiborn: Honorable and friendly teenage mercenary. Darkness generator, can see just fine in his own cloud and debuffs any powers that enter it, along with feelings of touch. Later gains the ability to actually steal the powers of capes who enter the darkzone, while they're in it.

                                        Tattletale, aka Lisa Wilbourn: Snarky, manipulative and actually rather kindhearted (to people she actually knows) tactician and planner. Her power turns her into Blonde Sherlock Holmes, to the point of being able to pretend she's a telepath because she's that good at reading people.

                                        Bitch, aka Rachel Lindt: Before you ask; she calls herself that, which should give you an idea of how misanthropic she tends to be. Has the ability to summon organic power armor for her dogs, turning them into enormous, resilient kaiju. She can't directly control them though, she's just really good at training them.

                                        Regent, aka Alec: Mildly sociopathic and even more snarky body puppeteer. Given time, he can turn an opponent's nervous system into what might as well be a second body for him over the range of several miles, in a pinch he can make someone punch themselves out.

                                        Imp, aka Aisha Laiborn: The above Laiborn's little sister, and the epitome of snark, and mischief. Living perception filter, if she wants to she can make you forget she exists even as she's kicking you in the nuts.
