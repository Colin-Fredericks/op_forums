    How common was teaching House Lore among Fallen?

    12-15-2015, 12:53 AM
    The Fallen are said to have shared Lore back during the War of Wrath. Which Lore was likely the most commonly shared? I can easily see Lore of Celestials being taught up to the third level, and probably everyone knew the first dot of Lore of the Forge so they could help with creating Relics. Lore of Awakening was probably taught in full to every Fallen who wanted to learn, if only by a large minority of Scourges who wanted to make sure that healing magic would always be available to humanity as long as a Fallen was nearby. Lore of Portals at 3 or even 5 and Lore of Paths at 2 or perhaps even 3 or 4 was probably quite commonly learned once the war turned ugly and the Fallen began to rack up casualties, particularly once the Malhim took the field. Lore of Realms probably wasn't shared much, perhaps not at all, due to the circumstances surrounding that Lore. What other House Lore was likely passed around?

      I don't know how involved humanity really were in the war, but if the Elohim was in the habit of keeping close track then I could see Lore of the Firmament being passed around here and there; possibly Lore of Longing too for those who wanted to focus more on babysitting than fighting their kin on the other side of the conflict. Depending on how it played out I could see Lore of Longing being some sort of pre-cursor to Lore of Humanity even, though that's mostly speculations. The final Lore I could see a lot of use for with the Fallen's goals back in the days is the Lore of Flesh as it allows both healing and improvements when used properly.
      As a majority however, I don't think they flinged the secrets of each other's Lores around too much given the focus of warfare but rather relied on the members of whatever House normally possessed when and perhaps occasionally picked something up along the way or shared one or two tricks with a fellow rebel they grew attached to. Once again this is my "only speculation" grain of salt coming into play.

                                                  I doubt that the Loyalists got nearly a fraction of the flexibility of the Fallen. It is every time and again said as the weapon of demons to fight against the odds. So I doubt that a loyalist would learn anything outside her House, or had the need to learn inside it. They probably didn't had even Rituals as the Fallen came to understand them, nor learned the new Lores of the Fallen.

                                                  Just remember that they had numbers (2 loyalists for every fallen just to begin with, Heaven knows what else God was creating to support its legions) and access to an infinite source of Faith (God itself), so energy wasn't an issue not even for the most powerful uses.

                                                  How much Lore the Fallen actually shared is hard to grasp, and actually irrelevant for almost all purposes. Sometimes they shared because they needed, and that is clear, as is that they altered their Lores to some degree (loyalists probably didn't had High Torment versions). It is also clear that they are fairly defined by nature, enough to justify a modern Fallen never relearning an out-of-house Lore she once possessed.

                                                  Some were certainly forced by circumstances to share against their wills. And that can happen again. But for me, at least, it seems that most (not all) Fallen were far more open to share with their peers in the past than they are now.
