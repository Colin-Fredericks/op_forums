    The Chimerical world seen by the Changelings co-exists with the mortal world. It's 100% present to the changelings (mostly made of incidental chimera) but is not an exact superimposition. Chimera exist independent of mortal forms and things but often there is a match up close enough that the differences do not matter. The chimerical world is something far more vivid than the mortal world (just check out the description of a tree in the core book), but that doesn't mean 'fluffy' or just 'colourful and pretty', for example SIN City is a very vivid visual experience. Chimera are made of emotional imprints and that's the world the changelings see with their faerie eyes. An alley may just be an alley to their flesh and blood retinas, strewn with refuse and so on, but to the faerie sight it may be infested with (or utterly composed of, or just be a) nightmarish chimera. It's also important to remember that the dimensions of your Fae Mein possibly (and probably) don't fit those of your human body, a 10 year old boy human could have a fae mein of a 6ft sidhe knight. Interacting with the chimerical world is where the perception of madness comes in from outside viewers, just like Don Quixote tilting at a windmill that he sees as a four armed giant, but ignoring your fae mein or that chimerical world gains you banality.You're equally limited by your mortal flesh in the interactions and also witnesses so no using a chimerical doorway if there is no corresponding mortal passage in a wall unless you Wyrd. Often it's easiest to keep things close with a dash of flavouring. A city should certainly be strewn with Fairy stuff (your wraith connection is pretty good actually Lunar) not perceived by any other supernatural, but only so much as it facilitates the game.

    Enemies? Changeling has plenty, but much like in Vampire there most common one (in the core) is themselves. The play of politics, duels, adventures, vendettas, balls and pageantry are the crux of the core book changeling game. Seelie vs Unseelie, Nobles vs Commoners, Houses versus Houses, Oathcircles vs Motleys, Court vs Court even personal conflicts, it's all there. Then you also have the chimera which gives you every creature of legend you could ever want. That alley I mentioned? What if it's effecting mortals? Or attracting dangerous changelings to the oathcircle? What if its nightmares are sentient? Just as the chimera are born of emotion so they can too feed and thrive from it and a wily or wary (or brave) had best be willing (especially as a PC) to deal with the ones that threaten themselves, dreamers or the Dreaming. You don;t always (or even often) gain Banality from killing Chimera, even finally. The Story is an important factor in what you can or cannot get away with in Changeling (much like in Discworld there is a force of narrative) because Changelings are literally living stories.

    So, we have Changelings with Internal (political/idealogical) Conflict and having to deal with the dangerous things that creep out of the minds of mortals (and themselves for that matter, see the intro story CTD 2nd). Be nice to see that still in the CTD20, but there are also a lot more. You mention the Fomori, well the WTA creatures are not the things that the faerie are talking about (it's something they touch on in the core, and in fact they're named for the creatues that the Fianna used to fight in the days of myth and legend) the real threat, the actual Fomori are something far more dangerous and we first see them really presented in Denizens of the Dreaming. CTD actually have a series of enemies that are their own.

    Chimera, literally can be anything you want or need for a story and you have the totality of myth, legend, urban legend, net myth and any unique ideas in your head to choose from. After all who can protect your from the monsters only you can see?
    The Fomorians. On a par with the Maeljin Incarna and so on, truly terrifying and needing heroes and antiherpoes of great talent to turn aside.
    The Denizens as detailed in Denizens of the Dreaming and would be nice to see them presented in CTD20 as it gives you a faerie foe on equal footing with the Changelings that are not themselves and are not chimera either. Can present interest situations of friend and foe, respect as per the old celtic tales and so forth.
    The Dauntain and Autumn People. Terrifying reflections of nihilism and people who suck the life and magic out of the world purely by existing, some of which (many in fact) may not even be "bad people". Need a mention in the CTD20 book, but nothing as in depth as Autumn People went to.

    And... of course... The ShadowCourt. This gives us the Thallain and the Sabbat/BSD/Nephandi version of the Changeling game line. The original book was nice and something of an addition to CTD20 would be cool (like the Rage across the world, book of the wyrm, vamp companion etc). Remember that the Unseelie Court, while they enjoy Dark Glamour and Nightmare type dreams and use Ravaging more often than Musing are not the same as the ShadowCourt. Both Seelie and Unseelie can be kind or cruel, good or bad depending on the fae in question, but the ShadowCourt are deliberately wicked and evil. Not in the way of the Redcaps brutal savagery but in ways far far more sinister.

            I could see CtD20 incorporating the Shadow Court and the Dauntain into the core book, at least enough for them to be used as antagonists. The Denizens are problematic in that they’re tied rather firmly to what I call the Latter Days Metaplot: they, and the fomorians that they serve, were introduced into Changeling as a result of the Week of Nightmares. I’d be fine with including them in an abbreviated Antagonists format (I’m picturing something akin to how the non-Solar Exalted were handled in the first two editions’ core books for Exalted) along with notes on how they were originally introduced into the game and how they might be incorporated into a Changeling game that lacks the Latter Days Metaplot. All this with one caveat:
            CtD20 needs at least two supplements: a book of Allies (non-Kithain changelings and Kinain), and a book of Enemies (Dauntain, Thallain, Denizens of the Dreaming, etc. in all their dark glory): in effect, books that are to Changeling as Changing Breeds and Book of the Wyrm are to WtA20. CtD20 itself should have more than just the original eight Kiths in it; but huge swaths of character types for Changeling (particularly ones that required special rules to use) oughtn’t be incorporated into the core book beyond an abbreviated “how to approximate them as Storyteller characters” section; there are just too many of them.

            My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

                    Changeling had probably one big problem. If you take a look to every WoD game each one of them has a big conflict, an human conflict. Vampire is about being a monster, fight that or embrace it. Wraith is about death and life, about find redemption from yourself accept the darkest parts of your soul or conquer them. You have hundreds of conflicts in the Kindred world or in the Shadowlands, but above every of them you have a conflict with yourself.
                    Most of people forgot that in Changeling, probably even the writers forgot it.
                    The problem with Changeling is not just everybody only took the bright parts and forgot the Shadow Court or the Fomorians. The problem is all of us forgot that conflict.

                    Changeling is about hope in darkness. You are a flickering hope, one of the last lights in a world sunk in shadows that are longer and longer each day. And you're forgetting you're one. Every year you're getting closer to be a grump, closer to forget you're not just another human, closer to forget all the wonders than surround you. The light ones an the dark ones.
                    But that doesn't make you good. Hope can be treacherous, just like dreams. You can hope for forgiveness when you know you'll never get it. You can hope to someone to overcome an illness that will kill him piece by piece everyday. You can hope hundreds of terrible things that will hurt you, because they'll never be, and you know they'll never be. You can even hope to never forget, but you'll do, because you're a god with alzhemeir's disease. And you know it.

                    Now look the world of Changeling. Look again knowing that.
                    You're a dream. And every dream can turn into a nightmare just with a blink of an eye. Today you can be Seelie, tomorrow Unseelie. The day after tomorrow you could have forgotten who you are. You have so many fights to fight and so little time. Because every day you're a little older and that means you're forgetting something more, another piece of yourself.
                    And that's the darkness problem. The darkness in Changeling should come not just from without, but from within too. Faeries had always swift their humour so suddenly, the've been so inhuman, so alien. If they're dreamstuff..., well all of us know how unstable a dream is, much more if you think that the human part of a changeling is in a perpetual state of knowing it's running out of time.
                    That could make you angry, that could make you wanna rebelt against everything and everyone, that could make you embrace all your darkest parts, enjoy all the pleasure while you still have time. Or could make you the kindest person in the world, trying to make this world a better place, because you'll forget but there'll be other that don't and they deserve a better place.

                    Changeling could be terrible. Sadly, we all forget that, and we only took the funny adventure part. And that's good, we enjoyed it a lot, but without this creepy dishearting part, it's not Changeling, it's another completely different game.

                    About Changeling 20th, I really hope we'll get it. I'm sure we'll do it.
                    The corebook should cover the kithain with a chapter explaining all the other changeling families. And then we should get a Book of the Gallain (in Changing Breeds style) so all the families get all the pages they need. The hsien, the adhene, the nunnehi, the menehune, the inanimae... Perhaps even some new families from those books we never got (I always missed Keys to the Kingdom so much).
                    I liked the magic system, but it should get some revision, no doubt about that. All that Book of Glamour should have been should be in Changeling 20th.
