                                It's a minor annoyance with not pay off for me. I'm sure, within a week, someone will host it somewhere else without the need for an account. That might be piracy, but it would be piracy of a free document that's meant to be an advertisement for an upcoming for-profit item. That says a lot of things about Paradox, and about Internet IP piracy, none of which is really "good."

                                I'm a customer. Their job is to convince me to buy their products. That's already a very high bar I'm not sure they could reach if they wanted to for me personally, but there's plenty of people between "I buy everything WoD 5 sight unseen" and "I don't buy anything WoD 5 period." Adding annoyances just annoys potential customers, and encourages and more insular fandom of those "in" that get all the free stuff right away, and those "out" that have to wait for, again a free promo pdf, to get to them in some form.

                                This is advertising 101 level shit. You make sure promo material is accessible as possible first, so people make accounts for more stuff (like buying directly from your online store instead of other places so you get a bigger cut of the sales) once you hook them in. You don't gate promos behind annoyances, or people will skip it for something else/wait for when they don't need to do that.
