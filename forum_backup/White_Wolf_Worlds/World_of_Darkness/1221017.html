    Would Like a Source for a Koldunic Related Matter

    05-21-2018, 09:28 PM
    Hello all. i've got a question for the scholars out there. I was looking at the White Wolf Wikia,and in the Koldunic Sorcery pagethere is the following quote
    The older form of Koldunic Sorcery practiced specific krainas instead of Paths. While Paths are standardized, each kraina is unique to the domain the koldun claims as his own and it often takes decades for the aspiring koldun before he has a bond with his domain close enough to properly use his primary kraina. During Kupala's Eve, some Tzimisce abandoned this form as weak and inefficient and instead turned to Kupala, who twisted the krainas into the elemental paths used in the Modern Nights. Only a handful of Tzimisce Elders still practices the old form, most of them in allegiance with the Tal'Mahe'Ra. Some have begun to instruct members of the Order of Moloch into the old art.
    Can anyone tell me the source of this information?

              The links for the books describe what's in them, and when released

              DAV: Players Guide to High Clans, 2003
              VTM: Clanbook: Tzimisce Revised, 2001
              VTM: Blood Magic: Secrets of Thaumaturgy, 2000
              V20: Rites of the Blood, 2014
              V20: Vampire: The Dark Ages 20th Anniversary Edition, 2015
              V20: The Black Hand: A Guide to the Tal'Mahe'Ra, 2016
              V20: Tome of Secrets, 2016

              So we can see that VTM Blood Magic: Secrets of Thaumaturgy was the first in 2000.
