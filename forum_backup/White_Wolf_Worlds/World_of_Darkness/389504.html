    Supernatural Relations

    02-02-2015, 02:52 AM
    Okay so I was wondering if there was any pregathered information about the different races of the World of Darkness (Specifically oWoD) and how they interact with each other. There is obvious things like the Garou and Kindred hating each other. But I was wondering how all the supernatural beings regard each other.
    Like, do Demons work with the fae on occasion, do they hate the kindred due to Cain. Do Wraiths and Mummies hang out on occasion? I am just hoping there is something out there so that I don't need to spend time reading through the Core Book and companions of every single oWOD system books.

      I'm not as up to date on the Amenti-Mummies, but the Shemsu-Heru from 1st and 2nd Ed Mummy were pretty much up in the other supernaturals' business.

      Mummies and Kindred tend to have cool relations to one another. Mummies seek to establish and uphold Ma'at - cosmic balance and justice - while the Kindred tend to be in it for themselves. Furthermore, the inherent abilities that the Kindred condition tend to mess with Ma'at. The Blood Bond creates a perverse addiction that feels very much like love to the victim of the bond. The Kiss too is a very addicting experience, and can leave mental scars on a blood doll's psyche. And yet, there is hope of salvation for the Kindred through Golconda.

      A mummy could become a mentor to a Kindred seeking Golconda, aiding the vampire in how to avoid the traps of the Beast. The vampire in turn is ageless, just like the mummy. While all of the mummy's mortal companions will eventually pass on, the Kindred will always be around, lessening the feeling of being alienated from the constantly changing world.

      The Kindred also make good catspaws in the war against the servants of Apophis and its servants, the Followers of Set. Most Kindred fear and despise the Setites. The mummies, who know the Setites all too well, could secretly feed information about the Setites to the Kindred, and subtly encourage the Kindred to oust the Serpents from their domains, and break their webs of corruption.

      Obviously, I've not yet touched upon the other supernaturals, but I'll come to them eventually.

              Mage interactions with other supernaturals can range from 'kill on sight' up through 'pacify and study' all the way to 'cherish and protect'. Just going to toss some off-the-top ideas out here, but keep in mind that with mages specifically it will depend highly on the individuals involved.

              ​Here are some general stereotypes:



              Akashics hate Vampires (and most Demons) and sometimes actively hunt them. They deeply respect most shape-changers (as they strictly abide by their intended purposes), but especially the Stargazers - whom they consider ancient allies and will pay some respect out of the box.

              Euthanatos deal with Wraiths often. They are somewhat fascinated by mummies, and do not hate them at all! (Mummies return to the wheel and reincarnate in a special way, sort of like rock stars for the Euthies.) They despise Vampires as an affront to the great Wheel, and will almost always try to kill them.

              Dreamspeakers deal with Spirits often, and will fight Demons with much fervor. They have some minor interactions with Garou, and while it doesn't always go well there is some potential. They are great friends with many of the Bastet, and respect all of the Fera.

              Hollow Ones are hard to generalize about, but in my world they often see vampires as sort of a romantic or wistful idea. Yes, this causes trouble (in my world).

              Celestial Chorus are extremely 'vigilant', although what is dangerous they may disagree on. There are a lot of Chorister concepts involved with 'Vampire Hunting' or 'Demon Hunting'. They are out there doing God's work.

              Cultists will fight against anything or anyone who tries to impose their desires onto another. This will vary, but they aren't typically the 'hold a grudge' type.

              Sons of Ether / Virtual Adepts generally don't have any major stereotypes for other supernaturals. Maybe they just don't have the history for it.

              The Order of Hermes... ahh, well... remember when I mentioned 'pacify and study'? Their hubris is generally so institutionalized that there is nothing they wouldn't see as an opportunity for greater knowledge or lore. They could literally go any way with any other supernatural group.

                  Garou and Fera, a general perspective.

                  Vampires are an affront to life, but seeking them out is usually a lot of trouble, so they typically only make them go from undead to all dead if they don't have anything pressing on hand, which is not a common occurrence.

                  Mages are caern-robbers and affronts to the order intended by Gaia, who know just enough to be really dangerous to what the Garou and Fera typically safeguard. Because Changers typically, again, have more pressing business, they generally don't mess with them unless a caern, wallow, etc. is in danger, in which case they will tear the Mage's arms off and stuff them down the willworker's mouth, if said willworker is lucky.

                  Wraiths other than Risen don't tend to interact with most Garou very often. Those Risen that do were usually killed by Garou, and you know what that entails.

                  Changelings are capricious bastards that might aid, hinder or worse. Not to be trusted, but generally not to be killed on sight unless they're particularly bad fae.

                  Imbued get treated like normal, mortal hunters (which is to say, usually ending up with a terrible case of being mauled), only with more general paranoia about how they manage to accomplish various feats and why, leading to many existential conversations between dealing with their actual business.

                  Demons would likely be seen as just another type of bane, assuming that W:tA ever acknowledged the existence of Elohim in the first place. Which it doesn't; to Werewolf, Demon might as well just not exist.

                  Mummies are all over the place. They facilitated a mega-crossover in Ancient Egypt, but that probably wouldn't fly with the lore as it is now. Would likely also be seen as an affront to life, albeit more in a confused manner than a "kill it yesterday" vampire kind of manner.

                  Some Changers will hate certain groups more or less. Silent Striders and Mokole are well known for being extra willing to summary execute every vampire they encounter, for example, with the latter far more exuberant about it due to their ties to the sun. Corax also harbor a distaste for vampires, but they're more likely to listen in and then use the 78 degrees and sunny grenade later. Most non-Fianna and non-Ceilican tend to be so confused about changelings as to not care. Some tribes and types of Fera can speak to the dead more readily than others.

                  Finally, Mage's word on crossover tends to, in the words of Team Four Star, make a lot of assumptions, with the first being that most monsters don't want to kill mages horribly. This is generally not the case. Even Akashics and Stargazers don't actually have a peaceful relationship of allies; at best, it's extremely tense and saying the wrong thing could make it blow up at any moment..
