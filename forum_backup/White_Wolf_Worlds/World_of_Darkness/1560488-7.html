        Originally posted by monteparnas View Post

        I understand this frustration, and it is valid.

        That said, let's unpack some things:

        1. Let's judge a work on its proposal. There are things we don't like because they're bad at what they try, and there are things we don't like because we personally don't enjoy what they try, but they're meant for people who do.

        This distinction matters.

        2. You're mistaking ambiguity as obfuscation with ambiguity as goal. The unreliable narrator in VtM isn't there to make finding the truth hard and frustrating for people like us, they're there to say the ST decides how much they're correct.

        The author isn't waiting that you understand the answer, they're giving you an open question to answer as you see fit, exactly like CofD does, if not the same style of delivery.
        Yeah, I know it's my personal hang up. I just wanted to voice my frustration on the matter.

        For what it's worth, though, I did originally enjoy the ongoing metaplot, until I started digging deeper and found out how muddled it gets.


        Originally posted by monteparnas View Post

        From this, the problem is words. Each splat or explorer meets different things and use the name they want for it. Just don't assume they're talking about the same place just because they used the same name.

        Example:

        - Werewolves know a Realm in the Middle Umbra that's a big chasm that spills depression. They call it the Abyss.

        - the Discipline of Obtenebration uses solid shadows the Lasombra claim to come from another realm that's partially confirmed by Abyss Mystics. As their name suggest, they call the place the Abyss.

        Wraith and Demon also have their own places they each call the Abyss.

        The thing is: they're not talking about the same thing. Each just has experience with a place they thought make sense to call Abyss. Those places may be linked, even aspects of the same realm, but that's not the reason they get the same name.

        It's just 4 different otherworldly experiences, by different groups that don't know what the others use the word Abyss for.
