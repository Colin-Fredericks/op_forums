                                            Ravana is not Zapathasura, (who himself may or may not be Ravnos), but the forth childer of Zapathasura the broke away and became an infernalist (or from a KotE perspective possibly a Yama King). It's a convoluted tale that's probably more BS than true, as Ennonia (also not the 3rd Gen Gangrel founder, but some sort of 4th Gen child of the Antediluvian that created both the Gangrel and the Ravnos as one line, thus making the two Clans actually two Bloodlines, with the Gangrel failing to fulfill their duty in destroying the KofE/Yama Kings/all supernaturals).

                                            Both of the Clan Book accounts on the bit of history well, self contradictory and convoluted. Most likely what truly happened is closer to Ravnos wanted India to be his exclusively, and was having a lot of trouble, both with other Cainites edging their way in and also the "indigenous" KotE. Ravnos created this huge philosophy amongst his Clan which was essentially justification to do whatever the hell they wanted, but aimed to focus his brood towards killing all enemies, again with auto justification. Gangrel shows up and Ravnos thinks, hey cool. They are just like me, they will probably help. One thing escalated to another, Ravnos convinces/tricks Gangrel into destroy the 2nd Gen (or helping), thinking that that final act will "free" Gangrel, (ie bringing her over to his POV and solidify their alliance), but Gangrel feels like she had been used and says screw this. 4 out of 5 of Ravnos's other "generals", (one of them is even believed to be Ennoia/Gangrel as a child of Ravnos!!!), likewise turn against Ravnos, one basically not wanting to fight, another (Ravana) having been tempted to the "demon's side, etc . . . and basically everything goes to hell between the Ravnos and Gangrel "clans". It's unlikely that Ennoia/Gangrel led an army of anything, as both accounts point out how Gangrel didn't stay with her brood after Embracing them, nor offer instructions for them to Embrace and raise others, nor did she generally stay in one place long enough.

                                            The Ravnos of India have a historical doctrine of "redeeming" Gangrel, which is to say to turn them to the path of slaying Elders to drink their blood, but not Clan Ravnos, so it's more likely that this skirmish was along the lines of Clan Ravnos hounding Clan Gangrel throughout their travels, and trying to force them into a role of fodder, until this point where the two Clans finally say "that's the last straw".

                                            The History on the Gangrel side is primarily from the perspective of an oral history, with multiple cultural contexts added in, and also generally more directed at continuing certain points and events of the story that the specifics of the story itself.

                                            The History of the Ravnos side is layered into a sort of cult of personality doctrine that contradicts itself a lot, and does not fit with anyone else's tales. It also has been edited to switch around a lot of names, attributing events to the wrong people, as well as switching the context and time frame of various things.

                                            Now, all that being said, we don't really know what "the truth" is, and the above is mostly what I think may have happened. For example, the Book of Nod suggests that Gangrel was possibly tricked into Frenzying to kill the Second Generation, (and by that I mean given false justification to take part), and thus cursed to be like a beast of the fields, where the Ravnos account says that the gods cursed her for failing her duty, and turning away from further diablerie.
