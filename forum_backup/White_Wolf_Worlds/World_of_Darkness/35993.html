    Merchandise

    12-23-2013, 09:37 PM
    I don't know if this is the right place for this thread, but I had some questions and I hope the staff and any collectors out there can help me.

    Does anyone know where I can find a list, or have created one themselves of all the White-Wolf classic Merchandise that was released?

    I've collected quite a bit myself, but the types of merchandise I have seem rather inconsistent and I'd like to make sure whether I'm on a wild goose chase with certain items that never existed, but it's hard to find any information. I know about a lot of the more popular things like the Tribe necklaces, Clan pins and the famous Werewolf Klaive and the Assamite kukri dagger. But what about the more obscure stuff?

    For example I own a Chessex trademarked Werewolf the Apocalypse coffee mug. I have seen a photo posted on the old forum of their Mage the Ascension mug. Are there other mugs that were created? Vampire? Wraith?

    I'm not asking to buy anything from anyone, I'm just trying to find a list of what all the merchandise is.

    As a side note I managed to find 2 sets of candle holders. One of them is for Vampire and another for Werewolf. The werewolf one is covered in carvings of all the tribe glyphs in pewter. However I noticed something seemed off about the glyphs. I checked a book and discovered that all of the glyphs were Backwards! XD

                                              The original Werewolf dice were produced in Europe. While the numbering and colors were not unique, the combination of colors is something that is not currently in production, which meant that a custom order has to be made to purchase them. Minimum quantities are 2,000 at a time and shipping to the US was in the hundreds of dollars for that 2,000 quantity. That was just for the standard 1-0 numbered dice. Unfortunately, the moon-phase dice were trademarked icons, so I couldn't get those made (plus, it would have been a separate run with custom molds of 2,000 or so of those too) so I didn't have those made when I reproduced the W:tA dice. However, I think that the W:tA dice were the only ones with a custom mold anyways. But others that were custom-colored would still have to be made in larger quantities, packaged, and stored.
