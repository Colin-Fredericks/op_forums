                    My two cents about the "political" argument:

                    Let's face it, WoD was always political, with a strong leaning to the left. I don't expect it not to be and frankly, I like it for that. One of my main gripes with NWoD back in the day was, that it detached itself too much from the real world. I like very much that WoD is heavily anchored in real-world cultures, cultural phenomenons (including political thoughts and social struggles), mythologies, history and contemporary things, like subcultures. It makes it more immersive and "real" to me. I don't think V5 will be less political, in fact, I think it will be quite (leftist) political. Martin, I think, for example is quite liberal (If I remember correctly, he even talked about how WoD inspired him to learn about leftist political movements and ideologies, when he was younger) and I'd even risk he identifies himself as progressive. Just read some of his facebook posts about GoT, for example.

                    What V5 won't do, in my opinion, is tiptoeing around hard topics and catering to certain sensibilities of certain contemporary political groups. Or, to put it bluntly, I expect V5 to be political, even leftist, but I don't expect it to be PC. Some would call that edgy and going for shock value and being heavy-handed and they are being upset about it. Me, I'll welcome it with open arms. Or, to be more precise, welcome back.

                    However, I do think WoD could and should be able to be played without always going for real-world political commentary and metaphors. One could totally focus on the supernatural side and internal politics without that. You just don't focus on how some of the splats and factions were being written as real-world metaphors and don't bring real-world political struggles into the game. It'll be there, of course, but you don't have to focus on it.

                    Also, about the notion, that every art and in general, everything in life is political... It depends. If you define "political" as everything that got influenced by a certain person's self-identification and worldview, then, yes, sure, everything is political, since it was made/said/done by humans. However, that is not the only, or even the main defintion of the thing. Also, sure, if you're strongly interested in real-life politics, maybe even connected to political and civil groups and you're viewing most of the world through those lenses and participating in pushing the agenda of that group or political side consists a big chung of your life, then, sure, for you, everything will be political and everything will be a part of the real-world political discussion. Simply put: if you feel strongly for a Cause, then it's likely that for you, most things in life will be connected to the Cause or even be about the Cause. The thing is, although, not eveyone sees the world that way.

                    If nothing worked, then let's think!
