                            Well, yes and no. The settings can be updated to account for real-world developments that have occurred since the game's last edition (e.g., Mummy's picture of the Middle East is badly out of date) or even merely for the passage of time: the first edition of Hunter relied heavily on the notion that the Imbued are a brand new phenomenon that nobody has had a chance to come to terms with; how do things change when there's a whole generation of young adults who were born after the Imbuement phenomenon began?

                            Also, I wouldn't be opposed to the various “latter-day games” (the ones that lean heavily on the Ghost Storm for their very existence) each getting an Appendix that's essentially a “pre-Ghost Storm Shard”: the Imbued recast as an alternate take on True Faith for “Year of the Hunter”-style hunters (essentially, a modern-day counterpart to “Dark Ages: Inquisitor”); mummies in the style of “Mummy Second Edition” implemented using the rules of “Mummy: the Resurrection”; and taking a page from “Devil’s Due” to present something not unlike the Fallen before the gates of the Abyss were cracked open. No more than an Appendix, though, and clearly presented as a variant.
