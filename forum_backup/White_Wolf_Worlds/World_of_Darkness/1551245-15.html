    Originally posted by Amalasuintha View Post
    If alpha playtests being this bad is the norm in this industry then wow. How does a multi-million dollar company not have a QA department to catch all this?
    Again, because that's not the point.

    Even setting aside how small they actually are, in the TTRPG market Playtests are what says on the tin. This is not a product, and isn't nearly close to release.

    The idea is half to get feedback in some mechanics while they can be changed early, half to give the fans a sneaky peak that helps keeping interest going.

    So no, the idea isn't to have anyone curating or cleaning, at least not thoroughly. What we see is legit an early prototype to test ideas.

    Hasbro did the same with D&D, by the way. 5th Ed started with a public Playtest years before release, then known as D&D Next, and if was such a success that they kept the idea as the Unearthed Arcana download area in the WotC site and used a series of Playtests again for 5.5. Same thing: the tests are minimalistic, full of badly written rules, etc.

    But the final product is polished, possibly more than would otherwise be. For example, right now you'll have an idea of what you think to be a bad idea, and what you think to be a bad execution. They'll not waste time refining the former, or discard the later prematurely.

    Not that Paradox gives me a lot of hope by itself: IIRC they tried a couple playtests during V5 and managed to screw it. But that's where I think the new art director is a good sign, as she has precisely experience with a successful development using plenty of Playtests.

        So, some things that can be clarified by the playtest packets, Demiplane, and the GenCon panel:
        • Tremere have a choice of Thaumaturgy or Necromancy. This is presumably building on the lore that the Tremere and Giovanni made a deal to take some apprentices in, IIRC, BJD?
        • Tzimisce have a choice of Vicissitude or Tellurgy. Based on linguistics, "Tellurgy" basically means "Earth magic". Its description mentions magic drawn from the earth, curses, and defensive powers. So it's Koldunism plus parts of various Thaumaturgy paths. It's weird that Ravnos get it, except for the stereotypical association of Roma people with "curses".
        • Outstar confirmed in the Discord that the Giovanni basically took over the Hecata/became the dominant faction again, hence the name change. Whether the other bloodlines remain a part of a sect-like Hecata or not, or went their own separate ways, is unclear. But all of this may change anyway.
        • The Beckoning is over.
        • The SI collapsed under its own weight, but individual groups still hunt Cainites.
        • V5 fans are losing their minds. The official WoD Discord is a cesspit, even as far as edition wars, with several "jokes" made about attacking developers, and people wilfully misreading or misrepresenting the text in some cases. (Made worse by a bunch of trolling from apparently regular users who kept saying untrue things while the GenCon panel was ongoing to wind each other up.)
        Like, yes, it's rough right now, but honestly, I hugely prefer it to V5 already. I can't describe the relief I felt knowing Hunger Dice were gone, which was a bit unexpected, as I've tried multiple ways of porting them into V20. I think they've come to symbolise the rift in the community between V20 players and V5 players. My eyes roll every time someone claims blood points are "mana points" or V20 is "supers with fangs", because it's obvious these are intended as thinly veiled insults. I also just really appreciate that, so far, this new edition doesn't want to actively exclude people who don't play a very narrow way.

        Also, things like Hunger Levels/Conditions, Beasts as customisable, Clan Traits, and so on, are things some of us have discussed and tried out ourselves here on this very board (which makes me wonder if they've been reading our posts). They haven't got all the element ironed out yet, and they absolutely need to separate health and vitae again, but this is a much stronger rules chassis to start from. Trying to homebrew V5 is a nightmare because moving one part can bring the whole house of cards down. This system is actually much easier to hack and pull apart.

        In the full game, I'd want and expect more skills (9 attributes and 13 skills is obviously unbalanced; if you reduced it to 6 attributes that might work okay), Protean should be separated from Animalism, Dementation and Chimerstry should probably be folded into Corruption (or they can make a new Nightmare Discipline), difficulty should be capped at +/-5 dice (not 10!), formerly core powers shouldn't be used to make Clan Traits (anyone should be able to access Feral Whispers, for example), no clan should get more than three clans Disciplines (and alternates should be accessed via Merits or Bloodlines), and Drama points should be dropped (Quickening can stay, but call them Story Points, because this is the Storyteller System!). But these are all easy things for me to house rule if they aren't fixed.

        And if you want some good news: M6 is slated to come after V6, followed by Dark Ages, and then C6. Dates are still TBC, but this is great news.
