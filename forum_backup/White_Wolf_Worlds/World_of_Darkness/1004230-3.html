                While I respect your opinion, I disagree. Real world events having a supernatural explanation has been a trope of the World of Darkness from back in 1st and 2nd edition, only really being frowned on in Revised and V20 (when it's addressed at all in the latter). Having more recent events follow that same trope is no more or less distasteful than not having them follow that trope, or ignoring that trope entirely. Different tables will have different tastes, and trying to imply that one of those tastes is inherently bad or should be entirely avoided accomplishes nothing, save to highlight your taste and present it as somehow innately superior to others.

                                                        Originally posted by The Laughing Stranger View Post

'Beethoven was a space alien spy' is something WW/Onyx Path has been moving away from for years, because it is tired and not particularly inspiring as far as stories go. There's a reason vampires didn't do 9/11.
                                                        So let's start off with the obvious: there is a massive difference than a horrible terrorist attack that led to wars, paranoia, increased surveillance of civilians, extrajudicial renditions, and far worse... And an election. While Trump's winning the EC vote could certainly lead to worse, it might also lead to nothing much of note. An actual attack against the country is far and away different, and not really worth comparing.

                                                        You say alternative history, IE the genre where a "Beethoven was an alien" would be categorized into, is tired and uninspired. The same could be said about, say, vampires in the modern age, which have done in every imaginable way at this point and nearly all "new takes" on the subject instantly bring to mind some other example where it was done the same way. However, there are far fewer examples of alternate history in fiction than vampires in the modern day, a far more specific example of a trope.

                                                        Revised did try and move away from the WoD's alternate history bits, true. I not only don't dispute that, I'm also the one who brought it up. But does their moving away from that trope over a six year period (the length of Revised) invalidate the seven years prior? (I exempt V20 from this because V20 has kept a metaplot-agnostic stance and really never addresses the subject either way in any noteworthy manner.)

                                                        So again, I get that you don't like it, and that's fine. But I disagree with that opinion, and apparently so do others.
