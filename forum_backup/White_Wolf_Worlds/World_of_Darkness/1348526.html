          Mind is easily the winner if we're assuming living in a modern country (others have a better contention with less technology and medicine).

          The ability to boost your mental and social aptitudes, multitask, and mind shields are all low level and insanely useful powers. As it goes up, you basically get half the core VtM Disciplines. It's extremely easy to keep coincidental. You can travel to the Umbra/etc. Even without Life to heal your body, as long as you keep your body on life support you can run around, possess healthy bodies, and do stuff.

          Without Prime, the basic Pattern Spheres are harder to use. Correspondence and Time without other Spheres lose lots of oomph. Even Spirit which is extremely flexible, gets hard without Prime to turn Quintessence into Gnosis/Essence to offer to spirits to get them to help you out.

          Entropy is probably the second runner up here, as it's insanely useful at all levels.

              Agreed, Mind and Entropy are solid choices.

              I would chose Entropy:

              You can always use luck, there's almost no situation in life where luck isn't something one would want to have. When you can literally see where "fortune" it's going to be, and being able to shift that course, it's easy to never make a wrong choice in life. As the level increases you start becoming able to affect the fate of your stuff, yourself and others. ​​

              Winning in the Casino to get easy money it's only the most crude way to manifest this, luck can help making the rigth choices at your work, at improving your relationships...everything. Everyone has a story about how a good day changed their lives, and you can literally sense and modify the concept of "a good day" (among other stuff)

              People that you want to succeed do, and those you don't want to succeed don't. You can't easily force people to comply, but manipulating them becomes easy with even low leves of Entropy and modicum social skill. Say the right thing at the right moment ( tolower social rolls), find the right people for your goals, get sure other courses of actions get immediately punished. People soon will want to be on your side

              You can make friends with people destined to be powerful or useful to you (or you can stir them towards that end very easily). Meanwhile, enemies find hard to touch you with their weapons, magick or scheemes (as a certain degree of randomness often factor at these attempts).

              W/o other Spheres the high levels become all the more relevant. At 5 you can start controling people's minds almost as if you had Mind, in a way so subtle as to make them think it was their idea all along.
              Perhaps more interesting: At 4 you become able to weave the entropy that affects Life, so you can avoid illness and all physiological problems (and psychological at 5). You ain't going to be immortal w/o Life, but with exercise and a reasonably healthy life you can be sure of aging like a champ, live a long life, and get a pretty good death.

                  I'd choose Life in a heartbeat.
                  It doesn't have the long-term advantages of Entropy in your life, nor does it have the social advantages that Mind can give you, but Life effects themselves have their own side of a good....well life and happyness. Not just for you, but also others.

                  Stay physically fit to the End (if there is one)? Done. Prevent diseases, aging, heal all kinds of injuries? Done. Play with your looks at will? Done. 20/20 vision? That will be a step down from what you got. Bad hearing? Don't even pay it much thought. Enjoy the best sleeps one can get, as your body chemistry just works like clockwork. Migraines? Nah. Also say goodbye to the dentist. Cancer? Won't be a worry. And your heart will never fail. Diabetes? You're going to eat all the cakes, because you can. Also, don't worry about those calories.

                  And the best part? All that stuff (and more) doesn't only go for you. It can go for your close friends as well. Or your family pets. Or offspring. Lovers. Parents.

                  And being fit and healthy and nice to look at will help in social- and job situations to a high degree as well - the human animal just rolls like that. Not as much as Mind and Entropy of course, but don't underestimate it. CEOs are taller than the average citizen for a reason.

                                  I feel somewhat betrayed that Tytalus isn't taking Mind

                                  I think people are giving Life a bit too much credit though.

                                  First, long term major alterations of Life Patterns don't require Prime, but they do require Quintessence, which is hard to manage (esp. in other people) without Prime. Life is still a pattern Sphere and limited without Prime.

                                  I also think the context of when/where matters here. Most of us are living in places with medical advances and general technology that a lot of what Life can do is already possible. If we're going to talk about improving the health and well-being of others? Mind's abilities to explore mental health could advance areas that we don't have a strong understanding of. A Mind mage can cure PTSD, and also examine the mind in ways science can't to help understand how PTSD forms and help design better therapies with that knowledge.

                                  A Mind mage can also go to government bodies, and super-power backed lobby them to increase funding to public health. More funding for organizations like the NIH, CDC and WHO. More funding for international medical aid. Get them to put politics aside to fix health care, medication pricing, end the War on Drugs, and so on. A Mind mage with a group of researches can boost their mental Attributes and increase their mental efficiency, allowing them to find cures faster. All of that's going to have a vastly larger impact on the health and happiness of as many people as possible compared to having Life magic to heal people.
