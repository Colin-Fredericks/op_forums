    Madoka Magica in the WoD

    09-27-2015, 11:11 AM
    So after watching the series and the movie I wondered what would happen if that was going on in the World of Darkness (And yes, I'm aware of Princess: the Hopeful, that's not what I'm looking for). What would happen in the Incubators and the magical girl system were active in the WoD? How would the various supernatural groups react to them? What would happen to mortals caught in web that the Incubators are spinning? Personally here's some of my thoughts:

    The Technocracy would take a dim view of a group of creatures creating reality deviants and wrecking the city particularly the Progenitors and Void Engineers. The former due to the fact that Incubators act almost like the vector of a plague (and the name alone is a spit in the Progenitors' faces) and the latter because it's aliens messing with humanity (and some of the crazier ones might think they have something to do with Threat Null). More compassionate Progenitors might try to free the girls from their contracts and convert them to the Technocratic paradigm. If they could accomplish this then the Incubators and the witches would have a powerfully motivated foe dedicated to getting in their way.

    On the Tradition side I see the Euthanatoi, Choristers, and Verbena having the biggest problem with the whole thing. The magical girl system inherently messes with the cycle the Euthanatoi espouse so most likely the Incubators (and the girls if they can't be helped) would be given the Good Death. The Choristers would see the Incubators as demons and respond accordingly while the Verbena might see the system and its end result as a perversion of what they hold sacred.

    I can see some demons getting into contract wars with the Incubators. Let's face it the demons can do many of things the Incubators can and they tend to have better rates than Kyubey and his ilk (Sure, Sayaka I can heal your boyfriend. The price? All you have to do is believe in me.). Then there's threatening (deliberate or not) whatever a Scourge or more guardianship minded demon is protecting.

    For werewolves it would mostly depend on whether the girls, witches, etc reek of Wyrm taint and whether the Incubators are targeting the wolf blooded. There's also the possibility that the Incubators' whole ridiculous plan is a trick of the Wyrm and meant to spread chaos and corruption rather than actually dealing with entropy.

    Dauntains might revel in the banality that witches represent and try to speed up the process for any magical girls they come across leading other changelings to try and counteract this.

    More crazy hunters would have little problem killing the girls but the saner ones would face a moral dilemma that would be interesting to explore.

    The girls themselves would likely try to destroy whatever supernatural threat appeared to them either mistaking them for witches or realizing the truth and simply deciding to protect themselves.

    Anyways let me know your thoughts! How so you think the other groups would react? What kind of plots would you run involving the girls?

                  Indeed, an interesting interpretation of the Triat, to be sure. Agents of Stasis, using Dynamism to combat Entropy. All the while sowing discord.

                  I'm reminded of The Spirit Ways (yes, I know it isn't a very good book), with some Shamans Awakened and instructed in magic by spirits. I can see the Incubators as powerful Umbrood that saw the sorry state of reality, and made it their mission to avert the heat death. But while they're capable of playing themselves off as good and even set up their system to "help" their "clients", they're ultimately looking out for their interests. They don't really care for magical girls, save as a resource.

                  They're also very much like Demons, except what they want are bountiful emotional energy, in the form of both Hope and Despair. They don't quite understand what these are, just that they are powerful sources of (Quintessential) energy. So they make contracts with little girls - they are the ones most vulnerable to the pitch, after all - providing them with magic and a few...let's call them Investments, perhaps. Or perhaps not.

                  After thinking about it, the magical girls themselves would probably need to be Mages, rather than just Sorcerers with a few select Paths and some Incubator Investments (or Blessings, mixed though they are). For one, Magical Girls in Madoka Magica are capable of seeing Witches and their minions, so they need to have Awakened sight. It would be slightly silly for one to be able to see the unseen and yet not have the wool pulled from their eyes. They can also shift into Witch dens, which at the very least are some sort of pocket realm or Marauder bubble of altered reality. If the Witches are just magical girls gone Maraud, one would need to be Awakened so as not to be pulled into their version of reality and think of it as perfectly normal.

                  And of course, Magical Girls have a built-in mechanism for handling Paradox: their darkening gems. Of course these Soul Gems would naturally be Phylacteries, containing the magical girl's Avatar. But these would be specially enchanted by the Incubators so that the Paradox these girls (or boys, if we're altering things) accrue, instead of being released in backlash, instead collects in their gem. And the only way they can remove the Paradox is to kill Witches - Magical Girls whose gems became too stained with Paradox and were shunted all at once into stage 6 Quiet. They must claim the gems-turned-grief-seeds and shunt Paradox onto them.

                  Well, that or employ some sort of Prime Effect to disperse the Paradox or force a backlash. Or just park their Soul Gems at a Node, and let the Paradox bleed off. But of course, the Incubators don't tell the Magical Girls that this is possible. To do that would be to potentially break the cycle of Hope and Despair, and allow the girls to go free. And the Incubators can't have that. It's why the Incubators work closely with their charges, guiding their development so that their Paradigms never slip beyond what the Incubators tell the Magical Girls is possible. Never allow them to know the Truth: that the Magical Girls are really Mages, people capable of altering reality as they please. That Reality itself causes their gems to darken as a result of showy, impossible displays. That they can massively extend their lives - perhaps indefinitely - simply by keeping their magic subtle. And that all of this was deliberately engineered by the Incubators to push the girls towards witch-dom.

                  But if a magical girl did learn the truth...if they met other Mages who could teach them what they really are...oh, what a reckoning would their be.

                  Magestuck: The Ascension

                                      My view of Mage is that anything is possible with the Sphere system, so long as enough successes are rolled. That being said, is it entirely necessary to remove a magical girl's Avatar from the Soul gem? Their current state would provide ample justification for the Phylactery Flaw, because in Madoka Magica, that is exactly what the Soul Gem is. The important point would be figuring out how to alter the Soul Gem so that it would no longer soak up the magical girl's Paradox, until it reached critical mass and turned them into a Marauder. In that case, we're probably looking at a high level Prime/Matter/Spirit Effect.

                                      Then again, that assumes the Incubators designed the Soul Gems to do that. It could very well be that there's little or nothing special about these Phylacteries. Rather, the accumulation of Paradox in the gems is a result of the magical girls' belief. The Incubators told them that every time they use their magic, their gems get a little darker. As the Technocracy has shown, Paradox backlashes tend to conform in some manner to a Mage's beliefs. Technocrats see their devices break down and blame "impurities of a non-sterile environment". Old school Artisans would blame the break-down of their machines in the hands of the unAwakened as the result of poor maintenance. A Chorister's backlash might take the form of spontaneous stigmata, etc.

                                      Given that the Incubators would try to prevent their charges from receiving any outside ideas, it could very well be that the effect is entirely a result of a magical girl's ignorance of what Paradox is and what it means.

                                      It's worth noting that Incubators in Madoka Magica don't really understand why magic works. They are playing with forces that confound them, but which they grasp just enough to exploit. While an Incubator might be able to pull a magical girl's Avatar from their body and plug it into a prepared vessel, that doesn't mean they'd be able to tinker with it enough to add extra features. They might not know how to make Paradox collect in the gem, just know that it can be made to so long as the subject is made to believe it is so. I can just imagine them experimenting, probably over years, until they stumble on a method for guiding magical girls towards Quiet. This is why they keep the magical girls ignorant, so that they fight witches, preferably never realizing that they too will become witches if they stay the course the Kyubeys set for them.

                                      It changes the magical girl's existence from an inevitable and tragic cycle into a problem that can be overcome, so long as the girls have the Will to impress their own reality on things. Something that Mage is all about.
