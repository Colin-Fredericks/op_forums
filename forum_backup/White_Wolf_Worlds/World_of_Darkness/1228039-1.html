                    I would also point out that lots of complex mechanisms are harder to sabotage than simple mechanisms. Sometimes complexity is added to add functionality or versatility, but sometimes complexity is added to grant extra protection. Complicated machines tend to have lots of smaller devices designed to protect them, such as fans and coolant and waterproofing.

                    Now if you make a reality-bending permadox ridden android, its complexity is the kind that facilitates protection and durability. It *has* to be, or all you've made is a really shitty robot.

                    Lastly, I don't think PC Mages should be Terminators. If you want to be a Terminator construct, justify it by taking the flaw which makes you a sleeper construct which can't awaken. If an android PC is present it should be one with limitless potential, starting off relatively weak and developing with time. If it's the kind of android which is a mega-tank-death-machine then that's an NPC, or a very specialist PC, if you see what I mean.

                    For example, an ST might let a player control an experimental Mark X Prototype, an iterator construct which has been designed to eradicate reality deviants more efficiently. The design paradigm excludes biological components entirely, focusing on mechanical might. They could start with an EXP "debt", high Attributes, plenty of Abilities (Energy Weapons, Jetpacks, Hypertech, Medicine, Firearms), Enhancements such as Primium and no magick.

                    These "terminators" are obviously the ST's prerogative, I don't think they should be thought of as normal.
