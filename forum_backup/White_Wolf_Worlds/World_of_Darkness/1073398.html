    Wanna do a fanproject. Mostly fun about revising Hunters for new classic WoD

    04-04-2017, 06:30 PM
    See title
    Having come in on HtR I DO admit some fondness. But I also know the frustration. For one its not JUST they were undermined and thrown into a mad prophet scenario. Its that they were locked in there. While having invisible, to them, signs saying "kick me supernatural"

    I get that Vigil basically hacking in the sights mechanical benefits and the general intended effects but tied to skills. And even the practical experience system AND the tiers are great things

    In fact I'd want to use HtR's predeccessor AND uh after.

    But I understand its a contreversial thing with both its fans, who have ALWAYS been on the defense. and appreciators of Vigil who, even
    I have to admit, have a better crafted and more elegantly compatible product. So much I've heard of *back* adaptions.

    Look I just wanna talk about my favorite owod game, maybe ways to refine it in light of the old setting's revival. And just run some ideas I have.

    If interested pm me so we can set up a space to hash out or share ideas.

    Have insights to the game. What attracted you to HtR and brings you back? Have your own homebrew or rules config?
    Love to hear from you

                      In the version of OWoD our group kicked up we did away with the Messengers and had the Imbued be Lucifer's analog of Thralls; free of will and instilled with the enormous and still intact divine power of the First Angel and Rebel, who as he wanders the world in the leadup to the end of days chooses to weaponize mortals against the depredations of the stirring Earthbound and other supernatural banes/predators. Given the narrative provided for Lucifer's post-Fall activities in Demon the Fallen, Imbued actually fit his M.O. and ideals pretty much perfectly; I imagine had Demon predated HtR Imbued likely would've been attributed to Lucifer's handiwork rather than a pair of remnant angels/whatever. Also the glorious conceptual irony of the Hunters being literal agents of the Devil (albeit benevolent and repentant ).

                                                      Some ideas for an HtR20 Nothing coherent just random stuff from the last few weeks

                                                      The Apocalypse happened but didn't "destroy the world" but more the status quo from a world of stable darkness to a world of flares and shadows. The age of chaos. The Ministers bosses came and while not "illegal" their actions were out of line. Their superiors intervened and punished them, not eliminated. While they suspended the imbuing project, eventually Upper Management released their version.

                                                      The Imbued have changed as a result. As per the End Times at least initially their plugs got pulled. After clearing out one area, getting some critical intel and one cure.

                                                      The Age of Chaos makes things easier and harder. Where as before you were a rat scrambling in the shadows of immobile pillars ossified and set. Now the world is full of shadows and flashes flowing against and past, like being in a flame.

                                                      As a result after the wreckage cleared and several riots something happened.

                                                      The ...thing the way people COULDN'T deal or see or remember, at all. IT was.. well fading.


                                                      Witness1 was arrested by the feds for being a key to several (very genuine) criminals with cyber networks. However his captors know this is some random but emergent phenomenon that, worse, seems to have both vanished... and resumed.

                                                      After some time after the Edges seem gone but the Sight and Conviction have returned but now with no prepackaged powers. Moreover it feels like a spark is lit through humanity and the world. But heat must be balanced or it will damage or burst a thing into flames.

                                                      New rules: Basically my big changes involve bagging 1st Edition Exalted AND Hunter the Vigil

                                                      Convicted Actions. Basically you can invest or spend convictions on Abilities and Attributes.Either
                                                      *adds dice to roll. Maybe Never more than three at a time of exceeding a pool of ten
                                                      *lowers difficulty (that is the number that must appear on die to count as success)

                                                      Trait+attribute max is ten if any combination due to situation stuff (stunts, specialties, equipment) including specialties- which can now stack- past that point it DOES lower the difficulty. A pool of 11 is represented by five rolled on a ten-sded die counting as a success, with 9 and 10 success plus reroll, A pool of 12 means 4 is the standard difficulty with 10-8 becoming success plus reroll, and so on. If combination of conviction, specialties or dice would drop the Difficulty to 1 in this case count the number on one die as the number of successes, if below that if noncombat or non-direct context just assume perfect success but is want to know degree just add the number of die who's number facing will count as a success as the difficulty lowers below 1.


                                                      Edges would be detailed but assumed gone, but supported or regainable. Replaced with specific tricks somewhere between Vigil's Tactics or Exalted's charms paid off in successes on relevant rolls with supplementation by conviction invested or spent or spent willpower

                                                      Imbued can buy off derangement with Experience. STs may ask it be relevant to the character's growth or struggles or at least take some time off to recuperate. For those who say "that's unrealistic" assume the derangement/afflictions were the minor and acute and short term version that with counseling, rest, and drugs were eliminated or managed when not diving into the crazy of using conviction or exposure to the supernatural.

                                                      Add a Modified version of Exalted's Stunting system. Basically when could stunt will earn a benefit to make success more likely (dice lower number of successes needed or negate a difficulty) in addition may take an immediate reward in 1 die stunts for 1 conviction, 2 die stunts 2 conviction or one willpower, and three die stunts a combo of the preceding. If delay gratification may cash in your would be gains in experience ponts. If delay until end of chapter or major part of the story (even if unsuccessful) than points banked are halved but spent like bonus points as from character creation.

                                                      Specialties can be as broad as specifics of equipment, conditions, setting or actions listed in the action section OR detail of the trait. They can only add three dice any additional applicable ones count to lowering difficulty.

                                                      Convicted actions tend to be miraculous in their limitless result. But that's part of your heart and soul you're spending and investing and you don't have a lot to spare at all times.

                                                      Convicted Stamina allows for increasing the effective score for determining derived values. And may act to lower the wound penalty, which in turn lowers healing times

                                                      Must maintain investment to benefit though and your pool is STILL capped at ten and you only have what you have.

                                                      you can buy conviction and virtues and edges and their replacements with exp in align with the player guide rules. Virtues are no longer required for edges, or more the "only way" to buy them. Edges or virtues bought with Exp don't gain derangements. Its the conviction system that seems to destroy the imbued's spirits. Don't worry you'll be facing enough madness, contradiction, chaos, and danger, and betrayal you will still be on the line of crazy.

                                                      Awareness is expanded and highlights for allowing Second sight and greater perception and so on. As is Alertness for noticing something physically in the area.

                                                      One idea for the edge replacements is that you can cash in successes on a roll to use the effects with conviction or willpower or health levels additionally able to buy off costs to perform certain actions.
                                                      Example: to through multiple items at once so sure and fast at a single target defensive actions cost the number of items thrown in successes to negate an attack success. Note this does nothing to increase damage directly, merely guarantees likeliness to hit. Cost could be 3 and that can come from success or conviction or willpower or health level

                                                      General rule of thumb for above on scaling costs:
                                                      1 conviction = 1 suxx
                                                      1 Health Level = 10 suxx
                                                      1 Willpower = 3 suxx

                                                      Creeds: the 'default will be creedless with all hunters; either veteran imbued or newbies or demibeings who hunt who are seen the truth gaining conviction. but you can adopt or devote yourself to a Creed. Not sure how to make them beneficial. Favored abilities? Different costs for different feats or Creed only feats?

                                                      Feats can be bought or be freely used if meet a general prerequisite. If you have a high enough ability you can usually attempt the feat. Not sure about rules for qualifying

                                                      Like with Vigil some tricks/feats will be more supernatural than others.

                                                      New skills/abilities like Alchemy, which are so hunters can practice the haphazard science or crafts of messing with magical ingredients to extraordinary outcomes. Such as making an elixir from Vitae that can keep in a fridge for a week, as to loose its properties in minutes, be administered an make someone a ghoul with minimal addiction and no specific blood bonding. the price is just a vampire's blood and some chemicals and prepwork. And this is well in the grey or black of the law. And they still become high/enraptured off of V.

                                                      Thought of some others but forgot. Basically there will be skills with dealing with the supernatural world and if not those specialties.

                                                      Special traits can be bought. This can raise or lower the level and focus of your game. One in particular I know I want is "Exalted Healing" which does what it says. gives you the standard nigh perfect and reduced heal times of the exalted.

                                                      Medical rules/treatments.
                                                      Set up difficulties and tasks for specific events. Basically make it so successes on a medical roll don't cure wounds but reduce their penalty allowing a patient to function better, and be stronger for complications or healing. this speeds up healing, SOME. Do recall even with this for basic mortals heal times are a bitch.

                                                      The code still works for veteran imbued and newcomers can be taught it.

                                                      Imbuing seems to be more catchy, as to before. Performing greater than 10 feats, encounters with a supernatural, seeding your conviction by communion; laying of hands; or some ritual of mutual belief (not necessarily consent), convincing arguments, and more can seem to ignite the long dormant flame in mankind.


                                                      Tuning into angel radio. Likely part of the Awareness extension. Allows you to 'hear' sense the supernatural back chatter with a special affinity for the "messenger channel/frequency" The visions are no more clear, reliable, or painless without investment or special traits and practice (abilities).


                                                      People can take up and abandon creeds but STs will be encouraged to bother players. usually their supernatural investments or even just Awareness or similar is so high they can't just "ignore it" and moreover someone will be seeking you out for what you've done.

                                                      Can more freely use and learn to use numina/ritual spells and so on. Though not sure why this doesn't make you a witch or sorceror. .

                                                      Some Hunters are just Ghouls, Sorcerors, Kinfolk or even part-time fomori...more on that later

                                                      While still orientated to the dead and the apocalyptic aftermath and ongoing struggle of taming the worst of the world as it changes and reformed, the Imbued are changing the world and being changed.

                                                      For Instance:
                                                      Jared Shoemaker's murder was discovered to be just that. And while his claims haven't been verified 100% there is no doubt he believed what he said and was attacked and killed by a very suspicious police activity. this is the result of several hunters "pulling the thread" at a coverup and the retaliation or their efforts bringing the odious misbehavior to light. This leads to a struggle

                                                      The shadow masters are still there but the system in place has run wild. Their control mechanisms are acting with interest, needs, and sometimes volition of their own- making them playable by the imbued.

                                                      his is signified by Witness1 being in custody but still running hunter.net as sort of flytrap-thing. His captors can see what he can see, but users are more wary than ever of sharing info and hiding their locations. Moreover his immediate handlers aren't supers or directly enslaved. And what they are seeing is...well its worrying YES but also not totally delusional too. evidence is piling up

                                                      Eventually extreme investment can allow someone to "exalt" though right now mankind is regaining its lost something from the beginning. In this case, going by Exalted being the future/past of the WoD, Heroism. The fire is Heroism coming to spread among the populace and with that the ability of Faith to worship and engage not just with the base and material but the spiritual and ecetera. This doesn't mean everyone becomes mages or all spectrum reality warpers. Mages are...well if Hunters are real fit men, mages are someone who experimented on themselves to gain free range of possible abilities but have to direct everything.

                                                      Reality is malleable by certain methods and tools. It is not just the plaything of human perception though.

                                                      Eventually spirits and mutants (Fomori) and the Dreaming will take greater prominence. Especially as hunters are the base emergence or inspiration of what fae long for.

                                                      Uhm..sorry like I said just a few half formed ideas

                                                      Grade them or add or substract and then replace with your own. Or just ask questions about clarification of meaning or intent.
