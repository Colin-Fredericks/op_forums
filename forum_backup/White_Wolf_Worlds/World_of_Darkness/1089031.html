    Tuatha/Fomorians and other Pantheons

    05-07-2017, 03:33 AM
    So while I do like the Tuatha and Fomorians as the default Changeling names for the Old Fae, I also really like slight references to other myths, like Aesin being themed after Aesir, perhaps with slight Vanir hints. Its kind of wishy washy, because they have things like Changeling Padshah's that are part of a Welsh house, so okay so the Noble houses are Universal. But wait... the Nunnehi don't acknowledge them... oh wait Neither do the Hsien...

    So its kind of weird having one of the Thallain be Polynesian but keep on referencing being of the Fomorians.

    I like the idea that the Tuatha Fomorian divide just became the prominent Zeitgeist of the Dreaming after the Sundering. While some Sidhe are Sidhe but are actually reflecting Hellenic Nymphs, and Indian Apsaras (Well Apsaras would probably also work as a separate kith but you get the idea)...

    So Tuatha is just shorthand for old Dream God, and Fomorian is shorthand for Primordial Nightmare God. So I would indeed like a Tuatha reflecting being a Vanir, and a Fomorian reflecting a Jotun. I remember in Shadownessence someone was doing a cool project on Fomorians and they had their Colored Courts but also Titans, and Jotun.

    So what are your thoughts on this?

    And was the War of Trees before mankind? Were the Fianna a Tribe back then?

    And What is the difference between a Denizen and being a Tuatha/Fomorian? Is it because Denizens are unaligned, or is it because they are a power league below them? Would Dark Age Fae become Denizens/Changelings while Gods become Tuatha/Fomorians? So True Fae being different then Tuatha, by True Fae being predecessors to Changelings, and the Tuatha/Fomorians being predecessors to the True Fae? Or are True Fae and Tuatha interchangeable?

    I'm a big fan of Exalted so I like the Idea of Fomorians being Raksha, while Tuatha are just Raksha that choose Creation over Rakshastan, and Hsien being Descendants of Raksha that somehow agreed to serve Heaven.

                      Tuatha and Fomorians make sense with most indo-european fae, but not with some other cultures, so I prefer to look at it on a case by case basis. It's often awkard to treat the celtic paradigm as universal when there are Trolls and Satyrs running around, so shifting the perspective to have the Fomorians be Jotnar and Titans by another name is a nice touch, but when it come to fae from cultures that don't have a race of antaonistic evil gods, it's just weird.
                      I guess the Eshu could see the Fomorians as Rakshasas, Asuras or Daevas, since they have Indian and Persian as well as African roots?
                      I don't know enough about Polynesian and Native American folklore to know what the Menehune and Nunnehi could equate the Fomorians to, though, but I've not read or heard of anything close to the concept, so it's perhaps just as well that it's not forced on them.
                      Hsien are another thing entirely, and I really wonder what they even do in a Changeling book. I really hoped they would be brought more in touch with the Dreaming for this edition.
