    Mage Ambient Music Dump

    08-18-2016, 01:47 PM
    Anyone else like to throw in a few tracks during their game? Be it for dramatic effect, setting the scene, or just to keep the tone going while checking rules.
    If so, then why not share it?

    Recently I thought of using a lot of Killer 7 tracks for Technocractic encounters:

    Technocratic base music: https://youtu.be/mt9cu3Yao8I
    Syndicate Boss encounter: https://youtu.be/z2694gX3Ego
    N.W.O Operatives: https://youtu.be/rBAwUYEk4NQ
    Being chased by a Cyborg: https://youtu.be/xVFbyvpkF88
    Void Engineer Ship: https://youtu.be/TxXawT1t12Q

    This is a track I imagine working well for a mage who Steps Sidways for the first time: https://youtu.be/hydO1JjMlno

    This for when you encounter a Verbena coven: https://youtu.be/jiwuQ6UHMQg?t=31m47s
    If said coven has a celebration: https://youtu.be/jiwuQ6UHMQg?t=24m40s

    Somewhere safe: https://youtu.be/WNl916LJSY8

    Trying to talk to a Marauder: https://youtu.be/jFcGytR-VtM

    That should be enough to start things off.
    Got any other tracks for other purposes? Post them!
