            No, that is not how the Mists and the Dreaming works in cWoD. Mage (Revised) was written after Changeling (2e), so if the writers had intended for Mages to be able to defeat the Mists, then they would have put it in the Mage books (just like they did with the rules concerning Delirium and the Fog). There is nothing, as it is written in Changeling or Mage, that Mages can do to defeat the Mists or to defy the Dreaming, so any attempts to avoid the rules as written is just headcanon.

            Now, Mages can perceive Chimera, the Kinain, the Kithain and the Near Dreaming with Spirit because the Dreaming is a spiritual universe, not a mental universe, but their ability to remember what they perceive is based on their Banality. Technomancers are incapable, because of their Banality, to remember anything about the Dreaming or the denizens of the Dreaming, beyond fragments of memory. Their technology fails and their magic fails because the Dreaming is greater than any Mage. Of course, STs can change any rules that they want in their games, but the canon rules are the canon rules, and no amount of argument is going to change what the writer wrote. In addition, I think that it is wonderful that there is something that is immune to the hubris of Mages.
