    Extra Actions in differnt Splats

    05-24-2023, 08:45 AM
    What power do you need in the different Splats to get more actions in combat?

    What is know is:
    • Vampires use the Discipline Celerity
    • Werewolf can just spend Rage
    • Mages need the Time Sphere
    • Kuei-jin use the Black Wind Shintai
    • "Gypsies" use the Dance of Knives Blood Affinity
    ​
    What powers are used by Changelings, Wraith, Mummies, Demons, Hunters and what ever other gamelines exist in the WoD?

    And are there any Gamelines where there is not option to get additional Actions?

      Garou have some modifications to how Rage is spent for this, such as the Rorg-taught Gift Savagery of the Taloned Hunter, which doubles the effectiveness of each point of Rage spent. Gurahl are also typically incapable of getting extra actions, but at high Rank, have access to Rage of the Mother Bear, which allows them to spend Gnosis to accomplish this (and typically results in some very, very dead enemies, since they can be rocking Strength 20+ with each hit because their Rage-based natural Strength boosting power lingers for several turns).

      Mages can probably also use Forces and/or Life to become faster.

      Hungry Dead have some other Disciplines to get extra actions, with the most immediate that comes to mind being Yang Prana. There's also a Tempest of Inward Focus Discipline, but it's kind of just like the former but altered a bit.

      I don't know anyone that would actually use WoD: Ethnic Slut in actual play, and I don't think that I'm alone.

      WoD: Combat allows for some multi-hit maneuvers, even for humans without occult powers. It's usually not that much and kind of expensive, but if you wanted you could even use its system to adapt the hyakuretsukyaku from Street Fighter (where it is known by the ancient Anglophone arcade name of "Lightning Leg"). WoD: C, oddly enough, already has Dee Jay's Hyper Fist in it (and some of the Focus maneuvers were made into Do maneuvers for Mages).

          Changeling's use the Art of Wayfare to get multiple actions. It's potentially one of the strongest because it's easier than Time magic, and can be used on your whole group if you're willing to throw enough into it. Wayfare 2, Fae 1-3 (depending on the make up of your group), Scene 1, and Time 1, would give the PCs and close Allies 1 extra action/per success on the activation roll for three turns. If you have a really good activation roll, three turns is probably plenty, and since only one PC needs it, unlike Rage or Celerity the rest of the group can save resources for other effects. With higher level Time Realm effects, you can be on par with a Time Mage's ability to cast ahead of time as well.

          Wraiths and the Imbued are both left out of the "gain extra actions" powers. There's a few places where they get something, but they're usually very specific; not the general combat actions of other splats.

          Mummies (at least in Resurrection), have it kinda weird in that they don't really have direct extra action powers, because they don't have a lot of "instant" powers in general. They make amulets and potions, or cast long rituals, for their powers. These tend to be extremely potent to make up for it. The default way they do it is that if they boost Attributes over 5, they gain special bonuses besides higher stats; extra actions for Dex being one.

                  As I noted the Imbued (or Numina users if you want to go there) don't tend to have flat out extra action powers. The Imbued have like... two IIRC "multitask" powers that let you do things like track someone and not have to worry about losing the trail just because you get in a fight. The Imbued aren't really meant to be competitive in hand-on combat. The Shih are the only Numina users that I think get something close to Celerity or Rage, but IIRC it's way costlier for them to use.

                  The Fallen are closer to the Wan-Kuei. They have very few straight up multiple action stuff like Celerity, and instead it such abilities are linked to their Apocalyptic Forms and High Torment power boosts.

                      Shih use the Qiao of the She* to achieve blanket extra actions at five dots (and you get what you've paid and been waiting for, because Thousand Swords gives you extra actions for the scene based on a diff 8 Strength + Occult roll, two traits which Shih are required to have at high ratings, though the blowback is like what Might of Thor used to be), but there are some combat maneuvers that provide extra attacks as well. Most notably, Hundred Star Shower, which, on account of the way its worded, suggests that it provides over a dozen light attacks for just two points of Yin (which inflicts a die of bashing damage on the user), provided you happen to have all of the projectiles on hand. Basically, it's Richter Belmont's dagger item crash from Dracula X, but requires Firearms for some reason, and is one of your optional Mo Chi kung fu maneuver picks at chargen.

                      DHX also suggests using WoD: Combat for more maneuvers, even though the two don't have super compatible systems. Which means you could also be rocking Dee Jay's Hyper Fist!

                                          I completely forgot about Tempus Fugit in Wraith... mostly because by WoD standards, it's really not a great power. It's a 5th dot power, has a high cost (4 Pathos and 1 or 4 Corpus depending on how powerful you want to make it), and you can only exempt [Wits] Characters which includes yourself. Wit's a Wits roll, so it favors characters with high Wits, but the more combatants the less effective it gets. It lasts 1/turn per success,and you can only activate it once per scene.

                                          It's a neat power, but it's fairly underwhelming. It's also really unclear what happens if you use it on characters with access to supernatural multiple actions.

                                                          Demons in Demon: The Fallen can spend Faith for Extra Actions provided they purchased the Visage that allows it on character creation. They also have Extra Arms Visage for extra attacks/parries.

                                                          As for Changelings they have Wayfare which can grant them extra actions if I remember correctly.

                                                          For Vampires however don't forget Temporis 5 (Clotho's Gift), which is far superior to normal Celerity and scales amazingly the lower your generation is (especially below 7th).
                                                          Also there's Dual Thought, a power in Path of the Focused Mind (Thaumaturgy) that allows you to take two actions in the same turn instead of one. Unlike Celerity however, it CAN stack with Temporis since it is mental and not physical... which can create an incredibly gamebreaking character
