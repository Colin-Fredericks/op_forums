                                              I was in highschool and I started hanging with this girl who was into RPG's. I thought D&D was dumb, but I was lucky enough that my first DM was an insane guy who loved Planescape. So I met some folks there and one of them brought me a copy of V:tM to show me how awesome it was compared to D&D. As a highschooler I was blown away by the moodiness, art and the awesomeness that were the Disciplines. I would have grown out of it pretty fast were it not for the fact that there was a bit about the Werewolves in the back with an awesome piece of art by Timbrook I think. The girl liberated her brothers clawed 2nd ed of Werewolf and we pored through it. That's when I got into Werewolf, realised how awesome it was and I've never really gotten out of White Wolf since.

                                              What doesn't kill you, makes you... stranger.
