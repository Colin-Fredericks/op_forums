        Originally posted by 11twiggins View Post

        Oh certainly, but the Spheres are so wide that a few impressive and great abilities are going to get drowned out. Stopping time is cool, freezing objects in time is great, but at Spirit 4 you're sailing the great unknown in the Umbra, and at Matter 4 you are generating feather-light armour with amazing soak, and at Mind 4 the emotions and beliefs of most people can be shaped and redirected. And Forces 4 pushes you into "directing a forest fire" levels of awesome, while Prime is an incredible toolkit at all levels and Life has you pushing your body to impossible feats.

        So it's not that Time is BAD. Time is awesome. But it's competing with some really really incredible Spheres.
        It all depends on the ST.

        Prime, for example, is terrible if the ST isn't willing to put a bunch of quintessence in their game like Nodes or Tass. Similarly for Spirit, if the ST isn't all that familiar on WoD spirits, or even spirit lore itself, this sphere can be a huge drag to use, and forget about gallivanting off into the umbra if your ST isn't equipped to deal with that.

        If any of my players chose the Time sphere, then just like is someone chose Spirit or Prime, I would be adding a whole host of content in there for them. I'd introduce the world of Time mages, mages that sit outside the boundries of time and manipulate things completely unseen and only even detectable by Time 1. I'd have creatures living in the corners between time, such as the hounds of tindaols or other such bizarre beings. I'd introduce ripples in the time stream, fluctuations where areas are slightly out of sync with the "natural flow", areas around nodes or even strange locations where time just halts for a brief second. These are areas where the arrow of time is curved, where the river of time forms eddies, or where the cracks in time form, depending on your mage's paradigm regarding how time works.
        Again, only detectable via Time 1. Time 2 might allow one to look upon these phenomena and investigate what they are, perhaps they are future or past echos, points of repetition which are doomed for repeat, or even an act of Time 4 magic down centuries past just waiting to go off when the right conditions align.

        And don't let me hear anyone dissing Time 2. It is by far the single most broken rank 2 sphere rank in the game. I abused the hell out of it when I was playing a time mage, it allowed me to know exactly what had happened in every mystery setting we had encountered, see what could possibly happen if we chose various paths (avoiding stupid decisions), follow people long after the trail had gone cold, learn if someone was still in the place we saw them enter, and even discover the possible answers of centuries old questions.

        My ST really should not have given me this much power. They ended up dreading every time I started rolling for it as it would, as they put it, ruin all the suspense that they were trying to build up. I just knew everything so there were no real surprises and we were almost always prepared for everything. But then, their mistakes will help me balance this thing if it ever comes up.

Extrapolating outwards, I bet you anything there is a group of N.W.O agents who specialise in men in black style time fixes, ensuring that knowledge of time travel and such never reaches the public outside of their control. Such departments in the year 2550 may even use a large number of Time related gadgets to prevent the world from falling away from the "alpha" time line. However, they must be very very discreet on how they do this in order to remain completely undiscovered.

                As for Time 3, it isn't just a "gain extra turns" or "Undo mistakes" kind of power. You can also speed and slow down objects and areas. You can slow down you opponent to a crawl, you can make a car speed up without affecting the physics of it, you can slow down the death of someone while you get them out of harms way, slow down a crumbling wall or the water falling from it, slow down bullets making it possible to doge, speed up your sword thrusts making it harder to parry.
                With other spheres you could slow down light around your opponents face making it hard to see what's going on, reverse the breaking of your bones, reverse someone's mind to before they were in a coma, slow down someone's thoughts so they are unable to react in time, speed up the rate of quintessence flow, slow down the decay of a collapsing building.

                Like any sphere, it requires imagination.

However the best applications of Time will always be incredibly Vulgar, and a tad antisocial. Terrible things happen to wizards who mess with time Harry.

                    - I've never actually STed Mage outside of some brief text stuff, so I can't say this with authority, but most of these feel Vulgar, and also sound like they could be achieved Coincidentally with other Spheres, saving EXP and avoiding Paradox. If someone slips into "bullet time" while everyone else is moving normally, or looks like they're running through treacle, that's really Vulgar in all Reality Zones honestly. Even in the most mystical magical parts of the world where people believe in spirits and magic, someone who looks like they're sprinting moving at a slow pace is not Coincidental. A Forces/Matter effect where your incredibly suped up car suddenly blasts out Nitrous and flies down the street? Coincidental. An ordinary looking car moving at 160 when it logically shouldn't get past 80? Could be Vulgar. If you're using Time to slow down a Sleeper's death, then that's going to be Vulgar, as they perceive the rest of the universe speeding up. Unless they're really far gone, in which case they might interpret it as blood loss or brain damage. Slowing down bullets, matrix style? Vulgar. Dodging those bullets "entirely by chance", or your thick leather jacket stopping the bullets from properly harming you, should usually be coincidental. You get the idea.

                    Tl;dr Time is situationally a powerful plot device (dealing with other Mages who are creating Plot using Time), and has a few powerful effects which are worthwhile (extra actions for example), and a bunch of potent effects which are highly Vulgar (slowing others down to a halt), and a few really tempting and awesome effects which are so Vulgar and difficult they're almost invariably pointless (time travel, rewinding time unless it's life or death).

                    When compared to a Sphere like Mind, which is a situationally powerful plot device (needing it to unwind the Pattern of a "memory worm" which eats people's pasts) AND has many great coincidental applications (small illusions, reading surface thoughts, projecting simple ideas, processing huge amounts of data, boosting your Mental attributes), AND has a few potent and Vulgar uses (making a sleeper your thrall, reading deep into a person's mind, generating powerful AI), I don't really think there's much competition.

                    That isn't to say that I hate Time, I just feel like it's the least powerful or useful out of 9 sets of really impressive powers.

                        twig

                        First thing to point out is that coincidental magic is all about circumstance. What was coincidental in one situation will be very vulgar in another. A car moving at 160, totally could happen, coincidental. But in the presence of a mottorhead sleeper, and it's their car you're time speeding. Vulgar with a witness. Slowing down a sleeper's death? Should be fine, they don't know what's going on, perhaps when you're close to death this is just how it feels to be leaving your body behind. "I felt like time just.... flew passed me... but at the same time... everything was very still...." Done on the operating table though, while doctors are watching... that could be suspicious.

                        Again, it's all about imagination and ingenuity.

                        The problem with Mind is that it is very difficult to do anything big with Mind until the higher levels. Mind 1 and 2 are almost entierly sensory, while only at Mind 3 do you get to flex your command muscles. Your memory worm technique would be a Mind 4 rote on its own, but arguably you could also have a "brain freeze" effect with Time 3 and Mind 1. (your ST may vary. That's what I'd allow at least). There's also the extra caveat that Mind magic can be Willpowered away fairly easily at low levels. You either need to make a very big effect, or be a very powerful mage in order to disregard the target's willpower entirely.

                        Also, coincidental does not necessarily mean more useful. In fact I've seen my players get into a hell of a lot of trouble because they tried a whole bunch of things in an attempt to be coincidental, when one good vulgar effect would have solved a lot of problems.

                        I agree that Time magic tends to be more paradox inviting than the other spheres. Every single sphere has some quirk to it. Prime needs quintessence to power many of its effects, Spirit deals with the gauntlet, Matter has more permanence than the other spheres, Forces is really painful and volatile, Mind is more coincidental than other spheres but has less permanence, Life can result in pattern bleed, Corr. magic augments your range, but makes it easier to trace you back. Entropy becomes less likely to work the more you use it, and Time is a paradox magnet.
