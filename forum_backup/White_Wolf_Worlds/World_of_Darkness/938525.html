                                          Originally posted by Magicballz View Post
                                          Interesting. Arhats are considered to be as powerful as Antediluvians right? What good is that power if they can't use it.
                                          The Kuei-jin are souls doomed to an eternity of torment in hell. They managed to get a chance to alter that fate by escaping from hell. However, if they screw up on their path to enlightenment, or if they die, they fall back into hell to suffer under the lash of the Yama Kings for eternity. Becoming an Arhat allows them to overturn that fate.Ultimately that's the goal for every Kuei-jin (or at least those who follow the teachings of Xue).

                                          Power comes with enlightenment, yes, but that's just a bonus. To cultivate power over enlightenment is to lose sight of your goal and ironically those who do focus on power over spiritual enlightenment are unlikely to become enlightened and therefore will have a difficult time becoming stronger.

                                          Also, Arhats aren't as powerful as Antediluvians. Antediluvians are able to continue to function within the mortal world. Arhats must sacrifice their ability to affect the world in exchange for the pinnacle of enlightenment.
