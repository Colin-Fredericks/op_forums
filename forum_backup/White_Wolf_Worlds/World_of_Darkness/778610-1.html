                                I do think that a conflict between a Fallen's corporation and Pentex, given any level of crossover in a chronicle, is very likely to happen at any time.

                                But from that, everything else will be dependent on circumstances and your particular crossover.

                                Lets see some possibilities, though. First, the Earthbound are really the first more probable to deal with Pentex. Sure, some Devils from any faction could begin the Fallen Co., but it is more likely to have a greater number of those being ruled by Earthbound (including through enslaved Devils). Them it depends largely on said Earthbound. Certainly Pentex and other Wyrm allies of it could be interested in using those ancient gods, but the Earthbound are a diverse and crazy bunch. Some can ally with the Wyrm, but a lot will either see it as a rival and enemy for the control of the world, and some will simply be too paranoid to deal with it in any fashion but obliteration (not that they will accomplish such). Not to mention the ones that simply see things through serious delusions and simply think they are in some sort of epic myth. Some of the Mad Gods are quite Mad.

                                From this point, the "human" Fallen can get involved in diverse ways. The Raveners can see Pentex as quite an ally, or simply not giving it much attention (except a large berth if they think it is a front for an Earthbound. The distinction between a demonic cult and Pentex can only be so clear when you find out the daily activities of the inner circle of the corp, and it is unlikely to happen so soon). In the worst scenario for them, Pentex is at least making it all the easier to destroy the world, if not destroying it outright, and even Raveners think on the long run.

                                Most of the other Factions are likely to be against Pentex sooner or later. Even for the more Tormented Faustians, they are still competing for resources. Pentex causes suffering in all too subtle ways, being disinterested on the overt misery that births Faith. They want people to only have faith in their products and in continual consumption, the Faustians can use it, but eventually they will try to at least take the Pentex more overtly to themselves. And probably they won't be very happy if they actually find about the Wyrm. Luciferans want to fight "for humanity", and probably also won't be very fond of a company making their source of Faith so miserable. Reconcilers are quite obvious. Even some Criptics will fight it, be it for personal interest or personal morality, be it to learn about Pentex by "dissecting" it. And even the Raveners can try to infiltrate and take over the corporation.

                                The presence of other splats in Pentex is a question of personal taste. It is unlikely that most of it isn't human, but it does have room for other splats, actually to all of them. I would only make it another iteration of the "Hydra" thing: the supernatural creatures of every kind are powerful heads, but Pentex is the immortal body of the beast. As long as somewhere a lone head remembers the ways of the Wyrm, the company can just survive the entire purge of the high echelons and will continue polluting the world and making it miserable. It isn't about powerful heads. It is about filthy lucre, and the efficiency in making profit by shitting in the world.
