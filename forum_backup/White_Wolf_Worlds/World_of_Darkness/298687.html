      Both were in 1996, when my friend who'd introduced me to RPGs two years earlier with WEG Star Wars, brought Vampire the Dark Ages to summer camp. First half the summer he ran a solo game for me, and we swapped for me first GMing every on the second half. I am getting close to my personal 20th Anniversary with the cWOD.

      I'm not sure it was that interesting... the stories were... kinda silly and ridiculous. I think my first WOD PC gained 20 dots in Disciplines in under a week of real time (we were playing ~2 hrs a day during free time).

      But I did get hooked on the WOD that summer. Within a year I'd moved on to WtA and MtA as my main games, and I've been playing on and off every since.

          First time I played cwod we started as humans visiting the new orleans carnival. During a gig of my characters band we got snatched and became shovelheads. We learned only the basic rules of the sabbat before we had to organize "beverages" for a big blood feast. During the blood feast a war party was started against a setite. We participated got to his haven, battled with another pack on the way. One of us was sent to torpor by a sniper who aimed for his burning cigarette (the player chose the flaw addicted to smoking, thinking it wouldn't be that bad since vampires don't have to worry about their health).

          I got low on blood and drank dry our Lasombra (god I hated this Character so much ^^) and when my Character didn't rage any more he realised what he'd done to his teammate. He decided to clean up his mistake by destroying him, trying to drink ALL of his blood. I ended up diablerizing him and botched my willpower roll so this guy endet up with a new good lucking body with a band and fame-background attached to it.
          We endet that game there and startet our first game where we actually created vampire characters instead of mortals.

          It was a lot of fun.

                  Vampire: The Masquerade in Gary and Chicago as the first edition adventures came out in 1991-92, with some friends in high school. Yeah, I'm old. (One of those friends has moved back recently and is in my V20 game, after Blood And Smoke last year. We try not to terrify the players who were born after that first game with too many stories of our ancient past...)

                  I still like the first/second edition rulebook's intro setting and story, in which the players' characters attend a party hosted by the Prince of Gary and interacted with the other vampires around, with no big action scenes it really set out what made Vampire different from other RPGs we'd played at the time.

                          I'm almost certain the first CWoD character I ever made was for Mage: The Ascension, but the game never got off the ground.

                              I have no idea when it was, other than in the 90's, but my first character was a Gangrel loner type, imaginative of me wasn't it, in a friends Camarilla game, not that there were any alternatives back then. The plot involved Tremere and their experiments, gone awry naturally, and in the end we broke into a Tremere research facility, with our on Tremere ally, although the experiment itself managed to escape it was a fun chronicle non the less and left a soft spot in me for the Gangrel Clan.

                              Not long after that I got vampire myself and ST'd a few stories but I did spread out my interest into other parts of WoD as well, with Wraith stealing my breath away still being my favorite RPG ever. Unfortunately the RPG circles around here were always rather slanted towards Vampire so that is what most games were about due to popular demand. Including a Vampire LARP I participated in here and for a brief time in the late 90's early 2k's even had my own Changeling LARP going on but it unfortunately withered away when I left for military service and the ST I left in charge was a bit over her head I guess as the game died soon after.

                                              According to some Google-fu, it had to be 1996 as I had to secure my own copy of 1st edition Laws of the Night to play the Vampire the Masquerade LARP and it had just been released.

                                              Threw together a Native American Gangrel that didn't even last thru the night before getting himself in so much trouble that he would have been executed if he didn't flee the city. A lot of that was due to not having read the rules before playing. Got my book, read the rules, came back with one of my all-time favorite characters that I've reused multiple times and kept going in that same LARP for a year. Alexi Shostakov, former solider/muscle/enforcer/general purpose person to hurt and kill someone for the KGB. Embraced shortly after the fall of the Berlin Wall into the Gangrel Clan.

                                              Shortly after the first couple of sessions of the Vampire LARP, someone else was starting up a Werewolf the Apocalypse table top. I created another favorite recurring character as a Gurhal (my buddy tried to talk me into a Child of Gaia, but since it was just him & me and the ST, the whole "other Breeds BAD!" didn't come up as much).

                                              Then I found Mage the Ascension and shortly after that Wraith the Oblivion.......and I was beyond hooked!

                                              I've had the opportunity to play or run every major splat of the Classic WOD but Mummy the Resurrection, and that was mostly from lack of interest.

                                                  My First campaign was a VTM game played via the multiplayer of the PC game Vampire the Masquerade Repetition back in 2001 which is also how I got into role playing and latter on actual tabel top games. I typically played with this one group which I like to call the Court of Azrural as that who the ST and head vampire of the city was called.

                                                  The main premise of the games was what if the Promethean managed to secure a city(in this case Prague) and rule out while keeping out invaders mainly the Tremere and the Capidotions. I participated in 3 of their games the last of which I can't really remember, each time as a different character because my role playing the same character was kind of schizophrenic back then. The first game had to do with us confronting a bunch of Tremere created constructs which had to do with the 4 elements that were led by Azural's brother who dubbed himself "The Firemaster". The second game involved onthoer group of Tremere who had made a giant war ghoul.

                                                  And in the sheer shot in the dark that any of the people I'm about to mention see this, I'd like to give a special shout out to Azrual, Toni, Karl, Raven, Arinna, Carry, Grrr, Jake, Luke and Josh for introducing me to the wonderful world of role playing games and putting up with my at the time annoying 13 year old ass.
