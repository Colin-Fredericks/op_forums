                                    Originally posted by Saur Ops Specialist View Post
                                    You're going to need several different social systems, and they might not all line up so well between corporate boardrooms, high society galas, drug deals, and hostage negotiation. For that matter, Attributes probably won't line up well on an axis other than Physical, Social, and Mental; there's no guarantee of Power, Finesse, and Resistance neatly divided up.
                                    The 'A Song of Ice and Fire' system is for all social interaction of a given magnitude. Smaller stuff can be handled with a single roll, though. But that's not different to combat or physical challenges, that STs might handwave if of minor significance.

                                    But the system actually involves social "hit points" called "Composure". And you have a kind of "Social Armour" called "Intrigue Defense".
                                    So, if you want to Bargain, Charm, Convince, Incite, Intimidate, Seduce or Taunt a person the ST first determines what kind of relationship you and that person have at the beginning of the social battle, called "Disposition", while the whole affair is called an "Intrigue".
                                    The Disposition determines your Social Damage Reduction, as well bonuses or penalties one might get (usually, if you like someone, he has it easier to affect you (lower Damage Reduction, and you might be more willing to persuade the person, than lying to her, while a person you hate can say just about anything she wants without affecting you...)

                                    So a Player chooses one of the actions above, selecting if he is using persuasive or deceptive and rolls the appropriate attribute+skill combination. If bypassing the Intrigue Defense, damage (called "Influence") is dealt. Influence is tied to another attribute, so Bargaining does Cunning attribute rating Influence damage, while Seduction would deal Influence damage tied to the Persuasion attribute. This damage is reduced by the Social Damage Reduction based on the Disposition and any remaining Influence is removed from the Composure. If the latter reaches zero, you have basically reached your goal with that person.

                                    Hope that gives a glimpse on the system, without knowing the mechanics of AFOIAF-RPG. Point is, that you can easily turn all kinds of social interactions into an Intrigue based combat scenario and you have relatively clear methods to evaluate the outcome of certain actions. And "turns" in this system can reach from the blink of an eye (in a scenario where taunting is relevant) to an hour at a banquet table, when trying to arrange a marriage. The system can be used for all, an possibly even be used alongside combat, if trying to intimidate an opponent, so he would flee.

                                    Obviously with the attributes, skills and other mechanics of the WoD such a system would need a complete rebuild, but it could work. And, frankly, I think a basic system is way less confusing, than having Werewolf's Staredown, Animal Attraction, Credibility, Fast-Talk, Interrogation, Intimidation, Oration, Performance, and Seduction all having their own rules entry in the Core Rules "System and Drama" section - with most not providing a system but leaving it to the ST to decide or make up a system on the fly.

                                    EDIT: I mean if each pack member would be tested in a challenge, an Ahroun would need a huge amount of game time for a combat challenge, since there are complicated rules. The Galliard rolles Manipulation + Performance once to see if he can make the Elder cry of joy or agony with his story. Hardly seems fair - drama wise...
