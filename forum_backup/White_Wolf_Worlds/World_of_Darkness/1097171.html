              Well, there is a legitimate concern about how many Kindred can live in an area without detection. In general, you do not want more than 1% of the healthy adult human population to be fed upon every year because of the risk of hospitalization or death due to overfeeding. When you look at the USA, that means a ratio of around 1:50,000. Of course, animal blood can allow for greater populations (animal blood can allow up to a ratio of 1:10,000 for non-Ventrue). I generally run Kindred at a metropolitan average of 1:50,000, with animal blood providing large reserves of sustenance to supplement the luxury of human blood among non-Ventrue (Venture should always have Herd 4+ in my opinion unless they want to become dependent on Kindred blood).

                  A few tentative ratios for cities by supernatural critter, mostly arrived at by looking solely at how many NPCs have been referred to in a locale - baseline 'bare minimums' to achieve the representation we see in the actual game world.

                  Mage
                  San Francisco: Mages have 1:47,000 for the bay area as a whole and 1:5,400 if we use just San Francisco's population. Add the other supernaturals and the Bay Area ratio becomes 1:13,000. If they all met in the city one day, just because, San Francisco would temporarily have a ratio of 1:1500. Let the freak flag fly.
                  Other mage ratios:
                  -Richmond, VA at 1:19,500.
                  -Prince Albert, Sasketchawan, at 1:3200.
                  -Hong Kong, at 1:28,500.
                  -Charleston, South Carolina at either 1:4800 or 1:33,250 for the greater metropolitan area.
                  -Tryon, North Carolina at a terrifying 1:130. All Verbena. There are more verbena in that particular backwater than there are Asians or Native Americans.
                  -Seattle, at 1:18,200.
                  -Las Vegas, NV, is at 1:19,000. That book, ironically, gave us the mage figure of 1:150,000, while even at the greater municipal area the ratio is only 1:81,000. Of course, those ratios are averages, etc.
                  -Greenland has 1:57,000.
                  Paris is at a minimum figure of 1:650,000.
                  The bare minimum global ratio, including mages we know nothing about or who live in the Umbra, is 1:842,500.

                  Changelings
                  Philadelphia is at 1:252,000 people.
                  Toronto is at 1:54,000.
                  Hawaii rises higher to 1:38,500.
                  San Francisco is higher still (noticing a pattern? If an Immortal Eyes novel or corebook was there, you get far better representation) at 1:16,500.
                  But strangely, the highest ratio is not San Francisco, the glamour-vortex and attention hog. So far, it's Richmond California. Just outside of San Francisco itself, it outstrips that city in proportion by far. There is 1 changeling for every 3,350 normal people in that city.
                  n America, there is a bare minimum of 1 Changeling to every 590,000 normal people.
                  In the UK, it grows to 1:390,000.
                  In Canada, it grows to 1:350,000.
                  Great Britain as a whole moves lower, to 1:430,000 - and England alone is 1:420,000.
                  Wales, however, is 1:89,000.
                  Scotland is even higher, at 1:70,000.
                  Ireland of course rises to a high of 1:52,000.
                  Our bare minimum global ratio is around 1:2,810,000.

                  Demons
                  We know very little here, and most places other than LA had maybe 1 demon referred to as in residence.
                  At its height, LA had 1 demon for every 30,000 mortals in addition to its vampires, garou, mages, and changelings. It's a crowded city if you count the entire oWoD as one setting. Add just some of the vampires alone and you go to a 10,000:1 supernatural ratio. A real rough figure for LA, assuming all lines exist, comes out to around 400 supernatural creatures at its height - likely to be higher than that in real terms once I process all the entries. For a population ratio, that's 1:9,500. Turns out LA has a shitton of monsters. If we use the county figure though that drops to 1:30,000. Still pretty high, but not as dramatic.
                  Paris has a minimum ratio of 1:10,000,000.

                  Imbued
                  There is an absolute minimum of 1 Imbued (including Bystanders) for every 6 million people if we take the global figure, and 1:1.3mil if we extrapolate from the disproportionate American weighting of the Imbued population.

                  Vampire
                  For St. Louis, if we wrap in the full greater area it's 1 vampire for every 96,000 mortals. The city alone drops that to 1:14,000. Add in ghouls and they respectively become 1:43,000 and 1:6,360. Toronto has 1:50,000 in the greater area with the local area dropping to 1:29,000. Sydney was at 1:82,500. San Diego was at 1:43,000. Stockholm, in an inversion of the ratios decreasing in modern sources, is terrifyingly low at 1:8,500.
                  Paris was 1:40,600 Vampire+Ghoul:Human. 1:45,000 without ghouls.
                  Mexico City sat at 1:42,500 Vamp:human in its first appearance, and an even higher 1:17,200 in its later appearance (1:14,500 with ghouls and revenants), and a bare minimum combined ratio of 1:12,300.
                  Sarasota at the highest possible is 1:1,185 and at the lowest, 1:1,700 vampire:human. I know it's got a fair number of tourists going through but jesus. Tampa's not much better at 1:7,000. Disney World, bizarrely, is at 1:20,700 when you count the staff as the population, using the expy in a Darkling Plain.


                  Garou and Fera
                  Like with Vampire, I haven't finished the data set for these guys.
                  Paris has 1:10,000,000 with ratkin, but that means there's probably a ton more we just haven't seen yet.
