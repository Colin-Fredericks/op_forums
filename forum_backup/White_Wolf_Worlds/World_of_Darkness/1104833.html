    Changelings as Angels?

    06-17-2017, 04:06 PM
    I run a Demon: The Fallen campaign. Huge fan of The Midnight Express Podcast, and they recent plugged a new podcast called Walking Away from Arcadia, a Changeling: The Dreaming podcast. One episode in, and it's really great.

    As I was listening, it made me wonder: Is there any way you could view Changelings as the Angels of Demon: The Fallen? I have some thoughts on this but I'm just wondering what the community thinks of this idea.

              I think you can make them work, in an interesting way. When looked at from a Werewolf lens, the Fae are generally linked to Wyld or creation. The Elohim are creation spirits as well, albeit essentially doing the work of God. As Lian says, there are a lot of similarities between both groups.

              If you're operating from a Demon the Fallen origin story, then I think you could easily translate the Fae as fellow Elohim on a different layer of creation, who, after the defeat of the demons, lost contact with Heaven, and started being molded in different ways by human belief/faith. (The Changelings could be a really interesting take on Nephilim, if you're inclined to go that way.)

              The relation of kithain to the Tuatha De Danaan could be a reflection of this. (The Fallen then could be connected to the Fir Bolg/Adhene/Formorions)

              Another option might be to have the Fae as having been a group of Fallen that managed to separate themselves from Lucifer's forces and therefore, wouldn't have taken part in the worst of the misdeeds. Thus, when the Angels won, the Fae might have had a completely different fate, being either left on Earth or sent to Arcadia, instead of the Abyss.

                  An idea I've toyed with, in relation to how Judeo-Christian thought would classify the Fae, is something similar to this. Fairies were angels that didn't Fall, per se. Rather, when the War in Heaven broke out, they were the angels who refused to take a side, either because of cowardice, reluctance to commit violence, or a desire to stay out of it until they knew which way the wind was blowing. When Lucifer's forces inevitably lost and were cast into Hell, God turned to the matter of the angels who refused to commit to a side.

                  They hadn't rebelled, as such, so they didn't merit being cast into Hell. But their loyalty and faith in their creator was shown to be faulty, and thus could not be permitted to remain in Heaven. As such, they were exiled to Earth (or under the Earth), or to an appropriate realm of dreams. Many of these Faithless Angels remained haughty, not believing they'd done anything wrong by refusing to fight. It helped to form their capricious and self-centered views, this unwillingness to acknowledge the gravity of their sins. As such, while not as loathsome as a demon, they are stilled mired in self-delusion and pride.

                  Or at least that's how a Christian theorist might explain them, and why they can't exactly be trusted.

                  As for how this theory would be applied to Demon: The Fallen, I neither know nor particularly care. I make no secret of my disinterest (bordering on disdain) for Demon. I'm just here, offering my two cents.

                              If I'm reading the OP correctly, (forgive me if I'm not), it seems like the idea is to relate Changelings to Demon's cosmology rather than the reverse.

                              I was thinking that there could be an interesting connection made between Demonic Visages and Fae Seemings. Particularly when we look at how the Fallen began taking new Visages based on the new Lores devised during the War.

                              Demon the Fallen does a pretty good job of incorporating different folklore versions of Angels and Demons, but one thing I've realized is that DtF doesn't really cover dreams at all. And that's a little weird, because demonic folklore often involves people being tormented or seduced via their dreams. But that could be a means to incorporate the Fae.

                              If we look at how the Slayers developed the Underworld via Demon the Fallen Cosmology, then perhaps something could be devised for the Dreaming as well. A heretofore unknown branch/Visage/Lore out of the Fourth House, perhaps?

                              An alternate option might be to look at the Houses, and then look at the Fae, and find correlations. For example, Defilers, like Merfolk, are associated with seduction and the sea (possibly Satyrs might fit here too.) Malefactors and Nockers. Devourers and Redcaps. Devils and Sidhe seem to work pretty well too.

                              If you're using the Demon the Fallen cosmology, then the entire Tuatha de Danaan/Fir Bolg war might be yet another version of the Angels vs. the Demons, with the Kithain types representing new Angelic visages (if Demons can create new Visages, why can't Angels). Perhaps then that answers "what happened to the Angels"? After the Angelic Host won the war, God disappeared, and having lost their connection to their origins and separated from their original purposes, many Angels ended up latching onto humans and their dreams as an alternate source of sustenance/purpose, causing them to gradually change into the Dark Ages Fae concept and then, possibly even the Changelings themselves. (Another possibility for the Changelings is that they're the Angelic equivalent of Nephilim, though more of a union of soul rather than body.)
