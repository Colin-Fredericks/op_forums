                    Looking this over the biggest issue I have is with the base premise itself, the Technocracy exists to dispel and remove mention of the supernatural. The Camarilla (and a good portion of the Independents, and some of the more adroit Sabbat) have a vested self-interest in maintaining the Masquerade. If the Masquerade has fallen, both sides have essentially already failed at their most basic mission. Additionally removing/greatly lessening Paradox eliminates the only check on magickal power, likely handing the victory to the Union since even without access to things like interdimensional vessels, they still have more than enough hypertech to eliminate all but the more potent elders with ease. Within the context of the parameters as given, I think the freeform nature of awakened magick (even limited by the Technocratic mindset) will eventually win out, the question is how much of humanity and the Earth is left after the fallout (both figurative and literally). Could vampires win? Possibly, with luck and guile they might work their way into dealing with the higher ups in the Technocracy before they can bring the full might of the Technocratic enlightened will to bear. That said the vampires are definitely the underdogs in this match in my opinion, if Paradox was still functioning in a manner as 'normal' I think the vampires stand a better chance (but are still the underdogs, magick's open ended nature is still a huge advantage).

                    <My two cents on how the failure of the Masquerade could play out in an interesting way>
                    With such a shattering of the screen which protects humans from knowledge of the supernatural, a more likely outcome is both sides fall prey to factionalization and infighting. The different conventions all blame each other for the failure. In the same way vampire society would likely come crashing down as well. The elders who remember the times of the Inquisition are likely to be in a panic, since humanity has come a long way from relying on pitchforks, fire, and faith to defeat vampires (especially those with knowledge of [Ravnos]'s fate). Younger vampires are likely to look at the event as the moment they've been waiting for (especially the Sabbat including some of their elders) where vampires can finally act like vampires without pretending to be human just for the sake of the Masquerade. Each side ends up paralyzed by infighting and finger pointing with those who aren't set about turning the world into what amounts to a dumpster fire.

                    Eventually after exhausting the initial arguing over whose fault the situation is the elders among the Camarilla and the higher ups in the Technocracy are likely to come to an ironic agreement to remove the wild and out of control elements. Essentially I see the two entering into a devil's bargain; the vampires get the right to exist and the Technocracy will begin researching ways to provide for vampiric needs (think a True Blood like concoction) in return the vampires being working with the Technocracy to eliminate the vampiric elements who wont play nice with humanity (as well as offering the ability to create essentially immortal assets through ghouldom/embrace) as well as removing the threat of other reality deviants. With the knowledge of the vampiric elders (and the sympathetic links through vitae) the Sabbat and the other inhuman vampires are likely to be wiped out (a handful might escape, but will not have the strength to provide any real resistance, since if they reveal themselves they would be destroyed). That said some of the battles are going to be quite brutal, a fairly sizable chunk of humanity is likely to die in the war. In the end you have a new status quo.
                    • Vampires exist and the Technocracy has created (or is creating) scientific ways to explain the various powers and abilities they possess, essentially vampirism is added to the Consensus.
                    • New laws are crafted and quickly passed to answer questions regarding vampiric legal status for things such as political standing and rights, taxes, estates, ghouling, embrace, use of supernatural powers, etc.
                    • The Technocracy has created an artificial substitute for blood that is palatable for all vampires (even those with feeding restrictions) and is provided after registering with the New World Order (registering is likely to be providing a sample of vitae and or flesh for tracking and surveillance purposes).
                    • The pro-Masquerade elders now have nearly complete control over younger vampires (since the agreement that protect vampires is vouchsafed by the elders and the Technocratic control). Failure to follow the rules will be met with extreme punishment (in most cases likely destruction of the offending vampire).
                    • The Technocracy, bolstered by powers such as Dominate, Presence, Obfuscate, Chimerstry, etc, begin to zealously crush knowledge of other supernaturals and likely refocus their attention at dealing with the Traditions and other reality deviants. Bolstered by their vampiric allies, the Traditions could be in a great deal of trouble (which could lead to a Tradition and Garou alliance in the face of this new Techno-vampiric Union).
                    • The rogue elements of vampire society (Anarchs, Sabbat, and Independents who refuse to play within the new rules) are either destroyed or decimated to the point of being nonfactors (exceptions might occur, those knowledgeable in Necromancy or Spirit Manipulation might flee beyond the Shroud/Gauntlet for example). In the same way rogue elements within the Technocracy are likely to be found and dealt with after the application of the various powers of their new vampiric allies (blood bond and Dominate are great ways to insure re-education proceeds at the approved Technocratic pace).
                    • The Technocracy might even be able to remove the vampiric clan weaknesses (while they might not be able to roll more successes than God and cure vampirism they should be able to get more than Caine).
                    • Within the Technocracy I see vampires ending up in one of three possible situations
                      • The vampires become their own Convention within the Union
                      • The vampires join the Conventions which match their mindset and skill set. This could lead to things like Ventrue or Giovanni being higher ups in the Syndicate, Tzimisce using Vicissitude to assist Progenitor experiments, Tremere techno-mages assisting Iteration X in developing ghoul/vampire HIT Marks, etc.
                      • The vampires do not join the Union outright but rather maintain a separate governing body, bound by agreements and treaties with the Union and possibly mortal authorities depending on how new vampire laws look.
