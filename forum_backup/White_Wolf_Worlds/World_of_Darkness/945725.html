          I'm a fan of assuming that every type of supernatural doesn't actually have a handle on the setting's Cosmology.                           Mage, Werewolf and Wraith all kind of complement each other, covering different parts of the World of Darkness, but being mostly consistent. KotE, likewise, seems to present the same model for the universe, but simplifies it into Yang World, Yin World and Yomi.

                          The system of Celestines, Incarna, etc, also allows for Vampire's cosmology (there are so many gods, having YHWH exist doesn't contradict that, and Revelations of the Dark Mother is particularly crossover-friendly with the Mage cosmology).

                          Demons can mostly be rationalised as delusional servants of YHWH, and lesser gods with some similarities to humans but without their free will (read: Enlightened Will). Perhaps they're twisted, mutated or else otherwise more primal forms of Avatar wrapped in celestial bodies. They could be the Psychopomps or at least be related to them. Most likely they are or contain fragments of The One, like humanity is or does, and affect the world through these God-shards (Avatars), but without the free will humans have to truly Awaken. They could very well be the lesser Pure Ones, or the Elohim of RotDM. But, being closer to YHWH (who seems to be a higher Pure One, although probably not The One) than humans, they seem more bound up in what he is and what he believes than humans are.

                          Humans, ironically, by being further removed from YHWH, seem to have more potential to equal or exceed his own power (e.g., as Lilith did, compared to the relative failure of Lucifer to truly become a god in the same way). Perhaps it's because gods will always, in this cosmology, be servants and slaves to the fragments of The One they serve or are created by (The One being, in this case, Adam Kadmon/Cosmic Man and not some separate supernal entity at all). If this is true, humanity is really God, and all the other gods are created by/imagined by humanity, rather than the other way around. These delusions just keep their true God from realising its true nature.

                          Changelings still fit into this cosmology rather well. The Dreaming still exists in the Umbra, except it cuts across multiple layers of it. The only problem with changelings is that they don't realise they fit so neatly into this cosmology. To them, everything else fits into their own conception of the Dreaming as central. In that respect, they're very much like Demons. Like Demons, they can potentially have great power in their true forms, but they're also too inextricably bound up with their god/uber-Avatar/Pure One (in this case the Dreaming) to truly Awaken.

                          The Neverborn, as they exist in Wraith and Mage, seem like the anti-Pure Ones. Fragments of The Other that stands against The One. The Qlippoth to the Sephiroth. Oblivion to Prime. Undoing to Becoming. And the fact that The One is fragmented, divided, spread far and wide gives them the balance of power at the moment, as the World of Darkness is leaning towards Entropy more than Dynamism. But when the Time of Judgement comes, the rift between The One and The Other may be healed as The One is devoured by The Other. Thus, it all starts again.
