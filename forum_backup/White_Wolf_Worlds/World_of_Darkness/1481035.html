    Adapt a show or movie's setting to the World of Darkness

    05-13-2022, 10:57 PM
    What the title says:

    Stranger Things is just straight up set in the World of Darkness with the Technocracy having a Construct there, a Progentior psychic experiment, and Void Engineers attempting to breach the Penumbra but attracting Nephandus/Wyrm creatures due to the cruelty of their human experimentation.

    Ironically, the Technocracy helps explain one of the sillier elements of season three as the Technocracy didn't think anything of using Correspondence "Enlightened Science" to move a large number of Russian assets to the area to resume their research as well as build a massive underground base under a mall despite the absurd impracticalities during the Cold War.

    Mind you, no one really wants to admit a bunch of frigging pre-teens helped bring down an entire Construct.

    Twice.

      I use a system of 'Eigenvectors' to analyse stories so that they can be replayed but in a different setting and with different characters. There are four types of colour-coded vectors - Quest, Hindrance, Advantage, and Adversary (yes it was inspired by Fate Core), if you're interested in learning more I've got a document about it all. As there have been a number of films of this classic story I think it's suitable for inclusion.

      Romeo and Juliet
      The protagonists and Romeo and Juliet and they share all eigenvectors unless otherwise noted. Both share an overall goal:

      [Quest - Two Houses Divided]
      Your house is at odds with another, do your best to avoid strife and bring them together.
      Blocked Reward: [Advantage - Two Houses United]
      Failure: [Two Houses at War]

      The setting is renaissance Italy, at night.

      Act I

      [Adversary - Toreador vs Tremere]
      You'd like to teach the other house's scions a lesson
      ST note - any combat leads to [Hindrance - On Pain of Death]

      [Hindrance - On Pain of Death]
      The Prince of the city has decreed that any further breach of the peace is punishable by death

      Act II - Dream

      [Juliet's Hindrance - Paris's Courtship]
      Your father, mother, and blood doll all think you should accept Count Paris's offer of marriage but you are not interested in him. You must find a way out before your parents force you into marriage.

      [Romeo's Quest - Romeo and Rosaline]
      You have an unrequited infatuation with Rosaline, find a way to woo her.
      - Attempt to meet her at the Capulet ball while in disguise
      - Avoid Tybalt
      Leads to [True Love Quest - Romeo and Juliet]

      [True Love Quest - Romeo and Juliet]
      You must find a way to meet with your true love, woo them, and 'live happily ever after'
      - Balcony scene
      - Get married
      - Reveal your union at the right time and unite your houses
      Reward: finishing this quest unblocks the Reward for [Quest - Two houses Divided]

      Act III - Frustration

      [Hindrance – Star Cross'd Lovers]
      The Prince has banned all breaches of peace in the city so your love must remain secret lest your families fall into fighting
      Progress: Turn the card to gain 1xp and fill in one section of the progress bar whenever your secret love is almost exposed
      [ ] [ ] [ ]
      Completion: When all the sections are filled in or your secret is exposed, gain 3xp, discard (burn) this card, and replace it with [Hindrance – Two Houses at War]

      [Romeo's Ally - Mercutio]
      Your old friend and ally, will defend your honour to the death.

      [Romeo's Adversary - Tybalt]
      Incensed that you sneaked into the Tremere ball, Tybalt challenges you to a duel. You must find a way to make peace with him because if Tybalt dies there will be serious consequences.
      Reward: ???
      Failure: [Two Houses at War]
      ST notes - possible outcomes:
      - Romeo reveals his secret love, Mercutio defends him, Tybalt lives > [Hindrance - Exiled]
      - Romeo surrenders, Mercutio killed by Tybalt, Tybalt killed by Romeo > [Hindrance - Exiled]
      - Romeo kills Tybalt, escapes execution > [Hindrance - Wanted]
      - Mercutio kills Tybalt > Mercutio executed and [Hindrance - Two Houses at War]

      [Romeo's Hindrance - Exiled]
      You have been exiled from the city under penalty of death should you return.

      Act IV - Nightmare

      [Hindrance - Separated]
      You are separated by time and distance and can no longer find consolation in each other's arms
      ST note - Our protagonists are now separated and can no longer share eigenvectors or information

      [Juliet's Quest - Two and Forty hours]
      To avoid marriage to Paris you consult with Friar Laurence who teaches you a spell that will put you into torpor for two and forty hours while seemingly in final death. The Friar also promises to send a message to Romeo to come rescue you.
      Reward: destiny fulfilled
      ST note - leads to [Quest - A Timeless End]

      Event - Juliet tcasts the spell and is placed in the family crypt but the Friar's message to Romeo is delayed by the rising sun

      [Romeo's Ally - Balthasar]
      Upon hearing of Juliet's death your ally Balthasar rushes to your side to inform you. You must decide on a course of action, will it be vengeance or death? Leads to either [Hindrance - Two Houses at War] if you decide on vengeance, or [Quest - What ho, Apothecary!] if you decide that only death can reconcile your destiny.

      [Romeo's Quest - What ho, Apothecary!]
      Heartbroken you vow to die in Juliet's arms. Buy silver nitrate poison from an apothecary, sneak into the Capulet crypt, and lay to rest beside her.
      Reward: destiny fulfilled
      ST note - leads to [Quest - A Timeless End]

      Act V

      [Juliet's Quest - A Timeless End]
      You awake to find Romeo beside you, dead by poison, and the sun slowly creeping through the door of the crypt. You must decide whether to live eternally without your true love or cast both of you into the sunlight.
      ST note - leads to either [Advantage - Two houses United] or [Hindrance - Two Houses at War]

      [Advantage - Two Houses United]
      Your two families are united.

      [Hindrance - Two Houses at War]
      Your two families are at war.

                              That show is probably largely responsible for my life long interests in history and supernatural horror. That and Frankenstein vs the Wolfman.

                              I think the remake film ends up offering the best possibility for a crossover via Pamela's necklace. Say she killed the two councilors responsible for Jason's drowning the next summer, causing the camp to close. But it ultimately doesn't help her loss, and several years later, a depressed Pamela wanders into Louis's shop, where he sells her the necklace. It starts whispering to her in Jason's voice, eventually leading her back to Crystal Lake. There she finds out that they are planning to reopen the summer camp, and her own rage combined with the necklace's influence sends her on a murder spree.
                              The necklace's power is, obviously, to bring back a deceased loved one, with the more murders you commit, the more "real" they become. (It's not really them though, just a simulation.) But Pamela's own death ends up being what brings this Jason fully to life. And he goes on to kill anyone intruding on his territory. His mother may whisper to him via the necklace, but the Jason doesn't have a soul to take so it has no need to bring back his mother and he's going to kill anyway.
                              You could probably somehow tie this in with some stuff the comic books came up with, about the land around Crystal Lake being corrupted or cursed, similar to King's Pet Semitary.

                                                  There's basically two Friday: The Thirteenth ideas for a series with almost zero continuity:

                                                  1:] Backwoods Psycho Jason: Jason Voorhees didn't drown and he actually ended up swimming to shore before living in the woods for twenty years despite his various "issues", becoming a feral wildman. This makes sense as campfire story logic but opens its own questions.

                                                  2:] Jason X/Evil Dead Jason: Pamela Voorhees somehow acquired the Necronomicon and proceeded to use the object to resurrect her dead son. Which has its own can of worms and insanity but would explain why Jason is a seemingly immortal killing machine.

                                                  My preferred version for the World of Darkness is that Jason Voorhees did survive the events of his "drowning" and ended up possessed by something in the Lake that turned him into a Formor. I'm going to go with W:TA and suggest that he was Child of Gaia kinfolk as was Pamela.

                                                  Surviving in the abandoned cabins on stolen food and dead animals, he was a fairly benign spirit or at least unimportant until Pamela Voorhees death. From there, the spirit inside him twisted and became malevolent and he began his killing spree. The legend of his evils combined with his weird connection to the Lake meant that when he was finally killed by adolescent Tommy Jarvis, he wasn't permanently slain and possessed a variety of people before being bonded with his original body.

                                                  The merged spirit of the original Jason and the corrupted Wyrm spirit are impossible to separate now and it's difficult to say where one begins and the other ends. However, it's a LOT more powerful than it should be as its ability to stand up to Freddy Krueger, Dream Demon, shows. The only way to permanently slay Jason is cleanse the spirit of Crystal Lake itself of the taint involved.
