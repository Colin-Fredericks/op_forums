    If you want to get a deep look at the differences between casting lead and silver, Michael Briggs did a blog series on the topic 15 years ago (give or take). He went over the engineering problems with casting silver, made up some test bullets, and did some test fires. It's a non trivial problem.

    I'm inclined to say that if an organization with significant resources was taking a crack at sniping with silver, they wouldn't just carefully design the bullet, they'd custom design a rifle for the challenges of firing silver bullets.

        There are numerous ways to make silver bullets lethal and viable. One could essentially make 50 caliber varmint grenades. Varmint grenades have a metal jacket with powdered metal inside and are used to disintegrate small animals. Fill it with powdered silver and it’s going to be extremely lethal to Garou and avoiding the issue of engineering silver bullets.

        Silver sabots could also avoid that problem but may not be as devastating.

        Really though, alpha strikes are not something that should be used against player characters. It’s just not any fun. It might be fun, though, for players to plan an alpha strike against otherwise unbeatable NPCs as long as it isn’t used to cheapen the thematic value of major villains.
