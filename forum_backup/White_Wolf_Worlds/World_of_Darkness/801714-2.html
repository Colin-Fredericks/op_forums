The Companion and Heresies of the Way were probably the books that tried the most to actually start considering the gigantic swath of Earth they are supposed to cover


                                                Originally posted by adambeyoncelowe View Post
                                                That was a problem too, although probably not the part that offended people the most. It was the Orientalism and yellow peril which did that. There's a big thing in Vampire about the invading Wan Kuei and their hatred of the West. That always seemed like the first and biggest issue to address. The narrow focus can be more easily addressed, and probably would have resolved itself after a time as more supplements came out and defined previously ignored areas of Asia.
                                                The Orientalism came from the Asian kitchensink and the desire to shunt inside as many kung fu tropes and asian myths as they could. It's not much worse then what's done in Vampire (especialy in the first edition).. With more research and context given to the cultures and mythologies of Asia, it could have been quite more engaging. And frankly the exoticism can be a positive quality as well - one of the main reasons we play these games we do is to be different from who, what, when and how we are.

                                                The Yellow Peril is a funny case. They really have a nasty vibe of "Kill Whitey" And "Kuei Jin are out to rule the world". But if you actually scratch below the surface (and San Francisco) you realise that came from a very misguided attmept by the authors to separate Kindred and Kuei Jin. They wanted to make them different and make it obvious they don't work together and that the Kindred are unwelcome in Asia. The core intent was to underscore how Kindred of the East was supposed to be a drastically different game from Vampire. Poor forethought combined with the fact that the authors lived through the Japanese invasion of the late 80s and the early 90s, and you suddenly have a number of books talking about Undead Fu Manchu The Revengeancing. Mind you, the line as a whole doesn't really give a fuck, it takes way more time talking about stuff it's actually about.
