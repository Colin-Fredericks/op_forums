            Whenever I see this sort of post, I always wish the OP would say something to the effect of "...besides/other than Gypsies", or even more broadly, "what are the worst books for reasons besides their problematic representations of one or more human cultures or societies?"

            Seriously, we get it, no one here is a racist--racists and racism are bad; real Gypsies are a nuanced human culture that should probably not have been "splatted" for the purposes of an RPG. But it's almost as if people mention the Gypsies book as an introductory paragraph before they get into discussing what is almost certainly more useful or interesting to OP: books the content of which was not systematically or narratively useful or interesting. Poorly written books (in the sense that they're hard to follow, poorly organized, boring, what have you).

            EDIT: WOD: Mafia is hot garbage--just boring and devoid of interesting material. I also never enjoyed Tradition Book: Cult of Ecstasy (Rev.) though I've had this discussion enough to know that few (if any) people share that sentiment.

                                                        Wod: Mafia was not terrible, but it also wasn't good either. Very dry. But I have a specific complaint about it. Ignore the stereotypes, ignore how it deliberately went for a more Sopranos/Godfather feel. My problem was that it was a Sicilian Mafia book, when it should have been an Organized Crime book. What if I want to include stuff about the Russian mob? The South American Cartels? The Triads or Tongs? The Yakuza? Even just the average, street level gangs? WoD: Mafia gives me no help, save for a few pages near the end talking about how the Mafia interacts with them.

                                                        This is especially galling because organized crime is a key facet of the World of Darkness. Many of the splats - Vampires, Kuei-Jin, Mages, Werewolves - exist in a world outside the law. Kindred can't exist without at least regularly committing assault and/or fraud, among many other crimes to make their unlives easier. Same with Kuei-Jin, who get most of their Chi from people's blood. Garou actively engage in corporate espionage and environmental terrorism in their fight against the Wyrm. Tradition Mages actively stay under the radar to avoid the Technocracy, who themselves have agents infiltrating many strata of society, including organized crime. Hunters are basically armed cells waging a private war against "monsters", and need heavy ordinance to fight that war. And you bet your ass crime produces more than its fair share of Wraiths. The only reason Changelings have no hand in crime is because most of them go into banality-induced hibernation for the rest of their mortal lives at some point during childhood or adolescence. And Demons? Well, make your own connections.

                                                        And yet there's no book specifically about organized crime, and how it fits into a world with all these supernaturals. We have a book about government agents (Project Twilight, a decent book I think), but the best we get is WoD: Mafia in terms of crime. A book that covers only a fraction of organized crime, and then only a heavily stylized version of it. If nothing else, I want a WoD book about organized crime because existing and working outside the law applies to both criminals and the supernatural splats. Thematically, tonally, the two are made for each other.

                                                        Magestuck: The Ascension
