                                                    Kindred of the East was really good overall.
                                                    Yeah, White Wolf botched on several cultural aspects and such... but honestly that was expected.
                                                    The one thing I couldn't stand was the P'o... and giving that potentially to another player to basically play.
                                                    That was not a a good move and is quite frankly a bad idea for several reasons... all player related.
                                                    The idea of the P'o as an active adversary on the other hand was a good one.
                                                    I just kept a lid on potential issues between players by acting as the P'o for each player character while I DM'ed.

                                                    The whole thing with the Dharmas and them being capable/expected to climb the ladder to greater understanding and power... THAT was awesome.
                                                    Overall, they should have been treated a bit different than just as vampires of the east, but that's not to hard to change.
                                                    In most instances I found that their disciplines are weaker to a degree, not as flexible or have inherent drawbacks compared to those of the Kindred.
                                                    Their lack of a distinct discipline enhancing their raw strength is putting them on the wrong foot against vampires and most shapeshifter.
                                                    There is also the issue that they have to mix and match their Yin and Yang intake and where they stand overall.
                                                    Overall, the Kuei-jin could be quite awesome, but I had the best results for my game when I rolled the Kuei-jin and Mummies basically together into one package
                                                    and found that a more or less perfect solution.
                                                    Thematically they are a PERFECT fit and quite frankly turning the Kuei-jin into immortal spirits fallen from grace and now trying to claw their way back, makes so damn much sense.
                                                    Even better the expanded options aren't going to turn them into overpowered beasts.

                                                    While I loved the concept of the mummy game line I found their different factions, unlike the general positive theme, not that evocative and rather generic,
                                                    the Kuei-jin on the other hand with their dharmas and strange moral codes didn't have that problem.

                                                    Now let's talk about the big one that concerns BOTH Kindred of the East and Mummy:
                                                    Both were rather centered on their areas and that's that, this led to a kinda messed up naming convention in case of Kindred of the East and several other fumbles in regards to location descriptions, cultural stuff and so on... also once again the whole skilltastic catastrophe that is brawl vs martial arts. If there ever was bullshit in the games it is that one.
                                                    Originwise it would have been better if they would have opened up the whole world for Kindred of the East gamers, instead of limiting it to most parts of Asia and a speck here and there.
                                                    For the Mummy game line they did try to do that with the Player's companion, with varying success but at least they gave it a shot.
                                                    If they ever modernize those lines or go for the whole 20th anniversary treatment, they would have to rewrite a good chunk here and there to make it palatable.
                                                    It could be done and it wouldn't be too much work, since a solid foundation is already in place, still it would need to be adressed.
