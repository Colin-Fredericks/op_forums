    I'd be interested in 20th anniversary version of literally all of the things. Having said that, it sounds like the only acceptable path forward for the IP holders re- KotE is a game about supernatural creatures in the east that are vampires... and that's it. Mind you, I'd be interested in seeing what they come up with--but the fact is that I am basically guaranteed to back/kickstart/buy every 20th anniversary game, precisely because I already know I like all of those games. It sounds like I would not have any way knowing whether I would like what they came up with next by dint of the fact that it would be top-to-bottom completely different from KotE now.

    Originally posted by tasti man LH View Post
    -Kindred of the East, from my understanding, neither OPP or nuWW wants to touch it. That because of the cultural baggage surrounding the original, neither want to engage with it.

    (aka from my cynical point of view: "We don't know any Asian writers and/or writers knowledgeable in Asian culture")
    I'm sure this is a not-insignificant part of it, but I doubt it's the whole story. Just think, if they poo-poo'ed classic V:tM because having keeping track of a two gauges (blood and willpower) was "not sexy" and too much like having a gas tank (or, you know, like any one of about millions things in every day life that require that one be somewhat mindful of how much of a thing they have), then managing like four different stats--and not only how much of each one you have, but also the ratio of one to the other (among other things)--is going to be least sexy thing ever.

    Having said that, while I readily admit to not being any kind of expert, and can at least see the arguments for some parts of the game being orientalist, KotE never struck me as being hamfisted with its cultural stereotypes in the way that Werewolf was (i.e., Celtic werewolves like to drink; being poor, as the Bone Gnawers, is a culture unto itself rather than something terrible that befalls millions of people; German werewolves are authoritarian fascists; feminist werewolves are gender fascists; etc.) I mean, what is it about the Resplendent Cranes (to name just one Dharma) that is supposed to get my hackles up? I assume that there's something ugly and wrong about it that I'm not getting because I'm not educated enough to recognize the insensitivity, and so the different philosophies espoused by the different Dharmas simply seem esoteric to me rather than making me think "oooo boy that's problematic." I don't need anyone to tell me specifics, or perpetuate the actual ugly stereotype--just get me into the categorical ballpark of which actual groups would stand to feel unjustly drawn upon or appropriated.

    Originally posted by IanWatson View Post
    if we wanted to do a new KotE book, we're not going to rely on the same tropes and stereotypes that the original 1998 release did. We'd pretty much want to do a full bottom-to-top rewrite.
    Ironically, a game which drew so heavily on the tropes and themes of anime would very likely be more popular and timely today than in '98. I know pen and paper games are quite popular in Japan at least--what are the odds that the sort of games that they'd like and enjoy wouldn't track along the same lines as what's popular in their various media? Isekai: The Harem-ing

                        Originally posted by Lian View Post
                        KOTE was already niche, with the PUSH to try and get everything behind their 5e version I can't imagine any new 20s coming out.

I think the WOD5 lines in general are making the same mistake pathfinder is with P2e, in that they're trying to follow D&D's runaway success with simplifying their game mechanics for a wider audience.

                        This doesn't exactly work the same for WOD and Pathfinder because they have entirely different audiences. Pathfinder's audience was the people who jumped off at D&D 4e and hate the watered down feel of 5e. WOD's audience is actually rather varied. While the wod writers seem Absolutely Convinced that everyone plays for personal horror And Only personal horror(*cough cough* avatar storm, w20 arcanoi, V5 in general, *cough*), a lot of their playerbase is actually spread on different "layers of play".

                        Some players want to play the neonate struggling with the beast, sure, but other want to play the rise of the new prince, be a dark super hero fighting the forces of corruption, or become great villains in their own right. Best way I can explain it is with VTM as a lens, Some people want to play neonate personal horror, some want to play the ancillae getting things done, some want to play the princes of political and social games, and some want to play the ancient elders pulling the strings towards a personal agenda.

                        Classic WOD and it's metaplot allowed all of this, V5(and w20 before that, and Mta Rev before that...) wants everyone to be a neonate.

                        Lots of people have problems with that.
