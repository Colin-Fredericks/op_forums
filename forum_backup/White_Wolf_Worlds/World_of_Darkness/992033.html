    So I finally got the M20 core book.. My complaints aren't what I expected them to be.

    10-23-2016, 05:56 PM
    So, thanks to the only local game turning to M20.... I had to make the plunge. Kinda annoyed me but.

    Now that I've had a look at it, I expected certain things to annoy me, and to a certain degree they do. The technephandi seem MASSIVELY overplayed here on these boards given it's essentially a disparate tinfoil hat theory.

    What annoys me instead are the Disparate Alliance, and the codified paradigms and practices.

    Firstly, I feel like the Disparate Alliance gutted a number of the independent crafts and combined them into a hamfisted alliance billed on equal parts paranoid conspiracy theory and "We're not white! The western devils are to blame for EVERYTHING."

    Secondly, And I feel this is largely connected to the first. The one size fits all Paradigm descriptions, while they speed play and character creation, distract from the internal philosophy of each faction and reduce them to interchangeable buzzwords. THIS is why the Disparate alliance felt so.... empty. Because all the intricate beliefs about how and why they do what they do were replaced with politics. Everything was about how the other two factions screwed them over and so they needed to make a third.

                          It's not a random appeal to real-world politics. These disparates are based on real world cultures, and the politics relevant to those cultures are relevant to the disparates derived from those cultures.

                                          It's also worth noting that despite the best of intentions, racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia, etc. still exist in environments where at least lip service is given to the idea of not allowing them. That the Technocracy has overt policy regarding these things is not a sign of enlightened (ahem) perspectives, but more likely a sign that they do exist within the ranks and leadership and getting rid of them is an ideal, and not necessarily one everyone has bought into.

                                          The bigot flaw you quote describes the overt, obvious type of bigot, the kind of person who often serves as a stand-in to prove that someone isn't really racist, sexist, etc. "It's not like I'm a member of the KKK" etc. Even though people hold a lot of racist, sexist, homophobic, transphobic, and ableist attitudes without loudly proclaiming them from the rooftops, and you're more likely to encounter that then the overt bigoted hatred. Or to quote MLK for an example of what I am talking about:

                                          "First, I must confess that over the last few years I have been gravely disappointed with the white moderate. I have almost reached the regrettable conclusion that the Negro's great stumbling block in the stride toward freedom is not the White Citizen's Council-er or the Ku Klux Klanner, but the white moderate who is more devoted to "order" than to justice; who prefers a negative peace which is the absence of tension to a positive peace which is the presence of justice; who constantly says "I agree with you in the goal you seek, but I can't agree with your methods of direct action;" who paternalistically feels he can set the timetable for another man's freedom; who lives by the myth of time and who constantly advises the Negro to wait until a "more convenient season."

                                          Shallow understanding from people of goodwill is more frustrating than absolute misunderstanding from people of ill will. Lukewarm acceptance is much more bewildering than outright rejection."
                                          From Reverend Martin Luther King, Jr.'s Letter from Birmingham Jail.

                                          Or more to the point, the Syndicate has a lot of influence over the economy, but the wage gap (both gender and race) still exists, and gets worse the higher up you go in any given corporation. Just as one example. The Technocracy may ideally want to move past these attitudes, but the reality is that they're firmly entrenched in them. So are the Traditions in a lot of ways.

                                              The reason bigotry survives in an organization that considers bigotry unscientific and absurd is multi-layered.
                                              (1)The guys at the top are often centuries old thanks to biological and cybernetic magitech. So their biases are very entrenched, even if they were politically liberal by the standards of their time.

                                              (2)The organization is secretive and top-down. This means that the more egregious acts of bigotry are often committed by men who are powerful, entrenched, and have institutions in place designed specifically to cover up whatever they are doing.

                                              (3)The very non-democratic nature of the organization means that successors are hand-picked by the older rulers, who in turn pick people they like. So even the younger rulers of the Technocracy have an old-boy mentality, having been hand-picked by the old-boys-club.

                                              The result is an organization that is deeply hypocritical. The guys up top are aware that many of the young science-nerds who make up their rank-n-file are often deeply liberal in every sense except perhaps loving order a bit more than freedom. So they pay lip-service to most liberal ideals. But they themselves often embody the worst aspects of old-boys-club establishment types, entitled and obsessed with keeping themselves on-top, rich, and privileged.
