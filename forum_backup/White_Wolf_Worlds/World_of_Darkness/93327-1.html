    Originally posted by Yue Ryong View Post
    I love the Week of Nightmares.
    I love the crossover elements.
    I love the apocalyptic feel.
    I love the fact that it made the Vampires feel IMPORTANT to the wider WoD, in ways that the other game-lines couldn't have understood before then.
    I love that it showed that the Garou's ongoing claim that the Final Battle was coming wasn't just empty talk... and that it galvanised them into preparing in full for the coming of the Wyrm.
    I love the fact that it demonstrated the Technocracy at their best and most hubristic - the day when the self-appointed guardians of humanity had to back up their claim that they were here for humanity's best interests... and the day when they almost broke the world doing so.
    I love the vast, grand scale, because I love the fact that while it may not be every day... it may not even happen for decades at a time... but on some days... Events are grand and vast.
    To put it in historical terms... the Cold War is scary because it could have been WWIII. That's what the Week of Nightmares is to me... the moment when things almost broke loose.
    This sums up my sentiments rather well.

                    oWoD isn't my specialty, especially since I'm coming in on the 20th Anniverseries where it seems like the Metaplot is being....squashed maybe? Whatever.

                    I do have feelings on the Week of Nightmares though, as I keep hearing about it. I may not know all about it, but it more or less comes down to this:

                    The story of it feels very cool. The comparison of Ravnos to a kaiju is oddly appropriate, and I'm filled with a sense of awe of just how much went into taking him out, an alignment of accords. Part of me wonders why it didn't start World War Three and the end of the world, as it seems like a great time to knock all the old competitors off their feet, but I digress(besides, I think everyone was just going "Look, we can kill each other later, JUST KILL THAT THING FIRST"). It's modern mythos in a strange and unusual world that promises the end days, and it feels like it. I'm intrigued by the idea of running a game set in that time frame. It's a very cool thing to look at.

                    The problems comes from it's utility. I don't feel like I have a use for it outside of running games explicitly in the week. It's not something I want to mandate or have to work around in my normal games.

                    So, yeah.

                                                        I'm kinda torn about the Week of Nightmares- from a reader's point of view, I think it's cool. It advances the plot, shows how the different supernatural factions of CWoD mix together when they fight some ancient terror, and it mess things up so much that everyone just feel "the End is Here!"

                                                        From the player/ST's point of view, however, I don't really like it. First, an Antediluvian, one of the ancient blood gods from before the Flood, a being which should be used in order to create horror and be kept as a ST's tool- is getting killed. I hate it when Gods are getting killed, especially while the other Antediluvians still have "loose ends" about their death. It feels like "oh, you wanted to use him? well you can't we killed him. enjoy". Also, if you already kill a Plot Device, let it die at its own home. Instead, he got killed by a great crossover event- in which the vampires did nothing but eating each other. I mean, if you are doing some giant crossover event, at lest give the vamps the lead role- for it is their boss fight! Finally, I think that the WoN has too much effect on the players. Sure, you can still play one of the surviving Ravanos, but playing a clan which has lost their Antediluvian is a story which is already been told by two different groups in Vampire (and even there we have some unfinished details about the death of their Antediluvians). It is like "hi, I've heard you lost your Antediluvian- join the club! we have cookies!"
