      Hmm.....

      Maybe start with the three factions who would work best at it: The Sons of Ether Ethernauts, the Nockers Æthernauts, and the Glasswalker Umbral Flyers*.

      With those three as the base, this would be something that draws heavily on Victorian scientific romance and science fiction (see the old Space 1889 RPG) and pulp era science fantasy (John Carter and others). For those who traffic in the World(s) Beyond, our solar system is a place of endless wonder and adventure. The Moon, Venus, and Mars are the mysterious caverns, lush jungles, and canal-crossed deserts of fiction and imagination, with their own native inhabitants. Even Mercury has a thin belt of life on the border between the molten sunside and the frozen dark. Hidden on the other side of the sun is the haunted machine world of Vulcan. The many moons of Jupiter and Saturn are mysterious new worlds in their own right. Uranus is a world of never-ending sky and Neptune the ultimate sea. Pluto holds its own secrets, including outposts for invaders from the Dark Between the Stars.

      Mage, Fae, and Shifter heroes travel these worlds in their self built ships (think more akin to the Millenium Falcon or Serenity than giant star cruisers), wielding laser swords, psychic blasters, and lens crystals.

      Anyway, that would be my basic pitch.

      *(The Umbral Flyers were the main rivals of the Wise Guys for tribal leadership in the 30s and 40s, essentially aviators, science heroes, and pulp adventures/explorers. By the 70s, they'd largely faded into the sunset, save a handful of Umbral explorers. Dr. Stratus, part of Vic Stryker's group in the revised Silent Striders book and Past Lives, was one of their leading lights.)

                                          Most Vampires avoid travel, of course, but they do live in well-funded colonies all across the system. Some social experiments towards their fabled Blood Utopia, secret dark retreats where undead cabals gather information mundane and arcane for whatever plans they have, hideouts for the spiders in the conspiracy webs within other communities, either in allegiance to local powers or against them.

                                          But with space-worthy tech it isn't hard to overcome Kindred limitations. Blocking light is an easier demand on a spacesuit than pressure control, yet both are a basic requirement anyway. Some Kindred vessels are luxurious fortresses purposefully badly illuminated, manned by Ghouls, Revenants and Conditioned mortals, others are as minimal as being just an escape module reconditioned for longer travel yet no life support whatsoever. Even the big ones are a dire prospect for invaders, as vampires can sacrifice any life-saving system and fill it with dangerous traps, making the entire ship a literal tomb with no risk to themselves.

                                              The Mythos Space setting uses the "classic" storyteller system, focusing on human and human+ level characters. Adding supernaturals from the WOD is easy as can be, and could fit quite well in the setting without much adjustment.

                                              You can find character creation rules, a basic rules booklet and a character sheet on my patreon, free of charge. Though I am greatly encouraged by patronage.

                                              This is the basic introduction.


                                              MYTHOS SPACE


                                              “The explorers and the drifters and the spacehands are misfits mostly, and, therefore, men of imagination. The contrast between the rigid functionalism inside a spaceship and the immeasurable glories outside is too great not to have a name. So whenever you stand in a ship’s control room and look out into the bottomless dark where the blinding planets turn and the stars swim motionless in space, you are taking a walk down Paradise Street.” ― C.L. Moore, Judgment Night
                                              A few years ago, I was tinkering with the Mythos Space concept as a kind of love letter to some of my favorite types of fiction, pulpy sci-fi, and cosmic horror. So while I am busy with other projects, I will also be posting about this setting, using the "Classic" World of Darkness game system, both due to its time tested nature and because it is fairly open source as you can find the rules info all over the internet. But to make things easier, I have attached a pdf of the basic rules, character creation rules and a character sheet below, and will be adding features as these articles progress. In the days to come, I will be discussing the setting, how it meshes with the Storyteller System, and eventually assemble a large pdf book that will be available to be downloaded. Members of this patreon will have access to a deluxe version, with artwork, fiction and other features.

                                              THE BASICS
                                              The setting of Mythos Space is a pulp science fantasy campaign inspired by comics such as Flash Gordon and Buck Rogers, and the Cosmic Horror stories of the Cthulhu Mythos*, and sci-fi movies and television shows from the 1950s and ‘60s such as Forbidden Planet, Outer Limits, Twilight Zone, and Captain Video. The focus is on fun, horror and excitement rather than scientific accuracy. Crazy retro gadgets, weird creatures, sexy alien princesses (or princes), and tentacled aliens are all par for the course.
                                              Space travel, ray guns and alien life is common knowledge and considered "normal" as far as Joe Average is concerned. But so is the existence of mind-shattering alien horrors, Deep Ones, Snake-People and all manner of weirdness. But most people try very hard to get on with their lives despite this.
                                              *This includes material from H.P. Lovecraft, Clark Ashton Smith, Robert E. Howard, C.S. Moore, Robert Bloch, Frank Belknap Long, Henry Kuttner, and Fritz Leiber.

                                              SOME SETTING TIDBITS
                                              Overall, the setting of Mythos Space has a "retro future" where retro, vintage or antiquated technology, styles and aesthetics are still used, but which otherwise is or at least resembles the present day. This is largely due to the mix of tech levels available across settled space, and as a direct result of conflict with hyperspace entities (who can really screw with certain technologies).
                                              Old Earth (Terra): Earth is a ruin, and home to only small communities in the 51st century. Mankind has spread across the stars, leaving their homeworld behind and hoping to make a better future.
                                              Space Patrol (Space Cops): Space Patrol is a private security company that provides armed peacekeeping, investigative and security services to private and public clients in outer space. This organization originally got its start as a police force created by a collaboration between the United Americans and the Afro-European Union, but has since become a private corporation that acts as a police force in space. In the 51st century, the Space Patrol is central to space travel.
                                              The Colonies (Separatists): Colony worlds can be found across space, settled by corporations, private individuals and even hard-as-nails homesteaders looking for a new start. Most of the larger and more developed worlds were founded during the mass colonial efforts of Earth governments of the 23rd century. During this exodus from Terra, Spacecraft were still only capable of sub-light speeds at the time. Voyages beyond Earth’s solar system took generations and the colonies that were established were effectively isolated from each other and from Terra. This forced the individual colonized star systems to be completely self-sufficient in all their vital requirements (food, medicine, technology, etc.) and allowed them to develop distinctive languages and cultures.
                                              The Hyades Star Cluster (Campaign): This is to be the main focus for my games. Mention of this region appears in the assorted Mythos, as well as UFO lore. It is a haunted region of space, with ancient ruins and some creepy locals. I will be centering on a colonized system that is close enough to Earth standards to be a good starting point.
