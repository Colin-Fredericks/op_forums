                                            RE: Yin and Yang and the Triat

                                            Yin is stillness, darkness, potential, cold, and a number of other things. It is nourishment and support. It's intuition and negotiation, ultimately indirect. Yang is direct and active. A candle is fire as yin. A wildfire is fire as yang. Also, they represent two different sexes. In Werewolf parlance, both would be Wyld, or perhaps both potential sides of each member of the Triat. After all, both have the capacity to be active and passive; the Wyrm has monsters for the direct approach and corruption for the indirect, after all. This distinction is present in the Kumo, incidentally, though it also has some... tangles to it (sunglasses, Meet the New Boss clip) since the ones that are killcrazy appear to be Yin-aspected, but their attunements are a matter of appetite, rather than approach. Marawa's Brood are destructive and cruel and prey upon the passive; the Blessed of Rati are big on talk and seduction and prey upon the active. The Chosen of Nareau have their pick of approach and victim. This is taking on the opposite to defeat the main.

                                            Incidentally, the other ramifications of the associations in DotE are that men are of the Wyld and women are of the Wyrm which would be a new dimensions of "holy shit, can you stop, take a breath, and listen to yourself?" even if you ignored the above issues. On a slightly less controversial note, it would also insinuate that water and metal are Wyrmish and that wood and fire are Wyldish; problematic, but in a somewhat absurd way, rather than one with super unfortunate implications.

                                                    Yeah - the Ministers map easily to the Metaphysical Trinity. Despite some superficial similarities, the Trinity doesn't at all map to the Triat. In particular, Pattern contains elements of beneficial stability, continuity, and parental nurturing which are completely absent from the Weaver. Dragon-Minister contains elements of law and consequence - the Wyrm (as corrupter or destroyer) only acknowledges the absolute natural law of eventual death. Mapping Yang onto the Phoenix Minister doesn't make a whole ton of sense, either. There are similarities, but I have yet to see a means of reconciling Werewolf's ecological animist cosmology with Mage that doesn't leave one of the two very much the junior partner.

                                                    Imagine a venn diagram in which the traits of the Wyrm, the Ebon Dragon, and the Dragon Minister share some overlap, but aren't the same circles. Dragon-Minister is performing a very different task in Mage's thematic cosmology. There are hints of intersection and cross-over, but those are suggestive or iconic, ways that each game dips into a shared well of common symbols and ideas, not a consistent singular cosmology that can be documented in a straightforward fashion. Trying to make sense of the game lines shared symbol set as if the same symbols express the same things in each game line is always going to be doomed to frustration. They express similar things, as suited for the themes and intents of each game line.

                                                    "Dragonlike thing what is involved in destruction" is basically the WoD equivalent of a cape in superhero comics; we understand that not everyone wearing a cape is the same character but understand that the cape is part of an artistic decision to communicate some basic ideas about what sort of character we are seeing.
