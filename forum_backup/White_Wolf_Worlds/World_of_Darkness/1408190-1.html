                            *If everything it's magick and werenches are Wonders, why would you need Hypertech to make Wonders?, just use regular tech if it's all the same.

                            *Where does magick end and regular stuff start? - Mage offers no solid answer. And really: do you really want to start asking yourself these questions if something like the much simpler RAW it's already confusing.

                                        There's also an issue in that the Quintessence availability in a given Mage game is a dial left to the ST to set based on the tone of the game. If dialed low, creating a Quintessence infrastructure for such an effort would be vastly more costly than the production would justify. If dialed to the max, it would be much easier to pull off so that the Technocracy has lots of non-mages armed to the teeth with medium level magical gear.

                                                The RAW answer is pretty simple, as you have two choices:

                                                1) A Node (or whatever high Prime stuff you use) is modified to create energy-tass that powers Hypertech stuff's Quintessence. Basically, you can't draw more out of energy than you put in at some other point in the line, and electricity is just delivery mechanism.

                                                2) You have to actually destroy the Pattern you're taking Quintessence from. If you draw Quintessence straight out of electricity, you're destroying the electricity so it can't do anything else. You can't do this in parts either (just like sacrificing Life Patterns, you've got to actually sacrifice them). If you want to destroy all the electricity in your building's main circuit for a few dots of Quintessence, causing a fairly obvious localized blackout, you can do that... but there's a reason why most mages don't really like this option.
