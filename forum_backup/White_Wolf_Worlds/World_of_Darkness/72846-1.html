                        Well... where to start.

                        In my end of the world campaign in the early 90's the characters had already aligned, according to a prophesy, with the Wyrm being comprised of a newly awakened Mage a kin to BSD's, a Lost Cub and a Fomor, all started as normal humans, with a single human left standing they had to participate to a holy communion to show their devotion to the Holy Divinity of the Wyrm which was held in an old Church where the Priest sacrifised a human baby to make the communion "wine" and "wafer". Earlier in the same game the Mage had already sacrificed one player to summon a Maeljin Incarna to aid them so just one more step on the path of Destruction.

                        One of my favorite Changeling NPC's is a Bogie named Marrow whose dining habits concerning humans should be obvious.

                        In a Sabbat game I had an Toreador Antitribu character who created his art of pain from human victims. His great masterwork being four pillars formed of preserved and sown together corpses made in the honor of the new Archbishop. Since he is a Toreador he lacks Vicissitude and so is limited to more mundane methods of creating his art through careful cuts, stitches and placement of human organs and parts.

                        Also my otherwise quite cheerful Devil Tiger, originally a Dhampyr, once carefully peeled the skin of his victim while salting his flesh and yes the victim in that occurrence was a supernatural being that to his ill fortune couldn't escape the pain through dying.

                        These are just from the top of my head and I'm sure are actually pretty far from the Darkest/grimiest thing I would have done or ST'd.

                        Oh in my last VtM game a Tremere researcher NPC managed to alchemically turn all the vitae within a test subject vampire into a solid material that one of the PC's had the displeasure of cutting out of the test subject collecting it for the next part of the experiment. Naturally the test subject did not survive and the whole experience was rather harrowing to the poor PC trying to cling to his humanity amidst all this.

                                    In our long running Dark Ages chronicle there have been various stretches of darker or grimier sessions which have included accidentally igniting a race war in Cordova, accidentally releasing a demon (also Cordova), watching Baghdad burn under mongol assault, being unable to stop the black death, one member of our party unleashing several earthquakes on Jerusalem by accident and dint of scientific method. However, the most recent arc has probably taken the cake for the small group of sessions which we've referred as 'The Trough'.

                                    It was Florence in 1387 with the first embers of the Anarch revolt stirring in the city beneath the guise of the Ciompi uprising. The Setite member of our party had been tasked with retrieving relics of the faith within the city and following the trial of a suspected heretic/infernalist, her fellow Setites within the city have been labelled the most incompetent vampires we've met to date. They'd both been effectively abandoned and discarded by their sire and therefore had not been trained properly in the ways of Set or in being Cainites. These individuals were known as Cavolo di Pavia and Tomassino, who managed somehow to fail at running running a brothel due to Cavolo's love of spending all their money and Tomassino... just... just being himself. To emphasise exactly how terrible this den of inequity known as the 'Golden Orchid' was: they sold watered down vinegar instead of ale, provided and smoked hemp rope, employed a single elderly prostitute with no teeth who specialised in gumming, their altar was a poorly painted golden snake with a smily face, their priestess was aforementioned prostitute, their grasp of Set was mostly focused around hemp and sex. They each possessed minimal discipline knowledge, one was unaware he even possessed clan disciplines, neither knew what serpentis was, one didn't even know he could ghoul people.

                                    Cavolo was the oilyiest, slimeyiest, most incompetent bastard in the city (or so we thought) who it turned out had been rapidly embraced to be used as a footsoldier in Naples against the invading Tzimisce, he'd then chosen to abandon the Setite ways in favour of walking the road of Sin. Tomassino had been a strippling stable-boy (about 11-13) who had been embraced by his domitor out of pity ('a pity fuck embrace' our ST said) who then was abandoned due to being no-longer interesting, he was bullied by pretty much every vampire going. So pitiable was Tomassino that we discovered he'd attempted to take our Setites lessons to heart on a small gang of young noble-men who had been victimising and gang-raping him over the last few months, he'd accidentally convinced them to do the same to a large number of other young-men of lower birth in the city because they wouldn't be caught (an actual historical occurance). When our Setite heard this she decided to enact vengance and used Tomassino to lure the perpetrators out one by one, pin them down and make Tomassino take his revenge upon them in kind.
                                    Then we investigated Cavolo to discover that he was in the possession of the sorcerous books we searched for... but it seemed was also an infernalist despite having no knowledge of such, when he attempted to jump and stake our Setite my Tzimisce rendered him into torpor with a single kick to the testicles. We proceeded then to track down his haven (beneath another shitty tavern) which was guarded by his ghoul and discovered his tomes, where it turned out that a unknown benefactor had been handing him books which contained infernalist marginalia which summoned particular spirits / efreet to bargain with and would cause the individual to end up selling their soul to apep. We discovered also that Cavolo had been tainting some of the hempen rope with his small supply of opium, we also discovered the individual who was referred to as 'Pet'.
                                    'Pet' had at one point six or seven years beforehand been a young noblewoman from the Frescobaldi family who had been slumming it amongst the seedier parts of the city, where Cavolo had attempted to seduce her into his twisted ways of Set (and the path of Sin). When she attempted to return home Cavolo had panicked and captured her, knowing that the vampire patriarch of the family would not take kindly to him poaching. He decided to ghoul her then and have a Cappadocian Sinner surgically remove her eyes, ears, nose, teeth alongside her arms and legs above the knees/elbows. She'd then been placed in a cage where she was fed blood and little food, subjected to repeated presence and used as a sexual slave while living in her own filth. His other ghoul routinely fed her his defications and glass when Cavolo wasn't paying attention. Needless to say she was put out of her misery, the other ghoul tortured to death by my character in a fit of rage, and the Prince of the city gave Cavolo a similar horrendous torturing before Cavolo was handed to our setite for merciful execution.

                                    There was a similarly unplesant and pig-headed individual known as Warden Du'Bois in a local town, who alongside his men was a serial rapist that almost ended up being fleshcrafted into a woman, embraced and handed over to the Fae (long story involving accidental murder of the Winter King's daughter) before our resident Brujah frenzied over the wardens foul-mouthed insulting despite the chances of redemption or death. The exact turn of phrase I believe was that the Brujah would be made the 'donky bitch', extrapolate from there, the warden luckily died horribly under the hands of the frenzying brujah.

                                    There was then the incident in which the malkavian elder known as Juliana Di Patsi after damning Cavolo by handing him tainted artefacts (for mocking her choice of hat and embarassing her two decades beforehand) and banishing the setites form the city with her favour from the prince, was murdered by our Setite during the revolution. She was forced to flee from assault by the elders twelve ghouled children bodyguards who proceeded before our eyes to tear and entire family apart in their own home. My Tzimisce and the Tremere bonded over the shared experience of trapping the children in that home and burning it down around them, as well as beating the escaped burning child to death with sticks while screaming in horror. This extended scene was made helarious/horrific by our STs extended mimicery of the childrens screams and shouts as they murdered/were burned to death.
                                    Florence has as such left something of a mark on our veteran PCs.
