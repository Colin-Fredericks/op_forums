    Data Base

    Purview: Data covers connections, with the implication that it also covers space as a byproduct.

    Special Rules and Limitations


    1 – Data largely functions like Correspondence but with different ranks of connection. So unless Data states that it does not have a power, or puts an effect at a different rank than Correspondence, it can be assumed to have the same broad capabilities as Correspondence.

    2 – To target something with Data it needs to be “on the grid”

    3 – Base Data does not say that when extending spell range a specialist needs to have a rank in Data as high as the highest Sphere being used. However, Data 2 does say this and Data 2 is the rank for extending range so the rule still exists.

    4 – Data does not say that it can move patterns without additional spheres the way Correspondence does. This can be inferred from rule 1, but strictly speaking it is not stated.

        Data 1

        • On the Ones and Zeros

        Through a haze of code, the Data initiate begins to see the interconnectedness of all things. Bringing that esoteric level of comprehension to the world beyond his keyboard, the specialist can determine the exact distance between points or the connections between visible objects. His understanding allows him to work on base-level projects without instruments, thanks to a growing familiarity with relationship information.
        Data 1 explicitly lets a mage:

        Know Distance Between Objects (Imm. Spatial Perceptions)

        Unlike with Correspondence, Data specifically limits this power to visible objects. It does not, as written, apply to other senses. Whether that omission is intentional or not, I don’t know, but spells default to sensory distance, so without specifically denying Data the use of other senses, I have to assume it can.


        Detect Connection (???)

        Unlike Correspondence, Data is not missing this power. A Data initiate may determine the connections between visible objects. The word “objects” may mean this power is limited to Matter patterns.


        Base Level Projects (???)

        This is my unfamiliarity with the Data showing. I suspect this simply means that for most effects at this level the specialist doesn’t need special instruments on site the way he does for later ranks. The later ranks are very clear about needing equipment on both ends of the effect.


        While the following powers are not given to Data, they may still be in its purview as it is supposed to function like Correspondence in regards to powers.

        Sense Things in Vicinity (Imm. Spatial Perceptions)

        Sense Hidden Objects (Imm. Spatial Perceptions)

        It is strange that the first of these two is not specifically called out as an ability of Data. Mapping out the surrounding area seems right up its alley.

        Sensing hidden objects may actually be an intentional omission, given that Data functions by already having information on targets. That said, the base rules of Data mean this should be possible too.


        Know Directions (Imm. Spatial Perceptions)

        While unstated, it would be almost laughable if Data couldn’t be used to pull up directional data. In fact, it could reasonably be an application of Detect Connection.


        Detect Spatial Anomalies (Imm. Spatial Perceptions)

        This is a very interesting one. Because Data has the abilities of Correspondence, it can do this. On the other hand, it makes sense it shouldn’t do this as well as Correspondence, and that Data and Correspondence should have increased difficulties in detecting each other, so it is plausible this is a deliberate omission.

        Even if it is, though, that doesn’t override Base Data.

                Data 2

                •• The Reach and the View
                With a few quick calculations, the specialist can assess theoretical and practical space – assessments that allow him to perceive what’s happening elsewhere. Expanding the metaphysical elements of this discipline, this understanding allows the agent to see and touch places in other areas of the world, so long as he has instruments that allow him access to distant locations.

                Combining Data with other Spheres, the specialist can extend the practical range of Technocratic Effects. Matter allows him to modify Union tech from a distance; Mind lets him communicate with agents or targets worldwide; Forces lets him attack distant targets; and Entropy allows him to predict and alter probability patterns and spot flaws in objects that he can perceive. As with regular Correspondence, the operator’s Data Rank must be equal to, or higher than, the highest Rank in a connected Effect..
                Another thing Data doesn’t suffer from: Unlike Correspondence, its ability to extend the range of actions, senses, and spells is not left to other sections.


                Extend Senses (Sense Through Space)

                Exactly what it says on the tin. Definitely not called scrying. Report to Room 101 if you called it that. As with Data 1, only vision is called out though the power wouldn’t make sense if at least hearing wasn’t also possible.


                Extend Range (Reach Through Space)

                Data separates out extending the range of actions and extending the range of spells, but they are both in the writeup. It is worth noting that a specialist has to have instruments that allow access to the locations.

                We could interpret this section to mean that a specialist already has to have equipment in the area that can do what he wants to do, and if he doesn’t he needs a remote Matter spell to modify any nearby Union equipment to set that up. I bring that up as a possible fun game element.


                Missing from Data but, as written, still possible:

                Block Scrying

                A mage can use Thicken space data manipulation to keep other people from using Correspondence or Data to spy on him. He can also use this to cover his tracks when spying on others. These seem like the same thing but the latter implies a mage can do a bit more.


                Pull Things Through Tiny Holes (Reach Through Space)

                Pull small items through space. This extends the ability of touching distant things to grabbing them. As written there is no reason Data can’t do this, though likely the equipment needed for it is more specialized.


                Minor Ward? (Thicken Space) from Common Magical Effects

                Data 2 never mentions thickening space, though since that was the basis behind blocking spying we can at least infer that it can do so in some applications. If it can use that for minor wards is less certain. One of the specific changes Data makes is moving Wards from Correspondence 4 to Data 3. Whether this also includes moving minor wards from Correspondence 2 to Data 3 is uncertain, as Correspondence 2 never specifically mentioned them so they would reasonably be omitted from Data 2 either way.

                    Data 3

                    ••• Quantum Teleportation / Firewalling / Surveillance Hub
                    Advanced Data transfer techniques allow the specialist to more or less sidestep conventional physics of spatial dimensions. In practical terms, this allows him to download himself into quantum teleportation mode; erect firewalls of particle physics that effectively ward objects, places, or people; and divide his perceptions across a multilocational surveillance hub. Combined with other Spheres, this expertise helps the agent move things, forces, and living beings through intervening space.

                    Again, however, these Procedures demand the appropriate gear on either side of the Effect. Such violations of conventional physics demand the proper technology.
                    Data 3 gives:

                    Quantum Teleportation

                    Allows a specialist to teleport himself (or others) along with any small items. Much like Correspondence 3, the text applies to the specialist himself, but later text implies other people or objects are possible


                    Manipulate Position (???)

                    Also like Correspondence 2, there is a section about moving objects through space and pattern spheres are involved. Data doesn’t say the key line about moving things without touching them, but it does specifically call out moving them through intervening space while teleportation usually bypasses that space, so if anything Data is more direct about what is happening here.
                    Data likewise doesn’t make the distinction that Pattern Spheres aren’t needed to manipulate full patterns but without a word to the contrary it is assumed. The extra spheres listed here, then, are most likely locking spheres.


                    Co-locality Perceptions (Co-locality Perceptions)

                    Perceive multiple places at once. It is not mentioned in this section, but handling multiple points of view at once is difficult without the addition of Mind.


                    Now we come to a big break from Correspondence.

                    Ward (Ward)

                    Data puts Wards… ahem, firewalls of particle physics… in Data 3. Whether this covers both Minor and Greater Wards or just Greater Wards is uncertain. This is explicitly different from Correspondence, one of the very few places that is.

                    These next two were missing powers from Correspondence 3. That is, they were implied by the bolded abilities but never described in the text. Data doesn't do either so it is unclear if it has them. I still default to yes, the case is just harder.

                    Collapse Gates (Close Gates)

                    Open or Close Existing Gate (Open and Close Gates)

                            Data 4


                            •••• Between Space/Dimensional Cohabitation/Multilocation Access
                            With such technology in place, the expert specialist can open quantum gateways between locations, create pocket dimensions between conventional space-time continuums, and disperse holographic manifestations of himself into several concurrent locations. With the appropriate Life, Mind, and Primal Force Procedures, those manifestations can even become as solid as the agent himself and perform different tasks in the finest Agent Smith tradition.
                            Lasting Quantum Gates (Rend Space)

                            At Data 4 specialists can now create gates with duration which can be used by more than one person and can accommodate larger things


                            Pocket Dimension (Rend Space or Ward)

                            A specialist can create pocket dimensions, which presumably does the same thing as in Correspondence: isolate things in spaces removed from the rest of reality.


                            Co-Locate Self (Co-locate Self)

                            A specialist can project holographic manifestations multiple places at once, which is quite useful. Well it’s actually of limited use on its own since he is still limited to the same mind and can only make the same motion in each location. Actually, for Data this works differently. Life is needed to make these manifestations solid, not to have them act differently from each other. Since Primal Force is listed as well, this version apparently creates new bodies.

                            I would think just using Forces would be easier in context.


                            Not directly stated:

                            Teleport Anything (Rend Space) (from Corr 3)

                            Like Correspondence 4, Data 4 should remove all the limitations from Data 3’s quantum teleportation.

                                        Data 5


                                        ••••• Redistribute Physical PropertiesFold Space/ Hyperdispersed Perceptions
                                        Data Mastery merges conventional physics with sophisticated hypermath. The few specialists at this level can stack physical locations into the same space, alter the spatial dimensions and properties of a target, or disperse their perceptions into so many concurrent locations that a specialist may truthfully be said to have eyes and ears everywhere.

                                        Data specifically gets:

                                        Superimpose Places (Co-Location)

                                        A specialist can stack physical locations into the same space. This is likely as vulgar as the Correspondence version, hypermath or no hypermath.


                                        Distort Spatial Dimensions and Properties (Spatial Mutation)

                                        A quick line that pretty much covers much of the spatial warping of Correspondence 5. This can reasonably apply to warping space and directions, altering size, stretching or compressing things, and connecting patterns that are not touching.


                                        Super-Mega-Hyper-Co-Locate Senses (Co-Location)

                                        Like Correspondence, Data 5 acts as if there is some sort of upgraded Co-locate Senses at this rank. This time the wording depicts the upgrade as a dramatic increase in number, which is strange, but also a dramatic increase in coverage, which makes more sense. Essentially it appears that Data 5 means the operative can have blanket coverage of an area rather than a discrete number of sense locations. It doesn’t limit Data 3, per se, but it does mean Data 5 is carrying out a fundamentally different effect.


                                        Data doesn’t mention these, but it should be able to do what Correspondence can here.

                                        Co-Locate Anything (Co-Location)

                                        In a sense this is the inversion of superimposing different things. Data should also be able to co-locate anything to multiple places.


                                        Everything Else (???)

                                        As with all Rank 5s, Data 5 should be able to do just about everything possible within the Sphere’s purview. The existence of Arch Multidimensional Hyperspheres could change this, but they aren’t listed in M20.

                                                Dimensional Science 1

                                                • EDE Scan/ Evaluate Gauntlet/Map Dimensional Region

                                                Through applied principles and technologies, the specialist learns to perceive EDE presence and potential incursions, evaluate the characteristics of alternate dimensions and thickness of the barrier that keeps them outside the Consensus, and perceive the essential terrain in the pocket dimensions on the other side of that Gauntlet.
                                                Dimensional Science 1 can explicitly:

                                                Sense Barrier and past Barrier (Evaluate Gauntlet and Map Dimensional Region)

                                                A specialist can both sense the Gauntlet and sense across the Gauntlet. Sensing the Gauntlet gives it’s local thickness and likely any meddling done with it.


                                                See EDE (EDE Scan)

                                                Much like Spirit’s Spirit Sight, a specialist can see spirits of all types and states.


                                                DimSci does not mention, but presumably can:

                                                See Auras (Spirit Sense)

                                                A specialist can also see the aura of people and other creatures, with all of the information that aurasight brings.


                                                Sense ED Component (Spirit Sense)

                                                With this power a mage can determine if an object has contamination from across the dimensional barrier.
