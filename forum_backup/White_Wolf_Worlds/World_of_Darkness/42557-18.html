    Ok, explain The game Persona 3 in WoD terms

    I'm still playing through it, but I'll try to do the basics, at least.

    Mage is once again the default explanation - when the characters are fighting monsters, they do so in an Umbral realm where Paradox from their spells is minimized, and they all have manifested Avatars that they summon through their Focus (they're all also part of their own Craft/Tradition, though which one is up in the air still - a Spirit-focused Son of Ether or Virtual Adept works, but they could also be Technocrats). Each one focuses on different spheres according to their personal element (and their Persona's/Avatar's Essence and Resonance).

    However, it's not the only explanation. Another possibility is that they are Eastern Changelings, possessed as children with the Elemental variant, and their Personas are the manifestation of their Changeling Soul. Alternatively, they could also be Demons, but (at least with the main character) whose demon selves have amnesia about what they truly are; they see their Demonic forms and Lores as "summoning their Persona." Finally, they could be Gnosis-bearing Kinfolk who all have personal totems, or Bastet with Jamak who simply don't shift into battle forms (possibly because they don't know how yet).

            Wow, it's funny when you look back at much older posts that you make in this thread. Since I have a really hard time fitting Hunter with Gravity Falls now since the show finally concluded.
            I'll stick to the list of characters that fall under the Zodiac so far then:

            Fiddleford McGucket: Dauntain, former Seelie Nocker.
            This one is so obvious once you get to the Society of the Blind Eye episode. Not only is McGucket excellent at building absurd machines a Nocker would love, but...
            McGucket saw something that made him unable to cope with a fae-like reality and builds a device to help him forget it. Then he tries to help other people to forget what they saw. He, however, eventually slowly gets over his Dauntain status with the help from Dipper and Mabel.

            Stanford Pines: Son of Ether Mage who was once very close to becoming a Marauder.
            Ford is certainly the sort of "SCIENCE!" or mad scientist type of character, which is why I went with the Son of Ether choice. But he was also close to being a Order of Hermes Mage as well.
            His brush with Marauder-ism was due to Bill getting him into such a paranoid state. Ford was lost in the Umbra and couldn't get back for some time. And Ford's Journals are certainly strong Mage Wonders. Stanford also "Awakened" first, so he cannot become a Garou like his brother, Stanley, did.

            Stan Pines: Ronin Garou, Homid, ex-Silent Strider. Also had the Rite of Renunciation performed on him. Stan has also dealt with Harano at certain times in his life.
            Soos: Mortal with Numina powers.Soos can often eerily predict things before they happen at times.
            Wendy: Fianna Ahroun Homid Lost Cub or Fianna Kinfolk.Wendy hasn't experienced the change just yet.
            Robbie V.: Risen... just kidding! That was a reference to a popular theory that went around in the early days of the show's fandom, though. Mortal with certain Numina powers dealing with the dead. Robbie probably has familiarity with wraiths, though, considering the work his parents do. Maybe, there's a chance he will become a Euthanatoi or Hollow One Mage?
            Pacfica: I wonder if her family has any connection to a Mummy with the karchakka flaw... 'get it'? Otherwise, Pacifica is just a normal very rich mortal with strong connections/influence.
            Gideon: Very young Sorcerer or Hedge Wizard.
            Dipper Pines: Because of the connection to two characters (See spoilers in this post if you have watched the show til the end), Dipper has the chance of either "awakening" or going through the first change. If he "awakens" first: Order of Hermes Mage, pretty much. If he experiences the "Change" first: Silent Strider Galliard. There's also the chance he won't become either as well.
            Mabel Pines: Because of the connection to two characters (See spoilers in this post if you have watched the show til the end), Mabel has the chance of either "awakening" or going through the first change. If she "awakens" first: Cult of Ecstasy Mage. If she experiences the "Change" first: Silent Strider Galliard. There's also the chance she won't become either as well.

            Bill: A really nasty aspect of the Wyld or a very strong Wyld Incarna that isn't your friend... and will resort to underhanded tactics to get what he wants. Bill's goal is to unleash the Wyld's power unto the whole world to have his 'fun' and to break the barrier between reality and the Umbra.
