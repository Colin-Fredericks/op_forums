                Originally posted by Dataweaver View Post
                Depends on how it's written. If M5 continues in the spirit of M20 (but without, you know, being a 700-page brick), I may leap at it.

                In any case, books that I'm hoping get pitched and approved for M20:

                • a Disparates guidebook, akin to Technocracy Reloaded
                • a Traditions guidebook, also akin to Technocracy Reloaded
                • Book of the Quiet, akin to Book of the Fallen but for Marauders. (“Quiet” rather than “Madness” for two reasons: first, it wouldn't be about mental illness, per se, but the Awakened phenomenon of Quiet; second, Quiet comes in multiple flavors, of which Madness is but one. I'd like to see an expanded take on the Denial-based Marauders known as Negation Men, and maybe the introduction of some Morbidity-based Marauders with some clarification of just how they differ from Nephandi.)

                I'd ask for a Digital Web 3.0, but we're already getting that in the TR companion. A new Book of Worlds would be great, yes.

                Another one I'd like to see would be a combination of and expansion on Initiates/Masters of the Art: M20's take on the “Path of Thorns” from Awakening to Ascension. If I were writing it, I'd include two optional systems: one for pre-Awakened Magick (distinct from Hedge Magic and Sorcery), and one for Archmastery of the Spheres. The goal for both would be to take the Sphere Magick system and extend it “down” and “up”, respectively — the former formalizing the Hedge Magician entry from Gods & Monsters as more than just a patch job to cover the lack of an M20 Sorcerer book*, and the latter taking cues from the Awakening supplement Imperial Mysteries with a focus on high-end Magick as a story driver rather than an exercise in rolling dice. In between these two things, I'd put a more in-depth look at the Avatar, Arete, and Seekings, and maybe something about Mentors and Grimoires: teaching and learning of Magick, and by Magick.

                * Charles Siegal (writer of Enlightened Grimoire, Mystic Armory, and Sources of Magick, has been working on a Sorcerer 20 book for the Storyteller's Vault; I'm hoping he takes this opportunity to turn it into a pitch for an official M20 supplement. But even if that gets submitted and approved, I'd still like some sort of “Acolyte Magick” optional extension for Sphere Magick.

                But in addition to those major projects, I'd also like to see a series of smaller projects: “Focus Books”, akin to the earlier editions' Tradition and Convention Books, but with each one dealing with a particular Practice (or set of closely related Practices) independent of the factions in the Ascension War. You would of course have to get into things like associated Paradigms, Instruments, and Abilities, common Spheres, and sample Effects and Wonders. There's already a supplement to this effect in STV: Paradigms Explored: Wyrd and Weft. I'd like to see more like that.

                And speaking of Storyteller's Vault: there are a number of supplements there that I'd love to see “canonized”: have Onyx Path offer to buy the supplement from its author; and if the author accepts, run it through a professional editing session and republish it as an official Mage supplement. Not only would these supplements get just a touch more polish, but they'd also get a lot more exposure: one problem that I have with Storyteller's Vault is that the fan-created stuff often gets lost in the shuffle.

                Unfortunately most of the books you've mentioned have already been pitched to Paradox and rejected, Between Charles and Phil I'm getting top quality M20 books through SV anyway, so I have no complaints. While it would be good to see SV products canonised Paradox doesn't seem to be able to restrain itself from futzing with to the detriment of every single book OPP sends to them for approval.....so I'm sceptical it would come out for the better.
