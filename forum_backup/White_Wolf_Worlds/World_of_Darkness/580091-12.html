                    02-02-2017, 02:00 PM
                    Originally posted by Dreaminggod View Post
                    So, how do you think Kuei-Jin and Demons interact?
                    That depends on how much credence you're going to give either side. From the perspective of KotE, anything declaring itself to be a demon and running about trying to absorb I Can't Believe It's Not Yugen from mortals is probably going to disturb the local Hungry Dead and get the Black Iron Talons on the tail of the Fallen, because demon hunting is their business, and business is good.

                    Do KJs get Golden Chi from demons?
                    Probably not.

                    What happens if a Wan Kuei becomes a Thrall?
                    Is that even possible? They're spiritually closer to the state of being ghosts than any non-ghost.

                    Can the Fallen go to Yomi and twist it into their own domain?
                    That's a very complicated question, because while KotE contains a living Umbra, Demon only has the Underworld, where all of the dead are. Other domains were never described as traversible by the Fallen, so it's possible that a demon wouldn't be able to go there at all, or else only ends up in Yin-aspected hells. Even if they do get back to the brighter and more vibrant ones, though, they would have to contend with the ruler of the realm of any given hell, which means that they'll probably lose and end up kicked out if they're lucky. Yama Kings and Queens can easily get into Incarna territory, which means that their character sheet is "Caine loses".
