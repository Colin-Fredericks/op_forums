      In my games, the Fallen were never angels. They are spirits of primordial origin locked away in the Abyss.           Here are some things I do when I run Demon:

          •I refer to their houses either by the houses’s title (I.E. The Devils) or the house’s order (IE the first house)

          •I open up Lores to be more like something between Changeling Arts and Mage Spheres: using the lores that Demon has as their ‘spheres’ with a sphere mastery scale akin to the sphere mastery scale that Mages use as well as a bunk-style system to effect the invocation of lores

          •Demons in their apocalyptic forms can soak agg and can use faith to heal it at a 3:1 faith:agg lvl rate in other forms

          •Mystery as to the events of the before, with conflicting accounts inspired by predominant religious cultures in surrounding areas

          •Demons can feed on Faith in a similar fashion to how Changelings do with Glamour

              I used hybrid rules from Devils Due & D:TF.
              This makes their powers more flexible. It also allows them to use their own Torment, which is almost never used in DTF. [Also it will allow them to heal all bash damage for 1 faith point and etc.]
              Let the angels do their angelic deeds. Perhaps he remembers Word that breathed life... Let him create an unholy parody that will raise the undead.
              Try to understand the spirit of the demonic Lores. It's strange that a demon can only do one thing per level, with divine knowledge of the world.


              Apocalyptic Form: Don't limit yourself to 8 abilities. Make them unique, but keep them part of House. (Otherwise it will be Earthbound) You can be inspired by the demonic forms of Kuei-jin.
              Gryffon15 has already proposed "agg soak" for apocalyptic form and idea of transforming Lore into something like Changeling cantrips.

                                              I tried splitting out the demon types so that rather than one house having three lores to choose from, you'd have three types of demon per house with one lore each. All supernatural powers were tied to the character's Faith rating; the first dot gave all of the basic powers, the second gave the first lore power, the third gave an apocalyptic form trait, and then it alternated between lore powers and form traits.

                                              All powers could be activated by spending a single point of faith, to avoid having to make the host coincidentally well-specialised to be able to use lore powers effectively. The faith expenditure route was only one automatic success, so you could still train up your skills to make more effective use of lore powers, it's just that I didn't want to force certain ability choices at character creation.

                                              Finally, faith rating and permanent torment were the same track, so eventually you'd reach a cap on faith that could be only overcome by reducing torment. Also eventually gaining torment meant actively losing your powers. My reasoning was that faith is the connection to the divine needed to access the former angel's powers, while torment represented the demon's hatred of the divine for their imprisonment.

                                              We didn't have any loyalist angels show up, but if they had I'd have used the same approach; giving them one lore path each and probably faith 10 to represent their devotion.

                                              All together it made for characters whose powers were much more specialised and who gained supernatural power more slowly, and it brought the conflicted relationship with the divine to the foreground.

                                                          So I’ve never written it down but my logic is something like this.

                                                          The demons took part in the construction of reality and the idea that they are still attuned to elements of it through their lores make sense. I imagine their lores being something like arcana, lenses through which reality are perceived and defined. So I imagine something less broad then Mage Spheres and more focused then Changeling Arts with the addition of a bunk system and maybe a variable modifier based on the ambient faith in the area, a similar model based on stuff like WtA Umbra density & WtO Veil density, or perhaps have that sort of 1-10 chart determine difficulty for invocation rolls.

                                                          Basically what I imagine is a system based on Lores where demons are able to use their Lores following a similar 1-5 scale as the spheres have: perception, manipulation, control, etc. but this power is balanced by more limited scope then spheres. Then you could have a bunk sort of system, though I’d probably have it be of limited potency, where using symbolism that is resonant to the lores being applied helps with invocation.

                                                          Again, this is still a very rough and conceptual model, but here’s an example of what I’m thinking:

                                                          [Damien the Devil is walking down the street in Detroit. He sees his old frenemy Francine the Faustian walking past an oil drum while on her cellphone. He decides to give her something better to pay attention to. Damien has Lore of Flame 2, enough to give him the power of perception & manipulation. He decides to begin invoking the Lore of Flame at level 2. Pulling out a cigarette and reaching up with his lighter he mutters an invocation, “As your eyes have become dull because of dead light, may your cinder become scorched because of scorching fire”. Because he’s on an ordinary street with a malaise of apathetic cynicism, his difficulty is let’s say 7. He lowers the difficulty by 1, using the cigarette lighting as a bunk. He rolls a number of dice (maybe willpower or lore) against difficulty 6. He gets 1 success and scorches Francine for 1 agg damage from the fire.]

                                                          While very much rough, this is a basic summary of the idea that I have.
