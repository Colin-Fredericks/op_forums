    Alt-Earth and Alternative Reality with the Time Sphere

    05-17-2018, 11:19 AM
    Had an idea for a setting... imagine if someone beat the Order of Reason to the punch. Imagine if the Order of Hermes had captured the consensus across Europe, and established itself as something normal. By the 21st century, a Hermetic equivalent to modern technology is standard "sleeper tech", so to speak. Where we have cars, they have enchanted carriages with hermetic mechanisms.

    An esoteric application of Time magick allows Mages to peek into alternate realities (Time 2 shows your world's past, and your world's possible futures; it can also show alternative presents if your paradigm accepts such things), and in extreme cases (Time 5 and higher) they may interact with them. Any Mage who becomes stranded in an alternative reality is in trouble. You do not belong within that consensus, and it will gradually reject you. An ST-determined amount of Permanent Paradox is applied to you (1 for a world that is nearly identical, perhaps one where another Mage didn't perish, ranging up to 5 for a world where the consensus and history are wildly different), and you take 1 point of unsoakable Aggravated damage each week equal to that number. A sleeper who is pulled into the wrong reality will gradually become assimilated, with their incorrect memories falling into the background and being eaten away, only appearing as the ghosts of memories. This might explain the Mandela Effect, or even Deja Vu.

    Now my first thought was "Technocracy, but Hermetic!" however that would be a lazy reskin. If the Hermetic Order were to become the most powerful force in pre-colonial Europe, and were to sustain its power for the next few hundred years, I don't think we'd have anything like the Technocracy. On the assumption that the very worst aspects of the order come to the forefront, I imagine we would end up with the following:

    - A feudal oligarchy with Mages and sleeper Elites at the top, a small middle class (more of a lower upper class) of bureaucrats and enforcers, and a huge serf class. Peasants plant and harvest crops, and are an expendable workforce used to harvest magickal foci and Quintessence en masse. Think of a Hermetic power center where serfs bring offerings to be converted into magickal energy.

    - Europe, Australasia and the Americas are the playground of Wizard Kings, with dynasties fighting for power, using armies and intrigue to gain an upper hand. Rulership is synonymous with magickal power, and rulers pass power to Awakened successors (ideally chosen from among their close relatives, but this isn't always possible).

    - In response to the rise of magickal authoritarianism among the Hermetics, the Akashic Brotherhood (and various pan-asian crafts) followed suit. In modern Asia, Tibet, China and Japan (etc.) have their own heavenly Imperial families. These rulers are figureheads (often even being Sleepers) and quasi-religious leaders; the apparatus of state, from their advisers to their generals, are Mages. Events played out similarly in the Middle East and Africa, with various powers seizing land, elevating their own magickal traditions and crafts. The state of global politics is largely in a stalemate.

    - Other Night-Folk fulfill similar roles as they do in our world, but secrecy is less important. Vampires maintain the Secrecy of the Blood, but there is no Masquerade. It's okay if Magi and Kine are aware of Vampires existing individually, and even generally, but the secrets of Vampiric society, their politics and powers and less well-known weaknesses, are not to be shared. The Changing Breeds generally stand against the magickal orders which are draining away Gaia's power; in this setting the Wyld is gradually being eaten away while the Weaver and Wyrm grow in power (however the triat is, overall, more balanced than in our world, since the global human population is smaller and the world isn't "industrialized" in the way we understand).

    - Consensual Reality IS Hermetic Reality in many parts of the world. Other forms of magick are often vulgar, however typical tricks for Coincidence still apply.

    - Education, magickal or otherwise, is restricted to the upper classes. The only sanctioned Mages come from the Elite class, with a few Awakened individuals being taken from the serf class and repurposed to support the status quo.

    - There is no publicly accepted presence of non-Hermetic magick, with the exception of the church's Chorus (which has been gutted and twisted beyond recognition). Non-Hermetic Mages hide their arts and work in the shadows. Recognizable shadows of the Verbena, Dreamspeakers, Crafts etc. exist around the world, but hold little power.

    - Perhaps the heights of the Hermetic Order are riddled with Nephandi... or worse still, perhaps they aren't. Whether or not this state of affairs is an unlikely and sad "1 in a million" reality where a few powerful Nephandi corrupted the Order, or an all-too-likely passage of history where the Hermetics had the chance to capture power before the Enlightenment happened, is down to personal interpretation.

    So hypothetically, a group of Technocrats might become stranded in this alternate earth thanks to a Void Engineer botching a roll. They have to survive in this hostile consensus and acquire enough Tass to fuel the wonder which can take them home.

                      It occurred to me that if Alternate Universes were an accepted part of the Mage universe, and Spherical Magick, then accessing them should be reserved for combinations of Correspondence and Time.

                      Correspondence: an alternate timeline is a sort of "otherspace", and so this is necessary to see into one or travel there.

                      Time: an alternate timeline is "othertime", so this is necessary to see into one or travel there.

                      A level 2 Correspondence + Time effect allows a Mage to perceive alternate presents and alternate pasts, and at level 4 they may move back and forth between worlds (such magick is ALWAYS vulgar since it defies the Earthly Foundations, so using Reality Zones, Chantries, Labs and Sanctums won't help avoid Paradox here). A simple level 1 effect lets them know their position in all-time/all-space, so Mages don't typically "get lost" as it were. You naturally belong in your own timeline, so short of a Botch return journeys should go smoothly.

                      Prime: using Prime a Mage may "consecrate" themselves with the energy of an alternate reality, delaying rejection. Prime 2 may Consecrate a Mage, and Prime 5 may obliterate rejection altogether. The same principles may be applied to objects and people, with some effort. An eccentric Etherite might fire up their probability engine and voyaging gear, fly into an alternate Paris and steal the minor and unimportant Mona Lisa (at least in this reality), bringing it back to adorn their office wall. It would be a lot easier (perhaps for this specific Mage) than copying it down to the molecule with Matter, or stealing the original, after all. Or they might find potential paintings that Van Gogh might have created if he had lived a bit longer, selling them to Ecstatics. Of course these objects would be worn away by a sort of Consensus Rejection unless they were consecrated repeatedly (Prime 2) or the disconnect was completely removed (Prime 5).

                      Entropy: with a strong understanding of fate and manifold destiny (or probabilistic analysis), simply understanding the different ways things could have played out (potential outcomes of a conflict for example) can be performed with Entropy alone. Successful use of Entropy may reduce the difficulties of the Time/Correspondence rotes for accessing alternate timelines, also.

                      Most Paradigms don't leave room for string theory and quantum analysis, although some mystic paradigms might accept that "the dream of what could have been" will form its own sort of reality. Generally speaking messing with alternate realities is the preserve of Void Engineers (perhaps gathering data for the NWO, and examining some events in other worlds to glean useful information) and crazy Etherites.
