                Originally posted by Heavy Arms View Post

                While not the only reason people are worried, this is a great example.

                On their blog, they said they welcomed &quot;some&quot; of the controversy they knew Zak would bring with him. Now, what they fairly clearly wanted, was the ire of misogynists, transphobes, and so on that Zak and Sarah would (and did) evoke by putting a trans character so front and center in the Prelude. But they did not think through how the execution of this would play with both the RPG community vis a vis Zak's history there, and the trans community that might not exactly like being used to troll bigots to make WWP look progressive. Sarah's reaction to the fall out from the preview was particularly telling; neither WWP or Zak prepped her in the slightest for the predictable backlash she was in for.

                Ultimately, while we don't have V5, so we don't know where it will end up, Martin and WWP keep promising things that feel either incompatible or exceedingly difficult to balance. What little evidence we have (one short story, two Prelude interactive fictions) consistently shows WWP failing to meet the expectations they're setting; and each time in the direction of being &quot;too much edge for the sake of edge&quot; than any other option. The LARP events seem to be doing better walking that line, but a lot more people are involved in making those happen than have control over the creative content in V5 (at the moment, we only know of three people actively writing V5: Martin, Karim, and Ken). If we also take in more than just the recent WWP involved LARPs... well Martin's history gets a bit more muddy.

                Is V5 oing to be too edgy? Who knows?

                But the combination of uncertainty, a lot of bad PR, and a lot of cherry picking of which parts of the community they actively engage in, means a lot of fans feel burnt on giving WWP the benefit of the doubt.

                The cherry picking is probably one of the worst things in all this. WWP hasn't ventured to any of the major RPG forums. They haven't even come here. They haven't engaged with plenty of the bigger LARP organizations besides chatting with their leaders at the Grand Masquerade. So they're taking zero control over the narrative here; which is very disappointing because the groups that are getting the most attention seem far more mollified about their concerns. Meanwhile those of us that have been playing the WoD in TT, or PbP, or chats, or whatever get told that WWP doesn't actually consider that contribution to the viability of the WoD IP important. LARP is getting the most support, and the most engagement, because they're the true torchbearers that have kept the WoD alive according to Shane (you know... one of the two LARP people WWP has pulled in from BNS).

                So my goodwill is gone. Until WWP proves to me that they learned why the anthology reissue, and the Zak debacle, and the meh-ness of the Preludes main content, were all problems, I'm going to believe we'll see more severed anuses (wish that was a joke...) than games that walk the line of edgy and well handled.
                Sorry if I missed the point on my last reply. Are you saying people would more accepting of new white wolf if they reached out more to people and explain their plans better?

                                        It is also biologically incorrect. That would be more of an insult for intersex people who identify as female. A transwoman would be better described as transitioning or transitioned from men (male gender) to women (female gender), whether or not they have gone through sexual reassignment surgery. Such a person would be tempted to join the Sabbat so she could benefit from Vicissitude in order to have the female body that she desires, but that may be just another form of insanity or suicide. That would have been dark and edgy without falling into 'hypersexual serial killer trans' stereotypes.
