    Affinity Sphere confusion

    10-12-2019, 10:51 PM
    I am a bit confused with the definition of Affinity Sphere definition, on M20 p. 257 is stated:


    All mages begin with one Affinity Sphere: the Sphere that
    character understands best. Generally, this Sphere is associated
    with a mage’s first experience with magick... The first Sphere dot you place is your
    Affinity Sphere.
    Your character must have at least one dot in one of her group’s
    Affinity Spheres. You can choose any allowed Spheres for your mage,
    so long as you place at least one dot under your group’s Affinity Sphere as
    well    .
    My questions are:
    1. Do you have one (by being a mage) or two (one by being a mage and one by being a member of a faction) Affinity Spheres?
    2. If you only have one Affinity Sphere, does it mean that you cant have for example a Celestial Chorus member with affinity for Entropy?


    Thanks for the help

      You have one affinity sphere - and the affinity spheres of each group are listed in the group's description

      List for the traditions (With their most common affinity sphere first):

      Akashics - Mind, Life
      Celestial Chorus - Prime, Forces, Spirit
      Ecstatics: Time, Life, Mind
      Dreamspeakers: Spirit, Forces, Life, Matter
      Euthanatos: Entropy, Life, Spirit
      Order of Hermes: Almost always Forces; Life, Matter, Mind and Spirit gets used by some houses instead.
      Sons of Ether: Matter, Forces, Prime
      Verbena: Life, Forces
      Virtual Adepts: Correspondence/Data, Forces

      What the text is saying is, that your Mage can have any spheres on their sheet on character generation, but they need at least one dot in their affinity sphere - and by logic, normally their affinity sphere tends to have the most dots. It's the easiest to learn, for them.

      A Chorister with Entropy as their affinity sphere would be very, very unusual - but depending on their paradigm and just how they tend to use Entropy, it could work. You really just have to find a good argument for your ST.

                  The idea, at least way back when, was that each Tradition's Sphere, especially the first dot of it, is innately tied to that Tradition's concept, paradigm and world view, and so every member of that Tradition learns that first dot as part of the initiation into the Tradition. For example, every Euthanatos has Entropy 1 because they have to be able to sense and recognize fate/fortune/destiny and causality in order to carry out the task of maintaining and protecting the Great Wheel, so that is the very first thing each of them is taught. The same with the other eight. An Akashic has to be aware of her own mind and how to center it, a Choruser has to be able to recognize the primal power of the One, an Ecstatic has to be able to feel the pulse of Lakashim, and so on. That is why every character got that first dot for free in addition to the five dots to spend however they wanted.

                  (The Hermetics were a quasi-exception, largely owing to their Ars Magica origins. Forces was the Ars Essentia/Essential Art, and The Gateway to greater power and mastery, but because several Houses carried over from pre-Mage material, their focus sometimes rested on other spheres. But even then, the idea was that an apprentice would demonstrate their worth - step through The Gateway - by first showing an understanding of Ars Essentia and then moving on to the specifics of their House.)

                  I have, for most of the past twenty years, had PCs start with both the Tradition Sphere and a Favored Sphere (one they are just naturally adept with or that they tend to focus on above all others), both with the same XP discounts.

                          Under my own personal house rules, yes. Under official M20, no. (Unless I missed something.)

                          Under 1st, 2nd and Revised edition rules, everyone got their Tradition's Sphere and that was it, with IIRC Revised introducing the idea that certain factions within a Tradition might allow for an alternate Sphere (House Solificati, for example, having Matter rather than Forces). M20, which I admit to not being overly familiar with, apparently takes this even further by allowing you to pick from three different ones for each group1. But, just speaking from personal opinion, I'm not entirely wild about the idea because that first dot of each Tradition's specific Sphere is really the cornerstone of everything they do. But on the other side of the coin, I recognize that you will often have a character concept where what sort of magic they focus on will not match with the Tradition's Sphere. The Etherite biologist who focuses on Life, the Verbena weather witch mastering Forces, or the Celestial Choruser using Entropy to try and understand the One's grand plan and the divine fate of souls. Hence my house rule, which always seemed to work ok.

                                                      You get a single Affinity sphere.

                                                      One.

                                                      You get another one if you take a 7 point merit.

                                                      "The Affinity Sphere entry under each group gives the default Sphere for this sect. Some groups have more than one Sphere listed, but only one Affinity Sphere may be chosen per character. "
