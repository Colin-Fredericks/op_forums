    M20 Sorcerer dropped, What are everyone's thoughts?

    10-18-2022, 03:38 PM
    M20 Sorcerer dropped last Wednesday and everyone who ordered an advanced copy had theirs updated to the final product the day before then.

    Personally, I'm mixed.

    They kept in the more fundamental issues that were in the advanced release and other versions prior. The weird aspect/difficulty interaction is still there in full force(and the "optional ̶f̶i̶x̶ /rule" misses the point Hard), some paths ignore the aspect system entirely even when it doesn't completely make sense(alchemy using duration would have been fine) and the turn-per dot casting that Everyone pointed out was a bad idea was left as-is(hand guns take only a turn to shoot, do more damage, and draw less attention than a fireball in comparison).

    A new issue has cropped up in that Sorcerers have no inherent means of gaining and storing mana/quintessence. The mana background was scrapped entirely and Avatar was one of few mage backgrounds that was Not made available for sorcerers to take. This renders the Merits Flow of Ki, Strength of Psyche, and Force of Spirit are now practically useless in most situations. It's currently impossible for sorcerers to use Quint without a specific path At the 4th dot or Higher(on a side note, the "draw quintessence for node" ability was placed at 4 dots, and the "store quintessence in object" was placed at 3 dots(2 with a ritual). This means you can't get the quintessence to store in an object until path level4). Sorcerers even at their maximum cannot hold quint for more than a turn, so they must spend a prior action gaining quintessence somehow before they can use quint for a merit or spell(effectively doubling quintessence's turn requirements in the action economy).

    On the other hand I like how much many of the systems have been cleaned up and organized. I really like how they handled the psychic powers system especially and how plug-and-play it makes creating a psychic character.

The time on Hellifre being multiple turns is also meh. The whole Quintessence Manipulation path.
                  My personal take for aspects is at least 1 success in the spell roll = All aspects start at 1 automatically without allocation, additional successes are added on.

                  I also pick a "Specialty" aspect for each path. That specialty is always has successes allocated equal to the dot rating of the path used, with an added downside that this aspect Cannot go over the dot rating, but non-specialty aspects can have more successes allocate than the dot rating.

                  A hellfire user for example would be able to affect multiple people or snipe from far away with Hellfire 1, but their damage would be locked at Hellfire 1 levels. A spirit binder might always be able to call a powerful spirit at SBW 5, but the duration of the spell would be variable based on successes.

                  This way difficulty is determined by the "power" of the spell being cast and successes are spent to see how well the sell went off. This avoid "I used Path 1 with 10 dice and nothing to spend successes on while the player next to me used path 5 and didn't get enough successes to justify using the spell at that level".

A thaumaturge's primary path is their most expensive by far(being dot x7 unless tremere), but secondary path XP doesn't depend on clan so everyone gets dot x4 after that.(maybe because ritual knowledge is based on main path?)

                  I'm thinking a fair amount for sorcerer might be dot x5, considering unlike blood magic every new path gives new rituals as well.

                  As for the Good bits in the book, Psychics definitely got the lion's share of the the love. Several powers are much stronger than before in addition to basing all their rolls on willpower now. A notable one is Synergy(the hold hands and share powers ability), the power that let's psychics combine different powers into one Super ability was reduced from the 5th dot to 4th and the New 5th dot ability allows Psychics to use any Synergy with unlimited range. With that a group could send their beefiest guy to pentex headquarters and load him up with unlimited willpower and every psychic power Ever or summon Captain Planet at his location while porting him out of danger. Psychic cults are a genuine Main-splat-level threat now.

                  Sorcerers have the comparable ability to cast grand rituals, which don't cost XP but does take several hours to do because of the sorcerer casting rule and can only be done with all the sorcerers in the same room actively helping. I think the writer had a bit of a bias.

                                          Tass *is* supposed to be rare. How you get what you need, the rare components of a planned working, is one place where the story can be a great deal of fun for sorcerers.
