    Originally posted by Dataweaver View Post
    We're told a little bit about mages in ancient Egypt: specifically, that they organized into two groups that we're the earliest example of what would eventually become the Traditions and Technocracy, and served as the roots of the Order of Hermes. They're also supposedly the reason why there are nine Spheres, as they pioneered a nine-fold division of magic.
    Interesting. I’m looking at Mage: the Ascension again after a good number of years, so there’s a lot I’ve missed. The Egyptian origin of the Order of Hermes makes sense. Also, I see that the Craftmasons (i.e. proto-Syndicate) trace their origins back to an ancient Roman group. What would we do without the White Wolf Wiki?

    It seems that, while Technocratic ideology came together and factionalized in the early modern, certain tendencies were always there, probably from the very beginning. It’s just that modernity provided a framing for real high-stakes conflict.

    The question of the “Westernness” the Technocracy is interesting because on the one hand it pretty much has to represent the cultural imperialism of certain Western European ideologies in the modern period. On the other hand, I can see how the core tendencies that gave birth to the Technocracy would have to be more or less universal. And it’s pretty much a global thing now, which means a lot of diverse groups would have to be a part of it by now. Global cultural hegemony isn’t a one-way street, after all. Things be complicated.

                                                        There are remnants still around, but they’re no longer extant as a pro-Antediluvian conspiracy operating within the Sabbat. That shadow sect, and their underworld base, were blown up in late 2e, coinciding with the end of the Wraith line, and since then the creators have been eager to show how the Tal’mahe’ra were actually wrong about everything. Much of the sect dynamics of 3e was meant to be the result of them no longer being there to undermine the Sabbat to keep them from making open war on the Camarilla.

                                                        V20 is metaplot/timeline agnostic, so they’re in there if you want to use them, but they won’t be a thing going forward.

                                                        In V5 the Nagaraja are still around as part of the Hecata.
