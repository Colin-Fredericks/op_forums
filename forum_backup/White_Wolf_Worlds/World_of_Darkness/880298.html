    My attempt at making a hybrid shifter type

    05-02-2016, 02:11 PM
    Around the time just after started getting all the breed books, and was interested also in Mage the Ascension, i started wondering WHY canonically you could NOT make 'mixed breed' shifters, and all the book says is 'gaia won't allow it'. BUT with Mages use of spirit, mind, body and matter magic, Why couldn't THEY force it? So for a fits and grins game (lots of beer drinking) i made up the Denier, my attempt at doing a 'hybrid shifter'...

    The Denier (pronounced De Nay Yar).
    History: Back in the early to mid 1950s, the Technocracy held a meeting of all their various mage traditions to discuss problems and achieve solutions, to hold their course for the next 50 years), during that meeting the Progenitors brought up the fact that a number of facilities in recent years were lost to Garou and other Fera attacks. After a long debate, it was decreed that the 'shifter' problem was to be their go to issue to resolve in that 50 year time frame.
    During studies of captured shifters, it was discovered that those of the "Metis" branch were not only deformed in someway, but the most significant was that they were sterile. So someone got the hair-brained idea to see what would happen if you combined 2 different shifter folk. Surely they would be even MORE hamstrung..

    Over the next 20 years, they spent time capturing more and more Fera, taking care to ensure they had an even mix of all the types they knew of, then started combining their magical powers over Spirit, Mind, Body and Matter to try and "crack the code". It wasn't till the mid 80s, when DNA manipulation in the world at large became more prevalent, till they made a break through.
    By the end of that 50 year time frame, the operation (known only as Project Eldorado), had almost 300 such cross-breed shifter offspring amongst their test subjects. BUT the overarching leaders felt that since these hybrids were not more deformed, let alone substantially worse off (well to what the Mages could see), that the project was a failure. So it was decreed that the project would get terminated... with extreme prejudice.

    During the next 3 months, many of the scientists (mostly mundane humans with little to know morals, but a love of science) along with well over half the cross-breeds created were killed off, and all 5 of the facilities destroyed. But just over 40 did manage to escape captivity and the termination process. After 1 year, depression kicked in and almost a dozen of that remaining stock had committed suicide, rather than live the life that they were 'dealt'.
    Now the remaining 30 or so, try to live on in a world that seems to shun the heck out of them, even to the point of hunting them down...

    Because of how they were created, Most other Fera do not see the Denier as proper shifters, and do not associate with them, though some at least take pity on their plight. Catiff and loner shifters though, do and have associated with them.

    Character creation rules:
    Of the 30 or so types that were remaining at the end of the Project, one was a Mokole Gurahl (father/mother) hybrid. Two were Corax Garou, 4 were Garou Gurahl, 5 Bastet Gurahl, and 6 Mokole Bastet. The remaining 4 were of 'unknown parentage (allowing PCs who select them to pick their parents, though note No Corax Gurahl, Ratkin anything else, Nuwisha - something else or Ananasi anything else were successfully created.

    Build attributes and abilities as standard for any shifter type. 7/5/3 & 13/9/5
    For the changing forms stat modifiers (Crinos etc) use the Father's breed type to get Strength, Stamina and Manipulation modifiers, and the Mother's breed type for Dexterity mods and any Appearance mods.

    For the Auspice roll 1d10 to get which it is; 1-2 Ragbash, 3-4 Galliard, 5-6 Ahroun, 7-8 Phillodox, 9-10 Theurge.

    Rage: By auspice

    Willpower: As per Father's breed (If Garou, this is 4 which is the average of all the 13 tribes), costs 20% more than normal to increase via XP regardless of if in a Sept or pack or not. Can only purchase up to 3 additional willpower with Freebie points.

    Gnosis: 4 starting up (1 more than Metis get, which is a lingering effects of all the spirit magics used on them, but it is offset with a higher cost to improve). If using Freebie points to raise, cost is at normal, but has a 7 cap. If purchasing/improving with experience in game, cost is Double until they have a MINIMUM of 6 months being cleansed in the silver lake known as Erebus.

    Backgrounds: 1 starting out only unless uses freebies to select more. This is placed in Past-life tied to their parents.

    Renown: 4 dots starting out, but gaining renown is hard as heck. If not accepted or a member of a pack or Sept, it is earned at only 1/3rd the normal rate. If they have somehow been accepted by a pack, its at half the rate. And on the rare occasion they may get accepted into a Sept, the cost is still only 3/4 the rate. When figuring out renown, go by Mother's breed for the types they have.

    Gifts: 3 starting out. As with Renown, cost to learn any new ones is higher unless in Sept or pack, this is cause spirits are harder to appease. Pick one from their Auspice, Father's breed and Metis. Cannot use Freebie points to acquire more. When using XP to buy more, cost is 2 times normal if not in either a pack or Sept. 50% more if only in a pack. If in a Sept, cost is regular though. These were picked up from either their parents, or from lone Fera who took pity on them.

    Additional Hindrances and advantages:
    For all the above few benefits (1 more renown and Gnosis than normal) and penalties (higher costs to gain any new stuff), they suffer a number of additional issues.
    1) Use Mother's breed type to see what additional NORMAL advantages the shifter would have, this does NOT apply if either parent was a Mokole or Gurahl (see below)
    2) Free merit of Silver (or gold if Mokole/Corax) Tolerance. This is a lingering effect of their spirits being messed with by magic
    3) Can not naturally heal wounds of Lethal or Aggravated level, only can heal with gifts or fetishes. Bashing damage healed at 3/4 the speed.
    4) Use Father's breed form for any Normal additional penalties they might have
    5) Like Metis they have a disfigurement, but they also gain a Derangement, which normally has something to do with Mages or magic.
    6) If either parent was Mokole, they only get the 3 changing forms like Mokole do, and as such gain archid traits, but one LESS than their Gnosis would normally let them purchase. Also their 'gator' side takes a more prominent position in any appearance they have.
    7) If either parent was Gurahl, they go by the bear-folk's way of spending rage.
    8) Besides the cost of Gnosis going down, if they ever spend 6 months (or longer) in Erebus getting cleansed, they regain their natural healing for Aggravated and Lethal damage.

      you put a lot of thought into something quite silly.

      If you want to make your own shifter, and blame mages, it'd be a from-scratch job of combining man,soul,spirit and animal. You'd need spheres at 6+ most like. Hey; If pentex can make mockery breeds, Mages should be able to make what they want (though getting it right or not is a difficult question).

      You don't need to do something quite so silly as having a hyena-crow. There's billions of species that you can use. Beetle,Mantis,Falcon,Crab,possum,wolverine, whatever. You could even (not likely intentionally) revive one of the lost breeds, though they might be different than before.

      Some thoughts
      -They won't be tied to luna or helios, so no gold or silver weakness.
      -The ability to step sideways is a possibility, but it's likely they won't be able to do it without spirit ties.
      -They likely won't start with good spirit relations. At best, no gifts beyond level 3 for the next few decades. At worst, spirits go out of their way to attack the offending changer. Banes might be generous though... but you're most likely to have them get no gifts unless you do a supreme job of it.
      - Chances are, the forms might be incomplete. You might be missing the animal form. You might be missing the human form. There might not be a warform, or they have an equivelent to hispo but not a glabro.
      - Deformity. an appearance rating of two might be the best they can get, or they might all be mad. Worst is most of them could be sterile.
      -They may or may not possess rage and gnosis (or a blood pool), but if you're doing this to a mage it'll be different. I think the arete would take precedence. The ability to heal quickly and soak aggravated is also a thing of chance.
      - you might be treading on ground already covered. maybe you make lizardmen despite the existence of mokole- I mean, Mokole aren't known about, but everyone want's a lizard men. Pentex did this with war-wolves, but they're somewhat incomplete.

      Read book of the wyrm. it's got some nice inspiration.
