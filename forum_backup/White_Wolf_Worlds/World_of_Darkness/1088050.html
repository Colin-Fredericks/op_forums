    Monte Cook's World of Darkness?

    05-04-2017, 10:15 PM
    So I've been interested in checking out Monte Cook's World of Darkness game, which I am lead to believe was either heavily inspired by World of Darkness, or is an alternate portrayal of the same game. I'm not completely sure, because there are far, far fewer videos out there explaining this game than there are for the White Wolf version.

    If anybody around here has checked out the game in more detail, I'd like to know if the game is worth picking up; or if its lack of attention is a sign of what I can expect. I know the gist of the story, being that a crap load of supernatural shit invaded the world just over a year ago, but beyond that, I don't have anything to go on. Just that it's a semi-post apocalyptic scenario with vampires, mages, demons, werewolves, and awakened hoomans.

                                                          The setting overall is a clumsy, chaotic hodgepodge of muddled ideas that don't play nicely together and which throws in names from the WoD randomly and with no relation whatsoever to their 'proper' use. It's like Monte couldn't decide between creating a WoD game, a variant of Dark Matter, and a game based around spirit possession, so he did all three at once, and it suffers from it heavily.

                                                          Also, 'the setting is d20 based' is not 'it uses d20s'. It specifically means it uses the D20 system from WotC. It's a much less generic statement and really says it all - if you like those mechanics, they're fine, and if not, they're not great.
