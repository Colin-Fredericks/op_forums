                                                There's a difference between trimming the excesses and wholesale extermination. Most of the Imbued tend to fall under the former category. The Technocracy tends to fall overwhelmingly into the latter, since it labels everyone a Reality Deviant and the party line that hard men making hard decision hold everyone to is to kill, kill, and kill some more. Anyone who doesn't think that all you need is kill gets reprogrammed or snuffed out. While some Imbued do take things that far, the members of the Mercy Creeds can be counted on to not do that, and they make up a significant portion of Imbued - certainly more than the Waywards. They also are regular people in the sense that they're trying to hold down jobs and don't have hidden doom fortresses and the support of societal influence at every level, that kind of thing. The Technocracy is a vast distance from that, being monsters that warp reality to try and make it fit their incomplete vision of safety uber alles.
