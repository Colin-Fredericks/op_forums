    [PEACH] Crossover Self-Insert (Mage)

    10-29-2019, 09:14 AM
    Greetings,


    We are toying with the idea of a Self-Insert chronicle, in which the players will be themselves as they are in the real world and their win condition will be to "fix the world" or at least prevent the end of it.

    Assume that collectively we own all the books of the oWoD, and we will use 20th anniversary rules (V20+V20DA, M20, W20, Wr20, C20).

    Characters will have the PC's real life stats (whatever they end up being, merits and flaws included), their supernatural template of choice (all are Mages), 7 Background dots (only to get supernatural Backgrounds, nothing social), Willpower at 5, no freebies (so Arete is 1 and so are the Spheres), the year is 1997.
    • What are easy/fast, non-evil power-ups for them?
    • What are their better/faster/safer bets to stop the worst case scenarios of the Time of Judgement? We were thinking:

    1. London. Find Charon/Charles Andersen, ally with him.
    2. Worldwide. Find Lyla Yvonne Tzigano and use her help to ally with Lilith.
    3. Worldwide. Find The Perfect Metis, and help him deal with many Apocalypse scenarios (this has to wait until 1999).
    4. Chicago. Find Menele, ally with him, he is a good guy that can help with future scenarios.
    5. Worldwide. Find Enkidu, ally with him, he is a good guy that can help with future scenarios.
    6. Hong Kong. Kill Oliver Thrace (avoid one of the worst Time of Judgement scenarios), request help of previously listed allies to achieve this.
    7. Switzerland. Ally with Horus, use his help to find and kill Eurydice (avoid two Time of Judgement scenarios).
    8. Finland. Kill Louhi (avoid part of one of the worst Gehenna scenarios), request help of previously listed allies to achieve this.
    9. Russia. Ally with Durga Syn, use her help to redeem Shazear and help Vasilisa kill Baba Yaga, find Koschei's Soul Egg and throw it into Malfeas.
    10. Kaymakli. Kill Augustus Giovanni (he is not that strong) and previous allies should make this possible.
    11. Berlin. Ally with Ankla Hotep, use his godly stats and disciplines to fight the Antediluvians when they appear.
    12. Worldwide. Find The Shaper Antediluvian and ally with her.
    13. Transylvania. IF [Tzimisce] is moving, release Kupala (as idiotic as it sounds) and let them kill each other (avoid part of two of the worse Gehenna scenarios).
    14. Worldwide. IF Caine and the Antediluvians are appearing, use Lilith's (point 2), Ankla Hotep's (point 11) and The Shaper's (point 12) help to destroy them (so most Gehenna scenarios never occur, and without Caine in most cases all vampires are destroyed).
    15. Yellow Springs. Ally with Chan and Lei Zu, kill Yu Huang, make Lei Zu queen. Use Charon help (point 1) to achieve all of this.
    16. Yomi Wan. Help Voormas kill Tou Mu, ally with him, and let him and The Unnamed fight each other; destroy the winner (two of the worse Ascension scenarios averted).


    Up to point 5, those are relatively easy (with a bit of experience, even up to point 8): they could still die horribly, but those do not require a lot of personal power, more a lot of meta-knowledge. After that, things are downright suicidal.

    We would love any suggestions on both questions.

      "What are the easy/fast, non-evil power-ups for them"

      Usually if you want something easy and fast....there is a price to pay.

      But for a few nonshitty 'I don't want to sell my soul' alternatives to power:

      Awakening is a pretty big power buff if they aren't already awakened themselves. If this is being done while they are still mortals that's a buff.

      Magick items, magick items, magick items. They usually don't have an arete requirement if you think they work, some take minimum training, and they can do some crazy shit.

      Fetishes. Basically magick items but with more baggage, but if the spirit inside is actually nice it's a pretty good boon.

      Allies.

      Question 2:

      Well for all of this: How the fuck do any of you know /anything/ about these people? If we are doing self-insert "Turns out real life is really dark and depressing" and none of you know anything about these characters...how do you intend on knowing about them? Your characters probably barely know magick exists and you basically posted every secret society/secret group in the World of Darkness. These are also all terrible ideas to begin with. #14 is suicidal as hell and you will fail. #10 is a pretty big assumption. The only one here that isn't suicidal or really dumb (Especially with 'real life stats' and starting mage stats. And if you aren't the ones doing the killing, your basically following a DMPC cool guy who does all the work for you) is #8 and Giovanni could still kick an entire load of ass. Sure he's 'weak for a 3rd gen' but weak for a 3rd gen is still capable of ripping off everyone of your starting characters heads.

      How to stop the time of judgement?

      Well it's easy.

      You hope the ST lets you.

      Barring that.

      Do some Time Correspondence shittery and timeline hop to one that isn't so shitty and pretend your whole life there isn't a lie. Or be an arch-mage/oracle and curb stomp every threat instead of being a background NPC like every other oracle.

          Jihelu Thanks for taking the time to answer, but you dont seem to understand what a Self-Insert is, it is a term in a fanfiction in which a real human being, you, me, anyone, WHO KNOWS/LOVES A FICTIONAL SETTING, is transported to such setting with all his/her meta knowledge.

          In this example, Jack, Chris, and Agus will be playing as themselves, KNOWING all they know about oWoD. That's the reason they can plan such things.

          Given that, Allies makes no sense, since they dont know anybody in the world of darkness circa 1997: the people they know have not been born in this world, their bank accounts (and banks for that matter) most likely don't exist, their IDs are not valid, their houses are property of someone else (if they exist at all), etc. I think they will make themselves, have their own backgrounds in stand by, and by dint of being strangers, everyone gets Arcane 5.


          The fun in self-insert adventures is the use of meta-knowledge to triumph the POWER OVERWHELMING!!! of other characters. Sometimes that is picking the low-hanging fruit, making connections that characters in the setting have no way of making, and knowing the answers to nearly all the scenarios, but being limited in credibility/power/time/skill. It is type of story that rewards creativity more than power.

                  All I can say (other than pray to the ST) it's that you need to ally with strong good people.

                  Your main problem it's that people it's not going to believe you. You need to get proof of your claims, revealing a great deal about what will happen in 1997 can help but it can also make people very suspicious of you lot. After all, this is WoD and paranoia it's a way of life.

                  You should use your time to try to prevent the smaller tragedies in order to have more people on your side. Try to solve the small plots and save people first. That way you will have credence for when you need help defeating the "big" bads (and will get you xp). Since mages suffered so much in 1999, you can gain a great deal by focusing on saving the people who died that year. And, if you think about it, much of that was caused by relatively small fry doing stupid shit

                  So, rather than the murderous and paranoid sociopath that it's Vormass, I would try to befriend Dante. He's powerful, relatively approachable, nice, very interested in information, and knows how to diseminate it in ways it will be believed. He's also likely going to become an Oracle, eventually. If you manage to befriend Dante and convince him of your claims, he can inform all the relevant people on both sides of the Ascencion War.

                  Try to avert the Horizon's Warrior exploits. Save Horizon (that's easy enough, you just need to deal with the Hollow One that betrayed the Realm to the Technocracy) and the Traditions (including the many Archmages that lived in Horizon) would owe you a HUGE one. Save Doissetep, that way you have Porthos on your side and the whole Order of Hermes will owe you

                  Now Doissetep it's full of pricks hardly worth saving, but their violent death it's one of the causes of the Avatar Storm. Preventing it strengthens Mage society as a whole. And it's not that hard: You don't want Ravnos to live (so, one of the causes it's going to be there), but you can totally rat out the rogue Technocrat that hurled a Spirit Nuke into the Labyrinth. With 2/3 of it's causes being averted, the Storm should either cease to be a problem or at least become significantly weaker

                  Averting the Spirit Nuke could also avoid or weaken the 6th Maelstrom, and could get a huge boon with the Hierarchy (if you can convey your deed to them w/o being murdered for trespassing on the Underworld). More importantly, it will stave off Oblivion's goal of eradicate all of existence, which it's a world end you forgot (ha!, how ironic), and it would prevent the horde of Demons being released and many Earthbound from awakening.


                  The disadvantage of becoming friend of Mage society by proving to be an "Isekai" it's that (knowing how mages are) many will want to dismember your patterns to see how you function...perhaps even try to invent a spell to travel to the 'real world' too :P


                  For 14: The Unnamed can be defeated by ensuring the world doesn't become a shithole. Hell on Earth points out that the Unnamed can't exist on Reality for a long period of time (not even in the Near Umbra) if humanity and the world in general doesn't become more self-destructive and hopeless than it already is (generating a Consensus of Shit, so to speak). That's why he hasn't killed the world already, he's been biding his time until the world it's sufficiently evil for his presence. Also, he uses the Avatar Storm to kill of the Technocracy and most of the oposition, that's a big part of his plot. So, if the Storm doesn't exist and the world looks better...he will keep biding his time, and so should you until you're ready to face him on the Qlippoth of Entropy.
