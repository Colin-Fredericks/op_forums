        I think crossovers with all the game lines would require the players be limited to certain compatible choices. For example, a Mage in a game with Werewolves and Wraiths should be required to have spirit 3.

        Similarly, depending on the factions, you might say only a certain archetype from each splat could be chosen. Perhaps a "technophile" game where you can only chose characters like Glasswalkers, artificiers, Etherites, Knockers, etc.

        Then you could make a threat that they would all have some stake in. Perhaps there is a group of Void Engineers which has discovered the Pattern Web, and is doing some strange experiments to it in order to test its parameters, unknowingly creating ripples in the stability of the telurian, causing all sorts of weird physics loopholes to appear.

                        Actually, according to the Book of Madness Revised (pp. 78-80), the Technocrats have specific and extremely violent responses to Demons (which they consider to be among the worst EDEs). Kindred are one thing, they are an established phenomena with quantifiable weakness. Demons are another thing because, quite frankly, the Demons are extradimentional body snatchers who stated goals are to establish their own dominion on Earth, which is antithetical to the plans of the Union.

                        I know that there are Demon fans that believe that Demons are not given their rightful due, but I really do believe that every major supernatural faction in cWoD believes Demons=Enemy. Whether you are Changelings, Fera, Kindred, KJ, or Mages, you tend to be considered enemy number one when you make a deal with Demons. The only thing worse than an Infernalist is an actual Demon, which is exterminated with extreme prejudice because they are Evil with a capital E to most splats.

                                                Man following this thread is weird.

                                                Anyway, Mage is actually one of the few games where dealing with demons can be possible without the default hyper-wrath most other splats default to. A skilled enough demonologist (at least non-Technocrat) mage is going to quickly figure out that "demon" is a very loose term in the WoD, and that the PC demons of DtF might deserve a shot as allies against the far more obviously bad demons out there.
