      The Billiwhack Monster (Aliso Canyon Road, Santa Paula, CA):
      The Billiwhack Dairy according to the legend is a decrepit ruin of what once was a state of the art, very advanced and modern dairy farm. It apparently contains underground rooms and tunnels, but is also rumored to be missing walls, etc, which could be attributed to the many decades of disuse. The dairy was started up and run by an August Rubel who moved to Ventura County in 1922 and established the dairy in 1924. Rubel was once a native of Zurich, Switzerland. He served in the American Field Service in France from 1917 to 1919. The urban legend connects him to the Office of Strategic Services or the OSS. The OSS was the precursor to the Central Intelligence Agency. If the legend is true, the OSS had Ruben performing experiments beneath the dairy, including trying to make a super soldier. The Billiwhack Monster is apparently a product of this research and experimentation that escaped to haunt the local area.
      Much like other secret organizations, the OSS is attached to all sorts of illegal and amoral experiments ranging from brainwashing techniques, use of various drugs on unsuspecting victims, studies of diseases for use in warfare, and other inhumane practices.
      The OSS was started by William "Wild Bill" (some state his nickname as "Mad Dog") Donovan who was made the Coordinator of Information or COI in 1941. This office eventually became the OSS. The OSS was responsible for much of the espionage and sabotage in Europe and Asia. The OSS was disbanded after World War II, but a proposal by Donovan eventually led to the formation of the present day CIA.
      As the urban legend goes, Rubel, after performing experiments in Ventura County, was eventually sent over seas and died mysteriously doing secret work for the OSS in 1943. According to a historical account, August Rubel actually died in Tunisia, when he returned to service and the ambulance he was driving hit a German land mine.....

      So if there is really a half goat/half man monster lurking in the depths of Santa Paula, there's a lot of questions regarding what it's doing there and how it got there. Although there's little to no evidence of its existence, some cryptozoologists believe it might be related to or actually a Bigfoot, granted a deformed or disfigured Bigfoot.
      Potentially for either Werewolf or Mage. Since August Rubel might have been a Progenitor, or the Billiwhack Monster is a very early Mockery Breed that's still on the loose.

      Olivas Adobe (Ventura, CA)
      A midweek afternoon visit, when the place is relatively deserted, is perhaps the best time to look for the disturbed ghost of Senora Teodoro Olivas.
      Senora Olivas had reason to worry: the poor woman pumped out 21 kids before hanging up her spurs. There may be another reason she chooses to keep watch over her home, which was completed in 1851. Her husband, Don Raimondo Olivas was deeded 2,200 acres of land bordering the Ventura shore in appreciation for his service in the Mexican Army under General Santa Barbara. He named it Rancho San Miguel. By shrewd political maneuvering and hard work, Olivas became one of the wealthiest ranchers in Alta California. Fiestas held at the adobe lasted for days. All of this of course made him a target for bandits, who staged a vicious raid on his home in 1855.
      The robbers rounded up the family and servants and then searched the grounds for valuables. Here, the story gets fuzzy. One version maintains that a trusted Indian servant was secretly given Don Raimundo’s treasure box, reportedly containing anywhere from $3,000 to $75,000 in gold coins (depending on which story you choose to believe) to bury on the grounds while Raimundo stalled the outlaws. When the servant successfully completed his mission, he returned to the adobe and was shot before the desperados realized that he was the only person who knew where the stash was hidden.
      The bandidos fared slightly better if you choose to go with the second version of events: After one of them struck Senora Olivas to the ground and ripped her earrings off, the group managed to carry off the treasure and make for the hills. They soon realized that a sizable posse had been rounded up to track them down, so the box was buried in the mountains somewhere between Ventura and Santa Barbara. No one has yet found the lost gold. According to Senate, at least one of the criminals got away, but not forever: “A fellow named Encarnacion Berryessa was in a bar in LA boasting of his many evil deeds—one of which was the robbery of Olivas Adobe. The good patrons of the bar took him out and hung him.”
      Olivas Adobe is a Haunt in Wraith: the Oblivion. And people in the Skinlands and wraiths in the Shadowlands are still looking for that lost treasure (Possibly a Fetter or Artifact/Relic), even if it still hasn't been found.

          Geez, there are literally thousands of places around the earth that fit, and not all are bad (per se). Not including small, local places (mostly because I'm fairly new to the area), a quick look at "Mysterious Places" on Google is all it takes.

          I'm a huge fan of Sedlec Ossuary (http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sedlec_Ossuary) in Eastern Europe - if that place wasn't built by either Cappadocians or, more likely, Tzimisce, I dunno what was. It's probably haunted as all hell too, and there's a fantastic chance it's a Haunt.

          The Yonaguni Monument (http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yonaguni_Monument) can be tough to get into a campaign, but no doubt weresharks and the Void Engineers have their own ideas of what should be done with the place.

          Kholat Syakl is just plain cool. (http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dyatlov_Pass_incident) I mean yeah, it's all been explained away scientifically.... sorta. But what really caused those disappearances? Spectres? Werewolves? Mages or Technocrats trying to make it look like something else? Spirits of the Mansi people? If you're more into mysterious deaths than the places involved, http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_unsolved_deaths. (Speaking of which, anything involved in the life of H.H.Holmes could serve as a springboard to horrible adventures.)

          There are tons of cheap, usually cheap, books with mysteries - and they generally detail the place where the mystery occurred (i.e., the Devil's Triangle, the Bermuda Triangle, Black Mountain, whatever). They practically beg to be used as resources in the WoD.

                                                      The Ghost of the 73.
                                                      This is a story of Sorrow, and hatred, rage and wrath. I wont go into much details since involves heavy politics from before i was born. Ill try to be as Subjective as possible since its a open wound still in my country.
                                                      Im chilean, keep that in mind and that english isnt my first language.

                                                      During the end of the 60's and the begining of the 70's, The president was Salvador Allende, a socialist president choosen by the People. Things on his goverment werent ok. There was a lot of conflicts between the left goverment and unions and syndicates, a lot of food strikes and such.

                                                      Things got worst.

                                                      September 11th of 1973. The military took over by a Cue De Gra,leaded by General Augusto Pinochet, La Moneda (our Presidential house. sort of like a White house but the president didnt lived there) was bombarded while the military was deployed onto the streets, killing anyone who supported Allende.

                                                      President Allende made a final radio statement wishing for a better world, on his own words and i Quote from Wikipedia.
                                                      "Workers of my country, I have faith in Chile and its destiny. Other men will overcome this dark and bitter moment when treason seeks to prevail. Keep in mind that, much sooner than later, the great avenues will again be opened through which will pass free men to construct a better society. Long live Chile! Long live the people! Long live the workers!"
                                                      Death of Salvador Allende - Wikipedia
                                                      http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_of_Salvador_Allende


                                                      After that, a gunshot ecchoed on every single radio connected to the presidental broadcast. He commited suicide with an AK-47 given by Fidel Castro.

                                                      General Pinochet took over the goverment, he improved things, he forced nightwatches and a lot of people dissapeared during his goverment. Theres hundreds of witness accounts of Camps in which the suspected suporters were tortured and killed, their bodies missing. some of the survivors changed forever.
                                                      This lasted from 1973 to 1990.

                                                      Movies were censored, books, media was over controlled, people got executed on the streets during the first days and the death camps were something everyone knew about but not everyone wanted to aknowledge or accept (even if Pinochet tried to hide this), The left keep fighting the right with everything they had. the military keep pushing them at bay.
                                                      During this time, around 3.000 individuals were Captured and vanished, other 2.000 were imprissioned and tortured through the country.

                                                      Places like The National Stadium were used as detention camps, musicians such as Victor Jara.
                                                      Some others exiled themselves out of the country.

                                                      Due to that, Some claim that theres a lot of strong energy and presence on places where people died during this times, places like The National Stadium, Augusto Pinochet's house (a building made of black stones which now is a college), La Moneda, and some of the mountains sorrounding the city, as well the Military Academy of Santiago among others.

                                                      Places like Patio 29 (Courtyard 29) were discovered during the following years, a sort of Unmarked graveyard where where political prisoners, especially those who "disappeared" during the 1973 Chilean coup d'état, were buried anonymously. The mass grave, the largest of Augusto Pinochet's military government, was used for unannounced and unmarked burials in the 1970s until an anonymous tip alerted the public to its usage.

                                                      some aprehentions continued during the 80's, protests were incredibly violent. and groups like the Dirección de Inteligencia Nacional (National Intelligence Directorate.) keept and incredibly tight grip by arresting anyone who they suspected of being leftists or to opose to Pinochet, from assassinations to make them dissapear with no trace. its well known that these secret police were instructed on techniques of assassination and torture.

                                                      This Era has ended, Augusto Pinochet lost on 1988 to the popular vote Yes (for him to stay in goverment) and No (for him to leave), The No won., the Democracy came back on 1990.
                                                      Every year, the Chilean September 11th is remembered, some people light candles for their missing relatives. others go to the streets to fight cops and do senseless destruction.

                                                      Thing is, For the events that took place in my country almost 41 years ago, we still live under the shadow of what happend, Buildings are haunted by those who were tortured and died.

                                                      Chile is a country of Mystisicm, of Mapuche Magic and Folklore mixed with political events. Its accepted that our country is very haunted, its accepted that places like Patio 29, or The National Stadium hold the souls of those who died and were tortured.

                                                      The haunting continues, and theres nothing to do. Only to try to forget the past, and avoid repeating it yet again. and teach the young generations about what happend and to avoid causing havok due the remembering of the September 11th (Many of the guys who do sensless destruction during that date, do it because they feel like it, no political inclination or anything like it.)
