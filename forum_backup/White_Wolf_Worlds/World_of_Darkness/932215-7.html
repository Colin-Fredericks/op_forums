                                                    And the Dark Ages book about the Norse vampires of ye olde (Wolves by the Sea? Something like that) features their own creation myth, where Odin gave a dieing warrior eternal life with his blood, and he became the first vampire. Then the Norse vampires actually were living with the normal humans in their villages as praised warriors of Odin, taking care of each other, and the Vampires going along the raids and traderoutes of the viking ships.

                                                    It's really neat stuff. So yeah, lots of fancy Vampire origin stories. Take your pick.

                                                    As for Caine,
                                                    If he were the de-facto real origin, just because his current myth itself is 2500 years old doesn't mean that it isn't much older and the story simply got changed and adjusted over time. Nor does it mean he couldn't have been the 8 - 9000 years old that he supposedly is. It's not like news spread via newspapers during those days, humans were much fewer, and neither can I imagine him running around the first hundred or thousand years shouting 'Look, I'm Caine!'. Myths build up over time, they change, and morph and adjust to the political and social changes of the world.

                                                    Hence sudden Christian myth around, well, that time. Perhaps the older tales about him are simply long forgotten and replaced, just like Christianity's holidays basically were made by taking pagan rites and dates and replacing them with their own versions.


                                                    That said, from a sober perspective...it's the WoD. Come on, folks;
                                                    All the books are written from a certain IC perspective of a specific faction.

                                                    Caine might be the real deal. He might not. Maybe the other creation myths are right.
