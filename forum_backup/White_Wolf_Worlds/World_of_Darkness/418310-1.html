                                            Originally posted by Magicballz View Post
                                            I know they hate each other, but can such event happen or vice versa?
                                            It isn't a question of hate. It is a fundamental matter of supernatural hormones. Consider any time you have met someone who just gave you a bad vibe. I once heard a psychiatrist state that she did not believe in God, but when she met Jeffrey Dahmer for the first time she felt some energy she could only describe as "palpable evil". Consider the chills that run one's spine in those brief weird instantaneous flashes in the dark abandoned places. Further, no known primate fails to make distinctions between members of its same species based on color patterns. These patterns set their bands/troupes apart. Often forced sharing of territory and resources results in violence to drive out the interlopers. Consider the results of this instinct in the actions of prejudice in the more civilized and developed of these species - man.

                                            While the Romeo and Juliet story is a wonderful tragedy - it is still a tragedy where intolerance exists in members of the same species, cultural outlook, and language. The very world view of Shifters, who denounce humans as prey because that is cannibalism, opposes the Vampiric nature wherein humans are prey. A wolf pup might play with a faun, but in the end one is predator and one is food - the momentary frolic will not last.

                                            To say they hate each other is to misrepresent the condition. It is like saying a solar system has a mild allergy to a black hole 100,000 light years in diameter. (That is by the way the size of our galaxy.) All vampires exude the corruption of the Wyrm. All ghosts radiate the corruption of the Wyrm. Their existence is not merely unlife, it is antithetical to the existence of the living. For the shifters, agents of the living Gaia, manifestations of Life in its fiercest defense of self the presence is intolerable no matter how good a guy the vampire might seem.
