                            This is another idea which I'm only experimenting about in my "cyclical Gehenna" idea:

                            The Blood God Ascension Theory of the WoD

                            Vampires aren't actually a race which is ten thousand years old but actually closer to a million with countless primordial pre-human man which was hunted and stalked by said creatures. Whenever they would compete for territory, they would devour each other's blood. The thing about generation is, and very few know this, it actually "stacks" so a vampire who ate a dozen 13th generations would raise a generation if he was 12th to 11th.

                            When you reach 3rd generation or so, you start becoming so powerful or dangerous that you can hunger for vampire blood exclusively and basically devour all of your childer to make the Ascension from 3rd to 1st generation when you promptly ascend to become a kind of supernatural godlike being. This can also be achieved with simple age or power as well, though most vampires do it that way.

                            This actually means the current crop of 4th generation Methusaleh like Menele, Helena, Hazimel, and Ur-Shulgi are the "Antedeluvians" of this era with the 3rd generation of the past having already effectively ascended past the point of relevance. It also means that the other Elders are going to start devouring neonates thanks to irresitable biological urge in order to become the next crop of clan lords.

                            When they devour the majority of childer in this generation, the 3rd generation will then sire their own new crop of offspring with their names as the Clan. They will rule for thousands of years until they, too, feel the need to transcend or are destroyed by their fellows. Indeed, some of the diablerie of the last Antedeluvians was simply because their bodies weren't in torpor so much as abandoned. This is why Saulot, Lasombra, and Tzimosce didn't give a crap about their deaths. Amusingly, this also means Lambach will be a Antedeluvian this cycle of gehenna and he's decidedly up for avenging himself on the clan that disrespected him.

                                My personal World of Darkness borrows from both the Old and the New World of Darkness:

                                - Vampire: The Masquerade exists as is, but the Gangrelare still part of the Camarilla.
                                - Werewolf: The Apocalypse is pretty much 1:1. Pentex and its subsidiaries are the primary business rivals of the various Technocratic fronts.
                                - Mage: The Ascension has Horizon still around (no Avatar Storm or anything). The Disparate Alliance is a thing, and there's no Technocratic civil war (yet). Reality Deviants are captures when possible, but are not actively hunted down.
                                - Wraith I'm still working on to see how I like it best.
                                - Changeling is going to be some kind of mix between Dreaming and Lost. The kithain exist, but there is also the Gentry who don't take kindly to their prizes escaping from their realms. Most of the kithain stay out of these conflicts though, but some cannot help but accidentally get involved,
                                - Hunter: The Vigil has most of its Compacts and Conspiracies exist, but a few are a bit different:
                                -- Aegis Kai Doru: Front for an Arisen cult.
                                -- Cainite Heresy: Run by Caine.
                                -- Cheiron Group: Run by... things. Tend to get into conflict with Pentex and the Progenitors.
                                -- Division Six: A front run by a high-up Technocrat to hunt down Reality Deviants.
                                -- Knights of Saint Adrian: Empowered by a Fallen (a Devil, but not an Earthbound) to hunt down other Fallen.
                                -- Knights of Saint George: Actually a cult worshipping the most powerful demon still in the Abyss.
                                -- Les Mysteres: Let themselves be possessed by Banes.
                                -- Lucifuge: The descendants of the Nephilim.
                                -- Promethean Brotherhood: Their rituals come from a Nephandi.
                                -- Talbot Group: The spirits they encounter are frequently Banes.
                                -- Task Force: VALKYRIE: The highest rungs are infiltrated by Ventrue.
                                -- Utopia Now: Seek to deal with Earthbound and turn their powers to their purposes. (This goes as well as you think it would).
                                -- The Imbued are their own Conspiracy. They have three Creeds, which determines which Edges you get to buy and use: Mercy, Vision and Zeal.
                                -- Finally, Slashers are around as well. They are immune to Disquiet and can form Kinship with Beasts, while at the same time being unable to sate the Hunger of said Beasts.
                                - Mummy is a mix of Resurrection and Curse: you are raised up at the instruction of the Judges but you mostly fight the forces of Apophis and collect artifacts to keep them out of the corruptor's hands.
                                - Promethean exists as is.
                                - Beast exists as is. Note that Beasts are immune to the Disquiet of the Prometheans, their Lairs are immune to the Wasteland and they will never be the target of their Torment.
                                - Demon: The Fallen is how Demons roll. No God-Machine or anything like that: Fallen do their thing in this World of Darkness while trying to deal with the other things that go bump in the night. Lucifer's reveal in LA did not happen.

                                            Currently running a Vampire: The Masquerade "mega-chronicle" (all the published scenarios, with some original stuff to form "connective tissue" between them, and tweaked to fit in Denver, Colorado), and really hammering the "personal horror" theme. Some of the things I've done:

                                            - Included the Fog of Ages thing from Requiem (which also helps explain the "Caine myth" and Noddist apocrypha)
                                            - Also included the Predator's Aura thing
                                            - Any and all blood expenditure counts towards the generational maximum (Want to spend blood to heal, but you've already spent it on Celerity this turn? Too bad, unless you're a low gen.)
                                            - Used my revamped (pun intended) combat rules to make damage more consistent and dangerous (which has lead my players to a) strike first, fast, and decisively; and b) don't get into a fight unless they know they can win)
                                            - Cyclical Gehenna (Fog of Ages again, and possibly canon depending on how one interprets certain source texts)
                                            - Willpower rolls are always done at the characters current (temporary) rating - permanent Willpower is more a "the size of the gas tank" kind of thing
                                            - Celerity works more like Dark Ages, where more dots negates multiple action penalties
                                            - Not really a modification, but different splats use equivalent (or close to) Disciplines and such, like how they're presented in the Antagonists chapter
                                            - Finally, if it's not in V20 (with very few exceptions), it's not in my game (so things like Abyss Mysticism and Anarch Sorcery simply don't exist. Which isn't a problem, as I don't have players interested in those things)

                                            A couple of other, smaller things I'm sure I'm forgetting, but those are the big ones.

                                            Cheers!

                                                            For my WtA LARP we work on an "all sources from 1st to revised are real" approach, working them out that all scenarios coexist.
                                                            • Albrecht has the crown, but he is a big deal mainly for the other septs in his direct vicinity. He is not a global leader. Neither are Gogol, the Margrave or any other NPC or Organisation.
                                                            • There are no global tribe governing bodies. Politics are made in septs, period. If they are bad, the neighbour septs will deal with it, most likely lead by the closest Silver Fang sept.
                                                            • Camps are not organised, for the most part. They are sub-culture like philosophies within the tribes. Some are multi-tribal, because it makes sense, others aren't because it wouldn't make sense, if the were.
                                                            • The Gleaming Eye/Austere Howl conflict isn't usually violent.
                                                            • There are no septs of predominantly regional tribes in parts of the world, where they have no right to be. No Uktena in Japan, or Russia, no Wendigo in Africa.
                                                            • Pure Breed and Past Live/Ancestors are tribe specific. If you don't life by the traditions of your tribe you loose Pure Breed.
                                                            • Septs are usually mono-tribal or bi-tribal. Only a minority of a 2nd tribe is usually allowed to settle within an given septs territory.
                                                            • Garou are born garou and can be identified as such. No "one can't discern the difference", like in W20.
                                                            • Game mechanics don't apply to character backgrounds. The story needs take precedent.
                                                            • All other WoD games basically exist, due to the cheer power we use mages only rarely. Since we ignore the 6th maelstorm and it's events just as much as the TB Silent Strider revised, we usually don't go into Orpheus and it's background.
