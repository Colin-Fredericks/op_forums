    C20 Tone deaf art choices?

    05-07-2017, 05:32 PM
    For the most part, I'm happy and excited about C20, after my first read through of the PDF. There were two art choices that smack me as, at the very least, a bit tone deaf.
    Those would be the pieces for the eshu (p. 92) and redcap (p. 100).

    If I am being generous, I can possibly see the eshu as attempting to be more middle-eastern, but that strains credulity. A kith that is described as taking their name from a Yoruba trickster spirit has basically been whitewashed.

    On the other hand, one of the most celtic in origin of all the fae, the redcap, is suddenly now a black thug. The implications here are mind-boggling, and I don't like the fact that the most violent and thuggish of the kiths is the one of the only representations of poc in the kith pages. (I'm not sure what the boggan is; it is the only other one that could be.)

    All in all, a great book. I just think that somebody fell asleep on those two pieces.

                      I have more gripes with how mundane they all look. Piskies, Selkies kinda fine.

                      Clurichauns, Sidhe (both), and pretty much everyone look, like LARPers with elven ears.

                      Compare that to Thallain arts.

                      Compare boggan with gorgeous Di Terlizzi works.

                      And kith that has inhuman traits... Satyr has something weird with their spine. Troll signature looks nothing, like trolls in their Kithbook, male or female. Redcaps had a specific style, recurrent across the books, now they have Cthulhu growing on their face. I'm not sure about Pooka art. I mean, I'm all about breaking stereotypes and you may say, that Pooka suffer from Fishmalk reputation, but it could've been so cool joke if text reflected that - when I was scrolling the page I was expecting to see something like Schrodinger from Hellsing OVA, not Wolverine Knock-Off. And don't get me started on Sluagh and their signature style is thrown out of the window...

                      What's important is that for changelings, kith is their race. And many Redcaps (Eiluned, Liam, Sluah) are experiencing distrust and discrimination. Being black Redcap only adds to the pool. Now, I would've left Redcap black, but put him in the rich lawyer suit and added Cheshire Cat grin on his face.

                      Even in the land of dreams, others think of you as a ganger and monster... Isn't that shitty? Isn't that unfair? Rising above what's expected of you, would be ten times as hard. But you have a dream and determination. You'll use every opportunity, every advantage at your disposal. You'll use your mundane influence to teach Sidhe, that you're not to be trifled with. You'll use your changeling gifts to outperform your mortal competition. They don't believe in magic? Lack of belief doesn't pardon them from consequences. Some might say, that what you do is questionable. You say: unconventional. And smile, always smile. Let them be always... on edge.

                      Tell, how would you draw the signature characters?

                              The eshu character is supposed to be Dice, and he's described in my art notes as Middle Eastern. I have no idea why he's so white, though I do know that the art-folks had trouble finding pictures of Dice.
