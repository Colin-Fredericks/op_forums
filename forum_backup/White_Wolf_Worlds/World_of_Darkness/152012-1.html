                                    Here's a few I picked up

                                    1. If it doesn't die from C4, thermite, white phosphorous, and a flamethrower being applied liberally to their face, leave the region
                                    2. If you shoot it in the head, cut off it's head, and then burn the body, and it gets back up, it's a good guy or the BBEG
                                    3. Don't underestimate a punk with a pistol
                                    4. Never think you're the top of the food chain
                                    5. Don't trust anything "holy" ever
                                    6. There is no rule 6.
                                    7. Always wear sunglasses at night time
                                    8. Always carry a lighter and a pack of cigarettes
                                    9. If it's moving and it's not suppose to, duct tape.
                                    10. Never go into the woods alone.
                                    11. Silver weapons aren't worth it, you just need to fire more bullets.
                                    12. Bend the pins on all your grenades so it takes effort to remove them or duct tape down the strike lever.
                                    13. If it's got an evil aura, don't engage it in melee ever.
                                    14. When in doubt, C4.
                                    15. The crazy homeless guy is probably right to some degree.
                                    16. Avoid the sewers unless you have explosives.
                                    17. Always carry enough C4 to blow up a building.
                                    18. Don't underestimate the power of 300 gallons of gasoline in a van tied to a cellphone detonator.
                                    19. Being shot hurts.
                                    20. Wear a mask when hunting. Any mask that covers part of your face is enough or see rule #7.
                                    21. Distance and a sniper rifle are your best bet
                                    22. Aim for the head.
                                    23. If it has fangs, it's not your friend.
                                    24. Never shake someone's hand, ever.
                                    25. Assume someone is always watching.
                                    26. Learn sign language.
                                    27. Only subdue allies, shoot/burn/disembowel the rest.
                                    28. Never take hostages from the firefight.
                                    29. Always carry a tool bag
                                    30. Leave your cellphone at home, use AES-2048 Encrypted Walkie Talkies, and even then speak in riddles.
                                    31. Change callsigns every mission.
                                    32. Never compromise in the face of armageddon. Mission isn't over till you're all dead or you're at a safehouse.
                                    33. Learn to use a hammer and nails.

                                    Check out my Sorcerer 20th homebrew and my update to Highlander: the Gathering for 20th Anniversary edition.
