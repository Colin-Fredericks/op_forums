    A Post-Apocalyptic Setting for Hunter: the Reckoning

    08-24-2023, 06:42 PM
    This setting is a basic Golden Age Sci Fi Post-apocalyptic setting. In this case a plague wiped out 95% of Humanity. The Wererats, allied with the Red Talons summoned the plague.

    Before finding out the Rat shifters and the Red Talons were responsible, the Mages of many factions allied with the Changelings destroyed the vast majority of the Nephandi and their Sabbat and Black Spiral Dancer allies.

    None of this restores civilization. The Changelings have gone into slumber for a few generations. The Mages created bases to both teach new Mages and to preserve and revive civilization. But the Mages needed allies. They chose the Hunters. It's worked out better than you'd think.

    Now the Hunters are hunting again. Disguised as peddlers and other commercial travelers, the Hunters both fight the monsters and help rekindle civilization.

              The most common supernaturals that the PC Hunters will be in conflict with are Wrights and corpses animated by either magic (Zombies) or Wraiths (Revenants).

              Wrights are discussed in the Vampire book Sins of the Blood. Basically, when a Cannite Vampire goes to zero Humanity they become a Wright.

              Wrights are basically cunning animals in human form. Most higher brain functions like language are gone. Empathy exists only as a predator analyzing its prey. Wrights still have all the vulnerabilities of Vampires and new ones too. Holy symbols from the culture the Wright was raised in terrify Wrights. Garlic repulses Wrights. Like many supernatural creatures of folklore, Wrights are normally obsessive compulsive. Throwing down a handful of small objects like seeds or stick pins can often distract a Wright who will obsessively count the lot.

              Wrights generally have the disciplines of Potence, Celerity, and Fortitude but rarely at high degrees. It isn't rare for Wrights to have the lower levels of Auspex, Obfuscation, Protean, Animalism, Presance, and Domination. But the higher levels require creative thought, and aren't possible for a Wright. Thaumuturgy and the other special powers of most obscure clans and Bloodlines require to much of a wright's brain for them to be used. The one exception is Melpominee. Perhaps music is simply more basic than most people think. However, the Daughters of Concaphaney are very rare.

                  Zombies and Revenants seem similar but they are different threats. Zombies ​​​​seem to be animated by the chaotic forces released by the plague. They are shambling mindless corpses hungry for the flesh of the living. They rot slowly over time and few new Zombies have been created in decades. They are now rare. But the outbreaks are still terrifying.

                  Revenants are animated by Wraiths and occasionally Spectors. Revenants rot far more quickly than Zombies but with the various powers of Wraiths, they can sometimes pass for human, if only for a short time. Revenants have the intelligence and purposes of the Wraiths or Spectors that animate them.

                  Risen ​​do exist in this setting, but they are, as always, very rare.

                  Wrights can occasionally embrace a mortal turning them into Vampires. These are always weak Vampires with low humanity and little understanding of their powers. Most end up as Wrights. Although a tiny minority to advance and become more powerful and skilled.

                  Vampires have survived, but very few. Most are in Topor waiting for better times. But a few are building up their powerbase. Hunters are determined to break these.

                      I'll describe the conditions of each of the major supernatural groups.

                      Vampires : Neither the Camarilla nor the Sabbat still exist in any real sense. The conditions of other smaller groups like the Black Hand or the Independent Clans aren't known or, given the conditions of this world knowable.

                      All known Vampire⁷s are trying to build up some kind of secure powerbase. They are both paranoid and generally sneaky. Watch the old film Vampyr. Look at how Marguerite Chopin (the vampire) manipulates the entire community and rules it from the shadows with a ghoul catspaw. Even powerful Vampires hide in the shadows until they have to strike.

                      Garou and Fera: Shattered and in isolation. A few groups, like the Werebears and the Children of Gaia have allied with the Mages. They many work to heal the environment. Some shifters are attacking human communities to limit human population numbers. Most other shifters hate these attacks but do little against them.

                      Some Garou hunt Hunters in order to weaken humanity.

                      Wraiths: The Shadowlands are in chaos. Vast storms have dominated the realms of the dead since the plague. A few places are somehow sheltered from the storms gather what Wraiths are near. These places tend to be the kinds of large buildings that attract ghost legends (read Ghostlands there is a type of building that attracts ghost stories even if they lack ghosts).

                      Risen in this world are sometimes just escaping the horror of these storms.

                      Changelings: The Changelings and related creatures are almost all withdrawn from the world. A few Changelings are still around, these are always working with the mages.

                      Mages: The Council of the Traditions is radically changed but still around. Several other groups have joined the Council (Now the Council of Seventeen, referencing West African numerology) and are mainly working for base survival. Some groups have become serious threats.

                          Mages are working under altered conditions. With such a low world population Paradox is massively weakened. Everywhere on Earth Magick is either Coincidental or Uncanny (to steal a cool term from Victorian Mage ​​​​​​). Difficulties for performing Magick is about as it was before the year 1000AD. (later on I'll post the book were these lower levels are stated).

                          A further benefit that effects both Mages and Hunters is slowed aging. Mages find their aging is slowed by the square of their Arete. Thus an Arete 2 Mage ages at one quarter the rate of Sleepers. ​Arete 4 Mages age at on sixteenth the rate of Sleepers. Hunters age at one third the rate of ordinary people.

                          On the downside, Quintessence is far rarer. Many Nodes have either lowered their level or gone dormant. Although not all Nodes or Caerns have been affected. It's dramatically harder for Mages to increase Arete or increase or learn new Spheres.

                          Several powerful Mages say it's as if the world was numb.
