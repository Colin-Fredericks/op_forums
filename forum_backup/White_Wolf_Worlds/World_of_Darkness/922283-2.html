    I have modified my WoD a lot and have talked about these modifications endlessly. But this is a thread dedicated to setting hacks, so here we go....

    1. The Revised Metaplot never happened. Gangrel are still in the Camarilla, the Stargazers are still in the Garou Nation, the Ravnos are still a full clan, there was no Week of Nightmares and no Avatar Storm. By and large, the entirety of Revised is declared non-canon in my games.

    2. There are no Kuei-Jin and no Fallen in my games. KOTE and Demon are declared non-canon, and East Asia's cities are dominated by Cainites.

    3. The themes of my games reject personal horror and instead embrace a mix of action-horror and dark politics. Nothing wrong with personal horror but it's not for me, so instead I go for action-horror and political intrigue, which I vastly prefer instead. My games can best be summed up as "Black Lagoon with Fangs".

    4. The aesthetics and atmosphere of my WoD games are neither Gothic nor Punk. Instead, I prefer a more anime-inspired approach. Again, nothing wrong with Gothic-Punk, but it's not for me.

    5. The fan-game Senshi: The Merchandising is at least somewhat canon in my WoD games. Sailor Moon is definitely part of my WoD head canon. It may sound silly, but I like it.

                        I modified the Humanity System of Vampire as such:

                        My modifications are simpler:

                        * A vampire does not need to make a Humanity roll if they kill in self-defense or the defense of an innocent person after Humanity 7. This doesn't mean they feel GOOD about it, quite the opposite, but it doesn't cause them axiomatically to become worse people. This prevents cops and soldiers from all becoming Humanity 5 psychopaths.

                        * Vampires do not have to make Humanity rolls for killing for blood or gain after Humanity 4.

                        * Humanity 1-3 vampires are only the most sickest murderous degenerates alive.

                        * Frenzying and killing someone in Hunger causes Humanity loss until you're down to Humanity 4 and 3 if it kills children other innocents.

                        * Executing someone doesn't lower your humanity past 6 if they're genuinely evil or a viable threat.

                        * Most Camarilla Elders stabilize at Humanity 4 and 5 with the general attitude of "Do not kill people for fun or in sick games but kill people to preserve the Masquerade or gain power." They have the same attitude as mobsters.

                        * Most Camarilla Ancilla generally have Humanity 5 or 6 with Humanity 6 vampires being ones who try not to be bad people but are tough enough to survive Kindred's harsh society. Humanity 7 vampires exist but they tend to be outliers.

                        * Humanity 7-8 "friendly" vampires are painfully moralizing and usually either separate themselves from Kindred society or fall off the wagon. These are the vampires struggling with their curse actively. They tend to be Neonates or Thin Bloods.

                        * Humanity 9-10 vampires generally are hermits among their kind or pacifists who do their best to subsist on animal blood and use their powers actively for good. They tend to be Thin Bloods.

                        * Suicide is not uncommon for Camarilla vampires embraced with high humanity after their first few (accidental-usually) kills. Those that survive tend to have strong wills to live or connections to others.

                        * Most Sabbat vampires generally go all the way down to 2 and 3 Humanity where they behave as serial killers and psychopaths. They tend to live fast and die young unless they have Paths, which are really hard to do. Wassail is not uncommon but taken care of by the Packs. The Vinculum holds and prevents them from attacking their former packmates, which makes them easily dealt with. Quite a few are staked then unstaked in Camarilla territory as the "leave the dog in the woods" thing.

                        * Humanity 0 requires an "Act of Ultimate Darkness" like diablerie, demonic pact, sadistic torture, or slaughtering a school bus full of children. Personal betrayals also work for this but generally its diablerie.

                        * Diablerie is not only an automatic Humanity loss, it's an automatic PATH loss even on paths that extol it. This results in diablerie becoming something which is done only as a very important rite or as an intense sanity-blasting experience.

                        With my modified Humanity rules, I might also steal something from Bloodlines but I admit I never implemented it.

                        * Points of Humanity can be restored to a Kindred which go above and beyond their current humanity score to do something good at the cost of an advantage or personal sacrifice.

                        A Kindred who rushes into a burning building to save a mortal, a Kindred who destroys an evil artifact despite its potential boons, a Kindred who helps a Salubri escape at the cost of his relationship with the Tremere, and so on can get a point back.

                                            Paths of Enlightenment

                                            * All Paths of Enlightenment have two variations. There's the Humane and Monster roads. The Humane Roads have Self-Control rather than Instinct even as they still have Conviction. They also have modified Hierarchy of Sins which justify murder under specific circumstances but tend to avoid mass diablerie or cruelty.

                                            * Paths cannot be sensed by other Kindred with no one able to tell when or where someone is a Path follower or a Humanity follower.

                                            * Path followers of 10 tend to radiate majesty the way other vampires radiate humanity at 10. They do not put vampires or humans uncomfortable but have a mildly beguiling presence. This has no mechanical benefit other than they do not look corpse-like but simply exotic.

                                            * Paths of Enlightenment are things which require immense acts of faith and meditation to achieve. To start on them is to essentially convert to a new religion and mindset before spending each and every day focusing on them.

                                            * It takes two to four months of intense study to convert to a Path and you can exchange a point of Humanity for a point of Path for each success on an Intelligence + Occult role when it's done. When done, you lose any remaining Humanity points. The difficulty is determined by your role-playing.

                                            * The Sabbat's understanding of Paths are actually incomplete with most understanding incapable of being achieved past 5 due to the Vinculum, Pack Politics, and Sabbat propaganda. To seek higher than Path 5 is to do your own research or have a mentor.

                                            * Members of the False Hand can achieve Path 10 as can True Hand members, Inconnu, Baali, Followers of Set, Assamites, Ravnos Elders, Old Clan Tzimisce, Nagaraja, and several Camarilla Gehenna cults (where Kindred spirituality survives).

                                            The late Cardinal Moncada was one of the few Sabbat experts on Kindred spirituality, not just the Path of Night, and his death has greatly weakened the Sabbat there.

                                            * It is suggested Path Followers can achieve Golconda while others believe they achieve something more akin to the Kuei-jin's Bodhivisata state. Given the nature of Golconda and whether it relates to God's forgiveness or not versus an Eastern state of becoming one with the Beast, it's anyone's guess which is true.
