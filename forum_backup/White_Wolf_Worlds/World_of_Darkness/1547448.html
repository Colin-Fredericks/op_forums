    Trench Crusade Meets World of Darkness

    01-02-2025, 05:07 AM
    So Imagine the following scenario. The history of Wod goes more or less how it goes in canon, vampires skulking in the shadows, werewolves guarding they forests and mages studying reality. Until the First Crusade where the Knights Templar found Something in Jerusalem that drove them to throw open the gates to hell in an act simply known as the first Heresy and user in a new era of madness and death as the demons run rampant in Europe and in response rally the forces of the faithful on a crusade to retake Jerusalem and kick the demons back into hell. The crusade current year is 1914 AD, Marking the 900th year of this Crusade.

    Now Most of Central Europe are reduced into a mess of mud and trenches as the Faithful and Heretic fight for inches of land. Infernal engines of war spew forth from ruined Jerusalem to do battle with they equivalents from blessed war factories of New Antioch and the many Homunculi grown by the alchemists of the Iron Sultanate and processions of armed Pilgrims still march toward the infernal ruins of Jerusalem still intent on reclaiming it from the forces of Hell.

          This would... take a lot more work than I think you're considering. The Trench Crusade's setup is functional for a small war-game that just needs enough window dressing to create a unique atmosphere to pull in some mini warriors from other games they're getting tired of. Most wargames never build up the following for deep lore exploration like Battletech of WH40K do, so they don't worry about how fast and loose they're playing with things.

          RPGs have to be more invested in functional lore because the focus on playing individual characters means you need to understand an AU well enough to actually play someone living in it. Wargames can get away with tossing around words like "blessed" or "infernal," to just handwave the logistical nonsense of a 900 year war at this sort of scale. In our real history, "The Crusades" in most people's minds are really just the first three ones that actually involved Christian military expeditions into the Middle East. They were immensely draining on Europe in pretty every imaginable way, and the rest of the Crusades were largely fought in Europe to either fend off powers trying to capitalize on that, persecute religious minorities (both non-Christian and Christian alike), or just Church sanctioned wars between Christian kingdoms as kings tried to rob each other to recover the former wealth of their nations.

          In a RPG, a 900 year war is either powered by forces some immense the PCs are likely unable to engage with them in a meaningful fashion (and thus vastly limit players that want to do things like end the war by doing daring raids on vital enemy strategic points), or is going to be so grimdark the Garou would all be dead of Harano a few centuries ago.

          And all of that is before we even try to get to melding these to settings which is... a bigger ask than I think it sounds like on the surface.
