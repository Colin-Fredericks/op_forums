    180) Sir Alfred of Winchester AKA Loki AKA Dance with Coyotes

    Born Alfred of Winchester in England in 1033 AD as the third son of an important noble family, he got "educated" about the unfairness of the world at large by a powerful Ravnos witch.
    Once a stalwart defender of the institutional order, his Clan Curse manifested in the vice of breaking the laws of societies.
    Traveling Europe in search of a land where he wouldn't be tempted to breaking its laws, he found out the problem wasn't in the law themselves but in the special gusto he felt in breaking the laws.
    After becoming an elder in XV century he adopted the name of the norse god Loki. He deemed it appropriate for himself, due to his mastery of illusions, his own growing ego and his penchant of causing "mischief" in every domain he visited. He was a well known troublemaker to every Prince of northern europe at the time, due to his temptation of breaking some vampiric traditions. But as a 7th generation elder he managed to survive and do what he wanted.
    When the Anarch Revolt exploded he was in truth envious of the rebellious faction that was actually shattering institutions reputed old as the world itself. He was very tempted to help them. He, in fact, did that and helped a few anarch packs destroy a few methuselah. But he was still an elder and the nascent sabbat was becoming increasingly diablerie-prone.
    He had to flee and sought new lands in the americas, one of the few kindreds to take that option.
    Some old feelings and desires resurfaced and he tried to live amongst the natives in search of a completely different society.
    Yet his vice hit again and again, tempting him into breaking the traditions of the american people. But he always tried to do so without killing and without violence, when possible. He sincerely enjoyed living amongst the natives, or at least as much as an old vampire can. He kept wandering from tribe to tribe, breaking local laws, but the population came to respect him as a wandering natural force of the night, who could talk with the animals, withstand any mortal weapon, and made dreams come true albeit for a mere moment.
    Amongst the emigrated europeans he still kept breaking the laws of the old world, and this new player, this new vampire society known as the Camarilla was... a funny plaything. With strongly enforced vampiric laws, Traditions. Laws to break. Alfred was descending into inhumanity, indulging in his vice too much. He wasn't violent of sadistic, yet. But it was a more subtle way for his Ravnos Beast to drag him down.
    Something changed when in following a new tribe of natives he met this shaman. He thought he was a shaman. What was surprising was not only he discovered Alfred was a Cainite, but that when he caught the vampire in the act of desecrating an important sacred ground in order to break very old traditions... The shaman joined him! He explained it was to teach the tribe a lesson. It was Coyote's idea, and that Alfred was acting his part as a trickster that must exist to subvert society's known and comfortable order and teach the people lessons. Or so the shaman, actually a rare werecoyote, believed. The Garou amongst the human tribe didn't. Outnumbered and on the run, the odd pair somehow ended up being vital for defeating a Wyrm creature brought to the americas by the Wyrmbringers that was hidden under the sacred ground and corrupting the humans slowly. The Garou thanked the Nuwisha and the Ravnos they called "Dance with Coyotes" (this legend still told amongst the Uktena). Alfred licked this new name and adopted it. He parted ways from the werecoyote a few months later, but he tried to learn more about this "trickster" role he talked about.
    In 21th century US he travels from city to city breaking Traditions. Being almost a millenia old there's nothing much anyone can do to stop him, but he's surely on a good path to enter the Red List and a few Archons actually wants his head. And some Sabbat people remember his face from his "Loki" days, and they're very old too now.
    But the trickster still wanders, and where he comes the unnatural order of society gets disrupted, new Ravnos get Embraced without permission, some minor Masquerade breaches happen without knowing who made them, some Kindred meet Final Death. But Dance with Coyotes is deceptive, quiet, careful, and powerful.
    No one knows how he survived the Week of Nightmares and maybe no one will, for he has no friends nor allies.
    He's not a person anymore. He's a natural force of the night, as the natives believed. Or so he fancies himself. He tricks himself into believing so. For the Ravnos Beast is a devious one and it will be what finally brings this old monster down.

        181) A Malkavian technomancer who was Embraced at age 15 or so. As such, he calls himself "the Eternal Boy Genius". Needless to say, his derangement is Megalomania.

        He just...showed up, one night, at a small domain. With his presence, the domain's Kindred population reached a nice, round half-dozen. Mostly, his "peers" are a handful of Anarchs and Caitiff, and one Autarkus with no apparent interest in politics. As such, when the "Boy Genius" came in and insisted on being recognized as the Prince, no one was in any position or inclination to argue. After all, he might have strange, technological blood sorcery, but he's still just one Neonate. If matters got really bad, they could all just leave.

        The source of the Malkavian's "Science(!)" is a mystery to everyone. Save an offhand remark, where he grumbled angrily that the "Sons" will rue the night they "denied true brilliance". What is known is that, when angered he can spit lightning like the best of them. He's not mad enough to endanger the Masquerade, but he longs for the day when everyone - Kindred and Kine alike - can witness and recognize his greatness.

        In the meantime, he toils in his Haven, expanding his knowledge of technomancy. The place hosts a lab, and a handful of ghouled assistants, eager to please (and learn from) their master's genius.

        But his activities are not all contemplative or experimental in nature. The Malkavian has designs for the nearest Kindred population center. Like all good Megalomaniacs, he resents being relegated to the outskirts of vampiric civilization, desiring to gain the power and adulation he "rightly deserves". So what if the Kindred in the big city have centuries on him? That just means they're horribly outmoded. Behind the times. Unfit to adapt to the age of technological wonders, as he is. They, too, shall fall, acting as stepping stones for his own glorious ascent.

            182) A Ventrue antitribu who, like his Bishop sire, suffers from the Flesh of the Corpse flaw (V20, p. 483). While his sire has weathered enough battles over the centuries that he must go covered in flowing robes and an ornate ceremonial helmet, the childe has only taken light damage that is visible on the face. Every night, he strategically applies band-aids and minor bandages, to appear as only lightly hurt. It's a serviceable cover for occasional mortal contact, but hardly a permanent solution. Which is unfortunate, given that the Ventrue is a member of the Order of St. Blase, who work closely with mortals on behalf of more inhuman Sabbat.

            Part of the character's desire to work in St. Blase is out of disgust for those same inhuman Sabbat. Every time he meets the more "typical" Cainite, they always give him shit for "playing with his food", and for him not being a warrior like his sire. His sire mostly goes fully clad for the sake of pragmatism, not because he despises his appearance (also, he gets sadistic glee subjecting the unknowing to his horrid countenance). The childe, however, tolerates his condition much less. He was vain as a mortal, and his vanity rankles at the loathsome state of his body. Not just as it is, but that it will only ever get worse from here. Over a long enough time frame, he'll look just like his sire, eventually. He hates that fact most of all. That and his sire, who inflicted the fate on him. This is not what he signed up for when he was promised immortality.

            As such, he avoids combat like the plague. Any sort of danger, really. He invests highly in Fortitude, simply in the hope of mitigating or avoiding further damage. Keeps ghouled guards around, just to be safe.

            All the while, he burrows his tendrils into mortal society. Not content merely pulling strings on behalf of the Sword of Caine, he's interested in acquiring power. It keeps various packs off his back, and alerts him to Camarilla spies looking to put down roots in Sabbat-held territory. Most of all, though, he has his sights on his sire. That hateful, hideous creature who inflicted the defective curse on him. One night, that old bastard WILL pay. Everything else is negotiable, including the character's own sect loyalty...

                183) They call him Johnny Reb, because he considers himself the last true Confederate soldier. At least, the only one "alive" who still refuses to surrender to the hated Yankees.

                During the Civil War (the "War for Sourthen Independence" as he calls it), he served in the Confederate army for his home in Alabama. Not for anything like "protecting slavery" (though, on par with the time and place, he has no affection for African Americans), but because the Union invaded his home. After years of fighting - wherein he took many bullets at different times and survived - he finally seemed to meet his match during a black powder explosion. Burned over much of his body, he lay dying in a hospital tent. For that, though, the fire in his heart for the hated Northerners still burned, and he approached death still trying to get up and fight. This display of rage - of utter commitment to his cause - impressed a Brujah that stalked the tents to feed on the dying.

                To this day, Johnny Reb sports a Confederate uniform (though a reproduction; all the originals rotted away long ago). Of course, he's inclined to cover it with a long coat, when he must go under cover. When on the prowl against Northerners, however, he wears his gray openly. He wants to remind the "damned Yankees" that the South WILL rise again. For his crusade - and because he cannot bring himself to feed from Southern whites (Prey Exclusion, V20 p. 485) - Johnny Reb spends much of his time stalking the streets of states north of the Mason-Dixon line. When he cannot stomach Yankee lands anymore, he stays in the moldering house of his family's ancestral state. Feeding from impoverished black farmers, and quietly disposing of tourists and travelers stupid enough to come trespassing on his property. It's not HIS fault the city declared his house abandoned.

                Presently, he's back home, working with Camarilla forces to jettison the Sabbat from Alabama. Some of the Camarilla are damned Yankees themselves, but others are right and proper antebellum sorts, so they're alright with him. It's those Sabbat who are the problem right now, who come out from Mexico to try doing what the Union couldn't.

                Naturally, very few Kindred born in the last hundred years can stand "Johnny Reb". Unless, of course, they are Southerners themselves. Especially the neo-Confederates. For his own part, Johnny Reb considers the modern acolytes of Dixie to be well meaning, but ultimately failures. "They just sit in their homes, watching this 'RED Network' on the Idiot Box, and bitching about how miserable they are. So few want to do a damn thing but whine. Those that do spend more time lynchin' ******s than something productive. A god-damned waste. Makes me want to stab my guts and puke blood."

                    184) It started as a curiosity, a one in a billion chance of ever coming to pass. But one night, the stars aligned and a member of clan Ventrue decided to see what would happen if they gave the embrace to one of the low born street trash.                     If somebody who displayed no history of leadership qualities was suddenly inducted into the clan of Lords, how would they respond to the situation? Would they remain nothing more than a common miscreant, acting no better than an oafish thug? Would they act like a spoiled child and go mad with power after discovering what they were capable of? Or, would they lift themselves up from such meager beginnings and do something more productive with their lives?

                    185) The wrath of a Koldun is not something one should ever brush aside lightly, but such lessons are wasted on modern kindred, who have rarely encountered such fury. Even worse is the fact Koldunic Sorcery is almost exclusive to the fiendish Clan Tzimisce, who have seen fit to let the art fade into obscurity.

And some among the clan have even begun to question the benefits of petitioning him for apprenticeship, so that other warrens might make use of such a rare and potent ability.

                        186) An amnesiac Caitiff, who awoke one night tired, hungry, and undead. He retains no recollection of who he was, how he got there, or how he became a vampire.

                        He's not completely ignorant, of course. He knows language - five of them - and understands how to operate machinery, perform basic computer functions, and navigate the urban landscape. He knows the state of the world in general, current events, and, most especially, History. He could explain the sequence of events that lead to the French Revolution, the dynasties of China, and the theories about the Bronze Age Collapse. About his own life, he can say nothing.

                        What he discovered quite early in his unlife, however, was that he was extremely physically capable. Highly skilled in Athletics (especially Parkour), Martial Arts (Soft Style), and fighting with sticks and staffs. He learned he knew them in his first nights, evading and repelling attacks by a Sheriff. Which was weird (not just for the obvious reason), because when the Sheriff elected to talk rather than hunt, the Caitiff learned that he was targeted for his face. A face shared by a mysterious malcontent and ne'er-do-well, who made trouble for Kindred and Kine alike.

                        Adrift in the world and without other purpose, the Caitiff elected to track down his doppelganger. Their shared visage provides the only lead concerning who he was, and perhaps why he cannot remember.

                                188) A Gangrel Mariner, embraced more or less in the usual Gangrel way. Her sire found her swimming at night, embraced her on a whim, then left her alone for months to see how well she adapted. Adapt she did, deciding to unlive in the ocean full time. For ten years, she pursued her great hobby: scouring the seabed for treasure. "Treasure", in this case, being whatever materials she considered interesting or historically relevant. Fancy lures, sunken cargo, anchors, the occasional statue looted from a derelict ship, bottle caps, etc. She stored it all in the bulk of a yacht, sent to the seafloor by an errant storm. It wasn't the finest collection in the world, but it was hers.

                                So imagine her ire when some divers came crawling around her hoard. She'd been away at the time, and only arrived in time to see the mortals hauling up the last of her treasures. She lashed out at one of them, clawing his face and ripping his goggles off. Alas, the humans managed to swim far enough towards the surface to reach the sunlit layer, where she couldn't pursue. It seemed as though her hoard was lost...except the goggles had the name of the diver written on them, identifying them as the man's property.

                                With a name, the man could be tracked. It probably helped that he now sported a wicked set of facial scars.

                                After a decade chilling under the sea, the Mariner reluctantly returned to the land. Warped by her Clan curse, she made landfall more fishy than when she'd left. But it wasn't a problem a stolen hoodie and other clothes couldn't hide. She's now tracked the man to a city, where he supposedly lives. Unfortunately, entering the city means she must now contend with Camarilla law, and the bothersome presence of other Kindred. So long as she continues her search, she had to play Camarilla games. She cannot wait to retrieve her things and leave, returning to the depths of the ocean once more. Until then, she makes her haven in a drainage pipe down by the river, where none but the Nosferatu would even notice.

                                                    198) A Tremere who was Embraced for her ability as a Natural Linguist, as Hermetics highly value extensive knowledge of various languages. But when the Embrace took, she discovered she had yet another Merit to her name: Magic Resistance. On the upside, it protected her from Thaumaturgy and other magics. On the downside, it prevented her from ever developing skill in blood sorcery of any sort. Given that this is Clan Tremere, it was far more of a bane than a boon. Ensuring that she'd never be able to advance in the Pyramid.

                                                    Not that she doesn't have her uses. Clan Tremere does not squander resources. In her early years, she was relegated to grunt work - keeping records, obtaining rare ritual ingredients, erasing inconvenient memories, and retrieving vessels on behalf of "her betters" (basically anyone with actual Thaumaturgy skill and a week of seniority over her). But eventually, her talents as a linguist were put to use translating Clan documents and magical texts. Because while most Hermetic sorcerers pride themselves on the ability to read books in their original language, this is not always practical. Especially in chantries stationed outside the English-speaking world.

                                                    Because of her responsibilities - and the often sensitive nature of documents in need of translation - she travels extensively. Hopping from chantry to chantry, doing her work and then being shuffled off to a different region. It's a thankless job, because a good translation is invisible, while a bad translation leads only to scorn (at best). However, her job has advantages. Namely that, especially when it comes to hoary Old World Tremere who stubbornly refuse to learn the vulgar English tongue, she ends up being the pathway through which considerably important information is transferred. A great deal of knowledge of operations and magical theory must cross her desk and be subjected to her full attention. She can turn such information to her purposes, even if knowing how a ritual is cast does her no personal good. Indeed, it's because she's known to be incapable of blood magic that makes others ignore her. So long as she draws no attention to herself, she's practically invisible.

                                                    Her mobility is also a hidden asset. Sure, she can't build up a local power base when so often on the move, but she can network like no one's business. She'd never be so foolish as to directly sell Tremere secrets to those outside the Clan, of course. That would be suicide. But she knows what she knows, as well as who else knows it. That networking comes in handy when allowing Tremere to get in touch with non-Tremere who might need their services, or vice versa. Not to mention how well she can network within the pyramid, since she knows lore that ambitious Warlocks would kill for.

                                                    She's a woman who, by twist of fate, slipped through the cracks. But in slipping through, she found her own niche.

                                                            200) Kate Summers (Stray Winfield) - Sheriff of Gary
                                                            Clan – Lasombra Antitribu
                                                            Concept – Runaway Sabbat

                                                            In life Kate’s family was well off enough and she had smarts that promised her a spot in a good school all in all things were looking good. But then it all came crashing down she was reject by her collage of choice and around the same time her parents were murdered. If that wasn’t enough she lost all her inheritance because apparently her parents had a back log of unpaid taxes (though she never knew her parents to have tax troubles). Left with nothing and all her hopes of a future dashed away she found herself forced into prostitution. One time she sold her body for money one time and something in her snapped she swore that she would never ever again degrade herself like that. The world was a cruel and unfeeling place and she would have to be just as cruel to survive. It started with small things mugging shoplifting but gradually she worked her way up in the criminal underworld becoming more and more heartless her thinking being in this world your either the abused or the abuser so she might as well be the abuser.

                                                            That’s when she was approached by her sire all of Kate’s misfortune had been no accident. In the Lasombra tradition Anastasio had destroyed Kate’s life to test her, as he had many other test subjects. It was Anastasio who was responsible for her not getting into collage and her parent’s murder and her losing all her inheritance and now after climbing the ranks to carve out a niche for herself in the criminal underworld he found her worthy of the embrace. At first she was delighted with what she had become thinking now she never be the abused always and forever would she be the abuser. But such fantasies quickly fall away as she realized not only was kindred society just as abusive as the kine's society but she was expected to be a foot soldier in some mad cult’s holy war. Kate never much bought into the whole religion thing so fighting and dying (again) because of some shit some asshole wrote down in a book a gazillion years ago didn’t sit well with her, “Cain and antediluvians my ass I know a cult when I see it”. So she ran away abounding her name so as not to be found calling herself Stray.

                                                            After many years she found herself in dump called Gary run by this guy calling himself Modius. Somehow he found out about her being a Lasombra and a former Sabbat despite her best effort to conceal those facts. Modius didn’t really find out about these things himself it was Annabelle who found out and told Modius to offer her a position as his sheriff. It wasn’t that hard to convince her after all the title appealed to her personality and Modius offered protection from the Sabbat. Really he will sell her out in instance if it will save his skin but protection is what he sold to her. Of course Stray returns the favor and will dump Modius should it ever become convenient for her but for now sheriff of Gary is a pretty sweet gig. The two Sabbat scouts in Chicago are under orders to kill her on sight but they think she is in Chicago itself rather than in Gary so they haven’t found her yet. Stray doesn’t yet know of the scouts existence but if she ever finds out she will rat them out to Modius, Lodin and anybody else that will listen (maybe she will even enlist the help of the players assuming their not trying to kill her as well).

                                                            Roleplaying hints – unless somebody is in your way or Modiu’s way be quiet and stick to the shadows you don’t want anybody to find out you’re a Lasombra ex Sabbat, be weary of reflective surfaces. However if do need to get involved be ruthless and tough it’s the only way to survive in this world, mercy is used only by the weak who simply get stepped on. But never use your obtenebration in front of others unless you absolutely have no other choice. Avoid talking about your clan but if you have to give somebody an answer tell them you’re Brujah.
