                              You bastard... I'd love a copy of that. Ravenloft and I go back to Day One when it was printed in '83. I had already been running it as a setting/"reality" for several years when the first black box setting came out in '90. Hell, coincidentally enough, I am staring at a stack of old Ravenloft models/books over in the corner that I (eventually) have to re-sort into new tubs for storage. (I have at least 3 copies of the original module -- math/architectural errors on the castle's height and all -- as well as this, that and the other from over the years... but not Carnivale... at least not in a solid form....)

                              A couple friends let out the "geek gasp" when I happened to take out the original black-box map of The Core, pre-Azalin blowing Markovia out into the middle of the "sea". It's one thing to have all of whatever crap on the computer, but something else entirely to have the physical item (book, map, module, what-have-you) in hand or on a table. It's just a completely different thing....

                              I have been around here for waaaayyyy too fucking long...
