                    There is a great children's series called Geronimo Stilton and the Kingdom of Fantasy. Mild mannered busy body mouse is suddenly taken to another realm of high fantasy and must overcome his own failings and help the people of the realm. My kid got into this one and so I was drawn in too. The Thomas Covenant Chronicles is an adult version. With books the genre of fantasy is rife with series about the things Changeling is trying to reclaim. I'm not really into the modern stuff so I wouldn't know much that would completely mirror the duality aspect.

                    Adventure Time and the Amazing World of Gumball are great childling primers. The episode of AWoG about the board game Dodge or Dare is great.

                    Music...Avantasia the Metal Opera 1&2 by Tobias Sammet.

                    The Lego Movie, Secondhand Lions.... There are honestly lots of fantasy themed movies that work better for the Dreaming aspect of the game. Too numerous to list. There are lots that hit on the duality or hiding what you are aspect. In Dreams is a favorite of mine. Not really a close corollary, but I could see lines that can be drawn.

                    Has no one mentioned Stranger Things?

                    The Fifth Element is a bit of a reach. But if you see Dallas as a retired Troll or House Scathach Sidhe who takes up a cause to help a newly returned Sidhe sorceress to prevent the Elder Dark from returning, Zerg being a House Balor seeking the return with the help of a group of Troll/Ogre/Redcaps and the setting being either the future or the Dreaming...then you might see where I'm coming from.

                        There is another series I forgot. Fablehaven by Brandon Mull. A secret nature reserve for the world's mythical/legendary creatures. Everything has to behave according to laws set forth in a treaty of sorts. Some grandchildren of the caretaker arrive when parents leave on a vacation and discover their grandfather is running this magically protected place of wonder.

                            I didnt mention the Anime Berserk. If you can find the original series from the 90s it is way better. They revived it as a concise three movie dvd and subsequently it is now a serial detailing the events in the Manga. Both the 90s series and the recent 3 dvd release are a prelude. The premise is heavily influenced by events from medieval Europe and is perfect for Dreaming heavy or modern settings that want to focus more on the Fae side of things. The current series is more of a Nightmare influenced setting, almost the Elder Darks won sort of thing.
