        Originally posted by 11twiggins
        That's actually a very good point! However, you could do it manually.

        Simply observe via Correspondence once the ritual is completed, waiting to put the finishing touch and fire it off. When one or more Vampires is JUST about to run into one or more other Vampires, fire off the ritual. You can construct the clean-cut appearance too; a mental illusion of a flash of blinding light, gunfire, disorienting acrid smoke, and when it fades we have our Sabbat Illusions in place.

        But yes, I see that making the ritual conditional would probably make it much stronger. Simply work together with another VA in your story I suppose? Not going to learn Time (which I find a tad niche myself) just to pull this off ;-)
        Here's an example step by step of how I think this could be achieved, I'm going to use Science, but it doesn't need to be the only option. It uses Mind 3 / Data 3. Lots of brain hacking (I imagine resistances could be felt as mental firewalls and such ), I don't think other Spheres would be necessary but Prime and/or Time could help, and it would be great to have ritual helpers:

        1. You need to discover a very crucial info on vampiric business in the city: Who they are.

        You can do this, for instance, by discovering a careless vampire and hacking her mind trough a street camera, reading her toughts to discover who knows the local faces of the Cam, if the vampire doesn't know that, you can discover who will know. Unless most of the Elyseum never hunt outside their havens this should be easy because of "the social bonds between vampires". Everyone has an idea about who is who.

        2. You have to set an unparaleled Correspondence spy net that would be the envy of any NWO agent. You may have to set the Correspondence/Data connections individually, a complex task made possible thanks to your prior research that may take days or weeks, a few Elders that do not leave home or manage to avoid all cameras and other "instruments" of yours may present complications. if you manage this you could put the local Cam to it's knees because...

        3. You can cast spells and read minds with impunity. You not only know who the Sabbat are, but, more importantly, you know what the local vampires think about the Sabbat (which may not be accurate, but you're playing to their expectations).

        (These points are key. If vampires can detect something it's going on, and detect where you are/what are you doing, they could potentially stop you. A Partial success with these shenanigans may be enough to start a ruckus, but would leave loose threads)

        4.Increase natural vampiric paranoia about a Sabbat infiltration in the city. Really, nothing should be easier, vampires live in constant paranoia.

        You could plant the idea that the sect it's present by fiddling with the memory of past hunts (construct a "encounter with the Sabbat" in a night where the vamp failed to feed. It doesn't need to be very realistic, it needs to live up to Cam expectations to startle the local vampires). You could even plant the suspicion that some fringe members of the Cam may have switched sides, just to make everyone a bit more paranoid

        5. At this point the fun has already started. Vampires and paranoia are like gunpowder and fire. Blood it's going to be spilled (perhaps even yours)

        If they realize something odd it's happening, they may suspect someone it's Dominating people (fiddling with memory), but vampiric suspicion starts at home, not at some other splat.

        Also, since you have their minds on speed dial (or, as an alternative, you could have their toughts being projected non-stop to your computers while keeping a map with their location-this would be very potent mojo!) you can make those toughts loose importance, a la Technocratic social conditioning.

        More than likely, they will search Sabbat infiltrators, either because they fell for it, or just to be sure. And, sure enough, they're going to find "the Sabbat", because you're going to make it happen. To engineer an encounter should be easy enough for someone that posseses this massive net (just take advantage that vampires are searching for Sabbat, induce a few vampires to go to some place without knowing that another group it's alread there).

        5. You project the illusion when you know vampires are about to meet using your gigantic net, overriding all five senses- you can do this as suggested by 11twiggins, after all, you know more or less where and when vampires are going to encounter, but Time 4 would make things much easier, allowing you to sleep and have sweet dreams while vampires kill each other)- Congrats, you've "found the sabbat", too bad that it wasn't the actual Sabbat.
