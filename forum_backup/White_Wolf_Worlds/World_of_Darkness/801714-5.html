                                I'm not quite sure which you're referring too. While the Setites play into the Western/White racial trope of, "other people want to steal our women," that's not a specifically Arab, Egyptian, or Muslim, racial or ethnic stereotype. It's also fairly distinctly different from how Western stereotyping of those groups go. Arabs stereotypes, especially as related to economics, are either the sleazy merchant, or the harem owning aristocrat/oil-sheik. Both of these are too overt for the Setite's as presented. The European based stereotypes were not of hidden corruption spreading cults of debauchery. The stereotypes in question are actually closer to the Ravnos: unscrupulous thieves out to screw you over in business dealing, and/or steal away your valuables before disappearing back to somewhere else (though often women are treated more as objects/valuables in these stereotypes). The Assamites cover the other major European stereotypes of Arabs, and Muslims: desert bandits and murders, or violent religious terrorists.

                                Basically... the Setites are a stereotype that doesn't have to do with distinct real world people they're associated with. "Vice pushing debauchery to tear down 'proper' social norms," isn't something Arabs, Muslims, etc. get persecuted for. The most recent form of anti-non-Jewish-Middle-Eastern stereotypes is pretty much about the exact opposite: fear that they're coming to the West to force their oppressive social norms via strict religious laws.

                                The Setites do play into nasty modern stereotypes, but again, they're not specific to the Setites or the ethnic groups/religions associated with them, they're the standard first line European othering that applies to everyone that's not considered White and/or appropriately Christian enough. My point is not that there aren't problems with the Setites, but that the stereotypes they use are either too old to matter to living people, or too disconnected from the Setites to matter specifically to that one Clan.
