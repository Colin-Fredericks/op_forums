                                                          Werewolves are by far the strongest sort of player character. At least in terms of what player characters do - fight, sneak into or out of places, get surprised, have to deal with proactive enemies, etc. Despite that, vampires and mages tend to have abilities which lend themselves better to a protracted conflict. Vampires can mass produce legions of human or ghoul servants, so if there is a full scale war between werewolves and vampires, werewolves would find themselves in a war with humanity too. Vampires can also replenish their numbers rapidly and can have the lowest generation vampires do all of the embracing - since they're completely unified.

                                                          That said, mages will win this most likely. Mages' extreme versatility, their unification, their ability to win with greater planning. These all point to them being able to crush the other sides unless we go with Antediluvians as plot devices. Mages could just scan the world for other types of creatures and then teleport bombs into every elder's haven or werewolf Cairn.
