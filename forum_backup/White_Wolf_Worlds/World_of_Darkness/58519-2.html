    I actually have a difficult time not seeing World of Darkness parallels/inspiration/theme in things I watch. Movies/TV shows I consider WoD as a whole, rather than just certain scenes or characters would include:
    • The Fountain
      Werewolf and Mage, especially themes of ancestors/past lives, Mnesis, Umbral quests, or a Seeking. Huge inspiration for Theurges with their theme of death and rebirth, and the necessity of cycles.
    • Ink
      Werewolf and Changeling. Umbral Realms, dealing with Banes, being a Theurge. Could also be seen from the perspective of a Fomori being redeemed.
    • Eastern Promises
      Werewolf. Shadow Lord / Kinfolk.
    • Hard Candy
      Werewolf. Black Fury / Kinfolk, incredibly Ragabash. Could also be good inspiration for a Nuwisha.
    • The 13th Warrior
      Werewolf. The obligatory Silent-Strider-joins-a-pack-of-Fenrir-on-a-quest. There's even great Auspice role representation.
    • Fringe
      Mage. A more Technocratic world - or possibly Glass Walkers and the Mirror Zone!
    • Constantine
      Hunters, and Mortals with Hedge Magic and/or Numina.
    • House of Cards
      Vampire. Ridiculously Ventrue. (Bonus Corax reporter.)
    • Princess Mononoke
      Werewolf. Themes of Weaver encroachment on the Wyld, Wyrm taint and corruption, spirits.
    • The Man From Earth
      Werewolf, Vampire.Themes of immortality that could easily translate into Mnesis as well.
    • MirrorMask
      Werewolf, Mage, Changeling. For similar reasons as Ink. Umbral realms, the Umbra in general.
    • Daybreakers
      Vampire. An actual vampire flick, too! Sam Neill is excellent.
    • Fright Night
      Vampire. Another intentionally vampire movie. I can't help but think of some Sabbat Lasombra out for entertainment.
    • Whale Rider
      Werewolf, maybe Mage. Lots of good mythos, prophecy, and spirituality themes. Made me think of Kinfolk.
    • Avatar
      Werewolf. Pandora is such a great representation of an Umbral realm, and a living, vital Gaia. Mnesis themes, animistic spirituality.
    • Cruel Intentions
      Vampire. How bored Toreadors (or their Ghouls) can be manipulative, abusive, horrible monsters; using and consuming others in their quest for entertainment and revenge. An allegory for the politics and boons within the Camarilla in general, and differing/changing Humanity.
    • Foxfire
      Werewolf. Anruth, Black Fury / Kinfolk themes with a little Rite of Passage.
    • Forest Warrior
      Werewolf. Bear with me here... this movie is basically terrible, but if you look at it as if it is a pack of cubs who find a totem of the wilderness, it suddenly becomes great for a laugh. You can peg Tribe and Auspice stereotypes to each of the kids. And keep in mind, if Werewolves are changing around puberty, the Nation is full of teens and pre-teens like this. (And suddenly the stupid problems of the Nation make so much more sense...)
