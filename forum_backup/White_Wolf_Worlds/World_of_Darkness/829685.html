    What are your favorite House rules for crossovers?

    02-14-2016, 09:08 AM
    Self explanatory title. We all know that in a cwod crossover, while possible and other splats are referenced in the story, are severely unbalanced as incompatible rules and themes get lost in translation.

    So how have you all personally dealt with the strange circumstances, competing themes, drastically imbalanced parties and player attempts at mix and match templates. What are your favorite tweaks, house rules, and settings for crossovers.

          I’ve mentioned these in other threads, so some of these bits may sound [annoyingly] familiar to forumites. For crossovers with the Big Three (and Wraith), in no particular order:

          Mages
          Consensus, paradigms, vulgar magick, etc., are central to MtA, but became convoluted when dealing with other PC splats. I use variants of DA:M rules (Pillars and Foundations) when running crossover’d Mages.

          Werewolves
          Mechanically, they play well with others…except for Crinos. Crossover’d Garou can only be in Crinos for [Rage] turns per scene.

          Vampires
          Again, like Werewolves, their mechanics aren’t too bad. However, sunlight is a pain in the narrative. Vampires take bashing damage from Sunlight and can soak with just Stamina. It might not kill them, but going in direct sunlight still causes some smoking, which could be a Masquerade breach. Thick clothing or at least indoors, they can still contribute to the story without having to sleep as with RAW.

          Wraiths
          Risen work well for an easy answer. If a player really wants to play a Wraith, I increase (a lot!) the duration of Embody. As long the player is fine with a heavy Embody-dump, it works out.

          [I used an NPC Mage that bartered for wraithly service by giving rings that granted an extended Embody-like power. Yes, Wraiths…that wore Rings. Wonder where I got the inspiration for that one? ]

          Others
          I tried adapting CtD for crossovers in a Steampunk-Weird West crossover game. Everyone had fun, but I had so many ad hoc rulings that consistency became a huge issue for me. Luckily, the player was gracious and forgiving. I don’t have much experience with the other lines.

                      For crossovers, I generally allow vampires to soak aggravated damage with Stamina, if not a more radical change to damage. It allows them to be less fragile against the kinds of things that werewolves or mages might deal with. I also let the vampire players have a ghoul character that they can play during the day.

                          -vitae, quintessence, gnosis, glamour, sekhem etc... all equivalent, and simply different names for the same thing
                          -ditto for banality & paradox.
                          -give all players a humanity score, and make rage the inverse of that.
                          -penumbra, dreaming and skinlands also all the same thing, just from different perspectives.
                          -remove all "paths of doing what I feel like"
                          -replace all vampiric thaumaturgy, necromancy etc... with hedge magic, but allow the use of vitae like mana/quintessence
                          -eliminate damage rolls, all damage values = 2/3 of what the dicepool would have been
                          -ST plays simplified versions of shadows
                          -eliminate trait bonus stacking: if one effect gives +2 str and another gives +1 str, the character only gets +2 str, not +3

                          That's all I can think of at the moment, I'm sure there's more...
