                    Just like getting all the Eshu to agree to settled down and stop moving around, wasting time and resources. Putting themselves into danger.                     What you're saying is that the Sidhe can't grow to be more or better than they are. They represent a dream that can't be part of the modern world without poisoning it. Dreams like Racial Purity, World Conquest, Monarchy, Civilizing Missions, A Universal World Religion, or Aristocracy have no valid place in the modern world.

                    It seems like another one of those tired "Hate the Man" situations in WoD. It could just as easily be "The Ventrue are the Greatest Horror of Vampire", "The Order of Hermes/New World Order are the greatest Horror of Mage", "The Silver Fangs are the greatest Horror of Werewolf", Etc.
                    It's not "I Hate the Man." It's the dream the sidhe represent is sick. To retain their unjust rule they bring banality and bedlam in their wake. Beautiful they are, and many, even many of the most monsterous, can be well intended. But they need to force the world into reinforcing their dream at the expense of human creativity and freedom. For the Sidhe to thrive humanity must give up all dreams except those that feed the sidhe. We must cast away democracy, justice, and freedom, in exchange for the honor of those that can never see us as anything but cheap toys.

                    Either let the sidhe move on to new dreams, or let them pass into storybooks and be placed on the shelf. The other changeling moved with the times.

                                                            It's certainly true that fae are among the least monstrous denizens of the Old World of Darkness, but the original premise of WoD was 'play the monsters'. So it's not totally unreasonable to refer to them as monsters.

                                                            By most definitions, trolls, redcaps, riverhags, boggans, etc, ARE monsters. Often mischievous rather than evil, usually capricious more than malicious, but the whole 'changeling' thing comes from myths of faeries stealing people's babies. Let's not forget that fae literally steal the bodies of humans. Sure, they claim it's fair on the human soul, but the sidhe at least might be doing it by slinging mortal souls into the Dreaming to grab their skin.

                                                            But honestly, let's not derail another thread by arguing the minutiae of semantics? Arguing whether the fae are monstrous or not is pointless, because subjectively everyone will have a different opinion on what a monster is and whether they play or interpret the game that way.

                                                            We were talking about the sidhe. In the real world, they'd be outdated tyrants (there are plenty of these still running our countries in real life, by the way, if you really want to get angry about something worthwhile...). But it's a game. Debating the evilness of fictional character types seems a little odd to me. Call for clarification, yes. But be clear about what you want clarified and move on. If you hate unearned privilege and entitlement, campaign against it in the real world, or roleplay it out in your games, but it seems pointless getting angry about setting fluff. A roleplaying game is meant to set up plot hooks and opportunities for conflict, not to create a setting for us all to roleplay utopia.
