                Does a good act become wicked because it was done for wicked intentions? Does a wicked act become good because it was done for righteous intentions? These are questions that have confounded people for centuries. Lets use an example of a Camarilla vampire. Jan Peterzoon, he's a ventrue. His Feeding restriction is Rape victims. Inorder to have easy and regular access to his food he funds and supports Women's shelters throughout the world. One can look at his act as farming these women, yet even they benefited when they had no place to go, and that ignores all the countless ones he never touches. If it weren't for his selfishness these shelters would not exist, these women would have no place to go.

                Jan is the camarilla in microcosm. It tries to make the world better for mortals in a purely selfish reasons. It improves neighborhoods because the Sabbat and to a lesser extent the Anarchs find feeding easier and more plentiful in shitholes. It spreads human habitat in order to make feeding easy. It fights demons and monsters because they are a threat to Camarilla power. Nothing they do is altruistic on a large scale(though vampires are like people individuals), but they can and do do things that people might call good.

                The Garou nation believes it works to defend the World or possibly all life in the universe. They are all so petty, vindictive, xenophobes who can't even work well with other people who change into animals because they had to dominate them. The Garou are very good at justifying their actions in righteous language claiming betrayals by other Fera, or that being corrupted is enough reason to die(vs being clensed). A sizeable minority feel that all humans should be wiped out and probably the majority while not willing to be part of a culling to more "managable levels" wouldn't shed any tears if that happened through more natural means. Most wouldn't cry tears if our civilzation colapsed most starved to death("Its natural!") and humanity was forced into a more simple existence..
