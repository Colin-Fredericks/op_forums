                                                        If you want to destroy a Mummy through eating it you have to use the Anthropophagi power used by the Amkhat found in the M:TR core rulebook (p.203). Every time you eat part of a Mummy you live longer and can steal from them: every time you do so you can steal one Ability point or one Attribute point from the Mummy until it's lost almost all of them. While this is possible in theory, in practice this means that you'd have to kill a Mummy over and over again and munch on the good bits (eyes, hands, body, brain or heart) over and over again. Plus, you'd need to beat the Mummy again and again, avoid other Mummies and their servants and have enough time on your hands to sit out the wait until the Mummy gets back each time this happens, which can range anywhere from a day to a year or more.
