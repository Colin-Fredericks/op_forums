                  If we see any of the rest.

                  There's a number of factors on why we might not get 20th anniversary edition of the remaining gamelines.

                  1) The current staff might not want to do them for various issues (see all the threads about KotE20).

                  2) All of them are essentially single edition games. They don't have multiple editions to collect, reconcile, and fixed up. They also don't have gigantic catalogs of books to try to distill. All of the Mummy books (including the 1e and 2e ones), total 784 pages. My20 would basically need to add material to get up to the ~500 page mark the current run of 20th core books are hitting. Even HtR with the biggest catalog of the remaining game lines, doesn't have a lot to do a 20th book with. I'm not knocking the style, but a huge amount of its page count is dedicated to in-character Hunter-net.org posts.

                  3) The new WWP is moving towards a 4th edition of WOD, and isn't going to want the 20th books to continue in perpetuity. While they're supportive of the 20th books, eventually they're going to want the 4e books to be the only ones being made. 20th books of the later WOD games delays that process further.

                  ---------

                  Something I've floated around in the past, would be more of a compilation of all of them together. It's not the best solution in many regards, but it might be easier to get them as one or two books that cover multiple lines.
