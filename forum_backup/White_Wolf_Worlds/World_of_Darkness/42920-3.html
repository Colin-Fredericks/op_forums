    Are the Infused basically Imbued?

    To answer your questions. It's interesting that you mentioned the Imbued. The Infused have some differences since they have a subtle vibe of the divine to them and a more varied purpose (than just stopping monsters) they can follow (healing, inventions, battle, etc). They have little guidance (given their lack of a connection) so they must rely a lot on their own judgement to make it. I'm sure both the Hunters and the Infused could find some common ground compared to the other Divinities.

    Since the Celestials have been just thrown into the mystical side of the World of Darkness, their society and culture is still building itself. While they have their Connections and the Ordu of Angelos and the media's interpretations of their kind, what it becomes can be shaped by the PCs. I guess you could say that it's like a company where each Divinity is like a different department that has to work together for the greater good (Heritages would be sub-departments that can crossover). The departments have different views and purposes of course and may clash. I hope this helps.

        I wanted to add some things about the psychological aspects of the Angels here.

        Gallidreil speaks to her Aasimar child...
        "My child, my dear, sweet Mortal. You have all brought me joy. You have also brought me pain. You have fascinated and frustraited me. And despite all these millenia, we still can't fully understand a thing you do. Yet our love remains.
        Sometimes that is our torment. To see you bicker, hate, destroy and die. Then watch you love, create and play. You awken something in us that we try to piece together, but can never fully explain. All we can do is trust The Host in its wisdom and this strange love we feel.
        Never forget, my dearest child, that no matter how hard it is to explain, that I will always love you all."

        Despite all the conflicts and complexities of conflicts of Heaven and Hell, the original Angels have always been the simplest. Lawful, reserved, single minded and innocent, the Angels faithfully served The Host in creating this world.
        So it came as a surprise as to why their master could create something as passionate, chaotic and complex as humanity. Since they were part of The Host, its love for these creatures passed on to them. Eventually, the Angels found that the humans managed to crack through their tough defenses and awaken something in them. At first they believed it was simple curiosity, but with in time they began to appreciate these new creatures in all their potential and foibles. And not just because The Host told them to.
        Maybe that was why so many Angels joined Lucifer when planned his little rebellion. Push them forward and in return your love will not only be even more rewarded, but you will finally understand what your purpose is. The ones that didn't join knew that this was not what The Host wanted. Humanity would grow on their own terms and so would their kind's own understanding. With in that time, these feelings would finally make sense so why put everything at risk?
        Now that Eden has fallen, the humans have become even harder to understand. Especially after Caine slew Abel and brought murder to the World of Darkness. It was the first time that they truly felt sadness and loss for the Mortals. These were new and frightening emotions for them to face that only fueled their curiosity and confusion. Why would Caine do such a thing and then reject their pleas to repent even when his brother forgave him?
        Sometimes all you can do is trust this love and believe in The Host and its grand plan. For look at what it did to the Demons when they rebelled: strife, pain, torment and madness was their reward and a frightening lesson for the Angels.

        Angel Psychology: The mind of an Angel by our understanding is quite "alien". To them, love is simple and just exists, because that is what they know. It is pure and needs time to grow to yield the best results. Same goes for concepts like hate and rebellion which do not exist, because they don't know what it is.

        Being the lawful and simple creatures they are, the complexities of the human mind are not the easiest things for them to understand. But they do try their hardest to guide and understand their Aasimar. And through their children, the rest of the world of darkness.

            Charon speaks to one of his children...

            "I have seen the wicked and I have seen the divine and it's masters and followers are no different from you Mortals. Always bickering like little children who think they are always right. Believe me, I know the pain of a broken heart and destroying yourself in the process. I was once something great and now it is all an empire of dirt. For countless years I have struggled in my bitterness and pain over what I have lost.

            Yes, my child, I can read your mind and you're wondering why I even bother with you. Let's just say that you lost men in a lost world fascinate me. Despite my past, I've always felt some kind of empathy for you. Sometimes it wasn't always pretty, but part of me has always cared. This is why I have given you this second chance to truly live in this pain-ridden world. Learn from it, heal it and just maybe I can experience them both and free myself again."

            Charon has always been a wild card. Few know who he truly is; let alone if that is "his" true identity. Just that he has an understanding between Lucifer and The Host, but the both of them are keeping quiet. He is a powerful being with his own hopes and tragedies. Like the Angels and Demons, he has created his own children to fix and find a meaning in this world. Whether his plan is successful is yet to be seen.

            Charon prefers to reveal only what needs to be known and let his Chari figure things out for themselves. He will only speak when he has something to say, but he is willing to answer their questions as long as they aren't too asinine.

            NOTE: I want to keep Charon's idenity open-ended and a mystery for the time being. Though I do have a few candidates in mind. Who do you think it might be?

                    Nephilim Memories
                    "So that imagination and memory are but one thing, which for divers considerations hath divers names."-Thomas Hobbes. Leviathan

                    Since nearly all of the First Born were transformed into Ferrymen, the Nephilim can't really speak with them. Instead, the Nephilims' minds are connected to the memories and thoughts of their ancestors (not just the First Born). They are treated to visions from the past in relation to their situation. The ST gets to determine what kind of vision it is and what happens in it.

                    Example: A Nephilim is about to be in her first shoot-out and can dig into the past where her police officer great-grandfather was in one during the prohibition era. Getting visions of how he handled it will give her an idea on how to survive. The problem is that the visions are brutally honest and not afraid to show her the gory details of his fellow cops getting shot full of holes with a Tommy gun.

                    Visions have also come in the form of dreams where more than one Nephilim have claimed to have seen bits of their First Born ancestor's past while asleep.While the memories aren't always happy, not all of them are miserable, either. Just honest, to the point while leaving enough to make the Nephilim want to know more.

                            The Touched

                            What Acolytes are to Mages, Bystanders to Hunters or Kinfolk to Garou, The Touched have their own special connection to the heavens. Some follow around the Angel of their choice around like a helpful puppy, others help from a distance and some just can't help, but to get dragged in. Regardless, the Touched's life will never be the same now that the blinders have been torn off.

                            Creation: One way is for a Mortal to witness an Angel's Revelation, where she will see the Angel's Apocalyptical form and hear the message that she receives. Many Touched have claimed to have heard a voice telling them that must also aid the Angel in her quest. The other is through a ritual (The Rite of Touched) where the Angel and Mortal must sincerely vow to aid and care for each other on sacred ground (churches, grave yards, etc) while the later spends three Faith points. (This method can only be used once every season.)

                            Abilities:

                            -From now on, all Touched see Angels and Demons in their Apocalyptical forms no matter how well the Angel/Demon tries to hide it. (Though the Touched can repress it for one scene with a Willpower roll vs 7.)
                            -Three times per game, a Touched can ask for an extra die (and only one) when making a roll to help out an Angel or go against a Demon.
                            -Can enter and leave the Umbra by holding on to an Angel as she enters/leaves.

                            Inspirational material: Highway To Heaven- While it presents a more idealized world compared to the World of Darkness, the show does give a look into how a Mortal's working relationship with an Angel can be like in it's joys, it's struggles and conflicts.
                            ---

                            New Merit
                            Celestial Leak (Aasimar, Chari and Nephlim only)
                            Cost: 4pt Flaw
                            Given how the Infused are created due to celestial exposure, your Aura might be one of the causes. It seems that yours leaks out more than just emotions. Once every four months (game time), you risk accidentally infusing a random Mortal with your celestial energy and turning them into an Infused. Since this is up to the ST, the chosen person can range from the Angel's spouse of 24 years to a panhandler she gave a dollar to. Since all of these Infused automatically know you were the one that turned them into this, this can lead to some sticky situations.
                            System: Once the ST picks someone with a Humanity score of at least 6, the player must roll their Willpower vs 8 to keep this from happening. Only one success is needed.
