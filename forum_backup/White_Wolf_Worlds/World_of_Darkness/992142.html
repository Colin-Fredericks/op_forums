    Earth and Umbra merged

    10-23-2016, 09:19 PM
    Okay this might similar to the "Endless Night" ritual from the Giovanni, but what if the Earth and Umbra (not just the shadowlands, but also low/middle/upper and deep umbras, including all the Horizion Realms, The Dreaming/Arcadia, etc?) are merged into "one world" therefore bringing back the "Mythical Ages" of the past?

    Of course all of this as much I hate to make this comparison but is inspired by the plotline of the recent "Shadow Warrior 2" FPS game where Earth and the Shadow World are merged which the world looks rather alien which I wonder how alien would Earth look like it's merged with the Umbra?

    I also wonder who in the WoD canon exactly has this goal? I think it could after serve as the "best case scenario apocalypse" especially averting the Technocratic predicted future of the Earth gobbled up by the sun six billion years from now with the world "now immortal and lasts forever" therefore not worrying any other ones.

    Of course with the Earth and Umbra merged, how would supernaturals cope with it especially Vampires? Well could have it business as usual with Vampires except it takes place cross between Earth and a alien landscape or maybe it could go back to the Dark Ages gamelines..

      Much of human society gets eaten by banes.       All in all, not really a good time, or even a promise of it. The Wyld is closer, but that's a mixed blessing, at best, given how even Garou and most Fera don't want Thresholds in their sacred spaces..

              It's highly doubtful. They basically have ten dice to soak and do damage, somewhere between an effective 30 and 100 health levels if the gauntlet is down - for which they don't suffer penalties, even on their last one - and the ability to warp reality on top of that. Anything that can't easily soak aggravated damage and deal it out in high enough quantities to stop the crawler from wreaking too much havoc from incidental warping aren't going to make it through; also, vampires would have to roll Courage to avoid suffering from Rotschreck from just from seeing one, much as Garou have to roll to avoid a fox frenzy. Even scrags are going to be trouble for most vampires, though, because they all have Rage 10, which means they deal out Garou levels of damage, but there are a lot more of them around, and if the Gauntlet is down, they have an effective 20 health levels. This is all assuming that a Psychomachia doesn't turn any given vampire into their own undead meat puppet, of course.

              Dream Makers are even worse, though, because they can get up to 300 health levels and are basically the antagonist of the Wishmaster series. They're such bad news, their Alter Reality Charm isn't even mechanically detailed anywhere! (^VVVVVV^)
