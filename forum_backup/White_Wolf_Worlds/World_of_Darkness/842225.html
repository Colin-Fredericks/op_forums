      Which country are we talking about? Conscription is technically on the books in some countries but hasn't been used in decades, while in other countries is an assumed fact of teenage life.

      That said, pretty much every country that has mandatory service in some form, as long since learned how to deal with individuals that shouldn't/can't be doing it.

      1) A lot of supernaturals take themselves off the grid, or were never on it. Lupus and Metis Garou, for example, might not have official papers, which means nobody is coming after them to serve in these things. A vampire that's 100 years old has already figured out either how to remove themselves from the system, or create new identities in the system which can be crafted to avoid service.

      2) In countries with universal conscription, there's pretty much always options for avowed pacifists, people with mental disorders, and people with significant medical issues. I have some in-laws in Israel. One of them didn't go into military service because they got recruited by one of Israel's intelligence services to work as an analyst instead. Another did a four year degree program followed by a graduate research project in agricultural studies (which the Israeli government is all for because high levels of STEM industry is important to their economy, and agricultural independence is important to their concept of national security). Another was exempted completely because of a serious spinal injury when they were younger.

      3) Government generally really don't want to deal with actively unwilling individuals. One of the reasons things like jury pools are as large as they are, is because they know some percentage of people will do everything to get out of it; and most of them that don't want to serve jury duty will manage to get out of it. Trying to use jury duty to pull Garou away from a Sept would be pointless. All of them just need to call up and say, "Hey, this date? It sucks for me, [insert one of dozens reasonable excuses], can we reschedule?" Because the universal response from jury selectors is of course you can reschedule.

                      This is why supernatural beings really need to go "off the grid" or pay their dues to other creatures of their kind who have Influence.

                      For Vampire, this can be easy. Pay your boons to the elders, probably Ventrue, who can put a stop to it using their Influence. There is a reason they are in charge, and getting mortal authorities to not be interested in you is one reason the other clans want them in charge.

                      For Werewolf, this should be easy. The game assumes most Garou are runaways who flee into the woods. They don't leave forwarding addresses. For most Garou this solves the problem. There is probably a minority who still have a real identity and paperwork trail in the world of the Flock, and they can be issues. However, sufficient Gifts and favors done to Glass Walkers (and sometimes others) can probably reduce the nuisance value.

                      For Mage, this shouldn't be much of an issue. It can be handled case by case. Some Mages can just eat this and move on. It's not like they can't handle it unlike the previous two. Others can handle it with Arcane or appropriate magic.

                      For Wraith, this is a non issue. But they have to put up with their equivalents for real under the Hierarchy.

                      For Changeling, it depends. Most Changelings are either under the age of this kind of thing, moved completely into the Dreaming with few ties to the mundane world, or are recognized as being mentally ill.

Granted, some Lasombra and Tzimisce are likely wary of teaching their respective signatures. But there are also plenty of young licks who give not a rat's ass about their Clan's history or whatever, and share them freely with their pack-mates.
