                                            And we're in the "explicitly stated as not working like real life," part of the setting so... yeah.

As a matter of tautology the universe we live in has to be one that supports human life, because we exist in it. That doesn't mean there's any evidence that the universe only has the minimum physics required for humans to exist because there's no evidence that the universe exists just to make use. Since we have lots of evidence to show we are not the destiny of the universe purely based on the laws of physics, there has to be the minimum for everything else we know can and could exist too.
