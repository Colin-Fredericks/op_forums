      My V20 campaign is a combination of all the Editions.

      1. The Assamites are still cursed by the Tremere and unable to drink blood. They also are capable of killing for hire but it's not all they do. The Vizier and Mage Bloodlines don't exist but they serve as functions within the clan which isn't just about the murder for hire.

      2. The major WOD powers are all vaguely aware of each other. The Kindred, for example, believe the Illuminati (Technocracy) exist and call them that. They have a formal treaty which keeps them out of each other's hair and protecting the Masquerade/Consensus. It was negotiated by the Tremere who are still laughing at the Heremetics from beyond death.

      3. The Ravnos had an ancient powerful member of their clan awaken who slaughtered a large number of Indian Ravnos as well as humans but there was no Week of Nightmares. There's a fierce debate over whether it was an Antedeluvian or not.

      5. Tzimisce are exactly like the Old Clan Tzimisce but possess Vicissitude. They're more cold scientists and transhumanists than freaks for the sake of freakydom.

      5. Independent clans are welcome to live in the Camarilla cities as long as they swear to abide by the Traditions. Hence, Assamites, FoS, Giovanni, and Ravnos are all possible to be found as citizens of cities.

      6. The Sabbat is basically a failed experiment as the founders were meant to be heroic wariror poets and philosophers but 90% of their thinkers as well as teachings go over its sects members heads. A small group of Sabbat are posthuman geniuses and Nieztschean supermen but they are disgusted with their own sect. The last Great Sabbat War caused the sect to collapse into smaller philosophical movements which are considered extinct (but aren't).

      7. The True Hand and Inconnu are two branches of the same organization. Basically, Kindred older than God manipulating the younger vampires against each other.

      8. No Thaumaturgy Paths exist but the Path of Blood and Necromancy with both requiring separate discipline points. Rituals are more powerful, though.

      8. Mummies exist but the 1E ones and a handful of ones they've made since Set.

      9. The Cappadochians still exist but in service to the Giovanni as a small number were allowed to survive in exchange for swearing allegiance. The Samedi are a result of a withering like condition which sometimes strikes them. No Harbinger of Skulls ever existed.

      10. The Salubri are still surviving but there's less than a hundred in the world and of all the variations of the original clanbook. The Tremere generally try to have them killed but make a half-hearted gesture about it at best. Most masquerade as Ventrue or Toreador with some weaker ones as Caitiff. They did not join the Sabbat.

      11. Sabbat Paths of Enlightenment are slightly less demanding of EVIL with the Path of Harmony considered akin to vegans in the sect but not worthy of destruction. Some Camarilla Elders still follow the Old Ones or have created their own.

      12. Anarch Free States don't exist anymore since they eventually faded to become identical to normal Kindred cities. Chicago's population was forced out by the Sabbat and migrated to New York City. Many Kindred are reluctant to return to the fallen cities even if the Sabbat have been driven out. Thus, New York is grossly overpopulated with 500 Kindred.

      13. Kuei-Jin Exist but are less racist: Kuei-Jin are basically just Asian Risen/Mummies and while more organized than their fellows are about the same level of numbered and far less numerous than regular vampires. Asiastic versions of the 12 Clans inhabit the Far East alongside the Kuei-Jin. I've canonized the Gentleman Gamer's Eastern Kindred.

      14. Werewolf: The Forsaken wholesale replaces Werewolf: The Apocalypse. Elements remain, though, with the Civil War between the Pure Ones and Forsaken having started in the 1990s and pretty much sealing the death kneel of the Garou race.

      15. Pentex has since been taken over by the Camarilla and is now an enviironmentally friendly corporation devoted to preserving mankind as food for the next few thousand years.

              I mix-match between old and new a lot with hints of homebrew all over the place.
              For VtM, the Requiem Convenants (sects) are existing alongside the classic ones. The Camarilla basically started as a large attempt to bring entire Clans together in united purpose; didn't quite go that way but hey, they're the biggest Sect today so something went right. The Cappadocians and Salubri still exist, though they're nolonger considered Clans; most hide with the Invictus cuz the Tremere can't touch them there lest they risk a Sect war which neither the Camarilla nor Invictus wants. Also there are a lot of Assamites hooking up with various Sects (most notably the Camarilla, Sabbat and Lancea Sanctum) what with the schism and all.

              Ashirra are a sub-Sect of the Lancea Sanctum, Baali were basically Dark-Ages Belial's Brood (who now carries the infernalist torch).
              Tremere Antitribu are dangerously low in number, but not eradicated. Gangrel are essentially one step from becoming the "Clan of the Circle of the Crone" since they left the Camarilla, some of the few eastern Ravnos are pondering options after the Week of Nightmares.

              The Carthian Movement is an evolvement of the Anarch Movement which aims to get more structure and "rebuild the system" which most of the hardcore Anarchs are not a fan of, cuz they think that having a system in the first place will lead to corruption eventually; so there are some conflicts there.
              Ordo Dracul is same old, but have some homebrew Coils and VII is an honored group of essentially kamikaze warriors of the Sabbat.

              Camarilla covers most of western Europe and the east coast-midwestern US. Sabbat Mexico, Spain, notable parts of South America, parts of Canada, and are fighting for control around the US. Carthian Movement hangs around the West Coast, Anarchs exist wherever more or less. Ordo Dracul are mostly in Romania and Oceania in terms of actual strongholds, but otherwise are found in lots of places since they pursue various knowledges world-wide. Circle of the Crone are the strongest around Africa, India and parts of the more rural US and Canada. Lancea Sanctum are mostly in the mediterranan and the Invictus mostly hang around eastern Europe and Russia with some encroachment into Asia.

              I also mix Generations and Blood Potency. All Vampires start with Blood Potency 1, but each dot in Generation places a hardcap on how far down it can fall in torpor and so on. So for example, a 10th Generation will still have to work his way up to Blood Potency 4; but once he has it he cannot drop below 4 under any normal circumstances, not even by torpor. If he goes above his Generation limit however, it can drop down to 4 again. This way Blood Potency allows for character progression with a bit of risk while still maintaining Generations and making having a low Generation (and Diablerie) highly desired.
              Also Kiasyd are Embraced Changelings a lá Dark Ages V20; thus their origin and Sect is a lot more up in the air. They all gain Mytherceria, but the other two Disciplines are chosen from the parent Clan. A Lasombra-Embraced Kiasyd for example could have Mytherceria, Obtenebration or Dominate, or he could pick Potence instead of Obtenebration or Dominate.
              Also Kuei-Jin are Sin-Eaters a lá Geist (see below) and Samedi, Gargoyles and Blood Brothers are the only Bloodlines that exist, the rest never happened; especially not the Daughters of Cacophony.

              For Werewolf I also mix-match old and new and got both "Gaians" and Forsaken running around doing their thing. Black Spiral Dancers are hated by everyone much like Bale Hounds and even the Pure can agree to momentary truces to hunt them down first. I also tend to play Spirits in a tad more one-dimensional human way than the corebook suggests which I imagine a lot of people would flog me for. Fera also exist btw; cWoD style.

              For Mages, same old. Mix factions etc. Pentacle Orders, Traditions, Technocracy, Fringe Factions and Seers of the Throne are the main ones. Banishers remain a thing but they're less "came back wrong" and more often former hunters or people who can't handle the pressure or so and lash out violently. Marauders are scary and nobody knows where they come from or how they do what they do; they're more akin to "came back wrong;" but I leave it to mystery on just what is wrong with them. Tremere Liches are renamed Styrian Liches to differ from the Vampire Clan and I use a homebrew Arcana of Abyss which belongs to a sixth Path called Scelesti awakening to the Watchtower of the Empty Road; there are still heavy persecution of them and discussions on what to do in the Awakened communities.

              Hunters are mostly Vigil with small Reckoning elements like Bystanders being a thing and some factions. Slashers are not supernatural (unless they take up one of the other templates).

              Demon is full Fallen, albeit heavily homebrewed to fit with the nWoD system. They're very close to Spirits, but different beings ultimately. They're wrong about God creating everything, rather he stumbled upon it and made some attempts at playing Sims with the world before the whole rebellion left a sour taste in his mouth and so he left. The Loyalists I'm still not sure if I want them around but distant or gone as well. There are some adjustments to Factions too. Pentients wanna get back in God's good graces, Reconcilers focus on humanity instead and wanna get cuddly with them again since the whole rebellion was about them and all (or was it). Renegades are unaligned and Faustians are more objectively cold and exploitive of humanity generally speaking.

              Infernal Demons (from WoD: Inferno) exist, but are a lot more Spirit-based and likely originating from Pandemonium. They differ in origin and splat from the Fallen.

              Geist is about 99% in effect, I rarely draw from Wraith but I have one or two really obscure elements here or there from Oblivion. All in all though, Geist is the deal. Few fan-made Keys around, and as said the Kuei-Jin are Sin-Eaters who are just about the only faction of Sin-Eaters with organization and "governmental" structure; for once putting an organization in Asia rather than the US or EU, which is also why the Vampires haven't been able to settle in any large scale in Asia without making heavy concessions to the Cathayans. Otherwise it's about the same as the core game.

              Changeling is full Lost.
              Prometheans exist.
              Mummy is full Curse.
              Princess the Hopeful and Genius the Transgression are in play; for the most part so is Siren though I'm largely holding it off until it's done other than occasional sightings.

              That's about all I can think of.

                                                      A couple of thoughts I had which apply to the broader World of Darkness I have:

                                                      1. The Technocracy and Traditions are at peace: Well, more like a Cold War but the Avatar Storm provides so much more interesting fodder with both sides having been decapitated and fully intending to get around to murdering each other but it's been 17 years since then and that means there's a slight majority of people now (albeit low-level ones) who have never been at war with the Union as well as see the Traditionalist-Union conflict stupid. Even more so, all the leaders who HATE each other were fighters in the 90s so even the Traditionalists love technology while the Technocrats are kind of wondering why the hell anyone gives a crap about mages versus the literal VAMPIRES nearby.
                                                      (Which kind of hurts the peace treaty between them and the Camarilla)

                                                      2. The Gangrel never left: I don't see why they would anyway because there's no chance they're really going to get any better offer alone than they are with the Camarilla, whether they take it seriously or not.

                                                      3. Imbued are a finite number: Imbued still exist in this game but actually take the place of True Faith for the most part. There's a bunch of Hunters blessed by what they believe to be God's grace which give them a variety of oddball powers against monsters. They can be psychopaths or serial killers or saints but they are real and there's always about a 1000 of them in the world. Other Hunters are uncomfortable with them but it makes them a little harder to deal with. Sullivan Dane is one in my games and he's immortal.
                                                      Literally. He comes back from the dead when you kill him.

                                                      4. "Cyclical Nephandi" replace regular Nephandi: I mentioned this in the mage forum but Nephandi are less a bunch of Always Chaotic Evil psychos than a bunch of insane Gnostics who want to destroy reality so they can transcend and become God(s) with a capital G of their own perfected universe. They believe casting this reality down to oblivion is the only way to do it as it's hopelessly broken and flawed with Paradox.

                                                      This argument ACTUALLY MAKES SENSE to mages (albeit is hugely extreme) and that's the terrifying part. The Cauls are ostensibly showing the true nature of reality which warps a soul to this goal forever.

Gehenna is Cyclical: The Methusaleh's Thirst is a curse of Caine on vampires so all vampires eventually develop it and must eat their own children to gain their full power on awakening. A truly ancient vampire like Menele, Helene, or Ur-Shulgi may well be Third Generation as far as anyone knows it.

                                                      6. Hazimel was destroyed during the Week of Nightmares: Because I like the idea a 4th generation vampire was that terrifying.
