(like how each game has at least one 'Hunters' group or faction) but the imbued where the will of God (?) and a force to Reckon when the Final Days came..Thats why, i would like to help backing this proyect, even if its a fan proyect..but i really wish we could pull something up.

              A lot of hate against HtR? Personally I've encountered very little hatred towards HtR. In my experience KotE, DtF and CtD get the lion's share of hatred among OWoD lines. In fact I haven't encountered anyone that's hated HtR. The most I've met are people who don't care about HtR. They don't hate it but it doesn't appeal to them either.

              As for changes to HtR I have only one, artwork that is actually consistent with the material.

              And for non-Imbued hunters, there actually is a book for playing them. It's called Hunters Hunted II. HtR is not HtV. It's about the Imbued, those men and women that have been chosen and blessed, or perhaps cursed, with the knowledge of the monsters of the world and the power and will to do something about them. The non-Imbued hunters are their own thing, having existed long before HtR came out and generally have about as much to do with them as a member of the Society of Leopold does with an agent of Project Twilight.

                              That was actually one of the things I loved about the game, and also that it added to the mystery of the Imbuing and built into the idea of the End. It gave the setting character, but wasn't so stifling that it required, or rather restricted player choice.

                              So, I'd say keep it, but not actually answer the question at the end, which is very likely what would happen in a H20 book, as they tend to remove the Time of Judgment aspects. However, there is also a very strong suggestion that Hunters are being Imbued as a direct reaction to The End, so I'm not sure I'd actually want that removed.

                              Also, something that needs pointed out, Hunter's didn't just show up in 1999ish out of nowhere. They have actually been present before (in Dark Ages Inquisitor), and in small numbers pop up throughout history.

                              I'd actually love a book that basically focused on the Year of the Hunter, combining Hunter's Hunted Project Twilight, the Arcanum, and some HtR. To me, this seems like a very dang solid way to continue the 20th Anniversary line, but kind of doing what the main books have done and combining a lot of the material into one book and then updating them to the modern world.

                              I' pretty sure that HtR outsold both Wraith and Changling, so I'm confident that when the time comes, we will see a H20, and from there, I think it will really be about how people back it. Being that Hunter is sort of the opposite of both CtD and WtO in that they got 1st Ed and 2nd Ed, but no Revised, HtR only got a Revised edition, ad so not sure just how much it would need to change/update. As far as technology goes, it's still pretty up to date with a few obvious tweeks. If I recall, it already talks about the risks of cell phone videos, and the assumption is that monsters just will not let these things be taken seriously on the internet. It already contains Text Messages, easy internet access, and <YouTube>. In the states, I'd think one of the few real changes might be things like the Patriot Act?
