    4th eddition: better one-world system.

    05-05-2016, 04:30 AM
    This isn't a cause for ballance. I'm not saying neonates should be on the same level as claith and that elder changelings should be as powerful as cathayans of the same age. Nor should the 4th dot of a mage sphere be equal to the 4th dot of a changeling cantrip. Nor do hunters with numina and formori...

    I could go on. Point is that the world of darkness is inherently not fair. But I think mechanics are strongly lacking in some areas, and it'd be nice if we got a better picture of some areas.


    I think the greatest lack in mechanics is of course powers with 'Difficulty=Stats they don't have'. We could go with the werewolf gift Mothers touch and how to heal someone, the difficulty is the target's rage, or five if they don't have rage. Or the gift Loki's touch where the difficulty is Rage+rank. Vampires fair somewhat better with Virtue+attribute (Since yeah, you could haphazardly assume someone's virtues through their rollplaying if you don't have them on a character sheet) But Ultimately I see this as a big problem.

    Secondly, well, some books imply more predators than others. Vampires in the VTM perspective know of werewolves,cathayans,changelings,mages,hunters and wraiths. That's really it if we don't count mummies, they're all wisely avoided.
    Now werewolf, on the otherhand, has all that, plus spirits which, to be fair, is a thing vampires are practically blind to, and then formori and pentex which some vampires work with.
    We go to mage and there's more nephandi and mauraders and the technocracy which totaly helps with the masquerade and their amazing clones and HIT marks which have found themselves in the hands of vampires and pentex...


    Now, It tends to be that vampire is first and everything's just added on to vampire/what came between. See the thing is that this keeps resetting so we've not really got a cool vampire setting with HIT marks and formori and Nephandi and the like.

    I'm still trying to struggle with what's in the sewers to be honest. With vampire; Just the nosferatu (and the niktuku hunting them, maybe tzmisce and an odd alligator) With werewolf it's the nosferatu, The ratkin, the Freakfeat, a few Gnawers... Really wondering how anything gets done down there.
    "Tony, you ghouled the wrong rat for the last time"
    *backstab*

    I'm not wanting anything equal or fair, I just want a clear modus operandi for playing all the games together. Nobody should give me weird looks when my Vampire tells the fianna, through medium of sluagh whom I communicate with through wraith intermediaries, of a mage plot that'll topple the environmentally friendly green government in the area, which will ultimately cause a fianna blood bath and allow me to get in and steal myself the magickal laser guns required to kill the nexus crawler i left within on of my ghouls after I thought I'd need a mighty formor to defend me against the might of stygia, who as you all know are quite pissed at me for using wraiths as emissaries to the unseelie.

    Alright, that's a joke. a massive mess. but, y'know, some books imply a mess. It really seems like we've got too many supernaturals sometimes and everywhere is something's territory. Unless it's vampire where you know Lupines stay out of the cities, Mages stay hidden, faeries are at best curiosities, advanced tech isn't really there and formori don't exist. I like that vampire focuses on itself, The courtly and inner struggles are a good place to focus; but there are way too many assets to take advantage of if you look out a bit, and every other line implies that vampires are in on their protagonist's problems.

    Using a HIT mark rather than a ghoul seems a bit more ethical. Making deals with pentex to get rid of the lupine menace seems like an ok idea. Finding out what owns the sewers seems like a solid plan. Having a mage hook you up with stuff is quite excellent. Having a vampire clean up it's businesses it owns in return for attacks on his rival seems like a good idea (not that garou were ever fond of those).

    As an added note- Mage was really more fun with it's whole huge, expanded weapon list. My friend was telling me I can't have a .50 anti-material rifle in vampire because the game Wasn't designed for that, even when I pointed to mage and said it's got a .50 weapon in it's rules. I really wanted a 5th gen dead, a .50 is big enough to justify lethal werever it hits and does more damage than silly-elder levels of stamina and fortitude can reliably deal with; and I like the tech advantage neonates have. But, some people are stingy. I liked Mage's approach. 20mm anti material rifle.

    TL;DR/Recap
    -Don't ballance supernaturals against supernaturals. Garou were meant to be stronger, Fae meant to be weaker..
    -Do put in new systems for when not using a power against the same kind (IE don't ask for a Kindred's Rage+Rank). Werewolf is a pretty terrible defender for this. Also don't do 'extra Dice is equal to valor' cause guys outside that renown system end up confused. Also don't claim damage unsoakable when it could be soaked by other supernaturals.
    - Explain the contested areas in a way that makes sociological,political... Whatever sense. How are the sewers shared? How much do secret organisations know/deal with secret organisations (ok, that one sounds like a WoD general book) How do the fleabags react when a cainite walks through a bonegnawer sept? What happens when X tries to takeover a company owned by Ventrue, Glass walkers, Pentex, Yeren, The Technocracy (or traditions) A sidhe (do Changelings do that?) or just humans that are damn wary about the shit they know some freaks pull. I mean multi-million dollar companies are a highly contested resource and I'm sure each group encounters things which shouldn't be when they compete for them.
    - I'm not asking for vampire to have the whole of every other book inside of it.

    Or maybe I'm asking for a generic Classic world of darkness 4th edition book to lay the groundwork.

      Considering what we've seen in the BNS Werewolf Delta Slice you'll probably have your wish. Martin has talked a lot about the One World of Darkness and how they are going to build it up from the bottom up so it works all together. Sadly, for me that will probably be the day when I stop supporting this gameline and invest into other things that actually interest me.

      The WoD as a completely interconnected and well reasoned whole is a pipe dream. The games themselves pretty much trample the basic societal and environmental constraints between them. Each of the games actually focuses on what matters for itself, and the other critters and their idiosyncracies are not it. I do not want a Mage or Werewolf game where i have to worry about the Masquerade, Antedelluvians ot the Hieararchy. Anytime another critter shows up it's supposed to be strange, exotic and understood within the terms of the game you're playing, not a mythical perfectly interconnected world.

                                                          The biggest problem with a single cosmology is, where do you start? Do you start with the Judeo-Christian cosmology of Demon the Fallen and Vampire and say that that cosmology is correct and werewolves (and others) are deluded and misunderstand what is going on? Do you do the opposite and start from the Werewold/Mage view with a sort of Triat? Whichever is chosen, it is going to alienate a large swath of players as their game is now objectively wrong.

                                                          The World of Darkness is meant to be integrated to a degree, but I think having a single cosmology is a bad idea. It was one of the worst sins Demon the Fallen did, trying to absorb other games cosmology into itself, effectively saying the members of those gamelines (werewolves, kuei-jin, mummies, etc) were objectively wrong and the major figures of their cosmologies (Gaia, the Triat, the Ebony Dragon, etc) were just angels and such.

                                                          Still, it wasn't that bad since Demon the Fallen was it's own game and had its own cosmology and it didn't interfere with the other games. The books never actuallt said, "these guys are right," even if it did heavily imply it. But with a "one world of darkness " that likely won't still be the case and we'll have one cosmology to rule them all.
