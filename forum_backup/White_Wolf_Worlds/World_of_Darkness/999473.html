    Demon the Fallen (generic questions)

    11-06-2016, 07:01 PM
    Hello!

    I'm back with some new generic questions about Demon the Fallen, let's start!

    1) I'm planning to settle a story with newbie demons in 2016, but malestorm apart,
    what else can drag out a group of demons from the Abyss? Do you have any suggestion?

    2) When a demon run away from the Abyss he's directly dragged to the most suitable
    vessel? Or the fallen has to wander among human in his spiritual form looking for a body?

    3) Faustian pacts can involve a third party? Something like "Heal my sister's leg."?

    4) How an enemy demon can be defeated? I mean, they're spirits, If you kill the host,
    they just need for a new body. There's a final way to dispose of an enemy?

    Thank you! I hope you can reply me!

      1- They can be summoned by mortals. There are lots of ways for this to have happened, from fake rituals made by adolescents that prove real, to an old enemy arranging for a summon ritual to happen, so they can get revenge on the newly-summoned demon.

      2- Wander around. They are very fast in this form, but it is mostly a "manual" task. And there is no guarantee that it is the most suitable vessel. Usually it is just the only option around. And not rarely a bad one.

      3- Yes, they can, but the demon needs to have the means to do it by itself. To make something with the power of Faith that the demon can't usually do, it have to be a change in the body or mind of the one who asks. You can't even grant things like Resources just with the power of the Pact. You can make it the hard way to accomplish your side of the bargain, though.

      4- You can literally suck an enemy if you're fast enough. You get a little more power and terminates your enemy. You're also vulnerable to this. I usually prefer to put this aside and let demons be immortal, but it is just personal taste.

                          Something like that would certainly be a major storyhook. Note that most Maelstorms didn't unleash demons from the abyss, only the last one did. Depending what the leanings of your PCs are there should be a plethora of possible hooks to get them together though.

                                          Originally posted by Ipergigio View Post
                                          1) I'm planning to settle a story with newbie demons in 2016, but malestorm apart,
                                          what else can drag out a group of demons from the Abyss? Do you have any suggestion?
                                          Basically anyone that knows the name of the demon in question can do it.
                                          Wizards, Vampires, Werewolves, plain mortals... just about anyone can do it.

                                          EDIT:
                                          A mummy with nomenclature might be capable of gleaning names by accident.
                                          By the same token they might be capable of accidentally breaching the prison that is hell.
                                          In any case those potential nomenclature powers are beyond 5 anyway.

                                          Originally posted by Ipergigio View Post
                                          2) When a demon run away from the Abyss he's directly dragged to the most suitable
                                          vessel? Or the fallen has to wander among human in his spiritual form looking for a body?
                                          Dragged to the most suitable vessel it is, BUT
                                          you could arrange it that those summoning your group of demons are considered "soulless" and inhabit the would-be summoners.
                                          It could be that the would-be summoners are ground down by life, only go about life and only one among them is still energetic enough
                                          to coerce the others into action. Basically, anyone but that guy - who could end up VERY, VERY unlucky - is soulless and counts as a suitable vessel.

                                          If you want to go with a seriously dark twist on the whole issue, I had a player who played a Rabisu and he asked for a twist on the whole summon thingy.
                                          He ended up in the body of a unborn child, summoned by the cultists of a earthbound demon who wanted a demon servant.

                                          Originally posted by Ipergigio View Post
                                          3) Faustian pacts can involve a third party? Something like "Heal my sister's leg."?
                                          Yupp... and you don't even need to use the faith of the potential thrall.
                                          If you can fulfill the conditions of the pact on your side without using the mortals faith or your own to seal the deal, all the better.
                                          A Asharu can heal with the right Lore and if someone is willing to make a pact to see his sister healed, he can do that with his Lore and call it a day.
                                          Technically, it could be done with mere mortal skills, especially given in your example.
                                          Unless the leg is in danger of being lost, there is probably no need to use lores or the faith of a potential thrall to undo the damage.
                                          Furthermore, ANY demon is best advised to keep as much faith of their thralls available for himself or to enhance them.

                                          Originally posted by Ipergigio View Post
                                          4) How an enemy demon can be defeated? I mean, they're spirits, If you kill the host,
                                          they just need for a new body. There's a final way to dispose of an enemy?
                                          Demons can - as already stated - commit an act similar to diablerie to get rid of an opponent for good.
                                          If you want to mess with them there is the option of using antagonists who are capable of necromancy.
                                          In that case Mummies can be surprisingly effective.
                                          Sure, most necromancers can't destroy the demon per se, BUT a good necromancer is capable of stowing the demon spirit in an item.
                                          Turning a demon into a glorified paper weight is awesome.

                                          In any case, demons are so limited in numbers that even really old vampires, wizards or something else
                                          aren't probably going to believe anyone that those truly exist.
                                          Even worse, they don't know about their powers... like nothing at all.
                                          A mage might be able to piece together that vampires on general are fast, tough and strong,
                                          but he will come up with a blank in regards to demons.
                                          This is their greatest weapon against other splats.
                                          Misdirecting their opponents into believing what THEY want to believe or playing up their own capabilities is the way to go.

                                          Also, as a demon fresh out of hell:
                                          Get a handful of thralls as priority number one, consequences be damned if necessary, because those bodies the demons use are rather fragile.
