    Dual Weilding

    05-26-2015, 12:11 AM
    Two pistols allow you to do more attacks in a round (and reduce recoil penalties if you using 2nd edition rules). So what's a point of melee dual wielding? Are there any bonuses? As i understand RAW there is +1 difficulty to offhand attacks, so if i have two swords i will use my main weapon every single strike. Did i miss something?

      Generally speaking in the cWOD rules in most editions... there really isn't a point to most dual wielding.

      For melee, the best use is to have something that offers a defensive bonus in you off hand like a shield or a parrying dagger. This usually gives you a better parry roll even with the off-hand penalty than not; or provides passive benefits in some of the systems.

                                                      I usually use these rules

                                                      Multiple attack (1 weapon)
                                                      Divide dice pools, but the attacks are divided along the Initiative of the Character (i.e. Initiative 12, character makes 3 attacks, 1 at Initiative 12, 1 at initiative 8 and the last at initiative 4).

                                                      Dual Weapons
                                                      Divide dice pools, the two attacks are simoultaneous, +1 difficulty to the lesser hand (+2 if one weapon require a strong grip or precision, or the weapons are different).

                                                      Dual Weapons with Ambidextrous Merit
                                                      Divide dice pools, the two attacks are simoultaneous (+1 difficulty if one weapon require a strong grip or precision, or the weapons are different - no malus if Perception is at least 3).
