                                                          Originally posted by Aleph View Post
                                                          Not so much for the time, but because the published material wasn't that big.
                                                          I think Demon doesn't need a compilation.
                                                          I think this applies to most of the post "First Five," cWOD lines. Hunter was probably the one with the most books of that group (24 not counting Apocrypha and published fiction). But the vast majority of its run was the core three (Core/ST Handbook/Players Guide), nine Creedbooks, and seven "crossover" books for the other game lines/existing monster hunting organizations.

                                                          KotE clocked in at 17 books (also with a large portion of that being splat books). Demon hit 8, Mummy 2 (4 is you count the early edition ones before it became a separate game line), and Orpheus (which seems to be getting worked into Wr20 anyway) got its 6.

                                                          Even if we were to get 20th edition books addressing those... HtR and KotE are the only two that you could even conceive of having 20th edition supplements for. The others should be simple to do in a 500+ page core tome for people that might want them while a brand new cWOD 4th edition is being worked on.
