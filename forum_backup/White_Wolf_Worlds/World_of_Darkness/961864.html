    Mortars and artillery..

    09-01-2016, 04:26 PM
    Over on one of my Star wars D6 sites, we are having a discussion revolving around artillery and mortars, and the discussion got into whether pcs would stand a greater chance than the normal person to 'detect' incoming rounds of artillery/mortars before they got hit...
    For a werewolf/Vampire game where these were used, what would you say the difficulty would be, for say a Perception+alertness check to detect them enough to get a dodge?
    Would you need a gift or discipline active to get said check??

                          I think mortars are much more survivable and more likely for a vampire to encounter than artillery. A vampire or werewolf that is hit by an artillery round should be considered to be a dead, as the standard US artillery is an M119 Howitzer, which launches a 14 kilogram projectile at 500 meters per second, giving it a kinetic energy of around 1,750,000 Joules (the average arrow possesses 75 Joules of kinetic energy by comparison). Nothing that takes 4 dice of damage from an arrow is going to have a chance of surviving a direct hit by artillery (superhuman senses may allow you to dodge it though, allowing a vampire or werewolf to have a chance of surviving). If you want though, let them try to soak around 120 dice of lethal damage, which is what I would consider to be a reasonable approximation of the damage from a direct hit of a M119 Howitzer.

                                          Originally posted by Aya Tari View Post
                                          Yes, but that is because no one in White Wolf either understood ballistics or cared about ballistics. If you want to make something more consistent with reality, I would really suggest going with 1 dice of damage per millimeter of diameter. If you want neonate vampires to possibly survive direct hits from a 122mm howitzer (a Stamina 4 neonate with Fortitude 3 will take an average of 12 health levels of lethal damage from a direct hit with one success, which is halved to 6 health levels because it is a ballistic weapon), go ahead and use the default figures.
                                          Artillery damage typically isn't from ballistics as in raw KE damage, but from the payload; it's the difference between being shot at and having a bomb detonated around you. Also, it's not reasonable to allow vampires to halve the damage of explosives and big guns, because those weapons aren't killing people because they're hitting vital organs or the like, but because support weapons and vehicular-mounted guns are so utterly destructive that they typically turn soft targets to hamburger or pink mist. Vampires would also tend to get turned into hamburger and pink mist alongside mortals, and should take full damage accordingly.

                                          I mean, it's true that WW was, and in many cases still is bad with math, but vampires should be getting splatted alongside normal humans unless they have plot device Fortitude. Also, the ST can always rule that, because the damage and breeching rules were on a different scale, that hamburger and pink mist automatically result from use of these weapons on a soft target, no roll necessary unless you have an outrageously high soak pool or somehow are closer to or even beyond the scale of the weapon extremely large Mokole might treat tank cannons as rifles or pistols, for example, and layered soak Gifts in Crinos might allow Garou and Fera to survive being in the effect zone (good thing no one can really justify an industrialized process for making silver artillery rounds, and gold flak is right out). There's also some indication that whatever scale of increase gets used for dice pools might not work with a strict bore size to dice system. The V Revised ST booklet with the screen suggests that approach, I think. For those who do get a roll, however, you'd probably be better served by re-scaling things to make them manageable.

                                                          I usually just handle Artillery (the one time it came up) as doing levels of damage instead of dice. It takes out a lot of the randomized factor in whether or not something can survive it. And damage, especially on the high end is more and more of an abstraction. 30 levels of damage does pretty much auto-kill anything outside of the truly monstrous enemies in the WoD, which works just fine.

                                                          There is the possibility of a soak specialized Garou or Vampire that is prepared for such an attack to survive, but at that point you are planning damage around an individual as opposed to the system.

                                                          Per dodging, I would allow dodging for mortars assuming the being in question has reasonable knowledge of the attack. They really do area damage anyway, so maybe just have the dodge grant soak dice against the damage. Dodging Artillery is a weird concept, but certainly some beings in the WoD can do it. I seem to remember dodging a bullet without cover being Dif 9, so maybe allow the same roll if the creature is aware of the attack. Possibly count every 2 successes as 1 if you really want to penalize the dodge attempt.
