        Originally posted by breggie View Post
        I guess it's just that I don't really care for the game design of "if you didn't realize you needed a node at CC then you are just SOL. Make a new character or wait until you can buy Prime 4".
        The thing is... you're not SoL if you don't start with a Node. It just means you lack one specific resource under your direct control. Your Mentor has one, but gets to veto you using it, let alone your whole Cabal using it. Etc.

        Then again, I can't really think of much that you absolutely need quintessence for except for making Wonders, and that likewise requires Prime 4.
        That is, ultimately, one of the reasons that the game goes with a relative scarcity of Nodes/etc. You really don't need them outside of very specific circumstances; those specifics are very important to mage society as a whole, but not that important to daily life as an individual mage.

        Originally posted by Prometheas View Post
        There's inherent issues with trying to assign quintessence value based on "worth" though. Worth is subjective, Especially metaphysical worth because metaphysic themselves are subjective.
        Eh. One one hand yes, but on the other if you're playing Ascension you've already mastered this skill because it applies to so much of the game as it is.

        Besides, if you look at prime too closely you'll notice quintessence rarity is as balance as a D&D gold economy, there are just too many ways to break it and undermine that assumption that gold/quint is rare. Quintessence being hard to get is entirely a setting handwave.
        I think there's a certain white-room analysis here that don't quite work. What high level Prime can do regarding increasing access to quintessence isn't 1-3 success simple feats of magic. They're innately high difficulty, and easily fall into lots and lots of successes needed to make work. The expense and difficulty of mastering Spheres also means there aren't that many Prime 4+ mages running around at any given moment in the setting to "break the economy."


        Originally posted by Kakost View Post
        Wait, cant you trade your own HP for Quint anymore with Prime 1?

        It use to be that you could keep burning your 1st health level to trade for Quint in a daily basis with only Prime 1
        It's still there, but a bit more constrained:

        1) It's 1 quintessence/HL, so it's only 1/day max if you want to rely on natural healing.

        2) It costs 1 quintessence to heal if you need to heal it early, so it nets to zero under stress.

        3) Healing agg requires significant rest. You're not supposed to do that, and then spend a day doing hard ritual magic, or getting into combat, and expect to regain that health.

        Basically, it's supposed to be much more of a desperation tactic rather than a normal daily routine thing for most mages, because most mages don't want to spend 24 hrs doing bedrest for 1 quintessence.

            The best way in the end is to seek Tass, it is the most versatile way to find free Quintessence for both characters and ST. Chantries and individual mages may have small (or not so small) hoards of Tass, sometimes you can find still usable Tass a certain distance from the source Node for reasons, and anyone keeping a Node also has Tass no matter how they call it, or even notice it.

            What you do once you find a source of Tass is up to you. You can take the place by force, sure, but you can also focus on stealing just the Tass, or trading it for something or some service. That basically means that a character looking for Tass will find it with relative ease if they're not picky and are ready to deal with whatever holds this Tass, and the ST may include some Tass behind any interesting task whatsoever. Add some complications on how to preserve some of it during the trip and you're ready to go.

                                                Originally posted by Dogstar View Post
                                                they've decided that they want a bunch of Quintessence to prepare for a trip into the underworld.
                                                I intuit from this that it is a Traditions Chronicle, since Technocrats generally don't do such voyages.

                                                Originally posted by monteparnas View Post
                                                What you do once you find a source of Tass is up to you. You can take the place by force, sure, but you can also focus on stealing just the Tass, or trading it for something or some service. That basically means that a character looking for Tass will find it with relative ease if they're not picky and are ready to deal with whatever holds this Tass, and the ST may include some Tass behind any interesting task whatsoever. Add some complications on how to preserve some of it during the trip and you're ready to go.
                                                Trade, pillage or theft... I have some ideas. From the quick read I just did, I think other posters have covered the subject of Trading for Tass better than I could. it's usually not advisable for Traditions to claim Technocracy ode by force, whether to hold or loot. That leaves stealing.

                                                Consider, if you have the Spheres, enchanting a Quadcopter. If you have both Prime and Correspondence, stick a Wonder-Drone over the Technomancers house, lab or what have you. Intercept the Quintessence coming down.

                                                Like mine, you're running a low resource chronicle from outset up to the point you are. Forcing players to be resourceful is actually way more fun than starting with all the goodies. At the very least, your Cabal can detect where Nodes are. There is bound to be someone in the Cabal who has a profession-related attribute at 3. Technology - Electrician (with Science, Electrical Engineer). Medicine 3, IIRK, is pretty close to medical doctor.

                                                Step 1: One character lands a high paying job. After the first pay-check arrives in their checking account, approach the bank to inquire about purchasing a new home with a Transferable mortgage. Use Mind if needed (the Mind Mage hangs around in line of sight). With support from the bank...

                                                Step 2: Shop for the lowest price property near the Node. Approach the city and, in the same way as with the bank, ask for planning permission for some renovations. Ask for a dual Residential/Commercial Zoning permit sufficient to run a business from this location.

                                                2A) A rural area: Renovate, which will involve Vulgar Magic Without Witnesses. Spend as much Quintessence as you need to improve the outcome of the magic-aspect. Raise the value in whatever way suits your Cabal's skill combination. You want be able to host parties, entertain business clients and have a workshop/lab for magic purposes (making bullet resistant armor, cellular sniffing rods, Quint-Syphon quadcopters, etc.).

                                                2B) Suburban: Focus on the commercial aspect. If you need a workshop, prioritize soundproofing.

                                                2C) Inner city: As an extra-special bonus, there is usually crime affecting property values. So, you will scout any gantsa-neighbors, learn their secrets, but hold off on shutting them down until you have expanded to your satisfaction. Generate business income and use it to officially hire other members of the Cabal so that they have taxable income and improved credit ratings. Rinse and repeat for every member of the Cabal, Acolyte or whoever.

                                                The owner of the Node you are starting to surround might take notice. On the other hand, they may not be there, having used the accrued Quintessence / Tass to fund similar expeditions to the one mentioned OP (Void Engineers going to Pluto). Either way, you are far enough back that they have to leave the Node if they want to "visit", forgoing the advantage that their Node provides where competing magic is concerned. What about the element of surprise? - Time 2.

                                                If you have a Virtual Adept or other spy-type mage, bug everything; police station, city hall, the bank office, etc. The Node owner may pull connections to thwart your expansion. Record all such conversations and retain a lawyer. At this point, you should be in an improved position to negotiate with Node Owner who your Cabal likely outnumbers.

                                                In the long run, they have two choices. They can sell, in which case you borrow and buy. They can try to buy up property as you are doing, in which case, you can sell, since what you own has no special metaphysical value and has increased significantly in dollar value.

                                                Returning to the subject of Trade / Pillage / Steal, there is another option - Subdue. Mages who have a Node, are usually well established enough that they would tend to fit the Network Type of Cabal or Technocracy Team (use to be called Symposium).

                                                From: Cabal Types: member-proximity, public association, role in Chantry/Mage Society

                                                Originally posted by HorizonParty2021 View Post
                                                I will just start with two completely different types of Cabals.

                                                The first kind is the one players usually play. The Mages live together, go most places together and cast beneficial magic on each other.

                                                The second more closely fits the definition of a Cabal. Members hide their association with each other and carry out a clandestine agenda in the community. They would make use of a Mindlink to coordinate their activities.
                                                The longer a cabal survives, the more it would stretch out, meaning members would be more likely encountered alone. Since, you are a relatively new Cabal, it stands to reason, you would stick together and keep an eye out for anyone with a grenade-launcher. You can gang up on a single mage and cast every type of charm, fatigue or short-term fate (Entropy) spell, only one of which can be countered in a single round. Bring them under your influence, one by one.

                                                I hope this offers a perspective on strategy that you can derive ideas from, suitable to your own chronicle.

                                                Thank you for passing time with me in conversation. My Hacks.
