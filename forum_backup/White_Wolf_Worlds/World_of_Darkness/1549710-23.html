    Prime 4

    •••• Expel or Infuse Energy/ Tap Wellspring/ Craft Tass & Permanent Wonders

    The terrible power of draining Quintessence from objects or forces (though not yet from living things) can disintegrate those targets, consume them in Primal flames, or decay them almost instantly. Reversing that flow, the mage can craft objects that cannot be broken, or bond organic and inorganic materials together to create cybernetic implants, nanotech, and other Wonders. At this stage, she is able to enchant items permanently and draw Quintessence from the energetic Wellsprings of exciting events. By infusing her personal Quintessence into a Periapt, the mage might use Matter 4 to craft a Soulgem: a portable vessel that’s filled with her own Resonance and energy.

    Drain Quintessence (Expel Energy)

    At Prime 4 a mage can rip the quintessence out of Forces and Matter patterns. It appears that the mage (or at least player) can choose the form this takes. There is no mention of supplemental spheres.

    It is not stated what the mage can do with this quintessence, but the operative term is ‘draining’ so presumably it can go directly into his personal pool, a reservoir like tass or a periapt, or even fuel spells directly.


    External Sublimation Engine

    One application of this is burning some kind of fuel for quintessence to reduce the difficulty of other spells. I could just about see an Etherite with a back mounted furnace that he keeps shoveling fuel into to power his gear (instruments).


    Infuse Quintessence (Infuse Energy)

    The mage can likewise channel extra quintessence into an object or force. It isn’t clear what this does for a force, but it makes objects unbreakable. This is an ability we could use more explanation but it appears that this refines, reinforces, or perfects patterns. Beyond just making things unbreakable, this could likely improve them in other ways.


    The Eternal Flame

    One possible way this could affect forces is make them continue forever, or at least until the quintessence and spell run out. Given there is no indication of this spell burning through the infused quintessence and that Permanent Enchantments are possible at this rank, that really could be forever, though. This would make flames that burn forever (temporarily), areas that always shove whatever passes through them, eternal voltage sources, etc.


    Permanent Enchantment (???)


    Most spheres don’t mention permanency until rank 5, but it is rank 4 for enchantments on items. That said, I think the standard questions apply. Are we talking about permanent spells or permanent results? That’s going to be an ST call.


    Craft Wonders (Craft Wonders)

    A mage with Prime 4 has the full ability to create wonders. The wonder rules are not in the sphere description,

    Interestingly, no supplemental spheres are required to bond organic and inorganic materials together, or create cybernetics or nano bots. I don’t take this to mean no spheres are required to set up the Wonder’s effects, but it does sound like none beyond those and Prime are required to make the wonder and, more notably, to install it where it is supposed to go.


    Cybernetics

    Ambiguity Resolution

    When I think of cybernetics I usually think of Matter and Life grafting, which would be in the Life 5/Matter 3+ range for a human target. It really appears that Prime 4 is an alternate method. Given we’ve seen Prime be used as an animating force before, this is not unreasonable.


    Craft Tass (Craft Tass)


    We have this solely from the headers, it isn’t mentioned in the text itself. Still, it appears that a mage with Prime 4 can turn quintessence into Tass. With no guidance I would think this applies to creating Tass from his personal Quintessence Pool, or directly from any source that he draws from.


    Custom Flavor Bomb

    Tass is often flavored with resonance. With control over where and how the Tass is made, the mage can potentially create Tass for specific purposes.


    Draw Quintessence from Events (Tap Wellspring)

    At this rank a mage doesn’t need to have a full fledged Node but can, at the appropriate moments, tap into temporary moments of power. These would seem to include both places that periodically bring forth quintessence, and moments, events, or situations that combine to temporarily bring forth that essence.


    Pink Slime

    The slime from Ghostbusters 2 is essentially congealed Tass created not by a Node but by the permanent Wellspring that is NYC in the 80’s, or rather by the temporary but frequent Wellsprings of pleasant social encounters, easy traffic, and polite window washers of NYC in the 80s.


    Conjunctional Effects:

    Craft Soulgem (Infuse Energy + Transmute Complex Matter?)

    Ambiguity Resolution - What is a Soulgem?


    I want to stress this wording because it is the biggest impediment to figuring out Prime’s periapt progression:

    By infusing her personal Quintessence into a Periapt, the mage might use Matter 4 to craft a Soulgem: a portable vessel that’s filled with her own Resonance


    This clearly sounds like the mage takes an existing periapt and turns it into a Soulgem, and that this Soulgem contains his resonance. That this starts with an existing periapt reinforces that Prime 3 is the rank to create periapts. The description of a soulgem as a container for resonance does the same. On the other hand there is no information on why a mage would want a container of his own resonance and what such a thing could do. Soulgems are not mentioned anywhere else in M20.
    “Legends tell that the Hollow Ones used to leave burning bags of their ‘resonance’ on the Chantry doorstep, ring the bell, then run away…”

    The main possibility based on M20 alone is that Soulgem is the term for a material periapt, and this is just a convoluted name for Craft Periapt. This is consistent with Prime 5’s description of a Soulflower as a living periapt and also makes it clear why a mage would want to do this. This interpretation, though less consistent with the text as written, actually has a meaning. This contradicts Prime 3’s stated ability to Craft Periapts, though, unless we assume that rank can only make temporary ones. It also doesn’t explain why this needs Matter when crafting a temporary periapt does not and crafting a permanent wonder does not.

    Now if we consult other sources we can get a few ideas. Revised does mention Soulgems as a type of periapt that can only be used by the mage who made it. That makes sense as a step up from a normal periapt, so Prime 4 makes sense, but would in no way need Matter 4.

    HDYDT depicts them as a periapt that travels with the mage as if it were consecrated. That is a terrible effect for Prime 4 alone, let alone Prime 4/Matter 4. IIf we make some key wording do a lot of heavy lifting we can infer that this is only usable by the mage. This also in no way needs Matter 4, but at least with that effect Prime 4 makes sense.

    Those are both consistent with what is written in Prime 4, though I’d probably never think of either one on my own.

    Thoughts?

                                        Prime 5

                                        ••••• Infuse or Withdraw Life Force/ Create Node & Soulflower/ Nullify Paradox

                                        A Prime Master can draw Quintessence from anywhere, at any time, and channel it into other vessels as well. A dark, vulgar aspect of that power allows her to obliterate a living being by consuming all of his life force, whereas the reverse of that power infuses him with life force so strong that he’s essentially blessed. Combining that ability with Life 5, she may turn complex organisms into Soulflowers: living Periapts who become walking batteries of boosted Quintessence. Such Mastery also allows the mage to create Nodes in significant places, and to nullify Paradox as described under Nullifying Paradox, p. 549.

                                        Zero Point Quintessence (???)

                                        The mage can now draw quintessence from anywhere and at any time and likewise channel it to just about any means. There’s not much more to say about this.


                                        Energy Vampire (Withdraw Life Force)

                                        This extends the energy draining powers of Prime 4 to life patterns. The text specifically mentions obliterating the being, though at such a high rank lesser scourgings should also be possible. Note that this does not require Life.

                                        Because a mage can draw quintessence from anywhere, drawing it from a creature is a deliberate choice. Notably the text focuses more on the destruction than on the energy gained but this does work by consuming the life force so the energy must be gained.

                                        ST’s are going to need to decide how much quintessence this gives. There is not guidance here.


                                        You Can’t Eat Just One

                                        The resonance of quintessence derived this way likely lends itself to destruction and consumption, so it will lend itself to helping in similar spells in the future. A mage who gains enough resonance this way will likely also have an easier time-consuming creatures and a harder time using tamer forms of quintessence. In the right story this could even become an important element. I suppose this is less an example and more a potential consequence.


                                        Bless (Infuse Life Force)


                                        While actual effects are thin, this appears to be the living upgrade of the Prime 4 power. Prime 4 could make objects nigh unbreakable, so this power would make people nigh unbreakable. It is reasonable that immortality is another possible option. Like Energy Vampire, there’s no indication this requires Life. As with Prime 4 there is no indication that the quintessence runs out... or that it doesn't.


                                        Plot Immunity

                                        This power turns someone into a cinematic hero (or villain) who can shrug off things mere mortals would be incapacitated or killed by. This is both a good way of thinking about what Bless is capable of and a possible mark to shoot for with Bless.


                                        Create Node (Create Node)


                                        At Prime 5 a mage can create nodes. The reference to significant areas indicates they can’t be created just anywhere. I suspect the lack of guidance isn’t an oversight as the decision of what counts as significant matters more to the table than to the game systems. The limitation turns what could be a broken power into a story seed instead.


                                        Create Wellspring or Juncture

                                        If a Prime Master can create a Node, he should also be able to engineer a wellspring or even a Juncture. The mage could take a moment not quite significant enough to be a wellspring and open it up as one. This could be either a one time event or a recurring one.

                                        He could also take a moment of seeming importance, like a longstanding tradition or a recently discovered astronomical phenomenon, and tip it over the edge into a Juncture. With time and patience he could even create a tradition to build this event around.

                                        While Nodes would seem to be superior, Junctures would have two advantages. The first is creating them is easier, the second is you don’t have to defend them when they are not active.


                                        Nullify Paradox (Nullify Paradox)


                                        The rules for this are given elsewhere and basically let a mage eliminate paradox in his own pattern, though at rank 5 Prime can probably eliminate paradox in someone else’s pattern as well.



                                        Everything Else (???)


                                        As with all Rank 5s, Prime 5 should be able to do just about everything possible within the Sphere’s purview. Unless the ST specifically wants something locked behind archspheres, it should be possible here.


                                        Conjunctional Effects: Create Soulflower (Create Soulflower)

                                        Ambiguity Resolution

                                        At Prime 5 a mage can extend the Soulgem creation powers to living creatures and create Soulflowers. All the same possibilities from Create Soulgem are present here. This time, at least, there is a direct statement that Soulflowers are periapts, though.

                                        The three possibilities from before are:

                                        1 – Soulflowers are permanent periapts made from living creatures.

                                        2 – Soulflowers are living periapts only the mage can use

                                        3 – Soulflowers are living periapts consecrated to the mage (and possibly only he can use them)

                                        1 and 2 still have no indication of why this would require Life 5 when the Prime 3 version does not. Consecration of living things requires Life, so at least possibility 3 has a reason for including it, though Life 5 is not consistent with what Prime 2 uses.

                                                            Spirit 1

                                                            • Spirit Sight/ Spirit Sense

                                                            To most of humanity, the spirit world remains invisible. Not to a mage who knows the Spirit Sphere. Although he’ll be most attuned to spirits with Resonance similar to his own, that mage can read the local Gauntlet’s thickness, discern auras, sense spirits of all types, peek into the Penumbra through the Vidare (see Chapters Three and Four), and determine whether or not a material object has a spiritual component (as mystic Fetishes do).

                                                            Combined with other Spheres, that mage can spot forces, places, or items with unusual ties to the spirit world, such as Awakened objects, elemental spirits, possessed organisms, Shallowings, Nodes, and so forth.

                                                            Sense Gauntlet and See Through Gauntlet (Spirit Sight)

                                                            A mage can both sense the Gauntlet and sense across the Gauntlet. Sensing the Gauntlet gives it’s local thickness and likely any meddling done with it.

                                                            Seeing across the Gauntlet allows to see the Penumbra of the Otherworlds using one of the Vidare as a lens. If the mage is in the Penumbra they should also be able look into the physical.
                                                            Note that seeing the strength of the gauntlet should include seeing an actual hole in the gauntlet (aka a Shallowing), which for some reason is stated to require conjunctional effects. Perhaps there are things about a shallowing that can’t be determined without help, but actually seeing one should be purely in the domain of Spirit.


                                                            See Gauntlet Realms

                                                            This is not really my specialty, but as I recall Werewolf has certain realms hidden in the layers of the Gauntlet itself. If there are any that apply to Mage, a mage would be able to see them with enough successes.


                                                            See Spirits (Spirit Sight)

                                                            While the header says Spirit Sight, the text says sense. This is not a contradiction or limitation but an invitation to tailor each mage’s spirit senses to their paradigm. This presumably applies both to spirits on the other side of the Gauntlet and spirits that are on the same side but in some way hidden.

                                                            I was under the impression that elemental spirits were still spirits and so this power should work on them with no need for another sphere, though if they are possessing an element that might be a different story.


                                                            See Auras (Spirit Sense)

                                                            A mage can also see the aura of people and other creatures, with all of the information that aurasight brings.


                                                            See Aura of Unseen

                                                            It is not stated that a mage needs to be able to see the creature to see its aura. In situations where the creature is hiding or invisible it may not have concealed its aura and aura sight could give it away.


                                                            Sense Spiritual Component (Spirit Sense)

                                                            Contradiction Resolution

                                                            With this power a mage can determine if an object has a spiritual component. Fetishes are given as an example of something that does. However, so would awakened objects and possessed creatures, which are for some reason locked behind conjunctional effects. It is possible that Spirit Sense is restricted to objects, so maybe it can’t see possession without Life, but awakened objects should still count.

                                                            The other possibility is that detecting the spiritual component requires close examination of the target, whereas the conjunctional effects make it obvious for all relevant objects/creatures in the area.
