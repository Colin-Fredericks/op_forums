    CtD: Reality Check - Moderizing without risking Banality.

    02-21-2015, 01:43 AM
    Going a bit backwards with my history project for CtD. Modernization.

    While most of the Courts and various subfactions and Motleys struggle with modernizing. Grumps want to stick with the old ways like the old farts they are; Childings love the whimzie and the pageantry like a social outcast at a ren-fair. Both fear that if we modernize too much that we will suffer another Banality event like the Shattering. Unlike the old farts and the kiddos, modernizing doesn't need to be feared, but embraced. Not in the manor of the LnL shorts featuring a Troll who is adept at HTML (sadly I know one who owns a website), there is plenty of imagination now than there is ever before. It's at an odd balance with Banality it's almost ridiculous. It blooms, thrives, and falls into Banality like nothing has ever been before. There are those amongst us who acknowledge that how fast Banality works in this day and age, and we fight to keep things fresh and new, and inspire others to bring wonder back into the world. We have various tv producers, film directors, authors, and internet media producers who fight daily to keep that spark alive. The Gentry thinks we are gonna to act like it's Romeo + Juliet, modern tech and weapons while speaking with Ye Olde English. News flash, Shakespear was around when firearms were in the early stages of development, there's no firearm brand name called Sword or Dagger.

    The Netweavers, as the Gentry and Grumps call us, are a semi-Court of those Changelings who can incorporate the old ways into the new. We're more like a mix of a Elks' Club and a speak-easy, not out to take over the world, but doesn't fear bringing change to the people, mortal or Changeling. It's a loose collaboration to keep the spark of imagination alive in this new age and stay off the Banality that consumes it. Within the "Lodge" (for lack of a better term), have several factions depending on which artistic medium we prefer. The Director's Chair are those who are adapt at film and television, are Changelings adapt at bringing the spark through various films and tv shows. The Acting Troupe are exactly as you think they are, actors. From theater, to television, and film; they are the largest chapter of the Netweavers, with both numbers and the experience to boot. The Quill are the authors, fan-fiction and blogs, the written word, novellas and novels, screen plays and scripts, they are the second oldest of the Netweavers utilizing the power of the printing press to the internet, and keep a vast wealth of imagination. The Bardic Troupe are the musicians and music producers. The Bards are the oldest chapter, with various skills from instruments, to singing, song writing, even radio djs fall under this chapter. They also suffer the most Banality since music is just as harsh if not more so than film and television. And finally the founders of the "Court" The Netweavers themselves. They use the internet to bring imagination to a very wide audience. Videos, blogs and vlogs, podcasts, they are the most neutral per say of all the chapters. I do mean per say cause there's stiff competition between the chapters themselves, all be it friendly competition. About the most that gets damaged between the chapters are just one's pride. Even then it's minor bruising when one of the chapters has a hit before the others.

              Unseelie side.

              Oh alright, if you wish to get more into the Gabit of Kings aspect of things and analyze the political situations. Like the Courts, there are Seelie and Unseelie sides. That is unavoidable since The Netweavers are "neutral". Then again, I wouldn't be doing my job if I didn't warn you about the Dark Side, as they say. While the Seelie side of the Lodge are more tame ventures like Japanese metal, superhero films, and others. The Unseelie are more on the hardcore, pushing the boundaries of what's acceptable, also some legal issues. It varies depending on the chapter. Hopefully I wouldn't scar you for life but don't say I didn't warn you.

              Unseelie of the Director's Chair and the Acting Troupe are the most powerful of the Unseelie Netweavers. For they tend to be the most visceral since their medium is not just auditory, but also visual. Their work ranges from hardcore pornography, to gore films. No I didn't say horror movies, while it falls under the purview of horror, gore films uses massive amounts of gore. Films like the Human Tarantula 1 and 2 (The Second Session), the remake of Deathly Evil, God's Rejected (and it's predecessor House of 100 Bodies), are the work of the Unseelie Netweavers. These works are awful to watch, they create things that shouldn't be. A large amount of the Unseelie Netweavers works for Slaughterhouse Productions, a film company that is responsible for making Formorians, on their sets or just simply viewing their films. I've encountered their creation for the third Human Tarantula. That amalgamation of bodies needed to be put down just because of the pain it was suffering. We've tried to shut down their studios, but it seems that they are backed by something bigger. And the bad news keeps on piling on. The Unseelie Netweavers are now planing to open their own studio. From the rumors their studio is going to be called Hacksville/Horndog Studios. Independent enough to operate outside of Slaughterhouses protection and guidelines, and small enough so it hasn't been found by the Seelie Courts or other authorities.

              Unseelie of The Quill are dying out, but still have a foothold in pornography. One magazine that still remains is The Satyr. It's very close to breaking not only the "Golden rule" as I've heard of the variations from other supernaturals; No Mortal should know we exist. But also the contents inside would bring charges of not only the person who bought the magazine, but those who have participated for their "big shot". Unseelie rapists ravenge the participants, and there's rumor of Formorian children from the unfortunate women who got pregnant from their photo shoots. The Unseelie Bards are the weakest of the chapters. They are more of the trash and heavy metal types. Some within the Unseelie Netweavers would call them tame. But with Unseelie music, it's the slowest to affect mortals. Remember the trials in the eighties that hair metal music is the Devil's work and it's affecting the youth of the time, that was the Unseelie Bards hayday. Some rements pop up from time to time, mostly the leftover Formorians and renegades. The Unseelie Netweavers are what you would expect of the modern age. True Trolz, as the internet spelling implies. Slut Shammers, forum Trolls, hyper judgmental cynics. Their main haunts on the internet is a site call Ch4ntz, Jezuztranzmitionz, and Dave's Ads. While the first two should be avoided if you don't want to feel your brain cells atomically explode, Dave's Ads you should not go or respond. All sorts of Unseelie prowl the Ads for fresh victims, in whatever way they want. Recruiters from Hacksville/Horndog and The Satyr place their recruitment advertisements on the Ads to seek "upcoming actors", "girls who like attention", and the ever classic "need quick cash". Many who respond to the posts are never heard from again, depending on the post, your last known images would cost $19.95 S+H.
