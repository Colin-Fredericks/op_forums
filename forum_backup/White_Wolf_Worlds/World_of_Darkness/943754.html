    Police in WoD

    08-04-2016, 02:58 PM
    I am just sort of curious how STs portray police in their games? In a lot of my games, the police end up being the allies of the PCs, though there is the occasional 'bad cop' that is corrupt and willingly works for the enemy. I find it easy for Werewolves to make friends with the local police and, in the case of rural cops, many of their Kinfolk end up being the law in the rural communities where they prefer to live. In the case of Vampires, it takes a little more effort, but injecting small amounts of blood in the doughnuts that they give to the cops for free will often make the cops friendly (the idea is to balance the level of the blood bond at level two without allowing it to fall to level one or rise to level three).

                              Vamps,demons, mages, werewolves and even organised crime all have their fingers in the police somewhere.
                              It's world of darkness. They're corrupt, and a good portion of the guys going into the police to do genuine good get it beat out of them or they go missing. Now, some of the police are the genuine real deal who're out to help people, and it's been established that the good cops can occasionally give out some decent info, because it's not like the vampires can get to everyone in a metropolitan police force.

                              5% Outspokenly good guys, who will likely die.
                              15% genuinely good guys trying to do their best for the world, but probably try to keep their heads down.
                              40% Not exactly a model officer. Hits captives, doesn't care for victims, would take a good bribe every now and then. Would probably be the perpetrator of a controversial shooting. Mostly 'Does his job'
                              30% Take bribes consistently, hide evidence, arrange accidents
                              10% genuine fucking psychos

                              Also, More police have guns. This is even in countries where the police don't normally have guns.

                                  For a short description of the police in my games, I go with, "Slightly more incompetent and corrupt than the ones in Gotham City." If the police arrive at a situation involving the supernatural they're likely to cut and run but if they do stay, then they're likely to random shoot at everything and make up whatever story they can. The police are almost always under the control of vampires in the WoD but they're unaware of the truth--instead believing they're taking bribes from the uber-rich and mafia of the city. In my WOD, every police station has a room for working over suspects, isolating criminals until they confess to whatever is done, and evidence lockers where cash as well as drugs routinely disappear.

                                  It's not at all how I feel about the police in RL save perhaps the worst places in America but it fits my view of the WoD.

                                                      There are places in the world where police kinfolks are pretty strategic.
