            Are you under the impression that the Avatar is some kind of alien symbiote? If so, let me correct that impression: the Avatar is a manifestation of the person's soul. It becomes part of the person when the person's soul forms. By definition, it's whatever it is about humans that enables their beliefs and will to shape reality. The Avatar cannot shape reality by itself; it has to be directed by the person's beliefs and fueled by the person's will. Far from diminishing humans, the fact that every one of them has an Avatar elevates them. There are plenty of creatures out there that have beliefs and will, from the mundane (animals) to the supernatural (vampires and werewolves, ghosts and goblins); and none of them have any ability to shape reality. Tremere found that out the hard way. Only humans translate their beliefs into change, and the Avatar is what does the translation. No Avatar, no magick.

            In the Consensus, the Avatar is kind of like citizenship: it's that innate quality of the person that allows her beliefs to factor in, for her vote to be counted. It's why spirits don't dominate the Consensus: without Avatars, their beliefs don't count. So yes, the Consensus is the sum of every human's convictions; and Avatars are why this is so. And the only difference between a Sleeping Avatar and one that's Awake is that the latter can consciously, deliberately, and actively shape reality; while the former only shape reality subconsciously and passively.

                    The thing about psychopomps (outside of MRev) is that they're purely theoretical; nobody has ever seen one, and while you can infer their existence, you cannot confirm it. I actually prefer this model, because it allows a wider range of paradigms to be potentially true.

                    The difficulty you're running into is that the materialist paradigms don't allow for a soul. As such, most of the Conventions lack an appropriate frame of reference to discuss anything having to do with the Avatar (or, as they call it, Genius). The best they can do is to try to reframe the issue in physical terms (e.g., midichlorians, or alien symbiotes).
