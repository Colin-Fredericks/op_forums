                  It's a personal dislike and probably somewhat of a grudge gotten from the fact that when the game came out it advertised itself as humanity becoming, at least in parts, more aware of the supernatural amongst them and normal humans taking up the hunt, basically it sounded like all the year of the hunter books taken together and expanded into a whole line about mortals confronting the supernatural. These were supernatural beings who could detect other supernatural beings, and hear whispers or see text forming with orders for them to kill them, and the ability to call for example supernatural flames to surround their briefcase making it into a lethal weapon and despite all of this rather obvious display of supernatural power they still had the audacity to speak of themselves s humans and make the claim that the supernaturals were all evil, if that were the case they should have counted themselves amongst the evil ones as they were like said clearly supernatural themselves.

                  I have no respect for these lousy supernatural serial killer wannabees, I reserve my respect for those mortals who despite the overwhelming odds decide to rise up against the supernatural with no supernatural protection from their powers and in many cases no weapons other than their wit's and whatever they may find handy.

                              Originally posted by Possessed View Post
                              After all the imbued are not "hunters" they are just slightly weaker bunch of supernatural creatures who are manipulated by stronger bunch of supernatural creatures,...
                              In this case, most of the Hunters Hunted organizations aren't hunters either, because they're slightly weaker supernaturals often being manipulated by stronger supernaturals to target their preferred enemies.

                              The Arcanum is noted to have many Numina users (mostly hedge magicians) in their ranks and their leader is mysteriously special (aka not much of a spoiler that he's more than human + maybe some extra stuff.
                              The NSA is often manipulated by the Technocracy (along with most of the other government organizations) and is responsible for helping create the technology that makes Orpheus possible. Psychic Numina are often present in government agents that deal with the supernatural.
                              The Children of Osiris are a vampire Bloodline.
                              The Society of Leopold are also Numina users and despite the Inquisition's efforts are constantly dealing with supernaturals manipulating the Church..
                              The Shih are chi-using wuxia badasses.
                              Strike Force Zero used cybernetics is manipulated by the Asian Technocracy.
                              Dauntain manage to wield Banality (aka supernatural power) as a weapon against changelings.
                              The Young Bloods are ghouls.
                              The Knights of St. George use Numina.
                              The Judges do as well.

                              The idea that HtR isn't about hunters because the Imbued have powers, while the Hunters Hunted/Year of the Hunter books are about real human hunters is kind of ridiculous.

                              ------------

                              As for the OP:

                              I'm with Kirby that the two approaches are both for very different things. The HH approach largely deals with organizations that have experience and resources to hunt supernaturals with. It provides a more stable in-character structure because even if you're the fresh recruit that just say a supernatural for the first time, you have all that to fall back on to help. The HtR approach is best of hunters coming out of seemingly no where to figure out how to handle what's happened to them.

                                          Yeah while HH hunter organizations are minor supernatural templates, aka Numina users mostly, they are still mortal enough to be able to Awaken, be turned into a Vampire, go through the first change or Chrysalis (if Kinfolk/Kinain in which case their already minor supernaturals basically even if they don't know it and have zero powers) and if they die they can become Wraiths etc. They are mortal for all intents and purposes and have no particular Mary Sue resistance against the powers of those they hunt. Reckoning Hunters are a full on supernatural template with superior resistance to the powers of those they hunt and complete inability to ever become another template, just like other full templates have.

                                          Only exemption to the above are Dauntain and Children of Osiris both of which I leave out when speaking of Hunters as they are not hunters they are full on supernatural Templates.

                                          What I expected from the advertising back in the day was mortals rising up against the supernatural, a full on game line about why Vampires have the Masquerade, the Changers keep up the veil and so on. These were not created to hide from other supernaturals they were created to hide from the throngs of humanity which while out-powered individually still have the numbers to wage and win a war against the supernatural especially in the modern times with modern technology. This is what I would have loved the Reckoning to have been, that is what it was advertised to be. Instead we got another supernatural template. But considering that if I want to play a supernaturally empowered hunter I can always go Mage so Reckoning hunters really have no actual reason to exists, they are in fact perhaps even less mortals than Mages are considering they are pretty much unable to succumb to most mortal weaknesses that bind even the Mages, like Blood Bonds, Dominate, Embrace, becoming a Wraith after death etc.

                                          i think the idea of hunter is that "The Guys Above" realized that Humans by themselves woudnt be able to Stand a Chance..
                                          And that thought is poisonous to the idea behind the other game lines. Masquerade is in place because Vampires know that should humanity rise against them they would likely win due to superior numbers alone and modern weaponry makes them even more dangerous. Similarly about the Veil, without it humanity could arm themselves with silver and could due to outnumbering the Garou by millions be able to hunt them to near extinction. Humanity does not need outside help to stand a chance and that is the whole idea of WoD all they need is to open their eyes and gather their courage.

                                                      Originally posted by Possessed View Post
                                                      They are mortal for all intents and purposes and have no particular Mary Sue resistance against the powers of those they hunt. Reckoning Hunters are a full on supernatural template with superior resistance to the powers of those they hunt and complete inability to ever become another template, just like other full templates have.
                                                      That's more than a tad of an exaggeration.

                                                      The resistance granted by the Second Sight power is not passsive/permanent. It's an activated power with a limited resource that does not regenerate on it's own. Running out of Conviction leaves an Imbued 100% lacking in any special resistance to mental powers (and it only applies to mind trick powers, it doesn't nothing to stop a werewolf from ripping you in half or a mage from fireballing you), and if they fall victim to those powers they can be very easily trapped at Conviction 0 for an indefinite amount of time. There's a story in one of the books where an Imbued runs out of Conviction, gets mind-whammied by a vampire and is stuck as a mind-controlled servant for months before she finally makes a mistake in his orders that causes him to be able to activate 2nd Sight again and snap out of it.

                                                      And... this is the cWOD. There aren't "templates" with consistent baseline stacking rules like the nWOD. A mages and werewolves can be embraced (even if Garou have resistance to it and the results if successful suck). Some dead "major" supernaturals can turn into ghosts and/or spirits. Sam Haight happened. The fact that the Imbued don't turn into other splats doesn't mean squat.

                                                      Only exemption to the above are Dauntain and Children of Osiris both of which I leave out when speaking of Hunters as they are not hunters they are full on supernatural Templates.
                                                      Which is just hypocrisy. Either the Year of the Hunter/HH material is about your concept of what a hunter should be, or it isn't. It clearly isn't, but the evidence that shows this is ignored.

                                                      What I expected from the advertising back in the day was mortals rising up against the supernatural, a full on game line about why Vampires have the Masquerade, the Changers keep up the veil and so on.
                                                      You expectations weren't accurate? That's not the books fault. Lets look at the back cover blurb:

                                                      "Take Back the Night

                                                      For centuries, supernatural powers have reigned, warring among themselves, culling the human herds and lashing out from the shadows. The creatures of the night have held court since before the dawn of history.

                                                      Nothing lasts forever.

                                                      From the throng of humanity, individuals emerge who know the truth. [They] know monsters exist. Enough is enough. The forces of darkness must pay their due.

                                                      You are the reckoning!"

                                                      The key point here: "...individuals emerge who know the truth." Not, "the masses rise up against the supernatural." So the claim that it was advertised to be about mundane humans using numbers to beat up supernaturals is outright shown not to be the case on the cover. It's a book about special individuals before you even crack it open.

                                                      But considering that if I want to play a supernaturally empowered hunter I can always go Mage so Reckoning hunters really have no actual reason to exists,...
                                                      Sure they do. First off, they don't have to deal with all that Mage stuff. HtR isn't a game about Paradigm wars and flexible magic slinging. Mage does not work well for the sudden "Holy Shit!" factor that the Imbued deal with followed by being lost in a world full of monsters. If anything, Mage makes more of the YotH/HH groups irrelevant. You could easily play most of them as a linears only game (Government agents/DNA/SF0 = Technocrats, Shih = Akashics, a small tweak makes the Arcanum = Hermetics, the SoL = Templars/Chorus, etc.) focused on going around taking out other supernatural threats.

                                                      ...they are in fact perhaps even less mortals than Mages are considering they are pretty much unable to succumb to most mortal weaknesses that bind even the Mages, like Blood Bonds, Dominate, Embrace, becoming a Wraith after death etc.
                                                      That's a fairly myopic definition of things. Imbued can't live for centuries, bend reality to their whims, travel to other planes of existence, transform into legendary mythical creatures, etc. Hunters also don't have any power that lets them soak damage better; even if they have some healing powers.

                                                      I've heard that Hunter the Vigil for nWoD might be more what I expected HtR to be but since I very much disliked the nWoD 1st edition system I have not yet delved too much into it rather awaiting 2nd edition of it though it did have a sort of update to GMC in Mortal Remains so it is likely not much of a priority to get a new edition.
                                                      Probably. Thought HtV is more HH than it is what you have said you wanted HtR to be. It's not about humanity rising up against the supernatural en masse. It's about groups of humans (and not so humans) ganging together against the more potent supernaturals out there.

                                                      The one thing it has that probably would appeal to you is mechanical support for completely mortal hunters with no supernatural powers (they do have some advantages normal people don't) as well as hunters that have access to supernatural boosts (whether through collections of mystic artifacts, crazy science, or being born special, or whatever).
