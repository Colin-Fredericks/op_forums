    Better rules for Sorcerers

    07-30-2018, 03:49 PM
    Okay, I'm trying to re-balance sorcerers for a crossover campaign, and would like help/reviews/criticism for the changes so far.(Yes, I know "Balance" and "Cross-Over" normally eat at different lunch tables, but they both have a lot of friends that want it to work out and help them to reconcile their differences.)

    So far I have:

    1.Throwing out the round per dot casting

    2. Lowering the XP cost to current dot level x5(most sorcerer paths are in general 1 dot weaker than most supernatural powers of the same level, so making them the same as mage sphere costs never made sense to me)

    3. Keeping no WP cost for powers 2 dots lower then your cap(hellfire 3 lets you cast hellfire 1 spells at no WP, and 4 does so for 2 etc.),

    4. All Paths have their ritual xp costs removed and replaced with a research-dice role mechanic similar to researching thaum rituals or true names.

    5. Anti-psi no longer prevents you from learning other psi-powers. It's now a "spend a WP to activate for a scene" power, it can negate any psi power(or mental magical mental attack{mind sphere/dominate/auspex/etc.}, but not physical non-psionic powers{a fire ball/celerity/etc.}) less than or equal to your anti-psi score, must role equal to or more successes on a willpower role than the opponent's activation role(difficulty the powers score in dots +3), and you can't use powers while it's active(but you can stop at will, requiring another WP to re-activate).

    6. Shih can gain the mana background and substitute it for Chi requirements in quao.(they must however declare if each point is yin or yang when it's gathered, of any mix equal to or lower than the corresponding virtue{Mana 4 can hold 2 yin+2 yang/3 yin+1 yang/4 yang/etc.Chi imbalance rules apply})

    7. Sorcerers can learn Chi manipulation(with virtue requirements) and substitute it for Mana(Yin/Yang however don't matter, It's all Mana to a Sorcerer). Lethal damage still applies(you Are using your life energy after all).

    8. Mana can now be spent to "Push" spells as well as lower difficulty(I.E. each mana spent allows you to cast a 1 dot higher spell without WP. Example: 1 Mana spent allows a sorcer with hellfire 4 to cast a hellfire 3 spell for no WP).

    9. Psychic Powers can now be used in rituals to combine them with hedge magic/quao/other psi-powers/etc, but require Synergy(the Psychic Power) Dot score greater than or equal to the highest dot power used(Synergy 4 is required to make a ritual using Divination 2 + Clairvoyance 4 and/or Divination 4 + Clairvoyance 2). The Ritual Psychic Merit allows you to bypass this.

    10. Sorcerers are their own crossover splat, Not mage lackeys(more WOD Sorcerer independents in the same vein as Amenti or Wan Kuei{with different secret societies, didn't really like the ones in WOD Sorcerer}, Less Sorcerer revised mage style ghouls/kinfolk)

    (Rules continued moved from lower post):

    11. Rather than have "power" be a separate aspect you have to buy into with successes, make it what sets the difficulty.

    Example: in normal Sorcerer Revised rules, you have to declare before the role how many successes you're allowed to buy into each aspect and that determines difficulty. A sorcerer with hellfire 5 that wants to spend 5 successes on damage, Type of damage, area, or distance has to roll against difficulty 9, while the same sorcerer just wants to be able to spend successes up to 3 rolls against difficulty 7. This rule makes it so that using higher path powers is exponentially more difficult than using lower path powers, and using lower path powers result in useless successes. Both of which are frustrating to players.

    In my system the above sorcerer would simply choose how much damage he want s to do, and roll against that difficulty to determine successes they want to spent. In this system, a sorcerer will have to sacrifice Fancy Damage/Area/Range for power(and vice versa) or spend mana. This way they aren't arbitrarily crippled.

    12. Change dice-pool of sorcery to Attribute + Path Rating. Mostly to simplify the system and further reflect the ease by which humans learn human magic.(As you can probably tell, I stole the Idea from wraith. whose arcanoi are weaker than normal supernatural's abilities, but are bought at 3X Current dot rating)

    13. Change the dicepool for psychic powers from Attribute + Ability to Permanent Willpower.

    14. Change the sorcerer "base" difficulty of paths to 3 + Path rating(same as vampire thaumaturgy).

    15. Have sorcerers use the same base stat spread as all supernaturals(Attributes 7/5/3, abilities 13/9/5, backgrounds totaling 7 dots, and 15 freebies). Not sure if they should use the sorcerer standards 5 numina dots, Mage/optional rule sorcerer 6 numina dots, or follow vampire's 3 starting dots with other rules considered

          Generic I guess? I'm trying to set up a "One world of darkness" setting, but I'm just starting off by throwing out any attempt to marry the cosmologies. Basically, No splat has the whole picture, Every faction of every splat has their own idea of "what Really happened", and tagging along with any splat you'll be able experience their side of things(which will be rather confusing for beings like mages that Think They've managed to map the penumbra only to follow a werewolf for 5 minutes and discover half a dozen places he's never seen before that they apparently frequent often.)

          Basically, The world of darkness is a world with an infinite number of overlapping and parallel timelines, an infinite expanse of space where various points exist in multiple(or even the same) locations at once, Multiple parallel spiritual dimensions that embody archetypes of everything that did/does/could have exist(ed) simultaneously, a theoretically infinite number of lifeforms all generating their own internal parallel dimension via simple mental existence, and several forces of nature that temporarily/permanently change the properties of every force of nature(including themselves) at unpredictable intervals/in unpredictable ways simply by force of existing/not existing(possibly all simultaneously at once).

          Anyone who thinks they have the "Big Picture" in that mess is just wrong, even if they're right. Especially if they're right.

          My focus as a ST is just to create inter-splat politics, relevant to the game, to embody the fact that all those supernaturals exist and are aware of each other. I'm just re-balancing Sorcerer because Hedge magic has varied wildly in viability depending on edition and what gameline it's attached to(of which I very miffed to see that the recent trend seems to be writers nerfing an already starved power set.)

                  I was kinda hoping that my players don't try to play hybrids, but since it's still a possibility I guess I'd run it with some house rules.

                  1. Only one "Heritage" per character(likely as a merit)

                  2. Mana acting as that characters "Magic pool" for some powers(Glamour for kinnians, Gnosis for kinfolk), Unless inappropriate(revenants, and dhampires/dhampyrs would use their own life force like shih, with no mana substitution. It's Suppose to be a curse after all.)

                  3. Some Hedge magics can "Raise the bar" for some powers, Alchemy 5 for example can give you the first 3 dots of a discipline(which I think would be a good enough excuse to allow buying disciplines up to 3 a vampire out of clan values). It can do dito for gifts,(and probably mummy Hekau?) but not "purely Spiritual" abilities(like demon lores, wraith arcanois, some wan kuei abilities{soul and chi arts specifically}, or changeling powers). Mage Spheres become an odd duck as you can already learn any appropriate rote if you have the corresponding paths to substitute.

                  4. Use The Nephilim/Laham Merit in Book of secrets/path of screams books as a viable way to run DTF nephilim(gen 7 mana a day, gets 7 "points" to spend on up to 4 apocolypic form abilities they have always on{not able to retract claws or re-absorb scales}, not access to demon lores{which In-setting, having faith 7 that you can tap from birth is already very powerful in DTF's thrall system.} It also doesn't keep you from awakening or using paths{It's in fact an excuse to start as a mage or sorcerer})

                  Beyond having the "Mana(Glamour)" specialized background, I'm not sure why a kinnian would learn hedge magic. As far as Hybrids go, a kinnian with a half-decent supply of glamour is in league with many other mid -tier full splats(even many changelings, most characters aren't going to max out 6 arts and a glamour 5 cap is still a very decent dice pool)

                  Also any character with the "Channeling" power is already a "medium" possibly already more powerful than the WTO version. Other than spectre bestowments(Which isn't so much hedge magic as malfean infernalism. Which is a Whole different beast all together).
