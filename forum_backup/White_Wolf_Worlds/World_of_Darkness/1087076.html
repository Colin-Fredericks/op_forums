    A World of Corporate Darkness (big image warning)

    05-03-2017, 06:05 AM
    Time for the latest in Crazy Uncle Loomer's Charts and Graphs. This one gets its own thread, though, since it's a doozy. Ever wonder where the money goes when your characters buy something? Ever need a new company to throw at your players when they can remember all the mainstream Pentex subsidiaries and affiliates off by heart? Ever need a totally mundane, non-evil corporation for your backstory? Well, now you can get them all in one convenient chart. Remember kids, capitalism is the real monster and you can't kill it with fire unless it's the fire of COMMUNIST REVOLUTION.


    You may need to click http://i.imgur.com/DY8Yjme.png for big.
    I may be missing a few or have misfiled them, and they're not all super legible, but I reckon that's the most comprehensive option out there yet. Anywhere that's mostly a 1-store business got omitted, with a few exceptions, which is why Siegfried's nightclubs aren't on there. As for Grimm's magic shop, well, I couldn't be bothered.
