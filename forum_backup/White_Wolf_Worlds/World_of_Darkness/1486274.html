    Catacombs Crossover Canon Conundrum

    07-12-2022, 09:15 AM
    I'm running a crossover game and there is a studious Giovanni Necromancer who wants to investigate the Catacombs of Paris, which are occupied by The Gamine, a Ratkin Plague, how would she go about getting access?

    I'm assuming any minor forays into the catacombs would go unnoticed/unchallenged at first but said necromancer would quickly discover the Ratkin presence and want to negotiate for deeper access.

    I'd like to give the necromancer access at a cost but I don't want to completely ignore canon/lore when doing so, what do you think the process would go like? When considering your answers please let me know how strict you are being with respect to canon.


    p.s. sorry for the title, I like to alliterate all around

      There's roughly four tiers of access for the Paris Catacombs:
      1. The tourist loop, about a mile long
      2. Accessible but officially closed parts. Still frequented by urban explorers, teenagers, homeless etc.
      3. Closed parts that the former stay out of because of structural instability, maintenance work, or other official worksites.
      4. Anything beyond the former. Collapsed sections mainly, and any fictional additions.

      The Plague probably hangs out in the deeper parts proper, with a few scouts in the second category. Depending on what the Giovanni is looking for and how much they invest into prep, getting into the first three tiers should be pretty easy, even with the Ratkin around. Maintenance of the catacombs is coordinated by the IGC (Inspection Générale des Carrières) and a Giovanni worth their salt should be able to pry their way into access of some usually off-limit areas.

      Things get more complicated when whatever the Giovanni is seeking is within the nest area of the Plague. The default attitude of the Ratkin towards a leech entering would be to rip them to shreds without much ado.

      I actually had a similar scenario with my group just a few weeks ago, they were questing for an item located in an underground section infested by spirit rats. My solution was to give the group an in by placing a threat in a section beyond the nest that the rats couldn't handle, but the group was uniquely qualified for. In exchange for disposing of the threat, the rats tolerated the breach of their territory- for exactly as long as it took to deal with the threat, and then kick them out.

      In my case the group had an Ananasi and a Stargazer, and the threat was a big spider spirit bane that had made a web in a large cistern and preyed upon the rats. For your scenario, having a malicious ghost or other undead threat which has been a pain for the Ratkin would be an option. If the Giovanni can convince them that they are able to deal with it (and do), it could be a strong enough bargaining chip for the Ratkin to turn a blind eye for a few days. Of course, the Ratkin would still hate the vampire, and hate this compromise equally. Having my group operate under the constant surveillance of beady eyes watching them from the dark was excellent paranoia fuel.

      Edit: As for canon conformity, I know from a Wraith perspective the catacombs are mostly Renegade territory, so you can be rather free with the motivations of local Wraiths. Ratkin tolerating a leech in their nest to remove a larger threat is.. a stretch, but not completely unthinkable. They would be very tempted to backstab the vampire, though.
