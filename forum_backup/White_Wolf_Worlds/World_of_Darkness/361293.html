    D:tF Possession Question

    01-06-2015, 10:49 AM
    Hello, I am asking for opinions an suggestions about a NPC I am creating for a game I'm running. I'm wanting to have a Demon possess a very religious person that has True Faith. Here is the situation. the person with True Faith has been kidnapped by a cult of demon worshipers who are trying to summon and bind a powerful demon. They have been researching and have found the true name of a demon and wish to bring this demon and have a host body for it and have it bound to give them powers. They believe that by kidnapping a virtuous person who is exceedingly faithful and then torturing, degrading, and defiling this person it will create a better host body for the demon. What I have planned is that my PCs will track down the victim to the cult and will interrupt the summoning ceremony. During the fight the demons will escape the binding circle and go into the hosts body anyway, but not be bound like the cultists were hoping. The victim is near death and has been abused to the point that physically they a totally open and helpless. Their True Faith has sustained them mentally, and has been what has brought the PCs to try to rescue him. Unfortunately, it has take the PCs too long to arrive. The victim has slipped into a coma. What I am thinking is that normally the victims True Faith would prevent the possession, but in his weakened state it's not quite enough. The demon, which is powerful, take over the body anyway. With that though, the victim's soul goes on to its just rewards and the Demon is now in possession of a body and memories of a exceedingly virtuous person. The Demon is weakened and the True Faith of the victim becomes extra faith for the demon on a one for one exchange. So you end up with a Demon who has a high (6) starting faith score and low torment. The memories of the victim are so strong that they remind the Demon of what it once was and with the faith that it could become that again.

    So, does this sound plausible? Does it fit? Am I missing something? Any ideas or suggestion would be appreciated. Thanks.

                  To act as a Devil's advocate and to counter a bit of Crowley's facts... but naturally this is without taking True Faith and the complications it may bring to the table into account.
                  The victim doesnt actually needs to be Near Dead to be possessed...he has TO BE DEAD. the demon has only a few seconds or minutes to take over the Host, gain his memories, and such and then start "Acting" like the Host, assuming his Personality.
                  Victim doesn't actually need to be near death at all. The Demon Player's Guide offers multiple options as to which kind of personas one could possess while still alive. These include the insane, only certain kinds of derangement's match of course as your soul and spirit must be left vulnerable, Brutal living, like that of debauched murderers, prostitutes and others who have "sold their souls" and lost their humanity, and the Victims of Trauma, which may be an emotional and/or physical Trauma possibly leaving the victim in a coma, sounds just what the OP was asking for. Then in addition to these cases there are those who wittingly or unwittingly invite demonic possession who are fair game for such, either through ignorance, design or just botched rituals.

                  So it sounds like a possible scenario, outside of the True Faith matter. Then again the point here is that hte Soul is left vulnerable and any person with True Faith must by definition have a high Faith potential as well so in the case where circumstances were uniquely conspiring to allow for this to happen the body itself might be a rather comfortable host. St discretion and all that. Still it could act to drive up the possessors Torment instead of acting as a good buffer due to True Faith conflicting with the Demons very nature reminding him of the touch of Divinity he once held and thus inflicting more Torment as the painful memories rush back.

                  This is btw a good opportunity to think about the consequences of possessing a still living host as mentioned in the Demon Player's Guide, and that I very shortly and swiftly brought up in another thread here on the forum's about chronicle ideas.

                          Yeah, Possessed pretty much got it all right and I know it's a bit late, but I don't see the harm in adding to the discussion. Technically speaking, Demon the Fallen doesn't even acknowledge True Faith as a separate thing. Anyone with Faith 3+ can potentially fight off a Demon/Vampire/what have you with Faith alone. So, if the dude was damaged spiritually/physically significantly enough (which, in the scenario you presented he would be) and the Demon was compatible with his soul (i.e. something in the person resonated on some level with the Demon. Maybe the man was a virtuous priest that championed the weak and downtrodden. That could easily meld with a Devil or Scourge that felt it was once their duty to protect the innocent.) the Demon could take the empty vessel. Or, if you wanted to, you could leave the man's soul in there as a constant 'angel' for the Demon to lean on when he started to waver from his path. They don't actually have to kick the soul out, they just typically do.

                          And yeah, your higher starting Faith thing would also work since possessing hosts with stronger Faith is what increases the Demon's starting Faith as well.
