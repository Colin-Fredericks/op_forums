    Just a thought, do you it's possible for a Technocrat to practice telepathy in the technocratic paradigm? What about other "mind powers" like pyrokinesis?

      Oh, definitely
      The Technocracy employs 'psychics' and other assets that might be..unorthodox and 'experimental' in most of their scientific regards.
      Probably more among their Extraordinary Citizens (Psychic numina et al) than their Enlightened Operatives, but they still probably have personnel among both that fall under that category.

      Depending on how the feat is performed, with or without technological aid.
      And those assets are probably constantly under close scrutiny and observation for derivation from Technocratic principles and loyalty...but they *are* valuable to the Technocracy in 'Special missions' where a Psychics insight or abilities are needed.

      And, considering that the Traditions usually do not expect the Technocracy to make a resource like that pop up in the field, might be more feared than what they've come to expect.


      As a reference, I'd like to suggest an old Anime - Witch Hunter Robin - which (aside of thankfully being low on Animeish tropes) is an excellent inspiration along those lines, and could be interpreted as a Technocratic experiment where the employed Psychics are not even quite aware of the full extent of the Technocracy, or the nature of Mages..

          Technocracy has psionics; they aren't in the spotlight though. The 2nd Progenitor Convention Book has a psionic in the introduction short story - it is mentioned that he needs to synthesize replacement spinal fluid for himself as a result of using his powers.
          Psionics could be the result of a permanent biological modification, a special kind of drug, or an implant put into the brain. You could also have implants or drugs shared by Technocrats to give them telepathy within a group. I'd imagine that it's Unmutual to hold the idea that unaided humans can access mind powers like telepathy or pyrokinesis with just concentration and training.

          Of course, the most Technocratic thing a Technocrat can do to read someone's mind, is to simply prod the target in the right way, and then apply knowledge, intuition and analysis skills! They are just that good. (Though I imagine this only works if they analyzed someone beforehand, or have lot of time and resources to interact with them.)
          For telepathy in regards to allies, cutting edge coordination and learned, minute cues can make it so that they know each others' thoughts without speaking. You could also have Technocrats capable of simulating conversations with other people in their heads. (or out loud)

              I imagine that the Union prefers technological tools for psionic powers, where it can. It makes them more controllable, and preserves the illusion that what the Union does isn't magic. That said, I can also see them telling their underlings that it's possible for humans to develop psionics through training, if push comes to shove. That way they can handwave away what the traditionalists do.

              Best case scenario, they prefer psychic powers as a result of artifice. Brain implants, nanomachines (son), gene engineering, xeno-grafts, specialized headware (I can see a metal interface band hidden in the lining of a hat), and perhaps even specialized spying equipment installed in a van. Many of these things can be taken away, one way or another.

                  It's rather strange, but psychic powers are more associated with the Technocracy than with the Traditions. They really shouldn't be, given than a lot of what we associate with psychic power grew out of Victorian and New Age spiritualism, both of which the Technocracy largely rejects. However, the untapped potential of the human mind is a staple of utopian thinking and as such appeals to the Technocracy. That's the key element element. The Traditions widely use abilities we would call psychic, but they come bundled with cultural, mystical, or mythical elements that take them away from what we usually think of when we hear 'psychic powers'.

                      Sure, it's not Telepathy per se, but to a Technocrat the results are the same.
                      In my headcannon it can actually become telepathy. As members of an Amalgam get better in reading each other, they tend to drift into using simulacrums - instead of reasoning "What is my partner thinking?", they turn on an approximation of their partner in their head and listen to it; letting their subconscious do the work. This even works for Technocrats who haven't talked to their partners for years. (Keeping tabs on them or reading their publications enhances the effect.) The Ivory Tower strives to explain the eerily accuracy and synchronization of those simulacrum techniques through psychology and sociology. However, unofficially it is known that agents conversing with a 'simulacrum' have received information that they could have not possible deduced from a mere intellectual simulation - like precise hints about a captured partner's location. It could be called "Technocratic Drill" or "Amalgam Drill".
                      And "Technepandi Drill" for the Technepandi. Technepandi, due to being initiated into mysticism, have tested and investigated how exactly it works. They prefer to use it in it's Technocratic almost-telepathy version...but when needed they can reliably direct/steer it with telepathic impulses or other mystic spells.

                      You can take it a step further, and say that when a Technocrat needs advice of a close friend, they construct a little shrine of photographs etc., while going through their past e-mails or chat conversations; this enables them to create a very independent and accurate simulacrum to get advice from.

                      This has precedence in the Order of Reason; Mind 3 effect Cry of Distress is described thus:
                      " (...) And as conspiratorial legends tell us, if a brother is in mortal danger, he may give the Cry of Distress, a secret gesture altering Daedaleans nearby that he needs immediate aid.
                      Brethren and initiates faithfully memorize these covert signals, but in truth, they vary widely from one lodge to another. Oddly enough, scholarly crusaders seem to have an intuitive grasp of nearly all of these signals, even if they're given by someone they've never met from a lodge from far away. Conspiracies are cloaked in mystery, so no one questions the details of such coincidences ...though the masters of the Inner Mysteries sagely nod when those stories are retold in private."

                          Another way to do Technocratic telepathy: Progenitor pheromones. A Technocrat would have modified organs that secrete chemicals etc. which induce different states of mind. Once they tag you - perhaps with some viruses, bacteria, or spores - they can also use a combination of smell and taste to read your emotions and thoughts from a distance; and adjust as needed. More concrete images can be put into the mind through a combination of a strong pheromone pulse and code-words; or seeding the wanted impression beforehand through sending appropriately-themed spam to the target's e-mail, or with SMSes etc.

                          NWO could use a gas or a liquid that makes you extremely suggestive to certain stimuli - it doesn't have to be outright commands...but more like tiny stuff that a trained NWO would work into the interaction to influence your mind. Another kind of substance could make someone more likely to show what they have on their mind.
                          Yet another possibility is a wave or radiation generator that sends the desired impression; a different type could give a read-out of someone's mind.

                              I think there's the usual conflation and confusion of the effect, as per the description of the Sphere, and the manner of implementation involved., e.g. the Focus

                              Telepathy in the sense of psychic mind-reading isn't different from "telepathy" using advanced knowledge of proximics, kinesics and psychology or through or through Muad'Dib's collection of mental "talking heads" or through some kind of at-a-distance electro-neural mapping and something like phreaking someone else's brain (put a chip in their head, or even get them to wear it near their head.. or maybe even just install them in wi-fi as a hidden feature... "What does that circuit do?" "I don't know, but everything on the market has one..."), or through some application of a cocktail of psychoactive chemicals... (in some ways, any truth-drug could be seen as "telepathy" in that it reveals a subject's thoughts, it just does so by getting them to verbalize them).

                              Sure, there are limitations on what can be achieved by the different methods used, but that's the way Focus and Spheres always interact. Some things are more difficult, or even impossible to do based on the Focus, but the effect itself is accomplished by any of these means, the others described above and ton of others.

                              As for the whole modern psionic telepathy... Well, it's in the books and it's considered a line of research that was previously pursued back in the MK-ULTRA, Cold War era, espionage times, even going so far as beginning to introduce it to the Masses. But it's also stated that it was deep-sixed to a large degree. Taken off the Time Table, etc. It seems like it's a smaller version of what happened to the Etherites, without the consequences... Which means there are definitely some Technocrats out there who were involved in the research (and they might be pissed off that their research was just defunded and de-authorized and all their work left hanging, unless that got conditioned away). The research is also still out there in some NWO Black Archive. There is also a reason why it was taken off the Time Table. if you are a Technephandi fan, maybe the Nephandi masters realized it might increase the chance of their exposure? Otherwise... something happened. Someone make an argument and it was accepted.

                              Seems like the fluff side of this issue, more than the mechanical, could lead to a lot of interesting questions that might keep an amalgam/cabal busy. (How about an Etherite looking to acquire all the research notes?)

                                              If psychic research became more than just fringe science - if governments, universities, and corporations could allocate funding to it openly, and not be laughed at - that affects the Consensus in a big way. Legitimize telekinesis, and every wizard on a broom or a carpet could fly around willy nilly. Same with telepathy, pyrokinesis, technopathy, and remote viewing. Let Psionics enter the Consensus, and the Traditions have carte blanche to use their backwards magics as much as they want. If the power is in the mind, then everything a Mystic does is Coincidental.
