    How Will the Gehenna War End?

    06-23-2018, 09:08 PM
    So. Let's make list about what we know of the Sabbat in Vampire The Masquerade Fifth Edition

    1-They will be scarier than ever according to the Gentleman Gamer
    2-They are on the Middle East fighting the Gehenna War for the Graves of antedilluvians according to Martin Ericson.

    Today i asked about the Lasombra on White Wolf's facebook page,they were very polite and told me "Sabbat is not covered in the core book (focusing on camarilla vs. anarch), so you will have to wait until the Cainites return from the gehenna crusade to find out how the Lasombra have changed." Now,it might be nothing however they did said the will return from the Gehenna Crusade.
    How will they change then? Will one of them be back with an Third Generation vampire's soul on his body? Will one of the antedilluvians awaken before the Sabbat manage to eat them an destroy the Sect?

    Maybe,i should ask,what do y'all plan to do with the Gehenna Crusade,after all it was said our chronicles would dictate the Metaplot from now on,what do you guys think?

                              I have a really hard time believing that the Gehenna Crusade is just going to end and the survivors are just going to come home. It was advertised as one of the two primary metaplot elements of V5, and it would be wholly disappointing if we just settled back into some sort of North American status quo. I really have no idea what is going on with the Lasombra, but that aside, I think the Sabbat book will give us a fully playable Crusade setting and perhaps information on the limited number of Sabbat that stayed behind in their domains.

                              I certainly hope, and don't think, they are going to become a pure Nephandi-esque adversary. Vampire has always, ALWAYS, presented the Antediluvians as bad news, and now we officially know that the Camarilla works on their behalf. So however scary the Sabbat may be, I still think you will be able to make a moral case for them. That may just be wishful thinking on my part, because they are my favourite sect. But honestly, reading the Gehenna War fiction from the Camarilla book, I would be very interested to play in that environment.
