    Originally posted by No One of Consequence View Post
    Well, by Roman Empire, I mean around 100 AD or so, during the "Five Good Emperors"/Antonine Dynasty period. And my thought was that, rather than the regular style clan books that the Cappadocians and Salubri did in V:tDA, they would get a book that was both a tribe book and went into detail about the tribe's fall, how and why it happened, how the other Garou reacted, how to run it as an epic campaign (complete with precursors and foreshadowing during the game's default time period and even do carry over epilogues in Dark Ages, Victorian and modern games) and so forth. The Rise & Fall of the White Howlers, or something like that.

    Also, the signature setting would've been Rage Across Britain, where you'd get to see a fair cross section of them, including interactions with Fianna and Roman influenced Garou.
    WW tended to prefer settings during a perceived decline rather than one of rising fortunes, though, along with historical upheaval. So it probably would have been the later empire, even before we get into the past need to make sure that no one ever plays a White Howler, at least not with official support.

        Yes, but I wanted to set it before the legalization of Christianity, and at what might've been the hight for the Warders and members of House Conquering Claw who'd hitched their wagons to Rome's fortunes, at the expense of those Garou who are more aligned with the "barbarian" tribes. And allowing people to actually play White Howlers - in the same way that V:tDA allowed you to play the doomed Cappadocians (and IMO should've included the equally doomed Salubri as part of the core book) - because it works as one of the selling points for the line and, in the case of Werewolf, playing one of the first Tribes to fall and knowing that your character is doomed from the start ties into one of Werewolf's main themes of being part of a dying race raging against inevitable extinction.

        My theoretical line up for said line would've been:

        [*=1]​Werewolf: Savage Rome corebook (with the Black Furies, Bone Gnawers, Children of Gaia, Fianna, Fenrir, Red Talons, Shadow Lords, Silent Striders, Silver Fangs (focused on Conquering Claw), Warders, and White Howlers)
        [*=1]Secrets of the Ancient World (with the Storytellers Screen, including notes on Stargazers, Fera and other odds and ends)
        [*=1]Rage Across Britain
        [*=1]Gods & Monsters (a collection of antagonists and other dangers, including other supernaturals, the forces of the Triat, and human threats)
        [*=1]Savage Rome Companion (more on the Fera, life in the 1st century, the ancient Umbra)
        [*=1]White Howlers (as discussed above)
        [*=1]Rome: The Conquering Claw (a guide to the Roman Empire and the Garou who've adapted to it)
        [*=1]Barbarians: The Savage Hordes (a guide to the Celtic, Germanic, Slavic and other tribes outside of "civilization" and the Garou among them)
        [*=1]Savage Rome by Night (a look at the city of Rome and a Vampire crossover sourcebook)
        [*=1]Gladiators (what they are, when Garou and other supernaturals become one, and running chronicles around the arena)
        [*=1]Savage Sands (Egypt)
        [*=1]Legion of the Sun Bull (about the militant Mitras cult that hunts supernatural monsters and demons)
        [*=1]Arcana Roma: Magic of Rome (a look at period magic and a Mage crossover sourcebook)
        [*=1]Amazons (see above)
        [*=1]Ancient Beasts (a collection of powerful monsters, some still existing into the modern nights)
        [*=1](Book about the East - Persia, India and China)
        [*=1]Necropolis: Stygia (the Wraith crossover sourcebook and look at the Dark Umbra)
        [*=1]Byzantine Moon (about the Eastern Roman Empire near the end of the ancient period and how to use it as a bridge into the Dark Ages setting)
        [*=1]Kingdoms of the Jaguar (a sort of stand alone historical sourcebook about Fera and Garou in the late preclassical Mayan civilization)
        [*=1]Soothsayers (about prophecy, including the Prophecy of the Phoenix, and how to incorporate them into a chronicle, as well as offering them as a PC type)
        [*=1]Children of the Gods (the Changeling crossover book)
        [*=1]The Black Spiral (a guide to Wyrm cults and other forces of the corruptor)
        [*=1]Age's End: Darkness Falls (about the end of the western Empire, the fall out of the loss of the White Howlers, and the collapse of House Conquering Claw)
