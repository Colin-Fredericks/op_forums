        Originally posted by Ajax View Post
        Ummm.... "Economy of scale". If they have the technology, they have an ENTIRE Convention that can make mass production, implementation and acceptance work.
        But it only works by making such things expensive. Especially if it's cutting edge research.

        Moreover, I would remind everyone that the Union, despite how much power and influence it has, does not have infinite resources. Even though the Union CAN authorize an unlimited budget, that is to be used only against threats against the entire world. Threats like a rampaging Antedeluvian. The Technocracy can't use that every day. It would destroy the world's economy, since you're flooding the money supply with a preposterous amount of (artificial) dosh. For normal purposes, there is only so many resources the Union is willing to use, and only certain things it will use those resources on. It may be on the order of billions of dollars, but it's still finite, and the Union wants to use those expenses wisely.

        They aren't going to blow their extremely expensive orbital death lasers or space weapons platforms on one nest of lycanthropes. In the same way you don't use valuable ammunition to shoot rats in the walls. Pest control is what rat poison and mouse traps are for.

            Originally posted by Bluecho View Post
            Problem with that reasoning: those spots also tend to be Reality Zones friendly to mysticism, and more hostile to high technology. "Nuking" such spots would be pretty damn Vulgar, even if no one was around to see it.

            That's assuming you even CAN "nuke" an extremely small area, like a single magickal site. I mean yes, the Technocracy could do that. But, again, Vulgar as hell, because nuclear weapons are pretty wide-ranging and indiscriminate in their destruction. This is established in the Consensus. If the Technocracy used extremely localized nukes, that would violate what the Consensus has to say about nuclear weapons. And if by "nuke", you mean employ more exotic kinds of tactical weapons like orbital space lasers, that's EVEN MORE VULGAR than actual nukes, because orbital space lasers are not a thing that exists (yet).
            But why that is "Vulgar as hell" if you nuke remote areas? And nuclear weapons are firmly accepted in Consensus.

            Originally posted by Bluecho View Post
            And that's dancing around the bigger issue, which is that orbital space weapons would not be cheap to create and put into position. The Technocracy may have Magical Science, but their Paradigm is also beholden to Magical Economics. Because, remember, they have an entire Convention devoted to that.

            The Technocracy's brand of engineering skews more towards the realistic when it comes to logistics, material and labor costs, and other factors. It isn't like the Society of Ether, who can produce war zeppelins or moon lasers, using nothing but junk and elbow grease. Building anything for the Union is an industrial project. Which tend to be expensive.

            Imagine for a moment that, in the Real World(TM), the US government had access to functional, practical laser weaponry. And that, with enough work and research, they could realize that type of weaponry on a massive scale. Let's say, further, that they could put one of those weapons in orbit.

            Are you really telling me that such a project would be cheap? That kind of thing would require a few billion dollars appended to the already bloated US defense budget bare minimum. Not even counting how many billions were spent beforehand in research and development. Actually building a space laser and rocketing it into orbit would cost, if I may borrow a British term, Loadsofmoney.

            THAT is how the Technocracy does things, because they need to wrap their technomagick in a veneer of plausibility. Not even that, they legitimately believe that's how things must be done. It's part of the Union's collective Paradigm: technological answers to problems scale in expense to the scale of the problem. Which is why the Union doesn't have a legion of laser and nuke equipped satellites orbiting the Earth, no matter how much they might want one. And why they don't blow these kinds of weapon on any old nest of Reality Deviants.

            In this case, a strike team really is the more efficient method of dealing with such problems.
            And why that is so expensive? They simply utilize already manufactured, equipped and launched orbital defense satellites and weapon platforms? Which they have a lot, and featuring diverse designs and purposes, even if calling that "a legion" might be a bit stretch.

                To set aside questions of expense and logistics - remember that the Union is playing for the Consensus. They need Sleepers to believe the world is static, well-understood, and scientifically understood. Disrupting that pleasant dream is the worst possible result for the Union. Why would they want to get down in the mud with the rest of these vulgar Reality Deviants? That would sacrifice all they've built for the last millennium. Tons of nukes is nearly as disruptive as tons of fire elementals.

                Think of the Union as trying to fight its enemies in a house without waking the sleeping occupants. Discretion is the better part of valor, especially when they believe that their carefully maintained Consensus will eventually wipe away all these Deviants because no one will believe in them.

                So - what enemies really threaten the Consensus? A vampire quietly feeding in New York City and occasionally exsanguinating a homeless guy and leaving him in a dumpster isn't a threat, and the Union isn't in the business of defending every single human life. A vampire who's already started killing half the people on the Indian subcontinent - well, that's a pretty big threat. To use the metaphor - he's already woken up everyone in the house, so bring out the big guns.

                Most of the enemies you described aren't major threats to the Consensus because they keep to themselves and are themselves invested in staying hidden. Blatancy is what will bring out the orbital death rays.
