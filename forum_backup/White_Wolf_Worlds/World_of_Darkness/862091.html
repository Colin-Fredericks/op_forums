          How is a high resources character maintaining high Resources if their employed by an underfunded agency?

          Also, one millionaire does not a well funded organization make.

          Beyond things like single investigations easily costing hundreds of thousand dollars - which would drain such wealth quickly - most federal agencies have strong rules regarding spending personal money on work. Law enforcement agents can't spend money to send samples to private labs so they'll get processed faster; it creates the appearance of tampering even if there is none.

          It seems like you can juggle that fairly easily: Resources can't be used heavily in an "official" context. And you can have other agents resent the PC(s) since one of them is rich on the side; without the ability to fund the agency as a whole, so it all just makes the PC(s) lives easier while everyone else is stuck in the mud.

                      You could pit them against equally wealthy enemies. You can't just "disappear" someone with their own team of flesh-eating lawyers on speed dial, and who have round-the-clock surveillance and security. Opposing them requires subtlety and skill - think "James Bond" or "Thomas Crown", not street justice.

                      Alternately, pit them against enemies where wealth is worthless. There's nothing to buy in the sewers, after all. And a powerful monster that has no social presence, and only emerges to feed, will quickly make the player wish they'd stocked up on combat skills, not cash.


                      Edit: Do keep in mind, though, that the player has paid to have a wealthy character, the same way another player might have stocked up on psychic powers, or contacts, or . . . So you need to give them a chance to shine, too.
