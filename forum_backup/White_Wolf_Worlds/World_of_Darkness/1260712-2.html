                -Progenitors are like being a mage based around Cantrip wonders or an alchemist in other games. You put time and effort into constructing drugs that temporarily alter you, implants that give you the ability to do something a limited amount of times, and treatments that can gradually alter you in a more permanent fashion. Increasing dosage or mixing procedures can create stronger effects, at the cost of unforeseen side effects(Paradox). Spending more time and resources into your medical procedures can refine them to lessen side effects.

                -NWO mages would be the most dynamic of Technocrats, as their foci would essentially be any repeatable procedure they can do. They can speak in a calm comforting tone to lower an enemies guard, present a argument with walls of referenced data and fact to cause people to question their world-view, etc. Basically slip suble magic into any otherwise mundane action they take. Big emphasis on "subtle" though as they wouldn't be able to overtly effect anyone in any overtly major way with specially crafted foci and wonders. To bad said foci are as common as the commercials on TV(which frequently they Are).

                -Technocrats not only have the advantage of being able to mass produce wonders, they can use any wonder compatible with their style of enlightened science as a focus as well. Now the NWO operative can use it as a focus to plant hypnotic suggestions and rewrite your memories While it makes you believe you're immune to hypnotism.

                                        It's worth noting that sometimes something seems to contradict science as understood by Sleepers, but doesn't really once you've got a deep enough understanding of Enlightened Science. Take FTL travel for a moment: for the past century, it has been the conventional wisdom of Sleeper science that it's impossible to move faster than light. Science fiction always handwaved this away because it got in the way of good stories; but serious scientists always knew that these were handwaves and that the stories weren't really scientifically plausible. Then, in the 90s, a couple of cases developed where scientists examined esoteric spacetime geometries, and it turned out that FTL may not be impossible after all. The Void Engineers have long known this and were using FTL drives to push into Interstellar Space long before any hint of the possibility to do so was introduced to the Sleepers in the scientific community.

                                        All that said, the Technocracy can and does outright violate Sleeper Science on a regular basis. When this is done, they risk Paradox under whatever name a given Convention gives it (e.g., the Syndicate speaks of Market Corrections). What sets them apart from the Traditions is their belief that their Science is built on deep, objective principles of reality — not so much in the sense that they're constrained by said principles as that what they build on those foundations is more sturdy than what one gets from willworking. The proof of that sturdiness is the ease with which they're able to incorporate their innovations into the Consensus.

                                        My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness
