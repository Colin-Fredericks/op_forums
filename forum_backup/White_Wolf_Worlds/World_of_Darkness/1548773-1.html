                            I've learned from experience with clever players that opponents being able to physically dodge some sorts of magical attacks and effects can be used against said opponents, under certain circumstances.

                            Provided that a character knows that they are facing a mage, and that they don't happen to have a sufficiently reliable means of sensing the mystical forces behind magical effects, it's possible to cause an opponent to dive for cover or enact some unneeded defense by doing nothing more than gesticulating madly and shouting in Latin, or something along those lines. Particularly sneaky mages with a good enough understanding of Prime can make this more believable, even to those able to perceive arcane forces, simply by creating a surge of quintessence for an opponent to detect. This would be less likely to fool another mage, especially if they happen to have the right pattern sensing effects "up" such that they can "see" that the mage isn't actually doing anything unusual to the tapestry, but to the uninitiated that's going to look awfully like the mage is about to do something, even if they do have high enough Awareness to reliably sense the use of magic.

                            Granted, this can be a very situational thing. It tends to work a lot better in circumstances such that it'd be easy to believe said mage would be desperate enough to risk a vulgar working. Still, an Etherite inspiring an enemy to dive behind a dumpster for dear life by leveling a perfectly mundane laser pointer at them is pretty amusing.
