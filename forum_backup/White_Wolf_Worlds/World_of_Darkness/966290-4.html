            This ongoing argument serves as a pretty good example of how technomancers function, in a way.

            One side argues that there are fundamental flaws in the game's system which undermine it to the point of meaninglessness. This is like the non-Etherite trying to use their machines and guns, finding that they don't work at all and explaining to said Etherite why their machine sucks.

            The other side is instead arguing that the system is fine, and that if they find fault with it, it's actually THEIR fault for misunderstanding it or applying nonsensical logic. So the Etherite response goes.

            The sleeper gets frustrated by the arrogance, the hand waveing, the dismissal of criticism. The Etherite gets frustrated with what they see as dismissal of reasoned responses, and that they direct their anger towards the creator for not being able to use their machines when it's actually the Sleeper who's at fault.

For example, a Hermetic who believes that Light and Dark are separate energies would need Forces 3, since it's changing one form of energy into another.                                                 And Etherite who believes in photons, however, would not see such a distinction between Light and Dark. As a consequence, they would be able to turn light into dark with Forces 2 by moving away the photons.                                                 Paradigm for me functions to limit the mage's thinking into a strict set of parameters. As a result, certain rotes will be impossible for certain mages to perform.
                                                A mage who thinks time is a constant forward movement that cannot be reversed would not be able to Loop time with Time 3.
                                                A Dreamspeaker that considers elemental forces to be the work of spiritual entities may need a bit of Spirit in order to control things like Fire and Wind properly (with Forces on their own being useful only for Kinetic energy, light manipulation, and sound).

The fact that none of their explanations line up even with fellow members of the same tradition is just proof that everything they're saying to explain their effects is just nonsense.                                                     That is what set's the Technocratic Union apart from the rest of them, they have internal consistency. While Etherites will argue about whether empty space is Ether vs Dark Matter, all members of the union will agree that it is the Cosmological Constant which holds the fabric of space together, and when the time is right, they will be able to utilise this fact to produce new experimental procedures, slowly introduce the ideas to the masses, and then, slowly or suddenly, incorporate them into the zeitgeist where they become mundane.

                                                    When a NSC talks about Post Life Entities, they are doing so not because they "Don't believe in ghosts", but because the term "ghost" is muddied with so many contradictory definitions based on culture, superstition, and heresay. The Akashics may speak of ghosts like a fox who can turn into a lady and eats the hearts of humans in order to steal their skin, of they may speak of women jilted in life becoming horrible spectres of hatred, with long black hair which obscures their face. The Euthanatos would speak of ghosts as beings who are simply waiting to reincarnate, and are only temporarily trapped until they can pass on. The Celestial Chorus would speak of ghosts as unfortunate souls forever trapped in limbo, or even purgatory depending on what they are taught, awaiting judgement until the end of time. While Etherites have countless theories on ghosts and what they are, some consider them to be simple emotion patterns left as a footprint on the mundane world, others would consider them to be the very soul of a human stripped of a body, other's still will state that ghosts are just memories, kept alive by the combined cognotive resonance energies of the human's who knew the deceased.

They are talking about an Extra-Dimensional Entitie which is native to the Shadow dimension, more formally known as Entropic Space. That's it. No talk of sentiments or purpose, simple clear cut analytics. That's how the Union get things done.
