                        One man's 'evil' is another man's 'mentally ill' or 'completely relatable'. A serial killer might not think he's evil. Good and evil only have meaning because we give them meaning. They're not universal constants. In the WoD in particular, 'evil' and 'hero' (or at least, 'protagonist') are not mutually exclusive. There's a great continuum of grey. Even the things described or portrayed as evil are shown to have some redeeming qualities (Grandmother, the Wyrm, Caine, Lilith, Lucifer, God?). Usually these things operate at levels where evil is meaningless.

                                Originally posted by adambeyoncelowe View Post
                                But honestly, let's not derail another thread by arguing the minutiae of semantics? Arguing whether the fae are monstrous or not is pointless, because subjectively everyone will have a different opinion on what a monster is and whether they play or interpret the game that way.

                                We were talking about the sidhe. In the real world, they'd be outdated tyrants (there are plenty of these still running our countries in real life, by the way, if you really want to get angry about something worthwhile...). But it's a game. Debating the evilness of fictional character types seems a little odd to me. Call for clarification, yes. But be clear about what you want clarified and move on. If you hate unearned privilege and entitlement, campaign against it in the real world, or roleplay it out in your games, but it seems pointless getting angry about setting fluff. A roleplaying game is meant to set up plot hooks and opportunities for conflict, not to create a setting for us all to roleplay utopia.
                                True, every kith has it's monster face. Any and all faerie folk can be monsters, but the Sidhe are in a special trap. The Sidhe need dreams of unjust social structures to thrive. Polkas, good, bad, indifferent, even Beasties, can make do with dreams about animals, or dreaming that you're an animal, heck, even Bugs Bunny cartoons. The Sidhe live on the dream of the "Good King" of monarchies, and traditions that can't be changed, even when they strangle society to death, dreams of privilege, and unearned advantage. Poisoned dreams the world needs to drop.

                                This makes them the greatest horror in the game. They are the greatest horror because they are both totally seductive and totally poisonous.
