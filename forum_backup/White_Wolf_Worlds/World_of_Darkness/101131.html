    Converting your CWoD Dead Trees Library to Digital

    03-29-2014, 04:16 AM
    Has anyone successfully done so (books yet to be converted to PDF notwithstanding)? I no longer have the room to display my game books, and they take up a lot of space, so I want to get rid of all but the hardcovers (and possibly the splatbooks). If you have converted your library, did you do so at great expense, or were you able to sell off your old books and break even?

      Taking advantage of the DriveThruRPG.com sales is probably pretty key to this. Stuff like the M20 KickStarter allow bundling of things where you can get the PDFs for half-price, or more.
      At different times of the year, different sets, or individual books may be dropped from $25 each down to $5, or even free.
      The pink elephant in the room is illegal acquisition of copyrighted material, which I do not condone, nor suggest.

          It depends as well what your collection consists of. I've bought second hand WoD books from as little as €3 before and seen them fetch as much as €100. If you've a bunch of mint condition rarities in the mix, you could well get enough to cover rebuying everything PDF. Issue is that a lot of the classical rarities like Dark Ages Fae are now in PoD which limits their resale value.
