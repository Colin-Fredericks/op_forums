                                Just as an aside, remember that Mages have the ability to see into the future (and the past), read minds, and detect Magick itself. Moreover, you have two major factions that have every reason to want to use those powers and others to find as many new recruits as possible. That's not even getting into the fact that some Mages use Entropy to manipulate their own fate, so that their Avatars will eventually reincarnate into someone who will be found by the same group they were in previously. Or the fact that both Traditionalists and Technocrats have all the best Primers, which can increase the likelihood the Awakening of any random individual they show it to (the Society of Ether basically gives a copy of the Kitab Al-Alacir to anyone who seems promising, just to see what happens). Or that, in addition to their magical surveillance, Mages on both sides (but especially the Technocracy) cultivate contacts and allies across the world, so it makes it easier to find out about strange, unexplained supernatural goings-on.

                                In light of all that, I'm surprised so many Orphans slip through the cracks.
