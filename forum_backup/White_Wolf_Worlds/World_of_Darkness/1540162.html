What can the Syndicate do to help anyone interested in taking down Pentex? They seem to describe themselves to be quite powerless in this matter, IIRC.

                  I second Saur Ops Specialist, the Syndicate as a whole wasn't all that informed about Pentex, and the part that was has defected. This is a current plot point, by the way, with at least one special ops group within the Syndicate working to find out what the hell was happening.

                  Now, if this group actually approaches the Syndicate as prospective partners instead of jerks, or better, approach amenable Syndicate operatives and/or supervisor, then the best thing they can get is the direct help of one Syndicate agent or, at most, a single Syndicate-controlled amalgam.

                      I think, if the assumption is that the PCs are the one's doing stuff, the Syndicate can actually do a whole lot more than "get out of the way" when it comes to aiding a group that's going after Pentex without overplaying their own hands. The biggest difficulty with Pentex is that very few people have the skill to ferret them out in the tangled web of major finance companies. The Syndicate happens to have a large number of experts on that. If the Syndicate is helping from the shadows by helping get rid of red tape, leaking documents that indicate links between companies that 99.99% of people would never figure out, etc. it can do a lot to cut through Pentex's primary defense of being hard to find in the first place.

                      If a third party is taking down Pentex companies with some sly help from the Syndicate, it's not major threat to the Syndicate or their position in the Union (esp since it means the Syndicate knows which companies are going down and can shift markets around to avoid disruptions), and it makes a group of monkeywrenchers way more effective in the damage they can do to Pentex's real bottom line.

                                          Originally posted by monteparnas View Post

                                          The Syndicate is partially aware of the true nature of Pentex, but as any Convention they're also pretty sure that they can and should solve the problem internally.

                                          And their fear that the others find out about the whole debacle is, again, an official plot point.

                                          So the Syndicate is aware of the problem, but will face any attempt at blackmail as a threat, not an opportunity. And they won't solve any threat by Reality Deviants by playing nice.

                                          Unless this group approaches a trustworthy Syndicate agent by more diplomatic means, they're just making a huge, unnecessary, enemy.
                                          The thing is, the Syndicate absolutely CAN deal with Pentex by itself.

                                          Pentex-SPD represents more or less 50% of SPD, which by itself is around 1/5 of the Syndicate itself. Therefore, we could say that the entire Pentex problem represents somewhat close to 10% of their capabilities.

                                          Now, that's not nothing - in fact, it is A LOT, but they absolutely can deal with it.

                                          And that's exactly the problem - mainly, because it ain't that huge of a problem. Because of that, this means that there are other problems that represent a bigger problem to the Syndicate.

                                          That information leaking to the other Conventions, for example, would represent a major problem to them - BIGGER than Pentex. And that would be a major destabilizing problem for the entire Technocracy.

                                          Because of that, they are trying to deal with this internally - but their main priority is NOT to root out the Pentex, but rather to not let that whole fiasco leak. Rooting out Pentex is merely their distant second priority.

                                          Over the long run, Pentex will be dismantled - but that's only over the LONG RUN. Which is frustrating to certain interested third parties that may not be as patient - and that may be suffering the consequences of the actions of Pentex. Also, while the Syndicate play their conspiracy games, all the damages caused by Pentex continue.

                                          This means that the Syndicate is proceeding extremely cautiously to the situation - one company here, one stock market crashing there, one propaganda in other place, and they are sloooooowly doing things, one piece of Intel and one lawsuit and one change of policy at a time. All for the sake of containing the threat without letting flak spill over.

                                          That's the main problem of it all. They are also not mobilizing their entire Convention to the effort - to all the other Conventions, it must seem as "business as usual" (pun intended), and the Syndicate has several priorities which they cannot pull from without sounding alarms. Not only that, as any good conspiracy (or in this case, a "counter-conspiracy), the more people you tell "the secret", the greatest danger you'll have of someone spilling their beans.

                                          They are, therefore, doing things by the books, with extremely care, with just a few select teams of trustworthy and competent personal and veeeeeery slowly. Which is frustrating. But in the long run they'll do it, but it is frustrating.

                                          Should the situation change, then all chips are off. The truth is that, for Pentex, the worse thing that they could hope for would be to be extremely successful. Because, if that were to ever happen, then the Syndicate's hand would be forced, if they perceived that the threat is getting out of control, then they would have to use brute force, since the priority would change. In that case, it would be much more likely that the Syndicate would change gears and this secret would spill over to the other Conventions, upon which at that point the full brunt of the Technocracy would be pointed at Pentex, and that would be their undoing.

                                          As it stands thou, Pentex is annoying but not an existential threat, therefore the policy of the Syndicate will remain that of cautious contending. Which may probably work on the long run, or fail and then yeah, the whole of the Union gets involved, and Pentex is purged by force. Either way, Pentex will inevitably lose, but that will take a very, very long time.

                                          If any outsider decides to try to contact the Syndicate, they may actually welcome a helping hand - and best of all, an EXTERNAL helping hand, which may give them plausible deniability. Just don't expect them to obsessively hunt the Pentex down like maniacs - they'll most likely just drip pieces of Intel here and there, and may even PROTECT Pentex assets on occasions - because remember, their priority is secrecy. Say, for instance, that blowing up a Pentex lab producing Fomori in industrial scale could potentially lead to the wrong kind of Intel falling in the leap of the NWO, than you can be damn sure that they will protect said lab from "bio-terrorists" with everything they have. Or a high profile Pentex Director being a target, it would raise suspiciousness if the Syndicate was not protecting that guy.

                                          Keep that in mind: while it would be highly beneficial for both parties, the Syndicate will want to do things their own way, and for their own reasons. And they won't hesitate to turn against their "partners" if they perceive that said alliance is bringing them more pain than it is worth it.
