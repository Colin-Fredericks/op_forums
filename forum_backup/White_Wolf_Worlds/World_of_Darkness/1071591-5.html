                                                        Originally posted by Beriorn View Post
                                                        I thought that 5e was going to be a platform for TV shows, with a potential market of tens of millions of customers.
                                                        To go into it a bit here, nWW stated way back in the early days of VtM5e and WoD5e rumors that they wanted video games, a Netflix-style show, a heavy focus (or maybe heavier focus) on LARPs, the full multimedia gimmick.

                                                        Which is kinda why some people look at the whole thing and wonder how much the tabletop game itself will suffer to make room for all that other stuff. If TV revenue exceeds tabletop revenue, why bother spending time on the tabletop game when you can focus those resources on another TV series or season? Same for video games, a potential movie (pretty sure they mentioned that too), more LARP stuff, etc. If nWW is spreading their focus around to too many areas, one will inevitably suffer and that one will inevitably be the one with the smallest return on investment. And realistically speaking, with the small profit margins all TTRPG makers talk about, it'll be the tabletop game.

                                                        Of course, this is only one scenario. Those other avenues of revenue might fall through, or maybe they actually will drive interest to the tabletop game, or any number of unseen variables could affect everything. It's all speculation, of course.
