    4th Edition?

    08-19-2014, 09:46 PM
    I mentioned this elsewhere, but I figured this would probably be a good place to isolate it as well...

    At the Gen Con "Classic World of Darkness Q&A", Rich happened to tease out two very eyebrow-raising words... "Fourth Edition".
    It will be on the Eddy Webb audio-upload, and/or on the Darker Days video of the panel, but it was brought up (if I remember correctly) in the context of releasing some books as "updates" so as not having to necessarily wait until their 20th Anniversary editions.

    Now, chances are good that this was mostly just something to grab people's attentions, or throw out a far-fetched idea that everyone's been mulling over for nearly a decade now anyways (as well as to answer the already-answered question of "is X20 actually 4th edition?")
    Releasing a 4th Edition seems a monumental task, and unless there is some amazing change to the system (like a central core rule book a la NWoD), it seems unlikely that they would still be producing X20 supplements while preparing to release a new edition of the same books.

    Still, it does bring up curious questions regarding how the plot would be handled (Mage: the Ascension Convention Books give a very nice blueprint for that), what sort of mechanics changes there might be, and if it would be best released as a "patch" than as a whole edition itself... Or should X20 just be officially revealed as "The Fourth Edition" officially?

                              The thing I see, regarding a possible Fourth Edition, is that each successive Twentieth Anniversary book has come closer to being a new edition than the previous one did. That is, the V20 didn't have a LOT of revisions in it that would count it as a proper next edition. Yes, some Disciplines found tweaks, and there's changes to Clans and some Sects, but it has far fewer alterations than we saw from the first Vampire: the Masquerade and the Second Edition, which was not a huge, sweeping change at that time. Werewolf 20 changed enough from Revised Edition to really cheese-off folks on these boards (a clear sign of a proper new edition, if history is any indicator), and I see M20 given a tremendous level of work on all fronts.

                              When announced, V20 was to be closer to a compendium of rules rather than a serious, company-wide return to the (classic) World of Darkness. The success of it allowed for some revisions to the game, and then it led to all of the resurrection of those lines – or that's how I remember it; do feel free to contradict me if I'm wrong. Please don't think that I have anything but love for V20, but also know that I can point out many instances of cut-and-paste copies from older books in that volume. Something like The Hunters Hunted II or Rites of the Blood IS a solid, completely revised book, no doubt. However, comparing V20 to M20 as being a 'fourth edition' is not a balanced equation, I think, at least not when looking at only those CORE books.

                              ...Just my 2p on the issue. It's still an exciting prospect!

                                  Rich and others have stated that V20 wasn't meant to be a full new version. If they had, they would have made more massive changes to the mechanics and setting. Instead, they went for trying to recreate what made V20 (and the later X20 books) so "classic" and combine the various versions into one. They made tough decisions not to change things too much just for that reason--it was a "love letter" to the fans who enjoyed the game over the last 20 years. (That's pretty much paraphrased from various posts over the years.)

                                  The 20th Anniversary Editions are a fourth edition (for most games, at least), but not the "Fourth Edition". If it were to be a full "Fourth Edition", we'd likely see a direct continuity of the setting (like we saw from the Convention Books from Mage: the Ascension Revised last year), and changes to mechanics that could be pretty significant.

                                  However, I think that we'd have to wait until C20 gets a year's worth of supplements under its belt before we ever saw anything officially announced as a "Fourth Edition* to the Classic World of Darkness"

                                  *I assume that they'd just call another edition of Wraith and such "Fourth Edition" for the ease of it all.

                                  But, again, while it would be interesting to see what would happen if all of the Fat Splats existed in the very same realm, and a lot of players play it that way, it's not actually how the CWoD is created to be (much to a lot of our displeasure.) However, if they DID all exist in one... I wonder how that would actually affect the worlds of the games that we enjoy if mechanics were changed, and organizations re-organized so that they all existed together... how does the Technocracy and the Weaver and Will-Workers fit into the same metaphysical world. How does the Virtual Web, GW.net, and Schrecknet not all run into one another? How does God fit into the whole "Weaver/Wyrm/Wyld/Gaia" thing? How do you fit the timelines together with such huge discrepancies (without it being a pretty weak excuse like Demon tried... which worked... sorta... or at least was an attempt.)
