                            It looks like I'm in the minority here, but I actually like it. I'm okay with removing the imbued from the equation, though I suspect that later supplements will probably either add options, or at least explain what happened. This feels more like your average, on the street Hunters who are tired of being prey to the supernatural.

                            The Desperation mechanic can potentially be pretty strong, even making Hunters equivalent to your average Vampire in that they can potentially have fairly large dice pools as long as what they are doing ties in to their Drive.

                            I do have some complaints; the Edges, Aptitudes, and Endowments seems a little bare...I think there should be more options. Out of the box, you are a little limited with customizability. I was really hoping they would expand on the combat system; in the act of trying to simplify combat for Vampire 5E, I think they made the system a little too simple. The lore was minimal; I think it would have benefited to add a little more about what has been happening in the past 20 years.

                            But those are minor quibbles. Overall, I like what they've done. I enjoy the section on Storyteller tips and how to build the episodic stories in the vein of the Supernatural series. The artwork wasn't too terrible, and it wasn't unnecessary (I'm looking at you, Vampire 5e with your 15 pages of fashion pictures).

                                                    06-18-2022, 10:11 AM
                                                    So the Antagonist section. Oh Boy. I have some deeply conflicted emotions about it, because the authors (again) didn't think it through. The format is essentially Night Horrors with an in character section telling tall tales, followed by stats, goals and objectives, how to use it in stories, encounters and additional complications. And honestly, I like most of them, and all of them are good enough to just lift up from the book and use in any game for a session and some for even whole campaigns!

                                                    However, there are some caveats.
                                                    • Each of the core antagonists is described in a single paragraph that's completely useless and generally countermanded by a description of the individual monsters (i.e. hunters don't encounter anything but lone werewolves, yet are shown in the art and in a writeup of another antagonist as fighting and hunting packs)
                                                    • the critters presented are quite a far stretch from your usual WoD fare, I honestly prefer that, I like the weird and quirky and the fact that I could drop them into any chronicle, but it's rather offbrand for the whole "One World of Darkness" spiel
                                                    • there is very little context given - they like to use words like "The Establishment Vampires", "The Blood Cult Vampires", werewolf packs, powerful ancestor ghosts, a dissolved ghostly bureaucracy and technomagic yet nothing in the text or anywhere else explains this. This ends up quite disjointed when comboed with the prior two points
                                                    • the tools for make your own critter are very simplistic, just a few generic powers mostly mapping to prominent vampiric discipline powers with a dicepool of X resisted by Y, a more robust system would have been nice but this is functional.
                                                    • All the in character stories are presented by either people in other hunter cells, orgs or even confessions of victims, and they consistantly present a much more cosmopolitan, interconnected and communicating world of Hunters, that the book and its storytelling section don't! It talks a lot about how rare the Hunters are, how special the PC's are for banding together and gives no context for this larger society that shares stories and tales. I'd love to know more about it as it would make a more interesting setting, but it would imply that groups banding together is good, and remember Orgs are BAD!
                                                    • Occasionally they speak of redemption and helping people out, but it's clearly presented as a throwaway because all presented characters it mentions are seen as actually backfiring on the PC's
                                                    • There is little special and magical about the Mages, they just get very very high dicepools for things they are good at. It's like Revised on steroids
                                                    • There are two settings quite clearly detailed here - North Mexico/American South + Phillipines, but neither has any further information or context outside the geographical ties for the critters
                                                    • out of 15 presented threats I feel that it's a very unfortunate implication that 10 of them are strictly coded as People of Color specifically in text and the rest are simply unspecified.



                                                    Vampires:
                                                    • a fire breathing, fire commanding Mexican human trafficker with a whole organisation, looking to make more of himself and to fuck over the Camarilla
                                                    • a constructed being made by a serial killer (explicitly said not to be a vampire) that hunts people in a small Mexican town
                                                    • A well connected Filipino secret society of cannibals led by an inner circle of Vampires that seeks to feed on the flesh of the "God Children" and has pet Hunters

                                                    Werewolves
                                                    • crazed old lupus driven from his hunting grounds and playing the Leatherface role
                                                    • sarcastic trickster loner that looks like and acts like wolverine and who is so much better than the Hunters at hunting

                                                    Mages
                                                    • a gun nut hunter of Hunters who has a bag of endless guns and wants to teach the Hunters a lesson for being xenophobic assholes
                                                    • a guy with high arcane that keeps houses in which he gently manipulates tenants to kill their loved ones to serve his dark masters
                                                    • a magic van owned by a group of Coyotes whose back opens into a strange dimension that carries people elsewhere and who might get eaten or changed by things in the strange dimension

                                                    Ghosts
                                                    • A haunted outhouse where a kid who died in the pit below kills people who enter it by drowning them in shit (yes, sigh)
                                                    • A serial killer victim Filipino girl who takes rides with stranger and eats their health and willpower
                                                    • a haunted mall built on the mass graves of Filipino victims of Japanese atrocities now eats people and enslaves their souls
                                                    • a slaver captain who torched his human cargo and now hunts anyone tied to the descendants of companies and cities of the slave traders

                                                    Fair Folk and Strange Things
                                                    • a fair folk that keeps falling in love and then painting his targets into paintings for his gallery replacing them with fetches
                                                    • a monstrous thing in a coat that collects eyes from its victims and adds them to its body
                                                    • a pair of books - one that makes anyone who reads it obsessed with learning more about monsters through vivisection and obsessive collecting until they move on to humans and themselves and another that cures you as long as you remove a body part

                                                    So yes, all in all there's interesting stuff here that's well presented and useful to start running immediately. However, as a whole it doesn't seem to be sure what it wants to be. I for one will definitely treat this as inspiration, but would advise against anyone reading anything into the other WoD games going from what is presented here..


                                                    Off to run a game, but planning to read the Orgs next

                                                    What doesn't kill you, makes you... stranger.

                                                            The quality of the art is a massive step up, but I struggle with the actual subject matter. H5 is supposed to be a toned down gritty street level game compared to 1e, where most Hunters don't have powers, just guts, specialized skills picked up the hard way, and a lot of luck.

                                                            Is Muslim-coded woman leaping off a crashed motorcycle ready to cut something off-panel in half with her scimitar a fantastic piece of art? Sure. But it doesn't seem to be any better at being in tone with the game than bland Liefeld-equse Hunter #5 jumping through a window and blasting away with a gun. Maybe it's the limitations of what I see getting posted here and elsewhere, but H5's art quality going up a ton doesn't seem to have actually stepped away from presenting Hunters as badasses going toe-to-toe with monsters instead of desperate normal folks trying to do their best with extremely limited options.
                                                            --------------

                                                            Also, something I've seen posted elsewhere: why the hell are so many rolls in H5 difficulty 4? It feels like the designers forgot the tone of their own game and back-filled it in by making your ability to bring awesome weapons to a fight contingent on a roll so it can fail sometimes and you forget your signature weapon at home this time.
