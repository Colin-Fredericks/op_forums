    For BOOKS I would reccommend:

    Which Witch by Eva Ibbotson (The spellcasters here are more like changelings and kinain that Mages)

    The Dark is Rising by Susan Cooper (already mentioned)

    The Neverending Story (Already mentioned, but important)

    Dreams Underfoot Collection by Charles DeLint

    What the Cat Told Me by Diana Wynne Jones (short story)

    Not What You Expected by Joan Aiken (shorts stories, all of which are suitable for Changeling)

    The Last Unicorn by Peter S. Beagel (fae vs mortal seeming is a big theme)

    The Big Over Easy by Jasper Fforde (Fairy Tale and Mythic beings living in a modern world)

    The Mythology Series by Jodi Lynn Nye

    Changeling by Delia Sherman

    Five Children and It by E. Nesbit (anything by Nesbit)
