                                          Originally posted by Konradleijon View Post
                                          What is their version of the Changling way like? From what I heard it’s disturbing

                                          So their version of Changeling Way is Alien Abductions. Pretty much new Countless (what the Aliens know themselves as) refugees to Earth have a Caste known as the Chirugeons perform a procedure where they implant their essence into an abducted Human Being. Early hosts had their Chrysalis' artificially triggered by bathing them in Zero Point Energy (Zed is their version of Glamou). This provided instant Countless ready to go, but this drastically reduced their lifespans so they mostly streamlined their process to just tagging pre-chrysalis Countless with trackers while they released them back into the world, they would be warned when their chrysalis is triggered. Their version of Enchantment is also abducted humans, whom they make regular and widespread use of as technicians and crewmembers.

                                          But it is disturbing in that they pretty much are like the Sidhe and hijack the bodies of Humans without concern for the Mortal's souls. They also aren't aware if they reincarnate after their first lifetime, they might as a side effect, but like the Sidhe they just don't know and they rely off of newly arrived Countless from Subspace. They also seem to only have independent self awareness as a side effect of becoming changelings, their version of Nightmare dice is Harmonic Resonance which represents their previous state of inhuman intelligence as part of collectives. They are also divided into two major groups, the Symbiosis Collective wants to better fit in and steer humanity and the Countless into a position of stability and coohabitation, the other side is the Prophets of Harmony and they want to make sure the Anthropic Radiation (banality) of humanity will not genocide the Countless again and that they can make it possible that humanity is no longer a threat.

                                          Also secretly some of the Countless arrived on Earth before their version of the Shattering (its called the Tidal event and happened during the moonlanding). The Clade (their version of Kith) Subspecies of Ancestors were the spirits that arouse from species going extinct and they seemed to undergo the changeling way as earthly changelings. Also all fae might actually have origins as the first Countless that went native on Earth.
