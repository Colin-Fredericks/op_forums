    "I roll Appearance+Science too blind him with Science!"

    12-15-2013, 05:56 AM
    So this question keeps popping up in my head as I gloss over the combinations of Attribute and Ability there could be. Of them, I have concluded that Appearance and anything that isn't some physical act of some form like dancing or something doesn't make too much sense to me. Appearance and Academics, what are you doing here? Making yourself look pretty with your college degree? Is the occult making yourself a sexy zombie? Technology a Transformer or something? This is really baffling me. Intelligence+Anything at first seems stupid as well, but at the very least it makes sense when it becomes the knowledge check. Intelligence+Brawl, "His kungfu is strong!".

    Can somebody clarify to me what one would even be rolling with something like "Manipulation+Awereness/Alertness", "Appearance+(Anything Really)", or even any Physical with anything else? "Strength+Finance" would be what? Me punching a guy with money?

    Excuse the sarcasm or whatever tone I am having. TIred, no sleep. And this question is racking my mind like crazy. It's like this system could theoretically allow for about 270 or so different combinations of rolls, but then there are things that wouldn't make sense together. So is it technically not logical or am I just not expanding my thought process and imagination here?

          While I'm with HOD on this, I think there is a slight issue with the cWOD Attribute line up. Or at least as it gets defined in the books (some of the other books use the same spread but manage to redefine things a bit to make more combinations make more sense).

          Appearance, for example, is heavily under utilized in the game and the main mechanic it sees (first impressions) is often punishingly stupid and gets ignored. This is mostly because the cWOD generally treats App as a pretty passive thing outside of seduction. In other ST games, it is used more actively as the default for basing your action on non-verbal displays or body-language. So you could roll App + Intimidation to look really scary (even if you aren't) where Manipulation would require having to convince them that you're threatening with words. App + Academics would imply knowing how to look the part and get people to take you serious as someone that probably knows what they're talking about.

          Some of the later games also try to ax the "Attribute : Ability" analogs like Perception vs. Alertness. This seems to help considerably in terms of cutting down on pairings that don't seem to have any practical applications.

          Even so, there are always going to be pairings that are either non-functional or exceedingly niche in application to be useless.

                                  Appearance + [any social ability like Subterfuge, Intimidation, Leadership, Animal Ken] makes a good deal of sense and tends to come up. Personally, I like the Appearance + Firearms idea, and would happily let a player roll that.

                                  Intelligence + Anything can be a figuring stuff out roll. For example, a Sabbat pack (PCs) was torturing an enemy captive for everything he knew about the Camarilla in the neighboring city. They had to roll Intelligence + Politics figure out the meaning behind what they learned, and understand the implications of messing with some of the named people. Failing the roll meant "You have a list of names and descriptions". Success let them figure out that the position of Brujah Primogen was in contension right now, know the who Prince's favorite is, and have an idea of the factions involved.

                                  Even though some trait combinations make little sense or are extremely esoteric, the flexibility and potential of the Storyteller system is still nice. The common rolls make sense. When the common roll don't apply, we've got lots to work with to find a roll that does. Some of the less common rolls that have happened in my games:

                                  Appearance + Melee when trying to show off with butterfly knives, and winning serious respect for doing it well

                                  Strength + Etiquette to help a lady climb through a window gracefully, without looking up her skirts or mishandling her

                                  Charisma + Occult to make a good impression on a blood sorcerer by showing how well you've studied. This earned someone a shot at apprenticeship.
