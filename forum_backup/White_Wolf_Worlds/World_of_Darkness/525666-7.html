        It is good to see that you did character sheets for the Nephandi, as well. I do consider them to be playable.
        Note that Pawn is not a rank that a Nephandus can have ; in regard to Mages it is a term ( or a role ) for non-Nephandi Mages. Though I guess that maybe you included it in the sheets to account for a possible Mage who is working together with or for the Nephandi, but isn't a Nephandus.
        The Gilledean rank is missing from the rank selection.
        I am not sure whether every Nephandus uses Qlippothic Spheres.
        You should include the Essences of Widderslaintes in the selection of Essences on the Nephandi sheets. These are : Destruction, Frozen, Chaotic, Tormented. ( And for that matter, these could be included in other character sheets ; for the off-chance that somebody might want to play a Widderslainte who is not a Nephandus, or at least doesn't want to be. )
        Additional Nephandi factions were added in the M20 Corebook and in "Infernalism: The Path of Screams" . I can list them if you would like to include them on the sheets.
