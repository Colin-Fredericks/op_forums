                                          Originally posted by Eldagusto View Post
                                          A functional system to represent people that could if they wanted Buy a Submarine or Cruiseship every now and then, or build and staff a skyscraper. OR Folks if they liquidated all their assets it would put their field of business into turmoil. Supermoney!
                                          I'm with the rest of the thread inasmuch as, from a dots-on-page perspective, it's mostly just a matter of having higher resources.

                                          But while all you need to build or buy a cruise ship or skyscraper is money (and perfectly attainable permits and junk, and you probably want to have the cruise ship built in Norway for various practical and regulatory reasons), buying a submarine probably requires some more backgrounds. First off, what kind of sub do you want? Do you want the submarine yacht, a crude narco sub, or do you want a bona fide war vessel? If the latter, you're probably going to need some combination of backgrounds to reflect that you know the proper channels through which to purchase a submarine--probably Influence and Contacts at a minimum. The technocracy has merits and stuff as well that deal with "Red Tape", which you may or may not need.

                                          The next thing to consider is the kind of full-blown war sub you want. Do you want some old school, half-century-old diesel electric thing more akin to what was being used in WWII, or do you want something more modern? Realize that no matter what direction you go, the purchase will be noted, tracked, and your activities closely followed for the rest of time by any and all intelligence service worth it's salt--minimum, the Five Eyes, China, Russia, and Israel. So even if you weren't on the Technocracy's radar before.

                                          As far the sliding scale of difficult, and amount of dots you need on the page to get different kinds of war subs, the low end would be the WWII- and thereabouts-era vessels, which you could probably buy one off of the dictator of some third-world country; other countries may try to block the sale, but it's not like you're necessarily an existential threat to anyone or shipping at that point since the capabilities of such an old boat can't really touch modern anti-submarine tactic and capabilities. The middle part of the scale are the very dangerous diesel-electrics that Russia will basically sell anyone (well, any country anyway), like Kilo-class subs. Based on what I've read, these things are pretty dangerous: whisper quiet running on batteries and traveling under 5 knots. The higher-end of the scale are the subs that you would necessarily have to steal (or bamboozle the hell out of someone to get): the newer UK diesel electrics that you would truly need to be in NATO to buy; any American sub; any nuclear-powered sub.

                                          If you're a hyper rich mage, I think you're probably be best off buying a Kilo or lesser, then using magic to make it better--or just steal some plans and make your own sub. If you want more systems to use as guidelines for the costs of constructing or heavily modifying a sub, I would use as my starting point the build-a-spaceship system found in the back of Void Engineers (Rev.), which breaks down different vessels attributes (size, speed, etc.) and features into a point system, and then assigns a monetary sum to every point. For reference, the cooler/more badass Voidships come in around the mid-10-figures in USD based on this point-buy system.
