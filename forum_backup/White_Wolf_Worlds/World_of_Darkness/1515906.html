    As Above, So Bellow - A New Spirit World Based on the Human Mind

    06-22-2023, 07:28 PM
    As Above, So Bellow.

    The mantra of real world hermeticism esoteric cults ever since the middle ages.

    So, thinking about that, I have had for a very, very long time, the idea of building the Spirit World based on the models of the human mind from psychology.

    I never actually had the time to put the effort into it... But I'll admit, I have been aided by ChatGPT in this effort.

    So, my basic idea was to have the "Material Plane" as a representarion of the "Body" (of "God").

    The exterior world, that which exists outside of our minds, will be "The Void". A "realm" of Qlippothic ("anti-existance") energies that literally and metaphorically exists "outside of God's boundaries".

    In this metaphor, The Void is the world outside of ourselves, outside of our minds.

    I have a list of realms I created with ChatGPT; our future Overlord helps me a big deal because he is much better at organising things than I am, my ideas are all over the place. The basic structure has been fluctuating as lose thoughts inside my brain for a few years now, and I already made a few changes, but they got lost in the mess of archives and consults with the ChatGPT archive.

    Before posting the many realms so far, I'll explain a bit of the basic structure: each realm is a combination of a "common fantasy trope" of spiritual places - things like "heaven" and "hell", lands of the fairies, the underworld of the dead and such similar concepts.

    Thus, it's a "mystic" concept mixed with an aspect of the mind from Psychology.

    The basic structure with the cosmos will be BOTH triadic AND dualist... Yes. That's right. So, when you ask "is the cosmos of your spirit world based on the concept of a triad, like in Hinduism, or dualist, like in Taoism?".

    And the answer to this question is... YES!

    Because it's BOTH.

    I'll explain: yes, we still have the good old "Creation/Chaos, Order, Destruction" from hinduism.

    However, that's NO LONGER the single aspect of my triad.

    Because... Now we have:
    Creation/Chaos - Id
    Order - Ego
    Destruction - Superego

    And, besides those, we also have:
    Struggle (between Id+Ego+Superego)
    Integration (between Id+Ego+Superego).

    Thus, Id (Chaos), Ego (Order) and Superego (Destruction) make the Triad system, but Struggle and Integration make the Dualist system.

    Besides Sttrugle and Integration, we also have another dual system in the psychological concepts of "Pulse of Eros" (Pulse of Life) and "Pulse of Thanatos" (Pulse of Death), forming a second layer of Dualism.

    So, Id, Ego, Superego, Struggle and Integration form the "basic structure" of the Spirit World. Yes, each has a realm of their own. For instance, "Integration" is the realm of "Transcendence", and Struggle is "The Abyss"

    But it's more than that.

    This structure will repeat itself over EVERY single realm. Yes, this means that, for example, the Underworld will have a part relative to Chaos (Id), one relative to Order (Ego), one is Destruction (Superego), another will be Struggle and a final one being Integration.
    * In the specific case of the Underworld, the realm of the Restless Dead from the game "Wraith the Oblivion" would belong ONLY to the "Struggle" section of the Underworld. Integration would be the "Elysian Fields" or something like that, Im not sure yet.

    As Above, so Bellow

    That's the main idea. Also, some realms would belong to the "upper" portion of "Eros" (life pulse) and "Thanatos" (death pulse). That structure will repeat itself over all realms.

    Now, the realms will be divided in ZONES. The first Zone is the "Conscious Mind", represented by the "Astral Plane".

    The first Realm anyone enters by leaving the Material World is the "Mirrorworld". This realm represents the Self-Image as a concept of the mind. It is thus how an individual would see himself at a mirror, coloured by his own perceptions. Thus, Mirrorworld is a copy of the Material Plane... With a few distortions.

    The next realm is the "Primal Wilds" (Im still working on the name). This realm is a metaphor to the world of the SENSES. Thus, in here the spirit world still ressembles the Material Plane, and anyone can wander from Mirrorworld into Primal Wilds without even noticing.

    The third realm in this first Zone is the Astral Plane, which is also the first Zone itself. The Astral Plane is special, being accessible by Mind magics too.

    The Astral Plane streches over all other planes and zones, working as a membrane that separate each one of the different places of the spirit world.

    Trying to look inside a realm from the Astral Plane will only show you the "reflective surface"; this works like the superficiality of the conscious mind in relation to the depths of the subconscious.
    (also, without spirit magic is not possible to access those realms from the Astral Plane)

    Moving "up", or "outward" (but I guess that it would be more accurate to say INWARDS), you get to the Subconscious Zone (I decided to call it "Wonderland"). In here you'll find realms like the Feywild and others.

    The last Zone is the "Collective Unconscious" holding several archetype realms "like the Shadowlands" (the Shadow) or the Anima/Animus, the Hero, the Self, the Persona, the Trickster, Wise King and Tyrant and Divine (Nurturer) Mother and the Dark Mother and others.

    Beyond that last layer is Nirvana, where the Ascended Ones are in a state of perfect Unity with the whole, and immediately above that (or intertwined with it) is Transcendence.

    Those last 2 realms however no longer are metaphors to the mind, but insted, to the SOUL ("true self"), with Transcendence basically being the "Soul of The One".

    So, that's the initial blueprint of it all.
