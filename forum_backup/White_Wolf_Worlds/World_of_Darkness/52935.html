          Jason Shadow-Walker

          Weretiger • Mage • Super-Twink with maxed stats.





          An Order of Hermes Mage, Jason was an officer of the law. An altercation in the streets happened to reveal several other un-affiliated magi running around, fighting a group of Technocratic agents who were seeking their mortal ends. Jason wound up getting involved with them and starting a cabal with them... almost immediately, things got very odd as the group went deep into a technocracy base to recover a captured master of magick. However, while within the Technocratic prison, they came across others that were captured, including shape-shifters. In a botched (7 ones, no successes) attempt to teleport Jason and a weretiger, the two were merged into one body (not all freaky-looking though) and Jason's Avatar defeated the weretiger's spirit for control. However, because the spirit wasn't destroyed, Jason had control over both his Avatar and the "avatar" of the weretiger, giving him access to both his Magick and to the spiritual and shape-shifting powers of the weretiger.



          The group emerged from the base and were lead by their new Master into a war in another world where an evil empire known as the "Sturg Empire" was fighting a rebellion that very much mirrored the Mages and the Technocracy. This "world" was actually more like a fantastic solar system of its own, with other planets, space ships, space stations and odd "stars" with their own strange properties (including the "Time Star" and "Void Star".) With the aid of the rebels, Jason became augmented with cyberware, allowing him to control a large combat suit through a data port tied directly into his brain. With it he could control computers, pilot spaceships, and access firing controls of large weaponry that he could only use while wearing the suit (or, in Crinos, cuz that was cool too!) However, in a terrible accident, his (new) girlfriend was killed during a fight. Her soul was captured and bound to the computer that Jason carried with him (now a fetish laptop), making it a sentient computer that could act without his needing to command it.



          However, even with all of their newfound technology and using their magick, Jason and his allies could not win the war as it was, and so Jason headed off to another world through the "Void Star" where he came across a world that was very open to magick and did not have paradox. It was, however, a strange place where mystical and magical creatures were the norm, and where elves, orcs, and other races lived. He stayed there for some time, building his energies and powers, until he left once more and returned. Even though he had amazing knowledges and understandings of the spheres of magick, Jason still could not defeat the enemies of the rebellion. Eventually he and his group were captured, and in an AMAZING and heroic act, his friend managed to save Jason, his laptop-bound-girlfriend and himself from a diabolical no-win trap and they escaped just as the colossal moon-base exploded.

          In case you hadn't noticed, I am unashamed of the awfulness that is Jason Shadow-Walker. He started as a 1st Edition Mage character in 1994 from my mid-teens and was played for several years in my earliest days of gaming. He's a superb example of a twinkish character that broke all of the rules of the game, in a game that didn't make much sense, but that was a lot of fun to play. I don't try to explain him in any way that makes him sound like he SHOULD exist in any WoD-canon setting, because he's just damned ridiculous. I didn't mention his sleeveless trench coat, his dual-katanas, his BFG Auto-Canon gun, or that other players were playing a vampire and a Mage-Xenomorph shape-shifter either.

                              Short Overview (her character history is *way* too long to post here, despite only being a vampire for a few decades, so I’m just going to summarize by general years):

                              1939-57: Grew up in northern Virginia, eventually moving to South Carolina; while raised Catholic in her younger years, her teen years were spent adapting to a southern-Baptist lifestyle via her stepfather when her mother remarried after Maggie’s father died in Korea. While not the brightest academically (and not attractive by most standards), Maggie possessed a certain disarming charm and wit that made her socially acceptable enough with other girls (who accepted her as a friend due to her relatively non-threatening-as-a-rival stature) and a “buddy”-girl for many boys (who saw her as a sort of tomboy who also happened to wear dresses and hang with the “regular” girls). Her attraction, however, to the emerging beat-poet scene – coupled with a desire to get the fuck out of dodge, as it were– led her to leaving home without so much as a goodbye after she graduated from high school in ’57: she took the bitchseat of a passing-by leather-daddy and never looked back…

                              1957-1963: Travelled up and down the East Coast, with cross-country jaunts to California, via several boyfriends. Highs and lows include: meeting Kerouac during a summer jaunt to Florida in ’57 (and becoming smitten, though he was indifferent to her; he did, however, appreciate her opinion about his just-published “On the Road”); surviving a motorcycle accident w/a drunken boyfriend; getting a bit too involved with heroin during a New York winter with some wannabe beat-poets; meeting a vampire and a witch in San Francisco, becoming briefly exposed to the supernatural world (though initially writing this off to alcohol and drugs, she later realized that what she had experienced was real when she…).

                              1963, December, California: …is nearly Embraced by a Brujah (Donald) after his newly-created childe (Ronny) almost kills her; Maggie was, however, just alive enough that the blood given her “merely” ghouled her, and allowed her to recover. She hit the road a few days later, wanting to not think about the incident, though deep down being very intrigued with what she had experienced…

                              1970-1990: Maggie adapts very easily to her new existence, traveling with some of the more fringe vampires (Brujah, Malkavians, Caitiff, and even a witch or two linked to Maggie’s loose network of “friends”), as well as mastering Protean with apparent ease (beginning with the ability to Earth Meld and take on a bestial mein upon awakening after her Embrace, and further developing shape-shifting powers in just a matter of months afterwards); she maintains her casual connection to various biker groups along the East Coast and in California, though more so begins traveling alone across the country to quietly observe different people in different places.

                              1990-1995: Becomes involved with the Sabbat, if only via the Gangrel and Nosferatu Antitribu, spending considerable time in Mexico (learning Spanish, more or less, as well as getting a harsh lesson in exactly how brutal vampires can really be… She also becomes fairly skilled at Obfuscating, gleaning it from her sometimes-comrades). Upon returning to the U.S., she finds herself embroiled in New York supernatural upheavals, as she makes a tentative acquaintance with a werewolf…

                              1995/96: Having learned some basics about Garou society (at least as far as New York), Maggie embarks on a very dangerous bit of infiltration by posing as a lone werewolf (vouched for, by omission as much as assertion, by her Garou companion, Gil) at several small meetings of other Garou; she is able to successfully pull this off for several months as the meetings are relatively short and those involved are too busy with ongoing supernatural struggles with the Sabbat and Pentex to scrutinize her too closely. She aids her ersatz pack in taking down not only several prominent agents of Pentex, as well as several rival Garou packs, but also preventing the release of a powerful Bane-spirit that would have slowly poisoned/corrupted much of New York City (though, admittedly, even the more mystic –oriented of the werewolves were unsure how to tell if any difference was, in fact, made…). In a climactic battle against a dark-embodied being, Maggie is “killed”, and her “packmates” leave it to Gil to treat her as would be appropriate for her upbringing/background; Gil, however, realizes that Maggie is likely not “dead”, as such, as her body doesn’t decay quickly to dust as occurs with most vampires upon final destruction, so he buries her body in Central Park, near the occluded Caern that his pack maintains, leaving her to rest in what he hopes is in peace…

                              2001-05: 5 years later, Maggie was roused from torpor during the week after 9/11: the social turmoil of the mortal world opened up the battlefield for the supernatural underworld, and she found herself quickly involved in Sabbat inter-clan war, along with Camarilla encroachment (the later sect she had never had proper contact with over the previous 3 decades); coupled with werewolves resurging (though her friend Gil was not among them), Maggie found herself taking ardent measures to both survive and gain her bearings in the weeks after rising (she commits diablerie – something she thought she’d never do – several times during that year, though mostly in affirmative defense against those seeking to do the same to her; this results in her boosting the power of her blood somewhat, though still not to a degree noticeable enough to discriminate from the power the single-digit generations possess – to date, Maggie is still very vague about the relative power/concept of “generation”, as it applies to vampires, as ironically enough nobody has actually ever really explained the concept to her). After a few years, she decides to go to California, to see if any of her old comrades are still skulking about there…

After several years of living mostly in the rural areas of CA (and even being brought into the Umbra several times via her now-aged witch acquaintances), she decides to take “The Long Sleep”, to see if she can re-spark some dormant aspect of herself, even if only in some small way. She enters torpor on the Autumn Equinox, 2008…

                              Merits: Protean Mastery (7pt. addended version: Maggie can assume several tweaked shapes, particularly after her exposure to the Garou during the 90’s: her bat form can vary in size from a standard, small bat to a monstrous looking, very large fruit bat-sized terror; she can assume an appearance approximating that of Glabro (by spending 4 BP to boost Str/Sta to mirror that forms adjustments; in fact, this occurs when she makes these expenditures whether she wants it to or not, as part of her Gangrel weakness), Lupus (by spending an additional 2 BP, above Protean cost, to mirror that forms adj.stats), and Hispo (by spending 5BP above Protean to again simulate adj.stats); Limited Unbondable (2pt.): Maggie is still blood-bound to her sire, so she cannot be blood-bound by others currently; however, the Vaulderie can weaken and possibly break this, per the normal processes, though Maggie has only participated in such a few times, two decades ago, which didn’t result in her bond weakening;
                              Flaws: Silver Vulnerable (4pt.) – Maggie doesn’t know that she has this vulnerability, as it only developed after her first torpor, post-werewolf alliance, and she has never been attacked with a silver weapon since then (while she will, of course, be initially surprised at how painful and slow-to-heal such wounds will be, she’ll greet it with a shrug and a “Yeah… that shit *does* kinda’ make sense…” attitude). Prey Exclusion: Mages (2pt.; this “flaw” is one Maggie accepted from her witch companions – though she doesn’t fully understand how deep in actually runs, nor that she is under a powerful enchantment, as such; Maggie is *incapable* of feeding from any Mage – and Sorcerers count in this regard – as their blood/essence simply provides no sustenance for her when not offered of the Mage’s own freewill during a ritual or similar situation, and Maggie will have an instant awareness of a human being a Mage/Sorcerer upon tasting even a drop of their blood; even in the throws of frenzy, she will not seek to feed on such a target, though she may otherwise seek to do them harm per the type of frenzy indicated); Repulsed Invitation (4pt.): Another new limitation – Maggie must seek admittance to any private domicile (though such admittance *can* be given by any mortal, non-supernatural being within), failure results in her meta-physically *and* physically being unable to enter; while she isn’t harmed, as such, she will find herself physically prevented – to the point of being physically expelled via the least-resisting exit possible – from the location; this weakness is peculiar and somewhat mercurial, as even some “open” locations – such as a well-kept, private garden – similarly bar her, while the “on-paper” owner of a building finds himself unable to repulse her/withdraw permission, as the more spiritual/psychic “owner” is the one more attached ( Ex.: Dad may own the deed in the mundane sense, but *Grandmom* is the one who has lived there since before he was born, and has considerably greater metaphysical investment in the house); once a general invitation is given, she may freely enter afterwards, though the aforementioned “proper owner” may revoke any invitation previously given, resulting in expulsion (If prevented by some greater means from exiting the premises, Maggie suffers a loss of 2 WP and a +2 difficulty on all non-defense rolls, though the penalty doesn’t apply if she is attempting to make an action to free/un-impede herself so as to exit).

                                          Since I have only played Mage the Ascension, because my old RP group mostly despises the dice mechanics for WoD, I only have one iconic character.

                                          Name - Matthew Burke

                                          Concept - High school dropout drug addict who awakened to time magic during a trip. Uses his skills and powers as a paranormal investigator.

                                          A perpetual smirk on his face, with tousled brown hair, stands 5'10", with a slightly leaner than medium build, stands in a boxer stance. Dresses in casual upscale clothes (black slacks and blue blazers over a white shirt with a tie usually) at his office, and I'm undecided about how he looks when hunting/investigating. Would a trench coat be out of place in this city? What about a fedora?

                                          His father was an alcoholic wife beater and his mother whored around out of resentment, but made it clear she would never leave him. He used to talk to spirits, at least, until they threatened to have him committed. In his teenage years, Detective Fiction, Noir Fiction and drugs kept him going, although he dropped out of school at sophomore year, and had several Near Death Experiences. The last straw to his failing interest in school and hope for his surroundings was when Men in Black with plasticky faces grabbed his girlfriend, Judy, for questioning, and then she was suddenly never seen again, and no-one would willingly remember even her name when he brought it up. It was like she hadn't existed!!! Dejected, frustrated, more depressed than usual, he struck out from school and his parents' thumb to live at the old city morgue on front street, hustling drugs for money and his own share of the "good stuff".

                                          One day, after taking double his usual dose of laudanum laced with cocaine and mescaline, he lay atop an old, long-abandoned slab from the old morgue where he made his home on the streets. He had visions of death, of coming and going, and of the warps and weave in the flow of time itself. He would see someone, fast forward to where their life and his no longer interacted, then jump back to their childhood, and so on ad infinitum, until he could no longer tell what flow of time he was even in. When he woke up, he almost didn't, he felt the need to drift deeper, into a warm sleep that would claim him forever, and had to fight his way to the surface of sleep like a drowning man. After several false awakenings, he finally did awaken, and not only to living (after 3 days of coma-like sleep), but to the flow of time itself. As well, he could see past the veil most people had over their eyes and perceive quintessence, the prime nature of things, bound up into patterns and making up everything around him. He finally knew that there was something weird about this city, and that he had the power to change it, to turn the tides, and thus, set himself up as a paranormal investigator, though he still supplements his income with drugs (possibly gleaned from Dan's character). He successfully gained critical acclaim among paranormal seekers in the area after successfully diagnosing a haunted house , and helping a priest to exorcize it, but is considered a high priced hack among the local law enforcement and mayor and his ilk.

                                          Preference to Justice with a capital J. Will treat others as they treat him, so will kill a killer and lie to a lier, but not steal from someone who is not a thief. Involves investigating their procedures and habits, like the detective did with the killers in Red Dragon. Ethics are on the questionable side, as he is not above smashing someone's nose to get info or dealing drugs to supplement his income.


                                          Treats others mostly as witnesses or suspects, always attempting to glean information and hatch plans of attack. Demeanor is glib and witty. Gestures wildly at times to get his point across, makes good use of his hands for gesturing. Has also studied military signaling for if and when he picks up a partner in investigation.

                                          Find out what is wrong with the city. Get back his girlfriend. Prove he's worth a damn. Master the flow of time.


                                          Ally, Cassandra Burnheart, a local assistant DA who has a crush on matt and sometimes pulls official strings for him.

                                          Contact - Conrad Gester, Curio shop owner and occult scholar, has knowledge of arcane thingamajigs.
                                          Contact - Billy Retton - Local Sheriff, sometimes collaborates with Matt in info gathering although he considers him(Matt) an overpaid hack.

                                          Spheres – Time(Primary), Spirit(Secondary)
