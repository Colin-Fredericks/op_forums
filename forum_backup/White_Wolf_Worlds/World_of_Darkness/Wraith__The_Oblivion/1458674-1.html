    Finally an actual wraith again, but still more of a crossover character:


    15. Klaus von Braun, Nazi occultist wraith
    A now deceased former Thule Society member, who through his grandchild is leading a skinhead motorcycle gang. Through them he hopes to fulfill a 60-year-old obsession; locating the obsidian sarcophagus.
    Klaus grew up in Berlin during Germany's tumultuous 1920s and 30s. Though belonging to a family of academics, moneyed and of noble descent, meant the boy was mostly insulated from these troubles. Not entirely though, cause like so many other Germans the von Brauns got caught up in nationalistic fervor. Klaus' father had already been a member of the Thule Society since the Great War's end. When his occult fraternity chose to back Hitler's rise to power this spineless scholar followed suit.
    And like father like son. After earning an ancient history degree, at Leipzig University, young Klaus joined the Society as well. By then certain figures within the regime, those aware of the supernatural, had extended their iron grip over Germany to the Thule Gesellschaft. Like the military they were to build up their strength, in preparation for the upcoming war. For Klaus that meant he was to study Hedge Magic, as to become a skilled field occultist.
    Klaus' first official assignment was in the summer of 1940, within weeks of France's fall. He was sent to Paris, where under the command of Alfred Rosenberg he'd assist in the looting of mystic treasures. Though the young Nazi was indoctrinated enough to have no issue with ransacking the belongings of Jewish families, he was instead allocated a research function. Making use of his university degree, and sorcerous ability, Klaus pored over historical archives and museum collections, in search of supernaturally potent artifacts. It's while studying an archeology paper he first learned of an object that had no name, only a description; that of "obsidian sarcophagus".
    The artifact had been discovered in the early 1920s, during a French excavation of ancient Carthage. It was transported back to Paris, where it was extensively studied and yet nothing of substance could be ascertained. However, where French academia failed Klaus was able to succeed. An occult tome, 'acquired' by the Thule Society, shed light on the mysterious sarcophagus. The text spoke of a secret demon war fought alongside the siege of Carthage. The archeological find matched the description of a powerful supernatural relic with which the Romans supposedly defeated the city.
    Klaus was sure he found the key to a swift Axis victory. Unfortunately for him the obsidian sarcophagus hadn't been seen since 1923. The Thule sorcerer had no choice but to continue performing his duties as before, hoping that in the process the artifact's current location would be revealed. As the years went on all the useful relics he did track down earned him a medal. Klaus cared little for this though, he remained obsessed with the sarcophagus.
    And then finally von Braun came across documentation which revealed it had been shipped across the Atlantic, to Mexico. The dire state on the Eastern Front helped convince his superiors he should travel in pursuit of this suspected wonder weapon. Hence why in the spring of 1944 the young occultist could trade in occupied France for the state of Chihuahua. The Nazi, under the assumed identity of a Swiss archeologist, spent the war's final year chasing down clues, throughout northern Mexico, about the artifact's exact location. Until the day he learnt of Germany's capitulation Klaus kept believing he could personally turn the tide, they just needed the sarcophagus' power.
    Unfortunately the young man didn't stop being a Nazi with the death of Hitler. In the post-war era Klaus remained preoccupied with what could be salvaged from the Thule Society and the Third Reich at large. Yet most of his old associates could only be reconnected with through necromantic means, if at all. If von Braun wanted to make a difference he'd have to build up his own powerbase. When the Cold War begun he saw an opportunity to do just that; the Americans were in the market for German expertise. Though Klaus soon found out this didn't include the mystical.
    That changed in 1952, with the creation of the FBI's Special Affairs Department. Being recruited by them as an occult consultant was his ticket out of Mexico, into the United States. Though this was still in pursuit of his white whale; that polished black rock. Klaus made himself useful to the federal agents, gradually getting into a position where he could ask favors in return. The intelligence SAD helped him gather pointed toward the obsidian sarcophagus being hidden in the southern US, near the Mexican border. So that's where the von Brauns, their name since changed to Brown, ended up settling.
    He remained on call for the SAD until well into the 1980s. When he finally retired the old man felt he had accomplished nothing, a life wasted. It's why he finally started to show interest in his grandson, young Heinrich. Klaus had never been much of a family man, but considered the impressionable kid perfect to continue his quest. By the time the von Braun patriarch was losing his battle with lung cancer 'Rich' had been fully indoctrinated. A neo-Nazi skinhead, and sorcerer, just as determined to locate the obsidian sarcophagus.
    The dying man knew exactly what awaited him after death, neither the heaven he hoped for nor the hell he deserved. After escaping his Caul the Enfant immediately sought contact with the Nebula Group, an underground Nazi Circle he'd long since been aware of. To his dismay Klaus discovered that those who lived to a ripe old age were accorded little respect, not having died for the cause. That he spent the war's closing year safely in Mexico, with nothing to show for it, didn't endear him to any Nebula wraiths either. They made clear no one in the organisation would risk their Corpus for von Braun, he's on his own in the Underworld.

    Image: A decrepit eighty-year-old who resembles little more than wrinkly skin loosely draped over bone. This frail wretch is unclothed except for a hospital gown, he hasn't been able to afford any Moliation yet. Klaus' wizened face is dominated by intelligent eyes, gray in color. His presence is always accompanied by the stale smell of old tobacco.

    Roleplaying Hints: You behave like a socially awkward nerd. So fearful of the Underworld that you hardly leave Heinrich 'Rich' Brown's, the grandson you're skinriding, body's safety. Though beneath that pathetic exterior there's still a ruthless Nazi who wishes to be respected, who needs to change the world. Until the obsidian sarcophagus has been secured you consider your entire existence a complete failure. Though that hasn't lead you to examine your own choices and beliefs. It's everyone else who's failed you, and continue to do so.

    Demeanor: Martyr

        16. Reut Cohen, the dybbuk whisperer
        A young woman belonging to the Hasidic Jewish community of Mexico City. As a result of her medium gift she's been contacted by an ancestor, a man murdered in the Holocaust. Under his direction she's now determined to oppose the Nazi occult.
        Reut was the first child born to a conservative family residing in Mexico City's old Jewish hub of La Merced. A neighborhood where the Cohens had settled, after fleeing Nazi Germany, during the 1930s. In the post-war era they were immensely grateful for having escaped the horrors in Europe, growing only more religious as a consequence. Hence Reut arriving in an extremely devout household when born in 1980.
        The first two decades of the girl's life were quiet and uneventful. She simply did what was expected of her, being taught how to become the perfect Hasidic wife and mother. But then suddenly Reut's predictable path towards marriage was upended when she started seeing things. The young woman watched the spirits of the dead walking the Mexico City streets. Reut immediately realized she wasn't going crazy. These were the dybbuks of Jewish folklore.
        For months the young woman didn't know what to do with her supernatural sight, considering it a curse she wished to keep secret. But then one of these ghosts showed up in her bedroom, introducing himself as Aaron. The dybbuk told her he was family, a great-uncle who'd remained in Europe and murdered by the Nazis. In life Aaron had been a member of the Arcanum, attached to their Chapter House in Paris. There he was studying a tome describing a secret demon war fought alongside the siege of Carthage. When the French Cohens were sent to their deaths, the text went to Klaus von Braun. He was a Thule Society researcher, the reason Aaron's soul was to remain restless into the 21st century. The then recently deceased Nazi occultist's ghost was still out there as well, determined to use knowledge gained, from that stolen tome, to unleash a great evil upon the world. Aaron convinced Reut she'd received her gift from G*d to aid him in stopping von Braun.
        However, Reut's sheltered upbringing had in no way prepared her for such a divine mission. Determined as she may be, the young woman has so far heavily relied on great-uncle Aaron's guidance. This is how she first came into contact with the Arcanum. At her dybbuk relative's urging Reut simply showed up on the doorstep of their Mexico City Chapter House. The resident Arcanists turned out to be most interested in meeting a direct link to one of their secret society's late members. Though unwilling to become directly involved in the Cohen duo's quest, the Chapter House's head agreed to an exchange of information. An arrangement the Arcanum formalized by granting Reut the rank of Associate.
        Through Arcanist aid, and interviewing a great many dybbuks, the two Cohens have since figured out what Klaus von Braun had been pursuing all these years; the Obsidian Sarcophagus. Other details about this artifact, or what the Nazi wraith's plans for it are, remain scarce. This has forced Reut to do the unthinkable; leave home, alone, without her living family's blessing. She's now travelling north, in search of both more information and allies.

        Image: A bespectacled young woman with brown hair down to her shoulders. She wears modest clothing, including long skirts and buttoned up blouses, in keeping with Hasidic beliefs. Despite her conservative appearance there's a certain intensity to Reut's hazel eyes, hinting at the medium's supernatural sight.

        Roleplaying Hints: You seem lost, torn between what you've been taught a good woman's life should be and the task G*d has chosen you for. This insecurity leaves people with the impression that you're extremely shy. Even though you're certainly softspoken, afraid you are not. Cause acting in accordance with what you believe is G*d's plan inspires fearlessness. Only your family's disapproval keeps holding you back.

        Nature: Fanatic
        Demeanor: Child
        Physical: Strength 2, Dexterity 2, Stamina 2
        Social: Charisma 2, Manipulation 3, Appearance 3
        Mental: Perception 4, Intelligence 3, Wits 3
        Talents: Alertness 2, Awareness 3, Empathy 3, Expression 2, Subterfuge 2
        Skills: Animal Ken 1, Crafts 1, Etiquette 2, Larceny 1, Performance (Singing) 2, Stealth 2
        Knowledges: Academics 1, Investigation 1, Medicine 1, Occult 2
        Backgrounds: Allies 3, Contacts 2, Resources 1
        Virtues: Conscience 3, Self-Control 4, Courage 4
        Morality: Humanity 8
        Willpower: 5
        Merits: Language (Hebrew, Spanish + mother tongue Yiddish), Medium, Spirit Mentor

            17. Henry Bates, weird weeaboo Bleach fan
            On a foggy autumn afternoon day on the 25th of March 2008, a college drop-out named Henry Bates got run over by a truck, while walking back home from his dead-end Walmart cashier job. He died in a very obscure part of the sidewalk by a major junction; most assuredly the truck driver at the time suspected he'd hit "something" that was more than just a "raccoon," but chose to continue to drive down the road and not investigate further. Better to not rock the proverbial boat when he was already so behind schedule with freight.

            Henry's family had put up Missing Person posters everywhere to try and find him. The local police department was involved every step of the way for the first couple of weeks, but that eventually petered out as routine and more pressing issues arose.

            Meanwhile during that same span of time in the Shadowlands, Henry was inducted into a Heretic band than claimed him as soon as they gained an inkling of his possible demise. He grew well into the mentality of this particular band, as he was not frightened by this strange afterlife, but delighted by it; this newly discovered deathly world, so similar but at once entirely so alien.. greatly reminded him of Bleach, in some twisted sense.

            He quickly went on to develop his Arcanoi and learn two-handed sword fighting from a circlemember named Flori, a German mercenary soldier all the way from 1402 at the time of latter's death. Honorably serving for years with this band, he decided to amicably split from it and begin traveling the Shadowlands, and then greater Underworld itself alone for a while. Henry spent another bundle of years taking job after job, growing his skill and gaining (in)famous notoriety as he progressed.

            After completing a job in a small Necropolis outside of Baghdad, Henry learns from a darkened, fiery smiled, well-connected patron that the Jade Emperor that rules over Yellow Springs may actually be a Spectre of somesort.. not just any Spectre, but a Malfean of all things. Knowing from prior knowledge of how Yellow Springs controls most of the East Asian shadowlands, to learn of such a rumor... is deeply disturbing to Henry, given his awful encounters with the Spectres over the years.

            Feeling a sense of duty toward Japan, despite never setting a foot in it and only knowing it vicariously over the years from anime (and with the fact he hasn't watched much anime at all since his death in 2008), Henry decides to head over to the Shadowlands of Japan with one mission in mind; free it from Yellow Springs..

            Image: A young adult appearing man with black hair, glowing orange eye scleras with no irises or pupils, and greenish grey corpus skin. The only apparel he wears is a sleeveless robe over a soulsteel chainmail shirt and sandals, along with a scabbard at his back holding a two-handed style sword.

            Roleplaying Hints: You are an aimless, but hardened survivor of the Shadowlands of Earth, never sticking in one locale and always on the move when jobs peter away. This self-imposed quest you task yourself toward.. is spotty at best and completely nonsensical at the worst. But at least now you have something concrete.

            Demeanor: Outsider

            Mental: Perception 5, Intelligence 2, Wits 3

                One of my Wild West Giovanni PCs had to sacrifice a ghoul, so:


                18. Giorgio Putanesca, the elder brother
                The wraith of a recently killed Giovanni ghoul, now a European Premascine's spy. Now finally released of the blood bond, he wants to make his own attempts at intrigue.
                Giorgio had the distinct honor of being the first American born Putanesca. His dear brothers, Guido and Giuseppe, being two and three. While growing up in New York City they admired their Uncle Ennio, who the Giovanni rewarded with the Proxy Kiss for the criminal empire he was instrumental in building. When that uncle was finally promoted, from ghoul to vampire, Giorgio offered his services.
                The young man didn't like who he became after partaking of Ennio Putanesca's vitae, fearing the dark impulses it awakened in him. But unfortunately Giorgio proved unable to decline further offers of the blood, becoming a slave to it. When his undead master thought it time to induct his younger brothers, Giorgio helped convince them to become ghouls as well. The true love for his closest family defeated by the artificial devotion his undead uncle.
                In the fall of 1863 orders came from Europe, Ennio was to travel West with the other American Giovanni Kindred. After the train journey it was the Putanesca brothers who transported these vampires, by stagecoach, deep into the New Mexico Territory. It was along that path where Giorgio lost his life. Uncle Ennio had ordered his brothers, as to appease a disgruntled Clan elder, to cut his throat. The ancient Giovanni was waiting for Giorgio across the sudario, giving the wraith his new marching orders.

                Image: A Mediterranean man who became a ghoul when he was just twenty. As a wraith he appears in the dusty rider's garb, worn while crossing the New Mexico wilds, and a bowler hat that doesn't quite match. Giorgio's throat looks to be cut almost the entire way through. A gaping gash, still bleeding profusely, his soaked neckerchief has no hope of hiding. The man's handsome face is decorated by a moustache and prominent sideburns.

                Roleplaying Hints: In the afterlife you still carry yourself with all the bluster of a street tough. But beneath that hard exterior you're first and foremost a loving brother. Hence why you're hoping to incite other Giovanni against Ennio, in order to free your kin. Though the ambitious youngster who thought to improve his lot through ghouldom is still part of you as well. On this side of the Shroud you still see a future for yourself climbing the Giovanni ranks.

                    One of the main antagonists in my Wild West chronicle:

                    19. Henri Corbeau, the manor phantom
                    A Spectre who in life was Mesa Trueno's richest man. In death a lieutenant of the Thunderbird, who seeks to drag the town into Oblivion. Though Corbeau seems mostly preoccupied by his former home, remaining close to his daughter.
                    Around 1800 Henri grew up on the streets of Paris, the child of a streetwalker and an unknown father. In order to escape poverty the boy joined Napoleon's Grande Armée at too young an age. The new recruit saw much death during the German campaign of 1813. When the Emperor finally abdicated Henri had his fill of war, and of France. The teen immigrated to the New World, arriving by ship in New Orleans.
                    Once in Louisiana young Henri was once again as penniless as the day of his birth. He struggled to survive, America not being the land of easy wealth he'd hoped for. Henri had no one to rely on, until he met Laurent de Mer. He was a fellow Frenchman, who emigrated nearly two decades earlier. The older gentleman helped the young man get on his feet. But there was of course a price. Monsieur de Mer was an occultist who required assistance in performing his dark rites. For this new master the younger Frenchman would end up taking more lives than he did under Napoleon. When years later de Mer left him behind in New Orleans, for a move to the Pacific Coast, Henri was glad to be rid of the man and his Enlightened Society of the Weeping Moon.
                    Henri wouldn't hear from Laurent de Mer again, one last time, until 1849. In the intervening years he'd married Martha and together they had a daughter, Mélanie. They'd moved West, into what was to become the New Mexico Territory, where Corbeau dug for gold. But he had little success. Hence why he was preparing to leave Mesa Trueno, to join in the California goldrush. But then a letter arrived from his former master, instructing to dig in a specific spot, high up the mountain overlooking the mining camp. There Henri discovered the richest gold deposit of the region.
                    Over the following year Henri lost no time utilizing the newfound wealth in consolidating his hold over Mesa Trueno's gold. Other miners either sold their claim or were dealt with by hired gunmen. While local Indian families were simply driven off their land. As Corbeau transformed the mountain, into a major goldmining enterprise, the camp at its foot grew into a proper town. Which was overshadowed on its far side by the construction of Corbeau Manor, where his family was to lead a life of affluence, though not necessarily happiness.
                    Henri had always been overly protective of his daughter Mélanie. But as the girl entered her teens his feelings degenerated into those most improper for a father. He never acted on them, except by jealously hiding the young lady away from the world. The ever expanding mansion became Mélanie's gilded cage, while her father pursued release to his frustrations in the town below.
                    During one of his visits to the local brothel Henri met a true lady of the night; the vampiress Mocel. No chance encounter, the undead beauty had deliberately sought out the town's most powerful man. By enthralling Corbeau she managed to become Mesa Trueno's true mistress. Even going so far as placing her haven in secret passages beneath the manor.
                    Unfortunately for him Mocel wasn't the only supernatural being who wished to control Corbeau. In 1860 the Enlightened Society of the Weeping Moon, although Laurent de Mer was apparently gone, had come to collect on their past aid. His goldmine was to expand further into the burial sites of the local Akimel O'odham tribe. Henri, knowing the society, dared not refuse their request.
                    The dynamiting of the indigenous sacred site was preceded by a short speech and ceremony, attended by Corbeau's wife and employees. The subsequent blasts were met with cheers. But in his soul Henri had already known something was amiss, moments before the ground started shaking. Subsequently came the avalanche, which hardly anyone present survived. He arrived in the Shadowlands amidst a Maelstrom, with the Thunderbird soaring overhead. There were Spectres everywhere. Henri Corbeau's Shadow made sure he joined them soon enough.

                    Image: An imposing skeletal figure, wearing a dark suit, overcoat and top hat. Henri's eye sockets are burning with a luminescent darkness. The only real color his Corpus shows is a purple necktie. True to the Corbeau name he regularly spies on the Skinlands by Puppeteering a raven.

                    Roleplaying Hints: You've often felt misunderstood. You were no monster, but simply a man trying to provide for his family and build a town. As a Spectre you've become the villain everyone considered you to be. Appearing most amused by people's misfortune, laughing menacingly at their terror. You say little though, cause it's no longer your place to give speeches. The Thunderbird is who you serve. You'll present him with every soul in Mesa Trueno. Only sweet Mélanie you'll keep for yourself.

                    Nature: Conniver
                    Demeanor: Bon Vivant
                    Physical: Strength 3, Dexterity 2, Stamina 3
                    Social: Charisma 2, Manipulation 4, Appearance 0
