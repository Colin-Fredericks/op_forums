    Could Wraiths be Fake?

    07-16-2024, 09:53 PM
    Let's try some imagination:
    Now, we know that some items destroyed in the Material World can end up "appearing" in the Underworld.

    But... Is that really true?

    Stuff in the Underworld are not really made of carbon and silicon... They are made of Corpus.

    And what is "Corpus"? It is some weird "spiritual" stuff.

    We also know that stuff in this plane of existence are highly affected by emotions... Memories, emotions, all of that shape the environment and even it's inhabitants.

    The Corpus of the Wraiths itself is highly dependant on their emotions - a Wraith that has no more passions simply ceases to be, their very "Corpus" dissipates into nothingness.

    Therefore, this is a Realm of pure emotion... It is an abstract Realm of thought.

    In that sense... It might not even be real! It could just be a "theater of the mind" for all we know. A sort of "collective acid trip".

    But that's not the point. The point is that Wraiths may not be "souls", but just simply a "mental reflection" of the deceased!

    This is a Realm that could copy the minds of those that died with far too strong will - so, if your will is too strong, it will "imprint" your mind into this Realm.

    But it is NOT "you", but a mental copy of what you once were.

    There's actually some indication that this might be true. First of all, everything points out to there being a difference between "soul" and "mind". Proof of that is that you actually need Spirit 5 in order to create a real human being, rather than just Mind 5.

    Secondly, there's indication that "souls" mostly don't carry over details of the "mind", except for few fragments, with a few exceptions. How do we know this? Because Mage actually has reincarnation, and almost all of the time save very rare exceptions, you NEVER get details from "past minds" except for some disconnected flashes.

    Therefore, Mind (memory and emotions) is NOT the same as "Soul" (whatever that might be).

    The thing is thou, that Wraiths keep their full Minds upon their "transition". They remain essentially the SAME as they "were" when alive, the same memories, the same psychology, the same emotions (sure, colourful due to their environment).

    Reincarnated Mages on the other hand, are different persons from their previous lives (different memories, different experiences, different minds)

    That is a strong indication that Wraiths are the "Mind" of the dead, rather than their "Soul".

    The other problem we have relate to "Avatars".

    What is an Avatar you might ask?

    I have no freaking idea, but but there's a strong implication that "Avatars" have something to do with "Souls", whatever a "Soul" is either.

    And while I might not know what a Wraith is... I'm sure as hell that they aren't Avatars (from Mage)

    Therefore, I believe that there's a strong possibility that Wraiths aren't really souls, but rather just "psychic reflections" of the living upon the dreadful Underworld... As in, this is a Realm that "copy" the minds of strong willed individuals.

    That would make Wraiths be something akin to the Chimeras from the Dreaming. The Spirits of the Dreaming, the Chimeras, they are "born" out of the dreams and imagination of the dreamers (or sleepers or just simply "humans"). Wraiths could then, be something similar

      I think it is a bit problematic to look at the question from the perspective of another system, and I disagree with the idea that a Wraith is merely a memory gestalt. If you would ask me for a game mechanic definition of what a Wraith is, I would answer something along the line of "an ephemeral entity expressing the soul of deceased person". I think the misunderstanding is casually referring to Wraiths and Souls interchangeably in a prosaic way. The rules in WtO clearly make a distinction between the two, though.

      A Wraith has a soul, rather than being a soul. The Corpus, Shadow and Psyche, Fetters and Passions are merely parts of the expression that define a Wraith as an entity in the Underworld, much like a physical body and mind does for a person in the Skinlands. Even removing some of these aspects does not erase the soul of the Wraith, only Oblivion does (at least until you get to a Ship of Theseus situation).

      The rules in all systems deliberately avoid making a definition of what a soul is, even if there are mechanics that directly affect it (ie diablerie in VtM or some Arcanoi in WtO). Soul remains an abstract concept, and in the most rudimentary sense it means the Thing That Makes You You. There is nothing stopping a Mage character from assuming your idea, but it does conflict with the rules as presented in WtO itself.

      As for Avatars, I personally treat them as separate entities that merge with a Mage's soul. Perhaps as something like a colloid rather than a solution, but that's probably something for the Mage forums.

                                      Khat, the Physical Body, was often preserved to allow a smooth resurrection - obviously a Wraith don't really have this. Unless they become a Risen/Kuei-jin
                                      Ba, the part of the soul that ventures into the Underworld - basically the Ba is a Wraith
                                      Ka, the part of the soul that remains close to the body and guards it from desecration - so we already have a "missing part"
                                      Ab, the Physical Heart, the seat of consciousness - see the Khat/body above
                                      Khu, the physical and mystical representation of a being - basically the Aura, that ties the spiritual and material parts together. Since Wraith don't really have a body (or had one untill it rotted away, turned to dust) this one is really weak to the point of almost nonexistant. Auspex 2 descirbes the Aura colour of a Wraith "Weak, intermittent aura"
                                      Ren, the True Name, a vital part of existence and the soul, a "brief" summary of what the person is
                                      Khaibit, the Shadow, which represent the darker urges and emotions of the soul - The Shadow of a Wraith (or the Beast of a Vampire, P'o of a Kuei-jin)
                                      Sahu, the indestructible part of the soul that will dwell in the fields of A'aru - The Eidolon of a Wraith technically (or the Hun of a Kuei-jin. tho' the Hun more like a bit of the Ka, since it lingers around the corpse, waiting to the P'o - the Hun could be the two combined)
                                      Between these parts flows Sekhem, the force of life itself that binds the other soul-parts together.

If this lore were correct, it would suggest, as the Wraith corebook does, that the Malfeans are indeed physical manifestations of the pain of death itself (literally taking on the form of flesh and soul forever severed).
