    I love Wraith. I think it's one of the best world buildings I have ever seen - the setting is incredible, so ripe with potential that it's almost overwhelming (in a good way).

    The only thing that annoyed me a bit was the shadow play. Wraith is something hard to find players for, so all my Wraith games were on online forums; and at the site we used at the time (Ex Libris Nocturnis, anyone?), the usual was for the player who was controlling the character to also control the shadow. The issue with this was how it massively derailed games - any given turn would be 90% about characters interacting with their own shadows, and 10% about characters interacting with each other and the world.

    How much is the shadow really supposed to represent? A constant voice that is always there and replies to every single thing that happens in the world? A little devil on the shoulder that only speaks or acts when there's an opportunity to do extreme mischief, staying hidden otherwise? Or something in between?

                        Guild by association...OK, not touching that one...yet :-)

                        Shadowplay: Optimum shadowplay should max out at 25-30% of the time. Where it's really useful, of course, is if the party splits and Shadowguiding allows all the players to stay involved, but really, if a Shadow becomes constant chatter its blandishments become white noise - easily ignored because they're just a constant buzz. Pick your spots and don't overuse it for maximum effectiveness. That being said, the rules and advice on Shadowguiding will be expanded.

                        Dark Kingdoms stuff - Hoping to have some nice meaty material on them. The question of how much is largely one of word count.

                        Child Wraiths - Some of them may mature as they age - it depends on experience and will to change. Others, not so much. And I can think of few things more frightening than an angry, resentful 6 year old in a Moliated body with all kinds of spiky bits.

                        I'd stay out of Florence if I were you.

                        Heretic Cults - well, the Riders were one of mine originally, so I'd be kind of a jerk to myself if I got rid of them :-) More seriously, yes, I want there to be more definition and more usable material for the Heretics.
