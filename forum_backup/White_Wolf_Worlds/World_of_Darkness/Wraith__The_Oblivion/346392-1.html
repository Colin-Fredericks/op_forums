                    The soundtrack to Alien 3 is the most beautifully haunting thing I've heard in a long time. I always loved that film, despite its flaws, and think the soundtrack was a big part of it.

                    I recently spent time rediscovering the soundtrack while producing a track for a drag number, of all things! I was doing Sigourney Weaver in Alien 3, so used a combination of sound effects from the four films, plus an underscore of the opening music from Alien 3 specifically, before the whole thing became Christina Aguilera's 'Fighter' (I was appealing to a populist crowd...). The results are on YouTube, as it happens, but I digress.

                    The blend of Catholicism, mediaevalism and occasional violence are great. Especially if there's a storm or spectral attack looming. It's also very cinematic, of course.
