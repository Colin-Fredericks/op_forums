                                            General notes....

                                            1-The main place to see design notes and whatnot from me is over at the KS site, either in the updates or the comments (when I'm feeling particularly verbose). So, some of the big questions that get asked here are covered there, and beyond that hopefully those posts will give you some insight into the design decisions I'm making on the book. Wraith 20 /is/ different from M20 or V20 or WW20 in a couple of ways, not the least of which it's also the Revised edition, so some of the word count that might be dedicated to other things in a book that already had a Revised is being taken up by those updates. As for the Arcanoi, the core plan for the book is the 16 core Stygian Arcanoi and the 5 base Dark Arcanoi, with additional ones added by stretch goal because unfortunately, design means hard decisions when you're having to choose "what gets the word count" between multiple good options. Lord willing and the creek don't rise, we'll hit all those goals and then some and I can keep adding the good bits.

                                            2-Khaleesi - Phantasm is still mid-rewrite. When I have an answer, I'll pass it along.

                                            3-Don't know about an art book as a stretch goal - right now we're focusing on stuff like the nascent Book of Oblivion. I will say that there's a lot of Ron Spencer art up in my office, though.

                                            -deadguy out
