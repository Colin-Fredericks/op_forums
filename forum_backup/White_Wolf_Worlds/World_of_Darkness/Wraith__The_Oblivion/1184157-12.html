                Page 339
                Crossbows take 5 rounds to reload, no excpetions, but with a successful Dex+Firearm roll you can shoot, reload, and shoot again with a Matchlock rifle in a single round.

                ... seriously?

                A handspan crossbow fires at roughly half the rate of a longbow. Or every other round, if you allow bows to shoot every round. There's a few good youtube videos on this, btw, and it only admittedly applies to the handspan crossbow, which has a light pull and doesn't have as much strength as larger crossbows. But 5 rounds seems a bit slow for crossbows.

                On the other hand, paper cartridges allowed quicker reloading of flintlocks, but in no way could you do 2 shots in a round as I understand it. 1 shot every 12 seconds seems to be the fastest I have seen anyone do it on youtube, and that assumes the right equipment, namely paper cartridges.

                So here's a thought:
                I think archaic firearms should probable be once every 5 rounds, with successes on the Dex+Firearm roll reducing that, maybe even down to every other round at the quickest (it takes one full round of reloading at the quickest).
                I think maybe a similar rule for Crossbows, with 5 rounds to reload, and a Dex+Athletics roll to reduce this time, again to a minimum of every other round, taking a minimum of one full round to reload.

                        Originally posted by fspardoner View Post
                        On the page 236 says &quot;In order for the Shadow to take control of a character, the Shadow and the Psyche face off against one another. The Shadow spends a point of temporary Angst and rolls on her adjusted temporary Angst rating. The Psyche may resist by rolling her permanent Willpower, plus any Eidolon dice she may possess (see “Eidolon,” p. 143).&quot;

                        And on the page 230 says &quot;When a wraith’s temporary Angst exceeds her Willpower, the Shadow can attempt to usurp control of the Corpus by means of a Catharsis roll (see p. 236)&quot;

                        For the sake of clarity, is there a need for the temporary Angst level to be greater than the psyche's permanent Willpower to enable the Catharsis roll? If the wraith has Willpower 10, the character will it be immune to Catharsis?
                        This problem existed in first and second edition, and I had a player buy up to ten Willpower with the hope that he'd never face Catharsis. So I house-ruled that when Temp Angst equaled or exceeded WP Catharsis could kick in.

                        It would be nice if this new edition tweaked things just enough to keep from needing that house rule. "When a wraith' temporary Angst equals or exceeds..." would make a great fix to the text. Seems like erata to me when the rules need this to work as intended.

                                            So here are the things I found in Book 3. As I said on my reviews of Books 1 & 2, I am sure that I missed some things, and I know that I am surely repeating things that have been said before, not to be dogmatic but because, in completeness, we don't want to miss anything. So here is the list I have from book 3:

                                            page 344 left column near top: "Birthed in agony, they desired only an end to their pain even at the cost of all existence." = I would put a comma after the word 'pain'
                                            page 344 left column: "While ultimately repulsed, they managed to infect the very darkness itself to rise up against the defending Legions." = Is 'infect' the best word in this instance?
                                            page 345 sidebar, last sentence: "... in for far trouble than they're ready for." = Probably 'in for far greater trouble than they're ready for.' Or perhaps 'far more trouble'
                                            page 348 right column: There is a break in the second paragraph under Lifesight
                                            page 349: "Each extra success you roll reduces the target's Willpower score by 1;" = I assume based upon the following sentences that this is actually Permanent Willpower. Usually I would expect it to be temporary Willpower, but since the follow-up sentence references Permanent Willpower, I suppose this must be Permanent as well. It would be clearer if this sentence specified Permanent Willpower.
                                            page 355 left column: The art Empower refers to basic Abilities. Those don't exist anymore.
                                            page 355 right column: Under Lurking Presence it says "While this art is in effect, subjects who view you are not affected by the Fog, as appropriate to their Willpower ratings." = So I can't tell if the word "not" was put in there incorrectly or if the phrase 'as appropriate to their Willpower Ratings' was put in their needlessly, since, if they are not affected by it, their Willpower ratings are moot.
                                            page 356 right column last sentence: ...and may use Navigate for basic maneuverability..." = What is Navigate? Is that a skill or an art in another Arcanos? I can't find that one anywhere. It seems a bit like Maelstrom Navigation, the basic Argos ability from Great War
                                            page 360 left column: "Spend 5 Angst and roll Manipulation+Leadership; each success represents one Shade that appears, to a maximum offive Shades." = the words 'of five' need a space between them.
                                            page 366 right column: The first paragraph under the heading "Horror" has been broken into two paragraphs
                                            page 368: right column: The paragraph under the sub-heading "A-Viking" has been broken into two paragraphs
                                            page 370 sidebar: "A destructive, treacherous Spectrecharacter is great,...." = The words 'Spectre character' need a space between them
                                            page 371 right column: "If a player is excited about turning her wraith character into a Spectre, she isencouraged to discuss it...." = The words 'is encouraged' need a space between them
                                            page 372 right column near the top: "If the other characters figure out what's going on by themselves, that's one thing and they should certainly encouraged to react appropriately." = I would put a comma after the word 'thing', and the word 'be' needs to come after the word 'certainly'
                                            page 374 right column under Shades: "A Shade character has access only to Dark Arcanoi. Y;ou may allocate five dots among them as you choose." = the word 'You' has a typo
                                            page 375 left column under Backgrounds: The word "Sspectre" has an extra 's' in it. Also, there is a reference to the Wealth background that apparently no longer exists.
                                            page 377 left column: Arcanoi Basic Abilities appear on the table
                                            page 378 sidebar: "But just because they believe it exists doesn't mean they're pursue it-" = the contraction "they're" should be "they'll"
                                            page 379 left column last line: "The points function just like a Spectre's Angst pool,...." = I wonder if this should say "just like a Shadow's Angst pool", since the Psyche of a Spectre is more easily compared to the Shadow of a wraith
                                            page 387 sidebar: "...the rabbi shouldn't out to screw over the Circle, not deliberately." = Probably should read 'shouldn't set out to screw over'
                                            page 391 left column at the bottom: "...and there's no telling what the individual Shadows will do to get the Psyche sin trouble." = the word 'sin' should be 'in'
                                            page 393 right column: "A Risen who chooses to go back isn't always successful in her driving mission, and not every mission results on a Resolved Passion or Fetter." = should probably read 'results in a Resolved Passion or Fetter.'
                                            page 395 sidebar: 'Endurance' should be replaced with 'Stamina'
                                            page 395 right column: There is another reference to the non-existent Wealth background
                                            page 395 right column: Here it says that a wraith needs 1 level of Puppetry and 2 of the three Lifeweb, Inhabit, and Embody. Page 389 says that they need 3 dots in Puppetry and one dot in each of the other three. These two pages (389 & 395) give different prerequisites for Rising.
                                            page 396 under Fascinate: "It a focused, distilled version of that obsession...." = should read 'It is a focused, distilled version....'
                                            page 396 under Fascinate: "Instead, it serves a mechanism for persuasion...." = should probably read 'Instead, it serves as a mechanism for persuasion....'
                                            page 397 at the top of the page: "A target can't receive more than one Driving Urge,...." = The name of this power is no longer Driving Urge; it is Overriding Behavior.
                                            page 405: "But there's people, and then there's people, and then there are the things that either used to be people or never were,...." = Was it intentional to repeat the first clause? I'm not against it if it was intentional. Reads a bit quirky.
                                            page 407 left column: "There are those who do it in exchange for protection of their Fetters, or favors done their living loved ones." = Maybe it reads better 'favors done for their living loved ones.'
                                            page 408 left column bottom: "While for many years, the PRW worked with what were called Ectoplasmic Converter Engines,...." = Normally this book uses far less commas than I would (a matter of taste), but in this instance the comma after the word 'years' doesn't belong.
                                            page 407 sidebar: The sidebar just ends mid-sentence. Also... should that be a Perception + Awareness roll (or Alertness even), or is it just straight Perception?
                                            page 409 right column at the bottom: There is a reference to True Faith, but there is no system for True Faith in this book. This was not the only reference to True Faith. It also appears on page 445 in the first full paragraph.
                                            page 410 left column: "The Arcanoi listings for the sample vampires below match vampiric Disciplines to those Arcanoi that arc most similar." = The word 'arc' should be 'are'
                                            page 410 right column: "Most also possess the Discipline Auspex...." = The previous line says 'Discipline of Thaumaturgy', which personally I think sounds better. 'Discipline of Auspex' probably sounds a bit smoother.
                                            page 412 right column near top: "Humans who view one of these nine-foot monsters, or indeed any Garou in Crinos, usually full into delirious fright." = the word 'full' should be 'fall'
                                            page 415 left column near the top: "...changeling cantrips arc less about brute force than they are about trickery,...." = The word 'arc' should be 'are'
                                            page 417 under Outrage: "She can, however, set the material in his pockets alight, and let him deal that as he may." = should read 'and let him deal with that as he may.'
                                            page 422 under the Kraken: I think the phrase is 'dwarfs the largest ship'
                                            page 425 Sweeny's Pride: "This unassuming straight razor, with a finely honed blade and mother-of-pearl inlay in the oak handle. appears fairly innocuous." = The period after 'handle' should be a comma
                                            page 425 Emperor's Nightingale: "It flies at a slow, but steady and unbroken pace so that its owner can follow,...." = Either put a comma after the word 'unbroken' or get rid of the comma after the word 'slow'
                                            page 427 right column at the bottom: The sentence that begins with "they can fly at 2x normal walking pace,..." needs to be capitalized
                                            page 428 Bloodwebs: The sentence that begins with "anyone hit with a Bloodweb...." needs to be capitalized.
                                            page 428 Bloodwebs: Sometimes a singular Bloodweb is referred to by the pronoun 'they' and sometimes by 'it'
                                            page 429 right column at the bottom: This is the only place that the ability "Dodge" appears, the irony being that in the combat section "Athletics" is used to Dodge. As has been suggested, this should be replaced here with the ability "Athletics" and the game brought closer inline with other WoD games in getting rid of the overly narrow skill Dodge.
                                            page 431 Maggot Revolver: The sentence that begins with "Based on a Colt Anaconda...." has no verb.
                                            page 431 Maggot Revolver: "Rather than shooting bullets, however, the gun launches revolting creatures called Shadow Maggots, one maggot is delivered on each success on a damage roll...." = This is two sentences; a period or semi-colon should appear between 'Shadow Maggots' and 'one', replacing the comma that is there.
                                            page 434 left column: "The p'o, on the other hand, represents the part of the spirit that identifies most with the physical remains of the wraith and to the physical world." = I would drop the word 'to' at the end of the sentence and say 'of the wraith and the physical world.'
                                            page 434 left column at the bottom: "But many choose to live in Shadowlands, adapting to their new situation as best they can." = Usually it would be 'to live in the Shadowlands'
                                            page 434 right column top: "Jade reapers can be found expanding as far as Polynesia as the emperor extends his influence,...." = Should we capitalize the word 'emperor' here, since it is THE emperor?
                                            page 434 right column bottom: "From the perspective of citizens in Yellow Springs,...." = Might read better as 'citizens in the Yellow Springs'
                                            page 436 right column bottom: "Fearful of death and of the many enemies he sent to the Shadowlands. Qin Shihuang started a two-pronged strategy." = The period between 'Shadowlands' and 'Qin' should be a comma
                                            page 438 left column: "And as such he as reigned, though Maelstrom, war, rebellion and even peace, ever since." = Probably the word 'though' should be 'through'
                                            page 440 left column under Ti Yu: the name Feng-tu should be capitalized as Feng-Tu to be consistent with earlier references
                                            page 450 left column: "Before the Heretics came, the wraiths of the Flayed Lands only knew one way to harvest Pathos; the sacrifices made by the human priests." = Should that be a colon instead of a semi-colon?
                                            page 453: In the last line the 'Mla Watu' are misspelled as 'Mia Watu'
                                            page 454: Sinkinda should be spelled Sikinda to be consistent with the rest of the book I think
                                            page 456 at the top: The first and second sentences are not separated by a period. 'themBut' appears
                                            page 457 left column: 'Les Imvisibles' is misspelled in the second full paragraph
                                            page 457 left column: in the same paragraph "iIsland" is likewise misspelled
                                            page 457 right column bottom: The second to last paragraph ends quite suddenly (and mysteriously, too)
                                            page 458 right column at the top: "With this power, the Scholar an issue a single command...." = should read 'the Scholar can issue a single command'
                                            page 459 right column: "If the Artisan is attempting to imbue an Arcanos power, it costs a Willpower point plus another Pathos for the level of the Arcanos power being imbued." = Should that read "...plus another Pathos per level of the Arcanos power being imbued"? I can't quite figure out what 'for the level of the Arcanos power being imbued' means in this sentence
                                            page 461 right column Coax: "To use Coax, rollWay of the Soul...." = There needs to be a space between 'roll' and 'Way'
                                            page 461 right column Alter Demeanor: Charisma + Manipulation roll. Really? I thought that was a typo in the Dark Kingdom of Jade Book
                                            page 461 right column: with the difficulty of the p'o's Angst +4, there is a chance for a difficulty of 14. Seems a bit much
                                            page 463: Too many references to the Great War, to Kinesis, and to Swar instead of Svarga. Under Wraithride there is a Sense Kinesis roll
                                            page 465: Two of the paragraphs in the left column are broken in two
                                            page 466 left column: "And in the European hells, time is not to be wasted, for as long an African wraith remains outside the Bush of Ghosts, his very soul mutates into the dyad soul of a Stygian wraith...." = the word 'dyad' is presumably the word 'dead' misspelled. I would also insert the word as to create the phrase 'as long as'
                                            page 469 sidebar: "Orpheus possesses little knowledge of Stygia, operating exclusively in the Shadowlands and only now slowly starting to scratch the surface of death's mystery." This sentence feels a bit awkward. Maybe "...Shadowlands, and is only now...", adding a comma after Shadowlands and the verb 'is'
                                            page 472 right column: "...Orpheus knew skimming was possible, and they ventured into Underworld." = Maybe they ventured into the Underworld'
                                            page 474: Lifesight and Death = Should be 'Deathsight' I assume
                                            page 476 right column: "Besides espionage, the NSA and through them the CIA and Homeland Security, continues to employ Orpheus for the occasional targeted killing." = There should be a comma after NSA
                                            page 477 lower sidebar: Bounces back and forth between referring to 'points' and 'dots'. Probably should mostly be 'dots'
                                            page 480 left column near bottom: "While in suspension, the sleeper cannot recover Pathos." ... "The projecting sleeper recovers Pathos as any other wraith." = These two sentences seem to totally contradict each other.
                                            page 484 Gossamer Webs: "On successful Brawl attack...." = Probably should say 'On a successful Brawl attack....'
                                            page 484 Spite-fueled Arcanoi: "This is common Stain, and rumors abound...." = should read 'This is a common Stain....' Also might consider capitalizing the F in Spite-fueled in the title
                                            page 486 left column at the bottom: A reference to the Resources background
                                            page 487 left column at the top: "...the projector loses a point of Willpower and take a level of Aggravated damage." = Should be 'takes a level of Aggravated damage'
