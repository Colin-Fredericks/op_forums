New Arcanoi arts therefore are more akin to scientific progression, as weird as it sounds.

            Ceremony might be a good descriptor. Rituals work with power from the performer, Rites call on external sources. A ceremony is more like a formalized action to support a technique; for the Shadowlands it would mean utilizing the native building blocks (like Memory, Souls, Pathos etc) in unique ways. Soulforging would be a particular example- and I like the idea that something like that has always been there without anyone paying too much mind to it. It leaves the setting intact.

            For example, a low-level ceremony employing Memory could be:

            Using a reed from the banks of the River of Death, the Wraith can focus the ambient memoria of a Haunt into a small soulfire from which they can regain Pathos.

            System: One reed creates a soulfire the size of a small campfire. It burns for a number of hours equal to the Haunt rating. For each hour spent in direct proximity of the soulfire, a Wraith regains one dot of Pathos. The reed is consumed in the process, and the ambient memoria need to accumulate for 24 hours before the same site can be used again.
            Stuff like this, actions that seem so mundane to the average Wraith, that they hardly pay any attention- but for those with a knack for metaphysics could scale up to the consecration of a Citadel.

                Brief coda, one thing I have ignored so far are Artifacts.There are some examples that are pretty interesting in the context of ceremonies:

                Fetterguard (Level 5 Artifact)

                Appearing as nothing more than a small soulsteel box, inside it holds miniature versions of its owner’s Fetters. Each of these must be made by the wraith herself, though whether she does it through the forge, through Moliate, or through putting together bits and bobs she recovered in the Underworld makes no difference. As long as these icons remain safe within the box, no harm will come to them in the real world, and even supernatural attempts at damaging them will somehow go awry.​
                This could easily be interpreted as a ceremony where the Artifact elements are components, and is honestly pretty close to any Ritual you would see with Path sorcery. It's probably possible to create new ceremonies by looking at Artifacts and transferring their mechanics.
