                                                            Grim Fandango is brilliant, and it was one of my main creative inspirations when I got into working in videogames.

                                                            Festivals for the dead, among other things, offer an awful lot of emotional energy directed towards the dead. There just might be some rituals developed around that...

                                                            I'd do a dicepool split for the jump/enshroud, but the jump should be ridiculously low difficulty.

                                                            The Dark Kingdoms are built on a combination of dominant cultural paradigm, ability to find & hold territory in the Underworld, and occasional bits of plain luck. The major powers have secured major real estate; that's not to say there aren't outposts of wraiths who came out of a different culture who interact with the afterlife in their own way, trying to carve out their own territory here and there. Of course, that sort of existence isn't likely to make them friendly to outsiders. As for the Dark Kingdom of Wire, that name is bitterly ironic, and the entity it describes exists within Stygia's borders.

                                                            I still have one of the old white Wraith t-shirts. At one point we were talking about doing a black long-sleeve, but it never happened. I think there was a black short-sleeve shirt that had John Cobb art on it back in the early days of the game, but that's ancient history and my brain is fluffy.

                                                            And on a completely unrelated note: Over the past year, I've had people send me pictures of themselves doing terrible things to my latest novel, VAPORWARE. You can find the greatest hits at https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WnbOSGSarYg. Be afraid. Be very afraid.

                                                            Deadguy out.
