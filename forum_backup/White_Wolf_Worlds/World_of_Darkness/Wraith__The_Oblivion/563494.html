    Sample Passions

    06-21-2015, 07:19 PM
    One thing I always struggle with in making Wraith characters is deciding on their Passions. I tend to give them three to four each, most often four and whilst I can generally give them one really good one, maybe two... I'd rather not play a Wraith with two Rank 5 Passions.

    So to remedy that and also for people to show off their own ideas on some cool Passions examples... Here's a thread to list some and also talk about why they make good ones. Bonus internet cookies will be handed out to those who link their Passions to Fetters too.

    Okay everybody... Show me some cool Passions!

          I always think of them as plot hooks primarily. As an ST I always ask players to go beyond the obvious Passions. 'Protect my family (Love)' is a bit simple for me, for instance, and provides fewer opportunities than, say, 'Guard my family (Jealousy)' or even the slightly sweeter 'Watch my family (Regret)'. For me, I always write down the obvious ones first, and then go back to them, and ask myself how I can change the tone of the Passion to create more nuanced motivations for the wraith. No is motivated by entirely altruistic emotions all the time. Although you have to be careful not to overlap with Dark Passions, of course. But I go even darker with these: 'Hurt my family (Masochism)' or 'Control my family (Spite)'.

              Note the difference in the two options above in regards to family. One is about possessiveness. The second is about regretting missed opportunities to live a life together, or failing to fulfil social expectations. In terms of narrative, they provide me with more interesting motivations than more straightforward emotions, because they add negative personality traits. Yes, shadows are negative personality traits personified too, but shadows have very specific end goals: feeding Oblivion. So Passions can be 'dark' or rather 'less than saintly', while Dark Passions are downright destructive and self-defeating.

                      I love the character concept. It reminds me a bit of John Rambo from First Blood (more the book and less the film). What follows are a few ideas for some tweaks; feel free to use or ignore as you see fit. This is only to get those tasty synaptic juices flowing…

                      Passions
                      In keeping with the idea of the Lost Soldier, the first two Passions could be combined into something like, “Find a Place to Belong.” It combines the idea of “Home” with some slight romantic connotations. For the driving emotion, maybe Loneliness?
                      Find a Place to Belong (Loneliness)

                      [If you want to annoy your troupe, you could use “Find a Place to Belong (Sehnsucht)” ]

                      Investigate Cause of Death
                      His cause of death came from exposure to the chemical weapons, right? Does he know it was the chemical weapons, or does he know that “something” happened that was ultimately responsible for his death? Or is he totally in the dark? If the latter, why would he investigate?

                      Curiosity: The character has an inkling that there was more to his death and wants to discover what…if he has time. If the opportunity presents itself. If he gets bored. If he happens to come across something. Etc. He’s curious about it, but it’s not the core of his psyche. Investigate Cause of Death (Curiosity)

                      Revenge: In this revision, the character has a really good idea about his exposure. He knows what happened, but he doesn’t know that bastards responsible. He’ll find them, on any side of the Shroud, and make them pay. Investigate Cause of Death (Revenge)

                      Fear: Maybe he wanted his death to be natural. At least, as natural as a wandering soldier’s life can be. He wants to keep his normal mundane death despite the whispers of his Shadow. He digs into these circumstances like a husband who *knows* his wife is cheating on him but tries to find evidence that “explains it away.” Investigate Cause of Death (Fear)

                      Make Amends for Nicaragua (Remorse)
                      It sounds like you have a good handle on this one. If you wanted to keep the character more aggressive and less weepy, you could do “Make Amends for Nicaragua (Self-Hatred)” but there’s nothing wrong with having a character like this have a softer side.

                      Fetters

                      "The Mourner"
                      Based on what we know about your soldier, this was probably the only significant source of social interaction the character had while alive. Which makes perfect sense. Someone who didn’t connect with other people would totally stick around the one person he did connect with.

                      Medals
                      Again, this Fetter makes perfect sense for the character, especially with its associated Passion (with whatever emotion you go with).

                      Car
                      Was this the car he lived out of? Like a tiny mobile home?

                      Scrapbook
                      I think characters need at least one semi-cheery Fetter. Something that reminds them of better times also helps make their current conditions (ectoplasmic and dead) more tragic. Nice touch.

                      Hope some of this helps.

                              Is the story being primarily told in one Necropolis?                               It sort of sounds like the Mourner is the crux of this character; the image I’m getting is this wandering, PTSD-riddled, vagabond badass finally finds a tiny place in the cosmos where he *could* have a home, complete with that [potential] special-someone (the Mourner?). Then, just as fate looks as though it might finally deal him a hand he doesn’t have to fold right away, complications from his former life (the chemical attack side-effects?) rapidly deteriorate his health.

                              Did you ever see the movie The Equalizer (2014), Ghost Dog: The Way of the Samurai (1999) or even Steel Dawn (1987)? They might give you some inspiration for delving into this character’s doomed relationship.

Take retribution on the family of those who hurt me (Anger) or Remind people the fragility of life (Pity), or even Take pleasure in the suffering of others (Glee) can be Passions if they keep you fighting the pull of Oblivion.

                                          Likewise, there aren't a lot of positive emotions that couldn't be in the hands of your Shadow. In one of the books (the Shadow Player's Guide I think) there is a Shadow with the Dark Passion Destroy the Jukebox at my favorite bar (Irritation) and is specifically stated that the reason it is a Dark Passion is because in life the Character thought it was extremely petty. So yeah, Dark Passions ore quite often inherently vile, but they don't have to be for a creative Shadow to make use of them. Just imagine how much damage a love you don't want to admit you have, or the false hope of a brighter future could do in the hands of your dark side.

                                                      Hello!

                                                      Yes, the issue of making a Passion which could be seen as a Dark Passion is a tough one. As stated above, I have four Passions for the character mentioned and really like to spread out their aspirations/motivations to that many anyway, as I like to play characters that can actually roll well enough to Resolve their Passions and eventually Transcend. That in itself 'Seek Transcendance (Courage) or (Curiosity) or (Hope), that can make a good Passion and I have seen some of the pregen characters in the 1st edition supplements that have a Passion that is a quest to Transcend.

                                                      A real issue for this particular character seems to be in how he deals with his involvement in the dark times of the cold war, whether he seeks to atone for his action (or inaction) by doing good or rather walks down a more self-destructive path to atone, which is more a Dark Passion. Either way, I felt confronting this weight on his shoulders and resolving the issue or his Shadow somehow wrecking it via Cartharsis forcing the Psyche to move on from this trouble is something I've wanted to delve into but has been really tough to try and put in a well-written way on the sheet.

                                                      The character's Shadow at this point btw is decided on being a Parent. The Psyche wants to know just what led him to the Shadowlands, to finally find a place to belong, to be forgiven of past sins, to find someone who loves him. The Shadow would then want to make the wraith forget about the circumstances of his death, it doesn't matter... They're dead now and they don't need anyone or anything. They have the Shadow now... Seemed very Parent-like, to make the Psyche a shut-in and I agree.

                                                      Anyway, one thing that was mentioned is the Shadow has a Dark Passion called 'Punish self over Nicaragua 3 (Guilt)' compared to the Psyche's 'Overcome Guilt over Nicaragua (Remorse)'. The Emotion was in some flux, as both Guilt and Shame had been mentioned for the Dark Passion, but Guilt was chosen as 'Shame' is somewhat of the Shadow's calling card, but the act of feeling it and hiding away was covered in other things. Guilt is more the shitty feeling of compromising one's own moral standards and thus was perfect for the Dark Passion.

                                                      Note also the slight change in wording of the Passion for the Psyche now... Perhaps less proactive than the older wording, though clearly states the end game for resolving it. Thinking on it though, I like 'Make Amends...' better... Haha! xD
