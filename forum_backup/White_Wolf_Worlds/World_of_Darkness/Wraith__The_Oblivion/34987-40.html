                                                        A couple of quick notes before I get on a plane for work (yes, the day job still calls on occasion):

                                                        1-I very much appreciate the enthusiasm behind any and all questions. I also appreciate folks on the list stepping up to answer stuff they feel comfortable with. Don't worry about asking too much; just bear in mind that I may not be able to get to every question or it may have been answered elsewhere.

                                                        2-All the crossover stuff back in the day was thoroughly vetted between line developers. There was never any disdain or disrespect for a particular game, in part because we were all writing for/playing lots of each others' stuff and getting into pissing matches over whose continuity trumped whose would have made for a ridiculously awful working environment. So it's cool that folks feel passionately about their favorite games vis-a-vis one another, but please don't ascribe intent that wasn't there. (I was there. I know what happened. Honest.)

                                                        3-The Legion of Fate is very picky and also understaffed. That being said, sending a fresh wraith off to the forges when they were clearly marked as belonging to Fate is not considered a wise career choice by anyone who wants to keep doing business in Stygia. And yes, sometimes what's fateful about a wraith is the use they will be put to after forging, or the act of being forged itself (if it, say, inspires another wraith to give up soulforging and go do something different and important). It's kind of horrible on a metaphysical level, but Fate's a harsh mistress.

                                                        4-Yes, there's a lot of taffy-pulling and other things during forging besides the usual hammering. Don't make me haul out the notes I've been kicking around on plasmic density and whatnot :-)

                                                        5-A wraith's corpus is largely based on their self-image. So a guy who got woodchipped a la Fargo probably would see himself as whole in the afterlife, rather than as the world's first oak-infused cannibalistic bolognese sauce.

                                                        6-OK, Ghost Towns flashback. I need to go sit down for a bit.

                                                        -deadguy out
