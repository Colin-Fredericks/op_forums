    1001 Wraith NPCs & character concepts

    09-04-2021, 10:34 AM
    A topic to share characters that could be useful or inspiration for our Wraith chronicles. Every level of detail is welcome. I'll start with this major NPC I wrote this week for an upcoming WtO game:


    1.     Thomas Börner, Emerald Anacreon
    A political mastermind who governs from his church Haunt beyond the northern frontier of the Leipzig Necropolis. For about two centuries now Thomas is the ranking Emerald official here, making him one of Leipzig's longest serving Anacreons. And although power still firmly rests with the Grim Legion, he's undoubtedly the most respected local Hierarch as well.
    Thomas lived during the first half of the 17th century in what was then the village of Mockau, having since become part of the city. The son of a miller, he followed in his father's footsteps and married a beautiful girl. Unfortunately Thomas came of age during the Thirty Years' War. Although feeling little appetite for leaving his young wife and daughter, the local pastor made a convincing argument. Telling the young man it was his duty to God to protect Lutheranism against the Catholic hordes. In taking up arms for his faith he and his family would earn divine protection.
    In his years away from home, fighting under Swedish command, Thomas did feel protected. Never suffering any serious injuries, while he saw countless others perish. When finally being able to return to Mockau he was horrified to learn the pastor's promise had in fact not held up. During Thomas' absence the bubonic plague had ravaged the village. His parents, his wife, his child, they were all gone. Ditched in a mass grave only a few weeks before his return. Thomas felt betrayed by God and the pastor that spoke for Him. The furious soldier went to the church looking to mete out his revenge on the clergyman. Only to discover he had long since been buried as well. Thomas instead screamed at the heavens, cursing God as a storm was brewing. Only moments later the raging blasphemer was struck down by a bolt of lightning.
    The Enfant had a Reaper from the Legion of Fate waiting for him. Once the situation was explained, Thomas vehemently refused to be inducted into that particular Legion. Insisting that his death was purely accidental, completely rejecting a higher power like fate or God. After arriving in Stygia, the Emerald Legion took possession of him instead.
    A couple of decades later he returned to Leipzig's Shadowlands as a Legionnaire. Part of a Stygian force that was to establish a Necropolis there. Sadly Thomas was unable to find any trace of his family. Which only made him fight that much harder, knowing who to blame. Years of religious strife had left the area a hive of heresy that was to be eradicated. The Legionnaire took particular pleasure in tracking down his former pastor, sending him to the forges.
    As the Necropolis grew, Thomas was dismayed to see the local Hierarchy's stance on Heretics softening over time. Multiple Circles of them were left in peace as long they didn't draw too much attention to themselves. This served as the impetus for the ambitious Lemure's long rise through the ranks. Hoping to one day find himself in a position where he could dictate a zero-tolerance policy toward heresy.
    Thomas finally achieved the rank of Anacreon in the wake of the Battle of Nations, after his predecessor lost the last of his Fetters. Unfortunately the astounding number of casualties also confirmed the Grim Anacreon as absolute ruler of the Necropolis. The influence any other Legion would have would be negligible at best. Except if one of those other Anacreons were to be a particularly shrewd politician. Exactly what Thomas has developed into over the last couple of centuries. Becoming especially effective in the post-war period. As the Fifth Great Maelstrom left the Leipzig Grim Legion with a far younger and less experienced leader. With all his schemes starting to pay off, it looks like Anacreon Börner might finally get his way.

    Image: A well-built figure wearing a wide-brimmed hat and 1630s soldier garb. As to command further respect from the ubiquitous Grim Legion, he always has a sword of Stygian steel buckled to his side. As well as wearing a skeleton mask similar in style to the helmets worn by the Grim military. Under the mask is the face of a man in his early twenties, sporting a moustache and short beard. Thomas' most fetching feature are his compassionate hazel eyes.

    Roleplaying Hints: You hate God, in your opinion it's just another guise of Oblivion. He's the entity that's taken everything from you, who you fear and hate. However centuries of politicking have taught not to reveal your true feelings about anything. Everyone should instead have the impression that you're on their side. Which means you rarely give a straight answer to any question. Your soft spot for women and children is the only aspect of your true motives you've been unable to hide entirely.

              3. Antoon Duval, Hunter of The Hun
              The leader of Les Patriotes, a Renegade group that hunts the former battlefields in the West of Flanders for the ghosts of enemy combatants. Now since the onset of the Second World War he's looking to drive off German forces in the Skinlands as well. Antoon sees his great-granddaughterAnna as a means to that end.
              Back when the rural Flemish village of Beselare was still the Marquisate Beselare, the Duvals were one of its most prominent families. Second only to the marquises themselves, who for generations they served as bailiffs. A glory period that ended with the French Revolution, half a century before Antoon was born. Stories the boy was raised on, teaching him to be proud of his family and how important they'd been to their village. Lessons which led to his decision to study medicine. Becoming a country doctor was not the most prestigious vocation a young man of his intellect could achieve. However he knew it was a role he'd be respected for in his local community like few other men were.
              Never straying far from home, Antoon's life would be far from adventurous. Filled with happiness though. He married, had many children, then came grandchildren. Only in those final years did things turn sour. Antoon lost his wife, also the physical ability to keep practicing medicine. And then the Great War erupted. Beselare lay right between Ypres and the advancing German army. The entire village was forcibly evacuated. Antoon didn't want to go, but was too feeble to resist. As a living man he'd never see his home again. Less than two weeks later he'd been buried in Northern France. A rural gravesite that would be lost during the course of the war.
              After Antoon's death the Enfant was swiftly Reaped and put in chains. Spending the next couple of years as a Thrall of the Douai Necropolis. When the Night of the Short Chains occurred he was able to use the chaos to escape. Antoon braved the Great Maelstrom, gradually making his way home. In the Skinlands his village was no more. The Beselare he remembered was by then a ghost like Antoon himself. Sheltering in these Relic buildings were both familiar faces, many former patients, as well as foreign soldiers. It was Antoon who was instrumental in rallying Beselare's wraith in defense of their home against the Maelstrom and the many Spectres. When those threats had subsided he turned on the German Restless in their midst. Souls he'd previously fought alongside with were sold off for weaponry. That had always been Antoon's plan.
              A couple of decades later Beselare is Renegade fortress existing in an uneasy peace with the nearby Necropoli of Ypres, Kortrijk and Roeselare. It's an open secret that several local Anacreons support Les Patriotes. Primarily cause these Renegades' depredations are seen as a check on the Grim Legion's power. But also because German and Austro-Hungarian wraiths are simply detested by much of the native Restless Dead. Besides Hierarchs, Antoon has good relations with the Masquers Guild as well, to which he owes his hunting pack of barghests.

              Image: Nothing like the decrepit old man he died as. His Corpus is that of a man in his prime, be it a white-bearded one. Appears well-dressed in turn of the century black suit and tie. When out hunting he brandishes a soulsteel hunting knife in his right hand. While in the left Antoon holds the leash to four vicious barghests.

              Roleplaying Hints: You're an old fashioned gentleman. Charismatic, yet obviously ruthless. With but few exceptions you consider all Belgians to be decent people. Germans and their ilk are all awful, with no exceptions. Hunting down their souls and condemning them to the forges is a pleasure. While for the dead of Beselare you'll fight till Oblivion takes you.

                  4. Jan van Speyk, the Sea Beggars' last hope
                  A Renegade captain sailing the Shadowland reflection of the English Channel. Continuously on the lookout for relic ships to salvage, as to counteract the swift decline of the Sea Beggars.
                  Originally an orphan from Amsterdam who during the early 19th century worked himself up in the Royal Netherlands Navy. Jan served in the Dutch East Indies from 1823 to 1825. Successfully attacking Bangka and Java, which earned him the nickname "Terror of the Bandits". During the Belgian Revolution, lieutenant commander van Speyk was given command of a gunboat deployed on the Scheldt river. In the winter of 1831, a gale had his crew lose control of the vessel. They drifted off toward Antwerp where the boat was besieged by a mob of Belgians. Rather than surrendering Jan instead threw his cigar into a barrel of gunpowder. In the resultant explosion all but 3 of his 30 subordinates perished, as well as a number of Belgian rebels.
                  In the Skinlands the Dutch hailed van Speyk as a naval hero. His physical remains, sans head, were recovered to be buried with honors in Amsterdam. Jan's Underworld reception was far less welcoming. His former crew felt betrayed by their commanding officer's actions, these wraiths wanted revenge. The Grim Legion was happy to oblige, putting van Speyk on trial for murder. Condemned to be soulforged into an ashtray, if not for the intervention by the Sea Beggars. These were seafaring Renegades who regularly raided Necropoli along the coast and major rivers of the Low Countries. Dutch nationalists who wished to add this fallen hero to their ranks. Besides freeing the convict they also made off with the relic of his gunboat.
                  Over the next century or so van Speyk took great pleasure in ransacking the Necropoli of the upstart Belgians. Until the Sea Beggars started rapidly declining in the wake of the Second World War. With all the relic battleships they'd recovered the Stygian navy had grown vastly more potent, overpowering the Renegades' fleet at every turn. With van Speyk being one of the senior Sea Beggars remaining in the Shadowlands, he feels obligated to improve their lot.

                  Image: An average looking man of about 30. Jan's eyes are pale blue, his hair and sideburns are brown. Jan's clad in a dark blue naval uniform, to our modern eyes Napoleonic era in style.A Stygian steel saber hangs from his belt. Between the fingers of his right hand the wraith's always holding a smoking cigar. This is not a relic, but a permanent touch of Oblivion on van Speyk's Corpus.

                  Roleplaying Hints: Prone to give grand speeches, preaching about country and faith. Within you're still marked by your humble beginnings though. Eager to prove yourself in the eyes of God and wraith. Never showing guilt about the things you've done, it all serves a greater, yet unknown, purpose. Perhaps Transcendence will be your final reward?

                      5. Marie Masereel, the waifish Reaper
                      A Skeletal socialite who has her Haunt in the Blankenberge Necropolis. She supports her lavish afterlifestyle by freelance Reaping throughout much of Flanders' Shadowlands.
                      The girl grew up among the moneyed elite in late 19th century Antwerp. Tragically she contracted tuberculosis during her teens. Though for years treatment kept the disease under control, she was unable to lead a normal life. Her formative years were spent in isolation from the world she yearned to join. Most of the time Marie's only companionship was her governess, also serving as a nurse. Much of her final years were spent away from home, at the family residence on the Belgian coast in Blankenberge, taking in the fresh air. It was during such a stay that consumption finally claimed her.
                      As a wraith Marie originally kept her distance from the Hierarchy. Until after the Great War broke out, when she volunteered for active duty in the Skeletal Legion. Fighting alongside her baby brother, who in his middle age had succumbed to the Spanish Flu. Marie eventually climbed to the rank of Centurion, her brother unfortunately fell to Oblivion. She hopes to one day redeem his soul. Part of her wider objective to reclaim the life she never had, in the Underworld. Hence why she left active military service to pursue a career in Reaping. Providing her with the contacts and funds to gather Masereel souls, creating a close-knit family from those few loved ones that became wraiths as well.

                      Image: A face which is the romantic ideal of consumption; pale with rosy cheeks. Marie's pallor forming a perfect contrast to her dark hair, slightly damp with sweat. This wraith generally appears in the white nightgown she died in. The only real color her Corpus shows are the spatters of coughed up blood on her chest. This is only her day-to-day attire though. Marie owns a wide selection of lavish gowns, souls expertly Moliated by Masquer craftswraiths. She slides one of these over her Corpus whenever attending a ball or other social function. She's generally not shy about her profession, only rarely leaving her Haunt without carrying Reaper's Chains and having a barghest at her side.

                      Roleplaying Hints: Old fashioned or what some might describe as an entitled brat. You believe in class. That there's an order to both the Skinlands and the Underworld, which puts people or wraiths like you at the top. Illness kept you from enjoying your god-given privileges, you're determined to seize them in death.
                      You'll at first approach them as an angel, yet you feel no pity towards Thralls. You love your family, you respect Hierarchs, anyone else is fair game.

                          6. Ivo, the Outsider
                          Sometimes a lonely child is comforted by what they'd used to describe as a goblin. However kids these days generally believe it's an alien entering their bedroom at night. Ivo's no extraterrestrial though and he certainly doesn't come in peace.
                          During the 1930s he was born in Berlin to the newlywed Stoltzes. Because of complications during the pregnancy Ivo was to remain their only child. Unfortunately Frieda Stoltz's difficult labor had also affected her boy's health. In the enfant's first months he developed hydrocephalus; fluid build-up within his brain. After a year of treatment the baby's condition was finally under control. But his enlarged skull would forever mark Ivo as being different, a dangerous thing in Nazi Germany. His father was a party member himself, though fought for his son to be treated right. Putting everything on the line when against the state's wishes he insisted Ivo get a normal education, not be shipped off to some institution.
                          Although Ivo was of normal intelligence, he had an awful time at school. Ostracized by students and teachers both, who saw him as subhuman. The loneliness this caused took a terrible toll on the boy. Yet far worse was still to come during the war.
                          One morning while Ivo was walking to school his home was hit by an air raid. With no family left who'd defend him, the boy's fate now rested in the Nazi regime's hands. As part of the T4 Program the authorities sent him to the Dösen Sanatorium in Leipzig. Their disabled children's wing had only one real objective; murder. Some young patients were outright killed, many were experimented on, all were withheld proper care. Not receiving enough food Ivo slowly starved to death. After which as a final indignity the attending doctor removed his head to be preserved in a jar of formaldehyde.
                          Ivo's head was first brought to the medical university in Berlin. Where it would remain until taken by looters when the Third Reich fell. Since that time the jar has moved from one private collection to another. Closely followed by a Spectral Ivo who does not take kindly to his physical remains being treated as a curio piece. The offending collectors tend to befall terrible accidents and children go missing.

                          Image: A sickly child of about eight years in age. His emaciated Corpus is naked except for a pair of stained briefs. Decades of Spectrehood have further warped the size of Ivo's cranium, the shaved scalp has multiple tears revealing the bone underneath. His sockets lack eyes, within instead burns the black fire of Oblivion.

                          Roleplaying Hints: For a Spectre you're unusually preoccupied by the Skinlands. Wraiths have little to fear, it's living children who're your focus. Approaching them as a friendly visitor from another world. They're of course unaware that you aren't describing an alien planet, but the Underworld. You gradually attempt to win their confidence, culminating in you luring them away for what you consider to be the ultimate bonding experience: Trapping them in a remote place to starve as you did.

                              7. Hubert the hue
                              A Restless resident of the NextWorld offices in Berlin. When not on missions he can usually be found there watching television. Unless of course he's out slumming the city's pigment hotspots.
                              Hubert Wagner originally hails from a rural village south of Leipzig, Germany. An economically desolate area during the 1990s, his parents were rarely employed. Hence why the boy grew up relatively poor. Getting into mischief with his friends was one of the few divergences he could afford. The back of the old cemetery became a favored hangout spot to those kids. Right next to an old Napoleonic war memorial is where Hubert smoked his first cigarette, drank his first can of beer, felt up his first girl,... By their mid-teens these youngsters had graduated to weed, occasionally experimenting with something stronger. So it was that at one time a friend of Hubert's returned from Berlin with a baggie of pigment. Soon enough each teen there snorted a line, right off their favorite war monument.
                              If one just wanted a relaxing high, they couldn't have picked a worse spot to try black heroin. The village's wraithly society centered around that very same graveyard. As the drug started kicking in, a couple of dozen strange figures came into view. Staring right at them, trying to gain Pathos from the hot-blooded teens, as these wraiths had done hundreds of times before. Panic broke out, all the drug-addled Quick fled the scene. Except Hubert, like always wanting to be the toughest of the bunch. Not truly believing what he was seeing, the young man was able to get a grip on his terror. Demonstrating his cockiness by striking up a conversation with the prettiest face among the dour crowd. She was a maiden wearing clothing centuries out of date. Eager to tell her tale of woe to someone who could help.
                              By the time the pigment wore off Hubert had accepted what he'd experienced; a real ghost girl was in need of his assistance. Kornelia had never gotten a proper burial. Her murderers had hidden away her body, trapping her soul on Earth. Or so she told him, as well as the exact location where these physical remains could be found. After a little digging Hubert did indeed discover an ancient skeleton at that exact spot. Once and for all confirming that it hadn't all just been a hallucination, as his buddies kept insisting.
                              Hubert went from the hole he dug right to the local dealer's door. He wanted to talk to Kornelia again, so he'd need to buy more pigment. Not something the guy normally carried; it would cost a pretty pfennig. Yet a week later the young man was once again snorting black powder. Kornelia seemed overjoyed to meet him again, requesting he rebury her skeletal remains in consecrated ground, setting her soul free. Hubert however declined, requiring something from the restless spirit first; hidden treasure. Hubert was a school dropout with no prospects, he had to get money somehow. A pet ghost seemed like the answer. Kornelia had been invisibly floating around for hundreds of years, she ought to know where he'd find riches.
                              Lacking someone to tell him he might have watched too many children's adventure films, Hubert kept pressuring Kornelia. And of course he had to keep doing pigment to urge her on. At least that's what he told himself, as the youngster sank deeper in debt with his dealer. Finally coming to a point where the crook couldn't take anymore of the lies, the promises of a big payday right around the corner. In a fit of anger the pigment pusher kicked Hubert's face in, not stopping until it was a lifeless pulp.
                              Hubert was Reaped by a familiar face; his long deceased grandfather. He told the Enfant to run and never look back. The local wraiths were already hunting for him, Kornelia was out for vengeance. Hubert's flight ended in Necropolis Berlin, hoping to disappear among the hundreds of thousands other wraiths that dwelt there. His ambitions eventually led this hue to finding a new home with NextWorld.

                              Image: Hubert literally looks like a ghost, an entirely translucent apparition. A tall, thin man who appears to be about twenty. With short cropped hair and a ring through his right earlobe. He's wearing the tracksuit and filthy sneakers he died in. When on missions Hubert usually has an Artifact rifle slung over his shoulder.

                              Roleplaying Hints: On the surface you appear to be a brash young man who's quite full of himself. In truth you never liked yourself much. In life the solution was to numb those unpleasant emotions, until an opportunity would present itself to escape your white thrash roots. Fortunately in death you've become a bit more realistic, understanding the worth of hard work. You're quite motivated as a NextWorld employee, the ticket to becoming a big man in the Underworld. And yet, another part of you just wants to do nothing but Skinride pigment addicts.

However it was her Internet searches that allowed the girl to dig deeper. In the message boards and chatrooms frequented by fellow occult geeks she first learned of the Arcanum and pigment. Both the elite scholarly organisation studying the supernatural, and the new drug supposedly allowing one to see ghosts, fascinated Yenifer immensely. The Arcanum was still out of reach, but the teen had no problem finding black heroin on Juarez's streets. At first she found buying and using the narcotic incredibly scary. But Yenifer kept at it, cause while under the influence she really was perceiving ghosts.
                                                          Keeping a drug habit secret from her parents wasn't easy though. Her school results hadn't started to suffer yet, but money became an issue. Pigment didn't come cheap, the girl quickly burned through her savings. The teenage occultist was already cataloguing all she witnessed while high, a research project she definitely didn't want to abandon. And by that time she was of course also already hopelessly addicted. Yenifer ended up solving her financial woes with their cause; she was to be her school's first pigment dealer. Selling just about enough, at inflated prices, to cover her own usage.
                                                          By some miracle Yenifer never got caught and was able to keep her grades up, she made it to graduation. Pressured by her father to follow in his footsteps the teen has recently begun studying medicine at the Juarez university. Her parents were generous enough to rent her an apartment close to campus. Yenifer has since made good use of the view. She's now permanently under the influence, obsessively typing out everything the nearby restless dead are up to. These reports are regularly mailed to the Arcanum Chapter House in Mexico City. She doesn't go to her classes anymore, or really outside much at all. This teen pigment dealer's supplier comes to her. Same with her customers, who she sometimes requests to bring food. Spectres also visit regularly, assuring Yenifer they'll do all in their power to keep her business and her research going.

Yenifer's black hair is usually greasy and unkempt, unless she's expected to meet family. Who also get to see her in other attire than the black tracksuit she spends most of her time in. Whatever she wears it's always long-sleeved though, as to cover the tracks on her arms.

Unless they can further your studies into the paranormal you also brush off everyone as soon as possible. After having used pigment for so long you don't come across as high though. You're an absolute wreck without the narcotic, but function relatively normal when under its influence. More alarmingly you seem to completely lack a danger sense.
