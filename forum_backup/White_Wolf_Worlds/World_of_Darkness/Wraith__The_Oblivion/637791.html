                          This is one of those strange, intangible areas of Wraith, because we don't actually know.

                          On the one hand, yes Wraiths are just plasmic things, they don't have bodies let alone the functions that bodies entail. They sustain themselves on emotion and anchor themselves with meaning. There's no leftover or residue, only pathos and angst.

                          On the other hand, they are defined by memories; it's really all they are. Memories define how they look, what they want, and most importantly, how they act. If self image can determine what they look like, expectation should also determine what hungers and desires they think they are going to have and how they react to those desires. Perhaps with experience Wraiths forget what it is to be bound to the demands of flesh, but in their younger years Wraiths could plausibly feel hunger and quite possibly its counterpart.

                          Mage: The Ice-ension: An Epic Game of Reality on the Rink

                                              You're thinking of Kindred of the East. The ruling Yama King of each Hell (out of the Thousand Hells of Yomi) are virtually omnipotent in their own realm, and get to set whatever rules they want. And most of them go for "psychotically deranged". Being used as toilet paper was even explicitly mentioned in the corebook.

                                              The poor bastards weren't soulforged (or Moliated), though. Doing so would spoil the fun! (At least in the demons' minds). Instead, a demon would just tear a bunch of skin off one of the damned souls, wipe themselves with it, and toss it into a thorny bush. The author (thankfully) left it up to our imaginations whether a damned soul could automatically regrow that skin, or if they'd someone have to "reclaim" it . . .
