    Wraith: which cities would you like to see as Necropoli?

    01-12-2014, 11:27 PM
    Due to the unique structure of the Shadowlands, living cities have their grim echoes for wraiths to live in. Buildings that have been destroyed stand in the Shadowlands, causing modern cities to instead appear as anachronistic hodgepodges. I quite like this, but we never got much material on them - Atlanta got a book, and the final Orpheus book presented a post-6GM New York (if I recall) - but few others. What cities do you think would make for the moste exciting Necropoli? Remember; the older and more troubled, the better.

                                                      Constantinople (if there is ever a Dark Ages Wraith released) or Istanbul (modern setting). It has always kind of annoyed me that Wraith completely ignored the eastern empire.

                                                      After all, throughout the Dark Ages and on into the High Medieval the population of Constantinople alone was more than ten times the population of the largest cities in the West. Sources differ, but at the time of the 4th Crusade the city still had at least 440 000 people and perhaps as many as double that. Imagine the number of Wraiths that ought to have come from the eastern empire, and there is zero mention of it. It is not counted among the first necropolis', even though it should have preexisted them by centuries!

                                                      Numerous fires destroyed important parts of the city throughout history (two "great fires" alone in 1203 and 1204) so the city would be rich in relic buildings, relic items and fresh recruits. Devastating plagues and the destruction and killing alone after the city fell in 1204 and 1453 would provide enough recruits and pathos for a great Wraith setting. In the former case, there was no official body count but the city was significantly depopulated, and in the latter case an eyewitness claimed that so much blood was spilled that "it ran like rainwater in the gutters as if after a sudden storm."

                                                      Yep, definitely Constantinople...

                                                      The Concord of Ashes, my Dark Ages Vampire Chronicle
