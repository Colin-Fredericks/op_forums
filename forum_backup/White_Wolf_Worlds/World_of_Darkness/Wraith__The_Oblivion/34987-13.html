            What now? This is an odd question.. Well Spectres are "born" either when a truly wicked person dies and leaves an equally wicked Ghost, that ghost is the Spectre, or a normal human suffers an especially painful and horrific death and leaves a ghost behind. The third way is that a person dies and becomes a Wraith and in time his shadow slowly gains power and ends up usurping the shared Corpus permanently pushing the Psyche to be little whispering voice in the back of his head.

            So I guess yes humans create Spectres just as they create Wraiths, after all the main difference between the two ghosts is who is in control and who is just on for the ride Shadow or Psyche.
