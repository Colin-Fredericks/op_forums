                            Originally posted by VoidEng View Post

                            If you're talking about relic Artifacts, I'm not sure rules would be helpful. Creating one requires large numbers of living people believing that an object has special powers, over many years, before its destroyed. So that'd be more Storyteller's discretion (Is fifty thousand people over twenty years enough? A million, over several centuries?)

                            Making one "from scratch" would probably be the subject of an entire campaign (or possibly beyond the scope of one, barring time skips). You might get an interesting adventure, or two, out of the destruction phase of the Skinlands version (to create the relic). Any object with enough living believers to create an Artifact is probably going to have a number of wraiths who'll object (strongly) to your attempts to destroy it.

                                Relics don't usually power themselves or move on their own. Artifacts, however, do - but they've been fashioned that way.

                                I'm not sure there are many relics that cross over with mystical properties because of belief. Although, theoretically, it's in-keeping with canon, it'd be a very inefficient way to obtain artifacts.
