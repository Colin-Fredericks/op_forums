    Originally posted by adambeyoncelowe View Post
    My suspicion is that the 'million wraiths' thing refers to *every wraith the Giovanni have every caught*, rather than all those currently in captivity. Otherwise it's simply a lie (remember how the Crazy 88 didn't really have 88 members?). Then again, there are potentially billions of wraiths in the Underworld, so maybe they do. Maybe they're all drones animated by the will of the necromancer who summons them? That would make more sense.

    I've also always played the Giovanni as a tiny clan, in comparison to the others. They aren't numerous enough to cause most necropoli busy. And those wraiths they do enslave are presumably unable to become spectres or, if they can, are unable to cause havoc because they're bound.
    The one million number is the goal. They aren't there yet, and may not be for centuries. There was a time recently when they were starting to get close, but the Week of Nightmares set a lot of their captured wraiths loose and the Giovanni have been struggling to recoup the loss.

    In my view of Necromancers and deeper understanding of the underworld, those veteran and well-read Necromancers who actually know about Stygia and its workings also know enough to leave it alone. It benefits them far more to poach weaker vulnerable wraiths from the more easily accessible Shadowlands than to try and dig up some unwieldy deathless monster from the depths. I also think Stygia mostly turns a blind eye to Necromancers because they provide an inducement for wayward wraiths to seek safety in Stygia away from the Shadowlands.

    If the Giovanni were to try and launch a full scale Necromantic invasion of Stygia I think they could probably inflict a cataclysmic amount of damage, but not without significant casualties and cost to their resources, something which would leave them vulnerable to reprisal from both any Stygian remnants and from their innumerable vampiric rivals.

                    vs.

                    Charon (assuming HE isn't indisposed) + the Deathlords + the gaunts of Stygia + the rest of the Hierarchy + (pretty likely) the Ferrymen + (possibly) the Lady of Fate

                    You'd have to run some Wraith version of the Fermi Equation to figure out how many wraiths are in the Hierarchy, but it's probably likely a lot more than 1,000,000 + the non-elder Giovanni and whoever else (ghouls?) the Giovanni could bring. So numbers would edge to the the Hierarchy.

That leaves the Giovanni fighting on Sun Tzu's "dangerous ground". In addition, the logistical problems of supplying manpower and power-power from Stygia pale in comparison to the the difficulties of projecting power across the Shroud. The relative actual power of the "Big Guns" would probably depend on perspective. Maybe Augustus is equivalent to, say Charon, but he might be more like a younger Deathlord. (Unless he's actually just Capadocious's finger-puppet, which would change things). This is definitely the part where the pissing match comes into play.

                    All in all, the Giovanni look seriously outclassed if their objective is to overthrow the Hierarchy. It's like having Uruguay take on Brazil or Cyprus itch to get into a fight with the EU or the Nabateans trying to take down the Roman Empire.

                    The only hope the Giovanni have to cause more than "difficulties" are if the can manage some asymmetric warfare, or, even better, play to their strengths and make some deals with specters and Renegades and maybe another Dark Kingdom. The Hierarchy would be much more vulnerable to a Giovanni incursion if they were dealing with Giovanni-assisted Renegade and Heretic insurgents while trying to fend off a war with the Dark Kingdom of Jade and specters boiling out of random nihils and causing all sorts of problems. Particularly if they can make some REALLY good deals... a hektonahire coming up the Veinous Stair or taking the Ferrymen out by "painting" with some Necromancy to make them become easier targets for their Pasiphae, or something else(perhaps, even, Enoch...).

                                    Originally posted by LordOfCinder View Post
                                    What resources except for raw manpower and relic suicide bombers?
                                    You have to remember that the Hierarchy is the only supernatural organization that is large enough that they have an actual government, economy, and military forces. They just don't have large numbers, they have organization. They have specialists and other highly trained wraiths. If they have trouble in the Skinlands, they don't send some ignorant and half trained circle of wraiths who have been around a few years. They have the equivalents of special forces armed with artifacts supported by masters of Arcanoi that have trained for decades or perhaps centuries along with Barghests or legendary creatures.

                                    They are heavily organized because that's what the Hierarchy needs to be to survive Great Malestroms, hordes of spectres, and the other forces of Oblivion. Any organization that creates a system with Equitae, Doomslayers, Office of Maelstrong Preparedness, Institute for Obliviographic Studies, Magesterium Veritas, and the Online Legion as well as builds Citadels, Beacons, and Tempest Ports is an organization that is on the ball.

                                    Operating in the Skinlands is difficult, but masters of Embody, Puppetry, Outrage, Pandemonium, and other disciplines that exist solely to screw with people in the Skinlands can cause a lot of damage. A well timed use of Outrage or Inhabit can cause a car crash, or a house to burn down. A Giovanni's Allies, Contacts, or Retainers can be puppeted to eliminate them through death, scandal, or criminal activity. Determined wraiths can find out information and secrets and pass them on to enemies.

                                    In other words, it would not be hard to more or less destroy any Giovanni's influence, allies, or support, and also convince people to hunt them down and kill them.

                                    Too many times in discussions about supernatural creatures in the World of Darkness and who is "tougher", people only compare direct attacks. In actuality, almost no supernatural creature is going to go one on one versus the other. They are going to strike in indirect ways that build on their strengths and their enemies' weaknesses.

                                    I am not saying that this would be easy for the Hierarchy. They are only going to mobilize such assets if the threats are worth the cost. But if the Giovanni really were as dangerous as the Clanbook "propaganda" makes them out to be, then the Hierarchy would do so.

                                    Sure, the Giovanni could fight back, but it is going to cost them a lot to do so which will just weaken them against their enemies. It is one thing for an arrogant necromancer to contact a few spirits and torment them. It's another to engage in ongoing predatory behavior and enslavement of the dead willy nilly.

                                    It's like the real world. The community and police tolerate a certain level of crime, but once it gets out of hand or cops get shot, then the hammer comes down and people accept whatever is necessary to restore order.

                                    So I think the Giovanni work within limits that the Hierarchy can accept. Maybe there is some small payoff with Drones, moliated criminals, or captured Spectres, but on terms the Hierarchy can accept.

                                    Of course, individual Necropoli are different. Some have more resources and others less. Some have good leadership and others bad ones. So Giovanni in one city can get away with more than those in another. But even here if things get out of hand, then the bigger Necropoli or even Stygia sends the necessary reinforcements to restore order.

                                    In times of great crisis, like during Great Maelstroms, this may fall apart and the Giovanni take advantage, but I think those times are the exception and not the norm.

                                                    I'd argue the reason they haven't already reached a million wraiths for the Final Night ritual is several fold. First, they need to be able to actually keep those wraiths enslaved/imprisoned. There are well south of a million Giovanni, and while I cannot recall off the top of my head if there is a mechanical cap on the wraiths they can individually control it seems reasonable that some sort of limiting factor should exist. Thus, they need to gradually increase their Necromantic capacity to the point where they can actually coral a million wraiths. Second, they don't just need one million wraiths between everyone in the clan they need one million wraiths all in a big bin. Most clan members, even under orders the family's hierarchy, are not going to want to donate their personal retainers. Thus wraiths have to be captured specifically to be used in the eventual ritual, which brings us to the last point. If the Giovanni were to suddenly go on a snatching spree to fill up their pens in short order it would almost certainly attract the attention of the Hierarchy, which the elders of the clan know enough not to do. Vampires are immortal, they can wait.
