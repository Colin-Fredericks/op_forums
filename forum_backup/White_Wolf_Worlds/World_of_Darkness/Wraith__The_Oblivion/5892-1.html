                                                I'd like to second Heavy Arms. Wraith seriously needs a re-work of mechanics. It also really needs expanding of the other Kingdoms and the societies and death rituals of those kingdoms. Not everything can possibly be set up the same as the "Western Way". Just because Charon set things up that way doesn't mean it's been static for everyone all this time globally. The descriptions of history and culture of the other nations and wraiths are very Americanized. In trying to do research for making a Euthanatos Agama ritual, I found all KINDS of death rites and shadow culture in India I wish was in some of the Wraith books. China and Africa have very long histories of Ancestor worship too that would make profound impacts on the power struggles of the Shadowlands. Those Kingdoms would actually probably be much more dominant in the setting than any of the Wraith material would have us believe.

                                                There are rules for Ferrymen and Specters, but it could stand to be added to more.

                                                Blackwood: Yes, it is kinda broken. Very bleak. When you've had a recent death in the family IMPOSSIBLE to play and not be depressed as all hell and have to quit. But, that doesn't mean that we should let it remain that way. Now's the chance to make changes and improvements and flesh it out more into a world we can handle and play with hope without the annoying distractions and ladling the angst on with a trowel just to be true to the setting. Besides, if we don't support the making of Wraith20, why should Onyx bother making Changeling 20 or Hunter 20? Wraith is my least favorite classic 5 game of WW's because of all the holes in it. I'll gladly support the chance to make it something better.

                                                Back to what I'd like to see:


                                                Crossover rules. I for one like mixing up my games here and there. I try and completely understand each setting so that should a Mage embark on a Death journey, there's a world they go into, not just a subliminal holodeck in the Umbra. If Werewolves descend into the Spiral, why not have them pop out of the Black Labyrinth? If a Ferryman travels to the Far Shining Shores with his charges, might they not wind up in Faerie? If a Giovanni rips the Specter from a dying man, how might it act? What do the Legionaries do when the ephemeral prison for a Demon cracks? How does a Risen deal with a Hunter of Judgement when their target is the same person? I crave to have these options and some guidelines for adding more depth to my games. The world is so big, let's play in All of it, or at least have the option.

                                                Shadowlands is described in Werewolf as the Low umbra... it'd be nice to have some sort of reference back to the Rest of the Umbra or how Wraiths perceive spirits of other things, if they can perceive them at all. It might be out of their reach or even perception until they finally cast off all of their Fetters, but it seems to be that Wraith's setting for all its universal geography is very Very narrow. For some reason I keep thinking that the Shadowlands should be like Silent Hill or movies like The Others or Constantine's visions of Hell, and yet Wraith's material doesn't really speak to me of those visions. More spooky haunted house and quiet graveyards or battlefields of the dead. Not really visual to me until you get into descriptions of areas that might as well be knock offs of other descriptions like Malfeas or Blood Dimmed Tides.

                                                Atamajakki: How about artwork less John Cobb and more like Stephen Gammell? I personally think the Wraith artwork could have been better even when they did have Timbrook.

                                                How about for the heck of it as a stretch goal: A cardstock Ouija board to go with the storyteller's screen. Why not? Bad taste? Mage had its own Tarot Deck. even New Mage has a Tarot deck.

                                                    Ola All,

                                                    My Wish List

                                                    I. Structural Changes
                                                    - Rule Updates: Plain and simple, update the present rules to the newest version of the OWOD standard.

                                                    - Rule consolidation: I think the present Wraith rules are great. My party has always had a wonderful time with them. I would simply consolidate material from various books into the same chapter/setting. Examples would be rules for travel in the tempest and or naval warfare. We have material in the main book, Sea of Shadows, Dark Kingdom of Jade, Wraith the Great War, etc... for ship to ship combat and varied aspects of tempest travel. I would be nice to see them in one place. The same concept applies to the varied thorns for shadows and specter abilities.

                                                    - Translation guide for the NWOD (I run my present Wraith game in the NWOD and plain and simple, its a huge amount of fun. Its not even that difficult to translate over.


                                                    II. Material to Add
                                                    - Information on the Heretics (as the poster above mentioned, we have a hierarchy book, book of legions and renegade book. It would be great to get a little more color on the Heretics.

                                                    - Additional material on the remaining guilds that did not receive treatment in the guildbook series (Harbringers in particular).

                                                    - Expanded information on the Dark Kingdom of Ivory along with unique arcanoi/merits/relics/artifacts for Ivory Kingdom characters.

                                                    - More sample artifacts/relics and perhaps a set of creation guidelines.

                                                    - Ceremonies/rituals for wraiths to perform (similar to those in the Book of the Dead from the NWOD)

                                                    - Legion/hierarchy symbols and rank insignia (mask styles, Stygian and Shadowlands version of sigils of rank and office).

                                                    - Blood Potency/Primal Urge/Synergy style merit/background to differentiate old ghosts from new (I just call it "Psyche" and basically use a hybrid of the Blood Potency system from the NWOD and the Synergy merit from Geist (aka, a gaunt with 3 fetters can have up to 100 points of pathos and spend 15 a turn...is this common...no...but it differentiates a death lord from the average wraith on the street).

                                                    - Expanded color on the shining ones and/or far shores (I draw on the Book of the Dead for sample far shores). I like the concept of going "rivers deep" through the tempest to travel to particular far shores, etc.

                                                    - Random encounter table for the tempest/labyrinth.

                                                    - A little more rule info on dark steel weapons/armor.

                                                    - When it comes to extra bling, I would go for some type of oboli counters or maybe a Stygian seal of citizenship ring or other wax seal with the symbol of the hierarchy.

                                                    - A new sample necropolis and starter adventure. Even something along the lines of how the cities are treated in Wraith the Great War would be very helpful.

                                                    - Per The Gentlemen Gamer's interview with Richard Dansky, some sample adventure ideas/adventures that represent that different aspects of the Wraith world and how it can be played (high fantasy, personal horror/resolve fetters, light hearted/Beatlejuiceish, quest for transcendence, etc...)

                                                    III. Material to Omit
                                                    - I would hold off on Orpheus with the possible exception of importing some of their more unique specter variants into the antagonist section as example villains. I think there is quite a bit to unpack here and that it deserves a book of its own. Maybe a mention similar to the Orphic Circle or the Giovanni with some light rules for sleepers, etc. Shouldn't be too tough.

                                                    - I would go light on the crossover mechanics. About a quarter page should suffice like the NWOD. I love me some antagonists and mortal foes/necromancers are great, but I think going too far beyond the ones presented in the 2nd Edition main book would detract from the setting. Wraith is Wraith and I think the setting has such a staggering amount of variety and diversity already without getting too deeply embroiled in the affairs of the living. It should be fairly easy to cross it over as is and I think more rules for plasmics or specters would better serve the setting.

                                                    - I would say no Ouija board because they are more closely tied to mediums as opposed to wraiths themselves. I like the idea of going with oboli or a seal of citizenship as above (although dressed up death certificates like that off the old game would be great too).

                                                    Aeoris

                                                            The main things I think Wraith always needed were A) more things tying the PCs to each other (e.g., some group chargen system/mechanic ensuring, say, common passions and fetters), and B) more things explicitly tying PC motivations to plot hooks in the source books.

                                                            Even if all the players were good at coming up with fetters & passions that translated well to usable story ideas, it was easy to wind up with a list of many goals everyone wanted to tackle, very few of which the PCs shared in common, and most of which could only be approached through highly-demanding roleplay (which is great, but, well, highly demanding). Then the source books gave us tons of setting detail which, even when I could translate them into usable plot hooks, could still be hard to tie to the various personal motivations which are necessarily the center of a wraith's universe.

                                                            Directing players to a list of common fetters and/or passions (in a more or less draconian way, as far as the char gen mechanics go) may make it easier to publish material with plot hooks that are likely to hook them.
