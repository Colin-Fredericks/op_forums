    Question on Flux, Intimation, and Mnemosynis

    07-06-2017, 10:26 PM
    So, I know that the "Great 13" arcanoi carry with them specific visages that are displayed on the corpuses of the wraiths using them (e.g., Black eyes for those who know Argos), but are there also specific features associated with the three forbidden arcanoi: Flux, Intimation, and Mnemosynis?
    Tags: None

      Yes. According to the Wraith Players' Guide, which first introduced the three, Intimation users have their left eye turn a blazing, poisonous green, and Mnemosynis users never blink unless they choose to (yes, this does resemble Lifeweb's mark, and the Mnemoi take full advantage of it). Flux didn't get one, but Wr20 may change that.

              If it may help, this is in my knowledge the most comprehensive list of pre-w20 Arcanos markings, compiled from books and developer statements.

              Argos: Jet-black eyes
              Behest: Black lines running from hands to heart
              Castigate: Black-tinted fingers
              Connoissance: A physical mark of the Shadow
              Corruption: Subtle manners from the consort
              Displace: Unknown
              Embody: Dappled patterns on skin
              Fatalism: Symbols of fate on forehead and arms
              Flux: Unknown
              Hive-mind: Tumorous growths on the wraith's head
              Intimation: Left eye is bright green
              Inhabit: Red welts or burned-in traces of circuit board patterns
              Keening: Musical instrument
              Larceny: Corpus begins to resemble a jigsaw puzzle
              Lifeweb: Eyes never close
              Mnemosynis: Never blinks
              Moliate: Soft Corpus
              Outrage: Bulky, knotted muscles
              Pandemonium: Peculiar mannerisms, black cloak
              Phantasm: Gossamer clothing, dramatic bearing
              Puppetry: Accents and mannerisms from former hosts
              Tempest-Weaving: Small cuts and scars on hands
              Tempestos: Scratch marks on the Corpus
              Usury: Speaking in precise, numerical terms, set of scales
              Way of the Artisan: Long, thin fingers
              Way of the Farmer: Aura of menace, vestigial demonic attributes
              Way of the Merchant: Faint smell of incense, deceptively outgoing
              Way of the Scholar: Dignified stance, long fingernails
              Way of the Soul: Eyes glitter with supernal determination

              I hope a few of them get thoroughly revised by W20, since I find them unsatisfactory, especially the ones that are supposed to be represented by carrying objects (Keening, Pandemonium. Phantasm, Usury), since they seem to create annoying complications in their management. i.e. are those objects changes of the wraith's Corpus that take the form of tools or clothing, spontaneously manifesting relics, or regular soulforged or relice objects? What happens if these objects are stolen, lost, or destroyed? For that matter, what happens if a wraith uses Moliate to remove Arcanos markings? Are the markings immune to Corpus changes, or they come back after a while, and if the latter, how much?

                  Behest's is mentioned on p.163 of Wraith: The Great War, just before the Arcanos writeup - black lines running from hands to heart.
                  Connaissance's is a physical mark of the Shadow ("As a result of this intimate relationship, a Serviteur always displays a physical trademark of her Mait' Tete.", and "A non-Creole wraith with Connaissance also manifests an aspect of his Shadow, usually related to the Shadow's Archetype or strongest Dark Passion.", Shadow Players Guide, p. 63.).
                  Usury's is set to be modified in Wr20 - the Usurer's lips and fingertips are stained by life and death energies in perpetual motion.
                  Inhabit also gets modified in Wr20, with users developing marks reflecting the objects they possess.

                  Soulforging leaves its own mark on those who practice it in Wr20, its practitioners having some mark of the forges on them.
