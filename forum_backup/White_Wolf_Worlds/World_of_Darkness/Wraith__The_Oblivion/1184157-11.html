                                        Okay, so here is what I have seen in Book 2. As before, I suspect most of this has been mentioned elsewhere, but just in case, I'd hate to miss something, so apologies for any repetition.

                                        page 253 left column: "The players may portray the heroes of a given chronicle, but it's the Storyteller who plays the villains, the noble allies, the informants and bit parts, and all the rest of the world entire." = the phrase 'world entire' is a bit awkward in English. It might have been the goal, as it is a bit poetic, but it isn't natural.
                                        page 265 right column, under Wraith vs. Spectres: "The conflict can here can be as simple as Spectres wanting into a citadel during a Maelstrom while the wraith wants to keep them out." = the preposition 'into' is a bit awkward here. 'to get into' seems a bit less awkward perhaps
                                        page 267 right column: "Maybe one side brings in powerful allies, or change tactics from sneak attacks to full on assaults," = the single third person subject 'one side' needs the verb to be 'changes'. And the phrase 'full on' should probably be 'full-on' with a hyphen.
                                        page 268 left column, last sentence: "The Circle works happily for the Hierarchy as enforcers of the Dictum Mortuum, and its good at what it does." = In this case the word 'its' is the contraction for 'it is' and should have the apostrophe so that i reads 'it's' (I get this wrong more than I get it right so I hope I'm correct in this case)
                                        page 270 right column near the bottom: "You can also try "Protect my lover (Hope)," wherein the wraith wants to and helps the lover succeed..." = I suspect that this should be either 'the wraith wants to help the lover succeed' or 'the wraith helps the lover succeed' but as it stands I think it kind of merges the two into a muddle
                                        page 281 right column: Halfway thru the paragraph the Reaper pronouns changed from 'she' to 'he'. Likewise the Enfant's pronouns changed from 'her' to 'their'.
                                        page 282 right column at the top: same thing. The Reaper goes from being a 'her' to a 'their',
                                        page 283 left column at the bottom: "The empire employs specialists that aggressively work to distort or suppress evidence from such incidents,..." = More of a question. Since this is the Empire of Stygia, should 'empire' be capitalized?
                                        page 286 right column: "Maelstrom winds behave like the winds of Skinlands storms in terms of battering structures and turning loose debris into dangerous projectiles, but they are also pose far more insidious threats." = need to remove the word 'are' from this
                                        page 288 left column near the bottom: "A cemetery poisoned with disdain and empty beer bottles can make a far worse Haunt than the basement of an old row home where once upon a time, a small child was locked in the dark by her friends." = either the clause 'once upon a time' needs to be set off on both sides with commas, or not at all. So either this sentence needs a second comma after the word 'where' or it needs none. Kind of a matter of choice.
                                        page 297 left column: "Indeed, scholars of the phenomenon assert that trying to define exactly what Transcendence is a bit like trying to precisely qualify a notion like zen...." = the word 'is' is trying and failing to do double duty here. You could repeat the word 'is' twice, but that always looks awkward even if it is grammatically correct. Might I suggest simply dropping the words 'exactly what', and if you want to keep the word 'exactly ' then place it before the word 'define'
                                        page 298 right column: "Aspirants sometimes add their own thoughts and experiences to the text when making new copies, for one, not to mention discreetly alter or even remove passages they consider erroneous. " = For starters, I would say 'for example' instead of 'for one'. I would also make the words 'alter' and 'remove' into 'altering' and 'removing'.
                                        page 302 left column at the bottom: "Requirements found in individual sections below." = Except there is no Merit section below. I wish there was, as Wraith always had the coolest merits, but I get that for space they may not be able to be reprinted. Might I suggest that the word 'Merits' be removed if there will be no Merits section.
                                        page 303 right column: "Improving Physical Attributes or the Appearance Trait is very different for the Restless than for the living." = It's less awkward if you say 'than it is for the living.'
                                        page 308 left column: "Each success restores 1 Willpower point, up the character's normal maximum." = should be 'up to the character's normal maximum'
                                        page 308 left column: "That doesn't mean it doesn't happen, of course, but it does mean an attacker tends to work as indirectly as possible in order to make it hard to trace back her." = Probably best as 'back to her'.
                                        page 308 sidebar: "This rule should not reduce a Fetter rating to zero - even if looks completely different,...." = should read 'even if it looks completely different'
                                        page 311 left column: "Referred to as Slumber, this state is a first and foremost a restorative one for an injured wraith." = There's an extra article in this. 'this state is first and foremost'. Drop the 'a' before 'first'
                                        page 320 left column: Under Lift/Break the phrase "Strengthtable" needs a space. Also the reference to the die roll seems unusual. Maybe 'dice roll'? Maybe you meant it to refer to a single die, although most people roll dice as apposed to a die for strength,
                                        page 323 right column near the bottom: "...attempts to track the Restless or Spectres without benefit of an appropriate Arcanoi starts at difficulty 8." = should read 'Arcanos' or remove the word 'an'

                                        I have a few other concerns on system that I'll leave below that are not typos.
