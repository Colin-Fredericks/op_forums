                            1)A member of Orpheus Group who has crossed over into the Land of the Dead is already there, and so interacting with that person does not cross the shroud.
                            2)If you follow this by spirit, then a member of Orpheus Group who is in the Shadowlands should not be spoken with at all. One could argue, however, that a member of Orpheus Group who is in the shadowlands has "passed over" and thus can be interacted with freely.
                            3)Note the word "in" the Land of the Quick. not "of".
                            4)Again, the use of the word "in". Also can be taken as a forbiddance against helping Orpheus Group to cross over into the Shadowlands...
                            The interaction between the Dictum Mortuum is something Rich and I discussed a lot while I was writing my chapter, because even a charitable reading of the Dictum is pretty clear about interactions with Orpheus agents being a violation. The big saving grace to a tolerant Anachreon is that Charon instituted the Dictum to stop the dead from gaining power from and over the living. He never seriously expected the living to come to the lands of the dead. Orpheus' existence is largely without precedent.

                            It violates the letter of the law, but whether it violates the spirit - or whether the spirit should even matter in a world with the Orpheus Group - is up for debate, and thus grist for the drama mill.

                            Neall Raemonn Price
