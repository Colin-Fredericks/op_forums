    Creating new Arcanoi

    08-20-2015, 01:41 AM
    --UPDATED--

    This took a while to make. I proofread it a couple times but there might still be some errors. I'm fairly pleased with it. It occurred to me that there was no Arcanos dealing with mirrors or reflections, with only one example I could find that was a tempered Arcanos with only one application. Much of my inspiration comes from sources like Poltergeist. I'm sure many will notice what I mean. Hope you all like it. Feel free to criticize, offer suggestions, compliments, use it, modify it, whatever. Enjoy!


    Arcanos name: Speculos
    User nickname: Mimics
    Arcanos marking: The wraith's eyes become highly reflective and parts of her corpus appear cracked as if made from glass.


    A bizarre Arcanos that had seen far less use compared to most others until sometime after the fifth Great Maelstrom. Those that know the art may enter a reversed Skinlands or Shadowlands in an attempt to terrify the Quick or hide from enemy Wraiths and Spectres. For this fact alone has caused some to speculate that it began as a tempered Arcanos mixing Argos with Inhabit. In some ways it would appear to be true, as the Mimic is essentially "possessing" the mirror or water, but simultaneously has crossed into a different yet paralleled location. Others have drawn comparisons to Lifeweb, Embody, and especially Pandemonium for other reasons. One fear the Hierarchy is loathed to admit to is that it was a merging of several Arcanoi into one brand new Arcanos, meaning that the original Mimic was highly learned in several Wraithly powers and could still be around in the Underworld somewhere.

    Whatever the case, a Wraith using Speculos will find that while they are granted many interesting and useful skills and opportunities, their ability to roam far from a mirrored plane's point of origin (the mirror itself) is limited. A Wraith would be unable to go far from the public restroom from within the reflection, finding a strange, eerie distorted world that darkens the further they leave the mirror's location. Some Mimics theorize that this "dark zone" is actually connected with Oblivion, and many whisper of a few unfortunate Wraiths that roamed into this void to never be heard from again.

    Should a reflective surface be heavily damaged or permanently rendered disrupted in a profound way while "possessed" by a Mimic, the Wraith will take appropriate levels of Corpus damage. They may roll Dexterity + Wits to escape unscathed. Difficulty is at 8, but they may lower the difficulty by 1 per every point in Dodge they possess.

    Botching this roll could have serious consequences, such as points in temporary Corpus being lost (one point for every 1 rolled) or thrown straight into a Harrowing if the Wraith failed in a particularly spectacular way. Becoming trapped in the mirror for a number of turns or an entire scene is entirely plausible. Discuss this with your ST and work out a fair penalty.

    Basic Abilities

    Detect Reflection: The Mimic is able to detect if the mirror or other reflective surface is being used by another Wraith or has been recently or regularly. Roll Perception + Speculos against a difficulty of 7.

    Copycat: A wraith may enter any mirror or reflective surface, no matter how big or small. The player rolls Dexterity + Speculos at the object's difficulty. The size and clarity of the reflection will determine how easy it is, with small puddles and glasses being a difficulty of 9 and large mirrors that cover an entire wall to a ballroom a difficulty of 4. As a general rule, water is more difficult than a mirror, but still useable. The ST should generally have the final say on what the difficulty is for all reflections, particularly distorted or constantly shifting ones. Botching the roll results in a point of temporary Corpus being lost as the Wraith "bumps" into the reflection clumsily. A wraith inside the mirror is invisible to mortals, but wraiths may see her regardless. In order to hide from other restless, a Mimic must use Ditto.

    Ditto: Once inside the mirror, the wraith can "assimilate" an object, animal, or person that is currently in view. The object or being must have been in the mirror for at least 5-10 seconds to be mimicked. The wraith is completely unseen while using Ditto, but will remain immobile (or fixed to a living target's own movements), completely merged and effectively the object/person's own reflection. No roll or Pathos is required. This art lasts until the wraith ceases or the imitated object is heavily damaged/altered or leaves the vicinity of the reflection. Being unwillingly ejected from a reflection costs one point in temporary corpus.

    Knock, Knock: The wraith exerts her strength onto the mirror's hard surface in order to draw attention to it. She may "knock" as many times as she has dots in Strength. Alternatively, if in water she may cause bubbles to form and pop at the surface. This art costs nothing and requires no roll, but can only be used once per scene and should be executed at just the right moment, otherwise the knocking falls upon deaf ears.


    Primary Powers

    *Malimagery: The Mimic plays simple visual tricks on the Quick, with their reflection becoming the key ingredient in this spectacular yet horrifying display. The wrong eye might close or when the mortal smiles to brush his teeth his reflection sticks out his tongue instead. More potent results might involve bleeding eyes, suicide or tearing off the mortal's face. All effects are brief (ten seconds or less) and cause no physical damage. The Mimic must use Ditto on their intended target before using this Arcanos art, meaning no chance in gripping their attention if they are quickly passing through. Roll Manipulation + Speculos against the target's willpower. Each success counts as one second for the illusion to last. If 1-2 successes, the player should think of an appropriate but subtle effect. If 3 or more, a more detailed and elaborate illusion may be described in order to horrify or warn the Quick. The wraith may spend one willpower to transfix the mortal on the macabre show, preventing him from fleeing until it is finished. Though no actual sounds originate from the mirror, the illusion may be so convincing to the mortal in question that they might hallucinate appropriate and expected sounds, say from a gun firing or flesh tearing. The wraith cannot convey any specific sound or vocalize any words through this art — all "sounds" are purely subjective and hallucinatory on the victim's part. This art costs one Pathos to activate and lasts until the mortal is no longer within the reflection's range. Rerolls are required to cause new illusions. A botch will deactivate the art for the remainder of the scene.

    **Abracadavera: The Mimic is now able to make herself visible to the Quick, allowing for silent communication and more vivid displays in the mirror. They are free to move around within the mirror's reflection, but cannot directly move/alter reflected objects or the living. They simply cannot muster any strength to move the reflected objects. Should they be using Ditto on any person, that reflection will now appear as the wraith does, perfectly mimicking any movements the mortal makes. They may activate Malimagery at the normal cost and combine both arts at any time (whether using Ditto or not) for more frights. Roll Manipulation + Speculos with difficulty at local shroud rating. Using Malimagery on herself while visible to the Quick grants a decrease in difficulty by -2 (but can never bring the difficulty below 4). This art costs two Pathos to activate and lasts for a scene. A botch cracks the mirror and adds 1 to the difficulty for all future rolls on that mirror.

    ***Specular: This art allows the Mimic to manipulate the mirrored counterparts to real world objects. The objects they may interact with are limited to the reverse world's range, but within it they are free to do as they please with all mirrored objects and beings. They may touch, grapple, manipulate, and attack what they desire. This allows a wraith to "play" with all reflected objects, stacking mirrored books, moving chairs, even turning off the reflection of a lightbulb. A creative Mimic will have many ways to make a mortal spin around in horror, such as combining this art with Abracadavera, opening a door and then stepping in while holding a weapon. They may also combine this art with Ditto and Malimagery to appear as a warped, distorted reflection of any object. Appearing as a humanoid chair, fan made of body parts, or even as a stretched out bed. The wraith spends 2 Pathos and rolls Dexterity + Speculos to activate this art (difficulty of local Shroud). Each success counts as a turn while a critical success (5+) lasts for an entire scene. Botching the roll will make a crack in the mirror, adding 1 point in difficulty to all future rolls on that mirror. All changes to reflected objects will remain in effect until the wraith leaves the mirror, meaning something like a mannequin could be long gone but persist in the reflection for hours should the Mimic stay put.

    ****Ersatz: With this art, a wraith may reach out a short distance from the mirror and plant a mirrored copy of a small object (generally no bigger than a breadbox — clear it with your ST if it is any larger) within the real world. The player spends a number of Corpus points it wants to grant to this object, spends two Pathos, rolls Dexterity + Speculos against the difficulty of local Shroud and reaches through the mirror (provided it is big enough for her hand). This act requires concentration and no sudden jerky movements, preventing the wraith from striking things in the Skinlands. An object put into the Skinlands serves as a vessel which the Mimic may use other Arcanoi through and see the surrounding area with at will. Only one vessel may be spawned and a wraith may not slumber in it (it is NOT a fetter). The object will also appear reversed, so care must be taken not to alert mortals to its otherworldly origin. A botch will result in the mirror becoming cracked and increasing all future rolls through that mirror by 1. This mirrored object will last so long as it is not damaged, and for as many days as there were points of Corpus invested in it. After the final day passes, the ST shall roll the Mimic's Willpower vs. the local shroud rating. Once the "saving roll" fails, the vessel will shatter like glass and turn into dust, and the Mimic will receive one point of Angst. Should the vessel be destroyed prematurely, this grants an additional point.

    ****Shatter: By summoning all her emotion and rage, a wraith may deliberately cause a mirror to break. This is not a simple crack, but a literal and vicious explosion of glass (or water) that may cause harm to the living (ST should decide what is appropriate damage levels for this art, depending on various circumstances). No roll is needed, they must simply pay the price for using it. This art costs three points of Pathos to use, and as this is a violent act, it grants one point of Angst to use + one more point for every mortal it injures (two points, should a mortal die). Finally, as this is a willful destruction of the reflective surface, the wraith must roll to escape unharmed or suffer appropriate Corpus damage.

    *****Doppelganger: Reaching from beyond her mirror's surface, a Mimic may now step outside into the living world as a doppelganger of some person she was actively using Ditto on, if the aforementioned mirror was large enough for an entire human to crawl out of (a large TV set, for example, will do). She sounds and appears 100% identical to the mortal in question, save being physically reversed in appearance. Right-handed mortals spawn left-handed copies and vice versa, offering another clue to the wraith's deception. This can be ignored for a minute at the cost of a Willpower point, but they will inevitably slip back into "mirrored" behavior should it apply. While the wraith is living, she suffers one other drawback; her reflection in all reflective surfaces will show the wraith as they really are. Not even previous Moliate attempts can conceal what the mirror reveals. The Mimic spends three Pathos and one point of Willpower, then rolls Charisma + Speculos with difficulty set at the local Shroud + 3. Botching the roll grants a point of Angst as using this art requires concentration and great will to accomplish. Other Arcanoi may be used (some like Embody, Argos, Puppetry, etc. cannot be for obvious reasons), but the Mimic must have an Ersatz vessel with them, otherwise they are 100% powerless and completely "mortal". This art lasts until either they lose all temporary Corpus or the doppelganger returns to the mirrored reality. Losing all Corpus results in a Harrowing. As the mortal must be present when this art is executed and they may not always be a willing participant, the wraith will have to deal with this witness to their ghostly shenanigans... somehow.

    *****Danse Macabre: As an alternative art to Doppelganger, a Mimic may briefly enter the living world as they really are, displaying all their wraithly glory. Their physical senses are deadened, their social attributes are 0, and they may only manifest for turns equal to their temporary Corpus rating. What they lack in negotiation and seduction skills, they gain in sheer fright and destruction. Their physical and mental stats remain the same and they feel no pain, but will shatter into glass upon the loss of their last Corpus point. Should they perish this way, they reappear in the Shadowlands with one point of temporary Corpus and a point of Angst for their trouble. To activate, the wraith locates a reflective surface large enough for their body to exit through and the player rolls Stamina + Speculos with difficulty set at the local Shroud rating. This art costs 3 points of Pathos and grants 1 point of Angst. A botch fractures the mirror and increases the difficulty of all future rolls by +2. Alternatively, if attempting to summon herself from water, a botch will cause the wraith to become confused from the rippling reflection and be unable to use the art for the remaining scene. Some Mimics have shown such prowess with this art that they could remain manifested for several minutes at a time. 5+ successes grants a bonus in time limit at the ST's discretion.

    And there you have it! I will eventually add on with some tempered Arcanos abilities using Speculos, but for now, I want to know what everyone thinks of the revised abilities.

      I like the general idea, but this feels a bit overpowered as written. A few descriptions aren't always clear, either, so could be rewritten.

      For example, physically pulling human beings into mirrors (if I'm reading it right) with level 5 is something we haven't really seen in Wraith. Although it's something that happens in Poltergeist 3, I feel it really should be a tempered Arcanoi. With level 5 you already get the equivalent of Embody 5 bundled in with Moliate 1. The power seems strong enough without trapping humans too.

      You could do with giving more info about how long each power lasts. Some descriptions have this, and others don't.

      The 'Ersatz fetter' might be best called a vessel to avoid confusion. I had to think twice about how it's a 'sort of' fetter, and it's really only one because a wraith can inhabit it. But fetter means something very specific, so I'd just use a different word altogether.

      Also, how does a wraith effect change in the Skinlands with Level 3? I know you kind of explain this, but I think examples would be useful. I assume the Mimic has to physically make the change or effect they'd like to create, and that the Arcanos rating replaces the normal Ability rating in such a roll, with success determining how well such an effect translates? Maybe give some more non-combat examples (move the subject's hat in the mirror, and their hat will move in the Skinlands).

      Levels 1 and 2 feel like step powers. Can you differentiate them a tad more so that Level 2 doesn't make Level 1 useless? The only real benefit of Level 1 is you don't get the Angst. I'm guessing the real benefit of Level 2 is the ability to transfix someone. With Level 1, it's the ability to confuse them, which isn't quite different enough. Perhaps roll Levels 1 and 2 together and make a Level 2 power that does something entirely different (say, communicating with someone via sign language and complex images in the mirror instead of merely showing them a brief image).

          Thanks for the feedback! I'll spread the responses apart.

          1: The part with trapping them I would be fine with making a tempered Arcanos. Perhaps with some sort of application from Argos? The mirror/reflective surface operating as a quasi-Nihil into the mirrored reality while the Level 5 ability is simply becoming the doppelganger in question? Moliate + Effigy to appear as the person, moving the mirror manifestation of the wraith from Level 4 to Level 5, and just leaving Ersatz as a way to summon a mirrored object only?

          2: Which powers were unclear about how long they last? I admit that I had been working on it for quite a while and it had been a long day, so I rushed with some of the descriptions after a certain point. I figured I would have to touch up something at some point.

          3: I agree. Vessel sounds like a good alternative word to describe it. I'll fix that one right away.

          4: I hadn't actually thought of the Arcanos rating modifying the Ability rating, but that sounds interesting. Good idea. As for examples, your hat example is a good one. Other examples would be like picking up an ashtray and throwing it across a room, readjusting furniture, flicking light switches. Poltergeist 3 was indeed one of my primary inspirations and now I am thinking over something that happened often in that film; the things that were moved/attacked/manipulated often only did so within the mirror, but still had an impact on the real physical world. Perhaps the number of successes affects how deeply it works. Like just one or two successes means that you moved the reflection (which would cause some confusion on the mortal's part) but higher successes would actually make the real world object/person be affected by the art? I kind of like that better because it helps distinguish this Arcanos from Outrage even more. Outrage gives you free reign to use it anywhere in the Skinlands and with a near 100% guarantee that something you hit or touch will be hit or touched.

          5: Combining Levels 1 and 2 would be fine, perhaps with the number of successes influencing how vivid, real, and trapped a mortal will be to witness the intended affect. A single success means it's a "blink and you'll miss it" kinda thing, while five or more successes forces the target to witness whatever was the intended vision in the mirror. As a replacement art for 2, I could definitely see a sort of silent manifestation being there. I do like that quite a bit.

          Let me know what you think and I'll get around to making some of these changes sooner or later. Again, thanks for the reply!
