    Originally posted by Possessed View Post
    I think Matt is referring to a Mage source-books, Book of Madness and Book of Madness Revised specifically, that tell us that the Outer Beings revered by the K'llashaa faction of the Nephandi are actually mere dreams of the Neverborn.
    When that retcon came down the pike (beginning in Book of Mirrors), it wasn't restricted to the things worshipped by the K'llashaa. It was said to apply to ALL the entities the Nephandi served. So, werewolves and mages were deluded about the nature of Maeljin Incarnae, Cthulhu Mythos creatures, demons, and the evil gods worshipped by dozens of human religions - they were all explained away as made-up hallucinations somehow projected into the Astral, Middle, and Deep Umbrae by Dark Umbral presences that should be even less able to interact with the worlds of the living than spectres. And I guess angels, spirits of Gaia and the rest of the Triat, and divine figures from those same human religions were also all completely ignorant of the nature of enemy figures from their various belief systems.

    Mind you, I don't object to the Neverborn as presented within the context of the Wraith game; it's when they get exported into Mage (and by implication Werewolf) as replacements for the existing mythologies of those games that I cry foul.

            IANRDG, but the Werewolf Storyteller's Handbook covers the first question and hints heavily at the second. Specters appear to Garou and Fera as like unto banes and always ping on SW. Wraiths don't, unless their Angst is higher than their Willpower. There's no mention made of Catharsis affecting this, but it might. As for soulforging, Garou are partially composed of ephemera rather than plasm, so it won't work on them any more than Moliate would*, most likely. Likewise, Middle Umbral spirits wouldn't be viable soulforging material, either.

            (Also, Garou and Fera don't melt into spirit form, they turn inside out. They're still meatbags at heart while in the Umbra.)

            *Moliate explicitly does not work on Garou and Fera.

                                I think it is important to point out the purpose of a fetter: It is something that ties a wraith to the lands of the living. This thing is important to the wraith in some way, that the wraith has difficulty leaving behind. Once that thing is gone, it no longer keeps the wraith tied to the Skinlands. A family heriloom melted down into silver is no longer an heirloom. A favorite tree chopped down and the surrounding area turned into a parking lot is no longer the place it was. By the same token, the corpse of a person is not the person any longer... the person is gone. There is nobody to stick around for. No heirloom to protect. No tree to rest beneath the shade of. It's gone.

                                In many ways I think most domems don't miss the ability to visit the shadowlands. After all, everything that kept them there in the first place is gone.
