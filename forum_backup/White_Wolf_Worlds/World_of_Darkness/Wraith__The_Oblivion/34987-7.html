                                                Let me see if I can help, since I'm re-reading all the Wraith material right now...

                                                NOTE: I don't work for Onyx Path, and I am not involved in any of the Wr20 stuff. Source material/Deadguy (the ultimate source) trumps anything I might be wrong about, of course. This is just musings from another fan.

                                                PaladinDemo - both, if I understand your question correctly. Using your example, collecting the dolls is the Passion, and the dolls themselves are the Fetter. Fetters are the physical objects, while Passions are the emotions regarding them.

                                                Crowley - I recall something from Mediums about Ouija boards and the like, you might want to check that out. I haven't hit that one in my (re-read) list yet.

                                                cryocube - Spectre or some sort of Unseelie Fae, definitely. The last episode of Supernatural had Slenderman as a thing (I haven't seen it yet. Have you? It sounded pretty awesome.). As to the rest of your statement, I don't work for OP, so I don't know.

                                                atamajakki - I agree, but there's systematic problems with it. As a background, Eidolon is optional (though you'd be crazy not to take it). Perhaps a more detailed explanation (and process) of Transcendence is in order?

                                                Khaleesi - Vulpines are basically made in the same manner as barghasts, so I would assume so (being Moliated Spectres and all). As to marriage... I'm sure some of the Far Shores practice it, but I can't imagine Stygia endorsing such a thing. At least, not with Charon gone.

                                                Aeoris - I can't think of a way to "buy off" Thorns, but there are ways to (permanently) reduce a Shadow's Angst, which would affect how often they could use them.

                                                urbanfractal - I can't speak as to the bulk of your post, but if any vampires made it to the Underworld, yes, I would expect them to continue the Jyhad.

                                                Katrina - Thorns can be purchased with experience, though Merits are usually Storyteller fiat, springing out of plot.

                                                Again, this is my opinion. Do not take anything I say as written in stone, I'm just trying to help people out.

                                                Cheers!

                                                If you don't use an Oxford comma, I feel bad for you, son,
                                                'Cuz I got ninety-nine problems, but clarity ain't one.

                                                        Whoops.

                                                        Big apologies for the long absence, folks. Part of that was due to a very bad week spent wrestling with norovirus - lots of Harrowing inspiration coming from that one, believe me - and part of that was me desperately trying to pick up pieces of everything that fell behind due to norovirus.

                                                        But, I'm back, and will be digging into the question backlog forthwith. I thank you for your patience, and hopefully this won't happen again.

                                                        Marriage - Avoiding the obvious Peter Cook/Princess Bride jokes, I firmly believe in the concept of marriage for wraiths. Leaving aside questions of consummation, the grounds certainly exist for emotional attachment between two dead folks, and people - alive or dead - often formalize behaviors with rituals. So I can certainly see wraiths falling in love and wanting to share their existence, and I can see them wanting to formalize their arrangement and celebrate doing so. Whether wraiths could develop Passions related to their spouse, well, that's an area for exploration. And I can certainly imagine Shadows making a normal domestic squabble worse...

                                                        That being said, I regard most episodes of "Say Yes To The Dress" as my own personal Harrowing, so I'm making an executive decision and banning that sort of thing from the Underworld forthwith.


                                                        Shadow stuff - this is a little bit of a spoiler, but here goes. We will not be making huge changes to basic Shadowplay in 20th edition. We are, however, going to be exploring and providing alternate methods of play for folks, and we're looking to deepen the RPing experience of Shadowguiding. Shadowguiding is one of my favorite aspects of Wraith and I don't want to do anything to take away from it; at the same time, it's also potentially difficult to find a good "level" for it within a group, it demands a certain level of trust between players that can be hard to come by, and you want to stay away from trigger-y badness. So there will be more on playing Shadows, and some rules variants to make sure the experience is as accessible and enjoyable as possible.

                                                        Deadguy out
