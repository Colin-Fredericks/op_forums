    Risen vs Vampires (As in what's the better "Undead" to play as?)

    01-16-2015, 01:28 AM
    Hello everyone, I got a question for everyone I've been wondering about recently...

    What Undead splat do you prefer playing as? Vampire or Risen and why?

    Since apparently, it seems Risen have the upper hand because they don't have numerous weaknesses Vampires have such as the beast, can walk in sunlight, don't have Jyhad politics nor are they shoehorned into inherit backstabbers/usurpers (which is also part of the Vampire curse), and even though they don't even require blood either they need patheos to fuel their powers instead and can even learn vampire disciplines as well. Although I also wonder how different playing as a Risen than a Vampire and if anyone enjoys playing as one more than the other...

    and lastly the biggest advantage is that Risen don't have to worry about the effects of Gehenna such as the Withering and such.

    Thoughts on the matter?

      They are two completely different beasts tho they do often, due to the efforts of the Puppeteers Guild, gravitate towards the same circles. I'd say it's a matter of taste and character concept, some concepts work better as Vampires others as Risen.

      Do note that while lacking the more commonly known Vampiric weaknesses they do have some of their own.
      Vampires have such as the beast
      And Risen have Shadows who are usually somewhat empowered by the experience of Rising and the greater ease to follow up on their Dark Passions on the Skinlands. In comparison the Beast is generally considerably smaller issue.

      can walk in sunlight
      But are still more dead than Vampires, no way to mimic life and such. But it is nice to feel the sun again.

      don't have Jyhad politics
      If they take the Guilds advice and masquerade as Vampires they kinda are drawn into the Jyhad politics by default. But just like Vampires they can try to avoid it by staying away from other Undead.

      nor are they shoehorned into inherit backstabbers/usurpers
      But thanks to their Shadows they are extremely unpredictable and likely to end up backstabbing someone/everyone at some point.

      even though they don't even require blood either they need patheos to fuel their powers
      True, and this does make it a bit easier on them as they do not need to attack humans to refuel their batteries but only to follow their Passions and be around people with strong emotions. But this is also a huge thematic difference between them and Vampires as they are seemingly less harmful parasites than the Vampires and it makes it easier for them to hide from hunters, htough them being a bit deader than Vampires and unable to mimic life to such extents tends to make up for that particular difference.

      can even learn vampire disciplines as well
      Only the three physicals and Obfuscate though and while similar to disciplines they technically aren't quite them but close enough to teach them crosssplats anyhow.

      But all in all it is quite a different experience due to Risen being dominated by their Passions and having things they do need to protect, Fetters and Conduit, and the difference in parasitism between them and Vampires. I kinda lean towards Risen, but that is because Wraith is my favourite line, but still I stay with my earlier opinion that it is largely concept driven choice.

                          In regards to the 'backstabbing' requirement, neither game outright DEMANDS it, as a matter of fact. Sure, it's a quick path of drama, but there's nothing that says the Beast forces vampires to eat each other. The Beast winds up being the excuse that many Kindred use for following their worst urges, just as you or I may say, 'the Devil made me do it.' *

                          The thing I see as the biggest difference between the two isn't mechanical, but rather it is one of society. Even if you choose to say that the Sixth Great Maelstrom booted huge numbers of ghosts into the Skinlands to become Risen, they won't find any kind of structured social system for their on this side of the Shroud no matter how many appear. The vampires, on the other hand, have had millennia to build their society of Clans and Sects and the Jyhad and whathaveyou. The Risen may either cling to the social structures of the Underworld or try to blend in with humanity (or the vampires as the case may be), but they don't have anything to call their own beyond that of a very small scale. Perhaps a gang or ad hoc familial group of Risen come together, but they never quite fit into any place of their own.

                          ...As stated above, it really depends on what you want of course. I tend to see the Risen as having a necessarily limited time in the world, as they are driven by Passions and goaded by the Shadow, it's an existence that leads to ruin – or drama, if you like. Please don't take that as how it must be played, though, as mileage always and ever varies, and it's your game.

                          * Not to presume what any of you might ACTUALLY say.
