        Hello Rich,

        Long time Wraith fan. I started in 1996. Helped me work through a lot of personal issues about my own emotional issues during being a teenager. So simply, thank you for that. The game will always be vastly important to me and is why I own a physical copy of nearly every Wraith book printed (a couple city books and Quick and the Dead slipped my grasp).

        My favorite book, was Midnight Express (next to Shoah...cause...whoa man. I just don't count black dog books with the main lines). I loved how it showed how to do a cinematic style of game which is what lead me to the bag of ideas I've lugged around for 17 years for games of a more ethereal quality.

        I love anything about the Tempest and the Labyrinth as wild teeming lands. I added that in some areas they bleed into The Dreaming or the various Umbral layers, etc. This allowed for me to tell stories which brought multiple supernaturals into contact, forcing them to come to terms with co-existence.

        My least Favorite bit was how mature shadow guiding needs people to be. Eventually, the few times I convinced people to let me run, I ended up running all shadows. It was hard at first but eventually I got a hang for it.

        I am most fascinated by how Wraith relates to other settings (Euthantos, Giovanni, etc) and how the wraith setting could continue forward post Time of Judgement.



        Now that I have fawned over you and Wraith, time for a few questions:
        1) What is your favorite Ghost Story?
        2) When developing the Shadow Players Guide (if I remember correctly) how hard was it with the Creole Arcanoi to avoid the topics of Zombism so much?
        3) Would you consider in Wraith20 including Zombism and/or Risen in the core book (ala W20 having Changing Breeds) or even expanding to wraiths of the various kingdoms or even spectres?
        4) Is there going to be a KS reward that would possibly include the backer getting involved with some of the design or is the plan to have the book largely written by the KS? (planning on throwing stupid money at this so always good to plan ahead )
        5) Monitors seemed like perhaps they might have been the biggest bad of the Guilds due to Lifeweb. Were they intended this way from early on?
        6) In your mind, how much interaction would a standard vanilla wraith game have with the Skinlands? I know there is variation but I like to know what was default during conception.
        7) How did you feel when Wraith was ended? Was is conflicted? Did you agree but regret? etc. I don't mean to open old wounds but it seemed so sudden to me (I was young and not as forward thinking at the time so there were likely signs) that I am curious if it was a surprise. I remember the forward to End of Empires feeling like it was trying to be put into words. Thank you for trying to do as much as was done with that book. It did make me feel that even thought my favorite was being put down, that I was not thought less of by the team working on it for liking it.

        Thanks in advance!

                        Basically a conduit is a special fetter created during the creation of a Risen. It is usually one of the wraith's already existing fetters and during the Risen creation process the wraith's Shadow enters the fetter, though it is possible for Psyche to forced in the conduit instead giving the Shadow the Risen's body*. The conduit serves a connection between the worlds of the living and the dead and the Risen needs to come in physical contact with on a regular basis or else its body will start rotting away. If it's destroyed will guarantee the end of that Risen and that will not return but not right away as their body will rot away over the course of hours before finally ceasing to function. But if the Risen's body is destroyed without the destruction of the conduit, the item retains its power and are desired by necromancers and others with similar powers/connections to the restless dead.

                        *Though as much as the Shadow would like to destroy the Conduit and Psyche with it while it's in the body it cannot.
