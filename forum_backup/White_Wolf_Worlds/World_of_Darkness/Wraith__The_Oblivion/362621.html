    The River of Death and other Byways: connection with the Shadowlands

    01-07-2015, 01:28 PM
    If I got things correctly, the River of Death and other well-kept Byways once led directly to the Shadowlands, but since the 3rd Great Maelstrom they have been disconnected from it.

    But how did they reach the Shadowlands before? There was a time before the Tempest came to be, and it was called the Darkness, when it was simply continuous with the Shadowlands, instead of "under" it? How did you reach the Darkness then, besides using a Nihil? Could you walk to the "end" of the Shadowlands (maybe to a remote area, a cave or the ocean, or a river), and eventually you would be on the River of Death, or some Byway, that then took you further into the Underworld?

    Or I got it wrong and these were never connected to the Shadowlands? Stygia uses Tempest Portals; were these always the standard form of access to the Tempest, especially at a time when Nihils were supposed to be more rare?

    And at the delta of the River of Death lies Stygia, but does it have a source? Does the River of Death "begin" at some point? What would happen if one would travel it upstream?

    I know many of these questions have never been answered in the Books (yet, the Book of Oblivion wil hopefully shed some light on these). But what do you think or have come up with on your games?

          The following response is “to my understanding:”

          Originally posted by valismedsen View Post
          But how did they reach the Shadowlands before? There was a time before the Tempest came to be, and it was called the Darkness, when it was simply continuous with the Shadowlands, instead of "under" it?
          Not quite. In this era, the Darkness and the Shadowlands were still separate. In fact, some parts of the Darkness were more distinguishable than they are now. Before the Tempest, the Sea of Shadows (the area around the Labyrinth), the Sunless Sea (between Stygia and the Far Shores), and the Sea of Souls (the area of the Sunless Sea that surrounds Stygia) had more definition between each other. After the Third Great Maelstrom, the “weather” became a permanent and horrible storm; in the modern era, it’s not inaccurate to simply refer to all three areas as “The Tempest.”

          You reached the Darkness then the same way you reach the Tempest now: Byways and Channels. Byways/Channels lead through the different levels of the Underworld, including the Shadowlands. The River of Death is the Grand High Poobah of Byways. Where it starts, I have no idea…

                  Byways that exist in the Shadowlands can take intrepid Wraiths further into the Underworld. Some are marked by Harbingers and others or not.                       Hi guys. Sorry in advance for my level of English, but I couldn't resist to join the conversation and give you my two oboli.
                      Everything stated below is just my personal interpretation. But before I start, note that like everybody else I'm talking to my understanding, which is in my opinion already an answer to your question: there may be NO definite answer...

                      Your question leads somehow to the geography of the underworld, where does the Tempest starts, how do the Byways/Channels appear when they reach the Shadowlands, etc.
                      In the universe of WtO, like in other universes set in the 'world of the deads', the geopgraphy is volontarily 'not understandable' from human standards. A line I found in the WtO novel 'Caravan of Shadows' (page 209) sum it up quite well in my opinion (quote from a specter): 'You musn't think of the Tempest as some boring piece of solid, three-dimensional earthly real estate. It's more like a four- or five-dimensional cloud in a constant state of flux, and at any given moment, I know all the shortcuts (...)'.

                      So to answer the question 'How do the Byways/Channels connect to the Shadowlands?': it can obviously be through Nihils. These can only open sometimes under certain conditions, or always be opened, but basically it is a 'regular' Nihil connected to the Tempest but with a cobbled way going through it and that was once built by the Hierarchy until Stygia, or a Nihil that for instance leads you directly into one of the many rivers of death. Byways are made out of souls, out of memories that can resist to the evermoving Tempest. They are for the huge majority of them cobbled ways and they start directly inside the different Necropoli. I see them starting from the gates of the historical core of Quick cities. But where do they lead exactly? Most of them through Nihils actually, see why below.

                      Before getting further, I think it's important to mention the 'bondaries' between the Shadowlands and the Tempest. Knowing the Shadowlands are, like Stygia, like the Far Shores, floatting inside/on it. So where do the winds of maelstroms that smashe Necropoli come from? In other words... where does exactly the Tempest start from the Shadowlands point of view? My answer to this question is (on top of Nihils): everywhere no human beings ever lived, ever once formed memories out of the place. Or really long time ago, the memories being now lost in the Tempest. Typically all the oceans, but also all the places really remote from civilisation, like the 'middles' of deserts or big forests. Of course, the sky above the Shadowlands as I picture it is also one of these bondaries.

                      So if you follow a Byway and if it doesn't go through a Nihil at some point*, it can be really really long to follow. And if it doesn't lead you through an ocean at some point, then the more you leave 'civilisation' i.e. places of the Shadowlands not inhabited by a lot of Quicks, far from Necropoli, the more the Shadowlands are decayed and covered with the mist that was there everywhere before the third great maelstrom. Like an 'external' layer of the Tempest, this mist becomes more and more windy as you keep going through places less and less remembered, more and more prey to Oblivion. These remote and misty areas are also where you can find the 'source', the beginning of Channels that will lead you throught the Tempest. Note that you can find such misty areas between two inhabited places but without any link to the Tempest.

                      *Because Byways have been mostly built at a period of time prior to mankind expansion on earth, it's more than likely than what was then a 'bondary' with the mist/Tempest is now an inhabited place, resulting in a byway going through a Nihil.

                                  My belief was the Darkness lay under the Shadowlands and the main Byway of the River of Death came from the Skinlands, leading through the Shadowlands to go down into the Darkness until it spread out to Stygia and the Far Shores.
                                  Before the 4th Maelstrom struck, railroads and new roads were being constructed, the Grim and Skeletal Legions were spreading Heirarchy Citadels amongst the Necropoli that had sprung up in the American frontier days. Although these roads and railways are newer and constructed, they're still classed as Byways as they do the same job as them. As the Legionnaires had to move with the Skinland people they were assigned to, it's no reason to doubt that the roads and railways constructed through the Shadowlands by them look too different from the Skinlands equivalents, though likely they are made from Stygian and Soulsteel as Thralls were forced into extensive mining back in those days and when burnt out from it and fatigued, they got thrown into the furnaces and smelted down too.
