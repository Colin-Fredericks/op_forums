    Fixing Tempus Fugit

    12-08-2014, 10:53 AM
    So, I think that Pandemonium 5, Tempus Fugit, is too good. Like, brokenly too good. Granted, I say that about all abilities that grant multiple actions and believe that they're anathema to good game design*. So that should give you an idea of what I mean by "fix".

    The non-combat use of it is fine, I think it's kind of neat. But I don't want to just rip out the combat application. I could shorten the duration of the effect to one round, which would make the cost prohibitive for all but the most important conflicts. However, that doesn't solve my core issues with the power (namely that it's too good). Instead I was thinking of changing it so that the user could choose where in the Initiative order they act for the rest of the turn, or give them a bonus to split dice pools.

    I doubt I'm the first person to have these problems, and was wondering what other people's workarounds were.

    *I am really not interested in discussing why I think that, nor will I bother responding to people asking: I just want y'all to understand where I'm coming from.

                                      Well, that'll be for the Deadguy to figure out for W20. I'm willing to take the game as-is, I just need to fix what I think is one glaring flaw.

                                      I've been mulling over some fixes. I want Tempus Fugit to retain its essence, so to speak. The primary aspects of the ability that I want to keep are:
                                      • Low cost
                                      • Applicable in virtually every combat situation
                                      • Always useful
                                      • Doesn't allow for resistance
                                      • Reliable
                                      • Multiplicative bonus
                                      The fact that it has all of these already makes it bonkers good to start with. All I need to do is fix the multiple actions part. To that end, my fix ideas:

                                      System: First paragraph as normal.
                                      This art costs 4 Pathos and 1 Willpower. Alternatively, by rolling Dexterity (difficulty 7), a wraith my focus this art on himself alone, decreasing the difficulty of all checks by the successes rolled for the rest of the scene. This method costs 2 Pathos and 1 Willpower.

                                      Alternatively, I'm thinking about keeping with the theme of a bonus on splitting your actions to represent super-speed or whatever. Perhaps you may use the highest dice pool to calculate how many dice you have instead of the lowest dice pool when splitting actions? Could combine that with the fix above. Hmmm...

                                                          You're right. I'm gonna go back to the drawing board and instead of trying to simply alter Tempus Fugit I'll drop it entirely. Let's take a look at the rest of Pandemonium to see what's going on:
                                                          • Basic Ability: Sense Chaos. Simple, evocative. Gathers information of a specific type. Other Haunters about? What about Spectres? Useful
                                                          • Weirdness: A straightforward power, this one. Do random ghost tricks, so that the audience knows that it's a ghost episode. It's fun.
                                                          • Befuddlement: A really solid two-dot power, though not as versatile as it seems at first. For instance, you couldn't use it on targets in a group because they'd probably realize something is up, especially if any of them are Haunters. Open-ended, continues with the theme of "weird, annoying".
                                                          • Dark Ether: Basically a scarier version of Weirdness that's more expensive and may gain you some Angst. Affects an area rather than an individual, which is nifty.
                                                          • Foul Humor: Powerful and in keeping with everything else. Affects an area with sick or vermin (or both). Does some light damage, though unlikely to bypass anybody's soak unless you roll stupid high. I really like this one.
                                                          So basically everything up until this part is kind of open-ended and emphasizes the theatrical nature of Haunters. The five-dot power should do that as well.

                                                          At first I thought of animating the environment all around you, being able to fling objects around and the like, but that's getting into the Artificer's territory, I think. Maybe a one-ghost stage show, with smoke and lighting effects? Uncontrollable, area-wide chaos of some sort? A kind of Foul Humor++?
