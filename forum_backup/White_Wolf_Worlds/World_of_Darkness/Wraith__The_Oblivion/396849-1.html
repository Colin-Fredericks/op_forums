                                                    Yeah, I always hated that Gorool was this finite, limited thing. Wraith had always made it quite clear that if a Malfean awoke, reality would be obliterated.

                                                    My ruling on this was either that Gorool was a onceborn/hekatonkhire or that in swallowing Charon's shadow, it become something less than a Malfean.

                                                    I always saw the Malfeans' consciousness/awareness of reality as the thing that was dangerous. Like the Red King in Through the Looking Glass, they have to be kept sleeping. Perhaps Gorool's Malfean mind remained sleeping and it was Charon's shadow that animated its bloated monstrous form. That's the only way I can reconcile the Gorool incident with what we know about the Malfeans.

                                                    Orpheus made things worse by stating previously Onceborn Malfeans were actually Neverborn, and then turned Grandmother into the Ultimate Bad.

                                                    Interestingly, though, I inferred from the cosmology section of Mage 20 that God is Prime, and has an equal and opposite in The Adversary/Oblivion (as a matter and anti-matter duality). Mage further suggests both might be aspects of the same entity or primal force. As Demon often refers to God as a She, and Grandmother is an anthropomorphicised aspect of Oblivion, it's entirely possible that they're the same thing (Grandmother is God, Oblivion is Prime, and the universe is the Ouroboros). It perhaps explains where God disappeared to in Demon (Her consciousness inhabited The End instead of The Beginning, as She readied herself for annihilating Creation).

                                                    I've read way too much into this, of course...
