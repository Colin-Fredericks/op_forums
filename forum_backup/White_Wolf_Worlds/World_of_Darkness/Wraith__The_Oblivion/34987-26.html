                            Delicious, delicious Pathos. Relieves the stress of being dead and having nothing. Or, at least, the stress of not having Pathos.

                            If it's a regular thing, take some Memorium.

                            If it's something where they destroy it in a way that deliberately wastes it and they get no advantage over it, possibly the genesis of a minor Relic. But there would have to be a lot of emotion involved.

                            Pouring out a 40 oz beer for a fallen homie would just generate Pathos. Making regular offerings, like say, old Egyptian tomb/temple style is Memorium (for more modern examples, see H.P. Lovecraft's or Edgar Allen Poe's graves). Ritually destroying a pot or bending/breaking a sword might get you a Relic (after the item in question is mostly gone from natural decay processes).

                                                    Ok Deadguy,

                                                    I has questions!

                                                    First, I bought WtO in college the week it arrived in the comic book store up from campus. I brought it home and proceeded to do No homework as I read well into the night. Then I laid it down, my eyes bloodshot and wearied from pouring over the illustrious tome - or is it tomb, turned off the light, and went to sleep still wondering why there wasn't any title on the cover. A couple hours later I woke up and rolled over, and jumped - the title was glowing in a ghostly green light! I was delighted even more!

                                                    How can that be duplicated in the age of Print on Demand?

                                                    Second, the group I gamed with through out college was a small cabal of thespians for whom the Story Teller system offered regular workouts in direction, method acting, and comedy improv. We all read wraith. We all discussed wraith. Then we all decided that it was unsafe to play unless the ST was a licensed psychologist... Handing your alter ego's alter ego to someone who knows you to play against you is not a game we were willing to play without professional help. Now let us assume your current and ex, plus your best friend who is now dating your ex, are all members of the gaming group.

                                                    Do you really want to hand one of them your alter ego's alter ego to use against you over three beers and a bag of chips?

                                                    Third, I have A Lot of the original first edition books for Mage and Vampire, some Werewolf and Wraith, and a few books for Changeling - as well as Gypsy. Then life took me away from gaming and I missed a lot of the books that came after the mid 90's. A couple years ago I started trying to finish acquiring the books I still wanted to complete a few sets from first edition. Now I find there is CWoD, Revised WoD, NWoD, and WoD20. I love the lore in CWoD. I have been collecting books from NWoD but don't care for it's lore. However, I like the NWoD systemically.

                                                    So:
                                                    What exactly is the WoD20 Lore in relation to both CWoD and NWoD?
                                                    What exactly is the WoD20 Systemically in relation to both CWoD and NWoD?
                                                    Is there going to be another splatbook to convert WtO20 between systems?

                                                    Last, I have a completely different take on Wraith I have been developing over the last several months. I titled this Wraith Unremitting. It is not yet complete, but I have thus far created:
                                                    1. A new Raison d'Etre for wraiths
                                                    2. A new structure for Anchors
                                                    3. A new approach for Fetters
                                                    4. 7 Houses (previously styled Guilds)
                                                    5. 15 Arcanoi
                                                    6. Adapted or Adopted around 60 Numina from NWoD materials
                                                    Working on development of:
                                                    1. How to hunt wraiths
                                                    2. Who hunts wraiths
                                                    3. 3 Dark Houses which serve the Wyrm
                                                    4. Wraith perceptions of: Garou Tribes, Vampire Clans, Mage Conventions and Traditions, and Changeling Kith
                                                    ROFL - Now that you are going to rewrite the rules, how much trouble am I going to have fitting the material in?

                                                        Lore-wise, the 20s are CWoD, not NWoD, using only the CWoD mythos. Revised was another CWoD edition, doing a cleanup of the mechanics and refocusing the games.

                                                        In terms of the metaplot, the 20s are situated around the cusp of the CWoD's change from 2e to Revised, taking a metaplot-agnostic approach. (This doesn't apply to Wraith, which never got a Revised edition; we don't know whether Wraith20 is resetting the metaplot to shortly before Ends of Empire, or advancing it past that, or doing both, as Mage20 is doing with its metaplot, projecting the 6th Great Maelstrom as a potential future.)

                                                        System-wise, the 20s are a tweaked CWoD Revised-era Storyteller System.

                                                        There's no translation guide, because there's no NWoD Wraith game to convert to/from (since that's what the translation guides are - guides to converting between CWoD and NWoD versions of games).
