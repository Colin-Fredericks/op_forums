    Some of the mechanics [in Second Edition] make me think that it can affect mortals.

    From Dirge (p. 146): “Five successes will affect a mortal with a negative emotion for a week. Wraiths, however, are not as affected.”

    From Ballad (p. 146): “Five successes can flood a mortal with cheer for an entire week. Wraiths are less easily cajoled…”

    From Muse (p. 147): “This art works equally well on wraiths, mortals, and other supernatural beings.”

    From Crescendo (p. 147): “Singing a Crescendo while Embodied inflicts listeners…one Health Level of damage per two successes.”
    This mechanic specfically states that wraith needs to be Embodied to affect the other side of the Shroud.

    Requiem doesn’t have anything for or against using it across the Shroud. I’d be inclined to say that it does; it’s a really cool ghostly power.

    Of course, this could all change in Wr20.

                            I just want to put forward that I've never read a Wraith book. All my info about Wraith is from internet sources like the various Wiki's and this forum.

                            I actually asked a similar question to this a while back on these boards. It was more a general topic of how Vampires and Wraiths might interact. The topic also had some discussion about how easy it was to cross the two games over. Both for PC/PC crossovers and using the either Vamps or Wraiths as NPC's in the other game line. Inevitably this turned to talk about Vampire Necromancers interacting with Wraiths. Link below:

                            Dead and Deader - Vampire and Wraith Interactions

                            The level to which Vampires with Necromancy interact with Wraith society has been something I've been trying to figure out (one of the reason I created the above topic) since I first started reading up about Wraith during the Wr20 Kickstarter.

                            I think its fair to say that the two game lines do not mesh perfectly. With the CWoD being originally built as (mostly) distinct settings for each game-line making it hard from the start. Add the fact Wraith never got a Revised Edition and it all gets pretty sticky and unreliable in general. It could be argued that the Wraith side of the fluff is "out of date" in regards to Vampiric interactions with the Revised Edition fluff.

                            On top of this at least some of the game sources that do provide information on this topic are written from an in-character/in-universe point of view. As such they are supposed to be biased and unreliable.

                            Short Answer:

                            Read what limited, bias and arguable out-dated fluff there is on the topic. Make up your own mind how Vampiric Necromancers factor into Wraith and how Wraiths (and their fluff) factor into your Vampire games.

                            Alternatively, hold off to after Wr20 and hope its got some new info. Even if it turns out to be a handful of paragraphs. Its still perhaps going to be new info (or a new take on old info) that you could use to decide.

                            Really Long Answer:

                            As far as I know with Wraith being cut off at the end of 2nd Edition it only really talks about three different groups of Kindred Necromancers:

                            1) The Giovanni
                            2) The Samedi
                            3) The Nagaraja

                            The Giovanni:

                            Other posters have pretty much covered the default Giovanni fluff and stance in this instance I feel.

                            They are dicks.

                            The majority of the Clan treat Wraiths like tools and slaves. From their point of view they are the masters of the Dead. From Stygia's point of view they are small fry it seems.

                            Personally, the Giovanni are perhaps my least favourite Clan. Their fluff is with the exception of a few recent additions is terrible. V20 Rites of Blood and V20DA add some stuff to them that finally managed to make them passingly interesting to me. I have yet to read their V20 Lore of the Clans entry, so maybe that will add something too them as well.

                            So basically I'm bias against them.

                            Meaning that IF I included them in a Wraith game I'd likely have an NPC Giovanni that was against type (actually friendly towards Wraiths, or at least reasonable). Likewise, I'd likely downplay their level of involvement in Wraithly affairs and make it very clear they have no real clue as to what is really going on with the Underworld.

                            The Samedi:

                            I'm actually very interested in Wraithly perceptions of this Bloodline. As they claim to have the patronage of Baron Samedi. I believe there is a group of Wraiths (Les Invisibles) that work for or are tied to the Loa as well. So I could see some interesting crossover material coming out of that.

                            If anyone does know more on this topic or has featured Samedi in their games I'd appreciate it. As its a really interesting bit of fluff I think.

                            The Nagaraja:

                            One of my favourite bloodlines. So like the Giovanni case I'm a little bias, though in the opposite way.

                            The older members where mortal Death Mages obsessed with exploring and harnessing the power of the Underworld before they were Vampires. They found Enoch about 2500 years ago (at least) and have hung out there on and off for that whole time. Its safe to say they likely know a whole hell of a lot more about the Underworld then any other Vampires.

                            However, there are maybe around 100 of these guys in the world. Likely a third (at least) of them at least are located in Enoch at any one time. Most Vampires don't know about these guys. So I can't imagine they are well known enough in Wraith society to have a prevailing opinion on them.

                            I can see the Tal'Mahe'Ra Wraiths obviously working for and allied to them. Likely some occult expert Wraiths and high ups in Stygia likely know about them.Other then that I can see them being a sort of "Urban Legend" in Wraith society. A type of undead necromancer who can EAT Wraiths, can take physical form in the Skinlands and can control the energies of Oblivion? Talk about the boogie man.

                            Consider that the Nagaraja eat flesh rather then drink blood. Also consider that the Underworld lacks the Sun, normal fire and trees. So its unlikely that any of the traditional Vampire weaknesses are likely to show up. I think its plausible given all this that its not obvious to a Wraith a Nagaraja is even a Vampire. Could be some weird plasmid creature from the Tempest, or a really, really odd type of Risen or Mage.

                            After that you run into the Kindred Necromancers that were never covered by Wraith. Mainly because they were added to Vampire after the Wraith line ended:

                            1) The Cappadocians
                            2) The Lamia,
                            3) Mla Watu
                            4) Impundulu
                            5)The Harbingers of Skulls

                            (The last four are all Bloodlines off of the Cappadocians, expect the Impundulu...maybe.)

                            So you have to make up your own stuff. Well, at least for now as Guide to the Tal'Mahe'Ra, Lore of the Bloodlines for V20 along with Tome of Secrets V20DA might help with this. Also of course Wr20 itself might mention and comment on them.

                            Here is my take on them:

                            The Cappadocians:

                            I'd say with the exception of maybe Cappadocius himself the "Clan of Death" are likely just as clueless to the Underworld as their Giovanni successors. As the point of the Giovanni getting embraced into the Cappadocian Clan was to give the access to Ghost/Shadowland Necromancy basically.

                            The Lamia:

                            The Lamia likely knew as little about Wraiths and the Underworld as their Cappadocian patrons. That being said you have a bit more wiggle room for interpretation with them. Since all the odd Lilith knowledge and sorcery going on with them means they theoretically "could" know more. That's not really supported or mentioned in their fluff though so YMMV on that one.

                            Mla Watu:

                            If I remember correctly (its been ages since I read KotEK) these guys are supposed to be pretty fascinated by the unique Underworld of Africa, namely the Dark Kingdom of Ivory. So they likely understand how things work in that Kingdom better then any other Kindred Necromancers.

                            They also came up with their own path of Necromancy that the Giovanni eventually stole called the Cenotaph Path. Which is all about interacting with a Wraiths fetters and sensing how close the Underworld is to the living world. So a pretty good Path for Crossover play really (more so then any other Kindred Necromancer save maybe Samedi). I'd say they likely have a solid understanding of Wraiths, though a Dark Kingdom of Ivory cultural filter at least.

                            Impundulu:

                            New bloodline only included in V20DA for the first time. Fluff has it that they are mainly concerned with the balancing of life and death in the living world. So I doubt they know much about the Underworld. Due to said focus they like the Mla Watu often practice the Cenotaph Path. However, they also have access to the Path of Haunting, which mainly effects living people, but the final power allows them to make it easier for Wraiths to effect certain people. So I'd say they have a pretty good understanding of Wraiths when it comes to Wraiths interactions with the living world.

                            The Harbingers of Skulls:

                            I would expect the Harbingers to know a lot more then the Giovanni but likely less then the Nagaraja.

                            Their origin is basically they are normal Cappadocians who get sealed into the Underworld by their Antediluvian. So I suspect their education about the Underworld and Wraithly society was rather "do or die (again)". School of hard knocks indeed. Some of them survived to form the Bloodline and get out of the Underworld 500+ years later.

                            Personally I can't see them doing that without getting at least some help from at least one Wraithly faction. They likely owe someone a lot of favours and likely made a fair few enemies.

                            The issue with them at current is that its not clear how "deep" in the Underworld they were trapped. Some of the fluff suggests "merely" them being trapped in the Shadowlands. Other fluff suggests something closer to the Sunless Sea/Tempest. Hopefully Lore of the Bloodlines will expand on their fluff and clear this up.

                            Final thoughts:

                            What I would stress with any Kindred Necromancer interacting with Wraiths is the individual differences of said Necromancer. Their are likely stereotypes about Vampiric Necromancers in Wraith Society no doubt. To the more well informed Wraith there are likely even stereotypes for the different Clans and Bloodlines that use Necromancy. Ultimately though all such Necromancers are going to be individually different.

                            A lot of the Kindred Clans and Bloodlines with access to Necromancy have strong "solitary figure" aspects, in particular the Harbingers and Nagaraja. The Samedi are do have a good Bloodline unity but they are still rare. Even the Giovanni, despite working for "the good of the family" have their own wheeling-and-dealing to manage and cultivate.

                            I'd say its fine to have the sterotype among Wraiths of:

                            "Vampires are asshats. Vampiric Necromancers are even bigger asshats. The Giovanni are of course the biggest asshats of all."

                            However, I'd say once you've planted that seed among your PC's and cultivated it with a dickish minor Vampire NPC enjoy subverting it:

                            Introduce a genuinely likable and useful NPC Kindred Necromancer (any Clan or Bloodline with Necromancy) that the PC's can gain as a contact or even ally.

                            Throw in an Elder Nagaraja NPC who they "could" make an enemy of to terrify the hell out of your PC group if they get overconfident about dealing with Vampires.

                            Have a powerful figure in one of the Legions the PC's interact with have a Harbinger "retainer" that owes him/her and is instructed to "help" the group (read - keep an eye on them).

                            Right done...sorry that was so long a post.
