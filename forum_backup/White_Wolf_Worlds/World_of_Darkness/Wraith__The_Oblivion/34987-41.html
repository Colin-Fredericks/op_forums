                                            Originally posted by atamajakki View Post
                                            How outlandish would you allow a wraith's Corpus to appear without use of Moliate? I usually let my players get pretty creative with it.
                                            Wraiths appear according to their self-image.... Which to me means nothing too outlandish. An anorexic person might appear obese, or a person with low self-esteem might find those physical flaws they worried about so much magnified in the afterlife.

                                            There are no doubt exceptions. I don't know much about them, but IRL there are people who call themselves "otherkin" who believe that they have the spirits of all kinds of fantastic beings inside them, like dragons or elves or what not, or that they are such beings shapechanged into human form. Such a person would no doubt look like the fantastic creature they believed themselves to be, if their belief was genuine.

                                            Some wraiths carry the evidence of the way they died along with them as well, within reason. A person who had been shot might still carry the bullet hole with him, but like Rich mentioned, a person thrown into a wood chipper would still have a body waiting for him in the shadowlands, and not a pile of ectoplasmic goo. I think it depends on how "Betelgeusian" you want to play it.

                                            But if you do, for instance, have a hole in your head because someone shot you in the noggin, I am not sure whether that hole is a "deathmark" or whether it is there because it is part of your character's self-image.

                                                Wraiths appear according to their self-image....
                                                But it is about their self-image as being dead. If you lived your life thinking you will only leave behind a rotting corpse you might look like one once across the Shroud, or worse you would get the Merit/Flaw body-less from the Players Guide which leaves you with no form at all. Someone who thinks she will rise into heaven as an idealized self would appear like that, might even have ephemeral, but useless, wings etc.

                                                Or at least that is how I have always seen the matter.

                                                But if you do, for instance, have a hole in your head because someone shot you in the noggin, I am not sure whether that hole is a "deathmark" or whether it is there because it is part of your character's self-image.
                                                I've interpreted that it's a little bit of both. Your self image might have that hole in you, which case you have a very literal hole in the head, or it might just be a deathmark in which case it might be a bleeding spot/wound in your head instead of having had half of it blown of for example. Again just my take on the matter.

                                                Overall I allow quite a bit player freedom in their Wraiths appearance though I do not remember almost anyone to have gone that wild with it, except certain people playing Haunters but that is another matter altogether.
