                                                            That's all your opinion as a critic of the various lines. You are deciding which parts of the story are okay with you and which aren't Which is absolutely what you can do. Dislike them all you want.

                                                            However, the metaplot IS one huge narrative (with space here and there for people's games to intersect on some level or avoid). It's not a great one as far as consistency goes, though it is a lot better than most people give it credit for. To critique the joins is fine, but it's not determinative or dispositive. If the metaplot saith "Nhudri = Nhudriel. Charon the Ferryman/Emperor has the same name as an angel. The Abyss is related dimensionally to the Labyrinth. Etc. Thus endeth the connections, both narratively and in setting, between WtO and DtF" then that's the way it is. In your game you can chose to ignore it or rewrite it. That's classic Golden Rule. If you do the later, you are veering off the metaplot path and might have to make adjustments and some adjustment may take you far enough off the path that the your game becomes disjoined from the metaplot. Which is largely intrinsic to CWOD games. It's one of the reasons people play them. There are lots of game systems out there with absolutely no metaplot and less to no story "built in" from the get go.
