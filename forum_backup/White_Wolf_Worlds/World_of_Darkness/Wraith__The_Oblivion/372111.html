                              While I'm a fan of the extra special deluxe books, they have so far been a massive sink of time and energy for the design teams to actually make work.                                               "Open development" is not an Oynx Path policy, it's something each development teams makes their own choices about. Some consider it worth doing, some don't, and there are reasonable cases on both sides. The more intensive the revisions really are, the less open development tends to be. The reasoning here is pretty obvious in that the fan community's random feedback isn't terribly useful because it tends to default to assuming everything is like the previous edition until a change is presented. The sheer amount of time needed to put extremely revised content up to community feedback with enough context for the community to give valuable feedback is often not worth it. Wr20 is probably in that category given the lack of a Revised edition leaving a lot of updating to bring it in line with the other 20th edition books.

                                              "Listening to backers" isn't the same as answer every question. That's sort of in the definition of the words involved. This is something you are kind of ignoring to say that nothing addresses your disappointment. The backers that want the UDEs are, generally, a constant but small percentage of the backers. The practical requirements of making the UDEs isn't something we, as the backers, have said is valuable enough to be worth doing every time. Most Onyx Path Kickstarters do not generate enough backers, to get enough UDE backers, to meet the minimum print run of 200. Not offering a UDE to avoid the W20 situation of being a few short of 200 and having to pick between no HME and disappointing a lot of backers or eating the financial cost of the last few books is listening to the backers.

                                              And frankly, UDEs aren't a development thing. They're a swag thing. There's nothing to discuss. Either it will happen for a book or it doesn't.

                                              As for not answering: there's a simple bit of PR logic there. The answer is basically "no" unless they say otherwise. If they wait for a project to hit X backers before Y date (aka what seems to be the sweet spot when UDEs work), they can announce they're going to do it and get a nice big bump on the Kickstarter. If they never hit that point, they can just go on without it.

                                              What answer are you looking for, really?

                                              -----------

                                              Dam - making your own UDE is both extremely expensive and questionable in practicality. Getting a simple print shop to do a nice print up of a clearly copyrighted PDF can be a significant pain despite it often being perfectly legal (IIRC all of the Onyx Path books are legal to print you own copy of as part of OneBookShelf's licensing). Getting a vanity press to not only do a proper printing, but to do a modified one with a custom cover using copyrighted logos and trademarks is going to be to questionable for most to allow it. Designing your own custom leatherette cover and slapping either the Wr20 logo on it, or any Onyx Path/CCP trademark on it, is pretty much a fast track to getting a C&D or sued; meaning any vanity press is going to keep the hell away from it.
