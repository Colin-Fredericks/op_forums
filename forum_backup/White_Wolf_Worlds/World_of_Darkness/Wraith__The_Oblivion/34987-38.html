                In the immortal words of Monty Python, "Stop that. You're not going into a song while I'm here."

                Now, questions.
                1-Certain buildings inspire more emotion over their lifetimes than others. That's the distinction as to what crosses over after it's destroyed in the Skinlands and what doesn't. Your average 7-11? Not likely to make the trip. A parish church that stood for generations? Better odds.

                2-That's "The Rule of Ouch" coming into effect. Basically, there needs to be some bending in one direction or another on this stuff or you end up with people getting knocked into Harrowings by a light rain shower.

                3-It's hard to stop a Risen. Really, really hard. You're better off severing the leg tendons.

                4-Not yet they don't....but damn, do I want to find a way to work some of those old Soviet Science Cities into Wraith.

                5-Spectres are all about tapping into the main vein of Oblivion, at least at the get-go.

                6-Fetters should stay in the Skinlands. Bad things can happen when you bring them into the Underworld. In other words, Don't Cross The Streams, Especially When One Of Those Streams Is The River Styx

                7-Spectres have Psyches, and Redemption is theoretically a thing. Theoretically...

                8-It would be very uncomfortable for a mortal to use something Soulforged. I'd wear gloves. Really thick ones.

                9-The cult model is one interesting one for Heretics, but we're going to be casting a slightly broader net.

                10-Considering what happened there, I can certainly see some folks sticking around post-mortem. And I highly recommend Tim Cahill's piece on the aftermath - It's in his book A Wolverine Is Eating My Leg.

                11-The dumb ones get caught and soulforged. There's quite the collectors' market for knicknacks made from famous jerkwads. The smart ones, on the other hand....

                -deadguy out

                                                @Katrina Let's see if I remember the answers to some of those questions, to help shoulder the weight for Deadguy. Please remember that I may be recalling incorrectly, though I'm pretty sure of the answers (else I wouldn't be posting them).

                                                1) Yes. Lifesight and Deathsight are inherent in being a wraith, no matter their corporeal state.
                                                2) Embodied wraiths look more metaphysically "solid." No, I'm not exactly sure what that means. Do with the information what you will.
                                                3) Yes.
                                                4) It may, though there is no mechanical benefit. It was created to specifically target Spectres.
                                                5) Yes.

                                                And @Matt the Bruins fan I'm sure you're aware, but metaphysical contextualization across game lines is... Well, White Wolf staffers have gone on record stating that the games were originally developed independently; that even if one had a Vampire game with werewolves that called themselves Garou, that were completely lifted wholesale from Werewolf: The Apocalypse, they were still not the Garou from W:tA. Each game line was it's own separate "mirror universe." Trying to make sense of one game's metaphysics and cosmology through the lens of another is a maddening (and futile) exercise.
