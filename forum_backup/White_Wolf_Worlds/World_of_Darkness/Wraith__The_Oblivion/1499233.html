    Characters That Have Visited The Labyrinth?

    12-09-2022, 03:29 PM
    Does anyone know of any non wraith canon characters (vampires, mages, etc) that have visited the underworld Labyrinth and come back, or at least characters you feel are capable of doing so?

    I’m a writer. A storyteller. They’re my passions. They’re what I do. The stories I tell, they are my mythologies. Like the Norse tales of Odin, Thor, and Loki, or the Celtic fables of Lugh or Cú Chulainn, I want them to be the kind that people retell. They are the legacies I wish to leave behind.

      The best canon example that I can think of is Xerxes Jones. Admittedly, he did not make it back, but that seems to have more to do with the specific VERY BAD IDEA he was involved with rather than a fundamental limit on his ability to return; it seems like the Void Engineers are capable of producing vessels capable of traversing the Labyrinth with relative safety. Based on that, I would assume that it would be at least hypothetically possible for others to do so, though I'd imagine the necessary protection would be considerable.

      Thought that just popped into my head as writing this: was Xerxes being able to get that close to Oblivion an elaborate case of "just as planned"?
