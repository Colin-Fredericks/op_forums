    A wraith could also Skinride a person as they get onto the train, which would be my method of entering it with my current character. They can then exit the person when they get off at whatever stop they happen to. You'd need a higher level of Puppetry to make the person decide what stop your wraith needs to get off though.

Venturing down into the Tempest through a Nihil will bring them to a realm they'll always be completely corporeal however. And as mentioned before, other wraiths along with Stygian and Soulsteel is very solid to a wraith, along with relics and Artifacts, basically stuff thats been destroyed and shifted over to be a Shadowland construct or things created over on that side or otherwise at home there.

                I had a funny moment in an RP yesterday when a Wraith named Carlos used Outrage. He was trailing a monster that was tearing through the streets (long story) and was attempting to subdue it before it hurt someone he cared about. He phased through things and became incorporeal, and while I didn't know if he should be able to "make his feet solid" rapidly or not, I simply played it like he was rushing himself so fast that he goofed. So he phased through walls, then attempted to use the basic ability of Outrage (Leap of Rage) to jump to the top of a building...                 It does raise another important question: If it means a Wraith would fall through floors, what is stopping them from just falling through the ground into a sewer system, or down even lower than that? Me, personally, I feel it should be an intuitive but concentration thing. A Wraith completely stressed out, or perhaps low on Corpus might find it harder to keep from falling through things because their feet aren't "sticking" to things too well in those emotional/metaphysical conditions. This is of course providing that they are still incorporeal at a moment before of the whole Rule of Ouch.

                            Originally posted by Ramnesis View Post

                            I don't think there's any problem with just boarding a train when its stationary. Clever use of Arcanoi should also work. But as for jumping through the side and landing on the floor, I don't remember how quickly Wraiths can recorporate, or if they can selectively pass through things. That said, I'm inclined to say that under normal circumstances Wraiths invoke the Hollywood rule of phasing, which is to say just because they are intangible doesn't mean they pass through the floor if they don't want to.*

                            I'm now imagining a fight between two Wraiths where they try to throw each other into the side of the train hard enough to make them discorporate and fall through.
                            *Otherwise Wraiths and multi-story buildings would not mix.
                            Rules say you stay incorporeal for (Stamina) rounds, but I'm generally liberal when it comes to selective incorporeality during that time -- and as you folks point out, there's a lot of support for that in the canon (WW-published and otherwise). After experimenting with writing up my own Arcanos: Insubstantiality and having people make Stamina + arcanos rating checks to do various things, I decided it was too much complication. I pretty much just allow things if I can't think of a reason why not. It makes the game more fun.

                            Guidelines I fall back on are: Shadowland physics (and for that matter, arcanoi and all WW magic) result from shared belief. It takes an act of will, with a cost, to pass through a wall because it doesn't feel natural based on life experience.* You can treat floors the same way, separately from any wall someone might be passing through at the time. And by the same token, I would let a wraith (or spectre) pick you up, spin you around until you're disoriented, then throw you through the floor.** My house rule: you always stop when you get to a sold dirt/stone/concrete barrier more than a couple of meters thick (that is, about 6 feet thick in the direction you're moving).

                            I'm also pretty liberal with most "can arcanos X do this?" sorts of questions:

                            Originally posted by The Dreamweaver View Post
                            Another question popped into my mind just a few moments ago. Let's say a Wraith has Outrage (3-5) and also has a relic fire axe or some other object that can be used as a weapon to smash or chop. Can their Wraithly powers extend to the relic objects and attack objects/people across the Shroud, or would their relic axe/bat/club simply pass through the Quick?
                            I would say it can, but treat damage dealt according to standard Outrage rules. So technically a karate chop is just as effective -- but aesthetically, I like the axe better and would encourage that kind of thing.

                            * - If a wraith has been dead a long time I might let them get away with more since death is normal to them, but you don't want to go too far in this direction because the setting is weird enough already. Most such wraiths are either drones, or too important for the PCs to have much contact with.

                            ** - And/or jump up from beneath the floor to attack you, then drop back through it and latch onto the rafters below. Whatever we find amusing/terrifying.
