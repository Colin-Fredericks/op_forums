    Wraith20: Can we fix the whole all specters are buddies thing?

    03-03-2014, 04:47 AM
    Okay so the biggest issue I had about wraith was all Spectres were on the same side... They would even try to lure you into giving in to oblivion for things like revenge, but that doesn't make a lot of sense in many cases because many of the people Wraiths would want to have revenge on would be specters. One of the things I liked about Po souls in Exalted is they weren't necessarily Buddies, they were just naturally pack oriented. But I have issues with it in Wraith. I get there is that whole Hivemind thing, but this needs to be touched upon so it feels less forced. Seriously why on Earth would the Damned get along? I mean in the Labyrinth they start developing tenancies towards soul cannibalism when they start evolving into Hekatonhires.

    I would like it if this was touched upon so the damned situation seems more natural, rather then all insane douchers get along.
