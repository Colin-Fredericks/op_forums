                                    Actually, we know virtually nothing about what happened in North America - the one source we have to date, Mediums, simply says that the Dark Kingdom of Flint withdrew to the Tempest, and still hold some Skinlands territory, but gives no reasons. When those got stamped out, the wraiths of the Flayed Lands lost their strength.
