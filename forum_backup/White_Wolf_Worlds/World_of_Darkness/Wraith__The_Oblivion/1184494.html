    Help fleshing out a PC idea.

    02-10-2018, 04:07 PM
    I am planning on getting Wraith 20 when it comes out and I am currently thinking up PC ideas for it. One idea I have is the Wraith of a Pro Wrestling manager named "Gentlemen" Johnny Bradshaw. Bradshaw, who was well known for both his eloquent Southern Accent and doing dirty tactics to allow his clients to win. Bradshaw had many clients over the years, the most famous however, was a wrestler known as Balor the Terrible, a brute who loved to brutalize his opponents in Hardcore Matches. Johnny and Balor proved to be a great match and had a run which only ended when Balor had cardiac arrest and died. As it turned out, Balor had been using steroids and the newest batch, which had been smuggled in from Mexico, killed him. Johnny, who knew about Balor's addiction and encouraged it, denied knowing about his addiction, but secretly harbored a huge amount of guilt over the death of Balor.

    ​5 years after this incident, Johnny was paired with a new client, Otis Cranston, a heel wrestler covered in tattoos who had the gimmick of a occultist. Otis, to Bradshaw's surprise, was a pretty good actor and genuinely creeped out audiences across the country. During this time, the stress of his job along with the ever increasing guilt over the death of Balor caused Bradshaw's marriage to fail, leaving Johnny all alone. On the night of Johnny's death, Cranston and him were meant to go out for a Promo for a upcoming match. But, neither Otis or Johnny ever came out. After a through search of the stadium, the bodies of both Otis Cranston and Johnny Bradshaw were found in a back room. As it turned out, Cranston was actually a Specter cultist who tried to kill Bradshaw for a ritual. But, according to the coroner, Johnny managed to mortally wound Cranston with a pocket knife before dying, with Cranston following shortly after. Long after the incident. there is rumors that the recently abandoned soundstage is haunted by the ghost of Johnny Brawdshaw, who is trapped on Earth due to his involvement in the death of Balor the Terrible.

    ​With this PC, I need help developing more fetters besides Balor's death and the stadium her haunts. I also need help developing his passions.

                      That would depend on the nature of the ritual. Work with your ST on what the purpose of the ritual could have been. Perhaps the specter behind the cult was trying to create a personal army of Mortwights through a ritual meant to bypass all the horror and trauma that Mortwights normally go through when they die. If so, then your character is obviously not a Mortwight, so the ritual failed. Perhaps the nature of the ritual is a secret to everyone but your ST and some NPCs out there, and your character starts out not knowing whether the ritual worked and what its effect was. Perhaps your character has occasional impressions of the Hive Mind when Spectres are nearby, a result of the botched ritual.

                      Or perhaps the ritual's interruption (or its completion) cause Johnny to immediately experience a Harrowing, which he survived through luck and determination, so that unlike most wraiths, he is a "newborn" who knows what a Harrowing is like.
