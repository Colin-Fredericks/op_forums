                    Originally posted by Crowley View Post
                    ok, next question..im confused as how the Harrowing works. its meant to Erase Corpus (per say), but how does one falls into the Harrowing? how does this one activates? what happend if the wraith wins over it?
                    The trigger for a Harrowing is if a wraith's being is endangered. There're two different types of Harrowing - a Target Harrowing hits when a part of a wraith's existence is under threat (last point of any of temporary corpus, Willpower, a Fetter, or a Passion, is about to be lost), while a Destruction Harrowing hits when the wraith's whole existence is under threat (last point of permanent corpus, last remaining dot in last Passion, last Willpower point spent on something useless or against the wraith's nature). A Nihil opens up and sends the wraith hurtling into the Labyrinth.

                    Resolution of Passions or Fetters doesn't trigger a Harrowing; neglect and harm do.

                    There is always a way out of a Harrowing. Always. They are never inescapable.

                    If the wraith wins, they return to a Fetter (or get thrown into the Tempest if they've lost all Fetters) with additional effects depending.

                    If it's a Target Harrowing, it depends what was targeted: if temporary corpus, they regain levels equal to Stamina, if Willpower, they keep the final point, if a Fetter or a Passion, then it's maintained and strengthened by a point.

                    If it's a Destruction Harrowing, the Shadow loses temporary Angst; if permanent corpus was the cause, the wraith also regains a point each in temporary and permanent corpus.

                                            The talk about Wr20 has made me extremely nostalgic. So much, in fact, that I’ve dug out all my old Wraith books [yes, I still have them].

                                            Crammed inside my well-worn copy of Dark Reflections, I found notes from a brief one-shot that my troupe did. One of the primary characters had been Harrowed and this session was the rest of the troupe taking on the role of PC Spectres orchestrating the Wraith’s Harrowing. It was a fun a little stint.

                                            One thing that I took for granted then that I’m questioning now: why do Spectres feel the need to put on these psychodramas for Harrowed Wraiths? Why not just shred the remaining bits of their Corpi and send them to the Void? My troupe went with it because….well…that’s what Spectres do.

                                            Also, Harrowed Wraiths (IIRC) get sent to the Labyrinth: does that mean another (un-Harrowed) Wraith could Argos his way into the Labyrinth and watch the show? Or help?

                                            For the sake of roleplaying opportunities, my answer to all these questions is still, “That’s what Spectres do,” but outside of meta-gaming, are there other reasons? Do Spectres have sub-conscious impulses to make Wraiths learn about themselves? Maybe a desire to vicariously conquer Oblivion? Thoughts?
