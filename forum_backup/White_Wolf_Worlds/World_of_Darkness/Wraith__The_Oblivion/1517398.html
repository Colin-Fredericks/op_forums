    07-07-2023, 05:02 PM
    Hey everyone! I'm gearing up to make some new Guild branded sheets for Wraith 20th Anniversary Edition and I have a quick question.

    On my original Guild sheets, on page 1 I had a small section for Guild Marks, and a section for specialty (or sometimes something else) and on page 2 there was a section for Guild Standing and Contacts.

    Do you think any of those section are still needed? I wasn't totally sure what people might want or need, so I wanted to ask before I started restructuring the sheet to use for the new branded sheets.

      I like the dedicated section on a Guild branded sheet. I would maybe prefer it to be a "Guild Backgrounds" block with Background pull-downs and 1 to 5 dot rating, this way you can select your Guild specific Status/Mentor/Contacts etc and have them all in one place. Write-in fields for Faction and Title would be nice.

      Guild Marks and Death Marks are not really necessary for page 1 imo, but would be better placed around the character description section.
