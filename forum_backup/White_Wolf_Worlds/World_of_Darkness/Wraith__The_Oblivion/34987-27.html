                    Originally posted by Whithers View Post
                    Now I find there is CWoD, Revised WoD, NWoD, and WoD20.
                    Unless we're getting into Monte Cook's WoD, there are only two Worlds of Darkness: Classic and New. They're different universes entirely.

                    Classic WoD includes a first edition, second edition, Revised edition, and the 20th Anniversary Edition.

                    New WoD includes a first edition and second edition.

                    So the "Revised WoD" and "WoD20" you mention are both part of the CWoD and are not distinct entities.

                    So:What exactly is the WoD20 Lore in relation to both CWoD and NWoD?
                    What exactly is the WoD20 Systemically in relation to both CWoD and NWoD?
                    Is there going to be another splatbook to convert WtO20 between systems?
                    - The X20 books are unrelated to the new WoD at all. Plotwise, Vampire, Werewolf, and Mage are all set at a nebulous point somewhere between the events of 2nd Edition and Revised.
                    - The X20 books are unrelated to the new WoD at all. The other three rulebooks are Revised edition rules with a few additional tweaks; Wraith never had a Revised edition, but as I understand it Rich is taking the opportunity to treat Wraith20 as sort of the Revised edition it never had, bringing it into line with the other X20 books.
                    - While there are differences between the second editions and Revised/X20 editions, they're minor enough that you can probably do any conversion in your head. You don't need a separate book. White Wolf didn't make conversion guides when releasing the Revised books from 1998-2000, and I don't recall anyone complaining.
