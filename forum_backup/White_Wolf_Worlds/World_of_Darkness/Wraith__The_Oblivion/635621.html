      1. Do I HAVE to look exactly human being like? Or can I just be a vaugly human shape of mist with a somewhat human looking face?

      Well, mostly no, with a little yes. In the Underworld (or if you cross the Shroud via Inhabit), you don’t have to look too human, but you’ll still be tangible.*1

      2. Can I float everywhere? I really dislike the idea of being forced to gravity when I am completely ethereal.

      Not so much with the floating. Check out the Rule of Ouch on page 232 in WtO Second Edition. You might be able to talk your ST into some narrative leeway.

      3. The important question is how do I make a wraith work in the skinlands world? What if I don't WANT to dwell in the shadow lands or just keep it as a second home?

      Without additional Arcanoi, your character can observe the Skinlands through the Shadowlands. Depending on the level of the Shroud, some humans might catch a glimpse (giving you that hazy appearance you mentioned earlier), but it’s sort of one-way thing. There’s a few Arcanoi that help with interaction*2

      What if some other super rich wraith somehow succumbed to his shadow and I just happend to be in the area when he went to oblivion....looked around and said this is mine now and his ghost mansion was mine and no one else in the area for whatever their own reasons just accepted the idea.....so I got bored and head out to the real world?

      I don’t see why couldn’t have a Haunt that’s a mansion. You’d have to work that out with your ST. It doesn’t even have to belong to someone else; maybe it was yours when you were alive.

      How would I make that work and how would my GM be able to juggle my shadow and my stuff while me being able to keep myself in this place? Especially if I wanted to be all.. oooouuuhh.....OOOooooHHHHHH!! I am a ghoooooosttt!! and then have people running out of the room as the walls leak blood and hot dog rat swarms out from the ceilings with floating furnature flying around the room?

      It sounds like you want your character’s day job to be “spookmaster for a haunted mansion” then hang out with other supernatural critters when you’re off the clock? Check out the Background Haunt and the Arcanos Pandemonium.

      Using the Rules As Written (RAW), the other characters will need some sort of powers to interact with you. Vampires need some Necromancy (maybe Auspex or Thaumaturgy), I think sluagh can see wraiths, Spirit Sphere for mages, and I’m not sure about the other Splats. Unless, of course, your ST wants to hand-wave it and rule that all supernatural critters can see wraiths (which wouldn’t be a bad way to go for a game like this).

      I wanna use wraiths in the real world and hang with vampires, witches and werewolves.....so how do I make that happen?

      Are you familiar with the Risen?

      *1 However, I think there was a Merit and/or Flaw in the Wraith Player’s Guide.

      *2 Off the top of head, Outrage, Pandemonium, and Inhabit.

                      Quick responses:

                      Floating: Get some dots in Argos. Problem Solved.

                      Interacting with the Skinlands: Embody:4 (incorrectly named "Life-in-Death", which should be the level 5 power, while *that* name, "Materialize" should be the 4th, but anywho -- that's an old discussion...) can let a wraith (with enough Pathos and successes) remain in a material form for even hours at a time. This is uncommon, true, but I played a character who started out the gate with Embody:4 -- he simply refused to let being dead get in the way of his plans/goals, and although he looked "unwell/somewhat unnaturally pale", we could still get certain business done and be with people for certain periods of time (he'd also "disappear" for days or even weeks on end, but much of that was covered by down time to represent his recovering his strength so he could continue doing his Skinland business).

                      Appearance/Mein: you *can* use Moliate to alter your features and even Materialize with said changes -- you just can't be in a radically inhuman form. That is, you can use it took look somewhat inhuman, or even look like someone else, but you can have multiple limbs, look shockingly horrifying (by WoD standards), or the like.

                      Haunts/Shadowland possessions: Almost anything is *possible*, but the thing to keep in mind is that the more you have, the more you have to protect/guard from others -- good Haunts are hard to come by, as are Relics/Artifacts and similar, so if you have a mansion (as a Haunt linked to the Skinlands, or a removed one in the Shadowlands), then it may well come to the attention of other wraiths who will want to take possession of it, themselves.... Be careful what you wish for....

                                                  You might also be able to placate the wraith if you can help it resolve its Passions or Fetters. It's a good idea to try to find out why the wraith is hanging around and why it's angry. It could just be a spectre, in which case it needs to be destroyed. But if it's just a wraith with a few issues, it could prove a useful ally if you help it.

                                                  Wraiths are also incredibly hardy. They don't 'die' easily. You usually have to reduce them from 10 permanent Corpus to zero, which is a hard thing to do, or have them otherwise taken care of across the Shroud (soulforged, Moliated, enslaved, etc).

                                                  A necromancer might also exact quite a high price for destroying a wraith, since there are possible repercussions for her too.
