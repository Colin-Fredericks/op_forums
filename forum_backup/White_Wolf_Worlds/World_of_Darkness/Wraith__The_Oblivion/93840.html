    Wraith Logistics

    03-18-2014, 06:23 PM
    Okay a lot of times it seems the authors of Wraith were not on the same page when talking about quantities and such of various things. For instance we have Soul Forging as the source of Most of the Material in the Underworld, with scavenged relics second, and harvested items originating in the Underworld a far third. But not everyone who dies becomes a Wraith, and not every Wraith that is reaped is made a citizen as many are enslaved or forged off the bat. And then we have Drones, where a lot of ghosts are mindless, these are often harvested and forged when found.

    But we have things like whole towers built from Souls... yet Soulsteel only makes an amount of Soulsteel in the volume of the ghost forged, often I get the impression at a lesser density. Not to mention White Jade makes only 10 pounds per level of corpus. Many seem to forget that items crafted from Soulsteel in general take a whole soul, regardless of how tiny as Soulsteel is very disinclined to be split up. So the logistics of having all these towers fully furbished with chairs and desks all made out of souls seems a bit much sometimes.

    And each Obol is a condensed soul and can be crafted into an object the size of the original soul, but some authors seem to forget this and it becomes clear.

    But with these logistics doesn't seem odd when you get things like a standing bounty of obols for Specter Teeth, I mean Dozen obols should be a friggin lot, thats a dozen souls! It seems a bit much to make each Specter worth so many souls...


    And specters we really have no indicator how many there are, sometimes you get the feeling that there are more specters generated then Wraiths. But a lot of this can be deceiving because you also get the impression that the turn over rate for Wraiths is much higher then for specters. Like if you know a wraith existed a year ago you have no idea if its still around because it could have been sent to oblivion, transcended, or soul forged, yet with a Specter... the assumption seems to be if you know a specter existed a year ago they are likely still around because the turnover rate of existing in a made storm riding horde for some reason is not that high compared to sane folks.

    And then you get contradictions, in the beginning of the Dark Kingdom of Jade Book they state Kuei can be forged into white jade but it isn't permanent and it often degrades over time, yet later in the book with they talk about the Way of the Farmer and how corpus harvested by Kuei can be used as material, not really clear on if this is normal white jade, and they seem to imply it works just like normal white jade with no issues. They don't even say if you can keep harvesting the Kuei, or if the Corpus you Harvest from them is permanently removed, I guess I'll wager on the conservative side and say its permanent loss.

    But the Angst accumulation gets astronomic for folks like Soul Forgers and Hell Torturers, often the NPCs are not written with the idea that they have to get regularly Pardoned of Angst into their character.

    And how do you explain the Logistics of making the Emperors Palace, its a virtual city hundreds of miles across with over 250 palaces made of white jade, and other buildings besides that... If a single souls can make a building block somewhere between the size of a bucket and a desk... you would still need an insane amount of souls to make even a single palace... not to mention 250 + Multi story palaces. And then they also paved an Imperial Highway!

    And then we also have the Pathos Economy which most of the books seem to forget exists. What with Pathos Money in the Dark Kingdom of Jade being impermanent if created by Wraiths. I think the Stygian version runs off of Pathos Batteries right?

    This really makes it sound like there should be a bigger business of Importing Building Material from the Isles of the Tempest, as well as smuggling items from the Living World into Relic form using magic, or at least emotionally resonant ritualistic destruction.

                                      This logistics debate came up several times in my troupe. We threw around a few ideas. One was “wraith-farming” (as Mr. D would call it many years later), but that notion never sat well with me. Mechanically, it was too similar to Moliate. Thematically, it caused soulsteel to lose some of its forlorn dread (having a sword made out of someone’s Corpus isn’t nearly as foreboding/cool as having a sword made from someone’s SOUL). The biggest thing that confused us was the economic factor of the Obolus; how did you take your averaged sized soul and condense into a coin. [We realized we were dealing with the metaphysical abstractions of ectoplasmic matter, but we still felt like there was a little gap that needed calking.]

                                      Here’s what my troupe came up with:

                                      -Forging Soulsteel-
                                      • Ectoplasmic being (Wraith, Spectre, Drone, etc.) is “sent to the forge.”
                                      • At the forge, it is melted down and cast into a soulsteel ingot (we were thinking about the size of a cinder block).
                                      • After the ingot is cast, it can be used to forge other objects. From this single ingot, several items can be hammered out (or material for one large item).

                                      We never did any number crunching, but the idea was you could get a few items from a single ingot, without losing the thematic elements of soulsteel. Off the top of my head, from one ingot, you might be able to forge:
                                      • 4-6 swords, depending on size. In our games, the standard-issue Stygian soulsteel sword was a Maintz-style gladius with a stylized bone handle. An ingot could make 6 of these. (I’m guessing maybe 2 or 3 two handed swords or a single Pyramid Head-esque cleaver).
                                      • 2-3 Breastplates
                                      • About 10 Oboli. The whole “worth one soul” becomes more of a metaphor.

                                      We ran a few Doomslaying excursions and it didn’t make sense that, even if the Anacreon was ripping us off, that we’d get two whole souls (two oboli) for one Spectre head. We went this route solely to help with the economic considerations of our suspension of disbelief.

                                      [Disclaimer: In the “Ask the Deadguy” thread, Mr. D has stated that it’s one soul per object, so this goes directly against the words of THE developer. The above was my troupe’s HR; its presentation is not intended as a challenge to the edicts of the RL Charon. ]
