    Maybe you could tie it into willpower&passions considering how important those are to wraith's themes?

    For example: have passions capped by the wraiths willpower and allow the wraith to buy an attribute, ability, or arcanoi higher than 5 by tying it to a higher than 5 passion. If Johny Drake had the Passion: Obsession(feeling powerful) 6, they could tie that with their strength and pay the XP to buy strength 6.

    I'd also have the wraith give up getting pathos from that passion if they choose to tie it to a stat, all that emotional power is going to the super-human stat after all.

                                                        As Tasti Man said, I dont exactly enjoy the idea of having high powers.

                                                        For instance, for Mage, I believe that it was in the 2nd edition, once upon a time there was the "masters of the art" book, with the alleged 6+ spheres, and the terrible things such as "throw planets" for Forces +/- 9... Which completely misses the point of Sphere levels adding function, not brute force (which is a matter of successes).

                                                        Thankfully, that book were slowly let to turn into a relique that got swallen by Oblivion and is ignored.

                                                        I largely dislike for instance all the 6+ disciplines for vampire for this very same reason. I prefered when powerful ancients were the stuff of legends. Therefore, I could say that I prefer to have some mysteries to be mysteries.

                                                        So, how can Charon, the Lady of Fate or the Deathlords do what they do? I dont know, it's a mystery, and you're not supposed to know anyway. I like that.

                                                        Now, on the other hand, we are players obviously, and as such we are powermongers of the worse kind, so it's only natural to dream with that juicy Argos 8 every time we save a XP point. I understand that urge - and that's perhaps even the more reason why I would rather NOT have the temptation of having those powers in full display in front of me, as to "avoid sinning".

                                                        That being said, all that would have to be built from scratch. For example, my mage games do have a lvl 6 for all Spheres... But that's a closed secret that only I as ST know. So, for all purposes, it doesnt exist for my players, but I do have them all on my notes, the level of archmastery. But those are not for mortal eyes...
