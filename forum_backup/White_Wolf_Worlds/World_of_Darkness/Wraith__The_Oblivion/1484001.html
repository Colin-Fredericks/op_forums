    What would Wraiths do with Mages in the Shadowlands

    06-14-2022, 01:03 PM
    My Mage/Vampire/Wraith crossover chronicle has got to the point where the Mages have ventured into the Shadowlands in search of their Wraith friends. The Mages need to find their way to the local Necropolis and in order to do so I'm suggesting they get a local guide.

    So assuming they're in some sort of non-Heirarchy village or outpost of Wraiths what would the locals want from the Mages in order to provide some help?

    Note: I don't run a standard Wraith game, it's more of a post-apocalypse setting where the local Wraiths have to scavenge for goods to sell to the Heirarchy and it's a lot further across the 'wilderness' to get to the Necropolis

      Wraiths will always need Pathos. Goods, whether in the form of relics or soulforged items, are always in demand. Some wraiths will want consors in the Skinlands. Others need someone to find, salvage, or protect their Fetters. Maybe there is a local Haunt that will need to be preserved from a wrecking ball so it will continue to exist. Some Wraiths may need people to do things in the Skinlands that will help them resolve their Fetters so they can move on.

      These are things all Wraiths will need regardless of their faction.

      I would recommend something that the PCs can incorporate into their activities or downtime in the Skinlands so it'll be a reminder of their adventure and commitments made here. If it is some action in the Underworld, it'll be easy for them to do it, move on, and forget it as unimportant. While Mages can do impressive things in the Underworld, wraiths already can do a lot of things on their own there. Someone doing something for them in the Skinlands might be much more useful.

          Wraiths have unfinished business. Given the chance of having "friends on the other side", they would certanly want to have those adressed.

          The first thing thou that these Wraiths would do would probably be to get perplexed, to panic, get paranoid or go nuts about a casual visit from "Quicks" into the Shadowlands. Unless your Mage players took the extra care to hide their aura emanations with Prime 1 to "disguise" themselves as regular Wraiths, ANY denizens of the Underworld will be able to spot them with Lifesight, and with their increased senses, those Mages will be like beacons of life from MILES of distance.

          To your Mages however, if they have never been into the Shadowlands before or havent properly researched all of its implications, this possibility shouldnt even cross their heads - until they are there.

          Initially, ANY Wraith that meets them will be freaking out. With the exception of very old Wraiths who have seen their fair share of weird, most Wraiths wont even know what a Mage is, let alone having meet one, given that Mages are the rarest critters in the WoD.

          So, the Wraiths will be absolutely perplexed by this visitation; and to make matters worse, add to the fact that their Shadows will be spitting paranoia poison in their ears, making them all the more wary.

          If the players manage to resolve that initial encounter and that they "come in peace" and want to see their leader, the Wraiths will have a million plus one questions before agreeing to help.

          If the Mages end up doing a few tricks to convince their Wraiths benefactors, the Wraith may end up asking:
          - Favors in the Skinlands: avenge me! (Kill my killer with extreme prejudice), say hello to my wife (convince the widow you can speak with her husbands ghost, confort her and make sure she will be taken care for), finish my book! (enter that abandoned house, find the unfinished book and right it down as the Wraith whispers in your ears) and things like that.

          If however, the players do the stupid thing of telling the Wraiths what they are (Mages) and what they can do (Magik!), on top of helping with their unfinished businesses, they may ask:
          - Magik assistance: kill the X Spectre, build a permanent byway/portal between Haunt and Necropolis etc
          - Magik items: create me an artefact to defeat Spectres, make me a Magik mask to disguise me into anything I want.

          No matter what, it's doubtful any Wraith would ever know the extent of the powers of a Mage - if they did, the amount of crazy they would request would be tremendous!

          Most likely, the Wraith will just try to "fish" whatever he can get. If the players say "oh, we are magic men!", the Wraith will just go "oh yeah? So make me a lamp that always guide me safely through the Tempest!" or "So prove it - silence my Shadow forever!". Those requests may be absolutely unreasonable, but will fall between the needs Wraiths have in their world and what sort of expectations a Wraith may have upon thinking about "Mages or whatever the hell they are".

          Now, to those the players may go "look, magic(k) doesnt work like that... How about we do X instead?" Which would be the smart thing, but your players may not be aware that they are dealing with a being that has no idea what "Spheres" are, let alone how many dots they have on those, and they may end up taking those requests literally, so good luck for them.

          Just keep in mind, the Mages will probably know a lot more about Wraiths than the other way around, so they should act accordinly.

          And lastly, ANY Spectres in the vicinity will probably enter a Rapacious murderous state just by spotting living beings shinning in the Shadowlands.

          PS: their "life-beacon" is something that their Wraith-Guide may eventually point to, if the players manage to get on good terms with him. After that it's up to the players to try a hasted Prime 1 to try to hide their auras, and an important lesson to remember in future trips.

              Skinlands access is probably the biggest thing Mages can provide. I don't mean bringing Wraiths over, I just mean getting things accomplished on the living side. That being said, many Wraiths will be focused on the more immediate element of Pathos.

              Mages are basically walking Pathos batteries. As living humans they burn brightly with it. In fact, imagine a homeless child sitting outside a hamburger shop only able to experience what is inside by the occasional wafting aroma or by going through the dumpster after hours. Now imagine that suddenly this child finds a freshly made burger right in front of him. That's what wraiths are experiencing. I'm not saying they are going to instantly attack, but they're going to be eying up the Mages in a way that it familiar to any Vampire characters nearby.

                  A Mage ally would be INSANELY powerful to ANY Wraiths - as soon as they begin to understand what a Mage is. And the Wraith can also become a POWERFUL ally to the Mage - a Wraith is after an invisible and immaterial spirit, but more than just that, it's a HUMAN spirit. This means that it is far easier to deal with a Wraith than with the weird Umbrood aliens and their non-human behaviors or logic.

                  Also, more than just being "good spies", Wraiths have useful Arcanoi, and communication is super easy - they can understand and be understood by any human language to begin with.

                  On top of that, your Mage can "juice" up the Ghost with a ton of cool powers, turning your ghost buddy into a true war machine!

                  And the other great aspect is, what Wraiths will ask in return will most of the times be far easier than what Umbroods would ask; Wraiths care about their Fetters and Passions; many times for a Mage, giving a hand on those would be trivial!

                  And if you're juicing your ghost pal with Supernatural "Mojo", not only you are refining your "weapon", but you're also making him happier too!

                  With Spirit, you can make Artifacts, Talismans, Charms whatever out of Relics or their Wraiths Artifacts. Such items would be IMMENSILY powerful in the Shadowlands, turning even a weakling newborn Wraith capable to vanquish elders.

                  With Spirit 5, you could rip away the Shadow from a Wraith... This is INSANELY powerful!

                  With Mind 2 you can "spread the love"... If you're subtle, you can create a powerful enough "environment" - like a club, school whatever - that will be the place to gather the emocions to fuel the Wraith's Pathos... So, if your Wraith pal thirsts for love, just Mind 2 a park to turn it into the "lovers park", where couples find an endless desire for romance... And your Wraith friend can engorge in the Pathos. Since he will be having plenty of it, you can even cut a deal with him to use your comrade ghost as a Quint batery.

                  If you have Life 5, you can create an empty husky of a body for him to Inhabit at will... That will definetly win his favors.

                  Now, take a Scyth, imbue it with Quint from the Wraith's Pathos, use Spirit 4 while destroying it to turn it into a Relic Scyth. Now, enchant your buddys Scyth with an Spirit 4 effect, and now his Scyth can cut enemies from across the Shroud! So now you have your own invisible, silent assassin that is as deadly against other Wraiths/Spectres as he is against beings in the material plane!

                  If your players manage to befriend a ghost... It's a piwerful relation for both sides!

                          Effectively they are relics with no special abilities. They will function exactly like their physical counterparts do, within reason, but without the emotional investment they won't manifest anything extra and they will probably fade faster than relics normally do.

                          Moving fetters, though... Nothing good is coming from that. There might even be an example of this in the books, though if so I can't remember where. The major issue I see is that Fetters attach Wraiths to the living world, they aren't functioning in that capacity unless they are in the Skinlands. Picture a platform suspended over a great pit by cables attached to large pylons on the edges. Now picture one of those pylons moved to the central platform. Even if the platform can support it, it isn't doing the job it used to.

                          Of course that analogy breaks down once we get into resolving Fetters, so take it with a grain of salt. At a minimum, though, I think Fetters are largely less safe in the Underworld than in the Skinlands.

With just a bit of Quint a Mage with Spirit 5 could create anything down there; with Spirit 5 and Mind 5 (which aint an uncommon combination, since many Mages like to create fully grown human clones), they could even create "neverborn" Wraiths! Spirit 5 Mind 5 can create plasmics or Wraiths that were never alive, it can be used to obliterate a Wraith's Shadow (this would earn *undying* gratitute from the wraith so blessed - pun intended), it could even be used to "heal" a Specter! Or, the Mage could "awaken" a Wraith's Eidolon. Or, if you want to be truly the "master necromancer", go ahead and create a sort of "anti-specter", an Underworld being that has the Eidolon in full control!
