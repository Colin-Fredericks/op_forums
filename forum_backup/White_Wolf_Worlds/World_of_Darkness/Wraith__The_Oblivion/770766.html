              Dark Passions can have good intentions behind them; the Shadow Player's Guide points out in its intro fiction that the Shadow can be motivated by a desire to help just twisted. That's the kind of thinking that has you refuse to leave your house ever again for fear of getting hurt, or sabotaging your relationships so that you can never be broken up with, but there's still a warped sense of logic to it.

                  Per some other responses: it is a common misperception (or, more accurately, a common two-dimensional perspective) that the Shadow is "evil": it often has evil qualities and may, in fact, be evil, but that that isn't always the case, and is an overly-simplistic view on the subject. Some Dark Passions *can* be "noble" or "positive", but the ultimate end-result is the (at least potential) self-destruction of the Wraith, itself. (A Shadow can have a Dark Passion to "heroically save others from dire jeopardy", which sounds all nice and everything, and may even result in other beings being rescued from "x" situations... but the underlying motivation *behind* that Dark Passion is the putting in jeopardy of the Wraith, *itself*, which is the by-product of helping others who are in highly dangerous situations. Most Dark Passions won't, of course, be that seemingly noble on the surface and can be "read" for the dark/destructive/negative things that they are; in some cases, though, a discerning perception is required to note the difference between a *truly* noble Passion and an ultimately self-destructive Dark Passion....

                                          Originally posted by Prince of the Night View Post
                                          glanced through a guild book or two...

                                          WHY is it one or two Wraiths have Dark passions that seem nobler then their passions?

                                          just seems odd... to me...
                                          Because a Passion is something that give a wraith a sense of "satisfaction" (oversemplification), while the Dark Passion is something that:
                                          -negate a passion in a mephistophelian / Freudian way
                                          -relate to the habits of a wraith, filtering it with some particular feeling (guilt, common sense, even conscience)
                                          -destroy a wraith ambitions, plans and projects

                                          so, if a wraith is a shitty person (for example a serial killer of grannies and dogs' raper) his Passion would probably be the worst.
                                          Contrarywise, the Dark Passion of his Shadow would be "higher" and "nobler". But the point is that the Oblivion is not Evil, Oblivion is nothingness. Thus, negating a Wraith's Passions, habits and ambitions, the Shadow bring the Wraith further near the nothingness, the Oblivion and his own demise.
