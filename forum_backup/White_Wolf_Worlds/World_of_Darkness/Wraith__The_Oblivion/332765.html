    Physical beings in the Shadowlands

    12-07-2014, 01:42 AM
    So, as I've always understood it - the Skinland structures and inhabitants are completely visible (if warped) in the Shadowlands, and exert physical force. Wraiths can't exert physical force back without Arcanoi, and spend corpus to step through said structures or beings if they can't step around them.

    So how does it actually work for others who PHYSICALLY travel to the "Dark Umbra", be they Mages, Werewolves, Vampires, whatever? How do they handle the closed doors in the Shadowlands that Wraiths spend corpus to step through?

              Well, in Mage, in the Book of Worlds, they detail the spheres and methods to travel to the various parts of the Umbra. The only method presented for the shadowlands is a rote called the Agama Sojourn (Entropy 4 Life 2 Spirit 3) which basically makes your character temporarily dead and sends you to the shadowlands. While there you have a corpus, just like a wraith, though you can still use magic. (Using Entropy magic in the shadowlands tends to open nihils and summon spectres, so beware) and traversing the other layers of the underworld requires a guide.

              The BOW doesn't provide any other methods of travel, and deals with it exclusively from the Euthanatos paradigm, and Entropy 4/Life 2/Spirit 3 as being the required spheres to make the trip. The Infinite Tapestry barely touches on the Low Umbra because the Wraith line at that point had ended, and there was more nothing to see.

              Xerxes Jones was able to travel to the shadowlands in some kind of voidship. Though if he piloted it as a psuedowraith, or had a real, physical body, I don't know. Maybe his voidship was specially designed to protect him from the dangers of the labyrinth (probably) and maybe he had such an excellent command of Entropy, Life, and Spirit that he bypassed the need for a corpus body... Or maybe the Euthanatos made it harder than it had to be and the writers of BOW didn't bother concerning themselves with any other method or paradigm of getting there. I think it's up to the individual ST.

              I know Xerxes is described in Mediums: Speakers with the Dead, but since I don't have that book I don't know if it details his methods of traveling to the Low Umbra.

                                                  Originally posted by Crowley View Post
                                                  Changelings: i have this weird theory that if a changeling steps into Wraithland, he would die out of banality quiicker than you can say "youre fucked."
                                                  Changelings can traverse from the Dreaming to the Underworld (and, rarely, directly from Earth to the Underworld) through the use of a Black Path of Balor. The mechanics once they are in the Underworld were, to my recollection, never described. However, I'm sure it wasn't instant death by banality. Otherwise, what would be the point of having Black Paths of Balor? Sluagh and Keremet in particular are described as using them. However, beyond that, I can't recall anything that describes what happens when a Changeling physically enters the Shadowlands. I'd say that most rules that apply to a mortal apply to them, with Wyrd effects adjudicated on a case by case basis.

                                                  I do remember that Sluagh can see wraiths by making a simple Perception+Kenning roll (I don't recall how difficult it is, though), and can hear them just by spending a point of Glamour. Pandemonium creates chimerical effects, and Keening is easier to use on a Changeling. I think it can also be combined with Ravaging to make it more effective, but I can't remember how. Phantasm has some weird effects when used on a Dreamer, but I forget what exactly. Nothing good for the Changeling trying to be the Dreamer's muse, if memory serves. On the other hand, using Phantasm to enter the Changeling's dreams is also possible, but usually not as safe for the Wraith making the attempt.

                                                  For an even more unusual case, see the Keremet. They're listed among the Adhene (AKA the "Denizens of the Dreaming"), but they aren't really- they're enchanted mortals who were brought into the dreaming at the moment of their death. They can see, hear and communicate with Wraiths with no problem (no rolls or expenditures needed), and frequently pop in and out of the Underworld using the aforementioned Black Paths of Balor. One thing they do is ferry the souls that the Sidhe displace when they take a human host. But I still can't remember any specific rules for what they did once they were in the underworld. They didn't get much detail, I'm afraid. Like so much else in C:tD. :-(
