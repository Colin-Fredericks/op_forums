    WtO: Tempered Arcanoi

    10-27-2014, 05:57 PM
    Share your Tempered Arcanoi you may have created over the years. This one was inspired from the 2nd Edition description of Embody where is was described as virtually useless in the Tempest.

    Virtually Useless (Embody 5 + Lifeweb 2)

    Description:

    Embody is thought of as an Arcanoi devoted solely towards interacting with the living world. A Proctor was once told their pride and joy was virtually useless in the Tempest and beyond. Said Proctor set out to prove otherwise. The name of this tempered art may be a bit of a joke, but the results are anything but.

    It is a well known fact that mortal souls slipped into the Underworld through the powers of Phantasm enjoy a certain resilence to damage. Those vampires that travel into the Shadowlands are often shown to share that same resistance, even taking into account the increased fortitude of the vampiric body. Skinland travelers into the realms of the dead are gifted with an innate resistance to most sources of damage whilst there.

    Embody forces the Wraith to assume a Materialized state. Lifeweb is utilized to provide a push upon the Wraith to keep from crossing the Shroud while in the Shadowlands, or if in the Tempest to metaphysicallly cheat on activiating the Embody power.

    System:

    The Wraith using this Art must have at least one Fetter present in the Skinlands. Those who have lost or resolved their Fetters must use Lifeweb 3 (Splice Strand) prior to activating this Art.

    Roll Manipulation + Embody. The difficulty is the local Shroud around the Wraith's highest rated Fetter. Each success is one turn the Wraith is immune to all damage that is not aggravated in nature. The sole exception to this rule is damage caused by the character going incoporeal.

    This art costs 3 Pathos and 1 Willpower. Maintain the Material Form may be used to extend the duration of this effect.

      The Harrowed Highway (Argos 5 + Connaissance 3)
      Description:
      A skilled Harbinger can ride secret byways to their fetters. With enough skill, this feat can be achieved almost instantly. Rumors have it that elder Gaunts are able to travel the Underworld at will with the very same powers. The Harrowed Highway are for those Harbingers who have not yet resolved their fetters, but are suicidal enough to reach for the same freedom. A byway through the Tempest is momentarily formed every time a wraith enters a harrowing. This byway always leads to the Labyrinth, but theoretically the path would apply both ways.
      This art takes full advantage of all the rules of a normal Harrowing. The harrowed will be brought to the Labyrinth, the Spectres must play within the rules of the harrowing, and the wraith will be released upon the successful resolution of the psychodrama.
      A mastery of Argos is essential to have any hope of navigation these temporary byways. An adept understanding of Connaissance is essential to notice the tell-tale signatures of the original path. This perception extends from Serviteurs basic ability to notice the concentration of oblivion in an area. A momentary bonding with the Hive-Mind replays parts of the harrowing the original byway involved, and allows for the Harbinger to follow their way back.
      This art allows for a freedom of movement unheard in the Underworld, and demands a corresponding cost in kind.
      System:
      Activation of this art requires a Stamina + Argos roll (difficulty 8) and the expenditure of 4 pathos. Travel times are dependent upon the number of success equivalent to the Argos 4 (Jump) art. The location must be clear in the Harbingers mind, even if through nothing more than an detailed description and general location. The Harbinger will arrive at the location nearest their target that a wraith has entered or exited a Harrowing at within the past month. Exact details are left to the Storyteller's discretion.
      A single success is enough to activate this power, with all of the resulting consequences.
      Upon successful use of this art, the wraith immediately loses all Corpus levels and enters a targeted Harrowing with themselves as the quarry. The harrowing will be blended with elements of another wraith whose byway is being utilized. Due to the unnatural blending, all rolls during the Harrowing are at +2 difficulty.
      Success or failure is irrelevant to arriving at the intended destination. The Harbinger may well botch the Harrowing and lose a permanent Corpus level, but they will manifest at the intended destination. Normal rules apply as if exiting any other kind of harrowing. Corpus levels will be at at the wraith's stamina upon executing this art.
      Notes:
      The initial activation of this art is meant to be reasonable for the benefit it conveys. The true cost of this Art comes in the fact that it amounts to an automatic Harrowing for the Harbinger involved, with all the difficulties that involves. It is this same requirement that keeps Spectres from using this art. A Harrowing must be initiated to use the Harrowed Highway. Spectres are the only ghost incapable of undergoing Harrowings. Any Spectre who successfully activates the Harrowed Highway is immediately swallowed by Oblivion. Any Shadow-eaten Harbinger who learned this art while a wraith is also aware of this fact.

          I'm working on a few of my own that I felt I could share with others. Feel free to critique and/or modify to your own liking.

          Chimera (Phantasm 5 + Embody 2)
          Description:
          For the Wraith that desires a more personal touch to their real life hauntings. The Sandman is able to channel his corpus and willpower together into a vigorous (and solid) apparition that may interact with the world and the target as if they were alive, though completely numb to most sensations. The form the Wraith takes is purely up to their imagination and for a short time they will be virtually unstoppable, making for brief but violent attacks should a Catharsis be involved, or just a vengeful spirit.

          System: The player rolls Manipulation + Embody at a difficulty equal to the local Shroud and spends one Pathos. The player must also spend one point of Corpus to become solid. Each success rolled equals one turn that this form will remain, though the player may add extra turns by making the usual "Maintain The Material Form" roll. A botch will disperse the form instantly. To use this power the Sandman must have either attuned himself to the intended target via previous Embody attempts or entered their dreams once before.
          Once manifested, the quasi-material Wraith may take an amount of damage equal to his current level of temporary Corpus and temporary Willpower combined. He will be immune to pain and his body will hold together until the final strike is delivered or he dispels the tempered Arcanos. At the cost of one Pathos per point, the Wraith may gift his new form temporary Strength, Dexterity, Stamina, and Appearance (maximum 5). He may not improve Manipulation, Charisma, or any Mental Attributes. While manifested, the Sandman may employ Phantasmagoria (Phantasm 4) illusions, but must be wary of overextending his Corpus. Should the target escape the vicinity (100 yards) or win a Willpower roll, the Wraith will disperse.

          Note: Damage he takes only applies to his temporary Corpus rating and has no impact on Willpower. This one took a while to think up. I think I struck a good balance in advantage versus disadvantage. I didn't want this to basically be Embody 5 "only better!", so I messed around with what it gave and how long it could potentially last.

          Sleepwalk (Phantasm 5 + Puppetry 2)
          Description:
          For the malicious Wraiths that desire life beyond the grave but at the expense of others, this twisted blend of two Arcanoi allows a Wraith to terrorize a mortal with a vivid and violent nightmare before planting her own soul into the victim's body.

          System: The player spends 3 Pathos and rolls Manipulation + Phantasm against a (sleeping) victim's temporary Willpower. 1-4 successes places the victim under a distressing nightmare, then extracts his soul from his body for a number of turns equal to the Wraith's temporary Willpower + the number of successes she rolled. 5+ successes forces the dreamer into a sort of temporary "coma" where they will be asleep and dreaming until he either wins a contested Willpower roll (limit of one per scene) or suffers an intense physical pain that jolts him back to lucidity. For however long she has the body to herself, she may use it as if it were her own.
          Like all other forms of Puppetry, she is physically limited to the host's own stats, but retains all knowledges, talents and skills. People might notice that the host's eyes seem heavy as if deprived of sleep and might assume that they are simply sleepwalking. Care should be taken not to experience an intense pain that might awaken the host. This art grants the user 3 points of Angst as the mortal will be fully aware of what is happening around him yet trapped in a nightmare. Botching the role will sap the Wraith of one point temporary Willpower, waste the expended Pathos, and grant her Shadow one point of Angst.
