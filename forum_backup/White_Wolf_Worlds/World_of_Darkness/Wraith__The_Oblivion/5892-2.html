                    What I want to see:

                    Detailed writeups of all the Guilds, especially the ones who never got a Guildbook.
                    Updated Arkanoi, with both new and familiar Arts.
                    A detailed section on the other Dark Kingdoms, with a focus on making them playable.
                    Material from Dark Reflections, the Players Guides, Doomslayers and the Hierarchy and Renegade books.
                    A detailed write-up of the Heretics faction, which never got a book of their own.

                    But most importantly, I want the game to be preserved like it was and is. I don't want any changes to the setting, especially not in the name of making the game more accessible.
                    Please, no Guilds as splats. Please. Wraith doesn't need splats.

                                        There's a few thing I would love to see in Wraith 20th.

                                        - First: I want to see more about the "Mnemosynis" Arcanoi as well as more about the Mnemoi Guild. Their official coverage left me...wanting.

                                        - Second a "crossover" covereage between Wraith and the Cappadocian/Giovani/Harbingers of Skulls (and Kaymakli and maybe Derinkuyu) as well as the Tal'mahe'Ra (and the City of Enoch)

                                        - Third another "crossover" coverage between Wraith and Werewolf...The Labyrith and Dark Umbra.

                                        - Fourth a "Dark Age" Wraith Coverage...something (like for Mummy) that was sadly missing in the DA line. As the Underworld was probably quite different between the First Maelstrom (Fall of Rome 470) and the Second Maelstrom (the Black Plague of 1342) than in Modern Night between the 5th and 6th.

                                        - Fifth a Necropolis From around the world book. to cover such spot at Istanbul, Paris, Rome, Herculanum/Pompeii/Stabiae/Oplontis/Boscoreale, etc.

                                        -A Demon/Wraith crossover (if there is a link) and an update on the Quick and the Dead could be nice too.
