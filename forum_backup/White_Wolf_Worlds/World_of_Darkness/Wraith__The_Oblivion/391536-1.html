                                                04-05-2015, 11:25 PM
                                                Well I played a WtO game tonight, and we playtested the new Argos rules. What struck me was how difficult movement was in the game, and how that really makes a difference to pace, crucially. Every time a wraith wants to travel, they have to plan a lot, and you either have to skip over big chunks of time or roleplay lots of the logistics (which isn't fun in my real life, let alone in a roleplaying game).

                                                There are wide stretches of land (particularly in the USA) where wraiths would be exposed to maelstroms and spectres. The pace would slow to a crawl as soon as any wraith left a necropolis, because they can no longer rely on the Midnight Express. Even crossing bodies of water is painfully slow without a relic craft. (Although I was very pleased that I managed to improvise a raft and use Phantom Wings as rudders to help steer the craft to shore.)

                                                I think, as a house rule, five successes on a Phantom Wings roll should be enough to allow its use for an entire scene. After all, it's still at normal walking (up to jogging) speed, so you really can't get very far. Crossing a 400m body of water would require 16 turns, if we assume speed of 25m/turn. Even as an extended roll, that's a lot of successes. Allowing Phantom Wings for a scene, but with Dexterity-based rolls whenever obstacles occur or whenever a character needs to manoeuvre/change directions, would be much better. Five successes is usually considered an exceptional result anyway.

                                                Any more thoughts on this?

                                                    Even crossing bodies of water is painfully slow without a relic craft.
                                                    How so? It is usually faster for Wraith than it is for humans without a relic craft as Wraiths may just walk/jog/run on the the water.

                                                    Also I have to repeat myself a bit here but traveling long distances isn't really a ghostly thing in myths or anywhere aside from following their Fetters, that is haunting people or vehicles. And when travel is needed Argos facilitates that through the Tempest and is pretty much the only means to travel to the Dark Kingdoms within, also it is the nature of the Tempest to defy time and distance which makes travel faster, and Argos helps with this too though not through granting flight and it does so at level 1 on the new version which also tends to guarantee safe travel. But like I said there are other methods of travelling as well, methods much more in theme with ghost than growing wings and flying to places like possessing computers and travelling through the Electric Highway, Possessing a human and traveling that way, just short cutting directly to that Fetter of yours that moved away through Lifeweb, possessing actual vehicles and moving like that or just plain not using any Arcanoi and walking into a plane or a train along with the living passengers.

                                                    Wraiths do not actually need sustained flight to facilitate movement and they require far less preparation and logistics than humans when travelling, or Vampires for example.

                                                    Sure you can extend Phantom Wings to last a scene, and in that regards the rule of it requiring 5 successes on the activation roll is a pretty nice ruling, but I still fail to see the need for this.

                                                        See, the 'water as solid' thing was never clear to me. I know there was something in Blood-Dimmed Tides, but I never read that book. Even if a wraith could walk through the water, wouldn't they pass through the surface and wind up on the waterbed? So either at the bottom of a sea or river, which would mean climbing out again. I'm not sure why water would be otherwise as solid as concrete for a wraith.

                                                        I also disagree that all ghost stories have ghosts as immobile. There are myths and legends about flying hordes of the dead, and in popular culture there are plenty of representations of hovering and floating ghosts.

                                                        But, more importantly, Wraith has a really vast universe, and a really treacherous one. There are enough obstacles already that a lack of travel just makes the game that much harder (and where time is limited, it eats into time that could otherwise be used for more rewarding roleplaying experiences). The alternative is that many wraiths would quickly learn one of the Arcanoi arts to travel. Especially wraiths used to the relative independence and freedom of having had a car in their mortal life. Many people drive to the corner shop now. Cultures where car use is prevalent would see more wraiths coming up with their own independent methods of travel that don't rely on the Quick or Hierarchy.

                                                        But I'll admit bias here, because I get serious commuter rage. The last thing I want when I want to get somewhere is to be travelling on someone else's schedule and surrounded by strangers.

                                                        I guess if it's really just me that feels this way, though, it's not the end of the world if I just house rule it. I only brought it up because others have pointed out similar house rules, and if we're playtesting stuff we should share our worries and concerns.

                                                        Alternatively (and this may be something that pleases everybody), maybe there can be a sidebar somewhere in Wraith 20th that offers some suggestions for non-Arcanos means of travel that are likely to be available? I feel like that information could be really useful for players and Storytellers alike (especially because the universe of Wraith requires the biggest learning curve for people to imagine out of all the cWoD games).

That is their mobility is often tied to either their Fetter, Haunt or a Relic in Wraith terms. But considering the staggering amount of ghost stories out there I admit there might be a few where the ghost fly on ghostly wings but these are usually exceptions not the rule.

                                                            But, more importantly, Wraith has a really vast universe, and a really treacherous one. There are enough obstacles already that a lack of travel just makes the game that much harder (and where time is limited, it eats into time that could otherwise be used for more rewarding roleplaying experiences). Seriously this one low level power is amazing.
                                                            But all Arcanoi aside Wraiths can always just catch a ride on any Skinlands vehicle that passes them, if someone else is boarding as well it's completely free otherwise it cost you a Corpus level to jump through the door. Aside from that there is the possibility of Relic vehicles, in which case the Argos 1 power can still help make it even faster and like said safer. All in all Wraiths still have easier time to travel than Vampires for instance their greatest problem while traveling is that they end up far from their Fetters, and this is where Lifeweb comes in handy, and should they face adversity may find themselves back at their Fetter after a Harrowing.

                                                            I've never seen this to be that problematic of an issue. You need to travel a longer distance as fast as possible? Hop a plane heading to that direction and your golden. No Arcanoi needed though Puppetry 1 Skinride makes this a more pleasant experience, as you get to enjoy inflight meal and other small pleasures of the living while traveling.
