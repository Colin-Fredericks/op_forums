    Well, I tried Wraith

    07-09-2023, 10:00 PM
    I got Wraith, a friend gave me the book. like two decades ago. I read most of it; it didn't really stick. I mean, the art is good, the writings are all the same as the other books. But it never really stuck with me. We like the movie, the Crow and Risen is an awesome book, but, it's just not interesting; perhaps just my personal-tastes. (And I feel the same way about Changeling, the Dreaming, even though it's a lot like Ars Magica).

              So for me there’s a difference between using a setting and playing a setting.

              In 30+ years we’ve never played a Wraith or Changeling campaign, however both are integral parts of our campaign mythology (Changeling with some heavy tweaking).

              From the time they were published we incorporated them alongside our own ideas, the shadowlands sit alongside the other two penumbras and the near dreaming on top of material reality.

              In our recent campaign both have taken a more prominent role, our Mage is Fey blooded with a Dreaming minor sphere so constantly interacts with the Dreaming, whilst due to currently being set in Victorian London the presence of the dead and popularity of spiritualism provides a dark backdrop from the shadowlands.

              So you might not want to play the settings but they can certainly add flavour or background.

You have to negotiate the viper's nest of politics that's been set up in the Shadowlands — a three-way clusterfuck between the oppressive Hierarchy, the revolutionary Renegades, and the devout Heretics — while trying to protect and, eventually, resolve that which ties you to this life and move on to the true afterlife.

                  Managing to resolve your fetters and passions, thus ascending out of the Shadowlands is a very attainable goal, putting it way ahead of the all but impossible existential goals from Vampire and Werewolf, the at-the-very-least highly unlikely goals of Mage, or the no-kidding-you're-screwed-no-way-out nihilism of Changeling...
