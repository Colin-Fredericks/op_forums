    Originally posted by RealDeadguy View Post
    Will I be going back through the thread and picking up old questions? If time permits, absolutely. If I don't get to yours, don't hesitate to repost.
    Will the focus be on wraiths? Yes, a thousand times yes. There will be material on crossover games and relations to the rest of the WoD because, well, it's a /world/ of Darkness, but my focus has always been and will always be wraiths. The other guys have enough books; they don't get the bulk of the content in ours :-)
    Will there be material on the Dark Kingdoms? If you add up the word count devoted to the various Dark Kingdoms in the DKoJ books, Ends of Empire, the WPG and SPG, you end up with well over 150K words. I don't think I can get /all/ of that into Wraith 20 :-) But the Dark Kingdoms will not be ignored or forgotten, and if we have some success with W20, I'd love to dive deeper into them down the line.
    Why all the Phil Collins hatred? Personal reasons. Also, even mention the word "Sussudio" to me and I'm ear wormed for a week. Damnit.
    This was the earlier response to the same question. Hope it helps!
