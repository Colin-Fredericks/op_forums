          These 20th Anniversary Editions aren't full new editions/revisions of the game lines, or rules, but rather a nod and modest updating of the game to a contemporary timeline and to combine all of what makes the game and it's core setting into one tome (or as much as possible.)

          I they follow in the footsteps of V20 and W20, you're likely not going to see a continuation events after End of Days... in fact, the Time of Judgement and much of that metaplot has been wiped clean or leaving only a shadow on the mechanics within the game, and hasn't shown in the "story" either. So... the rule book will contain the mechanics of everything, but as far as "setting" goes, expect that those "OMG, the end is here!" stuff just won't have happened and instead the end of the world was stretched out to beyond tomorrow.

          V20's SAS was "Dust to Dust", an indirect child of the original storyline of Chicago that ran through the V:tM books and pre-gen games. The rule book contains not only the lost Clans, but different versions of Clans (the different Malks), and (almost) all of the Bloodlines too.
          W20's SAS referenced the death of Sam Haight, but continued that story of "The Skinner". The rule books contain revisions to history, backed up and moved forward (the evil werelion Black Tooth is still alive, but the all-inclusive African Fera movement (the Ahadi) are around and active.

          This is all different from what the Mage the Ascension (Revised): Convention Books (everything but "Iteration X Revised") recently did, which was to finish up a line of books that were set exactly within the Revised edition, but rather than all taking place in 2004, they were updated to 2012-2013... and talked a bit about what happened after the ToJ.

                  A couple of notes:

                  - Wraith20 was announced in August at GenCon and has been on our schedule since.
                  - Wraith will be our first X20 for a game which did not have a Revised edition. We're discussing whether or not to take the opportunity to update it to Revised edition standards, or if being a nostalgia product should take precedence. So it's possible that it may end up being a proper new edition. We'll see.
                  - "Maelstrom," not "maelstorm." Common misteak.
                  - Undoubtedly we'll continue our metaplot-agnostic approach from previous X20 rulebooks.
