    Darker Days Radio

    11-01-2013, 04:25 PM


    Darker Days Radio is the World of Darkness podcast, providing industry interviews, book reviews, and player insight into the World of Darkness games, new and old. While you can subscribe through iTunes, this thread will also be periodically updated when a new show hits the web.

    But for now, why not check out our latest episode, Darker Days #52, where we cover three paranormal subjects ripe for the World of Darkness - The Alchemy of Paris, The Elemental of Leap Castle, and the Indigo Children.

                                                  Mage Across Russia
