    Question on Kuei-Jin metaphysics

    06-26-2018, 06:41 AM
    Here's a short question from me who don't know anything about the Kindred of the East, and I'd prefer not to have to go through that big book just to get this question answered.

    That is that in the 19th (and early 20th as I understand it) century lots of Chinese immigrated to the US and set up shop, among other things resulting in the creation of the Tongs which will be of some importance in my possible gangster focused chronicle in the 1920s and 1930s.

    BUT

    I have a question regarding these Chinese immigrants and Kuei-Jin. Is there any reasonable risk, or chance, that Kuei-Jin would arise among their dead or come with them this early in history? I understand that Kuei-Jin arises in "Asia" from India and on east and I know they made this move out from Asia into Anglophone aeas in the metaplot around the year 2000. But in the 1920-1930s period would there be any presence?

    I mostly plan on making the Chinese vampires as Cainites from the standard clans but I thought that I should check to got my base covered.

              Quick answer: kuei-jin are sort of ghosts that escape their local Hell and return to their own bodies and incidentally sustain themselves with the blood of the living, but they aren't related with Cainites. Therefore they don't have clans, but sort of distinction lines based on their beliefs (akin to Cainite's path of enlightment).
              Straight jumping all the metaphisic, a Kuei-jin could spontaneously rise from the asian communities, but he would be a sort of "caitiff", without no one to educate him on the way of his strain. Actually, most probably he would just stay around as some sort of wight (vampire whose Humanity/Enlightenment is down to 0 and is, effectively, a predatory beast).

              There could be some stray Kuei-jin going around in the era you mentioned, but they are just people going around to see the world. Definitely not the norm nor a common case.
