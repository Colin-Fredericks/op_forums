    Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post
    Not to be rude but that's literally why the Technocracy drove out the Sons of Ether. They rewrote reality so the Ether was no longer accepted science despite being real.
    For what it's worth, this is another issue that I have with the manuscript.

    First off, I don't buy that the Order had the power to do that. The manuscript's talk about the Union's leadership declaring that the Ether is null and void and all of the sudden Ether Science becomes Catastrophic (or, at best, Uncanny). That's a lot of mojo in a simple decree. If they can do that, what else can they make Catastrophic through a simple “make it so”?

    It runs counter to everything we've seen in M20 about how much time and effort is needed to alter a single Reality Zone, let alone making a global — nay, interplanetary — change.

    I could see that scenario playing out as the Union making a statement of intent, that the future plans for the Union no longer involve Ether and that the Etherites should sit down and shut up. That would still be plenty to outrage the Etherites and lead to them abandoning the Technocracy, especially since they had been growing increasingly dissatisfied with the Union anyway.

    And while I'm on the subject, there are a few other things about the treatment of the Etherites in this book that I take issue with. I've always had it as a bit of head-canon that the issue that drive the Etherites or of the Union wasn't actually the Union's rejection of the Lumineferous Ether; that was merely the straw that broke the camel's back, and the standard that they rallied around. The actual bone of contention was the Technocracy's adoption of Reductionism — or, phrased another way, “One True Way-ism” — as its official policy. And rather than the trigger being the Lumineferous Ether, per se, the real trigger was the introduction of Tychoidean Cosmology and the beginning of the Void Engineers’ efforts to isolate Etherspace from Conventional Space. Lumineferous Ether was merely the first step to the Electrodyne Engineers' plans to introduce the notion of Etherspace (and other related concepts) to the Masses, making space travel easier; and the Technocracy slammed the door on those plans.

    In comparison to that, the reason given for their parting in Victorian Mage feels… bland and uninspired.

    I'm also not terribly fond of the idea that they had been calling themselves the Society of Ether all along; I rather like the idea of them choosing to rename themselves as a reminder of what the Technocracy attempted to take away from them.

    *ahem* Sorry about that. It's something that's a bit of a pet peeve for me.

                        So naturally one of the Future Fates discussed is "What if the Technocracy DID accept Ether and thus the SoE stayed with the Technocracy?" One of the knock on effects being "ok, then what Craft steps up to take the Seat of Matter on the Traditions if the SoE takes it?" This Fate's answer...is the Kopa Loei. And that it goes on to say that with a bunch of Etherships in the Technocracy's battle fleet, that they're able to crush the Kopa Loei enough in the Pacific that they're forced to flee into the security of the Traditions and accept the Seat.

                        There are several issues with this:

                        1. Kopa Loei don't have an Affinity Sphere. Or at least, not one that the whole group agrees upon. This is backed up by not just their M20 core write up, but also their VM write-up they get in the next chapter. So why would they even take one of the Tradition Seats in the first place, let alone the Seat of Matter?

                        2. Canonically speaking...IC the Kopa Loei reject the concept of Spheres. They dismiss it as Hermetic nonsense and refuse to entertain anyone who brings it up. So why would they accept something they definitely do not believe in?

                        3. The Fate is trying to insinuate when it comes to Etherites staying on...that Etherships were apparently the decisive factor in razing the Kopa Loei? Like...was it really just about having more or a different kind of airships to fight them with? Because if it's about fighting in mundane reality...the OoR already has Voidships. Nothing strikes me as Etherships being easier to adapt to flying around in mundane skies over Voidships. I'm not sure if it's trying to imply that the Kopa Loei tried to flee into Etherspace/Deep Umbra and only the Etherships could chase them there but, that's not made clear in the text at all. And even if it was, again...they already have Voidships. So Etherships still shouldn't matter.

                        Nvm that in the canonical timeline, KL still would have had to have colonial violence happen to them, so logically they would have been put in a similar place, and it's weird that this Fate singles out the KL specifically and not the...other Crafts?

                        And, this is admittedly my personal interpretation: to me, the Kopa Loei were always characterized as being very isolationist and stubborn. That they would rather die then to ever allow themselves to be shackled or owed to a larger magickal organization. So to me, them throwing in the towel and seeking safety with the Traditions is OOC from them.

                        (and yes, I know them being in the Disparates is a Thing, but I also think the Disparates as a concept makes no sense, so my point still stands. but that's neither here nor there)
