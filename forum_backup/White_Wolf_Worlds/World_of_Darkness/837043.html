          Im a huge fan of all the sci fi of the 50's and horror movies of that era. Things like The Thing, or The Blob.

          so i was thinking, How would you make the WoD as something set during the 50's sci fi era, to Dm a more Tongue and Cheek/Cheesy game.
          We actually kinda did that. We called it Etherites: GO! It was an utterly pulpy and insane game about ancient tombs, giant monsters and alien invasions. The principals were all quite quirky (wo)men of Science, and the ST allowed us all one crazy over the top invention. Of the top of my head, there was an Aztec Hypertech Angel, a transformer, a village sized zeppelin, a molemachine, and a breeding project for the Creature from the Black Lagoon. Good times. Loads of fun.

          May i recommend trying out GURPS: Atomic Horror? It's an amazing resource for a game like this. There is also support for a campaign like this in the Revised Etherite book, but not as much as Atomic Horror.

              I've run games in previous eras 'straight' and playing with pop-culture jokes, including Vampire: The Masquerade in both kitsch and gritty versions of the 70s, and a 1950s Buffy The Vampire Slayer game in the style of teens-versus-monsters B-movies. (I used GURPS Atomic Horror as a sourcebook for it as well.)

              Think about how far you want to push the joke. You could describe the monsters looking like 50s-style special effects - The Thing From Another World is pretty serious but the Thing itself still looks like an off-brand Frankenstein monster in a boiler suit. Equally, the Ray Harryhausen animated ships in Earth Vs. The Flying Saucers are fantastic.

              Similarly, how much of the 'normal' World Of Darkness do you want to include? Retro-ray-gun Etherites fighting Technocracy MIBs and robots as well as threats from beyond the Umbra would be enough for a game by itself, but would you also bring in vampires in smoky jazz clubs or Christopher Lee brooding in an old castle, or have the PCs face A Teenage Werewolf? (Bell, Book And Candle, a 1950 play and 1958 film about an underground community of witches, is a very early example of urban fantasy complete with beatnik nightclub.)

              Craig Oxbrow
