    Originally posted by Heavy Arms View Post
    Ok, I don't want to derail too much in this regard, but I want to push back a little on something:

    Probability, from a scientific perspective, doesn't actually exist. It is a mathematical abstract that tries to predict complex systems that we cannot measure in sufficient detail to know the precise results of inputs into a system. In theory, the chance of everything that does happen is always 1, because if you had perfect knowledge of the system(s) involved, you would be able to determine the exact outcome without any question. Probability is necessary because we are incapable of perfect knowledge, and have to observe complex systems to see how they usually act to extrapolate what the odds are of something happening.

    Putting the majority of Entropy effects in Time is fine. Time could use the boost in low level stuff, and the scientific model of time is covers more of what the Entropy Spheres covers. It doesn't have to be a Matter/Forces/etc. though overlap there is probably something that the system will generate (and the RAW system already does).
    Minor quibble: perfect knowledge of the inputs, even when combined with complete knowledge of the physical laws of the universe, does not guarantee perfect prediction of the outcome. You still run into emergent behaviors that you can only determine experimentally by letting the system play out. A perfect simulation of the universe could still work, but that's theoretically impossible even without trying to get it to run faster than the universe itself.

    Second minor quibble: Technocrats are still Mages, and might actually try to manipulate an abstraction.

                On the naming thing: I'm operating on the premise that, as a practical matter, names of broad, general things (such as the Fields) should be short. Note that I reduced Dimensional Science to Dimension, and Primal Utility to Utility. Similarly, my inclination would be to avoid multi-word names for other fields.

                There's a principle in the Perl 6 design community called “Hoffman Encoding” which says, in effect, that commonly used things should have short names. The Fields are commonly used, and thus ought to have short names.

                The Fields are also broad in scope, which also suggests concision: usually, adjectives (as well as prefixes and suffixes) get added in when you're narrowing the scope of what you're talking about; and it's counterproductive to start with long names.

                        First time poster here. Although I'm still rather new to the franchise I have been reading extensively on the Technocracy. I believe if you wanted to have a more "Technocracy friendly" version of Entropy rather than Fortune or fate or whatever, you might think about calling it "Statistics". A key concept in statistics is that the larger the n, or sample size, the more predictive the statistic is. In my line of work we like to say that "Big Data is when n=all". That is, as technology improves and our ability to measure a wider variety of variables increases, our sample size increases to the point where our predictive models are ever more accurate. This seems to fit the technocratic paradigm pretty well; if all variables can be measured accurately, then all events can be extrapolated = static reality is maintained. A technocrat seeking to scry the stars or divine the future or whatever basically seems to be doing statistical analysis.

                                    In my first post, I mentioned that one of my inspirations for these Fields is Mage: the Awakening. I didn't go into detail about how Awakening informed my design decisions, because I've been working out the specifics. I still don't have everything nailed down; but here's a taste of what I have in mind:

                                    With the regular Spheres, each one has a unique progression all its own; to the extent that there's any sort of commonality among the various Spheres' progressions, it's entirely a matter of tendencies and trends. For the Fields, I want to go with a more standardized system akin to Awakening's Practices. As a first draft of this, I'm borrowing the Capabilities from Mage Translation Guide (page 41) and using them as a framework for defining the Fields:

                                    Field Rank Capabilities
                                    • • Senses: Detect and examine phenomena
                                    • Self Empowerment: Influence trivial phenomena related to personal capabilities
                                    •• • Minor influence: Command of basic phenomena or the ability to “nudge” phenomena, but not totally control them
                                    • Trivial Pattern Influence: Certain non-Pattern Spheres may affect Patterns in the most elementary ways. Examples: Enchant weapons, conjure objects from another place
                                    ••• • Lesser Pattern Influence: Certain non-Pattern Spheres may target Patterns with minor Effects at this rank (Pattern Spheres may already do so). Examples: teleportation, cursing machinery
                                    • Minor Transformation: Transform one minor phenomenon into another or with Foundation, manifest it out of nothing
                                    • Enhance and Repair: Improve phenomena, repair diminished or damaged phenomena
                                    •••• • Greater Pattern Influence: Certain non-Pattern Spheres may target Patterns with powerful Effects at this rank (Pattern Spheres may already do so). Examples: Convert matter to ephemera, curse living creatures with disease
                                    • Major Influence: Complex control over powerful phenomena
                                    ••••• • Major Transformation: Convert or reshape powerful phenomena
                                    • Legendary Influence: Perform a rare or exceptionally powerful feat that is normally not permitted
                                    The next step is to cross-reference each Field with these Capabilities, and to decide what each actually means, listing one or more Functions for each that applies (e.g., “Landscape of the Mind” for Space/Senses). I'm working off of the Fields list that Heavy Arms provided on the first page: Foundation, Space, Time; Forces, Life, Matter; Data, Mind, Utility. I won't necessarily be taking them in order, though; and if others want to propose write-ups, I'll welcome the assistance.

                                    I'm at work right now. But when I'm free, I'll write up a Translation Guide style comparison of the Fields and Spheres to make the task of coming up with Functions a bit easier.

                                    Note that one of my goals when coming up with Functions is to identify, but not necessarily eliminate, redundancies and near-redundancies. For instance, Foundation will include Functions pertaining to the summoning, binding and warding of EDEs; Life will include Functions pertaining to the summoning, binding, and warding of animals; and Data very well might include Functions to summon, bind, and ward digital entities. I want these Functions to have names that highlight the parallels, with the idea that the mechanics for two of them can eventually be written up along the lines of “like [the third one], but for [X]” — for instance, “like Summon Animals, but for EDEs”.

                                        Foundation: Prime, Spirit
                                        Foundation is, almost by definition, the Field of “stuff that's too dangerous for Consensual Reality”, and deals with those things that the Consensus isn't ready for. Its primary proponents are the Void Engineers; and in fact I'm thinking of renaming it as “Void” (though I'll hold off on doing that until I get some feedback). Prime-like Functions should focus on Tass, Synergy, and Nodes; Spirit-like Functions should focus on the creatures and objects found in the otherworlds. Note that Synergy lets you “tune” your Procedures to different kinds of exotic phenomena; for instance, tuning into Entropic Synergies lets you deal with entropic energies, ghosts, and ectoplasm.

                                        Space: Void Correspondence, Dimensional Science, and Forces
                                        The Space Field deals with location and motion. It is also a Void Engineer specialty — and in fact is that Convention's preferred Field. From Void Correspondence (a minor variation of Correspondence described in the Void Engineers Revised Convention Book) you get proximity-based Procedures; from Dimensional Science, you get manipulation of the Gauntlet and travel beyond Conventional Space (though these Functions might require conjunctional use of Foundation?); from Forces, you get manipulation of motion and gravity.

                                        Time: Time, Entropy
                                        Manipulating the flow of time. Can also be used to accelerate, slows halt, or reverse processes, including but not limited to decay.

                                        Forces
                                        The Field of Forces is likely to be very similar to the Forces Sphere, as I can't think of anything conceptually that warrants changing. Iteration X and the Void Engineers both like this Field.

                                        Life
                                        The Field of Life is very similar to the Life Sphere. I'm debating the possibility of allowing you to instill hypertechnological mutations into creatures (turning them into living Wonders) without needing to worry about Utility, though Utility should still be more effective at doing so: essentially, Life borrows “living Wonder” functionality from Prime, while Utility gets its “living Wonder” functionality from Primal Utility. Needless to say, the Progenitors are the main proponents of this Field.

                                        Matter
                                        The Field of Matter deals with mechanisms in a manner that parallels how the Field of Life deals with organisms. It also deals with the properties of substances, though it may not be as effective at this as its namesake Sphere is. This is Iteration X's favorite Field, beating out Forces by a hair.

                                        (In essence, the idea I'm considering is that most of Prime's Wonder-related capabilities get split between Life and Matter instead of being incorporated into Foundation, with the notable exception of Periapts. Utility would retain nearly all of the Wonder-related capabilities of Primal Utility, with the possible exception of Periapts.)

                                        Data: Data, Entropy
                                        The Field of Data can do some Correspondence-like things; but while it can be used to locate its subject and to affect him at range, its primary approach to Sympathetic connections has less to do with overcoming distance than it does with working with what the connection means. I'm still working out exactly what that entails, but it's potentially very powerful: consider Nomenclature from Mummy: the Resurrection for a sense of how far it might be able to go. Entropy's Ring of Truth and Shape Memes may well be part of this aspect of Data.

                                        Another aspect of Data involves the ability to interact with computers. This begins with the ability to access the Digital Web, and also interacts with software in a manner that parallels how Mind interacts with a person's cognitive processes.

                                        Finally, Data might include “statistics”, i.e. Entropy's chance-based capabilities.

                                        Mind
                                        I'm thinking of incorporating Bluecho's Thoughts on Mind: Grades of Intelligence into the Field of Mind. Otherwise, it parallels the Mind Sphere fairly closely.

                                        Utility
                                        More or less the same as Primal Utility, possibly without Periapts. Where Foundation specializes in Synergy, Utility deals with Resonance.


                                        I'm open to criticism.

                                                I'm very much aware of the “Gifted Science” article from MtAw's Mage Chronicler's Guide. I'm not particularly fond of overly verbose names, especially for broad topics (in general, I prefer to let the names get longer as the topic gets more focused); thus my current leaning toward single-word names for the Fields. Indeed, my thought here involves something akin to what the Perl 6 programming language community calls “Huffman Encoding”: the length of a thing's name should be inversely related to how frequently used the thing is. Put another way: if you reference it all the time, keep its name short; if you refer to it only occasionally, let its name get longer.

                                                Speaking of names: since nobody has commented on my proposal to rename what I've been calling “Foundation” as “Void”, I'm going to go ahead and do it.

                                                I'm also thinking of taking a page from MtAW's Prime to expand on the ability to manifest blades of raw Primal Energy to a more generalized “energy constructs” capability: force fields, for instance. Taking that functionality to its extreme would result in something like the “ring constructs” made famous (or infamous?) by DC Comics' Green Lantern. Forces would be able to do that, too (remember, I'm OK with some overlap, as long as it doesn't get out of hand); but it might not be quite as adept at it.
