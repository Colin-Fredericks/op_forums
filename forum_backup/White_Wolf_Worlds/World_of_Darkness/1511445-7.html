    Now, "soul" does give a sense of "consciousness".

    Because the MiB clones that are created without "souls" behave as beings that aren't self conscious.

    Could this be it? Most "lesser" spirits don't appear to be self conscious either. They don't have "free will", they just blindly follow their natures, even if they appear to be intelligent.

    Now, since it has been brought up, we now what Life 5, Spirit 5 Mind 5 does, and what it does is create a cloned person with intellect and personality and self conscious. Because it has a "soul".

    But... What if we do it to a rock? Or maybe not a rock, but rather a statue? Would then that piece of inanimate object become a "real person", in the sense that it can not only think (because even our computers can "think", which is just the capacity to process information), but also be self conscious?

    A self conscious piece of marble that has its own dreams and aspirations and don't want to become a sculpture like its father but rather loves advocacy and wants to become a lawyer to the horror of its painted father and picture mother? Could this be?

                Instead, incorporating that body and soul together into one pattern is much more complicated. It is a grafting effect and grafting effects typically require the relevant spheres of everything being grafted together.

I suppose someone could argue that [Pattern Sphere] rank 5 is necessary to give the body the exotic quality: "capable of having a soul" though I am inclined against that because that is classically a quality of living bodies.

                                        Time from M20

                                        Time is notable both in how much like Correspondence it is and how much not like Correspondence it is and it really shows how the Spheres are set up around intuition rather than consistency. Correspondence finds it remarkably easy to move things, but remarkably hard to alter space itself. Reaching across distance starts at Rank 2, but warping space is locked behind Rank 5. Time is close to the reverse as warping the flow of time starts at Rank 3 while moving through it is mostly behind Rank 5.

                                        There’s no real reason that warping space should be harder than warping time, but it is much harder to envision what warping space looks like, so it is higher. Likewise, there’s no reason time travel should be harder than teleportation, but time travel is more likely to derail plots, so it is higher.



                                        68 – At rank 1, Time initiates can sense the ebb and flow of time

                                        68 – At rank 2, Time students gain limited pre- and post cognition. It is unclear what the limit is.

                                        68 – At rank 3, the devotee can speed up or slow down time.

                                        68 – At rank 4, the adept can stop time.

                                        68 – At rank 5, the master can step outside of time, travel in time, and gain limited immortality.

                                        69 – Time combined with Matter takes objects out of the time stream. Depends on the effect.

                                        372 – Entropy 2/Forces 2/Time 2 as a description of the effect in the movie Ink. The effect is to manipulate events to cause a car crash. I have not seen the movie, but Entropy and Forces are there for the purpose of moving objects without touching them. I’m going to interpret this as moving some things directly and manipulating the probability of others, so that works. Time, though, is unnecessary.

                                        389 – Time 3 is one of the ways of getting multiple actions without penalties. This gets repeated a lot in this area of the book.

                                        417 – Time 2 and an active sensory effect in Corr, Mind, or Time lets a mage ignore the multiple opponents penalty.

                                        422 – Time 2 increases the damage of a punch by noting the perfect opening. This really isn’t a listed power of Time, and ideal moments are more the province of Entropy. However, Correspondence 1 can sense more than just distance and direction so it makes sense that Time 1 can sense more than just the time of day. A sense of opportune moments or junctures or just where things will be would be appropriate. It would not be Time 2 though.

                                        424 – Time Sphere perceptions can allegedly counter the effects of an opponent using Drunken Style. I object to this because Time 1 doesn’t do that.

                                        429 – Bullet Catching is listed as Time 3/Forces 2/Life 2. Time is stated as increasing the characters reflexes, which is a terrible way of saying slow down time. While it is reasonable to say Time doesn’t actually change the energy of a bullet, Life or Forces should be enough of an addition.

                                        438 – Time is a sphere that can be used for the Dodging the Blast Optional Rule.

                                        485 – Time and Forces have a +1 difficulty in Otherworlds. Time is unreliable in the Low Umbra.

                                        504 – Time and Correspondence inflict no damage unless combined with other spheres. It only now occurred to me how strange the wording is. As written, this means you can make direct attack spells with them, but they don’t cause any kind of damage without another appropriate sphere. It’s not a bad way of understanding what is going on, I have to admit, but it is a little strange.

                                        508 – Time 3 to increase or reduce speed.

                                        509 – Entropy 3/Time 3 to disintegrate an object. As I’ve said before, I think Entropy or Time can do this alone, but I also think the two in combination should actually do the job much better. That said there are no actual rules how. Call this one correct but less helpful than it should be.

                                        509 – Prophecy/Hindsight is Mind 2/Time 2 or Time 2. Time 2 is the only correct one here. Mind 3/Time 2 would be the combination to read someone’s mind in the past or future, which is a different effect.

                                        509 – Age or Reverse Age is given as Time 3+/Life 4 or Time 3+/Matter 2. Time shouldn’t need the help unless it is only altering the age of part of the target. If it is, the amount will be based on the complexity of the target so Life 2/4 or Matter 2/4. Also, why is it Time 3+?

                                        509 – Rewind Time at Time 3. This is also +3 difficulty, which is a really clunky way of adding difficulty.

                                        509 – Time Triggers are Time 4

                                        509 – Speeding and slowing time is Time 3. This is true, it is also the exact same effect as the effect on 508 to increase or reduce speed, just with a larger focus. I only bring this up because I think it is sometimes forgotten.

                                        509 – Time Travel is Time 5 and arbitrarily +3 difficulty. Again, I don’t like the wording. It’s not wrong because we can easily say that Time Travel is an Impressive Feat, but the terminology makes it seem like an arbitrary tax rather than a well thought out position.

                                        511 – Reprise of the time trigger at Time 4. The effect it is paired with is badly calculated but Time 4 is correct.

                                        515 – Time 4/Forces 3 to set triggers for energy fluctuations. A very useless description. If it means, transmute forces at a future time, this is correct. If it means trigger a spell based on energy fluctuations, I think it is too high. M20 is not clear on what spheres are needed for adding sensing to a spell, but it’s not going to be Forces at transmutation levels just to detect energy fluctuations.

                                        516 – Time 2/Life 1 to detect past or future states of life. Assuming this means extending basic Life senses across time, it is correct.

                                        517 – Life 5 plus Entropy 4 or Time 3 to radically age or de-age. This is just bloated nonsense. The three spheres are all capable of this on their own. Time would only need help if the goal is to preserve something like memory. Also this isn’t a substantial change of form or being, so it is an alteration not a transmutation. Even when Life is necessary it is 2, 3, or 4, not 5.

                                        517 – Time plus Matter alters the temporal state of matter. In most cases Time or Matter can do this alone.

                                        518 – Time 3/Matter 2 to move an object through Time. Matter is not necessary.

                                        522 - Note that unlike its compatriot, Correspondence, Time does not need to be as high as any other spheres in the conjunctional effect. Why it gets a pass on this and Correspondence doesn’t is anyone’s guess. It should probably be the same for both spheres.

                                        522 – There is nothing in the main Time section saying that Time needs Pattern Spheres to move things through time. Indeed in the paragraph about conjunctional effects moving things through time isn’t even mentioned. This isn’t as direct as in Correspondence but it certainly seems like Time doesn’t need help moving things through time.

                                        522 – Extending the duration of a spell until a condition is met is Time and Entropy 3. It doesn’t matter what the condition is apparently.

                                        522 – UberParadox rules for Time Travel. I don’t like it. I think it is a terrible way of handling the difficulty of Time Travel that mixes up magical Paradox with traditional sci-fi ideas of paradox. It is, however the rule given here.

                                        522 – Combined with other spheres, Time can detect the influence of Time on other spells or patterns. I really can’t see why Time needs the help. Seeing the effects of the sphere is a basic part of perception effects. I suppose it would be difficult to see that a rock that is no longer present was sent forward in time, but Time can at least see that something was sent forward and Matter isn’t going to make that easier.

                                        522 – The combinations of Pattern Spheres and Correspondence with Time 2 to view the past or future in other areas works.

                                        522 – Combining Time 2 and Entropy 2 lets a mage glimpse multiple futures and pick the one most likely to occur. This is an interesting idea, but its not clear what this means. If it means the mage can tell which one is most likely, this isn’t significantly different from Time 2 alone. If it means the mage can set which one is most likely to happen, that isn’t that much different from Entropy 2 alone. We need more information on how this works.

                                        523 – If thickening space is a justification for a Rank 2 Correspondence Ward, I have to read Thicken the Walls of Time as a justification for a low level Time Ward. This would be more relevant if it was easy to understand what a Time Ward would do.

                                        523 – Time 3/Pattern Sphere 2 to affect other Patterns. In context I interpret affect other patterns to mean apply time contraction, dilation, or rewind to other patterns and that these are locking spheres. That works.

                                        523 – Time 3/Correspondence 3 to cast effects across distance and time. Minor quibble here: While a mage would need to have Correspondence 3 to do this, they are actually using Correspondence 2 here.

                                        523 – Time 3/Life 3/Mind 1 to move back in time while recalling events from the future. The paragraph before this says that rewinding time already pulls the mage outside of the normal flow of time so in this specific case adding extra spheres is unnecessary. Also, if it was necessary it would be Life 3 or Mind 1.

                                        523 – Time plus Correspondence/Forces/Life/Matter/Spirit to capture things in bubbles of Time. Time hasn’t needed help from other spheres before now and there’s no reason to start here. Also the specific example of Time/Forces/Entropy to capture a tornado is particularly ridiculous.

                                        523 – 5 yard penalty for referencing HDYDT.

                                        529 – Spells with Time based triggers do not count towards a mage’s max # of effects.

                                        543 – Time only inflicts damage when paired with Life (for creatures) or Matter (for objects. This is correct, but the book says that the damage comes from advanced decrepitude and there are multiple ways these combos could cause damage, especially for Life. Indeed, on the next page it describes Life/Time effects as doing aggravated damage, which doesn’t line up will with decrepitude.

                                        562 – Corr 4/Time 3/Forces 2/Matter 2 to make a car go really fast. Really, Time can do this alone. Forces and Matter are just to compensate for the speed and that wouldn’t be necessary because the car isn’t really going faster, it’s just operating at a different rate of time. Since this is a player generated effect and players can use more spheres than necessary if they want I’m not going to complain about the addition of Forces or Matter.

                                        603 – Golden Gunman, Time 3/Forces 2. Time lets the operative take multiple actions without penalty while Forces eliminates and difficulty penalties from movement or recoil.

                                        607 – Bullet Catch. Same combination and same problems as on page 429.

                                        608 – Penny Dreadful’s Shiny New Penny at Matter 2 or 3/Prime 2/Time 2. There is nothing wrong with this use of Time. Only Prime is sketchy here.

                                        610 – Witch’s Vengeance is essentially a waste of wordcount as it isn’t a rote so much as an open ended idea of cursing. Time 3/Life 3 is listed as ageing the target, which either one can do alone.

                                        I haven’t usually gone into the Appendices, however:

                                        663 – An artifact that uses Life 5/Mind 5/Prime 5/Spirit 5/Time 4 to completely rebuild someone from their “save point.” Assuming Time is used as a trigger for when the person dies, its use is fine. The only real problem is that Prime is ridiculously overinflated. This is a Prime 2 effect.

                                        Mage: The Ice-ension: An Epic Game of Reality on the Rink

                                                            I am aware that you consider the sphere system to describe the underlying functioning of Mage and/or the WoD. I do not. It is first and foremost a rule system meant to allow for as many different types of mage as possible to use the same mechanics. It doesn’t always succeed, I’ll admit, but that’s what it is.

                                                            After going through the system I really can’t accept that these rules point at an underlying reality. A few do, specifically the ones that deal with a WoD specific thing like the Umbra. Most, though, make far more sense as the product of intuition, game balance, attempted game balance, and active hostility for specific effects.

                                                            That’s not a criticism, though, except for of the active hostility. The rules don’t need to be comprehensive or metaphysically coherent. They need to be understandable, consistent and useable. The very fact that I can go through and rate the entries for accuracy shows that they mostly are.

                                                            That I’ve marked so many of the entries as red indicates that they are not well explained, but that’s another issue.
