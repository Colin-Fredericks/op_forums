    Originally posted by Eldagusto View Post
    So how does Technocracy science still Doom humanity? Colonizing the stars is doable even without magic hacks.
    Well, while it might be possible *purely technically* to colonize distant systems in RL right now, it would also involve

    1) An insane sum of money.
    2) An insane amount of planning.
    3) People willing to go on a voyage that they, and a whole pile of next generations of their offspring, won't see the end of.
    4) Trusting *those* generations to actually do the job down the line, which requires them properly preparing each that follows, along with upkeep of the vessel and self-sufficiency. Those generations would need to be trained and raised for that one very purpose, not with the mindset of "I will give my offspring a happy life full of choices!". Because that generation and each that follows will only exist to maintain the vessel and prepare the next generation.
    5) Hoping that all those generations will actually be able to handle anything like normal gravity, among things, because we have absolutely nothing like a human actually born in those conditions right now; And the effects on the physiology could be immense, starting with bones and the heart not growing to handle proper gravity.
    6) Hoping that any repairs in this timespan necessary will not actually make the rest of the journey impossible, because there is no simple turning back. We already have a problem of modern computer systems being currently affected way too much by interstellar radiation, which is why so many computers up there are quite old models.
    7) Hoping that supplies last, even with the ton of recycling, re-use and potentially necessary growth of food involved.
    8) The will to do something like this, knowing that *we* on Earth also would (maybe) get to know the result of this whole endaevor only a whole pile of generations later, if we don't simply forget about it.
    9) The pretty sure knowledge that the humans that might manage to colonize over there have as much in common with us as any society that, for hundreds of years, was separate and isolated from us.

    ---

    EDIT-BONUS:
    Also, since it's such a long trip, we would have to make the choice what technology we would pass along, including exact descriptions of how it works and how to build it, along with the principles behind it. As well as anything cultural we want to give them. And all of it would definitely need paper form along with multiple digital copies.

    Because those that would maaaaaaaaaybe manage to arrive there, and then maaaaaaaybe colonize there won't be able to reproduce multiple key technologies that are standard for us. For a long time. Like..well. Computers. Those that they have on arrival would be priceless artifacts.

    ---


    So. It might be, in theory, technologically possible right now. But the way we would have to do it, *right now*, gives us absolutely zero reason to do so and would be an incredible risk and investment, to make a big understatement. All with a very very very high chance of "Well, never heard of 'em again".

    Let's try Mars first. That's already a trip with incredible hardships for generations to come after arrival, if we ever plan to colonize, and many chances for things to horribly go wrong.

And when you try to create too much density, you get a black hole.

                    This has been adressed, but to add remember that Technocracy are *the* Mage Hunters. Even if they were saints (which they most certainly are not) they would be your main concern if you were a Mage (and not one of them).

                    Remember, the World of Darkness is supposed to be a darker, more depressing version of our reality, which means that it's very unlikely that a group as powerful as the Technocracy are doing their thing in the interest of humanitarian ideals. They have ulterior motives, and it probably involves using us as sacrificial pawns of some kind.
                    Oh, they're very idealistic but very corrupt. Power corrupts and all that. They don't want humanity as sacrificial pawns, they want to control humanity as an enlightened elite. They want humanity to remain safe and cozy under their boots. For our own good, of course.

                        We can see pretty clearly in real life that tribalism is the biggest corrupter and danger to the modern world, not genuine fanatics for evil ideals, which are extraordinarily rare. Tribalism corrupt capitalism into crony-capitalism, because the first winners of the capitalist competition favor their family and golfing buddies, gradually accumulating more power, squelching competition, and creating a de facto oligarchy. Tribalism corrupts communism into Stalinism and near-Stalinism because those bureaucrats in charge of distributing the wealth favor their families and golfing buddies, need more and more power to protect the position that allows them to do this, and this spirals into a bureaucratic oligarchy. Tribalism corrupts democracy into demagoguery because people vote to favor the visible short-term interests of themselves, their families, and their friends over a more educated and nuanced view that takes the interests of humanity as a whole into account rather than the immediately visible tribe.

                        Never blame fanatical ideals when tribalism is the simpler answer. Tribalism and pragmatic nihilism are the most consistent predictors of human behavior on a macro-scale.

                        Technocrats care about technocrats. They care about their own power and wealth and the power and wealth of their friends and families. Other groups threaten that, either directly or by offering humanity other choices.
