    Kinain mages

    09-02-2015, 09:58 AM
    Hey folks,

    So I'm reading up on kinain mages, and there are a few contradictory systems. There's the Faerie Affinity Merit, the Fae Blood Merit and then the system for kinain in The Enchanted (which would appear to be just for unawakened mortals).

    I'm wondering how Mage STs manage this? Do you allow PCs to take Fae Gifts and Fae Marks? What about Kinain Backgrounds? Can they or can they not use cantrips (my feeling is that Awakened mages can't use Glamour, which prevents them using cantrips)?

    I posted this in the general CWoD forum, but no one replied, so thought I'd try here.

                  I'm not particularly experienced with Changeling, and I'd probably need to do a lot more reading before I had a Fae Blood character in one of my games. But that said, I think that Fae Gifts could be workable depending on the Gift in question.

                  I think that I would first discuss the nature of the character's fae blood on character creation, and have the player choose spheres that fit accordingly with the nature of the fae ancestry and the gift that he wants the character to have. If, for example, the player wanted the gift to speak to spirits of the dead, I might not give him the Gift outright, but I would expect him to have significantly invested in the Spirit and Entropy spheres, and I would reduce difficulty of any efforts at dealing with the Wraith and the Underworld. I would also remove Paradox as a factor with regard to the "gift" within reason. (I always reserve the right to tell my players "Okay, you're taking this a little too far." :-))

                          A cocaine chimera called Laughing Charlie? I love that so much!

                          Here's the stuff I wrote for the RPoL wiki for our game. Can anyone offer any feedback?

                          Glamour and cantrips: Sorcerers, psychics and mages can all be kinain. However, only unawakened kinain may use cantrips (and may take traits in Arts, Realms and Glamour) as they are under the system in The Enchanted. An Awakened mage cannot have a Glamour rating (because being tied to the Dreaming would prevent their Avatar from Awakening), but can learn cantrips as if they were rotes, and power them through Spheres.* Similarly, any Fae Gift or Background that requires the use of a Glamour trait is off-limits to mage characters (Fith-Fath, Riastradh, etc), but rotes can be developed to provide similar powers with Spheres.

                          Character creation: The character creation rules in The Enchanted are for mortal kinain only. You should create your character as a mage or hedge mage first, and then use Freebies to 'add on' the fae characteristics. You should consult The Enchanted for the specific rules on kinain-specific traits.

                          Apply the following template to modify your kinain character:

                          1. Create a mage/hedge mage character as normal, remembering that chimerical reality and/or their fae blood will be incorporated into their paradigm. The character's Avatar essence might also be informed by their fae heritage (a Sluagh-descended kinain may be Primordial, while a Satyr's relative may be Dynamic).

                          2. Take the 4-point Merit: Faerie Blood. This will grant the character a single dot in the Faerie Blood Background. Alternatively, you can take the Faerie Affinity Merit, which gives you a lower Banality (one dot lower than normal) and makes changelings more friendly, but does not allow the character to take any Fae Gifts or Kinain Backgrounds, nor to interact with chimerical reality.

                          3. Note your Seeming and Seelie/Unseelie Legacies. Pick a Heritage (Kith). Your Banality rating will be the lower of: a) the Banality rating for your Seeming in The Enchanted; or b) the Banality rating as defined in the Character Creation for Mages section (I use the lower scale presented in CTD, where mages have Banality ratings of 2-7 for the most part).

                          4. You start with no free Fae Gifts or Kinain Backgrounds. You can spend Freebies to take any Fae Gifts and Kinain Backgrounds you like (provided you already have the Faerie Blood Merit), but you may not take more dots in either of these than you have dots in the Faerie Blood Background. So if you have Faerie Blood 5, you can take up to five separate Fae Gifts and up to five separate Kinain Backgrounds. These tricks are Paradox-free. Kinain Backgrounds can only be bought from your Freebie pool, rather than from your normal Background dots.

                          5. Fae Marks can be taken to gain additional Freebie Points.

                          *In general, awakened mages who mimic cantrips can consider chimerical effects to be coincidental and Wyrd effects to be vulgar. Hedge mages never take Paradox from cantrips, of course.

                              Addendum: I'd probably clarify the overlap between Glamour and Quintessence, too. For example, an item can contain (or, more correctly, generate) Glamour without holding Quintessence. A picture, for example, would inspire Glamour that a changeling could harvest. But a mage looking at the same picture wouldn't be able to harvest Quintessence (they'd have to wait for the changeling to harvest the Glamour and then steal it from the changeling).

                              Similarly, a site of Quintessence might not generate Glamour in the same way as a freehold would for a changeling. The changeling would probably draw much less Glamour from a node than a mage would draw Quintessence, for example, because it's not in the form she's used to receiving it in. However, a mage could easily draw the Glamour from a freehold as Quintessence, albeit Quintessence with a particular Resonance (and the potential to cause side-effects), because mages don't really require their Quintessence to be refined.

                                                      Arise, necrothread! Arise!


                                                      On topic, when it comes to Merits that blend a Mage with another splat (Ghoul, Shapeshifter Kin, Fae Blooded, Medium, etc), I gravitate towards the option described in the Mage Revised Storyteller's Handbook. That being that the Mage is able to exploit the Mythic Threads created by their connection, and use Spheres to create Effects that mirror those powers ascribed to the night folk by popular culture. And have those Effects be Coincidental. I would personally rather use that option in a Mage game, rather than having the characters track their understanding of a whole other splat's supernatural advantages. It's easier from a book-keeping and systems perspective, and sidesteps the need to split experience between different kinds of powers. The mage is a Mage, and thus does everything using their Spheres.
