                                    Life 5

                                    ••••• Transform & Create Complex Life-Forms/Perfect Metamorphosis
                                    Now the Master of Life may adopt any form he wishes to achieve and may transform other complex organisms the same way. His expertise allows him to make permanent changes to life-Patterns, create complex life-forms from energy (with Prime 2), give them consciousness (with Mind 5), transmute them into other elements (Forces or Matter 3) or raw energy (Prime 5), radically age or de-age them (Entropy 4 or Time 3), or instill them with spirits (Spirit 3 or 5). Without such measures, however, his creations remain mindless, soulless sacks of life – alive, but nothing more.
                                    Transform Others (Transform Complex Life)

                                    Transmogrify Others (Perfect Metamorphosis)

                                    Transmogrify Self (Perfect Metamorphosis)

                                    The culmination of the Alter Self and Alter Complex Life tracks. At this point a mage can manipulate, change, and transform lifeforms in near limitless ways. There is no given limit for size or any other attribute.


                                    I Must Scream…

                                    There was a striking description from one of the Abyssal Exalted books (2nd ed I think) of a Necrotech airship kept aloft by screaming mouths. That is, the mouths on the top were convinced they were suffocating and sucked in air at a furious pace, while the ones on bottom constantly screamed the air out.

                                    Well at this rank, that kind of Biotech engineering becomes possible. Of course, there would need to be considerably more modification for the mouths/lungs to put out enough force to support themselves, let alone an airship, but that’s also within the scope of Life 5.

                                    I have no idea why any mage would want to make this idea work, but it is a good illustration of the level of creation possible at Life 5.

                                    A less horrific version is the Skewer from Expedition and the third form creatures from Tremors, both of which produce their own chemical propulsion.

                                    His expertise allows him to make permanent changes to life-Patterns

                                    Ambiguity Resolution - Permanent Changes

                                    This is one of those descriptions that should be clearer than it is. Per normal magic rules, duration can be made permanent with enough successes. There’s not usually a rank requirement. Except that Life and Matter both mention permanent changes at Rank 5. Now there is one other part of Life that mentions Permanent Changes, and that is Pattern Bleed. So, the one thing we can be certain of is that Life 5 allows changes that bypass the problem of Pattern Bleed. As for the rest, I see three options.

                                    Option 1 - The most unsatisfying is that Permanent Change doesn’t have anything to do with permanency and is solely a tag to overcome Pattern Bleed. I’m not going to spend much time on this other than to say that it would be terrible game design if permanent didn’t in any way mean permanent.

                                    Option 2 - Life (and Matter) are special and permanent spells are not possible until Rank 5. This is a reasonable approach. That said, if this were the case, I really would have expected it to be said directly somewhere.

                                    Also, by the methodology set forth at the beginning we’d need it to be said in the Base Life section. Life 5 can’t tell Life 2, 3, and 4 what they are not capable of. But that's just a limit on how we look at the rules in this thread, not how we look at them in general.

                                    Option 3 - The duration rules are about the permanence of spells. Life 5 is talking about a permanent alteration of the life pattern. The spell is over and done and the change is the new normal. This has the advantage of making sense within the context of pattern bleed. Permanency ends Pattern Bleed because the target’s form actually matches their true pattern.

                                    If you have any other options, let me know. I’m going to write this up based on option 3 for now.


                                    This is My Life Now (Perfect Metamorphosis)

                                    A mage can change a target’s pattern permanently. Unlike spells of permanent duration, there is no magic keeping the target’s pattern this way, and this cannot be dispelled or unwoven because the magic is over and done with already. The new pattern is simply reality now.


                                    …and I Have No Mouth

                                    “I am a great soft jelly thing. Smoothly rounded, with no mouth, with pulsing white holes filled by fog where my eyes used to be. Rubbery appendages that were once my arms; bulks rounding down into legless humps of soft slippery matter. I leave a moist trail when I move. Blotches of diseased, evil gray come and go on my surface, as though light is being beamed from within.”

                                    Of course, the permanent transformation at the end of I Have No Mouth and I Must Scream is possible at this rank. It is unclear if the narrator is simple life, complex life, or something unknown, but that doesn’t matter at Rank 5. He also appears to be functionally immortal, and though he has no eyes he can tell what he looks like, and these too are possible at Rank 5.

                                    Conjunctional Abilities

                                    Create Life (Transform Complex Life + Fuel Pattern)

                                    This is such a well-known power that it barely needs description. By adding Prime 2 a mage can divert quintessence to create an entirely new pattern. One important element is that at this rank these created life forms can be made permanent in a way that rank 3 could not accomplish.

                                    Instill Mind (Transform Complex Life + ???)

                                    This is getting into a hazy area of Mage because it isn’t automatically obvious what it takes to attach a mind or a soul to a body and what sphere is doing what. That will probably need its own section. For now, this is likely a grafting effect, hence the need for Life 5 for complex creatures.

                                    I’m at a loss for the next two. They both seem like something that the supplemental sphere can do on their own. We’re looking for some kind of effect that can only be done with both, or at the very least will work significantly better if both are involved.

                                    Contradiction Resolution – Prime/Raw Energy

                                    So what exactly is intended here with this ‘transmute them into other elements (Forces or Matter 3) or raw energy (Prime 5)? On its face, this seems like a standard transmutation of complex life into some other form, essentially destroying the person and turning them into something else. Life 5/Forces 3 and Life 5/Matter 2 work for that.

                                    Transmute Complex Life to Elemental Substance (Transform Complex Life + [Other])

                                    Standard transmutation effect.

                                    Contradiction Resolution Continued – Prime/Raw Energy

                                    This doesn’t work for Life 5/Prime 5, though. Converting someone into quintessence and sending it back wherever it goes would be Life 5/Prime 2 at most. Also, Prime 5 can actually pull all the quintessence out of a person on its own. I actually can’t think of an application for Life 5/Prime 5 that could reasonably fit ‘turn someone into raw energy.’ Any ideas?

                                    Added: monteparnas pointed out that Life 5’s impossible/exotic life ability would mean it can transform someone into another substance while keeping them alive.                                     There’s a similar problem here. Shouldn’t Life 4 be able to age or de-age on its own. And shouldn’t Entropy 4? To be a conjunctional effect this should achieve something neither one can do on their own.

                                    Missing

                                    Create Exotic Life (???)

                                    At Forces 5, mages should be able to generate Life patterns with impossible physics (like Matter can make substances with impossible physics) and Life patterns with completely alien biological principles. The former would include people with organs that phase through each other, allowing impossibly large organs to fit inside of them by overlapping. The latter would include silicon-based biology and non-nucleic forms of genetic storage.

                                    Everything Else (???)

                                    As with all Rank 5s, Life 5 should be able to do just about everything possible within the Sphere’s purview. The existence of Archspheres could change this, but they aren’t listed in M20.
