                                Hey folks! I'm Travis, the M20 Sorcerer developer. Sorry I'm just now getting over here, but I wanted to thank you all for the discussion, sharing your impressions, and especially for submitting errata. It helps immensely. A couple things popped up throughout the conversation, which I do want to address just for accuracy:
                                • The list of Backgrounds available to Sorcerers is on p. 104. Certain affiliations open up some additional backgrounds as well, noted in the section.
                                • The Simplified Aspects rule (p. 14) is meant to offset the difficulty of achieving your Path's potential. This is also the rule I personally prefer and use.
                                • Yes, shapeshifting is supposed to be that difficult. Turning a person into a chihuahua without breaking the rules of existence ain't easy. Yes, it's easier to give yourself cat's eyes or ears with illusion than Shapeshifting. It's very much intentional to the design.

                                I also think there's space for a lot of fun to be had in a campaign where starting sorcerers use full aspects, then as they improved they shift to the Simplified Aspects rule. We are doing this on the actual play I'm running and I'm excited to see how they take the shift next session (though only one spell was cast in session one)

I submitted errata on this, using Conjuration as an example: as written, if you don't get four successes (one for the effect, and one for each of the Aspects), you don't get to select even one object to conjure; you don't even get the minimum “less than an ounce” weight for that subject; and you don't even get the “within a few feet” distance for the Conjuration. Miss any of those — again, as written — and nothing happens.

                                            Something that you just brought up which wasn't clear from the text is that I didn't get the impression that you could take aspects other than the ones mentioned for a given Path.                                                     I think what's needed is to treat the way the aspects listed for each path are handled differently from any additional aspects that might be added on on top of them. You should get at least one level in each of the required aspect from the single success needed to make the spell work at all; and I might even go with having all of them operate at your rank in the path from that one success. The optional aspects would start at zero, and I would have no problem buying those up at one level per success.

                                                    That is: the first success on a conjuration roll would give you your rank in conservation as levels in all three of number of targets, weight, and distance; if you choose any additional aspects, those would start at zero and would have to be bought up one level at a time with additional successes.
