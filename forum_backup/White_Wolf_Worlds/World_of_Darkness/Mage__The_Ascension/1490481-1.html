                                So, my copy of Technocracy Reloaded spent quite a while in limbo and the physical one still isn't with me as I sent it to a buddy rather then to myself. This thread made me take a look at the pdf. There is actually quite a bit of text in the book about what the Technocracy thinks of sex, relationships and gender identity:
                                • Technocracy realised they had an issue with toxic masculinity from 1900s to1980s
                                • Chauvinism and machismo were seen as unproductive and wasteful and efforts were made to correct it
                                • No restrictions on sexual preferences or gender identity
                                • Only effective operatives are the ones who are comfortable with their identity, therefore technocracy offers hypertech gender-affirmation surgery to help their members with issues
                                • The former is actually seen as decadent and corrupt by certain Traditions
                                • Sexual preference stigmas are officially forbidden in the Technocracy
                                • There may be certain situations where the Technocracy restricts who you can be with (cowerkers, special mission types)
                                • Birth control and STD prevention/control for all
                                • Having children is limited by requiring a certain rank in the Technocracy
                                • Citizens have no limitations on having kids, meaning that low level mages are the ones most impacted by this
                                • All the above stuff - treatment, birth control and gender-reaffirming procedures - are available to the Citizens! "Happy healthy, satisfied workforce tends to be an extremely loyal workforce"
                                • Stigma and harsh punishments for any form of sexual violence
                                • Children are a priority and anyone in Technocracy is expected to educate and help with them
                                • Cannot date direct superiors or direct subordinates, harsh consequences
                                • If the other side is eligible, relationship status needs to be reported, and no sex before paperwork
                                • Sex =/= Relationship
                                • Sex and polyamoury are quite common, especially in Iteration X and NWO
                                • So is asexuality - be it due to being processed, traumatized or not built for it, asexual people feel especially comfortable here
                                • Syndicate is sex crazed and tends to ignore rules about relationships (except rape), love of abusing power dynamics,sex is a weapon and domination is a mark of success, this makes other conventions wary of them
                                • Progenitors have a almost creepy fascination with the mechanics of procreation
                                • The Void Engineers are both geeky cosmonauts and the horniest technocrats alive (also sex with extradimensional beings can make men pregnant)
                                • Marriage is only allowed for high rank technocrats with high loyalty, no boundaries on gender, sexual identity or number of people you are married to
                                • You get to work with the people you are married to and usually get joint assignments
                                • Joint quarters and
                                • Married people get licenses for kids more easily, also parental leave
                                • Partnership is the "simple" variant of Marriage without the bureacracy, less concessions from Technocracy
                                • Divorce is handled by giving the sides involved separate quarters and a mediator to establish if there is any damage to the Technocracy, relationship counseling is offered if it can help, if not communal belongings are shared equally unless there are infractions or abuse, Children become wards of the Union (many Technocrats prefer to stay married for this reason)

                                ... Yes, THAT IS A LOT OF WORDCOUNT if this is just a summary. Check it out.
