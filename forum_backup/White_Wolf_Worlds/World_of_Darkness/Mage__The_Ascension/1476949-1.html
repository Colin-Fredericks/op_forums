                                                            A vampires with Stamina 1 is actually worse off than a mage with Stamina 5, despite the vampire's soak applying and the mage's not. 1 die of soak isn't great, and Stamina 1 leaves the vampire extremely open to getting Stunned, while the mage is extremely unlikely to get Stunned. So the vampire is at serious risk of being forced to just stand that trying to spend WP and Vitae to do anything at all before their assailant stabs them into torpor, while the mage is at a slightly higher risk of death if they don't act fast, but they can actually toss everything they have into a spell to do something about the situation because they're not stun-locked.

                                                            These things are more complicated than what kinds of damage you can soak (and lets not get into the complexity of the Garou and different soaking by forms).

                                                            I think one of the reasons a lot of us are somewhat baffled by the insistence on sticking to the WoD, is that there are already systems that better facilitate this sort of idea.
