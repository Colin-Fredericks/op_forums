                                                    If Mage's history started with some multiverse/etc. phenomenon, there is no explanation in any of the books about how in the Dark Ages a consistent reality formed, and then in the Renaissance Reality Zones had distinct metaphysical impact on magical practice from place to place. Meanwhile the Reality Zones the Order of Reason created so they could then merge them into the Consensus they wanted explains just fine why there's evidence of all these alternate mythologies (because the local Reality Zones created them) without the multiverse approach.
