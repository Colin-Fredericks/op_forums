      Personally?

      By focusing on plain, simple humans that suddenly can do magic, and are trying to cope with managing life, mage politics, duties, trying to hide their nature from sleepers, and studies of new mysteries, with a dash of saving X from doom. Without going into the whole 'Zomg we are all ruthless, magickal combat veterans in an endless war' spiel.

      People tend forget that there are mages who don't see a single mundane violent conflict in their lifetimes, and others that do, but are better at simply running for their lives.
      Combat needn't be a bad thing in the roleplay, but people way too often tend to act like every paradigm of every mage allows them to suddenly drop their enemy into magickal bulletinfernodoomspacehell, and that every mage is a cold blooded warrior that just goes ahead and does that because they can.

                                  In my game, I go with Revised's timeline as well, storm and all (though it's potency has waned a bit).

                                  The way I've done is by having the Technocracy acknowledge the heavy losses that the Ascension War has dealt them and is now shifting focus away from outright killing mages, and instead belittling and incentivising sleeper interest in the paradigm of Science. It's become a war of ideas rather than a war of guns.
                                  Because reality zones are a thing, the Technocracy are trying to expand the Scientific zones and erode at or outright remove any other zones with conflicting beliefs. This is they idea behind enforcing every child going to school and normalising college, that all countries receive western medicine, and that western style journalism becomes the global standard.

                                  The Tradition mages have restructured and got their New Council to work on, so politically speaking there's still a lot of work to be done in figuring out their own infrastructure before organised attacks can be even thought about let alone coordinated. As the Avatar storm still rages, albeit less so, Space exploration is still considered far too risky for either group, especially because of the constant threat of Nephandi, Marauders, and Threat Null. For the Traditions, this too means that creating Horizon realms is more trouble than it's worth, though that doesn't stop a number of hotheads from trying (and getting pretty far with it).

                                  That's how things are on a global level, but at a more local level it really is a case by case thing depending on the specific interests of the Technocrats and Mages within the local group. They might even start working together in order to combat a bigger threat.

                                          Have any of us considered Peacemakers? That is, powerful Mages - on both sides of the conflict - who encourage their sides to make nice, for the sake of peace. Maybe they desire peace for its own good, maybe they have a vested interest in preventing more fighting. Maybe they've just gotten used to the idea of a cease fire, and want to protect that status quo. Regardless, they work to smooth over conflicts and discourage aggressive actions. And to act as intermediaries between sides, to broker peace.

                                          This can happen with talking with more hawkish elements, pulling rank, or even cutting deals.

                                          "Look kid, I understand your feelings. But this vendetta is only going to make innocents suffer, more of our guys buy it, and make the streets run red with blood. How about, if you drop this obsession, I give you access to copies of valuable arcane material? I know you've been looking to work your way towards Master training. I can make that happen..."
