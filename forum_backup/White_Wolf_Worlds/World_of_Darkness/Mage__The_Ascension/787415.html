    Funny moments at your table

    12-16-2015, 12:09 PM
    I had a splendid time running M20 for my group and we had a hilarious moment at the table I felt it would be a fun community post to share some of the hilarious moments from our respecive tables. Without further ado, the funniest moment from my groups first sit down with M20

    >ST'ing M20 with seasoned players
    >Throw Tzimisces at players
    >One player vocalizes the horrible realization that none of them put any points into Occult or a Esoterica
    >Players begin to dread this fight since they know what the Tzimisces can do but their poor characters have no idea what's about to happen

    10/10 would make my players regret overlooking vital abilities

          A few game sessions ago in the Mage game in which I'm currently just playing, I was playing an Etherite who was a Technocrat in everything but name, with Sanctum 3. The rest of the party -- five mystics and a gutter magick Adept -- due to circumstances beyond their control ended up having to bunker down in his sanctum.

          Then they got the bright idea to do magick inside his sanctum. Then they learned the downside to being in the sanctum of a Technocrat in everything but name. Then they kept trying to do magick inside his sanctum. There were like five or six Paradox backlashes in the course of two game sessions; one PC backlashed twice before getting the hint his character's paradigm was not my character's paradigm.

          It was like watching a little kid dual wielding forks against every electrical socket in sight. I laughed for a cumulative twelve hours straight.

              Our resident monster verbena was talking to his friend (now a malkvian) about how during his 10 years of absence he (the malkavian) was dating his sister.

              The DM played the malkvian beautifully as he talked to the verbena and in a moment when asked by the verbena why the vampire was dating the human the malkvian says

              "Dan, you dont understand......" a sad and pregnant pause filled the table before i screamed

              "DAT ASS!"


              Same verbena was fighting a group of sorcerers shapeshifters for control of his node. The leader of the shapeshifters was the previous student of the verbena master (who was an ex-nazi mage), the verbena had it on the ropes and when the shapeshifter is down the master approachs the verbena and offer him his sword cane as he was about to start a speech to temp the verbena to the "dark side" when the verbena says

              "Thanks, i was about to have to do it with my barehands" and proceed to stab the sorcerer 30 times before turning to his master and asking "so what you were saying?"

              From there on the tagline of that character was "he is a complicated man". Why does he sleep with his cousin and had a son? He is a complicated man. Why did he make a cult made of teenagers to sell drugs? He is a complicated man. Why he made a deal with a vampire to spy on his family? He is a complicate man. Why he decided to make an ent with the spirit of the San Fernando valley that could destroy los angeles? He is a complicated man.

              Post time skip, he left for 10 years and his barely legal cult evolve into a full blown organization and in the building there was a statue of the verbena stabbing the sorcerer with a plaque saying "Our leader.....he is a complicated man"

                                              Mohsen instantly becomes the cool guy of the chronicle.
                                              Oh, I have one funny-ass one for "breakout" NPC's. In my Sorcerer's Crusade game, the Void Seeker of the group had a retainer -- the boatswain of his void ship. Since the chronicle was set in colonial America (they were sent to Jamestown to establish a Lodge), they were almost immediately set upon by the local Black Spiral Dancers, and the Dreamspeakers hiding among the local tribes "NOPE"'d out of that fight since there were way bigger threats on the horizon (like Roanoke island).

                                              Queue the pack of werewolves shifting into Crinos form, and a truly astounding series of failures and botches on Occult rolls from all around. As a last-ditch effort, I gave the boatswain an Occult roll with his mighty three dice pool...and scored a success. Out of nowhere, he shouts "'e's a werewolf, cap'n! Use silver!" and clocks the werewolf tearing his boss a new supernatural asshole, on the back of the head with a silver candelabra...and one-shots the fucker.

                                              Which, needless to say, pisses the rest of the pack off who all turn on him. The boatswain proceeds -- thanks to shit rolls on the BSD's part, and really good ones on the boatswain's part -- to hold his own and take a second werewolf down until the rest of the party can Matter up and kill them. One of the other players asks, "who the hell is this guy and what is his name?". Not having figured that one out, I said the first thing that came to mind, having just seen Avengers that day:

                                              "Coulson. Bo'sun Coulson."

                                              The players in the group reacted with utter glee and hilarity, and Bo'sun Coulson went on to be the biggest badass NPC of that entire game, wracking up a supernatural body count Flambeau would be proud to have. Nothing could kill that guy, and I never flubbed a single damn roll -- to the point my players actually called bullshit on it during one game session, and I rolled everything right on the table in plain view. At one point, thanks to a little help from the party's Cosian, he took a full-power Qlippothic Rip the Man Body and all it did was piss him off.

                                              He died at the age of 130, as "the Grim Reaper was too afraid to collect his due and had to wait 'till he was too frail to fight back", and became a legend that survived through the ages as to what Extraordinary Citizens could do, and to the level of excellence they should all aspire.

                                                          Paradox was always behind the funniest Mage moments in my games. Our MtAsc game was always held at the home of one of our players, in a huge rec room that held couches, tables, a pool table, a suit of armor, and an Irish Catholic family of 8's worth of old board games, comic books, action figures and trophies. Because Paradox Flaws and Spirits were treated like free pulls on a slot machine, there was a LOT of Vulgar Magick thrown about-- too much for me to come up ahead of time with enough inventive ways for Paradox to manifest.

                                                          That's when our Hollow One player came up with the Paradox Comet.

                                                          The Paradox Comet was a mid-to-large sized superball with a tail made of mylar streamers. When a Paradox Flaw needed to manifest, someone would throw the ball as hard as they could at a wall, the pool table, the heavy bag, etc., and it would bounce around like Han's blaster shot in the trash compactor. Whatever it came to land on would inform the nature of the Flaw, Spirit, or Realm. Here are just three of the funniest results:

                                                          1) Progenitor bigwig is fighting HTH with our Akashic, taunting her as he counters her Do-channeled Rotes with mutagenic manipulation of his body with coincidental Life effects. Akashic swallows her pride, and defends with straight Do as she works her oldest Rote, a Mind 2 effect to urge the Progenitor into using Vulgar Magick. It works, and the Techie botches a clearly Vulgar hypersteroidism Life effect. THE COMET IS THROWN, coming to rest on the pool table, next to the 12 ball.

                                                          So the Progenitor's testicles undergo instantaneous accelerated growth. He literally sported 12 balls. This was obvious, because (A) his clothing had already been shredded to nothing, due to hulking out, multiple limb growth, etc. Also, since he had been established as actually being 120 years old, he was subject to (B) the Old Dude Scrotal Sag effect (dudes who hit the gym will know exactly what this is), but on a ridiculous scale.

                                                          Guess where the Akashic aimed her free kick? Hint: I awarded her a x6 damage multiplier.

                                                          2) The Hollow One botches a Spirit roll while attempting to cash in a debt from an Umbrood who owed the previous owner of his Avatar some serious Tass. THE COMET IS THROWN, coming to rest at the base of a cheerleading trophy. As a result, he is assigned a Gaffling who manifests as a relentlessly peppy high-school senior cheerleader, and who will parse out the outstanding Quintessence as requested, though each request had to be specific, and made in the form of a "Give me a Q!" style cheer. The word "Quintessence" has a lot more letters than most Mages realize...

                                                          3) Knock-down drag-out megabrawl with a Pentex strike team. The Hermetic finds himself 1-on-1 with a Nephandus he's fought before, and who he absolutely hates. Quintessence stores are emptied. Willpower points are burned. Vulgar Entropy/Life (Anti-Life Equation ray) and Vulgar Matter/Prime (Quartz Contagion blast) are cast simultaneously.

                                                          The Hermetic rolls four dice: 1, 1, 1, 4. Even with a Willpower point, he gains 4 Paradox to add to his 4. This is Backlash/Spirit territory.

                                                          The Nephandus rolls five dice: 1, 1, 2, 1, 1. Burning a WP still gains him 5 Paradox, on top of his 8 points. Definitely Backlash/Spirit-worthy, but his Qlippothic Correspondence lets him vanish a split-second before the penalties are paid...if he makes his roll. He rolls 3 dice: 9, 1, 1, and he can't skip out on the Paradox Spirits he's angered, as he's done in the past.

                                                          The Hermetic's COMET IS THROWN. As it's in mid-air, the Hermetic's player remembers a Chantry perk that he owned, enabling him to sacrifice a permanent WP for 2 successes (MY will be done, etc.) He is allowed to burn another WP, so his net gain is zero. But the spirits he called down cannot be un-sent, so a pair of crystalline angels coalesce into being. They note his extant 4 Paradox points, and will purge them from him in the form of Flaws. The Comet is hunted for and found. The tail rests atop a box of Lemonheads candy, while the ball is sitting on the box from that Christmas' cheese/nut log. The Hermetic's leg turns to pub cheese, crusted with slivered almonds. His head balloons to resemble a great, grotesque lemon.

                                                          The Nephandus' COMET IS THROWN, plunking onto a clipboard with Girl Scout Cookie preorder sheets. Frantic with terror at his long-delayed punishment finally coming due, the Nepnandus shrieks as infinite girl scouts emerge through infinite doorways, and proceed to taunt and kick the Qlippothic Mage with Primium shoes, silencing his screams with infinite fistfuls of Umbral Samoas and stopping up his eyes with Etheric Do-Si-Dos. By the time it was decided that they were tearing away bits of his corrupt Avatar and fashioning them into merit badges, much Mountain Dew had been coughed up through many nostrils due to hysterical laughter.

                                                          CODA: Limping home in victory much later, a recurring pair of Vhjunka invaders (nicknamed Kang and Kodos) slid into existence, spied the Lemonheaded, cheese log-legged Hermetic, shrieked "MARAUDERS!" and immediately retreated, closing their unholy portal with an audible series of "locking" sounds.
