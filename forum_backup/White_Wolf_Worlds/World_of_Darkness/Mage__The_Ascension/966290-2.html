    Originally posted by Ajax View Post

    This. 100% this.

    When Technomancers became playable and then again, when our players started choosing to play them, this whole "Technocracy says there is no magic" had to get kicked out the door, on its ass, into the gutter and get run over by a mack truck.

    Effectively, this requires a paradigm all it's own - "The Technocracy is willfully stupid in a way that makes their objectives much much harder than they otherwise would be". Effectively, Room 101 would require a revolving door and every time a Technocrat encounters any sort of supernatural manifestation, they would need to be brain-raped back into drooling idiots. Which becomes impossible when you consider that most Conventions actually have a division or sub-group (if not a Methodology) that is all about "protecting Humanity FROM the supernatural" in whatever particular form that might take. That's a cognitive dissonance it's just not possible to encompass.

    "What's our mission in their division?" says the new transfer.
    "We hunt PLEs"
    "What's that"
    "Post Life Entities"
    "Still not tracking"
    "The non-physical psychic remnants of the deceased. They exist largely in an adjacent dimension with a higher entropic coefficient."
    "So what do they do?"
    <Insert description of such activities>
    "You mean... they're ghosts?"
    ALL BRAINS IN THE ROOM SHORT-CIRCUIT. (Since EVERY Technocrat knows ghosts are "magic" and don't exist.)
    A door opens up and Room 101 tenders wheel in gurneys, load the agents onto them and wiskthem away for their 4th brainwashing of the day....

    The replacement is oh so easy. The Union knows what magic is and what mystics do. They know they do the same thing. They know that THOSE guys are doing it all wrong. They are unsafe. They are like handing plutonium to high school students to build A bombs as science fair projects. WE (the Union), do it the right way, the safe(-er/-ish) way, and we do it for the right reasons for the ultimate benefit of all humanity (instead of personal aggrandizement or the desire to monkeywrench the world back into the Dark Ages).

    Eh... That's a rather extreme way of looking at it. The Technocracy are just like the Traditions in terms of Will equals Effect. Similarly they are just like each Tradition in that every single mage beleives what they are doing is the only "real" form of magic, those with methods that are close are kinda there, but those with completely contradictory methods are just plain wrong. If a Spirtual Dreamspeaker and an Techi Etherite talk, Neither of them will consider what the other one is doing is actually all that effective compared to their own techniques. The Dreamspeaker would think that the Etherite was just making a lot of things up on the spot, making a lot of noise and talking a lot of nonsense about science and machines. Sure the effect will be palpable, put the Dreampeaker wouldn't really see a difference between them an any other scientist. The Etherite would, funnily enough, think pretty much the same thing about the Dreamspeaker, that they're babbling on about spirits and mystic stuff, and harp on about old superstitions that don't really have much merit to them. Sure they can see the effects they produce, but to them it's unreliable and wishy-washy. There's nothing these guys do with their houdou vodou that can't be done better and more reliably with a good machine!

    The same it true with the technocrats. Sure they observe the effects of the reality deviants. and acknowledge that they are able to do quite astounding things. But where the Traditions go wrong is by trying to claim all of it is mystical. They say that the spirits are helping them, that it's all in between the numbers, that it's about changing the universal source code, or whatever flimsy explanation they might have. The fact that none of their explanations line up even with fellow members of the same tradition is just proof that everything they're saying to explain their effects is just nonsense. Sure the magic is potent, but these mystics don't understand what they're doing any better than a Sunday driver understands the inside of their own car.

    That is what set's the Technocratic Union apart from the rest of them, they have internal consistency. While Etherites will argue about whether empty space is Ether vs Dark Matter, all members of the union will agree that it is the Cosmological Constant which holds the fabric of space together, and when the time is right, they will be able to utilise this fact to produce new experimental procedures, slowly introduce the ideas to the masses, and then, slowly or suddenly, incorporate them into the zeitgeist where they become mundane.

    When a NSC talks about Post Life Entities, they are doing so not because they "Don't believe in ghosts", but because the term "ghost" is muddied with so many contradictory definitions based on culture, superstition, and heresay. The Akashics may speak of ghosts like a fox who can turn into a lady and eats the hearts of humans in order to steal their skin, of they may speak of women jilted in life becoming horrible spectres of hatred, with long black hair which obscures their face. The Euthanatos would speak of ghosts as beings who are simply waiting to reincarnate, and are only temporarily trapped until they can pass on. The Celestial Chorus would speak of ghosts as unfortunate souls forever trapped in limbo, or even purgatory depending on what they are taught, awaiting judgement until the end of time. While Etherites have countless theories on ghosts and what they are, some consider them to be simple emotion patterns left as a footprint on the mundane world, others would consider them to be the very soul of a human stripped of a body, other's still will state that ghosts are just memories, kept alive by the combined cognotive resonance energies of the human's who knew the deceased.

    With all these convoluted ideas attached to the word "ghost". The Union does away with this nonsense by rebranding them in purely analytical terms. When one NSC talks about Post Life Entities, they can be assured that everyone in the room knows exactly what they are talking about without any preconceived notions muddying up the picture. They are talking about an Extra-Dimensional Entitie which is native to the Shadow dimension, more formally known as Entropic Space. That's it. No talk of sentiments or purpose, simple clear cut analytics. That's how the Union get things done.
