            But we do not need to wait for the Consensus because the technology for space colonization has existed since the 1960s (ever since the first flight of the Apollo Program). NASA has had the technology since the 1960s for Orbital Power Satellites, Moon Mines, Mars Colonies, etc. The only thing that has prevented space colonization has been political opposition, not technological difficulties, so the Consensus does not matter because the technologies have existed for fifty years. It is the opposition of the Union (through its control and influence over the political process) that is the only thing in cWoD that kept the Masses from space colonization because it fear the Dream of Space (and I would like a book and page number for the population of the void colonies if you would not mind).

            And when we are talking about crossovers, the Consensus is nothing but a magical artifact (fueled by the suffering over an ever increasing human population) that was created by the Technocracy in order to commit mass genocide against the magical creatures of Reality. In a crossover, Outer Space is Outer Space, Reality is Reality, and Science is Science, and the Technocracy is a genocidal order whose goals make the Nazis seem unambitious. In fact, the only difference between the Nephandi and the Technocracy is that the Nephandi are more honest with themselves about their eventual goals while the Technocracy uses double speak and mind control to keep its members from understanding the evils that they are committing.

            When it comes to Outer Space, only Mages are incapable of accessing the Deep Umbra within the Asteroid Belt and only Mages are incapable of accessing Outer Space beyond the Asteroid Belt. Fera are capable of visiting three levels of Outer Space (the Aetherial Realm, the Deep Umbra, and Outer Space) and, through traveling the Roads of Balor from the Dreaming, the Changelings are capable of similar explorations of Outer Space as the Fera. It is the beliefs of the Mages that prevent them from exploring as much of Reality as the Fera and the Changelings (and the Changelings get to explore the Dreaming as well).
