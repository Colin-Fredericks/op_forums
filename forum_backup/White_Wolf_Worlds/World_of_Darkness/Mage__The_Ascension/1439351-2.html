            One thing about M20 is it it is deliberately more open in terms of how it works then the other x-20 game lines. The unofficial tagline is “it's your game, do as you Will”.

            With that in mind, Threat Null can be thought of as what happens when Control is severed from earth, and co-opts Conditioned Technocrats it finds in the Umbra to use as a reclamation force. So while I don't feel the same need to have the technocracy be an evil fascist regime, if you want that you can easily justify it by saying that Threat Null succeeds in its mission, and Control reestablishes its dominance over the Technocratic Union.

            or you could go with the notion that the Avatar Storm never happened in the first place, and Control was never isolated from Earth to begin with.

            It's your game. Do as you will.

            My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

                                            Originally posted by Erinys View Post
                                            Peace, love, and understanding are the highest of goals, but the paradox of tolerance puts a hard and impassible limit on carrying it out. &quot;Common ground&quot; isn't &quot;common&quot; unless it's actually about tolerance.

                                            Just as the Mysticks in Mage can never find common ground with Technocrats (excepting VEs, apparently) as long as their goals include Pogrom/genocide, cultural eradication/forced conversion, and dictatorship. If the Mysticks and nonhuman sapients are still fighting just for their right to exist, they can't offer to make friends and shouldn't be expected to. Mysticks don't necessarily care about the rights of nonhumans, but they want to survive.
                                            Right. But there's always the danger that you only think that someone is intolerant, possibly due to having fallen for propaganda yourself. To me, that's a big part of the horror of Mage: the Hubris. Everyone is so cock sure that they are in the right that it becomes far too easy to rationalize violence and oppression in the name of tolerance: “they're the intolerant ones, so let's squash them like bugs!” at the very least has built-in irony.

                                            And even if the other side does consist almost exclusively of liars and dupes (which I never take as a given without first thoroughly vetting the issue), is punching a dupe in the face really the best way to go? You might argue that you don't have much of a choice, because they're doing bad things and need to be stopped. But is that actually true, or is it just another rationale? Might it be possible to get through to the dupe and get him to change his mind? (Of course, that way potentially lies the equivalent of Room 101, if you're so sure he's in the wrong that re-education is warranted. Be careful when fighting monsters that you don't become one yourself.)

                                            In terms of the Technocracy and the Pogrom, how would you know? We've been told that they stopped the Pogrom in MRev for logistical reasons, not out of ethical concerns; but if they had stopped out of purely ethical concerns (“the guys who were in favor of the Pogrom are gone! Now we can stop that nonsense!”), would the result have looked any different, in character? I don't think it would.

                                            And what if the justification is a mixture of ethics and logistics? How much of it needs to be ethics-based before you can accept that the Technocracy may not be evil monsters anymore? And again, how would you know what their reasons are? Are you going to break into various Constructs, abduct the Technocrats there, and mind-scan them in order to get a representative sample of what their motives were? That would be both impractical and unethical.

                                            So is it okay for the Traditions to automatically assume that the Technocracy is still evil after the Pogrom ends, or is that merely an understandable but still wrong assumption for them to make?

                                            My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness
