    Could I get some people to poke holes in my Head-Canon?

    06-19-2017, 07:10 PM
    SO, I'll admit I'm a bit of a latecomer to mage. And while I've somewhat voraciously torn into the setting there are some books I don't have, and some knowledge about the setting I simply lack. For example? Voormas's Exploits. I know some of what happened but fairly little.

    Anyway, My Head-Canon largely relies on a couple simple things.

    1.) The Anthropic Principle/Subjective Reality.

    For this Head-Canon to work, Reality must fundamentally be determined by what people believe. There cannot be any deeper secret of 'how reality works' than 'this is what people believe and so it's true.' This one is fairly easily justified, given the setting as a whole. Everyone is a mage, just not everyone is aware of it.

    2.) Extra-Temporality.

    Time. It's a bit of a murky sphere for obvious reasons. But this whole bit of thinking is dependent on the fact that time is much like space. Things that existed in the past exist still. Just somewhere else. This one is a bit harder to justify, but ties in with the above.

    3.) Mages can, to a degree, subvert the Anthropic Principle.

    Again, basic bit of the setting. Mages reject the reality created by the masses and form their own. Allowing them to steer reality if their vision is compelling enough to the sleepers.


    In essence, the idea is, that in the past. Humans were scattered. Separate. They came up with many different beliefs about the world, and accordingly, the world accommodated them due to the Anthropic Principle. The wilderness's were vast, terrible and full of monsters because that's what people thought they were. Some people awoke to the idea that things didn't have to be this way. First they made simple things. You could move thoughts from one mans head to another, by using noises. If you rub sticks together it makes fire and fire can fight off monsters. Etc Etc.

    These people grow old and die, and their children carry on. Maybe making up new things. Maybe disregarding what their parents believed entirely. But the consensus still includes their parents. The Consensus is not bound by time. And while parts of it are malleable, the 'universal constants' are those things that mankind has known to be true for time immemorial. The oldest ideas don't just rely upon the mystical might of those living today, but the force of every avatar of every person who has believed that idea from the dawn of time. This is why Spirits exist, why they have powers outside the modern consensus. Because animism is one of the oldest faiths of mankind. Spirits literally have untold billions of sleeping avatars behind them. Believing, "Yes, they exist. Yes, they can do this." This is why the Historical Constant exists. Because if you attempt to change the past, you are getting hit by EVERY SINGLE PERSON WHO'S LIFE WOULD BE CHANGED. Even unobtrusive things, You say "I want to be in the corner store on January 1st, 1944" Everyone who was there is smacking you with a counterspell every time they remember that moment for the rest of their lives and remember you not being there.

    And this also shows WHY the Technocracy is so hell bent on controlling the information of the masses rather than simply wiping out the magical community. If the Historical Constant is the weight of the forgotten dead trillions holding up the old ways. That means If the Technocracy can control the future generations, eventually they will have their consensus gain power to break it. Gaining the ability to rewrite the dawn of time. Compared to that, killing one more hermetic is incidental. If they want to play with their toys and snap up a random eccentric here or there, that's overall, ok. Technocratic control of the modern consensus on the other hand.... that's the cornerstone of their strategy. If they lose that the game is lost.

    Does this make sense? Or am I overthinking this?

                                      All of this philosophy inspired me to create a theory of my own...it takes the things to a more, let's say "mystical", and less subjective, perspective, but I will leave it anyway:

                                      We tend to think about time in terms of past, present,and future. But in truth we colapse time in past and future. Present it's just an instant, and in the argument of who pulls stronger, past or future, that small middle ground it's negelgible.

                                      However, the common binary conception of time it's not the only way to think this concept. Greeks had three different words for time. Our conception of time as a river that flows from past to the future (or it's the other way arround?) was understood with the word Cronos. Kairos and Aeon where the other two words that meant time, pertaining to very different concepts. I will concentrate on Aion.

                                      Aion, it's something like what we call eternity. But eternity in the sense of Aion it's not an endless amount of time, nor does it references the infinite space/time continuum-all of that it's Cronos.

                                      Aion it's eternity understood as something that exists, litaraly, outside time. As such, Aion it's simultaneous, and foraneous, to all Time and everything that implies. it's the true "Correspondence Point" for Time. Plato went so far as to say that Time(Cronos) it's but a shadow of Aion.

                                      Now, keeping that in mind, let's begin:

                                      Imagine that Magick, the Consensus, and thus (in a way) Avatars exist trully outside, above, and beyond, Time. This actually seems plausible, after all, you can't "ascelerate" your magick with Time (magick tend to follow esoteric rules that transcend Time to an extent).

                                      Imagine the Consensus as something that it's simultaneous to ALL TIME. This is even compatible with the multiple timelines and "not written future" theory, because eternity can easily encompas every possible timeline, even if these are strictly infinite...
                                      This Consensus of all Humanity compressed in less than an instant, abarcating the whole of Time, the Consensus Beyond Time it's something indistinguishable from THE ONE. Past, present, future, all are one in Yog-Sothoth the Consensus

                                      The Consensus exists outside time, and being outside time the People agreed with itself on the basic rules. In a way you could say that the Consensus of the "future people" sat to talk with the Consensus of "past people" leaving their guises and treating each one as an equal part of everything-not before time, but outside time-and ageed on certain things.

                                      They agreed on the concepts of Time and Space as we understand them (time flows from the past to the future), among other things like the existence of Prime and the human nature (and the nature of the spirits if you want to include them as part of the Consensus).

                                      It's not that an alien created the rules (in spite of previous jokes, that's not exactly my proposal). My idea it's that the cero-sum of everything that the People can possibly conceive in all eternity created the rules. The sum of all human belief as could only be seen by an hipotetical someone that exists outside time and can see the belief that every being held at every potential era will determine if the time has to flow from the past to the future, if the future isn't written, etc.

                                      In setting the rules, the Eternal Consensus fragmentation was the only logical conclusion. The existence that outside time it's compresed into eternity and absolute power it's the multiplicity that exists within the flow of time and space.
                                      It's not that the One "died" to create the universe, but rather than the One it's just the sum of the belief of untold beings understood as something that that transcends Time, and thus can interact with it from the perspective of someone that can see the beguining and the end of Time. The "fragmentation of the One" it's an apt methapor to understand that the One and the Multiple are the same.

                                      If you want the future to be uncertain, no problem, because, as I said, the Eternal Consensus can easily colapse into infinite potential universes that may or may not become part of the current wheel of time, but "exist" as "what ifs" (that can be explored as Everet Volumes).

                                      This theory agrees in the idea that the Consensus created everything, but a few things where created from a point that's outside time and outside the individual egos of singular people, like the human nature, and thus won't be overriden (unless they were meant to be). They're "Consensual" in a sense that's far beyond the power of any mage faction to manipulate. Maybe with Time and Prime Archspheres at 9-10 (or the all mighty 6) a mage could start to understand it's enormity, and what would be necessary to manipulate it (if possible)
