    So theoretically a Mage uses Spirit to evoke your character to have a passion [Serve Master X (5)] and orders you to fight the Technocracy, how would a Wraith fare against the fighting against the Technocracy? Reading the 2nd Edition of M:TA it says that Effect attacks that are seen whether coincidental or vulgar can be dodged with a regular Dex + Dodge. This is all under the assumption the Wraith is Embodied fighting the Technocrats

Assuming the Void Engineers aren't called in of course. Note aswell that due to the Passion and the Wraith doing objectives to take down the Technocratic machinations are purview of its master, it would be getting a 5 dice chance everytime to gain more Pathos to fuel Arcanoi.

                  Myes. The Wraith might quite possibly be able to suprise the technocrats the first time - generally speaking, the Technos don't tend to have Ghost problems on their home turf on average, simply because hauntings there are probably exceedingly rare in the first place.

                  That said, it also depends on what kind of technocratic facility we are talking about. Just some offices acting as a front for a cabal? A military instalation that hides out some serious Hypertech experimentations? The question wether or not the Wraith will be able to wreak havoc highly, *highly* depends on what the actual target is.

                  Also, the Matter sphere won't apply - Wraiths emotions are covered by the Mind sphere. Now, one thing that shouldn't be overlooked is the *Prime* sphere. Prime - or rather the force it controls - is the the very building block of reality. If the wraith crosses the shroud and is actually present in the real world, Prime *will* be able to damage it. Nastily so. And the Prime sphere is not restricted at all; NWO agents or Iterators with prime-charged ammunitions might be a threat.
                  But again - the presence of the same once more depends on the installation in question.

                  So, going through some of the appropriate spheres,

                  * Dimensional Science/Spirit - Mostly in the hands of VEs, but yes, there are exceptions; Still, the chance of encountering it is way lower than Technocrats wielding other enlightened sciences.
                  * Mind - Actually probably a BIG danger here, as the NWO specializes in this through and through, and NWO agents are never far in technocratic locations - unless it is a location mostly held by a single Convention, like a Progenitor lab.
                  * Prime - There's always a chance of encountering this across the board. In a combat scenario, in the form of aformentioned charged special ammunitions most likely. However, such ammo is costly in terms of Quintessence, so definitely for 'special situations' and accordingly restricted - unless the Wraith crosses a member of the Syndicate. Don't let the mobster pull out their Little Friend.

                  EDIT:

                  Actually, the best strategy for the Wraith is to take it *slow*. Like days. Weeks. Don't go full on 'Freaky ghost terror'. Be subtle. Cause accidents. Cause malfunctions at the worst of times. Depending on how much the Wraith knows about the details of the Technocracy (Chance is next to zero unless their Mystic master tells them...) the Wraith can use the paranoia of the NWO *against* them. Listen in. Find out which of the Technocrats might be rivals. Cause accidents that seem like one offed another. Focus on single targets and slowly whittle down their resolve. Do not make it obvious that a haunting is taking place unless when its time to release the brakes, and then make a hasty exit and let the Technocrats scramble to figure out what happened.

                              Shakanaka
                              ​
                              1º Let's cleanse some misconceptions:

                              So don't Technocrats almost always need Foci.
                              Yes. Always

                              and don't (but can't because its against their philosophy) use Vulgar magic?
                              They can and do use Vulgar magick, but will try not to. They have no more limitations here than Traditionalists, but it won't look good on their reports. Especially if it's in front of witnesses, "masquerade breaches" are as serious deal within the Technocracy as within the Camarilla

                              The thing is: Vulgar magick may not be Vulgar inside a Construct/Chantry because it most likely counts as a Reality Zone (and even if it *was* Vulgar, it likely won't be a "masquerade breache" there).

                              Moreso, frying a Wraith in the Dark Umbra/Shadowlands isn't Vulgar because most magick casted within the Umbra (even if the mage it's casting *from* earth) it's Coincident.

                              If you want to take advantage of Vulgarity you would need to Materialize (and even then it may not work inside their instalations)

                              What possible ward out of nowhere could an Agent use to stop a very combat or stealth oriented Wraith from getting close to the Installation (Satellite, coincidental Turrent, etc.?)?
                              Any "science looking" stuff would do. It doesn't need to look common unless it's to be used outside. It will likely be a machine of some sorts, but it doesn't need to be, a bureucratic memo could do it if the Ward was made by those rare NWO agents who have mastered DimSci. In general, Foci are too versatile to be called a weakness.

                              Beside VE, I thought Technocrats don't use Spirit that much - and if they do, they only ever have it at 1.
                              Levels 1 and 2 aren't *terribly* uncommon outside VE. Only the ItX were forbidden from learning it (and in Revised that law was lifted), the rest just don't care much about it, hence they rarely learn more than a few levels.

                              With an "influence" (Sphere) that low, they would only be able to sense if very close.
                              Sphere levels at 1 can detect anything in the range of sight. Combined with Correspondence 1+ they can detect anything in very, very, large areas. Because Corr isn't rare at all, you can't really take this for granted.

                              If this was a really OP or easy tactic against them, Dreamspeakers would have obliterated the Technocracy long ago. The fact that Dreamspeakers, Euthanatos and other EDE-using mages hate the guts of the Technocracy means that summoning entities only affected by DimSci (like a wraith) to haunt their bases isn't unheard off. There's probably a protocol for that in most Constructs.

                              It's important to remember that the technocracy it's a Union. Technocrats are rarely so broad in their powers as Tradition mages, but they compensate this by relying on other mages. Technocrats "in general" may not have DimSci, but VE work day and night to protect all Constructs from such "extradimensional" attacks. Even if they aren't there *personally*, there could be Wards, special cameras or other items to deal with this problem

                              And that's where Ambrosia's statement really comes into play:

                              Originally posted by Ambrosia
                              The question wether or not the Wraith will be able to wreak havoc highly, *highly* depends on what the actual target is.
                              A powerful Construct or any one that was intended to fight the Traditions will be hard for a Wraith to infiltrate. That place it's going to have VE OR VE-tech to protect against this kind of incursion because Traditionalists DO this thing a lot, and thus it would be impossible for them to survive w/o propper EDE defenses

                              A recent/low level Construct or one that wasn't intended for the Ascencion War MAY not have strong anti-EDE protections. This is the ideal target for a slow, subtle, infiltration where you pit one Techncorat against the other. If you start "misteriously" killing people, it's not going to last long until someone calls the VE.

                              A front where most of the operatives are human?. Feel free to camp there as much as you want. However, again, if people starts having gruesome deaths the larger Union WILL appear to cleanse the place.

                              In general, a haunting on a Technocracy base it's not going to last long (more or less will depend on how careful you are about revealing your presence, and the kind of attacks Technocrats expect), but perhaps small acts of sabotage or a sudden, strong and quick surprise attack it's all that you need to do in order to fulfill the desires. Because most Technoctats DO have a blind spot there, it's likely that you can get away with it (at least once).

                              That being said, I recomend not to be overconfident. These are mages, in the hand of STs that's basically "plot devices": If the ST wants to screw you with them, he has every tool to do so within the rules.
                              As for strategies:
                              Techncorats themselves may be too protected by Mind to make puppets of them (NWO sure are), but most likely the mundane/sorcerous staff isn't protected, and they actions would be less monitored.

                              It may be worth to note you *can* Inhabit Devices (not sure if it would work on conscious devices like the Hit-Mark, though)

                              Fatalism it's subtle, and that's good for many strategies (can be countered/detected with Entropy, though) but unless W20 made changes or you're using a Fatalism 5 power I don't know, my understanding it's that what Fatalism 5 does has little to do with Wards. Maybe a very good roll with Argos could be used as "countermagic" for Wards (though RAW countering a ritual it's nigh impossible. But if I was ST I would make so you can give a shot). Still, let's not assume a Ward until we see one. Sometimes you don't need to pick a lock to open a door.

                              Your best strategy may be to try to be subtle within their instalations, and only try to kill Technocrats when they go outside (preferably in ways that would seem like an accident, bonus points if they look like an accident from the PoV of a Time user too). The less they know, the better.
