                  Awakened magick was not under a threat in M20, which made the point that the “threat” that it was supposedly under in MRev was more a matter of shell-shocked mages in the immediate wake of the rose of the Avatar Storm assuming the worst. M20 emphasizes that magick is alive and well in the modern world, possibly even moreso now than it had been before the turn of the century.

                  As for making the game less mechanically confusing, there are simpler solutions than wiping out dynamic magick; especially since the Sorcery rules are just as complicated as the Sphere Magick rules, just in a different way: switching to them wouldn't simplify the game at all.

                  And as Tuch pointed out, if something like that were to be implemented, it would kill all interest from the existing Mage fanbase. It would also be the opposite of what V5 did: like it or hate it, V5 was an attempt to take Vampire back closer to its roots as a game of personal horror. Writing M5 as “Sorcerer, Third Edition” would be going wildly afar from Mage's roots, not back to them.

                                                      Looking at the current circumstances it appears that:

                                                      a) M20 still has a fair amount of mileage left in it. It was only released to the public in about 2015, and there are still supplements in the pipeline for it.
                                                      b) Nobody has bought the license to develop a M5 game as of yet.
                                                      c) Modiphius doesn’t appear interested in any line outside of V5.
                                                      d) Hunters Entertainment have only opted to develop W5 as it stands.

                                                      I can’t really see a new edition of Mage happening for at least a couple more years, therefore. If it does happen, it may be the case that its connection to V5 and W5 might be less fully intwined as in previous editions. There will still be a WoD connection, but the references to other games may well be more downplayed.

                                                      Speculation can be fun, nevertheless, but I’d suggest that linking The Second Inquisition to the Technocracy and developing mages to be like sorcerers could make some good sense in V5’s setting, it wouldn’t necessarily impact on any future development of the Mage game proper. Indeed, there is no guarantee that anyone will see a new edition of Mage.

                                                          Assuming they'd try to take M5 in a similar direction as V5 (which they may not--who can say?), I think they'd curtail magic heavily, but I seriously doubt they'd go to a Zero-Dynamic-Magic(k) setting.

                                                          That said, Revised was already an attempt to undercut Masters (read: "elders") and make the game more about street-level play, so truly the only way to go more 'down-to-earth' with it would require doing pretty extreme stuff: power levels and what can be accomplished could be capped (at the high end) at around the equivalent of having four or five dots in spheres, with none higher than Rank 3; as well as something akin to a ban on vulgar magic (i.e., rather than being more difficult, or punitive to perform vulgar effects, you simply can't do them at all). Paradox would now function like Hunger Dice, with casting literally any magical effect being equivalent to rousing the blood, and not being able to get to zero unless you've neither used, nor had magic used upon you, for a 24-hour period or something like that.

                                                          I've made no secret of the fact that I'm not interested in WoD5 to the extent that it follows in the footsteps of V5 (and why wouldn't it--they're committed to the course), but this is not me trying to poo-poo V5 or the new creative direction--I'm just starting from Mage Rev. and asking myself, 'okay, how do you scale this back even more'?
