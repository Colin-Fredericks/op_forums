        Sure, the Technocracy has surveillance everywhere in the developed world. But all that data is useless if they don't know what to look for. And Traditionalists do not register their identities and magickal affiliations with some central Mage authority. They pop up everyone - Awaken anywhere - and get recruited by whoever happens to find them first. By default, the Technocracy does not know about the existence of any given individual Tradition or Craft mage. Not unless they are given reason to know that a particular person - in an enormous sea of people - is one of their ideological opponents. Only then will they have reason to look through the mountains of surveillance data, let alone find anything in it.

        This is why laying low and being discreet can allow a Tradition or Craft mage to operate in the middle of a major metropolitan area, while escaping the Technocracy's eyes. Walking around town wearing wizard robes makes the stand out, so the mage doesn't wear them outside their Sanctum, save in circumstances where they need to draw upon the Mythic Threads far afield.

        Except "wearing robes attracts attention" isn't actually necessarily true either. Weirder crap goes on in New York subways and streets all the time. Ren fairs and fan conventions further populate the land with people dressing up in elaborate costume. After a while of this, the Technocracy has learned to tune out "false positives" in their surveillance data. They have to, or they'd spend an unacceptable amount of time and resources chasing dead ends. Indeed, this is how the Traditionalists make predominantly Technocratic areas amenable to Traditionalist presence: by invading their sober, serious reality with a dose of casual madness, that is perpetuated by Sleepers.

        On the flipside, Traditionalists have not been idle for the centuries they fought the Technocracy. They build wards to mask the presence their Nodes. They erect "Ignore Me!" fields around their homes and on their persons, so people don't notice them. They use simple Forces rotes (or Bafflejacks, as detailed in M20) to disrupt electronic surveillance. They use Entropy on their credit cards, so their financial transactions get harder to trace. You don't even need to be a technomancer, like the Virtual Adepts or Society of Ether, to interact with the technological world. Mystics, despite their reputation as regressive and unchanging, have been adapting their methods to modern problems. Most mystic Traditions have a faction devoted to blending their arts with tech, for exactly this purpose.

        And mages spread these rotes around, operating a brisk trade of tricks to dodge the Technocracy. If only to have more product to trade with, it spurns on further rote development.

            It needs to be remembered that the Pogrom is not in effect anymore. Or maybe "not in effect again yet", who knows.
            Either way, the Technocracy currently isn't on a "kill or process all Traditionalists" trip, and more on an "Observe, calculate threat level, Reality Deviance and statistical probability for paradox. Subvert or assault if they become an actual projected Problem"

            Magus McMagick practicing their craft in private and meeting in their circle of deviants isn't going to have much of a problem from the Technocracy. They will be observed if noticed, but that's about it.

            However, if they begin to try and influence sleepers in a public manner, the Technocracy will pull some levers to counteract that - mostly subtle levers. Deals falling through, required permits being really strict, sponsors for events jumping ship...such things.
            Also plain out threatening the Mage's relatives wellbeing, or putting the Mage under threat of losing their home and job ( Or even better, threatening to make the relatives lose their home and job.. ) can do *wonders* if the Mage's character is realistically fleshed out and still has a mundane life.
            Let's not kid ourselves. Even if you suddenly can do magic something has to pay the bills, you still love your normal friends, your family, like the home you have, and nobody wants to apruptly lose the mundane life they still can manage. Most people'd not be near the 'lol okay I use my spheres to make something new' kind of shallow.

            Only if the Traditionalist and their fellow ilk become an actual threat that can't be contained through more mundane means and influence do they send in the heavy hitters.

            It's more cost effective, too.
            And the Technocracy works much better in most stories as an ever-present oppressive element, than ActionMen In Suits that you need to fistycuff on a regular basis.

                                I have the view the Technocracy should also be thought of as an organization which is not actually in CONTROL but doing STEERING.

                                The Technocracy does not control the CIA, much less the United States of America. The NWO has agents in the NSA and taps all of their information but the vast majority of NSA employees work for the NSA not the Union. But its members probably include the guy who advises the guy who advises the President. Not enough to Mind 4 dominate POTUS into complete obedience (and risk massive paradox backlash) but enough to do some Mind 2 sympathetic magic which will work a bit in the long run to get their agenda passed through Congress if they do one or two successes every month for years. They also use Sleeper lobbyists and cash like everyone else.

                                The Syndicate has wires in Wallstreet so they can "Foci" causing the crash of stock or raising of it but it doesn't own maybe more than 1 to 3 Fortune Five Hundred companies. It invests in the other 497 (as well as launders its magically generated money through them) but Pentex is the group which actually bothers running day-to-day company policy for its possessions. The NWO has the ability to call the cops and have your license changed so you're a registered sex offender and wanted for murder as well as rape. It has better things to do than actually employ cops, though. The Syndicate can electronically alter your bank records and have funds transferred to theirs. However, the banks are almost inevitably run by Sleepers. Hell, the group more likely to control your local cops, news network, and bank is the Camarilla versus the Technocracy. The Technocracy is global while the Camarilla is feudal.

                                The Technocracy is a (comparatively) tiny cabal of mages at the heart of Western civilization with a few thousand operatives as well as researchers for each Convention. It just APPEARS huge because it hides in the WOD's genuinely massive secular capitalist 21st century society. The Men in Black aren't just their agents but also the FBI, SIS, Interpol, and NSA and anyone else who actually does the grunt work.


                                The Technocracy would also rather let 100 mages get away than risk someone discovering them. While it's good copy to send a bunch of Hit Marks to slaughter a bunch of fairies on paper, the simple fact is that those Hit Marks have to come back to a Construct somewhere. If that gets found out, then that Construct is compromised as well and will get an endless number of Marauders after it.

                                As such, for my Technocracy, they prefer to go soft if they can (try to buy the land, intimidate the fae into leaving, or yes stir up trouble with Dauntain) before launching D-Day. They have enough trouble with the Traditions, Nephandi, and Marauders before borrowing it from a bunch of reality deviants playing LARPs (even if they do have a node worth capturing). A game against the Technocracy would be built better about why the cops suddenly believe the place is full of drugs and endangered children with the goal of finding the ONE Technocracy agent involved and shutting him down.

                                I call this the "1st Edition Mage where combat isn't the focus" portrayal.

                                Mind you, it's still a bad idea to **** with them but they're a threat best thought in terms of influence rather than rulership. Even they sometimes forget it and their belief helps shape reality--it's just there's maybe 10,000 of them and 7 billion of us.
