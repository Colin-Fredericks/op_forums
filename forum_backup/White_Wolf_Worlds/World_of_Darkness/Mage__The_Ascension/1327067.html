    Overlaping instruments

    07-23-2019, 05:25 PM
    A post from another thread I created made me think about effects that can be made by more than one instrument. And the more I think about it, more I realise that almost every instrument is capable of producing almost any kind of effect.

    In M20, the player must choose at least 7 instruments. This means that probably the player will have more than one instrument for several effects.

    Is that right? How often this happens with you, do almost any of your effects can be cast by seversl different instruments?

    The post was this one:

    Originally posted by Gearbox View Post
    Since the instruments are so fuzzy I prefer to add rules to them to keep them easy to role play, to do so I usually write small paragraphs about each one and the possible abilities they may key of when using mundane skills to improve magic.
    I'll write a bit about my Akashik Sister, Jade, and her paradigm, practices and instrument as an example.
    Her paradigm is bring Back the Golden Age, once heroes and powers roamed the earth, but no more, but all hope is not lost since through hard work, careful brews and strife one can can purify and refine oneself to reach heights to match those ancient powers.
    Her practice is Martial Arts to channel the hard work and find strife and Witchcraft to brew and refine potions and make crafts to help her body.


    Her instruments are:


    Meditation, she uses it for large workings and rituals, is versatile with every sphere. this keys off meditation. Not useful to fast casting


    Martial Arts as a way to prove herself through strife that she is an equal to the legends gone by, this helps with forces entropy life and prime spheres.keys off with brawling, melee, martial arts or do.


    Bodywork is another instrument, coming from the need to perfect her body, With her control she makes her body/pattern asume certain forms. useful using life, mind and prime spheres. keys of esoterica (body control), athletics or acrobatics.


    Energy is her use of the mystical ki that flows in her body and that of others through careful control of her body and of that of other, useful in forces, prime, mind and spirit. keys of athletics, acrobatics, do and body control.


    Brews is the skill to find assistance in her journey in the natural world. making concoction, potions, brews and salves she can enjoy heightened states of mind or purified body to search for improvement. Helps with life, mind, prime and spirit. Keys off Academics (botanics) esoterica (herbology) and survival. not useful to fast casting


    Bones and remains, a more gruesome instrument allows jade to commune with things dead and decaying, to siphon off her own weakness or to affects the impure world outside herself using small crafts made of bones, flesh and fur, helps with correspondence, entropy, spirit and matter, keys off crafts. can also be used in potions. not useful to fast casting.


    And lastly her last instruments are weapons, contemplate weapons and use them in battle is useful to focus herself into turning into one. useful with forces, matter and prime.keys of melee and do


    You see, her paradigm, practice and instruments now can speak about her nature as a spellcaster. she is a magical gym sis drinking her ketamine and kicking ass, but also speak of her falencies, she sucks with connections magic, her paradigm is a personal one, and matter requires long work with crafts and meditations.
    Using this example, if Jade wanted to buff her strength for example, she could use energy, martial arts,brew,bodywork or even meditation, maybe even any of the instruments.

    While she have specific instruments for rituals, any of them still can be used in prolonged castings.

    Looks to me that the instruments are overlaping and the choice of which one to use depends on the conditions and situation the mage find herself.

    With that in mind, wouldn't the brew or bones instruments become obsolete for the other ones? Since they are harder to deal with. Afterall why brew when you can meditate?

    So, th final question is, is 7 instruments too much?

              Originally posted by Lord Revan View Post
              With that in mind, wouldn't the brew or bones instruments become obsolete for the other ones? Since they are harder to deal with. Afterall why brew when you can meditate?
              Meditating while in combat it's often going to be harder than sipping a potion or throwing a bone.

              That being said, yes, Instrument overlap it's likely to happen.

              This is good, the reason Instruments exists it's not to cripple the use of magick but to add flavor to the character. Sometimes it looks cool to meditate fire into being, but other times it looks cooler to drink a firebreath potion. In my experience it feels nice to have so many options to describe your magely shenanigans.

              So, th final question is, is 7 instruments too much?
              I used to believe that (used to design mages with less), but 7 Instruments it's a good number. Precisely because they are a lot, this forces the player to think different ramifications of his character's Paradigm. And that's the idea, to flesh out the character, to think different ways your Paractice traduces into magick.

              The temptation of having a "master instrument" (or two) that does everything with little fuss (and then surpass it/them as soon as you can) it's inherent to this system, but mechanical balance aside it's a pleasing number for those that want to have a lot of options to describe their casting.
