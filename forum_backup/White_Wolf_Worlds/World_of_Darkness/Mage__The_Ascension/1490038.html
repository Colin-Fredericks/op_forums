    Mages and Ghouldom

    08-13-2022, 01:19 PM
    I've got a question that's been plaguing me for some time.

    Mages, upon Awakening, lose access to any linear magic they had previously. Their magick is more "natural" to them, and all things are seen through the lens of the Avatar and their Paradigm. Fair enough, I can't argue with that, especially given linear magic doesn't have the one main drawback of dynamic magick and the game needs that balancing tool to remain important, so people aren't crossing the sub-splat and the main splat for power gaming.

    But... what about ghouls?

    Vampiric Disciplines are, if I recall from the book correctly, counted as a type of linear magic in Mage. At the very least, they're described as such. Would a ghouled Mage, then, be unable to use the Disciplines gained by becoming a ghoul? What, then, is the point of becoming a ghoul as a Mage? Pseudo-Immortality aside.

    What about Thaumaturgy, which functions more like a Discipline than a path of Sorcery? Could a Mage who has been ghouled learn Thaumaturgy and use it?

                          Originally posted by Screeb7165 View Post
                          Would a ghouled Mage, then, be unable to use the Disciplines gained by becoming a ghoul?
                          No. A ghouled Mage would be able to learn Disciplines up to the limit set by the Generation of the Donor.

                          Originally posted by Screeb7165 View Post
                          What, then, is the point of becoming a ghoul as a Mage? Pseudo-Immortality aside.
                          1. A cheat-code for access to Linear Magic (see below)
                          2. Access to vitae. That stuff is basically dirty Tass, with an additional heap of powerful magick properties. Problem is, it's probably in the Mage's body.
                          3. Limited Bloodpool as additional power reservoir
                          4. Healing
                          Generally not the greatest benefits in exchange for thralldom and addiction.

                          Originally posted by Screeb7165 View Post
                          What about Thaumaturgy, which functions more like a Discipline than a path of Sorcery? Could a Mage who has been ghouled learn Thaumaturgy and use it?
                          Yes. Unfortunately, it is still limited by the potency of the Donor vitae as any other Discipline. Although it is technically Linear Magic, it is Blood Magic. The 'power source' is vitae, and thus different from Faith, Will, or Formulae that fuel other Numina.

                                          Originally posted by Screeb7165 View Post
                                          (Yes, I know, out of context that faction sounds ridiculous, but we're having fun and that's what matters <3)
                                          It doesn't really sound absurd, even if the Tremere wasn't a renegade. Both factions can gain from it, in fact. The Tremere have struggled with their loss of Dynamic Magic for centuries (even though they would never admit to that openly) and the recent Sorcerer supplement for M20 contains a short box pointing out how Mages can develop new Paths, even though they are unable to use them. Having ghouled Mages makes perfect sense if the goal is to research new Paths and push the limits of Blood Sorcery.
                                          For the Mages, the negative impact of ghouldom sucks, but if they play nice, they gain the power of Clan Tremere as allies on a logistical and political level. The Tremere have immense resources and influence. The potential access to arcane knowledge alone is probably tempting enough for a Mage, add to that the ability of the Clan to 'pull some strings' within Sleeper society.
                                          The point is, aside from the mechanical pros/cons of the Ghoul Merit, it's easy to forget that there is also a potential social and resource power gain. It's playing with fire for sure, but for some the risk may be worth it.

                                              I don't think that the box in S20 would translate to Blood Sorcery: they're specifically about creating Paths for mortal sorcerers (where the power source is “formulae”, as voidshaper put it, not “vitae” — and also not Faith or Will, for that matter: just Hedge Magic; not Psychic Phenomena, True Faith, or Blood Sorcery). I also question the notion that the Tremere have any arcane secrets that mages would be interested in — again, largely because of the conceptual differences between Blood Sorcery and Awakened Magick.

                                              On the other hand, V20 Black Hand has the “dirty secret” of Blood Familiars (pp.152–154) which allows vampires to “borrow” a mage's Magick (or mages to “loan” their Magick to vampires). If a Tremere were ever to learn of this secret, that could easily motivate him to try to reconcile with the Order of Hermes or other Awakened Mages to try to “get back” what Tremere lost. V20 Black Hand frames this in terms of Archaic Sorcery (i.e., a hitherto unknown Dark Ages: Mage Fellowship that has survived into the present while still practicing DA:Mage-style Foundation and Pillars magic); but it could easily be adapted for “modern mages” with very little trouble. In fact, the only adjustment that would need to be made, in my opinion, would be to the “Offering the Awakened Soul” Rote: it's listed as “Deva •••••” which makes sense in the context of Itarajana Sorcery (pp.147–151) but not when the mage in question uses Arete and Spheres. The Deva Pillar is effectively a blend of Prime and Mind; so I'd make this a Prime/Mind Rote, requiring five dots in at least one of those Spheres, maybe both.

                                              You're right about the “mundane” benefits of being a ghoul, though.
