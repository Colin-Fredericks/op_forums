There are thirty official combat spacecraft (though the VE might have that many hidden away from the rest of the Union), 5 AUC, 5 Vaders, and 20 Qui La Machines. Even if you double that amount to account for the hidden spacecraft of the VE, you are still only talking about 10 AUC, 10 Vaders, and 40 Qui La Machines. We are talking more Star Trek levels of combat capabilities, not Star Wars levels of combat capabilities, because there just are not enough VE in existence to create, maintain, and operate more spacecraft, not when the vast majority of the VE have other duties that keep them from contributing to space exploration.
                                                            Macrotechnicians also construct and maintain voidcraft. Though for the most part they're restricted to conventional space.
