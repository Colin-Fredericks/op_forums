          I actually use the urban fantasy default settings fairly rarely. I either use alternate histories or Sci Fi backgrounds.

          I had a TORG influenced setting were paradox if it even existed was determined by the world axioms in that area.

                  Originally posted by Leofwyn View Post
                  Does anyone significantly change the MtA setting or use the rules in a homebrew setting?
                  I like the rules, but want to use them for a setting with different elements. (More Dresden Files/Harry Potter/Witchcraft rpg/modern urban occult) And wondering what changes others have made.
                  I have Saytor Phil's &quot;Mage Made Easy&quot; and I am adapting many of those suggestions. (Getting away from the vulgar and vulgar w/ witness paradigm to al little more &quot;magic&quot; to permeate the world)
                  Wanting to see what others are doing.
                  Yes. I've changed it into almost an entirely new game, with the same theme.

                  First of all, I use Gurps rules. But even those I custom taylor. For example, they have "Power Ups 9 Alternate Attributes", for creating different attributes, thus I use SEVERAL different attributes from both D10 and Gurps. I use Willpower Points like in D10, I use Fadigue Points like in Gurps and I use "Spirit Points". WP is for Mental tasks, FP for Physical ones, and "SP" is a sort of "will to live", leadind to depression and lethargy if depleted, and used in place of HP against spiritual attacks and some other things. Each one of those can be "burned" for extra effort (WP for mental tasks, FP for physical effort and SP for "inspiration").

                  As for the base attributes, I use ST (Strenght), I divide IQ (Intelligence) into Intelligence properly, Charisma, Perception and Will (permanent, not WP which is usable, but max WP is based on Will), DX (Dexterity) I divide into Dexterity and Agility, and HT (Health) I use as is.

                  HP = ST, FP = HT, WP = Will and SP = (HT+Will)/2 (Spirit Points thou are an average between mental and physical healthy)

                  Other than that, I RADICALLY altered the Spheres, using the "Realm Magic" system present on Gurps Thaumatology.

                  Basically, ALL magic Spheres, all levels combined, sum up to 900 points in Gurps. Thus, you can divide that in as many "Realms" as you'd like.

                  So, for the Hermetics it remains the 9 Spheres you all know - 5 levels each, which means that each Sphere costs 100 CP (character points), thus each level costs 20 CP.
                  For the 6th level I charge 50 CP, 150% more.

                  That's well balanced for the system, mind you, because you have to buy attributes, Arete, advantages (and disadvantages to gain points) and skills all from the same pool of points.

                  HOWEVER

                  That also means that I can divide those 900 pts in ANY way that I like.

                  And so I have. Now for example, my Dreamspeaker have only THREE (3) SPHERES: Mind, encompassing the illusions of the senses (you could call it roughly as encompassing Time, Space and Entropy - no, not Mind), Spirit, encompassing the illusions of the self or of universal consciouness (Spirit, Mind, Prime) and Body, encompassing the illusions of the matter (Life, Matter, Forces).

                  For 900 CP / 3 = 300 CPs total each, with 5 levels that gives 60 CP for each level on each Sphere. Again, balanced, fair, and now they dont all use the same cursed mechanics despite the fluff claiming that "many traditions dont even see it like that".

                  My technocrats have only 7, Space/Time (thanks to Einstein), Biochemistry, Entropy, Forces, Dimensional Science and Society (which ressembles the one in M20 that replaces Prime).

                  That means that they are NO LONGER INTERCHANGEABLE. A hermetic NO LONGER has any meaninful way to understand the "Body Sphere" of the Dreamspeakers, because now the mechanics favor an entirely different hollistic approach to it.

                  Also, the VAs only have DATA.

                  But that's not all: there are also SUB SPHERES.

                  For example, lets take the Hermetic Forces. It deals with: sound, movement, heat, fire, electromagnetism, gravity and nuclear. And it costs 20 CPs/level

                  Now, those are 7 aspects of it.

                  I can, however, say that "Fire" alone is suffiently powerful to allow for the "Fire Sub Sphere" for 1/5 of the price, or 4 CPs / level.

                  So, instead of buying Forces for the expensive amount of 20 CPs per dot, I make a specialized PYROMANCER for a lot cheaper. Or I can create my "Winter Wich" Cryomancer for the same amount.

                  Or, instead of paying a lot for general Life Magik, I can create my focused Druid with the "Plant Sub Sphere", or my "Zen Monk" with Chi attacks but no "magical items creation".

                  Or I can create my "Alchemy Sphere", that allows my mage to brew potions that would require. Prime, Life, Matter and more. Or my "Necromancy Sphere", with elements from Life, Matter, Prime, Spirit and Entropy, but without the stuff not needed.

                  Ie I turned the Spheres into a dynamic system.

                  Not to mention that under Gurps each routine is like a skill - but they are very cheap. That means that, if you want to be a super good mind reader but nothing else, you can just dump a ton of points in that alone.

                  Each one of my Mages now are totally unique. My Technos for example shine with their "Memetics" Sub Sphere for mass propaganda.

                  Another plus is that my Hit Marks and Victors are super well round

                      Oh yes, I also use "Corruption Points" and "Derrangement" from Gurps Horror for a well round mechanic towards sinking into the Marauders madness or the Nefandi darkness.

                      That's the part of game mechanics. As for the setting, I change them all the time. Most notably, I eliminated the Euthanatos, I dont think a tanatological cult combines with hinduistic faiths. I was supposed to delve a little deeper for a replacement into hinduism, but I never have the time, and none of my players ever use them, so I just took them out while claiming "oh yes, and also the mysterious guys from India are somewhere". The Ecstasy Cult I eliminated completely, and they now are a part of the Dreamspeaker, just as the Verbena are part of the Hermetics. Since the whole "9 keys to reality" is just hermetic propaganda in my games (since now each tradition has their own proper amount), it has no underlying metaphysical impacts.

                      Oh yes, I almost forgot, my game also has 2 secret Spheres. The first is "Paradigm", which is a Sphere used uncousciously by the Marauders, and althought it IS theoritically possible to learn it (without going crazy that is), no one has ever been able to (mostly because no one is even aware of that possibility).

                      The second one is the Qlippothic Sphere, the Sphere of non-existance of the Nefandi. That one you DEFINETELY cannot learn. That is, unless you're planning to join the legions of the fallen...
