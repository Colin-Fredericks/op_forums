      The Psychic Paradigm: the Mage, when he awakes, he believes he have developed "mental powers"... That kind of Mage comes from people that already had a belief on psedosciences, so he believes he is a telekinetic, pirokinetic/kryokinetic, clairvoyant, telepath and all those things that are popular on pseudosciences.

      His Foco are... Well, just his thoughts. He thinks... And it happens. In time, he may learn how to "better control" his powers.

      If he meets some traditionalists, they may tell him about magik and stuff. Well, the psychic may abandon his ideas after that (oh... I were mistaken all this time), or he may dismiss anything that shaken them (those idiots are psychics too!). If he meets the Union before, it's more likely that he'll go to room 101 and become a proud agent without such absurd (and illegal) notions.

      Gameplay: rather boring. It also may be abusive, if the player wanna make everything with just a simple thought (taking one turn only to make any roll for Arete), or needing just some minor concentration for a few seconds for greater effects (one roll per round on ritual casting). Of course the player may say his char believes his mental powers are incredible, thus allowing for this. As a ST thou, I would say that many things require several minuts of concentration because of their complexity, while others are beyond reach - the ST will say if in his game this Paradigm could open portals or turn lead into gold. I mean, there is nothing to stop someone to believe they can turn lead into gold with the power of their mind, but the ST will say if it is so or not. Just be careful when balancing things.

      There are many alternatives to make this paradigm more fun and complex thou; lets take Professor Xavier from X men for example. Althouth he is a VERY powerful telepath, he uses The Brain, a huge machine that amplifies his powers. A char with this kind of Paradigm may build such "psychic amplifiers" or "modulators". How would those work are also up to the ST - giving access to powers that otherwise he couldn't have (for example, he can use Corr 2 to see distant places, but can't teleport with Corr 3 unless he uses an psychic amplifier), or could just make some effects "easier" (for example, you may tell to your player that he needs 10 minuts of intensive concentration to see the spiritual world, but with his psychic modulator, he can do it in just a few seconds). Another option is the more "mystical" approach; drugs may "unlock" his hidden potential and meditation may help him focus his powers. Those additional options can make such a paradigm very interesting (and could be held by technocrats and traditionalists alike)

          The Techno Wizard: no, it's not a Hermetic that uses technology, but the opposite, it's a Scientist who uses "magic"

          How does that work? I already told a little bit about that in another topic, and actually, I played with a technocrat with this Paradigm.

          To put it simple: Science is everything. Every and anything can be explained by Science!

          So, when those damn RD (reality deviants) are doing all those incompreensible feats... They are doing things that Science can explain.

          For example: alternative dimensions are a scientific fact. Those weird realities have physical laws that adhere to quantum principles, one need only to unlock those principles (and translate them into maths).

          Those quantum dimensions, are both affected and interfere with our own dimension on deterministic ways, so, when a Verbena uses his blood for his rituals, he is actually using a substance that have a great lenght of quantum interference; on top of that, pain is also a powerful conduit for brain activit, and the human brain has a great influence on quantum fluctuations.

          The meditation technics of the Akashic, the methods of perception alteration of the Ecstatics, and even the enchantations of the hermetics or Verbena or the choirs of the Celestial Chorus, all those are sending quantum waves across dimensions when they do that, without ever knowing... Those "rites" simply were passed down from generation to generation, made on the basis of "atempt and error", the base of human progress. But they don't know the real basis of their beliefs, they just repeat processes proven effective.

          An iluminated scientist, however, can learn the ORIGIN of those so called "magics".

          Gameplay: please note, this guy is a scientist, not a mystical. In my own game, he were a researcher of the Technocracy, infiltrated in the Traditions to be able to complete his research. His most important Foci were his "quantum meter", but the importance of this small machine that fitted in his pocket were for roleplay, not to turn him into a superhuman. That little equipment let him use Prime 1 to measure Etherial fluctuations, just that. But, whenever a mystical traditionalist would use ANY effect close to him, he would use it, and register ALL data to use in his researches. On many occasions, he would ASK traditionalists permission to let him measure all the fluctuations while they performed their usual effects.

          With all that raw data, he could assemble enough information to even mimic some effects (but under very different principles). He could cut his own hand to summon some spirits like a Verbena, but unlike the Verbena, he doesn't think the spirits show because he made the right kind of sacrifice to some werid gods, but rather that pain changes his brain waves into the right wave frequency to ressonate with the emanations that blood naturally have.

          That allows him to go with shortcuts: he doesn't REALLY need blood and pain to achieve his desired result; all that he needs is the right quantum wave frequency. So, althouth he COULD make use of "blood Magic", it's far easier (and more precise) to regulate his machine to produce the quantum disruptions that he needs.

          ST, be careful: this can lead to SEVERE abuse. Don't let the player simply walk around with a "portable quantum modulator" device that does-it-all in a round (or a few for rituals), meanwhile having a char that can talk Enochian to "cast spells" in an emergency if he is catch without his toy.

          Tell the player that, althouth he "knows" the principle behind making earth golems, he doesn't know how to talk enochian to use the "trial-and-error" way, and that, althouth a Hermetic may take only a few minutsminuts to do that, his quantum modulator will need hours to generate the right wave lenghts; maybe another effect can't be done with a simple portable modulator, needing the huge lab version, and so on, whatever is needed for balance for you.
          Also, some effects may still be done by more "crude" ways - it will still be easier to send quintessencial bolts using plasma guns than trying to manipulate quantic energies after all.

          Reward good roleplay too; this scientist may learn some of the original effects.

          That type of char can even become a jack-of-all-trades, or a "paradigm Translator", even if only from mystical views to techmantic paradigms

                      That would hold true for ANY mage, it would be a matter of character concept. For example, an IT technic that awakens could be found by the Order of Hermes. All he knows is about computers... But know there is this bunch of weirdos saying that geometrical aureum draws, made to follow the pattern of the stars, can make you open portals, if you talk the right sentence in an obscure language called "Enochian", which no university in the world teaches and that shows no results on a Google search. Ok, they can do stuff... So... Yes... There is clearly a correlation between a perfect Pentagram draw with one angle pointing to Alpha Centaur and the other pointing to Mercury... But trying to memorize all the astral charts, the related geometrical patterns are a pain... And Enochian is beyond him.

                      So, such a Hermetic WOULD suffer the penalties... For using the tools of his OWN tradition...

                      Why don't he goes with the Virtuals?
                      Well... Maybe conjuring beings from a Virtual Reality like in the movie "Tron" is a little more than his faith can take...

                      Or maybe, a Dojo teacher here in Brazil, could awake, but stumble with a bunch of New Age hippies, talking about healing crystals and blood Magic... Well, who would've imagined right... So, there you go, the most unlikely Verbena ever... He finds it very hard to memorize and properly use all the crystals and sacrifices correctly, so he takes the penalty.

                      Why not joining the Akashics? Maybe his new friends never told him about them... Or maybe, he just thinks that all that Kung Fu thing and all the Chi stuff is a bunch of mambo jambo... Maybe he only practice karatê as a sport, without caring for their phylosophies.

                      And... What if our good ol' technowizard is an anthropologist that studied ancient religions with an PhD on ancient rituals under a scientific view? Thats a subject he may have studied all his life before awakening... Maybe he knows MORE about some blood rituals than many Verbena, or maybe he has knowledge about aureum geometry because of his studies on Ethrusc and Roman Architecture that would cause envy on many hermetics.

                      EDIT: that's not the same as dual Traditions merit, mind you. You won't get full access to two different sets of tools; the Akashic WONT teach you the Dim Mak nor will the Euthanatoi teach the Agama Sojourn, and you CAN'T use "blood Magic" or "Chi energies"; you can meditate to go on Astral Journey, or you can speak Enochian for effects, but you have to explain how you make those things to work using "quantum modulation" or "psychic emanations" language or some other pseudoscientific nonsense.

                      That kind of Paradigm suits very well for people who were parapsychologists before the awakening... House Ex-Miscelania for example, could follow that (which would mean that they don't REALLY believe in the Hermetic paradigm, but ratter, use the Hermetic Foci while believing those to be more conduits for an inner power, instead of being the original of the power itself)

                                  Yep, that's the idea.

                                  I think this kind of Paradigm is very open, and so it can use many Foci, but it would be most likely meditation, special prayers, ascetic lifestyle perhaps, things like that.

                                  Looking in other foruns, I saw some other interesting ideas, two to be specific, but only for NPC, because one would be a paradigm of Marauders (or almost) and another for Nephandi. Sadly I'm not a master of Time, so I'll just put brief descriptions and I'll try to expand a little more later

                                  Solipsism - that's a paradigm that will give you a one-way ticket to the Deep Umbra. If what you believe MAKES reality... Does that mean that "reality" is just a projection of your thoughts? If so, why aren't you God?
                                  (Goodbye... Enjoy your little self absorbed universe in another dimension)

                                  Niilistic Freedom - that's actually a belief of the real world, followed by some buddhist sects; basically, existence is suffering, desire brings suffering. The only way to end the pain is to break the cicle of reencarnation. The buddhistis make that even sound noble. In the WoD however, this can be a perfect paradigm for Nephandi. Even worse, unlike the common steriotype of them, this kind of Nephandi could be kind, peaceful. They invite all to jump in the Oblivion with bovine complacency. Any effort is futile. All is nothing. Its meaninless.

                                  This kind of Nephandi are "benevolent". To end all is not an agression; in fact, it's the only and biggest act of mercy that exist.

                                  Their foci can include meditation on the inevitable Void to come, focusing on the delight of letting your thoughts drift away from the ilusions of the ego, or you can also let the focus in the pain of existente; imagine the effect you wanna make; now, focus on WHY you want that (for power, to save your own life, to save another, for confort, for lust, whatever). And meditate on how futile that action is. How you'll die, only to be born again, only to desire the same things, only so that in the end you'll die again, never truly achieving anything, because nothing in this world remains, so you'll suffer forever in that cycle; now, think about the pleasure of the release, the final end for your prison. You must shatter the prison to be free, and to free all the others that are encaged for so long that got "institutionalized". Free the world. Use the ever changing aspect of this prison to change it according to your will, so that you way use it against itself.
