          The rule for rolling again with a +1 dif is for ritual casting. In those cases yes, you keep the successes you already had, but failure here means a complete lack of successes on a roll for a spell you were casting as ritual anyway.

          If you're making on spot castings of a single roll, then it is as Heavy Arms said, but then the ST may choose to let you roll at normal difficulty, their call.

                                              With all respect meant. I believe Monteparnas is just simply wrong about this. They can play it however they like, but this isn't how it is intended to function.

                                              There are two types of casting, Standard casting and Ritual casting

                                              In standard casting, you typically decide how many successes are required by considering how impressive the feat is. Most effects will be around the 3 successes area of impressive, but not always. You can also do this using the divided successes rule to consider how many successes you need in order to do all the things you intend. This number becomes your Target successes

                                              Each roll has 3 potential outcomes:
                                              1) You roll enough successes, if you do this then your effect goes off, any excess successes can be used to augment the effect in some way, make it last longer, deal more damage, or become more impressive at the ST's discretion.
                                              2) You do not roll enough successes (any number below the target, including 0) You now have a choice, you can roll again at +1 difficulty to accumulate more successes (This is what is meant by pick up where they left off), or you can stop and accept a partial cast (which is either determined by dividing the successes you did accumulate, or by using the table you quoted).
                                              3) You botch, no further attempts may be tried, enjoy your paradox.

                                              Rituals change this in the following ways:
                                              First, you only get an increase to difficulty on a failed roll (that is rolling no successes)
                                              Second, you can save your spell from your first botch, but you lose a successes and increase the difficulty, botching a second time destroys the effect and leads to ever more paradox
                                              Third, you can cast in tandem with other mages.

                                              A note on paradox: For vulgar effects you gain a point of paradox whether or not you succeed or fail the roll (This is specifically stated in Revised, and it exists as well in M20, though it isn't well communicated). One can interpret that on each failed roll in a standard cast, that is a point of paradox crewed regardless of whether or not you decide to pick up where you left off and roll again. Rituals on the other hand only give paradox per roll upon a botch. This means standard casting as an extended roll is actually far more paradox prone then ritual casting, which I also believe is the intent.

                                              Anyway, I hope that clears up a few things, or at least inspires you to run it in a way which best reflects the areas you wish to emphasise.
