            Originally posted by Ambrosia View Post


            Mmmmh, I think the Technocracy Party Line actually works fine, if you think about it - the fallacy one needs to avoid is, that the Technocracy isn't actually going 'All those things out there do not exist! La La la!'

            If you think they are saying *that*, *literally*, then yes..their partyline makes no sense at all, because it would take a Technocratic field agent a week or two before they need to be shoveled back to Room 101.

            But That's not what they are actually doing or saying.

            What the Technocracy is saying, is 'All this strange stuff out there exists, yes. But it is not what all those supersticious people think it is. It is all clearly concepts that work within our Hyperscientific paradigm, It is all explainable. Stop it with the suspersticious "Fairy" stuff and mumbo-jumbo. We can explain this.'

            And this of course includes everything the Technocrats themselves manage to do. You do not need to believe that what you do is Magick, just because what you do is eerily similiar to what the people out there calling themselves 'Mages' do - it's exactly the other way around. "Those other groups are clearly using principles of Hyperscience, psionics, field pyrokinetics etc. without realizing it, and they just won't accept that what they are dressing it as is absolute nonsense sillyness."

            And of *course* the Technocrats would believe that. They know what they can do. They know it's Enlightened Science and Hypertech. And when those weird guys out there are doing similiar things in a chaotic fashion, of *course* they must be using the same principles. They're just doing it in a delusional, wrong, hazardous and uncontrolled manner.

            So yes, their thoughts of 'We are not doing Magic, and neither are they.' actually work within their worldview. And in a way, it's just as valid - it is simply the flipside view of 'We are doing Magick. And so are they!' of the non-Technocrats. And each side wants to smack the other around with 'Don't you see what you are doing? Wake up!'

            The difference between the Technocracy and the other Mages is, that while other Mages accept other paradigms with polite 'live and let live' ( while probably at first explaining things in their own paradigm in their mind ), that the Technocracy loudly, officially and strictly denies all non-Technocracy paradigms among their members. Everything out there is explainable through Technocratic Hyperscience and Tech. If the Technocracy can't explain it yet, they must do more tests and theories. And that's it. It's all covered.


            In short:

            It's not the belief in the existance of things out there that is against the partyline, it's believing that those things aren't explainable through Technocratic paradigm that causes the NWO to pay you a visit. If you start trying to explain those things outside the Technocratic paradigm, you are starting to lose faith in said paradigm's all-encompassing validity. And that's the line of thought they are trying to suppress. The Technocracy stands and falls with their members' faith (heh) in Hypertech and Enlightened Science.
            Except that's not what the Technocracy party line is at all. You are making it sane and workable. Which is all the to the good, but it is quite clearly NOT what is stated in the game to be the case. Outright. With bells on. All sorts of crap dependent thereon. Like the getting in on the "Big Secret" after sufficient advancement. The same "Big Secret" that is completely obvious to even the most fanatical agent upon facing the enemy. The same "Big Secret" there is an entire institution (Room 101) dedicated to enforcing, even though it is completely unenforceable.

            I don't disagree with your position at all. It is one of many possible rationalizations that works much much MUCH better than the official stance in the game. But that's what the stance is. Official. The rest is Golden Ruling, which causes problems, because any Golden Rules changes need to be clearly labeled as such, since RAW is the fundamental basis on which this discourse is founded.

                        And yet.... The party is still EXACTLY the "head in the scientific sand" idea. Even though it's contradicted elsewhere, because, well, it HAS to be. Since the Union is non-functional in a world where belief may determine reality but non-belief doesn't un-determine reality in the short term. Technocract agents who are not yet privy to the knowledge that magic is magic is magic is reality alteration by any other name cannot shield themselves from a vampires fangs, a werewolf's claws or a wraith's possession by denying their existence. They can't make a Hermetic's fireballs or a VA's reality hack go away in a poof of scientific logic.

                        Which is why the internal contradiction between the reality of functionality in a world with magic vs. a denial of same is irreconcilable. And attempting to throw up the doublethink of Room 101 and the doctrine of unmutualness when denial won't work doesn't help. It makes things even worse.

                        Ambrosia's way is better. Similarly, the way the Laundry feels about non-computational sorcerers in the series of the same name works as a decent rationalization. But what's in place? Nope. It's just silly.

                        And, like so much else, it's going to stay that way due to inertia within the game's conceptualizations. New ideas about fundamentals just aren't something that is done with White Wolf games. What has been is and always shall be. Heaven help us all.

                                                    No, the Syndicate will call ghosts "ghosts" and changelings "fairies" because they believe propaganda-driven demands on speech are stupid. The Convention believes in following Sleeper culture and affirming whatever makes them happy. The insidious thing is that "ghost" is a *literally* meaningless to them outside of a brand or exploitable cultural designation. Things have names because Sleepers name them, and create cultural phenomena that can be exploited. So the terrifying shades of your ancestors become cartoons and cereal box mascots and the whole thing stops being believable, because the term "ghost" belongs to a consumer-driven hyper-reality with no substance worth paying attention to. If necessary the Syndicate treats ghosts as psychic phenomena and eliminates anything about them that "damages the brand," but they don't really matter as *things.*
