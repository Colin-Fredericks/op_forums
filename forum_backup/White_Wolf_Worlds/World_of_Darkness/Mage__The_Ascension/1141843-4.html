                        Originally posted by The Nilbog View Post
                        N-n-n-necro!!!

                        Anyway, I've always preferred a kind of weird interpretation of the TU, shameless Technocratic apologist that I am. Namely, that they would be considered paragons of progressive political policy and human rights. Maybe with a few skeletons in their closet, sure, but what organization can exist in some form for 500+ years and not have a few of those? But regardless, the Technocrats would be genuinely some of the most enlightened, progressive, open-minded and forward-thinking people you could possibly want to meet...

                        If they had had the good fortune of being born in the real world. Unfortunately, they live in the world of Mage, where all the pie-in-the-sky dreams about faith healing and perpetual motion are actually totally possible. They live in a fantasy world with real world expectation. They're villains because they dismiss outlandish things as "unrealistic" and try to stop them from getting out and "getting everyone's hopes up". Both the Techs and the Trads want the best for humanity, the difference is that the Techs think it's irresponsible and dangerous to rush things and the Trads want it right now!

                        So yeah, about as far from "the GOP with hypertech" as you can get, if you don't mind my political leanings coloring my interpretation.

                        I also make psionics and about 80% of alien related stuff purely Etherite territory, because to me having them acknowledge stuff like that takes away from their themes of "realistic progress versus childlike enthusiasm and fantasy" and turns it into a slightly more unpleasant (for me) "fun spiritualism versus boring technology". Of course, they get some ET action, because otherwise the Void Engineers would be pretty pointless, but I digress...
                        The problem with this is that if that was true, their would be no conflict between the Traditions and the Technocracy. In fact, if the Technocracy embraced modern progressive thought and dropped their whole cultural imperialism schtick aganist other cultures and beliefs, they'd be kissing the ass of the Dreamspeakers and apologizing for being asshats centuries ago. Instead, they still actively shun them because of vague notions of their magic being unrealistic, which is only true because the Technocracy manipulated the Consensus and the Sleepers to be that way.

                        I have nothing aganist this idea, to be honest, but it would eliminate the most interesting antagonists in Mage and it badly clashes with the Technocracy's actually actions. It's kind of weird to claim to be progressive and open-minded when you still disregard so-called unrealistic things that you know can work with some effort is all I'm saying. Really, a progressive Technocracy would just be a more organized Society of Ether, embracing any possible avenue towards human improvement, which could mean anything from cybernetics to shamanism.

What they emphatically DO NOT want is a Reality where mankind's ability to manipulate their environment is held hostage to the whims of alien intelligences from another dimension. That is a Reality where humans must live in thrall to invisible beings that share none of humanity's values and interests, when they aren't outright parasitic to humanity.

                                Which brings us to the next point: if the Consensus allows spirits to exist, it also allows BAD spirits to exist, and to affect the material world. And remember, the more people accept the idea of a spirit world, the thinner the barrier is between that world and ours. If everyone believed in an animistic worldview, the Gauntlet, if it existed at all, would be paper thin. Allowing all the blasphemous abominations from beyond reality to freely run roughshod over Earth and its people. Allowing Bygones to stride the land, unfettered and powerful and hungry. Allowing even the weakest of malignant ghost or bane to terrorize and attack and mutate the innocent.

                                The Technocratic Union opposes the Dreamspeakers because the Union has an entire Convention devoted to matters on the other side of the Gauntlet. And those Void Engineers have nothing but horror stories to tell about the Umbra, and why it needs to be kept well the hell away from mundane Earth.

                                        This does not excuse the Technocracy's actions, no. They ARE extreme, ARE taking their point too far, ARE corrupt. That is all true. In this way, I DON'T think the Technocracy should just be like the Etherites or the Virtual Adepts. The Union as it is provides ground for the telling of unique stories. In that manner, I'd glad that there are many different ways the idea of Science and Technology can be explored in Mage.
