        I suspect that the Doylean reason for it is that it's already been firmly established that the Order of Reason went solidly atheistic in the Victorian Age (see the demise of the Gabrielites in the 1830s), and that left the Choir Celestial as the last major Christian mage faction standing — meaning that if you're going to talk about the missionary branch of the evils of imperialism, it's the Chorus or nothing. Thus the reason why the Chorus kind of feels like an adjunct to the Technocratic Union in this material.

        (The Order of Hermes suffers from a similar repurposing, as there's nothing in the Union that could be used to represent the various elitist mystical societies that served as the mystical arm of imperialism. The net result is that the Hermetics and Choristers end up being portrayed as largely complicit in the Union's goals.)
