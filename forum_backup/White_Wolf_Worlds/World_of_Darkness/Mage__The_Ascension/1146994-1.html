        Originally posted by Aleph View Post
        VE: Convention Book (revised) do recognizes Etherspace, they call it Subspace. Interestingly, you don't need Dimensional Science to go to Conventional Space, you go there with regular Forces & Correspondence based propulsion. Thus we know they can travel trough both. VE may have problems going to the Middle Umbra, but they can totally invade Horizon (what Etherites call Etherspace).

        And "indians fighting starships" (engaging with their horses and arrows something that supposedly moves at FTL speeds ) it's a problem since forever. Ajax way seems like a realistic and sensible way to solve it (it's not that indians run faster than light, they're obviously moving through some "hyperspace" or alternative dimension of sorts where distances are shorter), but this wasn't really how things were in the RAW as far as I know (maybe M20 changed something?). Infinite Tapestry goes so far as to say that while mages see different things in "outer space", the distances are the same, and thus you better walk using Correspondence if you want to get anywhere. Mage Umbra it's a mess...
        Since Subspace/Etherspace is NOT Conventional Space, so wouldn't the VEs need Dimensional Science to go there? So they can invade Etherspace about as easily as they can invade the Middle Umbra or Narnia or Oz or Olympus or the flatlands around the River of Tongues. It's only slightly easier to get there than it is to the Shadowlands, though there is more point for point congruence.

        I can't say it's my idea. It's just based on the deductive reasoning around the material put out. It's a complete contradictory mess. It's irreconcilable. Arguing from precedent is untenable as precedent can be any of a number of mutually exclusive thing. That's an objective observation from sifting through the various sources and trying to find some inherent system. There are some things that are similar or congruent, but there is too much that is not to be able to pretend a system into being. (Which is the current party line... that there is a system and that the various contradictions are "ehancements" or "optional", even though there is not core coherency defined).

        In such cases, the most recent/current and coherent ideas govern. The only relevant precedent is the last one that makes any sense and that is an authoritative point from which to work.. So, I base my reasoning on the nice little graphic they floated around the the time the VE Rev. book came out and on the underlying logic of their core system of dealing with the relevant material, e.g. Spirit and Dimensional Science. It's not so much that it's my idea as that it's the only thing I've been able to take away from the stew of ideas that integrates with the rest of the rules set and the baseline assumptions of the setting.

            Originally posted by Bluecho View Post
            First, the colonies around Mars and Jupiter were in contest for a long time. VEs and the Traditions were fighting over these places since at least WW2, and probably much earlier.

            Do you suppose, perhaps, that is it possible that these places were not formatted yet? That there WAS NO Conventional Space when they started fighting over those territories? That, indeed, the fighting was happening BECAUSE the VEs needed footholds there - in space that otherwise did not exist on the material side - before they could format it into Conventional Space? From there, it was a matter of holding onto gains the VE already made - both as jumping off points for deeper Void travel, and for simple stubbornness and pride - that explains why the Void Engineers kept fighting over the Jovian areas of the Umbra until the Avatar Storm evacuated everyone from the Umbra (and turned those who couldn't evacuate into disembodied spirits or Threat Null).

            Second, the VE would want to enter Umbral space for the reasons I mentioned before: because all the Convention's coolest toys were totes Vulgar Science, unsupported by a Consensus not ready for starships and orbiting archologies and stuff. You can't have the Union's Colonies out in Conventional Space - the very Consensus it's trying to build prevents that. Moreover, they need the Umbra because those very Colonies are Horizon Realms, and obey laws tailored to the Union's desires.

            Third, you are flat out wrong about different mages not seeing different views of the same Umbral environs. This is canonical, and has been so forever. Two mages can meet a powerful lightning Umbrood, and one will see Thor while another sees Raiden and another sees the Thunderbird. A technocrat wearing a space suit can totally take a "walk" through the Void, and can totally just happen upon a naked Dreamspeaker sitting around a campfire. This is totally impossible, and entirely appropriate for the Umbra. A place where Reality reacts directly to people's perceptions, and all ideas are equally true.
            Or they were just using Spirit/Dim. Sci to affect each other?

            It was, kinda/sorta canonical inasmuch as anything was, but it sure seems like it's about as canonical as, say, the entirety of the NWO being dissolving albinos or It-X consisting of cyborgs with little tank tracks for feet and clamps for hands. There has been a lot since then that makes that not work so well. The entire concept of Vidare and Alt-Spheres seems to bend VERY far away from the direction you're proposing. And there is nothing to counter the idea that you and are saying different parts of the same thing. Sure the VE's and SoE's were fighting it out in the moons of Jupiter, but they don't need to have been the SAME moons. And they may have been fighting using Spirit/Dim Sci. Alternatively, the fact that Jupiter is out in the Deep Umbra may mean that they whole situation bends back towards unity. However, that creates additional problems as it would indicate that Deep Umbral formatting might be problematic, at best. And the idea that the Asteroid Belt is any sort of logical boundary escapes me. I leave the game behind right there and Golden Rule the Deep Umbra out past the heliopause. Though, admittedly, there has to have been some formatting/pushing outwards that moved it from outside the orbit of Jupiter and kept it moving as each new planet was "discovered" (formatted?). So maybe we are coming together once again and the VEs and SoEs were duking it out in the Deep Umbra, with the VE's trying to create the strategic situation necessary for them to format Jovian Space and "add" it to the Conventional Space mileu? Not sure. The rules and fluff and all the rest of the mess really doesn't deal with this very well at all. At least not in a coherent and decisive way.

            The Consensus they are trying to build doesn't have any problems whatsover with the concept of colonies of humans living out in space and hasn't for a very long time. There is nothing to indicate that the VE's are trying to build a viewpoint in the Consensus that space is uninhabitable Quite the contrary, with having space programs and appropriate fiction and and so on.

            The VE's don't need to be any more secret about their Mars colonies or space stations than the Syndicate or N.W.O does with their Amalgams fronted by offices on Wall Street or in DC. Or, Hell, than they do about their own sub-aquatic settlements. The VE's may have all our telescopes turned to THEIR channel on the Umbral Dial when we look up, but the parts they are formatting are vast and empty and full of nooks and crannies to get lost in when poor mortal Sleepers are peering up with their bleary 10 generations behind the times optics....

                        Originally posted by CaptOtter View Post
                        I take this as an invitation to head canon the whole thing as best as we can.
                        Unfortunately that doesn't work very well very often in Mage. As the design decisions in creating M20 show, dug-in partisanship is not only de jure, it's actually encouraged. (By contrast, witness the attempts made in Changeling20).

                        So, you can't really have a discussion about head cannon productively because someone will find some place where someone once said something that contradicts what you're positing and, for them, that will be THE MOST IMPORTANT THING ever said about the Umbra, VE's in space as the Umbra, the SoE and their various Umbral spaces or whatever.

                        In the end, you have to figure out what to toss and what to keep because you can't keep it all and even M20 doesn't really resolve anything. (How can it, when a guiding ethos is "everything ever done is super-fine if you want it to be!")

                        I usually ignore the problems until it's germane to the game. When I ran a SoE game that took place in a spaceship shooting around the Solar System, with a fair amount of time spent in the Jovian System, I worked out a head cannon that functioned for that game. In subsequent games, I could care less how Conventional Space/Etherspace works. If the SoE is more concerned with going into the Hollow Earth using an ersatz Zeta-Beam, these questions don't matter over much. If we're more concerned about the Middle Umbra, Shadowlands, dealing with the spiritual aspects of Changeling involement in the game, it's even less relevant.

                        But, man, that SoE Space game (I think the chronicle was called "The High Frontier") was one of the BEST Mage games I've ever run or played in. It was insane. It was hella fun.

                        And there was a lot of VE vs. SoE and VE working with SoE on the downlow and space-Nephandi slinking around....
