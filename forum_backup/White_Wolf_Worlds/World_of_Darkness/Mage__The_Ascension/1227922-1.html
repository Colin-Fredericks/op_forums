    So, I thought that instead of giving lists of spirits and personae there should be a description of locale and the actors on it (including institutions, supernaturals and umbral ecology). Much like city books in CoD, but shorter.

    Originally posted by Aleph View Post
    I like the concept - the search for enlightenment forges your own soul, altering the way magick works - but the execution needs to be adjusted to Ascension's philosophy
    I think it should be tradition specific, not available for other paradigms or orphans. It should be very paradigm specific and preferably non-vulgar, probably bar the last attainment. For example:

    1st Attainment - Arete 2, Sphere 2, Trait 3, Ability 3
    -Extraordinary perception, arete based buff to one trait or one covert modification
    2nd Attainment - Arete 3, Sphere 2, Trait 4, Ability 4
    -Defense, mobile buff to traits, one borderline modification
    3rd Attainment - Arete 4, Sphere 4, Trait 5, Ability 5
    -One vulgar magic for a scene, spend minimum 1wp, 1 Q and maybe (?) earn 1 (only 1) paradox if observed by sleepers.

    Quintecscence gathering rituals should also be somehow limited. Maybe spend 1 WP, roll Arete at 10-Sphere difficulty (minimum 5) and gain successes as Q points.

    Some subtler Legacies can be converted with very little problem, in particular those that deal with umbra or mental powers.

        Originally posted by Warpwind View Post

        I think it should be tradition specific, not available for other paradigms or orphans. It should be very paradigm specific and preferably non-vulgar, probably bar the last attainment. For example:

        1st Attainment - Arete 2, Sphere 2, Trait 3, Ability 3

        I like the first two, but I don't recomend anything that would alter the effects of Paradox. That doesn't jive well with Ascension's definition of Paradox (which it's not a personal relationship with the universe but relative to the area and beliefs), nor with the goals of the Ascencion War (the enlightenment to be able to cast without Paradox it's unimpressive in a Reality Zone that eliminates the problem) I would rather have Attainment's modify mechanical aspects magick that are unrelated to the whole Consensus thing and yet are the same for everyone disregarding the differences that Paradigm/Practice would suggest

        For instance: Heavy Arms had, in a previous post, suggested to eliminate the arbitrary bonus to damage that you get by using Forces, and instead get it as an Attainment (Say, Arete 3 Hermetics get 1 extra success to damage spells that use Forces). I like that idea.

        The Extatic bonus that allows to have one extra magical perception actived without penalties could be made an Attainment too.

        DO, instead of being a "super skill", could be weaved as potent bonuses to Martial Arts granted trough Attainments - which incidentally would reinforce the Akashick belief in that their martial arts and their magick are one and the same.

        Similar advatages would be easy to fashion and would allow to ilustrate the advantages of different Traditions without entering into issues with the meaning of Paradox.

        The only "spell-like habilities" that I would recomend as an Attainment under these rules would be those that alter how magick works without involving Vulgarity at all.

        For instance, an Etherite (or a Verditus) could get the Prime effects that allow to create magical items trough Attainments, in order to eliminate the eternal problem of having to get Prime to create their fabled aparathuses, regardless of your disinterest in manipulating the stuff of magick itself. Note that the effect itself it's never Vulgar nor Coincident, because it's vulgarity depends on the effects weaved into the object.

        In the same fashion, I would take the Prime 1 "advantage" of being able to absorb Quintessence beyond your Avatar limits (which was never depicted as a spell, but rather as something you just "can do" when you get Prime 1) and fashion it as an Attaiment. Same with Spirit "Extra Soak against Spirits".

        While Traditions should have specific Attainments (and have special options corresponding to their camps. I.E: so a Bonisagus could get different Attainments than a Flambeau), I think that some other groups should get them too.

        Crafts value their special practices even more than the Traditions, as many refused to join to preserve their own paths to enlightenment. Attainments to make them really different from the Traditions and each other would be very cool and would reinforce the idea that their stile of magick it's different. If anything, I would make more restrictive Attainments for them (perhaps heavily empowering one type of magick or action while weakening other types)

        Technocrats supposedly have a "Unified Paradigm", but they're very serious about enlightenment and there's no reason to believe they wouldn't be changed by it: Eventually a Technocrat does become Control. They should get a single set of Attainments regardless of Convention...except that all Conventions have their own views about enlightenment. An ItX that desires to follow the cult of the machine should have that choice reflected in his soul. These alternate paths to enlightenment shouldn't be cherished, as they're unmutual, but they should exist.

                        So, again, it's a matter not of mechanics but of theme: Paradox it's the consequence of Hubris. It's a consequence of following your own reality-warping credo.                                                         - Really carefully explained rules. Things should be clear for first time readers.

                                                        - Time needs some new cool effect. Prime 5 lets you get rid of Paradox, which isn't an effect Prime needed to have. It's simply a magickal effect which writers justified as being part of Prime. Something similar for Time would be quite cool; hanging effects and conditional magick are examples, but the fact that Time shines well for Masters and Archmages (who have a bunch of sphere effects they can hang and set conditions for) but is relatively lackluster on its own is a problem. For all of Correspondence's synergy with the other Spheres, it still lets you teleport and conjure and perceive all around the world. Time, to me, feels like the Sphere that requires the most work and creativity to make it viable as an affinity sphere.

                                                        - Make the Technocracy's punishments and activities more realistic. Nothing kills worker morale like people being blended up for dog food and replaced by a soulless clone which dies after 2 years. Traitors should be dealt with efficiently, not cruelly... assuming that the Technocracy isn't being controlled by the Nephandi. So essentially give a consistent vision of the "kinder, gentler technocracy", one with great pensions and healthcare and opportunities for its employees, one where you can request memory censorship so you can retire with a glowing reference, one where there is a timetable for eradication of humanity's problems. Show a softer side to the Syndicate, one focused on the finances of charities and pensions and similar. Show an anti-corruption movement in the NWO which wants to reduce the emphasis on "deep state" and bring the Technocracy out of the shadows. Show a Technocracy where people can raise ideas without fear of being killed or punished, where dogma is less severe. And despite all of that, still remain critical of the Union's flaws, and all of the evil stuff it does even when it is at its best and most moral.

                                                        For example, have really subtle and populist mind control being employed. Less Room 101 black site nonsense; you have enlightened science on your side, be subtle and charismatic! Carrot over stick. When betrayals and failures do occur, work with what you've got. A failure can be demoted, retrained, corrected with Mind, or their wages can be garnished to pay for semi-mandatory enhancements which will correct their failings. For example, a NWO agent who allows several reality deviants to escape them because of slow reflexes and poor fitness might be given minor enhancements and extensive conditioning/fitness training, with the relevant costs being deducted from their salary and the failure being placed in a file which is referenced when deciding on promotions and bonuses.

                                                        Traitors might be executed, but this is less clandestine. Have new employees sign a contract stating that a Level X (or whatever) infraction of Union protocols, a malicious and deliberate betrayal of the Union, will lead to their apprehension and eventually to execution. But add some due process. Oversight from other Union employees, the chance to appeal unfair decisions, you get the idea. Make things a bit more transparent, with the necessary measures to keep sensitive intelligence under wraps.
