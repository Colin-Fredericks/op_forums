    M20 Technocracy: human condition vs demonic influence

    05-26-2015, 04:49 AM
    This is a fork of the discussion about which book to use as source in a Technocratic campaign ( http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/m...nocratic-game/ )

    The new topic is about the M20 statement that Technocracy is infested with Nephandi, even in its Inner Circle. This statement is, as many other things, presented as optional, but seems to be the default option (two of the three options assume that Nephandi are in charge). This concept blames the Nephandi and their masters of the corruption of Technocracy's original purpose.

    For me, what this idea suppose, is a devaluation of the Technocratic themes and moral issues. It blames otherwordly evil instead of human condition for the flaws. And it removes most of the moral dilemmas (a Manager who do wrong things to achieve right ends is disputable, a Progenitor who experiments with kids to find a cure is disputable, a barabbi who do those things out of pure evil is not).

    I continue the discussion with Heavy Arms where I left it:

    Originally posted by Heavy Arms View Post
    Can you actually pay attention to what I said instead of arguing stupid strawmen if we're actually going to discuss this?
    Please, be cool. I am not purposely throwing strawmen in order to discredit you. I may happen not to understand you and misinterpret your points. It also happens that English is not my native tongue, so sometimes I think to understand something different of what is really said.

    Originally posted by Heavy Arms View Post
    I said since the Technocracy victory, aka the Revised metaplot events.
    In this case, I thought that with the Technocracy victory you were referring of how world changed due to Technocracy dominion, not just when MRev declared Technocracy victory.


    Originally posted by Heavy Arms View Post

    Global warming is getting worse, and there continues to be minimal effort to counter it even as it has stopped being a theoretical dangerous and millions of people have had their lives negatively impacted by it. We have entire coastal communities being displaced as minimum sea level rises puts them underwater, with no where for them to go.

    [and goes on]
    Ok, here is my point. All those are terrible things. But it is simplistic to rely in demonic influence to explain every bad thing in our world.

    In real life those forces of evil do no exist, and we still have global warming, economic crisis, wars, human exploitation, etc, etc, etc. All these things are explained by flaws in human condition, but not flaws that make them serve devils, flaws that make that things happens by themselves. This include human ambition and cumulation of power.

    I know Technocracy do not exist as well, but I think it is a close metaphor for the real powers. Even if you are not interested in the metaphor, removing the Nephandic corruption as the motivation, we have to look for more complex causes, and we have a Technocracy much more interested and centered in human flaws (which is appropriate since Technocracy aims to Empower the humanity, but they fall by very human flaws).



    Originally posted by Heavy Arms View Post
    That's not what anyone is doing. Again, stop with the strawmen. The Nephandi are a symptom, not a cause. They see these things happening and they step in to make it worse. The Nephandi aren't to blame for the flaws of the Traditions and the Technocracy. They're there to make them worse.
    Ok, maybe we are not discussing the same exact point. I will make me clear: I don't mind if some Technocrats are corrupted by Nephandi, it even can be a good paranoid mood. What I don't like is that the Inner Circle is at the service of the Nephandi or that the Technocracy wrong doings are their work. In the "Future Fates" box, two of the three options blame the Nephandi, I'll add emphasis to the exact phrases:

    • The Technocracy has Fallen: In this option, the Inner Circle is essentially a Nephandic playground. Most ranking
    Technocrats are either barabbi or were never true Technocrats to begin with.     The Union has been exterminating its
    rivals, purging dissent, and driving humanity toward self-destruction. Although some Technocrats mean well and have
    been fighting against corruption, it’s essentially a doomed effort. The Technocracy is fucked, and it has been for ages.
    • The Union has problems but is not metaphysically corrupt: Despite a few bad apples and a ruthless approach
    to Ascension, the Technocratic Union is not overrun with Nephandi. The corruption that does exist is an extension of
    hubris, not infiltration by the Fallen. Essentially, the Union means well but has a brutal way of taking care of business.
    • There’s corruption, but they’re dealing with it: Yes, some Nephandi have warped the Union’s purpose. And
    yeah, the Technocracy’s brutal. But still, the Union can be saved. Devoted Technocrats – inspired and sometimes led
    by Secret Agent John Courage – have been fighting a covert war within the Technocracy... and despite major losses
    and obstacles, there’s still hope for the Technocracy’s redemption.




    Originally posted by Heavy Arms View Post
    Likewise, Nephandi influence pushing the Tradition and Technocratic buttons to try to get them to indulge in their flaws doesn't down tone those flaws. It makes those flaws worse because it isn't just petty wastes of time and potential while good could have been done
    I understand your theory supposes that their flaws are there, but they would make no more harm than waste of potential. This is downtoning for me, because the good tone is where they arrive only by their flaws, only by themselves.

    Following the mafia analogy, you are admitting flawed Technocrats that can be bribed into doing bad things, but then, if they do that things for the bribe, the ultimate cause of it is pure evil and destroying the world. My point is that the Technocracy created the problem they alone.

    Originally posted by Heavy Arms View Post

At the end, they are evil evils of evilness dressed in some artificial excuse for a philosophy, and since that can have some appeal in some chronicles, I think that putting them pulling the strings of all the flaws of the Technocracy and the Council is a huge devaluation of the human theme.

    Aside from my personal tastes on Nephandi, the thruth is the truth, be it said by Agamemnon, or by his swineherd.
