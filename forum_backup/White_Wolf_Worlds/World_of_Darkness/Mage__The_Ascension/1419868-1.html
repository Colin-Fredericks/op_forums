    If I were to try and sum up the Euthanatoi in a single word, it would be Psychopomp. While the term - literally "soul conductor" in Ancient Greek - traditionally refers to beings who guide the recently deceased to the afterlife, it has also taken on a more modern meaning as "soul guide" to the living as well as the dead. While this does include the idea of administering "The Good Death" to those in need as a way of preserving the Great Wheel, it also includes healing in the physical, mental and spiritual sense - trying to alleviate the suffering, sadness and shame which can damage the Great Wheel - and helping individuals meet their destined purpose in each life.

    As far as non-assassin/gambler concepts, I'd suggest the following:
    • Fate-wise Matchmaker
    • EMT turned Chooser of the Slain
    • Former gangbanger who "died" at the crossroads (street corner) only to be reborn as a psychopomp for those impacted by inner city gang violence
    • Troubled diviner trying to work out the secrets of her past lives
    • Blues musician haunted by ghosts
    • Powerful matriarch of a well established New Orleans family
    • Thanatoic apologist Herald
    • International financier guided by her ancestors
    • Neighborhood midwife practicing ancient magics
    • Suicide hotline councilor
    • Human rights activist
    • Maiden initiate of an ancient mystery cult
    • Explorer of the Underworld
    • Professional bodyguard
    • Medical doctor ministering to the poor and destitute
    • College professor of advanced mathematics/chaos theory/thermodynamics
    • Sin-Eater who takes on the regrets of the recently dead so that they can pass on
    • Shiavist dance instructor
    As for the issue of Murder Hoboing, that was one of the reasons the second edition Tradition book introduced the concept of Jhor, which others have mentioned.

            Originally posted by Bluecho View Post
            Well first, I think you should go back and read that page again, because its conclusions were more nuanced than "the character should have killed the Nazis when she had the chance".
            At the time I read it, I was experiencing Nazi-fatigue. Having just re-read it, there is a lot to draw from.

            Originally posted by Bluecho View Post
            I can see this kind of character in the modern day being a kind of breadtuber, undermining the work of reactionaries and grifters through both education and sabotage. Taking away the tools of such people, while giving the general public the intellectual tools to identify such indoctrination methods and fight back against them. Inoculating their minds against the viruses of hateful, reactionary ideology and rhetoric.
            I often see the word "reactionary" used in condemnation of Nazis and other presumably dangerous people, as if reactionism is always incorrect. Reactionaries can be correct. The whole environmental movement is a response to the unknown future variable effect of corporate exploitation of the planet. Environmentalists were constantly being referred to as "crazies" and other such pejoratives. As predicted results increasingly manifest, hurricanes, etc., the phenomenon being reacted to is understood and acknowledged. It is partially the result of collusion between extractive industry and corrupt officials - a conspiracy. Does Al Gore's too-early prediction of sea-level rise make him a grifter?

            In the United States, there is a rather large overlap between people passionate about gun rights and those who agree with the message of (corporate sponsored) global warming denial. As someone who is concerned about the environment, it does not serve me to vilify the guy clinging to his gun and declaring his resistance to his way of life being forcibly changed by the rest of society. I have to consider, for example, what bad things could be perpetrated against a disarmed population by the worst imaginable government. He is reacting to an unknown and can't be faulted for imagining the worst.

            Reactionism can, of course, be a mechanism for the delivery of a destructive ideology - reactionism and bad ideology being separate things. "They are trying to take our guns," could be true. There are strong arguments for gun control, publicly expressed. "because they are in league with a foreign non-citizen population." is the ideology's message. The emotion of the first statement impairs analysis of the second.

            Censor has the Mind Sphere - Mind 2. She can read surface thoughts and perfectly understand the emotion of her target. Mage canon acknowledges hate as a legitimate emotion, experienced by humans. For the Cult of Ecstasy, who love weird substances, hate is the sacred passion associated with Matter. Censor has the ability to bypass the target's tools of communication, look at the Mind Pattern of the person promoting the ideology and make plans dispel it somehow. As a Mage, she has much greater awareness of what is going on than her target.

            She is grief stricken by the loss of her son. The part that I would add is that her grief is compounded by him being claimed, like a trophy, by the Nazis. 60 Minutes Australia interviewed the father of an autistic boy who was recruited by ISIS and used in a suicide bombing. In the interview, the father explains the goal of the terror cell. They did not simply perpetrate the bombing that a local jihadi would have. They used an intelligent kid, demonstrating their expertise in brainwash.

            Censor was targeted because she is a journalist. The Nazis demonstrated their expertise in brainwash on a kid raised by a journalist. She has an awakened avatar and has not completely overcome the damage inflicted by un-awakened grifters. If she had been aware of her son's affiliations, putting a hole in his head, even as a plan-b, would not have been a consideration. Revenge never fills the hole left by a loved one.

            If I used this NPC, I would make Time and Spirit the next part of her journey. She meets an Ecstatic who sees what is going on, having properly dealt with the passion that is acting on her. Together they defeat the ideology.







            Thank you for passing time with me in conversation. My Hacks.

                                Low-level "canon fodder" enemies for Mage:
                                • Lesser Malevalent spirits - demons, ghosts/spectres, gremlins, spirits of negative emotions/ideas (Envy, Greed, Murder, etc.) or corruption, and so on.
                                • Objects or humanoid collections of items animated by the above.
                                • MiB - and by this I mean the vat-grown pseudo-humans with cookie cutter personalities described in earlier material.
                                • Android replacement humans, akin to Ira Levin's The Stepford Wives, which are made primarily for passing for human rather than for combat.
                                • Smaller biological creations - released accidentally or deliberately - such as hive-minded rat swarms, attack raptors, or the like.
                                • Homunculi
                                • Deep Ones and other corrupt humanoid creatures.
                                • Actual goblin-like Bygones that can come from almost every culture or region
