    Tracer (Lena Oxton)
    -Etherite Adventurer
    -Paradigm: It's All Good - Have Faith, or Bring Back The Golden Age
    -Practice: Hypertech and Crazy Wisdom
    -Instruments: Devices & Machines, Gadgets, Weapons, Fashion (speedy clothes), Voice ("YEAH!" "T-Racer!" "Cheers up, love. The Heroes have arrived!"), Vehicles (Aircraft), Athletics
    -Time 3 for enhanced speed and her Recall, taking her back in time 3 seconds (conveniently the length of one turn); her ability to Blink around is, story-wise, said to be temporal, but would better mechanically be done with Correspondence 3
    -If her pistols are energy weapons, Forces 3 + Prime 2; ditto for her detonating them like a bomb
    -If her pistols are mundane firearms, she could "Recall" her ammunition using Time 3 + Matter 2, causing the bullets to rewind back into her gun, good as new.


    That does it for the Attack heroes.

                                        Orisa (modified OR15 defense robot)
                                        -Iteration X or technomancer Ngoma
                                        -Another character I hesitate to stat as a Mage. Luckily, we also have Orisa's creator: Efi Oladele, 11-year old genius who modified an existing defense robot with advanced Artificial Intelligence. Presumably, she might be the Mage in this situation, with Orisa as the technomagickally enhanced Familiar. Whether this is true or if Orisa herself is self-aware enough to Awaken, I shall stat her capabilities below, as usual.
                                        -Forces 3 + Prime 2 for the energy-based machine gun, assuming it isn't just a regular automatic weapon.
                                        -Forces 2 or Forces 3 + Prime 2, to release a ball of light that moves opponents, by shifting gravity briefly or creating telekinetic force, respectively.
                                        -Forces 3 + Prime 2 or Prime 3 to create a stationary energy shield. In the latter case, I'm assuming that, because Mages can create weapons from pure Quintessential energy, they can also create shields and armor from same, using the same level of Prime alone. The Forces version creates a shield from hard light. Both are highly Vulgar in the current Consensus, though not necessarily so in a hypothetical future Mage setting.
                                        -Matter 3 + Forces 2 to boost her durability (as a robot; for human characters, substitute Life 3 for Stamina buffs), while also shielding her from attempts to move or knock her down.
                                        -Life 4 + Forces 2 to "Supercharge" herself and allies, boosting characters' Strength and Dexterity. Forces makes their various non-melee attacks more powerful.
                                        -Orisa, being a large robo-centaur, should receive the Huge merit, and possibly Advantages like hooves and horns. Efi, being a smol child, should obviously get the Child and Short flaws.
