      Talismans require a Permanent Wiipower Point to create. BOS pg 161. This has been the case at least since FBDF (Forged by Dragons Fire)

                  I can see why the Traditions would be irked that the Technocracy has access to a trick that the Traditions don't; it suggests the possibility that the Technocracy might actually be right about some things. So yeah; they'd want to figure out the secret and replicate it for themselves.

                  I don't see it happening, though, for a simple reason: a Tradition mage would probably have to adapt something like the Technocratic paradigm to do this. In-game, it's essentially a manifestation of the technological innovation of the assembly line, a move away from Wonders being unique and personal creations and toward them being mass-produced. On a gaming level, it essentially serves as the Technocracy's compensation for its members not being able to Surpass their Instruments.

                  As such, I would propose that if you want a Tradition mage who knows how to craft Grimoires and Talismans without spending Willpower or personal Quintessence on the effort, you need to adopt a mindset toward your Instruments that denies you the ability to Surpass them. Not just “you start Surpassing them later”, as with Technomancers, but “you never Surpass them”. That's the price that the Technocracy pays for its ability to crank out Devices from a factory one after the other for as long as their Primal Energy supply holds out.

                  My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

                          Bear in mind that the following is my take on the matter, and may not be 100% compatible with canon. But then, canon arguably isn't 100% compatible with canon…

                          The core of the mage's worldview is that Magick is inherently personal: the mage can do what he does because he has an innate quality that lets him bend reality to his will. Everything else follows from that, including the crafting of a Wonder. As such, mass production of Wonders isn't something that's within his notion of How Things Work. Even Etherites and VAs have a belief in some sort of inner spark that empowers them, and as such each and every Talisman crafted is intensely personal and a supreme act of will — thus the expenditure of a dot of Willpower.

                          You want mass-produced Wonders from a mage? That's what Trinkets, Charms, and Artifacts are for. Talismans aren't just Wonders; they're the pinnacle of Wonders: items that can work magick on their own. In their worldview, the only way for that to be possible is if the crafter imprints the Talisman with a bit of his own ability to work Magick. That's what the dot of Willpower and points of personal Quintessence represent.

                          When that's your fundamental view about how magick works, mass production of Talismans is out of the question.



                          Technocrats have an entirely different worldview. To them, what others call “magick” is inherent in the world around them, just waiting to be unlocked. What makes an Enlightened Scientist special isn't an innate ability to bend reality to his will; it's an insight into the secrets of the true functioning of reality. Instruments aren't an aid to one's effort to focus his will; they're the keys that are used to unlock nature's mysteries. That's why you can't dispense with them: it's the Procedures that are central to Science, not some innate quality of the person performing them.

                          In that worldview, there's nothing all that unusual about a Device; it's “merely” a Gadget that can be used over and over again. That makes the notion of being able to mass-produce them a reasonable possibility.

                          That said, even they can't do it automatically. They need to set up an appropriate Technocratic facility to make it work — and that is a capability arguably unique to Primal Utility, a Sphere variant that's inherently tied to the Technocratic mindset. This is much like how Primal Ventures are unique to Primal Utility.

                          But the fact that they can do it at all is one of the reasons why the Technocracy is in the position that it's in: its ability to mass-produce Devices (given a sufficient supply of Primal Energy, which Primal Ventures go a long way toward providing) is the reason why the Technocracy has access to the Requisitions Background and nobody else does: it has so many Devices stockpiled that it can lend them out to field agents on a mission-by-mission basis. And it has those stockpiles because of the factories that let it mass produce them.

                          My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness
