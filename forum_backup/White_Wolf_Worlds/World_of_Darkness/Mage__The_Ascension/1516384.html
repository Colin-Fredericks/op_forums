                                  SoE: Possibly the most chaotic scenario, I believe these are the ones that would try to help the most if they could, but they have so many different lines of reasoning and conflicting ideas that the world would become a mess and maybe they would blow up, or at best would become a less efficient technocracy.

                                  Order of Hermes: I think the world would have evolved around the belief in magic as a science, the world would have been more elitist involving the hermetic houses, but it's possible that a good result would have come from that.
