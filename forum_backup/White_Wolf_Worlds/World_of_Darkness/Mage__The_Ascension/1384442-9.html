    The Order of Hermes has “one way” to do Magick. The Celestial Chorus has “one way” to Sing of the One. The Akashayana have “one way” to bring unity between body, mind, and soul. And so on: each Tradition has “one way” to do what they do.

    In a very general sense, at least.

    When you get to the particulars, there's a lot of room for variation within any given Tradition's Focus. In that regard, the Union isn't much different: it's composed of five Conventions, each with its own Focus that defines the “one way” that that Convention does what it does; and within the bounds of that Focus, there can be a considerable amount of versatility.

    There are two things that differentiate the Conventions from the Traditions where Focus is concerned. First, the Conventions' various Foci are complementary: they're designed to be pieces of an even larger and even more encompassing Union Focus. This mostly shows up in cross-training: according to the Book of Secrets, a Technocrat who attempts to learn from another Technocrat of a different Convention has a much easier time of it than does a Traditionist attempting to learn from another Traditionist.

    But second, the Union isn't content to have a strong, unified Focus within its own ranks. Its big failing is its insistence that everyone adopt that same Focus.

                                                I'm really enjoying the new/evolved take on the Technocracy, and especially the section outlining how a Union member's personal life and professional life interact. Very well thought out, and makes a lot of sense!

                                                As I was reading through the manuscript I got to the section describing some of the Procedures etc. and noticed a lot of the mechanics seem... clunky or outright wrong. I'm hoping this section is just pre-editing right now, but just in case, here are a few specific examples of feedback:

                                                * The Master’s Edge (p216) — why not just use magick enhancing abilities to get an uncontested roll to reduce appropriate difficulties? Being a contested roll, even a Master would have difficulty getting much use out of this against your average NPC. A Master is likely to get 3 successes on average, the target is likely to get 1-2... leaving the Master with 1-2 successes. Spending 1 success for duration, that means a Master will /maybe/ get -1 difficulty.

                                                * All Triggers Locked (p217) — This would require Forces 3, not 2. Also arguably Matter 3 since guns are not made up of a homogenous material. It says it takes 3 turns to cast because the effects happen in stages. Is it an extended roll over the 3 turns? It would be nearly impossible to get 6 successes otherwise. But the way it’s described, a different part of the effect happens on each turn, so... on turn 1 do all guns come out of people’s hands and come to the user with a single success, or does it take 2+ to do that?

                                                * Pre-designed Proficiency Packages (p222) has variable Enlightenment, Primal Energy, and Background Cost. Nowhere does it list how much it actually costs for each variant though — are the only versions available the 3 pt (minimum of all) and 12 or (maximum of all) versions?

                                                * Zipper Ties (p223) — Why are these one-use? For something made of Primium you’d think that the Union could design some way to take them off without destroying them. I see the note about recycling the materials, but that still doesn't explain why they can't just make a non-destructive version of these.

                                                * Pocket Drone (p225) — the Forces 5 Adjustment says that it can deliver damage, but doesn’t mention mechanics for that. The device itself has no Enlightenment, so would that then use the user's Enlightenment instead? The Mind 2 variant says it decreases the possibility of visual detection... but gives no mechanics for this.

                                                I can give other examples, but hopefully that's enough to show that this section could be made a lot better with some editing and revision.
