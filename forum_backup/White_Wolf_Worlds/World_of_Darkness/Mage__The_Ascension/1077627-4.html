                    With regards to the Order of Hermes preparations:

                    After determining that House Tremere should be destroyed and getting rid of the remaining members of House Tremere among their number in one way or another, they decide to retake the Transylvania tribune. They are aware that Vampires are undead, have supernatural powers, and die in sunlight. They are pretty certain that total destruction or complete separation of the head or heart will destroy a vampire but that even serious injury to the head and heart is probably insufficient. Whether or not they know of the death of the Avatar upon being turned into a vampire they will probably be expecting to fight something akin to awakened Vampires, simply because they have little else to compare what they are facing to. They have a map of Transylvania and will try to take Coeris but are expecting to find other Tremere strongholds throughout the place. They suspect a few areas as Tremere strongholds and plan to storm these places next while minimizing human casualties.

                    I'm expecting 4 of the 5 nearby Archmages to take a personal interest in the affair and bring their 2 most combat capable Master students, who will be assisted by their 2 Disciples who will bring their 2 Intitiates. The remaining 6 Houses will probably send a Master each as representative who would likely bring 2 Disciples who would bring 2 students. The Awakened side of the war party would then be 4 Archmagi, 16 Masters, 32 Disciples & 64 Students.

                    As the Roman, Greater Alps, Rhine, & Novgorod tribunal have rough control of 6 towns worth of people with a 2500ish people each and could have troops gather in the Greater Alps before heading to Transylvania, they are likely to conscript a 4th of these as troops for 3750 soldiers unless it's more likely that these people will just be in the way. Does anyone have an opinion on this?

                    The plan so far is to get up the Alps as quickly as can be, raise a fort of sorts on a mountain a fair distance away from Coeris where they can see Coeris from and keep casting spells (still figuring out their options) from their defensible location until they are fairly sure that resistance has been pummeled and they can sweep the place. Plan B, they turn Coeris into rubble. They will have sunlight 24/7, multiple mages ready to counter any spell they notice the enemy making, and will use magic to prevent the many logistics issues that may come up, including the need for supplies, travel problems, etc. Absolute worst case scenario, they fall back to a secret fort just outside Transylvania in the Alps where they can be reinforced by the rest of the Order.

                                                            Note that they don't have the same info gathering capabilities as some of the other mage groups of the time nor of the modern version of the Order. For one thing, their use of spirits wasn't quite what it is today.
                                                            I take note of that, but I think it's worthy to meditate in what they can't and what they can do.

                                                            It's a common mistake (and something that I did for some time) to think that the Pillars of the OoH are Forces, Life, Mind and Prime. They're not. The Pillars are conceptualy more broad than the Spheres, and this amplifies what they can do.

                                                            For Instance: You can summon an Astral Spirit with Corona 4/Primus 2. The very fact that you can do this proves that Corona can fully affect astral spirits (something that Mind alone can't do), as you're basically forcing one to come and then using Primus just to give it a sort of body.

                                                            After forcing a spirit to appear, you can affect them with other spells to doblegate the spirit against their will. Now, a spirit treated this way isn't going to like working for you, but it's going to work for you nevertheless. And if you can summon a fire elemental to attack, so you can summon a more subtle spirit to scout. Consulting spirits should also be possible (except that it's not so much "consulting" as it's torture and interrogation).
                                                            Thus, while it's true that " their use of spirits wasn't quite what it is today", an Adept can still use them to gather info

                                                            Since OoH hasn't a "Correspondence" equivalent: It's impossible for the OoH to affect things outside their "range of view" with magick? .

                                                            I ask this because, while the answer would normaly be yes, DA: Mage has very different rules. You can cast permanent enchantments without anything like "Prime", and they have a whole section about "casting at range" (I don't remember if it mentions the need of specific Pillars).
