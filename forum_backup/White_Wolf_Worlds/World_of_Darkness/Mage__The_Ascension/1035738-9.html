    Originally posted by Aya Tari View Post
    Wouldn't dream casting a ritual violate the basic paradigm of the VE? There is no technology to use for foci and, quite frankly, I am really skeptical about allowing characters to cast effects that affect the real world in their dreams. It is not only the potential game balance violations, but the potential scenario of anyone with Forces 3+ casting effects that affect the real world while experiencing a nightmare.
    It doesn't violete the paradigm of the VE... Look, he doesn't believe he is casting ANYTHING AT ALL. He just have an absolutely conviction that he is special... And reality seems to agree on that (except on those nasty situations that it doesn't. Better stay in the lab on those bad days).
    In his paradigm, he is special... In fact, paradox for him is having unluckness! Being just ordinary is what would REALLY contradict his paradigm. That's why it works

    As for dream-casting, that's a lot more common than you think. Many Ecstatics DO THAT ALL THE TIME. That's in fact, a very common foci for many effects. The Akashas learn to use Mind to control their dreams, and they can cast effects with no trouble while at it. Ecstatics use dream casting for Time divinations and Correspondence far scry, and the Dreamspeakers also use dream-casting on many spiritual effects, just to name the most common ones

    Now, the question here is: does your paradigm allows you to use dreams and sleep as foci? Your hermetic may dream he is sending thousands of fireballs on angry hordes of zumbies, but if he doesn't believe he can use dreams to make fireballs, you don't have to worry about being carbonizing by sleeping in the same room as him.

    Effects don't just come "by accident"... The only exceptions would be wild effects during the awakening, maybe on rare ocasions during epiphamies, or if the Mage is a suffering a Silence or is a Marauder. You won't risk turning someone into a stone because you had an espasm caused by a heart attack or something similar (again, unless you're a Marauder). So, when an Ecstatic go to sleep to try to see the future, he is focusing his mind into the desired effect, not just randomly letting his wild dreams starting shooting time distortions all around. I mean, he COULD send a lot of wild time distortions at random in theory. But, if he's paradigm only accounts for Time 2 effects while sleeping, than that's what he gets... And why doesn't the effect just go random? (since dreams are just weird). Because he doesn't believe that . He believes that, chanting the right chanties before sleep will let you see the future, so THAT'S IT. A botche would be seeing as bad concentration before going to bed (which could in turn give the Mage nightmares - or bad omens, again, that's paradigm based)

    By the same principle, my VE thinks he is special, and that his sleep reinforce his convictions and make his mind and sense's sharpen. He doesn't see the resultados as "magik", but ratter as the result of intensive training, wits, and a self esteem that borderline supersticious believe in "Divine Fate" that gives him an undermining resolution. The fact that reality seems to bend over to adjust to his needs, just reassure his special position on the universe...

    Note that, while that effect can have a potential for be a game breaker (and I admit it, which is why a let it totally in the hands of the ST), it is ABSOLUTELY COINCIDENTAL... My VE simply doesn't see anything that could be breaking his paradigm

    So... He is luck, because he believes he MUST be luck, and he believes he MUST be luck because he IS, in the pratical sense, a luck bastard. Chiken and egg...

    That guy is, of course, just one bad backlash away from plumbing in a very interesting Quiet...

                            So, no, Mages aren't prey for anyone. It's easier to accept the other way around. I can see Mages going into Caerns kicking the wolves asses to get some Quintessence.
