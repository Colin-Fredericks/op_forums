    Fields for the Technocracy, Reloaded

    05-03-2020, 02:17 AM
    This is a reboot of an earlier thread I set up, Fields for the Technocracy. Sadly, I lost my focus on the original thread and it derailed. I am attempting here a more modest proposal.

    The idea is that the Technocracy doesn't use the same Spheres that the Traditions do. This is because the Order of Reason predates the Traditions. Mind you, there are many similarities; these come from the fact that the order of reason was originally founded by the Craftmasons and other factions that had descended from the same group of mages that eventually became the Order of Hermes. But the two systems are not identical.

    I'm basing this on the variant technocratic spheres that are described in M20, although what I'm presenting here involves more extensive changes then what the Sphere variants provide. I'm calling them Fields instead of Spheres in reference to their somewhat different organizational structure, but in practice they use the same rules as Spheres do. The only thing that really differs is which ones you need to perform a given Effect: for example, there are things that fall under the Field of Time but wouldn't be covered by the Sphere of Time that the Traditions use. I've tried to keep the names distinct to minimize confusion; but it wasn't always reasonable to do so.

    The nine Fields of Enlightened Science are:

    Energy (Force Dynamics)
    Matter (Material Science)
    Life (Biological Science)

    Space (Void Correspondence)
    Time (Temporal Mechanics)
    Void (Dimensional Science)

    Data (Information Science)
    Mind (Enlightened Psychology)
    Utility (Primal Utility)

    They often have more elaborate names, especially when a Scientist is discussing a specialty of a Field; but per the principle of Huffman Encoding, the names that get used most often tend to contract in length the more they're used; so while I'm including long-winded alternate names for each, I expect that the short names will tend to be used in practice.

    Here's how the Spheres map to the Fields:

    Correspondence Effects get split between Data and Space, with Data getting the Digital Web stuff and the stuff about Sympathetic Connections, while Space gets the measuring and control of spatial relationships. Each gets its own version of the Correspondence Range table, with Data's table being based on the one first introduced in the Revised NWO Convention Book while Space's table is based on the one that first appeared in the Revised Void Engineers Convention Book.

    Entropy Effects get split between Data and Time, with Data getting those dealing with order and chaos — “fate” and “luck” — while Time gets those that deal with decay, but rephrased In terms of aging.

    Forces Effects mostly go to Energy; but the ones having to do with motion and gravity go instead to Space.

    Life Effects go to Life. It's possible that some Effects move to different levels in the transition (Shapeshifting isn't something that Technocrats are into; but they're arguably more capable with inflicting and curing diseases); but other than some internal rearrangement, they essentially cover the same ground.

    Matter Effects likewise go to Material Science. Much like Biological Science is less about shapeshifting and more about manipulating health, Material Science is less about transmutation and more about manipulation of mechanisms.

    Mind Effects go directly to Enlightened Psychology as is, though the Technocracy has gone further than the Traditions both in terms of teaching techniques and in terms of altering the Consensus. There are also some Mind-like Effects added to Information Science, such as sensor spoofing (manipulation of perceptions) and memory alteration.

    Prime Effects get split between Void and Utility, with Void getting the concept of Primal Energy (e.g., blades made of Primal Energy) and most forms of Tass, while Utility gets everything that Primal Utility gets in M20 (such as Primal Ventures). Utility also holds the secrets of the Technocracy's ability to set up mass production lines for Devices and Principiae.

    Spirit Effects go to Dimensional Science (or “Void”) as per M20.

    Time Effects go to Temporal Mechanics.

    Turning this around:

    Energy gets most of the Forces Effects, except for the ones involving gravity and motion.

    Matter gets all of the Matter Effects, but rearranges some of them to emphasize mechanics over alchemy.

    Life gets all of the Life Effects, but rearranges some of them to emphasize vitality over shapeshifting.

    Space gets geometric manipulation from Correspondence (including teleportation) plus gravity manipulation and conveyance Effects from Forces.

    Time gets all of the Time Effects, plus Entropy Effects pertaining to decay and death (rephrased as “age control”).

    Void gets everything from Spirit (but with summoning, binding and warding de-emphasized in favor of Gauntlet manipulation), plus aspects of Prime that deal with things made of Prime. In particular, where summoning something ex-nihilo would require two dots of the Prime Sphere, it requires two dots of Void (to extract the necessary “zero-point energy” from the Quantum Vacuum).

    Data draws Digital Web access and Sympathetic Connections from Correspondence, and manipulation of order and chaos from Entropy; it also gets effects for dealing with the manipulation of sensors and digital records that echo Mind Effects, and effects for summoning, binding and warding digital entities that echo Spirit Effects. It also gets the “spatial manipulation” stuff from Correspondence, but only in the context of the Digital Web: that is, it can teleport, create gateways, co-locate, etc., just like Correspondence can, but only in the Digital Web. This is different from the Data Sphere variant described in M20, drastically limiting its Correspondence-related capabilities, and is the reason why I'm okay with it having so many other capabilities derived from Entropy, Mind, and Spirit.

    Mind gets all of the Mind Effects (except possibly language manipulation, which may be ported over to Data) and a few new Effects that are unique to the Technocracy.

    Utility gets the bulk of the Prime Effects, excepting only the ones that involve some sort of tangible manifestation of Primal Energy. It also skews the results toward Wonder crafting and tapping into sources of Quintessence that Prime doesn't even consider.


    That's the basics.

    I was tempted to transfer the “tangible manifestations of Quintessence” to Energy instead of Void to help offset the loss of Forces' gravity and motion Effects; but I stuck to Void because I think it's conceptually a better for there.

    The added functionality that Mind and Utility get are there to help explain the Union's current dominance: the former has given them an edge with the Consensus, and the latter explains the Requisitions Background.

    The intended changes to Matter and Life are to bring those two Fields in line with how the Technocracy tends to use them.

    Space and Data are set up the way they are to reflect the very different perspectives on Correspondence found in the NWO and VEs respectively.

    The Fields don't have an Entropy equivalent because Enlightened Science doesn't really work that way. Manipulation of order and chaos went to Data by thinking of them as identifying and manipulating small-p patterns, while the death and decay stuff went to Time mainly to bolster it; but thinking of it in terms of age manipulation works reasonably well.

    Most if not all of these Fields have parallels to branches of science: Energy, Space, and Time together represent physics; Matter covers chemistry and mechanics; Life covers most of the Life Sciences (though ecology might require some Data to deal with the intricate networks of interactions between different lifeforms). Data is all about the formal sciences: logic, mathematics, computer science, linguistics, etc. Mind is all about the bulk of the social sciences, save only for economics which is handled by Utility.

    Void is the only Field that doesn't correlate with a modern branch of science; instead, it deals with those phenomena that the Technocracy is attempting to excise from the Consensus: spirits, ghosts, alternate realities, mystical energies, etc.

    Thoughts? Suggestions?

              I think I'm going to stick to the notion of folding those elements of Prime that don't fit Utility into Void. The Doylean rationale is that I don't want Energy to be the Field used for ex nihilo Effects, mainly because Energy is one of three Fields that gets paired up with another Field of you want to make something out of nothing (the other two being the counterparts to Life and Matter).

              The Watsonian rationale is that the Conventions see Void (or Dimensional Science) as a Field that deals with the fundamental structure of reality, with all the business about the Gauntlet and the Umbrae being a case of pockets of reality that are sectioned off from the primary reality of Conventional Space. In that view of things, Ephemera and Primal Energy are variations on a common theme: Primal Energy is to Conventional Space as Ephemera are to the Umbrae, and Ephemera is what Primal Energy becomes when it's cut off from the material world for too long.

              So in that regard, Void deals not just with the discarded realities of the Umbrae, but also with the primary reality of the physical world. It is in the context of “dealing with the fundamental structure of the physical world” that the stuff ported in from Prime comes into play.

                  Here's a “first draft” of the Data Field:

                  Data (Information Science)
                  This Field deals with formal sciences ranging from mathematics and logic to linguistics and information science. It provides the ability to detect and manipulate probabilities, and to access and manipulate all things digital. It can also cause changes to other information media, such the information being transmitted in radio signals and the words and images in a book — though for now, the latter is still firmly in the realm of Hypertech. In the Digital Web, it can be used just like Space.

                  Data is often used in conjunction with Space for physical travel into the Digital Web (using a combination of Data's Web Access and Space's teleportation or portal effects), and with other Fields to extend their effects to reach subjects that aren't physically present.

                  Rank One Effects:
                  • Sense Connection: the Scientist can detect effects utilizing Data to bypass distance.
                  • Analyze Chance: as per Entropy.
                  • Decrypt: as per Entropy.
                  • Nonlinear Prediction: as per Entropy.
                  • Ring of Truth: as per Entropy.
                  • Detect Digital Activity: as per Mind's Detect Thoughts, but for data processing machinery.
                  • Mind Mapping: the Scientist can run programs in his head, such as spreadsheets and databases. The number of successes required depends on how elaborate the programs are.

                  Rank Two Effects:
                  • Remote Viewing: like Correspondence Sensing.
                  • Mask Signature: the Scientist can hide his use of Data from those using Sense Connection to detect it.
                  • Web Access: A successful use of this Adjustment allows the Scientist to mentally enter the Digital Web in a form of Astral Immersion (M20 p.467). Where M20 “requires good VR equipment” and “an Intelligence + Computer roll (three successes, difficulty 7)” along with two dots of Correspondence, these rules treat it as a standard use of Enlightened Science, with the equipment requirements replaced by an appropriate Instrument such as Computers and IT Gear (M20 p.590) or Internet Activity (Book of Secrets pp.208–209), and the Intelligence + Computer roll being replaced by Abilities Enhancing Magick (M20 p.533). This effect is a Simple Feat, only requiring a single success; though additional successes may be needed to extend the duration. Adding Space ••• or •••• to the effect allows for Holistic Immersion instead: again, this is treated as an Enlightened Science Procedure, replacing the rules given on M20 p.467 with the usual “get the right Instruments, engage an appropriate Practice, and get enough successes on an Enlightenment roll” rules common to all Enlightened Science Procedures.
                  • Beginner's Luck: as per Entropy.
                  • Fortune's Protection: as per Entropy.
                  • Games of Luck: as per Entropy.
                  • Firewall: as per the Rank Two version of Mind's “Mind Shield” effect, but shields data processing devices instead of minds.
                  • Networking: as per Mind's Rank Three “Mental Networking” effect, but for data manipulation gear. In conjunction with Mind •••, “machine minds” and living minds can be networked together.
                  • Call Memophore: as per Spirit's “Call Spirit”, but for Digital Web Entities (M20 pp.638–639).

                  Rank Three Effects:
                  • Chain: as per Correspondence.
                  • Fake activity: as per the Rank Three version of Mind's “False Thoughts”, but for data processing machinery.
                  • Cunning Linguist: as per Mind.
                  • Sensor Spoofing: as per Mind's “Illusory Input”, but for sensory devices.
                  • Probe Activity: as per Mind's “Probe Thoughts”, but for data processing devices.
                  • Denial of Service: as per Mind's “Psychic Assault”, but for data processing devices.
                  • Technopathy: as per Mind's “Telepathy”, but for data processing devices.
                  • Harm Memophore: as per Spirit's “Harm Spirit”, but for Digital Web Entities.
                  • Heal Memophore: as per Spirit's “Heal Spirit”, but for Digital Web Entities.

                  Rank Four Effects:
                  • Alter Memory: as per Mind's “Manipulate Memory”, but for data storage media. Among other things, this is what lets you alter the words on a page.
                  • Control Memophore: as per Spirit's “Control Spirit”, but for Digital Web Entities.

                  Rank Five Effects:
                  • Probable Cause: as per Entropy.
                  • Forge Memophore: as per Spirit's “Forge Spirit”, but for Digital Web Entities.

                  Data Connections:
                  The rules for using Data to establish remote connections are the same as for Correspondence, with the caveat that if the subject isn't online, he's beyond Data's reach. The Correspondence Range table gets expanded to include more ways of establishment a connection to the subject:

                  • Subject's presence or body sample; source code; government ID database information
                  •• A treasured possession or intimate companion, sample code, personal email or social media account.
                  ••• Casual possession or associate, legacy code, Internet stock puppet, throwaway email or media account.
                  •••• Acquaintance of or item from subject, derivative code, IP address.
                  ••••• Operator briefly touched or meet subject, code from same language, rerouted IP string.

                      Hello Dataweaver, thank you for inviting me here. I've read some of your first post, I haven't read it all nor the answers to it, I'll do in a few moments, but first I want to add a few scientific definitions and perhaps some preliminary thoughts, just in order to create a guide that may serve as a pillar to order our thoughts. "Void" and "Utility", being more "arcane" Spheres, probably won't have "scientific" descriptions, but I'll try to come up with the best possible from philosophy or even pseudoscience if no better source can be find.

                      I'll be editing this comment for a while, so just stick with me for a little:

                      ENERGY

                      The word “energy” comes from the Greek enérgeia. Developed by Aristotle, enérgeia has no direct translation to English. It is frequently described as “being at work”.
                      What is energy?
                      Scientists define energy as the ability to do work.

                      There are many forms of energy:
                      • Heat
                      • Light
                      • Motion
                      • Electrical
                      • Chemical
                      • Gravitational
                      • Nuclear

                      These forms of energy can be grouped into two general types of energy for doing work:
                      - Potential, or stored, energy
                      - Kinetic, or working, energy

                      Source - U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) - www.eia.gov
                      Work, in physics, measure of energy transfer that occurs when an object is moved over a distance by an external force at least part of which is applied in the direction of the displacement.

                      Source: https://www.britannica.com/science/work-physics
                      So, very well. All of this is for us to understand that in science, Energy deals with everything that either causes or has the potential to cause MOVEMENT.

                      Everything about energy is related to movement in one way or another; everything in the universe is in constant movement; we could even say that the universe "vibrates" down to it sub atomic level (the String theory of physics deals exactly with that prospect, although... Well, let's not go into that bag of worms for now, because as it stands, String theory may end up becoming the Relativity of the 21th century, or the "universal Ether" equivalent of failed theory, so let's keep that aside for now).

                      "Movement" is Kinetic energy; it's energy creating "work" (work here in physical terms, aka the movement of something across space) over a macro scale.

                      "Light" is the movement of the subatomic particles known as photons.

                      "Heat" is the vibrational movement at a molecular scale. The more intensity in the vibration of molecules, the higher the heat

                      "Nuclear" energy is a bit more complex. There are actually two "nuclear" forces, the strong and the weak.

                      The strong nuclear force is responsible for keeping atoms together. Atoms are made of a nucleus that has protons and neutrons bound together, with electrons orbiting that nucleus.

                      Very well. But Protons and Neutrons are all made of a combination of 3 particles each, known as Quarks. So, each Proton or Neutron is composed of 3 Quarks; the types of Quarks will define if the result will be a Proton or Neutron.

                      The force that thus keeps those Quarks together is the Strong Nuclear Force. That Force is also responsible for keeping the Protons and Neutrons "glued" together inside of the nucleus of the Atom.

                      The weak nuclear force is the one responsible for the radioactive decay of particles.

                      And yes, those two forces are what fission or fusion liberates as energy.

                      Gravitational energy is an energy that comes from anything that has mass; most of everything in the universe has mass, even most subatomic particles, so most of everything has gravity - but there actually are exceptions. Photons, for instance, the very same particles that carry light energy, DON'T have mass, and therefore, have no Gravity.

                      ​​​​Electrical energy is the power an atom's charged particles have to cause an action or move an object. The movement of electrons from one atom to another is what results in electrical energy.

                      Now, in here I think it's important to talk about the 4 fundamental physical Forces:
                      - Strong Nuclear Force;
                      - Electromagnetism;
                      - Weak Nuclear Force;
                      - Gravity

                      And I purposefully ordered them from strongest to weakest.

                      But why is gravity the weakest if it is capable of moving galaxies?

                      Well, the strong and weak nuclear forces have a very short range; basically, they only affect stuff inside of the atom and not beyond.

                      Electromagnetism is far stronger than Gravity (that's easy to attest, a small magnet is capable of attracting a clip and thus beat the gravity of the entire Earth), but the reason to why it doesn't move galaxies is because there are the same quantity of electrically charged particles positive as there are negative, so they cancel each others out.

                      And thus, despite Gravity being the weakest, it affects everything because it has long range (the entire universe actually) and is not cancelled by an opposing force.

                      Now I must discuss a bit about chemical energy, and that's a bit trickier. Because that's NOT a source of "energy" that comes from physics, but rather from chemistry.

                      Chemical energy is present in atomic interactions in a "potential" form. There are 3 forms of atomic interactions upon which different atoms "bind" together (forgive me, but I don't know all the proper names in English). Those are:
                      - Metallic;
                      - Ionic and
                      - Molecular

                      "Metallic" refers to the characteristic of certain elements to be "negatively charged"... Hmmm... That's a... Bit complex to explain, but I'll try in a very simple way.

                      But... I'm afraid we'll have to go to the very beginning...

                      So, an "atom" is the smallest component of matter. Kind of... There are "subatomic particles" but... That's another story...

                      So, atoms have basically 3 components, protons, which are charged positively, neutrons, which have neutral ("zero") charge, and serve basically to keep the protons "glued" inside the nucleus, and electrons which are charged negative and orbit the nucleus in constant movement.

                      What defines one element from another is the amount of protons it has in the nucleus. So, the most common element in the universe is Hidrogen, with just a single proton. The second most common is Helium, with 2 protons.

                      That's what defines the period table. Each one of the elements there has a different amount of protons, from 1 (Hidrogen) to the hypothetical artificial ones (with hundreds).

                      So, Carbon for example has 14 protons, and so on.

                      There is a complication thou about how the electrons orbit the nucleus of an atom; each electron has 1 negative charge and each Proton, 1 positive, and they cancel each other.

                      Positive attracts negative, thus an atom of Carbon should attract 14 electrons.

                      However, since all electrons are negative, they REPEL each other, and thus they don't all orbit the nucleus in the same way, but rather in LAYERS.

                      And there's a specific distribution for that (which I won't get into right now), but on every atom, the last layer can only ever have a max of 8 electrons.

                      Now, atoms that have either 2 or 8 electrons in the last layer are highly stable and will NEVER combine with anything, so much so that on Earth you'll never find them on any state but as gas; it's the so called "Noble Gases", like Helium or Argonium - remember when I said that Helium has 2 protons? Well, that means that it has 2 electrons, thus it has only a single layer of electrons, and that layer has exactly 2 electrons, meaning that this is a stable atom.

                      Hidrogen, on the other hand, only has a single electron. This means that, in order for it to become stable, the Hidrogen needs to "borrow" an electron from another atom, thus getting is electron layer stable with 2.

                      In chemistry, we say therefore that Hidrogen has a "1 positive charge", because it has a tendency to "snatch" 1 electron.

                      The family of elements known as "metals", however, have "negative charges" because they have a tendency to "expell" electrons from their electron cloud. Such is the case of any elements with 3 electrons in their last layer for example, which will have the tendency to expell one of theirs in order to become stable with 1.

                      Metallic interactions are thus formed by those metallic elements. In a metallic interaction, all the atoms are, roughly saying, constantly "pushing" electrons between each other. The electrons are running wild between metals, which is why this is the strongest interaction. It's also why metals can conduct electricity - the electrons "travel freely" inside of them.

                      Them comes the Ionic interactions, which are the interactions of atoms with positive and negative charges (aka atoms that "snatch" and "repel" electrons). The most familiar to us is common Salt, which is an atom of Na (Sodium, Na is from the name in Latin) and Cl (chlorine? Damn, I don't know the name in English, sorry). Anyway, Na has 1 positive charge (it attracts 1 electron) and Cl has 1 negative charge (it repels 1 electron).

                      Because of that, they form a very strong ionic interaction between one another (salt dissolves in water because water, H2O, actually becomes dissociated into H+ and OH- ions, which will attract the Cl and Na ions from salt respectively)

                      And the last one is the molecular interaction which is the weakest of the 3 (but still very strong - our bodies are made of molecules after all), which is the interactions between positively charged atoms.

                      Our most basic components - Hidrogen, Oxygen, Carbon and Nitrogen - are all positively charged, thus they form molecular bounds easily


                      WELL...

                      What happens is that not all atomic interactions are born equal; some are stronger than other, more stable than others.

                      And external energy can break those bonds. That's the whole thing we call as "chemistry". Chemistry is, essentially, the study of the bonds between atoms.

                      So, for instance, a molecule of Oxygen is made up of 2 atoms of Oxygen (O2).

                      However, if you bombard the Oxygen molecules with energy (say, for instance, photons coming from the Sun), they may break up and form Ozone instead, which are molecules made of a trio of Oxygen atoms (O3) with a highly energetic and unstable interaction.

                      Now, nature always tends from "unstable" to "stable", but it is possible to move in the other direction by application of energy; with enough heat for example, you can undo even the metallic interactions.

                      So, Ozone for example has "chemical energy" stored in the form of unstable interaction between atoms. If another molecule that is more stable than Ozone and that has affinity towards it passes by, it can break the Ozone and "steal" that energy away.

                      Biochemical processes make use of that all the time. Sugar (Glucose, C6H12O6, meaning 6 atoms of carbon, 12 of Hidrogen and 6 of Oxygen) is the main source of "energy" for most living beings. That "energy" comes from the dissociation of this large molecule, either into alcohol by bacteria that doesn't use Oxygen (alcohol is C2H6O) or CO2 and water for all others that breath oxygen.

                      The extra energy that was stored in the glucose molecules is used for all the metabolic process of the living beings, used to build other kinds of molecules or for movement.
