                    Joined lately to the crowd, reading through the pages too.

                    I suspect it boils down to the average favoritism of players. A Werewolf player WANTS her splat to be the apex predator, a Mage player WANTS mages to be unbeatable gods. The truth is, as frequently is somewhere in the middle, and I think the majority knows that.

                    A mage could be very good at bashing things with proper build and foci, like an Akashayana, but ultimately they're humans and have more limitations, resource-wise and in other things. Werewolves are good at bashing per definition. That's their thing, that even their non-combat focused characters are better at combat than the other splats' similar ones with their forms and rage, while ALSO being good on their specific build. Mages would be better at other things, like sheer flexibility, insane ritual effects and information gathering,b ut they have to work really hard to be able to go toe-to-toe with a combat-focused garou, especially without preparation.

                    It's a little bit like the ranger/fighter debate in D&D. People are complaining since 3e that rangers are not as good as fighters, that instead of the ranger being the iconic two-weapon fighter, thanks to Drizzt, you could build a better two-weapon fighter with the fighter class. Well, duh, the fighter's purpose is to be the best at fighting, the ranger's purpose is to be flexible and being competent in more areas, including fighting.

                    If nothing worked, then let's think!

                                Yes, I agree with the idea that every supernatural creature has their specialty. Changelings are meant to be artists, Garou are meant to be combat monsters, Kindred are meant to be social manipulators, Mages are meant to be jack-of-all-trades but master-of-none, etc. Mages are capable of amazing feats, they can turn lead into gold and teleport from LA to London for tea, but they really should avoid combat with other supernatural creatures (and use diplomacy and bargaining to convince other supernatural creatures to fight on their behalf).

                                Most supernatural creatures do not care about Mages, they have other problems and Mages can get very capable allies by helping other supernatural deal with the problems that they are not good at. If Bone Gnawers are going to lose their favorite park, Mages can manipulate the system to stop them from losing the park. If Malkavians are worried about an asylum being closed due to lack of funding, Mages can literally make gold to donate to save the asylum. Mages can be the fixers of the supernatural world if they want to and can be the difference between a peaceful city and a city with constant warfare between supernatural factions.
