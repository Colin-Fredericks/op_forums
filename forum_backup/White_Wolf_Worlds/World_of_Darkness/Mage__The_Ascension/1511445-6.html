                The progression of Matter sphere is a question of how drastic the change is.

                transmutation of material is actually a very subtle change compared to anything else. You can only affect a single material, it’s shape cannot be altered, but the material it is made of can be altered for a limited amount of time. And you cannot transform it in to anything that would change its state.

                changing an objects shape is a more drastic change, and fits in with the other effects that Matter 3 enables. That of fusing objects together or breaking them apart, both of which enable the changing of the end product’s shape. In fact this breaking apart or fusing is essentially how one is able to cause an object to change its state, by dispersing the material into a gas or bringing a liquid together into a solid, though such a thing will only be temporary unless the melting/boiling point of the object is changed (which requires Matter 5 to do so)

                The Melt and Reform rote exemplifies the differences between said progression quite well. At matter 2 you can change a bar of metal in to clay. For the duration you may mould it or shape it using your mundane skills, before it returns to metal in whatever shape you shaped it.

                whereas with Matter 3, you can directly shape the metal bar without transforming it first.

                this fits the progression quite nicely to matter 4, where you can force the metal bar to essentially transform into whatever object you desire, such as the ever popular gun.


                i suspect the reason it feels strange is we consider an objects material to be less immutable than its shape, but in honesty, besides being harder, softer, or holding slightly different properties like whether or not it conducts heat or electricity better, changing the material of an object doesn’t really do very much compared to changing its shape. Once you have access to changing an object’s shape, you have a great deal of utility open to you, which to me befits a 3rd rank effect far better.
