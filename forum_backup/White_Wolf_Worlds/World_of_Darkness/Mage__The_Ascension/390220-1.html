                    I think a lot of people focus on the "decay" aspect of Entropy, when the fate/fortune order/chaos stuff is more interesting and has less in common with other Spheres. Here's an example of something you can do relatively easily that would be difficult with other Spheres alone: instant room (or lab, or Hermetic Library, or crime scene)-cleaning. Entropy 3, maybe Matter 2 or Prime 2 Forces 3 as well to cut down a little on the number of required successes--but take away the Entropy and you're looking at a very high number of required successes.

Any phenomenon that can be simply described in terms of probability is very straightforward to affect with Entropy 2. The best thing is is, Entropy is frequently Coincidental (the Etherite lab-organizing grenade being a notable exception).

                                                            Originally posted by Faradn View Post
                                                            I think a lot of people focus on the "decay" aspect of Entropy, when the fate/fortune order/chaos stuff is more interesting and has less in common with other Spheres. .
                                                            I agree 100%. In fact, I think the association with decay is an artifact of naming the Sphere "Entropy". If it had been named something (ANYTHING) else relevant, we wouldn't see decay brought in. I think the IP is facing some of those issues. The various levels of Entropy have one focus firmly aimed at "decay" and those are often the aspects of that number of dots that seems redundant with the other Spheres. (E.g. Why do I need Entropy to decay or prevent decay in a material object when that seems like something that would easily come under almost all paradigmatic views of Matter? Etc. Etc. )

                                                            The focus on decay is what gets Entropy uses, particularly the Euthanatos, stereotyped as necromancers, when, to most of the constituent groups in that Tradition, most of the powers people think of when they hear "necromancer" are downright anathema.

                                                            The problem is that there is a paucity of words that accomplish encompassing the dichotomy as described by Faradn... Fate/Destiny vs. Chance/Fortune vs. Order/Chaos. And selection of one does characterize how the Sphere is percieved from then on. If you name it Change or Chaos or Luck, then you have to define order, fate, destiny as "reducing the chaos in the system" and vice-versa if you name it Fate/Order/Destiny. Both make the Sphere harder to work with, since fully half of it is going to be counter-intuitive. Which is another of the problems with calling it Entropy. It characterizes it so that half the uses are actually "anti-Entropy". Plus that word means vastly different things to science/engineer types and everyone else. And if you pick one you really seem to be privileging technomancy or denigrating it (which isn't true, it's connecting paradigm to definition and that's not, strictly speaking, the case, or, at least, does not have to be.)

                                                            I'm sure that these issues are a major reason why Awakening splits off decay into Fate (which does take out the "interesting stuff" that Faradn pointed out and detail it out into its own Sphere, though there are some issues with crossover/redundency with Time and Mind) and Death (which has it's own problems.... since, once again, why do I need Death to decay an object when I can use Matter? Make darkness when that seems like a lock for Forces? Make people afraid instead of using Mind? etc. ).

                                                            I have toyed with a possible solution of just abolishing Prime and Entropy and having Order and Chaos, or something similar as way to deal with the situation, but it feels somehow ... off. I have never gotten past the stage of outlining it. I'm also mentally feeling out the similar idea of having Prime mean something more like " Pattern" and Entropy taking on "Chaos" with the sense of transgressing boundaries. Maybe "Change" would be a better name for the Sphere. All creation being a tension between the two....
