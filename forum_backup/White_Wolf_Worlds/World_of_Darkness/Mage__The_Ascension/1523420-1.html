                Originally posted by Psicose View Post
                Okay, this got out of hand a little. Anyway, here's another question. Supposing that a Technomage, whether traditional or a technocrat, wanted to download information from a person's memory and transfer it to a machine, like a smartphone for example, what spheres would be needed? I believe that as there is transference, so Correspondence 2 is necessary and Mind 2 (at least) since it involves memories, but would anything else be necessary? Life or Matter, perhaps.

                Also, let's avoid going into the subject of how much human memory fits in a machine's memory, that's not the focus.​
                Ok, so, you would like to take the memories out of someone's head and convert them in to something else which can be carried around with you. There are a number of different ways one could achieve this.

                First, you will need Mind sphere, this is a given. And in order to access memories you would need at least Mind 3 to view them, and Mind 4 to manipulate them. Whether you need to view or to manipulate will depend on what it is you are going to do else with them.
                Going with the idea of converting the person's memories in to something you can carry around with you, this would essentially be a form of transmutation, this would require manipulation levels of Mind, so Mind 4 for memories, Mind 3 for surface thoughts and Mind 2 for feelings, sensations, and vague impressions. What you transmute them in to to remove them from the person's mind would depend on which sphere you were using, transmute them into a solid object with Matter, a living thing with Life, or a form of energy with Forces, there are of course caveats for each.

                For Matter, the level of matter will dictate the form said memory will take. Matter 2 lets you transmute materials, so said object would be something quite simple like a stone, glass orb, a paper disc, very few features to speak of. Matter 3 lets you shape matter objects, and so you could make something like a key, a cup, a candlestick, a fine mist you can bottle or a water you can drink. Said objects would, if interacted with appropriately, may give you glimpses of the memory within, using the key on the correct door can give you an insight into what the memory contains, liquids held in a cup could reflect aspects of said memory, candles burning in the candlestick may let you see aspects of the memory play out in the shadows it casts. Matter 4 lets you create more complex objects, this would be how you could create a flashdrive out of it, and just as with the above examples, using it appropriately may let you experience the memories. Just double clicking on the file may sudden give you than sensation of living through it.

                Life would be very similar to Matter, but the steps would be Life 3 to transmute it in to a simple living pattern, such as a plant, seed, or an insect, or graft it on to a part of your own body like making this memory a third eye for your head. Life 4 to transmute it into a part of someone else's body or to transmute the memory in to some kind of animal, and Life 5 to transform it into a specific living creature of higher order, like a human. The exact level of Life required may be a bit debatable for some paradigms, but I hope this gives you an idea of the concept at least.

                Forces would provide you that option to transform the memory in to something energy connected. Now, the danger here is such a thing would not last very long without having something in place to contain it. Transforming the memory in to light will let you see the memory, turning it into electricity might let you feel the memory as it arcs around the room and hits whomever, turning in to fire would have it burn in a manner befiting whatever the memory depicted, etc. It might even be that some memories are so unlike a particular form of energy that to transform it into an opposing energy might indeed alter, change, or otherwise cause some form of reaction. A memory that was steeped in darkness, for example, might fade quickly when transformed in to light. A memory which is about a harsh rage could cause a great inferno when turned to fire, but might be altered to one of gentle loathing if transformed into cold energy. Stuff for the ST to play around with based on paradigm and resonance of course. And all these examples would only require the addition of Forces 3
                So, to transform your target's memories into electricity which can be stored on your flashdrive, Mind 4 Forces 3 would have you covered for that, however, if the flashdrive isn't suitably large enough to store it, or if the memories are so strong and violent they could cause damage, well, they might not stay in that flashdrive for very long, and even worse they might burst out at the first sign of failure of the flashdrive. Mundane objects like that were not really designed for holding electrified memories after all. Again, thing for the ST to play with.

                If you wanted to do any of this from a distance you would need to appropriate level of correspondence (corr = to higher sphere).~

                However, if your goal is to simply view these memories on a device, you do not need to transform memories at all. Instead, this could all be done as a form of scrying using your smart device as an instrument to enable scrying. To do this, all you need is the Viewing level of Mind (for memories this would be simply Mind 3) and the appropriate level of correspondence (in this case, corr 3).
                The memories aren't being taken away, changed, or stored, they are simply being accessed from a distance.

                                Originally posted by Psicose View Post
                                And here's a question: Aren't thoughts already electrical impulses?​
                                ​
                                Sorry, got a bit over-focused on just the system.

                                Thoughts aren't just electrical impulses. We have, IRL, wonderfully advanced machines for measuring the electrical activity in the human brain, but we keep hitting a wall when it comes to trying to decode thought. It is possible we just need an insanely larger dataset than we currently have and probably a high end AI algorithm to brute force it all, but it's far more likely that the well know chemical mediators of neural activity create too much "noise" in the electrical field to ever capture thoughts that way. Or, in other words, because our brains aren't circuits, the way the biochemistry of brain activity impacts the electrical part of the equation is essential to what constitutes a "thought."

                                As a basic example, if you're feeling cold, you might think of having a warm beverage, or to put on an additional layer of clothing. What you think of doing is impacted by mixture of chemical and electrical signals in your brain. We can follow the electrical impulse of your temperature sensory pathways from your skin to your brain, but once they're in the brain they get filtered through that complex mixture of chemical and electric filters that leads to a specific inclination towards what "kind" of cold you feel and thus what kind of solution you feel would be best to take.

                                Originally posted by Psicose View Post
                                I know that if you create a healing potion or something, you need life (variable level) matter and prime,
                                You don't need Matter to craft Wonders out of inert materials. The skilled manipulation of your materials via Abilities (even if sometimes things like Esoterica or Hypertech) is considered sufficient for something like a healing potion. While not specified directly, you would only need Matter in a case like enchanting a natural spring to produce water with healing properties. You can think of this as basically the rules' answer to a Ship of Theseus style issue. If the target for a Wonder is going to be made up of the same components a year later as it is now, you don't need extra Spheres beyond Prime and whatever Spheres for the effect itself. If the components would change over time, you need to add a Sphere relative to the target you're making into a Wonder. This is also the justification for why Matter is unique here: other Sphere's deal with phenomena that are in states of change. You need Life to turn a living pattern into a Wonder to address that life grows, changes, and replaces itself over time.

                                But suppose I don't want or can't use life, could I use time instead? Like returning the state of the wound to before it was made?
                                The problem is that you can't target a wound specifically without Life. You would have to rewind the entire target (and you need Entropy to set a conditional point in time like "when I got injured"). For an injury this might not be so bad since you're dealing with relatively short time scales,but there's a lot of problems with using Time because the books want Time magic to not get wildly out of hand. Considering how much the game does not like time travel or going overboard with reversing time and so on, avoiding Life is a really rough choice.
