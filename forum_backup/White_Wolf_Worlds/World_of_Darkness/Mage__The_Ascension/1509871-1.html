        I've long been of the opinion that Mage only works if you allow for the possibility that the Consensus isn't the be-all and end-all of reality. M20 addresses this with its Earthly Foundations, where there are certain aspects of reality that will be true even if the locals believe otherwise; though those truths oughtn't be as specific as “the laws of physics as understood by modern scientists are true”. They should be more broad-brush facts like “things fall down when you drop them” and “water flows downhill”. But even things like Newton's observation that the motions of the celestial bodies are described by the same mathematics as falling apples would be more of a Focus element rather than a fundamental truth: scientists see germs through their microscopes because that's what their paradigm says that they'll see, not because germs are actually real.

        I wouldn't want M5 going the route of Awakening and saying that the laws of science are true, and that Magick is a way of overriding them. But there should be something to the effect that the Consensus influences Reality rather than defining it; and the question of what else defines reality besides the Consensus should be left as an open question with several possible answers (e.g., natural laws, or godlike spiritual entities) but nothing conclusive.

        My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

            Thinking of how you could evolve / re-conceptualize lore for M5, and taking some notes from V5, I'd place both the Technocracy and the Traditions as an old crumbling order which is descending into Civil War as the Nephandi reveal the full extent of their infiltration into both Sleeper and Mage society.

Archmages and Control are openly speaking of evacuating what of the Human Race might be salvaged into Horizon Realms.

            Will you fight to create pockets of "safe and stable" Reality, stopping the descent of your place into madness and corruption, or will you start exploring new realms in which start over?
