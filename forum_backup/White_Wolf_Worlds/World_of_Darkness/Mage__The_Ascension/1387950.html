    M20: questions about casting spell?

    05-13-2020, 04:13 AM
    Gathering info about the 20 anniversary edition I came up with some question about spellcasting rules:

    1) To cast any spell you roll only Arete + bonus dice, it's correct? Don't you think it's a very minimal dice pool?

    2) A difficulty spell refers to the target number or it's about the ammount of the successes needed to cast a spell?

      1) Bonus dice? Well, it is a small pool. While a few successes can go a long way, I think the pool was kept small to make magic inherently unreliable and force players to spend time preparing powerful spells as opposed to slinging them on the fly.

      2) Difficulty refers to target number.

      Edit: Just to mention, despite the small pool for magic rolls, starting mages built and used right can be quite powerful.

                                                          A small but notable rules change in M20 is that affects on others require at least 2 successes rather than an additional success. The first target is part of the package but you need 2 successes or the spell fails as opposed to you need x successes +1 for targeting someone other than yourself. When you look at the damage chart, you'll note that the damage for 1, 2, 3... Successes is 0, 2, 6, 8 ,10. Meaning at 3 or more successes, it's like you didn't waste a success targeting someone.
