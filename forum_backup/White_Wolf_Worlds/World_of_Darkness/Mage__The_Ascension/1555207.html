          There's not much to say. It looks like your average Cabal.

          Two observations:

          Maybe the most polemical thing it's that the Verbena and Dreamspeaker "have found allies among the Garou due to similar worldviews".

          Garou dislike of mages has very little to do with having philosophical discrepancies, and a lot to do with deeply ingrained prejudice.

          In general trying to ally with Garou requires being incredibly close to their culture from the get-go (Easiest way of having that: the Kinfolk Merit ). Being successfull otherwise implies you've miraculously managed to avoided the minefield that it's Garou paranoia w/o even knowing there's one in the first place. Sharing a similar worldview (whatever that means) helps very little with that, and may even make things worse.

          Because, from the perspective of the Garou worldview, mages have no business existing in the first place (or, well, doing magick instead of accepting the laws of the world like good apes should). Humans having magick it's a mistake, it's fucked up. For some Garou, mages communing with the spirits and respecting Gaia it's the least they can do if they insist in using dangerous powers they shouldn't be using. So, sharing such a worldview may appease them a little. But for other Garou, that's even worse because communing with spirits it's their divine right and not of humans.

          Unless - of course - the ST it's willing to ignore all the potential problems present in the Lore in favor of a "nature-lovers together strong" sort of narrative ... then all I have said does not matter at all

          Euthanatos and the Brotherhood had an old feud, but it's mostly water under the bridge now since that war happened centuries ago (unless they have Past Lives that have something to say about that, or their mentors are/were related to the war)

                          Originally posted by Aleph View Post
                          Garou dislike of mages has very little to do with having philosophical discrepancies, and a lot to do with deeply ingrained prejudice.
                          The things you presented, while real, are far from a rule among Garou. They don't even understand Sphere Magic nearly enough to have a strong opinion on it as a general thing, or to understand they're all the "same thing" to begin with. So, many will find the ideas of humans wielding such powers abhorrent, but many won't have a strong opinion one way or the other.

                          But that said, they are overall very paranoid, and frequently with reason. Mages in particular pose another, more pragmatic threat to them: they drain Caerns. Caerns are powerful Nodes, and even like-minded Mages frequently don't understand their place and importance for Garou society, or for the world at large under Garou POV.

                          Now, that said, some Traditions do have a history of alliances with the Garou, so it isn't completely far-fetched, although it would need more elaboration upon.

                          Either way, any Mage working with Garou would be well advised to keep a wide berth from Caerns at all costs, never even mention approaching one, and if you ever get near one by any reason, even helping to save or recover it, first order of the day is to get away A.S.A.P.. And never, never, never, NEVER take any Quint from a Caern for any purpose, through any means.

                          Also, any Fetish found that was made by Shapechangers or owned by them is theirs, and treat it as sacred before delivering, because it is.

                                  While the Kinfolk Merit is useful for these characters we should also remember the Supernatural Ally merit. The second merit implies a certain level of trust and alliance.

                                  Frankly, if you're going this route have the Garou allies have their own ideas and goals. After all, if the Garou allies have some awareness of the capacities of Mages, then they might want practical help. Mages could use their abilities to get information for the Garou that's either unavailable or brutally hard to get for Garou. Mages could cause Pentex equipment to short out at the best times for Garou. Wards against Black Spiral Dancers that give Gaian Garou the edge. Garou would be delighted to get favors. A little harder to get favors from, but delighted to receive. ;-)

                                  Going in the opposite direction. I once ran a Werewolf campaign were a powerful local Hermetic had reasons to hate Pentex, or at least the local branch. He offered the Garou bits of Intel and made useful accidents happen on cue. The Garou Elders gave the PCs hell for the Mage interfering, but they accepted the results.
