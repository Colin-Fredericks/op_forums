          I would personally make sculpting Intelligence + Crafts, but improving the roll wouldn't require a lot by itself.

          For properties, I think being able to change colors to blend with local statuary, masonry or columns would be nice. Blend with stone and masonry would also be interesting, even if just superficially to become a part of the fixture without breaking it. And vibration-based senses, so their human-ish appearance doesn't actually give any clue about their sensory abilities and dependencies.

          While gargoyles are always a classic, I have a particular falling for caryatid columns, and enchantments to let them leave their places without causing structural damage to the location is important. Also Mind effects to either make them inconspicuous (people do not feel strange to have a statue or column there, maybe even half-remember some possible explanation), make people ignore objects they carry (they can appreciate the statue, but whatever it takes or carries that isn't stone becomes invisible or look like a part of the statue), and/or improve the feelings the work itself causes (whatever it is, just increases their artistic impact as taken by the observer).

          You could have an easier time just making the statue invisible with Forces, but I think it kind of defeats the whole idea of using a statue in the first place.

                          Well, Forces 2 Matter 3 is enough to allow the statue to move around as if alive. Since Alice doesnt have Matter 3, only Forces 2, she can enchant the statue to move as a solid block, as a big rock.

                          With Mind 3 she can connect the movement with the statue's "mind". If some other member of her Cabal has Matter 3, Mind 3 and Forces 2, creating the self moving statue would be a big favor.

                          As for the "intellect" of it, it can be replaced by a spirit obviously, since Mind 5 aint that easy to find. Spirit 2 is enough to summon, and after a while and a lot of perseverance, eventually you'll find a spirit willing and capable of inhabiting the statue by its own. To place UNCAPABLE spirits however, but who would otherwise be more suitable and more cooperative, you would need Spirit 4, that way you're not dependant on the spirit having the power to do it, and such spirit would be much more grateful, since it's only by your own grace that it is allowed such an opportunity.

                          Not of that is necessary thou, because with Spirit 3 you can simply awaken the Spirit of the Gargoyle itself, and let the statue decide by itself. The advantage here is that the gargoyle would most likely be fiercily loyal; Prisha, who has Spirit 3, would after all be her "mother". Unless treated as a mindless slave, such spirit would probably have undying loyalty, going beyond the "line of duty".

                          The best part is, the effect for making the statue move by itself (Mind 3, Forces 2, Matter 3) doesnt need to be made togheter with the one to awaken the spirit of it, therefore they can be done by different Mages.

                          Of course, all these effects have to be permanent.

                          Now that this is settled, let us see what else can be done to give some cool upgrades to this little thing...

                          Well, Mind 2 can be used to give it "Terror", an aura of fear.

                          Let Prisha use Entropy 3 to make it extra durable.

                          Matter 2 can "regenerate" it as needed.

                          There's more obviously, but I have to think about it, the reason being that I know all Sphere's levels by memory, but only from third edition. I need to check M20 to see in detail all the modifications in order not to pass some outdated information
