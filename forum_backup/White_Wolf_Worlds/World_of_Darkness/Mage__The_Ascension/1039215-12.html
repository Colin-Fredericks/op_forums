    Dear Journal.

    We're 12 pages in and there's no sign of a cease fire.

    Diplomacy has failed, Communications are tense, and appeals of "Guys! You're both right!" have fallen to these steadfast legions.

    On one side, the Anti-crats. Adamant in their assertions that Tech is only a solution for the destruction tech has brought upon the earth.
    While opposing them, are the Techno-pros, Unwilling to shift their claim that without Tech there would be no progress, and only stagnation.

    It's hard to think back, all those....4 days ago, how did this all begin? A simple comment, an expression of an opinion. an exchange of conflicting views... Then came the Anthropologists and the Historians, experts in their fields, willing to use real world claims to back up their notes. Then the Canon's were brought out, each one firing an impressive display of completely made up but generally accepted an adhered to facts. The carnage was brutal on all sides was brutal.
    Throughout the flurry of History lessons and Source book references, a number of voices were heard, appeals to emotion. Cries for the glory that is tech, anger towards the evil that is control. All of which built up a great catalogue of interpretive thoughts.

    We are all experts. We are all essayists. We are all the masters of our own downfall. Don't you see? We brought this upon ourselves. For each one feels entitled to their own interpretation, but to justify their own convictions, they must destroy any attempt to undermine our own theories. Our own theories about a world which we created, and we have the power to change because it doesn't exist.

    The facts are not facts. The History is optional. The main events are modular. And the masterminds are whatever makes sense at the time. Such ideas cannot be tolerated by either side. There is only one way to interpret this game, only one! Perhaps the only thing either end may agree on is that only one view can stand...

    We are trapped in a bitter war that has no end, no real purpose. As discussion continues to deteriorate, the massive walls of impenetrable explanation are being built, and cries of "Do what you're like, but this is why you're wrong" echo in all directions.

    What is there left to blame? Will an agreement ever be met? If neither side will back down I can only see one logical endpoint.

    Calling each other stupid.

    I pray it will not come to this.

    Signed
    No YOU'RE STUPID!
