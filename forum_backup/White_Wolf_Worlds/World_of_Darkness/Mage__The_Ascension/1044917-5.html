            Well, Glamour is the lifeblood of the Dreaming, it is not the lifeblood of magic. Linear Magic works by exploiting the Fundamental Laws of Reality, Dynamic Magic works by breaking the Fundamental Laws of Reality, and Glamour Magic works by imposing the Dreaming on Reality. Since the Dreaming seems to overlap everything because everything seems to Dreams (even the sleeping spirits of inanimate objects), Changelings are capable of using Glamour Magic everywhere. Conversely, Linear Magic and Dynamic Magic becomes unpredictable and unreliable in the Near and Far Dreaming and stops working in the Deep Dreaming (which is an argument against Linear and Dynamic Magic using Glamour and an argument for Linear and Dynamic Magic being a form of applied Banality).

            Now, I agree that Changelings are cool, I just do not think that they are the bee's knees. Changelings can counter Linear Magic and Dynamic Magic by using Glamour while Changelings can counter Glamour Magic by using Banality (or redirect Glamour Magic with Fae). Since Changelings use an opposing force to counter Glamour Magic, that suggests that they are using an opposing force to counter Linear Magic and Dynamic Magic. Autumn People might be able to redirect Linear Magic and Dynamic Magic by using a Banality control power, but I do not think that Changelings can do so with Glamour Magic. Of course, feel free to do what you want in your games.

                            Had an idea for a Sorcerer antagonist, would work in a Mage, mixed, Hunter or Vampire game.

                            Julian Chambers

                            Born: 1100 BC
                            Current Status: Unknown

                            Hermetic Sorcerer (Unaffiliated)

                            Julian was born into a family of Sorcerers and was schooled in the lower arts from a young age. Using the masculine principles of attraction, repulsion and purity he formed a somewhat hermetic paradigm which could be compared to the will-working of Hermetic Mages (or even the Blood Mages among Vampires). Precocious and powerful, he obtained a significant amount of magical lore by the time he was 23, mastering several Paths. As the years went by he became obsessed with Lichdom, Vampirism and the powers of Wraiths; all of the power in the world wouldn't mean anything if he was a footnote in history. So he captured and studied Vampires and Risen, he spoke (and sometimes dealt) with weaker demons, seeking for a way to extend his life.

                            Using a mixture of Alchemical principles he learned to slow his aging to a near-halt, however this wasn't enough. He knew that eventually some force would stop him from imbibing the draft on which he relied. He used the most advanced (and dark) Alchemical rites, slaying his blood relatives and sacrificing a captured Revenant in order to imbibe his blood lineage with some shadow of the Vampire's immortality. This ritual permanently scarred his psyche, however it did permanently quarter his aging process. This wasn't enough, so his efforts became more... extreme. He made deals with Vampires (any Vampire) to take care of rivals, no questions asked, on the condition that he could do with them as he wished. He acquired 5 torporous Cainites, one of whom was an Elder, and experimented with imbibing their blood. Eventually, after many tests he learned how to neutralize the accursed Fascination inherent to their blood, but the experimentation scarred his internal organs leaving him with a permanent reflux problem (which no amount of magic seemed able to fix). Using this draft (and keeping his "equipment" fed on animal blood) he extended his life exponentially, living right through the long night and into modern nights. He has acquired multiple derangements and supernatural flaws over this period, and if the Humanity system is being used he has fallen to level 2.

                            Hellfire: 6 (specializing in the masculine force of fire)
                            Alchemy: 6 (specializing in concoctions relating to health and longevity)
                            Mana Manipulation: 6 (he has used this to permanently strengthen his magic)
                            Shadows: 5 (a mixture of Obfuscate and Obtenebration effects)
                            Conjuring: 5 (focusing on propulsion of visible objects)
                            Conveyance: 5 (focusing on levitation and teleportation)

                            Mana: 10
                            Blood: 25
                            Willpower: 10
                            Disciplines: Potence 1, Fortitude 5

                            He has become interested in the Awakening several times, however he fears that the process will rob him of his immortality and kill him. He fails to understand how the Awakening works, and that his previous magics will probably make it impossible. Furthermore, he sees it more as a transformation people choose to undergo, and he views Mages as beings who have performed some trick upon the world. He is paranoid in the extreme, he feels that he has fought too hard for his immortality. Despite all of his power, he is oddly pathetic; he has done very little with his prolonged life and is a case study in existence for existence's sake. His only real devotions are his own survival and his pride. He runs the line between survival and proving his power over others, and tends to choose isolation where possible. Only a serious enmity or a threat to his existence would cause him to become the enemy of any of the Player Characters. He takes his privacy and his "domain" seriously, so another willworker or supernatural existing in close proximity to him (some highly fortified and well-disguised base, he did away with being obvious when he learned it reduced his survivability) would lead to a few cowardly jabs in the night. Remember that while he would want to destroy or chase away a Mage or Vampire or Sorcerer living within the bounds of his domain, he would never directly risk his life, and so he will attack while invisible or far away.

                            If separated from Vampire blood for a month he will age at a rate of one year per hour. If separated from his age-slowing concoction the same effect will take place; he has become reliant on both of them. If he is starved of both he will turn to ash in short order.

                                I think that he is not powerful enough for his age. If we have the example set by the Kindred (who probably should develop more slowly than Sorcerers because of the stasis of Kindred existence), an individual who is over 3100 years old should have 56 extra levels of Paths (62 levels total with 'Equality for All' rules). In addition, I am unsure that anyone who mastered the sixth level of Alchemy would have not become Kindred dozens of centuries ago if he has already transformed himself into a Revenant.

                                I would reduce his Mana to '5' and give him the following spread of Mythic Numina: Alchemy 6, Conjuring 5, Conveyance 5, Divination 6, Enchantment 6, Fascination 5, Fortune 6, Oneiromancy 6, Shadowcasting 6, Shapeshifting 6, and Weather Control 5. The process of becoming a Revenant should not have given him access to Fortitude 5 (Revenants have a maximum of level one in Disciplines unless they have a more powerful master and Fortitude 5 would require a 4th generation Kindred master), so I would actually have the study of Kindred just give him a better longevity potion (Level 5), where he aged one week every year rather than one month every year, before he perfected his unaging serum (Level 6) and just avoid the Disciplines and Blood Pool.

                                With his age, he would have 36 bonus levels of Attributes. 21 levels would max out normal human attributes but Sorcerers have access to the Legendary Attribute Merit, so I would let him purchase the Legendary Attribute Merits with Attribute levels (they cost the same amount of freebie points). His final Attribute spread would be Strength 6, Dexterity 6, Stamina 6, Charisma 6, Manipulation 6, Appearance 6, Perception 6, Intelligence 6, and Wits 6. Each Attribute would have an associated Legendary Attribute bonus associated with it (double Strength for lifting purposes, double movement with Dexterity, soak any damage with Stamina, etc). He would have three leftover Attribute levels, but I would just transform them into five extra Rituals.

                                With his age, he would also have 155 bonus levels of Abilities, which would grant him level 5 in 30 Abilities and a range of values in 10 other Abilities. In any case, he would probably have 11 dice in any combinations of Attributes and Abilities that predate 1800 AD. In addition to everything else, I would also give him 56 extra Freebie Points for further customization to reflect his great age (Ability Aptitude [X] would reflect the proficiency gained in his Abilities due to centuries of practice and Unaging would just reflect his unaging serum). At that point, you have an ancient and potent sorcerer (with an effective age of 45 due to his longevity treatments before he perfected his unaging serum) who is looking forward to an eternity of life as the perfect man.

                                The sorcerer would be looking for true immortality without selling his soul (which is why he is not a Ghoul Sorcerer or an Infernal Sorcerer). With his powers, he is capable of killing from a distance without leaving a mark, using Fortune 6 to wither away that targets of his ire without leaving his Sanctum (even major supernatural creatures would have cause to fear that level of Fortune, especially since his curses could potentially activate before they are aware that they have been cursed) or visit people in their dreams to give them messages. He would know that near future almost perfectly and would have blessed himself to a degree that he could avoid suffering death from practically any circumstance (Fortune 6 with dozens of buffer successes would probably be appropriate). He could transform into a dragon to savage his enemies or turn his enemies into ants before stepping on them.

                                                No, I do not think that Sorcerers are capable of using Rituals to do everything that Mages can do because Linear Magic is bounded by Reality while Dynamic Magick defies the boundaries of Reality. Sorcerers especially are incapable of creating effects that are the province of Archmages (such as the creation of Shapeshifters, which would be a minimum of Life 8 [required for creating physical shapeshifters] and Spirit 8 [required to unify the Spiritual and Physical Realms required to create Fera]). In addition, Sorcerers are explicitly forbidden from combining Numina (Sorcerer: Revised, p. 63) and, while they may be able to create hybrid Rituals at the discretion of STs, there is no reason to expect that even level 6 Rituals would be that terribly powerful (the one example of a level 6 Ritual is Revive which, while powerful, is painfully expensive for the effects that it creates)

                                                Sorcerers cannot create Fera because they could never develop a level 8 Ritual. The creation of Fera is something that could only be done by Gaia, the Supernals, the Celestials, and the occasional extremely ambitious Archmage who is willing to spend a few centuries on the project. In fact, there is nothing really to indicate that Sorcerers are capable of replicating the fifth level of the Spheres permanently, even with level 6 Rituals and level 6 Paths. Of course, Sorcerers could potentially summon entities capable of creating effects beyond that allowed by Numina, but that is more the realm of Numina and more the realm of Spirits.
