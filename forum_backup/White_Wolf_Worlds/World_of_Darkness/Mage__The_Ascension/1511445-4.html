    Entropy

    Entropy gets wrongly tacked onto spells a lot, because it seems like it is at least tangentially involved in many things even when the other Spheres involved are enough.

    407 – Entropy 4 attacks inflict Agg damage.

    413 – Entropy can be used to spot weak points. This is a consistent use and it makes sense, but often there is no functional benefit for using it to do so.

    415 – An example of the above: A Life 3/Matter 3/Entropy 1 that explicitly uses Entropy to spot weak points. Great. Life and Matter make the spell damaging to vampires and Life makes it Agg damage. The Enlightenment (Arete) roll is 4 so 8 damage, same as it would be for a Life 3/Matter 3 spell. The attack is a Dex + Martial Arts, with no indication of reduced difficulty. Mechanically, what is Entropy doing? It's not a wrong use, and it is the right sphere for the claimed effect, its just that there is no functional difference between the spell with the effect and the spell without it. Edit: Also, it only takes Matter 2 to make Life damage vampires.

    416 – On the other hand, here’s a good example of Entropy 1 to find weaknesses. A purely Entropy 1 spell. This spell lowers the difficulty of the next attack by -1 per success (presumably capping at -3 total)

    419 – Entropy 3/Prime 2 attacks do Aggravated and metaphysical damage to ghosts. This is odd because normally Entropy can’t even directly attack until level 4, though I suppose this is a special exception. Also the Aggravated damage is explicitly coming from the Entropy side, so Prime is unnecessary. This should have been a flat Entropy 3.

    422 (Thunder Punch sidebar) – Thunder punch is another good example of Entropy 1 to find weak points. It’s another -1 per success. Entropy 2 to adjust probabilities is also given as a potential way of invoking Thunder Punch, which is technically correct but functionally pointless. Using Entropy 2 ups the difficulty without changing the benefit.

    438 (Dodging the Blast sidebar) – Entropy is one of the spheres that allow mages to invoke the optional Dodging the Blast rule.

    440 – If using the optional Durability and Structure rules, Entropy attacks reduce object durability by -2, and Entropy augmented transmutation spells reduce it by -2 per success for the purposes of overcoming Durability.

    478 – Agama Sojourn – Entropy 4/Spirit3/Life 2 for self passage and Entropy 4/Spirit 4/Life 3 for others. As mentioned in the Spirit writeup, it isn’t clear what Entropy is doing, but we can infer that it is a necessary part of leaving the Underworld when you are done.

    480 – Taking items on the Agama Sojourn requires infusing them with Entropy 3, then binding them to the traveler with Prime 2. Consecration is Prime 1, and needs no special spheres. I suppose this section could be saying that Consecration isn’t enough for the Agama Sojourn or the Astral Sojourn, but it looks more like Consecration was forgotten when these sections were written.

    480 – Moving from the Low Umbra to other layers requires Entropy 5/Spirit 5 to open a gateway, or Entropy 5/Mind 5 to astrally project from a ghostly body. Since we are never told what Entropy is doing in any of these Agama spell it is impossible to judge what the right sphere should be. However, without explanation, this should be Entropy 4 like the Agama Sojourn itself.

    485 – Entropy is at -2 difficulty in the Underworld, but using it at all strengthens nearby Shadows and Spectres.

    491 – Calcify does not work on Mages with an Entropy of 4 or higher. This seems to be a part of a recurring idea that the Entropy sphere is intrinsically linked with the forces of decay and entropy, which is only fitting for some games, not all.

    508 – Entropy 4 to Rot Body. It’s a basic Entropy attack.

    508 – Alter probability at Entropy 2+. Alter probability is the explicit domain of Entropy 2. I think the + is referring to giving people good or bad luck which happens at Entropy 3, but that’s better described as another effect like Bless/Curse.

    508 – Bless/Curse for Entropy 3/Life 3. What’s Life doing here? This is possible with Entropy 3 alone.

    508 – Entropy 3+ to Bless/Curse but this time its listed as a Spirit Power… I don’t see why this is a spirit power though, or why this is listed twice.

    508 – Entropy 3+ to Cause Decay. I probably would have just said Entropy 3. Entropy 4 allows a mage to inflict decay on living things, but that is better described by Rot Body. Its not inaccurate to put it here, I suppose.

    508 – Entropy 1 to spot weaknesses.

    508 – A repeat of the Entropy 3/Prime 2 to harm a ghost. Same issue.

    509 – Entropy 3/Time 3 to disintegrate an object. I’ve said before that this works, but I’d like to see a comparison with what Entropy 3 alone and Time 3 alone would do. It seems like the combined spell should be better, but I don’t know how to adjudicate that. If its not better, this should be Entropy 3 OR Time 3, as there’s no reason to put more complicated methods in the common magical effects chart.

    516 – Forces 3/Entropy 3 to erode or enhance energies. Forces 3 can do this alone. Actually, Forces 2 can do this alone.

    516 – Life 1/Entropy 1 to see the potential for sickness and misfortune. Entropy 1 can do this without help.

    517 – Life 5/Entropy 4 to age or de-age a living thing. Life 5 can do this alone.

    518 – Matter 1/Entropy 1 to see the weak points in an object. Entropy 1 can do this alone.

    519 – When Time is used to prolong an effect, Entropy 3 can be added to set an end condition. I’m not sure why Rank 3 is the right rank, though I don’t know which rank would be better.

    522 – Time 2/Entropy 2 to see multiple futures and pick out the one most likely to occur. I’m not sure what this means, but if it is only sensing which one is most likely, shouldn’t it be Entropy 1? And if the mage is choosing which one is most likely, what does that mean?

    523 – Time 3/Entropy 3 to invoke multiple probabilities. To do what? This is so unclear it isn’t even wrong. I simply do not know what this means in any practical way.

    523 – Time/Forces/Entropy to capture a tornado in a bubble of time. I don’t see what Entropy is doing here. Just because a storm is chaotic doesn’t mean Forces needs help controlling it.

    524 – Data 2 and Entropy let a mage predict and alter probability patterns or spot the flaws in objects, and it doesn’t take Data 2 to see that Entropy can do all that without help.

    532 – Entropy 2 to increase the odds of finding a taxi. This is tricky. I’d say increasing the odds of a taxi driving by would be Entropy 2 while increasing the odds of someone finding a taxi is Entropy 3. That said, if there is an Entropy 2 way of doing it, it is a correct statement.

    546 – Entropy and Spirit to counter ghostly Arcanoi. I think this should be Entropy OR Spirit.

    562 – Entropy 2/Life 2/Spirit 1 to manipulate the probability of someone giving the mage a free lunch. This is an example of player thinking so the redundancy is fine. Still, Entropy alone can do this, and if Life is involved it is just a sensory effect, so it is Life 1.

    563 – Correspondence 2/Mind 2/Entropy 1 to curse people to accidentally reveal embarrassing info. This is another example of Entropy 1 searching for weak points but having no real effect on the spell.

    601 – Hands of Death has an optional Entropy 2 version that “spots weak points” to reduce armor. Spotting weak points is technically Entropy 1, but Entropy 2 is the rank for repeatedly hitting weak points so this works. That said, it reduces durability/armor/soak by -1 per success, in addition do damage, which makes this the third different system given for this effect. (-2 per attack and -2 per success being the other 2.

    603 – Hypernarrative Influence has an optional Entropy 2 that causes pop culture appropriate events to pop up. Some of the examples are actually not probabilities, but otherwise this works.

    610 – Witch’s Vengeance uses Entropy 4 to age or decay someone.

        Forces

        Forces has a magnitude problem. Most high powered phenomena should technically be possible at 2, so in addition to the normal progression, magnitude is often locked behind higher spheres, which is clumsy and arbitrary.

        68 - At Forces 1, a mage can note elemental phenomena.

        68 – At Forces 2 a mage can make burning candles flare or go out.

        68 - At Forces 3 can conjure winds or make fire leap. Both of these should be possible at Forces 2

        68 - At Forces 4 a mage can conjure storms.

        68 - At Corr 5 a mage can command firestorms, blizzards, and nuclear blasts. These are listed in the Forces 5 section but there's no real reason they can't be done with Forces 4. They're not beyond Forces 4 ranges.

        421 – Forces 2 to add velocity and momentum to a thrown weapon gives a -1 difficulty per success.

        422 (Thunder Punch Sidebar) – The same effect, but for any attack.

        429 (Catching Bullets Sidebar) – Time 3/Forces 2/Life 2 to add a bullet catch effect to the Arrow Cutting feat. This effect can be done with fewer spheres and Forces 2 on its own should be enough.

        438 (Dodging the Blast sidebar) – Forces is one of the spheres that allow mages to invoke the optional Dodging the Blast rule.

        440 – Forces 2/Matter 2 to make an attack on a pillar. Forces for the attack, Matter to reduce its durability (Optional Rule)

        457 – Forces 2 effect to disperse clouds of toxins.

        465 – Suggested Forces effect to augment a taser. One possible result is an extra damage die per two successes. It’s an unclear section.

        467 – Astral Immersion into the digital web is Life 4/Correspondence 2/Forces 2. Since the idea is that the character becomes pure information, this is a transmutation effect which requires Life 5 and Forces 3.

        470 – In the Digital Web, Forces effects that score more than 5 successes risk Whiteout. I would rule that this doesn’t apply to Forces 1 sensory effects, but that's personal opinion.

        485 – In Otherworlds, by default, Forces spells are at a +1 difficulty. Forces spells do not work at all in the Low Umbra.

        505 – Forces adds an automatic success for Damage.

        506 – “Physical Illusions” take Forces 2+/Prime2+ and Immersive Illusions (holodeck levels of apparent reality) take Forces 4+/Prime 4+. I covered this back on the first page, but its not clear what Prime is doing here, especially with the + sign. Possibly Prime 2 it is creating a Body of Light framework for Forces to map to, but I have no explanation for Prime 4. And even Body of Light is an unnecessary addition. Forces 2 can do all of this on its own; even Forces 4 is unnecessary unless the area of effect gets into a range of miles. Basically, both of these spells boil down to the same spell, which is Forces 2+.

        508 – Transforming an object into air is apparently a Forces 3/Matter 2 effect. Incorrect. Forces 3/Matter 2 is the combination to transmute a simple object into energy (such as making a brick disappear in a flash of light). Transforming an object into air is changing from one form of Matter into another and is the explicit domain of Matter 3. Forces would only be used if you were heating the object to the point it evaporated, in which case Matter would not be needed.

        508 – Immersive Illusions once again take Forces 4+/Prime 4+ but this time also take Mind 4. Prime 4 is just as nonsensical as before. Mind depends a great deal on what it is doing. No explanation is given. Since the idea of ‘holodeck’ level illusions includes autonomous reactive ones, maybe this is making the illusion adaptable? Whatever the case, Forces 4+ is still wrong.

        509 – Call Storm is Forces 4+/Prime 2.

        509 – Conjure element. Forces 3/Prime 2 to conjure fire is correct. Forces 3/Prime 2 to conjure wind is not. If you can levitate a person with Forces 2 you can accelerate air with Forces 2. If you don’t already have air then the spell becomes Forces 2+/Matter 2/Prime 2 to summon the air and accelerate it.

        509 – Conjure Physical Illusion is now listed as Forces 2+/Prime 2. This works.

        509 – Direct existing elements is Forces 2+. Again, if you can accelerate a person you can accelerate air.

        509 – Invisibility is Forces 2

        509 – Invisibility on a living being is Forces 2/Life 2. Since that’s a locking effect this is also correct.

        509 – Levitation or flying. I really think this was supposed to mean Forces 2+/[Pattern Sphere] 2 or Correspondence 3/[Pattern Sphere] 2. The distinction will matter when I get to Matter, for now Forces 2+ is the correct level for unlocked levitation/flight.

        509 – Speed or slow velocity at Forces 2+

        509 – Transform Forces at Forces 3+/[Appropriate Sphere] 2. Forces 3+ is correct. For once, though, the missing + is a problem. This should be Forces 3+/[Appropriate Sphere] 2+.

        509 – Create multiple images has a Forces 2+/Prime 2 variant. This is essentially another physical illusion (though purely light based). Prime 2 is unnecessary.

        509 – Telekinesis at Forces 2+. Correct. And for once, no superfluous Mind addition.

        509 – Mind 3/Forces 2 to translate language so that other people can understand it. This is an awkward description of a reasonable effect. Mind 3/Forces 2 is the method of translating the physical sensations of language: sound to sound, or text to text, etc. If the mage is just making the targets think they are hearing a different language, that’s Mind alone.

        513 – Forces 3/ Prime 2/ Corr 2 to start fires at a distance.

        513 – Forces + Correspondence 3 to levitate things at a distance. This would be Correspondence 2.

        513 – Forces combined with Correspondence 4 can create a ward or ban against forces and energies. No specific level is given though and advice to consult HDYDT which falsely implies that HDYDT is helpful.

        513 – Forces + Corr 5 to stretch or compress energies. This is too vague to be useful. If it means a special distortion locked to a pattern it technically works. If it means stretching or compressing a space such that those inside don’t notice, I don’t think you need a pattern sphere at all. If you are stretching or compressing the patterns you don’t need Correspondence at all.

        515 – The direct statement that large scale Forces effects require both higher ranks and more successes, which is a double whammy unique to Forces.

        516 – Forces 2 contains the stated area limits of “around a single human-sized character” or 20 square feet. It doesn’t use the term square feet, but since a 20 ft by 20 ft area is way bigger than a human I think it was the intention. This is an arbitrary limit and I don’t like it. Forces 3 is limited to several dozen yards (Again, square I think). Forces 4 to miles. Forces 5 has no limit.

        516 – Transforming people into electricity is Life 5/Forces 3

        516 – Changing water into air is listed as Forces 3/Matter 3. This is incorrect. Evaporating water would be Forces 2 OR Matter 3. Turning water into air would be Matter 3. Edit: Both changing the state of matter (solid ice to liquid water) and transmuting matter in one state to a different type of matter in another (solid ice to liquid gold) are the explicit domain of Matter 3.

        516 – Attaching elemental forces to a person or object is described as Forces 3/[Appropriate Sphere] 3, but if we read the description its basically just a force anchored to a pattern, which is [Appropriate Sphere] 2

        516 – Forces 3/Entropy 3 to erode or enhance energies. I can’t think of a meaning to this that can’t be done with Forces alone.

        516 – Forces 3/Mind 3 to manifest thoughts into energies. Really poor wording. This is the correct combination for transmuting a thought or idea into energy. It is not the correct combination for thinking up a Forces effect and then casting it. It is especially not the combination for casting a Forces effect if the Mage is a psychic.

        516 – Forces 3/Spirit 3 to transmute ephemera into energy.

        516 – Forces 3/Time 4 is probably the correct combination for setting triggers for energy fluctuations. I just wish I knew what energy fluctuations were or why they needed to be triggered. If the energy fluctuations are the trigger, though, Forces 3 is too high.

        516 – Transforming people into other elements is Life 5/Forces 3 or Life 5/Matter 3.

        519 – Transforming cars into robots is Matter 4 plus Forces and Prime. I don’t see what Forces or Prime are adding. Maybe if the robot in question has a specific feature this would be necessary. If the robot is somehow "alive," Prime might be an animating force, but this is not a listed ability of Prime and robot does not imply the need for an animating force.

        520 – Forces can be combined with Mind to pull off telekinesis, pyrokinesis and a whole host of things that Forces can already do without Mind.

        521 – Spirit 2/Forces 2+ to stir up elemental disturbances within Otherworlds… essentially cast a Forces spell across the Gauntlet.

        522 – Forces + Time 3 to cast across time.

        522 – Forces + Entropy + Time 4 to trap a tornado in a no-time space. The more I read this the more I think it is wrong. I’ve already covered how Entropy is wrong, but Time probably doesn’t need help moving patterns any more than Correspondence does. That said, this effect might need Correspondence as you are not just freezing the storm in time but also moving it to an extradimensional space.

        530 – Forces 3/Prime 2 to conjure a fireball with a lighter and spray can. The lighter and spray can are creating a small fireball already, it only takes Forces 2+ to manipulate it into a bigger one.

        533 (Axis of Coincidence sidebar) – Life 5/Mind 5/Matter 4/Forces 3/Prime 3 to create a fully functional taxi and driver. Unless you want to summon it with the engine running, there’s really no need for Forces.

        535 – Forces 2 to make a breeze or make a candle flame fly across the room.

        544 – Forces 5/Corr 2/Prime 2 to blow up the moon.

        562 - Corr 4/Time 3/Forces 2/Matter 2 focused through a hypercharged sportscar to give it a high speed getaway mode. The justification for Forces is not accelerating the car, Correspondence does that by skipping through intervening spaces. Forces is to counter the g forces. From the description it’s not even clear that’s necessary, the car is faster because it has a different relation to time and space, not because it is faster. As a player choice this is okay, but I wouldn’t use it as an example.

        563 – Forces 2/Prime 2 to up the damage of a bullet and give it Agg.

        601 - Corr 2/Forces 2/Mind 2 to hack a computer you can't connect to using a hollywood style device. I think this should be Corr 2 alone. Possibly Forces is involved in connecting the data stream itself. Mind is doing nothing.

        601 – Hot-Shotting at Forces 2. Each success reduces the difficulty of the next attack and adds an automatic success. These can be added to intimidation rolls, damage, or to expand the number of targets. My only complaint is that this is a lot of utility for a single success and it seems like two different effects, one altering the sound of the gun and one altering the efficiency.

        601 – Hands of Death applies the rules from the Damage or Duration chart to hand to hand damage, with one automatic success for Forces.

        601 – Right Click. The Forces 1 add-on gives infrared or ultraviolet scanning capabilities.

        603 – Sanitize Evidence uses Matter 2/Forces 2 to evaporate evidence. This should be Matter 2+/Forces 3 as it is a transmutation effect.

        604 – The Forces 1 part of Secure the Scene is for scanning, and appears to help detect blood and evidence. It can do this indirectly but is far from the best sphere. The Forces 3 add on adds a blackout effect that warps light and sound around the area. This should be Forces 3+ based on area of effect.

        605 – Dimension Bomb uses DimSci 3/Forces 3/Prime 2 to create a forces based explosion on the other side of the gauntlet.

        605 – Golden gunman uses Forces 2 to let an operative do acrobatic flips unhindered by the recoil of any weapons. No system is given for these acrobatic flips so presumably they are mundane.

        608 – Bullet Catch. Forces 2/Life 2/Time 2. Same effect as earlier. Same complaint as earlier.

        608 – Psychic Interface. Mind 3(4)/Data 2/Forces 2 to psychically interface with computers. I could see arguments for dropping Forces, but it also makes sense.

        626 – Matter 3/Mind 3/Forces 2 to reprogram a HIT Mark X. I don’t see what Matter and Forces are doing here.

                                    Life from M20

                                    422 (Thunder Punch Sidebar) – The Life 3 variant is to boost strength or damage the enemy internally. I think a Life 2 variant that allows a mage to go into levels of exertion that tear muscle and tendon, but heals the damage as it happens should be viable, but I’m not going to complain that no one thought of that.

                                    429 (Bullet Catching Sidebar) – Time 3/Forces 2/Life 2 to catch bullets. The role of Life here is in hardening the skin, which is unnecessary to the spell as written, and is also Life 3.

                                    435 – Life 2 can protect against blizzards, sandstorms and the like. Life 3 is suggested as a protection against Hard Vacuum or Deep Sea Pressure. This is shaky but potentially viable. Almost any change that would actually protect someone from extreme cold, heat, or scouring falls under Life 3, though Life 2 could heal the damage as quickly as it happens.

                                    436 – It takes Life 3 to protect against open flames, even with Body Control.

                                    438 (Dodging the Blast sidebar)– Life is one of the spheres that can be used for the optional Dodging the Blast rule.

                                    440 – When using the optional object duration rules, Life can be used to attack the durability of wood and plants. If those rules are used, Durability needs to be overcome to transmute those things.

                                    441 – Life 3 can restore someone to life within the first few minutes of death by suffocation. This isn’t so much a new rule but an obvious application of healing based on how suffocation works. After 10 minutes without air the brain begins to deteriorate and it takes Life 5. That’s ill thought out. That is indeed a point of no return with modern medicine, but modern medicine can't heal brain damage the way the Life sphere can. If the brain is alive it can be repaired. Life 5 is only necessary when it isn’t. It would be within the realm of reason to require Mind 3 (maybe 4) along with Life 3 to restore memory and personality, though.

                                    442 – Life 2 for a mage to cure a disease or toxin for himself and Life 3 for someone else. This is correct, although the phrase “Life 2 effects to alter the mage himself, and Life 3 to alter someone else” is the wrong terminology.

                                    443 (Awakening Substances sidebar) – It takes Spirit 3 to rouse the spirit of a drug but Spirit 3 to rouse the spirit of a disease in someone’s body. This appears to pertain to the difficulty of isolating the disease in a body, as if it is part of the pattern. Same for the Life 3/Matter 2 spell to enhance the properties of a substance in a body. Same for Life 3 to enhance microbes in someone’s body vs Life 2 to enhance them outside. Except… why. The disease/substance is not part of the pattern, so a mage isn’t affecting a partial pattern. I could see Life 1 in order to even see them, but nothing beyond that.
                                    Admittedly, I can see a counter argument where pulling the DNA, or the hemoglobin, or the oxygen out of someone’s body should count as part of their pattern and should require Life 3. That doesn’t mean that everything that is in the body is part of the pattern.

                                    467 – Astral Immersion into the digital web is Life 4/Correspondence 2/Forces 2. Since the idea is that the character becomes pure information, this is a transmutation effect which requires Life 4(5) and Forces 3. It also means a character will need Correspondence 4+ even if they only use Rank 2.

                                    478 – Agama Sojourn – Entropy 4/Spirit3/Life 2 for self passage and Entropy 4/Spirit 4/Life 3 for others. Life is keeping the body alive, so it is a healing effect.

                                    479 (Avatar Storm Sidebar) – Life magic to increase soak may help soak damage from the storm, but its an ST call.

                                    483 (Acclimation sidebar) – Optional acclimation rules. After 4 to 5 weeks in Otherworlds, mages have a +1 difficulty to pattern magics for 1-2 days.

                                    485 – Life and Matter don’t work on umbral creatures or objects unless the originated in the material world (or presumably in a realm where Life and Matter work).

                                    485 – Life and Prime are always vulgar in the Low Umbra.

                                    494 – Spirit 4/Life 3/Prime 2 to exorcise a spirit in a completed possession. Life 3 is likely to isolate the spirit, which in the case of a full possession might reasonably be integrated enough to count as part of the same pattern. Prime does nothing.

                                    505 – Mind 3/Life 3 for an illusory attack that does aggravated damage.

                                    508 – Life 2 (3) to adapt to an environment. This depends a great deal on the type of adaptation, but anything within the realm of a person to acclimatize to without magic should be possible at Life 2(3).

                                    508 - Life 2/Prime 2 to animate a corpse. I'd still like more details on how this functions. I need to know what Life is doing before I can tell if 2 is high enough rank.

                                    508 – Life 2(3) Cause/Cure Disease. Correct as long as we specify this is inflicting the symptoms/results of a disease. Creating actual microbes is going to be Life 3/Prime 2.

                                    508 – Life 2/Prime 2 to create a simple body. Transmutation of simple life is Life 3.

                                    508 - Life 5/Prime 2 to create a complex lifeform.

                                    508 – Life 5/Prime 2 to duplicate body. This is the correct combination to duplicate a complex body. A simple body can be duplicated with Life 3/Prime 2.

                                    508 – Life 3 (4) to grow new limbs. Growing limbs apparently doesn’t count as a radical transformation.

I can’t say for certain that this is wrong, but the entire VLC entry is written up in a hostile manner (including calling it a “truly stupid spell”) and invents rules to nerf the entire idea:
                                    • It’s allegedly always vulgar, even in sanctums. It’s just so vulgar no paradigm allows it. No really, reality itself hates the very idea of this. Have we mentioned how vulgar this is.
                                    • Vampires have natural resistances
                                    • It takes enough successes to damage the vampire to incapacitated. Transmutation doesn’t usually do damage, but you need to pay for damage anyway. This is not a rule that applies to any other kind of transmutation. Even in the optional durability rules, a spell has to overcome durability (soak) not structure (health levels).
                                    • If you are using the optional divided successes rule, you then need additional successes for duration.
                                    • If you are not using the optional divided successes rule… yes you are, because you still have to pay for duration with additional successes.
                                    • Vampires with Protean or Vicissitude can transform back at a cost of 1 blood point per success the mage rolled.
                                    • Kindred automatically have to make a Frenzy roll at difficulty 8, regardless of if the spell succeeds. This is at least partially due to the utter humiliation of being a lawn chair.
                                    • A frenzying vampire suddenly have the option of spending their entire blood pool to turn back.

After all, carriages, computers, and robots aren't on the Periodic Table either, but they fall under Matter 4 just fine.

This is either because the matter part of vampires counts as simple matter (unlikely), or because Life is the main spell and Matter is augmenting it to work properly. In the former case, going from harming to transmuting is a jump from Matter 2 to Matter 2, no change. In the latter case the augment is likely also Matter 2 (the first level of manipulation), though I could see an argument for needing Matter at one rank lower than Life. Whichever of those three we use, lawn chairs still fall under Matter 4, so the spell is Life 5/Matter 4.

                                                        Also, Matter has the same problem but solves it completely differently. The vast majority of the forms of matter mages will work with fall under Matter 2/3. Rocks, water, air, plastics, metals, etc are all there. Only radioactive materials are such a strange form of Matter they naturally go higher. So instead of making a division between simple and complex types of matter, Matter makes a distinction between simple and composite matter. Matter made of a single or simple substance, like rock, falls under Matter 2, but complicated assemblies of different matters, like computers, fall under Matter 4.

                                                        I'm not saying this is the right way of handling things because Matter certainly has its own problems, but if we applied the same philosophy to Forces, simpler applications of a single force would fall under Forces 2/3 and more complicated applications involving multiple forces would be 4/5. Levitation (momentum), invisibility (light), and lightning bolts (electricity) for the former, Storms (momentum, electricity, and heat) and multisensory illusions (light, sound, momentum) would be the latter.

                                                            Originally posted by Becksworth View Post

                                                            The thing is that "controlling the tectonic plates" is not "more complex", it is "simple" movement. Making a coin fly is NOT more complex than making a mountain fly, it's just a lot easier. But it is basically the same process.

                                                            In game mechanic terms, that is reflected in amount of successes required, instead of higher sphere / difficult requirements.

                                                            Just think about it in terms of any of the other 8 spheres. Imagine for example turning an entire city into Silver.

                                                            Hard? No doubt that would be hard. 100 success required? Maybe more.

                                                            Regardless, that would STILL be only Matter 2. Transmuting things into a simple pattern, no matter how outlandish the feat, is still only Matter 2, because basic transmutation is a fairly simple process.

                                                            On the other hand, turning a small knife into "Adamatium" or "Vibranium" may require just 3 successes, but it would be Matter 5.

                                                            Creating a highly improbably "Looney Tunes Coyote" style of a trap that require hundreds of sets of unlikely circumstances to set off would be Entropy 2 with a ton of successes, while making your new business prosper just a little bit beyond on a safe basis would be Entropy 5 with maybe just 5 successes.

                                                            In keeping up with this trend, there's no real difference between summoning a gentle breeze and the hurricane in Jupiter that is 2 times bigger than Earth (the red spot in Jupiter); the only difference between those is a matter of scale rather than complexity.

                                                            Likewise, turning a small rock into gold or turning the Mount Everest into gold, both are just Matter 2, the difference is simply one of scale, not of complexity.

                                                            It's like the difference between walking around the corner or doing a "Flash" thing of walking around the entire planet. It's still "just" walking. Sure, if you decide to "walk" the entire circumference of the globe (and assuming you can walk over water), that may take you a few years to accomplish, but it's still the same simple process of moving one leg after the other.

                                                            The difference between walking around the corner or around the world, is a simple difference between 15 minutes of effort versus a few years (AKA the "amount of successes").

                                                            Differentiating between "movement, light, heat, electromagnetic" and "nuclear" forces is a way to make that distinction being made for complexity.

                                                            We can after all say that nuclear forces, being connected to the interior of the atom, is more "complex" than forces that are inherently at the molecular level - heat is the movement of the particles, light is photons, electromagnetic is the movement of the electrons. Meanwhile, the strong and weak nuclear forces are the ones that allow all the different atoms to exist.

                                                            This is just an idea; maybe you could come up with some different categorization; the Life Sphere for instance turns anything other than Vertebrates as "simple life", while Vertebrates is "complex". That's likewise debatable, but it is very functional for game purposes. I favor the idea of having nuclear forces as "complex", because I also like to call it "Hell Fire" for traditionalist non-scientific views. Therefore, for non Technocrats/Technomamcers, "Higher Forces" would be something akin to the "Fire of the Gods / Demonic Fire" or something like it, and I really liked that idea (playing with the idea that "radiation" would be a completely alien concept for a medieval person, which would most likely view it as some sort of divine/demonic power). So, in my opinion, that would be an interesting division that plays out for both "scientific" and "mystical" classifications, which is one of the core points of the game.
