    Originally posted by Dataweaver View Post
    A minor nitpick here: my view of the first two-and-a-half editions is that they were written almost entirely from the Traditions' perspective. Even MRev was written primarily from the Traditions' perspective, though MRev was the first edition that made a serious effort to consider things from the Technocracy's point of view as well — though that was clearly a “first attempt” sort of thing. As such, there are a bunch of assumptions throughout those first three editions that may not be as absolute as they made them out to be. And one of those assumptions was that all mages pursue Ascension, each in their own way. Things like the philosophies and tactics of the Union were framed in this way, with the Technocracy's Timetable being thought of as their plan for Mass Ascension.

    It isn't, because the Technocracy doesn't think in terms of Ascension, individual or Mass. Their Timetable is set up around the notion of improving the Masses' collective lot in life. They have no intention of helping the Masses Awaken, let alone Ascend.

    But the Traditions don't get that, because Ascension is so central to their way of thinking that they simply can't grasp the idea that someone wouldn't consider it to be all that.

    And for what it's worth, the Disparates are all over the map on the subject of Ascension; but by and large, it's not as high on their list of priorities as it is for the Traditions.
    Agreed. I stand corrected.

    That does complement my point thou, although in a slightly different manner than I described. The Techiebois know what they want and how they want it. The Trads however just have some vague ideas about what they want and not the slightest clue on how to get it. Hence why there's no way they can win this, and their little enterprise was doomed from the start.

    The Disparates suffer from the opposite problem. Each individual group has their own well perceived goals and how to achieve those, but they lack the ability to properly cooperate. Thus, they too cannot hope to compete to the monolith Union.

    Sometimes I do wonder if the OoH wouldn't have more of a chance by itself alone...
