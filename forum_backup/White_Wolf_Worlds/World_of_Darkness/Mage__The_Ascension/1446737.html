    M20 Books for the future?

    05-01-2021, 12:45 AM
    Chatting with some RDs over on Discord and the subject of M20 v M5 came up.
    I am happy with M5 and do not feel another edition is needed. But I would like some additional supplements. Such as;
    • A city guide to Mexico City or Rio de Janeiro (more interesting than Las Vegas),
    • A book on the Umbra, all of it if possible,
    • A game similar to Coteries of New York or Night Road,
    • How to give back massages,
    • Information on chantries,

    But what do you think? What book or material do you want?

                  I would love a city guide.                                       05-02-2021, 02:05 PM
                                      A Tome of the Disparates and/or Unaligned Crafts would be great. A new book on the Umbra would be even greater, a Guide to the Otherworlds. Mage is the only game line that can literally go anywhere, but there is just too much to be included in just one chapter of one book. The Book of Worlds was my first Mage supplement, but it was only after I started getting into the other WOD games that I realized how much was missing. I mean, it came out just after Wraith 2nd and Changeling 1st, so details on the Underworld and the Dreaming were slightly off and incomplete. When Infinite Tapestry came out, so much material from Mage 1st and 2nd were lost, and details for the rest of the Umbra were relegated to "check the game line" for more information. There wasn't even information on the newest additions, such as the 1,000 Hells and Demon pocket dimensions. Don't get me wrong, it did provide some great information for the Revised setting, I just loved the first book so much. A new book updating every Umbra location from a Mage POV, particularly the Mage-centric ones, could bridge the differences between the two. I've been thinking about something along these lines for months, along with a totally new take on the WOD cosmology. A companion book focusing on a Mage version of Axis Mundi: the Book of Spirits would allow for more info in the main book. Maybe a second add on for a more detailed look on Bygones, familiars, and other Umbral beings in the material world. This is a dream project of mine, if you couldn't tell. I've also been thinking about related works for how other splats handle the Otherworlds, including Werewolves outside of the Middle Umbra.

                                      THWarted

                                          Originally posted by glamourweaver View Post

                                          so the Crafts book would be on those Crafts who aren’t in the Disparate alliance?
                                          I'd rather not have the official publication line make that distinction. What I'd like to see in a M20 Book of Crafts/Tome of the Disparates would be for the bulk of the book to be write-ups of individual Crafts on their own terms, save for each write-up having a section detailing what that Craft's relations with the Alliance might be like, which could range from “they've signed up” to “they're on friendly terms, but are keeping their distance for now” to “they actively distrust the Alliance, and want nothing to do with it” to “they actively oppose the Alliance” to “what Alliance?”.

                                          Then have a section on the Alliance itself, focused mainly on the various challenges it faces (“how it might fail”) and ways to deal with them (“how it might succeed”). This section would almost be like yet another Craft write-up in terms of its significance to the book: use it, or don't; your choice.

                                          My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness
