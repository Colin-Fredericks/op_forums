                    The closest thing you get to an exchange rate between Primal energy and resources is the Primal ventures. This is a use of Primal utility that lets you treat a business venture more or less like a node. But in order to do so, you must invest either time and effort into the company, or resources. And there's a chart showing how much resource you need to invest, and how big a node it counts as after you've done so. (Of course, another requirements is that the venture needs to be big enough to make effective use of your investment. You can't just dump a billion dollars into the corner grocery store and get a rack 5 node out of it. Numbers picked out of my head, as I don't have the book handy right now.)
