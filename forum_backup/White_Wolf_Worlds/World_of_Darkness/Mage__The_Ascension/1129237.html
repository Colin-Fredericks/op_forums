    What would your character do? (M: TAS ed)

    08-15-2017, 11:12 AM
    These WWYCD games I came up with were some-what popular on the Werewolf forums so I decided to do one on mage. The rules are simple, I provide a scenario and you have to tell me what your character would do.

    ​#1 You and your cabal have been tracking a courier service under the payroll of the Technocracy. After hacking their delivery logs, you discover they are about to deliver a payroll to a gang of smugglers hired by the Syndicate to smuggle less then legal goods across international waters. You decided to intercept the courier before he delivers the money. This, of course, will have to be done without alerting the technocracy, who probably have someone or something watching the courier to make sure he dose not run off with the money. What does your character do?

                                                  [For this thread, I'll be referencing a character I'd like to run, but can't because I have no one to play Mage with. He's a Celestial Chorus mage that is a Catholic deacon, who comes from a family that manifests physical traits reminiscent to portrayals of demons. Pointed ears, prominent incisors, and black, sharp fingernails (taken as an Advantage). And he, like much of his family before him, takes seriously the idea that he is descended from demons, and must be penitent for that inborn sin, and vigilant against temptation. He is generally meek, friendly, and desirous of spreading peace and resolving injustice. Focuses on Mind, Forces, and Prime.]


                                                  What to do about security guards? Act like a flustered audience member, who stumbled upon the private box after getting extremely lost. Pushes the sleepers towards believing this story using Mind 2, and try to get the nice men to help him find his seat. (Yes, he would have bought a ticket, regardless of whether he intended to watch the game. To do otherwise would be stealing. That, and to deal with this exact potential situation.) While he had the guards lead him away to get to his seat, hope that his allies managed to hide, and could use the distraction to reach the box. If they took him to his seat, he'd stay for about five minutes, then go to the restroom, go invisible with Forces, and walk back to the box.

                                                  Preferably, though, he would have had a "Don't Notice Me" aura running using Mind in the first place, so he could pass without trouble and avoid the problem entirely.

                                                  And if all else fails, use Mind to make them all fall unconscious.
