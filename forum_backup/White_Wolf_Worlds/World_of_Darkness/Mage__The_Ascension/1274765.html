    Chimerstry illusions : mental or physical ? Or both ? Or neither ?

    12-16-2018, 04:36 PM
    Hi,

    I'm running a chronicle where my players' characters will soon run into a Ravnos vampire who uses Chimerstry a lot, and I was wondering how to make it interact with my PCs' powers. Is it a mental illusion - that could be fought with the Mind sphere - or a physical illusion that would be the equivalent of a Forces effect ? Or something else ?

    The problem is that Vampire does not clearly separates physical and mental effects - for example, it is known that you can fight Chimerstry with Auspex, but Auspex is the discipline of true perception, both mental and physical. Chimerstry seems to fundamentally be an art of deception and manipulation, which would suggest mental illusions, but some aspects of it like the fact that it can affect an arbitrary number of people in the same way, or that some effects addresses specific senses, suggests that it creates sensory stimulations.

    My interpretation would be that Chimerstry is a form of "low level" mental illusion; it neither creates physical illusions nor directly manipulates the target's belief about what is there, but instead hacks the target's low level perceptions to make them consistent with the Ravnos' imagined reality and not the real world. I would allow it to be fought with both Mind (because someone is manipulating your perceptions) and some careful applications of other spheres, typically Forces and/or Matter (to notice that what your mundane senses perceive is inconsistent with your magickal perceptions).

    Any thoughts or official position about that? Thanks !

                                                          When we get the 5th edition clan writeup, we’ll have an official ruling in the form of how they reinterpret Chimerstry, since unique disciplines are out. My guess is that it’ll be reimagined as one or more Obfuscate amalgam powers with a Fortitude prerequisite. So it’s fundamentally mental manipulation (Obfuscate), but they can lend it a quality of solidity based on their experience using the Blood to solidify their own bodies (Fortitude). That also makes the Ravnos the most likely practitioners, as I don’t think another clan has those disciplines together.

                                                          And Obfuscate, as we know, is fundamentally a matter of subtly telling nearby minds what to perceive (or not perceive), so definitely under the purview of Mind. After all, it’s only an illusion if it’s perceived to be there but in some meaningful sense is actually *not* there. (Yeah, yeah, “the entire world is an illusion,” etc., etc., but you know what I mean.) Even now, both Chimerstry and Obfuscate are effectively illusion powers; Obfuscate just has a narrower range.
