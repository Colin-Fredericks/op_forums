        Originally posted by visigoth View Post
        I know it's a common trope and a cheap comparison, but the nephandic initiation is the only one without sleeper counterpart in culture or religions and that leaves permanent residual change.
        The sad thing is that Nephandi do have a sleeper counterpart. Terrorism. I'm not talking just about Jihad and other forms of political terrorism, but about all forms of terrorism from school shootings, to hate crimes, and even slavery and people trafficking. Any group that reduces human life to little more than a means to an end is the kind of spirit that the nephandi thrive on. While the Marauders can arguably be the psychopaths of the awakened world, the Nephandi are the sociopaths, the sadists, and the extremists.

        Originally posted by visigoth View Post

        This is why I can't buy the "destroy the world" objective. If they want to destroy it, what rape, corruption, suffering accomplish besides creating a small resonance and large attention? Breaking some irons bars doesn't destroy the prison. By this view, even being associated with infernalists is detrimental to the overall nephandic end game because this sticks evil to their image.
        Some nephandi want to destroy the world because they believe ascension to be a lie as you say, though others may do so simply because they believe humanity to be unworthy of ascension at all. They see the suffering that humanity causes and have come to the conclusion that humans are inherently evil, and in the same way the only truly noble act a vampire can commit is walking out into the midday sun, the only true act of selflessness is suicide. Humanity is a disease that needs to be taken care of at the source.

        Those with such a mindset may see corruption, rape, and other methods of abuse and control as tools for a greater plan. If they can bring others over to their way of thinking, either though pushing them to the very edge of madness until all they can accept is their own hatred for the world. Force them to become alienated and become ostracised by their peers until the nephandi are all they can turn to. Or simply allow them to realise what strength there is in admitting to yourself that humanity is fundamentally evil at heart. If you accept that you have evil within your heart, it becomes all the easier to see the evil in others. Destroy all notion of good, wake up to the inherent selfishness of all things everyone does, and get to work bringing about the end. Bring the people what they deserve.

        The clever nephandi will be able to reap destruction with a minimal of effort. All they need do is implant ideas in people's minds and watch as humanity tears itself apart, falling under the weight of their own inherent greed. But a less subtle approach can be even more effective if done correctly. Rape a woman, let her attacker go free. Have the police blame her for it, have her family so ashamed they disown her, have her realise how little society cares for her once she is tainted, show her how quick the world is to deny the legitimacy of her suffering and label her a fraud, a liar, and a whore. Let her internalise this deep injustice into a frothing pool of rage, and point it back at the very people who wronged her. Either she'll become a soldier for corruption, or she'll die in hatred and injustice, either way helps their cause. For no man is an island, and what one thinks is one's own private suffering will have knock on effect on every other person they come into contact with. Pass on the misery, never share it, just spread it. Let mankind's own selfish nature become its own undoing.

        While a lot of this is quite fitting for the Malfeans and their general desire to encourage the end through the strength of the Wyrm. The Infernalists too get something out of this. Their chosen dark ones gains power through a particular kind of suffering, be it the torture of an innocent child, the destruction of ones entire legacy, or simply the arbitration of the Faustian deal. The Infernalits are all out to make their Dark One as powerful and as influential as they can. After all, if the world is going to hell anyway, they better get themselves a damned good seat.

        Alternatively, some Nephandi are simply sexual deviants and get off on causing horrific acts. It satisfies them on many levels, not least because their avatar may reward them for such behaviour, but also because they just happen to be incredibly sick people even without an inverted avatar.

        Keepers of the Wyck: A Chronicle I'm running FINALE: Chapter 39: Green Fairy

                                                Originally posted by Saikou View Post

                                                As well. Psychopathy is an inability to distinguish between right and wrong due to insanity.
                                                This could fit either mould.
                                                Technically then, most Nephandi aren't psychopaths using your definition, as they clearly know the difference between "right" and "wrong" and choose to do "wrong". Not that that makes them, as keeps getting put forward, nonsense. That's exactly how "intent" works in any criminal wrongdoing beyond criminal negligence. And lots and lots and lots of people are choosing to do "wrong" all the time. Why should mages be any different? It's the method and the scale.

                                                Ya... that makes Nephandi little more than "reality criminals". So all the wailing and moaning and attempts to make them seem ultra-cool in a quasi-Nietschean way is pretty much just the same as being an apologist for any other perpetrator of reprehensible criminal acts. But, if the proverbial shoe fits....

                                                However, that doesn't make Marauders psychopaths. Or, at least, they don't have to be and typically aren't. The inability to distinguish right from wrong is not the same thing as the inability to distinguish what is real from what isn't. (Or what wouldn't be real if the person in question didn't redefine reality by fiat.) You can have Marauders who are psychopaths or sociopaths, but they are all disassociative to one degree or another., as their signature "bit' is coming at "normal" reality from some different angle.

                                                A decent example from Intro to Criminal Law - If a schizoid person who believes they talk to Jesus and Jesus says "Light that porn store on fire" and then the schizoid person does... That's a crime (e.g. a "wrong" thing to do), because, despite the "talking to Jesus" part, he knew that lighting a building, particularly a building with people in it, is "wrong". He's definitely not a psychopath. He's schizoid (and off his meds). And he's criminally liable for his actions.
