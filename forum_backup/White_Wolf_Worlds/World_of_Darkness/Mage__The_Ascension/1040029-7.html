    No, I disagree, both the Technocracy and the Traditions have fairly strict rules about dealing with Demons (Book of Madness Revised, pp. 74-83). Even summoning a demon for information is enough to get most Mages ostracized or re-educated. Actually making deals with Demons are an executable offense within both the Traditions and the Technocracy even if you do not engage in Soul Pacts. Actually engaging in Soul Pacts is one of the few things that the Traditions will Gilgul you for and the Technocracy will Mindwipe you for because you have thrown in your lot with the Nephandi, even if you are not officially part of their team yet.

    In addition to the legal consequences, the fact that your Avatar starts separating from your soul after (Avatar plus Arete) years and becomes the property of your Demon Lord within (Avatar) years is a bad thing for Mages. For Sorcerers, however, it is probably a sweet deal, because they can negotiate for Spheres without having to Awaken (getting a Demonic Avatar equal to their highest Sphere in the process). With a combination of luck and skill, Sorcerers can instantly become Masters in multiple Spheres without having to bother with any effort (or, with some bad luck, will end up becoming a demonic chew toy).
