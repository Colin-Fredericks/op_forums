    Spheres for computation efffects

    04-15-2019, 04:58 PM
    Computation is a part of all Sphere Magick, but I don't see any clear way to use Spheres for a direct computation, and that feels... wrong.

    After all, it is certainly possible to create a computer that can make the computation with Matter, or to even use a Mind effect that allows someone to do more complex math in their head. It's also possible with Entropy to "randomly" hit keys and get the first thousand digits of pi.

    What about solving the n-body problem for like 1000 objects? What about proving the Riemann Hypothesis? Proving that P≠NP? Calculating tip for a party of 16?

    It certainly seems like it would be in the hypothetical Information Sphere that the VAs are so keep about, but how would that even work? Given Information theory, and how it is tied to entropy, would the Entropy Sphere be the same thing?

      It might be unintuitive about how to handle calculations with Magick (aside of, yes, boosting your mind with the Mind sphere and such), but the actual solution is hinted at by a certain Technocratic Sphere that the VAs can also learn: The Data Sphere.

      I.e. 'Correspondence'.
      It makes sense, too - if you consider how calculations happen, you can see it as making a connection (Correspondence/Data) from the original Problem to the Solution, through a series of changes (Entropy).

      So it's either simply just the Data/Correspondence Sphere (To essentially establish a connection between the Problem Data and the Solution and thus 'finding' the Solution without any steps in between - which might be a flavor of mysterious Quantum computing), or possibly Data/Correspondence + Entropy to get all the mathematical steps in between the Problem Data and the Solution.

      If you combine Data or Data/Entropy with the Mind sphere, you could 'automatically' do calculations in your head instead of just relying on enhanced stats or the 'Lightning Calculator' through the Mind Sphere alone, and if you combine it with Forces you could aplly it to an electronic computer.

          I always took Mind as being more than the Sphere you can use to control and enhance minds but also the Sphere that manipulates abstract concepts and other constructs of the mind.

          I think that because in most old books (before Data was a thing) Mind was used for things like traducing or deciphering a text...and that can't be done by enhancing your brain (you need knowledge more than capacity) nor by "mind reading" (most books haven't minds to read).                       Conceptual effects get tricky precisely because they are conceptual. Even in the real world there is a strong line of thought that Mathematics is not real and exists entirely within the human mind. When you take this into Mage you start getting really strange questions. For instance, if an Etherite or a Hermetic invents a whole new type of mathematics, can you target that? Does it matter if you do?

                      The two Spheres with the strongest claim on conceptual matters are Mind and Spirit. Mind because concepts exist within and are products of the mind. Spirit because the High Umbra is the realm of the conceptual.

                      The answers would probably get even stranger if you wanted to alter mathematics, such as creating a zone where pi = 3.

                                      The power of math is seeming depth that can come out of simple rules, and, to quote Eugene Wigner, there's the question of "The Unreasonable Effectiveness of Mathematics in the Natural Sciences". We don't have much example to say that magic can do computation in abstract systems. For instance, I don't think it'd be reasonable to have a spell that could complete a book for an author that were not using Time to bring forward the work, Mind to compel the writing, or in some way creating something to finish the work that weren't either a proxy for the author or a substitute, either way, it's not suddenly generating the ending.

                                      Math to me is similar. Magic can not itself force actions from an abstract system but interface with things using it. When magic seems to be acting on abstract systems it's more acting on the embodiments of that system. Magic can speed up computation, but does not allow for sudden realization of answers except in forcing rules changes to allow for them. The 1,000 body problem may have no natural closed form solution but with enough successes you could create a system with a closed form solution because forces were shoving it into that configuration but that doesn't make the solution real in the general case.

                                          I think that this question is harder from a Mathematical Realist perspective, where math isn't a product of the mind, but actual objective "things" within the universe.

                                          The more that I think about it, Entropy is the sphere that handles calculations and logic. It's what is needed to have conditional effects, which implies that it works with if-then logic.

                                          Prime factorization is pretty trivial with Entropy, isn't it? Pick any integer that has two prime factors that you want. Then set up your computer to pick two random integers less than the original, and with Entropy adjust the outcome to so that their product is the original number, and then just recursively apply that. BOOM P=NP. Bye-bye, public key encryption.
