                    I had very much the opposite reaction. I find Entropy’s progression to make far more sense. Entropy is the sphere of controlling end points. That is generally what Fate really means, and death is the ultimate end point of one’s life. And the progression fits with this quite we

                    1: sense fate, lets you see omens, get a sense of endpoints, how significant things or events or people are to one’s fate, as well as when one deviates from one’s fate. Sensing disorder or weakpoints feeds in to this, as they are an omen towards the endpoint of the items destruction. You may also sense when something someone says does not align with fate, as per the zone of truth rote.

                    2: manipulate purely random events: This is because randomness has no “fate” associated with it. So your magic may be used to force an endpoint without clashing with the established fate there.

                    3: manipulate predictable events: at this level you can begin to manipulate events with simple fates, anything predictable such as the workings of a machine, when trains happen to leave, sudden meetings with friends, objects going missing, the likelihood of being caught while hiding. Anything which fate guides towards a specific and determined endpoint you can change or alter.

                    4: manipulate complex events: now your understanding of Entropy lets you manipulate more complex fates, things which are harder to predict but no less governed by that invisible hand, family curses, one’s eventual profession, the fate of their children, the progression of disease, one’s eventual death, the stock market exchange, one’s personal narrative set against the grander scheme.

                    5: determine fate: at mastery level you can essentially manipulate the highest level of events, whereas before you could manipulate one’s personal narrative, you may now dictate their narrative, determining their fate perhaps via a geas, also, you now can manipulate the grander scheme itself, you can affect the eventual outcome for whole cities or even nations, create narratives by ascending one person as the main character to a particular setting, or removing their importance in a role entirely, you can even shape how the population of a group reacts to particular events, and thereby shaping the end point of said event entirely.

                    to me this makes a lot of sense in a way that Fate arcanum felt a bit…lacking?

                    also, I really found the Death arcanum to be rather laughable. It is often claimed to be a separation of Entropy’s “fate and death” aspects, which as may be infered above I don’t actually see as a dichotomy at all, but they realised Entropy itself doesn’t actually have much Death specific stuff in there, so they had to scramble to incorporate Spirit and Forces effects to make the arcana even passable.

                    personally I find it really silly that Awakening distinguishes between ghosts and spirits in such a mechanical way. Ascensions lets spirit interact with all of them without issue and that feels far more consistent. If it’s ephemeral, it’s spirit, done and dusted.

                    and regarding conjunctional effects. I have tried to make them in awakening, and each time it has caused my ST to throw up a blue screen of death as they have no idea how to handle them. I wanted to use Space and Prime in order to determine the connections of a supernal object wonder equivalent (since they told me you need Prime to see supernal things so space isn’t enough) and they didn’t want to allow it because “it would open the door to just using space with everything and then getting the mechanical advantage for using major arcanum on all effects”

                    another time I tried using Matter and Death to get a sense for which objects made of Jade were also anchors and was told “no, those are two separate effects, you can use matter to check if something is jade, and you can use Death to feel anchors, and your gnosis is not high enough to combine spells, so you can’t do both.”

                    Then there is the whole “if the book doesn’t say you can add Death to this Spirit rote to affect ghosts, then the spirit rote can not affect ghosts” argument that makes me think some awakening players are almost incapable of off-book interpretations in a way that ascension not only allows but actively encourages.

                    I admit this might because every awakening game I have ever played in was with a bad ST, but these are problems I have never experienced with Ascension

                        Originally posted by Eric Ericson View Post
                        Sometimes I think they add extra sphears just to make casting spells harder. for a game about being a mage they really discourage useing magic.
                        Hdydt is a bit of a departure from the usual philosophy of how the spheres combine to allow effects. Generally speaking, spheres are a means to include the manipulation of specific aspects of reality in to an effect. For each rote I like to ask “what does each sphere contribute to the effect. How would the effect be possible if this sphere was reduced in rank, of missing that sphere entirely.”

                        for example, Clairvoyance with Corr 2 alone is essentially scrying, but if one wishes to read the minds of those they scry on, and thus reaching the “truer” definition of clairvoyance, then adding Mind 1 would allow for sensing emotions, Mind 2 for sensing surface thoughts, and Mind 3 for sensing subconscious thoughts/memories. And it is the Mind 3 version of the rote which enables what is classically described as clairvoyance.

                        While it does get a bit wibbly here, the wording on HDYDT’s “Perceiving from a distance” here does imply that the limit of Corr effects on range, where you can only use spheres of the rank up to your corr rank to access the corr range table, is specific to effects which “command” from a distance, not sense.

                        therefore, it is conceivable that Corr 2 Mind 3 would indeed be enough to read subconscious minds from a distance, but not manipulate their conscious thoughts (which would need you to have corr 3). Indeed, this would apply to any sensory effect done from a distance, they would only need the addition of corr 2 to pull off. However you would suffer the increase in difficulty for targeting things beyond sensory range without corr 3.

                        If you have any other particular examples, I’ll be haply to try and explain how I understand the sphere logic to play out. Though do allow that if they come from HDYDT, I might need to refer to previous editions to explain why I disagree with some of the decisions made for that book.

                                Originally posted by 21C Hermit View Post

                                Maybe I was dense, but I'm afraid I do not know of the last argument you mention in Awakening; I only know Ascension's rough equivalents of "You're a shaman/psychic, so you must add Spirit/Mind" comments that this thread seems to frown against.                                 This point if rather overstated and in my opinion somewhat of a misinterpretation for how spheres relate to practice.

                                any practice can be paired with any sphere combination, but certain sphere combinations allow one to perform the types of effects which are more in line with that practices’s tropes.

                                Without Spirit, a shaman may use Forces to encourage the spirits of fire to grow, move to avoid people, or strike specific targets. But with Spirit, they may also communicate with the spirits of fire, perhaps getting them to take the reigns of part of the effect and even act long after the shaman’s magic has finished. In this case, Forces allows direct manipulation of fire, while Spirit allowed the spirit of fire to do the work as they may negotiate, the two together allows for both to be part of the same effect, control and communication. The shaman’s practice allows this as they are in tune with the notion of spirits in all elements, and Forces is a way to manipulate the spirits of elements without directly breaching the gauntlet. Forces directs the mundane manifestation of the spirit, while Spirit allows interaction with the ephemeral aspect of the spirit

                                Similarly, Mind allows one to do what is associated with psychics, such as pyrokenisis.

                                With Forces alone, the mage may use their psionic abilities to focus on the flame and cast the effect to cause it to strike another, grow, avoid, or otherwise make a single movement. What adding Mind allows is for the mage to transform their thoughts into manifestations of Forces. Mind 3 Forces 3 is the transmutation of your conscious thoughts in to, for example, fire. This allows you to focus on a space and cause a fire to appear for as long as you focus on it, or for you to have the fire change shape and move around as you imagine with only the casting of a single ongoing effect. You can make the flame spell out your name, change in to the shape of your face, and then float towards someone before bursting into smaller flames, all in one effect, something that would be far more complex with Forces alone, requiring a new cast for each element of the change, racking up paradox with each cast.

                                                        Correspondence

                                                        68 - At Corr 1, a mage can spot connections and measure distances.

                                                        68 - At Corr 2, a mage can look across distances and pull small objects from nowhere.

                                                        68 - At Corr 3, a mage can step through intervening space.

                                                        68 - At Corr 4, a mage can appear in multiple places at once. No mention is made of processing senses or moving separately in each of these places.

                                                        68 - At Corr 5, can stack multiple locations in the same location.

                                                        417 - Active Corr 1 sensory effects cancel the Multiple Opponents penalty.

                                                        422 - Correspondence sensory effects allow the Read An Opponent maneuver to be used at at a distance.

                                                        422 (In the Thunder Punch sidebar) - Correspondence 1 allows a mage to judge the perfect spot to hit. This sort of makes sense and sort of doesn't.

                                                        439 (In the Dodging the Blast sidebar) - The book suggests Correspondence to teleport out of the area of a blast as a method of dodging blasts. No sphere level is given, so presumably Corr 3.

                                                        467 - Astral Immersion in the Digital Web requires at least 2 dots in Correspondence though this is not a magical effect and does not have an Arete roll. Holistic immersion, on the other hand, requires a Life 4/ Corr 2/ Forces 2 effect to convert and upload the mage to the web. Corr 2 is fine, though I think this would need to be Forces 3.

                                                        469 - Within the Digital web a mage can use a Corr 3 effect to backspace out of a hotlink. Essentially a specific application of personal teleportation.

                                                        478 - Correspondence 2 allows an Astral traveler to continue to sense the body they left behind. If this means they can perceive the senses of their body and their astral body at the same time, this should be Co-locality perceptions from Corr 3. Of course, since Astral Projection is usually Mind 4, a mage needs to have Correspondence 4 to do this either way.

                                                        482 - Verbena can use Correspondence 1 to find the Old Roads.

                                                        485 - Correspondence does not allow senses to extend between spirit realms and teleportation and colocation require extra successes. This definitely contradicts the previous rules on sensing your body during astral travel.

                                                        485 - Correspondence is unreliable in the Low Umbra.

                                                        504 - Correspondence is not capable of inflicting damage without other spheres.

                                                        509 - Correspondence 3/Matter or Life 2 as a levitation/flying spell. The Correspondence writeup does not mention this, but it is consistant with previous editions. As long as Matter and Life are locking spheres (and why should we think otherwise) this is reasonable.

                                                        509 - Correspondence 2 for Clairvoyance. Perfectly reasonable.

                                                        509 - Mind 3/Correspondence 2 for Clairvoyance. Utter nonsense unless it has some application the previous effect does not. This being the quick reference chart, no such application is given. If, somehow, this was supposed to add the ability to read the thoughts of the people you remotely view, that would reasonably be Mind 3/Corr 2. Note that it could not be cast unless the Mage had Corr 3, though.

                                                        509 - Affect distant object or being is Correspondence 2+. A quite unhelpful entry for its vagueness. If this means a mage can mundanely affect the thing as if it was right next to them, that is Corr 2.

                                                        509 - Conjure Earthly Being is listed at Corr 4/Life 2. Corr 4 can do this alone.

                                                        509 - Create Multiple Images is Correspondence 3. I only just realized that colocating the image of something isn't really listed anywhere in Correspondence. Whether this is the right rank depends on what is happening. If we treat this as a variant of colocality perceptions or co-location it should be rank 4 at least. However, it a mage were to take the sense through space of Corr 2 and extend it to other people, then that would accomplish the same thing at Correspondence 3.

                                                        509 - Create Multiple Objects is Correspondence 5/Matter 3/Prime 2. That has to have been Correspondence 5 OR Matter 3/Prime 2.

                                                        509 - Correspondence 4 to open gateways between locations. That is a straightforward application of Corr 4.

                                                        509 - Teleportation is Corr 3 (4). Assuming thats for self teleportation and teleporting others respectively that is correct. Note that no locking spheres are given.

Prime doesn't do anything. It is reasonable to need a sphere to set the nature of the ban, but I'm not sure we ever get rules as to what levels are required.

                                                        510 - Prime 2 or Prime 2/Corr 2 to employ a periapt. Presumably the Corr version is to do so at a distance. None of the other Prime effects on this page include a Corr version.

                                                        513 - When using Correspondence with other spheres, the Mage's correspondence rank caps the rank of other spheres in the spell. That is, a mage with Matter 5 and Correspondence 2 is limited to Matter 2 when using Correspondence.

                                                        513 - Unless other spheres are included, Correspondence deals with whole patterns only. No partial teleportations.

                                                        513 - Corr 2/Life ? or Corr/Matter ? to teleport housecat sized objects to the mage. This is nonsense. The book just got done saying that Correspondence alone can teleport objects, so this should not need Matter or Life. Even with that correction, there is still a problem in that this section doesn't specify the logic as it seems to do something that isn't available till Corr 4, just with an arbitrary size restriction. I think the idea is that this is is an extension of reaching across space, essentially adding picking things up to all the other ways a Correspondence mage can manipulate them at a distance. The size restriction isn't a size restriction at all, its the limit of what a mage could easily reach out and grab if it were right in front of him.

                                                        513 - Combined with Forces, Life, and Matter, Correspondence 3 can move things around from a distance, levitating, manipulating, or teleporting them without physical contact. Okay, this is a hot mess. Firstly, its Corr 2 to move things from a distance, not Corr 3. Using Forces rather than your hands doesn't change that. Secondly, Life and Matter, aren't necessary. The statement that Corr 3 allows teleporting objects without physical contact does support the idea that Corr 2 'teleportation' is just picking things up from a distance. If so it is the only meaningful statement in the paragraph.

                                                        Edit: 513 - Permanent gateways are stated to take 10 successes, which contradicts the 6+ (storyteller discretion) of the reference charts. No reason is given for the special rule.

                                                        513 - Correspondence 4 plus appropriate spheress to ward or ban. No indication of how you calculate the appropriate spheres is given.

                                                        516 - Life 1/Corr 2 to extend life senses across space. I think this was included for padding.

                                                        518 - Matter 2/Corr 2 to move an object through space. If the item is small enough, this is Correspondence 2 alone. If it is larger, it can be Correspondence 3 alone. Matter is, at best, a locking sphere. More padding, it seems.

                                                        523 - Corr is one of the spheres listed to help Time capture things in bubbles of time. I'm not sure what Corr would add to this. Possibly it would be used to capture locations, but by default spells are cast on locations.

This was probably meant to mean that a spell to take a mage from point A to point B does not accomplish the effect by summoning a taxi. Corr 4 can definitely teleport a taxi to you.

                                                        533 (Axis of Coincidence sidebar) - Corr 3 to find a taxi. Why is this not 2?

Correspondence is to allow it to "skip from one place to another through intervening distances in order to avoid hitting other vehicles." Poor wording, but Corr 4 is the correct sphere for teleporting the car and its occupants. The other spheres have much poorer justifications though.

                                                        563 - A Corr 2/Mind 2/Matter 2 for a locked spell that wards against scrying, pushes a general keep out vibe, and warns the caster if someone trespasses.

                                                        563 - A Corr 2/Mind2/Entropy 1 spell to mentally impulse to get the targets to say embarrassing things in front of cameras. Entropy is a superfluous add on, but since this is an example of a player making a spell, that's fine.

It ends with an alteration of US law with belief in political power as a focus. If its supposed to be a transportation spell it is barely a spell as he mostly uses resources he has available. At best there's a small amount of mental influence, which would be Corr 2/Mind 2. If it is supposed to be an actual mental manipulation of judges, it is a Mind 3 spell. No correspondence is needed because the mage is in the room. There's not enough detail to see why this should be the combination it is.

                                                            Special Mention - Consecration

                                                            Prime 1 specifically mentions that a mage can consecrate an item to himself so that it will change metaphysically when he does. This includes when he shapeshifts and when he steps sideways. Indeed, having an item (or clothing) that travels with you is one of the primary purposes of this. Well don't tell that to the Otherworlds section. In that section, it is unnecessary for stepping sideways and not enough for astral or agama travel, which require Prime 2 + 5 successes on a willpower roll and Entropy 3/Prime 2 respectively.

                                                            You can make the argument that the Agama Sojourn and the Astral Sojourn involve you leaving your body while just stepping sideways does not. That would certainly make sense. But then the two sojourn sections say to bring items they need to be metaphysically bound to the character and that's what consecration does.

                                                            Given the explicit wording of Prime 1, I think it overrides the Otherworlds possessions sections and a lot of wordcount was wasted.
