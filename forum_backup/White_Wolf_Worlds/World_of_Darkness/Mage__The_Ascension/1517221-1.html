    Originally posted by Dataweaver View Post

Still, that separate character need not be physical; and while it might be capable of interacting with others besides you, it doesn't have to be. Or it could simply choose not to.

    Regardless, this would make the Avatar more significant than a mysterious figure that shows up primarily during Seekings, and would warrant the Manifest Avatar Merit. On the other hand, if you're fine with your Avatar mainly showing up in Seekings and only occasionally showing up otherwise, then you need neither Manifest Avatar nor Mentor.
    Oh, I don't want the avatar always around, so no mentor, ally or manifested. It is more part of the backstory of the mage. Of course, it will still show when doing seekings or in the moments he sees fit.
