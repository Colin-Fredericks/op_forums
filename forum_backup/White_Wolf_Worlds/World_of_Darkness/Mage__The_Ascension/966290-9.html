            Originally posted by Lord Revan View Post
            Five Eyes That's one of the most difficult concepts of Mage.

            JoeDuncan Five Eyes got it. Will is a very broad and confusing term in Mage. The hermetic use it when pointing and shooting with the wand, of course. But when you bring this to etherite (or even worse, technocrats) the "Will" part gets more confusing.
            Your, and almost everyone here, answer is a valid one. I just don't think its the better one, I prefer the one I gave in this post.
            What makes "Will" such a "difficult concept" in Mage? It's pretty clearly spelled out in a number of places, but this will do:

            "While all mages are willworkers, bringing about effects by imposing their will on the Tapestry, it is hubris to imagine that an Awakened mage may simply think of something and have it occur."
            ...
            "Instead, each mage comes to an understanding of magic through the rituals and trappings of a particular style of magic, which focuses their will and resonates through the Tapestry..."
            ...
            "Integral to all paradigms, especially at the beginning of a mage's Awakened life, are foci: magical tools and rituals which focus the mage's will. Foci are the practical expression of the paradigm, the means by which the mage turns his internal understanding into reality."

            (from here: http://whitewolf.wikia.com/wiki/Paradigm)

            Seems pretty clear to me.

            A mage's "Will" is simply what they want, or wish to happen. For the vast majority of them (who aren't enlightened masters), they CANNOT *execute* their "Will" by simply thinking about it - they HAVE to use their foci in a *physical* expression/correlate of that Will.

            For Mages of mystic paradigms, they can eventually reach a level of enlightenment where they realize the "Truth" and are able to abandon their foci. The only reason mystic mages are able to do this is because their paradigms *allow* for magic to be accomplished by simply "thinking" about it.

            Technomancers are unable to abandon their foci because their paradigm doesn't allow for it, the technomantic paradigm rules out the possibility of firing laser blasts by just thinking hard enough.
