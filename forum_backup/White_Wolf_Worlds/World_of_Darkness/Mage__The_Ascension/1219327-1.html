        The Background says that if you don't give them their weekly Quintessence (which could be given through affection for a cat, or special batteries for a robot, a specialized waveform generator for the alien you picked up in Otherspace, or through intimate contact if they're human-like) they will leave. Now while such behaviour suits a cat or a spirit, I really don't see a robot dog suddenly running away b/c you didn't change the batteries. Technically speaking the Background should be lost forever if you fail to feed them, regardless of in-universe justification for getting it back... this is meant to balance out how much Paradox you can get rid of, and how good their traits are, and how obedient they are. A Level 5 Familiar has really incredible traits and is Mentor, Ally and Retainer all in one in some ways.

        In all honesty I would allow a Player to recover their powered down robot, or hop into the Umbra to find their spirit friend. Some mental or social rolls might be needed to justify the recovery.
