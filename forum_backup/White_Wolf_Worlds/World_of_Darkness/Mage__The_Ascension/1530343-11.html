Each of these portrayals can lead to a different image of the Technocracy. In the former portrayal, the Technocracy are earnest, and actually think that their cosmology accurately models reality. In the latter portrayal, the Technocracy does not necessarily have any ideological commitment to their cosmology, and may even recognize that it is not objectively correct, but they think that it would be the best reality for humanity to live in. These are two distinct lines of reasoning.

                                                    Mage generally presents the idea that physical reality is malleable. Moral reality is never stated as such. In fact, everyone in Mage acts like they consider morality as largely objective. That is, they disagree about what the right thing is, but they don't think the other side can become right if they convince enough people. The moral and philosophical part of the Ascension War can continue regardless. It is the strategy of the war that depends on a complicated reality.

                                                    The Technocracy has been portrayed as thinking that the universe is mechanistic in nature, and they have also been portrayed as thinking that Enlightened Science is a more useful method will-working than mystical types of will-working. Each of these portrayals can lead to a different image of the Technocracy. In the former portrayal, the Technocracy are earnest, and actually think that their cosmology accurately models reality. In the latter portrayal, the Technocracy does not necessarily have any ideological commitment to their cosmology, and may even recognize that it is not objectively correct, but they think that it would be the best reality for humanity to live in. These are two distinct lines of reasoning.
                                                    Those both will exist even in a part subjective, part objective reality, as you put it. And more besides. As long as reality is complicated enough that mages don't know what is actually happening (even if they think they do) we can have these differences of opinion make sense.
