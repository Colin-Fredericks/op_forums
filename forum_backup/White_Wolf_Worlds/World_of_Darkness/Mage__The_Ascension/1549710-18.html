                    Mind 2

                    •• Read Surface Thoughts/ Empathic Bond/ Create Impressions/ Mental Impulse

                    Now the mage begins to skim the contents of unshielded minds, discern emotional states, read memories that have been left behind on objects or places, and project single words or emotional impulses to other people. The simpler the emotional content, the easier it is to send or read; a flash of rage, for instance, is easy to project or receive, but the complex stew of reflective melancholy presents a challenge to inexperienced Mind-mages. (Game-wise, such complex feats demand more successes than a simple feat does.)

                    Read Surface Thoughts (Read Surface Thoughts)

                    It seems obvious what this power does on the, well, surface, but there isn’t much guidance for what constitutes a surface thought. Intuitively, though, it seems like this covers anything the target is actively thinking about, or can’t avoid thinking about. It also seems that deeper motivations and anything the target is trying to hide will be harder to access or off limits.

                    While this is supposed to be less powerful than later ranks, it is worth pointing out that if the mage can read both surface thoughts and memories (and there is reason to think he can) there is an awful lot he can pull out of someone’s head.


                    Detect Aphantasia

                    In reading someone’s surface thoughts, a mage will easily be able to tell if they form mental images or not, and if they have an inner voice or not. Since the presence or absence of either of these doesn’t have a practical effect, especially in game, this is unlikely to have an impact in play. I just find it interesting that mind mages would have known about this phenomenon since the dawn of time.

                    Read Emotions (Empathic Bond)

                    Contradiction Resolution - Mind 1 or Mind 2

                    Both rank 1 and 2 give the mage the ability to sense emotions. Mind 1 says ‘sense emotions and surface impulses,’ while Mind 2 is ‘discern emotional states.’ The best way of reconciling this I can see is that Mind 1 allows a mage to detect the presence of a strong emotion, with no real information about the reason. Meanwhile Mind 2 allows a mage to see the complex emotional state of a person, including some information on context. It is the difference between ‘he is angry’ and ‘he is angry because he feels slighted by his brother.’

                    Given that this ability is in the same list as reading surface thoughts, it is quite likely that any context has similar limitations.


                    Detect Foreign Emotions

                    Quite a few denizens of the WoD can manipulate the emotions of a target. An observant mage can likely use Mind 2 to tell the difference in context between a natural and an imparted emotion.

                    Read Memories (???)

                    Ambiguity Resolution - Memories on Objects, Memories on People, Memories of People


                    Mind 2 says a mage can read memories left on objects or places. Objects and places is a term we saw in Mind 1 - a mage could use Mind 1/Matter 1 to read psychic impressions off objects and places. Two things of note:

                    First, there is no mention of Matter here. Read straight, that means it isn’t necessary. That is an option. That said, the terminology is so close that we could also reasonably infer it. That’s an ST call.

                    Second, Mind 2 strangely leaves off reading the memories of a person, though it also doesn’t explicitly exclude it either. Again based on the patterns of Mind 1, if a mage can read memories off an object he should be able to read them off a person. Indeed, it would be very strange for Mind 2 to bring up reading memories and not intend that meaning.


                    Memories Bound in Human Skin

                    It’s not uncommon for someone to experience something profound or traumatic or overwhelming, then bury the experience so they can continue with their day. The memory was strong enough to read, but isn’t any longer. However, the mage could potentially read the imprint it left on the person’s clothes or even off the person's skin.

                    Send Thoughts (Mental Impulse)

                    At Mind 2 a mage has the ability to send thoughts and emotions. I split the two apart because Thoughts needs special attention. The mage has a one word limit, but in practice, I think this is functionally capable of more than one word if only because of how language works. A concept that needs a single word in one language might need several in another, but as a thought there is little difference between them. I’d go so far as to say that thoughts that have well known aphorisms, like ‘don’t make waves’ should be possible, but that’s an ST call. Indeed, some ST’s might think things should work differently in different languages.

                    On another note, the text does not make it clear if the mental impulse is obviously foreign or not, so a mage can presumably have it work either way. That gives two major uses of this power:


                    One Word Communication

                    The first is simple communication. Single words work very well as warnings and instructions, and can also be used to convey yes/no answers and simple ideas. Added: As Aleph points out, this would reasonably include talking with animals, though that might lean more towards the Send Emotion side.


                    Super Secret Handshake

                    A variant of direct communication, here the mage imparts a code word that verifies his identity, whether he’s been followed, etc. This would obviously need to be worked out in advance, but it is a great element of an espionage based game.


                    And Always Let Me Your Conscience Be Your Guide

                    The second main use of Send Thoughts is manipulation. The mage imparts a thought that is not obviously from elsewhere, and it is here especially where the one word count gets fuzzy. If the target is not aware the thought is from the outside, he probably isn’t perceiving the word so much as its meaning. So here the limit is likely thoughts that can be easily summarized in a single word, like ‘run,’ or ‘delicious,’ or ‘meh.’

                    Impart Emotion (Mental Impulse)


                    Imparting emotions, like reading emotions, has more range and nuance. With more successes a mage can impart more complex emotions. Given the examples, there doesn’t seem to be a limit to emotional complexity. Also, even if multi-word aphorisms aren’t possible with Send Thought, the emotional meaning behind them is still possible here.

                    It is not stated whether the mage can set the subject of these thoughts and emotions, but the degree of control makes that unlikely. At least directly.


                    Memory Seed

                    Actually implanting memories is a Mind 4 power, but Mind 2 has everything needed to ‘help’ a target develop their own false memories. By manipulating the emotional context of certain thoughts and memories, a mage can lay the groundwork for specific false memories to form. It takes multiple applications. First the mage uses this power to remind someone of a memory or thought or person or thing, then he uses it again to apply the emotion while the target is remembering. This is then repeated periodically over a period of time. This is a dark, dark power


                    Not My Circus, Not My Monkeys (AKA Return of the SEP Field)

                    Whether it comes as a thought or as an emotion a mage can project the sense that something is not worth getting involved with. Note that this version of this effect may not be able to set a specific target, but will instead apply to whatever the target is looking at or just in general.

                    Alternate title: ‘He has a Clipboard, Don’t Make Eye Contact’

                    The reverse of this gives the target or targets feelings that something is out of place and demands attention.

                                    Mind 3

                                    ••• Mental Link/ Project Illusions/ Dreamwalk/ Psychic Blast

                                    With increasing skill, the mage learns to link minds, forge telepathic communications, read or influence another person’s thoughts, craft mental illusions, enter someone’s dreams and explore dream Realms, and blast psychic assaults into an unwilling rival’s consciousness. At this stage, Mind attacks can inflict painful – though rarely fatal – damage. Combined with Correspondence, Forces, Matter, or Life, he is able to employ telekinesis, pyrokinetics, and psychophysical assault, influencing objects, elements, or people with the power of his mind. Unlike most other Effects, this sort of thing is not coincidental, although such talents do have a place within popular culture.
                                    Here we come to one of the difficult lines and of course it comes from conjunctional Mind effects. Before that though, Mind 3 can:


                                    Link Minds (Mental Link)

                                    Precious little information is given on what this means, so it is quite versatile. It seems to be the basis behind telepathic communication, reading minds, and influencing minds. That said, it is also listed alongside those, so it also must mean what it sounds like. A mage can link minds (his own doesn’t have to be one of them), which presumably lets the linked mind share thoughts, senses, or memories depending on how the link is set.

                                    I’m open to any arguments for limitations on this one, but I’m not seeing any at the moment.


                                    Sympathy Pains

                                    At Mind 3 a mage can curse someone to feel whatever he does to the mage, which is handy for making people think twice about hurting you. This could reasonably share wound penalties, too, so it would also ensure that the target is as incapacitated as the mage.

                                    Telepathic Communication (Mental Link?)


                                    Now the mage can fully communicate with others mentally. This expands the communication ability of impart thought to full on messages and ongoing dialogue. There's not much to be said about this as telepathy is common in fiction.

                                    Forge Telepathic Communication - Faking a message

                                    I’m not really sure why the word ‘forge’ was added, and while I presume it was meant in the creative sense, it can also be in the deceptive sense. Indeed, there’s no reason a mage with Mind 3 couldn’t use telepathy to pretend to be someone or something else.

                                    Read Thoughts (Mental Link)

                                    Now a person’s mind is an open book. There is very little a mage can’t pull out of it with enough successes

                                    Read Mortifying Moments

                                    Hey, you know those stupid moments from your past that make you cringe? The ones you still find embarrassing even if, intellectually, you know they were no big deal? The ones your mind will occasionally dredge back up from the depths seemingly to torment you?

                                    With Mind 3 a mage can find out about those. Pretty easily too, if they have that much staying power.

                                    Manipulate Thoughts (Mental Link)

                                    If Telepathic Communication was the continuation of the communicative side of Impart Thought, this is the continuation of the manipulative side. The mage can now actively alter the targets thoughts. He can twist the thoughts that are already there or implant new ones. At this point he can actively alter a target’s current motivations and goals.


                                    Ruthlessly Leave Someone’s Mind Alone

                                    Mind is very powerful, but sometimes its subtlety accomplishes even more. A clever mage may decide to leave a target’s mind entirely unchanged, except for the certainty that his head has been meddled with. This can cause the target to distrust his own memories and motivations. The mage doesn’t have to spend any time or effort making sure the target’s new memories match the old, because there are no new memories. He can just let the target’s paranoia work for him.

                                    Mental Illusions (Project Illusions)

                                    A mage can also manipulate minds to see and sense things that aren’t there. There is no restriction or limit given for how detailed these can be, so this can be anything from small tricks of the light to full on walking dreamscapes. Notably, these only affect the targets’ minds, so nothing else will see them. That’s what makes them mental illusions rather than all the other kinds.


                                    A Toddler in Motion

                                    A really simple but attention-grabbing illusion is a blur of motion in peripheral vision, either beside the head or near the feet. Since it is outside the range of clear vision the illusion doesn’t have to have much detail, nor does it have to stick around long.

                                    Enter and Control Dreams (Dreamwalk)

                                    A mage can enter another person’s dreams and live them or make alterations to them. Not much detail for the power is given here. Since projection is a Mind 4 power, I think this relies more on Link Minds to function. Where the limits between those two powers are, I'm not sure.


                                    “Prophetic” Nightmares (AKA Manipulate the Chosen One)

                                    This is the bread and butter of many (well, at least two, Voldemort and Sideous) dark lords. By manipulating dreams to repeatedly predict ‘future’ outcomes a mage can disorient and torment a target. At the very least he can keep them anxious and sleepless, and possibly manipulate them to take dangerous actions to prevent the false future.

                                    Mind Blast (Psychic Blast)

                                    Raise Combat Skill by +2 each turn. Some creatures may be immune to Mindblast.

                                    Wait, wrong game.

                                    This is the actual Mind 3 attack. It functions like any sphere attack except that without augmentation from other spheres it is always bashing damage.


                                    Got Yer Nose

                                    A mage can shape his Mind attack to look and feel like an attack of some other kind. In this case a disfiguring or mutilating attack that convinces the target that they are now missing part of themselves.

                                    From Forces 3:

                                    Transmute Thoughts

                                    As Forces 3 indicates, Mind 3 can transmute thoughts. It isn't unreasonable that shaping and manipulating thoughts and dreams would work this way, but it isn't obvious from Mind 3 that they could be shaped into other things. Forces 3 confirms that.

                                    From Common Magical Effects (lbeaumanior)

                                    Translate Languages

                                    At the level a mage can fully read someone's mind he can also fully understand relevant languages. This comes in two forms. The mage can use the target's thoughts to translate anything the target is saying or writing. He can also use the knowledge of nearby people to translate anything any one of them can understand or read. The former is very helpful when the mage finds himself in a strange land, the latter when he finds himself at an archaeological dig and he wants to read the artifacts without anyone knowing he can (assuming both the presence of ancient writing and someone who can read it)


                                    Conjunctional effects:

                                    Here we come to one of the difficult lines:

                                    Combined with Correspondence, Forces, Matter, or Life, he is able to employ telekinesis, pyrokinetics, and psychophysical assault, influencing objects, elements, or people with the power of his mind.

                                    I really don’t like this line and I traditionally view it as a wrongheaded attempt to incorporate Mind into psychic paradigms for no reason. However, the goal of this thread is to treat the language as intentional as much as possible, so…

                                    From the placement of the comma I am going to interpret this as telekinesis, pyrokinetics, and psychophysical assault are the examples of influencing objects, elements, or people with the power of the mind. Unlike other Spheres, Mind has to be intentional here.


                                    Pyrokinetics (??? + Transmute Forces)

                                    …pyrokinetics…

                                    Here I am saved by Forces 3, which already indicated that instead of using Prime to manifest something out of seemingly nowhere, a mage can take a thought and transmute it into a pattern. Here it converts thoughts to fire. The more I think about it, the more this seems quite reasonable, and it is refreshing that the two spheres are actually corroborating each other.

                                    That said, this only applies to actually creating fire. Just shaping and influencing it remains Forces only. Since most examples of pyrokinetics I can think of include creating fire, that works.

                                    Telekinesis (??? + ???)

                                    he is able to employ telekinesis

                                    Contradiction Resolution - What is Mind doing?

                                    The “transmuting a thought” explanation doesn’t work here. I mean, it does if the mage really wants to create the kinetic forces from seemingly nowhere, but that’s not necessary as there are always kinetic forces around to redirect and amplify.

                                    A Mind 3 attack paired with pattern spheres so that the blast affects material things works, but only for attacks, which really doesn’t fit the brief.

                                    Outside of those two, I can’t think of a situation where the same supplemental spheres couldn’t accomplish the same effect without Mind. If anyone has an idea, I’d love to know.

                                    Your Mind Makes It Real (Psychic Blast/Project Illusions + Life Attack)

                                    and psychophysical assault

                                    This at least I already have an answer for. Take a mental assault or a mental illusion and pair it with a life attack so that the wounds the target feels are made real. Here at least there are reasons why a mage might want to use a spell like this.
