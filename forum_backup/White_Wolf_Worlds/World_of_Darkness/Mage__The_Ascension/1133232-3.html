                        TO: ADRIAN NEWBERRY, CEO OF PENTEX

Some serious [expletive deleted] went down here and I'm not sure how to begin to explain it. Anna, she's our psychic, tried to read just went down and she ended up swallowing her own tongue. Not that such is uncommon but it's going to take awhile to regenerate. The best thing I can describe is the Jaw Tooth Hive is gone along with all of its members and we're going to need to find out why. I don't think the Puppies did this or we'd be in a hell of a lot more trouble than usual. It looks like Tunguska out here.

                        Memo 2#:

                        TO: MARCUS WHITAKER, PENTEX FIRST TEAM 38#
                        FROM: ADRIAN NEWBERRY, CEO OF PENTEX
                        RE: RE: THE JAW TOOTH HIVE INCIDENT

                        Leave this matter alone.

                        Memo 3#:


                        TO: MARCUS WHITAKER, PENTEX FIRST TEAM 38#
                        FROM: ADRIAN NEWBERRY, CEO OF PENTEX
                        RE: RE: RE: THE JAW TOOTH HIVE INCIDENT

                        I am frustrated to say I have been informed by our primary shareholder that you are to be brought into the loop regarding this incident. Apparently, the Breach Project were were going to use the Jaw Tooth Hive for is important enough that we need to find some substitutes and we can't have the individuals involved in this incident tripping over it. That is why you are going to read in on this information and you alone. Remember, we DO know who you tell these things. As you may guess, we on the board know more about what happened to the Jaw Tooth Hive than you do, and we know who is responsible.

                        In this case, it is a secret society called the Technocratic Union which, essentially, functions as a roughly equal-sized entity to Pentex and dabbles in many of the same businesses we do (bio-genetics, cybernetics, psychic abilities, and the extermination of troublesome supernatural entities). It is, despite this, incredibly stupid and in abject denial about other dimensions as well as beings like our Friends Upstairs. Despite its vast wealth and surprising military might, possessing a private army larger than any other on Earth (even our own), they are generally unwitting servants of the Weaver. Recently, we had to bring home a department we had infiltrated of their group and have been keeping one of its branches [see enclosed "Syndicate" file] pacified with large checks they are only too happy to take.

                        In general, the Union does not bother us or our allies. They are too interested in a ideological war against a group of mystical religious fundamentalists which occupies the majority of their time and resources. They have a "target of opportunity" policy with many supernaturals but the simple fact is they need the vampires to do the grunt work of covering up the supernatural's existence and misunderstand the place of the Wolves in the universe due to their, again, appalling ignorance. In this case, their discovery of the Jaw Tooth Hive seems to be due to one of their agents mistaking it as a "Nephandus Labyrinth" which functions essentially as a Hive for magic-users.

                        The Union dispatched a group of "Men in Black" to investigate, expecting minimum resistance but were immediately alerted by the Hive's banes and were promptly slaughtered by the Black Spiral Dancers within. If not for this action, they may have been content to simply monitor the Hive due to the aforementioned stretched resources. Instead, it became an issue of pride for the local Comptroller who promptly dispatched their local facilities resources to exterminate the Hive's population. Given this was a important facility with over 30 Wolves and several packs of Formori, it was a slaughter.

                        The Technocracy does not like to lose and what followed was an increasing use of larger, more nasty devices designed to reduce the target to ashes, including assistance from a group of theirs which actually is aware of other-dimensions called the Void Engineers. What was dispatched were devices designed to "sanitize" the Node plus large numbers of artificial beings essentially used as cannon fodder. With the assistance of weapons designed to kill spirits as well as devices capable of detecting them, they laid a number of explosives in the tunnels and collapsed them before burning the Spirit world for approximately 5 acres in five places. Casualties for the Union are difficult to estimate but I'd wager 6-1 in the Hive's favor with the majority of casualties being brainwashed soldiers and biological constructs.

                        You might call this a "victory" for the Union but, like their war with the Traditions, it has effectively crippled their operations in the area for the next few decades. My personal suggestion is we increase our presence there as well as work to "meditate" between the Black Spiral Dancers Hives wanting vengeance and direct them back to their traditional foes. The Union doesn't need more victories like this so let them congratulate themselves while we reap the benefits. They don't need to know there's hundreds of Hives like Jaw Tooth.

Garou that burn through their Rage, Gnosis, and WP quickly find themselves at fairly reduced combat ability as they lose access to all sorts of Gifts and the like once they run out of points to activate things with. Mages almost never need to spend points. Mages throw WP and Quintessence at effects so they're stronger, rather than as requirements (save for the rare spell that requires Quint). Quintessence in particular is less of a problem for most mages, because they can store large amounts of it (20 personally, plus huge amounts in quint battery Wonders if they want them or are provided them for a mission), and spells can be spent to gather more.

                                                            Battles of attrition are not in a Garou's favor against the Technocracy specifically.
