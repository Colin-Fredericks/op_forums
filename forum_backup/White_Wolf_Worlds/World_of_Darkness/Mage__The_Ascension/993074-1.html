    I wanted to throw in some adventure hooks to do with Abuela Maria (Derrick's grandma). The hooks mainly depend on what her feelings are about her grandson's path in life.

    If She Accepts It: Though it wasn't the route that she would've chosen, Abuela Maria respects Derrick's decisions and hopes that The Babel Center will bring him happiness. Unfortunately, she knows that showing herself to him would cause trouble for the both of them so she watches from a distance and keeps her communications subtle. She prefers to use the PCs and the local spirits as her go-betweens.

    Story Hook: The PCs have noticed that they have been getting a bit "too lucky" with the latest adventures in The Babel Center's realm. This so-called benefactor called, Momma, says that she wants to train them for the trails that will come as the Mate is being built. Do the PCs want to find out more about her or just go through her little quests with as few questions as possible? If they do find out her true identity, will they be able to trust her when the time comes?

    Story Hook: This works better if the PCs have had some past encounters with Derrick and need to get on his good side just as Molly has met the Babel Center for the first time. (Especially if the party has a decent number of Syndicates in it.) Abuela Maria believes that the Wonder would be better off with her grandson than some "useless slip of a girl". With some guidance, she plans to lead the PCs to the Babel Center in hopes that they can discredit or do away with Molly so they and Derrick can take the credit for the Wonder's discovery and control.

    There's Still Hope: The fact that Derrick misunderstood her message and joined the Technocracy hurts her so, but she will not give up on him. Abuela Maria will immediately contact Raleigh's local Mages (particularly the Dreamspeakers) to help her grandson see the light before it is too late. This route is best done in the early days of the arc before or shortly after The Five is formed. If it turns out to eventually be too late to convince him, she will take either of the other two routes.

    If the PCs are Mages, she will directly contact them to help her with promises that she can teach them her lore and give them what information she has on the Technocracy. While she will be kind and grandmotherly to the PCs, she will favor the Dreamspeakers. (Any Orphans will get advice to join her favorite Tradition.)

    Story Hook: The plans to create The Five are starting up and Abuela Maria has set up some major favors with some powerful spirits to allow her to possess one of the PCs or a local Technocrat of moderate power and influence and hold them into her personal Manchurian candidate. It's best if the ST drops red herrings to prevent the PCs from finding out who her pawn is and plays up on the paranoia that could prevent The Five from even being created. Or at least get them off to a bad enough start that it will give Derrick second thoughts on seeing it through.

    Story Hook: (PC Mages) Just like it was mentioned in the Mage section of the PC Involvement section, it is also possible to convince Derrick to leave the Technocracy for the Traditions, provided that the PCs and Abuela Maria intervene within the six month mark. This will be difficult even with her help, but one the best things to bring up how restricting the Technocracy has been and how often he has had to bite his tongue around them and remind him of the love and lessons his grandma gave him. If the PCs have already convinced Molly to switch sides, things will be a little easier and possibly bring an early war between the two factions for control of the Babel Center (if at least Malcolm knows about the Wonder).

    Curse Them All!: All Abuela Maria can feel is hate towards the Technocracy for taking her grandson away from "his true destiny" and vows to bring the Babel Center crashing down on their heads. This time she is not afraid to get the Garou involved in her little plans despite the massive damage they could cause. Despite her anger, she still loves Derrick and is torn on the idea if his possible death is just a mercy kill in the grand scheme of thisngs. Depending on the ST, this love could possibly endanger her and her plans if she chooses to intervene if he's about to die.

    Story Hook: It seems like The Five's enemies know a little too much about their plans and the PCs can't help, but to notice Abuela Maria's spiritual presence. If they choose to peruse and discover her identity, will they tell Derrick or try to get rid of her before he finds out in fear that such knowledge might endanger everyone?

    Story Hook: (Mage/Garou PCs) Abuela Maria has a risky plan: she will pretend that she has been captured by the PCs who plan to use her spirit as a bargaining chip against The Five. Once she gets in the Amalgam's possession, she's got a suicidal attack prepared that will shatter the Babel Center's realm to the core.

        I felt like playing around with this again and decided to put some alterations notes if you don't want to do the game with the revised setting (which it was made for).

        1st edition: Given how the Technocracy is portrayed in a much more negative and soulless light, the ST can choose to have The Five be a more sinister force with Molly being the innocent that they're using. (Though this would work better with Mage PCs.) Or they can be decent people who are hoping to use The Babel Center to help turn the Technocracy towards a better mentality. In this setting, Derrick will have more to risk due to the darker nature of the Technocracy as he tries to keep them from going further down their dark path over stopping a civil war. If you're going with the villain route, just have him want to line his pockets and get more attention for his trashy cable access station instead. As for Leah, have her accident be due to an encounter with an Umbral monster instead.
        Endgame: On the "It was me" path, the true awakening of The Mate will lead to a more positive Technocracy (much like it's 2nd edition and further counterparts). While "Doom-Bringer" creates a blow so bad that the Technocracy becomes even worse as they try to pick up the pieces.

        2nd edition: Given a more positive light, things can stay pretty more the same (use the 1st edition alterations for Derrick and Leah to have them fit in better). Since the Threat Null hasn't happened, helping the Babel Center gain it's hidden abilities will be easier since The Machine hasn't been cut off yet. Though this could be a problem if Interation X were to discover the true origins of The Machine they so admire.

        One of the great hidden powers of The Babel Center is it's ability to give vague glimpses into the future (provided the PCs can awaken that part of it) and with some work, it can give predictions of the upcoming Threat Null. If the ST wants to, a campaign to use The Babel Center to prevent or jump start this disaster can be done instead, after or along with the game's plotline.
        Endgame: "It was me" will grant the Technocracy a chance to prevent or at least weaken the impact of the Threat Null so less damage is done on them. "Doom-Bringer" can go two ways depending if the ST wants to use the Threat Null, the Babel Center's destruction creates a blast of powerful magic that awakens the Threat Null (or a similar disaster of lesser caliber) or the Mages take over the Wonder and have a better chance to win the Ascension War, instead.

        4th Edition: A lot of stuff can be kept the same (including the endgame). Just change Leah's encounter to having it be remaining parts of the Threat Null (or keep the original if you're fine with her and Gian being older) and give Derrick's mission having a lot more to ride on since the Technocratic civil war is more eminent. If the PCs would rather be part of the Disparate alliance, instead, just run it like you would with the Mage option.

        Sorcerer's Crusade: It can be done with plenty of alterations. Since America hasn't been made into a country yet, set the story in Europe and change some of the names if needed.
        Molly: Other than probably changing her name depending on where the story is set, she'll require the least amount of changes.
        Malcolm: Now named, Malawa, his family has recently moved from Africa to be closer to the Order of Reason and just joined the Void Seekers. While his family has a long line of magic users, they have yet to get the pedigree and prestige they have in original the adventure. This will make him more humble and not have to worry about his dreams of exploration being ruined.
        Gian: He's a member of the Cabal of Pure Thought and is more apprehensive about using a mystic-made Wonder at first until he sees what it can do for Leah and the Order of Reason.
        Leah: Despite being a Craftmason, she will have to bite her lip and let her future husband take the credit when the Mate's earthly body is made due to being a woman (given how the Sleepers are). Due to the setting, the Order of Reason doesn't have the power to fully heal her when an Umbral monster (instead of the Threat Null) sends her on her downfall, she's left paralyzed from the waist down.
        Derrick: Make him full or part Spaniard, join the High Guild and run a popular theater that specializes in the Commedia dell'arte. Since the Order of Reason has just started, Derrick is focused on making them a powerhouse he knows they can be.
        Endgame: "It was me" gives the Order of Reason more power and potential that will give them an advance in the upcoming Ascension war. While "Doom-Bringer" will cause enough damage to cripple them and if the other groups play their cards right, stop them before the OoR can truely get off the ground.
