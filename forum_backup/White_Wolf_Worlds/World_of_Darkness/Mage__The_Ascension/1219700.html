      1. I know that wich Correspondence sphere you can uso magick at greater distance but, what is the discance default for magick? (touch, a few meters...).
      The defeault are the Mage's senses - normally in most cases "line of sight" as we are used to using our eyes, but a blind mage might as well use their hearing or sense of smell.
      Theoretically it might even be possible to use a sound as a carrier for an effect, but an ST would have to rule if that was possible without Correspondence.

      2. With Forces 2 you can "push" things that come to you (altering his vector to move in anothe direction). What do I need to this happen in an object lige a shirt or a rock?
      Forces is enough for that. Forces 2 can move up to a human, or around 300 pounds/150 kg. Forces 3 can move two tons. Forces 4 can move 'small buildings'.

      3. How works visual effects trinkets?. If I understand well, a trinket youl be a objet in wich someone used Magick to give it specific properties such as extra resistance, fireproof, extra light, etc, however that some glasses let you use the Correspondence 1 of see the exact distance/measures is listed as a trinket in M20. There is something I misunderstood?
      Correspondence 1 might be enough for that, as long as the glasses touch you. Sphere 1 senses can be shared by touch, and the TruShades in the M20 book also use those.

          Your English is much better than my [whatever your Native Language is] I'm sure. You're doing great.

          As for Trinkets, it should be noted that TruShades are NOT a Trinket. M20 lists them as one. M20 does get a few things wrong, sadly. A trinket has a neat mechanical upshot. A javelin which flies extra far when you throw it, or a window which can't be broken, or a set of brass knuckles which do Aggravated damage. You get the idea. Glasses which let you see REALLY clearly (perhaps -2 difficulty to visual Perception rolls) would be a Trinket. Glasses which apply a Correspondence 1 effect, perfectly measuring the distances between objects... that's not really a Trinket.

          TruShades should be listed as an Artifact/Invention (if they need to be used by a Mage) or a Talisman/Device (if they can also be used by Sleepers). In this case it's much more likely to be a Device. That way the Technocracy can mass produce them for their sleeper operatives (unawakened constructs, extraordinary citizens, mooks).

          As an example...

          A special lighter which helps you start a normal-sized fire really easily is a Trinket.
          A lighter which lets a Sleeper generate a huge inferno is a Talisman/Device.
          A lighter which can start a huge inferno when used by a Mage is an Artifact/Invention.

          A ring that lets the wearer inflict Aggravated damage (passively) with punches is a Trinket. Here you're rolling your Strength (with any other bonuses) for Aggravated damage.
          A ring that lets a Sleeper activate a Forces 2 / Life 3 enhanced punch which inflicts Aggravated damage is a Talisman. Here you roll the Ring's Arete for points of Aggravated damage.
          A ring that lets a Mage do that is an Artifact. Here you roll the Mage's Arete for points of Aggravated damage.

          And out of those three examples, a Trinket could be best, or a Talisman, or an Artifact. It depends on the situation.

          The important distinction is that in the first case, a normal object is better than normal. It burns hotter, it sparks more easily, the fluid lasts longer... some Matter and Forces with Prime could sort that out. In the second and third case the object isn't just doing something well, it's doing something magickal and wondrous, you might say.

                                  Locking an effect requires a Patern Sphere at the rank required to make a change to the patern in question.

                                  To lock ANY effect to an object you need Mater 2 for a simple object (rock, shirt, hammer). For a complex object (car, gun, computer) you would need Mater 4.

                                  For living creatures you would need Life 3 for yourself or Life 4 for others.

                                  You only need to Lock effects if you want them to stay active away from you presence or if you don't want to maintain concentration on them.
