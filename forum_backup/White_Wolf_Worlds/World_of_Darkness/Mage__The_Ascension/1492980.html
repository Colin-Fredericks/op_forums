      I'll talk mostly from memory of another thread where we discussed precisely that:

      1 - Since it is stated directly and with no caveats in the description of level 4, I'd say it needs no other sphere;

      2 - Probably, I don't see why not. The ST would be justified in imposing this limit, though, simply because the rules are silent on the matter;

      3 - None. By itself it would just be governing systemic collapse;

      Damage is an abstraction and an organism is a system, those are the key concepts to understand damage/healing through Entropy. You don't die exactly because of trauma, loss of blood or infection. You die because at one point the whole system could keep itself despite failures and at another point it couldn't. And since every system isn't a thing in itself, but an emerging feature of the interaction between parts, this is rather hard to really predict. That's why sometimes people survive freak accidents and sometimes they die of getting up too fast, death is a systemic collapse.

      So what is Damage? It is an abstraction of how close you are to the event that will cause a systemic collapse. What kills you isn't the damage, it is the last damaging event. What hinders you isn't pain necessarily, but how much the last damaging events hindered the functionality of the system. How Entropy affects that at level 4? It affects the very governing force behind any systemic failure: yours truly, entropy itself.

      How does it looks like? Well, how does someone looks like when the doctors say they're out of danger? Missing limbs are still missing, gaping wounds are still gaping, but the body managed. Your body managed to stop blood loss, things are just as bad as before, but you're stable, you'll be fine. Your life hangs on luck alone, but luck is on your side, it seems. The building withstood heavy damage, but refuses to cease to be a building, the box was shaken by an earthquake, but the contents refused to mix or break, your body is in a terrible shape, but it just won't stop working. Because at the end, the transition from being to not being is entropy.

      One important aspect to it, though: contrary to Life, an Entropy healing effect isn't necessarily lasting by default. It may be powerful, but still be a temporary, or even permanent but dismissible. Let this sink and enjoy toying with the distinction between using one or another.

          My original answer was lost so I'm going to retry.
          1. Does it require other Spheres (Life, Spirit, Matter), or it does this on its own? Both for damage and healing.
            No. As monteparnas said, Entropy is given this ability with no restrictions. While M20 can trip itself up in the requirements section this time it tracks. Entropy is a sphere that can target patterns and this effect explicitly does, so there shouldn't be targeting spheres. You also don't need to anchor the spell to a pattern in this case.
          2. Could it cause/heal lethal/bashing also?
            Trickier. The reason that Entropy causes aggravated damage is that it attacks the pattern directly. It isn't spelled out as explicitly as it could be but pattern damage is aggravated damage and vice versa. I'm inclined to say you can't use Entropy directly on a pattern and not cause aggravated damage. That said, there are other ways of attacking with entropy and the writeup does indicate that Entropy 4 can affect living bodies. While you will probably not run across a situation where it matters, that is a different effect.

            The distinction might matter with healing, though, as you'd need two different spells to heal both Agg and other damage.
          3. On its own (no other Sphere needed), what other capabilities from the Life Sphere (if any) could it replicate?
            Very little. The effect isn't described as a graceful or nuanced, it is just increasing or decreasing the entropy in the pattern. There's nothing to indicate fine control.
          4. Reattaching limbs?
            Maybe. Just maybe. f it is a perfectly clean cut and you put the limb in place precisely. Otherwise no.
          5. Regrowing limbs?
            No. Human bodies don't normally grow back limbs so you can't induce them to with Entropy.
          6. Kill virii/bacteria?
            Yes.
          7. Revert localized damage? (Burns, lacerations, etc)
            Yes. If you get into the details, you probably couldn't target a specific wound. I don't think you need to get that granular at the table.

                  A small thought on the relevance of patterns to Entropy. As far as I can tell the concept of a pattern derives from the Platonic theory of forms (Theory of forms - Wikipedia) and so the form of a body represents the ideal essence, or perfection of that form. Thus, the pattern is the perfect form of the body, i.e. undamaged and in perfect working order.

                  Entropy 4 has the ability to directly affect body patterns either increasing or decreasing the entropy within the pattern, and this it's 'perfection'. This change to the pattern is reflected as damage (increased pattern entropy) or healing (decreased pattern entropy) to the body.

                  As a different example, imagine a photograph of a person, the original photo shows them in great health, but increasing entropy is like making a copy of a copy over and over again, so that eventually the picture is nothing but a smudge. That's how increasing entropy in a pattern damages it, by making the pattern less and less perfect.

                      I would have to disagree with my friends above in that Entropy 4 cannot be used to kind of replicate other uses of Life; I believe it can, albeit in a much more subtle, harder and less effective way, or at least in a much longer span of time to manifest, and a bit more restricted way.

                      In order to try to understand why, we must first ubderstand what Entropy is and does (the Sphere, not the real life physics meaning).

                      I always like to compare Entropy with Spirit, because Spirit is the most "mystic" of the Spheres, so in order to understand Spirit under techno paradigms - "Dimensional Science" - we must first understand it in a mystical sense to only then "translate" it to whatever technobabble desired.

                      With Entropy, the reverse is true, so it's easier to understand it using real life scientific concepts and after that "translate" those to a coherent mystical view.

                      What's important to know is that such "translations" are for us players and STs to understand them out of character, and they do not occur in the game universe.

                      Now, Entropy, in my experience, is the most misunderstood of the Spheres, even more so than Prime or Spirit. With that being said, we gotta ask, what is Entropy? What exactly does it control?

                      Well, it's chance, probability. Or, destiny and fate.

                      So, the first thing we must understand is that it doesnt control ANYTHING directly, but rather the "randomness" (if there is such a thing) all around us.

                      How does a living body act? How does it work?

                      When a molecule of oxigen is "captured" by a hemoglobine, how does that work?

                      Well, in real life, it certanly isnt "magic". The hemoglobine in fact have an electromagnetic affinity towards a molecule of O2, thus when they happen to be in close proximity, the hemoglobine "captures" it, like a magnet attracs an iron nail.

                      The thing is thou, both the hemoglobines and the O2 are RANDOMLY distributed over the body. See where this is going?

                      The use of Entropy does NOT make the Hemoglobines magically transmutate into something else, but rather you control the random distribution of hemoglobines in the blood.

                      Take that example and apply it to almost all molecular biochemistry happening in the body. For example, our DNA produces RNA by bounding with the building blocks bases present in our citoplasm, and those bound with aminoacids to form proteins. I wont describe the entire process because that would be tedius, but suffice is to say that those RNA and aminoacids molecules are "randomly" "floating" inside the cell. Now, our biochemistry have several catalyzers to speed up all those processes, a consequence of billions of years of evolution to optimize our processes, but all of those still act upon molecules randomly floating inside the cells.

                      And here is where Entropy acts: actively driving all the random processes in a specific direction.

                      Now, pushing a single molecule of O2 towards an hemoglobine 0,001 seconds faster wont make much of a difference, but if by "pure luck" (aka Entropy effect) you make EVERY single O2 molecule position itself in a more "favorable" way to be more easily captured, having every single aminoacid in the body be in a more optimized position by pure "random luck" in order to have just a tiny little edge in the protein syntetization inside the cells, and have every single cells of the immune system just fall in line, having that exact immune cell be in the exact place where an infectious virus just entered the body... Every single one of those tiny changes, by themselves, have a completely null impact.

                      However, ALL of those changing at the same time have a MASSIVE impact.

                      Thus, could you use Entropy 4 for example to create an effect similar to the vampiric discipline Potency - there is, add extra damage to a punch?

                      In my opinion, YES. The effect will have every single muscular fiber in your body move in the perfect way to maximize impact; every molecule of adrenaline hit the muscular cells in an optimized way; the very biophysics movement of your body to be optimized, and when you hit your target, instead of breaking your own hand, the energy gets dispersed in your fist due to many "random" events, like the angle, the fact that some additional fat was delivered by your blood to your hand in that exactly moment by "sheer luck", and another huge array of random tiny events that are imperceptible by themselves, but pile up upon each other to cause a massive emergent pattern.

                              Now, can Entropy 2 be used to create a hurricane, for example?

                              Well... YES!

                              Now, creating a hurricane with Entropy 2 would be a MASSIVE endeavor - meaning, requiring a TON of sucesses.

                              Why? Because, unlike using Forces to "force" the air into a massive kinetic force, as if the Mage became a massive magnet capable of attracting a huge mass of air molecules, doing this using Entropy 2 means that you are changing the random movement of each air molecule around in such a way that those pile up upon one another until they reach an apex on a huge Butterfly Effect.

                              How likely would that be to occur?

                              Well, in one place it may be very simple. Maybe there's already a hot air mass in collision course with a cold air mass, so changing just a few molecules can start the "domino effect" that would culminate in a chain reaction, unleashing a hurricane. In the Caribbean for example, that may be easily done, not requiring that many successes.

                              Here in Brazil, that simply NEVER happens - so it would truly require some exceptional collection of "random amount of events" to trigger it.

                              Lets put it on numbers: lets say that in a single moment, there's a 1 in 100 chance in a region of the Caribbean that a hurricane could spontaneously form.

                              Well, that's a probability... Which is EXACTLY the purview of Entropy.

                              So, just change the chances, get your desired effect.

                              So, maybe in this case you "only" need 10 successes with Entropy 2.

                              Now, in the case of Brazil, lets say that the chance is 1 in a million. Not impossible, just highly unlikely.

                              So, now you'll need a much greater set of improbable events to kick this off; the ST could say you need 10.000 successes for this (!). Or, more generously, instead of a linear escale of successes towards probability, say that this is a somewhat exponential or logarithmic scale, so say, 20 or 30 or 50 successes for pulling that one off.

                              Likewise, there are some things that are just outright "impossible" for Entropy 3, or just simply so unlikely to be virtually impossible. Can Oxigen naturally transmute into Hidrogen?

                              Well, the chance is close to zero... Or near so. In fact, given an infinite amount of time... It could. But that would only occur under quantic fluctuations, so it's so ridiculously low that I would require 1 TRILLION successes to change O2 into H2 using Entropy 3 alone.

                              Thus, while theoretically possible, it is impossible in practical terms.

                              Likewise, let us imagine for a moment DNA mutations. Let us put mystical interpretations aside for a moment and stick to hard science for a moment.

                              Now, a bird's wing is codified in their DNA.

                              It is possible to thinker with human DNA to create humans with wings.

                              So, what's the chances of thinkering with human DNA in order to create humans with functional wings?

                              Well, it's very, very low. Could be done, but highly unlikely.

                              Most of the times, DNA mutations cause CANCER, or genetic diseases when not cancer.

                              Once upon a time thou, such mutations actually breed some improvements to the organism.

                              Those are absolutely random - thus within the purview of Entropy.

                              So, using Entropy 4 to make someone faster or stronger is relatively easy... Growing wings spontaneously just by chance manipulation could well be in the realm of needing BILLIONS of successes however.

                              But, inducing cancer or a heart attack would be very, very simple.

                              In here, I'll use an example from Mage, Revised edition for Entropy 4: Blessing. With it, you could bless an unborn fetus with good health. That's a relatively easy thing to do - you just increase the chances that the body will develop in the most optimal way.

                              This example also proves in an official sense that Entropy 4 CAN indeed be used to alter live patterns besides just harm/heal
