As written and conceptualized in structure and philosophy, the current version of the VA and Etherites would take something pretty damn significant to integrate. And no, the reason actually given for their part in being Euthanatos is just not going to cut it. Trying to equate geasa (and buada, their reverse) and the concepts from Indian religion and mysticism that dominate the Sect.... it's just not functional. The Verbena DO focus on Death. It's part of the Cycle. It's what's going to happen when you go after a lot of blood. Saying that the Aided are so death oriented that they just don't fit is really undermining the Verbena as described and limiting them artificially to half or less of the Cycle, tossing out 1/3 of the Maiden, Mother, Crone style trinities, etc.

                                I do but that different groups might have split up into different Sects, and that might be interesting to explore (a la the Darwushim divide up between 3 Sects). But there would probably be a reason. And when there was the Grand Sorting Hat period at the Grand Convocation, it was all the groups being split up and sent to their respective tables. ALL of the Bjornaer moved from the Hermetic table to the Verbena table. Might a few have broken ranks and stayed? Probably. Ending up in Ex Misc almost certainly. There might even have been an iconoclast or two that said "Nope! Dreamspeaker all the way!" And, sure, it's possible that all the Bjornaer stayed buddies and there is a Bjornaer drinking club in some bar somewhere where everyone whose mystic mentor lineage goes back to House Bjornaer can take a seat and hoist a pint, but that's almost an entirely different matter. It's more in the nature of Allies, Contacts, possibly even a Chantry or even a Cabal of sorts, more than a matter of Sect. (Though, really, doesn't that seem like a great idea for a game? The Heirs of Bjornaer/Darwushim/Valderman/whatever as a cabal with members from all the Traditions that partook of the original group's mysticism?)

                                However, there is NO link by Practice within any Sect at this point, not the Hermetics, not anyone. As per M20 p. 159, Hermetics as a group demand Alchemy, Dominion and High Ritual (but no mage is required to have all of them, so, probably at least one, but probably more? Thanks M20 for the lack of guidance on this...), but venture out to.... whew.... Chaos Magick, Art of Desire, Hypertech, Craftwork, Weird Science, Yoga, and Malficia. So there is nothing determinative about the Hermetics practice(s) that sets them apart as a group from anyone else. At all. Any mage could have any of the above or any combination of the above and NOT be a Hermetic at all or even have magic that "looks Hermetic" for whatever that means. The idea that Sects have some kind of magical praxis that unites them and differentiates them from the praxis of different Sects is not a part of M20 at all. It's a remnant idea from earlier editions where the ideas that are now identified as Focus were handled differently. In essence, by not having a category of magical praxis that ties together the magic of members of a Sect, sort of an uber-Focus, M20 has de facto come down and set things up so that Sects are almost entirely political and social, not technical/practical/mystical groupings.(Which is something lost in the game IMO) I mean, COULD you have a Hermetic who uses Voodoo? Sure. Could you have a VA who uses Voodoo? Absolutely. They could probably be differentiated by their Instruments, but, just as likely, they could not be. So what makes one dude a Hermetic and another a VA? It's who they hang with. Not so much in who their friends, but more in the sense of which professional association they have a membership in and what union meetings they attend. Being a Hermetic doesn't unit them in any other way, anymore.

                                Which pulls us back to the OP. There is really nothing at all that, as a matter of how their magic is done, that keeps VAs from being Etherites. Even politically, as Dataweaver points out, the Etherites are very ecumenical in what the goal of all THIS is, so it would be very easy, ideologically, to fold them in. But, as a matter of ideology, it would be a lot harder to embrace all the goals of the Etherites under the umbrella of the set of Ascension-type goals prevalent in the VAs. What makes the two different? Really, it's the social ramifications of their history.

                                        How has M20 actually changed anything here?

                                        The Traditions have always been highly socio-political groups, whether a given one clusters around a style of magic or not. The Traditions have never actually strenuously demanded specific Paradigms, Practices, and Instruments as M20 defines them. M20's Focus creation system doesn't say - anywhere - that the Traditions no longer have internal cultural norms that members are expected to abide by.

                                        Not having lists of required and banned Focus elements for each Tradition/etc. isn't a message that any and all combinations of them work in every Tradition. It's just that the game expects us to use our brains and make sure it makes sense. The Yoga Practice makes perfect sense for members of House Tytalus to use, even if what they do wouldn't be seen as yoga in common parlance, if you look at what the Yoga Practice actually means.

                                        Sure we don't have M20 versions of the Traditionbook levels of detail on each Traditions philosophies and styles, but M20 has more about each by just recommending Focus elements for splats than any previous corebook really managed.

                                        They idea that anything has radically changed in M20 around this baffles me.

                                                            Can I get any argument that this is actually new? The Focus system in M20, to me, didn't change anything. It clarified what was already there. By Revised, every Tradition had technomancer styled factions. Every Tradition had been expanded a lot from their 1e archetypal imaginings. The whole line of thinking about something being different about M20 fails, because it seems to want to claim what was already there wasn't.
