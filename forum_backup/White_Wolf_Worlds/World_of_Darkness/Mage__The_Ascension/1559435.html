    Mage: Scarlett Dawns (A Post-Apocalyptic setting for Mage and Changeling)

    04-15-2026, 05:47 PM
    The subtitle refers to civilization slowly restarting after a century of plagues and terror. ("Red in the Morning/ Sailor's warning) This is a different Ascension War. A modified form of Smallpox came out of Russia in the later 2030s and wiped out billions in a few months. Vampires, (both Cainite and Cathayan), Wraiths, Risen, all being dead were unaffected by the disease organism. Ghouls, Dampir, and Mummies of various types were also immune. All Fera and Kinfolk, all Changelings and Kinane, and similar fay related folk were also immune. Why isn't clear. All Mages, Sorcerers, Psions, and anyone using numia, were also immune. Similarly, no one understands why. Theories are all over the place.

    With the planetary population plunging from around eight billion to 500 million in less than a year the Umbra went insane for decades. However, both Paradox and Banality seemed to be jamed in some way. Bedlam replaced Banality as the main threat to Changelings. Mages were often able to create areas of refuge and stability because of radically reduced Paradox and the lower population providing less resistance to magic.

    Over time communities stabilized. The modified Smallpox kept returning and it both deformed and sterilized most survivors. But in spite of that some children kept being born. So communities kept on going.

    Now in the 2180s there hasn't been a visitation of the plague in sixty years. The Shadow Lands feel nearer across a weaker Veil. But Specters and Wraiths no longer make all the world a nightmare. Vampires and their servants still roam. But most Vampires are in topor. Though the few active ones are hungry. Bedlam and Banality have faded. But Glamor is rare too.

    Most human communities are Mage led. These communities are like Crocus blossoms. The first flowers of spring in constant danger from frost.

    #############################

    More later. This setting is mainly Mage.

    Please free free to Kibbitze. I might use your ideas or not.

              We'll start with some basics. The plague was a modified form of Smallpox that first appeared in areas were Russian troops were fighting to hold on to Tatarstand and Chechnya. It soon spread into Southwestern and South Asia. As the modified Smallpox always took the form of the Black Pox ( a moderately rare form of Smallpox that kills far more quickly and brutally, you rot alive) it wasn't identified as Smallpox for awhile.

              Areas physically remote from Eurasia or Africa had longer to plan how to combat the plague. Mages and some other Supernaturals managed to create islands of safety. Thus, in the 2140s most surviving humans live in places like the Americas, Australia, New Zealand, the British Isles, Japan, and islands generally.

              #####################################

              The plagues brutally weeded out humanity. A group of Hermetics and Etherites came up with a means to modify human genetics to enhance survival. All surviving humans born since the 2080s are genetically modified.

              When creating a character under the age of eighty automatically add a bonus dot to Stamina. Also add one additional bonus dot to each of the three categories Physical, Social and Mental. Note, taking an Attribute above six still requires a Merit. However, two Attributes can be taken above six by taking a merit instead of one.

              Although Mages have high willpower (or they wouldn't be Mages) non Mages born after the 2040s get a bonus point in Willpower. This represents the kind of mindsets it takes to survive this world.

                  Each of the major Mage factions shattered. The Technocracy and the Disparate Alliance are gone. But they've left legacies. The Council of the Traditions is still around in a less unified form.

                  The present form of the Council is based around an Alliance of the Hermetic Order, the Etherites, and the Mercurial Elites. The Hermetics also maintain their traditional ties to both the Euthanatos and the Sahajiya and has formed the five groups into the basis of the new Council. The Akashayana are pragmatically friendly to the council. They are more semialigned than part of the Council. The Celestial Chorus is similar but less friendly. The Verbena and the Kha'vadi are on speaking terms with the Council. The Kha'vadi tends to let local tribal issues and needs determine the relationship. The Verbena see the Council as going off in the wrong direction but neither useless nor people they want trouble with.

                  The Council's main goals are the rebuilding of human society. The Hermetics have set up the Adocentyn Foundation. The name comes from a Hermetic Utopia in the book the Picitrix. The five core members of the Council are working to build a functional model for a new civilization. Democracy, Equality, Community, Education, and Enlightenment are supposed to be the foundations. Different communities seek different ways to make these ideals work. They trade information about both failures and successes.

                                  The members of the Council have all transformed.

                                  The Order of Hermes is strongly focused on society. Their goal is a better society that can rebuild the world better than it was. Education is vital but building functional communities is the goal. Utopia, the perfect society, is seen as a wish dream humanity might achieve thousands of years in the future. It's more important to focus on function than perfection.

                                  The Etherites seem the least changed but they're actually focused on the nuts and bolts of how societies survive. They come up with power sources, improved crops, functional robots, and the like. They create the material basis of society.

                                  The Mercurial Elites still value computer and numbers but they were always about communication. News services, shipping, travel and the like are their fields.

                                  The Sahajiya still about Ananda the sacred pulse of creation. So they're still using the arts and celebrition to connect the Sleepers to Ananda.

                                  The Chakravanti tend to focus more on
                                  teaching about living a life worth living than on their darker side. However helping to make sure the evils of the older world stay dead is a major goal.

                                  The Akashayana aren't as closely tied to the Council as they were but, paradoxparadoxically, that makes them vital. The Akashayana run what are basically Buddhist monasteries ( well, they represent a variety of faiths) near but not always associated with Council bases and communities.

                                  The Verbena, the Celestial Chorus, ​and the Kha'vadi are more distantly connected to the Council. They form their own communities more isolated from the main Council communities. These communities focus on religion and the cultures that support them. However the main Council communities are vital to supporting these communities so they say connected.

                                      The Disparate Alliance collapsed during the plagues but most of the alliance members are still around. Diminished and weaker but around.

                                      The Children of Knowledge are a craft that exists mainly within various members of the Council of the Traditions. The alchemists are mainly members of the Etherites and the Hermetics. They have their own cabals within each tradition acting alternately as members of the larger traditions or a seperate tradition according to the circumstances. In the few actual cities (New York, San Francisco, New Orleans) there are independent alchemist groups.

                                      The Bata'a they are around. In the inhabited Caribbean islands they are a functioning craft. In most of North America the Bata'a function as a faction of the most sympathetic local tradition.

                                      The Sisters of Hippolyta like the Celestial Chorus, the Kha'vadi and the Verbena, the Sisters form their own communities. The Council is friendly and supportive enough. The Sisters aren't happy but they find the situation tolerable. The Sisters are very willing to cooperate with the hospitals run by the Council or their individual members. In some ways the Sisters have taken the place in society Nuns held between the 1920s and the 1990s.

                                      Ahl-i-Batin were hit brutally by the plague. The lands in the web of Faith were almost completely empty. The present population of these lands is lower than at any time since before the earliest founding of Jerico or Göbekli Tepe. The Batini actually have their strongest numbers in the British Isles. In North America the Batini pass themselves off as orphan mages.

                                      The Kahu are mainly in the Pacific Islands including New Zealand. Australia and California also have groups of these Kahunnas. They maintain trade relationships with the Mercurial Elites. They don't intend to be caught by surprise again.

                                      The Wu-Lung have their strongholds in Taiwan and the Pacific Islands. However, as the Chinese American community was all over North America the Wu-Lung can be found anywhere. They often join the most sympathetic local cabal.

                                      The Knights of the Temple of Solomon are basically allies of the Celestial Chorus. The knights stay separate but they find the alliance useful. The knights are becoming more willing to work with the Council. Most of the knights core values remain the same, but pragmatism counts too.

                                      The Ngoma Africa was brutalized by the plagues. The Ngoma are struggling to rebuild. The Council can't provide much aid but between enhanced crops and medical aid it is seriously helping the situation. The main Ngoma presence in North America is House Ngoma in the Order of Hermes.

                                      The Taftani pretty much went back to their ancient ways just with a vague understanding that they will try not to cause too much chaos. So far the bloodshed is minimal.

                                      The Hollow Ones don't really exist much. In the few surviving cities of North America there are groups that are orphan mages that live bohemian lifestyles and seem rather morbid. But the popular culture and urban society that gave the Hollow Ones their context are gone. However, people say crows return.

                                          The Technocratic Union took a harder hit.

He was not "tyrannical" in the modern sense. Basically, the League promotes authoritarian government as the path to rebuilding the world. They aren't having much luck in persuading people. However, the Syndicate does money lending and banks. While the NWO sells "services" to various governments. They make money and have influence. They aren't respected.

                                                          Vampires

                                                          Both ​Cainite and Cathayan vampires were slammed hard by the plague. For western vampires the food supply vanished, well except for ghouls and other vampires.

                                                          There is no known example of a Ghoul surviving the plague. Either they were devoured, died of excellerated aging, or they escaped, broke the bonds of blood became fully human again and probably died of the plague. While it's possible one or two ghouls did survive, it's not something anyone has heard of. Revenants on the other hand make it through. All four families known to the general Sabbat, the three families known to the Inner Hand of the Black Hand, and the families that serve the Tremere (1) and the Baali (2). (Note: I think I missed a few Revenant families). However, not all of the Revenant families were stable enough to survive all that well. The Revenants are alive, but many are in poor condition. The Dampir are surviving as well. Given their advantages and tendency to be more or less sane, assume that they are doing relatively well.

                                                          The Cainites are much rarer and far less powerful in this setting. Although the few neonate vampires that get created are of higher generation and trained by powerful elders, the collapse of human civilization and the loss of the resources that the elder vampires controled stripped the Vampires as a group of power. While individually powerful vampires now lack the means to manipulate from the shadows. The Masquerade is gone, the strings to pull are gone, the wealth that controlled the humans used as herds is gone. What remains are small gangs of predators working as packs to survive while their instincts scream that they are solitary hunters.

                                                          At first the Cathayans laughed. The fall of humanity only took away one source of Chi. However it was soon understood that humans and their communities were the primary source of Chi. As the Cathayans grew weak the Beast Courts and the Hisen both came to understand that if the Cathayans survived the local humans might not. Humans, troublesome as they are, were chosen. The Cathayans were driven out of Asia. The broken remnants of the Cathayan vampires made their way to the Americas to find survival.
