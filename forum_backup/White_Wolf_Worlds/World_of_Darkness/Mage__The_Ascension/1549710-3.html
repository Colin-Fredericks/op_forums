                            So, for me, the problem with Forces Patterns and what level of Entropy effects them is that IRL we don't really know how predictable these things are. Physics is full of things that act extremely predictably... until they don't. Part of it is that in reality, it's nearly impossible to find a "pure" energetic "thing." Energy is always acting within a system. Particle wave duality means even the purest forms of energy sometimes get stuck bouncing around like particles instead.

                            A classic (and recently culturally relevant) example is the three body problem. Even with Einstein literally rewriting the scientific consensus on mechanics from Newton's classical version, he didn't end up getting to a solution to this conundrum. We can accurately predict the relationship of the sun and the Earth, as well as the Earth and the Moon... but if we put all three together it becomes mathematical chaos.

                            While normally it's a good idea to separate real world science and Mage when figuring out what level of Sphere to use, in this case I think it can help a lot. It's not hard to draw a line between when Forces falls under Entropy 3 as a Predictable Pattern and when you need higher level Entropy because you're dealing with an unpredictable Pattern: does classical mechanics have an easy to apply model to the system at hand. If yes, it's a generally predictable Pattern (even if within some assumed things like "on Earth"), if no, then it's probably too chaotic for Entropy 3.

                            The energy of falling while a small object is under the sway of an overwhelming gravity well (aka the stuff around us subject to the gravitational pull of our planet) is easily predictable. Even Einstein's theories of relativity reduce down to Newton's laws of gravity under those circumstances. Bump things up to orbital relationships and we get into the nth-body problems and predictability goes out the window. Meteorology is similarly full of relatively simply predictive models and highly variable models. There's stuff we can predict with relative ease, and stuff that's always more guessing than not. We know pretty exactly what conditions generate tornadoes.... but we have zero idea how to predict where individual funnels will form, and if a given tornado system will result in lots of smaller funnels or less bigger ones (or whatever combination).

                                To my mind there are two possible dividing lines between Predictable Patterns and those of higher Entropy ranks.

                                The first as Heavy Arms says, focuses on the practically predictable. Predictable Patterns are just that - things we can reliably describe and predict. If the system is unpredictable, or if it is functionally too complex, too reactive, or too difficult to calculate in any reasonable time frame, it goes in a higher rank.

                                The second is more of a philosophical or theoretical distinction. Predictable patterns are those that, no matter how complex, are unwilled and mechanistic or probablistic. We may not be able to predict them easily, but we assume that with enough time and information and research we could. Higher ranks are for things that defy prediction. Things with wills, desires, identities. Most notably this includes life, which is why rank 4 is Affect Living Patterns. Of course this assumes that life is not deterministic, which some people will object to, but I think it works for the purposes of an RPG.

                                I suppose there is a third option. Anything that either of the previous two would put in rank 4 or 5 goes in rank 4 or 5. So everything that is willed, responsive, or personal goes in Rank 4+, as does anything overly chaotic and anything with emergent behavior. Only those systems that are unwilled and coherently described go in rank 3.


                                I'm not sure which of those, if any, Mage intended, but I do have two indications from M20, both from the Spheres writeups. Unfortunately they aren't the best sources. They do associate Entropy with forces and weather, but they don't do so clearly.

                                Regarding Entropy and energy:

                                Forces 3 gives the possibility of Forces 3/Entropy 3 to erode or enhance energies. This is the entry that made me realize Entropy never explicitly mentions energies at any rank.
                                Now this is a rough entry to go through. For a start, it seems to be the wrong rank of Forces. Most interpretations of enhance or erode energies are possible at Forces 2+. Also, while it does indicate that Entropy 3 is the rank for affecting forces, it isn't clear how much Entropy is doing. The two main possibilities are:

                                1) This is one of those cases where M20 pairs two spheres that can accomplish the task on their own. Either Forces 2+ or Entropy 3 could accomplish the job.

                                2) While Entropy 3 can work on matter without help and Entropy 4 can work on Life without help, Entropy 3 can only affect energies with the addition of Forces. That's really janky, but it is a possibility.


                                Regarding Entropy and weather:

                                Time 4 gives the ability to capture things in bubbles of time, and gives an example of using Time, Forces, and Entropy to capture a tornado. Again, we are blurring the lines between Forces and Entropy, so we face the same two options as above. Also, while we get some evidence weather and Entropy are connected, we don't get any indication of what rank is involved.

                                    My approach to those options is largely based on game-play considerations. While the developers might have a different opinion on things, I assume that the present paradigms in M20 are supposed to be equally coherent to the setting.                                     In a deterministic paradigm all systems are predictable with enough knowledge. There is no random. There is no uncertain. There is a lack of knowledge and/or perspective necessary to know the predetermined outcome. This includes living creatures. While a deterministic paradigm would have to make some sort of exception for Awakened will as able to override determined reality, all unawakened Life Patterns would still be predictable with enough knowledge. We have sciences all about predicting animal behavior. It's not beyond some level of predictability.

                                    So, if we take a philosophic approach over a practical approach, the Sphere Rank needed varies by paradigm. And is something I think we can all agree shouldn't be the case for a host of reasons.

                                                            On another note, I should probably get Entropy 4 up as, unfortunately, energies are not the last undefined part of the Sphere.


                                                            Entropy 4

                                                            •••• Affect Living Things
                                                            At this Rank, the mage assumes the awesome power of blessing, cursing, and conferring outright health or decay. By influencing the flow of entropy within a living body, that mage can grant outstanding vitality to, or inflict sudden disease upon, his subject. Such influence, as noted above, inflicts or heals aggravated damage and may grant long-term luck or misfortune. (Storyteller’s option as to how this manifests.)
                                                            Unfortunately, ‘blessing’ and ‘cursing’ aren’t as informative as they first appear. We probably shouldn’t interpret Entropy 4 to be capable of any mythological curse we can find since some curses, like bad luck, are already possible at Entropy 3 and others, like transformations, are outside the realm of Entropy entirely. Given the examples I think we are meant to extend the possibilities of Entropy 3 to living systems.

                                                            Given that, Entropy 4 can:


                                                            Increase or Decrease Health (Affect Living Things)

                                                            Most directly, Entropy 4 takes the ability to speed and slow decay from Entropy 3 and extends it to living systems. This includes both speeding or slowing breakdown and sudden failure. Even if this is an extension, though, it is a very powerful and versatile one.


                                                            Quick Healer

                                                            Since the body can heal itself, Entropy 4 can also speed or slow that function. Most obviously this can be used to speed up healing times. More darkly it can interfere with healing, leading to lingering wounds that are slow to close and heal.


                                                            Shrug It Off

                                                            Given that entropy can slow damage and heal things, it should also be capable of making them resist damage. Entropy should be capable of giving someone a natural soak, though this could easily be flavored more as good fortune in how someone takes and moves with the hits rather than just greater durability.


                                                            Jam the Living Machine

                                                            Much like before, Entropy should be capable of triggering sudden failure modes in people and animals. This would include sudden impediments like sprained ankles, thrown out backs, and stiff necks.

                                                            It could also reasonably include major life altering events like heart attacks and strokes, though that could disrupt a game in more ways than one so that’s a table call. For my part, I would often handle those as magical attacks rather than events.


                                                            Eternal Youth

                                                            By slowing down ageing to almost a stop, or even reversing it, Entropy can grant functional immortality, subject to the usual terms and conditions of immortality. Immortality is not legally responsible for suspicion, jealousy, the pain of outliving loved ones, or being dumped straight into the Deep Umbra when reality catches on. Do not take immortality if you have or may develop an incurable degenerative disease. Ask your doctor about immortality... or better yet, don't. He counts as a witness.


                                                            Direct Entropic Attacks (Affect Living Things)

                                                            At Entropy 4, the sphere is capable of direct attacks on the target’s pattern. These are by default Aggravated. This is also a capability brough up in Base Entropy, and I should point out here that while Rank 4 specifically talks about it in the context of living things, Base Entropy does not. It is not clear, then, if Entropy attacks require supplemental spheres to affect other supernaturals.


                                                            Heal Aggravated Damage (Affect Living Things)
                                                            This is one I usually forget about, but Entropy can heal patterns and specifically it can heal Aggravated Damage. There doesn’t appear to be any restrictions on this, so it looks like it functions like Life healing, only more difficult (because of the higher rank)


                                                            Heal Vampires

                                                            This came up in another thread. Normally, healing vampires requires adding Matter 2, but at this level Entropy can tune up both living and non-living systems, so even assuming Entropic Healing functions like Life healing, there is a case for letting Entropy 4 heal vampires without help. This same logic would apply to direct entropic attacks on vampires. Similar logic would apply to fae at Entropy 5 and fera at… whatever rank Entropy can affect spirits.


                                                            Missing Powers?

                                                            Increase or Decrease Health… of Spirits (Affect Living Patterns)

                                                            Ambiguity Resolution – What Counts as a Living Pattern?

                                                            This is basically the same dilemma as Entropy 3, only here the question is do spirits fall under Living Patterns. This is considerably less obvious than Predictable Patterns, though, and that didn’t turn out to be obvious.

                                                            Because the Low Umbra is one of Entropy’s domains, we definitely can’t make the claim that it doesn’t affect spirits at all and we can’t claim it always needs help. At the very least it needs to be able to affect ghosts at some rank, and it should probably be able to affect other spirits too.

                                                            I think Entropy 4 is the better fit, but I could also see holding off until Entropy 5. What do you think?
