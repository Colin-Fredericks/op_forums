                Agreed. And each convention has their own take on what constitutes freedom and control.

                The NWO are advocates of socialism, and state that people should be free to do whatever they want as long as they can know about it, and ensure that people don't abuse their freedom. The people have a right to feel safe.
                The Syndicate are advocates of neo-liberalism, and state that people should be free to have access to whatever they want. The people have a right to the pursuit of happiness and the avoidance of stress.
                The Progenitors are advocates of medical science, in freedom from the worries of the fragility of life. The people have a right to a healthy life, even if they were dealt a bad hand.
                Iteration X are advocates of tech being incorporated into every facet of modern living, in the freedom to move beyond their human limitations and achieve what they yearn for. The people have a right to convenience.
                The Voice Engineers are advocates of exploration beyond the known for the sake of protection, and that people should be free to live safe in the knowledge that they are the masters of their destiny without worry of the cosmic horrors which lurk in dark corners. The people have a right to not be in constant fear of that which they cannot comprehend.

                                                        Originally posted by Saikou View Post
                                                        Dear Journal.

                                                        We're 12 pages in and there's no sign of a cease fire.

                                                        Diplomacy has failed, Communications are tense, and appeals of "Guys! You're both right!" have fallen to these steadfast legions.

                                                        On one side, the Anti-crats. Adamant in their assertions that Tech is only a solution for the destruction tech has brought upon the earth.
                                                        While opposing them, are the Techno-pros, Unwilling to shift their claim that without Tech there would be no progress, and only stagnation.

                                                        It's hard to think back, all those....4 days ago, how did this all begin? A simple comment, an expression of an opinion. an exchange of conflicting views... Then came the Anthropologists and the Historians, experts in their fields, willing to use real world claims to back up their notes. Then the Canon's were brought out, each one firing an impressive display of completely made up but generally accepted an adhered to facts. The carnage was brutal on all sides was brutal.
                                                        Throughout the flurry of History lessons and Source book references, a number of voices were heard, appeals to emotion. Cries for the glory that is tech, anger towards the evil that is control. All of which built up a great catalogue of interpretive thoughts.

                                                        We are all experts. We are all essayists. We are all the masters of our own downfall. Don't you see? We brought this upon ourselves. For each one feels entitled to their own interpretation, but to justify their own convictions, they must destroy any attempt to undermine our own theories. Our own theories about a world which we created, and we have the power to change because it doesn't exist.

                                                        The facts are not facts. The History is optional. The main events are modular. And the masterminds are whatever makes sense at the time. Such ideas cannot be tolerated by either side. There is only one way to interpret this game, only one! Perhaps the only thing either end may agree on is that only one view can stand...

                                                        We are trapped in a bitter war that has no end, no real purpose. As discussion continues to deteriorate, the massive walls of impenetrable explanation are being built, and cries of "Do what you're like, but this is why you're wrong" echo in all directions.

                                                        What is there left to blame? Will an agreement ever be met? If neither side will back down I can only see one logical endpoint.

                                                        Calling each other stupid.

                                                        I pray it will not come to this.

                                                        Signed
                                                        I'd buy you a drink for that if we'd be in the same city!


                                                        To me, it basically two different things:

                                                        1. Interpretations of the setting.
                                                        2. What the books say.

                                                        The authors always said that nothing in the books is the Holy Script, that you could change everything to your tastes. And I'm full supporting that. That's just a different thing than arguing about the setting-as-written.

                                                        Like, we could argue about that the Cain and the Antes story of Vampire is silly, that it had to be different than the myth. That doesn't change the fact the the game and the game and setting supports at least the existence of a first vampire and the progenitors of the clans and names quite a few and follows what happened to them.

                                                        So, in the official material, some things left deliberately non-clear to allow for different interpretations even within the official setting. However, the material supports some ideas more than others.

                                                        In the end, it's not about which is "right". All interpretations are right. It's about what the official setting says.

                                                        To me, the Reality Zones and Earthly Foundations version is more plausible, because I think it's more supported in the books and it's in-line with other gamelines. I just feel, that if you want the Fully Malleable Reality version, you must cut out and change and explain things as falsely created history in a quantity, that it ceases to be the official Mage, and, with a wider scope WoD setting. Every historical record, every other gameline, even Mage's DA version and even more.

                                                        To following up Ramnesis' request (if I'm remembering correctly) to speak about the whys of our choices as preferred interpretations go: aside from the above, because it's basically applying Occam's razor to the question. Moreover, I think that's the version that enables the most diverse games (in the sense of crossover) as it is the most compatible version with other games. Also, it allows for different magical thought schools to coexist without the need of constantly blowing each other up. Also-also, it's just makes the setting more plausible to me, more believable and I'm not THAT humanist, so I like the idea of having a World that's not just about humanity, because I think it's enough of a problem IRL that humans think everything is because of and for the sake of them.


                                                        At the end, I just want to add that Karlgust said somewhere there or on the other topic, that he likes these arguments. For once, I could stand behind THAT. Actually, these discussions, with all their heat, helps me to, for example, getting into the technocratic mindset which helps to portray them in games in a more nuanced way. So, thanks everyone, it's always a pleasure!
