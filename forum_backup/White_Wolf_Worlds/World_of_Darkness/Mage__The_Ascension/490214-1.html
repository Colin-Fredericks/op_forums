                                    Obviously going off piste by bringing up CTD, but Naming 5 could pull it off if the Kithain knew what a Mockery Breed was, and had access to spirits, animals and humans to Rewrite. Doing it without the spirits of the Middle Umbra taking note in the region and then telling on you to any Garou around... that's much harder. Basically there is a way to do it, but it'll wind up with you dead.

                                    As for doing it with Magick, you only need to make a handful, and then they can breed. However be careful! Mockery Breeds suck for a few reasons compared to 'true' changers, and here are two big hurdles you need to overcome as a result:

                                    - You need to create an Animal Kinfolk population. If the last Kinfolk Wolf were to die, then it would mean the current generation of Garou are the last. No Changing Breed can survive without animal kinfolk. Mockery Breeds seem to be an exception to this, but their artificial nature, and this lack of animal kinfolk, seems to have serious complications for them. A stable and safe population of animal kinfolk would help you to 'stabilize' your mockery breed. Say you were to convert 10% of Scottish Deer into Kinfolk to your new Weredeer - that would be a good start.

                                    - The spirit world will have no time or respect for them. Samsa barely scrape by learning Level 1 Gifts, and Level 1 only, because Cockroach is protecting them. He's a pretty powerful and respected Spirit, and that's all they have going. The Yeren have the full backing of one of the most senior members of Pentex outside of the Board (because he is one!) and they are Project Lycaon's biggest ever success, and yet they can never get above Rank 3. And why? Because the spirit world, even Wyrmish spirits, find them to be appalling artificial newcomers. They are only a few steps away from being as stable and workable as Corax, say, but they get no respect because they're new.

                                    So if you use Magick (or Cantrips/Unleashing) to make a Weredeer Mockery Breed, you'll want to do a big spiritual PR campaign for them, which might see some moderate returns within decades if you have Pentex or The Union or the entire Beast Courts or Garou Nation behind you. Genuine acceptance may take millenia, millenia the world doesn't have.
