    Garou do not control every forest. While they are likely to be dominant in the wilderness area of the continental USA (2.7% of the land area or 170,500 square miles), the forested area of the continental USA (34% of the land area or 1,180,000 square miles) is far too large to be claimed by the twelve hundred or so Garou who call the USA home. Of course, if you are a Mage and go hunting for Caerns, you will have a much higher probability of running into Garou (and Garou may be the least unpleasant thing that you run into when you find a strange Caern).

    I think 'prey' in this context means innate abilities. Any human can go into the wilderness with a Land Rover, body armor, and a small arsenal to slaughter wolves, and he will be the top predator (at least if there are no local Garou around). The same human who runs out of gas on a stretch of lonely road in the middle of the forest with just his street clothes and the stuff in the trunk of his car is probably going to be prey if the local wolves take exception to his existence. Even Changelings, since they have innate lethal soak and have their innate Birthrights, are less vulnerable than Mages when stripped to just their innate abilities (Mages are just humans with strange backgrounds when they are stripped down to their innate abilities).

                                        Even when Mages are in the Umbra, they still suffer pattern bleeding when they maintain augmentations for more than a day (one health level of lethal damage or one point of Quintessence per point of improved attributes and major change). Pattern bleeding has nothing to do with the Consensus, it has everything to do with the Pattern (only by permanently changing the Pattern, by accepting permanent Paradox, can you avoid it). Archmages avoid pattern bleeding by consuming large amounts of Quintessence or by accepting large amounts of permanent Paradox.

                                        As for creating multiple copies of yourself, I think that only one copy can actually use True Magic because there is only one Avatar and each would count as a 'major' change to the other copies (meaning that each copy takes a number of health levels of lethal damage or requires a number of points of Quintessence per day equal to the other number of copies). Like most ubermunchkingamer tricks, it only works if STs are willing to ignore the rules of Pattern Bleeding, Paradox, etc. When STs are unwilling to let players break the rules, then the trick is only somewhat abusive (it would still require 20 successes per copy though, as copying yourself is a godlike feat).
