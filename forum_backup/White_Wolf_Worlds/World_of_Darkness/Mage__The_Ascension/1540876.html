Now, I don't mean that this means completely wiping out all the Reality Deviants of said city, far from it and quite the opposite actually, doing that would be actually stupid. Setting 101 Rooms to recruit the strange "Enlightened" people of this reality - basically brainwashing centers to turn captured Mages into brand new Enlightened Technocrats.
