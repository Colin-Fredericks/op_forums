                            Originally posted by Dataweaver View Post
                            Odd how these threads always end up being about the Technocracy, even when they start out being about the Traditions.
                            I have a theory - Giles voice

                            Essentially, it's impossible to discuss the nature of the Traditions in these sort of things because so much of what they're justified in doing and what they're up to is in response to the situation. You need to define what exactly they're the RebellionTM against in order to talk about any sort of moral ambiguity or evil they may be. The Traditions also have a weird sense of where the fans can't agree on fundamental facts about what the Traditions goals, methods, and ideology is other than "Techno-Illuminati bad."

                            Which leads me to four separate Tradition views:

                            1. High Contrast: The Traditions are in a state similar to the Matrix. Once you are awakened, the Technocracy will track you down and proceed to force you to join them. They will black bag you over the head, kill and replace your family with clones, and torture you into compliance. Maybe you'll get the stock options and cloned sex slave/trophy spouse offer first. However, you must prove your loyalty first and repudiate anything other than total belief in the Union. Players are always on the run and the Union will frame them for being pedophiles, serial killers, terrorists, and otherwise turn everything as well as everyone against them. You're the Incredible Hulk and must constantly be on the move unless you're part of a chantry and even then, the Union will hunt that down to sanitize.

                            Clash of Ideologies: The Union's ideology doesn't actually matter weirdly, nor does the Traditions. It's really too much of a fight for survival to get into the subject of naval gazing over God or communism. There's your side and their side and never the twain shall meet.

                            2. Low Contrast: The Technocracy and Traditions are in a state of Cold War. The Traditions have huge chantries and other dimensional palaces while the Technocracy have their Death Stars and Enterprises. Big moves are fairly rare but do happen on occassion. Instead, this is all about the two elites fighting for rulership of humanity. Outcasts like the Hollow Ones may be in a fight for survival but your Tradition determines who you're supporting for when you rule the world. Foci are probably best thought of as tools rather than paradihm as the difference between both sides is just who signs your pay checks.

                            Clash of Ideologies: A lot of naval gazing and debate but at the end of the day, it's about who rules what. The Order of Hermes will eventually turn against everyone else or the Celestial Choir but we're allies today. A lot of questioning of whether any of this is helping anyone as you ALL live in Ivory Towers.

                            3. Evil vs. Evil: The Technocracy are fascists and the Traditions are fundamentalist terrorists. The Traditions blow up buildings full of cancer cures and the Technocracy gases minority churches. Both sides are complete scum and there's absolutely no value to siding with either of them save as a matter of survival. The Nephandi may have an appeal because it seems Awakening just makes you a fanatic who thinks that GOD is behind your magic and SATAN behind everyone else's or some variation thereof.

                            Clash of Ideologies: Everyone you're allied with today is an enemy tomorrow because there's only one true way of magic.

                            4. Good vs. Good: This is the one I hate the most and weirdly what I think Revised wanted to do. Basically, the Technocracy and Traditions both want to make the world a better place for Sleepers and their continued fight against one another is a waste of resources. The Technocrats want to cure all disease and make a utopian future where the environment is pristine and everyone eats while the Traditions want to make a similar magitech Utopian future where prayer does cure cancer and you can visit the stars with astral projection. There's no Enrons or vaccine deniers and it's only really belief the other side is a bunch of evil psychopaths that keep peace from happening.

                            Clash of Ideologies: Both sides see the other as way more extreme than they actually are and frequently throw out accusations like, "The Traditions want to throw us back to the Dark Ages" and "The Technocracy wants to eliminate human free will" which is about as accurate as saying Hollywood vampire pedophile rings for both.
