            Well, there are problems with creating silver bullets from lead bullets in the barrel of a LMG (and does the character have the Heavy Weapons Ability?). Silver possesses a lower density than lead, so the same mass will be a larger bullet, which will burst the gun when fired because the gases from the propellant cannot escape (turning lead bullets into silver bullets is Matter 2/Correspondence 1, turning lead bullets into useful silver bullets is Matter 4/Correspondence 1 to turn the excess silver into air). Of course, I would not tell my players that unless they possessed the right Abilities (Firearms 3+, Science 1+, or Technology 3+ would be sufficient).

                            I think it is more knowing the limitations of the character type. Mages are powerful with experience and preparation, much more so than equivalent supernatural characters, but they are not meant to be tanks. Mages survive through deception, evasion, and subterfuge.

                            I have been playing Mage since 1996, and I still find it odd that there are Mage players who think their character's are Superman. Yes, Mages are adaptable and flexible, but they go squish as well as any other mortal when going toe-to-toe with other supernaturals without their Spheres. Even Changelings without their Arts are tougher than Mages without their Spheres. And you do not need exotic powers, just getting rid of foci is sufficient (and you can take a Mage's hands, feet, and tongue if they do not have more separable foci).

                                                    Yes, Mages are better off avoiding drawing attention than looking for other supernatural creatures. Each level of Arcane should halve the chances of a random attack by supernatural creatures (which would probably be only 1:10,000 per day in a normal city). A Mage with Arcane 5 should be have less than a 10% chance of being randomly attacked by supernatural creatures during their life. The average human should probably survive one attack every quarter century, though the nature of the attacks may be financial (Mages), mental (Changelings), physical (Kindred or KJ), or spiritual (Demons, Spirits, Wraiths, etc). Attacks by Fera should be very rare, as Fera do not normally need the money, do not normally need to feed on humans, and do not normally need to possess humans.

                                                    Your character suffered from an accidental territorial intrusion. You probably accidentally stumbled upon their Caern and, after they warned you away, you pursued one of their members. While the final taunting was a bit much, they were actually merciful and restrained for Garou the first time, as they would normally kill uninvited Mages who stumble upon their Caerns.
