    Quintessence Quarry: extracting from matter, coincidentally.

    10-14-2020, 12:03 AM
    (Question at the end.)

    I have a storyteller-played support character for a chronicle with two other players. He is the workshop guy, a Son of Ether with Prime 3 and Matter 3 who creates body armor, spy gadgets and other necessities of an adventurous Tradition Cabal. We are playing without the "affinity sphere must always be highest" limitation. The point of taking the Prime Sphere is that I don't want my players to have to worry about not having this meta-Sphere or having to face a metaphysical learning curve in their first time playing. They can take the Spheres that actually do stuff and when I get Prime 4, we can obtain quintessence from matter. So, seeing as the Etherites have the whole Hollow Earth mythology, I thought I would be a mechanical engineer, able to create mining robots. Assuming we can buy some land and get a mining permit, I have a repeatable cycle of Magickal effects that I believe would be coincidental.

    1. Bring a caterpillar and ore truck. Build a workshop for assembling mining robots. Buy any components for a mining robot that have electrical charge or chemical instability, since I don't have Forces. The workshop hides what is happening inside and presumably makes the next step coincidental.
    2. Instantiate a mining robot using Prime 2, Matter 3. Bring the robot out into plain view, supporting the next coincidence.
    3. Extract a section of rock for quintessence using Matter 3, Prime 4, to be shared with the Cabal (the non-monetary payoff).
    4. Instantiate ore in the truck's bin of the same composition as the extracted rock, which I scanned with Matter 1. Drive to the ore processing plant and sell it (the financial payoff that also lets me hold onto the quarry).
    5. Buy more Forces-necessary parts for the next mining robot, head back and instantiate another mining robot. Extract a bigger hole. Rinse and repeat.

    As a storyteller, would this work in your chronicle?

    Thank you for passing time with me in conversation. My Hacks.
