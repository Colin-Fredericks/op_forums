                            I think it's important to remember that the technological progress and inventions were not gifts to the Masses to uplift all of humanity. They were products, sold to middle- and upper-class white people. Increased farm output has enriched some billionaires, given cheap (but not great) food and clothes to middle-class people, but deprived millions of poor people of access to produce, and undermined subsistence farmers in poor countries. Then the farming methods cause preventable environmental and public health problems for the colonized peoples.

                            With the Native Americans, Australian Aborigines, Pacific Island Natives, and other peoples suffering genocide via biological weapons like smallpox ... the whites in charge didn't offer to cure those people of those epidemics.

                            The inventions aren't an exception to imperialism's evils. They are an example of imperialism. The harms of stolen land, resource extraction, slave labor, and pollution are dumped on the colonized "primitive" peoples. That exploitation gave white elites the raw materials and fuel to build their inventions and infrastructure. It's true of electricity, water sanitation, railroads, medical advances ... It's not as obvious as the link between Southern cotton output and slavery, but it's often a similar link. The industrial goods produced by these actions are sold to well-off whites (and nowadays, other well-off groups around the world.) When those goods break, they are dumped in landfills and seas where black and brown people live - even some waste that could have been recycled.

                            These wonderful inventions have the potential to improve the quality of life for all humanity, and could be made sustainably too (although global overpopulation is becoming an obstacle to that). But in practice that isn't and wasn't how they have been produced or distributed. If you want to play a heroic Technocrat in any era, I think a good avenue is someone who wants these benefits available equally to all humanity, holding that up as the ideal the Union should strive for. True Utopians, in other words.

                                    The progressions aren't all linear.

                                    The Natural Philosophers Guild exists within the Electrodyne Engineers, and is basically the part that will eventually leave the Union to become the Sons of Ether.

                                    The Analytical Reckoners and the Electrodyne Engineers are each split between the League of Constructors and the Grand Faculty.

                                    When the Gabrielites were purged, those who were able to adapt their worldview to the Union's new insistence on atheism became the Lightkeepers; the rest more or less ended up joining the Templars. The Lightkeepers and the Skeleton Keys make up the bulk of the Ivory Tower.

                                    The International Brotherhood of Mechanicians has been retconned to just the Brotherhood of Mechanicians, and are primarily found in the League of Constructors.

                                    The Æsculapians make up the other part of the Grand Faculty.

                                    Note that for the most part, it's not the original Conventions that become the new Conventions after the 1897 reorganization; it's the voting Blocs. The Exploratory Society (Void Seekers and Celestial Masters) becomes the Void Engineers; the League of Constructors becomes Iteration X; the Grand Faculty becomes the Progenitors; the Ivory Tower becomes the New World Order. The Syndicate exists in the Victorian Age as a voting Blocs, combining the Invisible Exchequers and the Golden Guild. Only the Analytical Reckoners continue on as a separate Convention (and perhaps the Electrodyne Engineers; but they're gone within five years of the reorganization), renaming themselves the Virtual Adepts.

                                    The Craftmasons were destroyed well before the Victorian Age began, by a few centuries. If they survived in any form, that's still a mystery.

                                    The Ksirafai disappeared and infiltrated the Traditions as House Janissary right after destroying the Gabrielites. It wouldn't surprise me if some of them stuck around in the Technocracy among the Skeleton Keys; the M.O. is similar.

                                            The Blocs aren't official organizations, per se; they're more like political parties, with individuals joining. When I say that the Brotherhood of Mechanicians is in the League of Constructors, what I mean is that most of the members of the Brotherhood vote with the League. The Analytical Reckoners and the Electrodyne Engineers have members who tend to split more or less evenly between two voting Blocs. After the Grand Housecleaning in 1897, most of the original Conventions get dissolved, and the voting Blocs become the new Conventions; the Analytical Reckoners and the Electrodyne Engineers are the exceptions to this. I suspect that during the Great Housecleaning, there were vocal factions within these two Conventions who identified more with their original Conventions than with their voting Blocs, and insisted on keeping their original Conventions in place (albeit with a name change, in the case of the Analytical Reckoners).

                                            The voting Blocs come into existence in 1861 as part of the Victorian Reformation, when the Order of Reason becomes the Technocratic Union.

                                            And you're right about the Ksirafai; they disappeared right after destroying the Craftmasons, not the Cabal of Pure Thought.

                                                            The Voltarian Order was founded in 1806; the Difference Engineers were founded in 1823. They were not part of the Order of Reason, but were friendly with it and joined up with the Technocracy when it was founded in 1861. As far as I know, there was no single antecedent to the Difference Engineers: Babbage's Difference Engine inspired mathematicians and engineers to form a new faction. When the Technocracy was founded, the Voltarian Order joined forces with the Society of Ether to form the Electrodyne Engineers. The Society of Ether's history stretches back much further.

                                                            And yes, this is a retcon. I have no idea when the Natural Philosopher's Guild started calling itself the Society of Ether; apparently, it was closer to 1861 than to 1905. Given that Maxwell published his famous equations in the 1950s and 1960s, and that those were the origin of the beliefs that light is an electromagnetic wave and that the Ether is the medium through which it propagates, I could see them taking on the name somewhere around then.

                                                            The origins of the Skeleton Keys are unknown, other than that they were a technomantic faction allied with, but not part of, the Union — sorry of like the case of the Difference Engineers and Voltaic Order with respect to the Order of Reason. They worked for Queen Victoria originally, and ended up joining the Union at her insistence.

                                                            The High Guild was one of the original Conventions of the Order of Reason from way back in the early Renaissance, and became the Golden Guild in 1861. See Sorcerers Crusade to get the date of their founding. The Invisible Exchequers likewise trace their roots back to the High Guild; essentially, the High Guild split into two Conventions in 1861, which then remerged in 1897.
