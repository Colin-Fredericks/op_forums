    ASSASSINS CREED: SYNDICATE is an interesting possible take on Victorian Age Mage. You have the fact the British Empire is already in full swing and only growing each point. However, that is coming at considerable cost and there are people who support it not for racist reasons and want to use the "benefits" of technology to help the public (starting with the lower classes of the very white world but believing they're helping other people against feudal tyrannies in the brown and black worlds). You know, whether they want to be or not.

    There's plenty of people who could believe that the Union will ULTIMATELY be a force of good but those people are going to be outnumbered by those who are just....the worst.

    The Syndicate DLC THE LAST MAHARAJA deals with the tragic life of real-life Ranjit Singh who attempted many times to work within the system to restore himself as monarch of India as well as benefit the people of his home as a whole. He wanted to be part of it and make India part of the "Modern" world. And couldn't....because the British Empire was racist as fuck. That DLC pretty much dealt with what could be an interesting case for a Technocrat who ends up defecting from the Union or gets brainwashed into compliance.

            There's a reason I phrase things in an earlier post as one of the sources of cognitive dissonance being the contrast between the Victorian Technocracy being portrayed as evil as the Nephandi and them being portrayed as a playable faction: playing the Nephandi has been explicitly discouraged all throughout the Mage line.
