    Mage Ascension - Legacies

    05-29-2018, 12:35 PM
    Oh Hi!
    This is my first entry ever, its about an idea I had about the very essence of the Traditions on Ascension. My biggest issue is that to me, Traditions are quite weird, it talks about a traditional way o making magic, therefore developing groups dedicated to certain kinds of thought, philosophy and so on. HOWEVER, if you look closer they dont really make something unique at all, the nature of the avatar, true magick and paradigm makes so that each individual is a unique, which is good. The problem is that although in the fluff the Traditions are something important somehow at a "magick level" in reality they are not much more than politics, bureaucrats at most. It really annoys me that a group like Order of Hermes, having more than THOUSAND years of existence have not produced anything actually magical as a tradition other than: A) a Hogwarts in Mars, B) Reuniting the conception of magick in a concise system, C) Having grand respect for, who-knows-why, fairies.

    What is the MAGICK that Hermetics did that makes them unique? The Brotherhood of Akasha developed Do, which is not inherently magical, but the very pinnacle of their discipline, which is quite related to both their paradigm and magick without making them dependent of it. The Virtual Adepts may have not really created the Virtual Web but no one can argue their absurd close relations to such, as much as Totems to Khavadi are a thing specifically for them, not everyone else, or at least not that easy.

    For that, bear with me, for I tried something akin to the Legacies in Mage Awakeing. My people, I understand much of us quite dislike and hopes that somone starts an Index Prohibitorum and makes public pyres using Chronicles of Darkness, but that ideia of having groups with practices that are unique to each one is amazing, and they have like 140+ groups like that.

    Well, Legacies have a system, mechanics and fluff that are not really adequate to Ascension, but they can inspire something like them. So I came with a few Ideas and wrote those things below, I based it on the Mind's Eye Theatre book, although I never intended it or knew the existence of such books or their mechanics of Traditions boons to certain habilities hahahahah.

    So, here they are, coment what you think, hate as you want and hope my painful death, or may come up with your own ideias:


    People come here to present an idea and improvements to it with the help of the community.

    I have always wondered which magician had a great problem with the question of Traditions, because in fact these magician groups are a tradition only in the name and in the share of their used foci. That is, to be part if a Tradition is only made given to the stereotype and the sphere of specialization and not for other reasons what escapes of the plot of this group of mages.

    To try to solve this, I tried to take a look at other systems and I started to develop a house rule where each group of magicians would have a Legacy of Tradition, a real and tangible advantage to be and to continue in the group. Secrets.mystic developed by and for that organization.

    These are what I have mined on the books and remodeled so far:

    "Ivory Tower' Mandate"
    - Any of the agents of the Technocratic Union can enjoy the hegemony of the Scientific Consensus and be free of the possibility of paradox in using any technologies that are not alien to the current possibilities and even those speculated to exist (at storytellers discretion). The use of hypertechnology that is even short of conspiracy theories are not immune to paradox such as atomic disintegrators, dimensional or teleportation portals, indefinite extension of life (not aging, immortality, etc.).

    "Oh, I can feel it!"
    Members of the Cult of Ecstasy have a completely unique and often paradoxical ethic and vision of the universe even among other magi. Their ability to sense reality and modify their understanding of Tellurian allow them to be with one of the active mystical perceptions continuously. Systemwise a mage can choose a sphere she possesses and for the rest of hes life have the effects of said perception active. Ex. Spirit Sphere will make the cultist always be able to see a certain interposition between the world of spirits and material; someone from the Entropy sphere will continually feel the flow of causality between the infinite relationships that are near.
    Obs: To cancel this effect, the Magician must necessarily go through a ritual or routine to limit the advanced perception of some sphere, and if he does not, the madness will always be at his door because goddamit if spirits can be clingy.

    The One Song
    The Celestial Choir prays on the ideal that all is but a fragment of the True Self, the One, the ultimate god, for it is the very origin of divinity and everything else. This belief is so strong that the members of said tradition thinks they should work together so that His servants be in unity even amidst differences. This Idea coalesces in such a way that when one or more choristers works together they may attain greater proximity to the One, as they too focus their will but in one thing. Systemwise when at least two choristers do the same magick together they can cast with more power than alone, in such a way as if there was another ONE (pun inteded). The choristers must try to cast the same routine in unison, what does not mean mirroring each other, the result will be casting the said magick with one more dice and it wont add more even if you call the entire Tradition to help, however the price for such is quite obvious too. When the One Song is practiced a figure, as if a third person may become visible even to Sleepers, and this said figure may look almost transparent as if not material at all, resembling aspect of the routine used and the cultural references of the users. The choristers may think of this visage as a tiny spark of the One's manifestation, hermetics however thinks that its no more than resonance showing itself and that choristers thinks too much...


    "Blessing of The Great Mother"
    -The connection of the Verbena with the Great Mother goes beyond the use of natural materials and inputs. A Verbena in performing a ceremony during a natural event of importance and necessarily close to it will not be capable of generating critical failure in the midst of this single ritual.
    Ex. A ritual during a Blood Moon, an eclipse, a volcanic eruption, a storm, a flood of great proportions.
    Obs .: The ritual must have some relation with the natural event and mainly its resonance, as well as it can not be caused by the Verbena itself or by magic of any type.

    "Restructuring the Static Paradigm"
    "A member of the Ether Society does not do magick, but performs the purest Science. An Etherite can be immunized from the shock of paradox created when a Sleeper sees one of his experiments if he can convict it in some way that that realized can be explained scientifically.
    Obs: Extremely vulgar procedures can not be explained.

    This next one was something for the Order of Hermes, I wanted a thing that evokes that fact that they are well, the leaders of the Council and therefore should have something powerfull as well, since they are the guys who studied magick so hard that they formulated the Sphere System and the Purple Paradigm and many other shit that even the Union still utilizes. Well I didnt like this one I made, too long and too close to Charms, and a copy + paste of Vancian Magic.

    "Protocol of Hermeticum Formulae"

    - A hermetic magi has prolonged and endless studies of the magical theories of Porthos and Bonisagus allowing a unique perception of the mystical processes. This strenuous study of the Order of Hermes allowed the development of a limited but extremely functional magickal methodology. Once per session the character can use a pre-established routine without the need for a scroll at the cost of an effect completely limited by the amount of successes generated previously in the preparation of this mechanism. Due to its formation in the Forces Sphere it is very common that the magi of the Order have preference for some effect of attack in this area, although it is not a limiting in itself.

    Note I: the prepared effect must be a routine, that is, if the Magician is not familiar enough or has never done so before, a Protocol will not be formed.

    Obs II: a Protocol does not roll Arete at the moment of use, therefore it will be used only the results of the data obtained at the time of its creation.

    Obs III: a protocol is a quasi-static routine, so its use is by means of predetermined and fixed actions, evading or not obeying the commands that the magi established will generate a paradoxical effect applied to the Effect. Ex. A Protocol with the purpose of creating rain will be done on a long parchment that will be immersed in water while the hermetic recites the invocation of Archangel Uriel and bla bla bla, in case he fails it will rain blood or other things other than the intended.

    Obs IV: It is worth noting that although it is quite functional and pragmatic, the use of the Protocols is a call to the hubris of indiscriminate use and may generate dependent. The risk of producing an undesired result is spectacular even compared to a common effect in front of witnesses.

    Obs V: a Protocol will generate paradox in the same way as a routine if it is vulgar, the great advantage in it is that the mage wont have to spend time to activate the intended Effect.

    Obs VI: a Protocol can take advantage of physical objects such as containers, grimoires, books, scrolls, etc., but a magi who is not the Protocol creator can never use it because only the one that crafted it may. Protocols are not magical items, they are not transferable and although they are used in certain cases of quintessence expenditure they do so because the original routine would require or the absence of some of the materials that may be interchangeable by their use.

    Obs VII: The Protocol will be produced by means of a ritual in which the magician will use the routine in question and leave it ready for a later use and it is activated only by means of a formulaic magicka, that can only be activated by means of a rigid procedure. A wizard in case he fails or has weak results can not perform a new ritual to replace the current one, for a new Protocol it will be necessary to use the first one, even if it was a critical failure.

    Note VIII: A Protocol counts as an active effect, so it adds +1 to the difficulty modifiers for any subsequent Arete rolls.

    Obs IX: Yes they are overpower, it is not for nothing that the Order of Hermes is the Tradition that presides over the Council.

    Et cetera.

                                                      That's definitely the view of Ascension that the Traditions promote, yes. But as of M20, raising your Arete no longer means growing beyond your paradigm; it's just that among the Traditions, one's Paradigm tends to include what the Book of Secrets calls “We Are All Gods in Disguise”, an ecumenical view of magick that, as it strengthens, tends to encourage the mage to look at all magick as being fundamentally the same. Outside of the Traditions, though, this view isn't nearly as common. Taking an extreme example, Technocrats generally don't subscribe to the WAAGID Paradigm. But it's not just the Technocracy; the various Crafts tend not to view magick in this way (and the exceptions, like the Batini and the Hollow Ones, used to be associated with the Traditions).

                                                      If I were to try to import Attainments into Ascension, I'd probably use Mythic Threads and/or Earthly Foundations to justify them. Mythic Threads are basically “minority views” within the Consensus that are strong enough to support magick even when the majority view doesn't; and Earthly Foundations are aspects of Reality that are true regardless of what that Consensus says: M20 uses the latter to explain why certain Effects are always Vulgar even when there's widespread acceptance of them in the Consensus; but it can also be used to explain supernatural phenomena that are stubbornly resistant or even immune to influence by the Consensus. Mortal Sorcery is arguably a manifestation of Mythic Threads and/or Earthly Foundations; Attainments would be similar, but for the Awakened.

                                                          Both games, while still highly divergent, put Paradox as a fundamental result of mages channeling too much power for reality to accept their actions without response.

                                                          In Awakening you're uncovering deeper truths about the nature of reality and so able to find loopholes in the Exarchs version of reality you can exploit (i.e. Attainments); you gain power by cleaving more tightly to certain traditions.
                                                          This is, again, a very 1e take on Awakening. 2e changes these assumptions considerably, though the second was never really true in either edition (there are bonuses to using traditions, with drawbacks for doing so, which isn't the same as it being a route to more power).

                                                          In Ascension you're evolving past your previous limits and advancement in magic comes from learning that the traditions you first learned are the things you must grow beyond... you gain more power by abandoning your traditions in favor of a more holistic perspective.
                                                          But, M20 especially, challenges that interpretation quite significantly. M20 makes it clear that abandoning one's Instruments is not the same as transcending your traditional teachings. Ascension's few detailed Archmagi are not presented as having moved all in the same direction. If anything, they've all doubled down on their traditions heavily in their own personal ways. Porthos never stopped being a Hermetic, he never shed all those trappings, he just doesn't need them when push comes to shove (also, mechanically, you're still incentivized to use them anyway).
