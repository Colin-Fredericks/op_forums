                            Originally posted by Pleiades View Post

                            what, making Atlas units? you just said making Atlas units was a hypercharged version of the normal conditioning (like in dominate conditioning).

                            Okay, I may be the problem here due to unclearness.

                            Technocratic Conditioning works like this:

                            Conditioning goes from a scale of 1 to 10, where each rank adds a new nasty thing that your superiors can make you do. It looks kinda like this:

                            1: Will forego food and sleep if necessary to carry out the mission. Reality Deviants see a Technocratic version of their Ascension.
                            2: Will submit to most minor Technocratic Procedures without resistance.
                            3: Can be ordered to see an individual as an ally or enemy, as ordered by Control. A Reality Deviant will refuse to use minor Traditionalist Procedures if they require a non-Technocratic Focus.
                            4: Is installed with a code word that causes the character to see another person as "unmutual", whom he will avoid and ostracize at any point until reversed.
                            5: Is installed with a code word that causes the character to become docile and pacifistic. A Reality Deviant will use a Technocratic Focus for one of his Spheres.
                            6: Is installed with a code word that causes the character to enact an elaborate scheme against a chosen individual.
                            7: Is implanted with the idea that a certain group of individuals is an ally that must be trusted. A Reality Deviant has all of his Foci replaced with Technocratic Foci.
                            8: The subject can now be ordered to completely forget a specific individual.
                            9: The subject will betray any individual no matter how loved, if ordered to by Control.
                            10: The subject will treat a specific person, ideal or organization with almost religious devotion. Blah blah blah, resistanc is futile blah blah big brother.

                            Meanwhile, what the Technocracy uses to make ATLAS units is called Processing, and requires the subject to be Conditioned.

                            Processing (••••• Mind, •• Prime)
                            With Social Conditioning and Programming (see Guide to the Technocracy), the Technocracy manipulates proles and agents to be completely loyal. Indoctrination
                            takes a Reality Deviant and bends him to the Union’s will. The Procedure itself is not for the faint of heart. It used to require loud music, straps, holographic projectors, acupuncture needles, bad food, and a dentist’s chair — not necessarily in that order — and all over the course of one hellish week.

                            Today, the NWO finds it more efficient to disconnect a target’s senses from his nervous system (usually through forcible installation of a DNI) before employing many, many sessions of interrogation and personality correction. No small number of agents have walked out during field training because of Processing. Members of the Feed have gone so far as to lobby for its removal from standard protocol. The Ivory Tower’s counterargument has been “victory by any means necessary,” and the Operatives use it extensively for their Enlightened Shock Corps experiment. What happens when you strap a mage down and make him wish for death?

                            With this Procedure, eventually, you remove his identity. He’s left ready to be rebuilt in the Technocracy’s image. But this rebuilding is not painted over a blank slate; it’s painted over countless scars, resulting in an imperfect creation. This adds to the tragedy, as such newly indoctrinated agents are treated as disposable assets.

                            Some rise to greatness, but this throwaway treatment, along with the emotional trauma of the Procedure, lead to daunting suicide rates. These rates are even higher if one considers the number of new agents who die in a hail of fire from their former allies, ending them out of mercy.

                            This is cruelly referred to as “suicide by Reality Deviant.” The Procedure is never truly complete. The indoctrinated agent becomes a Technocrat, for all intents and purposes. However, in a time of massive stress, the player can spend a point of Willpower for his character to briefly snap back to his original identity for one turn.But because of the reprogramming, he’s unable to act against the Union. This often results in self-destructive behavior. Due of this, such assets are routinely re-Processed to ensure continued service
                            _

                            I hope this helped.
