        I do like the idea of Prime as Truth, since multiple competing truths doesn't bother me for MtAsc.

        The stage magician example is a tricky one, though. What does it mean to inject truth in In stage magic? It shouldn't just be directing attention to the actual motions, that's more of a Mind thing. It should probably manifest as more of a awareness of the existence of the false narrative and an unwillingness to go along with it. The audience doesn't actually see the trick any better, they just find it less convincing. If that is the case, though, then I would suggest not using stage magic as the example, because in stage magic the truth is often already a part of the act. The audience often knows they are being fooled and are there for the enjoyment.

        Perhaps using the convoluted cons of shows like Hustle and Leverage would be a better example. Injecting truth into the con might make it more real and more plausible. Pulling truth out of it or injecting it into the world makes it less convincing. What is reinforced or eroded in this case is the narrative. Essentially the spell creates a temporary Mythic Thread that either works for or against the endeavor.

        This does seem like a paradigmatic interpretation of Prime rather than the Sphere itself, though, so perhaps there's a better way of expressing the idea.

                            Great post and interesting responses! To touch back on NemoGG's initial question...

                            I would not like to see Prime deleted from the game. It figures into an awful lot of underlying metaphysics that would get complicated to explain without it. However, if I am reading the post correctly, it is not the concept of Prime that is the problem, but rather the mechanics associated with it, i.e. being a point sink to do the things you really want to with Pattern magic with no seemingly inherent value on its own.

                            While I could argue for all the really cool uses for Prime, instead I will suggest linking Prime to Arete and just making a house rule that for every point of Arete, the characters get a point of Prime. This fulfills the Pattern magic itch - you cannot fireball without Forces 2 or 3 anyway and it streamlines the play to focus on what your groups think is fun, which is the overall point of the game, without breaking or even altering the fundamental mechanics of the game.

                            This can be explained that since Quintessence is the underlying source of all reality, understanding in Prime is a metaphysical aspect of enlightenment attained with Arete, not a discipline to study by rote. In this case, a mage does not learn Prime, they grow to understand it as they grow to understand magic as a whole and grow in Arete.

                            This of course brings up the issue of what to do with all those points, if the mage gets this huge freebie? My answer to that is push Skills. If Arete is personal growth and understanding of Prime is a part of that deep understanding, it separates it from the other spheres. The logic follows that these other spheres are learned and need to be studied to be used.

                            This gives a mechanical use for rotes and for paradigm because Sphere effects learned and rely on a suite of knowledge skills to use properly. If a Hermetic mage wants to throw a fireball, it is no longer just Forces 2 or 3 and Prime. Now it requires an answer to this question: "does the mage know how to create a fireball? Yeah? How do they do it?" Fueling fire with Prime is all good, but where does that Prime come from and how does the mage harness it? A Hermetic could evoke the name of Marduk, or a primal celestial of flame, or Sol Invictus. However, in the Hermetic paradigm, all things are connected and sympathetic to each other. Is today a day that Sol Invictus is listening? What language is needed? What are the forms that induce Marduk to respond with fire instead of ignoring the mage? Does the mage know the True name of the fire spirit to reliably evoke its power? All of this needs to be researched ahead of time, which is why Hermetic mages have extensive libraries and speak several languages. It is rude to evoke a god with the wrong language on the wrong day and terrible to ignore the rituals agreed upon in a pact made in the days of Enoch that only a Hermetic, with her hours and years of study has mastered. Besides, the planets are not in the correct alignment for an evocation anyway so better to use the Latin rote for fire using transfiguration of earthly energies instead. Any first year acolyte would know this. (rolls eyes sardonically)

                            This would work for all Traditions. A Dreamspeaker might need to bribe a fire spirit with alcohol, which would ignite when spit at a target. Knowing which spirits like liquor and which do not takes research. A Celestial might only create fire to show the power of the Divine, perhaps to banish an unpure entity that is not part of the Design. Holy prayers can make unclean vampires ignite to banish them from the realms of men, but which prayer? Research. How much study is involved in making fire from qi (ki) for an Akashic Brother? Channeling inner energy in this manner is not done lightly or without extensive practice and understanding of several underlying principles.

                            This list goes on. The point is that the game could be geared to encourage study and to encourage use of rotes and paradigm for functional reliability as opposed to just for flavor.

                            Anyway, that is my suggestion for this. Whether you like my secondary idea or not, I recommend tacking Prime to Arete as a freebie and using the points for something else that encourages the game you and your players want to play.

                            Hope that helps.
