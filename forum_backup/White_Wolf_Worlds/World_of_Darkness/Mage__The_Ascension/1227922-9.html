    Originally posted by Onkwe View Post

    Yeah I wouldn't want the Technocracy to go back to being predominantly about just keeping power for power sakes. Certainly such Technomancers exist, but I do prefer the more idealistically driven Technomancers that most of us know and love. I would just like a combo of both that lost alieness with this idealism, which can be accomplished by making the Union driven by ideological fanatism.

    I also have no issue with Technomancers more interested in exploring enlightened science. It's just like I said, that I never felt like I had this Evul Technocracy shoved down my throat by the writers, having come to the game in its later years. It's been almost 20 years now since Revised, where they really emphasized a more human Technocracy. However, aside from finding aspects of the older view of the Union cool, the Ascension War should be/have been more than a conflict over if Sleepers reheat their leftovers using a microwave or summoning up a fire elemental to do so.
    A note about the term “more human” - I don't think “more human” necessarily means more friendly or cooperative. There are many, many examples of humans acting with general self-interest or pursuit of specific ( or vague ) goal or ideal. Humans can also act in a very hostile, unfriendly, or selfish way. A group being "more human" does not neccesiraly mean that the group would be more humane, to put it simply.

    What other issues and areas of interest you think the Ascension War could be in regard to ? I think the question how to treat mundane humans should definitely be described in more detail. Traditions would like to give mundane humans some amount of respect and even what could be called trust, initiating them into trying to reach greatness in different ways. The Technocracy thinks that mundane humans should be guided without them becoming aware of the various possibilities until “it is time” or “mundane humans are ready” ; and the full extent of many facts and opportunities is likely never to be revealed to them. The Nephandi think that the mundane humans should be exploited, except the ones who make themselves worthy or useful in some way.

    The factions also differ considerably in regard to what they think about Mages of other perspectives and factions, and their approaches and goals in regard to them. These distinction should be described in at least a few pages in one of future M:tA books.

        That's a rather one-sided description of the Traditions' and Technocracy's views toward Sleepers. It's also true that many in the Traditions don't give Sleepers any respect or trust; they see them at best as a “breeding ground”, as it were, for more Awakened, and are more interested in pushing the Consensus in a direction that promotes more Awakenings. Conversely, the Technocracy wants to make the world safe for the Masses, and are trying to make their lives less perilous and more comfortable. And the Nephandi just want to put them out of their misery.

        Frankly, I don't think there's any faction in the War that, as a whole, respects and trusts Sleepers. The closest you get, ironically, is the Syndicate: their Market-driven view is aimed at giving the Masses exactly what they ask for. And even they aren't willing to give the Masses things that they're not ready for (i.e., stuff that's still Vulgar).

                    I'm not up for deleting Spheres; but I wouldn't mind seeing Entropy reimagined and even renamed — though I've never been able to come up with a good name for what I'd like to do with it. Basically, my preference would be to expand the Sphere to encompass not just Entropy, but also Dynamism and Stasis in the abstract. Most factions that deal with this Sphere actually don't use it for its Entropic properties, but rather for its Dynamic effects (manipulating chance) or its Static aspects (imposing destinies); of the Traditions, only the Euthanatoi/Chakravanti consider all three parts of the Sphere. And for that, they're treated as if they were somehow fixated on the one spoke of the Wheel that everyone else ignores.

                    That's what I'd do with Entropy: it would be the Sphere of fortune, destiny, and doom, something that has been greatly misunderstood and maligned in-universe in the past, and is still struggling under those misrepresentations even today.

                    My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness
