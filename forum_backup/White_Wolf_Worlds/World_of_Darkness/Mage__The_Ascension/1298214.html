    Making Stereotypes *of* the Traditions

    03-20-2019, 07:16 PM
    So M20 has each Traditions' stereotypes of the Sects, but I thought it would be cool to write up the reverse; so under the Hermetic view of the other Traditions, a stereotyped response from the Traditions essentially. The original text is from M20, the alternating lines are my own.

    Order of Hermes.

    Fellow Traditions: “Despite venerable practices and occasional insights, our allies lack the discipline to achieve lasting power or control. Even so, they have stood with us for centuries, so they remain worthy companions.”

    Onacona "White Owl" of the Dreamspeakers: “They’re completely full of themselves, and to some of them we’re brainless savages who shouldn’t be allowed to command power. With that said, they’re at the core of the Traditions and when they manage to pull their heads out of their own asses they can be useful allies.”

    The Technocracy: “In the end, only one of us will survive.”

    Agent [Redacted] of the New World Order: “A holdover from a bygone era. The ideals of reason evolved out of that old House; emphasis on ‘out of’. They’re lagging a few hundred years behind the real world, and they might need our help realizing that.”

    The Disparates: “Talented amateurs, lost to their own separation.”

    Sarah, Orphan: “Did someone stick a white beard and some glitter on a Technocrat and hope we wouldn’t notice?”

      The Disparates: “Lost children and broken relations… yet there’s more to them than there might seem.”

              Celestial Chorus

              Fellow Traditions: “My Father’s home hath many chambers.” Though I might not understand them all… might even, God forgive me, hate a few… I choose to remember that we all receive Divinely guided grace.

              The Technocracy: Traitors to Grace who imprison the world with mechanical cacophony.

              Progenitor Report: “Faith healing? Religious mania? We’re looking at an enormous deviant cult that seems devoted to undermining the miracle of modern medicine.”

              The Disparates: Lost souls, one and all.

              Templar Devotee: “I have seen HELL in the works of these heretics. There is one body, one Christ, one redemption. Their "tolerant" sacrilege borders on the satanic, and they will burn for their crimes.”

                      Cult of Ecstasy:

                      Fellow Traditions: A bunch of tight-assed academics who’re often scared to push beyond the obvious limits of their disciplines and embrace everything they could be on the other side of those expectations.

                      Anonymous Virtual Adept: “A lot of the hate they get comes from losers not getting invited to all the cool parties. Unlike a lot of my peers I wash and shave, so you’ll hear no complaints from me.”

                      The Technocracy: Abominations of the human condition, and the source of almost everything wrong today.

                      Syndicate Manager: “Surprisingly good contractors... Just don’t draw too much attention to them. When the time comes to cannibalize what’s left of our competitors, I’ll throw a few of these animals a liferaft. Sex sells, after all.”

                      The Disparates: I don’t blame them for wanting their freedom. Too bad it’s cost them almost everything else…

                      Sarah, Orphan: “I met a beautiful dancer one night and... well I spent a few weeks following them around like an obedient little puppy. One night things got unusually wild, and it was like opening my eyes, and my body, for the first time. I’ll never forget them... but I couldn’t keep up with that lifestyle.”
