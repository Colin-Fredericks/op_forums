                        To get back on topic, I think the Awakening has the potential to both help and hurt one's ability to find other people attractive.

                        On the one hand, enhanced perceptions allow a person to see qualities in someone that would otherwise be missed. Life to see a person's health, vitality, and good genes. Mind to read emotional state, to find those who are more receptive to romantic advances. Spirit or Mind to read auras, or pick out auspicious Resonance. Entropy to see how significant a person is to one's destiny.

                        On the other hand, enhanced perceptions can pick out reasons why a person might be unsuitable, or put a damper on their attractiveness. Life to notice hidden physical ailments, genetic defects, or the presence of certain toxins (like from tobacco, or more stomach-churning chemicals). Entropy to pick up on dishonesty or the threads of poor fate. Mind to spot mental or emotional complications, or that reveal levels to which the subject's actions are calculated or artificial. The latter, while not an indictment of the subject, can be distracting to the Mage, who may be too aware of how genuine the subject's words or actions are. Much of modern dating is built around polite acting, levels of obfuscation that a Mage pierces through quite readily, whether they'd like to or not. A fact which can remove much of the enchantment of romance, and cause the Mage to become more cynical.

                            Something I also think could be relevant to consider regarding this topic is the reciprocal nature that the Awakened share with Reality, as in, getting out what they put in.

                            Even if you don’t use the Resonance rules, I think that one should also consider paradigm when thinking about this. While the progression of an Awakened’s Arete/Genius will determine how potent their impact is, anyone Awake will have an impact on their local Reality.

                            So you have to begin considering Paradigm, Resonance, and Essence then I think. For instance:

                            A Progenitor workaholic might have virtually zero interest in sex or romance, understanding that attraction is just a chemical reaction to incentivize reproduction and the survival of the species. Understanding that they have no obligation to heed the flukes of hormonal biology and that humanity is overpopulated as it is, the Progenitor avoids romance and as their Genius develops, romance avoids them. The potential partners they meet becoming increasingly infrequent and focused on short-term pleasure gained and so the Progenitor remains involved in socializing only to indulge the occasional desire for animal joy. Overall though, they aren’t interested in what’s outside their studies.

                            An Ecstatic celebrates love, romance, and sex not only as sources of pleasure but as avenues to broaden ones perspective. In a lover’s embrace the Ecstatic enters into a communion of two bodies as one and through such acts the Ecstatic expands their perceptions outside the limits of their own body and to the communion of all life. They put out waves of compassion and so those needing to be understood find their way to the Ecstatic through Reality’s slight manipulation as their Arete grows. More and more the Ecstatic develops their view of love as a timeless weaving of all life and Reality gives them more opportunities to expand that perspective and draw Sleepers and Sleepwalkers into that network of mutual understanding and euphoric compassion.

                            These Awakened are both getting out what they put in, no more and no less, be both are using it to further develop their understanding of the Reality that they shape around them as they heighten their Arete or Genius.

                                            I'm not sure how much I'd trust the statistics from W20; the average Willpower of a regular human, according to those numbers, is 3.965, or 4. That seems awfully high to me, and doesn't match the description of Willpower 2 being typical. (For those of you familiar with statistics terminology, 2 is the Mode of that distribution, but not the Mean.)

                                            But even if we take that distribution as being accurate for regular folks, mages aren't regular folks; conceptually, they're fundamentally willful people: it's generally taken as a given that the essence of Magick is Enlightened Will; and if you lack the Will, your Magick is hindered. (M20 even has a rule for this: your Arete cannot exceed your Willpower.)

                                            In an attempt to compare apples to apples, look at the starting Willpower of a mage vs. the starting Willpower presented in Gods and Monsters. Mages start with Willpower 5; Companions start with Willpower 3.

                                            This further supports the notion that the Awakened have more Willpower than Sleepers. So I'd say that Aleph's point stands.
