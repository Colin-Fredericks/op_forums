    About the forces 5


    Hi I would like to talk about anexo Idea I have been brewing for some time and sharing it will let me see if it is mechanicaly possible.

    The forces sphere has a very weird problem that others dont follow, as the "damages" sphere it becomes quite paradoxical as you level it up to the point many people argues that it is nonsensical to so since mechanicaly you have much harder difficulties, paradox and even strict control of the end effect.

    Lets see here:

    Forces 3 - diff. 7

    * if done vulgar an effect will be 4+3(sphere level)

    Forces 4 - diff. 8

    * if done vulgar an effect will be 4+4(sphere level)

    Forces 5 - diff. 9

    * if done vulgar an effect will be 4+5(sphere level)


    Why would someone game wise invest on something that actually becomes absurdly impractical, if not unusable, as it becomes stronger?

    Ps.: Oh and by such logic Forces 6, the archsphere, has base diff. 10. Beautiful.

    Well I have some ideias that can be used:

    Bring Back the Golden Age, make it so that higher leves give stronger modifiers
    I) instead of static +1 damage it scalates after level 3 (so Forces 4 has +2 damage, Forces 5 +3, and so on.)

    II) make it so that each level after 3 makes it so that the successes multiplyer is bigger.

    Therefore, Forces 3 x 2, Forces 4 x 3, Forces 5 x 4, Forces 6 x 5

    So lets get some examples:

    Forces 3 with average 2 successes = (2x2)+1 = 5 damage.

    Forces 4 with average 2 successes = (2x3)+1 = 7 damage.

    Forces 5 with average 2 successes = (2x4)+1 = 9 damage.

    Forces 6 with average 2 successes = (2x5)+1 = 11 damage


    But that is crude math, what matters is the game.

    I find it quite annoying that Forces 4+ becomes so impractical since effects 4+ are always vulgar, and level 5 are obviously with witness.
    I think that any sphere level should be usable at any moment instead of ritual space only. So what if in the middle of a battlefield someone wants to creste a nuclear explosion? Wouldnt that be really reasonable for a Master of Forces? And what if the player or NPC just wants to overkill for the sake of it?

                          Originally posted by menos125 View Post
                          Oh and what you think about those high lvl apheres, should they only be used on rituals or freely?
                          Both. It's true that high level effects often require a ritual, but no book says nor implies that high level effects are always going to be casted trough ritual

                          Ritual casting it's for "powerful" effects that have a trully epic scale. Like nuking a city or summoning an Archdemon. But that has little to do with Sphere level. I've never read anywhere that high Sphere level effects should be ritualized. On the contrary, I can name some effects that canonically use lvl 5 Spheres, yet are very easy.

                          You can, canonically, make someone vanish from Reality with Prime 5 and 4 successes - hard, but for an Arete 5/6 Master it's far from impossible to do that in a turn, esp if the Master expends Willpower and lowers the difficulty to the minimum. It's statistically worse than killing someone with prime 3 because of the added difficulty, but it's not canonically intended to be a ritual (or at least the rules don't seem to imply that you need a ritual to do that)

                          If you don't divide successes Time 5 classic "time stop" effect it's just 3 successes (relatively easy for a Master), if I remember the tables well.

                          Sphere level adds more options that should require big rituals, but nowhere it's said that they can't be used to fast cast.

                          The need of ritualization doesn't come from the Sphere Level but from the scale of the spell, which usualy implies ST judgement (as per the table of magical feats). It just happens that lvl 5 Spheres allow to cast far more godlike stuff than lvl 1 Spheres, and thus many "common" high level effects would, indeed, need a ritual. But correlation doesn't imply causation, and Sphere lvl shouldn't imply the need of rituals (neither the fluff nor the RAW point to this)

                          If you want a system where this isn't the case you need to do more than increase the damage. 1e Mage hadn't ritual rules per se, it hadn't a "magical feats table" ranging from 1 to 20 successes. Characters were theoretically capable of releasing any effect within a turn or so because of how the tables were built and intended to be used (altrough you would need Forces 6 to nuke a city under that system)
