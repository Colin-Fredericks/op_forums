I think that I could need some help and inspiration however how to use and fuse the various spheres for interesting results and usage. My character is a Dreamspeaker that deals a lot of therapeutic counseling and support for people in crisis and suffering various forms of trauma (supernaturally based as well as the more mundane).
    Her sphere mastery so far is; Spirit 2, Mind 2 and Prime 2.
    My question is basically just asking for advice and inspiration for how I could use these spheres in a creative manner to help her in her professional role as well as just generally surviving in the world of darkness. I know that all 3 spheres in general serve as more supportive and non-combat based roles, which is okay since she is not much of a fighter (she knows how to use a gun for when self defense becomes necessary) but I still struggle to come up with actual applications and rotes for the spheres.

          Mind 1 and 2 have a lot of applications for boosting a therapists mundane actions, even if not skipping over things or using magic to heal things quickly (that's higher level stuff). Mind 1 lets you temporarily boost your Social and Mental Attributes, perform Aura scans to determine emotional states, and so on. Mind 2, as noted already, can help adjust a patient's current emotional state to help keep treatment sessions on track.

          Prime 1 lets you sense if any of their emotional trauma is a normal response to seeing something that should not have been seen, or if there's a supernatural effect involved. Prime 2 (and possibly Mind as well) would assist in unraveling a supernaturally implanted mental trauma (aka undo a power that inflicts a Derangement on a character).

          Spirit 1, like Prime 1, would help you spot if a spirit is partially to blame because they're connected to the patient somehow. Mage has some funky rules for dealing with ghosts/wraiths specifically if you don't take certain Merits, but you'd at least be able to gather some clues along those lines. Spirit 2 would let you talk to spirits attached (and present) to the patient, and there's a chance you might be able to help them to stop negatively impacting your client's mental health. And as noted you can ask friendly spirits to help out as Spirit 2 (though spirits in the WoD are highly transactional, even if they are inclined to help, they need to be shown proper respect and offerings before they will unless you have a standing pact with them...which generally takes higher Spirit for a mage to codify on their own, don't think of Spirit 2 as an unlimited source of helpers).

                  One of the big things that slow down therapy is the tendency for patients to keep things back. Whether that's talking about events they've experienced/witnessed, personal anxieties, or thoughts they think are shameful. Mind 1 can allow the character to sense when the patient is holding back, a useful tool for helping them resolve issues. At Mind 2, the character can read the person's surface thoughts, getting a better insight into what, exactly, the patient is not saying. Mind 3 is needed to dig deeper, to explore buried anxieties.

                  Obviously, the point of gaining this understanding is not to immediately dredge it up. That sort of thing will not only cause distress, but will raise understandable questions about how the patient's therapist could possibly know these things. Rather, the "reading" abilities serve as a way to coax the patient into talking, which is the true goal. The first step to resolving their problems is to bring them out into the open. Even just saying what is bothering them can be hugely beneficial to their emotional state, as venting is cathartic.


                  Outside of the office, Mind 2 is very useful as part of the "adventuring Mage lifestyle". The ability to tell truth from lies, to shield oneself from mind- or aura-reading, to multitask (while the character's actions are still limited in the physical speed of their body, they can have many mental processes working simultaneously), and the ability to keep a "sense hostility" Effect running in the background to warn of danger.

                  Prime 1 can be used to "consecrate" personal objects. While this does infuse them with the mage's personal Resonance (and makes them count as a strong Correspondence connection), consecrated objects can "carry with" the mage when using Effects that non-bodily objects normally could not. (Shapeshifting, stepping sideways, etc). Prime 1 (or 2, I forget) has also been historically used to channel Quintessence from the mage's personal pattern, allowing them to sacrifice health for Quint on a one-for-one basis. It's a risky maneuver, "casting from hit points", but it might be necessary in certain situations. It's good to have the option, at least.

                              Also think about how your character does magic, as that will determine a lot about how she goes about using her magic to help people:

                              Things like incense, hypnotism, and drugs are at least somewhat appropriate in a counselor's office. Your character can use them to ritually cast during counseling sessions without raising any eyebrows, which will let her choose her spells specifically for the person in front of her.

                              If her instruments are odd but small, like sympathetic connections (using pieces of the target's hair or blood) and subtle but visible chiminage (offerings for spirits) she's can potentially hide what she's doing but risks scorn (or Paradox, depending on the spell) if she gets caught. Where possible she is going to want to use them out of the sight of her targets. She can probably risk fast spells without much of a problem (although at her Arete that won't do much) but ritual casting is a gamble. No one is going to question if she puts a little bird seed into a bowl once during a visit, especially if it is near an open window. It will be much more attention grabbing if she keeps filling bowls around her office throughout the entire hour.

                              Anachronisms like blood sacrifices, chanting, or obvious rituals have to be kept out of sight, so your character will be limited in how often she can cast that way. Because she has to cast them further out from the time of use she's going to need to get more successes to keep the duration going. These spells can be tailored for specific patients, but are in general better suited for general effects that can increase her own abilities. A spell to make her able to hear lies would be much better than a spell to make a specific person

                              Note that careful thought can negate a lot of these issues so don't feel too constrained. A lot of mages put in 'off screen' effort so it is quite possible for otherwise obvious anachronisms to be worked into her office in subtle ways. It's still something your character would have spent a lot of time working on so think of how it affects her approach.
