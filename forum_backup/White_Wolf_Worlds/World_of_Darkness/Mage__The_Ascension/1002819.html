      1- yes, charms can be played over the gauntlet, though the gauntlet rating will have an affect on how difficult it is for the spirit's charms to take effect. Materialisation will allow the spirit to attack physically and be harmed physically.

      2- you need Prime 3 Spirit 2 to pass quintessence to a spirit.

      3- it's true that most effects in the penumbra are coincidental, but opening a hole in the gauntlet may still be vulgar enough for a paradox backlash to occur, especially in places where the gauntlet is high.

      Also, the Avatar Storm may be a thing that discourages that.

      I'm glad you managed to inspire her with this game, and I hope you guys get a lot of enjoyment out of it.

      I reccomend looking the the Book of Worlds and the Infinite Tapestry for some cool Umbral locations. The Velevet Shadow is also good for that, as is Wraith The Oblivion for all things dead lands.

                          Depending on where you are in the Umbra, paradox may bite you in particular ways, being in the shadowlands may lead to a spectre attack, while being in the dream worlds may result in the character's nightmares manifesting. It's only by going beyond the horizon (past mars) that paradox stops being a thing entirely.

                          The chart for the Gauntlet rating will be in the book. You'll need extra successes to step sideways with anything more than just your body, including clothes, weapons, bags, etc, unless you consecrate them with Prime 1. Most Verbena get around this by going into the umbra skyclad.
                          Also, look into totems, they can be quite an cool thing for the Dreamspeakers to have.

                                      It can totally work that way.
                                      There's a whole system of chiminage in WtA which is detailed properly in Axis Mundi.
                                      I don't know how spirits work in Forsaken, but in oWoD, their essence/power is basically quintessence, and the way most can recharge is by absorbing more, either from another werewolf, a mage who gives them Quint, or from going into Slumber for a number of hours.

                                      But to me it makes perfect sense that a fire elemental would get stronger if there was a fire for it to feed off.

                                      All spirits want something, an the Axis Mundi details a lot both the material components and acts of Chiminage which will endear you to the spirit in question.

                                      For example, Raven spirits are pranksters who enjoy riddles, so they'll appreciate a quick game of riddles for small favour, or a pretty good prank on their enemies for larger ones. They especially like the kind of pranks which trick Banes into helping Gaia, for example.
                                      A Rat spirit, on the other hand, would be content with having a place to stay and a good supply of food. If you are known to attack rats, however, they will probably be quite hostile.

                                      Spirits talk, and there is near constant rivalry between the various broods.

                                      As this is Mage, you aren't limited to Werewolf spirits, you can incorporate spirits from all kinds of sources, any books you like or you know she likes, or even make a few up.
                                      For example, mages deal with angels, ancestral spirits, dreams, demons, abstract concepts, and even archetypes like Death.
                                      Choose a mythology and feel free to transplant its spirits wholesale.
