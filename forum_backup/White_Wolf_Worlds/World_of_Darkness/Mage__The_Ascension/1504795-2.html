        Homebrew mechanics

        Time-you require correspondence to prophesize events you're not personally involved in it's also generally recommended to have sympathetic links. Entropy is also useful to ensure you're looking at the most likely events

        You cannot see your own death unless its a specific as awareness of it will inevitably change it as you react to.it.

        Correspondence, any long range effect needs at least one dot in the relevant sphere to effect your environment. for example teleporting a human into your presence would require life 1 for a pattern lock.

        Mind effects are far more likely to cause quiet backlashes, especially if you're altering other peoples minds or yours on a regular basis.



        Counter magic
        you can counter spells with differant spheres from the spell but you must explain how it deconstructs the spell and is effectively a spell itself.

        -prime 5 and entropy 5 can be used as standard counter magic by simply pulling the spell apart. As can quithlopic forces.

        -other supernaturals have a specific sphere their 'spells' are vulnerable. This rules tends to apply more. to mystic flavoured powers such as Rites and pacts than inherant powers.

        Werewolves-spirit
        Fae-spirit
        ​​​​Demon-prime
        Wraiths-entropy
        Mummies-prime/time
        Vampires-entropy.

        -Destiny background provides counter dice to attempts to subvert your fate.

        -you cannot negate truth faith Full stop.​



        Nexus

        A nexus is effectively a highly important sleepers such as The Prophet muhammid, Socrates, Isaac Newton or queen elizibeth the 1st, a nexus effectively shapes the world in major ways as the inherent potential of humanity made manifest. Mages can kill nexus' but this will calcify whatever their objectives where for example assassinating a civil rights worker will substantially bolster the cause. Trying to use magic to negate a nexus will result in cataclysmic worldwide backlashes, for example the unions attempt to prevent the sleeper Alan turnings release of technology ahead of schedule resulted in an alternative timeline where an entire convention came into existence defected from the the union and the creation of the Internet well ahead of union timeline. Supernaturals can be nexus' but this is a rarity among rarities, more likely supernaturals will find their will subverted by the historical inertia of such beings

        The only real way to counter a nexus is either the sleepers themselves reject such an individual (such as Nicholai Tesla or Julian the apostate) or another Nexus (for example Adolf Hitler was countered by Winston Churchill)


        Mechanically nexus' have destiny 5 and are capable of having backgrounds beyond 5 and tend to have an incredible range of abilities non supernatural in nature.

                    So some more of our Homebrew and Headcanon

                    Magik Resistance
                    Due to the their inner power being Quintessential​ or laced with quintessence (vitae) most Nightfolk have an innate resistance to Magik - this takes the form of extra successes needed to affect the nightfolk.

                    Kindred generation minus 10, Garou, Wraiths and Changelings half Gnosis, Pathos and Glamour.

                    So this makes low generation kindred an undertaking for most Mage (which fits better).

                    I don’t weight this by Technomagik v mythic, other than maybe adding some additional effects (Technomagic might be more severe on Fey if successful).

                    Mage can overcome this resistance by fueling the effect with Quintessance equal to the resistance level, but it’s all or nothing.

                    Effects have to have an anchor (Entropy for Vampires, Spirit Garou etc)

                    Nightfolk Counter Magik can be performed by Thaumaturgists, Rite Masters, Occultists etc and purely increases successes.

                    Resonance/Disonance and Paradox
                    Our setting is in Victorian Times so we use Elegant, Uncanny and Catastrophic Magic.

                    Elegant accumulates disonance until it exceeds their Arête then manifests as a Resonance effect as well as making the mage noticeable to the aware.
                    Uncanny accumulates disonance and incurs Backlash.
                    Catastrophic provokes full blown paradox.

                    Setting
                    The Order of Reason fragmented in during Cromwellian times, when the British Order broke away from fractious Europe and have remained separate arms ever since.

                    Although on reasonable terms the British Imperium has grown powerful along with the Empire, whilst the European order led from Prussia is beset by internal strife orchestrated by elder Kindred.

                    The British Imperium is led by Lord Kelvin, or rather a Master who current form is Lord Kelvin.

                    The Imperium however has its own challenges as the efforts of the Celestial Masters and Explorators to map mythic places from reality are repeatedly failing and worse still areas of London are experiencing minor reality fluctuations (or temporary Reality Zones).

                    Other Nightfolk experience the fluctuations sometimes beneficially or otherwise.

                    This is caused by the spread of Spritualism globally amplified by the Storm Eater disrupting static reality.

                    The Voltiac League were allied to the Order but broke from the order with their denouncing Luminescent Ether, this led to a short magikal war which ended with a standoff between the Imperium and European Order, the Voltiacs using this as an opportunity to retreat to their Horizon Realm Etheria.

                    Kelvin (The Lord Protector) has a secret group of The Templer Knight craft who serve as his secret police.

                    Queen Victoria despite being the official figurehead of The Imperium is secretly disillusioned with them following Alfred’s death (she believes they allowed him to die due to his promoting the idea of the various Orders uniting as a Technocratic Union).

                    She has her own Mage helpers and has even tasked them with locating Albert in the Shadowlands, despite this being a deviation of Imperium dictum.

                    One severe side effect of the fluctuations are incursions in wilder places of deep umbral entities (adding a little Mythos by Gaslight to the mix).

                                Here’s my headcannon:

                                The Marauders are out. They strike me as not warnings of too much hubris, but of logical outcomes to a worldview of reality being whatever you believe it to be.

                                The Nephandi are gone, as far as we know. They make great boogeymen; everybody is scared of them, but nobody believes they are still around.

                                The Disparate Alliance doesn’t really exist. Instead, many crafts have seats within the Council of Traditions (more than 9). That council is not really a united front; they are closer to a nonaggression pact than anything else. Basically, they are a mystical United Nations – and about as effective.

                                The Technocracy’s not around either. They’ve been replaced by The Collective. Like the Technocracy, The Collective is ruthless in its domination. Unlike the Technocracy, The Collective does not have idealism. They are not ruled by good intentions gone astray. Think of the Party from 1984 and give them magic. They are truly seeking to create a world under anarchotyranny.

                                Someday, when I get around to running my campaign, I will make it that the players come from one tradition. I randomly picked the Cult of Ecstasy.

                                One more thing: while reality is malleable, truth is not. At least, not yet.

                                                Virtual Adepts

                                                My version of the Adepts differs slightly from the mainline. For one, they were never part of the Technocracy, instead being one of the groups (alongside the Electrodyne Engineers) who rejected the premise of the Union as it was antithetical to their own basic underlying philosophy.

                                                Information is Power because Information is Reality. If you can manipulate one, you can manipulate the other. Only the free and open exchange of Information will enable advancement and enlightenment.

                                                (In this sense, many consider the NWO to be their primary nemesis.)

                                                They have been constantly evolving alongside information technology. In the Enlightenment era they were the Sectors Guild In the early 19th century they became the Differential Engineers and after rejecting the Technocratic Union, the Analytical Reckoners. At the end of the 1950s, they became their modern incarnation of the Virtual Adepts and joined the Council of Nine.

                                                The Adepts' leader and philosophical prophet from that point until his recent assassination was Roger Thackery, a British mathematician, cryptographer, and friend of Alan Turing. With Thackery's death around before the turn of the millennium, leadership of the Tradition has fallen to former child prodigy and digital Ascension Warrior Dante.

                                                Almost all of their inter-Tradition interaction takes place online, either in the mundane deep web or in the Digital Web. Real world "meat-ings" tend toward a level of paranoia reminiscent of Cold War spy craft. Most groups run as democratic meritocracies, but are prone to arguments and egotism.

                                                All Virtual Adept Initiates learn the first level of Connection (Correspondence) in order to access and process the Information which makes up Reality.

                                                Their foci include any modern equipment or process which facilitates or improves the finding, processing, storage, or manipulation of information. This includes (but is not limited to) computers, surveillance systems, data networks, advanced mathematics, social engineering, bio hacks, cybernetics, quantum science, espionage tradecraft, and psychic studies.

                                                Their sanctums are usually personal labs and workstations, VR chambers, private survivalist bunkers, and (most especially) the Digital Web.
                                                Acolytes are typically those fiercely devoted to the freedom of the individual and of information, obsessive technology users, paranoid conspiracy theorists, investigators of the building blocks of reality, and digital AIs.

                                                The Tradition's primary chantry is the Crystal Palace, one of the oldest and most secure fortresses in the Digital Web. While the Palace is open to all Adepts and certain guests, many of its areas are locked behind firewalls and passwords known only to those who've proven themselves worthy and loyal.

                                                (Concepts: Wheel-chair bound web goddess, Walking encyclopedia of conspiracies looking to start his own, Underground music producer, Self-made cyborg, Bishonen biker boy street samurai.)

                                                Voltaire, "Tolerance" (1764)

                                                    Virtual Adept factions.

                                                    The Network
                                                    The old guard, upholders of the ideals of intellectual freedom and personal responsibility. They tend toward subtlety and intellectualism as they bury themselves in the modern information superstructure.
                                                    • The Iron Box - historians and archaeologists obsessed with reconstructing the true history of the world. Many are fixated on pet conspiracies and secret histories, but are all skilled researchers, linguists, and explorers. Members frequently dig through lost ruins and attempt to raid Technocracy information centers. However, they also tend to spend a lot of time arguing among themselves.
                                                    • The Memory Palace - linguists, psychologists, and researchers working to unlock the power of the unfettered human mind. They use mnemonics, hypnosis, advanced mathematics, and psychotropic drugs to investigate meme development, genius level intellects, psychic abilities, and past life incarnations. Many of them are deeply weird even by Mage standards.
                                                    • The War Room - the most militant of the Old Guard, rogue intelligence operatives and analysts who carry out sabotage, assassinations, and military strikes against Technocracy targets. Operating in cell structures, members are often experts in military tactics, strategy, and logistics, as well as electronic and asymmetrical warfare.
                                                    • The Watchdogs - masters of observation, surveillance, and security. Wired into the world's collection of cameras, microphones, and satellites, they constantly monitor activity and behavior online and off. In theory, this is to study human nature and counter Technocracy activity, but in truth more than a few are obsessive voyeurs and shut ins. Still, they are the closest thing the Tradition has to an internal police force.
                                                    • The Weathermen - data collectors and information analysts who work to use past and present trends to forecast future events. In addition to attacks, natural disasters, and other calamities, they also predict social trends, sporting events, and random occurrences. Often human calculators able to crunch numbers in their heads faster than most mundane computers.
                                                    • The Chaosticians - esoteric mathematicians focused on complex and chaotic systems. They try to understand the cracks and flaws in data and in Reality, working with fractals, chaos theory, and social engineering. They are often able to create large scale reactions through minute alterations. Many are high level geniuses with a distinct lack of personal skills, even by Virtual Adept standards.
                                                    (Concepts: Burned CIA agent out for revenge, Conspiratorial historian out to expose The Truth, Faceless voyeur waging the Ascension War through the information superstructure, Antisocial otaku with eidetic memory, Math prodigy predicting the end of the world, Parapsychologist psychonaut, Brians behind a highly successful anti-Technocracy t*rrorist cell, Shockingly conservative diplomat to the other Traditions)


                                                    The Information Underground
                                                    The new generation and the modern face of the Tradition. More radicalized and rebellious than their elders, they are devoted to empowerment provided by freedom, education, and technology. They prefer direct action to light fires within modern high tech subcultures.
                                                    • The Invisible Hand - economists and financial adventurers advocating laissez faire capitalism and free market libertarianism, with a doctrine of empowerment through economic freedom and property rights. They are zealous enemies of the Syndicate and its system of crony corporate pseudo-capitalism and government partnership fascism. Members work raw economic data and understanding of human nature into miracles of money making and information gathering.
                                                    • The Nexplorers - one of the larger and more vocal factions, advocating for the Digital Web as the future of humanity and Reality. Master programers, cyberneticists, and creative geniuses, they bravely explore, study, and defend the Web from Technocratic and Nephandic forces, while formatting new Virtual Adept strongholds and bringing the benefits of a free and open internet to mundane reality.
                                                    • The Firebringers - educators and activists preaching that Information is Power. Radical and often revolutionary, they work as community organizers, cultural historians, legal advocates, and professional troublemakers. Members cultivate skills as both high tech technomancers and masters of Ars Cupidae, both inspiring and coordinating social movements and uprisings.
                                                    • The Cypherpunks - master mathematicians and puzzle masters prone to excessive curiosity and arbitrary contrariness. They use bleeding edge computer hacking, code cracking, viral information, and sheer cleverness to operate as anonymous journalists, spies, whistle blowers, and revolutionaries. Heavily influenced by the 80s cyberpunk genre, some dress and act outrageously (even violently) while others favor social invisibility.
                                                    • Channel Z - an underground collective of outlaw artists, writers, and entertainers fighting a culture war against mainstream corporate-produced product. They frequently work subtle Mind effects into independent documentaries and films, self-published books and graphic novels, spoken word and performance art, bootleg music, viral videos, and graffiti.
                                                    (Concepts: Underground conservative podcaster, High tech performance artist, Outlaw hacktivist who has erased every trace of her original identity, Up and coming hot shot coder of the Digital Web, Crossword puzzle junkie turned code breaker, Angry young person fighting to take down The Man, Online graphic novelist, Cyberpunk war goddess)

                                                    Tomorrow's Children
                                                    Freaks, misfits, and revolutionaries pushing the limits of technology, information, and even Reality to the bleeding edge and beyond. Harbingers of the future, they try to overcome the limits of humanity in strange and dangerous ways, often far beyond what the mundane masses are willing to currently accept.
                                                    • The New Flesh - fanatical do-it-yourself transhumanists, they strive to evolve beyond mundane meat through the use of advanced cybernetics and bionics, bioengineering, and brain enhancements. In addition, they study artificial intelligence, nanotechnology, and xenobiology. Many rogue members of Iteration X and the Progenitors find a home here.
                                                    • The Reality Hackers - quantum philosophers and technognostics playing with the very building blocks or reality. Investigating quantum theory, superfluids, gluons, quarks, dark matter, DNA, and neurospace, to unravel and recode the Tapestry itself. Outsiders tend to view them as either brilliant or insane.
                                                    • The Dream Theater - investigators into the human subconscious, hacking into sleeping minds to experiment with dream constructs and subconscious programing. While many do this out of benevolent motivations, including healing psychological trauma and undoing brainwashing, others are motivated by pure curiosity or even amusement. The entire group is viewed with fear and suspicion by almost everyone.
                                                    • Generation Omega - Born from Progenitor experiments to breed and genetically engineer psychic abilities, the original members escaped and found sanctuary with the Virtual Adepts. Members are effectively an extended family, and tend to be distrusting of outsiders, including fellow Traditionalists. Their hyper-evolved brains enable them to perform telepathy, remote viewing, psychokinesis, and even manipulate their own bodies and Reality itself.
                                                    • Electric Voodoo - a cult centered in the Digital Web, built around the idea of temporary melding with a collection of artificial intelligences, digital spirits, and online elementals they call the Electric Loa. They work to expand their understanding of the Digital Web and of Reality while channeling the powers of modern concepts such as Credit, Nuclear Energy, and Plastic Surgery.
                                                    (Concepts: Second generation psychic wanting to learn more about normal people, Transhumanist demagogue rewriting his own DNA, Half-mad prophet of his AI god, Bionic bastard eager to go out in a blaze of glory, Morally questionable psychologist, Beautiful mind teetering on the brink of madness, Anthropologist studying high tech spirits, Mage who awakened during UFO abduction)

                                                        You just brought up my favorite Tradition.

                                                        I would add charts, tables of figures, and books to their Instruments, especially in the days before they became the Difference Engineers. Likewise, computational devices such as slide rules, sextants, and clocks. Incidentally, an appropriate chart can potentially complete calculations faster and with more precision than a digital computer can. The catch is “appropriate”: charts are very narrowly focused in their usage, while digital computers are general-purpose.

                                                        I do allow VAs to access the Digital Web (kind of; see below) using Instruments other than computers, such as the aforementioned charts, tables, books, slide rules, sextants, and clocks; even “advanced mathematics” can be used. The catch is that access to the Digital Web involves establishing a Sympathetic Connection to it, bringing the Correspondence Range table into play; and these non-standard ways of accessing the Digital Web have high Correspondence Ranges.

                                                        And ever since Paradigm Explored: Number and Shape was published, I have considered it to be required reading for VA players, with one change: the Platonic Realm isn't an Epiphany in the Astral Umbra, as it's described in that book; it's the “interior” of the Hypersphere described in the Revised VA Tradbook, serving as the connective tissue between the Correspondence Point at the Hypersphere's center and the Tapestry on its surface. And the Digital Web is part of the Platonic Realm; specifically, it's the part that corresponds to digital computers. When a VA uses things like charts or tables to access the “Digital Web”, what they're actually accessing is whatever part of the Platonic Realm that corresponds to the information present in the Instrument being used to establish the Connection.

                                                        Likewise, Mount Qaf (known to the VAs as “the Matterhorn”) is that part of the Platonic Realm that corresponds to human minds; and it can be accessed using a Conjunctional Correspondence/Mind effect analogous to the Correspondence effect that VAs use to access the Platonic Realm. Other Traditions can potentially access other parts of the Platonic Realm, such as Verbena using Conjunctional Correspondence/Life effects to access a “biocomputing sector” of the Platonic Realm, or a Wu Lung using Correspondence/Prime and feng shui to use dragon lines as a computational space. (As an aside, the notion of binary encoding is inherent in the I Ching's trigrams and hexagrams.) And there may indeed be an Epiphany in the Astral Umbra that the likes of the Older of Hermes access using Conjunctional Correspondence/Spirit Magick. But the VAs were the first to see the whole of the Platonic Realm for what it is; and their access to it is much more direct, requiring no Conjunctional Spheres.

                                                            In my head canon, I tend to think of the proto-Adepts as being parts of the Medieval Craftmasons and Subtle Ones, as well as some of the Byzantine Artificers, with a lot of them being monks, scholars, merchants, alchemists, and physics. The development of the printing press is probably what caused them to start to coalesce into a functional Lodge/Craft by the late Renaissance/early Enlightenment. The Sectors Guild would have been full of mapmakers, explorers, astronomers, clockmakers, and tinkerers in addition to natural philosophers, salon intellectuals, and revolutionaries.
