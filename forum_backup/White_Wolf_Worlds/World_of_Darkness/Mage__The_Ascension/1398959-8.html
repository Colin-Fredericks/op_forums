            Originally posted by Koronus View Post

            I strongly disagree with this on two major points. First, we do not want a repeat of the stupidity of V5 controversy. Blaming an real world outbreak, one that's killed thousands and thousands of people worldwide and is currently still killing people is a BAD idea. Not to mention that it such an absurdly risky and stupid move that barring the individuals in question spending a ridiculous amount of time and effort keeping their superiors in the dark, they're going to get shut down. Look at the opening/closing fiction of the Rev Ed Syn CB. It's literally a Syndicate agent shutting down a Progenitor team's efforts to fast track a cure for cancer to the Sleepers because they're ignoring the dangers involved in rushing things to the Masses before they are ready for it.

            The idea of creating a virus to kill Sleepers and scare them into embracing technology and science is... well at best something the more one-dimensional evil depictions of the Union would do. And even then that borders on sheer insanity.

            Second, I frankly believe that they would never do a Union Civil War nor believe that they ever should. Now I should start first, that I'm not against them giving us the tools to enact such a conflict in our games but actually doing it in the canon would be a terrible move. First off there are only three real board outcomes they do with such a thing:
            1. One side wins the war and becomes the new Union
            2. The war becomes a stalemate and the Technocracy fractures in two
            3. The Technocracy collapses from the infighting into a diminished state

            With Option 1, whatever the outcome, you're going to annoy people as it takes away their options for using the Technocracy. Those that want the Technocracy to be nuanced and/or more morally centered or light is not going to like it if the darker side wins and we're only left with that aspect of the Technocracy and vice versa.

            While Option 2 would see those factions preserved, it would also undermine the threat of the Technocracy to outsiders like the Traditions and Crafts as suddenly the weakness of the Technocratic Monolith has not been laid bare for all to see but has seen it torn asunder. Suddenly the Union is no longer the top dog and while in-universe that would be great for the Traditions and Crafts, it would also mean that one of the core elements of MtAs is lost, the underdog struggle of the Traditions against the dominant Technocracy.

            And it's the same for Option 3 but potentially worse as it isn't mutually exclusive from the other two options.

            Second is the fact that such a war would not be clean divide in the Union along Convention lines despite what some would claim. Indeed, much of the fighting between the NWO and the Syn is more over who should be the First Among Equals thing going on between the two Conventions, their particular world views and general bitching about the screw ups of the other. As a result if outfighting did break out between them, it's unlikely that any of the other Conventions would fully support one side or the other. Hell if anything, ItX, Prog and VE are just as likely to band together and tell the NWO and Syn to knock it off. And given the military might of just the VE, that would likely see the conflict end right then and there.

            And that's kind of the thing, for all the talk of a Technocrat Civil War, unless circumstances drastically change, all it will ever be is just that, talk and the jockeying of power between the NWO and the Syn.

            Finally from an out-of-universe perspective, the Conventions work fine just as they are. Each cover their own sphere of sciences, technologies and politics, and each Convention provides valuable things for the others. Taking them apart upsets the balance within the Technocracy.

            Frankly I hope the people that do work on M5 look to what's been done with the Union in recent years and use that as a springboard for them in M5. Keep them strong and unified to outsiders while inside they're struggling against themselves and their own flaws and failures.

            Homo sapiens. What an inventive, invincible species. It's only a few million years since they crawled up out of the mud and learned to walk. Puny, defenceless bipeds. They've survived flood, famine and plague. They've survived cosmic wars and holocausts. And now, here they are, out among the stars, waiting to begin a new life. Ready to outsit eternity. They're indomitable. Indomitable.

                                                    Not really, no. The Harbingers of Avalon are Enlightened Scientists just like any Technocrat, and their Practices work the same way that any Technocratic Practices work.
