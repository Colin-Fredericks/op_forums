    A question about boosting attributes using life 3.

    09-03-2017, 06:10 PM
    So, you can boost attributes with life 3. As long as it's within the human's body capabilities (upto 5 dots), its coincidental.
    Above that, and it becomes vulgar. Now, if it's permanent it causes genetic flaws and so on, however, if I give myself 10 dex for one day per say, I would get paradox, however there would be no genetic flaw, correct?
    And the book also mentions that boosting your attributes too much would cause your body to change in an odd way; giving yourself 10 str per say would make you muscular as fuck, ridiculously so - is that how it works? or that also only happens if the change is permanent?

    Basically I want to know what other consequences are there for boosting your attributes above 5, except for paradox. (Note that I'm not talking about a permanent boost, max 1 ic day, perhaps even just a scene.)

      1) No, you would not get Genetic Flaws for an Effect that is temporary. It's only for permanent Effects (which would probably mechanically be symbolized by the character gaining dots in the Enhancement background, along with the Genetic Flaws).

      2) I don't know for certain, but I would think that physical traits reflecting Attributes >6 would depend more on the character's Paradigm. If it's a Progenitor or Etherite using medicine to bolster their muscle mass, then obviously they would look as swole as they are. If it's a mystic channeling the strength of Samson, the beasts of the wild, or the celestial bodies, the character would probably not look much different. After all, from their perspective, the strength they gain has nothing to do with their muscles.

      3) I would assume "these changes becoming permanent" is the textbook definition of a Genetic Flaw. See item #1.

          Originally posted by Bluecho View Post
          If it's a mystic channeling the strength of Samson, the beasts of the wild, or the celestial bodies, the character would probably not look much different. After all, from their perspective, the strength they gain has nothing to do with their muscles.
          Hmhmh. I disagree for one reason - it's the *Life* Sphere.
          Or rather, let me rephrase - I disagree that there are no physical changes either way. The Life sphere's domain are either changes to the invisible lifeforce, or changes to..well, the physical pattern. And the former does not quite give you increased strength despite the 'force' in its name.

          Now, this does not mean that the physical changes are *apparent* at first glance! The mystic might not get any visual changes for the same reason a Progenitor can avoid it (Though I agree that bulk change would be more common among Etherites and Progenitor treatments): The muscle internals become different. Instead of bulk, there are microscopic changes to the build, the structure. Perhaps downright alien changes.

          But either way, if you want strength without any physical changes of any way, that would fall under the Forces sphere IMO. And *there*, the mystic could indeed literally conjure the strength of Samson into them - in the form of the sheer force of his strength. With the forces sphere involved, the Technocrat approach would in that case probably be cybernetics.

          At least, that's my interpretation. I think a more obvious example would be: It's the same reason why an approach to get 'The sight of an eagle' with the life sphere would definitely change the eyes at least internally - while a Forces or Correspondence based approach would not.
