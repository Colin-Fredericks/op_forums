    Originally posted by Lord Revan View Post
    The books don't give any detail how turning your body into an element works, other than saying it's Life 3/Forces 3. I have some questions that I know that have no RAW answers, but I still would like to know opinions and ideas.
    So, the mage can turn his body into any kind of element. And for each element the same set of questions applies:

    1- How tangible you are?
    2- How and how fast do you move?
    3- Do you take damage somehow?
    I'll actually answer the questions as asked, but first I'll say again that if it were my game the effect as you describe would require Life 4 (Edit: I can probably be convinced it should be Life 3) and Mind (at least 1). I would also rule that all such transformations, regardless of if they are Forces or Matter, require Forces 3 if you want to be able to move in a human-like manner in your new form. Otherwise you will move in the manner of the Force/Matter (i.e. Earth will just be an inanimate statue, Fire and Air will rapidly dissipate, and Water will instantly form a puddle and start to seep into the ground.

    I will also say that Earth, Air, and Water (and even Fire, probably) are NOT Forces, they are Matter. Even though they are the classic "Elements" of Western occultism/magic/Airbender lore, they are still matter. Earth is soil, dirt, stone - definitely not Forces. Water is the chemical H2O, definitely not Forces. Air is a mixture of mostly Nitrogen and Oxygen, definitely not Forces. Even Fire is not energy itself, but the material expression of matter combusting under extremely high heat. Though "heat" falls under Forces, you specifically state that you want to turn into "Fire", not heat. Fire is Matter, unless you are actually talking about heat, which is Forces.

    So to turn into animate Earth/Air/Fire/Water would be a Life 4(3)/Forces 3+/ Matter 3 Effect. To turn into a Force (Light, Heat, Gravity, Electricity, Magnetism, etc.) would be Life 4(3)/Forces 3+.

    "Shadow" is not a thing, it is the absence of a thing, and so does not fall under any of the pattern spheres. So, you cannot turn into Shadow unless maybe with high Entropy sphere magick, ala Obtenebration/Abyss lore. However, you can appear as a shadow - if you convert yourself to Light and control the forces around you to absorb light, you will appear as inky nothingness floating or walking around, which could very well be described as "Shadow".

    To answer the questions then!

    Quesiton 1:
    If you turn yourself into animate Matter (Earth/Air/Fire/Water), then it depends. Earth is quite tangible but can "flow" under the right conditions. Air, Fire, and Water are tangible, but also easily conform to shapes and can pass thorough small openings. Forces are in general less tangible than Matter. Light seems intangible, but cannot pass through opaque matter. Heat can pass through matter, but the speed it can do so depends on the matter. Electricity can pass through conductive Matter very rapidly and almost not at all through non-conductive matter. Magnetism and Gravity are not hindered by matter for the most part, but if you're out in the depths of space stay away from large gravity wells and high energy events, like stars, neutron stars, pulsars, magnetars, and black holes.

    Quesiton 2:
    For animate matter (Earth/Air/Fire/Water), not very fast. Probably just stick to normal human speed walking on ground, though since you are using Forces 3 to move, you can just as easily fly by manipulating how gravity interacts with you. I once calculated that with Forces 2 (manipulating the vector of Earth gravity, but not its magnitude), you can fly at approximately 125 mph (200 kph). Basically you max out at the terminal velocity of a human falling through Earth's atmosphere, since you can't enhance the magnitude of the gravity. With Forces 3 both manipulating the vector and magnitude of Earth gravity, you can fly much faster, at least 500 and probably 1000 mph (1600 kph). You need to protect yourself from the friction against the atmosphere at this point, so wear a helmet and try not to collide with a bird.

    For animate forces (Light/Heat/Gravity/Electricity/Magnetism), it's faster. So fast, in essence, that you don't need to worry about the speeds unless you are trying to get to another planet. However I personally would rule that you are not a "natural force" and you are in fact using Forces 3+ to control your "movement", and so are limited by how much Force you can apply. I'd use the 1000 mph (1600 kph) listed above for the Forces 3 movement speed. Higher Forces would produce faster speeds.

    Quesiton 3:
    Yes, but not generally from physical things. Even when turning into "Earth" you couldn't exactly be hurt by a hammer. If you were made of stone and got physically broken into smaller stones, then you would still be made of stone and so... no actual "damage". But weird things might damage you instead depending on the ST. For example, if you were Water and encountered sufficient heat, you might turn to steam or vapor and some ST's might call that "damage". I would say, however, that it's still water and so not "damage". Short of all the Matter/Forces of your new "body" being completely vaporized to nothing, you really can't be hurt or killed. Direct Effects though would still work - Entropy 4 or a Prime 3 energy blast still does aggravated damage to your pattern, and the use of Forces 3+ (against Light/Heat/Gravity/Electricity/Magnetism) or Matter 3+ (against Earth/Air/Fire/Water) can still tear apart your new Forces or Matter pattern, after all. You'd be immune to Life 3+ damage, however, at least without the aggressor combining it with Forces/Matter 3.

        My two cents:

        On the subject of what Spears should be required, I also would insist on Life as well as Forces, in that you are transforming a Life Pattern into a Forces Pattern; so you need the sphere that covers what you're transforming from as well as the sphere that covers what you're transforming to. I would not require Mind; but if you don't include it, your mind will gradually deteriorate the longer you stay in the elemental form; much like if you transform into an animal and don't take special effort to preserve your mind, you will gradually lose your higher reasoning facility and become an animal in mind as well as in body.

        Solids and liquids would be covered by Matter rather than Forces; but gas and plasmas (including fire and electricity) would be covered by Forces, as would the likes of light and darkness. (In the scientific paradigm, darkness is the absence of light; but in many other paradigms, darkness is a thing unto itself.) More exotic options, like turning yourself into sound, gamma radiation, or gravity, might be possible, but should be considered carefully.

        As to the effects: for light, materials are either transparent, translucent, or opaque. If they're transparent, you can pretty much ignore them: you'll pass right through them, whether you want to or not. If they're opaque, they'll block you; but on the other hand, you're able to exert your strength against them as if you were still flesh and blood.

            There are times when subtlety is called for. Vulcan's Hammer is not a rote for those times. This enchantment transforms a mage into a living weapon of iron and flame.                             And that's what I was asking in OP. Considering you made the spell in a way you keep your consciousness, how would you answer the 3 questions?
                            Assuming a body made entirely of a single Force:

                            1 - You're as tangible as any manifestation of that force, but also affected by the same vicissitudes. You can traverse any medium they can, you can't traverse any medium they can't. More importantly, you're "weakened" in the same way, bent in the same way, and affect the medium in the same way. Any of this may be a bug or a feature depending on the situation. You can't affect things in a manner that force can't.

                            2 - You traverse the same mediums. If you "let go" and just follow the rules of that force, letting yourself be carried to its natural path, you'll have the natural speed of that force. If you move against this natural path, as electricity ignoring cables or light making a curve, then you're limited to your rating and success in the effect as if you were trying to direct that energy with a pure Forces effect.

                            3 - Anytime you loose a sensible amount of energy you should take damage. So, if as light you traverse a material that lets just some light through, like paper, you take damage. As fire you don't want to be doused, as darkness you don't want to be directly illuminated.

                            Examples:

                            Fire - You can travel through living fire or through combustible materials, at a natural speed equal to the speed of its spread, but you consume fuel and burn things as you do so. You depend on the Fire Triangle, being barred from natural travel to any place that lack any of the three pillars (fuel, heat and oxygen), and taking damage when one is missing for any reason.

                            Sound - You travel through matter at varying speeds according to the medium, and blocked only by vacuum. But you loose energy when traveling any distance, quickly, and even more if you travel through any place that muffles sound, taking damage every time your potency in decibels is halved. Your movement have all the effects on the area of a sound of equal potency, and probably leaves sound behind you as you "propagates".

                            Electricity - Similar to sound, but with an added effect: things that take energy out of you keep it instead of dissipating it, which usually means structural damage. You can travel through air, but leaves an actual lightning in your path with all that entails.

                            Gravity - You travel at lightspeed deforming space where you stand. While you can travel through anything, in any direction, your only interaction with everything else is to pull things towards you. You also have extreme reach, but not so extreme strength. Remember that the gravity of the entire planet isn't strong enough to stop someone from jumping. It only wins out in the end because it keeps pulling after the jumper stops pressing feet against ground.

                                        I'll do my best to add one...

                                        Light - You can keep yourself contained in-place and move about slowly, but if you let go in any direction you travel at the speed of light until you collide with an object that blocks you. This will probably result in your death, so be ultra careful.
