                      No one of Consequence makes a strong basic argument. And Dataweaver is undermined by the fact that the Wu Lung are Confucians, and would sneer at Buddhist thought.

                      I'll go with the fact that in the original stories Dr. Strange goes to Tibet. In the early and mid Twentieth century Tibet was synonymous with mystic insight and ancient wisdom. When Mage first came out the nine traditions reflected stereotypes of magic users in American popular culture.

                      On that basis No One of Consequence wins hands down. The AK Brothers got all the Tibetan stuff.

                              As for Hermetics over Akashics, i would argue that the resource material used by the sorcerers is Hermetic and the Akashics aren't likely to worry too much about such written sources;
                              The Book of the Invisible Sun is probably based on Splendor Solis; a rennaissance alchemical text
                              Astronomia Nova is a western astronomy book
                              Codex Imperium sounds like a latin text
                              Key of Solomon is a real world Hermetic favorite
                              Book of Cagliostro is likely the tome of Giuseppe Balsamo, aka Count Alessandro di Cagliostro; who in WoD would seem more Hermetic than anything else
                              Maxim Gorky might be a fictional Theosophical work by Maxim Gorky... maybe.
                              and real world Hermetic occultists have a long history of looking into eastern esoteric material, thus requiring knwledge in Classical Sanskrit
                              The Akashayana would probably transmit most of their understanding through memorized forms, esoteric and mundane exercises, and meditative practices; and would likely have incorporated reincarnation into their protocol for what they do when their members die.

                              That said, the mystic group that Dr. Strange is from is like an odd mix of Akashic and Hermetic and I'd go so far as to ask my storyteller if my character can be trained under a (probably now dead) master who studied under both traditions.
