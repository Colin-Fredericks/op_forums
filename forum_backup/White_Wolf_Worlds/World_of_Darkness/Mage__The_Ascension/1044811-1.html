                Originally posted by Faradn View Post
                Theodrim, are you saying that the Ability system should actually require players to take correlated Abilities to raise certain other Abilities to a given level?
                To a certain extent, yeah. Once you go above three dots in a given ability, that represents a professional-level proficiency with that ability, which will invariably be the result of years' worth of education, experience, and practice. Even Talents -- you don't get four dots in Brawl without having done a lot of fighting over the course your life. That's why it's the break point for having specialties, and why PC's can't go above three dots at character generation without freebie expenditure. And honestly, absolutely, over the course of those years' worth of education, experience, and practice you'll have gained exposure and experience in correlative abilities that very well should be reflected on the character sheet.

                Somebody who's done enough fighting over the course of their lives to justify a Brawl rating of 4, will necessarily have learned how to bob and weave, rope-a-dope, and dodge haymakers, strong kicks, and tackles. Moreover, they'll have developed their reflexes, speed, footwork, and chin, and depending upon how they got that experience fighting, they'll probably have had to learn to run away from opponents, bouncers, and the cops. All of which will represent itself in at least some proficiency in Athletics.

                Likewise, they'll have to develop the ability to read an opponent, discern cues and telegraphs, and learn how to preempt and counter moves. So, there's proficiency in Alertness they will have developed over the course of their experience fighting. Then there's Medicine, in the form of knowing how to treat your own wounds, being treated, and seeing others treated especially in an organized environment like being in a boxing club or league, or spending time in a gym.

                Now, I'm not talking more than a dot or two, here, in any given case. You don't need to be an EMT to be a professional boxer. But on the other hand, the idea of being a professional boxer without having learned along the way to becoming a professional boxer, how to treat swelling, minor lacerations, broken noses and ribs, and how to tell the difference between a minor injury through which a person can continue to fight and a potentially serious injury which needs medical attention, is simply laughable.

                This is a part of creating a realistic, organic character. Nine times out of ten, a player acting in good faith is going to bring a character to the table already with those abilities, because to have correlative abilities is just common sense. The tenth time, it's a minor oversight which needs correction and it's not a big deal. At least, that's the way it is at my table.

                            >But without that theoretical understanding, they ain't doing advanced coding or networking applications.

                                                        Has anyone read the Cuckoo's Egg. It's the story of an American computer manager who figured out a Russian agent was using his lab's computers as a backdoor into secure government systems. It's a true story, too.

                                                            Theodrim (1) I was building things up from the ground logically. I read what you wrote. I was just stating what I thought about it all.

                                                            (2) Yes, those positions are complicated. Yes, you'd think that Yoga means "able to do a pressup". Here's why you're wrong. My friend DOES have Yoga 2 and Athletics 0. If you ask her to do a Sun Salutation, she doesn't just drop on the ground and do it. Like most Yoga practitioners she warms up. So yes, you can be a Master of Yoga and not be able to do a press-up, situp or pull-up (what I meant by lifting your own weight) under organic, natural conditions. Vampire pushes you off the edge of a building? You can't pull yourself up with Yoga, you haven't done your breathing exercises or warmed up. Drill Seargant tells you to "drop and give me 20!"? 'Fraid all of that time at the community center focusing on your Chakras won't help you do random exercise outside of the specific Yoga-based environment. Yoga isn't random, get on with it exercise by default. For some people Yoga can be 0 to Complex Yoga Position in 10 seconds, but for someone with Yoga 5 and Athletics 0 (still possible, if unlikely) it would be 0 to light stretches to bigger stretches to short rest to serious stretches to simple positions to The Position You Were Going For.

                                                            (3) The Martial Artist wouldn't feel like needing Athletics was a Tax, however, since anyone who is fighting in hand-to-hand combat will benefit from Athletics eventually. With the Dancer, I would have a hard time believing that Dance is purely Performance; my personal view is that Dance Moves are Athletics, Dancing to Impress An Audience is Performance. And you can't see why spinning around a pole at speed is different to spending 20 minutes breathing and then doing a few stretches which are grounded in Eastern Mysticism? Seriously? Would the Dancer feel taxed? Possibly. I would simply say that if Dance is your focus, Athletics will work for anything that isn't impressing an audience (so everything except Spirit and Mind), if you're using Spirit or Mind you'll roll Dexterity + Performance since in that case you're seeking to impress. I'd use the Complementary Rolls optional ruling, so if you get successes on the Athletics roll that will make the Performance roll easier, so I doubt they'll be complaining.

                                                            (4) I'm stating what I would do if someone came to me with that build. I wasn't saying anything about your argument; I was rationalizing why I think an ST should sometimes demand certain dots be on the sheet.

                                                            Overall I think everything should make sense, but it's important to avoid taxing. Taxing would be defined as "here are some dots you will never use/need, but I think you need to have them to be allowed these dots you actually will use/need". Making the NFL Player take Athletics and Brawl isn't taxing since they will use them. Making the Occult Researcher take Academics (as well as Occult) isn't taxing since they will use it. Making the Yoga Master take Athletics? That COULD be taxing if their character was conceived a certain way, that is "Master of Yoga As Focus, absolutely useless in combat, fighting/running/climbing is for other people".
