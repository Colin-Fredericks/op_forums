                                            There’s a spirit for literally everything. Be it angels, ancestors, nymphs, old gods, new gods, aliens, and even memes. Any pantheon of spirits can be used for any character build imaginable, it’s not as restricting as you’re making it out to be.

                                            Besides, every sphere will change its behaviour depending on paradigm. Forces: is darkness a force distinct from light? Is coldness a force? Is there such thing as a vacuum and gravity and magnetisism? What about fire? If fire a reaction, a spiritual energy, or a pure element? Each answer subtly changes how the sphere reacts, for example if darkness is a force pattern distinct from light, you would need Forces 3 to Change light into dark and vise versa. However you could use Forces 2 to grab nearby darkness and wrap it around yourself, something which would not be possible if you believe darkness to just be the absence of light.

                                            Similarly, is life powered by the four humours, individual cells working to a whole, or do we just have a general spiritual life force.

                                            Does Time fly in a straight arrow, or is it curved around major events? Do the events of the past stay solid, or are they as open ended as the future?


                                            With Entropy, is the world determined or random? Do we control and change an otherwise immutable fate? Or are we just pushing the odds in our favour? Does randomness even exist or do we all have a path, or perhaps our actions as individuals are random and arbitrary, but the big stuff isn’t. Changing history doesn’t change the big events, only the names.

                                            Is the mind the same thing as the brain, or does it reside in a parallel world, in perfect synchronisation, yet utterly separate? Are memories events or merely repeating patterns? Can they be changed or merely influenced? Does identity lie in the mind or the soul?

                                            Is Quintessence the most fundamental particle of existence, and energy which permeates everything, a left behind relic of the greater beings, or the residual power of possibility? When you absorb it, do you become more powerful, or simply more physical? Can it be used for anything other than what it was intended for? Does the resonance it leaves on you depend on the source, the method of extraction, or the will of the deity who created it in the first place? When making a wonder, are you imbuing it with power, inventing a tool? Or are you for assistance from beings beyond your comprehension so they may imbue your tools with power? If the latter, then is it impossible to make something you know they won’t like?

                                            No two mages spheres will work the same way. Even if they have exactly the same stats, the nuances in their paradigm will create different end results. A hermetic May cast a fireball which burns a blaze following an incantation of arcaic language. While a chorus member may ask for devine intervention, and before them their enemy now bursts into holy flames. An Etherite May instead zap them with a ray gun, while a Verbena may cut their hand and bleed out a wave of flame towards them.

                                            The Etherite would have to aim their shot, while the Hermetic may instead just need to focus on their linguistics. The choristers magic may only work if their deity wills it to, while the Verbena may need to take a single health level of damage to pull of the effect.

                                            Differences are built into each aspect of the character and every sphere, Spirit is not the exception, but the rule.

                                                Every Sphere varies from tradition to tradition, from convention to convention, and from mage to mage. But the vast majority of applications of Spirit, the core of the Sphere if you will (social interaction with Spirits) involves a huge catalogue of different important traits. Sure, you *can* summon up a potent alien/incarna/angel, but if your Charisma, Manipulation, Willpower, Wits, Perception, Intelligence, Enigmas, Occult, Etiquette etc. etc. etc. aren't up to the mark then you might get a negative result. If only you'd known that Lion would respond terribly to that sort of greeting. If only you'd known that this precise spirit has developed a (justified) hatred of your Tradition after "the incident". If only you'd known that this spirit would be offended if you didn't attach a certain title. If only you'd known that a blood offering would offend the "thing" you summoned. The Spirit Sphere opens up a complicated world; in fact, that's an understatement, a complex set of worlds, to the Mage. You're diving into a nest of pernicious demi-gods who (rightly?) take a dim view to interfering mortals in many cases.

                                                Part of the reason Void Engineers suck at dealing with extradimensional creatures is that they don't know to make offerings, and would actively reject such an idea. It seems so... mystical. What you're dealing with is a scientific phenomena, and paying a sort of homage affords them a degree of humanity which is unscientific, and falls in line with the works of reality deviants. There's a reason Dimensional Science is so focused on travel, and fighting spirits. The fact that they enter the game without the centuries of cross-tradition information available to members of the Traditions is a huge disadvantage. And in exchange, they are WAY better at other things, like weakening the Gauntlet and moving through other dimensions. But their inefficacy in dealing with aliens (although there are exceptions) is proof of my point.

                                                And when contrasted with Forces... you make stuff stop being alive, and you generally destroy things. Sure, your Focus can be as complicated as anything (mind-bending contortions, cries in dead languages, hypertech contraptions which weigh half a tonne) but at the end of the day the most complicated conjunctional traits needed here will be aiming, hitting, and whatever Ability you're using as a Focus.

                                                Now you could argue that Mind has similar requirements. Mind will be much more coincidental and powerful when combined with charisma, manipulation, certain merits, high Willpower... but interpersonal interaction is an inescapable aspect of a Mage character. It's part of TTRPG and human existence. Spirits are generally alien and have inhuman (although this doesn't necessarily mean inhumane) principles and interests.

                                                So it's not that Spirit is conceptually restricting or limiting, it's that Spirit requires quite a lot of conceptual and mechanical *investment* to be given justice. And that could look like a million different things. Just working off of "interferes with powers that shouldn't be interfered with" you can come up with a Hermetic (demons, infernal spirits), a Dreamspeaker (banes, corrupted spirits), a Void Engineer (performs unauthorized procedures with mysterious aliens which promise incredible power to them) and a Virtual Adept (viral, corrupted and generally unpleasant technological/data spirits) quite easily. But regardless of how it's done, in my opinion, it should be quite a big part of who they are.
