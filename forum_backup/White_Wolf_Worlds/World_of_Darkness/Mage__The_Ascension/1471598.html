    Hunters Hunted II, Kinfolk, and Ghost Hunters are not Sorcerer 20!

    01-01-2022, 12:15 PM
    Seriously, I am tired of WOD groups treating the HH II, Kinfolk: A Breed Apart, and Ghost Hunter numina as some sort of 20th update for sorcery and psychic powers. The numina that appears in that book, save for True Faith, should be not be seen as some sanctioned update for sorcery or numina given it is weaker and mechanically not the same. These users should be best seen as "dabblers" in numina who only use their power to hunt vampires and other horrible things in the night. They do not have the same mindset as a sorcerer who has the yearning and concerned with their own mystical objectives/enlightenment. The only exception is the Arcanum as they can be considered a proper sorcerous society with them capable of producing Master and Legendary Alchemists but even they tend to treat the other paths as more scholarly subjects.
    I understand it may seem simpler to use these numina when trying to run a 20th game, but I honestly feel it would cause more confusion and make psychics and sorcerers weaker and not able to keep up. It is not that hard to convert Sorcerer:Revised over to 20th and I have done it before without much difficulty.

                          Since the community, rather than the contents, seem to be a big sticking point here, I think it's worth getting something out of the way:

                          A lot of people don't like using the ST Vault because they are of an "official book only" default mindset. The late 90s/early 00s WoD fansites were full of really poorly considered material (before even getting into subjective thoughts like how well written it was, the mechanical merits, and all that). The 00s brought us the OGL boom where d20 got flooded with third party content and even for D&D it was hard to find a good trustworthy aggregator of reviews about all of it to know what was worth looking into and what was safe to skip. The ST Vault is still no promise of quality control, and while the cream of the crop has a lot of word of mouth on places like this, a lot of people aren't active members of the Onyx Path social media platforms, and that leaves a lot of books on there a gamble.

                          Secondly, a lot of people feel like the 20th editions are basically over with a few straggler books making it through the works before Paradox finally stops any new official books from getting made. On top of this, sorcerers do not seem to be a high priority for the M20 team (G&M seems to indicate this), nor have we heard of a less Mage oriented direct update to WoD: Sorcerer.

                          That means a simple truth: HHII, K:ABA, and WoD: GH are the closest we're probably going to see to an update to the Numina rules for 20th edition books. I would personally take anyone treating them as an update as implicitly meaning, "the best update we're going to get, and the only ones we currently have."

                          I could go into a whole debate about the contents of Sorcerer Revised regarding the attitude around whether or not it is meant to reflect an in-universe distinction between Numina users, or if it was a book meant to boost all Numina users to be more fun in both Numina only play to be more capable, and in mixed splat play to not feel overshadowed. But I don't think it would really mollify the core complaint I'm hearing, since that's not about the contents of the books.
