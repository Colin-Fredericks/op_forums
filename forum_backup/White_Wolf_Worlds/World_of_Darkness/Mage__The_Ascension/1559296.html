    Is there a way to rehabilitate The Syndicate?

    04-08-2026, 07:54 PM
    This question is not new around the forum, from what I could gather. I saw a post titled "Heroic Syndicate (or at least decent) characters?" which tackles somewhat what I'm gonna talk about here, with the difference that what I'm not tackling the issue from a character-level but in a convention/metaplot-level.

    The issue of corruption in the Technocracy is not new to M20, even though the creation of the Disparate Alliance gives renewed focus to this particular aspect of the metaplot. For reasons of better focusing the discussion and because the other two options makes the question itself completely irrelevant, I'm gonna (personally) assume the following metaplot option as true, from M20 Corebook (p.170):

    There’s corruption, but they’re dealing with it: Yes, some Nephandi have warped the Union’s purpose. And
    yeah, the Technocracy’s brutal. But still, the Union can be saved. Devoted Technocrats – inspired and sometimes
    led by Secret Agent John Courage – have been fighting a covert war within the Technocracy… and despite major
    losses and obstacles, there’s still hope for the Technocracy’s redemption.
    Alright, so going into a convention-to-convention basis, I can see how each one could be redeemed considering both how the Technocracy is handled in the Convention Books and in Technocracy Reloaded. Make the NWO less fascist, make Iteration X more accepting of chaos and human imperfection, make the Progenitors less of alienated mad scientists and make the Void Engineers less of a cosmic border patrol and focus on the ideal of exploring the universe. Of course, I'm simplifying a lot, but the point is I can see each of these conventions with a "core good" of sorts that would pave its way for rehabilitation, be it institutional stability (NWO) or ecological preservation (Progenitors).

    I cannot see a way to redeem the Syndicate. I can see a minority of heroic Syndicate agents, per the above mentioned post, but not a way to see this ideal being applied to the convention as a whole in a new Technocracy. Maybe it's my bias, given the current state of the world, but when I open the Convention Book: Syndicate (Revised) and see terms like "Guardians of the Status Quo" or "The Syndicate is about the almighty Bottom Line" or "everything is for sale, one way or another (this one is in the Technocracy Reloaded)", I get this visceral reaction of "right, these are the forever antagonists of this game, right there with the Nephandi". It doesn't help when I see the section of The Book of Secrets "Technocratic Influence Upon The Masses" and read this (p.230):

    Certain groups have their pet projects too – the space program for
    Void Engineers, endless indulgence for the Syndicate, fear of
    The Other for the NWO, biological potential for the Progenitors, and the
    reliable coolness of high technology for Iteration X. As a whole,
    though, the Union concentrates upon keeping the Masses
    controlled, comforted, and dependent upon technology for…
    well, everything. And in that regard, at least, they certainly
    do dominate the world.​
    This "endless indulgence" immediately makes me think of the Nephandic paradigm "Indulgence is Nature's Only Law", a fact that it's not lost in Technocracy Reloaded, for the Syndicate's Paradigms (p.157):

    Because such convictions often lead to corruption, certain
    Syndicate ops embrace the Sadean paradigm that Indulgence is
    Nature’s Only Law, believe that I’m a Predator and the World is
    My Prey, or — at its most solipsistic extreme — assert that I am
    All and therefore nothing else exists. These paradigms tend
    toward the Nephandic end of the belief spectrum, and so
    those holding them tend to be the most ruthless of their kind.
    So... how can anyone redeem this in a new post-metaplot Technocracy? It seems to me that a new and benevolent Technocracy is a Technocracy without its Syndicate.

    (Credit where credit is due, "fear of The Other" is extremely Orwellian and not a good look to the NWO, but then again they are not THAT much better than the Syndicate)

      To me, it seems like the Syndicate may be the most redeemable of the lot. Their whole focus seems to be around capitalism and making money. Which is a far cry from creating genetically engineered super soldiers, turning people into killer cyborgs, trying to impose a fascist regime, or whatever else the other Conventions have gotten up to over the centuries. In worst cases, it can even strip them of their magic completely. Paradigm is how you rationalize your ability to use magic, so anything that causes you to reexamine it is going to have dramatic effects on your entire world view.

          While I understand this could get into areas not necessarily appropriate for a deep discussion here, I think the core question is something slightly different:

          Can capitalism be redeemed?

          As I see it, there's only two ways to redeemed the Syndicate. If you believe capitalism can be a force for good despite how things are currently going, then to a large extent the Syndicate just needs to get their heads out of their asses. Far too much of history, but especially since the rise of liberal democracy and secular humanism, has been dictated by people that are powerful through wealth putting short term gains over both long term self-interest as well as any sort of moral compass. The Syndicate, more than any other faction in the WoD, can be fairly asked, "Why did you sit back and let all of this obviously prevented human suffering happen? Just to make the money line go up a bit this quarter?" Especially in recent history, the global economy has seen disaster after disaster that somehow keeps ending up enriching the rich, and making the poor poorer. This is obviously in the Conventions worst interests because the masses are losing faith in the value of the markets. We have multiple generations that have moved farther and farther away from participating in the activities that the Syndicate can turn into Primal Ventures because they get at best an unlivable pittance out of it.

          The other option is to ditch capitalism for something else. Which as DarkKing noted is going to be very hard on the Syndicate because it involves a paradigm shift in multiple meanings of the word. If capitalism is irredeemable, than the only way to redeem the Syndicate is to have them be the forefront of a new economy that actually works for everyone. This means a lot of purging of the current membership for clinging to old ideas (not that this is new for the Union), and a wide-scale concerted effort by the reinvigorated Convention to take a much stronger hand in pushing the global system into one that encourages human flourishing without appealing to base desires, greed, and large scale social manipulation.

                                                  Second, colonialism is a project that the Syndicate endorses as much as the NWO. One of the first financial markets that would model our current system of stock exchange was the Amsterdam Stock Exchange, that would finance much of the colonial projects of the Netherlands in Asia. The main impulse in a lot of colonial endeavors was to siphon natural resources and labor (a lot of the time, slave labor) from the colonies to the mainland. All of this is crucial to the development of capitalism in what today constitutes developed countries.

                                                  Third, I know that in Revised the metaplot converges to an enforcing of the Pogrom, resulting in the genocide of the Crafts. But this is a Technocratic Union at large action, not an specific NWO venture. I tend to agree with Brucato in M20 that this, even if made sense that Technocracy would do such a thing, I don't believe is a good idea because it completly undermines any chance of nuance in its portrayal, even if you believe that the Technocracy is hopelessly corrupt.                                                   First off, as bad as the Technocracy could be, the idea
