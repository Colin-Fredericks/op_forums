                Your test for magic isn't what you're saying it is. You want magic to be BREAKING the rules. But it's not. If it was, then ALL Magic would be Vulgar. It's not. You can consciously modify reality without breaking it. That's what coincidental magic is. That's why it's easier (which, is from a game design perspective ass-backwards, but... whatever. In Mage, as in all White Wolf, What is is what has been and ever shall be, bad design notwithstanding). And conscious beings who are not Awakened can manipulate reality. Static mages can do it by application of will to cause a change in reality that is, effectively, bending, cracking or breaking it, just not with the insane flexibility of an Awakened mage, but conscious beings who cannot do static or dynamic mages can still change reality, they're just restricted in the ways in which they can do so. Effectively, it has to be entirely coincidental and fall along long predetermined lines as to what cause generates what effect.

                You want magic to be special. And in Mage, it's just not. It's everything. A car is magic. A toaster. Rhianna. A vampire. The Dark Umbra. Albuquerque. The Moon. Tomorrow. Napoleon. Gerbils. What makes mages special is that they know how to get around the boring stuff and exploit the cracks to make things that could potentially happen, happen.
