    Syndicate-VE relationship?

    10-26-2016, 11:54 AM
    They keep submitting requests for weapons and military training in order to counter something they call “Threat Null,”
    but don’t shed much light on. Honestly, one of the few places where we see eye to eye with NWO is our distrust of the Void Engineers, but you know what they say: keep your enemies close.
    In this case, that means funding them enough to make them feel in the family, while keeping them on as much of a leash as possible. De-prioritizing space exploration in the West has helped to lessen their hold on government and public alike (though, not without resentment), and sends a clear message that they need to cover their bottom line or else.
    Firstly, why VE needs to request weapons and training to the Syndics in any way? They already have more than enough weapon foundries, arsenals and training facilities. Furthermore, VE's weapons and training vastly outclass the Syndicate counterparts...and for that matter, anything other Conventions could hope to provide.

    Secondly, text says as if Syndicate played an active part of de-prioritization of space exploration in the West. But I find that is implausible - if there is a Convention possess enough political power and will(and motivation) to wield it, that would be NWO, not Syndicate. That sort of thing is NWO's job, not the Syndics, after all.

      Firstly, who do you think pays for all these weapons, ammunition, maintenance, training, recruiting, facilities, staff, etc.? Without the funding of the Syndicate, the VE's would barely have the resources to build a single rocket let alone the fleet they require for Space Exploration, and gauntlet patrol.

      Secondly, While NWO can control the world from the shadows with their influence in universities, governments, information bureaus, and all kinds of surveillance from CCTV to internet security; it's the Syndicate that run the world in the bright of day. They are the reason Capitalism dominates the western landscape. Just to give you an idea of of the reach this gives them, think about everything you have, every book, every bit of tech, every piece of food. Does it have a brand name on it? The chances are the Technocracy can control it.
      They are the large corporations that get votes all to themselves, the news agents and media outlets which spin journalism to create a message the masses want to hear, they fund movies, tv shows, all kinds of stories designed to inspire people towards the Technocratic paradigm, and sell off the Tradition's arts and tools as simple magic shows with costumes and props.

      While the NWO can manipulate the leaders of the west into their way of thinking directly, the Syndicate can shift the opinion of the masses, making certain things less or more popular simply by increasing availability, or drawing attention to them in a negative way, and thus manipulate the leaders of the western world indirectly.

                                                      The greatest strength of the Union is that the Conventions work together. Everyone supplies things that the other Conventions require, so they can afford to specialize. NWO provides control, Syndicate provides infrastructure, Void Engineers provide safety, Progenitors provides medical and Iteration X provides materials. The problem with providing "safety" is that it's invisible and intangible unless there's a failure in supplying it. The Syndicate KNOWS that what the VE is doing is vital, but the VE has to keep secrets and that makes it difficult to understand the scope of threats that the VE holds back.

                                                      If you had a buddy who would vanish for months at a time to wrestle tigers that threaten you with minimal contact and who blew you off with vague allusions to things that they wouldn't fully explain, only to come knocking with some new scars, a hollow look in their eyes (proving that they are indeed wrestling tigers) and wanting to restock his cooler and recharge his phone, would you just be a-okay with letting them do so (considering that there's four other groups who also want to use the same things they want and are providing more concrete reasons for letting them have the stuff)?
