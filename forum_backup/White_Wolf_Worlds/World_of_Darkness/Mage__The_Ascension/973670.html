                              Originally posted by Posthumanity View Post
                              Do NWO and Syndicate have dedicated research scientists?
                              Yes. The NWO has the Collegia within the Ivory Tower. These are primarily academics who do heavy research into History and Psychology in order to better understand the transformation of ideas into reality. They also spend a great deal of time researching and writing essays on current affairs, political and sociological theories, psychological practices, media studies, and pretty much every other humanaties out there including Literature. They look for the ways in which the masses take to ideas and how ideas become status quo.

                              Syndicate have Economists of every sort, from global economists, to local trade researchers, and stock market trade economists. They watch the ebb and flow of global funds. They keep an eye of how cash flow affects things at local level, a city wide level, a corporate level and a global level. They research statistical models of growth and try to predict what kind of impact various business ventures and economic schemes will have on both the big and small pictures. They have marketing engineers and product design researchers who's job it is to discover the various kinds of markets there are and figure out the easiest way to sell to them, and manipulate them in order to groom them into "good consumers". They study the techniques of the past and try to adapt said technique or even create new ones for an ever changing and interconnected populous. For every new gains venture the Syndicate dabble, there will be a team of highly experienced business researchers behind them advising them on the best possible models for success.

Then there is the Syndicate's sticky fingers in the projects of other Conventions as the resource allocations branch of the Union as a whole.
