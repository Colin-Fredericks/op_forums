                            Originally posted by Numinous View Post
                            If Syndicate's magicians, agents, extraordinary operatives and private hypertech military are pitted against Pentex paramilitary, mercenary subsidiaries, BSD and Nephandi, who will ultimately prevail in the end do you think?
                            It really depends on the game and which of the two gamelines is taking the spotlight. My money would be on the Syndicate in the long run in both, but not without enormous collateral damage including a Technocratic Civil War. It depends on the rules of engagement for each side, how much collateral they're willing to cause (and it's pretty much an absolute given that Pentex's upper management is willing to cause a lot, lot more when it comes to human lives and the environment than all but the coldest Syndicate members. Only one of them is working towards the literal eradication of existence, afterall) and whether or not, in the Syndicate's case, the rest of the TU gets involved and if they can keep them from reacting with justifiable outrage and trying to destroy the Syndicate as well.

                            It comes down again to the issue of how deeply entrenched Pentex is, assuming the WtA version is accurate for this. It can be gotten rid of, but not easily. If it came to a real fight, they'd lose - but they're going to be banking on being too big to fail in the eyes of the hypercapitalists of the Syndicate, along with their blackmail material and their ability to do enormous damage on the way down. The only 'safe' way to do it is to slowly but surely unwind them from the greater economy like a guinea worm from a foot, or to completely eradicate and replace them in a single movement. That movement might be doable for the Syndicate as a total entity working the same extended ritual at the same time, but it would almost certainly cause the sort of Paradox backlash to make the 2006 Incident seem like a minor economic hiccup, if not one that would outright eradicate the entire Convention. That's if such a thing is even possible with Pentex's spiritual backing power in play.

                                        Pretty much... if it even exists. The WoD is crossover friendly mechanically and as far as making nods and winks at each other, but the games pretty much ignore each other once the details start getting worked out. Werewolf and Mage are probably the nastiest about this. But even in Vampire, the "mysterius technomages" who are all presented as Hitmarks are hinted to be a part of some secret organisation. It's just that on the scale of Vampire and how much they are integrated into society, the Technocracy of Mage has to be vastly smaller and vastly less influential.

                                        On the other hand the Vampires are much less influential and everywhere in Mage then they are in Vampire.

                                        The big bad technomagical threat in Werewolf are the agents of the Weaver, and while they on the surface might look like Technocrats, they are decidedly not such. I.e. their agents are Drones, their MiB's turn into goo on death and their operatives all act in perfect unison to the The One Song of the Weaver. There are some nods to the Technocracy as a possible antagonist, but it's mostly ignored by a vast majority of Werewolf books.
