                                              Easily my favorite Tradition is the Order of Hermes.

                                              Setting aside I like the idea of the traditional fantasy trope of the Wizard wearing a pointed hat, I made brief forays into Real Life Hermetic practices back when I first got into Mage. (Coming at MtAs through Vampire, by way of the Tremere, helped bias me toward this). I didn't get very far into these studies, but I found the systems of the Golden Dawn fascinating. And what we call "Hermetic" magic is a massive goulash of dozens of independent magical traditions (Alchemy, Kabbala, Tarot, Astrology, Enochian, etc.), giving Hermetic Mages a wide arsenal to draw from.
