    Absolutely, it could have been done without all of the colonialist and related stuff. And you wouldn't even have needed to go with a different group to accomplish that. If you read The sorcerer's crusade material, you'll note that the Craftmasons are depicted as the conscience of the Order of Reason; they're the ones who are looking at some of the goals of the other Conventions and saying, “that might not be such a good idea”, while organizing themselves in a way to counter the worst impulses of their fellow Conventions. From what I've heard, the authorial intent was that the purge of the Craftmasons was the point that the Order of Reason lost its way.

    In an alternate history where the Craftmasons were more successful in tempering the worst impulses of their fellow Conventions, you could easily have a modern day Order of Reason that introduced all of the good things that the Technocracy did in a more humane way.

    but again, the fact that electricity could have been introduced in a more humane way does not mean that it is bad that electricity was introduced.
