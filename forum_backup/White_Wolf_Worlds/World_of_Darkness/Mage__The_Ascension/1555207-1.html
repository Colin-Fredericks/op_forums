    A weird unconventional idea I had when thinking about how Garou legends and magic(k):

    Maybe the Dreamspeaker was taught how to control magick not by humans, initially, but by a Garou Ally and/or by a Spirit related to the tribe (sometimes spirits like to poach mages that are about to awaken - there's a whole camp of the Dreamspeakers based arround this). Of course, this would produce an unorthodox mage, but could also give interesting perspectives. One of the myths of the Garou about magic(k) talks about the original mages stealing their power from the great spirits (that's also a very common idea in many myths: Humanity stealing from the gods), they could've been educated into this.

    Verbena know the Wyck as half-human half-something that were the first to use magick, and mages are their legacy. Spirits/gods it's a possible source for the other half. There are a lot of concepts about how Gods could have mixed with humanity. Maybe humans made a deal to marry with the gods and have these children. If being sensationalist, one could say, the ancient humans seduced the gods to gain their power- and that would be relatable to the Garou mythos. Maybe mages are a a bit of "half-spirit" themselves.

    Extatics, Euhanatos and Akashick are known for scouring the past trough past lives and the Dream. Depending on their beliefs, they could be compatible with the idea of their divergent paths of magick coming as a result of connecting with a big "other", be it divine or enlightened.

    So, the Cabal could be joined to navigate the spiritual realms of history (the collective dream, the wheel of reincarnation), to find traces of this "missing link" of WHOM did the ancestors of mages meet to gain their legacy.

    ...

    This crazy idea it's just to show a seed for a Cabal that I could imagine going to be "out there" and doing things. That could have enemies other than Banes and "Pentex" (or it's subsidiary subsidiaries, as we all know, :P ), maybe history-spirits and past-lives/ancestor spirits, that either don't aprove the goal, or just ward the deepest recesses of history from trespassers, could be their particular antagnoists.
    A Cabal that's maybe peacefull but "figthing" would still easily happen as consequence of "being a mage", and trying to live and learn, and get the resources and mojo for lasting journeys to the far reaches of the Umbra. And that before thinking about the enemies of the Garou that are going to come along with the Ally or the spirit mentor.

                                        Yeah, I've sort of wondered about this myself. I don't think it's common at all for mages to deliberately damage a place of power. One could argue that maybe happened a few times and this is Garou Oral Tradition doing it's job (of preserving grudges).

                                        BUT there's a different way of looking at this:
                                        1rst. One has to remember a Caern it's more than a Node - it's a supernatural ecosystem, and a place of social and religious significance

                                        Sure, most mages won't drain Quintessence to the point of damaging a supernatural place, but they DO drain the "free flowing" Quintessence emanating of such places, and take the Tas. And maybe that's all "draining" means.

                                        I'm not 100% sure if taking a small portion of the free-flowing Quintessence of a Caern damages the Caern or not. I haven't found rules on that, tho I'm sure there were rules somewhere. Sure, Garou do this, sort off, when they regain Gnosis. But Caerns have their own properties on top of that. And maybe tampering with it using magicks could damage it's supernatural nature somehow.

                                        But let's say it doesn't damage the Caern.

                                        Caerns are incredibly sacred beyond their "properties" or "utility". Garou wouldn't even let normal kinfolk enter the Caern itself. Imagine if they're going to tolerate people going there and taking small rocks and stuff like careless tourists . Of course they're not going to let you smoke the place's Gnosis.

                                        Even if it was harmless, it's incredibly offensive for the Garou, and for the Spirits that made deals with the Garou.

                                        2nd. Mages definitely do tap into Nodes in more agressive ways.

                                        Technocrats would always "sanitize" a node before sitting on top of it. And that can't be good for the health of the Caern. Connecting a Node to a Realm would permanently drain part of it, and could also be problematic for the whole spirit ecology of the place. Of course Nephandi would wreck the place.

                                        Werewolf stories don't talk about this, specifically ... but then, I think Garou don't need nor want to know how Mages actually have developped a plethora of different ways to wreck or defile a caern. :P

                                        It's enough to know that they DO :P

                                        3rd. Garou don't know enough about magic(k) to know if this horribly disrespectful request is or isn't harmless.

                                        Mages are the only splat that can process any and all supernatural power into Prime. The way others see it, you're taking what makes the place "special" and leaving nothing. Even Demons are unnerved by mages draining the "holly" out of a place

                                        Sure, you can say "this ain't permanent, this is no different than when you guys do it". But they can't know if what you're doing it's actually harmless. And they aren't going to know until it's too late to do anything about it.
                                        So:

                                        Garou aren't going to be open to the idea this is harmless (even if it was). They're paranoid, and the stories about human mages aren't helping

                                        But even if they were, it's still bad because it's their holly place and of their spirits. It would be the difference between you politely asking if you can burn the church with the community inside, and politely asking if you can take a piss on the church and the churchgoers instead ... Either way, it's not a great way to start a conversation with someone already predisposed to think the worst.

One would wish there was a bit more material arround this "conflict", since it's supposed to be such a big deal, to flesh it out as more than just prejudice.

                                                            But there's enough to build uppon, if one wishes to do the homework - so to speak.
