    I figured out Chi Magic!

    08-08-2023, 04:17 AM
    When HDYDT came out, one of its biggest criticisms was Sphere bloat. This was the idea that the book demanded so many effects required far more spheres and at higher rank than was needed previously.

    one of the more noteworthy forms of this was elements in the book that implied one’s paradigm dictated when you needed extra spheres to do otherwise similar effects, this was what was cited for the Shaman’s ‘a bit of spirit’ ‘psychics’ need of Mind, and the ever infamous requirement of Prime 2 for daring to believe in Chi.

    Since then, I have reinterpreted these supposed rulings and found a way for them to make sense. For Shaman, the idea was without spirit, they were directing the spirits of, for example, fire, without direct communication, but Spirit enabled them that line of communication, meaning the flame spirit could take over if agreeable. For psychics, Mind allowed their effect to be more than a one and done. Forces 2 lets you move fire about in a single instant effecg, Mind along with this let you instead have an duration effect where you could telekinetically make fire move as you please. Without mind, the psychic could achieve the same ends, but they would need to roll arete for each and every movement of the flame.

    for a while, I was stumped by how to make sense of Chi magic. Their insistence to add Prime 2 to everything in order to “rearrange your energies” didn’t give much to see what Prime 2 actually did for the effect. Especially as the only mechanical difference between the effect with Prime 2 and without was now you had to spend quintessence just to do it.

    But now I have an answer. What Chi effects do is turn quintessence itself in to a single use trinket.

    to explain, a trinket is a wonder which has a ‘locked’ effect within it, usually some warp of reality like invisibility, reduced gravity, good luck, etc.

    what Chi techniques do is ‘lock’ an effect within your pool of quintessence, such that they manifest upon the expenditure of said quintessence. This enchantment on your quintessence pool remains for the duration of the effect

    lets use Chi Flight as an example. Life 2/Forces 2/Prime 2

    the effect allows you to do some of the wu shu style jumps to get to impossible hights and breadths.


    the Forces 2 allows you to suspend gravity for flight while Life 2 lets you modulate your weight in lock step, letting you jump about light as a feather, as is seen in many wu shu films where the fighters are jumping from tree branch to tree branch. Forces alone lets you make the jump, and if you affect the whole area, help with the landing, and Life 2 alone can make you weigh less allowing for easier jumps and landings in a way that only impacts you, though the two together allow you to jump and land with the grace the films portray without changing gravity for everyone in the area.

    however, you would need to cast this at the point of use. What I propose Prime 2 adds to the effect is it lets you enchant your own pool of quintessence with these effects, allowing you to cast the effect ahead of time, hold it for the duration, and then let it go upon the expenditure of the enchanted quintessence point.

    this would allow you to do this chi flight effect multiple times during the scene with only a single cast just by spending the point of quintessence.

    this can be applied to Chi Healing as well, essentially making quintessence behave like a vampire’s bloodpool for the purposes of healing for the duration of the scene.

          My first thought is that no single point of quintessence from your pool is now ‘locked up’ by the effect. This is because I see quintessence as a fairly fluid energy. As long as it comes from within, it is a part of your energy that the effect draws from.

          This means if you use quintessence for other purposes, you simply have less of this energy to draw from for you active chi effect, but you also don’t partition away quintessence for specific uses. So you could have a few chi effects running in duration and only trigger one of them a few times by spending quint as and when desire.

          I imagine this technique differs from Time 4 style suspended effects in the following key ways

          1. Time 4 triggers are inflexible once cast. Chi effects don’t have to be set off once cast, they can simply be on going effects you have access to but choose not to use, or choose to use several times until your exhaust your quintessence.

          2. Time 4 effects don’t need you there to be set off, Chi effects are still centred on you and your interactions. It is in this way much more similar to operating a Wonder than it is delaying an effect.

          3. Time 4 effects don’t require active concentration to maintain, but arguably Chi effects should. This gets a bit subjective as people can say technically any effect that has duration does not contribute to multiple effects limit, but I would argue that effects that require active concentration to be maintained are not included in this. With that interpretation in mind, concentrating your chi for an extended period like this takes your attention, and as such I would suggest it will contribute to that climbing difficulty.

          4. Time 4 effects will work regardless of your quintessence pool. Chi effects are entirely dependent on the quality of your quintessence. This means resonance will be a heavy factor. If your quintessence source matches your own, either because it comes from your personal pool, your life pool via haarts blood, or from a soulgem, then all should be well. If it comes from a node, periapt, tass, the air, or another person, then that resonance will have an impact on how the effect itself manifests, this will be very much up to the ST how that plays out.

          Regarding paradox. There are two ways to play it.

          one way is to use Wonder rules, where paradox may be accrued upon using the wonder (spending the quint). Whether it is coincidental or vulgar depends on how it looks at the point of expenditure. And beyond the initial cast, would not risk botches.

          second way is to use Time 4 casting rules. If there is a possibility for the effect to be vulgar once set off, then it is a vulgar effect, even if it never actually gets set off. If you want to cast it as a coincidental effect, then you simply won’t be able to use quint for that effect to do vulgar things.

                                      I like the principle of what Saikou is suggesting; but I'm not so fond of the implementation. Here's my take on Chi Magick:

                                      Chi Magick is a way to imbue mundane Abilities with Magickal Effects. Chi Flight isn't Flight; it tweaks reality in ways that allows Athletics to do something that it normally couldn't: run up walls, or along tree-tops, or across the surface of a pond; that sort of thing. The brute force approach to this would be to use Forces •• to almost-but-not-quite-fly; but Chi Magick uses a subtler approach by imbuing the ability to move along surfaces that would normally not support him into his Athletics. Likewise, Chi Healing imbues Medicine with supernatural potency, letting it go beyond the normal bounds of what Medicine can normally accomplish. The War-Power Chi Shout imbues Intimidation with a supernatural menace. And so on.

                                      The game system for this is rather straightforward: by incorporating Prime •• into the Effect and spending a point of Quintessence per use, the mage gets to add a suitable Ability to the dice pool for the Effect: for example, you don't roll Arete; you roll Arete+Athletics.

                                      It's not used all the time because it's expensive; Quintessence is something of a limited resource. But it has the potential to make your dice pool much larger, and thus pull off more potent Feats of Magick without having to resort to rituals.

                                      This is in keeping with some of the reinterpretations of Prime that started with Convention Book: Syndicate, where Prime takes on a more abstract sense than “merely” being a mystical energy. If you can “draw power” from human activities, then you should be able to imbue human activities with power.

                                      In a nutshell: this isn't “Abilities Enhancing Magick” or “Magick Enhancing Abilities”; it's “Magickal Abilities”.

                                                          Originally posted by Dataweaver View Post
                                                          Chi Magick is a way to imbue mundane Abilities with Magickal Effects. Chi Flight isn't Flight; it tweaks reality in ways that allows Athletics to do something that it normally couldn't: run up walls, or along tree-tops, or across the surface of a pond; that sort of thing. The brute force approach to this would be to use Forces •• to almost-but-not-quite-fly; but Chi Magick uses a subtler approach by imbuing the ability to move along surfaces that would normally not support him into his Athletics. Likewise, Chi Healing imbues Medicine with supernatural potency, letting it go beyond the normal bounds of what Medicine can normally accomplish. The War-Power Chi Shout imbues Intimidation with a supernatural menace. And so on.

                                                          The game system for this is rather straightforward: by incorporating Prime •• into the Effect and spending a point of Quintessence per use, the mage gets to add a suitable Ability to the dice pool for the Effect: for example, you don't roll Arete; you roll Arete+Athletics.
                                                          While I like the idea of an effect that creates a new, entirely-impossible enhancement, that game system for it doesn’t look like it fits, to me.

                                                          There is already a mechanism for spending Quintessence to improve an effect: -1 difficulty to the Arete roll per Quintessence. While it could be in keeping with general principles to allow the addition of a Sphere to allow the use of more advantageous mechanics, adding a whole new ability to the Arete roll will often double the casting dice pool. It seems like an outsized boost for Prime 2, and not really very representative of the effect. How does those extra dice rolled translate into the new application of an ability?
