                                        Just for fun:

                                        The formula for the Schwarzschild Radius of a black hole is r = 2GM / c²
                                        G and c are constants. Density is implicit, with a smaller radius for a larger mass (M). That also means you need less mass the smaller your black hole is. For a micro-black hole with a Schwarzschild Radius of 0.1 mm you would still need the mass equivalent of the Moon or a large asteroid. So I guess you could make really, really tiny black holes with Matter 3 - which would evaporate in a few picoseconds, but hey.

                                        I think that Sphere levels all have and need implicit effects from other Spheres (or properties outside their scope) to function. A chorister who turns water to wine or lets it rain manna from heaven doesn't care about physical intricacies that prerequisite or accompany those effects. A hermetic might care about the temperature of liquid nitrogen, and I have no problem with Paradigm making some effects simpler or more difficult to achieve.

                                            I’m actually inclined to say that density here is, in praxtical terms, more a question of hardness for solids, i.e. how structurally solid something is. You’re essentially compressing as much of it together as you can, but it will be within reason.

                                            for gases this will be a question of thickness, creating a dense fog vs a diffuse mist.

                                            for water I can see this as sort of like imposing a temporary change in local atmospheric pressure in a specific part of the water. The scale of which dependent on the magical feats table.

                                            as a point, I don’t see pressure as a Forces pattern but more a consequence of how much matter is around, just as falling isn’t a force, bur a consequence of gravity. You could increase or decrease pressure with Forces, but also just with Matter.

                                            But when quintessence gets involved, I’d say that these sort of things get easily mitigated as a result of incorporating the essence of reality. The object simply manifests itself as though it had always been there, no displacement needs be considered.

                                            Thinking about it, I now realise why 2e suggested changing state required Prime 2 along with Forces. If you want to increase density while maintaining volume as the thing changes state, you may need to create more matter.

                                            this would certainly be necessary for Liquid Nitrogen.

                                            this would mean another application for homogenous objects though would also be to shrink them down or expand them. They would weigh the same amount of course, but would be a way to carry things in a more covert manner (complex matter patterns such as guns, machines, or buildings would probably require Matter 4)

                                            the black hole argument though…I would say simply no. Especially if we consider that, in Mage, Forces patterns are distinct from Matter patterns.Gravity is a distinct thing not dependent on mass, gravity is only the thing that makes down the direction that it is. In fact you could replace the force with ‘directionality’ if it helps. For everything you are likely to create with Matter on earth, the difference is essentially non existence. On that note, making something more dense by magic does not affect how hot it is. In fact temperature is stated as specifically something that goes unaffected by Matter. So unless you apply Forces the matter pattern simply does not affect the forces patterns around it. Instead it might be affect by the forces patterns around it. Physics need not be considered.

                                            use of the spheres very much bypasses the impact the physics might otherwise expect.

                                            and I would be very reluctant to allow paradigms to change this, mainly because such a thing easily leads to paradigm specific sphere requirements such as the ever loathed Chi Healing rote which mixes up how Focus and Spheres interact in the first place.
