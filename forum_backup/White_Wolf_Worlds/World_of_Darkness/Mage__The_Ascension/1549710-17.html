                    Matter 5 doesn't specific you need Prime to make things outside of the scope of mundane science (it only uses Prime in one example). Using Matter 5 to make a blaster is probably more of a problem in terms of things like recharging/reloading it since that's also going to take magic on some level.

                    If you took Star Wars (since it actually tried to explain how its blasters work in a manner that could pass as realistic) as inspiration, the blaster fires a bolt of highly charged plasma excited by an integral laser. Those blasters generally require the follow that's beyond mundane science: a chamber that can contain the rapid charge and expulsion of plasma bolts (no reason Matter 5 can't do this alone), a gas that is both compressible enough to be practical and capable of turning into a plasma state that can carry enough energy to be a lethal projectile (also something Matter 5 covers alone), a laser strong enough at that size (also Matter 5 as long as we're getting the energy from somewhere else), and then an energy cell to supply the weapon and specifically the laser (here you can argue Matter + Forces or Prime as we're finally getting beyond exotic materials and into harnessing some serious juice).

                    That said, it's a lot more work than using Forces 3/Prime 2, and a blaster as an instrument instead. If a Matter 5 fictional gas is your ammo, then you have weapon that's a lot of effort to reload.

                                        Wow, we are into Mind now.


                                        Base Mind


                                        Purview: The Mind, the Astral, Consciousness, Illusion, Communication, Psychic powers

                                        Special Rules and Limitations

                                        1 - Mind attacks are limited to bashing damage unless other spheres are involved.

                                        2 - Mind magic is coincidental unless other spheres are involved

                                        3 - When Mind effects target an individual entity, Difficulty is based on the target’s Willpower rather than the usual calculation. Drastic mind control and effects targeting mages or other supernaturals are at a +3 difficulty. The target can try to resist with Willpower.

                                            Mind 1

                                            • Sense Thoughts & Emotions/Mind Shield/Empower Self

                                            With basic mental magick, the mage learns to sense the emotions and surface impulses of other people. Although he cannot read specific thoughts, he’s able to perceive psychic impressions about a person or (with Matter 1) a place or object. Through this perception, he can guess at the nature of weak Resonance signatures and read the stronger ones outright. Even without Resonance, that mage can scan auras, note mood shifts, discern truth from lies, or grasp someone’s overall state of mind by way of a successful Arete roll.

                                            Meanwhile, the mage also learns to shield his own mind from the thoughts and emotions of other people, constructing mental barriers around his aura, emotions, and consciousness. Each success on a coincidental Arete roll removes one success from any other character’s attempt to read those psychic elements.

                                            On a related note, he also learns how to multitask and absorb data with startling acuity. Each success he rolls allows him to either consider an additional subject or speed the processing time that a normal person might require. For a scene or two, he can even raise one of his Mental Traits by one dot per success, thanks to a Mind Empowerment Effect that concentrates his mental faculties.

                                            Mind is one of those spheres that gets a lot more than just senses at rank 1.

                                            Sense Emotion and Presence of Thoughts (Sense Thoughts & Emotions)


                                            With this power a mage can sense emotions and thoughts, or at least the presence of thoughts with maybe some information thrown in. It is possible that reading emotions is supposed to be similarly hazy, but that is not how it is worded here. Though limited in information, the mere ability to sense the presence of thoughts is quite powerful as it can alert the mage to who is around him.

                                            Also, while impressions of thoughts are limited, they is enough to determine intents and distinguish truth from lies, all of which are quite powerful in a social situation.


                                            The Life or Death of the Party

                                            The various emotions and intentions that Mind 1 can sense can easily help a mage control a social situation.


                                            Identify Trigger

                                            While Mind 1 cannot itself make attacks, it can help a mage to know vulnerable points for social attacks.


                                            Sense Psychic Impressions (Sense Thoughts & Emotions)


                                            A subtly different power than sensing thoughts, this ability lets a mage sense the psychic impressions and resonances left by minds. He can read these automatically off of people, though needs Matter to read them off of things. Whether one person can leave these impressions on another is not stated, though it sounds plausible. From the text it sounds like this is subtly different from reading auras, though from the sphere writeups alone it is hard to tell.

                                            That said, I’m going to include reading auras as a part of this power as it is similar enough that it doesn’t need its own section.


                                            Sense Arcanoi Remnants

                                            A mage should be able to, with difficulty, sense the remnants of Pathos and Angst used in various Arcanoi. Without help, I would limit this to Arcanoi that cross the Shroud.


                                            Mental Shield (Mind Shield)


                                            A mage can also use Mind 1 to shield himself from attempts to read and influence his mind. Defense of this kind usually doesn’t come until rank 2, but it makes a lot of sense here from a gameplay standpoint.


                                            Cool as Ice

                                            Oddly enough, while the one mechanical use is a defense against mind reading, the actual description is much broader. It reads like it includes incidental thoughts and emotions too. Practically this won’t come up much with thoughts, but imprints and resonance can have an effect. If, say, an area has enough resonance or impression to cause fear and anxiety in a person, Mind 1 can let a mage shrug that off.


                                            Boost Mind (Empower Self)

                                            This is such a notable power, at least in terms of day-to-day life, that I suspect most mages who have it abuse it. I certainly would. A mage can give himself the ability to multitask, speed his thought processes, and boost his mental stats. Honestly, there are a lot more mental processes that are likely enhanceable. In game terms it is a little bit less powerful as most changes would fall into the mechanics already stated.


                                            Brute Force Pattern Recognition (AKA Accuracy Through Volume, Mind Edition)

                                            By increasing multitasking a mage can run through more ideas and comparisons at a time. Even without boosting mental stats, this means he can go through more possibilities in the same amount of time, which increases his chances of discovering something.


                                            Invalidate Your Fellow Players' Experiences

                                            On a technical level, Mind 1 is capable of ‘curing’ many neurodivergencies and other conditions. It is also capable of replicating them (arguably Brute Force Pattern Recognition replicates part of the ADHD experience. It’s based on mine). Both of these need to be handled responsibly, especially if someone at the table has one of those neurodivergencies.

                                            Despite the name there are reasons for actually using these effects. The name is just there to point out that context and communication matter a lot.


                                            Conjunctional Effects:

                                            There is one listed.

                                            Sense Psychic Impressions in Places and Things (Sense Thoughts & Emotions + Matter Perceptions)

                                            I still find it interesting that reading impressions on objects requires Matter and reading impressions on people does not require Life. I don’t see this as a contradiction, though. People have minds and they respond to the minds and actions of others. Psychic impressions on mindless creatures isn’t covered, so it is ST call whether reading them off of, say, moss requires Life. The question is trivial if the mage has Matter, since there are always objects around, but it might matter if he only has Life.

                                            Mage: The Ice-ension: An Epic Game of Reality on the Rink
