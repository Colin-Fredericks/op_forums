          MRev's Initiates of the Art has a Dual Tradition Merit that you can pick up to represent a character who has joined more than one Tradition. M20's Book of Secrets revises that so that it's less about multiple Traditions and more about a dual Focus, which is part of M20's shift away from one's Tradition being the keeper of one's paradigm and practice: in MRev, your Tradition comes first, and your paradigm and practice follow; in M20, your Paradigm and Practice come first, and your Tradition (if any) follows.

          So to be clear: what are you actually interested in: switching your Tradition, or shifting your Focus?

          My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

                  As Dataweaver said, in M20 Tradition is much more of a political choice than a magical one. I'd say there's a fairly easy story to get from start to finish there:

                  Fall out with the Order of Hermes as a Solificati.
                  Spend some time with the Children of Knowledge (the Solificati that didn't join the Order).
                  Learn about a way more experimental and experiential approach to alchemy.
                  Decide that the Traditions are still the best place for you.
                  Ask the Cult for membership and impress them with some of the sweet shit the CoK taught you on your break.
