                    Decipher Notes [Mind 3] This Procedure of the New World Order requires notes - which can be handwritten, printed, or digital - of a person. Using the notes as a focus, the Technocrat locks on the person in question and glimpses understanding of the notes, including overcoming any ciphers used. More successes scored result in a greater amount of information. This Procedure is almost always Coincidental - after all, a lot could be guessed from a few pages somebody left behind. This Procedure can be used from afar with added Correspondence.

                        Form of the Succubus [Life 5] The Mage assumes the alluring and beautiful form of a mythological temptress. (Or tempter - the Mage can opt to assume the form of an Incubus) Her body is made shapely and attractive to an impassible degree, her feet end in cloven hooves, nails become more akin to talons, wings sprout from her back, and horns grow from her head. Despite these changes the Mage is still recognizable to those who know her. She gains three dots to spend between Social Attributes per success in this Rote. With Prime 3 added, Attributes can exceed scores of 5. This form lasts for a scene. With Prime 2 and Forces 3 added, flight is possible. Nowadays, this Rote is almost always Vulgar. Whether the Mage becomes a Succubus or an Incubus is their own choice - it is not predisposed to the Mage's sex or gender.

                        Obviously, this Rote is very useful for improving the carnal matters of the Mage. Unfortunately, using this Rote among the Traditions and the Technocracy certainly creates grounds for a lengthy and extensive investigation into the Mage, if not for outright accusations of Infernalism. On the other hand, it is extremely useful for a Demonologist as an aid in negotiations and confrontations with beings from below.

                                    Perceive Higher Paranoia [Mind 2 or Mind 3] This Procedure of the New World Order is a superb tool that allows a Technocrat to read thoughts and feelings of persons in regard to perhaps one of the most valuable kinds of information - the thoughts of a person has in regard to the New World Order itself. As a focus for this Procedure, a conversation needs to be struck with the target on the topic of conspiracies, plots, worries etc. The smallest idiosyncrasies in a person's speech and behavior provide the Technocrat with leverage through which she or he accesses the target's mind in regard to the NWO, using a specialized apparatus.
                                    The functionality of this Procedure can, at request, be included in various Hypertechnological Foci used by Technocrats of the NWO.

                                    The Mind 3 version allows for more distinct information to be learned, while the Mind 2 version scans the target for fleeting and relatively vague impressions.

                                            Seek out the Big Fish [Correspondence 3] - this Procedure of the Syndicate locates persons who are in charge of and are profiteering from unlawful activities which are part of the so called criminal underworld.                                                 Catch the Song [Prime 1 or Entropy 1] - this simple Rote pioneered by Ecstatics divines the title and the author of a song (or another piece of music) the Mage is currently listening to. Though simple to cast, some Ecstatics have been known to tire themselves out or suffer Paradox Backlashes after they used this Rote again and again at clubs or raves. The Syndicate does have an Adjustment which achieves the same result through principles of Enlightened Science.

                                                This Rote requires more success if the title and author of the piece of music somehow became unknown, with just the melody and the words being passed on.
                                                If the author at the time of composing remained anonymous, then this Rote will not inform the Mage who the author is or was. If a piece of music was created under a pseudonym or an alias, then this is what will be known. Likewise, if the author-given title of a piece of music is generally unknown, then it cannot be divined; only information known publically is provided by this Rote.

                                                        Track Belongings [Correspondence 1 or 2, Matter 1] - This Rote scans the nearest surroundings (the size of a drawer, a room, or a house, depending on the amount of success) for items belonging to a specific person. An important item belonging to the person in the recent past has to be used as a focus for this Effect. This Rote can be tuned to look for all sorts of items - these of mundane utility, of specific type or size, of a particular degree of importance etc. With enough success, particular items (keys to a car, a specific book, favorite writing implement) can be detected. There is also the option for discovering all nearby items belonging to the person.
                                                        This Rote is very useful in saving time while searching a place and when looking for hidden or misplaced items.

                                                        Using Correspondence 1 in this Rote allows for sensing items in the nearest vicinity (at most an area around the size of a house), while the Correspondence 2 variant allows for use of the Rote while scrying on distant location.
