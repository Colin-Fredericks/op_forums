        Here's how I understand it from my limited reading.

        The Consensus is made up of a few things. Basic Reality as determined by the laws of nature. Then you can add to that the viewpoints of Sleepers to get a more local consensus.

        The Earth IS round. However, if everyone in the world believed it was flat, and every Mage worked together to try and make it flat, that could work. Never happened however.

        The viewpoints of sleepers don't shape the world or "cast" magic. They are effectively like counter-magic for the big constants. Sleepers know that the moon doesn't just fall out of the sky, the sea doesn't boil with a click of your fingers, and so their views shape how a Mage can alter reality. They don't, however, shape reality.

        For example, around the time the theory of the 4 Humours was abandoned and modern medical science was becoming more and more accepted, the bile, pus and blood in every human in the relevant Reality Zones didn't transmute into something else. Gay people existed in times where the consensus was that they didn't, as another example.

        Simply put, you have a basal reality which can be explained multiple ways. Maybe it's all code, maybe it's all one big machine, maybe it's all one big Spiritual Entity or a creation of Allah/The Seven Heavens/God/Your Pantheon. Maybe none of that matters. But at the end of the day what goes up must come down, basic Newtonian Physics, celestial bodies etc. etc. are not up for debate. A Mage can play with these things (using Forces to stop Newtonian Physics, with some excuse about magnets or some made-up-effect (a surname followed by Effect or Law usually works)) but they cannot rewrite these things. Sleepers can be wrong about these things (and their being wrong can help a Mage; people with a sloppy grasp of physics can help a Forces user to remain Coincidental) but they cannot change these things.

                                    Originally posted by 11twiggins View Post

                                    So you see it as one paradigm simply having become dominant, and mundane science's efficacy is just an extension of that? Well that is perfectly reasonable, even if it's opposed to another perfectly reasonable view. Do the books ever fall on one or the other? Like "yes, science is all made up and is just as much a meaningless (or meaningful) paradigm as that of any random Mage".
                                    Yes, and I'd say there's plenty of evidence for it. Or, well, it depends. Let me split that into three questions:

                                    1) Does the modern world look and act like it does because of what modern people believe?

                                    This one is pretty certain. Between the descriptions of Consensual Realty, the descriptions of Reality Zones, and the rules for determining vulgarity, it's pretty well established. There are also numerous other nods throughout the books too, such as Traditions claim credit for getting the sleepers to accept certain ideas and technologies (Sons of Ether claim the EMP and Uncertainty), and Technocratic principles forever rejected by the Consensus and Paradox after the Czar Vargo event.

                                    I'd go so far as to say this is one of the core ideas of Mage.


                                    2) Does the Consensus make things real?

                                    This on the other hand...

                                    This one has been asked in a lot of forms. Is anything real? Is everything real? Is reality fully under the control of the Consensus? Is there a baseline reality that the Consensus overrides but doesn't erase?

                                    It's been asked a lot because the books are considerably less certain. While I could certainly mine up some quotes about the bulk of reality being defined by the Consensus, there are also references to Cosmological Constants and things that are remarkably hard to change. I do think Mage 2nd was written with the idea that the Consensus makes things real, but it does not say so definitively and M20 certainly doesn't invalidate anything it doesn't have to.

                                    So in this case the Consensus could make things happen but still be dead wrong. There could be a strong, well-defined baseline reality behind Consensual Reality.


                                    3) If there is a baseline reality, is it the one defined by our modern scientific understanding?

                                    It could be, but probably not. There are references, even in M20, of modern technology not working in the distant, unpopulated areas of the world. Technocratic Reality is considered to be different than Primal Reality. Mythic threads keep mostly forgotten magic alive. So there's good reason to believe that the Technocrats are not right*.

                                    However, gravity is often cited as one of the Earthly Foundations and the need for Prime 2 to create things out of whole cloth implies a kind of Conservation of Matter and Energy. So if there is a strong baseline reality, I'd say it isn't our reality but it isn't completely alien to it either.

                                    *Or, to paraphrase Douglas Adams, they are definitively right. In cases of major discrepancy it is reality that has it wrong.
