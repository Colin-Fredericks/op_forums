                    With Mana Manipulation 2, you can learn a Ritual that allows you to store your mana in a potion, which requires an action to consume, so you are running into issues of action economy. With Mana Manipulation 3, you can stored one point of your Mana in non-consumable objects (it does not say you get free Mana from the environment). You cannot drain environmental Mana until Mana Manipulation 4, when you can drain Mana from Nodes or people. Mana Manipulation is really only useful for people with the Mana Background, as the Mana Background allows you to generate personal Mana. Since Mana Manipulation requires 1 Willpower per use, you also start running into Willpower economy issues as well as Mana production issues.

                            Sort of bad economics for Mana Manipulation though. Since you only can only get 'free' Mana from nodes and the 'free' Mana only lasts for one turn per success, you are unlikely to ever come out ahead. I think Mana Manipulation is a rather weak Numina and I think that it is better to just have a simple recharge for the Mana Background (Meditation or Sex works well for most practitioners).

                            When it comes to level 5 Numina, I really cannot think of a worse investment than Mana Manipulation. You could store some extra Mana, yes, but you have spent 35 freebie points to augment a Background when you could spend 1 freebie point on Ability Aptitude (X), which will give you a permanent -2 difficulty and 5 freebie points on Allies, which would give you five assistants to help reduce the difficulty of your magic rolls. I think that there are much better investments of your time and effort.

                                                            Had an idea for a Sorcerer PC/NPC

                                                            Sister Joan

                                                            "I can't tell you if dogs go to heaven, but I can tell you that you can see the weight of a man's soul in how he treats dumb beasts"
                                                            "I'm here to look after your physical, emotional and spiritual health love. I'm not here to "kick ass"."
                                                            "They call it Magick. With a K. I've come to realise the K represents the trace amounts of Krazy that come with it."

                                                            A mid-ranking Sorcerer serving the Celestial Choir, her role is three-fold. She is a First Aider for groups of Mages sent on dangerous missions, a psychologist assigned to assist with mental degeneration and a pastoral (and yes, religious) carer. Having been crucial in rescuing several Mages from the edge of becoming Marauders, she is well-respected by those who have heard of her.

                                                            Abandoned in Warsaw in 1983, she grew up in an orphanage. She had a bizarre affinity with animals and was repeatedly punished for beating up bullies roughly twice her size. Precocious and head-strong, she managed to work part-time through a college education and obtain a degree in psychoanalytics. When she joined the Catholic Church as a Lay Nun in 1996 she took up a new name, as is traditional in many orders. Studying first aid with the Red Cross, she volunteered for several dangerous humanitarian missions. One such mission to war-torn Eritrea led to a very strange encounter. Children were being snatched away in the night, from various towns, villages and travelling communities. Upon investigation, she managed to track down a child who had just gone missing (feeling as though she was being guided by some unseen force). The creature holding the boy was a Nightmare come to life. A hideous, almost rotting creature with fangs and inhuman strength. "The child won't have felt a thing" was the mantra she repeated for years following that night she simply wasn't able to save him. With the child dispatched, the creature leaped at her. She would have died that night, had it not been for a miracle. The creature couldn't bear to taste her blood. By some intervention she was anathema to it. Mustering all of her strength, she beat the creature until it stopped moving, and then watched in satisfied horror as it burnt to ashes as the dawn approached.

                                                            From here on, she devoted herself to the faith, becoming a fully devoted Nun and studying religious doctrine. If creatures like that existed, there had to be someone to protect the flock. She learned scripture until she could recite it, and her faith became like a flame in her heart, a flame that she could rain down on the sinful. It wasn't long before her miraculous healing powers drew the attention of a Chorister, and her new life began...

                                                            In terms of the Character Sheet, Joan is a lost Revenant of the Bratovich line, abandoned upon birth (for some unknown reason). She has no clue about this, having always put her abnormal healing and strange abilities down to divine blessing (for some reason she felt compelled not to talk to others about her powers, which included speaking with animals). She has skill in the Path of Hellfire and the Path of Healing primarily, with some minor understanding of Mana Manipulation and Alchemy. She is skilled in First Aid, and combining this with Healing she is a potent 'support character'. Her own Soak abilities and rapid healing factor, along with her grounded sanity (there are benefits to avoiding Magick) mean that she is something of a Constant, a watchman who doesn't need watching. At ST Discretion she may have True Faith, if there are enough points left (and the ST has enough good will).

                                                            Roleplaying Tips: You're Matron, the rest of the group are very, very clever children. Be affectionate and harsh in equal measure, and always watch their backs. Keep an eye on their mental health, you've seen how terrible the Quiet can get. You are angered by foolish use of Magick, and you tend to view Paradox as a fair backlash against idiocy. On some deeper level you are jealous of the flexibility of Magick, but you accept that God has chosen this path for you. You feel blessed for those you've helped, you are haunted by those you didn't manage to save.

                                                            Healing: 3 or higher. May be replaced with True Faith if its M20 healing abilities (an optional benefit) are allowed.
                                                            Hellfire: 3 or higher. A last resort.
                                                            Alchemy: 1 or higher. Mostly for mixing up actual medicine in a herbalistic style.
                                                            Mana Manipulation: 1 or higher. Used for detecting the Supernatural. May be replaced with True Faith if its M20 "sense evil" abilities (an optional benefit) are allowed.

                                                            Focus: Prayer is the core focus of her work. Her powers mostly come from faith and scripture. The only exception is her Herbalism, which is a mixture of arts.

                                                            Animalism 1: Simply allows you to speak with and (within limits) command animals.
                                                            Potence 1: You have +1 Strength and can spend 1 Blood for an automatic success on Strength rolls.

                                                            Abilities: High ratings in Academics, Psychiatry, Medicine, First Aid, Herbalism and Occult. Awareness, Athletics, Empathy and other basic skills involved in a Pastoral role are desirable. The "Prayer" ability (meditation by another name) can recharge Mana and Willpower. Some abilities related to Animals, such as Animal Ken and Ride are relevant.

                                                            Backgrounds:
                                                            Mana: 5
                                                            Whatever best reflects the respect she is afforded for her Pastoral care.

                                                            Merits: Blood Magic. May fuel Sorcery with Blood if she is out of Willpower. Animal Affinity. True Faith 1 (ST Discretion).
