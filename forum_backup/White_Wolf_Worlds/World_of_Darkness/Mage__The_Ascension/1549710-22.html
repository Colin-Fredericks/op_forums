                            Originally posted by Dataweaver View Post
                            M20 doesn't say that Prime • can't do it; it also doesn't say that it can.
                            Strictly speaking, Primal Utility does say that a mage has to have Primal Utility to access the Quintessence in Ventures. Specifically "Only a character with at least one dot in Primal Utility can access the Quintessence from that Venture; another mage might sense the energies but remain unable to access their power."

                            Now, one of the rules of this analysis is that if a Sphere and Rank claims to be able to do something, no other entry can contradict it, so if Prime 1 claimed to be able to access Ventures it really wouldn't matter what Primal Utility said. Prime doesn't, though, so the Primal Venture restriction carries a lot more weight.

                            The only thing that muddies the waters is that Prime 1 merges the list of Quintessence it can perceive and Quintessence it can channel. That said it never actually says that any energy a mage can see with Prime 1 he can also channel with Prime 1. So even though Primal Utility gives Prime the ability to sense the quintessence in a Venture, that doesn't mean Prime also gets the ability to use them.

                            None of this invalidates RBGtM, by the way. That book is carving out an exception to the rules of M20, or rewriting them in one specific aspect and it can stand as an official source. It just doesn't mean much for the purposes of what M20 says.

                                Prime 3

                                [quote]••• Channel Quintessence/ Enchant Life/ Energy Weapon/ Craft Periapts &
                                Temporary Wonders

                                By tapping into the flow of Quintessence around her, the Prime-skilled mage can draw both free and raw Quintessence from Nodes, Junctures (special times), and Tass (solidified Quintessence)… and she may also channel that energy into new and existing Patterns as well. With such powers, she could (with Life 3) enchant a living thing so that it could inflict or endure aggravated damage; inflict aggravated damage by shaking up that organism’s life force; pull small amounts of life force from a living sacrifice or (with Matter 2) from inert objects; instill Quintessence into a vessel called a Periapt; or – with other Spheres – craft temporary Talismans or Devices by infusing them with Primal Force. (Permanent enchantments require Prime 4 – see Crafting Wonders on p. 508, and Quintessence Amounts on p. 507.)

                                In desperate circumstances, a Prime-schooled mage can also create temporary weapons out of pure concentrated energy – blasts of Quintessence or swords of light. Such weapons inflict aggravated damage (as per the Base Damage or Duration chart) and cost one point of Quintessence per use… or, for weapons that last for a length of time, one point per turn. When the mage runs of out Quintessence, the weapon disappears. Unless channeled through energy-guns or conjured as miracles among the faithful, such attacks are inevitably vulgar.[quote]



                                For the record, it really doesn’t help that Prime keeps introducing terms that are never used anywhere else in M20. This rank it is ‘Juncture,’ but future ranks will introduce ‘Soulgem’ and ‘Soulflower.’



                                Channel Available Quintessence (Channel Quintessence)

                                Contradiction Resolution

                                I want to start out by mentioning that channeling quintessence from Nodes and Tass was already something mentioned in Prime 1, so that's a problem. Now looking at the actual Quintessence rules, channeling quintessence is described not as a spell but as a meditation action, with Prime 1 allowing a mage to absorb more Quintessence than his Avatar rating. So one good possibility is Prime 1 essentially enhances the mage’s natural ability while the Prime 3 version magically handles the Quintessence directly. This makes sense as Prime 3 lets a mage move Quintessence to places other than his own pool.

                                The Tass discrepancy is harder as there isn’t an direct statement of how a mage would naturally use Tass. That said, the examples of refilling Quintessence from Tass in the Quintessence section seem to indicate that a mage can absorb appropriate forms of Tass (eating edible Tass, for instance). If so then Prime 1 would let him handle more than his Avatar rating. Meanwhile Prime 3 would let the mage absorb the Quintessence from any form of Tass or channel it anywhere he wants.

                                In addition Prime 3 mentions both available and raw quintessence, it may be able to pull more quintessence than a Node has as available quintessence.

                                One new thing mentioned in Prime 3 is Junctures. Mages can now draw from auspicious moments of particular potency. Prime 3 is the only place in M20 that even mentions Junctures so we never get a good description of what they are and what separates them from Wellsprings. Presumably they are things like alignments of the stars and other auspicious or metaphysically significant moments, but your guess is as good as mine.


                                I’ll Gladly Pay You Tuesday for a Quintessence Burger Today

                                A mage can channel Quintessence where he wants and that includes other mage’s pools. That is a mage can give another mage some of his own Quintessence, or channel the Quintessence from somewhere else into another mage. He may also be able to take from another mage, willingly or unwillingly, although he would not be able to forcibly drain a mage lower than their Avatar rating, it seems.

                                Enchant Creature (Enchant Life)

                                Contradiction Resolution - Wut?


                                Once again, channeling quintessence into a pattern takes a higher rank for living things than objects, and once again it takes Life supplementation. This time it takes Life 3 for all Life patterns, which doesn’t match up to Alteration, Transmutation, or Healing. I can’t say for certain the reasoning here, but when Matter is mentioned in another effect it uses the level for Transmute Simple, and that’s what Life 3 handles as well. But since this is a completely different ruling than in Prime 2, I can’t really tell what Life is doing so I can’t come up with a real resolution.

                                Thoughts?

                                Prime Attack (???)

                                Ambiguity Resolution - The Life of Prime


                                As with most spheres, Prime can be used for attacks, and this damage is automatically aggravated. Based on the placement in the sentence it is tempting to think this requires Life 3, but none of the other elements of the list seem to need Life. Also, Prime naturally does aggravated damage on its own. And even though that is not mentioned in the sphere writeups, we’ve already seen Prime used to augment other attacks into aggravated so we know that’s one of its natural capabilities.

                                We also can’t assume this is a Life attack augmented to do aggravated damage, even though that’s exactly what ‘shaking up the target’s life pattern’ sounds like. Life naturally does aggravated damage, so it certainly wouldn’t need help from Prime. So it really looks like this wasn’t meant to be a conjunctional effect.


                                Quintessence Vampire

                                The aformentioned unwilling draining of Quintessence, if it is a thing, would likely be handled as a Prime attack that targets the Quintessence pool rather than the Health pool.

                                Pull Quintessence From Pattern (Channel Quintessence)


                                Now Prime 3 can pull life force from living targets, or with Matter 2 from inert objects. Now if we were just talking about pulling quintessence out of a pattern, this would not make sense. After two ranks of effects that require Life for living things but not Matter for objects, this would be a strange turn. There is an important word, though: sacrifices. That indicates that this is meant to be a destructive act, not just a little drain. The reason, then, that creatures don’t require Life is that the mage is ending them in a non-magical way. Now I still don’t quite understand why breaking or melting or in some other way destroying an object doesn’t also work, but I can at least see the thought process.


                                Snuffing Out the Candle

                                Once again, the third pattern sphere is forgotten, but Prime should be just as capable as drawing from energies in the right circumstances. The hard part is engineering the ‘destruction’ of significant enough sources of energy. Despite the name I chose, a single candle isn’t likely enough.

                                Create Temporary Wonders (Create Temporary Wonders)


                                At Prime 3 a mage can craft a temporary talisman, which is different from a regular talisman because… because…

                                Ambiguity Resolution? - Temporary Talismans

                                The problem? M20 never defines what a temporary talisman is. It could be a Charm that is expended when used. It could be a talisman with a non-refuelable quintessence pool. It could be a talisman that only lasts for a set duration. All three would seem to fit the description.


                                I Prepared Explosive Runes Today

                                Perhaps the simplest example of a temporary talisman is one that destroys itself in the process of working its wonders.

                                Craft Periapt or Use Periapt (Craft Periapt)

                                Contradiction Resolution - Making or Using


                                At Prime 3 a mage can channel quintessence into a periapt. The language gets confused here. From the text it sounds like this is meant to fill an existing periapt with quintessence. From the headers, though, Prime 3 can Craft Periapts. From Prime 4 and 5 it appears that was supposed to be a higher rank power (though Prime 4 is muddled on this), but it is explicit here.

                                Added: The sticking point is Soulgems and Soulflowers from Prime 4 and 5 respectively. I'll cover this more when we get to them, but Soulgems are written as if they are something different from periapts, but Soulflowers are written as if they (and by extention, Soulgems) are the same. If they are something different, there is no conflict, Prime 3 is the rank for periapts and Prime 4 and 5 are talking about something else.                                 That is consistent with what is written here as it appears that Prime 3 is all that is required to create Periapts and that this does not in any way require supplemental spheres.

                                If we do want to split that power between Prime 3 and later ranks, the best resolution I can think of for this is that in the same way that Prime 3 can create Temporary Wonders (whatever they are) it can create Temporary Periapts. These would function like periapts but with the same time (or other) restrictions as temporary wonders.


                                And the Frame is Probably Worth Something Too

                                If the periapt is already a one-time use item then drawing a little bit extra out of it by sacrificing it might be a good move.

                                Manifest Weapon (Energy Weapon)


                                This is a quite expensive power. A mage can expend his personal quintessence to briefly manifest attacks or weapons. These cost 1 quint per turn manifested, or per point of ammunition, whichever is more applicable. It is worth noting that the Common Magical Effects table says 1 quint per point of damage done, but even if that is an option it is by far the worst unless the mage wants to limit the damage he does.


                                Lightsabers

                                Given how overt this spell is, most applications of it are likely vulgar. Ironically, there are enough prop lightsabers around that generating a lightsaber is one of the few applications with a chance of being coincidental. The mage would still have to justify pulling it from seemingly nowhere, but the weapon itself looks more reasonable than a longbow made of light.

                                                    Originally posted by Lord Revan View Post

                                                    How exactly does that works? The mage still needs Node background, right? He can't just tap into Quintessence from any abstract source, right?
                                                    The Node Background is for controlling a Node, not the ability to tap into one. If a PC belongs to a Chantry with a Node, but doesn't take any dots in Node, the PC can still use the Node when the NPCs allow it; it's just at their discretion.

                                                    Primaly Ventures are significantly different from other Nodes and Node-like things in that it's truly abstract. There isn't a place, or a thing, or a person, or a unique set of circumstances, it is reliably connected to that requires you to be present to access. You just need to be associated with the Venture in some fashion (variable based on your investments in the Venture and your Primal Utility dots). A Master of Primal Utility can literally walk into any nation wide restaurant chain, buy a meal, and then be able to access the equivalent of a Rank 4 Node; no Node background required. That said, some Syndicate member is likely not going to be happy about this; but the same could be said about any other case of using Prime to take Quintessence someone else has worked to cultivate.
