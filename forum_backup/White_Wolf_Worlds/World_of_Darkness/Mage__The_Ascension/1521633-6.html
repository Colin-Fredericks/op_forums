            One last argument is that other splats have easy access to natural weapons, and giving mages maneuvers that makes them better at hand to hand gives them at least something. It also fits a very cool matrix style.

                        What is the difficulty of blocking a kick?
                        While up to the ST, I would keep a Kick at 6. Blocking isn't really about trying to just stop an attack, it's about controlling the energy of an attack to minimize the damage dealt. The most basic blocks are all about redirection: you don't try to stop a punch by catching it in your fist, you sweep your arm into their arm at an angle that pushes their arm out of line with your body: either so the strike hits a part of your body more capable of enduring it, or so it misses completely. The same is true of defending against a kick.

                        What I see the book trying to do, is deal with larger objects. I would say a tackle or similar full body contact move would justify being a 7 because redirecting that energy without doing what amounts to a Dodge is significantly harder. Maneuvering yourself into place so you can apply pressure to keep the attack from landing fully is generally going to go up as the size and mass of whatever is coming at you goes up. With a such a small scale of 6 to 8 diff to deal with, the abstraction is going to be significant.
