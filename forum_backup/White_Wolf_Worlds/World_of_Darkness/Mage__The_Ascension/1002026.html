      I don't know if something in this avenue was officially created, but for sure it is completely feasible, and a lot of people use this already.

      Something being immune to condition, or completely negated at some facility, it isn't so simple.

      Few things can be assumed as binary in Mage. As a rule of thumb, you can't rid a Major splat of its Supernatural Type, or kill the Sun or something of similar magnitude. Anything else is feasible. Nothing is automatic. And if one of those things isn't so relevant in your game, they're fair play, too.

      That means that ways to counter condition are perfectly feasible, if you guess correctly how condition will be done, and that condition is a magical work, and can be adapted to deal with any given way to counter it, if the conditioner can guess what protective means were used. The same goes on to any container facility, with procedures for containment being created trying to guess what means of counter-containment will be used to overcome them.

              The SCP Foundation is built off the idea that we shouldn't simply eliminate everything anomalous, but rather seek to make the world safe from it. They don't just throw everything into an incinerator. So the question is - would the Technocracy be more interested in containment or destruction?

              I'd argue destruction. The Technocracy (at least, the higher levels) knows why weird stuff exists - reality is malleable and there are lots of Reality Deviants able to alter it in various ways. Is there really a lot to learn from some Hermetic's talismans and books? If they're doing the Pogrom they'd want to follow leads to his colleagues, but they're not going to be mystified by a bound Umbrood in a pendant the way the Foundation is thrown for a loop by nearly everything it encounters.

              As for the Reality Deviants themselves, if they can't be converted, why would they be of any use? I can imagine a darker Technocracy that drains them of Quintessence first or downloads their mental state to a hard drive for later simulspace interrogation, but there's no upside in keeping a reality-warper that will always be working against you. It wastes resources that could be better spent elsewhere.

              If you really want the Foundation or something like it in your game, you can do a couple different things:
              • Make the world weirder and/or the Technocracy less well-traveled. Maybe there are still plenty of Bygones out there, or legions of random Awakened not involved in the Ascension War and simply doing unique stuff. Or the Technocracy hasn't had nearly the history of success it's had in the books, and it's only now starting to understand vampires, werewolves, fae, etc. That gives your Technocracy a reason to want to know more about what it would find.
              • Give the Technocracy a reason to never give up on conversion. Maybe they hold Reality Deviants in prisons where resonance fields slowly alter allegiances or change perceptions. Now it's worth it to maintain a prison, because you'll get a slow trickle of new Awakened recruits from it.
              • Have reality be breaking down in a new and exciting way. The large-scale consensus is not as successful in homogenizing Earth as they might hope, and things come through the cracks. This puts the Technocracy in a position not of defining reality but of defending it from some sort of infection or decay that it doesn't understand - you'll get a reason for the research plus a good bit of desperation.

                          Technocrats who research Paradox would be the equivalent of the SCP Foundation, I'd say. Even if we assume that Technocrats suffer less Paradox, studying it heavily would still enable them to better manipulate, fight, and aid mystic Mages.                                   I think it might be a bit closer to what the SCP is if it was a separate organization from the Technocratic Union. An organization founded by mundane humans, who are ignorant of Consensual Reality and the larger Ascension War. They were just normal people, who saw weird things happening in the world, and vowed to contain what they see as threats to humanity. Kind of like the Arcanum, except instead of passively observing supernaturals, they act to bottle them up.

                                  Their only magical ability comes from captured items (Talismans, Fetishes, Charms, etc.), and from dedicated researchers studying captured items and creatures, as well as tomes plundered from occultists and the like. Sorcerers can be techno or mythic - indeed, they might mix and match whatever techniques they have available, simply because they are gathering such lore piecemeal. They have a couple scattered facilities devoted to research and containment, and fund themselves by deriving potential miracle products from materials captured. They wouldn't have access to unlimited Class D personnel, since they're probably not connected enough to make death row inmates disappear.

                                  Most probably, they've been infiltrated or courted by some members of the Technocracy, who seek to gain what they can from the Foundation's labs. The Syndicate might have reps who work with the Foundation (while pretending to just be interested business people, keeping the truth about the Union hidden), to help them bring their products to market. Indeed, they might serve in the same capacity that the SPD did - a place the Syndicate outsources its research and development work to - now that the latter disappeared mysteriously. And maybe the Union keeps an eye out for talented persons in the Foundation, snatching up the ones who seem likely to Awaken.
