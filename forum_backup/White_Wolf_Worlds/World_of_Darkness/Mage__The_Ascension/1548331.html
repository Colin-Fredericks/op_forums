    Basically, I'm doing a crossover between the WoD and Gama World (in all it's kitch splendor) on the GURPS board. So I thought I'd offer the idea here as well.

    Basically, this is a cross between the WoD and a very satirical take on Golden Age Sci Fi post-apocalyptic settings. Mutations are over the top and remnants of wild technology is scattered all around.

      This whole mess gets started when Tzimisce got into an epic spat with the Technocoracy. All details of why it happened have been lost. What is known is that all factions of the WoD got involved.

      A powerful Hermetic had found a major weakness of Cainite Vampires in the Shadowlands. With aid from Wrinkle the Paradox spirit of Time, the Lady of Fate and Gaia herself this Hermetic banished all Vampires of greater than the fifth generation from the lands of the living and imprisoned them deep in the Labyrinth beneath the Shadow Lands. Tzimisce, as he was dragged from the world, cursed all humans. This caused generations of monstrous twisted births world wide. The Gama World mutations.

      Mages, because they are Metahumans from the moment they Awaken, are immune to the Curse. As are Changelings, Kinaine, Fera, Kinfolk, and similar types.

      The backwash from all this magic created an ice age in the lands beyond the tropics and a mega-drought within the tropics. Now, two hundred and fifty some years later the world is beginning to heal and society is regrowing.

      The climate is warming beyond the tropics, spring has returned to many lands. Rain and fertility are returning to the tropics. Civilization had survived in underground shelters and small fragile enclaves, now it is spreading again and people are having more children. Better still, more of those children get to live to adulthood.

      Some celebrate, some mourn, others take up arms.

          There are two main human types in most forms of Gama World. Mutant humans and Pure Strain Humans. The very name "Pure Strain Humans" is meant to be ironic. Humanity in Gama World has enforced massive amounts of "natural selection" upon themselves through the power of stupidity and aggression. So the "Pure Strain Humans" (hereafter PSHs) are evolved humans adapted to a violent and turbulent world.

          In this crossover Tzimisce's curse has caused the mutations. The mutations aren't realistic, they follow the conventions of "Leggo Genetics." A person could have the legs of a deer, the teeth of a tiger, and a peacock's tail, and not particularly stand out in a crowd. Parents and children rarely have the same mutations. Look through the various Mage treatments of Sorcerers and use the various Psionic Powers and Sorcery Paths to use as the basis of mental mutations.

          Mix and match freely. A young man who is a bear beneath the waist and has horns might have healing powers and telempathic powers like Councilor Troi. A tiny young woman who looks like a storybook pixie might have superhuman strenght and an electric eel like ability to deliver electric shocks.

          For the PSHs allow the Progenitors, in an effort to prove their willingness to work with the other factions and permanently drop their pogom, offered a treatment for those to mutated to live. They would create "Victors" to place the souls and minds of such unfortunates into.

          Under the weaker conditions of Paradox less ambitious "Victors" (artificial humans created by the Progenitors) could, if given a human soul and mind, could become human and breed true. This worked best with infants and other very small children.

          These infant Victors were soon called "vat babies." And it was soon discovered that there wasn't enough available quintessence to meet the demand. So the "vat babies" were reserved for those infants with a Destiny or a very strong soul. Both of these qualities aided in the "vat baby" growing up to be a real person. The very few who fail to become fully human (the Pinocchios) tend to develop strange powers and serious mental issues.

          #########

          Mutants are built as Consors with Freebie points spent to gain advantageous mutations and Freebie points gained from disadvantageous mutations. If someone has clawed hands and feet like a bear's paws, they would have to pay Freebie points for the use of the claws as weapons and would gain Freebie points for the limits of their paws as both hands and feet. Mutants pay one third price rounded down for Numina (Psi powers and Sorcerer Paths) but pay full price to improve their abilities and double to gain new ones after creation of the PC.

          #########

          PSHs are also built as Consors. However, where ordinary humans get one dot in each Attribute, PSHs get two dots in each Physical Attribute and two in Perception and three in Appearance. Otherwise character creation is the same.

                      Each of the major Supernatural types has been changed by the disaster.

                      Cainite Vampires: No Cainites of the fourth, third or second generations are in the world. Cain is definitely known to have met Final Death. Fifth generation Vampires are rarer than they were. For a vampire in Topor to hide from Fera or Mages is far harder now than in the past. So Cainite Vampires are far more fanatical about the Masquerade. Also communities that could support a vampire regularly feeding there are few indeed.

                      About the one really good thing is that Cainite Vampires with a very high humanity and Virtues can will themselves to partially escape the curse. Some Vampires with high humanity and high virtues can become Revenants. A type of Ghoul that doesn't actually need vampire vitae. They have access to low level disciplines and age at one third the rate of humans.

                      However, it's far more likely in the wilds of Gama World that vampires will lose humanity and become wrights. Mindless animalistic blood thristy predators.

                      Revenants are described in the book on Ghouls. Wrights are described in Sins of the Blood.

                      Because Tzimisce's curse, all Cainite Vampires have Vicissitude. Whether they want it or not. If your level of Self Control isn't higher than your Vicissitude then you will occasionally find that your appearance has changed without you knowing it. Weak willed vampires with low self-control and high Vicissitude find it hard to retain a stable shape.

                      The only Available clans are...

                      Banu Haqin*

                      Brujah

                      Hecta*

                      Gangrel

                      Malkavian

                      Nosferatu

                      Toreador*

                      Tzimisce

                      Venture*

                      And other clans and bloodlines are gone. Clans marked with an asterisk are very rare. Less than ten on the continent. And only a couple of dozen worldwide.

                          The Fera including the Garou have reorganized themselves into Beast Courts as in Hengeyokai: Shapeshifters of the East. Eastern and Western ideas are strongly blended among the Fera including the Garou. The idea of the Wheel of Ages is a dominant idea among the Fera at this time. There is debate as to whether it is still the Sixth Age, The Age of Sorrows or the Seventh, The Age of Slowly Growing Light.

                          These Fera are on the North American continent.

                          Ananasi: Mainly MesoAmerica and the southern latorial of the old United States. Strongly support the Beast Courts, mainly from the shadows. Friendly to the Council of Traditions and socially cordial (friendly social events being good for information gathering).

                          Bastet: Strong presence anywhere near Mages. The Pumonca and the Qualni are exceptions, they stick to the descendants of the tribes they've always been related to. The Bastet are part of the Beast Courts and work to keep the Courts and the Mages working together.

                          Corax: Strongly allied with the Council Mages, they leave those outside the Council to the Bastet or the Nuwisha depending on the character of the Mage. Particularly close to the Mercurial Elites because of their focus on communication.

                          Gurhal: Strong allies of the Beast Courts, but more focused on the Council Mages and humans generally.

                          Kitsume: Mainly in Asia. All Kitsume in North America are working with the Beast Courts to extend their influence. Major players in Beast Court diplomacy to human societies and political groups.

                          Mokole: Memories and backbones of the Beast Courts. They plan to gain significant political and religious authority in human societies. The Mokole see the peoples of North America as flexible. Perfect for figuring out new social orders. Aware that Mages are Metahumans and the future Gaia sees for humanity, the Mokole are determined to get the Mages allied with the Beast Courts and Gaia.

                          Nagah: People know the Nagah are around. People know that the Nagah kill those they see as forces of corruption. People tend to look over their shoulders a lot. The Nagah, allied with the Chakravanti, like this. However, like the Chakravanti, slavers and dictators are the main targets. But killing corrupt politicians is always a joy.

                          Nuwisha: The Werecoyotes are not happy campers, unless they leave the camp the same way Wednesday Addams did in the Movie. The Nuwisha and the Ratkin see the revival of civilization in a strictly negative light. They accept the Beast Courts, they even approve of the Mokole's basic goals, but they keep it at arms length. The Nuwisha love humanity, but they think humans are on the wrong path.

                          Ratkin: Furious champions of the Wyld. The Ratkin see humanity as dangerously out of control. The Ratkin see Gaia as having spoiled humans and treating them as her Golden Child. The Ratkin don't seek the extermination of humanity. But they see that human populations and cultures should be severely pruned back. Mages and Changelings deeply upset Ratkin. If humans are evolving into Mages then Ratkin will have to make sure only the very best survive. And Changelings, products of human Dreams are proof that the pruning needs to start NOW!!!

                          Neither the Rokea nor the Ajaba are present in North America in significant numbers.

                          Garou and recovered tribes next.

                              The Garou tribes have taken different paths in this crisis. Some tribes have been retured from extinction due to Gaia empowering a vast Hermetic working and sweetly asking Wrinkle to cooperate.

                              Black Furies: The Furies are strongly allied with both The Sisters of Hippolyta and The Cult of Ecstasy. The Furies often find themselves suggesting moderation to the Sisters. The CoE is great at keeping a straight face.

                              The rights of women are more widely respected than the social set-up would normally suggest and children are better protected. The Furies and their allies have also championed LBGTQ+ rights. They also suggest that allowing young people to freely engage in gay sex until they are old enough to be responsible for raising children would protect from unwanted pregnancies without use of drugs.

                              Yes, conservative religious groups are sour on the Furies.

                              Boli Zouhisze: Found only in Asia. Disinterested in other areas as of yet.

                              Bone Gnawers: Widespread across North America. They support the economic recovery and the regrowth of trade. As both the Hermetics and the Celestials demand better treatment and more respect for workers and the indigent the Gnawers are allied and very friendly.

                              Bunyip: Restored to existence and largely staying in Australia.

                              Children of Gaia: Actively working to both reorganize the Garou tribes into a parliamentary system and to strengthen the alliance with the Mages, Changelings and Fera.

                              The American Trinity, a powerful Child of Gaia totem, is promoting the restoration of democracy and an American union. The Eagle, the Turkey, and the Dove, are moving the Children of Gaia towards a long term plan for a new and better America.

                              Fianna: Allied to the CoG, the Gnawers, the Gurhal, the Changelings and the Council Mages, the Fianna see a lively future ahead. Happy warriors and proud patrons of the return to the republic. They've built strong connections to the American Trinity and to spirits of Humor and Fellowship. The Fianna allied with the CoE are very focused on renewing culture and the arts because these are powerful social glues.

                              Get of Fenris: The Get have a healthier mindset these days. Leaders are more like the efficient tolerant Prussians of the early 18th century. The general tribe is more conventional but focused on building and preserving strong communities.

                              Glass Walkers: The Walkers have brought back the Rite of City Building. The other Garou aren't thrilled, but cities are different now. They no longer need to threaten either Gaia or the Wyld.

                              The Walkers are becoming merchant princes (which doesn't thrill the followers of the American Trinity) and are the main forces fighting to keep Vampires out of the embryo cities.

                              Red Talons: Allied with the Nuwisha and the Ratkin and out of the Garou nation. They realize that Gaia won't tolerate the extinction of the humans but the Talons feel civilization can't be allowed to recover or even exist.

                              Shadow Lords: They support the recovery of civilization. They are allied with the Mages. They want art, culture, education, and science to return. It's democracy they don't want.

                              Silent Striders: Largely absent from North America.

                              Silver Fangs: Unstable, glorious, and afraid. The Lords want their crowns. The CoG wants democracy. The Mages, the Changelings and the Fera complicate everything. Meanwhile the spiritual influence of the American Trinity spreads through the land. What is a king to do?

                              Stargazers: Absent from North America.

                              Siberark: Absent from North America.

                              Singing Dogs: Absent from North America.

                              Uktena: Actively seeking to join the Council of Traditions. The goal is to bring the Mages under stronger Gaian influence. The Uktena are reaching out to anyone they think can promote their twin goals. Keeping the Wyrm bound and quiet, and learning new mystic lore.

                              Wendigo: They've chosen isolation and are working to rebuild Native American communities. Racial purity has long since gone by the boards, but it's cultural purity the Wendigo are focused on.

                              White Howlers: Restored and active, in Europe, not in North America.
