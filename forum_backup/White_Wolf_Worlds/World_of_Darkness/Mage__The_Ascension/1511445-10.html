    This is probably not helpful, but I've always interpreted the Correspondence +/- life and matter to not be a contradiction so much as an alternate method. If you're using Correspondence with life or matter, you're just grabbing the object or life form, which is a fairly simple process. If you're only using Correspondence, then you're not grabbing the object/life form so much as transforming/moving the space around them. (Like the ship engines in Futurama.) The latter is possible, but it's more complicated and thus requires a higher sphere level.

                                is clear that it is about transmutation ranks of that sphere being required. To transmute matter is Matter 2, to transmute another person is Life 4. This is specifically stated in HDYDT Pg 31

                                M20 itself doesn’t give the ranks but just says it needs to integrate the spell in to the pattern. And that is entirely consistent with the need for transmutation levels of the effect.

                                As I've described previously, Locking as [Pattern Sphere] 2 came from M20. M20, in one of its great failings, never gave specific rules for locking. That said, every time an effect can be reasonably interpreted as using a locking sphere, it uses rank 2 (except for one time that it uses rank 1). Notable examples include the Common Magical Effects chart entries for invisibility and flight. This is the strongest thing we have to go on in M20.

                                Now I realize that HDYDT does things differently but it should come as no surprise that I do not consider HDYDT to be a good source. It's pretty close to the point where if HDYDT says something, its a strong indicator that thing is wrong. That's not a joke. I wish it was. In locking alone HDYDT sometimes uses alteration levels (e.g. Life 2 and 4), sometimes uses transmutation levels (e.g Life 3 and 5),sometimes uses a single level regardless of complexity (locked wards uses Life 4 for all living creatures), and sometimes makes something completely new up (in the section on flight it is Life 2 for self and Life 3 for everything else).

                                So going back to how M20 handles it we have the distinct possibility that locking is a simple effect that is always supposed to use [Pattern Sphere] 2 and not scale with the target. As a rule it is simple to calculate and simple to adjudicate, which is good in a gaming system. The only real downside is that it goes outside of the stated abilities of the Pattern Spheres, but that's not all that unusual.

                                Now we can view locking as changing the pattern, but we don't have to, and even if we do it is such a small change as to be really pushing the definition of alteration or transmutation. Doing so makes for a more complicated calculation, implies that it should be harder to lock a spell to a car than a rock (which I'm not convinced about).

                                For all those reasons, as well as it having the lower reasonable sphere combination, I favor [Pattern] 2.

                                                Originally posted by Ramnesis View Post



                                                Next up we have levitation/flight. Despite the sheer nonsense that is HDYDT's levitation section the Common Effects Chart is quite simple. Correspondence 3 (or Forces 2) to levitate things, Life or Matter as locking spheres. The only real problem here is that nowhere in the Correspondence writeup does it say that the Sphere is capable of this. The idea of Correspondence based flight has been in Mage for a decades, though, so I'm guessing this was an oversight.

                                                However, then the Sphere writeup says: "Also, by combining Correspondence 3 with Forces, Life, or Matter, the mage can move things around from a distance, levitating, manipulating or teleporting them without physical contact." That's cool, but levitation at a distance, manipulation at a distance, and teleportation at a distance are several very different effects. The recommendation works for remote levitation and remote manipulation since the first is the same spell as above (with a range augment that's already covered) and the second is a Forces, Life, and/or Matter spell with a range augment. On the other hand remote teleportation and just physically picking up and moving objects from a distance are merely Correspondence 2 on its own, no other spheres required.
                                                I've recently been going back over the sphere writeups and trying to reinterpret them while throwing out as little as possible and I realized that the section I had problems in the second paragraph may be the statement that Correspondence based flight is possible. I previously assumed that it was saying manipulation with Forces, Life, or Matter with a Correspondence augment (which would be Corr 2), but my first move should have been to check if this was manipulation with Correspondence with a Forces, Life, or Matter augment. And indeed, all four examples (move things, levitate them, manipulate them, or teleport them) can be viewed that way if we read this entry to mean that Correspondence 3 can manipulate a target's position without physical touch. That is a reasonable power for Correspondence.
