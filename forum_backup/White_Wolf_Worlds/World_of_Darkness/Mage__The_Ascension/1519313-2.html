    There are several instruments that could fit here, but again, you need the full foci to make a case. Having said that, I could see psionics foci in your example as being: Thought Forms, Meditation, Eye contact, Energy, Gestures, Voice, Nanotech even math.

    Now, the explanation will depend on the full foci:

    - Meditation doesn't require sitting in a lotus position with your eyes closed. You can meditate while running if you want. However, as a ST, you could ask for a roll in stressfull situations. In your example, the mage just succeded in the roll.

    - Eye contact arguably doesn't require eye-to-eye contact. The psionic gaze could be enough to start an effect.

    - Gestures is the classic one. Practically every telekinesis user do that Jedi hand waving. That would be the one I'd choose.

    - Energy could be the use of the psionics internal energy, and doesn't have to be an existent real life physics energy

    - Thoght forms are similar to meditation in a way and might not require any physical action.

    - If your psionic is tech based, having nanobots in his body, or some device in his brain, nanotech or any other technological instrment could fit (devices and machines)

    Note: I believe instrtuments works better when combined, so you could go the energy+meditation+gestures: The psionics focus his gaze into the soldiers and with a wave of his hand, he channels his energy into a burst of force.

        I suggested a possible solution for Chi Magick in another thread: say that adding Prime •• to an Effect and spending a point of Quintessence to make it a Chi Effect basically lets you combine the Effect with an otherwise-mundane task: rather than “Abilities Enhancing Magick” or “Magick Enhancing Abilities”, make it “Abilities as Magick”, and roll Arete+Ability instead of Arete on the one hand, or Attribute+Ability on the other. Chi Flight would be Arete+Athletics; Chi Healing would be Arete+Medicine; Chi Shout would be Arete+Intimidation; and so on.

        It's a potentially massive boost; but on the other hand, having to spend a Quintessence on each use is a potentially massive cost.
