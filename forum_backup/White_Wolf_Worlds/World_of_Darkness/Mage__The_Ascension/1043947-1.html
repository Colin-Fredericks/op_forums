    In some chat roleplay, some of the players have announced some downtime intentions.
    Arnold (Void Engineers) is planning to use Mind to not sleep, pull an all-nighter at the lab to process the data they collected. He is going to try to talk the others into coming back with him. (He secretly plans to spike the coffee with nanomachines that serve as his Mind Sphere focus so he can read them, telepathically and track them with Correspondence).

    Dick (Cult of Ecstasy), who has smoked a somewhat ridiculous amount of weed to make his Mind spells work, wants to stop for burritos. And ice cream. The anomaly is in a very rural area (National Forestland and a Superfund site), but there's a town with a Sonic on their way to Tallahassee. They decide to stop at Sonic.

    First order of business at the start of the next game session is to decide whether they're going to keep working or go home and sleep. The Traditions mages have been given lodging in the only thing that Driftwood has access to for free—a FEMA trailer in the trailer park where the displaced residents from the ruined town are living. The trailer park is a shithole of despair.

    Kia (Order of Hermes) and Dick both asked to use Prime to "wash" the resonance of their trailer to make a good meditation sanctum. This was a good call, because the place is bogged down with negative vibes from the Syndicate who provided the trailer.

        Dramatis Personae update

        0fe1:a —Familiar/Companion
        0fe1:a (pronounced "Ophelia") is Oliver's familiar. She's a free AI. Three dots in a Background buys a pretty good AI companion and it took some back-and-forth with Oliver's player about what she does. He carries her around in a mini tablet, but she can live in his implant or free out on the web.

        Mister Glass (NPC)—New World Order
        Mr. Glass is a Man in Black. His official job title is "Information security director" of the amalgam. He's a gigantic man who exists to intimidate. Is he helping Karen, or is he reporting her collusion with the Reality Deviants to Control? Glass is secretly (players don't know this at all) a member of the Harbingers of Avalon.

        Brenda McKinnon (NPC)—Extraordinary Citizen, Syndicate
        Brenda is the office secretary. She runs everyone's payroll, routes requisitions to the right place, answers the phones, etc. She's everyone's mom at the office. Surprisingly, the players really latched onto her and interacted with her much more than I was expecting. Unexpected, but I'm super stoked about it because she gets important later and I was worried that what happens would seem like it comes out of nowhere. Instead, they already seem to really care about her.

        Ami el-Haddad (NPC)—Progenitors
        Ami is a Technocracy trauma surgeon. The Driftwood Institute has acted as a "dispatch center" cover for operations inside the anomaly and her medical expertise has been needed. She is from Iran. The Technocracy's hold on the government is shaky to nonexistent since the election and it's entirely possible that ICE may try to deport her.

        Former Sheriff Bule Jackson (Larry)—Changeling
        The rural sheriff was a changeling; until he lost the election last November. He's been drinking a lot and spending time at a stripclub, where he's met a muse who is helping him find his Glamour. This is Larry's emergency backup character in case Arnold gets killed. This is silly and unnecessary since, a) Arnold is the most cowardly PC I've ever seen in any RPG, and b) as GM, I have no intention of killing Arnold until I've turned him barabbi.

            Season 1, Episode 2

            Scene 1
            Starting right from the end of the last episode. We discuss what we learned from the last episode. Arnold's (Void Engineers. Research & Execution) player hilariously translated everything into Technobabble as Dick (Cult of Ecstasy) explained it. Roleplay XP granted.
            The characters end up splitting up. Robert (Hermetic, House Ngoma) heads to his apartment or crashpad at the town with the Zimbabwean refugees (Robert's player had a cold and wasn't gaming tonight). Dick goes back to the lab with Grover (Progenitors). Kia (Hermetic, House Xaos) goes back to the accommodations that Driftwood got for him, a FEMA trailer in a hurricane refugee camp. Arnold brews some coffee and spikes it with nanoparticles, his Mind spell focus. They didn't indicate they weren't drinking it, so I'm assuming that Dick and Grover are now susceptible to Arnold's Mind Sphere Effects.

            Scene 2
            Kia uses Prime to wash the Resonance out of the FEMA trailer to make it into a ritual space. It's full of bad Technocracy mojo. It's also highly susceptible to Correspondence scrying, but he doesn't find a specific listening device ("it's the microwave"). He resigns himself to the possibility that the Technocracy is watching him and starts a ritual to make a repellant that will ward the chigger spirits off of him. He botches the second roll and the bug spray trinket he's making is spoiled. He is the only PC who gets a good night sleep before shit goes down.

            Scene 3
            The sun rises and the group at the lab are still awake working on their data and debriefing. Dick isn't able to meditate here (bad Techno-mojo), but he tries to help out. Mr. Glass (NWO) comes to work and tells Grover that he needs to get Dick out of here because the supervisors from the Symposium are coming for a surprise meeting today. Dick drives back to the FEMA trailer.

            At the FEMA trailer, a neighbor knocks on the door asking what drugs they're selling. (He figured that two guys moving in are a gay couple, but yesterday he saw three guys, thus, meth lab). Kia turns him down, but Dick invites him in and they get high together. I hadn't meant for this scene to go on as long as it did, but it allowed a great opportunity for a plot exposition of how the evacuation of Pryor, Florida happened after the hurricane. Dick uses some Mind magick to help him recall details and he works through some of his trauma from the even that led to his PTSD.

            Scene 4
            The Symposium arrives. Arnold's supervisor from Research & Execution tells him that budget cuts and internal shifts in power in the Void Engineers are going to result in his research being less and less supported. Arnold counters with the argument that an extradimensional incursion should be high priority, but he's told they take it seriously, but it's above his head. Arnold spends the rest of the scene selling out Karen and Grover telling of their treason in colluding with Reality Deviants. (This has a bit less traction then it should, as Karen has connections and alliances that Arnold doesn't know about. This is going to become a factor soon, though).
            Grover's research coordinator at the Symposium is getting pressure from the Syndicate to get a marketable product from his research as soon as possible.
            Oliver's former squad leader tells him that he has been on light duty and observation long enough and it's time to come back to his old marine unit. Oliver tells him he's fine with that, but he really isn't.

            (Spoiler alert: The PCs' obsession with closing the anomaly is pretty much just them. The Syndicate thinks the anomaly can be profitable if it were in a more optimal location, so they secretly don't want Driftwood to close this one before they learn how to open another one!)

            Scene 5
            There is no food in the FEMA trailer. Kia and Dick decide to go to the Food Lion to stock the fridge. As they pass an intersection, their mystickal instincts kick in (two and four successes on Awareness, respectively) and they discover a street that should be a loop, but has "One Way/Do Not Enter" signs on both ends of the street and strong Mind effects directing them to not notice it. They, of course, investigate. They find a subdivision with abandoned, half-finished McMansions and at the back of the circle, a path into the woods. There they find a swimming hole of crystal clear water and indescribably beauty. There is a manatee swimming. Kia with Prime sight finds it to be a powerful node. Dick scans with Mind and learns there are sentient minds here, but he can't find them (They joke, "It's the manatee!" but the manatee is a selkie, so indeed, they're right.) Dick strips nekkid and swings out on the rope swing and swims. Kia meditates in a more dignified manner. They charge their avatars with Quintessence in this way. (They got lucky this time and nobody was home. Once the selkie reports to the rest of the Freehold that they were trespassed upon, they're not going to leave it unattended again).

            Note, at this point Kia and Dick still haven't eaten anything.

            Scene 6
            Karen and the Void Engineer major have an argument and Karen comes storming out of her office. She orders her team to get suited up to go back into the anomaly and they contact the "consultants" on their burner phones to meet them at the Cracker Barrel near the anomaly. Kia and Dick are excited about this, as they are really hungry. They are closer, so they get to the Cracker Barrel first and Kia tries to order vegan with no success, or even understanding from the elderly waitress. (A half-hour side-conversation about Cracker Barrel worthy of No Context Games ensued).
            The Void Engineer marines tried one last time to sanitize the anomaly with overwhelming brute force without involving Driftwood. Karen is going to stop them, and rescue what survivors there might be.

                Season 1, Episode 2
                Scene 7
                It is night by the time they get there. They get lost trying to find the gateway and Arnold discovers that the event horizon has moved almost a quarter mile. He is surprised and alarmed by this.
                Inside the barrier are two humvees about 300 yards inside and about a half mile in they can see a firefight going on. The whispers are loud, but everyone passes their Willpower checks. They make a break for the humvees. Dick uses Mind to shield them from the whispers. They drive across the grassy estuary. It's tough driving.
                Kia sees a cyborg in the headlights and Arnold, sensing with Mind that he's under the influence of the whispers urges Kia to run him down. He does so with a string of ten-agains resulting in damage equal to "remember that scene in Robocop?"

                When they come upon the last survivors, they're trapped on the roof of a shack with their comrades possessed by the voices in their heads. Oliver sprays them with machinegun fire and in the next two turns puts down two. Kia gets out and creates a sigil circle that should pin one in place if it tries to step inside it. One attacks him and it does. The possessed marine falls to his knees and cannot stand. (Prime 2, Forces 2, Correspondence 2 opposing Strength).
                Meanwhile, in the other humvee, Grover, Karen and Dick see a giant looming shadow just outside their light. He swerves to see it and it is a wild boar the size of a Ryder truck. It goes to charge them. Dick tries to put it to sleep with Mind. It almost works. A turn later he tries again and it gets a net four successes to resist on a Willpower roll so it's just enraged now. (This is twice now that they've been faced with a wyld chimera and without knowing it, did exactly the right thing by attacking it with Mind Sphere instead of attacking it with force).

                As they flee down the field, Kia gets stupid successes on Drive and gets out ahead of Grover. The characters in Kia's humvee plan to guide it outside of the gateway in hopes to emphasize the magnitude of the threat that the zone represents. However, the boar gore's Grover's humvee and deals a fistful of damage dice to the person in the passenger seat (Karen). The humvee door soaks three and she soaks one so she only takes five health levels. She's got a broken hip and is bleeding badly.

                The boar is apparently satisfied with its revenge and doesn't pursue.

                In the real world, it was 11pm and we ended the scene there.

                        Recap:
                        Inside the zone, Karen is grievously wounded.
                        They notice that two of the three survivors look identical.

                        Scene 0:
                        While they wait for the helicopter to airlift their survivors to a hospital, Major Janusz of the Void Engineers Border Corps gets very upset and starts blustering about where his platoon is. Kia gets annoyed by this guy and draws a sigil on his hand and shows it to the Major, turning his volume down (Forces 2, cancelling the sound of his voice). They see him mouth "Who the f— is that guy?"

                        This is the point at which Arnold snaps.

                        Scene 1: Later that night/next morning, Grover and Oliver drive in Dick's car (a VW Westfalia with a mismatched red door and "All Acts of Love & Pleasure Are My Rituals" and "Visualize Whirled Peas" bumper stickers) to Jacksonville, where Karen is hospitalized. Robert drives himself. Kia drives himself. Arnold stays home at the lab.

                        Kia arrives first and Karen is sedated but is alert enough to praise Kia for being the most stubborn person she's ever met and that he needs to talk to Oliver and get to the center of the zone, where it all began.

                        Kia tries using some magick and Karen stops him and tells him he should leave right away. He does and he sees two men in black suits and sunglasses come out of the elevator just as he's leaving.

                        Scene 2: Arnold conference calls in to the Symposium and talks to the Director, a Syndicate VP named Rochester Stoneham III. He convinces him to make him Acting Director while Karen is incapacitated. He starts telling VP Stoneham of all of Karen's improprieties and trafficking with Reality Deviants. Stoneham answers by being annoyed that this call is still going on.

                        Scene 3: Dick, Oliver, and Grover arrive. The Men in Black are already in Karen's room when they get there. Dick messes with them, offering them a Snickers bar he got at the gift shop downstairs. They have no sense of humor and stare them down.

                        Dick tries something with Mind to act as pain management and Karen stops him, whispering in his ear, "Don't. There are thaumatometers in here."

                        Outside in the corridor, Robert hears "Hungry!" and using Spirit sight locates an egg sack the size of a beach ball just across the Gauntlet of that bug spirit they'd found when they were inside the zone. He tells the others. Grover, who is somewhat credentialed at this hospital, checks in on the Void Engineer Border Corps marines who have been been here for weeks. One has died, but three more survive. Robert can see that the bug inside of them has grown and is going to claw its way out and lay eggs (the hospital has been treating it as an aggressive tumor and radiating it, which has suppressed its growth rate). Robert thinks he can treat the patients, but they're going to need to do it someplace outside of where the thaumatometers can detect magick so they don't get caught.

                        The rest of the adventure is a heist, but instead of stealing, they're going to cast an exorcism on some Technocracy.

                        For some reason, Dick calls Arnold on his burner phone and outlines exactly what they're planning to do. Arnold calls the Symposium.
                        (At this point, if we'd been playing with the canonical Technocracy, we'd be TPKing in about 15 minutes. I'm not entirely prepared as a GM for this and, not willing to give up on all of my campaign planning, I'm forced to punt).

                        There aren't currently any Sanitation troops available on standby. The local Symposium is still reeling from their recent TPK.

                                    Season 1, Episode 4
                                    Cliffhanger resolution
                                    Robert (Order of Hermes) and Dick (Cult of Ecstasy) run upstairs to the 5th floor, where the egg sack was hiding, just outside the Gauntlet in the Near Umbra. Outside of one of the Void Engineer's rooms is another young man in a black suit with an earpiece. He asks, "Where is John Willoughby? The patient from this room, is he dead?" The guy sounds frantic. Dick tells him that the patient is in a group therapy session and will be back in a few minutes. They guy looks relieved, but before he can really respond, he puts his finger to his earpiece and rushes off down the stairs. They dispatch the egg sack without further incident.

                                    Oliver (Iteration X) is brawling with two Men in Black and winning. A third guy (the guy who was just talking to Robert and Dick) bursts through the door knocks Oliver down and the three dogpile on him and the fight is over.

                                    Kia (Order of Hermes) is in the chapel with the three Void Engineers, now healed from their spirit possession. Kia decides to avoid the scrum in the hallway and climbs out the window, (fails a Forces magick roll to jump; they have so much Paradox now it's not even funny), he mundanely parkours over to the awning over the main entry to the hospital and scrambles down. He escapes to his car and leaves.

                                    Robert and Dick came in separate cars and there's no way to recover their allies from the clutches of the NWO, so they leave.

                                    Scene 1: Oliver's punishment
                                    Next day Oliver is brought to a Colonel's office for an official reprimand. The Colonel praises his warrior spirit and how effectively he "showed the mirrorshade brigade that they're not in charge." The Colonel tries to recruit Oliver back to his old unit, which Oliver declines. "Look, this whole Florida thing is a hot potato and no one wants to be holding it when the music stops. Why don't you get out of there?" "I want to finish what I started," he replies. "You belong somewhere where that loyalty will be reciprocated, the Colonel says, (subtlely hinting at Arnold's repeated attempts to get them in trouble with Control for collaborating with Reality Deviants).

                                    Scene 2: Arnold gets smart
                                    Arnold (Void Engineers) finally gets through to to some Void Engineer directors. They expect a Risk Analysis and some evidence that they aren't just throwing more personnel into suicide missions and materiel into a bottomless pit. Arnold calls the Traditions consultants on their burner phones and convinces them to come in to help him make a plan. This works for Kia (Order of Hermes) because he wants to get to the center of the anomaly like Karen told him to last session.

                                    Finally we get to playing the kind of Mage: The Ascension I've been hoping for all this time

                                    Oliver and Dick talk over how Dick has been able to block the whispers in people's heads all this time in an attempt to replicate it. Maybe if they have some of the whispers they can invert the polarity of the brainwaves and neutralize it like with active noise cancellation.
                                    Arnold checks on his Procedure he started two sessions ago to track the voices with Mind+Correspondence, with Prime 2 making it autonomously running for the last three days. He got six successes on the roll two sessions ago so it makes sense that he can track at least where the voices aren't and can use that to avoid being where the voices can attack their minds.

                                    The anonymous whistleblower deaddrop contacts Arnold again (they've been typing messages on parking tickets left on his car) asking for an update. Arnold sends them a message blowing them off—he's finally getting who his allies really are.

                                    they also use Forces rituals to create mufflers for the four ATVs (two side-by-sides and two quad bikes) they are planning to drive into the zone with.

                                    Scene 3: Inside the Zone again
                                    They see the path of glowsticks and the two humvees just inside the breach of the event horizon. Farther in, they find the alligator-squid hybrid, dead, killed by the Neutralization Corps in the last failed attack. They ride into the pine forest and run into a clearcut. There are pine logs stacked neatly for removal and lots of slash (branches, tops, scrub) on the ground. "Are the Syndicate using a time anomaly to rapidly grow trees for harvest?" This is more difficult to drive in and everyone made Dex+Drive rolls to keep from falling behind. On the other side of the pine forest was the remains of route 15, over a decaying bridge and into the former town of Pryor.

                                    Scene 4: The Lab
                                    The former Technocracy lab is in an old Depression-era WPA building downtown. This facility's charter was to reduce prison recidivism by teaching inmates job skills in STEM fields (also, recruit potential Void Engineer cannon-fodder for their wars in space). They bust open the door and a cascade of dead pattern spiders pour out. Throughout the building, there are dead pattern spiders along the windows and doors—they manifested abruptly in the material world and were trying to get out of the building when they died. The lab where this all began has a large dimensional warp generator in the middle of the floor. They were not supposed to have anything like this. On closer review, they discover that it is entirely handmade out of parts machined on a lathe, 3D printer, or a CNC machine—all tools they were supposedly teaching the inmates how to use. It's missing a piece (Oliver saw him take this piece off and put it in his pocket in the first adventure of the campaign).

                                    Scene 5: The Building
                                    Arnold, Intern Dan, and Oliver disassemble the dimension warp generator and load it on the ATVs. Arnold finds a scientist's paper journal in a desk drawer. He pulls all the hard drives from computers he finds and looks for any safes or whatever else.

                                    Robert and Kia walk through the lobby and notices that the beautiful, marble floors are strange. The veining in the marble appears to be writing. It's ancient Enochian. Kia uses Forces to blast all the dust off the floor with wind. He tries to translate it magickally, but fails, so the three mysticks begin to copy the marble tiles with pencil and paper notebooks.

                                    Treasure: they find three magick items in the lab, a scientist's lab coat, a dowsing rod in a vent hood, and a magic 8-ball in a bench vice.

                                    Scene 6: The Voices are Coming.

                                    Arnold's tracking algorithm sees that the area where the Voice can be heard is moving toward them. The mysticks rush to complete their drawings of the marble veins and Dan and Oliver strap down all of the cargo in the side-by-side ATVs. They flee. Kia fails a Drive roll and can't keep up with the others. The Voice catches up and he hears it. We roll now for the ritual they did earlier to reverse the mind control effect. It succeeds and Kia is unaffected by the voice in his head. Kia then beats a roll against his own Banality to beat the Mists and remember what the voices say.

                                    Dick fails a Drive roll later in the clear cut and gets his vehicle high-centered on a rock. Oliver muscles it off and they drive on to safety.
