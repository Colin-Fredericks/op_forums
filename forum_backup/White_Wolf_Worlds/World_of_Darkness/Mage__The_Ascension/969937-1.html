                                            Originally posted by Quantumboost View Post
                                            Frankly, just the 'don't frenzy as much' and 'avoid dealing with Humanity loss' mods would make this an amazing deal for a lot of vampires.
                                            There is a question though of how to actually accomplish that. Frenzy and rotschreck are fairly straightforward, as they usually operates off of triggers, the responses to which can be fiddled with using Mind. Inuring them to effects which induce frenzy or rotschreck is more complicated, but making a "basic" frenzy-free vampire wouldn't be too hard.

                                            "[...]something to stop the Cainite from becoming Wight" is considerably more complicated, as you start dealing more directly with the beast. The beast is nebulous and tends to defy efforts to quantify it, something which is probably going to frustrate Enlightened Science. I'm not saying its impossible, but the magical effect is going to have be much more involved and complex. Then there's how to actually prevent the slide into wassail. Messing with stimuli responses like with frenzy and rotschreck isn't going to work because removing guilt tends to actually accelerate degeneration. What you would need to do is to basically create a new Path of Enlightenment that is taylored to your needs and coerce the vampire into adopting it. The downsides of this are that they will still have a number of behavioral ticks (in the form of Path sins) which may make them unwieldy agents, and in order to do this one would need to do some fairly deep research into Path lore to understand its underlying relationship to the beast and soul. That's pretty much the only way that's happening though.

                                                Yeah, actually permanently dealing with the Beast would be a hefty order to fill, requiring at least Mind 5 (to essentially restructure the basic nature of the vampiric mind). Restoring Humanity already lost might require similar levels, or perhaps perhaps Mind 4 to restore their conscious mind's memories to a state of innocence (I might rule that removing the karmic guilt/Resonance of the act itself might warrant conjunctional Prime). This might give the dots directly, or just ease purchasing it back up with experience, either reducing the amount a la Life 4 per Verbena Revised, or obviating the 'training time'.

                                                The easier method would be to use a Dominate-equivalent Mind 4 effect to attempt to control the vampire during a frenzy, or to use a Mind 2 emotional stabilizer to effectively reduce the difficulty of frenzy/rotschreck rolls (-3 diff to Self-Control or Courage rolls would do a heck of a lot there, putting them at similar difficulties to a Ghoul). Something like a Buffy-style morality chip might - if the designer in question understood the vampiric condition enough to understand that justification and repression is the opposite of what's needed - be able to do similar things for conscience rolls, inclining them towards a sense of remorse for inHuman actions.

                                                As implants, these can essentially be a permanent-effect Device, and as rank-2 effects they'd be reasonably cheap to make for a few exceptionally useful vampiric agents. As far as Devices go, that is.

                                                Extraordinary Citizens might be able to make similar things, since these are relatively low-end and fairly Coincidental effects.
