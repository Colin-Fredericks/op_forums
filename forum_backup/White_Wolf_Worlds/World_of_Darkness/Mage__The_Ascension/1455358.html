    101 Totem Spirits

    07-22-2021, 06:41 AM
    Post your totem spirits! Building on the North American list provided in M20 Corebook. Generally speaking they'll either be an animal (not one "done" by M20 corebook) or a conceptual spirit such as The Man, Mary Jane, Machine, Glitch and similar, which M20 covers.

    WTA is a good resource for learning about the "personality" of a spirit in the WOD lore, and that info makes building the MTA equivalent decently simple.

    1) Bear.

    Bear is wise in peace and fierce in war. They are a master of healing and mysticism, and encourage the practice of thoughtfulness.

    Manifestations: Huge bears appropriate to the local variety, large people with thick fur-like hair and other ursine qualities, nomads and wild people who are sleeping or resting on arrival.

    Associations: Wisdom, strength, protection of the young and vulnerable, ferocity, hunting, healing, food and drink, hibernation.

    Brood: Bear’s brood was damaged near-irreversibly by an ancient spirit conflict. They are hesitant to speak of this matter. Certain spiritually aligned creatures join their brood, along with lesser ursine spirits.

    Abilities: Athletics, Medicine, Meditation.

    Bans: Bear looks down harshly on those who shoot first and ask questions later. Their servants should be wise in peace and fierce in battle, taking slow and reasonable actions until the time comes to let loose and unleash their power.

    As a result, Bear punishes those who murder the defenceless, and those who fight without provocation or just cause.

      2) Cockroach.

      Cockroach is a survivor. Their plans and activities are all bent to this ultimate goal, and this manifests in all they do.

      Manifestations: Infestations of cockroaches, messages left in dark and hidden places, unkempt and paranoid messengers with an uncanny knack for slipping away.

      Associations: Survival.

      Brood: Certain Werewolves, horrific shape-changing experiments, cockroach spirits and people who survive at any cost number among Cockroach's brood. It is not in their nature to discuss other members of the brood with a Shaman unless it is necessary to ensure survival.

      Abilities: Stealth, Streetwise, Survival.

      Bans: Cockroach is fair and illustratively simple in their ban; their brood are forbidden from killing cockroaches.

          3) Brooklyn Knight

          The Brooklyn Knight is the stalwart subject and defender of Kings County, or more aptly known as, Brooklyn.

          Manifestations: Sudden strange appearances of pieces of medieval armor; saddled-up horses that seemingly have no owner and appear from nowhere; weird upkeep of people suddenly being honest with one another, when such a thing was rarely a case before; incidents of gang violence or warfare suddenly have less cases where guns are involved, where instead knives, bats, chains, trashcans as shields, and much more are used instead.

          Associations: Chivalry, Intrigue, Discipline

          Brood: The Brood of the Brooklyn Knight is usually that of his elite retinue of sergeant-knights; followed by his squires, men-at-arm spear footmen, and men-at-arm sword footmen. The Brooklyn Knight is also aided by Wind Elementals shaped as horses and Metal Elementals shaped as common spearmen levies.

          Abilities: Melee, Athletics, Leadership

          Ban: The Brooklyn Knight looks down on the use of guns. True battle can only be honored and meted out if you're up close-and-personal with the opponent, to look into their face man-to-man with pure determination and courage.

              4) Dana.

              Dana is a spirit of the natural world and of Fairy. She is a defender of beauty and inspiration and takes an interest in those who promotes art, protects the natural world, or aids the Fae.

              Manifestations: An ancient human crone, a sidhe noble woman of indeterminate house and court, surrounded by attendant chimerical sprites, a matronly Kithain woman, or even a Dryad.

              Associations: Wisdom, art, Fairy Lore, inspiration, teaching, nurturing, the Fae, seasons, gardens, nature, community, The Dreaming, patience, celebration, children, the elderly.

              Brood: Chimera from the Dreaming, spirits from the Arcadian Gateway, elementals, glade children, Wyldlings, the occasional Fiana ancestor spirits.

              Abilities: Faerie Lore, Medicine, Awareness.


              Bans: Dana refuses patronage to those who kill the Fae except as a last resort, and fighting fairies with cold iron is never justified. Also, her children must either be actively involved in either creating art or be actively assisting those who are creating art in some way (so hit that Patreon, folks!). Finally, Dana usually only bothers to offer her patronage to those who are involved in protecting the natural world, so she is often sought out by the Verbena who believe her to be the spirit of one of their Tradition’s forebearers.

                  5) The People

                  A Totem embodying the common good, the beleaguered masses, and large gatherings breaking their silence and taking direct action. "For the people!", in one form or another, is the rallying cry of the civil rights protestor, the fearless whistle-blower, and the martyr. Like many Totems this spirit has multiple facets, from peaceful sit-ins to defiant boycotts, and all the way up to armed insurrection.

                  Manifestations: Ordinary (in the real sense of the word - picture the 99%) people gathering into a crowd to show their discontent, an inquisitive reporter, a retired civil rights activist, a volunteer, graffiti and street art, a person with a covered face who disappears as quickly as they arrive.

                  Associations: Rebellion, justice, righteous violence, truth, vandalism, civil disobedience, large gatherings, demonstrations, oppressed groups.

                  Brood: "The People" is a spirit which has gained the aid of various data spirits in modern times, such as Glitch and many lesser manifestations. Where once a town might have come together in rebellion against a cruel baron, now whole movements form across the globe with the power of the #. In a more traditional sense, this spirit's brood includes animal, nature and elemental spirits which assist in trickery, passing along information and fighting the good fight... so long as their nature is aligned with the common good.

                  Abilities: Leadership, Melee OR Firearms, Investigation

                  Bans: This totem cannot abide "selling out" the movement. A shaman or adherent has broken the ban if they take action or inaction, especially dishonestly, which assists in maintaining an unjust hierarchy or order. For example, refusing to reveal a political corruption scandal because of threats from a Technocrat would be a breach of the ban - finding a way to do so safely would not.

                          6) The Sandman

                          A spirit which was largely limited to Europe until gaining a small amount of pop culture notoriety, The Sandman, or Ole Lukoje, is a friendly spirit who sprinkles dust or sand into the eyes of children. This is purported to give fantastical and peaceful dreams, and to aid people in going to sleep. And if you wake up with "grit" in your eye, perhaps the sandman paid you a visit too...

                          The Mage who forms a totem bond with The Sandman is likely an oneiromancer of some kind, a lucid dreamer who explores worlds beyond by night. Since Sandman is a spirit of folklore and dreams he has collected immense knowledge on spirits, bygones and matters of the supernatural, which makes him a good teacher of Lore, Cosmology and the Occult.

                          Manifestations: Traditionally a man in a pointed cap, though he could appear as an adult of any appearance or age so long as they have some interesting headwear, including pyjamas with a nightcap. He might otherwise appear as a sleepy individual who wishes to impart a message before saying goodnight, or appear entirely within a dream.

                          Associations: Sleep, Dreams, Folklore, Bygones.

                          Brood: Various fae and spirits related to sleep and good dreams. His followers sometimes appear a bit more sleepy or peaceful, but not "tired". Whether a spirit such as Wee Willie Winkie is another facet of the Sandman, or part of his brood, is a somewhat academic question.

                          Abilities: Meditation, Lucid Dreaming, Occult.

                          Ban: A tad alien in his morals, The Sandman cannot abide a Mage who uses their arts to spread nightmares or insomnia. A shaman who breaks this Ban might not only find their connection to the totem weakened, but also find that they wake up with their eyes missing until reparations are made...

                          Though less extreme, he dislikes those who awaken people from a peaceful sleep. Repeated and flagrant violation of the restful may result in revocation of the Totem benefits until reparations are made, typically by taking over the Sandman's work for a night. Easier said than done without the powers of a spirit...

                              7) Horse

                              Horse represents power, and runs the fine line between wild strength and tamed devotion. In a sense Horse is akin to a mighty champion of an emperor, or an exemplary servant of a god - powerful, dangerous, obedient. It is also worth noting that Horse has adopted certain associations because of mankind's obsession with virility.

                              Manifestations: wild horses of striking size, speed, or quality. Long-haired people who are powerful yet servile. Dreams of swift movement, battles, travel and even carnal matters. Certain types of imagery associated with horses.

                              Associations: Loyalty, Speed, Strength, Pride, Sport.

                              Brood: Lesser equine spirits, naturae which are compatible with domestication and mankind, wild-eyed jockeys, "horse girls".

                              Abilities: Animal Ken, Ride, Athletics.

                              Ban: Horse places great value on the pacts between animals and humankind - as a result he bans shamans from killing or otherwise harming domesticated animals, with legitimately compassionate exceptions.

                                  8) Invisible Hand

                                  The Invisible Hand has existed in its current form for as long as there has been trade, emerging in ancient civilizations such as Egypt, China and Mesopotamia, and as such predates Capitalism by many thousand of years. Where there are bargains, bartering and exchange of currency and goods, the Invisible Hand plays a part on some level. Mercurial in some senses of the word, this spirit is unpredictable in where it spends its energy and where it places its attention.

                                  Generally this spirit is aligned with concepts such as fairness, mutually beneficial exchanges and growth. That said, growth of an economy might come at the expense of the earth or nature, and a deal which is deemed to be fair by all present might infringe on parties unknown...

                                  Manifestations: strange and sudden changes in value, mysterious merchants and enigmatic brokers, headhunters for brilliant opportunities, friendly strangers with useful tips, an abandoned briefcase or device filled with spreadsheets.

                                  Associations: Trade, Wealth, Bargaining, Economies, Honesty, Oaths.

                                  Brood: Tech spirits aligned with modern economies such as online stores and cryptocurrency, naturae with a proclivity for trade such as domesticated animal spirits. Hypereconomists, mercantile shamans. All in all Invisible Hand is more interested in people than spirits.

                                  Abilities: Expression, Finance, Politics.

                                  Ban: Invisible Hand will never tolerate an adherent who reneges on deals. The worst kind of vulture capitalist is anathema to Invisible Hand, despite many such people paying lip service to the ideals of free trade.

                                  Note: This totem can be taken by Technocrats, standing amongst totems such as The Man and The Machine. A hypereconomist might align with this totem as easily as a technoshaman or mercantile mystic. That said, many members of The Syndicate are not suited to bonding with Invisible Hand because of the day to day realities of controlling broken and corrupt economies.

                                      9) Chameleon

                                      Versatile, perceptive and stealthy, Chameleon is a planner and thinker. They are best embodied by a clever spy, adopting a disguise, watching carefully and learning whatever they can, and then acting at the best opportunity.

                                      Manifestations: People who are so ordinary they go unnoticed, silent observers who move or disappear on approach, messages hidden in plain sight, camouflaged animals.

                                      Associations: Adaptability, Stealth, Perceptiveness, Planning.

                                      Brood: Chameleon's brood is predominated by natural spirits with proclivities for hiding and observation. Their human adherents tend to be people who know when it's best to stay out of sight. To simply be hidden or observant is not enough - a stick insect is admirably hard to spot, but if it moves without realizing that it's walking into the jaws of a hungry spider...

                                      Abilities: Alertness, Disguise, Stealth.

                                      Ban: Chameleon politely advises their shamans to watch, plan and think before acting. They know that this is wise and will lead to the best results, so in cases of minor infringement they view it as the shaman's loss. Particularly noteworthy acts of thoughtless or rash behaviour will result in the totem taking action to punish the shaman, typically by withdrawing their wisdom until the Mage makes amends.

                                          10) Sphinx

                                          A dangerous predator who prefers to sit and and play games, the Sphinx is an enigma in and of herself. She possesses the head of a human (though other variations exist) and the body of a lion with the wings of a falcon. All in all she prefers to use her human head, saving her terrifying body and wings for those who fail a particularly high stakes game...

                                          The Lore and Cosmology provided by the Sphinx particularly pertains to matters of life and death, opposition to necromancy and breaking of boundaries, and matters which defy understanding.

                                          Manifestations: Sphinxes in dreams, uncannily intelligent cats and birds, fiendishly difficult puzzles such as cryptic crosswords on an abandoned page of a non-existent newspaper, people posing riddles, philosophers, playful muggers with strange mores, intellectual bouncers, and allegedly the so-called "Rogue Council" which sent mysterious messages to Mages around 2002.

                                          Associations: Puzzles, Doorways, Battles of Wits, Balance Between Life and Death.

                                          Brood: Sphinx is a solitary spirit, and she entertains those who can solve her puzzles and receive the next bread crumb along the path. As a result she does not cultivate a brood so to speak, more a group of pursuers following in her wake.

                                          Abilities: Enigmas, Esoterica, Etiquette.

                                          Ban: The Sphinx places her ban on rejecting a true challenge of wits or intellect. An adherent who shies away from an invitation to play chess, engage in a game of riddles, or otherwise test the strength and versatility of their minds will earn her ire and have to prove themselves worthy of forgiveness.

                                          Acts of necromancy, that is reversing deaths which have come to pass and fully resolved, or manipulating the bodies and souls of the departed in ways that step over the boundary into the world of living, will earn the everlasting hatred and distrust of the Sphinx. This law is more strict than a ban, and is listed because of its severity and deep importance to the Sphinx, who guards the path between life and death. An exception to this law might be gained, in extremis, by defeating the Sphinx herself in a battle of wits.

                                          Note: The Greek Sphinx was portrayed as a woman, and the Egyptian Sphinx was generally depicted as a man. For the purposes of this entry the Sphinx is a "woman" however such limitations rarely contain a powerful spirit, meaning that they will appear as they wish, or perhaps in accordance with or defiance of the expectations of the dreamer.

                                              11) Bull

                                              A spirit of rage and unbridled passions, Bull is power. To bond with Bull is less a matter of accepting a spiritual mentor than forming a relationship with a wellspring of fury which might strengthen a shaman.

                                              Manifestations: Dreams of violence and battle which teach strength and resilience, furious muscled strangers ready to throw down a challenge, wild and rabid animals, signs of recent fights which might be interpreted or learned from.

                                              Associations: Anger, Power, Charges, Loss of Control.

                                              Brood: He is also followed by rage-filled spirits who have accepted that the time for negotiation and peace has passed, a growing following given mankind's onslaught against nature. Bull is also a war totem which is popular amongst the violent "werewolves" of the world, though mutual kinship with him is not often recognized.

                                              Abilities: Athletics, Brawl, Intimidate.

                                              Ban: Bull is enraged by those who repress their anger; Shaman adherents who stifle their anger and exert self-control break his ban.

                                                  12) Mantis

                                                  A predator renowned for their reverent stillness and sudden explosive movement, Mantis only interests themselves in Mages who know how to stand still and master self-discipline.

                                                  Manifestations: People or animals demonstrating remarkable patience, dreams and omens, cunning traps.

                                                  Associations: Patience, Lethality, Self-Control, Detachment.

                                                  Brood: Mantis is a solitary spirit, and instead of cultivating a brood they simply give attention to mortals who properly emulate and bond with them. A person who lacks the patience to wait for their prey, literal or figurative, is of no interest to Mantis.

                                                  Abilities: Martial Arts, Meditation, Stealth.

                                                  Ban: Mantis expects their adherents to watch and wait before acting, showing patience, cunning and discipline. This is not advice politely given by a loving mentor, but a strict dogma which is intuitively understood by those who bond with them. Acting recklessly will cause Mantis to lose interest, withdrawing their support until the shaman proves themselves to be worthy of further tutelage.

                                                  Note: While Mantis gives an adherent support in the practice of Martial Arts, the rare talent that learns Do (an art exclusively taught by the Akashic Brotherhood, who rarely instruct outsiders) will find the bonus dice to Martial Arts checks will be replaced by a bonus to Do checks, but only once their rank in Do exceeds their rank in Martial Arts.

                                                      13) Hidden Glade

                                                      Call them "Beauty spots", "natural wonders", the world does have some especially beautiful places, some of which are charged with quintessential power. "Hidden Glade" is a totem spirit with myriad names and faces, from quiet coves to caves behind waterfalls, and of course glittering clearings which look to a newcomer like the forest's special garden.

                                                      A favourite of shamans who enjoy wildlife and the beauty of nature in balance, it is hard to extricate Hidden Glade from nodes of power, or "Caerns", which are a major focus of the spirit's interest.

                                                      Manifestations: Places of relatively impressive natural beauty, regardless of the biome. People enjoying those places, such as a swimmer in a secret beautiful lake. Friendly people and animals who hint at the locations of such places.

                                                      Associations: Beauty, Power, Balance, Secrets, Nature, Purity.

                                                      Brood: Any spirit, animal or person who basks in the beauty of such places without harming them is a friend to "Hidden Glade". Any who helps to preserve the balance, and when necessary the secrecy of these places numbers among her brood.

                                                      Abilities: Awareness, Meditation, Esoterica*

                                                      Ban: It should go without saying that despoiling a place which is sacred to the Totem will result in a withdrawal of the bond, and likely come along with further repercussions from angry spirits and shapeshifting monstrosities. Her true "Ban" is that she expects her adherents to be selective in those they alert to the presence of a sacred place, especially one which is a node. An example of failure would be revealing the location of a caern to a self-interested and mercenary wizard who only intends to draw power to fuel his creations.

                                                      *For a Shaman bonded with this totem, appropriate specializations of Esoterica include those such as Herbalism, Yoga, Stone Lore, Astrology and similar. As a general rule of thumb the specialization should relate to something which benefits or benefits from such places, ideally both.

The entity revels in tabboo underground music styles and the dark borders of mainstreme. Despite his name the name he is quite the renaisance man in terms of music.                                                           Associations: Faustian pacts, exceptional music, mysterious deaths, crossroads.
