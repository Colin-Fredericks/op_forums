                                                    Originally posted by Aya Tari View Post
                                                    Life is needed for living substances (fruit, nuts, yeast, etc). Urine is rarely living.
                                                    Depending upon version and sourcebook, this is not strictly the case and largely depends upon paradigm, and the nature of the Pattern. The primary hard-and-fast rule is the Pattern in question has to be irrevocably inert; Life magic can and does (depending upon paradigm) govern "dead" organisms, or constituent parts of that organism, that retain enough viability to sustain or propagate other Life patterns.

                                                    That biology as a properly-defined modern natural science is a newcomer compared to physics and chemistry,massively complicates matters. Yes, the Consensus holds the existence of microorganisms, biotic processes, evolution, and the like, but individual Paradigms don't and mages tend to be all over the place when it comes to Life, compared to Forces or Matter as the other two Pattern spheres.

                                                    For example, you can use Life magic to preserve, say, a pound of fresh meat. The animal that pound of meat came from is most likely dead, so that particular Life pattern is gone. It doesn't matter precisely what Effect the mage uses to preserve the meat, what matters is they can. So, strictly speaking, as a constituent part of an organism that retains enough viability to sustain or propagate Life patterns, you could technically use Life magic to make (fresh) urine. Humans can survive (in the short term) drinking urine for wont of potable water, and fresh urine due to its urea content is excellent plant fertilizer. Cold, stale piss would obviously be a Matter pattern, however (which bears out, considering urea naturally decays into ammonia).

                                                    Don't ask me why in God's name a mage would want to use Life magic to make pee-pee. Maybe an Ecstatic with a golden shower fetish would want to do it, I dunno. Or maybe a Tradition mage a bit lacking in the wits and intelligence department, caught in a survival situation and didn't catch onto the notion the same Life magic they're using to Bear Grylls that motherfucker could be just as easily used to purify ambient water.
