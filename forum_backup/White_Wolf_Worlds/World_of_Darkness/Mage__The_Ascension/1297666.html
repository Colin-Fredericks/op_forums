      Hmh. Let's see...

      Iteration X
      --------------
      Whenever I think of a high-end ItXer, I think of Metal Gear Rising's Raiden, and of course Tony Stark.
      Examples of a (very) high end cyborg in Raiden's case ( Just watch a few clips of MgR: Revengeance and enjoy the insanity...), and Stark's more artisan-inclined ItXer who just breaks conventional physics on several levels on his own, because he can. Er, because technology can. He's just SO GOOD at it.

      EDIT: And of course someone like Motoko Kusanagi/The Major - also a high end cyborg - but while being capable of superhuman physical feats, is more enlightened in her mental capabilities, being able to understand and manipulate data in ways and at speeds that make her essentially demigod-like in that regard. She is rocking Data 5 - at least. If you use one of the two systems of Archspheres, she has Data 6 or higher, depending on the GitS source material (Anime movies, Mangas, Stand Alone Complex series, Arise series...) you want to use. It makes her almost alien in a couple of them, more grounded in others - but even in the latter case, you sometimes get the vibe that she is close to a sort of second awakening. She would be ItX's actual Dante - especially due to her having been a cyborg since a young age.

      Progenitors
      ----------------
      I can't think of something that *really* fits off the top of my head, but Progenitor-like characters like Poison Ivy (with some leeway), certain baddie masterminds of the Resident Evil series (without implying the Progenitors are bad, we're just going off abilities here..), and other experts at downright unreal medicine, genetic manipulation and biology. I am sure there are some characters that are absolutely over-the-top that would fit the bill, but I just can't think of anyone specific right now.

      Syndicate & The NWO
      ---------------------------------
      This one is...tough! Due to the fact that both groups prefer subtle magics all around, it's hard to pinpoint good character examples, but if you want to a general concept of a high-end Syndicate member, just look in the direction of conspiracies. The Illuminati and other groups that control the economy (and indeed the world) at a whim through godlike influence, intimidation, financial power and seemingly having their fingers in everything. Now that I think about it, David Xanatos from Gargoyles might very well be a high Arete Syndicate member. There is a reason he has a trope named after him. Actually, Several of them.

      Now, the NWO has a strange example I can think of. It's not literally an NWO-like character, but if you've seen the movie 'Upgrade', the whole fighting style the main character displays is definitely something a high-end White Suit would pull off - combined with downright paradoxical applications of unrealistic damage. Add a splash of super-human moves and senses of the Agents from the Matrix, along with impromptu gadget-tinkering from MiB, and psychological control along Dark City, all wrapped into a single person. Scary. Really scary.

      Void Engineers
      ----------------------
      Hummh. Admittedly? I have no idea. The child of a W40k SPESS MEHREEN and MacGyver. In spaaaaaaaace!

                                  To me, a character at this level can do things like:
                                  -Create large scale changes in subconscious feelings towards things
                                  -Exhibit almost arbitrary technopathy
                                  -Detect cosmic phenomenon through how they affect flow of forces far subtler than mortal science (detect hawking radiation)
                                  -Dispel quantum randomness - Generate systems that function perfectly without flaw even when intruded on by outside forces (meissner effect something something)
                                  -Create next level meta materials like room temperature semi conductors or room temperature stable ice
                                  -Thumb through the collective consciousness/zeitgeist to find a piece of information known by anyone
                                  -Leverage non-locality of spacetime to move resources between non-comoving spaces or treat areas like closed spacelike (not timelike) curves.
                                  -Codify seemingly random universal phenomena into forces
