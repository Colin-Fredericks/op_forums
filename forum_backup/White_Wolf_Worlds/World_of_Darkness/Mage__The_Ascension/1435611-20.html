                                                            How I run Technocracy games

                                                            I always run Technocracy games with generally the appeal of being government agents crossed with Vampire the Masquerade. You are part of a secret government agency like SHIELD and are either one of its scientists or field operatives. However, the fact is your superiors are evil. They will often try to get you to do things that are blatantly immoral or that benefit them personally. It's a bit like working for a cyberpunk megacorporation where if you're LUCKY then they won't sell you out after asking you to kill a rival superior.

                                                            However, the Technocracy is not an organization that you can just quit. You have credit cards, social security numbers, mortal relatives, and friends. If you defect from the Union then you will find yourself wanted as a pedophile, your family will be brainwashed into believing it, maybe your girlfriend will be replaced by a clone with orders to kill you when you pick her up, and that's assuming you can physically defect in the first place without them activating the microbomb in your brain that you never even knew was there.

                                                            But maybe you can do some good in the Union if you're not a monster. The Technocracy does oppose the Nephandi, Marauders, renegade Umbrood, and a lot of Traditionalists who think they're heroic freedom fighters but blow up computer factories that are only tangentially associated with the Union. Also, how would you even know if they were lying that the Order of Hermes member doesn't sacrifice babies. I mean, it says he does right here in his file.

                                                            Plus, you can be a moderating influence a lot of times. Sure, it says to gas the church with nerve gas because they believe in the faith healer! However, maybe you can figure out a way to convince them that it was all placebos as well as mind-control! That's, in a way, saving dozens of lives! Imagine if you weren't there.

                                                            And, at one point, you realize that you can just go over your superior's head and get him cancelled.

                                                            Then YOU'RE making the decisions.
