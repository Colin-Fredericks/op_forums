                            -If the mages and union have the upper hand, and they attack the caern by surprise, then the bsd are fucked.

                            - if by any chance the bsd are aware of the assault even less than an hour before the actual attack, the union will encounter serious resistance and might not even be able to win the fight or they will suffer lot of casualties, and their best resort is attack from distance, bomb, and retreat.

                            -if by any chance the bsd pit is fully aware of the incoming attack hours or days before, and even worst they have nephandi allies and powerful spirits on their side... well then somebody will probably have a hard time explaining to the technocratic board how the heck an entire army of cyborgs, scientists and special corps simply disappeared without trace...
