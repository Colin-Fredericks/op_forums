            Is it really childish to point out that Mage and Werewolf operate on very fundamentally different setting conceits? Differences that are not immediately obvious due to the fact the way the games are typically played are not dissimilar and the setting information that paints the picture is somewhat obscure?

            in both cases, you have a team of 3-5 people, each with their own special powers who run around having adventures. Because that's what you do when you tabletop game. So you go "Oh hey, there are these guys with powers that seem to counter mages. Cool."

            I like the Union. I will freely admit to that, I am a TOTAL Union fanboy.

            But the reason the Union is strong isn't munchkin tricks. It's because the union operates on a modernized industrial scale. They don't make a relic uberweapon and hand it down to the champion of the convention.

            No. They build 2000 slightly less good ones and pass them out to every soldier on the front lines.

            You don't see this outside of the fluff. Because that's not the way the game is played. The game is played with small teams of 3-5 people. With fleshed out characters who all know each other and generally have some plot they follow.

            They have some unique rules that are cool and help to represent this facet but it's not really the same as looking it straight in the face.

                                In cWoD, every splat has their own problems (Traditions versus Technocracy, Camarilla versus Sabbat, Seelie versus Unseelie, etc). While one faction could probably launch a war against another splat, they would leave themselves open to annihilation by their opposite faction for questionable gains. Yes, the Technocracy could possibly wage war against the Garou, but they would likely weaken themselves to such a degree that every other Mage faction would eat them alive. The same applies to the rest of the splats, so you end up with a five-sided Cold War (Fera vs Mage vs Vampire (Kindred and KJ) vs Wraith vs Changeling). While there are individual and local conflicts, each faction avoids all-out war with the other splats for fear of what their opposite number would do. That is why the appearance of Demons and Hunters upset things, they disturb the balance.
