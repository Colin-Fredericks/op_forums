                                                  Imagine the scene.

                                                  The room opens up to an incredibly white space, a single comfortable chair sits in the centre, with a simple warm and inviting sign stating "please take a seat". The air is warm, and the chair inviting. The doors close behind the subject with barely a sound as the subject is met with a sudden urge to rest.

                                                  They take a seat, and from within the corners of their mind a soothing voice speaks softly.

                                                  "You have done so well" it says.
                                                  "Please take this moment to relax and reflect on your hard work. Would you like some tea?"
                                                  Before they have a chance to respond, their choice appears before them, perfectly made and at the exact right temperature.
                                                  "Your service to the union has been a valuable and significant one. The Union is grateful for your services"
                                                  Everything within the room is comforting to the subject, they feel as though they could leave any time they want, but would very much prefer to stay.

                                                  "We understand you have concerns, and the Union understands that perfection has yet to be attained. We value your concerns and would be happy to discuss them with you."

                                                  At this point, the subject is invited to speak their mind, talk openly about how they feel. The conversation continues and the subject will feel as though they are truly being listened to, that their concerns are being met. That the Union is there for them, and they are there for the union.

                                                  If this does not work, then the chair will shift into restraints, the soft white glow falls away, and a more mechanical scene comes into view. Perhaps a more direct approach is required.
