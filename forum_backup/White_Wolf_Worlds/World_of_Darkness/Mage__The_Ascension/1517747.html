    Alternative Spheres

    07-12-2023, 05:36 AM
    One of the benefits of the magic system in Mage: The Ascension
    is that it encourages the players to think outside the box and
    challenge their assumptions about how the world works. The
    magic system is based on nine spheres of influence, such as
    Correspondence, Forces, Life, Mind, and Spirit, that represent
    different aspects of reality. Each sphere has a rating from
    1 to 5, indicating the level of mastery and control that the
    mage has over that aspect. The higher the rating, the more
    complex and powerful effects the mage can achieve.

    Mage: The Ascension is a role-playing game that explores the
    themes of magic, reality, and belief. In the game, mages are
    individuals who can manipulate reality with their will, using
    a system of nine spheres that represent different aspects of
    existence. However, some mages may choose to use alternate
    spheres, either as a supplement or a replacement for the
    standard ones. In this article, we will discuss some of the
    reasons and implications of using alternate spheres in
    Mage: The Ascension.

    One reason to use alternate spheres is to reflect a different
    paradigm or worldview. The nine spheres are based on the Hermetic
    tradition of Western occultism, which may not suit every mage's
    culture, background, or philosophy. For example, a Native American
    mage may prefer to use the four elements of earth, air, fire, and
    water instead of the spheres of matter, forces, life, and spirit.
    A Buddhist mage may use the five skandhas of form, sensation,
    perception, mental formations, and consciousness instead of the
    spheres of correspondence, mind, entropy, prime, and time. A
    cyberpunk mage may use the five nodes of data, hardware, software,
    wetware, and netware instead of the spheres of correspondence,
    matter, forces, life, and mind.

    Another reason to use alternate spheres is to create a unique or
    specialized style of magic. The nine spheres are broad and
    encompassing, but they may not cover every possible effect or
    expression of magic. For example, a mage who specializes in dreams
    may use the sphere of oneiros instead of the sphere of mind. A mage
    who focuses on healing may use the sphere of salus instead of the
    sphere of life. A mage who deals with fate and destiny may use the
    sphere of fortuna instead of the sphere of entropy.

    Using alternate spheres can have both advantages and disadvantages
    for mages. On one hand, it can allow them to access effects or
    abilities that are not possible or difficult with the standard
    spheres. It can also help them to avoid paradox, the backlash that
    occurs when reality rejects their magic, by using more subtle or
    appropriate forms of magic for their paradigm. On the other hand,
    it can limit their versatility and compatibility with other mages
    who use different systems. It can also expose them to new dangers
    or enemies that are related to their alternate spheres.

    Using alternate spheres in Mage: The Ascension can be a rewarding
    and challenging way to explore different aspects of magic and reality.
    However, it is not without risks and consequences. Mages who use
    alternate spheres should be aware of their benefits and drawbacks,
    and be prepared to face the challenges and opportunities that come
    with them.

    Using alternate spheres can make the game more realistic in
    several ways. First, it can reflect the diversity and complexity
    of human cultures and beliefs, and show that there is no one
    true way to understand and manipulate reality. Second, it can
    create more interesting and dynamic conflicts between mages who
    have different paradigms and systems of magic, and challenge them
    to find common ground or overcome their differences. Third, it
    can encourage creativity and experimentation among players and
    storytellers, who can explore new aspects of magic and reality
    that are not limited by the standard rules and assumptions of the
    game.

    I've created a system of 12 spheres which I use in my game.
    They are listed here, and described in detail:

    01. Sphere of Prediction
    02. Sphere of Feeling
    03. Sphere of Production
    04. Sphere of Influence
    05. Sphere of Penetration
    06. Sphere of Control
    07. Sphere of Restoration
    08. Sphere of Vanishing
    09. Sphere of Travel
    10. Sphere of Transformation
    11. Sphere of Destruction
    12. Sphere of Revelation​

    There are 12 Spheres in this system. Each Sphere connects to one of the 12 signs of the Zodiac. And each Zodiac sign connects to one of 10 planets in Astrology. The 10 planets and 12 signs connect with the 22 Major Arcana of the Tarot.

    Everything in the universe of Mage exists as Body Mind and Spirit. Events in this universe follow patterns of Dynamism, Stasis, and Entropy. Each Sphere of magic follows into these categories. Outlined here:

    Code:
    Body Control: Energy / Telekinesis (Aries / Emperor) + (Mars / Tower) Transformation: Developing / Shapeshifting (Taurus / Hierophant) + (Venus / Empress)
    Code:
    Spirit Prediction: Wisdom / ESP (Libra / Justice) + (Venus / Empress) Revelation: Visions / Rapture (Pisces / High Priestess) + (Neptune / Hanged Man)
    Code:
    Mind Feeling: Empathy / Telepathy (Virgo / Hermit) + (Mercury / Magician) Influence: Charisma / Hypnotism (Leo / Strength) + (Sun / Sun)
    Code:
    Dynamism Penetration: Escape / Intangibility (Gemini / Lovers) + (Mercury / Magician) Travel: Direction / Teleportation (Sagittarius / Temperance) + (Jupiter / Wheel of Fortune)
    Code:
    Stasis Production: Attraction / Creation (Capricorn / Devil) + (Saturn / World) Restoration: Renewal / Healing (Aquarius / Star) + (Uranus / Fool)
    Code:
    Entropy Vanishing: Stealth / Invisibility (Cancer / Chariot) + (Moon / Moon) Destruction: Curses / Annihilation (Scorpio / Death) + (Pluto / Judgement)
    Each Sphere is ranked by a scale of 5 dots

    Code:
    • Awareness •• Sympathetic ••• Coincidental •••• Manipulative ••••• Miraculous
    The Spheres are further defined here:


    Power of Prediction - Wisdom/ESP

    Realization - Unveiling the inner knowledge within.


    The Power of Prediction manifests as inspiration and insight, granting effortless access to answers and discoveries beyond conventional understanding. It endows the practitioner with the gift of foreknowledge. The key to unlocking this power lies in the realization that all knowledge already resides within oneself.

    Code:
    • Elevated Mental Functions •• Increase to Skills ••• Influencing Situations •••• Seeing the Future ••••• Slowing Time
    Power of Feeling - Empathy/Telepathy

    Association - Recognizing the interconnectedness of all things.


    The Power of Feeling enables one to empathize and understand others deeply, perceiving their thoughts and emotions without the need for words. It stems from the profound insight into the Association, the intrinsic link that binds all living beings together.

    Code:
    • Reading Mindstates •• Bonus to Social Abilities ••• Mental Shield •••• Reading Thoughts ••••• Mental Link
    Power of Production - Attraction/Creation

    Synchronicity - Uniting the threads of existence.


    The Power of Production empowers a wizard to draw situations and desires into their reality. This ability stems from a profound understanding of Synchronicity, the cosmic principle that everything is interconnected and influences each other. It acknowledges that the power to attract lies within, awaiting manifestation.

    Code:
    • Sensing Opportunity •• Arranging situations ••• Attracting Wealth •••• Produce Small Objects ••••• Win Lotteries & Elections
    Power of Influence - Charisma/Hypnotism

    Possession - Will shaping the realm of the mind.


    The Power of Influence grants a wizard the power to captivate others. Known as Possession, it allows one to enchant and charm, eliciting positive perceptions or even gaining control over another's thoughts and actions. The essence of this mastery lies in the understanding that one's will can wield dominion over the minds of others.

    Code:
    • Sensing Willpower •• Social Influence ••• Hypnotic Suggestion •••• Mind Control ••••• Altering Identities
    Power of Penetration - Escape/Intangibility

    Spiritualism - Perceiving the world as a realm of spirit.


    The Power of Penetration delves into the realm of Spiritualism. It unveils the truth that the world is fundamentally composed of spirit, allowing a wizard to transcend physical boundaries and become intangible. Chains and locks lose their hold, dissolving effortlessly under the wizard's touch.

    Code:
    • Find Exits •• Open Locks ••• Escape From Restraints •••• Escape From Prisons ••••• Becoming Intangible
    Power of Control - Energy/Telekinesis

    Evocation - Harnessing the forces of the spiritual realm.


    The Power of Control enables a wizard to command powerful forces. Through evocation, the practitioner can manipulate energy and bend it to their will. This understanding arises from recognizing that spiritual entities are themselves embodiments of force and possess the capacity to shape and impact the physical world.

    Code:
    • Quick Reactions •• Bonus to Physical Abilities ••• Influencing Physical Outcomes •••• Moving Objects ••••• Controlling Natural Forces
    Power of Restoration - Renewal/Healing

    Forgiveness - Liberating from suffering caused by transgressions.


    The Power of Restoration holds the gift of healing. It grants the ability to alleviate pain, cure diseases, and bring renewal to the afflicted. This power emerges from the understanding of Forgiveness, recognizing that suffering often stems from transgressions. By embracing forgiveness, a wizard becomes a conduit for healing and restoration.

    Code:
    • Sense illness •• Relieve Pain ••• Protection from Disease •••• Heal Sickness / Damage ••••• Resurrection
    Power of Vanishing - Stealth/Invisibility

    Misdirection - Manipulating attention and perception.


    The Power of Vanishing encompasses the art of Misdirection. It allows a wizard to control focus and attention, rendering themselves unseen and secrets concealed. Meticulous study and practice grant the ability to achieve complete invisibility as the wizard manipulates perception and directs attention elsewhere.

    Code:
    • See in The Dark •• Safeguarding Secrets ••• Stealth •••• Disguise ••••• Invisibility
    Power of Travel - Direction/Teleportation

    Unity - Dissolving the illusion of separation.


    The Power of Travel operates on the understanding that all separation is illusory. By harnessing this power, a wizard can navigate without traditional directions and locate objects or places that are lost or hidden. A skilled practitioner can transcend distance, stepping from one location to any desired destination.

    Code:
    • Locate Lost Objects •• Find Directions ••• See at a Distance •••• Teleport Objects ••••• Make a Portal
    Power of Transformation - Developing/Shapeshifting

    Personification - Authoring one's own identity.


    The Power of Transformation allows a wizard to assume the form and essence of another being, whether it be an abstract concept, an animal, or even a deity. The essence of this mastery lies in the understanding that one has the power to create and define their own identity, refusing to be shaped by external forces. The power of transformation can also be used to improve who you already are.

    Code:
    • Sense Physical Attributes •• Bonus to Physical Tests ••• Developing Physical Traits •••• Shapeshifting ••••• Transmutation of Objects
    Power of Destruction - Curses/Annihilation

    Pragmatism - Dissolving the constructs of right and wrong.


    The Power of Destruction holds immense power that should be wielded with caution. It encompasses the ability to curse, annihilate, and disrupt the natural order. However, the utilization of this power draws one away from their true path and blurs the distinction between right and wrong.

    Code:
    • Sense Black Magic •• Protection from Curses ••• Curse Enemies •••• Cause Physical Damage ••••• Kill & Destroy
    Power of Revelation - Visions/Rapture

    Above as Below - The symbiotic connection between gods, angels, and humanity.


    The Power of Revelation unveils profound visions and grants enlightenment to the practitioner. It is rooted in the understanding of the symbiotic connection between gods, angels, and humanity. By tapping into this power, a wizard gains access to celestial knowledge, perceiving the intricate tapestry that binds all realms and entities together.

    Code:
    • Sense Spirits •• Speak with Spirits ••• Banish Spirits •••• Summon Spirits ••••• Spirit Travel

      I've dabbled with this idea myself over the years. What I've settled on is the idea that the Traditions use the standard nine-Sphere model, though some mages supplement that with a Minor Sphere or two: see the Book of Fragments for a robust take on Minor Spheres, noting that this is a Storyteller's Vault product, and strictly unofficial.

      If I had the time and energy to flesh this out, the Crafts, meanwhile, would tend to feature modern-day versions of the Foundation and Pillars system found in Dark Ages: Mage, with each Craft having its own Foundation incorporating elements of a Focus that its members can't stray too far away from, and a set of Pillars that function more or less like modern Spheres but with a different organizing principle for each Craft. In effect, Alternative Spheres.

      For the Technocracy, I have worked out a system of Enlightenment and Fields that started out by taking the nine Spheres, subbing in all three of the Technocratic Variants from M20, and then tweaking them a bit further so that they'd reflect the way that Technocrats use Enlightened Science. That said, Enlightenment and Fields is, in practice, not really any different from what the Crafts do: the Technocratic Foundation is Enlightenment, and its Pillars are the Fields.

      The problem here is that making Alternative Spheres is hard, as in labor-intensive. Making one alternate set of Spheres is almost overwhelmingly difficult; and making one set per Craft is too much work.

      And then there are the Orphans, or independent mages. My thought for them has been to see if it's possible to do away with Spheres altogether: let them learn individual Rotes, without needing to learn Spheres in order to do so, but then give them an improvisational ability to perform Rotes that they haven't learned by using the Rotes that they have learned as a starting point, increasing the Difficulty based on how similar or different the improvised Rote is from known Rotes.

      This idea is very much a work in progress, currently in the alpha stage of production; I don't know if I'll ever get it to work. But if I can, I suspect that the end product won't be restricted to Orphans. If Orphans can learn Rotes without Spheres, then so can Traditions, Crafts, and even Technocrats — and there might even be some benefit to doing it in moderation, even when you have Spheres or Fields or Pillars available to you.

      My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

          I once, though I don't think it's saved anywhere, wrote a layout for a type of traditional Daoist mage that instead of using the 9 sphere system basically used a quasi pillar system, with there being only 3 (And an altered point buy to account for the massively decreased amount of spheres).
          Honestly in retrospect? Fits better for a sorcerer using sorcery paths. But they revolved around the Man/Earth/Heaven trio. Earth had underworld aspects in it IIRC, and dealt with certain parts of the soul. Heaven dealt with a few mind aspects, and other parts of the soul. Man did traditionally magic-y things, this is where the cool thunder magic would be.

          I've honestly enjoyed the concept of, but never had a chance to use in practice, the simpler examples of minor spheres. 'Sphere of fire' is pretty dang easy to conceptualize. Let it do something with fire forces can't normally do till 3, at like 2. Make it more niche in regards to fire. Let it do a bit more than it normally could do at different sphere levels to represent specializing in fire. And bam you have yourself a custom sphere.

          I find it easier to just write out what the spheres can do per level like the core book does instead of just coming up with rotes for each level, helps keep things fluid.
