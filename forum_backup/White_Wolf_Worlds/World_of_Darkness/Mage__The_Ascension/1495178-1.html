    Frankly, my single biggest “complaint” with the removal of the Mana Background with nothing to replace it is that it doesn't support the playstyle that Sorcerer Revised did. As I see it, M20 products are supposed to be backward compatible; you might change how some things are done from older editions, possibly even relegating some previous choices to “optional rule” status (preferably with some explanation as to why); but you shouldn't change if they're done at all. When I have to go back to SRev to do something that S20 doesn't allow, that's a defect in S20.

    That said, what's done is done. And I have S:PoP, which doesn't try to dictate a particular playstyle to me. I'm just disappointed that S20 does.

        Originally posted by Dataweaver View Post
        Frankly, my single biggest “complaint” with the removal of the Mana Background with nothing to replace it is that it doesn't support the playstyle that Sorcerer Revised did.         That's completely fair. We made design decisions that I feel support most styles of play while clarifying the differences between hedge magic and True Magick, and also providing casting rules that allow for personalization of character through Paradigm while keeping all Path Magic running on the same engine. The function and description of the Mana Background didn't fit with the narrative or mechanical vision we set out to create. We included Artifact precisely because it functions on its own set of rules, mechanically distinct from Wonder. Having said that, you can always use the Mana Background from Revised or the Avatar/Genius background from M20 to grant sorcerers a Quintessence pool in your game.

        As for the backwards compatibility argument, I'm not sure I follow your point. You *can* use the Mana Background from Revised if you choose to. If you want to stick completely to M20, use the Avatar/Genius Background from M20. It's not included in the official list because sorcerers don't have Awakened Avatars. The only mechanical element that Background provides is the ability to absorb, store, and spend Quintessence. What this says to me is that those are the mechanical functions of the Awakened Avatar, so it doesn't make much sense to have sorcerers have the same ability under a different name.

        But you don't have to go to a previous edition to make M20 Sorcerer work the way you want it to. You only need to add a single word to a single list on p. 104

            I've always taken it as a given that, barring Gilgul or an equivalent extraordinary circumstances, every human has an Avatar. For most Sleepers, the ability to store and channel Quintessence isn't something they'd have much use for; they don't have any way of spending it, and nobody has any way of taking it from them. So for most Sleepers, it's not so much that they can't take it as that there's little point in taking it.

            But sorcerers, though Sleepers, are different: with their Hedge Paths, they do have something that they can spend Quintessence on. Likewise, characters with The Flow of Ki, Force of Spirit, or Strength of Psyche have things they can spend Quintessence on, and so the Avatar Background becomes relevant to them.

            What's different between a sorcerer's Avatar and a mage's Avatar isn't whether or not it's capable of storing Quintessence; it's whether the Avatar is dormant or active — that is, the narrative aspects of having an Awakened Avatar: its tendency to involve itself in your life and to push you to grow. And unless you have Manifest Avatar or something similar, that's not much of a difference: by default, the narrative effect of the Avatar is subtle and easy to miss.

            In short, I do think that it makes sense for sorcerers to be able to access the mechanical benefits of the Avatar Background, but not the narrative effects.

            My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

                                                        So my book arrived and I went through it and despite my initial 'worry' about things the changes I made were actually much less than I feared they would be based on quick glancing and also my changes to the previous edition. Going from start to end these were the sticky notes I made into the book.

                                                        Casting Rules
                                                        Dice Pool: Attribute + Ability
                                                        Cost: 1 Willpower
                                                        Time: 1 Turn per 2 Path levels.
                                                        Difficulty: Path level + 3.
                                                        I used some of the sidebars from Sorcerer Revised to make the above. I also wrote this out because the info is in a paragraph and not in a sidebar or in an immediately seen section.

                                                        Aspects
                                                        Distance - Less a house rule and more a note, but I am all for allowing scores or hundreds of miles to be teleported through if one wants to say travel regional or continental distances.
                                                        Passengers - Move dots upward. So o is "Yourself, your clothing, and a small amount of stuff" and ooooo is "Five passengers". I have less than zero interest in not allowing clothes to transport with the person.
                                                        I might do some changes to some of them but in general they work as they are.

                                                        Complications
                                                        Simplified Aspect - Always considered in play. Never a time when it won't.
                                                        Witnesses - These do not matter! A sorcerer can cast their magic in the middle of a baseball stadium and disbelief will have ZERO impact.
                                                        Fast Casting - Is obviously changed slightly due to the 1 turn per two Path levels rule change.
                                                        My only other note is that all the modifications, minus Witnesses, are applied.

                                                        Paths
                                                        Conjuration - The modifier section about Witnesses was removed.
                                                        Divination - Less a house rule and more an easier way to implement things. So I am thinking of bring over the Trinity idea of 'Dramatic Editing'. This would represent 'I planned ahead'. Maybe successes on a Divination roll would grant a pool of successes that can be used at other points.
                                                        Fascination - If dislike/hate can add to the difficulty then like/loved can decrease difficulty. Also I added a note that Duration is an acceptable Aspect.
                                                        Healing - I didn't so much change as add this note: Mostly so I can easily see what can be spent and for what.

                                                        1 success per Bashing
                                                        1 success per 2 Lethal
                                                        1 success per other healing
                                                        +1 for Aggravated
                                                        +2 for Regeneration
                                                        Hellfire - Spells take One turn to cast.
                                                        Quintessence Manipulation - Spells cast in/at a Node have reduced Difficulties based on rating.
                                                        I didn't make any notes on Psychic Paths or the Practitioners chapter.

                                                        I will note that I really like how Psychic Hypnosis works and I also really like the Arcanum group.

                                                        I will also note that if I was to run a Sorcerer game I would not touch the Traditions/Technocracy at all. Maybe there could be an Elder Sorcerer with special abilities but yeah, as it is Awakened Magic would not be a focus or have a presence in such a game.

                                                        Character Creation
                                                        Process - I'm using the variant rules in Revised for point spread. That is 7/5/3 Attributes, 13/9/5 Abilities, 7 Backgrounds, 6 Numina, 15 Freebies.
                                                        Secondary Numina - I'm removing this 21 XP cost surcharge. If you want to spread out between sorcery and psychic stuff go right ahead at normal cost.
                                                        Backgrounds - Those available to Traditions can be gotten by anyone, no need to restrict Chantry, Node, Retainers, and Sanctum.
                                                        Avatar - I am specifically bringing back the Avatar background to represent personal Quintessence collection. I'm thinking of adding a / to the name for all that mechanically its the same.
                                                        Merits - I specifically made a note that these are available and that they cost 3 XP per dot.
                                                        And that's it. Most of my notes are clarifications rather than outright changes.

                                                        So yeah basically I really enjoyed the book and I am glad it was made and that I have it in my collection. Multiple ideas came to me while reading the material.
