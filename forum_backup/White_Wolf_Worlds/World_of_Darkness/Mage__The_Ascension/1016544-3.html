            Originally posted by KingJohn View Post

            Mind 2 it's also for instilling/reducing emotions

            What is 'rage points into napalm" mean?
            Don't know for sure, but I assume it's to use Forces 3 to transmute the psychic energy into fire. Which would both reduce it and damage the werewolf (which would increase it afterwards, so I'm not sure how efficient would this be. I'm inclined to say "not much", but these things can vary).

            I don't think Mind/Forces alone should work to transmute Rage because it has a strong spiritual aspect (I would add Spirit 3).

            And the Thing is, I assume, how they shift?
            I think it refers to the Beast/Rage (was mentioned in the previous sentence).

            One would need to use Spirit 5 to do that, perhaps coupled with equally great levels of Mind and some Prime

            Also, given Werewolves's high soaking dice, is it even possible for a spirit mage to touch them?
            Yes, to touch them you can use your hands. It's Spirit 2 if they're on the Penumbra. Keeping your hands (or life) after doing that it's not guaranteed

            Damaging them may be harder, though. It depends on the nature and characteristics of the attack
