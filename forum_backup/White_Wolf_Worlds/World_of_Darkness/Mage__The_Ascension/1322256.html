                                          Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz View Post
                                          Which book is that in?
                                          Mage: The Sorcerers' Crusade's core book, which actually has a really in depth multi-page timeline covering the history of the Awakened up until around the 16th century AD, including a bunch of details about stuff from ancient Egypt, parts of Africa and Asia, and various obscure factions throughout history.

                                          My own theories are that anything before around 2000 - 3000 BC is probably not exactly Reality as we know it, and that what archeological and other evidence of these prehistoric periods that exist in the WoD are probably remnants of a multifaceted reality full of blood gods, dragon kings, fey lords, angels, demons, and who knows what else.
                                          After this antediluvian age collapsed into a single reality, you probably have a long period in which history and myth are close to the same thing. In the West, especially around the Mediterranean region, this probably ended in the Bronze Age with what we remember as the Trojan War and the Bronze Age Collapse. In India, you have the end of the Vedic period, and in China, the Spring & Autumn period.
                                          I think the Mythic Age really goes back as far to Classical Greece and Late Period Egypt, as well as Sixteen Kingdoms/Mahajanapadas era India, Warring States era China, and Persia's Achaemenid Empire. This period is constantly interspersed with various empires (Alexander the Great, the Romans, the Qin dynasty, etc.) trying to assert a certain degree of cultural dominance, and a recurring theme of factions within those empires often clashing over influence of the culture as a whole. Rome had its Cults of Mercury, the Brotherhood of the Rule, and rival Celestial Chorus forerunners, while China has its Legalists, assorted Akashic factions, and so forth. I tend to place the 13th and 14th centuries, especially the Black Death and the Mongol invasions, as the end of the Mythic Age in Eurasia.
                                          Such are my thoughts, at least.

                                          I think a clearer division would be something like:
                                          • The Unknown/Antediluvian - before @2500 BC
                                          • The High Mythic Age - @2000 BC - @400 BC
                                          • The Mystic Age - @300 BC - @1350 AD
