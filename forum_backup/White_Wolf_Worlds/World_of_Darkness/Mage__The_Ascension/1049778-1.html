    Originally posted by Dogstar View Post
    >why Entropy 3 instead of 2
    For the Headshot rote you're right, it should be 2, however for the two gun rote I'm requiring a higher level of entropy as it is more like controlling then influencing. Entropy has some pretty subtle distinctions between 2 and 3 and I'm choosing that this effect is more difficult to pull off than a similar but easier Mind 1/Entropy 2 effect.
    Entropy 3 is control over matter. Entropy 2 is control of probabilities, control of odds. It doesn't matter if an effect is easier/harder to do, that is represented by a higher amount of successes needed, no by requiring bigger Sphere levels.

    For example, look at the rote "octopodes" that I put just a little above. Turning a squid into a freaking killing machine is by no means easy... But it still requires only Life 2... Because that's what Life 2 does (change simple life - invertebrates and bellow).

                Well, possibilities are limitless, actually. As an effort of creativity, I always try to come with the best uses from levels 1-3 of Spheres, specially levels 1 and 2, for maximum impact over all those green Mages out there. So, lets try a little list

                Correspondence 1

                Sonar Vision - Remember the Batman movie, the Dark Knight? His sonar vision, that allowed him to see foes over the walls and in total darkness? Yep, that's not an insignificant thing.
                Also, use Correspondence 1, Forces 1, and you can have that machine that he built that can track anyone by the voice, which he used to track the Joker (in the case of the device, it would be a wonder built using Prime, but anyway).


                Forces 1 - Forces 1 need some talk about, because the amount of things it can do is impressive. So, here are some of the top of my mind, but certanly not the only ones

                Heat Vision/X Ray Vision - very useful to see in the dark; alternatively, Dark Sight for more mystical paradigms, that allows you to see at total dark as if it were totally bright day (with color vision).

                Seismic Sense - that is the same sense that many animals (some reptiles, insects, arachnide and several others) have to perceive tiny vibrations on the soil. This sense can even be used to perceive Vampires using Obfuscate or other creatures with similar supernatural means of concealing. With this sense, you can locate precisely where the source of the vibrations come, as well as many other informations - size, weight.
                What's more, you can have this toghether with Sonar Sense, allowing you to sense any sound vibrations, like an owl or a bat.

                Forces 2 - that's also another level VERY useful (and I say this as someone who doesn't like the Forces Sphere at all). A few examples of this versatility, beyond the classicals telekinetic, pirocinesis/cryocinesys

                Invisibility - just like that. Unlike a power like Obfuscation, this is not just a mental trick, it's true light invisibility.
                Toghether with Muffled Sounds (that works like level 1 Quietus, removing all sound), it means that you can walk around COMPLETELY indetectable by regular senses (you can still be detected by several supernatural means)
                True, absolute invisibility (meaning: being totally indetectable by ANY means - unless the one trying to find you can surpass your successes in your supernatural invisibility) would require... Hum... Let me think... Mind 1 to hide all your psichic emanations... Prime 2 to hide all your ressonance... And maybe Life 3+ to make your vitals undetectable? Maybe you'll also need Correspondence 2 IF you wanna be absolutely sure that not even Correspondence senses can pick you up, and Entropy 2 to "disguise" your "destiny role".

                So, there you have it, True Invisibility - Forces 2, Mind 1, Prime 2, Life 3, Entropy 2
                That's a lot of spheres but... This effect don't need to be applied as a single one, because in fact, each of those spheres can be cast as a different effect. That means that, a group of 4-6 initial PCs could do it without trouble.

                Concussive Grenade - you don't even need this as an effect, because you can ACTUALLY purchase this kind of mundane grenades. But, since military grade equipment may not always be a viable option, you can do this. A strong blinding light, with a strong, deafning sound. The purpose of this is to disorient the enemy. Enemies with heightned senses on will suffer.

                Simple Ilusions - the famous "hologram-like" illusions. Those are not mental tricks, but rather visual and sound illusions. Therefore, mind shields and other kinds of mental defenses won't show it's true nature (which could make it more believable). Hell, you can even have this effect with a kind of kinect force, making "solid light", so, an illusory brick wall made of light would be as "solid" as a real one (that will work as a force field, toghether along the light manipulation). Complex illusions (like illusions that can react to the viewer, like an illusory cat that can talk with the person), will need constant concentration of the Mage, unless he adds Entropy 2 for some simple interactions (like a robot that allows anyone who says a password to pass) or Mind 3, Entropy 2 for really complex things (like engaging in conversation).
                And, if you wanna go really hardcore on the level of weird and ridiculously vulgar that you can get, make your illusion permanent (with lots of successes), and use Spirit 3 to awaken it's spirit ()

                Wind Message (Forces 2, Corr 2) - that's not so useful or so powerful, but it's a classic. When you enter the evil necrimancer's Tower, the lich's cave... And you hear his evil laughs echoing all around? Yep, that's it...
                Talk hear, and be heard over there. Simple.

                    Life 2 - Plants

                    most people see Life 2 only as a source of self healing. That Sphere level, however, probably is one of the most insanely powerful levels that are.

                    With Life 2, you can control plants to a huge degree, in that manner of fantasy descriptions of druids that make the florest hunt his enemies. While in the woods, you can make plants entangle enemies, binding them, suffocating, and attacking.

                    You can also make plants cover your body, giving you a kind of "wood armor".

                    And that's just the beggining; after that, you can make all the modifications

                    It would be really hard to list all the modifications you can make to plants, but there are almost infinity possibilities; plant guardians for your chantry can be very Powerful (does anybody here remember that crazy Oak from Harry Potter that would smash anyone that get close with it's colossal strenght?). A Chantry protected by plants permanently modified with Life 2 will be a hellish place to try to invade (actually, the chantry itself can be made into a living organism that can fight to defend itself). And, a simple Life 1 effect (Pherormones!) can be used to control the chantry's pets, allowing them to tell friends from foes.

                    Other modifications to plants include:

                    Special drugs and substances production - did you know that Kevlar is an organic substance? You could have plants that produce chitin (the same substance of nails, insects exosqueletons and ivory, a very tough substance that could be used even for making armors), spider silk as strong as steel (more actually), alcahool (very useful is a fuel and power source), several kinds of acids, poisons, drugs. And they could produce those on massive amounts. All those plants could be modified in a matter of days, maybe even hours or minutes (well, since you'll be doing all this in your chantry, you won't need to worry about the paradox).

                    With just Matter 2, you can have your precious plants to produce gasoline, oil, kevlar, plastics of all sorts. You can have an industrialized raw matterial production fully grow... Just "cultivate" your "oil refinery".

                    And that's only with plants

                        Life 2 - Microbes

                        Well...

                        What's really the limit here? First of all, just build your very own custom-designed disease. Contagion agent (air, water, blood, skin contact, animal transmissor - insects, birds, livestook, consuption of contaminated food, etc). Determine the symptons (hemorragic, behavioral changes, alergic reactions, necrosis of tissue, diarrhea, fever, convulsions, heart attack, coma, death - whatever). Has anyone saw movies like "I'm the Legend" or "Z War" (I guess it was that, with Brad Pitt)? About insanely angry zombies? Well, there is a disease of the real world (in portuguese it is called "Rage", I don't know in english) that changes the behavior of the affected (it's the mad dogs disease, that makes dogs extremely violent, trasmitted by their saliva). So... You could turn people (or dogs) into mindless drones of pure rage (again, life 1 - Pherormones! - can keep those zombies from attacking you). Sífilis is another behavioral changer disease. It makes people affected to be... Always horny. A pitty that both of those diseases will cause a horrible death to those affected, because I would love to be affected by a "lust disease" without further side effects.

                        Alternatively, you can use Life 2 to make yogurts, bread, cheese, ale, wine.

                        You can use Life 2 to become the enviromental cleaner too. Use it to purify an area of any "wyrm taint". You can do this by creating pollution-eater microbes, that can eat plastic and, since you're a Mage, maybe even plumb and arsenic.

                        You could also produce microbes that can do things that would need bigger Life levels, in theory, but thats up to the ST. You could in theory, produce a vírus that increase your brain capacity (giving more mental attributes), or bacteria that can increase your physical attributes, or that can índice specific changes. Too overpowered? Maybe, maybe not. First of all, it's not direct control. So, the ST can state that any such changes take days, or weeks, or months, or even years, to happen (so, no super mutant vírus that gives you super strenght or steel skin in a few seconds only). The attributes increase may be capped to only 1 by this method, and without the capacity of going above 5. The change may linger for a few days... And disappear forever afterwards (your imunologic system made you "immune" to those blessings... Build a new virus pal - or use Life 3 to change your imune system, but at this point, its easier to simply use Life 3 to those effects). And... It's possible to have some side effects (but I would leave those for paradox).

                                                Luck Aura - Entropy 2

                                                Whenever a good thing happens, it seeks you out first. And bad things will dodge from you.

                                                Each success grants you one random positive event, or divert one random negative event. For example, if in a scene, someone would find a $100 note in the floor, that would be you. If someone would be hit by a car - that WOULDN'T be you.
                                                This effect makes more sense as an ritual with at least 1 day duration, and it's a great way to ensure survivability (at the ST discretion) by giving you an edge were you'll always seem to be on the right spot at the right time.
                                                It is recomended that the ST do the rolls in secret, and that the ST also keep the "good luck" account in secret from the player. On many occasions, a fortuitous happenstance may have just occured without the ST even being notified (like a guard that took a turn it otherwise wouldn't, at the exactly same moment the PC was sneeking inside the compound), so the player will never really know how much "good fortune" he still have left.
                                                That's a very powerful effect, althouth those that come to rely on luck too much may become too overconfident, and end up oon trouble when their luck runs out.

                                                (Of course, the player can use Entropy 1 to have a vague impression of how much "good luck" he still have left)

                                                        Improving the Machine - Entropy 3
                                                        This effect can have VERY powerful consequences. It is used to improve the capacity and the quality of ANY equipment, or to manufacture, using apropriate skills, some equip. So, here's a small (but not complete) list of what it can do, and the equipments may have SEVERAL of those improvements at once; when building a machine or piece of equip, all those modifications are permanent, otherwise, used on already made things they just give temporary boosts
                                                        - Give an additional +1 damage to any kind of weapons (be it a sword, fireweapons or a club), above any bonuses it may already have
                                                        - Give +1 soak for armor
                                                        - Remove any flaws from the equipment, even if they aren't visible;
                                                        - Make a broken machine work
                                                        - Make it easier to use, reducing dif by -1;
                                                        - Making it possible to built the item in a smaller/bigger or lightier/heavier version, as wished (only when used to build an item)
                                                        - Increasing the speed/efficiency of a machine (faster computers, with programs with fewer bugs, firearms with a better rate of fire, gatlin machibeguns less prone to overheat, vaccum cleaners with more sucking power, cars with more acceleration, increased potency, more fuel efficient, less polluent, etc)
                                                        - Increased durability
                                                        - "Self-correction System", giving 1 extra success in any skill use of the equipment (self balanced swords and knives, guns with correctional damping, cars with balance self adjustment, computers with inteligent directional self corrector software - whatever it is, that one will need a good description from the player).
                                                        No matter what changes, this effect only make POSSIBLE changes (meaning, improvements that would be conveivable with high levels of skills for manufacture - even if it would be epic beyond 6+ levels). For impossible changes (like making a battery out of milk or making your wooden sword as strong as steel and as sharp as a lightsaber, or making your computer be able to hack people's minds) you'll need other spheres (mostly, Matter)
