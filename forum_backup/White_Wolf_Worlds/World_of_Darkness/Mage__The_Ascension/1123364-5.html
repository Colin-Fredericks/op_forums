                                Originally posted by 11twiggins View Post

                                So the mental illusion is entirely sensory, not conceptual. They don't see their friend. They see a member of the Sabbat. Same goes for their other senses. And the same goes for their friend's perceptions.
                                So you're forcing an illusion upon all the senses of Steve the Ventrue that a sort-of-friend, Josh the Brujah, has a werewolf fang necklace and tons of body mods and tattoos and is screaming about how the Camarilla will fall. Steve has worked with Josh off-and-on for maybe 70 years in this city, during which time both have been ally and foe at various times. And you're presuming that this hallucination is enough for Steve to kill Josh? I really don't see how years upon years of experience with someone can be overridden that simply. Mind 3 does not grant enough control to keep Steve from saying, "What the hell, this makes no sense."

                                If you really want to cause vampire civil war with Mind and Correspondence from the comfort of your own home, I think you have a few options:

                                First - Mind 3/Correspondence 3 to throw sufficient traumatic thoughts at a vampire to get him to frenzy. Having lots of successes would make the frenzy check harder, and it makes more sense to save up one big hallucination rather than little ones - because he'll start to distrust his senses after it happens the first time. One vampire in frenzy isn't enough, though, and you'd probably need to frenzy most vampires in town - otherwise the remainder will band together and as an organized force, they can take out the others individually. So while it's not a bad way to take out a couple isolated vampires, it doesn't quite create your complete civil war. (You might be able to do this with Mind 2/Correspondence 2 by simply sending the emotion of rage, depending on your ST.)

                                Second - Mind 4/Correspondence 4 to forcibly control the conscious mind of many vampires and force them to take a bunch of destructive actions. Again here, depending on what you make them do, they'll probably get a roll or two to break the control, but you could do it on vastly fewer Kindred and turn them into your remote-control special ops squad. You'd need to know how to use a spec ops squad to take down lots of vampires quickly, so lots of research, but in theory plausible.

                                Third and most reliable - Mind 5/Correspondence 5 to slowly convert individual vampires into Manchurian candidates by rewriting their subconscious with specific triggers and new personalities embedded underneath. Nobody realizes anything is up until they all activate. You no longer need to directly control them - which would be a stress on the mage's own mind - because you've written new personalities specific to the tasks you've set them. You don't even need to know that much about HOW they should go about their destruction, because you're giving them a genuine motivation and letting them figure it out to the best of their abilities.

                                So it's not that you CAN'T do what you want with lower-level spheres - it's that your results are cruder and less effective, and require more planning/successes than they otherwise might.
