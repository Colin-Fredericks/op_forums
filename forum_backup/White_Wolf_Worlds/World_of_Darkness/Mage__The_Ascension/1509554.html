    How would you calculate strength for Telekinesis

    04-25-2023, 12:38 AM
    So, as the title suggests, how would you calculate the "strength" of a Telekinesis pull if a mage uses it to pull another creature against its will?
    Say the creature is fighting against it (as they naturally should), so what would the creature resisting roll be in your opinion? Rego Motus's text in Vampire states that if you want to force somebody with it, it's a contested Willpower roll to see if you can control the creature, I don't remember having read anything similar in Mage.

    A friend of mine says the test should be the Strength against the successes in the Mage's test, but I argued that it would almost always give the Mage a huge disadvantage as if you roll 3-5 dices for Arete against the Strength of the creature, that if, for example, is a werewolf or worse a vampire, would make it VERY hard to control. Let's imagine a Theo Bell with Strength 5 (that is, if he doesn't use blood to increase it) and Potence 4 and giving that the Potence could be considered automatic (depending on the ST), it would make it nearly impossible to move him with Telekinesis. I've never found a satisfactory system for such a situation. How would you treat this situation in your games? Have you found a good system for it?

                  I think it greatly depends on if the defender sees the attack coming and what capacity he has to resist it.

                  In normal circumstances I'd handle this as Dodging a Physical Attack (complete with the Perception + Awareness roll to even detect the attack if they don't already know its coming) with the dodge roll representing the defender getting out of the way or bracing in time. I'd only consider getting into contested strength rolls if the defender is already braced against something and the attacker keeps pulling.

                              There is one other system you could use for quick adjudication. I hate referencing How Do You Do That because it is a mess, but there's a chart in there for the lifting values of various levels of Forces. It goes something like this:

                              Forces 1: Nothing.
                              Forces 2: Around 300 pounds.
                              Forces 3: Around 2 tons
                              Forces 4: Small buildings
                              Forces 5: Most things smaller than a mountain

                              What you could do is use that chart to determine how many successes someone resisting with raw strength must muster to be able to resist a telekinetic pull. So if the Mage uses Forces 2, the Vampire would need to muster 4 successes. If the Mage uses Forces 3, the vampire would need 12-13. The feats of strength chart doesn't go high enough to cover Forces 4 and 5.

                              Is that the kind of scaling you are looking for?
