    Just do it the high-tech way

    04-25-2017, 12:21 PM
    So I have been thinking about magick in relation to my work. I work at a bank, and when people come in asking for things like cash, paper statements, checks, etc, I always wonder what's keeping them from doing things the more high-tech way. This lead naturally (of course) to me thinking about the Syndicate, and then to how the Technocracy works.
    So I know that per canon most Technocrats don't see what they do as "magic" it's Enlightened Science, which is cool on it's own. But, what if the Technocracy viewed themselves as just the advanced modern magic users? Basically, why are you using dirt and trees and crystals when you can use computers and guns and stuff?
    I know this changes a lot, making the Technocracy maybe a little kinder and gentler, and I know a lot of people won't like that. And I know this could potentially not work with the idea of consensual reality, which has always been confusing and hard to do right for me anyways. I guess this could be a more recent development. This doesn't mean that the Pogrom had to go away, obviously, particularly since Technocrats view the Traditions as unstable and dangerous to everyone magic users.
    Thoughts?

              You're assuming that modern is better inherently, or that it's true in the world of darkness, which isn't true for Magick. That is true for technological practicioners. That is true for our world (since the technocracy is a super conspiracy to explain our modernization!)

              "Better" is what fits your focus. Your modern verbena witch-detective obviously has a computer and ocasionally finds partners for holy communion on Tinder. She still bleeds for power and draws from her connection to the earth to see what the small animals of the neighborhood have seen recently. She starves naked under the moonlight for three days because that's how you build up for a prophecy.
              Your urban akashic sees the endless cacophony as just another hurdle to centering and emptying his mind, which will make him stronger. Modern medicine gives an useful framework for him to apply his chi channeling techniques where applicable. He'll still order his preferred incense from amazon since that nice peddler that smells faintly of rotten clove stopped coming to your neighborhood.

              Because that's how it works. Modernization of magickal practices must occur under their limitations. (just like how IRL modernization of productivity involves automation, since bettering people reached a viability cap)
              Who's to say the current grimoires on Simplified Enochian don't have more extensive, specific and effective entries for establishing dominance over dream-entities? Your complete facebook profile is definitely a level 4 simpathetic connection instead of 5.


              As the setting is drawn by standard most mages embrace technology. It just happens to be separate from how they do magic. For a mystic they're handy tools at best and deviations from the Way Things Should Be at worst.


              That said, you definitely have space to widen that gap between mysticism and the modern world. That is the primary consequence of the technocratic struggle and is to the detriment of the traditions. It's just that I agree with Aleph and feel that making the tech-haters traditionalists common instead of minoritary is kinda meh. The technocratic zeitgeist admitting what they do is definitely as impossible as what the mystics do is alright, and brings lots of uncomfortable questions to the upper echelons. They also very definitely succeeded at mundanizing many things they would call impossible a few decades before though, so "we do magic" is probably going to be as rare and scoffed as it is by default.
