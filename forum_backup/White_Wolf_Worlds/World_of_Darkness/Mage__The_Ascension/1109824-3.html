        On my way home I was pondering this discussion some more, and I think I boiled it it down to an essential question that shows the two different viewpoints that seem to be happening.

        We'll take the Choristers again, since they are our prime example in this discussion:
        Faith. Faith is the thing that defines the Chorus as a group. It's their shtick, their garb, the keyword that, as a group, separates them as a prime identity trait from 'Witchcraft' Verbena, 'Personal Dominion and Structured Rules' Hermetics, 'Shamanism' Dreamspeakers, etc. etc.
        That is fine and we are in agreement. Now, taking the Choristers again, is 'Faith', the word and concept that is associated with them the most...


        A) ...the common paradigm of the Celestial Chorus.

        or

        B) ...the thing that all the paradigms in the Celestial Chorus have in common.


        To me it always has been 'B', and the basis and line on which the different paradigms in that Tradition manage to communicate and get along with. It's the guidelines under which the many different groups got put together as 'Traditions' in the first place, back after ye olde dark ages.

        So far my viewpoint. What I wonder is, if you answer 'A'...what is your word for what's called 'Paradigm' in answer 'B'?

                                Originally posted by Muad'Dib View Post
                                Why would the Chakravanti (or other Tradition members) consider the Nephandi 'the opposite' of following a dharma/dharmas?

                                Where exactly is this written?
                                The job of the Euthanatos is to safeguard the Cycle of Existence, the dharma of the universe.

                                The avocation of the Nephandi is to derail the Cycle of Existence and undo the dharma of the universe, starting by accumulating and encouraging the accumulation of as much karma as possible and moving on from there.

                                There is no varna with that dharma. That's un-dharma. There really isn't a conceptual framework for it beyond karma, but it is actually so much more. If the purpose of existence is too move souls through the cycle of reincarnation, hopefully to higher and higher dharmas, but, if not, there is a system in place to encourage that movement and to regulate it if it's not in the "wrong" direction. Nephandi are that wrong direction. They aren't people getting rewarded with promotions or demoted for lack of competence (with the lure of future reward if they demonstrate competence in their new, lower position), they are terrorists, not just subverting the system, but bringing it crashing down into ruin. "Destroying all jobs and tearing down the factory" is not a valid job description.

                                As to how to suss them out. There is the problem of edition with that. So, "it depends" is one answer. Usually it's something to do with Resonance, though the meaning of that changes. So, using all the appropriate Spheres for detecting that (whatever those might happen to be, usually something to do with Mind and/or Prime). Not foolproof. Karma is going to show up pretty much the same in Nephandi vs. non-Nephandi and various forms of Quiet are going to look like/are karma. So offing everyone in Quiet might make things easy. (Though, if expiation of karma during a lifetime is a thing, then maybe not.)

                                If Qlipothic Spheres are a thing, then looking for the consequence of the use of those Spheres. (An Alternate Sphere system now usually doesn't mean just getting a two-in-one deal, so nuts to that, I say, but that's just what I say.) Of course, conjunctional effects with Prime and/or Entropy can pretty much do everything a Qlipothic Sphere is said to do (and often said to be the only way to do, but, nuts to that too, because there are always many ways to accomplish anything in Mage). That might be fairly easy to detect with just 1 dot in the Sphere in question, particularly a Pattern Sphere.

                                Just using the right Spheres in the right way, might catch them. Time might be a great way to do it. Look down the timeline and weigh the deeds. With conjunctional Mind, weight the intent as well. With Time and Spirit follow the soul into the next incarnation. Or use Mind. Some thoughts are Nephandi thoughts, though the thinker may not be one, they could be...

                                Sp, no easy way. If there were, Nephandi would get caught to the point of extinction. So use a few of the above. Get a friend or two that can use different detection methods and compare notes. And you still might be wrong. Especially since, when what you are doing is using a variety of hammers to hit a very particular kind of nail and a lot of other things also look like nails.
