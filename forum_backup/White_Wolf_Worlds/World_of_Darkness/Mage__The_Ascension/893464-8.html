    114) The Abomination

    "When we awaken, taking that first breath and opening our eyes, we inherit something special. A fragment of Creation, of God, of the Universe. It isn't always very powerful, most don't come with a grand destiny, but it's the greatest mentor you'll ever have. A force pushing you towards enlightenment and apotheosis. A warmth that keeps you company when all seems lost. It breaks my heart though... some magi inherit a poison, a darkness, a corruption. There is no crueler jest, and nothing makes me question my faith more. Innocent and weak, they embrace this new power and are lost before they have any chance whatsoever. Pray for them."

    "Nothing pisses me off more than listening to ecstatic types talking about their awakening. How they found it at the peak of a guitar solo, or when they first got laid, or the first time they danced in front of a crowd... how special and intimate and scary it was. They can't even begin to understand "Scary". Finding out that your new "friend" is a demon gnawing at the very core of your soul is scary. Jesus wept..."

    When Addison's awakening began, the situation was already far from pleasant. Wracked with chronic painful illness and a bitter seething anger, an idea resonated in his head. "Life really is shit." And that idea stuck...

    Opening his eyes for the first time, properly, his view became more and more pessimistic. Look at these people. How vapid and selfish they are. Isn't it a mercy that within 60 years most of them will be dead? It was only an abnormal force of will that stopped him from falling prey to his own Avatar (as most widderslainte do), that stopped him from becoming a Nephandus. Resisting descension came with a price; Addison is wracked and haunted by his own Avatar, and finds it opposing his growth and ascension at every turn. Practising magick is a painful and dangerous business for him, and he knows that he walks a dangerous tightrope. His avatar attracts all sorts of unsavoury spirits, and eventually he might just slip...

    Magick:

    Paradigm: Creation is deeply flawed, and we're on a One Way Trip to Oblivion, but I'm taking the devil with me! Addison may have a dark paradigm, but he entirely blames the Nephandi as a collective for his situation, rather than reality itself. Whoever fell and created this cursed mess of a soul is to blame, and he can use his curse to put that right.

    Practise: Gutter Magick, Maleficia; Addison's magick is cruel, but this cruelty is generally targeted at those who deserve it, or at the environment and animals. When all else fails the cruelty may be targeted at Addison; the only thing Addison really shares with his Avatar is a negative view of himself, so such a focus can act as a rare case of them working in harmony.

    Instruments: Ordeals and Exertions, Runes and Symbols, Blood and Fluids, Meat and Remains, Weapons, Animalism, Social Domination

    Addison applies his effects through a variety of stereotypically dark means. He might attack someone with a swarm of insects or vermin, command fate to curse someone by consuming a sacrificial animal or call upon the aid of a bane.

    Roleplaying:

    What matters to you is destroying Nephandi and their pawns. You are skeptical of those around you, and skeptical of yourself. You struggle to open up to people or trust them, and you resent other Mages for having a special connection with the universe. You hold no hope for a cure, and have no real faith in a higher power; you have faith in your own resolve and grit.

    If you were to learn to trust another person, you'd have an unconventional friendship. The greatest intimacy you're currently capable of is trusting another person to watch over you and do what needs to be done if you lose your footing.

    "Promise me that if I go off the rails, you'll try to capture me rather than kill me. I don't want some poor kid to inherit this mess."

    Mechanics:

    - Arete 3, Entropy 2, Spirit 1, Life 1, Forces 2

    - Advantages (M20) or Powers (Fomori: Freak Legion) to reflect the limited upsides of your body being warped into something monstrous. Lethal vomiting, a hardened body, warped teeth and fingernails... These may end up being mechanically very powerful (with the right combination of Powers a character can be incredibly tanky), however the social and mechanical downsides of being a widderslainte are huge; many important Arete rolls will be +1 difficulty due to the Avatar opposing the action, and Seekings will be nearly impossible without extreme preparation and outside help. On top of all of that, you look incredibly evil and dangerous at a glance...

    - Flaws which reflect all of the problems that arise from his condition. You are monstrous at a glance and suffer from chronic pain, for example.

            [This one is inspired by fairy tales in general, but more specifically by works like the anime Princess Tutu, and the upcoming game The Liar Princess and the Blind Prince.]


            115) In the Renaissance, Little Mouse was just what it sounds like: a mouse. Until, however, a beautiful prince showed her kindness, and Little Mouse fell in love with him. Because in those days, sometimes wild animals were not only intelligent and aware, but could be moved to human passions. Desiring to just be with the prince, Little Mouse sought out a fairy of the forest. It was an epic quest, by mouse standards, since she had to travel all the way from the prince's castle to the deep forest. Little Mouse came to emulate the courtesans and nobles of the royal court, and wielded a sewing needle to defend herself from threats.

            Impressed by her bravery, the fairy of the forest granted Little Mouse a boon, and changed the rodent into a girl. The fairy taught Little Mouse how to speak and employ witchcraft, and transformed the mouse's sewing needle into a fine human rapier, called Shirt Piercer. But the fairy also gave Little Mouse a weakness and a geas, as was common in fairy enchantments. The weakness: the old fear of cats could not be overcome; Little Mouse was as powerless in the face of felines as she ever was as a rodent. The Geas: Little Mouse would only ever have one chance to confess her love to the prince, and she must reveal her true nature when she does so. If the prince rejects her feelings, Little Mouse will return to being a mouse, forevermore. She would only have one opportunity to get him to love her. With determination, Little Mouse returned to the prince's castle.

            Unfortunately for her, getting a member of royalty to love her is easier said than done. Getting through the front door was the first challenge, as guards don't let just any...peasant?...see members of the nobility. Second, even when she finds him, he is hardly impressed by a girl who is short, unkempt, uneducated, of ignoble birth (which can't even be determined), and possessing of a "mousy" look. It doesn't matter how much she claims to know of the blade, or of magick.

            Oh, and there's that matter of Little Mouse being, for all intents and purposes, a witch. In Renaissance Europe. She was lucky to escape the castle with her life.

            But the winds of fortune shift, and Little Mouse got her chance. Political turmoil rocked the land, and the castle came under threat from without...and from within. The prince only dodged the blade of treachery most foul, by the intervention of Little Mouse. Fleeing the country, the prince and mouse sneak across wilderness and scattered villages, hoping to gain asylum from the prince's distant relatives. Mayhaps they gain assistance from other mages along the way, but Little Mouse is the prince's first and foremost protector. She guards him with her life. Hoping against hope that, when she finally confesses her love, the prince will reciprocate her feelings.

            Will her dreams come true? Or will she be reduced to a rodent once more, heartbroken and alone?

            Magestuck: The Ascension

                Note: this one isn't appropriate as a PC for two major reasons. Firstly, she works best as a villain due to her genocidal paradigm and warped vision of Ascension. Secondly, her concept demands traits and advantages which lie outside of starting PC territory in most games.

                116) Concept: The Plague Bringer

                "There's this old Iterator motto... "What intelligent creature would choose its flaws over perfection?" I couldn't agree more. If the homo genus is going to survive, it must adapt. The strong must survive, and humanity must be shepherded into a bright future under a program of rational and benevolent eugenics. I only wish that the progenitors and iterators had stayed their course, and not backed down from the cusp of human excellence..."

                "If you were thinking about escaping and 'saving the day', you're too late. The pandemic has already begun. Patient zero is on his way to a seminar... "Facing the Future" I believe. A load of overpaid academics and politicians pontificating about saving the world. The great irony is that simply by gathering together, they have. The infection will spread virulently, with the strong acting as carriers, angels of death, spreading my magnum opus into the blood of the weak as they travel back to their home countries. Within a few weeks, only the strong will remain. Most Magi will be spared, don't worry about that. The Revenant families, the lycanthropes, and the strongest homo sapiens, they'll all live. And I'll never be thanked for this, but I *have* saved the human race from itself, trust me on that. I've tried to make the course of illness as painless as possible, but I prioritized virulence and selectivity above all else. Those destined to die from their infections will exhibit a highly aggressive and irrational state, perfect for spreading the gift.

                In two days time you'll be free, and you can see for yourself the wonderful world I've created."

                Name: Katarina Obertus (and various aliases)

                Ostensible Affiliation: Euthanatos, Progenitors

                True Affiliation: Herself, her family's dark vision of Ascension, and (in her view) the human race

                Katarina Obertus, like many Mages, is excellent. A starred first class degree in biomedical sciences, a masters in genetics, and a lauded PhD dissertation focused on biological anthropology and evolutionary biology, all achieved by the time she was 20 years old... or so it would seem. Katarina looks like an ordinary woman in her early 20s (officially being 23), but she is actually 52. Like all of the members of her family, a vestigial link to the Cainite race has left her to age gracefully from puberty onwards, at 1/4 of the rate.

                Before she reached the appearance of adulthood, she cloistered herself away in the family's libraries, studying works on physiology and eugenics. Inspired by the philosophy of her family's patron clan, she began to focus attention on Azi Dahaka, the "perfect form". Rejecting the idea that she would find some enlightenment in fetishizing the form of a dragon, she began to Awaken as she imagined humanity freed from weakness and sickness, brought into a modern age of enlightenment and strength.

                Katarina's main inspirations are the philosophy of Clan Tzimisce and Family Obertus. The Tzimisce believe that ascendance can be found in the flesh and the mind, through constant alteration and reinvention. Changing appearance, ability, preference, personality, obsession, memory. The Obertus believe that homo sapiens is simply a precursor to homo obertus, and that humankind will naturally give way to the stronger Revenants. In recent years she has learned about the philosophy of the Euthanatos while undercover; this has simply fueled her resolve. She believes that she is bringing the great cycle into balance, and that there is no sin in what she is doing. All those who die will return stronger in the next cycle thanks to her work, so in the long run any "evil" she commits is moot, and the ends more than justify the means.

                As she emerged into the world, she used her many advantages to secure phenomenal qualifications, and was eventually approached by the Technocratic Union. Making a name for herself among the Progenitors, she gradually grew in power and influence, eventually reaching a point where she could form aliases and use them to gain a presence in various sects and key groups. Making use of Vicissitude, she became a master of disguise, creating doubles who could stand in for her when necessary, and changing identities as most people change clothes.

                Magick:

                Paradigm: We Are One Flesh, Might is Right, Humanity must Evolve; Katarina believes that natural selection is a natural law, and this guides her magick and her entire philosophy. Humanity is like a single organism, and it must self-regulate. As her Arete improved, she gained a greater understanding and began to see past the illusory separation of flesh, seeing more and more clearly that humanity must be reshaped in her vision if it is to become healthy and strong.

                Practice: Enlightened Science, Mad Science

                Instruments: Organisms (especially pathogens), Lab Gear, Calculations and Projections, Blood and Fluids, Eye Contact, Surgery and Vivisection, Brews and Concoctions

                Roleplaying:

                Katarina's aliases are intentionally unassuming and don't attract much attention. She is skilled in making people rely on her, and winning their trust.

                When she is herself, Katarina is friendly and honest; she's no Nephandi, and her vision is one of Ascension, simply a terrible and dark one. She argues for her beliefs as convincingly as possible, and is well read in terms of utilitarian philosophy. There's no superficially obvious hatefulness or malice in her; she is "doing what must be done". To her, the only path to humanity's survival and success is the path she offers. This prediction is backed up by various statistical models she has produced using Time, however it's likely that her obsession and personal belief in this genocidal ascension have biased the results.

                Katarina places a lot of emphasis on politeness and being a good host. She has a great deal of respect for other magi, and exceptional human beings. If she were to talk to a player character, she would discuss their goals, their beliefs, and the significance of magick in their eyes. How they see humanity's future, as things stand.

                Mechanics:

                Arete 6, Life 5, Time 2, Entropy 2, Forces 2, Mind 4, Correspondence 4, Prime 4, Matter 1, Spirit 1

                - Katarina is an Obertus Revenant, and she has 1 dot in Vicissitude; this allows her to disguise her face and her voice as she desires, given plenty of time and a few Intelligence + Medicine rolls. She also has 1 dot in Auspex, which allows her to reduce the difficulty of a mundane Perception roll by 1 when she wishes.

                - Katarina's "magnum opus" is a viral pathogen, the work of someone who could rightfully be called an Archmage, which reads the code of the host's DNA and chooses different regulons (large overarching groups of genes programming different kinds of "modes" into a single virus, which are activated in different contexts) depending on the target's nature. Individuals with supernaturally enhanced bodies or abilities (sensed with Life and Spirit), exceptional abilities (sensed with Life and Mind), a special "cure" she has prepared for those she has promised to keep alive in exchange for their help (sensed with Matter 1) or amazing health (sensed with Life and Mind) will act as carrier hosts, simply spreading the disease at an alarming rate through sneezing, coughing, skin contact and all bodily fluids. Those who are both mundane and have notable flaws in their health (carriers for serious genetic disease, for example) will experience total organ failure. Her predictions indicate that 1.3 billion individuals will survive this pandemic, bringing an end to global warming. Her attempts to steal the necessary materials to bring about this great plague and introduce it to the population (samples of a certain viral pathogen which she needs to steal from a Technocracy stronghold, samples of tass she needs to acquire from the Verbena, the expertise of several leading virologists, and other requirements) will form the basis of a chronicle in which she acts as the villain.

                    117) Concept: Unknown

                    Name: n.a.

                    "Invisibility is a mixed blessing. When I was homeless I would go days without anyone speaking to me. People would rather have some blissful ignorance, and avoid confronting society's failures. When I opened my eyes for the first time, I found I could do anything without anyone noticing me. Walk through a fancy gala, help myself to champagne, sit next to the string quartet... you know I've been staying at this chantry for 3 years now, and none of you know my name? It's like I slide out of people's minds. I've tried dying my hair, but within a few minutes I'm "that girl, you know, the one... well she looked sort of..." and before long I'm gone, invisible again, forgotten."

                    "We got through a lot of candles and twine. I mean I could swear I'm the only one who uses them, but we don't keep too close an eye on ritual gear. And the Apprentices are all useless; when I come into the high ritual chamber it's always in a state, salt and wax left out, and they all claim they left it tidy. Otherwise you should find everything is in order."

                    Magick:

                    Paradigm: Perceived Reality is an Illusion; what we experience, the "sensory reality" we construct, is far from total truth. We add and remove things. We approximate. We fill in gaps. That's why optical illusions work, and it's also why we don't always get the head count right... some people just blend into the background. There's something quiet about their presence, they don't give off that "I Am Here" signal the way everyone else does. And sometimes that is taken to an extreme...

                    It's possible that this illusion can be changed by thought and will, reshaping the perceptions of others to create a new reality.

                    Practice: Shamanism, self-taught, with elements taken from constant observation of Mages of many practices

                    Instruments: Books and Scrolls, Runes and Writing, Circles and Designs, Thought-Forms, Transgressions, God-Bonding and Weapons

                    Unknown is a Shaman since it brings the company of ineffable spirits which she feels are like her kin. Unobserved, impersonal, observant, quietly powerful. She is naturally used to her own company, favouring solitary pursuits such as reading, writing poetry, creating art and committing petty crimes against those around her, with all of these forming the basis of her magick. Her own Avatar is personified as the Quiet One, a silent mentor which guides through an ambiance and cryptic clues, giving a sense of companionship to a deeply lonely woman.

                    Roleplay: if you're part of a group of Mages, then you know a lot about them. Since they can't perceive you unless you directly grab their attention, your relationships with them are stuck in the first stages (at least for now). Over time through familiarity they may have developed a weak sense of your identity, recalling you vaguely each time you grab their attention.

                    You're a relatively quiet and introverted person, but when you do have a conversation with someone you've been observing you enjoy having a chance to use your voice. You're generally good-natured but have little respect for privacy or boundaries. You know that any transgressions you commit will slide out of people's memories over time.

                    Your goal is to form true and lasting relationships with other Mages using Mind as a crutch to overcome your natural tendency to slip out of people's memories.

                    Mechanics:

                    - Arcane 5, Dexterity 5, Stealth 5, and possibly a totem animal relating to camouflage or stalking like Tiger or Mantis. The net result is that unless you are deliberately grabbing attention, or are in a room with a fiercely observant person, you will almost always go unobserved. Furthermore, your Arcane rating means that you slide out of people's memories over time, becoming more featureless and vague as they try to hold onto the memory. Mundane and Magickal rolls to try and sense or investigate or learn about you are opposed.

                    - Arete 3, Mind 2, Spirit 2, Forces 2; Forces forms the basis of illusions (from total invisibility, to avoiding tripping laser detection systems), Spirit forms the basis of Shamanism, and Mind forms the basis of her control and her limited relationships; by using Mind 2 she tries to plant enduring memories of herself in other people (this can be taken for the sake of RP). She still has a lot of control over what is remembered and what isn't however (unless the other Mage were to use Mind and Time to try and remember absolutely everything about her, however her Arcane rating would oppose these attempts for good and bad).

                    - No Backgrounds, and 2 in each of the Social Attributes. She once tried to store some money in a bank, but somehow her application process didn't end up going through; being inherently forgettable makes such processes very difficult. So she simply borrows from other people, staying in empty spare rooms, taking what she needs, using magick to make up for it (curing a minor mental funk with Mind, bringing good luck upon a household with the help of a spirit).

                    "Even talking about her is difficult. I remember us going to great pains with some sort of ritual, and I can just about hold her in my mind. I'm worried that she might be some kind of Marauder living in our midst, driving us all insane... I mean how would I even know?! When I go to check on the chantry's security system, I can see her plain as day, but when I look away she kind of... fuzzes. I mean this has to be some sort of insanity right? She thinks she doesn't exist, and she's projecting that onto every mind in the vicinity..."

                        I thought it would be fun to do one for Mage (all factions and the Sorcerers) as well. Please, no more than two at a time.

                        1) A Cultist has discovered a way to help people who've lost certain senses (taste, touch, etc) feel again through him. Example: A person who lose their sense of taste can enjoy a candy bar with the Cultist eating it and then mentally passing his feelings to them via the Mind sphere. Interestingly, this talent has gotten the attention of members of the local Vampire community who would like the "Pleasure Dealer" to help them feel again.
                        1.1) Concept: Pleasure Dealer, Sensual Collector and Librarian

                        Magick:

                        Paradigm: Reality exists in the Mind; Consciousness is the only true experience.

                        Practice: Sensual Magick; lots of tools and behaviours that heighten and alter the sensory experience.

                        Instruments: Sex and Sensuality, Bodyforms, Thoughtforms, Eye Contact, Food and Drink, Vocalizations, Gems and Stones, Symbols, Aromatherapy...

                        Using Mind and Life, this Mage collects and shares experiences. With a vast mental library of experiences taken from his own mind/body and the minds and bodies of others, he is a sensual zenith. These can be used to help or to harm, from inducing the sensation of euphoria to bringing about the deepest dread.

                        He is a great believer in the idea that sensation and feeling should be diverse, and that a bit of pain or sadness can be good, that these are useful feelings.

                        His idea of Ascension is becoming omnipotent through the recollection of All Sensation, to know through personal and borrowed experience what it is to be anything and everything, to know the beginning and the end of sensation, and to move past it into godhood.

                        His Demesne, a great mind palace, is a space within the maya which stores millions of experiences, and will store many more as time passes...

                        - Arete 4, Mind 3 (Mind 2 extends senses to others, Mind 3 extends them to those who are unwilling), Correspondence 3 (allowing sensation to be shared across great distances), Spirit 2 (opening up a whole new realm of sights, sounds, ideas), Time 3 (extending sensation, shortening it and recalling past sensations), Prime 2 (controlling the flow of energy, bringing it to great peaks, merging it, draining it, gifting it), Forces 1 (adding new sensory experiences), Life 3 (physical experiences, sharing the physiological experience along with the mental one, heart rate, peripheral sensation...), Matter 1 (adding new sensory experiences), Entropy 1 (giving a sense of destiny and significance, drawing him towards important experiences).

                        - The Cult Background, to reflect his acolytes who go forth and seek new experiences to add to the library. If he has taken his quest far enough, or he has warped those who follow him too much, then he might be considered a "villain" by some players. His personal belief is that the whole breadth of human experience should be accessible to all, and that will inevitably lead to him trying to open the eyes of the masses if he is left unchecked...

                        - The Demesne Background, and the Lucid Dreaming skill.

                        - The Dream background, to represent his ability to pull abilities he doesn't possess from the many memories he has collected. He can only do this with one ability per scene, but it opens up many rare talents and a lot of flexibility, making him a terrifying villain or adversary, or a powerful ally.

                        Roleplay: You've heard better, you've tasted better, you've seen better. Digging through every experience you can find, in the minds of sleepers and mages and wraiths and vampires, you've experienced so much that everyday enjoyment is lost to you, unless it is heightened through use of magick. The extremes that the dealer can offer will ruin people given time, himself included.

                        His clients include the celibate, who wish to know what it feels to experience the most potent embrace. The occultist, who wants to know how it feels when a Lupine goes through "the change", or how a Cainite feels when they are embraced or feed or commit diablerie, who wants to know how Glamour tastes to a Fae or how Passion tastes to a Wraith. The Setite who wishes to feel the rush that his clients feel when he makes love to them or feeds them his blood, or gives them narcotics or alcohol. The dying woman who wants to experience that beautiful evening where her first husband proposed to her in Paris, all those years ago. The addict who wants to kick their addiction but needs to feel something. The ancient supernatural being that wants to recover its memories. The historian who wants to examine the sights and sounds and people of times past.

                        He charges in money and favours, but also in experiences. The memories he brings back, the feelings he transfers from the Vampire's ghoul to the Vampire, and the odd experience he finds in a client's memories... he keeps them. Locks them away. No need to mention that to those who use his services; they might not be happy knowing that their experiences will be blended into the experiences he offers his other clients.

                        Most of his cultists were once clients, and they are truly devoted to him, including a few supernaturals and people of significance. To them he is the core of all experience, and they have few memories which trump those he has gifted them with.

                        While he cannot make Wonders himself, with the help of another Ecstatic he has created Charms and Wonders which give a certain experience either once (in the form of a small vial of fluid that you drink to experience the sensation or memory) or many times (each time you touch it, for example; these are given to those who have earned it). The cult is very much like a real cult, with levels and hidden teachings and demands that you give up resources to the great leader...

                        His enemies may be unfortunate enough to experience how it feels to be harmed in the worst ways imaginable. How it feels to go through the Torment or Oblivion, or how it feels to be warped by a Tzimisce or a creative Nephandi. Alternatively, they may experience terrible pain on top of each sensation.

                        Ultimate Goal: he could create his own Realm with mastery of the Spheres. This realm could be a paradise of perfect sensations, stitched together from memories and experiences and emotions. He would exist there mostly, and his followers would bring new experiences to the realm. Alternatively, "Paradise" could be a lie, and those who "go to paradise" are being destroyed, their Quintessence and memories being absorbed by their master. This would solve the problem of the cult becoming too big, or anyone becoming too senior or experienced and therefore threatening. Or "Paradise" could be a testing ground, and those who are sent there are trapped and put through situations which are so extreme that they elicit new emotions and experiences which he can harvest, with Time flowing differently so that he may gather new experiences more quickly as they are tortured (among other things) for eternity.

                            Note: playing this one could be really divisive at some tables. Whether this character *is* spiritually related to the High Umbral Spirit of Christ, or simply *thinks* she is, will likely depend on the taste of the storyteller and the players.

                            Furthermore, this might stray into uncomfortable territory since a key part of her concept is "what if a person running a cult, and acting as a Televangelist, were actually supernaturally gifted and virtuous, and not a huge con artist". Considering that there are people who perform and "cure" people of their cancer on-stage in real life, and those people are abusing people and committing theft, it's possible some players might not want a PC at the table to be doing that "but for realsies".

                            118) Concept: The Pop Messiah

                            "My faith is a big part of my work. And faith evolves. I know a lot of you have been hurt by the church, and that's why I want to lead people into a new kind of faith. One that's fit for the modern era, and one which preaches to the marginalized, as Christ did."

                            Tradition: Choristers, Ecstatics, loose affiliations.

                            A potent pop idol carrying what she sees as a messianic secret; she thinks she is the second coming of Christ. She might also be a functional Marauder with some subtle quiet, and she might be turning her biggest fans into an international cult. Who knows.

                            "Of course I'll be called a false prophet. Most of the other Choristers will never believe me. But I am growing into my power, and in time I will have my own chorus singing along with me. They'll Awaken, and be my apostles. I'm revealing this secret to you, Andrew, because you will be my first true follower, like your namesake."

                            She's enigmatic, she's inspirational, she practices what she preaches. Big donations to the needy, a relatively modest lifestyle considering her income, advice which helps dysfunctional people to fix their lives. She presents herself as both a musical and style icon, and she mixes her faith with everything she does. A lot of what she does could be labeled as "Christian Pop" and "Self-Help Pop". Lots of empowerment, faith, political and personal messages.

                            "All of you are capable of it. You can change the world. I had nothing when I started out except a god-given talent for singing. And I'm doing what I can to help people with it. You all need to find that special gift and do what you can with it."

                            Magick:

                            Paradigm: Creation is Divine and Alive, It's All Good; Have Faith, I am the Saviour, I am a God in Disguise

                            For her, magick flows from the divinity of the universe, of certain acts, and from her own divinity. Through living her life she is essentially writing a new testament, a third testament. As Jesus came to fulfill and rewrite the old laws, she has come to do the same. She believes that Christ was limited by the time he preached in, and that the world wasn't ready for certain teachings. Christ's message was deeply controversial already (turn the other cheek, love all humanity, accept those who are marginalized) and including ideas that would still be controversial in the 21st century would have hampered his central message.

                            Practice: God-Bonding, Social Engineering, Faith

                            Instruments: Voice and Vocalizations, Dress and Style, Dancing and Bodyforms, Prayers and Invocations, Eye Contact, Symbolism, Ordeals and Exertions

                            Mechanics:

                            - Arete 3, Spirit 1, Forces 1, Mind 2, Correspondence 2

                            - Avatar 5, True Faith 1

                            - Resources 4, Cult 2 (aides, groupies, stylists, backup dancers and vocalists), Fame 3

                            - Potentially, the Nephilim Merit to represent being the spiritual child of some High Umbral representation of Christ or the Christian God. Alternatively, she's a Marauder and has a Permanent Quiet rating of 1, with her central deviation from reality being the belief that she is the Messiah, including a Sanity Sink which makes those she has prolonged contact with believe it too (if not explicitly). Or... both. There's no reason that she couldn't have a legitimate link to Christ in the High Umbra and also be a tad insane; it's possible that a paranoid schizophrenic could believe that MI5, the CIA and the KGB were watching them and also be correct (if very unlikely) after all.

                            If it's the latter, then she has some weak protection from Paradox (detailed rules in M20) and she also extends her personal reality to those she spends a lot of time around; if any of her fans were to Awaken, they would likely do so as Marauders with the intense belief that she is some kind of prophet or messiah.

                            If it's the former, then she gets the benefits of Nephilim as described in M20. 7 points of Quintessence every 24 hours (and can store 7 points as personal quintessence), easier to command using rituals or spells which are designed to work on high umbral spirits (say, the ritual of an infernalist who was used to capturing and destroying Angels), capable of physically entering the High Umbra with the right spheres (normally impossible, most Mages use astral projection), has an easier time commanding spirits of all kinds (from asking for an angel's aid to casting out demons or ghosts who have possessed mortals), may have Advantages (from angel wings to rapid healing), may have a Derangement (from paranoia to megalomania), -1 difficulty to rolls relating to the High Umbra, including Mind and Spirit effects, a burning powerful aura, several points in physical flaws to reflect how unstable the form of a half-spirit human is...

                            - Enchanting Feature, Voice. Her voice, in singing and speech, is a Unique, Personalized Instrument. This means -2 difficult to mundane and magickal rolls involving her voice.

                            Roleplaying:

                            An embodiment of positive and kind faith. Warm, nurturing, thoughtful. Somewhat holier-than-thou; when she is rebuking another person (say a hateful pastor) she adopts the kind of tone someone would if they were absolutely certain that they were right and the other person was wrong. Certainty characterizes a lot of her behaviour; she is *certain*.

                            She uses live performances as an opportunity to perform rituals (which are typically coincidental thanks to a local Reality Zone generated by the performance), and to ask for miracles with True Faith. Praying that someone in the crowd will recover from a serious illness, praying that someone in the crowd will rediscover their passion for life, that sort of thing.

                            Example Effects:

                            - Examining the crowd, she uses Mind and Spirit, along with Correspondence, to try and quickly single out a person in need of help. Finding a person who has been deeply unhappy for a long time, she invites them to speak, with an aide bringing them a mic. Using Mind 1 she gleans information about what's wrong in their life, and gives vague but helpful ideas about the changes they could make. Using Mind 1 to reduce the difficulty, she attempts a Charisma + Expression roll to inspire them to improve their life (with bonus dice from Fame and the situation), and gets many successes.

                            - During a performance, she uses Forces 1 to perfect her command of her voice through listening to the tones and using this feedback to perfect her singing technique temporarily (reduction to the Performance difficult). She then begins to perform, using a song as a focus for an effect to create religious hysteria and devotion in the audience (this is an out-of-character description; she wouldn't describe what she was doing this way at all, and she would be being entirely honest... she is potentially delusional remember) using Mind 2 to heighten existing feelings, and Correspondence 2 to spread it across a wide area. With the support of her Cult she has an Arete pool of 5, and she can attempt the roll several times before her Stamina becomes a limitation.
