    Search inspiration for Psionic power/rotes

    08-03-2022, 05:31 PM
    Dear psionics. I ran into a lack of ideas. I have a psionic, but I want to give him psi powers or rotes that fit the paradigm. I have not yet decided whether it will be a normal psionic or an awakened one.
    The task looks simple, but the problem is that I'm trying to create some non-standard powers.

    I'm trying to come up with a couple of concepts:
    - Use of force (gravity, kinetic force, possibly with other parts of physics; but without "elemental" magic - fire/lightning, etc.) This is not a psionicist who creates fire and lightning, we are not reality deviants.
    - Attack psionics or perhaps tormenting psionics? The concept of calling suffering, pain and other things. The problem here is that I can't think of anything more than instilling pain into the target's brain with Mind.
    - However, I will not refuse other ideas (healing psionics?, well, we saw this) and advice.

    Mages have the ability to combine spheres and create interesting effects. And I could take ideas from other lines, books, movies or whatever, but sometimes I need a lot of spheres to mimic those powers, which limits me a lot. So I thought it might be easier to start with a regular psionic with 2 "psi-paths". But such psionics do not have the "paths" I need.
    That's why I hope that maybe someone here created homemade psi-powers or interesting rotes. As a technocrat, he has Mind/Force/Life, but maybe I'll replace one of them with Data.

      So, in my games, I actually consider that most of the awakened ones that never meet another Mage for a long enough period of time, most of the time develops "psionic powers"; there is, this is what those people believe. With a total lack of any explanations as to all the crushing amount of weirdness and bizarre, those poor sods try to make some sense to the madness around them.

      And Mages are too rare, too rare to be able to be everywhere that a new awakened shows up.

      So, those without mentors end up believing they developed psionics. At least for the most part, and at least in the modern age. Sure, some still think they've been possessed or cursed by demons or blessed by angels, but a good amount nowadays go for "I have psionic powers" and become self made "mages".

      As for rotes, psionics could in theory do ALL the effects from ALL spheres. "Psionic teleport" with correspondence, psionic transmutation with matter and even psionic spirit.

      What can or cant be done in a Technocrat paradigm will have to be determined by you.

      That been said, some effects are more adequate than others. Spirit and Mind powers in particularly are excellent for the most part, especially Mind. Telepathy (Mind 3) and Telesend (also Mind 3, the ability to send your own thoughts into other minds). In other uses of Telesend, it's the creation of mental illusions - which are a form of sending thoughts into other minds, but "sensorial thoughts" instead of "memory thoughts".

      Now, on the other end of the telepathic powers, you have those psionics who are Empaths. Empaths deal with emotions, emotional manipulation rather than the more crude mental domination. This is all done with Mind 2. An Empathy look into his victim, press his finger to his head and makes the victim get an irresistible sexual drive, or explode into a mad bloodlust rage attack. Or anything more subtle than that in the inbetween. The empath can cause excruciating pain, can put a person to sleep with a relaxing effect, can cause fear, rage, sadness, joy, induce frenetic maniac episodes of boundless energy as if the victim has snort cocaine etc. So, while Empaths do not exert direct control on others, it is a massive power still nonetheless (if you play VtM, just think that everything that Presence does, an Empath can do with Mind 2). Empaths can also "read" a person's general mood and superficial thoughts.

      You said that "fire is from reality deviants", but I totally disagree. Have you ever seen the first Hellboy movie? Not a great movie but alas... Anyway, Hellboy's "girlfriend" was a psionic girl that could control fire... Well, she wasnt very good at the "control" part, which also means she's dangerous, but...

      Anyway, in here we have two powers that are classic psionic powers, and those are: Pyrokinesis and Cryokinesis. Respectivelly, the power to control Fire and the power to control Ice, to heat up and to cool down.

      Moving stuff around is Telekinesis, which is more know than the two powers above.

      Likewise, there's also a psionic power that deals with electromagnetics, but I dont remember the name. If you dont want that thou, it aint a problem at all - the guy is still a tech-boy, right? Well, for electromagnetics he may still need to use physics and engineering in order to build machines, for the rest he simply uses the "power of his brain".

      Gravity is just "high level telekinesis".

      "Psionic Healing" is another classic. Some psionics have the ability to "strenghten their bodies" using their psionic powers - that's Life 3. Life 1 can serve for psionic divination powers - so for example, guiving a medical diagnosis, Psionic Life 1, or Sense Life, also Psionic Life 1.

      If you think that shapeshifiting is out of place, your tech can still go for the good old mad lab for those, that's your call, althought I wouldnt say that it is something completely off.

      Now, lets add some variation. Instead of just simply placing his hands in his head and shitting his pants, there are some alternatives for rotes.

      Remember Professor Xavier and the "Brain Computer"?

      Well, the Computer is what we call a Psicothronics. Psicothronics usually are electronic devices (machines, not to be confused with techie wonders) that "amplify" psionic powers.

      Now, those are just simply custom taylored focuses; the whole "amplify psionic powers" is just "techno-babble", like everything else about magik (enhancing crystals? Special herbs? All mystic babble).

      Be that as it may, your psionic user may be a Telepath... But he's not very good at it.

      But worry not, because his "Telepathic Helmet" (a ridiculous metal hat filled with silly circuits and buttoms) helps him "focus his powers".

      Those could even be standard equipment for Enlighten agents - in a less silly version, the trademark signature of the NWO, the dark glasses, could be powerful "psicothronic devices", serving as the focus of several psionic powers (and possibly enhanced with several other electronic functions, both magik and mundane, like a HUD screen with internet connection and facial recognition program, similar to the screen of Iron Man, night vision etc).

      Besides that, other good focuses are always some good old DRUGS. "Hyper Glandule Stimulation" drug to induce your Empathic powers to kick in, etc.

      And lastly, self hypnosis techniques. That's basically a special form of meditation. Those can range from looking to a computer screen with randomly generated colors, to closing your eyes and saying "ohm... ohm... ohm" in order to get glimpses of the future or "feeling thoughts" around you.

          Originally posted by Alphari View Post

          Mages have the ability to combine spheres and create interesting effects. And I could take ideas from other lines, books, movies or whatever, but sometimes I need a lot of spheres to mimic those powers, which limits me a lot.
          The Spheres can actually create A TON of "psionic powers". In fiction, while some psiquics have a ton of powers, many times they only have one or two. You can represent this in 3 ways:

          Your tech is a Telepath, and ONLY a Telepath. That's fine; this means that the only effect that he can use which will have Psionics as a focus will be "Mind 3: Telepathy". Fine.

          Your psiquic may have LATENT powers - there is, he has the POTENTIAL to "have" psionics (there is, he believes or is led to believe in that potential), but is unable to naturally access those; in that case, he needs some sort of Psycotronics (silly "telepathic hats", drugs or special meditation techniques). Once again, this may be limited to a few effects or not; he may be a Cryomancer, Telekinetic and Telepath, but not an Empath or Pyromancer.

          Since in the universe of Mage "psionic powers" are just a form of self illusion - but then again, so too is the Hermetic "magic" or the Technocratic "Science" - the extension of the "latent psionic powers" a techie have is entirely what the techie believes himself to be capable of doing. If you go to the Progenitor doctor, and after testing you he says "Johnny boy, you are a natural Cryomancer and Empathy", you'll interiorize that, and from that moment onwards you'll use your "psionic abilities" to create ice and manipulate emotions AND NOTHING ELSE, because that's what you believe that it is what you can do. And, for manipulating electromagnetic energy, you'll need your "energon-3000" machine to so, while for creating ice you just close your eyes and contract your muscles.

          And, it's even possible that, someday later, you end up discovering that you're also a telepath, and that you probably had this additional "latent power" which the good ol' doctor failed to identify in the first inspection.

                          One of the cool things about Mage it's that you, the player, gets to think about, and to some extent decide, how the world works. A mage doesn't simply "have powers", a mage defined on individual powers would need an endless character sheet, what they have instead it's a whole cosmology (or a whole narration, in the roleplaying sense) that they can use together with the rules as a "well" to exctract and excert powers

                          So: Think about how your character believes the universe works trough psionics, rather than just how psionics works inside the universe.

                          What IS "a cursed flame that can burn souls"?, What are "curses"?, What are "souls"?, do these things even exist?. Is it superstitious thing to define something they don't understand, but that could be studied and then defined as a phisical element that then could be the subject of your "psionic waves" or whatever?

                          Psionics in fiction (and even RL) it's so random, that you can easily adapt pretty much anything.

                          Like, one philosophical concept that can be adapted for psionics that I really like it's that of the Noosphere(that's a real life concept, but also used in D&D and Numenera for Psionics). Trough a psionical manipulation of it it you could access boundless information about the world, and even alter it in a very flexible way.

                                  Originally posted by Alphari View Post

                                  I read. There are variations of different types of psionics and ways to enhance it. This is a good text with good examples of possible powers. But that (my last) question is about usage or flexibility.
                                  Mystics can do many thing with fire - turn it into swords and animals. But, I look at this gravity and my psionic... and I think - gravity is a useful thing on a global scale, but there is little that can be done with it. I came up with options with the creation of grav-sphere or field, grav-wave or remote explosion due to a pressure / gravity difference. But, either this is the limit for gravity on such a scale, or I have a lack of ideas.

                                  Perhaps the psionics (or techno paradigm) is somewhat limited in physical manipulation. I'll try to compensate for this with cyber-implants or a useful device.

                                  Changing the direction of gravity for the psychic so he can walk up walls. As an effect it is nearly identical to putting a constant force on himself so that should be Forces 2 or 3.

                                  Indeed, gravity manipulation can be the explanation for a lot of standard telekinetic effects. Usually there wouldn't even be a change to the spheres.

                                  A simple manipulation of gravity in the vicinity of someone's legs would be a good justification for increasing difficulty levels or tripping them up. Strictly speaking that's not targeted at their legs directly so it should just be Forces 2.

                                  You can also rapidly alter the pull of gravity on a subject's digestive tract which will very likely cause abdominal distress and an intense need to go to the bathroom. That's a Forces 2/Life 2 or just Life 2 depending on how you are adjudicating (that's a topic for another thread).

                                  If your gravity manipulation includes mass manipulation, you can get into a bunch of the Matter effects as well. Dropping the mass of an object just long enough to throw it or fire it normally would be a good way to use Matter to increase successes on a ranged attack. You could accelerate it magically too, but I'm not sure there's much benefit to using a Matter/Forces combo to do something you could already do (in paradigm) with Forces alone.

                                  Depending on worldview, you could even justify using your gravity manipulation to make insubstantial things like spirits dense and coherent enough to interact with. That's is, you can use it as justification for some Spirit/Dimensional Science effects.

                                                      And I just found a little thing that is a great example of how those can help, just a small detail but that can help give you some color to your campaign

                                                      Originally posted by Gurps Psis pag 13

                                                      Power Based Disadvantages

                                                      While a psychic will generally have several “normal”
                                                      disadvantages – mental hang-ups, social problems, and physical ailments that anyone might have – it’s just as appropriate for him to take disadvantages that represent “pside effects” of his power(s) or a particular mindset associated with its abilities. In addition to the traits below, any psi might have Code of Honor (Psychic’s) (p. 6), unless his abilities are strictly self-directed.
                                                      Below, exotic and supernatural disadvantage are
                                                      marked with an asterisk (*). These are appropriate only for those who possess the associated power; e.g., a psychic vampire can take Lifebane, while an
                                                      esper cannot. The GM (Game Master/Storyteller) is welcome to rule otherwise, of course. Other disadvantages are available to anyone – they just happen to be particularly appropriate for certain types of psi.
                                                      Anti-Psi: Intolerance (Psis); Mundane Background (when combined with nothing but passive abilities). (My own personal note: "Anti-Psi" could be attained using Prime as "countermagik". Your psionic ability is geared toward nulifying other psionic abilities)
                                                      Astral Projection: Compulsive Snooping
                                                      (p. 6); Enemies (Astral entities).
                                                      Ergokinesis: Compulsive Snooping (p. 6) (for cyberpsis); Electrical*; Epilepsy.
                                                      ESP: Chronic Pain (p. 6); Compulsive Snooping; Confused; Curious; Epilepsy; Flashbacks; Nightmares; Phantom Voices.
                                                      Probability Alteration: Klutz or Total Klutz (Personal Note: this is a Gurps Disadvantage for disastrous people, like Goofy and others), Phobia
                                                      (Number 13); Unluckiness; Weirdness Magnet*.
                                                      Psychic Healing: Charitable; Chummy or Gregarious (p. 6); Pacifism; Unusual Biochemistry*; Vow (Major; Never refuse a request for healing).
                                                      Psychic Vampirism: Callous; Chummy
                                                      or Gregarious (p. 6); Lifebane*; Uncontrollable Appetite (Life Force)*.
                                                      Psychokinesis: Chronic Pain (p. 6); Pyromania; Trickster.
                                                      Telepathy: Addiction (Telepathic Contact) (p. 5); Chummy or Gregarious (p. 6); Compulsive Snooping (p. 6); Phantom Voices; Supersensitive*.
                                                      Teleportation: Overconfidence; Phobia
                                                      (Enclosed Spaces); Sleepwalker.
                                                      So, this small table was just a small sample of fitting disadvantages, but already we get plenty of information; for example, a few examples of "psionic powers". Anti-Psi, the ability to nulify other psionic powers. This can be done with Countermagik (with Prime). Astral Projection - self explanatory (but the book have more examples of uses to that other than just getting your mind out of your body, such as "astral possession" and others). Mostly Mind 4 or 5. Ergokinesis. I'll be honest, I saw this a long time ago, but I dont remember what it is; Im not sure if it's electrical manipulation or something else, sorry. ESP: this is the power to "see the unseen". Remote vision, looking into the future, precognition, that sort of things - divinations basically. Probability Alteration: this is a great one. Need I say anything else? Entropy all over. Psychic Healing: As the name says. Life 2 or 3 - but not only that. Psychic Vampirism: use Life and or Prime (or Spirit, or even matter perhaps) to "suck the life force" of others. Life + Prime can serve to "fuel" your own "Quintessense" (Psi Energy?) out of the life force of your victims. Psychokinesis - if Im not mistaken, this one cover Telekinesis, Pyrokinesis, Cryokinesis and a few others. It's basically the full range of Forces effects. Telepathy - that's perhaps the most famous psi abilities. Read and send thoughts, sometimes also control minds, etc etc. Mind Sphere all over. And Teleport is correspondence, althought the book has many clever uses of it besides just instant travel from point A to point B

                                                      Edit: those Disadvantages above are also some interesting ideas for Permadox Flaws or Genetic Flaws, particularly for "Psionic Constructs" of the Union.
