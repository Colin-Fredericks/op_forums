                                            Like others have said, the Technocracy would win, mostly due to the rules OP defined.
                                            • Paradox is the main limiting factor for Mage in general - removing it basically makes Mages unstoppable.
                                            • Removing Antediluvians is much more painful for vampires than removing Oracles is for mages. The power of the Technocracy isn't in Tychoides personally but in the organization he created. There is a hell of a lot of power in, say, [Tzimisce] that is not within all of his childer.
                                            • Vampires are a fundamentally social (whereas mages are mental and werewolves are physical, in the original triad). They rely on subtlety and pressure, not on open war. Their innate powers, like blood bonds, are built around influence rather than, say, a werewolf's Crinos form being good for pureeing foes.
                                            There are lots of cool Methuselah powers they could use but most vampires have levels of those powers that only allow minor contamination (Dominating one general, using Lure of Flames on a couple black helicopters). The high-level Disciplines that really threaten the Technocracy are only held by a couple elders, so you only get a couple shots at them. You can Embrace armies in a night, but they're just cannon fodder; nobody's getting Embraced and then throwing around Chimerstry 9.

                                            The best part of this war would actually be chasing down the holdouts in the city of Enoch in the Shadowlands. We're already going gonzo, why not refit a fleet of voidships for combat with post-life entities and go strafing?

                                            In a more balanced scenario - if the Technocracy slowly begins considering Cainites as a threat worthy of their attention, and vampires slowly start trying to gain influence/minions in the Technocracy, and this cold war boils over into a hot war - that is much more interesting and even if you keep the Antediluvians out, could still easily be a vampire victory.
