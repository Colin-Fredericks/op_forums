                        Vampires have a lot of weaknesses, and Mages are mere humans 'til they're not...

                        So.

                        A mage that summons sunlight inside a vampires haven it's a walking target, and if you can't just shoot him down...well, then you might have bigger problems than just the sunligth. A mage that does...all the shit HorizonParty2021 mentions in the first posts...it's way more dangerous. Not the least because it implies a quite intimate knowledge of how vampires move that most mages shouldn't have.

                        A lot of this thread assumes it will be super easy to pinpoint the business, associates, and *specially* the home of vampires. I would think that for some vampires this is kinda true, because they are new to the supernatural power, and most assuredly don't know how much "sigth beyond sigth" the mages have. I would also think not all vampires are ignorant, and some could know how mages move, and migth be able to play the counter-intelligence chess using that potent perception to feed false data.

                        So, one reason to tolerate vampires: It's not like mages don't have enemies, and it's preposterous to think you will always come on top if you keep making more. If only because, someday, you will fail at your sensory spells, and then you will end facing vampires with your metaphysical pants down. Or you will try to ritually nuke a vampire's haven, botch, and die of Paradox. Or you will face a vampire that knows how to figth mages and/or has mage allies. One has to think if it's really worth the effort, if it wouldn't be better to just cut a deal with their authorities ("this neighborhood it's no feeding ground from today on"). Most mages aren't *that* much into vampires as to dedicate to a dangerous (and hardly profitable) cruzade agianst them

                                            Some things are backwards, OP

                                            Humanity is partially overpopulated because of the meddling of vampires. Sure, they kill off a few, but they expand urbanization and may or may not be involved in trying to increase local population through immigration or breeding schemes (in more stable domains such may be discouraged)
                                            More importantly; The world can afford to feed everyone on it. It just doesn't happen. In part due to logistical problems, but more in part due to the dominant economic model of the world and the vampire love of it: vampires crave huge gaps between rich and poor, because the richness of the rich give them entertainment and power, whilst the poorness of the poor give them easy feeding zones with 'policing' that works in the favour of the vampires.


                                            But, OK.
                                            In the instance the OP is in, it would be very wise to just dust the vampires... and then distance themselves from the conflict. This was the result of Vampire's foolishness, not mage agression; They had it coming.

                                            In terms of why you should live alongside vampires in peace and joined prosperity.

                                            -Because those same economic conditions that vampires encourage are good for mages. Any mage worth their salt should be spectacularly rich, and it would be a shame if one of the greatest forces for perpetuating the status quo and increasing gap between rich and poor disapeared along with their influence and suddenly you're being taxed 90% and all the loopholes have been closed. Suddenly you can't bribe a police officer because they all make good wages and are held to a higher standard...

                                            -Because vampire bodies are a treasure trove of resources and it'd be a shame to be rid of them entirely.

                                            - Because although vampires lose battles, they always win wars.
