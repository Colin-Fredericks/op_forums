                                  Originally posted by Dataweaver View Post

But I don't think an “Unawakened” Flaw exists. It didn't make sense: taking a Flaw because you're a mortal with Backgrounds that mortals normally don't have? How is that a Flaw?
                                  I found it! It's a flaw in the Technocracy Revised book intended for PCs who are playing things like Hit Marks. And mortals can have Enhancements, there's nothing magickal about possessing them. Same goes for plenty of Wonders, and even arguably some things like Dream and Demesne. None of these things have Sphere prerequisites or demand that you're a Mage (well some Wonders do).

                                  It's 7 points and raises the max freebie total to 21, 7 higher than normal. You're meant to be a powerful non-magickal ally who the other PCs can work off of essentially, it's very far from playing a "mortal" PC. It's a flaw in the sense that you lose a free dot of Arete (worth 4 freebies right?) and 6 dots of Spheres (worth 7 each for a total of 42 freebies), and place yourself on a very linear power progression vs other characters who are more exponential. The big upside is that in a collaborative game with little PC conflict that won't matter since PCs can apply their buffs to you, the perfect damage-resistant combat-blender canvas for their effects. Furthermore, any PC who can create charms or wonders, if given enough time and quintessence, can give you the ability to work "magick". It's probably underpowered overall but definitely interesting.

                                  For example you could play an Enhancements 5, Hypercram 5 cyborg with plenty of dots invested in Advantages for extra soak. The Hypercram is justified by the "online access" enhancement and a body/mind designed to integrate data into muscle memory. For the sake of balance such a PC couldn't awaken or learn sorcery, or anything along those lines. Many Mage PCs place their freebies into Avatar, Spheres and Arete so such a PC would probably have better Abilities overall.
