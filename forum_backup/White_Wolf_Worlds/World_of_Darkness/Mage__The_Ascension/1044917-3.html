Just take the dots in "Meditation" and say "my character's meditation state is to sit in a dark room and listen to jazz". It is, however, a 0-5 skill which is different from passively watching a film or praying without purpose.

                                        And... that's a slightly Mage perspective, it acknowledges the dynamic, but on a Mage's terms. Here's a Sorcerer perspective.

                                        Bunch of psychos. Like... my powers can turn my fingernails into claws, maybe give me wings at a push. I can sense magic from a mile off and sometimes, if I'm lucky, see what's coming just around the corner. But there are limits. I feel like if you push too far, try to do too much... a screw comes loose. That's what "True MagicK" is. It's not hard to see... you ever met a Mage who isn't at least a bit crazy? These are people who are so full of themselves that they think they can speak for reality. They all know best, naturally, and their views on magic just have to apply to the whole world. Fuck'em. I don't need to be able to bend myself into 15 dimensions to put a Leech down or guide a kid down the right path.

                                        That's the dynamic I want. Infinite Potential vs. Finite Potential. Hubris vs. Humility. A Wide View vs. A Personal View. Now obviously there will be those on both sides who don't fit these generalizations, Sorcerers who want to be God-Kings, Mages who don't think their Paradigm could ever speak for all other people or that they know they way to Ascension... but it's a nice balance all the same.
