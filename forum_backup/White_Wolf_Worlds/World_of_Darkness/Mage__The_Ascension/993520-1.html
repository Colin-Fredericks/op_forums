        You don't need Primal Energy to materialize anything. You just need Prime and Matter. Prime isn't a restricted Sphere. Tons of VE's are going to have it. In fact, they can probably make pretty much everything they want with just Matter. They don't need dick from the Syndicate or the world to just make any sort of mundane stuff. They have infinite supplies of normal materials and normal energy and normal foods.

        What they don't have is Quintessence and, unless you are tossing EDE's into a "Gnosis to Quintessence" converter or use your fellow VE's as batteries in a Matrix-like fashion, it's pretty damn hard to get. Also, as pointed out, when fighting the good fight is pretty much all you're doing, you can't afford to have your best Device builders sitting there churning out Devices. You need them out there on the ships acting like Scotty, doing wild upgrades and repair work on the fly in the field. (And that was probably what most of them were supposed to be doing back in the good old exploration days as well.). Last, but not least, they REALLY need some way to keep Void Adaptation from setting in. (Especially now they've seen where that all ends in the form of Threat Null.)

        That's what the Syndicate is doing. It isn't supplying them with the normal stuff. (In the Technocratic paradigm, they've made getting resources into space prohibitively costly in any case.) The economy of Earth is effectively a Quintessence farming operation through use of Primal Utility. Though the VE's objectives aren't the only recipient of that (or even the one with the largest market share), that's what the Syndicate is doing. Of course, that Quintessence has to take some form and it's not all in charged-up Matricies. A lot of it is in the form of complete Devices (with/through Iteration X). It's also in what might look like raw materials, but are actually very much more than that. (Create a factory that makes parts that are used in the manufacture of Voidships that is a Primal Venture. Use Primal Utility to funnel the Quintessence generated into the parts that are being made... they aren't Devices, more like super-dedicated Tass. Shoot the parts into space They are definitely worth it.) In fact, those Matricies are being produced the same way... the Quintessence filled "reactor cores" or whatever are the parts in question.

        It's also why the other Conventions are pretty important as well. Either the NWO ("political officers"), Progenitors (those archetypal medical officers) or both are probably helping out with making sure that VE personnel are able to rotate out so Void Adaptation doesn't come into play.

                                    Originally posted by Aya Tari View Post

                                    Canada is different from the United States. The UK is different from Canada and the US. Europe is different from the UK. The constituent parts of Europe are different from each other. Europe is very different than Africa. Super-Saharan Africa is different from Sub-Saharan Africa. East Africa is different from West Africa. Somalia is different from Tanzania. The Hadza are different from the rest of the Tanzanians. All of East African is different from the state of South Africa (which includes several nations, since nation = ethnic group + land).

                                    Mexico is different from the United States. Mexico is different from the other countries of Central America. Central America is different from South America. The different nations of South America are different from one another....

                                    And so on.

                                    Why is Asia so special and so different? So special as to have separate sourcebooks (at LEAST 1, sometimes more) in 4 different game lines and have it's own complete spin-off line from another? If you are going to shoehorn werewolves into Asian culture despite without any respect whatsoever as to how werewolves fit or don't fit into Asian culture? Why not shoehorn in everything else and make your global game lines actually global? Asia is definitely picked out as different and lavished with time care and attention. Particularly in areas that are "bright and shiny" to Westerners. Without much sensitivity to the broad variety of actual cultures involved. China has 56 separate identified ethnicities within it's borders. That's identified by the Han majority. Treatment of the others in game products is sparse to non-existent. We were given the Wu Keng but completely ignored, say, the Mosuo who have very interesting and unique takes on spiritualism, etc.? (Guess they're just Dreamspeakers.... but, clearly, the Syndicate is displaced by the Zaibatsu and Asia's economy is separated from that of the world at large. Take that Hang Seng!) And that's not to mention the treatment that is given to, say, Viet Nam or Indonesia. (How about Papua New Guinea? ANYTHING? Nope... Not even in an Australian book as an aside in box as far as I can suss out.)

                                    Africa is pretty much an afterthought. What minor nods to Africa came much much later and aren't even a handful of books, a few chapters here and there and some off-hand mentions. It's better than, say, what almost all of South America ended up with, or Central or North Asia, or the Indian subcontinent, or really, to a degree, the Near & Middle East, but only just.

                                    If your standard for inclusion is effectively a mass of cultural appropriation (which is a BAD thing) and material cherry-picked based on it's perceived current (to that time) "coolness", then sure they did a bang-up job on China and some other places that people have heard of. And they poked at African like the broccoli on your plate you'd rather not eat. In fact, it has been said (again and again) that there are definite and definable ways in which the treatment of Asia as presented perpetuates the stereotypes created during imperialism, not in any way negating "centuries of suffering" and "negating the autonomy" of the region in question. And not just here and there, as thick veins throughout.

                                    The question is not whether not this is the case. It is. That's a professional opinion as a professor of anthropology and archaeology and it's definitely one that has expressed by many many others, including game designers in various ways here and there over the years. The question is how much can be used as is, salvaged vs. how much should be discarded, paved over, forgotten, tweaked, etc. It's not an issue of lumping, it's chosing when and how you lump. There's no way that every culture, ethnicity, nation can be included. They can't. There isn't a Ju/'hoansi sourcebook and there never should be. Particularly not one for Vampire, another for Werewolf, and still more for Wraith, Changeling and Mage. It's breaking the world down into chunks and managing it in a way that is doable in the game and is more reasonable and aware of what is actually out there in places where the situation is overall more different from the modern Western "baseline" from which the game can (and should, and, in fact, must) operate.
