          Well, Awareness can detect that some magic it's in use, Auspex it's no exeption. It can't detect the "type" of magic, however (you can "feel a disturbance in the force", not that that Auspex it's being used to spy you). To detect that, you would need Correspondence 1, perhaps Prime 1 can give some hints too.
          Also, usually, the use of Awareness needs to be declared. This depends on the Storyteller, but I think that a Clairvoyance would be subtle enough to pass under the radar, unless the mage has some reason to think it's being scryed (or it's just very paranoid) and decides to "check".

                              Originally posted by Aleph View Post
                              To detect that, you would need Correspondence 1, perhaps Prime 1 can give some hints too.
                              Also, usually, the use of Awareness needs to be declared.
                              Is there somewhere where this is specifically stated?

                              Based on the description of Awareness (and Alertness) and of Perception in the core book, they can be used actively or reflexively. "I have a bad feeling about this" of "I sense a disturbance in the Force" both imply reflexive use, in fact. (And those are used as examples of 1 dot...) In neither case were the characters in question consciously and actively examining and analyzing the situation or the state of the Force. The situation just pinged their "always on" radar.

                              In fact, Obi-wan is a pretty good example of how this might play out in Mage... His Perception + Awareness is high enough to feel a small moon sized light saber blow up a planet. He doesn't have to actively use the Force to monitor the universe for exploding planets. Then, he either actively used Awareness, probably Intelligence + Awareness based on the description of Perception as "notice and Intelligence as "analyze", get the "as if a thousand voices cried out and were silenced" bit. (He must have gotten a lot of successes, since that was 100% accurate.) That seems like it fits under Awareness as described. Alternatively, he could have used some kind of effect (Prime? Life?) to analyze the "blip". I guess you could require MASSIVE amounts of Correspondence or just say the "wavefront" of death hit HIM not so much as he subconsciously reached out to it. So it was effectively a localized effect. It would take Correspondence to find out WHERE the the 1000's of voices were silenced.

                              Meaning, I would probably say that a vamp using Clairvoyance would potentially trip a mage's Perception + Awareness reflexively. Probably not easily. A lot of those kind of roles seem to be set as Diff. 8 in the rules. Then the mage could quickly do a "from the hip" analysis using Intelligence + Awareness to get a vague idea of what is going on. ("I feel a presence in this room. It is watching us.") And/or the mage could start using Foci to put up effects that would get real info. (Correspodence would pinpoint the origin of the effect. Mind and or Prime to sense the effect and its nature. Etc. It's Mage. Mix and match to your heart's content.)
