        Exactly. Given that Mages tend to have multiple different kinds of Instruments (7 minimum, as of M20), it's harder to assume any given Mage is totally without magical options.

While this might not allow him to pull off all his more outrageous feats of Science(!), it does make him capable of defending himself. Possibly more trouble than he's worth, to any casual predator.

        Moreover, the scenario placed the attack in a supermarket. Any Inspired Scientist worth his salt could MacGuyver a way out of that situation with half the contents of a grocery store. Including, probably, with actual salt. Again, this isn't a perfect fix for the Mage; he's still in mortal danger and must be very quick to do said MacGuyvering. But it does mean he's not helpless. And what predators hate more is &quot;prey&quot; that can bite back. Why do you think most predator animals target the youngest, oldest, or sickest prey animals?
        The reason why a used such an ABSURD example of an archmage, that probably took 3 life times to reach his level of power, were to ilustrate exactly what kind of an absurd scenario to make such a claim of "beeing prey" true.

        So, unless you are in an absurd and total unrealistic situation, Mages are Dangerous. Very, very dangerous
