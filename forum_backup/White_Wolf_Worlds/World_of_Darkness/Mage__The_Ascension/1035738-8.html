            They should never get into fights with other supernatural creatures without proper preparation, should always avoid unnecessary fights with other supernatural creatures whenever possible, and should never fight fair against other supernatural creatures when they have to fight.
            As someone else said (in all these pages), I feel like this statement generally holds true of most splats. Any amount of combat between different supernaturals vary so much that any hypothetical presented will typically be flawed due to some advantage given to one side or the other. At least when it comes to Vampire, Werewolf, and Mage (I'm a lot less sure of the other lines), they all typically possess some powers that can waste someone who isn't prepared. Arguably mages can benefit the most from preparation and what they can do with their powers. There really isn't much even footing in fights because the element of surprise is so utterly massive in the game. And being aware of who/what your enemy is also matters so much. But the generalization of 'mages are prey' is entirely too situational to make. I typically dislike any situation where one side just knows someone else is a Mage, Werewolf, Vampire beforehand anyway.

            Even Karl's situation takes a lot for granted. Numerous things could have gone differently that results in him being dead. But noting all those things isn't necessarily productive because the permutations are countless. The scenario is neither here nor there in the discussion.
