    Would you allow a Mage to do that!?

    10-15-2024, 01:06 PM
    So, here I am again with some crazy question trying to see what other fellow Mage players and Storytellers alike think.

    As I have posted here many times, I'm a Storyteller with about 20 years of experieence with the game, I've been a storyteller since I was a child, at first because I noticed my friends were both not very good at it or didn't seem to like the idea of being a storyteller that much (probably both things combined, as both are linked). So I ended up loving it since I was very young and now I'm funny enough more open to crazy ideas my players come up with for stories. I also love crossovers and as such a player of mine came up with a very crazy idea, because he loves "a certain vampire" and his mage character came up with something I felt very, I mean very relutant to even bother considering. He wants to know if it would be possible to make a ward to protect his vampire lover from the sunlight, yes you read that right, he wants to ward the vampire against one of the banes of a vampire existence. I'm naturally, totally, utterly against such idea, but he wanted me to consult other fellow storytellers and here I am. What do you guys think of this absurd idea?

                              I can see various ways of accomplishing this, in order of ascending difficulty.

                              1) Warp light around the vampire. This is essentially the same principle as making the vampire invisible, which would be a Forces 2/Life 2/Matter 2 spell. Life 2/Matter 2 are locking spheres in this case. The basic principles of light beams and lenses have been around since the 11th century, so this shouldn't be out of reach in the 16th. Downsides: No light reaches the vampire so they are arguably blind unless they have Auspex or the mage incorporates a sensory spell for them.

                              2) Warp just sunlight around the vampire. It's essentially the same spell as in 1, but only warps sunlight. This one depends a lot on what makes sunlight special as the mage will have to be able to discern what counts as sunlight and what doesn't. This may require a lot of research, if it is possible at all. If he can do it, though, this is the same Forces 2/Life 2/Matter 2 just with extra successes required for complexity. Downsides: The vampire will look markedly more shadowy than anyone else nearby.

                              3) Convert all light that approaches the vampire into something else and back again. If the mage can't easily create sunlight (and Mage suggests that they usually can't), he can only convert it back into regular light. This would be Forces 3/Life 2/Matter 2. Downsides: Not much that I can think of mechanically. Narratively there are a lot of ways this could backfire over time.

                              4) A sunlight ward. M20 doesn't have a clear explanation of how to set ward conditions, but I'd recommend Corr 4/Forces 2/[Whatever makes sunlight special] 2 + Life 2/Matter 2 for locking. The Correspondence Ward blocks the light, Forces 2/[Something] 2 defines what light is blocked. Like (2) this will likely require research to properly specify. Downsides: Like in 2, the area around the vampire will look darker than everything else.

                              All of this assumes that it is the light and not something that travels with it that injures vampires. If it is actually something light-adjacent, the only one that will work is (4) and then only if the mage can figure out how to identify the light-adjacent thing.


                              Mage: The Ice-ension: An Epic Game of Reality on the Rink
