                            Jumping onto Spacecat's post above: I think a big issue is simply that the vast majority of material about the Massasa War is from the Tremere side.                             Sadly, there is no "House Tremere" type contemporaneous supplement that can illustrate the movements of the Hermetic Houses. Tosia's entry at least indicates that there is some suspicion/movement on that side. But that's a small hint compared to the focus on Tremere's schemes.

                            I can buy that the Hermetics are willing to downplay their own schemes and stratagems in their accounts to their modern day fellows though. Even granted that the Tremere are probably the worst of the Order, we've seen enough of their pre-Embrace practices to realize that these are NOT nice people. I suspect most modern Hermetics would be disgusted at the rite to consecrate Ceoris and the callousness toward human life that we see among the mortal mages wouldn't sit well either.

                            Sadly, until or unless we end up seeing some Mage-side supplements for this time period, storytellers are stuck deciding the Hermetics' side of the war. That said, they did make it to a stalemate, so that would at least imply they put up a fight.
