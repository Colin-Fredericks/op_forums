    Originally posted by Kharnov View Post

    A Taftani could work with a Traditions cabal, but they couldn't get the larger forces of the Traditions to act for their sake without surrendering a significant degree of autonomy. A single cabal is not an alliance.

    And the point of the Disparate Alliance is, as far as I can tell, that they get to keep being holdouts for the most part. Just now they're holdouts sharing information with other holdouts and working together towards common goals when the opportunities are available. The point of the Disparate Alliance is that they never need to surrender their autonomy to a higher political order, just a general prerogative of sharing information and working together against common enemies. Otherwise, they seem to just keep doing their own thing for the most part. Some groups are more involved with keeping the whole thing working than others, but they aren't giving orders to the individual Crafts. A comparison was made to the Anarchs earlier, and it's actually not a bad one. Just Anarchs with more of a cultural-spiritual thing because, ya know, they're Mages.

    Some groups probably will be more proactive about working as an Alliance, but others can still be part of the club and enjoy some of the benefits without having to make any big promises. It is actually a pretty reasonable move in an increasingly hostile world.

    But why would they need to? Pre-M20 the Taftani were utterly wiping the floor with the Technocracy. The middle east was slowly but surely reverting to a Taftani aligned reality zone under their efforts, and the Taftani had been making inroads into America, one of the centers of Technocratic power, since the 1940's.

    Again, I don't know the other crafts to say with as much confidence, but IMO a big part of their draw wasn't that they were 'Stubborn idiots who needed help but wouldn't ask for it.' which is pretty much what M20 portrays them all as.

    When before, the Crafts were utter badasses who could stand on their own.
