    Mystery/Meta Spheres.

    05-04-2017, 06:46 PM
    So, I've been considering the idea of 'secret' spheres. As it is, most mages abilities are fairly cut and dry. You generally can at least GUESS at 'how' that was done. Someone hurls a fireball? Forces was probably involved. A car was transformed into a pig? Life and Matter.

    But then you have the oddball cases where the spheres don't seem to easily apply.

    And it occurs to me. That there could be secret spheres. You make them up as ST at the start of the game. Throw in NPC's who make use of them. For example, a Meta Sphere that manipulates magic itself. In general, they should be formulated in such a way as to not make sense to the PCs ideas of how magic works. For example, being able to do something that in one of the standard spheres requires a very high level of mastery. But in this new mystery sphere is fairly low key. For example, a mystery sphere of Transferrance. It deals with taking something from one thing and giving it to another. You can create a material that's radioactive with fairly basic Transferrance + Matter. But normally that takes high levels of Forces and Matter. This is probably a terrible idea but it was something I thought of given a few minutes.

                                          Has anyone ran a chronicle with the minor spheres? I created several of them and used them alongside the major spheres in a chronicle that ran for over 40 sessions (I made minor spheres 25% cheaper than the nine major spheres).

                                          I found that the minor spheres were interesting but the interplay betweven the major spheres was greater in most cases. The minor spheres were often used without other spheres (with the exception of correspondence or the sphere of Travel which allowed for magic to be done at range), I found them less likely to be used in multi sphere rotes which made the game feel less satisfying.

                                          Does anybody have any thoughts on this. I like the minor spheres but my major experience in using them was somewhat disappointing them.

                                          Lastly, I created numerous minor spheres, here's a list of a few for relevance

                                          Travel: Allows one to travel distances, move unimpeeded and and perform magic from a distance. No manipulation of space directly but does allow for teleportation in the physical world and the umbra. Inspired by the Valderman pillar Fara.

                                          Beast: Life, Mind and some Spirit effects as they relate to animals.

                                          Storms/Weather: Forces, Prime and Spirit as it pertains to weather and to some degree motion. This one was basically the sphere of fire with a different element.

                                          Body: Life and Prime effects with an akashic focus on chi and life force, very limited shape shifting, largely based around humanoids.

                                          Fortune: Curses and Blessings, inspired by the Valderman pillar Forlog which also allows one to be well received by others.

                                          Harmony: This focused on healing with life and mind as well as some prime effects that could remove resonance that was deemed harmful (Dreamspeaker and Verbena based)

                                              Body sounds limmited, but I can see as working for a ton of enhancements and crazy matial arts movements. For instance, I may let someone with Body enchant human life pattern at Body 3-4, like as if he was using prime 4-5 (Training from hell that get's supernatural results ), obviously, this may need other spheres to get realy weird gains. Shapeshifting may be limmited, but at least at level 5 I would allow it to transcend the human form (to have a body of light, become like the river, join with nature, be the mountain, and stuff like that)-of course, in conjunction with the propper Spheres. As more concrete examples: I can see a Body 1-2 / Time 1 rote to adapt the rithm of your body to that of the opponent (timing the opponent's moves and adjusting your own body so you can counter them), a Body 3 / Forces 2 that allows to add the force of your opponent to your next blow (possibly trading damage received for extra Strength in your next blow to a greater degree that what you could get with Body alone)...etc.

                                              Travel it's self explanatory, but don't let it be just "the teleport sphere", travel includes knowledge of the path, alteration and creation of pathways, an understanding of movement, including the potential to block or alter the travel for someone. No manipulation of space?, then what happens when I want to destroy the possibility to travel somewhere (essentially ending the posibility to reach a place)?

                                              Storms/Weather: This one I find interesting because it's hard to make rain with the Spheres, I guess this makes it easy. In conjunction it's could be more impressive and vulgar...like, say, combine it with Fire, and get a rain of fire (at a ludicrously low level for such a massive shenanigan, I imagine), with Life and get a rain of frogs or to alter the relationships between weather and life (like,say, to create plants that can grow in the worst dry wheaters).                                                   I'd use minor spheres only in a game, where the mages would have a rather limited paradigm. You could replace the old sphere system to allow players to actually build their own spheres. This could either be in an orphan game, where other mages, like the Traditions, still use normal spheres and are very powerfull in comparison.
