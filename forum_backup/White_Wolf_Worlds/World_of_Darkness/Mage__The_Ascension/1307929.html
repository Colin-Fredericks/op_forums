    Hanging spells (Time 4)

    04-25-2019, 11:18 PM
    Hi, give ideas of uses for the Time 4 effect of hanging spells, besides hanging buffs with maximum successes to be used quickly.

    Also, we have examples of effects with duration bigger than a turn that can change after it is cast. In HDYDT there are examples where the mage shapshift into some animal that can breath fire, or engulf his body in elecricity or even teleport at will (no need for another spell). We also have wards, that is active and trigger some other effect when some condition is met (like some kind of patter of Force,Life,Matter, etc. entering/touching/etc.), all of them work without Time 4 (except for one kind of ward).

    Having said that, couldn't the mage use shapeshift to give himslef the ability to buff at will?

    For example: Let's say the mage wants to use Life 3 to turn his skin into a thick leather, making it able to soak lethal, whenever he wants. He must cast a Time 4 spell to hang this effect until he needs.
    OR he could just do like the shapeshift example an turn into a human being that is able to thick his skin at will, instead of the fire breathing tiger, using only Life sphere.

    Why would anyone use Time 4 in an effect, then? Am I missing something here?

    Dunno if I did explain it right..

      Implanting spells into shapeshifted forms certainly is an edge case, really. I guess it boils down to paradigm. *And* it is limited to implanting effects onto beings that these beings can then trigger on *themselves*. Hung spells are way more flexible.

      Now, on the topic of Wards, you can't just put any effect on a Ward/Ban. Their primary function is to encourage things to stay away from an area through various described methods, to block them outright, or to banish/lock spirits. Hung spells are, again, way more flexible than that.

      Now, why you would use Time 4 in an effect?

      First off, hung spells can be tied to a trigger. It doesn't need to be an active decision by the Mage to trigger the spell - it can be caused by any event, if you have the according Sphere(s) to tie it to the circumstance.
      This is *big* for any kind of automatically triggered defensive spells for example - and unlike permanently running defense spells, are potentially less obvious to those that can see ongoing magicks.
      Heck, they can trigger at the time your mage is asleep in their bed, unconcious, has sensory impairments or is simply *too wounded* to properly perform their practice without difficulty increases.
      And again, neither the spell nor the automatic trigger need to be actually put on your mage. Having an enemy as a temporary ally, and wanna make sure they don't backstab you? Time 4 spell with a trigger to make them melt as soon as they try to attack you. Traps around your sanctum? Done. Want to put 'special' safety into your car that magically keeps you from harm in case you crash? Done. Car's too precious to get a scratch? Have a hung spell that triggers as soon as the car *would* be about to crash and stops it.

      And those are just the examples dealing with damage or damage prevention. Hung spells can be about anything.

      Second, as example'd above, hung spells can not only be put onto your Mage, but also...pretty much anything else. Places, forces, objects, combinations thereof. With a trigger, again, to make the spell trigger under specific circumstances.

      Third, the hung spell *does not count against your one-Arete-roll-per-round limit*. You can trigger the Time 4'd spell in the same round you do an Arete roll. Or several. Like Wonders. With preparation, you can let out a barrage of effects at once. Or tie multiple to anything else to trigger all at once - or in series.

      Fourth, Time 4 hung spells have, aside of Time 4, no other strict requirements. You are not bound to also take Sphere X or Y - it entirely depends on what your character usually does. No *need* for Correspondence like with Wards. No *need* for Life like in shapeshifting. You're completely free to hang *any* kind of spell that you could do before with your sphere setup already, and for the purpose you previously used it.

                                                  It's worth remembering that every Effect a mage has active - all the ones they are "concentrating" on - count towards how difficult it will be to cast additional Effects. Every two simultaneous Effects make the next Effect accrue a +1 difficulty. Hence why Mages don't always have a huge list of Effects they cover themselves with every morning. One or two, maybe. Really, any more than one makes creating Effects in the moment more difficult, and the mage never truly knows all that they'll need beforehand.

                                                  By virtue of being Time 4, a hung effect does not apply here. The Effect isn't present but dormant, like a high-duration one. It exists outside the flow of time entirely, much like an object or creature can be so removed at the same level of mastery. It's not there, until it is.

                                                  This also makes hung Effects harder to detect and, more important, unweaved. A constant Effect is like a beacon to anyone with magickal senses. That makes the Effect vulnerable to attempts to dispel it. Even someone without Prime knowledge could notice some magick is going on, either through other Sphere senses ("the person's biology seems weird somehow") or through logical deduction given evidence. Hung spells are less likely to be noticed even by the mystically sensitive, because there's no Effect to sense. Yet. Only someone with knowledge of Time could reasonably be expected to spot the irregular knot in the timestream.
