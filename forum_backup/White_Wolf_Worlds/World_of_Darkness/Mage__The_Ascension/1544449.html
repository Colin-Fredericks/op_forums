                  In my games, the Technocrats and the VA make extensive use of all the above. The Technocrats have extensive libraries of abilities for the use of their agents, held under 7 keys and dispensed upon need, while the VAs have a vast network of trade of abilities at the Web, which they trade among each other and even with other Traditions.

                  Finally, the least sophisticated method is to call a Spirit for it. That's not as simple as it sounds. Good luck finding a Spirit of "rocketry ballistics"...

                  Once you found the proper spirit thou, and once you managed to strike a deal or subjugated it to do your bid (and deal with the consequences of either choice), then you can use Mind 3 and Prime to storage the knowledge for later use just the same.

                  And finally, at last, if you're looking for something Matrix style, all that you need is the proper ability stored and Correspondence 3 to download it from afar.

                                              "Muscle memory" is a thing, even if the name is poor from a scientific perspective.

                                              The problem is that this treads deeply into the fact that Mage heavily embraces Mind/Body duality via the Spheres, but there's very little evidence for it in real life. Motor skill retention might be part of the nervous system... but the nervous system is still cells and bodily functions (Life)... so when does it become a Mind Pattern instead? And if anything the whole point of motor skill retention training in the first place is to encode tasks so that you don't have to actually think about them. Martial artists train up from the basics so they can encode each part of a "punch" into automated pathways so they can fire off a whole series of functions with minimal processing. Can you Mind download neural processes you don't even consciously think about doing? Or are those Life processes? Is downloading all of the individual steps of a punch with Mind the same as encoding them as a retained motor skill, or is that second part Life?

                                              Another way to look at the problem is that the WoD system in general treats Abilities as body intellectual knowledge of a subject, and experience with practical application of the subject. It's not hard to accommodate the Mind/Body dualism of Mage if there was some sort of neat separate between "theory" and "practice" on terms of how Abilities are expressed. But since the WoD system blends the two, the line between what's Life and what's Mind is ultimately going to be highly subjective.

                                              Edit:

                                              You're over-stressing the brain, as the whole nervous system is involved and not everything goes to the brain, as well as "explosive" movement (physical training also involves flexibility, bone strengthening, slow-twitch muscle exercises for endurance, etc.), and broad generalizations (all species have a spectrum of physical abilities, there isn't one reaction time for cats, and one for humans, and training can increase reaction times; at least in specific circumstances).

                                              You're not at all addressing Mind vs. Life as Spheres.

In Mage, where mind-body dualism is hard coded into the rules, the nervous system and the mind are not the same thing. Something that is purely done in the brain is not the same as something being a Mind Pattern that is primarily governed by Mind: you can't expel an infection of the brain with Mind, you need to use Life, even if the primary symptoms are neurological.

                                                          You can't say, "it's in your brain, thus it's in your mind, thus you use Mind," because that's not actually how the game is structured. That's not to say people arguing for Mind being the primary Sphere for the effect are wrong in that conclusion (Dream and Hypercram certainly imply there's more validity to that on their own) but that the explanations given for what Spheres to use don't really align with the text.

                                                          Then again Athlethics for example would be almost pure life. Its just a trained body with maybe some technique to it.
                                                          Like this. The whole point of Athletics is that it's the techniques that allow dedicated athletes to excel beyond their raw physicality (as expressed via Attributes). Dismissing the technique side of Athletics (and potentially any creative uses like knowledge of the rules of different sports, or similar trivia) to try to claim it's clearly a "Life" Ability doesn't match up with the text. "Trained body" isn't just physical fitness, it's techniques for various athletic endeavors. Anyone with high Dexterity can probably be a decent hitter in baseball, but you buy up Athletics to become a great hitter because you have technique both for making contact, and for knowing how different swings can be used for different strategies (knowing how to maximize your chances for a solid line drive vs. going for the fences vs. purposefully fouling the ball vs. bunting vs a purposeful midfield blooper are all things that come from technique, not just having a trained body).
