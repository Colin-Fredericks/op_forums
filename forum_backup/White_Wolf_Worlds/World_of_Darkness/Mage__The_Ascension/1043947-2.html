    So Oliver's player contacted me and is dropping out of the campaign. I'm sad about this, but if he wasn't able to come and have fun with it, it's better. I hope he can jump back into this group soon, as we're planning some stuff with other games (Call of Cthulhu, the 1980s TSR Marvel Superheroes game, and Starfinder, the space Pathfinder that's coming out this fall). This changes the story arc slightly with what I was planning to do, effectively causing the Technocratic Union to deteriorate faster than was going to happen otherwise. This is because the mystics outnumber the Technocracy in the party, and the Technos who are left are about done with the Technocracy anyway.


    Season 1: Episode 7 The Machine

    Dramatis Personae
    PCs
    Arnold (Void Engineer): acting director of the Driftwood Institute.
    Kia (Order of Hermes bani Xaos): Cryptographer, puzzle-solver, consultant to the Technocracy on matters of the occult.
    Grover (Progenitors): Driftwood's chemist, pharmacopeist, still recovering from his time in Room 101.
    Dick "DAP3" (Cult of Ecstasy): burnout psych professor. Consultant on faerie phenomena hired by Driftwood.
    Robert (Order of Hermes bani Ngoma): Social worker aiding the Zimbabwean refugees in the area around the anomaly.
    Penny Layne (Euthanatos): Sister to Arnold. Not officially a Driftwood consultant, but random bystander to their activity.

    NPCs
    John Glass (NWO): security director of Driftwood Institute
    Dan Dorr (Void Engineers): Arnold's intern, Army vet

    Scene 0.
    In chat roleplay on Facebook, Penny found a cool t-shirt that she likes and orders it with Arnold's credit card, which she stole from his wallet. Arnold goes to work without any money or credit cards.

    Scene 1.
    Arnold and Glass return from Wyoming with the medallion they confiscated from Kenji Harada. Arnold immediately calls the consultants to look at it. Dick doesn't have the right Spheres to look back in time to see what it looked like before it was defaced and the coordinates wired into it were lost. However, they notice there is something odd and asymmetrical about it. They though there were 360º marks cut around the perimeter, but it's not symmetrical. Kia asks to count them. The circle is divided into 359 equal segments with the tic marks, not 360.
    Arnold (genius) and Kia (cryptographer) are aware that 359 is a prime number. 360 makes sense for dividing a circle since it's evenly divisible by many numbers, but why would you divide a circle by a prime number?
    Robert has 3 dots in Occult and Belief Systems—this appears Qlippothic, he reports.

    Much discussion about what this means and how it explains the anomaly.

    Arnold tries to order lunch for his staff and discovers his money and cards missing.

    Scene 2.
    Penny orders a pizza with the stolen credit card. The pizza arrives, but the credit card receipt has a long missive printed after the total (where would otherwise say something like "Thank you" or whatever). The receipt is about 2' long. It offers Arnold help, encouraging him to continue to send updates to the whistleblower dropbox in exchange. She hides it and doesn't tell Arnold about it.

    Scene 3.
    For several weeks, Arnold has been trying to call for material and personnel resources from the Border Corps and other Void Engineer higher-ups. He has left some messages, no one has called him back. He isn't able to get through as these offices are being reorganized and the unEnlightened staff are leaving for other jobs, reassigned, etc. The Enlightened staff are working by themselves. Their paychecks might start bouncing soon.
    Arnold wants a generator that thickens the Gauntlet. The person who approves such requests doesn't actually exist. There's a position for such a manager, but it's vacant and the hiring freeze means no one is in that job position right now.

    Arnold gets a call from a Void Engineer manager, Mr. van der Baas. (Arnold rolls many successes to detect subterfuge). Mr. van der Baas sounds frightened. He offers a Gauntlet machine. Arnold is suspicious. The guy insists that they take it.
    Arnold goes home to get his credit card so he can rent a Ryder truck to pick up the machine. Penny confronts him with the note and she's upset that she got a delivery order of Illuminati Pizza.

    They go to a restaurant to meet with van der Baas. Dick gets grits (to go, because they're in a hurry, apparently). Van der Baas is kinda freaking out. They try to mess with his memory, but it doesn't work.

    Arnold reveals his plan to the others—"I'm gonna drive this thing into the anomaly and turn it on."

    cont'd

        I got busy and didn't finish my summary

        Scene 4.
        Arnold is in a rush to get the mission underway; so much so he forgets to even put on his quantum superposition suit. They move the Gauntlet device from the Ryder truck to a Driftwood 4WD pickup. Of all the characters, NPC Dan is the best driver (how smart of December-me to make an NPC with dots in all the things players don't usually bother to commit to their PCs).

        They drive to the gateway/breach in the event horizon. Dan botches his Drive roll and gets the pickup stuck. Kia uses a sigil drawn on the tire to increase the traction with Forces magick and it pulls itself out.
        It's raining inside the anomaly. It is very rough going. They pick their way up the skid trail through the clear-cut. There are already starting to be saplings and scrub growing in despite the pine forest having only been clear-cut recently.

        They get to highway 15 and it's much rougher than it was before. The approach to the bridge has washed out into a sinkhole. Rather than try to go around or do any shenanigans, Arnold chooses to just turn it on right here. Arnold is starting to get a bit careless in his haste, so the others convince him to take it out of the truck, turn the truck around, and prepare to escape before turning it on. They do.

        Arnold switches on the machine.

        …and, scene

        Scene 5.
        The next thing Kia becomes aware of is he's in a tent on a beach. The sun is up, filtered through the colored nylon of the tent. Grover and Robert are asleep beside him. He assesses his situation. He has bruises he can't remember geetting and more Paradox than he had before. He looks outside the tent. There is a young man in board shorts cooking over a campfire. "Hey, you're up! Want some bacon and eggs?"

        He wakes Grover and Robert up. They each have injuries they don't remember getting. They all have lost Willpower points and gained Paradox and two experience points somehow.

        "Did we get Lost Season Fived?" Dick's player asks. "It's more like The Hangover. What happened and how did you get here?"

        Kia asks the man his name. "Oh, man. You finally lost your shine. That's okay, it happens." The young man introduces himself as Solar. ("Never go with a hippie to a second location" the players joke).
        "What happened?"
        "You came out of there," he points to up the beach, "and it was the most radical thing I'd ever seen. We had a wild rumpus here."
        Breakfast is almost ready. He makes a big gesture to a woman surfing.
        "That's December. She's been riding that same wave for an hour."
        Sure enough, the woman is surfing a wave that appears to never reach the beach. It's like reality is a treadmill and she could travel in that direction forever without going anywhere. She's surfing the spatial distortion at the leading edge of the event horizon.
        To get out of the spatial distortion, she flickers out and reappears 30 yards to the side and paddles her board into the beach. Solar serves some eggs and bacon onto paper plates.

        meanwhile

        Arnold and Dick wake up in jail. They're in a holding cell with two young men, possibly teenagers. One, Moog, is wearing a camo Monster energy drink T-shirt with the sleeves cut off, boots (laces missing), and the shape of a baseball hat permanently molded into his greasy hair. The other, Kev, is a skinny, pale, goth kid.

        Arnold raises a ruckus and a deputy shows up. He demands his phone call.

        They get styrofoam trays of yesterday's mac-and-cheese (this is leftovers from the larger prison kitchen, which is elsewhere). Dick tries to persuade the deputy into bringing seconds. "Our last sheriff coddled you people. Not anymore. We're treating you freaks the way you deserve now."

        Arnold asks what charge they're being held for. The deputy replies, "I don't know."
        "No, that's not how this works. If you're holding me, you have to tell me."

        Eventually, they allow him a phone call. He calls his apartment at talks to Penny, who agrees to come to where he is. Dick, taking the role of "good cop" talks the deputy into giving Arnold his phone. They bring back a manila envelope, he pulls out a waterlogged phone that won't power on, and hand it to Arnold. This is Technocracy issue, so being waterlogged is only a temporary problem. Arnold uses a Prime Effect to charge it enough to turn it on.
        They discuss whether they're escaping or talking their way out.

        meanwhile

        At the beach, Kia, Robert, and Grover are bombarding Solar and December with questions. Solar is vague but eventually relents and says, "I guess I owe you after last night." So he takes each of their plates and sprinkles some salt over the bacon and eggs as he hands it to them and they eat.
        The sky turns purple. A dragonfly passing by becomes a diminutive woman with dragonfly wings. They notice that Solar has horns on his head like a goat and hooves. December is a tall woman with elongated ears and nearly lit from within with radiance.

        Where Solar had been gesturing earlier, there is a tall, brambly hedge or thick plants and thorns going all the way to the beach and into the ocean. There is an opening in the hedge up the beach leading to what appears to be a maze.

        This is the "event horizon" surrounding the anomaly.

        It has grown again. It's reached the Gulf of Mexico.

        End of Episode 7

            An aside
            Last year, Larry, who plays Arnold, the Void Engineer geographer, ran a one-shot Pathfinder game which was an epic haunted house crawl. At the center of the house was a Pillar, which was absorbing souls to feed a Old One (Yog-Soggoth, I think) to bring it into the world.
            Bryan, Kia's player, arrived late. We knew he was coming late, but he ended up coming even later than we thought; so he missed all of the plot exposition up to that point.

            His first scene basically went like this:
            "The pillar is speaking directly into your mind. It is telling you to approach and enter. What do you do?"
            "I enter the pillar."
            "Wait, what? You were supposed to make a Will save to resist it."
            "Oh well, too late. I enter the pillar."

            It was the funniest damned thing that's happened in our group in possibly ever.

            Since then, we've used "Enter the pillar" as shorthand for giving in to evil. That'll come up in this episode.

                Season 1, Episode 8: The Hangover

                Dramatis Personae
                PCs
                Arnold (Void Engineer): acting director of the Driftwood Institute.
                Kia (Order of Hermes bani Xaos): Cryptographer, puzzle-solver, consultant to the Technocracy on matters of the occult.
                Grover (Progenitors): Driftwood's chemist, pharmacopeist, still recovering from his time in Room 101.
                Robert (Order of Hermes bani Ngoma): Social worker aiding the Zimbabwean refugees in the area around the anomaly.
                Penny Layne (Euthanatos): Sister to Arnold. Not officially a Driftwood consultant, but random bystander to their activity.

                NPCs
                Dan Dorr (Void Engineers): Arnold's intern, Army vet
                John Glass (NWO): security director of Driftwood Institute

                Solar: Surfer vagrant. (Satyr)
                December: Surfer vagrant. (Sidhe)
                Moog: Redneck in jail (Redcap)
                Kevin: Emo kid in jail (Sluagh)

                Not present
                Dick "DAP3" (Cult of Ecstasy): burnout psych professor. Consultant on faerie phenomena hired by Driftwood. (Larry just moved into a new apartment and is up to his neck in boxes)

                Scene 1:
                Last session, Arnold had been given back his phone as a joke by the sheriff deputy. It's got water in it and won't power on. Arnold charges the battery with Prime 2 and powers it on (Technocracy gear is more robust than that). It alerts him that 22 attempts to hack it were stopped (searched by whatever cop tech tools the sheriff has for mundane searches). He has many messages
                Immediately, Oliver's AI familiar, 0fe1:a sends him a text message. "Are you there? Are you alive?"
                "Yes. I'm in a local county lock-up. Need extraction."
                "We were worried about you. You've been gone for three days."

                Arnold demands to know what charges he's being held. The deputy who brought him the mac-and-cheese earlier says he just got on shift and wasn't there when the brought him in and doesn't know.

                Kevin, the emo kid, tells him, "He thinks you're one of us."
                "And what's that?"
                "Weirdos."
                "He's not wrong there. Did we get arrested together?"
                "Yeah. So, can you do anything, or are you useless?"

                At this point, Arnold comes up with a plan to use his phone as a spatial something something and make a warp portal to escape. We decide he needs the phone to be outside the wall and it can make a warp straight outside. Kevin then squeezes through the bars (causing his skull to compress about 4" narrower, which he just shakes off). They hear the deputy returning and Kevin slides back inside.

                "Which one of you is Layne?"
                "I'm Arnold Layne."
                "Lawyer's here."

                The "lawyer" is a man in a black suit who Arnold has never seen before.
                "We're here to extract you."
                "Good. We've been waiting."
                "We?"
                "Yeah, I'm with a consultant and two others who have knowledge related to my mission."
                "We're not concerned with the others."
                "The consultant is vital to the mission. And I'm missing three days of memory. The randos might be able to fill in important details."
                "You have all the information we need. You were once committed to finding traitors in the Union. Are you still?"
                "I've been submitting regular reports."
                "We've read the official reports. It's the unofficial reports which are most interesting. If we're going to release you, what can you tell us about John Glass?"
                "I think I'll just go back to my cell."
                "That's your prerogative, but if you change your mind, have this."

                From a folder from his briefcase, he hands him a piece of fabric-like material with a circuit board etched on it. "Keep this. Activate it if you change your mind."
                "Will this work for me and all my compatriots?"
                "Sure."
                (Rolls to see if he can tell if he's lying. He's not, but he clearly doesn't care.)

                He sneaks it back to his cell under his shirt. He analyzes the circuit board. It's a transporter pad.

                Can I reprogram it?
                The coordinates are printed right on the circuit. It probably would be difficult even with your tools in your shop.
                Can I tell what the coordinates are? (Rolls)
                It's not Earth. It's transdimensional.
                Okay, I'll get on the pad and activate it.

                Did I just enter the Pillar?

                Yes. Yes you did.

                Arnold finds himself in a dim room. Feels like a soft, carpeted floor. The walls are mildly luminescent. As his eyes adjust to the darkness, he realizes that the floor is a surface like a tongue, and the walls are some sort of skin, like intestinal lining. He can see veins pulsing inside the walls. There is a door, which is a vertical slit in one wall. It's growing hair around it.

                Here ends the story of Arnold for now. Let's check in on the others…

                    Sorry. Busy with work and play and family commitments. Time to finish the synopsis.

                    Season 1, Episode 8,
                    Scene 2

                    Meanwhile at the beach, Solar and December are talking with the other mages about what they see under the effect of the Glamour enchantment.
                    The event horizon now looks, rather than an invisible barrier, a gigantic hedge wall surrounding a maze.
                    Grover tries to take a biological sample and cuts himself on the thorns.
                    Solar describes the Hedge as a "cosmic no-tresspassing sign" and he's not keen on going inside of it. "Man, anything you hit over that fence is gone forever. But it is making for some gnarly surf, though." Where the Hedge touches the water is an infinite space so one could ride a wave forever without reaching the beach (as December was doing in the last episode).

                    December sits off to the side having a conversation with the Magic 8-Ball that had fallen out of Robert's backpack. Apparently it's telling her about how it escaped from the Technocracy lab.

                    The Enchantment begins to wear off. Dan rolls up driving Dick's car, Penny is with him. (Please excuse the GM handwaving here, I'll figure out some way that this makes sense later).

                    With their memories of the last three days lost, they decide they need to go back into the Hedge and retrace their steps and see what happened to the machine and what happened to them.

                    The mages on the beach came out of the Hedge to the south, while the portal they had been using before (and that Dan, Arnold, and Dick escaped from) is northwest.

                    Scene 3

                    The players have been teased with some hints about what happened. Burns and tears in their clothing, Kia's fingers are covered in paint, a missing backpack.
                    They decide rather than drive all the way around to the northwest entrance, they're going to re-enter the way they came out and retrace their steps.

                    Penny's Entropy effect reading the winds of fate tends to square with Kia's Correspondence sense which leads them up the highway inland from the beach. The highway turns into undeveloped area of pine forest with scattered businesses along the highway. They come to a cement manufacturing plant
                    (I walked this entire adventure in Google Street-view. This part of Florida is startlingly sparsely populated. This cement plant is really there.)

                    At the fence is the torn remnants of Grover's backpack, snagged and torn open. The contents of the pack, including several fruit, are scattered on the ground. Grover picks up the fruit and immediately eats it. "It's like Eddie coming out of the tunnel into ToonTown" and Grover gets the first real look at what "The Anomaly" actually looks like.

                    Far off on the horizon, Grover sees a very, very tall tree and he insists they go toward it. The others want to explore what the deal is with this concrete plant and why they went there. Kia sees a sigil spray-painted on the side of a building. It's a Forces sigil for igniting.

                    There's a crater at the back of the building. The main building is badly damaged. There appear to have been a large propane tank at the center of the blast.

                        Scene 4
                        They realize why the propane tank exploded as they are confronted with a chimæra. Not like the mythical beast, but a Cronenbergian nightmare of a Florida panther, cottonmouth, and goat awkwardly fused together with vestigial limbs. The snake protrudes through the ribcage of the panther, its tail on the opposite side, black and necrotic. A goat is fused sideways across the torso of the panther with the legs sticking out uselessly.
                        The creature is burned badly, making its skin even more horrifying as the places where the creatures fuse together have exposed muscle and bone. They must have blown up the propane tank in an attempt to kill it. While the mages remember none of this due to the Fae Mists, the beast remembers all too well and is looking for revenge.

                        Robert rushes into the cement plant to find cover and to look for a way to use the building to his advantage.
                        Penny slashes the creature with knives charged with Prime to make the damage aggravated (her signature move) on her way toward the building.
                        Kia… Kia goes super-Saiyan and shoves it with Forces 2 rolling absurdly well, throwing it 30 yards backwards.

                        Then next turn Kia announces "I'm gonna boop it on the snoot…by which I mean I'm going to slam it to the ground by increasing the gravity."
                        Five. Successes. 0, 0, 9, 8, +1 for Willpower expended.

                        The beast rolls its Strength to resist the gravity but its highest die roll is a 5. It's pinned in place and cannot stand.

                        The sigil which Kia learned as an apprentice appears glowing on the ground spontaneously, encircling the beast.

                        Dan empties the Doorbuster (a Technocracy rifle with an underslung shotgun) into the beast's heads. It is slain.

                        end of session. will continue next time.

                            Massive thread necro, but this is the home of my plot synopsis. My game ran a few sessions longer than anticipated then got dropped from our regular gaming schedule. Then Dick's player moved out of state before it came back around, so we've been on hiatus for two years. He was back in town for a visit so we ran a special session.

                            Episode 9 (I apparently didn't type up the last scene two years ago, but this was the last session.)

                            Scene 1: Dick, along with his new friends Kevin and Moog are still locked in the county drunktank with the brimstone-smelling teleporter pad. Kevin says he can get out if they had a distraction. Dick reaches out with Mind sphere and discovers the deputy looking at Internet porn on the computer and pushes his thoughts in that direction. He leaves to go to the bathroom. Kevin and Moog, who have been in this drunktank before, know that they need to act now, early in the morning, before the rest of the staff show up for work. He squeezes through the bars, apparently crushing his own skull to do so and returns with keys.
                            DAP ducks into the evidence locker and grabs their stuff, including Arnold's Technocracy-issued pistol. On his way out, he also takes a box containing a brick of hashish. Kevin insists that they hurry, but Moog also comes into the room and grabs a red snapback Make America Great Again hat; refusing to leave without it.

                            Outside, Moog smashes out the window of an unmarked sheriff car and they jump inside. He then growls a string of profanity-laden threats and insults directed at the car and its engine starts. They drive off, just missing the sheriff himself coming in. (World of Darkness meta: Dick has no way of knowing this, but Kevin and Moog are Sluagh and Redcap changelings, respectively. Dick's player missed a lot of the nuance of what was going on, but Robert's player, who has played Changeling The Dreaming got a good laugh from watching this scene).

                            Dick uses the hashish then falls asleep in the backseat as Moog takes the car down some country side-roads to hide.

                            Ordinarily, you need higher Mind sphere to do this, but Dick dreams himself into the Astral Plane and finds himself alongside his comrades, Dan, Robert, Kia, and Grover.

                            Scene 2: Grover, still high on the Glamour-filled goblinfruit, looks up and sees the Astral form of Dick and is able to communicate with him. No one else can see this, so Grover just looks insane.
                            They make their way north from the cement plant. They detect a radio signal that is transmitting the same verse that they found in the stone floor tiles and the voices. Weird. It appears to be coming from a radio tower on their way, so they make their way to the top of a rise (it's Florida, so overall it isn't that hilly). Somehow, the tower is running without power.
                            Dick and Grover, who are able to see things' chimerical faerie forms, see, instead of a radio tower, a tree extending to the sky. Dick, much more of an occultist than the Technocrat, Grover, identifies it as an avatar of Yggdrasil, the World Tree, except it's a pine instead of an ash. Dick hugs the tree. Grover cuts some sap from the tree and eats it (which the others see as scraping paint off and eating the chips. You know that paint probably has lead in it, right?) Both absorb a point of Correspondence for their efforts.

                            Scene 3: They reach a massive sinkhole that has collapsed route 15. At the bottom is their pickup along with the generator, lying dormant on its side in a puddle. Dan climbs down and tries to repair it and get it started again.

                            Suddenly, the voices, which had been held at bay by the Mind devices Oliver developed, get more insistent, trying to stop them from what they are doing. Not louder, they remain whispers, but Grover struggles to make his Willpower rolls.
                            Dick, because he is in the Astral Plane, sees the voices in a completely different way. He sees a massive, minotaur-like beast screaming the words instead of whispering them. It appears to have shackles around its arms and legs leading off into the distance somewhere.

                            Scene 4: Dan completes the repairs on the device and turns it on. Dick and Grover see a hedge full of brambles grow up to surround the generator to the edge of the sinkhole. Dick sees the minotaur scream and starts punching and clawing at it until its fists bleed. The others see nothing but can sense that the spatial anomaly has returned. Dan gets very excited that there was a positive result and sets about measuring it.


                            End of session. To be continued—I didn't know at the time—two years later.
