                Originally posted by Quantumboost View Post
                What I'm saying is, if you're making it so every possible method of resisting the Beast aside from greeting the sun is actually the Curse letting the vampire resist it better, it removes some amount of responsibility - their fault.
                Yes, they have little responsibility for their curse (except Tremere). They're still responsible for what they do with their curse.
                It's these aspects of their existence that gave rise to organizations such as the Sabbat (we are not responsible for being monsters, but responsible monsters nonetheless), and the Laibon (who can give up on their power from Orun to resist the beast with Aye).

                Originally posted by Quantumboost View Post
                Moreover, in using that interpretation, I'm not focusedly and deliberatey disregarding what the merits in question actually say they entail.
                I really wanted to avoid discussing this point at all costs. Because, last time I did something similar, I got banned...
                so...but...you know, I'd like to carry on to the conversation...screw the mods.

                But, before I go deeper, let me remind you that, while v20 rules allows you to play curse-less paradox-less brainwashed vampires. These same vampires can also find a way to break their hunger, their clan curse, their shackles, use magick (yes "magick", not "magic"), bloat the sun, turn the tide, lower their generation with an attainable ritual, raise an army of mages, take over the technocracy and one-punch an Archmage...

                is this the kind of vampire you want in your game?...simply put, I won't talk about v20. Like I said in my previous posts, there is no curse in V20. And, like the previous vampire vs mage thread pointed out: v20 vampires are broken as fuck.

                Calm Heart, V20 p. 485. Exception here. This one existed in Revised along with the Guide to Technocracy so...

                It's obviously a permutation of the curse, it's a natural state where the beast's urges are weaker. Brujah cannot take this merit (their beast is stronger instead of weaker).

                It's not an aspect of the vampire's personality, this is supported by the Trujah, Unifiers (PatIV), Martyrs (Redemption) and Necronomists (Death and the Soul) which are known for their coldness and extraordinary calm, yet they get no bonus in resisting the beast (unless they take this merit).

                Lion-Hearted, Player's Guide to the High Clans, p. 204. I believe it's a permutation of the curse, which is similar to the Laibon curse. Laibon don't have self-control, they have the Aye score (which is a substitute to humanity that can go up to 10) to resist the beast (but they pay a big price for it).

                Flock, Player's Guide to the Low Clans, p. 186.permutation. The curse is weak, the beast is not powerful enough to override the I.F.F, which, normally, is only possible for vampires on Instinct (who can let the beast take over while staying conscious).

                Controlled Thirst, Player's Guide to the High Clans, p. 203. Permutation. Hunger comes from the curse, hunger is dulled, the beast's hunger is dulled. It seems like a different version of prey exclusion and methuselah's thirst.

                Note that, while it does mean you frenzy less for blood, it also means the beast is going to be a freaking killing machine when frenzying for anything else.

                I would count it as a flaw for vampires on Instinct (especially Road of Sin). Harder to tame the beast. If you can't satisfy the blood lust with human blood, he's going to satisfy it some other way (murder...).

                Philosophical Humanist, Road of Humanity p. 73. This one doesn't look like a permutation. But at the same time, is not an aspect of the vampire's personality.

                It does seem similar to calm heart, especially since Brujah can't take the merit (so it could be a permutation). And several Paths are all about judicious application of reason and do not believe in neither Caine nor God, yet they get no bonus against frenzy, so...I don't know, it doesn't make sense.
