                            But, it's just that those who favor the Traditions, don't understand that it's more complex than simply "right vs wrong".

                            Besides... Why have the Union always been one step ahead in the Ascension War?

                            The answer: the Masses.

                            The Union always had the unconcious support of the masses. The Traditions, for all their efforts, never TRULY answered the needs of the masses.

                            Hey, when the old OoR arose, the Order of Hermes alone could'd take them down.

                            But the Hermetics were busy with political pettyness.

                            The OoR went for the little guy. They gave those dirty peasants, who were unworth of the attention of the magestrocracy, tools. Protection. Education.

                            The masses, by and at large, gladly embraced the new Consensus.

                            That surely says something.

                            About the Union being fascist: that's a reflex of people who thinks they are better than the others. That, in fact, seems to be a flaw that all awakened society have, it's not exclusive to the techies.

                            And why? Simple... Paradigm. When you have a worldview, which you think it is THE ONLY TRUTH, or at least "the best truth", it's hard to consider any other view just as valid as yours. All the traditions think they are the coolest guys ever. And not only that: they think the sleepers are misguided, blinded fools. That's the truth: the traditions as a whole, hold the sleepers with contempt.

                            Do you doubt? Well, just consider how the Traditions call the mundane humans as "sleepers", and themselves as "awaken". Sort of like "those fools follow the technocrat's Paradigm because they are blinded to the thruths that only WE know".

                            Mages are elitists by nature. Why would a Hermetic, or Chorist, or Etherite, or any other, ask the opinion of a dumb sleeper? What the hell do they know?

                            That's why the Union thinks they have the right to be humanity's "sheppards". Because, like all enlightened/awakened, they "know better".

                            Every mage knows whats better for the inferior sleepers, better than the sleepers do, after all, what those deluded apes know right? And this is the root of all the opression of the magestrocracy.
