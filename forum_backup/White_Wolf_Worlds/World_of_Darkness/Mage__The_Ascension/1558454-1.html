    Originally posted by Dataweaver View Post
    ​​​
    "Picking up where you left off" isn't a non-Ritual extended roll: all extended rolls are Rituals. What "Picking up where you left off" is is a means of resuming an Extended roll after you've suspended it. (See p.542) The Wits+Esoterica/Technology roll isn't about accumulating successes on the extended roll; it's about whether or not you get to keep the successes you've already accumulated. On a success, you keep them, and your next Arete roll will add to them; on a failure, all previously accumulated successes are lost and your next Arete roll will start things over with a new first roll.
    Strictly speaking, there are two sections that deal with Picking Up Where You Left Off, one in basic spell rolling rules (pg 537) and one in the ritual rules (pg 542). That said, I agree that the one on pg 537 is best understood not as an extended roll but as an urgent attempt to finish a spell that should have been complete in a single roll and fell short. It's not an intentional situation.

            If you can only reduce your difficulty to -3, then Rituals would be always better if you have the time. It's better to cast at -3 than to cast at -3, -2, -1, 0 ...

            Mechanically rituals are better when the amount of rolls you will have to make can't be "tanked" by your means to reduce the difficulty, AND you have the time. It depends a lot on how many successes you need and how much Arete you have.

            *A "brief rite" (up to 5 successes) takes 5 minutes tops. So, in this case there's no reason to think a ritual would take longer (could easily allow one roll per turn)

            *"Ceremonies" will almost always take longer and I think are at the soft spot where this could lead to abuse because up to 10 successes aren't that hard to get on a few rolls, if you have a lot of modifiers on your favor. Even then, if you have the hours to spend I think it's still better to not risk several rolls and pile up difficulties.

            *A "Great Work" most certainly takes longer, but it's also for stuff that requires a lot of successes (and here's where cults and working in concert shine). For most cases it wouldn't be wise to try to "fast-cast" this sort of stuff, even if it wasn't completely against the spirit of the rule. But there could be some fringe cases where "picking up" could be better...if it's allowed

            Let's not forget that the "Picking Up Where You Left Out" rule itself asks from the ST wheter a Ritual migth be in order after the first retry. So, RAW, the ST has to adjudicate wheter you can use one or the other. It's not a "default option" you can choose to do w/o asking the ST.

            RAI, it's likely intended for things you can reasonably do in one roll (hence why it asks you if a Rituals shouldn't be in order after the first retry, if your retry wasn't a Failure).

            Mage isn't a bulletproof game, but I think this one ain't so problematic.
