    Originally posted by quelsec
    If its a universal truth then it can be changed with consensus.
    Not necessarily. There are “universal truths” that the Consensus can't change; they're called “Earthly Foundations”. The thing that often gets overlooked when discussing Earthly Foundations is that such “universal truths” can be changed, by a mage who has sufficient dots in the right Sphere. All that the Consensus can do is to decide whether or not the mage will be slammed with Paradox if he changes it; and even then, only if “it” isn't an Earthly Foundation.

    The only thing, besides insufficient Spheres, that restricts what a mage can do is his own beliefs: if a mage doesn't think he can do it, he can't.

    Except, it seems, for morality. Again, in 30 years of publication, I have never once seen an Effect that changes morality. What's right and what's wrong is a metric that mages often disagree on; but it's one of the very few things in the setting that no one changes. The Technocracy, for instance, doesn't go around Terranorming reality into one where they're morally in the right.

    The closest I've ever seen to such a thing even being contemplated was the Tenth Sphere of Telos in Ascension; and note that that Sphere was name-checked, but not detailed. And rather than being something that mages can harness and use to their own ends, it was presented as something that shows up and passes judgment on everyone at the end of all things, not unlike a Supreme Being.
