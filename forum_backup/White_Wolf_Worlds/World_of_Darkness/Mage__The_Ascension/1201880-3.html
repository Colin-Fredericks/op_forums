                                Originally posted by Lian View Post



                                I Profoundly disagree. While the Technocracy are presented as a playable option. I would say they are Presented as the Default bad guys.
                                Objectively, in M20? No.
                                Unlike previous editions of Mage (aside from Sorcerers Crusade), Mage 20 does not default to a Traditions-centric viewpoint. Instead, we're presenting a wide range of potential Mage characters. With that range comes a bigger perspective on the Awakened experience, so the following chapter features as objective a view as you could possibly get from a world in which reality is anything but certain.
                                — page 120
                                The chapter in question covers Awakened history and the various factions of the Ascension War. But the even-handed approach isn't confined to that chapter: throughout the book, you have statements like “mystic mages blame the Technocracy, the Technocrats blame the mystics, and both sides of that divide are right.” (p.75)

                                See also the Common Ground section on pp.349–353, which includes several examples of how a troupe including both Technocrats and non-Technocrats can be brought together and given enough rationale to stay together to hold a chronicle together. Chapter Seven ends with summaries of five types of Chronicles: the Traditions, the Technocracy, the Disparates, the Orphans, and the Mixed-Faction.

                                The closest M20 comes to an anti-Technocrat bias is the way it occasionally presents Technocratic terminology off to the side, and tends to use the Traditions' terminology by default. But that's more a case of practicality than bias.

                                You're free to choose to see the Technocracy as the bad guys; but that's your choice, not something M20 pushes on you. You might even decide that the Technocracy is objectively bad, and that anyone who sees them as the good guys is delusional. M20 actively warns against such certainty, citing it as a symptom of hubris while indicating that it's an all-too-common flaw among mages of all stripes.

Men and women so obsessed with their pursuit of Magic and Wonder that they lose sight or simply don't care about the damage they could cause to those around them. Even when they try to use their Magic to help the Masses it can lead to disastrous results.

When faced with that, it's not really that hard to understand why they would eventually become what they are now. The world is a dangerous place and the Union for all it's faults and evils is trying to make it safer for the Masses which makes them one of the few major groups in WoD to have that as a principle mandate.

                                    After all, the Craftmasons and the Order of Reason were ultimately born in part because of the cruelties, excesses and hubris of Mages. And that had the situation been reversed, with the Traditions becoming the dominant Mage group over the Order of Reason, I could just as easily see the Traditions becoming just as bad as the Technocracy is.

                                    Homo sapiens. What an inventive, invincible species. It's only a few million years since they crawled up out of the mud and learned to walk. Puny, defenceless bipeds. They've survived flood, famine and plague. They've survived cosmic wars and holocausts. And now, here they are, out among the stars, waiting to begin a new life.
