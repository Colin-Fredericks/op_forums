    Faverite use for a Demesne

    10-12-2021, 07:10 AM
    I always look at the Demesne background and I'm never sure what to do with it. I think it's conceptially really cool, but mechanically a bit.... EH. As you don't really need it to do cool stuff with mind.

    So whats your favierite thing to do with a Demesne? Or what cool things have you done with the background?

      I think some of it depends on the storyteller, but I had an Akashic Brother who would use Mind to commit the text of many many books to memory, and store them in his Demesne. In this way, he gained a Library background rating. Added to this, almost every time he slept, he would enter his Demesne (he also had the Lucid Dreaming talent, though it's not strictly necessary) and he would study. For him, no time was ever wasted just sleeping - in the waking hours he would do physical things like train and kick ass, and while he was sleeping he would study and gain knowledge in his Demesne.

      If you can pull others into your Demesne, you can use it as an extremely secure place to have discussions or to do things to their subconscious sleeping minds that might otherwise be impossible. If you can pull an otherwise unbeatable enemy there, you may be able to destroy them because within your own Demesne you have godlike power. It's essentially your own personal horizon realm that adjusts to your every whim. Bringing others there can carry risk though - you simultaneously give them a way to more easily find it again on their own, and like a Node, such a place has power and someone with sufficient ability may try to take it over for themselves.

          I have a story with an NPC who can access a dream realm very much like a Demesne, but it doesn't perfectly fit the Background description, because the dreamer can access knowledge from outside his own experience about a specific subject (unlike Dream which can be changed from day to day).

          The description says that it exists as a semi-permanent realm in the Maya. Does it disappear when the Mage dies? If so, how soon after?

          If the dreamer shapes the whole realm into a building with windows, can s/he look out the window and see parts of the Maya outside the realm?

          If you equip invited guests with tools, can they assist in building out the realm?

          Rotes with 6 dots or less in Spheres:

          Prime 2, Time 2: Conjure the ideal version of yourself in the dream and ask how s/he became so amazing - the path to follow.

          Prime 2, Matter 2, Life 2: Make food eaten in the dream sustain your sleeping body.

          Mind 3, Forces 3: Give the realm a gravitational effect that holds even the most willful dreamers there.

          Spirit 2: Summon a friendly Spirit who can use the realm while you are awake.

          Mind 3, Data 3: Use a mainframe computer to expand the size of the realm. Duplicate parts of the realm in a fixed state so that they can be experienced in a video game.

          Mind 3, Correspondence 3: Chose a recently published work of fiction by a popular author and scan the world for different dreams readers have, inspired by the same book.

          Mind 1, Prime 1, Entropy 1, Time 2: Analyze a dream to determine your relationship to the experience, the Resonance of the dream and how your actions in the dream change your personal Resonance. Use the Demesne to develop strong personal Resonance without acting in the world.

          Entropy 1, Forces 1: Correctly guess how the word "Demesne" is pronounced.

          Thank you for passing time with me in conversation. My Hacks.

                                  A Buddhist Chorister in my game has the Demesne background, and it's become a major aspect of the story over the past several years. It began in the form of a small village with classically Far Eastern inspired features, with her home represented as a small Buddhist temple at the center. Gradually it expanded and began to take on a recognizable form, mirroring New York City (where the chronicle takes place) in its general shape, with large pagodas standing in for skyscrapers and rock gardens and ponds filling in for parks. Manhattan is part town and part mountain, with a winding path that has provided spirit quest landmarks and staging grounds for Works, as well as just a place to get a nice view. The demesne also sometimes incorporates features from the PC's experiences: a school inhabited by dark things where the PC's and allies staged a rescue is in town, while one face of Jupiter is close in the sky.

                                  Over time, dreamers started to manifest. At first, they would pop in and out, but over time many learned to stay, and because it's a dream realm, they learn to lucidly dream, creating their own mini-realities within the greater environment (the PC is able to nix anything she doesn't like, however). One dreamer even thought she was in Heaven, and took it as a sign that the End Times were nearing, necessitating an encounter in the real world to get her to cool her jets. As the demesne has expanded and more dreamers regularly entered, people in NYC have realized that it's a Real Thing; this understanding went international after the last NYC Comic Con brought thousands of new dreamers from all over into the realm. The Technocracy doesn't really understand what is happening here, but they've been on their back feet ever since Iceland seceded from the Consensus (having renewed their ancient ties to the Fair Folk) a few years ago -- a whole 'nother story.

                                  On any given night, dreamers manifest their imaginations, with the most skilled able to mimic their favorite superheroes and villains, play Quidditch or Transformers, and so on. No one can die in the dream realm, so blowing up your friends is great fun. There's probably a Mage LARP happening in there somewhere...

                                  This just scratches the surface of what's been going on in the demesne. The "tree that would be" (the shadow of a Hollow World shrub that manifested in the demesne and just kept growing) and the "soul of Bo" (a rocker whose essence, but not his body, was saved from a Nephandus) have both played large roles in the story, along with many other features. Most significantly, long-time efforts finally achieved a desired result: there now exists a mandala-portal on the mountain that connects the demesne to the Digital Web.

                                  Later in the year I'll begin what is probably the final story of the chronicle, which began about 10 years ago. The Annunaki (my Antediluvians) have risen, the Fair Folk are returning to take what land and minds they can (humans have just learned about the Goblin Market in NYC), and the forces of Darkness -- while partially thwarted -- still hope to corrupt the world entire. If it comes down to it, the demesne may serve as a failsafe, a place to "upload" as much of humanity as possible if the planet cannot be saved.
