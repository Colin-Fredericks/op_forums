    Storyteller Vault Book Idea: Book of Spheres: The Disparate & The Mad

    03-03-2024, 02:04 AM
    Hi y'all. So I haven't posted here in nearly 10 years! How have you been?

    So anyway I had this idea for a Storyteller's Vault book rattling around in my head for a few weeks and I thought I give an outline of it here to kind of workshop it.

    Book of Spheres: The Disparate & The Mad

    The basic idea is there are three Technocracy focused variant spheres already, so this book would explore variant spheres for the other six: three spheres utilized by different crafts, and three spheres for different mad/fallen mages. My current working ideas are:

    Disparate Spheres
    Forces -> Atar: A specialty of the Taftani, it envisions forces not through a modern scientific but rather a product of the holy flames of Ormazd, resulting in some differences in the exact type forces one can weild.

    Mind -> Soul: Used by faith oriented crafts and focuses more on a spiritual interpretation of the mind.

    Entropy -> Telos: The alleged 10th sphere of the Ixoi/Ksirafai/Janissaries can really be viewed as variant of entropy, where the ultimate fate is not the heat death of the universe but rather apocalyptic judgement.

    Mad Spheres
    Life -> Undeath: I actually already made a thread about this way back when. Used by high morbidity mages, liches, and lost books about it possibly tucked away somewhere in a Tremere chantry. Functions like the life sphere, but something is very... off about the supposed living beings made or enhanced by it.

    Time -> Multiverse: Basically the time sphere operating under multiverse logic. Utilized by some Marauders and potentially explains their penchant for finding long lost bygone creatures, as well as tendency to seemingly blink out of existence.

    Matter -> Antimatter: Perhaps the most dangerous variant, allowing user to manipulate antimatter, though there is the slight problem of what happens when matter and antimatter meet... Only the Nephandic Exies are demented enough to explore this variant in any sizable number.

    Any thought on my ideas so far would be highly appreciated.

          Soul seems pretty good as just a a different take on Mind, but you could check out how different religions have conceived of the soul(s) and use that to extrapolate some different effects that might not be possible under Mind. I know some religions envisioned some souls provided the animating spark, while other souls were the conscious and/or unconscious mind. I'd take a look at the ātman from hinduism since that is a major religion with a billion believers, buddhism also uses the same term but has a different take and also different buddhist traditions can be pretty divergent. The ancient egyptian souls would be good since egyptian practices inform a lot of modern occult practices. A lot has been written in WoD about the hun and po from taoism already, but maybe worth skimming some of that. Shinto has the mitama where all spirits (human and animistic) have four parts. I'm less familiar with early christian thinking on the soul, but I know there was lots of hellenistic influence on early christianity so there might be some interesting ideas there that small crafts might preserve that the mainline Chorus has abandoned over time. Actually, on that front gnosticism probably preserves a lot of those ideas so maybe start there, and gnosticism is pretty rife for mystic practices.

          Undeath made me think that from a certain perspective, the body is just made of matter. It's not like chemical and electrical reactions aren't happening once something dies, death is more of a gradient between two arbitrary definitions imposed on the world. You could maybe spin that idea out further to a Marauder take on Matter that encompasses Life as well.

          That also got me thinking there might be meat for a Nephandic take on "time is a flat circle." There is no choice, no free will, you're just a machine with the illusion of consciousness and that illusion thinks it makes choices. That feels more like a Nephanic Mind rote that creates philosophical zombies though, obliterating someone's consciousness and leaving a shell that acts like them but isn't self-aware.

                          I just fully customize my "Spheres", to the point that they don't even "exist" at all in a sense. For I deal with each group having their own "Realms". For instance, my Techies have "Biochemistry" and "Space/Time" rather than Life, Matter, Corr and Time. My Dreamspeakers have only 3 Realms, Body, Mind and Soul (and here the names are a bit misleading; the "Mind" realm deals with the "world of the senses", so roughly speaking it would embark the "traditional spheres" of Time, Corr and Entropy, while "Soul" embarks Mind, Spirit and Prime, or that which creates the "illusion of the self". Body, obviously, deals with the "illusions" of the Material World, therefore it is roughly Life, Matter and Forces). The OoH still have the "vanilla" 9 Spheres, but pretty much that only applies to them.

                          This makes MagiK a LOT less communicable between different magical visions, and a lot more unique.

                          I also use a lot of Minor "Spheres". In my games, you'll hardly ever find a "Forces Sphere Generalist" Hermetic; most of the time, what you can expect is to find a "Pyromancer" (Minor Fire Sphere) or a "Cryomancer" (Minor "Cryomancy" - Ice - Sphere) or a "Telekinesist" (Minor "Telekinesis" - Movement - Sphere) and some such.

                          So, the Mages on my tables will probably be an "Cryomancer, Empath (Minor "Empathy" Sphere, aka the ability to manipulate emotions), "Seer" (Minor "Divination" Sphere, aka the ability to see into the past/future) and "Chlorokinetic" (Minor "Chlorokinesis" Sphere - aka the ability to manipulate plants) rather than generalist Forces, Mind, Time and Life users, so such a Mage is way more restricted in the areas they can act, but can pack a bigger punch in those niche areas.

                              To give a status update as to where I'm at on this, I've actually made some good progress on a rough manuscript. I'm about 4 and 1/2 way through doing the rough write-ups for variant spheres and got an outline for the book.

                              My rough idea is to start with a chapter broadly discussing variant sphere and how the Traditions, Technocracy, and Crafts broadly view things. Then a chapter with some Craft write ups based on groups that likely aren't going to get 20th Anniversary edition write ups at this point and made a good fit for various variant spheres. The 3rd chapter being the write ups for the craft variant spheres and some example rotes. The 4th chapter being the same for the same for the mad/fallen variants. Finally an appendix for any additional odds & ends like merits & flaws and wonders.

                              Some ideas for crafts I like to include:
                              The Ixoi - I mean Telos was one of the variants I settled on...
                              Miarainohmen - Wouldn't use any of the variants I came up with, but would be a good example of mixing technocratic variant spheres on end and mystical practices on the other end.
                              Red Thorn Dedicants - Their connection to Lilith/Bahari would make them a good fit for Undeath.
                              The Hui:xa - The "aliens" that the Thal'un/Star Council were reseaching. They could use Multiverse, and I got the idea of making it nebulous on whether they are truly extraterrestrials, or possibly humans from an alternate earth.

                              I would be interested in hearing people's thoughts on those.

                              Also as an aside, I had the idea of "fringe walker" being an old tradition term used to describe crafts that utilized magick that didn't neatly fit into their sphere model, just as a nod to earlier editions.
