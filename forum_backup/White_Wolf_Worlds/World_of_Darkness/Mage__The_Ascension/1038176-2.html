                                We would have experienced the growth in global GDP from 1960-2015 without the extra 4.5 billion people because they just represent subsistence farmers and the urban self-employed (we could actually maintain our civilization with just 1 billion people, but that is a different argument). While the standard of living would likely be unchanged in the developed world, the standard of living in the developing world would have likely been 2.5 times higher. The average nominal income in the developing world would be $17,500 instead of $5,000, which would be 50% of average developed world nominal incomes instead of 15%. Everyone would be wealthier, the environmental would be cleaner, and the world would be a better place.

                                If we had promoted reproductive freedoms for women along with the Green Revolution, we could have saved the starving Masses as well as allowed women to have the choice of how many children they wanted. While some would have continued to have eight children, most would have stopped at two children. Without reproductive freedoms for women, however, the Green Revolution helped to create the monstrous population growth of humanity.

                                The end result has been the cancerous growth of humanity and the death spiral of the global ecosystem. Humanity will experience a die off before 2100, the only question is whether we will die back to 3 billion or if we will die back to 300 million. I doubt that the Technocrats can do much to stop it, as they have done nothing to promote beneficial technologies like fusion energy or space industry, just technologies that increase the banality and suffering of humanity.
