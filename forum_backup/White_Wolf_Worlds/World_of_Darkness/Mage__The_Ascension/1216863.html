    The Unconventional Cult

    05-08-2018, 08:18 PM
    I like that M20 calls a "sanctum" a "lab", an "avatar" "genius" and so on. Alternative names for Backgrounds are neat and help to reinforce the divisions between different paradigms, while reinforcing satirical elements; everyone is doing the same thing differently.

    However, Cult is presented in a pretty by-the-numbers way. It's just a cult of adherents who believe in your teachings and help with rituals, when the cult is gathered. No surprising examples. Sanctums? They're mystic grottos, dark basements filled with tomes, attics filled with old machines, cutting edge labs, dojos, new age meditation spaces... Cults? They're just... cults.

    Personally I would keep the mechanics, but reframe the concept. All the Cult background does is give you a group of people who support you to a fault (ranging from ordinary and unremarkable Sleepers, whose devotion and limited ability is purchased with this mechanic alone, up to and including more competent cultists who must also be covered by Ally, Retainer or certain Merits), and whose support gives you +1 die per dot in the background to Rituals when they are gathered.

    Take Sarah, an Ecstatic idol. She takes 3 dots in Fame and 5 dots in Cult, along with Resources 4. Now all of her Groupies, Entourage, Publicists, Backup Dancers and Vocalists act as her 5 dots in cult. If she performs a Ritual Effect while on stage, then all of this synergises to make magick simple and spectacular. If she performs a "ritual" while her glorified servants are fetching things for her and making themselves useful, it acts like a traditional cult, even though it wouldn't LOOK that way to an outsider. "Oh she makes a big deal of her morning routine. Everything has to be done right, and in the right order. But hell, you can justify being a diva if you've got talent."

    Now take Eliot, a post-doctorate Etherite. He takes 1 dot in Cult and 5 dots in Laboratory. Now he has the most advanced laboratory most scientists will ever see, and 6 devoted and useful PhD students working in their lab. Easily justified of course; doing advanced research for a respected professor comes with great experience, access to top-notch equipment, and helpful oversight in your dissertation. When the "cult" is in action, Eliot is directing his unenlightened assistants to perform simple procedures which make his acts of magick simpler and more relaxing, adding 1 die to all of the ritual rolls he performs. And if he needs someone to watch the lab while he's away for a day, that's easy enough... just don't expect this PhD student (who is Cult, not Retainer or Ally) to be an expert security guard.

    What's important is that the same fundamental mechanics are being applied differently, with aesthetic and thematic diversity. A good scientist would never call their assistants a cult, much as they wouldn't call what they do "magic". A pop star might have a "Cult Following", but they would never put it that way. "People just love me, and who can blame them?" And there really are limitless variations here. All of a dom/dommes' subs. A hypereconomist's devoted employees, interns and assistants. A ruler's advisers. A religious leader's congregation (unless it is more of a cult, in which case we've come full circle).

    All that really matters is that it's a group of sleepers who support the Mage, believe in the rituals they perform on some level (this does not necessitate a belief in Magick!), and/or are able to practically contribute to the ritual. This leaves room for lots of variation and experimentation.

    With all of that said, I'd like to hear people's experiences with the Background, and ideas on different types of cults for different types of Mages.

                              Originally posted by Ambrosia View Post

                              Well Cult doesn't reduce difficulty, it's actually better than that. Mage is DROWNING in difficulty modifiers. Nothing is going to make you get more successes on a Ritual once you've dropped the Difficulty by 3, except more Dice and Automatic Successes.

                              Cult adding dice is actually the best thing it could do, IMO.

                              Arete 3, Cult 5, a Coincidental Level 3 effect. Say it's a metal concert where the lead singer (the Mage) is conducting every aspect to kill someone with Correspondence 3 Life 3.

                              So it's Difficulty 5, reduced to Difficulty 3 by any of the 100s of modifiers that can do that.

                              Now each roll in the Ritual is 8 dice at Difficulty 3. Forget killing one person; you could kill a group of people at this point.

                              And it only gets more intense if you have Mages working with the Cult. If your Mage helpers are weaker than you then they can add One Automatic Success each. If you're working equally they can add their dice. If a band of four are all Mages, working together on this death curse, then we could have 17 (3 in Arete each, plus 5 from the Cult) dice per roll, at Difficulty 3.

                              And I agree! Cult's mechanical benefits in terms of Dice might cap at 5, but it would be cool if you could push it up to 10 to reflect enormous followings. At 6+, if your Cult has its own community (a compound, a village, a trailer park) then it begins effecting the Reality Zone, generating local reality where your magick is usually coincidental.

                              The point of Cult would be quantity over quality in aid. An Ally is a highly competent person who you know well. A cult of 100 is made up of... well sometimes people whose houses aren't entirely in order. The competent members of the cult (your right hand man, the recruiter) could be reflected by Ally of course. But numbers have their advantages! If you are arrested thanks to NWO manipulation, an enormous protest can form within an hour. If you need a bunch of devoted people to simply attend a place en mass, that's always an option. What sane Night Folk will attack someone who is surrounded by 100s of supporters? And it seems likely that in a massive cult, you would find Sorcerers and newly Awakened Mages who will fit into your Paradigm and make a good Apprentice. Participating in magick as a cult member is as good a reason to Awaken as any.

                              I read a bit about Witch-House in M20 core, but I didn't know she got more space in BoS! I'll have to take a look.

                                                          Originally posted by TheVarulfen View Post
                                                          Well, a technocratic "cult" could be an enterprise or a business (or a mafia organization). A marauder cult... could be anything. There's so many possibilities and the name shouldn't limit your creativity.

                                                          Also, for cult size, sure it could go beyond anything described in the book, but how many people will you be able to gather for the ritual? Where will you perform a ritual with 200 people and who's gonna keep an eye on all those people doing stuff? There's lot of mistakes waiting to happen.
                                                          I feel like any sort of "cult" should have people working with you. So a congregation or a group of assistants makes sense, but framing all the employees of a Venture as a "cult" seems a bit weird. How do you describe their contribution to a Ritual? A hypereconomist would be better off having their PAs, interns and direct subordinates as cult, even if that limits them in terms of numbers.

                                                          As for the logistics of a huge cult, you don't need to use all of them for a ritual. One thing I strongly dislike about the M20 writeup is that it tells you to gather the whole cult to use the Background. Instead it should just give you whatever dice modifier applies to the number present. If you have a 5 dot Cult and only gather 3 dots worth of people, then you get +3 dice. And if you have a 10 dot cult, then you may as well gather 5 dots worth of people for a ritual, as more than 60 or so people draws attention and invites disaster in a single complex ritual. Others can simply observe, sing hymns, watch in awe... if you need them present at all. Remember, cults have hierarchies!

                                                          The "inner circle" of the cult can be your first few dots, and the lower ranks can be the higher dots. A big part of a cult's strength is that people will give years of their life, and huge amounts of money, to climb up the ladder.
