    I want to break down the similarities and differences in Correspondence and Time in a little more depth.

    First there are the situations where they are equivalent. These powers might have different names, but they are similar enough to consider the same thing at the same rank:

    Rank 1

    They each provide basic perceptions of their respective mediums (distances and directions vs an internal clock) and the ability to sense changes and manipulations of that medium (sense spacial anomalies and sense disturbances in the time stream).

    Rank 2

    Each sphere allows a mage to extend his senses across the respective medium (extend senses across space/time) and both can thicken their mediums to resist others senses (these are even named, Thicken Space vs Thicken the Walls of Time)

    Rank 3

    Time and Correspondence have almost nothing in common at this rank. Time can speed or slow time, which is equivalent to two different things, one of them is changing how fast things move through space and Correspondence can do that. There’s also one ability Correspondence has that Time may reasonably have as well. More on that later.

    Rank 4

    Both spheres have the ability to make pockets of their own mediums to trap things in (“isolate [things] into their own tiny realms” vs Time Bubble (named ability). Both can also stop movement within their mediums (ward vs Time Determinism/stopping time).

    Rank 5

    No real similarities.


    Now here are the abilities that Correspondence gets and Time has to wait on.

    Rank 2

    At this rank of Correspondence a mage can extend his reach and his spells through space. Time can allow sensory effects across time but can’t really cast on anything in another time until rank 3.

    Rank 3

    Correspondence gets the ability to teleport across distances at Rank 3, but Time can’t move through time until Rank 5.

    Rank 4

    Correspondence can open up gates between two spaces. Time is never stated to get this ability, though it can be safely assumed to be possible at Time 5. Likewise, Time never gets the stated ability to co-locate in time, but it can be assumed possible at Time 5.



    And the ones Time gets that Correspondence has to wait on:


    Rank 3

    At Rank 3, Time can speed up or slow down time, this is also equivalent to stretching out or contracting space, and Correspondence can’t do that until rank 5. Time can also reverse time, and reversing directions is also a Correspondence 5 thing.


    Finally, here are the ones that one sphere has and the other may reasonably have if we can figure out what it would do:

    Rank 1

    Correspondence can sense things within the vicinity. Time is not stated to see the outlines of things that just happened or are just about to happen, but it isn’t unreasonable.

    Rank 2

    Thicken space makes scrying and presumably reaching through space more difficult, but Thicken the Walls of Time makes all Time effects more difficult. Thicken Space probably has the same reach.

    Likewise, Thicken Space was intended to allow minor wards so Thicken the Walls of Time can probably do minor time wards, whatever those are.

    Rank 3

    Edit: At Rank 3, Correspondence gets Co-Local Perceptions. Presumably, Co-Temporal Perceptions can also happen at Time 3. Given that both can see across their respective mediums at Rank 2, this would be reasonable.

    Rank 4

    Correspondence can do Wards, Time probably can do the same but for time. Possibly that is what stopping time is.

    Rank 5

    Correspondence can superimpose two locations on top of each other. Presumably time can do this with two times.

                                Part of it is that Time really doesn't have a parallel to the Correspondence Range Chart; and for good reason, because the latter is a bit of a misnomer: what the Correspondence Range Chart actually measures is the strength of the connection or familiarity. By contrast, the Time Sphere operates in terms of “how many years can I transcend?” This makes it more akin to the Void Correspondence chart found in the Void Engineers Convention Book, p.83, where “Ranges” phrased in terms of “line of sight”, “very familiar”, “familiar”, “visited once”, and “described location” get replaced by Literal Distances (1 km, Earth's horizon, low Earth orbit, high Earth orbit, near translunar space, etc.). Conceptually, “how far away is it?” is very different from “how intimately aware of it am I?”, and Time operates on the former basis.

                                            Mind in M20


                                            311 – Mind 3 to pull a sleeping target into a Demense. Mind 4 to pull one who is awake.

                                            408 – Mind does bashing damage and is soaked with willpower, not stamina.

                                            417 – If a mage has Mind 1 and an active sensory effect in Corr, Mind, or Time can ignore multiple opponents penalty.

                                            418 – For some reason, Mind attacks on umbral entities tend to affect the mage. This is contradicted later.

                                            422 – A dot in Mind or Correspondence and any perceptual effect lets a mage read an opponent from a distance. I really don’t like these natural abilities inherent in knowing a sphere.

                                            422- The Mind 2 addition to Thunder Punch sends a mental impulse to surrender.

                                            424 – Mind reading cannot bypass the advantage of Drunken Style Martial arts on the grounds that the martial artist doesn’t know what he’s going to do next. This says more about the assumptions the author is making than Mind.

                                            438 – Mind is pretty much the only sphere that can’t be used in the optional dodging the blast rule.

                                            442 – Mind can make psychotropics more or less effective. The way this is written it sounds like a mage can use Mind on a substance to make it affect the mind more. This doesn’t work as the substance has no mind. However, if the intention is the mage can use Mind on the person who takes the substance, that does work. Also, if the mage locks a Mind effect on the substance so it affects whoever takes it, that also works though likely needs Time 4.

                                            442 – A mage can use Mind to counteract a mind altering toxin. This does work. It is also said to need Int+Pharm to make sure he knows what he’s doing. This is teetering on the edge of reasonableness and I don’t think I’d make it a hard roll.

                                            476 – Astral Travel, via projecting consciousness takes Mind 4 for the self and Mind 5 for others.

                                            477 – Astral travelers do not bring objects with them without a Prime 2 ritual that requires 5 successes and a willpower point. This is incorrect, There's already a Prime effect, Consecration, that accomplishes something like this and it is Rank 1 and there’s no reason to consider it a Mighty Feat.

                                            477 – The Astral Sojourn projects the astral form in a more robust body made of primal energy and ephemera. This is Mind 4 or 5/Spirit 3/Prime 2. Prime 2 is the correct level for Body of Light so this works.

                                            477 – It takes Mind 5 and a direct psychic attack aimed at disconnecting the target to break the silver cord.

                                            477 – Mind 2/Prime 2 to set up an Astral Beacon to attract a lost astral traveler back to their body. Mind should be capable of doing this alone.

                                            477 – Mind 4 for a drifting mage to possess a different body. But it takes Spirit 2 to see potential bodies across the gauntlet… which isn’t really thematic to astral travel. Honestly this is a really big hole in astral travel as if a mage can cross into the High Umbra with Mind alone, they should be able to see into it or from it with Mind alone.

                                            480 – Entropy 5/Mind 5 for someone on the Agama Sojourn to move from the Underworld to the High Umbra. Without an explanation for why this suddenly requires both higher levels of Entropy than the Agama Sojourn and higher levels of Mind than just going to the High Umbra, this is wrong.

                                            484 – Mind works normally or at -1 difficulty in the Otherworlds.

                                            485 – Mind works normally but with a +2 difficulty in the Underworld.

                                            504 – Restatement that Mind attacks do bashing damage. I only just now realized that the Magical Damage chart doesn’t mention that Mind attacks are soaked with Willpower.

                                            505 – Mental illusions are Mind 2+. Assuming the illusions are small enough, short enough, or purely impressions or emotions, they should be possible at 2.

                                            506 – Immersive illusions with “holodeck” levels of apparent reality are Forces 4+/Prime 4+ and can probably be done with Forces 2 at smaller levels. Importantly, Mind is not listed here. Remember this for later.

                                            505 – Adding Mind 3/Life 3 makes the damage from mental illusions aggravated.

                                            508 – Life 3/Mind 3 to harm fae. No reason is given for why this needs Mind 3 rather than 2 like it is for the other supernaturals.

                                            508 – Mind 2 to suggest sleep, Mind 4 to command it. Mind 4/Life 3 is given as a possible but unnecessary option.

                                            508 – Mental illusions that cause bashing damage are Mind 3 or Mind 3/Life 3 for agg damage. The latter should also be capable of lethal.

                                            508 - Immersive Illusions are now listed as Forces 4+/Mind 4/Prime 4+. Prime is still wrong. Mind is also wrong since it is clear Forces doesn’t need the help. Of course this all depends on what Immersive Illusions means, but since it was already stated that this is supposed to be Holodeck like, Mind doesn’t have a place here.

                                            508 – Mind 4/Spirit 4 to command spirits. Spirit 4 can do this alone. Mind is either capable of doing this on its own, completely incapable of doing this, or can do this but needs the help of Spirit, depending on how seriously we take the statement on page 418. Even if we take that last option, though, it is a joke to require Spirit to be so high that it could do the task on its own.

                                            509 – Astral Projection is Mind 4+ like before.

                                            509 – Astral Body of Light is Mind 4+/Spirit 3/Prime 2 like before.

                                            509 – Clairvoyance is Corr 2 or Mind 3/Corr 2. The former is correct for distant viewing, the latter is correct for distant mind reading. However, if the intention is that psychics need to add Mind 3 for distant viewing just because they are psychic (which M20 does quite a bit) that is wrong.

                                            509 – Conceal Aura is Mind 1. Since Auras tend to change appearance with emotion, it makes sense this would be possible at the same level as conceal thoughts. I’m honestly not sure why Mind gets so many little perks at Rank 1

                                            509 – Conceal Avatar at Spirit 2/Mind 1. I brushed this off earlier as Avatar’s being strange, but really, I don’t have an explanation for why this needs Spirit 2. Mind 1 works, at least, as it matches the level of concealing thoughts.

                                            509 – Conceal Thoughts is Mind 1.

                                            509 – The third appearance of Mental Illusions at Mind 2+

                                            509 -- Enter a dream is Mind 3.

                                            509 – Influence mood is Mind 2 which tracks.

                                            509 – Influence subconscious mind is Mind 3.

                                            509 – Mind control is Mind 4.

                                            509 – Prophecy/Hindsight is Time 2 or Time 2/Mind 2. Psychics don’t need to add Mind just because they are psychic. Even if I give this the benefit of the doubt and say that it is meant to read the mind of someone in the past or future, that takes Time 3.

                                            509 – Psychic Pain Blast is Mind 3 or Mind 3/Life 3 for agg damage.

                                            509 – Scramble thoughts is Mind 3 which is appropriate if we interpret that as an attack, though I imagine something very similar can be accomplished by sending a lot of mental impulses with Mind 2.

                                            509 – See Auras is Mind 1 or Spirit 1.

                                            509 – See Avatars is Mind 3/Prime 2/Spirit 1. Again, avatars may be strange, but there’s no reason why it would take Mind 3 to see one or why seeing one requires Prime and hiding one does not.

                                            509 – See through another’s eyes is Mind 3.

                                            509 – See through mental illusions is Mind 4… why? You can detect them at Mind 1 and shield your mind at Mind 2.

                                            509 – Share perceptions is Mind 2 + the intended perception effect combo. I initially considered this fine, but I'm less certain. 1) I don't see a good reason why those perception effects can't be cast on someone without Mind. 2) Sharing your actual perceptions should be Mind 3.

                                            509 – Shield Mind is Mind 1 or 2 for others.

                                            509 – Telepathy is Mind 3.

                                            509 – Translating Languages is Mind 3, or Mind 3/Forces 2 to translate it so others can understand it too. Mind 3 should be enough to make it so that others think they’ve seen or heard it translated. The Forces variant, though is the one to manipulate the light or sound such that they actually do see or hear it in a different language. We do run into a conundrum here, though... where is the mage pulling the translation from? The collective unconscious? Or does someone nearby need to actually speak/read the language?

                                            509 – Notably Telekinesis is correctly stated as Forces 2+.

                                            511 – Mind 2 can influence someone’s mood.

                                            511- Manipulating biochemistry to influence mood is Life 3/Mind 2. This is wrong, Life 3 can do this on its own.

                                            513 – Correspondence 4 needs the help of other spheres like Mind to isolate things or people in a pocket realm. This is incorrect, by default spells can be cast on areas so the pocket realm can just isolate anything it is cast on, it doesn’t need tailoring. However, if the mage wants to cast the effect on an area but only isolate some things in that area into the pocket dimension, then additional spheres would be necessary. Isolating only those people with bad intent would be Mind.

                                            513 – Correspondence 4 needs the help of other spheres like Mind ward against certain things or people. This is fine. Without help, the ward would just block everything. The reference to HDYDT is as wrong as HDYDT's Ward rules.

                                            513 – It takes Mind 1 to be able to process the input from Co-Location.

                                            516 – Mind 3/Forces 3 to manifest thoughts into energies. It really isn’t clear what this means, but if it means take the idea of a fireball and turn it into a fireball (without Prime) or take a dream or a memory and convert it into a fireball (again without Prime) or to turn a point of Willpower into a fireball (again, without Prime) it works.

                                            519 – Matter 5/Mind 3 to melt steel with a thought. Matter 3 is the rank for changing the state of matter, but more importantly, Mind isn’t doing anything here.

                                            519 – Special Mind Rules. The difficulty for Mind effects against a single target is their Willpower, unless it is an extreme effect which has a difficulty of Willpower +3. Mind effects against multiple targets is calculated like normal.

                                            520 – One of the infamously badly written sentences. “Combined with Correspondence, Forces, Matter, or Life he can employ telekinesis (doesn’t need Mind), pyrokinetics (doesn’t need Mind), and psychophysical assault (possibly legit), influencing objects, elements, or people with the power of his mind (unhelpfully vague, and only influencing people would use Mind and then only sometimes).

                                            520 – Mind 5/Life 4 to swap someone else's mind. I’m not set on if Mind 5 needs Life to swap minds, but if it does it would be a grafting effect and would require Life 5 for others (complex) and Life 3 for others (simple). Life 4 would only apply to the self, and the mage would still need the requisite level of Life for the other target.

                                            521 – Projecting thoughts across the gauntlet is said to be Mind 4 or 5/Spirit 2. The Spirit level is correct, but projecting thoughts is a Mind 3 effect.

                                            522 – Spirit 3/Mind 2 to read resonance, synergy, and spiritual energies. Spirit 3 should be able to do the last one on its own, and Mind might be able to as well. I’m not convinced this is the combo for the other two.

                                            523 – Time 3/Life 3/Mind 1 to move back in time while recalling events. 1) Time 3 doesn’t move anyone back in time, it rewinds time. 2) The previous paragraph already said Time 3 lets a mage pull themselves outside the flow of time during the rewind so there are no special requirements for memory. 3) Life 2 or Mind 1 alone could accomplish memory preservation.

                                            525 – Life and Mind and Data allow for the mage to manifest multiple versions of himself. This is the same effect as correspondence so presumably the same Life and Mind requirements for independent thought and motion. Unfortunately this also includes Primal Force and that shouldn’t be necessary.

                                            533 – Axis of Coincidence Sidebar – Correspondence 3 to find a taxi (too high), Mind 2 to nudge the driver over and Entropy 2 to increase the odds of finding one.

                                            533 – Axis of Coincidence Sidebar – Matter 4/ Forces 3/ Life 5/Mind 5/Prime 2 to create a taxi and driver (presumably a soulless one as Spirit isn’t involved). Forces is unnecessary unless it is vital that the car already be running.

                                            543 – A new method of making Mind attacks is mentioned here: adding Prime 2 and a point of quintessence.

                                            557 – Mind 4/Prime 4 to soothe Quiet by driving out the paradox in the target’s mind.

                                            559 – Mind 3 to dreamwalk into a Quiet mindscape, Mind 4/5 to astrally project into one.

                                            563 – Correspondence 2/Matter 2/Mind 2 to set up a ward around a location that blocks scrying, sends out a go away impulse, and warns the mage if anyone breaches the barrier. Correspondence 2 tracks with setting the ward, the impulse and making the warning long distance. Wards are not a stated power of Corr 2 but apparently that was part of what thickening space was supposed to mean. Mind also tracks with both uses in the spell. Matter has a use, but not a necessary one. It’s clearly being used as a locking sphere. However, since the spell is explicitly on a location, it doesn’t need to move with any object. That said, there are other benefits to locking, so this isn’t completely superfluous.

                                            563 – Mind 2/Correspondence 2/Entropy 1 for an effect that gets opponents to reveal things they least want to reveal. Correspondence is for distance, Entropy to find the weak points, Mind 2 for an impulse to slip up. I’m not convinced Entropy is vital, but as a player action it is reasonable, I think

                                            601 – Little Black Card – Corr 2/Forces 2/Mind 2 – The mage still has to hack like normal, so Mind isn’t translating code or altering his mental skills. It isn’t doing anything as far as I can tell.

                                            601 – Hot-Shotting – The optional Mind 2 effect here adds a psychological intimidation threat.

                                            602 – Right Click – Mind 3/Data 3 for a readout of data pulled from the internet and nearby surface thoughts. Because this explicitly reads surface thoughts, it should only require Mind 2.

                                            603 – Hypernarrative – Mind 4. The Mind 4 effect influences the target to follow a the script or expectations of a popular meme or trope.

                                            604 – Secure the Scene – Mind 2 or 3/Forces 1/Matter 1. Mind is discouraging trespassers. Mind 2 suggests avoidance, Mind 3 drives them away. I didn’t catch this before but Mind 3 doesn’t control people, so this effect is actually Mind 4. This would have been a great place to show how to use a ward too.

                                            606 – Social Conditioning – Initial Processing – Mind 3/Prime 2. Since this could be considered, broadly, as a willpower reducing attack, Mind 3 is the appropriate level. Prime 2 does not make sense here, there is no listed power of Prime 2 that is doing anything.

                                            606 – Reinforcing the Programming – Often uses Mind 4 instead of Mind 3. That’s good as controlling a mind actually is Mind 4. The wording does not mention Prime, but implies it is still there since Mind 4 is only replacing the Mind 3 part. Prime is still nonsense.

                                            607 – Breaking Conditioning – Willpower or Mind 3 effect to break a level of conditioning. This is, strictly speaking, the wrong level. This is a control mind effect which is Mind 4. However, spells on the self are often one rank easier than usual, and this is true in Mind (projection is Mind 4 for the self and Mind 5 for others). This is not true as written, but it is quite plausible.

                                            608 – Chain of Whispers – Mind 3/Correspondence 2 – Send a message by rumor. Mind is a compulsion to share the information, Correspondence pulls it from person to person. I would have thought speaking the information, sharing it online, or in any other way sharing it is what moves it from person to person. This isn’t an ability of Correspondence. Also, I think I missed this the last time, but compulsions are Mind 4 not Mind 3, though something close probably could be done at 3.

                                            608 – Psychic Interface – Mind 3 or 4/Data 2/Forces 2 to mentally interface with computers without a physical link. I think I’ve said before that Correspondence could probably substitute for Data when Forces is involved though it would have to be Forces 3, and Data might not need Forces. The Mind ranks are at least correct, Mind 3 for a psychic interface and Mind 4 for self-projecting into the Digital Web.

                                            612 – Jodi’s Riot Sidebar – Mind is used to seduce and Life and Mind effects are used to push people over the edge or tempt them.

                                            626 – Hacking a HIT Mark is Matter 3/Mind 3/Forces 2. I still don’t know what Matter is doing here but Mind and Forces are at least plausible.

                                            653 – Trushades use Mind 1 to see auras.

                                            654 – Bafflejackets use a Mind 2 effect to blur perceptions. That’s reasonable for influence mind.

                                            655 – Falconi Elite Business Attire uses Mind 2 for Empathic Bonds.

                                            656 – VDAs use Mind 1 for multitasking.

                                            661 – DDGR Cards use Data/Forces/Mind Effects to pull up information form an ATM. If we presume that Mind can just translate information without needing a source for how, this is possible.

                                            662 – Madness Grenade – Entropy 5/Mind 4/Matter 3. Given all the effects, I think this is a reasonable spread, though some of the effects do seem to overlap quite a lot.
