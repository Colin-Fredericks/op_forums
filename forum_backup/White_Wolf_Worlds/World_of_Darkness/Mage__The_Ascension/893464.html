    1001 interesting character ideas

    05-18-2016, 02:16 PM
    I thought it would be fun to do one for Mage (all factions and the Sorcerers) as well. Please, no more than two at a time.

    1) A Cultist has discovered a way to help people who've lost certain senses (taste, touch, etc) feel again through him. Example: A person who lose their sense of taste can enjoy a candy bar with the Cultist eating it and then mentally passing his feelings to them via the Mind sphere. Interestingly, this talent has gotten the attention of members of the local Vampire community who would like the "Pleasure Dealer" to help them feel again.

    2) An Ineration X member who specializes in psychology (especially in realm of emotions) has created technology to help sense hidden emotions, body language and psychological issues to help in negotiations, interrogations and treating the mentally ill. But her desire to "help everybody" is starting to lead her into Marauder territory (though she can still be talked out it).

      3) A pair of Iteration-X members, together with a Tradition Cabal, have found way(s) to help blind people by substituting their lack of sight with a combination of hearing and touch. All would be well, except that the Iteration-X members get an anonymous tip (through an SMS) that they will soon face charges of making deals with the Nephandi.


      4) A Frozen Widderslainte has joined the Technocracy! Having made the choice to be a member of the Technocracy rather than the Nephandi she seems to a model young member of NWO. A Tradition Cabal arrives in the city to judge her - through Chakravanti divinations, Chorister ordeals, Verbenae gaeses... This is excellent time for the young NWO member to use her training to either help the Tradition members abandon their views, or to neutralize them; and perhaps to remove them from the city.

              6) A young Magic Man of the Syndicate has sworn to himself that he will establish a legendary Prime Venture as a video games developer. Due to an amazing stroke of luck, he managed to contact and secure funds for a daring group of individuals. A Verbena, a Virtual Adept, a Son of the Ether, a Cultist of Ecstasy and a member of the Order of Hermes.

                              10) A blank slate artificial person of obviously non-Technocratic origins. Internally made up of what looks like some kind of joint venture between an Etherite and a Hermetic, displaying machinery, strings of runes, and what might be a phylactory at the heart of it all.                                       14) A trickster Marauder who believes herself to be the living incarnation of Coyote. One of her latest tricks is drugging random Sleepers and then inking them with these beautifully done tattoos. (Example: a ten-year-old boy now has a pin-up girl version of Lee Ann Milner on his lower right arm, a late middle aged CEO has tribal tattoos on his face, a 30-something Jewish housewife who has cherry blossoms swirling around the Akashic Brotherhood logo on her throat, an elderly church pianist with Technocracy symbols on her hands in a rainbow of colors, etc.) The only real magic any of the Mages, Technocrats and Crafts can see is that the tattoos will never fade and all the targets feel compelled to keep them. So while these groups are worrying, scrambling and some even blaming each other as if this will lead to some hidden plan, all our trickster is doing is leaning back, laughing at them all.

                                                          17) A Hermetic of House Thig/Verditus who was the product of a secret government cloning experiment that went nowhere (feel free to use this to justify any sort of Merit or Flaw you wish, as a result of the selective breeding). When the project shut down, the Hermetic and twelve of his surviving "siblings" were placed in homes across the country. After being picked up and trained by the Order of Hermes, the new Hermetic decided to take to the road, looking for his fellow clones. He drives a magically enhanced sports car, with a suped up engine and a Trunk of Holding, and the ability to use Correspondence to cut down on travel time. Because all his fellow clones share the same genetic lineage, they share a Sympathetic connection, through which he can track them all down. A compass sits on his dashboard, dowsing towards the closest "sibling". Our Hermetic wanderer won't rest until he's met and dealt with his "family"...one way or another. Not all of them look forward to a family reunion...and not all of them are nice.
