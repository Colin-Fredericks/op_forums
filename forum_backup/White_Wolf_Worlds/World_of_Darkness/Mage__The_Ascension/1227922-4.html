    I like the idea of Paradox Dice, but it would have to take some work to ensure that any changes were properly integrated into the Mage gameplay. We don't want a setting being shoe-horned into an existing type of system - we want a system to emulate the concepts of the setting. Then again, I do sometimes feel that the Quintessence/Paradox wheel that is currently used is a bit vague, so a more concrete set of rules that make Paradox effects more dramatic and imaginative would be great.

    With regards to the Spheres, I feel they could definitily be reworked in some cases, even changed. However, my personal drive would be to accentuate the freeform nature rather than stipulate more rules and definition. It was this that ultimately made me feel that Mage: The Awakening was too rule heavy in its magic. I'd actually try to encourage players to move away from Rote casting, towards a more broad application of Spheres and then make the various effects more intuitive to remember in a game concept (less rule book referencing). This may be tricky of course!

        I like the idea of Paradox Dice as well. Though with what I have in mind, they'd not replace paradox points. They handle backlashes.

        A way I can see it working, off the top of my head, is:

        * Paradox accumulation (1 for vulgar without witnesses, more for botches, etc.) works as usual M20 rules.
        * After(!) every time you perform a Vulgar magic roll (or botch on coincidental), one of the Arete die gets replaced by a Paradox die for further vulgar(!) rolls. This accumulates.
        * On a '0' on a paradox die, you get a backlash, and the paradox dice get replaced with normal dice again.

        That way you can have streaks of using vulgar magic without any backlash happening - but paradox accumulates - or you can have a series of quick discharges with low paradox amounts. Up to Lady Luck.
        Either way, after a backlash the next arete roll won't have any paradox dice. It's up to the mage to risk gaining some again while they possibly have a pile of paradox left.

        As an optional system, it also takes the 'responsibility' of deciding on a blacklash or no-backlash away from the ST. Food for thought.

                    Arete probably needs changing to implement a Paradox Dice system. I'm not entirely OK, conceptually, with having more chances to Backlash the bigger it's your Arete pool.

                    Surely it can be justified but the old system never intended your chances of getting Paradox to be directly proportional to your Enligthenment (that having a higher dicepool increased the chances of Botching was solved in Revised - no need to bring that back.)

                    There could be some way to balance this:

                    Perhaps dusting the old rule that allowed you to roll less dice than you need to cast an effect: Say, if you have Arete 6 and 4 Paradox Dice, you can choose to forfeit 4 dice to null your Backlash chances (until you get more 'dox dice) - of course, then you're casting with a meager Arete 2.

                    Or perhaps something nasty happens when your 'dox dice fill your entire Arete poll - and thus having a bigger pool (with a bigger chance to Backlash) has it's advantage.

                            So for example, Sarah is running with 10 points of Paradox, and has 3 paradox dice and 5 arete. When she tries to turn a person who is attacking her into autumn leaves so they can blow away in the wind, with a simple look and the word "die", she rolls her 5 dice. 2 normal dice and 3 paradox dice.

                            She succeeds, getting 4 successes (Willpower + 4 successes and a botch die). The botch die is a paradox die, so she then suffers a backlash. From here it's the same as M20.
