    Originally posted by monteparnas View Post
    Considering all the shit that Reality Zones and Paradigm Manipulation can do on Earth itself, with all of humanity living here, why Mars wouldn't be more flexible for humans living there?
    Because Earthly Foundations (M20 pp.612–613) are specifically not about what people believe, and in fact frequently stand in spite of what people believe. Granted, the text as written assumes that we're talking about Earth; but it's one small step to extend the same logic to outer space and other planets: the basic way that physical reality works isn't set by human belief. And in an environment where exposure to human beliefs is almost nil, this baseline state of reality dominates. (Victorian Mage even goes so far as to say that if a region is depopulated, it will gradually revert to its Earthly Foundations.)

    And just like the Earthly Foundations on Earth establish that some things are and remain Vulgar even when the locals believe otherwise, so too with their Martian counterparts. The issue isn't about having to overcome human beliefs; it's about having to overcome the fundamental laws of reality. Gravity isn't as strong on Mars as it is on Earth; but water still flows downhill, not uphill. And that remains true even if you indoctrinated your colonists into believing otherwise. Likewise with other aspects of the environment, such as how cold it is and the lack of breathable air. The existence of these “Martian Foundations” is the whole reason why the Technocracy needs to consider Terraforming the planet at all, instead of “merely” Terranorming the colonists into believing that the Martian environment is hospitable.

    Remember that, at least as of Technocracy Reloaded, the Union can and does involve itself in the game of reality alteration; so Terraforming a patch of Mars can't be as easy as convincing your colonists that it has already been Terraformed. If such a thing was possible, the NWO at least would be tempted to do it: altering reality by altering peoples' minds is what they do. Instead, any such efforts will run counter to the Earthly Foundations and will be Vulgar.

    My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

                    My main point is the variety of things happening. Breathable air was an example that can be extreme, but the main point is the same: Mars' environment wouldn't be uniform, and the presence of Mage lead colonies would alter things significantly.

                    It's environment would be inhospitable, but not as inflexible to magic as Earth's. Without humans it is probable that many rules are naturally less strict, as Ramnesis exemplified. Building colonies would probably be feasible to every Tradition according to its own paradigm, even if not easy or completely safe.

                    And with the practical limit on size of such colonies, it would be probable that most of them become Chantries. The environment around may suffer some changes, not as intense as my previous hyperbole, but still enough to make contact between colonies weird.

                    That's to say nothing about Mars itself. Given that even on Earth we have bygones and weird stuff.

                    By the way, the idea that breathable air, or any other particular condition is supported by Foundations is an assumption. Even the Earthly Foundations have quite some flexibility, and Mars isn't devoid of atmosphere, despite having less than 1% of our surface pressure, nor a completely alien composition. It lacks enough oxygen, but isn't directly poisonous (the trace amounts of carbon monoxide aren't enough to be a danger, the carbon dioxide can be used for oxygen production). So despite it being completely unbreathable for real, it's hard to say how much this would be a Foundation in Mage. Especially considering that it wouldn't make Mars a lot less dangerous as an alien planet-size wild desert.

                        If you want the challenges that the technocracy faces in terraforming Mars to resemble the real world challenges in terraforming mars, they need to be Earthly Foundations.
