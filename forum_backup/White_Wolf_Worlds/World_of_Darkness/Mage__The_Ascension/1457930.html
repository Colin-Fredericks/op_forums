                      Two important questions to begin with regarding Paradigm, in my opinion: What is Magic? And where does it come from? Sometimes those two combine into one question.

                      An important thing to consider is whether or not, from the perspective of the Paradigm (regardless of actual mechanics about the Avatar, etc.), Magic is Internal or External, or the extent that it is either. Does the power come from within the Mage, or is it the power of spirits, gods, etc., that the Mage is granted, wields, controls through science, etc.? Is it some combination, with an Internal component required to use the External power?

                      I tend to lean towards chaos magic myself these days, an individualistic and pragmatic approach fit for a wishy-washy skeptic.

                                  It sounds like a blend between External, the majority of the Collective Unconscious being made up of other minds, but also Internal, to the extent that your mind is part of the Collective Unconscious. My personal Chaos Magic Paradigm also sees the mind as a significant component of Magic.

                                  I'm not an expert, but Psionics are most often associated with more scientific Paradigms IIRC. They're rare among the Technocracy, and heavily monitored. I could definitely see a psychic theorist among the Etherites as well. Don't know as much about the Virtual Adepts, but I don't know if it meshes as well with their perspective.

                                  Depending on what Edition you're looking at, you're going to need to consider that you will often be required, by game rules, to use Instruments other than pure thought. Or if you really want to go that route, considering ways to balance it against other characters with a less flexible Paradigm.

                                  P.S. It's actually not a super weird Paradigm, and I can even probably think of a way to describe it in the terms of Mage mechanics. Mage already includes the concept of the Consensus, that what is possible, impossible, and supernatural is at least significantly influenced by the collective beliefs of humanity. Your Paradigm could possibly be described as the idea that the Consensus is the Collective Unconsciousness that the Psion manipulates, and Magic is essentially the consequence of temporary alterations of the local Consensus aimed towards a particular result.

                                                      My last two characters were both Etherites, but each with a little spin.

                                                      The first was a ghost hunter. He ran with a crew of sleepers on ghost hunting trips, exploring abandoned buildings, uploading videos to youtube, etc. He designed, or at least modified, all of their equipment, and was generally the man behind the camera. Some of his devices were pretty normal- specially designed microphones, cameras, night vision goggles, etc which he modified to tap into "etheric frequencies." He also borrowed a lot from the trappings of spiritualism and mediumship and the like, and tried to create devices to emulate these practices. For instance, instead of holding a proper seance, he might surround a crystal ball with etheric resonators to replicate what (he thinks) is going on when a seance is held. Or he might hook a spirit board up to a machine with a keyboard and printer... to help facilitate communication, you understand.

                                                      The second was for a one-shot set in Berlin shortly before the fall of the wall. He was a psychologist who also helped people cross over with his credentials as a practicing psychiatrist as well as his talent for hypnosis. This Etherite was much less focused on gadgetry and more about connecting his consciousness with the ether. He created custom drugs, used meditation, psychotherapy, psychoanalysis, etc. He believed he could harmonize his mind with others by getting them to think the same thing he was thinking at the same time, creating "etheric synchronicity" allowing him to communicate telepathically. He would do this by showing his allies Zener cards and trying to guess what card they were seeing. He didn't announce his guess or anything, in fact, he usually just assumed he was right and put the card back in the deck. But one of my favorite effects he tried (and failed) was a scenario where they were meeting someone and he wanted to read his mind on the sly. He couldn't use his usual instruments for mind reading (electrodes placed on the forehead, or synchronicity) so he got creative. They were enjoying cigars and bourbon, so he dropped one of his pills into his own drink, dipped the cigar in the bourbon (like you do) and blew a puff of smoke at his host. Unfortunately the effect failed and worse, he recognized the Effect for what it was, and got sent packing.

                                                      I should mention both characters at one time or another made use of a colander placed on the head connected with wires and blinking lights to "enhance" their psychic powers.

                                                          Players and STs face conflicting requirements:
