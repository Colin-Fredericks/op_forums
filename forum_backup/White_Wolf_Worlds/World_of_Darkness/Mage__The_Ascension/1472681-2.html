    Originally posted by Dataweaver View Post
    I have mixed views on this. Not on what I want to see, mind you; I'm entirely with you on that. No; the past where I'm conflicted it's that while the latest interview does provide some reason for hope, the first interview had the guy in charge saying that one of his bits of advice for the authors was “blame the Hermetics” when problematic material came up. I'd much rather they don't try to dump everything on the Hermetics, for the same reason I prefer that the Technocracy owns its own failings and doesn't foist then off on the Nephandi. In both cases, I'm not a big fan of scapegoating. Own up to your own problems, and then move forward.
    That was exactly the thing that triggered this post. Fine, the Hermetics sucked in the past. But, they must also have done some good and right things, or why didn't the other Traditions hunt them all down and Gilgul them all? Why aren't they all Nephandi or Marauder?

    My theory is that the Order of Hermes still has the stench of the Tremere on them from White Wolf's Vampire line. And the Tremere were supposed to be untrustworthy assholes. But the Order of Hermes is not the Tremere, and should have done enough good things to balance out the bad stuff at the very least to still actually be part of the Council of Nine.

    Let all the Traditions own their own shit, and let's end the practice of sticking an entire Tradition with the Hat of Assery. That's what the Nephandi are for. Occasionally, the Marauders as well. If the Traditions fuck up, let it be bad/wrong individuals who need to get corrected by good/right individuals, preferably the player-characters.
