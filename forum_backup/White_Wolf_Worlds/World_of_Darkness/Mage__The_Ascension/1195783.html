    About Raising Attributes and Abilities: Life, Mind and Merits?

    03-15-2018, 01:36 PM
    So this is going to be a rather broad topic but I have a lot of questions and I think I'd rather not spam the forums to get them all out there and get answers. So my questions are this: 1. How in the nine-fucking-hells does Pattern Bleeding work? I've seriously been looking all over, and while How Do You Do That and M20 core offered some help, it was not nearly enough to alleviate my confusion. So here's what I think I know. if you raise an Attribute or drastically modify yourself or others (drastic being subject to ST discretion) permanently, then you suffer the equivalent of pattern bleeding or something like it, wherein you take 1 lethal damage per day per effect until you pay EXP or gain Permanent Paradox. But what about raising them for, say, a month, or 6 months? Do you only suffer pattern bleeding from Attribute increases if they exceed your natural maximum or is it any time you increase it for a length of time? The game has been quite lacking on details for less than permanent modifications that stay within the range of human norms.

    Second question, more related to permanent increases: Can Mind Magick increase certain mental attributes in a similar fashion to Life Magick, and if so would it be reasonable to apply the half-cost to raise them that Life has for it's attribute increases? Do you think it might be reasonable to allow Eidetic Memory to apply an EXP break on certain abilities too, the more cerebral ones being the obvious choice for said select Abilities, like Occult or Academics? It seems that while Eidetic Memory might not make it free to raise such things, It seems to me that it sure as fuck would make it require less effort by far. It would reduce the training time as well, but I think that just reducing the training time fails to really encapsulate the effect. I'm sure Mind effects to replicate the merit could be argued to do similar.

    Anyway, thoughts, input and clarifications greatly appreciated, and if I've asked a question the book obviously answers I do apologize, and would be grateful if it could be pointed out to me.

      Here's how I run pattern bleed.

      If you raise any physical attributes at all with Life magic, you are subject to pattern bleed. This is also true if you give yourself claws, wings, tough skin, or whatever other non normal perks. It doesn't matter if it's vulgar or coincidental, any Life based augmentation results in pattern bleed.

      If the Life magic is vulgar, then you get pattern bleed AND permanent paradox for the duration of the effect. Permanent paradox here does not mean paradox that never goes away, but rather paradox that cannot be lost from a backlash. If you have 5 points of permanent paradox, then those points stay with you even after the backlash might have resolved them. (e.g. I got 5 points of perm paradox, I accumulate a total of 11 points, ST rolls 11 dice and gets 9 successes, I get a 9 point paradox backlash. Normally I would rub off 9 points of paradox from the sheet, leaving me with just 2, but since 5 of those points are permanent, I can only rub off down to 5).

      Pattern bleed gives you a points of lethal damage per degree of change. The way I run it is that for every extra point you gain in an attribute, that's an extra point of lethal damage you receive each day. So if you're going from strength 4 to 5 strength, that's only 1 lethal point per day. But if you're going from 1 strength to 5 strength, then that's 4 points lethal.

      I think M20 added the rule that you can exchange quintessence for pattern bleed, and I really like that rule. I didn't incorporate it into any of my games yet so I can't say I know how well it works. But the way I'd do it is that you'd need 1 point of quintessence per point of lethal damage, with the usual avatar limit on quintessence use applying. I'd also argue that, without Prime 1, the mage would have to do a meditation roll or something more fitting to focus the quintessence into their damaged life pattern.
      As for paying exp, well, I don't really see it as paying to get rid of pattern bleed so much as just buying the attribute as though you were levelling up. So I guess that's how it works in my mind.

      To make your life augmentation to yourself permanent, you need to put a tonne into duration. If the augmentation is vulgar then the permanent paradox becomes truly permanent, but the pattern bleed is gone. I'd say you would also need a tonne of the appropriate type of quintessence to pull this off, especially if you're combining spheres, like making yourself able to breath fire.


      Regarding Mind magic augmenting mental things, I run the exact same rules for Life magic, except I make the damage Bashing rather than lethal. All other rules apply. And I don't believe merits can be bought post character creation. Mind 1 can give you eidetic memory, but if you want it to be permanent, it's going to take a lot of quintessence, or else be an ongoing effect.

      Also, no, Eidetic memory would not allow for an exp break. Just because you can remember everything doesn't mean you can learn from it any quicker. I would however allow people with the Eidetic memory merit to do investigation or enigma rolls on things they remember to find new information they didn't focus on the first time round.
