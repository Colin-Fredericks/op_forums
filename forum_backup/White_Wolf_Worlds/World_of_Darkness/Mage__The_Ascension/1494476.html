                              I've got a blast from the past... Malcolm Shephard actually wrote up the Chakravanti and the Akashics for DA: Mage

                              Chakravanti? Sure, but remember that they're one of many groups during the DA period. The Euthanatos Tradition has not been created yet. In Europe in the period covered by the line, there are: # The Knights of St. Gregory of Nyssa (a recognized knightly order that serves as a cover for the worshippers off Radamanthys)

                              # The Ixoi (they have not yet split into the Golden Chalice and Ksrafai)
                              # The Pomegranate Deme and the The Aided (probably both Old Faith right now, but with a slightly different Foundation. The Aided are actually outcast from the main Old Faith, because they violate the pagan virtues. The Pomegranate Deme are protected by the Gregorian Knights).
                              # The Valdaermen (part of the Fellowship is incorporated into the Euthanatos Tradition as the Gallowsmen)
                              # The Pollottino family (a small family of "white necromancers who use Etruscan rituals," and enemies of the Giovanni family)

                              The Chakravanti comprise the:
                              # Lahksmists (followers of the divine principle of fortune)
                              # Sapindya Sadananda (the Fellowship's warriors and assassins -- also known as the Consanguinity of Eternal Joy. Currently ruled by the necromancer Helekar).
                              # Natatapas (your "average" Tantrist Chakravanti, somewhat matrirachal in outlook).

                              We can give them all the same Foundation and Pillars, though.

                              Foundation: Duty (Dharma)
                              The Chakravanti believe that mystical power and enlightenment come through adherence to one's karmically ordained duty. Average people perform the duties mandated by their caste; Chakravanti have the onerous duty of correcting people and groups when they fall astray of that duty through corruption or by clinging to a distraction. When performed correctly, the Chakravat Duty is equated to the positions of the gods and beyond that, the uncreated power that exists beyond the gods. Thus, proper performance of Duty gives godlike attributes.

                              Pillars: Lokha

                              Lokha (worlds) are layers of existence that are both states of being and actual places that the soul (Atman) incarnates. There are six Lokha in total:
                              # The demonic Lohka (Naraka)
                              # The ghostly Lokha (Preta)
                              # The animal Lokha (Triyagoni)
                              # The human Lokha (Manu)
                              # The Lokha of the warring demigods (Asura)
                              # The divine Lokha (Deva)

                              The human Lokha is already within the mage. It is practiced through adherence to Dharma. The Asura Lokha is a false path practiced by other supernatural beings. So the Pillars are:
                              # Naraka: Punishment and destruction, cursing.
                              # Preta: Necromancy
                              # Triyagoni: Healing the body and controlling base desires.
                              # Deva: Manipulating the fundamental forces of the universe (like Quintessence), taking in divine characteristics, blessings.

                              Foundation:
                              In this period, we call the Foundation the Vajra ("diamond" or "thunderbolt"),
                              represented the dynamic potential of anlightenment. Some Tibetan Akashics will
                              call it Dralaphurba ("windhorse-thunder") which will later be known as
                              Draladhrama, the modern syncretic term. Their aura penalizes rolls which require
                              expressing intimacy.

                              Akashics have an aura of emptiness, like they are a vast, empty space that
                              consumes the unprepared.

                              Rank 1: Normal and supernatural Social rolls have a +1 difficulty due to the
                              Akashic's subtle nature.

                              Rank 3: Attempts to alter the Akashic's mind with supernatural powers (Doiminate,
                              Obligation, Crona, etc.) face a +1 difficulty penalty since the Akashic's mind
                              becomes consumed with esoteric symbols wrapped around an empty space.

                              Rank 5: The Akashic's body now obeys formless, esoteric patterns that are almost
                              unpredictable. Opponents now face a +1 difficulty penalty to strike with physical
                              attacks, unless they directly target the character's pattern.
                              Pillars:

                              The four pillars are called "kaya" ("bodies"). These correspond to the different
                              paths to enlightenment and the different elemental symbols of reality.

                              The Nirmanakaya is the Scarlet Phoenix-Body. This corresponds to the the physical
                              body, as well as practices based on everyday observance and society at large.

                              Many physical applications of Do sping from this Pillar. This Pillar is rigid and
                              emphasizes custom.

                              Rank 1: Sense the flow of propriety in a being or place; whether a person is
                              healthy, whether Quintessence flows naturally through a place (instaed of being
                              twisted or snagged) and whether a person is speaking honestly.

                              Rank 2: Can use proper medicine and exercise to heal oneself of bashing or lethal
                              damage, can smooth chi flow along its proper course (but not determine what that
                              course is; he can return strength to a Dragon Nest, but not add extra strength to
                              it) and can detect supernatural compulsion in others and ward it off in himself.

                              Rank 3: Learns proper rigid forms to increase physical prowess, medicine to heal
                              bashing and lethal in others and aggravated in himself. The Akashic can also ward
                              off supernatural compulsion in a person or area.

                              Rank 4: The Akashic can force people or spirits to obey any vow they willingly
                              make. He can heal aggravated wounds in others by using traditional medicine and
                              sustain his body with discipline so strong that it can ward off fire and shatter
                              swords and spears.

                              Rank 5: The Akashic is a disciplined master of external rigid power and internal
                              sensitivity to the structure of the Tapestry. He can enter Heaven (the Astral
                              Umbra) at will and cannot be cut off from the universe, since he can use rites
                              and exercises to take chi from anywhere.

                              The Sambhokaya is the Pearl Tiger-Body. It is the Pillar of communication and of
                              opening oneself to invisible realms. Akashic who deal with the gods make use of
                              this kaya.

                              Rank 1: The Akashic can peer into the Umbra and can improve his ability to
                              communicate (-1 difficulty/success), but not use telepathy or languages that he
                              does not know. He can see if active spirits are possessing people or places.

                              Rank 2: The Akashic can sense emotions and can engage in voluntarily telepathic
                              speech with others, including spirits on the other side of the Umbra.

                              Rank 3: The Akashic can telepathically communicate with anyone and can even
                              overcome resistance. He can enter dreams and send terrible visions (inflicting
                              bashing damage) to people or spirits. He can even reach into the Umbra while
                              staying in the normal world.

                              Rank 4: Communication with even slumbering spirits is possible, and the Akashic
                              can even speak to past incarnations of entities through the Akashic Record. The
                              mage can makes spirits visible and tangible to others, as well. All this
                              information allows him to avoid surprise and improve alertness with bonuses to
                              defensive dice pools.

                              Rank 5: Akashics can communicate with something to the extent that they become
                              that thing, animating a statue, rock, person or spirit, whether they like it or
                              not. The character can also speak directly to patterns allowing him to animate or
                              destroy them (aggravated, resisted by Willpower).

                              The Dharmakaya is also called the Dragon-Body and represents the dissolution of
                              preconceived ideas. Akashic tap this with internal martial arts, quiet meditation
                              and austerity. The student leaves behind illusions to see things as they are,
                              leading to simple but powerful magic.

                              Rank 1: The Akashic can see through illusions, including mundane disguises and
                              magical deceptions, and even fients and distractions in combat (grants an
                              initiative bonus)

                              Rank 2: You would say that the Akashic could "divide" his mind, but really, the mind expands to encompass many things. A spell allows the character to fully concentrate on multiple nonmagical actions (1 for each arm in addition to one spell, usually). The Akashic can also let his sense of self dissolve, becoming silent or invisible.

                              Rank 3: The Akashic abandons egotism in movement and sees that time is arbitrary (allowing multiple actions) and sees the natural flaws in structure (allowing extra damage). He can also honestly judge the abilities of others (determining their traits with a spell, so long as the character could see them in action).

                              Rank 4: The character's sense of place is an illusion; he can teleport from place
                              to place and affect things from a distance.

                              rank 5: The mage can now be many places at once and can provide minds for each
                              node of experience. He realizes all living chi belongs to a single, cosmic flow
                              and can siphon it to and from other creatures.

                              Swabhavakaya, the Windhorse-Body is the path of black magic and quick, brutal
                              esoteric training. It tranforms the Akashic into a living mystical symbol, able
                              to change other symbols and thus, the creatures attached to them. It includes
                              dangerous arts like karma manipulation.

                              Rank 1: By studying omens and meditative visions, the character can sense the odds of a future event and recognize mighty or infamous destinies.
                              Blah, blah, blah. You guys do the rest.

                              What doesn't kill you, makes you... stranger.
