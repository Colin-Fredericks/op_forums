            124) A Syndicate woman who hasn't left her home in thirty years.

            A trauma sufferer, she developed agoraphobia early in life, leading her to become a shut-in. When her parents died, she used her meager inheritance to play the stock market. A well-educated and savvy woman, she became very good at it. In order to ensure her financial stability - and simply out of boredom and a desire to have an effect on the outside world - she started using her money to form a network of connections and employees. Through intermediaries paid from shadow accounts, she gathered information. About people, places, plans in motion. She extorted powerful people, sometimes for money, more often for power.

            It was when she made the mistake of unknowingly extorting a high ranking Technocrat that the Syndicate investigated and uncovered her web of influence. The Technocracy was impressed, and offered her a job.

            These days, being able to run an information and financial empire from a phone isn't too impressive, unto itself. Many people do it all the time, and many not even Enlightened. She's just very good at it, and the additional powers afforded to her by the internet and telecommunications technology have only enhanced her reach. With a few phone calls to the right agents in her employ, and she can effect change on a large scale. She takes a certain pride in the fact that she's becoming a player on the world stage, despite being no one of importance. Unlike politicians, executives, media personalities, and state intelligence agents, she holds power while remaining in the dark. The intelligent and well-informed can intuit her position as a player in what she calls The Great Game, but are stymied by her lack of public presence. She merely sits on her web of influence, earning her the moniker "Spider Executive" among members of the Syndicate who are aware of her.

            These younger members sometimes even receive orders from her, but always over the phone or in emails. Few Technocrats have met her in person.

            The chink in her armor - the fault in her web - is that she didn't always have the benefit of Technocratic telecom technology. Ergo, her earliest operations were done on the grid, with known accounts. Some of the records of her activity persist in physical form, where they can't be easily hacked and altered or expunged. Despite all her power and the Technocratic support, she has never moved since first withdrawing from the world. Meaning it's entirely possible for a persistent investigation to find out who she is and where she lives. There's a reason her home is surrounded, at all times, by Technocrat guards and agents in disguise.

                        126)

                        Back before he Awakened and was recruited into the Technocracy, this Technocrat of the New World Order Convention wouldn't think that his theories about philosophy, the point and the goals of existence, and the universal similarities and themes woven through both the human mind, and inanimate matter and energy would contribute to changing his life to such a large and significant extent. Yet his zeal and persistence in following and delving into what might have seemed at times like obscure and irrelevant topics and ideas have truly paid of, as due to this that his eyes and mind were opened during this one day, as he was able to recognize a Technocrat he met while studying late in the library at the university he attended back then.

                        He still remembers as he noticed a slight, but deeply characteristic quality in her gaze, and her eyes, as she gave him a cursory look while he took a place at a table next to where she was sitting. Putting aside his work, he studied this woman as she was writing notes while reading through notebooks and sheets of paper. He tried to apply his philosophical theories and ruminations to who he was seeing and what it could have meant, both in itself and in relation to what he lived through. He noticed a slight, but very distinctive soft glow around her pen as she used her Primal Energy modulator to enhance her comprehension and focus. His years of studying philosophy and thinking have prepared him to not being surprised at all, and thus he was able to keep observing the white-blue glow until it faded as the Technocrat woman expanded the Primal Energy from the battery inside the pen. This observation was the final trigger that led to him Awakening and becoming a Mage ; within five minutes of this, he mustered the courage to talk to the woman, who quickly recognized another Enlightened person. She invited him into her apartment for the night and the next few days ; and during the course of the next few weeks initiated and recruited him into the Technocracy, and the New World Order Convention.

                        This Technocrat's background, knowledge, and insights in regard to philosophy has provided a good enough basis in regard to him comprehending the knowledge, and the practice, of Mind Procedures. What matters more to him is how philosophy has tied into his understanding of, and the ability to create and use, technology that enables him to perceive and manipulate Primal Energy. With it, he has been able to call upon Primal Energy to provide him an edge in regard to carrying out Mind Procedures, and to stabilize the results of his Mind Procedures. He also developed technology that gives him other capabilities related to Primal Energy. He is also able to perceive Primal Energy, store it in an apparatus create an idealized image of himself, energize his body, transfer Primal Energy to other Technocrats or to their apparatuses, and receive Primal Energy from other Technocrats through various means.

                        Currently he is researching practical applications of Inspired Psychology and Inspired Philosophy in regard to resolving conflicts and disputes - those internal inside the Technocracy, and those occurring between Technocrats and other groups or individuals ; and also those that occur between individuals or groups without Technocrats being involved.


                        [ This Technocrat Mage of the New World Order Convention has Arete 3, and Prime 3 and Mind 3. ]

                            127) A priest in the ranks of the Cabal of Pure Thought, his duty often calls him to thwart sorcerers and heretics. Unbeknownst to his Convention, he carries out a heresy of his own. A small one, he swears.

                            See, his father was a scholar and copyist working for the Catholic Church, working every day of his life to preserve humanity's stock of lore. Ever since the mage was a child, his father stressed to him the utmost importance of preserving knowledge. Especially classical texts, and written copies of oral traditions. "The Lord giveth wisdom upon all folk, according to His design. Even pagan words carry a wisp of truth, if wheat be sorted from chaff. Never allow a codex or scroll or note succumb to rot or flame. For all knowledge is a gift from the Almighty. To squander it is to spit on His kindness."

                            Such lessons the mage took to heart. Even now, when protocol demands he capture the books of Tradition mages and set them to fire, he cannot bring himself to the task. To set page to flame goes against everything he was taught. So when he takes the possessions of a felled sorcerer or loots their libraries, he does not build a pyre for them. Instead, he quietly commands secret hirelings to take these texts away. He retrieves them at his convenience, and stores them in a private property he retains, just beyond the limits of a certain city in which he's based. A stone building, covered in ivy and moss, with a secret library hidden in the basement. He even learned a few tricks from allies in the Craftmasons, so he could employ them for disguising his private storage.

                            The Order of Reason would not understand his reticence; they consider wrong knowledge categorically harmful, and thus in need of immediate destruction. This mage disagrees. Mayhaps one day, some useful scrap of data can be derived from a Verbana witch's book of herb lore, or from a Hermetic wizard's codex of demonology. The priest is no sorcerer, and definitely no infernalist. He has no interest in their spells or blasphemies. He just supposes that it's better for the Order of Reason to have the books and not need them, rather than need them and not have them.

                            Moreover...though his allies are loath to say so, are their mechanisms and natural philosophy not bordering, even now, upon the truly fantastic? And it would be so much easier to combat a hostile sorcerer's blandishments...if he understood them. He is a faithful man, and loves God...but the temptation to read the books is strong. God give him strength, to resist the allure of sorcery...

                                Usually Technocrats experience working with and learning from members of their own Convention before they take part in cooperative efforts with Technocrats of Conventions other than their own. This member of the Iteration-X is an exception to this, as she took part in joint effort of Technocrats of different Conventions ( The Iteration-X and the New World Order ) in the very situation that she Awakened ; and has kept doing this since her recruitment into the Technocracy.

                                She still remembers it clearly and vividly. After finishing university with Ph.D. s in physics and materials science, she started working in a superconductor and magnetic materials research facility; that unbeknownst to her was used by Technocrats as a base of operations and testing facility. At the end of her work shift she, due to tiredness, absent-mindely went into a room where two Technocrats were observing results of latest tests and tuning the machines and aperture to the new results and calculations based off them. Her entering this Technocratic facility was only possible because the Technocrats, due to tiredness, left the automatic door on a let-in-anyone setting.
                                Upon seeing her they, also due to tiredness, assumed that she was a research assistant knowingly working for the Technocracy, as only such person would be let in by the doors on the usual setting. Immediately after her entering the Technocratic facility a situation - a little crisis - presented itself.
                                Due to mistakes made by the member of the New World Order Convention in accessing the new data, the resonance regulators and chronoscopic sensors at the facility become desynchronized. This caused appearance of chronal anomalies - ghostly images and voices of the two Technocrats from the five previous minutes ; and also resulted in a rapid loss of data from the data storage servers. The member of the Iteration-X sought to stabilize the situation, but she needed another person to operate the technology. Not only was the member of the N.W.O unsuccessful in helping his fellow Technocrat ; his actions actually hastened the ongoing loss of data and caused further desynchronization of the aperture ; resulting in a disruption of overall functioning of the technology in the little Technocratic research facility.

                                -Well, by the rate it is going, and as far as I know him, this is going to take more than one person to sort out and fix what he did.

The young scientist's Enlightenment occurred as he Iteration-Xer explained to her how to operate devices and technology for carrying out Matter 1 and Forces 1 Procedures ; so that the machines could be monitored, in order to identify and prevent any malfunctions and problems, while the technology was adjusted and set back into the usual configurations. Not only was the newly Awakened scientists able to understand and carry out Matter 1 and Forces 1 Procedures ; much to the Iteration-Xer's surprise, she was able to quickly grasp the analysis, assumptions, and calculations required for carrying out Time 1 Procedures. This made it possible for her to monitor the situation of chronal technology in the facility while she calculated and inputted the correct data and settings to deal with the desynchronized premature and the chronal anomalies ; the Iteration-Xer also managed to restore the entirety of lost data. Meanwhile the member of the New World Order was literally taking notes, trying to both comprehend and memorize how to operate the technology in the facility.

Within the next few hours, the two Technocrats brought out food and drinks, and were well into convincing, fact after fact, of the existence, capabilities, and influence of the Technocracy.

While doing research and work within the Iteration-X Convention, she focuses on Matter Enlightened Science and Forces Enlightened Science. She hopes to be able to keep aiding the N.W.O in the future, without this ever being a problem in regard to her achieving her goals and presenting satisfactory results as a Technocrat of the Iteration-X Convention.

                                    129) He is called the Sleep Buster, for he is the enemy of all slumber.

                                    A Marauder, he began as a psychologist in the early 20th century. His Awakening occurred when an obsessive patient at his asylum whispered his madness into the doctor's ear. Or perhaps the Marauder was the patient. He is too far gone, now, to adequately recall. All he knows is that the inmate feared to sleep, for he had dreams. Dreams of...things, from the realm of sleep. Things that wanted to break him. To make him the servant of their dreaming lord. And to use him as a gateway through which their kind could enter the waking world. These maddened ramblings brought the doctor - or the patient - to an Awakening, and to a Quiet from which he has never recovered.

                                    Whether he was a doctor to begin with, or picked up the knowledge later, the Sleep Buster wanders the world, maintaining a series of hidden laboratories. He believes fully in the ramblings of that raving lunatic, from that asylum, long ago. There is another world - a world of sleep and dreams - filled with beings that desperately want to gain access to the material plane. He believes they use sleeping people as bridges to cross over, in addition to means of contacting and corrupting sleepers. To him, a sleeping person looks as though a strange, unearthly smoke rises from them, bearing an unnatural hue not of the waking world.

                                    As such, the "good doctor" has not slept in decades, keeping himself alive and conscious with a steady and regular cocktail of drugs, chemical baths, self-hypnotic procedures, and sonic influence. He wears a breathing mask when he is out and about, that feeds him a steady stream of gas, formulated to keep him alert. He is also armed with a selection of geometric and mathematical formulas and designs. For he believes that the anti-logic of the dream beings cannot abide the rigid order of the waking world; numerical and geometric complexity confounds them. He believes this is why humanity - almost subconsciously - designed their cities with rigid order in mind. The better to protect themselves from the dream things, so they may achieve restful sleep...if there is such a thing. Nonetheless, there is power to be derived from the dream logic the other world operates on, and in imposing such logic on the waking world. In controlled doses, of course. Hallucinogens are useful for this purpose, he finds, for they give a person a measure of dream madness, while keeping them safe and awake.

                                    This is ultimately his purpose, and his mission. To use his research to solve the bane of slumber, and protect mankind from the dream world for good. To accomplish this, though, he needs test subjects. He often stalks the homeless and mentally ill (often times, they are the same), for they are persistent and vivid dreamers. If he can cure them of their addiction to slumber, helping the rest of humanity do the same should be a simple matter. If a few people must perish, that is the price of progress. If nothing else, the dead cannot dream.

                                    He is greatly distressed by seeing anyone asleep in his presence, and will attempt to wake them, and keep them awake, if at all possible. Humanity has been sleeping for millennia - collectively and frequently - so he has much progress to make up. A great deal of ground to cover, to stymie the efforts of his dreamworld foes and their abominable sleeping lord.

                                                    133) Following the Renaissance, in the evolution of the Order of Reason into its modern incarnation - the Technocratic Union - it slowly, one by one, began to prune away the parts of itself that it no longer saw as necessary. The populist and ritualistic Craftmasons fell first, followed thereafter by the faithful of the Cabal of Pure Thought. Those members who resisted change were eliminated, while those who could be "salvaged" were broken and rebuilt in line with the Union's modern, secular vision. The least discussed of the Conventions who "fell", however, were the Ksirafi, the "Razors". The knives in the dark, that once watched Daedaleans for betrayal, gathered knowledge, and performing the dirty deeds necessary for the greater good. Those at the top of the transformed Order of Reason saw these perennial spies and closet mystics as a liability. Naturally, the Razors knew their time was up, and were accustomed to fading into the night. Their libraries were burned (save the extra secret caches). Some allowed themselves to be absorbed into what would become the New World Order, while the more mystical obtained positions in Hermetic House Janissary. The rest perished (sometimes willingly, for the sake of their secrets), or went to ground for good, in order to guard what they had for the rest of time.

                                                    One Ksirafi, however, would not allow himself to simply die. He was one of their masters, and held vast stores of knowledge and experience. He went into hiding, of course, but only so long as was needed to finish his last great work. A volume of text that acted as combination biography and treatise on the Ksirafi's philosophy. Weaved into the text was an enchantment of staggering complexity. A memetic agent that, when the text was read and understood, would imprint a personality overlay on the first person to do so. The plan was to place the text in the charge of his most loyal and secret agents, who would guard it, for centuries, if need be. Until, long after the Technocratic Union had given up on finding any more Razors, they would find an initiate who displayed a personality close to the old master's. And who would be prepared to give everything - even the sanctity of his own ego - to protect the world, through any means necessary. The two persons - old master and new initiate - would become one, and rebuild their order.

                                                    Naturally, no plan survives contact with the enemy. In this case, the enemy was time itself. The vagaries of age and fortune took their toll. The text was lost, the servants dying and not being replaced. Or else "lost the plot", along with his resources.

                                                    We pick up the "plot", as it were, in the most unlikely of places: modern Japan.

                                                    Among the more enthusiastic otaku circles, a young woman sat at her computer, working as a contributor to an anime fan site. Her passions included cosplay and magical girl shows, but her hidden specialty was in identifying and compiling texts the anime studios would appropriate in their productions. If a fan needed to know what the arcane glyphs being used in a show were, she was the person to go to. She'd poured over medieval European documents and familiarized herself with esoteric lore. Not because anyone asked her to, but because it provided a potent intellectual exercise.

                                                    So when the anime studio got their hands on a western document for cheap - one they clearly neither understood nor cared about - they stuck the text in the show, as backdrops and space filler. Such things lent an air of sophistication to shows (not to mention were cheaper to anime than actual activity). For the sake of completion, our otaku poured over the show, frame-by-frame, in order to compile the text's contents and identify their meaning. She managed to gather enough to make sense of the text, and you see where this is going.

                                                    The old master Razor never intended such a...different vessel for his knowledge and personality. The result was not so much an overlay adding itself to a like-minded vessel, so much as two radically different personas being jammed into the same head, and left to integrate as best they could. They did not, as one might imagine, resolve by breaking into separate personalities via dissociation, but it wasn't a clean mixture either. The sheltered young lady was inundated with visions of complex symbology and real memories of having killed people. Many of them. The old master, meanwhile, was baffled by modern technology, cultural trends, and the...complexities of being a Japanese high school girl.

                                                    Indeed, it's only because she had the benefit of his experience that she didn't completely fall apart or reveal what had been done to her. If nothing else, the newly minted Razor knew how to hide her feelings and intent behind a mask. Everything was fine, she'd say. Everything was not fine, of course, but she knew to just smile and nod. She was very used to smiling; she may have been an otaku, but she loved to get into character when cosplaying at conventions. As far as she was concerned, she was just cosplaying. Full time. As herself.

                                                    Suffice to say, the amalgam of two people is quietly wrestling, not just with the cognitive dissonance between the two life experiences, but between vastly different personalities. She doesn't want to be playing a role all the time, just to get by. It's just that no matter how much fun she's having with friends or family, she can't help keeping a running mental record of everything they say or do around her, and making detailed notes of it later. What is he - sorry, she - supposed to do? NOT diligently maintain the shadowy, spy-like lifestyle honed over two centuries of life in the Order of Reason? On the other hand, she finds herself emotionally confused all the time, as the Ksirafi's detachment from interpersonal investment is challenged by her genuine, youthful desire for friendship, love, and validation? How is that old man supposed to react to the desire to get with boys?

                                                    For that matter, why does their Daemon - the grim and warlike angel - now appear in visions as positively...bishonen!?

                                                    On one matter, however, the two sides of this coin agree. They do not like the Technocratic Union. She finds their dismissal of magic and wonder to be repellent, and their abuses of common people to be obscene. He, meanwhile, still holds a grudge against the group that abandoned their roots and disregard the angels of their better nature, not to mention cast out their most loyal members when they were not longer convenient.

                                                    So recently, the Tradition chantries of Japan have a new arrival. An orphan with a strangely broad - if inconsistent and archaic - understanding of Hermetic praxis. Out in Japan, no Order of Hermes members in the region are intimately familiar with the works of the now decimated House Janissary, so they presume she's a mere self-taught magician. A magus with some potential, perhaps, if educated in proper Hermetic forms. She's certainly bizarre, though, for a Hermetic. Then again, chantries throughout the world are no strangers to oddballs.

                                                    Exactly what she's counting on. She may appear like just an otaku who learned all her praxis from anime, but that's only because she keeps all her deepest arts - and more subtle Ksirafi tricks - in her back pocket, to be employed in secret and at a time and place of her choosing. She observes her new colleagues - old habits die hard - but is really interested in learning about the Technocrats in her city. As well as making sure that no infiltrators from the Union can act unnoticed within the Traditions's ranks. That she's a flamboyant and, shudder, cute personality is just one more tool in her arsenal. No one would suspect the chantry member that comes in wearing cat ears and tail to be observing anything, let alone be an actual threat.

                                                    In short, she's an emotionally conflicted magical girl, who just happens to be knowledgeable in centuries old techniques of subterfuge and assassination. Her cosplay costumes all come equipped with places to hide blades, potions, poisons, lockpicks, and grappling hooks. She's compassionate and loving, unless provoked, where she can dispatch a foes with cold, detached efficiency...and then cry about it later. It would almost be funny, if she wasn't the most dangerous otaku this side of the Virtual Adept hacking scene.
