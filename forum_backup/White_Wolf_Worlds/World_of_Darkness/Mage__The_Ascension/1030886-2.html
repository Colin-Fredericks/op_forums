    Saur Ops Specialist See, here's the main problem I have with your conceit. You're taking the view that middleground cannot exist because middleground existing = Mage. Werewolf says it's like this, therefore any attempt to deviate from what Werewolf states is Mage.
    It's a really narrow way of viewing things, and closes the door to any kind of extrapolation, interpretation, or reexamining.

    Also, what are you talking about regarding the other cosmologies? Wraith takes place almost exclusively in the Dark Umbra, their penumbra, the Shadowlands, is basically the Videre Mortum, but they are unable to communicate with the spirits because they can only see the living or the dead, not the spiritual. In order for a mage to interact with the shadowlands properly, they must either go through the Agatham Sojourn or die. They can cross the gauntlet and gimpse at the Shadowlands, but crossing the Veil to walk around there is a different matter.

    After that point, things are basically like they are in WTO, Nihils to get to the Tempest, Walk around Stygia, get drowned into a Harrowing, fend of Spectres in the Labyrinth, all the while being attacked by any wraith who doesn't like who alive you happen to be. Werewolves can visit the deadlands too, but usually don't have much cause to do so. There are theories that the Wyrm and Oblivion share a strange relationship, and that while one pushes forward for corruption, the other slowly pulls. It may even be the case that after the apocalypse, the Wyrm may plunge itself into Oblivion, thereby unmaking itself in its final act, returning all to nothing.

    The Astral Umbra is basically its own thing, but Werewolves are able to visit if they tried. It just makes absolutely no sense to them. There is reason to say that the Far Shores of the lower umbra are connected to the Afterworlds of the Astral Realm. Where concepts of the underworld and the memories and passions embodied may intermingle. It may even be possible for a Wraith to escape the afterworlds and find themselves in the astral realm proper. Though such a journey is likely to be fraught with peril and may even be impossible for most. Alternatively this could be where the thoughts of Wraiths who transcend end up, making up the fabric of the astral realm, from the Vulgate to the Epiphamies.

    Let's not forget the Dreaming though. The Dreaming probably has the most overlap with the Middle Umbra, but it is possible to approach both the Astral and Dark umbra is one was so inclined to do so.

    You might say "It doesn't work like that". I say it can totally work like that. Then you say "Only if every game is Mage" to which I say every game is WoD, it's just that Mages get to go everywhere.


    You don't want it to work. That's why it doesn't.

                                                    Well, the Garou would probably think that the Mages were insane after the first couple of trips to the Umbra and would likely avoid traveling to the Umbra with their Mage friends to avoid too much confusion. The exception may be Dreamspeakers and Verbena who share a Gaian paradigm, as they might interpret things close enough to the Garou that the Garou might not think they were insane. The fact that Mages are vulnerable to the Avatar Storm also means that the Garou may want to save their friends from the pain associated from having their souls shredded as well.

                                                    One problem that Mages have in a crossover is that they find it difficult to keep up with Garou in physical combat and the Umbra. Mages cannot regenerate damage as easily as Garou do, and they do not naturally soak lethal or aggravated damage. Even a relatively weak combat Fomori will be able to kill a combat expert Mage, so Mages really need to leave the fighting to the Garou.

                                                    The utility of Mages to their Garou allies is in their flexibility, not their power, and one Mage can provide support equivalent to dozens of Kinfolk for their Gaoru allies. Mages end up dealing with normal humans, spying on Pentex locations, negotiating for them with the local Kindred, etc. A Garou group supported by a couple of properly designed Mages will likely be much more effective than a Garou group supported by a couple dozen Kinfolk.
