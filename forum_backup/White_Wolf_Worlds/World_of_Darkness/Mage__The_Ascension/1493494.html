This feels like a situation where you'd have to convince each Convention separately, no?

                          I think this question is similar to comic book fans asking why hasn't this powerful superhero of the Avengers or Justice League gone after and defeated this dangerous, but far less powerful foe of Spider-Man or Batman. The real answer is the need for each comic book to have its own identity and signature villains. It's not out of any sense of realism. The Technocracy doesn't go after PENTEX because the Werewolf game needs it as an antagonist. It's completely for OOC reasons, not IC reasons. Any IC reason given in game is just to help with the suspension of disbelief.

                          Once you accept that, then you can provide any answer that makes sense to you why the Technocracy isn't taking down PENTEX. Likewise if you want it done for your own chronicle, you can justify it however you want for the Technocracy to do so.

                          Personally, my head canon is that the Technocracy has identified PENTEX as a threat, but in their threat matrix that identifies priority targets, PENTEX doesn't yet qualify as one of their priorities. At best, they perform low risk raids every now and again to thwart PENTEX plans and arrange it so that it is blamed on the Garou. That delays PENTEX becoming a real threat while they take care of higher priority targets. Even the Technocracy has only so many resources. Triage is expected.

                          It's entirely possible the Technocracy has misevaluated the situation and should move PENTEX as something they should move against now, but it is OK in the setting for the Technocracy to get things wrong. They are not omniscient. If you want that to change, you just need to provide enough proof that PENTEX poses a greater current, immediate threat than some other targets or projects they are working on.

                                          The problem with PENTEX, beyond what Black Fox explained, is that it has two fundamentally contradictory roles in the setting, so you have to work out how they actually work in your chronicle before even considering how the Union works (and I disagree in many points with Saur Ops Specialist here, unless you want a game of utterly delusional Technocracy).

                                          On one hand, PENTEX fills the role of the Corporate Conspiracy, a board of devil-worshiping directors hidden behind labyrinthine layers of subsidiaries and contractors, muddying connections between their wyrmish activities. This PENTEX is secretive, shrewd, has the bulk of its operations free of taint, few people would ever guess what they're doing, less would find enough evidence to be sure, and anyone trying to accuse them would have such a hard time piecing evidence that at least before humans would seem like crazy.

                                          But at the same time, PENTEX is a joke. Like, a literal joke. It is an on-the-nose satire on all perceived problems in the corporate world. It is shameless Office Dark Comedy, plus Consumerism Comedy. This PENTEX is just as important in WtA because the point of the game is to make obvious enemies obvious (it isn't to only have obvious enemies, just not to waste nuance with some types). This PENTEX has the subtlety of Tom & Jerry and its continued existence is solely due to social and institutional incompetence intensified by Cartoon Logic.

                                          Granted, being the Wyrm what it is, you can introduce supernatural elements that explain this Cartoon Logic at face value in a relatively serious setting. You can also keep it rare so such moments can be written off as a fluke. But the setting itself never really addressed the matter of how much Canon PENTEX is Conspiracy PENTEX or Comedy PENTEX.

                                          However, at the end of the day the Union isn't completely oblivious to such things unless you want it to be. The Syndicate keeps the stuff they get from the dealings with PENTEX a secret precisely because the Union won't accept it. A highly competent version of Conspiracy PENTEX would be able to avoid the Union's scrutiny for some time in a game that takes both at face value and seriously, remember that they do have Awakened resources with them, almost always at least one skilled Nephandus within the Board of Directors and the pawns they got within the Syndicate. But enough evidence in the hands of concerned individuals within the Union would be enough to get at least some help, any amalgam convinced to investigate.

                                          Also, the actual approach of the Technocracy to supernaturals varies a lot, but generally unnecessary confrontation is discouraged if the amalgam isn't specifically there on a cleansing mission, which is rare. A Garou approaching the Union with evidence may not be taken as seriously as they hope, but would have at the very least the benefit of them not shooting first. It isn't unlikely that the evidence will be at least taken into custody and examined internally. And even if it can't move the whole Union to action, it can still move as many amalgams as you can convince, to help in a limited capacity and sometimes even to dedicate fully to this new mission at the cost of reprimands if it is found out.

                                                  Originally posted by HorizonParty2021 View Post


                                                  Personally, I find the NWO's Operatives and Watchers missing Pentex previous to it folding up a little hard to believe. They are the Convention who looks over everyone else's shoulder inside the Union. If there is supposed to be an existing rivalry between them and the Syndicate, you would think they would have done an investigation thorough enough to find such a huge bedfellow as Pentex. The NWO has the advantageous role of screening most new recruits to determine which Convention they are best suited for. This is usually at a stage when said recruits haven't developed Mind-Shields. NWO knows most of their colleagues very well. If they want to know something, they know who to ask and how to ask them.
                                                  A valid critique, but there are other factors in play that I haven't mentioned:
                                                  • The majority of Pentex is exactly like any other greedy corporation in the planet, there's nothing really suspicious on the surface.
                                                  • All the illegal and supernatural stuff represents only a very small part of it, a conspiracy within the corporation.
                                                  • Also, the conspiracy has been divided and compartmentalized in different parts in case if one part is discovered, it can't be linked to the rest.
                                                  • It's one of the wealthiest corporations in the world, and money is a very powerful asset.
                                                  • This money gives them a lot of influence with politicians, media, law enforcement and other influential figures.
                                                  • They have access to resources that few other organizations have, like hypertech and magick, and other that no one has, like corrupted fera.
                                                  • Its leadership is formed by powerful nephandi and supernatural creatures with plenty of experience about working in secrecy.
                                                  Also, to be fair, in my games the New World Order does suspect that there's something weird going in Pentex, but they haven't found any conclusive evidence yet. As such, it's considered a low priority target, and most missions consist in gathering intelligence and keep watch in case they make a mistake and reveal something.

                                                  Now, to answer the original question, how to convince the whole union to go after Pentex?

                                                  I think that the best strategy would be to guide the Union agents to the evidence instead of handing it to them, they're more likely to accept it this way. Once they have enough evidence about what's really going with Pentex your players can jump in to offer more evidence and their help. The idea is to build an alliance, but keep in mind that this will take time, even if the agents have seen enough to realize how bad Pentex is and how the players are truly just people trying to bring it down, they will be slow to trust. Also, Pentex is not the only enemy here, Project Invictus will go after them too.
                                                  Another option of course is to convince Project Invictus that the only way they can take down Pentex is if they tell the rest of the Union and get their help, (it's true, they can't do it alone). The problem is how the hell do you do that, also the players have to find out about project Invictus first and not get killed. The players could bring them enough evidence to prove how they're way over their heads and how badly they need help before Pentex does some real damage to them, or maybe using blackmail to force their hand. In any case, this approach will be the quickest, but also the hardest.
                                                  It must be said that there's always the possibility that during this process the Syndicates and project Invictus operations to keep Pentex hidden could come to the light. As I said before, this would paralyze the Union while they're doing an internal audit and purge or even go to civil war, which would render their help mostly useless.
