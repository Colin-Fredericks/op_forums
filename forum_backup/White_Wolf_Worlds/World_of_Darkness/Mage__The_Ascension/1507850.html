                  -the laws of physics are not set, but oppose each other in a sort of cosmic darwinian struggle where the winner is determined by the ammount and structure of information or modeling of said law. The loser still exist appart of the material world, in the umbrae.

                  -spirits are in reality extradimensional aliens, either born naturally thanks to the discarded laws of the umbra, or modelled into existence directly from human imagination and belief.

                  Following all these axioms, the technocrats using dimensional science are using exotic physics and mathematics to shape the state of the gauntlet, disrupt the ephemera of spirits, which is simply umbra's matter, and call them by shaping their informational state to allow their existence in the material world for a time

                                  As long as you don't put focus on the Void Engineers, I could really see leaving the Umbra as a weakness of the Technocracy — though that also potentially excludes the possibility of Black Hats doing the Ghostbusters thing.

                                  That said, be aware that Pillars are even more versatile than Spheres. The Pillars of the Order of Hermes (the Forma) are close parallels to Forces, Life, Mind, and Prime; and according to DA:Mage Grimoire, the Order of Hermes can use conjunctions of the Mind and Prime equivalents to mess with denizens of the Astral Umbra.

                                  If I were to create Pillars for the modern Conventions, I'd call them Sciences. I'd start by building three of them around the three Technocratic Sphere variants found in M20: Data, Dimensional Science, and Primal Utility. Maybe rename and expand some of them; for instance:

                                  • I could see expanding Primal Utility to Hypereconomics, a Science that covers not just Prime-like Effects but also Entropy-like Effects (at least in the sense of Entropy as manipulation of fortune). This would be a mainstay of the Syndicate.
                                  • Expand Data to Information Science, and have it include Mind-like Effects as they apply to digital phenomena: e.g., manipulation of senses becomes manipulation of sensors; altering memories becomes messing with stored data; and so on. Both Iteration X and the NWO would like this one; though as I'll address a bit later, it isn't the primary Science of either of them.
                                  • And incorporate “Void Correspondence” (a variation of Correspondence that focuses more on its manipulation of space and less on its Sympathetic Links; described in the Void Engineers Convention Book) into Dimensional Science. This would be the Void Engineers' go-to Science. Also incorporate some elements of Prime and/or Forces into this, with a focus on the luminal aspects of Prime: Dimensional Science should allow for the likes of force fields, laser swords, reactionless drives, phasers, and similar “space opera” hypertech. In fact, relabel it as “Energetics”.

                                  Then add three more Sciences to cover areas that the above miss:
                                  • the primary Science of Iteration X would be Mechanical Science, which would deal mainly with mechanisms (making them and messing with them).
                                  • the Progenitors would favor Biological Science, which deals mainly with organisms. In conjunction with Mechanical Science, this allows for cybernetics.
                                  • the New World Order would focus on Noetic Science, which covers the likes of psychology and sociology.

                                  So, in summary, six Sciences:
                                  • Biological Science (Progenitors and ItX)
                                  • Economic Science (Syndicate)
                                  • Energetic Science (VEs)
                                  • Information Science (ItX and NWO)
                                  • Mechanical Science (ItX and VEs)
                                  • Noetic Science (NWO and Syndicate)

                                  My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

                                          Probably, yes. Fold that into Energetic Science as well. (I was thinking about that after I made my last post.)

                                          Expanding on this idea, maybe the Technocratic Foundation should be called Logos, from the same root as “logic”. It features an approach to the Sciences that's meticulous and which involves something akin to the Scientific Method, with higher levels of the Foundation aligning with a stronger tendency to think critically. So “Logos” is the Foundation, and the Pillars are the Biological, Economic, Energetic, Information, Mechanical, and Noetic Sciences. Each of the Conventions favors one Science (and for this purpose, I'm including the Virtual Adepts as a Convention): ItX favors Mechanics; NWO favors Noetics; Progenitors favor Biology; Syndicate favors Economics; VAs favor Information; and VEs favor Energetics.

                                                      For the Technocracy, they absolutely should have a single Foundation. Book of Secrets talks about how the various Conventions have an easier time of training each other because of their shared Focus. And if the existing Foundations, the Order of Hermes' Modus is a remarkably good fit.

                                                      Your analysis of the early Order of Reason works well for the Artificers, Cosians, and Craftmasons; not the Gabrielites or Ksirafai (which is fine; you've said as much); but also not for the Celestial Masters, High Guild, or Void Seekers.

                                                      The Celestial Masters and Void Seekers need something like Correspondence/Connection: much like the Batini have a Pillar of Mastery of Mind that's similar to, but distinct from, Corona, this Forma should be similar to, but distinct from, the Batini Mastery of Space (Al-Hajj).

                                                      I'd run the High Guild opposite of the Craftmasons: instead of replacing Primus with Materia, replace Vires with Fortuna, a Forma counterpart to the Batini Mastery of Fate.

                                                      The Gabrielites should probably use the Messianic Voices Foundation and Pillars; and I'm thinking that the Ksirafai should use the Batini Foundation and Pillars (Fate, Minds, Space, and Secrets).

                                                      My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

                                                          The High Guild ends up with Anima, Corona, Fortuna, and Primus: the first has to do with the Art of Desire's focus on self-perfection, specifically perfection of the body; the second deals both with their self-discipline as applied to the mind, and also to their ability to suss out and manipulate other people's wants and desires.
