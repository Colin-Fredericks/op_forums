    Mind magic is seriously hard

    11-28-2017, 06:37 PM
    hey guys, wanted to ask your opinion and advice on this... i've been playing mage the ascension for some time now and i have a friend of mine who would really like to play a mind mage kind of enslaver of minds and hidden puppeteer..

    only problem is that, by reading the how you do that guide and the m20 core book, my player got really frustrated and basically said to me "why is mind magic so hard, it will never work in a real game situation or a fight! i should stick with forces or other spheres..".
    after reading all the rules listed and stuff from various books, i kind of agree...
    maybe the mage who focuses on mind can play his schemes or influence weak humans but he is basically fucked if he tries some mind manipulation against night folk or other nephandi and technocrats..

    so i was trying to come up with ideas and startegies to make mind magic work and mind control more possible to use (not necessarily in a combat stressed situation) and wanted to ask you guys what are your strategies to make a mind mage work and not feel useless in a cabal where the other guys can simply seal u away in a wall of lava or paralayze you with life effects or curse u with entropy.

    what i mean is, since the roll to influence or enslave other mages and nightolkf is so hard, what are your strategies to make them weaker and more vulnerable to mind influence/control?

    I was also wondering if the difficulty rule to influence nightfolks apply also to ALL mind effects, including illusions and psyichic assault... cause creating illusions might be a good way for a mind based magic to save his ass in a fight and flee.

    One of the best strategies i think it's simply to accumulate successes quietly targeting the enemy from distance, and learn the space sphere. then u won't even need to go out and hunt your target, and u can keep on spying him and scry all while influencing his modd and thought.

    another way i found is by simply lowering his willpower and then enter his mind. By using the emotional surge p.121 of HWYDT, the mage can lower his victims temporary willpower accumulating successes, and he can then try to enslave him.. kinda make sense to me, but im not sure its possible,
    cause the entry on affecting mages and nightfolk doesn't specify if the willpower trait used for the difficulty on the arete roll is the temporary or the permanent trait.. but i would honestly allow him, it sounds like a vampire enslaving another being through dominate and presence, and it should work, slowly eroding the willpower away and chanting "you will fall.. you will be under my control.."

    for sure, a mind mage sucks big time in a real sudden fight on the street or against even the more weak willed of supernatural foes.. and overall feel very lame compared to what other mages can accomplish with other spheres...


    u might wonder if my player is just a min-max powerplayer but she is not, she cares about the game and the story, but she told me she also wants to be useful in a battle and not be scared when she encounters even the weakest of ghouls armed.
    thing is, we come from mage the awakening 2nd edition where a mage mind mastigos can simply outright dominate in one turn even an elder werewolf and force him to shoot himself, or rewrite entire memories on the spot in one turn even against a vampire or another mage... so naturally when my player saw how hard was mind magic and slow and difficult to use, she was kinda shocked and asked me if the creator of the game hated mind mages and mind magic.

      Not every sphere is combat oriented. To be honest, mind magic in Mage the Ascension is not really geared to direct combat uses in my opinion though I concede that’s a matter of opinion.

      I think that if you want to look at it from my perspective, you’ll see mind can be useful in combat (just FYI, I use revised edition rules for the most part).

      It takes 1 success to effect another person, 1 success for duration to make the effect last for a scene (in revised, an effect lasted a turn even if you spent no successes on duration) and 4 successes to force someone to perform an action. This means it takes 6 successes to really make someone do something they don’t want to do, probably rolled at a difficulty of 7 because it sounds fairly vulgar to me. To do this in 1 turn would require a dicepool of 10 with 1 quintessence spent to lower the difficulty to 6 and 1 willpower for an auto success (this is why I use sphere + Arete for magic rolls when using revised edition success slitting rules).

      I get that this means that the vampiric power of dominate 2 is more effective for DIRECT mind control than mind 3 (which is what I decided to use for my example up there) and that sucks.

      But maybe, like Ppirit or Prime magic, mind isn’t so much about combat, maybe it’s about all those other great things you can do with it too.

      Then again, remember, if you want to use mind 3 to mind blast someone, which is a great direct attack, all you have to do is spend a point of quintessence to take that damage from bashing to aggravated!

      Mind is a lot like Prime (using M20 rules) it takes Prime 3 to do any real damage and you have to burn a point of quintessence to make that bolt or weapon out of prime. Mind is that same, you can do bashing damage with mind but not lethal.

      It takes entropy 4 to do direct harm to someone, so entropy mages often make guns jam or tires explode in fights (ent 3) as opposed to using their magic to do direct damage, many of the other spheres are similar in that regard.

      It takes Life, Prime or Mind at 3 to do real harm to someone and you can’t flip cars or stop bullets with those spheres.

      Spirit, Time, Correspondence and Matter just can’t do damage directly. Aging someone, teleporting people and turning the ground they stand on into water are all potentially lethal but just as costly as the mind control effect in way of successes.

      No sphere stands up to forces when it comes to killing people or causing damage, not Life, not Entropy, not Prime and not Mind.

      Tell her if she wants to throw out the hits, she has to use Mind 3 and it’s bashing, but otherwise, just as effective and Entropy 4 or Prime and Life 3 for doing direct damage.

              The idea that Mind is under powered because direct mind control is hard in M20 just... baffles me. Mind is one of the most versatile, potent, and useful Spheres in the game. It's just a Sphere that favors subtlety (and is incredibly coincidental for it).

              Like, want to be really useful in combat? Use Mental Empowerment (only Mind 1) to pump your whole cabal's Wits to 5 for the rest of the scene, for a group Initiative buff, and make sure they have some Mind Shields so enemies can't trying to screw with their heads either.

              The difficulty increase for Nightfolk as written would apply to direct mental illusions, but not physical illusions.

              It doesn't apply to mind blasts, or mind reading.

              The lesson here is simple. Mages are best when they're subtle. If you want to get into the business of mind controlling other mages and Nightolk, you have to manipulate people instead of just tossing Mind 4 at them to get them to obey your every command.

                  The thing is, the author of M20, Phil Brucato, probably wanted to avoid having players with Mind magic easily take over hordes of mages and other supernaturals, and to be fair, he has a right to be concerned. I’ve had disruptive characters who went out and tried to mind control hordes of NPCs and even the other player characters and then try to shove it down my throat and make me, as storyteller, go along with it. Maybe that’s why Phil nerfed mind control a little.

                  My advice, scrap what you don’t like in M20 and look at previous editions, I think you’ll find that the Mind sphere as depicted elsewhere is much more fitting for your game.

                  That being said, I think you (at least seem to) have a very combat centered focus, the spheres of mage give you incredible and diverse powers, these powers are so diverse that in many (but not all) ways, combat is irrelevant in terms of what they can do. You can do so much with Mind, it is perhaps the single greatest sphere for acquiring power and influence in the mundane world. Given how you can shape thoughts and opinions over sleepers, this sphere has incredibley vast utility.

                  I hope I don’t sound preachy and arrogant when I say all this but I really think it’s worth considering.

                  When asking yourself how Mind magic is going to change your game, ask yourself how the magic the players can do is going to change the way they approach your world. You might just surprise yourself with how OP the sphere can be! LOL

                      As Heavy Arms stated, you can use Mind 1/2 for a number of empowerments that aren't evident and are easy to pull off. Increasing Wits it's one.
                      You can also use Mind to gain mental prowess, like "multitasking" (which can be represented by lowering the difficulty of multiple actions), or increased concentration.
                      With Life 2 and Mind 1 you should be able to increase your coordination for easy attacks.
                      With Mind 2-3 you should be able to read other people's mind to discover how will they attack and thus gain several bonuses. While the diff for this would be affected by enemy Willpower + super bonuses, Perception based effects need only 1 success to do something in M20 (you can get at least that with Willpower).
                      You can affect other people's emotions, thus creating fearless soldiers, and with Mind 3/Time 1 you should be able to coordinate a taskforce perfectly.

                      But I understand that this isn't your cup of tea.

                      While M20 increases the difficulty compared with revised, keep in mind that it drastically reduces the number of successes needed because you don't need to divide sucesses between duration and targets (it's optional, ST choice). You should be able to do some minnor mind control at 3 sucesses (In M20 you affect others at 3 sucesses, at ST discretion it may be enough for misdirection).

                      While I'm not a fan of min /max, In this case I would look at everything that reduces difficulty to make this concept work. Just out of spite:
                      Make it a mystic and state that your Instrument for these effects has been overcome, yet use it anyway for the -1 to diff. Make it your Specialized Instrument so you get another -1 for Mind effects. You could make it Unique for another -1 (but this it's risky)
                      Use in the concept a Mythic Thread to get another -1 (look into my eyes/pull a spinning disk or a pendulum in front of the target).
                      Research your targets if you can to get from -1 to -3 ("vampires are aroused by blood, maybe an illusion that includes the smell of blood can affect their feral emotions more easily").
                      If you can, get a personal item of the target to get from -1 to -3 to diff..
                      Have a potent Avatar, Prime, and some Quintessence source. More so, be a Legend strongly dedicated to "mind control", so your own resonance gives an aditional -1 to the difficulty.
                      If you can get a consistent -6/-8 to diff you should be a master mindbender with all and M20 rules pushing to the other side (while the modifiers are limmited to +3/-3, that's after all modifiers are aplied). Take that Dracula!.

                      OR you could just change the rules as suggested, back to the old: "Difficulty depends on vulgarity and the sphere level used". Nothing breaks. This it's the best solution if you want to play with that concept.

                          I agree with Zennis. HDYDT is a mixed bag, because many of the decisions made for that book add needless complications, prerequisites, and hurdles. And attempts to rein in player abuses - an admirable goal - often go too far in the other direction. The stipulations placed on Mind are particularly egregious. Let's just say it incensed me, reading that section of the book.

                          As for whether Mind is useful in combat, it totally is. You can assault a person's mind directly at Mind 3. Granted, such things do only Bashing damage, but it's perfectly serviceable for the purposes of putting a person down. An enemy who dropped into unconsciousness because their health levels filled up with Bashing is as much out of the fight as an enemy who dropped into death because their health levels filled up with Lethal or Aggravated.

                          In some ways, knocking someone out with Bashing can be MORE advantageous, both from a practical and role-playing perspective. Leaving bodies around can be inconvenient in the long term, since corpses attract attention and bring murder charges. Even a police investigation that ultimately ends in the character getting off from self defense will bring inconvenient levels of public and municipal scrutiny, that most mages will find undesirable. And not every character will necessarily be okay with killing people, even in self defense. Indeed, an Awakened character may find killing more unacceptable for them, because they had the personal power to solve their problems without taking a life. While other Spheres might be very good at ending someone's life, Mind is naturally built towards resolving conflicts with minimal bloodshed, either by tricking or compelling someone into ceasing hostilities, diffusing conflicts before they escalate, or taking people down without killing them. All of which can happen in a more subtle, possibly coincidental fashion.

                          (Granted, nothing in the other Spheres says you MUST use the maximum level of damage type available to them. Life could be used to do Bashing damage instead of Aggravated, if the player is creative in what exactly the character is doing with Life. Even Prime 2 has a "Rubbing the Bones" Effect, that explicitly does Bashing instead of Aggravated. And Forces can be used to make blunt force attacks.)
