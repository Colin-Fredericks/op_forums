                                  Actually Wolfgang Von Reisman was a big leader in the Brotherhood of the Rule/La Compagnia, the group that would become the High Guild and the Syndicate. He and the Brotherhood were involved with Craftmasons as they were forming themselves. They paid for the mercenary soldiers the Craftmasons used at Mistridge and a century later, helped them reach out to various other Philosopher-Scientist groups, forming the Order of Reason. In-universe, the High Guild/Syndicate rewrote history once the Craftmasons were disbanded, attributing their accomplishments to themselves and claiming that they created the Craftmasons.

                                  As for Von Reisman's son, he's not talked about. The 1E Syndicate CB also talks about Joseph von Reisman, one of Wolfgang's descendants, who is the VPO of Europe West in the pre-Avatar Storm. He disappeared like much of the Union's leadership in the Storm.

                                  The status of the old leaders/founders of the Order of Reason is largely unknown as the only time they've ever clearly explained Control, the top of the Union's pyramid was in Ascension and in that book we got to different versions of Control, depending on the scenario. One it's a faceless, formless force created by the paranoid subconscious beliefs of the Union while in the other Control is the original founders of the Order forced off Earth because of the Paradox of their life-prolonging Procedures and because of getting cut off by the Avatar Storm have basically become Spirits of Reason.
