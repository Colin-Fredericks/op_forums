                            And the fact that Sleeper scientists and technicians work with such things regularly and in scale does seems to indicate they're already a part of the Consensus. Which goes back to the question of where they should be on the Matter rankings.
                            Part of this is contingent on the presence of unawakened scientists in the first place. Some could say that if one is a scientist in WoD, they are either a sleeper, meaning they would likely only to technician work or general dogs-body things, carrying out the experiments at the behest of others; sorcerers the technocracy refer to as extraordinary citizens, at which point they are simply using paths which are essentially procedure based wonders designed by the technocracy; or awakened technocrats, in which case all bets are off.

                            We could extrapolate this further and say any cutting edge technology which has yet to become available to casual consumers is a form of technocratic sorcery, with the use by extraordinary citizens required to normalise the idea so that it can be accepted by the public not just as a form of linear magic, but as a simple way in which the world operates, becoming one with the consensus in a way that forgoes the need for belief entirely.

                            How this would break down is like this. In the victorian era, using a microscope would absolutely be an act of magic which was slowly becoming a sorcery path, enabling anyone with the correct understanding to operate one. These days however, the concept of a microscope has become so widely accepted and the things one would see through it so certain that anyone who uses it would see what the consensus expects them to see. But if you went back to the dark ages with a microscope in hand, it simply would not work, no one would see anything through it unless you, as an awakened individual, prepared it for them as a way of enacting your will on to what they see, you would essentially be casting the spell for them.
                            Any analysis these days which relies on genetic analysis, mass spectrometry, quantum physics, etc, all would be sphere magic with a few of these applications turned in to paths. PCR to determine the presence of viral DNA would be an example of a sorcery path, one which is only fairly recent, about 50 years ago it was full on sphere magic.

                            Regarding the consensus being real, yes, it absolutely is. But only in so far as things done using the rules of the consensus. Doing awakened magic specifically subverts the consensus and creates a result that sits beyond what the consensus usually enables. The only difference between coincidental and vulgar magic in this regard is whether or not one could easily come up with an explanation that fit the consensus, but both types of effects are indeed subverting the consensus, even sensory magic subverts the consensus as to gain such insights you are generally looking beyond what people believe is possible to see. But the consensus is not the same thing as capital R Reality, at least not yet. The umbra, the periphery, spirits, and many other aspects of the other worlds exist regardless of sleeper consensus, but the consensus does affect how much they can interact with particular areas or people. Similarly there are elements of Reality which shine through beyond the consensus such as the nature of the Avatar, and actions of Paradox, the machinations of death, the permanence of quintessence, the existence of the mind, the fact that the consensus exists and that it can be manipulated. All of these elements persist regardless of popular belief, beyond the thing veil that the consensus provides.

                            What the Earthly Foundations refers to are elements of reality which seem so shared across humanity that they are consistent across local beliefs. This includes the idea that everyone is drawn towards the ground, that night is dark and day is bright, that water flows from high to low and always in one direction, that time too flows in one direction, that death is not reversible, that spatial dimensions are conserved across places, etc. All of which are things that magic can subvert, but cannot be explained away by any belief system to the point of becoming coincidental, they are not, however, indicators of what is true Reality, more things that are so strongly embedded in to the daily existence of people that, as of the current era, no reality zone exists which would make such things possible.

                                    On the same way, Earthly Foundations have no explanation. They're not specially common beliefs, this is a you thing. They're aspects of reality that remain constant, and to each their own on explaining this away.
                                    The way I am approaching this is to present an outlook that lets things work together. Something that makes sense given what is presented. When people find an inconsistency or some way where the world doesn't seem to make sense, I like to imagine what would need to be the case for it to have made sense, and use that as the model going forwards. To be clear, you do not have to use this, and you especially shouldn't consider this the only option available. And I do encourage people throw away the aspects of the game that do not work for their own conception of the world. But for me, it's more fun to try and figure out what the implications of it are, and see if those assuage the feelings of it initially not making sense. I don't mind if you dislike what I come up with, that's fine. But I want to make sure I am at least being internally consistent, and explain how it is I come up with what I come up with, to show there is precedent for the ideas.

                                    With that in mind, I'll now present how it is I came up with the answers
                                    Going off of what the book says, Earthly foundations is cited as "The core reality zone" M20 Pg 615. Which is why I say they aren't elements of capital R reality so much as elements that lay the foundation of belief for the consensus.


                                    M20 Sorcerer has this section on Page 16

                                    "While most new Paths are created by hedge magicians themselves, mages can also create them. The process is arduous and resembles the creation of a Talisman (Mage 20p. 652-653,Book of Secrets p. 151-158). However, the “Talisman” in this instance has an ephemeral form: the concept of the Path. The mage must spend a similar amount of effort defining the Path as they would creating a Talisman’s physical form, and then they must use Prime 5 to infuse this ephemeral Pattern with magick. The Technocratic Union particularly favors this method. They’ve built a pipeline 2wherein Enlightened Scientists create fundamentally new science and then formalize it into Paths for Extraordinary Citizens. Those Citizens then bring the Path out into the world, making the new techniques first coincidental, and then mundane"

                                    This pipeline is the process of introducing things to the consensus via scientific means. in the modern world, Scientific advancements come from 3 places, Univeristies, Research institutions, For-profit companies. All universities are under the control of the NWO, for-profit companies are under Syndicate control, and research institutions could be Progenitor lead, Iteration X lead, or any other convention depending on the focus, it is said as much within each conventions revised book, which also talks about the rise of the Extraoridnary Citizen as a great success for the union. Scientific development is very much in the hands of the technocracy. Where else would scientists be within the setting of Mage?
