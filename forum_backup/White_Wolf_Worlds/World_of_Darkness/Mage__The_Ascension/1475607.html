                                  If that is what's happening, I think that it's a good thing, with one caveat. The upsides are that Onyx path is getting another official mage supplement “on the cheap”, in that all of the writing has already been done, and has even been given a couple of editorial passes — albeit from freelance editors (so an official editorial pass would be a good thing). The benefits to Charles is that his book will end up getting better publicity. As nice as it is to be able to publish through storytellers vault, fans of the world of Darkness aren't even aware that it's a thing; and many who are tend to be dismissive of it. So getting and official step of approval through re-release as an official book can only help sales.

                                  The caveat is the question of what happens for those of us who purchased PoP. Personally, if M20 sorcerer is indeed just an officially edited PoP, I would hope that those of us who purchased PoP would get the official book as an update to our PoP purchase. I would actually have to think twice about purchasing a book that I have already bought once. I would probably end up fighting the bullets and doing so anyway; but I wouldn't be happy about it.

                                  If M20 sorcerer is not “merely” an officially sanctioned version of PoP, either because it uses PoP as a baseline but makes significant changes or because it was developed independently of PoP, that's a whole other matter. As a purchaser of PoP, getting M20 Sorcerer will be lower on my priority list than usual; but in that case, I won't feel so bad about it. I might, however, feel a little bad for Charles, as one of the top sellers in the storytellers vault will have been taken out from under him. That said, that's one of the risks that one takes publishing Storyteller Vault material.

                                                  My thought about feeling bad for Charles if M20 Sorcerer isn't a revised PoP goes double for Falco if they end up borrowing material from his book that undercuts any new sales he might otherwise get. Though I suspect they won't; Onyx Path tends to be very considerate of the Storytellers Vault content creators.

                                                  What I would consider to be a better solution would be for them not to borrow anything from Falco, and to let him release an updated version of his Sorcerer's Companion that is designed to work with the official M20 Sorcerer rather than Sorcerer Revised. That would largely involve ditching the M20 chapter from the existing supplement, which is almost exclusively about adapting SRev for use with M20; but I could see Falco expanding on the remaining chapters, too, building on what M20 Sorcerer introduces that SRev didn't have.

                                                  As for S:PoP, if Onyx Path is using it as a baseline, the recently pre-released Operative's Dossier has something in it that I hope becomes a change in M20 Sorcerer: on SPoP pp.79–80, there are rules for mages creating sorcerous Paths. These are an adaptation of the Talisman creation rules found in the Book of Secrets. If mages creating Paths is a thing in M20 Sorcerer at all, I'd like to see it based off of the Terranorming process outlined in the Operative's Dossier rather than the Wonder creation rules. Conceptually, it strikes me as a better fit.

                                                  Another thing I'd like to see M20 Sorcerer do differently than SPoP is the dice pools for the Paths. SPoP introduced the notion of basing a Path's dice pool off of the sorcerer's Practice, a move that I agree with in principle; but in practice, this resulted in all of the dice pools being changed to strip out any reference to specific Abilities. For example, SRev's Path of Alchemy lists the dice pool as “Intelligence+Alchemy (Alchemical Procedures), Intelligence+Herbalism (Herbalism Procedures), Intelligence+Science (Advanced Chemistry Procedures)”, while SPoP states it simply as “Intelligence+Ability”. For M20 Sorcerer, I'd like to see SPoP's Practice-based Abilities notion implemented, but with each Path write-up being given at least one example (“coincidentally” aligning with the dice pools provided in the original material). The M20 Sorcerer write-up for Alchemy, for instance, might say something like “Intelligence + Ability (e.g., Esoterica (Alchemy) for traditional alchemy, Pharmacopeia for herbalism, or Science (Chemistry) for advanced chemistry)”.

                                                  That said, I'd be inclined to replace the use of Occult in dice pools with Esoterica: the Occult Knowledge in M20 is described as “a generalized comprehension of the mystical world as most Sleepers understand it” that is “often misleading, incomplete, or flat-out wrong”. By explicit contrast, Esoterica “represents the practical application of unusual fields”. I'd make an exception of the sorcerer's Practice is Gutter Magic, as the whole point of Gutter Magic is that its practitioners don't really know what they're doing, but somehow make it work anyway; though ironically, Occult isn't listed among Gutter Magic's Associated Abilities (something I'd tweak in M20 Sorcerer).

                                                  I might also include a box somewhere talking about how HH2, Kinfolk, and Ghosts Hunters Hedge Magic relate to Sorcery, with the main takeaway being that in general, “Hedge Magic” is a pejorative term used by sorcerers, who see it as Sorcery using the Practice of Gutter Magic. (It isn't, for what it's worth: in particular, the Path of Divination is wildly different in each of these books. Heck, Ghost Hunters classifies it as a Psychic Phenomenon rather than a Hedge Path.)

                                                  And speaking of Practices, I'd love to see each Path include a “Common Practices” entry that talks about good fits between Path and Practice.

                                                  My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness
