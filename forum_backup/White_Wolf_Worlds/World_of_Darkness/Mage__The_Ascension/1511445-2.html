                That said I think we do have to use that statement (“Acts like Life or Matter on spirits”) as an actual description of Spirit's capabilities. If we do, it closes up most of the holes in the Sphere by copying whatever pattern sphere best fits. Matter for ephemeral objects, Forces for ephemeral forces, and Life for spirits. It is consistent with what Matter and Forces say about transmuting between ephemera and the material

                It's not a problem free solution. We have to decide for ourselves which spirits count as simple life and which ones complex and we have to ignore what Spirit 5 says about forging ephemera. It largely works, though.

                The alternative is we ignore the line and try to extrapolate from what is written in the Spheres. Things get very odd that way. You can clearly change spirit into matter at rank 3, but you can't change spirit into spirit until rank 5... except as a part of the process of converting it to the material (at least by the wording in Forces). This method is counterintuitive, it directly contradicts the numerous times M20 says Spirit acts like Life and Matter, and I do not think this is how Spirit is meant to function.

                But...

                I suspect it may have been the intention at one point.

                Going back to the "create short-lived objects from ephemera" in Spirit 3, I think I misinterpreted the intended effect. I don't think this means create a physical object from ephemera, I think this is supposed to forge an ephemeral object. This tracks better with the wording which says the object is constructed “as if” it were material and that it fades away at the end of the duration. Now Spirit 3/Matter 3/Prime 2 is still a bad combination for creating an ephemeral object, but it is a reasonable combination for making a physical object and turning it into an ephemeral one. In a system where you can't create ephemeral objects until Spirit 5, this is a good workaround.

                            Two more oddities I've come across.

                            Seeing Weak Spots

                            Entropy has the ability to see weak points. This seems to come in three flavors:

                            Entropy 2+ to reduce the Durability of objects, armor ratings, etc. by 2 per success in addition to any damage. Pg 440 and (with diminished effect) pg 601

                            Entropy 1 to reduce difficulty of a future attack roll. Pg 416

                            Entropy 1 to do absolutely nothing. Entropy is still used to see weak points, but there are no mechanical effects and the rest of the spheres alone could accomplish the effect. Pg 415, pg 563.


                            Durability and Transmutation

                            It's not stated in the Casting Spells Step by Step section or in the quick charts, but apparently to transmute objects you need to overcome durability at a cost of 1 success per 1-2 durability (the section says 1, but then says Matter lets it be 2 and all object transmutations involve Matter. I haven't been able to find an equivalent requirement for overcoming soak in order to transmute living creatures, though transmuting vampires apparently requires overcoming health levels for... reasons.

                                I scanned through M20 for Spirit examples like I did for Prime:

                                Green is for effects that fit the writeup.
                                Red is for effects that have something wrong.
                                Purple is for abilities that are apparently intended but not clearly written in the Spirit writeup

                                417 – The Spirit sphere can be used to soak a spirit’s Rage based damage.

                                418 – See invisible spirits

                                418 – Spirit 2 is required to touch most spirits, even in the Umbra. Restated in multiple places. The way Spirit 2 is written it is easy to miss that this is an all the time thing.

                                418 - Spirit 3 allows for a Life 3 type attack but on spirits

                                418 – Spirit 2/Prime 2 acts like Life magic on umbral entities. Doesn’t really make sense. The way everything else is written, Spirit 2 alone should be like Life 2 for umbral entities.

                                443 – Spirit 3 to rouse the spirit of a drug.

                                443 – Spirit 3/Life 3 to rouse the spirit of a disease in someone else’s body. The Life requirement is sketchy. The only way this makes sense is if we consider the disease to be part of the pattern of the person, in which case it requires Life 4+.

                                475 – Spirit 3 to step sideways, Spirit 4 to open a gateway. Both effects convert the travelers to ephemera and back. However, apparently if someone opens a portal for a Mage, they still need Spirit 4 to use it.

                                477 – Mind 4 or 5/Spirit 3/Prime 2 for an Astral Soujourn. As I covered in the prime sphere, this essentially forges an ephemeral body and projects a mind into it.

                                478 – Spirit 2 to see across the gauntlet.

                                478 – Entropy 4/Spirit 3(4)/Life 2(3) for travel to the underworld by dying but staying tethered to your body. How well this works depends a great deal on what’s happening. The target is taken killed so that their spirit goes to the Underworld, while magic keeps their body alive so they can return. The Spirit requirements make sense because this is crossing into one of the Umbra’s. Theoretically it isn’t involved in getting the spirit to the Underworld, as that can happen without magic, but it is necessary to come back. Life is keeping the body alive while dead, essentially killing and healing at the same time. Life 2(3) is the right combo for both. Entropy is the odd one, but either it is contributing to keeping the body alive, which Life is already doing, or it is a necessary part of opening a path out of the Underworld. The second one actually works but there's no explanation of why it is the rank it is.

                                479 – Spirit 4 cannot ward against the Avatar Storm

                                479 – Prime 3/Spirit 2 to use a familiar or Totem to shield from the Avatar Storm. Spirit 4/Prime 3 to force one. Spirit 2 was probably chosen because that’s the level to manipulate the Gauntlet. Spirit 4 is necessary to command a spirit.

                                489 – Make a fetish.

                                508 – Life 3/Spirit 2 to heal a werecreature. Why this is only Spirit 2, I don’t know. It’s Spirit 3 to heal a spirit.

                                508 – Life 4/Spirit 4/Prime 3 to revive the recently dead. What is Prime doing? Life is too high a rank, it is presumably healing the body and that can happen at Life 3. That effect is no different than pushing somebody through a door.
                                                I wouldn’t even agree with the shrinking example. Only if you are somehow messing with the pattern itself would Life be necessary.

                                                This, by the way, is the main argument why Correspondence can't make direct attacks. Direct correspondence attacks would mean actual pattern alterations and Correspondence needs Pattern spheres to make pattern alterations.

                                                And there's several other cases more, where the description of the spheres themselves dont make sense, let alone the effects. Forces may be the most egregious example. So, the rules say that, the strenght of an effect is a matter of "number of successes" right?

                                                Well, what's the difference between the highly subjective "minor" and "major" forces? What's the difference between a gentle breeze and a hurricane if not for the intensity - which should be translated into 1 success versus 10+ successes for the same effect?

                                                I agree with you completely on this one. Most of Forces' limitations on size and successes appear to be hacks to get around problems with the system. It often seems to be more hacks than actual substance.

                                                I think that division of minor vs major forces is actually supposed to be in the game too. I’d have to check but I think it is in Revised but somehow was left out of M20.
