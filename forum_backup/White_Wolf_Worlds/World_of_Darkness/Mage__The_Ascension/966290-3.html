    Originally posted by Quantumboost View Post
    If the instructions are simple enough to put into an algorithm a Sleeper can reliably and repeatably perform to full effectiveness, it shouldn't qualify as Enlightened Science in the first place. It's just something Sleepers can do. I thought I already answered that, but apparently it fell out in the revisions. Actual Enlightened Science should entail some form of deep understanding or intuition.

    Maybe player instruments aren't normally going to be very complicated. Unless it works on its own with absolutely no adjustment and the character is just using it well, though, that is a flaw as well. Because that's how you make technological paradigms self-consistent within the setting and with available observation.

    Moreover, if you teach a Sleeper how to use your device with enough fidelity that they can operate it themselves... congratulations, you've just taught them a Path of Technosorcery. Maybe you showed them how to operate your array of high-energy weapons, and they learned the Path of Hellfire Missiles. Maybe you taught them how to use your manufactory, and now they have the Path of Nanofabrication (Enchantment) and can make a few low-end Devices of their own.

    Extraordinary Devices are also things. See Demon Hunter X for... basically everything in Strike Force Zero's arsenal; all of that shit is Extraordinary Devices, which any Sleeper who knows what they're doing can use. Same with the things listed as such in Iteration X Revised, and a bunch of equipment in Guide to the Technocracy. You want to make a 'ray gun' which requires no understanding of its inner workings to use, and doesn't cost the extensive time, effort, expertise, and cost as a proper Device? Make an Extraordinary Device. It won't be as good as Enlightened tech, but it'll work, and maybe your Scientist can make it work better on-the-fly (read: use it as an instrument for a Forces attack).

    Charms also exist, and are producible with Tass at Prime 2 (game term). If you can get by via giving your invention a limited number of charges, that's not a harsh requirement at all.

    That's an entire spectrum of things that can be retrofitted to make a coherent technological paradigm, if you're not actively opposed to fixing the seeming paradox after pointing it out.
    That's actually something I was intending on doing in a game once. Make Charms/Gadgets in the form of Ammo Packs or Energy Cells for energy weapons. Then distributing them to a characters unenlightened backup.
