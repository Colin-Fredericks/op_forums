    Enhancing Yourself to "heal like a garou"

    09-18-2016, 10:18 PM
    I'd like to see how some of y'all would handle this from a player:

    He would like to, through Sphere magick, give his character the ability to regenerate damage "like a werewolf."

    He has Life 4, Spirit 3, Prime 4.

    He has taken the heart from a garou and has been using it in his sanctum to try to figure some things out. He's come up with the idea of giving himself a second heart and he wants the following effect:

    Whenever hurt, heal one health level per round. (He also wants (eventually) to gain the "revive recently dead" power).


    Specific Questions:
    1. What are the sphere effects you'd require for the above power?
    2. Would you use the "enhancement" background to cover this (representing permanent modification to his pattern)?
    3. Player is trying to argue that he shouldn't get permanent paradox, but instead it should generate paradox when healing...
    4. What would you suggest for the mechanics of power like this?



    My take so far:

    I'm inclined to let the player do this, if only because I think it's a horrible idea. I told him we'd work on things... but I'm assuming that we'll start with creating a wonder and then making it a permanent change in his pattern.... (to figure out costs). I think this should then be handled through the Enhancement Background.

    It feels like, maybe, this power should require Time 4 as well... so that it only heals him when he's hurt...

    I'm considering letting him take Genetic Flaws instead of the Permadox. (I really don't like the idea of having this sort of power without a significant price (and not just in a dangerously difficult casting...)

    I'm also considering making him "Wyrm Tainted." (It's mostly a Mage game, but there is significant interaction with Garou...)

    I'm also thinking that if he wants to heal Ag damage, then it would cost Quintessence per health level...

              If you know what each Sphere does, it's easy to answer.

              To get "Garou healing", you need Life 5 + Spirit 5 + Prime 4/5.

              Life 5 - because you're mutating your pattern, giving it new properties. Read about it in "How do you DO that?", same way you can give someone innate abillity to breathe fire, teleport around, sidestep into Gauntlet (which is what Garou do), etcetera. Basically, giving innate abillities (like this regeneration) is Life 5.

              Now, Spirit 5 is shaping soul, molding it to your liking. Garou regeneration isn't only "genetics" or anything, it's a blessing from Gaia. You need to mold your soul to be blessed like that. After all, Garou are half spirits and have their own unique soul.

              Prime 4/5 is required to lock the Effect and possibly make you a travelling Wonder, or simply fuel the process thus avoid Pattern bleeding.

              You might even need Entropy 4/5. Entropy 4 is blessing physical Pattern, whereas Entropy 5 is capable of blessing the soul. Read above on why would you need to bless body and soul.

              It ain't easy, I know, but it's not supposed to be easy to give yourself blessing that was given to whole kind by Celestial.

                                              Speaking to flavor for a second - in the CoD Hunter game, there's a faction called the Cheiron Group that implants parts from monsters into its hunters to level the playing field ("Thuamatechnology"). One of them is sort of like this; they've found that werewolves often have "knots" where flesh has healed over on itself, and figured out how to implant them in a human. The hunter with one of these Regenerative Nodules can squeeze it and spend a Willpower to start healing a point of bashing a turn and a point of lethal every 15 minutes. To fuel it after use, they have to eat a hell of a lot of food or they take deprivation damage like they're starving.

                                              In that game, it's a four-dot Merit and not a "castable" thing, but that might give an idea how to roleplay this. Implant the werewolf heart in himself and hook it into all the normal veins and arteries. When he wants to heal, he starts up the werewolf heart and pumps blood through it for a while, then stops it when he's done.

                                              Mechanically you could still do whatever you want (implanted heart as a Wonder/Talisman that he fuels by injecting werewolf blood into the heart itself, implanted heart as a focus for somewhat less vulgar healing).
