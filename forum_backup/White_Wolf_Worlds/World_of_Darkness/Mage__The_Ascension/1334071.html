    09-05-2019, 11:16 AM
    How to go about Founding 'em? I've a player interested in being a Boni who has it as a goal to go about Founding a Minor House in Ex Misc, and I thought I'd pick at the Hive Mind to see how yinz would play it. I know they don't necessarily need a lot of members and their M.O. can be all sorts of queer subjects viewed through a Hermetic lens (I'm looking at you, House Skopos!).

    So what do you all think? Thoughts? Please & Thank You!

      For various reasons, I've never read the revised Order of Hermes book, but almost all of the "lesser" Houses listed as part of Ex Misc in the earlier material (as well as in Sorcerers Crusade) were all all major founding Houses who fell on hard times. (Golo is one of the main exceptions, IIRC.) The newer Houses that started as Ex Misc offshoots have their founding detailed in their various write ups, as far as I know.

      My general take is that in order to make an Ex Misc House, you have to be an already established member of some other House - and it usually helps if one or more of you are already at Adept or Master level - and be willing to give up membership in your existing House to go to Ex Misc and totally devote yourself to whatever your new take on The Art is. Actually starting a House in Ex Misc is fairly easy, and there are probably countless "Houses of One" throughout the past few centuries. The tricky part is attracting enough followers to actually be taken seriously by everyone else. I think once one gets about a dozen full members, you're probably big enough to start popping up in other people's mentions of what is going on in Ex Misc.

      One Ex Misc House I use in my games is House Hekate. They were originally inspired by a comment writer Deirdre Brooks made some twenty years ago, about "Thrice-Great Hekate/Hekate Trismegistus" (basically the idea of Hekate-Isis as a female counterpart to Thoth-Hermes), and later built on with some of the material about Hermetic witches in Ex Misc from some of the Ars Magica material. I put their founding in the late 70s/early 80s, from a coven of feminist Hermetics who decided to start their own House based on Feminism, Wiccan ritual, and fostering the growth of one's inner goddess. As this was part of the cultural zeitgeist of the time, they were able to find a number of suitable recruits over the next decade or so, and currently have maybe twenty or so full members as well as various apprentices and acolytes.

                  Basically I would have it need more than one cabal of Hermetics who ally with each other and start to form their own subparadigm or themes. The more power they have the quicker I can consider them a Minor House even if they are still hammering out their gimmick. Like I can see hermetics interesting in cloning, fossils and genetics, and start as one cabal with an interest in sciences. They then take apprentices and start using more magic to help with their research but not coming up with unique wonders or rotes yet, but if their apprentices choose to follow their lead they could say shift from a four mage Cabal to 5 four mage Cabals and start training a new generation of Apprentices and have the numbers to call themselves a Minor House. Later they can come up with Grimoires, unique rotes, and develop multiple chantries.

                  So basically I think once they have multiple chantries under the influence of a group that is defining themselves as something other then generic Hermetic they can start calling themselves a house. Most however don't reach the point of having controlling power over more then one chantry and their apprentices start to diverge.

                  Conversely 10 mages could be a minor house that has been around for over a Century but they are spread out between various Cabals and Chantries, but their history has developed a unique score of achievements magical and mundane.

                          I have been meditating on ideas for the Order of Hermes in 2020. I want to mix some Ars Magica lore into a chronicle I am planning, with a McGuffin of the OoH trying to reorganize (or assimilate) what remains of the Traditions. My first thought for that is a splinter faction from the Verbenae joining the Order as Ex-Miscellanea House Bjornaer. Then I got to thinking about how that would work out. And how does a group elevate to a Major House? I really digthe presentation of House Verditius in OoH: Revised, how they merged with House Thig and reclaimed their status.

                          So, without going further into what I want to do, here is my initial idea on how to do it...

                          * Membership in House Ex-Miscellanea is the defacto status of any Hermetic who is not a member of a Major House.
                          * A new House Ex-Miscellanea must consist of at least 12 Mages, representing at least 4 different Chantries or Cabals.
                          * A Major House must have at least 33 Mages, representing at least 7 different Chantries or Cabals.

                          Actual status then depends on a ruling of the Hermetic Grand Tribunal, based upon the aforementioned qualifications. There is a lot of politics involved, and perhaps exceptions made. The idea is that this ruling was made to allow some of the weakened founding Houses (Jerbiton, Criamon, etc) to consolidate their resources and stay alive. The idea is being used again tokickstart the Traditions to reviving. Or, failing that, replace their absence.

                                      It's mostly a matter of which ones you want to be the full Houses and which you want to be part of Ex Misc (or to have died out, or what have you). I have, for example, seen people who ignore the line up in the original Order of Hermes Tradition book and use the set up from Ars Magica (sans the Tremere, for obvious reasons). I've also seen people who have thrown out all of the Ars Magica Houses in favor of those of their own creation. Personally, I usually go with the line up from the original Tradtion book, with Bonisagus, Flambeau, Guernicus, and Tytalus as the surviving old guard, Fortunae, Shaea, and Thig as the new blood, and Ex Misc as a mix of fallen Houses (the original Bjornaer, Criamon, Jerbiton, Mercere, Merinita, and Verditius; the recently fallen Janissaries) and of original creations (Hong Lei, Hekate, Futhark, Kukulkan, Natura, and others), as well as the refugees of House Batini (who are actually almost all sleeper agents for that Craft). I tend to waffle about House Solificati being a full House vs being part of Ex Misc like the Batini.


                                      (Officially, the original Order of Hermes Tradition book listed Criamon, Jerbiton, Merinita, and Verditius as being part of House Ex Misc, with Mage: The Sorcerers' Crusade listing them and Mercere as part of Ex Misc, and a large number of House Bjornaer having joined the Verbena. The Tremere were kicked out around the time of Dark Ages: Vampire, and the founding House Diedne were destroyed around 1000AD. However, Guide to the Traditions picks up a plot thread from Changeling's Isle of the Mighty sourcebook about some Diedne seeking sanctuary in Arcadia and they or their decedents recently returning. The only problem being that whatever it is that's come back claiming to be the Diedne are no longer really human. And are really mad at Flambeau and Guernicus/Quaesitori.)

                                              For what it's worth, my own head canon on what happened to the founding Houses:
                                              • Bjornaer: As the other of the non-Roman Houses, most of them were wary of the rest of the Order after what happened to House Diedne; At the Grand Convocation, a large number defect to the Verbena, rejoining the mystic shapeshifting sect their founder originally came from, and the remaining members are demoted into Ex Miscellanea.
                                              • Bonisagus: Still a major House and power player, owing to their twin focus on advancing the boundaries of the Art and being really good at politics.
                                              • Criamon: Never all that large to begin with, in the lead up to and during the Grand Convocation, they are demoted to Ex Miscellanea as punishment for not foreseeing the rise of the Order of Reason and the Ascension War.
                                              • Diedne: Owing to their non-Roman origins, they were never fully accepted by most of the other Houses and tended to be a bit insular; The Tremere take full advantage of this and frame them for Infernalism as a way to distract the other Houses - especially Guernicus - from their own vampirism research; A handful of survivors seek sanctuary in the Fae realms, with their final fate unknown.
                                              • Flambeau: Still a major House and power player, owing to their martial might and near-relentless pursuit of the Ascension War, as well as the fact that, for all their faults, they are also relentlessly honest and everyone generally trusts them.
                                              • Guernicus: Still a major House and power player, owing to their role as enforcers of Order law and protocol, and their not entirely undeserved reputation for being incorruptible.
                                              • Jerbiton: Looked down upon by some for their continuing interests in mundane culture, art and politics; This was only aggravated when some members opted to join the newly formed Order of Reason; The Renaissance era Courtier Wars (involving Jerbiton, the High Guild, Ksirafal, the Golden Circle, and others) takes a heavy tole and they are demoted into Ex Miscellanea shortly after the Grand Convocation.
                                              • Mercere: Owing to their founder somehow losing his ability to use The Art, they were always viewed as being something of a second class House; In spite of this, they focused on keeping the Order as a whole running smoothly, and voluntarily joined Ex Miscellanea at the Grand Convocation in order to smooth over the demotion of several other Houses.
                                              • Merinita: Originally focused on the magic of nature before taking a somewhat obsessive interest in the doings of the Fae and their magics; the House was horribly mauled by Wyndgarde's March in Britain and other attacks, with the survivors being demoted to Ex Miscellanea (and some later splitting off into a new House, Natura, to resume their founder's original focus on nature magic).
                                              • Tremere: Always fixated on the accumulation of personal power through any means necessary, the House fell to corruption as the founder turned himself into an undead blood-god and dragged at least half of his House with him; The entire House was banished from the Order, with those Hermetic loyalists being adopted into other Houses (most often Tytalus) or into Ex Miscellanea (and forbidden to take on students).
                                              • Tytalus: Still a major House and power player, mostly owing to their collective competitive streak and pursuit of personal perfection which has caused every attempt to demote them to end in failure.
                                              • Verditius: Moderately looked down upon for their insistence of focusing their Art through objects and devices, a sizable number of apprentices and younger members defected to the Order of Reason during its formation; This resulted in the House being demoted to Ex Miscellanea during the Grand Convocation as punishment.

                                              Voltaire, "Tolerance" (1764)

                                                  Thank you for sharing this material! I have the two OoH books on PDF. I like the second one much more than the first, but the original is full of little gems that are easy to miss.I love mising in a bit of Ars Magica with Mage. Just a bit for flavor. I try to mix some Mage in my Ars Magica too, but I have to be sneaky about it. There tends to be some animosity towards White Wolf in the Ars Community.

                                                  Playing with the little differences intrigues me, and makes it easy to provide delightful surprises to veterans of this or that game. For example, there is a fundamental difference in the Ars version of House Bjornaer that would be fun to surprise players with. In that game, the Bjornaer sort of detest shape shifting. They have one Heartbeast form they can change to without effort. In their "Paradigm", they are not truly changing shape. Both forms are their true actual identity. Magic cannot tell the difference, and other metaphysical unique properties along those lines. There is a whole slew of specific mysteries to augment this form. Using spells to take on other forms is considered "trashy".
                                                  Oh, and they cannot bind a Familiar.

                                                  The part that makes the Ars version of Bjornaer interesting in a White Wollf setting is their relation to Werewolves. There was a long bloody feud between the Werewolves and Barna & her followers. There is a strongly implied relation between them.

                                                  I am getting a crazy plot twist idea from this. Started writing it, but it is still to amorphous at the moment.
