        Originally posted by Enginseer-42 View Post

        Essentially. They're logical extrapolations of the rules of mage. Glamour is tass with a particular resonance. As is Vitae and Gnosis. By changing that resonance, it is no longer Glamour but simply Tass. Alternatively, one could CREATE glamour if they knew what resonance to give the quintessence when creating tass and had the ability to.

        Prime 2 makes weapons that extend into other realms of existence, as the dreaming is one such realm....

        I do not recall where I've seen mention of the dreaming before, however I have seen it. It's a realm. The MISTS are an entity, however like any entity they can be countered with sufficient application of magic. In this case a simple mental defense or mental backup.

        Like every single member of Iteration X has. and some other conventions besides.
        I'm not sure changing the resonance of Glamour would stop it being Glamour. Glamour itself could have all kinds of resonance depending on who was using it. It's like how the Gnosis can have different kinds of resonance depending on what the Garou has been through. Wyrm Taint being a particularly strong and volatile one.

        But Wyrm tainted Gnosis is still Gnosis. The resonance doesn't stop it being so.

        See, I go with the idea that Glamour, Gnosis, Vitae, and Pathos, all of this to the mage is just Quintessence under different names. The TASS is just how it's stored, so Blood for the Vamps, and Ectoplasm for both the Wraiths and the Werewolves. But essentially it's all Quintessence.

        "Creating" Glamour should be as simple as having quintessence to give them. The Verbena Book even talks about a small coven of Verbena who try to feed a starving Chimeric creature some of their own quintessence to restore its glamour.

        The only thing about taking Quint from other Supernaturals is you'll need more than Prime to do it. Vamps need Prime, Life and Matter. Werewolves would need Prime and Spirit (and maybe Life). Wraiths could be just Prime and Spirit (though you could argue you'd need Entropy as well, especially if they were a Spectre). while Fae would need Prime and Mind (and possibly Spirit).

        I think it's intentionally designed for sucking away Quint from other Supernats to be a fairly difficult thing to do.
