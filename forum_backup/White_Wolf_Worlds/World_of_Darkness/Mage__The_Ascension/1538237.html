    Quintessence and Tass Usage

    07-08-2024, 12:33 PM
    As usual, I am sorry if this is a conversation that has been done to death, but I want to double-check what I've managed to figure out over the last few days. And maybe the next person trying to find these answers via web-search will have a bit easier time!

    I'm hoping to get to run a game of M20 soon, so I'm trying to get a more comprehensive handle on the mechanics. One of the things that's been a bit confusing has been discussions around accessing Quintessence with the Prime Sphere. Between M20 Core and HDYDT(yes, I know, but I work with what I've got), it feels like there are some ambiguities about what you can do with each given level of Prime when it comes to using Quintessence and Tass.

    I think one of my main points of confusion comes from conflicting statements that I've found in M20 and HDYDT regarding Tass (and also Periapts):

    Originally posted by M20 Core, p.520
    A beginning study of Prime allows the mage to perceive and channel Quintessence from Nodes, Tass, Wonders, and magickal Effects. She may spot energetic ebbs and flows, can sense and at least try to read Resonance and Synergy signatures, and could also absorb Quintessence into her personal Pattern. Mages without at least one dot in Prime cannot absorb Quintessence beyond their Avatar Background rating. A Prime-skilled mage, however, may do so.
    So it sounds like you need at least Prime 1 in order to make use of Tass. It also doesn't help that they seem to be using the words "absorb" and "channel" in a casual sense here, while also using them in a slightly more official/technical capacity when referring to specific Effects using Prime 3, on M20 Core p.510.

    But then, in HDYDT:

    Originally posted by HDYDT, p.45
    Under normal circumstances, a mage with Tass, a Periapt, or a Matrix on his person can access that battery’s Quintessence reflexively – that is, without using an additional Effect. If that battery is within reach but not touching the character, he can use a Prime 2 Effect to get the energy. And if the battery is some distance away, he can use Correspondence 2/ Prime 2 in order to draw upon the energy within the Periapt.
    (Emphasis mine)

    Now it sounds like any Mage is capable of using Tass that they have on their person, without any ranks in the Prime Sphere (it also seems to imply that the "Employ Periapt" Effect listed on p.510 of M20 Core specifically refers to using over distance).

    So, the questions are as follows:

    1) Can any Mage, without the Prime Sphere, consume edible Tass in order to add its Quintessence to their own Pattern? I feel like this is an idea I've encountered before, but I haven't found any specific rules or clarifications in M20, so far (the only specific memory I have is drinking Vitae for Quint from Blood Treachery, but I can't recall if that was supposed to be exceptional).

    2) Do you need at least Prime 1 in order to use (inedible) Tass at all, or can any Mage use the Quintessence inside of Tass as long as they have it on their person?

    3) What rank of Prime do you need to absorb the Quintessence out of (inedible) Tass and directly into your Pattern? Does that require Prime 3, or is Prime 1 enough as long as you are holding the Tass and remain within the Quintessence/turn limits imposed by the Avatar background? It kind of sounds like the Prime 3 reference on M20 Core p.520 is talking about absorbing at a distance, like the quote about Periapts from HDYDT mentioned above, but I'd appreciate a second opinion.

    Part of why I'm so focused on Tass is because I encountered some threads pointing out how working for Tass is one way that Cabals of Mages without access to a Node or high level Prime magic can still get the Quintessence they need for their spellcasting. The details of how said Mages can use the Tass they worked for are kind of important in that context.

    I'm looking for a mixture of M20 interpretations from more experienced players, but I'd also appreciate if people could point out older sources that provide better explanations. After getting some feedback, I'll probably try and write out a list of how you can use Quint and Tass with each rank of the Prime Sphere, hopefully something folks can easily refer to in the future if I don't make a mess of it

          Well, I think the rule for Nodes is that a mage can meditate in the presence of one to absorb Quint even without Prime, but the limit is the Avatar rating. With Prime 1 there isn't a limit anymore. Converting Tass, though, still takes Prime 3. While the description of Prime 1 does indicate otherwise, the first rank of Spheres generally doesn't augment a mage's capabilities like that. General Tass conversion should remain Rank 3.

          That said, using Prime 1 to absorb Tass by eating it (or in some other appropriate way consuming it) makes a lot of sense thematically. I think it might even be a rule in previous editions or in Awakening. If you want to rule that Prime 1 allows a mage to absorb the Quintessence of Tass when it is used/consumed in a dramatically appropriate mundane way, that would be quite reasonable.

              When it comes to Tass, how you use it is important.

              My understanding (I don't remember if and where I read it) is that Tass can be directly used in spellcasting, as long as it is done in an appropriate way. And that would make its Resonance very important to consider.

              So, let's say you have a vial of Vampire Blood counting as Tass, and you want to make mind-altering spell. One way to do so could be to inscribe runes in the tied up target's head with red ink, and then you incorporate the Vitae by using it as said ink. The resonance of the Vitae would be relevant in the casting, though, helping or hindering depending on what kinds of thoughts you want to create.

              This would be a Prime-Free use of Tass. Vampire Blood is a special case in that a Mage that consumes it will keep it as Vitae, which is Quintessence in itself, but most other Tass can't be consumed for later use without Prime.

When you "liberate" the Quint, it is in a extremely "concentrated" form, which can be captured and used. However, if not used, it just dissipates into the overall "cosmic background radiation" that is too "dispersed" to be usable, unless you use Prime 4 to "suck up" from that same "cosmic background radiation". Prime 4, in this instance, would function as a "condensator" of that "cosmic background radiation", concentrating it in a form once again usable)

                              As long as the Tass is in the form of one of your instruments and you can incorporate it in to your practice. You can just use the quintessence it contains. So if you have a node that produces quintessence apples, you can turn them in to cyder and use said cyder as an offering to the gods. If you have a cave node which make chalky deposits as tass, you can gather up that chalk and grind it to a fine powder which you can then sprinkle over your ritual circle to incorporate that tass in to your effect.

                              You do not need to have quintessence in your wheel in order to use it, you can just use the quintessence from the tass. That is one of the reasons tass is used as currency in mage society, it's a way for mages to make use of quintessence even if they have Avatar 0 and now sense of Prime
                              Speak

                              The important thing is that you have to destroy the tass to release the quintessence from it in to your effect.
