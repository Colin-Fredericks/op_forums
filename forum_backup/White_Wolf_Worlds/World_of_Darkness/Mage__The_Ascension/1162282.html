              With Life 2 you can get a nearby tree to grab or swipe at someone or cause insects to swarm.                               Here's another question: what are these two characters' political affiliations? What groups, if any, do they belong to?

                              Depending on the answer, it could mandate certain Spheres, on account of how groups have their particular Specialty Spheres. M20 allows more flexibility in this regard than previous editions (which had only one Sphere to choose per sect), but it's still a choice between a certain number of them. If the witch is a Verbana, they HAVE to take either Life or Forces for one Sphere dot. A Hollow One or independent witch, though, could choose any Specialty Sphere they wanted. Same with the religious character, which could be a Chorister, Templar, a different group, or none at all.
