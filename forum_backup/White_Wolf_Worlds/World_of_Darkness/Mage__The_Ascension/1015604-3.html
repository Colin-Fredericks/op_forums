                                This thread has helped me understand what I think of when I think of the Hypothetical Average Perceiver. To some extent without realizing it I was ignoring the fact the HAP isn't just in the area looking around, they're actually perceiving the actual location of the magic. But what that means can vary. Most people would agree that you can conjure something in your pocket Coincidentally under the gaze of the HAP, that the HAP isn't perceiving the inside of your pocket even though that's where the Effect is happening. Similarly the average person who is close enough to someone in a crowd to see him--if the crowd is sufficiently dense and active--is much more likely to be occluding the mage than perceiving him. In specific circumstances--with high numbers, lots of overall distraction, good stealth rolls, and a geographical location where the bar for Coincidence happens to be lower, it would be possible to teleport... maybe further along in the same crowd.

                                Because the HAP perceives the Effect and not the subject of the Effect, the HAP should follow the subject to the new location. If the surroundings are completely different then it's Vulgar. This tends to limit the range. There are--I think--reasonable kludges that help limit teleportation further. We acknowledge even under a mostly HAP system that there are things that are inherently, objectively Vulgar such as crossing the Gauntlet or healing Aggravated damage. One could decree that you can't Coincidentally teleport farther than you could travel by foot in half an hour,* say, unless you're doing something like traveling to the kind of Al-i-Batin meta-maze mentioned earlier. In that case it would have to be a temporary Effect that would reverse before you could possibly leave the chamber or the chamber would have to be inescapable (without more Correspondence Effects) at the other end. By setting these conditions ahead of time, retroactive Vulgarity no longer becomes necessary.

                                Admittedly this is mashing together several heterogynous ideas to arrive at circumstances where teleporting coincidentally (particularly in crowds since that's where the conversation went) is sometimes Coincidental but not reliably so and not a free pass to go wherever you want to go. To me, that is fine. Trying to use perfectly objective and consistent rules for something inherently subjective doesn't make sense to me. I place more stock in intuitive coherence. And I'm with Saiku in one sense, the ultimate goal is gameplay. That means balancing the need to limit characters with the need to let them do cool stuff.

                                *Here you're using a bit of HOP as a kludge, by taking the perspective of the whole world into account. Or, looked at another way, envisioning the HAP seeing the person on live television a few minutes after running into them in person. The distance would have to be somewhat plausible by that test.
