    Increasing Soak- Relearning the Game

    03-31-2020, 04:39 PM
    A player wants to use Matter and Prime to strengthen his clothes to act as armor and allow him to soak Aggravated damage. Its seems doable with Matter 2/Prime 2, but I am unsure.

    Does this effect create additional health levels (like Skeletal Enhancement), create additional soak dice, or increase an attackers difficulty? Can any of these be picked?

    What if he uses a ritual to buy the necessary duration and effects to make it last the whole story? Is this a trinket and requires Prime 2, or does the extended duration mean he can carry it with him?

    Can he use a similar effect to sharpen his knife (Matter 2/Prime 2) to deal lower the difficulty or add extra damage levels? Both? Neither? Again, can he increase the duration and just take it with him?

      It would add increased soak dice. Enhanced health would be if one used Life, or Life + Prime to also soak aggravated damage.
      Increasing attack dificulty could be done with Forces (blurring the Mage, making them appear slightly off to where they are etc.) while making dodging easier would be using things like Correspondence 1 or Time 1.

      As for the question regarding the Trinket, yes, the enhanced clothes would be a trinket - and the effect could be made permanent.

      The sharpening of the knife would add extra damage dice, probably.

      The duration can be done as per the Damage & Duration table - and as I said, could be made permanent, at least when it comes to the enhanced clothes. The knife, technically too - but it would probably still dull over time, just like the clothes would get shredded over time one way or the other..

              Making normal clothing act as armor just takes Matter 3. You can add Prime 2 too, but that would only protect your clothing-armor from direct attacks; armor generally applies to soaking Agg if armor would apply at all, so adding Prime just to have armor apply to Agg is unnecessary (though not pointless).

              The default option would be to increase soak dice, and would certainly classify the clothing as a trinket. Trinkets don't require Prime 2 if you can do the effect(s) you want without Prime. Trinkets are also generally permanent (Book of Secrets has a full crafting system if your ST doesn't want to use the normal duration chart rules and success just makes a permanent Trinket).

              Increasing health levels or increasing an attacker's difficult would be beyond just Matter to accomplish. If you want to increase health via armor, you'd need to add an effect that would redirect the damage from you to your clothing (thus risking destroying it) if you're not going to just use Life magic to do it. Increasing the difficulty to hit you would likewise be an effect beyond just making your clothing into armor as you'd need to add something that actually impedes the ability to hit you (Entropy or Correspondence are good choices here, though Forces can work too). You're also getting beyond Trinkets here, as these start to require the active effects found in more potent Wonders.

              A Trinket knife made with Matter 3 could probably get a -1 diff to attack/defend or +1 or +2 damage dice to represent how high quality it would be. Doing extra damage levels, applying Prime 2 to make it do Agg damage, and so on would elevate it beyond being a Trinket into more advanced Wonder making.

                      The game also has a bias towards offense to avoid situations where it takes lots and lots of rounds to do anything between offense and defense are too evenly matched and nobody ever gets an advantage.
