The setting has other sources of inspiration, like the already mentioned trope of human decaying with time and the End of History mood of the 90's. What we're specifically observing in this thread is the result of two specific inspirations for the setting not exactly meshing very well, maybe. IF you take this aspect of Hermetic Paradigm at face value, which as everyone I agree to not be the case, no matter how influential it was.
