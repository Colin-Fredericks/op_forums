    Originally posted by Ramnesis View Post
    Note that seeing the strength of the gauntlet should include seeing an actual hole in the gauntlet (aka a Shallowing), which for some reason is stated to require conjunctional effects. Perhaps there are things about a shallowing that can’t be determined without help, but actually seeing one should be purely in the domain of Spirit.
    My view on this is that the Shallowing itself (whatever form it takes) and the effect of it's existence (the Gauntlet being zero) aren't the same thing. A Shallowing generally refers to a persistent if not permanent Gauntlet zero area, not something like a Garou or Mage using a power to open a hole in the Gauntlet that lets others pass through without rolling for a few minutes. Given most durable Shallowings are "attached" to something like a Node, I think this would be much like your Sense Spiritual Component underlined interpretation (something I find the most reasonable given the rest of the text): Spirit alone can detect the reduced Gauntlet and with time and study could deduce the form of the Shallowing. Conjunctional Spheres let you detect the full nature of the Shallowing direct.


    This is not really my specialty, but as I recall Werewolf has certain realms hidden in the layers of the Gauntlet itself. If there are any that apply to Mage, a mage would be able to see them with enough successes.
    "Realms" in the Gauntlet itself are a fairly rare thing to exist. Usually it's either something like the Bastets' Den Realms - which exist in the Penumbra but can't normally be found or accessed from that side and can normally only be found by figuring out their physical entrance on the material plane - or any patterns that have been caught in the Gauntlet during transit and are stuck in the web until something else can free them (which can, in theory, apply to "pocket realms" created by various effects, not just supernatural entities crossing the Gauntlet). I think being able to notice a "flaw" in the Gaunlet where something is stuck is definitely within Spirit 1's purview, but I'm not sure Spirit 1 alone should be able to find specific things caught in the Gauntlet, or know what an encapsulation contains, by itself.

    See Aura of Unseen
    This feels like a bit of a stretch. "Sight" is of course a misnomer in the sense that mages are not just limited to their eyes, but the default rule is that you need to be able to sense something to apply magic to it. If you can't see it (though of course Spirit 1 lets you see a lot of things you can't normally see already), you can't scan it's aura. Having the aura extend beyond the subject enough to give the target away isn't really in-keeping with anything stated in the text IMO, and being able to override stealth powers (esp, since aura-senses aren't part of many WoD games, so those games don't have any discussions of hiding auras or powers that specifically do so) feels unintended at best to what Spirit 1 describes. Given that other Spheres are very explicit when they can find things that cannot normally be sensed, I'm not sure this reading holds up well.

    Sense Spiritual Component (Spirit Sense)

    Contradiction Resolution

    With this power a mage can determine if an object has a spiritual component. Fetishes are given as an example of something that does. However, so would awakened objects and possessed creatures, which are for some reason locked behind conjunctional effects. It is possible that Spirit Sense is restricted to objects, so maybe it can’t see possession without Life, but awakened objects should still count.

    The other possibility is that detecting the spiritual component requires close examination of the target, whereas the conjunctional effects make it obvious for all relevant objects/creatures in the area.
    I side with the underlined version. Given that - while not quite as stressed as it is in Werewolf - the WoD is an animist world, the vast majority of things have a "spiritual component." Unawakened objects have spiritual components that are dormant too after all. This would also in many ways override aura-sense otherwise, because you could sense the Beast/P'o/chimerical nature/spirit-half/etc. that is inherent in most other WoD supernaturals directly instead of having to infer from your aura roll.

    A Fetish is something with an extra spirit shoved into it (maybe even more than one) that is only bound by magical means and that binding can be broken and the spirit freed/left without its comfy home. Full spiritual possession fuses the spirit and subject into one new entity, and awakened objects don't have any new "components" they didn't have before. I think there's definitely room for a range of necessary Spheres to instantly recognize something for what it truly is, instead of having to rely on impressions and existing knowledge to puzzle it out.

                Spirit 2

                •• Touch Spirit/ Manipulate Gauntlet

                Perception moves to contact. The Spirit-savvy mage can now reach through the Gauntlet for a turn or two; call across the Gauntlet; speak to spirit entities, or touch them for a brief moment; and thin or thicken the local Gauntlet. In the latter case, each success lowers or raises the Gauntlet rating by +1 for each success rolled. (Four successes would raise or lower it by four levels, for example.) That said, a human mage cannot lower the Gauntlet to less than 4 within the mortal world.

                By adding other Spheres, that mage could project thoughts across the barrier (Mind 4 or 5); stir up elemental disturbances within the Otherworlds (Forces 2 or higher); imbue material objects with ephemeral power (Matter 2); drain Essence from a spirit (Prime 3); or help other living creatures sense or contact the spirit realms (Life 2).

                Reach Across Gauntlet (Manipulate Gauntlet)

                While a mage can’t pass through the gauntlet at this rank, he can reach across, cast across it, and call across it. Indeed, this seems very much like the Spirit equivalent of Correspondence 2.

                Call across the gauntlet is an odd word choice, but would seem to include speaking and making connections. It is definitely different from speaking to spirits as that is a different power in this list.

                Ambiguity Resolution – Reach

                In Correspondence, Reach Through Space includes being able to move small objects across that distance. Since the same terminology is used here there is at least the plausible reading that a mage can move small objects or creatures (housecat or smaller) across the Gauntlet. Whether or not this would convert them to ephemera depends on if moving into the umbra automatically does this (which is apparently a matter of debate in universe according to Stepping Sideways on pg 88 for whatever that is worth)


                Will a Mr. Umbral Entity Please Come to the Courtesy Desk

                Call Across Gauntlet could include putting out a request or invitation for an entity to come. Actually forcing it to come would be Spirit 4, but the request could reasonably happen at Spirit 2. For what it is worth HDYDT seems to think this is the purpose of this ability, though take that with a grain of salt.


                Hide Contraband

                One use for moving things across the gauntlet is quickly stashing problematic items. That said there’s nothing keeping a local spirit from taking the item so a wise mage will use this power carefully.

                Speak With Spirits (Touch Spirit)

                This is a deceptively important power. The section on spirits themselves makes it clear that they communicate in an entirely different way than people and that unless they themselves can translate, Spirit 2 is necessary to communicate with them. That’s not directly stated in Spirit, but the wording here is consistent.

                Dig beneath the surface, though, and this isn’t just a translation of spirit speech, though, it is a translation of spiritual impressions and meanings. So this power isn’t just limited to translating what a spirit says, it also can translate information about what the spirit is or even translate what it is thinking, provided the mage has ways of finding that out.


                Change Spirit Appearance

                This is a bit of a stretch, but spirits often appear in different forms that fit the spirit’s nature and the expectations of the viewer. In some ways, how it appears is another form of communication and so can be translated accordingly. Provided the new image fits the spirit’s nature and doesn’t mechanically change the scene, a mage may be able change the spirit’s appearance for himself and anyone he shares this with.


                Speak Without Spirits

                In theory at least, two mages with Spirit 2 can communicate in spiritual impressions in a way that no one without Spirit 2 could understand.

                Act on Spirit (Touch Spirit)

                This power lets a mage touch and affect spirits regardless of their state. This is also the ability to enable spells to affect spirits (if the spell couldn’t already).


                Quit Hitting Yourself

                I’m struggling to find a use for Touch Spirit that isn’t obvious, but bullying a spirit by smacking it around with its own limbs, tentacles, or other appendages has a certain stupid charm. If Cobra Kai was an actual Akashic faction I could see this being a rote they would use.

                Gauntlet Thickness (Manipulate Gauntlet)

                A mage can also alter the thickness of the gauntlet up or down. The two biggest impacts of this are that spirits will have a harder or easier time crossing, and most Spirit effects are based on the thickness of the gauntlet.

                This is technically self-referential. The difficulty should be the current gauntlet rating, and the spell is directly affecting that. That said there’s not really a need to get into that unless the table really wanted to. What does matter is that if a mage thickens the gauntlet it is harder for another mage to undo that change, while if a mage thins the gauntlet it is easier for another mage to thicken it again.


                Breach Gauntlet Pocket

                Since the base power is technically called Manipulate Gauntlet, not Thicken Gauntlet, there's the potential for affecting more than just gauntlet rating. If a mage managed to find a Bastet Den Realm (thanks to Heavy Arms for the reminder of them) or other like pockets in the Gauntlet, this power may let him alter or manipulate the way into them and thus break in.


                Open Existing Portal

                A mage can't open a path to the Umbra by himself yet, but he should be able to open one that already exists, or close it for that matter.


                Selective Gauntlet Protection

                It may be possible to use this power like Correspondence 2’s Minor Ward to selectively change the gauntlet difficulty for certain creatures. As with wards, setting those conditions may take other spheres.

                Conjunctional Effects:
                Imbue Object (??? + ???)

                Ambiguity Resolution or Lack Thereof

                I’m not really sure why a mage would want to do this. It’s not wrong, per se, I just don’t know what the practical outcome of the effect is. One possible result is that the object can touch spirits, but that’s speculation.

                This idea doesn’t come up again within Spirit, so this is not a power that is one rank higher when done to life forms. Either it is only possible with objects, or it is possible with any pattern sphere at this level.

                Drain Essence From Spirit (Touch Spirit + Pull Quintessence From Pattern)


                If a mage can’t mundanely kill the target of a Prime 3 sacrifice he needs an appropriate sphere to apply the effect to something. At least, that’s why I think the Prime 3 power needs Matter for objects but not Life for living things. So here it needs Spirit 2 to touch spirits.

                Extend Spirit Senses and/or Contact to Others (??? + ???)

                Ambiguity Resolution​

                There's a lot opened up in a very few words.

                There are two powers extended here: senses and contact. Senses clearly means taking the sensory powers of Spirit 1 and giving them to another creature. This appears to say that Life 2 is enough to extend these senses to any living creature including people. At the very least most who read the text would be thinking of giving people the ability to see spirits, though I am sure there are some niche applications for crabs that can see across the gauntlet.

                Contact has to mean either speaking or touching, both of which are Spirit 2 powers. Again, the clear text seems to say sharing this ability is Life 2, regardless of what kind of creature it is shared with.

                I am not at all sure why the Spirit 1 power needs an extra rank to be shared but the Spirit 2 power does not.

                There’s no indication this Life ability is restricted to Spirit, by the way. And while perhaps only Correspondence and Time have contact powers that could be shared, all the spheres have sensory powers.

                                    Spirit 3

                                    ••• Pierce Gauntlet/ Step Sideways/ Rouse & Lull Spirit

                                    Now the mage can cross over, transmuting his living tissue to ephemera. He may carry a few material possessions, although transmuting them as well raises both the difficulty of the roll and the number of successes required for the trip. (Normal clothing and items raise both factors by +1; bulky clothing and items raise them by +2.) That traveler must step sideways on his own; bringing large items or other people across demands a higher Spirit Rank. Meanwhile, a combined Spirit 3/ Mind 2 Effect allows the mage to read Resonance, Synergy, and other spiritual energies (Essence, a spirit’s place within a hierarchy, etc.).

                                    At this Rank, a mortal mage can also harm an Umbral entity as if he was using Life 3 against that entity. While Spirit 2 allows the mage to touch that entity, Spirit 3 lets him actually damage its ephemeral Pattern’s integrity the way that Life 3 damages a physical creature’s form.

                                    By combining this Rank with Matter 3 and Prime 2, the mage can also create short-lived objects from ephemera; such creations must be constructed as if they were material things, and they fade away at the end of the Effect’s duration. Finally, this Rank helps the mage rouse the slumbering spirits within objects or places, or else put active spirits to sleep. (See Awakening Substances in Chapter Nine, p. 443).

                                    Step Sideways (Step Sideways)

                                    This power is as iconic to Spirit as casting from a distance is to Correspondence or casting ex nihilo is for Prime.

                                    Contradiction Resolution

                                    Notably, there is some contention between Prime and Spirit on this. Spirit says that clothing and carried items automatically make the journey with the mage (though at increased difficulty) while Prime lets a mage consecrate items so they pass with him automatically. Presumably Consecration bypasses both the size restrictions and the difficulty increases, as well as letting the mage bring a creature across the umbra at Spirit 3.


                                    Transmute Ephemera (???)

                                    While it is only described as a part of stepping sideways, Spirit 3 can transmute physical patterns into ephemera or vice versa. However, this does not work the same as other transmutation effects.

                                    Unlike other transmutation effects, this does not require the pattern sphere appropriate to the thing being changed. A mage does not need Life 4 to convert himself in the stepping sideways process, in either direction. It is possible this rule only applies when Stepping Sideways and other conversions would require Pattern Spheres.

                                    I am putting this in as a separate effect from Stepping Sideways because one of the conjunctional effects (create short lived ephemeral objects) appears to use it that way. I’ll get into this when I cover that effect, but one way or another it is using Spirit 3 for transmuting in some capacity.

                                    Said conjunctional effect also implies that there is a limit on transmutation that other spheres don’t have, but I’ll cover that when we get to the effect.


                                    Force Materialization

                                    If indeed a mage can transmute ephemera to physical matter then he can force a kind of materialization on spirits at this level. Since it takes Spirit 4 to move something else across the gauntlet, this would only work on spirits on the same side as the mage.


                                    Harm Spirit (???)


                                    This is one of the simpler powers of this rank. At this level a mage can directly attack a spirit’s pattern. This is explicitly equivalent to a Life 3 attack on a living pattern.


                                    Rouse or Lull Spirit

                                    As per usual, attacks don’t have to be direct damage attacks. The example given of rousing or lulling a spirit is a good example of a non damaging spirit attack spell.


                                    Conjunctional Effects

                                    Sense Resonance, Synergy, and Other (??? + Read Surface Thoughts?)


                                    I’m a little unclear on this one. Prime 1 can explicitly read Resonance and Synergy, so it is possible that most of this is just a much more expensive alternative method to accomplish the same thing. However, this also lets a mage divine things about a spirit such as its place in a spiritual hierarchy, which is a completely different thing. Overall, it seems like two different effects mashed together.

                                    Conflict Resolution - Two different effects

                                    Resonance and Synergy

                                    Spirit ? to sense resonance and synergy.

                                    This is largely a sensory ability so it is probably Spirit 1. That rank seems to have most of the relevant abilities.


                                    Spirit Data - Spirit 2 Mind 2 (Read Surface Thoughts + Speak to Spirit)

                                    Pull identity information from a spirit and translate it. This also includes reading the inherent resonance and synergy of the spirit. It’s arguably that identity and place in the hierarchy are deeper ideas than Mind 2 can access so Mind 3 might be necessary, though that’s not the way I picture spirit minds working.

                                    The odd thing is that neither of these makes sense as Spirit 3. In other words, while I’m sure Spirit 3 Mind 2(3) can be used, I don’t see the ability of Spirit 3 that is being used and it looks like the same effect would be possible at Spirit 2 Mind 2(3).


                                    Short Lived Ghost Things

                                    Ex Nihilo creation of a thing composed of ephemera with Spirit 3/Matter 3/Prime 2. For that small effect this is going to be a big section.

                                    Contradiction Resolution - Ex Nihilo


                                    This is a really strange spell and its existence has ramifications on how we interpret Spirit 3. I’ll say at the outset, it is possible that Matter was just an error. If it wasn’t though…

                                    Requiring both Matter and Prime is a very strange choice. If the idea is to create ephemera out of nothing, surely Prime alone is all that’s required. That’s all any other ‘something from nothing’ effect needs. There are basically two possibilities I see:

                                    1 – Spirit 3 can’t be used to directly create something ex nihilo. This spell is a workaround that creates physical matter ex nihilo and converts it to spirit matter.

                                    2 – Spirit can only create generic ephemera ex nihilo, defining it down to an object requires Matter as well.

                                    Note that both of these are using Spirit 3 in a transmutational capacity. The former as a matter to ephemera transmutation and the latter as a quintessence flow to ephemera transmutation. Since there is no indication this spell is a gauntlet crossing one, that implies that Spirit 3 can transmute even when the gauntlet isn't involved.

                                    As for why either of these would be the case, my best guess is that if Spirit 3/Prime 2 could create ephemera and Spirit 3 could convert ephemera to matter, forces, and life patterns without the use of Pattern Spheres, then Spirit 3/Prime 2 could be used to create almost anything without pattern spheres. One of those two needed some kind of restriction.

                                    Or maybe I’m completely off base. This is a strange effect, both in what it appears to do and the method it appears to use. If anyone has another idea of what this spell is doing and why Matter is involved I’d love to add it here.

                                        2 – Spirit can only create generic ephemera ex nihilo, defining it down to an object requires Matter as well.

                                        Note that both of these are using Spirit 3 in a transmutational capacity. The former as a matter to ephemera transmutation and the latter as a quintessence flow to ephemera transmutation. Since there is no indication this spell is a gauntlet crossing one, that implies that Spirit 3 can transmute even when the gauntlet isn't involved.
                                        I have no idea what the authors had in mind with this limitation, but I'm tempted to say it may be both?.

                                        If I'm not wrong, both to create a spirit and to directly transmute ephemera are mentioned as Spirit 5.

                                        Spirit 3 transmutative powers don't change the object in any way other than "Matter to Ephemera" and viceversa. It doesn't alter the object as much as it alters it's relation to the Cosmology, so to speak.

                                        So, you're able to "create physical matter ex nihilo and convert it to spirit matter". Arguably you could also use Prime 3/Spirit 3 to channel Quintessence and "translate"​ it to spirit Essence or a "raw ephemera" of sorts - and viceversa. But you can't create actual spirit matter nor shape it, atleast not with Spirit 3 alone.

                                            Spirit 4

                                            •••• Rend Gauntlet/ Seal Breach/ Bind Spirit

                                            As the mage approaches Mastery, he can tear Gateways in the Gauntlet, allowing groups or large objects to pass through… or close such breaches, too. Both applications, of course, are deeply vulgar.

                                            At this Rank, the mage may also compel spirits to appear, bind them into Fetish objects, or tie them to certain spots or prisons. For obvious reasons, such bondage is risky, especially if the spirit is powerful. A brave or foolish Spirit mage can even turn himself into a temporary Fetish, channeling a spirit entity into his mortal body; in such cases, he loses his ability to use true magick, but he may employ the capabilities of the spirit inside him. On the flipside, he can also exorcise a spirit that has possessed a mortal host. In all cases, the mage enters a series of resisted rolls against the spirit, pitting his Willpower against that spirit’s own.

                                            Gauntlet Gateway (Rend Gauntlet)

                                            The escalation for crossing the gauntlet for Spirit is much like the escalation for teleportation for Correspondence. Rank 3 allows personal instantaneous portals or travel, while Rank 4 allows lasting gateways.

                                            This power does not specifically say that these gates convert the people and things that pass into ephemera so that is technically up to ST discretion. It is probably intended that this gauntlet crossing functions mostly like the crossing of Spirit 3.


                                            Create Shallowing

                                            One application that could be possible is creating a shallowing, or at least creating a place where the gauntlet is gone. Even if a shallowing doesn’t count as a gateway, this should fall under rending the gauntlet.

                                            The other more narrative, mystical, and associative elements of a shallowing may have to wait for Spirit 5, though.


                                            Close Shallowing

                                            Whether or not a mage with Spirit 4 can create a shallowing, he should be able to close one. A shallowing may be more than just a place with a gauntlet of zero, but that is one of its necessary components.


                                            Compel Spirits (Bind Spirit)


                                            At this level a mage can compel behavior out of spirits. I think there’s some rule that at this point I’m supposed to say that this is likely to anger spirits, especially powerful ones. I’m not going to say that, though, because 1) spirits are alien and what they consider respectful and appropriate is similarly alien and 2) because it is possible to command without being demeaning.

                                            One particularly important application of this is summoning which works on spirits not in the current location. It is possible this requires Correspondence, but given the behavior of Spirit 2, probably not.

                                            I’m also going to include binding and trapping spirits to specific spots or prisons part of this power. That doesn’t really need its own section. Same for banning.


                                            Humiliate Spirit

                                            While I did just stress that commanding spirits does not inherently anger spirits it is definitely possible to anger them with this power either unintentionally (if the mage doesn’t know enough) or intentionally (if he does and chooses to).

                                            He could also just compel the spirit to feel humiliated regardless of circumstances. This would of course wear off when the spell does. Whether the spirit continues to consider the spell a humiliation after that will depend on the spirit.


                                            Create Fetish (Bind Spirit)


                                            Mages can bind spirits into objects, and in doing so create a wonder. While how a fetish works is (to a limited degree) found elsewhere, the text here indicates that the binding process functions much like any other attempt to compel a spirit with Spirit 4.

                                            Unorthodox Fetish

                                            There’s a lot of gray area in the word ‘object’ and given the capacities of Spirit 4, it is likely a mage could bind a spirit into counterintuitive things like air or a location. The fire of an eternal flame (either the kind in cemeteries or the kind in burning mines and natural leaks) could function as a pattern to bind a spirit to. I suspect the reason this is not talked about is less because it is not possible and more because it is inadvisable, but YMMV.


                                            Exorcise Spirit

                                            In many ways this is the opposite power of creating a fetish, it is separating the spirit from a mortal host (or presumably item). This should apply both to spirits that have possessed a host under their own power, spirits that have possessed a host with the help of another mage, and spirits that have been bound into a host with Compel Spirit.


                                            Exorcise Awakened Item

                                            I’ll admit, this one probably doesn’t make sense but I don’t have another unusual idea for Exorcism, and I’m curious. Say a mage awakens the spirit of an item, then exorcises that item, separating the spirit from the item it shared. What does that do to the spirit and what does that do to the item?

                                                Spirit 5

                                                ••••• Forge Ephemera/ Gilgul/ Break the Dreamshell

                                                The Spirit Master is now able to command ephemera itself, creating, challenging, and destroying spirit matter as he Wills. With such power, he may craft Realms, imbue or drain a spirit of Essence, instill a soul within an empty shell of Life or Matter, and bestow the awful sentence of Gilgul – the destruction of a mage’s Avatar. Such powers are always vulgar and feature devastating consequences even when the mage succeeds.

                                                This power also allows the mage to break the Dreamshell and venture beyond the Horizons, wandering into the Deepest of Umbrae. Such tasks demand at least 10 successes, but a courageous Master can travel as far as his soul wants to go.
                                                We have a problem here in that supplemental spheres are completely unmentioned, even for effects we already know are conjunctional, like putting a soul in a body. On a strict reading that means Spirit 5 is capable of everything mentioned without help.

                                                If we do take the more reasonable stance that some of these are conjunctional we are left to guess which ones.


                                                Create or Destroy Spirit Matter (Forge Ephemera)

                                                At Spirit 5 a mage has full transmutational control of spirit matter allowing him to create and destroy ephemera, spirits, and souls.

                                                On straight reading this is Spirit 5 alone, but contextually this may require Prime for creation effects.


                                                Create Spirit

                                                Spirits are creatures of ephemera, so they should fall under this ability. Further, if a mage can create a soul at Spirit 5, he should also be able to create a spirit.


                                                Challenge Spirit Matter

                                                Ambiguity Resolution - Challenge?

                                                I have no idea what this means, but I suspect the word should have been ‘changing.’ Changing and sculpting is a power that usually goes along with creation and destruction in other spheres.
                                                If ‘challenge’ is intentional, possible meanings could include:

                                                Spirit 5 lets a mage claim status in spirit courts, enabling them to take formal actions.

                                                Spirit 5 lets a mage make formal challenges that spirits must respond to.

                                                Spirit 5 may let a mage set spirit related bans and wards.


                                                Remake Spirits

                                                At Spirit 5 a mage can completely reshape spirits not just in their appearance or form, but in their identity and purpose, even into diametrically opposed ones. Pain spirits can be remade into healing spirits, mountain spirits into ocean spirits, etc.


                                                Create Realm (Forge Ephemera)

                                                We get two words for a power with so much scope. With no boundaries given, this would seem to allow a mage to create Horizon realms, regular Umbral realms, small zones of influence, and really anything.

                                                Whether this can create the entire realm or just carves out the space for other spheres to define is left unsaid. I’m fairly certain any time this is brought up in previous editions it does involve other spheres, so that’s the safe bet. As written, though, no other spheres are mentioned


                                                Create Shard Realm

                                                I’m not saying that the Shard realms were actually created by mages, but it does seem to be within the hypothetical capabilities of Spirit 5 as written here. The number of successes required would be absurd, though.


                                                Imbue/Drain Essence (???)

                                                A mage can now pull or impart essence from or to a spirit directly rather than by damaging or (presumably) healing said spirit. Essence is not quintessence, but it can probably be turned into quintessence with the help of Prime.


                                                Sacred Hunt

                                                Between Compel Spirit and Drain Essence, a mage can probably simulate the hunting rites of werewolves where the spirit agrees to be drained in a specific way with the condition that it will not be destroyed. Careful negotiation of agreements and treaties to allow the mage this right are likely better long term options than Compel Spirit, though some spirits may relish certain competitions enough that they would welcome the opportunity.

                                                Werewolves tend to use actual hunts, but the spirits mages work with might prefer other competitions like chess matches, improv theater, interpretive dance, Greek wrestling, etc.


                                                Create Soul (Forge Ephemera)

                                                At this level a mage can create souls. On a technical level, ‘instill’ has connotations that could limit this. It could be read to mean that souls are slowly grown in the shell, for instance. Nevertheless, it appears mages can create and impart souls.

                                                As with the rest of this rank, no supplemental spheres are listed. Contextually, though, Prime or Mind may be.


                                                Convert Spirit to Soul

                                                If a mage can create a soul and change ephemera, he can probably convert a spirit into a soul, or at least into something that functions like a soul rather than a spirit.


                                                Gilgul (Gilgul)

                                                Because Mage is a little cagey on what the Avatar is, it is unclear if this is destruction of a separate entity or soul surgery that amputates part of a person’s soul or something inbetween. Technically all of those would be possible at Spirit 5.


                                                Avatar Inversion

                                                We know this is possible because the Cauls do it. We know it manipulates a mage’s Avatar, which puts it in the same rank (or higher) than Gilgul. I’m not saying that Spirit 5 should give easy access to this, because as a storyline element it is next to impossible and should be the stuff of legends, at least on a level with curing vampires. What I am saying is that, barring Archspheres, the Spirit component of this freakishly difficult effect should be rank 5.



                                                Journey Beyond Horizons (Break the Dreamshell)

                                                Exactly what it says. When using Spirit to break through the Horizon it takes Rank 5. This would presumably apply to any Horizon like barrier found out in the reaches.


                                                Everything Else (???)


                                                As with all Rank 5s, Spirit 5 should be able to do just about everything possible within the Sphere’s purview. Unless the ST specifically wants something locked behind archspheres, it should be possible here.


                                                Missing

                                                Move Soul

                                                If a mage can create and impart a soul, he should also be able to take and move one. Possibly even destroy one, though that may be a bridge too far as a game element for some tables.
