    Retaining Film Footage from a Time Loop

    08-16-2021, 03:06 PM
    I want to have an antagonist, who is an unregistered lobbyist, bribe a judge in a Time Loop. He will set an Anchor Point for himself, pull the judge into the loop and then successfully bribe the judge, the exchange being filmed by a hidden digital body-cam. He sends the footage sideways in time to himself - his laptop is the storage medium. He then returns to the Anchor Point, before the judge was made complicit, but retains the film from the Loop. A different conversation happens in the normal flow of time parallel to the Looped Event. The judge, who would have succumb to persuasive influence, isn't guilty of anything, but the Mage (A Syndicate Enforcer) walks away with a video tape that passes digital analysis as being real.

    1. What Spheres would he need, besides Time and what rank in Time (I think it's 4)? For digital footage, Data seems like the required other Sphere. If it were chemical film, I suppose Forces and Matter would be necessary.

    2. Does the storage medium, the hard drive of the laptop, have to be a Device or other kind of Wonder?

    3. Does the judge count as a witness if he is never shown the film? Can the Enforcer Mage pay a Paradox cost only once and them be able to show the film to select people without incurring Paradox each time?




    Thank you for passing time with me in conversation. My Hacks.

                  Time shenanigans are always tricky, but things get easier once the spell is over.

                  The video itself is not going to trigger Paradox. If there was something really unnatural about the video it would degrade due to unbelief, but I don't think it is uncanny enough for that. Most people will think it is either real or a fake, and both of those are natural, believable possibilities. Even the judge himself won't assume something supernatural happened, he'll just assume it was faked really well.

                  Think of it this way. If a Mage sets a dumpster on fire with a fireball, that's vulgar. The fire it leaves behind, though, is a normal part of the world. If a sleeper comes around the corner in a minute and sees a dumpster fire, they might panic but they will not trigger paradox or even unbelief because the fire is completely mundane.

                          What makes a spell vulgar isn't the witness, they only worsen the effect. Assuming local Paradigm isn't being applied, a fireball in the middle of nowhere is as vulgar as a fireball in Times Square. Paradox occurs in the moment a spell is cast, not when it is witnessed, what happens when it is witnessed is Unbelief, if it applies. It seems that you get the impression that the core mechanic is the witness, but is the spell casting.

                          You Time Loop will probably generate Paradox. I would personally rule that in this case the judge counts as a witness for Paradox effects. It is really, really hard for time travel of any kind to be coincidental. Your video, though, will be coincidental.

                          For Spheres, you can do the video with Time, Data 2 and Forces 2, or dump Forces and use Data 4, because either you need to transfer energy (Forces 2), or you need to make the information be in two places simultaneously (Data 4, not 2). When interpreting Data, remember that it doesn't work better on information, it works at all because everything IS information.

                          The chance of the effects being detected, though, will be high. It can be detected at the moment of the loop, it can be detected at the place of the loop but later, and it can be detected through the video, even a mundane copy of it, or just a still, because a mage can analyse it and conclude that the image came from an erased timeline.

                          But I fail to understand why your plan involves a time loop in the first place. I get that you want for there to be no other evidence of the contract ever happening, but why?

                              Okay, so I have changed some of this. It won't be a judge, but a U.S. State Governor who faces a reelection campaign. The Enforcer enlists an Extraordinary Citizen (EC) to do the talking. She is a wealthy residential land developer and landlord. The Governor responds to a letter she sent him, talking about some problems and inviting him to meet her. A check is made out to his campaign.

                              In advance of the meeting, the Enforcer wires up a room in the EC's house with cameras and microphones. She is given two different proposals for the Governor's consideration. Her phone is in her pocket, set to vibrate once or twice, once indicating she should pitch the first proposal, twice for the second. The Enforcer hides in an adjacent room, observing via video-feed. He casts the Loop Effect to encompass both rooms and causes her phone to vibrate once. She obtains the Governor's agreement to participate in Plan A, which is more damming for the Governor. The Enforcer sends the recording to himself in the real timeline that is allowed to unfold at the conclusion of the Loop. Upon receiving the data from his Looped self, the Enforcer causes the EC's phone to vibrate twice. The Governor agrees to Plan B, which is more altruistic and perfectly legal.

                              Background, LOPII:
                              Years ago, the Technocracy started a front group, Low Outcome Prediction Intervention Initiative (LOPII). It advocates the short-term use of steroids and amphetamines to open up opportunities for youth facing stagnant wages, rising housing and tuition costs. The message is essentially, "You are so screwed you need a shortcut to avoid destitution. Try this.". It acts like a support group that attempts to prevent addiction and teaches a personal development philosophy. Members are convinced the world is against them. The Technocracy no longer has any of its own people inside LOPII whose victims are disposable to them and a liability to the Traditions.

                              A few officers in a city police force are former steroid users with ties to LOPII. The Technocracy have fabricated evidence to convince both city and State Police that there is a much larger number of cops on all of the forces with LOPII ties. They are aware of the spread of dangerous drug use, but unable to plan a proper investigation. The Traditions, who currently hold sway over the State Capitol, resist any idea of asking for FBI help, fearing the NWO will use FBI-cover to oust them.

                              Plan A - The Governor will use discretionary funds to hire Means Logistica, an out-of-state private intelligence firm owned by Syndicate Enforcement to conduct a quiet operation against LOPII that avoids any scandal for the police. Means Logistica will buy airtime and air adds made by Media Control, promoting the Governor for reelection.

                              Plan B - The EC will buy land and partner with the State Government to fund and build a drug treatment center specially equipped to deal with LOPII victims - (It will be run by Progenitor Pharmacopoeists and NWO Watchers.). The Governor leaves the meeting happy at having a plan to solve this problem and enthusiastic about working with the EC who he's impressed with. A married man, he is beginning to fall in love with someone new.

                              This chapter of the Chronicle is called, "What You Would Have Done.". The Governor is going to be haunted by the decision he would have made, had the EC pitched the more corrupt proposal. The Governor won his first election with the help of Prisha, a Euthanatos Mage, lawyer and PR-Firm owner. She is the target of the Enforcer's scheme. The Governor's plight pulls her into a web of intrigue.

                              When Prisha uses her Sphere Perception abilities on the video of the Governor, she is forced to acknowledge that this is an accurate representation of the Governor's true character when a bit of pressure is applied by the Technocracy. This version of the Governor is a source of corruption that would risk spreading to the whole society in her state. Her Tradition's teachings mandate that such a person be eliminated. Prisha must consider whether the Governor will face further attempts by the Technocracy to influence him. Before she can get to the Governor, Prisha is framed for the murder of LOPII's leader. From prison, she appeals to the players to check on the Governor and investigate the conspiracy.

                              The Governor is not yet aware that the recording of Plan A exists. If players meet with the Governor and use sensory abilities, they will discover his plans to partner with the EC to build the treatment facility. They will also detect the EC's influence on him. One of the players is friends with the EC's son who she and some friends travelled with on vacation - financed by Mom.

                              With Prisha in jail, awaiting trial, the Technocracy has some access to the State Government. The recording that implicates the Governor is now a tool through which Technocracy Mages can diffuse Magick. Since the Governor's claim that he and the EC are only working toward a good cause is honest, Technocracy Mages can work Magick in the opposite direction as well. State bureaucrats, police and employees of Prisha's firm are caught in a techno-magical vice. So are Prisha, the EC and the players.

                              In mundane reality, the case against the Governor is hard to defend against and the players have to consider letting him get pushed out of office, possibly losing control over the state government. They are facing an invasion and takeover by the Technocracy with everything they value at risk. The Paradox incurred by the Enforcer is also a potential plot device that I can use if players find the scenario to hard. He is their main adversary. I am considering a scene in which Wrinkle, the Time Paradox Spirit sides with the players against the Enforcer.

                              The Enforcer is hard to catch, because he can freeze time and escape almost any encounter. He is using his Magick to keep everything in an indeterminate state. Is Prisha going to face pressure from her own Tradition to eliminate the Governor, the EC or both? How does this change her relationship to local Tradition Mages who previously relied on her to hold their territory?
