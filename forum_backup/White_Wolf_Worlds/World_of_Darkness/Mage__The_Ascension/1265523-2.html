    A book about the war between Horus and Set would probably be a World of Darkness book, talking about the ancient Setites, who/what Osiris might've been (probably with 3 or 4 possibilities that Storytellers could tailer to fit their needs), the ancient shapeshifters of Egypt (including the pre-curse Silent Striders, the Mokole of the Nile, the Bubasti back in their zenith, and what other breeds like the Ananasi or Nagah would've been doing at the time), the concept of Apep as a avatar of the Wyrm, all of the mystical sects devoted to the various Egyptian gods (Isis, Osiris, Thoth, Ptah, Sebek, Ra, etc.), the original Mummies and what they were like back then before having centuries of experience under their belts, the Egyptian underworld of the time (including Anubis and the first Ferrymen), various Egyptian bygones, the djinn and other spirits/fey. Such a book has the potential to be pretty cool.
