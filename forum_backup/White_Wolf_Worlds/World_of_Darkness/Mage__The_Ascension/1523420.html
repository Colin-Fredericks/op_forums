      The book do address this:

      Basically, yes. If you scare someone with Mind, their body will respond to fear as it would to natural fear. If you use Life to increase someone's cortisol levels they'll have a negative mood response.

      The line is that this is indirect and individuals don't react to stimuli in the same fashion. If you're just using Mind, you don't know what someone's baseline hormone levels are and other factors, so you don't have solid control over what the biological response will be. One person might pass out from fear, and another respond by becoming biologically activated and ready to physically respond to danger.

                  Generally speaking a lot of it will depend on your mage’s understanding of the nature of Mind Body Duality. If your mage considers the mind to be an entirely seperate thing from the body, then no, Mind effects will not be able to affect how the body works.

                  If instead you see them as linked inherently, then there is room for some influence, but without Life, that influence will be like hitting a billiard ball with a cue. You can set it up, but it will be outside your immediate control once you let the ball go, whereas having Life in addition would let you control all the balls in the table.

                  some example paradigms:

                  Humourism, an hippocratic idea where the body is composed of four humours which have an influence on the welfare of the body and the mind. Such mages would probably see affecting the mind via the humours as having knock on effects on health, but without Life they would be unable to control said outcome beyond a being more melancholic making the body less capable of fighting disease, or sanguine making the body more probe to fight or flight response, for example.

                  Platonic dualism states that the mind is trapped within the body, and that everything perceived is filtered by the body, abstracting it away from truth. Mind magic would therefor be a way to move beyond these limitations and experience that which the body conceals, not enhancing senses so much as bypassing them. Such mages would be unable to affect the body via Mind magic, but could definitely affect the Mind via Life

                  Mind body indivisability states that the two are seperate but inherently linked entities, and that some, such as Descartes, proclaim these is a physical link between the two, such as the pineal gland, but others may instead argue that they are tied together via the soul, one’s chakras, or even their past lives. Such mages would see mind affecting body as easily as Life affecting mind, so long as their magic stimulates aspects of what it is they believe connects it, such as stimulating the pineal gland, or rerouting energy through the soul (would not require spirit unless you wanted to change the nature of their soul at the same time) humourism can be considered to fit here, though I included it separately since someone specifically cited it.


                  Property dualism is the idea that the mind cannot be constrained to physical phenomena, that the mind exists outside of the physical, possible within the astral umbra or within its own unique realm deep in the dreaming, for such mages Life magic would have very limited impact on the mind, but Mind magic may indeed still affect the body as while the mind is outside of the body, it also heavily influences the material bound mind. The mind can supervene upon the body. But perhaps only when it comes to perceptions, such as Mental sensation of pain translating to physically felt pain, or mental sensations of cold becoming actual hypothermia

                  this is not a complete list nor is it a comprehensive account. But the idea is the possibility of Mind and Life spheres influencing each other’s purview.

                      And in game terms, a mage who has the ability to affect a living body in ways that are beyond consensus reality is using Life sphere Magick. A mage who has the ability to affect a person’s mind in ways that are beyond consensus reality is using Mind sphere Magick. This is so even if the mage’s paradigm says it isn’t; even if the mage’s paradigm doesn’t include the spheres at all.

                              By that logic magic shouldn't be able to cause damage to living things without Life. "Shooting someone with a hypertech pistol," and, "casting a fireball," are just how paradigms explain Life 3 damage effects.

If you use Mind to cause mental stress, the body will respond naturally to increased stress triggers.

                                  It’s consensus reality that allows everybody, including Mages, to do things without having to roll Arete, and without accumulating paradox on a botch.                                   And if I Magickally create a sword, I only have to worry about the vulgarity and paradox of the creation. I can attack with that sword, and doing so remains within the consensus, and I don’t accumulate paradox with each attack (even on a botch). Likewise, if a mage uses Life sphere Magick to conjure hormones, that initial conjuration is magickal, subject to vulgarity, paradox, the limits of my Arete, but once summoned those hormones can affect someone’s emotions just like any other (non-magickal) hormones.

                                  However, we also have to bear in mind that working this way is subject to the constraints of the consensus. Hormones are not a terribly reliable way to spur a target into a particular action, in consensus reality. If you want a super-specific result from applying hormones - something like what Poison Ivy does in Batman comics - you’re probably going to need to go beyond consensus reality, and for that you need Mind.

                                  But once conjured with Forces, fire burns without paradox, because that’s consensus reality.                                                   This is a problem I have with this game, and I think that players have, but it is also a problem that the writters themselves have (and which end up confusing the players and thus infects the players with the same problem) of just selecting a random collection of Spheres for effects based on the "feeling".

                                                  Like for example, "Chi healing" requiring Prime because of the "feeling" about "Chi".

                                                  No, to determine what Spheres are necessary we need strict adherence to what each level of a Sphere does.

                                                  What does Forces 3 do? It turns one form of energy into another. For example, heat turned into light, or "cold" turned into "shadow" (scientific paradigms may see "cold" and "shadow" as mere absence of heat and light respectively, but mystical paradigms may see those are active and just opposed forces).

                                                  If you combine with other Spheres, you could turn a non forces pattern into a forces pattern or vice versa. For example, Matter 2 Forces 3 to turn a lightning into silver (or if you want a more coincidental effect, to make glass if the lighting is in an area with sand). Or, you could turn an apple into an explosive device with Life 2 Forces 3 (transmuting the apple into heat+kinetic force).

                                                  With that knowledge, I ask you: are you turning thoughts into electricity or something like it?

                                                  Well, you may say that you are, but the problem is that, once you do it, those thoughts are GONE.

                                                  Mind 2-4 + Forces 3 would just be something Matrix-like, turning human brains into living batteries. And that's it. You do NOT store those thoughts.

                                                  Imo you would need to use Mind 2-4 to "extract" the thoughts obviously (Mind 1 even, for superficial thoughts and impressions, Mind 2 for emotional states of the target, Mind 3 to capture dreams and whatever the target might be thinking at the time of the effect, but if you want to extract all of the most hidden and deep thoughts of the target, Mind 4).

                                                  Then, to imprint those mental patterns into an object (be it a pen drive or the vase of the ancestors memory) you need Prime between 2 to 4 (up to Prime 5 if it is to imprint onto a living being - in a manner different from giving the living being those memories - aka the living being becomes a vessel for those mental patterns, but not a user).

                                                  Prime is needed to change a pattern and to allow imprinting something into a pattern.

                                                  Forces just simply doesn't do anything like it.
