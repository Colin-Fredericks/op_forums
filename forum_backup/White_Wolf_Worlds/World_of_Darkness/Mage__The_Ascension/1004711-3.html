                                                        Concerning further the matter of mentally ill patsies to Nephandic plots, I would like to point out that not everyone with a Delusion that can be exploited is Schizophrenic. Not all of them have hallucinations. Someone with Paranoid Delusions is otherwise in proper touch with their senses and thought processes, but has come to irregular conclusions about Reality.

                                                        I say this not without a sense of irony, given the game we're talking about.

                                                        Some may believe that someone is out to get them, or that "They" secretly control the world through bizarre means, for unknown purposes. That the world actually IS controlled by at least a couple of monolithic conspiracies can work in the favor of a Nephandic manipulator. As can the fact that, unlike the person suffering from hallucinations, does not have experience with voices that tell them to do horrible things (and therefore are not to be trusted or obeyed). The person with Paranoid Delusions believes in a certain view of the world, and that view can be exploited so long as the manipulator knows the delusion. (It's here that the Mind Sphere becomes very important, as a tool for reading thoughts and charting out what the person believes).

                                                        The Nephandus could contact this poor person, claiming to be working for or against the shadowy conspiracy the person believes in. That the conspiracy is a conspiracy, and "anyone could be watching, listening, or in on the plot", would be all the justification the patsy would need for why their new "friend" needs to minimize contact and limit traces that could come back to them. From there, it's a matter of telling the person what they want to hear, validating their beliefs, while introducing new ones. Then the Nephandus starts giving the person instructions, giving them hints on how they can help or hinder "Them".

                                                        It's important at this stage for the Nephandus, as with all pawns, to start the delusional person off small. Telling them to go out and kill is too much for anyone to expect from the average person (for a certain value of "average", when paranoid conspiracy theories are concerned). They would resist. But if the tasks are small, manageable, and seemingly benign, the person would go along with it. Many of these could be nonsense tasks, assigned for no reason other than to get the pawn used to being a pawn, and to deepen their loyalty. Which each communication, however, the tasks become increasingly large...and increasingly immoral. Petty theft here, destruction of property there, knocking out electricity to this Technocrat Construct, burning down that Tradition magic shop. So long as the Nephandus can justify it, the pawn will grow willing to do worse and worse things. This is an established part of psychology: each task is an incremental compromise of their values, until they have been built up into doing just about anything.

                                                        Eventually you have the patsy kidnapping innocent people, assured that, as they hold a gun to their victim's head, they are ultimately working to neutralize some puppet or another of "Them".

                                                        And that's all before the Nephandus starts sending the pawn a few "tools" to help them out in the war. Charms, Trinkets, and minor Talismans that can increase their odds against the conspiracy. It shouldn't need to go that far, of course, unless the pawn is especially valuable. Magical or technomagical items, after all, can be traced back to their source. A lone gunman against the world, though, can be effective on his own, if fed the right "intel". The best part being that he'll never betray his controller. Both because he knows nothing (and fully believes that this ignorance is justified, for the good of "the mission"), and because he thinks anyone who would want to stop or interrogate him is one of "Them", and not to be helped.
