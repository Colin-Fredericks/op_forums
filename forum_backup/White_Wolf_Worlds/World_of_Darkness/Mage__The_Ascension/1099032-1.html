Something along the lines of: "It's better you move to this state, it's been decided...oh and by the way, if you choose this city, then there just might be a Cabal there who are in need of your expertise in regards to you-know-what.").

Let's say you want to punish an Arete 6 Mage for torturing and murdering two Sleepers. He/She has at the very least Time 4, Forces 5, Correspondence 4, and is one of the sought-after Mages with Prime 5. The Mage won't go down easily or quietly. Some Mages have his back for the time her/his actions saved an entire Cabal from a Technocracy dragnet in the days of the Pogrom; and she/he is a fine teacher of multiple Spheres. The lives of the two Sleepers can, in the end, seem insignificant...
