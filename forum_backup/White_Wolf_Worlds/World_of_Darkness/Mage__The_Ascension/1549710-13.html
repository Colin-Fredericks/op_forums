                        Know Material Properties (Matter Perceptions)

                        A mage can get detailed material information on matter. This is stated to include structure, properties, integrity or lack thereof, composition, hard to see structures, and hidden layers. We can reasonably infer that other properties like mass, melting and boiling points, chemical and elemental composition are possible as well.

                        There is an implication that matter that is integrated with living matter cannot be sensed, or at least cannot be analyzed, without Life. The wording here is key, as it indicates the difficulty lies in situations where it is difficult to tell where one pattern ends and the other begins. This would include magical fusions, cybernetics, medical implants, and also, possibly, matter that has spread throughout the body such as inorganic toxins. It would not include clearly distinct patterns like bullets, hidden microfiches, and Terminators.


                        Sense Hidden Compartment

                        Since Matter 1 can explicitly see structure and hidden layers it is quite clear that it can sense things hidden within objects, or at the very least sense places where things can be hidden.


                        Sense Weak Point

                        Matter 1 can also see where the structure of a substance is weaker or stronger, or where it might have special properties.


                        See Weak Points (Matter Perceptions + Sense Flaws) Matter 1 / Entropy 1

                        Contradiction Resolution - See Weak Points

                        You might have noticed, I already put Sense Weak Points as a power of Matter 1. It really appears from the wording of both Matter 1 and Entropy 1 that either one can detect the weak points of an object. Matter 1 can explicitly sense integral stability, after all. What Entropy can bring to the table that Matter can’t do is see probable places an object will be damaged, or to put it another way, to sense the object’s fortune. So the combination would show both where it is easy to damage an object and where an object is likely to be damaged. It might be better to call this Deathsight, Object Edition


                        Analyze Cybernetics and Medical Implants (Matter Perceptions + Sense Life) Matter 1 / Life 1


                        When something is both matter and life it is difficult to analyze without both. This is a problem with any integrated pattern, but in the case of life and matter there are many situations in the modern world where this can arise, so it makes sense that it get special mention.


                        Missing:

                        Sense Matter Patterns (Matter Perceptions)


                        Strictly speaking, there is no mention at all of mages using Matter 1 to sense objects around them. That said, an absence doesn’t mean it’s not possible and if Life can do this for living things, probably Matter can do it for objects. At the very least, if a player were to ask to scan for nearby Matter patterns, I would assume that to be a Matter 1 effect.

                        Since it isn’t directly stated, though, this is definitely an ST call.


                        Expand Senses (Matter Perceptions)


                        If Forces can expand the energy sensing senses (vision and hearing), Matter should be able to expand the matter sensing senses (touch, taste, and smell). Taste and smell should be able to be tailored to substances and concentrations that can’t ordinarily be tasted or smelled, and touch can be refined to sense textures and qualities that are normally imperceptible.

                        This is not stated in the Matter 1 writeup, but in this case it is just another way of perceiving the information Matter 1 can already give.

                                                Matter 2

                                                •• Basic Transmutation

                                                With advanced knowledge, the mage can transmute one substance into another, so long as she doesn’t alter its essential shape, temperature, or basic state (gas, liquid, solid). Depending upon that mage’s practice, she might reshape lead into gold through alchemy, wood into stone through a hyperpetrification process, or water into wine (and, with Life 3, wine into blood) through a sacred miracle. Rare and/ or complex materials are more difficult to fabricate than simple ones; it’s easier to turn stone into iron, for example, than into gold. To reflect that challenge, such refinement requires a greater number of successes than a transmutation into a common material.

                                                (On a related note, the mage cannot yet fabricate radioactive materials. Such elements merge their essence into the Sphere of Forces, and thus demand a greater level of expertise.)

                                                When combined with other Spheres, this basic level of understanding allows a mage to conjure base materials from energy or dissolve them into Quintessence (Prime 2), transmute matter into living tissue (Life 4 or higher), move an object through space (Correspondence 2 or higher) or time (Time 3 or higher), change ephemera into matter and matter into ephemera, or awaken the slumbering spirit within material objects (Spirit 3 or higher). In all cases, Matter 2 allows the mage to work with simple, homogenous, non-living substances. Complex mixtures of various elements usually require Rank 3 or higher, although simple combinations (like those found in bread, milk, paper, or gunpowder) are possible if the player rolls plenty of successes.
                                                There is one major power for Matter 2, but it has a lot of utility and it comes with some strong limitations. Between those two, this is going to be a long section. Matter 2 has:


                                                Limited Transmutation of Substance (Basic Transmutation)

                                                A mage can transmute one simple substance into another one of the same temperature, shape, and state (solid, liquid, gas, plasma). Simple mixtures are possible but may require more successes. Radioactive materials are also explicitly off limits, with the specific rationale that their connection to another sphere (Forces) means it takes higher levels of Matter to work with them. Matter 2 doesn’t claim to be able to make substances that don’t exist and it only uses examples of substances that already do exist. Matter 5 claims jurisdiction over substances that don’t exist so that is likely intentional.

                                                Despite the many restrictions, this can be quite powerful depending on what does and does not count as simple.


                                                What Did They Make This Out Of?

                                                By changing an object or section of object into something easily degradable a mage can quickly destroy something solid. Sand or powder is a good choice. Choosing something reactive, like white phosphorous, could easily make this a quite pyrotechnic endeavor.


                                                Quick Conduction

                                                By changing insulation and wall materials to something conductive like copper a mage can quickly change the flow of power. The simplest use of this is to pop breakers and drop a house into darkness. With more finesse a mage could also electrocute a target or turn on a device that is not plugged in.

                                                Added: (from Heavy Arms) the same effect can be used on air (or really on plasma) to change it into a highly conductive pathway. Matter 2 can’t change a gas to a plasma, but a laser pointer can do that in small quantities, and a mage can change the plasma from there. At Matter 3, a mage can forego the laser pointer.


                                                Removed: Non Newtonian Fluids


                                                Quick Dali

                                                Take a model of a clock and turn it into something that melts easily, like wax or butter. Aim for a duration such that it hasn’t finished melting by the time the spell ends. The object will revert back to what it was before but retain the sagging and melted appearance.


                                                Build Your Own Archaeological Fraud

                                                Since radioactive material is specifically off limits, anything a mage conjures will show up as older than 50,000 years old if it can be carbon dated, or 4.5 Billion years old if it can be uranium dated. This was probably not the intention behind the radiation restriction, but it is fun nonetheless.

                                                Note: Even if we say that a mage can replicate reasonable levels of common radioactive material, like Carbon 14, he doesn’t have to and can deliberately create an obvious forgery.
                                                ​

                                                Explosive Decompression

                                                Interestingly enough a mage is only restricted to the current temperature, not the current pressure. That means a mage can create a liquid under pressure that would (and does) rapidly expand into gas when that pressure is removed. That is an ability with an explosive potential.

                                                Added: (from Heavy Arms) By creating a situation that changes when a spell ends the explosion can be put on a time delay. Examples include mentos turned into an inert stone and turns back into mentos when the spell ends. Put that in a bottle of diet soda. The same effect also works with sodium turned into an inert rock and put in a bottle of water.
                                                ​

                                                Conjunctional Effects:


                                                Create Simple Object (Basic Transmutation + Fuel Pattern) Matter 2 / Prime 2

                                                This is such a well-known power that it barely needs description. By adding Prime 2 a mage can divert quintessence to create an entirely new pattern. Prime 2 specifically uses the terminology of diverting quintessence into patterns, by the way, so arguably it is just providing the energy while Matter is doing all the transmutation.

                                                Dissolve Simple Object (Basic Transmutation + Fuel Pattern) Matter 2 / Prime 2

                                                Ambiguity Resolution: Dissolve Simple Object

                                                That terminology becomes important here. From the description this is a reverse process to creating matter, so instead of diverting quintessence from somewhere to fuel or weave a new pattern, this is unweaving a pattern and diverting the quintessence back to wherever it came from. Matter 2 is just leaving that second part out of the description. Perfectly reasonable, and I mention the ambiguity only because it leaves open the question of what Matter 2 can do on its own.

                                                Stone to Flesh (Basic Transmutation + Transmute Simple or Complex Life)

                                                Contradiction Resolution: What level of Life?

                                                Stone to flesh itself is straightforward, though this section doesn’t distinguish between simple and complex life and doesn’t choose the proper level of life for transmuting either (Life 3 or Life 5, respectively). Life 4 could reasonably be the level for a mage to transmute a stone into himself, though. So, if he had a co-location effect or split his mind among multiple clones, and one of his bodies was turned to stone, Life 4/Matter 2 would be the correct combo to turn it back. The more classic giving life to the statue of Galatea would be Life 5/Matter 2 (or Life 3/Matter 2 if Galatea was a crab).

                                                Move Object Through Space (??? + Reach Through Space)

                                                Contradiction Resolution - What’s Doing What Now?

                                                Correspondence explicitly doesn’t need Matter to work on whole patterns in this context, but sometimes a locking sphere is useful so we can get to the Correspondence/Matter combo that way. Unfortunately, Corr 2 works by letting the mage physically reach out and pick up the object manually, so it doesn’t use locking. Correspondence 3 based levitation and motion matches the description and does benefit from locking, so we can assume Matter 2 just got the Correspondence rank wrong.

                                                Move Object Through Time (??? + ???)

                                                Contradiction Resolution – What is Happening Here?

                                                Much of this depends on what is happening. Actual time travel is Time 5, and it’s not at all clear that it needs Matter to send an object forward or backwards. Time 3 lets a mage cast through time and Correspondence can move objects through space at the same rank it can cast through space, but there’s really no evidence Time is meant to have the same progression. The best match I’ve found is a rewind time spell locked to an object so that it rewinds time just for that object rather than a specific area. That’s not really moving an object through time, though, and it would be the second time Matter 2 is reduced to a locking sphere in its own writeup.

                                                Convert Matter to Spirit or Back Again (Basic Transmutation + Step Sideways?) Matter 2 / Spirit 3

                                                Contradiction Resolution - Awakening Spirits

                                                This is a contradiction I almost missed. Spirit 3 can already turn things to ephemera and vice versa without Pattern Spheres. That’s what it does in gauntlet crossings. Possibly the intention was it can only do this on its own during gauntlet crossings, but if so that is not a stated limitation of Spirit.

                                                I don’t have a good application for Spirit 3/Matter 2 in this context.

                                                Awaken the Spirit of an Object (??? + Rouse and Lull Spirit) ???

                                                Contradiction Resolution - Awakening Spirits

                                                This normally doesn’t require Matter either, neither in Spirit 3 or in the sidebar Spirit 3 references. I’ll add that the sidebar does say that awakening the spirit of a poison involves Life 3 if it is currently spread out in a life pattern, so if there was a situation where the substance a mage wanted to awaken was suffused in another object, for instance awakening the spirit of the trace bits of gold in a piece of ore, Matter 2 would be involved.
