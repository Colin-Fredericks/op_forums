                                            You're entitled to your head canons, but the fact that Mages can use magic(k) to get sunligth that burns vampires w/o Archspheres is not a "matter of opinion".
                                            It's RAW data.

                                            Vampire ST book (Revised edition) has creating sunligth as Forces 3/Prime 2. And that's Vampire the Masquerade. The game where you play a Vampire and where Vampire Lore comes from.VtM has the most lenient rules about creating functional sunligth of the whole WoD

                                            Various Mage books have it at Forces 4, or even 5, and also high levels of Prime. There are many metaphisical reasons given for this (from God to Paradigm), but my theory it's that Mage has become more protective of vampires and their "specialness" than Vampire itself, after the Paradox Backlash of the infamous 1e lawnchairs. But even at it's most protective (HDYDT, the book where vampires get special rules protecting agianst Mind, that no other splat gets), summoning sunlight with Correspondence works.

                                            You can see how this "teleporting sunligth" works it in sources as varied as Blood Treachery and HDYDT.

                                            That's the RAW data - how does that relate to the orbit of Earth or Helios authority it's, literally, up for metaphisical debate. To be discussed alongside of how many angels can a Hermetic summon on top of a hairpin
