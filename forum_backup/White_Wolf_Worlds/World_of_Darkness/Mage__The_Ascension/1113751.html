                          In my Sorcerers Crusade chronicle the Players share a potent Avatar (Twin Souls merit but geared towards 5 chars).
                          One player took an enemy, an old Tremere sorceress. I decided, that she had the Avatar of this player in life.

                          She will try to get “her“ Avatar back.

                          So I am in favour of your idea.
                          You can give each player the shattered Avatar merit and force them to start with a low Avatar trait.

                          I gave the players Daemon 2 plus unbeknownst to them the Twin Souls merit for free.

                          If you give out Avatar 1 and the merit shattered Avatar for free, they will not complain.
                          Each time they find an Avatar of some old Tremere they divide the rating between themselves and go to a seeking, probably group style. Sharing the Avatar, it makes sense that they share the Seeking as well.

                              Originally posted by Mercurial View Post

                              The original members of the House, and since the Tremere's Avatar "died" as he himself put it in Blood Treachery and in other places the Avatars are described as being destroyed, I assume their in pieces somewhere in the Umbra. Realistically, something probably ate them, but maybe the pieces can still be recovered.
                              Yeah, while the other books were fairly ambiguous on the point of the Avatar's persistence post-Embrace, Blood Treachery put its foot down squarely on the "Utterly Annihilated" side. Indeed, it went so far with that idea, it made even the presence of vampiric Vitae in a mage's system as being toxic (yet also addictive) to the Avatar, until it slowly destroyed over time.

                              I believe I've made my disagreement with this mechanic known in the past, since a destroyed Avatar doesn't lend itself to as many stories as a displaced one. (Not to mention I find Blood Treachery's treatment of mage ghouldom to be overly punitive in general, when the very act of being a blood junkie provides for more than enough pathos). So I prefer to just have the Avatar move on, allowing a vampire to eventually cross paths with it again.

                              Still, this doesn't necessarily preclude the idea of specific Avatars coming out of the Embrace worse than others. Especially since the creation of the Tremere vampires was an unusual one. If we assume that Avatars cannot stand the full infusion of Vitae - hence why they don't stick around even though an Embraced mage is "revived" - the Avatars of the Tremere founders could have violently ejected themselves as they performed their grisly rite. So violently, they broke apart upon exiting.

                              It would easily justify a character having the Shattered Avatar Merit. I don't think Avatars would appreciate absorbing shards of Avatars other than themselves, though. Maybe if the PCs don't have shards of those Avatars, they could still profit from retrieving them. Either by performing some kind of rite to communicate with the shards (and thus derive memories from them), or by bringing them to mages who share the Avatar, in exchange for knowledge.
