            Base Forces

            Purview: Energy Patterns. Actually the initial tag says ‘Raising Storms’ which is only the tiniest fraction of Forces.

            Special Rules and Limitations

            Two rules are given here:

            1 - Forces based attacks get an additional success of damage. While important, this doesn’t change much about how the Sphere progresses.

            2 - Large scale effects require higher levels of expertise (and often more successes). This is less a rule and more an explanation of why larger uses of low rank abilities take higher ranks, and it has quite the effect on how Forces works.

                Forces 1

                • Perceive Forces
                Unlocking basic perceptions of the elements, a Forces savvy mage can sense the flow of forces in her environment. She can boost her perceptions into the infrared or ultraviolet spectrums, notice electrons, see in the dark, view X-rays, discern the flow of sound or radio waves, spot kinetic energies, and hear frequencies beyond the normal human range.
                The descriptions here are mostly about expanding the senses that already deal with forces, sight and sound. There are a few possibilities left out, but from this description, Forces 1 can:


                Expand visual range and sensitivity (Perceive Forces)

                With this power a mage can expand the spectrum he can see to just about any wavelength, letting him see heat (infrared), ultraviolet, radio waves, and even cosmic radiation. He can also increase his sensitivity to pick out the faintest of signals.



                Stuck in a Skittles Commercial

                Despite being written as expanding vision, this could apply to any sense. If someone really wanted to be able to taste the rainbow, he could use Forces 1. Presumably, some CoXs frequently use sphere sensing to give themselves synesthesia. It seems like their thing.

                More seriously, being able to feel out heat or electromagnetic fields with touch or smell might suit many mages better than using vision.


                Added: Vastly Expand Spell Range (Lord Revan)

                By default, a mage can cast on anything in sensory range. By dramatically zooming his sense of vision or enhancing his sense of hearing (or any other sense) a mage can increase his sensory range and thus cast much, much farther than usual without the use of Correspondence.



                Expand aural range and sensitivity (Perceive Forces)

                Pretty much the exact same thing as expanding visual range, but with sound.


                Echolocation

                By enhancing his hearing (and his processing with Mind 1) a mage can build a physical map of the surrounding area.


                Other Mediums

                Sound doesn’t travel the same way in water or other mediums, but Forces 1 should be able to make adjustments for this.


                Super Slow Play

                The human brain usually doesn’t recognize beats below 33 beats per minute as rhythms. I’m not sure how helpful it is, but a mage should be able to use Forces 1 to extend that limit down.
                Note: since writing this, I’ve grown less convinced it works as a Forces effect. Let me know what you think.


                See Other Forces (Perceive Forces)

                A very important catch all, for two reasons. First, not every force is light or sound, and second, not every mage will think in terms of the electromagnetic spectrum. This power lets a mage set his senses to types of energy he can’t normally sense. The two examples given are seeing kinetic energy and noticing electrons, but others, like seeing sources of gravity or magnetic fields would also be possible.


                Neutrino Blinding

                Fun fact, it is possible to share sphere senses with another person. The Spirit 2 writeup even suggests a method: tacking on Life 2 (though it is Spirit talking about Life, so take it with a grain of salt). An overwhelming input like, say, seeing neutrinos could effectively shut down someone’s senses. It doesn’t have to be neutrinos, just boosting sensitivity to light to extremes would work as well, but where’s the fun in that?

                Missing Abilities:

                Sense Forces Patterns (Perceive Forces)
                Sometimes it isn’t as important to be able to actually sense a force as to know it is there. Forces 1 is also capable of sensing patterns themselves, even ones the mage hasn’t already keyed himself to see. For instance, with this ability a mage can detect the presence of a fire on the other side of a wall, without using his eyes. With more successes he can also tell if a force is being manipulated.

                                                Forces 2

                                                •• Manipulate Forces/ Elemental Touch

                                                Although she cannot conjure energies just yet, the mage may now alter the flow of existing forces. Sound can be muted or amplified; shadows can be gathered, shaped, or dispelled. The mage can warp light to make things invisible, change their colors by altering the spectrum of localized light, or render them silent by bending sound waves in their vicinity. With a wave of her hand, she can disrupt electrical currents; flare flames; or direct the course of winds, momentum, or gravity.

                                                Combining this Rank with other Spheres, that mage could also make an object (Matter) or organism (Life) attract or repel forces. Thus, she could weave minor protection spells or force-fields… or, conversely, turn that target into a magnet (perhaps literally) for the forces in question.

                                                Despite its powers, this Rank is limited in scale. Assume that the mage can command energies around a single human sized character or within a small area (20’ or less). To affect a larger area, you need a higher Rank in Forces.
                                                For all the verbiage, this is basically one ability, which is Manipulate Forces. That one ability comes in a few flavors though. Forces 2 can:


                                                Enhance or Suppress Forces (Manipulate Forces)

                                                A mage can power up or down a given force, flaring matches into bonfires, dissipating the electricity of a circuit, or pulling the kinetic energy out of a moving object.
                                                By a strict reading, there is no indication of a limitation on this power other than area, so the only thing standing between a lit match and a small blast furnace is successes.


                                                There Is Always Light

                                                There really is always light of some variety around, even if just that of ambient infrared. By shifting it up in frequency and amplifying it, a mage is never without a light source. More mystically inclined mages can do the same by dispelling darkness.


                                                Power Surge

                                                Enhancing the current within the wires of a building can easily pop a breaker and enhancing them within an electronic device can easily damage the device. Also, enhancing the static charge around someone could lead to quite a static shock.


                                                Redirect Forces (Manipulate Forces)

                                                The mage can also move forces around and change their direction or flow. Moving objects can be redirected by changing their kinetic energy, electricity can be arced wherever the mage wants, and light can be concentrated or wrapped around a target.

                                                By a strict reading, there is no given limit on the complexity of these manipulations, so barring some ST ruling the only thing between a mage and a very complex bit of manipulation is successes.

                                                This is the power behind the suggested force fields and magnets.

                                                Contradiction Resolution

                                                This is barely an issue. It’s clearly Forces 2 to direct forces towards a certain point, and it doesn’t stop being Forces 2 if something is at that point. But if you want that point to move with something else… that is describing locking spheres. I don’t like it when M20 pads out sphere descriptions with locking spheres, but that’s what’s going on.


                                                Holograms

                                                By manipulating light, or sound, or momentum, or all three a mage can create physical illusions much like Star Trek’s holodecks. There is some indication that this may be easier if Prime 2's Body of Light is used as a base, but as written Forces 2 should be capable of this on its own.


                                                Flight

                                                By manipulating momentum with a small amount of finesse a mage can move himself or others around through the air. This is a spell that works much better with locking spheres. Without those it often turns into:


                                                Yeet!

                                                By manipulating momentum to provide a short burst, a mage can launch a target in a given direction. He can also cast this on a location such that anything that enters that spot gets launched in that direction.
                                                ​

                                                Vastly Augmented Spell Range

                                                Forces 1 let a mage expand his visual range. Forces 2 lets him actually bend light, meaning he can now expand his visual range around corners and around the horizon. ​
