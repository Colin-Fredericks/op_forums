                                  Originally posted by Spacecat
                                  You should Probably find a way to use Mind 3 and Correspondence 3 to ban mind reading and control, as I think the ban is much tougher than your usual mind shielding
                                  Pilling on this:

                                  Mind 1 it's enough to shield/ban, but because Mind it's the Sphere of illusions, at higher levels you can control what the spy sees instead of merely causing the attempt to fail. An example would be the Mind 2 No Mind of the Akashicks that makes potential mental spies detect no toughts at all instead of knowingly fail. I would say that at 3 you could do things like craft fake tougths and memories to be read.

                                  Corr 2 bans scrying (may be essential in magely warfare). Arguably with Mind 3 you could make failed would be scriers see an illusion instead - which not only controls the flow of information, but also discourages trying again with more successes.

                                  With Corr 3/Mind 3, I think you could arguably be able to cause damage to anyone that tries to control your mind, as per the more aggresive wards of HDYDT (Aggravated with Prime 2, ouch! not very nice, but then, neither it's mind control).
                                  For guns Corr allows amazing shoots. You can potentially teleport a shoot anywhere in the world you have a connection with, but that's likely going Vulgar. Less Vulgar would be to make the bullets change trajectory, which Corr can also do. Eventually you may want to get Forces if you want to go for the gun toothing Mage.

                                  OP already said scanning, still it bears repeating: Knowing where the targets are it's very good for sniping, to ambush enemies, and to counter potential snipers and other ambushes. And about ambushes, Mind being the sphere of illusions, at 3 it arguably should be able to hide your pressence from the minds of others.

                                  It may be relevant to mention that the spells you will cast and how you will cast them it's largely informed by your Focus (and general approach to magick). If you tell us what's your Focus, that could be a source of more thematical ideas for your character
