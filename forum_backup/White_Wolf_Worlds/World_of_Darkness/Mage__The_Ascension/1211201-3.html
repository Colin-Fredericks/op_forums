So instead of casting their Arete against all effects, they only roll Arete against effects which are Vulgar, and they always count as a Witness. They are also stricter than the HAB.             As stated in the revised book of madness, nephandi are perfectly capable of using the magic they had before they passed through the cauls, and in many cases they absolutely must in order to avoid detection.

            The qlippothic spheres function almost identically to the standard spheres though may leave a more bitter resonance, but then most great mages have all kinds of resonances so that alone won’t be a great indicator.

            What truly sets the qlippothic spheres apart is that, by the sheer power of destruction, they can push beyond the limitations of the standard spheres.

            Q-Prime, for example, can rend the quintessence from ones avatar, draining a mage of quintessence entirely. While Prime cannot take the quintessence within the avatar by force.
            Q-Mind can ransack the targets mind of memories and identity and willpower, leaving them a hollow shell fit for any purpose the mage sees fit. While Mind is usually unable to completely remove all fragments of memory, even at mastery, as WillPower can still be used to attempt resistance.
            Q-corr. can, as you say, tear holes in the fabric of space while traveling between them, rendering the passage permanently forged, burnt into the very fabric of being. While standar Corr. Such a feat would only be possible with high ritual, and even then be simple to dispel.
            Other examples include Q-Spirit allowing nephandi to ignore the avatar storm entirely, or at least suffer marginal effects from it. As well as corrupt spirits towards their line of thinking (known to some as wyrm taint, known to others as just accepting reality).
            Q-Forces being able to counter and absorb any force based attacks by being their complete opposite, attacking primarily with void energy, stillness, and anti-heat, not just cold, but the inverse of heat itself.
            Q-Life can be used to absorb life from those nearby.
            Q-Matter can make fractals of complexity by breaking things down smaller and smaller, in much the way they build their labyrinths.
            Q-Time allowing for the complete removal of time within a localised area, trapping enemies into a perpetual “now”.
            And Q-Entropy, of course, is ravenous undoing. Chance is destroyed, as is fate, as all things that were being built up simply disperse. They can cause all things to crumble around them, be it buildings, plans, or alliances. Hope itself can be destroyed through this sphere alone.
