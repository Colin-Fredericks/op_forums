    Originally posted by Ramnesis View Post
    There are a lot of positives for the Traditions in seizing all of NYC from the Technocracy. In one blow they crack the Syndicate's global infrastructure and the NWO's media network. They open one of the biggest travel locations as a melting pot of ideas and cultures. The subsequent crash devastates many parts of the world, giving the Trads an opportunity to step in and show alternate ways of living. It's a bold move that has two major drawbacks:

    1) The reprisals from the Technocracy would be massive.
    2) They would have to ruin the lives of a lot of sleepers to do it.

    The first is bad, the second is worse. Some Trad members would be okay with that kind of collateral damage, many would not.

    On the other hand, there is an occupation of NYC that is even more effective for the Traditions. The Technocracy gets to keep their infrastructure, but the Traditions hold the rest of the city. This leaves much of the Technocracy infrastructure intact, but that infrastructure can be monitored and sometimes even used by the Traditions. By not isolating the city, they give their new reality machinations a chance to spread.
    Can the Traditions use the Technocracy infrastructure without changing it so much that it's effectively gutting it and, effectively causing all the problems that you postulate?

    The biggest example: Wall Street is set up entirely along Syndicate lines. Sure, it's possible to exploit that system, but those exploits (VA data manipulation, House Fortunae statistical manipulation, anoyone using Time 2 or Entropy 2, etc.) don't really change the Syndicate's system, they USE it. Wall Street and all those little tricks and games Wall Street people play with economics are all the Syndicate dug so deep into their Foci and Instruments that you can't even see the tips of their toes. Other mages (particularly Tradition types) don't use those Foci and Instruments, and, even when they do, they don't do so from the "same place". (Yeah, there is definitely something above/beyond mages' personal foci... Someting associated with their Sect that is sort of a mini-Consensus zeitgeist that creates the "Hermetic-ness" of the OoH, and so on....) There are essential aspects of Wall Street that function because the Syndicate is pushing, prodding, tweaking, massaging, manipulating, breaking, fixing, etc. their big "machine". Can House Fortunae take up the slack for that? And what would that look like? Would the "new brokers' start seeming like fortune (ha!ha!) tellers using complex diagrams, magical numerological formula, gematria, etc. while structuring their bids in Arabic, Sanskrit, Ancient Greek, Egyptian, Persian and Enochian? Even if that is going on behind closed doors, won't the mundanes of Wall Street have to realize that something BIG has happened and things don't work the way they used to? Or even the way they are working in Hong Kong, Chicago, etc.? That lack of confidence in the system is enough right there to bring it crashing down. The panic as most of the big firms go down when NOTHING works right anymore... The new players and the attempts to co-opt them into the current power structure....

    I have to admit, I really do love my image of the "New Wall Street" as recast by House Fortunae.... But if I try to think about it without a really artificial sense of isolation that ignores the interconnectedness of things, that Wall Street is going to come crashing down and, due to it's fundamental incompatibility with the rest of the world economy as dictated by the Syndicate that didn't get ousted, isn't going to climb out of that hole. Perhaps the Syndicate can do significant damage control and, say, shift the focus of the world economy elswhere, but, in that case, there is definitely a second part to the game.... The mess OUTSIDE this New New York....

            So the whole thing smacks of just replacing vampires with mages and using the set up from the Original World of Darkness...

            The problem with that is... well, there are lots of problems. Like why two buildings blowing up is going to subvert the authority, much less the actual magical power of the Union and, conversely, why that same scenario should give Traditions mages opportunities to take any sort of power held by the Technocracy in mortal society, much less how such an event could promote the many different paradigms of the Traditions over that of Technocracy. And if it's more a matter of BOOM! BOOM! go the planes, BOOM! BOOM! the towers fall, and, for some reason, that weakens the Union's paradigm, so that, effectively, the Traditions all get a "boost" by default..... Well, that means all the mortal stuff that goes along with the Union's paradigm is going to take a hit, probably an initial one, particularly dealing with technological connections with the outside world and, probably, over time, a general degeneration of the underlying pervasive technological infrastructure.

            Assuming the situation as originally laid out, this seems less like a "Cool! Tradition mages don't have to worry about the Man, anymore! Let's chat about it on our cellphones, go get some lattes and post pics on Instagram of some little ritual magics we do out in open, BECAUSE WE CAN! <Insert rude hand gestures at absent Technocrats", and more "OMG! OMG! The towers are fall...." (static... then dead air as all cell phones and other forms of radio communication go dark... forever) set-up for a Post-Apocalyptic game with hard scramble Tradition mages trying to carve out enclaves, the most successful of which are going to be based around the paradigms of singular Traditions, with all sorts of enclave to enclave politics and conflict and lots and lots and LOTS of people suffering and dying and trying to escape...

            And, if those things aren't true, there is really no way to keep the Technocrats from coming back in... They've got Correspondence, too. Plus all the other ways creative mages can do that sort of thing, particularly when they've fundamentally rigged reality to ease their way.

            To get the particular feel I am reading into the OP, you are going to have to be very careful. There has to be a reason the Union can't just show back up. There has to be a way to keep the city intact so that it feels like an "NYC" game (Friends meets Mage?) and not a game set in something that is somewhat like NYC but, for most purposes, is something completely "other" with NYC landmarks. Those two are hard to balance against each other within the constraints of Mage's fluff and crunch....

            Maybe the blast released "something" that is creating a "get the F out Union" field. (Was it intentional? Was 9/11 really a Taftani act of reality insurgency? Or is it something to do with history of NYC? There are a LOT of weird things in the history NYC that might play well into something like that.... Or even something to do with why the Lenape might have been willing to sell Manhattan in the first place.... Hells, could be both. Stuff gets complicated fast.) Maybe the Union is still able to send in Extraordinary Citizens to keep its interests going. Maybe some of the new Traditions leadership in town realize that it's going to take letting the Union hold onto things like Wall Street and the UN, etc. even if they have to do so by proxy, or else the city/world is going to go to Hell in a handbasket. A new group promoting a parallel to the Union status quo, backed by the Law of Shadow... Meet the new boss(es), same as the old boss.... Sets up a nice conflict with these status quo high and mighty and some Trad mages who see opportunities to make "real" change (and damn the consequences... or, "let's examine my careful ten point plan to make NYC a Hermetic wet dream without making it into something like the shell of a city in the midst of a war"). Fun for the PCs to get caught up in. Plus, you KNOW the Union is trying to take this down from the outside... carefully. Or else they might be breaking what they are buying,

Intel on reality deviants suggests they inevitably start fighting each other over meaningless differences (completely unlike the Technocracy... completely), so a waiting game or a psychological war might be the way to go.
