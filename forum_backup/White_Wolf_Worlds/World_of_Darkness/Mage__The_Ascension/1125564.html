    True Faith for each Paradigm/Tradition/etc.

    08-06-2017, 08:52 AM
    So I'm pretty fond of M20's True Faith. I think it does make Faith very powerful indeed, but it stays within the limits of what it should be capable of. Naturally True Faith makes the most sense with the Celestial Chorus (and those Disparates who behave like Knights Templar), but I was thinking about how Faith could be applied to the different types of Mage. True Faith of course cannot be an antagonist to your paradigm, and there has to be some synergy... any conflict would mean a low Arete at my table, which you could increase through seekings which reconcile the differences between your Faith and your Paradigm.

    For example, in the Cult of Ecstasy you could have a liberal Christian who believes in God-given tools. Conscience, pleasure, love, passion, these are all sacred gifts from God, and a large portion of Christianity is missing the point on this. Yes the union of a husband and wife in matrimony, conjugal love and children are all great, but it's too SPECIFIC a form of "hedonism" and it only works for some people. This Ecstatic believes in worshiping the wonderful things God has given us, and seeking "inclusive hedonism" as a form of ascension (people who are starving can't enjoy themselves, ergo we should do something about that). This would work well with Creation is Divine and alive, with Prayer, Meditation, Sex and Sensuality etc. Their Faith would be likely labelled as profane by many more conservative Christians, especially a Chorister. I could imagine them being deeply antagonized by the hedonistic "you do you" type being able to call upon divine aid.

    Any ideas for the other Traditions? How about the Conventions and the Disparates?

          M20 says the belief must be sincere, and it must be a religious tradition with some temporal depth; no True Faith in post-modern concepts like the Flying Spaghetti Monster. True Faith in a fictional (one of the mythic NPCs in the WoD) or outdated (a religion which is practically speaking dead, like Roman Polytheism) deity? Cool! True Faith in any of the major world religions? Cool! ST can always say that a type of Faith isn't suited to their table; True Faith in Bulgroth the Unclean is certainly possible, even thematically appropriate, but it might not be good for your game.

          The question is how you make that system fit the different paradigms, if you want to do so. For example, I can't see any of the Abrahamic Religions and Hermetic Sorcery being perfect fits, however I'm sure there would be an interesting way of doing it.
