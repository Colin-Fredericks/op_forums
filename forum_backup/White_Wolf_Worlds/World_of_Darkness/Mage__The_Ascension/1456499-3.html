                Ascension's Landscape (Storyteller's Vault) talks about this issue in a more general way: celebrities and the wealthy have tremendous mundane power, and most of them don't have the means to defend themselves against supernatural influence; so why isn't every last one of them in the thrall of some supernatural forces or another? The book goes through several possible answers for that, all of which boil down to some variation of “there is some one or some thing that's looking out for the rich and famous, and anyone who decides to target them will end up running into it.”

                One fairly simple suggestion that out makes is that there might be an ongoing worldwide Effect that automatically provides countermagic against mental influence based on how much Resources you have: in practical terms, it lets you use your Resources Background as countermagic dice on any attempt to apply mental influence to you. I mention it to illustrate the sorts of possibilities the book suggests; I would definitely recommend picking it up and considering its advice where this diva is concerned.

                And again, the Technocracy has been in a war against supernatural powers since its founding; and at least as of Technocracy Reloaded, the notion that they don't believe in the supernatural has been buried. It stands to reason, then, that they would have developed countermeasures against supernatural interference. See my earlier post for some further thoughts on this direction.

                But another possibility would be to say that there are some Technocrats who flat-out don't believe in magic, and who are in Denial; and that some of these end up weaponizing their Denial along the lines of the way that Marauders weaponize Madness: see Negation Men in Book of Secrets. Including one Negation Man in her entourage would throw a monkey wrench into the plans of any magickal threats aimed at her. (Though you might want to tweak the Negation Man concept slightly if you do this; as written, they're more like the Autumn People from Changeling, wed to extreme mundanity and equally a threat to technomancers and mystics alike.)

                My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

                    That going to the extremes. Protections against mental tampering and some similar resources are enough to deal with most supernaturals whose goals and methods are still civilized. Your main Toreador threat is an uncouth fan? The role of her security is to keep the vampire from harming and Ghouling, not from being annoying.

                    Instead of focusing in security, or better, on her bodyguards, think about her staff as a whole. Have someone analyzing her food and drinks before she consumes, if it can't be done from a distance with Enlightened methods, then pose a poisoning attempt to justify this level of paranoia as an empathetic quirk. Hack cameras in the area to detect supernatural presences before they strike. Remember that you don't have to take BS from RDs if they're too troublesome, you can call a squad to hunt down the problem if need be. This Toreador mere existence is only acceptable to the Union as far as it stays out of the way and out of problem.

                    Some problems are still her in the end, as they are for any celebrity. Problematic fans happen. Her security system is there for the protection of her integrity, but she is her own main public relations guy. Talk with the vampire, try to reason, and use Magick if needed.
