      I don't complicate myself too much. I just show my players google images of the offices and headquarters of both fictional and non-fictional organizations. Depending on the theme of the adventure I go for different examples: if it's your typical spy or crime investigating game I pick the FBI or CIA, if it's more militaristic oriented then I use military bases, and if it's full-blown fantasy or sci-fi I use Men in Black, Mass Effect, or the Control videogame. Originality is good and all, but don't bog yourself down when you have so much material to work with. You can always adapt it to meet your preferences.

          This approach works well for my current Construct Adventure, because the players will already have been supplied a map by a Macro-Technician mole. Likely, the small Construct will be an outpost in a realm whose denizens are hostile to Technocracy and Traditions alike. I am thinking Hollow Earth with an Etherite who didn't get the mass-defection signal, a Progenitor and It-X. Plot-wise, I'm thinking that the team was separated for a long while.

          Another sort of open concept would be a Tourism and Diplomacy Construct. The Ascension War is a debate as much as anything. Why not showcase the best your faction has to offer and allow guests to explore that ideal place.

          For a longer and more challenging infiltration adventure, I think a map is a good idea. It is funny how, back when I ran D&D, I had plentiful resources for making low level dungeons, but no ability to justify why and how such dungeons came to exist. Now, I can easily explain how a Construct came to be, but have scarce resources for their design. What I am considering is to make some posts others could contribute to with the following titles:

          Rooms List (simply that - security booth, lab, living quarters, hallway, etc.)
          Effects List (Procedures cast on the environment and architecture)
          Realms List (a list of ideas for Horizon Realms or other places where Constructs could be located)
          Denizens List (creatures that exist in the Realm, Construct or both)
          Objects List (furniture, equipment, tools, toys and weapons)

          Another thing would be to search out links on labyrinth design and just post those here. All of that is if there would be enough interest.

              One thing that I want to point out is that if you follow the metaplot it should affect the designs of the constructs. I do, as such I have two approaches.

              First, my technocracy has become very "down to earth", as in almost all of their bases are situated on earth, and they have been designed to rely on cutting-edge sleeper tech first to avoid magical incidents. The avatar storm put an end to the age of big and extravagant horizon bases. Nowadays, horizon bases are few and far between, with ten times the protocols and security measures that they used to have before the storm. They exist only to conduct the research and activities that absolutely can't be done on earth. So, it's either technothriller spy bases on earth or sci-fi fortresses in the horizon realms.

              Second, since the avatar storm has calmed down, what happened to the other horizon constructs and the people that lived there? Ah, that's where I make things really weird and surreal. My interpretation of "void adaptation" is that without proper protection, the horizon morphs things into their "ideal" or metaphysical aspects at the expense of their physical ones. So when agents travel to these constructs to conduct S&R (salvage and rescue) operations, what they find is an exaggerated parody or horror version of what that construct used to stand for. Picture the oldest house in the Control videogame, the SCP headquarters (with all the cages open), or even a bootleg version of the death star.

              I hope these examples help.

                  In case you're looking for a more recent example, I highly recommend the videogame Deathloop. The whole story could be seen as a technocratic agent trapped in a broken horizon realm ruled by 7 marauders and trying to escape while being hunted by another marauder.
