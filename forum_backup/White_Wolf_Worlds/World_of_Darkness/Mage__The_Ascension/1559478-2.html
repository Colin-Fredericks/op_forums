                    The first and primary reason for the thread is to look at the game reasons, game balance or thematic or otherwise, for the Otherworlds being described the way they currently are. Then, in light of those reasons, look at ways of reworking Otherworlds travel to eliminate the various contradictions and special pleading that Mage is full of.

                    The Penumbra has taken center stage because of how the Vidare Mortem works with the Shadowlands, or more accurately, how it doesn’t. It makes for a very good example and poster child, but there are other issues too.

                    The Dreaming Penumbra is a lesser issue because we can reasonably go either way. Mage is explicit that the Underworld is an Umbra (or part of the greater Umbra). It says no such thing about the Dreaming. Now it is rather easy to view the Dreaming as an Umbra in its own right because it has that broadly similar structure that runs from largely earthlike and even mapping to the physical world on one end, to increasingly divorced/rarified as you get farther out. We can push it into that mold, but we are not obligated to like we are with the Underworld. Dreaming travel itself is important, but we can consider that whether there is a Dreaming Penumbra or not.

                    As an aside, even when I do consider a Dreaming Penumbra I don’t think of it as a reliable way of getting to the rest of the Dreaming. It would be by far the rarest to have a connection with the physical and the hardest to find from the rest of the Penumbra. I think entering through dreams and demenses would be the preferred method.

                    Following up on one other thing, if the Penumbra is an interstitial or liminal space between realms, it would make sense that there are frequencies of it beyond the big three. It also means something doesn’t have to be an Umbra for the Penumbra to touch upon it. In fact it would open the possibility that the Astral, Wilds, and Underworld are fundamentally different things that happen to touch on the same space. I still like that idea, though it is just one possible view. However, it is also worth noting that even if it is such a space, it isn’t the only one. The Midrealm and Mount Qaf are both said to touch upon many other realms. It isn’t necessary that all of them touch upon everything with the same level of ease.

                            I like the idea that the Dreaming has interactions outside of what the Fae and other glamour aligned creatures know. The Otherworlds often are bigger than any one group can see, even one that is closely intertwined with them.

                            Whether other Realms (and here I'm using that term for the broad Realms, not the individual realms within them) have Penumbrae is going to depend a great deal on what Penumbrae are. I think in this thread we've seen a few general possibilities.

                            1) The Penumbra is a transitory zone between the physical and multiple Otherworlds. Because of the interaction of those Otherworlds it can take on the flavor or frequency of them.

                            2) Each Penumbra is an interface between the physical and another Otherworld. For various metaphysical reasons it is often possible to move from one Penumbra to a different Penumbra associated with the same physical location.

                            3) The Penumbra is an interstitial space between multiple Otherworlds. A location in the Penumbra frequently takes on elements from the closest Worlds to it. One of these locations is almost always the Physical, which is why it usually looks like a shadow of the physical. Whether or not that's an actual metaphysical rule is unclear.

                            Those are very broad strokes and I could easily make the list longer just by subdividing them.

                                One of the things that complicates the Otherworlds is the methods of travel, because they are bit strange, particularly the big three (Stepping Sideways, Astral Projection, and Out of Body Experience aka The Agama Sojourn). If I had to hazard a guess why, it’s because they are a mix of bringing in elements from other game lines, protecting other game lines, and replicating real world practices.

                                For the big three, each one corresponds to one of the three main Umbras, though they are not always exclusive to them.

                                Stepping Sideways means piercing the Gauntlet and stepping to the other side, transforming from a physical body to a spiritual one or back depending on the direction. Despite the change, mechanically the body uses largely the same rules. This is the method that leans towards the spiritual, but it is also the generic method. A spiritual body will function in the Penumbra, the lower reaches of the Astral, and all of the Spirit Wilds.

                                This is the simplest method, in terms of Sphere cost, at Spirit 3 (or 4 for others).

                                Much like the Middle Umbra this one seems to be largely a port from Werewolf.



                                Astral Projection means projecting the mind from the body to float around on its own, though still connected by a silver cord. This body is largely ephemeral and does not interact with the physical. This method leans towards the mental/Astral but it is not limited to that and doesn’t even have to be a form of Otherworlds travel at all. It can float about the physical, the Penumbra, and the Astral as well as project into other things, like objects, people, and dreams. A mage can build himself a spiritual body to project into, though, which will let him traverse the Spirit Wilds as well.

                                Astral Projection alone requires slightly more training than Stepping Sideways, at Mind 4 (or 5 for others). Building a spiritual body requires Spirit 3/Prime 2 (plus Mind 4(5) to project into it).

                                This one is the most Mage specific.



                                OBE means bringing the body to a state of near death or suspended animation, allowing the soul to project directly to the Shadowlands as a ghost. This body is very much like that of a wraith. This method leans the most towards the Underworldly.

                                The reason I’m calling it OBE rather than the Agama Sojourn is that Mage, for some strange reason, assumes the only reason anyone would want to do this is Chakravanti Initiation. Considering there are enough other reasons to be the basis of an entire WoD game line, Orpheus, that’s a strange assumption.

                                This is the most stringent method, coming in at Entropy 4/Spirit 3/Life 2(3), and its the one I'm most inclined to change. There’s not an explanation for Entropy I’d suggest dropping it entirely. In light of Orpheus Sleepers, I’d also consider letting Life 2(3) alone let a mage make the initial projection into the Shadowlands, but any subsequent Shroud/Gauntlet crossings would still take Spirit 3.

                                I can't know the minds of the authors, but it seems like this one was set up begrudgingly. It is in many ways a port from Wraith, but with the decision to make it very rare and difficult.




                                If we drop M20s Schrodinger’s Penumbra approach to the Shadowlands and just say they are Penumbral, that would mean that the Astral and Spiritual bodies can visit at least the Shadowlands, while ghostly bodies can visit the rest of the Penumbra. There is no guidance for how far the former can get into the Underworld or how far the latter can get elsewhere.
                                I can actually think of some novel uses to adapt these more broadly, but that’s for another time.



                                There are other methods of Otherworlds travel, though I largely don’t think they have the same kind of problems. The main two I can think of are:

                                Realm Specific Methods. This covers travel to specific Otherworlds based on their themes and metaphysics. Access to the Digital Web falls into this category, as would accessing the Dream Zone through a demenses. I think there are some realms within the larger high and middle umbras that can be accessed this way, but I don’t recall for certain.

                                Extant Gateways and Portals. Previously set up portals can take a mage just about anywhere, including to any of the Otherworlds. These could be naturally occurring Shallowings, or Gateways created by long dead mages, or moon bridges, or anything. They could require specific actions to open, or key spells, or anything. These are largely dictated by the narrative rather than any system. This can include the gateways into Horizon Realms (though that might also fall under Realm Specific Methods).

                                I may be missing some others, but I think that covers most of it.

                                        First a correction: Astral Projection was ported from Vampire, even if there is no mention to other realms, but is where the idea of being immaterial in the material enters WoD.

                                        As for missing methods, you left out the Paths of Wyrd, that seem to let you travel without any Magic if you know the labyrinthine steps, but may also be just a very weird Instrument.

                                        That aside, for the methods I agree with Aleph that using Spirit as an all-purpose baseline and then explore others as tradeoff alternatives with less reach is ideal. In a sense you're robbing Spirit of its whole shtick if you say an otherworld is off-limits to the Sphere of otherworlds.

                                        What I would do:

                                        Stepping Sideways - go to an "adjacent" realm. As a rule of thumb that's the Penumbra, but certain places can offer stranger options. The rule of Frequency applies and is built in the Sphere.

                                        Astral Projection - leave the body behind, has the benefits and drawbacks associated. You can start your journey at mind-oriented places or as a disembodied mind in the Material. You can start your journey in the Astral if you want, the Dream Zone or the Dreaming depending on where you are, or the respective Penumbra frequencies. This converts your astral body into an actual local body, but the silver cord remains. You can use other available methods as usual to travel further, or repeat the spell to change the mental Realm you're in.

                                        Greatest advantage: your body is where you left it, and you can at any time Magic your way to your own mind, though this may not be much safe if you don't have a Demense. That's a safe-ish travel.

                                        OBE - It deposits you in a death-related Realm, which includes, but isn't limited to, the Underworld. This is essentially your disembodied soul, which makes it Wraith like and even susceptible to the same rules. You can use other Magics to travel to other Realms, but you can't repeat the spell during your travels, so brace yourself to danger.

                                        Particulars of this:

                                        1. You're not limited to collocation, your arrival can happen at any point of a Death Realm you are familiar with, without extra Spheres.

                                        2. Body behind is a slight protection. Having your soul body destroyed can have all sorts of repercussions, but will generally wake you in your body with whatever they are. Having your soul trapped, though, is another story.

                                        3. Probably an easier time with Effects that a Wraith could do without much XP, at least. For a lot of effects you can pass for one or Magic yourself into imitating one with ease.

                                        4. I'd seriously consider lowering requirements but making it dangerous. Putting yourself in a near death state isn't without risk, not only of dying in case something goes wrong and your body don't get help, but of coming back with some consequence of the physical side of the experience.

                                        Paths of Wyrd - at baseline do not require any Magic. If you do find a true Path, it's just a matter of following it and dropping wherever in the way you want. Maps for those Paths exist, but errors can't be retraced, if you step out and leave the Path at some point, going back isn't just a matter of going back, at each point the entering has a proper method. You're traveling bodily, so take care.

                                        Particulars of this:

                                        1. All sorts of Magic can interact with the Paths of Wyrd or guide you through them. You can use Corresponce to find them, map them or manipulate them, you can use Entropy to avoid leaving the Path or even finding a "good" one without knowing or mapping it.

                                        2. Paths are locked. You don't control where you go in any special way.

                                        3. Paths are potentially dangerous as they have environment, travelers and inhabitants. Since this can be quite a roundabout journey, that's not a small thing.

                                        4. Time is wonky in a Path, each in its own way. Outside that, they're also quite long to walk.

                                        Transition Zones - several, maybe infinite "all-touching" realities exist. Those places connect to other worlds in particular points, and internally follow their own spatial logic. A castle full of doors that deposit you elsewhere with no way back. A seemingly infinite staircase in a void where each landing has a door that does exist in the other side. A giant World Tree where branches do follow some structure you can follow, but guarded by strange inhabitants. A misty expanse where time doesn't exist, full of portals you can only have a rough idea where they'll deposit you.

                                        Particulars of this:

                                        1. Each Transition Zone has its own entry and exit rules. Some can be accessed with ease by anyone, some even by accident, while others require special Magics.

                                        2. They can be explored more or less freely, as wandering around won't kick you out (usually).

                                        3. Distance between exit and entry points are unpredictable, though most are faster than a Path of Wyrd.

                                        4. To find one of those you can use Spirit, though it's usually necessary to know what you're looking for, as they have their own Frequencies.

                                            I wasn’t specifically thinking about Paths of the Wyrd. I did consider Transition Zones, though, I just didn’t enumerate them. Realms like that have their own specific entry and exit rules which falls under Realm Specific Methods. I didn't flesh those out too much, though I may come back to it later.

                                            In regards to the utility of Stepping Sideways, I didn’t try to curtail its range. Mage locked that form out of the higher reaches of the Astral. Same for the Underworld, though we are discussing fixing that one.

                                            That said, I’m not completely on board with it being the baseline. It might be the most comfortable or versatile, but I’d rather lean into the three as fundamentally different approaches with advantages and disadvantages.

                                            I do think projection and OBE should be better at navigating the Astral and Underworld respectively. That doesn’t have to be handled by locking other forms out of those areas, though.

                                            This is what I see as the major advantages and disadvantages of each method.

                                            Stepping Sideways
                                            Advantages
                                            • You mostly have the body you are used to (its made of spirit but it feels and functions mostly the same)
                                            • You can cross back over anywhere; you don’t have to return to a body
                                            • This form is the best at navigating the Spirit Wilds
                                            • This form is potentially the most versatile or functional of the forms when it comes to working outside its ideal World
                                            • You can bring things with you without issue
                                            Disadvantages
                                            • You mostly have the body you are used to
                                            • There’s no quick way back or emergency escape
                                            • If you are trapped, you have to deal with acclimation
                                            • The cost of death is death

                                            Astral Projection
                                            Advantages
                                            • This form is naturally capable of flight
                                            • This form can operate in the physical world
                                            • This form has direct and quick access to large areas of the Astral
                                            • This method can bypass the gauntlet difficulty (I think)
                                            • This method has an easy form of return (by the silver cord)
                                            • This form is the best at navigating the Astral
                                            • The cost of death is going adrift
                                            Disadvantages
                                            • This form loses access to physical traits
                                            • This form has a vulnerability in the form of the silver cord
                                            • This form has difficulty interacting in more physical realms
                                            • You have to return to the point you started from
                                            • The physical body is left vulnerable
                                            • Being adrift means a limited amount of time to find your way back before death

                                            OBE
                                            A lot depends on details of how the spell works. Going with a version where you automatically manifest in the Shadowlands, and if the spell ends you snap back if you can...
                                            Advantages
                                            • The ghost body is potentially the most durable form
                                            • The ghost form may be able to cross back over and operate in the physical world
                                            • OBEs bypass the difficulty of the local gauntlet/shroud
                                            • This form has direct access to the Shadowlands
                                            • Return is automatic if the spell ends
                                            • This form is the best at navigating the Underworld
                                            • This form can potentially pass as that of a normal ghost
                                            • The cost of death is harrowings
                                            Disadvantages
                                            • This form has some interaction with the Physical world even from the Shadowlands
                                            • This form is subject to harrowings and long term damage from them
                                            • You have to return to the point you started from
                                            • The physical body is left very vulnerable
                                            Since part of this is hashing out how OBE should work, this is all subject to change.

                                                I think this approach for now has two problems in the concept, not execution:

                                                1. Astral Projection and OBE are things that exist in real life occult and have actual cultural practices. Stepping Sideways is a holdover from Werewolf. Actual shamanic journeys are more linked to trances of drug use, with the same effect of the body being left behind. It changes more in the sense that its felt as an embodied experience, as if the shaman inhabits a spiritual form that's solid there.

                                                2. We lack rules on how those forms would be able to traverse between Realms and Umbrae, except for Stepping Sideways, so even without being called baseline, in practice it is.

                                                Also we have to think about Sphere requirement, and I feel that if the other two get to travel everywhere in some way, they'd have to also include Spirit.
