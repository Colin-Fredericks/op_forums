    Originally posted by voidshaper View Post

    You are missing the point, imo. Acting righteous is devoid of considerations of morality or justice for the actor.

    If God tells you to go from door to door and murder every firstborn, you can do so without moral consideration because God is the highest moral authority, so acting amoral on his word is impossible by definition - even if your personal moral compass tells you otherwise.

    That's exactly why worldly arms of religions and secular governments throw out their deus vults and mashallahs to whip people into slaughter.
    IRL, it's also not something that Christianity or Judaism accept: “if you're sure God is telling you to do it, then it is by definition moral” is, in fact, an Islamic belief, whereas Christians and Jews add a layer to that of “God has told us what he considers to be good and evil; and if you think that God is telling you to do something that he has said is evil, don't listen; you're being deceived.”

    But again, that's IRL. The World of Darkness deviates from that. In MtA, moral uncertainty is cranked up to 11 such that anyone who claims to know for certain is probably wrong — about the certainly, at least. It's not that there isn't an absolute truth, such as am absolute morality; only that nobody can be sure that what they think is true actually is.

    And to me, that uncertainty is part of the horror of MtA.

    This isn't a DtF forum; but since it's was brought up, the DtF position on this is that God is literally dead: not in the philosophical sense that people have abandoned belief in God, but in the sense that shortly after God created the universe, God ceased to exist. The angels have been acting in their own ever since, attempting to implement what they believe would have been God's will if He were still around.

    VtM takes yet another route, where God exists and is a jerk; though that is compatible with the notion that what the vampire think of as God is in fact what Gnosticism identifies as the Demiurge.

    I don't go as far as Magus Maximus does in claiming that the World of Darkness is distilled Gnosticism; but there are certainly elements throughout the setting that are compatible with Gnostic beliefs.


    With all that said: if you want to portray an actual evil angel, there's support for that in the World of Darkness. I'm not inclined to, as it's becoming a tired cliche; But I'm not running your game.
