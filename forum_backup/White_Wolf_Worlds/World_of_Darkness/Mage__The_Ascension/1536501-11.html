                Matter 5 is to change properties of the Matter. Anything you can think of. You can for instance make Matter that don't interact with a certain type of material.                             No, you can definitely create the "True Essence Substance". In classical (real life) Alchemy, there is the Alkhalest (something like that), which is the "true acid" or the "essential acid". "Orichalcum" is the "true gold" or the "essential gold". There's also one for true Silver, which name I have forgotten, but it exists. All of those are mythical theoretical substances from the medieval times of European Alchemy - much like the idea of the "Philosopher's Stone" - which very few people know, would be a "rock" or non-biological matter that is alive. I claim that our smartphones are all philosopher stones, but I digress.

                            Anyway, with Matter 5 you can, indeed, create any and all of these mythical substances. "True Poison" or "The Essence of Poison" simply kills; if you so much as smell it, you're instantly dead. "The Essence of Acid", or Alkhalest if you want to sound Nerdy and New Age at the same time, corrodes. Without ever stopping. And if you decide to create Adamantium, it is absolutely unbreakable.

                            What "Orichalcum" (True Gold) does is up to you to decide. In fact, these mythic substances could be a part of your gameplay, then all you need to do is to determine their properties. And their place on your games.

                            A "Essence of Termic Resistance" for example could be a material that is simply 100% immune to any and all sorts of Termic Energy. This "Ceramichalcum" (or "True Ceramic" - which I just invented right now) would allow you to walk over the surface of the Sun. Maybe an "Ironchaulcum" ("True Iron", but don't use that name, I'm pretty sure there is one historical name for "the essence of iron" from the alchemists of old, I would just need to research) could be a material that is absolutely immune to ANY kinds of energy - that would be basically like Adamantium + Vibranium. A metal that is completely indestructible and that also protects against ANYTHING. You could walk inside a Black Hole wearing an hermetic suit made of that thing, since it would even protect you from the gravity itself.

                            And yes, with Matter 5, all of those go. Any sort of "Unobtainium" is possible with it.

                                    Oh yes, the medieval Alchemists all had that idea of the "true essence of things". That's because Alchemy is basically a Gnostic religion; in a very shallow explanation, they believe in Plato's allegory of the cave. Thus, this world would be a fallen, lesser world, and there's a "true world" outside of the reach of our senses; the entire effort of alchemists was to reach this "real world", to shed the veil of falsehood of our fake fallen world and reach the world of the true divine. No wonder Alchemy was persecuted by the Church. It was 100% Gnostic heresy.

                                    But for them, there's this "true essence" of everything. And that was the gist of their work, to try to "distill" stuff into its "true essence".

                                    Water would be an interesting thing for one to reach its "true essence". What would "true water" have as its properties? I'll leave that idea to you, but whatever it might be, Matter 5 can do. Maybe its the perfect purifier, removing all impurities that it touches? Maybe something weirder and more arcane? I don't know, I'll let you yourself create. But that is a good starting point to think about strange new materials based on their "true essence", so use it so that you can play with Matter 5

Thus, the Solificati are much more than just the "Alchemy guys". Alchemy was NEVER a materialistic discipline, it follows much more the ideas of Immanuel Kant about "Immaterialism" or the idea that the entire existence is a figment of imagination - which is 101% Gnostic fantasy. In that sense, Alchemy is the perfectly exact opposite of chemistry. Alchemy is pure fantasy, pure subjective reality, while chemistry is purely materialistic.                                             These special substances, particularly of the Alchemy-Fantasy type, could be incredible additions to any Mage game.
