          For all that the Technocracy tries to appear like an omnipresent force, it is a shadowy conspiracy which relies on influence and association far more than authority. Indeed, what authority it does impose on its members must be reserved for the things it thinks are most important (e.g. the Timetable). What that means is that most of the bigotry (and there are quite a few examples of bigotry in the Technocracy) are systemic, not foundational. They don't come from the top, they come from bigoted members and the top allows it to happen.

          In general, though, the Technocracy is going to be looser and more cavalier about sex and preference and identity than the Traditions. The Traditions (many of them, anyways) see sex as spiritually or metaphysically powerful and the Technocracy does not. The Traditions are much more likely to see meaning in sex and identity while the Technocracy is much more likely to see utility.

                      I see the Technocracy is largely in line with more modern viewpoints and/or those of the establishment. This means that the Technocracy would largely be (and ideologically support) permissive views of sexuality, tolerance of LGBT, birth control, alternative relationships and families, and the like. I think that Iteration X may be a bit of an exception in that they would see more open sexuality as a personal failing, though not really in any sort of traditional way.

                      As for using sex as a weapon, I imagine that both the Syndicate and New World Order as making extensive use of sex. Pornography is a big business and I'm sure that the Syndicate has extensive influence in the sex industry, including control over content. The New World Order likely monitors the sex lives of allies and enemies alike and is able to use information they gain to put pressure on people. Much like how someone like Jeffrey Epstein (and whoever is affiliated with him) would be able to exert massive pressure over powerful people who engaged in elicit activity on his island. I imagine that the Technocracy has a number of similar individuals working for them who provide sexual services to powerful people in exchange for power and control. Depending on how dark we want our setting, we could get super dark with Syndicate controlled sex trafficking, organized crime, pedophile politicians, and related topics.

                                  I would suspect that the Progenitors would be one of the most "progressive" groups, in terms of sex/gender. In reshaping bodies, ideas like sex and gender become equally fluid. They also have ways (via cloning, IVF and other methods) that sex is no longer necessary under their paradigm for the continuation of the species. The sex drive is a natural part of all species, so I expect that most Progenitors would recognize and accept that. For them, sex as pleasure ends up being the focus rather than sex for procreation. This could end up getting pretty dark with some Progenitors creating groups of functional sex-slaves and whatnot.

                                  Iteration X is on the opposite extreme. I would expect that for younger members, things like cybernetic dildos, sex-bots and the like would be common jokes or things they work towards. As they become more machine than human, the sex drive begins to vanish with their flesh. For older members, sex simply becomes a necessary means to continue the species and sex without the goal of procreation becomes pointless. Surprisingly while you'd expect Iteration X to push for things like sex-robots and whatnot, the oldest (most machinelike) members would feel that sex beyond the singular goal of procreation is completely pointless.

                                  The Syndicate would view sex = money and that would probably be the extent of an overall design philosophy. As long as sex is providing money, either directly or indirectly (from new people being born) then it's all okay. Obviously the Syndicate would be big into prostitution, though I expect the Union would be split between the desire to legalize it and the desire to keep it illegal. Individual members might bring their own ideas on sex, and what forms of sex are acceptable or unacceptable, but overall I don't think you'd see anything resembling a consensus in the Syndicate beyond the idea that sex should be treated like any other commodity.

                                  New World Order would probably focus more on traditional CIS heterosexual marriage. Their primary focus would be more on the demographic aspect and they would be pushing for American-style nuclear families (as those are easier to maintain and deal with - parents are kept busy working, children spend time watching TV or playing video games or watching social media so it makes it easy to indoctrinate them, etc). Families that are deemed "deviant" (whether homosexual or large extended families) would be pushed against. A lot of their focus would be in propaganda (idealizing the traditional nuclear family) or in city planning (more suburbs forcing people to have cars, etc).

                                  I suspect the Void Engineers wouldn't care much at all. As long as it doesn't interfere with the mission then they'd have a fairly laissez faire attitude towards sex. I expect that there would be some members with the "find alien races and fuck them" mentality, but it probably would be seen as fairly deviant. I'd expect most recruits would be shown some videos of what could happen if they decided to put their penis in the alien monster. Some would inevitably ignore the advice and they might end up in a warning video for the next batch of recuits.

                                  Overall that would be the Union's personal opinions on sex within each Union. New World Order would generally control the Technocracy's position on sex outside of the Union, or at least it's the only one that has any real reason to care about what Sleepers do in regards to sex, and that would skew towards modern CIS focused traditional marriage with a focus on ensuring procreation and demographics.

                                              It is worth pointing out that the idea is much older. In the Republic, Plato advances the idea that pursuing an a just society requires separating children from their parents and raising them in a correct way. It's a common theme throughout history, if you can't convince adults that your ideal society is good, bypass them and raise the next generation as believers. It should be obvious I don't think much of the idea (at least when it involves separating children from their parents) and view it as a warning sign of tyranny.

                                              More generally, and more applicably to this thread, when a faction sees a disparity between humanity as it is and humanity as it should be they often start trying to correct things by attacking the bonds between people. The generous interpretation of this is that they are trying to stop social ties from reinforcing the societies mistakes, the ungenerous interpretation is that isolated people are easier to indoctrinate (see cults). This is a subject where things can go very wrong very quickly in more than one way. "The Technocracy is full of repressive prudes who are trying to force a restrictive, cis-normative, and false nuclear family to box people in" and "The Technocracy is full of puppet masters trying to degrade human sexuality to better manipulate damaged people" are both potentially alienating depending on the life experiences of the people playing the game.
