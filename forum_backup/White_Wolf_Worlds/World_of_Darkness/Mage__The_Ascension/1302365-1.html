            Originally posted by Lian View Post

            ...this is true of all tools. Tools make things easier. Would your character learn to use a hammer or "PUNCH NAILS" because its more efficient use of his time?
            I think it's worth remembering that physical objects help avert Vulgarity and avoid paradox. It's weird if someone pulls out a katana in public and slices someone's arm off (horrifying even), but it's less weird than someone chopping off someone's arm with a karate chop. Weapons just make good excuses for swift death.

            Naturally the devoted martial artist will know at least a little bit of everything, to get a well-rounded approach to their preferred method. The expanded rules in M20 include this idea that you can roll Perception + Ability to tell what your opponent might do next, to get a bonus to your response (for example, the Soft Fist move where you redirect their attack back at them). Well, it's hard to predict what someone will do with a knife without Melee; Perception + Melee one turn, Dexterity + Do the next (with a difficulty reduction) and now their stab towards you ends up buried in their gut. Can't say it was entirely your fault either; they tried to stab you!

                        To begin with Melee is very useful in Umbra, where many tech just doesn't work. melee is also great vs undead, demons and such who can close into melee Really quickly. I personally may take Melee, if playing combat mage, but generally prefer to take Dream for duels and planned raids.
                        But, one shouldn't underestimate melee oriented combat mage. If you stack points cleverly, you can get a fighting monster in very short order that could go toe-to-toe with nearly anything. So if you want a frontline fighter don't forget to take Niphilim and Genemods (mostly dexterity), as well as Life 3 and Spirit 3 with spirit 4 specialized foci for making Fetishes. And don't forget at least Blatancy 3.
                        In very short order you mage can pump out awakened weapons and armor that soaks/deal aggreviated and is better then mundane, alongside an array of battle drugs, talens (for extra actions and buffs) and a pack of possessed dogs for support. Most effects with appropriate props would be very coincidental, but still enough to turn whole groups into kabab with preparation.
                        I would also recommend to create Mask of The Wyrm, that has -4 dice terror on any melee enemy that sees it, and a few awakened smoke granades ($20 sold freely). You can also place a pact with some war/predatory spirit for max hax. and that with starting character no more then a week in game time.

                            They cannot *soak* the damage, but the text says that they can parry Lethal brawl/melee attacks, which is the same as a dodge roll essentially, just using Dexterity + Brawl/MA/Do instead of Athletics.

                            The text specifically says Lethal however, so if the weapon does Aggravated damage then I assume the block won't work at all, unless they've taken steps with Life or Prime to allow their body to handle the strain of deflecting such blows with the maneuver.

                            Furthermore, the block/parry maneuver is a standard one (although it's limited to blocking bashing damage normally), so the Well Trained Advantage should kick in and lower the difficulty by 1, which is helpful.

                                    And to top it all off, guns need ammunition, and make a lot of noise. They're also incredibly expensive and heavily regulated.

                                    A knife is reliable, at the end of the day.

                                    Melee weapons have advantages over Brawl (parrying, Lethal damage without magick, easier/safer to enchant a knife permanently compared with your own pattern, weapon length advantage, relatively coincidental source of killing vs a punch), and advantages over Firearms (issues with undead, regulation, cost, maintenance, ammo), but they do also have their own problems.

                                    In an ideal world, a character would master a mixture of close range combat (Do and MA), long range combat (crossbows are ideal, and fit into traditional paradigms, plus they're silent and lethal, with their ammo being replaced more easily than bullets with Crafts or Matter), and weapon use (weapons that fit into Do practice).

                                    With the crossbow (Archery skill) and various MA weapons (Melee), they'll get -1 difficulty if you increase your Do high enough, and you can learn a Do technique that gives them an extra die of damage, which is neat.
