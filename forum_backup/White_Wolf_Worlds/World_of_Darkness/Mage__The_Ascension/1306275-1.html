            Personally I’ve always thought Perdo Magica to be strong as hell in what it does, but like said it is kinda one trick pony and those exp could have been put to more generally useful powers.

            Still despite it’s incredible strength Perdo Magica can be over powered by extended effects and rituals of Mages. And like said you need yo be able to detect the attack which is generally possible through Auspex 2, Thaumaturgy 1 combined power (if I remember the requisities correctly ) That detects magic. Otherwise you have no sure way to know if magic is really happening or their just chanting to distract you.

            Well you might know something is up through Awareness roll but it gives you no specifics of what is happening just that something is.

            All in all it is powerful in it’s limited scope, it does not necessarily be able to help you every time you are targeted (like as an extreme example during the day through a correspondence spell, or as less extreme example just hit by a spell you did not notice) but whenit does it tends to work great. It is also points away from something else so in the end it kinda does balance out in the end.

            Though still I must admit that when I first read it in House of Tremere I too thought it way too overpowered but over the years I have started to think that perhaps, due to reasons above, it is not that overpowered afterall.
