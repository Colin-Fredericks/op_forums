            I also vaguely recall there being a Rote in one of the Dead Magic books, that allowed a mage to enter the Shadowlands with just Spirit 3. It was described as being something possible because of the original culture's different outlook on the underworld, which may or may not work for other mages. It may also be that the ancient culture existed in a time where the spiritual make-up of the world was sufficiently different that one COULD just cross over to the land of the dead as one could cross over to any other part of the Umbra.

            Personally, I would imagine it's possible for someone to use something other than the Sojourn to go to the Shadowlands. It's just difficult, and methods for doing so would require jumping through a few extra hoops. It's worth keeping in mind that while we like to think of the Umbra as one huge place with consistent rules, it's actually made up of different broad categories with their own quirks and requirements. You can't really get very far in the High Umbra, for instance, without using Mind instead of Spirit. So the Low Umbra have similarly specific prerequisites seems reasonable.
