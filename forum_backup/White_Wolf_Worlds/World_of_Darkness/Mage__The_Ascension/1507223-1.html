                Focusing just on the Traditions is also forgetting that there are other playable factions.                                                     (I would, personally, in the context of a vampire game, literally give the Nephandi an anti-conscience virtue if that makes representing them easier.)

                                                    So, making a Nephandus "grow" as a person, ironically produces a monster, whereas making him more venal and "lazy" i.e making him fall from his path, may give you something remotely normal. But that person can still find it deep within him to surprise you with some great monstrous act, just as the most venal among as can sometimes surprise us with some great virtuous one.

                                                    In short, they can conceptualize it, in my opinion, but the results are problematic to say the least.
                                                    Add to this the ingrained prejudiced against inverted individuals and, well... there is probably not much good will on either side
