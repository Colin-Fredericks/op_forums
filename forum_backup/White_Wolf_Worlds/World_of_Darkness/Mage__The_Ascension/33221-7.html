        And, yes, real world business in the sense of capitalism in the sense of competition over limited resources to produce gain ('supply' and 'demand') does inherently have issues in that the bottom will always fall out of it when any participant stops behaving ethically. Starting with the Tragedy of the Commons and moving onwards. To most of the people here it seems to be seen as an inherent bug in the system that they want to wish away. To a lot of very rich people and many economists, it's a design feature, not a flaw.
        I feel like the qualifier "the bottom will always fall out of it when any participant stops behaving ethically" can be applied to every human system everywhere. Governments, social clubs, courts, sporting events...
