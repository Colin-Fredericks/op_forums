I am thinking spirit 5, Life 5, Prime 5 as the prereques on spheres but am open to other options. Anyone have any thoughts going forward? I imagine at least one "the wyrm tricks you into making a new tool" will come up and its a cool angle for a plot but other options might be interesting, available patrons, why they'd want things etc

                  There's only one group that could create a Mockery Breed race, the Technocracy. They have the infrastructure to do so. Others can create such creatures but they'd be hard pressed to create them in real numbers. And the Progenitors have created artificial life already, from clones to the genegineered creatures.

                  They could create a Mockery Breed of their own, they have the means and the know-how. The problem is that Dimensional Science (Spirit) is largely the domain of the Void Engineers. Yes there are those outside the Convention but there are few DimSci masters outside of the VE. So they would probably lacking when in comes to the Spirit side of things. However that can be made up with various cybernetics and biomods that can simulate all kinds of powers.

                  And of course its unlikely the Garou would see it as Wyrm-tainted, something made by the Technocracy would more likely be seen as Weaver-tainted.
