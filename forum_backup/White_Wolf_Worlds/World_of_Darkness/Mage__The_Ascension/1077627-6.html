            To Warpwind,

            Maybe it's not clear. but we haven't run the 1st part of the Massasa war yet. So far, we've only built roughly what the Hermetics are planning to do.

            I'm still making the Vampire side of things and do need help.

            Currently, Coeris is not meant to be the site of any sort of Tremere last stand. The idea was that they'd removed any important documents from the place some time before the start of the war and have transformed it into some sort of death trap in an attempt to cause casualties among the mages. To properly pull this off, they have to offer some resistance to the initial mage attack, then fall to the Hermetic attacking force, all the while fooling the Hermetic spies who will be scouting about in the Astral plane. Their main advantage is that they know a lot of the tricks that Hermetics can do, just not exactly what the Hermetics will do.

            The lack of the Countermagic discipline in V20 Dark Ages is confusing and I'd like to hear more opinions on that.

            Auspex 5 will likely be used for spying on the invading force
            Dominate 5 may become useful for possessing the enemy if the opportunity presents itself.

            Other than that, Coeris will be defended by a number of Elementals Summoned using Potestas Elementorum
            There will be gargoyles hiding in the castle, ready to pounce upon unsuspecting invaders
            They will probably call for fog and a storm to mess with the attacking forces using Potestas Tempestatum

            Defense of the Sacred haven will be used on Coeris so that Sunlight will be rendered harmless
            Wake with The Evening's Freshness will be used to prevent the usual dice penalty problems of being attacked in the day
            Wards will likely be set against those with Intelligence less than 5 and Strength less than 5 or something like that.
            Scry the Hearthstone will probably be important with regards to noticing invisible things in Coeris
            Dedicate the haven makes any haven rituals permanent

            I am yet unsure how mortals will play a part in defending Coeris. I'm guessing, the mortal support staff will be Dominated so that every man, woman, and child serving the castle will be a mindless or zealot soldier willing to die for Coeris.

            At least one of the Hermetic Strongholds will be attacked by a large Tremere Force, likely one employing Tremere Vampires specialized in Attack magic, Gargoyles, and Mercenaries or Dominated Conscripts.

            I'm also trying to figure out how best to have a small number of vampiric agents hide amidst the breweries and inns of towns supporting the Hermetic strongholds so they can slowly blood bond the populace and any mages who procure drinksfrom these places. So far, it seems simple in theory with dominate and a few rituals but I have to work this out

            Also, I haven't worked out any of the numbers in any parts of the Tremere end.

            This is what I have so far. Suggestions are welcome.

                                    Originally posted by Spacecat
                                    Actually, what's your opinion on the interaction of the Tremere Ward ritual and astral travelers. Does it keep them out?
                                    I think it does, but I won't assume my rulings will be what everyone else thinks should be so.
                                    I guess this makes sense, a Ward against Spirits has to work on the other side of the Gauntlet, so it stands to reason that the rest also work that way.

                                    I was thinking more of an initially strong resistance to attack that gets weaker and eventually breaks into a retreat. Do you think the Tremere would try this?
                                    I think this makes sense, but in the Astral Plane it would be harder to do...most vampires can't become astral (neither can most mages, but still the numerical advantage of vampires get's reduced. Vampires lose most of their advantages here (no physical attributes, no gargoyles or neonates, no ghouls, you can't carry most of the stuff you need to cast rituals-mages only lose the potential to empower normies and can't bring their disciples).

                                    As a way to (temporarily) create confusion to the astral travelers, you could leave most of the vampires defending the Chantry believing that they, indeed, have to defend the Chantry to their deaths. Tell them a plan to defend the Chantry that isn't the actual plan. Keep them in the outher perimeter, and Ward inner regions of the Chantries. That way, mages would discover the "true plans to protect the chantries" from the minds of genuine Tremere vampires, and would think that the Warded areas are important, but won't know why (they are important because here's where most of the traps will be laid, and where the few Elders that know the plan are hidding to enact any ritual that needs to be enacted, pull any lever than needs to be pulled, and then try to flee). Warding the entire chantry isn't a bad idea either, not because it will work, but because it would be a credible effort, and the least Hermetics know the better.

                                    How it can be used to counter Thaumaturgy and was in the other vampire books and suddenly it's gone in V20 Dark ages.
                                    So, do you think they should have it from the get go?
                                    While it may not be in V20DA, I think it was present in DA,(in the book of the Low Clans?). I think they should have it.]

                                    As for suggestions:

                                    Hermetics have the best Pillar to detect and counter any form of supernatural power. Don't neglect sensory magic and unweaving. Attacking a mage stronghold should prioritize these two from the get go (and, quite frankly, it would be a shame to have all those Sun Elementals unable to work just because the enemy casted magical darkness ).
                                    Knowing this, Tremere could built mundane, mechanical, traps to argument the defense. While these could potentially be detected by any human, they won't be shining like fireworks (well, mages could try something with Vires to sense mundane traps as weirdness in the forces present in the architecture, but most Hermetics lack good masonry skills to understand the data...hears Craftmason laughing far away).

                                    Also, while Hermetics don't want to damage the chantry, start the main wave of the attack with a bang could be appropiate. Blowing up the outer walls shouldn't damage important books and artifacts, and would create big holes from where the beasts and elementals can enter easily (and to retreat, if it were necessary). It's also a way to see how well warded it's the fortress, to dismantle mundane weapons laying near the walls, to destroy guard rooms that are usually near to the outer perimeter, and to provoke a reaction. You can always rebuild them again if you want to stay (with Vires and earth elementals, it shouldn't take too long)

                                    I'm also trying to figure out how best to have a small number of vampiric agents hide amidst the breweries and inns of towns supporting the Hermetic strongholds so they can slowly blood bond the populace and any mages who procure drinksfrom these places. So far, it seems simple in theory with dominate and a few rituals but I have to work this out
                                    In order to figure this you need to think on how it's the relationship of mages and these places, and how this changes with war. Hermetic Chantries are supposed to be in isolated places, so the mages probably don't go a lot to the town (young mages do, of course, to have fun), since chantries are supposed to harbor a small population of mortals alongside mages, it's likely that they send people to get drinks and food to supply (a big chantry may be like a small town unto itself).
                                    If they're waiting an attack from vampires, it's likely they will sleep at day, and go out at night. Going to the town it's to be discouraged because it would risk being attacked by surprise (nor that mages will always comply) .

                                    It's also likely mages will be searching for magic in the surrounding areas, because being in war with a House it's to expect a magical attack. This has some implications. Like sneaking drinks with vitae to the Chantry might not be possible (evil death magick it's not something you want to find in your drink, and guards are going to be searching for magic).
                                    The Chantries may have animals, people, and perhaps even a spirit or two, looking for big movements of "people" in the night. And for magic. So laying low may be the best strategy.

                                    A lot of Rituals protecting areas may be detected, so it's a risk to employ them in big numbers before you're ready to make the attack.

                                    It's...debatable...if Blood Bonding an entire village should attract attention of someone searching for Tremere movements in the surrounding area. It's magic so it can be detected, but it may be subtle enough to not be (you don't need to have Vitae in your system to remain bonded, but it's still a form of strong mind-control of mystical nature).
                                    Personaly, I think that it should be possible to sense these kind of stuff , but Hermetics (not knowing the blood bond and not caring for mortals) aren't going to carefully search the villages, focusing instead on keeping tabs on the roads, and on big acts of magic. Eventually, the ressonance of an entire bonded population may be too much to not be detected casually, but when the village reaches that point it would be already too late (and, I guess, the Tremere would launch their attack)
