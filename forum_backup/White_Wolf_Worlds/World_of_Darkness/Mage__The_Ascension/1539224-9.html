        And there's also a few other "heresies", here in Brazil for instance we have "Spiritism", which believes in Reincarnation and past lives and some such, clearly some eastern Syncretisms, but if you ask, they'll declare themselves as "Christians", and they believe in Jesus and whatnot, when in practice their belief system reassembles more Gnosticism or something like it (oh yes, Spiritism could be a good background for Hermetics here in Brazil).

        And what's more interesting, particularly for those in Candomblé, Umbanda and Spiritism, is that those are somewhat... Interchangeable.         Or the typical northern obsession about the Amazonic forest. I mean, here for us it is just a green hellhole, but Americans and Europeans think that it is some sort of mysterious Shangri-La Wonderland. Which is why there are special kinds of unicorns in WoD, mostly in WtA, just around it
