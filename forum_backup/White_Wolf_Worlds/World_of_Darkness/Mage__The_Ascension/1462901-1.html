    To the last point first: there's actually three different rules for archmages. (1) There's just a 6th level and you enter ST fiat levels of power (2) there's a whole 6-9 level to each Sphere like Elder Disciplines and (3) there isn't even a 6th dot, archmages are just a combination of high levels of Spheres, experience, and magical resources, to the point of being ST fiat level powerful if prepped.

    This all due to the fact that #3 was going to be the original intent, but someone gave Porthos Forces 6. Went detailing what that meant, #1 became the default. Later, to make archmages more playable, #2 was introduced in Masters of the Art following the model of Elder Disciplines. Revised kinda kept clear of taking a position using the Avatar Storm to make the archmages a non-issue, though the Revised stat block on Voormas has him with Entropy 7 implying that MotA is still official. M20 has stated that #3 is the basic stance of the game now, and isn't going to support 1 or 2.

    Also, in terms of adding up to Porthos, his Spheres are: Correspondence 4, Entropy 5, Forces 6, Life 4, Mind 5, Matter 3, Prime 5, Spirit 4, Time 3. Plus Avatar 5, and his real big thing: Arete 9. That's not actually that much more powerful than the suggested stats in the OP:

    "Maybe assume 1 6 dot sphere, 2-3 5 dot spheres, 1-2 4 dot spheres. And Arete, Avatar 7+."

    He has one 6, three 5s, three 4s instead of two, and two 3s. His Arete is obviously way above 7, but for instant casting it's just two more dice. It's rituals where he's going to over power them individual with multiple rules and more rolls possible in a ritual. Avatar 5 is actually below, but also not that relevant in at the 5+ level to a mage with Prime.

    So, sure, they PCs are right on Porthos's level but they're pretty close, and as I noted before, them being a cabal is a huge advantage if they can leverage it properly.
