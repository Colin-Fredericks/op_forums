                    Originally posted by Ambrosia View Post

                    Depends on wether you use M20 or Dragon's Fire rules;

                    In M20, Arete score equals Background Dots of the Wonder, and Background Dots equal the highest sphere used for the Talisman effect.
                    Optionally a higher Arete score can be infused by using a higher Background rating for the Wonder, despite the lower effect, with all requirements adjusted for the higher rating.


                    Also, you bring up my main problem with Dragon's Fire:
                    You only need 'one success' to fully trigger a Talisman's effect in Dragon's Fire. But if you roll that one success, what's the actual efficiency and score of the Wonder's effect? Duration? Damage? Targets etc. There is no roll for the Effect at creation time.

                    P.s.
                    And just as a logical houserule, I would say that a Wonder's Arete cannot be higher than the Arete of the Mage that created it. It just makes sense, theme-wise. You cannot put more metaphysical excellence into an item than you, yourself, even comprehend and possess.
                    Eh, not much interested in M20 for a variety of reasons.

                    Those are all determined by the effect. Suppose you want a laser gun. It fires a beam of forces damage. Much like anything else, you hash out what's reasonable with your ST and then go. The Go-to Guide I go with is You accomplish the effect. And distribute a number of successes worth of stuff equal Arete + Willpower For Talismans, just their Arete + 1. for things like Damage and Duration and Targets.

                                                Originally posted by Lord Revan View Post
                                                Do trinkets need Prime to be created, or just any Sphere? In M20 it seems any Sphere can create trinkets, how does this works?
                                                Trinkets in M20 are simply items that have been enhanced with an effect. They are not magickal in themselves, and don't really have special rules to create. You're handling everything with normal sphere magick rules.
                                                Put semi-permanent or permanently running effect on object to make it more special/useful = Trinket.
