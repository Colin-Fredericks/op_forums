                                            Here's how we've been handling it.

                                            ​Spirit 2 allows to you summon spirits and bargain with them. If the spirit is willing and has the Possession charm, it can enter an item and effectively become a fetish. The fetish doesn't have an owner and isn't specifically tied to the mage, but otherwise activates in the usual way. Of course, spirits with the Possession charm tend to be unpleasant, so be careful with your bargain.

                                            ​Spirit 4 is for binding an unwilling (or slumbering) spirit into a fetish. It forces them into the item and turns it into a fetish belonging to the caster. The spirit is likely to be unhappy unless the mage mollifies it somehow.

                                            ​Prime 4 is for a willing spirit. The Effect binds it to the object and turns it into a fetish belonging to the caster. So long as the caster keeps to their bargain with the spirit, all is fine.

                                            You can of course also use Spirit 4 to bind a willing spirit. Prime 4 is an overlap and workaround for mages who (for example) have Spirit 2 and can summon and bargain with a spirit, but lack Spirit 4.
