    The Hit Mark V *actually* was a cyborg model for a while - in some way.
    Mark V housed a human brain inside a terminator body, with living skin around it. It was a joint op between ItX and the Progenitors. So, technically a GitS-style full-body cyborg.

    The mentioning of human brains in them faded out at some point - so wether that's still the case or not is up for grabs. But they get thoroughly explained in Guide To The Technocracy - including some ItXers voluntarily becoming HIT Mark V cyborgs as a life saving measure once they get too old. Probably without their intelligence getting...dulled.

                            Originally posted by Astromancer View Post
                            Thank you Ambrosia.

                            I have suggested that in the 1930s that Hit-Marks might be modified humans rather than disguised machines. Of course from, E.T.A. Hoffman on, androids would be in the Zeitgeist. I just think a cyborg would be the deadlier for in period.
                            My point is that HIT Marks are already defined in the setting, including early models. Cyborgs would probably exist and be more implemented than Marks by far, indeed HIT Marks wouldn't have the same role and widespread use in the Union until the Mark V. So all those ideas work fairly well as the go-to of the time, maybe even all of them as things were still less uniform (they still had the constraints of the time).

                            Just those things wouldn't be called HIT Marks then or earlier. Pre-V HIT Marks are robots, they are defined as such. Even post-V HIT Marks may have some biological tissue or a human brain, but their bodies are almost entirely artificial. Not because the Union doesn't use other things, just because they don't call other things HIT Marks. And when in the past they even started to call anything a HIT Mark is up to debate still.

                            This is the kind of detail that helps to establish the setting in such a game. Using modern terms to refer to things because they fill similar roles is simple, but degrade credibility under scrutiny. Stating that this is a time before HIT Marks being called HIT Marks and with other projects filling its role, with what will later be called HIT Marks having other connotations, help to establish your feel for the era and create a sense of history: this is not just the same thing but steam-punk-turning-diesel-punk. This is the foundation that'll be transformed by the times into our future selves.

                                                    Sources will say that, in the 1930's the Technocracy is just about recovered from the exodus of the Electrodyne Engineers. However, the fact is that the exodus caused damage that the Technocratic Union would never really recover from. Of particular importance to this discussion is the fact that the Electrodyne Engineers had helped create some early models of HIT marks, (I also don't recall if they were actually called HIT marks though) necessitating a near complete redesign when the Sons of Ether began using flaws in the designs against Iteration X. This wasn't the only problem though; the fact is that the Sons of Ether had been integral to almost every aspect of the Technocracy.

                                                    Notably, another technology that the Eloctrodyne Engineers had created for the Technocratic Union was the predecessor to "control". This meant that the Sons of Ether were able to tap into Technocratic communications for quite some time before the newly created "Operatives" were able to shut them out. During this time however, the Ivory Tower's and Operatives' lockdown on information is what caused the Virtual Adepts to begin to rebel. By the time the Etherites lost their tap, the Virtual Adepts had already begun talking to the Sons of Ether and feeding them information.

                                                    During the War, The Ivory Tower and the Operatives would eventually merge and become the N.W.O. The Virtual Adepts feuded with Iteration X about the possibility of Virtual Reality during the war, but they didn't leave the Technocracy until the end of the war. So the Technocratic Union at the start of the war was the Syndicate, Iteration X, the Void Engineers, The Ivory Tower, the Operatives, the Progenitors, and the Virtual Adepts. Some time during the war the Operatives and Ivory Tower turned into the N.W.O. I believe many records don't really consider the Operatives as a full convention, because they were spawned by, then reabsorbed by The Ivory Tower.
