      Name: Moss "Ratchet" Charles
      Nature: Martyr
      Demeanor: Survivor
      Eseence: Primordial
      Tradition: Orphan

      Physical: Strength 2, Dexterity 4, Stamina 3
      Social: Charisma 2, Manipulation 2, Appearance 3 Mental: Perception 4, Intelligence 4, Wits 3

      Talents: Dodge 3, Awareness 3, Intimidation 2, Brawl 2, Athletics 1
      Skills: Survival 3, Melee 2, Firearms 2, Stealth 2, Drive 2, Mediation 1, Do 1
      Knowledges: Medicine 4, Occult 3, Computer 3, Lore (roughly All) 1, Linguistics 1, Science 1

      Backgrounds: Avatar 3, Allies(Hunters) 3, Destiny 2, Arcane 2, Contacts 1

      Spheres: Prime 6, Life 4, Mind 3, Entropy 2, Spirit 1, Time 1
      Willpower: 8
      Arete: 6
      Quintessence: 12
      Paradox: 0

      Background:

      Moss was born the daughter of a perfect family in 1964, but everything changed when she was fifteen years old: A vampire broke into her house and killed her parents, Moss in tears hid in the wardrobe and had nothing to do--But she awakened.

      Moss, who was filled with remorse and angry, swore that she would revenge and killed this bloodsucker. She erased all her tracks and became an Orphan and a demon hunter.

      But learning more powerful magic isn't easy, she found, collected any resource as possible as she could. She used to meet many Supernaturals, and had a glimpse on the Word of Darkness. She also robbed some Tradition and Tech Union Chantries to fuel her own libraries.

      Now she thinks she already has a real understand of avoiding "Bound" (Aka. Paradox), and it's very useful to revenge. She recently joined a group of hunters as a doctor--After all, she is rather a human than a magician, and more guys in the same camp means more guarantee.

      Notes:

      1. Seriously, Moss never knew anything like "Umbra" or "Gauntlet", she spent all her life on Earth. But her Spirit 1 made her have a very infinite understand of spiritual things...

      2. Moss doesn't receive very formal Magic training, so she may have some informal understanding of Magic.

      3. Moss's Prime 6 explains why she can survive on Earth. And her Life 4 and Mind 3 could make her a good doctor.

      4. Due to her Life 4 and Prime 6 and Mind 3, she has a 20's appearance.

      5. She is alerted--won't use powerful magic in most public conditions. She knows that this World of Darkness is very dangerous.
