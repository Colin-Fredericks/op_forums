    M20 First character - Critique request

    12-15-2025, 05:55 PM
    Hello everyone. I’m a beginner to Mage and i’m hoping to start looking for a group to play with, but before that, I wanted to make a solid character I can take into games. So below is my first attempt at making a Mage: The Ascension (M20) character. I’d love it if you guys could offer feedback, and point out if i’ve forgotten anything or gotten anything wrong. I’m a bit of an introvert, so I tried making a character who’s extremely extroverted, since it would make for an interesting change of pace and should help me build my roleplaying skills.

    While I know he isn’t 100% rule legal according to the M20 sourcebook, I am hoping to make an argument for thematic integrity to the ST.

    Thanks in advance for your feedback.




    Name: Magnus Reinher AKA “Magnus the Magnificent”

    Player: (That’d be me)
    Chronicle: I hope

    Nature: Sensualist
    Demeanor: Rogue
    Essence: Dynamic

    Affiliation: None
    Sect: None
    Concept: Ecstatic Adversary


    Appearance: A picture is worth a 1000 words, so I had the GPT-Toaster cook me up some pictures:






    Height: 205 cm
    Weight: 140 Kg
    Bodyfat %: Hah, no.


    Personality: Magnus is a man who NEEDS challenge the way regular people need food and drink. He is a man who is always looking for the next opponent, the next obstacle, the next goalpost, and his medium of choice: Physical competition, especially combat. Magnus is not an agonophile, but he CRAVES adversity. Every blow struck against him is a gift, every Defeat an inspiration. Battle is his love, and a worthy opponent is his lover. Magnus is always in motion, always seeking a new obstacle to overcome, or training to overcome the one he has found. His self-improvement is almost pathological, and he seeks to forever expand his limits.

    But magnus does not seek strength for the sake of domination or self-glorification. He wants to enjoy life, and his body is the tool through which he does it. Thus, to seek greater challenges, he must ever make himself greater as a means. Nor does he bully the weak, or wield his strength solely for selfish reasons. Magnus is aggressively friendly, and will gladly employ his might to help his friends or protect the innocent. Magnus does not see enemies, merely adversaries, and he is actively bored by politics and intrigue. While he is not stupid, he is a man of very specific focus, and he will move as his heart dictates, with little consideration of the greater picture.


    Backstory (Simplified):

    Magnus Reinher ihas always been a man with a big heart and a big appetite for life. Already large as a kid, Magnus enjoyed brawling with bullies as much as he did playing sports with his friends. Throughout his childhood and adolescent life, Magnus threw himself into every kind of physical sports and disciplines he could, yet despite excelling in many, he always seemed to soon lose interest. However, in his late teens, Magnus found something that struck a true, instinctual cord with him: Pro wrestling. He obsessed over the WWF and WCW, working ceaselessly in his free time to copy and learn the moves of his favorite stars, determined to one day join the circuit himself.

    In his early twenties, he finally got his chance, and after being scouted to a minor league, Magnus was ecstatic to start his new career and chase his dream. It didn't last long. When Magnus learned that wrestling was essentially scripted, combat stunt-choreography, he was devastated, and quit immediately.

    But Magnus has never been a person that lets setbacks slow him down. If Wrestling wasn't real, he would MAKE it real. Magnus began attending street fights in the rough part of town, looking to get into underground fighting rings and test his might against new opponents, using his own improvised style of combat pro-wrestling under the wrestling name he never got to use: "The Magnificent"

    Magnus’ awakening came when he decided to attempt an extreme weightlifting challenge by bench-pressing the back end of a car. The jack he was using as a safety slipped, and while Magnus should have been crushed, he poured every ounce of his strength and willpower into lifting the car off of himself, triggering his awakening. The surge of life magic that coursed through him as he saw the vision of his avatar allowed him to empower his muscles and save himself, and set him on his path of greater challenges as a mage.




    ATTRIBUTES


    Physical (Primary)

    Strength: 5 (1 point bought for 5 freebies)
    Dexterity: 2
    Stamina: 4
    Specialties: Strength – Overpower, Stamina – Tireless

    Notes: Magnus is meant to be a physical powerhouse. An absolute brick of a man. He is an accomplished athlete, and while he is not clumsy, strength and fortitude are his primary focus.


    Mental (Secondary)

    Perception: 3
    Intelligence: 2
    Wits: 3

    Notes: Magnus is attentive and able to make quick judgements (when his interest is engaged) and while he is by no means a genius, neither is he a fool.


    Social (Tertiary)
    Charisma: 2
    Manipulation: 1
    Appearance: 3

    Notes: Magnus is absolutely guileless. While he is smart enough that he could attempt to manipulate people, he has absolutely zero interest in doing so. While he is not conventionally handsome, his physical presence is imposing, and his natural warmth and enthusiasm lends him a certain charm.


    ABILITIES


    Talents (Primary)

    Brawl: 5
    Athletics: 4
    Expression: 2
    Alertnesss: 1
    Awareness: 1

    Specialties: Brawl – Power move, Athletics – Bodybuilding

    Notes: Magnus is using pro-wrestling as an actual combat style (Think King from Tekken 8) and is a consumate bodybuilder. Expression is meant to signify his showmanship as a wrestler. While Magnus isn’t inattentive, he usually doesn’t care enough to pay that much attention, hence low awareness and alertness. I am aware that these stats are over the 3 point starter limit, but given his specialization and theme, I am hoping ST would approve.


    Skills (Secondary

    Meditation: 3
    Research: 2
    Survival: 2

    Notes: Meditation is meant to be a measure of Magnus’ ability to “get in the zone” more than any cerebral discipline. Magnus is mostly autodidact, which is the purpose of his research skill, and he likes going camping. I know I didn’t use all the points available here, but adding more skill points, or different skills, didn’t feel like they fit his theme.


    Knowledges (Tertiary)
    Medicine: 2
    Esoterica: 2
    Occult: 1

    Medicine is for his knowledge of nutrition and supplements, knowing which stimulants are safe or harmful, and any chemical enhancements (Magnus does NOT use steroids. This is meant for peak HEALTH not short-term gain) Esoterica is for his knowledge of natural suplements, folk medicine, etc. Occult is due to his interest in supernatural training methods and suplements after awakening as a mage.


    SPHERES


    Life (Affinity): 3
    Forces: 2
    Matter: 1

    Note: Magnus’ style of magic is meant to be focused around two core concepts: Long term ritual Life magic to incrementally enhance and rebuild his body beyond human limits, and short term power boosts via Life and forces for combat performance. Essentially he is using life magic for supernatural bodybuilding to turn himself naturally superhuman, while also using it to boost his performance in critical situations when necessary.

    He is meant to be paradox light, disguising most of his magic behind wrestling theatrics, and making life magic effects look like stunt choreography, adrenaline spikes or extremele physical effort for someone who’s already absolutely massive. he prefers not to use magic unless he runs into a threat he can’t overpower naturally, after all there’s no fun in just beating an opponent down in an unfair fight. Magnus understands that paradox is a thing, and will save overtly impossible feats for fighting supernatural threats.


    ADVANTAGES


    Arete: 3 (2 points bought for 8 freebies)
    Willpower: 9 (+4 points bought for 4 freebies)

    Note: Magnus is meant to be a naturally powerful mage in tune with himself, but without any formal training. His style of magic is very instinctual and intuitive.


    Resources: 2
    Fame: 1
    Contacts: 1
    Node: 1
    Avatar: 2

    Note: I gave him resources 2 because Magnus lifestyle requires him to have things like gym equipment, supplements, and food (A LOT of food) and he should be making some money off of his underground fights.
    Fame is for the same reason, he’s basically a known quantity in the underground fight scene. Maybe a few old promo posters from his Pro wrestling debut, that kind of thing.
    Contacts is his fight promoter in the underground, essentially his “agent” who gets him fights.

    His Node is a spot he found in an overgrown corner of an old run down park that he uses as his favorite training spot, because it just “felt right” and he had an easier time getting in the “zone” when he trains there.
    Avatar is to signify that while the Avatar is an important presence in Magnus’ life, it is not the driving force behind his lifestyle.


    MERITS AND FLAWS


    Merit: Code of honor (Cost 1 freebie)
    Flaw: Driven (+3 Freebies)


    Note: These seemed appropriate given his personality, even if they aren’t listed in the M20 sourcebook.


    FOCUS


    Paradigm: The World Exists to be Experienced

    Practice: Form in Motion

    Instruments:

    Doing reps
    Diet and Rest
    Flexing
    “Hype up” slaps
    Proclamation
    Cutting a promo
    Pep talks

    Note: Magnus magic is based around bodybuilding and his pro-wrestling persona. If people are interested I have some Rotes i’ve been working on too that illustrates this.


    Resonance: Dynamic 3
    Resonance flavor: Joyous Challenger (i’m not entirely sure how this works)

    Note: Powerful dynamic resonance seemed fitting given his personality and high starting Arete.


    Avatar description:


    Magnus' avatar is a distant, towering shape that radiates power and vigor. To Magnus, it is a distant goal, the final challenge, the promise of a great, climactic battle of his life. The avatar rarely speaks, Magnus senses it more through abstract attentions. When Magnus grows and triumphs, the Avatar watches him with interest. When he fails and falters, it disregards him. Only a worthy rival has significance, and that is what the Avatar is to Magnus: Not a friend, but the promise of an opponent that rivals and matches him intimately.

          To begin with, I like it. For a first ever it's quite good.

          My observations will have a few goals, then: verify if you understood everything, clarifying rules and setting in case you didn't, help you to round up the character concept, and increase your chances of having this character approved for a game.

          1 - A Mage without affiliation or training is an Orphan. And depending on the chronicle and the ST an Orphan may face a lot of extra problems. You shouldn't make one and not expect to play through the hardships of being marginalized in Mage society. They're also unlikely to know a lot about what they are and how it all works, as they had no formal explaining. Again, it depends on the ST, but you shouldn't expect the Avatar or anyone else to have supplied the character with any information at all about what Magic is, related phenomena and the factions around you.

          So, are you sure this is what you want? By definition this isn't something he'll overcome easily or through Magic or at all. Your concept works just fine within a Tradition, even within the Technocracy.

          2 - For whatever it's worth, fight is fight, bodybuilding is bodybuilding, weight lifting is weight lifting. They require distinct training regimens, diets, skills and muscles. This distinction is both in terms of description and of what traits in the sheet they justify or need. It's also a social distinction, as there's a lot of contempt between those three groups. Bodybuilding also isn't healthy in any way.

          In game terms, you'll need Dexterity to be a good fighter, as it's still what you'll roll for attacks, and even a wrestler can do with slightly less Strength, especially an Awakened wrestler. I agree that Stamina being high is good for a physical character that's a generalist and a healthy person, but I'd move at least 1 dot from Strength to Dexterity if the main idea is for him to be a good fighter. It also begs the question if your idea really require him to start play already at the absolute peak human form, or if he has still some space for growth before inhuman performance starts to kick in.

          Of course, it may just be a case of clarifying the concept or adapting it a little to reflect the actual dots. A character that primarily seeks fights would be on a more classical fighting mindset, but for a Mage it isn't unsuitable the idea of trying to be everything.

          3 - IME there are two kinds of STs when it comes to the 3 dots limit on starting Abilities: those who ignore it altogether and those who never do. The first will probably tell you ahead of time that you can have starting Abilities above 3. The second, though, won't be happy to see you already built your character like that without at least a back-up plan in case of a hard "no", and they'll give you a hard "no", no discussion.

          So, I'd have a back-up plan. Take another Flaw, move some dots, again rethink if you need the character to have extreme levels of skill right out of the bat. 3 dots is plenty for the concept as it is now, anyway, a fighter with 5-6 dice for attacks and 3-4 Strength is plenty to be a locally successful Sleeper wrestler.

          4 - King is not a Tekken 8 character. They're Tekken characters, period (two characters wore the mantle, or the mask, of King). King is in every single entry of the franchise.

          5 - Don't dismiss the value of 1-dot Abilities both as game stats and background elements. The character had a whole life before play begins and not every waking moment was about becoming what they are today. You can use those two dots to put some extra color on him, a bit of trivia, talk about upbringing, anything.

          6 - Occult isn't just interest on the supernatural, it may be even the simple fact that he's aware of it as, being an Orphan, this isn't a given for him.

          7 - You must define what is Magic for him, especially he being an Orphan. Magic is a malleable concept within the game, it means different things for different Mages. Does he even thinks about it as anything but an extension of his training?

          Ascension's Mages aren't like D&D Sorcerers. They didn't discovered they have strange powers (well, unless they did). They noticed the world was more, that they could be more, but what it means is extremely personal. For someone like Magnus the notion that the human body and mind simply is capable of doing exceptional feats if you dedicate and learn how to seems like a fitting Paradigm. In this case, it could well be that he doesn't hold back his Magic because he doesn't even understand it as "magic", but as the simple result of his constant self-improvement.

          Magic is central to a Mage, not just in the sense of being the core powers, but it isn't really magic. It's their worldview, it's drive, it's your destiny, things Magnus already have, but you describe as distinct. Your Avatar in particular doesn't simply exists to help you improve, it exists to help you improve your Magic. It's also how the world works for you, not just in the sense of your opinion, but that Mages literally can't be proven wrong, what they believe they make real in an undeniable, concrete sense, and so it does work like that in an absolute sense. It's the ability to make wondrous things, not because you have "strange powers", but because you have drive and vision and you know how the world really works, which may or may not be some sort of power.

          It isn't even necessarily the same phenomenon despite using the same rules. The Technocracy stance is that what they do is Science and has nothing really to do with what Mages do, the Order of Hermes defends that everyone is fundamentally doing the same thing and this is formally the official stance of the Traditions, many Mages believe what some groups do is the same as them, but others not, and many believe whatever they do is absolutely unique to them. And any of them may be right.

          Someone like Magnus would probably be completely out of this discussion, but would still have an opinion about what he does. But what he does, while Magic for game terms and Hermetic theory, isn't necessarily magic at all.

          That's why we always use Magic with uppercase M, and many with the pretentious K in Magick. Pretentious or not, it's a conceptually extremely important distinction in the setting. There are plenty magic users, by the way, who are Sleepers.

          8 - I'm sure it would be good to see your rotes.

          9 - How Resonance flavor works is an ST decision with some of the most vague and inconsistent rules through the history of the game, don't sweat it.

          But Resonance value, not so much, and 3 is A LOT.

          The normal Resonance level for the most ardent normal humans and sites of legend is, still, 0. 1 is possible, but requires some je ne sais quoi to happen.

          At 3 Magnus is already becoming overtly supernatural just by being there. Not a big thing, but enough for some normal, but perceptive people to notice he is undeniably inhuman. It isn't just a measure of how much he has some personality trait, it's a measure of how much this seeps into reality passively. It lightly alters the environment, it affects the mood of people around him, and it shapes his Effects, not just methods, regardless of his will.

          I do not recommend arbitrarily increasing Resonance without experience with the game and the ST, it isn't a measure of personality or awesomeness.

              I don't have much to add that isn't covered in the two posts above, but I think it's worth stressing a few things:

              First off, how is this character going to work in a game with multiple PCs? This can be a problem for many types of mages, as it's not uncommon for mages to have such a strong drive towards their goals. But what does he do within a group when there's no fighting to do? Being so narrowly focused as a character can make it hard to find a place with other characters to form the basis of a PC group. Whether or not going for an Orphan is what you're after, he's going to struggle to be part of a functional social unit because he's busy chasing the high of his next fight and might not even realize the impact he'd have on people trying to work with him (Empathy/Subterfuge both as 0 means he's not very good at figuring out how people feel, and Streetwise/Etiquette 0 means he's not great at navigating social norms). Pulling back a bit not only would give you more room to grow in his primary focus in-play, but also free up points for him having a bit more capable in other situations.

              Second, a bit beyond considering higher Dexterity and having Life magic to buff Soak and healing, I would not considering this a character that's really ready to survive in the World of Darkness. This applies very much to surviving serious fights (esp. unregulated fights where an opponent that palms a razor could easily mess up another), but also to the broader brutality of the setting. The setting is even more full of liars, cheats, and con artists than our own. It's a world where most people that are ready to use violence to solve their problems aren't going to care about fighting fair or the mutual growth of mutually agreed to fights. The setting is also full of people that will easy tease out how driven he his, and without him knowing it, then point him into dangerous situations while they keep their hands clean. Even taking his promoter as a Contact instead of Allies or a Mentor means his promoter isn't in this for Magnus's well being. From both mechanical and narrative perspectives, this is a character that's going to get chewed up and spat out by the dark nature of the setting.

              Lastly, I would consider that Skills and Knowledges have penalties for being rated at 0. Knowledges in particular have the harshest penalty as you don't get a roll at all if you don't have any dots. Academics or Science 0? Unless your ST considers something such basic knowledge you don't need to roll for it at all, you auto-fail. It's an incredibly risky thing to not even leave your character a chance to make basic checks on things like their legal rights, operating computers, knowing what's in famous works of art, doing math, and so on.
