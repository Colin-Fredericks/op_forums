                        Originally posted by idpersona View Post
                        Thanks. Then yes, the dream attack thing might work on them too. I would bet most or all of their mental Disciplines would be fully functional in a dreamscape though. If a strategy like this was a fool proof plan against them, then they'd have been dream killed out of existence.
                        I disagree. How does one use Disciplines in a dream? Would the vampire even remember they had them? Dreams are slippery places, where one's memory and thoughts are jumbled. Unless the vampire was trained in Lucid Dreaming, they might not even remember they're vampires. Even if they did, how does one make eye contact for Dominate if the dream takes a non-visual form? Or if the Mage appears as an eyeless monster? Or doesn't appear at all, but instead hides, or appears as a cloud of smoke? I would grant you Auspex - assuming the vampire was lucid enough to think to activate it - as well as Dementation maybe, and the Path of Oneiromancy. Anything else, though? The powers may not apply.

                        I certainly would never give the vampire access to physical Disciplines. Those are enhancements of the body, which is stuck asleep in the vampire's haven. Of no use in a dream. At best, the vampire would start sleep fighting, and tear their room apart. Fortitude, perhaps, to soak the Aggravated damage from the Mage's magickal assault. Anything else, however, is unhelpful. When on a mental battlefield, you fight with Wits and Willpower, not Dexterity and Strength.

                        The real reasons why vampires don't all get assassinated in their sleep is because 1) you need Correspondence 3 or Mind 4 to enter the vampire's dream (that is, without needing to be physically present around the sleeping vampire, which is extremely dangerous), and 2) few Mages would either think to or bother to do it. Not every Mage is even aware that vampires can dream. Mages don't commonly know much about vampires in general. Mages also tend to avoid vampires for much the same reasons vampires avoid mages: tangling with them is often more trouble than it's worth. If the vamp finds out what the Mage did, the Mage just earned a powerful, tenacious, immortal adversary.

                        Moreover, the sort of Rote devoted to sleep assassination sounds to me like esoteric knowledge, even among Mages. Let alone one created that can affect Vampires (it requires both Life and Matter, while mortals only require Life). Not that it wouldn't exist. It's just that it's more likely to be stored away in an obscure tome, sitting forgotten in some wizard's library. Waiting to be unearthed, should a Mage think to go looking for it.
