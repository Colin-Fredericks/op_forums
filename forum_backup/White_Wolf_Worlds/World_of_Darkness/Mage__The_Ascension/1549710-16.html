    There's also a quality issue. Could a Medieval blacksmith make a functional gun with the right knowledge? Sure. Could they make a semi-auto? No. That's a few different kinds of smiths to handle the intricate blowback mechanics.

    But you know what a that means? You have a paperweight. The hard things with guns is the ammunition and fuel. The first black powder projectile weapon was recorded in history during Europe's Medieval period (though in China of course). It did not take long for the vast majority of what goes into the body of the gun to start getting figured out. Modern gun bodies are basically tweaks of designs 100+ years old or older. Ammo is what's radically changed (and changed what the body of the gun looks like more than anything else). And no Medieval smith can make modern smokeless powder or modern primers. It's not that you can't make them yourself, but smokeless powder involves a bunch of dangerous chemicals in a complex process to maximize the pressure that expels the bullet (and has to be tweaked between revolvers, gas operated blowback, and impact operated blowback). Making primers is making a miniature explosive device and even preppers advocate just stockpiling primers instead of trying to learn how to make your own because it's too hard to do by hand at home.

    So, yeah, maybe Matter 4 listing "gun" is a bad idea because instead of "gun with ammo" (honestly a basic combustion engine isn't that different either, but a modern ICE to run a modern vehicle is a separate level of complexity), because the gun isn't the hard part of making functional firearms.

        Again you can make the components with Matter 2 and assemble them with Matter 3, that still makes 4 redundant.
        I've been thinking about this a lot. The need to assemble a device is itself a constraint on how the device is designed. I've worked with designers who have struggled to make sure there is enough room for tools to fit in and fasten parts in place, and with enough assemblers cursing because the designers missed an important dimension.

        Matter 4 doesn't have that constraint. It can make devices that are physically impossible for a person to assemble in the first place. It can mount pieces so close together it would be physically impossible for anyone to get a tool in to fasten or unfasten the connectors. It can make interlocking parts that pass through each other in ways that are geometrically impossible to put together (like those interlocking piece puzzles, only there isn't a secret trick). Instead of using screws and threaded holes to hold pieces in place, it can instead make the screws and frame all one piece so that there is no way of taking the mounted part off (or of ever putting it on in the first place). It can make outer shells that are a single piece with no way of opening or closing them. Etc.

        So yes, Matter 4 can make things that Matter 2 and competent assembly cannot.

            Matter 4

            •••• Complex Transmutation
            Complex and radical transformations now become possible, especially with the addition of other Spheres. Pumpkins can be changed into carriages (Life 2), people into thrones (Life 5), cars into robots (combinations of Forces and Prime), or thin air into banquets, so long as the mage doesn’t mind racking up a little (or a lot…) of Paradox. Different principles can be combined in complicated ways, creating cybernetic machines or electrified gold. Complicated devices (guns, cars, computers) may be conjured out of empty space if the mage understands the principles behind such things (in game terms, possesses the proper Abilities). As usual, though, complicated creations demand extended rituals and many successes.

            Full Transmutation of Complex Matter

            At this point a mage can use Matter to create complex arrangements of matter that involve multiple different forms of matter. Examples include carriages, thrones, cars, robots, banquets etc. There appears to be no limit to the complexity. Unlike Matter 2 there are no limitations on temperature, though radioactive materials may still be off limits. Even Matter 4 cannot make smoke detectors and glow in the dark watches.

            I was having the hardest time coming up with an application not already covered in the examples, but thanks to recent conversation I do have this:

            Impossible Mechanisms

            The design of complex mechanisms and computers is functionally limited by a lot of design constraints: what shapes can be made easily, how difficult is it to assemble, etc. Matter 4 doesn’t have to concern itself with manufacturing techniques or step by step assembly. It can make mechanisms of interlocking pieces that can’t be taken apart (and indeed couldn’t be assembled together in the first place, at least by hand). It can fit things close enough together that there is no room to assemble in any kind of step by step manner.

            On a similar note, Matter 4 is also free from price constraints. Bespoke circuit boards that elegantly curve in just the right ways to fit into the device that no company would make (at least not for any reasonable price. Intricately complex gears that would be ridiculously expensive to to make in one piece can be not only made by Matter 4 but made already in position and ready to work.

            As a game mechanic, I doubt this will come up in play, but in universe it would be highly prized.


            Conjunctional Effects:

            Thing to Life to Thing (Complex Transmutation + Transmute X Life)

            At this point any type of matter can be transformed into something living with either Life 3 (transmuting pumpkins is Life 3, not Life 2), Life 4 (for self), or Life 5, or back. I would point out that by the rules in Base Life, only fresh pumpkins count as life. Desiccated pumpkins or canned pumpkin would be Matter.

            I want to take a moment to point out that the example for complex life is a regular person into a piece of furniture meant to be sat on. Matter 4 uses a throne, but lawn chairs are also possible. I bring this up because it will be relevant in Matter 5.


            Cars to Robots (Complex Transmutation + ??? + Fuel Pattern?)

            Contradiction Resolution - Where’s All the Prime Gone?

            There is no problem at all with cars to robots at Matter 4. I think that the spell is assuming a running car turning into a running robot, hence the addition of Forces. However, an unpowered car should turn into an unpowered robot with Matter alone. There’s no need at all for Prime, though, as nothing is being conjured. Even in the case of an unpowered car turned into a powered robot, the addition of Forces is enough.

            It is possible, though, that this spell is supposed to be interpreted in more of a science fiction or cartoonish sense, i.e. turning a car into an animated robot servant capable of obeying limited commands or having a small amount of instinct or agency. Prime 2 never says anything about animation, but the magical effects table ties it in to that ability anyways, so possibly that is what is going on here.

                    I would argue the difference between Matter 2/3 and 4 is a Ship of Theseus situation; the sum of the parts is more than the parts themselves. On a metaphysical level, a mechanical clock has a different pattern than the parts of a clock - even if all the parts are created in the right place. Manual assembly of the parts would create the emergent mobile clock pattern, which is different from shaping the parts into existence: you're creating 'clock parts', not a 'clock'. tldr: the difference is metaphysical, not physical.

                                    Matter 5

                                    ••••• Alter Properties
                                    With Mastery, the crafter can create substances that transcend the limitations of scientific possibility, conjure materials unknown to Earthly reality, or share the deadly legacies of radioactive matter. Such Masters can shape armor out of air (with Prime 2), turn vampires into lawn furniture (Life 5), or melt steel with a thought (Mind 3). Again, such deeds remain incredibly vulgar, but they recall the godlike feats of legendry.
                                    A very thin section with questionable examples. Matter 5 can:

                                    Transform and Alter Special Materials (Alter Properties)

                                    At this level, no material is off limits. A mage can use Matter 5 to create and transmute any known substance. Radiation is now explicitly on the table, and since none of the others claimed it we can infer it was off limits until rank 5. That said, by the methodology of this thread Matter 5 cannot tell Matter 3 and 4 what they cannot do.


                                    Radioactive Materials

                                    Mages can now create all forms of radioactive materials, which already means he can create nuclear fuel and nuclear weapons, but it can actually skip those steps too.


                                    Transuranic, Heavy Elements May Not Be Used Where There Is Life

                                    Certain heavy elements have half lives so short they would fully decay in seconds. In the words of XKCD’s Randall Munroe “The result wouldn’t be like a nuclear explosion – it would be a nuclear explosion.” So mage with Matter 5 can skip the bomb and go straight to the nuclear explosion, and it doesn’t take much material to get to the level of a medium sized nuclear bomb.

                                    Of course, if he did this without Correspondence or a method of soaking a lot of damage, it would be the last spell he ever cast.


                                    Alpha Radiation

                                    Alpha radiation itself is helium nuclei, meaning there’s a good case it is the province of Matter rather than Forces. Of course, Alpha particles are easily blocked by skin and clothing, but turning matter already inside of someone into alpha particles could be a lot of radiation damage. Since the target's skin is an effective shield, this is much safer for the casting mage.

                                    Impossible Materials (Alter Properties)


                                    At this level a mage can also create materials that are impossible. This would include bricks that don’t interact in any way with glass (they pass right through), or magnets that attract specific things, or objects that are quantum locked.

                                    Note that many impossibilities were already possible at earlier levels, for instance ice that is lighter than air could be created by Alter Density, so the statement that Matter 5 can create things ‘unknown to Earthly reality’ cannot be used to limit the stated power of earlier ranks even outside the methodology of this thread.


                                    Neutronium and Psuedoneutronium

                                    The exotic state of matter that neutron stars may be made of is possible at Matter 5, as is normal matter that somehow has the density of neutronium. The distinction between those two is sometimes quite important since neutron star material would catastrophically expand outside the gravity of the star.

                                    While Alter Density is a Matter 3 ability, keeping the atomic structure of the material intact while bringing something to that density is the impossible part that makes psuedoneutronium Matter 5.



                                    These next one is here because I’m going to use it in an upcoming contradiction analysis.


                                    Heliophobic Materials

                                    Otherwise ordinary materials with the unusual quality of combusting or degrading in sunlight (and only sunlight) are the stuff of Matter 5.

                                    Imbue Properties (Alter Properties)


                                    In addition to being able to create special materials, a mage can also give properties to materials. This is mostly a semantic difference as functionally, giving a brick a special power and transforming a brick into a brick that has a special power are the same.

                                    Because it is functionally the same, its examples are interchangeable with Impossible Materials



                                    Psychoreactive Materials

                                    Otherwise ordinary materials with the unusual property of heating, melting, or combusting when exposed to concentrated mental energy would also be the stuff of Matter 5. This is another example I am using because it will come up in contradiction analysis.

                                    Everything Else (???)


                                    As with all Rank 5s, Matter 5 should be able to do just about everything possible within the Sphere’s purview.

                                    Added: From Matter 3:

                                    Matter 3 says Matter 5 is necessary to make permanent changes, which puts Matter alongside Life as the two spheres that directly lock permanent changes behind Rank 5. There is the same uncertainty of what this means as with Life (though Matter doesn’t have to deal with Pattern Bleed), so I’m not going to go through the possibilities again. Here is the permanent change possibility:


                                    From Here to Eternity (Alter Properties)

                                    A mage can change a target’s pattern permanently. Unlike spells of permanent duration, there is no magic keeping the target’s pattern this way, and this cannot be dispelled or unwoven because the magic is over and done with already. The new pattern is simply reality now.


                                    Conjunctional Effects:

                                    Unfortunately, the suggested conjunctional effects for Matter 5… aren’t. Not good ones anyways.


                                    Impossible Materials From Nothing (??? + Fuel Pattern) - Matter 5 / Prime 2

                                    Contradiction Resolution - Armor Out of Air

                                    If we assume that the armor in question is made of impossible materials, and is created ex nihilo rather than from actual atmosphere, then yes this is Matter 5/Prime 2. It would have been good to have a suggestion of what impossible material would make for really good armor.

                                    Regular armor converted from breathable air would be at least Matter 3 (for gas to solid) and possibly Matter 4 (for complex arrangements of materials), and no Prime would be necessary.

                                    Armor composed of actual air that nonetheless had the properties of combat armor would be Matter 5, and if for some reason it was conjured in a place with no atmosphere would also take Prime 2, so that’s another niche option.

                                    Vampires to Lawn Furniture (??? + Perfect Metamorphosis - Matter 5 / Life 5?

                                    Contradiction Resolution - Lawn Chairs Are Not Impossible Matter

                                    Transforming a human to a chair was already established to be Life 5/Matter 4 in the Matter 4 section. The complexity of the chair has not changed, only the target. Life attacks on Vampires do require a Matter augment, but it is Matter 2, not 5. Even if we required Matter at 1 less rank than Life to affect Vampires (and that’s not stated anywhere) that would still mean Matter 4.

                                    Perhaps the goal is to change the target into a chair made of typical materials that nevertheless combusts in sunlight. That wasn’t part of the brief, but it would take Life 5/Matter 5 to do that to a human. Ironically, though, a mage shouldn’t need Matter 5 to do the same to a vampire, since the indication is that the chair would automatically keep its vampiric nature regardless of what the mage does. In that one regard, the spell is easier to accomplish on Vampires.

                                    Melt Steel With a Thought (??? + ???) Matter 5/ Mind 3?

                                    Contradiction Resolution - What is Mind? Doesn’t Matter. What is Matter? Never Mind.

                                    If I were challenged to incorporate Mind into a Matter effect that melted steel, I could do it, but outside of that challenge I would never even consider Mind for such a spell. So, while it is certainly possible, there’s just no way I would classify Mind as a need. Nor Matter 5 for that matter. Matter 3 is certainly capable of reshaping steel, including in a way that we could call melting, and there’s nothing Matter 5 has that adds to that. This really should be a Matter 3 spell.

                                    If I had to rescue this… well I suppose Matter 5 could give steel the quality of “melting when thought at” and then Mind 3 provides the psychic blast. I question how often the need for this would arise, though.

                                    Any thoughts?
