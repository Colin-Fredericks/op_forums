                89) A sample of an engineered flower - the product of 5 years of botanical breeding work - was stolen from a Progenitor lab through unknown means. The trail ran cold quickly, and the Union gave up the search for the culprits, deciding it was better to beef up their security and move on. The Progenitor in charge of the project, though, was significantly more incensed, but could do little about it. Then, one year later, instances of the flower began showing up in public botanical gardens and horticulture shows throughout the state. They garnered a great deal of interest from Sleeper florists and biologists for its mysterious origin and eye-catching traits (it had a pitch black petal coloration, and a singularly unique scent). The Progenitor tried to get the Union to pick up the investigation again, but was stonewalled again. The plant had no inherently dangerous or impossibly unusual traits, and so its release into the public space was a non-issue for the Technocracy. Especially when so many other problems demanded the Union's attention and resources.

                Not willing to let the matter go, the Progenitor has decided to employ...radical means to find the ones who stole their work. Means that could involve reaching out to the other side. Maybe those superstitionists could find a lead where the Union could (or would) not. If they were given the proper incentive...

                Magestuck: The Ascension

                            Originally posted by Muad'Dib View Post
                            Why do you think so? What I wrote is certainly different from a group of True Mages deciding to go somewhere for a dinner.

                            Because your recent posts weren't plots. They're not even singular plot points, just most vague ideas from where a plot could be developed. To make a plot out of the "Verbena want someone to awaken" idea, there has to be something more. What's the catch? Where's the problem? There are techniques in every tradition to try and awaken apprentices, it happens all the time.

                            So, a Verbena plot.
                            The local Verbena cult leader is trying to have an apprentice awaken. She's kind of harsh with that poor guy, which kind of worries the rest of the Traditions in the city. The apprentice is supposed to survive in a particularly dangerous forest or swamp or whatever and the rest of the Traditions want's him to survive, because missing people could mean the Technocracy comes in and investigates.
                            So, to make sure the apprentice survives, the PCs are supposed to watch over him, without the apprentice or the Verbena noticing.

                            While watching over that guy in the swamp, the PCs notice some magic or strange phenomenons. Could it be, someone else is interfering with the trial? Could it be Werewolfs? Gangrel? Or maybe even a Nephandus, trying to corrupt the apprentice?

                            There's a plot. Also, no need to mention everyone is a true mage. Kind of is expected in a Mage: The Ascension game.
