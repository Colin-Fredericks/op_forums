                                I'm sorry, I missed this entirely.

                                It's in the rules for countermagic. Prime one allows for prime-senses, the effects of which are.... A.) You can countermagic things that are not targeting you. and B.) if you get more successes than the opponent you can 'flip' the spell turning it back on them. With every success beyond those needed to cancel theres counting as a success towards the effect.

                                So say someone throws a fireball at you. They roll two successes, you roll three. You negate their fireball and throw a 3 damage fireball back at them. (1 success + forces bonus damage.)

                                The point is not that you can 'flip' the spell, though that's what the wording is there for. But that it confirms that countermagic can also counter abilities if you get more successes than the opponent rather than just removing successes.

                                Seeing as the rules for cross-splat countering are essentially stillborn and they can only do one magical action per turn, (Barring talismans.) and countermagic takes that up.....

                                Though mages with the Kinfolk merit, IIRC can make always coincidental spells if they use sphere magic to recreate Garou gifts.
