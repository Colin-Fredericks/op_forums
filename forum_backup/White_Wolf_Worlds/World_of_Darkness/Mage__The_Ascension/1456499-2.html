                                She's a Manager, works in media together with the amalgam, is an extremely public figure, with Resources, Fame, Cult, Status, Mentor, Retainer and Backup, and her main shtick is being a huge celebrity.

                                A HIT Mark would be a liability. The problem isn't if the robot can pass for human, but your general level of exposition. Since you're a celebrity you're not the only one under exposure. Your security team's primary activity isn't even protecting you from danger. They must deal with all types of weirdos and fans, and they will be under continuous public scrutiny too, even if from a lesser degree. They're harassed, questioned, sometimes even interviewed themselves, they're not just security, they're YOUR security.

                                Your work isn't to stay in a war zone, either, under attack your security team's function consists on getting you out quickly and safely, not engaging. Assessment and repositioning are more valuable skills than facing heavy artillery, in a pinch the whole team should be out of Dodge as fast as possible and call in reinforcements. The places you go are also a thing, your security team must be able to work with the staff of other places and events, they have to be accepted, make your work easier rather than harder by, lets say, punching your mom in public.

                                Don't try to overkill. A staff member that leaves a body is better for you than one that can survive a machine gun assault in the face in front of the cameras, the best is if they can get injured and be visited by your fans in the hospital, for even better media coverage.

                                            Earlier, someone made a brief mention of SPD — or rather, they talked about how the Syndicate has ties to Pentex, which unfortunately tends to be treated as synonymous with SPD. I have my own take on what SPD is; and if you can talk your Storyteller into accepting this notion, it opens up some additional opportunities.

                                            In my view, SPD is less about Pentex and its service to the Wyrm, and is more about fringe science: the Fringe TV Series would be one example of an SPD-centered chronicle. Your diva isn't SPD; but she could have her security team requisition some great from them; and that gear could include countermeasures for Magick. If you're familiar with Hunter: the Vigil, Task Force Valkyrie's Advanced Tech includes a lot of stuff that would work well as products of this not blatantly corrupt take on SPD.

                                            In short, if you're concerned with your diva being attacked by supernatural forces, you can equip her security detail with gear that's designed to deal specifically with supernatural threats. The Syndicate would still likely insist that whatever you come up with be as Coincidental as possible; e.g., special rounds for their guns that harm spirits and ghosts of you're concerned with the Traditionalists using Spirit Magick against her, and/or visors based on the principles of Kirlean photography that let the agent see so-called “mystical energies” so that he's aware of incoming threats of that nature.

                                            My earlier mention of how the Management & HR instrument can be applied factors in here, too: if the security chief is Enlightened and has some knowledge of Dimensional Science, he could use Management & HR to make the security team's perimeter patrols double as a ward against intrusion by spiritual entities.

                                            The point is that you don't need to have the security team be ignorant of our defenseless against supernatural vectors of attack; they're just not going to be blatant about it.

                                                        Just to clarify, I work at a big bank IRL. We have a concept in the industry where starting at a certain level of assets a client gets very special treatment. It isn't simply special service, it is every rule for the business being different to account for for two factors: how much increased risk the client is at because of their position and how much the economy as a whole can suffer if something is out of place.

                                                        Of course this is partly the bank giving nice VIP treatment to the über rich, but this is also because if the owner of a factory chain have a silly problem and can't access her bank account for a couple days this can effectively disrupt jobs, payments, negotiations and food supplies in a large scale. This person is so important that we actually cannot give her normal treatment not even if she asks. She isn't a person, she's a community's asset.

                                                        We are not the only industry to do this, either, and in several instances this treatment is required by law. That's the thing I said about her being too VIP. Her security IS top priority. If she is that influential and rich she is at least like Oprah. She can be the target of a terrorist attack, she can be targeted by foreign countries, she attracts wacko fans that aren't just kids anymore. And her work moves an industry. As OP said, she already brought BILLIONS in revenue to the Syndicate, and that through absolutely clean, proper and public means, so she pretty much moves a sector of the media industry.
