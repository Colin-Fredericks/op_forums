                I'm having trouble finding where social Attribute boosting is covered... in might not be M20 core (though it was that way in previous editions).

                The problem is that the Physical/Social/Mental Attribute divide has nothing to do with the way Spheres are designed, but the Sphere rules use those clusters anyway. The Attributes aren't designed with any sort of philosophic position on the relation of mind and body. If you want to perform a Feat of Strength greater than your current Strength allows, you roll Willpower to see if you can pull it off. Appearance is both your literal physical appearance and your intuitive understanding of non-verbal communications and norms (how you dress is not a physical activity but absolutely impacts how people perceive you). Perception is a neural process even if the mind is what makes sense of the raw data the body picks up.

                So the Attributes categories are meant to be broad generalities that allow for lots of edge cases (specifically to allow for fun Attribute + Ability combos where in the right situations any can be paired up). Solidly pegging some Attributes to Life and some to Mind is always going to cause dissonance within the rules. But that's what we're stuck with.

                                                        So, here's the thing. You don't need a deep scientific explanations. You need the most basic of science explanation and leave the cultural myths to the Mythic Thread/Hypernarrative mechanics.

                                                        Here's the simple science: there are zero human pheromones, and zero non-human pheromones that cause pheromone responses in humans. It is likely impossible for humans to be impacted by pheromones because what few mammalian pheromones we have identified in other species are processed by the VNO... something that's near vestigial in humans and in humans doesn't have the ability to access the neural pathways that non-human pheromones operate on.

                                                        Using Forces to manipulate ambient light levels and colors "buffs" social rolls (Magick Enhancing Abilities exists for a reason) more than pheromones ever will when dealing with humans... but I don't think anyone's going to claim that Forces can give you extra dots of Charisma or Manipulation (even with a literal glow up!).

                                                        As is usually important to note here, this isn't to say that the chemical excretions of humans don't matter to social interactions. Obviously they do. If someone walks in and you perceive them as smelling bad, you are negatively inclined towards them socially. The problem is that "bad" is an experiential memory mediated response. What you think makes someone smell good or bad in ways that impact your impressions of them are formed from a lifetime of unique personal experiences, direct socialization, and cultural norms that vastly overwhelm any potential instinctive reactions to specific chemical signals (which is also a real thing, but pheromones aren't just chemicals you have a response to).

                                                        There is no "trust" smell, there are the smells you associate with the people that you trust(ed) deeply and the situations you associate with them. If you really trusted your grandfather, and you grandfather was a woodworker, you likely have strong positive associations with the smell of fresh wood shavings. If you were forced into shop class because you were AMAB and the shop teacher was the football couch treated you like shit because you did theater instead of playing sports like a boy should... you probably don't think of the smells of woodworking the same way.

                                                        The same broadly holds true of things like body posture. There isn't a single "good" body posture that makes you more positively attention grabbing or trustworthy. It's your ability to modulate your posture to the social context you're in that matters.

                                                        Again, the Magick Enhancing Abilities is an obvious place for a lot of these things. The problem is stretching them to a full "increase dots in" effect when they're so contextual.

                                                        The logic of why Mind 1 can boost Attributes is beyond me, but Mind lets you directly influence emotional responses without having to mediate for specific individual associations. While we're not there in the thread yet, I don't think it's jumping the gun to say Mind can do, "everyone look at me," or, "aura of trust" effects that don't need to worry about the nuances that attempting such influences with Life would.

                                                        IF we were to have in the real world the capabilities that Life Sphere expouses, then modifiying the bodies will make you more intelligent, charismatic, manipulative, funny, witty, etc. In game terms, only on this topic I agree with HDYDT.
                                                        Even if we take a scientific paradigm on this (something I don't agree with for reasons both Darth Revan and monteparnas have elaborated on), the science doesn't back this up.

                                                        Every attempt to come up with a scientifically valid definition of "intelligence" has failed utterly and most tests of intelligence are testing much more specific things that humans culturally associate with the concept of intelligence. For example there's a neural cluster in the brain that's frequently larger in "smart" people... but it has nothing to do with how well you understand concepts, or whether or not you can solve a problem. It essentially governs how many different thoughts you can consciously juggle at once. The ability to get to the solution faster because you can handle more parts of the problem at once is seen as "smart" and thus intelligent but... it by WoD definitions it's really Wits not Intelligence since we're talking about speed of thought instead of depth of problem solving skills.

                                                        Increasing perceptiveness runs into similar problems. We already sense orders of magnitude more stimuli than our brains can handle. We spend far more brain power sorting noise to find important details than we do scanning our environment. Those filtering processes are complicated, use multiple parts of the brain, and there's no simple way to increase their effectiveness across the board.

                                                        M20's Enhancement Background touches on an important thing here (interestingly Vicissitude also deals with this): living organisms are hard to flat out "improve." While not a zero-sum situation, drastic alterations generally improve one thing at the cost of something else. Dwayne 'The Rock' Johnson once put out the diet he ate during his wrestling career, and the specific needs to attain a current physical appearance and the physical ability to perform the high intensity stunt-athletics of it. It's an insane diet to try to replicate without including the years of working up to it, but it's also a necessary style of diet for that kind of physique. It would an extremely unhealthy diet outside of that context (he doesn't keep it up now that he's an actor and has stunt teams to do cinematic magic instead of needing to perform things himself live). If, in a scientific lens, you gave someone what the WoD defines as haveing 2/2/2 in physicals Strength 5 and didn't modify them into not just inhuman but impossible biological ways, it would either not work (the rest of their body wouldn't be able to actualize the increased power) or would destroy the body that can't handle the radical changes.

                                                        A fictional example I like is one from the X-Men and specifically a scene where Quicksilver is in a therapy session, because he's an antisocial asshole and people think he needs to work on that. Something he points out though, is that his powers extend to his mind, and he can't completely turn them off. His social problems are because his mind is constantly moving at the speeds necessary to function so that he can physically move as fast as he does and still know what he's doing. Having conversations with normal people is so excruciatingly slow for him that he just... can't stand most social situations.

                                                        Yes, we're talking about magic, and magic doesn't have to be constrained by such things, but if you want to rely on a scientific POV you end up having to consider them. If you want to use magic to manipulate real world events and increase the impact by taking advantage of scientific principles, the magic doesn't change what those scientific principles are (unless you can use more magic to address those, but that voids the point of taking advantage of existing science in the first place).
