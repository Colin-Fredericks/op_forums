    Originally posted by Nyrufa View Post
    So not only did they screw over humanity's potential in the name of 'protecting' us, but they also saw fit to screw over the potential of countless other sentient life forms.
    The Technocracy are humanists, first and foremost. Protecting humanity comes before anything else, just as the Union expects every other potential sapient beings to be protecting itself before anything else. Given that the Union tends to be cynical, assuming that other beings are preoccupied with their own self-interest comes naturally to them.

    And they jumped to the conclusion that these life forms were out to kill us, rather than being interested in establishing intergalactic diplomacy?
    It's not really "jumping to the conclusion" if the Technocracy, in one form or another, has been making forays into the Umbra for centuries, and they've generally encountered "Lovecraft was right" as a rule. They aren't being paranoid or xenophobic when their fears tend to pan out.

    Maybe their own practices predispose Umbral creatures to despise them. Maybe some werewolves have been making sure that the Void Engineers only ever encounter dangerous stuff in the Umbra, and not the idyllic gardens or peaceful races. Maybe the Technocracy's own fears shape what they find, as the nature of the place tends to make an Umbral traveler find what they expect to. Or maybe the Void Engineers have been very unlucky.

    Regardless, no matter if their experiences are skewed towards the negative, those are still their experiences, and they've made an entirely valid reaction to being confronted with a galaxy that largely hates them and wants them to die. Even though I'm sure the Void Engineers encounter plenty of peaceful or benign creatures too, on the whole they see the Void as a dangerous and unwelcoming place in aggregate.

    If they don't get to conquer the galaxy, then nobody does. That's their line of thinking?
    I must remind you, there IS no normal space beyond a certain distance from the Earth. It's ALL Umbra beyond Mars.

    Sure, within the context of that space, there are vast distances to the next galaxy. In terms of what we call the non-Umbra Material World, though, humanity is taking up a relatively small chunk of cosmic real estate. And the Union knows this. It's the arrangement they prefer. When taken from this line of thinking, the Void beyond the world is mostly in another dimension. It's only the Sleepers who are under the impression that space is an uninterrupted expanse you can just go to, if you pilot a shuttle far enough.

    As such, the Union knows that "preventing alien races from exploring the galaxy" doesn't really work that way. It requires a certain degree of Enlightenment to break into the Horizon, and yet more to reach the Deep Umbra. And recall, in the Deep Umbra, Here Be Cthulhu. Nephandi ride living abominations as their space craft, and any kind of rules of Reality as people understand it break down. To the Union, the farther you travel, the more incomprehensibly alien you get, and not in a good way.

    Moreover, even if we assume that beyond the Deep Umbra, there are other sapient beings, they would probably be inhabiting their own Tellurian, with its own set of beliefs. From the Technocracy's perspective, those guys can attend to their own affairs, because THEIR beliefs make the rules for THEIR part of the cosmos. Creating a Paradigm of vast interstellar distance only really matters when it comes to strengthening Earth's Gauntlet.

They had an empire that spanned across literally billions of planets. The various alien races were being pushed back or destroyed, belief in the supernatural was almost completely abandoned in favor of secular reasoning and hard sciences. Pretty much everything the Technocracy is currently striving to accomplish.

                Then the Emperor's greatest followers learned that there were a few trust issues between them, which lead to them questioning their faith in him. Admittedly, the Emperor's grand plan was to create a pyschic barrier that would have basically neutered the Chaos Gods' influence, but he refused to divulge that information to even his most trusted warriors, whom he genetically modified using his own DNA and who revered him like he was their own father. These seeds of doubt were all the Chaos Gods needed to manipulate half the empire (which again, billions of inhabited planets) to turn against him, thus resulting in a civil war that even 10,000 years later, they have not recovered from.

                In fact, it's so bad that humanity began to see technology itself as being inherently magical and started praying to it on a regular basis. If your gun jams when you pull the trigger, it's not due to a technical malfunction, it's because the spirit of the gun isn't angry enough to spit a chunk of exploding metal at your designated target. So you have to perform a war chant in order to psyche the spirit up for battle. And, because the supernatural in 40k is fueled by the thoughts and emotions of those who believe in it, this actually gets the gun working again!

                    Going on about something that's going to happen in -- worst estimate -- four billion years*...is this an April Fool's post?

                    Because, I'ma take this seriously for a minute. The Technocracy already has extrasolar colonies, populated by enough EC's to create a localized Consensus and be self-sustaining. Sparing that, if humanity survives that long, by the time the Sun starts expanding Jupiter's moons will be well within its habitable zone and would probably make nice places to live with a little terraforming and/or habitat construction.

                    [* Yes, I know most astronomers' and physicists' estimates are that the Earth will be destroyed by the Sun going nova between five to seven billion years from now. But, that doesn't take into account the possibility of the Earth's orbit being shifted well before then due to the effects of increased solar winds and the Sun's loss of mass during its expansionary period. Nor the fact that, long before this happens, the Sun's habitable zone will be pushed outwards to Jupiter's orbit.]

                    Originally posted by Nyrufa View Post
                    ...And, because the supernatural in 40k is fueled by the thoughts and emotions of those who believe in it, this actually gets the gun working again!
                    That works for Eldar and Orks, who are all innately psykers on some level, not humans. Not even human Force weapons work that way. What happened, is that all but a small cross-section of humanity (the Adeptus Mechanicus) forgot how AI works, or even that AI exists. The Adeptus Mechanicus knows full well what's up; it just happens to be the case such knowledge is heretical enough to get even Mars on the Exterminatus short list.

                    Thus, the Adeptus Mechanicus promulgates a big lie (even internally, invoking heavy doublethink and newspeak among their number) there is no such thing as AI, but instead machines have spirits of their own that have to be prayed to and appeased to work. Less knowledgeable people take that big lie and run with it, attributing any mechanical fault or software glitch to the machine spirits. Or, for that matter, how vehicles up to and including titans "become full of the Emperor's righteous fury and will themselves to fight on, even when the crew is completely incapacitated or dead". The AM lies their asses off about how STC's work, and how hardware produced by STC's work, or that anything bigger than a Mars-pattern boltgun has onboard AI for standard operation and troubleshooting procedure.

                    [Edit, for those unfamiliar with Warhammer 40K. STC stands for Standard Template Construct, which was in-universe humanity's key to conquering the stars. STC's were, in their purest form, miniaturized refineries and manufactories coupled with an AI-assisted database containing standardized blueprints and schematics for anything a human colony would need to construct on-site for survival, habitation, and defense. Moreover, the AI assistance came into play especially for altering those construction templates into forms optimized for their humans' survival and tactical needs, as either an automated, or manual but incredibly user-friendly, process. And, they were designed in such a way anyone could operate one and alter schematics to fit needs, regardless of education or technical expertise.

                    Basically, they were the equivalent of Star Trek's industrial replicators.

                    All human colonies and colony ships were equipped with one or more STC's. But, in the few dozen millennia since, they were almost all lost, destroyed, or broke down over time, and almost none remain intact and operational. But, even finding a defunct STC is a Big Fucking Deal, since their databases may be intact and containing their original, or even updated, schematics that can be replicated and uploaded to other, functional, STC's.]

                    Hence why those righteously zealous self-driving vehicles are those that come exclusively from STC's, why the AM has such a hard-on for nobody but themselves having STC access, and why STC vehicles are "holier" than non-STC vehicles or refurbs. (Genuine) Baneblades and Land Raiders being the "smoking gun", as they can only be produced via STC and are the ones most notorious for continuing to operate after their crews were killed.

                    No, the only god or spirit humanity is memeing into existence -- and even then, it's the human psykers being sacrificed by the millions, not the run of the mill evolved monkey -- is the fetal fifth Chaos God.
