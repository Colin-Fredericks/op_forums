      So, this is a question about DtF, not MtA? If so, it should probably be moved to the general WoD forum, which handles all gamelines that don't have their own forums.

                                  What I meant to say is that for demon's their Torment is a representation of entropy and corruption caused by The Fall and everything that came after, particularly their time in The Pit.
