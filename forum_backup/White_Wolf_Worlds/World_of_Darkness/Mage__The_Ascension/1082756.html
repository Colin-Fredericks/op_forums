    Several questions regarding the Syndicate(and others)

    04-23-2017, 04:37 PM
    Currently I'm re-reading revised CB to Technocracy play. And I have discovered a number of intriguing statements related to the Syndicate. Here are some of the most curious.

    Every answer, help, advise, opinion, supposition, speculation and conjecture would be gladly appreciated.


    Syndicate to Iteration X:
    They craft critical components to the world of finance. Iterator engineers provide active security for all the world’s financial institutions — custodial and investment banks, financial services firms, government treasuries, stock markets, etc. (the so-called “bulge bracket”) — from Virtual Adept assholes. Of course, the smaller, consumer-level banks and credit unions get looked after too, but they’re not priority targets. Iterators also work with Financiers to pioneer the future of digital banking, one that will instill in the Masses trust rather than fear. Without them, the Syndicate would not be able to forge the next great age of commerce.
    Does that mean the Syndicate is controlling and directing all the world's financial institution and subcontracting the security work to the Iteration X? Or they are merely arranging those institutions' contract with the Iterators?

    Syndicate to Void Engineers:
    They keep submitting requests for weapons and military training in order to counter something they call “Threat Null,” but don’t shed much light on.
    Why the Void Engineers need weapons and military training from the Syndicate? Both their weapons and training are infinitely more advanced than the Syndicate. It is likely comparing Star Trek Federation military to US "Future Soldiers". Really, Syndicate has nothing to offer to the VE except Primal Energy.

    Another interesting quote is:

    De-prioritizing space exploration in the West has helped to lessen their hold on government and public alike (though, not without resentment) and sends a clear message that they need to cover their bottom line or else.
    I have no problem to understand Syndicate possesses more than enough political clout to de-prioritize space exploration in the West, even though political and governmental sphere is usually NWO's purview. However, I'm not able to see why the Syndicate moved against and sabotaged VE's efforts.

    Especially if we take account Void Engineers opinion to the Syndicate;

    Frankly, that makes the Syndicate easier to work with. They’re trying to sell us a future; it’s not hard to see the Enlightened puppet strings around the birth of commercial space flight.
    So the Syndicate is de-prioritizing government space exploration while establishing commercial space flight? It is a pure inconsistency...but one could think it is correspond with capitalistic agenda of the Corporate Convention.

    Syndicate to Vampires;
    On the other hand, some of their longer-lived ilk have entrenched themselves in Consensual economy. Honestly, it’s hard not to respect the power that comes from being able to invest in an institution for decades or even centuries — though don’t mistake that respect for vampiric reverence overall.
    So why the Syndicate respects elder vampires? Being able to invest an institution for centuries is nothing exceptional or outstanding by itself, if your have indefinite time.

    Syndicate to other Deviants;
    In spite of the Void Engineer’s reports, there are no such things as ghosts, faeries, demons, etc. They serve as entertaining (and profitable) fiction, nothing more.
    Does that mean the Syndicate is completely ignorant about existence of such entities? Or they simply do not acknowledge these as mythical, arcane beings? After all, VE use terms such as PLE or extradimensional creatures. And even if the Syndics are woefully unversed in DS, they can see and interact with ghosts, spirits(well, if they choose to take a form and wreck havoc), faeries and demons - and in case of ghosts, they are fully capable of sensing and sterilizing Haunts with Primal Energy.

    Disbursements and Primal Venture:
    Disbursements also contains the largest and most secure Ventures under Technocratic control, packaging and distributing the very essence of reality to aid our efforts and projects across the world.
    Does it mean Disbursements directly manage and control certain international corporations and global conglomerates and extract Primal Energy from the operations?

    The Syndicate controls the largest collection of Nodes and Ventures of any Enlightened organization in the world, and they are maintained almost exclusively by the Extraction Division. These specialists not only arrange for the security and secrecy of node locations, but also harvest, distill, and distribute the resulting condensed Primal Energy to operatives within and without the Convention.
    Do it means 1) Syndicate separate natural nodes and industrial Ventures. 2) Syndicate has trained experts of node extraction. 3) Aforementioned 'the largest and most secure Ventures' are under direct control of Extraction Division. 4) Syndicate provides secrecy and military security of not only Enlightened Prime Ventures, but also Primal Node locations. 5) Syndicate paradigm of Venture is compatible with Nodes...or node extraction specialists have different paradigm of Prime from other, 'normal' Syndicate members. 6) Technocratic Unions generate several orders of magnitude greater Primal Energy than any other supernatural/paranormal("Enlightened" - or this usage is solely limited to the Technocratic Conventions?) organization in the world, but it is still not sufficient to fund various Technocratic projects and endeavors?

    Media Control and Newspaper:

    When Media Control was in its infancy, the original amalgams attempted to assume control of what was then the cutting edge of journalism:
    newspapers. They met with stiff competition in the form of the news conglomerates of the day, especially William Randolph Hearst, who along with his colleagues rebuffed any offer of a merger.
    So William Randolph Hearst and his ilk was a non-aligned cabal of Mind mages? If not, how they were able to resist against powerful Ars Cupiditae and Mind Procedures routinely utilized by the Media Control?


    And finally, why the Syndicate is so deeply involved in organized crime and illegal drug trades? Given the Syndicate's scope of operations and revenue it generates, it is redundant at best, degenerative at worst. If profit is mattered, they can reap even greater profit via investing international corporations, global financial institutions, Primal Ventures, etc. Is it merely another means of Mass control?


    Besides, Progenitors CB makes me wonder.

    We had the best of intentions. The need for the release and glamor these street drugs provide is an incurable part of human existence. Curbing demand was dismissed as impossible, so the only recourse was to make them safer, cleaner, and with minimal side effects. That was until the Syndicate got their grubby mitts in the business. They have changed the street labs into the biggest source of income for not just the Progenitors, but the Union as a whole. Long-gone are the days of concern over the purity and safety of the product. The street lab organizations take their cues from their Syndicate masters, who allow the Progenitors in the field only enough authority to insure smooth daily operations. The strategic direction is solely focused on profit, and has been for decades now.
    I can readily dismiss it as a gross overstatement or anti-Syndicate propaganda. But still, the statement itself is confounding as hell. How on earth drug trade is more profitable than controlling global finance and industries?

Plus, they have to have the chops to find all the embezzlement that their mage colleagues are using Enlightened economics to cover up.
