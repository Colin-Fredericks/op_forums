                                                        The Syndicate

                                                        We've never been this low. And people see money as a joke. But they still use markets, they buy this and sell that, that's money. But they say keeping count of points in separate accounts isn't the same as money. Counting the beans isn't the same as counting the interest payments, this city is a lifeboat. We've got to bring back the respectability of money, it's economic value that makes people value their time and seek better ways to do things.
