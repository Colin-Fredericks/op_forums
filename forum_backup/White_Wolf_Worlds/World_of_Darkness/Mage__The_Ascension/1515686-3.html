The material plane is an illusion and a "prison for the soul". Gnosticism. A great cataclysm separated matter and spirit for WtA. Akin to "God (the real God) fragmented himself" from Gnosticism.

                                                            All games of WoD reinforce that. Changelling is the same, "the Shattering". It's all a work of the evil Demiurge that set that terrible scourge of vampirism into the world and that trapped the "good guys" following Lucifer (the saint) into the Abyss. That's gnosticism 101.
                                                            Ascension (the final book of the original run) had a section in it suggesting a Fractured Cosmos, where each gameline exists in its own universe, with its own metaphysics; but with a resonant effect that causes the events in the various parallel universes to rhyme. For instance, Awakened people in the Mage universe have counterparts in the Hunter universe that are Witches; and while there are no Garou in the Vampire universe, there are lupines, which are similar but not quite the same.

                                                            This is a meta-concept, implementing in-setting the following idea: the World of Darkness isn't one thing; each gameline is its own thing, which is consistent with other gamelines in some ways and inconsistent with them in other ways. Trying to come up with a single, consistent cosmology that fits every gameline is an exercise in futility.

                                                            And that's why, this being the Mage forum, I'm not going to discuss the Gnosticism that may or may not be present in the other gamelines: it's not relevant to a discussion about Mage.
