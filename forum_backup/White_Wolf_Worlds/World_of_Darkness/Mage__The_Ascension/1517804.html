    Ahl-I-Batin and Taftani's success makes me question the Council of Nines competence

    07-12-2023, 03:13 PM
    Now it’s well known that the Ahl-I-Batin left the Council of Nine due to the general lack of concern the Council of Nine had towards the Middle East. Long story short, they ended up using the Taftani as Union bait, and with their aid, they were able to push the Union out of the Middle East. But it doesn't just stop there, the Ahl-I-Batin decided to cement their status as protectors of the Middle East by getting involved in its politics and have been shown to increase their web of influence. The Taftani however seem to be going in a different direction, while most Taftani are more interested in staying in their little territories, a growing number seem to have decided to spread their influence outside of the Middle East.

    Afridi, The drug lord of Afghanistan, who sells opium and hashish to the Russians and Cartels, who would then distribute it in Technocracy entrenched countries, believes by getting the citizens of those countries addicted and weakened, he can undermine the Technocracy. He's not above-using mind magick to do it either. This has both made him rich and #1 on the Technocratic kill list, although it doesn't seem like they will be able to get rid of him anytime soon, as they have already sent three assault teams with all of them disappearing. He also had 3 out of 4 Russian soldiers who were occupying Afghanistan addicted to smoking opium or mainlining heroin, it was stated that this addiction stayed with them even when they went back to Russia. He also takes credit for their defeat as he would cut off the supply of any soldier he felt fought too enthusiastically, sending them into withdrawal.

    Bin-Jadis used the wealth he gained from the Oil Crisis, to create a sanctum in the Arabic district of Harlem in New York. He proceeded to buy a theater to turn into a second sanctum and has become a major political player in New York, with a liberal use of mind magick of course. He has made it to where the Arabic district is now recognized as “Little Arabia”, and is now reminiscent of what you would see in the Middle East. Even the street signs are in Arabic. He was also the catalyst behind Disney releasing Aladdin and has already talked the local school board into teaching Arabian Nights. This all goes towards affecting the Technocratic Paradigm on a larger scale.

    Now you must be wondering, how can I claim that a growing number of Taftani are spreading their influence when I only mentioned two? Well, there's not just those two but also their apprentices. It was explicitly stated that Afridi does not hold the same tendency as most Taftani for isolationism, as he has a small army of gunmen, apprentices, consorts, and djinn, and no doubt Bin-Jadis will take on some of his own. No doubt their apprentices will follow the same path as their masters and will likely decide to keep in contact with their peers to further each other's political power and weaken the technocratic paradigm.

    All this is to say, is that from the way I see it, the Ahl-I-Batin and Taftani have accomplished more in 60-70 years, than the 5-6 centuries the Council of Nine have existed for.
