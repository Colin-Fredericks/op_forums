                                  Originally posted by CajunKhan View Post
                                  I'm looking at the Sons of Ether book, and one of the pregen characters uses money for Matter and Prime effects. How does that work in practice?
                                  Prime is, among other things, a highly valuable and rare energy source, so even if you can't use dollar bills to soak up quint directly, you can use money to buy the equipment that lets you manufacture tass or enchant an item.

                                  * "I can build that laser gun (read: Artifact) for you, but the exotic reactor you'd need to keep the thing operating would cost a small fortune" (Prime)

                                  * "I've got a nanotech process that will construct food or metals from raw etheric energies. Just need to buy some exotic materials to power the initial fabricators." (Matter/Prime)

                                  * "Transparent aluminum? Sure, sure, no problem, no problem at all. Ah, er, the chemicals I need do run a bit pricey, and I left my wallet in my other ethersuit..." (Matter)

                                  As with Syndicate ops, your character doesn't necessarily need the technical knowhow; they can have a bevy of contacts and business partners who can do the impossible, all of them merely held back by a lack of funding. Note that M20 generally expects these "focus characters" to be substantiated as a Background; Revised doesn't seem to.

                                  The Syndicate can also use Matter/Prime to simulate being in a hypertech supply chain - i.e. you "create" the item (the effect) but the actual in-universe action is using $$$ to pay a high-tech manufacturer to print you whatever you want and a cutting-edge delivery service to drone drop it at your feet within the hour. There's no real reason other technomancers couldn't do roughly the same. If you need it now you can go vulgar and use exotic methods to have it teleported to you (or, as the example in the Syndicate book suggests, use cutting-edge VirtAdept math to refine it out of raw nothingness with sophisticated models.)

                                      Originally posted by 11twiggins View Post

                                      Isn't the Etherite thing... SCIENCE! Equivalent Exchange is... well I mean I accept "this thing spins, therefore science", since it's thematic and cool. A series of tubes and glowy things which spin making lightning is awesome, and technobabble explains that.

                                      Putting... money in a... in a box. And then just stating what you... want? Beyond being horribly Vulgar no matter how you try and sell it, it's also just a bit out of place no?
                                      For Science! to work, all you need is a theory. Not all Etherites are gears, electrodes, and steam engines. If your theory is that the value of money and the value of an object makes them inherently linked in some way. In the same way that one can turn lead into gold just be rearranging the molecular makeup of the lead to resemble gold, so too then can you rearrange the physical make up of substances to reflect their inherent monetary value. If that's the case, then it should be possible to go the other way, and turn them back from monetary value into real objects.

                                      Currently all objects exist in a state of "value" that lies beyond our physical plane. In order to bring these items forth, we require a way of transferring their "value" state into a "physical" state, and the best way to do that is to convert them via the following equation.

                                      Object = Money^(e x SquareRoot(Time)) where e is the exponential constant.

                                      Now, of course, it is a well known that Time is in some way equatable to money, and though some...lesser scientists may disagree, it is quite possible to convert the lengths and measures of time into a value containing the same units as money. In this way, we can simplify this equation and work out the exact monetary value of the object as it exists within this "value" state. Once that is worked out, we can then begin the process of converting the "Value" state of the object into the "physical" state we are more familiar with. This can be described by the application of a very familiar formula used for the Theory of Relativity, here adapted for our purposes.

                                      VObj = PObj(C^2)

                                      Where VObj is the number given for our "value" state, PObj is the number for our "physical" state, and C is the universal constant known better to some as the Speed of Light, which, as I'm sure everyone is well aware, mediated by the transfer of particles within the ether that exists all around us. This formula describes the Value state to be the same as the Physical state which has been exposed to the universal constant, i.e. the Physical state transfers to the Value state becoming exposed to the particles of light within the ether to an extreme degree. This may be the reason why objects which are burnt begin to lose value. As they burn, their Physical state is transformed into the Value state, and what is left then contains less monetary worth than what it was before being burned. The fire allowed a greater amount of ether to interact with the object, and thus, for lack of a better term, it "bled" value. Physical state worth transformed into Value state worth, joining the epherial realm and thus leaving behind only that which had less exposure to the Ether.

                                      So how would one reverse this process and turn a pure Value state back into a Physical state? Well, as with any equation, you would need to perform a little algebra.

                                      PObj = VObj / (C^2)

                                      So while Value states are objects which have received a great deal of ether, Object states must then be Value which have been removed from Ether.

                                      Now that presents a problem because Ether is in essence all around us. In order to get an object into being, we would have to in some way remove Ether exposure. However, there is another problem. In order for the object to come into being, we would need something to have that objects same amount of value. Now, in theory you could use any objects of equal value for this, however in practice the value of individual objects is so specifically small and ever fluctuating, that it is simply more practical to use cash, especially as cash can be devided into several denominations such as the farthing, and the ha-penny, making it far more ideal for this kind of intricacy.

                                      To make this work then, we would need:

                                      A) Some way to equate the Value state of the object we are trying to transform towards the Physical state
                                      B) A way in which to remove Ether from the system so as to allow the conditions for such a transformation to take place.

                                      And this, ladies and gentlemen, is precisely what I have done. By placing the exact worth of the Value state of the object you wish to transform within this tightly sealed Ether Isolation Cage enthused Box, We can create the exact conditions necessary to conjure practically any object we would need. In many ways, it's like paying the universe for an instant delivery.

                                      The values converge, and once the box is open the object is solid enough to remain Physical even after being reintroduced to Ether.
                                      There may be a few paradox bugs around, especially if the object itself receives too much ether in too short a time, but then this principle still needs a little more exploration before it can be fully understood.

                                          Remember that a mage with sufficient money can just BUY hypertech, provided they have a supply chain in place or sufficient teams of unAwakened but Hypertech educated researchers/tech people. Just like other mages might have what M20 calls Minimum Abilities to do many of their Effects, a mage employing Hypereconomics (like a Syndicate op or a former Syndicate Etherite) might make use of Minimum Background scores to do theirs. Retainers, Spies, Cult (of personality), Influence, Requisitions, Contacts, and, yes, Resources. All of these can allow an Enlightened person to do great things.

                                          In the case of the Syndicate, they believe that "you get what you pay for". Given that the Syndicate bankrolls the rest of the Union, it's not surprising Syndicate ops believe entirely in the efficacy of the technology the other Conventions produce. The tech better work, they paid enough for it. So while a more coincidental application of Matter + Prime would be to use supply chains to have materials produced and transported over, a more vulgar application might involve just turning on the hypertech devices (if not Devices) and letting the tech earn its price tag. Teleporters, atomic reconstructors, 3D printers, and good ol' nanomachines (son) will make something from nothing in a flash. It's going to invite Paradox, because the Syndicate op is "buying prototypes ahead of when their bugs are worked out", but it can be helpful in a pinch.

                                          If you're an Etherite employing these methods, it's largely the same principle. Sure, the supply chain isn't as regulated and expansive in the Society of Ether as it is in the whole of the Technocratic Union. But the Etherites DO have supply chains - even the most brilliant Progressive Scientist won't be building an Ether Ship in his basement, at least not without help. Whole heck of a lot of Consors, Acolytes (or "lab assistants"), and techno-Sorcerers running around, helping more Enlightened Etherite scientists do their work.

                                          Moreover, Etherites tend to generate more ideas and plans than they have time or resources to implement. There's likely a brisk trade in blueprints among Etherites, just as the Order of Hermes has its brisk trade in Hermetic treatises. Designs for gadgets that only require a bit of manpower to put together. A sufficiently wealthy Etherite who doesn't have enough time to invent himself could always buy these blueprints, hire Consor lab techs to construct them, and then pick up the results afterwards. Sure, there's little prestige in using the inventions of others, but some Etherites (like the Adventurers) see the actions they perform with powerful tech to be more important than making it. (You think Batman sits in his cave and invents all his own stuff? Not likely, when there are CRIMES that need fighting! That's the advantage of being rich.)

                                                      I think the example of a Syndicate member using money for Mind procedures is excellent, and it makes perfect sense to use it as a focus for that.

                                                      What I myself am actually struggling with is the idea of using Money for aformentioned Matter/Prime effects under the Syndicate paradigm.
                                                      As was explained before, the Syndicate member essentially uses money as a focus for an investment that, using some already existing supply chain or another, produces the item he wants. Okay, alright.

                                                      But.

                                                      My simple question is: "How does this make the action any different from any unawakened big investor?"
                                                      1) Toss enough money into existing supply chain
                                                      2) Get result.
                                                      Or, put differently, it would not matter if the money comes from the Syndicate member or some unwakened money spender, the already established (possibly Hypertech educated) supply chain would be able to create the same items from the investment.
                                                      So where does the Syndicate's member's actual Matter/Prime sphere expertise come in? If it's about an awakened sense of investment, it sounds to me more like a Mind 1 effect to boost your Wits/Intelligence and then making an excellent choice.

                                                      All actual requirements for Matter/Prime spheres would be more technical in my mind - establishing the supply chain itself, for example.

                                                      EDIT.
                                                      You could of course say that the money as a focus gets used by the Syndicate member to produce a Matter/Prime effect that subtly influences the workings of the supply line to produce amazing results - probably even unbeknownst to the Syndicate member themself when it comes to the details - but this would then simply come from the Syndicate member's basic paradigm of 'Invest value to get good value'. Of course you could say that the spheres on the sheet get increased values from the suit's growing experience in investments..
