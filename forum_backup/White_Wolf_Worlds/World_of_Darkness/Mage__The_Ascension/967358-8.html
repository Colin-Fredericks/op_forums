            Well, I think that it is more that Mages can make reality bend to their will up to the limit allowed by their paradigm. Technocratic Archmages can build devices that levitate entire aircraft carriers, but they cannot simply levitate grains of sand by pointing their fingers because it is against their paradigm. As for spontaneous human combustion, the Consensus does not accept it because there have been no scientifically verified examples of spontaneous human combustion (it is, as yet, simply urban myth). The reports of humans achieving temperatures of 2000 C are absurd and, quite frankly unnecessary, as human bodies will comfortably burn at 250 C (if spontaneous human combustion was real, the incidences would have probably just resulted in an oily fire, rather than the clean fire that is claimed, as humans do not burn clean unless they have been mummified).

                                                Strange, I would think that a computer scientist would know the difference between entanglement and tunneling. I will explain though why your example did not work. Entanglement is a form of quantum communication between two related particles so, if a scientific paradigm was used, it would allow for instantaneous or interdimentional communication (not travel). Tunneling is a form of quantum teleportation that occurs when a particle encounters a barrier with a thickness less than its wavelength so, if a scientific paradigm was used, it would be used to justify instantaneous or interdimentional travel.
