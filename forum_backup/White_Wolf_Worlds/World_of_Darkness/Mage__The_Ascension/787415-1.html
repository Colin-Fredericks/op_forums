    A couple of funny ones (I know this thread's going on old in forum time, but heh, who isn't up for a laugh?).

    The Titty Twister:

    Contemporary game with 3 PCs: Magalie and Richard (Euthanatos) and Ewan (VA). Magalie has a Flaw causing her to relive the worst moments of her past lives, such as being burnt at the stake as a witch. Undergoing such a “regression”, she botches her WP roll and ends up going into cardiac arrest because her mind is so convinced she's really dying.

    The other two, having no dots in Medicine whatsoever, try to figure out what to do. Since we had just come back from busting a Chimera out of small a Progenitor construct, and were also heavy on Paradox and bordering on constant backlash, we had to lay low. Characters go into a panic...

    … And Richard decides to wake up Magalie by twisting her nipples. (Epic fail, btw, she was still dying.)

    Meanwhile, Ewan recalls his House M.D. Meets Pulp Fiction reruns, says “hey, Magalie stole stuff from the hospital, I know she has a bottle of adrenalin or shite somewhere, let's shoot her with that”, and... well, let's just say the Dexterity roll, for want of anything else, was a very good one, and Magalie was lucky. Also because the next step would've been to shock her using the toaster.

    IRL, there was much tea spillage, as well as Magalie's player literally rolling over the couch and falling to the floor out of laughing.

    Everything's Better In A Kilt:

    My alternate history/Victorian setting. The PCs had spent the night helping defend Oxford from a surprise attack, and were now facing the actual villain of that scenario, a Nephandus who had taken two VIPs hostage. Exhausted from their fights, low on WP (and thus on Arete dice to roll as well), the PCs were failing their rolls and the Nephandus was winning. All they needed, though, was a few more seconds, because reinforcements were on their way...

    That's when the Orphan's player said: “My character's still wearing the kilt he wore to the party, before we were attacked, right? So I stand proud, look at the Nephandus, shout 'Behold the most secret weapon of Mighty Scotland!', and... I lift my kilt to show him my dick. Maybe this will distract him.”

    First and only time I decided my NPC botched his WP roll without my even rolling the dice: I was laughing too much. Actually, everybody was laughing so much we decided to resume the game another day. Sometimes you can't even...

                        A magic armor was stolen from a small family home in the suburbs of Stockholm. The owner a female mage. Players do have a name, but didn't ask about her faction, nor age or anything else. She is an old lady and a Faithful.

                        A NWO agent was tasked of taking down the thief, who is said to be a Reality terrorist, having hit several outposts of TU and Traditions alike.
                        A Solificatus is tasked of retrieving the stolen good and hires two Euthanatos.

                        After the Alchemist explained the situation, the mystics set out for the house. The resident Euthanatos goes ahead on his motor bike while the Alchemist and the Euthanatos follow by taxi. The NWO is indepently in place staking out the house.

                        Euthanatos 1 arrives at the door. A young woman opens.
                        "I'm coming for the assignment."
                        - "Assignment...? Oh, yes. The bathroom is over there and the main tap is on the right hand side. I'll be in the kitchen."

                        The player takes some time before he figures out that this probably is not the person. His first reaction is in fact going to the bathroom. Meanwhile the agents stakeout drone gets eaten by the ladies cat, as soon as it enters the house proper. The player feels droneblocked and, having heard the discussion about assignments and plumbing, puts on a boilersuit and goes to the house, pretending to be the real plumber.

                        The young woman welcomes him pointing him to the bathroom where his "colleague" is already at work. The agent goes on to confront the Euthanatos.

                        Meanwhile the other two characters arrive and actually ask the womans name. She confirms that the characters are looking for her grandmother who is currently in hospital after a stroke. The player in return try to pose as officers of the law, investigating a theft. Of course, the granddoughter doesn't know anything about theft of arcane items, and the players fail their Subterfuge check big time.

                        The granddoughter procedes upstairs to call the police, asking the characters to wait.That at least the players realize and all PCs leave the house, accusing each other of their many shortcomings.


                        Ingredients for total chaos at zero preparation:
                        - One mundane person.
                        - One familiar.

                            A few others I remembered:


                            The Sauna Incident

                            Mage 2009 setting. My VA Ewan has a SoE friend, Bee, who's currently living and hosting her huge machine B.A.B.Y. in a warehouse/lab nicknamed “Warehouse 13”. We've been using it as a HQ cause it's big, and at some point Magalie (the Euthanatos PC) had a young Nocker named Michael, stay there with his Chimera.

                            Magalie unfortunately stumbled upon the corpse of another Changeling, one of Michael's friends, his Fae mien dead, and his human body dumped in the canal. Since the canal's not far from WH13, she, Michael and the Chimera brought the corpse back there. They didn't dare tell Michael his friend was dead, and Magalie managed to convince the kid that “your friend is very tired and needs to sleep."

                            Several hours later, as Ewan went to WH 13...

                            Ewan: “Is it me, or is stinking in your office?”
                            Bee: “Dunno, been working in B.A.B.Y. Go check if you want.”

                            So Ewan goes to check.
                            Indeed it stinks like mad in the office.
                            Whose other door opens on the new sauna Bee had had installed a few days before those events.
                            And the smell's coming from there...

                            Michael, thinking his friend must be cold, had put the corpse in the sauna to keep him warm, and turned the heat full on.
                            I don't think we've used that sauna since.


                            Allergies

                            My alternate/Victorian/Silent Hillesque setting again. Abigail (Verbena) and Aidan the vampire NPC met a soldier from a mirror reality, at the time when everybody was lost in a Silent Hill-esque/nightmarish version of London. (Also don't ask me about the Verbena and the vampire. All my Verbena PCs seem to be attracted to that guy. Whatever.) Ensues this dialogue:

                            Soldier: “If you're going back out, be careful. Here, take this weapon.” *passes strange-looking rifle to Aidan*
                            Aidan, interested: “Hm, I've never seen such a rifle. What does it do?”
                            Soldier: “Why, it's a flamethrower, press this trigger and a huge flame—”
                            Vampire. “...SORRY CAN'T AM ALLERGIC TO FIRE -meep-.” *throws rifle to Verbena in a reflex*
                            Abigail (traumatised by fire in her childhood): “...Meep?”

                            Instant Frenzy. Just add water.

                            Some time later, the same Verbena and vampire met with the other PCs. Ensued this as well:
                            “Ah, Abigail, Aidan, you're here, good. We have something to show you. Come in.”
                            *PC leads both to a room where two NPCs had killed each other*
                            *vampire steps into puddle of blood*
                            *vampire looks down* “...Aw, dammit.”

                            Guess who then proceeded to roll Frenzy, with the players throwing a collective prayer to my dice. I don't think you need to have Lore: Vampire or anything to realise that showing that guy a puddle of fresh blood was a good idea, but maybe that's just me.

                            Loser McPhail Botcherton:

                            Usually, I botch about 30% of Ewan's dice rolls, including getting into the Digital Web (just getting in there, not even doing anything). But the other PCs weren't bad either as serial botchers on that day.
                            City sewers were infested with giant ants who tended to eat homeless people. We decided to capture one and study it. The local Chantry's Primus even nicely lent us a cell to hold it captive. So there we go: Ewan the VA, Magalie (Euthanatos), Leolio (SoE), Valerian (OoH NPC) and Daniel (VA NPC).
                            This ended up in:
                            - Ewan botching as usual and accidentally 'porting one of those enraged ants somewhere in the Thames (we were 1500 km from London). Followed by this reaction: “Nah, no worries guys, no need to bother about it, nothing can survive the Thames.”
                            - Ewan also botching a Matter roll to reinforce the cell's door, after a successfully 'ported ant started to ram it.
                            - Magalie wanted to put the ant to sleep, so she threw a bottle of chloroform at it. Didn't do anything to the ant. All the characters around, though...
                            - Daniel botching his Correspondence roll as well to send the ant back. And proceeding to scream like a little girl: “Aaaah Ewan Ewan I can't sendit back I can't send it baaaaaack!” *flails and runs around in a panick*
                            - Domino effect kicks in. It's raining men, I mean, Paradox.

                            When we finally managed to set things back to normal, we met the Primus in the corridor:
                            "So, young people, how did it go?"
                            “Yeah, uh, you know, your reinforced cell... not so sturdy... just saying... we'll be on our way now... also don't look for us, like, ever.”

                                The Potted Flaw

                                Same 2009 setting. As usual, I botched some dice roll with Ewan, who went into Backlash and ended up with a Flaw for an hour or so. That was basically "everything you touch with your right hand is 'ported in the last place where you colocated an item or yourself."

                                For the record, the last place was a warehouse at night, in which we needed to teleport a friendly NPC who had gone missing; We had found her in the Umbra, but due to certain circumstances, suspected she might be under Mind control from an enemy. So we had also planned a cage, just in case. For some reason (dice again, probably) Ewan didn't manage to 'port her in the cage directly, and also the enemy had plotted and schemed so that he could hitch a ride on our op. Long story short, the enemy also being Ewan's ex, Ewan kind of lost it and added a little Correspondence effect to gun him down. The guy never knew what hit him. Everyone around him was coverd in blood and gore.

                                12 hours later... The workers had come back to the warehouse, found a mess there, including some old rusty cage à la Silent Hill and a large puddle of blood and guts splattered on and in front of it. Ensued a call to the police, then the arrival of a team of MIBs to investigate...

                                That's when the Paradox Flaw kicked in. It was light at first: a bottle of ketchup, a jar of mustard... Ewan realised soon enough that he hadn't just misplaced the items, though, oh no.

                                That's when his CURIOSITY flaw kicked in: "Hey those are small items... I wonder where they're going? But mostly I wonder if it works WITH SOMETHING BIGGER." Like the huge green leafed potted plant that had mutated in the living-room after a Paradox backlash from an NPC. (Tbh I was a bit of troll here, as I decided "no I'm not even rolling Wits, I just do it.")

                                Of course, it worked.

                                Of course, right after that, the SoE character managed to determine where all that crap was going: the warehouse teeming with MIBs.

                                We scrammed the hell out of the place, knowing that the MIBs may not investigate a bottle of ketchup (that may or may not have fallen out of a worker's lunch bag, after all), but would undoubtedly investigate a 4-meter tall Moses-in-the-Cradle appearing out of thin air.

                                Stupid decision, but a good laugh. Also because we had "Yakety Sax" in mind at that moment as the characters were running out of the house in a panic.

                                    A Night in the Park

                                    From my very first Mage Chronicle, back in 1998.

                                    The PCs had raised too much havoc, and as a result, the Chantry's Primus had sent them and a NPC on a 4 am Tass chore, from an oak in a nearby park that only wielded Tass for a couple of days a year (hence the stealth op in the dead of night, because everybody and their dog would soon try to lay hands on it as well).

                                    While in the park, the characters stumbled upon a fight scene, during which a NPC got stabbed to death. They managed to send the murderer on the run, but the other NPC died in their arms, leaving them with the Mandatory Mcguffin. So we have those 4 mages, in a park in the heart of London, knowing that other mages and potentially Technocrats would soon come sniffing around... and here they are, with a fresh corpse. Guess what happened?

                                    First they tried to dump the corpse in the Thames. They never managed to take it out of the park, though, due to several failed Athletics rolls to carry it above the metal railing. One of the PCs almost lost his balls botching.

                                    Then another PC decided to use Matter to fuse the corps in a patch of earth. Botch.

                                    Finally, your truly used her NPC to suggest "hey, guys, you do realise we were here to gather Tass from fallen leaves, and that we have trash bags and a couple of shovels, right?"

                                    The corpse ended up buried in the park. Still, the whole scene was hilarious.

                                    Vampire, Flat Tyre

                                    Same Chronicle. The VA and the Euthanatos PCs had suspicions about a vampire NPC and decided to shadow him, thinking he was involved in a series of murders. Turned out he was, but as a potential target, and at some point, when he went out in his car, a common enemy tried to kill him. The PCs, not knowing what was happening, followed them. Ensued car chase scene, during which the PCs caused the enemy car to end up in the Thames. However, the Euthanatos, still wanting to know what exactly the vampire was up to, shot one of his tyres flat to force him to stop. The vampire came out, ready to fight...

                                    That's when the VA suggested that "you know, be careful: he may be old... and powerful."

                                    That one word did it. The Euthanatos sobered up, and came out of his car with a heartfelt "Ah, Mr Vampire, so sorry about your tyre, that was a mistake, ha ha ha, let me help you with that."

                                    The two mages actually did replace the tyre, under the watchful eye of one very puzzled vampire who had no idea what to do with those clowns.
