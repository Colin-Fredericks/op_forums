    Syndicate and various supernaturals

    10-14-2016, 10:07 AM
    In spite of the Void Engineer’s reports, there are no such things as ghosts, faeries, demons, etc. They serve as entertaining (and profitable) fiction, nothing more. Now,
    in the laughable event that credible proof comes to light, that would be another matter. Some Financiers have written various marketing plans just in case, usually as a test by a higher-up to see what they can do when given completely ridiculous parameters.
    Does that quote mean the Syndicate don't know and can't see ghosts, faeries, demons, etc or whilst they can see them and know something ghosts, faeries, demons, etc. like exist, but deem them as scientifically-comprehensible creatures, not some supernatural, mystical, unknowable phenomenon?

    Because, well, they are obviously able to see VE-provided "evidences" and have to deal with ghosts and faeries on occasions - certainly, if need arises.

      Outside of the Main Supernatural Splats, i.E. Mages, Shifters and Vampires, the Union actually has only miniscule knowledge and incidents with the other supernaturals.

      The exception is the Void Engineers pretty much, be it Necronauts, their 'Ghostbusters' division or those out and about in the Umbra.
      The other Conventions are rather sceptic, and/or simply don't have those splats on their radar.
      The Convention that would be in second place are the Progenitors. Their Damage Control division might encounter those strange creatures on their missions, but you won't find them labeled as 'Fairies' or 'Demons' in their documents.

      EDIT:
      That said, I presume the Syndicate, going by the paste, knows that there are weird fairy-like and demon-like creatures out there, but simply refuses to literally label and acknowledge them as such - yet.

                  If I remember correctly, Iteration X began to look into extra-dimensional beings in their Revised Convention book, following the loss of the Computer and the lift of the ban on learning Dimensional Science. Granted, this was years before the rest of the Revised Convension books were developed, so how much this fits with other material is debatable. If it's true, though, then that means Iteration X is aware of things like Post Life Entities, in some capacity.

                  Then there's the Book of Madness, which states that, despite the Union's general dismissal of superstitionist claims, there are plenty of Technocrats that know of the existence of "demons" and the like. Mostly from personal experience. These individuals are usually assigned afterwards to continue dealing with the same unexplained phenomenon, both because they are the most experienced in fighting them, and as a means of isolating that information from the rest of the Union. Naturally, this leads to an X-Files situation, where Technocrats are stuck in departments and cases that make their colleagues not take them seriously.

                  Still, when there are events that smack of "extra-dimensional corruption" (read: possession and infernalism), the Union has people to deal with it, one way or another. While the anti-superstitionist party line is all well and good, these beings whose natures are not yet understood by Science pose a tangible threat to the Technocracy.

Same thing for vampires, changelings, wraiths, etc. They don't exist because they can't exist.

                                                          Mmmmh, I think the Technocracy Party Line actually works fine, if you think about it - the fallacy one needs to avoid is, that the Technocracy isn't actually going 'All those things out there do not exist! La La la!'

                                                          If you think they are saying *that*, *literally*, then yes..their partyline makes no sense at all, because it would take a Technocratic field agent a week or two before they need to be shoveled back to Room 101.

                                                          But That's not what they are actually doing or saying.

                                                          What the Technocracy is saying, is 'All this strange stuff out there exists, yes. But it is not what all those supersticious people think it is. It is all clearly concepts that work within our Hyperscientific paradigm, It is all explainable. Stop it with the suspersticious "Fairy" stuff and mumbo-jumbo. We can explain this.'

                                                          And this of course includes everything the Technocrats themselves manage to do. You do not need to believe that what you do is Magick, just because what you do is eerily similiar to what the people out there calling themselves 'Mages' do - it's exactly the other way around. "Those other groups are clearly using principles of Hyperscience, psionics, field pyrokinetics etc. without realizing it, and they just won't accept that what they are dressing it as is absolute nonsense sillyness."

                                                          And of *course* the Technocrats would believe that. They know what they can do. They know it's Enlightened Science and Hypertech. And when those weird guys out there are doing similiar things in a chaotic fashion, of *course* they must be using the same principles. They're just doing it in a delusional, wrong, hazardous and uncontrolled manner.

                                                          So yes, their thoughts of 'We are not doing Magic, and neither are they.' actually work within their worldview. And in a way, it's just as valid - it is simply the flipside view of 'We are doing Magick. And so are they!' of the non-Technocrats. And each side wants to smack the other around with 'Don't you see what you are doing? Wake up!'

                                                          The difference between the Technocracy and the other Mages is, that while other Mages accept other paradigms with polite 'live and let live' ( while probably at first explaining things in their own paradigm in their mind ), that the Technocracy loudly, officially and strictly denies all non-Technocracy paradigms among their members. Everything out there is explainable through Technocratic Hyperscience and Tech. If the Technocracy can't explain it yet, they must do more tests and theories. And that's it. It's all covered.


                                                          In short:

                                                          It's not the belief in the existance of things out there that is against the partyline, it's believing that those things aren't explainable through Technocratic paradigm that causes the NWO to pay you a visit. If you start trying to explain those things outside the Technocratic paradigm, you are starting to lose faith in said paradigm's all-encompassing validity. And that's the line of thought they are trying to suppress. The Technocracy stands and falls with their members' faith (heh) in Hypertech and Enlightened Science.
