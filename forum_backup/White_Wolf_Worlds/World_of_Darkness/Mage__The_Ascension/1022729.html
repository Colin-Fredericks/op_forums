    messianic voices intolerance makes no sense.

    12-27-2016, 10:02 AM
    at least in my opinion.
    from dark ages mage core rule book: these guys believe in universal reconciliation
    in lay terms this means that(eventually) everyone gets saved.

    but here is the issue. historically the justification for christian intolerance(and torture and executions etc) were the following two things.
    1)jesus said"love god and love others as yourself"
    2)jesus said "i am the way and the truth no one comes to the father etc" or "whoever denies me i will also deny before the father" or "but he who does not believe is already lost for he has denied the son"

    these are generally interpreted to mean that non christians(and heretics or at least serious ones) go to hell. even if said hell is a limbo/paradise of the unsaved"
    so if we accept the above premises it makes perfect sense. to try and destroy other religions by any and all means. because you genuinely wish to save these people. even if a "few" have to be "lost" to make an example to the rest.
    even in versions of christianity(such as eastern orthodoxy i believe) where non christians can be saved this logic can still be applied to an extend. after all the new testament's morality is still the best way to avoid hell.

    but if premise number 2) is entirely wrong...then what is the point of all that violence?

    unless you are a gabrielite who believes premise number 2) is correct
