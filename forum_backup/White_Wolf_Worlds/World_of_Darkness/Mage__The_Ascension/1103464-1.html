            Going back and skimming that section over, there do seem to be at least some rules variations for the techno-Wonders, not all mechanical. The differences are minor, and perhaps in a vacuum things would be better if the differences were more standardized (like dropping Instruments), but between M20 still being a legacy product, and feeling the diversity of magical items as being beneficial complexity more than not for players to buy/make the Wonders that most suit what they're trying to do, I think it's fine.

                                                    From the FAQ:
                                                    Why Didn’t You Detail the “Camps” – That is, Groups Within Each Tradition, Technocratic Methodologies, and So On?
                                                    I tried. But between the two-page spread limit, the sheer number of those smaller groups (the Order of Hermes alone has 13 Houses and a number of smaller fellowships!), and the need for at least 200-300 words per group in order to describe a “camp” with any degree of detail, there was just no way to fit that material into Mage 20… or, for that matter, into the book in your hands right now, which was originally supposed
                                                    to contain that material until I added up the words and realized it wasn’t going to fit.

                                                    As of this writing, the plan is to put that material into a book dedicated specifically to the “camps.” That project has not yet been approved or scheduled, however, so the best response I can give right now is “We plan to get it out there eventually.”

                                                    My advice: give up on trying to fit it all into a single book. Give us the Nephandic factions in Book of the Fallen, the Technocratic factions in Technocracy Reloaded, the Traditional factions in a Traditions-centered counterpart, the Disparate factions in a Disparates-oriented book, and the Marauder “factions” (such as they are) in an M20 Book of Madness.
