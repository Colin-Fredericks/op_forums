    Forces 3

    ••• Transmute Minor Forces/ Telekinetics/The Dragon’s Touch

    Now the mage can alter and invoke the energies she could only touch before. By adding Prime 2, she may conjure winds, fire, electricity, gravitational wells, and so forth. Combining this Rank with Life or Matter, she can transform people into electricity (Life 5/ Forces 3); change water into air (Forces 3/Matter 3), or attach elemental force to material forms – making them fly, pinning them to the ground, and having other, similar effects (generally with Life or Matter 3). And by adding an ephemeral Sphere to this Rank, she might erode or enhance energies (Entropy 3); manifest thoughts into energies (Mind 3); transmute spirit-stuff into physical energies (Spirit 3); or set triggers for energy fluctuations at some later interval (Time 4).
    At this Rank, the scale of effect grows larger, now encompassing several dozen yards or a handful of characters.


    Now this is a tricky one. Again, there’s only one real power here - transmuting forces - and I just realized it’s not directly stated in the text, just the initial ability names. Apparently ‘alter and invoke the energies she could only touch before’ was supposed to mean that.

    Aside from that line everything else is a combination with other spheres. That’s a problem because the suggestions range accurate, to inaccurate, to indecipherable. In particular, Forces 3 blurs the lines between the capabilities of Forces 2.

    The upshot of all this is that I’ll spend most of this section on dealing with contradictions and ambiguity. On that note, let me start with the most important:

    Contradiction resolution - Forces 2 or Forces 3

    Forces 2 can explicitly mute or amplify sound and flare fires, and by implication can enhance or suppress most forces. On several occasions, though, Forces 3 acts like suppressing and amplifying energies is a rank 3 power. While we could say that Forces 2 can only enhance and suppress heat and sound, and is limited to just redirecting all the other energies, I don't think that’s reasonable.

    Instead, we can focus on the fact that Forces 3, in addition to its new abilities, can do everything Forces 2 can but over a larger area. Thus, it is technically true that it can cause ‘energy fluctuations’ though it is generally more appropriate to say that is a Forces 2 ability, unless it exceeds the area limit of Forces 2. So:

    Everything Forces 2 Can Do but Up to a Few Dozen Yards in Area(???)

    That covers the times that Forces 3 is given Forces 2 powers without needlessly constraining Forces 2.



    Now on to the new ability:

    Transmute Minor Forces (Transmute Minor Forces)

    With this power a mage can turn one force into another, or with the help of other spheres, turn a force into something else, turn something else into a force, or conjure up a force from nowhere. Now this specifies minor forces, but that includes most forces we experience on a day to day basis.


    Blocking Their Communications with Their Own Bullets

    A mage can convert kinetic energy (say, of a Technocrat’s bullets) into radio waves (say, the same frequencies they communicate on), thus reducing the damage of the bullets and disrupting communications. While this is a particular case, there are many times a mage may want less of one type of energy and more of another.



    Forces 3 also lists multiple conjunctional effects for this power. I’m going to tackle them in order, except for one: Manifest thoughts into energies.


    Manifest Thoughts into Energies (Forces 3/Mind 3)

    The last time I took a trip through the Spheres, I missed the importance of this one. I correctly interpreted it as transmuting a thought or idea into energy, but I missed what that accomplishes. This is an alternative to Prime 2 for creating Forces from apparently nothing.

    I’m so used to seeing Mind forced into psychic spells it doesn’t belong in that it is honestly refreshing to find it actually having a use.

    Change water into air (Forces 3/Matter 3)

    Contradiction Resolution - Water to Air

    It is allegedly Forces 3/Matter 3 to change water into air. Water and air are both forms of matter, so Forces is not involved. However, if we take this instead to mean water to wind, which has a kinetic component, Forces 3/Matter 3 makes sense.


    Attach Elemental Force to Material Forms (Forces 3/Matter 3)

    Contradiction Resolution - Attach Elemental Force

    or attach elemental force to material forms – making them fly, pinning them to the ground, and having other, similar effects (generally with Life or Matter 3).

    From before, manipulation of forces is rank 2 unless the area gets to big.

    This one is tricky, because the examples are of things that can be done with Forces 2. Indeed it looks exactly like the kind of effect that would be accomplished with locking, but with inflated spheres. The best explanation I have is that this is supposed to be a grafting effect. That is to say, the mage is creating a combination Forces/Life or Forces/Matter pattern. That would reasonably require Forces and the appropriate Pattern Sphere at transmutation levels. That would at least justify Forces 3/Life 3 for grafting a force into a frog, though it should be Forces 3/Life 5 for complex life, Forces 3/Matter 2 for simple objects, and Forces 3/Matter 4 for complex ones.

    If it is a locked effect, though, the sphere requirements are suspiciously high. Matter 3 and Life 3 aren’t contradicting the locking rules per se, as those never actually say what rank is required, and they don’t contradict Matter or Life, because those don’t cover locking ranks either. They do contradict the Common Magical Effects chart, though, which puts Levitation and Flight at Forces 2+/Life 2 or Forces 2+/Matter 2.

    That’s the best I’ve found so far, anyways. Suggestions?


    Erode or Enhance Energies (Forces 3/Entropy 3)

    Contradiction resolution - Enhance or Erode Forces

    From before, manipulation of forces is rank 2, unless the area is too large.

    A lot depends on what this means. Forces 2 can enhance or suppress energies with no need for additional spheres. Possibly, though, this is meant to replicate Entropy 3’s ability to increase or decrease the rate something decays at, such as making a fire have a problem burning and go out faster, or making it burn more cleanly and efficiently so that it lasts longer, or making it more likely that the fuel source will collapse in a way that quickly makes it sputter out.

    Now, this is likely within the capabilities of Entropy 3 alone (see previous discussions in this thread). It also might be within the capacities of Forces 2 anyways. If neither, though, then maybe this is a valid way of accomplishing the effect?


    Set Time Triggers for Energy Fluctuations(Forces 3/Time 4)

    Ambiguity resolution - Time Triggers for Energy Fluctuations

    From before, manipulation of forces is rank 2, unless the area is too large.

    There are two things this could mean. The first is energy effects on a time trigger. This would be things like flickering the lights at a certain time (Time 4/Forces 2) or having a car discharge it’s kinetic energy as a lightning bolt at a certain time (Time 4/Forces 3).

    It is also possible that this is a trigger for some other event and the trigger event is the energy fluctuations. Here we have a problem, as there aren’t clear instructions for how to set the required spheres for a trigger. That means it is up to the ST. I default to one rank higher than required for the mage himself to sense the trigger, so Forces 2. On the other hand, we could take the punishing requirement of ‘setting the trigger takes the same spheres as making the trigger happen,’ though that would still be Forces 2. Only if we go with the most punishing ‘triggers cost whatever sphere governs the effect at transmutation levels’ do we get to Forces 3.

                                            Forces 4

                                            •••• Control Major Forces/ Gift of Zeus/Weather-Witching

                                            Larger Effects become possible. Our mage may now use the lower-Rank Effects over larger areas – a mile or more – to change weather patterns; conjure darkness; drop or raise temperatures; protect locations… or destroy them; and otherwise employ the previous levels on a much greater scale. For obvious reasons, such Effects tend to be vulgar and demand many successes to achieve.
                                            At this point Forces gets a little thin. Many of the high-level Forces effects are just low-level Forces effects but bigger. All of them, in fact. Previous editions would lock certain ‘major’ forces, like radiation and nuclear blasts, behind Forces 4. There are indications this is still meant to be the case, but none in the actual sphere writeups.

                                            Some STs will also put more complex workings into Forces 4, but on a direct read there is no indication of that here. Some also limit Forces 2 to single forces while effects that handle multiple forces are in 4, kind of like Matter’s progression. Again, it’s not in the text here or there.

                                            The main features of Forces 4 are:


                                            Weather Control (Weather Witching)

                                            One specific ability that really needed Forces 4 scales to accomplish is weather manipulation. Few pieces of weather are easily restricted to a handful of yards. Note that there is still a range limit here, so the really large storms have to wait until Forces 5, but there are still many weather effects that are possible on the Forces 4 scale. There are also a handful that are arguably possible at earlier ranks.


                                            Everything Forces 2 and 3 Can Do But Up to a Mile or More(???)

                                            This is a common grammatical pitfall. On a technical level, there’s no upper bound here; a thousand miles is ‘a mile or more.’ Here, though, I think it’s meant to be a representative area, so we should take this to mean that Forces 4 can act over areas miles in radius, not hundreds or thousands of miles. Still, technically there is no actual limit, so STs should be prepared in case that argument is raised.

                                            Also note that this specifically scales Forces 2 and 3 up to this range, so even though Forces 4 is not, itself, a transmutation rank, it scales up Forces 3, which is.


                                            Graft Kaiju

                                            If a mage wanted to make a city smashing monster with giant wings of fire, this would be the level of Forces necessary to graft energy wings of that size to a living pattern. Life 3 or 5 would also be necessary depending on type of kaiju.




                                            Missing:

                                            Control Major Forces (Control Major Forces)

                                            Most forces that we interact with regularly are accessed with Forces 2, but there are a few that are so exotic or powerful that mages can only access them at rank 4. What these are, offhand, I don’t know as M20 doesn’t list any.
                                            However, if a Master of Forces created a brand new force with Forces 5, Forces 4 should be capable of manipulating it, in theory.

                                                Forces 5

                                                ••••• Transmute Major Forces/Conjuring Infernos


                                                With godlike power, a Forces Master works her Will upon vast areas. She can conjure tornados on a clear day, calm tides, and ignite the very air into a firestorm. Such mages earn the title “Nuke,” though few of them survive long enough to enjoy it. It’s easier to invoke such forces under the right conditions, of course – a cold snap comes more easily in winter than in summer - and such radical alterations have repercussions that often outlast the original Effect. In game terms, massive Forces spells should disrupt the weather and leave massive Resonance echoes behind.


                                                Once again, the purpose is muddled as all the examples are possible at Forces 4, Forces 5 is just a much larger area.

                                                Everything Forces 2, 3, and 4 Can Do but With No Limit (???)

                                                ‘Vast areas’ opens up the area to pretty much anything, limited only by successes. This could be interpreted to include some truly obscene distances. Consider the phrase ‘the vastness of space,’ for instance. Since there is no sphere above this unless optional archmastery spheres are used, this time the lack of limit makes sense.


                                                Transmute Major Forces (Transmute Major Forces)

                                                Convert one force into another or, with other spheres, into other patterns. This time there are no restrictions on size and if there are any exotic forces beyond the reach of Forces 2 or 3, they are available here. That’s a big if, though, because there’s still no indication of any such force.


                                                Missing

                                                When we get to rank 5 there shouldn’t be much off limits.


                                                Create Exotic Forces (???)

                                                At Forces 5, mages should be able to generate Forces that don’t actually exist, like gravity that only attracts chickens, sounds that only interact with the ears of small mammals, etc.


                                                Everything Else (???)

                                                As with all Rank 5s, Forces 5 should be able to do just about everything possible within the Sphere’s purview. The existence of Archspheres could change this, but they aren’t listed in M20.
