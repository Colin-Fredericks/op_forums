    Karmic Decoy [Life 4, Entropy 3] This rote would be used by a conscientious Life Mage who has extended their lifespan and needs a new identity. The Mage and a willing target swap appearances and the Mage takes on the liabilities of the other. Example, the target is wanted for a murder they committed. The Mage turns themselves in as the target and stands trial, with the advantage of not sharing the fingerprints or genetic markers of the real person. They must work to atone for the crime in some other way, but thereafter have the use of the target's identity. Spell can be cast as a ritual to get enough successes to make the change permanent.

            Perceive Lies of the Past [Entropy 1, Time 2] - Now, a person might be honest about a topic in a particular situation, to a specific person...but what is the guarantee that she or he has not lied or misled others in the past? When there are such doubts, this Rote performs two functions - first, it checks whether a person statement is truthful. Second, it reaches out into the past and gives the Mage a cursory but intense glimpse (filtered through the Mage's Paradigm and Foci) of whether the person was honest about this topic in the past. The casting Mage can control where and how far into the past she reaches, and choose which conversations she wants to divine with Entropy 1.
            The Rote tends to provide very scarce information about the past situations - it is focused on checking the degree of honesty in regard to a topic.

                Some Nephandi rotes from an old antagonistic nephandi cabal i ran. Naturally they're all bloody awful to descretion is advised.heavy usage of mind.

                Summon 'Satan' Spirit 2 correspondance 2
                A nasty little rote planted in the Hollow one community by a Fallen member, the spell is a trap designed to ensare the naive and unseasoned. Theorectically the spell is designed to summon the devil in a context were he/it is unable to harm you. In practice it turns the user into a flare for Spectre's, Banes, Demonic entities and whatever else is nearby. What happens next is up to the GM and the relative power of the poor sap who thought she could call the Devil and control it. The spell itself is simple enough drawing an inverted pentagram in blood (any blood will suffice) place the candles around the points in a holy place at midnight and simply call for satan in a suitable dramatic way. The 'ward' is a red ribbon wrapped around your waist.....it does not work.

                Reverse gaslight Quithlopic mind 4/optional entropy 2/Correspondance 2
                An insidious Nephandic spell. The Nephandi supresses a trauma memory in the victim, preferably one which has been resolved and then over a period of time the memory of the event slowly re-emerges. The intent is to undo the years of growth and healing by dragging the victim back to initial trauma of the event. It also damages the victims perception of reality by briefly creating a false more pleasant perception against reality. The ritual requires acquiring a mirror from the victims house smashing it, repairing it and smashing it again. Preferably with some sort of additional sympathetic link included. With entropy you can build in a contingency to trigger the break down and correspondence allows it to be done from a distance.

                "somethings in here with us." Quithlopic Mind 2 forces 2.
                A simple defensive ward designed to scare of sleepers or simply put people on edge. The spell simply amplifies bangs, creaks and groans in an area and combined them with a mind effect to ellavate fear. All that's required is a simply glympth drawn somewhere in area you wish to cast it in.

                Such sights to show you Quithlopic Correspondance 2 Spirit 2, mind 1
                This spells opens up a direct line of telepaphic link with the Nephandis patrons and a target, communication is not the aim here. The ultimate objective is massive psychic assault. The caster must had either one eye missing of a third eye hole carved somewhere into their flesh. Damage is bashing but derangements and extreme spiritual corruption are common side effects. Victims must role Diff 6 stamina+Arete test not to black out.

                "The Caul for all" Mind 5, Quithlopic optional spirit 3 and life 4
                Somewhat frustrated by slow rate of sleeper corruption, Nephandic magus Steven betruger built a psuado caul to hot box sleeper moral degeneration. The structure/foci itself can vary a great deal in terms of design-sometimes it's a torture box box or an back alley operating theatre or even a room fully of acolytes catering to you every desire or anything the Nephandi can think of to encourage you to be the best you can be. but the ultimate objective is to warp sleepers into more twisted individuals. Typically rather than re-writting the personality the structure is designed to emphasize and unlock the darker aspects of a sleepers personality: hidden and shameful desires, illogical views, personal fears etc and incorperartes them into the psyche at the same time the stunting and minimising the more noble aspects of a sleeper. The person may not necessarily become a Nephandic acolyte (in fact the creator explicitly avoided recruiting from subjects-typically just releasing them back into the wild....a nice and subtle push towards descention) but they're certainly a lot more sympathetic to Nephandic world views afterward
                the Life allows for increasingly dramatic body alterations to better suit the new you, Vanity corrupted individuals or may be extremely beautiful, violence junkies may enjoy increased strength, predators may enjoy a lack of fingerprints and so on more drastic changes have been seen but often result in pattern bleeding.
                By adding spirit to effect a Bane may be attached to the host for a quick source of shock troops.

                Victims of the effect are genrally quite 'content' with the new them and the process is described as extremely pleasent. Naturally individuals of strong moral character may resist with varying degress of sucesss.

                I'll try and dig up the counter wrotes the Cabal came up with to fight these scumbags.

                    Swallow the Lies and Cough Up the Truth [Entropy 1, Mind 2 or 4] - This Rote checks a statement for truthfulness and honesty. If the statement turns out to be false, the affected person admits it to the Mage, and then is influenced to tell the truth. The number of successes indicates how extensive the admission is, and how substantial the honest statement will be. The Mind 4 variant allows the Mage to ask a question or two, and the targeted person is compelled to elaborate further in regard to the statement or topic.

                    If this Rote is used and the statement turns out to be honest, then the casting Mage suffers a strong but brief headache as the Mind Magick element fizzles out.

                                    City Stride [Correspondence 3] - This Rote makes the Mage able to walk much more quickly through a city, walking through many kilometers long streets as if they were just 100 or 200 meters long. With enough success, entire streets or areas can be skipped as the Mage is focused on the destination. This Rote only works if the Mage has a clear intention of traveling to a visited before area during the cast. For the Rote to be maintained, the Mage must keep going towards the goal without straying from the shortest path; though taking breaks from the travel is acceptable. This Rote only works while traveling on foot - taking a vehicle for transportation ends this Effect.

                                            Okay, here are copies of a couple of my priest's rotes.

                                            Rites of the Dead Religion. Rotes for Some Hermetical mages, rare Dream-speakers, some specific religion/spiritual Orphans or Demon's priests. Sisters of Hippolyta - for Zodiacal asylum & Call of Nergal-Kalba (If any of them follow Hellenism).
                                            For obvious reasons, technomages (and pure techno-shamans), Celestial Chorus, Taftâni & etc are unable to recreate or use these rites.


                                            «Call of Nergal-Kalba» (Spirit ●●●●, optional Entropy ● and / or Prime ●) — A rite created at the end of the 16th century by the cult of the Hellenistic priests, an old magister-hermeticist and his only disciple. These records are kept in the hidden and inconspicuous Chantry due to the risk of being accused of summoning demons by other magicians. It is an adaptation of the Babylonian rites, partly distorted by the Greek faith. The ritual is needed to attract the demons of Nergal and the self-possession of the magician; optionally, the magician can draw out his life-force to enhance the ritual and give it a connection with death (a feeling of decay / death during the time of possession).

                                            System: The magician summons a demon by the Babylonian-Hellenic rite. At the end of the ceremony, the magician falls under partial control (one of the forms of possession, like bane's fomori) by one of the demons close to the Babylonian faith and the world of the dead (that are able to haunt souls of the dead) or by spectres and very rarely - barghests (ghosts turned into a beasts).
                                            To successfully complete the ritual and summon a weak demon to one scene, the mage needs 5 successes. Additional successes (with the addition of the correct big ceremony) can be spent on extending possession time or to summon a specific/powerful spirit. If desired, the priest can sacrifice himself - they inflict wounds and bleeding on themselves. Using Entropy, the priest focuses on dying and bounding himself with death, beginning to feel decay/ill fate in others (in the form of feeling wounds, illnesses or other events). By adding Prime, the priest can sacrifice his life for the ritual, decreasing the difficulty of the ritual. The priest who uses both spheres reveals the ideal sense of death, seeing how decay affects the environment (selective vision of the aura, mainly focused on the extinction of life and the resonance of death). Depending on the demon, the magician falls under the influence of the spirit and its desires, the body of the magician occasionally receives minor temporary changes. There is a risk of losing control over oneself, then the spirit will guide the magician. Charms or arcanoi remain at the disposal of the spirit and only it can use them at any time during possession.

                                            Foci: Dagger or curved sword, sacred text, sacrifice


                                            «Zisurrû / Zodiacal Asylum» (Prime ●●●, Spirit ●●●●) — Two protective rites. The first ritual (Zisurrû) is different ritual of the same name that performs the same function. By infusing the power bestowed by the gods into the chosen place, the priest protects the place from demons. The second rite (Zodiacal Asylum) is a complex rite of protection recreated (and changed) from damaged texts by the Hellenistic cult that combined Greek and Sumerian beliefs. This ritual protects a room, a house, and even majestic buildings and fortresses. A small circle and statues of gods are enough to protect a small room. To protect a average temple or fortress, large statues 3 (or more) meters high may be required. The larger the area of protection, the more quintessence will be required and the more complex and detailed the circle needs to be recreated.

                                            System (Zisurrû): The priest draws a protective flour circle around the place of sleep and recites an ancient spell (difficulty - 8). This rite protects the place from demons, but every success spent to increase barrier level by 1 requires spending of quintessence; this rite can't protect more than one lunar month (29-30 days) and is not able to raise the barrier above 9 (however, the number of successes required to pass will increase). All sacrificed quintessence becomes a spiritual shield, which increases the difficulty of any attempts to destroy the barrier (works as an anti-magic; optional - separate successes are needed to form the shield).

                                            System (Zodiacal Asylum): Someone should draw (or better cut out in the material) a zodiacal circle with constellations divided into segments, and also divide the circle (possibly also the ceiling) into 3 «spheres» of the universe (Upper World - Heaven, Middle World - Earth, Underworld - Irkalla). Statues of gods (syncretic combinations of the Greek and Babylonian gods were often used) must be individually consecrated with the correct rituals (and imbued with their power - by using Prime ●●●, Matter ●●) or crafted from the correct materials (tass + craft roll; Prime ● to identify the correct material. Over time, such statues will "deplete" and lose their power).
                                            After completing the preparation, the priest (but more often the priests) perform a ceremony at each statue and donate a quintesense or tass to them (preferably with the correct resonance for each statue). The statues will create an aura that will increase the difficulty of any corrupting spiritual powers (as well as suppressing demonic diseases and powers), and even scares away some spirits. Maximum duration of protection for such a circle is also equal to lunar month, but priest doesn't need new statues (if the old ones remain intact). Statues made of tass have a chance of collapsing at the end of the lunar cycle if their quintessence was used in the ritual.
                                            The simplified version (mostly for one room protection) allows priest to use a statue of one god (although a full circle is still needed), but this protection only works during the time period of that zodiac sign.

                                            Foci: For Zisurrû — flour, sleeping place and god's blessing (quintessence); For Zodiacal Asylum — sacred statues of gods, a zodiac circle built in the correct position relative to the signs (the sign on the floor should be directed to its constellation in the sky, at the right time), a holy place or washed materials (tass), possibly several groups of workers.


                                            «Weapon of Destruction» (Prime ●●●, Spirit ●●) — Calling to Nergal seeing his manifestation in heaven (Ṣalbatānu — Mars), the priest-warrior can get his favor and protection. For this, the priest sacrifices an animal and inflicts a wound on himself or sprinkles with his blood the copper sacred weapons of the god (a curved sword, mace, wand, dagger). Priest's life go away, bringing him closer to the god, but also grant him the strength to fight the spirits (the weapons are able to attack the spirits). Each success is one turn during which priest can attack demons, but he need to spend at least 1 point of quintessence per turn. The ritual creates a flow of quintessence, along the contour of the blood on the weapon, in the spiritual plane.

                                            Foci: curved sword, mace, wand, dagger


                                            «Udug-ḫul» (Spirit ●●●●, optional Mind ● and / or Prime ●) — An ancient ritual for driving out evil spirits. To drive out the evil udug, the priest turns to the good udug (often asking deities for it before that). The ritual allows the priest to "attach" the spirit to the person, but in the end, the priest cannot force the good spirit and it is free to leave early if it wants to.

                                            System: Priest summons the good Udug (spirit / demon) who comes to drive the evil Udug out of the person. To summon the correct spirit, the priest needs a minimum of 2-5 successes. This number of successes varies depending on the patronage of the gods, knowledge of the name of desired spirit, and the power of the evil spirit. On rare occasions, the summoned spirit may be weak and unable to drive out the evil one.
                                            On the other hand, the priest can turn to the gods and ask them for such spirit; perhaps the gods will send spirit. In this case, the priest only needs to establish a connection with the deity, in any correct way (with the help of magic, messenger spirits, direct spiritual connection, etc.). The power of such spirit, as well as debts and consequences, are at the discretion of ST.
                                            Banishment itself differs from others old and modern exorcism in that a good spirit (not priest) drives out an evil spirit and takes its place; it does not take possession of a person, but will patronize him for some time (from a couple of minutes to days; but udug will never protect him more than the lunar cycle).
                                            The addition of Mind                                             ● allows mage to sense thoughts of possessed person & demon, and Prime ● allow to feel the spirit and its nature (reading the shade of spirit resonance).

                                            Foci: Spell text, kind Udug, connection to the Babylonian / Mesopotamian(?) gods

                                            «The Phenomenon of Place» (Spirit/Prime/Entropy ●) — for the perception / feeling of the spiritual resonance of the place, the priest must stop and create a circle (from a mixture of milk, sand, special herbs; the mixture must be made of materials that have no infernal corruption) covered with symbols of constellations and tune his feelings to surrounding flows. Having tuned in, the priest will be able to feel the shade and movement of the flow, getting an idea of ​​what kind of force circulates in the environment [elemental (fire) / evil (curse) / holy (divine healing in a temple or a good ritual of purification) / chaotic (disincarnation / breakthrough of Wyrm's forces into the reality / Storm of the underworld)]
                                            If the materials have been tainted by entropy, the priest will sense his own circle, but on rare occasions he will be able to better see the effects of withering. This entropy ritual is very difficult to read (roll has additional diff modifier +2 and every 2 successes equals 1 success in terms of the information received). If the materials were corrupted by infernal forces, then the priest will feel the presence of this power around him, this will make him believe that the place is in the power of demons / genies / devas. Moreover, any infernal forces will be hidden in a false aura (+ 1-2 to the difficulty of detection) and if they are not corrupted more than material of the circle, they will not be detected by this ritual.

                                            Foci: powder

                                            «Revelation» (Spirit/Life ●) — Revelation allows the priest to feel a spiritual presence that overlaps reality.

                                            System: The priest reads in his mind a prayer appeal for entering a short trance. In this state, the priest is able to feel (like those who have awareness) the touch of the spirit in our world. This allows the priest to feel the touch of spirits on the living (bonds or remnants of a recent influence), to feel a spiritual presence in people (obsession); feel the echoes of the spirit in its body (when spirits or fairies create a material body for themselves). This power cannot reveal the secrets of these creatures, it only indicates that the spirits were in close contact with a certain creature.

                                            Foci: Trance, prayer

                                            «Washing/Hiding the Soul» (Mind ● or Prime ●● or both) — [as Shroud the Soul, M20 Fallen p.151] — The old Greek priests (as well as the seekers of the Sumerian-Akkadian knowledge) taught that demons, ghosts and attentive people can feel fears and thoughts, and they can use them.

                                            System(Washing): To protect against manipulation, priest performs washing [Mind ●] that calms mind and removes unnecessary thoughts from the mind (and also suppresses part of his aura). The duration of the effect is limited by the maintained concentration.

                                            System(Hiding the Soul): Sometimes priests get negative resonance or their soul has a noticeable distinguishing feature, be it a demon's mark or an echo of carnage. In such cases, the priests perform ritual [Prime ●●] aimed at hiding the nature of these traces, directing their will to replace them with others (Roll: Will, difficulty resonance + 3 = -1 in resonance for each success). The effect will last no more than an hour, or until the priest does something that could reveal the original mark or destroy its "mask".

                                            The combo version clears the mind and hides spiritual traits. The ceremony is complex and lasts no more than an hour, but it allows the priest to hide his soul and mind.

                                            Foci: Concentration, water

                                            Complex of rituals: «Irkalla's Appeals» (Spirit ●●● — ●●●●, Entropy ●●● — ●●●●●, optional Prime ●●●) — a list of rituals for interacting with the underworld and the demons living there.


                                            «Death of Inanna» (Spirit ●●●, Entropy ●●●) — The priest performs in a place (room, street, grave) a rite with the participation of objects similar or copying the «Inanna's Seven Secret Powers» (a representation of a powerful me she possessed). The priest of the rite conducts a ceremony honoring and telling about the sacrifice of Inanna, her departure to Irkalla and her subsequent return. Completing the ceremony, the priest can call the departed soul into the world of the living, but in return he must offer himself. An important aspect of the ritual is going to Irkalla, for which the priest needs to injure himself. Often the ritual ends with hanging the mutilated body on hooks [and losing at least 5 health blocks].

                                            The success of the ritual will lead to the fall of the ghost of the priest (who becomes an ordinary ghost) to the area closest to the world of the living, and the summoned ghost will appear at the place of the ritual (which does not guarantee its visibility to others).
                                            So it is impossible to call souls captive outside Irkalla or located in its depths or the Storm. Also, due to the fact that Irkalla cannot «just» be left, the priest is obliged to sacrifice something of his own (temporary will in the amount of 3 or more; valuable thing, friend...), if the sacrifice turns out to be incomplete, the world will take the difference in the form of (permanent) Will, his mystical powers (will put a part of the avatar to sleep; Or, if he used more powerful rites, put the priest before the Torment [Harrowing]).

                                            If the priest has attained the greatest spiritual secrets Spirit ●●●● - he can learn the complex «Rite of the Seventh Gate» (Spirit ●●●●, Entropy ●●●) dedicated to the conflict between Inanna and Ereshkigal; then he can offer his body (if the spirit has the power to possess), help the ghost to get into his body, or forcibly shackle in his body. If successful, the spirit takes over the crippled (but possibly treatable) body, it can feel and has limited control. The Ghost does not become Risen and cannot heal wounds on its own.

                                            Priests who know the Entropy ●●●● can memorize the «Gift to the Gatekeeper» (Spirit ●●●, Entropy ●●●●) — rite dedicated to Nergal. Only this rite, in which “10 first-class, fat, ritually clean sheep, whose horn and nail are not damaged” are sacrificed, is able to open the gates to almost any place in Irkalla. This allows priest to summon ghosts and specters who have gone into the depths (although, those whom the priest did not call are often called back). Also, few of the priests spoke of other creatures living in that world that are similar to beasts, demons, or "unseen before"; some managed to call, albeit not for long, it is obvious that these are the servants of Nergal.
                                            By combining the «Gift to the Gatekeeper» with the Spirit ●●●●, the priest (who brought even more sacrifices from pure creatures) is able to briefly open a passage for the demons of Nergal or the souls of the dead. These entities do not obey the priest and are free to do what they want, in the end, the priest asks only to let them go for a while.

                                            Mastering Entropy and Spirit ●●●● allows spiritually enlightened priests to perform the «Seventh Gate» and «Death of Inanna» rituals bloodlessly. The priest gradually breaks the bonds that bind the soul. The priest must inflict important injuries (hanging on hooks), but he is able to descend into Irkalla on his own. But this descent takes the priest's soul closer to the Storm and depths of the Underworld - where he will almost certainly be harrowing.

                                            Having approached the divine and having mastered the Entropy ●●●●●, the priest can use ideas as a sacrifice (instead of health, quintessence, will or things) in the «Seventh Gate» and «Death of Inanna» rituals. Irkalla's call will erase him from someone else's life (or erase his memories; however, both options can happen).

                                            Prime ●●● is needed to collect the outgoing life and direct the sacrifice to the object of the ritual. The priest performing the rituals of Inanna can risk and offer his life as a sacrifice (1 quintessence point per health block), this will create a shroud of wind and dust that will protect the priest from the Storm and ghosts (The Storyteller's Job; simply creates a ghost shroud passing into chains). Such wounds cannot be healed by ordinary methods and rituals, and the priest runs the risk of leaving on himself the stigma of the Afterlife (Example: illness, loss of fertility, scars).

                                            The priest of Nergal can direct the outgoing life of the sheep (Prime ●●●, Entropy ●●● - ●●●●●) into the Kingdom of the Dead to God or his intermediary, which helps to strengthen the rite. Such a sacrifice allows for additional successes to extend the existence of the passage to Irkalla by 1 turn for each point of sacrificial quintessence or to reduce the difficulty for summoning a ghost from Irkalla. It is also possible to attract the ghosts or demons of Irkalla, if the resonance and form of quintessence suits them.
