            So I have been thinking about magick in relation to my work. I work at a bank, and when people come in asking for things like cash, paper statements, checks, etc, I always wonder what's keeping them from doing things the more high-tech way. This lead naturally (of course) to me thinking about the Syndicate, and then to how the Technocracy works.
            So I know that per canon most Technocrats don't see what they do as "magic" it's Enlightened Science, which is cool on it's own. But, what if the Technocracy viewed themselves as just the advanced modern magic users? Basically, why are you using dirt and trees and crystals when you can use computers and guns and stuff?

            Do remember, the Tehnocrats are such great villains because they see themselves as the good guys. all the best dramatic villains see themselves as the true hero. The idea behind the Technocratcy is that they ARE WORKING for ascension, but they are working to make everybody do it in one way only. The Disperates Alliance is in fact closer to the Technocracy than the Traditions, they feel that they alone can define a worthy and proper Ascension.

                    Originally posted by Enginseer-42 View Post

                    A rather silly theory considering the spheres are just artificial constructs used to help the users codify and make use of magic.
                    Are they? Considering Spheres are identical in Mage-the-game and Mage-the-game-setting, they are, for all intents and purposes, intrinsic to the construction of reality. Maybe they weren't always... (but even then they kinda were) During the Dark Ages the world's magic was a mosaic of similar but not congruent mini-praxes that were a fusion of Focus and Sphere, but, by the time of the creation of the Order of Reason and the Traditions, the Spheres seem to be THE accurate way of describing the way the universe actually works. Witness the fact, that, (with the possible exception of some dudes hanging out with vampires in the Shadowlands), everyone in the modern world uses the same framework, including people (like some Disparates) that deny that they are doing so. The Big Change seems to be that Focus was stripped out and the Spheres were revealed for what they were, the underlying structure of everything. Focus was also revealed as the way in which it was possible to utilize the Spheres.

                    If the Spheres were just artificial constructs, then there would be someone out there who would have figured out different ways of doing similar things. (Dataweaver loves this, wanting a setting where what you said is fundamentally true and there are groups out there using the Foundation/Pillar system, those using the Arete/Sphere system and another as yet undefined system for the Technocratic Union that works on slightly different axes.) It's Focus (or the collected Foci of groups with similar magical methodologies) that are the artificial constructs. Spheres just are. Or, at least, they are an artificial construct that is a very close approximation of the way that the universe works to the point that, having come up with a way to articulate them, they effectively serve as a functional way to discuss something that ALL magic users have in common.
