                                    For me, the fact that Foundation only goes up to 5 goes hand in hand with the pool being Foundation + Pillar, whereas Arete going up to 10 goes with the pool being just Arete: the former starts you with larger pools up front, but caps them at the same ten dots that the Arete pool is capped at. The idea of allowing Foundation to exceed 5 (letting the pool offer up to 15 dice) is troubling; letting Pillars exceed 5 as well is just plain crazy.

                                    Conversely, I generally don't consider Archspheres to be a thing. What you can do at Mastery is already insane; if there's a “sixth dot” in a Sphere, it's not something you can reach just by spending experience points: it should be akin to undergoing a second Awakening, putting you in the same general territory as achieving Ascension. And its effects should be comparable to Rank 6+ spirits, which are no longer in the realm of what player characters can directly challenge: it should basically be an “I win” button against anything short of the likes of the Wyrm or God. In short, it's not really a sixth dot so much as it removes the character from the realm of playability due to sheer overpowering.

                                    And I tend to treat “arch-Pillars” the same way: there are no “sixth dots” for either Foundation or Pillar, not without achieving something akin to Ascension; and at that point, you're not playing Mage anymore.

                                    My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness
