            Indeed, Psionics is practically a blank check for explaining what the mystics do. It's also why the Technocracy suppresses it in the Consensus, alongside realistic holograms and nanomachines. If those three got out, everything could be explainable (and thus coincidental).

            And really, the Technocracy's upper echelons couldn't ask for a better cover story to feed to the rank and file. It neatly explains patently absurd feats, and undermines the fundamental paradigms of their competition. The Practices of traditionalists are painted as crutches to focus psionic ability, rather than being the outgrowth of valid world views. Moreover, it can imply that the Traditions are not only delusional, but possibly mentally unstable. After all, that much channeling of Genius through raw thought (assisted by superfluous mystic trappings) can't be good for the psyche, and there are plenty of psychopathic Chakravanti, obsessive compulsive Hermetics, and shamans who talk to themselves to support the point. From that perspective, any given Traditionalist is only a few steps away from going full Marauder.

            No, it's better for good Technocrats to channel Inspired Science through technology, which won't twist the user's mind into knots until they have delusions of godhood.

                    ((Am I even in this conversation anymore?))

                    1. No he cannot. You cannot enchant anything without prime. The wand is but a focus, it hold no power outside of the mage who wields it.
                    2. Again, No. The gun is but a focus. The time he'd put into making the gun would essentially be like putting time into making a rote. The effect only happens as he turns on his gun, and thus starts focusing on the effect at hand. Without Prime, the gun itself hold no intrinsic power (unless he did some mundane technology stuff to it in addition).
                    3. No, the mage must be the one to use their focus, and only those who use similar focuses or have aligned paradigms would be able to get an effect out of them.
                    4. No, the same mechanics of the game apply to all.

                    One thing to keep in mind is that not all Etherites with Force 2 would be able to get the gravity gun to work. Some would consider the science behind a gravity gun impossible because they don't subscribe to the specific theories behind what is supposed to make this gun work.

                    This is not unlike one Verbena thinking that the Crystal magic another Verbena does is silly and rather trivialised the more primordial practices involving blood rituals and runes. This Verbena may consider crystals to be pretty, but on the whole not all that effective compared to their tried and true methods.

                    Same thing with Etherites, One might say the gravity gun is quaint, but rather antiquated compared to their Relative Field Theory of Etheric Particle Physics. With their newest device, they'll be able to rend an objects momentum in physical space changable via electrode impulses that disrupt their etheric fields. None of this "Gravity particle manipulation" stuff, which while effective enough, doesn't really make all that much sense on the grand scheme of things.

                    Etherites are really difficult to get to work together, so many competing theories usually results in more arguments than actual work being done.


                    So to answer your question for the Nth time. They could think the following:

                    - These sleepers are idiots! There's nothing wrong with my machines! My machines are perfect! It's their lack of Genius which is at fault. Yes, everyone! True Genius is so hard to come by these days...
                    - The gun is dealing with delicate forces, so the slightest mistake in its use will obviously result in in failure. Pulling the trigger before the power is at capacity, or letting the power reach over capacity, firing the gun at an inappropriate frequency, These machines can be quite temperamental... Hmm... Perhaps I should invest some research time into these Primal Field Theories I keep hearing about, from the cursory reading I've done, they seem to produce more robust machines with out as much need for specialised training or Genius to operate.
                    - If they don't understand the theory behind the machine, then how in the world can they know what they are doing? Without theoretical understanding, of course they won't know what to aim the gun at. Not the object itself, but the field that hold the object within gravity's constraint!

                    The one things they are very unlikely to think is that their devices are irrelevant when it comes to effects. The only thing that might grant such an insight is a Seeking and a sufficiently high level of Arete.

                    Even then, their theories will still be at the core of their practice, machines or not.
