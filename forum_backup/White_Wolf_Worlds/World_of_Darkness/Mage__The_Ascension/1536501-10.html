                                You can do stuff like playing with the properties of matter in ways that aren't natural. Like, you can create liquids that act like a solid on certain circumnstances, make transparent metal, create substances with 0 mass, create ice that never melts, make bullets that treat bulletproff vests as air .

                                This does interact with forces a lot. And you most definitely can do some "forces related" shenanigans with pure matter. A pretty blatant example would be Antimatter (Matter 5, creates big boom in contact with matter). On Revised there's two interesting examples that use pure matter: Weigth Alteration (it is what it sounds like), and changing how patterns interact each other (this can do stuff like making pattern matters insubstantial in regards to other patterns, but also allows to do things like giving anything the properties of a superconductor)

                                Considering the examples I think it would be within reason to allow stuff like creating a floating surfoard (like back to the future) by changing how the matter of the table interact with solids and liquids by "repelling" (as magnets), instead of making the board insubstantial as per the "changing how patterns interact" rule, with a dash of "radioactive implies Matter can go a bit into Forces"

                                But I wouldn't allow to diverge too much into the realm of ephemeral spheres. Like, I wouldn't allow "a helmet that isolates from mind contact" w/o Mind.

                                Primium does still need Prime 3 added to Matter 5, after all. Same with creating a Matrix (Matter 5 (inanimate) or Life 5 (living) + Prime 3. Effect fuses Tass into foundation, creating permanent vessel for Quintessence.)

                                But, by combining Spheres, you can totally create a substance that would protect against psychic assaults by simply existing, which can then be used to make whatever such a substance could make. Which it's still pretty special, I would say, normally you would need Prime skill to make a "Wonder" to do anything remotely similar, and I'm not sure average Wonders can be changed form freely w/o loosing their properties.
