    Wonder 101

    07-26-2016, 03:18 AM
    Hi, I've been reading lately about Wonders in "Forged by Dragon's Fire" and in M20, but I took notice that there might be several differences, so I couldn't quite grasp it completely how a Wonder is made.

    Seeing this as example:

    • to ••• Trushades (Trinket)

    Arete N/A, Quintessence N/A, Background Cost 2-6 pts.

    In a simple, yet potentially powerful, variation on the old
    “X-ray specs” concept, many mages keep glasses or goggles that
    have been modified with between one and three perceptual
    Effects. The name Trushades comes from a variety marketed to
    Awakened customers by Greer Laboratories – a cabal composed
    of Ecstatic, Etherite, and VA mages whose slogan is “Look
    closer.” Other, generic varieties exist under many different
    names. Although such shades – which range from simple reading
    glasses or sunglasses to stylish shades and wild eyewear – look
    perfectly normal, their enhancements allow the wearer to view
    things no human eyes should be able to see.

    System: Depending upon the Spheres involved, a pair of
    Trushades can employ any Rank 1 visual perception Effect. The
    most common variations incorporate Forces (electrons or other
    physical energies), Life (overall health and the presence of life),
    Matter (the structure of inanimate items and surfaces), Mind
    (auras), and Prime (Quintessence and Resonance/ Synergy
    forces), but really – any visually perceivable Sphere works.

    Generally, a pair of modified glasses also features an
    activation word or touch; this way, the wearer can turn the
    Effects on and off at will. A one-dot pair holds a single Effect,
    and the three-dot sets feature three different Spheres working
    toward a single purpose. Three Spheres, however, is the
    maximum capacity for a non-Talisman pair of glasses… and any
    character who tries to use all three Spheres at once will suffer
    both a -2 penalty to her Perception rolls and a nasty (though
    non-injurious) headache.
    So, as far as I understood, you can make a Trinket with one-two-three Effects and keep it at that, without having to spend Quintessence or roll Wonder's Arete (which is not so good to be honest. Wonders usually have unimpressive Arete and you're forced to difficulty of 6).

    Me and few other Mage players were discussing possibillity of other custom Wonders, and here's what we came up with:

    Our characters are Verbena, Dreamspeaker and Euthanatos. We follow "Creation is Innately Alive and Divine" belief (which is quite common for our Traditions), so we planned to create a tattoo-trinket with picture of panther on character's back that blesses with speed of a wild cat. (Game-wise speaking, we read chapter that certain Sphere Effects can simulate Gifts. There's a rote of Correspondence 1 + Time 1 Effect to increase your initiative, and we wanted to copy the Effect from Gift "Spirit of the Fray". Basically, that tattoo as trinket will passively give +10 to initiative, just like this rank 2 Gift does.)

    I don't see any reason why this cannot be legit (considering that the example with Trushades above directly from M20 book says that Trinkets always carry the Effect of spells passively), but I'd like to ask anyway if this idea is decent or it just won't do. We decided that tattoo as Trinket could do because it fits our practice "Bodywork" and "Artwork".

    What do you think about this and why? Is there any additional info about crafting Wonders, and if yes, where? (except "Forged by Dragon's Fire)

    Oh and as bonus, if you've got any ideas for Wonders, we'd greatly appreciate it! We have plenty on our own, but we sometimes doubt if they're legitimate.

    Thanks in advance.

      The Trushades are broken, in the way that they are not actually a Trinket by the definition of Trinkets.
      Trinkets are supposed to be normal items, with a magickal effect having changed their properties. They themselves are not technically magickal at all,
      The Trushades however get 'activated' with a word or touch. This makes them a triggered Wonder(!), not a trinket. Trinkets have no effect on them. They simply got permanently changed somehow.

      But, aside of that:

      Tattoos-as-a-trinket does not make sense.

      Here's why:
      As stated above, trinkets are normal objects that got changed or enhanced with an effect. They themselves are not inherently magickal. Basically you have normal objects, whose normal properties got changed.

      A hoodie that's bulletproof. Glasses that constantly give you night vision by showing infrared falling through them.
      How are the changed properties of the tattoos supposed to give extra speed?

      So, tattoos can't really be a trinket that gives you extra speed, no. You can make tattoos a trinket by making them change colors. Or making the tattoos be a substance that hardens on impact, forming a sort of exotic armor (which sounds kind of horribly painful...might be a bad idea)

      For extra speed, just skip the tattoo phase and cast an effect with a long duration on the person themselves


      Or what you want to do is, turn the tattoos into a full wonder. That would work perfectly for your purposes.

              Yup yup. The old Mage Revised ed books even had examples of Mages turning their tattoos into wonders. It can be VERY befitting some paradigms.

              More stuff
              Careful
              Dragon's Fire and M20 Wonder rules are not inherently compatible.

              With the M20-described Wonder (As in, Talismans/Devices) process, Wonders don't necessarily spend Quintessence, but basically their Arete roll determines the duration, potency etc. of the unleashed effect. (I highly prefer this)

              With Dragon's Fire, a single success on the Arete roll unleashes the 'Full Effect', but sadly Dragon's Fire is broken in the way that it never describes how things like duration et al are handled. Yay.

              And both systems suck at explaining just when or how it's determined if using a Wonder requires expenditure of Quintessence or not.

              I really hope the M20 Book Of Secrets will clear things up more regarding M20 Wonder creation.

                                          Alternatively, an 'always on' artifact could do your speed boost tattoos handily. But it's much harder to make those than a standard trinket.

                                          And what do you mean wonders have less than impressive Arete scores?

                                          Artifacts use your own personal Arete. And only require one success to succeed. Or alternatively, you can build them a bit more and have them simply be 'always active' The standard +1 sword.

                                          Charms require you roll Arete beforehand, and again, only require one success to accomplish the effect, though they are single use.

                                          Talismans use their own Arete yes, but the Arete Score on them is limited only to how much Tass you funnel into them. There's no reason you can't make a Talisman with an Arete score of 100 save for practicality. It's not hard to get more reasonable but still potent arete scores in them. AND they also only need one success.

                                          Wonders are by and far the easiest way to cast.
