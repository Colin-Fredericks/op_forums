    So since it’s come up, how I’ve wrestled with climate change in the context of MtA, since it’s an objective reality that seems resistant to whatever the Consensus believes, is that the Union interprets it as Paradox fallout from the industrial revolution. The Syndicate in particular views it as a “market correction” as the Consensus adjusts to accept post-industrialization levels of consumption. Having it firmly take root as part of the consensus would be the worse thing possible because then it will stay after the correction runs it’s course - so they fund and support climate change denial. Environmentalists in ItX and the Progenitors are trying desperately to push real environmental reforms, but the Syndicate keeps blocking them.

                            I wanna be clear that I in no way have any dislike towards Technocracy players. Or Vampire or Hunter players. Like, I need kitten-video-brain-bleach after reading too much about those games. That's just me.

                            I am confused how the guys who rule the world have supported clean energy all this time and not made one micron of actual difference with any Sleeper government? Because if any environmentalism in the Union predates the Avatar Storm.... that just makes no sense to me. Doesn't the Syndicate have the magic to create unlimited relative wealth for themselves, even more than Pentex? Don't they basically own all the Sleeper governments? Aren't the Progenitors the inventors and purveyors of all things pesticide? Despite having the magic to rewrite reality so pesticides aren't even necessary? Despite real-world science now having proven they were never necessary*? And yet... the WOD is still even worse environmentally than real life. Like, lots worse. Like, Deep Horizon wasn't a unique or even unusual spill. Likewise... genuine religious freedom and tolerance isn't doing great in real life or the WOD, so if the Technocrats aren't really all about forced conversion... what have they been up to this whole time? Maybe I'm overestimating how powerful they are. Are the Ventrue actually the real rulers of the WOD?

                            Even back in the Victorian Age, solar energy was invented in the same year as the internal combustion engine. In real life, I think around 1901. Pentex didn't exist until 1915. So if the Technocrats weren't utterly enamored with coal and oil, despite the visually and odorously obvious problems, why didn't they ever promote solar panels or electric busses in their Time Table? They ought to at least have just as much influence as Pentex and the Camarilla. If they have any environmental interest, they ought to have something to show for it.


                            *Not to mention generations of organic regenerative agriculture practiced by some Native Americans before they were conquered.
                            A number of factors, some of which I've touch upon already. The Technocrat control over the world, much like their internal unity, is greatly over exaggerated especially from the outside. The Traditions, our main viewpoint in MtAs, sees the Technocracy as this great monolith that dominates the world and certainly the Union likes to present that image but the truth is far from that. While they do have a great deal of influence over the world it's nowhere near as strong as they play it up. Worse, the Masses are increasingly rebelling against them and the Belief they're trying to push. Of course they're not rushing off to the Traditions either. Instead the Masses are embracing a self-destructive spiral of apathy and irrationality.

                            It's telling that M20 Technocracy Reloaded starts with a Technocrat straight up saying:
                            We’re losing.

                            Yes, I know what you’ve been told, kiddies: Our magnificent Union cornered the market on Consensus. Mankind’s curled up with their smart phones, everyone loves technology, and cybernetic monkeys fly out my ass on command, raining fire on the poor little sorcerers cowering in the shadows of our magnificent Technocracy.

                            I ask you this: Does it look like we are winning?

                            Irrationality rules the day. Our world unravels at the seams. If our sad-sack species has formed any kind of consensus, it’s that cutting off your nose to spite your face is much more fun when you use a chainsaw in public to do it.

                            We’re partly to blame for this, you know. We got stupid, careless, so caught up in our own propaganda that we refused to see the infection eating us alive.

                            Worst of all, we got complacent. We believed it would only take a few surgical strikes on the worst offenders, and a wealth of luxuries for the Masses, before humanity would come around and enter a grand age of civil stability.

                            We were wrong.

                            Man is fundamentally irrational. He wants ghosts and vampires and boy wizards with English accents. He wants to believe that genital configurations or melanin production define him as blessed in the eyes of nonexistent gods. He doesn’t fear Apocalypse — he craves it, so long as he doesn’t have to live personally with the results. He’s more fascinated with wanking off in the mirror than with looking toward the horizon of what he could be if he’d just get his hand off his cock.

                            Man cannot be trusted with the future. If we are to have a future, that future must belong to us.
                            They're not just fighting to protect the Consensus they've built from Realty Deviants that want to tear it down, they're fighting against the apathy of Masses that's causing it to rot from within.

                            As for introducing technologies that could overcome climate change there's a problem with that, Paradox. While Enlightened Science and Hypertechnology can evade Paradox more easily than Magick can, it is still subject to Paradox. In fact, some speculate that Climate Change is a Paradox reaction in and of itself.

                            The opening and closing fiction of the Rev Ed Syndicate book is a great example of this. The story revolves around a Syndicate agent sent to investigate a Sleeper medical research lab that is getting very promising results in a cure for cancer, very sudden results. As it turns out that's because a group of Progenitors are covertly helping the Sleepers with Enlightened Science.

                            The agent shuts them down, hating herself for doing so. Not because of some nefarious Technocrat scheme these Progenitors were disrupting but because it wouldn't work. If if can't be done through mundane science and technology it will cause Paradox and people will die because of it. To paraphrase the agent, the Masses dream of a world without cancer but they don't actually believe it. So the Technocracy cannot make a world without cancer. They need to slowly feed to slowly feed breadcrumbs to get them there so that they can have a cure for cancer by their own hands. That's the only way the Consensus would accept it.

                            Climate Change is of that vein as well. The Technocracy could fix it with their Hyper Technology and Enlightened Science in a relative short order but the Consensus would not except it and the world would be hit with Paradox on a massive scale which would likely make things a thousand times worse.

                            Which is also a factor in their frustration at the growing apathy and irrationality of the Masses. They're trying to put out dozens of the growing fires as the Sleepers are running around and throwing gasoline all over the place.

                            As for solar energy vs. fossil fuels, it's again a matter of what will the Consensus accept and what it won't, what it will accept more easily and what will struggle to be accepted. More importantly what could be done most efficiently with the mundane science and technology of that time. Then of course you have hope that it catches on with the Sleepers.

                            There's also the fact that Technocrats and their Time Tables are not perfect. They are regularly revising the Time Tables all the time because even with their Enlightened Science they cannot predict every variable that could affect their predictions.

                            Again, the Technocracy likes to present itself as this All-Seeing, All-Powerful Monolith that rules the world from the shadows when in reality while they are powerful, they are no where near as powerful as they claim. Doubly so in a post-Avatar Storm world.

                            Homo sapiens. What an inventive, invincible species. It's only a few million years since they crawled up out of the mud and learned to walk. Puny, defenceless bipeds. They've survived flood, famine and plague. They've survived cosmic wars and holocausts. And now, here they are, out among the stars, waiting to begin a new life. Ready to outsit eternity. They're indomitable. Indomitable.
