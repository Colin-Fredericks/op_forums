                                              There are a great many things that are covered by multiple spheres... and if we focus on the desired result instead of the process, that number grows to infinity.

                                              Regarding Entropy 5 being replaced by Mind... maybe with a butt-load of successes. Mind can create thoughts and remove thoughts, but can it affect the probability of a thought NEVER or ALWAYS occurring? It needs at least Time to do that, and even still... you would have to forcibly change every possibility, which with time goes to infinity.


                                              Entropy and Prime both cross a lot of borders. Entropy often combines with other spheres to provide them precision in targeting, choosing, or blending.

                                              Take for example an engine. You could Matter/Time it with some awesome power, but it would still break down. Everything breaks down. So to keep that engine running at a supernatural level you need either continuous Matter/Time maintenance to keep each component in tip top condition, or to make it self-replicating and feed it Quintessence. With Entropy though, you can remove the inherent imperfections in the system (which is all the little components AND how they interact) resulting in a system that does -not- break down over time.

                                              Entropy in common language is a word used to signify disorder, but scientifically it is a unit used to measure the amount of disorder. All systems (patterns for our discussion) have SOME level of disorder to them (low levels generally called order)... Entropy changes that.

                                              It can also radiate the same. At higher levels you can attach an "entropy" aura to living creatures, and even minds. That means you can apply curses, blessings, and such. Add some Prime and you can make these permanent.

                                              Entropy deals with a quality of the patterns that other spheres create. It deals with versions of primal energies.

                                                      Oh lord. Did you at least try and sit down with the folks and go through the basics of the Spheres and their levels? Spheres are horrible to new players unless someone can take them by the hand and exactly explain what they are about.

                                                      It's not annuled by saying "a lot of what it does can be done by other spheres" as the same argument applies to every other sphere. Magic is a tool to solve problems. When you have a hammer you use a hammer. That's the approach that can be taken with the spheres quite easily.

                                                      Entropy is chance/luck, fate and decay. In practice this means that this sphere is usually more roundabout in its applications. For instance, while you'd use forces to zap someone dead, you'd use entropy to have a support beam crack and fall on top of his head. When it comes to Fate you use Entropy to see the fate of people or objects and to twist and turn what they may or may not do. For instance you can use Fate on a person to ensure that circumstances conspire that he is at the right place at the right time. You can use Fate to get a gut feeling which is the right choice at a given moment. In essence you use Fate to ensure an outcome or judge the most likely outcome for a given situation. Chance/luck is similar and can be interpreted as an application of Fate. It's all about rigging things so they work in your favour or the favour of people you like - the dice are always double 6s, you always have a winning hand in poker, you hit more often then others, your bullets amazingly ricochet to hit someone in the most improbable manner. Or you inflict the same on others, be it for them to have rotten luck or putting a curse upon their house, where their crops will wilt and their loins will wither, for they angered someone they truly shouldn't have. The final application of Entropy is the literal meaning of the word. Entropy is about decay, studying the process of decay and stopping decay. This means that you can find stress points, push them, have things fall apart, kill people with massive organ failure, decay things into husks, corrode materials and so on.

                                                      Prime on the other hand requires that you actually understand the underlying metaphysics of Magic. That everything in reality is made of primal energies and primal energies are usually quantified as points of Quintessence. Quintessence can be stored within a mage, within an item, within a magical creature or within a whole place. This energy can be harnessed by Mages to improve their powers and efficiency of their spellcasting, as well as to build magical items of their own, this energy is also what makes magical entities tick. Now what does this mean in practice. For one, Prime is the sphere that equalises the playing field for mages with low avatar ratings. It allows them to use more quintessence then their avatar would allow them. For the other it allows them to use any and all forms of quintessence around them as power source - be it blood within their veins, the goat next to them, the Tass they harvested in a forlorn castle or the energy of a deep, calm forest. Prime allows you to manipulate with such core concepts as Paradox and Resonance as well, broadening the esoteric applications of the sphere. It also allows you to counter all magic and dispel all ongoing effects, as well as block and interact with supernatural creatures and their sources of power. It allows you to enchant weapons to do horrendous harm, and it allows you to take ambient quintessence of anything around you and turn it into something else. You know that classic mainstay, the fireball? Prime 2 in that spell is all about allowing you to create "something out of nothing" - you're actually using prime to transmute ambient quintessence into energy for the spell, then forces to turn this energy into fire and toss it at what you want it to blow up. And that's before getting into the fact that Prime also allows you to build magical items and nodes (the afforementioned sources of Quintessence).
