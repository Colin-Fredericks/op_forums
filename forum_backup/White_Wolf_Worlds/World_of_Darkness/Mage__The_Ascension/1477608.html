          The Mount Qaf/Web of Faith example from the Ahl-i-Batin suggests that Correspondence could play a role in it (or, in the case of Technocrats, Data). Something to the effect of “an organization is a group of individuals who have certain characteristics in common” (the main one being that they're all affiliated with that organization). Therefore, you ought to be able to use a symbol of that organization to target its members. It's not going to be easy; the more people the symbol encompasses, the less perfect the connection to any one of them is, which translates to a poor sympathetic connection to any one of them. Still, I'd say that Correspondence in its role of dealing with Sympathetic Range, as opposed to dealing with Space, would be a good tool for dealing with an affiliation.

          Bear in mind that if you're trying to target everyone in the organization, you don't just need the Sympathetic Connection; you need additional successes sufficient to affect all of them. If using the MRev-based “Dividing Successes” rule where you allocate successes to different casting aspects, this would mean that you need a number of additional successes equal to the number of additional members affected after the first. This quickly becomes prohibitive. But the idea that the Feat requires more successes the larger the affiliated group is strikes me as reasonable.

          I'd go with the rule of thumb that the number of additional successes needed is based on how many dots the group would count as if it was a Venture, at (say) two successes per dot. I'd also say that targeting someone by way of their group affiliation requires at least three successes: Familiar Range or a Connection such as a possession or a friend. Again, of you're targeting an organization rather than an individual, you can't do better than that. Combining these two factors would mean, for example, that targeting everyone in a street gang would require an additional five successes above and beyond however many successes are needed for the Effect itself: three for the group affiliation, and two more for its size. At the other extreme, an international crime cartel would require thirteen successes above and beyond the successes needed for the Effect itself.

          That may actually be a bit too low, though. Maybe three successes per dot? The street gang would require and extra six successes, while the international cartel would require an extra eighteen. Yeah; that's a bit more reasonable; it puts Magick influencing an entire street gang near the middle of Mighty Feats (one example of which is “commanding a mob”), while the same Magick influencing an international cartel would almost certainly be a Godlike Feat.

              Originally posted by Dataweaver View Post
              The Mount Qaf/Web of Faith example from the Ahl-i-Batin suggests that Correspondence could play a role in it (or, in the case of Technocrats, Data).
              I highly agree with this part. People tend to forget that Correspondence is the Sphere of *relations* (Or as Dataweaver here calls it, 'Sympathetic Connections').
              Usually its default major association in player's heads is with space, long distances, shaping space, compressing space, overlaying space etc. etc. but all *that* boils down is relations..in space (Sorry: "IN SPAAAACE!" ). Spatial relations are one thing - but that's not the end of that Sphere. It covers all sorts of relations and connections as its base, which is the very reason that, say, having a personal possession of somebody in hand makes targetting them with Correspondence easier.

              Now, depending on paradigm, Correspondence on its own or combined with other Spheres could also be used to target all people that have something in *common* - i.e. a factual relation. The Technocracy's Data variant is essentially this concept driven to the extreme of relationship- and connection-of-Information - up to allowing mere isolated facts about an object or person to connect with them.

              What we *do* know, because it's the Ahl-I-Batin, is that their communication did *not* use Correspondence in combination with *Entropy* - which is ironically what I would have suspected the ideal combination to be to create the group association. A sad victim of Entropy at times being written as an overly dark and negative thing, while Entropy can ironically also be used for concepts and creation.

              So, I presume Correspondence plus Mind - not only as the additional Sphere to conduct the telepathy, but also as the common relation between the targets, i.e. their mindset - would be perfectly fine for this sort of whole-group way of communication. Perhaps Prime would be also a good component for the effect, as it could be used perhaps to connect the common magicks of a specific group.

                                                  I don't think it's so much a question of defining which Sphere has more to do with association, but how each Sphere deals with it, if at all. Six Spheres may have something to say about affiliation on their own:

                                                  Correspondence is the Sphere of Relations by default. While dealing with space is its most common use, we have to remember that users of Correspondence do not define it as understanding of space, but instead define space itself as an illusion of Correspondence. This is common to them all, from Batini to Adepts and Data experts. Affiliation defines a relationship, so this one is obvious.

                                                  Entropy deals with organization and fate, both aspects of affiliations, as those groups organize individual effort and affect the fate of their members to the point of having fates of their own. The connection is easier to understand at Entropy 5, when you start to mess with ideas, and groups are ideas, they exist as patterns of meaning beyond existing as their members.

                                                  Mind works through several layers. The most obvious, sure, is the fact that your affiliation is an information in your mind, but also your mind holds the ideals and ideas, the culture, the webs of symbols and meanings, the emotions and associations related to that group, associated just with a sense of belonging even when not agreeing to them, as you either relate to something about your group as being about you, or as being about your people even if you disagree with them on that. Criticism on your own doesn't have the same quality as criticism on others in your Mind Pattern.

                                                  Prime is a curious case. As said before, groups exist as ideas and symbols too, things that have their own Pattern, and Traditions and Conventions, as magical groups, put their metaphysical weight on many of the things they do. They develop signature rotes, associate with given Spheres and magical styles, they share ideas on how magic works and how it should be used and taught. For everything that Prime can detect, there's at least one signature the group created at some point in time, even if you don't target the group's Pattern directly.

                                                  Spirit mostly have the same considerations as Prime, with the addition of any interaction members of the group had with the Umbra while identifying as such, that can leave all sorts of spiritual impressions behind.

                                                  Time will capture the shared History of the group, and the many individual histories that crisscross it and give it weight. It fixes the group's identity as an actor in itself, formed by others but still independent from them.

                                                  Any and all of those can work to make for the component of a spell that delivers a message to all group members but no outsider. They can combine between them or with the other three, or work as standalone, depending on the particulars of the effect, what you want out of it and how you understand the effect, but there are way too many options to work with using all sorts of combinations and levels.
