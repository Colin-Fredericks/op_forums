    "Possibly" M5

    08-17-2023, 08:24 PM
    This was something that crossed my mind and making it clear that I don't want to imply anything by this, I'm just talking about a thought of mine. Using the W5 as a base, what would the M5 look like if it had to also strip away its cultural traits? Traditions have a lot to do with belief so I really find it surreal to think about taking that away from them, I can imagine the Hermetics as a more traditional mage archetype, or SoE as technomages, the Verdana as a druid archetype, and maybe the euthanatos as vigilantes or necromancers, but while for the rest? Thinking now about technocracy I imagine them turning into a kind of illuminati that manipulates the world from the shadows for the personal gain of their leaders and who want to monopolize the use of magic and that's why they persecute the mages. Their SI would be an order of witch hunters or knights templars who wield magic, but it comes from the faith, or so they believe, basically the chorus. Anyway, this strange concept came to mind, how do you think it would be?

          If they decouple Mage factions from their cultural roots, it will effectively cease to be Mage: The Ascension.

          Hypothetically, you could make a Werewolf: The Apocalypse game where Garou tribes aren't specific to any given culture or ethnic group, and still have it hit many of the same beats. (It would require you to keep around things like making deals with spirits and fighting the Wyrm/Pentex/Black Spiral Dancers. So, not W5 in the slightest).

          Everything about Mage: The Ascension revolves around the way it roots itself in Real Life cultural practices. The draw of playing MtAs, more than in other splats, is this authentic specificity. The degree to which it uses actual world views and practices to inform your characters.

          To flatten Mage groups, as they've done with Vampires, Hunters, and Werewolves would be abominable. It's a waste of the greatest asset MtAs has going for it. If I wanted to play as a non-specific Wizard or Druid, I'd play more D&D.

          I guarantee M5 will go this direction, too. Not just because it's been the modus operendi of WoD5 so far, but because Paradox has exhibited profound cowardice when it comes to portraying non-European cultures. To make MtAs work requires a deft hand and lots of research and cultural consulting. Sounds like a lot of work. Not to mention asking experts for their input and following through with it.

          Paradox won't even keep a Werewolf tribe playable if it's patterned after a Native American culture, even when they've got folks telling them how to do it well. Do you think Paradox will have what it takes to do the Dreamspeakers justice? Would they even try? Or will we see more erasure of these cultures, in a misguided attempt to dodge controversy?

          Also, imagine the worst examples of Enforced Street Level Play from the Revised era, post Avatar Storm. M5 would almost certainly top that. Forget potentially getting your Avatar shredded by the Avatar Storm, you're unlikely to be allowed to attempt Stepping Sideways at all. Getting off the Streets and into anywhere else is BadWrongFun.
