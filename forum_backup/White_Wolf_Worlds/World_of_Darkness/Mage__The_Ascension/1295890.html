    Vulgarity and Sanctum; are some effects ALWAYS Vulgar?

    03-11-2019, 03:07 PM
    So in M20 some effects are labeled Vulgar automatically. Certain Time and Prime effects for example; pulling Quintessence out of the universe itself is Vulgar Prime 5 magick.

    But part of the Sanctum background is this space where your work is always Coincidental, since you define reality. Which takes precedence? Also, do people know all of the effects that are always Vulgar? I know a few but I'm sure I've missed most of them.

      The short answer is, yes, they are still Vulgar in your Sanctum. Something that is always Vulgar is Vulgar despite local reality zones so is unaffected by your Sanctum.

      The longer answer is that when you dig into the always Vulgar spells there is usually a specific reason they have that status. Something about them is specifically rejected by some aspect of reality. For instance, in the case of Time Travel the Mage is creating a temporal paradox, which is not specifically the same thing as Paradox but it does violate expectations in a broadly similar way*. In some way the Paradox is coming from something other than the spell itself.

      *I'm actually against the idea of using Paradox as the method for dealing with temporal paradoxes, but it is the rule right now.

              Yes, there are effects that are Vulgar even in Sanctums. For instance:

              *Healing Aggravated wounds it's Always Vulgar.

              *Very Epic Stuff, like breaking the Curse of Caine and other "imposibilities" that may or may not go against the will of God are also signaled as Always Vulgar

              *Gilgul it's Always Vulgar

              However, the limits between Vulgar and Always Vulgar are very dubious and rely a lot on ST fiat. These are exceptions to the general rule (much like gaining extra Paradox point when using Time 3 to rewind time) and should be treated like that.

              The language itself can be misleading:

              Sometimes it's said that something it's always going to be Vulgar because it's nigh impossible to hidde as a Coincidence, like when more than 10 points of Quintessence are moved in one round and magick starts to spark. In these cases it's not going to be Vulgar within a Sanctum (unless you yourself have a strong disbelef in such displays)

              Other times it's always Vulgar because it breaks Earthly Foundations...which it's a Vulgarity that won't necessarily apply in the Other Worlds. For instance, temporal and spatial distortions are very common in the Umbra, and magick that aligns with these isn't going to be Vulgar. As a more specific example, the Vulgar+ effect of rewinding time with Time 3 can be Coincident in the Digital Web (i.e: restore a saved game)

              Another thing of interest it's what happens with Always Vulgar in the Deep Umbra - where you can't gain Paradox because it doesn't exist there (as per Infinite Tapestry). An argument can be made that the spell it's still casted at the difficulty of a Vulgar spell, but you don't gain any Paradox. However one could contest that w/o Paradox the difference of difficulty understood as the resistance of Reality to the change has no reason to be.

              So, sometimes Always Vulgar may as well mean "Most Of The Time Vulgar" , and the limits of such category are heavily dependant on the ST. But yes, Vulgarity that transcends Sanctums and perhaps even Realms it's very much contemplated.

                                  Nope. It's important to decouple Consensus, Reality and Paradox. These are very related but are different words for a reason.

                                  The Consensus it's determined by human belief. There's no question about that, that's why it's called "Consensus". This is explicitly stated in ALL Mage books ever (even talking about unreliable narrators isn't helpful here because mages invented that term - they presumably know what they meant)

                                  BUT Reality (and Paradox, as the force that reacts to counter alterations to Reality) MAY not be entirely determined by it.

                                  The idea behind the Cosmological Constants that were introduced in Revised and latter traduced into (somewhat weaker) Eartly Foundations was that there were stuff that seemed to resist change regarldess of the local Consensus. It was thoerized that perhaps these came from forces transcending the Consensus itself (the Triat and God are mentioned in Revised). But it was also theorized that perhaps these were just things that were ingrained in the human subconscious and thus always present to some extent (Vampires exist becuase humans subconsciously know that they have to - bullshit, I know, but canon bullshit) but not inherently beyond the Consensus power to change

                                  For those that believe that the Consensus it's omnipotent or nearly so there's nothing that's Truly Always Vulgar. Even stuff like lifting the Curse of Caine may be made Coincident, eventually. For those that believe that there are cosmic laws precluding humanity the "Always Vulgar" category does represent a fundamental change of the universe that Reality resists at a fundamental level. But there's no doubt about what the Consensus *is*

                                  When I'm not agreeing with Enginseer-42 interpretation of MotA fluf, I'm not agreeing with the notion that believability has anything to do with it.

                                  MotA it's talking about Archspheres, btw, not about the Always Vulgar category. It's important to note that Archspheres AREN'T Always Vulgar (altrough their Vulgarity does transcend Sanctums). Archspheres are Vulgar on Earth, but can be Coincident within Realms attuned with the mage's Paradigm, as per MotA.

                                  In theory, regenerating Aggravated would be Vulgar even within the Umbra (perhaps even in the Deep, where Paradox does not exist).

                                  As you see, this IS a complex issue. As it often happens with exceptions to the normal rules
