            Originally posted by Aya Tari View Post
            It really, really does. If technology is a form of True Magick, how could Changelings, Fera, Kindred, Wraiths, etc use it? That is why I think Hyperscience is True Magick with a funny hat rather than real science or real technology.
            I think that each gameline runs on different "fuel" for magic.

            I don't know much about Vampire Disciplines, so I can't speak for it.
            But the Garou's powers come from spiritual empowerment, from the Umbra, from Gaia, and from the trinity. In many ways, the werewolf perspective is that Science is simply what humans interpret as the work of the Weaver, they do not question why things are consistent, they just accept they will be and leave it at that. Though any philosopher that has stated "God keeps the world consistent" is basically acknowledging the work of the Weaver.
            When Garou do magic, they call upon spiritual entities that have given them favour.
            Mages do the same, but the main difference is the method. Mages draw power from the avatar (or vice versa) in order to WANT things into being. The Garou use the relationship they have with the spirit world to FEEL them into being, be it through rage, piety, promises, or simply hope.
            Yes, there are many Mages who would say they use Emotion of Feelings as their focus, but the difference is that they still need to believe it works, they still need their will to guide it. Garou do not, they just Feel it. They are their powers.

            The arcanoi of Wraith are also emotion based, but it's not the same as the Garou's empowered stance. The Wraith's powers are more about trying to hold on, about desperately clinging to what you have so you don't lose it or get lost yourself. Their powers are about trying to reclaim control of that which they have lost, stay focused and not lose their way in the twisting worlds of the Underworld. While the Mages's powers are fuelled by a form of drive and determination, and the Garous by a need to fight on for that which they need, the Wraiths are fuelled by loss, or rather, by a desire to reclaim what was once lost.
            The Wraith acts not to empower itself like the other two, but because they refuse to be without.

            So Mage's powers are forces of embodied Will, Garou's are forces of Nature's Empowerd Emotions, and Wraith's are the pure Need to Remain Alive.

            The Changeling, on the other hand, have their magic from the emotion of whimsy, theirs is a life built on the old world dreams, when anything and everything was possible. Banality harms them so because taking things are part and understanding their limitations goes against this prospect. Theirs is the magic which exists in the "could be" and the "try not to think about it too hard" school of thought.
            The fuzzier the logic, and the greater the potential, the more power they are able to use. Theirs is the world of mystery and enchantment.

                                                            About Changellings vs Mages:

                                                            Mind 1 can protect Mages against the Mists. Just read the description of Mind 1.

                                                            Mind 2 could generate glamour OR banality, creating a kind of "creativity aura" or "dull aura". Most Mages don't know much of faeries to know that, they would probably do such things by accident, as an indirect action learnt true attempt-and-mistake (so, I've made an aura of dullness around me; those *faeries* or whatever they were runned away, cursing me). This would be an indetect effect; a Mage with profound knowledge of the kithain and the Dreaming however, could created glamour or banality directly, meaning that each success on Arete would create one temporary banality or glamour. The necessary spheres for that can be debatable - one could say you only need Mind 2; others could argue that Glamour and Banality are not just psychic emanations, but they are psychic emanations turned into a form of Ether; in that case, the Mage would need Mind 2 to generate the psychic emanations, Prime 4 to turn them into the "quintessence" (by this view, Changellings and chimeras are a sort of periapts that transform psychic emanations into quintessence - and banality and glamour would BOTH be quintessence, of diametral opposed ressonance). Others can say that Prime 2 is enough to generate the right ressonance to be "condensated" into banality or glamour, so you only need Prime 4 to generate Glamour or Banality. Mind 2 can still alter the banality/glamour of an area.

                                                            Mind 3 can be used to "enchant" anyone, both the Mage himself and others. Oh yes, and Mind 1 can already perceive chimeras around.

                                                            With Spirit 1, you can see the creature of the Dreaming and the fae mien of changellings.

                                                            Mind 3 can create chimeras; with Prime 2, you can turn them permanent.

                                                            With Mind 3, you can enter the Near Dreaming in Astral projection, tough that will be extremely dangerous if you don't know exactly what you're doing

                                                            With Spirit 3, you can enter the Near Dreaming in physical form.

                                                            With Spirit 4, you can "Call the Wyrd" yourself.

                                                            For any of that, all you need is the knowledge of the Dreaming. For example: just turning your spiritual senses with spirit 1, will make you see the Penumbra. You need to know what you're looking for to be able to "see fairies".
