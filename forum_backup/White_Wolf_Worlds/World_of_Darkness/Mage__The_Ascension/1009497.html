    The Traditions in Charge

    11-28-2016, 05:59 PM
    I had an idea for a Mage setting I was hoping to get some help on.

    In the wake of 9/11 (totally sleeper based) a powerful Technocratic Cabal was destroyed and the Technocracy lost a primary power base. A few of the most numerous Traditions (the Order of Hermes, Virtual Adepts, Chorus, and Akashic Brotherhood) in New York City was able to get an alliance to oust the Technocracy in the city.

    The Traditions have been able to hold parts of New York City since but their hold is tenuous at best. As the alliance starts to crack because of ideological differences, the Technocracy is starting to make headway against them.

    The game starts as the Traditions begin to lose ground and the Tradition leadership becoming more draconian.

    Is this possible? Any ideas of how to make this work? How the Traditions could hold the city for so long? How the area would be affected by Tradition magic in charge?

Despite the fact that Brucato maintains that "Mage is not a city-based game" it can easiy work as one, particularly if leaving the city is a HUGE deal. Like "Escape from New York" level deal.

                                                  Hence my scepticism regarding New York. The city *is* probably divided into a lot of smaller reality zone groupings along the lines of Irrationality Zones, haunted spots, the nature of certain areas and spots at night, but..changing NYC into a primal reality zone?                                                   That said - I *have* pondered one way to actually put a HUGE dent into reality in favor of willworking and miracles in NYC.
                                                  Armos mentioned that the whole scenario is more or less in the wake of 9/11. The whole world's eyes was on NYC and Ground Zero.

                                                  What if suddenly the towers had been rebuilt, live on TV, in a Miracle?

                                                  That alone could make for an intense Mage campaign and what-if setting. Consider the stakes: Unlike the Week of Nightmares, this location was square and center of every live news broadcast in the west for weeks. A chance for the Traditions to get straight, realtime access to countless people through the Technocracy's own ways of information and propaganda. And even a few seconds of broadcast are enough, shuld the Technocray pull all the plugs - if they can in time.

                                                  Now, the risk is insanely high: Rebuilding everything in front of everybody, in the course of a few moments, would take an insane amount of successes, and even more preparation to ward of insane amounts of Paradox Backlash along the lines of the Czar Vargo incident.
                                                  Even more so, most of the preparations couldn't be done on-site because of sleeper and Technocratic security everywhere, so it'd need a plan for preparing big-time in advance, then infiltrating an area dripping with tension and vigilance, and suddenly performing the last part of that great ritual. Extraction is another can of worms entirely, but this act alone could have been a major, major turning point in the Ascension War. Insane risk, potential but not guaranteed insane reward.

                                                          There are a lot of positives for the Traditions in seizing all of NYC from the Technocracy. In one blow they crack the Syndicate's global infrastructure and the NWO's media network. They open one of the biggest travel locations as a melting pot of ideas and cultures. The subsequent crash devastates many parts of the world, giving the Trads an opportunity to step in and show alternate ways of living. It's a bold move that has two major drawbacks:

                                                          1) The reprisals from the Technocracy would be massive.
                                                          2) They would have to ruin the lives of a lot of sleepers to do it.

                                                          The first is bad, the second is worse. Some Trad members would be okay with that kind of collateral damage, many would not.

                                                          On the other hand, there is an occupation of NYC that is even more effective for the Traditions. The Technocracy gets to keep their infrastructure, but the Traditions hold the rest of the city. This leaves much of the Technocracy infrastructure intact, but that infrastructure can be monitored and sometimes even used by the Traditions. By not isolating the city, they give their new reality machinations a chance to spread.
