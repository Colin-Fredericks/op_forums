    All out war : Technocracy against Vampire

    09-07-2016, 10:10 AM
    It is war of the extermination. Without mercy, without compassion, without remorse. A dual to the death. Think Total Annihilation.

    1. Technocracy's object of mission is complete and utter eradication of the Leeches.

    2. Vampire's object of mission is survival at all cost.

    3. Maintaining masquerade and kine/sleepers collateral damage is no concern of the both sides.

    4. Thanks to global shattering of masquerade, Paradox effects are dramatically lessened; now even the most ignorant and skeptical sleepers know and accept existence of the supernatural world. Consequently Void Engineers could operate on Earth with impunity - though they could not bring their (most of) Universal vessels on the earthy sky.

    5. Both sides will utilize every tool at their disposal and that is not just limited to martial power - money, corporations, control of economy, political clout, social influence, mass media, the internet, sleeper governments, law enforcement agencies, military, and other myriad powers will be fully brought into war.

    6. Both sides will use and exploit kine/sleepers and earthly assets to the utmost degree. Petty conscience and war crimes are no concern of the combatants; Technocracy will push conscripted sleeper soldiers into meet-grinder that is devastated cities without hesitation, and nuke said cities from orbit or raze it through other methods when absolutely necessary. On the other hand, Vampires will commence mass ghouling/embracing/blood bonding blood cattle with frantic speed.

    7. Other greater powers, such as Traditions, Mad Ones, Garou and Pentex, will not be involved. Technocracy and Tradition call a truce, while Garou and Pentex are busy killing each other, as always.

    8. Oracles and Antediluvian will not intervene worldly affairs.

    What do you think will be the outcome of the conflict? How the said conflict will affect mundane and supernatural worlds?
