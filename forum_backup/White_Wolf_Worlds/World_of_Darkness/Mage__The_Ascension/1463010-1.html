                            Putting "Virtue Signaling" aside, the Disparates don't really add diversity. Even the most seemingly West-centric Trad are shown to be multicultural. The Hermetics are shown to have had members from multiple different cultures all held together by their mystic focus on High Ritual. The Dreamspeakers and Verbena have members in every culture on the planet. We were shown Sons of Ether who were Native Brazilians from the Amazon rainforest. On the contrary, it's the Disparates who have single culture groups. An Hermetic can be a scholar from anywhere. A member of the Ali Batini has to be an Arab or at least from the Arab influenced cultural area that runs from Morocco to Pakistan (yes I know they don't all speak Arabic, I focused on Arab cultural influence on a profound level). That one European, who totally immersed himself in Arab culture was accepted was a big deal. The Wu Lung are explicitly and proudly racist. Even groups like the Ngoma and the Polynesian group (whose name I can't spell) while not bigoted don't accept actual outsiders. Contrast that with both the Traditions and the Union who proudly embrace all Humanity. The Union may be dangerous fools but they understand racism is evil.

                            In the end, the Disparates aren't a group that can achieve cohesion. Only a few of the groups like the Children of Knowledge and the Ngoma get the value of alliance with others. Most, like the Ali Batini and the Wu Lung, can only see value of using others. Or like the Hollow Ones, the Sisters of Hypolita, and the Taftani, they simply wish to shout at others.
