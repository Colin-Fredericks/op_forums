                        I see the Technocracy as having a grand strategy, and what they do is based on operations that fit that strategy. They don't deviate from it unless a large enough threat develops that they must deal with it.

                        So that level 5 Pit is ignored unless it starts threatening critical things like overall belief in the supernatural or an important asset. They rely on the normal opponents of their enemies to deal with that. So if a level 5 Pit gets frisky with the locals, they might arrange for some information to be handed over to Gaian Garou surreptitiously so they can be dealt with if it doesn't look like the Garou are already planning on doing something about it.

                        But if the threat level increases and the foe's natural enemies can't handle it, then the Technocracy eliminates the threat. But that always come at a cost as they lose resources, assets, and delay other operations as a result.

                        I see the Technocracy as being very powerful, but most of their power is used in only two things. The first is maintaining what they already have and protecting it from attack. The second are reserves saved for truly awful and unexpected threats like Nephandi raising Cthulhu. In terms of what is available for offense, it is limited and is deployed according to plans already made.
