    My initial read of the RAW was that Archspheres were nixed, and I even stated as much to my group. But the other day I discovered the part mentioned in this discussion, where they technically might exists but it is suggested to avoid them. M20 does a lot of that. It *almost* makes a command decision on several things, proposes a new way to do something and states it is now the standard presumption, but then includes a small line or two about how to do it the other way anyways. I *do* like the flexibility, but it takes up space and causes confusion. Still, I would rather have it than not have it.

    So I suppose Arch-Spheres are to be treated as a concept on the background as a potential possibility. As GM tool for world building, intended for NPC's, they do not need in-depth definition. So if the subject ever does come up, I am just gonna stick with ranks six and seven. Six allows you to do those things that were just beyond your reach with standard magic rules and limits. Seven is some omega level shit that you can just fiat.

    Horizon had an Arch Sphere system that went up to seven,and that is the way it works in Awakening.

            It's not just about not liking high power levels; it's also that the five Sphere levels in the core book are already deep into the “high power level” tier, and doesn't leave much if any room for the sixth dot to mean anything. To the extent that Brucato accepts that a sixth dot should be allowed in the game at all, the philosophy appears to be that it should be nearly impossible to attain, and should largely be defined in terms of what the Storyteller wishes to forbid mages from doing.

            And if you don't want to put any upper limits on what mages can do, you don't need the Archspheres; just allow everything to be done with five dots or less. Remember, Magickal Feats include Outlandish and God-like Feats, too.

            As I recall, Horizon: Stronghold of Hope was written with this mindset. By contrast, Masters of the Art was written with the notion that there ought to be nine dots in every Sphere, and that player characters ought to be able to continue the progression all the way up to 9 in the same way they progressed from 1 to 5: get a sufficiently high Arete and spend 7× or 8× the new rank in the Sphere.

            My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

                I didn't like the archspheres as written so I made the following as my version of archspheres, more to figure out what an archmage game might be like than anything else.

                Correspondence
                6: Correspondence can be used to access unconnected worlds and realms of existence
                7: Wards, environmental effects, and other similar hindrances to perception or travel can be ignored
                8: The Mage can perceives & reach everything in the same world or realm of existence
                9: All is one. Obstacles and barriers are suggestions
                Entropy
                6: Entropy can be frozen or reversed. Fate may be predetermined and sealed
                7: Things can be removed from fate (retroactively annihilated, severed from history, or made “Arcane”)
                8: Things like destinies & binding oaths may be reversed, redirected, transferred, altered, or removed
                9: The irreversible can be reversed, irresistible resisted, immovable moved, and unbreakable broken
                Forces
                6: New kinds of minor forces may be invented that directly affect Correspondence, Life or Matter
                7: New kinds of minor forces may be invented that directly affect Spirit or Mind
                8: New kinds of minor forces may be invented that directly affect Entropy, Prime, or Time
                9: New kinds of major forces may be invented that can affect any of the Spheres
                Life
                6: Reality defying creatures of legend with and their qualities become accessible to the mage without need for other spheres
                7: Dynamic systems of Spirit, Force or Matter can be treated as life
                8: Anything that can grow, change, evolve, or die off can be treated as life
                9: All of reality is alive in a way and falls under the purview of the Life sphere
                Matter
                6: Forces and Life can be treated as Matter
                7: Correspondence, Prime & Spirit can be treated as Matter
                8: Mind, Entropy & Time can be treated as Matter
                9: All of reality can be treated as Matter
                Mind
                6: The universal subconscious becomes a valid target for Mind
                7: Thoughts can be converted into reality
                8: Real things can be made imaginary
                9: Everything is information contained within a single supreme consciousness
                Prime
                6: Paradox can be predicted, transferred, and manipulated
                7: The Mage can perceive & understand all supernatural powers and can suppress, redirect, or counter these
                8: Quintessence can be created from nothing. Personal Quintessence can be stolen
                9: The laws of magic can be bent, broken, appended or rewritten
                Spirit
                6: New Souls and Spirits up to the level of a Seraphim, Praeceptor, or Demigod can be easily created or remade (with 1 success)
                7: An Avatar can be created and Awakening can be forced or reversed.
                8: The Mage can easily make or become a spiritual incarnation of a concept or thing, like a Totem, Archangel, or Demon lord
                9: The Mage can easily make or become something just short of the Essential Divinity, equivalent to a Godhead or Celestine
                Time
                6: The mage can access the Coming or Previous “Age” such as one of the Impossible Futures or Mythic Ages
                7: The mage can access parallel timelines, difficulty based on how far back the divergence was
                8: The mage can reshape an entire timeline, rewriting events therein, connecting it to a desired future, or even looping it
                9: The mage can access and reshape all of time of all ages and realms

                Its a bit off in points, looking at it now, but easily "corrected", not that I found much point in doing so after a bit of hypothetical use.

                I soon decided that old replaced timelines branched off every time new ones were made so sometimes an archmage seemed to simply vanish when desiring to live in a different timeline.

                These days I'd just rather that each dot above 5 lowered the difficulty of use of that sphere by 1 or something like that.
