                    Err no. Low Light vision is not a rote. QUANTIFY FORCES. Is a rote. I'm not pulling that out of my ass. It's literally right there in the book as the only level 1 forces rote. And it explicitly detects every kind of energy including kinetic energy in the area it's used on. To a mage, electricity and heat are just two seperate aspects of a universal energy.

                    Err no. It's slightly time consuming. You build the production facility which is a wonder in it's own right, admittedly requiring some setup time. And then it really is insert tass, push button, get wonder. Again, if you look at the rules for wonder creation and wonder use, this is how they interact. The primary limit is how much tass you can obtain and the actual mundane crafting of the shells. The quickening itself is ludicrously trivial with the aid of wonders.

                    At the levels of prime we're talking, the resonance of a particular batch of tass is irrelevant. You break tass of the wrong resonance down into free quintessence. Then remake it in whichever form is required. Prime 4 allows the creation of tass using quintessence.

                    Literally, how the heck would an EMP stop something that doesn't work on electrical principles? You could try and countermagick it, and that would certainly be attempted. But they would have to countermagick each and every individual wonder. You're correct in saying they don't give you free resources, but spheres can GET you resources. the time the hermetic would require to hunt down tass of the appropriate type is sidestepped by machines that simply remake it. Sources of quintessence can be made. Whether as simple as drawing electricity from the power grid into a capacitor and breaking it down into quintessence and then converting it into tass for use in machining new devices. Or as complex as creating a wonder with an internal periapt that uses the font of heaven rote to fill itself with quintessence, before dumping that quintessence into the stores of a nearby manufacturing wonder.

                    Spheres grant options for gaining resources. Therefor something that let's you grab those sphere levels faster DOES in fact grant you more resources.
