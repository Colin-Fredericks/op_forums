    Preparing for my first time as a Mage Player, advice on Focus and Magick

    08-02-2017, 05:49 PM
    So I've STed a one-shot Mage game that was very rules lite, but this is the first time I'll be playing a Mage. I have two concepts in mind, but I want to ask how people deal with the Foci and magickal practises.

    I'm very keen on not just listing off Sphere Effects as though I can automatically do all of them. It feels antithetical to the point of having Paradigm and belief-based magick. So I wanted to ask how people would approach using the Spheres with my two concepts.

    Concept #1 is a Virtual Adept with the Everything is Data Paradigm (ground-breaking, I know). He has Reality Hacking as his Method (reasonable combination to say the least) and his tools are as follows:

    - Books and Periodicals (magazines and textbooks relating to coding, computer technology, anything relevant to hardware or software)

    - Computer Gear (a few external hard drives, some data-handling equipment, an audio system)

    - Meditation

    - Brain/Computer Interface (home-made neural net built from cast-offs at his workplace)

    - Brews and Concoctions (caffeinated beverages)

    - Voice and Vocalizations (voice command of tech)

    - Toys (fidget-related paraphernalia, anything you can play around with while thinking)

    Affinity Sphere is Correspondence, and I want him to develop Correspondence, Mind and Prime. I don't really feel like the other Spheres are good fits for him. So, with all of that out of the way, if I wanted to perform some typical feats of those three spheres:

    - Teleporting myself somewhere
    - Creating a portal between two locations
    - Scanning a large geographical area for a specific "term"
    - Erasing someone's memories
    - Fortifying my mental faculties
    - Creating mental illusions
    - Creating a Wonder
    - Getting rid of Paradox
    - Harvesting Quintessence

    How would I go about this? I have some ideas already (brain-computer interface, change the spacial coordinates of my "file" in the matrix of reality, a coffee-based ritual that restores Willpower, focusing on multiple tasks simultaneously by using multiple monitors as a focus) but I feel like I need some inspiration to really make it shine... the last thing I want is to pause and drag out the magickal process while I try and come up with a method that sounds half-decent.

    Concept #2 is a Cultist of Ecstasy with the Creation's Divine and Alive Paradigm, with Chaos Magick as her Method.

    - Meditation

    - Voice and Vocalizations (Singing)

    - Art (Sketching, photography)

    - Dance

    - Food and Drink (Alcohol, consumption and preparation)

    - Music (playing the violin, putting on music)

    - Sex and Sensuality

    Affinity Sphere is either Forces, Life or Correspondence for the flow of the dance, which is a central feature with her... hard to choose. BUT I want her to have 1 dot in all but one Sphere at character generation (skipping Spirit since it goes beyond my interests for this concept)... I know that this seriously limits her capabilities at first, but I love the idea of her as a creature based about the sensory and the sensual; she's listening to the rhythm of creation, learning and moving with the beat... her solo (Spheres at 2+) comes later.

    With that in mind...

    - Predicting the future
    - Teleporting
    - Creating a portal
    - Creating a Wonder
    - Slowing down time
    - Reading someone's mind
    - Boosting her mental faculties
    - Assessing someone's health
    - Finding the flaw in something
    - Creating a Forces attack
    - Transmuting something with Matter
    - Eliminating Paradox
    - Absorbing Quintessence
    - Shapeshifting
    - Healing
    - Changing memories

    I do already have some ideas for these, but they're a tad vague. I don't want her dancing for everything, or randomly humming to make effects! I want everything to feel... right. Any ideas?

    Finally, a question about Tools; we have a list of 7 tools, but do we have to use all of them? Is there anything stopping a Celestial Chorister from performing every effect they want using Prayers and Invocations for example? What does the list of 7 actually mean in real terms?

      Virtual Adept: Correspondence 3 does that scanning and teleportation. Correspondence 4 is needed for portals. Mind 3 or 4 are needed for the Mind effects, probably Mind 4 unless you're just doing a temporary 'forget what you just saw, these aren't the hackers you are looking for' effect which might even be Mind 2. Serious Mind manipulation is typically Mind 4, but simple illusions should be Mind 3 if they are in someone's head. Prime 3 is channeling Quintessence and creating things and Prime 4 are where you get into artifact creation at a serious level. You need at least Prime 2 to create any kind of Wonder. Prime 5 can use Quintessence to destroy Paradox, but you might be better bleeding it off over time or entering Quiet--after all if everything is Data your mage just hacks their disconnect with reality through Meditation and drops some Paradox. This character shouldn't be hard to grasp in most chronicles, but at starting point totals, you'll have to decide which tricks to do less often. If you have the standard build rules, I'd do Correspondence 3, Mind 2 and Prime 2. That would allow for subtle Mind effects, creating some weak wonders for Correspondence or Mind and teleportation and scanning multiple areas with Correspondence. Depending on the ST, you might use Data instead of Correspondence, since that's the Technocracy equivalent and your VA fits how data is used.

      Cultist: Many spheres at 1 gives you perception of many things and sphere on sphere countermagic. Correspondence 2 is primarily for scrying and counterscrying, but could allow an understanding of the room useful to someone who moves. Forces 2 or Life 2 tend to do more. Forces 2 lets you do minor force manipulations--turn up or down lights and control energy of motion or sound. Life 2 allows creation of simple life forms, self-healing and probably can help entering meditation. Life 2 also lets you use other spheres on your own body conjunctionally.

      The 7 tools method is new to mage 20. Essentially, as a mage, you need to focus your will. Having a paradigm, method and tools makes it a lot easier to roleplay how your mage does their magick. While prayers and invocations work for a lot of things, there are many types of prayers and many religions have offerings and sacrifices. While game rules say you have to have 7 tools and get to avoid use tools once sufficiently enlightened (thus reducing difficulty if you use a tool you don't need), a Chorister could do everything by prayer or an Akashic could do everything using Do if it's fine with your group. 7 tools is a finite group of foci, but large enough to do different practices.

      For example, I have been working up an Order of Hermes chantry leader, Devon Ian Rae, PhD. He's basically an environmental studies professor (environmental history as the advanced degree and undergraduate knowledge of biological science) who keeps an eye on what is taught at universities so that the right students can be recruited to the Traditions and the dangerous ones can be put on the watch list. His paradigm is 'Turning the Keys To Reality,' which basically is a Deist or Foundationist from B5 conception that God created a complex multilevel universe but those who work hard to study the work can find the ways reality works and transform it so it gets everyone closer to Ascension and eventually to Ascension. Since it's hard to have just one practice, he has 3: Dominion, High Ritual Magick and Yoga. Dominion fits the traditional Hermetic social control and he's in places to control people and aid them and eye contact (see the anthropologist Clifford Geertz for the importance of winks), Group Rites (bind members together) and Voice can be used to get to know someone and to subtly influence behavior. These work nicely for Correspondence and Mind.

      Celestial alignment, languages, offerings and sacrifices and writing and inscription are for High Ritual Magic. These can work for Correspondence and Mind, but are primarily used for Forces, Prime and Spirit (only 1 rank in Spirit so he can see spirits but do little except holler in Enochian right now). Forces are the energies of the universe, so these are brought forth by offerings, by using the names such as a doggerel 'let there be light to smite' in Sanskrit and other uses that are old-school Hermetic and thus likely vulgar. For other Forces, gutter magic might pop up depending on PCs in the Chronicle, as Dr. Rae has a good knowledge of Science and can certainly Science b.s. an exploding gas main. Yoga is for Life and Mind personal effects, making life 2 and Mind usable. Essentially, Dr. Rae is getting in touch with the universe through Yoga and improving his body performance. With Life and Mind, he could also suggest others using Yoga to calm themselves or to focus.

      As for tools, consider what other players in the group are playing. Mages, even those who are not the most brilliant from an intelligence perspective are learners and dreamers. A mage won't necessarily grasp a completely different paradigm and be able to use it, but if a Virtual Adept and a Chorister and a Cultist of Ectasy all have mediation as a focus, they might be able to help each other think of how to mediate more effectively. The VA could space out on a video game, clearing the mind so they just play the game. The Chorister could pray to the One for guidance, asking for help being faithful. The Cultist who dances could dance a dervish dance, letting them focus on their motion and the flow of the dance instead of how many people are in the audience or how the dance troupe's finances stand.

      I hope the Chronicle goes well.

          As for the Affinity sphere for the Cultist, whatever you want to do most is the best for the affinity sphere. In Mage 1e, you didn't get a choice about affinity sphere. Now that you do, if you have a Cultist mostly concerned about light and motion and sound, Forces would be your affinity. Time is the traditional Cult of Ecstasy affinity sphere and if you want to see the future or past, that's only level 2 for basic operations. The Life sphere is for healing and boosts and the shapeshifting and you need Life 4 or Life 5 for serious shapeshifting, so that might be worth having. Again, since it's a group, if playing the Cult of Ecstasy dancer, who will have a lot in several spheres, see what areas the others are specializing in and you can fill the voids.

                  Hmm so I suppose I could come up with a distinct theme for how she does each of the Spheres.

                  Forces: Dancing for most, and Singing for sound-based stuff. So if she was creating fire to attack someone she could focus her will through movement, dodging and weaving as an inferno rises from her footsteps... if she was deafening someone she could hum a folk tune...

                  Life: Sensuality; sex, food, massage. She could heal herself by softly running her hands over her skin, or fortify herself with a swig from a hip flask.

                  Correspondence: Sketching and Meditation. She could map out the city in a trance or sketch something she wants... only to wake up with it sitting where the page was. If combining with other Spheres, Sketching is her go-to. Draw a vivid scene of that pesky Vampire bursting into flames at midday, and before you know it...

                  Matter: Singing. A soft hum makes everything resonate in her mind's eye, and reveals the properties of the material world. A shout can shatter stone or turn cloth to glass.

                  Time: Creating art while listening to classical music for predictions, Dancing for slowing down or speeding up time.

                  Prime: For Quintessence and Paradox, Meditation within an installation of art created specifically for that moment, ideally. For creating Wonders, an extended artistic process focused more on the other Sphere(s) than Prime.

                  Entropy: Defacing something with "gutter" art for cursing. For examining, determining flaws, measuring probability, she looks at something and writes a stream of consciousness in her notepad, somewhere between prose and a report. For altering probability she writes about possible outcomes and places emphasis on the ones she wants (or doesn't want). The power is in the flow of language, poetry, art.

                  Mind: For personal effects, Meditation. For reading others her ideal method is observing their artistry; watching them seduce, paint, joke, fight, dance... they become an open book. For controlling others, she simply applies whatever artistry she can in that situation.

                      It might be cool if he did his Meditation with the help of a Brain/Computer Interface. This gives more variety of description to a Focus which tends to resist descriptions. Also maybe he could do computer programming as a kind of ritual prayer, to whatever entities he believes programmed our reality--higher-dimension beings, future humans, spontaneously self-generated AI, etc. Reality Hacking is more of a mystic paradigm than a technomantic one. The heart of mysticism is the belief that the material world is an illusion. You really want to flesh out what he thinks are the nature and causes of the virtual world he believes we live in.

                      Anyway.

                      I'll go through your Effects list and give my suggestions with reference to your tools list. Keep in mind with reality hacking you can really just say "I computer it" for any magical effect. It's a very versatile paradigm, somewhat balanced by its usually being as Vulgar as any mystic paradigm.

                      Teleporting myself somewhere
                      -Accessing the source code of some kind of mapping software, or satellite data directly.
                      -Using photos of a location and blending them into a virtual environment, and focusing on that until his experience becomes reality.

                      Creating a portal between two locations
                      -This is a pretty big change to the fundamental source code of the universe itself. Before the very meticulous programming, make sure you perform the right ritual actions that your purity may please the God Swarm.
                      -Rather than traditional programming, pray to the High Programmers directly. Preferably in a recording studio with good equipment. Kind of the opposite of voice commands.

                      Scanning a large geographical area for a specific "term"
                      -See the first part of my Teleporting response.
                      -Maybe he's like Mona from Mona Lisa Overdrive and has direct intuition about the nature of the Matrix, and simply by focusing his mind with brews and concoctions he can get insights like this.

                      Erasing someone's memories
                      Depends on how you view thought. Is it a programmed illusion below even the illusion that is physical reality, or something more real than physical reality, perhaps originating in the level of reality that created this one?
                      -If the first, perhaps modifying your brain-computer interface to be used offensively, perhaps at a range of a few meters. Once you've captured someone this way, it is a simple matter to remove certain memories.
                      -Alternately, using the brain-computer interface to do a temporary mind meld; through sheer mental discipline, you absorb and immediately "forget" the target's memories before releasing the target.

                      Fortifying my mental faculties
                      -Brews and Concoctions. Have you heard of nootropics? A lot of overlap with software designer types. If you haven't already, do enough research to give your character enough vocabulary to give his concoctions some variety.
                      -Books and Periodicals, but in a more mindful meditative way than just the usual reading. Or maybe a virtual program that flashes a bunch of information too fast for your conscious mind to process, but strengthens your mind on a deeper level.
                      -Use of fidget objects. In the hands of someone with a bit of imagination, everything is a fidget object.

                      Creating mental illusions
                      See "Erasing Someone's Memories." Except instead of absorbing memories you are foisting your own visualizations on the target. After all, it is all part of the thought-cloud that generates the universe.

                      Creating a Wonder
                      -First, build the thing in a virtual world, using whatever fantastical equipment and materials available there. Then, 3D print the thing. Now the programming and prayer/meditation begin.
                      -Using the Internet of Things, invest normal technological objects with programming from realms beyond and power from your own Awakened Will.

                      Getting rid of Paradox
                      -Performing tasks that reinforce the Will of the High Programmer.
                      -Vocalizing in a ritual manner statistics about the reality you have disrupted, as a way of appeasement. Mathematical formulae, chemical compositions, etc.

                      Harvesting Quintessence
                      -Directly tapping into the dimension of the Creators, through deep meditation. Use a virtual environment that surrounds you with stimuli that free you from the rules of base reality, and allow you to touch the deeper truth. Abstract images, a sense of weightlessness, etc.
                      -Running electrical current through your body. Not in a harmful way. Just medical-grade electrodes all over.
