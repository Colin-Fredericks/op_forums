            Dimensional Science 2

            •• Dimensional Vibration/Modify Dimensional Gauntlet/Transdimensional Field
            At this level of expertise, the specialist can use radiation pulses, hypermath, and samples of transdimensional matter to grant access to those alternate dimensions. With the correct instruments, that specialist can send out artificial ripples into nearby subdimensions in order to attract EDEs and leave signals for other Dimensional Specialists.

            Thanks to the hypermathematical models and recordings involved in Dimensional Science, a specialist can also manipulate the Gauntlet, raising or lowering it, even as low as 0 – a feat mystic mages cannot duplicate. The Gauntlet 0 Effect lasts for only one scene, but it counters the punishment of the Dimensional Anomaly (that is, the Avatar Storm) if that phenomenon is still in play. However, the specialist herself still suffers feedback damage as she works the Effect. (In game terms, she takes the usual Avatar Storm damage, though she prevents other characters from taking it during that scene.)

            On a related note, the specialist learns to also surround herself (or, with the appropriate Spheres, other people or objects) with a transdimensional field; this, in turn, allows her to interact with subdimensions on a limited level (brief touch) without suffering harm from the Dimensional Anomaly
            The stated abilities of Dimensional Science 2 are:

            Reach Across Gauntlet (Manipulate Gauntlet)

            When it comes to acting across the gauntlet, has the same main capabilities as Spirit in that a specialist can speak to or touch things on the other side.
            Dimensional Science is also explicit that it is capable of calling or luring spirits that are not immediately present on the other side of the gauntlet.

            Added Benefit

            If the Avatar Storm Dimensional Anomaly is involved, Dimensional Science specialists can block damage from it when reaching across the gauntlet.


            Gauntlet Thickness (Manipulate Gauntlet)

            Added Benefit

            Not only can Dimensional Science lower the gauntlet, it is explicitly better at it than Spirit. Dimensional Science can lower it all the way down to 0. This can be used to bypass the Dimensional Anomaly, though the one who actually lowers it still takes the damage in the teeth.

            If there are any limits, such as that a person cannot leave the affected area on the Extra Dimensional side, it is not stated here. This means that taking people across the gauntlet is possible at a much earlier rank for Dim Sci than for Spirit.


            Dimensional Science does not mention these abilities, but given Base Dimensional Science it likely has them:


            Communicate With EDEs (Touch Spirit)

            Those specialists who actually consider trying to communicate with EDEs will be able to work out at least some method of translation with this rank.

            I mentioned in Spirit that since the way a spirit appears is at least partially a form of communication Spirit 2 might be able to change a spirit’s appearance to other thematically appropriate ones. I suspect Technocrats would be more likely to use this.


            Act on Spirit (Touch Spirit)


            It would be quite strange if Dimensional Science didn’t follow Spirit on this one. A specialist should be able to use this rank to touch and affect EDEs regardless of their state. This is also the ability to enable procedures to affect EDEs.


            Conjunctional Effects:

            None of the conjunctional effects of Spirit are mentioned in Dimensional Science, though we have to presume Dimensional Science is capable of its part in these, even if the other sphere is not.


            Imbue Object (??? + ???)

            In previous discussions it looks like this creates an object that can affect spirits. Dimensional Scientists would definitely see the utility of this. I'm sure they would describe it differently such as using special energies or forms of radiation rather than actual spirit energies, but the effect would be the same.


            Drain Essence From Spirit (Touch Spirit + Pull Quintessence From Pattern)

            It is not at all clear that a Technocrat can do this but if there is a deficiency it is in Primal Ventures, not Dimensional Science. Using Dimensional Science to allow spells to affect EDEs should carry over from Spirit.


            Extend EDE Senses to Others (??? + ???)

            While there would definitely need to be appropriate gear, Technocrats tend to think of sharing their effects more than Traditionalists do, so there's no reason DimSci wouldn't be able to do this.

                Dimensional Science 3

                ••• Dimensional Shift/Manipulate Paraphysical Phenomena/Phase Disruption Field
                Increased understanding allows the specialist to step sideways into alternate dimensions, manipulate the paraphysical matter (that is, ephemera) of those dimensions, and – with the correct weaponry – send out destructive phase disruption fields of vibrations that disorient, injure, or disintegrate EDEs. (In short, her attacks can harm spirit entities.) Thanks to the Stun/Kill/ Disintegrate settings on those weapons, she can choose whether to inflict bashing, lethal, or aggravated damage on Umbral targets… and although EDE’s experience all forms of damage the same way, dimension-hopping humans and Deviant werebeasts do not.
                There’s not much change at this rank, except that Dimensional Science spends no time on example conjunctional effects, and it clarifies something about direct damage spells that most spheres don’t mention.


                Step Sideways (Step Sideways)


                Oddly enough, the name doesn’t change even with the Technocratic focus. Although, I suppose that’s not really surprising as they are viewed as subdimensions that exist alongside the physical.


                Harm Paraphysical Things (???)


                Dimensional Science is a little more informative than Spirit on this. First, it establishes that the ability can cause damage types other than Aggravated. Second, it establishes that it is not limited to targeting spirits.
                Now this does raise the question of when it can target things other than spirits and the wording leaves open a reasonable interpretation that it is essentially targeting things made of ephemera. That is, it can target anyone who has stepped sideways and been converted into ephemera.


                Manipulate Ephemera (???)

                Not a lot is given on what this means, but specialists can manipulate the substance of alternate dimensions. In this case it almost certainly has whatever ability Spirit has, with little to no variation.


                Most of the conjunctional effects from Spirit 3 needed resolution and there’s nothing in Dimensional Science 3 that indicates it works differently than Spirit 3 in this regard, so I’m not going to copy them.


                Dimensional Science 4

                •••• Alter Dimensional Topography/Dimensional Gateway/Stabilize Dimensional Field

                Increased understanding and appropriate technologies help the specialist make lasting changes to alternate dimensions. Reworking the paraphysical constraints of a given space, she may craft pocket domains and dimensional walls in order to block or catch EDEs. A skilled specialist can do this even from the Earthly side of the Gauntlet, thus avoiding the effects of the Dimensional Anomaly. Using similar techniques, the specialist can also carve out rapid-transit paths; in game terms, this Effect cuts down the specialist’s travel time within the Otherworlds by (normal travel time divided by successes +1).

                The specialist can also open large gateways into the subdimensions, although the people passing through those gates suffer the effects of the Anomaly unless the Gauntlet has already been lowered to 0 by a Dimension Science 2 Procedure.

                Channeling Primal Energy (Quintessence) through appropriate technology, the specialist can also strengthen the Enlightened Anthropic Principle enough to prevent Void Adaptation (that is, disconnection and disembodiment; see Disembodiment in Chapter Four, pp. 88-89; and Disconnection and Disembodiment in Chapter Nine, p. 483.) Each point of Quintessence allows one character to resist Void Adaptation for one week.
                Things get really interesting here. Dimensional Science 4 puts its focus in different places than Spirit 4. The one ability they both mention is:

                Gauntlet Gateway (Rend Gauntlet)

                This pretty much functions exactly like the Spirit 4 variety. Most of the different word count focuses on the Dimensional Anomaly.


                Dimensional Science 4 also lists a few abilities that Spirit 4 does not. These are not listed as unique to DimSci so presumably Spirit 4 can do them as well.


                Manipulate Extra Dimensional Topography (Alter Dimensional Topography)

                While this is described in terms of how it affects spirits, it clearly focuses less on manipulating EDEs and more on manipulating the way other dimensions work. Indeed, one application is simply reducing the travel time of otherworld travel.

                Create Pocket Dimensions or Dimensional Walls (Alter Dimensional Topography)

                These may or may not be separate powers. They primarily deal with blocking spirits, so this may just be DimSci’s method of binding. Nevertheless the wording implies more. A specialist may even be able to create small ED realms, though where these would be is never stated.

                Prevent Void Adaptation ​

                Another Dimensional Anomaly specific power, specialists can put off Void Adaptation at the cost of Primal Energy. There's nothing here specifying this as unique to Dimensional Science.


                Meanwhile Dimensional Science mentions nothing about manipulating EDEs themselves, though we can presume it is still capable of it.


                Compel Spirits (Bind Spirit)


                A specialist should be just as capable of summoning, compelling, and binding EDEs as Spirit is. If it is not directly mentioned, it may just be because few Technocrats would do this in any traditional way. Combined with the previous abilities of creating dimensions to trap EDEs there is a very Ghostbuster quality to DimSci at this point.

                Create Fetish (Bind Spirit)

                It should be at least within the abilities of Dimensional Science 4 to create fetishes, even if isn’t a particularly technocratic approach.

                Exorcise Spirit

                This goes unmentioned but it would be unthinkable if Dimensional Science couldn’t separate and banish EDEs​

                    Void Adaptation isn't unique to the Dimensional Anomaly; it's always been the case that spending too much time in the Umbra presents an escalating risk of becoming a spirit. MRev leaned into it; but it didn't create the phenomenon. If I recall correctly, it didn't even change how it works; it just pointed out the logical consequences of the existing mechanics.

                    "We can presume that these DimSci things can also be done using Spirit"/"we can presume that these Spirit things can also be done using DimSci": I'm not going to rule it out; but that's not what the book says. It's silent on the matter, meaning that you can run it either way and still be fully compatible with what the book says. On the one hand, disallowing such overlap limits what both Spirit and DimSci can do, which I find problematic; but in the other hand, it helps make the two feel distinct from each other.

                                            Dimensional Science 5

                                            ••••• Anthropic Field/ Breach Spatial Horizon/ Cosmogenesis

                                            Utilizing the ultimate applications of Anthropic Principle Fields, the specialist can generate her own Anthropic Field (with the right instruments, of course) that allows her to venture beyond both Horizons and venture into the Deep Universe. By crafting a reality bubble around herself, that specialist can keep other Earthly beings alive with her as well (two passengers per additional success beyond the second) and can engineer and pilot vehicles for Deep Universe travel. For design and construction systems, see Inventing, Modifying, and Improving Technology in Chapter Nine, (pp. 463-464).

                                            Employing the arcane technologies of Cosmogenesis, that Master Specialist may also engineer and create Horizon Constructs and custom-designed and constructed EDE organisms. She must employ Primal Utility energies as well (often including Life and Matter if the EDE construct is to survive outside the subdimensions… that is, if it’s going to be more than simple ephemera), but these cosmic computations allow Void Engineers to harness powers not unlike those of legendary gods.
                                            Dimensional Science 5 can:


                                            Breach Horizon (Breach Spatial Horizon and Anthropic Field)

                                            Dimensional Science dedicates an entire paragraph to this power, and includes some details that Spirit left out, like what it takes to bring other people along and that vehicles can be made for this purpose. Considering that non-technocrats make ships for the Deep Umbra as well (most notably, but not exclusively, the SoE Etherjammers) these are definitely not rules exclusive to DimSci.


                                            Create Construct (Cosmogenesis)

                                            Much like with Spirit, there is a lot of possibility hidden in the words Horizon construct. The scope here could be anything from small zones of influence to full realms. Similarly, no mention of other sphere requirements (for realms) is given but is likely intended if other editions are considered. As written, though, it is unstated.


                                            Create or Destroy Spirit Matter (Forge Ephemera)

                                            In DimSci the creation of Ephemera is lumped in with the creation of realms. DimSci is even more clear than Spirit that this includes making spirits. It does contain an interesting line that if the creation is intended to survive outside of the ‘subdimensions’ it often requires Life or Matter.

                                            Ambiguity Resolution – Spirit or Other

                                            It’s a little unclear what this is referring to. It could mean that unless spirits are created with Matter or Life, they don’t function the same way when crossing the gauntlet that regular spirits do. That’s a strange interpretation, though it is within the given wording. I suspect this means that if the Operative wants the creation to be more than a Spirit he needs to include other spheres.


                                            Dimensional Science does not mention the following powers, but because it operates like Spirit we can presume it has them.

                                            Imbue/Drain Essence (???)

                                            A specialist can now pull or impart essence from or to an EDE directly rather than by damaging or (presumably) healing said spirit. Essence is not quintessence, but it can probably be turned into quintessence with the help of Prime.


                                            Create Soul (Forge Ephemera)

                                            It is possible that the ambiguous wording for creating EDEs was intended to include creating the transdimensional elements of living creatures that the overly poetic Traditions insist on calling souls. Whether or not this was intended, Dimensional Science should be able to accomplish it if the specialist even considers the need to do so. Gilgul (Gilgul)

                                            There’s no reason I can think of that Technocrats wouldn’t be able to do this, even if they’d have completely different terminology for how it works.


                                            Everything Else (???)

                                            As with all Rank 5s, DimSci 5 should be able to do just about everything possible within the Sphere’s purview. Unless the ST specifically wants something locked behind archspheres, it should be possible here.
