    Choosing Tradition

    03-01-2019, 06:34 PM
    Hi Which standards you normally follow to choose your character tradition? Do you go by her nature/demeanor/concept? Her practices and beliefs? Her character sheet and sphere dots?

    I am thinking which would be the best tradition to fit a character that follows those descriptions:

    He is(was) and engineer/scientist before awakening, who values his physical development as much as his mental. He is very determined and competitive, even a little bit egoistic and sometimes aggressive. Before awakening his goals were to perfect his body and his mind. He did that by working out and practicing martial arts to get the best of his physical abilities, and meditating/studying to keep his mind sharp, always eager for more knowledge.
    After his awake, one more goal was added to his list: to obtain more magickal power and knowledge as possible. Now he has three fronts, three aspects of his life that he wishes to develop.

    I had in mind some Order of Hermes house or Akashic. Going by his personality, I would say he's and hermetic. He doesn't care about any dogma or religion, he cares only for his improvement. Having said that, I would say one of those 3 houses: Tytalus, because of his strong will and desire to improve; Flambeau, because of his agressiveness and his practices (I'll cover that later); or Bonisagus, because of his thirst for knowledge and magickal power.

    Now, going by his practices, he`d fit more an akashic, since most of he would use martial arts and psyionics as practices, and most of his instruments would be about his body or mind (gestures, meditation, energy, etc.), but he definitely isn't asian or cares for anything they teach and follows. His selfishness wouldn't go well in this tradition.

    By his concept he could be an etherite because of his scientific past, but I think this is not enough.

    What you guys think? Tell me if more info is needed. Also, feel free to post examples of how you decide your tradition.

      Concept it's the key. While it hasn't any mechanical advantage I think that Concept it's one of the most important lines of the Character Sheet as it's basically asking the player to describe in a short line what he wants to play. Everything else aligns either alone or with a little shove.

      With that character and aspirations he would make a great Tytalus, but if SCIENCE! it's his call theres no reason whatsoever to think that an Etherite can't care about self perfection more than anything else: In fact, I invite you to read, if you have it, the writeup of "Invigoration" in Book of Secrets. It's a primarily Etherite Practice, practiced by luminaries like Doc Eon (a great martial artist) and it's basically your character. In a short resume: It's a Practice based arround the idea that both the body and the mind have a hidden potential that can be unlocked to do incredible, seemingly superhumaan, stuff.

                                                          Originally posted by Lord Revan View Post
                                                          I see. I thought the houses and traditions would be more restrict about tje mage beliefs and foci, I think.
                                                          Not so much. In general, a Tradition's philosophy, beliefs and paradigm are more about why the foci, tools and rituals work rather than exactly what they should be. At least this is the way 1st, 2nd and Revised editions were set up; I've no idea about M20 specifics. (And yes, there are also cultural considerations as well.)


                                                          There is anorher source of information about the houses besides the hermetic tradition book?
                                                          The Order of Hermes originated in the game Ars Magica, and have been the subject of several sourcebooks over that game's long history. The 2nd ed book Order of Hermes, the 3rd ed book Houses of Hermes, and the most recent and most in depth 5th edition trilogy Houses of Hermes: Mystery Cults (Bjornaer, Criamon, Merinita, Verditius), Houses of Hermes: Societies (Flambeau, Jerbiton, Tytalus, Ex Misc), and Houses of Hermes: True Lineages (Bonisagus, Guernicus, Mercere, Tremere). Ars Magica's history is a bit different from that of Mage and the World of Darkness (the founder of the Tremere having died several decades ago and them still being part of the Order is just the start), and the rules systems are radically different, but the information about their philosophies, mindsets and structure are still useful. You can find PDF copies of Ars Magica books at the Warehouse23 online store. (Of the books, I've found the three 5th edition ones the most useful, and the 3rd edition one probably the least useful of the group.)

                                                          The more modern Houses only appear in the Tradition books, but House Solificati was originally the Mage: Sorcerers' Crusade Tradition of alchemists which briefly held the Seat of Matter. After collapsing, they became the Children of Knowledge Craft detailed in the Book of Crafts.
