                    Ok, so you don't know what either of those two are.

                    Ascension is a road, one filled with revelation, joy and cosmic understanding. Seekings are trancendental moments of knowing in which you explore yourself and what your relationship is to you and to the nature of reality filtered through your own head and views of how reality works. Each of the Seekings you take takes you closer and gives you a greater comprehension of yourself and the universe. It's many things for many people, and many never finish the road, but everyone on it has a deeper relationship with the nature of himself and reality than a corpse will ever have. Each of them is a rush way beyond The Kiss. And as harrowing as some of them may be, and while most will not Ascend, being on that road with what it challenges and ultimately validates You and your own Comprehension of the Cosmos.

                    And in addition to this you get magic. Free, true magic stemming from your beliefs and who you are. Magic that does what you want it to do, what you need it to do. Magic that changes the world into what you dream. You unlock a vast cosmos of impossibility and it's your oyster. What is a bit of paradox here and there if you can change the world and be better? You can actually teach others, and the more they believe with you, the more powerful you all are and the change becomes the world.

                    You want to exchange it for linear, highly specialised magic, slavery to the vampires who give it, slavery to blood, a distaste for life and self that only grows as you turn into a monster or an alien. And even if this was good enough, you are trying to sell it to people who are defined by being highly stubborn True Believers who Know their view of reality and Path are the True Way.

                    So even if True Magic wasn't vastly more powerful than Blood Magic, there is so much more on that path than just base power. Heck, anyone focused on base power will always end up plateauing on the road to Ascension.

                    What doesn't kill you, makes you... stranger.

                                    The Consensus, Paradox, Quintessence. They are all big deals. But each for their own reasons. If you were versed on the reasons, then you would see why it may be hard to imagine a situation where Blood Magick would be so tempting as to kill one's Avatar for it.

                                    As an example, vampire's immortality it's tempting (maybe not so much as to consider loosing your True Magick and path to Ascencion, but still I think it can be said to be tempting).                                             Originally posted by Hello View Post
                                            Then why is consensus a big deal? If that has no true bearing on powers you use, why is it such a big deal? Why is that whenever I read a mage book consesus seems to be held up to some high standard?

                                            So let me put it like this. To do what you are passionate about and to experience all life has to offer you need money. Society forces you to contribute to society by being a productive member and generating value from your work, thus generating money and allowing you to experience what life has to offer at the cost of your time (and nerves, and health and...). What would life be like for you if you had infinite money, or if you didn't need money at all to fully experience everything it offers?

                                            This is the relationship between a True Mage and the Consensus. You can do all sorts of crazy stuff, but wouldn't it be even better if you didn't have to work extra hard or rob a bank to fuel your passions? That's where the Ascension as a communal goal of Humanity comes rather than just the individual path of the Magus. For the average Mage Consensus isn't even that big of a deal as their magic is quite easy when they are in their own places or in places where they are surrounded by their own people. But eventually they want more. They want to go out into the world and change it. And to do more they need other people that believe what they do or even better, other people that can do what they can do! This is what Mage is all about - overcoming boundaries and becoming so much more than the small minded unimaginative petty masses are right now.

                                            Consensus is a combination of two forces - cosmic constants and collective belief. You can avoid cosmic constants by changing where you are dimensionally or by tapping into enough belief that you bend the constants to your own preferences. This is done by creating Reality Zones.

                                            So what is Paradox? Paradox is reality hitting back when you push too hard. It can be anything from strange happenings around you, voices following you around, colors twisting a bit, your thumb growing like a root, becoming so mad you redefine reality to be your madness or being hijacked into a different reality to learn a moral story about being more polite to reality. All of these can be minor temporary effects or permanent ones, and it entirely depends on how much total Paradox you have accumulated. The more you have on you, the worse the effect. You can actually have certain amounts of Paradox "clinging" to you and slowly dissapating or being removed through the help of familiars, nodes or even certain costly rituals.

                                            How do you get Paradox? Now, If you do magic that's sort of kinda of acceptable, you get hit by no paradox. If you do magic that's weird you get hit by small amounts of paradox. You try to rewrite the cosmic constants in our own reality you get a ton of Paradox. None of these cases mean that it explodes in your face immediately or that its necessarily even negative! And you only really gain significant amounts when you do weird stuff over and over again in a short period of time. You take a break, find ways to get rid of Paradox and use slightly more conservative magic outside of your own places and you're fine.

                                            Going back to my original metaphor Paradox is the cops going after you for robbing banks to fund your crazy lifestyle. Lay low for a while, change your appearance, or even do the time and get out and do more crimes!

                                            What doesn't kill you, makes you... stranger.
