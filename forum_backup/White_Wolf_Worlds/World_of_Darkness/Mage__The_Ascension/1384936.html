    10 conventions instead of 5?

    04-30-2020, 06:18 PM
    Reading the preview of the Kickstarter got me thinking about one thing: Why does the technocracy have 5 conventions when there are 9 spheres (or ten if you count the "ultimate" sphere)? and just for curiosity, what new conventions would you create to fill the missing seats?

      I wouldn't necessarily try to structure them according to the Spheres. That said, TR does make a couple of suggestions such as splitting the NWO up into an espionage-oriented Convention (Panopticon) and a bureaucracy-oriented Convention (the White Tower); and these do correlate fairly well with Data for Panopticon and Mind for the White Tower. There's also a suggestion of splitting off the study of Matter from the Progenitors and expanding it from just pharmaceuticals to all things chemistry-related. I'm not sure any of that is warranted; but that's not really the point of this thread, right?

      So at this point, we would have seven Conventions: Panopticon for Data, Void Engineers for Dimensional Science, Iteration X for Forces, the Progenitors for Life, a new Convention for Matter, the Ivory Tower for Mind, and the Syndicate for Primal Utility.

      That would leave only Entropy and Time.

                                          Also, for game purposes, it's a lot easier to use that Spheres model for all characters. If you have a variant sphere such as Data or Dimensional Sciences to replace Correspondence or Spirit, respectively, it's a lot easier to play the game than if you had separate powers for each faction in a nonlinear magic system. In the end, gameplay also influences the spheres.

                                          Otherwise, they could have just stuck with noun-verb like Ars Magica and other games that do noun-verb magic.
