                                    There are three circumstances I would think about and equip the team accordingly, taking into account It-X connections.

                                    1) Driving in a car: Purchase a drone with some totally mundane features. It films the sky and displays the footage on a screen covering its underbelly, making it invisible. A laser weapon to subdue or kill attackers. A microwave gun that can disable the engine of the pursuer's vehicle.

                                    2) At home (The drone is also used here.)

                                    - If she lives in a celebrity ego-mansion: A tower-guard at the only entrance gate. The tower is bulletproof and has a gun-turret. Belowground dog-shelters with bulletproof roofing, inhabited by guard dogs, which can be augmented. The dogs are trained to attack the legs or crotch so human security can fire guns without hitting them.

                                    - If she lives in a condo: Have two, across the street from each other. The one she lives in has a window that is a vantage point into the other. A sniper surveils the other pad, which is her listed address (or unlisted, but findable address). A decoy lives there and is ready for action.

                                    3) At public events:
                                    With Progenitor contacts, you could have dedicated, self sacrificing vat-grown humans lacking real sentience who are the biological age of teenage fans. They are trained with simple phrases like, "I won't let hurt (name of celebrity)!". They get in the way, grip attackers' arms and have strength 5. Since there are cameras everywhere, the attackers have to decide if they want to risk creating a news story in which they are filmed killing their way past such an obstruction.

                                    Besides standard bodyguards with guns and body-armor, the team could include people equipped with laptops used to control swarms of quadcopters that can navigate around crowds of people, fly up to attackers and electrocute them.

                                    When the principle participates in their own defense, a security team is more effective. This can mean knowing hot to duck and having it be reflexive or pulling out a gun - before you get to Magick. She could have a constant Mind effect on that detects murderous intent that excludes people already accounted for. Alternatively, depending on other Spheres, a telepathic message anchored to her body or clothes that reads, "That's not her.". The person searching for her thinks this as if it his own thought. People she wants to interact with are made immune.



                                    Thank you for passing time with me in conversation. My Hacks.

                                        I would like to point out that Extraordinary Citizen or Technocrat bodyguards do not have to be empowered by what are often considered the default options of the Technocracy - cybernetics or superior training. There is also the option of the Progenitors being involved. Considering that Progenitors can use Treatments that modify the body and the mind, pretty much any sensible power or capability could be provided for. A Progenitor agent - Enlightened or not - could be capable of going into an Entropy 1 trance/sense that could detect hostile intent. A Life 1 or Mind 1-2 Effect could sense stress and urgency of possible attackers. They could have a broad Time 1 enhancement to know the precise time and have perfect reflexes. A specialized Time 2 divination/danger sense. Or a Time 3 haste Effect.

                                        The great benefit of having a Progenitor bodyguard is their great expertise in healing, of course. And as another benefit - the creation and providing access to psychoactive substances, just to have a bit of fun and variety on the side .

                                        Considering the influence and position of this particular Syndicate woman, a bodyguard sent by the Progenitors could definitely do more than just protecting her. She or he could be schooled and advised in regard to manipulating the Masses, organization, influencing the mass media, and overall details of the Syndicate Paradigm (nothing that is considered a secret, of course). This way the groundwork is laid for further cooperation between the different Conventions in the Technocracy.

                                            Originally posted by Heavy Arms View Post
                                            HITMarks aren't cheap or unlimited (esp. newer models that can actually blend in well with Sleepers).
                                            That's... not a simple point. (and, well, even if the mark V's can't hold a conversation, they can pass as a stoic guard)

                                            First, Hypertech is bullshit and thus the economics of making these things isn't limited by money. Technomagic is, from a certain perspective, all about circumventing the effort needed to create normal tech. You're cutting corners like crazy. The reason the Technocracy doesn't spurn Terminators out like they're iPhones is probably because of Quintessence limitations rather than money.

                                            There's obsolescence to consider. HIT marks I through III are questionable historical footnotes that no longer fit the technocratic paradigm, and IV probably wasn't worth using. V is where they really begin. The Not-T-800 and it's many minor variants. Only thing is, according to M20, the technocrats now have... are they on IX or X? Bearing in mind none of these are straight upgrades. The liquid metal one was too vulgar, and the newest one doesn't use much metal IIRC. You wouldn't recycle a V to make a later model, and you likely have to come up with an all-new factory for each itteration. There's going to be a lot of Obsolete Vs lying around, because unless you're extracting primium, the smartest thing to do with these old HIT marks are to send them into missions till they bust, or sell them in an attempt to recoup their loss/fund the next one: You might even be able to profit, given how even the oldest V's exceed the limits of today's tech.

                                            Lastly, and yeah this probably goes against the mindset of MAGES because they've got egos to the moon and would probably push for Gagetbahns over conventional transport every day of the weak, but the HITMARK V is a 35ish year old technology, and sure the Technocrats evidently have some "Nanomachines, Son" updates, but for a lot of things that work well, the next step is to make them cheaper and easier to produce.
