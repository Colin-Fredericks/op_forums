                So, just to lay my cards all out, since there is confusion on my stance regarding the Order of Reason:

                -Yes, I am fine with OoR's goal to be to try to stomp out mystic Paradigms and Paradigms not part of their organization. That's kind of the point of the Ascension War.

                -I'm fine with them taking advantage of the reach the British Empire provides them to wage their side of the Ascension War.

                -I am not saying you're not allowed to portray OoR as villains period...in a Traditions/Crafts game (hell I'm in the middle of a Mage game right now where the STs only allow Traditions and Crafts and no Technocracy PCs). I'm saying that it shouldn't be the ONLY option to portraying the OoR.

                -No, I am not ok with them saying that they're directly responsible for real-world atrocities because that DOES whitewash and oversimplify the actual history behind these real world events, and is as bad as V5 Chechnya, and goes against the stance the WoD has up until now, like what's stated in Wraith's Charnel Houses of Europe, ergo "the Holocaust was not directly caused by a WoD splat, please don't do that".

                -No, I am not ok with them being completely lock-in step with ALL British Victorian ideology. The way how the entire gameline presents Enlightened Science, that they discover scientific innovations decades, if not centuries before Sleepers do, so they would have to know that all of the now-debunked Victorian science theories like """scientific racism""" are inherently untrue. And that it contradicts Mage as a gameline with "belief changes reality". Because if OoR logically doesn't believe these debunked theories, why would they promote them? If they did try to promote them, then their magick shouldn't work. So why would it?

                -And to lead into the above: in order for players to play and RP as OoR, their PCs have to believe their characters are doing the right thing. If they don't believe they're doing the right thing then they can't perform magick...you know, the Thing-That-You-Do-the-Most in a Mage game.

                -I prefer their mentality on waging the Ascension War to be less "oppressing colored people is KEWL!!!" and more "I just want you to understand, that this is nothing personal. This is simply...business."

                -I also prefer, as mentioned above on Skeleton Keys vs BoM/EE regarding labor unions, I absolutely also prefer cases of "the right hand not talking to the left hand" because, yes, in-fighting between whole factions IS an interesting conflict to play out, and not just restricted to individual Luminaries against the entire Order.

                -And finally, yes I prefer if the leads at the top of the organization were corrupt and maneuver the organization to do questionable things, but not the rank and file members...aka the typical Luminary PC.
