    Two Minute to Midnight; my current M20 Campaign - Suggestions, critics, ideas?

    09-30-2015, 11:37 AM
    So,

    I have recently answered a question on the Topic: "How deep is the corruption in your campaign?"

    [http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/m...your-campaign]

    My answer ended up being a little longer than I expected, and it worked as a summary of my current campaign. I am copying it below and would love to receive thoughts, opinions and suggestions, specially concerning one particular issue which I will discuss below:

    Here goes:

    __________________________________________________ ___________________________________________

    I'm running a slightly unorthodox campaign, which just started. As a caveat: if you are one of my players (yeah, you, over there!) STOP READING RIGHT NOW.

    The campaign is called Two Minutes to Midnight and, in our game, the (Ragnarok/Apocalypse/Gehenna/BigFuckUp) almost happened during the first years of the 21st century, but was averted by a coalition of Tradition and Union members, who discovered that most of the Union's Control (which I call The Board, in my game) and some of the Masters were corrupted and actively working towards ending the world in Oblivion (my term for what they call their Ascension).

    Many events of the original metaplot happened: the Ascension War was, in many ways, won by the Union, whose wide Progrom managed to either reprogram or exterminate many Council mages. that there was an Avatar Storm, most masters from both sides where killed/vanished/became sentient amoebas, etc.

    It was at that time that the New Control (which John courage had a big part in creating) discovered that their Progroms and the systematic slaughter of Avatars was killing humanity's creativity in general and, in fact, weakening reality, as it was becoming more rigid and, therefore, brittle. This had been the plan of the corrupted mages all along, of course.

    So, the uncorrupted members of New Control instituted a New Policy: a Truce with the Traditions. They would no longer be hunted and would be left in peace to follow their individual Paths. In return, mages from the Traditions would stop seeking collective Ascension and agree to work in secrecy while the Traditions and Union recover from their close call with annihilation. The Traditions would be allowed to policy themselves in order to keep the terms of the Truce, with the Union providing attaches offering support and supervision.

    The world is still around and the Masses are still blissfully unaware of the truth. The Tellurian, however, is hanging by a thread, with many mages - and the PCs - working desperately to both keep it together and to heal some of the damage done during the worst stages of the war between the Union and Council. Others, however, are working toward ending it all, and only require a little push to succeed.

    Seeing as both sides are quite weak, with low numbers and very few masters and archmasters, each major region of the world counts with only one Cabal working for that end, usually created by the regional masters with support from local mages and what passes for authority in the region. The U.S is an exception, with two whole cabals dedicated to this end. The group is the "B Team" of this initiative.

    That is to say that, in my campaign there was a huge level of corruption before the game itself began. The top levels, however, were purged in the event that occurred when the Apocalypse was prevented. Many mid-level and lower agents, however, were and are still corrupted, with many sleeper cells of corrupt mages - and other stuff - still present on both sides of the fence. To provide an example, it is a little like the Marvel Cinematic Universe SHIELD. Both the Union and Council unwittingly served the purposed of the Nefandi and similar for a long time, but their top levels are now aware of this and grudgingly working together - to some extent - to clean their houses and attempt to keep the world in one piece.

    __________________________________________________ __________________________________________

    I am currently running the introductions for the players, their Awakenings, meetings with their masters and teaching them both the rules of the game and how the setting works. They are all new Mage players, although very experienced RPG and WoD players.

    After this initial story arch, I intend to run an arch where they'll meet, in odd circumstances and will subsequently be "invited" by the Traditions to become investigators and enforcers of the new "Masquerade".

    My intention is for the game to run half as "Supernatural meets X-Files" with Mages, with them investigating occurrences in the East Coast of the U.S. and half in their home base in N.Y., dealing with the current friction caused by the truce between the Council and the Union. The main themes for the game will be conflicting loyalties, mistrust and the unending battle to keep the midnight clock from ticking.

    This is the group as it currently stands:

    Margaret Leslie was a nun, and also happened to be a Widderslainte. Her Avatar was corrupted during the Middle Ages and every one of her incarnations in the past has become corrupted by it. One of these reincarnations was a Nephandi known as Kemmler, a name well known by The Dresden Files fans. As most of you know, in the official cannon, a Widderslainte is thoroughly corrupted and lacking free will. In my game, this is 99% percent true. She has a very powerful avatar (rating 5), but has to fight against her past lifes, which remain tethered to her Avatar, in order to access its power. Although the Choir would be the natural option for her, she was rescued from a cult (ironically founded by one of her past lifes) by a Templar mage, who guided her to a Hermetic mage, who became her master. He refuses to destroy her Avatar, as he fears that this would cause great damage to the Tellurian. Therefore, he is trying to teach her and prevent her soul from being corrupted. Her Avatar appears as her many past lifes, a brilliant female angel held by dark chains or a dark male angel.

    Patrick Boyko was a descendant of eastern European immigrants, a U.S marine veteran who deserted and became a hitman. After his sister was murdered when he was in jail - for possession of an ilegal firearm, as the Arcanum he already had before Awakening meant the NY police had a hard time finding any evidence against him - he went looking for revenge. The man who killed his sister, however, Awakened as a Sahajiya during the time Pat was imprisoned and repented for his crimes. He came back to NY in order to save Pat from his life of violence and was almost managing to do so when, sadly, they were both gunned down by their former gangmates. Patrick was brought back from the dead by a Chakravanti Thanatoi mage and has since repented his past life, abandoned the way of violence and become a nurse. His Avatar is the spirit of the mage who tried to save him and who he wanted to kill.

    Michael Turner is a physicist, who, along with his wife, created a dark energy reactor, which would create infinite renewable energy for the Masses. Interestingly, as this player is a doctor in Physics and Engineering, his character's theories are actually quite sound! Consensual reality, however, didn't agree with him. Both him and his wife suffered massive Paradox backlash when they attempted to activate the reactor, and were thrown into the Deep Umbra, in the Impossible Junk Yard, a grave for inventions that were never created.

    There, in the left hand of the Machina ex Deus, he was hunted by Pattern Spiders and the Guardian, a mechanical spider made of impossible pieces. He was saved by a mysterious figure, whom he learned was him from the future (actually him from an alternate timeline which he himself made impossible by being saved) and ended up back on reality, five years later, with no idea where his wife is. He is currently interned in an Asylum - a realistic one, not an American Horror Story version - and being taught by a retired Etherist, while being probed for recruitment by the Union. His Avatar is an O.S. created by his wife and which became an A.I during their visit to the junk Yard. It has the form of a digital version of his wife and appears in electronic devices.

    We will also have two additional players in the future: a technocrat, belonging to the NWO and a Solificati chemist / alchemist.

    __________________________________________________ ______________________________________________

    So, my main problem, if someone has the patience to read all I've written above, is: I am quite good at intrigue, but am not sure regarding my skills in investigation and detective games.

    If anyone is interested I'll also give more information on the setting, such as their foci, NPCs and etc.

    I would love to receive any feedback, suggestions and ideas!

    Thanks! =D

                      Well, first of all, thanks for the kind words!

                      When writing on the internet, one always fears the usual "troll treatment". It is great to be able to discuss ideas with other storytellers without fear of trolling. And, most of all, it is always wonderful to have one's ideas appreciated by our equals!

                      That said, I'll try to answer all comments below:

                      Kalinara, initially they will appear not to tie with the weakness in the Tellurian. The main purpose of the team will initially to handle dangerous supernatural occurrences and breaches of the secret. This is where the game resembles Supernatural, seeing as, apart from the Union liaison, they will have no formal authority. The X-Files side will come from the liaison having to deal with his superiors while, at the same time, trying not to antagonize the rest of the group.

                      Anyway, what I intend to happen is to progressively have them dealing with more important cases and noticing that what they are doing is a much more serious kind of damage control than what they initially thought. They will start to realize that the disturbances are increasing and eventually - much like the new XCom game - they will have to start choosing what cases to follow up on and which ones to delay investigating.

                      That is when, ideally, they will note that there are forces working behind the stage purposefully attempting to weaken the Tellurian and will begin investigating. And that is when the "A-Team" will disappear...

                      They still haven't started working together. They are currently apprentices for each of their masters. But I'm working on it!

                      My intention is for all of them to be recruited, one way or another.

                      Patrick - now Philip, as he had to acquire a new identity - will simply be hired by the Union. His master, after working for 10 years as a pathologist without taking a life, has just exterminated the entire gang that was chasing him. Her Paradox crash was Bad after that, and finally convinced him to abandon his old life and seek a path of redemption. The police never went after them, but reckoning is coming. After he looses his master, he will be a little lost in the world, and the Union will offer him a chance to help joining the team and being a gun pointed at Maggie.

                      Maggie, as you rightly thought, is something of a center for the group. As an Archmage and one of the people responsible for the first peace thoughts between the Union and the Council, her master has a lot of pull. Therefore, it would be too costly for both sides to press the issue of a gilgul. So, the Council (yes, the Council), which was forcing the issue much more strongly, demands that she have some sort of safeguard.

                      That will be Philip's (Pat) job. As a sort of "pacifist zen assassin", he is in a perfect place for it. That is why her master will suggest the creation of the team, to offer an excuse for them to be together.

                      Mike - our Etherist - meanwhile, is currently still being contested by the Union and the Society. He has, however, just decided to join the Society after receiving guidance from a Virtual Adept - the same that gave Philip his new ID (this adept, by the way, deserves a discussion all zer own. Ze became of my favorite new NPCs). The man that is trying to recruit him to the Union - his old minder and friend from DARPA - will require that he receive supervision in order to be released from the asylum where he currently is living. Also, he wants Mike to see how dangerous unguided magic can be. So, it will be "strongly recommended" that he joins the team.

                      And by the way, great ideas concerning the metaphysical elements of their union! I already know that one of Maggie's past lifes met - yeah, things will get a little convoluted here, sorry - a past life of Mike's master, and tried to avoid his death. Mike's master's past life was actually Pliny the Elder, and her past life tried to stop the eruption of Mount Vesuvius, which destroyed Pompei and killed Pliny.

                      My idea for Pat's connection to Maggie is that he will see her as his responsability. Cleaning her taint would let her Avatar return to the Wheel and, if that is not possible, it would be his duty to remove her from it.

                      Regarding the dark energy part, some of it I based in actual physics, being influenced by the Consensus. His experiment failed because he didn't account for the effects of the Anthropic Field (an Etheric theory derived from the Anthropic Principle, stating that the presence of humanity works as a fifth Quantum force that, by itself, affects the four others), which he is currently starting to understand. He is still starting to grasp the idea of the Paradox Effect.


                      Now, regarding the Impossible Junk Yard: I'm very happy that you liked it! My players loved the Sandman feeling of the place, I hope yours like it too! And BTW, I'll be stealing the "Anachronization Influencer" name, no doubt about that.

                          Thanks, you can definitely have the Anachronomitization Influencer. Here's the description from the session notes, in case you are interested:

                          The five characters appeared atop a strange silver clockwork device approximately 30’ in diameter, located in an abandoned warehouse. The device consists of nested “gears” moving in a very complex manner, clockwise. The largest of these was the center was about 5’ across (and was the only part moving counterclockwise). Next in size were several gears were slightly less than a yard across. (These were where the characters appeared). The remaining gears were substantially smaller (the largest were less than a foot and most were smaller, including a multitude that were much smaller). These gears articulated with several wide circular “bands” nested within the circumference of the device. These bands were not all concentric; some were “off-kilter” with the center, moving within the overall pattern. They slid over and under one another where they crossed. All of the above were engraved/inscribed with some kind of writing or symbols in a band around the outer edge. As a whole, the device glowed with silver light and white-silver “lightning” raced through the gears as they turned. When a new person appeared, the lighting “flowed” to that gear and substantially increased in brightness , culminating in a “reverse lightning strike” in which the character appeared. The machine made a non-unpleasant sort of whirling, grinding noise that could be heard through the cracking the lightning.

                          They have also discovered that the Anachronomitization Influencer is controlled through something called the Chronomatrix, which they currently have. It looks like a silver pocketwatch but they haven't figured out how to open it much less use it, yet. (They do have the instructions, they just haven't figured out that's what they are). Apparently, it is much older than the device it controls, but, though they know that, they haven't twigged to the fact that that is probably important. Also, it took another Device, called, so far, "the Node Tap" to power the Influencer, which was liberated from someone's collection by means unknown (and possibly not against the will of the collector; there are just people who consider it important enough to keep an eye on, who have hired some mage mercs to track the Node Tap down and figure out what it's being used for.... and the mage mercs have met the PCs in a non-hostile sit-down, but there is certainly going to be some more developments in that situation as well...)

                                      Now that I have some free time at the office (good think my boss is not an RPG player), I can post my ideas for some of their first cases. I would love to receive some feedback on them, as investigation archs are not my specialty. Those cases will be given to them after they are already united, a story that is still currently being developed.
                                      1. This may be their first case, as I love to disguise ideas behind cliches. A TV team making one of those mockumentaries about haunted house was killed in a fashion befitting the stories told about the place. They will be sent to investigate in order to discover if it was an actual supernatural happening, seeing as, after a TV crew was killed, there may be a lot of publicity on the case.

                                      My idea is for them to discover that the murders were committed by regular human beings, after profit, revenge or just craziness. I like the idea of something like the copycats from American Horror Story. The problem will be when the ghost kills the murderers...
                                      1. This was a suggestion from my best friend, who is an avid Mage player who couldn’t be in the game: zombies! Because what kind of RPG campaign worth its name doesn’t feature zombies?
                                      Actually, a child will start developing necromantic powers after losing her parents, and raising the dead. Initially it will be just stray animals and her cat, killed by bullies. After that, as the strength and range of her powers start to grow, people will start rising from the dead. And it’s only a matter of time before her parents come back to take their child.
                                      1. This was an idea pitched by my mother during dinner – the benefits of having a nerd family! The mayor of a small town was replaced by a Mage. This Mage killed the mayor and has been slowly eating him in order to maintain his appearance. As long as he can eat close to a pound of flesh per week, he can keep his form. If he doesn’t, his stolen form starts to rot and he will eventually revert to his usual self.

                                      Ironically, usually they wouldn’t touch that kind of case, as it is not breaching the Truce or risks revealing the secret. The problem is that he did that in order to take over a very powerful Node close to the city, destroying a nature preserve in order to absorb its power. The Garou who live in this Caern, however, haven’t liked this idea very much.

                                      This will be the first time when the group will start to see a bigger pattern happening, when they discover that this Mage wasn’t working by himself, but under orders.

                                      Those are my most developed cases at the moment. I already have some hooks and ideas brewing, that need a little more work to become stories.

                                      One of the most important one will be a Cabal of Tradition Mages who refuse to obey the Truce and work to undermine the Union’s work and reveal the secret to the Masses. Something like a mystical version of Mr. Robot. I still need to work a lot on it, though.

                                      There is also the Nefandi cult looking for Maggie, the Church of Kupala, who wants to have one of her former lives take over her and continue his holy work.

                                      And our Chakravanti friend Philip has just met his first vampire while driving his Uber (he left cabs behind and is now an Uber driver), a newbie vampire named Desdemona (actually, her name is Beth) who may have killed a John without meaning to. This is actually where our game stopped last session.

                                      There is a LOT more happening at the game right now, but concerning possible cases I believe that’s the most important part.

                                                          Suggestion, start with this That's the first novelette written by the author and it's a standalone story about the main protagonist of the series. It actually won a Hugo, and it's an amazing place to start. I actually feel it's better then the first novel as an introduction. He has made all his novellettes available online, actually. (Down at the Farm, Overtime, Pimpf and Equoid)

                                                          The core concept is that magic in the universe is real and that ritual sorcery is actually mnemonics and excessive fluff that actually codifies complex mathematical equations that allow you to connect different universes with our own. Britain has always been protected by sorcerors from the things beyond, but it was only after WW2 that they organised a full government agency that deals with occult threats. This agency uses mathematical modelling, Dho Na curves and a lost Turing theorem as a basis for all kinds of magic. This magic is tightly controlled and performed on machines rather then on human brains allowing a modicum of control of the consequnce. What the people who use this do is summon things or energy from beyond and force them to do their busywork - therefore Computational Demonology.

                                                          This secret service recruits or kills people who dabble in the occult, and is filled with clueless idiots who got a government job because they coded a screensaver that could eat Bristol, played too much with grandfather's strange eyebending knives or inherited a strange genetic condition that made them yearn for the sea and have a strange, rubbery complexion. From this cesspool of bureacracy you end up having a couple actually competent people trying to stave of the end of the world. The only problem is that it has already started, and the stars are nigh. The only question is if the Laundry and its agents will manage to keep humanity alive until the stars are Wrong Again. Now add an American secret service that runs zombie and possesion drones and tends to be really messed up, EU meetings with other agencies that fight against the Things From Beyond, diplomatic meetings with the Deep Ones, the Russians and the Chinese who cannot decide if they want to help you or kill you and endless cults who suddenly have their spells and rituals working.

                                                          This is the setting of the Laundry series. There's also an awesome RPG set in the same setting done by Cubicle 7. The RPG has the single best monster manual I have ever seen - 140 pages - 4 pages of rules, 136 pages of interviews, emails, commercials, photographs, investigations, reports, fliers, scrawled text and ebay auctions.

                                                          The Laundry would be a pretty cool inspiration for a Mage game like you're positing. It has the X-Files and Supernatural vibe you're looking for, it just adds the Cthulhu Mythos and British humor to the mix.

                                                          Oh and if you like the concept, you might also be interested in looking up Delta Green - a X-Files meets Supernatural meets Cthulhu RPG about a conspiracy in the american federal agencies that fights against things that go bump in the night and covers up their existence no matter the cost to their private lives or job security. It's pretty cool, but I'd be writing another wall of text about that then :P There is a kickstarter for the new edition, though.
