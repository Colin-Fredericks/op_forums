    Questions on Nodes

    04-11-2018, 12:13 PM
    - Are there Nodes outside of the mundane realm? So is it possible to harvest Quintessence while inside of a dream realm, like your Demesne? Can your Demesne Background be linked to your Node Background, for example? The idea would be that your dream realm occupies a point of power within the Maya. And the same applies to the Low Umbra, Middle Umbra, High Umbra. And if a Mage is creating their own Realm, can they make the Realm a Node, or add Nodes to it?

    - Is it okay to make your Laboratory or Sanctum the same place as your Node? It makes sense mechanically and thematically, like a Technocrat building their laboratory complex over a node so that they can gradually change the resonance and harvest it (naturally pissing off any associated Mages or Werewolves of course).

    - Mechanically speaking, what does the level of a Node/Cairn actually mean? So what's the difference between a level 1 node and a level 3 node for a Mage with Prime? Furthermore, the difficulty modifiers for working around a Node... I'm assuming that you need to have a matching Paradigm?

    - The Ventures of Primal Utility are Nodes, essentially. Can the energy be harvested by other Mages? So a conglomerate HQ which is bursting with Quintessence, can it be stolen by Mages with mystical paradigms? Does this make it vulgar, or demand a greater level of Prime?

    - Are there mechanics for upgrading Nodes? For example, if a lab is a node, does deepening the academic and scientific excellence of the lab, pouring more Quintessence into it, tapping the wellspring etc. eventually push it up a level? If a Node is filled with a violent resonance, would arranging a violent confrontation at the node help to strengthen it? And along the same thread, can a node be weakened or destroyed?

      Originally posted by 11twiggins View Post
      - Are there Nodes outside of the mundane realm? So is it possible to harvest Quintessence while inside of a dream realm, like your Demesne? Can your Demesne Background be linked to your Node Background, for example? The idea would be that your dream realm occupies a point of power within the Maya. And the same applies to the Low Umbra, Middle Umbra, High Umbra. And if a Mage is creating their own Realm, can they make the Realm a Node, or add Nodes to it?
      I imagine so. Masters of the Art made mention of Archmasters moving their Nodes out into pocket Realms, so they didn't have to bother with defending locations on the material plane. Presumably, various locations in the Umbra may be places of power. That said, such places will probably already have "owners". Spirits have desire for such resources, too.

      I would not allow a Demesne to host a Node. Sounds like the sort of thing a player would try to do to get away with having a Quintessence source that couldn't be touched. On the other hand, one could easily argue that while a Node in a Demesne might be safe from attack from the material side, it's open to assault from residents of the dreamlands. My problem is that while that could create conflict, defenses mounted around one character's Demesne wouldn't be so exciting for an entire Cabal of PCs. Not everyone could join in, like would happen with an Earthly Node. Not unless everyone had Mind 3.

      Speaking of, I find it compelling the idea of venturing out into the dreamlands with Mind 3, and gathering wisps of Quintessence like one would gather wild mushrooms or blackberries. The same could be said of the rest of the Umbra, which could play host to Quintessence gathering missions. Specific parts of the non-material world would have their own flavors of Quintessence, with their own Resonances. Whole stories could be spun from searches for Quintessence with specific, rare Resonance.

      - Is it okay to make your Laboratory or Sanctum the same place as your Node? It makes sense mechanically and thematically, like a Technocrat building their laboratory complex over a node so that they can gradually change the resonance and harvest it (naturally pissing off any associated Mages or Werewolves of course).
      It's not only okay, I imagine it's standard procedure for mages who are in a position to do so. It's why Chantries are often built around Nodes, as a Chantry is somewhat like a Sanctum. Those individual mages with private Nodes have incentive to build their Sanctum around them, not just for the convenience of having their work space on top of a place of power, but because they are in the best position to defend that Node with their full magickal might.

      - Mechanically speaking, what does the level of a Node/Cairn actually mean? So what's the difference between a level 1 node and a level 3 node for a Mage with Prime? Furthermore, the difficulty modifiers for working around a Node... I'm assuming that you need to have a matching Paradigm?
      Each dot of Node provides X points of Quintessence per week, half as "free" Quintessence and half as Tass. The value of 'X' is determined by the Storyteller, as a function of their decision for how powerful a campaign should be. It can be as low as 2 per dot per week for a gritty street level game (where magical resources are scarce and precious, and every Node is a heavily guarded treasure), or as high as 5 to 10 per dot per week for a high fantasy game (where things like Realms are easy to maintain, spells get thrown around gregariously, and Wonders are relatively cheap to make).

      I imagine the difficulty modifiers are affected, not by Paradigm (Nodes don't really care), but by Resonance. A Node with bloody, violent Resonance will make violent magick easier, but life-affirming and peaceful magick harder.

      - The Ventures of Primal Utility are Nodes, essentially. Can the energy be harvested by other Mages? So a conglomerate HQ which is bursting with Quintessence, can it be stolen by Mages with mystical paradigms? Does this make it vulgar, or demand a greater level of Prime?
      I don't think a mage not working with Primal Utility can derive Quintessence from a Venture. While a Venture has some qualities as a Node, it's not exactly the same. A Node is a weak spot in the Tellurian, where the energy of creation seeps out. A Venture is more like a system for drawing excess Quintessence out of the people involved in it, and from their activity. The Quintessence only flows into a concentrated form when the Hypereconomist draws it out. The mystic likely wouldn't even notice what was happening, or would only see it as a curious manner in which Patterns interacted with each other. Only when the Syndicate op went to "collect" would the mystic realize what was going on.

      I'd imagine, in any case, that Ventures represent a magickal (or rather Enlightened) exploit that is inextricably tied to the Alternative Sphere of Primal Utility. If you don't understand reality in that way - if you don't also have Primal Utility, rather than Prime - you can't do anything with it. Not unless you found some sort of Rote that uses conjunctional Spheres as a workaround, or even somehow expanded your understanding of Prime so that it gains qualities usually reserved for Primal Utility. The latter would require a fairly fundamental change to a mystic's Paradigm, and not something a Storyteller ought to just let a player handwave away.

      - Are there mechanics for upgrading Nodes? For example, if a lab is a node, does deepening the academic and scientific excellence of the lab, pouring more Quintessence into it, tapping the wellspring etc. eventually push it up a level? If a Node is filled with a violent resonance, would arranging a violent confrontation at the node help to strengthen it? And along the same thread, can a node be weakened or destroyed?
      To my knowledge, there are no rules for strengthening Nodes. The game tends to treat them as static fixtures, their only changes up to Storyteller fiat.

      Going by my gut, I would not allow playing to Resonance to make a Node stronger. If it were so, I imagine the various sects of the world would prioritize making their Nodes stronger through this method, and there wouldn't be any Nodes controlled by the sects that were "weak". There'd be too much advantage in playing to Resonance, even if it takes lots of time and effort.

      Rather, I imagine that if it were possible to make a Node more powerful - to increase its dot rating - it would require Prime 5 magick, and probably a great deal of successes. If Prime 5 can be used to make Nodes, it can probably be used to make existing ones bigger. This would make it possible, but not easy or common. (You have to have a Prime Master to do it, and those guys tend to have other stuff they want to be doing that just upgrading all the Nodes. Plus, that kind of thing is Vulgar, which Masters get reluctant about.)

          Originally posted by 11twiggins View Post
          - Are there Nodes outside of the mundane realm?
          By definition, no. However Nodes are not the be all and end all of such things. Wellsprings could occur anywhere, including the Umbra or Maya, but are less predictable than Nodes are. Generally, combining the Node Background with Demense or other Realms means the mage has actually linked a Node in the Earthly Realm to their external bastion to access it's power in either location.

          That said, I think it would be reasonable from a strictly mechanical sense to use the Node Background to represent a Demense or Umbral Realm with a high rate of Wellsprings or Junctures (esp, if purposefully created that way through use of various skills and Prime).

          - Is it okay to make your Laboratory or Sanctum the same place as your Node?
          Yes, though most mages will prefer to share a Node through a Chantry and have multiple Sanctums on the same Node to maximize the benefits for multiple mages. This is SOP for the Technocracy and common place for other mages out of the need for mutual cooperation and defense.

          - Mechanically speaking, what does the level of a Node/Cairn actually mean?
          The rating dictates the amount of Tass and Quintessence that can safely be harvest from the Node over a period of time.

          For Caerns, using the WtA mechanics, it also dictates the size of the area of influence of the Caern, the approximate number of werewolves that reside there, and the level of spiritual upkeep needed to appease the spirits that empower a Caern beyond just being an energy source (this is how it also predicts the number of Garou, since it can only provide so much energy, and their pacts require upkeep, there's a cost/benefit balance of numbers).

          Furthermore, the difficulty modifiers for working around a Node... I'm assuming that you need to have a matching Paradigm?
          No. Generally speaking the benefits from Nodes in this regard are universal because Nodes are areas of mutable reality from the different Realms mingling.

          - The Ventures of Primal Utility are Nodes, essentially. Can the energy be harvested by other Mages? So a conglomerate HQ which is bursting with Quintessence, can it be stolen by Mages with mystical paradigms? Does this make it vulgar, or demand a greater level of Prime?
          This isn't really specified, but I would rule that raiding a Venture requires at least a thorough understanding of Hypereconomics on top of the normal Prime rules for such things. The Quintessence generated by Ventures is something that requires either Primal Utility or at least significant understanding of the theories behind it to interact with. Vulgarity and level of Prime would depend on approach.

          - Are there mechanics for upgrading Nodes?
          No, though you've suggested plenty of reasons to allow the Background to be increased over play.

                          Mechanically as it relates to magic, they already are. Unless you're using M20's Sanctum rules, there would be no purpose to this, and even with M20's rules it doesn't seem worth the cost.

                          Some yes. Though magic that has a constant effect (like giving yourself wings) has to stay in the Sanctum to avoid issues.                           Permadox only had a real effect when the Cyborg invokes Paradox. Permadox is still a threat when effects are coincidental, because coincidental magic can botch and provoke Paradox.

                                              Sanctum Advantage: The mage's paradigm rules. Also, if another Mage has the same Paradigm (and paradigmic variation, it should be very very close), it will be also a good place for them.
                                              Disadvantage: That other mage better not be a rival
                                              Demesne Advantage: The mage's paradigm rules. Also, they can more or less shape the realm at will at high ratings of the Demesne background, making them almost a godlike figure.
                                              Disadvantage: The demesne is a dream, literally. It's hard to bring anything physical in there, and the mage's actual body is usually asleep and vulnerable. Best visited while in a Sanctum, or if the mage is inconspicous and has a safe(ish) life.
                                              Shade/shard realm of <Sphere> Advantage: Holy balls, if your mage has a big affinity for a specific sphere, their according effects might go Full Throttle, potentially with big bonuses.

                                              General Umbra Advantage: A lot of things are coincidental for everyone involved on a base level.
