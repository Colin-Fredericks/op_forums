      Life can always add more (not sure if at 3 or 4 - 5 can make 'em skyrocket, as that would be needed for the most outrageous self-transmutations like becoming a dragon), I think Prime can be used to "reinforce your pattern" for the same effect at high levels. Prob a combination of these are used in the bio-mods and some of the Cybernetic implants that do just that (I think the one that reinforces the bone structure gives 2 extra hp)

      Armor, ironically, doesn't (unless I'm missing something): It adds Soak to keep 'em safe, wich it's different - albeit if by "armor" you mean a giant "mecha", that could probably be better represented trough the rules about objects (like with cars or tanks), which would provide a layer of "hp" over your hp against some kinds of assaults.

      If you're interested in protection, well the best way it's not being there (and there are a lot of magical means to that end). But that's a bit of a moot answer, isn't it? - still, making the attacks fail trough increasing the difficulty of the attacker it's a compromise, and one that can also be achieved trough a lot of means (including low-key forcefields). Entropy it's the queen here.

      The Rote "Gift of Prana" (Life 3/Prime 2) provides an interesting alternative in a "pool of hp" that's used as a reservoir of regeneration that activates on harm (doesn't give you more "ticks", but renews the ticks you lose)

      Then you have those that decide to ignore the process altogether by negating the result of loosing them all: Like with the self-resurrection machine in the basic (a techno-magical Talisman with a TON of Spheres on it that allows you to pretty much "save" a copy of yourself to activate at the moment of your death) - you can prob. have "cheaper" alternatives to not die when you die, like shifting to an astral body triggered on the moment before death with Mind 5/Time 4/Entropy 1, and things like that.
      Some mages-turned-undead-abomination can keep alive with no HP as long as they didn't "die" of aggravated damage. Prob. there are more than one way to similarly negate (or delay) the "you die" effect on loosing your hp

                          Yes. If you want to cast on yourself Life 2 it's enough. (And it's Prime 3, not 2 - it's an enchantment on a living being). The system it's: Spend success on duration and targeting (it was a Revised rote) and spend a point of Quintessence to enchant the target. After that each succcess creates a healing pool of 2 hp. If the target it's injured before the end of the duration this pool heals the injury as much as possible. This rote can be Vulgar or not depending on the kind of wounds that would be able to heal - defined at the moment of casting (Bashing Damage it's coincidental, Lethal it's usualy Vulgar unless it's only for minor wounds, Aggravated it's always Vulgar). Also, if the effect it's Vulgar the caster has to decide if it's going to work in pressence of Witnesses. If yes, then it's Vulgar with Witnesses even if there wasn't anyone present at the moment of casting

                                  I think just about every Sphere has some defensive application against physical injury.

                                  Correspondence can be used to make your sim more accurate; it should also be able to mess with someone else's aim, making you harder to hit.

                                  Entropy can cause misfires.

                                  Forces can erect force fields.

                                  Life can directly boost your toughness — as well as your ability to dodge, when that matters.

                                  Matter can strengthen your garments, letting them serve as armor — or better armor, if it's already armor.

                                  Mind can let you cloud men's minds; if they can't see you, it's harder to shoot you.

                                  Prime can imbue weapons to make them inflict aggravated damage; I would think that it ought to be able to reinforce armor to better withstand it.

                                  Spirit… I'd have to think about that one.

                                  Time, as stated, can be used for a Mulligan.

                                  Most of those don't give you health levels; instead, they make it less likely you'll get hit in the first place. Which might be better; if you can avoid being hit, you won't need health levels.

                                  My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

                                                      Originally posted by M20 pg 511

                                                      If, however, you want to cast a lingering, mobile Effect that has been integrated into a specific Pattern and which involves a Sphere that is not normally related to that sort of Pattern, then the Effect must be “locked” into the target’s Pattern by a Sphere that does apply to that Pattern.
                                                      The pattern of a sword moves with the sword. That's why you only need Prime 2 to enhance a sword.                                                       When the sphere rules are contradictory I use the following test: When you see a sphere combination, ask what each Sphere is doing there.
