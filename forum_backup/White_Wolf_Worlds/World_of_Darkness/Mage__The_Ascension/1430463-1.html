    Originally posted by No One of Consequence View Post
    IIRC...
    That is how I generally handle the issue as well.

    Vampires can't learn Sorcery. That is what Thaumaturgy is for. Though they could use their study of sorcery as a very hard way to unlock the secrets of Thaumaturgy. This is more of a roleplay justification of learning it separately from one of the established thaumaturgical traditions. I would allow ghouls and Revenants to learn this. But what regnant would want their ghoul to become so independently powerful? Too much blowback could happen. But I definitely see reclusive Obertus and the like learning such powers. I think they would need to tread carefully though since any visiting Sabbat pack might destroy them for such temerity.

    Werewolves and other Changing Breeds could, but their culture is biased against it. Other than Uktena and other exceptions, they'd be discouraged and perhaps even be hit with Renown loss. Occult oriented characters would be pointed towards more acceptable ways to explore their interest. If you want to create fire when you need it, it's best to exchange chiminage with Fire elementals and the like, or create a fetish. You don't learn Hellfire. Likewise, Kinfolk would need to be wary of learning any Sorcery that seems counter to Gaian principles. But Kinfolk who safely limit known Paths to things the Garou would approve it, would probably be considered to be valuable members of the kinfolk community as long as they don't try to usurp perogatives best left to the Garou.

    Changelings should have their own innate ways to duplicate anything Sorcery could do, so it should always be a suboptimal choice. I don't know if the actual mechanics bare that out, but they should.

    Wraiths only have Arcanoi as such magic is only for the living. I wouldn't allow Risen to learn either. Nor should they want to - they have to fulfill their mission and return to the grave so such characters should never want to divert themselves by such studies.

                                                        Just because it is paradox free does not mean that the immortality itself is free and permanent. Immortality using sphere magic is the only thing that is maintenance free and permanent, but it comes at the cost of permanent paradox unless you go to the umbra. Immortality using linear magic always has a huge cost. It is not a matter of not being able to pay the cost as even if a wealthy and talented Legendary sorcerer could pay the costs, it would eventually get to a point where even they would slip up or the consensus would force them to. Take the alchemy path for example. A regiment of elixirs that must be consumed once a week or month, would require a sorcerer to spend weeks within their laboratory creating multiple elixirs to stock up on and this would have to be done constantly for the remainder of their existence. Unless you had a cabal of fellow sorcerers who also had Alchemy 6 helping you, doing this alone would be expensive or extremely time consuming. This is what happened to the Tremere as they relied on elixirs of immortality that allowed them to live for centuries, but the elixirs began to fail and they turned to other methods to maintain their immortality.

                                                        And finding other Alchemy 6 sorcerers would be nigh-impossible as such mastery is the very pinnacle of linear Alchemical tradition that only a handful have ever achieved. And once you achieve it, awakening is literally around the corner.
