                Right, we're having one of those discussions I see...

                Time 1: Like all spheres, Time 1 is a sensory sphere. It allows you a perfect an intuitive understanding of time and the effects of time manipulation. You can tell what time it is with perfect accuracy no matter where you are. You can also feel how long something has taken or if you're familiar enough with the process, how long it will take. You can do multiple complex things at once staggered and rest assured you will never miss a single step. You can move in perfect time, keeping a steady beat or a proper frequency up longer than humanly possible without need of reference. Also, Time 1 can be combined with so many other spheres to make effects that last for exactly how long you want them to, or change every hour, every minute, or every day. Time 1 is so very underappreciated.
                This is on top of being able to sense the Time stream itself, which means being aware of any Time 4 effects in the area (big deal to avoid nasty surprises), sensing anyone scrying on you from across time, on interlopers from other times, etc.


                Time 2: Time sense. Just like Corr. 2 allows you to scry through space, Time 2 allows you to scry through time. This makes Time 2 one of the most amazing second level spheres in the game. You can look at possible events as well as past events, and the standard chart gives you a huge range to look through. You can use Time 2 to solve mysteries simply by rewatching the crime as it comes to pass. You can hear the last words of dying mages straight from the horses mouth. You can follow kidnappers who left long ago, allowing their "time shadows" to lead you right to their base. Similarly you can look into the future and be prepared for all kinds of nasty surprises and eventuality. Time 2 has the potential for being the single most Chronicle breaking sphere in the game if it isn't handled correctly. It's gloriously powerful. Combined with other spheres and you can read the minds of the past, see all possible destinies laid out before you, learn how long one has before they die, see exactly what a book looked like in the past (so if the words have faded, that's not a problem, you can read it as though it were newly printed).

                Time 3: Like all 3rd level spheres, you're able to start manipulating Time in much more obvious ways, this is where you get to start really messing around with the Time stream. Time 3 allows you to speed up and slow down events that play out around you. This could be just speeding yourself up, allowing several moves per action without penalty, or slowing your opponents down so you get the same benefit. It could also mean slowing down objects as they drop or speeding up cars as they travel. In the most extreme cases, you could even make a room which is 7x faster on the inside. (1 week inside is 1 day outside), allowing for weeks worth of research/preparation/invention/what have you, to be done without it causing delays in the real world. Heck, taken to the extreme, you could probably fit a whole day into an hour, allowing for days of practice and preparation to pass in the span of an afternoon. Combined with other spheres, you could speed up your own mental processes, slow down your own fate, speed up your own natural healing process (a clever way to heal Agg damage without needing Quintessence).
                And that's not even mentioning the best feature of Time 3. Time looping! The single most headache causing mechanic in the game! Just fell into a trap causing you 5 points of lethal? Nope! undone. Got ambushed by someone? Nope, Undo it and run! Oh no, that guy killed my friend! Nope! Undone, quick now prevent it! Looping time is one of my favourite aspects of Time 3, and if it weren't so heavily regulated, it would destroy a session before it even starts.
                if you

                Time 4: Pretty standard progression here. Time 1 lets you feel time, Time 2 lets you see through it, Time 3 lets you warp it. Now time 4 lets you twist time entirely! You can do the whole "programmed events" thing that's been talked about to death already. But you can also do that with normal things as well. You can pause people, things, events, places, or whatever! You can make explosions stop exploding and move them elsewhere. You can get people to freeze in place, monsters to stop, great cataclysmic events to halt, all in the aid of time. If a crystal vase about to fall and shatter? Time 4 it before it hits the ground, then pick it up and put it back. Is your friend about to die ? Freeze em and fix them with more time to deal with things. Oh no! That portal in the umbra is about to close! Quick! Time 4 it so we can run through it first! Time 4 is everything you want a high level sphere to be! It's so simple, and yet so wonderfully potent!

                Time 5: The best goddamn Master level sphere in the game! Yes you get Time Travel and all the trappings that come with it. Jump around changing things, undoing mistakes or making sure things go different, getting wisdom for the future, even casting spells directly through time into the past or future. Everything you'd expect, and yeah it's fun and all, but it pails in comparison to Time 5's other amazing trick. Time Immunity. It's glorious! You just stop time. You can do whatever the heck you want after that. Take your gun, walk up to them and shoot 5 or 6 times in the chest. Walk right through a chantry's defences and steal whatever you want without ever being stopped. Stop someone from making an escape just by casually searching for them and then tie them up. With Time 5, you are untouchable, and no one can do a thing to you. You always get the last word, and if you're ever surrounded or in any danger, just sidestep time and walk away.
                Best part is that there's nothing the other party can do about this, they won't even know this is happening...unless they have one thing, you know what that is?
                TIME 1! Heck yeah that's necessary!

                If you imagine Time as a stream, then 1 lets you dip your toes in Time, 2 lets you drink from the stream of Time, 3 lets you narrow or widen the banks of the stream and cause eddies in the flow. 4 allows you to move the stream around, making it bend to avoid certain things or places. 5 lets you disconnect from the direction of the flow entirely, allowing you to walk upon the water, standing over its frozen surface either to explore new parts or simply move things around before the stream unfreezes.

                That's how the progression works, and it is beautiful!

                                        Ajax
                                        ​Entropy is actually pretty nifty if you're inventive enough. While the other spheres focus on getting specific things to occur, Entropy works the other way around. It controls the outcome of events, but not the events themselves. This makes it one of the single most subtle spheres in the game. It's also the ultimate sphere of "you can't tie that to me, it just happened"

                                        Also, the things it affects actually can't be done with other spheres.

                                        Entropy 1 allows you to sense events, importance, fortune, and how much chaos there is in any given thing. No other sphere can do this. No other sphere can help you find something specific without knowing what it is first. If you're looking for a sacred idol, sure you could use Matter or Corr to search for it manually, or you could use Entropy to know exactly which box is more important than all the others because it has your sacred idol in it. Job done!

                                        Also, if you're planning an escape route, Entropy 1 makes you basically not have to worry about stealth. You can sense out where the right place to be is and the right path to go down. No need to mind magic yourself easy to ignore or Forces yourself as invisible, you can just get away by knowing where to be and not to be. No other sphere can do that.

                                        Entropy 2 is all about makes things happen the way you want them to. You want the perfect hand in a poker game? Boom, you have it. You want the traffic lights to change just after you cross them, slowing down your pursuer? Boom, it is done, and no petty fiddling around with Forces, Corr to target, and Technology rolls to know if you have the knowledge required to know where to put the Force power to change the lights manually. You need to meet people in a crowd without having to find them? Look who you just bumped into! Oh, but what if you don't want to get found? Well, I'll just decrease the chance of my pursuer coming anywhere near me. Nice! It's all coincidental, you don't have to target anyone (meaning no Corr requirement or proximity to target). Uh oh, I lost my talisman! Let's just Entropy 2 it so I just happen to find it in the first place I look. This is your basic "Get Stuff Done" sphere, no other 2nd level sphere grants you this.

                                        Entropy 3. Now we're getting into the cool stuff. Anything predictable is under your purview. Make machines work better or stop working being the most obvious one. It sounds drab when you put it like that. But think about it. There's a security camera up there, Boom, All it can see is static, no need to break it with Forces. Coincidental and Innocent. Just what you want for stealth. Quick we have to get away! But all we have is this rickety old car! hmm. Either I could try to fix it up mundanely with some Matter assistance, or I could just Entropy it to work well enough while I need it. Which one do you think is going to take less time? (read fewer successes, less need of an extended ritual, and requires a 3 dot sphere rather than a 4 dot sphere to do efficiently)

                                        However, this can also be applied to predictable processes, so computer programs that run on a schedule, you can totally cause that schedule to donk up, meaning suddenly the entire office's computer system is locked out due to "scheduled" maintenance, The perfect way to delay your Technocrats. Cause guns to misfire while they're in their holsters, get walls to collapse with exactly the right hit. Or do the opposite, make your walls uncannily strong, have your guns fire so well and so precisely it is literally impossible to botch with them. Make your phone work even if it's been plunged into the ocean. So subtle but so very effective. Entropy you beast!

                                        Entropy 4. Life is Dynamic, it kinda does what it wants. All you can do with Life magic is say what you want it to do and try to guide it down that route. Entropy, on the other hand, lets you control the outcome (and leave the life pattern to get on with it on its own). You want to get rid of disease? Bam, done. You want to give someone a disease? They be sick! You want someones skin to just start pealing off? Off it goes! You want to look great? Bam, you do. You want the garden to grow beautifully? Bam it's there. With Life magic you'd have to control each and every single thing directly, and chances are it'll be vulgar as hell. Not Entropy 4 though. It gets stuff done, and no need to multi sphere to do it!

                                        Entropy 5. This is where things get scary. You basically control people and events as though they were a hive mind. You can make everyone decide to put up their umbrellas at exactly the right time to hide beneath them. You could manipulate ideas so that they just happen to come up at the opportune time. You can basically inception the Mind mage without them ever knowing what you've done. Oh yeah, you can also just straight up rewrite peoples fates. Damn that's powerful. Sorry, I don't feel like being a martyr today, I'll just make it so John is the martyr now. Good luck John!

                                        That's the other big thing about Entropy. It's the get stuff done sphere that lets you touch a whole host of things that, for any other sphere, you'd need other spheres to do. With Entropy 5, you don't need Mind, Matter, Forces, or Life to still affect the outcome of these things. Talk about sphere economy! No other sphere allows this level of fluidity. This also means getting around wards and blocks made with other spheres, which can always grant on the edge.

                                        Mixed with other spheres, you can reek all kinds of havoc that can't be done without Entropy.

                                        1 for sensing weak areas in structures, feeling out cracks in the gauntlet, knowing where or when important things are going to happen.
                                        2 for randomising your own thoughts, controlling the randomness of umbra portals
                                        3 for Strengthening or weakening bans or wards, making certain processes like Time 4 pause effects not succumb to the effects of Paradox (allowing them to last longer).
                                        4 for making pattern bleed happen less, or not at all depending on the strength of it all.
                                        5 for basically becoming the god of circumstance. Added with mind and you can make specific thoughts and ideas just happen. Make specific events in Time or Space go more or less the way you want them to. And oh yeah, basically not have to worry about Spirits getting in your face.

                                        While the other spheres deal with patterns directly. I like to think of Entropy as dealing with the space around these patterns, the things which govern how they work.

                                        If you don't like Entropy just because "other spheres can do it" then it could simply be that someone's playing Entropy wrong. It covers a really specific and unique aspect of the game that some of the other spheres may allude to, but none of the other spheres grasp.

                                        But hey, if you want Entropy to be useless in your game, by all means have it be useless in your game.

                                                        Flames of Love Forces 3 or 5, Time 4, Prime 2 ; possibly Correspondence 2, 3 or 4.
                                                        This idea behind this rote is simple - use the passion of sex to power a fireball or firestorm effect. In the case of Forces 3, the focus for this spell is a single cigarette left from a pack smoked during an amorous meeting. (The pack doesn't have to be a full pack - a pack of two cigarettes works just as well, though not for those still learning the rote.) The fireball is contained within the cigarette, and using Prime and Forces sights allows to know how strong the blast will be.
                                                        The cigarette can be smoked at any time, the act of which hangs the fireball at the place of the smoking, centered at the point where the butt is dropped. The time until the explosion is determined by the length of smoking the cigarette - so burning through one in 20 seconds will mean the Mage has to get out of there are very quickly, while a normal smoke of 2-3 minutes will give the Mage plenty of time.
                                                        (The length of smoking the cigarettes with one's paramour does not matter.)

                                                        In order for this rote work correctly, the Mage can't move more than 3 meters out of the point where he lit the cigarette. The butt must also be dropped - not flicked or thrown - for this rote to work. Trying to drop the butt from a skyscraper or a higher floor causes the rote to fail or botch.
                                                        If Correspondence 2 is added, the Mage can move around after lighting the cigarette, as much as he wants; but he must keep smoking and holding the cigarette normally - no hiding of it under the coat or inside a bag! The fireball is hanged at the point where the butt is dropped.
                                                        If Correspondence 3 is added, the Mage can move around, and the butt can be flicked - the fireball is hanged on the butt the moment it leaves the Mage's hand. The butt can be flicked from a bridge or into a building through a window etc., and the spell will work.
                                                        With Correspondence 4, the Mage can hang the spell so that, by lighting a lighter and whispering his name, the Mage's paramour can alert him to danger to her. This allows him to view the location of his lover, and, if he lights the cigarette in response, creates a co-location of the Mage next to her. This allows him to smoke the cigarette and hang the fireball at the location - hopefully in such a way that protects his lover.

                                                        If the cigarette is smoked by anyone else but the Mage, it is the Mage who suffers the consequences, if any occur. (Sometimes the spell simply dissipates.)

                                                        With Forces 5 and organizing an intricate orgy, a Master of Forces can unleash a firestorm. A minimum of five cigarettes are needed to store the flames; examining them with Forces and Prime sights inform of the spell's potency. The inferno can be shaped by dropping the cigarette butts in various locations - or stacking them all in the same place. After smoking the last cigarette, the Mage can decide when the blaze will occur - but the countdown can be no shorter than the length of the quickest smoking involved, and no longer than the combined time for which all the cigarettes were smoked. The Forces 5 version can be combined with the Correspondence additions.
                                                        The persons who took part in the orgy can later help in unleashing the blaze by smoking the cigarettes - even if they are unaware of what they are contributing towards.
