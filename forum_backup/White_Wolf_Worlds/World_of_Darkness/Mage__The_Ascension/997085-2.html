    I actually understand the difference between a Time 3 "Time Loop" and a Time 3 Mind-sphere-requiring "Reboot" like this:

    * In the Time-Loop, the Mage themselves stay in place while their surroundings change back. The Mage themselves don't rewind, only the events around them.
    * In a Reboot, the Mage's body itself also rewinds, moving back into place where they were before - And to prevent the mind from re-setting in this case as well, the Mind sphere is needed.

    Now, why should anybody use the Reboot then, if it requires the Mind sphere?
    There are a few advantages:
    * Any damage and physical afflictions they caught during that time are healed, as their own body is also re-set.
    * Re-setting their own position and state makes sure that everything will happen exactly as it happened before, and how they remember it. Which is not a given in a Time-Loop, since the mage's position and physical state might be off, thus causing others around them to behave differently this time around.

            Originally posted by tzizimine View Post
            Example (The Book of Chantries, pg. 35) : Magickal Ratings: Correspondence -2, Forces +4, Life +2, Matter -1, Mind +1, Prime +2
            Ohh, that book is oooold. Like, First Edition old. Really old. Old old. Use it for fluff, but not for rules, I'd say.

            As for the Sphere ratings per realm in it, they are explained in the 1st Edition corebook of mage (Page 181), and...they *COULD* be valid in your Mage game, but are kind of an exotic mechanic:

            * Using Magick of a sphere that has an equal or lower rating than indicated by the number succeeds automatically, without a roll. We can already see how broken that is.
            * Using magick of a sphere rating that is higher than the number needs rolling and rules as usual.
            * The indicated number being negative actually means that even mundane tasks that fall under the sphere's purview would require magic to do. Example from the book: A realm with Correspondence -4 is essentially a 1-dimensional realm with two directions: Forward and back. Actually lifting something would *require* the use of Magick.

            Interesting, but broken due to the very first point already.


            My suggestion:
            If you want to use the ratings in a modern edition Mage game, switch the negative numbers to positive, and positive to negative. Treat them as difficulty modifiers from the base. I.e. Life +3 would *lessen* difficulty by 3.
            Remember that the max difficulty difference from base is +/-3, so in a realm with Matter 4, you would actually get 3 less difficulty for matter magick, but a situation that would bump the difficulty up by 1 from base still gets negated due to the 4 being one higher than the max of -3 difficulty from base..

                                Moving on to another blurry rule... Changes in Coincidence/Vulgarity After Casting?

                                So, Joe Q. Mage casts Effect X at/on Target Y. The only requirements for Effect X are that is is long lasting (probably more than a scene, but definitely more than a single turn) and that Effect X starts off coincidental in one set of completely natural circumstances and would be vulgar (with or without witness) in another. There are no requirements for Target Y other than to support the duration of Effect X.

                                Would Joe Q. Mage get paradox when Effect X, starting as coincidental, is revealed as vulgar?
                                On a related note, would the change in vulgarity have an impact on successes already rolled?

                                Example 1:
                                Joe just made a really elaborate sidewalk chalk drawing on a nice clear day on the boardwalk of his local beach. He's proud of it and wants it to stay intact while he gets his friends to come see. He weaves a coincidental Matter effect to make the chalk drawing impossible to be erased. The chalk becomes connected to the surface of the sidewalk. He gets enough successes for a whole day and gets on his bike and rides off to the bar down the street to get the gang, People walk on the chalk drawing, but it doesn't get scuffed or smudged and no one really notices or cares. Effect: Coincidental

                                About an hour after Joe leaves, a car driving on the boardwalk swerves to miss a pedestrian and crashes in a fire hydrant, which is now spraying water all over the chalk drawing.... which doesn't wash away even after prolonged exposure. Effect: Vulgar with Witness.

                                Would Joe get paradox from the washing of his art project?

                                Would Joe notice the change in vulgarity before the paradox hits, giving him a moment to dismiss his effect?

                                Could Joe even dismiss his own effect, since he isn't near by when the vulgarity changes (assuming he doesn't have Correspondence at all) ?

                                While it wouldn't really matter in this example, would the change in vulgarity effect the number of successes that Joe rolled back when it was still coincidental?


                                Example 2:
                                Jane knows she is going into combat against some vampires and weaves a Life 3 effect to enhance her skin/muscle tissue/bones to make it impossible to breach the skin or break a bone, thus converting most kinds of lethal damage to bashing damage, thus soakable. Again, the duration lasts for a day (long enough for 2 scenes). During the first scene, she takes some punishment (stabs, claws, a gun shot), but ultimately she is goes down from breathing some toxin. One of her non-mage allies gets her out of combat and races to the hospital. Effect: Coincidental or Vulgar without Witnesses

                                At the hospital, the nurses manage to get Jane on an oxygen feed and begins filtering the toxins out, but are unable to get a needle into Jane's skin, since her protection effect is still preventing it. Effect: Vulgar with Witnesses

                                Would Jane get (more) paradox from the nurses witnessing the effect on her skin?

                                Would the effect collapse because Jane is unconscious and it's being examined?

                                If it collapses, would the wounds that Jane has already soaked as bashing become lethal? or would they stay bashing?

                                        tzizimine

                                        My thoughts

                                        Example 1 (the chalk)
                                        The effect is not necessarily vulgar since there are ways to have waterproof chalk. If you think the water is too much/the force of it too powerful than it exceeds the parameters of the initial effect, veering from resistant to erasure to nearly impossible to erase at which point the drawing is erased because the chosen effect wasn't powerful enough to protect the chalk art (ie coincidental vs vulgar).

                                        Example 2 (Soaking Lethal)
                                        Vulgar without witness, and the needle wouldn't necessarily be prevented from entering. Often shrugging off damage makes what would be a fatal blow into a flesh wound. The needle goes through because its not 'damage' in the mechanical sense, this is because the effect you described was not her trying to make her skin bulletproof, but rather make bullet wounds less fatal. Wounds mitigated in this way remain bashing since they were already altered by the effect.

                                        Example 1 (fireproof coat)
                                        If he wants the coat itself to be repelling the Forces he needs Matter 2 to lock the effect to the coat, otherwise he is using a Forces effect around the area of the coat that he has to direct. If he locks it to the coat then the effect is probably coincidental so long as the coat appears as if it could be made of fireproof material (so a nice trenchcoat would whereas a battered one probably not). Also it would only protect the areas covered by the coat, so unless hes ducking down and pulling his head, hands, and feet in then those parts are still going to be burned otherwise the effect is vulgar since it is obviously pushing the flames away not merely resistant to them.

                                        Example 2 (Coat for a friend)
                                        It depends if you want the coat to actually repel the flames, or simply have extra soak vs flames

                                        Repel Flames - Wonder (Talisman) Prime 4 Forces 2 Matter 2, 10+ Quintessence
                                        This would be crafting a wonder (device or talisman) and would work as follows. You need Prime 4 in addition to Forces 2 . You need 5 quintessence to be infused into the object per dot of the wonder as well as 1 quintessence per roll made to craft it. Each dot represents a level of Sphere effect, Arete, and number of effects the item can have. So your Matter 2 Forces 2 effect would require 2 dots thus 10 quintessence. The wonder would have an Arete of 2 and 10 quintessence and would activate when a fire would affect it, rolling its Arete as a Forces 2 effect to repel the flames. Again the effect is likely coincidental if it would make sense that the jacket could be fireproof and the protection only applies to what the coat covers. (Similar to Bafflejack)

                                        Soak Dice - Wonder (Trinket) Prime 2 Forces 2 Matter 2, 10+ Quintessence
                                        If you wanted it to be a trinket (requiring only Prime 2 in addition to Forces 2 although still requiring 5 quintessence per Wonder dot and the 1 quintessence per roll made while crafting) you could have it grant soak dice vs Fire (again only applying to flames that the coat could appear to protect against), as a level 2 wonder you could have 3 dice to soak Fire damage (since its aggravated damage you can get 1 dice per 2 background points using the advantages chart as a guideline). (Similar to Bulletproof Hoodie)

                                        Example 3 (Discharge damage knuckles/bullet) - Wonder (Trinket) Prime 2 Forces 2 Matter 2, 10+ Quintessence
                                        A single use trinket that requires re-enchanting/is consumed by the use (knuckles/bullet) would require Prime 2 Forces 2 Matter 2 and 10 quintessence plus 1 per roll made while crafting. The item then contains the successes until it hits the target at which point the effect is expelled (remember that M20 suggests a cap of 20 health levels of damage on a Magickal attack). The limits on bullets is the available quintessence, time, and the ST.

                                                11-17-2016, 10:39 AM
                                                HDYDT states that paradox only gets applied retroactively in the rarest of cases.
                                                Pattern Bleeding, and similiar things, are examples of paradox troubles long after the spell has been cast. But usually, a paradox backlash only occurs on casting the effect.

                                                However, that doesn't mean that I'd not use certain mechanics on the discovery of a clearly vulgar running effect by sleepers.
                                                I suggest this:
                                                An ST should keep track of the actual result of rolls that made up an effect, which can get potentially discovered by sleeprs as vulgar at a later time.

                                                Once the sleepers spot the IMPOSSIBLE THING, look at the rolls that were made, and raise the difficulty to Vulgar With Witness levels, which will usually reduce the successes of the effect.
                                                Does the effect still have enough duration left to sustain itself?
                                                If not, have it crumble and vanish under the weight of disbelief. Poof. This also kind of thematically mirrors the way that Bygones got/get purged over time by the consensus.

                                                EDIT:
                                                Last but not least, be critical of judging things as vulgar too quickly.
                                                Always ask yourself: 'How would I react in RL'.
                                                Example: How would you react in RL if you saw a chalk drawing, and a burst of water does not wash it away?
                                                I highly suspect you would simply think 'Oh neat, they are making waterproof chalk now'. Or probably not even spend that much of a thought on it.

                                                Always, always consider your hypothetical mage-neutral subjective RL reaction first. We live in a time where technology and general belief in New Things Everyday does not only benefit the Technocracy...
