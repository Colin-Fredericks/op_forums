    Originally posted by Hello View Post
    Since pc's can simply use magick to help generate wealth, such as using entropy and time to help play the stock market, why would players want to take resources as a background and not invest in something different?
    This frees up time and effort in regard to Magick Effects that would be used to generate or secure resources and wealth.
    EArning money, creating resources, engaging in trade, and crafting item also helps hone and improve mundane skills.
    Generating wealth fairly and in a mudane way also helps secure social networks and group meetings, and is verifable proof of responsibiltiy, desire, ambition, and patience.
    In many completely or mostly mudane earnign and handling of money, resources, and valuebles is faster and more reliable compared to Magick Effects that can be used for these purposes.

            Originally posted by Dataweaver View Post
            I spoke specifically of owning an apartment. Not “a property” or “a house”; an apartment. Apartment buildings are owned; but I've never seen individual apartments in a building sold to their occupants.
            I lived in an apartment complex that sold its individual units to investors. I know of apartment complexes that run as condominiums in the US, Canada, and the UK. And I've heard of other systems of ownership in Denmark, Italy, and Ireland. So owning individual apartments is a thing. And it is a commonplace thing.

            Also, the one- and two-dot levels speak of “you've got an apartment”, which is at best about as to ownership: colloquially, someone who's renting an apartment speaks of “having an apartment”. It isn't until the third dot that ownership is explicitly mentioned; and there, it's “you own or have significant equity in a house”.
            This makes sense. Maybe specify that 0 in resources means an efficiency (a bathroom, a closet, and a bedsitting room with kitchenette) or a roommate. Actual homelessness could be a separate flaw.

            Logically, a Mage might have good resources and still be homeless. Think of wandering mages that never stay anyplace for more than a few weeks. Other mages might simply be outside the economy. And a few dead broke. But homelessness for a Mage is somewhat separate from resources.

            Also, a Mage might be destitute but have a place to stay. Both V and Gene Hackman's Lex Luthor made abandoned subway tunnels into comfortable homes. You say that's not realistic. I say we're talking about Mages. The Mansion merit already covered this. There is a background in Adventure! 1st edition that covers hideouts etc. It could be adapted to Mage.
