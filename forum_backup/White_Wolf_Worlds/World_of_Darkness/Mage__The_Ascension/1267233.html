    Help! with my Super Chronicle

    11-14-2018, 05:59 AM
    I've embarked on a massive chronicle that revolves around metaplot but still leaves room for regular stories. the plan is to take the characters through 30 years ending with the apocalypse scenarios, with the twist of actually saving the world, or with the traditions rebuilt from ruin



    The Story Thus Far
    it's 1993, The Luxor and The Black Pyramid Chantry, yes i'm using fallen tower, has just opened and the pc's where invited to join after taking out a nephandic proffesor. They met important npc's at the grand opening of the luxor. and then were sent by Ralph Cannon to help Red with his "UFO investigation" Red presented the cabal with a crazy plan to shoot down a ufo with a jurry riged missle launcher, reds a tech sorcerer with hellfire, and then scavenge the ship for alien tech. He gets abducted in the middle of the night and in the morning the cabal tries to save him by shooting down a UFO that their radar picked up. It turned out to be a void ship investigating the spacial disturbance left over from reds abduction, they killed 5 of it's crew in the initial blast. The dreamspeaker character had shape shifted into a crazy chimera monster after the ships initial defenses tried to capture them. so when they boarded the vesel he was still in chimera form so it's sole survivor Major Thomas Houston knew they where devients and attacked the pc's knock him unconscious they loot the ship and get out befor the technocracy sends a recovery team.


    The outline for the chronicles.
    The PC's will go to Doissetep shortly before it's fall where they'll uncover a conspiracy to assassinate porthos fitz empress this story would climax with Doisseteps fall and the cabal escaping to Horizon. I would love some ideas for a story their, i havn't gotten through much of horizons book yet. by 2000 the pc's should be back on earth. the first idea i had was their forced to flee Horizon Mid avatar storm The archmages would combining their power to hold off the storm while Horizon is evacuated many will be shreded by the storm in the attempt to flee and eventualy the archmagi will be overrun. the players survive the evacuation.
    the other idea i had is that the players return during the week of nightmares and witness Zapathasura and his destruction first hand, possibley raiding horizons armory in hopes of fighting him
    I would like to know what book covers the week of nightmares, and if they don't already exist come up with stats for zap, and yes I want the players to survive possibley even actually dent the damn thing befor the nukes and sun sattelite and i know that's not what whitewolf intended but "fuck you it's a creative medium" XD love that quote. after the avatar storm the players will be investigating the transmissions of the rouge council and rebuilding the traditions potentially becoming leadership figures for their respective traditions. The Chronicle could end their but i have a crazy idea. Now is where the outline blurs into just an idea I haven't looked into it too much, and i'm not sure if it's even feasible but the awsome idea is that after the traditions revocer from winter into spring, each and every one of the end time scenarios from the Ascension book will play out one after the other, with custom victory endings, the Chronicle ends proper after Hell on Earth again with a victory option.

    that's my crazy overly ambitions Chronicle outline. any ideas for how to get it form A to Z. the imediate goal is getting it from A(story thus far) to B (Doissetep)

    PS apologies for any spelling mistakes please don't point them out.
