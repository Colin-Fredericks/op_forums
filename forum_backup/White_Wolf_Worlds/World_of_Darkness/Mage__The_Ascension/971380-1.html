                                                        There we go, sense at last! Still not sure how it relates to the original point, but let me take a crack at it. You already touched upon their rejection of Antediluvian mythology, the weaknesses of which are more a product of not being adequately explored in the text. As an extension of that Elder and Antediluvian cults are present beneath the surface of the sect, as are Infernalists and secret Path followers, any of which if exposed would threaten the Camarilla's gilded edifice and compromise what little ideology they profess to have. Somehow I don't think that's what you're looking for though. The Camarilla doesn't really have skeletons in the closet because their skeletons are on the living room couch. The Camarilla only maintains the barest pretense of being a morally upright organization, their foundations are built on power and realpolitik. The only secret that would really shake those foundations is if it turned out they really had no power at all. Certainly the Antediluvian-denial is related to that, but in the end the Camarilla's pyramid scheme nature makes their power and strength (while not at all monolithic) a self-fulfilling prophecy.
