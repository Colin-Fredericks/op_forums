    Originally posted by Aleph View Post
    While a technomancer can grab the latest invention and scratch a rune at it, the Technocrat has to use the invention the way it was intended to be used, and has to work hard for years to create and popularize the next big thing. And there's nothing they can do to prevent the shaman from invoking the spirit of the next big thing and quickly advance magick both in his eyes and in the eyes of his people.
    Right, a Technocrat has to work hard for years to create AND popularize the next big thing, but really, they don't need to popularize something to be able to do it. One is praxis. The other is adherence to the Time Table. Technocrats are making up next big things all the time in seconds flat. Note that using their magic does not demand "Extra Time: Months" and/or "R&D lab with appropriate materials required". They can jury-rig thing X to have it do function y, even it's designed to do function x in the field. Even in the space of several seconds if the need it pressing.

    Which means they really don't do things very different from other mages at all. In terms of Focus as defined as a game concept, the Union's insistence on the blinders on what they are doing fundamentally are really just rank sophistry. And counter-productive at that, because the fact that those other guys who do magic are obviously doing exactly the same things functionally means that Conditioning and Reconditioning for ANY operatives that come into contact with other ways of doing things is going to be frequent and brutal.

    And there are so many EASIER ways to explain the whole situation that work much, much better. "We do this stuff (change the nature of reality) the SAFE way. That's what technology is. They are playing fast and loose with the rules of reality and it's DANGEROUS. That's why they need to be stopped. For the good of reality as whole. For the good of humanity. For their own good."

    And that's just ONE.
