    Sure thing. I enjoyed your debate too.

    But I don't think the issue is really between your version of the Technocracy and mine. I'm perfectly fine that the Technocracy has evolved into a tyrannical, authoritarian organization based on control doing all the evil sorts of things you mentioned in your first post. (And the fact that they cause great harm doesn't mean they can't do good at the same time - Mussolini made the trains run on time, and the Nazis instituted the world's first environmental laws, animal protection acts, restrictions on tobacco because of health issues, and improved welfare. But that didn't change that they were criminal tyrants.)

    I think the difference is that your Traditions are too nice. It's that you are insisting the Traditions are this group of goody White Hats who only want the best interests on mankind as opposed to flawed people who are sometimes prone to abusing their own powers, or concentrating on their own solipsistic obsessions. To me, the Traditions are much more grey despite being spiritually enlightened to a degree and many coming from altruistic backgrounds. There are good members and bad members, and even the good members sometimes end up doing bad things. And there are simply limits to what they can achieve because of Paradox or whatever.

        Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post
        It's a fundamental fact that the Traditions are built around the idea of Mass Ascension. They are not, never, and never will be about hoarding magic since the establishment of the Nine. The Technocracy is also built around this idea. Neither party actually has any disagreement that everyone in the world should Awaken, theoretically.
        Yes and no. First, I never said that the Traditions are about hoarding magick: anyone who can Awaken is welcomed with open arms, and the Traditions encourage Awakenings to the best of their ability. It's just that the primary value they attach to Sleepers lies with their potential to Awaken.

        Second, the Traditions may express an interest in theory of everyone Awakening; but most acknowledge that that's an unattainable ideal; certainly not something that's going to be achieved in the foreseeable future. And not just because of existing conditions in the ground; unless and until they find a reliable way to Awaken people, there's no way to attain Mass Ascension.

        As far as the Technocracy goes, I'm not sure that they are interested in Mass Ascension. Sure, they've got their Utopian visions of the future; but by and large, every last one of them would put some sort of restriction on who can Awaken. Not because they don't want to empower people; they arguably do, even now. But from their perspective, Awakening everyone would be the height of irresponsibility. So you really want to give that kind of power to madmen and monsters who just want to see the world burn? Because Marauders and Nephandi are a thing, even if you're willing to accept that the Traditions' behavior is tolerable (which, outside of the Syndicate and the Void Engineers, isn't a popular view).

        Meanwhile, the Traditions are like the gun nuts who want everyone to be able to own a firearm, no matter what.

        Put another way, Mass Ascension may not be the great good that the Traditions make it out to be.

        Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post
        There was the EVIL Progenitor plan in the original 1st Edition books to get rid of avatars so the Sleepers could be controlled but that was an example of EVIL [mind you, in RL, we know there's actually plots by the Far Right to destroy education in America and other countries because they believe it will help the Masses be easier to control].
        Actually, that would be plots by the Far Left to pervert education in America and other countries because they believe it will help the Masses be easier to control. See my suggestion to review the Politics in M20 thread: the Technocracy isn't Right Wing, and the Traditions aren't liberal champions; nor is the opposite true. Setting aside whether or not the stereotypes you ascribe to the IRL political wings are overly simplistic and/or just plain wrong, the in-game groups bear at best a passing resemblance to them.

        Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post

        No; it's saying that the Technocracy may have a valid point or two, which is something that the game has done. But I'll note that you didn't actually address my argument; you just said “it makes the Technocracy look good, so it must be wrong” without any mention of what's wrong with it.
