    I think they operate at different levels. The Syndicate doesn't care (much) about a huge Swiss bank account that's never accessed, or a basement full of Nazi gold. It's dead, just like the vampire that controls it. The Syndicate cares about money as an object of universal desire, and the transactions being performed with it. Every time someone exchanges something valuable for money, they cement the power that the Syndicate has. So why would you want a basement full of gold not being transacted? It's like a beautiful cathedral in which no one goes to worship. You want a respectable GDP growth rate; you want high consumer confidence; you want low inflation and a sale at Wal-Mart. You don't need to have all the money and all the businesses; you need to people to want money more than anything else, and for money to buy the stable existence they crave.

    Vampires want money as another way for them to exert control. The Syndicate wants money itself to be able to exert control. The two goals don't conflict.

                        The funny thing is, in a Mages vs Vampires game, I'd actually say that the Mages might have the most trouble with the Gangrel over everyone else. They will be hardest to track, effectively vanishing from even magical eyes each time they Earthmeld, (and being immune to even Astral attacks). They have the supernatural toughness that they can ignore/mitigate a lot of the physical attacks (as a whole), have the ability to form a spy network through animals that Mages are much less likely to try to protect or defend against, tend to communicate in ways that the Technocracy might think is too old fashioned to consider, and unlike many other vampires, do actually communicate with each other fairly regularly, do have the potential to actually make it into outer space on their own, might easily be misidentified as a Garou, (meaning a lot of their Magik will not work, focusing incorrectly on a living target rather than an Undead one), and capable of sending Mages into a berserker state at a distance.

                        It's a bit far fetched, but just imagine a horde of Gangrel flying through space as bats with sonar and nightvision, turning to mist or earthmelding with meteors when the sun is a little too much, tracking down satellites and space stations to unleash bestial unholy hell. I find that idea funny as heck.
