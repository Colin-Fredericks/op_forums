                Regarding Technocracy versus Werewolf, whoever sees and shoots first is probably going to win. Both possess powerful enough alpha-strike capabilities and countermeasures that the side which has the opportunity to prepare said options is going to be the one with an advantage. And in the case of both groups being surprised, it will be a crapshoot depending on the tools each group happens to have in their toolbox without anticipating the situation. I'd be inclined to agree that Jam Technology should work on Technocrat gear, but I'd be surprised if Technocrats built their nanobots to be vulnerable to mundane magnetism.

                Back on topic!

                I feel like the question of whether or not Mages/Technocrats can create some kind of facsimile of a Changing Breed, some kind of shapeshifter based on a particular type of animal with multiple forms and other supernatural advantages or powers, is obviously true. So I think the more interesting question is whether or not they could create a genuine Mockery Breed, an artificially-created Fera that possesses traits like Rage and Gnosis. And whether or not that should be possible is going to depend on a particular question: whether or not the creation of the original Mockery Breeds required the involvement of a high-level spirit like a Maeljin or some other kind of Wyrmish Incarna.

                If Pentex could do it without needing some kind of powerful spirit patron to provide traits like Gnosis for their Mockery Breeds, then I feel like it should be possible for a Mage. If they weren't relying on a powerful Bane, then Pentex probably did use the Technephandi from the Special Projects Division as part of the process. And if they didn't receive help from an Incarna or a Mage, then I'd have a hard time buying the idea that they could do it but a Mage could not.

                    I think a better framework for addressing the "But why didn't Mages do it?" would be the Alchemical Demiurge trait from Exalted (specifically, the second edition presentation of Alchemical Exalted). Only mortals can have it, so even Solars that have every Craft, Lore, and Occult Charm and cough out five dot Artifacts can't just make their own Champions willy nilly; in fact, they can't do it at all, because it requires being linked to Autochthon in a way that only mortals without power are, and Solars vastly outstripped that the day they took their Second Breath.
