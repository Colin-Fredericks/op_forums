    Blood Treachery

    10-13-2019, 02:11 PM
    I am seeking advice from my fellow gamers. Tomorrow night's game, my character and the other players are facing off against a gang of vampires. Last week left off on a cliff hanger. I am pretty confident that I can handle it. I *am* a Hermetic Magus after all . And I have a hunch the GM knows what I am going to do. The title of this thread, Blood Treachery, is an old M3 book I got used in great shape. I forget the name of the spell (I will print it out to bring to the game), but I intend to fill the room with sunlight. (90% certain the GM is expecting it, but there is a 10% doubt. As a long time GM myself, I have sympathy for the SG challenge of dealing with random ideas from players. I also try to bump the other players into the plot hat hand (beneficial metagaming). That is background info. Also, I am a Sorcerers Crusade mage, and the setting is New Jersey 1775.

    My question is not about tactics though. It is about strategy. If I do this, and I realize it will be as vulgar as fuck, I am pretty sure that I have to wipe out every single one so word doesn't get out that I cast spells like that one. More sticky points. I have been saddled with two apprentices that were given to me by an ancient vampire. One that is waaaay more powerful than I. I want to burn it, but I knew I had to parley. I am guessing he would be resistant to the sunlight spell? If not, clue me in. I know little about vampires.
    And these two apprentices, I guess the fiend has some lingering humanity, and wanted to remove these mortal children from the horror they faced for their future.Out of game, I know they are Revenants. In game it was hinted to me. I did not know what that meant, until I read about it in Blood Treachery. I need to keep my character from knowing what I know, or he will set them on fire.
    So, BIG question. Is there any way to cure them? Or at least, is it feasible for my character to *think* it is possible. Is setting them on fire the better option? Am I overreacting in sheer horror from what I read in Blood Treachery?
    As a side note, these two kids (actually adults but they look like they are 10), they are with my character in this scene. My plan is to use them as shield grogs to give me time to cast the spell. And, from what I read, they should be able to handle themselves. If they are to be *my* students, they need to learn that to be a Magus Flambeau, one must be both soldier and sorcerer.

                  Mechanically it has always been lackluster. Basically, sunlight hits a vampire and they take damage. They can soak it, but only with fortitude. The Soak difficulty varies based on how they are dressed. Basically naked is like DC 8 or 9, heavy clothing + sunscreen is like 6-7-8 (I forget which).

                  The damage is rather lackluster but I don't think the damage is rolled, only soak is rolled. It's like 3 aggravated a turn. I know I said lackluster but in the lore a vampire hitting sunlight basically explodes and dies, while in practice if you are lucky and have some fortitude you can see the light of day and get away pretty easily.

                  Resistance: Not that I'm aware of. There are some thaumaturgy rituals that may help and maybe high level fortitude. If you are a thinblood there is a merit (Atleast in V5) that the sunlight doesn't hurt you, but you can't do supernatural shit while in it and have half health.

                  Damage in mechanical terms: They hit sunlight, their only hope is to have fortitude and soak. Barring that: They take like 3 aggravated, it may be a different number and it may vary by how much sunlight hits them. They also have to roll to resist fear frenzy.

                  How does sunlight effect vampires: Unless thinblood + Merit, they explode. (Well, burn quickly). Vampires with 10 humanity (Atleast in V5) take half damage from sunlight. In V5 they also take damage equal to their blood potency, so they literally take more damage the stronger their blood is. Take that you cursed fucks.

                      Sunlight, like fire, is a powerful existential threat to a creature that doesn't fear much so you'd hope it would cause a panic response at least. This is called Rotschrek (The Red Fear) and according to V20 rules the Vampire flees in a blind panic from the source of their fear. The panic can be avoided with a Courage roll. Whether a panic response is useful to you, depends upon too much for me to comment on here...

                      As a side note, do you know whether your ST is running the full Vampire the Masquerade rules? When I've ran cross-overs I've tended to keep to the spirit of the other splat's rules rather than their specific rulesets for various reasons.
