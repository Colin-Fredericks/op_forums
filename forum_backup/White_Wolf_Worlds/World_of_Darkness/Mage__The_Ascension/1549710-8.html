                            There is definitely a certain amount of there being a difference between "My Focus is built around compelling spirits to enact my will, but a fireball is still Forces 3/Prime 2," and, "I'm trying to use Spirit 3 to convince a fire-spirit to burn something," going on here.

                            While Forces can certainly influence fundamental subatomic interactions (gravitation and EM are already explicitly so), there's still a difference between manipulating the gravitational interaction between a person and the Earth (allowing for levitation/flight, or pressing someone into the ground) with just forces, and completely altering a human's personal gravity well (would require Life to lock on at a minimum if not more-so as you're altering the Forces within a Life pattern).

                            Unraveling a target by destroying the forces holding their atoms together just sounds like a basic Forces attack with a unique choice of energy due to Focus rather than anything else. Trying to completely restructure aluminum into combat viable steel without using Matter would - like cajoling a fire-spirit into burning something - require a specific set of circumstances where you can control such interactions on a fine basis without any interference or any collateral impact (and frankly without Mind or Time, even that's a bit of a stretch considering how fast everything is going at that scale). The idea that you could go beyond that into fictional hypertech materials with just Forces is pushing it... manipulating strong and weak interactions isn't going to let you make Vibranium or Adamantium because if they could, those materials would actually exist. Convincing a fire-spirit to burn something for you with Spirit doesn't mean you can get the spirit to exceed its own capabilities.

                            I don't have much problem with the idea that a Forces 5 character that's done the work to create a highly specific prepared ritual, can copy what a Matter 2 or 3 mage could do with a fraction of the successes and no need of all those extra steps. Just like I don't mind if a Spirit focused mage puts in the work to rely on spirits to do stuff they don't have the Spheres (or don't want to risk the Paradox) to do. The costs are generally going to avoid breaking the game by letting one Sphere do too much.

                                        Base Life

                                        Purview: Living Things and Biology

                                        Special Rules and Limitations

                                        1 - Anything with enough living cells in it to transplant, preserve, or cultivate counts as Life, if it has passed that point it is Matter. This line seems to vary over the book, but that's the distinction given here.

                                        2 - Life attacks naturally inflict Aggravated Damage.

                                        3 - Any radical alteration that is not part of a creature’s original pattern causes Pattern Bleed unless the change is made permanent or the target is supplied with quintessence.

                                            Life 1

                                            • Sense Life

                                            Basic Life knowledge allows a person to read the presence and health of nearby life-forms. With such knowledge, the mage can discern a living being’s age, sex, and overall health. By combining those perceptions with other Spheres, he can also sense distant organisms (Correspondence 2), guess their potential for sickness or misfortune (Entropy 1), perceive them through past or future states (Time 2), or read the streams of Quintessence that bind them to the universe (Prime 1).


                                            This is one of the shorter rank 1 sections and it is padded out with conjunctional effects. Nevertheless, the two main abilities are quite versatile:


                                            Sense Nearby Life (Sense Life)

                                            A mage can sense the presence and patterns of nearby lifeforms regardless of visibility. As with many sphere senses, we can assume the information can be curated in some way, such that the mage can sense the ubiquitous surrounding bacteria but is not overwhelmed by the sheer amount.


                                            Changed: Recognize Non-life

                                            In being able to sense life, a mage should also be able to tell when something he expects to show up on Life senses doesn’t fully. That is, if he’s looking at a wax statue, or a vampire, or an animatronic, or something else that looks alive by his other senses, Life can tell him that it isn’t alive in the classic sense. This wouldn’t tell him much about why it isn’t, that still requires other spheres. This also wouldn’t detect strangeness from things that are fully alive and have special properties.


                                            Vital Statistics (Sense Life)

                                            A mage can also use Life 1 to sense biological information about nearby lifeforms. The examples include demographic data like age and sex, but should also reasonably include things like pulse rate, blood pressure and really anything that can be measured about a life form.


                                            Replacement: A Certain Kind of Scientific Mood Ring Heavy Arms Lord Revan

                                            By parsing the various physiological reactions of the target, the mage can get a general sense of their mood and preparedness for action. Thes will give a mage an advantage in social situations, as well as advanced notice if the target is preparing for physical action.

                                            Human Polygraph Machine

                                            A mage can also read all the vital statistics that a polygraph test reads, like pulse, blood pressure, breathing rate, and skin conductivity. This wouldn’t tell a mage if someone is lying, it would give him the results a real polygraph test would which, if he had the skill to read one, would be exactly as accurate as a real polygraph. How accurate that is depends on the story. Realistically, they are suspect, but many a movie treats them as nigh infallible, so depending on ST this might be quite a useful power.


                                            Added: Sense Natural Weapons

                                            A mage can use Life 1 to sense the various offensive (and for that matter, defensive) capabilities that a target has. This can tell a mage the abilities of creatures he's not familiar with, as well as hidden weapons like retractable claws and venom. This normally only applies to natural weapons, though with enough successes a mage may be able to detect a non-living weapon hidden within (not on, within) a pattern.


                                            Conjunctional Effects

                                            It doesn’t usually work well when M20 pads out the capabilities of spheres by tacking on well-known augments. Casting through space and time is one of the signature purposes of Correspondence and Time respectively. It’s not wrong to say this is a combo, it just is a combo for almost every possible spell and it doesn’t say much about Life 1. I’m also struggling to see what Entropy is adding here, although if the text was ‘sickness and misfortune’ that would make more sense.


                                            Deathsight, Life Edition (Sense Life + Sense Fortune) Life 1 / Entropy 1

                                            Ambiguity Resolution - Sickness and Misfortune

                                            At first glance it seems like Entropy can do this on its own and Life can do most of it. However, they do pair well. A mage can sense the health of a target with Life 1, as well as the strength of their immune system. He can sense their likelihood of being exposed to sickness, or developing a disease by chance, or being injured with Entropy. The two together can sense both, creating an ability something like a Wraith’s Deathsight


                                            One With the Living Quintessence (Sense Life + Sense ???) Life 1 / Prime 1

                                            Ambiguity Resolution - Bindings of the Universe

                                            I think the big problem here is that this doesn’t appear to be a Prime augment to Life, but a Life augment to Prime. Reading Prime 1 carefully, it doesn’t actually mention the ability to see the flows of quintessence within patterns. We could take this to mean that seeing the quintessence within patterns requires a pattern sphere. This could also be pure padding. What I'd most want to see is what the main utility of this is.


                                            Missing?

                                            Added: Detect Life Augmentation (Sense Life)

                                            Many Spheres have the ability to detect uses of that Sphere. It's unclear if Pattern Spheres normally can, and Life is not specifically given this capacity but... the existence of Pattern bleed shows that there is some important difference between an untouched pattern and a pattern that has been altered. It is reasonable that Life should be able to detect this difference, if nothing else.

                                            The full capabilities of this would be up to the ST, but it is worth noting that if this ability is based on the disparity that causes pattern bleed it would be harder to sense changes that don't cause that bleed.

                                            Alternately, this could be the important use of One With the Living Quintessence above.

                                                        Originally posted by Ramnesis View Post

                                                        Read Muscles

                                                        ...

                                                        Human Polygraph Machine
                                                        I would combine these into one broader effect of "Sense Mood." I don't think it's justified to go farther to the extent of specific emotions (I don't think Life 1 can sense the differences between happy tears and sad tears for example, both operate on the same systems, though it can tell the difference between tearing up because of allergies vs emotions).
