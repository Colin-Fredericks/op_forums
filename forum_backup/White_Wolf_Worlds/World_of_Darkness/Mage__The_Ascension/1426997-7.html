                                    That would be the Traditions' argument, yes. Obviously, it's not the argument that the Technocracy would lay out. Their argument hinges on the technomancer/mystic divide, which is key because the reason for the divide is that one side views Magick as an exercise of Enlightened Will while the other side views Science as an exercise of Enlightened technique. Mystics put intent (what you're trying to do) front and center; technomancers put method (how you're doing it) front and center.

                                    On the one hand, this distinction means that technomancers don't get to discard their Instruments or to Work Without Focus; on the other hand, it's entirely possible that this technomantic mindset is necessary for learning and using the Technocratic Spheres (which aren't better than their traditional counterparts, but are different in useful ways such as Primal Utility being better at making Wonders than Prime is, and providing access to Primal Ventures).

                                    The closest thing the Technocrats come to abusing science is in their decision to stick to building on the Earthly Foundations (or phrased another way, being reductionist). From their perspective, the Etherites aren't true Scientists precisely because they reject reductionism: their theories are built on shifting sand, and thus are more susceptible to paradoxes, something that a sufficiently refined Technocratic innovation doesn't have to worry about.

                                    By contrast: to the Etherites, the Technocrats are abusing science because their insistence on conformity to a single universal Focus blinds them to alternatives.

                                    That, and Etherites tend to be much more comfortable with the Avatar than Technocrats are with Genius: it's entirely possible that one of the purposes of Conditioning is to harness (Etherites would say “muzzle”) the Genius, making it easier for the Technocrat to direct her Scientific efforts as she sees fit instead of having spontaneous sparks of inspiration mucking everything up.

                                    My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness
