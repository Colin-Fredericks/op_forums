    Demon: The Fallen V20

    09-11-2018, 11:41 AM
    Hello, now that Wraith: The Oblivion V20 is out, can we hope that other games of the Wolrd of Darkness universe could emerge?

    I thought about Demon: The Fallen V20. The game offers a captivating universe (on the mythology of demons and reality), interesting concept on good and evil, an interesting character creation (far from the nice / nasty classic). In the end, it's a balanced game and quite successful, a joy for those who want to play demons (like me).

    I read that many people on the forum (and elsewhere) would be interested in a release of the game in V20.
    WE WANT DEMON: THE FALLEN V20 !!!

    What is your opinion on a possible exit from the game?
