        Attempted to write up a chronicle seed for this. Suggestions for real world cities appreciated, location can be changed.
        Chronicle Seed

        Cliveton, Illinois (fictional city)
        Vibe: A veneer of normality, hiding pervasive isolation

        Premise: Few kindred travel to Cliveton. The city has been cut off from the rest of the sects since before 2010 and many kindred has vanished. The rumor say Hunters and wolves and worse things still swarm the city like rats and its inhabitants have learnt to keep their head down. It is difficult to travel into the city and dangerous to try and leave.

        Thematic things

        Close to a giant: Only an hour or so from Chicago. Despite this it has proven very difficult to reach or even talk to the city, and it might as well be on the other side of the country. (Original city was Aurora, but didn't quite fit)
        Lots of hidey holes: The city should contain lots of secret passageways, far away buildings, unknown spots, gardens or houses with no way to see inside and more
        Danger abounds: The streets are not more safe for the Kindred than the Kine. Incident keeps happening and those that move aroind recklessly disappear.

        Locations

        The Zoo: The local zoo should have been a small attraction for the town but across the years it has recieved a lot of support from unknown benefactors and has gained a large number of exhibits. It contains bisons, mountain lions, a large selection of birds and reptiles, hawks, owls, snakes, lizards and more. An attached pet shop sells traditional pets and - if you know the right people - smuggles in illegal or hard to obtain animals.

        The kindred use the zoo to gain animals, for ghouls, to posess or even to keep for food.

        Part time agency
        A shady building squeezed in between two much larger ones hosts a number of offices in varying degrees of disorder. These compose not one temp agency but several. Here desperate or hopeful workers try to get well paying gigs. Persistent rumors abound of 'black jobs' off the books tasks that pay many times more than a standard job, but at some great risk to the worker themselves. Offered only to those who know the right people and know to keep their mouth shut.

        Kindred use 'the agency' as a recruitment spot for ghouls, but also to hire deniable assets to do some small task and even sometimes to get some work for themselves

        Some independent ghouls are attached to this agency and sell their services through it with taciturn tolerance from the camarilla.

        The mill - the culture house
        Once an old sawmill that fed the town with timber, it long stood abandoned but have now been renovated and is used as an art gallery and hangout spot. in the evening it hosts special exhibits and the upper floors occasionally holds conferences or provides rooms for various clubs.

        Kindred commonly use it as a hangout spot, as it is easily accessible by both possessed ghouls and animals, being at the edge of a park. A room in the upper floor has been enchanted to allow those using auspex to render themselves visible for extensive periods.

        Problems:
        Weak masquerade. The Camarilla's grip on the masquerade in this city is much weaker than usual. Those who move to recklessly in the city will be tracked, and worse still so will whoever they visit.
        Many mortals have become aware 'something is out there' even if they dont know what and superstition has increased over the years.

        Dangerous streets: Many hostile factions maintain a presence in the city. Worse still not all of them default to murder. Kindred may be killed, captured, recruited, tortured, black mailed, have their haven attacked, set on fire or demolished.

        The hostile factions includes independent hunters, society of leopold, the second inquisition, the new world order, a pack of lupins, a murder of wraiths, and the occasional sabbat pack.

        Unknown presence: Some kindred - including elders - simply vanish. It's usually put down to one of the many hostile factions in the city, but not everyone feel it makes sense that so many disappear without a trace.

        Seed NPCs

        Maurice:
        Concept: Inexperienced chantry leader.
        Embraced: 1960s
        Motivation: Keep the chantry members safe

        Maurice took over the chantry after the former head disappeared. He's been forced to lock it down tight since. As few Tremere as possible venture outside. Everyone has had to learn Auspex or dominate. They rely on possessed ghouls to bring in necessities. They've had to relocate at least once and have managed to set up a handful of other safe houses in case they need to evacuate again.

        Secret: He knows of the Great Spirit and its vendetta. He has attempted to kill it twice already, and also tried to bind ot several times, angering further. He believes if more people knew of it they might make it angrier (correct), fears more hostile factions will ally with it (unlikely) suspects it was the fault of the former chantry head the spirit was angered in the first place (partially correcy, the former chantry master botched a ritual to seal it and was swallowed up by it, but it was already hostile)
        Wants from pcs: He'd like to use the pcs to kill, bind or weaken the spirit, but he also want them to remain ignorant of what they are doing. If a pc is a Tremere he wants their help with securing the chantry.

        Great Spirit of Art
        Woke up: Late 1700s
        Drive the kindred from the city
        Secret: Its existence itself is largely unknown. The Tremere and the Lupines are aware of it and the NWO suspect its existence. Everyone else is ignorant.

        It relies on a wellspring within the penumbra and on a handful of passionate mortals its inspired to help empower it. If these were destroyed it would weaken, but as long as they remain it is difficult to kill it or drive it away.

        It can move through the penumbra and peer through images and statues into the mundane world. it can also manifest in the mundane world, taking on the shape of a human or an image of a landscape. it can take on these shapes in a variety of styles, though at the end the motif or base remain, same landscape, same person. That manifestation can be destroyed but this doesn't kill the spirit only forces it back into the penumbra for a while until it reforms.

        Its true shape is as a mist of shifting colors.

        It can communicate with the lupines and will tip them off about kindred movements or lairs it discovers. it can also send visions to the mortals it inspires. the society of leopold has one such mortal as a member and believes he recieves visions from an angel.

        It is responsible for some of the disappearances. It can shunt a person into a pocket realm, usually an endless art gallery, and then collapse that realm ontop of them. the end result is that the person is trapped in an image or statue or other art work. This takes a lot out of it and is not guaranteed to work so it prefers to do it rarely and not against more than one person unless its hand is forced.

        Allies: Lupines can talk to it. The hunters don't know what it is but sometimes receive information from it. It has a few inspired mortals to which it sometimes acts as a muse.

        Gunter
        Concept: A miserable and downtrodden Tzimisci.
        Embraced: 1991
        Motivation: Teach and learn
        Secret: He taught some thin bloods Viccisitue once, a fact that would have his sire brave the city to end him for the shame of it. He has taught some of the independent ghouls animalism as well, a fact that'd render many kindred suspicious of him if it was found out.

        Gunter is far from the ideal Tzimisci. He fled into the city to get away from his sire.

        In blood form he travels through the sewage pipes of the city, He teaches anyone who asks Animalism and Auspex, usually in return for learning something of theirs. Vicissitude he claims to never teach, though this is not entirely true.

        He also acts as a messenger and a gopher.

        While he has high ranks in his clan disciplines, and should be considered to know a great deal of out of clan disciplines, he does not have high ranks in the latter nor does he posess the abilities to apply most of what he knows in an adept manner.

        What he wants with the players: he may wish to deliver a message to them from someone else, sell his seevices or learn what they know

            Originally posted by Tjalei View Post
            Attempted to write up a chronicle seed for this. Suggestions for real world cities appreciated, location can be changed.
            Disclaimer: I like this idea a lot. The criticism below is in the hopes that you improve it, not to discourage you.

            I think you need a reason for so many Kindred to be there, first.

            The small town has a lot of appeal as setting, but makes a lot of Vampires a very unlikely thing. The dynamics of it (fewer people and more connections between them) mean they're unlikely to have more than a coterie. With a hostile environment this gets worse.

            But a good reason, something that attracts vampires here, even magically like Presence 4, would change things and tip the balance against the Great Spirit goals.

            It's even more likely if so many heavy hitters are present. Lupines in numbers in a small city is common. Hunters like the Society of Leopold mounting permanent camp, definitely not. The NOM rarely even cares about Vampires due to how discreet they are (even the Sabbat, in the end), staying close for an extended time would likely mean they want something here and have reason to be after the Kindred so viciously when they're usually far more hostile to the Werewolves.

            I'd get rid of sects entirely. At this level of danger the locals are in high survival mode. No point in trying to be fancy or ideological when you're safe in the streets and calling any attention is a horrible idea. Hierarchy is loose and practical, even if there's a nominal Prince or something. Politics exist and always will, but they change with the extreme context. If the attraction factor is sect neutral, it's likely that all sorts of vampires arrive, including Sabbat, independent and rare Bloodlines.

            Cleaning the Masquerade is secondary. You'll rather damn the entire Kindred society of tomorrow to live today. Secrecy is kept to not attract hostiles, but anything left behind is acceptable loss, no one's going to move in and risk exposure for that, a servant will clean it later if feasible.

            Use mortal servants, not only Ghouls. There's little reason for a Vampire to Ghoul everyone who works for them, this is a heavy cost when plenty of work can be done by normal people. Especially when survival is at risk. Keeping a distance between you and your servants to cut loose compromised ones without risk is more important than fostering loyalty that actually makes them a greater liability.

            Write down a reason for each faction to be around, and how at risk they are themselves. Also their relationships, as many of those are mutually hostile. Also give the Kindred some breathing space, the tighter their situation, the harder it is to have politics rather than pure survival horror.

            Think why the spirit of Art is hostile and to whom. Spirits care about vampires way less than the Garou do, this is not a normal situation. Most Gaian spirits are content at ignoring and being irresponsive, some even do have a relationship with a Kindred, even if this is more common for Banes. Also think about its relation to whatever attracts each faction.

            Other hidden threat besides the spirit may loom. The NOM made me think of Threat Null, but it can be something else, or several somethings else.

            Remember to keep it Vampire. The WoD isn't really a unified setting and you should change anything you need about any faction to fit the scenario, no matter how big the change from the perspective of other game lines. Just be forward about any change.

                Certainly there's a lot that can be developed on.
                Though if I was to run this as an actual campaign I'd just do some prep and start running, fleshing things out as I went.

                Let's do some possible chronicle scenarios and then some more stuff on the city.

                I'd present these three to players and let them pick. Expectation is most will pick the last one as it's the most straightforward.

                Tremere chantry in lockdown -
                PC blurb: You're part of the local Tremere chantry. It's been closed down for over ten years now. Your characters have been stuck inside for that long. The outside is dangerous, most who leave the chantry either puppets ghouls, or send only their spirit. The few exceptions must take the greatest care to not be found. You are cut off from the clan at large.

                The chantry is split between trying to retake the city, finding a way to evacuate, or remaining passive and unnoticed. Your characters has settled on one of these goals and wish to both gain more support for it, but also pursue it on their own, preferable under the nose of your squabbling comrades.

                Everyone may play as Tremere, but Gargoyles and Ghouls could also be options, group willing.

                (changed time of lockdown from 20+ years to 10+ to make character creation easier, not so fun if everyone spent way to long locked inside)

                Ghouls for hire
                You're independent ghouls of Cliveton. Vampires here hides deep in the city, deeper than you've heard they do in other cities, and you know no vampires in any other city, and so your lot is slightly more tolerated than they are in other places. You take odd jobs, mundane and other. Those who pay the best are always wrong somehow... unnatural, and nearly always dangerous, but it's the only way to get blood or real amounts of cash and you all need both.

                Not all of the ones who take these black jobs are independent, and some are barely ghouls, having taken blood as payment but never yet met an actual talking corpse. Players can decide to play independent ghouls, bound ghouls, or even mortals in over their heads who has yet to take the red.

                Cliveton Regulars
                You're kindred trying to make your way in a city that never got the message your kind is supposed to rule the night. Kindred in Cliveton has learnt to lay low. You may be outsiders, another coterie of expendables sent to investigate the city with no real expectations on your success or even survival. You may be the odd fugitive who managed to reach the city to escape some great judgement, or long term residents trying to gain some peace of mind. Regardless the place is a mess, and that mess affords plenty of work, grave danger and that rare opportunity to climb the ranks of kindred hiearchy.

                Camarilla default, characters should have some means to move using a pawn, either Dominate(posess ghoul), Animalism(posess animal), Necromancy(posess corpse), Auspex(Astral Travel) or any equivalent power. On storyteller's approval you can just play a minion (ghouled, dominated, or mezmerized) as a second character.

                The City.

                The location of Cliveton - being a fictional city - is flexible but thematically it can either be near a larger city, or very isolated.

                Being near a larger city highlights its isolation through contrast. It's close to this open, important place, yet unable to reach it. Like someone feeling more alone in a crowd than on their own.

                Being isolated highlights the isolated theme in a self evident manner. This also provides the opportunity of the classic horror milieu of a strange town with an uneasy atmosphere in the middle of nowhee

                Nearby a larger city
                Cliveton is around 200 000 people. An old industry town, lots of red brick buildings, old factories, cramped alleys, iron fire escapes, and a decent sewage system. All in varying states of maintenance or decay.

                Due to the weaker grip the kindred has on it several factions have attempted to use it as a foothold against its larger neighbour. Most of these attempts have become bogged down in the city's own internal issues. The Lupines maintain a permanent presence due to the Great Spirit, Hunters use the city as a training ground and occasionally strike out against its larger neighbours, the NWO attempts to figure out what is going on while protecting the few assets they have in the city - chiefly a hidden away construct on the outskirts of town. Other supernaturals have their individual motivations.

                Isolated
                If it's more rural it should have a much smaller population. Exact demographics can be left somewhat ambigous and you could add a larger geographic area, a partial wilderness interspred with small villages and homesteads. Put it somewhere cold and add some pine forests and it's good stuff.

                Kindred would go to such a place to hide, get away, escape punishment, debts, or bad treatment. Long term residents may embrace more due to larger than average losses and the inability of local authorities to limit the number of embraces. Hunters go to the city chasing specific targets, stay to purge the town of all its monsters. The Lupines are more at home in the forests and the NWO has some X-Files style investigation team stationed in the town.

                    Going to make a small session for this but before then I need to detail the motivation and atmosphere of the antagonists (so some fitting conflicts can be created)

                    Great Spirit of Art.

                    Inspires wondrous and erratic creativity, both by its presence and through direct action.

                    Originally a landscape painting from the 1700s before it awoke and gained awareness. Slowly accrued more power over time.

                    The original inciting incident for its anger was some obscure or petty thing. The landscape painting that was its original form was stolen by some ghoul for no reason other than he wanted it. Or one of the people it granted inspiration had their life ruined by a kindred plot entirely incidentally and without any involved kindred even noticing.

                    Then when the Tremere elder tried to enslave it and botched it went from anger to pure hatred towards every kindred in the city.

                    It wants to drive them away but have no real plan aside from supporting others to do so for it, or the odd attack against a particular infuriating kindred. It has a very inhuman way of thinking.
                    ​​
                    The Society of Leopold

                    The Society is a confident and patient presence in the city. It is playing a long game and wants to secure the city as a foothold in the region to strike out against the undead and the infernal within both the city and the state.

                    It supports local expressions of Christian Faith, including churches, events, and capable believers. It runs few things directly, but works through intermediares, both to hide itself and to not paint an obvious target on its actual assets.

                    It maintains a tense alliance with the Second Inquisition, born from mutual need and strained by different priorities.

                    It culls the kindred populance when able, but are aware the decay runs deep, and so also seeks to identify and target the interests of the Kindred.

                    It's plan is to force the hand of local kindred by pin pointing their interests in the city, feeding supply lines, financial assets, pawns, 'domains', and more. Target those to provoke some responce, then use that response to their advantage. The damned can be terrifying when they move, but they also expose themselves. Pawns can be captured, masterminds sniffed out, and from the wreckage there will always be new recruits. Thus the ideal situation for the society is they hit the kindred where it hurts, and the kindred hit back at something expendable, fresh recruits, a prepared ambush, willing martyrs.

                    The Society capture Kindred and their minions when they are able. They do not torture their prisoners. Instead they practice a particular ruthless kindness. The damned are given a chance of absolution. Young ones are reminded of their old lives, reminded of what they lost, sometimes even brought into contact with old relatives. More than one kindred, steeled against simple pain, has broken down when faced with their old mother, or real children. Older kindred are also given a chance, but little time is wasted on them. They are to dangerous, and have been undead to long. Those kept alive are fed the blood of lambs, and if repentant offered sacramental wine. Vomitting it out is viewed with pity, and they are encouraged to keep trying. None is kept alive for long, weeks if kindred, months if ghouls. The executed are offered the choice to die by sunlight, otherwise they are staked, blessed, beheaded and cremated. Mortal minions are shot or - if available - given lethal injections. If repentant their last rites are read for them, and they are given a Christian Burial. If not their ashes are scattered in the wilderness.

                    Aside from mortal informants and the SI the Society gain knowledge of their targets from a seer. An old religious artist known for his intricate glass work, and his beautiful oil paintings. He receives feverish dreams of the corpses that hide within the city. These are often confusing but are noted down and interpreted by the followers of Leopold.

                    The leadership are aware no angel sends these visions, though they leave the question open to other followers. Their official stance is he may be divinely inspired, but this has yet to be decided.

                    The Society within the city is chiefly composed of 'moderates' within the organisation, but this should not be taken as being meek. They are patient, faultlessly pragmatic, ruthless, and with a clear vision of what they want to do in the city and why. Their humility does not beget mercy nor does it carry a shred of naivity.

                    The Second Inquisition.

                    The Second Inquisition is a subtle and unseen danger within the city. They focus on surveillance and tracking of the Kindred where they are able. They want to map the network of vampires operating in the city to the point they can decapitate the kindred organization in one large scale operation. They focus on surveillance, commonly trough digital means. There has been a continous back and forth between the SI and Kindred over control of relevant digital infrastructure, in particular cell phone towers. The interplay of various intermediates battling it out can become both lethal and frustatingly complicated on both sides.

                    The SI's ideal situation is that their target never becomes aware the have become known to them. All their movements are tracked, their associates are found, rinse and repeat as more and more of the Kindred network is mapped out. When this is not possible, they'll do what they can to cover themselves.

                    They may capture a target if able, or tip off the Society of Leopold of their existence. Unlike The Society of Leopold they commonly keep captives for long periods. A few captives become tagged as potential defectors and are targeted for recruitment. The rest are used as research materials, tortured for information (usually through repeated starvation) or executed. Defectors are offered the carrot of no longer being forced to rely on their sect, hide what they are to every human, being able to talk about their situation to medical professionals (psychologists), being given set amounts of blood so as never having to risk hurting another human being, or going hungry. A salary, an actual employment and a chance to serve their actual nation as if they were still alive. All at the small cost of being permanently watched for the rest of their existence, and the high risk of dying or worse at the hands of their former kin. Whips are not uncommon either though these are usually tailor made to each individual. Some extortion material that would see them killed by their fellow sect, or threatening misfortune on a loved one, perhaps.

                    In truth there has been very few defectors, most who can't even be used as moles with great effect. The very few such rats the SI have on hand they have found extremely useful.

                    The S.I will want to share their information with other ​​​​​​S.I cells external to the city. They know from experience this is extremely dangerous. They are paranoid, and so will only send data by courier, irregularly and when they have something they feel is worth the risk.

                    Independent Hunters

                    Happy amateurs with a flamethrower and a dream.

                    The Garou
                    A frenzy of panic and chaos. There's a single pack which stalk the city. They speak directly to the great spirit and use it to find worthwhile targets.

                    (I am not as familiar with WtA as I'd like to be, don't know enough to really detail them beyond 'here's a free chase scene, scene of violent horror, extra reason why kindred don't leave')

                    The NWO
                    Cold and calculating. They seek to maintain what the kindred term the masquerade and they term the consensus. They want to protect their lone construct hidden away in the city. They suspect there's some unusual extradimensional entity active in the city but they don't know its nature. They will move against any faction that to overtly threaten the veneer of mundanity. They maintain some line of communication with each other faction save the werewolves, in all instances one way and without hinting at their presence. A kindred has one of their 'sources' hand them some information, an S.I asset gains a sudden idea. A local priest contacts The Society with an urgent message. They greatly wish to remain unseen, but will move with force if they have to.

                    Other Notes
                    Everyone gets some way to work through I termediaries. Leopold should maybe get something more than mortal believers, some custom powers or extra seers or something. The Garou could have parts of the local animals aligned with them to give kindred with animalism something to manage, could be fun.

                        The Society of Leopold isn't just a generic faction of Christian hunters. They're in the service of the Catholic Church. And not the kind that supports ecumenism.

                        Usually they don't act as manipulators because that's not their point. Their greatest asset is True Faith, so their actions should always conform to their interpretation of Catholic doctrine.

                        Also they wouldn't use lethal injection. No one uses lethal injection outside the US government. Search about this topic if you want, but long story short it's extremely cruel and the allegations that it is a peaceful death are false.

                        It's kind of strange the SI avoiding digital comms when it is their whole point.

                        Don't call your Werewolves Garou if you don't know them, this will avoid headaches later. You don't need to know, but you do need to make up something, or they'll just be irrelevant as a faction.

                        Still don't see any interesting plot line to make this tick. No connective tissue, no recognized mystery, everything just works. But if works for you, just address the above, those errors go from serious plot holes to outright offensive.
