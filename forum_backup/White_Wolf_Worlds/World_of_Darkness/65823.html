          Thank you for support! Hope developers of DAV/20 will also find something useful here.
          Now i will float around general theme, later will develop sub threads focusing on certain themes.


          Let's start with most important: Why run stories in Livonia and Lithuania?
          Because located in eastern europe, those regions are crossroads of cultures, religions, trade and also a constant battlefield of political powers.

          1. Already local ethnic groups Finno Ugrians (Estonia+Latvia) and Balts (Latvia+Lithuania) have separated roots and core culture.

          a) Finno Ugrians arrived in the region 10.000 BC just after ice age ended and represent animistic shamanistic culture of hunter geatherers from most ancient times.

          B) Balts arrived in the region 3.000 B.C with indo-european migration wave, bringing Battle Axe culture and represent agriculture, animal husbandry, metalworks and developed religion.



          2. In medieval times those two cultures interacted with :

          a) Scandinavian Viking culture by trade and wars (in early medieval times, and later as Catholic Denmark)

          b) Russ Orthodox Christianity culture by trade and wars (as forever plotting power monger neighbors)

          c) West Slavic Catholic culture by trade and wars (more for Lithuania, which ended later in alliance and commonwealth with Poland)

          d) Germanic Catholic Christian culture by trade and bloody crusades (this left biggest impact and is felt even today)

          f) Mongol, Tartar, Turkic nomadic shamanistic culture (ended with Lithuanians taking lands previously conquered by Mongols from Russ)

          h) Hanseatic League or Hansa as monopolistic super trade organization (this comes like a greedy tentacle beast)


          Optional possible fiction :
          Last Druid from Ireland could sail to Livonia or Lithuania likewise could do Cathar heretic from France.
          Hashashin assasin could come with Teutonic crusaders from holy land to take out pagan leader.
          Arab or Venetian merchant could search the land for priceless amber using Varangian river trade route from Constantinopolis.
          Known are cases when Christians join pagans and pagans take the new fate for various reasons.
          Pope in Rome is visited by important ex pagans to prove theyr loyality to Chatolic church, before execute "holy" plans back home.
          Already Greeks and Romans have visited and traded with these lands. Moors from Spain and wise man from Alexandria searched for wisdom.
          Chinese firepowder have been brouht in region by mongols who search long lost bloodline of Finno Ugrian shaman, following spiritual prophecy of reunion and conquer world.

          Main medieval trade routes (Silk road is connected by Alexandria, Tyre, Antioch and Trebizond)


          Extreme spice here is a fact, that this region is the last Pagan stronghold in europe. In case of Lithuania even with a possibility to strike back any invaders very hard,
          (Lithuanians annihilated crusaders Swordbrother order and later with the help of Poland destroyed Teutonic order. In this we can see long forged and eventual victory.)


          What would be mood and theme of Livonian and Lithuanian chronicles?
          Everything mentioned in DA books and more :

          "Opportunity"
          Power struggle here makes Leaders and Nobles fall in battle or die by assassination, treasures is looted and lands are taken from defeated foes with regularity. War is giving opportunity to gain reputation, wealth and even claim the lands with title, to any lucky lowlife, poor Noble or make double gain for already powerful personalities.

          "Hunt"
          Hunt (or be hunted by) certain person or monster, be it disappeared Hermetic Tremere branch, pagan priest, infamous crusader or mythical beast for reasons of money, fame or ideology.

          "Revenge"
          As conflicts spread and ravage those lands, revenge is common goal in many lives(or unlives) be it last survivor of tribe or insulted Noble.

          "Rebellion"
          Slightest misstep by conquerors can cause large scale rebellion which is in planning already.

          "Discovery"
          Everything here is new as forests, swamps, sacred groves, pagan rituals for newcomers same as weaponry, technology, cities and church power for pagans.

          "Every move matters"
          This is the time when future is forged and empires carved. With every unused moment enemy is growing strength, with every choice destiny can be changed.

          "Dirty Politics"
          This is bloody playground for chess of many players and etiquette of "not to use backstabbing against weak allies" does not work here very well,
          even enemy can be useful sometimes for gain.

          "Conflict of Faiths"
          Whose God and beliefs are stronger than others ? What is the truth? Miracles of demons or misinterpreted heavenly signs?
          Only weak will submit to opinion of others.

          "Tactical Warmongery"
          Successful warfare is leadership, discipline, bravery, cleverness and used opportunities.

          "Glorious Heroism"
          Nothing feels better than victory against all the odds.

          "World of Mystery"
          Ancient rituals performed here for millennias still give results. This is land of pure magic, strange artifacts and wondrous creatures,
          the earth itself is alive here and every tree have personality.

          "Greedy Powergrowth"
          Livonian bee wax candles for Cathedrals in France, their wine in exchange for church masses in Riga.
          Amber, furs, salted fish, grains and slaves ... so much to claim.

          "Building Base"
          If strong fort with keep is built here it can be developed in to castle. Long term control of region is solid victory against enemies.
          Just need project, materials and builders.

          "In guard of Ethics"
          a) Did the Christ mean exactly this massacre and power greed ? NO. Real christians know what to do.
          b) My ancestors lived and died by laws of gods so its my honor to do the same.






          Hope you find some intresting ideas.

              DA Supernatural Creatures:

              1.Vampires
              --------------------------
              Local:
              a) Gangrel (Free creatures of the night hunting in the forests and fighting for survival. Fearsome Bearslayer is their leader )
              b) Tzimice (Deep rooted rulers of lands and humans. Kolduns and warriors of great power. Old Vala holds the greatest power)
              c) Telayvelic Tremere (Devoted researchers and defenders of ancient magic, wisdom and culture. Bloodmage Magda leads the group)
              d) Malkavians (Nobilitys of Local Vampires and Oracles of great influence. Most notably, stands highest Krive Adrojai)
              e) Noiad (While now mostly in exile, Noiad still come back to secretly visit their folk during long winter nights)

              Invaders:
              a) Ventrue (Teutonic knights invading Prussia and Livonia. Lord Horst of Novara is their main leader here)
              b) Lasombra (Guided from Rome, any important position is controlled by Lasombra clan. Just embraced Bishop Albert is their puppet)
              d) Brujah (Leaders of Brujah share their blood with crusaders from Swordbrother Order to create new Chartage in Livonia)
              c) Tremere (Usurpers hunt local Tzimice and Telyavs. Ventrue support them in such activities and use their services.)
              d) Anda (Mongol horde, greatest warriors, thirsty for blood and new victories. Challenge anybody with their warfare skills)

              Visitors:
              a) Toreador (Courtiers and Diplomats for Prince of Riga. Or maybe passionate collectors of fading Pagan culture)
              b) Nosferatu (Underground of any city has them. As spies in symbiosis with local prince they will endure)
              c) Ravnos (Curious travelers and trouble makers. Can visit a city or sacred pagan grove in the most inappropriate time)
              d) Giovanni (Opportunity to gain influence through growing trading organization Hansa could bring some Giovanni to Riga)
              e) Cappadocians (Some refugees from Constantinople could hide in Russian orthodox monastery or church)
              f) Assamite (It's possible that Assamite is in region to revenge some crusader or as a head hunter)
              g) Followers of Set (Snake worship is known amongst pagans and corruption doesn't know borders)


              2. Mages
              --------------------------
              Locals:
              a) Old Faith (Most priests and talented people of baltic tribes are core of this fellowship in Livonia and Lithuania)
              b) Spirit Talkers (Finno-Ugric people in connection with their ancestors and parallel worlds, for good or evil causes)

              Invaders:
              a) Messianic Voices (Secret backers of christian activities and supporters of crusading forces in supernatural way)

              Visitors:
              a) Valdermen (Adventurers seeking refugees from Scandinavia or glory, and using freedom offered by Lithuanian pagans)
              b) Order of Hermes (Curious potential mages, they sniff out magical energies in pagan lands and claim them if possible)
              c) Ahl i Batin (Search of wisdom or some magical reason could bring those far travelers here)
              d) Craftmason (Many castles, forts and keeps is build in great speed with help and knowledge of Craftmasons)


              3. Inquisitors
              --------------------------
              Invaders:
              a) Poor Knights of Acre
              b) Red order
              c) Sisters of St. John
              d) House of Murnau
              e) Oculi Dei
              (In general all Inquisitor orders have common cause and more or less they all help crusades in Livonia and Lithuania in the name of God)


              4. Werewolfs
              --------------------------
              Locals:
              a) Shadow Lords (Overlords of local lands from ancient times, this tribe still holds big influence here, not officially)

              Invaders:
              a) Fenrir (Restless killers of wyrm. They invade here and there and challenge any weakling Gaurou)
              b) Silver Fangs (Position as a Leader must always be proved to those who forget it, especially here)
              c) Red Talons (Wast forest lands of siberia grows large amount of wolves who search for new hunting grounds)

              Visitors:
              a) Warders of Men (Building of new cities and gathering of human masses attracts this tribe to Livonia)
              b) Bone Gnawers (Its good possibility to do nothing and gain everything, especially if you don't need much)
              c) Children of Gaia (All this war and chaos will bring great misery for every Gaurou. It needs to be fixed)
              d) Fianna (Search for lost entrance to Faerie world or accidental wrong turn in Umbra can lead Fianna to Livonia and Lithuania)
              e) Black Furies (From Greece straight to north over the mountains to the land of goddess Leto foremother of Letts)
              f) Silent Striders (Curiosity can kill a cat and Silent Strider from Spain or Arabia is curious enough)


              5. Fae
              --------------------------
              Locals:
              a) Court of Spring
              b) Court of Summer
              c) Court of Autumn
              d) Court of Winter
              (Livonia and Lithuania are still full of various kinds of Fae and local pagans respect them and ask for help if needed)

                                                      Here is map of Lithuanian medieval settlement - Kernave.

                                                      Posible fiction :
                                                      There is some speculations that it name meaning is - "kjer naave", or in english "catch death". Maybe its realated to trap used against enemy in siege or raid.

                                                      Usualy Livonian and Lithuanian pagan setlements can be divided in two groups:
                                                      A) Single castle based on hill and settlement around ( clasical and used all around medieval world using any natural advantages like rivers, clifs etc.)

                                                      B) Scattered forts and castles next to settlement (can be seen here in Kernave exsample also using natural advantages but in larger tactical scale)
                                                      From first look it can be seen not smart way for defense...but even Maori had stunning victories while defending similar "Pa" forts against superior British army.
                                                      All are conected with underground passages giving posibility to reposition forces, lure enemy to seemingly weaker positions, attack from unexpected direction, creating crosfire and of course to retreat. Its worth to mention that pagans was rather leaving doomed positions than face certain death. Livonian and Lithuanian lands was full of small castles and forts and was easy to rebuild. This kept enemy bizzy taking each defensive position while defenders could regroup and call for help. Crusaders soon started to use scorched land tactics and burned grainfields. Genocide and lack of food in long term turned out to be more devastating for pagans than some fort loses.

                                                      For DA roleplay scattered settlements could give easy navigation for players to encounter various sub factions, characters or services or claim some part for themselves. (For exsample Chieftan keep, Warrior keep, Priest keep, nobility settlement and simple folk settlement. etc.)
                                                      The area of Kernavė was sparingly inhabited at the end of the Paleolithic era, with the number of settlements significantly increasing in the Mesolithic and Neolithic eras.
                                                      The town was first mentioned in 1279, when, as the capital of the Grand Duke Traidenis, it was besieged by the Teutonic Knights. In 1390, during the Lithuanian Civil War (1389–1392), the knights burned the town and its buildings, including the castle. After this raid, the town wasn't rebuilt, and the remaining residents moved to the top of the hill instead of staying in the valley.

                                                      Kernavė has the oldest known medgrinda, a secret underwater road paved with wood. The road was used for defense and dates from the 4–7th centuries.
