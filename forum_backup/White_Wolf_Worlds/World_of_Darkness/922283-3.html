                Revised Golconda

                Golconda is a state where a Kindred is able to mystically join his Beast to his conscious mind and have the two become as one. If a Kindred is not possessed of sufficient willpower to tame the Beast then he is consumed and dies during his Sunspire. The state of Golconda was mentioned in the Book of Nod as being a Gift from God which would allow Kindred to be forgiven but a similar state called The Black Rebirth exists for Path followers which is functionally identical.

                The first recorded Kindred to achieve Golconda was Zao-Lot, the Salubri Antedeluvian who travelled to the Eastern Lands and went on numerous spiritual quests and journeys, studying with both Euthanatos mages and the Kuei-jin. His achievement of Golconda apparently removed the Clan Flaw from his childer and replaced it with their love of Humanity instead. How the Black Rebirth came to be is questionable, though some believe it was achieved by Set, Caine, Malkav, Haquim, or perhaps just Kindred who weren't so famous.

                The effects of Golconda are manifold with success on the Sunspire immediately granting a choice between a Kindred reverting to human or becoming a kind of spiritually enlightened Kindred instead. The benefits of this transformation are not quite as grandiose as some individuals claim and many Kindred have actually achieved it over the centuries. Hundreds, if not thousands, with the majority of the Salubri, sadly, having been since eradicated.

                Requirements

                * A Humanity or Path rating of 7 or above.
                * A Willpower score of 7 or above.
                * The Sunspire ritual to be successfully RPGed.

                Benefits

                * Reversion to humanity with all Disciplines remaining as a form of hedge magic or a full awakening as a Mage if mortality is chosen.
                * Vampires lose their Generational barriers on raising Disciplines.
                * Vampires suffer lethal rather than aggravated damage from sunlight.
                * Vampires require one Blood Point per week rather than per day.
                * Vampirs no longer suffer Frenzy
                * Vampires no longer suffer Rotschreck.
                * Malkavian characters lose their derangement but all other clans keep their flaws.

                Flaws

                * A Kindred who fails their Sunspire ritual is instantly consumed by wassail and reverts to Humanity 0 or is destroyed outright.
                * Golconda and the Black Rebirth require you to keep your Path or Humanity above 5 or you will lose this state and quickly degenerate to wassail afterward.
                * Tremere, Followers of Set, and Baali all consider Golconda to be a dangerous heresy which must be destroyed.

                                                How Vampires and Other Supernaturals Interact in the "Merged WOD"

                                                I run a full-on interconnected World of Darkness.

                                                1. Mage: The Ascension: The Technocracy (known as the Illuminati to Vampires) have a treaty with the Camarilla which they cooperate in enforcing the Masquerade. This is the major reason why it's damn near impossible to expose the supernatural. The Tremere are constantly infiltrating and manipulating the Traditions to their own ends with many lower-level mages Embraced. Roughly 80% of the Traditions and Technocracy are Hedge Mages too so vampires are actually pretty top-shelf anyway. Sabbat Cities are often at war with Mages as well with vampire hunters of the Akashic Brotherhood, Celestial Choir, Euthantos, and Technocracy often cooperating. The Kue-jin and Akashic Brotherhood are particularly hateful toward one another. Notably, the Hollow Ones tend to be friendlier with vampires than they are with their fellow mages and many are either ghouled or associates with Anarchs.

                                                Notably, in my games, the True Hand and have vampires and Euthanatos as equals.

                                                Note: In my games the destruction of the Ravnos Antedeluvian (actually just Hazimel) was a joint Camarilla/Technocracy action.

                                                2. Werewolf: The Apocalypse: Most Camarilla cities have treates between the Lupines and the vampires, which is something that the lesser ranks of the Garou Nation only begrudgingly admit. These treates are designed to enforce the status quo of, "Not my problem." The Sabbat, of course, have no such treaties and the conflicts with them are much-much worse (are we seeing a pattern here?). Pentex is mostly controlled by vampires now that the Technocracy has eliminated the majority of the Nephandi, Baali, and Wyrm cultists in its upper ranks with the Camarilla using it to increase their power dramatically.

                                                The relationship between shapechangers and Kuei-Jin are pretty friendly actually as the latter often employ the former as mercenaries and assassins.

                                                3. Changeling: The Dreaming: The Sabbat is actually quite friendly with the Shadow Court and the most hardcore Unseelie. Parties with the Lasombra or Tzimisce often include members who are judged off-limits. This is basically a reversal of their relationship with every other supernatural and why the Sabbat often have an abundance of magical trinkets which dwarfs the Camarilla's own members. Weirdly, the Sabbat cause the Unseelie to prosper in their territories and they tend to have a 2-3 times the size of normal feelings as the Sabbat's hellraising cause people to believe more in the supernatural.

                                                House Gwydion, House Scathatch, and House Fiona often have vampire hunting as a rite of passage with most Camarilla Kindred assuming they're just particularly skilled hunters or hedge mages. The Tremere have been known to ghoul or teach House Eiuland kinian with some small overlap between the two. Sluagh also deals with vampires, of all stripes, selling magic or necromancy for blood. Contrary to popular mythology, Malkavians and fairies have very little to do with one another. The Kiyasad are also considered something of a myth by all factions.

                                                4. Wraith: The Oblivion: Ghosts are the most encountered problem by vampires, even if it's not particularly important in many respects. Ghosts routinely harass vampires as they tend to accumulate victims over the years. This often results in things like burning havens (less so since electrical lights became fashionable), horrible nightmares, visions, and just trashing the places. As such, the Tremere do a thriving business in exorcising havens even if they often have to subcontract the work to mages or Sluagh. The Giovanni have more direct methods of doing so and often develop a thriving trade in boons for it. Anarchs are forced to rely on Hollow Ones and just burning their former havens down.

                                                Roughly 80% of all vampires become wraiths when they die, higher than any other group except mediums. This is due to the high amount of concentrated deathly energy they have. Vampires tend to form the backbone of the Hierarchy when they don't become specters.

                                                5. Demon: The Fallen: Demons actually avoid vampires for the most part because every vampire reminds them of Caine and they tend to have a superstition that to harm a vampire is to invoke 7 times the wrath of God, which most vampires would find ridiculous. However, it's not entirely unheard of as bad luck seems to have followed most of those demons who have dealt with them. The Baali, obviously, a brisk relationship with the Infernal but are most interested in either keeping the Earthbound imprisoned or freeing them along with Demon Lords. The Tremere, by contrast, also deal with Demons but use Goetism and very finely worded contracts to keep everything on the level. The Giovanni, similarly, have many deals with the infernal but seem to consider the destruction of reality by them a good thing so they take less precautions. The Tzimsice generally prefer to deal with Banes than true demons even as they often know all of Kupala's demon servitors (Kupala being an Earthbound in this game).

                                                The Salubri Antitribu, missing the memo the Sabbat's titles are ironic, have devoted themselves to eradicating the demonic infestation on Earth.

                                                Interestingly, the Followers of Set HATE demons and have a large number of sorcerers devoted to their banishment or destruction. They call these the Children of Apep and consider it their duty to ward the planet against them.

                                                6. Hunter: The Reckoning: There are 1,000 Imbued in my world and whenever one dies, another is Imbued. They basically form about 10% of the Hunters in the world even if they're not terribly effective. Vampires are a common enough enemy for them even though they primarily deal with wraiths and zombies and banes. Obviously, their relationship with vampires tends to be on the bad side.

                                                Interestingly, the Camarilla has a standing policy of not killing Hunters versus just sending them against the Sabbat or Werewolves or other parties. They generally consider Hunters to be more annoyance than enemy and help keep the vampire population down. The only exception is when they try to break the Masquerade and then the Hammer comes down.

                                                Have you noticed the Camarilla prefers diplomacy and manipulation to open conflict?

                                                7. Kindred of the East, Risen, and Mummies: In general, I tone down the Kuei-Jin "Yellow Peril" elements and highlight they tend to get along with Western Vampires the way any Prince does to people muscling in on their territory, not well but they're willing to get along as long as the niceties are followed.

                                                There's Western vampires in Kuei-Jin cities and Kuei-Jin in Western cities with no one engaging in any large-scale wars between the two sects. In general, Chinatowns and neighborhoods tend to be territories other Kindred avoid unless they have permission of the local Kuei-Jin. I also make Bloodlines canon with the destruction of the Anarch Free States done by Ming-Xiao working with the Camarilla. However, this was actually due to the fact Ming-Xiao was an Akuma working for the Yama Kings to kill as many racist young Kuei-Jin warriors as possible while also making large numbers of concessions from the Camarilla. It also didn't work in the long run thanks to The Neonate.

                                                Mummies in my game are a bit more NWoD than OWoD as there's Egyptian mummies, Incan Mummies, Celtic Mummies, Russian mummies, and others working for the Gods of Old. There's also Christian Mummies who the most famous of is Sullivan Dane. They're generally hostile to Kindred but not interested in outright war.
