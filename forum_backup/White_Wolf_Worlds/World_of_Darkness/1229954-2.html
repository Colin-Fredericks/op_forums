    Honestly I really don't see a problem with the WOD cosmology. The Meta-Plot have many strange story elements that conflict, run in circles, or leave off in dead ends sure, but the cosmology is Supposed to be far beyond any one group.

    IF all the settings are cannon, it really puts effort on the storyteller to try to balance the different tones of each game. They aren't necessary though. However if you include everything, the setting becomes bigger than any group will tell you, Big bads stop be "The BIG BAD" and become "one of the Big Bads", history becomes muddled and full of inconsistent viewpoints(a lot like real history honestly), and many of the different splats are just Wrong at some points(which kinda humanizes them).

    The biggest change I'd make though is information available about different factions, the Camerilla can't Really be unaware that pentex or the technocracy exist or vise versa without making them all seem incompetent.

        Originally posted by Prometheas View Post
        The biggest change I'd make though is information available about different factions, the Camerilla can't Really be unaware that pentex or the technocracy exist or vise versa without making them all seem incompetent.
        Yeah that's one of the greatest WTF? of crossoverland. Major supernatural forces that supposedly "rule the night" can't possibly be irrelevant to each other. To say: "yeah, we know there are other mysterious beings out there, but we only know rumors about them and they're largely unimportant" strains the suspension of disbelieve. Eventually their so called areas of influence overlap and they do have conflicts of interest.

        Best solution it's to say that humans are the ones who trully rule the night and big supernatural organizations opperate like huge mafias that like to think they have more influence than they really have but know well that the others exist and do have to deal with them if they cross the line. That way you don't have to think about who owns the world between the big three.

        TheTe chnocratic answer to the Sabbat can't be "oh well, the Camarilla have them under control so there's no problem here. Better concentrate the majority of our resource on oppressing the hippie terrorists", if they trully care about protecting humanity. This makes them look either incompetent or cynic beyond redemption.

                                                        I think you are misconstruing Aleph.

                                                        Yes the Technocratic Union is about "Protecting Humanity & Reality from the Things" but this is covered in the front of the Guide to the Technocracy where it presents the single biggest threat to the world being Reality Deviants and the biggest threat to the Union (given the Ascension War for one thing) are the Reality Deviants of the Traditions.
                                                        It then goes on to talk about the other supernaturals and the problem they have with dealing with them coming down to manpower, intel and even parts of the union accepting that things like the Walking Dead and Shapeshifters exist.

                                                        It also comes down to how BIG you want the Union to be I suppose and what it's immediate, far reaching and true agendas are, because whenever the Union is brought up in these discussions as the hammer that smacks supernaturals that is an actual problem. While most of the Conventions have 'field agents' a lot of what people are thinking of is not what the Conventions deploy. You have a limited number of MiB clones, a more limited number of HiTmarks, only so many MiB-MiG-MiW Enlightened Agents from within the NWO and only so many Void Engineer marines you can drop in. They do have a lot of 'mundane' agents and operatives for a given value of 'a lot' in the same way that say the FBI has a lot of field agents. This is why from the outside when you come up against the Union they are a Conspiracy Theory with a few Agents in Mirrorshades and some local law enforcement back up who may also have a specialist SWAT team and a Terminator if things get REALLY bad.

                                                        Don't forget that, with crossover 'numbers' supplied Mages are rarer than Garou to something like 1-5 and the Union has, at best really half of the Mages in the world, where as the Garou have over 90% of the Garou in the world. The Garou are in a more open war with the agencies of Pentex (given that few of them will actually know Pentex itself) that's been going on for over 100 years now and Pentex has gotten stronger and more nebulous. The Union is in a more or less open war with the other half of the Mages in reality and a bunch of the dark nasty stuff that's in space and they attempt to get involved when supernatural stuff happens (because we are told they do) even with some of them not believing in it AND they do not want any of Humanity finding out what they've been dealing with, except for those few directly linked to the Union and even then they probably do psychological things to them (Mind effects). Not to mention they love their internal red tape and they are not quite as omniscient as they would have everyone believe. This is all while their membership as a whole are working through their Genius to grow and learn (because an Avatar doesn't stop being an Avatar that drives a Mage to new heights just because it's a technocrat) and you want to keep your membership happy, or at least compliant, else they could go rogue (or worse Marauder or Nephandic given the right circumstances). Plus the Union itself wishes to remain hidden.

                                                        This is why you get the "Vampires police their own" from the departments that don't just assume it's a drug deal gone bad when they hear about a monstrous killing. Why their not kicking down the door of every Pentex subsidiary and why they are not always chasing after reports of urban 'bear' attacks. But they are VERY concerned about the people who want the Consensus to change, don't have real issues with revealing to parts of the world that Science isn't all there is. This is also why they ARE an interesting antagonist to throw into another splat's game on occasion because part of that is looking at WHY they have shown up, what part of the global conspiracy have the characters inadvertently stumbled upon or upset. Nothing needs to be altered in this case, because it rides off the back of how things are; when the Vampire coterie helped the wrong mortal, or killed the wrong person, or influenced an affair and suddenly have the MiB tracking them. Maybe the garou pack destroys a Weaver facility with a supposedly harmful product, not knowing it was a Lab (or attached to) of the Progenitors who were dealing with an issue that could wipe out that town or city so the NWO requests aid from the Void Engineers and Iteration X and suddenly the pack have HITmarks and Power Armoured umbral adversaries to deal with and again it's all par the course for what's already written rather than having to re-imagine things.

                                                        OR you play a Union game and stumble across some of the Pentex stuff you'd normally only see in Werewolf and all the threads you could use from that, maybe there is internal corruption with the local Syndicate that's support this subsidiary, maybe someone has gone Malfaen Nephandi, maybe because a lot of the Pentex stuff is actually quite covert themselves and that they are killing local supernatural oppositions (Vampire AND Garou) without being obvious themselves AND that their business is good for local economy (and therefore human well being, for a given value) any side effects are being written off or let slide because there are bigger threats and issues on the plate. This even works in a Traditions game but I think is more fun when applied from a Union character PoV). Your not taking anything away or even changing anything when things are used this way.

                                                        In fact the existing scope for using the tools of the different games as they stand is rather broad, the cosmologies (which is the spirit world affairs their of) fit with minimal work if any around the core games. Game mechanics are the trickier side of things but there is advice there too.

                                                        I think, in relation to the OP's question (the intended one anyway). Is still that I wouldn't change any of the Cosmology but I think it's a worth while look at how to use the extant information, splats, game lines and settings in a cross over capacity for the best new story ideas, twists on classic story ideas and so forth.

                                                            Originally posted by No One of Consequence View Post
                                                            My biggest alteration to Mage cosmology has been that "consensus" and belief impact how magic works. Reality is what it is, but belief influences how the Awakened are able to use magic to manipulate the Tapistry which underlies it. I did this just because it saved me a number of headaches over the years.

                                                            Oh yeah, I've used this for a while. Belief *molds* reality, but it doesn't *create* it. There are so many things wrong with how MtA presents consensual reality that can be fixed with this simple shift in the premise.
