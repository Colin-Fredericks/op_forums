    All you need to know about Evil (Wyrm, Earthbound, Nephandi, Infernalists etc.)

    09-17-2015, 02:38 PM
    Hi fellow forumites.

    I'm trying to compile everything it's possible to know about the big bad ones in the CWoD.

    Until now, I managed to acquire both Infernalism: the Path of Screams and Book of Madness (Revised), for Mage; the Baali clanbook and Sins of the Blood, for Vampire; the Book of Wyrm (second ed.), W20 Book of the Wyrm, and the Fomori Players Guide, for Werewolf; and for DtF, Devil's Due and Earthbound, of course.

    What other books should I read about the big bad ones, or even about the small bad ones? Do Axis Mundi and the various books on the Umbra shed some light on the issue? How about the Book of Worlds and Infinity Tapestry (Mage)?

                              Not necessarily.
