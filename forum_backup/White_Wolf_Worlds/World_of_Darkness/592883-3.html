                    Originally posted by DarkExcalibur42
                    They're people. That's the difference. Mocking philosophies and ideologies is perfectly fine. Caricaturing an entire race of people is not cool. This can get fuzzy at times because of how much overlap there often is between the perception of a people and their ideas (which include faiths). If I published a WoD book that talked about how all the Italians were greasy crime bosses, that would be wrong. If I published a book that made fun of organized crime, saying "hey it turns out they're actually all unwitting dupes working for a Demon and bringing about the apocalypse" that's fine. If I published a book that portrayed all Christian characters as religious zealots who go off on killing sprees, that's wrong. If I publish a book that says "hey, all those Christians? They don't know it... but they're following a sham," that's fine. There's a difference between racial stereotyping and and hypothetically questioning a system of thought, faith, ideology, gov't, etc. One is about people. The other is about things.
                    Well put. I note that, while Demon mocks (to some extent), it doesn't stereotype. And that's a world of difference.
