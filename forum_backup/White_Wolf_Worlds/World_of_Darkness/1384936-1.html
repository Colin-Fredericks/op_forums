                                            “Minor Spheres” was something introduced in the MRev Storyteller's Handbook. They were never “canon”. The three Technocratic Sphere variants took three Spheres that are problematic for the Technocratic worldview (Correspondence, Prime, and Spirit) and reworked them into something more in sync with Enlightened Science: Dimensional Science was originally introduced as a way to address the fact that the Gauntlet is stronger in civilized areas and weaker out in the wilderness, which meant that the worst place for the Void Engineers to try to use it to cross over into the Umbra would be at a Technocratic stronghold. The original version of Dimensional Science was basically Spirit with a slight tweak to allow VE bases to be reasonable launch points for expeditions into the Umbra. And for over 20 years, that's how things stood: other than the Foundation and Pillars system from Dark Ages: Mage, nearly everything Mage-related used the nine Spheres as written, with the VE's Dimensional Science being the sole and extremely minor exception.

                                            (Incidentally, Dark Ages: Mage is technically a Dark Ages: Vampire supplement in that you needed to own the latter to have the full set of rules to play the game; but I say “technically” because the book wasn't written the way you'd expect a supplement to be written: it wasn't written in terms of “here are some guys to serve as allies and/or antagonists for your vampire protagonists”; it was written with mages being the protagonists in their own right, and any mention of vampires in the book was largely incidental.)

                                            It wasn't until the Revised Convention Books came out in 2012 that the Technocratic Sphere variants really became a thing: the NWO Convention Book introduced Data, which was conceived as a variant of Correspondence that was introduced to replace the more mystical Correspondence Range table with something more suited for Enlightened Science; the Syndicate book introduced a variant of Prime (“Primal Utility”) that replaced earlier attempts to talk about Prime as a kind of energy field with something that aligns better with the economic theories of the Syndicate. And the VE book expanded on Dimensional Science's differences from Spirit and introduced a different variant of Correspondence that made distance a bigger part of it — though that variant just replaced the Correspondence Range table with another one, making it a “variant Sphere” to about the same extent that the original Dimensional Science was a variant Sphere. And then M20 went and promoted the Sphere variants from Convention-specific things to “this is how the Technocracy sees the Spheres”.

                                            There's room in M20 for some revisionism as to the Technocratic Spheres; but I'd still want the result to resemble, if not entirely parallel, the breakdown used by the Traditions. But that's a topic for another thread.

                                            My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness
