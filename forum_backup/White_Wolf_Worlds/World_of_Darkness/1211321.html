                              Originally posted by Batjutsu View Post
                              I'm very impressed with M20, I'd definitely back future projects, particularly anything Technocracy
                              Whilst I don't think a 5th Ed Mage by new White Wolf is needed, since M20 is so wonderful, I'd definitely give it a look and probably buy regardless
                              M20 is a lot of things, but I wouldn’t go so far to say it was beyond improvement. While they have a huge amount of information condesning the metaplot of the game and explaining many of the factions in much greater detail than any core source book that came before it, it certainly suffers from being much too all encompassing. It tries to touch upon everything and so most topics only get the briefest of mentions before being set aside, such as the umbra, spirits in general, and unfortunately the spheres themselves.

                              This book may be great as an all in one reference, but as an introduction to new players it falls short in many ways. Too much breadth, not enough examples, and more tables than strictly necessary, making the whole system look way more complicated from the offset than it needs to.

                              I think a 5th Ed would serve the game best by scaling back a bit of that breadth and focusing more on the core stuff. Enough to explain each sphere and true factors that are important to the core experience like the domains of the traditions, the important details that are necessary to understand core sphere abilities like the umbra and spirits, wonder crafting, and perhaps some better rules for time travel and Entropy style fate changing.

                              An update on what mages have been up to for the past decade, and maybe some details for plot ideas.
