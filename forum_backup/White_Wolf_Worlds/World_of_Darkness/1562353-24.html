        Originally posted by GreatHornedRatkin View Post
        Come to think of it, it should be mandatory for all Elders and Methuselah characters to become Bi / Multi Lingual.
        Not necessarily. Some people actively forget how they used to speak a few decades ago, it isn't unlikely that a vampire with little attention to such details would do the same.

        Also, VtM books are quite clear that vampires in the setting adapt to language abnormally fast. While anachronistic speech is a recurring motif among players, the actual books make it mostly non existent. Any Methuselah speaking in outdated fashion either woke very recently or are uncommon exceptions, not the norm, even possibly more affectation than real difficulty to catch on.

        V5 is the only corebook that doesn't talk about this directly, and even it has makes no reference to the related Flaw being common, just that it exists.

        Overall, though the cost of learning languages in the WoD systems is overall too high to make it a regular part of the game. Which I actually find quite boring. As hard as actually learning a new language is, making it costly in the game only results in this being done sparingly or never, what removes the whole theme from the game rather than giving it teeth.

        I think that deriving languages from something other than exclusive language traits would help a lot. For example, We could have Language as a specialization for Intelligence and Academics that, if chosen, let you know as many extra languages as your dots in those traits. If you don't have them, you still get an extra language at 4th dot in each. And outside it you can learn languages without XP or at a fixed 1 XP per language cost.

        The world has more than 20 thousand languages in use today. Giving people a dozen extras won't make anyone an universal translator, but will increase enormously the presence of languages as an element in game.

                What's even slang for someone who saw millennia of linguistic drift?

                Everything regarded now as proper form was at some point (and sometimes not even that long ago) slang, neologism, accent, what have you.

                They didn't saw people change from Old English to Modern English. They saw slang and all sorts of linguistic "vices" being added, build up over each other, go from reviled to widespread, until the "same" language was but unrecognizable from centuries before despite being perfectly understandable, just with a new fad, from one year to the other.

                Such a Methuselah either never paid attention and just adopted whatever people use around them, or they noticed long ago that the "proper form" of today frequently has little to do with the actual past, as even old people who speak like in their young days actually don't, but rather mixture their old accents with adaptations they took as time went on.

                Better to say they keep up with a given class or environment. Maybe they keep business lingo, or academic, or talk like octogenarian farmers from the southern frontier. But "no slang", just as "no accent", is not actually a thing and both most multilingual people and any Methuselah with this kind of attention, would know it too well.
