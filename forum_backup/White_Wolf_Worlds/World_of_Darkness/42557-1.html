                    I remember years ago that someone made the start of a World of Darkness: Springfield (The Simpsons). Some friends (at the time) and I were building on the idea and here's some of the things that we came up with...

                    -Springfield is pretty much where the Technocracy dumps all of it's losers. When you got a Progenitor like Dr. Nick Rivera and the NWO group, The Stone Cutters, (who are more interested in "drinking beer and playing ping-pong") you can see why.
                    -Mr. Burns is the Ventrue prince of Springfield who can only drink from people who are loyal to him. The ritual he uses "to cheat death for another week" (the episode where Homer thought he saw an alien) allows him resistance to the sun for a while. Of course, Smithers is a ghoul.
                    -Professor Frink? Son of Ether all the way. Herb Powell (Homer's half-brother) may also be one.
                    -Lisa could possibly be a hedge mage.
                    -The Insanity Peppers are a magical creation of the Nuwisa (sp) that allow eater (even Mortals) to mentally enter an Umbral realm. The werecyotes will play around with the eater for a bit before giving them a quest that will grant them wisdom.
                    -A Treehouse of Terror where Homer becomes a Mage and ends up pissing-off every Tradition he tries to join.

                    Mage: Welcome to the Euthanos, Mr. Simpson.

                    Homer: Can I kill Flanders?

                    Mage: No, it is not his time on the Wheel of Life.

                    Homer: Fine. I'm not joining some creepy death cult that won't let me kill Flanders.

                    When the Technocracy won't take him in, Homer starts his own group. He gets a big head from his prestige and accidentally causes a major paradox backlash that forces the Traditions and Technocarcy to form an allegiance and in the end brings them closer together. So they both permanently lock Homer's powers and his and the town's memories away of this.

                                                        Sleepy Hollow (Burton, 1999)

                                                        Lady Van Tassel
                                                        A vindictive Verbena (why not) who uses the Spirit Sphere to compel the Wraith of the Hessian mercenary whose death she witnessed (and caused).

                                                        The Hessian
                                                        A notorious mercenary in life, in death he has been enslaved by a vengeance seeking witch, acting as her otherworldly executioner.

                                                        Relics
                                                        After his death, his favorite axe was briefly taken as a trophy. Shortly after, its carrier was slain and the axe destroyed, crossing the Shroud into the waiting hands of the Hessian.

                                                        Although animals in the Shadowlands are rare, the Hessian’s destrier was more than a possession; it was his only steady companion for the many campaigns he was contracted to fight in. As their lives and deaths were so closely linked, the Hessian’s steed was waiting for him when Colonial forces finally laid him low.

                                                        Grave Goods
                                                        After the Colonial minutemen killed and buried their unholy opponent, they marked the site of his grave with his own sword. In life, the sword was the source of his livelihood so it is fitting that the same weapon is also the marker for his death. This crossed the Shroud along with his beloved horse and trusty axe.

                                                        The Tree of the Dead
                                                        At the site of his grave grew a twisted and gnarled skeleton of a tree. Whether from the festering anger of a seasoned warrior being unceremoniously slain or from a seed of evil planted by a young witch, none dare say. What is known is this site has become a Shallowing - a gateway between two worlds. The Shroud is significantly lower here than anywhere else in Sleepy Hollow. He uses this (at the witch’s behest) to use Embody more effectively than would otherwise be possible.

                                                        His Skull
                                                        In life, the Hessian knew he would be defeated one day. By an enemy musket, an opponent’s blade, or perhaps time itself. He never imagined that we would suffer the indignity of being decapitated by his own sword. The Hessian’s skull is his strongest Fetter to the Shadowlands and the Skinlands. The witch who controls him uses it now to compel his service. If he ever regains it, he will take her screaming into the Underworld.
