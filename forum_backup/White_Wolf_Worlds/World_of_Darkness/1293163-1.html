                            Well, Etherites will frequently employ fringe or discredited sciences as part of their praxis. And if we're talking about defunct science that lets you manipulate electricity in the body, you'd be hard pressed to find one more on the money that Animal Magnetism.

                            It's a theory and practice that revolved around manipulating "vital fluids" in the body through the magnetic fields that all living things possess. It's inventor, Franz Mesmer, promoted it as a method for healing (by running magnets or even just one's hands over a patient's body). But proponents of the theory ("Magnetizers" or "Mesmerists") claimed to be able to use it for all sorts of tasks. Like levitating objects, conjuring objects, communing with the dead, mind control (especially from great distances), and the like.

                            I've insisted in the past on this forum that plenty of Etherites are likely to employ Animal Magnetism. Using it to shoot lightning from one's hands is totally on brand.

                                The biggest difference is that the vast majority of the time the Etherite is going to think of what he is doing as external and the Hermetic is going to think of the effect as internal. I don't mean that in the sense of happening inside or outside the body, I mean that the Etherite doesn't think he's part of the Magic/Science and the Hermetic does.

                                For the Etherites effects are based on external scientific (Or Scientific!) principles that, in theory, could happen without them. Their enlightened minds let them see and calculate the necessary actions to make them happen on command, but they aren't a vital component of the effect. That's part of why Etherites have a hard time dropping foci; they can't internalize something that isn't internal.

                                While the Hermetics are just as interested in researching the fundamental forces of the universe they also include an Awakened Will as a necessary component of any effect. Someone without that Will could try to perform the ritual moves for as long as they like, with 100% accuracy, and they still wouldn't accomplish anything because they don't understand what they are doing.

                                The Etherites think their effects require an fundamental enlightenment to understand, the Hermetics think their effects require a fundamental enlightenment to do.

                                                Interestingly enough, actual, Real World medieval Hermetics would be in accord with the fictional Etherites. Old literature on the subject of magic from that era approached magic as a kind of Natural Philosophy, the precursor to what we in modern times would call "Science". The Greek and Roman philosophers believed in an understandable universe, with explicable laws of nature. Medieval occultists followed suit (because of course they'd put stock in Classical thinkers implicitly), and wrote of magic in the sense of it being the understanding of natural laws. It's just that their laws included things like the Laws of Sympathy, Antipathy, Humorism, Contagion, Spontaneous Generation, and the inherent, mystical properties of stones, herbs, and metals. Even the calling of spirits, demons, and angels was merely an exercise in exploiting divine wisdom revealed to mankind.

                                                It's only much later that Aleister Crowley came up with the idea of the "True Will", and made magic(k) more a function of willworking than mere knowledge. The writers of Mage: The Ascension (and possibly also Ars Magica) then back-ported this idea, so it retroactively "always" was was medieval Hermetics believed in-universe. There is a LOT of what these two games owe to the syncretisms pioneered by the Golden Dawn and later writers, when it comes to the conception of Hermeticism.

                                                That said, as it stands now, in MtAs, Hermetic rituals and tools are at least in some respect a means by which the magician focuses their Will. Hence why their magick is Intrinsic to them, allowing for the more rapid overcoming of Instruments than an Etherite can, with his Extrinsic understanding of magick (or Science!). The Etherite believes that his body-lightning works because that's just a part of the physical laws of the universe, that could theoretically be used by anyone if they just studied enough.

                                                Technically, he's not exactly wrong. Sorcerers are a thing that exist, and are capable of using their Linear arts without Enlightenment. It's just that this approach also holds back the Awakened scientist when he's trying to overcome the need for his tools.

"You have to know what you're doing", they might say. Whether it's a selection of knobs that's needed to calibrate the machine (remember, the ray gun is an Instrument to be used for a broad range of Effects, not just a single-use zappy gun). Or it's considered a "prototype", so one needs to handle it correctly.

                                                        And of course a Sleeper CAN "know what they're doing"...if they've invested in the proper Sorcery knowledge. Probably Path of Hellfire. The Technocrats have a similar angle with their Extraordinary Citizens. You don't expect lab technicians to not be trained in the high level sciences the Union is working with, eh?

                                                        Yes, it's kind of a cop-out argument - that all the Instruments technomancers use immediately fail in the hands of normies, because they don't get how to operate them. But then again, if the machines were intuitive enough to employ by the layman, they'd be Devices.

                                                        This also made me think about my post about technomancers and the martial arts practice. Maybe technomancers cannot use some effects at all without a external apparatus. How could a technomancer explain turning his body into a dog with meditation??
                                                        Technomancers differ from mystics in that they believe that Instruments have a value unto themselves, independent of the Will of the caster. Martial arts, meditation, and even co-opted ancient artifacts all obviously have power. It's up to the technomancer to create a theory for it. Even if that theory has "psychic powers" somewhere in there.

                                                        A technomancer might subscribe to neuro-linguistic programming, to explain that his tight focus upon the form of a canine could cause his body to reorder itself. Basically a combination of self-hypnosis and a juiced up Placebo Effect. Body Control is such an established part of Reality that it's a mundane Ability, rather than any sort of hedge magic, so an Etherite would be well within his rights to question just how far a human's mutability can be stretched.

                                                        Alternatively, the technomancer might have previously fiddled with his own DNA, laying in a series of functions that lie dormant. Until he uses mental exercises to release the proper cocktail of hormones, that trigger the dormant function, inducing change into a dog. Moreover, that same technomancer may believe his theory is borne out by the existence of the changing breeds, who "must have a naturally occurring genetic predisposition towards changing shape".

                                                        Alternatively alternatively, the technomancer could pull a Miracle Man, and be employing meditation to draw upon a quantum-displaced version of himself, as a dog, from another dimension.

                                                        Or still more alternatively, the technomancer could be a Virtual Adept who employs Reality Hacking. Changing into a dog is as easy as editing their own "file" in the grand simulation that is the observable universe, changing them from "Human" to "Canine". The meditation is simply one method of accessing this data. Whether because they've "programmed" their brains to emulate a computer operating system (a neat idea I stole from Serial Experiments Lain), or because they just think that, data being data, thought is no less a viable strategy for interfacing with the simulation as any computer. The "meditation" part is simply them needing to focus really hard on the exact mental inputs needed to accomplish their goal (hence why they aren't "casting without Instruments").
