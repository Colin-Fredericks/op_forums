                                                    Delusions of Grandeur:
                                                    Yang imbalanced Face of the Gods it is, me thinks.
                                                    Several options are awesome, like the possibility to interact with both Spirits and Ghosts.
                                                    Rites are also a nice touch and overall survivability is not an issue... especially for a Yang imbalanced Face of the Gods.

                                                    Being a Demon would also be very, very nice.
                                                    Faith is rather plentiful, the powers are neat and some are seriously powerful.
                                                    I am looking at you Rabisu, because they look at Viccisitude, laugh their asses off and do their own thing.
                                                    And you can totally start with a Rabisu who has Lore of the Flesh 3... cue the immediate enhancement of physical and mental stats.
                                                    And even then being a Rabisu it is entirely possible to get the Lores of the Beast, Wild and Flesh easily up to 5 with dedication.
                                                    That's a killer combo if I have ever heard of one... hell, even having ONLY Lore of the Flesh 3 would be bad enough.
                                                    For Demons in general it is their greatest advantage is that only their level 4 powers and higher incur any faith cost at all.
                                                    Making some pacts is probably damn easy and ensures a steady source of host bodies if things go south.
                                                    To bad that we never got powers beyond level 5.
                                                    Yeah, Demon it is. Overall they are the pragmatic and easy mode choice.

                                                    The third option would either be a mummy or a being a mage.
                                                    Being a mummy can suck, due to the fact that you lack firepower and Balance is a shitty concept which almost ensures that I am not making it very far power wise.
                                                    And being judged over and over again is going to suck for sure.
                                                    Also, regenerating Sekhem is slow and a serious bottleneck for every endeavour.
                                                    So yeah, not a mummy.

                                                    On being a mage: well, Paradox sucks but it is most of the time rather easy to keep in check... unless you HAVE to roll out the big guns once in a while.
                                                    So totally doable, but depending when and how I run into some meanie out for blood this might not end well.
                                                    Then again, there are many ways to ensure safety depending upon the spheres in question.

                                                    Any option with Rage is right out.
                                                    It's a total bummer for interacting with people in normal ways and jumping into killy mode is not fun.
