    Silent Hill its the best depiction of a Harrowing there could be on Wraith, concider the plot of silent hill 2 , the creature being spectres and well..as for your shadow...you gotta play and see.
    Heavy Rain: Wanna do a dark campaing with no supernatual elements what so ever? there you go :P

    Portal 1/2 and Half Life: Technocracy at its best
    Grim Fandango: Wanna give a new and refreshing twist to Stygia? check this lucas arts classic.
    Dante's inferno: what is down the abysm?well.... i always seen dante as a Doomslayer of some sorts...

      This is probably the only video game mentioned in a CWOD book that I can recall: The Secret of Monkey Island was in The Changeling Storyteller's Guide. However, I don't know why they did not mention the sequel (LeChuck's Revenge) in conjunction with the first game. Simply because of that ending... hoo boy.

      Final Fantasy VII: A cyberpunk fantasy with a lot of environmental and corruption issues with it. Very in tune with Werewolf: the Apocalypse or can be used with Mage: the Ascension. Shin-ra Electrical Power Company = Pentex has taken over as a government entity or the Technocracy?
      Final Fantasy Tactics (Also known as the War of the Lions): Dark Ages line: Demon, Inquisitor and anything else in that line. Something seems to be rotten within the church. When the players find the truth of the matter and have to fight Demonic monsters, they later get branded as heretics. Vagrant Story, which is also part of this universe, also works with the Dark Ages line.
      Puppeteer: A colorful platformer for the PS3. This one would take place in Changeling: the Dreaming as you have to deal with a unseelie bear pooka that has taken over a Lunar realm.
      Folklore: Another game that's Changeling: the Dreaming-like.

      I'm surprised you didn't mention the Persona games, which is all about dealing with one's "Shadow".

          The World Ends With You was really, really good. It's an RPG that was released on the Nintendo DS and kater got a (surprisingly good) iOS port. You play as Neku, a dead, amnesiac teenager in the fashion district of Tokyo who is unwilling cast into the supernatural Reaper's Game; survive seven days, and you get to come back to life. It's a lot of fun and the story is fantastic Wraith fodder, revolving around what truly matters to people and how important accepting yourself is. (The monsters are animal-graffiti hybrids called Noise that flock to negative human emotion; they're a nice visual option for Spirits). The soundtrack is ace, too.

          Remi. she/her. game designer.
