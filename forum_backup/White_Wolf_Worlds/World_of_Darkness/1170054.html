    Advice on a PC idea

    12-23-2017, 09:14 PM
    I am thinking about getting M:20 for Christmas and while thinking up possible characters, this one stood out to me. His name is Basil Wittlebone, a 35 year old Security Guard and Navy Vet who is about to Awaken. Basil himself is usually a no-nonsense sort of man who uses both his military training and physical size to his advantage. Basil is usually gruff and often times, downright rude, to most people in the street. However, he is a kind, loving individual to one person, his daughter, Veronica.

    Basil and his daughter share a special bond due to the fact that he is a single father, having divorced with her Mother after she cheated on him. As a Mage, Basil would have to balance both his responsibilities to his Tradition, Craft or Convention and taking care of his beloved daughter. My question is how to make this PC interesting and what Tradition, Craft or Convention would really take a member who has a child?

      The PC it's already interesting, in my opinion, but it lacks a fundamental aspect of a Mage character...his approach to Magick(tm).
      As a Mage, you should have strong opinions about how the world works and how to make it work to your benefit. Mechanically, this will reflect on your Paradigm/Practice/Instruments .That's what makes you a mage vs an interesting dude.

      Mages are valuable and thus being attached with your family isn't going to ban you from any group. The Technocracy will make sure that you understand that the work comes first and family later, of course (and they have ways to ensure such loyalty).

      Mages are very varied, some factions may be somewhat more inclined to accept a family than others, but ultimately it isn't a heavy factor, so better think about what kind of magic you want to do, choose a number of factions that share such paradigm, and then let's see how these factions deal with family issues.

          Well...this depends on the reading of the Technocracy you're going with. Back in Guide to the Technocracy, operatives basically HAD to abandon their Oldlife in order to serve the Union in their Newlife. If this is true, then while the Union might set up the man's daughter with everything she'd need for a comfortable life in order to ensure his eternal loyalty, he may very well be forced to take as trade that he'll never be allowed to see her again.           As such, if presented with the option of joining the Technocracy or another group, he would be faced with the choice of giving his daughter a good life he can never be a part of, or taking the risks of a more informal organization in return for continuing to be in her life.

                  Why the Celestial Chorus or the Akashics? Is that because the image of those groups appeals to you as player? Or is there something in his background that you haven't stated that segues with monotheistic-like faith-based magic or introspection-based-East Asian-oriented/martial arts mysticism? "I love my daughter" seems to be the thing that defines him the most so far and that doesn't really have very much to do with magic of any sort at all. So, if the daughter is so core that she has to have something to do with his Awakening, you need a hook for that.

                  Seems like the Chorus would be easier. Add in a religious upbrining to him. Doesn't have to be anything overwhelming. Just enough to know the fundamentals. Like, say, a Sunday/Days of Obligation-style Church of England (his name is Basil after all...) Then, hell, I dunno, his daughter gets hurt or very sick and he falls back to the only thing he's ever known that promises something miraculous and prays for her and BOOM, gets something, somehow, religious-like. And, hopefully draws the attention of a Chorister or someone who will get him to a Chorister for his Mage orientation.

                  Otherwise, you need to work on some backstory that just ain't there so far. Like his devotion to his daughter doesn't have much to do with his magic at all. Maybe he feels like he mostly abandoned her as a child while he was off a navy-ing and now he is (trying?) to make up for lost time. (Honestly, killing the mom off might be better than just a cheating spouse. Navy-man ends up getting saddled with a daughter and after the WTF! shock ends up realizing that being a dad is the most fulfilling thing possible....) Then you are going to need some hooks to the Akashics. Something that happened while he was in the navy that set him up to think of magic in Akashic terms (being present during a wu xia-style battle on the docks of Hong Kong after a night of drunken merriment) which is there in the back of his head until something happens.... there's Awakening.... Whatever. It's pretty sketchy because the connection between a doting dad security guard and what the Akashics are about is pretty tenuous with a lot more to the story.

                                  Originally posted by Killerclown View Post
                                  My question is how to make this PC interesting and what Tradition, Craft or Convention would really take a member who has a child?
                                  "Which Tradition" mostly depends on the PC's approach on Magick. Does he know he's able to do Magick or he merely thinks he's doing mundane, even if well-trained, things? If his magick is Corrispondence 1 sniping, Force 2 body throws or Life 3 tsubo punching he may just be an Orphan following his own Military paradigm. So I'd define the kind of magic effects he's used too at first and maybe then decide who's going to find him first.

                                  Choristers and Templars can fit if you see him as a religious man, Euthanatoi are a good pick too but only if he can see murders as a necessity; Akashayana... could be, but then again it depends on personal philosophy because their ascetic attitude is just not for everyone; if none of the above, then the CoX can probably have him. Technocracy would probably use him in NWO or Void Engineers (depending on the campaign setting), Iteration X may be a nice third choice if you cyberize him a bit.

                                          From my experiences with vets which is highly anecdotal I'm going to make these suggestions. There were some general repeats I'd say, some were particularly faithful, some very pragmatic some very disciplined etc.

                                          IF Faith is part of his lens of the world then unless he had an unusual upbringing the Chorus or the Knights Templar are really his only option. I mean it is america he could be a neopagan so Verbena could be an option(Thor's hammer is a recognized sign for military headstones!) here but Chorus is probably your go to.

                                          If he pragmatically wants to "now learn magic" the two Traditions that look like Magic to your average American would be the Order of Hermes and the Verbena. There's a strong faith side of the Verbena but there's also plenty of pragmatic wisewomen/men handing down their way and adapting with the times.

                                          There's plenty of tech options if he was in the Navy. I mean the "i was trained to operate and maintain state of the art military technology and all it gets me is a job as a Rentacop" rather writes itself. This would easily give you an inroad into The Etherites, the Adepts, or even the Technocracy,

                                          The Euthantos are odd, but since most of them require a near death experience to join its a pretty simple story there. He dies he sees the wheel, he comes back and is trained in the ways of the Euthanatos. I would point out In the game I'm currently playing in there's a euthanatos who was an army medic, so I very much see the soldier traditions of discipline and duty as tying very well with the Euthantos also in m20 they are no longer tied to Entropy alone as a favored sphere, a would be healer is very much at home among them as a would be killer. Hell maybe he has a couple of buddies who didn't come back who are doing the ghost thing.

                                          The Akashics are rather simple with the various factions built around enlightenment through martial training. Still "That zen bullshit" may not be the sort of place for a gruff navy man.

                                          The ecstatics well here we have another direction to go, rejection. One of my friends who is an exMarine is very much against any form of heirarchy joining wanting as much freedom as possible. I can rather see this as leading into someone on this Tradition. There's also all sorts of methods of obtaining Ecstacy that wouldn't limit yourself to music, or drugs but overall I think it would be perhaps one of the weaker fits.

                                          Finally the Dreamspeakers, generally fairly outside the bounds of most Americans in mindset, I mean he could always have been part of some culture he's rejected(Native American being teh most obvious but there's other factions) on the other hand, for many a shaman the Spirits choose them. If he can see and hear them and they make demands well there is a pragmatic need there.
