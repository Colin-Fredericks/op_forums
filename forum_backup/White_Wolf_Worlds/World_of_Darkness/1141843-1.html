        I think the best take on John Courage is that he's actually a spirit created through the beleifs of the technocracy.

        He Fights corruption! Badass martial arts! He took the red pill! He's an amalgam of every badass secret agent, from the Prisoner to James Bond. He's a rogue agent!

        But he isn't real. Thats how he's still operating. He's a figment of mystical imagination thats been given so much strength because, no matter how much Technocrats repress their Avatar, they have to believe in something.

        All John Courage is an abnormally powerful spirit affected by belief. Which is why sometimes his backstory is contradictory and insane, his strength fluctuates.

        This way he can be used in the game without making him yet another "schemer of schemers." Instead, he's the idealization of every technocrat, but also a reality deviant of the highest order.
