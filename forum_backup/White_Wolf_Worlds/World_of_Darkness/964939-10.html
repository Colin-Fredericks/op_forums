    Okay, I'll have to step in here, and in a surprising twist of events against the Union.

    I have no boat in this race anymore, but I am however a huge Union fan.

    But calling the Union, unambiguously good is doing it a disservice.

    The sins of the Union are the sins of the west, it is a patriarchal, explotative organization, and the entire point of many Union games are whether you can tolerate what the Union has become or whether you want to attempt to restore it to it's glory days as the Order of Reason, when nobility and Reason wasn't buried beneath layers of "to break an omelet" and "for the good of the Union" propaganda. Like, this is some of the first things that Guide to The Technocracy tells you, if you refuse that the Union is just as morally ambiguous as it's enemies, you refuse the central point of what Mage is about.

    The Union is not fascist however, it truly wants to elevate all of humanity to the ranks of the Enlightened, but the problem is that you have a core of Enlightened people who have reached Enlightenment 6 and can thus see that when you use a plasma cannon, all that you're really doing is that you're saying Forces 3/Primium 2 in a different way. And these people, realizing that they are the one-eyed men in a world of the blind, believe that there must be an Enlightened elite to shepherd the masses, guiding them through the terrors of the night, without realizing that they have become exactly like the ancient old tyrant-wizards that they rallied their fellow men to arms against during the Sorcerer's Crusade.

    Neither is the Technocracy un-democratic. It believes in democracy because the Union represents the sins of the patriarchial west, enforcing it's "One True Way" one everyone else, it's CIA infiltrating other countries and bringing them down so that USA can implement "democracy", it's the USA backing terrorists to fight a greater enemy, and then being unwilling to fix the problem it created in any way other than it created it in the first place.

    It is not pro-rich, though the Syndicate might tell you differently depending on who you ask. The Void Engineers are Space Marines and Ghostbusters fighting a desperate battle for humanity's survival (and being space conquistadors), the Iterators are transhumanists who want to help humanity to remove it's flaws (whether you want it or not), the Progenitors are biologists and doctors, who want to free humanity from the yoke of death and disease (if you show up on a list of Reality Deviants, you count as disease btw) and the NWO support a strong state that can help it's subjects when they are in need, so they don't ever need to experience sadness or poverty or war (who cares if you have to sacrifice a little freedom to get it?).

    Of these, only the Syndicate is pro-rich.

        ManusDomine Lots of good points.

        One of the main reason I find my self siding on the Tech side of the question is really simple. Their strengths, triumphs, and faults are Humanities' Strengths Triumphs and Faults. Collectively I can get behind the message of trying to make things better / making sure it all doesn't go to pot. Say what you want about the limitation of reality underneath the Techs. but technology has become the great equalizer. Deeply rooted in Rationalism. Its the magic anyone can use. You don't need to be a wizard to cast Message. You just pick up your cellphone and give them a call.

        And I believe the reason the Techs are on top is not because they got lucky or were ruthless. Its more simple than that. They gave humanity a Choice: Reason or Belief ? Reason says you can do something. Belief says hope for luck. The Tool became the implication of Will. Just as it always was. Mankind was always a Tool Orientated creature.

        And before someone else brings up room 101 again. Everymage with at least Mind3 has a room 101.

        Vamps has all the control of the Techs and even less humanity.

        Now if we're done talking out of our collective asses. Lets focus. The OP post didn't give us a situation to work with. its just gave us the 'rules' but not the setting. and before you save reality as a whole. no. that's too broad and unhelpful.

        Let's look at resources:
        Vamps need blood. end of sentence
        Tech need food and quint

        Both of these things can be found in an abundance in urban setting. Vamps eat humans and Techs piggy back off of economy. So I propose setting the stage of the conflict with in a city.

        Lets also give the Vamp home field advantage. not because it's theirs but because before the conflict had happen they would entangle themselves into the City's political structures and industries. The tech would be playing second fiddle, and gaining influence behind the vamps.

        In the opening days of the conflict. I can see Vamps striking out taking a number of Tech Agents. Certain vamps can notice when things are Off/Odd/ Not of them. So in a conflict situation, I can see them trying to nail down everyone that isn't them. Once that has happen. The remaining Tech start employing obfuscation tactics, to keep themselves safe. A regular game of cat and mouse. Each must investigate the other.

        And like my post previously, Who has more information?

        This isn't about who is more powerful. it's about who can ID targets better and faster than the other side. When you have two glass cannons, it's not about who shoots first it's about who shoots better.

        Both sides can can play the espionage game pretty well. But I believe the Mages have some of the best investigation tools at their disposal. It takes very little investment for a new mage to be able to ping other supers. and be a PI. But it is an investment for vamps to do so.

        That being said vamps have a lot more out of the box combat tools. For Mages its an investment to be combat focused.

        Once the vamps find a target, they can swarm pretty quickly and over run him. but mages can more consistently find targets and plan the encounter, which they'll win because they'll plan. It's the Batman V Superman argument. In chaos situations Supes wins. In order Bats wins. Thrown in Fate/Time Risk assessments and chaos becomes orderly chaos.

        Then you find a flipping of the momentum. and Vamps have to def.
