My idea is that Paladin ends up inspiring people to become costumed vigilantes, resulting in a situation similar to Kick-Ass. I would like help determining how the CWod would react to this development

      How big a role do you want these people to play- are they antagonists? Foils? Random background noise?

      If these would-be 'superheroes' run afoul of a werewolf pack, they aren't going to live very long.

      I'd imagine these vigilantes would be treated like 'real world' superheros, I,E, no one cares and they are largely ineffectual. It could be a bit funny if they are murdering people in the name of 'justice,' and actually making situations worse... kind of like holding up a cracked mirror to the Garou's own position.

      There is always the opportunity to pit scrappy but misguided people against the Wyrm. Maybe some of these heroes try to take on what ends up being a fomori nest and they get their asses kicked... after they barely escape with their lives, a few enterprising banes visit them and offer them a chance to gain some real superpowers.

                              What do you want to achieve with "Paladin" Because having a complete mortal doing this stuff is a pretty good way to have the werewolves reflect on their own actions.
                              If he's spirit touched, they can rationalise that it's just another bane/drone/Ghoul, but the humanity of it should make for better roleplaying. And less likely to just kill him for being different.
                              If you keep him mortal, you could look at unusual merits to represent the special-ness though.

                              If you want him to have powers: stick to what you know, keep it as the werewolves understand. Gifts from spirits and the like. Crossing things over from other books can be difficult and you don't want to buy a whole book and get a new set of rules in your head for just one NPC
