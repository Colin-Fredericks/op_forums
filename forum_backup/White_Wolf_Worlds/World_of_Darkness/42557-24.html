                    Hellsing/Hellsing Ultimate Ideas and Question (and spoilers galore)

                    The villains who make up the "Werewolf" unit are pretty easy to stat-up, IMO, especially using the BNS LARP rules. There's actually a couple of options for each one - although all but one is actually a vampire and the nickname of the unit is just a nickname, there is an argument for them actually being Abominations, especially in the BNS rules (though it requires a bit of breaking the rules presented on Abominations there... they are villains so I'm cool with it). Not only is their unit name "Werewolves," they often fight with rows of fang-like teeth. This is arguably a style choice by the manga-ka, but visually it strikes me as them taking on Glabro before going into battle to get some stat boosts.

                    Tubalcaine Alhambra [EDIT: who actually isn't a werewolf but is distinct enough to define] is mostly likely an Assamite. His deck of cards could be steel throwing weapons coated in Quietus-active blood (which would explain how they cut into Alucard and keep him bleeding). He also seems to have a great deal of Presence, and in BNS likely has the power Unnatural Grace as well. If statted as an Abomination, the cards can also be Fetishes. I'm thinking he'd be a Glass Walker, given his influence over the police. Chaos Mechanics would allow him to utilize different types of fuel at once, as well. For an Auspice, either Ragabash or Galliard could work (Ragabash for stealth and infiltration; Galliard for social manipulation factors.

                    Lt. Rip Van Winkle was the easiest. She's an 8th Gen Toreador - Celerity and Auspex for sniping/shooting, and a love of opera? I considered Assamite for her but it doesn't really work anywhere near as well. In the LARP rules, 8th Gen also allows her to have a single Elder Power - Projectile - and still get Celerity techniques - Bullseye is a must for her. However, in the LARP rules, she is the one who needs to be an Abomination most - the Black Furies have a merit called "Arrows of Artemis" which can significantly improve damage output (Rage is a track as opposed to fuel in BNS rules, for those who haven't played; as it goes up, you increase regeneration, get extra actions, and do extra damage. Normally the damage is Melee or Brawl only, but Arrows lets Black Furies add it to any ranged attack). In this case, her rifle also becomes a Fetish. Her Auspice could be Ragabash or Ahroun.

                    Zorin Blitz is a bit trickier. She's either a Ravnos with Dementation, or a Malkavian with Chimerstry training. In the LARP I would likely go Malkavian because they have way better Merits. She's clearly Elder level, given how widespread her attacks go. If incorporating Abomination status, I think Fianna makes an oddly appropriate choice; there aren't a ton of illusions in BNS Werewolf, but Fianna has one (and access to Spiritual Emblem, which could make any single tattoo of hers a fetish). Probably Galliard or Ahroun (I'm noticing a slight pattern here).

                    The one I need help with is Walter. What clan does he fit at the end?
