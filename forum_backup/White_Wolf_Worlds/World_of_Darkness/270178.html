                  Do you want him to succeed? We can simWod all we want but it the history of WOD isn't a "people pull off the best possible thing always" there are mistakes, errors, misplannings.

                  So the first thing you have to ask yourself is do you want the game to move into that sort of direction? IF no tell your player that's a bit too overt for the game you want to play, rather than coming up with all sorts of SimWOD rocks fall everyone dies stuff.

                  IF yes decide the level of opposition you are comfortable throwing at him. You have of course the Technocracy in all its flavors. The Camarilla and Sabbat both having theirown reasons for going "DEMON PRESIDENT BAD". Same with Garou, Same with Tradition Mages same with the countless wraiths of the whitehouse, Hunters of various stripes(Both reckoning and other). And of course Lucifer's got to be seriously pissed if this goes through.

                  Now many of you are looking at this long list of potential list of obstacles as insurmountable, I will of course present to you the rise of the Ravnos Antedeluvian and the clusterfuck around it. A bunch of KJ came to kill him, they were winning then everyone else showed up, made things worse and worse, until the technocracy dropped a bunch of nukes broke a chunk of the universe and still failed. They eventually used a sattilite to melt him with sunlight but signfiicant damage had been done that didn't have to be if people had just left the KJ to murder Rav.

                  So I imagine one advantage your player has is all those groups firing off at the same time. The technocracy isn't going to just "let the camarilla handle it" they will get involved. So will random heroes who "Know better" and so on and so forth at the very least there should be a complete and utter clusterfuck of supernatural proprotions. This may be enough of an edge for the player to get through.
