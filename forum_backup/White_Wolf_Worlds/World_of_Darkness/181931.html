    Purified in the cWoD

    06-27-2014, 10:32 AM
    Why hello there! I have been doing my own personal edits and rewrites to cWoD material for a game I intend to run at a later date (as I am currently running both a D20 Star Wars and a DnD game). This is one of my many projects (such as writing an Elder Scrolls template for nWoD) and I am doing a little changes to the overall fluff as well as mechanics. One of these changes is that I intend to introduce the Purified (the Immortal splat) from nWoD. The setting will be Dark Ages or Victorian Era, and the majority of the fluff for each respective game holds true. Save for my inclusion of a few nWoD templates. Other nWoD templates include a conversion of Hunter the Vigil (with conspiracies) into the cWoD and Prometheans.

    As to the part of the project specified in the title, I am currently working on the Purified systems and fluff. My intent is for them to use the Demon/Spirit mechanics of Gnosis, Rage, and Willpower as well as having an Essence score (like all other Demons and Spirits). They will have Charms instead of Numen, and I am currently on the fence about whether to use the Siddhi as are (with a few fluff changes) or to have them simply rely on Sorcery Paths for performing their magic. After all, they were all once Sorcerers.

    What I would love from any fellow cWoD players and storytellers is a bit of aid on expanding and fitting their backstory into the cWoD. They aren't mummies, as they deal with the Middle Umbra and not the Underworld (at least for the most part) and they are involved in the spirit politics that exist in both Werewolf and Mage. What should the Garou, and other Fera, think of this handful of mortals who've achieved immortality through aligning with spirits. Are they abominations unto Gaia or are they worthy chosen of loyal Incarnae? That sort of thing. I would also welcome any advice or criticism on this little project. Does such a small edition to the cWoD really require any special attention or can it be left to performing some alternate versions of the Possessed/Sorcerer rules?

      Most Garou, upon seeing the Purified, would probably freak out a bit -- "Humans? In the Umbra? As spirits? Wort wort wort!" and, you know, be generally confused by the whole thing. Theurges, Uktena, Stargazers, etc. and probably the rest of them after a bit of understanding might actually be very impressed by the Purified. They're humans who have recognized the existence of the spirit world and are trying to balance it with the mundane world. And they've succeeded. In fact, they've succeeded so much that they've become part spirit. They have (for their personal selves, at least) reversed the Sundering. That's pretty impressive, and it also shows that these individuals understand the importance of the spirit world; it's not just some resource to be abused.

              Oh, let me explain that bit then. Purified are mortal sorcerers that gain the assistance of spirits to enact a ritual that takes their soul into the Middle Umbra and remakes it into something very much like a spirit. Their body becomes young and vigorous, and remains so forever more. Should their body be destroyed their soul enters the Middle Umbra until they regain their strength and effectively re-corporate in the material world. They can see into the Middle Umbra and interact with spirits. They lose the ability to ever perform true magic, become vampires, or anything like that. Instead they retain their hedge magic and learn Spirit Charms and other spiritual abilities. They tend to serve a spirit court of some sort, acting as envoy and servant of a particular Incarna. Meaning that a Purified could have been taught to come across and become Purified by one of the spirits that serves Gaia and then become the personal agent of one of her loyal Incarna or such.

                                  I think, when referring to the Purified in OWoD, it is vitally important to keep in mind one single fact:

                                  The Purified are NOT a Combo Meal.

                                  By which I mean, don't compare them to absolutely anything in Possessed, or really even most of Werewolf. The Purified =/= (Human + Spirit). The Purified are not a normal human that has been possessed (or Possessed) by a spirit; they are humans that have, for lack of a better term, become spirits. If we were going to compare the Purified to anything in OWoD (which might be a bad idea, since they're relatively unique) I feel that the only thing that can come close are the Undying, the Amenti of Mummy: The Resurrection. The exception to this analogy would be that the Purified did not have the intervention of Osiris, Isis, or any other entities; they Purified their souls on their own.

                                  So, while it might make a good place to start, one probably shouldn't linger too long in the descriptions of Kami, or Ferectoi, or really any of the half-spirit or embodied spirit templates from Werewolf.
