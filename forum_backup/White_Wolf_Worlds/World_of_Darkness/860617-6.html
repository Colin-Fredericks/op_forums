    89. The Feline Ventrue (Optimist/Architect) She always felt a sense of nobless oblige, an abolitionist from a wealthy in the antebellum South (not unlike Mary Todd Lincoln) who firmly supported the Union. For this reason she impressed a rebellious Anarch who embraced her. What he did not realize was that she was Bastet Kinfolk, and the nature of her taste became to drink only from felines. Yet upon learning her heritage, she made an alliance with a certain group of the shape-shifters. In her Southern city, she would protect "her" people and their allies from other Kindred. In return they would give her some of their blood as tribute. In fact she's a charming, rather high-Humanity person rigidly true to her word but time is wearing away at her optimism and conscience. She can feel herself changing, very slowly, and intends to set up some method of countering herself should she lose her battle with the Beast. Yet at the same time she has plans to take the throne and reimagine her home city as an oasis of human dignity and peace. The fact she has fallen in love with one specific Bastet, who is now aging, both helps her preserve her Humanity while whittling away at it.

        90) Sane Malkavian Primogen
        The Malkavian Primogen apparently is perfectly sane. No detectable derangements. He's a capable player of the city's web of intrigues and power struggles. He dismesses the Malkavian clan curse as an urban legend. He says he is not more mad than any other vampire he met. Many Kindred believe is not really a Malkavian, but he's credited as being the childe of a well known Malkavian elder and so there is doubt. He receives many phone calls and letters from a mysterious figure. From the looks of it the Primogen is a tool in a Methuselah Jyhad, and the player characters can learn about it. But plot twist: the old phone's cable is cut, and the paper of the letters is actually empty. The Primogen just imagines of being manipulated by ancient vampires. He's delusional about being a doomed tool in the legendary Jyhad. That's his Malkavian derangement.

            91) Those two Brujah you really don't want to meet (Dark Age)
            Coriolanus was embraced during the 5th century BC, a 6th gen vampire. A Roman general, he hated the Plebs and was ultimatly ousted by his own people. He turned against Rome, then decided against it when his mother convinced him to spare the city. He was then Embraced by a Brujah who felt that some good roman-hating guy could be handy. Coriolanus has since then been obsessed by betrayal; betrayal by your troops, your allies, your fellow officiers or your Lord. At the end of the 8th century, he embraced a young lord who was "betrayed" as well, Roland de Roncevaux, who would later inspire the Song of Roland and become a symbol of bravery.

            By the time of the game, however, Roland isn't exactly the hero of the song. Well, he never was this guy to begin with, but it's now blatantly obvious. Coriolanus has been at low humanity for centuries and Roland is now dangerously close to the beast. Both of them are too bitter and dangerous to be accepted anywhere, and both are ready to do anything to satisfy their fancy of the moment. They are dirty, they sleep in the ground, and Roland will feed on anything or anyone, and stop only when his sire commands him to stop his glutony. Coriolanus will frenzy at the first show of disrespect, and if you happen to own a red cloak, he will bully you until he can get it, like a sorry reminded that he was once Roman.

            Perhaps you met them my mistake. You were on this road, and they were on this road, and something must go wrong since one of them is a bitter, angry beast and the other one wants to eat everyone he sees. Perhaps one of your enemy promised to give Coriolanus a shiny, sadistic trinket to convince him to kick your ass. Or perhaps the enemy you killed was supposed to make a shiny trinket for him and now, he is anry at you because he won't get it.

            Perhaps you have been betrayed, and that makes you Coriolanus's friend - a mixed blessing at best, since he is persona non grata with basically every other kindred.

            Perhaps you know of a way to save someone from the Beast. This may the only way to truly get to him; as degenerate as he is, Coriolanus would to everything to save his Childer.

            92) The Tremere Caitiff
            You were a Chantry's regent faithful ghoul; not only faithful but useful, clever, agressive and brilliant. The Regent believed you were very promising, so on the day he was named Lord, he decided to Embrace you.

            But things didn't go as planned. You are immune to blood bonds and the Transubstantiation failed. Your Sire could not bind you, and if he could not bind you, then he had to destroy you. Yet he didn't; for decades you served him and House Tremere. In his eyes, you are faithful to the House, and an asset that doesn't have to be destroyed.

            Your hopes have been destroyed. You are alive, still serving your Sire, and he protects you, but you will never belong to the House. You will never have access to Thaumaturgy and you will never climb the Pyramid, and if anyone knows who is your sire... you may both die from this.

            93) A Ventrue with a taste for Brujah
            You were a loyal, young Child of Hardestadt - that is, until he sacrificed you to the Inquisition to protect himself. You were bound to the stake and the sun was rising when you were unexpectedly saved by two Kindred. They slaughetered the churchmen and managed to hide with you and, as soon as the sun was down, you fled the city.

            One of them was very disappointed to discover you were a Ventrue. He was a Brujah anarch, and not very fond of your clan, but the other one had always wanted a Childe and apparently that was to be you. You have a very strict tastes, and can only feed from human and vampires of noble blood. Being on the run with tastes like that is hard; but lucky you, your two Brujah friends were noble-born, and you can find your preys by smell alone.

            By the end of the Anarch Revolt, the three of you were blood-bond to death, and you took a liking to Brujah blood. You like to feel the rage burn in your veins. You are still angry that Hardestadt betrayed you, and the Brujah rage is helping you keep that anger alive. Year after year, decade after decade, you gave everything to the Sabbat, not because you believe in everything the Sabbat says about the Antediluvians (that would be your Brujah pack mate), not because you despise the Camarilla (that would be your Brujah "sire", who never joined the Sabbat and is still Anarch), but because one day Hardestadt will burn. Until then, well... that Brujah neonate just there really does seem super tasty, doesn't she?

            94) False Ventrue, True Brother
            You were Embraced during WW2 because you have a twin brother. You two were always very close and your sire believed you two would make an interesting team. But the thing is: you aren't a Ventrue. You never developped the specific tastes and your Sire noticed.

            But you were lucky! Your Sire died in the last days of the war! Now no one will know, because you like... blonds. That's it, you feed from blonds, and no one will ever know you aren't a true Ventrue, and you and your brother will be safe.

            What you don't know is that your brother killed your Sire to protect you. What you don't know is that you aren't a Caitiff; you are a Malkavian, born of the vampire you Sire ate a few weeks before he made you. What you don't know is that your bond with your brother is far too strong. At first he was just "you" during the day, but for you, you are him and he is you; should he die, you will as well, and you are as "blood-bonded" to him as he is to you.

                95) Anark, True Climber of the Pyramid

Then you fucked up. Etrius saw you as a rival and as a possible ally to Goratrix. His schemes got you a letter. You were called to a reunion with your elders to respond for a crime you did not commit. You didn´t understand but you were not a coward. Stoically, you traveled to your doom and were judged by the Inner Council of the Seven. Etrius wanted you dead but your past merits convinced the rest of the council otherwise.

                You were punished by the Ritual of Quenching the Lambent Flame which turned you into a 13th generation vampire. You were ordered to return to Ceoris were the mock continued. Your new "status" was a permanent job as "The Guardian of the Gate", a mere sentry to announce newcomers to Ceoris. This position was reserved in the past for the lowest of the acolytes but you swallowed your anger and served the House as well as you could. You knew your time would come. Almost a century passed but finally Ceoris was under a new Regent and in dire need of powerful magi.

                The permanent attacks from Tzimisce and Assamite were the perfect opportunity. First as a battle mage at the frontlines and later as a leader of a small group you climbed again up the Pyramid showing the great abilities that turned you into a Pontifex the first place. You made clear your opposition to Goratrix when he defected the clan and you even hunted down some antitribu. It took centuries but you were again a Pontifex and a 5th generation tremere. Etrius thought he was wrong about you in the past and sure that you didn´t know who was the one which bring you down in the first place. So the Inner Circle left you on your way.

                But he is so wrong. You know it was his fault. His arrogance has made him forget that he sought your doom once but you are not going to fail to remember it. Your path is clear, now that you are in a position of power you will plan the fall of Etrius and all of his lineage. You will not attack directly. You are weaving a web and your enemies will be soon entangled in it...

                                99) Jonathan von Eisenberg

                                Born in to a medium sized noble Prussian family way past its prime. You studied your families history and it had the perfect opportunity to become a major house in Prussian when they gained the rights to an iron mine some 220 years ago. But since then? No minor skirmishes, no duels out of honour, no ambitious marriages... Nothing just content and mediocrity.....you hated it.
                                Even if you only were the 3rd born son and youngest child, this would not be your fortune. No you aimed higher.
                                You found your passion in battle and commanding your troops after joining the Prussian military and fighting in the three German Unification wars before leaving the now German military with hounors in 1872. The state pension and your small allowance from your family allowed you to invest in new buisnesses. Initially with no success and even with a complete loss of wealth after a scam, which was initiated by your soon to be sire as a test of character. You learned, imporves and finally were able to succeed. In 1875 you were embraced and joined the ranks of the Ventrue.
                                At the beginnig your were delighted and proud that you were chosen for your skills. But in the first 30 years you learned the harsh truth. Vampire society is feudalism all over again even harsher since nobody ever dies. For a time you found solice in the battle against the Sabbat and other monsters in the chaos of the Great War and even got some boons and status in- and outside of the Clan. But the strict hierachy was slowly but surely killing you. So you decided to something about it, you wanted more democracy and more rights based on merit instead of age and power.
                                You started to secretly suppporting the growing anarch movement while keeping a loyalist apperence in the Camarilla. You created a network, where you were able to support them with money and more important information. To your surprise you whole-heartly supported the ideas and ideals of the movement.
                                The last 80 years you played this dangerous game becoming a diplomat between the old and young in the process but its getting harder to sympathize with the ideals from year to year. For now you fight these thoughts as much as you the Sabbat if they are in town but if you're not careful you will become one of the beasts you loathe and that would be so....well mediocre.

                                    100) Silas Smith - Tremere Enforcer

                                    Description:
                                    Silas is 5'6" and well built. Despite his century of undeath, his many years of riding and working under the Sun has left his skin tanned and with the texture of old leather.

                                    His body also sports several old scars (bull horn gouge on his lower left calf, knife, arrows and bullet wounds from bandits and indians weapon).


                                    Background:
                                    Silas Smith was born in 1809 in Asheville in North Carolina. Growing up he learned how to read, ride a horse/tend to animal and shoot. At the age of 16 he left with Dreams of making in in the West (he had a glorified view of the Colonization) and for about 5 years he worked as a cowboy/ranch hand, then worked a couple year to build the railroad. In 1832 he moved to the Iowa Territory to help remove the Indians/settle the Territory and fown in the Blawk Hawk War.

                                    In 1834 as he was in Cleveland, drinking in a bar, he got into a nasty barfight, with some pretty mean ruffians after knocking several out, he was himself knocked unconcious.When he work up, he found out that he was buried, after the initial panic, he quickly dug his way out...not realizing he was no longer breathing. He was one of 7 who got out of the ground before sunrise...5 more cooked to death and 1 never came out.

                                    His pack was composed of him (a Tremere Antitribu), of 2 Country Gangrel, 1 Lasombra, 2 Brujah Antitribu and a Kiasyd. Strangely enough, despite his Clan, he was never inclined towards Thaumaturgy and for several years his pack was pretty much Sabbat enforcers and raiders causing trouble in Camarilla cities as well as back West in the new colonies...several of the reported indians and bear massacres, were really just his pack having a bit of fun.

                                    in 1902 His pack and 5 others were sent to Chicago to liberate it for the Sabbat.                                             102: AnnaMarie Librizzi was born in Sicily in 1899. Her mother was a simple cleaning woman for a rich family who lived in a villa high atop the nearest hill. Her mother was also a not-so-secret secret alcoholic. Their father was nowhere to be found, and her mother could just barely hold herself together enough to get up every morning to go scrub the marble floors of her boss's house. When Mama got home each night, she would retreat to her room and not come out again until the next morning.

                                            From an early age, AnnaMarie learned that anything she (or her twin brother, Alberto) needed they would need to provide themselves. The rest of the village called them "bastardi", some of the children threw pebbles at them when they tried to walk the main street, and every few weeks AnnaMarie would have to scrub the word "Puttana" from the front wall of her family's little house. The only comfort they could find was from their Aunt Sofia, who was as poor as they, but did what she could.

                                            That was, until 1905, when Sofia announced she was moving to America with her new husband. AnnaMarie was heartbroken, but Sofia promised she would write. This only made AnnaMarie cry more, as she could not read. Sofia arranged for a local priest, a cousin of her husband, to help with the letters. (And, unknown to AnnaMarie, to check in on the children regularly.)

                                            AnnaMarie and Alberto got by the best they could, by the age of seven, they were picking through trash for rags, bones, and old bottles they could sell. She gathered wild herbs, mushrooms and ground bird eggs in the country-side to sell door to door. When they were twelve, Alberto snuck into a train station being built. He just smiled and kept his head down. He swept the floors and carried bricks, one-by-one. By the time any of the men realized he wasn't meant to be there, he had done a full day's work. He was hired on the spot.

                                            AnnaMarie was heartbroken when Alberto signed up for the army at fifteen. She was terrified to be without him. She was waiting tables at a restaurant, and taking in washing. But without Alberto's income, how could she make do? The day Alberto left, AnnaMarie decided to leave, too.

                                            She cut her hair short, memorized her Aunt Sofia's address in New York, and put on Alberto's clothes. She signed up with a freighter headed to America, using her brother's name. After months of wandering and diversions, caused mostly by the war, she finally arrived in New York harbor on 24 October, 1918. She jumped ship and swam ashore. It was after midnight when she knocked on her Aunt Sofia's door. Having spent the last of her strength climbing the fifth floor walk-up on Mott Street, AnnaMarie collapsed in the doorway and slept for two straight days.

                                            When she awoke, Sofia explained she had heard from Sicily. Alberto was missing in action and presumed dead.

                                            You might expect this is the part of the story where AnnaMarie collapses and wails and... well, she did all that, of course. But, as is the way of things, life went on. She married a man, a good man, Luigi. They started out installing carpets, then opened a little carpet store. There were children, of course. She learned to read from them, really. The Depression was hard, but to AnnaMarie, it felt like winning the lottery after her childhood. They worked long hours, opened seven carpet stores, and raised eleven children. She lost a son to the Japanese in World War II, had twenty-seven grandchildren, and moved to a little house out in Ozone Park, Queens, with a garden and a driveway.

                                            Luigi was shoveling snow during the Blizzard of 1960 when he died of a heart attack. The next day, she gave all of her clothes to the church, walked into the Gertz's Department Store on Liberty Avenue, and bought an entirely black wardrobe. She never wore any other color for the rest of her life. Other than that, she simply swung into action, organizing the funeral, taking control of the "Happy Lou's Discount Carpet Mart" empire. She expanded the chain from seven stores to twenty-three by 1974, when she finally retired, at the insistence of her children.

                                            She was in the hospital three months later. The doctor said it was diabetes, asthma, and heart disease, but she knew better. It was boredom and the frustration of having others do for her. She was a tired, sick, bored old woman. She was laying there, in the middle of the night, counting the holes in the ceiling's acoustic tile, when there was a knock at the door.

                                            She turned, and there stood Alberto. She crossed herself and clenched her chest. He must have been there to escort to heaven. But, if that's it, then why did her chest hurt so badly? And, though he was as handsome as ever, he was wearing modern clothes... corduroy bell bottoms and a cable-knit sweater? Shouldn't he be in his uniform? She tried to scream to make the pain in her chest stop, but couldn't and then there was just darkness.

                                            She awoke in Alberto's haven. He spent the next few months walking her through a brisk agoge. When the time came for her to choose a feeding preference, Alberto begged her to think broadly. He had ended up only able to feed on veterans of world war one, and times were getting hard. She found her ideal vintage was women with both children and a job. She completed her agoge by researching methods of changing a Ventrue's feeding habit.

                                            She practiced on her brother, and he now feeds on any veteran. She has traveled the world, and has gained quite the reputation (and a great deal of favors) around the clan for helping those clan-mates who find their tastes exceeding their means.

                                            She is known professionally by her clanmates as "Nonna Vedova", and still dresses all in black. She appears to be a frail old woman, with olive skin and shoe-polish black hair, which she swears is not dyed, but is not to be trifled with. Alberto trained her well in martial matters, and she walks with a sword-cane, which no airline attendant would dare to ever take from the old lady on the red-eye flight. She has been using her travels, and her "help with kitchen problems", to amass piles of favors and bigger piles of secrets about her clanmates.

                                            AnnaMarie is strongly considering returning to Sicily to establish a small domain. She already knows exactly the house on a hilltop she would like for a haven.

                                                103) To say that Marshal Knight has commitment issues would be an understatement. Since joining clan Malkavian, he has found himself with a compulsive urge to side with the antagonist of any group he's faced with. When surrounded by the Sabbat, he favors Camarilla philosophy. When surrounded by the Camarilla, he favors the Anarchs, and when surrounded by Anarchs, he favors the Sabbat. His stance in such matters changes so fluidly that even Marshal can no longer recognize what he truly believes in anymore. In a desperate attempt to add some form of meaning to his existence, he has taken up the role of an 'agent of chaos'. He takes Malkavian pranking to an art form, in which nothing is considered sacred and all organized factions are a viable target.

                                                Oh how fun it was detonating that car bomb that took out the prince's newest childe and writing the phrase "Beware the Sword of Caine" in red spray paint at the scene of the wreckage. How widely he smiled when he tipped off the sheriff about the meeting place for a vaulderie he was invited to attend later that night. And how long he giggled to himself when he told a bunch of young Anarchs to meet him there under the pretense of having a late night party. Ultimately, the night concluded with all three groups blaming each other while Marshal sat back and enjoyed the show.

                                                Most of Marshal's pranks have been designed in such a fashion. The only way he can satisfy that antagonistic urge is to simultaneously piss off as many people at the same time as possible, usually without letting them know that he was behind it. He can't explain his reasons, only that it felt good to stir up chaos among the kindred population. Had he been left to his own devices, Marshal would surely have brought about his own destruction eventually. But there was one group who knew about Marshal's activities and decided he would be a perfect fit for their organization.

                                                The Followers of Set may not have been a perfect fit for Marshal Knight, but they were the best opportunity he had at the time. An entire cult dedicated to spreading chaos and corruption, it seemed that Marshal was finally surrounded by kindred spirits who felt the same was he did. In truth, Marshal still isn't too sure about the religious spin the Setites put on their motivations, but he can't deny that their assistance has greatly improved his survival chances. So for now, he's willing to overlook the cultist brainwashing. How harmful can paying lip service to a dead god be, anyways?

                                                    104) Ethan Barrett was a grizzled old detective with over 40 years of service on the streets of Detroit. Most people have probably heard the stories of what a terrible place the city had become over the decades, but Ethan had witnessed its decline first hand. And with retirement being just around the corner, there wasn't much the city could throw at him that the detective hadn't already seen. At least when it came to mundane threats, that is.

                                                    Ethan's sire, a drifter from parts unknown and who would only identify himself as 'Mane' had rolled into the city and was looking for an easy meal. Seeing that Ethan was close to his 60's, he assumed the old detective would make for a light snack before he moved on to something more fulfilling. This did not prove to be the case, as Ethan was determined to make his attacker fight for his meal. By the time the vampire finally managed to sink his fangs into Ethan's neck, the detective had lost more blood through battle injuries than from the feeding process. Impressed by his fighting spirit, despite his age, Mane embraced him into clan Gangrel, before stealing off into the night to recover from the ordeal.

                                                    Over the next several weeks, Ethan would be contacted by his sire every few days, who made sure to educate him about his new condition. The detective was forced to retire early, as he could no longer perform during the day shift and couldn't think of a proper excuse to be transferred exclusively to night shift on such short notice. But his transition into one of the undead has filled his aging body with renewed vitality. Shortly after quitting the force, he opened up his own office, where he now serves as a private investigator, utilizing his Gangrel abilities for something more positive than running around in the wilderness and picking fights with werewolves, or whatever crazy shit lurks beyond the city limits. Mane still contacts him about once every few weeks, but Ethan gets the feeling that his 'apprenticeship' is nearing its end. Soon, his sire will cut him loose completely, and he'll have to figure out the rest of this stuff on his own. Some nights, he almost feels like a fresh faced rookie again, stumbling into grizzly crime scenes that would churn the stomachs of most decent folks. Only this time, the crime scenes make a lot more sense, now that he knows what really prowls the streets at night.

                                                        105) Nosferatu Museum Director

                                                        Phil, for want of a better name works for a very exclusive museum. The artifacts found within can originate from the grand gestures of the Jyhad or the small petty squabble of a backwood Domain. Every item within the museum holds a secret. Sometimes the secret is small, sometimes large. Some patrons pay Phil or the museums owner to keep an artifact locked away and hidden. Others pay Phil to highlight the item so to boost their fame or to spread the secret the item contains to all and sundry.

                                                        Phil takes his role as curator of the Museum very seriously. He does not own the museum but he does care for it. No matter how mundane or insignificant the item Phil cares for them all equally. All are important for the history and secrets each items contains. Attempted theft or destruction of any of the Museums artifacts are met with a quick and deadly response. Phil has a love of history and a disdain for any who would destroy it.

                                                        Although Phil is not the owner of the museum he has made a significant contribution to the collection. Phil is responsible for the Museums special collection of portraits and accidental snapshots of kindred and other supernaturals. The organization of the collection has been very lucrative to the Museum. Nosferatu, in particular are willing to pay large sums of money to see the living sculpture of Absimiliard created by an unnamed childe of Tzimisce. Most leave screaming.

                                                        As the director and front man of the museum Phil is often the target of others who wish a secret destroyed or brought to light. As such Phil has become a little paranoid. Although outwardly gregarious, Phil is terrified of people. He wonders which one of them is going to try and kill him. Phil hopes one day he will be disappointed and he will meet someone who shares his interest in history without ulterior motive. So far that has never happened.

                                                            107) Tzimisce Justicar

                                                            Quinton Delauny is a rare sight among the esteemed ranks of the Camarilla. When confronted about his lineage, he proudly confesses to being a member of clan Tzimisce, presumably undaunted by the horrific reputation of his Sabbat cousins. Any comparison made between Mr. Delauny and those of the Sabbat, however, does provoke feelings of disgust from him. In truth, Quinton is actually descended from Old Clan Tzimisce, those who scowl at the use of fleshcrafting as a blight upon their clan's once great name.

                                                            Quinton may not have been raised in the old world, but he does come from old money, and his sire thought that was suitable enough to warrant the embrace. He was to act as his sire's agent in the new world, hunting down and eradicating the 'diseased' members of his clan who twisted the body with their perverse arts. The easiest way to do this, he learned, was to align himself with the Camarilla, who had frequent skirmishes with the Sabbat. Having already been exposed to the Machiavellian ways of the Camarilla due to his mortal upbringing, Quinton was able to rise through the ranks faster than most neonates his age and was eventually chosen to serve as a justicar, where he had nearly unlimited power to carry out his mission. Unfortunately, his station isn't as permanent as he would like. By Camarilla tradition, a justicar only serves for 13 years before a new group is elected. That means he's operating on a deadline, and his sire won't be pleased if they hear he's been dragging his feet on this matter. To that end, Quinton has exhibited an almost fanatical zeal since his appointment to office and hunts down the Sabbat with neither pity nor remorse for his actions. They are a plague upon this earth, and like all plagues, they must be cleansed.

                                                            The Delauny family is one of French ancestry, though Quinton's branch resides in America. They made their fortune with the merchant industry, importing goods from their homeland to the colonies and gradually expanding from there. Today, Quinton runs a small shipping empire that delivers goods from all over the world. It has also proven to be most beneficial in assisting the migration of kindred across the continents, and a few members of Old Clan Tzimisce have even been customers of his in the past. His sire retreated back to his homeland a few years after Quinton's embrace, but promised they would see each other again once Quinton had paved the way for his return. And next time, he'd be bringing a retinue of soldiers and servants to help carry out his work!
