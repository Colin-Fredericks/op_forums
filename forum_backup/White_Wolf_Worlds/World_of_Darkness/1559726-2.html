                                    ^This.

                                    The Technocracy, when it is villainous, is not so because of abstract ideas of "killing wonder". They're villainous because they marry scientific advancement to authoritarian systems, unjust hierarchies, and (especially in the 20th century) White Supremacy. And because they apply that "join or go to Room 101" mentality to their Mage enemies, usurping their autonomy "for their own good".

                                    When the New World Order are evil, it's because they use their mastery of information, psychological, and social sciences for control. They're evil for the same reasons the CIA is evil, not because they believe in Psychology or because Q-Division likes to make gadgets that fit in your wristwatch. They CHOOSE to put a bomb in that wristwatch, rather than smartphone technology.

                                    When the Syndicate are evil, it's because they support and undergird exploitative and extractive business practices, and consolidate industries under increasingly smaller number of huge corporations. Economics ITSELF isn't evil, nor is a business degree. It's evil when you CHOOSE to make Profit the only goal worth pursuing, at the expense of everything else.

                                    When the Progenitors are evil, it's because some of them peddle illicit drugs, others replace your loved ones with clones, and others were largely okay with Modern Medicine being a commodity withheld for money. (That last bit, by the way, basically stopped being a problem everywhere in the world besides the US, because Universal Healthcare is a winning policy both Liberal and successful Authoritarian governments tend to agree on. It's only the US, with its for-profit healthcare system, where Progenitor work is withheld from the masses.).

                                    These are not problems inherent to Modern Science. They're problems of POLICY. Of the goals to which the Union applies their Sciences, and how the benefits (and detriments) of Scientific Advancement are distributed.

                                    The Revised era (including the post-2004 material) onwards is about using the fact that Control was lost (and became Threat Null) to divorce the Technocracy from its worst impulses. (Except the Syndicate, who keep on being Capitalists, and thus the worst). They can be the Science Heroes they want to be, no less than the Etherites or Virtual Adepts.
