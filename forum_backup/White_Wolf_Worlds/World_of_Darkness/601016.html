              The other Umbras are never-ever-mentioned in Demon. And, unless you use a more "dynamic" approach to Lore, or create a ritual or an artifact, they're unreachable.

              In fact, they're non existent. As a proof, note that Demon version of Werewolves travel to the Dark Umbra when they step sideways. And if werewolves doesn't travel to Middle Umbra, then who?

              In the end, it all depends on if you want the other Umbras to be a thing in your game or not. Demon doesn't support them (no written rules), but it isn't difficult to imagine ways to reach them, where they to exist. Using rituals or artifacts you may craft a way to get there (probably involving the Lore of the Realms, along with any other Lore that the Storyteller deems appropriate)

                      It's been posited the many other times this subject has come up as a thread or ended up being part of another thread, that it is very likely that the Demon line hadn't gotten around to dealing with the Middle Umbra yet before it got the knife across the throat with the rest of the CWOD. There are some placeholder ideas, like Werewolves going to the Dark Umbra (which is about as unlike Apocalypse Werewolves as Vampire Lupines... in other worlds "totally in line with many similar WoDs" view of hyper-reality). There are also some indicators that it was something that would be dealt with later in more detail, such as Earthbound as Matt pointed out (and if any demons should know about the Middle Umbra, it would be Earthbound, being as they have been on Earth for centuries.)

                      DtF definitely indicated that the demons were from a time when the Umbra and the "Real World" were completely integrated One of the conciets of the game appears to be that demons feel the world has changed so much for the worst because all of the "depth" has been erased. When pressed for what they mean, what they seem to mean is the aspects of reality that have "broken" off and become the Umbra. It's a sort of "mind blindness". Their perceptions are too focused on the "material" world.

                      If they became aware of the Middle Umbra, you might see a whole faction arise that would be trying to re-integrate the two worlds, probably with disastrous consequence for both, but, oh well, to those demons it would be "better" because that's the way "things are supposed to be". Everything would just have to deal with it, "for their own good."

                      Also, the demons are equally blind to the mages' High Umbra as well.

                                          My general way of looking at it is that the Demons simply aren't accustomed to having a *completely separate place for spirits*.

                                          In their heyday, Reality was unified. Every person, place and thing functioned on a material and spiritual level. There was no need to distinguish between "Reality" and "Umbra" because it was all one and the same. The humans that existed believed only in God and the Angels, there was no Gauntlet or Paradox. Matter that decayed and animals that perished didn't *go* anywhere, they were simply recycled directly into new energy. There was only Creation and *not Creation*, AKA the Abyss.

                                          Now the Demons emerge and find the Engines have broken down. Creation is no longer unified, somewhere along the way the Gauntlet went up, separating Spirit and Matter, and the humans forgot what God truly is, focusing entirely on themselves and their own needs rather than something greater.

                                          So they don't have any experience with the Umbra. The Slayers have experience with the Shadow Realms, of course, and the Malefactors have pocket realms hidden all over the Universe, but not on the same scale as the Umbra.

                                                          In Houses of the Fallen it is described how, with Paradise already broken (Paradise being the whole realities unified), Slayers and Reapers fought each other throughout other realities before the creation of the Shadowlands. This ability (one maybe or maybe not shared with other Fallen) let the Halaku build a fortress at the center of the Earth and ultimately design the Lands of the Dead which they view as their new Heaven.

                                                          It can be asumed that, as spirits (and spirits that forged cosmos), the Angels and the Fallen were able to travel to these pieces of the shatered reality (much like Garou can). Nonethless the Umbra they knew then was one more pure, recently separated pieces of the world, like the peel of a fruit discarded aside, sharing with material world a weak realtion but in no sense other worlds on themselves.

                                                          The modern Umbrae (for cWoD) has in some way crafted itself from this layers just as the Slayers created a Heaven for their allies. The emotions, dreams, hopes and fears of the denizens of the world found their way to the forgoten skin that once was a piece of Paradise, forming the spirit realms which in time would become to be conected at varios points of their vast geography.

                                                          So the Fallen will no longer recognize this layers of reality if they see it: rind rotten, infested and festering with a new kind of existence that never was really intended to exist (or maybe it was. Long before the Fall, Usiel noted that the cosmos was changing without angelic intervention). Besides their power is diminished for the Fall and Hell and they are riding mortal flesh, so it is unlikely that they can interact or even percive Umbra. At least soon after the walls of Hell let them scape from their prision.

                                                          Indeed the discovery of the ephemeral realms and their implication is source for vast amounts of advetures.
