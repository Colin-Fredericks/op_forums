    Share your Demon: The Fallen characters!

    07-15-2015, 12:50 AM
    Unless you hadn't already noticed (seeing as every one of my posts is about Demon), I really like Demon. And have not once had the chance to run or play a game of Demon. But I've put a lot of thought into it and read many of the books. So, I thought I'd share my character here! And in return, get a chance to see everyone else's demons!

    Name: Joshua Marcus / Kimaris
    Demeanor/Nature: Visionary / Director
    House/Faction: Namaru (Devil) / Independent

    Strength 2, Dexterity 2, Stamina 2
    Charisma 4 (Admirable), Manipulation 4 (Convincing), Appearance 2
    Perception 2, Intelligence 2, Wits 4 (Quippy)

    Talents:
    Alertness 2, Awareness 2, Dodge 1, Empathy 2, Leadership 5 (Inspiring), Subterfuge 3

    Skills:
    Drive 2, Melee 3

    Knowledge:
    Finance 3, Investigation 1, Law 4 (Corporate), Research 2

    Backgrounds:
    Eminence 1, Legacy 2, Resources 2, Pacts 4

    Lore:
    Radiance 2, Humanity 1

    Virtues:
    Conscience 3, Conviction 4, Courage 2

    Faith: 3; Willpower: 7; Torment: 4

    Apocalyptic Form:
    Low Torment: Dread Gaze, Inhuman Allure, Lordly Mien, Spark of Faith
    High Torment: Cast No Reflection, Horns, Teeth, Voice of the Damned

    Merits & Flaws:
    Corporate CEO (+5): Owner & Founder of the Prometheus Group
    Natural Leader (+1): +2 dice on Leadership rolls
    Hunted (-4): A Hunter believes Kimaris to be a force of evil that must be eliminated.
    Rival (-3): Panacea Pharmaceuticals, & by association Pentex


    Joshua Marcus, CEO & founder of Marcus Pharmaceuticals was approached by Panacea Pharmaceuticals (a subsidiary of Magadon Incorporated*) with a generous buy-out proposition. Insulted by the terms of the agreement and concerned about the effect the new arrangement would have on his employees, who would have suffered pay cuts and loss or reduction of many benefits, he refused. Furthermore, Marcus saw the inclusion of his company into a Magadon subsidiary as a threat to the free-market values he had dedicated his business to and refused on grounds that it would bring Magadon closer to true monopolization of the pharmaceuticals trade.

    Dissatisfied with his response, Panacea Pharmaceuticals attempted a series of dirty legal tricks on Marcus. All of which failed due to Joshua's quick wits and expertise as a corporate lawyer. When these failed, they determined the best means of a take-over was to cause his stock prices to drop dramatically and allow them to buy in. Of course, this wouldn't work as long as Mr. Marcus retained his controlling interest of 68%. And nothing drops a company's stock values faster than the death of a charismatic leader...

    Panacea Pharmaceuticals, with some assistance from its parent corporation Magadon, employed a top-notch assassin to execute CEO Joshua Marcus. The assassin, of course, succeeded. He infiltrated Marcus Pharmaceuticals after hours, entered Joshua's office, and shot him three times in the heart. In the moment of death, Joshua knew he was paying the ultimate price for his ideals. And he smiled, knowing he would do it all again. Which was when things started to go wrong for the assassin...
    * * *

    Before the rebellion, Kimaris*** was an Angel of the House of the Dawn alongside Lucifer and served as a Messenger of divine will directing lesser angels in the work of creation. When Lucifer chose rebellion to protect mankind from harm, Kimaris followed believing whole-heartedly that it was their place to look out for humanity and defend them from unjust suffering. Kimaris most certainly did not spend so much time and effort on the divine plan to stand by and watch it be destroyed.

    As the rebellion continued, Kimaris remained a close follower of Lucifer's and followed him into the creation of the Crimson Legion where he served for the greater good of humanity. As time passed, Kimaris saw the battles between Angels & Demons needlessly claiming the lives of the very humans the rebellion had meant to protect and could not abide such travesty. He worked to shield people from the worst of the battles and moved them safely away from the worst of the fighting. When Caine slew Abel and began the Time of Atrocities, Kimaris like Lucifer was utterly repulsed at the murderous and vile behavior of many of his brethren. As the fighting expanded, and humanity became hopelessly intertwined in the fighting Kimaris found his calling. He knew of Lucifer's ultimate goal to raise mankind to their divine potential, but such lofty ideals were far from any hope of completion. Instead, he approached the problem practically and strove to gift mortals with the powers and skills needed right now to defend themselves against the terrible supernatural threats of the world. Kimaris distinguished himself notably during this time as a Fell Knight of the Crimson Legion and as an extraordinary leader of men. This reputation tying him so closely to mortals often weakened his influence with his own kind, especially at times when he would aid his mortal allies in defense against or retaliatory strikes against more corrupt demons who assaulted them.

    When the rebellion reached its end, crushed beneath the forces of Heaven, Kimaris was lined up with his brethren as their sentence was carried out. One by one they were cast into the pit as Lucifer was forced to watch. And when it was his turn, Kimaris winked to the Morningstar. He strode into the Abyss, knowing the terrible price it would exact upon his soul. And he smiled, knowing he would do it all again.
    * * *

    Joshua Marcus gasped to life, rising to his feet much to the assassin's shock. And Kimaris looked out from behind the CEO's eyes.

    The following day, Mr. Marcus made a press release detailing his severe injuries and the death of a would-be murderer in his own office. He made light of the incident, saying he "never knew corporate espionage could be so 007." He also gladly showed off his wounds, which were in remarkable condition. Bragging, he declared his recovery was no miracle; simply the result of spectacular new experimental treatments under development by Marcus Pharmaceuticals which he promised to be available to the public in the near future. Overnight, his company's stock values tripled and he was forced to make another press release to respond to his fortunes. Marcus Pharmaceuticals had outgrown itself, and with the resources available he saw no reason to remain limited to pharmacological products only. He renamed his company to the Prometheus Group and expanded their production aims to include a wide range of medical technologies.

    Meanwhile, the Panacea Pharmaceuticals & Magadon executives were reeling from embarrassment. To cover up their failure, Magadon down-played their involvement in the incident by a series of judicious firings (with some key witnesses mysteriously disappearing). It was to their best interests that superiors at Pentex** never realized the extent of their failure. Although Marcus pressed a lawsuit against the company, there was barely enough evidence for conviction. Rather than shooting for a win, Kimaris mostly pressed the issue as a bit of public humiliation for his rival and to give them a PR nightmare to worry about.

    With his rival company preoccupied, Kimaris moved to examine his new corporate empire and the world around him. The conversion of Marcus Pharmaceuticals to the Prometheus Group allowed him the flexibility needed to weed out a few undesirable employees (whom were detectable thanks to his demonic senses). With this complete, he strove to create the ideal company where his employees could have real faith in what they were doing. To garner faith, Marcus began visiting a select few mortals (specifically people with chronic injuries like paralysis or blindness). He offered them the opportunity to participate in new experimental medical treatments. Once they'd made all the necessary agreements, many in obfuscating yet honest legal terms, he revealed to them the truth of the matter and offered to heal them through their own faith. Through this means Kimaris received followers and employees in the know who can be relied upon. The most adept of these are recruited for another secret project of his.

    Meanwhile, Kimaris used a goodly portion of his profits to provide increased security (supernatural and otherwise) for his corporate headquarters using the assassination attempt as justification for providing tighter security for the safety of all his employees. These diverted funds were applied in secret to a second project of his, one which reflected his history in the rebellion of old. He began granting his diabolic powers to a select few followers so they could use their gifts to defend humans from supernatural threats. Some of these were recruited by the means described above. In addition to these individuals, Kimaris keeps an eye out for others with potential talent like psychics or individuals adept at the use of magic. All deemed worthy are offered the chance to work as a member of the Prometheus Group in holding back the evils that lurk in the night.

    To this day, Kimaris is a strong believer in the ability of mortals to shape their own destiny and sees the divine potential in every one of them. He knows Lucifer was right in his desire to protect and enlighten humanity, although since Lucifer's disappearance Kimaris has had many questions and no longer knows where the Morningstar stands. While a reunion with Lucifer would be a happy moment, Kimaris remains cautious and is withholding judgment as to whether or not he would still side with his old master. Times have changed, but hopefully Lucifer's ideals have not. As for the Creator? Kimaris understands his disobedience, yet despite the agonies of the Abyss would gladly risk the same punishment again and again in the name of his ideals. He cannot begrudge the Creator for actions against a traitor, however he cannot fully forgive the tortures he suffered for his good deeds. In the end, he considers the whole matter a difference of opinions that could not have been resolved any other way. The Creator's apparent absence from the modern world serves as a great relief to Kimaris, who has no desire to be cast into the Pit once more. Instead, he sees this negligence on the Creator's part as the ideal opportunity to fulfill Lucifer's Grand Experiment.

    *Magadon, Incorporated is a pharmaceutical company based in New Jersey. It is the second largest Pentex subsidiary, Endron being the largest. Magadon operates in nearly every field of medical science, from genetics to nutritional health, psychiatric therapy and even veterinary health. It also quietly works on developing biological weapons. Their company slogan is "Magadon - Building a better you."
    **Pentex is a multi-national megacorporation, one of the largest in the world. Its main agenda is the spiritual, moral and environmental corruption of the planet. Originally an oil and mining company, Pentex is now a holding company with a host of subsidiaries covering every industry. Its paper trails are labyrinthine and its directives subtle; few are aware of its true nature, and fewer still have any idea how to combat it.
    ***From Wikipedia: Kimaris, also known by the alternate names Cimeies, Cimejes and Cimeries, is most widely known as the 66th demon of the first part of the Lemegeton (popularly known as the Ars Goetia). He is described as a goodly warrior riding a black horse, and possesses the abilities of locating lost or hidden treasures, teaching trivium (grammar, logic and rhetoric) and making a man into a warrior of his own likeness.

          Mine is an NPC since I ST.

          Vashiel, Arch Duke of Secrets, the Hidden General, Hell's Gatekeeper, Archangel of the Abyss
          His host is a retired Soldier, 38 yo man. His experiences with war have made him very spiritual. When he retired though, he struggled with the lack of structure... lack of purpose, direction, and motivation. During his time in the military and also as a rebellious youth, he had always been intrigued by the occult. He met a man that would visit him on occasion and they would have the most interesting of conversations about faith, God, redemption, and the like. And it was this man who offered him a purpose after his retirement. His friend Revelaed himself to be none other than Lucifer.
          Lucifer's plan was to summon his most Loyal follower, and his secret General. Vashiel once served Raziel, the Archangel of Secrets and as such was a double agent during the War of Wrath. He was found out at the end. He was the last to be sent to the Abyss before the Gate's to Hell were shut. He alone knew Lucifer would not be coming. The Soldier offered to be his Host if Vashiel proved to be worthy. In the end, both man and Fallen were visionaries. As a man of Faith, he was a powerful Host which allowed Vashiel to hold on to much of his power.

          Once again, Vashiel served at Lucifer's behest, seeking to uncover the secrets of his enemies.

          Needless to say, Vashiel is either a powerful ally or enemy, depending on the needs of the story. Rather than overawe the party by having Lucifer reveal themselves to them, he makes them his pawns through Vashiel to disrupt the work of the Earthbound, wherein Vashiel binds Lucifer's enemies back to Hell.

During the fight, Raziel will possess Ethan's body. Raziel/Ethan will awaken screaming and incoherent. Confused and fearful. His friends will help him escape and get home. When Raziel took Ethan's body, he was flooded by Ethan's faith and love for humanity which mirrored his own. He was surprised by the faith of Ethan and of learning that there was a faith that believe that God had sent his own son to redeem them. Through Ethan's faith, Raziel believes that even he too may be forgiven and maybe even the rest of the Fallen. .

              What happens next will be up to my players. Raziel can be a great friend.

                          I get tapped as Storyteller all the time, but I did have a chance be a player in a Demon: the Fallen chronicle. My character was named Ninonael, Killer of Rabbits, a Slayer who got caught up in the War in Heaven because she wanted to fallow her beloved superior. Going off the top of my head, she was something like this:

                          Name: Shelton E. McCranen/Ninonael
                          Demeanor/Nature: Caregiver/Bravo (very much, 'I'm doing this terrible thing for YOUR benefit, you tosser!')
                          House/Faction: Slayer/Cryptic

                          Apocalyptic Form: Namtar, Visage of Death. I can't remember all of the details, but Ninonael was small, slim and black, with black wings that trailed off into smoke and eyes that burned dull grey. She had flight and some power of voice that caused fearful obedience, I think... It was a scary yet understated form that was very 'bare bones' in terms of what could be done from the Players Guide rules.

                          In Heaven, Ninonael followed the ordained 'chain of command' (not that the angel articulated it that way), and worked to make sure that various small animals found their lives closing at the proper time and place. Then when the man and woman appeared in Paradise, Ninonael recognised that they were different than the creatures the angel shepherded from life to death. These new creatures had sex differences, but they also had... what's the word? Ninonael discovered gender, discovered that an angel can be 'he' or 'she' and discovered her own gender. None of the Hosts spoke of this, and Ninonael decided to not speak, either. However, she felt something that she could not explain, something that seemed to light smoldering blaze insider her every time she perceived or communicated with her direct superior, Anaksos. Ninonael's feeling grew into longing, which grew into something she could not name, something that would not allow her to think of much else besides Anaksos. She fulfilled her duties, hoping always to draw the attention of Anaksos, ever longing for contact with her superior.

                          When Lucifer's rebellion came, when lines were drawn, when angels fought angels, Ninonael followed Anaksos into the rebel camp. She didn't think much of it in terms of 'why' or 'how' but only in terms of following her beloved Anaksos. Her fall came about by an unconscious emulation of a very human notion.

                          Imprisonment in the Abyss was torture for Ninonael. As she watched Anaksos chafe under the rigors of confinement, as his—yes, of COURSE Anaksos would be ‘he’—very form twisted and grew, Ninonael learned to feel pain as another felt pain in her presence. No, it wasn’t really that easy, in the timeless and formless chaos of the Abyss, but that’s how she remembers it. Strangely, Ninonael also remembers that Anaksos was not there with the rest of them… As if he vanished from the Abyss at some time.

                          When Ninonael drew breath for the first time, she felt physical pain, felt cold, felt alone, all things that were unknown to an angel. Her body healed quickly, and the memories that invaded her mind brought with them a pain that the fallen angel did recognise, a fundamental question of ‘who am I?’

                          She found her host’s identity appeared fractured, somehow both ‘he’ and ‘she’ in one form. Most of this human’s short life had been spent as Shelly, but this person died as Shelton, even if the body’s shape was that of a female. Shelly never CHOSE to be ‘he’ that Ninonael could tell, but the fact that the gender didn’t match the body somehow made Shelton into some kind of pariah. For all her difficulty understanding humanity, Ninonael’s host causes more challenges.

                          Things got worse when Ninonael found her beloved Anaksos as one of the Earthbound. That’s where her story really began…

                              I just finished writing my very first Demon character, an NPC for my Sabbat game:

                              Lupe Fuentes (Shuwala), Santa Muerte demon
                              A fallen Angel of Death who dragged her infernal court into a hopeless war with the Sabbat of Juarez, a Black Hand dominated domain. However, this Cryptic has more interest in collecting Noddist lore than in winning any conflict.
                              Lupe didn't have a family until she started working the Juarez streets. As a baby she was left on the steps of a Church orphanage. It's where the girl grew up, until escaping that oppressive place during her mid-teens. Though she doesn't regret running away, a priest had been taking for free what she'd henceforth be paid for, what followed were the hardest years of her life. During that time the young woman was losing friends left and right. Some got sick, others disappeared until turning up weeks later in a shallow grave. If their murderer had even bothered with that. Lupe urgently needed protection. Having lost all faith in the Church she turned to 'Santa Muerte' instead.
                              Her local shrine was a strange place. Saint Death's devotees were an eclectic mix of sex workers and goths, of queers, gangsters and even cops. All were people living on the edge of society, who had spiritual needs not fulfilled by proper Christianity. However, unlike many of them she wasn't a true believer. Not at first, someone had to introduce her to the Saint's dark miracles. That person was Catarina, a muxe or indigenous trans-woman from Southern Mexico, who had built this shrine. Lupe was granted Santa Muerte's blessing, with nothing but Catarina's touch an old scar was instantly healed.
                              Most members of the Juarez Santa Muerte congregation kept a wary distance from their priestess. The same magic they relied on also unsettled the Saint's devotees. Lupe was different though, she became absolutely enthralled with Catarina. Feelings that were reciprocated, leading to a happy period in which they managed the shrine together as partners and lovers.
                              Unfortunately those blissful years eventually came to a tragic end. Whatever sorcery Catarina wielded was of little use when a vicious pack of vampires descended on their shrine. Lupe was there, alongside the priestess, when the fanged monsters burst in. Before anyone could react both women were being violently fed on, until their heartrate slowed and then stopped.
                              At this point Shuwala took possession of Lupe's dying body. The demon had been a skilled fighter during the War of Wrath, like so many Slayers serving in the Alabaster Legion. Before the Fall she'd been a lowly Reaper, ending the lifecycle of plants. But often working invisibly in the presence of mankind. Shuwala loved them dearly, wanting so much more for them, motivating her to join the rebellion. The consequence of which, humanity suffering under the curse of mortality, pained her greatly. It's that anguish that had kept her going as a warrior, until all was lost and the millennia long imprisonment of the fallen began.
                              Shuwala sensed Lupe's intense anger and sadness about Catarina's murder. But the demon herself felt more fascinated by these undead creatures around her. It immediately became clear to Shuwala that Caine's plight had somehow spread far and wide. In that she saw an opportunity to save the entire human race, the one curse countering the other. The fallen angel theorized that after Abel's murder the Creator had seen the error of burdening mankind with mortality. The fact that Caine's condition was transferable proved it wasn't meant as a curse, but as some kind of fix.
                              As Catarina's closest confidant, and miraculous survivor of the massacre, the possessed Lupe immediately had the Santa Muerte congregation rally around her. She had no problem convincing them to take the fight to the vampires. Through some clever politicking by Shuwala the other fallen of Juarez were soon forced to follow suit. A supernatural war that serves the demon one specific purpose; unlocking the secrets of immortality for the benefit of all mankind.

                              Image: Everyday is Día de los Muertos, her style's been described as dressing like a "goth Virgin Mary". She favors long flowing dresses, dark colored satin and lace. On Lupe's head rests a wreath of orange flowers, dried marigolds, from which hangs a veil halfway down her back. Sugar skull face paint the demon tends to reserve for ceremonies, and other rituals, too public to take on her apocalyptic form. Under all that there's a pretty Latina nearing thirty, with large dark eyes.

Hence why you employ a cartel torturer to extract the necessary information from vampires and their retainers. Though these practices are not untainted by feelings of revenge remaining in Lupe's heart. You've found that in dealing with some incredibly scary people, and other creatures, the trick is to show no fear. You are fear! Your host understood that in this underworld religion a flair for the dramatic is key. A skill that serves you well when harvesting Faith, pretending to be Saint Death in the flesh.

                                  Simone Jennings (Bayemon), the demon double agent
                                  A House Namaru demon who's possessing an employee of Division Six, the UK government's counter-supernatural agency. Caught in a web of intrigue the Devil is ready to escape, in favor of a personal quest for an ancient love.
                                  Simone's father was a British diplomat, moving with his young family between a dozen different nations. Though she was a timid child, a natural knack for languages helped the girl adjust relatively quickly wherever they moved. What was far more challenging to cope with was the prestigious boarding school Simone was sent to in her teens. Though the other students were all of similar age, and social class, she couldn't relate to them. Those were loneliest years of her life, lasting until the young woman started university. In Oxford she still wasn't a social butterfly, but free to fully delve into her linguistic passion, forming friendships with similarly driven classmates.
                                  After graduating, with honours, Simone hoped to follow in her father's footsteps, exercising her language skills in service of Her Majesty's Government. Of the agencies and departments she applied to it was MI-5, the Security Service, that was the first to respond positively. After a long process of testing, and vetting, the young woman was hired as a translator.
                                  The division Simone was assigned to wasn't what she'd expected. They didn't seem interested in the type of cases MI-5 was known to pursue. The young translator wasn't even at all sure what Division Six did do. Cause nobody cared to inform her either. Sir Ian Lennox, the man who commanded the division, seemed to think Simone was little more than his personal secretary. Most of the time Sir Ian had her getting coffee, or copying documents she didn't have the context to understand.
                                  Elsewhere in the city there were forces who're far better informed about Division Six than Miss Jennings would ever be. The London infernal court wanted to extend its influence over the nation's supernatural defenses. Bayemon was the Devil chosen by the Ministry of Dust for this task. A Silver Legionnaire during the war, who under Kel Asuf the Deceiver's command climbed up to the rank of Fell Knight. This demon had only recently escaped the Pit, taking up residence in a homeless man who'd overdosed. It was in this guise Bayemon stalked Simone for weeks, until the ideal time to strike presented itself.
                                  The young woman was walking home late one night. She'd spent the entire evening translating some theological text, which Sir Ian had urgently required, from Ancient Greek to English. While passing a canal she was suddenly grabbed and dragged under. Simone was held firm until she stopped struggling, after which the dark waters claimed her life. A minute later Bayemon, now possessing Simone Jennings, emerged from that London canal, leaving behind the homeless man's corpse.
                                  A changed woman arrived at the Division Six offices next morning. Someone who started asking questions, who counteracted Sir Ian's patronizing attitude with her feminine wiles. This wasn't just Bayemon gathering intelligence for the city's infernal court. The demon had personal interests as well; what happened to Dimme? She was a fellow Silver Legionnaire and Bayemon's lover. They'd been separated well over two millennia ago now, when this fallen Angel of Death suddenly disappeared from the Abyss. In the translator's guise the agency's vast archives could be utilized to solve the mystery of Dimme's fate. And meanwhile none of Simone's superiors seemed to notice anything out of the ordinary.
                                  One person who did detect the change in Simone was Magda, her Polish cleaning lady. The woman was a devout Catholic who had a talent for sensing the supernatural. Determined to save her boss from demonic possession, she confided her discovery to a priest. Unfortunately for Bayemon he took Magda seriously, leading to the Society of Leopold being informed. Who in turn, after learning she was a government employee, reached out to Division Six. A nasty surprise for Bayemon, but not the disaster it could've been. Cause it turned out Sir Ian chose to see the demon working in his offices as an opportunity, not a threat.
                                  Bayemon was presented with a choice. Either the full force of Division Six, and their allies, was brought down upon the demon. Or there was the avenue of cooperation. Then Sir Ian would keep his translator's true nature a secret and not allow the Society of Leopold intervene any further either. In return Bayemon would have to help Lennox out with a sticky situation he's gotten himself into with some vampires. An offer the Namaru accepted.
                                  Since then Bayemon has been expected to silence anyone coming close to learning Sir Ian is being blackmailed by the undead. The demon was also supposed to find a way to break their hold over Division Six's head. In actuality Dimme remained the focus of the fallen angel's research. With success too. There's clear evidence pointing toward the lost lover being active in the US-Mexico border region. Bayemon is about to leave Lennox's employ, going AWOL overseas.

                                  Image: A petite brunette in her late 20s. The woman's fair features are adorned by striking green eyes, usually obscured by fairly thick glasses. Although still favoring professional attire, Bayemon dresses noticeably more stylish than the old Simone did. The demon has splurged on designer silk scarves and tailored skirt suits. Always combined with sensible shoes however.

                                  Roleplaying Hints: Social advancement appears to be what drives you. You have no issues with using people as tools, that's how they once treated Simone as well. Hence why her prim and proper manners have since gained a seductive edge. Though there is a higher purpose to this ruthlessness; being reunited with your long lost love. Everything you've ever done, as far as you remember, you've done for Dimme. So it was in Eden, so it is still at the end of days.
