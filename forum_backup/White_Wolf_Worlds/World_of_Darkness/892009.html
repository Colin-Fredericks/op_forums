          Zeal isn't the same as thinking of monsters solely as evil. Zeal is about dedication to an ideal. Defenders and Judges are both noted as being prone to having the idea of "acceptable" monsters that don't violate those Hunter's ideals. If a particular character's ideal that they're zealous about is, "protect humans from monsters," they're probably going to not treat all supernaturals are monsters, and only target ones that actively prey on humans, a well as go after humans that cross enough lines.

          If you have access to the Creedbooks, they detail "camps" for each Creed to demonstrate how different individuals interpret their Virtue, Creed, and place in the world. Temperate Defenders tend to cling to something from before their Imbuing and tend to assess supernaturals based on the level of threat they pose to that anchor; such as a cop that that sees keeping their precinct clean of corruption (supernatural and not) is the best way to keep people safe. Tolerant Defenders are generally Defenders that started as more intellectual and thoughtful in life, so they tend to value practicality and planning over rashly chasing every monster they come across; they're noted as being the Defenders most likely to pick up Innocence path Edges to help them ensure maximum information gathering where most other Zeal based characters look down on the idea of adopting the thinking of the Innocents.

                              If you are going for a Defender then what is he going to defend? Each Defender has the one thing that he wants to preserve above all else, so IF you indeed have supernatural contacts and allies they may be involved with the community you want to preserve in positive fashion. Let's say it's a community. Maybe the werewolves have kinfolk in there (then the kinfolk would probably be your contacts) or a vampire has claimed it as his domain for personal reasons but actually feeds somewhere else.

                              Virtue wise you probably should mix your zeal with a bit of mercy considering your approach. Also you can invest one of your starting Zeal points into the Edge "Discern" which helps you discern between good and evil monsters. Alternativly you could start stacking Mercy in play to go either for Witness or Illuminate.
