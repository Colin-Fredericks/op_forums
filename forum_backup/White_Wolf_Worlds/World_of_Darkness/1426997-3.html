    I think that as a splat design, it would be easier to depict Technocratic PCs and NPCs as good guys if there was more space for Utopians to continue working within the organization. From the defection of the Etherites and VAs and Navalon, and the comments of that ex-VE woman in Book of Worlds, it looks like you can't be a Utopian in the Union anymore, the same way you can't openly oppose the Communist Party in China. That would put Utopians in roughly the same powerless position as neonate vampires. They can't change the world or the Union, they can only defect and fight against it from outside it. And that constrains PC options.

    And unfortunately, since the Technocrats are also the main face of science, it also makes the whole game look anti-science. A nWoD fan friend of mine has zero interest in Ascension and Apocalypse because science is made to look so bad in both games. There are Etherites and Navalon, of course, but the Technocrats are much more prominently about SCIENCE (and Etherites look like pseudoscientist cooks).

        For what it's worth, Navalon is only a thing of you want it to be; if not, the Harbingers of Avalon are still within the ranks of the Union, giving the Utopians more of a place in its structure. In fact, they may still be there even if Navalon is a thing; one trend that the Disparate Alliance seems to feature is pocket groups within other factions such as the Bata'a faction within the Dreamspeakers or the Templar faction within the Celestial Chorus. Navalon might still have Harbingers of Avalon within the ranks of the Technocracy even as the new Craft seeks a place among the Disparates.

        That said, one reason I prefer the Navalon option is that Navalon has a chance to be in competition with the Union for the face of Science: unlike the Sons of Ether, their brand of Science (as I envision them) follows the same form and function as the Technocracy's; in M20 Focus terminology, they use the same Practices and Instruments. Even their Paradigms are quite similar, the key difference being what I outlined in my earlier post: Navalon is attempting to restore what it sees as the Craftsmasons' ideals (though given its name, it's phrased in terms of Camelot's ideals), with an ethical foundation to combat hubris.

        I'm still working on some of the details; but that's the gist of it.

                                                            Originally posted by Resplendent Fire View Post
                                                            Scarcity isn't real. The enforcement of false scarcity is a critical part of capitalism, which the Syndicate promotes.
                                                            Well, it's not so much a false problem as a problem of logistics that should have been solved by our technological development, but it wasn't thanks to how the Capitalism system - understood as more than just Liberalism, but as the way the superpowers that control humanity's vast resources interact in a worldwide scale - works.

                                                            So, yeah, I do think capitalism it's to blame. But this is a tricky subject, and you will see it if you bother to debate with any relatively smart libertarian - but I don't want to waste my time being the Devil's advocate here. Let's talk about the problem:

                                                            As I understand this, it' s a problem of logistics: There's always food, and the stuff you need to live, on the planet - somewhere. But sometimes it's winter where you are, plant's die, animals that eat plants die, and there's no food where you are - hence the scarcity. This is a problem that long predates Capitalism.

                                                            Now, our technological advancements allow us to have food all the year, everywhere, and in suchn quantities that it would be enough for everyone. But, of course, this is not God given, this costs energy - understood as fuel and also human resources. The System (Capitalism, understood as a global phenomena) manages this energy.
                                                            In our current global society, all this energy it's moved by the desire of individuals to get richer - hence the problem: It's never going to be proffitable to give stuff to the people in need, you take the bother to move all that energy and human resources to give the stuff to the people that can pay (which historically are always going to be the people that won wars - phisical, comercial or otherwise - in the past, and got all the loot and the rigths).

                                                            Now, I've higligthed the word techno - because in our world scarcity would not be possible to solve without technology- And , in fact, it's only imaginable to solve it trough it. Otherwise winter (to put an example - also plagues, drougths, etc. You know) it's always going to kill your food unless you live on one of those rare paradices of abundance where that never happens. The fact that hungers always existed should be proof enough that scarcity wasn't invented by Capitalism

                                                            In WoD the Technocracy made possible for us to imagine the defeat of scarcity, by employing human resources to trasport food and other basics to everyone. But what if other, mystical, Mage factions made it possible? - Energy & human resources are ultimately represented by Quintessence and Will/Arete. So, it would be disingenuous to think a mystical paradigm would't need to face it as a real problem - before solving it with magick. In order to solve this problem you need a way to administer this energy that's not based so much arround the desire of egoist material gain...

                                                            And, yeah, the Syndicate's Paradigm it's based arround the kind of egoism that justifies the way the system works - so maybe they're to blame for the demonstrated lack of capacity of the Technocracy to end scarcity (also they killed the only ones that wanted to do so). But they say this is a natural law, like an Eartly Foundation. People just works like that, and they have to work arround this fact.

                                                            This may be wrong, or even a lie - BUT I'm willing to entertain the posibility it is a Foundation because I think that the best way to demolish someone's argument forever it's if you can face them at their best. I say: Even if it was a Foundation, by believing that this human problem can't be solved you're weakening your own magick. It may be necessary to make some workarrounds to solve Scarcity, if it's such a thing, but you still need a firm Will to do whatever it's necessary to defeat it, if you want ever be able to - at least - make things better for most people. By defending the status quo, and the privileges of the elite, they're defending scarcity too. Even if it was a real problem.
