                  - A Garou who is embraced becomes the clan of its sire (or potentially Caitiff, as usual). He develops three dots in Disciplines appropriately, and inherits the clan Weakness.

                  - Technically he becomes ronin; the tribe and totem refuse to acknowledge him (Except as an enemy, of course.) This means he loses the powers of Pure Greed, probably loses his tribal weakness (maybe not?) and cannot gain rank or renown. The exception is if the Abomination was a Black Spiral Dancer, and even then they are fairly despised.

                  - The abomination can shapeshift to all five forms normally, and they function identically.

                  - The Abomination retains its vulnerability to silver, gains an allergy to sunlight, and can treat assorted other forms of damage as a vampire does (Bashing is halved, electrical damage is no problem, etc)

                  - Abominations cannot regenerate as Garou do. They can only "naturally" heal by spending Blood

                  - The abomination has a blood pool appropriate to his Generation, and may spend blood the same as any other vampire - increasing attributes, mimicking body functions, healing, powering Disciplines, etc.

                  - an Abomination retains its knowledge of all Gifts and rites it knew before the Embrace, and could theoretically teach them to other Garou. However, unless he entreats banes, his rites will not work for him. His Gifts are by default functional, though there may be exceptions - hard to imagine a silver Fang abomination would be able to use Wrath of Gaia, for instance.They cannot learn new Gifts, unless taught by banes or other Garou

                  - The Abomination retains his Rage score, and can use Rage points normally.Abominations do not have Self-Control scores, and for them, Frenzy works just like it does for other Garou (including difficulties being based on moon phases.) House rule - an Abomination's effective Self-Control score is equal to (10 - Rage) / 2

                  - The Abomination lacks a Courage trait as well, instead using Willpower in its place. No conversion here, werewolves are simply badass enough to have big ratings.

                  - An abomination has never been human - they do not have a Humanity score. Instead they utilize their Gnosis score as a morality trait, in addition to its regular uses. it functions as Humanity, determining torpor length, dice pools during the day, blood costs for appearing alive, etc.

                  - Abominations lack a Conscience trait. Instead when they commit a sin against gnosis, they roll gnosis at difficulty 7 (or higher, for egregious offenses). if they fail the roll, they lose a permanant point of gnosis. When a roll needs Conscience, utilize Gnosis - As with willpower, Garou are just cool enough to have big scores there.

                  - Upon the embrace, all fetishes attuned to the Garou are freed. The abomination may attempt to re-attune them to himself, but the difficulty is 10, and a botch strips away a permanent point of Gnosis. if his gnosis is 6 or higher, it is also a violation of Gnostic morality, and calls for a check.

                  - An abomination cannot spend experience to increase / regain lost Gnosis. Once it's gone, it's gone for good.

                  - Abominations exist in a state of permanant harano - this is a semi-supernatural state of depression, where a garou realizes - and embraces the futility of existence as a garou. A character suffering harano must make a Willpower roll (difficulty 7) every scene to function normally; failure means that all of his dice pools are halved for that scene. he may spend a point of Willpower to overcome this for a scene. Unlike other Garou, an Abomination has no hope of alleviating this condition.

                  - ...Except selling itself wholly and willingly to the Wyrm. In effect, this palces the Abomination wholly in the thrall of the Wyrm (or at least a very powerful Wyrm minion?) removing the burden of Harano, as well as preventing Gnosis degeneration.

                                      Instead of the rules for violating/losing/gaining Humanity, they follow the Garou Litany. Note that includes "Ye Shall Not Eat the Flesh of Humans." Depending on how strictly the ST interprets that, it could include every feeding from any human (or ghoul, or wolf, or werewolf).

                                      Instead of losing Humanity, they lose Gnosis.

                                      I'd rule that some Gifts stop working. Some Gifts don't work for fallen shapeshifters (like Mother's Touch). A quick-and-dirty rule of thumb might be anything with Gaia, Wyld, Luna, or a tribal totem in or referenced by the name.

                                      Can't spend Blood Points in the same turn as Rage or Gnosis.

                                      Permanent Harano only sets in at less than 7 permanent Gnosis.

                                      Once they hit 0 Gnosis, in addition to presumably becoming a Wight they also can't step sideways, use Gifts or fetishes.

                                      On the "Path" of being a slave to the Wyrm I'd guess they have to follow the Dark Litany (pre-W20 Black Spiral Dancer Litany) to avoid losing Gnosis. In any case it loses free will to disobey whatever the Wyrm demands.
