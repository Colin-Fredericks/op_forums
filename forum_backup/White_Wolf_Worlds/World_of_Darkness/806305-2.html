                            A novel which should be taken with a grain of salt. After all, it has a Garou who is also a Bubasti and can shift into both forms. And that same character can also change into a Khara... double whut?
                            Call to Battle and Conspicuous Consumption are some of the most head-scratching Werewolf novels out there if you know how things work in W:tA very well.
                            The author for Conspicuous Consumption even mixed up aspects of the Weaver for the Wyrm...

                            None of the events in those two novels were ever confirmed by W:tA Metaplot or RPG books (Whereas the events in the Silver Crown and Breathe Deeply novels are confirmed.).
                            So yeah, the NPC profile for Passer in Rage: Warriors of the Apocalypse is correct and he never died like he did in Conspicuous Consumption.

                            Secondly, the Progenitors don't view what they are creating as "monsters" and neither do the majority of Technocrats view them as doing such. If and that's a big "if", the Progenitors left or ever got kicked out of the Technocracy, then the Technocracy might view their creations as something they might have to hunt down.
