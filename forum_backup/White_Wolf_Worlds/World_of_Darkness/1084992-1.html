    In my games, the Fey, Chimera and everything related to the Dreaming are effected by the Mind-Sphere instead of Spirit. I use the same rules as for spirits, just swap the spheres. Effectively this also means Glamour is just a form of Quintessence, modyfied by the Dreaming, so you would need both Prime and Mind to manipulate it. Since the Dreaming is thought-stuff, it makes just much more sense this way.
    So to modify a Kinain or Kithain soul... that's another thing. For the soul you'll definetly need Spirit at Level 5, also Prime, but to affect a Kinain, you also need the hight levels of Mind and the expertise of affecting a pattern like this, so Life in this case (as in: you can only do with Spirit/Mind in the Umbra/Dreaming, what you can already do with Pattern-Spheres in the material world).

    Also, there's always an increase of difficulty, when handling prodigals/supernaturals. If they accept you trying to change them it doesn't matter, but if they resist add a +3 modifier to the difficulty or roll their glamor or willpower against the effect (resisted roll).

        (Just jumping in...)

        Previous writers-hitting-the-forums from about 15 years ago (plus the cross-over stuff given in the ST Handbook) suggested that Mind and Spirit were necessary for Fae manipulation (though not always, depending on specific effect; Life/Mind were suggested for a number of things). To "elevate", in a true/permanent manner, a Kinain into a Kithain (and assuming one is not wanting to use the guidelines for Arch-Spheres from the 90s), then Prime:5, Mind:5, Spirit:5 and Life:4 (assuming a physical manifestation is desired, which requires altering the target's physiology to a degree; if wholesale alteration is required, then Life:5) would be required, along with a shit-ton of successes, a given amount of Quintessence and/or (preferably) Tass with the appropriate resonance (such as would have a connection to both the Mundane World as well as to The Dreaming -- this is more likely to be found in places like the British Isles, and/or by making certain... sacrifices... of those who possess/carry Fae Blood, such as those with the appropriate Merit). How many successes/Q/T would be required depends on the setting/specific metaphysics in play for a given ST's world/time period. For the early 21st century, likely at least 50-100 successes (as you would be forging a new metaphysical essence, bonding it to a physical/living form, and doing so in an Age(tm) wherein such metaphysical shenanigans is nigh unto impossible (as it would be similar to creating a new type of vampire sans vitae, a new changing breed, or similar). If earlier (particularly at least during the Victorian Era, or even better during the pre-Renaissance), it's "easier" but things like Paradigm(tm) become something the ST should really, really be harder on than in current times. (Using the DA, official mages, likely only The Old Faith or some Valdaermen would have the knowledge/wisdom to know how to do something like this... and, as is often the case when poetic irony comes into play, they are the mages who would be least likely to *want* to perform such a feat as many of them have at least passing connections to many fae beings in the first place and wouldn't need to perform "x" in the first place.)

        And, of course, it goes without saying that even if executed properly this sort of magic(k) is highly Vulgar and likely to become subject to Disbelief over time, if not outright undone by Paradox spirits who determine this to be too offensive to Reality(tm) over time.

        Still, though, it *could* be done... it's just a question of "Is it worth the blood, sweat and likely tears to make it happen..."?

        I have been around here for waaaayyyy too fucking long...

            All of what John said. And that's just the mechanical aspects. The parts that have to be played out using Focus are going to be just as complex and convoluted.

            Considering DA Fae as a template, probably the easiest way to do it would be to use minimal amounts of Mage magic at all. "Changelings" (as an Origin) could be humans that were abducted by the fae and kept around long enough for their human soul to change, with the missing bits "filled in" with fae energies or whatnot. So, technically, it might just take the ability to go into the Deep Dreaming, find an appropriately powerful True Fae and leave the character there with said entity to marinate forever and a day (or no time at all... there may not be much difference). I mean, it's probably going to end up something like a Changeling the Lost character in many ways, including the whole experience breaking the new changeling in all sorts of interesting ways (and by "interesting" I mean "probably tragic" for those who knew him or her before the mage went off and sold their friend to the whims of the most darkly whimsical imaginable beings...)

            Then all it would take is the appropriate Spheres to get into the Deep Dreaming. Or just making friends with a changeling who can get you there, no mage magic needed at all.

            But that's a LOT easier than doing something than Tremere tried to do and he was taking more shortcuts (using already extant vampire blood) than just "making a new fae with magic" would entail.
