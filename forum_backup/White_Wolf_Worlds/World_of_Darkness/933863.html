    Universal Humanity

    07-19-2016, 10:27 AM
    I just wanted to throw out a few ideas about how to universalize Humanity across the supernatural types (I will add universal rules for Virtues later, as they are a little more complicated, but assume that every supernatural creatures that can develop Humanity have the Virtues of Conscience, Courage, and Self-Control and that their base Humanity is equal to Conscience plus Self-Control while their base Willpower is equal to Courage). The problem with adding Humanity is that no one other than vampires gain anything from following Humanity, so I have created a few rules to encourage other supernatural creatures to keep their Humanity. Of course, it is up to every ST to decide whether they want to add Humanity to their non-vampire chronicles.

    Humanity Benefits:

    Changelings: If temporary Banality is less than Humanity, a changeling will lose an additional point of temporary Banality every day, to a minimum of '0'. If temporary Glamour is less than Humanity, a changeling will gain an additional point of temporary Glamour per day, up to their permanent Glamour.

    Demons: Demons may not take Humanity.

    Hunters: Hunters may not take Humanity because the process of Imbuement replaces their Humanity with Conviction.

    Mages: If temporary Paradox is less than Humanity, a mage will lose an additional point of temporary Paradox every day, to a minimum of '0'. Mages reduce their maximum Humanity by their permanent Paradox and, if their maximum Humanity is ever reduced to '0', they are ejected into the Umbra.

    Mortals: It temporary Willpower is less than Humanity, a mortal will gain an additional point of Willpower every day, up to a maximum of permanent Willpower.

    Mummies: Mummies do not have Humanity because they have a superior form of Humanity.

    Shapeshifters: If temporary Gnosis is less than Humanity, a shapeshifter will gain an additional point of Gnosis every day, up to a maximum of permanent Gnosis. Shapeshifters do not check for losing Humanity when they commit necessary Sins on behalf of Gaia (no cannibalism, rape, torture, etc.).

    Wraiths: If temporary Angst is less than Humanity, a wraith will lose an additional point of temporary Angst every day, to a minimum of '0'. Wraiths reduce their maximum Humanity by their permanent Angst and, if their maximum Humanity is ever reduced to '0', they become Specters (when using Humanity, Wraiths automatically begin with a permanent Angst of 1, though they may gain five extra bonus points for each additional level of permanent Angst that they take during character creation, up to their permanent Willpower).

    Vampires: Eastern Vampires may not take Humanity. Western Vampire receive their normal benefits from Humanity (in my games, I allow vampires who mimic humans to ignore daylight penalties and sunlight damage, and I allow vampires who maintain Humanity 8+ to produce Dhampir children, but that is just me).

      It seems kind of silly to make Humanity "Universal," and then have four groups that don't use it.

      A lot of these bonuses make things far too easy on the "good" factions of their games. They'll have the most members with high Humanity, and these bonuses basically let them ignore some of the largest drawbacks they have to deal with. They have minimal reasons to engage in Humanity risky behavior, especially because they now gain so much for having high Humanity. Why is there even an Unseelie Court if being "bad" screws you so hard in comparison?

      The WtA caveat basically makes giving them Humanity pointless, since as long as their doing what the PCs are supposed to be going they can ignore the majority of the chart that would actually being interesting to struggle with.

      -------

      If you're going to go here, the best start is to not use VtM's systems as the starting point and use something that works better for a universal mechanic that can be tweaked for each game as needed.

                          One of the reasons for universal Humanity is that everyone, except for Demons, were once mortals. Mortals have Humanity, it is part of being a mortal, and the fact that vampires can be more human than mortals is one of the themes of Vampire the Masquerade. If we start from that position, supernatural creatures that lack Humanity are more alien to mortals than even a low Humanity vampires, so it makes sense to have Humanity (or some replacement for Humanity) for each supernatural entity, so they can avoid alienating human beings. With the Kuei-jin, it is the higher of their Dharma or their Hun. With Mummies, Balance is a superior form of Humanity. With Hunters, Conviction overwrites Humanity just as the Hunter Virtues overwrite Mortal Virtues. With Demon, they are demons, so they have no need for Humanity, but you could imagine that their Faith overwrites the Humanity of their host. The other supernaturals, however, do not have sufficient equivalents (neither Banality or Glamour are a replacement for Humanity, neither Gnosis or Rage are a replacement for Humanity, etc).

                          If you do not want to have crossovers with Vampire, you do not need to have Humanity in your games. However, like many of the people here, I have been playing White Wolf for over twenty years, and I would imagine that the majority of the people here have wanted better crossover rules. If I send Shih against a Hengeyokai group, the Hengeyokai group will probably appreciate that the reason why the Shih are not slaughtering their Kinfolk is because the Shih want to keep their Humanity. That is the advantage of Humanity is crossovers, it sets the guidelines that prevent one group from committing atrocities against another group and, when the rules are broken, there are consequences within the mechanics of the game as well as within the story of the game.
