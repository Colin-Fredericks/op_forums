    DD (which in the same section specifically mentions that Lucifer is trying to take down the Church), doesn't say that demons can't feed from Abrahamic faith, just that in the DA era faith is, in general, harder to harvest than it was in the past; requiring a larger amount of offerings from humans where one used to be enough. The closest it gets to pointing to who's responsible is it says that the longer things stray from the time of Adam and Eve where humans and the Heavenly Host walked side by side, the weaker humanity's power of faith has gotten because the divine is less and less absent in their lives. There is a line about faith in the Lord not being the same as faith in a demonic master, but that doesn't really change anything; non-Eartbound demons can siphon off of any faith by the regaining Resolve rules, and Earthbound are limited to gaining Resolve from getting people to do specific rituals performed for them which can be accomplished via deception and overwhelming mortals with former-Angel powers. Earthbound can't just sit around in a holy place of any religion or culture and sup on faith. The process of binding themselves into a vessel to resist the Abyss cuts them off from what free-roaming and human-possessing demons can take advantage of.

    The book also really don't jive with the idea that Abrahamic faiths in general are special because it calls out Europe as being special, while Africa and Asia have plenty of people of multiple branches of Abrahamic tradition (including a bunch of smaller groups besides the big three), but are apparently just as easy to get faith off of as polytheists and ancestor worshipers. Being a Coptic Christian in Egypt apparently doesn't help you against demons try to use you for food, but being a Moorish Muslim in Spain does.

    So... yeah... just being told "the books disagree with me," is a bit grating. I'm reading the books. It's hard for me to C&P directly from DD or Earthbound, but it feels like we're looking at completely different texts. While it's entirely possible the books have internal inconsistencies, in fact the absolutely do between DA and modern times, I really am not finding what passages you think are so conclusive.
