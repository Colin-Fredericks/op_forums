                                        It's a personal taste thing, so I respect your opinion. I just don't like how edgy has been thrown around a lot in the (admittedly many) topics discussing the interview. It's not edgy to say that, for example, it's much riskier to use Dementation in downtown Athens because half of the people residing there are already contemplating suicide.

                                        Or that walking through certain parts of Glasgow while wearing a suit (like the good Ventrue you are) will probably result in a stab, final death, and run scenario. You would regret celebrating Maggie's victory back in the 1980s, if you didn't truly believe that it's their fault they (or their parents, more likely) didn't adapt to a changing economy. How hard is it for a 40+ y.o. shipyard worker to go to uni and study Finance, after all?

                                        Urban heroism and escapism is all fine and well, and I enjoy the occasional break of writing up such a chronicle. But does it really sound like you guys need a metaplot for superheroes with fangs? Aren't those stories more about the characters anyway?
