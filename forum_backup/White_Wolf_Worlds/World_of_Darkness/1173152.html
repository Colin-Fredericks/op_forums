    Ratkin Uprising: Apocalypse Now

    01-03-2018, 11:35 PM
    So, the Ratkin are absurdly numerous these days compared to other changing breeds and supernaturals with nest of hundreds under major cities, with some nests approaching a thousand under the largest megacities like New York.

    Has anyone ever played a game where the Ratkin actually unleashed their plagues on humanity and rose up to paint the streets with the blood of the survivors (actual goal to sow chaos, massacre health care professionals, decapitate leadership and inhibit any coordinated response by human governments). How did it go?

    Simultaneous outbreak of Spanish Flu, Smallpox and Pneumonic Plague in cities across the world would likely kill at bare minimum a third of the world population and kill who knows how many else indirectly as the over worked health care system can't deal with normal issues. The economy would collapse, trade would grind to a halt, famine would break out over much of the world, secondary disease outbreaks would occur, anarchy and warfare would spread. And of course, a hundred thousand plus Ratkin rising up would inevitably reveal the existence of the supernatural to the mortal population.

    How would the Garou and other Changing Breeds respond to this?

    If a crossover with Mage, how would the Technocracy respond? If a crossover with Vampire, how would the Camarilla and the Sabbat react?

                                                          (If they're going on disease-spreading sprees, they're far more likely to be quiet about it until the incubation periods are up.)

                                                          I'm assuming that we're supposed to assume that all the other organizations exist? Hmph.

                                                          The Technocracy would not be able to keep up as easily as they're presented as, for the same reason that they can't react very well to anything in Werewolf, they're not operating with very open eyes when it comes to anything from the Umbra, and even when they do have DiSci, their assumptions make them Wrong Genre Savvy about what's going on. This is particularly true if the Ratkin start spreading diseases that rats don't usually spread, like Ebola. The Progenitors could probably patch a lot of flare-ups, but they'd be running in circles if they did, and that would entail diverting funding from their shady bioweapons research, as well as leave them overextended against the Traditions, so they probably wouldn't max out their efforts. By the time they realize that they should have and that the Traditions are small potatoes in this scenario, they won't have the capacity to manage it. If any part of the Technocracy survives, expect them to make an instructional film entitled "When Artificial Scarcity is Not Your Friend!"

                                                          Iteration X might not be affected by diseases to the same extent, but they do rely on infrastructure that Ratkin will probably chew through and set on fire. "They cut the power", and "That's inside the room!" ensue, with a side of bricking everything computerized. ItX does not do well against enemies that can look at computers and brick them; even countermagick is not likely to help, since the rats will be rolling about twice as many dice for their bricking powers at half the difficulty, likely while firing old surplus support weapons and AKs at their targets.

                                                          Also, Death Ray!

                                                          Kindred of all bents will likely find their hunting capacity compromised and end up killing their own to thin their herds, either directly or as sacrifices in suicide missions. Some vampires who were feeding indiscriminately might also be carriers of deadly diseases; those would probably be cut first, where they become known. Expect blackmail of this variety to be the hot new thing in Cam backstabbing circles.

                                                          The Beast Courts will probably be caught in a huge "What the hell Nezumi?" moment. That could potentially touch off another powder keg, assuming that the courts don't fine sufficient venting against everything else that tries to drag itself out of the shadows. Punishment of any instigators would probably have to focus on a few key conspirators as scapegoats, lest the most populous breed not be around to help with their court duties when everything starts going out of control. Suicide missions might be the atonement of choice, as getting together for the Rite of Hell Made Flesh is quite time-consuming, and as the Nezumi would likely point out, this does knock the Kumo down quite a few pegs, since they no longer have vast stores of blood to freely drink, much as with the vampires.

                                                          The Shih are quite hardy, and will likely survive. They probably won't particularly want to work with changers much, though. SF0 might not be so lucky, and in any case, much like ItX, their infrastructure dependence is going to limit their projection capacity, which, considering they're already overextended, means that a lot of them will probably die very quickly..
