    Originally posted by TwoDSix View Post

    Yeah, I've said before that I expect M5 to take heavy cues from Unknown Armies. Which wouldn't necessarily make for a bad game, but 1) people want to play Mage for the ideas Mage brings and 2) UA3 isn't that old, those of us interested in it's ideas likely own it.

    I really like comparing UA3 to WoD5, because both make an attempt to focus the games on street level play. But UA3 played heavily into existing elements of the game: it's occult organizations were already small, obsession was a constant theme from first edition, and power always had a price. While there's some setting elements that have been pushed aside there's still acknowledgement that they exist and I get a feeling of 'this will come back if a writer is interested enough to make it work. Meanwhile WoD5 feels like it's carving large chunks out of the setting because it didn't fit one person's views.

    I suspect M5 might have had trouble getting off the ground when they tried to make the Technocracy into TNI, and failed to realise why the two organizations vary so much, alongside with a difficulty in downplaying all the nonwestern cultural ties. Neither are issues that can't be solved via a hatchet, but I wouldn't be shocked if they're really struggling with groups like the Akashic Brotherhood and Dreamspeakers.

    But my money is still on M5 being next. Maybe C5 if they just decide to turn it into CtL.
    They could make the sidhe work like the Tzimisce, picking up random humans to magically mutilate!
