            Objective reality is a reality based on consistent (if unknown) principles that can be approximately described through a scientific method. It does not matter if the scientific method is animist or materialist in perspective as long as the results are reasonably accurate and reasonably valid. Subjective reality is reality without consistent principles.

            Objective perceptions are a hypothetical omniscient and omnipresent state where everything is known and understood (the perception of 'God'). While it is a useful hypothetical construct for explaining an objective reality, it does not exist at the human-level. Subjective perceptions are perceptions that are bounded by innate limitations. Humans have subjective perceptions because our sensory organs evolved to sense specific forms of information and each of our brains have finite capabilities related to analyzing, extrapolating, forecasting, interpreting, and storing sensory information.

            In effect, humans have two major limitations when it comes to perception. We use instruments to attempt to attempt to circumvent our biological limitations (with a limited amount of success) and the scientific method to attempt to circumvent our cognitive limitations (once again, with a limited amount of success).

            Mages gain an extra nine senses with their Avatars (the Nine Spheres) but do not gain improved cognition, which is why they suffer from the same subjective perceptions as normal humans. While they have circumvented some of their biological limitations, they have overwhelmed their cognitive limitations, and the Spheres also make them vulnerable to the Consensus. In effect, Mages gain more information, which allows them to manipulate reality, but they do not gain better information.

                                                            Human beings belong to one species (with limited genetic diversity), which means that every individual basically shares 99.9% of their genes with every other person. We evolved to create societies, to support each other and to take care of each other, and violations against other human beings are violations against our nature. We have adaptability and exploratory behavior, they are the biological principles that create free will, so we can choose to act against our nature, but we pervert and pollute what we are when we do.

                                                            Societies exist to regulate human behavior in groups larger than our evolution makes us comfortable with, but societies can twist and wrap humans because humans can rationalize exploitative behavior for their own benefit because not enough people recognize their behavior as wrong (evenyually, an entire society can go wrong). Societies that do not stamp down on exploitative behavior rot from the inside becomes the exploitative behavior becomes a cancer where every human being rationalized inhuman actions because every other human being is acting in an inhuman fashion.

                                                            Thus, humans are biological evolved towards compassion and equality, but socialization shapes us from the beginning away from our innocent natures. Humans learn to be human from other humans, so exposure to 'good' humans will likely result in growing up to be 'good' humans and exposure to 'bad' humans will likely result in growing up to be a 'bad' human. Of course, we socialize with many people when we are children, either directly through communication and proximity or indirectly through books, internet, radio, tv, etc. Free will, which is a biological phenomenon, not a spiritual phenomenon, allows us to either return to the goodness of human evolution or devolve to the wickedness of exploitative behavior.

                                                            Of course, this is a skeleton explanation because we have limited space. The basic concepts expressed are grounded in biological evolution and human psychology. We have evolved with one universal ethics, which free will and socialization can pervert, which is based on our evolution and which is dependent on the groups that we were evolved to encounter and on us deciding to do the right thing. Deviation from the universal ethics may benefit the individual but it harms the group, so groups have the right and responsibility to correct the exploitative behavior.
