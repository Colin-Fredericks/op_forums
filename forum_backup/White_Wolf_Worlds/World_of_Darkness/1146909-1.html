            It makes some sense for sorcerers to have more difficulty awakening. A lot of awakenings are essentially the explosion of a pent-up feeling of "there must be more to this". A sorcerer has an answer, and a strict path to faux enlightenment. It's a bit of a trap. Of course, a paradigm that, in it's apex, has actual awakening (see Hermetic apprentices post theurgy) would save the sorcerer from this, if they had any potential to awaken in the first place. However, keep in mind that sorcerers are the only people who can actually take a merit and awaken - as in, the only ones who can sort of choose to - it's just that their illusions are more elaborate, so most think they have already seen beyond the veil so to speak. Hell, a lot of mages can't tell sorcerers apart from the true awakened.
