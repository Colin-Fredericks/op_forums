                            My post disappeared.
                            Or maybe I imagined posting it.
                            Or maybe it was moderated into oblivion without warning.
                            Maybe it was the bit about my theory on 'exoticization' not being inherently bad and actually being a component of creative process, due to a lot of stories being spurned by such kind of dreams. To be clear I was talking about CtD.
                            Or it was the bit on the flat-earthers, conspiracies and MtA.
                            Or it was something else that I don't remember.

                            Anyway, to make it shorter than before:

                            1) Aside from the Traditions depiction, MtA can be seen as problematic because the premise of the game can be perceived as a whole big conspiracy theory being true. I won't try to hint again at why it can be problematic in our times. But if we take that from MtA, we basically take away the whole point of it.

                            2) Changeling is too hard to sell.
                            Either it's too hopeful and dreamy, and so doesn't fit WOD5 which goes hard on the dark and gloom and brooding. Or it can become the truly more depressing one, because it sort of forces roleplayers to look at themselves and how they see and live their whole 'imaginary worlds' passion.
                            Also it's a 'high concept' game. But to be 'high concept', you have to make hard statements about things.
                            For example to conside going to your mundane job every day a total drag, an absolutely Banal thing, and by Changeling POV basically a torture.
                            It is not a mature thing to say, it is not necessarily the opinion of the writers of the game, but it is part of the point.
                            If you make hard statements someone can be offended, there is no way around it.
                            I don't think it's going to happen unless they discard the whole concept and do their own thing. Changeling the Lost was a good game, so I'd suggest to take a similar route and make their own thing, instead of sullying someone else's creation.

                            3) Wraith is probably the easier one. While it's not really a game of "Street Level True Art Personal Horror", or at least it's not fully committed to it, much like Vampire it can be taken partially as it is and made like that just like V5. The transition is easier. Because while there are multidimensional overarching or world ending plots like in VtM, there is also the niche of "anarchs against the Man at street level and we do our own thing in our turf" side of things. It is indeed the more similar to VtM, because in the other three games the world-scale concept and theme is too much part of the game/setting itself.

                            I'll add that I wouldn't mind a Mummy game mixing stuff between the old mummies and Mummy the Curse to make their own thing, with something like Doom Dice that makes you go wrath of the gods mode.
