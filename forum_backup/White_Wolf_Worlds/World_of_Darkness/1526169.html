                  I feel like Promethean is strong enough, and they really did a frankenstein game, that it could be its own game line. Also people arguing endlessly about if Mages and Vampires could make them is very WoD.

                  I’m not sure much would change though. Promethean’s themes and mood fits very nicely into the WoD as is.

                  I think Deviant fits better into Mage or a generic WoD crossover book. The focus is people taken by shadowy organizations and transformed via magic or science who are trying to understand what happened to them and get revenge. That fits so nicely into a not quite neutral faction in the ascension war, a rogue element punishing either side for using normal people as weapons while not fully understanding or caring about the bigger picture. The loyalist supplement would be rules for playing someone transformed that are fine with it, giving people who don’t want to play a mage or whatever options to join those games.

                  The core themes of Deviant would probably be the same, made a bit more edgy since you’re a monster driven by revenge alienating people around you. Since CoD didn’t have a ton of globe spanning conspiracies secretly pulling the strings of humanity Deviant had a ton of rules for building unique conspiracies that were creating deviants. I feel like that would go out the door in WoD and deviants would be tied to specific existing lore. If you made Deviant a book that wasn’t tied to a specific game line then it would make the most sense to use the antagonist factions from each game as the source. Pentex (and maybe a new Weaver faction), Technocratic conventions, and the Sabbat (via flesh crafting).
