                                              The Albigensian Crusade, even if it is medieval. I mean, I know that it may seems strange, but at that time, Languedoc (the South of France) had a very distinct culture. I could totally picture the Vampires here being a lot more open-minded that the Frankish's ones, with the Kindred of the South using troubadours to communicate with one another, through coded poems and songs, accepting "heresies" more easily (the Jews were better treated here, before the creation of the very first Inquisition). Overall, the fall of a more open-minded society of Kindred (and possibly Werewolves) to the hands of Frankish Vampires and Pures could be interesting.

                                              Or the colonization of Indochina by the French. Here, we could have the various mages/Vampires/Werewolves etc. coming to term with very different civilizations with an history of resisting oppression by a powerful nation (China). Something about the War of Secession could also be quite interesting.
