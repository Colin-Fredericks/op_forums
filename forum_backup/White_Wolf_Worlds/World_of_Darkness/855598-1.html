    2Luna's armour
    2Troll skin (GF)
    2Steel fur (GW) (also note that having cockroach makes the roll really easy. from 7 to 5)

    There's also steel blowfish, which is Steel fur but your overall soak tripples and you can't do anything. You're just a metal ball of fur.

    Also you can increase your stamina.
    4 Hero's stand. (willpower difficulty 8, 1 in EACH physical per success) also nobody get's back attack on you, but you stand still.

    There's also a bunch of really cool fera ones.
    Khan- skin of Jade- spend a willpower, two soak. Instant armour.
    The Rokea had a few cool things. They had trollskin, but also a bunch of other armour related ones.

    In revised, the Fianna had this fun one where they'd have each alternating hit that wasn't silver heal them rather than hurt them.

    There's also a gift that gives you two soak dice against manufactured weapons not made of silver (IE guns) but it's not really worth it.

                                                            Personally. I have run several games where characters have had Luna's Armour + Another Soak gift, mostly because the Pack had Flea as a Totem who grants Luna's Armour. The experience was that it made the Garou considerably harder to hurt, which meant that they manages to go on quests into parts of Malfeas and over very hostile umbral locations where the environment itself causes turn by turn damage. Without this ability the games wouldn't have gone to those places as surviving for a minute or so would have been more or less impossible (other pack members had to use other tricks to achieve the same ends). Challenging the pack was never an issue though, they just got to face bigger physical threats and ever greater moral and spiritual ones. Plus anything they could do a BSD could do too (oh how they HATED Mr Morden, the reoccurring SilverSpiral Ragabash).

                                                            Personally I have had a single character with multiple soak gifts that combined got my soak stat in Crinos to somewhere over 20, which came in really handy as a Rank 4 Glasswalker hero facing down a Zmei type Weaver/Wyrm beastie while trying to release Turtle from within the corrupted and calcified pits of the Pattern Web.
