        Originally posted by Penelope View Post
        Quick question: the Church of Caine is basically just a modern extension of the Cainite Heresy from Dark Ages, right?
        yeah, but instead of "modern" extension, I'd say "fallen" or "broken" extension,

        the Cainite heresy saw themselves as gods/angels on earth,
        the sabbat see themselves as damned creatures

        you could say the sabbat is the Cainite heresy admitting that they were wrong after their spectacular failure during the Inquisition

        at least, that's the big picture

        if we're going into details, the Path of Caine is supposed to have had influence from the Cainite heresy,
        the path supposedly was started by Assamite Noddists who allied with Cainite Heresy remnants,
        the path of Caine is the most influential in the sect, since sabbat aren't required to be on the path to heed the word of caine (you can be on humanity, and still follow your noddist priest's words)

        however, while the Heresy lives on in the sabbat (to some extent), so do their detractors,
        the path of Redemption is the broken legacy of the Road of Heaven (which was the main enemy of the Cainite Heresy during the Dark Ages),
        the path of Cathari is supposed to be born of a heretical sect of the Cainite Heresy (a heresy of a heresy), which has now become one of the main paths in the sabbat

        so, yeah...I'm not sure how much we can really say that sabbat is a true extension of the Cainite heresy

                                                    When I see Anarchs I just ask this question: What are they really in the game for? Why are they a thing?
                                                    Because the game is about resisting being a slave to the Elders, to the people who want to control or destroy you. It's about freedom and slavery. Unless a PC is a toady or a kiss up, they're almost certainly Anarchs by default by resisting.

                                                    Sabbat and Camarilla are enemies for supernaturally purposes that fit into the theme of the game.

The Anarchs also make the Sabbat's claims of freedom and liberty look like a joke, which also makes the Sabbat more interesting.

It just seems illogical to me they'd make the Anarchs one of the new central sects when that was never the case before. Anarchs were the less extreme portion of the Sabbat that split off.
