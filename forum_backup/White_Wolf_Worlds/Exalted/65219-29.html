            Was saving this for something, but I'm kind of burning out, so I might as well share.

            The mystery of the Scarlet Empress' aspect is neither the result of any magical accident nor deliberate obscurement.

            During the late days of the Shogunate, the Empress' mother was a Wood Aspect of impeccable breeding and who had a passion for adventure. While on an expedition, she found herself hunted by a terrible silver-haired beast. When she turned the tables and captured the beast instead, it took the form of a man. Enthralled by each other's beauty and overcome with the rush of the hunt, they lay together. The one-day Empress was born with porcelain skin and silver hair like her father, though she had her mother's keen emerald eyes.

            Her mother had doubts that the child would Exalt. Certainly her own blood was strong, but the child's father lacked the blessing of the Dragons. Come Ascending Wood of the fifteenth year, the girl was nevertheless touched by Sextes Jylis as her mother had been. Yet by Descending Wood, something had gone terribly wrong - the girl's powers were rapidly waning. Imagine both their surprise when she threw a tantrum at the beginning of Ascending Fire and indeed, her anima came alive with the breath of Hesiesh.

            For fear of her peers, the eventual Empress concealed the Moon-curse upon her Exaltation. Only the sworn brotherhood with whom she entered the Palace of the Anathema were privy to it. Yet it was more terrible than even she knew. As her companions fell one-by-one to the manse's terrible traps, she was compelled to devour them, body and soul, until only she remained, thoroughly drenched in their blood. The stains have never washed out, and their own, pure, blood has given the Empress the unique ability to control her curse and change her aspect at will.

                                            I like the replacement Infernal animas that some other folks (I forgot who) came up with on the old forum. I modified them slightly and saved my version:


                                            Slayer caste mark: A burning green sunburst, but unlike the Dawn mark. Instead of a circle with little rays, it’s all rays from a tiny pinpoint center. Maybe with a circle around the outside, holding it caged. Or maybe four of the rays are longer than the others, making it rhombus-shaped.
                                            Slayer anima: Green flames. Objects within take on a verdigrisy-brassy sheen. People nearby have difficulty looking directly at the Slayer (blinded by glory or greenish sunfire, or it’s painful to look at, whatever).

                                            Malefactor caste mark: A stylized hourglass in a circle, not too detailed, bright glowing azure, painful for 1st circle demons to gaze at.
                                            Malefactor anima: Swirl of silvery sand. Objects within grow dry and cracked, as if they had been left in a hot, waterless environment for a long time. The Malefactor seems... not taller or more glorious, but more important than the viewer, and the viewer feels inconsequential and empty.

                                            Defiler caste mark: A flickering, symmetrical, heatless, white flame burning from the center of a white circle. It could have a few smaller flaming circles arranged around it. They could even start orbiting around it on the Defiler’s forehead when they hit higher mote numbers.
                                            Defiler anima: Pure white flames, with a faint outline of a silver sphere around it. Objects within rearrange and warp subtly, shifting into subtle mathematical patterns and losing flaws and imperfections. The Defiler seems always at the center of some great pattern, even if the pattern can neither be seen nor glimpsed.

                                            Scourge caste mark: A scarlet swirl over a vaguely circular/square area, made of swirly bits which are perpetually in motion from the moment it becomes visible to the moment it fades away. It doesn't swirl in a repetitive pattern. It’s never the same exact shape two seconds in a row, and never repeats itself. It’s never truly circular.
                                            Scourge anima: Reddish wind, whipping about the Scourge. Objects within shift and warp subtly, giving an overall impression of being carelessly made, as if unimportant. The Scourge seems to always have mortal viewers’ best interests at heart -- often clashing horribly and disturbingly with what they’re actually doing.

                                            Fiend caste mark: Ebon Dragon as an ouroboros. It doesn’t look like a black mark on their forehead, it looks a Dragon-shaped empty hole into lightless nothing where their forehead ought to be. The space inside the circular Dragon is in shade that grows slightly deeper as the Fiend’s anima flares, without becoming dark enough to mistake it for the Midnight mark.
                                            Fiend anima: The Fiend’s shadow grows up from the ground to surround them like a shroud. Objects within grow darker, lose color, and no longer cast shadows. The Fiend seems to be the antithesis of all things with which they interact, regardless of what they look like or do.

                                            She/Her. I am literal-minded and write literally. If I don't say something explicitly, please never assume I implied it. The only exception is if I try to make a joke.
                                            My point of view may be different from yours but is equally valid.
