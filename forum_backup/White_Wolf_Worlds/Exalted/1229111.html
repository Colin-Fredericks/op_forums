                  An axe...on a rope, so you can swing it while you swing it. Or an artifact one on a bladed soulsteel chain, name it Silent Fall and give it evocations based around causing battle groups to fall without making a sound.

                  Orichalcum helmet whose evocations are entirely based around headbutting your enemies.

                  Moonsilver dentures that shapeshifting to give you a terrifying bite attack, and have evocations built on biting off limbs and parrying blades by catching them in your teeth and then either disarming it breaking them.

                  Armor made of white Jade, causes people who attack you to become gravitationally bonded to you so you can body slam them into things. Capstone evocation called Emerald Prince's Duty let's you roll up armies Katamari style.

                  A volleyball pole, complete with rope and a ball. For smiting, tripping, unexpected attacks from behind, and just tying people to the pole. Artifact version is made of Starmetal and rapidly ages or de-ages opponents based on whether you're spinning it clockwise or counterclockwise.

                  Artifact boots that just make you jump real high...but all their combat evocations are built around coming back down from that height and punching folks from above with meteoric momentum.

                  Magic eyepatch made from Malfean brass. Solipsistic magic makes it so you can't be hurt from the side you can't see, screwing over enemies who try to take advantage of your blind side. Mechanically just call it "wielding an artifact shield", and give it some evocations for lighting nearby people on green fire if they beat your awareness with their stealth because you not noticing them means they must not be real and so it starts burning them out of reality.

                  Fancy twirly skirt with Moonsilver blades woven into the hem. Evocations for dancing and dance fighting, twirling and sashaying across the battlefield like an exquisitely choreographed blender.

                  A goremaul-harpoon. You can smite with it...or throw it and impale the enemy with a barbed pole that's got nine hundred pounds of blue Jade on one end, and evocations based on hindering mobility or dragging a foe (or a ship?) to the bottom of the sea like an anchor.

                  Glass eye with orichalcum Iris. You can shoot it out of your head to smite people at range (artifact sling/sling bullet stats), and blind them with sunburst flares and purple after images. Evocation tree lets you use Awareness to see through it at a distance and attack with it once it's out. A bit like that whistle-arrow from Guardians of the Galaxy, only grosser.

                  You know those nutcracker implements they give you to crack shells with when you order crab at a seafood place? That, but six feet long. Uses seven section staff stats, evocations for breaking enemy armor or deforming it to reduce mobility.

                  So I'm making God-Kicking Boot, an Exalted webcomic, now. Updates on Sundays. Full-color, mediocre but slowly improving art. It's a thing.

                  The absence of a monument can, in its own way, be something of a monument also.                   -Roger Zelazny
