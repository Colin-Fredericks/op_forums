                        Introduction: A small circle of three Lunars serving MaHa is his wars against eastern powers. They have all the tools they need to wage guerrilla warfare, collect information of enemy movements, infiltrate cities, gather spirit allies and so on.

                        Full Moon: [Stam & Charisma]


                        Basic Idea: The face of the Circle (though the New Moon is a good wing man) – the Full Moon keeps the New Moon alive, his troops alive and can act as Yojimbo. While his charms are focused on being a sponge – his abilities are focused on being a consummate statesman and keeping the camp fed and in line.
                        Charms:
                        Hard Mode: No DBT


                        Stamina Excel (second)
                        Armor Forming Technique – Damage Mitigation
                        Limb Shielding Growth – Damage Mitigation
                        Mother Grizzly Attitude – Hard lock against spell disruption
                        Bruise Relief – Speed Bump
                        Halting the Scarlet Flow – Bodyguard for days
                        Snarling Watchdog Retribution – Mostly to fuel Scarlet Flow in absence of DBT.
                        Charisma Excel (First)
                        Maintaining the Pack – Ablative armor regeneration

                        New Moon: [Charisma & Intelligence]


                        Basic Idea: The New Moon is tied to the Full Moon very closely in this circle. He trains the men (lol, no, animals mostly) and even recruits them. He is able to set the Changing moon up with a lot of opportunities to ply his trade with tricks of the trade – acting as his wing man. Without the New Moon the other two would be not even half as effective. This guy is a force multiplier on so many levels.

                        Synergy: The lack of Beast Form on the Full Moon in order to allow defensive actions such as body guarding lends itself to the need for a respite for powering up.

                        Charms:


                        Charisma Excel (First)
                        Dog-Tongue Method – Mostly a speed bump charm to the impressive animal training charm. Animal intelligence being what it is a ST can easily make this charm worthless or godly (ex: cephalopods have terrible memory and forget what they were just doing after only a few seconds - making them worthless for the purpose of gathering information) further many ST’s do not know much about animal behavior.
                        Nature-Reinforcing Allocation – Fixes the above issue of animal intelligence and opens up the tool box that is the mutation system. Given allies, friendships and familiars – this can provide the Circle a great deal of flexible power in terms of: brute strength, spy network, rudimentary construction and resource gathering.
                        Wolf Pack Training Technique - Training the elites
                        Cobra Hypnotic Method – Social surgical strike to remove influence of enemy factions or simply roadblocks – also allows you to set up deathblows for the other two in the Circle.
                        Perfect Fear Scent – Combos with Wasp Dance nicely and could be used as a moral hit in mass battle or as part of a terror campaign (in or out of your own community – control) therefore hitting Social/Martial and Social all at once.
                        Terrestrial Circle Sorcery:
                        Impenetrable Frost Barrier – Suppress Withering Fire
                        Impervious Sphere of Water – Everything the Full Moon needs to power up.
                        Commanding the Beasts – Turn any battle into a mass battle, send a tide of rats and alley toms to disrupt a chase, used to build long term army and play off Nature Reinforcing or even steal a town blind (with intelligent rat thieves).

                        Changing Moon or Full Moon: [Dex, Stam]


                        Basic Idea: This Lunar is the eyes of the circle and ghosts into locations to kidnap or spy. He is also counter ops, being able to sniff out enemy spies and even spirits. Finally the Lunar is the hammer to this ‘Anvil and Hammer’ Circle - sneaking about the edges of the combat lobbing stones with explosive power. Between supernal smell and the ability to see immaterial – nothing should sneak up on them and mundane closing actions are foolish against the Lunar’s speed. Further, having only essence 2 allows for a lot of room to spend BP on artifacts/contacts/charms and so on.
                        Charms:


                        Perception Excel (Second)
                        Inevitable Spoor Discovery – Most stealth charms seem to diminish sound and sight but leaves smell wide open. This charm is useful for sweeping the perimeter of an armed camp, smelling out the guard rotation of enemy camps, faultless tracking and other cleverness. Being able to identify beasts also allows one to locate potential targets for the New Moon to bind pact with.
                        Eagle Eye Advantage – While the New Moon and Full Moon act as the anvil and herd in mass combat – this charm allows the stealthy Changing Moon to murder face hard.
                        Wolf Eye Advantage – Shrugging off drugs (dulled senses) is useful for a changing moon – like the Vizzini scene from Princess Bride. Seeing through fog and foliage is a great boon to the above.
                        Eye of the Cat – See what you’re really getting into. Attacking a sorcerer with a score of dematerialized Demons is a sad way to die. And yes, ST’s should have sorcerers be intelligent and kill you that way.
                        Dex Excel (First)
                        Instinct – Driven Beast Movement – Kite and find new sniper nests
                        Impressions of Strength(Ogre’s Loving Caress) – Artifact sling with piercing and high strength will roll tons of dice.
                        Shell-Crushing Atemi – Artifact Sling picks up piercing (welcome to exit wounds)
                        DBT and tons of camouflage mutations…cause I’ve never seen a Lunar stealth charm (like one you could use in combat) outside of customs?

It's also a lot more interesting to play than a wailing silver Soulshitter. Works best when outshining the munchkin's combat monkey and then leaving enemies alive just to torment the munchkin.

                                    Compassion ●●, Conviction ●●●, Temperance ●●, Valor ●●, Essence ●●●, Willpower ●●●●●●

                                    Personal/Peripheral/Committed/Respire: 20/38/0/2

                                    Backgrounds: Artifact (hearthstone) ●, Tattoo Artifact (hearthstone bracers) ●●, Contacts (typically spirits in local courts) ●●, Manse ●●

                                    Knacks: Deadly Beastman Transformation

                                    Charms: First Strength Ex, Second Dex Ex, First Perception Ex, Eye of the Cat, Devil-Restraining Grip, God-Cutting Essence, Righteous Lion Defense

                                    Whys and whyfors:
                                    The wandering exorcist who protects mortals from the ravages of unscrupulous gods and demons. You are a specialist in fighting those which evade the notice of mortals. From this point forward your progression moves very much like a Full Moon's, although you'll obviously be slower to achieve your ultimate martial prowess than the Full Moon, but the trade-off is obvious. This is the kind of character that a campaign has to work around, of course, which means you probably need to make sure this is going to be okay with your Storyteller and warn the other players that a significant amount of encounters will be with things they can't hurt through normal means. At least until their exorcist gets a hand on them and drags them kicking and screaming into material form, anyway.



                                    So you've probably picked up on a few recurring themes in chargen. I'll address all of them here:

                                    Why Athletics, Awareness, Dodge, Integrity, and Martial Arts for every character? Also, why is Martial Arts always favored? Because these are your basic "being Exalted and not immediately dying" Abilities. You need to them to get 2-die stunts consistently and not get wrecked in combat, physical or social. Martial Arts is always favored because Martial Arts are such a huge Charm investment that not making them favored using the standard rules is like shooting yourself in the face to avoid shooting yourself in the foot later. Also Martial Arts controls using your natural weapons in your non-human forms, should you have any, which makes it a good investment period.

                                    You don't pick up Perfect Defenses. Why? Because in 2.5e if your Storyteller expects you to be able to survive paranoia combat out of chargen, you should be looking for another game, not building a character whose only trick is Flowing Body Evasion and the defense-enhancement Dexterity Charms on the way there. Honestly, it shouldn't even be on your "want" radar until around E4 when you're likely to face opponents with perfect attacks. Particularly as a Lunar the best investment is probably soak Charms/armor and Ox-Bodies instead of the entire Dex dodge tree.

                                    You're deep in Willpower investment out of the gate. Why? Because Lunars need to use Willpower to be able to get more than 5 dice enhancing their actions. Stupid but true.

                                    Dex 5 and Second Dex Excellency? Spend four motes to get DV buff. A no-brainer since you'll stunt those motes right back.

                                    You sure like Manses. Why? Because not taking Manse 2 and a hearthstone at chargen is hard mode. Lunars are premised on the idea that they have lairs to crawl back to. Have a lair. Get free motes.

                                    How can you have a Lunar with no Heart's Blood!? Easy. Don't worry about shape-shifting except to DBT because you probably won't. If you do, it won't be for long since you can't maintain a non-native form while expending motes. Crappy answer, isn't it? Welcome to Lunars.


                                    If I didn't address any questions, feel free to ask.

I always start character gen by drawing a picture and then deciding what sorta character the person in my doodle is, so that's what the links on the names are (hey, it looks like you're looking at people's chargen processes, so I'm sharing my whole process).                                                     Nergui: Changing Moon (Changing Moon, Sea Leopard Totem)

This guy's a Strong Silent Type, which is a bit odd for a Changing Moon, but that's why I took Glance Oration Technique. Nergui (not his real name-it means "No Name," for he is a broody exile) loves his loyal dog, fishing, and watching the night sky. He's a high-compassion high-temperance type who's got a soft spot for women and children that gets him into lots of trouble, because he lives on a boat and he always ends up trying to solve the problems of people he meets on shore before the Gosling ships out. If playing Nergui, I want to keep in mind the things that attracted me to the Sea Leopard as his Totem: he's a deadly lone hunter who's all about endurance and stamina rather than grace, he's playful even in combat, and he's got Selkie mojo which means he's always accidentally falling into tragic romance and then leaving for the sea. He's not a roguish chatterbox like so many Changing Moons, he's just smooth and sincere and easy to fall in love with.

                                                    Role In The Circle: Nergui puts on false faces, using his Anima and Hide of the Cunning Hunter to act as an information-gatherer in new ports. Sometimes he flies ahead of the ship and determines what goods a town is in desperate need of so he can come back and tell Tu Nung what cargo to forge. He tends to grease the palms and deal with authorities when needed, because he smells friendly and he's the most physically attractive person on board. In ship-to-ship combat he's decent enough with his wavecleaver and his huge pet dog does a good job tripping up the enemy and ripping out jugulars. He's a bit of a tank: slow and solid and possessed of a strength Excellency, he uses Butterfly Eyes Defense and Glance Oration Technique in combat to try to scare powerful foes into thinking they're dealing with someone much more skilled than he actually is. He'll occasionally turn into his seal-form to smash into a ship from underneath. Usually, though, he bribes a flock of gulls to patrol the waters for miles around the ship and let him know if there's any other ships out there. They come in handy for avoiding the Realm navy and spotting fat merchant ships. Also?
