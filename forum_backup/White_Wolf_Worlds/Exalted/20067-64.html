                                        09-21-2015, 09:20 AM
                                        Your anima is an invisible aura that surrounds you. When you spend Peripheral Essence, it begins to fill with light, first with your caste mark glowing, then a halo of appropriate-colored light wreathing you, followed by a display that can be seen for miles with a feature iconic of your character (a swan for Swan, occult diagrams for Arianna, being crowned by rainbow thunderbirds for the Storm King).

                                        Depending on which caste and type of Exalt you are, your anima can trigger a number of effects (your "anima banner"): Dawn Caste Solars can appear fierce and terrifying; Eclipse Caste Solars specifically trigger ancient pacts made at the dawn of history that grant you diplomatic immunity in spirit courts, can sanctify oaths with the authority of Heaven, and can learn Charms from other splats.

                                        Fiends, by dint of their status as discount tainted Eclipses (no, but seriously, sometimes they come across like those counterfeit Yu-Gi-Oh! cards made just convincing enough for your dad to buy three boxes for your birthday; Fiends even use the Midnight Caste Abyssal caste mark!), get a similar anima effect, except they can also suspend magical oaths from beings of lower Essence (meaning they can't suspend their own oaths), but lose the ability to learn Sidereal Martial Arts specifically.
