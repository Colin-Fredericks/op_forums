    Lunars who ride

    05-05-2015, 04:47 AM
    What is a Marukani master horseman supposed to do when Luna Exalts him?

    Supposing that his totem is, quite reasonably, a horse, his riding and mounted archery skills are useless in his spirit shape (or in any other animal form).

    If he uses Deadly Beastman Transformation, his horse is now burdened by a much larger, heavier rider. Also, it's a horse riding a horse. Horseption.


    For cases like this, it might be cool if a Lunar could temporarily lock out his shapeshifting ability by splitting his spirit shape off to act as a familiar. So he can ride his own bestial soul into battle.

    formerly Tornado Wolf, formerly Inugami

                      Speaking purely for myself, I would be amenable to thinking of a horse-based war form that is still quadrupedal, while still not having the whole "human torso at the top/end of an animal" thing.

                      I mean, barring some very interesting carvings taken from the Parthenon*, centaurs and their derivatives just look distractingly goofy to me. And the idea of a person shapeshifting into one... it would strike me as less "impressive and fearsome transformation into battle form" and more "regular person who grows a horse out of their behind".

                      I mean, with some exceptions; a serpentine body feels like a more organic transition to me, because it's a lot less busy and skewed, and follows the lines suggestive of legs that have blended together.

                      I mean, the asymmetry of a centaur alone...

                      * And that's mostly for the sake of my admiration for how much thought and creativity the sculptors obviously put in to imagining what a centaur might look like in a battle, how its muscles might have connected and contorted in the course of such exertions, as well as the attention to detail in how the still poses look like the transitions in dynamic and realistic fighting.

                                  Just gonna drop in my contractually obligated blob of EX3 What We Know stuff. It makes up most of the Lunar Charms subsection, at the moment.

                                  Originally posted by EX3 What We Know Wiki
                                  Q: (ChrisARose)
                                  Will Lunar's have anything like a ride charm or a beast-master charm - or is this not really a focus for Lunars?

                                  A: (John Mørke)
                                  Taming Charms? I can dig it.
                                  Riding Charms? Only if you can come up with awesome mounted Lunar ideas.

                                  Q: (ChrisARose)
                                  So like a No-Moon with something like Sleipnir - a blazing pineal eye swirling with the spark of the Wyld within - showing paths unseen. They plod past war torn fields, a living soul never gracing their presence. Following not land mark or star - but the undulating song of what is to come - the after images of dreams so vivid they have subsumed reality.

                                  A Full-Moon with a demonic beast that shifts and changes. Even as a sword pierces its hide, a pulse of flesh, and a chitinous plate erupts to the surface forcing the blade away. Awakening the spark of Chthonic Baara is dangerous, it can never be coaxed to sleep again, always hungry and ever changing. It is troublesome finding new beings for it to consume - to sate its need to bring all the scattered parts of itself back together...one beast at a time.

                                  Or the Changing Moon who's never truly alone. The voices howl and coo in her head even as the moonsilver tattoos show those outside a stable form. A thought, a break in the skin, and they can come rushing out - a Kirin to bear her away from a timber fire or some lesser beast to give warm and comfort on a cold stormy night. Sometimes she visits them in her dreams - for they are of her blood.

                                  When I hunt, Strig is always at my side. Luna watches us, stalking the prey by her light alone. I can feel its heart beating - the heat rising from it body. It is over quickly - ritual is a clean thing. I bite down upon the leathery heart and look up to my brother...he's already cracked the skull like a nut and devoured the meat within. Even as the blood of the hunted now courses in my veins - the light in his eyes shows that the memories of the slain live on.

                                  Or am I way off base on what your talking about here?

                                  A: (John Mørke)
                                  You're not off-base. This is closer to what I was looking for than any other idea I've seen. I mean, Lunars riding horses and tigers, sure. But I wouldn't give them ride-enhancing Charms for that, because those are off-beats. They don't really fit the iconography, since Lunars can become animals. So I am more compelled by ideas that proceed from the basis of Lunars who ride creatures that compliment them.
                                  Originally posted by EX3 What We Know Wiki
                                  Q: (Matt the Bruins fan)
                                  Razzin' frazzin' Solars... I guess this means my Lunar needs to get cracking on his plan to make off into the Wyld with a viable breeding population of chameleons and start selecting for gigantism. Let's see your golden camo dinosaurs climb redwoods, spot opponents over a 360° arc, and use their tongues as ballistic seige weapons!

                                  A: (The Demented One)
                                  I don't think Lunars will be disappointed with their familiars. Not if I have any Charms to write about it.
