                                                    You can't use Supplemental Charms to enhance attacks made with another Ability. So while you can enhance an EBA with Thrown charms, or with Archery Charms, you can't enhance it with both (making the stacking question irrelevant). As for Firearms, allowing Firearms Charmns to enhance EBAs would be a perfectly valid houserule.

                                                            Originally posted by Citadel97501 View Post
                                                            I want to make sure I am reading these Infernal Charms correctly after the Scroll of Errata altered them?

                                                            1) All Things Betray x 2: So for 1 motes, I get an action long Sueprise Attack Immunity, If I spend 3 motes I get my essence in automatic successes? Sounds like a great deal to me.

                                                            2) Bitter Heart Unbleeding purchased x 2: So for 3 motes, I can add my negative intimacies to my soak against a single attack, or for 8 motes I get a Perfect Soak?

                                                            3) Bitterness Beyond Wounds: I don't see the point of this charm as the perfect soak of BHU, would negate bad touch effects for the same cost or am I missing something? I am also confused as to why it is noted as having a flaw of invulnerability since its not a perfect effect?

                                                            4) Which upgrade charm for Verdant Emptiness Endowment allows you to either give a target active essence, or increase their essence? I see how to give Essence or Mutations, and that along with mutations could be very useful?

                                                            5) Since we are discussing VEE, how many points of mutation do you allow with Scoured Perfection of Form, I don't see any notes on how much these mutations cost in experience? I am concerned and want to make sure I am not missing anything? For instance if there is no exp cap, would I not be able to transform myself, & my followers and all of my clones into Dragons? (Giant, Sturdy, Armored Hide, Wings, & Dragons Breath)

                                                            Oh and I can cap out the relevant attack and attribute pools for my minions, giving them 12B, 8L, 8L breath weapon, that can fly very fast.)
                                                            1) Yes. Note that using the surprise defense against Beloved characters gives you a point of Limit (and makes you hate them, so they're now Reviled).

                                                            2) Yes.

                                                            3) The perfect soak is in Step 7, the cap-breaking dodge is in Step 2. With enough negative Intimacies, it's semi-perfect: you can dodge anything that can be dodged.

                                                            4) Scoured Perfection of Form can grant the Enlightened (or Awakened?) Essence mutation. There is no way to increase permanent Essence, although according to Scroll of the Monk Infernals (well, all Celestials) should have a Charm that can grant E2, an equivalent to Soul-Enlightening Beneficience.

                                                            5) It's a Desecration effect that grants permanent mutations. The rules are in the Charm keyword section on p103: Training effect costing (point cost x2) experience, negatives offsetting positives but not granting general experience. Note that the Creature of Darkness mutation is a negative mutation, so it offsets the cost of other mutations.
