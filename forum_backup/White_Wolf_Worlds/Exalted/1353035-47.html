                Currently, there are going to be 7 Virtues, including the original 4 and also "Ambition". That leaves 2 unknowns.

                I'm really skeptical, because I felt that Principles and Ties were superior to the four Virtues in terms of function and verisimilitude.

                Valor had nothing to do with courage and everything to do with self-assertiveness, aggression, and establishing one's place in a hierarchy of dominance and authority. But while we could argue about the definition of courage, at least no one was confused about what Valor looked like.

                Conviction and Temperance, on the other hand...

                And "Stupid Useless Compassion" was a meme for a reason.

                I'm not sure how they plan to keep Valor and Ambition distinct enough from each other such that you don't feel punished for having high Ambition without the Valor to see it through, or high Valor without enough Ambition to think of DOING anything with your position of dominance.

                        Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz View Post
                        Have they said that the original 4 are included? I remember they said there'll be Virtues, but not that Conviction, Compassion, Temperance and Valour are included.
                        I checked, and the exact wording was that that old Virtues would be returning, but no mention of how many of them.


                        The seven Virtues are supposed to define or categorize Intimacies in some way, which I remembered. I also remembered how Neall couldn't answer what this meant for the distinctions of "Ties and Principles", but I suppose the seven Virtues replace those two categories.


                        "Ambition Intimacies" makes some sense. They could take the place of Principles which reflect goals you want to achieve in the world, and a general strength of Ambition would make it easier to form and keep Intimacies representing grand Ambitions.

                        "Temperance Intimacies" could take the place of Principles which reflect the creeds by which you live, such as "Honesty is the best policy". Again, the more Temperate you are, the easier you find to structure your life according to strong principles, and to not waver from them.

                        "Compassion Intimacies" could take the place of Ties, specifically positive Ties to people or beasts. The more Compassionate you are habitually, the easier you find it to care about other creatures in general, even when you've just met them, but especially if they're already big parts of your life.

                        "Passion/Lust Intimacies" could take the place of positive Ties for objects and activities, reflecting a character's favorite possessions like manses or artifact weapons, and favorite pleasures like exploration, battle, gambling, sex, or food. The more lusty you are, the more you seek to enjoy life to the fullest.

                        "Conviction Intimacies" could take the place of negative Ties in general. The Virtue of Conviction represents your rejection of everything you fear, and your commitment to avoiding or destroying those things you perceive as aberrations and detriments to the world.


                        Alternatively, fear and hatred aren't backed by any Virtue at all, which would be an interesting commentary.

                                                            I wholeheartedly agree with you. As far as a team-based tactical wargame goes, DD4 has a pretty good balance between player characters.

                                                            Also, one thing that ought to be said here is that balance between player characters doesn't exist in a vaccum.
