                                                    Originally posted by Prince of the Night View Post
                                                    Think threaten would work well in a combat "Disperse let fall your arms and I'll let you do so in peace Come against me and you will die"

                                                    Yes, you can use social influence in combat. Of course, if you're going against a group, you'll take the usual penalty for group influence, unless you can use a charm or something that negates that. And to threaten someone, you'll need to present a credible threat that you can deliver, so a reasonably ordinary-looking person might have to do a couple of attacks to show that yes, they probably can kill every single person there, or otherwise have already established that reputation.

                                                    Personally, I usually apply a penalty on threaten rolls specifically equal to the Size of any battlegroup you're trying to intimidate, as there's security in numbers, and even if you have established yourself as a credible threat, people in larger groups are generally more willing to stick together and not break.
