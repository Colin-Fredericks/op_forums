                    Oh Jesus, did I seriously argue with somebody about the minutiae of Dark Souls upgrade paths? Maybe I shouldn't participate in the network test next week...

                    ​(imstillgoingtoiloveitsomuch)

                    So anyway, one thing that I've been thinking of is the depiction of magical weapons that I don't actually like and have been critical of, for how insignificant their powers seem, both in a general sense and particularly when they seem to be overshadowed by the capabilities of those using them.

                    ​So I'm wondering about possibilities in taking those insignificant things and redeeming them with the kinds of expanded powers that they might have here.

                    ​Off the top of my head... the sword of Gryffindor from Harry Potter. The idea that it imbues qualities to make it stronger and responds to displays of need and worthiness might be something that can be expanded upon, perhaps in a combination of temporarily adopting qualities from enemies (perhaps especially or exclusively creatures of darkness) that it is up against, escalated to varying degrees (I could see it, say, starting of as bestowing bonuses to attack rolls by absorbing power from parried attacks, building up towards something as dramatic as being able to ape an enemy's attack power for a scene), combined with certain enabling of or incentive to attack with fierceness and bravery.

                    Hmm, maybe give it some chimera symbolism; a leonine core atop which various monstrous oddities can be placed.

                    ​I'm seeing... moonsilver, with orichalcum accents.

                                    An artifact quiver of trick arrows.

                                    The attunement bonus is that it never runs out of high quality arrows of the piercing or fouling types.

                                    Each Evocation represents a trick arrow you can pull out, each one with its own reset timing and conditions. Grappling-rope arrows (with the quiver producing lots of rope), explosive arrows, net arrows, glue arrows, scatter arrows, sonic arrows...

                                                Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post

                                                ​That's really cool.

                                                Would you see it as a case of Evocations expanding laterally to acquire different types, or vertically based on how useful and powerful the arrows might be?
                                                Mostly lateral, since this is explicitly designed and intended to be a literal grab-bag of tricks. The only common element is “at least vaguely like an arrow”.

                                                But not entirely lateral. Some of the trick arrows might need to be broken into multiple Evocations to balance their power or the number of tricks you can employ with a particular arrow. And some trick arrows are reasonably represented as advanced combinations of lesser trick arrows.

                                                It might also be cool if the Tricky Quiver is unusually capable of forming relational Evocations with unrelated artifact bows, to provide them trick arrows uniquely suited to the kinds of tricks those bows usually produce entirely on their own.
