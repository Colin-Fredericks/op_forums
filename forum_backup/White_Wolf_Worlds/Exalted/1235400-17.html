            Nothing you said alters the situation at all. Lunar's dedicated to sorcery still out-perform Solar's dedicated to sorcery in the first 2 circles. So I'm still in a situation of creating sorcery charms for Solars, or invalidating those of Lunars. Normally I don't mind creating charms, but those are usually unique to specific individuals. Creating charms that will be accessible by all Solars, across all of my games, is a bit more daunting. My initial thoughts on the Lunar and Solar sorcery charms are as follows:

            1) The first sorcery-enhancing charm Lunar's have allows them to capture/store sorcerous motes in their animas. This actually sounds like a charm that I might convert for all exalts. Tailor it's capability down a bit for Dragonbloods, and up a bit for Solars. It would give all exalts a bit more versatility in sorcery.

            2) The second charm allows the Lunar's shadow to perform a shaping-sorcery action, freeing the Lunar to do other things except an attack action. This charm does feel very Lunar-y to me. So I'd like to leave this one unique to Lunars.

            3) Coming up with a Solar sorcery charm isn't something I've had time to really consider yet. I've been busy with other things. My initial thoughts are a charm that enhances a Solar's ability to counter-spell other sorcerers. A charm that allows the Solar to treat all spells as known for the purposes of the counter-spell action, and possibly provides additional benefits if the Solar actually does know the spell he's countering. Like I said though, this is my initial thought, without having had the time to actually give it a lot of thought. I'll probably start another thread when I'm ready to fully workout Solar sorcery charms.
