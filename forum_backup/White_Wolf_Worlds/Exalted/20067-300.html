        I'd compare that to the War Charms League of Iron Preparation and its upgrade Tiger Warrior Training Technique. The former specifically calls out that, despite its indefinite duration, its effects become permanent without committed Essence once the training is complete, and presumably the latter works the same way.

        Sea Devil Training Technique is essentially just a Sail-flavored counterpart to Tiger Warrior Training Technique, so I'd say that its effect also becomes permanent after the training period, allowing the Solar to release her commitment. Keep in mind, though, that the fourth option (boosting Larceny, Resistance or Survival) is actually three separate effects, so you'd need to train your crew for seven months to really get the most out of the Charm.

        If you like, you could then also pick up Tiger Warrior Training Technique and make them elite troops as well. :-3

        EDIT: If training Charms become a thing in other Abilities as well, such as a Larceny Charm to create a band of thieves, a keyword that makes the rule concrete would probably be in order.

            Originally posted by Yatogami Odin View Post
            Other Martial Arts Charms that "should" benefit from HPM:

            - Terrifying Battle Shriek (Silver-Voiced Nightingale pg. 447-448: A single-target threaten action using [Charisma + Performance].)
            - Haunting Heart-Rending Melody (SVN pg. 448-449: Group-wide inspire action using [Charisma + Performance]. Note that this Charm costs 1m to begin with,)
            - Righteous Devil Form (Righteous Devil pg. 451: Borderline case, since it allows a [Charisma + Presence + Essence] roll to intimidate/inspire enemies the first time it's used in a fight. The answer is most likely "no" because that roll is more of a rider than the primary effect of the Charm, as with HEC. Essentially, if it doesn't create/enhance social influence each time it's used, it's probably not a social influence Charm.
            - Outrage-Kindling Cry (Black Claw pg. 455: Another [Manipulation + Presence] counterattack, this time to persuade enemies to turn on her target.)
            - Fragrant Petal Fascination Kata (Dreaming Pearl Courtesan pg. 459: A Martial Arts Charm that supplements inspire rolls, or other actions with appropriate stunts. This one clearly falls under HPM's umbrella, even given its combat utility.)
            I think Righteous Devil is a yes. It literally just lets you make a normal social influence action with the appropriate attribute + skill. I don't think it qualifies for the mote discount but you definitely get the +3 dice, and I don't think "only once in a fight" should be held against it.

            I'd also note that despite the dev reference to social influence rolls, when it comes to what a social influence CHARM is, I still don't know. Charms that create rolls always? No supplementals or reflexives that merely enhance? Charms that "try" to create rolls? Is an instill using an unusual dicepool not applicable for a discount, but is applicable for a roll bonus? Or neither or both?

            Originally posted by Yatogami Odin View Post
            To address Guarded Thoughts Meditation, I don't see anything that would prevent its use together with Black Claw Style.             This is also a yes. Doe Eyes Defense just checks your Guile and applies it, it doesn't care whether it's boosted or how so long as it wasn't by a combat ability. With three "social" styles and a few more that benefit heavily from social actions in combat time, it really seems to strain disbelief that Martial Arts isn't meant to freely combine with everything except its very specific restrictions.                                                 Originally posted by Aranfan View Post
                                                If I wanted to make a maid character with a specialty in cleaning, which ability would that be attached to?
                                                Depends on the cleaning implement of choice, I suppose. For instance, a White Reaper master might be able to use Martial Arts for the sweeping strokes of brooms, mops and the like.

                                                ...that was a joke. Mostly. In all seriousness, it'd really depend on what you're trying to do. Keep in mind that you don't need to (i.e. "shouldn't") roll for any action that doesn't have a significant bearing on the narrative. Keeping one's apartment clean on a daily basis probably doesn't have that kind of impact, but having it ransacked the night before an inspection would put some pressure to get it back into shape. Even then, I'd try not to put a hard Ability on it and just leave it up to stunts.

                                                If pressed, I'd say that cleaning resembles a conceal evidence action, and indeed concealing evidence probably does involve a degree of cleaning up. However, it'd be kind of strange if expert maids also happened to be master thieves as well. Another possibility is that cleaning could be handled as a basic crafting project, or perhaps major for truly ambitious cleaning like the entirety of a mansion. However, I'd almost certainly not make cleaning its own area of expertise, and so probably default to a minimum effective Ability rating of 1 and go with whatever Attribute suits the character's stunt best.
