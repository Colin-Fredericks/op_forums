                                OPP also isn't the original WW despite being run by Rich Thomas.

                                It's smaller than oWW when it got started; though with a larger stable of freelancers. It's smaller than nWW before Paradox absorbed them back in after the behind-the-scenes troubles with V5; and doesn't have the massive financial backing of Paradox (relatively speaking).

                                Keeping OPP stable means Rich has to keep work flow at something OPP can handle (and OPP has a huge amount of books it's working on for a company it's size, even if spread out around a lot of properties). Hiring people to do more has limits to what the company can absorb without straining their resources. And some of this is outside of OPP's control. OPP could get ten Exalted books ready by throwing lots of money at them... but WW still has to approve all of them; which slows down the process even with WW not seeming to have much editorial concerns over Exalted.

                                                            For antagonists I'm not sure how useful having the full books even is that much. The QC antagonists are just way easier to run, they have a nice little power boost by not being penalized for making decisive attacks and often times their charms are just like the ones printed in the books except a little stronger, so their straightforward strength helps make up for their lack of complex power.
