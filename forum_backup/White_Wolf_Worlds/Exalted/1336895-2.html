                            The Solar Charm, Thousand Forge Hands, reduces artifact 2-4 to (6-Essence) weeks (this is months) and Artifact 5 to months (this is years), is available at Essence 2 (this is Essence 4, and DBs don't have Supernal), doesn't require teamwork from skilled artisans (this does), and can be used on Legendary projects (this can't).

                            DBs shouldn't be able to make Artifact N/As, and this Charm doesn't change that. Artifact 5 should be a massive endeavour: this Charm requires three DBs working together to guarentee the normal 2 year minimum for Artifact 5, four DBs to cut the Crafting time in half, and Essence 5 and five DBs to get this to six months where a Solar can do it in one month on her own.

                            Plus DBs can't just crap out Craft points like Solars, and are pretty effectively gatekept out of First Age Artifice due to a lack of White Points. I think it scans.

                            But you could force the contributing Companion Crafters to pay Gold Points to make the Superior Slot and/or make them pay on the finishing rolls if you wanted to handicap it further. Or make it only applicable to Jade artifacts. Or borrow from Savant and Sorcerer and say the resulting artifact has to be oversized and bulky. Or just bump the Craft times up.

                            Your original idea is fine. But it means DBs will be twice as fast as Solars at making Artifact 2 (four times as fast for Jade artifacts if it stacks with Forge Hand Prana), about the same for Artifact 3, slower for Artifact 4 and much slower for Artifact 5. Which might fit perfectly with your homebrew DB/Alchemical set-up because Artifact 2s will be WAAAY more common (as your hundreds of DBs will be better than Solars at making them) but Artifact 4-5 will stay rare. But for an actual default game, I'd rather the PCs invest in one or two powerful artifacts than equip their followers with excess weak artifacts. And too many cheap Artifact 2s risks tipping that White Point balance -- I think it's better to incentivise PCs to go for quality over quantity.

                            Also, the 1E Alchemicals book had rules for making Autochothon-Akuma that might be useful for you if you were looking to boost DB crafting.

The Dragon-Blood can only create ONE trained elemental at any one time. The Dragon-Blood can still elect to create a ‘normal’ elemental of any base type or element that has not received any training for the original cost listed above and can create and maintain as many of these as their mote pool, willpower and anima resources allow.

                                                        - A trained elemental always carries over its consciousness, personality, memories, and Intimacies each time it is created.

                                                        - Training a created elemental requires specialties in Occult (Elementals) and a Survival specialty specific to the base type of elemental being created, animal husbandry for a wolf or horse and falconry for a raiton.

                                                        - Any training that is undertaken applies ONLY to an elemental of the same base type AND element that has received said training. For example, a Dragon-Blood creates a wolf-based Wood elemental and trains it to use the unhorse gambit. The Dragon-Blood is now able to summon one wolf Wood elemental trained in the unhorse gambit in addition to any number of untrained wolf, horse or raiton elementals of any element at any one time. Later, the same Dragon-Blood creates a wolf Air elemental and trains it to use the distract gambit and the disarm gambit. The Dragon-Blood can now create a wolf Wood elemental trained in the unhorse gambit OR a wolf Air elemental trained in the district and disarm gambit in addition to any number of untrained wolf, horse or raiton elementals of any element at any one time.

                                                        - The number of trained elementals a Dragon-Blood knows how to create is limited to her Essence.

                                                        - Training a created elemental functions exactly as described in Exalted, pp. 554 with the additional caveats that the difficulty to train a created elemental is always difficulty 4 (reflecting the deep focus and discipline required of the Dragon-Blood to shape the elements and infuse it with knowledge each time she fashions an elemental); the Dragon-Blood does not receive any bonus dice for Intimacies; the elemental must be created in order to receive training but any successes rolled carry over each time an elemental of the same base type and element is created, albeit fading at the usual rate if the specific elemental being trained is not created again within two or three days. Any relevant charms can be used to enhance the training of the elemental in the usual way.

                                                        - Should a trained elemental created by this charm be destroyed, the Dragon-Blood who created them suffers one automatic health level of bashing damage, but othewise there is no effect and nothing to stop the Dragon-Blood from creating another example of a trained elemental of the same type later on.
