            Originally posted by Irked View Post
            Yeah, as others have noted, there are definitely places where a reread clarifies matters somewhat - price of doing a stream-of-consciousness read-through, I guess. I think there are also some places where intent is... sussable?... but where the actual wording doesn't seem to support it, which may be some of what you're saying, as well.

            Case in point: let's say that you are intended to eventually get back all the XP you spend on FMP. The Charm says "At the end of each story, the Solar may roll a number of dice equal to experience points spent on this Charm, regaining a number of experience equal to her successes, but not beyond the total amount of experience she expended in training." I was reading "experience points spent on this Charm" as "experience points spent on this Charm this story." If it's not that, we could parse it as "experience points spent on the Charm, period," but that's a terrifying thought - once we pick up Tireless Learner Method, we have an uncapped XP-generating engine that gives us back 75% of all of the XP we've spent on the Charm since the start of the game at the end of every story, no matter how much we've recovered already.

            The sane version of that translation, then - and the one I think you're suggesting, if I read you right? - is that it means something like "experience spent on the Charm and not refunded yet, no matter how far back it was spent." But that's troubling as well - most centrally, because nothing in the Charm's text says anything like the proposed rider. And that's bad for a host of reasons! (It also makes Tireless Learner Method pretty ugly - we're now spending XP in hope that a biiiiiig pool that averages 75% successes will actually earn 100%+.)

            All that said, I think there's a reasonable chance that you're right that this is the intent - but I don't look forward to trying to have that argument with someone.

            I had originally read it as XP roulette. You are likely to just plain lose XP, but might gain more. This struck me as a bit silly.

            I had not considered that the roll could be cumulative (roll ALL the XP invested, instead of just for this story), nor did I consider that you would get more than one chance to roll the pool.

            I read it simply as "You get to roll the XP you spent this story. Every success gets you one back. The others are lost, period."

            So, what, do we think, is the intent of this charm, as in what purpose does it server for players in an average game? Is it supposed to be a XP engine to feed other effects that cost XP (such as WST), or does it simply allow someone to play the "support" role within Exalted and transfer their XP to someone who might get more use out of it ( like the tank)?

                                            Socialize

                                            Number of Charms: 50
                                            By Ability requirement: 0/0/4/4/42
                                            By Essence requirement: 10/12/20/5/3
                                            Nonstandard dice tricks: Enemy 1s are negative successes. Whatever that… thing… #25 does is. Recurring 5s and 6s.

                                            Themes: Be unreadable; hurt people who try to read you. Help your allies resist influence. Know what everyone is thinking. Develop artificial personalities, and equip them with their own Charm trees.

                                            Hoo boy. 50 Charms, 42 of them requiring Ability 5 – that’s ~84%. This is a chunky tree – let’s see what it does.

                                            Charms:
                                            1. You are incapable of faux pas. People with Ties to the local culture have Minor Ties to you.
                                            2. Read intentions with double-9s.
                                            3. Sense any application of Resolve or Guile in the next turn.
                                            4. Know the intensity of all Intimacies you’ve Instilled yourself in the target.
                                            5. After Reading Intentions of the target while he was talking to someone else, he takes -2 Guile when you Read Intentions while he’s talking to you.
                                            6. 1m for +1 Guile, or 2m for +2 Guile.
                                            7. Ignore all penalties to Guile, except wound penalties and the penalty for thinking you’re alone.
                                            8. Detect when an NPC Reads Intentions on someone else nearby.
                                            9. On observing a ritual, discern its significance. Bonus successes if you have to perform the rite.
                                            10. Recurring 1s any time #1 would apply. (Always?)
                                            11. Intimacies towards you will not drop as a result of any Persuade attempt you make, unless characters pay 1wp.
                                            12. Retry a Read Intentions action on the same target.
                                            13. Gain 2m when you succeed at a Socialize action, to a max of the Socialize motes you’ve spent this scene.
                                            14. Read Intentions on everyone in the scene.
                                            15. Boost an ally’s Guile. By glaring daggers at him. Awesome.
                                            16. Bonus dice when Reading Intentions on someone who failed to do the same to you.
                                            17. Infinite Socialize Mastery; only applies to boosting Guile.
                                            18. Perfectly conceal an Intimacy.
                                            19. If an Intimacy would be detected, you can change it to something else to fool the person looking. If you Limit Break, though, your pretend Intimacy might become real!
                                            20. Witnessing a social action, you may guess at the Intimacy behind it. If correct, you discern an Intimacy without having to roll Read Intentions.
                                            21. Preempt a target about to perform social influence; that influence is treated as if it had already been tried and failed.
                                            22. 1/scene, intervene after social influence to boost the target’s Resolve with a counterargument.
                                            23. Advise another to grant bonus successes for use on future social actions. Target gains an Intimacy for you.
                                            24. Anyone you defeat socially becomes obsessed with you, taking die penalties and coming to love or hate you.
                                            25. Got a pencil handy? Okay, take your 1s and 2s, and give them to someone else nearby. He rolls; find his lowest success number, and for each 2 you have, cancel one of those. Find his next success number (i.e., 8 if the first one was 7), and for each 1 you have, cancel one of those. You gain successes equal to those canceled for him; if he doesn’t roll well enough, he looks ridiculous.
                                            26. If a target tries to turn a third party against you, the aspersions fall back on the target instead.
                                            27. Bonus dice to Join Battle and Awareness if you sense hostility in the room.
                                            28. When you block another’s Read Intentions, you may reply with one of your own.
                                            29. Compel another to Read Intentions on you.
                                            30. If a target fails to Read Intentions on you, he believes he can use you to accomplish some aim.
                                            31. Hypnotize a character who fails to read your Guile.
                                            32. Know what it would take to persuade someone to take a particular action.
                                            33. Sense the Ties, mannerisms, and behavior of everyone involved in court, including those currently absent.
                                            34. 1/scene, free full Socialize Excellency. This Charm references “venting a point of Limit” by achieving a legendary social goal – is that a thing mentioned anywhere else?
                                            35. Over the course of a few hours, shift into a different persona with its own Intimacies.
                                            36. Your personas have some of your Ability dots + ½ your total XP to spend on more.
                                            37. Your personas may learn Charms.
                                            38. Someone who loves someone who loves you also has a Minor Tie for you.
                                            39. Curse someone to be hated by a social group.
                                            40. 1/scene, double-8s on a Socialize roll.
                                            41. 1/story, add your Guile to your Resolve or vice-versa. Ignore effect of Intimacies on Resolve.
                                            42. One persona has 75% of your total XP, plus 2/3 of any future XP. (What?)
                                            43. Craft a custom persona to meet someone’s needs.
                                            44. Access a persona’s Abilities and Charms for one tick. On Limit Break, shuffle your Intimacies with the persona’s Intimacies.
                                            45. On death, you regenerate into a persona as per Doctor Who. Like, blatantly as per Doctor Who.
                                            Mastery of Small Manners (#1) is one of those rare Charms that actually inspires me to change the way I play – inventing new cultural norms for players to suss out (or supernaturally bypass). It’s a fun idea!

                                            The upgraded version of Motive-Discerning Technique (#2) says it lets you speculate on the existence of Intimacies and be told if you’re right. But that’s already part of the base Read Intentions action, isn’t it? What is this adding? (Is it that you get both “motives in this scene” and “an Intimacy, maybe” with one roll?)

                                            Umbral Eyes Focus (#4) implies that you don’t normally get the intensity of an Intimacy when you Read Intentions. Do you not? The rules for Read Intentions just say that the ST “reveals” an Intimacy – I would have thought that included strength.

                                            Shadow Over Day (#6) is a one mote discount. Edit: At Essence 2, Deep-Eyed Soul Gazing (#16) is a situational one-mote discount.

                                            Intent-Tracing Stare (#8) switches back to feet as a unit of intra-character distance – I’m not sure any other Charms measure that way.

                                            Eh heh. “Indecent Proposal Method” (#11) is pretty aptly named. Again, you have to be a particular kind of character to want this, but it supports that archetype pretty well.

                                            Dauntless Assayer Method (#12) says it lets you “reset [your] attempt” on a Read Intentions action. I had to go back to the social actions section to make sense of this – it notes that once you fail a RI action, you can’t try again on the same character this scene. That’s probably a good rule, but it took me a bit to get that this Charm wasn’t just a reroll.

                                            Preeminent Gala Knife (#13) asks me to keep track of how many motes I have spent on Socialize Charms this scene. Um, pass.

                                            Wise-Eyed Courtier Method (#14) is great stuff. Walk into a room, and instantly know everything about everyone. Fabulous. (This charm is half the reason I’m sad that the Second Apocalypse books don’t make the recommended reading list – in the first two books, Kellhus lives and breathes Solar Socialize, and there’s at least one scene that’s a beautiful illustration of WECM. The books in general parse very well as the story of a newborn Solar – and given that the first trilogy is The Prince of Nothing and the second is The Aspect-Emperor, well.)

                                            Okay, there are some really great powers here in the teens through early 20s (edit: or 30s!). This is your bread-and-butter stuff to dominate the social scene – forget Presence and Performance, this is hands-down my favorite social Ability so far. I can see some players pulling really fun stunts with some of these, as well.

                                            Effective Counterargument (#22) is great, but we really need a concrete answer to the question, “How do die caps interact with mortals?” Can this Charm be used on a mortal at all?

                                            There appears to be no way to counteract Endless Obsession Feint (#24), right? Edit: Or Selfsame Master Procurer (#30), or, gosh, Soul-Void Kata (#31)? (What is up with the “Selfsame Master” Charms?)

                                            So, Fete-Watcher Stance (#27) grants bonuses to allies as long as you warn them “at least one round before any hostile action occurs.” So, it grants bonuses as long as you warn people one round before Join Battle. But, um, if no one has Joined Battle, time isn’t measured in rounds – there are explicitly no social rounds. So when do you have to warn them?

                                            Know the Soul’s Price (#32) is still fabulous. Instant story material, highly useful power. Feels like this should interact with Bargain somehow, though.

                                            Good gosh, Venemous Rumors Technique (#39) is vicious – you can’t even resist the effect yourself.

                                            Oh, Socialize. We were doing so well, and then we hit the Persona tree. I don’t have any problem with the idea that the Solar socialite can flip around all his Intimacies into a whole other self – that makes a fair amount of sense. But with Draw the Curtain (#42), it’s quite possible your persona has more XP than you do – and you can have five of these characters. Bookkeeping entirely aside, why does Social Guy get to field six Solar character sheets simultaneously, any one of which could plausibly have more XP than any other member of the party? (Also, why does Draw the Curtain grant past XP and future XP at different rates?) And the ability to buy whatever Charms you want handily sidesteps the dot restrictions – sure, maybe I need to buy Melee 5 so my Persona can have Melee 5, but that persona can have those dots and also all the Melee Charms it can afford, while I’m sinking mine into, y’know, Socialize. (My other personas can specialize similarly – heck, I can have one specialized in each caste, if I want!) That just seems… a little unbalanced, y’know?

                                            Okay, so Socialize has a lot of stuff I really like in it, and a handful of stuff I really don’t. My suspicion is that you could prune out personas and some of the other weird stuff, and be left with a really solid tree of ~35 Charms.


                                            Yes: Winner: Wise-Eyed Courtier Method. Runner up: Discretionary Gesture (#15). You can kick your ally under the table to raise his Guile – that’s both useful and hilarious.
                                            No: Aspersions Cast Aside (#25). I must odd, for I cannot even.
                                            Just Plain Weird: There’s a lot of powers here that have no good way to stop them from wrecking you, other than maybe “never fail a Read Intentions roll.” Several of these feel like there should be some Solar way to throw them off, without necessarily going the full-on Integrity “opposed Willpower rolls” route.
