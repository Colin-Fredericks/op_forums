    Originally posted by Prometheus878 View Post
    Is there a specific bureau in Heaven responsible for curses? Do they subdivide based on who's doing the cursing to what?

    And just who is responsible for revenge stories anyway?
    Well, the curse in question here was Sidereal Astrology. Violet-Eyed witch, and all that.

    As for who is responsible for revenge...I'm pretty sure Granny Bright used to be a goddess of vengeance, but there may well be more than one. There might be local, regional, and directional gods of vengeance just like there are for other important human centric concepts like war and peace and disease. I'd say Abstract Matters and Humanity are probably fighting over it, just like everything else that's cool.

    So I'm making God-Kicking Boot, an Exalted webcomic, now. Updates on Sundays. Full-color, mediocre but slowly improving art. It's a thing.

    The absence of a monument can, in its own way, be something of a monument also.     -Roger Zelazny
