                                                        Originally posted by Gigaton-Falcon-Emu View Post
                                                        Should Immortality Hearthstones still be a thing? I really liked them but I am unsure if they still fit the game...
                                                        I don't like that hearthstone, but eternal life is not impossible to achieve by other means - Sorcerous Working or Peaches of Immortality come to mind - so it is not exactly broken. But I'd add some more interesting limitations to it: Workings tend to have all sorts of weirdness (hide away your heart and live forever as long as it is safely stored in an urn, live forever but must bathe in the blood of a virgin dancer every Calibration, or whatever) and Peaches of Immortality usually involve stealing from Yu-Shan.

                                                        Making it Linked, so that the Manse becomes a complication that can disrupt the hearthstone is a possibility. However, if you don't want to handwave away the possibility of creating a whole bunch of those by a skilled Solar Manse-maker, making it Wild-born and giving each one of them that happens to form some particular limitation/ maintenance requirement or whatever to make it more interesting might be better. Making it so you have to carry it with you constantly might be enough as well, as the possibility of someone trying to steal your immortality for themselves can surely be used to create some stories.
