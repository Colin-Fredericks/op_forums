    Solar Awareness as a combat ability

    06-21-2020, 11:41 PM
    I've always felt a little disappointed with the Solar Awareness charm tree. The Unsurpassed Sense Discipline charms are awesome, but everything else felt kinda meh, and for such an important ability it has one of the smallest trees (second smallest behind Investigation I think?). It also felt weird to me as a Dawn Caste ability since outside of maybe Awakening Eye there are no combat-specific effects anywhere in the tree. This feeling was reinforced after seeing the Perception - Senses tree in the new Lunar book -- that tree has a bunch of fun things, not just better join battle, but also stuff like aiming reflexively, aiming while moving, rolling JB after crashing someone, gaining a free full excellency when going first, etc. I also never liked that Awareness as a whole is a very subtle and internal powerset and then the capstone charm has you turning into a living fucking lighthouse.

    With all that in mind, I tried my hand at homebrewing some charms to flesh out the awareness tree. Not all of them are combat related but rather they're just ideas I had lying around. As always feedback is appreciated.


    Mystic Eyes Unveiled:

    Cost: -; Mins: Awareness 5, Occult 5, Essence 2
    Type: Permanent
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Permanent
    Prerequisite Charms: Keen Sense Technique (any one), All-Encompassing Sorcerer’s Sight

    For a Solar attuned to the supernatural, there is no barrier between the material world and the spiritual world. She exists in both simultaneously, and magic and sorceries are as tangible to her as the wind and the waves.
    This charm permanently upgrades Spirit-Detecting Glance and All-Encompassing Sorcerer’s Sight, allowing them to be activated with a duration of Indefinite and to benefit from the cost reductions of Surprise Anticipation Method and Unsurpassed Sense Discipline. The Solar may explicitly use awareness charms to supplement any Occult rolls made to detect, track or analyse supernatural entities or phenomena.

    Incomparable Battle Sense:

    Cost: -; Mins: Awareness 4, Essence 2
    Type: Permanent
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Permanent
    Prerequisite Charms: Awakening Eye

    Further attuning to her second sight, the Solar foresees all avenues of attack and defence and surpasses them all with skill bordering on prescience.
    This charm upgrades the pool of stored automatic successes created by its prerequisite. The pool now starts at Essence successes and stores additional successes equal to the 10s in her initiative roll, and 9s as well if Sensory Acuity Prana is active. She may also apply the stored successes to attack rolls or spend them to increase her defence against an attack at a rate of 1 point per success spent.

    Untying-the-Knot Meditation:

    Cost: 5m; Mins: Awareness 4, Essence 3
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: Dual
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Incomparable Battle Sense

    The Solar focuses her awakened senses and singles out an opponent, seeing through to their very heart to find the flaw in their style that will allow her to defeat them.
    She takes a special aim action targeting one character she is aware of and rolls Perception + Awareness against their Guile. Success allows her to pinpoint a vulnerability that she can exploit: her next attack against that character will gain Essence non-charm dice to the attack roll (in addition to the normal effects of aiming) and double extra successes if withering or add extra successes to damage if decisive.
    Once per target per scene, reset by learning a new piece of information on her opponent’s capabilities.

    Harm-Unravelling Insight:

    Cost: 4m 1w; Mins: Awareness 4, Essence 3
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Incomparable Battle Sense

    Sharpened by imminent danger, the Solar’s senses zero in on an incoming attack. In an instant, she understands her opponent’s offensive completely, and then moves to unravel it with minimal exertion.
    She clashes an incoming attack rolling Perception + Awareness, with 1s on the attacker’s roll subtracting successes. If she wins, she deals no damage, instead she steals initiative from her attacker equal to the 10s on her roll, and 9s as well if Sensory Acuity Prana is active.
    If this crashes her opponent, she may take a reflexive aim action against them. If she knows that charm, she may pay a 3i surcharge to reflexively activate Untying-the-Knot Meditation, ignoring its once per scene restriction.
    Once per target per scene, reset by defeating that opponent or causing them to flee.
    Alternatively, she may use this charm to defend against an environmental hazard, including uncountable damage, rolling Perception + Awareness at a difficulty set by the Storyteller. Success lets her find the perfect course of action to emerge unscathed. This use of the charm does not require a reset.

    Blazing Eye of Conquest:

    Cost: 5m 1w; Mins: Awareness 5, Essence 3
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: Perilous
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Untying-the-Knot Meditation, Harm-Unravelling Insight

    Danger and adrenaline spurring her mind onward, the Solar recollects her focus and surveys a battlefield changed by her actions, finding new insights and opportunities to seize.
    So long as she has made at least one decisive attack and reset to base initiative this scene, the Solar may use this charm to roll Join Battle supplemented with Awakening Eye at no additional cost and add her result to her current initiative. If this brings her to higher initiative than all enemies, she gains a point of temporary willpower and resets Untying-the-Knot Meditation and Harm-Unravelling Insight.
    Once per scene.

    Incessant Focus Strike:

    Cost: -; Mins: Awareness 5, Essence 4
    Type: Supplemental
    Keywords: Uniform
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Blazing Eye of Conquest

    When she focuses her attention completely on a target, there is no escaping from the Solar’s gaze. Her strikes are unerring, unstoppable, unmatched.
    Once per scene, she may add a free full excellency to an attack roll benefiting from an aim action.
    Reset by shedding her Iconic anima and rising back to that level.

    Fivefold Unity of Self:

    Cost: -; Mins: Awareness 5, Essence 5
    Type: Permanent
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Permanent
    Prerequisite Charms: Unsurpassed Sense Discipline (all three)

    The Solar has moved past the limits of her earthly perceptions to find that the boundary between the self and the outside is merely an illusion; herself and the universe are one and the same. Realizing this cosmic truth, she closes her eyes and sees for the first time, and knows the world as she knows herself.
    When the Solar has all three Unsurpassed Sense Discipline charms active at the same time, she sublimates her five senses into a perfect unified perception. This extrasensory barrier extends Essence range bands in radius regardless of obstruction. Within it she is instantly and simultaneously aware of every object and phenomenon and suffers no environmental penalties. This automatically sees through any use of Stealth or Larceny not enhanced by magic, and ensures that no matter by what means something is concealed she will always be able to roll to detect it.
    While this charm is active, she adds Essence automatic successes to any Awareness roll directed at something within range of her extrasense and may use any Awareness charms to supplement it regardless of what sense they belong to.

      -I like the Combat Charms and it feels right for them to be this strong since there aren't many. That being said, they will require responsible players to use them, because a greedy min-maxer could use them to make their broken fighter significantly more broken.

      -Incessant Focus Strike's reset condition feels disconnected from the Charm's effect. With a condition that requires you to go iconic->dim->iconic, I would expect the Charm itself to expend your anima and/or to only be usable at bonfire. Also a reset condition that requires other Charms to achieve (shedding your anima) feels pretty bad when you don't have those Charms.

      -Fivefold Unity of Self feels like by the time you reach it, you can already get anything it provides from the rest of the tree. What is its intended niche/purpose?

But yeah those trees both have ways to expend your anima while Awareness doesn't. If anyone can think of a more fitting reset condition, I'd love to hear it.

          Originally posted by Epitome View Post
          -Fivefold Unity of Self feels like by the time you reach it, you can already get anything it provides from the rest of the tree. What is its intended niche/purpose?
          It does a few things: firstly, there are ways to cheese stealth to become completely undetectable (easiest one is Invisible Statue Spirit + Sound and Scent Banishing Attitude). There are also some effects that hide you automatically without contest (like Flashing Nocturne Prana and I think there was something in Survival for hiding caches of stuff). And lastly, there are some stealth-like effects that bypass Awareness entirely (like Mental Invisibility Technique or whatever the Forgotten Blade does). This charm would let you roll to detect in these types of situations where you would otherwise have no chance.

          Second, it provides an Infinite Mastery-like effect which can really save you some motes in scenes where you wind up making a lot of Awareness rolls, and with a few of these charms you have the chance to roll Awareness several times during a fight. It helps make up for the fact that in asks you to commit a lot of motes before you can use it.

          and third, it allows you to combine sense-specific charms with each other, similar to how Penultimate Unity of Form lets you combine Performance charms from different arts. It enables some useful combos like using Genius Palate Summation by seeing or hearing a meal being served, keeping a library of sounds instead of scents with Unsurpassed Taste and Smell Discipline, or using Eyeless Harbinger Awareness against opponents you can see, etc. I'm sure somebody will find an absolutely broken combination that I didn't think of but for now I'm pretty excited about this effect and its possibilities.
