                                                        I have a question about the Exigent book. I assume that the idea is both to provide playable Exigents but also to provide a helpful source for how to create your own Exigents. The trick isn't really to design an Exigent, but to design an Exigent with enough content for a player to happily be able to play it in game. At the same time, we can't make a bunch of Exigents that each have hundreds of charms because the book would be too big.

                                                        Are you working on a specific structure to present these Exigents to us, or are you going to mix and match the presentations with some more established than others?

                                                        My idea for an ideal Exigent book would have perhaps 5-6 established Exigents with enough content to be played for a while (these 5-6 would be very different in playstyle from one another), then maybe a few more less established Exigents presented as suggested Exigents with focus on how the reader can establish them further (a little more substance than a listed antagonist and some comments about how you as a player/storyteller can take it further, and how you can draw inspiration from the more established ones).
