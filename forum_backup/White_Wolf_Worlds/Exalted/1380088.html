                                                          Trevor and Sypha fighting demons in Castlevania season 3. It's got almost everything Exalted: A chosen hero using a blessed Artifact that deals aggravated damage to Creatures of Darkness. A scholar blending control of the elements with kung fu. A Sorcerer interrupting a demonic ritual in a Demesne. A blood sacrifice to summon a Deathlord from the Underworld. Great stunts, great fights.

                                                          Season 2 has three heroes taking on a Deathlord. Alucard, Trevor, and Sypha are basically an Abyssal, a Heroic Mortal, and a Dragonblood. Alucard is simply more powerful than Trevor and is much faster, but Trevor has an Artifact and a ton of tricks from countless battles. Sypha has elements magic and martial arts.

                                                          Cho demonstrates how terrifying it is to battle a master of an Exalted Martial Art, and in this case its also a flawless representation of Pearl Courtesan!

                                                          The Morningstar Flail (Trevor's Whip) and Sword Familiar (Alucard's Sword) are both amazing examples of artifacts that going in completely different directions. The Whip deals aggravated damage to Creatures of Darkness and extra to vampires, and it makes them explode, while the Sword can defend or attack on its own. Watch them attack a battlegroup (a Might 2, Elite Drill, Size 2 group at that). Dracula serves as a perfect Deathlord- he has mastered dark science, powerful sorcery, and has enough Brawl, Resistance, and Presence that its insane to even think about opposing him. His Castle is a Manse that can teleport.

                                                          The whole series is amazing. Isaac and Hector (poor puppy) are excellent looks at Demon Summoners working for powerful dynasties. Dracula and the Vampire Nobles are prime Deathlord/Abyssal relations and politics. Saint-Germain is a great look at how a Mortal Sorcerer might fare in a world defined by badassery. Isaac and FlysEyes discussion on the nature of sin is a legitimately bone-chilling social combat scene (I was a scholar, in a place called Athens....)

                                                          I strongly recommend giving it a watch if you haven't.

                                                          Please be warned: this is not champagne, this is most likely a duck.
