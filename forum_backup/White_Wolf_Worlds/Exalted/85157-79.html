                        Well there are also the various demon spider subspecies that make up the Anuhles. Those just end up being made by various 2nd circle demons, so not unreasonable to presume that certain subspecies of mimic might arise from different demons. Granted this would end up making a case whereby you'd have mimics that only look like beds, only look like chests, only look like doors, or only houses as opposed to just the ones that swap form as they get bigger. Still it would cover the check a player might make to figure out what line the chair that just tried to eat them came from.

                        "You're not entirely sure which line. You've heard stories of these kind of monsters, but they never seem to agree on who makes these things or even if they're actually demons and not just fucked up arts and craft projects." With the possibility to add in the fun nod of "There was also some debate on whether they were actually from Malfeas or if that was just the blanket assumption that arises from trying to go to the bathroom and the bathroom trying to eat you."
