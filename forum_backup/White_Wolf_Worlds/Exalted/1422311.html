    A question of speed

    11-06-2020, 06:45 PM
    What's the fastest a PC Exalt can travel in 3E, canon?

    The best I've got is a Lesser Elemental Dragon (Fakharu, Corebook page 521; or One with the Blue Dragon, Mela's Coil, Arms of the Chosen p107) with its Soaring Dragon Flight merit with a Solar rider using the Charm 'Wind-Racing Essence Infusion' (Core p380) for a journey that would usually take a week.

    That gets you to a speed of 3500 mph.

    If the journey would usually take less than a week, the speed drops to 3000 mph.

    If the rider is an Essence 5 Dragon-Blooded (using Seven-League Gallop) then it's 2500 miles in a single hour, or (regardless of Essence score) 1000 mph for longer journies.

    I don't think anything else comes close to that. An Agata with Wind-Racing Essence Infusion is 210 mph for a week-long journey (180mph for a shorter journey), or 150 miles in a single hour with Seven-League Gallop for a Deeb at Essence 5 (or 60mph otherwise). Stormwind Rider is 100 mph. Wings of the Raptor (four dot artifact) are capped at 80 mph. Chariot of Aerial Conquest (5 dot FAA) is 30 mph.

    Have I missed anything? Anything in the Lunar Charmset?

    I will update the table below as folks add their own Fastest Methods of Travel.
    Method Speed Notes
    Travel Without Distance 36,000 mph First 50 miles in an hour only (i.e. 50mph for journies over 50 miles, and not quite as fast for journies under 50 miles).
    Ascendant Nova Phoenix 20,000 miles Capstone: Reach any point in creation in 1 hour or less (Creation is 20,000 miles long on the diagonal)
    Lesser Elemental Dragon 3,500 mph Solar rider, see above
    Ascendant Nova Phoenix 2,500 mph Warstrider, Essence 5, high-altitude cruising speed only, carries 5
    Lesser Elemental Dragon 1,000 mph Dragon-Blooded rider, 2500 mph over short distances at Essence 5
    Lesser Elemental Dragon 500 mph Base speed (One With The Blue Dragon)
    Agata 210 mph Solar rider, see above
    Stormwind Rider 100 mph Flat speed, available at Essence 1 to sorcerers.

                                                          Originally posted by JohnDoe244 View Post
                                                          Would you be willing to discuss the themes and other Charms of Emissary's Mask?
                                                          Sure.

                                                          Originally posted by JohnDoe244 View Post
                                                          What's it made of?
                                                          The outside is silver. Inside, a special gold glass, created by layering sorcerous orichalcum between lenses of glass surgically removed from two of the floating eyes of Orabilis.

                                                          Originally posted by JohnDoe244 View Post
                                                          Where did it come from?
                                                          Once, in the Celestial City, the goddess Shalrina was visited by the Exalted lord of a distant city. He sought her assistance in creating a unique artifact that he could grant to one of the mortals employed in his bureaucracy: a mask that would empower the wearer to serve the city.

                                                          Shalrina agreed to do this thing, and traveled with him to his city, but realized halfway through the endeavor of crafting the mask that she had been deceived; the design of the mask would indeed imbue the wearer with unique power, but also enslave them to the will of the lord.

                                                          Enraged by the deception, Shalrina sabotaged the mask in a subtle way; its wearer would appear to be loyal to the Exalted lord, but their ultimate allegiance would be to a group of mortals.

                                                          The tyrannical lord was unaware of her careful treason; the day after his Emissary donned the mask, the goddess told some mortals oppressed by his rule how they might command the Emissary.

                                                          It was wise that she returned to Yu-Shan in the hours following that, because the battle between Exalt and Emissary was vicious enough to reduce the city to a smoking ruin.

                                                          One day, a sorcerer stopped his travels to rest in the ruins of the city and take shelter from the storm. When he awoke the next morning, the rain had washed some of the rubble clear, and he saw a glint of silver…

                                                          Originally posted by JohnDoe244 View Post
                                                          What powers lead upto this teleportation one?
                                                          Each person to wear the mask gets a pseudo-Excellency, and a unique set of Evocations.

                                                          In Lady Kratz’s case, for example, she can spend a mote to succeed on her Read Intentions action against a person to know their goal for the scene if she’s read their destiny via her astrology (which is itself mechanically identical to the Reading the Tea Leaves ritual), ignore environmental penalties to track a person by following their thread of fate, create phantom “echoes” to assist in a Case Scene action, and the aforementioned teleportation.

                                                          When Brueghel was wearing the mask, he could cast sorcery up to the Celestial level, and gained access to a Burning Name-like attack that did aggravated damage, afflicting the target with gruesome, and painful transformations.

                                                          Ephiselle the Midnight Queen can, when wearing the mask, see through the “eyes” of any statue of herself or the Emissary, turn water into agonizing poison, and hear her name or office being spoken anywhere in the city.
