            OK, so...if the objection not "I don't get why Solars are the best at this particular thing." but just "I hate that Solars are the best at nearly everything", I am going to have to direct you to almost any other game, and especially to any other game published by White Wolf. "Solars on top" is one of the core things that differentiates Exalted from other games; it was a radical and decisive reversal of everything else the company had ever published and ties into the core themes of the line as a whole. The other splats should be fun and powerful and have their own thing, but they should be generally weaker than Solars, because that is hugely important to the setting. The people who really press this complaint generally seem not to be upset about the quality of life as non-Solars so much as they just resent that the game explicitly makes one Exalted type better at all, and under the circumstances I'm not terribly sympathetic to that complaint, because it's asking the game to be something it's intentionally not.

            That said, suggesting that the other splat-specific subsystems don't count because they're poorly implemented is kind of a low blow. That's like saying that Solars being the best sorcerers doesn't count because sorcery is a crappy XP sink -- it's not a design goal, those subsystems just happened to be handled badly, and if heaven forbid they're handled badly this time it will still be a fluke. Of course they aren't going to design 10 pages of specialized splat-specific mechanics and then make them pointlessly awful just to drive home how much better Solars are.

                                    Chill out, dude. I get mad at the forums too sometimes, but just...log off and punch a thing, and commiserate with your friends about how awful I am, or something. This isn't a knife fight.

                                    The Solars being on top is not specifically about the Solars, though obviously that's core to the game's setting structure. Rather, the Solars are the purest expression of the game's themes: overcoming adversity and achieving the impossible, humans defeating powerful monsters, mythic heroes, and so forth. These themes apply to all Exalted, but they appear in their purest and most unadulterated form in Solars, so making Solars the most powerful is making a thematic statement about human power overcoming esoteric power, the weak vanquishing the strong, achieving the impossible through your spirit, etc. Lunars are less powerful than Solars because their power is channelled through monstrous aspects, and that reduces the degree to which they can express the core themes. This is a calculated reversal of many other games and oWoD in particular, where the more esoteric and sinister-seeming your power source is, the stronger you are.

                                    The fact that the Solars are the most powerful does not mean that everybody else falls over themselves prostrating themselves to the Solars, or that the game exists entirely to show how awesome the Solars are, or anything like that. If you're playing a different type of Exalted, the return of the Solars provides a context for you to establish your own awesomeness, and not only does that not require that your splat be as powerful as Solars, in many cases it works better if they aren't. The return of the Solars is meant to be a challenge and a question to the established splats, but it is not meant to be a challenge they will surely lose, or a question they will answer with "let me hold your coat". But the dynamic established there just does not work if the other Celestials are on completely even footing. Making Lunars and Sidereals totally on par with Solars, in particular, would be tremendously damaging to both of them.
