        Originally posted by Hill Crushing Hero View Post
        Would you please tell us more about the Lunar-foil Exalted (I believe it was a Lunar-foil, but could be a foil to another type of Exalted?) that have worked alongside the Dragon-Blooded?
        None of them work alongside the Dragon-Blooded.

        (During development of Third Edition, there was an additional kind of "Mystery Exalt" that intermingled with the Dragon-Blooded, but it was cut for not being a sufficiently quality concept).

        Are any of the new Exalted considered 'Terrestrial' and recognized by the Immaculate Philosophy? Or maybe seen as allies to the Dragon-Blooded yet outside the Anathema-category?
        "Terrestrial" isn't a category the Immaculate Order, or anyone in setting, uses.

        The Immaculate Order's knowledge of the Getimians comes from the Bronze Faction, and is thus, uh, biased.

        Liminals are disturbning and unclean by nature, yet they seem driven to fight the hungry dead. The Immaculate Order doesn't know much about them, but it doesn't label them Anathema.

        The remaining "Mystery Exalted" aren't sufficiently numerous or prominent in the setting for the Immaculate Order to know of them, I don't think. All three of them could easily be found to be Anathema, and it wouldn't be wrong, but some of them might be sympathetic to even the most devout Immaculate.
