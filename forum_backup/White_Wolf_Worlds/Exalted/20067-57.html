        so Shifting Ghost Clay Path is a set of ghost arcanoi that allows the ghost to reshape their body. in it is the arcanos Weighted With The Anchor of Flesh which allows them to take on a material form, much like the spirit charm Materialize. so heres a few questions:
        A) could a ghost using these charms shape themselves into a weapon then become material that another character could then use?
        B) would hitting an enemy whilst in a weapon-like shape be enough to affect the enemy with charms the ghost knows, ranging from touch based TMA to other combat charms?
        C) could said ghost also become a mount for another character if they had already come up with that shape to change on the fly? and could a ghost become a flying mount with Ghost-Devil Form?

        I would say, Yes, Yes, and Yes, absolutely. Heroic Ghosts that have spent years-centuries perfecting these moves can and should be terrifying opponents, doing things such as forming armor over each other, or merging into a well-coordinated gestalt being (probably wiht the help of that mind-reading charm).

        As to MA, the last word is in Scroll of Errata, pg 191. While it could be read to allow new MA charms, the lack of a cost for them indicates that Wizard is correct, and that they can't learn them (Deathlords, as always, are a unique case). Personally, I'd ignore this, because Heroic Ghosts are just more interesting if they can. I'd allow it, but make it really hard, even more difficult than learning their own charms, *unless* the style is Death-aspected, like Gentle Caress; Deathknights should be able to train their followers to be kung-fu ghosts (maybe only they can teach ghosts?). I'm also pretty sure there's some fluff somewhere about ghosts learning new styles after death, but I could be wrong...
