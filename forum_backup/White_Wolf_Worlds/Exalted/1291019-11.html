                        Originally posted by DrLoveMonkey View Post

                        I think it's probably worth saying in context to the thread. Clownpiece was saying that one of the narrative purpose of the Solar Exalted is to return and tip the balance of the world to either salvation or destruction. They do this because their power is so much greater than anyone else's in the setting at the moment. Just look at Solar Circle Sorcery as an example. Since it is totally unfair that sorcerers are the only people who get to have that kind of impact and everyone else is a chump, Dawns must be similarly more powerful, and the combat rules should support this. That's why Solars need to be as much better at fighting as Ligier is more powerful than Octavian.
                        So, you see, the ability to summon Ligier should not be the measuring stick of Solar power. The rest of Solar Sorcery isn't even that much more powerful than Celestial. Rain of Doom can destroy a city but Cantana of Empty Voices can kill everyone inside it so it's not like an insane step up in power.

                        But that's actually okay, because Solars don't NEED to be able to summon Ligier to tip the scales. They are more powerful fighters than Full Moons, better sorcerers than No Moons, etcetera, but not by an insane degree, and certainly not more powerful than a lot of current Lunars. Leviathan would roll any Essence 1 Dawn. That's okay though, the introduction of 60 Dawn/Dusk/Slayers into the world is a huge deal anyway. The scale is ripe to be tipped in any direction, and even the introduction of 60 new Sidereals or Lunars would do it, but Solars are even more powerful than that!
                        Clownpiece's argument does not seems to be about what kind of change any solar will bring in short term after reappearing but about the way as a player you feel being a solar PC makes you someone who changes the context through your solar particularity. Pointing this out about solar circle sorcery does not change that. And as you point it out, in fact, the kind of power he needs for the solars doesn't need to be that much more powerful, at least in short term use, for it to be felt as a solar player.
                        Another problem here is that I don't think that comparing solar powers against lunars' or whatever in a competitive way is what makes things matters the most in that issue rather than their way of being significant for each other and in stories (and maybe their setting too).

                        I'm not a huge fan of the solars being the most powerful things in the world to such an extent that nothing else than them matters and, according to what he said, it seems Clownpiece either. (I think that the dawns are not that bad either at telling solars' stories except maybe a dawn martial artist.) I also don't think that there is no other way to make solar martial artists matter than making of them the absolute best in a way nullifying the interest of any other exalt martial artist fighting against them. In fact I have the feeling that wordings like "much greater than", "much more powerful than" in non that quantitative measurable things are a little bit lacking when taken too litteraly and used to make points in such a competitive way about such complex effectivities.
                        I have the feeling that there is a "solars are the most awesome even for us but not capable to beat one of us head-on without really working a lot for it" thing in celestial exalts and it kind of express itself in a good way in combat solars charms. A good example is those about bigger closed intervals than any other exalt with maximum numerical outcomes being better than the others, but with such a sensitivity that it lets a huge amount of possibilities for any fight to become fair most of the time when fighting against any other celestial exalt equipped (intentionally or not) to fight against them while not impeding solars' ability to produce under some conditions the biggest outcomes. I have the feeling that this way of expressing solars' awesomeness mostly disappear when solars are practicing martial arts, the mastery effects in known martial arts not having this thing enough and being too often lacking. It gives off a feeling of "what's the point of a solar martial artist then" when compared to the other celestial exalts.

                        I hope it's readable again, I wrote it without any help this time.

                                    I really, really like this edition's Lunars, they are appropriately powerful, but honestly, I truly can not understand how anyone thinks Solars are still not a whole tier more powerful than them.

                                    Solar Circle Sorcery is not just marginally more powerful. The step up from Celestial to Solar is huge. From the workings to the spells, really, there's no comparing them, and yes, Lunars can do Solar workings, but the speed difference matters. When it comes to combat Solars just have so, so, so many ways of reverting the game back to a sustainable perfect or die or just plain making a mockery out of the possibility of defeat. Lunars simply can't reach that level, and we've seen enough of their charms now that I feel pretty confident making that statement. I've seen several Solars played who would be more than comfortable facing a Wyld Hunt on their own, and rightly so.

                                    Not all, but some of their Charms sets also have a scale that Lunars simply can't attain. Lore destroys cities. If you are a Spirit, no matter how powerful, Occult simply owns you, Bureaucracy can take over any organization and forget about returning the favor, Socialize goes to downright scary places and arguably lunars better than Lunars if you keep to human forms, etc. The Solars are still the undisputed leaders of the pack, and I've seen in actual play how blisteringly fast a Solar Circle can transform some backwater into a regional superpower. The tools for that kind of dominance I simply can't see in the Lunar charms.
