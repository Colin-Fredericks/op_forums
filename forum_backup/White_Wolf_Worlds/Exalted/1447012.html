    Solar Lore Revamp

    05-02-2021, 02:42 PM
    Lore is now redone.

    Lore, like some other abilities, suffered from poor wording and definitions. I’ve tightened up the meanings behind fact rolls and knowledge rolls and gave them a list of sample difficulties and penalties. I’ve also clarified what “common” knowledge a character typically knows, guidelines of how specialties can work in your game, and how one can retry a failed knowledge check.

    Charm wise there are a total of 23 charms in this rewrite, just a little under half of the 47 in the original book. A lot of dice tricks were pruned and replaced with charms that enable more horizontal rather than vertical power and are brought into line with current trends for the game line (I.E. teaching charms). Wyld-Shaping is now two entire charms for example.

    Lastly, for modern or sci-fi games, I’ve included a collection of Computer related charms. They can work with attached subsystem, or just have many of them work with a far more simplistic system “roll to see if you pass/fail”.

    https://docs.google.com/document/d/1iY9UOE8tC2qmM10kBfAhy6fHI-n3nq_DaBEs6z1se6Y/edit
