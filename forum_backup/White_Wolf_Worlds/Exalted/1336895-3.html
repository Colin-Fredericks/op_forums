    I have an idea for a gourd. Like Suika's endless gourd of wine.

    At lower levels, it simply gives good wine. Resource 4 worth of any kind of liquid inside of it, whether its Fire Beer, Honeywine, or any other kind of valuable alcohol. Maybe its water, or maybe its delicious soup (ignore how the small spout can spew out meat chunks). Either way, delicious, tasty, and pretty useful in any situation where supplies are hard to get.

    At higher levels things get funky. The stuff it spits out turns magical. Water you drink from it lets you go for days without drinking again. Good, if bog-standard alcohol turns into Celestial Wine. You can spew out alchemical products using this. You can use this gourd to throw out water-bending attacks, like using it to create oil slicks or sprays of deadly caustic poison.

    At even higher levels things go crazy. Your gourd can supply an entire city with wine, synchronising with every single wine cup to make sure that they never go empty, letting the entire city party for days on end. Your gourd doesn't just spit out deadly burning oil or wine that the gods will fight to drink; it can also spit out storm clouds, hurricanes, or sandstorms, capable of turning armies, cities, and entire kingdoms to deadly ash as you drink and make merry atop of their charred corpses.

            Wilderness-Progeny Dragon Prana
            Cost: 1m, 1a, 1wp; Mins: Survival 5, Essence 3
            Type: Simple
            Keywords: None
            Duration: (Essence + Wits) days
            Prerequisite Charms: Mother-Of-Beasts Mastery

            The Dragon-Blood draws upon the potent forces that course through Creation's wilderness, weaving raw essence into the form of a bestial elemental servant to act as a companion, mount or guide.

            This charm functions in identical fashion to Dragon's Egg Hatched (Heirs of the Shogunate, pp. XX), with the exception that the base traits of the created elemental are either those of a wolf, horse or raiton (Exalted, pp. 562, 567, 570).

            Dragon-Elemental Union Meditation
            Cost: –; Mins: Survival 5, Occult 3, Essence 3
            Type: Permanent
            Keywords: None
            Duration: Permanent
            Prerequisite Charms: Wilderness-Progeny Dragon Prana, Elemental Bond Meditation

            The Dragon-Blood partitions a portion of their own soul and infuses it into their chosen elemental servant, magnifying their bond and allowing them – with practise – to fashion their creation into increasingly potent forms.

            The Dragon-Blood can now train one elemental created by Wilderness-Progeny Dragon Prana. This elemental must always be of the same base type and element and carry over its personality, memories and Intimacies whenever it is created, although its charm-granted mutations can vary to reflect the specific elemental manifestation it has been created from each time.

            Training functions as described in Exalted, pp. 554 with the additional caveats that the training roll is difficulty 3 and requires a specialty in Occult (Elementals) (however no Survival specialties are needed), no bonus dice are granted for intimacies and the elemental must be created in order to receive training – any successes carry over each time the elemental is created (albeit fading at the usual rate if the elemental is not trained again within three days). Charms can be used to enhance training as normal. The elemental can be trained in the latent or magical special attacks or merits of its base type (wolf, horse or raiton).

            The cost to create the trained elemental is increased to 5m, 1a, 1 wp, while the distance such an elemental can travel from the Dragon-Blood is increased to (Dragon-Blood’s Essence x 10). Should their trained elemental be destroyed the Dragon-Blood suffers one automatic health level of bashing damage and cannot create the elemental in question again for one week.

            A Dragon-Blood can undergo a cleansing ritual – perhaps a day’s meditation in the presence of a suitable expression of the relevant element, such as a waterfall or mountain range – to purge the elemental from their soul. After this ritual the Dragon-Blood relinquishes the knowledge of how to create the trained elemental and can begin again with a new one. Any experience invested in the training of the original elemental is refunded. For example, a Dragon-Blood begins to train a ‘wood wolf’ elemental she has created. She cannot train any other type of created elemental unless she undertakes a cleansing ritual, at which point all experience points invested in training this elemental are refunded. After the ritual she can no longer create the trained wood wolf elemental but she can begin to train another elemental of any base type and element.

                                            A spear according to Japanese Folklore. At first pretty normal looking, with some pretty good stats. Then you eventually gain evocations. First evocations lets you unleash an AoE effect upon anything you strike, basically releasing an explosion of energy. Higher evocations let you manipulate the environment around you, raising great walls of rock or turning entire sections of the area into swirling whirlpools of quicksand. And at the highest levels, you gain the power equivalent to wyld-shaping. By stabbing your spear into the ground and rotating it, you can transform the area around you in miles into the border marches middle marches, or the wyld, and do the reverse, turning sea into earth.

                                            SWLIHN planning charm:
                                            Essence 3, Bureaucracy 4

                                            A charm that encompasses all forms of pattern, cooperation, and hierarchy. The Infernal takes notice of all possible variables, putting them into practice, and then putting in the optimal path to victory, using her own understanding and logic. This supplements any wide-scale activity involving great masses of people, whether its the creating of a manse, evacuating an entire city, setting up a boot camp, or drafting in new tax law. Following the Infernal's plans, the speed and efficacy of the activity becomes superlative. The time taken to complete it is reduced by an order of magnitude, work that should take years taking months, work taking months taking weeks, and work that takes weeks takes days instead to complete.

                                                Written for a character of mine, a sorceress who tried to fake being a Dragonblooded:

                                                HESIESH’S GIFT OF DIVINITY
                                                Cost                                                : 15sm, 1 wp
                                                Keywords: None
                                                Duration: Scene

                                                The sorcerer calls upon the great Immaculate Dragon of fire to cloak herself with raging flames, granting an assortment of benefits:
                                                • She gains immunity to mundane heat hazards
                                                • She may add her Essence to her soak and hardness against fire attacks.
                                                • Her unarmed strikes gain the lethal and flame tags, and deal additional damage equal to her Essence.
                                                • She may add her Essence to the damage she inflicts with mundane and magical fire attacks. This bonus is already included in the fire damage from Hesiesh’s Gift.
                                                • When a character begins their turn in short range of the sorcerer, they take [1/2 Essence, rounded up] points of initiative damage from the erupting flames. If they are in initiative crash, they instead take 1 level of lethal damage.

                                                A sorcerer who knows Hesiesh’s Gift as her control spell adds two non-charm dice to their rolls against mundane heat hazards even when the spell is not active. When it is, she may choose whether or not characters in short range are affected by the aura.

                                                Distortion (Goal Number: 15): Distorting Hesieh’s Gift of Divinity causes the sorcerer to be consumed by flames. A bondfire hazard appears at their current position, affecting all targets within short range.


                                                And for a player who wanted to lean into the dance-fighting idea of Battle Dancer Method a bit more:

                                                DANCE OF FLASHING BLADES
                                                Cost: 5m, 1wp; Mins: Performance 5, Essence 2
                                                Type: Simple
                                                Keywords: Uniform
                                                Duration: One Scene
                                                Prerequisite Charms: Battle-Dancer Method

                                                The Solar makes a ballroom of the battlefield, incorporating the rhythm of steel-against-steel into her dance. As long as she remains on her feet and dancing, she may use her Performance in place of Dodge when calculating Evasion and when taking any combat action. If she makes a withering or decisive attack that overcomes her target’s Resolve as well as their Evasion or Parry, they suffer a one success penalty when resisting her social influence for the duration of the scene.

                                                Performing a Dance of Flashing Blades counts as dancing for the purposes any charms which require the Solar to be dancing, such as Battle Dancer Method or Shining Expression Style. If she is knocked prone at any point while dancing, the effects of this charm end.

                                                UNBROKEN MARTIAL PERFORMANCE
                                                Cost: 2m; Mins: Performance 4, Essence 2
                                                Type: Reflexive
                                                Keywords: None
                                                Duration: Instant
                                                Prerequisite Charms: Respect-Commanding Attitude

                                                The Solar is grace and motion incarnate, her every movement a symphony for the eyes. When flurrying a Performance-based social influence action with another action, she may activate this charm to negate the usual flurry penalties.

                                                            Originally posted by Accelerator View Post
                                                            DEADLY BALLET SWORD SWING
                                                            Essence 2, Performance 3
                                                            Cost: 4m
                                                            Duration: 1 scene
                                                            Keywords: Reflexive

                                                            The Dragonblooded are consummate performers, and where the elements dwell, there can be threatre. Even in the field of battle. This must be activated before combat. After this, the battle counts as one scene of using performance to convince onlookers of a single minor intimacy. This can be things like 'Bright Flowing Snowflake is the most beautiful and graceful woman of all' to 'Flaming Stone is a pathetic, cowardly scoundrel'. However, the intimacy must have something to do with the situation at hand and the performers. A brawl in a bar in the West will let the dragonblooded create intimacies of fear, respect or awe towards him, but will not let him instill intimacies of 'despise the realm'. Conversely, a duel in public with a Dragonblooded of the Realm will let him instill contempt in all citizenry who witness the duel.
                                                            Okay... but why?

                                                            I'd rather take one measly action, or flurry, to take an Instill action than be 4m down for an entire fight. Heck, why can't I win the bar brawl, then use Performance to Instill an Intimacy of Fear on the observers by growling at them? Or win the duel then give a speech on the evils of empire?

                                                            This Charm is "pay 4m COMMITTED to avoid the Flurry penalty for circumstances where you don't have to Flurry anyway".

                                                            I'd drop the restriction on it only being to Instill a new Minor Intimacy (to let each fight count as new evidence towards stronger Intimacies), and have it effect your foes (win or lose) as well as bystanders. Either drop the logical connection (so your bar Brawl can inspire people to topple the Realm) or have it Instill the Intimacy automatically without a roll. You could probably stand to also let applicable Performance Specialties (i.e. "Acting") apply to combat rolls whilst the Charm is in effect, so the committed motes do something for you.

                                                            It's a really good idea for a Charm. It's clearly based in enhancing the mundane with supranatural powers. Good thematics. Just needs a little more punch to be worth the XP/motes.
