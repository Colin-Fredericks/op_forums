    [PEACH] The Great Solar Exalted Tournament

    05-02-2016, 11:20 PM
    Preface: for fun, I got together with one of my players and built two solars for the sole purpose of fighting each other and slugging it out till one fell. It was super fun, though a little disappointing that one came out on top each and every time with seemingly little effort. This tournament is an extension of that exercise as an idea I had while under the influence of Alcohol* and may have made certain promises to certain players that is see it through to the end.

    *We here at T.G.S.E.T. advise to Drink Responsibly

    Concept: 26 solar warriors gather together in the 'Austere and Glorious Arena of the Fifth Hand' each championing a different ability to the extreme of hyper-focus and putting them against each other's an elimination-style ladder tournament until there stands only one winner at the end who will be crowned "Most OP Build"

    Rules: Each Solar contestant is allocated a single ability which becomes their Supernal (relevant castes required). They follow the core book character creation process with only two exceptions.
    - No Charms purchased can be from an ability other than the one assigned to you (which also excludes Evocations)
    - No merits may provide an asset your ability cannot take advantage of directly (eg; Familiar for Survival Solar and Command for War Solar)

    To levy a fair and impartial battle, any abilities which require extreme situational environments take precedence over those that do not (Sail will always have a watery arena to battle within). Otherwise environments are allocated randomly from a set list.

    Method of Running: All battles will be run live at my gaming table, with one (maybe two) battles each week with the results and Play-by-Play being posted here for the forum to evaluate and dissect to their hearts content. If someone is interested in giving this a swing, send me a PM or post in this thread and I'll contact you. Games are run at +10GMT Melbourne Time so scheduling can be an issue for our international friends - you will be dialed in via Skype/Steam/ whatever voice chat you have access to.

    If anyone wants to submit a build, feel free.

    Disclaimer: I am in under no illusion as to the utter unfairness of pairing certain abilities against each other, but the purpose of the exercise is to see what abilities bring what elements to battle and to have heaps of fun doing it. Besides, the great and powerful Gee Says, God of Random Numbers will personally oversee all match ups and ensure no priority is given to to any particular ability.


    Let the Games Begin.

    Brawl VS Melee

      Brawl, Melee, and Martial Arts are all definitely strong contenders, since they're chock-full of combat Charms that are useful for both attack and defense.

              Okay so, no one has put their hands up for Melee so I'll handle it, but one of my players is a Brawl fanatic so he'll be handling the opponent.

              First off; we're probably going to get quite cheesy with our builds given that they are not intended to be long-game sustainable builds, so expect min-maxing.

              Secondly; I didn't mention that even with the merit rule, I'm not banning artifact arms and armour otherwise we'll probably see a bunch of OHKO which is less dramatic and fun. The trade off of course is the mote limitation which, as a starting character we already don't have much of for sustained combat.

              With that said, let's meet our first contestant.


              Cathak Katsuko (Melee Supernal, Dawn Caste, Solar Exalted)
              All the way from the Blessed Isle, Cathak Katsuko was a prodigy from the House of Bells who never exalted and at the age of twenty joined the Vermilion Legion to prove her righteousness for the next life. Little did she realize she's prove her worth in all the wrong ways. Touched by the Unconquered Sun after leading her brotherhood to a pyrrhic victory against a Raksha incursion in the northern isles she stands before us today as the champion of Melee Combat.

              Build: We're going to be focusing on the basic Melee multi-attack strategy here. The strength of this is we don't really mind how we engage, *something* sets off a chain of attacks and so landing those hits is more important than the individual damage they deal. Because we are a flexible ability, we can afford to load so heavily on offensive power because it doubles as defense and we have a quaint get-out-of-jail-for-a-small-fee card in Heavenly Guardian Defense.

              As long as we can hit with withering attacks or withering counters, we should be able to keep up a decent initiative pool to defend any such attack at our leisure. Part of this is starting with as hefty a pool as possible. Our reliance on DEX will factor well into combat movement rolls as well, making it hard to break away. One weakness is no charm support for snap movements, if a ranger attacker can pull away, we are in a seriously dangerous position... But there is a charm for that too.

              Attributes: We're going full-on twink here and dumping physical as primary, social as tertiary. I'm leaving Int and Per at 2 simply because I can't stand to have an Int 1 character... for some reason.
              Str 3, Dex 5, Sta 3; Cha 3, Man 1, App 3; Per 2, Int 2, Wit 5

              Abilities: Numbers in brackets are points bought with bonus points. Took all the important bits for combat and a smattering of one dot skills to support background (cause I felt like I had to).
              Melee 3 (+2) - Specialty: Swords; Awareness 3 (+1) - Specialty: Strike First; Socialize 3; Brawl 3; Dodge 3 - Specialty: Disengage; Athletics 3 - Specialty: Rush; Resistance 3; Integrity 3; Linguistics 1; Lore 1; Bureaucracy 1; War 1

              Merits: Medium weapon for superior defense, I went for heavy armour solely for the hardness (though the soak is nice too) and offset the mobility penalty with fleet of foot and my specialties. That's 11 motes committed though, ouch. This build initially had two daiklaives for the clash bonus but the motes lost were not worth it. Splurged on two WP to fuel my best charms.
              Artifact 3 (Red Jade Daiklaive)
              Artifact 3 (Red Jade Reinforced Breastplate)
              Fleet of Foot 4
              Willpower 5 (+2)

              Now the meat-
              Charms: I bracketed ones I felt I could switch out. I'm uncertain if I should completely forgo the 'attack more people at once' charms because it might come in handy for later battles. Call the blade is purely for the daring disarm gambit and I feel a little too necessary to sub out, it's bracketed because it's such an edge case but disarming the Melee Supernal negates their entire kit so... Yeah... I considered grabbing Iron Raptor to deal with ranged attackers but I don't want to put myself in a situation where I've reset to base and haven't killed them while they are at medium range or more. I feel I would be crashed and killed soon after. I have forgone the defend-other branch as it is useless for this particular scenario.
              Dipping Swallow Defense; Bulwark Stance; Excellent Strike; Fire and Stones Strike; One Weapon, Two Blows; (Peony Blossom Technique); Solar Counterattack; (Call the Blade); (Rising Sun Slash); Iron Whirlwind Attack; Five-Fold Bulwark Stance; Heavenly Guardian Defense; Hail-Shattering Practice; Invincible Fury of the Dawn; Perfect Strike Discipline; Flashing Edge of Dawn; Fervent Blow

              General Strategy: I'll be looking to mostly just not get hit. Most of the kit is built around hard offense with a smattering of defensive charms. I have two avenues, if I hit first I can open up with many multiple attacks and if I don't hit first, the counterattack tree sets off the reflexive hurricane. I grabbed HGD to burn away those few attacks that might get through and threaten to deal some real damage. Combat movement pools are at a comfortable size as the penalties have already been accounted for.

              If I end up tweaking anything before Saturday's match, I'll let you know. Tomorrow I should have up the Brawler's Build.
