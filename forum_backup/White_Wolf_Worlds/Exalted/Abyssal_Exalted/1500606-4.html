                                                    Presence has sexy vampire Charms, so that's nice. I like that the old "more pretty or horrifying with rising Essence" still exists in the form of Death's Unholy Visage.

                                                    Night-Horror Summons is weird; I like it.

                                                    Melee is worth it simply for Soul-Drinking Blade.

                                                    Occult, however, is a big disappointment. I like the Whispers-boosting effects of Howling Silence Meditation, and the telepathy Charms, but otherwise it is largely a retread of Solar Occult. The necromancy Charms likewise just feel like buffed versions of the Lunar sorcery Charms, rather than adding any unique capabilities for the Abyssal necromancer; hopefully, War has something to net you more zombies when you raise them by any means.
                                                    I'm also mildly confused that there's a mirror to Soul Projection Method, but nothing comparable to Ephemeral Induction Technique. Don't get me wrong, I like Soul Projection/Domination Method, but I like EIT more, and I don't think the Cantata of Empty Voice-alike of Life-Annihilating Susurrus can compare.
                                                    Actually, you know what would be cool? Something to build on Spirit-Devouring Appetite to make like the Bishop and turn into a whole freakish ghost-beast from the spirits you've devoured.

                                                    The fleshcrafting in Medicine, other than Necrotic Graft Technique, is really underwhelming. 5m for a E3 Charm to make a zombie, when a Midnight and anyone with Barrow-King's Authority can do it instantly for 1m? Essence 5 to make a zombie cool?
