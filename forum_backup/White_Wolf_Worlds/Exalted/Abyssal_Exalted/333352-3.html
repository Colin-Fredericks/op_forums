                            In kortekore's defense, I am infamous in every game I play for racking up unintentional collateral damage. The whole "nuking an entire metropolis" thing was very much avoidable, I just made one or two fatal tactical mistakes...

                            Zelbinnean: It sounds like we both agree that serious mistakes were made in binding and gagging abyssal character themes to the notion of "Deathlord's whipping boy." I still want Abyssal characters to face sacrifices in game, but I want those sacrifices to be varied and not always rooted in "evil king NPC is feeling spiteful today." If you took away the Deathlords entirely (or just wrote them better) then most of what you're saying bothers you with regards to Resonance goes away, I think. I see a lot of problems with MoEP:A in that regard as well. So maybe we are actually on a similar page there. Unfortunately, fixing the Deathlords is a massive undertaking such that I don't think we can really address it in this thread. My fingers are crossed Onyx Path will pull it off in Ex3 though.

                            Xanroth: I'm coming around to the idea of a game where you are part of your Deathlord's running joke, but I still don't like how few alternatives to that remain in Ex2 for playing renegades. As written any time you and a Deathlord square off and they feel like being even a little bit serious you should die by rights, and the abyssal exaltation is just too valuable a thing to ignore forever. At that point your character's survival hinges on seeming "cute" to your Deathlord which is kind of not that satisfying when you know that if you ever do manage to severely hurt your nemesis the immediate result is probably certain death. This actually comes back to the earlier criticism of my own idea: it's not a very fun game when "winning" equates to "can't play that character anymore."

                            Astralporing: Can you post page numbers referencing these rules on "some touch effects" and especially about the monstrance shattering if the exalt dies? That really does not sound right to me, to be honest. I was under the firm impression that the monstrance was where the abyssal exaltation auto-parked when it wasn't in use.

                            General: As much as the Deathlords are imbalanced v Abyssals they are somewhat balanced on the "us versus Creation" front by the everything else that would resist them. Like I'm willing to believe that the collected might of Creation could perma-kill these guys as written in Ex2, if motivated to do so. The thing is that they are smart so they haven't made any half-hearted attempts to follow through on that plan which would bump them up to the top of several Incarna's "kill now" list. Like the last time they gave it a serious go was the Great Contagion which damn near worked and only didn't because they got in each other's way. Since then they've gone back to the proverbial drawing board so we probably won't see another attempt for a couple centuries or so in canon.

                                    Originally posted by kindledRadial View Post
                                    Astralporing: Can you post page numbers referencing these rules on "some touch effects" and especially about the monstrance shattering if the exalt dies? That really does not sound right to me, to be honest. I was under the firm impression that the monstrance was where the abyssal exaltation auto-parked when it wasn't in use.
                                    It's not "some touch effects". It's "some charms" with no further explanation, and the only charm i know having it flat out specified in the charm description. And it's right in the Monstrance's writeup (page 117 of MoEP: Abyssals).

                                    "Arcane Link: Certain Abyssal Charms (and all necromancy spells) treat a deathknight’s Monstrance as if it were a physical extension of her body, transmitting the effects of the magic directly to her. Deathlords use this dread mystical correspondence to communicate with and punish their deathknights, and to appear in visions to candidates who have not yet chosen whether to die or accept Exaltation. If such effects kill an Abyssal, the cage shatters and the freed Exaltation must be summoned to a new Monstrance. The possibility that another Deathlord might poach the Exaltation, however, usually deters such extreme solutions to treachery."

                                    See as it does not specify any class of charms - only say that some of them have that ability. And it uses a known term ("arcane link") that has a specific meaning when socery and necromancy are concerned (and that meaning is not "you can cast any spell through it as if the target was there").

                                    Also, to "summon an exaltation to a new Monstrance" you need to capture that exaltation first. This generally means capturing (alive) its current bearer and placing physically within the Monstrance.

                                                            Astralporing: Thanks for the reference I looked it up myself and you're right, using a monstrance to kill an exalt directly does ruin the monstrance. I would say though that this is very little deterrent for a Deathlord committed to the task, since they can still use an active monstrance to track an abyssal and kill them through any indirect means (summoned horrors, social attacks lined with suicidal orders, even disease effects, arguably) and the exaltation will zip right back to the coffin. So it adds a step, basically, but again if you ever really offend your specter patron you are looking at a pretty immediate death sentence. You might have a few days to gather your allies and plan a raid to destroy your monstrance but it's still a necessary step.

                                                            General: The actual wording is that such charms and all necromancy spells treat the monstrance "as if it were a physical extension of her body, transmitting the effects of the magic directly to her." So Pestilence-Bearing Touch still works, since if you affect one part of an exalt's body with the great contagion it will spread to the rest of her, for example. By that same token I would say things that hurt the monstrance still probably hurt you, at the very least insofar as carrying over wound penalties form being on fire on one part of your body. Honestly thereis some room for interpretation here but not much and it doesn't in any way stop them from crippling you creatively and indefinitely.
