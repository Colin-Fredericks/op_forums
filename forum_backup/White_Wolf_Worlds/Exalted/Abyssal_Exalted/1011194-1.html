                                    Hrm. Well, first this is just the opinion of some random dude on the internet, and I mean no disrespect to you or your game. But IMHO you're looking at this too superficially.

                                    Personally, I always thought that Exalted being the "original history" of the WoD was one of those internet myths that ran amok. Granted, we could see a fair bit of crossover from W;tA for the Lunar, but then again White Wolf's handling of the Lunar was shall we say in a modest effort at civility, ham fisted and clumsy. And yes, Abyssal's muck about with blood sacrifice and essence draining, but its only similar in the sense that frog legs taste like chicken.

                                    The Abyssals were not designed to be the vampires of Exalted, that line of thinking had its roots in some misguided fanboism of the vampire line of product. If anything Abyssals in a WoD relation was from the Wraith line. No seriously, it was. says so right in the front of the 1st edition book which I still own, it attributes most of it's inspiration to Wraith which the author felt was a "vastly underrated game" (I'll spare you my opinion on that matter). The only reason why blood was a thing, was because it was a form of sustenance to all the denizens of the Underworld, and the Abyssals were created under such vastly different conditions that any (IMHO) similarity between them is on the order of apples and walnuts. Case in point, Vampires have a say in creating their ersatz progeny and its been that way since Cain first got lonely way back when. Abyssals have no say in who inherits their divine shard, not even the Unconqured Sun has a say in it, they're a force of nature that seeks out a host by some set of metaphysics that is firmly seated in the concept of reincarnation, not familial inheritance.

                                    To be consistent you'd also have to answer the Lunar/Garou equation. Garou have kinfolk and both have genelogical purity ratings to breed future Garou because its a genetic inheritance. A Lunar, just like a Solar, has a divine shard that seeks out a host that "is worthy". No genetics involved in the slightest bit. A Lunar can have 1000s of kids in their life time, and not a one of them becomes a Lunar because its all about the divine shard baby, not the little squiggly guy having a certain extra gene. Further, Lunar (1/2E admittedly, we don't know about 3E yet) gain the shapes of those who's hearts they ate. Garou don't. Can a Garou now eat the heart of a person and change into them? What about a Garou eating the heart of a Bastet? Can he now go AC/DC wolf/cat themes? How about Mages? Can they absorb the life force of another mage who mind you, is on a path of enlightened self discovery, and get a power up that way?

                                    But sticking with just vampires and Abysals. Abyssals are corrupted Solar shards, so suggesting a Vampire could drain a Abyssal and get anything other than a drug rush is to suggest they could do the same thing to Solars, Lunars, Sidereals, Exigents and Liminals. Doesn't add up.

                                    Personally, I never felt compelled to say that EX and WoD are the same continuity, they're each their own thing, with such vastly different metaphysics that trying to fuse them would (IMHO) result in a mess. But if I did, because a Abyssal is what he is, some fang succeeding at bleeding a Abyssal dry would only get a very powerful blood pool in his system, I'd let him use just 1 blood point for fueling disciplines at their maximum, or that they're at a +1 rating in potency, healing aggravated damage per day equal to his stamina, stuff like that. And he'd have a addiction for his troubles that he'd have to come to grips with or feed. A warlock would have some damn potent blood to do some pretty amazing rituals with and that's without drinking it himself as they'd be aware of the inherit dangers.

                                    In short, as Egon says, "Don't cross the streams, bad things happen."


                                    Reality is a matter of perception, challenging one's own or that of others is not a crime, it is a service.

                                        Chiming in on the point Seeker made about the pre-history, what becomes what, etc.

                                        The "Abyssals=Vampires, Solars=Imbued, Lunars=Werewolf" was strictly a design space thing that was mentioned in The Making of Exalted. Kind of a "If you like X, you'll probably dig Y."

                                        In Exalted-as-pre-history, the Abyssals didn't BECOME the vampires, they were the Age of Sorrows pulp fantasy splat that had a similar personal horror head-space. Hell, if you really want to get into a Timeline, Exalted pre-dates the Abrahamic God by an indeterminate amount of time, possibly an entire turn of the Cycle. Demon the Fallen really hard set a lot of the WoD's timeline. Vampires come from Caine, and did not exist prior.

                                        Assuming a vampire somehow actually managed to bite, drink, and drain an Abyssal, the most likely result would either be a finally dead Abyssal and the shard flying off to find a new host, or possibly just a really, really pissed Abyssal (depending on charm progression.) The vampire would likely be tripping balls and mad with power for a while, much like when they drink lupine, mage, changeling, etc blood, but nothing else. I'd argue that they couldn't even Embrace the Abyssal, since things like Risen, Mummies, etc cannot be embraced and an Abyssal is just a person who's death was stalled half way.
