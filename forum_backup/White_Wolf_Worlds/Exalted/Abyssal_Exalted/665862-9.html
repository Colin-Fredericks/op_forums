                                Originally posted by Erinys View Post
                                So now you're moving the goalposts to say that everything legal is morally good, and nobody should ever attempt to have a personal moral conscience. I reject this utterly.
                                You'll notice that not once have I said anything about "good" nor about morality.

                                Businesses exist to make profit. They don't exist to improve society or do "good" things. What is Walmart's goal? Is it to keep the environment safe? Is it to improve America's way of life? Is it to make sure that the people in a community are gainfully employed?

                                Of course not. Like any business, Walmart's goal is to make as much profit as possible, and everything they do is calculated towards making money. And businesses will do anything they can to raise profits. The only things they won't do are those things which have been made illegal. If slavery were legal in the US, and if Walmart thought it would be more profitable to employ slaves, that's exactly what they would do. The reason they don't is because the US government has made that illegal.

                                If Gerber (the company which produces baby food) discovered a way to double profits, but it would involve putting a tiny amount of poison into babyfood, and they knew with 100% certainty that it would never be traced back to them, and no one would ever blame them, it would be incredibly naive to believe that they wouldn't put that poison in baby food.

                                Companies are completely amoral and do whatever they can (legally, but also illegally if they think they won't get caught) to make the highest amount of profit. Once in awhile you'll come across a company that has other goals (such as Chick-Fil-A, which refuses to do business on Sunday's due to the owner's religious beliefs) but that is very, very much an exception to the rule.

                                Basically expecting companies to act in a moral manner of their own accord is hopelessly naive. That's why we have all kinds of laws to prevent businesses from abusing their workers or hurting the communities that they're based.

                                At the moment, it's not illegal to draw pictures of sexy women in America, and if businesses believe that by including pictures of sexy women with their product will improve profits or help them lock down market shares, that's what they're going to do. If you feel that drawing sexy pictures of women is morally wrong, then you should maybe contact your senator and try to pass a law making it illegal. Because that's the only way that businesses will ever stop doing it.

                                But that is at the core concept of "freedom" in western society. As long as something is legal, people will do it. Spitting in public might be gross and unsanitary, but unless it's illegal (and unless that law is enforced) people are going to spit in public. And ultimately I feel that it's better to have more individual freedom (like the ability to draw sexy women) than it is to make lots of things illegal because some people find it offensive or hurtful.

                                For the record though, as an artist myself (who has drawn a fair share of sexy men and women in the course of my life) there's nothing morally wrong with drawing sexy women. I don't feel like a horrible person for doing so. In fact, I reject the idea that drawings directly lead to an increase amounts of rape or that those drawings lead to men hurting women or that I should feel or share the blame of any man that goes out and commits rape.

                                That's because it's important to remember that it's not only guys drawing sexy women, it's also women drawing pictures of sexy women. Melissa Uran has drawn plenty of sexy or naked pictures of women in Exalted and other White Wolf products (and even posted some on the forums back in the day). Would you say that her drawings are encouraging rape? Should she stop drawing pictures of sexy women? When a man commits rape should she hold up her hand, take responsibility and apologize?
