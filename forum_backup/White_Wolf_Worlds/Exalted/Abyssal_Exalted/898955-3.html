                            What if the Abyssals were more directly a product of the Usurpation? The Underworld was created by the pseudo-death of the Primordials, the Usurpation was the single biggest instance of death and destruction since that time, what if the near-overnight death of so many Solar exalted had far more of a straining effect on reality than just 'lotsa Solar ghosts now roaming the underworld being ideal candidates to become Deathlords', and so many severed Exaltations flying around at once, with powerful ghosts all dropping into the Underworld, started getting tangled with each other and picking up threads of Underworld-aspected essence?

                            The Bronze Faction had their plan and all, Chejop was set to catch nearly all the Solar Essences in the Jade Prison (physical, a cut off destiny-loop, or whatever you want it to actually be) and keep them there indefinitely, but since Sidereals can't predict anything having to do with the Underworld, they didn't see how such a massive Solar die-off could affect that until it was already happening. Some attentive Sidereal noticed, and relayed to Chejop, "Hey, something's not quite right, the sudden death of all these Solars is doing something to their exaltations", and Chejop made the conscious choice to say, "Stick to the plan". Sidereal hubris prevented him from seeing that a Jade Prison specifically calibrated to contain Solar exaltations might run into problems containing exaltations that had been warped and tangled with threads of the underworld during the massacre, which would introduce weaknesses in the prison that might slowly grow over time, and get to the point where some knowledgeable, powerful being with a reason could break open the prison through those weaknesses.

                            It gives an added layer of emotion, as on some level Chejop has to realize he's the one ultimately responsible for the creation of the Abyssals. And in my personal headcanon, I like to think it was the Scarlet Empress who kicked open the prison, physical or not. For whatever reason, she was tired of Sidereal meddling, she knew of the prison through her close association with Chejop, and since she didn't suffer the Great Curse of hubris, she saw the weaknesses in the prison that he blinded himself to.
