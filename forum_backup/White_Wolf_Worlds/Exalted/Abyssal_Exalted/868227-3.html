                            Originally posted by Murcushio View Post
                            Maybe it should do that.

                            The Underworld should not, in my opinion, be a place you can just walk into for a day trip. Even if they aren't going to go the full "the fact that the Underworld exists at all is a fucked-up mistake created by the Exalted murdering things that shouldn't have been capable of being murdered" on it, it shouldn't be the kind of place that the quick, even the Exalted, can easily penetrate deep into.

                            Because the Underworld isn't for the quick. It's for the dead. There is no food for the quick. There is no water for the quick. The air and land itself oppresses them. The very essence of the place is dark and alien and doesn't nourish and sustain even an Exalt in the same way it does in Creation. If you're quick, and you want to go to, say, Stygia, that shouldn't be "it's a long trip, pack a lunch." That should be "this is a lengthy and terrifying journey through an alien realm that rejects you even as it tries to draw you in." It should be the kind of thing that even makes experienced Sijanese Funerists blanch away from you for suggesting it (that's the kind of journey they put off onto the dead members of their order, because trying it as one of the quick is a great way to become dead) and the sort of thing that even Lunars and Sidereals should react to by wanting to talk to one of the few specially trained experts in that kind of thing among their own ranks. (This is the reason dudes like Black Ice Shadow exist.)
                            This presentation isn't without its virtues, but it's wholly incompatible with making the underworld a place where I could run a campaign of any given Exalted, so it definitely has to be one or the other. For my money, making the danger of the underworld "you can't get the basic necessities of life or the things you need to power all the game's mechanics" is probably the least interesting way to go about it. You can, in fact, make the Underworld a deeply creepy, dangerous and unpleasant place for the living without making it a bad place to set a Lunars game, but it means prioritizing playability above aesthetic statements, as is done with all the other best-designed parts of the setting. Doing it that way also has the upside that you don't need to give Abyssals, who are alive, pages of baseline exceptions just to let them play in the location designed especially for them.

                            Here's the thing: if you want the underworld to be so deleterious that it's a harrowing journey even for a circle of Celestial Exalted to visit, then it's absurd for the underworld to be a world at all, because the game is entirely about the living. at that point you might as well have the entire Underworld just be the labyrinth and shadowlands. And, in fact, that's pretty close to what we got in previous editions, except that for every such place they felt obligated to add a thousand miles squared of bland Ghost Prairies full of plasmic animals (which are like regular animals, but spooky) and green peasants who have spent 1000 years of undeath pretending to farm turnips. The scale and potential of the underworld, when contrasted with its unusability as a campaign setting, is absurd, and sure enough, nobody uses the underworld as a campaign setting.

                            I bet the Malfean acid-sea Kimbery is full of ancient sunken cities and weird demonic races and stuff, and you can't ever go there because it's a sea made of acid, and if you did it would indeed be a harrowing "get in, get out" journey racing against the clock. But the game doesn't waste any space talking about that, because that's not a setting, it's at best an 0D&D dungeon crawl. We already have the Labyrinth for that; dedicating the whole underworld to the same purpose would be wasting it.
