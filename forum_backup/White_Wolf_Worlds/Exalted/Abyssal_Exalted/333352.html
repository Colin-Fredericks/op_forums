    3e Abyssals: Exalted Hauntings

    12-07-2014, 05:02 PM
    Here's what I'd really like for third edition Abyssals:

    Deathlords cannot directly influence the exaltation process to choose hosts for every exaltation. This has always been an extremely limiting setting point which ties abyssal motivations directly to the whims of narrowly-focused entities and has NOT been productive for expanding the range of Abyssal character concepts.

    Treat Abyssal exaltation more like a powerful, horrifying alternative to lingering undeath as a ghost. When a mortal dies with unfinished business they might come back as a ghost under their own power or, if their motivation is sufficiently epic enough to impress a corrupted solar exaltation, they might be granted a new lease on life and powers unimaginable so as to fulfill their unfinished purpose. Once that purpose is fulfilled though the contract ends and the exaltation abandons its host-soul to the waters of lethe, or hurls them into oblivion if their goal becomes impossible to achieve.

    Resonance in this case does not come from the Deathlords or the Neverborn but rather from the exaltation itself as punishment to those souls who try and forestall their death by putting off the business the exaltation reanimated them to conclude. Eventually they MUST achieve their goal and in achieving it die finally and permanently. The core principle of destruction is achieved by turning inward, towards the abyssal's own inevitable demise and their struggle between the desire to preserve their life and to achieve their goals.

    I think that about covers it. I'd like opinions if possible. Who would play this? Who sees problems with it? Criticisms welcome especially if you feel I'm missing the point of this splat.

The book plainly states that Resonance is an incurable, innate part of the Black Exaltation itself - it can't be an Abyssal Exaltation without Resonance by its very nature.

          My complaint about 2e abyssals is that ALL of them are tied to monstrances and thus freely manipulated/tortured/killed by E10 Solar-Abyssal hybrids with no conscience and no real need to retain their servants' free will. This is in and of itself an interesting idea that I want kept, but saying that it is true of the entire splat is limiting. So to clarify, I would want both to be valid character choices.

          Resonance IS an innate feature of the abyssal exaltation but as was commented in I believe Shards of the Exalted Dream, which gave alternative resonance rules which were less "kill everything," the Neverborn have direct control over it in 2e canon when acting through an abyssal's monstrance. This means that all abyssals with a monstrance (which again, are all abyssals in 2e canon) are JUST as much slaves to the neverborn as they are to whatever truer or baser concepts are represented by the "dark fate." Again, not a thing I want unwritten, but definitely something I would like a workable alternative to in game.

          I don't think the two ideas are mutually exclusive. Maybe the Deathlords botched the operation to seal away all the Abyssal exaltations and are scrambling to collect them all. Maybe some abyssals willingly submit to Deathlords and the neverborn as a way to prolong their lives and put off their inevitable demise after achieving their goals. Maybe some of them just want soul steel pantaloons and they signed the dotted line in blood and now they have buyer's remorse. Point is, I want more, not less.
