The effect is generally dependant on the spell in question, but is not so simple as "cast magic at arcane link, target gets affected".

            "ARCANE LINKS
            Hair, blood, excrement and bits of flesh possess a mystic connection to the person they came from. So do favored tools, beloved dolls, well-worn clothing and other possessions, as well as relatives or lovers. Some spells require this “arcane link” to affect their target at all. More importantly, arcane links enable some spells to affect targets at great distances, even if the spell caster
            doesn’t know the subject’s location. Most such spells require a specific arcane link, as described in the spell’s text, but some spells allow for any arcane link." (White and black treatise, page 6).

            Second, it mentions that only some charms can use Monstrance as arcane link. Which ones? It is not specified... but the charm description of Screaming in Silence (abyssal communications charm) and False Heart Mien (disguise charm) do mention that they can use Monstrance that way. Which imples that every charm that lacks that information cannot.

            So, no, if you were to use Pestilence-Bearing Touch on Monstrance, the effect would be the same as if you used it on any other block of metal - nothing.

                The important part is that the Monstrance can be used in any place where you could use a (cut-off) part of the Abyssal's body.

                Most spells that ask for an Arcane Link ask for "at least X, or better" - so, while a favourite tool might be good enough for a tracking spell, another spell might at least need a lock of hair. Some spells need actual bits of flesh.
                In general, a "better" Arcane Link can be used in place of a "weaker" one - so, you can replace a favourite tool or lock of hair with a bit of flesh, but not the other way around.
                The Monstrance here is on the same level as "bit of flesh", so it can nearly always be used as an Arcane Link - it always counts as a recently cut-out piece of flesh (which is pretty much as strong as an Arcane Link can get).

                But, in the end, it's still just an Arcane Link.

                            AstralPoring: First of all the words "interpretation" and "incorrect" don't belong together when we are discussing tabletop setting alterations.
