    EX3 Abyssal Charms

    06-26-2014, 01:34 PM
    I may have watched an episode of Hannibal.

    CRAFT

    Magnificent Shrike's Display
    This Charm enhances a creation of the Abyssal's, making it fascinating and frightful in equal measure. It haunts the dreams of those who experience it, causing a madness of the deathknight's choosing.
    The target of this Charm must be subtly or overtly morbid, but needn't be a traditional Craft project -- written works, corpses, and dances are all appropriate vectors of madness.

    SOCIALIZE

    Songbirds and Corpses Decorum
    Once activated, this Charm renders those in the Abyssal's presence incapable of fear, offense, and disgust, as long as he seems courteous and maintains hospitality. Freakish contortionist-dancers elicit polite applause from those affected, while meals of live insects and human flesh seem to them as exotic curiosities.
    Piercing the deathknight's veil of etiquette requires an appropriate Major Intimacy.

    SURVIVAL

    Thorns Like Heavy Chains
    Watering the earth with her blood, the deathknight creates patch of thorns and pale flowers. The affected area becomes difficult terrain, and those who blunder through it are affected by a somnolent poison.

    Thorns Like Long Knives
    An upgrade to Thorns Like Heavy Chains that allows the razor garden to fight like an immobile battle group. The Abyssal may use her Charms to enhance its attacks as though they were her own.

    Thorns Like Red Poppies
    With this Charm, the Abyssal may sleep among her summoned briars without harm, shielded from environmental effects and dreaming of nothing. She heals faster when doing so, and can rid herself of certain Intimacies in her slumber.

                  PERFORMANCE

                  Insidious Terror Narrative: The Princess Magnificent with Lips of Coral and Robes of Black Feathers learned from her humiliation at Great Forks. She has studied the art of tale weaving and perverted it to her own ends: disinformation, manipulation and terror delivered through a pleasing, desirable package.

                  After all, who doesn't love a good story?

                  Abyssal with this charm emulates the Black Heron by laying siege to the minds of their opponents through rumor, prophecy or story. The death knight tells a story foretelling some doom happening under some omen or portent. Those listening resist a social attack as normal, except if they fail they do not suffer the effects immediately, the story buries itself in their mind like a latent disease... and gives them urge to retell the story.

                  When they do, they use the same dice pool as the Abyssal who originally told the story. Even the most inept or introverted story teller will find themselves telling this wonderfully chilling campfire tale with the skill of a practiced master as friends crowd around to hear. Those who do also resist the story as a social attack, and, if they fail, become carriers as well.

                  If the story carriers see signs that the events of the story are true, or coming true, they immediately suffer the effect of the dormant social attack. If no such sign occurs with (abyssal's essence) days the attack harmlessly dissipates.

                  Ex: The Death Knight Secret (full name too long to be written here) needs to rob a heavily guarded vault-wagon being transported in a guild caravan. Infiltrating the night's campfire under the guise of a humble caravan flea, she tells them the story "Magistrate Bloody Fingers": a tale of particularly cruel and corrupt local judge who mutilated the hands of his victims in horrifying ways. He was eventually defeated by a band of heroic bandits, who threw the wicked judge's corpse into a bog on the night of a crimson moon. "But beware!" Secret says, "On nights when the red moonlight shines on the bog, he walks again! Hungry for victims!"

                  The tale is a thrilling one, alleviating the numbing boredom of the road, it is soon retold to every member of the Caravan. On the third night, when the caravan is camping next to a swamp, a red moon rises and a cry rings out "A hand! Someone nailed a hand to a tree!" Guards rush to investigate, the priest begins reciting terrified prayers to the caravan god, and the caravan master hides in his wagon mumbling bargains of repentance to the Unconquered Sun if only his life will be spared.

                  A chill falls on everyone in the caravan, all are too distressed to notice the shadow slipping into the vault wagon, disarming its locks and traps and then slipping out into the night...

                      AWARENESS

                      Hideous Nightwalker Gaze
                      For a scene, the Abyssal inverts all sight-based penalties from darkness and dreary weather, as well as from incense-smoke and other funereal sources. However, direct illumination turns the effect Obvious, as her eyes smolder, bleed, or otherwise reject the light.

                      Dread Hunter's Samadhi
                      With this Charm, Death's Lawgiver can percieve fear, feeling her victim's pounding hearts in her bones and tasting their dread in the sunset smoke coalescing around her. Difficulties are reduced for all rolls to notice and track the fearful, and the deathknight may discern if a fear is connected to an Intimacy with a standard (Perception + Awareness) roll.

                      MELEE

                      Jackal-Blade Ecstasy
                      Touching a weapon, the deathknight enlightens it to the wonders of blood and death. As long as he commits motes to this Charm, the weapon deals more damage (lethal, if it didn't do so already). Further, anyone but the Abyssal who touches the weapon is considered to have a minor Principle of bloodlust while it remains in their possession, increasing to a major Principle if they actually wield it in battle.
                      This Charm cannot enhance improvised weapons.

                      SOCIALIZE

                      Wraith-Skin Regalia Assumption
                      Summoning the spiritual detritus of his victims and former selves, the Exalted weaves them into baroque spectral finery around himself, or the body of another. In either case, the ghost-robe provides a circumstantial bonus to social rolls during court functions and other formal events, as well as any rolls to gain entry to the same. Additionally, the robe ensures that no infection spreads from the wearer, allowing even lepers to walk in Dynastic halls.
                      As a final benefit, living wearers appear as ghosts to the dead, if only at first glance.

                      SURVIVAL

                      Dragons at Lord Death's Feet
                      With a whisper, the Abyssal summons a beast to her side, calling down a vulture from the sky or a swarm of moths from the nearby woods. The animal (or swarm of lesser animals) must be a predator or scavenger, or bear some other symbolic connection to the dead.
                      The omen of the deathknight's summons puts onlookers ill at ease, reducing their Resolve against fear.

                          BUREAUCRACY

                          Coins from Open Graves
                          Even away from her coffers, the deathknight is never wanting. She may turn any appropriately morbid materials (vials of blood, braids of corpse-hair, scraps of parchment bearing the names of the dead) into legal tender at will, sacrificing something in her distant treasury to use its value in the here and now.

                          RESISTANCE

                          Scar-Tallied Sins
                          Activated in response to a damaging attack, this Charm creates an unyielding intimacy of vengeance against the attacker and all they hold dear. Its intensity derives from the momentum behind the original attack.

                          Broken Bodies Satisfaction
                          Letting go of one of his Scar-Tallied Sins, the Abyssal adds the momentum of the original attack to one of his own. This attack must be directed at the original attacker or one of their loved ones.
                          Onlookers know that this is just retribution, and cannot move to prevent it.

                          Sycamore Sellsword Oath
                          In return for some payment or favour – a name, a kiss, a bag of silver – the Abyssals swears to protect another. The steely cold of his promise marks his charge, as a warning to potential attackers, and he can sense when she is in immediate danger. The deathknight can send his shadow to Defend Other from afar.
                          The Abyssal is wracked with pain when his charge comes to harm, doubly so if he is the cause of it.

                              INTEGRITY

                              Darkness Swallows The Light
                              Light, unlike darkness, is finite. There are some depths which it will never penetrate. This permanent Charm counters any Holy effect that directly affects the Abyssal, negating whatever additional effect it might ordinarily have against Creatures of Darkness. (This explicitly does not extend to any sort of detection magic.) The Abyssal also gains the incidental ability to ruin holy symbols of the Unconquered Sun with a touch (1A damage per round of sustained contact, for particularly large and/or durable icons).

                              I was also considering an expansion Charm that negated any effect with the Holy keyword entirely, but I guess I'd have to see the prevalence of Holy Charms in Ex3 to decide whether or not that'd be OP. Should be alright with a cost attached though, I'd think.

                                          INVESTIGATION

                                          Assassin's Arrangement Appreciation

                                          This charm supplements an investigation of a murder scene, adding Essence automatic successes. Threshold successes gained while using this Charm allow for insight into the murderer: Minimal successes reveal only physical attributes, while higher successes detail the murderer's intentions, modus operandi, and connections to the victim not obvious from merely the physical evidence (for example, that the murderer loved the victim, even if this love had never been expressed to the victim).

                                          Empathetic Adoption Epexegesis

                                          As an upgrade to the preceding Charm, the Abyssal may choose to assume one of the murderer's intimacies, and may add a number of successes to her investigation attempt equal to the strength of the new intimacy. Doing so also allows insight into the murderer's likely future actions, including potential victims or likely hiding places.

                                                  Something that popped into my head while listening to post-rock

                                                  PRESENCE

                                                  Paint the Heart in Mourning

                                                  This Charm targets any Intimacy related to someone that has died or something that has been destroyed. The Abyssal infects the Intimacy with profound melancholy, making it a rocky place where hope can find no purchase. Any attempts that appeal to the Intimacy fail, as the targeted person can only consider what has been lost.

                                                  A repurchase of this Charm allows the Abyssal to infect not just the specific Intimacy, but any emotions/feelings attached to it. Those who have lost lovers will consider all future attempts at romance pointless, while the merchant prince who saw a treasured enterprise destroyed will be unable to muster any enthusiasm for a new economic endeavor.

                                                  Solar Mirror: The Heart Is Not a Cold Dead Place

                                                  As its Abyssal counterpart, this Charm targets any Intimacy related to someone dead or something destroyed. Instead of causing a permanent change, the Charm allows appeals to the Intimacy as if there had never been any destruction and they were still at their original strength. A repurchase allows the Solar to target the Intimacy as if she was the Intimacy, appealing to the widower with the strength of his lost wife and addressing the failed revolutionary with the force of his past conviction.
