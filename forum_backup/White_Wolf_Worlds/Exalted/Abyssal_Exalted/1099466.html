    Abyssal Awareness Charms

    06-02-2017, 08:41 AM
    I am currently working on Abyssal charms for a homebrew conversion. Most recently, I have been pondering Awareness charms. Solar awareness covers three main areas - Stealth & Surprise Nixing, Sense-expansion and Tracking. I've tried to address the same areas using these charms with (hopefully) some success. I'm hoping for some constructive criticism about these charms so that I can do a good job on them. There are only 18 charms, so the coverage isn't as detailed as the solar tree; this is a deliberate step - a taste of the charms that I can develop later on if needed.

    I'm specifically looking for feedback in the following areas:
    • Are the charms thematically sound? Do they look/feel/sound like abyssal charms?
    • Are the mote costs in the right sort of region? Have I under/over estimated the cost of charms?
    • Is there a deficiency that needs addressing in coverage? Have I missed an important use of an ability in the charm tree?
    • Are they cool?
    Here is the link: Abyssal Awareness Charms

    Thanks in Advance!

      Some of the ideas here seem really cool to me. I'm not the best personally at costings and balance but you look like you're on to some really solid thematics with charms like Siakal's Scent, Tick of the Fragile Clock and the ones that interact with ghosts. You've managed to play into the abyssal wheel house and avoid simply aping solars too much which is nice to see.

      My biggest nitpick would be that recreating a scene like with Undying Oracle, or working out the cause of death with Void Stares Back (I'm a fan of the rest of that charm) seem a little more like Investigation than Awareness. It might be worth taking another pass and either justifying to yourself why some of these charms are one and not the other, or tweaking them to be less Investigation-like.

      Have you considered charms based around fetters, locations or other such things? Charms to allow an abyssal to have a sense of who is moving around their creepy castle or crypt. Or to be aware when someone interferes with the keepsake they have of the person who allowed them to resist the call of the void and take the exaltation offered to them. There's a certain amount of thematic gothic potential to be played with there you might want to toy with.

                                                      My intent is to complete a home-brew of the entire splat eventually. My thoughts on resonance are still somewhat nebulous though - In 2e, the device was a leash around the Abyssal's neck, killing off people that she had spent time developing relationships with. This isn't rewarding roleplay - but largely was damaging to it, I find. With Resonance, I want to change that. Instead of making the deathknight have a 'doom radiation sphere' around them, I want to empower roleplay.

                                                      To this end, I thought of some rough ideas like
                                                      • Resonance twists realtionships, changing the intimacies others hold towards the abyssal. Love becomes obsession, annoyance becomes hatred. Friendship turns to paranoia. Mortals are very suseptible to these effects, while the exalted are less so.
                                                      • Bad things happen. People don't just fall down dead, but the Abyssal has some forewarning of how many will die that he cares about (Such as a mine collapse in town) and he knows its fated to happen - if he does nothing, can his intimacies stand so strong? If he acts, does he not cause the disaster he seeks to avoid?
                                                      As you can see, my ideas are very nebulous at this point. Any ideas?
