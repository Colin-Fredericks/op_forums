    Abyssal: the Vampire in Third Edition, Yay or Nay ?

    04-28-2014, 01:28 PM

    Why does Anja is such a moeblob who is good for nothing but sex appeal in Second Edition ? Hmmm, a rat-theme Abyssal and a cat-totem Lunar, will they put aside their differences and unite in making Nightwarden and his ghost-blooded fiance having the best honeymoon ever ?

    Oh wait, this thread is about "vampire theme stuffs" and Abyssal, not about some silly love-hate shipping !

    So yeah, everybody know that Exalted is the pre-history of WoD, and Abyssal is the ancestor of Caine so it's a given that they will have vampire-themes and gothic horror mofit (which is very out of place in Exalted) in as their aesthetic.

    But those "vampire theme stuffs" is kinda........shallow in Second Edition, me think. The blood-sucking thing appear once in the Trait chapter and also in the expansion charm of Dark Messiah style (which is very boring by the way), the draining charms in Lore has some potential but the Mirror keyword keep whispering in my head "a bunch of goth Solar charms, you looking at a bunch of goth Solar charm".

    The allergic to sunlight turn into a "Witness-to-Darkness level annoyance" internal penalties, but since burial clothing is so diverse around the world, I suppose this won't be so restrict IC. And the whole Appearance 0/5 seem like a reference to the Toreador/Nosferatu, though I'm not really sure about that.

    So yeah, Abyssal got three "vampire theme stuffs" (four if you count being a bitch to your Elder), and all three appear only one or two times in the manual and then never got talked about again, when was the last time you see a picture of Abyssal sucking people blood.

    And thus, it's my tiny little wish that the "vampire theme stuff" will be enforced more ham-fist-ly in Third Edition so I can proudly sparkle when I walk under the sun ~ <3

                  Abyssals should have Vampire aesthetics. And also Wraith aesthetics. Definitely some Slasher-flick aesthetics. Some Japanese horror aesthetics. Some Spawn and some Punisher as well, and even a few zombie aesthetics. You also need some mythological underworld aesthetics (from Greece to Egypt to Japan to the Aztecs).

                  Vampire has its place in the Abyssal themes, and it's okay if it's a fairly important one (I mean, vampires are pretty cool and popular for a reason), but vampires should only be one source of inspiration among many.

                  I think, generally speaking, Abyssals did a fairly good job of drawing from lots of sources of inspiration for Abyssals, unlike say Lunars, who really drew inspiration from shapeshifting mythology (and especially Werewolf the Apocalypse) and felt lacking of much else.

                                          I like to tie the theme of the void and vampires for Abyssals. Like the void they are consuming things, and they drain from the living essence of Creation, leaving it necrotic and dead. By inhaling deeply near a subdued victim, or by touching plants with bare skin they can choose to drain essence.

                                          I also use the vampire aesthetic in the Monstrances of Celestial Portion. Mine are not cages, much more akin to coffins, sarcophagi, and iron maidens. Made from Labyrinthian ores, Orichalcum, and Soulsteel, they are capable of changing shape to suit the Abyssal when first attuned. Abyssals who perish brings the Exaltation back to the Monstrance, which dark maw consumes any imprint on the Monstrance, reverting it to a blank form, until a new Abyssal slumbers within it. Abyssals can respire essence quickly and heal from nearly any wound, even from the brink of death if they rest in their Monstrance.
