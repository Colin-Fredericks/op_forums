        As for me:

        Dusk - The Weapon:
        While I actually like the Maiden ("It amused me", has always been a favourite motive), I'd move the obligatory sociopath into Daybreak. While I could make something up whole cloth, the setting already has almost exactly what I would have built.

        You are her greatest piece of work. Art, tool, philosophical statement. She came for you one night, your small village slaughtered, not by the zombies under her, not by the hungry ghosts, by the children. You look back at that night night and remember parents kneeling before their offspring, begging for the knife. You remember a boy "Mother" renamed Adder, staring at a bloody pitch-fork, while his father choked out what a good lad he was. You remember your mother. Cursing you out as you cried, stained carving knife in hand. An entire village sacrificed itself for its children. But your mother. Why? Was something wrong with you? Was your life worth so much less than the other kids?

        "Mother" renamed you, she did all the children. It gave you some measure of peace. The girl that was cursed, that was worthless, was dead, she should be dead. You screamed at her every night, cursing her as she deserved. Perhaps that was why you were taken to see "Grandfather". "Mother" wouldn't tell you. Only that, "Of all my Seeds, you have come closest to the truth". The trip the "Grandfather" was enlightening. You saw majestic vistas, and grim wonders. The Hand-Bone forest, so large that you would not have thought the whole world could hold it. The slave-trains of the Timeless Order, an endless line shackled together, driven ever forward by the whips of coin-eyed overseers. You saw the sea. Smooth and still as glass. Each galley oar that cut the surface felt like a personal insult to the peace of sea, and you felt somewhat pleased the oarsmen were under the lash. But something felt off about everything. A desperate denial, everything was to much. No-one suffered, they agonised. No-one had hobbies, they had obsessions. It wore you down just to watch.

        As alien as the world outside was, nothing could have prepared you for "Grandfather" himself. You could hear him long before you reach what "Mother" called the Mouth. Pain, hate, desperate to make it stop. By the time you can see him, your thoughts have been soaking in "Grandfather's" pain for days. Face to "face" you did the only thing you could, you took mother from her sheath and cut the screaming out of your head.

        "There are more in pain. More that suffer. Is your peace worth so much more than theirs?"
        "Mother, I... I can't..."
        "You can't? You would curse the world to save yourself? What are you worth?"
        "Nothing"
        "What are they worth?"
        "Nothing"
        "What is Nothing worth?"
        "Everything"

        You allowed yourself a faint smile as you watch the Mound be consumed by Pyre Flame. Your purple dress was stained with ash, the last of the Seeds had stopped screaming, finally at peace. This was the truth "Mother" had taught you. To exist, is to suffer. Your purpose is to remove suffering. But there is so much of it, and so much seems impossible to end. You sigh and turn from the Mound, you couldn't stop until you had saved everyone. You couldn't curse the world for not saving you. You would not become your mother.

        .

        Midnight - The Speaker:
        One thing I've always felt the sig Abyssals needed, a completely sincere devotee of the Ancestor Cult.

        "He accepted, go and return to your tribe, Axe-Biter-tak, and know you are a true Tear Eater." The youth looked at the gibbering dead-thing, probably wondering how you knew what he was saying. Still, she didn't question, and strode confidently out of the cave.

        You looked at the deranged Great Dead, "Honestly, Axe, 'She looks tasty' is not a good reason to deny her gift."
        "Braytip yolle givder stek."
        "It's an excellent gift, I... don't interrupt... I'm trying to keep your tribe alive you stubborn ass! A warrior that can dive into a first age tomb, alone I might add, isn't someone you waste because you're feeling peckish, and your last food offering was a month ago!"
        "Fentig forsate ginriga welam."
        "Tell you what, I'll get Biter-tak's sept to burn a captive in your honour as a celebratory gift. How's that?"
        "Rel tika."
        "Good," you sigh and shake your head, "Lover preserve, your daughter doesn't give me this much trouble."
        "Tellak dibur."
        "Oh, she likes something all.. ahhh, oh shit, gotta go! See you later, Axe-Biter! Celebratory ritual! Planning! Not getting eaten by over-protective fathers! Important things!"
        You sprinted out of the cave.

        You stood a little ways off from the celebration, thanks for a new warrior, and a successful raid. The living tribes tribes didn't know how to treat you. You weren't a shaman, yet you spoke with the dead. Not a chieftain, though no sept would gainsay you. You were an anomaly. You helped the living honour those whom came before. You help the dead remember why the living are important. But the Moon-Slaves come. Tongues drip with the gods' poison. Preaching obsequiousness to bullies and tyrants. The armies of the Bull spread ever further. Even the Immaculate Order, with its worship of the Exalted, and praise of effective suicide, comes this far north. The living and the dead must stand together. Or they will surely fall alone.

        .

        Daybreak - The Dictator:
        Because Emperor Scientist is still close enough to stereotype to work as a sig.

        You could still hear the screaming. It echoed around the halls, the agonised wail of ghosts being smelted together with labyrinth ore. The plates for the Lion's new war machine were coming along nicely, soon this force would be ready for deployment.

        She was beautiful. That was the only way you could describe her. And you wanted her. Oh, how you wanted her. She was presently owned by another, but both her and he were ghosts. And you had advantages over ghosts.

        "Proctor, the supply caravans have been ambushed. Forces have been deployed, but it means a delay in..."
        The ghost stopped mid-sentence as you looked at him.
        "Find who did this, if they are dead, they can replace the lost supplies, if alive, correct it."
        "Yes, Proctor," The ghost saluted smartly and left.
        This delay would be costly. Part of the frame was unstable and needed to be reinforced. This meant more resources would have to be dedicated to maintenance. Silver lining? More time for Necromancy.

        Ghosts they were. But ghosts of obscene power. You had only dabbled in Necromancy before now, the Lion didn't value it much and you found artifice much easier, Necromancy was what was needed. Necromancy and power.

        The Legion marched under the bloated red sun. The dry sand cracked underfoot. The nomad tribes of the desert fled before the inevitable march, but they are not the target. The mining city of Gem lies to the north, it will be a city of the dead before next Calibration.

        She hated you. You were sure of that now. It would have been simpler if you could convince her you were a saviour, but Necromancy demands its price. You took out the single most valued object you own, a single black feather. You would prove yourself the Lion's superior, and the Neverborn would give you what once was his. If not? You would take it by force.

        .

        Day - The Bounty Hunter
        No excuse, just think it's cool.

        The knife sunk into the matron's lower spine, severing the cord. She tried to scream as her legs went limp, but no sound escaped the alley. You quickly withdrew and passed the blade along her neck. She wouldn't be found until morning, plenty of time to leave town. Even if you hadn't killed her. No-one trusts a stranger.

        "Here, take a look at this!" You peer over the Prioress' shoulder.
        "Ok, I'm looking. What is it?"
        She looks at you like you're an idiot. "Isn't it obvious?"
        "Hey, you're the genius. I just stab things."
        "True, credit where it's due, you are very good at stabbing things." She giggled.
        "So..."
        "So, what?"
        "What is it?"
        "Oh, right! This little device is a modification of the Monstrance, while an Exaltation is bound within it enables the possessor to find all the Hun souls that have ever been bound to that Exaltation!"
        You blinked at her. "Please tell me you have something in mind for this other than your constant revenge games?"
        "Nope."
        "Good grief."
        "Cheer-up, Stabby, you'll get to kill and kidnap more people."
        You sighed, "No worries, boss."

        "Look, mate. I've been ordered to take you to Cold House alive and mostly intact. Stop screaming before you find out how liberal I can interpret that." You turned to the burlap sack. "Besides, there's no-one alive in miles, and the dead are on my side." The man inside suddenly got very quiet. "Thankyou." So this was you life now. Kidnapping and wetwork for a brilliant, but completely obsessed, dead exalted, at least it paid well. Besides, if they put as much effort into their job they might actually be able to destroy creation. And you like creation. All your toys are here.

        .

        Moonshadow - The Prophet
        Because, in the end, it all is ashes and dust.

        You smiled at the people around you. "Be at peace my brothers and sisters. For today, we release a dear loved one from the cruelties life have inflicted upon her." You turn to the elderly woman lying on the table before you. Her body was wracked with disease. "Are you ready sister?"
        She coughed, body filling her mouth. She'd be dead in minutes regardless, but this would help the living as much as her. You placed a small bowl below her neck and drew a soulsteel straw. Wiping part of her neck you insert the straw into the vein. The blood flows into the bowl and in moments her eyes close.
        "It is done, remember, brothers and sisters, though today we may seem to have lost a friend, she resides now in the Lands of the Dead. Her eyes will shine with the wisdom of her age, her body, hale beyond anything known by the living, but even in death there is hunger, there is thirst. The dead want as the living want, and so the cycle continues."

        "In death you have time, honoured one, time to progress further along the path on enlightenment." You smile at the new ghost, they always needed time to adjust.
        "I know, Anagami, I just guess I thought death would come with some inherent revealation," she laughed, "I suppose I should not be surprised at even the Dead-Land's callousness."
        "If it were easy, we would all have achieved it. I find reciting the Path's End helps when I'm discouraged, perhaps you would care to join me?" She nodded and you chanted together.
        "Without good, no evil. Without want, no lack. Without desire, no need. The Absolute is one. And one is the Void."

        .

        Hmm, ran out of steam a little at the end there. But it late, and I'm tied

                    I can, and will, continue to believe that Artorias from Dark Souls makes a great Dusk caste. The broken left arm that doesn't seem to encumber him at all, the utter disparity between everyone claiming he's a hero for dying in battle against horrendous forces versus the addled berserker you are forced to fight, the dark aura which almost seems to ooze and leave a trail of inky blackness like a slime trail behind him. The fact they tempered his rage with sorrow (Sif waiting for him in the Abyss and by his grave) and heartbreak (Ciaran mourning over his grave). That's a proper Dusk caste.

                    That being said, what needs to come across in a new Dusk sig is the feeling of sorrow. Maiden of the Mirthless Smile was, for her set, seemingly the most willing and delighted to serve the end of existence; I'd want the Dusk in 3e to seem the most despondent, the most unwilling, and yet perhaps the most damned, to serve the Neverborn... or give him a hopespot towards the end. Start with a pair of shortswords or a greatsword, but have him /her be shown, in chapter stories and pictures, to be using a wide assortment of weapons; make it clear to the reader across the breadth of 3e that nothing, and I mean absolutely nothing, cannot be turned into a murder weapon in his/her hands, and he hates himself for this. Give him a face like an Adonis, marred by parallel, linear scar-marks as he he's just sent all morning trying to claw his face off with his own spiky-fingered gauntlets, rather than look at the monster in the mirror one more time.
