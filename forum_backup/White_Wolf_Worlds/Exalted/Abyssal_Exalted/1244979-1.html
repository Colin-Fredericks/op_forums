It'll only ever create a minor intimacy by itself, so the temptation is still present to build higher up yourself to gain some bonus dice. But I do see your point, hmm. I might, were I writing Exalted 3.5, change the Solar caste spreads some and thus modify the Abyssal ones as well, but that's well outside the scope of this project. I tried to handle that issue, with admittedly varying degrees of success, by shifting the focus of those abilities so they didn't feel like they needed to be Dusk abilities.

                        Whispers in the sense of "I was touched by the Neverborn and I can use that to my advantage" got turned into lore charms rather than a merit, since I wanted that to more specifically be a thing for Death's Chosen, the high priests of the Neverborn, who can use that dark blessing to do incredible things beyond what any merit would be able to confer. Whispers in the sense of "I strayed too deep into the Labyrinth and now oh god the voices they won't stop kill kill kill kill" is something I'd model in other ways. Derangements, necromantic initiations, buying occult dots with appropriate death-related specialties, nihilistic intimacies and the like.

                        Might throw in a little sidebar for having been Teaching Incomprehensible Truth-ed as a merit, though. Need to do some considering first.
