                                    Resonance was kind of a half-assed system. The goal was for Resonance to force Abyssals to kill and destroy no matter what. So even if you wanted to be a good guy, you'd still have to kill and destroy stuff. Which, honestly, doesn't make Abyssals much different from most any other Exalted; I mean, I haven't seen too many Solars, Lunars, Sidereal or Dragon Blooded who are pacifists and refuse to ever kill someone.

                                    Really, when you're a PC Exalt in your average Exalted game, you're going to be killing people or destroying stuff at some point. So the idea of Resonance making an Abyssal have to kill people, honestly, just isn't a big deal. Why is a Day Caste Assassin "worse" for killing people than a Night Caste Assassin? Really, when you get down to it, he's not.

                                    So what Resonance currently is, is a bit of a clusterfuck. The Abyssal gets to pick what Resonance eruption he gets, so why not pick Stigmata? Now you don't have to worry about Resonance killing people. Ever. And at that point, Resonance stops being a particularly terrible thing and just something you check off. Heck, you can even use Resonance in positive ways, like opening up Shadowlands to help out your ghost buddies, or creating a positive intimacy towards a jackass mortal, just so you can kill them with a Resonance eruption and have no one be the wiser.

                                    Unfortunately, the fluff tended to present Resonance in a different way, implying that it was central to the Abyssal storyline and that it tended to destroy the things and people that Abyssals cared about. "Oh, you care about that person? They're dead." We had people in previous threads try and argue that Resonance is so overwhelming in an Abyssal's storyline, that Abyssals should be unable to create or run kingdoms or organizations because anything they care about, or even just find useful, is supposed to be destroyed by Resonance.

                                    On the subject of Abyssals promising to destroy the world in exchange for power... eh.

                                    Literature and media is filled with examples of characters who make a deal with the devil, then give the devil the finger and go off to do their own thing. There's nothing terribly evil about promising to destroy the world in exchange for power and then, rather than destroying it, using that power to save the world instead. And yeah, Abyssals should be able to do that, presuming they want to.

I want them to be condemned because of who they are, not what they are. I'm fine if Abyssals are dangerous and volatile and uncomfortable to be around. I just don't want the problem to be "being an Abyssal" instead of "being a murderer of epic proportions".
