                                                        Well thats just another thing I don't understand:

                                                        Why use the word "human" as opposed to "person"? I get that you use it that way, but I don't see why its so often used as a shorthand for some norm of personhood within our species. Particularly in relation to the word "Inhuman". Often "Inhuman" is equated with immoral or insane behavior and thus humanity is somehow equated with moral behavior. You could argue that hypothetically there might be inhuman behavior that could be moral, but I have yet to find any evidence in this world that anyone considers any inhuman behavior to be moral. Morality itself is a human construct under this consideration and thus is defined around human needs and desires.

                                                        Thus it shows a bias towards our own species, disturbingly similar to how a culture's view of morality would be biased around themselves. Now you may think it silly because no alien species have ever been discovered- negative information does not prove or disprove that aliens exist- But for some reason, my mind is uncomfortable with tying morality to the biases of a single sapient species.
                                                        You cannot accuse an alien or a robot of being "Inhuman" as they were never human to begin with and therefore cannot make a moral judgement based on the word "human". To me any moral judgement based solely on humanity rather than personhood in general is suspect, as what exactly is important enough about humanity as opposed to personhood to judge someone on the former rather than the latter?

                                                        Is it the morality aspect? Why then would one say "your inhuman" as opposed to "your immoral"? if humanity is nothing but another word for morality, why used humanity at all? What exactly is the difference? Is it the sanity aspect? Why then not say "your insane"? Why not say "when you upload your brain you'll be a person too?" It doesn't make any sense to me. Now I may be arguing semantics, but I think its morally important that we be moral for the sake of personhood in general rather than just humanity, if that makes any sense to you. I hope I'm explaining myself clearly, and I still apologize for this.....perhaps we should continue this in PM, Alucard?
