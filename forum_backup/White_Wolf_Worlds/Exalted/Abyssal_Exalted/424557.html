    Wolf amongst sheep - playing an abyssal posing as a solar

    03-01-2015, 06:04 PM
    Hello guys.

    tl;dr : help me hide my chars abyssal nature.

    My group is gonna try our hand at Exalted.
    I've played 1st ed a bit, but this will be the first time I try 2nd edition, and none (including the ST) have played Exalted before. We will be playing solars... or rather THEY will.
    I was tossing around concepts and realised that one almost felt like an abyssal, and then I got the idea of playing as an abyssal, and soon after I found the Solar Impersonation Style charm.
    So in short I'm gonna print out a solar character sheet and write all my stuff on that.

    The story will start (and probably stay) in the west.
    My character idea so far is a day caste. Hulking, but suprisingly sly, and basicly a one man pirate riding a Folding ship (since my circle will need a boat).
    My deathlord will probably be the Boddisvata and I will come from one of his islands. Exactly what my mission will be, or how/if I ever gonna turn on my liege and try to be a hero, or if I will stay bad is not decided yet. We will have to see how things work out. The only thing I know is that the first adventure will be a mini-treasurehunt, but otherwise the ST is both tight lipped, and has not 100% decided yet. Probably help him create war between other countries and get people to hate/fear solar.

    Since I might have to turn "mini boss" if my party finds out my nature and try to kill me, I have to build a somewhat efficent killer.
    I will have high melee, and will be sporting the five point artifact sword Soul Mirror (since it's decent as is AND I can boost it and go boss mode).
    I will have kinda high sailing, since I will be our groups "cab". My deathlord will provide me with the Folding ship.

    Now comes the part where I'm having trouble.
    How will I create a charcter able to hide in plain sight?

    I will of course get Solar Impersonation Style, 5 points in larceny and high manipulation.
    I will also spend points to get a hearthstone so I can regen essence even in creation.
    But otherwise? I don't even know what skill governs bluffchecks, nor what could make me get caught so I don't know how to build my character smartly.

    Here are some random thoughts:
    have atleast decent awarness so I can notice if someone (for instance: my circle) is spying on me?
    spirits, will they and their ability to be invisible, be a problem?
    get decent stealth?
    get a cheap item made from oricalcum, having it kinda like a meta-game bluff (the same as when I use a standard solar char. sheet)?
    other artefacts that can help with hiding my nature? Maybe Everyman Armour?
    My concept started out as a one man pirate and I don't wanna loose his badassery, so I'd get 8 points to spend on physical attributes, but otherwise I'm kinda open to suggestions.

    So that's kinda where I am.
    Hope y'all have some nice tips since I'm a wee bit lost about how to hide my nature best.

      Lying is usually covered under manipulation.
      If you're considering an artifact just go with soulsteel for it. If it has an attunement cost then soulsteel costs less, and the rest of the players really shouldn't be checking your character sheet to tell how much your attuning your artifacts for anyway. In game you're a pirate call it loot from a previous raid and that you've gotten used to the fight it gives you. I doubt a soulsteel artifact will make you stand out too much and who knows if you do it well enough they might give you an orichalcum version of it if they ever find one. The other advantage being that attuning the "offtype" artifact will give you larger essence pool than actually attuning one. Whether you choose to just take advantage of the extra motes or use it as bluff so you can have an extra charm up your sleeve in an emergency is up to you.

      I wouldn't invest charm space into countering spirits, but if you play off the superstitious angle then you could justify having some counter measures against spirits around your quarters as a case of "I have killed a lot of people and I don't need their ghosts coming after me." You're going for the one man pirate raid, so I actually wouldn't bother with stealth unless the failing is something that makes your character try and learn it. I would actually stick to becoming real good at fighting from multiple ranges. Like a sheath of throwing knives and ranks in throw so you can just knife people coming up on the other side of your ship.

          If you're going to be a Day then you already have a lot of the bases you want to cover, covered - Awareness, Larceny, and Stealth.

          It should not even be that big a deal to be more overt in your allegiance toward Skullstone - there's a canon Solar named Moray Darktide who is basically a privateer for Skullstone. That the rest of the players haven't played before should be a good method to telling them that the Deathlords don't need to be inherently evil (and really, shouldn't be but that's a whole other story). I mean, being a privateer for Skullstone comes across like a very reasonable "real job," in the end.

          You're going to want to invest in a degree of combat and Dodge (it's Caste, may as well go for it). You might look into favoring Sail and a social ability to go with that Manipulation - I seem to recall Abyssal Presence and Socialize as being pretty good.

          Just keep in mind that a supposed party member who suddenly goes PVP on the rest might lead to bad feelings OOC. Talk it all over with your ST privately first - the ST definitely has to sign off on it.
