                    Xanroth: While you make a good point about hiding in Creation and that the Deathlords can reasonably wait for young deathknights to get themselves killed, I want to point out a couple things.

                    1: Any discussion about free abyssals normally has to begin with destroying your own monstrance, since otherwise our first question becomes "why hasn't your deathlord touched your monstrance and rewritten your personality and/or killed you yet?" because that is what the canon Monstrance allows them to do. So before we even begin to talk about long-term survival remember that just playing this character concept requires that your character destroyed an artifact N/A and then ran off with an irreplaceable abyssal exaltation. That sort of thing does not sit well with the Deathlords. We can assume they will be motivated to get you back.

                    2: Deathlords are NOT shy about starting wars with each other. At all. You would think that the serious implications of a resonance eruption for them would limit this but no, as the Mask and Walker have proven throughout history there is no power that can put a limit on Deathlord pettiness and spite. It would delight any Deathlord to snatch up another's Abyssal exaltation, since this wouldn't count as working against each other (the exaltation was acting disloyally at that point) but an act of reprisal would. So hiding out in another Deathlord's domain is very much out of the soul steel frying pan, into another soulsteel frying pan.

                    3: While Deathlords do have "all the time in the world" they very rarely have the patience to act like it. Case and point, FaFL's invasion of Creation via fairfolk accidentally curing the Great Contagion. They all want the glory. And it is a slap in the face to any of them having a disloyal servant out among their enemies in defiance of their edict. What's more, any patience they might have flies out the window when the Neverborn come calling for results and want a head count on all those abyssal exaltations they were entrusted with. A Deathlord who loses track of an abyssal and has no plan for retrieving them soon is going to be in very serious trouble with their bosses.
