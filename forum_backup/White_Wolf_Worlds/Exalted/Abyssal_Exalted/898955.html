    Alternate Origin for Abyssal Exaltations

    05-25-2016, 11:36 AM
    I had an epiphany this morning when someone brought up the "Abyssals and Solars are two sides of the same coin" metaphor. What if, instead of Abyssals being corrupted Solars, Abyssal origins instead are a result of the Usurpation, the sorcerous working the Sidereals used to imprison the Solar Exaltations, and the first mass murder of the Solars. Essentially, the Abyssal Exaltations are grave goods copies of Solar Exaltations. The Abyssal Exaltations were imprisoned in the same way that the Solars were until, by sympathetic response, the Solars returned, which explains the only recent emergence of the Abyssals.

    What sort of implications would this have for the Abyssal condition? What changes in themes would you expect? Would any traditionally Abyssal feature no longer make sense?
