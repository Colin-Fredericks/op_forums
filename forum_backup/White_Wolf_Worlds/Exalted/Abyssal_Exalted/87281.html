    I'm Not Getting Abyssals

    03-09-2014, 03:25 AM
    Been thinking a lot about 3rd editions take of Exalts lately, namely Infernals. Now that pretty much everything I hated about them is being removed and the devs are abandoning the Reclamation/Infernals 2.0 concept, I'm really looking forward to using them. Even with what little we have to go on, I already have several ideas for Infernal characters villainous, heroic, and everything in between.

    Every time I look at Abyssals though, the only thing I can think is "why are the hell are you guys still around?" Yeah, I know Abyssals are the classic foils to the Solars and they have different themes an' all, but looking at the two previews, it's hard not to notice the overlap between Infernals and Abyssals. To be frank, Infernals make better foils to Solars than Abyssals. A vision of a corrupt, decadent First Age Solar recklessly weilding the power of the capacious mad Titans they once warred against is far more of a compelling image than an undead demi-god that channels the power of thirteen pissy Solar ghosts who in turn serve the Neverborn. It certainly doesn't help that Abyssals have traditionally protrayed as murderbots constantly being screwed over by Resonance.

    Now I'll go ahead and admit that I have a bias in that I've always preferred Deathlords as being powerful ghosts who rule over kingdoms of the dead and not a little over a dozen ghosts of First Age Solars, so there's that. I think part of my problem is how the Underworld and Neverborn have been presented in editions past. There are good bits to both, yes, but on a whole, what should have been interesting instead became boring and inconsistent at best. The Abyssal preview did a little to alleviate my concerns, but I'm still not really sold on Abyssals. Is there something about Abyssals I'm missing or not understanding here?

                              Originally posted by Two-Shadows View Post
                              but looking at the two previews, it's hard not to notice the overlap between Infernals and Abyssals. To be frank, Infernals make better foils to Solars than Abyssals.
                              I see them as incorporating two different, but related, functions.
                              My view of Abyssals is that of the anti-thesis to Solars. Like some kind of karmic force of balance to the 'Good' of Solars entry into creation (even though this karmic balancework took some time, but it's what's going on now that matters from this point of view ^^ ). This is especially hightened by the choice of word/names for each type*

                              But infernals, to me, harken back to the First Age Solars, with all their excentricities unleashed. Now powered even more by being brought to light (and probably encouraged by their "blessers").
                              So while Abyssals serve as direct opposition, and thereby define Solars as what they are not, I view Infernals as the looming reminder, and threat, of what they may yet become.


                              *"Solar" does not mean simply "of the Sun", but is in creation what the word "stellar" is in our world ("stellar" meaning something along the lines of "at the height of the stars", i.e: as high as you can rise. Since the sun is at the top in Creation "Solar" instead fills the spot of being "at the highest height".) While an abyss is still an abyss. that is to say, "Abyssal" means something along the lines of "the lowest depth". Understood within this is a definition of the value of height as being; high = good, low = bad. solar = the ultimate height to which a wo/man can rise. abyssal = the lowest depth to which a wo/man can sink.
                              Ofcourse this is not to say that a Solar has to be a good person. And in the same way an Abyssal does not have to be an evil or bad person. But generally speaking, it is, more than just a fight between individuals, a clash between two metaphysical, oppositional forces. This is all in my personal interpretation, note.

                                              The Solars have returned to Creation as a blank slate and carry with them the hope of Creation's future. Theirs is a struggle to resist the temptations that corrupted their predecessors.

                                              The Infernals emphasize the excesses and debauchery of the First Age Solars who used their powers for selfish ends. Theirs is a struggle to learn to use their powers for the sake of others rather than petty indulgence.

                                              The Abyssals emphasize the callousness of the First Age Solars who turned their powers against Creation instead of protecting it. Theirs is a struggle to learn to use their powers to protect rather than destroy.

                                              Or they can all fail and doom the world again. Their choice.

                                              In any case, the Abyssal narrative is a positive one; it seeks to achieve something rather than to avoid something. The Abyssals who are doomed are those who choose to use their power solely for the sake of destruction. The Abyssals who are on the path to salvation are those who learn to connect with something and use their powers to protect it. It could be anything from their Deathlord liege to their undead followers, as long as they're trying to preserve it.
