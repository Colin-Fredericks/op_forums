                              well for investigator, there are a few styles:

                              1. The Cloak Look
                              For the cheapest, most low-tech style possible, you can always just put on a cloak and pull its hood over your head so that your face is eternally hidden in shadows while most of your body is hidden under the cloak, real popular, and mysterious and its Ringwraith recommended!

                              2. Wide-Brimmed Hat And Trenchcoat
                              For those who want to be a little more elaborate and high tech, you can't go wrong with having a big cool-looking hat while wearing a long dark trenchcoat, works in both cowboy and pirate settings so all you need to do to adjust from South to West is exchanging your flame piece for a cutlass. you may have to act a little stoic to preserve your aura of mystery though.

                              3. Cloak and Hat
                              If you think a trenchcoat is not period-appropriate, you can always exchange that for a cloak while keeping the hat. add in a long thin blade like a rapier or saber and suddenly you a dashing swashbuckler!

                              4. Tight Leather
                              Alternatively you can ditch all the billows and mysteriousness and just wear form fitting leather for that secret agent look and for complete freedom of movement complete with boots with hidden, belts full of knives, and you can combine it with the hood style to look like an assassin, and aren't all assassins investigators in some manner? This look also comes in ninja.

                              5. Sedge Hat and Kimono
                              If you really want to play up the East Asian fantasy aspect of it, you can't go wrong by taking style no. 2 and shifting it a little. add in a katana and you look like a samurai.

                                      I had a "sig" Midnight Caste Abyssal in my little Creation, a pestilent and vile creature, ridden with disease and festering sores. Kept hidden under cloaks, hoods and generally being a bit like an open-sore-crossed-with-Mum-Ra. He was ... vile. A true servant of the Abyss and his Deathlord, he regularly took to the Labyrinth to breed new diseases, find old ones long gone from Creation or to speak with the things that dwell there. His services were often hired out to other Deathlords.

                                      The Solar PC, who fancied himself as something of a witty farmboy-turned-Zenith, crossed paths with the Abyssal many times; even started calling him "Old Puss Boy". Soon changed his mind when a plague swept through his home town whilst he was away doing Solar stuff. The disease was fast and, due to being necromantic in origin decomposed the bodies very, very quickly. Cue lots of hungry ghosts and no bodies to burn when the Solar PC returned home. He had to dispatch the ghosts of his family, his friends etc. He was not happy ... but then again don't piss off an Abyssal....
