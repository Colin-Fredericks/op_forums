If destroying a portal is simple the problem that I see is what happens to the Exaltation I seem to recall if an Abyssal is redeemed the Monstrous cage of &quot;evil&quot; shatters freeing the Exaltation. So I would wager that while the Deathlords could destroy an Abyssal this way it would cost them an Exaltation.
                                                            The official Ex2 canon rule is that a Deathknight who dies has their exaltation returned to its monstrance unless they are killed "directly" through their monstrance, in which case their cage breaks and the exaltation is free but still a corrupted abyssal exaltation. A free Abyssal exaltation can be summoned back into a fresh monstrance with a Void circle spell by any void circle necromancer, which is the main reason a Deathlord would not play this card: they are worried another Deathlord would pilfer their exaltation.

                                                            As you say, Redemption also frees an Abyssal exaltation in canon, and there is no spell that can summom a solar exaltation back intobits monstrance. In other words, Redemption is the only canon way to actually escape a Deathlord's hold over your exaltation in the long term. Which gives them a very good reason to stop a Deathknight from achieving this.
