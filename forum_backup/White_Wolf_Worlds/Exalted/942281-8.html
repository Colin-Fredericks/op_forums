                Just to throw my 2 obals in, I would bring back the Manga strips, more of the old artifact system (with rank 4 and 5 having evocations) and how thaumaturgy worked in the old edition, Manses having 5 levels, magitech, magic and motes being creations physics, and do a better job bringing the expanded setting in line with how it was in 2nd. Also the old Anime influence is so missed, Make the setting less milque toasty, and back to how it was, a terrible place that abused and hurt people, because Creation was a broken world inherited by and ruled by broken people who are trapped in a cycle of abuse and power dynamics.
                God the things that chapped my inner thigh was the tinker bell magic system (it's a miracle just clap your hands there is no science) and the remodeling of the various societies to be more "progressive" (removing the less pleasant qualities of the various groups and cultures instead of giving the players a chance to fix it in game, its) while at the same time erasing the elements that made each group unique causing all the groups to end up kind of samey.

                The new evocation system, fine for high end artifacts, but a pain in the ass for the "mass" produced stuff used by rank and file dragon blooded or alchemicals. While saving the evocation gear for the Lostech.
                also the the Core book is too flipping big, it scares every prospective player, and I broke its spine with in a month of purchase/printing.
                I felt the guys who initially wrote 3rd did not like the 2nd edition of the games lore or metaphysics or anime/JRPG inspired roots.
