                        All ghosts are considered Essence users and can attune
                        to manses and artifacts as easily as any other Essence
                        user. Ghosts can attune to items made of jade or soulsteel
                        at the item’s normal attunement cost. The attunement
                        cost is doubled for orichalcum, moonsilver or starmetal
                        artifacts that were forged in Creation. Any activation
                        costs for such artifacts is likewise doubled. Ghosts never
                        gain any magical material bonus associated with any
                        attuned artifact, not even soulsteel ones. These rules
                        apply even to the ghosts of deceased Exalted. However,
                        a ghost can attune to an artifact made of orichalcum,
                        moonsilver or starmetal at the item’s normal attunement
                        cost if the item was originally forged in the Underworld
                        and was especially designed for a ghost to use.

                                        A bowl that can produce 3 servings of band, fairly tasteless gruel, which was enough to prevent starvation but still would still see someone who survived only on the bowl still begin to gain penalties would probably be a 1 dot artifact. It's neat, but not terribly impressive. Even if you could theoretically survive indefinitely on what it produces, nobody would want to do so for long.

                                        I think that a large cooking pot that can produce enough food to feed a dozen dozen people, and which has a wide variety of different dishes would probably be Artifact 3.

                                        What you seem to be looking for is an artifact that produce enough good to feed a castle worth of people (what, around 100 different people, or even more) every day for 3 meals, and give it the ability to produce a vast array of different kinds of food. I would probably put that at Artifact 4.

                                        If you wanted a similar artifact capable of feeding an entire village or town, freeing the populace from needing to worry about food, I would probably make that Artifact 5.

                                        I think, while that is a "quality of life" type Artifact, being able to produce unlimited food is actually a pretty big deal for kingdom and nation building in Exalted and I would try to avoid making it too common and easy to do.
