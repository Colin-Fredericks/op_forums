                    Originally posted by prototype00 View Post
                    (+3 major Intimacy vs -2 Minor Intimacy = +1 to Resolve).
                    As a side note, that intimacy math there is actually a bit off. Intimacies target resolve directly, not dice, so attacking using a minor is -1, major -2, defining -3 directly to resolve, and intimacies raised in defense of resolve enjoy a natural +1 bonus to their level, so it goes +2/+3/+4. I'm just going to assume they don't have any intimacies to bring to bear, but what intimacies you can bring up might be a bit more broad than you think.

                    For example a lot of the more powerful DBs have intimacies of pride for themselves, and that might help them defend against a tie of fear to what they assume is nothing more than a mere animal. Also some Dragonblooded charms let them reflexively develop intimacies on the spot if they see another terrestrial in danger, even social danger.


                    If you are using intimidate as an instill action though you DO get appearance dice, since they apply to all instill actions I'm pretty sure.
