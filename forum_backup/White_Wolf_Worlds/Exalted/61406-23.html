    Originally posted by TheCountAlucard View Post
    Gaming groups in my past have been divided on whether it fit better as Abyssal or Infernal, before coming to the reluctant conclusion of "mixed circle of both."
    The world of Dethklok doesn't have this rigorous categorization where one set of spells is arbitrarily sorcery and one set of spells is arbitrarily necromancy. There's rather a lot of fiction in which a character who is called a necromancer or is said to practice necromancy does not spend any portion of the fiction divining information by communing with the dead.
    but in exalted we do have an example of someone raising a defeated behemoth, and that was done with necromancy.

    Just the exalted community tends to warp and twist pretty much everything to be infernal as mary-sues tend to do. I would at the very least give "people die from simply being around you, in mass when playing music" as good examples of resonance and abyssal performance charms respectively since this is pretty much what they are designed to do. Including giving the band members nihilistic apathy. Infernals on the other hand are about intentional cruelty and corruption.

    Infernals ape the themes and concepts of all other splats, with examples of death, shapeshifting, fate altering, excellency, even flavors of the elements. They'd be easy to spin just about anything as infernal so if anything it should be the last thing you jump to as an analogy.

    Sorry thats kind of aggressive and ranty but "everything is infernals" has become a pet peeve of mine. An example of "ruined by the fandom" for me.
