                            Hi! I have a couple of (possibly stupid) questions about Clashes. But me and my players had different reads on this.

                            1. In a mundane fight between two mortals 2 warriors happen to act on the same tick. This is a Clash according to pg 202, but how exactly do we decide if its a Withering or Decisive Clash? My gut tells me if Mortal A wants to throw a Withering and Mortal B wants a Decisive then that is exactly what they get, with A rolling his Withering pool and B rolling his Decisive pool?

                            2. What about when we add in Charms such as Force Rending Strike. My Supernal Brawler Player felt that because the Charm had the Decisive Only tag, then he himself would have to roll his Decisive Pool when it came to the Clash. I am pretty sure this isn't the case and the Brawler can respond with a Withering Attack. Otherwise it would need the perilous tag?

                            3. Also would Force Rending Strike work vs a Gambit attempt like a Grapple or Disarm? I posed a question earlier in the thread and the consensus seemed to be that Gambits arent strictly decisive 'attacks' per say. My gut says it would work, but wanted peoples opinion.

                            ​Thanks for folks time!

                                1. You're right, they each just do the one they want to do. Which generally means the withering attack has an advantage.

                                    1. You're right, if one does withering and the other does decisive, they roll respectively.

                                    2. You're wrong, your player is right. Decisive attacks can't be used in crash unless explicitly stated, so they don't need a perilous tag.

                                    3. What do you mean, work? They both roll their decisive attack rolls, and then the winner rolls damage, which, in the case of the gambit user, is their initiative against the difficulty. Because winning a clash gives a bonus, I would decrease the difficulty of the gambit.
