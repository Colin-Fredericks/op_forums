                                    I don't think they've lost their love for the project. But I do think they suffer from a bad case of "love is blind".

                                    The script and story needed more editing. The visual editing needs to be more confident. The fight scenes need to get back on screen where they belong, for which they'll need better choreography. And they need to cut things that clutter up stuffed scenes or requires exposition time they don't have.

                                    It's strange that since the team moved from one crazy men sleeping at his desk to (I'm told) a full on production team, the end result appears more and more amateur level. I believe that the team is on a mission of passion, but while the technical quality and tool proficiency improved a lot, I don't get the feeling that the team knows what to do with what they have.

                                    For all the exposition dumping (from the Man himself), what have we actually learned about what's going on?
                                    For all the action, what did we actually see on the screen in the way of awesome fights?
                                    For all the drama, how many character arcs have actually meaningfully progressed?

                                    The answer isn't nothing, but it's far short of where the show could have gone. Season 1 did more on every count with less screen time.

                                    I thank the Devs for the great game of Exalted!
