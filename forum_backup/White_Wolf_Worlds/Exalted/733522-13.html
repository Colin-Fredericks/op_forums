                                            03-18-2018, 10:16 PM
                                            Originally posted by Citadel97501 View Post
                                            Hello all, I was wondering what circle and ambition would you think is appropriate to transform an NPC's Volcano Cutter into an arm for an NPC? One of my players just assisted one of my NPC's, saving his life but the npc was nearly killed and still lost his right arm, but has Volcano Cutter so one of the players wanted to transform it into a new arm, ala Wormtail from Harry Potter.
                                            From just a personal perspective I would have someone do a craft: Artifacts roll to remodel the volcano cutter for this. But to stay on theme of the discussion for workings I would have to ask how the player would go about it. I found with the game I play in my ST has regularly told me workings have two components. The idea of the working, and how is it carried out. So you have the idea together but how would your character or the party carry out changing a powerful artifact 5 sword into a PC's arm ? With no additional information I would say low end would be Celestial 1 and high end would be Solar 1. Also I would set the finesse at 5 for anything under solar 1 on this one.

                                            The things that affect what I would ultimately peg the level at would be:
                                            1. How much of the Volcano Cutters abilities would the arm retain? From a scale of all to none. At none I would let it go at a celestial one.
                                            2. How is the weapon getting reforged into an arm?
                                            3. How will the player equip and use the arm? Detachable? Permanent?
                                            4. What materials or items will they be using to do this?

                                                Originally posted by Accelerator View Post
                                                ok! now, second question.

                                                Let's say I wish to make a crown, produced by a sorcerer. Perhaps lunar, perhaps sidereal, perhaps solar.

                                                The Exalted, wishing to help the king of a certain country, produced this crown for him. The crown, when put upon his head, assists him in ruling the country. Basically, it does stuff like speed the wheels/ bureaucracy and social excellency/ bureau rectifying method. Thing is, how much is too much? Its from a terrestrial circle sorcery.
                                                I wanted to chime in on this one as I was reading through the posts. I like the idea of this and the idea is solid but the effect the player wants to try to do is really nebulous. I thought I would offer a few ideas I would try to guide them on to help narrow down what it can do.

                                                * Allow the King to guess an intimacy of anyone who comes to petition him in court.
                                                * Anyone who fails a resolve role against the king when he makes a decree gains the minor intimacy Respect: King
                                                * Allow the king to make a roll against a targets resolve to levy a minor curse or geas that makes the target do something beneficial for the kingdom.
                                                * Any decree the king personally puts into effect make a roll. Multiply the speed at which it would be implemented by the number of successes.
                                                * As long as the king wheres the crown he is immune to mundane poisons and diseases.
                                                * As long as the crown is worn while having private time with his Queen / Consort / Concubine if a pregnancy occurs he can choose for it to be a boy or girl.

                                                There are a ton of ways a crown could support or help a ruler the effects above are just a few. I would put it from terrestrial 3 to celestial 2 for pretty much any of the effects above.
