                                                            Originally posted by Hand-of-Omega View Post

                                                            Why not? He has the Backing of the Bronze Faction and several of the most powerful gods in Heaven, who's going to prevent him from getting some Peaches to preserve stability in Creation? Or are you claiming that the Peaches just wouldn't work? If not, there are other options, the Immortality Hearthstone being one.

                                                            I get that this doesn't work for the setting, it resolves the situation far too easily. I just think 3E should be an opportunity to explain how this works more clearly, by either altering Cainan's situation or suitability for the throne, or (more likely) the game's available Immortality options.
                                                            Well, for one thing prolonging the life of a new Emperor is a gross violation of Heavenly law and the Violet Bier of Sorrows is the strongest bailiwick of the Gold Faction. Saturn's Chosen handle internal auditing of the Bureau of Destiny AND matters of fate regarding when things should properly end. If you need an in-game reason for why it's unfeasible, it's not hard to spot one; Kejak would be setting himself up for an enormous indictment and an easy political win for his opposition. And afterwards, Cainan still has to die because that's what's fated.

                                                            As far as the logic goes, "The Bronze can't prolong Cainan's life" is not my conclusion but one of my premises. Kejak's decision-making in Aspect Book: Fire (page 58) makes it clear that Cainan is set to die "soon" and it is not something avoidable. Exalted has plenty of setting facts that appear in contradiction ("Princess V'neef is 60 years old" vs "V'neef Jegan is 300 years old") but there's a lot of fun to be had in trying to reconcile them. Trying to argue from game-mechanics that setting elements are untrue leads to the Usurpation never happening and neomah breeding farms; its far easier to just decide that particularly annoying crunchy bits, such as the Gem of Immortality, do not invalidate large swathes of written material.
