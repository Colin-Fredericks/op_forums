                                    Some things I'd like to use as inspiration for Exalted games:

                                    The Stone Gryphon has heroes who are so competent and knowledgeable people find it bizarre and are figuring out how to deal with suddenly getting the memories of kings and queens. Susan basically attempts Letter-Within-A-Letter Technique- writing a letter which most people will think is just a whimsical story but which contains a complicated system of allusions to things only her family know from which her brothers should be able to deduce state secrets. The Four all have symbolic images a lot like Solar anima banners (the Rat, Crow, Sword and Heart), although they only show up in dreams. And they seem to pretty much form a solar circle with every caste except Twilight- a warrior, a priest, a diplomat and a spy (the only thing which makes it even slightly awkward is that the diplomat is also a spy).

                                    A lot of Sailor Moon fanfic has heroes trying to come to terms with memories from past incarnations and trying to figure out to what extent they're a new person and to what extent they're an ancient one. Also, heroes struggling to hide their identities. (I don't know whether canon has those things too, since I'm not very familiar with it)

                                    Avatar also has a hero hiding his cosmic powers and destiny. Also, as in Exalted, the hero is hiding his nature because his enemies would kill him for it, while there are others who worship him for it. Also, imperialist villains, elemental magic, and elementally themed geography. As with Sailor Moon, fanfic has heroes figuring out how much they're a new person and how much they're an ancient one.

                                    Ami in Dungeon Keeper Ami seems like she'd make a really cool model for an Infernal PC; a normally superhumanly smart hero who foolishly turned herself into a champion of Hell, but is trying to find ways to do good in spite of that.

                                    Steven Universe has another hero trying to figure out how much he's a different person from his predecessor, of course. It also has someone who inherited is powers from his mother (like the Dragon-Blooded) and heroes who were made artificially to serve a specific function (like the Alchemicals). It also has supernatural beings who long ago rebelled against their masters (so many examples in Exalted), heroic supernatural beings who fought to free humans from the tyranny of their own kind (like some of the the kami and divine ministers in Exalted, presumably), and ruins of an ancient age which some remember as a glorious golden age but others remember as a time when the world was almost destroyed, and now struggle to prevent the return of. And, of course, it has magic which can temporarily combine two people into one.

                                    I've also made a PC loosely inspired by the "X is a Changeling" genre of My Little Pony fanfic (in being a genuinely well-intentioned hero hiding important stuff from her fellow heroes), and tried so-far-unsuccessfully to figure out how to make a hero based on Psi in One of our Submarines- a hero (with memories of a grander past life, 'though that's not particularly central to the concept) who got magically enslaved but figured out ways to circumvent that.

                                    It just now occurs to me that Nepeta in Herding Cats, a writer of shipping fanfic who has been required by mysterious cosmic forces to secretly matchmake all her friends in a complicated pattern, could be an interesting model for a Chosen of Venus.
