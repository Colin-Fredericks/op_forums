    Charms Enhancing Workings: Is this Ok?

    03-22-2016, 08:04 PM
    So, I noticed that the Solar Occult Tree includes zero charms, aside from excellencies, that assist in accomplishing a Working. Nothing to shorten intervals or mess with Finesse, Means, or Ambition. And, because of this, I feel like it has to be intentional and not just because of lack of wordcount.

    And, I can see the logic. The way the Working system is set up, you can't get away from adding Time based Means for the top of Solar Ambition even if you have the Means bonuses available to a First Age Solar. Which means that the Salinian Working makes sense and remains a numinous awesome thing that required a team of Solar Sorcerers toiling for years to accomplish. Which is as is should be. Even in Exalted you don't really want people reshaping the fabric of reality on a whim.

    But..... I can't help but notice that Craft, which also generates permanent items, can get up to some crazy, crazy things with the set of existing Solar Craft charms. Including things like "Here, you've been working so hard, have a daiklave!" basically. Or "Ha! You didn't actually kill me! This body is simply a tele-operated robot clone! With a Self-Destruct Mechanism!" *BOOM*

    So, I'm not entirely sure that easier access to Workings would necessarily be that much more problematic than the kind of things you can do with Supernal Craft. And, even if we do want to keep Solar level Workings rare and special, I still kinda wonder if warding a town against fleas should really need to take an entire week, minimum. There might be some things we can do where making Terrestrial or Celestial Workings gets a bit easier, without creating a charm that lets you perform the Salinian Working as a simple action.

    So what do you guys think? Are charms that mess with Workings bad design space? Would a Spell be a better idea?

    Are charms that enhance spells bad ideas too?

              Are charms that enhance spells bad ideas too?
              I think we'll probably see a thing or two like this, but I would hesitate to just throw stuff in because it looks like things you can do in Craft. Craft very much gives me the impression that the baseline constraints were designed to allow charms to remove them, key to lock. Workings are probably calibrated under the assumption that those things do not exist (perhaps because they are their own XP tax), so adding them risks making it too easy.

              The other thing that I think needs to be accounted for is that with sorcerous workings, while there's a work-time attached, you are more or less assumed to have the ability to get right to work immediately, and any extra prep you put in manifests as Means. In Crafting, this isn't the case -- the amount of prep needed is entirely fiat-based and implicitly significant in many cases, which means that it's easy to put the brakes on crafting if your speed gets too out of hand. (On the other hand, sorcery has the XP sink, so that might have the same effect?)

              All that being said, I totally think there's room for working-enhancing charms, and I do expect we'll see some eventually. But I would hesitate to use the Craft charms as a model, because I expect they'll operate on a rather smaller scale and not be direct force multipliers in the same way.

Of the 3 FSSA is supposedly being amended in some way.

                  The consensus seems to be that Workings are balanced around not having Charms. You have the Ambition, Finesse and Means to play with. Including Charms may make those irrelevant.
                  And, ignoring FSSA for the moment, you'll note that all the other charms simply add more charm dice (or the equivalent) to the roll, and thus are effectively equivalent to an excellency in effect, if not in mote cost.

                  Thinking on it further, while Spells that enhance Workings seems kinda thematic, it also means that Workings would have to be rebalanced around assuming everyone had that Spell, which pretty much becomes a tax on being good at Workings.

                  A few charms, on the other hand, seems like they could be introduced that wouldn't end up tossing the whole system out the window, just tipping the Solar end of the scale a bit.

                  Currently, I'm thinking of one or two charms which branch off the Sorcery access charms that give you boosts to the circle of Workings under the one you just got access to. So, you learn Celestial Circle Sorcery, now you can get a couple of charms which boost your ability to do Terrestrial Workings. This way, at least, no-one is introducing any charms which make Solar level workings easier.

                  As for what the charms might do, well, here are some possible ideas (and note that these would be limited to certain circles of Working):
                  • Double 9s on Workings and/or Shaping Rolls.
                  • If you roll a set of 666, you get 3 non charm successes on a Working and/or Shaping Roll.
                  • Each pair of a 10 and an 8 counts as 4 successes on a Working and/or Shaping Roll. (Possibly this fits better as a Sid charm).
                  • Lower the Interval of your Working.
                  • Subtract 5 from the Ambition Number of your Working.
                  • Turn a Botch into 5 successes.*
                  • Treat a Working as if you had the proper Equipment, raising Means by 1.

                  I suspect that messing with Finesse is probably gonna be broken. And, hmmm,,, Of the things I came up with, I think that what I want the most is a way to reduce the time on some Workings, which might well be a warning flag. I think that a charm which lets you count as though you had a particular type of Means when you don't might be the least disruptive to balance, especially if it was limited to certain types of Workings.

                  I think the effect I like the most is a charm that turns a Botch into some number of successes, because I realized that it could be fluffed as the person having a big insight into the Working that causes them to reevaluate the Working. Maybe it doesn't actually add successes, it just lets the Player or Character dictate the change to the project as a result of the Botch, turning it from a bug into a feature. Yeah, I think that's a charm I could introduce without breaking the system too horribly and injecting a bit of fun into the game. Especially if it was 1 use per Working.

                                                  It's worth noting that having relevant charms, spells and other resources already boosts the means of a project. So we do already have that tie in, and it's one that encourages you to make workings in alignment with what you already do/have access to.

                                                  The headache(as others have already pointed out) is that for solars it's trivial to just Throw an Excellency at it, as this is a longer-term project so generally motes are not an issue, so while charms that give you automatic successes or more dice cheaply might be a thing you can do, it doesn't add a lot to the game to do so. On the other hand, you want to be careful about non-charm dice or successes, as suddenly that makes very high level projects a lot easier/more probable to do.

                                                  So that really only leaves the ground of decreasing the XP costs, decreasing the time they take to make or maybe making it easier to juggle several at once. Which is good grounds, just not as much as we might like.
