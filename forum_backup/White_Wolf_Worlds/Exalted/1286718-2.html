                        Re; topic in the thread where, to paraphrase, other Exalted can be OKd to have social powers Solars don't, because other Exalted have powers more suited to bending or ignoring others preferences, my feeling on this is it really depends on the Exalt type Solars are being compared to.

                        Like, I think anything that is acceptable for a Dragonblood should be acceptable for a Solar because, to my mind, other you can make a system statement that Dragonblood essence lends itself more to coercing people against their will than Solar. To my mind, that really does not fit with or constructively add to the dynamic between the two splats, and its really quite a bad statement to add there.

                        Likewise really for Sidereals and Lunars, although I think it's more acceptable given how their patrons work and the kind of magic their themes and feel promise.

                        On the other hand of course, it's pretty clearly OK for Abyssals and Infernals and really some of the stranger 3e splats (Hearteaters) to have this property.

                        The more important thing of course, rather than splat dynamics and themes, is to complement, rather than obviate, the richness going on in the standard social game though! Even for splats that are relatively "Mind Control OK", diabolical cleverness can be cooler to play with that brute force.

                        (Edit: If it helps at all to explain, when you pick up a Dragonblooded, you do so to be a elemental warrior, or a Sidereal to be a subtle genius of the stars, or a Lunar to be an flexible shapeshifter. Those are your core pitch for those character types.

                        That doesn't come with the expectation that your Charms should be violating in a way that the shiny Solar Exalted's are not, and it's probably mostly unwanted for you to be told that they are, if my intuitions and expectations are much like the typical ones.

                        But when you play an Infernal or Abyssal or similar, you are probably coming in from a point of view where you expect and are mostly cool with being somewhat inherently a thing that violates mortal will. YMMV tho.)

                                I'm not so sure. The core weakness in the Solar Charmset that I think is often overlooked is that, relative to other Exalts, their Core Charm Identity (CCI) is excellence. This means that they should be the most efficient/powerful at being excellent. This is generally translated as 'more successes', and not 'cool new things you can do with your Essence'. So the issue in this thread is, apparently, this weakness is being exposed. Other Exalts generally have a CCI that makes it easier/more likely to have 'cool things with Essence' rather than 'more successes'. The DBs are an excellent example of this, with almost every Charm having some Elemental interaction.

                                I think the major issue here is the fact that Intimacies are the 'working parts' of the Social Interaction System. I feel like many in this thread who want to shorten the mentioned 4-step system are moving in the direction (knowingly or not) of a 'skill roll resolution' social system, which is explicitly not what Exalted is. Using an analog to Exalted's combat system, wanting to 'automatically create' Intimacies you can then exploit is akin to wanting to be able to just gain Initiative so you can make more Decisive Attacks, ideally without having to make Withering Attacks at all. That just isn't how the system works.

                                Now, someone who wants to be very good--and self-sufficiently so--at Social combat simply cannot ignore Socialize. Under the social system, knowledge (of intimacies) is power. And Socialize does double duty as both gaining knowledge of others and hiding knowledge of self. It is very much the Melee of Social combat. People who ignore it just... I mean, I feel that that can't really understand the social interaction system if they think they can be 'a social expert' and not have it.

                                I can't help feeling that someone who wants to be able to generate intimacies on demand doesn't really understand the system. It feels very much like, 'I don't really care what this guy thinks; I just want him to do what I want.' I mean, how different is this, really, from overt mind control?

                                For myself, this feels like people wanting to drive a bulldozer through a Zen rock garden. The social interaction system lends itself to indirection, wheeling, and dealing. It is not this 'hit this guy over the head with my great idea until he caves' type of approach. My approach to social situations in Exalted 3E works like this:

                                Step 1) Determine your goal and identify your target.
                                Step 2) Gain as much information about the target's Intimacies (and others' Intimacies affiliated with the target) as possible while hiding your own. Primary Ability: Socialize
                                Step 3) Start Instilling Intimacies that are potentially supportive of your goal. Primary Ability: Presence or possibly Performance. NB: This is the primary location for draining your target's Willpower.
                                Step 4) Once the target's social situation (Intimacies and remaining Willpower) are sufficient, Persuade. Ideally, the target is low on Willpower and/or has a very difficult time resisting your influence.

                                This is the basic game plan. Note that Step 3) is the most optional, in some ways. If the target's Intimacy array greatly supports your goal already, Instilling may not be necessary. It can still be useful to drain Willpower, though. Ideally, your target should not know your goal until the Persuade hits. Well, ideally, your target should not know your goal even after the Persuasion. But you get the idea.

                                I feel that there also needs to be an obligatory reminder to STs that providing their NPCs with few if any Intimacies and/or having them spend WP all the time is not really playing fairly. I view it as the Social analog to fudging numbers so PCs miss in combat, etc. The social system is quite rich, and while the 'hard pass' of WP can be annoying (and tempting), I have not seen any other system that does better to capture the dynamics.

                                So... I don't know. I do hope that the ST book coming covers issues like these in a lot greater detail.

                                                Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz View Post
                                                Essentially, Solar Presence charms do four main things well:

                                                Buff your friends
                                                Help you get lots of successes for cheap
                                                Help you intimidate people
                                                Help you seduce people

                                                None of those let you deal with the difficulty of persuading people to take significant actions when they don't already have a Major intimacy. And if they already did have a Major intimacy? There's a decent chance you wouldn't need to Persuade them anyway.
                                                What about Bargain actions? Like Threaten actions, they bypass the Intimacy requirements of Persuade actions. It's not as convenient as getting them to agree to do something for essentially nothing, but causes fewer complications than Threatening them to do something lest you hurt them.


                                                Right off the bat, the Solar Presence Excellency is vital to Bargain actions.

                                                Harmonious Presence Meditation and Awakened Carnal Demiurge will help, but the middle point between them, Threefold Magnetic Ardor, excludes Bargain actions from its assistance.

                                                Enemy-Castigating Solar Judgment would help you Bargain with creatures of darkness.

                                                Fulminating Word specifically includes Bargain actions among the social influence it may supplement.

                                                Crowned King of Eternity helps.

                                                (Huh. What is Worshipful Lackey Acquisition doing in Presence when it requires a Performance action to function?)


                                                But for toothier Bargain-oriented Charms, Solars might need to look to Bureaucracy.

                                                Frugal Merchant Method and Insightful Buyer Technique help you determine how valuable items are in your target's market of choice.

                                                Deft Official's Way can point you to the best person to bargain with, which may not be the person you thought you needed to influence.

                                                Enlightened Discourse Method and Semantic Argument Technique are just more dice-adders. Not sure how much you need that if you're using Solar Presence.

                                                Irresistible Salesman Spirit lets you double 7s on a Bargain action (no dice-limit concerns here), which Solar Presence definitely doesn't do.

                                                It also doesn't have anything like Soul-Snaring Pitch, which is a persuade action that lets you set up a bargain action by convincing someone that a Trivial reward is worth agreeing to perform a Life-Defining Task. Of course, you do run into the usual limitations of persuade actions and Intimacies, so maybe that doesn't actually bypass any of the problems players are having with Solar Presence. At least having Listener-Swaying Argument and Impassioned Discourse Technique helps ram this one through.

                                                Subject-Hailing Ideology helps get around Intimacies by letting you treat former or eroded Intimacies as though they were at their former strength, under a particular condition.


                                                The Terrestrial Bureaucracy Excellency can't help your Presence-based bargain actions with that sweet, sweet "re-roll all 6s" effect. Instead, you can enjoy the Terrestrial Presence Excellency's benefit of adding an additional non-Charm die for every 10 you roll.

                                                But at least Terrestrial Bureaucracy in general perhaps gets to the heart of the matter sooner with Finding the Water's Depths, to help you figure out what your specific target might specifically want in return for performing the specific task you want of them.

                                                Shells-for-Silver Ruse lets you deceive your mark about your exact level of wealth, and leverage that lie as a Minor Intimacy.

                                                Thoughtful Gift Technique builds upon Finding the Water's Depths, avoiding dice-adding limitations by doubling 9s.

                                                The Water Signature Charm, Distraction of the Babbling Brook, lets you bypass certain Intimacies in the target with a bargain where they unwittingly agree to more than they think, but you're probably going to want to throw Finding the Water's Depths and Thoughtful Gift Technique behind that dice-roll.

                                                Drowning In Negotiation helps enforce any bargain, including onerous ones imposed with Distraction of the Babbling Brook.

                                                The Wood Signature Charm of One Forest, Many Trees might allow you to get what you want without needing to persuade or bargain with anyone, if what you want can be gained through this Charm.
