    Originally posted by WalkingEye View Post
    So Spirit Stoking Elevation Allows you to replace the experience point cost of certain charms with expenditures of white points at a rate of 5wxp:1xp. Does this allow someone using it on Flowing Mind Prana and rolls to regain spent xp do I regain 5wxp per success on the roll or 1xp per every success on the roll? Because it is replacing experience points with 5wxp all the way through the charms effect or only on the initial cost?
    This is one of the many things on which 3E is frustratingly unclear.
    The way my group has decided to read it is this: you can replace the cost of Flowing Mind Prana with an expenditure of wxp, so you won't pay anything upfront. However, when you "roll to regain xp" Flowing Mind Prana tells you the dice you roll depend on how many xp you spent. Since you spent no xp (you spent wxp!), you regain no xp! And you regain no wxp either, because Flowing Mind Prana does not give you back wxp.
    I think this is the strictest possible reading of the rules, and the one that least sacrifices game balance. That said, even with this restrictive reading, if you play the Flowing Mind Prana game with your circlemates (particularly if you add Tireless Learner Method) you'll still gain quite a bit of extra xp over the "standard" 5+4/session even without using Spirit Stoking Elevation.

                                                        Originally posted by Elfive View Post
                                                        Except that not what you're doing at all. It's more like going to the restaurant and buying a $200 dinner but paying in euros.
                                                        Not quite. You can freely convert back and forth between Euros and Dollars. For almost all legal purposes, they are "equivalent" (with a lot of caveats).
                                                        You can't convert back and forth between wxp and xp.

                                                        Remember: Spirit Stoking Elevation does not allow you to convert wxp into xp.
                                                        It does not allow you to replace xp with wxp. Almost, but not quite.
                                                        It allows you to replace an expenditure of xp with an expenditure of wxp in Charms satisfying certain conditions.
                                                        One expenditure becomes another expenditure. The simplest, most straightforward interpretation is that everything else remains the same.

                                                        That said, it's by no means the only interpretation, as I started by saying. One of the things I strongly dislike about Exalted 3E is that, of the editions so far, it's the most ambiguous in its mechanics. In some sense, I find that more annoying than being poorly balanced.
