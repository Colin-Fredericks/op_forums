    Originally posted by TaintedBalance View Post
    I just added some Shaping Rituals that were specific to Martial Arts and you'd do shape sorcery actions but roll (Stat specific to the Shaping Ritual+MA) instead of (Int+Occ) and you could actually hold onto them instead of doing it for a specific action (they'd all drain after a scene). Then the motes could ONLY be spent on MA charms. All of this is just systems, change out the facade and keep the same scaffolding.

    Also, if you really do want MA to be cool and interesting, you should absolutely get rid of the merit, its a real terrible tax. I also actually got rid of Archery, Brawl, Melee and Thrown, combining them all under MA, with your style determining what you knew how to use and what you had access to but this is getting pretty far off topic. The point being that adding in ways to model out Mortals getting supernatural MA and MA charms is already in the system, you just got switch out some pieces.

    We also did this, Mortal enlightened MA as an alternative form of sorcery and it worked well. Besides, if you can learn to draw in motes to power reality altering to create a magical phoenix to blow up shit, why not to power up your kung-fu, right?

    We used sorcerous workings with this for various sorts of training and self-improvement, but most importantly to attune artifacts and unlock/learn charms & evocations . Anyway, it was fun and felt balanced enough. You are still far from the power-level of the actual exalts, but a cut above the norm even if the world is still pretty, pretty scary!
