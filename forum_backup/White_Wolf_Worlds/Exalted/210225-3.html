                Originally posted by TheCountAlucard View Post
                "As long as I maintain this zero-mote commitment, none of my Martial Arts Charms cost any motes at all!"


                "As long as I maintain this zero-mote commitment, this guy is trapped in a mirror with no way to escape!"

                Ugh, definitely don't need the sutras to be represented by flat discounts anymore.
                To be fair, those were broken even without the sutra issue.

                CPoC had a charm that devastated a wide area and could be activated repeatedly on successive actions to affect a wider and wider area. It cost 6m 1wp IIRC; the author apparently assumed it would be all right because you would run out of motes or WP if you kept doing it. Whoops, the Elder Sutra makes it free and turns it into single-charm, slightly-slower Creation-Slaying Oblivion Kick.

                Inner Dragon Unbinding was basically a 12m 1wp action-long perfect, which became a 2m action-long perfect with the Elder Sutra. It had limitations, but they were no worse than a FoI -- it only worked against attacks enhanced by magic in some way, and they could get through it by rolling their Essence against a difficulty of your Essence, which... I think the author didn't realize how dice worked? Especially since it was most often going to be used by elders with higher essence than you, meaning you'd need to roll all successes and multiple 10's? Good luck rolling 10 successes on 5 dice in order to hit Kejak Chejop! Or you can just not use your Excellency, any artifacts, or any other charms, I'm sure you'll have no problem hitting him like that. Don't worry, he's paying 2m an action to do this, so he'll run out as long as he doesn't do any one-die stunts.

                (It also had other benefits -- it basically cured almost all debuffs IIRC, or something like that -- so it was all right with no sutra as an emergency button, and very usable as typically-costed 2.0e action-length perfect with the Student's Sutra. It was only the Elder Sutra that broke it.)

                                    Originally posted by Weimann View Post

                                    By the way, isn't Fate more like the physics of Creation? If you wanted to punch someone into their partner breaking up with them, wouldn't that be more to do with their Destiny?
                                    Yeeah, either they were house-ruling that charm, or they got it confused with another one.

                                    Pattern Spider Bite changes target's Identities, so you could turn that Deathknight into a duck or a normal little girl or whatever. The writer specified that this was effectively an outward change, so you'd now have a duck or little girl *with the character sheet* of a Deathknight, but that isn't actually in the charm text, and so isn't canon. Still, it is a cool way to have your Animal/Kid Exalted!

                                    The charm does say that you can change a target's memories, so you could make the Deathknight think he was always your pet duck or your daughter, and he would act appropriately. But if you accept the writers' intent, then they would still act like a Deathknight, at least occasionally, presumably to protect you, their owner/father. And you might have to explain to them why blood is pouring from their forehead...

                                    Oh, you can also turn someone into an actual Elemental, so you can turn your Deathknight target into, I dunno, a Fire Butterfly, which causes them to lose Exaltation, even if you turn them back (or someone else does, say with Perfect Reconstruction Method, which can undo PSB). And, as noted, you can just utterly disintegrate them.

                                    Wasn't Exalted fun before Shaping defenses?
