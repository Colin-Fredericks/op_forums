                                                Anyway can we at least agree that if Lunars need more charms for sneakiness, these charms should absolutely not be in Dexterity?

                                                As I pointed out, Lunars have tons of sneaky-bastard charms in Manipulation, Appearance, and Charisma. (It's reasonably obvious that eg Clover Can't Be Found is meant to cover a lot of what you want. You might take issue with the design of that charm or argue that it doesn't do enough, but that's another question.)

                                                But clearly given how Lunar abilities are laid out and given that Dexterity could in theory do basically everything, I think it's clear that the Lunar design chose to instead assign certain capacities to specific abilities as a unique Lunar theme, and that, for Lunars, Dexterity does not generally get sneakiness charms, regardless of its application in Dexterity + Stealth rolls in the larger system. I find it hard to see how anyone could disagree with this decision -- are you going to argue that Dexterity doesn't do enough?

                                                (Making Lunar sneakiness charms exclusively dependent on social abilities also gives it a unique flavor compared to the Stealth charms of many other splats, which tend to focus on the physical act of sneaking real well. Not everyone should do stealth the same way; there's no reason Lunars should need things like that if eg. Appearance charms like Clover Can't Be Found can cover what's necessary.)

                                                Notice that these charms tend to focus on situations where you have a reason to want to keep your human form -- they're disguise charms with some stealth applications. I think that makes sense, because, again, if you just want to use straightforward stealth you're probably relying primarily on shapeshifting. (Although Clover Can't Be Found -- conceptually, putting aside arguments over its specific mechanical implementation -- is still very useful either way, whether in human or animal form.)

                                                Regarding the sneaking-as-a-rat issue -- what do you people think happens if someone rolls successfully to see you as a rat? Seriously. Unless they're looking for a Lunar specifically, their reaction is going to be "ugh, another rat; this job sucks", not "ugh, a rat; it must be a shapeshifting Anathema!" Assuming you're sneaking into someplace where rats are common (or at least not so rare that a guard will drop everything to hunt one down), it's not a roll that actually matters, because nothing will happen when you fail... so it's not a dramatically-appropriate thing to actually bother rolling.

                                                Even if they are looking for you, jumping at every single rat is going to cause problems for them. (And you can, with the right knack, become a fly instead. Are they going to treat every fly they spot as a full-scale Anathema invasion?)

                                                As a ST I might roll to see if they spot you as an innocuous animal, but the only way it would matter is that, the next day when the guards discover what you've done -- and if they realize they were hit by a shapeshifting Anathema -- one of them might slap his forehead and say "That rat! Oh, dammit dammit dammit!" But it will be too late for them to do anything at that point.

                                                Also notice the particular mechanical intersection of taking an innocuous animal form and using Clover Can't Be Found. If the guards aren't looking for you, they're not going to care about a rat; if they are looking for you, Clover Can't Be Found steps in and makes their job more difficult.
