            So I'm about halfway through the rules. And I think they're good?

            Social Influence seems pretty similar but a bit simpler, though I think the way you ignore influence might lead to some arguments. It may be more interesting than "spend 1wp and ignore, except when you can't". I can see people giving out extra dice to the person trying to convince them a lot because in an entirely social scene, does it matter if they get other dice?

            I think combat will be quicker. The basic idea of how Withering works seems good, and much quicker.

            But honestly, there's a lot of things I just don't quite understand because they're not well explained. And when I hear the writers explain them, it makes other things not make sense. I have been told some of this is versioning issues, but that does make it hard to judge, and without seeing the real version
            There's also some things that have been "simplified" but not in a way that I think actually makes things simpler. A lot of the static values for example. That's pretty minor.

            For example, a Lunar, Kovan the Librarian, swipes a demon with his mighty bear paw (Overwhelm 1). The demon has Defence 4. Kovan rolls 5 successes to hit. Does he get 1 power or 2? I'm not sure.

            His ally Fulgens Cruciatus, Dawn Caste warlord, swings his mighty artefact weapon (Overwhelm 3) at the demon. He rolls 5 successes, 1 more than Defence. Does he get 3 power or 4?

            The demon attacks Kovan (Defence 3). It rolls 3. Does he get 1 power or 0?

            Next round, Fulgens attacks again with his artefact weapon but rolls terribly and only get 1 success, missing. Does he get 0 Power? 1 Power? 3?

            I would really like to see the real, current version, because I don't know if that makes everything clear or not.

            Edit: I found the answer to my Decisive question, it seems Decisive attacks are explained in two different places.
