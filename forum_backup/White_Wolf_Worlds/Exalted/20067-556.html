                    Originally posted by Klaek View Post

                    Ex3 Core pg426: - "Martial Arts cannot be combined with other combat Abilities, they can be freely combined with one another,..."'

                    emphasis mine

                    We know the definition of combat abilities here has as you have listed. However this doesn't say martial arts can be freely combined with any abilities. just freely combined with one another. As in martial art + martial art. The exception to the base rules here is not that martial arts cannot combine with brawl etc, by the base rules they already cannot do that unless a martial art charm was written in a way to say it could. The exception is that martial arts can combine with martial arts, constrained by style weapons and armour.

                    As the charm we are talking about here is a reflexive charm we refer to the rules on reflexive charms.

                    pg253: "Reflexive Charms which enhance static values may generally only enhance static values deriving from their governing Ability, unless otherwise indicated. For example, Dipping Swallow Defense will only remove penalties from a Melee-based Parry attempt. "

                    Explicitly allowed by the Devs:

                    Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz View Post
                    A question about MA. Some Martial Arts charms enhance your Evasion. Can you benefit from Dodge charms and Martial Arts charms that enhance your Dodge on the same attack?
                    Yes.

                    I suppose this question should also apply to whether a Lunar could benefit from both Soak-enhancing stamina charms and Soak-enhancing MA charms against the same attack (or hardness).

                    Also yes. Same for Resistance Charms.

                                    Originally posted by Einlanzer View Post
                                    Like Difficulty 3 is a task that would daunt even heroes. Is repairing a suit of superheavy plate armor really a task that would daunt even heroes?
                                    I think that under normal circumstances, you probably can't really repair plate that's been heavily broken up, in realistic terms. I believe that if a piece of solid metal armour has been sufficiently damaged to compromise its effective defence, then normal capability would only have recourse to welding the pieces back together, which would not be much good for withstanding the rigours of battle. The gameplay act of restoring a thing to working order would be relying on genre convention short of actual magic, hence a somewhat higher difficulty.

                                    ​See also the idea of repairing a broken sword; shows up in a fair amount of fantasy fiction, which tends to skimp over detailing how the pieces are actually reconnected, and is quite untenable in real life.
