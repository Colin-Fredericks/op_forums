                                        I have not given Warstriders much detailed thought yet, but I do have a few generalities for how I currently envision them:

                                        I would like it if Warstriders in general had a clear primary purpose of being used against armies, giant monsters and geographic features. They shouldn't be useless in personal combat against a single formidable human sized enemy, but I'd like to see them somewhat out of scale for such a foe.

                                        I imagine that achieved primarily by making their stronger powers overkill against a single normal-sized person, and costed appropriately for that large scale, in resources like essence/willpower and/or tactical restrictions like set-ups (similar to Volcano Cutter, for example), charging time, difficult reset conditions, lower accuracy against small targets except in certain circumstances, et cetera. For instance, a hit-everything-in-a-mile Evocation that costs a lot of motes or requires quite a bit of set-up or charging or whatever to execute, when you really don't need to hit everything in a mile if your opponent is a single person.

                                        They should still have normal attacks and often lesser powers/Evocations that are appropriate for a fight against a dangerous normal-sized enemy, But I'd prefer if their best powers are usually too wasteful, large-scale, costly, and/or hard to use that it is often not worth applying for things other than vaporizing vast armies, mountains, Behemoths and things like that.


                                        Ideally, I would also like for them to feel a bit slow and clumsy, but powerful and hard to put down, compared to a similarly skilled character that is not in a Warstrider, unless that particular Strider's gimmick is exactly in being super fast/agile/mobile for such a huge thing, of course (though that probably means it has less in powers of utter destruction and invulnerability). Not sure if that feeling would just be a matter of stats like accuracy bonuses, mobility penalties, damage and soak/hardness or if some other mechanic would be needed to really make it feel right during play.

                                        Of course, a Solar with the right charms if gonna minimize or eliminate most of that relative clumsiness, but that investment could be going somewhere else and that else could be where a non-warstrider Solar gets the edge they need against the larger opponent.

                                        Finally, I want a "pankration" match between a DB brawler in a Warstrider and a Brawl/Resistance Solar to be an epic, drawn-out, slug-and-smash-fest for the ages.
