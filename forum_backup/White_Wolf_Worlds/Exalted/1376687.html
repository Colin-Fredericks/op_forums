    Evocations of the Talisman of Ten Thousand Eyes (Homebrew)

    03-26-2020, 04:35 AM
    This is my version of Evocations for the Talisman of Ten Thousand Eyes. Grateful for feedback and comments. Evocations in the spoiler bars for space.

    The Talisman of Ten Thousand Eyes
    (Orichalcum Artifact • • • •)

    A phylactery of great sorcerous puissance, this talisman is wrought from rune-etched orichalcum and set with a gleaming ruby of ten facets. Each facet seems to reﬂect ten more within its depths, which reﬂect even more, creating the illusion of infinity that gives this relic its name. You have claimed the talisman as your own, using it either to initiate yourself into sorcery or as a wellspring of power to draw upon. Its ten thousand eyes are ever watchful, giving warning when malefic forces roam unchecked, and serving as a lens through which you may cast your sight or presence.

    Attunement: 4m
    Tags: -
    Hearthstone Slots: 0
    Era: The Shinma Crusades
    Special: Only those who know the Old Realm language can attune to the Talisman.

    Evocations of The Talisman of Ten Thousand Eyes
    When attuned to the Talisman, the bearer’s irises turn ruby red. She enjoys an automatic success on sight-based Awareness tests and may roll a special shape sorcery action; once she gathers 10sm any sorcerer or active sorcerous working within her field of vision glows red for the scene.



    The Facets of Essence
    All-Tongue Technique
    Cost: 2m; Mins: Essence 1
    Type: Reflexive or Supplemental
    Keywords: Mute, Psyche
    Duration: One Scene or Instant
    Prerequisites: -
    The bearer whispers the secrets of the universal language into a target’s mind.

    For 2 motes, those who hear her speak always understand her word’s meaning for the rest of the scene, as if she spoke a language they know with a slightly odd foreign accent. Moreover, by spending 2 motes she can instead make her words echo in the back of their mind, allowing her to speak a few sentences in total silence via telepathy as a miscellaneous action.

    Reach Beyond Flesh
    Cost: 4m; Mins: Essence 2
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: -
    Duration: One Scene
    Prerequisites: -
    Channelling her essence through the talisman, the bearer reaches beyond the limits of her physical form.

    Extending tendrils of shimmering ruby-red essence she may manipulate any object out to short range as if she were using her hands for the scene. Her effective Dexterity equals her Perception, and her effective Strength equals her Wits. She may attempt Feats of Strength using Wits+Occult, but may not make attacks.

    The bearer can conjure (Essence-1, min 1) tendrils without penalty. Each additional tendril reduces her effective Dexterity and Strength with all of them by 1, min 0.

    Walk the God’s Road
    Cost: Varies; Mins: Essence 2
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: Perilous
    Duration: Indefinite
    Prerequisites: -
    The bearer cloaks herself in the Essence of the Gods and vanishes. She pays the value of half her Personal mote pool rounded up (either Personal or Peripheral motes may be used) and becomes immaterial. These motes are not committed, but she must pay it again to end the Evocation..

    Whilst dematerialised she may see but not interact with the physical world, but can interact with dematerialised beings and objects, including entering Spirit's sanctums. Maintaining this state requires careful focus and any rolled actions take a -1 penalty. If she botches a roll, her concentration breaks and she materialises immediately, suffering catastrophic wounds if she is partially inside an object.

    The rush of essence in combat makes this state fallible. Upon joining battle she becomes visible as a translucent silhouette. Any material creature may pay 1WP to be able to attack her for the scene, albeit with a -1 penalty.

    Caress of the Spectre
    Cost: - ; Mins: Essence 3
    Type: Permanent
    Keywords: Mute
    Duration: Permanent
    Prerequisites: Reach Beyond Flesh
    The Talisman reflects a false reality, concealing the bearer’s actions.

    When using Reach Beyond Flesh, she may decide to no longer show any visual display. A Perception+Occult roll of her (Essence+1) difficulty, can identify the mystic emanations and their source, but only if the sceptic has reason to suspect magic or other powers to detect it first.

    Resonant: If the bearer is resonant with orichalcum, her invisible tendrils can pass through mundane matter unimpeded. The Magical Materials and those reinforced with essence remain impervious.

    Special: Awakening this Evocation prevents the bearer from ever unlocking Fist That Shatters the Veil.

    Fist That Shatters The Veil
    Cost: 1i; Mins: Essence 3
    Type: Supplementary
    Keywords: Uniform
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisites: Reach Beyond Flesh
    The Talisman brings the bearer into sharp focus, infinite reflections multiplying her power.

    When using Reach Beyond Flesh, the bearer may pay one initiative to strike with a tendril as if she were using a heavy artifact melee weapon, her attacks and parry use Perception+Occult. Tendrils have the Grappling, Lethal, Natural and Reaching tags and attacks are limited to close range. This Evocation does not grant her more than one attack per round.

    Dissonant: A bearer dissonant with orichalcum uses the stats for a heavy mundane weapon instead.

    Special: Awakening this Evocation prevents the bearer from ever unlocking Caress of the Spectre.

    Magick From Within
    Cost: 26m, 2WP; Mins: Essence 4
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: Perilous
    Duration: One Scene
    Prerequisites: All-Tongue Technique, Walk the God’s Road, Caress of the Spectre or Fist That Shatters the Veil
    Merging the Talisman with her flesh, the bearer becomes a font of mystic energies. Her Talisman’s gem and her caste mark (or other similar display) become amethyst purple and her eyes flash with magic.

    Each round she may choose to be either material or immaterial, taking the benefits that best suit each moment. Whilst immaterial, she may rise or descend one range band as part of her movement action.

    Resonant: For those resonant with orichalcum this Evocation does not have the Perilous tag.

    Special: The bearer cannot combine this Evocation with Magick from Without or Magick From Beyond.

    The Facets of Spellcraft
    Arcane Awakening
    Cost: -; Mins: Essence 1
    Type: Permanent
    Keywords: -
    Duration: Permanent
    Prerequisites: -
    The bearer looks into the ruby and sees ten thousand reflections of herself, unlocking a deeper understanding of the world. If she is not already a Sorcerer she initiates as one at the Terrestrial level, gaining one of the Talisman’s shaping rituals and a Control spell.

    If she is already a sorcerer then she gains an additional one of the Talisman’s shaping rituals and both the Astral Meditation and Eye of Crimson Warning merits.

    Special: This Evocation is not the charms the Exalted typically use to initiate into Sorcery. It does not qualify as the charm “Terrestrial Circle Sorcery” for the prerequisites of other magic and does not grant the Thaumaturgy merit. If she does subsequently learn that charm, then she does not gain a new control spell but gains the second benefit of this Evocation.

    Cunning Wizardry
    Cost: 4sm; Mins: Essence 2
    Type: Supplementary
    Keywords: -
    Duration: One Spell
    Prerequisites: Arcane Awakening
    When casting a spell, the sorcerer conceals it’s pattern within her Talisman’s hidden refractions, triggering one of two effects.

    Countermagic: By sacrificing the first four sorcerous motes she gathers (this does not end the shaping attempt if it reduces her back to zero), she reduces the successes of an opposing sorcerer’s countermagic rolls by her (Essence).

    Distortion: By gathering four additional motes above the total required, the sorcerer adds her (Essence x2) to the Distortion goal number of the spell being cast.

    Resonant: A sorcerer resonant with orichalcum’s Control Spell can never be distorted if cast with this Evocation.

    Warlock’s Invocation
    Cost: 8m; Mins: Essence 3
    Type: Supplementary
    Keywords: -
    Duration: One Spell
    Prerequisites: Cunning Wizardry
    The Talisman is a wellspring of power, this Evocation lets the sorcerer tap into that.

    By doubling the sorcerous motes required to cast a spell the sorcerer adds her (Essence) to any relevant aspect of the spell that is based on her stats. This Evocation has no effect on spells of Ritual cost.

    Eg. casting Corrupted Words adds to the binding roll, Flight of Separation adds it to the distance she can travel, Seafoam Sculpted Eidolon adds it to the number of replicas a sorcerer can control as a control effect, etc. The Storyteller is the final arbiter of what constitutes a relevant aspect.

    Omniscient Spellweaving Technique
    Cost: 13m, 1WP; Mins: Essence 3
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: -
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisites: Cunning Wizardry
    The sorcerer sees alternate realities within her talisman’s jewel, choosing a preferred pattern of arcane essence.

    By meditating on these reflections in her talisman for 7 hours, she may change her control spell to another spell she knows for each circle she is initiated into. As well as motes and willpower, she must also sacrifice a minor Intimacy; rearranging her essence patterns causes irrevocable changes to her mind. Using this Evocation alters her anima display (if applicable) to reflect these changes.

    This Evocation may only be used once per story, unless reset by sacrificing a Defining intimacy with an appropriately significant display (ie stunt) - casting a piece of her soul away for sorcerous expedience.

    Magick from Without
    Cost: 23m, 2WP; Mins: Essence 4
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: Perilous
    Duration: One Scene
    Prerequisites: Omniscient Spellweaving Technique, Warlock’s Invocation
    Merging the Talisman with her anima, the bearer becomes a font of mystic energies. Her Talisman’s gem and her caste mark (or other similar display) become emerald green and her eyes flash with magic.

    She automatically gains (Essence) sorcerous motes every round regardless of whether she is actively shaping a spell or not, and the WP cost of any spells she casts are reduced by 1 (min 1). These motes are stored in her Talisman and may be claimed for any spell she casts or are lost at the end of the scene. Outside of battle she cannot botch a sorcerous working roll whilst this Evocation is active.

    Resonant: For those resonant with orichalcum this Evocation does not have the Perilous tag.

    Special: The bearer cannot combine this Evocation with Magick from Beyond or Magick From Within.

    The Facets of Senses
    Mindful Insight Meditation
    Cost: 6m, 1WP; Mins: Essence 1
    Type: Supplementary
    Keywords: Psyche (Profile Character only)
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisites: -
    The Talisman allows the bearer to briefly transcend the limits of her mundane senses.

    Case Scene: As part of the dramatic action to case a scene, the bearer observes her location through the gem in her talisman. On a successful roll shadows of the moment when the clue was placed appear to her. They are distorted, but offer a more specific clue, or perhaps allow her the chance to try and identify those present or some other materially beneficial factor.

    Profile Character: The bearer places the talisman to her ear and hears whispers from the target’s mind. On a successful roll she receives a relevant clue or detail based on what the character is thinking. If that is not relevant then she receives some equally difficult to recognise clue that would not normally have been available based on his thoughts and feelings.

    Senses and Soul Entwined
    Cost: 2m; Mins: Essence 1
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: -
    Duration: Reflexive
    Prerequisites: -
    The bearer may see through any mundane impediment. She sees in pitch darkness as easily as she does in daylight, fog and smoke do not hamper her and she may see the bottom of a lake when submerged. This Evocation does not defeat mystical darkness and other things, but she adds half her (Essence) rounded up to her Perception rolls.

    Further, with a dedicated miscellaneous action she may see through any mundane material out to short range; the Magical Materials and those reinforced with essence remain impervious.

    Through Distant Eyes
    Cost: 10m, 1WP; Mins: Essence 2
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: Psyche
    Duration: One Scene
    Prerequisites: Mindful Insight Meditation, Senses and Soul Entwined
    Staring deeply into the jewel, the bearer’s senses transcend mundane distance. The bearer focuses on someone important to her, represented by a Major or Defining intimacy. After one minute she rolls (Perception+Occult) vs their Resolve and on a success her senses leave her, transported to a new body anywhere in the world by the Talisman.

    She sees with her target’s eyes, but has no control over his actions; she cannot make him look in a specific direction and if he is asleep she sees nothing. With 2 excess successes she may also hear with the target’s ears. 3 adds his sense of taste, 4 his sense of smell and 5 adds his sense of touch. If the target is missing a sense then the user does not gain that benefit.

    Whilst using this Evocation the bearer has no awareness of her physical body, she is unmoving and helpless (parry/evasion 0). She may end the Evocation at will or with an instant reflexive action if she is harmed.

    Those with an Essence score equal to or greater than the user’s may instinctively and automatically prevent her surveillance. Without Occult rolls to figure it out they might not know exactly what is happening, but they feel their feet itch or their hair stand on end and know 'something' has just happened. If they have an intimacy towards the bearer, they feel her presence and know she caused this sensation.

    Resonant: This Evocation is normally limited to the same realm the user is in (Creation, Underworld, Yu Shan, Malfeas, etc.). Those resonant with Orichalcum and who have a Defining intimacy towards their target may observe him regardless of these boundaries.

    Ever-Watchful Guardian
    Cost: 3m or 6m; Mins: Essence 3
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: -
    Duration: Instant or Indefinite
    Prerequisites: Through Distant Eyes
    The Ten Thousand Eyes are vigilant for those that seek to do their bearer harm.

    The bearer cannot be ambushed, activating this charm reflexively for 3m automatically makes her aware of any attack that would normally set her defence to zero. If the attack is supplemented by magic that allows an ambush regardless, then she rolls (Wits+Awareness) to oppose it.

    The indefinite version costs 6m and adds half the bearer’s (Essence) rounded up to her Parry, Evasion, Resolve, Guile and any other unrolled defence.

    Magick From Beyond
    Cost: 34m, 2WP; Mins: Essence 4
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: Decisive-only, Perilous, Psyche
    Duration: One Scene
    Prerequisites: Ever-Watchful Guardian
    Merging the Talisman with her chakras, the bearer becomes a font of mystic energies. Her Talisman’s gem and her caste mark (or other similar display) become sapphire blue and her eyes flash with magic.

    The bearer may use her movement action during her turn to instantly move anywhere within medium range that she can see without crossing the distance between, without needing a combat movement or go to ground roll. Once per session she may perfectly defend against a decisive attack by projecting herself into the attacker’s senses, distracting him and causing him to miss. This immediately ends this Evocation.

    Resonant: For those resonant with orichalcum this Evocation does not have the Perilous tag.

    Special: The bearer cannot combine this Evocation with Magick from Within or Magick From Without.

    The Facets Aligned
    Special: Artifact •••• is limited to 15 Evocations..To unlock this Evocation, the bearer must transform her Talisman, replacing the sigils and runes it bears with her own legend. This requires a precise Solar Circle Working (Ambition 1, Finesse 5), the xp spent on this working contributes to the cost of this Evocation. The Talisman’s gem (and the bearer’s irises) become diamond white as she unifies its powers.

    It becomes a Talisman of One and One Million Eyes (Artifact •••••). Truly legendary sorcerers may thereafter unlock even mightier evocations as the Talisman’s wellspring of power becomes infinite. At that point the Shinma and principles of essence itself bend to the will of these Archmages.

    The Eye of Infinity
    Cost: 55m, 3WP or 11m; Mins: Essence 5
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: -
    Duration: One Scene
    Prerequisites: Magic From Beyond, Magick From Within, Magick From Without
    The bearer becomes a singularity of supreme power as her magic shapes the cosmos like clay. The least effect of this Evocation is that once per story, the bearer may unleash the powers of Magick From Beyond, Magick from Within and Magick From Without simultaneously for a scene.

    But the true power of this Evocation is felt outside of combat. Forgoing the above effect, once per session she may roll (Intelligence+Occult) taking an extended action (Difficulty: 3, Goal Number: 7, Interval: one minute, Terminus: Essence). Success allows her to instantly bring any Terrestrial Circle, Finesse 3 Sorcerous Working into effect without any other considerations. The bearer reshapes reality to simply bring her vision to life without cost, the Working only lasting until the end of the session. Repeating workings is impossible, if the bearer tries the ST must require them to pay XP to make it permanent. Workings that create or summon beings cannot be used with this Evocation.

    Alternatively, for 11m the bearer may make the same roll to create a portal to another dimension (Creation, Underworld, Yu Shan, Malfeas etc.). The portal lasts 1 round, 1 minute or indefinitely at the bearer’s discretion. Unlike the above two effects, portals may be created as often as desired.

    Resonant: Those resonant with orichalcum may increase the Goal number to 21 and undertake Celestial Circle, Finesse 3 workings.
