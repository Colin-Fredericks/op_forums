            Even to the extent that a creature is inhuman, it must still be comprehensibly a creature. Its motives would include self-preservation, often including perpetuation of itself or species through some form of reproduction. It can seem incomprehensible to the extent that the people encountering it do not understand its language, customs, traditions, or the methods and means by which it sustains itself and makes more of its own kind.

            A creature can be made inherently hostile to the extent that its survival depends on preying on humanity exclusively or at least primarily, rather than preying upon easier targets like whales, cows, zebra. Prey that gathers in herds, but that does not have the capacity to organize a counter-offensive.
