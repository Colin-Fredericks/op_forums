                                        Originally posted by Sakii View Post
                                        What would be the level of a working to create a size 3 supernatural army?

                                        Hmm. Celestial 2 grants a supernatural power to one individual. Celestial 3 actually creates an individual with significant supernatural power. I would say take those, bump them up to Solar, and you should be fine. Solar 2 would probably be Might 1. Solar 3 would probably be Might 3.

                                        As an ST, I would work with the player to define this quite a bit more. A generic size 3 supernatural army is kinda silly. It should have some definition about how it is supernatural. What powers does it have? What weaknesses? Are they free willed? Do they require upkeep? Lots of possibilities. Part of that would be guided by the Finesse chosen as well.
