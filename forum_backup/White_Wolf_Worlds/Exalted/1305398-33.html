                Normally with really powerful singular enemies these problems don’t exist. The enemies that can boost their defense high don’t also have cheap penalty negators, if they have any at all, and the ones that do are usually legendary size things that have a resting defense of no more than 5-6. So you get to use all your really cool charms without wasting them.                             I wanted to see a little fight with Buck Ogres for myself so I ran one, I figured that work might as well not go to waste so I'll post it here. I was trying to see if Buck-Ogres had enough oomph to actually be a drain on motes, or if fighting them would actually give the party more with regen than they spent on attack and defense. To that end I didn't want the PCs to just blow 50 motes and 3 willpower on turn one and win, which they could have easily done, because that's an unrealistic amount of effort for combatants who are expecting maybe to fight something else later, so I used motes sparingly.

                            I feel like I might have misplayed the ogres a bit, their ability to spend a willpower for two attacks is really good. I honestly wasn't expecting them to live past turn 3 anyway, so that's why I blew all the willpower right away.

                            Anyway

                            Darius

                            Join Battle 10 +10
                            Short Daiklaves 16 +10 (Damage 13/3)
                            Parry 6
                            Soak 14/10


                            Excellent Strike
                            Fire and Stones Strike

                            Peony Blossom Technique
                            Dipping Swallow Defense

                            Hungry Tiger Technique

                            Reed in the Wind
                            Shadow Over Water

                            Darius 7i 31m 5wp
                            Twila 3i 14m 5wp 10L (-2)

                            ----------------

                            Darius 7i 22m 5wp
                            Twila 3i 14m 5wp 10L (-2)


                            So being down on average more than 20 motes each and a big chunk of health levels the ogres did surprisingly well. Of course they weren't facing optimized combatants, but not everyone in a group will be sporting that kind of punch anyway. If I fought 4 PCs against 4 of them, and had one of the ogres maybe as an ogre champion with a bonus 2 dice to attack and parry, that might work out.

                            The ogres have really crappy accuracy on average, but with 13 soak and 5 parry they're actually reasonably tough. If you don't spend anything on attack you're going to be a while whittling them down. Also just the factor of luck, if they're all making 2 attacks every round some of them are going to get lucky, but you don't know which ones those are until you've already spent your motes on defense.

                            It could just be a fluke, of course, the Ogres got pretty lucky, and the Solars were somewhat badly suited for fighting.
