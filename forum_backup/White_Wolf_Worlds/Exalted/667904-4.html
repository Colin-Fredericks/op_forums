                                        Taking my own suggestion on 'How to Abyssal Sig'
                                        Here’s my suggestions:
                                        * The Bloody-Handed General (Loyalist):
                                        A Daybreak Caste who specializes in mass combat. In life, she served as a distinguished general for the Perfect of Paragon, with a distinguished career. Not the most personally martially inclined (although no slouch in a fight), she was more noted as being a brilliant tactician and strategiest, inventing new gambits and often anticipating her opponents traps and counter-trapping them. She lived for the praise and the limelight, easily acquired in her youth and middle age; lost when she was forced into retirement. Old now, a fever took her…

                                        Except the First and Forsaken Lion knows talent when he sees it. The Blood-Handed General is one of his most loyal servants, leading the Lion’s war machine against their neighbors, and basks in the adulation of her immortal troops.

                                        * The Fourth Terror of Night Eternal (Malcontent)
                                        A Day Caste who specializes in inspiring revolutions. In life, he was a nobody; a slave-farmer in a village near Whitewall, he had no great aspirations. That changed when a priest-magistrate from the city came through his village, seizing the salt and supplies needed for his family to survive the winters for repairing the Great Northern Road. He struck the magistrate, and was summarily hanged by his guards, his goods and family forfeit to the City of Whitewall for striking it’s representative. As he was dancing on the impromptu gallows, he heard the Bishop of the Chalcedony Thurible whisper an offer- join me and you will have your vengeance.

                                        Since that time, The Fourth Terror of Night Eternal has moved through Whitewall, finding the desperate, the criminal, the forgotten of that sterile city, and spreading seditious words. Additionally, he walks more openly under a false name as a foreign prince, nominally representing the interests of a distant land while encouraging the upper classes to grow only more brutal, paranoid and repressive. Bloody revolution that will crack the walls of Whitewall and leave the city ripe for the taking seems to be only a matter of time.

                                        But The Fourth Terror has a weakness now; he has fallen in love with a young nobleman who works in charity for the underclasses. At first he tried to divert his love away from his mission, but now finds himself questioning it entirely; he well knows the pain and destruction that will accompany this revolution, and is certain his lover will perish in those flames. He continues for the moment, but with far less fervor than before.
