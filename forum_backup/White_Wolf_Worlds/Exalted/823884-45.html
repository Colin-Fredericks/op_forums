                I’m working on the final evocation for a fairly complex 5-dot artifact one of my players purchased, and could use some outside input on how it should function.

                The artifact concept is a daiklave constructed out of the dragonfly wing of a raksha lord, transmuted into a blade via Wyld-shaping charms to imprison him in a tortured stasis for all time. Each charm in the tree is keyed to a Grace, a spiritual focus comprising one of the raksha’s Virtues, with a power themed accordingly, which can be purchased after cultivating a relevant Intimacy – what would have been getting a 4+ in the relevant virtue in 1e/2e. I’ll post the whole thing here if there’s any interest.

                The charm I’m working on now is the capstone. Conceptually, it’s about forcing open the raksha’s sealed up Virtue-eating Graces, waking it up enough to realize it’s starving and imprisoned, and letting it rip the consciousness and dream-stuff out of anyone sufficiently heroic to provide it with sustenance. It’s an ongoing Willpower and Intimacy drain on multiple targets acting on a separate initiative tick, and the more it drains, the more it charges up the other four evocations. The drawback is that the more Willpower / Intimacies it drinks, the harder it is to turn it off, and if it gets enough to first open all four evocations, then unlock the fifth, the imprisoned raksha escapes the sword by fashioning a god-monster body and porting his Heart Grace over to it, then going on a Wyld-spewing kaiju rampage.

                I’m currently struggling with two issues: targeting the Willpower drain, and pacing the rate of evocation buffs and the eventual “Oh shit I created a behemoth” doomsday clock. The two are so closely linked that I’m having trouble pulling them apart.

                Targeting: Who gets hit by the Willpower drain, and why? I want it to be algorithmic rather than controlled by the swordsman, but predictable in such a way that it can be manipulated through clever play. Is it based on something the target does, like mote or Willpower expenditure that draws the raksha’s attention? Is it based on something the target is / has, like a Major or Defining Intimacy – possibly ones the raksha is particularly interested in, such as ones related to Valor (personal prowess and greatness, terror and dissolution, etc.) or Conviction (upholding a principle of governance, cultural structure, etc.) ? Or is it based on something that is done to them, like being the target of earlier evocations in the tree? And if multiple targets fit the criteria for draining, can it affect them all on a single turn?

                At the moment I’m inclined to have it be some combination of something the target does, and something they are or have, but I haven’t been able to nail it down yet. Partly that’s because the choice of who gets drained influences…

                Pacing: How quickly does the drain fill up the Grace pools, empower other evocations, and unleash a monster? Given that the pace of any combat serious enough for a capstone evocation to get brought out is probably going to be pretty slow and rocket-tag-y, should the Graces unlock rapidly (a turn or two of successful charging) so that they can actually get used? Or should it be more gradual, so that the PCs have more time to dance on the edge of impending doom, balancing the build-up of the evocations with the growing threat of their own weapon turning against them?

                Other Considerations: The swordsman in question is a Melee- and Presence-focused Eclipse Caste of the “flare up hard and Terrifying Apparition everything” variety, paired up with a pretty standard stealth-archer Night Caste, in a two-player game with long (8+ hour) sessions. Earlier evocations in the tree are:
                • Rush enhancer that penalizes Disengages and prevents some onslaught from resetting.
                • Reflexive Persuade after crashing someone to get them to surrender or switch sides, and empowers them if they do.
                • Bulwark Stance equivalent that substitutes Resolve (+ applicable Intimacies) for Parry, and uses Intimacies to defend unblockable attacks.
                • Expend a resource built up with the other charms to create an AoE environmental hazard / difficult terrain that protects the subject of an Principle Intimacy.



                Thoughts? Suggestions? This is my first evocation-building attempt, and I've certainly thrown myself into the deep end to learn how to swim, so I'm open to critique.

                            #1: It suffers from lack of context. There's lore behind it, but it's irrelevant to anyone outside our game and to mechanics design, so I won't waste anyone's time with it here unless requested.

                            #2a: Emphatically not, hence the risk presented by the countdown. The concept is a charm that whose effects are tempting, but which is actively dangerous to keep running too long and inclined to China Syndrome if you aren't careful and a bit lucky.

                            #2b: Good question, actually. Killing the damned raksha once and for all, probably. There's what remains of a Lunar trapped in there somewhere too, so they could in theory free what's left of her as a grandiose act of compassion.

                            #3: Again, long story. The evocation unlocks are triggered by cultivating intimacies keyed to the virtue the charm represents, either in a form in the sleeping raksha approves of (the easy way) or which it must grudgingly accept (the hard way). It's a very slow, subtle form of manipulation designed to either make the swordsman into itself - beautiful and terrible and heartless and mad - or make its captor do a stupid, terrible, eminently Solar-Great-Curse thing by accidentally or deliberately letting a powerful enemy out of its cage. More directly, the behemoth itself takes its shape from the swordsman's intimacies, and directs its rampage at them.

                            #4: A principle is a form of intimacy, as opposed to a tie. Beliefs and ideals for principles, attachments to concrete entities for ties. Read it as "a Defining Principle-type Intimacy."

                            #5a/b: The mechanics are draft-one complete, pending a second-draft pass once the fifth evocation is done. They're in draft form rather than completed write-ups, however, so it would take a lot of formatting and copy-editing to get them in a state where I'd want to post them on a forum. I'm in the midst of that, but I can answer questions about them in detail in the mean time if necessary.

                                                    Sorry if this breaks up the flow of the discussion, but this is still a workshop right? Here's a few new ones I threw together recently, all once again based off a musical soundtrack.

                                                    __________________________________________________ ____________________

                                                    Death Mocking Shroud (***dot) Five Jade Mask
                                                    Attunement Cost: 5m
                                                    Hearthstone slots: NA
                                                    Story:
                                                    Originally crafted to be a death mask for her husband’s funeral, the item took on a new life when the would be wearer seemingly returned to life. Named the Death Mocking Shroud in celebration of this event, the title quickly took on a new meaning when everything began to spiral out of control. The man who had seemingly returned to life had been missing for close to a decade, but despite the embrace of his wife and children, claimed to be a different man entirely. Whether or not this was a cruel charade or not is lost to time, as the great celebration the renamed reviver threw for unknown reasons quickly became a bloodbath. If there were any survivors, time does not recall them. It does however leave us with this item, burned with the blood of the lover who forged it and the man whom it was for.
                                                    The Death Mocking Shroud was originally designed as a death mask, an item that would cover the face of the recently departed as they were sent on to the whatever lies beyond. This mask was as much a work of love as it was of skill and talent. The majority of the mask is comprised of White Jade, giving the appearance of a pale specter. Two great tear like streams of Blue Jade descend from the ovals of Black Jade that surround the eye holes. Bright with passion, or perhaps embarrassment, are two rings of Red Jade, one on each cheek. The interior of the mask is a mirror of the exterior, different only in the fact that where there had once been White Jade flesh, there was now Green Jade flesh. The mask is surprisingly flexible and could be worn with either side facing away from the wears face, but neither side holds even the slightest hint at a gap for one’s mouth. It is considered a great work of art by many, and ownership is a symbol of status within the Scavenger Lands. Who holds it now is unknown, though it is likely to change ownership thrice over within a passing of the season. By what means does it change hands is… not always pleasant.

                                                    Evocations:
                                                    On attunement the exalt gains no noticeable affects. When attuned and wearing the Death Mocking Shroud however, the exalt must choose to automatically activate one of the evocations “I Know Those Eyes” or “This Man is Dead.” The evocation activated remains in effect until the mask is removed.

                                                    This Man is Dead
                                                    Cost: - (2m x hour) Mins: Essence 1
                                                    Type: Simple
                                                    Keywords: Mute, Psyche
                                                    Duration: Indefinite
                                                    Prerequisites: None

                                                    “This man is dead. He is no more. He died a little each day…”

                                                    A mask makes the wearer a new person. Does this not mean the person they once were is, in some way, dead? By donning the Death Mocking Shroud the exalt kills the person they once were. All their intimacies, while they still exist, no longer matter to the exalt, and no new intimacies can be instilled. This blissful state of uncaring can only be ended by the removal of the artifact, a feat impossible for an hour after first donning the mask. For an incremental payment of motes when donning the mask initially, the removal can become impossible for great spans of time. Whether this is to escape ones reality, or cut out the feeling heart for the sake of the mission… is arguable at best.

                                                    I Know Those Eyes
                                                    Cost: - Mins: Essence 1
                                                    Type: Simple
                                                    Keywords: Psyche
                                                    Duration: Indefinite
                                                    Prerequisites: None

                                                    “I know those eyes following me, Dark and familiar and deep as the sea…
                                                    I know that face, strange though it seems, Younger and kinder it haunts all my dreams…”

                                                    The eyes are said to be the window to the soul. No matter the training or mental fortitude none can hide the flash of emotion or spark of passion within their own gateways. So long as the exalt wears the Death Mocking Shroud they can reflexively make a Read Intentions or Profile Character action on any individual they can see the eyes of, gaining 3 none-charm die in the process. If the exalt has an intimacy towards the target, or knew them in a past life (Storyteller digression advised [this does not assume Eidetic Memory includes past lives]) they gain extra die equal to the strength of the intimacy.

                                                    Walk Among the Living
                                                    Cost: 5m Mins: Essence 1
                                                    Type: Supplemental
                                                    Keywords: none
                                                    Duration: Instant
                                                    Prerequisites: This Man is Dead

                                                    “I am a ghost; just a mirage… Who chases traces of you.”

                                                    None notice the passage of the benign spirit. None would notice the passage of the vengeful spirit if the specter could mask their intent. With this, you have learned to be both specter and hider of dark thoughts. The exalt is allowed to make a Stealth action, adding their Resolve as non-charm die. As this evocation can only be used if This Man is Dead is in effect, attempts to raise or lower the exalts Resolve automatically fail.

                                                    Strange Form, Familiar Eyes
                                                    Cost: 3m (5m) Mins: Essence 1
                                                    Type: Supplement
                                                    Keywords: Psyche
                                                    Duration: Instant
                                                    Prerequisites: I Know Those Eyes

                                                    “In eyes once familiar a stranger I see,
                                                    With so many words left to say…”

                                                    Time can change us in many ways. It can find features far better than any disguise ever could. But the eyes… the eyes never change. The exalt gains 3 non-charm die to a disguise roll, be it themselves or a single individual. By paying the listed additional cost in addition to the base cost the exalt, should they have disguised themselves, will have a disguise flawless to every being of Creation that has not already seen their eyes. Those who have not seen the exalts eyes before will have their attempts to pierce the disguise be non-magic means reduced by 2 successes. Those who have become familiar with the exalts eyes will gain 2 automatic success on attempts to look past the disguise. This evocation can only be used if I Know Those Eyes is active.

                                                    Fool the Stars Above
                                                    Cost: 7m Mins: Essence 2
                                                    Type: Simple
                                                    Keywords: Psyche
                                                    Duration: Indefinite
                                                    Prerequisites: This Man is Dead / Walk Among the Living

                                                    “The mind plays tricks, You are confused,
                                                    The man you seek is long gone!”

                                                    The mind is a feeble thing, easily tricked by light and sound. The heart is not so easily swain, but for many it is by far an easier task to twist the hearts of others than their own. The exalt may make a reflexive Instill or Inspire action to convince the target that they are not the person they believe them to be. If the exalt has an intimacy towards the target, they make convert that intimacies level of intensity into automatic successes. If the target has intimacies towards the exalt, the intensity of those intimacies are treated as one level lower than normal for the purpose of changing Resolve or Guile. The effect of this evocation lasts until the exalt has enter Extreme Range from the target, Short Range if successfully used on their celestial mate. This evocation can only be used if This Man is Dead is in effect.

                                                    Lovers Take Wing
                                                    Cost: 10m Mins: Essence 2
                                                    Type: Simple
                                                    Keywords: none
                                                    Duration: Indefinite
                                                    Prerequisites: I Know Those Eyes / Strange Form, Familiar Eyes

                                                    “My Gods, my heart beats faster! And my mind is racing…
                                                    Could it be…?
                                                    Could it be that you’ve come back to life?”

                                                    When the heart runs wild, and mind flees from logic, it is the actions of emotions alone that fuel the form. The exalt locks eyes with a target, and until they release this evocation, that target is all they can focus on. Any and all actions related to the target gain 2 non-charm die, while actions not related to the target lose 2 die. Should the action be a Rush, or Defend Other, the exalt gains an additional die for the action. If the exalt has a positive intimacy of major intensity or higher towards the target, they are able to communicate with the target through glances alone for 3 rounds. This evocation can only be used if I Know Those Eyes is in effect.

                                                    Two Faces, One Heart
                                                    Cost:                                                     15m Mins: Essence 3
                                                    Type: Simple
                                                    Keywords: none
                                                    Duration: Indefinite
                                                    Prerequisites: Fool the Stars Above, Lovers Take Wing

                                                    “And why does the truth seem too hard to be true?
                                                    With so much broken, And so much damage…
                                                    There are no words, Left to say…”

                                                    Discord and turmoil and burned for long enough. A two faced hero is nothing more than a villain of different colors. Bind together the chaos, or sever it forever. The exalt may use this evocation once, and once only. This evocation has two different effects that the exalt must choose from.
                                                    The first effect is to bind together the two facets of the Death Mocking Shroud, removing the requirement to use “This Man is Dead,” or “I Know Those Eyes” when wearing the artifact or as a prerequisite to other evocations. The artifact also becomes naturally impossible to perceive by non-magic means to all but its attuned user. This invisibly effect is lost if it is not attuned to at all. However, choosing this option removes all benefits granted by intimacies within the previous evocations.
                                                    The second effect permanently separates the two facets of the Death Mocking Shroud, effectively rendering the artifact useless. Doing this however, does not only grant the artifacts weight in all five types of Jade, but reveals a core of Starmetal hidden within the masks forehead, large enough to made into a ring if used in its entirety. If the exalt has at least 5 Craft charms, choosing this option generates 15sxp, 10gxp, and 5wxp (see Crafting tables for uses).

                                                    Embrace of the Stardust Oath Keeper (**** dot) Starmetal Chain Shirt
                                                    Attunement Cost: 4m (+3m)
                                                    Hearthstone slots: 1

                                                    Story:
                                                    An ancient item that has been passed through the hands of many a Chosen of the Maidens hands, even the long records of heaven have forgotten the maker of this wondrous raiment. Forged for a star of Mercury who ran alongside a star of Jupiter long ago, it is believed that the masterwork of Starmetal was created to protect and remind the allies of Jupiter’s Chosen in tasks they alone could not endure the hardships of. Others believe that it was crafted to be a parting gift for a close ally, perhaps something more, of a Chosen of Venus. There are as many tales about this garment as there are stars in the heavens, but only the Chosen of Mars and Mercury have ever been recorded to don the garment in the recent past.
                                                    A sleeveless tunic, near a shroud or vest in appearance, it is forest green in color and was once matched with a cloak of Cornwall blue. It is unique among many similar garments as it reflects no light but starlight, even when placed into the heart of great fires or set before the light of the sun. When records appear relating to its former owners, each has claimed in similar words that the garment stills their heart to calmness when worn, as though by the embrace of a loved one. Beyond this though, little else is known about the item; known to anyone aside from its present owner.

                                                    Evocations:
                                                    The flowing, liquid like nature of this armor allows for it to reform itself to include pauldrons, vambraces, bracers, and a great flowing cloak in a moment’s notice. While these can be used to deflect oncoming attacks in the form of blocks and parries, they do not count as true armor. When attuned a Sidereal, Solar, or Lunar Exalted may pay an additional 3m to change the armor from a Chain Shirt to Silken Armor. This payment must be made when donning the armor and is automatically deactivated when crashed.

                                                    Lovers Embrace Anew
                                                    Cost: - Mins: Essence 1
                                                    Type: Permanent
                                                    Keywords: none
                                                    Duration: Permanent
                                                    Prerequisites: None

                                                    “In the light that falls at moonrise, In the rhythm of the rain,
                                                    In the miracle of ordinary days, In the hush of night,
                                                    I will be in the whispers of lovers…”

                                                    Time heals many wounds, but the ever constant wound of true love only grows greater with time and distance. Once donned, the Embrace of the Stardust Oath Keeper recognizes the attuned exalt as though they were a long lost love. Wrapping its body tight around the exalt, the armor applies a reassuring pressure to the body at all times, as though forever held within the arms of another. All social actions that would attempt to threaten or scare the exalt wearing this artifact have their difficulty increased by 2.

                                                    Speechless is the Heart
                                                    Cost: 3m Mins: Essence 1
                                                    Type: Simple
                                                    Keywords: none
                                                    Duration: Instant
                                                    Prerequisites: Lovers Embrace Anew

                                                    “In a language never spoken, With the promises we made
                                                    In the endless low that owns me heart and soul,
                                                    In the certainty I will always be true and as near as my next prayer…”

                                                    For those that truly care for another, they need not words to communicate… but the lovers separated know not if their prayers reach their partners ears. The exalt may make a social influence roll that relies on a targets positive intimacy without observable action. The target is unable to act against this social influence roll unless they can observe the intent of this evocation via a Read Intentions action. This action goes unnoticed by the target should it fail, and the same target becomes immune to this evocation for the remainder of the scene.

                                                    Step Across the Starlight
                                                    Cost: 5m Mins: Essence 2
                                                    Type: Supplemental
                                                    Keywords: none
                                                    Duration: Instant
                                                    Prerequisites: Speechless is the Heart

                                                    “In the rustle of a curtain, In the bustle of the world,
                                                    In a thousand little unexpected ways, When you lift your gaze,
                                                    I will be like the shimmer of one small star!”

                                                    One came run a million miles again and again when Mercury and Venus work in turn. Only Jupiter knows how far one most run to reach their goals. This evocation supplements an athletics roll related to running, or a stealth roll related to crowds and similar conditions. These actions gain (Essence) non-charm dice under normal circumstances. If there is no other light source out to Long Range of the exalt aside from the moon and stars, these actions gain (Essence) automatic successes instead.

                                                    Wave Bending Practice
                                                    Cost: 4m Mins: Essence 2
                                                    Type: Simple
                                                    Keywords: none
                                                    Duration: One Scene
                                                    Prerequisites: Speechless is the Heart

                                                    “In the echo of the ocean, In the haunting of the wind,
                                                    In mysterious extraordinary ways, Through the darkest sky,
                                                    I will be like the shimmer of one small star!”

                                                    Everyone has been given a mission once in their many lifetimes that must be seen to the end, no matter what may come to cut them down. The armor has seen too many of these missions and the carnage they cause its beloved bearer; so it has learned to move its bearer before they themselves realize they need to move. For the remainder of the scene, so long as Embrace of the Stardust Oath Keeper does not change its form (see attunement bonus) it forgoes defense in favor for the ability to avoid conflict.
