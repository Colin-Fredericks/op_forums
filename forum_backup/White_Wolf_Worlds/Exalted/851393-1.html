                                            Yeah, Supernals are basically the big thing that makes them a threat- they are masters at something out of the gate. The Dawn super combat then diversify strategy is probably one of the more dangerous Solars out there because they are one of the ones more likely to survive in a straight fight and only get more powerful as they round out their weaknesses. they have a strong early game and can diversify to get a solid late game

                                            The Nights are another harder threat that is most likely to survive early game, because they can probably hide so well, that they can just lay low until they do enough shenanigans to get into power without anyone knowing and when they are in a position of power, start shoring up their combat weaknesses for the late game.

                                            The Zeniths and Eclipses are terrifying in the long term, because while they are the ones more likely to get killed in the early game, if they make enough friends they can start building empires and organizations far better than a Dawn or Night even though those two are better at the early game survival, Zeniths and Eclipses are great late game powerhouses.

                                            Twilights are the ones most likely to die in the short term due to not having the ability to hide, the social connections or the fight abilities of the other Castes, but in the late game they are even more terrifying than the Zeniths and Eclipses due to their ability to call down cataclysms through Lore or Sorcery, make spirits do what they want, find out any secret, and create anything they need.

                                            and even then, the ones with Weak Early Games are the brainier Castes, who just need clever people to do them right to survive with the Supernals given to them- a Twilight with the ability to predict your hometown being destroyed is not someone you want to tick off, even in the Early Game, they just have to prove that they can do that. its just that Dawns and Nights are easier to succeed in the early game to survive to the late game, because the applications of their skills of how to do so are the most obvious. but all of them pose long term threats by simply having one Supernal ability that they can exploit to survive as long they can to really get going when they have enough experience and power to have a wide range of answers to what you can throw at them.

                                            I know that I'm probably going to be playing Dawns with a combat ability Supernal and Occult 5, because that combination means I'll get both a short term skill that will make sure I can probably defeat anyone in combat, and if I get to Essence 5,I'll be able to learn all of Sorcery to truly destroy my foes with demons and powerful spells while getting other utilities like transportation. Pairing up Supernals with Favored abilities that synergize well is important- if your Supernal skill is short term, select long term ones to grow into, if your supernal skill is a long term one, select short term skills to make sure you survive to use it effectively.

                                            but yeah, basically? Supernals are scary, whether long term or short term in their effects, and smart Solars know how to use what they are good at to get through the challenges they face.
