                                            Nice, found an interview with the Author where he reveals a lot of spoilerish info.


                                            Into the mind of a mad genius
                                            Vylus Wiki
                                            I spoke to Abbadon last night and here's what he told me. None of this stuff is controversial or overly spoiler-y, so I think it's okay for me to share.
                                            • He has enough material right now for five more years of KSBD
                                            • He wishes he gave the comic more urgency, wishes Allison could play a more active role since she doesn't have her own character yet
                                            • A print copy of KSBD is set to come out mid to late 2015; he intends to revise, clean up, and color the early pages
                                              • When this happens, he does not intend for the rate of the webcomic to change
                                            • The ending of the first "book" is coming near; each "book" will have Allison spending time with primarily one person, including members of the Seven
                                            • He knows the comic's full of errors, jokes that tends to happen when you finish at 4 AM
                                            • Takes a lot of inspiration from readers, but lots of ideas are difficult to use, like a 30 foot monster made of ash and bones
                                            • Jokes that he really just ripped off Buddhism and that he mainly spends time drawing dudes fighting each other when asked what he does
                                            • Main cast is all female because he likes the idea behind female protagonists
                                            • Jagganoth is as powerful as the rest of the Seven combined, but Zoss could "shit on" all of them
                                            • Jagganoth is somewhere on the level of Superman or Goku, as he is truly immune to harm and has access to immense magical power
                                              • Jagganoth was designed to avoid the dumb brute trope
                                            • All of the Seven have mastered space-time
                                            • Incubus is a dick that everyone loves to hate; his name was originally supposed to be Michael
                                            • The Seven, including Zoss, are sympathetic characters when you learn their backstories
                                            • Some of the Seven are implied to have been born during the Universal War
                                            • The Universal War happened 3000-4000 years ago
                                            • The Universal Conquest in the comic is just 2/3; the first was when Zoss conquered Throne and the third is the prophecy foretold by Jadis
                                            • Throne is its own universe with hundreds of billions of people
                                            • Devils and demons are now the same thing
                                            • Sweetie Pie (the white one) and Honey Bunch (the black one) are actually devils and the first devils to be shown masked; they are coming back at a later time
                                            • All devils are ruled by some scary motherfucker named Himself who is literally locked away in an obsidian coffin because he's too much of a beast; Himself is the only devil without a mask
                                            • The Undying Lords are the kings of the undead
                                            • The little blue devils running around Throne can be thought of as servants with menial tasks; when a summoner dies, the devils can often be left to their own devices
                                            • The symbol on Maya's back and Incubus' chair is a reference to the "Middle Army"
                                            • The many universes branch out from Throne like spokes on a Wheel; everything that is not a universe is considered part of the void
                                            • When physical beings enter the void, they cease to exist unless they cast a shade, which is the black "psychic imprint" of a person
                                            • When angels enter the physical realm, they manifest as white flame that explodes continuously unless bound to their armor
                                            • Angels are said to have been born from the corpse of UN when the gods breathed life into the pieces of him
                                            • Angels and devils reincarnate because they're born in the void; when people die their energy enters the void but only a select few have enough willpower to maintain their sanity
                                            • Confirms the void is filled with god corpses
                                            • YISUN's energy permeates all of reality (hence, the secret name of God is 'I') and is known as the Flame Perishable
                                              • The Flame Perishable manifests in all living beings; it can be hot/cold and black/white: angels are cold and white, devils are hot and white, humans are (?) and black, and servants are (?) and black
                                            • Servants are powerful and not passive as their name implies; they fulfill the same role as classical mythological entities and were the first to be made by the gods, hence the poem
                                            • Praman Nand is a actually a servant; he calls Nand the Gate Keeper several times
                                            • The Art involves "lying to the face of God" and is a reflection of how reality is an illusion
                                            • Allison's boyfriend really doesn't have a name yet
                                            • The Pursuers will team up later and fail miserably
                                            That's all I can think of right now.

                                            • Vylus Wiki
                                              Hahaha, the dude is straight nuts, but in a good way. He's frickin' hilarious and the kind of guy who takes 2 minutes to give a direct answer because he's excited about everything.
                                              I forgot to mention his creative style is very fluid. Lots of reader input and just making stuff up as he goes along, though there is an overarching storyline that's set in place.
                                              One of the challenges noted with the print copy is the difficulty in including bonus material. It may or may not make its way in.
                                              He also said he's moving back to the US in August to make his rounds on the comic convention circuit.
                                              Oh, oh. The summoning of devils. All devils belong to a mass of essence, for lack of a better word. To summon one, a devil must be bound to a mask and given a name. I think he said this falls in line with classical demonology because I read a series of books which just so happens to explore the same concept.

                                                        Interesting....

                                                        When Alison was last seen, her soul(I think that's what she is right now) was bald. Now, she has hair again.

                                                        Additionally, white cracks/lines/thingies seem to be crawling up her legs from the ground.

                                                        Final thing I wanted to note, in Jadis's prophecy, Kill Six Billion Demons(Alison) is mentioned to wield "the terrible Blade of Want" and in the images of the Glass Coffin, Alison is seen with a vaguely sword shaped mass of light/energy in her hand. Maya is also depicted, wielding something that looks basically the same. Further, in Meti's Sword Manual, the Blade of Want is referenced under the section "Mastering the Sword." In a previous except, Meti mentions her apprentice whom she describes as "an idiot speck of a girl with more talent for eating than skill with the blade" which may be a reference to Maya, who spent the vast majority of her time on screen eating.

                                                        What this leads to believe is that Maya may come to be a teacher and ally for Alison, who teaches her to wield the Blade of Want.
