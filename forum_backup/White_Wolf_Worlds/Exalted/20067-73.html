        They don't, a.f.i.k.. An anima banner will automatically (and unavoidably) flare up when the DB exalt draws from the Peripheral Essence pool, but the anima powers have to be activated manually. This costs essence (5 mote standard), which may naturally increase the anima banner's display level, if drawn from Peripheral pool, but it doesn't artificially lift it.

        Now, Lunar anima effects do activate automatically on reaching the 11+ degree, and the Scroll of Errata updated Dawn, Zenith, and Twilight anima effects to do the same; as well as the equivalent Abyssal and Infernal caste. ( It wouldn't make sense for Night caste anima effect, and Eclipse caste, as well as Sidereal, anima effects already had a cost reduction at post 11+ peripheral expenditure. )

        There is no mention of Dragon-blooded anima effects receiving the same treatment. It may have been intended, but if so, didn't make the cut for SoErrata.
