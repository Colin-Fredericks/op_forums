                        For Martial Arts, Earth Dragon is worth it for the capstone alone. It has many effects, all of them excellent, including a Crippling defence DBs otherwise lack. As mentioned above, Crane is also a very good choice.

                        I have a couple of docs of custom Charms that you might make some use of. I developed them in a game where I play a DB alongside a Celestial group and I seem to be able to keep pace. A few of them do rely on house rules peculiar to that game so use your discretion. In particular I revised a few War Charms to keep them useful in a game that steers clear of Mass Combat.

                        I tend to find going artifact light is the best approach, especially if you have Charms like Perfection of the Earth Body that give you artifact-scale weapons naturally. Those commitment motes eat into your precious mote pool. Hearthstone Bracers are always a solid option though, the dodge boost and Speed reduction are invaluable.

                        Oh, and don't forget Eric Minton's DB hearthstone Charms here and here.

                        Hearthstone selection is tricky because there is a terribly fine grey space between the three axes of dull, broken and useless. For a social boost, the Jewel of Lawgiver's Authority is pretty nice.

                                                        Side question: Opinion on 2.5 Violet Bier of Sorrows + Rings of Saturn(Glories book)?
                                                        Personally I've always liked VBoS, and if you're going to be learning from a sidereal master it makes a lot of sense and would be a good choice.

                                                        Also if you're going for an immaculate grandmaster style character who's mastered all the dragon styles you won't be using form weapons. Instead you'll use earth dragon to make your fists like unto goremauls except faster and more accurate, fire dragon to ignite them blazing hot and air dragon to extend their reach out over a dozen yards. What wood dragon will let you do is heal wounds instantly, and even heal the wounds of others, and water dragon has a perfect defence and ways of stealing your opponents essence and stuff. Really they're all good.

                                                        Normally I would never suggest taking all five, but you actually can with your super chargen, and they cover a lot of ground. Stealth is in air and earth, presence is in wood and fire, resistance is in wood and earth, dodge is in air and fire, athletics and awareness charms are also in air, survival in wood, and they've all got a bit of occult.

                                                        On the other hand it's a massive investment, you might be better off with VBoS and RoS.
