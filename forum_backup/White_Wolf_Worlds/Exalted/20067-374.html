    Originally posted by Octopoid View Post

    To my knowledge, there's nothing like that published in any edition. Some of the Directional Compasses will go into local dietary cuisines, but nothing on the biology of people. I think it's assumed they function the same in Creation that they do in the real world.
    The devs have addressed "similar issues" (scientific stuff, for example, but it's generally the same for biology). It's best to shrug it off. The reason things work is because of essence and gods, but it still works like it would on earth for the most part. Your heart pumps blood. You can suffer brain damage if you get hit on your noggin. Ignore things like "cells and quantum mechanics and chemistry" and whatever and just go with gods and essence. That's also the reason why the world can't be industrialized. Ignore talk about DNA, about strong and recessive genes, and say things like "My warlord father had seed like a bull's. That's why my hair is black like his." Or if you're a Dynast, "I have the blood of the dragon, sweet sister. Don't wake the dragon."
