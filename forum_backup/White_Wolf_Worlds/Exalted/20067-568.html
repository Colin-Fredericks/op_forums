    I definitely know that the idea of each satrapy being administered by a satrap and three advisors, who are all supposed to report on the situation (and each other) separately as a check and balance, for which they would all come from separate Houses, was described in the Second Edition core book. I couldn't say if it originated there.

    ​I believe that the manner in which satrapies function in this Edition is different to before, although I'll need to wait for The Realm to confirm what I'm inferring from What Fire Has Wrought.

    ​In the prior Editions, the Realm's method of reigning over satrapies was two-tiered; you had the farming of tribute collection duties to Great Houses (something from which a major portion of their wealth was drawn, and that they would bid over the rights to for any given satapy), and you had the satrap appointed as imperial representative, to observe on behalf of the Empress, advise local rulers on how to keep in line with imperial policy, and ensure that tribute would be ready for collection by the appropriate House. The satrap was specifically supposed to not come from the House that had tribute collection rights, as a way of ensuring that they wouldn't do anything that favoured the tribute collectors over the throne.

    ​It appears to me that in Third Edition, these functions have been consolidated and streamlined; a given Great House is granted authority over a satrapy, including collection of tribute, a function that it exercises through its own appointed satrap. It would appear that the distribution of power is now achieved via appointment of local military commanders who have to come from different Houses, unless there's something that I'm misremembering.

                                    Originally posted by Accelerator View Post
                                    Question. Is an essence 5 solar equivalent to a 3rd circle demon? Or a 2nd circle?

                                    Equivalent in what way? This will vary so much depending on both the demon and the Solar that there's really no one right answer. An Essence 5 Dawn focused in Melee is probably a match for a combat-focused 3rd circle, or a Craft-focused Twilight could compete with a 3rd Circle who's thing was making stuff. But even that's going to be a bit of a toss-up, and depend heavily on how long the Solar has had at Essence 5 to master the high-essence stuff, whereas most 3rd Circles are probably as big as they're going to get.

                                    And, of course, there's advantages that don't stem from raw spiritual power. That Melee Dawn might have a lot more trouble if they're fighting the 3rd Circle in Malfeas, where it can call on all its 2nd Circle souls and literal armies of 1st Circle demons, but the Dawn might be able to swing things back with some really badass artifacts, their own armies, and so forth.

                                    Long answer short, Exalted doesn't really go in for a "power stat". Essence is a very rough measurement of potential power. You can't activate your scanner, determine someone's power level is OVER 9000, and conclude that therefore, they will win.
