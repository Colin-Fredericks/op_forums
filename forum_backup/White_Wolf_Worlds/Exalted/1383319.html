    Essence: Exalt Advantage wild speculation

    04-23-2020, 06:04 AM
    The Exalt advantages we know so far...

    Solars
    Supremacy of Ability: Solars do not pay the mote cost to use Excellencies
    First Among Equals: Solars always win ties, Solar PCs win against Solar NPCs

    Infernals
    Devil-Body. For a scene Infernals may change into a demon form, causing the effects of some of their charms to go differently or have cheaper costs.
    Unwoven Coadjutor: the Infernal may once per session ask the Dm a question and they must answer.

    Sidereals
    Arcane Fate: after leaving the scene people start to forget about the Sidereal, explaining their actions as the work of passing strangers as they forget all details of the Sidereal. For Exalts this may be contested by a Difficulty 5 Integrity roll. If they have an Intimacy to the Sidereal, they always remember.
    Weaving Destiny: the Sidereal may weave a Destiny modeled after an archetype, profession, or social role and give it to another character with a touch. When attempting a task which resonates with that archetype, the target may transform dice into automatic successes up to the Sidereals Essence. A destiny lasts for 5 years, up to the Sidereals discretion. They may only be subject to one destiny at a time. A Sidereal may apply a Destiny to themselves and with it they lose access to Arcane Fate, allowing people to remember their fake destiny.

    Liminals
    Undying: Liminals are able to get up and can't be incapacitated as long as their brain remains intact.
    Child of Death: Liminals may interact with Incorporeal ghosts as if they were corporeal



    So, what could the others be? Speculate away!

      Some things I noted...

      The term DM is used, not ST.


      A Sidereal giving the Dawn the destiny of a soldier, the No Moon the destiny of a witch, the decadent Fire Aspect aristocrat the destiny of a Noble, etc, seems very powerful. But maybe it's meant to be.


      Unwoven Coadjutor was, in the previous edition, a first-circle demon that was merged into your head. Which is really, really cool. Especially as you can choose the demon, and get mutations.
      So you could choose your favourite demon, and use it as a bit of a theme for your Infernal. Have red blood-ape hair and a demon in your head shouting "stomp, crush, kill!" Or a weird tragedy-mask-face and a treacherous cunning demon in your head.
      Now, Unwoven Coadjutor was originally I think going to be the demon in your head giving advice. But people said to the writers "but Blood Apes are stupid, how will they know the answer?" Which is fair. So now it's been changed to being a connection with the Yozis, which could work well.
      But I really hope you still get a First-Circle Demon in your head.

                      I don't know the Alchemicals enough.

                      Getimians
                      For what we know about them, probably an advantage that grant them bonus toward actions they did into their original world, and the ability to ignore fate when they want. No clue how that would work mechanically.

                              I was thinking about this earlier, because of the way Lunars and animal forms interact with dice caps, which is a giant mess with not a lot of pay-off for its complexity.

                              I think actually the best way to do Lunar shapeshifting is that there are a number of merits (no more than 10), and a list of common animals and what merits they have. And when you take an animal form, you get its merits. Like big, small, tough, extra damage, poison, camouflage, super-sense, etc. And stick those on top of your normal stats.

                              So like, you turn into a bird, you now have small (gives bonuses to stealth, penalties to attack and strength) and you can fly. Turn into a dog, you get a bonus on smelling and can do lethal with your unarmed attack. Turn into a grass snake, you've got Small, Camouflage and Poison, and can do lethal unarmed. Turning into a big animal could, say, boost your Strength and Stamina to 4, or give you +1 if it's already at 4.
                              And maybe there's a Legendary Size which gives you big minimums/boosts to strength, stamina (or their derived things if that works better), but costs more.

                              Shapeshifting can be really simple if you're happy to not fiddle around with stats too much, and think about why people actually shapeshift in-game.


                              (A note on Attributes... there's a few comments that have been made which make me think that, while Abilities are just conflated a bit, there's big changes to Attributes.)

                                                  Alchemical
                                                  Community: Choose a community as the anchor for your Exaltation. While within your community you gain benefits and may rest to swap Alchemical Charms.

                                                  Abyssal
                                                  Underworld Kings: Gain benefits while in the Underworld. So long as you remain clothed in the trappings of death, you maintain these benefits after leaving the Underworld.

                                                  DB
                                                  Dragon's Hearth: Form a Hearth with other Exalts, you gain benefits when you are with them. Grants a few extra benefits if the Hearthmate also has the Dragon's Hearth advantage.

                                                  Getimian
                                                  Shielded from Heaven: Natural resistance to Fate-weaving attacks. Magic that would spy on you or reveal information about you is twisted by your origin world, giving wrong information or no information at all.

                                                  Lunar
                                                  Lunar Mate: Choose a single non-Lunar Exalt to bond with permanently (this choice cannot be changed). You both gain strong benefits towards each other.
