            Terrifying Argent Witchblade

            Creation has fallen to a dark time. The Ebon Dragon, understanding the limits of his prison, asked of his brothers that he be wholly forged into a single Exaltation, which would wreck destruction upon creation and be carried through a single family line: one of sins and chaos. Seeing the power wrought upon by The Ebon Darkness, Sol Invictus took the same steps as his enemy and gathered all of his exaltations to fuse back with him and become one. Sol Angelus was born and carried the purest light with it. The fight between both forces went on for decades, destroying everything in its path. At some point, The maidens contacted Luna and told her that they saw a future, a future in which either of them won, but destroyed everything.

            And so Luna took the same steps as Sol and the Dragon, gathering all her forces to before a single gauntlet of power, a balancing force between good and evil, a tool of ultimate adaptation which would serve only to make sure the balance was kept. Thus was born the Argent Witchblade.

            With time, the war torn appart sky and land, but creation still stood. Other gods, demons and elementals decided to take the Forging and become invulnerable Artifacts, but recently the world discovered one of terrible power. The Thirteenth one was created by the Deathlords conspiring against one of their own, soul-crafting him into a single sphere of pure Soulsteel. Now, the maidens have told their Sidereals that no matter what happens, the power of all these items should never be gathered together, as that would be terrible and omen the end of the world...

            Glorious Useless-Post Prana

            Keywords: Mute
            Duration: Instant
            Prerequisite Charms: Ten-Characters Methodology

                        This is gonna take some narrative backflips, so I hope it's not lost in the shuffle...

                        Near the end of the First Age, a powerful Twilight Caste peered into the Loom of Fate, seeing the Reclamation millenia in the future. Knowing that creation would need assistance in fighting back the demons, he begun work on a great weapon: a powerful warstrider that could take on the greatest of behemoths and Third Circle demons on it's own. However, when the Ursurpation occured, the Solar inventor launched the warstrider from his palace so that it could never fall into the wrong hands. A young Chosen of Battles, knowing of the visions of the Reclamation, stole the Solar's Gem of Immortality and set off on a journey to find the legendary warstrider: a journey that could take her thousands of years....

                        Thousands of years later, the ruined warstrider is found on the outskirts of Nexus by a young Twilight Caste and his mortal best friend. Seeking a 'project' to work on, the Solar repairs the warstrider, gutting the controls in the process and adding his own. When the Sidereal reaches Nexus, she attempts to take the warstrider only to find out she no longer knows how to drive it. With the Reclamation fast approaching, the Sidereal vows to train the Solar in proper combat so he can stand against the Yozi and their armies. However, the Twilight Caste is more interested in lazing about and enjoying the many vices available in Nexus. What's most frustrating for the Sidereal is that despite this carefree attitude, the Twilight is able to handle the numerous threats to Nexus with relative competence.
