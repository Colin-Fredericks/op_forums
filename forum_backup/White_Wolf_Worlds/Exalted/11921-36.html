    In the most untamed part of the Scavenger lands, someone wakes. A Solar veteran of endless wars against the Fair Folk, sealed along with their family just as the Usurpation began to retain knowledge of the First Age past the destruction. Their last memories were not of the war, though, but of the taking of their son and death of their spouse at the hands of a mysterious Terrestrial mercenary. As the old veteran steps out into a strange and devastated world, they find an old Familiar of theirs who explains how long they've been asleep, and from there learn that the mercenary worked for a hidden detachment of Autocthonians. Now, they must find their son, all the while matching wits with the sapient golems the Autocthonians use to replace those they kidnap for soulgem implantation...

    Led by the son. now a Twilight and a luminary of the Sodality. The Autocthonians had nothing personal against him, they just needed his Essence to perfect the ability of the golems to pass as human, and once they had what they needed, they adopted him as one of their own. He's gone native, and while he would like to see his parent again to know where he came from, they're strangers to him-if it's his origin or his home, his home will win. Every time.

                            I had a good one.

                            A great ship has arrived at a port town near the Imperial City. While the ship arrived and pulled into port with no trouble, dockworkers boarded the ship to find all crew and passengers dead seemingly for no reason, save for four survivors. Sidereal and Dragon Blooded investigators find nothing strange on the ship, save a large soulsteel box filled only with dirt. Within the next few days, the soulsteel box vanishes and the four survivors each Exalt as Abyssals, spreading a plague the likes of which are the worst the Realm has seen since the Great Contagion, causing the victims of the plague to rise after their death as zombies or hungry ghosts, each one carrying the plague as well.

                            Meanwhile, a supposedly ancient peddler in the Imperial City hears of the 'disease' and immediately sets to work. Truly a Twilight Caste in disguise, the peddler survived and staved off the same plague in the Northeast, knowing it's source to be a Deathlord transported to the Blessed Isle in the soulsteel box aboard the ship. The Twilight caste sets off into the Imperial City, ready to destroy any of the plague carrying undead or Abyssals he encounters. Little does he know that the Deathlord has help, from a powerful wealthy Sidereal working very closely with the Deathlord's chief lieutenant: a Moonshadow Caste who the Twilight fought in the Northeast many years ago.

                            "Oh boys! Look what I got here!"
                            "Hey, where the Dynast women at?"

                                                A Twilight Caste Sorcerer who made a deal with a Fair Folk to defend himself from his evil mentor, and as a result got judged by a secret organization of Sidereals, some who wanted to kill him for the danger he posed, but some defended him and he was taken under the wing of a Chosen of Endings who taught him responsibility and discipline, but was of course put under a curse that would fall upon him if he prove a threat, and afterwards and constantly watched by a Chosen of Battles to observe and determine if he is evil and needing to executed. He now takes anyone coming for help in dealing with supernatural problems from various spirits, fair folk, demons, ghosts and other such troubles, and works with a Zenith Caste wielding a sword given to him by the Unconquered Sun himself, a Dawn Caste guardswoman who distrusts him half the time, a spirit that dwells in a skull, his Abyssal brother in dealing with the problems they cause.

                                                A mortal Dynast apart of the Scarlet Empire, in exile in an oppressed nation conquered by said Empire when he was a child along with his sister, saves a strange girl who is really a Malefactor Caste and Exalts as an Eclipse Caste with War 5. He then proceeds to try to use an alternate persona as a symbol to rally the peoples of this nation into a fighting force to destroy the Scarlet Empire while dealing with getting through the academy he is enrolled in, but is consistently foiled by a Dawn Caste who fights for the Scarlet Empire, who thinks he can change things from within. Together their fight makes things worse and worse, as the Eclipse's power makes a Zenith Caste princess kill everyone in the oppressed nation on accident, while the Eclipse caste makes more sacrifices to advance themselves politically and militarily to defeat the Realm.

                                                A Twilight Sorcerer who can summon twelve spirits representing astrological signs, a Dawn Caste Fire Dragon Stylist, another Night Caste using Infinite Glacial Armory Style, and a Dawn Caste who specializes in using artifacts and evocations, all join a guild of Exalts who all take jobs to fight threats across the land and fighting other Exalts who take advantage of others. They have a flying cat companion, and their guildmaster is a high-level Sorcerer capable of casting Adamant Circle Spells. they are joined by a guy who knows Iron Dragon Style, another who knows Water Dragon Style and is in love with the Night Caste, as well as three Lunars, one of which is still getting a hang of their war form, another who occasionally comes out of retirement to unleash theirs and kick ass, and the third who was recently rescued from a foreign lan after being gone for so long, and is close friends with the Fire Dragon Stylist, as well as a child Twilight Caste who knows Air Dragon Style and has her own flying cat companion.
