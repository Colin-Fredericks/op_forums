        Cost: 4m Mins: Essence 1
        Type: Reflexive
        Keywords: Counterattack, Decisive-Only
        Duration: Instant

        The Brand of Elemental Flame suffuses the sorcerer’s Essence with fiery power.                                     So I'm thinking "actually, 10 xp for the ability to spend even more motes on an already costly Evocation that isn't guaranteed to hit (and which comes with the implicit additional cost of the health levels you lose - after all, if you take no damage, you can't activate it) just so you can ignore Hardness sounds like a fairly crappy deal". I'm also thinking the more fireblood you have gushing out of you, the easier time you should have hitting your opponent with it, which is the exact opposite of how it actually works now (Sta+Occult is a rolled action, thus subject to wound penalty).

                                    So how about "Whenever you activate its prerequisite, you can spend additional motes equal to your current wound penalty to make Elemental Flame Suffusement ignore Hardness and convert wound penalties that would apply to your Sta+Occult roll to bonus dice instead"? Probably way too cheap (you effectively gain +2 dice per mote spent, plus the ability to ignore Hardness), but making it double your current wound penalty would make the ability hideously overcosted (12m, Xi at -4). Making it cost an extra WP would run into the problem that the ability in its current form is completely useless against anything with Hardness at low Essence, and WP is an extremely limited resource, so this would effectively shut down its usage in such cases.

                                    All things considered, I'd go with "spend additional motes and anima levels equal to your current wound penalty, with bonfire counting as 4 levels for the purposes of this Evocation".

                                                Bolt Lash (Blue/White Jade Direlash ***)

                                                Another glory from the legendary twilight Bright Judgement, the Bolt Lash is a miracle born of a thousand near-death experiences. Bright Judgement sought to harness a true bolt of lightning. However, the power of an elemental lord was insufficient, and lightning created by sorcery would not do either. Trial after trial, it seemed nothing could create the weapon she envisioned in her mind. At least, the Twilight committed herself to catching a true bolt of lightning.

                                                And so, Bright Judgement built a tower of cold iron high into the sky from atop her mountain in the North. And atop that spire she waited, for this bolt must be pure and natural. She could not conjure a storm or it would be tainted. For days and weeks she waited patiently, until the first storm finally came. The iron spire drew strike after strike, but quick as she was, Bright Judgement could not outpace the sky. Her attempts to catch a bolt of lightning were too slow, and eventually the storm passed.

                                                Two years went by, with Bright Judgement standing on her spire passing the days and weeks between storms, and risking death time and again to catch a single pure bolt of lightning. The day she finally succeeded, she almost believed it to be a dream. The quivering bolt seared her hand and struggled to escape, but her conviction held her hand true.

                                                Bringing the bolt back to her workshop, she hammered the bolt into a large ingot of Blue Jade. When the fusion seemed it would hold, she spent another year carefully forging the ingot into a weave which she then turned into a long Direlash. Despite the might of the Blue Jade, the angered bolt of lightning was nearly too much for it to handle, and so she anchored the Blue Jade with a handle made of White, to keep the power in check and direct it. It was not Bright Judgement’s greatest triumph, but it was her hardest earned and her hands proudly bore the scars from that project for the rest of her days.

                                                Bolt Lash is a Blue and White Jade Direlash. The length is an almost pure 10 foot length of woven Blue Jade, capable of channeling an uncanny amount of electric energy, while the handle is a rod of White Jade wrapped in insulating mammoth leather. It has a single hearthstone socked in the butt of the grip. When unattuned, Bolt Lash lies curled in a neat coil and cannot be extended. It also acts as a very strong lightning rod until it is attuned, causing few to be willing to store it unused.

                                                Evocations of Bolt Whip
                                                A Solar or Dragon-Blooded who attunes to Bolt Whip burns away an extra point of initiative from foes on successful Withering attacks against prone targets. This extra point of initiative damage is not gained by the exalt.

                                                Shock Thrash
                                                Cost: 1m Mins: Essence 1
                                                Type: Supplemental
                                                Keywords: Withering-Only
                                                Duration: Instant
                                                Prerequisites: None

                                                The power of lightning is awe-inspiring, and brings its victims to their knees. Each successful Withering attack enhanced with Shock Thrash causes a jolt of electricity in the victim, forcing them to roll (Stamina + Resistance) to avoid being knocked prone. The difficulty of this roll is 1, but is increased by the number of shock charges held by Bolt Lash at the beginning of the attack.

                                                Each use of Shock Thrash regardless of success of the attack increases the number of shock charges by 1 after attack resolution. Bolt Lash can hold a maximum of 5 shock charges, which fade after combat ends at a rate of 1 charge per minute.

                                                Stunning Flog
                                                Cost: 3m, 1+ Shock Charges Mins: Essence 1
                                                Type: Supplemental
                                                Keywords: Uniform
                                                Duration: Instant
                                                Prerequisites: Shock Thrash

                                                There is no bolder way to proclaim your might than to keep your foes down. A foe that is truly beaten, cannot raise a hand against you. Stunning Flog can only be activated against a foe who is already prone. An attack so enhanced adds the number of expended shock charges to the difficulty of the target’s attempts to rise from prone. Regardless of whether the target succeeds or not, the difficulty lasts and applies to future attempts should they be knocked prone again, fading at 1 point of difficulty per round.

                                                Each time the target fails to rise from prone while affected by Stunning Flog, Bolt Lash gains a shock charge.

                                                Thunder Crash
                                                Cost: 5m, 3+ Shock Charges Mins: Essence 2
                                                Type: Simple
                                                Keywords: Decisive-Only
                                                Duration: Instant
                                                Prerequisites: Stunning Strike

                                                Thunder resounds and with a deafening crash it brings even the mighty low. Thunder Crash creates a Decisive attack that expends at least 3 shock charges. If the target is not prone, on a successful hit, the target is automatically knocked prone and the difficulty to stand up in increased by 3 for 1 round. If the target is already prone, the target has no choice but to remain prone for their next action and cannot even attempt to stand.

                                                If Bolt Lash has extra shock charges in excess of the cost, they may be expended to convert up to (extra expended charges) extra successes on the attack roll into damage dice on the damage roll.

                                                Lightning Arc
                                                Cost: 5m, 1wp, 5 Shock Charges Mins: Essence 3
                                                Type: Reflexive
                                                Keywords: Dual, Perilous
                                                Duration: One Scene
                                                Prerequisites: Thunder Crash

                                                Suffusing the whip with the fury of the sky, the weapon transforms into a solid bolt of lightning which twitches and arcs from nearby surfaces. This grants a variety of effects to Bolt Lash.
                                                • Bolt Lash may extend to attack foes up to Short range. Additionally, prone foes at Short range are considered embattled with the exalt for all purposes
                                                • The white-hot light of the whip is searing, doubling threshold successes on Witheringattack rolls for purposes of determining damage
                                                • Disarm gambits that utilize Bolt Lash gain 1 automatic success
                                                • Decisive Savaging attacks against foes that are grappled by Bolt Lash gain 2 extra dice of damage
                                                • Stealth is rendered impossible for the wielder of Bolt Lash for the duration of Lightning Arc
                                                This evocation lasts until the end of the scene or until the exalt is Crashed.

                                                    Savage Wolves Assail
                                                    Cost: 5m Mins: Essence 1
                                                    Type: Supplemental
                                                    Keywords: Uniform
                                                    Duration: Instant
                                                    Prerequisites: None

                                                    The master commands and the pack obeys. When this Evocation is used four distinct grouping of sickle blades gather around the main claws and sail through the air on a path towards the wielders target on a trail of silvery white essence.

                                                    The attack roll gains (Perception) double 9’s. If used against a battlegroup, the battlegroup’s Size is added to the number of doubles rolled and the roll gains double 8’s as well.

                                                    Defiant Mother Bear Stance
                                                    Cost: 6m, 2i, 1wp Mins: Essence 2
                                                    Type: Simple
                                                    Keywords: Psyche
                                                    Duration: Instant
                                                    Prerequisites: None

                                                    As the two begin to feel the ebb and flow of each other, so to does the bond of faithful beast grow stronger towards its master. With a wide stance, the wielder takes the weapon in both hands and flares of silver white essence begin to gather before wielder and weapon roar out in unison, the ground shuddering at the release.

                                                    Defiant Matriarch Stance causes a social influence action that affects all who can see and hear the wielder. The wielder rolls Appearance+Presence gaining (Essence) automatic successes and rolls against Resolve of those present, on a success those affected gain a Major strength Intimacy towards the wielder of Fear. The effect can be resisted by spending the wielders Essence in Willpower.

                                                    At Essence 3+ the wielder gains automatic successes equal to the affected targets level of intimacy on social influence to do with instilling actions or threatening. Any attempt to bargain with the prey proves the wielder weak, and the weapon puts this as a curse, removing successes equal to the intimacies strength.

                                                    Pack Retaliation Technique
                                                    Cost: 5m, 1i, (+1wp) Mins: Essence 2
                                                    Type: Reflexive
                                                    Keywords: Decisive-only, Counterattack, Perilous
                                                    Duration: Instant or Until Next Round
                                                    Prerequisites: Savage Wolves Assail, Diving Raptor’s Descent

                                                    The pack retaliates, attacking with fury and grim determination against those who to seek to injure or kill their master, throwing themselves into the fray. The blades follow the attack to slay the aggressor pulsing with silver-gold light, to punish him for such an act.

                                                    Pack Retaliation Technique is a counterattack that is usable after the wielder takes withering damage and can make an immediate decisive attack action dealing a base amount of damage equal to the number of withering damage the wielder just took. This attack does not use up the wielder's attack action, nor does it reset their initiative value.

                                                    At Essence 3+ the wielder may spend a point of Willpower to make the Evocation last until their next turn.

                                                    Primeval Spirits Merge
                                                    Cost: 10m, 2wp Mins: Essence 4
                                                    Type: Simple
                                                    Keywords: Perilous
                                                    Duration: One Scene
                                                    Prerequisites: Pack Retaliation Technique, Defiant Mother Bear Stance

                                                    Meditating on the beast spirits that reside in the blade, the wielder release a loud call; a growl or shrill shriek of some kind and summons a sleeping beast from the shards. The Essence of both mingle, causing the wielders anima to take on a brilliant combination of their natural essence plus the silvery red contours, lying over them like a ghostly cape and hood of the ancient beasts they have released back into Creation. The blades gather in a large circling flock, the centre of the wheel in line with the root chakra of the wielder.

                                                    When the wielder activates Call Forth the Primeval Beast they gain numerous static benefits for the remainder of the scene. The first is that they gain 6 hardness that doesn’t stack with armour, as well as +2 charm based dice on all combat related actions and gaining an additional 1 point of Initiative every turn.

                                                    On top of this the wielder also converts 2 dice of withering damage into auto successes and lowers the enemies hardness by 2 for decisive damage. The wielder gains +1 to their Resolve when a situation arises of someone or something attempting to intimidate, threaten, plead with or lie to.

                                                    Finally, due to the intense and powerful connection to the myriad of ancient beasts, the wielder receives a Defining level Intimacy for the scene of “I must shed my prey’s blood”. The wielder instinctively ignores allies and will viciously assail the nearest targets, starting with those who have an Intimacy of fear, whether naturally or through Defiant Mother Bear Stance, and then working through the rest of the enemies present. If an ally attempts to stop them through calming words or begging for compassion, they can succeed through an instill or bargain social action but the wielder gains the extra dice associated with the Defining level intimacy. If violence or threats of violence are issued then the wielder adds them to their “prey list”, unless the wielder spends their own (Essence) in Willpower to ignore it.

                                                    If the wielder is put into Initiative Crash, but are put into -3 rather than usual. The effects of Primeval Spirits Merge ceases and the wielder is left fully aware of their failings to shed their prey's blood, and for the rest of the combat scene suffer a -2 on all dice rolls.

                                                    Special Activation Rules:
                                                    Call Forth the Primeval Beast is a tiring Evocation, and takes its toll on the wielder's body and the connection between warrior and weapon. To be able to reuse Primeval Spirits Merge the wielder must find a worthy prey animal and kill it, bathing the weapon in its blood and feasting upon the raw meat.
