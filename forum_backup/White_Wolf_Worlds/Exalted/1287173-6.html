        Okay; the rest exhausted me, but I’m determined to keep to my schedule, and there are a lot fewer Subterfuge Charms, I checked.

        First up is Subtle Silver Declaration, controlling concealment of the Tell. I’m feel I won’t be able to fully appreciate it until I see complete Tell rules and details, but for the time being it seems like low anima (but not no anima? Maybe it’s a matter of anima increasingly revealing Tell) has it almost perfectly concealed, with those familiar having their attempts to find it weakened, and higher anima not automatically showing it and also weakening attempts to find it. Perhaps the Tell works as no anima: unseen, can be spotted by people that know of it, dim make it a bit more apparent to the unfamiliar, glowing shines a light on it. It’s probably a little useful, but not to the point of being mandatory (even if literally every Lunar is capable of buying it).

        Shifting Penumbra Stance is back as a nice kind of disguise by obscuring in light, and I think its interesting that some forms of read intentions can still figure things about them out, and how beating the Lunar’s roll only gives you awareness of the kind of Exalt. Ah, and Essential Mirror Nature, to help improve some of their disguise against figures such as Dragon Blooded. Let’s see… Caste Mark and anima change appearance for some other Exalt, they keep their banner but it takes minor necessary cosmetic changes (probably hardly a problem, since many others can include some animal stuff). Ah, and they can alternatively do some god or fae stuff, even if they can’t shapeshift into those. It’s Protean, so you can get it to last longer if you’ve got the shape of a particular Exalt to imitate (including taking their iconic banner), and Totemic so that a spirit shape with natural mimicry can take it with Manipulation.

        Moon’s Hidden Face is curious. I guess it’s a case for if you want to keep your hybrid body on standby, but want to retain discretion, or else you’re a Lunar who naturally possesses mutations. Sprit shapes with camouflage get to learn it as Manipulation. I think I may want to seek some dev clarity on how this might be employed.

        Huh, I’d wondered if Changing Plumage Mastery might return if it wasn’t a Universal Charm. Oh, but it does use Many-Faced Moon as a prerequisite (makes sense for something that takes on a bit of its old function). So, it’s a permanent extension of transformative prowess, allowing for alternately assuming a boring, forgettable appearance that is hard to recall or investigate, going back and forth between being Hideous as desired, freely changing the appearance of clothing (with the amusingly rare Appearance + Craft roll for making especially lavish changes, and can commit some motes to substitute their scent for that of another form. I’m interested in digging out my copy of Manual: Lunars (the only of my Second Edition Manuals that isn’t a pdf currently in my reach) to see how it compares to prior writing; I have the vague impression that it’s overcome some shortcoming or made a bit of an in-joke.

        One of the Herd can be used to blend in with even a small group so long as one acts the part, only discernible through their Tell; The Totemic effect lets it be Manipulation for mimicry animals, although I have a mild impression it might include things like zebras. Then there’s Scent-of-All-Nations Attitude, which is actually a bit reminiscent of a previewed Solar Charm that never made it to the final product, with that effect of causing strangers who encounter them to automatically assume they share a culture, or else present oneself as belonging to a specific one, and benefiting from associated positive Intimacies. It can be seen through with read intentions or profile character to discern information contradicting that identity, although doing so for one aping a specific culture is more difficult. What I like particularly is the flavour text saying that people gather the impression of shared background from things like subtle cues, and completely overlook stuff like language and appearance; it’s just fun to imagine a Southerner going around with, say, an icewalker tribe who accepts them as a fellow without question. I was never too into the worldwalker thing for Lunars, but if it’s manifesting in ways like this I’ve no problem. It’s Totemic in the same manner as the prior Charms.

        Up to Essence 2, and Enticing Butterfly Trick, that name sounds familiar… in with the pheromones again. Did we see this before, the effect also seems familiar… actually, I think it’s vaguely similar to a Terrestrial Charm. So let’s see if I have this straight, a Lunar concealing tracks or evidence can spray it with pheromones, people working to uncover them get a whiff and risk having a Tie instilled, which can also inflict a penalty on actually figuring out the lead. I’m glad that something reminiscent of yet another Ability has been rolled into the mix, and a kind of animalistic thing to be put down behind a Lunar trying to evade detection. I can also see some characters that would deliberately draw attention and run, just to lure people into the little pheromone bombs.

        Subtle Chameleon Practice is something that Lunars would be absolutely remiss without, getting some of that Randall-style of skin concealment (although mechanically modelled in a way that isn’t absolute), and a nice Protean effect to let it overlay and improve the camouflage of other animals; I like that sense of the two kinds of skin modification blending together to shore up one another’s limits.

        Ah, and another delightfully witch-like one in the expected Mirror Slip Trick; literally merge with a reflective surface, becoming highly stealthy and difficult to effect. I was wondering about how vulnerable the actual object would be, but that’s covered by the Lunar’s Essence shoring up the structure; I had a mind that clear water was a bad idea for being messed up with ripples, but it seems a strong Lunar would give even that a bit of hardy surface tension. It’s actually interesting to fix of this being applied to stealth, considering that Creation is likely to have few silver-backed glasses of the kind we’re familiar with; most mirrors are going to be polished metal, which even at the best quality will produce a somewhat murky image (although the idea of lurking in the back or the corner of even clear mirrors is effectively creepy). A bit of higher Essence lets them move between different mirrored surfaces, and it even has an obscure use of functioning as a means of escape if some other power traps them in such a surface.

        Cuckoo’s Nest Hospitality, that sounds rather ominous. Ah, it’s basically similar to Scent-of-All-Nations, but for being assumed in a social role of prominence rather than a particular group, and letting it broadly function akin to a Minor Tie in addition to any relevant pre-existing ones for a given character. Its weakness is about the same as using Scent to imitate a specific nation, it only lasts a day but doesn’t need its Willpower cost paid consecutively to maintain, and is Totemic for mimicry.

        Hmm, Fickle Lady’s Shifting Star starting Essence 3… I think it’s both more specific and less comprehensive than it (or its closest analogue) might have been in the past. It builds off of Essential Mirror Trick, and extends the deception to divinatory or scrying magic. I like some of the odd places it can extend to, such as making a power that only responds to a specific individual or type open for a properly disguised Lunar, even if it’s primarily for things like keeping a Sidereal from foreseeing you doing something out of keeping with what you’re pretending to be. And with everything it can do, it’s fine to be unable to stop something that specifically breaks through disguises. Mimicry Totemic.

        Dreams-and-Expectations Stance… ah yes, another brought in from Glories, the one to let you be seen as the individual most expected or desired, modelled as a disguise roll with double 8s (I think it’s good to differentiate from full shapeshifting), and very difficult to see through. It can even be used to maintain multiple disguises to different observers simultaneously, although keeping them from figuring out something amiss has to be a bit of a balancing act (if helped by the difficulty in seeing through it). At this point, it would be easier to say what Charms are not Totemic.

        Ideal-Made-Flesh Apotheosis is another means of raising Appearance through meditation, and even removes the Willpower cost of one of the prior Appearance-raising Charms. So that’s three now which can do that, capable of giving a Lunar a particularly formidable Appearance, and all kinds of Charms that capitalise on that. It’s certainly one way to set these Attribute Exalted apart from the Solar capacity to raise Appearance. Not Totemic.

        Huh, that’s very new and strange, a Charm to make Lunars completely undetectable to taste and smell in Shadow Wisp Sublimation. The flavour text is a bit whimsical, but the effect is actually remarkably powerful, being infallible even against magic to enhance them. Then there’s Moon-in-Well Emanation for… huh, that’s really strange, making a god in their image by pulling out their reflection; wonder what the theological implications of that are. The mirror god has one of each level of the Lunar’s Intimacies (with associated memories at their leisure, and otherwise a blank slate), all of their non-magical or social traits, and a decent mote pool. In addition to Materialize, Hurry Home and Mirror Slip Trick (which can be used for free), the player and Storyteller design a number of additional Charms depending on the strength of the Lunar’s Essence, and able to grow stronger as the Lunar does as well as the passage of stories in which it played a major part; they even get to retain those Charms if recreated by the Lunar. For a final uncanny detail, bringing their reflect to life means the Lunar doesn’t have it (must lead to persisting legends that Anathema on the whole have no reflections), and only returns slowly if the spirit is destroyed, with recreation only being possible when the reflection returns. I know it’s only one Attribute in, but I feel as though all of the more wordy Lunar Charms so far have been a good deal more focused and personal than comparable ones for other Exalted. Not Totemic.

        Now, subterfuge’s Essence 4, and what does Clover Can’t Be Found do now… penalises trying to see through stealth, disguise or notice the Tell, and Ties that motivate the person to find the Lunar actually weaken their perceptions. That kind of turning strength into weakness and undermining other efforts… it’s reminiscent of another old idea for Lunars, but somehow everything is holding together coherently. Ah jeez, in an animal shape with camouflage the damn thing saps successes. Got to be really hard to find Lunars at this level. Not Totemic.

        Shifting Constellation Raiment builds off of Dreams-and-Expectations Stance and Fickle Lady’s Shifting Star to enhance the former prereq with the effect of the latter, without needing Essential Mirror Nature. Furthermore… this is very strange, the Star interference with magical effects such as individually calibrated locks respond in ways that the observer desires or expects, rather than what it would do for the identity of the disguise, although doing so can actually give the game away, so it’s a bit of a balancing act.

        And Silver Ripple Mirage… I’ve already talked about.

        So, the Subterfuge Charms are also interesting, and often delightfully weird. It’s still relatively early, but I think the Lunars are making a strong showing so far for variety with a good thorough line.[/spoiler]

        Won’t take too long to go through the last few of these Appearance Charms. Jeez I’m tired.

                Now, quickly finish Appearance off with the warfare Charms. Might have been judged a curious fit in the past, but hey, it’s one of the Attributes that can be rolled for command actions. I’ll be interested if they all strictly feel like they’d go with War.



                Start with Glorious Battle Presence, allowing some failed dice to be rerolled, and possibly gain Initiative if the result turns up well. Finally seeing a break from Totemic Charms for mimic animals, with the alternative Attribute of Strength for those with a pack hunter spirit shape; wolf, claw strider, various kinds of squid… I wonder if the discount for Caste or Favoured is necessarily worth it here, given you’d still need a decent Appearance to make a good roll, but then maybe… hmm, I want to say that you might have an incentive to stunt Strength into your Excellency, but doesn’t having the Appearance Excellency imply either a discounted Attribute or high rating… ah, no I misread it before, you don’t need it at a rating if you’ve got a few Charms in it, so a Lunar with two of the most basic Appearance Charms could use the Excellency to enhance their command actions, and then add their Strength to it.

                Anyway, Wolf Devours Shepherd, a Charm to penalise commanders trying to order battle groups that see the Lunar (I like how they don’t actually need to be fighting against the Lunar), and weaken them if the resulting roll fails. In other words, a scary Lunar off in the distance makes troops less receptive to instructions from their commander, and when they don’t hear it properly they start to become restless and uncoordinated. Apart from the basic concept, I like how the Lunar hardly has to do anything, their mere visible presence capable of being a disruptive influence; I think that sets it apart from some of the image of action in any similar Solar Charm. And hey, warfare without War, delightful.

                Building from that is Panicked Herd Incitement, targeting the battle group itself when it tries to do anything, penalising it with fear, and causing the beginnings of rout if their action fails. Protean effect allows shapes with strong Ties of fear to remove successes instead of dice. Again, Lunar just has to be seen, doesn’t have to do anything else, doesn’t have to be under attack. I really like this idea that part of how a Lunar might conduct war is by basically conducting themselves like a fearsome spectre or omen of doom; a hooded, hatted and cloaked figure wandering the margins, a grim black dog on the crest of a hill, a lone raiton circling overhead and cawing incessantly. The troops see these omens, it awakens a nameless dread in their hearts, and it just takes one bad manoeuvre to have them feeling as though they’re cursed or hunted and start disintegrating.

                The last two Charms were also Totemic, of course.

                Victorious Beat-King Spirit takes things along a different path. A more conventional path, by the looks of things, being able to reflexively make rally actions upon successfully landing attacks, with their own success capable of enhancing the roll (generously including 10s from both hit and damage rolls to determine the bonus). Followed by Diving Hawk Inspiration, following the logic of a Lunar’s haste into battle guiding followers by allowing them to apply 10s from their Join Battle as successes to their battle group, with a high enough result letting them add a reflexive order action. Deviating from the last several, that one can be taken as a Dexterity Charm by group hunter totems.

                Now it’s Essence 2, and Panicked Herd Incitement gets to develop into Pride-Scattering Approach, which actually does specify and invoke the Lunar being an opponent in battle, allowing attacks by themselves or associated army to benefit from a bonus success on a damage roll, increasing difficulty if that causes a rout check, and penalising attempts to rally if the enemy does start to flee, with Protean human and animal shapes weakening the rout check further. Given the order of activation, it seems like a bit of a gamble, but potentially one with some big returns.

                Oof, Rank-Sundering Carnage sounds nasty. Represents the idea that successful attacks are opening holes in the ranks to let friendly troops in by allowing half of the successes on a withering damage roll to be given as bonus dice to the troops who attack immediately. I like the image of a Lunar being like a heavy weight swinging away against the front line of an opposing army, or the idea that a Lunar can confer the benefits of good command just by being a roving force of destruction. It gives a nice image to the efficacy of some Lunar guerrilla armies.

                Argent Battle Exemplar lets a Lunar that has successfully attacked to automatically use excess successes as the result of a reflexive command roll, although doing so can remove them from something like a damage roll where they might have applied. So there’s still the element of a Lunar Appearance-based commander giving orders, but they feel like they can function as a kind of instinct emerging from successful individual fighting prowess. I mean, Appearance is supposed to represent leading an army by example from the front anyway, but I like to extrapolate from this that the Lunar is doing so with a bit more precision (even apart from it being not just looking good at the front, but out of how well they land strikes).

                Oh, hey, it doesn’t actually have to be used when the Lunar and troops are fighting a battle group, and if they are the Lunar doesn’t even need to pay motes for it. Yeesh, that makes it sound particularly nasty to fight certain kinds of Lunar, if they’re doing that to your army with impunity.

                Finally there’s the Essence 3 Charm Silver-Maned Warlord Glory, allowing the Lunar who is shining with their anima to employ that in being the standard for their troops, using it as the metric for bonus dice to command roles and an army’s rout checks. On top of that, there’s the option once per scene to expend some anima in order to reset rally for numbers actions, with the idea of pulling back together even far-fling troops. And those dice bonuses are scene long, with a relatively small commitment of motes. It’s interesting how this resolves some of the old issues with such extended bonuses by being very conditional and variable.

                And that’s the last of the warfare Charms. I feel as though my assessment of them can’t be summarised more readily than what I said throughout.

                What does warrant summary is the end of the Appearance Charms. What I find most remarkable here is what it introduces about Lunar Charms as a whole, and a possibility that I had never before fully conceived of, fully visualised; that a single Attribute could encompass such range and diversity that a Lunar could conceivably focus entirely on that one Attribute, and have a large array of powers, with varied effects and applications and imagery, available to explore. Social adaptation and engagement, emotional cultivation, being the centre of attention, disappearing completely from attention, multiple identities, repulsing attack, sabotaging armies and leading troops.

                And that’s just from being a Pretty Kitty. Appearance 5 alone opens a huge vista of narrative worlds for a Lunar to explore.

                I finally understand how you do them as diversity and adaptability Exalted. In aggregate, the Charmset as a whole might not include a wider range than that of something like the Solars, but for an individual Lunar, the cross-section of competencies represented by the smaller investment in a small number of Attributes permits a higher capacity to be diverse and adaptable as a distinct example of a Lunar.

                It’s quite remarkable, and I enjoy seeing it play out. Stuff like this is why I was averse to those playtest threads.

                    A few thoughts I had on possible implications for the nature of Lunar social prominence and government, and the form of their competencies.

                    Originally posted by Isator Levie, post: 22405467, member: 55610
                    I'd have to go back and compare at some point, but I rudimentarily think it might contrast distinctly with a lot of Solar social Charms that are predicated on things like reading into and harnessing existing social systems, and arguing to people on matters of Principle, as well as cultivating forms of dedication to and being impressed with the Solar's person. I think there may be a lot of things to allow a Solar to govern or be a champion of people more systemically, allowing projection of power beyond their direct person.

                    I've not yet seen the Charisma Charms, but just comparing Appearance to that there's a sense that a Lunar can be an extremely compelling presence, but it kind of necessitates them being present; they'd have to distribute themselves around in order to effectively exert influence, and the scope or form of that influence that they can precisely control or plan for might end up being limited.

                    It paints an interesting picture of what some dominions might be like, and what prospective Lunar governance of the world could be.

                    Mind, there could still be room for Attributes such as the No Moon ones to add some variations or additions. Still, some of this points to another interesting difference; for a Solar or Terrestrial, whatever things like Bureaucracy, Presence or Socialize say about their formats of government and social prominence, they're all consolidated. If you've got one of those Abilities, you've got its whole package. Other Attributes might make up for synergistic things that Appearance lacks, but it means that you need to stretch further afield to cover all of those bases (possibly barring the right spirit shape).

                    So it's also a way of realising the idea of Lunars actually often being a bit more generalist, but in a more interesting manner than them not being good at any specific thing. Rather, with an Attribute such as Appearance, the Lunar can be good at a number of things, and be very good at them, but not be good at every part of any given field. You're very good at commanding armies from the front in battle, and sabotaging enemy morale, but don't have anything at all for strategems or siege engines. You could develop Intelligence to get at anything that might empower those, but is it not more convenient, more you to develop skills at infiltrating a society or becoming a celebrity?

                    Heh, the ironic thing is that specialising in a small number of Attributes makes a Lunar an extreme generalist.

                                                All right, Charisma. Got a tough act to follow, but I’m interested to see what expansions to the Lunar social suite might provide. I’m going to divide the Influence Charms at the end of Essence 2, just to make it more manageable.


                                                Already seen the Heart’s Blood Charm, so skipping right past that to Argent Songbird Voice. Fairly basic Charm to modify addressing crowds, although the idea of changing the function of the Excellency strikes me as having interesting possibilities. Poised Lion Attitude seems to already getting to something more expansive even if it functions simply; reflexively making influence rolls, what might that look like?

                                                Despite the name, Arrogant Lion Bearing is the start of a new cascade, and is the first Attribute replacement Charm, allowing this one to be used to calculate Resolve, and I like how it uses the idea of such comparison through force of personality to have the option to increase Resolve in the face of less charismatic people. Then another root with Beast-King Dictates, allowing failures on influence rolls to get somebody to do something to be re-rolled; the Protean effect is interesting in being something that could thoroughly belong to the Lunar’s default form and identity, expanding the parameters for dice re-rolls.

                                                And from that is Guiding the Flock, to double 9s on rolls to get people to act in a manner according to the standards of their own culture, with a handy little sidebar to provide an expansive definition of what qualifies as a culture, as well as what I think is a pretty good guideline on how to approach influencing a group’s customs in play. I’m remembering some things from previews, some that gave me a notion of Lunars effectively guiding people from a position as outsiders.

                                                Ahh, Herd-Strengthening Invocation, that was the one. Being able to cultivate a Principle towards a custom that can only be beneficial, by way of either praise or criticism. I’m interested in trying to think through scenarios in which a Lunar who hates a culture would want to instil in its people a stronger value for one of its customs; maybe a form of sabotage if it’s viewed as detrimental, maybe trying to get people to see its shortcomings. It invites intrigue, if nothing else.

                                                Heh, I’d forgotten to consider things to interact with animals. Pack-Calling Cry which basically does what it says on the tin (albeit only with one animal at a time). I’d also say that it is fairly accommodating with getting a large or slightly magical animal. I enjoy it having a bit of an uncanny edge in the way that the animal can arrive with unnatural speed. Personally, I’m imagining it being particularly interesting for the Lunars of Luthe, but that might just be because I’m picturing a clever little cephalopod or cetacean being very confused by why it’s going and how it’s suddenly becoming so much faster. Or maybe just a Lunar impressing a date by calling a nice bird to their side and having it perform a quirky trick.

                                                Anyway, Untamed Soul Unity, to form a familiar bond of up to two dots between an animal and a person (not necessarily the Lunar). That it functions much more strongly when used to benefit a Solar Mate, being able to make the bond permanent from the beginning and can connect a three-dot one, has me thinking in terms of one of the possible values they can provide being as a kind of intermediary between man and beast.

                                                Back to the bottom with Charismatic Lunar Trick to give double 9s for rolls based around providing emotions, especially for something the Lunar is feeling themselves. I find that a bit resonant with some of the Appearance stuff, that for all of Lunar dissembling there’s something powerful that they can convey about what is sincere or meaningful about themselves. Or they can encourage a bit of that exposure in another through Rapture of the Nightingale, lowering their Guile if a Lunar inspires emotions that might naturally reveal their intent through resonance or interference.

                                                Crowd-Inciting Fervor also seems particularly strong as something revolutionary or Dionysian by inspiring those who hear the Lunar to feel rage and direct it at something they’re naturally inclined against, with caveats against necessary Intimacies and requiring a Decision Point to not do so. I like the idea of ways in which this can be applied, whether it be helping people to get over fear or decorum or any other such restraint in order to feel allowed to express their frustrations or outraged, or inflicting anarchy by having a community just explode in the multiple directions of their individual rancour (especially if it’s a Lunar that has been going around spreading such things beforehand).

                                                Let’s see, Soul-Baring Testament is a Charm purely dedicated to the Solar Mate, and feels the most reminiscent so far of the old Background by providing dice to enhance influence rolls in line with the mate, as well as making it harder for others in future to disregard them. Mind, the Protean effect makes it wonderfully strange with the idea of the Lunar being able to benefit from the Resolve penalty if they’re using the Charm while looking like their own mate. I’ll also note the fun implications on how the Charm doesn’t mandate positivity; if you don’t like your Mate, you still get bonuses from going around trying to convince people that the Solar is a jerk and act against them. I wouldn’t even view this still ending up with a Resolve penalty against that Exalt in future to be contradictory, with a few ideas metaphysical and interpersonal behind why the Lunar might still convey a bit of susceptibility: “I agree this Solar is a jerk… but there does seem to be something a bit interesting about them”.

                                                Beast-God Idolatry provides the first Charm to benefit a Lunar’s crafting, and I think there’s something wonderfully egotistical in the idea of a Lunar imbuing an object crafted in their image (for any of the multifarious values that can have) with a capacity to instil a Tie to the person depicted. Doesn’t have to be positive either, so you could always use it to cultivate fear for your own benefit, or hatred of a still living prey that you want to sabotage.

                                                Shining Moon-Child Mark, that was previewed as well. As I said back then, it strikes me as the ultimate realisation of something suggested back in the days of Lunar errata, albeit less violent and less specifically amorous. I also like the sense that it has a certain confluence between the general thesis of Charisma as an Attribute and an animalistic quality of marking your territory, albeit in a more understated, mystical manner.

                                                Hard-Nosed Denial Style functions as an interesting kind of social clash, resisting an argument with an immediate counter that also has a capacity to make the other side respect your conviction. This one covers a couple of the trends that the Charms have been making good use of so far, dealing with problems sideways, Charms that are multi-faceted and able to convey an influence underneath their more direct function. It’s neat.

                                                Oh, that was Essence 2. Okay, halfway along, more or less.

                                                Next there’s Silver Soul Conviction, again with the Lunar’s displays of themselves; ah, it’s like that Appearance Charm, success reaping back motes, just this time for influence to uphold an Intimacy (if to a Solar mate, treated as maximum or one step higher). Yes, keep going with this idea that a Lunar can be empowered by revelry in their success, in living their truth, a surge of spiritual energy.

                                                Lightning-Calling Challenge builds upon Crowd-Inciting Fervor in a novel way; rather than inspiring anger towards what people have reason to be angry at, call all of that shit towards the Lunar herself. In particular for the sake of making hostiles incapable of attacking anybody other than themselves; I particularly like the way that interacts with area-of-effect attacks, that idea that they can only be made if the Lunar will also be hit. I like the way that it models the resistance, the sense that you’re not actually overcoming the anger but ignoring the impulse to attack the Lunar specifically, and it’s still a distraction from other endeavours. And there’s a Protean effect to harness a pre-existing rage or hate to make the impulse harder to resist, and more distracting if it is.

                                                Now, Heart-Stirring Expression, a broader kind of emotional incitement, but still a kind of lunatic fervour, what with balancing the bonus to non-physical action in complement against making it harder to focus on oppositional action. I’m curious, it’s an Instant Charm and no other time span is suggested… perhaps the base inspire influence rules would provide more insight.

                                                It looks like it’s going a bit bigger with Creation-Spanning Passion… so first, a bonus is provided when a Lunar is trying to guide somebody that they love, hate or otherwise with the same feelings in response, as well as making it easier to strengthen an existing form of that Tie. From there, there’s how strong Ties allow both individuals to have divinatory dreams about one another (although a Solar mate shares those even with a basic Tie), so long as they have regular personal interaction. This one feels like an especially compelling application of the Charisma idea, the “see me, know me, feel me” thing. The fact that it’s a two-way connection inherently conveys a fascinating sense that a Lunar can’t open their heart to another without needing to expose a bit of vulnerability, but what I find most fascinating about this is the idea of using it on an enemy; the idea of a Lunar cultivating and strengthening a rivalry with a powerful Dynast, and then being able to divine information about their status and plans in dreams, at the cost of needing to deal with similar information being conveyed back. God, the implications of that… Dynasts who are aware of the connection, but keeping it under wraps for fear of being viewed as compromised, Dynasts who know how it might be broken but just can’t help but find the intelligence provided too valuable to pass up… oh dang, you could do something like the Rey-Kylo visions from The Last Jedi with this (presuming a later Charm doesn’t make it full-blown), and that stuff was when that movie really started to pop for me. This is a good one.

                                                Hmm, maybe I jumped the gun before and Herd-Reinforcement Stance was what was previewed… yeesss, that was the one, the idea of being a Lunar on the outside who is able to act on members of a culture through a provisional Principle of one of their customs. That idea that an external perspective lets one get through to a person via the patterns of their society, even if they wouldn’t care strongly in everyday life. I think it can have a surprising amount of variation to help or hinder members of a group.

                                                Okay, not much left now. Lion’s Voice Command finally gives something Totemic… did I see this before… anyway, fairly basic function, become loud enough to hear over long distances, cheaper if you’re in the shape of an animal with Legendary Size, can be taken with Strength for spirit shapes that can intimidate. I like how it emphasizes the fact that it’s a form of bellowing with the idea that the only influence that can come across being inspire and threaten; you’re not making nuanced appeals to fulfil complex instructions while your voice is ringing in a person’s ears.

                                                Hmm, Psyche seems to be coming earlier for Charisma with Moonstruck Reverie Trick. Getting a little neatly transgressive there, like a time-release Cupid’s Arrow (albeit not in a manner I would expect for a Chosen of Serenity). Sure, that a Lunar has a capacity to grant themselves virtually any trait that they would instil an attraction towards in another’s dreams, but there are surely many benign and malevolent applications to letting such a platonic or amorous love at first sight form for a particular person or random stranger. I also like how, while the Psyche effect creates the inciting incident and lets the resulting Tie skip to Major, it conveys no other obligations or endurance, so the affected character is free to act on it as they please and it can erode naturally if the relationship proves incompatible. Maybe it’s the romantic in me, but it gives me the image of a situation in which a person learns that their infatuation started with a bit of witchcraft employed by a Lunar, but the person affirms that their wicked magic is not the reason why the relationship could blossom and endure.

                                                Going back a few steps to return to the standby of Lunars generating fear; from the way that it not only enhances a threat or instil action, but denies the protection of Intimacies of courage or fearlessness, I like how Silver Thunder Roar’s basic enhancement still conveys a certain texture of striking on an instinctual level that the rational consciousness can’t fully deal with. Then it’s Snarling Outburst Rebuke to let a Lunar boost their own influence complementary to how another resists it, and I’m seeing another trend in which Lunars can draw strength from a kind of mirroring of an opposing character.

                                                Another Psyche Charm for the attractively named Endless Nightmare Hunt, which has another multitude of effects; increase the grip that an existing Tie of fear has on a character’s Resolve (that they don’t need to see the Lunar bringing in another fun instance of the “nameless fear” element), making instil rolls to strengthen that Tie while observing the character sleep, and afflicting them with nightmares to deny Willpower regain. It’s curious that it’s an Indefinite Charm, but given how the different effects likely have to take place on different stages, I’m guessing that it’s intended for the dedicated stalker of a Lunar, marking an individual as prey to follow and harass and weaken over an extended period. It’s good then to provide the victim with a process for fully shaking it off, and not too much of an expensive one.

                                                After something heavy like that, it’s nice to see a return to something a bit more pleasant in Songbird’s Eternal Refrain… although that’s mostly just an impression given by the first line. The actual effect of resetting emotional influence rolls to instil or persuade, and weakening the response, can just as readily be applied to get another shot at somebody who managed to shake off your attempts to hurt them through it. I think the idea of getting a Willpower refund from a shape that a target has an emotional Defining Tie towards might be the most… marginal Protean bonus yet. By that I don’t mean it’s not fitting, or not valid or useful, just that it’s a nice side bonus rather than something that can function in the core of the power.

                                                It’s interesting that Charisma is getting more Psyche than Appearance, I guess it constitutes a more assertive Attribute. Anyway, Unceasing Hunger Affliction, what could this possibly entail? Heh, I like that image of a Lunar’s eyes flashing with ghostly fire… so in the base form, an inspire roll to inflict a basic Obsession towards some good or service, that can fade after a few days if the victim doesn’t fail a test against it; not as devastating as the Appearance Charm to inflict Obsession, but it is after all only Essence 2, and can probably be effectively inconveniencing under the right circumstances. It picks up a bit more with an Essence 3 repurchase, letting the original target turn it from an obsession into a social craze; it might only transmit from the first, but point it at the right strategic factor and it can effectively spread far. I’m imagining the devastation a Lunar might cause from things like spreading a desire for some limited resource; a bit of rioting over shortages, a bit of market speculation that turns into a bubble. Immaculate monks are meant to serve as cultural enforcers, I can imagine that more than a few see sudden surges in popularity for a given thing as a warning sign of Anathema activity.

                                                Of course, even at the individual level… make a prince obsessed with a market you have cornered, and then leverage it against them.

                                                Finally for Essence 2 there’s Argent Revelry Festival. In contrast to a lot of the other Charms for a society at large, this one is for when the Lunar is right in the thick of things, an active participant; by leading or taking part in a customary event, all other participants can be inspired with pride in their society or culture, and given a temporary pool of dice for social or mental pools in actions in service to that society. I’m particularly fond of this Protean effect, and its specification of an animal shape; I’m seeing Coyote from Gunnerkrigg Court leaping and laughing about in the revelry, for the sake of the Lunar potentially maximizing the bonus dice that they provide for their fellows and followers.


                                                It’s good stuff so far. My conception of Lunar social activity and governance is filling out a bit, although not fundamentally overturned. So far, I think that Charisma Influence is a bit more focused than its preceding counterpart, but makes up for that with a bit more social variation, and it will be interesting to see any unexpected sub-categories.
