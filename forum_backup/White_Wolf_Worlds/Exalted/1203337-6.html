    Originally posted by Saipjas View Post
    Xerxes, Saying sorcery makes you buy just one charm is incorrect, you pay 3 points into occult just like you do MA, the cost for charm/compared to merit is relatively equal. Edit: another way to look at it is the charm is expressing the cost to gain sorcery in the mechanical sense, the initiation is the narrative (how) that it represents.

    Furthermore, you discounting how sorcerous initiations change the character in fundamental ways. A sorcerer initiated through Mara is by definition going to look at the world a hell of a lot differently than a person initiated through say a pact with an ifrit lord by virtue of the how (narratively) one needs to craft their initiation into their backstory.

    Further note: a SP stylist has around 10 total charms they can invest in, smaller in total than the amount of spells (and definitely from a utility aspect) and charms available to a solar sorcerer. Their MA charms at incompatible by design with both other MAs, and with all other ability charms. Are you telling me a sorcerer who invests in the 6 occult charms (six eternaties, ancient tounge, occulus, and all three circles) plus any additional spells, merits, and rituals, is less invested than a SP stylist?
    The difference being SP charms aren't their entire skill set, they'll usually end up picking up another martial art and even if they don't they still have the entire melee charm tree which also applies to their martial arts

                        Originally posted by Epee102 View Post


                        ...no it doesn't. I'm mostly tapped out here, but...like...no. Melee charms don't apply to Martial Arts attacks. Athletics charms, ironically, do. And if you want uber death kill atheletics is mandatory for those sweet sweet damage multipliers.                         It doesn’t even really combine well with steel devil, due to how it’s design really wants you to stay sheathed in between blows. Alucard made a good pint on Black claw, but even then it’s a hard sell imo. Lion Hamster as you have stated you don’t have much experience with combat based chars I can kinda see a little bit more where you are coming from. Based upon your previous commentary you equate a DnD wizard with a sorcerer and are looking for sorcery to be able to function in combat analogues to that correct?

                        A couple of brief things on casting in 3rd ed (I also play a sorcerer btw)

                        At essence 1 a solar can chuck 20 dice while generating 1 SM from ancient tounge, have 1 exploding 10 from six eternities, and theoretically can generate an additional 1-3 SM from their sorcerous initiation. The total successes needed to gen up Death of Obsidian Butterflies (or Brilliant raptor) is 15 SM, given the previous math it takes less than 2 full rounds on average to sling a high power attack spell.

                        For non-combat spells generating SMs is not a significant inhibitor. And thus your efficiency as a caster is honestly irrelevant in most cases.

                        So what are you needing heavy boosters for your spells? The most costly Combat spell (Incomparable Body Arsenal) lasts 5 hours, in the event you are caught by surprise and are trying to gen it up fast, your sorcerous mote gen will be even faster due to have a secondary initiation, more draw from Ancient Tounge, and atleast 3 exploding 10s on your 20 dice that you are chucking around (the amount of exploding 10a granted from Eternities).

                        If you are worried about Sorcerous workings at the solar level (let’s go with ambition 3, and say you are wanting full control in how it is done so finesse 5)

                        You need 75 Successes at the end of terminus for it to work, taking off 5 from each roll for finesse. If all you have are 5 rolls you would need an average of (napkin math) 20 success and yes that’s pretty hard to get.

                        However with 6 additional means (extending the interval 2 steps, cooperative caster, complementary spell, exotic components, complementary abilities) I just need an average of 12 successes to make it, easily within the scope. Now, does this take a metric assload of set up time? Absolutely but for changing the very rules of creation it should.

                        My point in this long diatribe is to show you that the level of mechanical investiture you are asking for is a nice to have because the system as designed doesn’t infact need more depth of charms to be able to do its ultimate schtick.

                        Now, can you bemoan the lack of additional spells? Absolutely, but those will be forthcoming. I would urge you to please re-examine the crux of sorcery to be through the initiation rituals rather than charm support, because the system has to make since for sorcerers of ALL walks of life (not just Solars).
