                1: Nope. Once you are out, you are out. No rolls.
                2: Yup. Stranger spirits might not go KO like mortals do, but they do stop functioning for a bit.

                I believe you are misunderstanding what's being meant with "all health levels of one type" in "The Tower Still Stands". They (presumably, I'm no dev but this is the way the charm makes sense to me) mean: every health level of a single numerical value. So, -0 is one "type", -1 is one "type", and so on and so forth.

                Edit: Also wanted to clarify: Bashing and Lethal Damage affect the same health levels. There isn't a set of health levels for Bashing and one for Lethal.

                                    Originally posted by Leetsepeak View Post
                                    Are there any illustrations anywhere of what soldiers of the Realm look like, like their armor?

                                    Anything like that for Immaculate Monks?

                                    What about Lookshyan Troops?

                                    Lookshyan armor tends to be heavily inspired by Japanese samurai designs. Below is the cover of the Outcaste book (spoilered for size, it's a big picture) which has some Lookshayan soldiers on it. There's a couple of more pictures in the Outcaste book of Lookshayan armor, but not nearly as many as you would think. In general though, if someone from our world saw a Lookshayan soldier, they would probably think, "Oh, that guy's armor looks like a samurai's."



                                    The Realm's armor looks like a mishmash of Roman, Greek and Chinese armor. Probably the best picture I've seen of it was on page 56 of the First Edition Dragon Blooded hardback. Unfortunately I can't think of any other places where we got a good look at standard soldier armor. One of the problems with the Exalted is that many Exalted either wear custom designed armor, or armor handed down by their family's which may have been designed centuries ago, or even back in the First Age. Very often, a Realm Dragon Blooded's armor has a different design from that of the soldiers he's leading.

                                    Below is the best picture I could find of Realm soldiers through a very quick internet search. It's there in your 3rd edition corebook on page 70.

                                    [/url]


                                    Immaculate Monks tend to wear monk robes, karate gi's and cool flowing clothing. Just think like, generic kung-fu movie protagonist. When Immaculates do wear heavier armor, it typically doesn't have any sort of distinguishing design that I'm aware of.
