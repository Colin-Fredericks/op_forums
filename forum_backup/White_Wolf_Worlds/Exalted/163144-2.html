    Blinded By Rage Vendetta is a Malfeas charm that gives the user hyperfocused tunnel-vision on a single entity who has harmed or insulted him in that scene. Upgrades at higher essence can extend the window of use. It gives him significant bonuses in combat against that individual... and equally significant penalties against everyone else.

        Mining the Heart of Gold Technique
        Infernal Fiend Charm - This charm enhances a social influence action against a target who has a positive intimacy towards the Infernal. If the influence action would benefit from such an intimacy, the intimacy is treated as one grade higher (Minor - Major - Defining). In addition, if the influence is successful, one of the target's other positive intimacies is downgraded by one level. If the target has no other positive intimacies then the Exalt may choose to upgrade one of their negative intimacies instead. The Fiend may not affect intimacies stronger than the one the target has for the Fiend (however, this is including the tier upgrade granted by the charm).

            Shadows Cast in Ash
            Infernal Thrown (Ebon Dragon-flavored)
            Upon causing the destruction of something or the death of someone, the Infernal may activate this Charm to take the shadow of the thing destroyed into her own. She may save (Thrown) such shadows in this way. At some point in the future, she may pay X motes to shoot her anima through with darkness from such a shadow. Within these shards of murk, most see nothing, but anyone with a positive Intimacy for the shadow's former owner watches the death or destruction play out in excruciating detail. Provided she can perceive them, the Infernal immediately becomes aware of all such individuals, the relevant Intimacies, their contexts, and degrees.

            The Infernal has already pierced the target's heart: the actual attack is just a formality. For the rest of the scene, when the Infernal throws projectiles from her shadow with <prerequisite Charm> at any so affected, her target's (some sort of defense/momentum) is reduced by (progressively more, depending on grade of Intimacy). If the target was previously unaware of the death or destruction of the subject of that Intimacy, treat the Intimacy as being one grade higher than it was for all purposes until the end of the scene. The shadowy projectiles vanish as they typically do, but leave an ashy smear wherever they strike, requiring extensive scrubbing to remove.

            A given shadow may only be used once in a given story.

                Firmament-Shattering Roar: Lunar Strength, possibly Elder, or maybe just Ess 5. An upgrade of Lion's Roar Rebuke, it allows the Lunar to roar so loudly that they actually crack the foundations of the world. The area around the Lunar is treated as one step more "Wyld." It is stackable, though the terrain decays back to its default state at the rate of one step per scene. As a side effect, any creature within the radius has to roll Stamina + Resistance or go deaf (one scene for supernaturals, permanent for mortals).

                                    Valiant Blood-Stone Vajrayana
                                    Raksha Sword
                                    Though Creation wears down upon all Princes of Chaos in its midst, some have found relief through religion. Specifically, pilfering the religion of others. This Charm may supplement a raksha's efforts to introduce new rites, implements, and other rituals into the existing worship of Creation-born. When these rituals become a prominent feature of their worship, the prayers they issue reach not their intended target, but the raksha, who likewise acquires any appropriate dots in Cult.

                                    As an additional benefit, should any of the new rituals clearly promote the death of a Creation-born, a stone of blood will appear on the raksha's person. This object will protect the raksha from Creation's ill effects (as a Cyst/Pennant...?), though it will degrade after a number of months equal to the Essence of the deceased, not to exceed the raksha's Heart Grace. The death of an Exalt counts as though that Exalt was 1 higher in Essence and ignores any limit from the Heart Grace. This blood-stone possesses no supernatural protection against being destroyed like an ordinary rock and the raksha cannot reflexively dismiss it from his person. The raksha may willingly crush it to recover a number of health levels equal to the deceased's functional Essence. A raksha may possess only one such blood-stone at a time.

                                        Beasts From Gluttons Revelation: Is an Ebon Dragon charm that expands on sorcerous activations of Want Becomes Need. During the activation of the former, the Infernal may choose to spend 1ahl to enhance the effect. Every time the victim of this charm indulges in the targeted act they must roll temperance at difficulty 2. Failure earns them a single point of mutation with aesthetics appropriate to their particular addiction. Those who suffer more mutation points this way than their willpower+the sum of their virtues are transformed into strange beasts that no longer resemble or count as human. Their motivation becomes to indulge in the behavior targeted with Want Becomes Need and they have an intimacy of hateful subservience to the infernal. Only removing the effect of Want becomes need may end this charm.
