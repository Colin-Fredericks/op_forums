    Looking for players! 1-3 for new solar game

    12-11-2013, 03:41 PM
    Our gaming group just started a new game, and one of the players had to drop out. Now we need a few more.

    This is a straightforward 2.5 Solar game (which, oddly enough, even though we're all pretty experienced with Exalted, none of us have ever played!)

    So far we have the GM, a neurotic, combat-heavy Night caste, and a cheerful Zenith with imperialist tendencies.

    Thanks for the interest!

    Edit: Its 2.5/errata. We voice chat over skype, and use roll20 for maps and dice. The ST is on the east coast, the other 2 players are PST. There's not a set game time yet, because we're still ironing out schedules.

    We've had exactly one session, so there's not much of a backstory to tell. Its set about 50 years after the core books, but no major changes to the world - anathema are slightly more well known, and we're expanded the number of solaroids in the world. The action so far is in the east, just north of Lookshy.
