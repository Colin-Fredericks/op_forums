    Originally posted by Walker-of-Ebony-Clouds View Post
    Is there a Martial Arts style in Exalted that revolves around crafting?

    I noticed most styles have complimentary abilities or completely revolve around a ability like stealth or social even performance but is there one that makes use of craft? Maybe a chef style or naruto like style that lets you summon/craft walls or structures like their earth styles?
    That sounds like a cool idea for a Celestial level, Terrestrial themes martial art. It could have a central tree of charms but five distinct starting points, each one that grants some bonus to a Craft (element) skill that can be used in either an offensive or defensive manner. As you learn the central tree, you can apply those charms to each element/craft that you have mastered, but you can only master as many elements as you have Essence, so it takes an Essence 5 Martial Artist to control all five craft skills through their martial arts.

    The only problem is that there are few craft applications on the battlefield that really suggest a martial arts approach. Weapon striking and shattering as per the Earth Immaculate style (in 2e, at least) but a lot of other craft applications would be preparatory, rather than 'in the moment of battle'.

                                Originally posted by Nekraa View Post
                                On Lore subjects, anyone know how broad/narrow they can be and how should one add more subjects that a character knows?
                                They're meant to be closer to narrative justifications for the character knowing a thing, not mechanical categories. More "categories" are gained simply through roleplay.
                                E.g. "You, an eastern Scavenger Lord with Lore (Scavenger Lands History and Geography) find a book on western history and commerce practices, add that to your Lore Background"

                                        Sorry, I don't quite understand your question. Are you asking for the pinyin of 1, 2, 3, etc?
                                        So you want to say Yi Shifu, Er Shifu, San Shifu, Si Shifu, Wu Shifu, etc. But I can't write pinyin on this keyboard, so I can't put in the tones. Can you read pinyin? Because the way it's written can be deceptive.
                                        It sounds like "ee" (high tone), "are" (falling tone), "san" (high tone), "suh" (falling tone) "woo" (falling/rising tone).
                                        Same as Yi Ge, Er Ge, San Ge, etc (first brother, second brother, third brother, etc).
