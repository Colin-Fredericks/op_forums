                            Originally posted by mrttao View Post
                            Some questions
                            1. Is it true that dragonblooded exalts are all descendants of the 5 immaculate dragons who reproduced with humans, or is it fanon? If it is fanon, what is the actual source of the dragon blooded?
                            2. Are greater elemental dragons and lesser elemental dragons just really powerful elementals not directly related to the 5 immacualte dragons or are those greater and lesser dragons in fact subsidiary souls of the the immaculate dragons (who are themselves some of the primary souls of gaia AFAIK)
                            3. If a lesser elemental dragon married a solar, would their children be dragonblooded? (I am guessing that they would based on what I think the answers are to the above 2 questions are)
                            Keep in mind, this is all going off of 1e/2e lore and might not be applicable for 3e.

                            1: No. The Immaculate Dragons as written/spoken of in the Immaculate Doctrine never actually existed. The names of the Five Elemental Dragons appear to have been used by the sidereals in their creation of the Realm's Terrestrial touting religion. If I recall correctly (and I could be wrong about the numbers), in preparation for the Primordial War, the Five Elemental Dragons gathered ten thousand hale, hearty, and libidinous individuals (9,900 women and 100 men, if I recall), granted them their power and then directed them to start breeding an army.

                            2: No elementals (Greater/lesser dragons or not) are related to the Five Elemental Dragons. All elementals spring from the destruction of five elemental behemoth type beings created by the Incarnae.

                            3: No. If a lesser elemental dragon (or any elemental really) married a solar, their child would either be an elemental godblood (if the elemental's essence were higher) or a solar halfcaste (if the solar's essence were higher).
