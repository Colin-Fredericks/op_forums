    I think one can assume that most familiars have some positive intimacy to their masters, because being a familiar probably reflects them having been raised by and lived with their master for a long time.
    I would certainly assume that any Familiar a PC bought at the start of the game has a Major or Defining intimacy of loyalty (might depend on the animal; a dog would probably have a Defining Intimacy, but a tiger might only have Major!).

    But using a charm on an animal that makes them a Familiar (or the Twilight anima banner) doesn't automatically give them an intimacy. That's what Spirit-Tied Pet is for.
    So, for example, if you make your bound demon a Familiar with the Twilight anima, then they're already loyal (from being bound). But if you use it on your enemy's bound demon, well you can now summon it to you and so on, but that doesn't suddenly make it loyal to you. Though you can now target it with Spirit-Tied Pet to make it loyal (though that costs 2xp).
    I think. Not 100% sure.
