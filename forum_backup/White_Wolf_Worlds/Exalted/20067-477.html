        Originally posted by Sunder the Gold View Post
        Can stomach bottle bugs handle any kind of poison?
        They consume poison, but have no particular immunity to it; in fact, their dice pool to resist poison is abysmal.

        Generally what happens is, if you summon a bug to cure someone of poison, it will do so by swimming around inside their body, consuming the poison, and very possibly dying from it.

        And of course, dying in droves from ingesting poison is how they reproduce.

        Originally posted by Sunder the Gold View Post
        Is there a demon that can perform surgery?
        I don't recall any with dots in Medicine aside from stomach bottle bugs, but I would hardly proscribe others from being able to do the deed.

        Originally posted by Sunder the Gold View Post
        One one that eats diseases like the bugs each poison.
        Not any that I recall, but you might homebrew one if you feel it's needed.

                                        Originally posted by Greyman View Post
                                        A rapier is a slashing sword, though with a straight, narrow blade, three to four feet long, used more for thusting. Weighing substantially more than a short sword, it is not a light weapon.

                                        Don't confuse it with a fencing foil.
                                        Actual weight doesn't have that much to do with weapon classifications in exalted. Note that short swords were also mostly used for thrusting. Great example is the Gladius to be used in close quarters when you've got a dozen other guys with armor and giant shields surrounding you. The cestus is also very much a thrust weapon.

                                        I can see why you might want to classify it as a medium weapon as rapiers would be in the same length range as most of the other swords, but to me the purpose and style of rapier use still seems to lean in towards the light weapon category.

                                        With things like this you gotta go with what's best for your game but I feel like there's arguments for both categories.
