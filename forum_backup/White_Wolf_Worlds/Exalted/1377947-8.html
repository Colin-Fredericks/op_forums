                                                        Here's a quick run-down of the Artifacts :

                                                        1) The Dragon-Dancer’s Wings (Blue and Red Jade Typhoon Fangs, Artifact •••)
                                                        Fire and Ice War Fans that allows dancers to rouse the depressed or becalm those taken by rage. Its capstone cures Psyche effects and can lower Derangements for a whole Story.

                                                        2) Empyrean Aegis (Blue Jade Thunderbolt Shield, Artifact •••)
                                                        Ice Shield which the user can throw at enemies ala Captain America. Its main function is to be thrown in the path of attacks to protect allies. The capstone lets you Defend Other a whole group of allies from range.

                                                        3) Majestic Nightmare Visage (Green Jade Devil Mask, Artifact •••)
                                                        A Mask that lets you take on the persona of a mythic fae lord schemer and plotter. The mask's power are especially effective against fae. The capstone grants a reservoir of points to be spent in schemes, similar to the Solar Larceny Charm Master Plan Meditation.

                                                        4) Sunset Blaze (Red Jade Direlance, Artifact •••)
                                                        A flaming spear for an army general, it rallies troops and hunts down enemy generals. The capstone can empower all allied battle groups with many benefits, including +1 Size and Perfect morale, and it can be used every battle.

                                                        5) White Lizard’s Tongue (White Jade Direlash, Artifact •••)
                                                        An Earth Whip for the Exalted herd wrangler in you! Brands allies and cattle to protect them or brand foes to weaken them. The capstone triggers a large earthquake that deals damage and sends enemies prone.

                                                        6A) Windblades (Artifact §••••)
                                                        Windblades are a category of First Age Artifact, a disc-shaped Heavy Thrown Weapon which can be ridden as a flying platform and shrunk down when not in use.

                                                        6B) Blazerider (Red Jade Windblade, Artifact §••••)
                                                        Blazerider is a flame themed Windblade which heavily specializes in aerial combat. It can be used as a weapon even while you are flying it! Its capstone creates a massive flame tornado.

                                                        7) Conquering Tide (Black Jade Warship, Artifact ••••)
                                                        A ship for policing the seas and enforcing justice, it helps investigation and hinders manipulation. Its capstone forces a criminal to atone for their crimes by subjecting them to a geas proportional to the gravity of their crimes.

                                                        8) Fivefold Dragons’ Grasp (Green Jade Smashfist, Artifact ••••)
                                                        Elemental gloves that greatly enhance Elemental Bolt and greatly loosen the restrictions on Auras. The capstone allows the wielder to fully ignore the bonfire Aura restrictions and it allows you to count as being in multiple Auras at the same time

                                                        9) Glacial Mantle (Blue Jade Articulated Plate, Artifact ••••)
                                                        A frozen armor for a general, it manipulates ice, creates environmental hazards and enhances battle groups. The capstone can grant battlegroups many benefits, including Might 2 and Overwhelming 5, and can be used every battle. A General wielding both this and Sunset Blaze is probably an absolute terror.

                                                        10) The Revelation Sphere (Blue Jade Puzzle, Artifact ••••)
                                                        A utility artifact for sorcerer-scholars, it helps the wielder track down ancient places of power and shape sorceries, especially investigative sorcery. The capstone enhances spellcasting in various ways for a Scene, including the possibility of reducing the willpower cost of spells by 1.

                                                        11A) Gunzosha Armor (Artifact § •••-•••••)
                                                        Gunzosha Armor is a category of First Age Heavy Artifact armor. It can be used by mortals, but only if they undertake a surgery that halves their lifespan. All Gunzosha Armor shares a base set of Evocation reflecting the advantage of fighting in a Power Armor, however each armor also unlocks its own set of Evocation based on its own legend.

                                                        11B) Seralawi, the Earth’s Beating Heart (White Jade Gunzosha Armor, Artifact §••••)
                                                        A defensive Gunzosha Armor whose 3 Evocations are all E1 and all have an alternate cost of 1wp instead of motes, so they can all be used by mortals. It focuses on Soaking hits and granting benefits when the wielder successfully soaks hits.
