                                                    Bleeding in general, considered when a target has a wound penalty of at least -1(see p. 271 for reference on the charm Unsurpassed taste and smell discipline). Mortals unlike exalted can bleed profusely at the rate of 1L per minute, if take a single injury higher than their stamina rating (see mortal healing p. 174). For infection you can see the rules in the disease section p. 234 but it should be noted as described on infected wound p. 235, Exalted and other supernatural creatures never suffer from infected wounds.
