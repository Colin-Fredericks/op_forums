                                            Originally posted by Accelerator View Post
                                            Now, the question, is this. Is it alright to make a disease that can spread through electronics? Especially for a medicine supernal?
                                            For a Solar? It's almost certainly not possible. The logical leap of connection there is just way too far. A Solar medicine supernal could create the greatest engineered disease ever that spreads like wildfire and kills almost everyone it touches, but using electronic signals to transmit a biological virus...no. A Solar medicine master can reattach somebody's severed arm, in pitch blackness, with no tools, in a mater of seconds, and do it so well that the patient doesn't even have visible scarring on the microscopic level.

                                            Or as an Example of the awesome power of Essence 5 medicine they can commit a bunch of motes and fill a patients body with sustaining Solar essence. This puts them into a coma where no disease, poison, or injury will advance in any way, they won't even age, until the Solar drops commitment of the charm or dies. Somebody on death's door due to a plague could be kept alive for thousands of years that way.

                                            But they can't, for instance, work their healing essence and fix a broken relationship, or regrow an amputees arm as a wicked armoured claw or something.


                                            For a Sidereal though, they might be able to transmit real disease through an electronic message.

                                                    Agreed with DrLoveMonkey that it's utterly beyond the scope of Medicine, for the reasons he cites. Disagreed that making any disease is in Solar Medicine. Solar Medicine seems exclusively focused on healing.

                                                    I'd say that at absolute minimum you were looking at three Exalts working together.
                                                    First you need an Abyssal to do death-Medicine and whip up a superplague. Might be a necromantic working involved, who knows. Maybe an Exigent of Plague instead, that'd be fitting. I'd absolutely not let it be anywhere near 90% lethal. 40% tops. 2% per Sux? That sounds about right. That's still absurdly lethal and capable of being the worst thing in an era (or ending an era).
                                                    Then you need a Solar to write the hypervirus that somehow infects everything at once. If I were doing a modern setting I'd have axed something to slot in a Computers Ability but Craft or Lore should work in a pinch. Craft probably, high-ranking project.
                                                    Then you need a Sidereal to make "file attachment" literal. (You also need somebody to convince the Maidens to make that Charm, but any of these Exalts should be capable of this.)
                                                    Then you've got a killer virus that kills a bunch of people but spares anyone with their MP3 player turned off, a bad network connection, or just healthy enough to shrug it off. And a pissed off alien civilization who is likely to respond to your WMD with their own WMDs and oh my everything just went mushroom-shaped.
