                                    Originally posted by Alistair View Post
                                    the actions themselves are not worth it when you could just attack, attack, attack, particularly when using a style as defensive as Swaying Grass is.
                                    Not in the combats I run. A pretty common tactic for any tactically minded enemies enemies like humans or fey is to have tough frontline enemies trying to engage the PCs while ranged fighters sit back, preferably in an elevated position, even more preferably with some kind of cover, and then rain death down, picking off easy crashes as people reset after decisives and then launching deadly decisive attacks themselves. Ambush on the road with forest strider spear throwers in the trees, defending a settlement with archers up watchtowers, frontline heavy infantry supported by snipers in the rear, or even just hurling down rocks from machicolations. I also have people retreat when they get in trouble or feel the tide turning, unless they have a decent reason to fight to the bitter end, and use environmental penalties whenever I can justify it. Even if it wasn’t a common tactic in real life and gaming, it just makes fights more dynamic and lets the people who invested really heavily into athletics and mobility do cool stuff.

                                    If you just start the fight and everyone charges into close with eachother and attack until one side wins it can get a bit repetitive. It also helps even the odds a bit, since Exalted PCs tend to be a fair bit more badass than their opponents, clever use of positioning and range can help a lot.

                                    The reflexive attack alone wouldn’t be worth the cost for a Solar or a Sidereal, their willpower buys more powerful effects because they’re more powerful exalts. You’re looking at the difference between Crimson Fang Bite that doubles (Strength) 10s on a decisive attack versus Iron Whirlwind Attack which gives you a multi-attack that multiplies the benefits of all charms applied to each attack and can be activated reflexively after Peony Blossom Attack. Fivefold Bulwark Stance gives you a scenelong penalty negator, while discounting Dipping Swallow and Bulwark Stance, and upgrading Dipping Swallow whole active. They’re all Essence 2 charms that cost 3m-5m and a willpower.
