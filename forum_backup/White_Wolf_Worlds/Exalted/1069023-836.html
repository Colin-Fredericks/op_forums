                                    07-04-2018, 01:05 AM
                                    Originally posted by Gonzo View Post
                                    What's your opinion about Martyr Dragon Blooded charms? Any chance something like them could be included in Ex?
                                    Things that require you to die to use them aren't great options for PCs. I try to avoid designing effects that really are only worth taking if you're an NPC.

                                    About Lunars, will we see the return of the brain-parasite charm that appeared I think in an Ink Monkeys post? I think that kind of charms were a step towards an interesting direction.
                                    I don't recall a brain parasite Charm ever. Wasp of the Labyrinth Trick, maybe?

                                    Overall, the Ink Monkeys era had a lot of Lunar Charms that, on their own, were pretty darn cool, but that didn't really fit into any cohesive idea of what the Lunar Exalted are, because Second Edition had trouble with that. There's definitely some I'm bringing back, but not all of them can be made to work with Ex3's vision of Lunars.

                                                            Originally posted by Epimetheus View Post
                                                            Do charms that automatically move a character up range bands automatically succeed at disengages or do you still have to roll it before you can use them?
                                                            The only time you need to disengage is when you're moving with the basic, reflexive "move action." Charms that just have you move without having to take an action, like Leaping Dodge Technique, don't require a disengage.
