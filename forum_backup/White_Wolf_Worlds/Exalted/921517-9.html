                        Originally posted by Kyman201 View Post

                        I loathe this "GM vs Players" mentality with a fiery-hot intensity. Absolutely detest it. It's part of why I've made statements about how if a ST lets you invest points of some kind in an artifact and evocations, and then destroys the artifact without any form of refund, that ST is a jerk who honestly shouldn't be running a game.

                        I'll just say that if a person looks at a rule and says "Oh GOOD, I can thoroughly dick over and destroy the hopes of my players with this", then the problem isn't the rule. No amount of rules finagling will keep that mindset from fucking the players over. They will find a way. There's a difference between toying around with Characters for the sake of drama, and ruining the fun of the PLAYERS. One of my big things for my players is "If they're not having fun, I'm not having fun".

                        So, about Warstriders with Intimacies... I wouldn't make EVERY Warstrider have them, but I like the idea of some Warstriders were built for some purpose. Maybe it'd be something like how with Black Wind, it unlocks its Evocations for someone who acts in a certain way. Maybe a Warstrider would grant Evocations to a pilot that upholds one of the Warstrider's Intimacies. I wouldn't go so far as to say ALL Striders should. But hell, personally? I'd like room for it. I'd like for Evocations, materials, and construction aesthetics to allow the Warstriders to be as varied as artifact weapons so far.

                        Like, technically Spring Razor and Beloved Adorei are both daiklaves. But their Evocations make them look and feel distinct from one-another. I'd like that with Warstriders. I'd like a system wherein one person can say that they stand in the chest cavity of a massive terracotta colossus inscribed with prayers to the Unconquered Sun, so that the Warstrider awakens and moves while in the sunlight, and another person can put together whirling gears, pounding pistons, hissing steam vents, and gets all Super Robot.
                        Very well stated. Also the reason I don't get into such arguments, because they are frankly ridiculous.

                        Everything you said from "Warstriders with Intimacies" down is perfect. Exactly what I want, as well. Warstriders are magic. They can be magitech, but they don't have to be. I love that.

                                                    Originally posted by LadyLens View Post
                                                    ...I consider it to be far too readily abused.
                                                    While I think it is important to realize how something might be abused or misused, I also think that it is important not to focus so much on trying to curb potential abuse that other aims are given less focus as a result.

                                                    So a game's rules would be better served by focusing on what happens if they are used as intended, and pointing out potential abuses or misuses to ensure they aren't done by accident, than they would be by focusing on trying not to be harder for someone to abuse or misuse, in my opinion. Especially in cases where trying to make the rules harder to abuse makes them less engaging to people not interested in abusing them - because nothing has quite the same degree of disappointment as knowing the reason something isn't as cool as I want it to be is because some other person might do something stupid with it (though I've noticed that the only example I can come up with right now that illustrates what I am talking about is an analogy about the medication that helps me through sinus infections, and how I have to drag myself to the drugstore multiple times while feeling awful and having my ability to drive safely impaired because the rules regarding purchase of medication are written not for me, but for those that want to misuse and abuse them - despite that the effect of the rules is not actually reducing misuse or abuse in any meaningful way).

                                                    Not so noble anymore.
