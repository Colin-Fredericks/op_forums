                        On Lunars and Heart's Blood, to try and articulate my incoherent notions on this, generally I'm not so keen on what feel like "sacrifice powers" like this, just in general. And that goes for Riding The Dragon and other such powers, as well as Heart's Blood.

                        I expect Exalted to often be violent - they are kung fu demigods and Princes of the Earth, and, unvarnished, that implies brutality. But the type of violence that interests me about them is the type that is driven by the same forces that cause people to use violence - to kill that enemy, to make that person afraid of you, to defeat the ruler crushing your land, to get rid of that girl pursuing a vendetta against you because you murdered her dad, just because you *can* and *want* to, etc.

                        So I think I'm a lot less interested in the sort of "You have to kill someone or something to gain access to this loosely related power" sort of Charm / magic violence that's removed from these normal human considerations and cause and effect on the use of violence.

                        Now that probably just means I will remain more interested in Solars and Dragonblooded and most of the Sidereals than types of characters (Lunars, Infernals?) where sacrifice and predation and violence either fuel or else are inescapable side effects of powers!

                                                          I don’t know if anyone is assumed to hate the Realm. But even if you have no negative exposure to them, your first meeting with the Wyld Hunt might convince you it’s in your best interests to oppose them! Failing that, the Silver Pact members you actually meet might convince you based on your own experiences!

                                                          Or maybe you don’t believe either of them, you go to the Realm expecting everything to be fine and get a Daiklaive in the face for your trouble, hahah.

                                                          Or maybe your character is smart and doesn’t do that but also doesn’t care about the Realm. That’s fine! They’re probably not participating in the Silver Pact, which doesn’t pressure you to participate once they give you the Moonsilver Tattoos.

                                                          All that said, I’m sure there’s plenty of Lunars who don’t necessarily have a horse in the race of hating the Realm, but through a desire to access the resources of Silver Pact members and or through pragmatism (because the Realm wants to kill them) they find themselves opposing the Realm.

                                                          Apparently there’s a narrative effect that might also provide them with an Essence fever like desire to oppose the Realm, but there’s a half-dozen practical responses to this stuff before you even take that into account!

                                                          EDIT: I can edit my post now, yay!

                                                          Anyway, this is all to say that the Lunar Exalted don't automatically hate the Realm. The practice of existing as one of the Lunar Exalted just strongly encourages an antagonistic relationship with the Realm, whether a character feels strongly about them or not. So if you really want to play the Lunar that doesn't hate the Realm, you definitely can! You can even participate in the Silver Pact and not hate the Realm! Your motto is probably just gonna wind up "Nothing personal."
