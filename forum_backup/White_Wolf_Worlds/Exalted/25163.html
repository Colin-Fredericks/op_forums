    What Lunar Charms do you expect will or will not return?

    12-07-2013, 12:25 PM
    I suspect that the space-time Charms of Second Edition will not reappear. That is, such reality-cheating shenanigans as Spreading the Wyld Roots, Moonlight Hunt, Stars Fall Through, Secure Den Prana (and all derivatives) and Blinking Eye of Chaos look to me like they will instead return as Getimian tricks resulting from using their personal, internal Looms of Fate to counter the designs of Heaven's Pattern Spiders. They not only overwrite their personal relationship with gravity (as teased) but potentially other natural laws as well -- space, time, momentum, friction, etc.

    In any case, nothing about "Half-Beast-God Trickster-Witch-Shaman" suggests space-time manipulation to me. I think the "intensely physical" Lunar Exalted will etch their legends into the world with "bestial, inhuman" feats of strength and speed. If those feats are inferior to those of the Solar Exalted, that's nothing we haven't been told before. You don't play a Lunar to be the strongest or fastest hero in the universe, you play to be a hero who is Half-Beast-God.

    As to what tricks the Lunars may have for dealing with the Wyld, I can't say until I know what form the Wyld takes, now that it is no longer a "lava-lamp with infinite numbers of fairies in it".


    We have been told to not expect "illusions", but rather a trend of Lunars "changing themselves to change the world". So I'm pretty sure we're not ever going to see a Lunar mirror of Phantom-Conjuring Performance, and also that all disguise-magic will involve physically reshaping the Lunar's appearance rather than layering over false images and sounds. I suspect that Lunars may cause people to see things which are not there through inflicting delusional or hallucinatory insanity rather than fooling the eyes with tricks of light.

    To that end, I don't expect to see Inexplicable Lunar Wonders, River Reflects Desire, Dreams and Expectations Stance, Shifting Penumbra Stance, One Of The Herd, Clover Can't Be Found, the second half of The Spider's Trapdoor, Treasure as Trash Misdirection, or any of those Butterfly Eyes Charms.


    To be clear, I don't think they were talking about all forms of the Illusion Keyword, since some of those were more about beliefs inflicted through lies and misdirections rather than through sensory illusions. So I still expect to see Perfection of the Mockingbird, Mockingbird Persona Mimicry, Scent of All Nations Attitude, and Falling Leaf Distraction.

    False Burrow Pursuit, Mirror Sight Dismay and Lost Mirror Flight can also make returns so long as the Lunar has to actually speak to his victim with a social action. Oh, and let's not forget brutal traumatizing through Mind-Blanking Terror Technique.

    I also think we can still look forward to sleights of hand, as with Face Of The Moon Concealment, School Becomes Shark Formation, School In The Reeds Technique, Thieving Magpie Prana, the first half of The Spider's Trapdoor, and possibly Rats In The Basement.


    I think, with the greater emphasis on one's Totem as an inescapable starting point for every Lunar Exalted character, Wasp of the Labyrinth Trick might not necessarily always work by temporarily giving you an egg-implanting stinger. It doesn't quite fit with any but an extremely small selection of Totems, whereas a necromantic entry Charm like this ought to be fairly popular among the Lunar Exalted who want to explore the Trickster-Witch-Shaman part of their Essence.

    On the opposite side of the death/life line of "barbaric magic", given the hints of greater connections to Gaia, we might be seeing the return of Moon And Earth Song, the Charm which blesses a territory with great fertility.


    I wonder, though, if the territory claiming and enchanting Charms will be Essence 5 or even 6+ effects, reflecting that Essence 6+ characters are generally supposed to be sedentary while Essence 5- characters are geared more towards adventuring across the world on a moment's notice.

                  Do you think Monkey Arm Style will return?

                  The hatewheel has expressed contempt for and exasperation with "elastic body" powers, but the hamster has said that something like the elastic body Shintai from Kindred of the East is not out of the question (though he didn't say for which splats).

                  Monkey Arm Style does seem problematic for me in regards to the Lunar Exalted. The Alchemical Exalted have an excellent reason for an extending arm feature to NOT include the option to extend one's other appendages or body parts, which allows for it to be a properly self-contained Charm, with an entirely separate cousin Charm that extends the Alchemical's legs. Easy to design, balance and write.

                  But unlike the Alchemical Exalted, the Lunar Exalted can easily switch back and forth between forms that technically don't have any arms at all, or at least where their arms would be legs or wings. It's also harder (though admittedly not impossible) to justify why a Lunar Charm which extends an arm (which could be a foreleg) doesn't also extend the Lunar's legs. And a Charm that allows an Exalt to extend both arms and legs is an extremely complicated mess to design, balance and write.


                  What I'm getting at is, I think the Lunar Exalted may already be so variable in form that giving them "elastic body" Charms might not actually over-complicate things. Whereas Exalts who don't have the innate power to easily assume any inhuman form can freely accept such tricks into their arsenal (especially the Liminal and Alchemical Exalted).

                                  For me, I believe that "spirit shape" refers to the basic animal form that you can assume from the start, whereas the Totem is the entire image that your character has built around that shape.

                                  As an example, Silver Python's Spirit Shape might be a normal-sized python, but his/her Totem is the gigantic, world-spanning serpent that embodies strength, wisdom, patience and flexibility.

                                  So I guess "Totem" is basically the combination of your Spirit Shape and Caste, with the Favored Attribute and any Totemic Keywords added in.
