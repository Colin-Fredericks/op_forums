                            Originally posted by Sunder the Gold View Post
                            Is there any helpful rule of thumb or thought process behind "How Much" an Artifact's Evocations should be capable of enhancing an Exalt's native Charms?
                            My process was to figure out the basic themes and concept of the artifact, figure out what Abilities it would want to interact with, and then scan through their Charms looking for places where I could write neat upgrades. There's no hard-and-fast methodology beyond that.
