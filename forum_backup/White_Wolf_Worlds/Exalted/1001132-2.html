                        IT'S ALLLIIIIIIIIIIIIVEEEEEEEEEEEEEE

                        -ahem-


                        So, "force vs words" it is, with a slight hint of "call the militia", seasoned to taste.

                        .....

                        Frowning at the display, you look to Saiko, who is watching the spectacle with a grimace.

                        "Quickly, girl, mount Xuansu and go find a militia patrol. We passed one back at the last plaza. Tell them to come here with all haste."

                        She looks at you, wide-eyed, "Wh- what are you going to do, Lord"?

                        You stride forward," I am going to make sure the priest doesn't kill the woman, for a start.. The militia will break up any quarrelsome townsfolk. I need them here in case this gets violent."

                        She hesitates, " Don't you want me here, in that case?"

                        "No, having the militia here would make the odds more even, as opposed to two versus fifty. Quickly, Saiko!". With that, she hauls herself awkwardly up into the saddle and sets off back down the road. You continue towards the crowd, cursing under your breath.

                        She headed off none too soon. The crowd, agitated to a fever pitch by the tirade of the priest and the cries of the puppeteer, are milling about anxiously, with some of the angrier townsfolk picking up chunks of firewood and pushing towards the two figures in the center. You elbow your way through the gathered bodies, some people starting to complain, but silenced by your wrathful expression and the sword at your waist (more likely the latter). The Immaculate Monk has worked himself into a full-blown fury, all-but frothing at the mouth, bending down to scream at the woman, inches away from her face. She sobs in response, until the monk rears back and drives the heel of his foot full into her face, breaking her nose with a crack that can be heard even over the angry shouts of the crowd. Stunned, she drops back into the dust, blood pulsing from her face, and the monk turns to address the crowd, which had been stunned into silence by the sudden brutality of the blow. You take the opportunity to push into the open center of the crowd which closes in behind you, sealing off all retreat, and raise yourself up to your full height.

                        "Tell me, preacher, is this how we bring new followers to the faith? By beating them bloody in the marketplace for minor transgressions? Now, I might not be a monk, or even truly a devout follower of the Immaculate faith, but to me it seems like there must be a much better way", you say, in a voice that is calm and level, with only the set of your jaw and the fire in your eyes revealing your inner feelings.

                        "Pah, merely educating this wretch in the proper displays of faith! She had the nerve to display the figure of one of the Most Enlighted Dragonblooded and the Anathema in public, for money!" The Monk rants, lifting his foot to kick again. Quickly, you place yourself in between the monk and the woman, preventing his kick from connecting.

                        "It was a godsdamned kids show, you crazy bastard!' replies the woman, voice hoarse with pain, who has sat up and is attempting (and failing) to stem the tide of blood from her nose.

                        You spread out your hands, indicating the crowd and the mess caused by the monks "education". " Surely, Enlightened Father, this mild transgression did not warrant quite this level of a response? A discussion of the tenets of the faith, or even a penance? Not a beating. This is not the conduct of a monk. Are you not supposed to be a man of peace, of progressive enlightenment and meditation?"

                        The firebrand snorts, " The Immaculate Faith is beset by foes on all sides. Anathema, the Hundred Gods Heresy, the degradation of moral and spiritual values." He turns to address the crowd ," Because of the focus on meditation, peaceful and quiet enlightenment, that the faith has been allowed to fall for so long! We are as mice, and we must be as LIONS! We must root out any threat to the faith, wherever it may hide, with fire and sword! FIRE AND SWORD!" He turns to address you,

                        " So, no, Oathsworn, I am not a man of peace, not when my faith, the one true one, needs, no, demands,, my righteous anger! For too long has this filth, this HERESY, been tolerated! Words and chants can only be so effective, only for so long, before we mortals seek to subvert any messages the faith tries to purport. Look! Look at what this woman has done! She has reduced the figure, the very purpose, of our Dragon-Blooded saviors to a public mockery, for children and prostitutes to gawk at and throw coins! Such ignorance, such disrespect, cannot go uncorrected!"

                        He breathes, for seemingly the first time of his rant, and gestures at the sword at your waist ," Besides, complaining to me of 'peace" is awfully ignorant of your purpose, Oathsworn. Were you not pledged to be a defender of the faith, to uphold the tenets of the Immaculate faith and to live your life by its creed? By those standards, by the Oath you swore, you are a sword, you are of violence, and you should be up here assisting me in administering righteous punishment for this disgusting act of profligacy!",

                        You frown," Do not presume, monk, to speak to me of my Oaths. I swore them, not you, and I understand their demands with much greater clarity than you do." You bend down, pulling a swatch of cloth from your belt and passing it to the woman, an impromptu handkerchief. " Yes, I swore to defend and uphold the Immaculate faith ...... but I also swore to defend the weak, help the helpless, and uphold the small. I am doing that now. Some Oaths take precedence. And, yet again, this woman was just trying to earn a few coins for her daily bread. Such a response is, yet again and as always, completely unnecessary".

                        At the mention of money, the monk sneers. " Ah, yes. Money. The root of all evil! Every mortal is in pursuit of wealth of some kind, be it gold, food or pleasure! This desire, this LUST, distracts us from realizing the true purpose of our existence! We MORTALS, BLINDED and WRITHING in the DUNG OF OUR OWN MORTALITY, cannot see that life is pain, is suffering, and it is only through realizing the fragility of our existence that we become Enlightened. If we had no ....." He trails off and stares at you, as if noticing you for the first time. He raises his hand, fingers trembling, to point at your face, the clear indicators of your ancestry. With a shaking voice, he shouts to the crowd ;

                        " Anathema!"

                        .........

                        "...... what."

                        The bemused statement is all you can muster. You have heard some insults in your time amidst the Lowlanders, but nobody has had the stones to directly compare you to a demon before. At least, not to your face, or in public.

                        The firebrand continues to point, distracted entirely from his earlier point of hatred. He backs away, head twisting to address the crowd, who have backed away in fear, confusion, or both.

                        " You carry the mark of the Anathema, the ones that come from beyond the boundaries of the world! The Corruption, the Twister, that which turns the mind and body away from the Dragons and to the purposes of evil! " He makes the sign against the evil eye, twisting his finger beside his temple in that age-old symbol. " You cannot be trusted! Your words drip venom, poison, and you seek to turn me against the Faith, against the Dragons! You try to draw me to the bosom of the Anathema! I reject you! I reject you and your lies!" He spits on the ground.

                        This is ...... almost funny, in some deeply ironic fashion. You hook your hands under your sash, exhale in a loud sigh, then start to talk to the monk," Listen, preacher, this is all well and good, and you can speak to the crowd of the futility of existence at your leisure, but I will not let you beat on this woman any lon..."

                        He spits in your face.

                        Mind blank, you blink once, twice, three times.

                        It feels like the world has come to a stop, then sudden fire burns through your body, and you rip your sword from its sheath and sweep it aloft, all form and composure lost in sudden, blinding fury. The surrounding crowd scrambles back, throwing themselves out of range of the blow. Some people scream at the impending bloodshed.

                        "What the everloving FUCK is going on here!"

                        The shout, resounding off the surrounding buildings, cuts through the rage clouding your mind, and is the only thing that prevents you from sweeping your blade down and wiping that smug look off the monks face. Your entire body trembles, withholding the force and desire to cut. The monk smirks, either unmindful or unaware of his close brush with reincarnation.

                        "Look, see how the Anathema-spawns true nature shows itself, prevented from cutting down a holy man of the faith by the command of the terrestrial authority!. It is only barely in control of its bestial desires!" He turns to address the crowd. " This is why, people, we must be willing to combat evil, whatever it's form! This spawn of evil is allowed to walk among us true, honest folk, and subvert us with it's lies!" He turns, as the crowd is broken up by burly men with spears and clubs, who shove and push aside the gathered onlookers. The sergeant in command of the patrol strides forward, along with Saiko leading Xuansu.

                        The sergeant bawls again, "Somebody better start talking, or by the gods I'll haul everyone here to gaol!" Saiko steps forwards and takes ahold of your arms, whispering in your ear.

                        "Lord Kasai, please lower the sword and talk to the nice militia officer." You snarl in response, lips pulled back from the teeth and eyes fixated on the monk. If you could start a fire with your glare, the monk would be on fire, hot enough to crack the flagstones of the street.

                        "Kasai", she pleads, finally pulling your eyes away from the monk. After a moment of eye contact, you suddenly relax, dropping your sword to your side, drained of all energy. All of your muscles are suddenly stiff and sore, and you realize you have been tense like a drawn bow for the last few seconds. You pant, barely able to remain standing, much less answer the sergeant.

                        " I was putting on a show, when this monk attacked me for "heresy". He started beating the crap outta me, and only the intervention of the Oathsworn here prevented me from getting seriously injured." the woman interjects. " Well, more seriously injured." She says, muffled by the kerchief. " Everything was going as smooth as silk up until the monk spit in his face. Regardless, if it weren't for his Lordship, here." She bows painfully. " I probably would have been dead."

                        The sergeant huffs," You got a permit for that stand?"

                        She stares at him flatly, ":....... nope."

                        The sergeant sighs and rolls his eyes," Normally, I'd collar you, but in this case I think you learned enough." She acquiesces, starting to pick up the remnants of her overturned and broken stand.. With that, the sergeant turns to the monk, still standing there with a look of smug self-satisfaction on his face. Said look is suddenly wiped away by the blunt end of a baton being driven into his stomach.

                        "That is for assaulting two different people, as well as almost causing a riot".

                        "I .....-wheeze- I was preaching!" complains the monk from the ground.

                        " Preaching is acceptable. Causing a public disturbance, interrupting the flow of traffic, and almost causing a riot is not." He jerks his chin at two of his men. " Boys, bring this rabble-rouser down to the Temple, tell the abbot what happened. If he wants a written report with my signature, he can pick it up tomorrow morning."

                        The sergeant turns to the still-sizable crowd. " What the hell are you are all standing around for?! Show's over, go back to your own damn business!" With that, he turns back to you, where Saiko is busy wiping the spittle from your face. Much more respectfully, " My lord, do you mind coming down to the gaol, in order to give a statement?"

                        You shrug. You just want this over and done with, so you can find a hostel and a bath, some food, and a bed. Suddenly bone-tired, weary beyond all measure, you allow Saiko to lead you, mounted on Xuansu, down to the gaol. Giving a description of the events and getting them transcribed take a couple of hours, and it is well-past sundown when you finally make it to the hostel (well-recommended by the sergeant, and you are pretty sure you got a reduced rate as a result, by far the only good thing to happen today.) There, after Xuansu is stabled, food is brought and a bath is drawn, you are relaxing in your rented room when there is a knock at the door. Saiko answers it, to see one of the hostel staff. They explain that they have a visitor asking for you downstairs, and if you would like for them to be sent up. You agree, and a few moments later, the woman from earlier, looking rather worse for wear, comes up.

                        She enters the room, then slowly and painfully, bows formally, forehead to the floorboards, waiting for your permission to rise. At your gesture, she kneels, then ,

                        " I just came by to thank your lordship for helping me. If it weren't for you, I would be injured far worse, or even dead". You acknowledge this with a nod.

                        "....... and, if I may, without being too forward, I would like to ask your lordship for a boon."

                        You sigh. " What is it?"

                        She hesitates, then, " I wish to follow you, serve as your retainer."

                        "....Why?"

                        " Well, you are the first figure of authority I've met that has actually tried to do right by me. From my sergeants and commanders in the army, to the militia here in town, everyone so far has been out for themselves first. Follow the 'letter of the law" first, and the actual people second. Meeting someone that actually sticks to their word is ...... impressive, if that makes sense. Much less someone that sticks to their word, even then they are inconvenienced by it." Again, she hesitates. " And ..... to be honest, I don't exactly have anything else to do or anywhere to go. After I got discharged from the army, I got stuck here and I've been slowly but surely getting hungrier and poorer ever since. Getting my face fixed up used up all the money I had left, so I will be left to begging if I stay in town.

                        "You were in the army?"

                        She nods," I was a crossbowman, under Lord Iysen. With her daughter getting married to another noble, she suddenly didn't need so many soldiers. I got mustered out with nothing more than 3 months of levy-pay and the clothes I had on my back. That money disappeared pretty quickly, and I have been reduced to odd-jobs and street-work."

                        She sighs, then, " I can fight, not that your follower here can't, but I actually saw battle, "in the shit" as we called it, and I can use the backsword and the crossbow equally well. Actually won the company marksman contest 3 weeks running, and take a rabbit on the run at 50 paces. I can march for days with a load on my back, set up and tear down camp, as well as care for horses and armor. And." She admits," Not to make it seem like I am trying to bribe you, your lordship, but if you take me, I've got a little nugget of information I found out a couple of weeks ago that you might find interesting, help you climb the social ladder as it were. If you take me, I'll tell you."

                        You ponder the decision, resting your cheek on your fist. On the one hand, building up a retinue of loyal followers and retainers is part of an Oathsworn's success. From men-at-arms, to grooms and valet's, to pages and such, having a retinue would mean you would have to spend less time on "preparing" to do something, (caring for Xuansu, maintaining arms and armor, writing letters, buying provisions, carrying things, etc), and more time actually "doing" things, such as performing great deeds and getting your name known. On the other hand ........ keeping a retinue is expensive. In just food cost alone, your expenses have doubled since picking up Saiko. Now, if you were to take this woman as your retainer, she would need arms and armor, equipment of her own. The only reason you could afford to take Saiko along with you is she could kinda-sorta fit/use some of the Oathsworn-bandit's gear. And you don't really know this woman from a person in the street.

                        On the third hand, between the gear either you or Saiko couldn't use, and the possibility of winning something in the tournament in a couple of days, you could be set for a while, money-wise. Enough to start building up a reputation and a retinue, which is the separation between being a "swallow-sworn" and a "proper" Oathsworn warrior.

                        You straighten," Before I come to a decision, I need to know your name. Calling Saiko "girl" is starting to wear thin, and I don't think I could go through that again" Saiko snickered in response.

                        She nods," My name is Blue Pines, your lordship".

                        You nod, then:

                        1) Accept her oath of service. Why not? Besides, her information could be useful.
                        2) Deny her. You don't "need" another retainer, not now
                        3) Tell her to come talk to you after the tournament. This is a big decision to make on such a short notice.
                        4) Something else?

                        OOC:

                        Technically, if Kasai felt he was sufficiently disrespected or offended (and he was, by the end), he could have turned to any of his hecklers and cut them down. He wouldn't have gotten in any particular trouble for it, legally or socially, (more of a wag of the finger), as long as he was insulted before getting all stabby. Technically, if the two parties are alike in social rank, there is supposed to be a dueling process that takes place, with formal challenges, meeting places, and conditions, but if some peasant in the street got uppity, he could respond via sword to the tender bits. But, as we have seen, Kasai tends to ignore most insults, so long as they aren't blatant. Getting spat on in the face was very blatant, and the monk could get in some pretty severe trouble for it.

                        The crowd is complicated. On one hand, they were definitely not in favor of the monk beating the ever-loving shit out of Blue Pines, even the Immaculate-fearing townsfolk. On the other hand, they were not too fond of Kasai, being a Wyld mutant-descendant and all. On the other-other hand, Kasai made a good impression on many of the rubberneckers, stepping forwards to help Ten-Pines like that. Oathsworn that actually uphold their Oaths to defend the smallfolk are ...... rare. Think "Dunk and Egg" by GRRM, if anybody is familiar with that.
