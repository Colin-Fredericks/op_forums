                        Originally posted by Sunder the Gold View Post
                        His technique can only be unrefined if he and his opponents are operating on the same basic principles, and Guts simply has fewer dots in the shared Ability, or fewer Techniques.
                        I wouldn't call melee so much unrefined as...uncodified maybe? Somebody who has melee 5 and a specialty has certainly refined their ability to use a sword, but it's different from Single Point or Steel Devil. The guy who's gotten melee 5 has got there through intuition and experience, and training in it teaches more principles than specific techniques. Like you might learn about lines of attack or something.

                        It differs from Martial Arts in that MA is almost always about unarmed combat as well as weapons, with currently 23 publishes martial arts and 3 don't use unarmed attacks. It also differs because MA tend to have somewhat discreet techniques and accompanying philosophy. A brawler wouldn't lean forward and kick over their own back while saying "Quickly Strikes the Scorpion!" or roll their body with a blow in a really specific way, taught as Ocean Rolls with Tide technique.

                        In the real world that kind of thinking probably only really helps in training your body to move a certain way, and the philosophy behind emulating the ocean, or a tiger, or whatever is ultimately not going to be as good at fighting as somebody who's just made a martial art for pure combat. In Exalted this is not the case. In Exalted you really do gain power from emulating an eagle. Mechanically this doesn't do much though, except let you block lethal without a stunt, which in universe is actually pretty badass, being able to block a sword blade or spray of needles with your bare hand.


                        Again, this isn't how the real world works, but it's how Exalted works. European knife fighting could be refined and defined in the real world as a martial art, but in Exalted it would just be melee, because it doesn't act like a fantastical mystical martial art.
