                    Hungry scream of the blood-thirsty missile

                    The sorcerer speaks the word of prey tracking and sends her mind towards the missiles and ranged weapons of her and her companions, up to Size (occult) people. The ranged weapons and munitions began to let out a screeching noise and glow red, obviating stealth. From now on, any and all ranged weapons used are considered to be enhanced by the spell.

                    The spell makes the arrows personally track down and search for prey to destroy, allowing them to ignore cover/ environmental penalties, and to add the sorcerer's occult score in terms of successes/ dice to hit the prey. As they scream in triumph and rage through the atmosphere, they leave behind a crimson trail connecting the weapon and the target.

                                Conjuring the gaze of the Unconquered Sun

                                Sorcery can never affect the Heavens. Those are beneath the purview of the Incarnae. But this doesn't meant that they cannot imitate a small fraction of their power.

                                The sorcerer chants the word the rooster sings as the sun appears over the horizon each day. And within the palm of her hand, appears a miniature sun. Then she throws it into the air, and it floats there, the light penetrating all solid materials up to long range. Within that range, it is as if that portion of the world is exposed to the noonday sun, along with all its penalties. Servants of darkness suffer, while ghosts and beings of the underworld begin to dissipate.
