    Some Deathlord Ideas for Storytellers

    01-17-2018, 05:58 AM
    Been thinking about death a bit lately... IN EXALTED! DEATH IN EXALTED (figured a need to clarify there!) and did some research. That led me to think of some ideas for Deathlords based on a lack of one in the following locations (the other directions have at least one Deathlord influencing the area in some way).
    Put simply there is NO Deathlord in:
    • In Creation's South - specifically the Southern Corners (East and West). Life doesn't flourish as much down here, due to the harsh climate, but death is certainly present!
      • The South Underworld IS inhabited by the First and Forsaken Lion, and Princess Magnificent - but in positions of exile; unable to easily enter Creation).
    • On the Blessed Isle; understandably due to their high Dragon Blood Population. However, an arrogant Deathlord could chose this as a perfect hiding spot to manipulate elements from behind the scenes; maybe even stiring and encouraging the infighting of the Noble Houses?
    • In Yu-Shan; which is even LESS likely than a Deathlord on the Blessed Isle. Yet again, if one could hide here, they could have a means to manipulate the Loom of Fate, or even work their way through the Celestial Bureaucracy and (in the worst case scenario) win the Games of Divinity for dire consequences!!
    • In Malfeas; which is further unlikely as there is little influence here for one to affect Creation. Its more likely they would exist in Underworld Banishment (like the First and Forsaken Lion, and Princess Magnificent), but maybe there is a plot to turn a Yozi into a new Neverborn - maybe its a loophole in the prison; Yozi's stay, but Neverborn must leave?
    • ​In Autochron; which is divide from creation. Autochron himself is verging on becoming a Neverborn due to his failings, which could result in a Deathlord appearing FROM him. Maybe the hidden 4 are not present, but rather have been "Deathlords Prophesied to Appear?"

    Also another thought - while the Liminal Exalted are the result of failed attempts on one of the laws of Magic (the dead do not live again), they are Terrestrial Exalted. As the Abyssals are the result of Corrupted Solar Essences, could a Liminal-Equivilent be made by attempting to create "Death-Blooded Exalted?" - not Ghost-Blooded, but dark Dragon-Blood Equivilents?

    Maybe the "Dark Mother/ Maiden" that the Liminals link themselves to is a Deathlord? Maybe a subtle design created by Princess Magnificent that would go unnoticed by the Neverborn as an escape plan?

    I even had a nice pun for a corrupted "Jade" equivilent - "Deadwood". Grows in the Underworld, solid like iron-bark or red-wood, made stronger through arificing (maybe lackered with the Essence/ Ecto-plasam of Ghosts?)

    Food for thought anyway....

                  I think you are trying to bring 2nd Edition Deathlord lore into 3e where it doesn't need to be, nor is desirable. The idea of all the Deathlords having malign interests in Creation isn't really a good one. It leads to the existential doom syndrome that plagued 2e writing, where all of Creation needs to unite to defeat a million threats to the entire world.

                  ​3e has even unshackled itself from the idea that the Deathlords are Usurpation-killed Solars hopped up on dead Yozi souls. Now, the Deathlords can just be incredibly powerful and ancient ghosts of any stripe, some with absolutely no desire to invade Creation in any way and which are perfectly happy to play out their melodramas and intrigues in Underworld kingdoms.

                          In general I agree with the OP's point that Deathlords and their servants should be things encounterable in the world, and the lack of one with interests in the South does feel like a real lack (especially now that the Southeast is a real place). However, I agree strongly with Kyeudo that they shouldn't all be obviously evil/have necessarily malign interests. 1e Silver Prince, for example, makes a good example of a creepy-but-not-Skeletor dude who makes the area around him more interesting and is an entity with whom player characters can have a variety of interactions: he's got a set of goals that don't necessarily support or contradict PC goals, and therefore might be someone they work for, against, or alongside (or all three at different times!).

                          Also this is tangential but I love Eye and Seven Despairs and his utter dedication to being completely ineffectual at anything that isn't "furthering his personal angst." He's grandly petty, if that makes any sense, in the best tradition of gothic melodrama.

                                  One thing I like about the Deathlords (and many others things put out by Onyxpath/white wolf) is the fact that we have a lot of examples of them and how they differ from one another and how they are alike. Since we have those examples but there are several Deathlords left completely unexplained leaving us with plenty of creative potential. For example one of my players is a reincarnation of Brigid/Salanin. He is a sorcerer of course and has a lot of talent for it but has strong negative opinions against Necromancy. That led me to create a custom Deathlord who goes by the title of The Black Crone who was the previous incarnation of that exaltation. I've used them now as an antagonist once and their powerful connection to magic wielding powerful sorcery and necromancy casually has managed to tempt one of the other players to bargains and seeding the fun risk of serious party conflict in the future. I look forward to seeing the fallout of the Zenith dumping that potion into the well of a southern city. It should be beautifully dramatic. My point is a lot of the areas are left "untouched" by Deathlords so that the ST can make the decision to use whatever antagonists they desire, and the undefined Deathlords are rich for creative opportunities.

                                      Let's see what 2e had as doomsday plans:

                                      The First and Forsaken Lion has an army so large that it can readily conquer the whole of the South but the Lion can't find a shadowland big enough to use as a beachhead. He's got a soulsteel version of those First Age Independence Day doom beams under construction. He'll probably slaughter Gem to create that beachhead.

                                      Bodhisattva Anointed By Dark Water is building the fleet of doom on Isle 4 and will use it to conquer the oceans.

                                      The Bishop of the Chalcedony Thurible is distilling a nihilistic doctrine that will cause anyone who hears it to commit suicide and plunge their ghost into Oblivion.

                                      The Lover Clad In the Raiment of Tears is about to destroy Lookshy using a doomsday device built into a soulsteel warstrider, destabilizing the power balance of the Eastern Threshold.

                                      The Mask of Winters has the single largest army in the Scavenger Lands, kept from moving on any of its neighbors by the military might of Lookshy.
