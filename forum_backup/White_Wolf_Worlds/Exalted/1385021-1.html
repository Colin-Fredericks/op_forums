                                                            Originally posted by Caffeine Delusions View Post
                                                            Totally legit point. My best guess would be that you could model something similar using the mechanics for Intimacies, specifically principles. Charms that require a given number of Virtue-based intimacies at a given intensity as prerequisites... which could open the door to Eternal exalts who have to be very deliberate managing their beliefs and values, because they could actually find themselves cut off from accessing powers they know because they no longer meet the prerequisites to use them (or, as such a condition is something we haven't seen in Third Edition's rule model as yet, you could bake the modality of that powerdown into the charms, so that an Eternal still has access to SOME of that power even if they don't maintain the practice of those 'virtues').
                                                            This is actually quite similar to what I had been thinking for a 3rd ed port, but it would be by no means a quick fix. As you mentioned, there will always be situations where none of your Intimacies apply to a given situation, whereas the Virtues are so broadly applicable that its legitimately hard to think of a situation where NONE of them apply. That said, I think 3rd ed could do with a quick and dirty term for the "point value" of sentimentality: you add up all the Intimacies that support the action (1st tiers are one point, second are 2, third are 3), subtract any Intimacies that oppose the action, and what you're left with is your "Emotional Investment Value" or some such. The whole point of Eternals was that they were passion-powered, so whereas 2nd ed models that with Virtues it would be interesting to use Intimacies in 3rd ed.
