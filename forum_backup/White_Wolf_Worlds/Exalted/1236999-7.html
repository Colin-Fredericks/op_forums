                                A thought on stipends:

                                Metrics aren't exactly provided, but the general byline on stipends appears to consistently be "stipend alone can barely fund the lavishness of Dynastic lifestyle, and living more comfortably and with a capacity to save for the future depends on an income, payments or loans from your peers, or both". Stipend is referred to as increasing with marriage and having children, but the byline would seem to imply that it barely covers expenses.

                                There's a question on how consistent those expenses are, given Dynastic social values. Consider: everybody wants the best for their child: the best tutors, places in the best schools, greasing the wheels for acceptance into the Big Four somewhere down the line.

                                The best for their child is in limited supply: I would find a degree of bidding war or similar to be a regular consideration, and would think that the "covers the bare minimum" stipend is not the best thing in the world for outbidding somebody else that has their eye on the same thing that you do. For a family with more than one child at a time, it raises questions of whether or not one of them is relegated to the cheaper option (with the idea that one has taken on such a burden in the first place and failed to live up to it, as well as the idea of having wilfully let one's child go without, being ripe fodder for social embarrassment; a similar thing probably goes if one's recourse is to seek a loan).

                                Mind, even in the default case, something to relieve the financial burden of child-rearing is probably for one of the parent's to go off on a pilgrimage or adventure or similar socially acceptable means of living frugally, relieving the costs of their own domestic lifestyle, and maybe occasionally earning a bit of extra money to send home.

                                Either way, it does seem like the kind of thing that would present enough of a headache for a lot of couples that are already dealing with one child to not want to take in another. Hence, it's a convenient recourse for the matriarch to have basically childless couples to give responsibility for a Dynastic orphan.
