                                        I can't believe I didn't think of this sooner.

                                        One computer game that really captures the feel of Exalted, to me, is Divinity II: Ego Draconis (also known as the Dragon Knight Saga). In it, you play an elite soldier, and on your first mission, are unwillingly imbued with the essence and power of your order's sworn enemies; the Dragons. Throughout the game, you have to struggle against both a hostile world, and your own former comrades, who would all see you killed for a crime that was committed before you were born. You are guided by the ghost of the woman whose draconic magic you inherited, on no lesser a quest than to destroy the incarnate god of evil, and redeem the tarnished name of dragonkind.

                                        In Exalted terms, you are a mortal Wyld Huntsman, who gets the Exaltation of the Lunar your leaders killed, along with the most badass spirit shape ever, and determines to redeem the name of the Celestials, while bringing down the Infernal overlord who turned Creation's defenders against each other.
