        See, the thing is, how exactly Yasal crystals work is worded extremely vaguely. The exact phrase is

        whoever touches the stone can use a captive spirit’s powers as if they were his own
        "As if they were his own", taken literally, would require the wielder to pay all the costs. Vance has advised against this reading, as it makes the crystals near useless to mortals, which is fair.

        But if we're not taking that in a literal mechanical sense, what about "Powers"? We're currently assuming that means all the captive spirit's charms, but does it have to? What if we interpret "powers" to be only the magical environmental effects the spirit is capable of producing? That would mean no principle of motion and other such charms. It would be as if the spirit is in the rock using the charms themselves on command.

        That, I feel, is more in line with the intended effect, and a lot less broken.
