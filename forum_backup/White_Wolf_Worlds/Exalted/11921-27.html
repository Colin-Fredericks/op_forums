    An Infernal Caste destroys roughly a fourth of the blessed isle in an instant, sending it falling into the Wyld. At first, all the powers of the world try to kill him, but the Infernal is too fast and skilled to be killed by even the greatest solar assassins. He claims to be perfectly capable of destroying all of Creation, and threatens to do so at the end of Calibration. However, Chejop Kajak seeks him out to learn what he wants. The Infernal strikes a deal with him: He will be allowed to teach a very specific class of incredibly weak Dragon-Blooded, if that entire class is allowed to try and murder him throughout their lessons. In exchange, the Infernal will hold off on destroying the world until the next calibration.

    So the race is on: one class of dragon-blooded. Their goal: kill their teacher before Calibration.

                The Reclamation has begun with a full-on strike on the Blessed Isle. Thanks to a powerful First Age defense system, the Isle is defended from the majority of the demon armies by a large force field, but numerous powerful 3rd Circle demons and their minions managed to reach the Isle before the shield activated. With their own armies faltering against the might of Malfeas, the Realm deploys their secret weapon: a team of 100 of the greatest Dragon Blooded warriors from across creation. Working as a team, the Dragon Blooded are able to use special Essence projectors to turn their collective power into large Essence-weapons to combat the Yozi armies. Now only they can stop the armies of the Reclamation before they reach the Imperial Manse and destroy the shield protecting the Blessed Isle from complete destruction. Among this special team include a headstrong Fire-Aspect martial artist who is commanding the team for the first time, a smooth talking Water-Aspect swordsman and a portly Woods Aspect with incredible Archery skills.

                        A young Sidereal, watching the Loom of Fate, falls in love from afar with a young man destined for Exaltation, though she knows he is destined for another. Try as she might, the whispers of Isidoros beckon to her, to take what she wants, and damn Fate and what it says.

                        Knowing what awaits her if she says yes, but unable to bring herself to say no, she splinters her Fate, surrendering to the Black Boar, becoming one of his akuma, and tearing the Loom of Fate asunder to pull everything she can to bow before her. Demons and undead alike march to her will, along with a proud Elemental Dragon and a wicked Raksha, both kept tightly enthralled through sorcery.

                        Normally, this might be overkill in the pursuit of just one young fresh Exalt. But not only does the object of her desire...well...object, the foreordained lover of her obsession is herself a puissant Eclipse with a loyal army at her beck and call. And then there's the Isidoros's own agenda; when the Sidereal's actions tear the fabric of Creation asunder such that time itself frays, she may find that the heroes across this Age and the one before gather to stop her...

                                                During the First Age, a Twilight summoned a host of spirits, both elemental and celestial, to bind them in an enchanted book.
                                                Many years after the Usurpation, his Shard's next incarnation- a young girl- discovers the book and inadvertently lets the spirits free. Confused and maddened by the time that has passed, they begin to wreak havoc on the unfamiliar world.
                                                The book's guardian spirit charges the girl with finding the escaped spirits and returning them to him. It is a mighty task, but she is not alone- a fledgling Chosen of Serenity, an amnesiac avatar of Luna, and a mystic No Moon (who happens to be related to her prior incarnation) are guided by fate to the girl, and become caught up in her quest.

                                                        All Hell has broken loose. A rite of Sorcery meant to merge the Underworld and the living one via sacrificing the Mouth of Piece has gone horribly wrong, ripping a giant portal to Malfeas in the sky. Thankfully for Creation, the sole witness and survivor of the rite, seems to have a smaller portal embedded in their very hand and soul, allowing them to control and treat the rift and all of its smaller cousins. After barely managing to stabilize the portal with the help of a devout but disaffected agent of the All-Seeing Eye, a Night Caste storyteller and irritant of the former, and a strange old New Moon who believes that all three worlds should live in piece with one another, said witness is made the field leader of a reborn Wild Hunt, using their fame as the so-called Blessed of the Five to happily ignore the grumblings of the remaining Immaculates angry about having to assist an apparent Anathema. Their mission? To find the mysterious sorcerer responsible for the Rift, and possibly end a giant civil war between God-Bloods and a faction of the Wyld Hunt corrupted by a powerful and darkly natured Magical Material before the mysterious malefactor bends the remnants of each side to his will. Along the way, the Blessed picks up, among other people, an idealistic Dragon-Blooded dynast sick of the Realm's politics, a confused demon but good-natured who used to think he was a ghost, and a boisterious Varangian beastman caught between loyalty to his nation and to his corps of mercenaries.
