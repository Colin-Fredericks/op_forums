                                                    05-04-2019, 06:20 PM
                                                    Well, since nobody took me up on my offer, I decided to be proactive.

                                                    Introducing: Immaculate Styles vs 2 blood apes!

                                                    The goal of this is to see how an Immaculate might fare against two blood apes straight out of character creation.

                                                    Basic assumptions:
                                                    - Attributes are restricted to values that are achievable with a chargen 8/6/4 spread without spending BPs on primary and secondary attributes.
                                                    - Characters have a 5 in their most important ability, 4s in everything else I can think of at the start of the combat, and no specializations. This may not be an accurate representation of how typical player characters are actually created, but my experience is that players who want their character to actually be able to do interesting things are often forced to compromise, and in these cases, interesting things usually come out ahead, because combat competence has diminishing marginal utility.
                                                    - In cases where I realize that I actually need an extra ability score mid-combat, it has a default value of 3.
                                                    - Characters start with 10 combat-applicable Charms, including excellencies. This is not a lot, but it's still more than what I usually see straight out of chargen. (Of course, the smart thing to do when playing an Immaculate is to only buy the good Charms and pick up the speedbumpy pre-form crap pretty much every style is riddled with during play, but let's ignore that.)
                                                    - No Artifacts. This is somewhat unreasonable, but no more unreasonable than artifact arms and armor on every character, and I have actually seen players actively forgo combat artifacts in favor of cool utility stuff anyway.
                                                    - combatants start at short range, there's infinite room to maneuver but no terrain features that help or hinder either party

                                                    Air Immaculate:

                                                    Attributes: Str 3 Dex 5 Sta 3 | Perception 3, Wits 4
                                                    Abilities: Air Dragon 5, Awareness 4, Dodge 4, Stealth 4
                                                    Charms:
                                                    Air Dragon Style: Air Dragon's Sight, Cloud-Treading Method, Wind Dragon's Speed, Breath-Seizing Technique, Air Dragon Form, Shroud of Unseen Winds
                                                    Resistance: Ox-Body Technique
                                                    Awareness: Precision Observation Method
                                                    Dodge: Threshold Warding Stance
                                                    Brawl: Become the Hammer


                                                    The combat:

                                                    Join Battle!
                                                    Air Dragon dumps a full excellency into JB, knowing that winning it is of supreme importance if she doesn't want to get her face pounded in by massive ape fists launching four attacks before she gets to act.
                                                    She garners 3 successes on 14 dice. Oops.
                                                    Ape #1 and #2 get 6 and 4 successes, respectively.

                                                    TURN 1
                                                    Ape #1 at initiative 9, Ape #2 at initiative 7, Air Dragon at initiative 6.

                                                    Ape #1 moves to close range and launches a flurry of 2 attacks with Principle of Motion.
                                                    Air Dragon uses a full excellency (peripheral); first attack rolls 13 dice vs Evasion 8 and misses.
                                                    Second attack sees Air Dragon add Air Dragon's Sight and a full excellency from personal to keep her Evasion at 8 without flaring. Sadly, the ape rolls exactly 8 successes.
                                                    Damage 15-6=9 dice, for a pathetic 2 successes.
                                                    Ape #1 at initiative 12, Air Dragon at 3.
                                                    Ape #2 moves in and does the same as his companion.
                                                    Air Dragon fires off ADS+full excellency again, but the ape miraculously manages to roll 9 successes. 10 dice of damage garner 5 successes.
                                                    Air Dragon is crashed at -3 initiative, Ape #2 goes up to initiative 18 and still has an attack to make.
                                                    He fires off a decisive at 13 dice plus a WP for an auto-success versus Air Dragon's base 5+1 Evasion minus her onslaught of 3, which she can't even negate because being crashed shut down ADS. She still tosses a full excellency at it, because it's not like her motes will do her any good once she's dead, bringing her up to 5.
                                                    The ape rolls 10 successes.
                                                    18 dice of damage can't reliably kill through an ox-body without double 10s, but a -2 wound penalty is still a fight-ender. What an anticlimax, I sigh as I pick up roughly ten million dice.
                                                    Then I proceed to roll 4 successes.
                                                    Air Dragon is wounded but not out on her last -1 health level, and her opponent just reset to base, potentially setting her up for initiative shift! Reluctant to let her anima banner rise in case she needs to hide later in the scene, she tosses 4 peripheral motes at the attack, bringing her up to 19 dice. Ape spends a WP, but he's still at a less-than-amazing Parry 6.
                                                    19 dice somehow manages to roll only 7 successes, for a total of 11 raw damage, which is brought down to 4 post-soak by the ape's thick hide. This might still be enough for a crash with a tiny bit of luck...
                                                    ...Alas, the dice gods fail her. She rolls 1 success and faces the very real possibility of another brutal four-attack beatdown, so she reflexively uses her anima power to summon a swift breeze to dry off the rapidly gathering beads of sweat on her brow, allowing her to enter Air Aura at the end of her turn.

                                                    DING DONG NEXT TURN, everybody regains 5m

                                                    TURN 2
                                                    Ape #1 at initiative 12, Ape #2 at initiative 3, Air Dragon at initiative -1.

                                                    Ape #1 launches another Principle of Motion flurry.
                                                    First withering rolls 13 dice vs a full excellency'd Evasion of now 7 due to her wound penalty... and fails to connect with 5 successes.
                                                    For his second action, he'd prefer to go for a defend other on the other ape, but Principle of Motion is very unclearly worded and doesn't say whether otherwise non-flurriable actions can be placed into the flurry created by it, so he goes with a grapple because I'm too lazy to hunt for dev quotes.
                                                    10 dice plus a WP versus a full excellency'd Evasion of 6 doesn't bode well for the Air Dragon, so she tosses in a WP as well, because a successful grapple at this point pretty much spells critical existence failure for her.
                                                    He rolls 2 successes for a grand total of 3. Sigh of relief.
                                                    Ape #2 uses his last WP for another Principle of Motion flurry. (The assumption is that they started the scene materialized, but haven't had the time to regain the WP from using the Charm.)
                                                    As usual, a 13d10 attack vs a full excellency, Evasion 5. Attack garners 7 successes, post-soak damage pool of 11 rolls 8. Air Dragon watches helplessly as the prospect of initiative shift sails further and further away from her eyes, as if carried by a playful breeze.
                                                    Knowing how close he got to handing her the means of his destruction last turn, ape #2 decides to play it safe and smack her with another withering attack. Air Dragon is tempted to put no effort into her defense because 13 dice is going to hit an Evasion of 4 anyway, but then decides to toss those 4 motes at it because she'd feel very stupid if he ended up hitting her with 3 successes in the end.
                                                    Alas, he rolls 5S and hits, post-soak damage 11 turns into a profoundly underwhelming two successes, but it's not like she isn't screwed anyways.
                                                    Air Dragon attempts to enter concealment with Shroud of Unseen Winds, rolling 7 dice after penalties with 2 auto-successes against the apes' 8d on senses.
                                                    She gathers a total of 6 successes, while the apes both roll 5. She's safe for now.

                                                    DING DONG NEXT TURN, everybody regains 5m

                                                    TURN 3
                                                    Ape #2 at initiative 14, Ape#1 at initiative 9, Air Dragon at initiative -11 and on her second turn in crash

                                                    Since the core has no rules for using an action to try finding someone, the two apes are neutered. In the absence of a better option, they both pay 2i to delay their action.
                                                    To make matters even more confusing, the rules are incredibly unclear about resolving disengage attempts while concealed.
                                                    After flipping through the book and scratching my head for quite some time, I come to the conclusion that a concealed character still needs to disengage, and if she wants to maintain concealment, she also needs to succeed on a Dex+Stealth roll to change hiding places, but the two rolls do not need to be flurried, since reflexively moving away is not an action. In hindsight, I probably should have applied a penalty for blindness on the roll to oppose her disengage, but as we'll see, this hasn't changed anything.
                                                    In any case, seeing that if she wanted to go to ground, she'd need to pass three Stealth checks at steadily mounting penalties, Air Dragon decides that attempting to do so while in close range of two demons who are both sitting on a considerable pile of initiative they can immediately unload into her the moment she fails even one roll is a sucker's game. (Air aspects are universally well-versed in probability theory. Having Lore as an aspect ability will do that to you.)
                                                    So she disengages with 11 dice and 1 auto-success (keeping her remaining 9 motes for Cloud-Treading Method and Shroud of Unseen Winds) versus her opponents' 9d on combat movement.
                                                    ...Well, she spends a willpower just to be safe. Discretion is the better part of valor, after all.
                                                    She rolls a total of 7 successes versus 3 and 5. That's a willpower wasted, but at least she can run away.
                                                    Now for the stealth. Apparently Shroud of Unseen Winds does not negate the penalty for crossing wide-open terrain despite literally making you invisible (maybe you shimmer like the Predator while you move?), so that's 9d base +2 stunt -1 wound -5 crossing wide-open terrain, of which Cloud-Treading Method negates 3, for a total of 8d and 2 AS.
                                                    She rolls a total of 7 successes.
                                                    To her dismay, so does ape #2 (can we take a moment to appreciate how tremendously unlikely it is to get 7 successes on 8 dice?). Moreover, since he delayed his action, he can now reflexively use this opportunity to strike at her as she tries to run away.
                                                    Decisive 13d claw versus a completely mote-tapped Air Dragon's total Evasion of 5, rolls 6 successes.
                                                    12 initiative is swiftly turned into another 4 levels of damage, dropping Air Dragon to her last -4 health level.

                                                    DING DONG NEXT TURN, everybody regains 5m.

                                                    TURN 4
                                                    Ape #1 at initiative 7, ape #2 at 3, Air Dragon at -13 and on her third turn in crash. She'll reset to base next turn.

                                                    Ape #1 is still completely unaware of what's going on. He drools a little but otherwise does nothing useful.
                                                    Ape #2 moves closer to Air Dragon. She moves away reflexively. If he still had a Willpower, he could use Shattering Roar and have a good chance to finish her off even from base initiative; alas, he doesn't, so the only option he has is rushing with his 9 dice versus her 7. Accepting that she might die here, Air Dragon decides to spend no motes, because I only realized that the opportunity to use Shattering Roar existed when I started to ponder her options, so I didn't check whether he has the WP to actually use it. Thus, I decided to prioritize crashing him in order to shut it down over avoiding the rush.
                                                    He rolls 3 successes; she rolls 4. The rush is somehow still avoided.
                                                    Air Dragon rolls a chakram attacks with a 1m excellency, because at this point I remember that the ape has no Willpower left. Still, the prospect of an initiative shift is too tempting to not try.
                                                    Alas, she rolls 3 successes on 13 dice, and her weapon is unceremoniously swatted away by a hairy paw. She uses her reflexive movement to escape to medium range.

                                                    DING DONG NEXT TURN, everybody regains 5m.

                                                    TURN 5
                                                    Ape #1 at initiative 7, ape #2 at 3, Air Dragon resets to 3 this turn.

                                                    The chakram attack revealed Air Dragon, but at this point she's at long range from him. Still, he starts moving in case Ape #2 catches up to her and she fails to disengage.
                                                    Ape #2 moves closer and rushes, failing once more with 4 successes versus 5.
                                                    Air Dragon moves out to medium range once more and starts to withdraw. At this point, I realize that withdraw inexplicably uses Athletics rather than Dodge. She defaults to a score of 3 and no excellency, rolling 6 dice and gathering 2 successes.

                                                    With this, the fight effectively ended - ape #2 attempted to rush but she had more than enough motes to consistently rebuff him, and eventually she managed to gather the required 10 successes to move out to long range, at which point she became impossible to catch up to.



                                                    Final result: both apes remained unharmed. Air Dragon managed to escape with her pride and last -4 health level wounded, but at least she didn't have to take crippling.

                                                    Notes:
                                                    - quick characters retaining accuracy bonuses on decisives is incredibly painful for deebs
                                                    - in future engagements, I probably won't give stunt bonuses to the apes, it's not like they're dramatically significant characters here
                                                    - Air Dragon deserves a rematch at some point in the future, those first few rounds went incredibly poorly for her
