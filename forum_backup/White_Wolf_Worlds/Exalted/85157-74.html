            I wouldn't want to speculate very much, but I tentatively suppose that Beast might be Mammon's word, not so much for his being as the connotations the word has to materialism, in a certain biblical sense.

            Originally posted by Kleptomania
            The Blade of Want seems appropriate to the King of Greed.
            ​I don't think Blade of Want refers to the desire to possess things, I think it refers to the terrible existential dilemma of the desire for meaning and purpose, with the blade both serving as a tool to impose your will on the substance of creation and a means of ultimately becoming at peace with your own finite existence.

            ​The Blade of Want signifies Royalty, and the path to achieving it is something that all of them have stalled on.

            ​I think the frequent attribution of divinity to Cutting, itself reflected in the concept of Blade of Want, is why the term is among the divine words, but it is only an incomplete fragment.
