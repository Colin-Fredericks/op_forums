                I talked about this idea a while back, but I've finally gotten around to writing up some stats for this creature. (Please note that this is a first draft, so let me know if you think anything in it is a bad idea or if it's missing something it should really have.) This breezy beast is meant to be the result of an Ambition 3 Celestial working and broadly on par with a second circle demon; it's nowhere near as deadly head-on as Octavian, but it can really move.

                Storm Horse

                The storm horse is the creation of an eccentric Marukani sorcerer, the result of crossbreeding a (very confused) prize stallion with an actual cumulonimbus cloud. The resulting creature is a magnificent warhorse, with a stormcloud-grey coat and a mane the color of blue-tinged lightning.

                Although it's easy to mistake for an air elemental at a glance, the storm horse is a creature of flesh and blood -- essentially of the same nature as a (very powerful) God-Blood. While hardly an academic and probably not literate, the horse is as smart as some humans and can understand spoken Riverspeak well enough. It cannot speak human tongues but is quite expressive with its body language, and a capable horseman will usually understand what it wants to convey.

                Essence: 3; Willpower: 8; Join Battle: 8 dice
                Personal Motes: 80
                Health Levels: -0x2/-1x5/-2x5/-4x3/Incap.
                Speed Bonus: +5

                Actions: Feats of Strength: 12 dice (may attempt Strength 5 feats); Senses: 8 dice; Stealth: 7 dice
                Resolve 4, Guile 3

                Combat:
                Attack (Kick): 12 dice (Damage 16, minimum 2)
                Attack (Lightning blast): 14 dice (5m per attack, Damage 18, ignores 3 points of soak or hardness from metal armor)
                Combat Movement: 14 dice (see Swift-Hooved)
                Evasion 5, Parry 3
                Soak/Hardness: 12/4

                Special Attacks

                Brutal Kick: Enemies smaller than human size that take 5+ damage from the storm horse’s withering stomps are knocked back one range band and fall prone.

                Merits

                Swift-Hooved: The storm horse applies the double 9s rule on all rolls to disengage, withdraw or rush.

                Noble Steed: The storm horse has a Defining Tie of loyalty to the sorcerer who created it, which cannot be reduced or altered by any means.

                Like the Wind: Outside of combat, the storm horse can generally gallop at half again the speed of a normal horse, and go at least twice as long without requiring rest.

                Offensive Charms

                Hooves of Thunder (5m, 1wp or 10m, 1 wp; Supplemental; Instant; Essence 3): The impact of the storm horse's hooves unleashes a brutal thunderclap, affecting everyone within close range (for 5m, 1wp) or short range (for 10m, 1wp) other than the horse and its rider. This effect may optionally be used in conjunction with a normal kick attack, using the same attack roll for both, but dealing damage separately. The thunderclap is unblockable, and any opponent whose Defense is overcome suffers 5 dice of unsoakable withering damage; victims who suffer damage greater than or equal to their Stamina are knocked prone. Battle groups instead suffer 10 dice, and if a rout check is provoked, the +1 difficulty for a supernatural area-of-effect attack applies.

                Crackling Fury Discharge (5m; Simple; Instant; Dual; Essence 3): The storm horse unleashes a bolt of lightning from its nostrils, rolling 14 dice to attack at up to medium range without requiring an aim action. A withering attack deals a base of 18 damage and ignores up to 3 soak from metal armor (including alloys made with the five magical materials); a decisive attack converts extra successes on the attack roll into additional damage dice, and ignores up to 3 hardness from metal armor.

                Foe-Scattering Tempest (10m; Supplemental; Instant; Essence 3): The storm horse may use this Charm to supplement either a normal movement action or any sort of combat movement action, as apprpriate; regardless, the horse moves through a battle group's space without treating it as difficult terrain. Additionally, the storm horse rolls its Combat Movement pool with double 9s against the battle group's Defense; if successful, the battle group suffers 10 dice of unsoakable withering damage (as with Hooves of Thunder, above). In the alternative, this Charm may be used when an enemy or battle group attempts a grapple or engage gambit against the storm horse and/or its rider; the horse resists the control roll with its Combat Movement pool (with double 9s), and deals damage as from Hooves of Thunder if it successfully escapes.

                Defensive Charms

                Swift Cyclone Defense (2m+ or 5m; Reflexive; Instant; Essence 2): The storm horse may increase its Parry or Evasion against an attack by spending 2 motes per +1, to a maximum of its Essence (i.e. +3). It may also spend 5 motes to negate all penalties to its Defense against one attack

                Impact-Dispersing Exhale (10m, 1wp; Reflexive; Instant; Uniform; Essence 3): The storm horse may shunt the force of an incoming attack into the air around it, halving the damage dice rolled (round up) after applying soak or hardness.

                Mobility Charms

                Sky-Treading Style (2m; Reflexive; One turn; Essence 2): The storm horse can run across the air itself, requiring no solid ground to take movement actions. The horse cannot hover or ascend straight up or down; it is generally limited to an angle no sharper than 45 degrees. This effect cannot be maintained if the storm horse is knocked down. However, if the horse falls from a height for any reason, it may reflexively spend 2 motes to perfectly negate falling damage for both itself and its rider, assuming the rider remains on its back. (This effect is not generally useful against other effects that deal the equivalent of falling damage, such as being smacked into a wall with Heaven Thunder Hammer, unless the Storyteller deems it appropriate.)

                Inescapable Whirlwind Motion (2m+; Supplemental; Instant; Essence 2): The storm horse may supplement any roll for combat movement by spending 2 motes per automatic success, up to a maxmimum of (Essence, i.e. 3) successes. This Charm may also be used to supplement any feat of demolition in which the horse bodily crashes through the object it intends to destroy, in which case the horse may attempt Strength 7+ feats, and the cost of using Impact-Dispersing Exhale to resist damage caused directly by this foolishness is reduced to 5 motes.

                Miscellaneous Charms

                Wind-Body Prana (10m, 1wp; Simple; One scene; Essence 3): Although it is not a spirit and cannot actually dematerialize, the storm horse can transform itself physically into a gust of wind. Anything worn by the horse (such as a saddle, barding, or relatively light saddlebags) transforms along with its body, but the horse can by no means bring its rider along (unless they have some means of riding the wind itself) or carry anything else in this form. In wind form, the horse can get through any gap that isn't airtight, and can't be affected by attacks that would not harm the air itself (physical attacks are generally of no use unless capable of striking a dematerialized being, although energy attacks can be effective). Being a gust of wind does not make the horse any stealthier; the wind remains audible and tends to blow light objects around as it passes.

                Swift Gale Approach (10m; Supplemental; One day; Essence 2): Unifying its Essence with the wind itself, the storm horse can travel around 100 mph overland, and gallop all day without tiring. The horse also applies double 9s to any roll necessary to overcome obstacles or hazards that would slow it down; it may reflexively spend 5 motes to apply double 8s instead. (If the rider must also roll to resist a hazard, he also gains the benefits of double 9s or 8s as appropriate, as the horse manuevers to protect its master.) A rider with appropriate Charms of his own can achieve incredible speeds on the mighty horse's back.

                Author's notes:
                - I'm wondering if Hooves of Thunder should have some kind of limit on how much Initiative the horse can reap at once if it smacks a large group of people. Many similar area effects have one.
                - I'm also wondering if the horse should have slightly more offensive punch when it comes to single-target attacks. I didn't want to overdo things Charm-wise, but a really brutal stomp might be a fair addition.
                - If a Solar with Wind-Racing Essence Infusion takes this thing for a spin, it's going to get ridiculously fast. I don't think this is necessarily a problem, but it's possible I went a little too far.
                - I very nearly wrote a Charm for what happens if you hitch the storm horse to a chariot or something, but eh. I'm not sure we even have normal rules for land vehicles in this edition. It probably should be able to pull one through the air with Sky-Treading Style (possibly for an extra mote cost) though?

                            It wasn't especially intentional, no (at least, I didn't deliberately plan on that being a weakness for the horse). I didn't think too hard about hardness -- I ended up giving it the same base soak as Alvuea, since she's a second-circle demon who is a fair scrapper but not really focused on personal combat. Of course, Alvuea has a Charm that gives her artifact armor, so she doesn't need innate hardness...

                            I think giving the storm horse a hardness of 4 would be reasonable. It is supposed to be a rather hardy beast. I've made that edit above.

                            The horse's health levels were pretty much eyeballed as well. I considered giving it a few more, but I didn't really have a guideline in mind other than "a lot tougher than a normal horse, but not nearly as tough as Octavian." I admit I don't have a tremendous amount of experience with the combat system, so I wasn't sure how major an effect Impact-Dispersing Exhale would have (although it seems fairly strong to me).

                            Incidentally, as a formatting issue: I guess I should have included the lightning attack from Crackling Fury Discharge in the section on combat traits. I wasn't sure if Charm-created attacks belonged there, but Amon Mora has his elemental bolts listed.

                                            A sorcerer eats a specially prepared cock's-egg omelette and gains the hypnotic, burning gaze of a cockatrice.

                                            The sorcerer's attacks use (Wits+Occult) to target the burning gaze and (Intelligence+Occult) for the control roll. For the purpose of withering savaging attacks, the gaze is treated as a light weapon with damage based on Intelligence instead of Strength. This ability otherwise works like that of an actual cockatrice; the sorcerer can maintain both a physical grapple and a gaze if he chooses.

                                            ...I don't know, should the withering attacks bypass armor soak somehow? The actual cockatrice stats are notably missing a withering damage value for the gaze entirely. It doesn't make sense that armor would do any good, though, since the victim burns from the inside out.

                                            Celestial 2 or 3? I'm kinda thinking 3 since, if nothing else, it allows the sorcerer to wield traits he's almost certainly good at and force his opponent to resist with a dice pool not normally applicable to physical combat. Still, unless it bypasses armor it's not fundamentally better than a physical grapple, and it has the downside of not being usable for anything but grappling. It'd give the sorcerer a hell of a reputation, though. ("Did you hear? Sharp-Eyed Corwin killed some dude last week just by staring him down...")
