                First Circle Demons: weaker spirits, basically homogenous, easily defeated by Solar combatants. Blood Apes are the go-to example for foot soldiers.

                Second Circle Demons: unique, more powerful, vaguely on par with Solars, Octavian is the go-to example of a highly combat focused 2nd circle pushing the limits of his kind

                Bonus: Yozi: the progenitors of all demon kind, worlds unto themselves, incredibly powerful but basically irrelevant. Base assumption is they are stuck in a box, never to escape.

                    Some additional details:

                    Third Circle demons are the souls of the Yozi, of which they have an indeterminate number. Tend to keep to themselves. If you see big natural features in Hell (canyons, mountains, forests, the sun, etc), there's a chance they're actually either Yozis or Third Circle demons. Or maybe they aren't. It can be a good idea to check beforehand.

                    Every Third Circle has seven souls embodied in Second Circle demons. Those are the movers and shakers of hellish politics, for the most part.

                    First Circle demons aren't part of the soul hierarchy, they're species of monsters that were created by the Second and Third Circle demons.

                    So luminata (1st Circle) are the progeny of Octavian (2nd Circle) who is the soul of Munaxes (3rd Circle) who is the fourth soul of She Who Lives in Her Name (Yozi).

                    Some inhabitants of Hell aren't part of this system. Adorjan (one of the Yozi) has daughters, for example. They aren't her souls, so they're not Third Circle demons. They're just her daughters.
