    So I'm doing the Lilith fight. And have come up against a difficulty in regards to beating her. Lilith can fly.

    This makes her basically impossible to hit, since I didn't give Topaz Blazing Solar Bolt. Lilith can just retreat to Medium range and rain down arrows.

    I asked around and you can't double jump with just the charms Topaz has available, not without something to jump off of. The only way I can think of for Topaz to get to Lilith is this dumb, super anime bullshit where she tosses the utility/backup knife I gave her into the air to provide a platform for the second jump.

    Which I would totally allow if I was ST and a player tried it, but feels like cheese when I'm running every character involved.
    If you can't win mechanically then you'll need to create a narrative opportunity, possibly by using a Gambit. For instance, you could retreat towards an area with strong weather to force Lilith to lose altitude, or lure her towards other threats she wouldn't want to face at the same time as Topaz, creating a virtual wall to back her against.
