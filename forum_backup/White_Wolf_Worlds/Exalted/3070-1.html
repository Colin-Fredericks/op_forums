    Name: Yuan, the Mind's Spark

    Purview: Artistic Inspiration

    Appearance: Yuan has no identity of his own, or at least none that any has seen. Instead he reflects the minds and souls of those present, manifesting as an abstract sweep of vivid colour or an oddly familiar melody when alone with another, or a tapestry of interlocking scenes and shapes accompanied by orchestral symphonies.

    Reason He Needs An Exigent: Yuan is suffering from writer's block by proxy; works of art produced in Creation have not been as daring or groundbreaking since the fall of the First Age. His Exalted will help turn the mediocre into the magnificent, raising humble artists from scratching charcoal on rock to rearranging the stars in the sky.

        Name: Eternal Dove
        Purview: The Ivory Fang mountain, the village of Spring's Hearth
        Appearance: Eternal Dove appears as a young maiden of great beauty, with flowing, silver-white hair and large, almond-shaped eyes shimmering in shades of silver. She wears flowing robes made from white silk, with silver stitchings of prayers in Old Realm characters lining the sleeves and collar. On her back are two large wings, adorned with bright white feathers, that she uses both to accentuate her gestures, as well as taking flight for travel, and during combat.
        On her hip she carries a sword of great beauty, a hilt of ivory, a blade made of interwoven glass and silver, long and slender.

        This god is the patron god of a small mountain and the single village that is sustained by it. It is a humble village of light wooden huts with roofs made from woven reeds, each outfitted with prayer mills designed by Eternal Dove herself, who keeps the Fair Folk at bay, for the Ivory Fang mountain lies in the far west on an island that borders the Bordermarches of the West.
        She instructs a small coven of priestesses in sword-based martial arts, and keeps the ice cold water fresh, that comes from a spring deep within the mountain.
        She has been revered by the villagers for millennia, and while she doesn't command any following outside her domain, here she is the most important entity in the lives of the villagers.

        Reasons she might need an Exigent: The god herself is not strong enough to guard an entire village against the forces fo the Wyld. Now that the Empress has disappeared, even more dangerous things than the Fair Folk prey on the islands off the main trade routes of the Empire. Be it Lintha, or worse, the time might soon come that enemies reach the mountain's base. If it came to a last stand-off, the God would rather consome itself in granting an Exigency to one of her priestesses than have her domain sullied by the unclean.
        She'd primarily have charms considering melee, integrity, and medicine.


        This god actually appeared in one of my campaigns, I tend to have a rather shintoistic take on the Little Gods of Exalted, and once my players leave the Blessed Isle they tend to encounter a lot of very local Little Gods with their own shrines. Despite not having a lot of influence Eternal Dove had quite the impact on one of my campaigns, so I thought she deserved her place here. In hind sight, she seems a bit like a ripoff, but she's actually a couple years old, one of the first Spirits I fully fleshed out.
        She's nothing hugely creative or far out, but rather a clear-cut Little God that would give a player a good idea of what to expect of her Exigency.

            Name: Mariaska, Our Lady of The Honed Edge
            Purview: Knives, in all their forms and uses. The southwestern city of Fralba, by her marriage to its city god (and higher popularity).
            Appearance: Mariaska is more often than not in an elegant white dinner dress, made to match the fashions of the town of Fralba, with red fringes dyed in crimson tiger lilly pastels. Her blood-hair hair drapes to the shoulder or longer on occasion, while her damascus steel skin retains the form of a young wife in her mid twenties, beautiful and yet homely as well. Rarely in public, however, her dress is replaced with that of a blacksmith apron as she quietly reforges her fingernails (and sometimes her entire hands) into Edward Scissorhands blade-edged weapons.
            Why would she need an Exigent: The town of Fralba has always been noted for its skill in forging blades and knives from the iron of the nearby mountains, with its signature being a steel with rings through it like tree rings. Most of the time, this has been the town's saving grace, as its weapons and armor would the city to defend itself from the occasional band of wyld barbarians, and the Realm was more than happy to 'purchase' the ringsteel swords for its officer caste and garrisoned soldiers. However, the Realm has recently been increasing its demands for tribute, even as the actual number of soldiers it sends diminishes. More and more native sons of Fralba are dying in the growing tide of Wyld barbarians an beastmen, instead of attending the forges or engaging in the town's other industries of entertaining Dynasts at parties, or training assassins. Mariaska wants to see the town thrive, and make/use the blades that are the town's legacy. To that end, she requires an Exigent to demonstrate these skills and protect the town, if the Terrestrials are too greedy or busy to do it themselves.
            -The First Exigent would be encouraged to focus on Craft and Melee. However, Mariaska's complete suite of Charms would also grant bonuses to Stealth, Socialize, and Survival. As the saying goes in Fralba, 'the knife can be found in five places; on the dinner table, on the battlefield, on the cookfire, on the forge, and in the back." Mariaska hopes to, eventually, build up to two or three Exigents, depending on whether or how drained the First makes her.

            I used to know things. Forbidden things.

                Name: Dutiful Nhan

                Domain: Shadows, dust, and forgotten corners

                Description: A short and slender figure, mostly humanoid, whose body and gauzy robes are both comprised of a homogenous tangle of dust fluff and cobwebs. His head and features have a vaguely feline cast to them, but he has no eyes nor mouth nor nose, merely the rough shape of a face. despite this, he can and does speak, but his voice will always, always come as a soft whisper behind the shoulder of the one he addresses.

                Why would he need an exigent? Nhan's duties frequently place him in contact with the Forbidding Manse of Ivy. The secret and lost places of Creation frequently fall under his gaze, and he may well feel it in the world's interest that what is lost and buried should perhaps remain lost and buried.

                    Name: Alidrisi
                    Domain: Maps and cartographers.
                    Description: Alidrisi always appears as a man in a traveling cloak, wearing a conical straw hat and a face wrapped in a scarf. He carries a walking stick in his left hand and a calligraphy brush in his right. Both hands are ink-stained. His skin is darkly tanned and weathered, and his eyes have black irises with silver scleria.
                    Why he needs an Exigent: To make the maps. His dual domain of maps and cartographers always pulled him in two directions- half the time, he was far afield making maps and ignoring the needs of his worshipers. Somewhere between his third and fifth audits, heaven very pointedly suggested that he make himself an exigent to do the legwork. Alidrisi finally agreed around audit seven, and finally released his Exigence into the world just in time to stave off audit ten. He considers his Exigence a masterpiece, a perfectly plotted design that will allow him to map all realms of existence, in time. (Except the Wyld, obviously.)

                            Name - Baramue, Shogun of Accursed Inspiration
                            Purview - Absurdly Bad Ideas
                            Appearance - Mighty Baramue takes the form of a ram with a coat of thunder. The head of the Ram of Ill Omen is crowned with golden horns, and between them rests a clay tablet inscribed with potent sigils of power and authority. The eyes of the Ram are benign- kind, generous and utterly devoid of wisdom. Each of the Ram's hooves are bedecked with rings of gold and electrum, but his feet disdain the earth, and he has grown fat with worship and lack of exercise.
                            Why He Needs An Exigent - Because there is literally no good reason for him to have one.

                            Campaign History - Baramue was created by my first Sidereal character; in a fit of inspiration, I attempted to game the system by making a god of bad ideas and then using his power to figure out what not to do. The ST told me that 'bad ideas' were taken, but offered to let me use a Sidereal charm to create the 'Office of Absurdly Bad Ideas' and place my sheep-familiar at its head. I did so, and my contributions to the campaign took a turn for the weird. It turned out that not only were most of my Circle's ideas absurdly bad, absurdly bad ideas were often the absolute best way to accomplish our goals. Very useful and powerful god to have around, not terribly good for morale. I've since had him appear as comedic relief in a few other games.

                            Baramue holds an office as a Celestial God of relatively high rank in the Forbidden Manse of Ivy, but he is barred from actually using the library out of a well-justified fear that he'll get ideas from the books. His office consists of about a dozen extremely overworked gods who ceaselessly compile The Roll of Inexcusable Follies, as well as a secretary reporting to Nara-O who is tasked with keeping Baramue from ever actually engaging in politics. For PR reasons, the Bureau heads have labelled The Roll as a restricted book, but it is still used referentially in Yu-Shan as an unsubtle insult.

                            Baramue is responsible in large part for the creation of the Highland Bamunae, a hardy breed of plateau sheep with a valuable coat of luxurious mirrored wool and the ability to emit rays of intense heat from their eyes. He is paid incredible sums of money to never publicly reveal his political affiliations.

                                    *** VOID CIRCLE THREADOMANCY ***

                                    So I've been taking pottery classes and doing a lot of independent research for the hell of it, and after some inspiration struck me felt like reviving this old thread. It's a fine resource both for Exigents (whenever they happen), and gods that may be characters of their own instead of sacrificed on the altar of backstory like parents.

                                    Name - Alabaster Faience
                                    Purview - Pottery
                                    Appearance - Alabaster Faience most often appears as a snakelike dragon, but this has changed much over the centuries as he has grown in power; while most of his scales are the shining white of porcelain, he is either unable or unwilling to wholly doff the terracotta which he originally mimicked, and over time and the sacrifice of devoted worshipers, he has accumulated black patches that appear to be soulsteel; the overall effect is akin to a koi fish or calico cat. He has also appeared as a more humanoid form, a well-sculpted staute, masked and robed, that never seems to move, merely change between perfectly-held poses in the space of eyeblinks.
                                    Why he needs an Exigent - He doesn't, really. Not anymore. In the First Age he was prepared to sacrifice himself to empower a champion, and petitioned the Unconquered Sun for the Exigence. This was granted, but in between the prayer and the action falls the shadow, or in this case the unexpected intervention of existing Chosen. His existence spared, Alabaster Faience still has this Exigence; less noble than he once was, but calculating as ever, he considers it a great treasure. The Ages have not proven him wrong: as the holder of one of the few Exigences of the First Age, other gods seeking it have attempted to curry favor with him, and he's used this to great effect; he now has a palatial temple that doubles as a dragon kiln, which fires some of the finest stoneware goods Creation has seen, and more than one artifact bears his blessing.
                                    Mortals know that he still has a great gift to share, though the exact terminology and its significance is lost upon them. For his part, he has sworn to bestow it upon a mortal who asks him as he fires his kiln and works his magic. Scores of women and men have opened the front door of his temple during this sacred time; a few have even survived to step inside.
                                    His relationship with the Realm is somewhat tense; he clearly abuses his status, and the Sidereal Exalted are well aware that he could choose a champion if he desired it. On the other hand, his personal and political power, coupled with his lack of desire to empower a champion, means that he is untroubled as long as his kiln-temple is open to the Dragon-Blooded for the crafting of works marvelous and mundane.

                                        Here's one
                                        ​Name​ - Grom, God of Steel (yes it is a misspelling of Crom)
                                        ​​Purview - Worked Steel
                                        ​Appearance​ - Grom appears as an old grizzled blacksmith with worn clothes and a partially burned leather apron. He carries a 10lb one-handed sledgehammer in one hand and a pair of tongs used to hold hot metal for working.​ The God of Steel cycles out. Every few generations the current Grom has a number of God-Blooded children. These children go out into Creation to let the world temper them, or break them. The ones that show strength are given the chance to become the next God of Steel. When one is chosen, they gain the power of the previous God of Steel.
                                        Why he needs an Exigent - The last God of Steel was a jerk. Bitter from the fall of the Solars he was often inebriated and cruel to his followers. Yet, he would offer them great boons for surreptitiously worshiping him in direct violation of the Immaculate faith of the Shogunate. When the time for the cycle came he asked to have an Exigence. The Unconquered Sun saw this as nothing more than an improved version of what the Gods of Steel had always done and agreed. When the God of Steel chose one of his children to replace him, he split his essence. Half the power went to make his child the new God of Steel and the other half went to a mortal blacksmith that he'd often tormented and blessed in turn. Now the current Grom has an Exigent that he can command. If the Exigent dies then the God can assign it to a new owner without devoting more power to the Exaltation. It is now considered a tradition of the Steel Gods to award the exaltation to a favored follower and Godhood to a favored son at the end of the cycle.

                                        ​I created a God-Blood for a group of Solars to face early on in a campaign. His name was Katashi (twentieth steel). He was the twentieth son of the current God of Steel and was working toward being the next in the line. Though the god never appeared, I liked the idea of a god who "tarnished" in old age.

                                            My son is running an Exalt of the Village Well in 2.5E game. When the Usurpation went down a Solar entrusted a minor well goddess with a Starmetal cog to a Doomsday Weapon. Promised her he'd be back for it soon. Of course, he never called her again. Typical.

                                            Centuries passed. The goddess learned how to embue mortals with power. She could uplift an extra to the dizzying ranks of unenlightened Heroic Mortal. Her Champion would protect the village to the best of his/her ability until death or retirement...which was usually death.

                                            -Name: Anatraie

                                            -Purview: That one village's well (and allegedly unbeknownst to all, the keeper of the Starmetal cog)

                                            -Description: Complexion and hair dark like the water of a well. Clothes made from the shadows of a well and the light reflected off the surface of the water on the well's interior.

                                            -Reasons why she needed an Exigent: Someone discovered the hiding place of the cog and sent a group comprised of Dragonblooded and godbloods to retrieve it. Sensing her destruction was nigh, she besought the Unconquered Sun for the Exigence, the divine fire to raise her Champion to an Exalt. She was surprised when she received it.

                                            In our game, her Champion was lured away prior to the destruction of the village and her death. Before her demise she communicated telepathically with her Champion explaining the cog and the seriousness of retrieving and unmaking it. The Exalt/godblood group stole the cog, the Exigence and bolted.

                                            The Champion pursued them across the Scavenger Lands eventually retrieving the Starmetal cog and achieving his belated Exaltation. While searching for a way to destroy the cog, he had it repurposed into a Starmetal Thunderbolt Shield of the buckler variety. Eventually he found someone capable of unmaking the cog.

                                            Having achieved the purpose for the creation of his Exaltation, and not having a home or goddess to return to, my son's PC is now searching for a new purpose before someday passing on the Exaltation to another.

                                            Good night sweet Green Sun Prince: And flights of demons beat thee to thy rest...with billyclubs!
