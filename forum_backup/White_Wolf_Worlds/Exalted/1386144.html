    Just For Fun- GoT/SoI&F Great Houses in Exalted

    05-06-2020, 03:19 PM
    So while I am far from being a big Song of Ice and Fire/ Game of Thrones fan, the one thing which I really liked in the series were the noble Houses, with their symbols, traditions, motto, history and other characteristics, which is also why I am a big Dragon-Blooded fan in Exalted. Lately, I have started wondering about how, perhaps, it could be possible to take the Great Houses of GoT and translate them into Exalted in the form of Cadet Houses, Fallen Houses, Patrician Families or households of the Great Houses- or even as future Great Houses, as replacements for the ones who would fall in a Civil Wars scenario, and that without mentioning the possibilities of Outcast clans (such as the Wanasaan and their like). Overall, that's what I currently have in mind, but please feel free to present your own suggestions and ideas on the subject! Names, and a more detailed writeups, may show up later :P

    House Arryn- with their Coat of Arms and words, it is clear that they should be an Air Aspect House, and the region in which they dwell feels a lot like the North. I assume that they could be adapted as a Cadet House in some outreached Northern satrapy, with perhaps some focus around hawk training or something like that. I could see them as originally having Tepet as their patron, but they have quickly moved their support to Ragara after the fall of Tepet looked eminent.

    House Baratheon- a lot of their aesthetics feels like an Earth or Fire Aspect Cadet House to me. I can see them as a warrior House, whose members are known to act out of passion instead of reason. They could probably be found in an Eastern satrapy, maybe near the Dreaming Sea, known for a rainy weather, and have a violent history in which the House overtook the place of a former Cadet House which has fallen from grace.

    House Greyjoy- easily a Western Cadet House, I wonder if making them Sea Aspect is a bit too on the nose, and would step on the fingers of Peleps. Perhaps they could be used to develop House Zihao of Huang Hei? Or maybe in the form of the Peleps Kaizoku Household? On the other hand, maybe one of Cherak's Cadet Houses (such as Japor) could do a better work.

    House Lannister- a lot of their themes feels very Ragara, which more or less fill their niche perfectly. If they have to exist as their own thing, they could probably be a Southern Household of the House or something. On the other hand- Chanos is famous for its mines, and Sesus did used her espionage skills to bring down the once Great house- so perhaps we could develop the fallen Chanos through the Lannisters?

    House Martell- definitely a Southern Cadet House. I assume that some Southern satrapy which was attacked by the Realm (maybe Cathak?) again and again, until an uneasy truce was established. Probably a Fire Aspect, I think. On the other hand, they could be some Outcast group which is officially registered as a Cadet House, but never truly accepted the Realm in their hearts

    House Stark- a lot of their themes of coming disaster, rigid honor and fallen glory could fit the Tepet, even though I feel that some of their "oldness" hints about some necromatic themes- which makes me think about the Wanasaan, who are also facing their own fall due to honor. At any case, that niche is already covered, IMO.

    House Tully- I think either some patrician family or a Cadet House in the East. Water Aspect, probably, which rised from a number of small local Dragon Blooded families, or perhaps a number of patrician families who moved to the satrapy. I could see them having Tepet as their patrons, who still remain loyal to them even after their fall. While they generally have a good reputation, the fall of Tepet and the coming Civil War while demands for tribute increase start strain the House, especially as the Ragara or Sesus start to unofficially take over the satrapy in the name of Tepet's debts.

    House Frey- yeah, they would probably be some Cadet House who gained too much power, and start to try and show presence, while never being respected by the other Houses. Heck, I could easily see them as House Margard- a mortal House which may betray Ferem/ Tepet/ some other House during the Civil War in order to get some Dragon Blood into their family together with them rising to a Cadet House status- only to discover that the Realm despise them for the way they took power.

    House Bolton- a Northern Cadet House rising from a dynasty once known for their sadistic traits, after the Realm conquered the satrapy and elevated the local Dragon Blooded to a Cadet House. They are ruthless and cruel even after centuries of being "tamed" by House Tepet, and they desire power once more. With Tepet failing, they are likely to plot a way for them to gain a Great House status on the exapns of their former "patrons".

    House Tyrell- I get a strong Cynis vibes from Tyrell, and whatever they (or V'neef) do not cover, House Desai probably covers, so the niche is already filled.

    House Targaryen- easily a fallen Great House of the Fire Aspect. Known for their brilliance and cruelty, they would probably have been a once well respected House. Considering that it takes something in the scale of using the Eye of Autochthon in order to get a whole House "put to the sword" instead of simply broken apart and demoted, I guess that they have done something really, really bad, which caused other Houses (led by Cathak) to go out and attack the House. Maybe less of an attempt for a rebellion as much as a destruction of the Imperial City? Or maybe them trying to harness the Realm Defense Grid- and actually managing to do so, to some degree, even though it led to ruin, their defeat and the execution of almost the whole House?

    House Baelish- formerly a patrician family, they have maneuvered their way into becoming a Cadet House through trickery and guile, in an attempt to gain enough power to return one day as a Great House, while still being merchants by nature, even if they deal with some of the darker aspects of the trade. On the other hand, we could say that House Nissar covers some of the themes as this House, which saves the work of converting them.

    House Royce- as an old House, I could see them as as a sorcerer-warrior House, rising from a caste of tradition keepers and historians who are trained in the art of the sword. I kind of envision them as a Southern Cadet House, for some reason.

    House Yronwood- a Southern Cadet House rising from a caste of sorcerers who guard the resting place of ancestors. Perhaps they could work better as an outcast group, but some development is required in order to make them into different from the Wanasaan. Perhaps removing the sorcerer focus, and instead going with them guarding the halls and mausoleums of the dead?

    House Mormont- I could see something in the style of Bear Islands as a island satrapy in the Northwest. On the other hand, they could be presented as one of Cherak's Cadet Houses, perhaps with some emphasis on hunting.

    House Reed- the marsh land on which they rule could easily be found in the East, and while they could be a Cadet House I think they work better as some sort of an outcast family of some kind, which have mostly avoided the Realm by the lack of interest in their swamp.

    House Caron- I could see them as a Cadet House devoted to Cathak, as a military bastion in the South. However, their Coat of Arms and motto may also lead to an "House of Performers", known both for their archery skills and artistic talents, especially music.

    Well, that's what I have for the Great Houses and chosen former Great Houses and minor noble Houses. Again, any suggestions and ideas would be welcomed.

      So some extra thoughts about developing the original pitches-

      [House Arryn]- so the more I think about it, the more I tend towards turning them into something different than a Cadet House. With around seven Imperial Legions going rogue instead of being dissolved by the Realm, I think it could be interesting as seeing them as one of those legions. Is there any official material about Fray other than the fact it is in the Northeast, and was taken over by a Dragon of the Maelstrom Legion? If not, we could probably present it as a cliff satrapy in the depth of the forest, surrounded by "barbarians" and which was found under the protection of the legion- and when the the command came, Galina and her soldiers decided to (literally) overthrow the satrap (over the cliff) and take over the satrapy. The locals of Fray would be falconers who use the raptors both to hunt and commune across the cliffs, and the legionnaires (who have immersed themselves in the local culture through the years) have been seen as "winged knights". If there are actually some details about Fray, we could easily just move it to another place with another legion. At any case, the members of the rogue legion are more interested in solidifying their own power in the region, and won't get involved with the coming Civil War unless forced to, or while under attack by the Realm or one of the Houses.

      [House Baratheon]- while the Dreaming Sea could have been a good place to put them, in second thought I wonder if it would overstep Prasad, as I assume that part of the unofficial understanding between the Dragon Clans and the Realm is "leave the Dreaming Sea to us". At any case, the East is general still could be a good place to fit them as a Cadet House, IMO. I could see their satrapy somewhere is the Scavenger Lands, with a common practice of hunting rare beasts in the forest in order to show one's worth. The starapy has known its share of outcast families, where every one and awhile one of those families overthrows the current Cadet House, absorbs the remains and proclaims itself the new ruler of the land. As the official satrap is usually left outside of the infighting, the new Cadet House always swears fealty to the Empress and the locals are accustomed to the situation, the Realm usually avoids getting itself involved in those uprisings. However, now the Empress is gone, the current head of the Cadet House is dead, and three different families all claim themselves as the rightful heirs- and while one of them is supported by the Throne, another has gained the support of Lookshy and the third has the help of a strange yet powerful witch, which some whisper is, in truth, an Anathema.

      [House Greyjoy]- while Ferem is the most well known of Cherak's Cadet Houses, I really do think that something in the style of the Greyjoy could be adapted to this region. While neither Japor or Nandorun feels a lot like the Greyjoy, they are not the only Cadet Houses around, and there could be some island satrapy in the White Sea- perhaps one which is not tied directly to Cherak, but has been considered its ally, with perhaps having Peleps as their patron. While Ferem tries to recruit their help to their vision of Grand Cherak, this Cadet House has plans of their own- they want independence from the Realm, but they seek to stand on their own. The Wanasaan have a strong presence in that land, and even though their influence has declined after their own civil war, the Cadet House has respected the exorcists and have sent some of their scions to serve the family in the same fashion that Dynasts send their kin to the Immaculate Order (they could even share a common ancestry, with the Cadet House being descendant from a branch of the Wanasaan who joined the Realm). While some want to return to the "old ways", others, who have a more dynastic attitude, seek to join Peleps's "western empire", or fund one of their own through conquest and blood.

      [House Lannister]- yeah, I really dig the idea of using them as House Chanos. One a proud Great House, known for their alchemists and wealth from the mines, after House Sesus have uncovered some "embarrassing details" about the House (let's go with them having too close relationships with demons and their like), the House was stricken from the Imperial Ledgers, their land seized by the Sesus and their status reduced to a patrician family. The Agate family may be the descendants of House Chanos, the last reminder of power which they once held over their Prefecture. While House Sesus rules their home, the memory of what was taken from them still lingers, and with the coming Civil War they may very well support the Ragara prefect in order to destabilize the Sesus's rule (perhaps by sharing them some of the occult knowledge they gathered from their infernal ancestors). A long time may have passed since their fall- but a Chanos never forgets their debts.

      [House Martell]- I think that in the end, they would be less of a Cadet House and more of an outcast family, or even a gathering of number of Southern families which united together under a single banner to stop the advances of the Realm to the South. Each of the Wasteland Princes has their own freedom to lead their tribe in the desert, but they all agree to gain the authority of the First Prince as their representative before the Realm. After long war between them and House Cathak, a truce was achieved, which was slowly on the way to turn the coalition of families into a Cadet House serving under Cathak. However, the disappearance of the Empress has shuffled the deck. The Princes see that the Cathak has more pressured things to deal with, and it may very well be the time to take back what was taken from them.

      [House Stark]- again, I'm a bit blank in here. A noble House who face its own fall while bound with honor? Tepet. A family known to protect the north from the powers of the dead? Wanasaan. While some outcast families with ties to wolves could be cool, but if that's all I have to work with it won't be much like the Stark, IMO.

      [House Tully]- coming from a fertile satrapy in the East, it was once ruled by a number of proud outcast clans, each claiming itself to be a King. However, then the Realm came and crushed the local rulers, unifying them many small city states into a single satrapy while establishing upon them a single patrician family as a Cadet House. However, instead of scattering the local clans, the House have intermarried with them, understanding that there were far more outcast Dragon Blooded in the satrapy than their own numbers. That have established a system in which most of the clans have mantained their own identity, while still being connected through blood (and eventually, faith) with the Cadet House. While traditionally allies of Tepet, with the fall of the House they see how their allies have slowly been replaced by Ragara and Sesus members in the name of the debts of their patrons, and force and ever increasing tribute. The House, motivated by both loyalty to Tepet and seeing how the clans may revolt against them, starts making plans to overthrow their satrap and governors with the help of the outcast clans- but to do so, they must have the trust of all families, Will they manage to do so, or will they be betrayed by one of their own?

      [House Frey]- House Margrad may be mortals, but they are known for their cunning and joy in showing how they can outwit House Ferem, no matter that they lack the Dragon's blood. However, as proud as they may be, the fact that they fall behind the Ferem because their lack of Exaltation is a sore wound in their flesh, one which drives them to work twice as much in order to turn the life of the Ferem into ever more difficult. That was when their rivals have came to them with a proposition- join us in our conquest for a Grand Cherak, and we will merry our scions to your House. With that being the only thing which could get the loyalty of the House, the Margrad have kept their mouth shut.. officially. Unknown to the Cadet Houses of Cherak, House Ragara have already presented a similar proposition to make sure they would be on their side, and when the time would come they would betray Ferem, causing them to be banished from their ancestral home (with many of them actually losing their lives) while taking over the city for themselves. Only then they would discover that they should have read the fine prints- House Ragara have never promised scions of a dynastic origin, and would instead marry them to just enough outcasts to call them a "Cadet House". Without the respect the Ferem once had, the general hate of the locals due to the betrayal, and the fact that the Ferem have managed to get their navy out of the city during the massacre as they escaped, the current matriarch of the House starts wondering if she chose the right side.

      [House Bolton]- while officially a Cadet House under a Tepet patronage, they have never truly accepted the authority of the Great House. Once an outcast clan known for skinning their victims in the name of the dark gods of the North, the Tepet has spent a century in bringing them under the Realm's heel, and another century in order to "educate" them in the proper ways of the Immaculate Philosophy. While they were eventually granted the status of a Cadet House, they haven't forgot what they witnessed as a shame brought upon them by the Tepet. When the House's legions were decimated by the Bull of the North, the House has started to get the mood of the situation and contacted with the Ragara Soras family, shifting their loyalty to a stronger House which cared less about their practices. In the dead of the night, they got rid of the Tepet in their satrapy, leaving no survivors. Together with the Ragara, they have styled the massacre as a work of some Bull loyalists or other barbarians, and the Ragara claimed the satrapy quicker than the Tepet could react, as the Tepet "could no longer protect it". While the Tepet are suspicious of what actually happened, there is nothing they can do- and the worst is still before them. After all, the Cadet House has now higher ambitions than just being freed from the Tepet- they want to take their place as a Great House, and let them feel how sharp their knives are.


      Well, that's for half of the House, I think. I'll add the other half later.
