    Not much change here, but...
    First, my Creation is slightly curved. Solves a few interrogations about the horizon and daylight. Was Creation ever spherical in its existence? That's another question that I'm leaving open.
    Secondly, some mortal martial artists can use MA charms. It requires intense breathing exercise and whatever else the philosophy of the style may require, and help channel peripheral Essence since your chakras are otherwise closed, so to speak. It's not reliable, and only masters of a given style manage to pull this off on occasion, increasing their prestige. Only a true master can manage to briefly interact with the essence of the world and let out a true Terrifying Battle Shriek or pierce armor with their bare hands without breaking them. That being said? An Exalt beats them all the time. (This in small part helps justify the continued teaching of styles in mortal populations, and most importantly, helps give mortal masters some teeth beyond just having more dots to whack your face with a form weapon a little better, without negating the usefulness of being Exalted as a dedicated martial artist.)

        In my creation, the Incarnae are the 7 souls of Gaia, and Creation is literally the grandest primordial, who was taken over by her children, the Primordials, and made into a slave. The Divine Rebellion was literally Creation revolting against its monstrous Primordial enslavers. When the Celestial Revolution ended, the Yozis broke their words and treaties in one very important way - they forced the Games of Divinity into Yu Shan, knowning the gods could not resist its addictive poison. Enraged, the 5 Incarnae left 5 proxies, and swore that Gaia the mother of Creation and true empress of all reality would never set foot in Creation again unless the Games were destroyed. Being that the treasonous, feckless gods of Yu Shan desired the games above all, and believed the lie that the winner of the games would be transformed into a Primordial if they won, the game was avidly played and all sorts of rationales were delivered. As a last insult, when the Solar purge was conducted, the Bronze faction cripped the Mask because Chepok Kejak dreamed of transcending to become a Primordial and ruling Yu Shan under his will, with only the Solar Exalted standing in the way. The Great Curse emanates from the games.

        The 7 Incarnae (Sol, Luna, Jupiter, Mars, Venus, Mercury and Saturn), who are the soul of Gaia, have resided outside of Creation in the deep Wyld, until now. Luna has always created Exalts at his whim, and now Sol calls to herself her old champions. These Exalts who are given the 2nd Breath directly by the true incarnae instead of their proxies are coordinated by Sol, who whispers to them in dreams and insulates them from the Great Curse, of which they are informed of. Needless to say, the Yozis and Malfeans regard the Proxied Solars (who can have their limit reduced by the intercession of a noble, courageous human adviser, who is immune to all of their charms) regard Gaia taking direct action as a dire threat. The Proxied Solars, Lunars and Sidereals are hunted even more than the usual escaped Celestial. Those who oppose Gaia fear her not just for selfish reasons - Sol, her shining heart, has warned that if Creation is not saved, she will need to take drastic action, recreate the world as a globe, and end the Age of Sorrows.

                    The Immaculate religions do take the Immaculate Dragons literally. They arose allegorically in the Shogunate (the deeds of any Air DB seen as achieving unity with Mela were described in text as actions of Mela), but by the time the Order was founded after the Shogunate they were taken literally.

                    A major religious difference between the Philosophy and the Faith, is the Immaculate Philosophy reaches the Elemental Dragons incarnated themselves as the Immaculate Dragons., while the Immaculate Faith teaches that they were “normal” (though exceptional) Dragon-Blooded who achieved unity with the Elemental Dragons through their virtue and enlightened deeds showing the way for others.

                    The ancestor worshipping heresy in the Riverlands that Charlie’s follows plays off the Faith’s take on the Deagons to pray to and honor ancestors believed to have achieved unity with the Dragons as retroactive incarnations of them. The door to ancestor worship opened they also honor the dead in the underworld as still pursuing their enlightenment, or as having achieved unity with the dragons but choosing to stay around as protective bodhisattva for their families.

                    The Pure Way believes that Terrestrial Exaltation is literally the divine presence of the Elemental Dragons, thus making all DBs the literal presence of the divine, and that the Immaculate Dragons were the children of the Elemental Deagons conceived by the Elemental Essence itself in their Exalted mothers. This helps them branch the state of Terrestrial Exaltation with the honoring of God-Blood in the older Pure Way.
