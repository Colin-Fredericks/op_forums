    With DBs, it's not as bad, because you probably went to one of the world's best schools anyway, so if your Tepet swordsman also has War 5, Lore 5 and Linguistics 5, it's not that out of place.

    Plus, because specialities count for DB excellencies, I tended to buy a lot of them (you get a 6xp per bp rate of return), so that meant the character was more specialised, rather than really good at tons of stuff that didn't really come from the character.

    My first Solar was amazing at loads of stuff his background gave little justification for, which made the other players tease me a lot about how I must have had an amazing library in my Yurt and a martial artist living next door.
