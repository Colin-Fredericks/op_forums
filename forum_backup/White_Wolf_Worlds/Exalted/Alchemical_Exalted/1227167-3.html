                    How would people feel about this setup:

                    All alchemical charms are artifacts with evocations. Like regular artifacts they have attunement costs and a theme based on material composition and function. Most of these physical 'charms' have attunement bonuses similar to artifacts.

                    Unlike normal artifacts they need to be installed surgically and they only function while attuned to an alchemical.

                    Champions themselves have the following abilities with regards to alchemical charms: whenever they drop an attunement, they save the number of evocation purchases they've made form the charm. Then when they attune a different charm, they can invest those same evocations into the new charm. For instance, if you're got a champion with Thousand Courtesan calculations with 5 evocations, you can drop TCC and attune Piston Driven Megaton Hammer instead and instantly buy up 5 evocations out of PDMH evocation tree. You'd essentially have a pool of purchased evocations that you can allocate to different charms.

                    Now this takes care of the problem of xp being sunk into evocations that are unavailable but what about the charms themselves? I have two possible ways to deal with this:
                    Either 1) alchemical charms are requisitioned from a VATS but do not cost xp to purchase or install. They'd be limited purely by the cost of attunement vs their limited mote pool.
                    OR 2) most alchemical charms can be installed without being attuned and you still get the base 'attunement' bonus but you cannot use any evocations form them and perhaps some charms would impose a penalty if not attuned if they're physically unwieldy or potentially hazardous or something (voidtech still imposes gremlin syndrome for instance, attuned or not).

                                                While access to every one of your charms is always nice, not having that feels like a very Alchemical thing. They balanced not having all the charms by making them dirt cheap. If an Alchemical can buy 2 charms for the price of one compared to all other Exalted, the ability to only use it half the time is a fair balance, and it was a unique thing to Alchemicals, much like Aura is now for DBs.

                                                Keep the charm purchase price cheap, 3 or 4 xp, and/or let it be bought with RP xp but not the installations you need to purchase.

                                                Keeping with the Evocation model, make them pay for each Alchemical Evocation, but make each one cost like 3xp vs 8xp of the great at it solars. Then if they switch out between two Alche charms sets, they had plenty of XP to purchase both to the level they need.

                                                            If we are proposing other options:

                                                            What if an Assembly can share charms but only install charms from others in general slots which cost more than dedicated?
                                                            Correlary: All Vats have a stock of spare charms you can install in general slots.

                                                            You buy evocation "upgrades" on a per slot basis and they are expensive. So you could buy up one slot to have a bunch of evocations/submods but would have only basic functionality in all your other trees.

                                                            Each "charm" is actually a mini charmset with evocations/submods and the number of slots and charms is sharply limited. You could use the artifacts are all unique to allow players to "customize" their example. So your PDMH might have plasma jets owing to its red jade while mine has health drain because of its soulsteel.
                                                            Possible Correlary: Charms are mixed material but evocations/submods do have material much like the mixed element DB trees. This affects install/attune cost. Perhaps you favor your base material and can favor another?
