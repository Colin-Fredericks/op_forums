                                            Solars get flight because, basically, without it your athletics guy would be hilariously chumped in many situations by anyone with a pair of wings. This isn't true for telekinesis -- it's not something you really need in and of itself.

                                            A few Solar charms might show themes you could call telekinetic, like weapons returning to your hand, thrown / archery bending to hit its target, or larceny grabbing stuff at long range, but I don't think you're likely to see Solar charms justified by telekinesis in the sense of "I can do XYZ, because I'm telekinetic"; instead, you might have charms that have "I can do XYZ, because I'm awesome at this" where XYZ is something that incidentally happens to be telekinetic.

                                            The closest stuff they're likely to have to general telekinesis would probably be in larceny rather than athletics. "Manipulate things without touching them" isn't an Athletics feat at all, but it could be justified as a feat of epic legerdemain and therefore fall under Larcency at times -- eg. "I make that thing on the other side of the room fall over to serve as a distraction" seems like a valid Solar Larcency charm to me, if slightly fringe. Even then, you would want to avoid the word telekinesis for the same reason that Solar Athletics charms can sometimes do flash step-style "I'm already there, right now" effects but usually avoid calling it a teleport.

                                            The other thing to consider is the various applications of telekinesis and what it makes you good at. If you have superhero-style telekinesis capable of lifting cars, crushing them into little balls, and hurling them over the horizon, that could be a problem in the Solar charmset because it doesn't fit well into their Ability-based paradigm -- that kind of telekinesis would make you exceptionally good at fighting at range, stealing things, attack, and defense, to a level that wouldn't make much sense in any one particular Solar ability tree -- Athletics is not meant to let you do Darth Vader chokes. Subtle "snatch small objects" or "create a distraction" effects, though, fit into Larcency.
