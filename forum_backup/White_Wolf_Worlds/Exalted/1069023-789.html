            Originally posted by ReshyShira View Post
            What situations are and aren't valid for a refund? If you do a working to bless a town's crops, but the course of the game will never have you return to that town again, are you entitled to a refund? What about other situations that don't involve the utter annihilation of the working (a destroyed town, magical construct, etc.)
            Determining whether a particular working is no longer relevant to an ongoing game is a decision that the Storyteller is always going to be in the best decision to make. Maybe that village was just an encounter along a journey; maybe it's becoming part of the fleshed-out setting of the campaign and your working is going to be crucial to its development. There's not a one-size-fits-all answer for it.

            Also, how often should a Limit Trigger be activating in a situation that would activate it? Some of then could be construed in such a way that they could be activated multiple times in a single scene (such as being insulted by a crowd of people).
            There's no "right" rate, although Storytellers shouldn't deliberately try to screw over players by atomizing one event into multiple Limit-producing activations. Unless that's what the player wants, of course.
