                                                            As others stated, just post the idea.

                                                            Originally posted by vampire hunter D View Post
                                                            I remember back in 2E it was stated there are no other worlds like Creation out in the Wyld
                                                            Define "worlds like Creation"?

                                                            Cemunian, Constellation of Sinew and Dreams was a Primordial who created it's own alternative-Creation, seeking to shove it over the default one. When the Unconquered Sun visited this false-Creation, he purified it via holocaust, annihilating it in a cataclysm of fire. Note - this does not mean it was dissolved into the Wyld.... but that a post-apocalyptic ruined world might exist out there, scorched beyond recognition. Cemunian was said to have fled to the Wyld (no note on whether it was ever slain - or simply created it's own pocket-world again, ignoring Creation).

                                                            Autochton, the Great Maker is another Primordial who famously has entire civilizations of humans within himself.

                                                            There are unknowns like the Star Spanning Serpent (which Merela climbed to reach the Daystar), the Clouds that Rain Teeth (which the Daystar could blow out of the sky), Hunanura, the Heart-Frost Unending (whose chill the Daystar could resist) and Mabhaddoth, Architect of Locks (who could close Creation off from the Daystar - but the Daystar could still spite it and return home). These are possible Primordials. In context, the Daystar & Sol write-ups explains how the Primordials made themselves antagonists to them - to test - each time more amazed until finally acknowledging and naming him "the Unconquered Sun".

                                                            Ramethus, of the Aftershock War, was mentioned in Dreams of the First Age - but for this topic, I believe they alluded to "at least one escaped Primordial" in addition to Ramethus. This could be (any of the above) or yet another unknown entity.

                                                            And if Cemunian's creation survived the "purification by fire", it's not hard to imagine another Primordial tripping across it and deciding "eh, I wouldn't build one myself, but I could totally refurnish this place to my liking".


                                                            As others said, golden rule it.

                                                            And the whole "Creation feeling small" and "authors intent" bit... decide if that matters to you. It's your game. Your narrative.

                                                            Yu Shan is fairly big, but it's never been more than "that cool place we'd like to wage a war of genocide upon" for Abyssal / Infernal campaigns. I'm sure lots of Solar games look at the Realm the same way (just one giant prolonged war-game of sequential slaughter). I think hunting in Szoreny's forests would be awesome, but I'm sure some players would only want to shatter/burn the mirror/forest down. Mileage varies.

                                                            And truth-be-told, the Underworld (with it's long-dead civilizations) is potentially vaster than Creation (imagine entire cities and roads and the like filling what - in the Age of Sorrows - are ruins or grasslands).
