                                A sorcerer, the child of a simple farming family, has spent weeks upon end repeatedly tilling and plowing one acre of farmland; on the four corners of this plot of land, the sorcerer has placed the statues of four divinities: the Sextes Jylis, Pasiap, Venus, and the Unconquered Sun.

                                The enchanted plot of land is absurdly fertile. If seeds are planted and watered before the sun goes down one day, the crops will be ready to harvest as the sun rises the next morning. Plants with a longer life-cycle may take proportionately longer; if apple seeds are planted, it may take a week or two before the trees are bearing fruit, but once the trees are grown they will grow a fresh crop of apples each morning. Crop rotation is not necessary, and while fertilizer and perhaps some bees certainly wouldn't hurt anything, the plants will grow just fine with only water.

                                Little does the sorcerer know that a local Guild factor has already heard about the miraculous field, and will stop at nothing to acquire the land (and possibly convince the sorcerer to make more) for a new opium plantation.

                                (I'm actually thinking this could be Solar Ambition 1? It might not be game-breaking in mechanical terms, but it would be kind of a big deal -- everyone needs crops grown.)
