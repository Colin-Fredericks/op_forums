    [Solar-ish] Which castes do you want to see least at your table?

    06-30-2016, 01:22 PM
    Exalted may not be Dungeons and Dragons, but often times we take some of our assumptions about Caste as though they were "class". So I decided to follow that line of thinking and came to an interesting question. In D+D, as a player, sometimes when another player comes into the game with particular classes you wince a little. Maybe because you think the class is broken? too weak and will "drag down the group"? That people who disrupt games tend to play that class a lot? Things like that. So lets port this idea over to Exalted.

    Which, if any of the Solar Castes, set off red flags or warning bells as a fellow player in a group?
    How about as an ST?
    What about other Caste-level choices in Exalted for other splats (using prior edition knowledge)?

    Some pre-rebuttal ahead of time:

    Yes, I know there are only 5 castes. Yes, I know that problematic behavior is not inherently isolated to Caste choices. This is a question of experiences and preferences. Yes, I know there is an aesthetic thing with the "perfect circle" so there is social pressure not to demonize any one caste. Even so, natural reactions to experiences interest me.

    Incentive is not permission or justification.

                  Well, in WW games in general, I get a bit nervous when someone wants to play something that wasn't on the menu, so to speak. Everybody knows the type.                                           Originally posted by Kyman201 View Post

                                          Well since a lot of us are sharing, got any really good stories on Problem Twilights?
                                          Sure, I didn't want to overfill the well early on with my own stories before people had a chance to respond and a tone-set had occurred. This might take a second to explain, but I swear it makes sense.

                                          So, a long time ago, a WW book, I forget which, once was going over "problem player types" and one of them was "The Wallflower". This is fairly standard stuff.. until they mention a caveat (to paraphrase): "...now if the PLAYER is portraying the CHARACTER as a wallflower, that is incredibly cool..." implying division between whether something is a problem depending on which angle you take it, with the understanding that it fits into the game theme, it's probably fine. It was a good lesson for me at the time and I took it with me moving forward.

                                          So how does this link to Twilight players and problematic behaviors? You're probably thinking "Twilight players are all wallflowers". Actually, no, it's the opposite! In my experience, the bad Twilight players I encountered had the problem of conflating in-character elements into their out-of-character behavior, while in-character their characters are fairly boring and drab and scene-stealing at the same time (large scale projects! Crafting scenes! Doing stuff to change the world because I'm SO DAMN SMART"!

                                          To clarify further, multiple of these Twilight players are, as players, a fount of lore, facts, and general hyper-specific knowledge about Exalted from an OOC perspective, and it's just ASSUMED as a Twilight, they know this stuff, and it is their "role" to act in this hyper-specific and confident manner about this minutae. Almost like the Twilight is the skin they wear to justify their particular brand of annoying gaming, like how Tot above described a Caste managing to play badly to the proclivities of the player. Twilight is the signpost for me that maybe this person might "love the game too much" and have no social sense about scene stealing or making other people's experiences worse with their authority, or wants to use the game to dramatically edit the canon setting with their projects and (importantly), that is the primary or only reason they play.

                                          I hope this made sense at all.

                                                          I find that there are 'bad sterotypes', but nothing that would throw up a red flag to me. It's more like 'if someone is going to play one of these, if they're one of THOSE players, they're probably going to play up on these stereotypes'; so these are just 'if I see someone saying that they have a bad player in this caste, I'm gonna assume it's this issue'

                                                          Dawn: Gotta kill Everything. No Peace, No Negotation. 100% Fatality Rate, kill, maim and murder anything that even slightly stands against you, never give a second chance. Generally gonna be the most 'Murder-hobo', will probably have just 'found' an Artifact somewhere and not really care about it. Most likely to have already killed a Wyld Hunt in their backstory before the game started.

                                                          Zenith: If the Dawn browbeats everyone outside of the group, the Zenith browbeats everyone in it. They play up the fact that only they get messages from the Sun and that this means they expect everyone to fall in line and listen to them immediately; their goals are the most important and they will never change their mind. Most likely to demand the recreation of the First Age. In 2E, expect them to favor Melee and other Combat Stuff and expect the Dawn to allow them to be their equal in combat. Equally likely as the Dawn to forgo redemption in favor of blowing the shit out of everyone.

                                                          Night: Spend as much time as possible hiding from everyone, including the party. The longer the party doesn't even know you exist, the better. Sneak around, try to spend as little time with the party as possible. Lie to the party and otherwise misinform them, be clear that you trust no one and nothing in the party. Often has some other 'boss' they're reporting to. Despite this, will often not actually DO anything; expect them to hide but only hide around the party, instead of going to retrieve information.

                                                          Twilight: Hide in the Manse until you're Essence Five, then churn out infinite Artifacts and built servitors to make everyone else obsolete. Ignore anything that isn't 'know everything about the First Age' and 'Remake the First Age'- tied with Zenith for the whole 'Recreate the First Age'. Most likely to demand trips to Malfeas to make pacts with Demons for artifacts or w/e; I expect plenty of Transhumanism and Industrial Revolution literature and stuff from them.

                                                          Eclipse: Social Fu everyone and everything, the same as the Zenith. Get into fights with the Zenith over who the actual leader of the Circle is, invest in Beaucracy and expect infinite everything to fall into your lap. Use Oaths on Everything you can find, go to Heaven and find Gods to learn whatever charms you can find from Gods, preferably the most overpowered ones. Secretly as munkiny as the Twilight but using the God Charms instead of Crafting stuff
