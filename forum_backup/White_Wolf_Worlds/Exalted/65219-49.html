        By 'blood' i mostly meant bloodline, not the literal blood (especially for the purposes of breeding), but this is an interesting question, so...

        A Terrestrial's blood is a powerful thing. You can't Exalt someone with a blood transfusion (though naturally it's been tried on many occasions) but you CAN empower them to an extent. Grant them some extra essence, maybe some powers related to the given aspect. Transfusing enough Terrestrial blood will prove too much for the mortal to handle, however, so a full transfusion is not recommended. One dragon-blooded can transfer their essence to another in this way. DB can transfer their blood to other Exalted types as well, but it provides heavily diminishing returns (and most Exalted types are stronger than individual DBs anyway) so the benefits are usually not worth it. It's rumored that the Scarlet Empress regularly employed this technique to maintain her power, youth, vitality, etc, though most believe it would only somewhat explain here power.

                One that I ran with for the game I'm GMing to free up some more interesting origin stories.

                Abyssal shards aren't all entirely handed out by the Deathlords. There are a few bouncing around like a standard Solar Exaltation, but their transmogrification by the Neverborn has changed them. Instead of being drawn to Excellence, the Black Exaltation is drawn to those who resonate with its Neverborn creators. People who, in the moment of their deaths, refuse to peacefully pass on and accept their fate. A slave, about to be slaughtered like a pig for some perceived transgression. A mortally wounded villager as raiders pillage and burn their home. A man confronting the fact that his terminal illness has finally come home to roost before his life's ambitions are achieved.

                These are the ones who curse their fate and mortality's inevitability. That refuse to accept that Death has come calling. They spit and rave that it isn't fair, and deep in the Labyrinths, something answers. Primordial beings who thought they would live forever, who hold nothing but spite and jealousy for the living and refuse to pass through to let their Creation move on without them. Whose denial of the natural order brought the Underworld itself into existence. It is at that moment when the wishes of the dying resonate with the spiteful denial of the Neverborn that the Black Exaltation finds one who would be its host, giving them power to throw in the face of their demise. The deal is struck. The Abyssal-to-be would do anything to avert their fate. And the Neverborn will give them everything to avert theirs.
