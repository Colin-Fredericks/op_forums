        Current game I'm ST'ing - its a 2.5 ed. game, with a few extra homebrew rules, set in the South. All 4 players are engineering students and had never played the game before.

        4 alchemicals (my hapless players) each manage to take just about three steps out of their vats for the first time, when they get plot-zapped to Creation.

        ...cue trying to figure out what the hell is going on, because they don't have that many memories of Autochtonia, nor do they have much of a clue of how or what Creation is.

        They meet some hesitant natives, turn them into friendly natives after showing what an iron hammer does to a bunch of fair folk and rescuing some kidnapped villagers, and then its off to the nearest big city.

        There they meet the Sultan, present the barely 'alive' remains of the fair folk lord they unknowingly stripped of graces during their fight. This earns them the Sultan's good will, granting them ownership of a particularly potent but similarly dangerous demense up in the nearby mountains.

        To construct their own vat complex manse there, they'll need cap-stones - who has those? Why, the realm does! Cue fun lil raiding party up north to Harborhead, where a realm legion is building manses because reasons.

        a bit of 'friendly' mind-control on nearby tribal chiefs starts a nice diversionary rebellion, giving the players room to make off with the cap-stones... or that would have happened, if not for one of the players being a prideful brawler who doesn't really do stealth, or the fact that assassinating the legion's commander made the legion's ranking immaculate monk (well, abbot) really mad and apparently he could actually fight back. This made the brawler and the rest of the players run off, luckily that was with the capstones - and they managed to avoid detection - allowing them to return 'home' with their booty... and with the knowledge that they are totally not the most powerful warriors or exalts around.

        Cue a time-skip for building a manse - which wasn't very long due to a friendly sorcerer they 'rescued' from the legion 'helping' by summoning demon workers. Only five babies nearly got killed because of that.

        with the vat-manse complex done the players blew most of their XP on attribute upgrades and new charms, plus they managed to get into contact with Autochtonia via a hack to the noetic charm network.

        ...and that's roughly where the game has reached so far

        up next is them being contracted by the Varangian city states to 'handle' a 'bandit king' known as "Admiral Sand" - he will of course give them a different offer, and they'll be meeting a 'villain' in the form of a fifth alchemical who has some very specific ideas of what should be done to Creation - and that alchy will of course demand the player's cooperation, otherwise its apostasy.

                                    So, my other campaign. I started running this at university when I was like 21, and 10 years later it's still going, though there were a couple of hiatuses. Recently, my friend has taken over, as I found running an Essence 5 game of Solars just too damn awkward (there were, at the time I stopped running it, no books beyond the Core and Miracles).
                                    It's a long game, so I might have got some chronology mixed up.

                                    Originally the group met in the Hundred Kingdoms. After some initial changes and deaths over the first story, the group settled into this:
                                    Brasidas, Dawn leader of a mercenary army
                                    Dupree, Twilight mad scientist
                                    Thothmet, one-eyed albino Eclipse sorcerer/necromancer
                                    Prince del Fuego, aristocratic casanova Eclipse followed by his faithful retainer, Bumbling Sage
                                    I'chan, Twilight Djala alchemist and archer on his mighty Tyrant Lizard, J'Quon.
                                    Babar, drunken Full Moon elephant-totem ex-Immaculate monk followed by his cult of nun-wives.
                                    Timid Silk Blossom, graceful rabbit-totem casteless and faithful member of the Cult of the Illuminated. Brasidas's Lunar mate (but her love was unrequited)
                                    The Ebon Prince Clad in Savage Crimson, deicidal Zenith from a desert monastery that worshipped the "Old Masters" and had a shadow-demon advisor, Zavis, the Black Flame.

                                    They wandered the Hundred Kingdoms, fighting an Abyssal circle serving Walker in Darkness, defeating a mad Dragonblood king, fighting the Wyld Hunt (and rescuing them from the mad King, who wanted to harvest the shikaris'... progenitive essence), and replacing hostile rulers with friendly ones.
                                    Babar disguised himself as Rabab, priest of Endings, and learnt kung-fu from a depressed Dynast, Cynis Danu. He then faked his own death at the hands of Babar the Anathema so as to motivate Danu to join the Wyld Hunt and make something of himself.
                                    Then they discovered that Ma-Ha-Suchi had invaded and razed Greyfalls, and was poised to invade the Hundred Kingdoms. They'd need to unite the Hundred Kingdoms to battle him.
                                    They went and asked the Confederation of Rivers for help, but the Confederation was too busy panicking over Walker in Darkness. They also met a rogue Abyssal, Murder of Crows, who became an occasional ally (with some unresolved sexual tension with Dupree).
                                    So the party said "let's just go kill this Walker in Darkness chap, then they can help us fight Ma-Ha-Suchi."
                                    They dropped a superweapon on Walker in Darkness's army, defeating that force.

                                    Brasidas and Prince del Fuego stayed in the Hundred Kingdoms, while the others left. Timid took I'chan to the Cult of the Illuminated training camp for a bit, and he kind of semi-converted.

                                    Dupree, Thothmet, I'chan, Babar, Timid, and a new Lunar, Silver Shark, went to Great Forks next, but found that Ebon Prince and his Infernal mates were doing a rock concert devoted to Malfeas, which unleashed a massive slave revolt. The PCs took some slaves to freedom, leaving Ebon Prince.

                                    They then travelled west to Nexus, along with another rogue Abyssal, Silver Shark's mate. On the way, Cynis Danu tried to murder Babar at his wedding, but was driven off. They stopped by at the Cult headquarters to find everyone had been killed or kidnapped by Walker in Darkness. Also, Sidereals used Shun the Smiling Lady on Babar and all his wives left him.

                                    On the way (I think, can't remember exactly when this happened), the PCs were taken to Heaven for the Calibration party. Babar got thrown in prison for brawling. The others went to see the Unconquered Sun speak; but Lyta and some other Solars turned up and threw Chejop Kejak's head at his feet, saying they'd killed Kejak as a sacrifice to him. But he was not amused, had them arrested, and chose the PCs as their judges (this was actually a test to see if Solars could be fair when judging other Solars). The PCs had Lyta executed, and her accomplices Eclipse-task-bound to serve Heaven for a set number of tasks each.
                                    Then they returned to Creation.

                                    In Nexus they destroyed the Guild's headquarters, and had all their artefacts stolen by Harmonious Jade in revenge, fought some Immaculates, and helped Murder of Crows protect a mysterious coffin (it had Lytek in, but the PCs didn't know that) from Walker in Darkness. The Emissary also turned up, and Walker killed him, but Murder of Crows escaped with the coffin, and the PCs also escaped. Also, a god turned up and handed Silver Shark his son by Siakal.
                                    Shortly afterwards, the young shark-boy was kidnapped by an evil nanny who everyone knew was a Sidereal except Silver Shark.
                                    As they were leaving, Bronze Faction assassins tried to kill them. They incapacitated half the party, and killed Silver Shark's Abyssal mate (or so he thought; actually they just knocked her out and Timid stealthily killed her because she hated her). Most were driven off, but during the fight, I'chan limit broke and insisted on an honourable duel with the last Sidereal. He stabbed himself to make it fair. Babar was not cool with a hot chick being killed by I'chan, so hit him over the head to knock him out. But Babar's very strong, and I'chan's head went splat.

                                    Silver Shark stayed behind while the others went south, and met another group of Celestials who they joined up with.
                                    So the group was now:
                                    Dupree, sarcastic Twilight mad scientist-craftsman and doctor
                                    Thothmet, one-eyed albino Eclipse sorcerer/necromancer
                                    Babar, drunken Full Moon elephant-totem ex-Immaculate monk and Dupree's Lunar mate
                                    Timid Silk Blossom, graceful rabbit-totem casteless and faithful member of the Cult of the Illuminated
                                    Janis, octopus-totem Full Moon
                                    Gentle Brook , Night caste musician and Silver Nightingale master
                                    Righteous Beacon of Unconquered Virtues, Zenith priest and teacher

                                    They travelled south, to take I'chan's body back to the Djala jungle, having some minor adventures, and clashed with Walker's forces again. Also, Beacon and Babar both kept trying to seduce Timid, who was not interested (I'chan had also been in love with her, but backed off because he could see she needed to get over some issues first). They asked her parents for her hand, but the parents stayed non-committal. Also, it turned out some of Babar's ex-wives were spies for Mask of Winters, and others for Walker in Darkness.
                                    Silver Shark ate one of Babar's ex-wives, and the party hunted him down and butchered him.

                                    The party met Prince del Fuego again, who along with some ex-Cult Solars, had been turned into Abyssals. Babar agreed (and was Moonshadow-bound) to talk to them peacefully. Then killed del Fuego. This caused Babar's one last wife to die horribly.

                                    Then Dupree, Gentle Brook and Timid were kidnapped by Walker's remaining Abyssals, and taken to a tower in the Labyrinth. The Night was turned into an Abyssal, but Dupree managed to resist the power of the Monstrance long enough for Timid to free everyone and escape.
                                    However, when the Abyssals came to hunt them down, they grabbed Timid's dad and took him back. So the circle went to the Labyrinth to destroy the tower and kill the Abyssals. They won, but found Timid's dad had become an Abyssal, Tarnished Pariah.
                                    Also, as the party was killing the Abyssals, they saw Bumbling Sage, del Fuego's servant, escaping on a demon-wasp.
                                    Then Murder of Crows turned up with some friends to take all the Monstrances.

                                    Having left the tower in ruins and killed most of the Abyssals (except two who escaped), the now-Abyssal Gentle Brook begged the party to kill him, and they made Janis do it.

                                    So, finally the party was finished with all the Abyssal stuff (and received a congratulatory message from Mask of Winters for having wrecked Walker's forces) and carried on travelling south. Thothmet, however, went off to do his own magical things, take over a manse, and set up a large humanitarian charity across the Scavenger Lands.
                                    He later created his own race of three-eyed dragon men.

                                    The party was now:
                                    Dupree, Master of Lunars and Butcher of Lookshy, sarcastic Twilight mad scientist-craftsman and doctor
                                    - , Full Moon gecko-totem assassin (I can't remember his name)
                                    Babar, depressed Full Moon elephant-totem ex-Immaculate monk and necromancer, Dupree's Lunar mate, in love with both Beacon and Timid
                                    Timid Silk Blossom, traumatised rabbit-totem Casteless Lunar, motivated to become a fanatical killer in service of the Solars
                                    Righteous Beacon of Unconquered Virtues, Zenith priest and teacher, in love with Timid
                                    I'chan, Djala nemissary possessing his own corpse, with his ghost-tyrant lizard familiar J'quon
                                    White Lion, northern Full Moon warrior

                                    They went to the Djala jungle, and set up base in an old ziggurat-manse, built by Babar's previous incarnation.
                                    Timid met and fell in love with another Lunar, Lysa Bloody-Claws, and together with Dupree they went and healed many members of the Jackal Tribes to gain their help. Then they went to Varang, and began to cause trouble, manouevre, and rescue Djala, while the Lunar assassin murdered police-caste and soldier-castes. I'chan demanded "let my people go!" Eventually the government of Varang let all Djala return to the jungle, where I'chan was hailed as a national hero.

                                    Lysa had to leave, and without her, Timid was too depressed, and went into hibernation.

                                    Beacon and Babar got together, but Beacon was still in love with Timid. He went off to try stopping a war between Harborhead and Varang, by himself, introduced himself to some Dragonblood Dynasts, and they killed him.

                                    The rest of the party teamed up with a rogue Infernal Queen and had some adventures south of the jungle, fighting other Infernals, some Abyssals of Eye and Seven Despairs, etc.
                                    Dupree did a mighty experiment to create a genetic virus that would change humanity so everyone would have Exalted Healing and Enlightened Essence, and unleashed it to slowly spread across Creation.
                                    (However, later the Sidereals stopped it, though it did change all the Djala, and random other people)

                                    But then they learnt that Walker in Darkness, and his two lieutenants (one of whom had destroyed Lookshy while disguised as Dupree) were planning to destroy the world using the Realm Defence Grid. They chased his lieutenant through the Labyrinth and killed him, then carried on to Stygia.

                                    So the party was:
                                    Dupree, Master of Lunars and Butcher of Lookshy, sarcastic Twilight mad scientist-craftsman and doctor
                                    Babar, depressed Full Moon elephant-totem ex-Immaculate monk and necromancer, Dupree's Lunar mate
                                    The Northerner, Night caste thief
                                    Mara, Slayer, cult leader, and queen of Irem of the Pillars
                                    Virtuous Viper, snake-totem Full Moon and Robin Hood-esque bandit

                                    They left Stygia for the Imperial City, watched the Palace of the Anathema, and saw Walker in Darkness and some Abyssals appear and fight their way inside.
                                    The PCs followed, fighting/sneaking past various traps.
                                    They fought some Abyssals, and defeated them, but the Northerner took an arrow in the face then fell 100 foot and died.

                                    They reached the antechamber to the Panoptican Apocalyptic, and discovered a whole bunch of dead Sidereals. Only one, Red Rust, who they knew previously, survived. He explained that they'd been betrayed by Sidereal Akuma of the Ebon Dragon. He also had several potions of Rune of Singular Hate, made in the Crucible of Tarim.

                                    The PCs reached the control room, finding Walker in Darkness feeding his last Abyssal (one of the ones Timid had once known in the Cult training camp) to the Sword of Creation to gain control of it. The PCs attacked... and he killed Virtuous Viper (an Essence 5 Full Moon) in one round.
                                    Mara, Babar and Red Rust all used Rune of Singular Hate on Walker, and he exploded.

                                    The PCs began to fight over what to do with the Sword of Creation. Babar used it to destroy all entrances to Yu-Shan with the World-Denying Solipsism Protocol, and Dupree shot it at the Wyld a lot.
                                    But Murder of Crows, Ledaal Kes, Ragara Szaya, Nellens Poromo and the Prophet of Falling Stars (who they'd heard of as a servant of the First and Forsaken Lion previously) turned up. The Prophet of Falling Stars revealed himself as the ghost of Chejop Kejak. He explained that Murder of Crows' master Mask of Winters (she'd been lying about being a rogue), the First and Forsaken Lion, Eye of Seven Despairs, and the ghost of the Scarlet Empress had teamed up with him to put the Neverborn to sleep by, among other things, severing the Underworld from Creation using the Sword of Creation combined with Void-Circle workings. To do so, of course, would involve killing everyone who lived in a shadowland, but he considered that a small price to pay.
                                    Babar did not, and a fight ensued.

                                    By the end, only Babar was standing (with the unconscious ghost of Kejak inside him), with everyone else either dead or unconscious, except a servant. Who revealed himself as Bumbling Sage, Fiend of the Ebon Dragon. Babar grabbed Mara and Dupree, and fled through a broken Portal of Infinite Passage.
                                    This left Kes to wake up, kill the unconscious Poromo, use the Sword of Creation to destroy a bunch of Shadowlands (but not all), and declare himself the Azure Emperor.

                                    And that was the end of the game.

                                        Timid Silk Blossom, beautiful swordswoman and fanatical devotee of the Cult of the Illuminated. (Casteless)

                                        There was a brutal campaign combining armies and magic against the Crimson Masters. Whole armies were destroyed, Fakel was wrecked, and several PCs were badly injured, but none died, and several Crimson Masters did.

                                        Afterwards, Enhenduanna returned to her mountain kingdom to continue magically improving it and consolidate her political control, Light and Loyal Fox stayed in Fakel to fix and run it on behalf of King Babar, and the others returned to I'chan's temple-city.

But he told his guards to kill Timid, so she could show off her stabbing skills, and Babar panicked, grabbed Timid, and jumped back to Creation.

The PCs survived, but had to tromp to a Shadowland and back.

                                        This of course, was all a plan of Jin the Just to get the PCs out of the jungle because he was fed up with their interference (in his plan to become supreme ruler of the Djala) and hoped without I'chan around, his friends would have now power, being non-Djala.

In the following fights, trying to defeat all the Imperatrix's other allies, Timid was knocked out, and Ahlat and 100 war-gods ambushed the PCs. Babar was permanently blinded by a burning spear to the face, and they lost track of Dupree.

They realised Bronze-faction Sidereals and Ledaal Kes had manipulated the whole thing so as to pit the Realm's enemies against each other, ready for Ledaal Kes's legion to attack later. Babar decided to leave so as not to attract the Realm to the Djala jungle. But once he was gone, Jin the Just announced that the council had exiled and driven out the evil debauched sorcerer Babar. Babar was so insulted that he came back and killed Jin, throwing Djala politics into turmoil again.
