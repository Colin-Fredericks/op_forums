                                            I'm always careful to check if my feelings towards a show like RWBY are motivated by my love of Exalted.

                                            The reveal of Qrow's semblance last season made me go "yes, this is what I want from Abyssals he's a cursed man with a sword/scythe who feels he needs to keep his distance from his loved ones" and I know I was disappointed when Raven didn't have a similar deal because among other things that's cooler to me than a random superpower lottery.

                                            So I initially felt I was being unreasonable for feeling this way, I liked Qrow but I shouldn't just want Raven to be a female Qrow after being in the background for four seasons. Then we got to Downfall (more on that in a bit) and I still didn't understand her, initially it seemed like Adventure Time's decision to portray Finn's dad as a disappointing fuck-up of a human being rather than give us real answers about where he came from or even give us another big damn villain which I also didn't like but respected after reconnecting with my biological father much later in life. Except that's not who Raven is either, she's clearly been watching over Yang for years clearly checking in enough to sweep in for the rescue against Neo, maybe she took a break when Tai Yang (I love how his name is Big Yang, since learning this I've wanted to start calling someone in my life Big (Name of their Child), but it's not stuck yet) moved on with Summer Rose?

                                            I'm also not saying she can't be conflicted, but her goals? They're boring and it's arguably even worse once we learn she's the Spring Maiden. If she had someone extremely powerful but inexperienced at her side then that banditry could be training, gradually building up to something bigger. Instead she's one of the most powerful people in the entire series and isn't really doing anything with that.

                                            But yeah I dunno, it feels like we could've given them both the bad luck semblance possibly as something Ozpin gave them or enhanced and have it turn Raven into a bitter misanthrope.

Don't die? if she was a "survive at any cost" type she wouldn't have picked up a superpower that is primarily transferred by murder. If she was truly a Nietzschean power-seeker she wouldn't hide out in secret and let the world pass her by. She's been running a bandit camp for more than ten years, but she doesn't seem to have a clue how to manage or manipulate people. She knows that Yang is desperately searching for her and her plan for convincing her huntress-daughter to join up is "how about stay with me forever and let your sister DIE", no deal, no plan, she doesn't even pull a "I'll help you to prove how much better an option I am then they are".
