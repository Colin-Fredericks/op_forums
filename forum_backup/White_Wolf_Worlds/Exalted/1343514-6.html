        I can't imagine telling an Exalted in the modern world (not our world, but an analogue) that wasn't already better told in Kingdom Come. (You could argue Watchmen, but then we'd have to knife fight).

        Personally, I'm fiddling with the idea of an Exalted in Chronicles of Darkness crossover that reads like something between Chrono Trigger (G-M as a Lavos analogue, obviously) and cosmic tier Unknown Armies. It's a silly-silly idea that mostly tugs at the heartstrings of my trench coats and katana sub-genre loving self, and it would be an absolute trainwreck, but I would pay serious change to run (or better yet play in) such a setting.

        As for the more thematic / meta elements, all this talk of how the Exalted would or wouldn't effect the setting (or our world) really comes down to a sociological perspective. Do you believe in the Great Man... eh, ideology? Do you take a wider assumption to societies? How far do you think supernatural aptitude could carry a person?

        Like, no three folks can agree on how society runs or what moral legitimacy means, but stortelling is an exercise in exploring these concepts and pinning down a narrative that at bare minimum gas stuff to say about these questions. Even if you disagree with the ST's views, the result can still be fascinating.

        Granted, that all comes out in the breakfast, not the sausage making process we're discussing...

                                I'm not quite sure what the discussions on god is about. I mean, I presume that we're talking about the modern setting, where we don't have the Exalted rpg. Why would we even call it gods? Look at our current religions. Christianity, Buddhism, Taoism, Islam.... none of them really have any mythos or religious rules for when people start glowing a certain colour and carry out miraculous actions. And atheism is of no help. I mean, I made an atheist exalt, and she has literally no idea she is a Copper Spider, Twilight Caste, Chosen of the Unconquered Sun, inheritor of a legacy a thousand years in the making.

                                And besides, let's be honest here. From what I can remember of my old Revelations sermons, before Christ returns to earth as the second coming, there is the anti-christ, who will basically take over the world. And I'm fairly sure the anti-christ is described as having supernatural powers. Which spells bad things for our Exalt if he believes in this.

                                I mean, even if all is fine and dandy, they don't know of the Unconquered Sun, the primordial war, or the Usurpation. Sidereals just start glowing and have nothing told to them by the Maidens. Lunars get a visit from the Witch-goddess, but I'm not quite sure how much she talks, or that she'll talk in this new setting. All in all, for all intents and purposes, Exalted do not know they are Exalted. They may say 'magic' or 'aliens' or 'psychic powers' or 'the devil!'. But 'Chosen of the gods Sol Invictus, Luna, and the 5 Maidens' is pretty darned out there.

                                            Originally posted by Chausse View Post

                                            Well, there's kind of an absolute ton. It's the world's most influential book (and of course, it's not even one book with one author, it's 66-72 books with authors seperated by hundreds or even a thousand years, and they're all different genres) after all, different cultures have interpreted it differently, and people within one culture interpret it differently. In Britain we've got about 7 or 8 significant Christian denominations, and a few different Jewish groups, but when I talked to Americans you guys seem to have about a million different kinds of Churches.                                             For the purpose of Exalted, you probably mostly want to look at Judges and Kings anyway, rather than most of the Bible. I found rereading Judges in light of having read about the collapse of the Bronze Age and the invasions of Sea people very interesting, as there's little references to it (like mentioning how everyone's stopped using the roads). You could use that as an inspiration for a post-Contagion world. And Kings works well for general Southern swords-and-sandals games.

                                            I would say though, that while one of my friends is doing a Noah's Ark thing with his game, and I've used references to Enki a bit, generally for Exalted the influences are more Chinese and Graeco-Roman mythology. I guess Solars have a bit of an OT-Prophet thing going on.
