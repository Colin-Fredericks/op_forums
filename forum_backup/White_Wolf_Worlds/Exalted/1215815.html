    Character advice needed (aka "How well do Immaculate MAs work for Solars?")

    05-06-2018, 12:07 PM
    Hello All,
    After a long time of running Exalted with 1st and 2nd Edition, I may finally have the opportunity to get into an Exalted game as a player again and work my way into 3rd Ed at the same time. But I need your help for some conceptual choices.

    I am going with a classic base concept: a Dynastic scion who joined the Immaculate Order and found himself exalting into the Zenith caste under yet to be determined circumstances. Most of the resulting drama and self-doubt will already be behind the character when the game starts and he now wanders the Scavenger Lands as an itinerant priest/monk, who does not outright detest the Immaculate teachings but sees the Immaculate Order as a flawed construct with many good ideas and good people - so his long-term goal is a reformation of the IO ;-)
    Short to mid-term, he will likely serve as a mentor and moral compass to a freshly exalted member of the circle ("No, you are not a baby eating monstrosity." ... "No, we do not have to kill everybody that saw your castemark - remember, *not* a monstrosity!")

    The character will likely be the fightiest in the circle (the other members being an Eclipse diplomat and possibly a Twilight sage), but combat is not the primary focus of the campaign. So I'd like him to be able to hold his own as a Kung-Fu Monk, but also branch out into Presence, oratory Performance and some utility on the side.
    Therefore I am now pondering which Martial Arts style to choose. With his background, one of the Immaculate styles would probably be most fitting, but I am not sure how well they work for Solars (being unable to use the Aura boosts of the charms) and which one to choose?
    Among the non-Dragon styles, Snake looks like a solid all-round choice, eventually complemented with a bit of Crane if defending others becomes a priority. Are the mundane versions of these styles even taught within the Order?

    What style(s) would you recommend in terms of style (i.e. coolness) and synergy, both with the native Solar charmset and with each other?
    What else should I keep in mind?

      The IMAs are still a solid choice for a martial arts style if you're a solar.               Earth Dragon + Crane is a really good combo thanks to the cheap penalty negation from Stone Dragon's Skin that you can't get anywhere else with MA. Crane also benefits from Presence for instilling intimacies for Humbling Enlightenment Commentary and Kindly Sifu's Quill, and overall has dibs on the "wise monk" archetype which your character seems to fit. It's also really good when paired with not-very-fighty party members due to its ability to defend-other, and going unarmored + unarmed gives you a lot of flexibility to branch out into other MA styles.

              Other than that core combination, I highly suggest dipping into White Reaper for Revolving Crescent Defense, which has a crazy strong defensive Mastery effect, and to invest into Resistance, at least up to Diamond Body Prana, to compensate for not being able to wear armor.

              Are you in the market for some Martial Arts? Perhaps some custom Artifacts for your campaign?

                      So as a zenith you won't have Supernal martial arts, and therefore I'm going to be concentrating on the low essence charms of the styles here. Air dragon at essence 1-2 has more utility charms than other styles, so it's more something you want to go for for a complete Kung-fu package. Like it lets you dodge attacks blind, run on water, dissapear in a flash of smoke, etc. If you don't want to take athletics charms and stealth charms and stuff but still feel like a ninja I'd go for this one. Combos well with snake for the evasion buffs.

                      Earth Dragon's low essence charms give you a solid soak based defence, and has a good foundation for the style's smashing attacks. This works especially well with heavy armour, so if that's a look that you don't mind you can get really hard to crack with it, but as pointed out it synergizes well with tiger that doesn't allow heavy armour. otherwise it's a great style to supplement any other unarmored MA because normally soak is hard to come by.

                      Fire Dragon has a good setup of fast punishing attacks that work really well with Steel Devil. They both use paired swords and fire dragon let's you apply onslaught penalties before the attack, not after, and steel devil loves racking up the onslaught. Also fire dragon has a good way of getting high initiative off the bat, which Steel devil enjoys.

                      Water dragon is interesting in that it kind of supplements everything. It has a charm that lets you use dexterity for damage, which is always great, it's got a charm that lets you flurry an attack and defence. Really all I can personally see for more synergy than that is form weapons. It's just solid.

                      Wood dragon is particularly cool though, since its form weapon is a staff, or a bow used as a staff. The thing is you can reflexively swap between bow and staff at any time, so if you take archery charms too then you can just flip to bow-mode and fire off a volley, and then bam, back to staff mode for a crushing strike. It's low essence charms make you very resilient, you can heal yourself and get some damage mitigation too. It combines well with anything that lets you absorb even more damage, which you then heal right back up and totally waste their turn and initiative.


                      Anyway that's sort of how each of them play out at low essence. I definitely think they're worth it for Solars, and especially if you're not a martial arts supernal. A lot of the mastery effects that would push other styles into probably better than IMA tend to be later in the trees.


                      Oh and also basically all MAs are taught in the realm. They like their Kung-fu. So don't worry about taking other styles.
