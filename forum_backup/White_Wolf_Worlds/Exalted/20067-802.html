    Originally posted by Grelkis View Post
    Is it possible for Fair Folk to survive in Creation without permanent damage to mortal souls?
    2nd Edition had the "Banquet of Crumbs" charm, which allowed a Fair Folk to subsist on ambient emotion, rather than draining any one person too badly. I don't know if it will make the transition into 3e. So far, none of the published Fair Folk I have access to have any comparable effect.

    Personally, I think it's probably reasonable to have something similar - allow a Fair Folk to "feed" off of strong ambient emotions. I feel, though, that for it to be strong enough, it should be a) coming from a reasonably large population of humans, and b) be strong emotion, not just the day-to-day passions and sensations. Bare minimum, it should be the equivalent of an inspire action (that is, emotion equivalent to a Major Intimacy), and affect at least 10 people, for a scene. While that sort of thing might not involve actually eating the humans' souls, it's probably going to leave permanent marks nonetheless. Imagine being in a state of terror, the equivalent of the climax of a really good horror movie, perhaps, for a hour or more. And then doing it again, at least once every day, and probably more often. You'd have PTSD, basically, except it's not even that "P". And even if it's not a negative emotion, there can still be lasting effects. Being driven into a state of euphoric ecstasy every day for hours is going to leave a mark too.

                                                Nilihum Sorry, I forgot I was reading the Ask the Devs thread, and answered your question. I didn't want to keep a non-dev answer in there, but I figured I'd throw this in here, just in case a non-dev answer might be helpful.

                                                Originally posted by Nilihum View Post
                                                Razor Dancer and Wise Steel are a pair of soulsteel short daiklaves with a suite of evocations that is, for all intents and purposes, a martial art style. Is it possible for an evocation suite to be a sidereal martial art style with all that that might entail?
                                                Razor Dancer and Wise Steel work by distilling a pair of heroes' personal fighting style into them. It makes sense to treat that as a martial art, but personally, I think it wouldn't work with a Sidereal martial art - while a regular martial art could simply be a style that someone somewhere worked out and taught to other people, a Sidereal martial art is much more significant. It's not just some combat manuevers someone put together that worked, or even an attempt to emulate the essence of some creature or being in Creation. A Sidereal martial art is the expression of a cosmic concept, in the form of a style.

                                                As such, I would need a very very good justification to allow such a style to be "encoded" in an artifact. It would definitely not be enough to just grab a practitioner of the style and melt her down into the artifact. I think I'd require, at minimum, that the artifact crafter develop a new Sidereal style, or at least be working with someone who did, and instead of transforming it into martial arts charms, somehow "encoded" it in the artifact. It would have to be a style whose cosmic truth was somehow connected to the artifact's form, as well. Since only they can create new Sidereal styles, you'd definitely need a Sidereal involved in such a project (or probably a Getimian - they can definitely use Sidereal styles "natively", and I'd guess they can create new ones too).

                                                Originally posted by Nilihum
                                                If so, would this require any special restrictions on the artefact, such as always being a certain minimum dot rating or being a first age artefact?
                                                I wouldn't allow this in a less than 5-dot artifact. First Age Artifice would only be necessary if the artifact could also teach the relevant style to a wielder (who would have to be capable of learning a Sidereal style).
