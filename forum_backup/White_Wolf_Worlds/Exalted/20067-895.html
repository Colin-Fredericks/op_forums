        Originally posted by Braininthejar View Post
        When counting soldiers in a legion, do officers count? (for example, a fang is "5 soldiers led by a seargant". Does it mean 4+1 or 5+1 ? )
        Legions are often under-strength (The Realm, Page 71): "These numbers are largely theoretical. In peacetime, as much as 20 percent of a legion’s strength may be unavailable due to troops on leave or detached duty. Numbers can drop even more precipitously in wartime, when there aren’t always enough competent skirmishers or recruits available to replace casualties."

        Sargeants (Fanglords) are NCOs (The Realm, page 70).

        Commissioned Officers are Scalelords and above, and they are not included in the count (Word of Vance: http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/m...e-devs/page177).

        A full-strength legion is 4000 rankers and their 1000 Fanglords (The Realm p73), plus engineers, plus officers, plus command staff, plus auxilliaries.

        A Fang is 5 (4+1).
        A Scale is 25+1.
        A Talon is 125+5+1.
        A Wing is 250+10+2+1.
        A Dragon is 500+20+4+2+1.

        A Legion is 4000 enlisted infantry, 1000 NCO Fanglords, 200 Scalelords, 40 Talonlords, 20 Winglords, 10 Dragonlords, and one General.
        Each senior officer has an aide-de-camp and uses dedicated messengers.
        "The general has a larger entourage — including aides-de-camp, valets, and a personal scale of elite bodyguards — and directs other experts such as chirurgeons, diviners, spies, and experts in local geography and culture."
        Talons and larger use musicians to co-ordinate troop movements.
        Each legion has between 750 and 3750 engineers attached to the fighting troops.
        They also have at least one attached sorcerer and "a handful" of Immaculates.
        There's also the baggage train which hauls the rations, includes the Quartermaster and "Smiths, bowyers, fletchers, and other artisans".

        Then you've got "four to six dragon's worth of skirmishers", which is adding another 2000-3000.

        A Legion is more like 7,000-10,000+ all in.

        Then there are Auxiliaries... but let's not get into that.

        [Edit]

        Minton below describes six to a Fang (The Realm p72). Which does square with the numbers on p73 so... add another thousand to the numbers above, I guess. Pretty sure this is a change from 2E, but it works so... 5000 Enlisted Infantry PLUS a thousand NCO Fanglords.

                                        Originally posted by autXautY View Post
                                        How do Craft and Martial arts interact with dice caps? Does a character have a &quot;Martial Arts&quot; dice cap, which is based on their highest martial art and applies to any martial arts roll, or a &quot;Snake Style&quot; dice cap which applies to snake style rolls but not martial arts attacks using weapon incompatible with snake style? Or something else I'm not thinking of?
                                        Your dice cap applies to the roll you are actually making and the pool you're actually using.

                                        If you have Tiger Style 3, Snake Style 2 and Single Point 5, then you use your Tiger Style 3 for your unarmed attacks. You're not capped at 2 because you are using a Snake Style Charm, but you're not rolling Single Point so you don't get to use it for your cap.

                                        The Crafts and Martial Arts don't work any differently to any other Ability. If I have Athletics 1 and Dodge 5, I don't get to add upto 5 dice to Athletics rolls -- I use the ability I am actually using. (Sure, that gets a little screwy when it means I can always run away from someone but can never catch them.)

                                        But no, you don't get to use your Snake Style dice cap on your Single Point attacks, and you can't just take one Craft at 5 to max out the Excellency on all your Craft rolls.
