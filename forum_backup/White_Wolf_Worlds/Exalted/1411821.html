      I've played several OWoD games and read a lot about exalted

      they are pretty much entirely different games, there was a point that Exalted was kinda tied in as the WoD prehistory but that fell apart pretty quickly

      the basic thematic difference in Exalted and WoD games is in Exalted you can quickly become a powerhouse that can challenge the very concepts that created the universe and in WoD there is a bigger fish just waiting to take notice of you and devour you in some way

                  I'll be honest... my feelings about the book are heavily biased by the fact that Holden subtweeted me on Twitter for not knowing what I was talking about when I pointed out to him that he left himself open to a legitimate lawsuit from WW for how he originally posted Ex vs. WoD... but within 24 hrs later took my advice anyway.                                   As I kind of understand it, it's written on the assumption that the Exalted are fully the Exalted in their power, and the World Of Darkness things are fully what they are. And the Exalted are limited by slow Essence mote regeneration.

                                  Then the default play mode that Holden envisoned, for Solar Exalted, which I think he described, was something like - "They gather power somewhat slowly, then unleash a badass denouement on a local bunch of World of Darkness supernaturals running a local scene, then they go to ground again. Rinse and repeat.".

                                  So if that's what you want to do, it might work really well! It seems like a pretty niche story to me, but if you were a WoD + Exalted fan running scenarios in your head about Solars whaling on all the nasty WoD things for the 15 years or so that the gamelines have existed in tandem ("Here comes the righteous justice of the Sun, blood drinker!"), it might well be your thing.

                                  If you want something more like "Marvel vs Streetfighter" in which somehow assumptions of the two universes are bridged such that Ryu vs Thor somehow really is a fair fight (despite Ryu being just this guy who is a kind of street fighter, you know), then "Exalted vs the WoD" is really not it at all!

                                                  Yeah, I may be underselling the raw powah of some SF characters here (particularly their "stuff I can do only in cutscenes and OVA!") and also there could be other matchups in that analogic frame that would better illustrate the point. General point being that some crossovers do things to the worlds being crossed over to kind of "balance" them, while ExVWoD really doesn't - it's very much "The Exalted against the entire World of Darkness" and not "Exalted meets World of Darkness".

                                                  (The latter being a thing which Holden could've written but I guess didn't because 1) he didn't want to and 2) the sheer obscurity of rewriting the mechanics and universe to affect tweaks rather than just plonking the Exalted into the WoD - as an Exalted fan I am of course into writers deciding to write *extremely* obscure and niche stuff because its an idea that must be written, goddammit, but that definitely means a lot of uncompensated labour among writers that absolutely no one is entitled to.)
