                                Really? Because it makes perfect sense to me. If I was a god, and had imbued some exaltations with my power. Then the other gods said to me; "hey, lets setup a system to auto-select candidates based on certain criteria". My response would have been something like; "The hell with that. I only have so many of these, I'm going to choose who gets them myself!" The Incarnae don't follow the same rules as we do. They don't even follow the same rules as the other gods. It's entirely possible that Luna is contemplating her next move in the Games Divinity, observing Creation for potential new Lunar candidates, and shining her light into the wyld as a beacon for Gaia (assuming that story hook is still a thing).

                                While the Incarnae may choose potential exalts as they wish, I don't believe they have a morality sensor, or good-guy/gal detector. They choose candidates they see some potential in, and exalt them as they choose. However, they don't have any special incite into the human soul, nor can they see the future (with some caveats for the Maidens of course). I really like that the Incarnae choose their exalts. I also like that, sometimes, those they choose turn into complete ass-hats. It would mean the Incarnae simply don't fully understand the human psyche. This to makes sense to me. How could a god, created at the beginning of time with all the power of one of the Incarnae, ever understand what it means to grow up poor, homeless, starving, and treated like living garbage! TUCS saw Havesh, and saw a cunning young man, who grew up in terrible conditions oppressed by the rich, yet he had the will to fight back. Any human could have pointed out that Havesh had never had a positive moral force in his world, and may be prone to answering violence and oppression with more violence and oppression. However, TUCS lacks that perspective. None of the Incarnae truly understand mortals. This makes their chosen exalts all the varied and interesting.
