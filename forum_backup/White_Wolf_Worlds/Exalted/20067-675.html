                                Originally posted by Siegward of Catarina View Post
                                Does anyone know if there are established social connotations/expectations with a Lunar's Tell? Particularly between two Lunars or within say the Silver Pact?

                                For instance, is a Tell something deeply personal? Something you might only tell a very trusted friend or lover? Or is it treated more as an open part of your identity? Would it be treated like a name in that it differentiates you from others that may appear similar? Or does it fall somewhere in-between? Or maybe there is no defined social expectation among Lunars and it is something that varies by individual. Or is a Tell something more abstract? Would a Lunar even be aware that they have a Tell?
                                I don't think there's canon info on the social status of the Tell. I believe that the Tell is known to exist, and I would assume that a Lunar can probably work out what it is, even if they aren't mystically told what it is. You're probably going to pick up on it when people keep going "Hey, that cow has the same feathered, golden eyes as that owl... and they're the same as that wandering warrior woman who came through a couple days ago and shamed the magistrate! Maybe they're all the same!". Basically, the Tell can be a bit more abstract than just "an animal or human part where it shouldn't be", but it's concrete enough that it can be explained and passed on to other people. Which means it can be conveyed to the Lunar, too. Bear in mind that various things can make it harder to notice, which suggests to me that the Lunar pretty much has to be aware of it - if you don't know you have a Tell, how can you make it harder to notice?

                                For the social stuff, this is how I'd handle it in my own games. First, Lunars tend to be individualists, so nothing said about any aspect of them will really be universal - there are always exceptions. That said, I would think that most Lunars have other Lunars who will know their Tell. The Lunars who found them after they first Exalted, their shahan-ya, and any current Circlemates are all almost certainly going to know, if only because they'll be looking for it. Whether other Lunars know will be a bit more iffy, I'd say. Probably, if a Lunar is sent out looking for another, they'll be given the details of the Tell of the person they're looking for. And I would tend to think that sharing your Tell with other Lunars when you meet and expect to be doing any shapeshifting around them would be pretty standard. It displays trust in them, and suggests that you're not planning to trick them with your own shapeshifting. A Lunar who refused to do that would probably be suspected of being up to no good.

                                I don't think that Lunars would regularly share others' Tells outside those circumstances, though. They know that they are involved in a war with people who have their own disguise and deception magics, and who would be very eager to get a list of clues to recognize them. Your shahan-ya isn't going to sit you down and say "now here's a song-list of all the Tells of various other shahan-yas in the Silver Pact, just in case you ever need to check on them". That's need-to-know information, even if it's not going to be extremely tightly controlled.

                                Edited to add:

                                Originally posted by Siegward of Catarina
                                I'm interested because I have a couple Lunar PC's in my game who are working together on routing a local city of Dragonblooded through subterfuge and debauchery. They're having some trouble communicating while constantly changing forms though and running around the city though, and were wondering about simply sharing their respective Tells to make recognizing each other easier.
                                As I just noted over in the Headcanon thread, a Lunar can always spend 1 mote to "Make her Tell apparent to all onlookers for as long as she desires." The way I read that, the duration is indefinite (and since this isn't a charm, that's not a committed mote or anything), and they can selectively choose which onlookers to stop making their Tell obvious to. So all the PCs would need to do is make their Tells obvious to each other but nobody else, and then they'd always be able to recognize each other, as long as they got a good enough look at whichever form to have any chance of spotting the Tell at all.

                                    There's also dead drops; agree ahead of time upon certain locations to regularly check on in which discrete messages can be left, in order to inform one another of plans, progress or places to meet face to face. Those messages might take the form of things being literally written (either encoded or sealed inside of some unobtrusive container), or objects with agreed upon meanings. Turn up to the dead drop and find a discarded bone that has had some symbols etched upon it, and you'll know your partner is engaged in killing somebody. Find a particular variety of flower with its bulb pointed towards the sunrise, and it means... probably something completely arbitrary, the best codes are actually things that don't employ clever symbolism that an outsider might suss out. The killing message is better off being something like a loaf of bread or something.

                                                    It's probably worth noting that it's difficulty 7 to notice that the cat you just found in your room has the exact same ragged left ear as the Lunar you fought earlier;
                                                    but on the other hand it's only difficulty 4 to notice that the bird on your window has the same white streaks on the feathers on its head as your good friend Wise Duke, the badger-Lunar who you know always has white streaks in his hair no matter what form he's in.

                                                    (Essentially, you get +3 successes to notice Tells when you know what someone's Tell is.)

                                                    I think probably whether Lunars tell their friends their tell is going to depend on them, and how paranoid they are. Certainly it's not something they'll want the Wyld Hunt to know.

                                                    Of course, if you spend a lot of time with a Lunar, it's going to be obvious. My Solar has often worked with a Changing Moon with a fox's tail; she's never told him it's her Tell (not sure he'd know what that is anyway), but it's pretty obvious what her Tell is.
