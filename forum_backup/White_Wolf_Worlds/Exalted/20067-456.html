                        Originally posted by MoroseMorgan View Post
                        A blood ape is summoned to go kill someone. Would they wait to materialize until they are upon their target? Seems a little sneaky for a combative brute type, but I could see either way.
                        ​Blood apes have a preference for being materialized, and will generally avoid it only if ordered directly, or if they judge it to be very necessary for fulfilling an order. It should also be noted that even when compelled to dematerialize, their form of it is incomplete relative to most other spirits, often still being accompanied by a stench, the sound of heavy footsteps and grunting, and maybe occasionally leaving bloody footprints; they can even sometimes still be glimpsed out of the corner of one's eyes.

                        ​I'd note that all Editions give blood apes halfway decent Stealth stats; they're not ninjas or anything, but they're capable of moving quietly if they need to. I personally extrapolate this to the idea that blood apes understand discretion sufficiently to make it a valid thing to order of them. If you bind one to guard a certain building in a manner that will not draw attention, I can see your general blood ape both understanding and being capable of fulfilling the need to approach intruders quietly and unseen, taking them out in a manner that doesn't give them a chance to scream, and then consuming the body without making a big fuss over it.
