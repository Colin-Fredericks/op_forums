                                        The Blades of Twilight from Arrixam's works. It's a weapon that can take the form of the most powerful weapon the user can imagine and imbue it with the power of Dusk and Dawn.

                                        The one that uses it in the Arryverse chose humanity as his weapon, and thus it could take on the forms of any human he has absorbed through his other powers. Besides the whole 'create doppleganger' aspect, he can also channel some of his other powers through his 'Dusk Blades', switch places with them at will, and have dozens of them active at a time.

                                        I could see it as a Soulsteel/Orichalcum + Moonsilver weapon, with a particular affinity to Lunars and Abyssals. I would probably need to limit the number of active Dusk Blades though, maybe remove active Forms from a Lunar's Heartsblood Libary temporarily as well as limiting the channeling powers option by the proxy's own Traits, but it can probably work well enough.
