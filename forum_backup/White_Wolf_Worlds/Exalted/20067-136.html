As far as the role of the exalt in those memories it'll range from a slightly altered view of the memory (the person remembers being saved from a volcano, but for one reason or another doesn't recall who did it) to a somewhat embarrassing lapse of memory. Your parents will remember having a kid just for some reason they can't recall the child's name, but they insist it's all on the tip of their tongue and not to worry it'll come to them. I'm operating off the idea that the check at least somewhat requires an attempt to remember the person, so a random person in the crowd with no connection beyond having passed the exalt in the street once is probably not making this check.
