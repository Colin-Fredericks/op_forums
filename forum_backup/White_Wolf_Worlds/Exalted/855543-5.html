                    Trying to think of something useful and constructive to contribute... Re: Charm sharing between Solars and Abyssals, it seems like one of those things you can't know what level is actually appropriate it is until you go through and look at it Charm by Charm?

                    More broadly I think the essential thing with the Abyssals, like all Exalted, will be for the designers to imagine what the Abyssals will actually be doing in the stories - imagery, scenes, stories - and then work from that to give them the powers. For example, if you start with seeing an Abyssal full of undead resilience, as the Things That Will Not Die in your mind and then work back from that to Charms, if they converge with Solar Resistance, then OK, but they probably (or at least hopefully) won't. Where their stories and scenes naturally diverge from Solars (which they should), they'll naturally get Charms and magic that supports that. If their stories and scenes and images don't then, oh well, try and think of more stories. Take a Storytelling first approach - if you start with the idea that they should do this, this, and this and not this, as an abstract principle, without thinking of the images and stories first, you're constraining your view of things and maybe you'd end up with "This is great, but what actually happens in their stories?" sort of result. The 3e Solar Charms seem to work so well for all the fans (who like 3e's mechanical approach) because they're specifically inspired by being able to emulate specific stories.
