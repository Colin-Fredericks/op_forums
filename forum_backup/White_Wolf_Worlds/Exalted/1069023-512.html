        Originally posted by Robert Vance View Post
        The significant benefits of gaining Caste Attributes and a suite of anima powers tailored to the archetypal role you choose for yourself.

        (on top of the benefits of the tattoos themselves)
        Does that mean that you get more Favoured/Caste attributes when you get tattooed (like in 2nd ed)? Or that Caste attributes are somehow better than Favoured attributes (as Solar Caste abilities are better, seeing as how one can be supernal).

        Because I've found in 2nd ed that changing a Lunar's XP progression halfway through their character, by them suddenly picking up a new caste attribute when they get tattooed, is a bit awkward. I think it can lead to people picking odd choices that don't seem to fit the character (you don't want to feel that you've "lost" the XP for buying something up before it was caste, when you could have waited for the cost to go down).


        I ended up just giving Casteless 4 favoured attributes, 2 of which become Caste (whichever 2 are from the same caste, of course) when they get tattooed, which avoided the issues of changing PCs' xp costs halfway through the character. Getting anima powers seems to be enough of an incentive to want to get tattooed.


        Also, on the subject of tattoos, will Lunars now be able to let themselves be modified if they want? For example, casting Invulnerable Skin of Bronze on themselves, or getting punched with Order-Affirming Blow to get rid of permanent mutations they acquired before being tattooed. Because that was a bit of a disadvantage of moonsilver tattoos in 2nd ed, and not, I think, an especially interesting disadvantage.

        (I assume the plan is also still not to force every Lunar in Creation to have the exact same style of tattoos. That always irritated me.)

                                                            Are there are any plans for Siderials and Getalids to have limited Lunar-esqe shapeshifting. I remember in 2.5e that the Sid's Resplendent Destinies were more weak essence-free or low essence cost one-off power-ups, than anything for their original mission of helping the Siderial integrate with society without a) being a complete stranger, b) being seen as an Anathema (per Immaculate doctrine, everyone with a Caste Mark is an Anathema).

                                                            I'm hoping that as Lunar Shapeshifting is developed, the system is robust and nuanced enough for Siderials to have human-shapeshifting or (think: job, status/role, fate/destiny, and appearance). I'd imagine a form if the Subtle Strike between factions is getting rival faction members assigned to cases that either requires them to adopt embarrassing roles/forms or are out of their specialties.

                                                            Some examples: A female Siderial is assigned a case investigating a fate error in the West. This will entail lots of travel by boat, and magic disguises do not fool the Storm Mothers. She needs, at the very least, a man's fate.

                                                            A Chosen of Battles needs to help an aspiring warlord win a series of battles. Except this warlord think he's hot stuff and taking advice from a pier is an unacceptable order. But, this warlord has a weakness for women. Our favorite of Mars needs the appearance of a woman, and quite possibly either the job or the or role of a courtesan.

                                                            A Relic dug up in the Scavenger Lands only affects children. To better learn how to the Relic, so to be able to turn it off, a 1000-year-old Chosen of Secrets adopts the fate and role of a child and thus becomes a valid target for the Relic's effect.

                                                            I could probably make more examples, but I'd rather not waste your time going on. I hope the rather lackluster Resplendent Destinies of old can be something fun and exciting in the future
