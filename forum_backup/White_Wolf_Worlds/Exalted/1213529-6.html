                                    Thinking on it, it would probably be worth writing up a short summary of “This is what your character knows about the setting” for each character at the beginning of the game. It wouldn’t need to be long, just a paragraph or set of bullet points about the local area. It’s probably more useful to new players than trying to cram the entire cosmology down their throats all at once and, if they’re experienced players, that can help them play more accurately so they don’t have their dude from podunk nowhere rattle off the history of the Primordial War without thinking about it.

                                                    Lukkychukky re: power levels, there are some brief guidelines in the Antagonists section when it comes to dicepools and competency, but one thing that will greatly help in judging a combat's difficulty will be some basic dice math. With a target number of 7 and double 10's, a dicepool has an average success rate of about 50%, with a slight swinginess upward. Depending on how into probabilities and statistics you are into we can pull in charts and stuff, but even without that you can know roughly how easily someone can hit or be hit based off that number. As pools get higher, most of the dice tricks add about another 5 or 10 percent on top of that.

                                                    If you feel like playing with it, https://anydice.com/program/1046 plots the basic probability of 10d10 in Exalted. If you select "At Least" you'll see percentage chance of success for a given value.
                                                    So you'd see that 75% of the time, 10d10 will get you 4 or more successes.

                                                    So you can kinda SWAG some stuff based on that.

                                                    PneumaPilot Not to be the loathsome forum canon police, but in all editions Creation has always firmly not been the body of Gaia. Her and Cytherea did some sort of primordial crazyness and created it, and she has some strong ties to it given that the Elemental Dragons are her children, but it isn't a similar situation to Malfeas or Autocthonia.

                                                    Really digging the show guys, and I should have a question recorded for you soon! And it won't be forum nitpickyness, or moderately advanced math based!
