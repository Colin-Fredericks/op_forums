            True, but the thing is with what I meant it wasn't that you characters couldn't have a cultural background to explore, it is that there isn't one which they can interact just because there a Solar. Theres nothing stopping the other splats from coming from those backgrounds as well, though its a bit harder for some then for others, but still you can be an Outcaste DB, or an Independent Lunar, they can come from those same backgrounds. And yet theres always the option to be part of something more, of cultures that it is built just for and revolves around them. Going by what you pointed out yeah the Solar's probably can get the most out of normal backgrounds because they don't have such extra baggage so you can fully develop their original cultures more, but it also makes them as a splat straight out kinda directionless, you can't find generalizations between Solars, they all have to figure out their own motivations. Now while that does fit in with Solars themes quite nicely I do have to admit that it does make them seem not that interesting compared to the others. In that when looking at them I can't really see any depth so I have trouble when comparing them to the others, who all have cool quirks and cultural structures that makes them feel like a part of the world and the setting. While it makes sense for Solars to be as they are given their lore and history and I repeat that it absolutely makes sense, it doesn't stop them from feeling out of place when compared to everything else. Maybe it's just a matter of appeal because I know that if you wish to explore the various other cultures in Creation that isn't under the heel of an Exalted Solars are the best picks in that and thats probably what they are designed for and they do great at, still the wish of seeing something just Solar dependent like there is with the other Exalteds is something that appeals to me, so when I wish to do delve into that the best choice I see without contradicting anything in the setting is to use the First Age, which I found fulfilling for that need. So I admit feeling a little down on the fact that First Age is becoming more ambiguous despite me largely agreeing with the Devs on the matter, but hey maybe that is just the appeal of Solars and I'm just not getting it that well.
