    Thoughts on chapter 1 and 2 of volume 5.

    The way these two episodes have kicked off the new season has really managed to pull me in. I wasn't that hyped before, but now when they've made it clear where the volume is going, I'm very interested.

    To begin with, both episodes have been over 20 minutes. That alone makes it much easier to invest in.

    Also, related to that: tempo. RWBY has never been a particularly fast show; even if the pacing with the set-ups and payoffs have been decent most of the time, each season is usually dedicated to one big thing happening in increments. That's why I'm pleasantly surprised by the sheer amount of key stuff that's already happened. Qrow and team RNJR have set a target, Weiss has arrived and been captured in a way that progresses the reunion of team RWBY and Yang is actively seeking out her mother in a manner that also progresses that reunion. Not only that, we see action on the part of the villains, with a recuperated and highly vengeful Cinder among other things. And, of course, the deposition of Sienna Khan as leader of the White Fang. I mean, all that could have been most of a volume previously. It's the second chapter! It all has a feeling of momentum and a motion towards a climax that's really pulling me along.

    Next point. Man, they stepped up their animation work. That's happened every volume, don't get me wrong, but they seem to have gotten much more familiar and comfortable with the Maya engine, and it's really showing. Yang's character short combat scene was fluid as hell, and Weiss' battle against the Lancers was some of the best I've seen her style looking, well, ever. I'm also really a fan of them working 2D images in; they're gorgeous and so much better than than stock 3D assets.

    That said, nooot to keen on the cartoon physics on that one bandit Yang clocked. I'm all for bringing funny back to this series, but that attempt felt like a swing and a miss. The context was much to badass for that to work.

    All in all, volume 5 feels very different from volume 4. I was afraid it wouldn't, but it's clear that CRWBY was working very deliberately to create these different feelings. You can hear it in the title songs as well: For season 4, we get "Let's just live", and the season deals with the aftermath of trauma and very human and personal things. This season, the first lines we get are "Back to the fairy tale", and the tone shift couldn't be more evident.

    I'm in this. Let's go.

                                        To me RWBY was simply a choreography show, a kind of expansion over the Dead Fantasy series. Plotwise, the first season didn't catch my attention really, I found it sub-standard but I kept watching because of the cool ideas, weapons and fights in the show. Although third season I though was a step in the right direction.

                                        Later seasons, in my opinion, have become... a little more "pretentious". Last season, and possibly the fifth one too, have become pretty boring for me to watch. RWBY's plot isn't something never seen, or very deep really, but I keep getting this feeling that the creators suddenly decided to put focus on the intrigue and different plotlines. Honestly I never saw the lore and story of RWBY as nothing more than an excuse for more coreographed fights to happen.

                                        Last episode I saw had this chase between some hornets and one of the protagonists. Argh... boring as hell... maybe in the old days the scene would have contained some high action ice-skating-rapiere-girl-stabbing the hornets with grace and power... Now we got a generic "throw-them-some-crates-and-make-them-explode-approach" (nice name for a combo though). It felt like I was watching some ordinary low action level kids-cartoon show.

                                        I may be a minority here, which I don't mind really, as I see that most people still enjoy RWBY. However, for me this has become a shadow of what I found interesting. I don't care about the knight guy, unsufferable thor-chick, the mute ninja, or even the cat-person. I just enjoy when they are well animated and kick somebody's ass.
