            Alright, so!

            A friend of mine asked me to help put together a Reaper Daiklave, Orichalcum + Starmetal (+ Black Jade?), to be used with her Single Point Shining Into the Void style. Concept: Singing sword.


            What I've started on so far is the attunement evocation and one of the actual evocations (maybe first, maybe later). As always, I'd really appreciate any advice and suggestions with where to go.

            First up: Attunement!


            Evocations of [Unnamed]
            A Solar or Sidereal attuned to [Unnamed] unleashes the singing spirit of the blade, whose ethereal voice sings to the mind and soul of its wielder with enchanting power. As the Exalt earns an advantage, the spirit’s voice shifts within her. The following are the weapon’s Aria levels.

            • Sotto Voce (0 or fewer Initiative): +1 non-Charm bonus to Parry.
            • Agitato (1-4 Initiative): +1 non-Charm success to rush actions.
            • Subito (5-9 Initiative): The character may aim reflexively for a withering attack.
            • Bravura (10-14 Initiative): +3 bonus to Parry.
            • Trionfante (15+ Initiative): The character’s aim actions also convey the benefits (but not costs) of a full defense action.

            The Aria levels are given quantities 1 (Sotto Voce) through 5 (Trionfante) for the purposes of evocation calculation.


            As is pretty clear, the inspiration was drawn directly from Moonlit Huntress (pp. 617-18) and, for the last bit, Shining Ice Mirror (pp. 618-19). For reference in power comparisons:

            Evocations of Moonlit Huntress
            A Solar or Lunar attuned to Moonlit Huntress gains a blessing from the weapon, which fluidly shifts depending on her standing in the ebb and flow of battle:
            • No Moon (0 or fewer Initiative): +1 non-Charm success to Stealth rolls.
            • Waning Moon (1-4 Initiative): +1 non-Charm success to disengage actions.
            • Half Moon (5-9 Initiative): +2 non-Charm successes to Awareness rolls.
            • Waxing Moon (10-14 Initiative): +3 automatic successes to rush and disengage actions.
            • Full Moon (15+ Initiative): The character may aim reflexively from up to long range for a decisive attack. Doing so grants no aiming bonus.


            Evocations of Shining Ice Mirror
            A Solar who pays two extra motes to attune to Shining Ice Mirror may partake of its perfect balance and discerning spirit so that an aim action taken with the intent to strike with the blade also conveys the benefits (but not costs) of a full defense action.


            The latter (Shining Ice Mirror) is thematically perfect for the martial arts style, and potent enough to warrant an additional +2 attunement cost. However, in synthesizing this and Moonlit Huntress' tiered-attunement system for the singing concept, I figured that by putting it last (and requiring the most Initiative) would perhaps balance it out (at least compared to reflexively-aimed long range shots).

            Honestly, though, I could use suggestions regarding all of the bonuses. I tried to convert them as equally as possible (1 auto-success = 1 static boost = 2 dice), but even then I'm not quite sure if they work thematically. What might work better than rush? Should I grant ability roll bonuses instead? Etc.


            Next up, though, here's the first actual evocation I've developed for it. As you'll see, I'm going for a bardic type of plan, with some of the evocations being maintained songs or connections.

            Haunting Refrain
            Cost: 3m per turn; Mins: Essence 1
            Type: Simple
            Keywords: None
            Duration: Indefinite
            Prerequisites: None

            The wielder ushers forth the spirit’s voice from the blade, establishing a mystic link to her target so he hears the blade’s unsettling song. The wielder rolls [Essence + Aria] against her target’s Resolve. If successful, the target hears the refrain at full volume, losing Initiative equal to half the weapon’s momentary Aria (rounded up) at the beginning of his turn, for as many turns as she maintains the link. If the roll fails, the target only hears the murmuring voice at the back of his mind; the roll repeats every turn the evocation is maintained until successful.

            This evocation can target multiple foes, with the cost per turn increasing by 2 motes for every additional target.

            This evocation can target battlegroups, with the cost multiplied by Size (minimum 3m).



            Immediate concerns: Cost?! Initiative loss amount?! Understandable concept?!?! And so on.

            Hella PEACH, yo.

                                    Noticed a lack of Sailing artifacts so I thought I would try one out.

                                    Skidbladnir, Scepter of the Dreadnought-Blessed (Artifact ●●●● Green Jade Goremaul)

                                    Legends of mighty warships are ample in the great oceans of Creation, what many fail to realize is that many of these legends refer to the work of the same weapon. This ancient Goremaul was created for an ancient admiral from a time before Shogunate who wished to grant the vessel that had served him faithfully in his mortal life the power to rival the finest warship from amongst his glorious navy. Since that time this Scepter has passed through the hands of hundreds of wielders who used it to elevate many mortal vessels to glory, from massive capital ships to impossibly nimble coursers. Most recently Skidbladnir has come into the possession of a successful Outcaste reaver based near Wavecrest who has recently begun to prey of Guild shipping.

                                    Skidbladnir appears as a Goremaul of considerable ornamentation engraved in with ornate silver inscriptions retelling the epics of its past masters. The majority of the weapon is an alloy of darkened Green Jade with a shaft wrapped in the tanned hide of a long since extinct species of God-Blooded shark.

                                    Evocations of Skidbladnir

                                    Attunement: Attunement to Skidbladnir is only possible if the wielder already owns a vessel (granted this claim need only be enforceable and not necessarily legal). Attunement automatically unlocks Majestic Warship Ascension.

                                    Majestic Warship Ascension
                                    Cost: - Mins: Essence 1
                                    Type: Permanent
                                    Keywords: None
                                    Duration: Permanent
                                    Prerequisites: None

                                    The Captain raises her weapon into the air and conjoins its soul with that of her ship, the vessel groans as planks broaden, twist and fuse into a single being of living wood. The vessel’s appearance transforms into a masterwork the likes of which has not been seen since the death of the First Age, details of the ship’s form are left to the Storyteller but ultimately fit the soul of its master.

                                    This Evocation grants the following benefits to the vessel until it is destroyed or the master dies, at which point it would fall apart, dying with its owner. Firstly the vessel now regenerates lost Hull Points at a rate of (Essence) Levels per month. Secondly the vessel no longer requires a helmsman with the master controlling it reflexively via the Scepter as long as they remain within (Ex10) miles of the vessel. Lastly the vessel gains (E) permanent points to the highest attribute of the ship between Hull Points, Maneuverability and Speed and +1 to the remaining traits. Only one vessel may be blessed with Majestic Warship Ascension at a given time and the wielder gains a Major Tie of affection for the ship.

                                    Plank-Feast Assimilation
                                    Cost: 6m/Hull Point Mins: Essence 2
                                    Type: Simple
                                    Keywords: None
                                    Duration: One Scene
                                    Prerequisites: Majestic Warship Ascension

                                    To mar the glory of the Blessed-Vessel is a madman’s endeavor for it will devour the vessels so foolish as to stand against it. This charm may be activated after successfully incapacitating an enemy ship, regaining a Hull Points at a rate of 6m for each level restored. This charm cannot regenerate more levels than the incapacitated ship had to begin with nor may it regenerate more levels than the Master’s Essence rating at any given time. The Incapacitated vessels are destroyed as they are ripped apart by the power of Blessed.

                                    Faultless Hull Invigoration
                                    Cost: 8m, 1wp Mins: Essence 3
                                    Type: Simple
                                    Keywords: None
                                    Duration: One Scene
                                    Prerequisites: Plank-Feast Assimilation

                                    The power of the Scepter commands the mighty ship to stand steadfast against those who would fell it. For the duration of the scene the Blessed-Vessel gains (Essence) hull point levels which explicitly allow the vessel to exceed its base number of hull points. The hull points gained in this fashion cannot be damaged by conventional anti-ship weapons or ramming actions (this protection does not extend to vessels composed of First Age alloys or broadsides employing Sorcery or First Age weaponry).

                                    Masterful Flagship Transmutation
                                    Cost: 16m, 2wp Mins: Essence 4
                                    Type: Simple
                                    Keywords: None
                                    Duration: One Day
                                    Prerequisites: Faultless Hull Invigoration

                                    Embracing its fullest potential the Blessed-Vessel undergoes a glorious transformation to achieve its truest potential. For the duration of a single day the Blessed-Vessel enjoys a bonus to its Maneuverability and Speed equal to its Master’s essence rating. Furthermore the costs of Faultless Hull Invigoration and Plank-Feast Assimilation enjoy a 2m discount for as long as this charm is active.
                                    This charm may only be reset by Calibration as the strain of its transformation would destroy the vessel if used more than single time in a year.

                                    Special Activation Rules: Upon learning this charm the Master may choose to pay 8xp to gift true life to her beloved ship. The vessel becomes a 3-Dot Familiar which is only capable of Sail-based actions (for the purposes of which it possesses a Sail rating of 4, Intelligence 3 and a Wits 4) with a Defining Tie of loyalty towards its master. Although it may evolve over time the vessel will initially only possess an intellect akin to a very loyal pet and only understand simple commands and instructions unless controlled via the Scepter.
                                    A final benefit of this charm is that the familiar may choose to forcibly activate Masterful Flagship Transmutation for a second time within a year to protect its master but this course of action will result in its destruction.
