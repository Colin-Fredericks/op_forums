    It suddenly occurs to me how strange Lunar Castes are when compared to the other Exalted.

    ​When the Caste of a Solar is indelible and present from the outset, it's really just a description of who the character is, rather than something that they benefit from. Anima Powers go alongside that, but I don't get a sense of that in terms of something that they can't do without as just... an addition to the description.

    ​But when you have Lunars, who form their Castes some time after Exaltation, and even reformed them in response to an event, it does create a certain expectation of there being something useful or necessary to having a Caste. With the description of "these Castes better suit them to survival in a hostile new world"... if that had to be expressed through Anima Powers, it would seem to me that those powers would need to be a fairly big deal. When assessing Caste Abilities or Attributes, I still feel as though that's a largely descriptive thing, and not solid as a foundation for defining an in-character draw to a Caste. I mean, even if it could... they've only got so many Attributes, and it does raise a question of why any given combination that is slightly easier to become better at would be less suited for survival than all of the physical or mental ones bunched together.

    ​So I propose... that in a future mechanical setup, a major part of actually defining a Caste is not how you spend the XP, but how you earn it; their form of Lunar XP. A major importance of Caste would be in the patterns of behaviour that define you as a member of that Caste and get rewarded with additional XP to greatly improve upon the structural development of characters (representing an in-character sense of stability and wellness), that in their current situation would be largely uncapitalised upon by the old Castes.

    ​With this assumption, for Castes to be made to not matter much, one would go with a singular for of Exalted XP that rewards general Lunar behaviour while being unconcerned with Castes. If the addition of a Caste only gave a Lunar the Caste Attributes and an Anima Power, I would already view that as not mattering very much for them.

                                        But anyway, I'm happy for them to have anima powers. It was never a problem before, in 2nd ed (and honestly, I felt they worked better than the Solar caste powers, because they mostly fit better... well, except for the No Moon one, which was pretty rubbish. But it fit better than the Twilight power!).

                                        If the Caste Effects aren't powerful enough to be about this useful, I don't think the Lunar Exalted would have bothered breaking and replacing their First Age Castes. Or, for that matter, making the First Age Castes after winning the Primordial War.
                                        While perhaps true, I think the main point is giving Lunar PCs powers that are cool, rather than justifying something that happened in the setting ages ago and could be justified some other way.

                                        Unless you were to divorce favoured/caste attributes from caste entirely, I guess. Which seems to be a little what you're suggesting?
                                        (Though is that a bit... too free? I don't know. It's probably fine.)

                                        But I'm not sure that, it therefore demands that the Caste's main benefit is how you get Lunar XP. Tbh, I think it's a bit restrictive (which is not what Lunars are about). I mean, I don't detest it. But I don't really see the problem with the main benefits of the caste being the cool/useful anima powers you get.

                                        And as Sunder says, it screws the Casteless Lunar a lot when they're getting less XP.                                                 • For five motes, the Changing Moon can adopt a subtle nature that seems to make them inherently trustworthy; while impersonating another being, any Manipulation rolls made by the Lunar to perpetuate the belief that they are the being they seem to be may lower the target's Resolve by the Lunar's essence, unless they are given reason to disbelieve the Lunar's subterfuge, in which case they may spend Willpower to cancel this effect.

The Night Caste of the Solar Exalted have that as an anima effect because Larceny and Stealth are their hallmarks among the Solar Exalted. But all Lunar Exalted can use Heart's Blood to assume effectively perfect disguises even without Charms. The conceptual distribution is extremely different.

                                                This is better by far than their original power, even if it runs into the same core problem of being sorcery-specific.

                                                    Caste Attributes: Strength, Dexterity, Stamina.

                                                    For 5 motes, the Lunar may add half her essence (rounded up) in dice to all movement pools and feats of strength (both the dice pool and the minimum strength for particular feats) for one scene.                                                     For 10 motes, the Full Moon may reset all Strength, Dexterity and Stamina charms with outstanding reset condition.                                                         Well, by Immaculates. They don't generally call themselves that. It's deliberately dismissive.
                                                        I suspect in practice physical charms will have more rage-based themes than others (though not entirely: there may well be a good few social ones and I can see it in Wits for their social defence charms), but, if it's a pan-Lunar theme, I don't think the Full Moons need anima powers that express it more than others.
                                                        Not saying they can't, either. Just that they don't need to.

                                                        Saying this, I think these are reasonable summations of the castes that you've written here:
                                                        Originally posted by Arian Dynas
                                                        Full Moons...barbarians... hunters and predators...
                                                        ...
                                                        they're not just great speakers and skilled orators; that's the vein of the Eclipse and Zenith castes... the Changing Moons are *tricksters* they're shysters and con-men
                                                        ...
                                                        And the No Moons? They're a war of a different kind; a spiritual one. A mental war. They plumb secrets and mystical knowledge from intentionally dark and hidden places. Yet they're also most in touch with the spirits themselves. Priest and sorcerer all in one; a shaman. The wise man and witch-doctor.
                                                        There's a couple of things here that I didn't really like, but thinking about it I've changed my mind.
                                                        Manipulation charms are about trickery. Charisma charms are generally not (though they could be, in a different way). So I was resistant to Changing-Moon-as-tricksters. But then, if not, what are they? Of course Changing Moons are Tricksters! And everyone I know who's played them has been at least a little tricksy (whereas Zeniths generally are not... even Eclipses tend to be less tricksy than Changing Moons), even if some have embraced that far more than others.
                                                        So I'm happy for them to have trickster-based caste powers.

                                                        The No Moons... yes, I think that's exactly what they are. I like "they plumb secrets and mystical knowledge from intentionally dark and hidden places." I think it's a)appropriate for Lunars, and b)a bit different from Twilights.
                                                        I was resistant to No Moons being too occult, because what if you want to play the polymath, the general genius (you are, after all, the caste that's about Mental Attributes, not just sorcery). But... that's kind of what Twilights do. And it does fit with the Moon-symbolism.

                                                        So, let's get to the actual powers.

                                                        Full Moons
                                                        I think their 2nd ed anima power was actually pretty fine, it just needs mechanically updating. I don't like "double their strength" or whatever, because anima powers are generally essence-based.
                                                        So I'd suggest something instead like:

                                                        For 5 motes, the Lunar may add half her essence (rounded up) in dice to all movement pools and feats of strength (both the dice pool and the minimum strength for particular feats) for one scene. At bonfire/iconic level, this ability is activated automatically at no cost.

                                                        Basically the same conceptually (not sure if it's a bit weak, maybe it should add your whole Essence).

                                                        What other powers though? The one you suggested about silver fire-damage vs people they hated seemed too much like a charm. The other one had no rules. And using latent powers of shapeshifting is too messy.

                                                        They could have something about resetting initiative like Dawns, or a reset (not combat powers, but physical attribute charms generally, so it could also be used on non-reset stuff). Or intimidation!
                                                        But those aren't very striking. I'd like at least one thing that's a bit more flashy. But then, Dawns don't have very flashy powers I guess.

                                                        I'd also like, as Sunder said, their powers to not be too based around combat. The nice thing about their 2nd ed power (which was not very good mechanically, but was good thematically) was that it wasn't actually about combat, because Full Moons are in practice the best warriors generally, but that's not their theme: their theme is their physical superiority. Strength, speed, stamina, not skill with swords.
                                                        I think 1 of 3 being a combat power is okay (as long as it's clear that it's about their physicality, not skill: for example, Irked's idea of them getting bonus withering damage based on their anima level is fine, because it represents their superior strength being used in combat, but it is still about strength).

                                                        Anyway, I suspect Full Moons are the easiest to work out.

                                                            I was going to talk about Changing Moons next, but I changed my mind.

                                                            No Moons
                                                            So, No Moons. They were always the bastard step-children of the Lunar host: worst anima powers, worst charms, and a theme of "uh... dunno, let's make 'em sorcerers so we don't have to write so many charms for them."

                                                            So, their two powers were:
                                                            Be better at sorcery (this power was actually utterly rubbish, I think it saved me 2 motes in like 5 years of playing a Casteless/No Moon, but the theme is be better at sorcery, anyway. Actually, it was specifically supposed to be being good at combat sorcery, but that's stupid, so let's ignore it).
                                                            Be surrounded by an aura of darkness, which gives you +1 DV (actually a decent power, though Dawns got +2DV, so... and still not as good as the Changing Moon power. But the Changing Moon anima power was one of the best in the game).

                                                            Now, I always thought "grrrr, why do No Moons have a sorcery power, that's ridiculous. Firstly, what if you want to play a non-sorcerer No Moon? Or you're just Essence 2 and can't get it? Secondly, it makes no sense, because sorcery didn't exist when Lunars were made!"

                                                            But I think this had a lot to do with the reasons I disliked/dislike (even more than in 2nd ed) the Twilight anima power, which does seem thematically designed for sorcerers, and not for the greatest detective/doctor/smith/teacher.
                                                            But I think No Moons are more inherently "occult" than Twilights, actually. I mean, to be a Lunar is to be a magical shapeshifter: you're already pretty damn arcane in a way a Solar is not.
                                                            And secondly, as Sunder pointed out repeatedly in this thread, this isn't (unlike 2nd ed) the anima power No Moons always had: in the Primordial War, Lunar castes and anima powers were different. It does make sense that the No Moons would give themselves a new power useful for sorcery.

                                                            Also, you can actually get sorcery at character gen know without having to spend 10 freebie points!

                                                            So... I realised I am actually okay with No Moons having a power about sorcery. But I think only 1 out of 3. And preferably one that's not entirely useless for non-sorcerers.
                                                            Actually, their 2nd ed power was technically applicable to spells and charms that used an Occult roll.
                                                            The issue was, there was only a handful (3 maybe? Max. There might have been less) of No Moon charms that actually used Occult rolls.

                                                            So, then we come to this darkness power that gives you +1 DV.
                                                            An aura of darkness doesn't seem very... Lunar. But it does seem to me appropriate for the Lunars of the No Moon caste to have some link to darkness and night (though not a very strong one).
                                                            I would suggest something else though. Perhaps a bonus to stealth in darkness, or something like that. Or this could be folded into their sorcery power: maybe they get sorcerous motes equal to their Essence (min 3) in darkness.
                                                            Not sure about that.

                                                            Anyhow, moving on.

                                                            Let's talk about No-Moons-as-Shamans-and-witches.
                                                            Dealing with spirits (as in making deals), plumbing forbidden knowledge...
                                                            Honestly, I think the best way to reflect that would be to let them buy charms with the Eclipse keyword, but that's kind of already taken.

                                                            How would people feel about them having an anima power related to spirits? Maybe a social bonus against spirits? Or some kind of ban that makes it difficult for spirits to attack them unprovoked, so they can actually make deals with forbidden entities without being eaten? That kind of sounds a bit too similar to the Eclipse power, again.
                                                            Spirits need to spend 1wp to attack them? They get (Essence) bonus dice on social dice against spirits, and Lore/Occult rolls to know about spirits?
                                                            I'd suggest letting them see and talk to spirits, but that's a charm (I expect) anyway.

                                                            Admittedly, I personally am pretty bored of dealing with spirits (my playgroup made my No Moon deal with spirits a lot, as he was the Solar Khan's official shaman, and he hated it, because he didn't like negotiating and making deals. And neither do I). But it is possibly appropriate for No Moons.


                                                            The third thing I was thinking about is something that Sunder made me consider, talking about how the Lunars remade their castes to give them more applicably useful powers.

                                                            So... how about a power relating to the Wyld and Fair Folk? It's not something Solars have that many powers relating to (they're more focused on CoDs), and the Lunars were fleeing into the Wyld, so I think it'd make sense that they'd want a Wyld-related power. And as Luna has some thematic power over the Wyld (the Moon affects the tides of the Wyld), it's not inappropriate. Certainly dealing with strange and alien entities (like Raksha) seems appropriate to me for No Moons.

                                                            In 2nd ed, originally the Waxing Moon caste had powers against the Wyld, but that can easily be written out: it makes more sense to me that the original Lunar castes had nothing to do with the Wyld (as they made to fight Primordials) and the new Lunar castes are more connected/resistant to the Wyld.

                                                            Also, I think it'd make more sense for No Moons to have a power that helps them make deals with, do diplomacy with, or protect them from, Raksha. Rather than the Waxing Moons "extra damage vs Raksha" which I think isn't quite what No Moons are about.

                                                            Anyway. So I'm suggesting (and you can see that this is a bit light on the details) that maybe No Moons could have:

                                                            1) A power related to sorcery, though preferably one that's useful for some other stuff (like the 2nd ed power was supposed to be, it's just that it was utter rubbish)
                                                            2) A power related to spirits. Probably more towards making deals than beating them up.
                                                            3) A power related to the Wyld. Probably, again, more towards protection from the Wyld or making deals than just punching Raksha.


                                                            I might talk about Changing Moons in a few hours when I come back from church, I'll see if you guys have anything to say about my ideas. You can see that none of these are very solid: I'm just making suggestions and throwing out ideas.
