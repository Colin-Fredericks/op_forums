                        Shadow of the Colossus


                        I just beat the HD version and once again I am reminded how glorious this game is, and how its one of the finest examples of a story that could take place in Creation. Wanderer seems like a good fit for Solar at first, but probably would work even better as an Abyssal as he uses a Title rather then a name, seems damned, and starts to develop enhanced vitality due to Abyssal powers. The game is actually the finest example a Soul hierarchy, their connection to a Primordial, and their abstraction from the Primordial as they become Hekatonhires. At first I was thinking Dormin would be a Neverborn, but just now I think maybe it would work as a situation where a Primordial perhaps rendered of all its souls and kept in that limbo state due to ritualistically fettering its souls as Hekatonhires.

                        Its a fine example of the moral ambiguity of Exalted's setting, and the wonders of ambiguous legendary story telling. And in 3rd ed you could really experiment with the system such as making Wanderer or Mono unique beings, using the Exigent rules. I always wanted to make Exalted Characters that were Primordial's reincarnated into human flesh.

                            Originally posted by semicasual View Post
                            I remember suggesting this in the last thread, and I'll promote it again: Jade Empire. It's not Bioware's most polished game, but it's well written, the Wuxia aesthetics are great, and I haven't played any other game that did magical martial arts so well.
                            Other than the usual complaints about it being too short, I felt that Jade Empire wasn't much fun to play. There is a serious problem when Dynasty Warriors has richer gameplay, not to mention that Jade Empire doesn't have the flashiness to match the gut-based appeal of power-bombing absolutely everyone as Ding Feng.

                            I also felt that it wasn't wuxia enough, but that's a personal opinion.

                            Originally posted by HK-87 View Post
                            The one and only reason I would disagree with using Jade Empire as inspiration is because the story vests absolute moral rightness in heaven and going against heaven's will is automatically evil to the point where you can't be Closed Fist without being a crazy-stupid-evil ancient Chinese Sith Lord. That might work for ancient China, but it doesn't work so well for Exalted. In general though I would absolutely recommend it.
                            Chinese cynicism with the divine is kind of a thing.

                            There is a sense that Heaven's will is for the better, but there's no promise of sunshine and rainbows for all individuals out there. You can fill literal libraries with stories about the people who end up suffering because of their opposition to something that can be construed as Heaven's will. Furthermore, consider the example of the Four Classics. Journey to the West is stuffed with corrupt bureaucrats from start to finish, while Romance of the Three Kingdoms shows that Heaven's will is inevitable, no matter how hard humans might strive against it.

                            That said, I'm not a fan of the Open Palm/Closed Fist split.
