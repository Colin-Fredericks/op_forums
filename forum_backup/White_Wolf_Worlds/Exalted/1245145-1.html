        So, in 2e, these are the substances the Art of Alchemy canonically covers making (#/# is degree/difficulty):
        1. Alchemical Touchstone (0/1; a general category of items, both stones and liquids, that can be used to identify a specific substance).
        2. Life's Little Luxury Blends (0/1; another general category, including deodorants, cleaning agents, dyes, trick powders that foam when put in alcohol or vinegar, etc).
        3. Blood-Staunching Compress (1/3; keeps wounds from re-opening).
        4. Draught of Blessed Respite (1/2; a sleeping potion that has a weird effect taken at a half-dose, allowing the character to act at high penalties while getting credit for sleeping).
        5. Eagle's Eye Potion (1/3; bonus die to vision-related Perception rolls, also versions for other senses).
        6. Hero's Recovery (1/3; potion that lets mortals heal as if they were Exalted).
        7. (Type) Venom-Allaying Draught (1/2; antitoxins, each specific to a poison).
        8. Wound-Cleansing Unguent (1/1; character doesn't have to make infection rolls after surgery).
        9. Age-Staving Cordial (2/3; as per the core rules).
        10. Ardent Embrace Resin (2/3; D&D-style "alchemist's fire")
        11. Final Vengeance (2/3; ignore pain, including wound penalties. Stamina reduced to 1 after it wears off, healing at half rate until recovered).
        12. Munificent Antivenin (2/3; a universal antivenin that makes the user "painfully inactive" for a few ticks).
        13. Philtre of Desire (2/2; one makes you better at social stuff against one specific target when imbibed, another lowers the drinker's MDV).
        14. Tiger's Heart Elixir (2/3; +1 to Valor; other versions boost other Virtues).
        15. Valiant Warrior Formula (2/2; turns an Extra into a Heroic Mortal).
        16. Deathlord's Breath (3/4; works better than salt as a undead ward, but a deadly poison to the living that makes those it kills into zombies and hungry ghosts).
        17. 8-Scream Devil Powder (3/4; WotLA item, inflicts -4 pain penalty until scrubbed off).
        18. Seven Bounties Paste (3/4; core rules in 2nd, Arms of the Chosen in 3rd).
        19. Sweet Cordial (3/4; core rules in 2nd, Arms of the Chosen in 3rd).
        20. Wind-Fire Potion (3/4; +1 die to physical attributes/Valor/Wits, -1 dot to Compassion/Temperance/Intelligence, gives heroic mortals two -4 health levels, addictive).
