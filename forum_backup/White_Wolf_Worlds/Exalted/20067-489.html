                    Really, the question is just about the base effect of Ferocious Jab and when the onslaught applies. Damage isn't a separate action, it is part of the resolution of an attack, so-
                    Originally posted by Exalted Third Edition, p. 184
                    onslaught penalty: A -1 penalty to a character’s Defense, lasting until his next turn, that is incurred every time he suffers an attack.

                    Since the attack isn't over, the onslaught penalty hasn't been applied. So option 2.
