                        You know, while I'm sure that there are some areas in which differences in shapeshifting really do have meaningful mechanical distinctions, I do think there's also something to be said for how sometimes a minor mechanical difference with a larger aesthetic modification saying a lot for different Exalted being different, and the greater implications people draw thereof.

                        The difference between Fire and Stones Strike and Green Sun Nimbus Flare is fairly minor in mechanics, but in character one of them is exceptional skill with a sword (with a bit of Solar Essence put in its edge by such), and the other is wreathing an attack in toxic green flame, and these give different perspectives on what the character behind the powers is.

                        Lunars being presented as primal shapeshifter heroes rather than protean alien monsters creates different priorities in who they are and what they do, and that has its importance.

                                                    The bit to which you're responding was actually Isator's paraphrasing an official statement from the line's co-developer that EX3 Lunars are not really focused on Alex Mercer nor on The Thing. It's tucked away in here, somewhere.

                                                    Originally posted by What We Know Wiki
                                                    Q: (HK-87)
                                                    So is Alex Mercer still a valid character concept for 3E Lunars?

                                                    A: (Holden)
                                                    Not tremendously so. We prefer mythic shapeshifters and primal gods to John Carpenter's THE THING.
                                                    This is a common thing with Lunar discussions I have observed over the past... ever: it's really important not to assume what someone means when they say, "Lunars are/aren't X." Depending on context (which can clarify, but may not), one could be saying anything from "This is how Lunars are/were in 2e," to "This is how Lunars are/will be, based on comments from the EX3 Team," to -- with great and depressing frequency -- "This is what I am personally asserting Lunars are/were/will be." The third of which a) may or may not have firm grounding in one or both of the prior two, and b) is often delivered with the sincere belief that it has firm grounding in one or both of the prior two.

                                                    I realize this is obvious and a concern at all times -- especially around the corner of an edition change -- but it really comes up with nigh omnipresence* in Lunar discussions. It feels pertinent to stress that. I have collectively seen pages lost to this kind of misunderstanding.

                                                    * Seriously. Really common. Like, anyone who's thinking about a clever meta commentary about these discussions? Something in the vein of, "Even conversations about Lunars often turn into things other than you thought them to be?" It has been noted before. Many times. I'm not saying don't share such things (I'll probably be amused, regardless), just underscoring how well-worn this ground is.
