        ORION X. JAEGER

        There are hundreds of stories throughout the Central Empire that tell of how Commander Jaeger was exalted as a Lunar, each of them more outrageous than the last, yet none of them so implausible as to be untrue. After all, in his hundreds of years serving as a special operative, Jaeger has probably done more to protect the Empire as a whole than any other single individual being.

        It was he (often she, thanks to casual use of the Twin-Faced Hero knack) who exposed the Iselsi Conspiracy to destroy the Thousandth Dynasty, he who rescued the Son of Heaven's many daughters from their ransomed captivity by a Death-Avatar (and then publicly made out with them because he just does not give a fuck!), he who drove off a nightmare horde of Shrieking Ones back into the strange unreality of the Canals, and he who smashed several of the massive terra-forming engines that were attempting to absorb outlying worlds of the Empire into the God-Constellation Autochthon.

        Jaeger isn't a glory-hog, however, and will be the first to give credit to his team, a small unit of humans, aliens and Exalts (including an Alchemical "unplugged" from Autochthon) that crews his Artifact Starship, The Mishaka. While perhaps too eccentric to serve in the regular Legions, their talents and loyalty make them the surrogate family Jaeger lost long ago, and he'd lay down his life for them just as they would for him.

        Most recently, the Mishaka encountered a strange new type of Exalt never recorded before, burning with the green sunlight of the Yozi-Stars. Jaeger reported this to Imperial Center, only to have the massive bureaucracy there unsurprisingly spin its wheels while awaiting more "proof". Looks like it's time for Jaeger and crew to go back to work again...

        Notes: Jaeger is a middle-aged Lunar with lots of combat experience, making him one of the foremost warriors in the Known Worlds. He is a Full Moon, with a Honey Badger Spirit Shape, whose Tell is Being the Most Badass Thing Around (or, An Unblinking, Intimidating Gaze/Presence), which also happens to be what his high Reputation represents. Jaeger has War Specialties of Small-Unit Tactics, and Charisma/Manipulation Specialties of Leadership/Intimidation, respectively. He also enjoys the Common Sense and Daredevil Merits, and has many Connections both in the Empire and the Frontier.

        Jaeger has many custom Lunar-Hero Charms, as well as combos with the many supernatural martial arts he has learned, and he has a unique variant of Relentless Lunar Fury that he has developed. He often goes into action with the Beast-Power Concentration knack that allows him to channel his full Lunar powers through his human shape, and his many Stamina Charms make him all but unkillable.

        Jaeger has a high Arsenal rating, giving him access to requisition nearly any Artifact or Equipment he needs for his missions (The Mishaka being regularly stocked with any types of weapons and armor), but he only keeps one Artifact, a Moonsilver Switchklaive that was the first Artifact he ever owned, and is perhaps his oldest Intimacy, besides the Central Empire itself (whose protection forms his Motivation). He once had a powerful Familiar, but he never talks about the mission when he lost it, even though he still has intense Nightmares (as per the Flaw) that indicate that it may still be alive, if no longer what it once was...

            JAEL, THE MOON-MOTHER

            After finding their way in the world, most Lunars who survive establish a legend for themselves within it. Many are based on mastering the violence and savagery of that world, but some are renown for their boundless compassion for it, instead. Of these, perhaps the most-loved is Jael.

            For the last few hundred years, Jael has been a shining beacon to the Silver Pact of the best the Chosen of Luna can aspire to...although not all agree with the lengths she is willing to go to her nurturing nature. Not only does she treat her fellow Lunars with her healing arts that enable them to recover from injuries and maladies that even their Charms cannot deal with, but she is also known to succor mortals, exalts and spirits of all sorts...even those who are enemies of the Silver Pact and Creation itself!

            These last are meticulously cared for while also being subjected to her soothing manner and incisive questions, all subtly enhanced by the power of Luna. By the time their convalescence is over, many of them have gained a new found appreciation and respect for Creation, some even going out of their way to protect it (or at least lead less harmful lives).

            Jael's moniker of "moon-mother" is not just figurative; she gives her love freely and without prejudice, and has embraced virtually every type of sentient being in Creation, and borne children to many, perhaps more than any other living Moonchild. Most of these creatures, both Beastmen tribes and singular entities, regard Jael as a divine being and give her their utmost love and loyalty, and she could amass a sizable army of her children if she felt the need.

            Jael can take many forms but is most often encountered as a matronly woman of middle years and pleasant cheer, plump and attractive. Her Spirit Shape is a cow, and her Deadly Beastman form a massive bovine woman with a muscular build, snorting nostrils and silver horns. Jael's Tell is a strong smell of milk in her presence, her Limit Trigger is exposing her children or patients to danger, and she most often suffers The Curse of the Mother Hen in Limit Break.

            The Moon-Mother is a Changing Moon, due to her social manner of dealing with problems, but she also has focused heavily on the development of Stamina magic, focusing on endurance, breeding and reproducing drugs and medicines (including Sweet Cordial) with her own biology; these last she often feeds to the sick and injured as sweet milk from her bounteous breasts. Jael enjoys a very high Reputation in the Silver Pact, esp among the Wardens of Gaia, and many Allies and Contacts amongst all sorts of creatures and beings, many of whom owe her Favors.

            Although Jael prefers to avoid combat, she is well-versed in the Art of Victorious Concession, and quite practiced with spells such as the Mists of Eventide, Commanding the Beasts and Summoning of the Harvest. She enhances her healing talents with Artifacts, such as a Satchel of Resplendent Healing, wears a Moonsilver Chainshirt, and can produce a Moonsilver and White Jade Goremaul from Elsewhere, usually to deal with those recalcitrant patients who will not reform their ways before they can inflict more damage upon Creation...

            (This is actually another character I came up with during Mother's Day, but I hesitated to post it because of certain elements that could be seen as silly or juvenile at best, if not outright offensive at worst. But she wouldn't let me go, so I said "screw it", since it's not like I'm could lose my nonexistent RPG job over fan backlash, and besides, it's in-theme for Exalted's sources, which contain many similar oddities. Still, I feel for the devs/writers who do have to worry about such things...)

                        What IS this "Undertale" I keep seeing references to?
                        Indie game available on Steam for $10.

                        Extremely reminiscent of the Earthbound/Mother series on the original Nintendo/Famicon system.

                        It will be best for you if you continue to avoid any spoilers for the game, at least until you've seen its trailer and teaser on Steam and decided whether you want to go into it blind or don't mind being spoiled. But seriously, it's just $10 bucks if you're willing to buy it at all.

                        Length-wise, it's kind of like Chrono Trigger, with a story that is just long enough, while being short enough to encourage the replayability the game is definitely meant for. It will take more than one pass to get the whole story.

This is, in fact, the true source of the game's "puzzle" genre, more than the obvious "puzzles".

                            Well, no longer Moonday, but I've been working on this guy as part of another project. So if I may...

                            Regis ap Sora: White Jay of Nightfall

                            A wandering scholar in a dark leather coat, with long white hair concealed beneath a floppy hat, Regis ap Sora is a genuinely happy man. The pale skinned No Moon has status behind him; he has lived for close on a millennia now, and is one of the Crossroads Society's oldest surviving members. He may be the greatest student of medicine amongst the moon-chosen, and his magic is nothing to sniff at either. By his hand many unblooded Lunars have been brought into the fold, lives have been saved and enemies slain. He has honoured each and every debt he has ever acquired, and the call of a white feathered bird has become a sign of ill luck for Dynasts across the East. There are few Lunars in all of Creation who can match the White Jay's record of commitment to the Silver Pact and its goals.

                            And yet, all of this can not erase one simple stain on the No Moon's character. Regis is a necromancer.

                            He was so before his Exaltation, at least so he claims. A ghost-blood born upon the isle of Nightfall, it was expected that he pursue the black arts to help defend his nation. Luna chose him to be her herald, necromancy and all, and those spells are useful to a wanderer who relies more on his wits than skill at arms. To the best anyone can determine, ap Sora has made no attempts to contact the Deathlords and does not consider them allies. And yet, if he were to do so, how could even the Silver Pact find out? There is no amount of good that could remove that worry from the minds of elder Lunars, and no amount of reputation that can excuse necromancy to the young, still fearing Thorns and the emergence of the Abyssal Exalted

                            Regis ap Sora knows his reputation well, and has stopped trying to fight it. He interacts less with the Pact these days, preferring instead to focus on his own projects and ideas. Many argent eyes, young and old alike, are trained on Regis' growing dominion to the south of Linowan. He has claimed the shadowland known as Ashwater Vale and has brought Tanlin refugees, the tribes of the Llesanti and the ghostly kingdom of Akiden together to found a new kingdom beneath the forest canopy. Regis' expressed hope is that Ashwater will become a civilization in its own right, a safe haven for those who oppose the Realm. The different factions for the moment are held in check by mutual respect for the White Jay, and the construction work is proceeding in accordance with his plans. Those fellow Lunars who are sceptical of ap Sora and his motives are now considering how best to intervene.
