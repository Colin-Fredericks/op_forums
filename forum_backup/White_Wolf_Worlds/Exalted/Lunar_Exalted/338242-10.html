                        Originally posted by Zelbinnean View Post
                        I just hate the notion that the preeminent shapeshifters among the Exalted keeping a form too long basically gives you an Animorphs curse.
                        It's definitely a challenge because no one likes being told how to play their character, but I think it's appropriate to play up this idea that being a Lunar makes you less grounded and stable than other splats. Like shamanistic shapeshifting lore where you had to wear the actual hide of an animal and act like it to don its shape, a sort of sympathetic magic. That said, this might be better represented by including incentives than drawbacks, like allowing you to add mutations or merits to a known form by spending more time in it.

                        If shape shifting could include "meta magic" knacks, like techniques in the new Martial Arts system, that unlock as you use a form more often I'd personally be delighted. Example: spending more time in your tiger form gives you enough "shifter XP" to unlock tiger claws on your ape form, and spending more time as an ape unlocks the ability to use your tiger's tail as a prehensile appendage.

                                            Originally posted by Elemental View Post
                                            Apart from the usual statements of "no formlock", "get rid of the tattoo-hiding tax" and "make their history something other than 750 years of failure and irrelevance", there is a small one--I'd like to see tattoo artifacts stay. Always liked that concept.
                                            I want to say something like "make Tattoos artifacts not specifically a Lunar thing", because everyone should get in on the fun.

                                            Yet tattoos are supposed to be a big or at least common thing for Lunar Exalted.

                                            It would help if we knew what purpose the Lunars will need the Tattoos for. I certainly hope it's not for keeping internal Wyld Taint at bay, but I could accept that some form of tattoos are necessary for locking the Caste of the Exaltation your previous incarnation jail-broke.

                                            But if tattoos are going to be mandatory for Casted Lunars, I want them to have an auto-hide feature. That's a lot more relevant and useful than... pretty much any of the other features they've had in the past. Including "reject all outside physical-shaping effects", and ESPECIALLY "keep a record of your life-debts".

                                            You know; as if the Lunar Exalted had more reason to be worried about Dragon-Blooded and Sidereal Wyld Hunts than about the Wyld or other Lunars.

                                            Besides, the shaping-rejection was a hindrance almost as often as it was a help, and never really protected against the Shaping effects Lunars would have needed to worry about. Their own Charms should have been enough to deal with the passive effects of the Wyld, and any Lunar that would Tattoo a newbie would teach them those tricks if necessary.

                                            The life-debt thing, like the Renown thing, was invested too deeply into the idea that Lunars were numerous enough to live together in familial tribes like the Garou. Rather than the reality of them being spread so far across Creation that they often don't need to give a shit what another Lunar thinks about them.
