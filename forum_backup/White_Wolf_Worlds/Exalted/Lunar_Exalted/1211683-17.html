                                    I have two different Lunar Concepts I want to play that are arguably on the opposite sides of the spectrum of Lunarness, so I am hoping both will be playable.

                                    One is basically a pastiche of Dio Brando from JoJo's, across all his different incarnations. A charismatic velociraptor guy who grew up in luxury, who uses his powers to turn other people into velociraptors as well, or does things like throw flies that turn into mini velociraptors. I don't imagine this being against people's will or being brutal or uncaring; I'd be pulling more of his personality from Diego Brando then Dio, who was generally more affable and just kind of a dickbag instead of a literal monster.

                                    The other I'm imagining as some combination of Medusa and an Arrancar from Bleach. A Lunar whose Warform is so terrifying that it's literally sealed away inside her sword, and she only lets it out in deadly situations because she has a lot of trouble controlling it. In her normal times, she's a former child soldier of the South who runs an orphanage, taking care of kids that she rescues from the same armies she was a part of.

                                    I really hope both of these things are valid, because I'm excited to play them.

                                            I could see the moonsilver bow highlighting individuals with moonlight or marking them with tattoos and each attack increasing what the tattoos can do to the person. Or maybe the moonlight first causing them to illuminate then attract predators or lunar themed creatures to start hunting them lol. Kind of like Jake from Adventure Time calling snakes to Finn when he played the violin. A moonsilver train would be badass!

                                            I kind of hope the book does focus more on the new lunars because the previous ones really are raid bosses. A player meets a baby eater or a man-goat rapist is probably going to have a very limited story there. I mean those kind of stories might not even make it to some player groups and definitely could prevent certain players from even picking up the game. I just hope there is a lot more infrastructure that players can work with in place. A lunar city, with a moonsilver train , that allows players to explore the setting sounds way better than bringing more info about NPCs you probably don't want to meet anyway/might be hard for a ST to roleplay in a way that isn't just roll initiative.

                                            I would also kind of like a lunar base in the wyld where lunars work with fair folk and local tribes. An actual place that stands as a testament to Lunar in game mechanics to resist being forced to play in creation.
