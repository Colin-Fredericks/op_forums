                            Well, we all got a bit baited into derailing the topic.

                            For what it's worth, my two cents on physicality - I didn't really love what I read about the plans for Lunars from the devs at first. But that was mostly due to me mentally equalling "physicality" to "brute force", which is stupid. This thread has helped me in that regard, because once I was actually forced to stop and think about it more than 3 seconds, I realized my initial assumption was, well... dumb.
                            Physicality is, as I now understand, meant as opposed to the unreal, and the metaphysical flavor other Exalts might have, so this is where the "no illusions" doctrine came in, I think. I didn't like that at first as well.
                            But I now see how it makes sense. Lunars are physical beings, so they don't craft illusions, that's what Raksha do. But that doesn't mean they can't trick people. A Lunar might shape himself into something else to fool an opponent, so he'll actually change shape, instead of crafting an illusion, which makes sense for, well, shapeshifters.


                            So this physicality actually lends itself well to tricksters, who might be able to wear another's face to fool their victims, become supernaturally desirable or terrifying, use their magically honed body to perform feats of great dexterity, pickpocketing things that shouldn't be able to be stolen, though I'm still having difficulties to come up with tons of ideas in the broader sense.
                            Shamans might fashion a tincture out of their blood that lets one perceive spirits after consumption? I'm not great at this, but I see how sticking to a physical, and "real" set of powers can still leave them with a huge variety of capabilities, and by now, I'm really excited and looking forward to it, and my initial disappointment has completely vanished.
