        For consideration:
        Originally posted by Holden
        I don't know if you're familiar with professional wrestling, but for such a lowbrow thing it has a very, very highly evolved lingo for describing story-building techniques within a complex milieu, which has proven very useful to me over the years. One of the pro wrestling concepts is that of 'building heat' i.e. generating interest in and support of a character with the audience. There are a number of ways of going about this. The most common, and by far the worst, is to steal heat from someone else. For example, you bring a relatively new guy out and have him beat the snot out of one of your established stars. If you are very lucky this builds heat for the new guy without hurting the old star too badly. More often it's seem as the cheap manipulation it is, and it just makes everyone involved look bad.
        Good heat-building methods usually take longer and involve an established property 'giving the rub' to the guy the company is trying to 'push'—rather than just having the undercard guy go clown an established name, you have him start hanging around with the bigger guys, maybe tag-teaming with them, put them in some three-way matches where they're not carrying all the weight. By association with bigger names, some star power rubs off, and the transition isn't jarring. When they finally beat one of the bigger names, it seems like they've arrived where they ought to be.
        In other words, you've elevated one of your properties without losing anything. This is better than building up one of your properties at the expense of another.
        Lunars have primarily been given cheap heat throughout 2e—they blame the Solars for holding them down, they claim they invented sorcery, they claim Solars stole this, stole that, held them back. They're secretly responsible for Chiaroscuro. Etc. This tarnishes the Solars and makes the Lunars look like crap, because no, we don't actually buy that they're as big as they're trying to act.

                        As for what I'd like Lunar's to be able to do, here are a few examples of the flavor of their shape shifting blending with charm effects:

                        Telling of the Blood's Tale: The lunar, upon consuming the blood of a hunted target, can internalize a memory they correctly suspect a target has. (Pretty much a refluffed Theft of Memory, since I don't think we will recognize the form Sorcery takes in 3E)
                        Transitive Property of Shape: Because all forms the Lunars possess are one and the same being, a Lunar invoking this charm can alter their presence within a targets memory to appear as another shape within their heart's blood library. This could be used upon a sleeping target to invoke nightmares of the freezing vagabond they left out in the elements.
                        One With the Moon Method: The Lunar can enhance any artifact composed of Moonsilver with their own Protean grace. A lunar may apply shape bending benefits to that of their panoply, such as enlarging a daiklave to the height of a sky-scraper, reinforcing their armor with a layered carapace, etc. (To be a little more specific on this one, I'd like to see are effects that can increase attack range without making extendable limbs the only flavor, especially when you consider how retarded that looks with archery weapons. The same charm may even allow for elemental damage to be add)
                        One With the Earth Method: The Lunar learns to channel their fluid nature into creation, allowing them to change an area of non-magical material into other non-magical material with as little or as much change to the visual appearance as desired. The fortress's fate was sealed when the unsuspecting woodsman turned the flank of their hardened walls into the supple composition of down pillows.

                                        Originally posted by Paradim View Post
                                        I would like it if Lunars had Charms that let them affect people's dreams, able to send nightmares or hold dream world conferences, which would be a great way that Lunars could keep in communication and coordinated with each other despite the vast distances across Creation they would be scattered over. It'd further show independence from infrastructure and dreams and nightmares have always had a connection with the symbology of the moon.
                                        This is an excellent idea.
                                        In the last 2 books, there were plenty of good charms for Lunars in combat, and a fair amount when it came to social-fu, but their mental charms were pretty weak and lacking. I think No Moons are definitely the weakest Caste. So they kind of need some thematic resonance on which you can build that. And dream-based stuff seems like a good thematic base for that.                                         I'd also like to see them keep their Wyld-based stuff, and perhaps expand on it. Luna is the Goddess that controls and influences the Wyld, as opposed to the Sun's more aggressive stance. So I don't want to see them using Wyld-Shaping to destroy the Wyld like Solars, but influencing it, making it flow back and forth, that's got mileage.

                                        Another way to mine themes from the Moon for their mental charms, would be madness. Luna is the goddess of madness, the moon is associated with lunacy, etc. So charms which inflict or cure madness could fit.

                                                        Originally posted by Couch View Post
                                                        I brought this up in the other Lunars thread, though it got buried under a fresh wave of lapidary minutiae.

                                                        Lunars have the core conceptual issue that their theme cannot be applied reasonably to all areas of character competence.

                                                        Lunars have the theme of "shapeshifting into animals and creatures of legend".
                                                        Not quite.

                                                        First of all, as you say, shapeshifting into animals isn't their theme; it's their tool. It is an outgrowth of their theme rather than the center of it.

                                                        Second of all, Lunars do not shapeshift into legendary beasts. They are legends who shapeshift into beasts; the mythic might originates from the Lunar, even in the form of Gaia's humblest creatures.

                                                        A No Moon might kill and become a Nemean Lion, but a Full Moon in the form of an average member of panthera leo becomes the equal or superior to that great monster.
