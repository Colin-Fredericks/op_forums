                Originally posted by hippokrene View Post

                Yes, that's what I'm getting at. As I said, turning into a Lion is fine at Essence 1, because it's a badass fighter/ambusher, but without charms to improve it, it's not going to work so well at high level.

                Then you want a shantai type animalistic form that you put XP into. We might be getting something like that.
                I think I would be fine with that as one thing Lunars can do. Though I don't know what you mean by Shantai? ​

                However, lots of players would like be able to use that stone shattering cry when they're a lion, falcon, or oxen. Not simply when they're a lion.
                That's not what I'm getting at. I'm not saying "buy a charm that buffs one animal form". I'm saying charms should buff any form when you turn into it. So you buy, say, Mountainous Spirit Meditation, and you can turn into a giant Lion, a giant Falcon, or a giant Oxen (or regular ones). Or you buy Claws of the Silver Moon and you can turn into a Lion with deadly silver claws, or a falcon with deadly silver claws, or an oxen with silver... hooves, I guess? Or you buy Hide-Toughening Essence and turn into the Nemean Lion whose hide no sword can cut, or the Bull with Iron Skin, or whatever.

                Whereas in 2e you turn into a lion. You can use Mountainous Spirit Expression if it's your totem, otherwise not. Then you spend another action growing your deadly silver talons which turn off at the end of the scene. Also, you can use Hide-Toughening Essence reflexively (though you'll probably wait until someone hits you), which again turns off at the end of the scene (though this is less the issue than the simple action to grow your talons). This doesn't really make it feel like you've turned into a super-lion or super-ox or super-falcon, it feels more like you've turned into a lion and then throw stuff on top of it as necessary. I'm saying they should work more like Gift charms* to make it feel like part of the transformation.

                This isn't really a big change, though it leads into some thematic significance. But then, its thematic significance than I think the devs would like to go into: I think they'd like to see Lunars changing into animals for a lot more than sneaking, flying and disguise (since if you want combat, you turn into DBT).

                *I mean, maybe the mechanics will change entirely and this won't make sense, that's fine. But I hope you can see what I'm getting at in terms of how the mechanics express themes?

                                                            Okay, I've used terminology without considering what it means to others... when I said "meat and potatoes" what I meant was... Lunars are probably going to have Charms that, whatever their actual mechanics, just plain would not look like supernatural powers to an external observer. Say... a bunch of Dexterity Charms that could be used to enhance sword fighting in a manner and with precepts distinct from Solar Melee Charms, and to a person looking would "just" seem like an unusually fkexible sword fighter. Those are the potatoes. The stuff that is very apparently, overtly the expression of the furious, bestial might of a Lunar, whether it be shifting to a war form or ripping a bear in half accompanied by the spectral image of a rending animal, that is the meat. What I am suggesting is that you can have the potatoes, and actually make them significant, even iconic Lunar Charms, the kinds of Charms you could build a character around, and dodge a lot of the Silver Solar problems by surrounding such Charms in incentives and conditions that make the character feel like a Lunar.
