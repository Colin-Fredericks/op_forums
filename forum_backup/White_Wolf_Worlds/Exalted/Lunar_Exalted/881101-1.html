        Originally posted by Sanctaphrax View Post

        Thing is, what Lunars were originally meant to be was bad. But don't take it from me, take it from Holden.
        Thing is no, the approach that 1e had was terrible, on account of no one who was hired wanted to really work on it. I've already read that page many times, thanks, I know what it says. 2e followed it up with a tepid, retiring approach that didn't try to take what would have been an easy road toward fixing the problems, and tried to make Lunars into something their inspiration never ran with. We got a Luna from Werewolf, and the Lunars need to follow suit on growing from that kernel.

        Yeah, but that's GURPS. I think that I, or any other reasonably competent Exalted guy, could write some decent shrinking magic without any major complexity issues. Bonuses to stealth and Evasion, penalties to durability, Parry, and Strength. What's missing?
        Coherence. Animalistic shapeshifters don't necessarily just shrink themselves down; again, they are not fucking Ant-Man, or Mystique, or Colossus, or The Thing From Another World. They are not blank templates to apply any special effects to.

        Like?

        I wouldn't be surprised if there was something, but nothing is coming to mind right now. So it doesn't seem like any worse an issue for shapeshifting than it is for, say, elemental themes.
        Elemental themes tend to nail things down a lot more than "become anything else, alter shape in any way you see fit". They do things in certain and definite ways, even when operating on a more allegorical level. Being able to turn into a table or shoggoth would be to Lunars like Dragon-Blooded getting Aspects that line up with elements from the periodic table. It's not playing along with the theme of the character type, it's not keeping the feel of the setting, and it doesn't make the splats better, it just introduces a confusing inclusion of details that tries to knock the inspiration points out and replace them.

                                                    Originally posted by Saur Ops Specialist View Post
                                                    That tends to devalue turning into smaller forms. Leave it to spirit Charms.
                                                    This is one of the reasons I say "about half size or double" as the limit of where I still consider it potentially Lunar-y (though I could see about a third or triple, at the outside range). At that size, I don't feel you're seriously devaluing smaller forms, particularly given that they tend to have their own powers and be far, far less conspicuous than a dwarf human.

                                                    Also, their healing isn't like T-1000, and they aren't made of amorphous liquid metal.
                                                    "You injure them and the wound just flows closed instantly" is what I was going for -- not the clearest metaphor, but I couldn't think of a better one. They're not literally made of liquid metal, but you can absolutely do the shotgun scene from T2 with a Stamina-focused Lunar, it's just that there will be blood. My point is that it's clearly a power related from shapeshifting, but not from animal shapeshifting.

                                                    In addition to straightforward Attribute charms, it seems likely that Lunars are will have shapeshifting charms in this game, since the only reason they didn't have more or these historically was charmshare. Some of these will be like 2e's Knacks in that they just straightforwardly enhance your existing shapeshifting ability, but others are probably just going to, you know, shift their shape. Stapling an animal metaphor to all those charms would be kind of weird, IMO. Lunars have always had some non-animal shapeshifting, even in cases where animal shapeshifting could theoretically accomplish something similar, and it seems likely that they always will. The only real question is what is within that scope. I'll leave that to the developers, but Solars got broadened an awful lot in 3e, so I would not be surprised to see every splat get some things that we wouldn't traditionally have seen going to them.
