    Originally posted by Epimetheus View Post
    The problem is that dragon blooded at those numbers don't have the ability to coordinate effectively. This is less about centralizing power and more about the fact that that dragon blooded have limits and with the size of creation changing, the realm Dragon blooded numbers cannot realistically be at only at ten thousand. This also relates to the issue with the threshold because there's no real way for dragon blooded to have enough troops to prevent the strapies from rebelling, even with legions at hand. The space to cover and the times of mobility would make it impossible to control it all. As well as the distance between them makes what the dragon blooded even have hard to maintain.

    This all relates to the point of how the lunars have been actually dealing with the realm because logistically, even mortals with their own cultures who have their own troops would be able to rebel down the line. Lunars shouldn't be having much of an issue with most of this. The dragon blooded outside of a super weapon the empress couldn't casually wield just don't have the ability to grow at their numbers.
    I know that members of the team have put a lot of time and thought into getting numbers to be sensible, but as I wasn't one of those team members, I won't speak for them.
