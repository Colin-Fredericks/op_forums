    03-14-2019, 04:52 PM
    So, Lunars need to stunt to include off-attributes in their actions for Excellency purposes. This will require practice.

    Thus: A game!

    Give a brief stunt demonstrating how to include a given attribute in a given action, and then provide an attribute and action for the next poster.

    I.E if the previous post requested "Stamina" with "A melee attack (Dexterity + Melee)", my stunt might be

    "I let the incoming attack glance off my essence-hardend shoulder, forcing an opening for stabination."

    First up: Charisma and Parry Defense!
