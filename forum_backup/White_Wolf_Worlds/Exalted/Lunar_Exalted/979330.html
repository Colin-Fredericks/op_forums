    Approximating the Lunar Charmset with rearranged Solar Charms

    09-30-2016, 07:50 PM
    How many people actually try this when they homebrew Lunars, or quickly mock them up for a game?

    For example, trying to model a branch of Lunar Strength Charms with something similar to Triple-Distance Throwing Technique, followed by Charms which are similar to Iron Raptor Technique, Strength-Hying Heave, and Crashing Wave Throw?

    (The logic of that progression being that it's easier to start with throwing thing that are designed to be thrown, and then working your way into throwing things which are not.)

                                  Originally posted by MonkofLords View Post
                                  There are Strength based thrown charms yes, but you can use thrown plenty fine with Perception and Dex effects as well. (I am aiming for each Attribute getting a combat tree, and two Utility trees.
                                  As for Perception, I'm thinking that Lunars could bring back First Edition's "Blood-Singing Instincts" as a more flexible version of the Solar Archery Charm, "Blood Without Balance".

                                  BWB lets a Solar archer make a reflexive Aim action and act earlier than their Initiative if she becomes the beneficiary of a Distract gambit. "Blood-Singing Instincts" would let a Lunar do the same with any sort of attack, like a wolf taking advantage of a packmate's feint.

                                  Solar Charms are efficient -- Archery Charms do what is best for archery, which means providing for Aim actions, which Melee and Brawl don't bother dealing with. Holden said that Lunar Charms were going to come off as "less polished" than Solar Charms, and he might have even said "less efficient". It's still a Charm that can be used with archery and throwing attacks, and Aim bonuses are still useful for close range attacks, but it's not as focused as BWB and its not directly linked to Charms that extend range, provide ammunition or even bows, or turn arrows into explosives.


                                  But Lunars CAN pack all of their perceptive Charms under just one Trait. Awareness, Investigation, Socialize, Occult, Medicine, and more.

                                                  Must admit i have been kind of lazy when it comes to NPC exalts - for starters because i tend to play the Chosen as something of a quite rare breed. With their numbers at the tens of thousands at most - and the Terrestrials filling the lion's share of this total - through the whole of Creation, spirits, god-bloods, mortal sorcerers and thaumaturgists tend to make for far more numerous antagonists or associates touching on the supernatural, meaning i spend far more time toying with mixing & matching or tinkering spirit charms.

                                                  When i do feel the need for some extra color/weirdness in the powers of a NPC, be it a elemental, demon, fae, ghost, god, sorcerer or exalt, i either rake others of their kind (recombining stuff between the full moon warrior and the changing moon trickster for example) or throw in a bunch of stuff taken from Sorcery, martial arts charms and merits according to taste.

                                                  Also, evocations - the fact some shaping rituals or control spells can give a NPC, be it exalt, mortal or spirit, acess to a whole new set of custom powers opens room for a not insignificant venue for homebrewed insanity. In fact i ended up developing a sort of "exigent/terrestrial wannabe" antagonists through them in the Bezoari, a race of people with a natural affinity for hearthstones and gathering power from them that works in ways akin to the gifts and mysteries of the Talisman of Ten Thousand Eyes.
