    Originally posted by Saur Ops Specialist View Post
    Would it not be rather difficult for Lunars to train any given Solar in the most elementary tactic they have for covert operations? "Step 1 - hunt your target, kill target, eat target's heart to gain its shape." Even if Solars can get awesome disguise capabilities in Larceny, learning at the feet of Lunars doesn't exactly help a Solar acquire or reach these methods, since Luna's Beloved just do as they naturally can.
    I dunno. I mean, yeah, obviously being told, "Oh, just turn do what I do," isn't going to take a Solar very far, but I could see Lunars being very helpful in Solars bettering their skills. Some people learn a lot and learn it quickly from setting themselves up against extreme difficulties in training exercises. If a Solar sets his sights on being able to sneak past someone with the eyes of an eagle or the ears of a fox,* or hear someone as silent as an owl or spot someone as small as a mouse, that will show them a lot of growth in the area. Particularly as the teacher can later give insight into where the screw-ups happened. That Solar is in a much better place to spot or sneak past the Dragonblooded. And that would be if the Lunar stuck only to shapeshifting.

    Honestly, I could also see some Lunars training themselves in the same way. Shapeshifting is always an option and frequently a better one, but staying in human form is sometimes more practical and useful. Their skills in Larceny and Awareness certainly aren't going to make them worse if they apply those as animals, it's just a smaller marginal benefit than Solars would see.

    * The Lunar may or may not handicap himself by excluding "the nose of a wolf" and the like from that list.

This would likely make them hesitant to accept training from someone with a Step 0 that is "be a magical skinchanger."
