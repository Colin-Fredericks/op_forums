                                        Originally posted by Isator Levi
                                        It's a mandatory school subject and required for a number of job types (almost everything government related), and it's not taught well.
                                        That's pretty much why most (not all) of my Welsh friends dislike Welsh.

                                        Originally posted by Isator Levi
                                        I want to say that it's not helped by being very different from English, but they don't have this problem on the continent, do they? It's just a bad curriculum.
                                        Actually, Irish is more different from English than Germanic or Latin languages. It's still Indo-European, but it's probably much harder to learn for an English speaker than German, Dutch, French, etc (Dutch and Danish are the easiest for English speakers).

                                        Also, while some countries in Europe don't teach English well (some do), the students are often motivated by external factors; pop stars they like, TV shows, computer games. I doubt many Irish students are really keen on understanding an Irish computer game.

                                        Though I can well believe it's taught badly. Foreign languages often are in secondary schools (though I may be biased; we ESL teachers are quite snooty towards the way foreign languages are taught in British schools, and I imagine Irish is done the same way).

                                        Originally posted by Isator Levi
                                        I think it’s helpful to introduce the Silver Pact as a middle way between complex organisations and going it alone.
                                        Yeah, I think that makes the most sense as a presentation.

                                        Originally posted by Isator Levi
                                        Yes, start off with a bit of history, a bit of background for why the Silver Pact is the way it is, coloured with some namedrops for ancient wonders.
                                        I thought the history parts were very good; both in terms of namedrops, style, the process of history (2nd ed just went Primordial War, High First Age, Contagion, now, with little in between), and having famous characters who are now dead (2nd ed had Ingosh, but that's it). And most of those historical characters were quite interesting (though I'd like to have known their totem animals).

                                        Originally posted by Isator Levi
                                        A bit of jargon is welcome for cases where a lack of good terminology would feel a bit stale.
                                        I actually didn't like the jargon. I feel it's something players will continually forget or not use.

                                        Originally posted by Nabirius
                                        the last vestiges of monogamy in the Exalted universe, given that the Realm has a lot of polyamorous and frequently bisexual relationships
                                        Firstly, I don't think I've ever actually seen any polyamorous relationships in the Dragonblood stuff. I don't think "living in an arranged marriage but having a lover" really counts as polyamarous.

                                        Also, I don't really see what being bisexual has with not being monogamous.

                                        Originally posted by Nabirius
                                        Though in retrospect it seems weird that Solars and Lunars would have a pair-bond thing going on, given that the Unconquered Sun and Luna had, at various points, Gaia, Merela, one of the Maidens (I think), and some others that I might have forgotten as a part of their relationship.
                                        The Unconquered Sun and Luna were never even together really.
                                        The UCS had all kinds of (female) lovers; handmaidens, goddesses, Merelea, the Maidens. Not really Luna.
                                        Luna was with Gaia.

                                        Basically, I don't think the Solar-Lunar bond is supposed to evoke a romantic relationship between Luna and the UCS.

                                        I'd also point out that the very first reference to the Solar Bond is Gilgamesh and Enkidu, not something romantic.

                                        Originally posted by Eric Minton
                                        I'll need to revise the initial sidebar appearance of Raksi and Ma-Ha-Suchi for clarity. The intent is that while they Exalted in the First Age, they weren't yet famous luminaries and heroes when the Usurpation took place, so they were overshadowed by other First Age Lunars in the early days of the Silver Pact. But with the loss of more well-known Lunar champions, Raksi and Ma-Ha-Suchi had an opportunity to step up to leadership roles in the Pact
                                        After reading their later descriptions, and coming back to the early descriptions, that did make sense to me (and it makes a lot of sense), but I misunderstood when I first read about them.
