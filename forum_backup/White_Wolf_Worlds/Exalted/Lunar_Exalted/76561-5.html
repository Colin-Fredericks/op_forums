            I said this before, a while ago, but I'd really like to see the Lunars be the Myth to the Solars Legend and the Sidereals Prophecy. I'd like to see them as the strange characters in mythology who step in and do the impossible or illogical, like swallow the ocean or run so fast they have to unstrap their legs to sleep. I'd like to see them made up of not one mythos but many, each Lunar being several mythical figures they build over the years. And I'd like their less organized structure to have a reason beyond animalistic traits, or barbarian traits, or arbitrary reasons.

            But really, make being a Lunar mysterious, evocative, and cool and I will be happy.

                        I again want to state that I found Lunar Exalted to be more or less 'there' before. They had some mechanical issues, not as bad as a few other splats (which got fixed in 2.5), but they were solid protagonists and worked well.

                        The main issue was with the fiction and the 'Assumptions of (in)competence' thing in the fiction, which hewed very close to W:tA thematic conventions of the 'outsider fugitives fleeing the hostile world which threatens to crush the near-extinct race at any moment'.

                        Which, by the way, kinda worries me a bit in regards to the comment...'

                        Originally posted by John Mørke View Post
                        The Realm was falling apart and discarded throughout 2e. This is not how we plan to treat it in EX3. Don't count on the Wyld Hunt being a marginalized non-factor.
                        ... because when I think of the issues of 2e Lunars, 'The Lunar Exalted were being way too successful against the Realm and Wyld Hunt' is DEFINITELY not one of them.

                                            The entire basis of the Lunar fiction is that the Wyld Hunt was -so effective- they were forced to literally ditch creation and run.

                                            "The Lunars, while still free to reincarnate, instead faced
                                            death by attrition followed by an eternity of rebirth as ignorant
                                            pups, helpless before the Wyld Hunt. They avoided this fate
                                            only thanks to the priests of the Waxing Moon Caste who
                                            kept their heavenly connections. Initially, all of the Waxing
                                            Moons’ entreaties to Yu-Shan were ignored. Eventually, a
                                            god in the Bureau of Nature, distraught over the Sidereals’
                                            perfidy, answered the prayers of a Waxing Moon priest and
                                            revealed the secret of the Sidereals’ special knowledge and
                                            how to defeat it. For the first time, the elders of the Lunars
                                            truly understood how the Sidereal Exalted used the Loom of
                                            Fate to track the Lunars across Creation. And so, these Lunars
                                            realized that safety from the Wyld Hunt lay in the only place
                                            beyond the gaze of the Five-Score Fellowship—someplace
                                            outside Fate itself—the Wyld."

                                            Not exactly a 'marginalized non-factor'.

                                            One of the major issues with Lunar Fiction, in general, is cribbing W:tA tone like this. Examine the immense assumptions of incompetence and competence which are necassary here; Lunar Exalted are essentially helpless babes against the ubercompetence of the usurpers, who scourge them from Creation with ruthless and terrible efficiency.
