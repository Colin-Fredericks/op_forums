                                          This is my version of the style:


                                          FOOT-TRAPPING COUNTER

                                          Cost:                                           3m; Mins: Dexterity 3, Essence 1; Type: Reflexive
                                          Keywords: Combo-OK
                                          Duration: One scene
                                          Prerequisite Charms: None

                                          Combat does not end until the Lunar allows it. Against an opponent within hand-to-hand range, the Lunar's skilled footwork prevents that opponent from moving away from the character.

                                          This counts as a reflexive Blockade Movement action, but it also works in order to prevent targets from fleeing. In order for an opponent to flee from the martial artist's great footwork (or try to get out of melee range), they need to make contesting rolls as for a Blockade Movement action, though the Lunar adds half his Dexterity, rounded up, in automatic successes.


                                          RABID BEAST ATTITUDE

                                          Cost:                                           4m; Mins: Dexterity 3, Essence 2; Type: Reflexive
                                          Keywords: Combo-OK
                                          Duration: Instant
                                          Prerequisite Charms: Foot-Trapping Counter

                                          The grace and speed of the Stewards is such that wounds, weights or weather cannot hinder them. The Lunar ignores all penalties to a Dexterity-based action in combat, including supernaturally induced penalties.


                                          THOUSAND CLAW INFLICTION

                                          Cost:                                           1m; Mins: Dexterity 3, Essence 2; Type: Reflexive (Step 1)
                                          Keywords: Combo-OK
                                          Duration: One action
                                          Prerequisite Charms: Foot-Trapping Counter

                                          Only the Lunar's skill limits him, not the restrictions of capability. The character's attacks each use his last attack position, defeating normal limits on the weapon. Activating this Charm allows the character to increase all Rate values to 5 if they are lower. Clinches get a Rate value equal to the number of limbs capable of grappling; a Lunar with two arms has Rate 2 and can simultaneously clinch two targets.


                                          LUNAR HERO FORM

                                          Cost:                                           5m; Mins: Dexterity 4, Essence 2; Type: Simple (Speed 5)
                                          Keywords: Form-type
                                          Duration: One scene
                                          Prerequisite Charms: Rabid Beast Attitude, Thousand Claw Infliction

                                          Drawing the Essence of Luna into him, the Exalt becomes an endlessly fluid warrior, seamlessly flowing from one movement to the next.

                                          The character reduces Defense Value penalties from onslaught by 1 and from coordinated attacks by 2. He adds (Dexterity) to his Move and Jump ratings and his natural weapons become as powerful as a bull's, increasing minimum damage by 1.

                                          If a Lunar is in Deadly Beastman Transformation, all Lunar Hero Style Charms have their mote cost reduced by one (to minimum one mote), excluding this one.


                                          ARMOR-RENDING CLAW FIST

                                          Cost:                                           4m, 1wp; Mins: Dexterity 4, Essence 2; Type: Supplemental
                                          Keywords: Combo-OK
                                          Duration: Instant
                                          Prerequisite Charms: Lunar Hero Form

                                          With a single swipe, the Lunar's attack moves so quickly that it tears through even steel armor as though it were paper.

                                          This Charm supplements an attack. If the attack hits the target (it does not need to deal damage), the target's armor soak is reduced by an amount equal to the Lunar's Strength (this does not affect natural armor). This soak reduction is permanent for mundane armor (or until repaired) and scene-long for artifacts or magical armor (including charms that give external armor). If the soak value is reduced to 0, mundane armor is completely broken beyond repair, artifacts lose their soak but retain their other effects. Charms break.


                                          CROUCHING TIGER STANCE

                                          Cost:                                           7m, 1wp; Mins: Dexterity 5, Essence 2; Type: Simple (Speed 3)
                                          Keywords: Combo-OK, Counterattack
                                          Duration: One scene
                                          Prerequisite Charms: Armor-Rending Claw Fist

                                          To strike a Lunar is to taunt a tiger. All attacks against him suffer a -1 external penalty. In addition, when the Lunar successfully dodges or parries any close combat attack, he may launch a counterattack against the attacker. This Charm can be activated reflexively if activated together with Lunar Hero Form.


                                          DEN MOTHER METHOD

                                          Cost:                                           3m; Mins: Dexterity 4, Essence 2; Type: Reflexive
                                          Keywords: Combo-OK
                                          Duration: Until DVs refresh
                                          Prerequisite Charms: Lunar Hero Form

                                          When a companion of the Lunar is in danger, he runs to his friend's aid. This Charm allows the martial artist to make a reflexive Defend Other action to a target within the range of his Move rating. This is independent from the Lunar's own actions. He can only defend one specific target until his DVs refresh.


                                          RUNNING THROUGH THE HERD

                                          Cost:                                           5m, 1wp; Mins: Dexterity 5, Essence 3; Type: Reflexive (Step 2)
                                          Keywords: Combo-OK
                                          Duration: Instant
                                          Prerequisite Charms: Den Mother Method

                                          In a combination of leading the attack, fooling his attacker and quickly dodging out of the way, the Lunar redirects a close combat attack that would otherwise have missed or been blocked toward someone else. The Lunar must be aware of the attack, which must have another valid target within the range of a Move action. That attack then plays out as normal. An attack cannot be redirected back at the attacker.


                                          TERRIBLE WOLVERINE ONSLAUGHT

                                          Cost:                                           --; Mins: Dexterity 5, Essence 4; Type: Permanent
                                          Keywords: None
                                          Duration: Permanent
                                          Prerequisite Charms: Crouching Tiger Stance, Running Through the Herd

                                          When the martial artist activates Lunar Hero Form, he may choose to enter a state of bestial fury. Doing this increases the Move and Jump bonuses from the Form-type Charm to (Dexterity x 3) and his natural weapons do lethal damage and may parry ranged and lethal attacks without a stunt. If the martial artist is a Lunar, he may also benefit from Fury-OK Charms as if he were activating Relentless Lunar Fury.

                                          The drawback to this Charm is that in order to acquire these benefits, the Exalt must enter a state that works like an uncontrolled Berserk Anger Limit Break (Exalted, p. 105). This effect can be broken mid-scene by spending three points of temporary Willpower, but doing so also cancels Lunar Hero Form.

                                              Originally posted by Dayen View Post
                                              By obviating the need and desire for them?
                                              MA is still valuable to Lunars when they want to practice strange and 'external' powers, or when they want to study a formalized universal fighting style alongside of others.

                                              The Lunar Hero problem is that Lunars can already do everything a Lunar Hero style could grant them using their own charms -- there's not really enough of a thematic statement in saying "I'm a Lunar using Lunar Hero" vs "I'm a Lunar using Dexterity and Strength charms." But playing a Lunar using Fire Dragon or Ebon Shadow style offers you thematic and mechanical options that your regular Lunar charms wouldn't.

                                              (There is one advantage to hero styles -- they can be taught to outsiders and practiced with them, in theory -- but in practice that just led to serious mechanical and balance issues. Of course, that's not a problem unique to Lunar Hero, but there was less need for it than there was for the Ability-based hero styles, so it was always kind of a solution searching for a problem.)

                                              That said, if you want a replacement / reworking for Lunar Hero, I think the way to go would be something like the Immaculate styles -- MA styles that work well with existing Lunar abilities. I'd probably want to make it thematically distinct from the basic Lunar charmset, though, since the Immaculate styles also have this thematic that they're a way for DBs to transcend their limitations, and any Lunar-focused MA style isn't going to really offer that (so it needs to offer something else instead.) Certainly there's a lot of room for an MA style or two that happens to be useful if you've, say, got wings or claws or can charge on four legs.
