    2E Lunar - The Gravehound

    07-02-2026, 08:07 PM
    THE GRAVEHOUND

    Tandem vincimus — “In the end, we win.”

    No Moon Caste Lunar Exalt. Errata is definitive throughout.



    WHO HE IS

    He is known publicly as a Scavenger Lord of unusual refinement — a patron of scholars, a financier of expeditions into lost ruins, a collector of recovered texts and artifacts whose interests span every serious field of inquiry simultaneously. This is accurate. It is also incomplete.

    Among those who know more — and very few do — he is something else: a repository of knowledge so vast and so deep that even Exalts with centuries of personal experience and access to First Age libraries sometimes come to him in silence, asking questions they cannot answer through their own resources. He does not advertise this. The consultations happen privately, are never spoken of afterward, and both parties maintain the polite fiction that no meeting occurred. His interlocutors leave with what they came for. He leaves with a new name to investigate.

    What he is beneath both of these layers is what only his innermost circle and his own absorbed dead know: a No Moon Lunar Exalt whose spirit shape is the Gravehound — a necropolis scavenger that consumes the flesh of the dead and absorbs their memories, personality, and voice for weeks at a time — who has spent his life building a systematic methodology to convert those temporary absorptions into a permanent and continuously growing archive of everything exceptional minds have known across the history of Creation. He has consumed hundreds of lives. He has bound their ghosts. He has built three Revenant Families and trained their successors to continue expanding his network while he tends to higher-value acquisitions personally. MOTIVATION AND METHOD


    His Motivation is encompassing and synthesizing all knowledge in order to become the greatest loremaster in Creation — surpassing even Yu-Shan’s records. This is not ambition in the ordinary sense. It is a project specification, and it is well underway.

    The necromancy, the Memory Strategy, and the Revenant Houses are his method. They are the mechanisms through which the Motivation is pursued. The Gravehound is, first and last, a scholar. The fact that he achieves this by consuming the dead and binding their ghosts is simply his methodology — the most efficient path to his goal that he has found, and one that has proven extraordinarily effective.

    The encyclopedic recall. Counting the Elephant’s Wrinkles gives him perfect recall of anything he has ever observed. Everything he has read, every conversation he has had, every dossier he has written — all available on demand with complete accuracy. He does not misremember. He does not have to look things up. The roll (Intelligence 3 + Lore 4 = 7 dice) is a formality; the practical result is an infallible library with instant retrieval. The absorbed minds compound this beyond what any individual memory could contain: he has directly experienced the expertise of historians devoted to eras he had never studied, engineers who understood First Age artifacts from the inside, political operators who knew where the real leverage was in organizations that no longer exist. When he recalls these experiences, what he retrieves is not notes about a field but the living shape of another expert’s mastery of it.

    Counsel sought in secret. Word travels carefully among those who know. A Solar with a First Age library who cannot explain an artifact brings it to the Gravehound through intermediaries, quietly. A Sidereal with a gap in the celestial records consults him through one careful contact. A Dragon-Blooded magistrate whose family has held a position for eight generations without understanding a particular ancestral decision brings original documents to a meeting she will never formally record. What they receive is first-hand internal testimony from inside the minds of people who were present. He presents the answers as the product of his Scavenger Lord expertise and his extensive network of scholarly contacts. The answers are accurate. The provenance is unspoken. His interlocutors are sophisticated enough not to press the question too hard. II. NPC STATISTICS — |Experienced|

    IV. TACTICS — HOW THE GRAVEHOUND OPERATES IN CONFLICT

    V. SIDEREAL INVISIBILITY AND THE SALARY BACKGROUND

    SOURCE-VERIFIED SIDEREAL MECHANICS (Manual of Exalted Power: Sidereals)


    Arcane Fate is the effect by which people forget the Sidereals, not the mechanism by which Sidereals track others. Creatures outside fate are immune to Arcane Fate’s memory-erasure. Entities fully outside fate include: Primordials, Yozis, Neverborn, Deathlords, Third and Second Circle demons, noble Fair Folk, and Wyld-native creatures. Ghosts and First Circle demons are explicitly in a different category: “Lesser entities such as First Circle demons, ghosts, hobgoblins and Wyld mutants either retain vestigial connections to Creation or lack the power to resist the Loom of Fate.” Ghosts are thus not fully outside fate — they have vestigial connections. However, these connections are substantially weaker than those of living people whose fate threads are active and ongoing.

    Sidereal bandwidth: only 80 to 90 Sidereals are active at any given time, against 500+ Solars, Lunars, and Abyssals “running roughshod over fate.” The Sidereals book explicitly notes that the typical response to a difficult target is inflicting two short-lived curses and then moving on to the next of the 500-plus. The Convention on Deathlords is the largest of the special conventions and consumes significant Sidereal resources monitoring actual Deathlords — entities that spring from beyond Creation and rarely leave their shadowland fiefdoms. A No Moon Lunar conducting careful, low-profile necromancy through family intermediaries simply does not register as a priority concern against this backdrop. REASON ONE: METHOD OF OPERATION


    The Gravehound’s primary operations involve living people (whose fate threads look like unremarkable scholarly patronage), dead people (whose fate threads are complete and resolved), preserved bodies being consumed (physical objects; not fate-bearing entities), and ghost servants (vestigial fate connections, not the active threads of the living). The Revenant Families are ordinary Eastern families with ancestor cult traditions — one of the most culturally common phenomena in the region. Their supernatural members have attenuated fate connections by nature.

    The Icehouse model eliminates the most obvious investigative thread entirely. He is almost never present when his targets die. The Rune of Sweet Passing is applied during the patron relationship, potentially months or years before death. The Revenant Family receives the body, packs it in salt for preservation, and manages the newly-formed ghost through offerings while the Gravehound attends to other business. He may arrive days, weeks, or well over a year after the death to conduct the consumption in private. The death happens among the target’s own colleagues and loved ones. His later visit appears as a memorial or archival review — entirely normal patron behavior. A Sidereal investigation would need to separately establish: the dispersed pattern of natural deaths among his former contacts; his subsequent private visits to those families significantly after the deaths; what those visits actually involve; and the subsequent ghost bindings conducted in the Underworld. Each step is harder than the last, and the temporal gap severs the most obvious investigative thread at its first link. REASON TWO: BUREAUCRATIC LEGITIMACY — THE SALARY BACKGROUND


    The Salary Background is confirmed in the Manual of Exalted Power: Sidereals and the Compass of Celestial Directions Vol. 3 (Yu-Shan). It represents income paid in Quintessence and Ambrosia by the Celestial Bureaus. The Yu-Shan book notes that Salary is worth approximately Resources +2 in Creation; Salary 2 provides meaningful material support while keeping the arrangement modest.

    The Department of Universal Human Affairs within the Bureau of Humanity is the canonical home for this arrangement. The Department covers “gods of concepts and ideas developed or used by humanity.” It is, crucially, nearly empty: its director Agralzo (God of Discovery, whose domain encompassed discovery, creation, and invention) was assassinated during the Usurpation and has been imprisoned for millennia, weakening the entire Department. The Yu-Shan book describes the Department as having “echoing corridors and dusty offices,” with some deities expanding their personal workspace simply because no one is watching and others keeping to their own offices “ignoring the empty spaces on all sides.” It is the least supervised department in one of the Bureau’s least supervised divisions.

    Minor deities of concepts related to preservation of knowledge, recovery of lost understanding, or custodianship of intellectual heritage would find the Gravehound genuinely useful: his Scavenger Lord work produces information relevant to their domains, and his periodic reports strengthen their conceptual holdings. In return, they provide a legitimate employment record and modest Quintessence payments. The arrangement is real — he provides genuine value — but the bureaucratic record is what matters for operational cover. A Sidereal pulling his fate-thread sees: authorized consultant, Department of Universal Human Affairs, Bureau of Humanity, Eastern field operations, knowledge preservation. The record is clean. The deeper operational reality is not visible at this surface.

    A further useful nuance (Yu-Shan p.62, describing the Bureau of Nature): an alliance between a Bureau and the Silver Pact is not in itself against the laws of Yu-Shan, but deities providing significant aid pay the customary bribes to the censors and celestial lions of the Bureau of Heaven — the limits of what a god may do in Creation being vaguely defined and readily interpreted to suit the censor in question. The operative risk to an arrangement like the Gravehound's is therefore not illegality but a lapsed bribe or a censor looking for leverage.

    The Manual of Exalted Power: Sidereals confirms that a small number of Sidereals — the Silver Faction, few enough to count on one hand — actively trade favors with Lunar allies. A Silver Faction Sidereal who has come to the Gravehound for counsel (as others come in secret) represents another potential layer of protection: not institutional cover, but a personal relationship with someone who can quietly ensure that any astrological investigation of the Gravehound is routed toward low priority or buried in procedural delay. REASON THREE: BANDWIDTH AND FOCUS


    The Convention on Deathlords is the largest of the special Sidereal conventions, consuming significant resources tracking actual Deathlords who “spring from beyond Creation.” These are entities with armies, shadowland fiefdoms, and active campaigns against Creation. Against this threat profile, a Lunar necromancer whose operations look like scholarly patronage and ancestor cult practice from every external angle is not a priority. The 500+ Celestial Exalted overwhelming 80-90 active Sidereals means the Viziers must be ruthlessly selective about where they invest investigative resources. The Gravehound has deliberately structured his operations to be unremarkable at every level of casual observation, which is precisely the profile that gets deprioritized in favor of more obvious threats.

    His operational invisibility is a consequence of method, not of supernatural concealment. It is maintained by structure: operations conducted in the dead space outside active fate threads, institutional legitimacy from a legitimate celestial employment record, and activity that looks like exactly what it appears to be on the surface — a remarkably effective scholarly patron whose subjects die naturally and whose family networks maintain strong ancestor cult traditions. VI. THE MEMORY STRATEGY — OPERATIONAL IMPLEMENTATION

    PRESERVATION — ICE AND SALT


    The Revenant Families maintain two standard preservation methods, with salt packing as the default.

    Salt packing: the primary historical meat preservation method prior to refrigeration, requiring only a sealed container and coarse salt. Pack the body completely in coarse salt. The salt draws moisture from tissue osmotically over three to seven days — the purging phase. Once complete, the flesh enters a stable state maintainable for months to years without ongoing attention. Salting does not destroy physical structure; salted meat retains biological integrity. Viable in all climates. The Gravehound arrives days, weeks, or well over a year after death and consumes from the salt-packed body — a small amount only, as no minimum quantity is specified in the source text. The body remains substantially intact, maintaining appearances and preserving the physical link for Summon Ghost.

    Ice storage: cold cellar or icehouse slowing decomposition. Requires ongoing ice; most effective in cold climates or where ice can be stocked. Used alongside salt packing for maximum preservation time.

    Whether preservation maintains supernatural memory content: a Storyteller call; no freshness requirement is specified in the source for the Gravehound’s ability. The assumption that methods maintaining physical integrity also maintain supernatural memory content is reasonable.


    THE COMPLETE OPERATIONAL CYCLE


    Pre-operational: relationship cultivation as a legitimate patron; Rune of Sweet Passing applied during the relationship at a natural private moment, early — not at the deathbed; Revenant Family icehouse prepared with salt packing as default.

    At death: the Rune’s bees notify the caster instantly, at any distance. He does not need to be present.

    Preservation window: the Revenant Family manages the body and the newly-formed ghost. Ghost-Blooded family members conduct regular offerings at a shrine near the icehouse, sustaining the ghost’s Essence and keeping it oriented toward the family rather than wandering. Days to over a year may pass.

    Consumption: a private visit; a small amount consumed from the preserved body; the body remains substantially intact. The memory window opens (days = victim’s Willpower; typically 7-9 days for the scholars and officials who are primary targets, providing one to three weeks of active window time). The absorbed personality functions as a living internal model — the character can ask what the victim would have known but never written down and surface the answer from that simulation. The voice is available for impersonation, correspondence management, and the appearance that the target has departed on research or is in extended illness.

    Dossier construction: structured written output of the window, organized around formal profile (genuine expertise versus nominal authority), organizational intelligence (informal power structure, real priorities, actual resource flows), personal intelligence (Motivation and Intimacies, private arrangements, vulnerabilities), operational intelligence (caches, credentials, access procedures), and target acquisition intelligence (names and approaches for the next tier of persons of interest). Each dossier naturally provides the targeting information for the next operation.

    Team debrief: colleagues with specialized knowledge can question the character during the window from within their specializations, receiving answers from the absorbed perspective — an interactive debrief of someone no longer alive to be questioned.

    At window close: Summon Ghost ritual begun during the final day; the intact body serves as the physical link; the ghost has not had time to wander. Contest (WP 9 + Essence 3) vs. ghost’s resisted pool; additional 5m expenditure removes one die from ghost’s pool; Peripheral 50m allows forcing difficult bindings.

    Post-binding management: Gathering a Ghost’s Strings (10m) profiles Fetters and Motivation immediately. Five Gifts (12m) before any interrogation. Seat of Deadly Splendors (15m) for formal sessions. Smoothing the Crease-Worn Mind (20m) for Void-touched or Labyrinth-exposed sources.

    For existing ghosts encountered in the field: Bonfire Visions draws them; Trolling the Dark Water forces materialization; Summon Ghost binds. No consumption window — ghost knowledge via interrogation only. VII. THE ACCUMULATED MIND


    After absorbing dozens, hundreds, eventually thousands of exceptional minds, the Gravehound carries a reservoir of lived experience functionally similar to what Frank Herbert described as Other Memory in the Dune novels — the Bene Gesserit Reverend Mothers’ access to the accumulated consciousness of generations of ancestors. His version differs: his absorptions are chosen, not inherited; they include people entirely outside his bloodline; his recall is perfect via Counting the Elephant’s Wrinkles rather than requiring the agonizing integration the Reverend Mothers undergo. But the functional result — a mind that has lived many lives and has complete access to all of them — is similar.

    Emotional regulation: having experienced suffering, ambition, love, fear, and loss from hundreds of inside perspectives, and observed from each where those states led, he has extraordinary control over his own responses. Not coldness — he feels things — but he knows what he is feeling, knows its origin, knows exactly what acting on it would produce, and makes a deliberate choice rather than a reflexive one.

    Lie detection without supernatural aid: having experienced deception from both sides in dozens of versions, he recognizes its texture through pattern-matched experience. Combined with the Gemstone of Surface Thoughts when acquired, effective deception against him in face-to-face interaction becomes extremely difficult.

    Non-linear ethics: having lived inside the ethical frameworks of saints, criminals, soldiers, and administrators, and observed how each justified itself from the inside, his ethics are empirical — refined over hundreds of observed lives into an understanding of what actually produces flourishing. He is not bound by any tradition’s specific prohibitions.

    Temporal perspective: many absorbed minds worked on projects spanning decades. He does not rush targets, organizations, or relationships. He has experienced the cost of premature demands from inside the minds of people who made them.

    Language facility: having experienced the thought patterns of many languages from inside, new languages are less alien. Storyteller discretion: XP discounts or bonus dice on Linguistics are defensible given the accumulated linguistic experience.

    Identity and the self: his answer to the philosophical challenge of containing many absorbed minds is that he is the thing that consumes and preserves. The Motivation is not just a mechanical trait but the existential organizing principle that defines his self in the face of what would otherwise be a destabilizing multiplicity. He is not any of the people he has absorbed. He is the entity that contains them all and puts them to use.

    The predatory dimension: his relationships with targets are genuine; the Intimacies are real; the benefits he provides are real. The relationship ends in consumption, and he has known this from the moment it began. He has made peace with this because, from inside hundreds of absorbed minds, he has directly experienced how those same exceptional people would have felt about preservation over oblivion. Most of them, he knows from personal internal experience, would have preferred this.

    When he decides someone is ready: the patience is analytical, not sentimental. When he determines a target has reached peak value, or that circumstances threaten to remove them before they are secured, he acts without hesitation regardless of the state of the patron relationship. The warmth of the relationship does not interfere with the calculation. He has absorbed too many people who delayed a necessary action out of misplaced sentiment to mistake sentiment for wisdom. VIII. PRODUCING SUPERNATURAL LINEAGE

    GOD-BLOODED CHILDREN


    Fathered in human or near-human form with mortal women arranged through the Revenant Families. Resulting God-Blooded: physically exceptional; confirmed eligible for Terrestrial Circle Sorcery (White Treatise). They serve as the inner organizational tier — managers, Terrestrial Circle sorcerers, liaisons between the patron and the families’ operations. Each represents a sorcerous investment requiring no Charm slots, XP, or Essence from the Gravehound himself beyond fathering them and providing the training environment. BEASTMEN


    Fathered in DBT war form. Resulting offspring carry Gravehound-human hybrid characteristics. Possible inherited traits (Storyteller call): enhanced Perception (Gravehound template Per 4), sensitivity to supernatural activity, possibly partial ghost-sight. Serve as physical agents, enforcers, and field operatives in roles where their nature need not be hidden. GHOST-BLOODED THROUGH THE STABLE


    Ghost servants who have developed the Evoke the Ancient Clay Arcanos (specifically Birth the Warrior Form) are selected and conditions arranged for regular contact with willing living family members.

    Finding — conception requires a second Arcanos. Corporeal form alone does not produce Ghost-Blooded offspring. The canonical mechanism is Fertile Soul Endowment (Books of Sorcery Vol. V — The Roll of Glorious Divinity II; Essence-Measuring Thief Arts cascade: Aura-Reading Technique → Delicious Essence Scent → Essence-Devouring Ghost Touch → Blending the Streams of Essence → Feeding Life's Fountain → Fertile Soul Endowment; cost 10m, 1wp, 1lhl). A ghost selected for the breeding program therefore needs both a corporeal-form Arcanos (Birth the Warrior Form, or Weighted with the Anchor of Flesh) and the completed Essence-Measuring Thief Arts cascade. This is a substantial Arcanoi investment per breeding ghost and should be treated as a limiting factor on lineage expansion rather than an assumed capability — a practical reason the families cultivate a small number of dedicated progenitor ghosts rather than breeding indiscriminately from the stable.

    Resulting Ghost-Blooded: natural ghost-sight always; confirmed Shadowlands Circle necromancy access (Underworld compass source-verified); near-certain Fetter formation at death; deep cultural connection to both sides of the family. Self-sustaining lineage over three to four generations. Ghost-Blooded are the Memento Mori network’s independent necromantic operatives, conducting Rune of Sweet Passing, Summon Ghost, and ghost management operations without the Gravehound’s personal presence. IX. MEMENTO MORI — THE REVENANT FAMILIES AND STRATEGIC DOCTRINE

    WHY THE REVENANT FAMILIES EXIST — THREE PURPOSES


    Purpose One: Operational concealment. The Gravehound never acts directly where a Revenant Family can act instead. Every contact, every identified candidate, every icehouse, every summoned ghost is a layer of separation between observable action and its architect. Anyone investigating a given operation encounters the relevant family. Anyone investigating the family encounters their legitimate public activities. The Gravehound appears only as a patron at arm’s length. His Bureau of Humanity employment record provides a further institutional layer: the patron is also a minor celestial official engaged in authorized knowledge-preservation work. The organization is structured so that the further anyone digs, the more legitimate everything appears — right up until the point where they would need to understand what a No Moon Lunar necromancer’s spirit shape actually does.

    Purpose Two: Sorcery access without direct investment. The Gravehound has devoted his Essence and Charm capacity to Shadowlands Circle necromancy. He has not purchased Terrestrial Circle Sorcery — a deliberate prioritization. Through the Revenant Families, he has access to an expanding pool of Terrestrial Circle sorcerers (God-Blooded children in the Atelier) and independent Shadowlands Circle necromancers (Ghost-Blooded members across all three families). Their elemental servants and utility spells extend his operational reach without requiring any of his personal Charm slots, Essence commitment, or XP investment.

    Purpose Three: Networked intelligence expansion — ghosts finding ghosts. Each bound ghost generates targeting intelligence for additional acquisitions. Each new acquisition generates more. Ghost-Blooded members conduct follow-up summonings the Gravehound never personally attends. Ghost operatives actively recruit from their own pre-death networks, approaching former colleagues in the Underworld and creating voluntary cooperation before any binding attempt. A ghost who arrives willing requires none of the coercive infrastructure and can be trusted with more complex tasks. The system is self-directing and self-expanding, operating continuously across multiple regions without requiring his personal presence at each node. THE THREE FAMILIES: POLICY AND METHOD


    Each Revenant Family shares the same fundamental organizational principle as the Gravehound himself: a Policy (the institutional motivation — encompassing and synthesizing all knowledge in their specialized sphere) and a Method (the practical operational techniques through which that Policy is pursued). This is not coincidence; it is deliberate organizational design. Families that pursue their own institutional policy goals with genuine commitment are more effective, more resilient, and more trustworthy than those that merely execute orders. The Gravehound’s Motivation and the families’ Policies are aligned, not identical; each family pursues its own sphere of knowledge-synthesis, and in doing so serves the greater whole.

    Recruitment across all families draws on the same principles. The long slow build: the ask comes after months or years of genuine benefit delivered without visible strings. First contact looks like a resource offered, a problem solved, a connection made. Nothing more. The MICE framework (Money, Ideology, Coercion, Ego) applies — with positive inducements strongly preferred, since cultivated loyalty produces enduring relationships that eventually yield willing ghost servants, while coercive recruitment creates unstable assets. For Ghost-Blooded candidates, the most compelling inducement is facilitated contact with their ghost parent through the organization’s resources. For God-Blooded, it is sorcerous training unavailable elsewhere and a community of others like them. THE GREY WARDENS — Intelligence, Espionage, Guerilla Tradecraft, and Creation Influence


    Policy: Encompass and synthesize all intelligence, tradecraft knowledge, and operational expertise bearing on the security and influence of the Memento Mori network and its interests.

    Method: Active recruitment of intelligence professionals and their social networks; systematic ghost stable development focused on operational experience; Ghost-Blooded agents with natural ghost-sight for spirit-world surveillance; living operator networks for Creation-side intelligence gathering; ghost-to-ghost referral expanding the stable’s intelligence coverage continuously.

    The Grey Wardens are not merely a service for the Gravehound. Their intelligence archive and operational network constitute genuine power in Creation. They know things about current political configurations, security arrangements, organizational vulnerabilities, and interpersonal dynamics that no other entity in their operational area has. Their living operators provide real services to real clients who value what they can deliver. Their ghost advisors have operational experience spanning decades or centuries across the organizations they penetrated in life. This influence provides another layer of insulation around the Gravehound: an organization that matters in its own right, with its own stakeholders and clients, is much harder to dismantle cleanly than one that is transparently a front.

    Every ghost in the Grey Warden stable generates targeting intelligence for additional acquisitions: who else knew things, who operated in adjacent domains, who should be in the stable and isn’t yet. A dead intelligence officer knows their entire contact network. A dead military commander knows their chain of command, their rivals, and which of their subordinates were actually competent. Failed Grey Warden operatives are preserved and bound — their specific failure modes become training material for successors. The organization does not waste its dead. THE ATELIER — Sorcery, Magewrights, and Essence Technology


    Policy: Encompass and synthesize all technical, sorcerous, and essence-technological knowledge — with particular emphasis on recovering what was lost at the end of the First Age.

    Method: Terrestrial Circle sorcerers from the Gravehound’s God-Blooded lineage as the organizational backbone; magewright craftspeople and artificers in living membership; ghost stable systematically focused on First Age engineers, Shogunate sorcerers, and esoteric science scholars; deliberate ghost-to-ghost knowledge synthesis as the primary mechanism for expanding the stable’s collective technical capability.

    The Atelier’s living sorcerers provide immediate practical magical support — elemental summoning, artifact maintenance, Essence system management — without requiring the patron’s involvement. Their ghost stable is the organization’s most technically specialized resource and its most valuable long-term asset. The synthesis is the key feature: newly acquired ghosts are introduced to existing specialists in the stable; they share knowledge across temporal gaps; the combination produces insights neither held individually. The Favored Student Charm formalizes these relationships; Eternally Loyal Student Prana embeds specific technical knowledge permanently in ghost-student minds. The Atelier’s collective expertise compounds continuously. THE BONE HOUSE — Ancestor Cult, Funerary Traditions, and Ghost Mastery


    Known to itself and to outsiders as the House of the Gravehound. Fully developed in its own companion document, Bone House, which covers its internal culture, the salt interment and reciprocal-harvest practices, its spectral ranks, and its current Shadow-Patriarch, Malachi of the Gravehound — a Ghost-Blooded necromancer with all thirteen Memento Mori spells, who conducts the family's bindings without the patron's presence. That document is written from inside the House and deliberately reflects only what the family knows of its founder (see Information Compartmentalization, below).

    Policy: Encompass and synthesize all knowledge of the dead — their traditions, their wisdom, their accumulated experience, and the practices that allow the living and the dead to share knowledge and power across the boundary of death.

    Method: Generalist ghost collection across all fields and eras; Honored Ancestor Ways Arcanoi development as the primary value-creation mechanism for the family’s ghosts; systematic ghost-to-ghost Arcanoi propagation; Essence sustenance infrastructure that sustains the entire Memento Mori network; active recruitment from all confirmed Shadowlands Circle practitioner categories.

    The Bone House is responsible for the Essence sustenance of the broader Memento Mori network through offerings, maintained shrines, and funerary practice. Its ghost stable develops Honored Ancestor Ways over time — Courier in Dreams, Honorable Descendant Blessing, Dishonorable Descendant Curse — creating observable, verifiable reciprocity between living family members and their dead. The living maintain traditions because the results are demonstrable. The relationship does not require faith. The Bone House recruits from all confirmed Shadowlands Circle categories and actively investigates under-investigation categories; any practitioner with affinity for death Essence is a potential candidate. THE BINDING STRUCTURE — THE NETWORK AS A PARENT DOMINION


    The three families are held together by a structure rather than by any treaty between them, and the Gravehound is the structure. Under the dominion rules (§XIII.14) the Memento Mori network is statted as a parent dominion in its own right — Magnitude 4, Military 1, Culture 4, Government 2 — with the Bone House and the Grey Wardens as its children at Magnitude 3, and the Atelier at Magnitude 2 as a child of the Bone House. Treating a covert, non-territorial network as a dominion rests on Magnitude being an abstract measure of capability rather than of territory, and should be confirmed at the table [ST choice].

    The arrangement is chosen because free parent-and-child backing is enormously better than the treaty that nominally provides the same thing. Tiger Shelters Cub Patronage requires Government 4 and Craft 3 that no family possesses, costs 2 Limit and a committed Willpower, demands an underlying treaty, and loads the recipient with 1 Limit every turn it runs. Backing between parent and child has no minimums, no cost, no treaty requirement, no Limit burden, and runs indefinitely.

    What each family receives. Backing raises a child's Attributes by the Magnitude difference, but only where the parent's rating is higher — so the parent's profile determines exactly what each child gains and what it does not. The Bone House takes Culture 3 to 4. The Grey Wardens take Culture 1 to 2, repairing their weakest Attribute. The Atelier takes Government 1 to 2.

    That last figure is the deliberate one. Government 2 is the precise minimum for trade rights, so the Atelier can hold a treaty of any kind only while the Bone House backs it, and the Bone House can only do so while the network stands above them both. It is the clearest illustration of the design principle: a child positioned one dot below a critical threshold and carried across it by backing alone is permanently dependent while being treated generously.

    What the structure deliberately omits. The prohibition on hostile action runs between parent and child, not between siblings. The Bone House and the Grey Wardens remain entirely free to act against each other, as do the Grey Wardens and the Atelier. Every relationship the Gravehound occupies is protected; every lateral relationship is left open. A network in which the families could not fight would not require an arbiter, and he intends to remain one.

    Severability. Withdraw the backing and each family loses its Attribute lift immediately, the Atelier falls below the treaty threshold and out of every agreement it holds, the trade flows between them decay unreplenished within a few turns, and all three become able to act against him for the first time. He is entirely content for the families to use and profit from everything he supplies — the profit is what makes the arrangement hold — but every element of it terminates with him.

    The thing to watch. Any family investing in Government toward 5 alongside Occult 3 is reaching for the supernatural alliance that would let the families bind directly to one another rather than through him. All three sit far short. A Shadow-Patriarch buying Government dots for no operational reason is a Shadow-Patriarch worth a visit.
    INFORMATION COMPARTMENTALIZATION — WHAT THE FAMILIES KNOW


    The concealment described in Purpose One is not directed solely outward. The Revenant Families are themselves compartmented, and none of them holds the whole picture.

    What a family knows: that its founder and patron is a No Moon Lunar called the Gravehound; that his spirit shape consumes the flesh of the dead and takes their memories and voice; that he established the line and continues to sustain it; and that he takes a close, continuing interest in the smooth operation of that family in particular — shrines maintained, interments properly kept, the ghost stable growing and well-ordered, the bloodline sound. He is generous to a family that runs well and coldly attentive to one that does not.

    What a family does not know: his Motivation; the scale and purpose of the archive he is assembling; the existence and specialization of the other two families, beyond vague awareness that the patron has interests elsewhere; and that its own work feeds a project far larger than itself. Senior members generally infer that there is more to him than they are told, and have learned that inquiry is unwelcome and unrewarding. They do not inquire. The arrangement holds because it is genuinely good for them: each family receives a patron of immense capability who asks only that they excel at what they already wish to do, and is otherwise left to govern itself.

    This is deliberate design rather than mere secrecy. A family that pursues its own Policy with real conviction is more effective, more resilient, and more durable than one executing orders it half-understands — and a family that cannot describe the whole operation cannot betray it, whatever pressure is brought to bear. The Shadow-Patriarchs' incuriosity is itself an asset. COMPOUND GROWTH — GHOSTS FINDING GHOSTS


    The referral network is the most strategically significant element of the entire operation. Each bound ghost knows who else knew things. A bound First Age engineer can identify ten former colleagues whose ghosts are potentially findable in the Underworld and can describe their specific knowledge domains, their characteristic Intimacies and Fetters, and how to approach them in ways that create voluntary cooperation before any binding attempt. Ghost operatives in the stable can conduct Underworld-side surveillance, approaching identified ghosts proactively on the organization’s behalf. Over years and decades, this becomes a self-directing network whose growth is driven by the stable’s own knowledge of what it is missing.

    Each Revenant Family operates its own version of this expansion model tuned to its specialization: the Grey Wardens follow professional intelligence networks; the Atelier follows scholarly and technical lineages; the Bone House provides the generalist referral hub that services the other two families when identified persons of interest don’t fall cleanly into either specialized domain. Failed members contribute to the stable as cautionary and institutional archives — their specific failure modes become training material, and their inside knowledge of the organization at the time of their death provides a continuous internal audit capacity.

    The long-term trajectory of this system is a privately maintained institution with access to knowledge and expertise that no other entity in Creation can match — staffed by dead specialists from across every era, self-directing in its expansion, and operating through an organizational structure that makes its ultimate controller effectively invisible to casual or routine investigation. This is what the Revenant Families exist to build. The Gravehound’s Motivation — surpassing even Yu-Shan’s records — is not ambition. It is a project specification, and it is well underway.
