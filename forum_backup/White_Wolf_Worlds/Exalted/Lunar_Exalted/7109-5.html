                                                            Originally posted by Valles View Post

                                                            ...I'm not sure if you're trolling or you genuinely can't see that you're using exactly the same logic and language that gave us Epic Motivations in the first place. Either way, I don't see any point in my continuing the conversation. Have fun, folks.
                                                            Enh...I think there's a distinction there. It was not immediately obvious, but I think it's been teased out with time, and if I'm reading Kyeudo right, it's best demonstrated by am example:

                                                            Say there were two farmers, Farmer A and Farmer B. Both are happy to sit on their farm, have a small family, grow vegetables. Neither have an Epic Motivation (unless it's, like, "CABBAGES!!!" or something). A Dragon-Blood and some bandits come by and burn the farms to the ground, and kill the farmers' families. The farmers stand up for themselves in a moment of heroism, but it's not enough.

                                                            Now, in a purely hypothetical time-pause, Lytek or someone comes by and tells the farmers, "I've got a shiny new Exaltation you can have. But first, I need to know what you'll do with it?"

                                                            Farmer A says, "I'll get the biggest sword I can find and kill that Dragon-Blood and those bandits!," or "I'll rebuild my farm, even better (or maybe just faster)!," or maybe "I'll persuade kings and princes that this should never have come to pass and unite all the local provinces!," or perhaps "I'll fill out the VERY BEST forms I can to petition the government for financial aid packages!," or "Fuck farming, I'ma learn Sorcery!", or any of a million other things.

                                                            Farmer B says, "Eh, I'll probably go lie down, have some drinks, maybe cry a little. Eventually, I might get up and putter around, or fix things up a bit, though I'm in no hurry, and I might starve to death first. You know, maybe instead of Exalting me, you could make all of this just not have happened...?"

                                                            Farmer A Exalts, and still doesn't necessarily have an Epic Motivation. Farmer B does not.

                                                            I'll admit that it might be a silly distinction-- I can imagine a lot of people having trouble imagining that Farmer B would even exist. But I -think- it was the distinction being made.
