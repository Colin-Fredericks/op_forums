                    Originally posted by Sunder the Gold View Post
                    Luna would have similar standards. You have to value freedom more than life itself. Why would she settle for lesser heroes?
                    Because being a lesser hero often signifies one's potential to grow into a greater one. Because opportunities for meaningful, more dramatic acts of heroism are often limited. Because Luna's available talent pool (people who are oppressed/trapped- oppressed by people specifically threatening death- who value potential freedom over concrete things like any family they'd be leaving behind if they died, who are oppressed so egregiously and completely that they're motivated to die fighting it rather than go on living in such a state, who have absolutely nothing of worth in their lives other than the idea of freedom, who also somehow have the opportunity to show this feeling in a big risky stand against their oppressors) is otherwise pretty small. (And lessened even more by the fact that people willing to risk their lives over 'the principle of the thing' tend to be utter maniacs. A Lunar should be the guy who persuaded his village's satrap to lower its unfair tax rates- not the frothing fanatic who attacks that same satrap and gets killed in a meaningless, morale-shattering display of defiance. Heroism is about preserving the good parts of life- or life in general- not just tearing down the bad. Especially when doing the latter might only make things worse by getting everyone else punished for your actions.)
                    Would it be nice if, say, all the applicants for a robotics job had brought homemade A.I in to let the interviewer judge their skills firsthand? Yes, but in practice that will never happen. So humans invented things like diplomas and references as a cheaper, more portable way of doing the same thing. We have to extrapolate on some level, or the post will never be filled.

                                                "Action" was never a requirement for Exaltation in past editions, whether as a Solar, Lunar or Dragon Blooded and I am uncertain if it is mandatory in this edition. Of course, I admit that I'm not even really sure what you mean by "action" in the first place. After Exalting most Exalted do undertake various actions to change the world around them. In fact, I can't think of a single one who hasn't tried changing something about the world and their place in it after Exaltation, even if it's just using their powers to do the exact same things they used to do only better.

                                                In other words, I think action is a very natural consequence of Exaltation and the change in perspective, power and magical abilities it brings rather than a requirement.

                                                In my mind the most important thing for a would-be Lunar is the decision to break free from their chains, even if they don't have any other goal or desire in mind at that time. What they choose to do afterwards isn't as important the initial chain breaking - that's what attracts Luna's attention to them.

                                                Oftentimes decision is coupled with action, such as the woman who leaps into a pool of sharks to escape slavery. Certainly among Solars I would guess that the number who Exalted "while taking action" exceeds the number who didn't. I also accept that Luna and the Sun may have different standards for Exaltation. Still, I think simply recognizing that there are no chains on you, that the only person keeping you chained is yourself and casting those chains off can be just as worthy of Luna's attention.

                                                        Kratos, from the God of War series.
