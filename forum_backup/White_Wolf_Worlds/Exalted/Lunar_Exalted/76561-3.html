            Originally posted by Saur Ops Specialist View Post
            Territory is another possible pillar, Within lands claimed by Lunars, their opponents are at a severe disadvantage. Den-Realm shenanigans galore.
            If territory Charms below Essence 6 remain a thing, then they generally need to be effects you can plunk down and leave behind.

            Boundary-Marking Meditation is such. You use it to instantly claim an uncontested land as yours*, but the Charm doesn't require you to stay within those borders to retain that ownership.

            So, Watchful Spider Stance should probably work the same way, because young adventuring Lunars and PCs in general need to be highly mobile. If there are territory-awareness effects that absolutely need to require your presence within the territory, that's what the Grandfather Spider Mastery upgrade Charm is for.


            * Incidentally, it should probably help you with efforts to claim contested land, as well. See, Boundary-Marking Meditation only expedites a process that even a mortal could accomplish with some time, let alone a Lunar Exalt who uses Charisma. As-is, the Charm completely fails if local terrestrial gods (or other creatures tied to the land) simply refuse acknowledge your right to rule their domain.

            Boundary Marking Meditation, I think, should thus provide you bonuses towards social actions (including Intimidate) that get gods within your marked boundary to acknowledge your dominance. This preserves the story potential of struggling against local spirits for control of the land while also making sure you don't resent your Charm being utterly useless in the face of resistance.

            However, if BMM is deemed powerful enough because it lets you instantly claim small territories (such as houses, boats, or inconspicuous stretches of road where you plan to ambush someone), then claiming stretches of land large enough to spirit's strong and proud enough to contest the Charm probably does require an upgrade for dealing with such problems.


            Given the general want to not build or produce infrastructure, Lunars might even end up benefitting more from having uncapped Demesnes. Take the Manse, break everything, and let the energies run rampant. They're not going to be able to use it, but if you have some method of channeling the power through your body...
            Even better if those Charms also benefit from having a manse after all.

            The same method that lets you respire energy from an uncapped demesne that you own, as long as you and it are within the same owned territory, could also double to let you respire as though you were in your manse, as long as you are in the same owned territory while holding its hearthstone.

                                                    Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post
                                                    Then the Lunar tactic of "flare anima and then fly away giggling at the silly Dragon Blooded who'll waste time going to where I flared my anima" won't really work, will it?
                                                    That's like, the entire point I've been trying to make.

                                                    ​The Wyld Hunt doesn't chase after anyone who flares their Anathema anima precisely because they've discovered how that's a great way to be drawn into a snipe hunt, because they've run into Anathema who have done that sort of thing in the past.

                                                    It's important to remember that the Wyld Hunt has changed radically since the Scarlet Empress vanished. It's also important to remember that the Wyld Hunt has changed and morphed in various ways over the last 700+ years (as, inevitably, any organization will over time), plus it has adapted to various tactics used by their enemies in order to change how they engage them.

                                                    There's nothing wrong with describing tactics that the Lunar Exalted came up with in the past to thwart the Hunt, and the ways in which the Hunt adapted to overcome those tactics, along with tactics that Lunars still use today. That sort of thing is very useful for, first, showing that the Lunars aren't totally helpless in the face of the Wyld Hunt, and second, for giving players and storytellers ideas for how to deal with or use the Wyld Hunt.

                                                    This is good for Dragon Blooded PC's, Solar PCs and especially Lunar PCs, since Lunars have traditionally been forced to bare the brunt of the Wyld Hunt much more than anyone else.
