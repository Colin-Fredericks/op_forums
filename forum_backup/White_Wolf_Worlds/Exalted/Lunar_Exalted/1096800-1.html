    Originally posted by Jen View Post
    I'm still new to this whole RPG and story-making thing so my lazy way is that I usually find a base/default reaction for each splat (DB Dynast want to kill Solar due to propaganda for example), create a character then see what they think about the default reaction, so a "atheist" Dynast who don't believe the Immaculate will probably more friendly to my Circle of Solar.
    Ah, I understand. Well, though the reason you can talk about the default reaction for Dragonblooded or Sidereals is that they have big groups enforcing a similar view; the Immaculate religion, the Factions. That's why it kind of makes sense.

    For the Lunars in the books that's less pronounced, so it feels a bit harder to talk about a default view without getting into this idea of "Oh, there's a stereotypical Lunar reaction that's not really based on the individual circumstances and characters at hand. Without any big social group really enforcing uniformity.".

    I think if you did want to make characters who have a default view, there's a bunch of different Lunar factions in the books (depending on your edition!), and then you can make a character's default view very strongly driven by his faction ideology (though that maybe probably tends to be pretty unlikely in the canon).

        Okay, one thing I always think is this:

        The Pact probably has no official line on Solars. They probably don't have a general training policy of telling Lunars to work with Solars, or fight them, or whatever.

        Because they've spent 1500 years living in a world with 400 Lunars and about 10 Solars. They're pretty irrelevant. The main purpose of Solars in Lunar historiography, is to prove how awful the Realm and its Sidereal masters are, for brutally massacring the Solars.
        Most Lunars never expected the Solars to return. They're used to a world without Solars.

        So, sure, now the Solars are coming back, their common attitudes are likely to be on a general level a)can we use them as a weapon against the Realm, b)are they going to try and rule us again, because screw that.

        But that's not going to be any kind of general policy, which is probably more a bunch of Elders arguing about what to do, and not having decided yet, because they're over 1000 years old and 5 years is nothing (not to mention that in the first year, it probably wasn't 100% clear what had happened).
        It's not like the Lunars have any easy way to force everyone to follow any kind of general policy.


        I also expect that the older Lunars are likely to have a lot more general view of the Solars (ie those guys who ran the Old Realm), while younger Lunars are more likely to view them much more individually (this Shining Spear of Victory girl is great, whipping up the natives against the Realm with her golden powers. Not like that Sun Magus in his tower always stealing magical texts. Those texts should be mine! I've been a sorcer for 100 years!).
