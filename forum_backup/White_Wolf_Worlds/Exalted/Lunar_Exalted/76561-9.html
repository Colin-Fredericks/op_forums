    Lunars are defined by the Wyld Hunt in that almost any Dragon-Blooded in Creation could murder them and get commended for it. That does not in any way mean that every Dragon-Blooded in Creation is a threat. A Wyld Hunt lead by Realm shikari is far more dangerous than a gang of drunken outcastes lynching people over rumors. The Realm is scarier, but the fact remains that, if those outcastes managed to kill you, they'd be hailed as heroes. That is what's defining about the Wyld Hunt: It's a predominant culture that holds that even the scummiest Dragon-Blooded in the world would be justified in killing you, simply because of what you are.

                                Originally posted by Fitter Happier View Post

                                I'm gonna admit Fitter, at this point I have a tough time parsing what you're arguing. So let me go back and repeat your arguments and mine, as I understand them.

                                I said that the Wyld Hunt is a major tradition that, over the last thousand+ years has had a massive impact on the current social climate in which the Lunars find themselves.

                                You said the Wyld Hunt wasn't much of anything, that it was limited to an arm of the Immaculate Order.

                                I pointed out that it's a Creation-wide tradition carried out by Dragon Blooded from one end of the world to another.

                                And I'm not sure what you are arguing at this point? That those Outcaste Dragon Blooded don't engage in "real" Wyld Hunts? That they don't pose a threat to Lunars? That they don't kill Lunars? That Lunars don't care when a bunch of Outcastes stab them in the face? That non-Realm Wyld Hunts just don't matter?

                                Obviously those Outcaste Wyld Hunts drive Lunars from the territories those Outcastes claim. And obviously Lunars have died to those Wyld Hunts. And while a Realm Wyld Hunt is probably much more dangerous than an Outcaste Wyld Hunt, a group of Dragon Blooded trying to kill you is still a group of Dragon Blooded trying to kill you, doesn't matter if they have a Realm name or not.

                                I felt like the entire point of that Wyld Hunt spoiler was to show that the Realm wasn't the only enemy that Lunars, Solars and other "Anathema" can have. That, even if a Lunar or Solar tries to avoid the Realm and tries to go somewhere far beyond its power, he is still going to have to deal with the fact that the local Dragon Blooded may get together and try to kill him. "Just because the Realm isn't around doesn't mean that the Anathema are safe." That the Wyld Hunt is an ancient, world-wide tradition, that the Realm doesn't have a monopoly on it, and that Anathema need to watch their backs even if the Realm is far away.

                                If that's not what the writers intended, that's fine. But I think they will need to figure out a way to rephrase before the corebook is released.
