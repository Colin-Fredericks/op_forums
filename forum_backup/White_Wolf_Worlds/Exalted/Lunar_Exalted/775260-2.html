    Originally posted by WarDragon View Post
    I fail to see how Perfect Symmetry and Shaping the Ideal Form don't fit under Appearance? That wouldn't even necessarily keep them from having a Manipulation charm as a pre-req (Solars get some cross-Ability stuff in 3e, iirc).
    The Charm I've labeled "Perfect Symmetry" is about manipulating other people into thinking you aren't injured.

    And back when I was writing these concepts, I had to answer the question of "what determines whether it is Charisma, Manipulation, or Appearance"?

    The best answer I came up with was Sincerity, Lying, and Either/Or.


    Let's not get too literal about the word "appearance", since Manipulation is the Attribute that lets you "appear" honest so long as no one beats your Guile. Should the Appearance Attribute and its Charms affect your Guile just because the normal word "appearance" has a broader use than the Appearance Attribute's name?


    But whether or not Shaping the Ideal Form counts as lying for a Lunar Exalt is a matter of interpretation.

                Lunars should not have anything comparable to Wyld Shaping Technique. But I feel there's a middle-ground between Wyld-Shaping Technique and Ritual of Lunar Stability that hasn't been sufficiently explored.

                Lunars can't create permanent, ordered lands out of the Wyld? Fine. But could Lunars at least have the power to reshape the local Wyld into a particular brand of wonderland more favorable to them than to their enemies?

                Preferably with the ability to use Ritual of Lunar Stability not only to protect Creation from the Wyld, but to prevent raksha from trying to change altered stretches of Wyld into something unfavorable to the Lunar.


                Creation's history is being rewritten such that the Lunars actually fit within it, and so they do not need to be swept under the Wyld rug until the setting is ready for them to sudden start existing. Previously, Lunar history was written so that the Wyld pretty much stomped all over them, and Lunar Charms had to be written such that this made sense. Previously, Chimerism was made worse and more important than the Lunar Great Curse.

                That's not the case anymore. Now the Wyld Hunt and Sidereals are the chief historical dangers to the Lunar Exalted. Now the Lunars broke their own Castes themselves as a deliberate action to reinvent themselves for their new circumstances. Now Chimerism is similarly deliberate, and the Great Curse is the proper source of horror. Now the Lunars can be allowed to resist the Wyld more completely than before.


                I'm certainly not asking for the return of the Wyld keyword, but the Solar Exalted never needed that keyword to have good reasons for seeking out the Wyld, nor to have the power to make it their bitch.

                            Originally posted by Sunder the Gold View Post
                            Sleeping Dragon Awakens was a very flavorful and powerful Charm from 2.5 that didn't get long to shine. But I have recently realized that the character of the Charm can be preserved by replacing the Overdrive mechanic with bonuses to your Join Battle roll. Rather than building up a stock of Offensive motes to blow away invaders to your territory, you can build up a stock of Initiative to seize the first strike and possibly end the battle with a single Decisive blow. Or at least take out one of the invaders before engaging the others.


                            Who Disturbs My Slumber, created by Reminiscent Oasis, can do the same. Instead of gaining offensive motes upon being rudely awoken from your sleep, you gain automatic successes to your Join Battle roll.


                            Beast Calming Method would be a Charisma-based Charm that functions like Solar Friendship With Animals Approach, but without that Charm's Survival Ability limitations. With the Charm active, the Lunar doesn't register as a threat to anyone or anything -- wounded beasts, small prey animals, and mother hippos watching their young will allow him to approach and touch them, and territorial predators will allow him to pass through their hunting grounds unmolested so long as they are not hungry. The Lunar could even break into someone's home in full armor and bearing a bloody sword, and the homeowner will react with indignation regarding the trespass instead of fear for her life. Even the most convicted Immaculate, if she learned he was a Lunar Anathema, would believe that the silver demon meant her no harm. Those so affected will not mistake a lack of harmful intent for a lack of danger, however; Immaculate devotees will remember that devils are inherently corruptive, just as beasts that smell an infectious disease upon the Lunar would seek to flee or drive him away, and mice will take care that they are not trodden underfoot by accident.

                            The effects of this Charm end immediately upon the Lunar deciding to engage in hostilities with any other present character, or otherwise take an action that would indirectly harm them. Studying someone with premeditated intent to harm them later with the information counts, though plotting murder against someone not present would not. The Charm cannot be reactivated for the rest of the scene, and will not work again on witnesses to the Lunar's hostility until the next day.


                            Dog-Tongue Method. Speak any human language (or your own true voice!), so long as your current form is capable of vocalizing at all. Likewise, communicate with any animal species regardless of your form, so long as they communicate in a manner even vaguely possible with your form -- wearing the form of a wolf would be ineffective for conveying ideas to a species of octopus that exchange information primarily through changing color patterns. This Charm doesn't itself grant the Lunar knowledge of an animal species's language, nor incline animals to listen.

                            Stance-as-Thought Stride. As the Solar Charm, Poetic Expression Style, but it additionally allows the Lunar to communicate simple ideas to animal species even without knowing their language. Having the appropriate Survival specialty allows the communication of complex ideas beyond what the animal's own language ought to convey, though the animal's own intelligence still limits the complexity of what they can understand.

                            Glance Oration Technique. As Single-Voice Kata, but through body-language. The Lunar can speak, sing, or play an instrument in order to attract the attention of those who can hear but not immediately see him, but this Charm doesn't enable him to convey two different messages at once. Unlike Single Voice Kata, spirits and fair folk actually like this method of communication, but the Lunar's Appearance becomes capped by his Performance since these exotic beings are judging the delivery of his arguments based on the beauty of his interpretative dancing.

                            Secret Speech Method. A Manipulation Charm that allows the Lunar to communicate one message to an individual or small group, and an entirely different message to everyone else. Explicitly compatible with Glance Oration Technique, allowing the Lunar to hide the message of his body language from the broader audience, or from a particular individual or subgroup.
                            I'd like to see:
                            -FLESH-SCULPTING ART
                            -LUNAR BLOOD SHAPING TECHNIQUE
                            -INSIDIOUS LUNAR TRANSFORMATION
                            -FORM-DESTROYING TOUCH
                            -CLAY-WETTING PRACTICE
                            -SCORPION AND TOAD MASTERY
                            -MOON AND EARTH BLESSING
                            -LIMB-SHIELDING GROWTH
                            -SILVER COIN BARGAIN

                            When will you rage?

                                            Originally posted by Sunder the Gold View Post
                                            Lunars should definitely be able to trick and fool and feint, but their sleight of hand isn't going to involve phantasmal illusions of light or sound. (Hallucinations born of inflicted madness, however...)


                                            But we've been told that Lunars can shape how others perceive them, and of course they can shape their own bodies, so...

                                            I'd like Lunars to be able to completely soak an attack but make it seem like they were badly wounded. They might actually have to take a wound penalty to their next couple of actions to "sell" the damage to their opponent, but they can drop the act to launch a surprise offensive.

                                            In reverse, I'd like Lunars to be able to become badly wounded but make it seem like they soaked the damage completely. They still actually suffer wound penalties, and the greater the penalty, the easier it is for the opponent to see through the deception.

                                            And you can upgrade it still further.
                                            Perfect soak AND make it look like you're badly hurt sound a bit to much for one charm. I'ld say split it into two charms. But yeah saound good.

                                            I would also suggest if you like those kind of tricks : charms that trick the opponents into thinking you're crashed when you're not (and/or the opposite)

                                            Originally posted by Sunder the Gold View Post
                                            The 2.5 errata introduced an Abyssal soak Charm that, as a knock-on effect, would inflict an attacker (and witnesses?) with horror if the Abyssal completely soaked the attack with the Charm. If that can be an incidental effect of a Resistance-based soak Charm, then a Lunar could use a whole Charm to upgrade his "pretend I wasn't hurt" social-Attribute Charm to have similar effects. Terrify your enemies or inspire your allies with your appearance of invincibility.

                                            On the flipside, use your facade of injury to inspire your enemy to overconfidence.

                                            Naturally, these Charms have cousins and allies in my earlier suggestions for Charm that you use to fool your enemy about whether you're using a hit-and-run strategy out of fear or whimsy.



                                            I also want a Charm for convincing my opponents that I'm stalking and striking at them from the shadows because I'm too weak to face them directly... so that they'll underestimate my actual strength and lower their guard.

                                            Conversely, I also want to convince my hunters that I'm hitting and running because I'm so much stronger than them that I feel like having fun... even though it's really because I'd be totally screwed in a direct confrontation, and I need to discourage them from taking the initiative.
                                            I'm always a bit cautious about tricks about "strategies", and fight styles. They seem too convoluted and possibly railroad the enemy into stupid choices (ok) but for dubious reasons, or limited "counterplay" (not ok) and if you "nerf" them they become too situational and weak. Just like I said for the arms of the unconquered sun style.
