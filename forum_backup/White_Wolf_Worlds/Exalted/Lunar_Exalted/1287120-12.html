    Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post

    One, reminiscent does not mean identically the same.

    Two, that fight has Alucard straight up shoot a big mutant dog out of his chest while dissolving into a pile of snakes and bugs. The limb doesn't float around in the air, it springs out of the dog's mouth.

    Lunars now, or will in the future, have Charms to turn into swarms of stuff, and we've now been shown a Dexterity Charm in which you can respond to attacks by having stuff like animal heads or insect swarms spring out of you.
    Fair, but his head and limbs did fall off with fine wounds like a cut to each where the joint meets the trunk, which is probably not very Lunar.

    Like... just because they're called familiars in the source material doesn't mean they have to be modelled with the game's Familiar rules.

    I don't think Lunars really need ghost powers considering that they can internalize a person's shape by eating them. And their memories and personality traits, with the right Intelligence Charm.

    There are already a few Charms based on the image of evoking your entire Heart's Blood library at once in some kind of terrifying display. Those aren't based on having a command of the dead.

    I've not even started reading the proper Dexterity and Intelligence Charms yet.

    For that matter... can you not have as many Familiars as you care to pay for (which I presume is largely predicated on enhancements through Charms, since Familiar is a Story Merit)?

    Actually, looking back... yeah, explicitly a Merit you can have as many times as you want.
    One giant sticking point is that all of Alucard's #Lifehack is transient. He takes in lives, uses them up as shields or temporary minions, and if they get killed, they're gone. There is no library of forms tied to an inner self, and no duplication or customization of appearances, with the possible exception of Girlycard.

    So, yeah, I think that familiars in both settings is about the right place to go. XP debt may be involved, and they might not actually be ghosts, but they are lives ripped away, and each has only itself to give before dying.
