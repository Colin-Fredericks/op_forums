        I just switched to Windows 8 and I'm still trying to figure out how to juggle multiple PDFs, so I might forget something. Hopefully not.

        ========
        Full Moon:

        Name: Southern Stampede
        Caste: Full Moon
        Spirit Shape: Bull

        Attributes
        Strength 5, Dexterity 5, Stamina 4
        Charisma 2, Manipulation 2, Appearance 3
        Perception 4, Intelligence 3, Wits 3

        Combat is the only thing the Full Moon Caste is good for, so physical attributes are primary. Social charms have nothing important to contribute to combat, so they're tertiary. Mental attributes are more of a mixed big. Wits provides some stealth, but there's a lot of overlap with dexterity. Intelligence has the UMI defenses, so Intelligence 3 is mandatory. Perception charms start at Perception 3 and a lot of them require Perception 4, so that gets the loose Attribute dot Caste Lunars get.

        Used 9 bonus points to bring Strength from 3 to 5 and Dexterity from 4 to 5.

        Abilities
        Martial Arts 5 (Claws of the Silver Moon +3),
        Integrity 3,
        Presence 2,
        Resistance 2,
        Survival 2,
        Lore 2,
        Athletics 2,
        Awareness 3,
        Dodge 3 (While Heavily Armoured +3),
        Linguistics 1,
        Socialize 2.

        Ability-wise, I like well-rounded characters. Full Moons don't really need that many abilities, so they have a lot of dots to spare. I picked Martial Arts as the favoured ability so Martial Arts charms would be favoured and because Full Moons really benefit from unarmed combat. I took a Martial Arts speciality in Claws of the Silver Moon and a Dodge specialty for While Heavily Armoured because I couldn't think of anything else, mostly.

        Then I realized I had a spare bonus point and switched them around. +3 to Dodge While Heavily Armoured and +1 Martial Arts while using Claws of the Silver Moon. Used the Bonus Point to bring Claws of the Silver Moon up to +3.

        I used an additional 2 Bonus Points to get Martial Arts 5.

        Virtues
        Compassion 2, Conviction 2, Temperance 2, Valor 3

        Nothing special here. Lunars only need one high virtue for their Essence pool, so Valor 3 and the others at 2 is fine.

        Backgrounds

        Backing 1 (Swords of Luna)
        Mentor 1
        Reputation 1
        Tattoo Artifact 2 (Moonsilver Hearthstone Bracers)
        Artifact 3 (Moonsilver Superheavy Plate)

        Backing, Mentor and Reputation are standard for Lunars. This build doesn't require disguises, so the Tattoo Artifact isn't really a problem, plus it looks cool. It also means that I don't need to bother trying to hide the tell and moonsilver tattoos at all, so that's one more charm and one more knack to spare. I had two spare background dots, so I went for armour. Fuck Heart's Blood, it's just a waste of points and the only useful shapeshifting is DBT anyway.

        I used my last Bonus Point to upgrade my armour to Superheavy Plate.

        Knacks
        Deadly Beastman Transformation (Gazelle's Pace, Armored Hide)

        Terrifying Beastman Alteration and Devastating Ogre Enhancement are probably the only other knacks I would ever get for this character. I could probably pick better mutations, but I don't really have time right now and I'm juggling three different PDFs as it is.

        Charms
        Excellencies: Dexterity (3rd), Perception (2nd)

        Strength: Claws of the Silver Moon, Impressions of Strength

        Stamina: Ox-Body Technique (x2)

        Dexterity: Golden Tiger Stance

        I went for the 3rd Dex Excellency because my gut impression is that Lunars can add plenty of dice to their attacks without Excellencies and it's more mote-sustainable with Instinctive (Attribute) Unity. I've never actually run the numbers on that though, so I might be wrong. I picked the 2nd Perception Excellency just to get it out of the way and two Ox-Bodies because you can never go wrong with those. Claws of the Silver Moon buffs damage, Impressions of Strength adds some versatility and Golden Tiger Stance is a basic defense to supplement the massive soak.

        Essence 2 (Personal Motes 22, Peripheral Motes 40)

        Willpower 10

        Base 5, plus 5 bonus points.

        Very quickly eyeballing the numbers: 19 dice to attack, 8 dice of damage, 24L/24B Soak and DDV 6.

        Incidentally, while Full Moons are the easiest Lunars to make, I also find them the most boring. It's like all the decisions are pre-made.




        ========
        Changing Moon:


        Name: Shimmering Feather
        Caste: Changing Moon
        Spirit Shape: Peacock

        The Spirit Shape is completely irrelevant when making a Changing Moon, so I just went with something pretty.

        Attributes
        Strength 2, Dexterity 2, Stamina 3
        Charisma 5, Manipulation 5, Appearance 5
        Perception 4, Intelligence 4, Wits 2

        I wrote off combat entirely and decided to bet on Stamina for basic survivability. Social Attributes are primary for obvious reasons. Mental Attributes are more complicated. They all have charms that are useful to social combat, but none of them have a lot of charms that are useful to social combat. Wits seemed the least useful, so I dropped it a bit and brought Perception up to 4, because once again, there's no point in having Perception any lower. Added the extra dot to Intelligence and used 12 Bonus Points to max out the Social Attributes.

        Abilities
        Thrown 1,
        Integrity 3,
        Performance 3 (Swaying the Crowd +2),
        Presence 3 (Negotiations +2),
        Resistance 2,
        Survival 2,
        Lore 2,
        Investigation 2,
        Occult 1,
        Bureaucracy 2,
        Linguistics 2,
        Socialize 2 (High Society +2).

        Survival is only there because I'm forced to get it and Performance is only in there because it's exactly like Presence, only it deals with groups. The choice of a favoured ability is completely meaningless. I just picked Performance on a whim. I used 1 Bonus Point to get a specialty for Performance.

        Virtues
        Compassion 2, Conviction 2, Temperance 3, Valor 2

        Same deal as above with the virtues. I just prioritized Temperance over Valor. Self-control seemed important for someone who probably hangs out at parties a lot.

        Willpower 10

        Base 5, plus 5 from Bonus Points.

        Backgrounds
        Backing 1 (Winding Path)
        Mentor 1
        Reputation 1
        Resources 3
        Influence 1

        Standard Lunar Background Package, plus something that looks appropriate to a rising local socialite. I'd add Contacts, but I had no more dots to spread around.

        Knacks
        Subtle Silver Declaration

        Gotta do everything possible to hide the Tell.

        Charms
        Excellencies: Charisma (2nd)

        Stamina: Ox-Body Technique (x2)
        Charisma: Terrifying Lust Infliction
        Appearance: Hide of the Cunning Hunter
        Intelligence: Righteous Lion Defense, True-to-the-Pack Dedication

        UMI defenses, plus a charm to hide the moonsilver tattoos. I wanted some Manipulation, but then I remembered I needed basic survivability and got Ox-Body Technique twice. Terrifying Lust Infliction seemed like the most useful charm for a socialite. Rouse up the party and all that.

        Essence (Personal Motes 22, Peripheral Motes 40)

        Willpower 10

        Base 5, plus 5 from Bonus Points.


        ========
        No Moon:


        Name: Thousand Maws
        Caste: No Moon
        Spirit Shape: Spider

        Attributes
        Strength 2, Dexterity 5, Stamina 3
        Charisma 2, Manipulation 3, Appearance 2
        Perception 4, Intelligence 4, Wits 4

        Mental primary, Physical secondary and Social tertiary, which is fine because we're making a GIANT FUCKING SPIDER and nobody wants to talk to a GIANT FUCKING SPIDER anyway. Betting on Dex for combat. Spent the extra dot on Wits and 3 bonus points to bring Dex from 4 to 5.

        Abilities
        Archery 3 (Greater Natural Missile +3),
        Integrity 2,
        Survival 2,
        Investigation 2,
        Lore 2,
        Occult 3,
        Athletics 2,
        Awareness 2,
        Dodge 3,
        Stealth 3 (Ambushes +1),
        Linguistics 1.

        Stealth build designed for long-range combat.

        Virtues
        Compassion 2, Conviction 3, Temperance 2, Valor 2

        Same deal again with the virtues.

        Backgrounds
        Backing 1 (Crossroads Society)
        Mentor 1
        Reputation 1
        Tattoo Artifact 2 (Hearthstone Bracers)
        Manse 2 (Gemstone of Surface Thoughts)

        I ran out of ideas after the Basic Lunar Background Package, so I just went with a safe bet. Disguise is not a priority, so tattoo artifacts aren't a problem.

        Knacks
        Deadly Beastman Transformation (Greater Natural Missile, Spinnerets), Perfected Hybrid Interaction

        The Spinnerets could be replaced with Toxins.

        Charms
        Excellencies: Dexterity (3rd), Wits (1st)
        Strength: Claws of the Silver Moon
        Intelligence: Terrestrial Circle Sorcery
        Wits: The Spider's Trap Door

        Spells: Death of Obsidian Butterflies

        Claws of the Silver Moon can buff the Greater Natural Missile to buff its modest basic damage and The Spider's Trap Door handles stealth. It's a good combination for someone who fights through ambushes. I picked Death of Obsidian Butterflies to add some artillery power.

        Essence 3 (Personal Motes 23, Peripheral Motes 44)

        Ten Bonus Points to get up to Essence 3 so we can have Sorcery.

        Willpower 10

        Base 5, plus 5 from Bonus Points.


        ========
        The Full Moon is a basic CQC monster. His job is to stand in the middle of the fight and take all the hits that the others can't take.

        The Changing Moon is there for social combat and can't do anything else. He's the most likely to further develop his shapeshifting in the future. I can't say he really complements the other Lunars - he has absolutely no synergy with them - but he's the only one who can deal with social situations that might come up.

        The No Moon is there to back up the Full Moon with ranged attacks. Perception and Wits have good synergy for a character that relies on ambushes, but they have very little synergy with Intelligence, so I added sorcery to make Intelligence more useful and increase the options for ranged attacks.

            Tiger Mother of An-Teng - Warrior

            Motivation: Drive the Realm from An-Teng. She has Intimacies toward tigers (Adoration), the Pale Mistress (Appreciation), rebels against the Realm (Respect), the Scarlet Dynasty (Hatred) and those who willingly serve the Realm (Disdain).
            Caste: Full Moon
            Anima Banner: Rapidly flashing images of people and prey being torn to bloody giblets from the perspective of a tiger.
            Spirit Shape: Notably large tiger with well-kept coat
            Tell: Glow-reflective eyes
            Attributes: Strength 4, Dexterity 4, Stamina 4; Charisma 2, Manipulation 2, Appearance 3; Perception 3, Intelligence 3, Wits 4
            Virtues: Compassion 3, Conviction 2, Temperance 2, Valor 2
            Virtue Flaw: Curse of the Mother Hen
            Abilities: Athletics 3, Awareness 3, Craft (Wood) 2, Dodge 2 (Against Immaculate Martial Artists +1), Lore 1, Martial Arts 5 (Tiger Style +3), Presence 2 (Scary +1), Resistance 3, Socialize 2 (Read Intent +2), Stealth 3 (Ambush +1), Survival 5 (Raising Young Animals +3), War 1
            Backgrounds: Allies 3, Heart’s Blood 2, Influence 1, Resources 1
            Knacks: Deadly Beastman Transformation (Acute Smell, Acute Hearing, Fur, Talons, Wolf’s Pace)
            Charms:
            Excellencies: Dexterity (1st)
            Dexterity: Ferocious Guardian Beast Stance, Golden Tiger Stance
            Stamina: Hide-Toughening Essence
            Tiger Style: To Form, plus Celestial Tiger Hide
            Essence: 2
            Willpower: 6

            Rationale: Learning and relying on Tiger Style gives her room to grow into other, Lunar-specific routes of aggression. As a dedicated warrior, she cultivates methods other than the admittedly useful Claws of the Silver Moon, changing into beastman or spirit shape and calling upon her Style’s Charms to shear through the enemy and cushion most of the damage. As a figure of guardianship, she can also easily swat aside challenges to her allies or charges, particularly if those with more blatant shortcomings.

The Maneater has Intimacies to Coral’s Society (Hate), the other young women in the government counting house where she works (several Protective Friendships by individual), and the concept of the Tya (Resentment), the Realm (Sneering Joyous Criticism).

            Anima Banner: A serene splash of color that ripples like water, with an ominous shadow swimming underneath.
            Spirit Shape: An extremely well-fed pointer shark
            Tell: Rough skin
            Attributes: Strength 2, Dexterity 3, Stamina 3; Charisma 3, Manipulation 5, Appearance 3; Perception 3, Intelligence 4, Wits 3
            Virtues: Compassion 2, Conviction 4, Temperance 4, Valor 2
            Virtue Flaw: Curse of the Heartless Weasel
            Abilities: Archery 1, Athletics 2, Awareness 2, Bureaucracy 5 (Accountancy +1), Lore 2, Martial Arts 3 (Chomp Chomp +1), Melee 1, Performance 2 (Political Grandstanding +1), Presence 2, Socialize 2 (No I Am Not A Lunar +1), Stealth 2, Survival 2, War 1
            Backgrounds: Allies 2, Contacts 3, Heart’s Blood 3, Mentor 2, Resources 1
            Knacks: Prey’s Skin Disguise, Towering Beast Form
            Charms:
            Excellencies: Manipulation (1st), Wits (1st)
            Strength: Claws of the Silver Moon
            Manipulation: Cat-Face Presentation, Mask of White Jade
            Appearance: Hide of the Cunning Hunter
            Essence: 2
            Willpower: 10

            Rationale: This character is meant to pull Havesh-style shenanigans, but she also has ambitions that go far beyond just being some spook that assassinates people. Her aim is to use the shapes that she collects as a stepping stone to becoming like His Divine Lunar Presence from Heaven’s Reach. Claws of the Silver Moon is an excellenct one-stop shop to be threatening in combat, which suits her assassination methods well enough and can provide some defensive measures if she is without a weapon as well. Her social Charms help to keep her true nature concealed from prying eyes and at the same time allow her to maneuver in political arenas while using the faces of her victims. Finally, she’ll need to invest in Wits in the future to conceal her magical nature more effectively, so she has an Excellency there.

                                    Oh, already over? Thank you very much for letting us know; I'd just been planning things out. While looking things over made me more enthusiastic about the Full Moon and Changing Moon than expected, the No Moon teacher/martial artist left me just as disappointed as last time. Was not looking forward to that. For some of my grievances/complaints as I went through, see the spoiler below.

                                    Wolf Pack Training Technique is strictly martial-related teaching, which -- outside Martial Arts -- isn't at all what the concept is after. Still, it's the only canon people-teaching Charm so hey. For its prerequisites, there's Dog Tongue Method (I'm perfectly fine with this), Nature-Reinforcing Allocation (doesn't fit the concept I wanted at all), Sharing the Gifts of Luna (a cool thing I would totally snag down the road, but wanted more personal prowess before picking up), and a Charisma Excellency (handy, but really doesn't feel necessary). That's a lot of Charms to get at the only teaching Charm. After a survivability purchase or two, there's no real room for MA or much else to start.

                                    Sorcery doesn't suit the concept, which is good and bad. If it did, there'd be no real Charm decisions at all. On the other hand, the No Moon anima is mostly dead weight (a -1 external penalty to attackers is something, at least).

                                    Was also not looking forward to deciding on a Favored Attribute that might not be Dexterity (already kinda invested in Charisma...). Ditto deciding on a Favored Ability that might not be Martial Arts (Lore/Occult on a teacher just makes sense). Glah...

                                                    • Howl Triumphant (physical, wits): Ram-totem Full Moon. Born in Great Forks to a minor noble house, she exalted while on a hunting trip with his Immaculate private high school. Her former peers turned on her, calling her an Anathema. Four years later she now eschews the pampered trappings of his youth, instead she studies to be a warlord under her mentor the Clawed Czar: a powerful Lunar based in the Northeast. Her previously calm demeanor has turned brash and a bit feral, although she still remembers a surprising amount from her noble upbringing. Howl has focused her training on war and leadership: war to bring the surrounding tribes under her Czar's rule and leadership to turn these swords outwards to strike at the rest of the world. She uses her charms to intimidate, to subjugate, and to kill. She is the only one of the group to have mastered the Deadly Beastman Transformation, and is debatably the most deadly of the Circle.
                                                    • Kit Dayan (social, dexterity): Fox-totem Changing Moon. Kit is a charming young Haslanti, newly arrived in the Northeast. His fortunes were changed drastically by his exaltation – the formerly worthless son of a poor airshipman was now one of the most powerful people in the North. After a short time 'consulting' with the Haslanti government, made more difficult by his casteless nature, he found an elder to tattoo him. This elder gave him a mission: to infiltrate the war council of the Bull of the North. Unfortunately Kit overestimated his abilities, tried to seduce the Bull, and had to flee the North. Now he is a wanderer, having recently arrived in the Northeast he has cast his fate with his circle in order to accomplish daring deeds. Kit is devilishly clever and somewhat selfish. However, he's everything but stupid and does value and rely on the worth of his companions. He uses his charms to deceive, to hunt, and to seduce. Kit has drank the most heart's blood and has the largest library of animal shapes.
                                                    • Whispering Mask (mental, manipulation): Owl-totem No Moon. Mask has known no life but that of the savage. Born into the rule of the Clawed Czar, she knows the code of honor that binds the Northern barbarians better than either of her comrades. Trained as a shaman, she exalted while imprisoned after a tribal raid. In her fury escaping, she ripped apart the rival tribe into pieces with her claws, with her tongue, and with her mind. Her display of might, both physical and sorcerous, has caused them to deify her and they now serve as her cult. Returning to her home, her argent anima banner whipping around her, she has assumed the mantle of shaman and is now working towards unlocking the power of First Circle Sorcery. Mask is strong-willed and forceful. Her only goal is to grow in power and to bring the rest of the world under the heel of the Clawed Czar. She uses her charms to perceive, to protect, and to ensorcel. Mask already has knowledge of sorcerous workings and is close to learning First Circle Sorcery.
