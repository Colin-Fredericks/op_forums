    Okay, some shape shifting inspired mental powers, many tapping into the Moon's themes of madness!

    1. Social defense charm-Rewrite your brain to induce synaesthesia, rendering your enemies (or allies!) attempts to convince you of thing merely an unpleasant odor or flicker of swirling color or the brush of silk on your skin. Fun for role playing!
    2. Mad Science! Alter your brain chemistry to eliminate the need for sleep as long as you're working on your current project! Gain a derangement/whatever 3E uses for insanity of obsession towards said project!
    3. Lunacy! Convert one of your intimacies into a harder-to-oppose madness! Your love for your daughter becomes creepy and overbearing and paranoid,but nobody can talk you into eating her!
    4. Super smarts! Turns out that if you shut off the parts of your brain that are working on unnecessary stuff (read: your intimacies, mostly, and digestion, and breathing, and your heartbeat, etc), you can devote the extra processing power to genius works of induction! A Lunar can roll her eyes up in her head,hold her breath, suffer a surely-minor epileptic fit, and then predict the immediate future like a sci-fi military supercomputer doing combat simulations. Mentat, go!
    5. Speaking of Wits, turns out converting your nerves to living silver speeds up your thought processes and reaction time enough for you to "stop time" briefly." Doesn't let you move around or anything, but it can give you a moment to get your bearings or take mental actions. Think the Robert Downey Jr Sherlock Holmes fight scenes or clue connecting bits. Higher levels might let you do sorcery stuff with less ritual casting time or do fancy "actually moving around while everyone is frozen as a flash of moonlight" shenanigans.
    6. People would be a lot smarter without all those distractions! Blind and deafen yourself to go into a meditative fugue state to perform projects entirely in your mind! This is Int, not Wits, by the way. Make blueprints for your manse, do months of research on your new spell in mere hours, internalize the contents of the library you just read so you can write it out later (burn the original so those meddling snake bloods can't get at it), write your great novel in thirty minutes in your head, etc.
    7 Mental compartmentalization! Divide your super-brain into two personalities! One of 'em handles everyday stuff while the other designs artifacts and does research projects in the background...and takes over the controls if you get mind-controlled to fuck up whoever did it! Alternately, split your brain up and let your patsy-personality get seduced/mind-controlled/tortured and then take back over once the time is right! Bonus points if you have to choose how you divide your intimacies between your split personalities when you're not merged.
    8. I'm running low on time, so, um...go look at Mega Int and Mega Wits from Aberrant? Lotsa good ideas there.

    So I'm making God-Kicking Boot, an Exalted webcomic, now. Updates on Sundays. Full-color, mediocre but slowly improving art. It's a thing.

                Stereotypically intelligence based superpowers are a good start for this. I feel there's a bit of thematic risk here, but things like Psi powers and cobbling together one-of-a kind devices out of scraps are the sorts of things generally attributed to those with superhuman minds. Mind reading (or accurately modeling someone elses's mental OS in you own more advanced machine to predict their responses in something like mindreadin), prophecy, mind control, mental blasts, etc. The superhuman mind has some tropes to it that might not work for Lunars...but these sorts of Supers genre characters can be a good jumping off point. Maybe telepathy is out of theme...but sending messages across the world by visiting your friends's dreams seems Lunar enough. A good desk inning can make most powers fit.

                            Perception is a matter of increasing either the amount off neurons associated with, say, the olfactory bulb, or introducing aspects like the tapetum lucidum for night vision (or octagons to the rod-cone range in Pratchett books to see magic). Intelligence is a matter of temporary dendrite growth, increased NT production, faster transition from short-term to long-term memory, transdimensional brain folding (the Beyond in the brain, so to speak), and higher conductivity of the neural coating. Wits might revolve around NT (dopamine and ACh especially), fast-twitch muscle development, increased short-term memory spacing, and better pattern-recognition.

                            I honestly see Lunars deliberately invoking 'fight-flight-stall' via adrenaline, with further enhancement costing 1hl to replicate the surge naturally being caused by actually being injured, a viable option for a battle-length surprise anticipater.
