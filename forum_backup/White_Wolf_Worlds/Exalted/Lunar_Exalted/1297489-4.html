    Hm yeah this is looking very good. Seeing how Lunars are turning out to be more of equals to Solars but through different ways. Really excited to see what they would do with Sidereals since I would think it would be appropriate for them to be so likewise as Celestials.

    DBs falling behind is expected, since that is always there thing, but thats fine. Solars and Lunars can become monsters in a fight, but not all can. They have Castes for a reason. While they they can expand their capabilities through Favoring it does encourage you to focus on what the Castes are good at. While DBs encourage you invest on multiple areas no matter what Aspect you are, making you more well rounded, and while making it your already weaker abilities less pronounced, it does create synergy in groups where everyone can be expected to pull their own weight. Which is where DBs advantage lies. Over all very excited about all this.

                                I could try running a few more white rooms later where I use 2 younger DBs against one Lunar, but I'll have to tone back the DBs attributes at least, because I think it's unreasonable for a young DB to have 5s across the board. It's still going to be a major problem for the Lunar though, just for mote reasons, I'm almost certain of it. The Solar fights that I did there was some pretty knife edge hits and misses there. There were times when the Solar hit the Lunar with a good withering attack, but then couldn't land the decisive to finish it off, but he only missed by 1-2 successes. The theme of the DB fights were pretty heavily biased towards Lunars hitting and DBs missing, although the DBs artifact armor helped a LOT I have to say. I thought for sure that bear would have gotten that claw grapple combo off first turn but she just couldn't deal enough damage.

                                The only reason that could happen though is because the Lunar used a full excellency on every attack, along with charms like Finding the Needle's Eye, which ignored the defense bonus for the DBs medium weapon. Also she could pop off an Agile Beast Defense and full excellency and stuff any decisive attacks that came her way. That's just not possible against 2 opponents. If she had infinite motes, or maybe even just Essence 5 numbers of motes, then maybe.

                                                Yes, but that kind of exacerbates the issue that the DB is having with accuracy. So when a one of the characters misses it isn't just that they wasted an attack action, it's a big waste of motes. Like if a DB attacks with Oaken Thew Exertion and Hammering Wave Technique, and a full excellency, they've committed 15 motes to that attack, which are just lost if it goes south. Missing an attack is punishing in that way.

                                                You, on the other hand, are wasting less motes into attacks that don't hit, since when you declare that you're attacking with Divine Predator Strike, Armor Rending Claw Fist, and a full excellency you're committing 15m 2i, but can be much more sure that it will hit. It gets even more extreme when we start using reset charms too, since if you attack and miss with Flying Whirlwind Razor, not only did you waste the 5m, 5i, 1wp, but what was probably your only chance to use that charm in the whole fight.
