    Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz View Post
    This thread is quite long now, with a lot of stuff going on... Would anyone be willing to make a kind of "top 10" animal forms for Lunars? Including choices for different tactics (grappling, soak, poison, fear, ambush, Legendary Size, etc).

    I don't own all the HDNPs but I'll make an attempt, splitting legendary size and the rest because the mote/wp investment and practical problems of being huge means they have to be a lot better than non-Legendary size animals to be worth it. Also I'll need a break in between.

    In general by my judgement powerfull special ablities and merits >> big dicepools. This is assuming you get brawl 5 dex 5 and a specialty. If you don't max your combat dicepools animals with big pools like Elephants get more interesting.

    Legendary Size:

    1. Tyrant Lizard, for many reasons. Best ledendary size grappler. IMO the strongest form there is.

    -Deadly Thrash is bananas. Deadly thrash lets you get around one of the core mechanics of the 3e combat system; making decisive attacks normally leaves you vulnerable to initiative shift and you have to make withering attacks to work up the initiative for another decisive. Then it also doubles your initiative for damage. Like either one of those would be great getting both is nuts.

    (Look at the tiger-style E3 charm Prey-Maiming Frenzy, at 8m 1wp. While they're a bit different with one enhancing a savaging and the other a slam, Deadly Thrash is pretty clearly superior, while being free as long as you're in tyrant lizard form.)

    As long as no one crashes you and you keep landing your grapples and spend a couple motes on the control roll, you could just keep Deadly Thrashing every round for ~20 decisive damage against anything that isn't legendary size (or one of the ~10 or so solar brawl grapplers in creation).

    -Apex Predator. Most animals like f.ex. Elephants have to spend WP for their best attacks. Tyrant Lizards OTOH actually gain WP when they use their big attacks

    -Terrifying Dragon Roar: I already made a post on how stupid this gets if you can stack it with predators menace. If you (sanely) rule that you can't stack them it still automatically defeats any number of mortals who automatically flee with no chance of resistance.

    -Furius Rampage: 3 auto successes on attacks against crashed enemies really is quite a lot. Very likely to be active after you use Dragon Roar and/or Predators Menace.

    2. Sky Tyrant. High mobility with ability to attack at short range. Bad soak/hardness for legendary size but you can get around that. Overall a weaker but more mobile (and stylish) alternative to T-Rex.

    -Spine-Shattering Emperors Beak: Can attack from short range and reset at 6, or make withering attacks that hit as hard as a hippos from the same range.

    -Clamp Down: Resetting at higher initiative is as previously noted quite good. But it's no Deadly Thrash.

    -Horizon Sweeping Wingspan: The basic damage of wings is only 14 but lunars have great withering boosters and when you're hitting multiple people with one attack its worth stacking them up. Since the option to flurry it with a decisive doesn't say anything about not taking flurry penalties its probably not worth the WP unless you take extra limb mutations and the charm that reduces flurry penalties.

    -Fearsome Display: If you buy predators menace its pretty much Terrifying Dragon Roar, and unlike TDR you don't need to spend 6xp for a latent limited to one animal.

    3. Siege Lizard. Tough, hits hard. Access to a really good solar charm at reduced cost.

    -Spiked Tail Slam: It's Heaven Thunder Hammer, a notorious solar charm at 7m, for 2i (ok also a defense penalty but you'll find some way around it). At E2+ with Octopus and Spider Barrage it really gets crazy when you HTH 3-5 times in one round for 8m, 1wp, 6i-10i. At E3+ with Running Through the Herd you could juggle some sucker over 4-5 range bands with repeated hits.

    -Death Stomp: If you only want to HTH the first of your Octopus and Spider Barrage attacks, you can do the rest of them with this against your now prone opponent for +5 damage on each attack. Lunars also have some decent charms to prone people with like Tiger Claw Swat.

    Edit: I missremembered the cost of smashing attacks, its 2i not 1i.

                        Ok continuing with non-legendary size because I don't think the other LS animals measure up to those first 3, except Kraken for underwater and naval combat but that's going to be pretty niche in most campaigns. Also an honorable mention to walruses for non legendary-size aquatic forms but again that's kinda niche.

                        4. Hippopotamus: Offensive powerhouse. Amazing persistent withering and decisive attack bonuses.

                        -Downsides: Not the best creature for Predators Menace since they don't get the protean bonus for Terrifying Bestial Visage. Might be nerfed when Lunars is actually released.

                        -Crushing Bite: Because 19L wasn't nearly enough, lets ignore 4 + margin of success soak on top of that. Unlike piercing attacks not limited to soak from armor so the bite is practically just 23L and double extra successes for damage against mid-high soak enemies.

                        -Bone-Crunching Bite: Anyone you bite is cursed with scene-long suck unless they can ignore crippling penalties. Wonky interaction with the fact that hippos can grapple since its based on margin of success. Having a crippling attack also gives you the protean effect of Limb-Maiming Flourish (E2 lunar decisive damage adder), which means you get the WP you spend on it back if incapacitate your foe.

                        5. Pestletail: The tankiest of tanks, high soak and reduce min damage by 1. If it wasn't for the defensive benefits of legendary size itself it would be tankier than any of the legendary size animals with its latents. One of the best forms for a combat sorceror.

                        -Downside are it's best abilities are Latent so you're going to have to spend some xp and you can't use Predators Menace since it has no intimidation pool.

                        -Hardened Shell Superiority: This right here is what it's all about. Like 3-4 permanent solar charms worth of effect for 6xp; does everything Searing Quicksilver Flight + Force-Stealing Feint does while working with either parry or evasion, plus it also works when an attack hits you but fails to deal damage, plus an extra 4 initiative if its a decisive attack.

                        -Bony Shell: If you're a sorceror and aquire the natural shield mutation, this + Shadow-Hands Invocation lets you flurry Full Defense + Shape Sorcery with no defense penalty and no initiative cost for 2m/round and give you Soak/Hardness: 21/14 in addition to the normal +2 defense. Combined with Ferocious Guardian Beast Stance* you make yourself and your ward a nightmare to attack.

                        -Smashing Tail Hammer: It's Heaven Thunder Hammer again, but this time it costs xp to learn and WP to use so its down from amazing to pretty good.

                        -Claw Stomp: Like Death Stomp this is amazing if you can prone someone and then throw an Octopus-and-Spider Barrage at them.

                        *This and War Lion Stance seem to me sometimes underrated charms, they're not bad if you're all combat badasses; they let you keep eachother safe when you go all-out offensive incurring defense penalties and resetting to base initiative. Also for Lunars it significantly improves your penalty negators.

                        6. Cats: Tiny ninja warriors. Can be ninja sorcerors if you want them to be since the attack bonus as-written works with spells that make attack rolls.

                        -Downsides: You have to rely on your own strength, stamina, and artifacts for damage and soak/hardness. The aesthetic might get old after a fight or two (IMO aesthetically it works better if you go for combat sorcery but obviously tastes differ).

                        -Underfoot Menace + Tiny Creature: This is why you're a cat. +2 evasion +3 attack against anything larger than you are. Persistently for the 4m commitment of shapeshanging.

                        -Tiny-Creature + Ambush Hunter: A lot of lunar stealth charms have protean bonuses for being an Ambush Hunter. A few other stealth and defensive Dex charms have protean effects for being TIny. Cat's are the only tiny ambush hunters.

                        7. Strix: The flying grappler. I did a big writeup on them earlier in this thread so I'll try to keep things short.

                        -Downsides: Non-functional indoors. Have to get your soak somewhere else because these guys only get 3.

                        -The ability to Kraken-Arm Lash from Medium height, flying up to Long and carrying your victim while at the same time making a savaging attack and then dropping them is a very cost-effective way to deal a lot of decisive damage.

                        -While doing the above you stay quite safe from most enemies on the ground since even if they've learned archery they still can only attack you every other round unless they have some charm that gets around the aim requirement.

                        -You get some abilities that make you even scarrier at night when you can grapple from stealth.

                                8. Hellboar. The berserker. Decent but not as good as Hippo or Pestletai as a primary combat form, but it's great to slip into with Quicksilver Second Face at the same time as you activate Relentless Lunar Fury when someone puts you down to your -2 or -4 health levels.

                                -Downsides: Mediocre withering damage and soak.

                                -Berserker: Lunars can have a lot of -2 or -4 health levels with their ox-body* and when you're at those health levels this is a ton of free dice.

                                *Side note ox-body for lunars is a better purchase than it is for solars since 1. lunars have an easier time getting Sta 5 and it gives them 4 health levels if they do, and 2. lunar charms are generally weaker than solar charms so the opportunity cost is lower.

                                -Death stomp: Again lunars have some decent ways to prone people and if you also have berserker active you can roll an Octopus-and-Spider Barrage with a lot of free attack and damage dice on a prone opponent with this.

                                -Bone Crunching BIte: Same as for HIppos except Hellboars can't grapple.

                                9. Chimeric Spidersquid: The poison assassin.

                                -Downsides: Need use tiny-octopus as base spirit shape and take the chimeric charm for Extra Limbs and Enhanced Toxin mutations and pick up Shifting Breath Inversion to move at full speed on land with no penalties. Like a cat it needs last-warriors unity for withering attacks (and even then some might rule those weapons should do less damage when you're tiny anyway) and soak, also aesthetically its an aquired taste.

                                -Poison: With Enhanced Toxin mutation and Mamba-and-Cobra the octopus poison is doing 4i Aggravated with a 6+essence+(10s on attack and damage roll) duration and -1 penalty for 3m 2i. The penalty is low but other than that it's a hell of a poison.

                                -Stealth: With Skulking Rat Spirit, tiny size, and the Camouflage merit spidersquid rolls stealth with double 9s, rerolling 1s and 2s, and -2 successes on enemy rolls to notice it. That's more than a little stealthy.

                                10. Death Moa: Situational death machine. Again like Hellboar not the greatest main combat form, but in the right situation it's amazing to slip into with Quicksilver Second Face. That situation is when you want to finish of a crashed enemy. Once you get to E3 and can take Constant Quicksilver Rearrangement situational forms like this will really shine.

                                -Downside: Mediocre withering damage and low soak.

                                -Blade-Beak Frenzy + Furious Rampage: Against enemies in initiative crash Death Moas get 2 auto successes on attacks, plus extra successes and double 10s to damage on decisive attacks. Like all decisive boosting merits/abilities this gets nutty with Octopus-and-Spider Barrage.

                                -Lunging Beak: Can make peck attacks and grapple from short range.

                                                            Originally posted by Reminiscent Oasis View Post
                                                            Anyone have commendations for the "familiar 3" rank animals you can get with Heart's Blood 2+, if the Lunar doesn't have Towering Beast Form?

                                                            It's definitely a tricky question! Given that great cats are canonically examples of a 2-dot familiar, it's tricky to find creatures that have better stats that aren't Legendary Size. Given that Lunars can take the form of mutated animals, you might end up having to draw from that well a fair bit - claim that you went into the Bordermarches and Sacred Hunted a tiger that was perpetually on fire or something. That said, there's a couple of creatures in the books that I'd peg as 3-dot Familiars without being Legendary Size.
                                                            • The pestletail doesn't have the dice pools for combat that a great cat does, but its damage is very nice, and its Smashing Tail Hammer latent ability is enough by itself to push it up to 3-dot Familiar for me.
                                                            • A hippopotamus, for similar reasons to the pestletail: high damage if it lands a hit, and an impressive natural ability in Bone-Crunching Bite.
                                                            • Northern ursidae (from Hundred Devils Night Parade) strike me as 3-dot familiars, since they're both very impressive bears and can swim underwater indefinitely.
                                                            Those are all I can find that I'd personally rate as 3-dot Familiars, though.
