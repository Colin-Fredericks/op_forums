                                            Originally posted by TSk View Post

                                            The odds I mentioned assumed the character was from one of the Great Houses of the Scarlet Dynasty. Someone whose siblings are all Exalted, parents are both Exalted, grandparents and great-grandparents Exalted. Lunars of less auspicious parentage, those not of the blood, would find the task more difficult, of course.
                                            It's still too high, though less so. Consider that there are, by the books, ~10,000 such Dragon-Blooded in the Realm; at a (pretty conservative) 3% reproduction rate, that's 300 kids per year, somewhere between 30 and 270 of whom would Exalt. Split the difference and call it 150.

                                            "Well," you may say, "that's not so bad, is it?"

                                            Okay, now how many unExalted members of the Great Houses are there? Are they, say, 1% of the population of the Blessed Isle? That would be a million people, having, conservatively, 30,000 children per year. 10% Exaltation rates would mean 3000 new DBs every year, i.e., a population roughly a third the size of the entire host. Even a 1% rate would imply a pretty terrifying mortality rate for the Dragon-Blooded (4.5% is pretty steep), and that 2/3 of the Dragon-Blooded are actually from families where they're the only Exalt, which is not the picture the books paint.

                                            Then consider that there are ~100 million other people on the Realm, who by that idiotic table still have non-negligible chances of their children Exalting; if even .01% of their 3 million kids per year Exalt, that's another 300 DBs annually - that is, a total of 750, or 7.5% of the total population. To maintain a stable population, that means another 7.5% of the total population has to die every year, which is a terrifying mortality rate.

                                            Then add Lost Eggs. Then you add the books' claims that most Dragon-Blooded live to retirement age, at around 250. It's an impossible hash.

                                                    In order for Dragon-Blooded populations to stay stable with any kind of reasonable Exaltation rate, reasonable enough that children who don't Exalt are considered a disappointment rather than the norm (so let's say 50% and let confirmation bias take care of societal expectations), the collective 10,000 Realm Dragonblooded need to have 80 children a year if we assume that there is no possibility whatsoever of non-Dragonblooded bearing Dragonblooded children. In other words, 3% reproduction rate is incredibly huge, it needs to be about 0.8% to maintain a steady population if we assume that Lost Eggs are not a thing. MoEP: Dragonblooded cites the insane rate of the average Dragonblooded female having a child every twelve years, which translates to about a 10% reproduction rate.

                                                    There is almost no way that Creation should not be overrun with Dragonblooded under the constraints that some significant percentage of Dynasts Exalt and that Dynasts have large families. I'm not sure there's a viable solution to this other than to not think about it too hard.

                                                            Again to go by numbers, the only one I can find is a 50% mortality rate before retirement age.
