            My take, which doesn't necessarily have to be your take, is as follows:

            There is nothing in Solar thematics that make Elsewhere a thing for them too.

            I am god-king, ruling benevolently by the Power of the Sun, Secure in my Ability to be the Very Best at what I apply my Skill to... Also Hammerspace.

            See, nothing about it makes sense. But, because Elsewhere is a thing in Creation, they get to interact with it in a way that kind of links back to their thematics (i.e. they can build a sanctum in it, because great at building e.t.c.)

            Now, I agree with you that nothing about Elsewhere links back directly to Lunar thematics. But if they use it like either as a place to store things (that they steal like Ravens, or collect like squirrels or eat and it disappears like some Great White behemoth) or as a den (which connects many lands like a Rabbit's many burrows, or is warded by their scent/essence like the lair of some Beast out of hoary Lore), then I am about as okay with Lunars getting it, as Solars getting it.

            TL: DR If Solars can justify having Hammerspace, Lunars sure as heck can as well.
