    Originally posted by Lioness View Post
    Ignoring Evocations for a moment, the Solar needs to invest in another ability to substitute it in when confronted by one of the style's shortcomings.
    However, they do pay 1 less xp per MA dot and 2xp less per charm. So it's more a question of how effectively the most appropriate secondary ability papers the style's holes vs. the Lunar charmset and we still don't have a complete picture there for every style.
    Well, DEX charms are out, so we can say certain things for sure. We can say that Lunars are more accurate than solar melee with every combat ability, for one, and that this includes Martial Arts. There is no mastery effect that provides equivalent power to this. This is a problem that actually came up a long time ago, with the sidereal QC excellency - people would use it to stat up a martial artist, they pick Snake Style, and then found that this utterly broke the game because SStH became an unbeatable defence. Lunar accuracy is absurdly high, and this is the case here too. There is no mastery effect in Snake Style that is as strong as making SStH unbeatable.

    On XP, I don't think that having to pay a few XP extra to be massively powerful is a concern for anyone in any real game. I've played a lot of Exalted, and as the game goes along you always end up with loads of spare solar XP. I'm playing an eclipse, for instance, who has mastered a martial art, bought a large number of evocations, maxed out all her abilities + bought a few unfavoured ones, learned numerous spirit charms, and I still have a pile of unspent solar XP. The gap between a solar and lunar mastering a full MA is (assuming 9 charms like most MAs are) 18xp. Not much at all. I would trade mastery for the lunar dex charms in a heartbeat and we haven't even seen how STR breaks things even further.

        I should probably also add something else that occurred to me.

        The more powerful offensive styles (Righteous Devil, Single Point) are generally the least combo-able. Sure, you can have two katanas and use Single Point/Steel Devil, but why would you bother when you can just take Dodge or Resistance... or even Melee defence charms? (Attack with Single Point, parry with Melee). And Righteous Devil can't be combo'd with any styles at all.

        Whereas I think the ones that are easier to combo are generally either not the most powerful offensive styles (all those unarmed styles; sure, you can build plenty of combos, but they don't include the most brutal charms generally). Or they do stuff that doesn't go together all that well; yes, Black Claw and Tiger can be combo'd, but they don't really help each other that much. And Ebon Shadow's defence is mostly about Evasion, which a Solar can use Dodge charms with anyway.

        So anyway, I think the ability to combo, say, Snake, with Lunar attack charms, isn't really that much better than combo-ing with other Martial Arts.

        (Armour-Penetrating Fang Strike, Striking Fang Technique, Eyes of the Wood Dragon, Falling Scythe Flash, Searing Edge Flash... your unarmed withering damage is 7+Strength+Strength+Perception+Dexterity, ignoring soak, and if you do even 1 damage they lose another Essence initiative. Pricy, but so brutal. I can't see that replacing one of those charms with a Lunar strength charm is going to make much difference.)

        On the other hand, combo-ing Single Point or Righteous Devil with Lunar charms could be absolutely brutal. Normally there's no way beyond Evocations (and some of the effects in Sirrush kind of overlap with Righteous Devil. Sekhem's good though. And I guess Stormcaller, but I haven't really looked at that with Single Point).

        Saying that... I wouldn't be shocked if most of the Lunar damage charms in Strength are applicable to Single Point, but not to Righteous Devil. After all, you don't actually add your Strength to the damage of firewands, so it'd make sense that you couldn't apply damage-boosting Strength charms.

        I guess there could be a tree off Bombardier Spittle Alchemy, but that'd be in Dex.
        So, if you're wondering about Lunar damage charms with Firewands, I suspect it's all already there.

        They might have damage charms for ranged weapons in Perception, but I suspect that it's just the ones from Dex that are Totemic for the eagle-eyed. Not sure though.

        Anyway, you can do some pretty good stuff with Dex-based ranged charms and Righteous Devil, but you've got to pay for it (as opposed to spending that XP on, say, Solar Dodge or Resistance), and you're not getting the strong Mastery effects of Cloud of Ebon Devils, Kiss of the Sun Concentration, and Caress of the 1000 Hells.

Even with just these, lunar MA is capable of numerous very, very powerful things that utterly eclipse mastery effects! How much are you willing to bet that STR charms will not have anything at all that's better than mastery? Here's a free prediction: lunars will have, in STR, charms relating to grappling. These charms will, unlike solar brawl, combo with Mantis and Tiger Style. The result will be that lunars are significantly stronger with both of these styles.
