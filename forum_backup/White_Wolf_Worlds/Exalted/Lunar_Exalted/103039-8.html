    Yes. The topic. There was a topic.

    Originally posted by The MG View Post
    "Chimerism is accepted, but seeking it is stigmatised" seems mighty counterintuitive.

    Better to tie the whole thing into the rage and storming power of the Lunar Exaltation. I'm picturing a thing where the Casteless are always playing with fire, awash with unbound and self-destructive power, while those with Castes have bound their rage to their wills, turning it only against their enemies1. The self-destructive bit may involve turning into Koizilla under pressure, but it doesn't have to.

    1: Mostly. The Great Curse is still a thing, after all.
    My reading of chimeraism being an accepted state was quite different. I took it to mean chimeraism might simply be an accepted state for all Lunars, one every Lunar Exalt has to control, rather than being a problem only for the casteless.

    Given how it looks like Lunars are expected to have some kind of rage mechanic, and how Morke says they'll be much more capable in a fight, it's hard to not see those three things being connected.

    I really hope it doesn't turn out that way, though. I like werewolves, but this is Exalted, set in a mythic age of heroes. Lunar Exalted are supposed to be so much more, frankly so much better than werewolves and it just seems so terribly simplistic. If they're going to have a mechanic like that, I'd rather see them changing in more directions and in greater ways than simply getting angry.

    Originally posted by Thesaurasaurus View Post
    I dunno, maybe it's like steroid abuse in career sports, where some degree is tacitly accepted or even encouraged but there are still lines you don't cross because goddamn, son?*

    *Note to devs: please don't do this, or at least don't make the analogy too blatant; I don't think the Lunars could survive being made into an After-School Special on top of all of their existing issues.


    Oh, right, this thread had, like, a topic, didn't it? Well, rhetorical grousing notwithstanding, I'd like for gradual abandonment of identity to be one possible expression of the Lunar Curse - not the only one by any means, but there's a lot of powerful storytelling potential in the risk of 'going native', especially in a setting like Creation where most factions have at least some sympathetic qualities, and there's already a surfeit of splats vying for the limited thematic space of a hero who's SO ANGRY, ALL THE TIME, YOU DON'T EVEN KNOW HOW HARD I CAN BROOD WHEN I REALLY TRY
    Aside from loss of identity, what other possible explanations would you suggest?

                                            I think there was a thread about Lunars in here somewhere.

                                            At any rate, my core problem with Lunars, and I think one of the core problems with Lunars in general, is this: shapeshifting is nice, and gives you all sorts of neat tricks, but all of those tricks are inherently physical rather than mental. There's almost no examples of shapeshifting to become smarter. Tony Chopper and Mangaverse Reed Richards are the only two I can think of. This means Lunars are kind of fucked when it comes to a large chunk of areas of character competence, most notably crafting and lore. Shapeshifting has no means of addressing these areas. No other Exalt type is hamstrung in this manner, because all of the others have themes that are able to cover these conceptual spaces without any stretching required. Their themes can be directly applied to all areas of competence, while Lunars are saddled with a theme that only covers being the Dumb Brute Exalted and the Sneaky Rat Exalted, forcing them to make up the missing areas of competence by being Silver Solars.

                                            This is why it is highly important that Lunars have a theme that can be described beyond shapeshifting and that can be applied across all of their Charms the way that the other Exalts are able to apply the theme of "superhuman excellence/demonic power/manipulating destiny/power over death and the dead/control of the elements" across all of their Charms. The only problem is I have no idea what that theme could possibly be.
