            Originally posted by Aquillion View Post
            Anyway, my point is: If you're going to allow Solars to learn SMA from a Sidereal tutor, it should at least be a sane, reasonable option for it. Spending XP through the nose for "versatility" doesn't make any sense at all (especially in a world with both sorcery and evocations, not to mention Sorcerous Workings and regular martial arts to diversify yourself with using the same XP.) Obviously SMA can't actually make a Solar stronger than a, well, Solar, that's a given, but if it's going to be an option then it can't be an actively bad place to spend your XP.

            Regarding your other quote, I don't see how you could interpret that to mean Solars learning SMA wasn't a big deal. It implies that Sidereals were willing to murder over it. (Granted, Sidereals have sometimes been portrayed as perhaps a bit too murder-happy.)
            Agree that definitely however SMA *is* telegraphed for Solars when it's properly and fully described needs to be followed up by mechanics.

            (As discussed I'm actually OK with "These secret super style are not built for Solar Exalted, and though you can learn them, it is perhaps not your best option". Transcending your natural *level* through MA through tutelage of the Sidereals as an important element for Dragonblooded, probably yes, for Solars, probably not so much.

            It's basically what seems to happen with Immaculate MA and I'm OK with that, and it avoids two-tier Solar MA where SMA tutored Solars are better than merely Celestial MA using Solars, or two-tier Solars generally where SMA tutored Solars are better than other combat focused Solars.)

            But whatever is shown up front should be followed through with; it's obviously not a good idea to sell players SMA as an upgrade for MA using Solars and actually find in play it's a downgrade or even they are just roughly equal to Celestial MA and more generally not particularly amazing and impressive for them.

            Re; this Players Guide quote, I have to admit that I read its Sidereal death squad comments very much in a spirit of "Solars are not actually meant to learn this and get a tutor in your game if you don't want" (though with some hilarity at the "When were Sidereals not meant to be trying to slay the Solars again?" aspect). To me it's strongly de-emphasizing that any "Sidereals serve as as sifus to Solars" is any degree a setting element that you can expect (that doubles down to me in turn on this not being very important to preserve). But you could read it as "Encouragement, but with consequences" alternatively.
