    Before that under the general rules for actions in animal form (which apply since attacks are actions the animal has a dice pool for) it says

    "When she takes an action the animal has a listed dice pool for, she may use its dice pool. Any dice over her own base (Attribute + Ability) pool count as Charm dice and are subject to her dice cap (p. XX), potentially preventing her from using a form’s full dice pool. If the animal’s pool is lower than hers, or it has no listed pool, she may use her own dice pool, although the Storyteller may assess penalties if her shape is ill-suited to that action."

    so you can clearly use your attribute + ability pool for them, almost certainly without penalties since saying an animals shape is ill-suitted for its own natural attacks is just weird.

        - You want to aim for some accurate animal like great cat for your day-to-day
        - Tyrant Lizard has pools that are bigger than any possible human combination if you need a panic button and is an all round stellar shape.

            Being a cat, especially when you buy the Underfoot Menace and Stalking Shadow Hunter latent abilities, is actually hilariously powerful.

            For 4m committed:
            • Your attack bonus, provided your dex+brawl is higher than a cat's, is 1+3 (always on Ambush Hunter)= 4, which is equal to a mortal light weapon.
            • Your damage bonus is +12 for your bite, which is equal to a medium artifact weapon.
            • Your evasion is increased by 2.
            • If you want to hide during combat, enemies subtract 2 successes from rolls to notice you
            • You Double 9s on hearing rolls to notice hidden enemies.

            However this isn't the end of the benefits you get from being a cat. Lunar charms often have Protean effects, and cats are both Tiny and have the Ambush hunter traits.

            Being Tiny allows the Lunar to activate a lot of defensive Dexterity charms after the opponent has rolled instead of before, which greatly increases mote efficiently. If you are hit by an attack, you activate charms until in no longer hits you.

            The Lunar Stealth charms in Dexterity, especially those based around Stealth in combat, have protean effects based on Tiny size or Ambush Hunter.

                                                Kylina Sele (Note: Kylina is built with 3 sessions under her belt of shadow walking, and so she spent the 12 Lunar XP on getting one of the two latents she needs and one extra Charm)
                                                Spirit Shape: Cat
                                                Tell: Silent Movement OOO

                                                Come Back Adam Warren! There's more Lunars Art to be done!




                                                Attributes

                                                Physical (Secondary) (3bp spent)

                                                Strength OOO
                                                Dex OOOOO (Favored)
                                                Sta OOO

                                                Social (Primary)

                                                Charisma OO
                                                Manipulation OOOOO (Favored)
                                                Appearance OOOOO (Favored)

                                                Mental (Tertiary)

                                                Perception OO
                                                Intelligence OOO
                                                Wits OOO (Favored)

                                                Abilities (8bp spent)

                                                Athletics OO
                                                Awareness OOO
                                                Brawl OOOOO (Specialty: Bite)
                                                Dodge OOOOO (Specialty: Small Forms)
                                                Integrity O
                                                Larceny OOO
                                                Linguistics O
                                                Occult OOO
                                                Presence OOO
                                                Socialize OOO (Specialty: Cat Got your Tongue?)
                                                Stealth OOO (Specialty: Small Forms)

                                                Merits (4 bp spent)

                                                Artifact (Belt of Shadow Walking) OOO
                                                Heart's Blood OO
                                                Stolen Faces OO
                                                Silent Movement OOO
                                                Backing: Silver Pact O
                                                Latent: Stalking Shadow Hunter
                                                Latent: Underfoot Menace

                                                Charms

                                                Excellencies: (Yes, she has Excellencies in all attributes. She is literally omni-capable. No, it wasn't easy, as you'll soon see)

                                                Strength
                                                Dexterity
                                                Stamina
                                                Charisma
                                                Manipulation
                                                Appearance
                                                Perception
                                                Intelligence
                                                Wits

                                                Universal:

                                                Quicksilver Second Face: Never be left without your battle form.

                                                Strength:

                                                Ferocious Biting Tooth: Eventually this will be our main source of Overwhelming, for now its merely decent, but it also gives us our Str Excellency, which we can stunt for +4 damage.

                                                Divine Predator Strike: This is basically also Overwhelming, and these two charms go well together to boost our damage a long with the Strength Excellency.

                                                Stamina:

                                                Impenetrable Beast Armor/Durant Quicksilver Adaptation: The wonder combination is the basis of Lunar Soak. In this case, we are hoping we don't get hit (9-10 Evasion), but if we do, this should take some of the edge off.

                                                Dexterity:

                                                Nest Raiding Slyness: What Master Thief would pass up this Heart's Blood/Stolen Faces source? Its basically what you were going to do anyway.

                                                Emerald Grasshopper Form: The Ultimate infiltration method. Enemies have a -3 success to see her at all when she uses this.

                                                Sinuous Striking Grace: One of the top tier Dex Offense Charms as go first and free dice is always nice.

                                                Agile Beast Defense: Our 1m workhorse will always give us +1 to Evasion and we can apply it after we see what our foe rolls (if it isn't 9, don't bother)

                                                Bending Before the Storm: Our Onslaught Negator.

                                                Charisma:

                                                Argent Songbird Voice: Our omni-applicable success-on-social actions generator.

                                                Debt of Borrowed Skin: Our altruistic method of stealing other people's faces. A thief with a heart of gold, indeed.

                                                Perception:

                                                Perceiving the Hidden World: Not only does this let us see immaterial, as a Cat we auto-detect out to Long Range!

                                                Spirit Rending Fury: For 1 mote per attack, this lets us attack with impunity with the Belt of Shadow Walking if the enemy can't strike Immaterial.

                                                Intelligence:

                                                Memory Drinking Meditation: If you ever replace someone and don't want their spouse to suspect, accept no substitutes. Also super good at collecting information on others (just steal the faces of those around them and reap the intel).

                                                Terrestrial Circle Sorcery: No need to belabour the usefulness here. I chose Mists of Eventide as a Control Spell as it will serve you well in stealthy infiltrations if you can put people to sleep and they can't see where it is coming from.

                                                Finale:

                                                So there you have it, Kylina is more or less done. She is great at basically everything (thanks Excellencies) and superlative in a few areas:

                                                - Stealth: With her 14 pool (5 Dex + 3 Stealth + 1 Specialty + 3 Belt of Shadow Walking + 2 Stunt) and her ability to eat 2 successes from enemies trying to detect her (4 if they are trying to do it by hearing!) without spending a single mote, Kylina is an infiltrator of the highest calibre (she eats 3 successes if she wants to go tiny)

                                                - Fighting - As stated before, she has the highest accuracy and highest Evasion of any animal form thanks to merits and latents. Damage is a bit lackluster, but hopefully the strength Excellency (+16 damage with that, which is considerable) and the Strength Charms cover for her a bit. Her Soak is always going to be bad, but I did not know of a way to fix that. *sigh* glass cannon it is then.

                                                - Femme-fataling. Look at that Manipulation and Appearance, look at it! I wasn't even able to get Charms in these two attributes, but they are favored anyway. Just go out there and make men and women melt.

                                                I'm sure there are lots of ways I could have built her better, why not take the time to tell me? Always welcome comments!

                                                Edit: For all that effort, she has basically the same Defense as Aguile one page prior. Full Moons, man, Full Moons.

                                                            Wood Dragon's Claw is a reasonable replacement for an artifact weapon, I think. I don't see why you couldn't use it as a cat. And has +5 accuracy, not +4.
                                                            Actually, the overwhelming might be almost a bigger boost than the damage - one of the scariest things for this build is someone with 15+soak, because gaining initiative will take you forever, and one hit loses it all.

                                                            Yah, armor does have sneakiness issues. Potentially you could get an evocation to hide/disguise it? Many Pockets Meditation + Quicksilver Armor Approach can don hidden armor in a turn, but several charms and motes is a bit expensive.

                                                            Alternatively, at Essence 2: Perfect Hybrid Interaction (Unusual Hide). Or find a mutated cat and eat it. Or find a cat, mutate it with Sharing Luna's Gifts, and steal it's food while taunting it.
                                                            Or, if you're going for sorcery: Invulnerable Skin of Bronze: +6 soak, and an extra +essence if you aren't wearing armor.

                                                            EV is mostly good enough, but someone if an enemy solar throws 28 dice at you, or an enemy sidereal throws 21 dice with TN 4 at you, you'll want soak. Even if they just send 16 dice at you, you'd rather not choose between a slight chance of taking 10+ dice of withering damage, or spending 6m straight to prevent the hit.
