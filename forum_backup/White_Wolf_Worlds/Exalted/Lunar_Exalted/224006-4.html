    Originally posted by Fata-Ku View Post

    I dont't know. It's true for 2e and 2.5, but I'm kinda hoping Solars and Lunars are more on even footing in terms of charms in 3e -- in the sense that Solars are good overall and Lunars have peaks and valleys (and broadness) that puts them roughly on the same level (and from Dev Comments about power gaps I sorta hope that's what's going to happen). Then Evocations and DBT would roughly balance out, so that it doesn't matter, balance-wise, that you don't have one while you're in the other. Likewise with sorcery -- Lunars would get (advanced?) shapeshifting that puts Green Dragon's Claw, Skin of Bronze and Imbue Amalgam to shame (e.g. Claws of the Silver Moon, Stamina Charms/Gifts, DBT respectively), so it doesn't matter that you don't get them, because you already have things that are equivalent or better. It might be a little boring (which is why you mod the mechanics a bit) but it's be a not-too-bad way to compromise between people-want-things and flavor.
    Yeah, dev statements seem to have been "Solars on top, DBs at the bottom, other Celestials in the middle, with some effects that may surprise you, i.e. a DB Charm that is comparable to Solar stuff within a specific theme or motif." I like that, because 2e's strut, stratified power tiers got so boring so quick.

    It's just hard to say whether Evocations and DBT balance out. What about Solar Evocations vs Lunar Evocations (for non-DBT time)? That's what we can't gauge. Though we assuredly do know that Sorcery is supposed to be a lot better, that Solars will definitely have SCS and Lunars won't, and it's hard to argue that Solar Charmset + SCS + best at Evocations > Lunar Charmset + DBT + other shapeshifting. At least generally speaking, since I expect Lunars to hit the Solar effect level on some stuff, as has been implied.

    It does matter though. Sorcery is universal; we haven't seen this to be different - if anything, it's becoming more true with the comments on mortal sorcerers. It's not helpful to "put it to shame" if a concept could be built around DBT and sorcery combinations, and I feel like the DBT/Evocation split is a lot more explainably thematic for Lunars than keeping the shitty "can't buff yourself with spells because of these tattoos lol" drek that we've gotten, that only serves to narrow what Lunars can do more and more.

    TL;DR the tattoos are shit.
