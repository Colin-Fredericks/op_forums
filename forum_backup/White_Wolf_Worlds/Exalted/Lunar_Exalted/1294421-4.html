            Tips for Wyld Hunting Lunars

            1. When the Astrologers tell you of the appearance of a new Anathema, make haste. The Ogres amongst the Anathema use their profane magics to learn of these new Moon Mad and send forth their minions to capture them, so it is imperative to get there quickly.

            2. Knowledge is key. All Silver Anathema have an identifying mark upon their bodies that cannot be altered, the Tell, so it is paramount to watch for this, but do not discount any other information that can be gleaned. The demon often retains the memories and some of the relationships held by their host body for a time after possession. These ties can be used to lure out the Anathema from their lair, to determine their likely habits and strategies, and what goals they might initially pursue. However, be forewarned. The warping that occurs when the demon takes a new host is most severe in the Silver Anathema, such that the final demon may not retain anything of the original host, not in body, mind, or soul.

            3. Nearly every Wyld Hunt that has pursued a Silver Anathema has lost it at one point or another. Do not think of this as failure, but as another opportunity to learn. Most young Moon Mad instinctively know to steal the form of a bird or fish as soon as they can, but most only gain one form. When they change, watch their new form as much as you can, both to learn its markings and to see their Tell. This will help you to take them down a second time.

            4. Practice hunting. Learn to track, hunt, and kill every animal under the sun, so that you cannot be surprised later. Learn the habits of animals, such that you may spot them acting strangely or flush them out depriving the Anathema of their concealment.
