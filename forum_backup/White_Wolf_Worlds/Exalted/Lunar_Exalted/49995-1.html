                            I'm strongly of the opinion that Hybrid Body Rearrangement, by itself, should not allow size mutations. You'd need the appropriate knacks such as Humble Mouse Shape to expand your size limits.


                            Some of my Hybrid Body Rearrangement tricks:

                            -First off, Lunar Blood Shaping Technique. Ask your ST if you can use LBST+HBR to mutate your friends. (Potentially get an upgrade to combine them.) If you can, awesome.

                            -Anything with Multiple Limbs (usually Arms). Asura's Wrath combat style FTW. Reduces multiple action penalties and lets you wield a two-handed weapon together with a shield, while leaving a hand free to make obscene gestures.

                            -Emerald Grasshopper Form to turn into something small, then manifest Scorpion's Tail (twice to give it Piercing if you want), and Toxin however many times you feel like stacking that. Instant assassin skills, no training required! (It's never spelled out anywhere specific that EGF tiny forms are particularly stealthy, but the guideline I'd use is that enemies have -3 or -4 external penalty to attack or spot you, based on the Minuscule mutation in Compass:Wyld.) You can top it off with Chameleon if you want even more stealth.

                            -Inexhaustible. Not the most powerful, but definitely one of the most convenient mutations out there. Never suffer fatigue. Run all day. Stand watch all night, or at least those nights where you can spare the willpower since you won't get to roll Conviction. (For extra fun: Lunar Blood Shaping Technique to give your lover Inexhaustible as well. Impossible Joints makes this even more fun.)

                            -Mobility mutations: Gills and Wings are in the corebook, yet still largely overlooked in most of the games I've played. Being able to traverse sea and air opens up a lot of new travel options. On land, Cheetah's Pace increases regular travel speed. If you want to remain stationary up where you are, or lack Wings for whatever reason, Spider Legs (in Manual:Lunars) lets you climb a vertical surface and hang from the ceiling, also +4 jumps. Brachiation belongs on this list too; it's perhaps not all that good compared to the others, but funny.

                            -If you dare touch the PITA that is clinch rules, combine Serpent's Body (+4 on clinch rolls), Prehensile Body Hair (+3 str for clinch) and Acidic Pustules (all in Lunars) with Quills from the corebook. Note that Quills is stackable. You may want to get a few purchases of Tentacles and Extra Arms too if the ST will let you clinch more than one person that way. Hilarity ensues if this is done while a small form, as the +7 bonus to clinches probably outweighs whatever Strength loss you get from the small size. (Idea: describe this as being a thorn vine wrapping around the opponent.)

                            -Dragon's Breath. Not very effective, but awesome. Notable in that it can be aimed with Athletics.

                            -Night Vision, Keen Senses, etc. and the big one, Lidless Demon Eye. Very hard to get (you'd need Green Sun Child at a minimum and some demon-sorting... or maybe Taste of Luna's Champions and ask an elder) but very powerful. Automatically detect invisible creatures, see dematerialized creatures, sense Essence as though you had used All-Encompassing Sorcerer's Sight.

                            -Speaking of Taste of Luna's Champions, consider asking your ST to waive the per-shape experience cost in favor of paying xp for increased Heart's Blood background, and perhaps paying temporary Willpower for the individual shapes you're copying. As written, I find this Knack very unappealing due to the disproportionate XP cost compared to hunting the form, especially considering as you'd need the appropriate hunting knack for esoteric shapes anyway.

                            -Armored Hide, +4L natural soak, always nice if you get into a fight, only costs 3m and isn't committed. Also +4 dice on Survival rolls, which should probably be interpreted narrowly. Upgrade is Stone Body for +6L, but that penalizes Dexterity which is usually a bad idea.

                            -(Element) Adaptation. Definitely requires Luna's Hidden Face (LHF makes several of the above easier but probably isn't required for them), and functions sort of like the discount version of Directional Mastery Technique.

                            -Semi-Bullshit: Depending on your ST, learn Green Sun Child so you can hunt demons, then pick some of the demonic mutations from Manual:Infernals. (I wouldn't allow this because I think they should be Flaws, but it's worth mentioning.) Ordination of Lies boosts your social rolls, Blurred Fate helps hide from divination, Gatekeeper shows you portals to Cecelyne, etc.

                            -Total Bullshit: Scroll of Heroes mutations pretty much universally fall into this category. Ban them. Ban them with the great banhammer. In particular, ban Essence Abundance and its +16 motepool. Should definitely not be possible to manifest this for 4m with Hybrid Body Rearrangement, even if the Lunar does hunt and eat a God-Blood. Should probably not be a mutation at all, but a god-blood-only merit. Only use these if you're playing in the sort of game where the Brutal Attack merit is allowed.

                                            This one's not so much the form itself, but tricks done with it.

                                            My Lunar is Essence 7 and yet still does not have a warform. He has no need for one.

                                            Instead, he hunts river dragons, taking their shapes and twisting them - giving them arms and speech, and basically molding them into warstriders, which shape he then lends to troops under his command via Lunar Blood Shaping Technique. These are awakened mortals with artifacts to hold warstrider weapons Elsewhere. Tens to hundreds of warstriders out of nowhere, without needing to maintain them, is useful for many things - such as ambushing Wyld Hunt legions by letting them try to invade his city. (They didn't even get to the walls.)

                                            When he travels, he has a stryx form: strong enough to carry significant payloads, airborne so he doesn't care if there's a mountain range or a sea in the way, and fast enough (with Charm help, motes committed for the duration) to cross a Direction in under a week.

                                            True, he lacks conventional combat prowess. His personal combats tend to turn out like his most recent combat: he stole incriminating evidence from some raksha and lead them on a short chase - kiting them into a waiting Dusk with 8 30+ die attacks per tick, a Solar with Sorcery ready, and a Lunar with...well, let's just say the combat was short, and that was before his Mate showed up. That, in turn, was before he left for about 2 actions, bringing an entire fae court down on what remained. (Or at least their queen, but she came alone since there wasn't need for more power by that point, and was ready to summon her guards if needed.) His spirit shape is a housecat for many reasons.

                                                    A couple years ago I was running my annual Halloween game, so I tried squicking the players out a bit. This game was a side story. They were playing Dragon Blooded members of a Wyld Hunt who had been long reoccuring foes/occasional friends with the player's characters in the main campaign.

                                                    I created a Lunar antagonist based around the Rhizocephala. A better description of one can be found here.

                                                    She functioned like a parasite taking over people's bodies and turning them into walking incubators for her creepy monster-babies.

                                                    I constructed a new Knack, which allowed her to merge her body into another and take control of the victim as a Disease effect. She had further improved the technique allowing her to merge with and control up to a dozen victims who would explode into monster babies when killed. Her goal was to eventually be able to infest everyone in Creation, though fortunately the players managed to stop her well before she had the chance.

                                                    It was just a oneshot, but she became a memorable character and even now every time Lunars get mentioned someone will say, "The Immaculate Order is right! KILL THEM ALL WITH FIRE!"
