    Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post
    In particular, are you still talking about Dexterity when you suggest removing the memory of your presence from somebody's mind?
    Point of fact, I don't know if he was still talking about Dexterity Charms, or just Lunar Charms in general.

    I do know that Second Edition Lunars had Forgetful Victim Prana for making people forget the next several moments (or few minutes) after they happen. Like a time-delayed Nueralizer that doesn't touch any memories from before the flash. Lunars in both editions also had Mind-Blanking Fear Technique for traumatizing people into forgetting memories completely unrelated to the current situation. Both of these are social Attribute Charms rather than Dexterity.

    I hope that 3rd Edition Lunars have a bit more flexibility and flavor in regards to memory-manipulation.


    Originally posted by Aquillion View Post
    More seriously: Lunars do have sneaky charms, but they tend to not be in Dexterity. And, honestly, I think that's a good thing -- you're not seriously suggesting you want Dexterity to do even more, are you? Given the way abilities are structured and the desire to give them a distinctive theme, it makes sense to me that the writers decided that Lunar stealth focused more on (Manipulation, Charisma, etc) trickery and illusion effects rather than Dexterity-style "I sneak real well" charms.
    At the very least, it makes sense that Dexterity would provide the Lunar way to move silently and to squeeze through cracks or escape out of binds they're not yet small enough to slip through.

    But I said above, yes, social Attribute Charms are better for altering memories of you ever being present at the scene. An Appearance Charm that controls your scent would be good for eliminating one sort of trace of your passage (or laying down a misleading trace). And Lunars can have Wits Charms for the entire concept of "always standing in someone's blindspot" for things like personal stealth and Rats In The Basement army-stealth.

    ​
    Originally posted by Zironic View Post
    Personally I think it would be easier to balance if Lunars didn't get "Turn into almost anything!" at chargen, but instead 'Turn into small things like Rats' was a charm in the stealth tree and 'turn into fast things like Cheetahs' was part of the move fast tree etc. It's the front-loaded part that makes it so awkward and also makes it hard for Lunars to distinguish themselves from each other.
    I agree, and I've agreed for a long while. But John and Holden have told us that Lunars don't spend XP to acquire and assume the forms of cheetahs and birds, so they're sticking with that direction with a different idea for making it work.

        Originally posted by SuddenThunder View Post
        I think of stealthy Lunars and I think of Alien, or Predator, or the Thing. Knowing that you're hunted by something but not knowing what it is, or where it is. Slowly being consumed one by one, not because it can't take you all at once but because it wants to play with you. Maybe it wants to let one of you get away and run back to the Realm with stories of how it terrorised you. Or maybe you'll be the last one left and you'll worship it, because what else can you possibly do?
        Well there's that. I also feel that there's a proper space for a Lunar turning out to be somewhere where you really don't expect or want them to be...

        But that's really endemic to any kind of stealthiness, isn't it?

        I guess looking at the aforementioned example, I don't quite feel so off about how they're getting in as what they're doing once there.

        ... No, no that's still not right. Becoming something kind of spectral, that still feels like a raksha and Abyssal thing.

        It's hard to grasp at suitable examples at the moment, given prior Editions that have been fairly bad at given textured approaches to stealth in general and distinctive and consistent things for Lunars in particular.

        I suppose, at the most basic level, I can say that I dislike the idea of Lunars having Charms that get too much into them having excessively magical substance. The idea of a Lunar transforming into smoke or light or shadow... it feels somehow incongruent with their core sources. A stealthy Lunar is something I feel should be fast and slick and able to slip into tight places and blend where they shouldn't...

        See, another thing that feels like it's making it hard to distinguish from Solars is that stealth and disguise and all of those kinds of things already inherently go into a space of looking like something you're not. I suppose, from there, I can understand the impulse to want all Solar Stealth to be explicit commands to be ignored, even if I vehemently disagree with it (Solars should be credibly good at all of their skills).

        Though there is some of the obliquely magical stuff as well; misdirection by leaving your skin behind, perhaps. Maybe shaking off a few of your hairs that crawl away as vermin to distract people.

        I don't know... maybe there's a mindspace to be taken from rough draft Infernal Stealth Charms, like turning into a sharp crimson wind, or being invisible and blind and attacking the first thing you bump into.

        Maybe it would also help to approach from an idea that Lunars won't have things that could be explicitly labeled as stealth Charms, even if stealth is their primary function. It's the ultimate purpose, but the motif and the Attribute aren't treating it as the central mechanism...

        Hmm, going back to that idea of more textured stealth challenges, a system or general play/storyteller mindset in which the actual tasks necessary to overcome stealth challenges being more varied would likely be useful. I mean, if it isn't, then the only reason I can ever really see for a Lunar not to just elect to have one or more small animal forms as their way of sneaking around is personal distaste. So I guess that for Charms that help you sneak about to be considered worthwhile compared to that, there needs to be a deeper sense of how you can be spotted or exposed that Charms would be more useful for going against, and a small animal form is there as a fallback for those Lunars not dedicated to facing those challenges.

                I believe we were told that Lunar tyrant lizards aren't going to be balancing on tree leaves with a single talon, even when using Graceful Crane Stance or whatever passes for it.

                Assuming that it's true, and Lunars can't use forms in counter-intuitive ways like that, perhaps they NEED to use forms in intuitive ways to get the most of their Charms.

                That is, a "moving silently over gravel, without displacing a pebble" Charm* might be mediocre in human form, impossible as an ox, and only truly effective if using the shape of a cat, mouse or snake.

                Sure, every Lunar can easily have all of those animal forms without spending XP, but the battle-focused Lunar will buy the Charms that work with combat-capable forms, and the infiltrator Lunar will buy the Charms that enhance his use of vermin forms. In this way, the samey Lunars are given a way to distinguish from each other despite their samey-ness.


                * Incidentally, that seems like a good way to avoid Graceful Crane Stance and Easily Overlooked Presence Method. Give Lunars a "good balance" Charm that provides certain benefits to stealth, without covering ALL the bases of stealth.

                                                Part of the problem, I think, is that actual characters are supposed to wield custom Charms. I see the core book Charm trees as outlines, giving you a sense of the general theme and power scale, rather than a selection of the actual powers available to any given Lunar. Attributes are far more flexible than Abilities and I feel that this should be reflected in the Charms.

                                                For instance, a Full Moon brawler might have a set of Spirit-trapping and -killing Charms building off his basic damage enhancers. He doesn't need a ton of Perception to hurt ghosts, that's silly - but on the flip side, no matter how strong he gets, it won't help him see them, much less track invisible ephemeral beings across the millennia.

                                                That's the sort of thing I'd like to see. Elder Lunars should not have every stat maxed out for the sake of covering all of their bases - but they should take their main Attributes to the limit and find weird and interesting ways of applying them to all situations.

                                                And, for the record, True Form, Form Lock and the current version of the Tell are all Anathema to be killed with radioactive hate-fire.

                                                Aretii: "As one of the players described it, 'After initiation, it was actually a relief seeing who had eyepatches or bandaged hands - you know what they sacrificed, instead of wondering what bits people cut out of their own souls to gain power.'"
