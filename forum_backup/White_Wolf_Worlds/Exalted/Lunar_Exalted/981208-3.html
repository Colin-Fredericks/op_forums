                            Originally posted by Gigaton-Falcon-Emu View Post
                            There is risk in relying too much on the 2ed First Age stuff as arguably it made any pro-usurpation force seem flat out insane or at least really short-sighted.
                            I disagree. I think the First Age should be pretty great, just so the Sidereal Exalted actually RISK something and make an actual dramatic decision when they decide to do the unthinkable.

                            The entire POINT of the Great Prophesy is it seemed, on the surface, like a pretty stupid idea when held in a greater context. What's the big deal about predicting an age of darkness when an age of darkness is already here? It becomes the Great Suggestion of Doing What We All Knew We Needed To Do Anyway.

                            The High First Age had many glorious heroes, and many were prone to vice or (more often) uneven tempers that could draw nations into war. Even given this, the good these luminous beings did far outweighed the bad. More to the point, even if it didn't, the astounding manipulative power these people have... primarily Solar, but also Lunar... ensures the vast majority of people will love them. They aren't living in hiding like the modern age, remember, they are essentially celebrities.

                            But that's good for the story actually. It makes the Great Prophesy a truly meaningful decision and sacrifice rather than just an acknowledgement of the current state of affairs. It makes it a prophesy rather than a status report. After all, it was a prediction of the future, not an appraisal of the present. It makes what the Sidereals saw actually horrifying rather than just 'duh'.
