Growing into an X00-foot-tall monstrosity with X heads and X^2 arms or having your blood turn into monsters is just the beginning! For example, Sun Wukong can use either his blood or his hair to change an object, allowing to take any one of his 72 forms, and then proceed to take any action he wills. Is there any reason that a PC with the power couldn't trick someone into swallowing said blood hair first, then have a Yeddim rip out of that person's stomach? That only differs from Alex Mercer turning you into a bomb in visual aesthetic.

                                                    Originally posted by While My Guitar View Post
                                                    That does raise the question, would an Exalted splat with that particular aesthetic work, and if so, what would they be like?
                                                    Alex Mercer is a Flesh Aspect Chernozem.

                                                    He fits to a T. He is a completely different person than the man from whose flesh he was born, despite having the same face and some memories. He was created as an act of vengeance, with the original Mercer injecting himself with Blacklight to make a monster that would kill the men trying to murder him. Flesh-aspected Charms are about physical power, violence, and efficiently incorporating the bodies of others into yourself to gain new strength.

                                                    The Liminals are easily the most appropriate splat for bio-punk science fiction flavor. Anything straight out of Prototype, Resident Evil, and Parasite Eve (at least, the first one, before Aya got classical-elemental powers).


                                                    Lunars are more like Mortal Kombat's T-Wolf, with his combat-shamanism and Wolf Totem "Animality", or Brave-Star invoking the spirits of beasts to become stronger, faster and farther-seeing. They belong to a more spiritual, animistic world, where Heart's Blood has nothing to do with DNA and everything to do with the essential spirit.
