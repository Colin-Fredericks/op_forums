                                        Originally posted by Lioness View Post
                                        So where does "inherently smart" go in terms of charms besides the excellency?
                                        In the abstract (before getting down to mechanical brass tacks), anything an Intelligence charm can do is likely to be valid design space for some kind of Ability charm, but I think you can make some of those things feel more like Intelligence Charms, and the mechanical effects can reinforce that.

                                        It's not as dire a state of affairs as Stamina vs. Resistance or Perception vs. Awareness, happily. If I were going to pin down the difference between "Intelligence" and high ratings in knowledge abilities, I'd say it's that Intelligence represents a tireless, dynamic mind and a capacity for leaping insights and unexpected synergies. Feynman famously reduced most of the math involved into physics into a small set of elemental equations that could do everything, because he was extremely good at grasping, distilling, and repurposing the essence of complex systems. Being able to do that in the first place requires a firm grounding of the Ability, but it's an Intelligence task in itself.
