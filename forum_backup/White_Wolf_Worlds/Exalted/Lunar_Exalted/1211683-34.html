            Sorry, I edited my post after you posted without realising, so you probably missed my edit where I addressed that.

            As I said, their purpose being to fight Fair Folk is not much use if your game doesn't feature Fair Folk.
            In none of the games I've played, did Fair Folk feature much. A bit, but not much.
            The only game where we had Fair Folk a fair bit was the one where a Raksha was the boyfriend of a Solar PC (then a Lunar PC, now another Solar PC), and another Raksha is a member of the party.
            So if the Lunar PCs' purpose was defending vs Raksha, it'd be pretty awkward.

            I'll also say that a lot of our group has played Lunars, and when people have not played Lunars, it's because a)their concept didn't feature shapeshifting, b)Solars are more powerful, or c)it was a DB game.
            If your character concept is "general" or "swordsman" or "ninja"... that's probably a Solar. It's not a Lunar. It doesn't involve a totem or shapeshifting. You could of course make it "general who is also a werewolf" or "cat-swordsman" or "rat-ninja", but that's changing the basic concept.
            When people wanted to play a shapeshifter*, or a character with an animal theme, they played a Lunar.
            And you can't really change that; if people don't want to play a shapeshifter (even occasionally) then playing a Solar and not a Lunar is fine.

            *Except when one guy played a Slayer who could turn into a massive 30ft monster. But he only did that occasionally, shapeshifting wasn't really a thing.

            Now saying this, the one thing that did put people off Lunars, which 3rd ed can fix, was the power level. They were just a lot worse than Solars at everything that wasn't a)being really tough, or b)being really pretty, or c)shapeshifting.
            Now, it's not a big issue if they're a little worse, shapeshifting makes up for that. But if your concept was "sorcerer", and their excellencies were way worse than Solars, and they paid more XP than Solars, and they had less starting spells than Solars... you're probably not going to play a Lunar.
