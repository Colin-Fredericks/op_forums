    What is a Lunar?

    12-08-2015, 09:57 AM
    What is a Lunar? I know they are not simply Shapeshifters or Animorphs. They often get Pigeon-holed into "Savage Barbarian Warrior", but that is a very two dimensional view of the splat. Lunars are not Garou, Warriors/Protectors of Gaia.

    So what are they? Are the the Premier Predators of Creation? A malleable Jack of All Trades, in contrast to a Solar's Supernal master of one? The Exalted Equivalent of a Swiss Army Knife?

          Monsters with virtue to them, or the virtuous with monstrous inner features. Have you read through Cult of the Illuminated? The Lunar companion Faithful Pia is a fairly good illustration of this. She's noted as being desperate to be harmless, but can turn into what is at least textually, if not mechanically, described as a tail-lashing monster that can tear things apart, should someone push her too far. While her original 1e description was a bit too much of a curse-type werewolf story (not a Werewolf story in caps indicating W:tA), you don't have to remove or change many things to fit the essence of what it seems that the developers were going for as a concept for Lunars into EX3. She has a strong duality that makes her seem completely innocent yet has seen some shit and can fall into pure viciousness. Anything about attempted abduction by the Silver Pact or chimerae can fall to the wayside, if you get your hands on CotI and check it out.

          So, basically, a unity of conflicting opposites at the most extreme ends, combined with some aspects of various werebeast legends because Luna loves Gaia and her Exalted are explicitly shaped by this affection.

                          Lunars are people who broke their chains.

                          Luna looked down upon the world and saw heroes after her own heart; people willing to face the consequences of running away from their enslavers, their stifling homes, their controlling parents, their oppressive religions, their financial debts, their sworn oaths. These people were brave enough to run after freedom, even if it killed them.

                          One of the Lunars in the corebook was being shipped back to the Realm to serve as a sex slave. She threw herself into shark-infested waters to be eaten alive or drowned to death. Luna decided that such courage should be Exalted.

                          To these brave souls, Luna grants the freedom of shapeshifting. No longer are you confined to the form in which you are born. With your Tell as your kite string, you soar around the full spectrum of human appearance with your true form. With your totem, you can automatically assume the form of the animal that most reflects you, and even assume a were-beast form of incredible power. Heart's Blood allows you to take any animal form you want as well.

                          She also offers the freedom of raw power and versatility, because Lunar Exalted are second only to Solars and their ilk in that regard. The Sidereals and Alchemicals don't come close, because of the restrictions on each of their Charmsets.

                          The primary Lunar narrative is ALSO about freedom, because they're faced with the Realm and Sidereals who want to deny them the freedom to LIVE, and the return of the Solars threatens the Lunar freedom to choose to become something different than they were in the First Age.

                                          There are a few major ways to look at Lunars.

                                          The Military Outlook:
                                          Consider that all the Exalt types were designed to fight in the Primordial War. Thus each type had to have some sort of function, a role to play or a job to do. Lunars are the ultimate spec-ops exalt type. They infiltrate enemy territory, they cause massive supply disruptions and they can turn produce their own armies out of the locals (or local wild life). This is further supported by the fact that out of all the exalt types, Lunars gain the least benefit from artifacts. Why purchase an artifact skyship when you can fly on your own, why get a daiklave when you can create claws, etc. This means that they are self contained and not dependent on supply lines or access to friendly military support.

                                          The Easy Story Outlook:
                                          A different method to look at Lunars is from a Storytelling perspective. Nothing is expected of them in the greater narrative of the Exalted setting. A solar is expected to reshape the world, an Abyssal is expected to end it, an Infernal is expected to unleash hell on Creation, the Sidereals are expected to maintain the status quo, and the Terrestrials are supposed to breed and make bad decisions.

                                          But a Lunar can just be a barbarian, they can be a scholar, they can be anything. The amount of animalism or barbarism in any given Lunar is completely up to the player. It makes just as much sense for a Lunar to build a City State as it does to be a murder-hobo. Thus Lunars are the Exalt type for Players who don't want any of the other exalt types preconceived notions and for the STs that want to run a Celestial Exalt game without the Celestial Exalt expectations. I mean you can run a Lunar game with all the characters being in Realm Secondary school complete with anime high school tropes and it wouldn't feel that out of place.

                                          The Business Model:
                                          Consider that Furrys, transhumanism, Totems and other related themes tend to sell well to certain aspects of nerd culture. The Lunars are there to check a box so that the animal focused players have something to default to when looking at Exalted as a game system to invest in. Speaking of niche appeal, this also applies to Alchemicals. Funny how both niche types are so similar in mechanics design and concept.
