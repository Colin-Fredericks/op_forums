    What if the Lunar War Form had nothing to do with your shape?

    12-19-2015, 11:51 AM
    From the very beginning of the game, Lunars could always become werewolves or other werebeasts without Deadly Beastman Transformation. An individual Lunar could always, in fact, have multiple werebeast forms which had nothing to do with their Totem.

    It was (and still is) as easy as taking the Heart's Blood of beastfolk.

    If you're a Lunar with a songbird totem, you can easily have an elephant-man form to use for stomping faces. A Lunar with a rhinoceros Totem could take the Heart's Blood of a weasel-man or eagle-woman if he wants a combat form with plenty of agility or combat flight. A Lunar with a fast and lethal werewolf War Form could switch to a more defensive strategy by turning into a turtle-man.

    Hunting beastfolk for their forms shouldn't be more distasteful than hunting other human beings, not even for a Lunar who started as a beastperson.


    This has never been seriously considered before, either by writers or players, because of Form Lock. But in Third Edition, Form Lock is dead; you will be able to use ANY form from the beginning of combat to the end. Further, if the developers really do decide that ANY form-related dice in excess of your natural dice pools counts against your dice-adding limit (to further avoid encouraging people to pick only Spirit Shapes with high Attributes), then the old concept of "true and false" forms would become entirely meaningless and perish right along with Form Lock.

    I would like us to consider what this means for the War Form. Specifically, what "shape" it actually needs to take.


    At present, I am not convinced that "shape" in the physical sense will practically matter all that much to the War Form anymore, because of Evocations. Beastmen of all sorts, and Lunars in all sorts of mortal beastmen forms, should be able to use practically any Artifact and Evocation without major balance considerations, just as any Solar beastman could. The physical Merits used to represent beastmen features are simply not that overwhelming in conjunction.

    Furthermore, what sense does it make that donning a particular physical shape would suddenly cause your Flying Silver Dream to fall from the air to the ground as a totally inert sword? Or revert your moonsilver snake familiar back into its conventional direlance form? Or close your Eye of Omens, denying you Sight Beyond Sight?

    I can no longer find the relevant quote, but either Holden Shearer or John Morke explained the conflict between the Lunar War Form and Lunar Evocations as one of "internal versus external focus". The Lunar Exalted were already being envisioned as Chosen whose magic had a more internal focus than the Solar Exalted ("changing themselves to change the world", rather than changing the world more directly), which is why Lunars wouldn't be as good at Martial Arts and Sorcery and Evocations as Solars. Further, that assuming the War Form meant the Lunar was turning the focus of their power even further inward than normal, cutting them off more completely from external forms of expression, such as Evocations.

    So what if the War Form was entirely about that inward focus, rather than a physical transformation?

    What if every Lunar gained a hybrid shape as automatically as they gained a fully animal "spirit shape", but that the true spirit of Deadly Beastman Transformation's Evocation-exclusive power was attained with a Charm more like Relentless Lunar Fury?


    Have you ever wanted to use your Spirit Shape as a War Form? As good as DBT was, forms like the tyrant lizard had a lot of appeal, and now they appeal even more. With Relentless Lunar Fury functioning as the "War Mode" of the Lunar Exalted, you could enjoy your full "Lunar Demigod" combat power in either your dinosaur-man or full dinosaur forms as you please. (Or as your situation requires, because ceilings are a thing.)


    Do you remember the 2.5 Knack, Beast Power Concentration? It allowed you to enjoy all of the non-mutation benefits of the War Form in your human shape. Which at the time was rather weaksauce, since it was really only a way to increase your Attribute ratings and cheese your Excellencies.

    But consider what Beast Power Concentration would mean in Third Edition, where powering up your human form with "War Mode" means that you are committing to using your fully unlocked magical Evoking sword as little more than a slab of sharp metal in your superhumanly powerful fists.


    Furthermore, this means that the Lunar War Form is entirely about FIGHTING. In the past, the War Form was locked to your hybrid shape, but you could use those bonus Attribute dots for anything, like crafting. Perhaps ESPECIALLY for crafting, with that bonus Dexterity dot.

    But as a Relentless Lunar Fury sort of Charm, the purpose of the power up is entirely clear and focused: It is all about beating the SHIT out of something, here and now. The War Form becomes completely, utterly, and exclusively about WAR.
