Would it really be that far out there from Lunars using their regular charms in animal form.

                      a lunar can use martial arts charms while in warform - that's basically an animal form

                      the trick is that martial arts charms require human or humanoid form - a lunar in an animal form... simply doesn't have the range of movements required for martial arts. Sure, a few might sort of fit the bill, but overall animals simply do not have the bits for it.

                      Maybe an essence 5 custom knack would allow for it - to a certain extent.

                      The thing is that it just doesn't make sense for a mouse, or a cat, to punch and kick as hard as a human. A gorrila? Ya I can see that, maybe, and with a knack that increases the range of motion of a human-sized animal.

                                      Yeah... the general point seems to be 'MA are designed for humans to emulate animals. Not for animals to use. Which leaves me... baffled, quite honestly. At least in terms of MA charms. They use Kata's that reflect an animal and its movements. Shouldn't actually being that animal and moving like it make those charms still accessable? Why is Emulation better able to handle that?

                                      There's the question of physical strength, but that's ultimately a subissue. Lunars having the strength of their animal form or true form is a different design decision. It shouldn't affect MA though. A snake lacks a human's strength... but it's still fast, and still moves in a manner similar to a snake stylist.

                                      IDK, I'd just like to see Lunars having an incentive to fight in smaller animal forms, rather than doing the traditional thing of going straight for warform or the biggest creature they can find. It's a cool visual to me.
