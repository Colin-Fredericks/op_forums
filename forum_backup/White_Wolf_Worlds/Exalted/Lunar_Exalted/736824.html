    [Ex3] Lunar Crafting Charm concepts

    10-29-2015, 12:28 AM
    Shaping the Once-Living Form
    This Charm represents the Lunar discovering a supernatural talent for working with organic materials. Antlers into spears, hides into clothing, trees into log-cabins, flowers into perfumes, animals and plants into food. Mechanically expressed through dice-tricks that help Craft 0 Lunars compensate for a lack of skill through sheer Attribute talent, and which of course continue to work fine for master craftsmen Lunars.


    The Hunt Is My Muse
    This Charm represents a Lunar's ability to use hunting not only to supply his craftsmanship, but also to inspire it. Mechanically expressed by gaining Silver crafting points when he seeks to make practical use of the carcass of any creature he killed; fewer points if he merely drew blood against the creature without personally dealing the final blow. In essence, this lets the Lunar substitute hunting for minor crafting projects in order to skip ahead to major projects and earning Gold points.

    It might be going too far to have an upgrade which lets the Lunar specifically hunt magical prey in return for Gold crafting points, in order to skip straight from "killing the Elemental Dragon of Fire" to "fashioning armor from its scaly hide". But even if that's fine, the Lunar probably needs to be the one to take the prey's Heart's Blood.

    A different sort of upgrade would allow the Lunar to spend crafting experience gained in this way as regular experience points, towards unlocking Innate and Magical abilities for his false forms (or at least specifically the form of the animal he just hunted).

    Another sort of upgrade might let the Lunar gain the full crafting point reward for participating in a hunt, provided the Lunar has tasted the Heart's Blood of the one who killed the prey. So the Lunar is well-equipped to provide for a blood-brother, or not punished for bringing his overly-enthusiastic familiar on the hunt.


    Argent Muse Approach
    The Lunar can give his crafting experience away to someone else, especially a Solar Mate?


    Weapon Shaping Prana
    Full Moons may be warriors who prioritize physical power above other methods, but all Lunar Exalted are hunters in their own fashions, and No Moons prefer to hunt with the fruits of their inventiveness. This Charm represents an expansion of the talents of Shaping the Once-Living form for the purpose of fashioning weaponry and armor. Perhaps also lethal traps, such as pits embedded with sharpened sticks, or poisons. Mechanically represented with more dice-enhancing tricks.


    Secure Den Prana
    Lunar Exalted instinctively understand the importance of shelter. As Weapon Shaping Prana, but enhances "turning trees into log cabins" and isn't stingy about working with earthier materials like dirt and rocks. No Elsewhere Dens -- the Lunar is carving out physical shelters within the normal world.


    Invisible Warren Creation
    Following Secure Den Prana, the Lunar develops her talent for building shelters into a talent for building hidden shelters, and disguising existing ones.


    Frozen Ripple Lair
    The Lunar progresses beyond building boltholes in the world, and into the realm of building boltholes from reality itself. Alice explores the white rabbit's hole at her peril. A little more like Elsewhere Dens, but still with physical entrances and ontological existences independent of the Lunar herself.


    Earth and Moon Blessing
    Talent for creating or altering demesnes and manses, especially of the Lunar aspect.


    Luna's Blessed Hands
    Expands the talents of Shaping the Once Living Form to include moonsilver. Since moonsilver can only be used for superior projects, and the Lunar may not even have the many Ability minimums required for such a thing, this Charm should probably provide some other kind of benefit as well. Or else it might double-down on rewarding the Lunar for having the skill to attempt making an artifact.


    Moonsilver Shaping Rite
    Talent at changing moonsilver artifacts into radically different forms. May enhance attempts to simply upgrade such artifacts instead.


    Moonsilver Blade Reconfiguration
    Talent at radically changing the nature, personality and therefore Evocations of moonsilver artifacts. Would definitely enhance attempts to simply upgrade.

      So, you may or may not be aware that the last time I spoke with the developers and writers, "anything that isn't human or animal" was basically off the table for Lunar Heart's Blood shapeshifting. If Lunars are going to turn into magical beasts, then the magic needs to come from their own powers rather than the Blood they steal. Given Lunar Charms, sorcerous initiations and workings, Hearthstones, artifact tattoos, and the potential for Lunars to buy the "magical" abilities which mundane beasts can be trained to enjoy, this limitation is fine by me.

      But it also means a lot of old Knacks are now sitting around, waiting to be given new purpose.


      Hearth-and-Flame Shell
      Follows after Luna's Blessed Hands. With this Charm, the Lunar can kill an elemental and force the material corpse to remain intact, even if the elemental's spiritual essence employs a means to resurrect itself. Further, the Lunar adds jade to the materials he can work with Shaping the Once-Living Form, provided that he is using the color of jade which corresponds to the elemental's aspect.

      Ash-and-Tears Banquet
      As Hearth-and-Flame Shell, but for ghosts. The Lunar may also now work more easily with soulsteel, whether when fashioning artifacts from the materialized shells of ghosts or the fleshy corpses of dead mortals.

      Green Sun Child
      As Hearth-and-Flame Shell, but for demons of any circle. The Lunar may work their flesh with orichalcum.

      Heaven's Servant Imitation
      In this case, imitating the Sidereal ability to turn gods into artifacts. As Heart-and-Flame Shell, but for gods and starmetal.

      Hungry Dream Cloak
      As Hearth-and-Flame Shell, but for Fair Folk and works of glamour. Using the gossamer dress of the raksha noble you slew when stitching her dream-corpse into a fancy dress will not count as a magical material, but it will at least count as an exotic ingredient and fall under the dice-bonuses of Shaping the Once-Living Form. If you want to use a magical material, there's always moonsilver, OR you can use any of the other Charms above to mix in corpses of other spirits; a fire dragon with a watery raksha might yield a jade-threaded cloak of steaming mist?


      Again, none of these Charms prevent these spirit creatures from resurrecting -- they simply force the shell of their materialized forms to remain intact, whereas normally they would dissipate.

      And if the devs rule that materialized spirit forms ALREADY remain intact, then these Charms still provide plenty in terms of working more effectively with those corpses and their associated magical materials.

          Why 3E Lunars shouldn't have 2E's Clay Wetting Practice

          Ability-based magic is, at least in some ways, more restricted than Attribute-based magic. A Solar Exalt can learn to create any sort of Melee weapon with Glorious Golden Saber, but because it is a Melee Charm, the Solar cannot expand upon this power to create armor, bows, or desks out of Glorious Solar energy.

          Attribute magic doesn't care. If a Lunar has the power to soften materials, this power cannot be restricted to crafting actions. Indeed, 2ndEd's Clay Wetting Practice had a secondary function which aided Lunars in feats of strength to break objects by making them weaker and less resistant to force.

          That's just in one Dexterity Charm, and it wouldn't stop there. The themes of that Charm logically expand into the power to soften the world around the Lunar, allowing manipulation of the environment and even the laws of reality. It goes further and further away from the internal focus of Lunar power, where they are supposed to change themselves first in order to change the world, rather than changing the world directly.

                          Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz View Post
                          That's an interesting idea for the knacks, I kind of like that. I wonder if it's powerful enough though?
                          I've reconsidered something that should make them more accessible and convenient, if not more powerful.

                          None of them would require Luna's Blessed Hands, so that Lunars can start using spirit carcasses immediately, well before they're capable of making artifacts. The supernatural bones and hides of elementals and demons should be worthy materials for Major Projects that produce mundane-level weapons as good as steel blades and mail.

                          But if that's the primary draw, then perhaps instead a singular Charm for preventing spirit corpses from dissolving -- Form-Fixing Method.



                          In terms of Craft charms generally; if the power is all inside the Lunar and not external to them, how does this affect crafting (which is something external), without meaning they come across as weak Solar charms (since Solar craft charms are internal).
                          Notice that the emphasis has been on the Lunar's own talents. Each Charm is an expansion of their personal talent, insight, and understanding. They discover new realms of instinct, intuition, an creative inspiration which guide them.

                          Making these Charms weaker than Solar Charms is both missing the point and part of the goal, actually.


                          It's missing the point in that Lunar Charms are less restricted than Solar Charms. Lunars really COULD have Flesh-Sculpting Art follow after Shape The Once-Living Form, representing the Lunar developing his talents towards working with flesh that still lives and bleeds with the intent to keep it that way, with endless numbers of Charms following after that tangent, and perhaps even branching off into more tangents. But Solar Craft Charms can only narrowly stray away from crafting, and then only so far.


                          But the general weaknesses is also intentional, since Solar Exalted are supposed to be stronger. But in places that meet the Lunar themes, they can rival the Solars.

                          Lunar aesthetics are supposed to be "the barbarian, dressed in feathers and armed with wood and bone". Lunars should be able to do amazing things with organic materials, and perhaps could even carry forward the "vegetative technology" of the Dragon Kings.

                          But mostly, we're looking at Monster Hunter levels of "turn dragons into swords". Lunar versions of Herakles, wearing the impenetrable hide of the Nemean Lion as a buff jacket.



                          On the other hand, the fluff of Flesh-Sculpting Art is that, well, you sculpt flesh (or bone) and I see your point of making everything about changing the Lunar, not changing the environment (though the changed Lunar might better change it).
                          The developers were quite firm about the Lunar focus being internal.

                          That focus fits with the sheer amount of power to be found in Heart's Blood shapeshifting, and with how Deadly Beastman Transformation (internal) clashes with Evocations (external), and with how Lunars can't quite reach the same levels of sorcerous power as Solar Exalted (external).

                          I would definitely keep the name "Flesh-Sculpting Art", but the justification that the Lunar is literally sculpting flesh like wet clay will have to be replaced with something much more metaphorical.

                          Placing the medical Charm after the crafting Charm shouldn't be a hindrance to Medicine-focused Lunars, either, since physicians will need the ability to improvise bandages, build stretchers, fashion surgical tools, and create medicines and poultices and drugs. All of which is assisted by Shape The Once-Living Form.

                                              Had a different idea for Luna's Blessed Hands.

                                              It allows the Lunar to work with moonsilver as though it were an organic material, but the Lunar isn't limited to using moonsilver to make artifacts. In fact, nothing technically limits the Exalted from using the magical materials for mundane weaponry.

                                              Moonsilver is much harder to come by than steel, but as the Tribal Hero Exalted, the Lunars have no supernatural talent with steel. And the Lunars of the Second Age probably have a lot of raw moonsilver stockpiled or scavenged from First Age artifacts they've destroyed.

                                              A moonsilver broadsword isn't as good as a moonsilver daiklave, but it's cheaper and easier to make, and a lot of the time, Lunars either won't have hands for swords anyway, or they're in DBT and unable to use Evocations.

                                              And of course, Form-Fixing Method and using the corpses of spirits opens up the opportunity to use the other magical materials.
