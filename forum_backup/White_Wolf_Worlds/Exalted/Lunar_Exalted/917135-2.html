                                            Train Heartnet of Black Cat is very thoroughly a Solar Exalt in aesthetics, but he can be used as inspiration for as a Lunar.

                                            His backstory is that he was the thirteenth member of the Chronos Numbers, the special enforcers and assassins of the Chronos Organization. Train was especially talented and skilled at assassin -- enough so to be one of the most successful of the Numbers despite lacking any of their passionate loyalty for the organization. He went about his job mechanically, unable to imagine any other life even as he died a little more inside each day.

                                            One day, he met a bounty hunter full of compassion and carefree joy. Inspired and challenged by her example, Train worked with her to capture bounties without killing them. Train began to imagine a life of a freedom as a bounty hunter who could choose where he went, who he hunted, and whether or not he killed.

                                            Then one of his creepy admirers, horrified by Train's change of heart, murdered Train's new friend and mentor. That was the last straw for the 13th Number; he killed the murderer (or so he thought), announced his defection, and fought every loyalist assassin who took affront at his treachery. None of the other Numbers stepped in to stop him or those who attacked him, so Train walked away a free man.

                                            Now Train makes a meager, hand-to-mouth living as a bounty hunter, letting whim and whimsy direct his every choice and action, going anywhere and doing anything simply because it makes him feel alive.

                                                What you own, owns you.

                                                The more you own, the more you have to fear losing.

                                                Rich men live in constant fear of losing their fortunes -- to tax collectors, to foreign conquerors, to thieves, to gold-digging lovers, to impatient heirs, to fire and other natural disasters. The larger their wealth, the more that other people scheme to take it, and the more difficult it is to defend. Often the greed of rich men is enriched by their simple paranoia -- the more wealth they can accumulate, the more they can shore themselves up against potential losses and the better they can recover. But this only creates a vicious cycle, as increasing their wealth only makes it harder to defend and more tempting to steal.

                                                Few rich men truly admit this trap to themselves. Fewer still admit that it is making them miserable.

                                                Luna Chose one of the rare-as-hen's-teeth men who had the strength to simply walk away from it all. He gave away all he had, paying back all of his debts and giving the rest away to various loved ones and worthy causes, and requested to join the local Immaculate monastery so that he might find freedom in spiritual enlightenment.

                                                Becoming one of the Anathema turned that plan on its head, but learning the truth of the world set him freer than he could have imagined.



                                                Also, Luna probably Exalts no few number of heroes who kick an addiction, such as to drugs or alcohol or more unspeakable or exotic things.

                                                    Do you know what it is like to feel trapped within your own body, since the day you were born?

                                                    You have never known the vigor or vitality of your friends and neighbors. You tire quickly, injure easily, heal slowly, and catch every seasonal illness. Every time a real sickness starts to spread through town, you must lock yourself away for fear that if you fall under it, you will never wake again.

                                                    You are cursed with the congenital disease of pale blood; if pricked, you bleed, but not crimson red. Your essence is a vague, pearly-grey; watery and thin. This public knowledge, the deathly pallor of your skin, and your frequent bouts of infection have ensured that you grew up a pariah within your community; a figure of pity, scorn, and fear. No one would miss you.

                                                    As an adult, the day came that you could bear your life no more. So one night, with the full moon high in the sky to light your way, you traveled alone through the wilderness, braving brigand and beast, to seek out the infamous, dangerously xenophobic city of Yharnam.

                                                    Few who dared venture to that mad city ever returned, and none with their miracle cure-all. The Yharnamites were as notorious for their stingy control of Blood Ministration as they were for the rumors of monsters lurking in their woods and sewers.

                                                    Under the cover of night, you managed to avoid the beasts and the hunting mobs long enough to find the one man in the whole city who could be begged or bartered into treating your illness. Your hope begins to rise when he recognizes your condition.

                                                    "Ah, yes. Paleblood. Well, you've come to the right place. Yharnam is the home of blood ministration. You need only unravel its mystery. But where's an outsider like yourself to begin? Easy, with a bit of Yharnam blood of your own. But first, you'll need a contract."

                                                    Nothing about this seems on the level, especially when the blindfolded blood minister insists that you be fully bound to the sick bed for the procedure. But you would rather die than live another day as you are; even the slimmest possibility of freedom is too sweet to refuse. Resolving that any risk is worth it, you accept the contract, lie down, and reign in your panic as the old man straps you down and sticks the needles into your arms.

                                                    The Moon watches it all, and Chooses.

                                                    As the Yharnam blood begins coursing through your veins, you fall unconscious, and dream feverishly.

                                                    A growing pool of blood, in the richest crimson hue you have ever seen. You can scarcely begin to covet the color before a monster rises up out of it; a wolfish Beast which seems made of sticky, hardened, clotted blood. Just looking it seems to remind you of everything dark and base and nasty about your fellow Man.

                                                    The Beast growls languidly as it approaches you, taking its time as if to draw out the pleasure of your fear. It reaches out to your face with a claw, but turned backwards, as if to brush your cheek in a horrid parody of a woman's alluring invitation.

                                                    At the moment of contact, your Essence sets the Beast on fire. The tempting sickness it represents is helpless to overcome or withstand the Lunar Essence which imbued you first, and so the Beast collapses and dies in flames.

                                                    Then you hear a woman's voice, kind and gentle. "It seems I have found myself a Hunter."
