    Random Lunar speculation

    03-08-2014, 07:31 PM
    A few days ago I was going over the "what we know" wiki and I came across two quotes about Lunars that struck me as rather interesting. The first mentioned Lunars still having change as a theme, but with an emphasis on change over time. The second quote mentioned that the loss of two of the Lunar Casts would be an significant part of their backstory.

    Taken together, these two quotes imply to me that the loss of the Casts for Lunars is a deliberate thing, either built directly in to the Exaltation itself as a kind of evolution/adaptation to their circumstances, or that the Lunars can deliberately pick and choose their Casts, and that the Lost Casts have simply fallen out of use thanks to their current war with the Sidereals and the Realm (or some other option that I haven't thought of).

              My guess is that they're going to go beyond the "band-aid" solution modeled in previous editions and attempt to portray the Lunars as actually having performed reconstructive surgery on their Exaltations, as previous editions claimed.

              No Wyld Taint, no Wyld-related Chimerism, no Wyld-related Lost Castes.

              After the Primordial War, the Lunar Exalted reinvented themselves to make a place for themselves in a world where Exalted and humanity ruled Creation. After the Usurpation, the Lunar Exalted reinvented themselves again, to survive and conquer a world where the Solar Exalted had vanished, the Terrestrials and Sidereals ruled, and the Lunars themselves were hunted like beasts.

              The devs have told us before that a First Age game would operate on a scale that vanilla Exalted is not designed to properly handle. They said the same thing was even more true of the Primordial War. Storytellers and players are free to explore those eras, but the game isn't optimized for them the way it is for the pre-Shogunate world. So the developers are never again going to waste time providing content for playing the preceding eras.

              That means the Lost Castes (both sets) are supposed to be just as big a "white-space" as the exact number and roster of Primordials and Death Lords. You're free to make it up yourself, but it doesn't work for the game as a whole to nail that down.

              Whereas the first two editions bemoaned the loss of the Lost Castes and suggested recovering them as a worthy (if not required) pursuit for Lunar players wanting to "win the game", I feel the third edition will say, ""You don't need the old Castes. These new Castes are rad, and you shouldn't take anyone pining for the old ones seriously."

              More, I feel the next edition will reverse the old ball-and-chain into a new and better challenge, "If your current Castes aren't working for you any more, why try to recreate the failed past? Make your own Castes, to fit your needs. Do better than the old guys!"

              formerly Tornado Wolf, formerly Inugami
