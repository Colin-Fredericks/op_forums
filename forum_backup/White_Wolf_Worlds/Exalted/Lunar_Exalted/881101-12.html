    I'll probably make a longer effortpost on this later, but a big part of the issue with W: tA as Lunar inspiration is that removing the Garou from the context of the World of Darkness also removes a lot of their justifications for existing.
    "Luna loves Gaia and made her Exalted in Gaia's image" is explicitly spelled out. It's the likely reason for the similarities one sees to Garou and Fera in this game.

    They don't fight to defend nature, because nature can defend itself just fine in Creation.
    Technically, what the Garou and Fera are there to do is combat primordial corruption. In a modern context, it often involves prevention of environmental damage, but they were doing it long, long before any of those things were a problem. They tangled with Zmei, they imprisoned the Talons of the Wyrm when it tried to put a hand through (vaguely kind of; it could easily be more allegorical), and stopped an incursion of the Beast of War as well. Mokole had an even longer tenure prior to modern times, stretching back to when reptiles first appeared. And, like the Lunars, they did undergo quite a few changes (notably, though this is recent in how it was detailed, in how Mokole were once able to reproduce among one another and call in wondrous and impossible things into the world, until the Dragon Wars happened and they fucked up even worse than the Garou have).

    What this translates to in a "squint and look sideways" is conflict against an order gone wrong. Which is what their struggle with the Realm is.

    They don't champion or defend the dispossessed, but instead sacrifice them in droves to buy a few more years for themselves.
    Not a few more years for themselves, a few less years for the Terrestrials and the Sidereals behind them. As you note below, they don't rely on reproduction to exist. They can keep weathering the Realm's First Age artifice down until it collapses and they finally free themselves of their entanglement to the state of the world, becoming something else yet again.

    They don't represent any tribal societies or lineages because they're Celestial Exalted, set above and apart from the rest of their peoples, and the only marks of their heritage are beastfolk, born as living weapons for their forebears' sakes and basically as wronged by their progenitors as anyone they're sent to fight.
    Lots of beastfolk are slated to have nothing to do with Lunars in this edition (thankfully). Holden mentioned a few days, maybe a week ago that one of the reasons could be that the Wyld sweeps through, among other, non-Lunar causes.

    Even when they do represent, defend, or otherwise give patronage to mortal societies, the devs themselves are on record that it's never made sense to want a Lunar in charge of anything - say (caveat: the devs have not said this, and it is purely my inference based on their statements), if someone in Perfect Soul's position were to be chosen by Luna instead of Sol, they would be wrong to do what she did; to kick out the occupiers and reassert themselves as the legitimate authority.
    It's not their greatest strength, but it doesn't mean you don't want one in charge at all.

    Barring some major setting revelations to shake things up, 3e Lunars don't come across as Werewolf: the Apocalypse. They come across as Beast: the Primordial. I have nothing nice to say about Beast.
    Read more W:tA? It has old legends of battle, a changing society (there used to not be tribes, after all), and a breed of changers that's slowly incorporating humans, maybe (Rokea). The parallels are far from absent, even if they don;t leap out at every reader quite so obviously.

                                        Originally posted by Amine Hsu View Post
                                        Po-tay-toe, Po-tah-toe? You can say it isn't shapeshifting but you can enact such radical changes it's as much shapeshifting as many of the things you've already described as shapeshifting.
                                        The terminology matters, for a bunch of reasons. Thematically, it's important that Solar charms be presented as a supernatural extension of skill, which shapeshifting isn't a good fit for; "I am so skilled that I can even disguise myself as a dragon, and nobody can tell the difference even if they try to touch me" (which they can't even actually do with their published charms, but which might make sense somewhere in their charmset) is very different, thematically, from "I bend and twist my body into the form of a dragon"

                                        It also matters because it tells you where your charms go from there. "With my peerless talent at disguise, I can disguise myself as the Scarlet Empress so well that nobody can see through it" might go to "With my peerless talent at disguise, I can even disguise myself as a dragon!" It probably does not extend to "I can turn into a dragon, then breathe fire at people as an effective combat trick."

                                        Whereas, say "I can shapeshift into a perfect duplicate of the Scarlet Empress" might go there.

                                        I mean, I also have severe doubts about the underlying mechanism here. If I had to nail down the exact supernatural methods used by Solar disguise charms any more specifically than "peerless skill at disguise", I would probably say that they work through supernaturally-intense deception (sometimes automatically-successful deception), akin to illusions -- not through changing any aspect of your physical body. If my character is short and I use Solar magic to disguise myself as someone much taller than I am, it doesn't let me reach things on the top shelf; it just ensures that I'm so persuasive and clever that nobody will notice or think it odd that I don't seem to reach things on the top shelf. If I'm a huge guy and disguise myself as a tiny girl, I won't be able to slip through tiny crevices -- I'll just be very good at coming up with excuses why I can't.

                                        (Granted that in that last case in particular "slipping through small areas" probably fits into Larceny, so I might have other magic that would cover me. But I'm not actually tiny, I'm just convincing people I am, and there are serious implications to that even if it serves some of the same purposes as shapeshifting.)

                                                        Strength

                                                        Devastating Ogre Enhancement - for the rest of the scene, the Lunar swells to twice her mass, her bones thickening and her muscles doubling. Her Strength and Stamina are increased, but the bonuses count against her Excellency dice-adding limits.

                                                        Emperor Ox Expansion - for the duration of one attack or feat of strength, the Lunar explodes in size, striking with far greater mass, lifting with superior leverage, or absorbing damage with thicker flesh.

                                                        Mountainous Spirit Expression - the Lunar may use Heart's Blood to reflexively shapeshift into a creature larger than a man, but not so large as to have the Legendary Size merit.

                                                        Towering Beast Form - the Lunar may use the stolen Heart's Blood of a creature with the Legendary Size merit. Shapeshifting into such a form requires an action which cannot be flurried.

                                                        God-Beast Transcendence - the Lunar may now flurry another action -- including an attack -- with an action to shapeshift into a creature with the Legendary Size merit.

                                                        Body-Breaking Kata - the Lunar enjoys greater faculty when attacking or grappling an opponent smaller than herself

                                                        Angry Rhino Charge - this Charm may supplement the first non-projectile attack the Lunar makes in the same turn that she entered close range with the target smaller than she is. The attack's damage is greatly enhanced. This can also be used to enhance a grappling gambit.

                                                        Flying Tiger Technique - supplementing a Move action or a successful Disengage or Withdraw action, the Lunar leaps one whole range band in any direction, regardless of terrain, as long as there is a clear path. This Charm likewise allows a swimming or flying Lunar to force his way through one range band of water or air currents strong enough to qualify as difficult terrain, or for a swimming Lunar to leap from the waters into the sky. Finally, it allows a flying Lunar to trade altitude for forward movement at a superior rate.


                                                        Dexterity

                                                        Humble Mouse Shape - the Lunar may use the stolen Heart's Blood of a creature smaller than a house cat.

                                                        Emerald Grasshopper Form - the Lunar may use Heart's Blood reflexively shapeshift into a creature smaller than a man, but not smaller than a house cat.

                                                        Butterfly Eyes Defense - the Lunar avoids an attack by exploding into a swarm of smaller creatures, either reforming instantly after the danger has passed or remaining in that shape amidst a distracting cloud of duplicates; they quickly fade, but may offer an opportunity to escape from sight.

                                                        Butterfly Eyes Feint - the Lunar circumvents a defense in a similar fashion to Butterfly Eyes Defense, allowing them to strike from unexpected angles. This may be used against a Clash attack.

                                                        Tyrant Mouse Dominion - the Lunar enjoys greater faculty when attacking or evading an opponent larger than himself
