    Lunars, Breeding Projects and Lost Eggs

    07-24-2014, 06:03 PM
    Seeing as Lunars have the best tools Creation-side to; locate, collect, and breed the Lost Eggs into a might host to bring down the Realm - one wonders why not? Beastmen are a thing that is used and Lunars are the unparalleled masters of all things, "breed that supersoldier". With the Lunar connection to the land/Gaia and their history both breeding beastmen and being curators of the Age of Dreams Dragonblooded House Armies it seems something they would do. Fight the Realm with their own host of Dragonbloods - laugh mightily at the Sidereals and their pawns as they throw their own upon the field. What are thoughts? It seems PC's often collect 1-2 lost eggs over the course of a short game to be trusted seconds and the like - what says the forum?

              Because expecting Dragon-Blooded, even outcaste lacking the institutional training and logistical support of either the Scarlet Empire or the Seventh Legion, to just fall in line with your plots is likely to result in...a number of complications. They're still Exalted, after all, with larger-than-life goals, agendas, and passions. It's not necessarily an easy sell to convince them to just hang out somewhere and breed rather than pursuing their personal goals, especially if the Lunar isn't offering to help them with those.* On top of the fact that virtually every civilization is "aware" that Lunars are a breed of Anathema, given the spread of Immaculate teachings (even if your society doesn't follow the Immaculate Faith to the extent of the Realm, it's hard to just set aside millennia of stories of heart-drinking demons that wear the skins of men and beasts).

              And I don't think Lunars can just restore the Breeding 5-6 blood purity of the pre-Usurpation time when most (non-PC) outcaste will probably lack Breeding entirely...hell, I don't think even DBs can really replicate that level of blood purity without strict eugenics regimens, a lack of non-DB sexual partners, and infinite time shenanigans. Even then probably not because not everybody will care about someone's grand ole plot especially if it means they have to give up their mortal wife, or are forced to pair up and breed with an opposite-sex DB when they're homosexual, etc.

              Hell, outcastes have good reasons for joining up with the Realm if they're not born there or turned over by their communities (i.e. Paragon and An-Teng at least have a policy of giving discovered DBs to the Realm, at least in part because of Realm policies): they automatically get status, probably training, and likely to get a good job and income. It's a pretty sweet deal for them.

              *I am approaching this question from a narrative perspective, not discussing the implications of 2e social combat convincing them to do so, because that's more interesting to me than having an argument about the bad mechanics associated with infinite brainwashing lasers and UMI.

                                  Originally posted by The Despot View Post
                                  But the Realm can just get them to fall in line with its plots? This is how it works in Core. Lost Eggs are collected all the time from the Threshold and there is no issue there when the Realm does it or Lookshy. I believe Yurgen Kaneko is attributed to having several Lost Eggs within his host. Is it just an issue with Lunars have fingers in that cookie jar? Sidereals have fingers all up in that cookie jar, long fingers reaching all the way down from Heaven. Surely it would be easier for Lunars to mess with all that - what with those Lost Eggs in their back yards?
                                  There are some differences there, between the Realm and other powers fostering Lost Eggs. Not just Lunars. And certainly this is a generalization. Any individual Lost Egg could choose to sign up with a Solar warlord, or a Lunar prince, or whatever, if the individual connections are there. I'm talking generally, why many Lost Eggs would see the Realm as the best possible deal for them.

                                  An outcaste Dragon-Blood taken to the Realm is now among thousands of "her own people." She will attend a prestigious institution with centuries of experience training Dragon-Bloods. She is worshiped as an intercessor between mortals and Heaven. Many laws flat-out do not apply to her, or what would result in severe punishment for a mortal will be a slap on the wrist for her. If she gets through school, she'll either have a good (if entry-level) job and all the benefits that come with it, or become a monk (and be trained to not care about worldly pursuits to the same degree). If she goes to the legions she'll probably be provided with (state own) artifact weapons and armor; if she goes to the Heptagram, she'll come out a sorcerer (probably), etc. And they'll benefit from the training in skills and enlightenment (represented by Charm and Essence training) of other Dragon-Bloods who know how to foster and teach one of their own optimally. However they will probably have to give up a lot of their own personal plots - if you go to the Realm, you probably won't be able to get revenge on that slaver in Harborhead who kidnapped your mother. Eventually you might get back there, and when you do you'll be in a much better position to kick some ass, but that is definitely going to be delayed if you go to the Realm and work your way up in its institutions. And of course, unless you're adopted by a Great House, you'll only ever go so far.

                                  An outcaste Dragon-Blood signing on with a Lunar is not signing on with an institution of Lunars. Her Lunar mentor may have friends who really hate Dragon-Bloods. Her mentor may be in control of lands and armies, but likely will not have support equivalent to the Manses, organizations, and armories of the Blessed Isle. There will be an understanding that if the Realm looks too close, her Lunar mentor will become a Wyld Hunt target. You may be more able to pursue your personal agenda--or you might not, depending on how much you're entangled with your mentor's stuff and how much they need you around; presumably they will try to convince you not to go run off to deal with the aforementioned slaver when they're counting on you as a force multiplier in whatever way. Sure, you may be taught sorcery and esoteric martial arts and may command an army in your mentor's name, but you probably don't have access to the ancient Dragon-Blooded arsenals like the Realm would offer, you probably have no shot at getting a good arranged marriage that gives you financial and political benefits (in the Realm, there's always a chance), etc.

                                  So for a Lunar, or the Bull, or whoever to have Lost Eggs, those Lost Eggs want to be there for some reason. Maybe their mentor saved their life; maybe their mentor was the only parent they knew; maybe their mentor paid them a couple times and they stayed on for consistent money; maybe they're from a remote community and want to stay and protect it because they're invested in it; maybe they lived in a satrapy and have absolutely no interest in working for The Man (figuratively) who's kept a boot on the nation's neck for 500 years, whatever. I'm not saying those aren't perfectly valid reasons to give the bird to the Realm and be off on your own. What I am saying is that when asked "do you want to go to a place where you're treated like a living god," most people--especially people who have lived kinda shitty lives until Exaltation--are going to say "fuck yes!"

                                  This is entirely ignoring the satrapies who flat-out handover newly-Exalted DBs (who are usually teenagers, mind) to the Realm. In those cases, the new Exalt probably has no real way to oppose being taken and indoctrinated. Even having grown up in a satrapy, as a vassal to the Realm, suddenly becoming one of those people who runs the show versus just some peasant...it's awful tempting to go and seize the destiny that the Immaculates have preached you're entitled to as a DB.

                                      The real reason? Because Lunars don't do stuff. Or because they have a pathological hatred of Dragon-Blooded. Or because the Lunars all reverted into animal mentalities and hid. Or they ran into the Wyld and became Fair Folk allies (or enemies, or wardens). The real problem is that Lunars never had a theme, or a firm idea, and the breeding programs is just one idea thrown at the wall that at least stuck a little bit. None of it is terribly well thought out, and most people are just grasping at straws to try to figure out where Lunars fit in a world that was never designed for them (that's where the outsider theme came from).

                                      In reality, a Lunar could do that no problem. At least, with no more problem than with the other breeding programs they have. It is WAY harder to make Beastmen than to make Dragon-Blooded. And why not Dragon-Blooded beastmen? They'd be no less loyal than in the First Age.

                                      Here's the real answer to why they wouldn't have done it: The Realm might have noticed, and then the Realm would have absorbed it, and then the Lunars would have had to run away. If they didn't run away, they would have had a major effect in Creation, and that's just not how they roll anymore.
