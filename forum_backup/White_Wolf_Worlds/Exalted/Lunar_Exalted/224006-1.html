                            Originally posted by Fata-Ku View Post
                            I'm pretty sure that if evocations get shut down in DBT, it'll be because DBT offers something that would be devastating with evocations, or offer something comparable to them power-wise. The devs aren't exactly the types of people to go "let's screw over lunar fans AGAIN! This time with more forethought! Trolololololololol".
                            I think I'll cross-post some speculation of mine from another thread. I feel like there's a lot of potential for fighting styles dependent on a given Lunar's DBT, represented by a tree of Charms with DBT at its root, beyond more general Strength and Dexterity Charms (much as they'd also be applicable. This could also lend itself to another sort of "giant spirit shape with sword" tree of Charms for a style like Sif above.

                            Originally posted by Gaius View Post
                            So, looking at DBT as another form, it stands to reason it, too, would have some minuses to go with its pluses. In EX3, one of those minuses is going to be a lack of Evocation access. It might also help to consider that it may be getting more than just a new minus. Personally, I have a hunch DBT is going to be even more powerful than it's been before. Aquillion had a very good point about one way that might be: in a land without common persistent dice-adders, the physical* Attribute-enhanced DBT-user is all the mightier. But it might extend beyond that. If Evocations are the big deal that they're meant to be and DBT is meant to be balanced against the lack of them, perhaps it could go even further than buffing up Attributes? I could see Charm trees building off of it,** developing powerful effects usable only while in DBT based off the unique synergy of human and beast it allows, much as Evocation arrays draw on the relationship of a wielder and her Artifact.

                            From that perspective, a Lunar wouldn't be able to use Evocations while shifted into DBT, but would have access to something that might be seen as analogous, while still being able to shift back to human form if circumstances change and an Artifact's Evocations are needed. In this way, a Lunar swordsmaster with Evocations unlocked in a daiklave and great proficiency in DBT would be much like a Solar swordsmaster with Evocations unlocked in two daiklaves: neither can use both at once, but can gain clear benefits from being able to switch from one to the other as circumstances demand. It'd also be reasonable that such a hypothetical DBT-rooted Charm tree might be able to go places that Evocations wouldn't.

                            * Sidebar: I wonder if it will still be restricted to Strength, Dexterity and Stamina next time. Perhaps it might be "pick whichever 3 (or whatever) to boost and justify how;" e.g. reaction time may quicken (Wits), as senses heighten (Perception) and the Lunar becomes all the more visually striking (Appearance). Even if the form is generally meant for combat, if there are numerous capabilities across the Lunar Charmset that would be great for battle and would benefit from heightened Attributes, I could see something like this working out. Eh, just musing.

                            ** Fair warning: anyone who says something like, "It's a Knack" will make me laugh at them. We don't know if Knacks will even be a thing in EX3, much less if DBT will be among their number.
                            Edit: or possibly slightly weaker than evocations, because of the "outgrowing it" angle.
                            I don't know. That's not really how I took the "outgrowing it" idea. I interpreted it more as not locking Lunars into going DBT as their one meaningful and obvious route of combat effectiveness, not that all Lunars gradually abandon DBT for Evocations. Besides, we already know Lunars aren't the greatest at Evocations (nor do we know exactly what that means), so options in or building off of DBT could very well be a match for those Evocations that they can access. They'd just presumably go to different places.

                                Originally posted by Foorish Shamurai View Post
                                I think the choice was made probably because DBT is an Evocation on you that grows with native Lunar charms (figuratively and mechanically). Maybe Evocations will have to do with the "human spirit" and Lunars who have assumed their DBT are just enough of a step away from human that they can't invoke the Evocations.
                                That does seem to be where they're going, but I'll just point out again that unless this changes, your DBT is considered your true shape. Meaning it's not a mix of you and something else. It is you. If you're killed, your body reverts to one of your true shapes because it is, truly, what you are. That's the whole point of the distinction between true and false shapes.

                                So, if they want to say you can't use evocations while in your DBT or spirit shape, that's fine, I just hope they come up with some other rationale for saying it. Because "it's not really you" or "you're not entirely yourself" undermines the relationship between Lunars and their spirit shapes in a way I wouldn't like to see.
