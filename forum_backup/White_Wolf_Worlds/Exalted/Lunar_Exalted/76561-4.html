                                                            EDIT: And of course if neither characterizes your position, kindly forgive my cluelessness! Some help getting on the right track would be welcome.
                                                            From the perspective of a young Lunar, Ma-Ha-Suchi and Raksi are most definitely a couple of sick puppies. They are vile monsters and everything points to them being beyond redemption, but...

                                                            1) ... they are not monsters due to the influence of the Neverborn or the Yozi.

                                                            Most evil in Creation can be traced back to the Neverborn or the Yozi. The Deathlords, the Abyssals, and all varieties of Infernals chose their own paths and were granted powers as they were corrupted by dead or twisted Primordials. Unlike the Deathlords, the Abyssals, and the Infernals, no one wanted the mad Lunar elders to become what they now are. Ma-Ha-Suchi and Raksi were humans before they became monsters, but they are not corrupt. No deals were made, no allegiance was pledged, and no power was given. And that is interesting because it is somewhat unique in Creation.

                                                            2) ... they are your elders and you should respect them.

                                                            They are both senior members of the Silver Pact. If you are a Lunar, you are obliged to respect and honor them as your elders. They are very obviously fucked-up, but if you want to be part of the Silver Pact you cannot disregard them entirely and you most certainly cannot just go and kill them. You may feel that it is time to clean up the ranks of the Silver Pact, rooting out the crazies and the monsters, but why now? Why haven't the other Lunars done this already? Why do they tolerate the monsters in their midst? What will they think of you if you try to start cleaning house?

                                                            3) ... they are movers and shakers in Creation.

                                                            They do not exist in a vacuum. They might have things that you could use such as armies or access to magical lore. You could do great things with their resources, but at what cost? Will allying with a monster make you a monster? Furthermore, how will it upset the balance of power if you decide to kill them? Who will take their place? Who will come after you? Maybe you could keep them alive and sort of... aim them at your enemies. Why fight them and the Realm if you can make them fight each other?

                                                            4) ... one is a hero, the other is a child.

                                                            Can you hold it against them? Really, Ma-Ha-Suchi and Raksi are the victims here and rather than destroying them outright, do you not owe to them to at least certify that they are, in fact, beyond redemption? They never chose their current path; rather they are the rape babies of the Usurpation. They are the results of a horrible misdeed done to the Solars and Lunars, and yes, they have some issues, but they are not the enemy. It is not their fault.

                                                            5) ... they kind of have a point.

                                                            Why pull your punches? We call Ma-Ha-Suchi and Raksi monsters, but aren't they just doing what all of us would do if we could? Are these two elders mad or is Creation mad? The Lunars were betrayed, and why shouldn't someone have to pay for it? Maybe we only thin Ma-Ha-Suchi and Raksi are monsters because the Immaculate teachings are so ingrained in us. Maybe they represent what it is to be a Lunar.
