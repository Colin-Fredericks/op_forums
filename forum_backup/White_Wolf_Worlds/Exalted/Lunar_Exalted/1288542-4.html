    I can't believe I didn't have this idea before:

    A Nexus street rat who was lucky enough to be the reincarnation of a First Age shaman-technician and thus had an instinctive understanding of Artifacts and minor wonders she used to become the leader of the most competent cat burglars in the city, only to be targeted as prime slave-agent material by the Guild; she Exalted and escaped as a white-furred rat, reunited with her gang and made them into ratfolk with a new mission - to spread across Creation and gnaw the Guild's heart out!

    If you want to know why I feel ashamed I didn't think of it, I'll give you three hints:

    1) She likes building bases underground and her standard offered mutation package involves digging claws.

    2) She's obsessed with tech and mass-produced devices, especially firewands.

    3) Thanks to Nexus thief's cant, a favorite insult of hers is calling people who are just her marks "things" (as in, "die, Realm-things!").

    The punchline is that she's way more like actual rats than her inspiration. Which is to say, one of the few animals scientifically proven to have altruism.
