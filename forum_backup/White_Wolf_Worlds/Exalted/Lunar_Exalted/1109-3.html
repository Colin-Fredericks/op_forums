                    As I was falling asleep last night a couple ideas popped into my head.

                    Luna as the Horned God Archetype
                    We are told that Luna appears as female just as often as she appears to be male. The 2E Lunars Book presents five of her most common personae: Three female, one male and one intersexed. I also find the one male persona to be feminine in both appearance and demeanour. I think it would be really interesting if one of Luna's persona would be a well-known one, and he would take the form of a wild god of the forests sporting animal horns. Most often this muscular brute would have the horns of a stag, but in cultures further south there'd be depictions of him with ram horns. This isn't Luna masquerading as a male divinity, this is the real deal as well. In this way, one must use various pronouns when speaking broadly of the Silver Celestine.

                    Silver Fire Blood Charm
                    This one came closer to actually falling asleep in something of a dream sequence. In it, I saw a Lunar shed blood in a circle around a Celestial God, then ignite his blood. The circle burned with silver fire and prevented that God from leaving or using any Spirit Charms. In the dream it felt as though the Lunar was going to be interrogating this God for reasons I never got into before the dream faded and I was awake enough to write this down.
