    So, if Lunars can't use MA in animal forms... [divine person forms VS divine beast forms]

    01-24-2015, 01:30 PM
    Lunars can't use Evocations in war form, and cannot use Martial Arts Charms in sufficiently inhuman forms* when the Styles were designed for humans; because animal shapes can't use human Martial Arts even when the animal is really a Lunar.

    [lightly joking] At this point, I'm wondering what forms lock Lunars out of sorcery! [/lightly joking]


    * Technically, Morke and Holden are saying "animal forms", but I figure that Dragon Kings can still practice just about any human-made Style, because there's been no word that DBT locks out martial arts. Tails and digitigrade feet notwithstanding, I think there's more alike between the upright posture of a human and a dragon king than between a human and a gorilla. (Though just as cranes can't practice the human-designed Crane Style, humans definitely have worse odds of ever practicing the Pterok-designed Dagger Wing Style than cranes do.)


    Now, I don't have a problem with this, and I'll explain why in terms of Fluff and Crunch.

    In terms of fluff, humans designed Snake Style to be a range of human movements that took inspiration from snake movements, but which keep in mind that the human has arms and legs and does not slither on the ground. Because a snake does not have arms and legs, a snake is better off fighting like a snake as it always did before, rather than pretending to be a human (with limbs it does not have) pretending to be a snake (with limbs it should not have). Why go through a middleman when the snake is already where it wants to be?

    In terms of crunch, we know that animals are going to have unique-ish abilities that they can unlock with XP. Familiars can learn these tricks under the guidance of Exalted masters and Lunars can learn them after taking the creature's Heart's Blood and assuming its form. These abilities represent a snake mastering fighting like a snake (rather than like a human failing badly at being a snake), and represent a potentially competitive option for Lunars as an alternative to Snake Style. Especially since those snake abilities can be made as strong as they can and should be without any consideration of how they might have been too powerful in combination with Snake Style Charms.


    So, there's no need for panic, and instead, there's cause for celebration. Because this means that those Lunar players who want to invest more into their various animal forms for a stronger Beast Boy fighting style can be assured that they will have the option to do so without needing to master multiple Martial Arts Styles (and design several more for the forms that didn't have any).

    Also, the developers have explicitly not ruled out the possibility of Lunars developing Styles for use in distinctly non-human forms (much as how the Pterok can design a personal Style humans likely can't use). So you could have a Fog Shark Style designed entirely to be used in the form of a shark. It's just that the developers are not going to make such a thing FOR you, because it would be decidedly niche.


    But now that I've laid all that out, I want to explore the question in my mind which quickly arose from this issue. Namely, the forms of gods, elementals and demons.

    Spirits come in many forms, and not all of them humanoid! Many spirits will be much stronger than the mortal humans or beasts they resemble, whether thunder god or thunder bird.

    If a Lunar eats a thunder god and takes his shape, we presume that she can learn to use some of his divine powers by paying XP for them, just as she could do when mastering the tricks of her stolen mortal eagle shape. So when wearing his form, she could use those powers in addition to any Martial Arts she knows (since his form is humanoid), and quite possibly add any Martial Arts he knew by using his stolen memories as a mentor for her self-driven training.

    Now let us suppose she instead eats an elemental with the mind as well as the shape of a beast; the aforementioned thunderbird. It didn't know any Martial Arts, and the Lunar cannot perform any when using its shape. But the Lunar CAN invest XP into two different sources of power -- the elemental beast's animal form (as per the eagle's tricks) and the beast's elemental powers (as per the god's divine powers).

    Also, there's precedent with things like the Kukla and raksha behemoths for Exalted to support animal-minded creatures having higher stats and generally superior physical power compared to sapient characters. It's just "in-genre" for the dumb beast to have more brute power to throw around.

    Thus, even when moving up from mortal to divine forms, I don't see Lunars suffering from "no MA as animals".


    Thoughts?

                          I don't think a Thunderbird Elemental or a Horse God should have the same potential for "Animal Techniques" as the Animals they represent, because they are not that Animal.                           Firstly, the word for a horse-shaped being or object in English is actually hippomorphic (sort of like Japanese matching native for native and borrowed Chinese with borrowed Chinese, you're matching borrowed Greek with borrowed Greek, rather than with borrowed Latin). Secondly, there's no guarantee that any kind of spirit will have this same utility for Lunars. The word on the subject was that animal shapeshifting was going to be an awesome advantage, and giving even bigger boosts for non-animal forms would tend to undermine that. At best, the gods with that shape would get the same bonuses that Lunars would, and it would carry over to Lunars using the form, so that the divine shape would end up like a particularly strong beast.

                          I expect that most Gods will have either a humanoid form or the ability to assume one, and thus shouldn't be bared from Martial Arts unless their divine body is, due to their purview, to frail to preform the techniques.

                          I Expect that most Elementals won't know Martial arts unless they posses Humanoid Form, like the Kings of the Forest, and thus I expect most Elemental forms will be bared from taking martial arts, unless the style was invented or designed for that Elemental.

                          I expect that Demons might be able to use martial arts, but some of them may be restricted to certain styles-a Blood Ape is unlikely to be able to use any style that wasn't designed for a gorilla like shaped body.

                          My preference would be, that if you can use the absorbed memories of a mortal martial arts master to tutor yourself in the style, that you'll be able to tutor yourself in a spirits powers in the same way, and in the animal techniques as well.
                          Charm-share took a major hit in EX3. If Eclipse Solars aren't absorbing everything, I doubt that we're going to be getting anything like the Lunar Knack to use spirit Charms.

                                          Originally posted by Rater202
                                          I'm not saying that Spirit forms should be superior to animal/beast forms, I'm saying that their needs to be some incentive to use them.

                                          If you want to disguise yourself as a god for some reason, you only need one or two Gods in your library. If the taking of the Heartsblood of Spirits is to remain a thing, their must be a reason to use it.
                                          There's a pretty significant potential use you're neglecting: a Lunar isn't always taking on a different form because its stats are better/different or it lends unique physical capabilities. If a Lunar gains the Heart's Blood of a mortal ruler, there's a very good chance the ruler's physical strengths don't factor into the Exalt's reasoning at all. The Lunar's instead turning into that ruler for all the social power that form can wield within that sphere of influence. Even if it's only a very short-lived experience and the character never uses that form again, it's not like that shape cost the Lunar any XP to acquire, and it offered some very serious gains for the time the Exalt cared about it.

                                          A god can occupy a similarly prestigious position, so the same principle applies. In a particular situation, a Lunar may not be interested in "just any god," so much as, say, "Telios, God of the South Wind." It's also probably noteworthy that there's a lot of variety among divinities. I feel that's neglected in trying to coast on some sort of baseline divinity clout from being any old god. Like, a priest-king of a nation that mostly imports its metal equipment and chiefly exports wheat may show some cursory degree of respect to a Lunar if she shows up in the shape of a god of the forge, but there's only so much to expect. He's going to lend much more deference if she looks to be a goddess of the harvest. Naturally, probably still less than if the Lunar makes the effort to become the specific goddess with whom the priest-king communes, but hey. It illustrates that there are upsides to having a wide array of divine shapes in one's possession, regardless of whether any powers come into it.
