                                                            02-17-2019, 02:47 PM
                                                            I think that it’s fitting that the arrangement in alphabetical order should start things off with Appearance, the originally unwanted child of Lunar Charms.


                                                            Heart-Drinking Allure has already been seen in preview, the first of our new sacred hunt Charms, and I’ll reiterate here my preference for it being a bit more strictly conditional to ensure that it doesn’t crowd out the basic hunt. I sincerely hope that a lot of concerns are relieved by the sidebar being explicit that the necessary intimacy does not need to be sexual in nature.

                                                            Ohh, that’s right, every Attribute is getting at least one method, isn’t it? So they go first, and then the rest are divided by broad application. So let’s see what they can do for Influence.

                                                            Glance-Oration Technique, for sophisticated communication through body language that can overcome language barriers. This one primarily interests me just in the territory that it’s extending the umbrella of Appearance over; it’s a bit non-descript, but that’s entirely acceptable in such a low level Charm. It already starts to differentiate a bit more with a follow-up, Argument-Eclipsing Shadow, based on using beauty or menace to disrupt somebody else’s influence, made more pointed with the distant possibility of immediately forming a powerful Tie. And it’s going deeper still with Reflection Gazes Back, where a person trying to get a read on the Lunar becomes powerfully open to being beguiled or threatened by them. What I like about these Charms is that, while some feel roughly analogous to things that Solars might do, they still end up not quite feeling Silver Solar because the descriptions and functions of them actually do feel more like preternatural extrapolations of what description is described as being (form in combination with deliberate poise), which also gets to feel like a low level of precise and deliberate body control (even if elements like actual communication and a person’s failings in a roll making them more susceptible put the Essence into it).

                                                            Okay, different branch from Glance-Oration Technique for Predator-and-Prey Mirror, letting a Lunar alternately project an impression of being somebody socially threatening to the target or somebody that they would feel powerful or in control in the presence of. I feel it’s lending validation to my speculation that Lunars would have a fair few to create phony Intimacies, as it were, and the prey posture does put me in mind of ideas I’ve had in the past about Lunar body control letting them do things like certain portrayals of Superman. What especially interests me in this is the fact of it being a scene-long Charm that lets you shift freely, and the implications that has for a Lunar who goes back and forth during the course of a single encounter. That feels like it can be a distinctly Lunar way of interacting with people; not just leveraged for advantage, but just basic presentation or being. Also nicely draws something from the idea of a mercurial personality.

                                                            Ah, and Glance-Oration has yet another branch to Perfect Symmetry; for years, I’ve been wondering if they were ever going to fulfil that statement about the never realised Lunar errata of Charm trees that would resemble sea urchins. As for this Charm… just the basic (but powerful) extra dot of Appearance, with a nice sideline of making its prerequisite free for the duration. Most I can say about this is liking the image conveyed by the combination of the enhancement creating a base for even more precise body control.

                                                            So, a new root, and our first official Protean Charm with Feral Smile Tactic; double 9s to threaten or instil fear, or use to drain Initiative from somebody who already has such a Tie towards their current shape. What I find interesting about this one is having never considered the possibility for Protean Charms (probably exclusively the social ones) to not merely provide an enhancement or alternative, but to actually capitalise upon the base use. So you’ve got a Charm for making somebody fear you, that you can then continuously apply while in that shape to make efficacious threats to them. What I like about this is not just the way in which it ties animal shapeshifting to a social power, but bringing us into the significance of Lunars having multiple human shapes that a given person might have different relationships to. It’s laying the groundwork for a thing I’ve been thinking of for a while; that such shapes aren’t merely about what standing is attached to the person you got them from, but can be used as the basis for cultivating a base of connections and identity, to be adorned or discarded at will.

                                                            Out of that Charm we get another two paths into Outburst-Provoking Posture and Terrifying Bestial Visage. The former inducing outrage in the Lunar’s cocksure attitude, modelled as intilling certain negative Ties alongside successful influence in general (I’m not entirely clearly, but it seems to work on any witness to the influence, and possibly many at once), as well as penalising their mental and social rolls from how distracted they are; this I like for both the sense of personal image control and being a bit of a troll. The latter functioning both to enhance rolls to threaten and instil fear (with non-Charm dice if in a predatory shape), and inflict the person so affected with a block against initiating hostilities. I’m guessing that with a Charm such as this, the Lunar has the option of assuming the animal shape in the course of the scene to make their threatening action (which it ought to be, shapeshifting should include a capacity to make a point as well as disguise), and still benefits from the block when they resume any other form. It’s a nice bit of paralytic intimidation tactic there, and I particularly like the encouragement to shapeshift in the middle of conversation.

                                                            Now back to something a bit more benign (as well as Appearance covering something a bit Linguistics related), Peacock Quill Flourish… oh, that’s interesting, the idea of making writing so evocative of the memory of a person’s look that they get to apply Appearance bonus in a roll where it normally wouldn’t apply. Leaving aside the mechanical advantage, I think that’s a very interesting way to realise a Lunar being able to manifest power through writing. I am guessing from the sidebar, though, that written stuff isn’t a common channel for Lunar influence; I kind of hope so, for the sake of some stuff concerning the whole thing about Attribute Charms.

                                                            Another base is in Seductive Moonlit Dream, the more positive counterpoint to Feral Smile, doubling 9s for rolls of seduction or instilling things like love and desire, and through Protean potentially improving the Appearance bonus against people with those Ties. What, no love for animal shapes? Way to kink shame, writers. (In all seriousness, for anybody who thought that it was appropriate, it has to be the most trivial house rule)

                                                            Thinking more about the process of distinguishing between the Lunar and a given shape… I’m thinking that if a Lunar is known to be themselves even while in an alternative shape that is recognised as being them (either through familiarity or direct witness), Intimacies apply across all recognised shapes, whereas if a shape is being utilised as a disguise, they only apply towards that identity. It raises an interesting question to me about how to resolve Ties towards a disguise and false identity (not exclusively, but very pertinent to Lunars) if that ruse should be unveiled. I don’t think it’s a rules thing as much as a story one, depending on the demeanour of the tricked person and how much the revealed character can convince that the thing the Tie was towards was their true self underneath the trappings. I think it has potential for interesting and dramatic stories, even without being leveraged into bigger things.

                                                            It also gives me an image of a Lunar who conducts a relationship with somebody who knows them for themselves through a different shape every meeting. Like, even if the fellow isn’t familiar with that shape, the Lunar can introduce themselves through briefly flaring their anima. It would be an interesting and off-putting social demeanour.

                                                            Anyway, carrying on, the old Lunar standby of pheromones is here in Alluring Scent Technique. After a lot of the more heavily mystical stuff, I do actually like something that is a bit more rooted in the physical with wind conditions affecting the range, and the animalistic quality of it being tied to common species. I also think it’s interesting how it doesn’t put full control in the Lunar’s hands, being dependent on pre-existing feelings, so it can’t just be “arouse anybody you meet”; I particularly like the idea of being able to reduce the effect of Minor Ties against Lunars.

                                                            I’m just skipping ahead a bit to check something… right, I’m decided to approach these sub-categories of Attributes the way that I did for Terrestrial Abilities, one entry devoted to each. I still intend to finish each Attribute in a day right now, but not going to pack the entirety of Appearance into a single post.

                                                            Moving on (maybe pick up the pace a bit), Attention-Demanding Presence… ah right, that fun idea of being the centre of attention, drawing all eyes on them to weaken general Perception, and another possibility of instantly forming a Tie if their rolls go poorly. It’s funny how it’s a Mute Charm to make a character dazzle.

                                                            Mysterious Stranger Intimation can build off of the curiosity provoked by pheromones to cultivate further attractive mystery for the sake of improving Guile… oh, it’s a Permanent enhancement, okay. Also extends the range, both spacially and across species barriers, creates a mild, circumstantial compulsion to act on the resulting curiosity in a fitting manner… yes, I like this idea of exuding pheromones as a way for a Lunar to be surrounded in an aura that drags attention along with them. Both in form and function, it feels very much like them.

                                                            And the already previewed Divine Paramour’s Embrace, to give the Lunar even more positive association through being able to bolster and reinforce others, especially their Mate. I wonder… if the Essence minimum is lower here than in the preview. As I said before, the thing I like most about this is the sense of placing the power in the hands of the Lunar, but not being a predatory thing.

                                                            Finally, an Essence 2 Charm; I was starting to wonder if they would all be 1. It’s getting off to a strong start with a name like Devil-Queen Rapture, and it… oh that’s interesting, so it’s a kind of ecstasy arising from their own social success, being able to reap motes back from it. I’d never considered such a thing as a Charm for anybody, although it feels particularly suited to Lunars. The more I think about it, the more I’m fascinated by what it says about the experience of being a Lunar, and how it might shape behaviour.

                                                            Heh, Hunted Stag Majesty letting a Lunar respond to ambush with a flash of beauty or horror to throw them off; it’s a nice idea for a Lunar counter to such a thing, and the sense of mythology behind the name, and I think the mechanical resolution of making an influence roll that can automatically block the ambush if successful is very vivid.

                                                            Irresistible Silver Spirit goes a bit back to the body language stuff with the ability to make seduction attempts stealthily, that’s nice and straightforward. Let’s see, Protean Charm Honeyed Lips Inquiry allows for adding questions that are hard to not answer directly and honestly to highly successful seduction attempts, with an existing Defining Tie to the Lunar removing one of the conditions for resistance. I’m liking this line of giving Lunars social Charms that can layer a few effects atop one another, especially bringing more out of seduction. It’s like a thing of Luna’s seductiveness being a base through which Lunars can accomplish a wider range of tasks.

                                                            Hmm, wonder where Lion-Mouse Inversion can go from Predator-and-Prey Mirror… ah, that’s neat, convey some of that sense to alter people’s perceptions of another person that the Lunar interacts with. Feels like another kind of outsider social shaping thing. I’m not entirely sure what the last line means… can the person being interacted with otherwise be made to see themselves within those boxes?

                                                            Argent Muse Approach is a nice synthesis of some of the previous trends, namely support for another and pulling in an Intimacy from success, conveyed through the general “all eyes on me” thing. I also like the social implications of standing at the side of another, and being so compelling that it makes them look better. Where Kneeling Lion Auspice does something similar, enhancing instil attempts to create positive Ties to somebody the Lunar likes; also my first new Bond Charm, making Decision Points necessary to resist forming the Tie. And then Eye-Catching Appeal goes and lets them basically apply a kind of social Defend Other action to intercept and redirect read intentions actions against allies, an influence that is harder for the reader to resist if they’re already strongly inclined towards the Lunar’s shape. So far, I’m liking the implementation of this thing of Appearance as a means of making the Exalt really hard to ignore.

                                                            Ah, so Myriad Graces Appeal enhances the capacity to improve the Excellency with stunts to… ooh, add that bonus to the Appearance benefit to social influence, that seems very powerful. And Incomparable Paragon Assurance lets them meditate to enhance Appearance? How does that compare to the prior Charm… right, so the prior one was immediate and enhanced another Charm, this one can add yet another dot to the Attribute, and lets Appearance provide extra dice in social rolls where it normally wouldn’t. Nice bit of synergy there.

                                                            Further enhancement to the pheromones is in New Friend Aroma, with a surcharge to change the emotional context for strangers from curiosity to friendliness and a desire to make overtures, helping add a distinct method of fitting in wherever they take root.

                                                            Heh, I like the name of Obsession-Building Prana to go with that effect of instantly resetting failed seduction or threaten rolls and weakening the Intimacy benefits to Resolve a bit, further penalising Resolve if they have a strong Tie to the Lunar. Plus the facet of the unignorable presence of leaving an endurance presence in their minds.

                                                            Starts going in a different direction again with Perfect Fear Scent, weakening Resolve against threats and relevant fear-based Charms, and creating an aura of fear that is difficult to fight or argue through; its Protean benefit of being used reflexively in an appropriate animal shape that wins Join Battle seems particularly useful for a Simple Charm. Then there’s the charmingly named Inchoate Horror Embodiment, to do a bit of a Boggart thing of shifting through myriad shapes until getting at one that is close to their strongest fear… ahh, I see, it’s a threaten roll that will automatically benefit from the character’s strongest fearful Intimacy, without needing to already know it (and refunding a bit for characters that don’t have such a fear). I like the description of how it might go for more abstract fears, as well as the whole Protean notion around the possibility that the Lunar is already the target’s greatest fear.

                                                            So, what does Wasp-and-Termite Dance do…ohh, finally a Totemic Charm. Anyway, this can- ah, right, we’re back into communication stuff, this one letting the Lunar function as a kind of conduit for a whole group of indeterminate size to communicate through such indirect means, although such diffusion makes it easier for outsiders to realise that something is amiss. Oh, and the Solar Bond can serve as an even more powerful channel for communication, letting them speak telepathically over tens of miles, which I think would make for some very nice interpersonal scenes. Obviously to get it as a Charisma Charm, you need the spirit shape of a eusocial organism; thinking on that, I’m struck more with the nice choice of representing the forms of silent communication through things like scent and dance.

                                                            Hmm, what mystical place of mystery is within Essence 3’s Moon Beckons Tide? Ah, to summon people with appropriate positive Ties to their side from a long distance, improving their ability to make such the approach, and with a bit more Appearance lets them call even strangers who fit certain designated parameters. I don’t think it’s too coercive, I think the idea of them having a more dream-like quality is a good fit to Lunars, and I think there can be something alternately profound and really creepy in feeling a certain tugging impulse that brings you upon a Lunar.

                                                            Not far now.

                                                            Resplendent Soul Reflection, a bit more demanding in the prerequisites. Aww, the description of that opening line is nice. Heh, even if the application is a bit sinister, using the enchanted reflection to leverage influence against the enthralled person. In any case, this actually feels like the most witch Lunar Charm so far.

                                                            Totem Beast Embodiment has already been seen, nice way of invoking animal forms. And back to fear with Divine Terror Avatar, spending motes, Willpower and initiative in order to make enemies with Ties of fear (don’t even need to be strong ones) to make them incapable of attacking and drain their initiative if they don’t run away, and even requires a Decision Point to resist it. And it’s scene long and all. I guess Lunars have to be among the more effective exploiters of fear, particularly effective from all the ways they have to improve cultivating it. There have to have been more than a few armies that were beaten just from a solitary Lunar standing in terrifying mien before them (a nice culmination to a campaign of harassing attacks, especially if they go for things like leaving mutilated bodies to be seen), and can probably clear the odd town neighbourhood by making all the regular people flee.

                                                            Heh, the names are starting to become rather elaborate. There’s Visage Great and Terrible, said to impress the image of the Lunar deep in a target’s mind, rendering it more difficult across several fronts to get them to overturn seductive or threatening influence (and it’s Instant, so the Lunar doesn’t bear any burden from doing so). Followed by Passion-Unbinding Bacchanalia (finally getting some of that Dionysian hero vibe I see), to inspire powerful primal urges of lust or fear in large crowds; the fear can be focused on something other than the Lunar with a stunt, and sets people fleeing in a panicked mob that won’t stop until they’re far away, and the lust side… is more subtle and carefully designated, focused on characters losing a sense of decorum, propriety or reservation rather than becoming mindless or predatory, as well as a compulsion to engage in displays to attract amorous attention if it can’t be acquired otherwise. I think that wording covers the problem bases while still having a meaningful impact. Either way, it’s got an unusual basis for resistance, depending on how high a character’s Willpower is, and it’s a very interesting Charm for something, well, more specifically Luna idealised to put out on people.

                                                            Into Essence 4 now, starting with All-Consuming Obsession. Hmm, have any of these actually been Psyche Charms so far… I think I’d have noticed. So, the first Psyche Charm. A Tie of fear or lust is instilled, and the person afflicted with it becomes incapable of effectively processing anything else, making everyday experience feel like a weird dream-like haze. And this damn thing is Instant, the Lunar doesn’t need to commit anything and it can last forever unless resisted. And the only method for that resistance is for the Intimacy to be eroded, which can’t be done without assistance, and even then has powerful barriers against it. This sounds like a very potent method to mess somebody up, for purposes of attack or sabotage.

                                                            Eternal Companion’s Embrace is quite expensive, what does it buy for that cost? Ah jeez, using seduction on a Tied person to inflict an Obsession that not only focuses them on the love of the Lunar, but can gradually wear away their affections towards anybody else, and eventually starts building a Principle of not wanting to share intimacy with anybody besides the Lunar. It makes changing the attitude towards the Lunar more costly and difficult, and that’s only a first step towards the long road to recovering from the Obsession. I’m all for Charms that do some dubious stuff, really messing around with people’s relationships and emotions and general psychology, and even I find this one particularly nasty. It’s good stuff.

                                                            Oh dear, what is Soul-Scarring Monstrosity going to follow it up with? Errr, same general principle as above, but for instilling fear that breeds Paranoia, wears down other positive Ties with the terror that loved ones might just be disguises of the Lunar, and eventually builds a Principle of never feeling safe. Has the same difficult process to escape from. Yeah, this one is kind of worse, ehe hehe {nervous laughter} Nice… job. Hmm.

                                                            Okay, bring it home with the Essence 5 Charms. Beauty to Break All Hearts, commit motes indefinitely to make all characters with strong loving Ties to have penalties to Resolve and increase the necessary Willpower to resist the Lunar’s influence, and weakens their attempts to act contrary to the Lunar’s will, known or speculated upon, and need Decision Points to be able to attack them. Under such an influence, weakening the Tie becomes impossible without particularly firm evidence that the Lunar has personally harmed the subject or something they have a Defining Intimacy to, and it has a Protean effect to have the same power (at the greatly limited scope of being seen by the subject) when the Tie is not to the Lunar’s true identity, but one of their human shapes. I think this one is interesting for having a broad and enduring scope, without completely superseding a lot of prior Charms that are more focused.

                                                            And finally, Devil Body, Terror Face, which has similar effect oriented towards fear, although not an exact mirror. Penalties to Resolve from fear are doubled, characters suffer from nightmares that require a difficult Integrity roll to reclaim Willpower at night, and eroding the Ties require successfully asserting themselves against the Lunar or their projects. The Lunar also gets to use some other fear Charms at premium costs. The Protean effect is about the same, but extend to animal shapes.

                                                            And that’s the Influence Charms. I think they’re a very good showing so far; Appearance is redeemed quite a ways with interesting, distinctive and often powerful Charms (even at lower Essence), and even as a subcategory it’s fairly broad while still keeping on message. It’s actually interesting to see such diverse applications within such a category, effecting emotions, drawing attention, and even communications. I myself have a fondness for there only being one focused on writing, how it doesn’t need to feel out of place but also doesn’t obligate including a whole bunch of other ones (although I suppose there might be in some of the other Attributes). I’m very interested to see the rest a little bit later (looking at the layout, I’m hoping that there’s not as much left to the other categories).

                                                            Aw jeez, that took up 4,000 words? I might need to think of a different way to divide these.
