                                        Dammit, I'm really frustrated by the whole David Attenborough thing, because all of this stuff I'm learning about fauna across the world is absolutely fascinating in the same way that learning about historical and anthropological and economic things is...

                                        But unlike those things, I can't apply any of it to Exalted! Exalted can be a lot of things, but it's not really capable of incorporating the activities of wildlife in any particularly important manner. I doubt there are really any roleplaying games that can, except Chuubo's*.

                                        Yet, I can't help but feel that some of it could be salvaged for Lunars. Not so much with using their shapeshifting to put them into stories of wildlife survival as applying some of the broad concepts evident in the variety and survival strategies of living things as a guiding image of what the Lunar milieu is.

                                        At the very least, I'm attaching to the image that the Lunar presence in the wilderness (to which the politics of the Age of Sorrows has forced them to flee and which they can make themselves at home in by their own nature) resembles some of the activities of animals, as opposed to Solars who seem more likely to follow the form and function of hermits, or even characters like Tarzan. In this way, I can reconcile the idea that Lunars have an advantage, or at least a resonance, with these scenarios that doesn't rub against the fact that Survival is still an Ability Solars can have, with all that that implies.

                                        * You can portray basically any story with Chuubo's.

Like, there are frogs that live in the rainforest that don't give a fuck about the lack of standing water being kind of impractical for amphibians; they just raise tadpoles in the water that accumulates in bell shaped plants, feeding them on their own unfertilised eggs.
