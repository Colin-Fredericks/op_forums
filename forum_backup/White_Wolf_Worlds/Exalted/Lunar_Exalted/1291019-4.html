                Okay, let's say we didn't let Lunars use native charms with Martial Arts.

                That means, for Lunars;
                They can't use an excellency to attack when using the Martial Arts ability (unlike Solars, Dragonblood, etc)
                They can't combo Martial Arts with Evasion-boosting abilities (unlike Solars, Dragonblood, etc)
                They can't combo Martial Arts with Soak-boosting abilities (unlike Solars, Dragonblood, etc)
                They pay 10xp per Martial Arts charm (unlike most of the Solars, Dragonblood, etc, who are taking Martial Arts, and also unlike their native charms)

                As I said, if it is broken, I'm not utterly invested in this set-up. I don't think, generally, Lunar martial artists are better than Solar ones, but I can believe that

                But if the solution is just to take away their ability to use native charms with Martial Arts, then they're going to need some other fixes, or Martial Arts becomes a pretty suboptimal build for Lunars.*

                Otherwise, I'm going to feel like I'm playing with a foot removed for making a different character choice (focusing on Martial Arts with a Lunar), which, as you said, isn't really what you want. All character choices should ideally be balanced and interesting.

                *Someone earlier said that we were trying to make them more powerful than Solars, or at least as good. That's not what I'm talking about. I'm talking about Martial Arts on a Lunar being a competitive build vs Lunar native charms+evocations, or Lunar native charms+shapeshifting, or even Lunar native charms+combat sorcery. I don't think Lunar native charms+Martial Arts is OP compared to Lunar native charms+evocations, or Lunar native charms+shapeshifting.
                But if it is, sure, nerf it a bit... but not too much.

                                        My two obols on the matter: Lunars with MAs are indeed strong but when you invest in MAs in general, your fighting style is usually geared towards that. It means being restrained to the weapons and armor of that style, having charms that help you in its thematics, and so on.

                                        This is more limiting to a Lunar than for a Solar, if you ask me. You'll probably never again fight in animal forms, unless you really need to. DBT is strong alongside MAs, but its activation is a Simple charm so it can disrupt the flow of your actions. What if you can't manage the special activation of your Form charm, for instance?

                                        Your MA is not gonna be good in all situations, either.
                                        • Mantis is really good for restraining single opponents, what with the grappling and disarms and stuff, but it's gonna have a heck of a harder time against multiple enemies, battle groups, and such.
                                        • By contrast, White Reaper's full potential is only displayed fighting against battle groups - a single strong opponent will usually be much tougher than an army.
                                        • Styles dependent on weapons like Righteous Devil and Single Point are pretty strong but offer less combo potential with other styles, and Luna help you if you get disarmed.
                                        • Golden Janissary is amazing at its mission statement: killing creatures of darkness, but outside of that it shines rather less.
                                        • White Veil, Black Claw, Dreaming Pearl Courtesan and Crane are all fairly strong but either require you to be unarmed, or have a social context they're strongest in - you'll have a harder time facing monsters who dont care for social interactions or nonsentient creatures.
                                        You probably get my point. Lunars have a lot of inherent flexibility that makes facing them hard and you become a bit more predictable if you choose to become a martial artist, which I think is a fair trade-off.

                                        I'm not gonna claim that I think I'm absolutely correct or anything, but I do think this is being blown a bit out of proportion.

                                                    Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz View Post
                                                    So, in a sense, the fact that Lunars become powerful martial arts when comboing is a feature, not a bug. It's the only way they can approach Solar competency.
                                                    No, wait, that's no good either, isn't it?

                                                    Part of the problem of "Martial Arts are ridiculously good for Lunars" isn't how it compares to other splats - part of the problem becomes "is it ever reasonable to play a combat-oriented Lunar and not take Martial Arts?" To me, that's a far more serious problem than dick-measuring contests between splats.

                                                    As I understand it, a huge part of the reason why other splats don't get to combine MA with their native charms is because if they could, then absolutely every combat-oriented character would 100% want Martial Arts. Splitting them off ensures that you have to actually specialize in Martial Arts to get full benefit from it, and discourages a Melee character from grabbing one strong early MA charm purely for power reasons (because if you allow that, everyone will do it and MA will lose its unique meaning.)

                                                    I think that line would outright kill Lunar MA since why go spec into something when you don't get mastery and can't favored.
                                                    I feel like that could be solved by more subtle changes. For instance, just choose an Attribute and say "if you favor this, then you can also treat MA charms as favored."
