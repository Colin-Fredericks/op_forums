    Using Lunar Sorcery

    08-15-2017, 07:16 PM
    So, for 1,500 years, the Lunar Exalted were the premier sorcerers in Creation (the Sidereal Exalted are more the premier sorcerers of Yu-Shan who occasionally visited Creation for work). Because they can use Celestial Circle Sorcery, they can potentially create Ambition 3 Solar Workings. If we assume that 20% of the Lunar have Celestial Circle Sorcery, that would mean that there are around 60-80 sorcerers on Creation capable of doing an Ambition 3 Solar Working. If we further assume that 10% of them make one Ambition 3 Solar Working every century, that would mean that Lunar Exalted would have produced 90-120 Ambition 3 Solar Circle Workings, which means that they could have reshaped a fair amount of Creation. What Ambition 3 Solar Workings do you think that the Lunar Exalted have accomplished over the last 1,500 years?

                          As a note, assuming Lunars keep their dice adder from the core book, they can hit solar level dicepools. Assuming a maxed out Sorcerer, the math works out to a Finesse 3 Solar 3 Working taking about two years to accomplish.

                          A Lunar who managed to get 17 Means (which is basically impossible) could theoretically achieve a Finesse 1 Solar 3 Working with just Int 3, Wits 3, Occult 3 and a good level one stunt on each roll, but it would take 85 years to perform (and probably another couple of decades to gather the supplies, so call it a century).

                          So, those should be the two ends of the spectrum as far as time required goes and it should be noted that it's very, very difficult for a Celestial Sorcerer to pull off a Finesse 5 Solar 3 Working, which means that most of these things are not going to be exactly as desired by their creator.

                                              I think that it would be a location based effect (changing the metaphysics of a specific location) which would allow Celestial Sorcerers to treat Solar Circle Spells as Celestial Circle Spells for the purpose of learning and casting them (which would be a good Ambition 2 Solar Working). As long as Raksi is in her fortress of doom, she can learn and cast Solar Circle Spells as Celestial Circle Spells (but she is incapable of casting them when she is outside of her fortress of doom). It would explain why Raksi is reluctant to leave her base of operations, as she would lose access to her Solar Circle Spells when she is outside of its metaphysical borders. I would not suggest allowing them same for Solar Workings, as it would be odd to allow an Ambition 2 Solar Working to make Ambition 3 Solar Workings easier.

                                              On a slightly related note, could a Terrestrial Exalted Sorcerer use an Ambition 2 Solar Working to allow them to learn and use Celestial Circle Spells while they are within their base of operations? It would justify the classic hermit wizard archetype, as the Terrestrial Exalted Sorcerer would be much more powerful in their base of operations that outside of it. You could then end up with hundreds of small areas, probably centered around powerful manses, whose metaphysics allowed practitioners with Terrestrial Circle Sorcery to learn and use Celestial Circle Spells.
