    PEACH - assorted Lunar fixings for 2.5

    12-24-2013, 01:34 AM
    I've been slowly updating Lunar Charms for playability reasons. Welcoming comments and second looks.

    WOLVERINE GAIT (formerly LIGHT-FOOTED MOUNTAIN GOAT STANCE)

    Cost: -- Mins: Dexterity 3, Essence 2; Type: Permanent
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Permanent
    Prerequisite Charms: Graceful Crane Stance
    This is a permanent upgrade to Graceful Crane Stance. The character ignores environmental penalties to movement and actions.

    CAT-FALLING ATTITUDE

    Cost: -- Mins: Dexterity 4, Essence 3; Type: Permanent
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Permanent
    Prerequisite Charms: Graceful Crane Stance
    This is a permanent upgrade to Graceful Crane Stance. Divide the number of yards the character falls by two to determine appropriate damage from falling. The fall does not inflict lethal damage unless the fall covers a distance of at least 50 yards, and the Lunar always lands on her feet.

    MONKEY-LEAP TECHNIQUE

    Cost: -- Mins: Dexterity 4, Essence 3; Type: Permanent
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Permanent
    Prerequisite Charms: Graceful Crane Stance
    This is a permanent upgrade to Graceful Crane Stance.     When using Graceful Crane Stance, the character may take a single jump each turn reflexively, and she doubles the length of all jumps.

    IMPRESSIONS OF STRENGTH

    Cost: 2m Mins: Strength 3, Essence 3; Type: Reflexive (Step 1)
    Keywords: Combo-OK, Fury-OK
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Any Strength Excellency
    Lunars instinctually understand how to do damage, even in situations where normally it would not be possible. Use of this Charm adds one die of post-soak damage and allows damage dice that show 10 to count as two successes. When activated as a part of Relentless Lunar Fury, the Lunar gains the full benefit of this Charm on every attack but must expend motes to access its expansion Charms as normal.

    UNSTOPPABLE LUNAR WOUND

    Cost: -- (+1m) Mins: Strength 4, Essence 4; Type: Permanent
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Permanent
    Prerequisite Charms: Impressions of Strength
    This is a permanent upgrade to Impressions of Strength. Chosen of Luna are capable of inflicting incredible amounts of damage in an instant. For every mote spent, up to (Essence), the Lunar can add three dice of raw damage and a single die of post-soak damage when using Impressions of Strength.

    BIRTH OF FLIGHT TECHNIQUE

    Cost: -- (+1m) Mins: Strength 4, Essence 3; Type: Permanent
    Keywords: Knockback
    Duration: Permanent
    Prerequisite Charms: Impressions of Strength
    This is a permanent upgrade to Impressions of Strength. Stewards are possessed of boundless strength and capable of repelling any foe. By expending a single mote, the Lunar may add (Strength) yards to the knockback effect of their attack. If their attack does not normally provide a knockback effect this activation costs 2m.



    New Charms:

    NINE LIVES UNDERSTANDING

    Cost: 1m Mins: Perception 3, Essence 2; Type: Reflexive (Step 2)
    Keywords: Combo-OK
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: None
    Some Lunars have all the luck. Identical to Reflex Sidestep Technique.

    FELINE INSIGHT PRANA

    Cost: 6m Mins: Perception 4, Essence 3; Type: Simple (Speed 5, DV -2)
    Keywords: Combo-OK
    Duration: One scene
    Prerequisite Charms: Nine Lives Understanding, Eye of the Cat
    The perceptions of cats aren’t limited by mundane distractions, the borders of what might be deemed reality, or the Wyld. As such, a Chosen who learns to emulate the cat’s observational techniques becomes virtually impossible to fool or deceive. A Lunar utilizing Feline Insight Prana adds (Perception) successes in any opposed Awareness check, even if they would not typically be able to discern the presence of the hidden party or object. These successes do not count towards dice caps. This Charm does not allow the Lunar to see through walls or the like.

    WATER OFF A DUCK’S BACK UNDERSTANDING

    Cost: 10m Mins: Stamina 4, Essence 4; Type: Reflexive (Step 7)
    Keywords: Combo-OK, Obvious
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Frenzied Bear Fortification
    While the urge to resist physical attacks with physical force is natural and laudable, some Chosen of Luna learn that the blase disregard practiced by ducks is a better approach to dealing with such transgressions. The Lunar lets the attack roll off their hardened body. This Charm perfectly negates all damage done to the Lunar post-soak by a single attack. This Charm suffers from the following Flaw of Invulnerability: the Lunar cannot attack anyone or anything until their DV resets twice, with the following caveats; the Lunar may counter-attack anyone who attacks them directly, their Defend Other ward, or someone or something for whom they have an Intimacy, including as designated by Charms such as True-to-the-Pack Dedication.

    WISDOM IN THE MOMENT APPROACH

    Cost: -- (+1wp) Mins: Stamina 5, Essence 5; Type: Permanent
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Permanent
    Prerequisite Charms: Water Off A Duck’s Back Understanding, Wound-Mastering Body Evolution
    This is a permanent upgrade for Water Off A Duck’s Back Understanding. For a surcharge of 1wp, the Lunar can activate Water Off A Duck’s Back Understanding during Step 10 of resolution.

Does it make them valid, so you roll and gain successes? Does it make them valid only enough to apply the bonus successes to, but doesn't otherwise allow the Lunar to roll? Is there a default difficulty to finding a thing or person hidden by means of making it invisible (or otherwise imperceptible), or is it treated as visible (or otherwise perceptible) for the purposes of this Charm, (thus most invisible etc things/people would probably be trivial to find unless actually hidden as well)? Making invalid Awareness valid is always weird.

      Water off a Duck's Back Understanding would probably be fine at Essence 3. It's upgrade looks fine too, though I don't even think it needs Wound-Mastering Body Evolution as a prereq.

              Now for something totally different.

              I've been running around my head looking for interesting ways for Lunars to supplement their non-physical excellencies. I've got two.

              The first is a direct expenditure-equals-result equation and as such I'm pretty certain it's balanced. Also there are all kinds of crazy caveats with what constitutes a difficulty and what's not, so, yeah.

              The second is an approximately 11.8% improvement in the probability of success on any one die; in actual rolling practice this can feel like a real boom-or-bust Charm, so I've got it tagged as a permanent upgrade to First Excellency.
              NATURAL (ATTRIBUTE) EXPLOITATION

              Cost: 2m+ Mins: Attribute 4, Essence 4; Type: Reflexive (Step 1 or Step 2)
              Keywords: Combo-OK
              Duration: Instant
              Prerequisite Charms: Second (Attribute) Excellency
              Sometimes you have to cheat to win and Lunars know this best of all. Instead of adding successes to their roll, a Lunar may instead lower the difficulty of the roll by expending 2m per every one fewer success required. This can drop the difficulty to zero and there is no cap on the number of motes that may be spent in conjunction with this Charm. This includes opposed rolls.
              IMPROBABLE (ATTRIBUTE) APTITUDE

              Cost: -- Mins: Attribute 5, Essence 5; Type: Permanent
              Keywords: None
              Duration: Permanent
              Prerequisite Charms: First (Attribute) Excellency
              Cheating is an art, a pursuit, something that must be mastered on both sides to be truly effective. This Charm allows the Lunar to count all successes that come up ‘9’ on a die as two successes for the purposes of determining roll results. This does not apply to damage rolls unless the Lunar is also using a Charm that allows such use.

                  Improbable (Attribute) Aptitude is fine, although the last line is unclear. Do you mean "Lunar is using a Charm that also gives 9s as two successes" or "Lunar is using a Charm that gives 10s as 2 successes, and this Charm will make 9s two successes too". It's also never stated in the Charm that its rolls including the appriopriate Attributes that are enhanced. Technically, you buy this Charm and it'd affect all rolls no matter the Attribute, which I'm pretty sure isn't the intent. Stating that would also make the damage qualifier unnecessary as damage rolls aren't linked to any Attribute (though leaving the qualifier in for clarity's probably not a bad idea).

                  Anyway, it's fairly equivalent to Sidereal's World-Shaping Artistic Vision, though being able to buy it for more than one thing makes it very good. On the other hand, yours is E5 vs WSAV at E3.

                  Natural (Attribute) Exploitation has issues, I think, mostly stemming from being able to reduce a difficulty to zero. First, while its conceivable I don't even know if it's possible to have a difficulty 0 roll. (Corebook doesn't even mention it as far as I can find.) Second, technically difficulty 0 means you'd need zero successes to succeed in it, meaning you don't even need to roll at all (or your roll doesn't matter). What this results in is that you essentially also negate all penalties, internal and external, making the reduction from difficulty 1 to 0 far better than any other reduction. I'd either drop the diff 0 thing, or make it cost a Willpower to drop it to zero.

                  Second is the "This includes opposed rolls." I'm not sure what you mean by it. If you mean "you can reduce the difficulty of opposed rolls but not the effective number of successes your opponent rolled" then it's fine. Opposed rolls do have difficulties (as well as penalties) besides the opposed successes, after all. Different difficulties between the two in contest, even, like pickpocketing: the thief's difficulty can rise independent of the one trying to spot them. Now if you mean "reduce the number of successes the opponent rolled as if it were a difficulty," that's a very bad mechanic as it's effectively an uncapped success adder.

                  There aren't that many crazy caveats with what constitutes a difficulty, are there? I always though it was just unclear if an ST should make a roll harder with increased difficulty or external penalties, since they effectively do the same thing.

                  Anyway, I think a Charm that can so generally reduce difficulty could be problematic. Might be better to have it be a Charm that can generally lower penalties? 1m for internal, 2m for external? Still really good. And probably lower mins.
