        So on the topic of Tiger- Tiger Stylist, the germ of the idea is that Lunar Exalted have a couple of withering damage enhancers and can get that number pretty high.

        Especially since withering damage isn’t capped by dice pool.

        What else has withering damage enhancers...

        Oh yeah, literally the first charm of three or four martial arts!

        So here is the breakdown:

        Deadly Beastman Transformation:
        5m 1wp (I’m including the cost for assuming Hybrid Body Transformation) (Simple Activation)

        Strength Excellency
        5m (for the whole stunted shebang)

        Tiger Form (Tiger)
        10m (Simple Activation)

        Falling Scythe Flash (White Reaper)
        5m

        Striking Cobra Technique (Snake)
        3m

        Elegant Weapon Repertoire (Dreaming Pearl Courtesan)
        3m

        Force of the Mountain (Earth Dragon, but get this when you are Ess 5 or something. Not worth it before then)
        3m

        So to merge all the above into a straight up “Return of the Bad Touch Combo” Attack, you’ll have to use unarmed strike, so no artifact weapons but what you get is....

        A Total of 5 (Base Str) + 5 (Str Excellency) + 5 (Tiger Form if you roll 5 extra successes on the attack roll) + 5 (Falling Scythe Flash w/ 5 Str) + 5 (Striking Cobra Technique w/ 5 Dex) + 4 (Elegant Weapon Repertoire w/ Unarmed) + 5 (DBT) + 7 (Base Damage from Unarmed)= 41 Base damage! (Oh and you had to roll 5 extra successes to get the whole Tiger Form bonus off so it’s necessarily 46, really)

        I mean it’s not a sustainable technique and honestly two simple activations to get there is a real downer, but you can pare it down to just DBT + the supplemental stuff you get something that deals 36 base damage on a hit which, honestly, is impressive enough.

        Thoughts?

        Prototype00

                        Hey Ho, Back to it again. Have been thinking about the Tiger-Tiger Stylist build in the last couple of days, but not going to commit anything to paper. Just using this thread as a notepad for collecting my thoughts, really, if folks don't mind.

                        So we're all aware of the combining potential of Martial Arts (to wit, you can stack these effects from basically numerous different sources altogether in a way you couldn't with Melee and Brawl, say). It has always been my belief that the MA merit was a sunk cost that should be made good on, and thus dabblers in the Martial Arts always were getting the short end of the stick (by dabblers I mean people who pick up one MA as a secondary or tertiary combat ability, I mean I see the value for flavour, but... anyway, thats another thread).

                        But what is a good practice I feel is to max out one style and then pick a couple of low hanging, synergistic fruits from other styles. Low hanging in this case still being expensive enough at 3 ability dots, but that's the price of doing business.

                        Anyway, that's a bit of a look at my philosophy behind this build, but it's also by the by. Here I'm going to just list the "low hanging fruits" culled from the choicest of styles for my reference later on...

                        (Note: For those of you just joining the broadcast, I'm merging together the withering boosters found in about 30% of all MAs with the Lunar Exalted's ability to boost withering via DBT and Str Excellency to reach some impressive base damage numbers, stick around, I find it's nice to get comments and learn from others)

                        Core MAs:

                        Snake

                        Striking Cobra Technique / MA 3/ 3m:
                        - For Dex 5 Exalts, this is a good, discounted deal, as long as you go first (higher init or other tricks like Sinuous Striking Grace for Lunars) - +5 Withering Damage (If you go first and Dex 5)

                        Tiger

                        Overall review: TBH this style is not good to dip, because you need everything up to the Form Charm to work your damage Mojo. Good candidate for full investment and not dipping though.

                        White Reaper

                        Falling Scythe Slash / MA 3 / 5m:
                        - For Str 5 Exalts, this is about as good as the Lunar Str Excellency. No discount, but no conditions either. - +5 Withering damage (If Str 5)

                        Revolving Crescent Defense / MA 3 / 4m 1i:
                        - It's not exactly cheap, but this is a great Onslaught negator (because it actually gives you a bonus to your parry if you are suffering from an onslaught penalty) Certainly something for consideration (most other MAs don't have an onslaught negator this early)

                        Dreaming Pearl Courtesan

                        Elegant Weapon Repertoire / MA 3 / 3m:
                        - Unexpectedly DPC has something for this purpose in Elegant Weapon Repertoire, affordable but might not be as much damage as either WR or Snake if you are using Unarmed to deliver the blow. - +5 Withering damage (if artifact light weapon) or +4 Withering damage (if unarmed)

                        What Fire Has Wrought MAs: Now for a Celestial, going whole hog on an Elemental Dragon style is... not to be recommended. The aura effects will never come into play. But... there are some unexpected gems here...

                        Earth Dragon Style

                        Force of the Mountain / MA 3 / 3m:
                        - Now I know what I said about Auras and it's still true. This is a high Essence purchase (for y'know theoretical games), where it becomes +5 withering damage for 3m, no strings attached. - + (Lower of Ess or Str) to withering damage

                        Wood Dragon Style

                        Eyes of the Wood Dragon / MA 3 / 4m:
                        - Not bad, not bad. If you have max Perception (a pretty nice stat for Lunars) this is a 1m discount on a full damage excellency. - + 5 Withering damage (if you have 5 Perception)

                        Fangs at the Gate MAs: ...unfortunately out of all the styles there, nothing fits this specific purpose. But you do have:

                        Deadly Beastman Transformation / 5m, 1wp (effectively)
                        - Now the strength of DBT is undeniable, +5 Withering, +5 Soak, if 5 Str, but it is Simple Action activation unlike everything else on this list. Still, if you can buff up before combat, accept no substitutes.

                        Strength Excellency / 5m for +5 Withering damage.
                        - Basic bread and butter withering damage booster for Lunars (and only Lunars)

                        Phew, so lets list the effects in terms of mote cost (to determine the sliding scale of what to use when).

                        Striking Cobra 3m / 5 damage (Dex)
                        Elegant Weapon Repertoire 3m / 4 damage (if unarmed) or 5 damage (if artifact)
                        Force of the Mountain 3m / 1 damage (based on Ess until Ess 5)
                        Eyes of the Wood Dragon 4m / 5 damage (Per)
                        Falling Scythe Slash 5m / 5 damage (Str)
                        Strength Excellency 5m / 5 damage
                        DBT 5m 1wp / 5 damage (and other things if 5 Str)
                        Tiger Form 10m / 5 damage (up to Str extra successes doubled)

                        So for an unarmed Lunar MAist with 5 Str you could:

                        Value Option (Striking Cobra and Elegant Weapon Repertoire): +7 (Unarmed) + 5 (Base Str) + 5 (Striking Cobra) + 4 (Elegant Weapon) = 21 base damage for 6m

                        "You're going down" Option (as Value option + Eyes of the Wood Dragon) = 26 base damage for 10m

                        "All In" (as "You're going down" + Falling Scythe Slash + Str Excellency) = 36 base damage for 20m

                        And of course if you do have a round to prepare and can use DBT (or just devote round one to it) all the above values go up by +5.

                        Okay, now to see what I can build around this...
