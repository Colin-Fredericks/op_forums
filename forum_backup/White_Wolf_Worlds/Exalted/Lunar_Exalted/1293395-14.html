                                I was looking throught the books for MA that can be competitive with animal forms and I think I found something (other than Single-Point being Single-Point).

                                So Full Defense as an action has this funny thing where even though a lot of abilties and shield-tag weapons interact with it, it's not an action taken with any particular ability or a particular weapon, so when anything lets you take a full defense action that full defense action applies to defenses with all your abilities no matter the source effect that let you take a full defense. Which is what lets the lunar Crane sorceror flurry full defense with shape sorcery and get counterattacks despite the shield-tag weapon from natural shield mutation not being a crane-compatible weapon.

                                So when Flowing Water Defense (from Water Dragon) lets you flurry an attack with full defense while ignoring the defense penalty, and you're a lunar who can sprout Extra Limbs for only a -1 penalty on your flurried attacks, and get Natural Armor to have decent soak despite being unarmored for Crane Style, you can make regular attacks on your turn in crane-form and keep your counterattacks.

                                As a lunar you also have acces to Many-Armed Warriors Panopoly which lets you attune Razor Claws + Hooked Daiklave at reasonable cost (7m).

                                So if you set it all up you're running around with 4 arms, one pair with Razor Claws and the other pair with your Hooked Daiklave, Crane Form active and using Flowing Water Defense + Golden Tiger Stance every turn, you're sitting at parry 12 (7 base, 1 crane, 2 full defense, 1 GTS, 1 stunt) with no onslaught from successfull parries, and -2 to attacks against you if you're no-moon, while making attacks as normal + counter attacking every attack against you for 7m 2i per round.

                                All in all this is pretty mote-hungry, but it should also be inititive-positive with all the withering attacks you get to make which means you can throw in Theft-of-Essense Method whenever you crash someone with your Razor Claws to get some motes back.
