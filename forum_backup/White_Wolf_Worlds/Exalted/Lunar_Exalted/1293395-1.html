    Y'all will have to pardon me, I'm just using this space for some thinking about the Intimidate trick using the following Latent Ability.

    Predator’s Menace: Animals with an intimidate pool can
    be trained with this attack, causing an intimidated enemy
    to lose Initiative equal to the extra successes over his
    Resolve, even if he pays Willpower to resist. Once per scene.

    So the non legendary sized animals that can use this are:

    Bears
    Boar Tusked Crocodiles
    Death Moa
    Dog/Wolf
    Gorilla
    Great Cat (Intimidating Growl, close enough)
    Hellboar (ditto Intimidating Grunt)

    Okay, well and good, so you can't use the Join Battle trick as per word of Vance, but you can use it as a Reflexive in the first round thanks to:

    Poised Lion Attitude (Cha, 3m) which has a requirement of

    Argent Songbird Voice (Cha, 2m) well and good, as this charm allows you to buy successes with the Excellency and to affect a crowd without the usual -3 penalty.

    Other useful charms are:

    Feral Smile Tactic (App, 3m) which doubles 9s and is a pre-req for

    Terrifyng Beastial Visage (App, 5m) which gives 3 non charm dice (eventually) and if she beats them on Join Battle and uses this, they can't move (? checking) until they spend Willpower or someone else attacks.

    Predator and Prey Mirror (App, 3m) is interesting, would it act as a Tie of Fear (if you activate Predator mode) for the purposes of Feral Smile Tactic? (Allows you to strip an extra point of Initiative from all foes affected.)

    Perfect Symmetry (App, 5m) is of course useful for Pretty Kitty tactics, but we're getting real expensive now.

    Surprisingly

    Beast King Dictates (Cha, 4m) is not all that useful as you want to instill a tie of Fear, not ask for something (persuade, bargain, threaten)

    and

    Charismatic Lunar Trick (Cha, 4m) is strictly worse than Feral Smile Tactic + Argent Songbird if we are planning on blowing a stunted Excellency on buying successes.

    So lets start putting numbers to paper.

    Base Stats and stuff: (5 Cha + 5 Pre + 1 Specialty + 2 Stunt) = 13 base as usual.

    Now for Charms stack on Feral Smile for double 9s and activate Predator and Prey (for an extra initiative that doesn't count as a dice bonus), Activate Terrifying Beastial Visage in Gator form for 3 non charm dice extra and then spend 10 motes on a 5+5 excellency to buy 5 successes.

    So that is 5 (effectively 6) successes plus a 16 dice (double 9) pool for a grand total cost of (3m Poised Lion Attitude + 2m Argent Songbird + 3m Feral Smile Tactic + 3m Predator and Prey Mirror + 5m Terrifying Beastial Visage + 10m Buying successes with Argent Songbird) 26m, (which technically don't all happen in the same instance because Reflexive, otherwise I see anima flare in your immediate future?)

    in order to crash a (if Shikari) say 4-6 Resolve (and I guess you can add on a dice or 2 if you have max Appearance).

    So 6 successes + 8 more (ish, on 16 dice double 9s) is okay? I mean you are blowing basically more than half your mote pool to crash all of them first round if you go for the full combo. Hmm, needs some more consideration... (Note: Charm wise, I am only going for the low hanging fruit, Ess1, not too many pre-reqs)

        Further thinking about this very topic led to some interesting realizations:

        1. Whether the Scenelong charms are considered activated in the same instant or not after join battle is kind of moot. If they are not in the same instant (due to each Reflexive activation being it's own timing) then well and good, blast them through your Peripheral pool. If they are considered in the same instant just use (Inexplicable Mystery Incarnation, App, 2m) and you can Mute the whole darn gang of them (as they all enhance an Influence roll with any Attribute).

        2. Terrifying Beastial Visage is expensive at 5m, and actually all it does is it gives 3 non-charm bonus dice. But I think Predator and Prey Mirror is quite a bargain in this regard, not only does it give you that 1i stripped away bonus, it also penalizes their Resolve against your Intimidate based roll to Instill a fear based effect (since the recognition of a natural predator and fear are aligned influences), so actually it gives you 4 dice effectively (1i is 1 success=2dice, and -1 Resolve is 1 Success=2dice) for the roll.

        3. This trick (intimidate and crash) should be done (as I see it) only in the following circumstances (otherwise it is way too expensive to throw around!):
        - You are facing a small group of skilled foes (like a Wyld Hunt Shikari circle)
        - You are facing a foe with Legendary Size (crashing them with this does not fall afoul of the rules of Legendary Size regarding Withering attacks as it isn't a withering attack, then your buddies can round 1 start decisively hacking away at them)

        You should definitely not use it against:
        - Battlegroups (Static Initiative)
        - Immune to fear enemies (natch)
        - Insignificant foes (Its a big outlay, and if it isn't a significant fight, don't use your nuclear bomb)

        Hmm, I'm actually quite satisfied with how far I've brought this tactic, expensive but crashing up to 4+ dangerous foes or Fakharu round 1 is probably worth it.

                    Great synergy prototype00, but because the Supplemental charms that you are using on the threaten roll are Appearance charms, the roll must use Appearance, not Charisma. I also think that there might be a less nova style of play here. I'll only describe charms you didn't use in your combo.

                    Charms:

                    Quicksilver Second Face (Universal) [pay 2i to reflexively change shape]
                    Unwavering Predator's Eye (Perception 2) [Use perception in place of wits for JB. pay 3m to double 9s on join battle.]
                    Glance Oration Technique (Appearance 2) [communicate in animal form]
                    Perfect Symmetry (Appearance 4) [use Glance Oration Technique free for whole scene. +1 Appearance]
                    Feral Smile Tactic (Appearance 2) [Double 9s on intimidate. If in shape they fear they lose 1i]
                    Predator and Prey Mirror (Appearance 2)
                    Argent Songbird Voice (Charisma 2)
                    Poised Lion Attitude (Charisma 2)
                    Sinuous Striking Grace (Dexterity 2) [move in turn order as if initiative was Dexterity higher]


                    Join Battle 5 Perception + 3 Awareness + 2 Stunt = 10 dice X 0.6 probability = 6 starting initiative [3m]

                    Activate Sinuous Striking Grace 6i + 5 Dex = 11i for turn order, likely go first.

                    Activate Quicksilver Second Face to reflexively become a Boar Tusked Crocodile [2i]

                    Activate Argent Songbird Voice to remove multi-target penalty to social actions [2m]

                    Activate Poised Lion Attitude to create reflexive threaten action [3m]

                    Supplement with Feral Smile Tactic to Double 9s [3m]

                    5 Appearance + 5 Presence + 1 specialty + 2 Stunt = 13 dice x 0.6 probability = 7.8 successes + 2 successes from Argent Songbird Voice = 9.8 successes [4m]

                    (I assumed 2 successes because 4m does cost anima to flare)

                    Miscellaneous action to become a pestle-tail.

                    Total motes spent 22m. No anima flare.

                    Next turn: Regain 5m

                    Activate Perfect Symmetry for 5m (use personal motes to avoid increasing anima)

                    The pestle-tail doesn't lose initiative for taking a full defense, and gains extra soak and hardness for doing so. Full defense can be flurried with social influence, so you can do that every round to convince your opponents that they don't want this fight.

                    Possibly:
                    Activate Predator as Prey Mirror to give opponents fearful intimacy towards your pestle-tail shape. [3m]
                    Feral Smile Tactic each round to strip initiative from all opponents. [3m per round]
