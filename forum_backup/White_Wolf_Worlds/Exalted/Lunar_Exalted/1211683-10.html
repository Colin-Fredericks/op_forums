                                                            Originally posted by TGUEIROS View Post

                                                            I might be mistaken, but I believe Vance himself said that resonance also serves as a way to control who has access to what effect. So if something is Orichalcum resonant, it is a pretty surefire way to guarantee that only Solars have access to it, and therefore create something on a Solar power level.
