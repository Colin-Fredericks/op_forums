    Lunar hustles

    12-11-2016, 07:11 PM
    So... Creation is a sort-of medieval asia... well, that should leave ample room for performers with trained animals!

    And what better way for a lunar to rake in mad money, than to 'perform' using a paid lackey/follower/worshiper/circlemate/whatnot who's in on the scam as the animal handler.

    Just imagine the kind of tricks this could result in.


    I'm imagining a regular big tent act in Nexus, where the animal handler - fabled for his private collection of exotic animals and his skill at training them - attracts a very large paying audience every week, as well as curious investors and others who wish to have their exotic animal trained.

    See a furnace rhino blow smoke rings on command, or a raiton do basic math, or a bear juggling, or a tiger herding sheep without eating them!


    ...all of which of course generated a lot of money for the Lunar who in turn uses that wealth to fund all kinds of fun lunar operations throughout Nexus, the Scavenger Lands and the east in general.

    The animal handler is known as the wisest loremaster on the topic of exotic beasts and their care - so of course your circle is about to seek him out for advice on where to find some rare creature you're looking for.

    thoughts?

    This could be a plot-hook generator, a powerful potential ally, or perhaps a very dangerous enemy in case the circle somehow threatens to reveal the Lunar's scheme.

          Originally posted by Boston123 View Post
          Almost all of the world had some sort of trained animal performance
          ​Indeed; humans all over have trained some kind of animal for one thing or another for as long as humans have had the mental capacity to train, and since that corresponds handily to the time when humans have wanted entertainment, the have likely tended to go together.

          Originally posted by webkilla View Post


          I'm imagining a regular big tent act in Nexus, where the animal handler - fabled for his private collection of exotic animals and his skill at training them - attracts a very large paying audience every week, as well as curious investors and others who wish to have their exotic animal trained.
          ​The thing about the kind of big circus act that is familiar to us from within the last couple hundred years is that it requires a population that is fairly affluent and has a reasonable amount of free time to sustain.

          ​Creation is really going to be home to societies in which that kind of thing is the province of itinerant performers, who need to travel around between various societies during major festivities and public events, in which they're likely to either work on a form of commission, acquire a license to perform in the area during the period, or often busk illegally.

          ​You're not going to make much money setting up on a street corner and performing tricks for the small crowds of commoners on their day off, and the large, populous entertainment venues are unlikely to exist for this kind of thing. Somebody's best bet is having an act novel or renowned enough that they're invited to perform at the courts of royalty or nobility or other major local officials (and even then, that's the kind of money that you're most likely able to use to live it up for a couple of weeks before returning to your natural state of virtual poverty, unless you decide to give up on performing and invest your money in land or a business).

          ​Professional entertainers as people who can make large amounts of money in their own right, rather than off of the patronage or gifts off of some wealthy benefactor who takes a shine to them (and will possibly pressure them for sex), is a relatively recent phenomenon.

          ​Hell, even today, a lot of circuses using performing animals don't really operate the year round or in a single location.

          ​I would see a Lunar being more likely to use this as a method of infiltrating a society (or a high society), or as a cover for their movements in areas closer to Realm control, rather than away of making money.

          ​To be sure, this is a fictional setting in which one can explore cultural directions that did not happen and conceivably could have, so you could possibly have something like animal entertainment having the same draw as chariot races (although that's still a pursuit that requires a surrounding culture that can support the existence of its fanbase), but I can't entirely see it for this particular thing.

          Originally posted by webkilla
          The animal handler is known as the wisest loremaster on the topic of exotic beasts and their care
          I could see somebody incorporating that into their patter, to drum up interest for the annual visit, even if it has about as much credibility as that barber in Sweeney Todd claiming that he has serviced kings and popes.
