                    More seriously, I think it's just that there's a kneejerk reaction on the part of fans whenever "should your favorite splat have this?" comes up to say yes as loud as possible unless it's something that clearly and obviously violates their theme.

                    It's important to remember that whatever charms Lunars lose in the edition change, they'll get new stuff to replace. That's why I said that in my view there's maybe a dozen charms worth keeping, if that -- the others are often not terrible or anything, but I feel that they could probably get better stuff starting mostly from scratch than from trying to revise the old things, and there are very few charms that are so iconic that I'd say "if they lack this charm, they don't feel like Lunars to me!"

                    (Whereas there's, say, a bunch of iconic charms in the Sidereal charmset, which I expect will be adopted in some fashion.)
