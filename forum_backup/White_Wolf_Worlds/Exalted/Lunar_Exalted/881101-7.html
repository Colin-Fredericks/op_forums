            Little people don't interact well with Exalted's 1-5 dot rated traits or the combat system which uses those traits, and entire societies of little people working to build your clocks will get EXACTLY as much mechanical rules-support as a normal city's ability to build your fleet of sail boats.

            Societies of little people are also much easier to accidentally kill. One house fire that kills a human family would destroy a metropolis of little people. Little people would also find it harder to migrate across Creation, which means that fewer environmental disasters are needed to make them extinct.


            As for the Lunar ability to grow large or small, consider how far 3rd Edition is stepping away from the escalation and post-Essence 5 power levels of Second Edition, and ask yourself what would be the REASONABLE limits for how much bigger a Lunar could make any of their forms. Especially when turning into Godzilla can easily be left in the province of "killing and drinking Godzilla".

            There's only so big or little a given form can become before it falls outside of the scale at which it actually WORKS, and the Lunar Exalted of all people are best equipped to assume forms which are actually DESIGNED to operate at any given scale without needing to resort to growing or shrinking.

                                                    For me... it goes back to Charms/Knacks being "tools" rather than "tricks." They're something you have the capacity to do, and then apply in various ways.

                                                    If you have the "turn into a giant mouse" Charm, that's a trick. It does one thing.

                                                    If you have the "turn into a mouse" Charm and the "turn giant" Charm, though, that's a combination of tools that happen to let you become a giant mouse...but also let you do a number of other things. Like become a giant mosquito, or a giant T-Rex, or a giant human, etc.
