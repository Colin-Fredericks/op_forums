    Should Lunars be "the Wyld Exalted"? Their patron has a clear and direct relation to the Wyld, so, if not, why not?

    More specifically, does it make sense that Lunars don't deal with the Wyld at least as well as Solars do? Even the Lunars' scene long Shaping Defence, Laughing Into the Teeth of Madness, is nowhere near as good as Integrity Protecting Prana. In fact, that charm is a late retroactive explanation for the need for Moonsilver Tattoos, which is why it's deliberately flawed in the first place.

    Are Moonsilver Tattoos so vital to the Lunar concept that the writers have to bend over so far backward to justify them as making intentionally flawed charms for them? Must Lunars have the tats? Or can't the tats be given some other function, if their presence really is needed?

        Are Moonsilver Tattoos so vital to the Lunar concept that the writers have to bend over so far backward to justify them as making intentionally flawed charms for them? Must Lunars have the tats? Or can't the tats be given some other function, if their presence really is needed?

        No they should not, because while Luna has a connection to the Wyld, it is one of her many connections, and not her entire portfolio. Being a hypothetical Chosen of the Wyld brings connotations of the Wyld that don't really fit the Lunar gamespace, or just dilute the concept of what Lunars are. Lunars are the Chosen of Luna, their powers are inspired by Luna in the same was Solar charms are inspired by UCS. They can have powers that work with the Wyld like Luna does, but that should not be their focus.

        As for Solars being better shaping defense than Lunars, the UCS burned away at the edges of Creation, enforcing reality and protecting Creation. Luna works with the tide of the Wyld, not defending against it. If anything a Lunar's shaping defense should be a more fluid thing.

        Moonsilver tattoos are a visually distinct characteristic unique to Lunars. They do need to be reworked, perhaps keeping hidden and emerging like the caste-mark, but that kind of design is too distinct and interesting to do away with.

            I really like the tattoos, and definitely think they should stay. It allows reincarnations of Lunars to have different castes, which is something I find interesting*. (Having played in a game where a character died and the circle searched for the next person bearing his exaltation, I find the moment of "Oh shit, the full-moon is a no-moon now" can be cool).

            I like what it says about Sol and about Luna - the UCS deals in absolutes, strict castes that you can never get out of; Luna allows her chosen to define themselves as who they want or need to be and works with the host, making the Caste an emergent pattern rather than an imposed one.

            So I like needing tattoos to define one's caste, I like the casteless, and I like the added benefit of wyld-protection.
            I'm against the idea of needing tattoos to avoid becoming an eldritch abomination because Luna thought it'd be funny.


            *AFAIK the books don't contradict this... if they do, treat it as head-canon.

                        12-19-2014, 01:13 PM
                        Originally posted by Hand-of-Omega View Post
                        Should Lunars be "the Wyld Exalted"? Their patron has a clear and direct relation to the Wyld, so, if not, why not?
                        Because if anyone would be the Wyld Exalted, it would be the Chosen of an Ishvara or some other Creature Of The Wyld. Luna is not a creature of the Wyld; she is something else, which happens to have facility with dealing with the Wyld.


                        More specifically, does it make sense that Lunars don't deal with the Wyld at least as well as Solars do?
                        Yes, though it would also make sense if Lunars could deal with the Wyld more strongly than they have in past Editions.


                        Even the Lunars' scene long Shaping Defence, Laughing Into the Teeth of Madness, is nowhere near as good as Integrity Protecting Prana. In fact, that charm is a late retroactive explanation for the need for Moonsilver Tattoos, which is why it's deliberately flawed in the first place.
                        Lunar design was hobbled in many ways in previous Editions; they didn't know what to do with Lunars, and struggled to make them seem relevant while also explaining why they hadn't actually been relevant.

                        Third Edition is a chance to wipe the slate clean and fix the problems from the foundation upwards. Holden has promised that Lunars will actually feel like an integrated part of the setting now. Pervious Editions needed to rely on the Wyld as a place to shove Lunars out of the picture, and as a leash to keep them from returning to the picture before the Solars returned.

                        That kennel and leash are no longer necessary. The Lunars don't need to be hobbled in regards to the Wyld anymore.

                        In part because the Dragon-Blooded, Sidereals, the Shogunate Wyld Hunt, and the Empress's Wyld Hunt will be very sufficient threats to keep Lunars on the run and hiding.

                        Also because the devs don't need the Wyld to break the Lunar Castes; not when the Lunars themselves are going to break their own Castes as a deliberate step towards creating new Castes with which to wage war against the Terrestrial Host.

                        This time, history is not something done to the Lunars. This time, Lunars are making their own history in defiance of what others would do to them.


                        Are Moonsilver Tattoos so vital to the Lunar concept that the writers have to bend over so far backward to justify them as making intentionally flawed charms for them? Must Lunars have the tats? Or can't the tats be given some other function, if their presence really is needed?
                        The Tattoos are important, but they were given bad reasons to be important, and a bad backstory. Lunars can still have Tattoos, but with a proper backstory and good reasons for using them in the present.


                        Originally posted by Fata-Ku View Post
                        I really like the tattoos, and definitely think they should stay. It allows reincarnations of Lunars to have different castes, which is something I find interesting*. *AFAIK the books don't contradict this... if they do, treat it as head-canon.
                        It's not made very explicit, but yes, Lunars can have different Castes with each incarnation.

                        That was never because of the Tattoos, though. In previous Editions, Lunars always started Casteless before settling into the Caste which best fit their current incarnation. Tattoos only later became necessary as a way to keep that one Caste.


                        I'm against the idea of needing tattoos to avoid becoming an eldritch abomination because Luna thought it'd be funny.
                        It was never because of that, either. But it's also not going to be a thing in the next edition.

                        formerly Tornado Wolf, formerly Inugami

                                                    Honestly I think that Lunars would still use tattoo artifacts pretty commonly even if they weren't necessary to preserving their sanity, because artifacts in your skin are very practical for shapeshifters. A daiklaive or super heavy plate quickly lose utility when you change size, or add/lose appendages.
