                                    Originally posted by Jefepato View Post

                                    What's so powerful about Spinnerets? It's cool, but it doesn't do much that you couldn't do with mundane equipment, aside from letting you use Stamina and Craft in feats of strength (doesn't seem like a major issue).
                                    Take a look at this Dragon-Blooded Charm:
                                    Grandmother Oak Exertion
                                    Cost: 3m; Mins: Athletics 4, Essence 1
                                    Type: Supplemental
                                    Keywords: Wood
                                    Duration: Instant
                                    Prerequisite Charms: Strength of Stone Technique
                                    The Dragon-Blood’s strength is inexorable, finding points of leverage like an ancient tree digging roots into stone. She adds a bonus success on a feat of strength, and may use her Stamina in place of her Strength rating to determine if she qualifies to attempt it. If she has Strength of Stone Technique active, she counts the dot of Strength it grants as a dot of Stamina for the purposes of the feat.
                                    In Wood Aura, this doesn’t count as a Charm bonus.
                                    An Athletics 5, Essence 4 repurchase lets the Dragon-Blood spend one Willpower to add her Stamina to her Strength to determine if she may attempt a feat, rather than substituting it.
                                    Spinnerets does what this Charm does, but also allows you to skip out on Athletics, gives you a non-Charm bonus to various other actions, and grants an innate Thrown weapon that has more tags than you can get with the Natural Missile mutation.

                                    This for 3 BP (or motes, if you're using Perfected Hybrid Interaction).
