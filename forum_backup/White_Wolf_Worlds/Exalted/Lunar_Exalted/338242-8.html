    Originally posted by Zelbinnean View Post
    Leaving aside how shitty it is for a Celestial Exaltation to be warped to high levels of functionality loss by the Wyld, there's no reason why each individual story writ on each Lunar's skin should appear the same. If anything, that makes no sense at all to me.
    I'm guessing that in 3rd Edition, the Lunars themselves broke their Castes deliberately, for the specific purpose of creating new Castes better suited to their unenviable position in the Second Age. This might not have even been the first time they did this -- I think the devs have hinted or outright stated the the Lunars of the First Age eventually abandoned the Castes they needed for the Primordial War and adopted Castes suited to the relative peace and growing indolence of the Old Realm.

    Which is a much more awesome way to "build heat" and sell "we are amazing survivors!" than "we were chased into a metaphorical pool of acid, didn't realize what the acid would do to us, just barely managed to figure out a way to survive the acid, and now live permanently crippled by the acid."
