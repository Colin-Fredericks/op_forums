    Is the Caul singular or an example of what a Lunar domain might be like?

    04-28-2016, 10:45 PM
    So, Lookshy sort of started as an example of what a Dragon-Blooded-run state outside the Realm might look like, and morphed into the only Dragon-Blooded-run state that existed (well, besides Cherak, but nobody paid attention to Cherak).

    Tepet Ejava sort of started as an example of what a claimant to the Empress' throne might look like, and morphed into the only claimant besides Little Pink.

    Which is the Caul? Is it a sample of the sort of weird, separated-from-the-laws-of-Creation, inhospitable military quagmire separated from Fate that one would just generally expect of a homebrewed, player-run Lunar dominion, or is it a singular achievement that is the sole instance of Lunar infrastructure in the face of Realm hostility? Is it the biggest example of its kind (and then maybe other Lunars have attempted to export its success, to varying degrees of efficacy), or the only one?

    I guess the real million-dinar question is, if my Solar accidentally blows it up, can they get another one?

                                  The Caul seems an atypical Dominion for a number of reasons. One, the concentration of Lunar activity. Most dominions seem much more individual/small group projects. Two, the religious dimension. The Caul is sacred to both Lunars and Dragon-Blooded. Its conflict isn't JUST about tearing at the Realm (though that's, of course, a huge, huge part of it, given its strategic value), it's also regaining the land where Luna once walked. Three, the... presence of His Divine Lunar Presence. And of course, four, there is the whole "has it's own Loom of Fate and is outside the Sidereals' control" thing.

                                                  Originally posted by Zeesun View Post

                                                  I'd enjoy hearing your reasoning on this one. I don't necessarily disagree (the West is only going to become more important, economically, moving forward assuming a New World model of relative economic impact), but I am curious!
                                                  Aum-Ashatra managed to wrap his hands around one of the North's major caravan routes. It's the North, so sailing past the road is an iffy proposition at the best of times, and the other, smaller roads are likely to be filled with your choice of barbarians, Fair Folk, undead, or any other manner of horror that you probably don't want to think about. If he wants to wreck the economy of everything west of Cherak and affect the course of the Realm Civil War in the northern satrapies, he can probably find some way to do it. The Black Shale Road is more likely to carry luxury goods than essentials, but the Great Houses and the Guild are going to have to make up for their losses somehow. On top of that, every caravan he assaults and seizes gives him more resources he can use to back other Lunars' barbarian hordes. That would give him a tremendous amount of political power and influence among other Lunars. If he wants to step out of his caves and wage a serious war against the satrapies, shit is going to go down.
