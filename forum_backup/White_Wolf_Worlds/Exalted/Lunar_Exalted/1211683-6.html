    Originally posted by wastevens View Post
    Chapter One: The Lunar Exalted

    * History of the Lunars
    *** Specific focus on the role of Lunars in the Age of Dreams, response to the Usurpation, and life since the Contagion and the rise of the Scarlet Empress
    *** (Sidebar: Solar and Lunar relationships in the Age of Dreams. Touch on origin of the Solar bond. Most were primarily political arrangements, seen as especially auspicious; several with matches founded on love, or at least amicible friendship. A few were abusive, but those were profound outliers)
    I'd imagine that any information on Lunars re: the First Age is going to be very brief. That time is gone, it's not coming back, and the Chosen of Luna aren't what they were then. Related to this...

    * Chosen by Luna
    *** The qualities most commonly found amongst those Chosen by Luna, and the experience of Lunar Exaltation
    *** (Sidebar: The essential humanity of beastmen)

    * Broken Castes
    *** Touch on the breaking of the castes between the Usurpation and the Contagion
    *** Description of the Castes, their roles and tendencies, common totems
    *** (Sidebar: The original five castes)
    I doubt that we'll get much word count devoted to the former castes, either. Especially since in this case, the "original five" weren't actually the original castes that the Lunars had when they were first around; they changed after the Divine Revolution was over. The present set of castes is going to be the third such division, not the second.

    * Essence Fever
    *** Animalistic insticts, a need to tear down the status quo, extraordinary intolerance of anyone or anything acting against her intimacies
    "Animalistic instincts" is at once an overly broad and misleadingly attributive description of behavior. One thing we might see, which 1e touched upon, is that everyone becomes a lot less squeamish about hunting and acquiring forms. Maybe they'll also "toughen up" in a manner similar to Akira Fudo after absorbing and overcoming Amon at the beginning of most iterations of Devilman, but that's even more of a personal interpretation on my part.

                Originally posted by wastevens View Post
                *** (Sidebar: The original five castes)
                No. That's all dead a millennium, and should be left dead, especially as it suggests modern Lunars are broken and a patch job.

                Instead, I'd explicitly call the creation of the current castes something like the "Third Remaking", suggesting that the Lunars are on their fourth set of castes since their creation. When Creation next changes, the Lunars will of course change to match it . . . but it's never going to be a matter of "restoring" one of their many past sets of castes, because Creation's never going back to the First Age, it's becoming something new. I'd then slip in a statement that Lunars had seven castes or something as of the Usurpation, just to cut off perpetration of the old canon "these are the now-missing castes" stuff completely.

                    What are people's thoughts about the possibility of an ancient, noble-class warstrider being included in the Lunar book? (Not royal-class like the Walking Devil Tower)
