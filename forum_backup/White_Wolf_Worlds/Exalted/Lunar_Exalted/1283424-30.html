        Speaking personally another thing against the Caul is really just that it is eating word count in a already weak chapter. Like the Core book presented Dominions as the 10 000 fangs of the silver pact that encircle and harry the realm and almost none of them live up to that ideal.

        1. The spider king is at "war" with the realm but is effectively just a bandit whose most pressing problems are food shortages and managing immigration.
        2. Skhandar-Bal is not at war with the realm but maybe in like 50 years? who knows.
        3. The Touman clans are also not at war with the realm but like any day now they might be I swear.
        4. Iscomay is basically just the realm lite because it turns out lunars are not so good at this whole people management thing and ended up losing control completely. So four dominions in and Lunars have managed to unintentionally create more imperialist powers than they have hindered.
        5. Ma ha suchi is not at war with the realm because he is tired of getting his ass kicked and so sits and sulks.
        6. The bronze tide did not go to war with the realm and in fact never will since that would take away too much time from being GOT wildlings running from the white walkers.
        7. The caul is at war with the realm but as stated before there is nothing interesting to do there so I would basically have to create an entirely new dominion and then slap the word caul at the top.
        8. Mahalanka, luthe and the eskari are at war with the realm but this is still only 3 out of 10 spotlighted dominions. Two of which are only important because they have a 1000's year old first age elder there.

        Like one of the strengths of WFHW was that it really hammered home the idea that the empire is full of interesting NPC's doing their own things but it was still a time of tumult and a bunch of young dynasts could make a difference one way or another.

        The dominions chapters should of been something I could point players to as examples of cool things lunars can do but it is filled with stuff they either can't do because they are not E6 elders with a 1000 years or prep or stuff they don't want to do because it would mean being a weird ineffectual uncle trying to convince random people its in their best interest to throw themselves against the imperial meat grinder.
        I'm going to point out that one criticism of the Silver Pact chapter was that it seemed like Lunars are utterly obsessed with fighting the Realm. Realm, Realm, Realm, that's all it seemed to be.

        (And, in fact, the very first two words of the Dominion chapter are "The Realm's")

        So the Dominions chapter presents a number of Lunar-influenced societies which present a number of different models of interaction with the Realm - war, piracy, raiding, blocking, thinking about war, or completely ignoring it.

        If your main objective for the Dominions chapter is "How does it show the Lunars awesomely fighting the Realm?" then, yeah, it's actually not that great.

        But I actually wasn't that interested in that, and I really liked it.

        On the other hand, I didn't like the Silver Pact chapter as I found an entire chapter of fighting the Realm... not bad, per-se, but not that exciting. Whereas I guess you preferred it?

                                                            The Silver Pact ...really isn't particularly cohesive. It's a group of 400ish people, most of whom generally agree on two things:

                                                            1) The Realm and Sidereals need to go down!

                                                            2) We shouldn't kill each other and, by extension, we shouldn't let the Realm kill us.

                                                            Everything else is up for debate, petty politicking, and so on.

                                                            And... I guess I feel like that comes across fairly well here to me. Each place has a Lunar who is doing their own thing and might be inclined to help with your thing, but they could use a favor or two in return or some persuading or whatever.


                                                            Here, let's see what we can see as regards each one dealing with the Realm, if you're playing as Pact Lunars:

                                                            1) Mountain of the Spider King: a Lunar ruler uses his well defended home to deny a valuable trade route to the Realm and help others. However, the help he freely gives can get stretched at times, so if you could find a way to increase their food supply, they would likely be inclined to help you out. (Maybe you could get True Voice to help him out with some geomancy for the right price?). There's also a nearby Realm outpost that's trying to keep the sea trade route open. If you can break that place in half, that will end Realm trade in the area for years. Or maybe just help fund the pirates.

                                                            2) Skandhar-Bhal: Not!Shangi-la where an ancient Lunar retired from the world, sickened by violence. However, said Lunar has been training a formidable fighting force ...but one that has not seen any actual fighting beyond what others would consider a bar rroom brawl or minor skirmish. The Lunar considers coming back to the world and bringing these troops to war, but are they really ready? Can you help them become ready? Should you convince them to stay where they are?

                                                            3) The Touman Clans. This place has a proud tradition of raiding others but some want to remain in this very nice city they've claimed and start building an empire. Their Lunar cohort is thinking about trying to get them to go on the road again with her and some of the people would like it. Can you tip the balance one way or another?

                                                            4) The Empire of the Bear: I built this place to fight the Realm and now they don't want to. So, anyone up for toppling a small government before we topple the big one? But, really, helping Truce Voice retake control of this empire and send it against the Realm is going to be a job and a half. Alternately reforming it to be less evil and/or unlikely to implode due to Janissary revolts in a few decades is a thing you could try that might be more doable? Maybe you could take part of the army and go conquer the nearest Satrapy so you can lower taxes or something.

                                                            5) Nameless Lair: This place is why the Realm and Prasad are two different nations instead of one bigger one. They also lead irregular raids on outsiders and Realm Satrapies, all the more troubling for their apparent randomness. And Ma-Ha-Suchi is there to be negotiated with, approached for resources, and so on.

                                                            6) Mahalanka. It's Raksi. She is a source of knowledge and power. Her empire is strong. When she thinks the time is right, she will pour over the Realm's eastern satrapies like a wave. Until then, none would dare attack her.

                                                            7) The Eskari are a nomadic people that a Lunar is turning into a horde of raiders who will attack the Realm's southern trade and satrapies. Pretty straightforward. You can get involved in planning this or try to get them to target a particular place.

                                                            8) The Bronze Tide, if one could treat with Lukha Palash and his council, might be turned away from their conquering of the Isles and put towards the destruction of Realm fleets or the besieging of Faxai. There is likely much they would do if you could present them the broken heart of of Bhadri. With some cleverness one might even use the Tide to turn the Spear-Empress against another hated enemy and let two problems solve each other.

                                                            9) Luthe. Leviathan has a nice city here.and with his new motivation, it's going on a war footing. There will be lots of issues to iron out along the way that one can help with or one can just ride along on raids against Realm shipping.

                                                            10) The Caul. We'll be getting a full chapter as a how to book for doing this later on, but, now, let's start with murdering every non-native who sets foot outside Faxai, infiltrating the city to find out more about the local DBs, air dropping firebombs, cutting off shipping, and so on.

                                                            So, yeah, I see plenty there for stuff to work with for going against the Realm. It may not all be perfectly obvious, but the Realm is present in most of them, in one way or another. They're also all interesting places to be from and each has some kind of crisis that could be meddled with.

                                                            I think they all work just fine.
