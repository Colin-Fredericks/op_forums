                So first and foremost, remember target one for a Wyld Hunt is a new Lunar ideally before they've gotten any training. Doesn't matter if between shapeshifting, sorcery, and necromancy a Lunar could become literally impossible to pin down;* a new Lunar doesn't know most of their own tricks and can't apply them as well. So you hunt the new bloods first since you catch them before anyone's explained and well the guy turned into a mammoth, but if that's all they can do that's not too much of a problem compared to worrying about the dude seagulling it to the ocean or a river and fleeing at the speed of angry fish. Also remember no plan is 100%, your best odds of killing a Lunar involve finding them while they're asleep and decapitating them, barring that amount of great fortune one has to accept a bit more chance.

                So sorcery is also a fun trick to employ for countering lunars. The devil maw creates a glorious suction of all water in an area around it, so smaller escape animals are potentially dying like mad ,if you're lucky one turns back into a lunar, and if it tries to burrow out then you conveniently have a lot of liquid to flood their escape hole with. This is also not counting demons or elementals that might be able to track and hunt the lunar in other ways.

                Now your primary tactic is gonna be overwhelming force and surprise. Not just ambushes just stuff they didn't expect. Remember a Lunar isn't necessarily going to cut and run as soon as there are signs of a Hunt. After all killing, crippling, and/or maiming all the members is going to be a moral damager not counting any and all resources as a result of those individuals.Of course this means actually having to engage the Wyld Hunt and if they aren't expecting it getting caught by someone casting Mists of the Eventide is gonna be rough and the Lunar wouldn't have been able to plan for that if they didn't know, the Hunt could have. Overwhelming force is what it sounds like, a shapeshifting bug out plan doesn't help much if you've got a half a dozen people on you at that exact moment to strike at your new beast form, or just have to contend with a mixture of DB anima banners in full force.




                *Exageration for the sake of making a point.
