    Lunar Exalted and the power of being small

    05-27-2017, 03:49 PM
    SHAPESHIFTING (excerpts)

    A Lunar may pay four motes to assume any form it has taken. This transformation lasts indefinitely, until the Lunar reverts to his true human form (which is free and reflexive) or shifts into a different shape. Shapeshifting can be flurried, but not with an attack.

    Animals with Legendary Size are beyond the skill of most young Lunar shapeshifters, though this limitation can be overcome.


    DEXTERITY CHARMS (ideas)

    Humble Mouse Shape
    This presumes that the power to don the form of a creature with the Tiny Creature merit is initially locked, like Legendary Size. As a reminder, this merit belongs to most creatures at least as small as a house-cat, granting them +2 Evasion against larger foes. Larger characters must also subtract two successes from any Awareness-based roll made to notice a Tiny Creature.

    Rat's Head Technique
    Essentially a mirror of Solar "Door-Evading Technique", but it requires the Lunar to be at least as small as a mouse. The Lunar twists and shrinks as much as necessary to squeeze through any barred passage. Dealing with locks as per "Lock-Opening Touch" is an entirely separate matter that Lunars will have to approach from a different direction.

    Stealthy Fox Method
    If the Lunar attempts to go to ground in combat in the form of a Tiny Creature, when all his opponents are larger than house-cats, the penalty for doing so begins at -0 instead of -3.

    Traceless Passage Technique
    While in a form with the Tiny Creature merit, the Lunar ignores the -3 penalty to Stealth rolls for moving while concealed. Alternatively, she can apply this Charm to a single Survival roll to conceal her tracks, gaining (Essence) automatic successes and is bolstered by the double 9s rule. The Lunar cannot be tracked conventionally, not even with the aid of tracking animals or other means—mundane attempts automatically fail. Only those with supernatural prowess can hope to find the vanished Lunar.

    Emerald Grasshopper Form
    The Lunar can reflexively assume any form at least as small as a house-cat, potentially gaining the benefits of Tiny Creature's Evasion bonus against an attack after it has been declared.

    Becoming the Swarm
    The Lunar subdivides his body into a swarm of lesser creatures, or multiplies from one small creature into many. These creatures individually may be no larger than a house-cat. The Lunar is free to mix and match species in the swarm, but if he wishes to use unconventional forms of locomotion (such as flight or crawling up walls) all creatures comprising the swarm must be capable of such movement. The Lunar adds half of the Dexterity rating (rounded up) of the swarm's primary species to his Evasion against attacks that are not large enough to encompass the entire range band. This bonus counts as non-Charm dice and can stack with the bonus of Tiny Creature.

    Becoming the Swarm is explicitly allowed to combine with Ant and Starfish Trick (mirror of Solar "Faster Than Self Technique"), allowing the Lunar to become a swarm large enough to constitute a battle group.

    Agitation of the Swarm
    The Lunar supplements a Distraction gambit with his swarm-form, using his dispersed mass and irritating assaults to conceal the approaching attack of an ally.

    Swinging At Flies
    Upon successfully dodging an attack with an Evasion Defense that employed the Tiny Creature merit, the Lunar can retroactively and reflexively activate Becoming the Swarm. She dodged the attack via becoming too numerous as well as too small to strike.


    Dancing Beneath Giant's Feet
    A dice-trick Charm that improves upon the Evasion bonus of the Tiny Creature merit, or alternatively allows the Lunar to enjoy similar bonuses against a creature of Legendary Size.

    Wasp Sting Blur
    A dice-trick or counter-attack Charm that the Lunar can use against an opponent who failed to strike him thanks to Dancing Beneath Giant's Feet.

    Climbing the Giant
    A special grappling maneuver which leaves a larger victim completely unhindered versus anyone else, but heavily penalizes his attacks against the clinging Lunar, while also making it effectively impossible to Disengage from the Lunar.

    Giant Swats His Face
    The Lunar activates this Charm in response to a failed attack from the victim of "Climbing the Giant", causing the victim to hurt himself instead.

    Swallow A Spider
    The Lunar invades the body of a victim of "Climbing the Giant", entering through a nostril or mouth or some other orifice. A different and much more destructive form of grapple, with the Slam maneuver representing the Lunar shapeshifting into a larger form to burst out.

                  I think I misremembered the spell is terrestrial and remember I'm more of a first edition aficionado

                  Basically it's rather inefficient and compared to later movement charms not really that good. It causes your body to split into a flock of birds or raitons and transports you some distance away.

                  I understand the appeal of making that an actual maneuver that shapeshifters can do, at the very least being able to pull some mr. fantastic style dodges.


                  Making the charm or relatively small charm tree into a collection of abilities that at the same time create as much complications as they do advantages strikes me as a great trade-off to make the swarm charms distinct from the spell.

                  With a spell you do become Birds but they don't really matter it's just a Teleport effect with some really cool decals. With Swarm charms you are actually creating a mass amount of life, but you're creating a mass amount of life. Sure that's an advantage for the magical lunar Trickster totem warrior, but life is always complicated and demanding and costly.

                  You know I would actually disagree on the idea that you need to put a charm cost on the stop hitting yourself or the orifice entrance. I'd assume that defending against such is exceptionally easy.Reflexive bites, or clinching of orifices to block holes and create more contested rolls. The inherit drawback after all is that you can't do this while you're in your 16 Strength 18 armed & dexterity build but in the form of a mouse or a spider. You are trading a grapple for pinching. Giving yourself and your opponent Mobility and losing hardiness. Your tiny form will naturally have very low defense, be very vulnerable and you are actually sacrificing your difficulty to be hit by directly touching someone. A terrestrial exalted can easily kill you just by contact. Heck even a solar could probably stunt a blinding or sensory overload effect just by flaring their anima. And the most common basic defense of wear clothing/coverage. Let's not even get into what a lunar or Fair folk could excrete or turn their skin into.

                  Moreover what's the stop someone that you're trying to jump down the mouth of from vomiting you out hacking you out or actually eating you. Or drowning you in some thick poison that they're currently immune to or just a lot of highproof wine that hurts you thanks to your low body weight?
                  Of course a lunar will be able to compensate for this by having their own soak or dexterity or whatever charms. But that strikes me as no worse than someone investing in a particular martial arts style. (again I don't understand Third Edition.)

                  You can't just throw on some tiger claws and go, "I use tiger style to defeat my enemies." you usually have to invest in the right attributes, get a large amount of the charm tree and have supplementary natural charms to make sure that you are exceptionally good at a very specific version of fighting along with the right artifacts.
                  I don't think its too threatening that a setting where lunars can do this means that, for me, it's something that inhabitants have to think about and consider when they're fighting or, more accurately, when they're living their lives. Also solar's already have a charm to deal with invasive pests three of them technically hardship surviving mendicant spirit, immunity to everything, and hypnotic tongue technique after all if you were touching them

                  Oh well I guess it really does depend on aims and effectiveness.
                  I think making it so that something is more inherent in the actions that are taken is better than gating them behind charms.
                  This prevents something were New charm spaces and Technologies develop in later material that open up a line that you need to be competitive in in order to never fall behind. But your charmset wasn't designed for it. In first edition sometimes this was the leaping dodges and midbattle feints or tricks that charms about those actions were made to not reliably work in battle. Another example of necessary post established charm tech would be the targeting or crippling charms that became apart of martial arts and then later Abyssals trees. Apparently learning how to disable your opponent by doing damage to a limb was something that only the darkest murderers could get by selling their souls to monsters from beneath the void. 🙄::rolleyes::

                  Making it so that you have a system for what happens when a small fighter takes on an exceptionally larger fighter means that you not only give a system for when Lunars try to become Mighty Mouse or Reepicheep. Or... (I think I'm forgetting some heroic bug folk hero or something...)
                  But it also prepares all Exalted for when they eventually get into the type of battles where it's relevant. This is likely to happen as little guy versus giant monster is starting to become a lot more of a fad thanks to Dark Souls, Attack on Titan, god of war and so on.

                  What are peculiar situations that seem unique to Lunar Exalted may very well end up situations that any Exalted could benefit from having firm system approach in.
