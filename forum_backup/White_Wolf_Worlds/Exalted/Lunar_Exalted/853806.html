    So the Lunars are probably as well aware as the Sidereals of the returning Solar Exalted, but I don't recall them organising any effort to recruit and train impressionable, just Exalted Solars.

    Am I just misremembering or is there an in lore reason they are ignoring this potent resource?

      Lack of access has always been a big thing. In past eds, the Lunars were pushed to the edges of the world, and focused on keeping their culture alive, as well as continuing to resist the Realm. They just didn't have the same access to easy transit that Sidereals did as well, being basically locked out of Yu-Shan.

      In 3e, they're very much active in the world doing guerrilla warfare all over the world against them Realm, and this is more their main focal point than anything else.

      There certainly also is somewhat of a notion that the Sidereals are institutional, and probably are better at creating little projects like that since they have persistently had somewhat of a pro-Solar (to some degree) camp, which isn't a thing for 3e Lunars and didn't make sense for them anyway in past eds.

              I don't doubt that some Lunars have tried, but I think that large groups have probably had that as a debate point.                       Actually, it is briefly mentioned that a few of the Elders are trying to.
                      The organisation in question? The Sun King Seneschals.

                      So, for 1500 years, the Solars were essentially gone. The chance of meeting your Solar mate was virtually nill. And yet the Sun King Seneschals fought the Realm in their name. Why? Because it was a useful ideological battle cry. Revenge for the Solars. Tell young Lunars "you would have a perfect, holy, god-king hero as your mate, but the Realm stole it from you, the Dragonblood destroyed utopia." Not to say the members didn't believe that, but essentially, the organisation's purpose was fighting the Realm: Solar veneration was more a religious/ideological thing than a practical thing, because there just wasn't any Solars.

                      But... suddenly the Solars are back. For the young members, great, their prophecied lover of 100 lives has returned, it's time to rebuild utopia. But the Elders are wiser, cannier, and more cynical. Do they really want to be slaves to the Solars? To fight under Solar command, when for 1500 years they've been running things just fine on their own? Do they really want to return to number 2, especially under Exalts who are just a few years old? Not to mention that it turns out that just because the Solars are mighty shining warriors... that doesn't mean they're necessarily moral. Or sane. Or wise.
                      So, it was mentioned in one book (I forget which) that some of the Sun King Seneschal elders are thinking it's time to bring the Solars in (using their Lunar mates to do so), to get them firmly under the Lunars' control before they put the Lunars under the Solars control. Train them up the way the Sidereals do, to take advice from the caring Lunar elders who just want to help them out, to learn about the evils of the Realm and how beastmen are totally lovely, and that the Usurpation was ultimately the fault of the Sidereals (so you definitely can't trust the so-called "Gold" faction).

                      Of course, I wouldn't be surprised if the Sun King Seneschals (and all the other factions) simply don't exist in 3e. But I could still definitely imagine a Lunar elder deciding she needs to take a naive young Solar under her wing so as to mould him into her loyal apprentice.

                                                      I think that ultimately it is (as others have indicated) a question of different approaches.
