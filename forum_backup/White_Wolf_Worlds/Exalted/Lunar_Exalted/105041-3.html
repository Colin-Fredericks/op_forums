    Exalted don't "need" to be capable of covering every base if they go off on paths that Solars can't access. Solars don't teleport, but Sidereals can. Conversely, Sidereals don't use most Abilities for the purposes that they were intended to cover when it comes to magic.

    For Lunars, they get monstrous and outright inhuman capabilities like the quickest healing in the setting, turning into daikaijuu or swarms of small animals, being able to reanimate corpses with magical larvae and other freakish capacities. Some capacities that they share with Solars are accomplished through the overarching theme of being feral, vicious, mysterious, seductive or fascinating. Luna has a general focus on storytelling, too, likely in a trickster's capacity.

            Every type of Exalt should be able to do everything the others can (of course more or less difficult and by other means to the same goal). At least all Celestial Exalts. Because every Exalt is also suppossed to be a playable character. And hard limits forcing you to never be able to reach something... that's stupid.
            For example: Why should a Essence 5 Solar be able to use Solar Circle Sorcery, but not a Essence 8 Lunar? Why should a Solar never be able to change his shape while Lunars and Infernals do? Why can't Sidereals invent their own Charms (seriously, major flaw!)?
            Setting outright limits to what an Exalt can do in the long term is not helpful or balancing. You could balance that SCS issue for example by not letting your Lunars reach Essence 8 in the game, or at least not that easily.

            The most insane thing? Those 60 Exalts of the Eclipse Caste CAN do all these things. Of course Charmshare is broken, but what about Charm adoptation?

            That all being said, I'd really like something like Primordial Principle Emulation for Lunars, but with different mechanics and different targets. I want the Lunar to grow like an organism, to adapt, to evolve. Maybe this means copying some Solar Charms. Maybe I want basic astrology. All that should be possible. It should not be easy and it should not be gamebreaking. But something like that should always exist as an option.
