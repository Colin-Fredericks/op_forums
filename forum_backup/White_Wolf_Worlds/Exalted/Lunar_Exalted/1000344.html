                                                      I like Beast. There I said it. But no I don't think the theme or mood of Beast is a good fit for the Lunars. Raksha, and other denizens of the Wyld certainly. The only thing I might go with is the territory of the Lunar and the Beast's Lair giving the Lunar certain charms or workings that make the Lunar more or less the master of the land that they claim as home. A Beast in it's Lair is a terrifying thing as it can unleash the full force of it's powers and impose a number of tilt conditions on invaders to the Lair.
                                                      For example in Exalted: A Lunar that claims a chain of islands has charms or enacted a working that allows the Lunar to:
                                                      Raise rocks to smash the hulls of ships as they enter the only harbor.
                                                      Turn the water of the harbor against ships by having the tide shift instantly.
                                                      Have the water from the springs turn to blood when invaders drink from them.
                                                      Have storms with blasts of lightening and hail rain down on the Invaders.
                                                      Distort the distance so that anyone scaling the cliffs of an island find themselves never getting close to the top.
