    Originally posted by Saur Ops Specialist View Post
    If we get the Super Swole Strength Charm that had a boring, reused name in 1e, would that be big enough? Its principle cause would be to increase Strength in any form, but it might give some people what they're looking for.
    Fine by me.


    How much bigger are we talking, anyway?
    Iunno.

    Using Solar familiars as a metric, the current limit with printed Charms is an optional permanent size increase of 50%, with a total temporary size increase of 140%.

    [y * 10%] (Life of the Aurochs) + 25% (Saga Beast Virtue) + 15% (Deadly Predator Method) + [y * 10%] (Deadly Predator Method), where "y" = number of times you purchased Life of the Aurochs.

    There may be some confused editing with regards to the interaction of Deadly Predator Method and Life of the Aurochs; it may only be intended to be counted once rather than twice. In which case, the maximum temporary size increase is 90%. Which is just barely doubling the familiar's size.

    I think Lunars can safely double the size of any form they can take without straying outside of their aesthetic themes, especially since that's just about perfect for "clearly gain advantages in might and toughness without becoming so much larger that you need to adjust how the form interacts with the game's basic systems."


    Most human forms, though, seem to be taken for the sake of deception and blending in with crowds.
    That's not a reason for Lunars to lack the option; it's just a reason why most Lunars won't ever bother USING the option. You don't need to outlaw revolvers when everyone wants to use automatics.


    Originally posted by Sanctaphrax View Post
    Becoming bigger is pretty key to the whole "mythological monster" thing. Lunars like Leviathan are supposed to be able to emulate mythological creatures like the leviathan.
    Form lock is dead. You don't need to use your "true forms" for EVERYTHING now. You can freely use stolen forms, even when those stolen forms are much larger than your original ones.

    If Leviathan doesn't think being a twice-as-large-as-normal killer whale is enough, he can turn into a sea serpent he ate once.

    Or he can use Sorcery. Or artifacts. Or divine blessings. Or Wyld Mutations.
