    I have come back from seeing Alita Battle Angel, and feel as though I have seen the face of my new Lunar patron. Time to chase that feeling into the Dexterity Charms that in no way resemble a single thing she does in that film.

    Okay, finishing off Dexterity a bit with the Swarm Charms, looking like an elaborate extension of what previously only functioned as a couple of Knacks.


    It gets right into things from Essence 1 with Cunning Anglerfish Decoy, allowing one to pull off the Sun Wukong trick of snipping off a piece to form a personal duplicate. It’s necessarily limited, not having a capacity to act independently, can’t perform proper actions at all, doesn’t withstand more than one successful hit, and unable to move very far from the Lunar, although its defensive capabilities can be decent, and depending on the original Lunar it might be surprisingly good at not being sussed out. My initial thought on the ability to hold conversation is that results might be limited with the Lunar’s bound range, but on further though, quite apart from medium being a decent range, I’d say the option for the Lunar to be following or ranging ahead in an alternate form can help preserve the ruse. I also think that it’s clever to model forming a duplicate with witnesses, and banking on enemies thinking that they’re the real one, with a Stealth action to become concealed even in plain sight. And I find the number of duplicates to maintain at once, and costs of producing them, to be quite generous.

    Still… considering the premise, maximum Dexterity doesn’t not make sense, but creates a bit of a flat curve. At least as far as enhancements to human duplicates go.

    Oh hey, and to let more people in on the fun, it’s actually a Totemic Charm for two Attributes, with small creature totems having the option of Manipulation or Wits. So under the right circumstances, any of the Castes can get in on the fun.

    Yeah, I’d actually been thinking before that the picture of the Lunar breathing out a swarm was for one of these Charms rather than Intelligence (which actually makes me even more excited to read that one). Thousandfold Wasp Dance is fairly straightforward in function, produce the creatures as an environmental hazard with some of the qualities of a battle group, that can partly move by the Lunar’s will. Definitely think it makes more sense for dispersal to utilise a gambit with something like fire, rather than the idea of swatting down the whole swarm in bunches.

    Oh, that one was Essence 2, and used the prior as prereq? Mildly curious choice.

    Essence 3 is also following directly from Cunning Anglerfish, for good old And-and-Starfish Trick. It functions as an enhancement to its prerequisite, allowing a not too expensive surcharge to be paid to give the initial duplicate something like true independent life. {rereads} Ah, CAL is able to duplicate animal shapes, the Lunar just has to be in them at the time. As for what independence entails, the duplicate can think for itself as make choices about actions, if human gets copies of everything inherent to the mind and body, as well as the Tell, and an unencroachable Tie of loyalty to the original. It’s still limited to the Lunar’s proximity, but measured now in miles. Equipment gains functional duplicates (although Artifacts are just replicated cosmetically), but possesses no supernatural powers. Mundane observation of personality can’t distinguish the copy from the original, and if the Lunar wants to internalise what the replica experienced while it was separate, it’s necessary to end the Charm while touching them for absorption, otherwise everything is lost. This one also gets to be used repeatedly, but loses the generous discount.

    This would be something where I’m starting to buy it being Totemic for small animals a bit less, even if I can suppose some logic of having mass to spare.

    An alternative path for CAL that incorporates one of the counterattack Charms is in Living Hive Transformation… which I already saw and commented upon. Neat form of area denial and punishing attackers, fun creepy image, putting it amongst the Swarm Charms actually helps to settle it in a bit more amidst all of the other Dexterity Charms that are less overt.

    Essence 4 provides Hungry All-Consuming Cloud, enhancing the duplicate Charms further, and… hoo boy this is a long one. Okay, work through it, errr, ah, Lunar becoming a swarm; alternative way to activate Emerald Grasshopper, that sounds reasonable. In place of the usual effects of Miniscule Size, the Lunar is stuck with… oh wait, there’s good stuff too; it goes extra health levels for the more diffuse form, gets a swarming attack with decent stats and a whole bunch of flexible tags (including the Flexible tag), which can hit multiple enemies at once in the typical manner, and is incompatible with Simple Charms. There are a few enhancements to grappling (which does give a certain impression of these still being super strong vermin)… oh, it’s actually basically all good stuff; the Lunar can’t Parry, but Evasion goes up, minimum withering damage is halved, and decisive attacks are slightly weakened, although the benefits can’t apply to things like fire, large projectiles, multi-attack or area-of-effect powers, and whatever else might strike a large portion or otherwise mess up a swarm. Grapples become impossible barring a power that could grasp or scoop up a swarm, and anything human size or smaller has difficulty getting away. I’d say that this effectively makes a Lunar becoming a cloud of little devils seem like something impressive and powerful, after the previous Charm took up the space of modelling it as an environmental hazard. I think a fight involving two Lunars doing this in opposition would be neat.

    Oh, aaand Essence 5 provides Locust-and-Starling Legion, allowing a Lunar to branch out as a whole battle group (for all intents and purposes, one fixed at Size 3), albeit one with some limits: can’t have Drill or Might, Initiative works in the normal manner, has to divide between withering and decisive attacks, and can’t be targeted with command actions or similar Charms. Also can’t combine area attacks with Simple Charms. Magnitude (pop pop) still confers a noteworthy benefit of being separate from the Lunar’s main health track, as attacks disperse copies rather than hit the true self (which going by the Charm’s mechanics, I guess is effectively non-existent while spread out as about a hundred creatures), and filling the track to reduce Size creates a risk of the Charm ending prematurely in lieu of rout, with such a sudden collapse causing the Lunar to take a level of damage. It can only be used once per scene. I know that Vance went back and forth on letting Lunars become battle groups, but I’d say this came upon an effective compromise.



    So that’s the Swarm Charms, a nice consolidation of what were some previously distributed and slightly meandering effects, and rounding out Dexterity with some very overt transformation shenanigans.

    I have approximate knowledge of many things.

            Okay, messed up a lot of stuff with my time, so… going to be a little bit of a strain to fit this in.


            So Intelligence. It’ll be interesting to see where this one can go, considering that its purely cerebral quality has been a frequent source of consternation in assessing the Lunars in the past.


            The Heart’s Blood Charm is Blood Geas Binding, allowing for shapes to be taken in payment from people that break oaths sanctified by the Lunar. I think it’s interesting how this one could be taken to a few places, such as the shape being employed for retribution, or possibly even the people bound by oath knowing the consequences and being incentivized by them. It makes me wonder if knowing that a Lunar can look like you is a scarier prospect than the curse of an Eclipse Caste.

            And it makes sense that Memory-Drinking Meditation is part of this category. I’ve already talked at length about the implications of that one, but see that this more finished version has an upgrade at Essence 3 allowing for a slightly larger mote commitment to acquire the entirety of a person’s memories in one go, although it seems at least one Tie still needs to be acquired. I think it’s good for the experience to be kept grounded in experiencing the memory as the original person did, find the idea of using the other Charm to go diving through an entirely new set of impersonal experiences to make an intriguing image, and think there’s something neat and unique in the idea of teaching yourself a skill by consulting adopted memories of having already learned it.

            Stolen Voice Echo helps the Essence 2 Lunar maintain the viability of their disguises by being able to use the Memory-Drinking Charm (at higher cost) to acquire a target’s languages in addition to memories, which can ultimately serve as a means for a Lunar to retain that language permanently. I like this better to the last Edition’s method of language acquisition, which was more straightforward and dull. Huh, Intelligence sure gets a lot for Heart’s Blood… ahh, Lessons in the Blood extends the prior element to a more mystical side, as the Lunar is able to steal a target’s knowledge of a spell, Martial Arts Charm, or some of the thaumaturgy they knew. What I like about that one is how it synergizes with Charms to acquire shapes without killing, such that it might imply even a somewhat voluntary exchange for the sake of expediting instruction.

            And a sidebar addressing the subject of experience debt, which functions the same here as in the prior Edition.

            Okay, Knowledge Charms, just going to skip ahead a little… not that many, I can cover all of them before posting this.

            Starts with Inevitable Genius Insight, taking a syncretic approach to Lunar mental capability by letting them add part of… just about anything with its own knowledge base to a Lore roll, as well as treating their specialties as a basis for being able to use something as a Lore field. If there was going to be any Attribute that said “it really helps to have some Abilities to go with this”, I guess that Intelligence would be up near the top. At least at this early point.

            Wayfaring Sage Meditation is similar, but in a more instinctual or holistic direction, as the Lunar is permanently enhanced to be able to treat profound personal experiences or achievements as a basis for Lore fields, which also feels like a very good characterizing element, a picture of how an Intelligence Lunar might live their life (which has some use for a concept I’ve had in the past). Also lets that experience provide specialties to other Abilities for dirt cheap.

            But Dreaming Wisdom Revelation takes things into the more strange and mystical by providing the starts of a Lunar divination. The Lunar gets to perform some appropriate divinatory ritual, make a dice roll, and bank the successes as points representing foresight that can be used for a variety of things based on the image of having seen something cunning or gotten a direct channel to some source of knowledge; stuff like getting more fact introduction, contributions to spells or craft projects, improves some information based activities, get to use a few Charms retroactively for the image of knowing ahead that you’d need something, or make fact checking automatically successful. I know it’s early, but I think this is a good place to start with clarifying or consolidating some ideas from prior Editions with regards to Lunar divination or encoding universal knowledge, that kind of just floated around vaguely, never quite being fully realised. I think it’s good for something like this to be limited to once a story, and like the reset condition being acquiring the lore field from the prior Charm, both for giving that more impact and adding to the incentivized activity.

            Tale-Spinning Mastery pulls in an element hinted elsewhere that I’m interested in seeing realised, Intelligence as the Attribute of the teacher and storyteller. I do actually think that it’s good to put this element here rather than in the social Attributes, for a few reasons; those are already focused on their own thing, it helps realise some of the foundational concepts of the No Moon Caste, and it lets Intelligence have something to not keep it too internally focused. I also think it can be a useful way of characterizing the Lunar take on the Attribute as more than just something clinical, and if it’s fudging the lines a bit… Lunars need it, and Intelligence Charms need it, so I won’t keep score if you won’t. As for the effect, it’s a distinct way of providing a listening character with Ability dots, especially if the Lunar tells about something that happened in the game (or… just learned a story in play) or using a fact that they introduced. Whatever the level, it greatly reduces training time to model the character developing skill in that unique manner, and balances the subject of experience debt not being tracked for NPCs by limiting the use on them per story. The base Charm can only teach one at a time, but purchase at later power can widen the instructed audience.

            Let’s see, Night’s Mercy Panacea… I think this was previewed before, but the wording seems a bit different… the mechanical element of getting non-Charm dice for using a treatment based on an introduced fact seems new, and I’m sure that before it referred to the treatment based on exuding something from the Lunar’s body… maybe I’m mixing it up with something else. If not, it’s a small change, but I’d have some thoughts… perhaps I’ll go over them later. As for the Charm, the basic effect is fairly straightforward, but I think the Lore element colours it a bit towards this Attribute’s character.

            Plague-Swallowing Kiss gets something more overtly them, with being able to cure a person outright by absorbing the malady directly into their own body, which I’d say is both flavourful and can have some further applications based on additional Lunar powers. I particularly like the Totemic option based on a spirit shape of a carrion feeder.

            Moonlit Cauldron Apothecary is extending some of the prior significance of the Lore side with allowing one to introduce a fact about the existence of some rare herb or mineral that can be used to roll to cure a familiar poison or disease instantly (which I think also gives a certain attunement to the natural world quality, but not a very heavy one), although it still needs to handle the subject with the normal rules. My first impulse is that, even if this is appropriately flavourful, it’s a much more roundabout way of getting at something that’s very straightforward for Solars. My second impulse is “it’s an Essence 1 Charm, you prig, give them a chance”. There are some thoughts, but I’ll wait until the picture is more complete. Speaking of, there’s a reference to interaction with another Charm, but I’ll wait until I see what that does.

            Oh, maybe that Charm I saw previewed was this one that it’s saying can be used to directly synthesize one’s discovered medicine.

            Okay, moving on. Bone-Mending Technique: administer a treatment that can greatly increase a character’s rate of healing at rest. Like, assuming somebody with a minimum of Medicine of 1, their patient is healing nine times faster. Even more if they incorporate something from an introduced fact. Sounds useful. And Flesh-Sculpting Art makes a return; I think there are some neat ways of systemizing the way that flesh is rendered malleable here, rerolling 1s for surgical rolls, don’t get penalties for no tools, able to do hours of work in minutes, and the surgery itself never causes injury. On top of all that, the stuff with being able to alter faces or add little body pouches is just gravy.

            For Essence 2, Moon-Kept Mysteries… okay, I’m seeing it now, using some of these avenues for Lore checks to represent all of that long talked about stuff of No Moons extracting hidden wisdom. The mechanical effect of double 9s for a Lore or recollection roll and getting Willpower from a big success is simple, but it’s building up the image.

            Ah, Counting the Elephant’s Wrinkles, that’s the good stuff. Gets to reroll dice on rolls to introduce facts or other things including mental Attributes related to a recalled memory… I’m liking the image of somebody pulling up a key fact about warfare to help bring their command roll up. It can also be a basis for enhancing Resolve against effects that would alter memory. Have to wait until Essence 3 to get it in a form that allows one to experience a memory again at length and in detail, though, which also gives a once-per-story option to use it to negate memory-altering powers when attention is drawn to them; I’m liking those together for the image of diving into one’s memory and manually correcting the out of place elements. I think this is a bit narrower than some prior Editions have made it, but it benefits from being systemized.

            Huh, into Essence 3 already. Burning Moonfire Mind, that’s another good name. A Lunar gets to make an Intelligence roll with a couple of options for Abilities (including Integrity, interestingly), and turn the successes into motes that can be held for a scene to be spent on later mental Charms to enhance a variety of cerebral actions, or put into sorcery. This feels like a Charm that can only be fully appreciated with some good experience with managing mote pools in play, and the value of some useful things not taking a knock out of it. It would probably feel more overt to me if it was phrased as a discount, but this way it gets to be more overt in building up a spell.

            Okay, what does Midnight Soul Reprieve do… ah, a kind of medicinal action crossed with a bit of the storytelling to help treat mental illness. And it manages that with… right, so in the short term, a character has some of the symptoms relieved, and in the long term (upon success with a somewhat lengthy extended action for treatment) downgrades its intensity to a minimum of Minor (except for Addicitions, which can be removed completely). Relieving the symptoms of a Minor level illness doesn’t require dice action, just paying the Charm’s cost. It can also help fight off Psyche effects or other powers to alter the mind, with a successful roll reducing the Willpower needed to resist them. I overall appreciate this for the same reasons that I did the similar Terrestrial Charm, and would be interested in seeing how those compare.

            Oh, and it’s finally an Intelligence Charm with a Protean effect, giving the dice rolls double 9s if in a shape the patient has a strong Tie to; apart from the human side, you can make for an effective therapy animal.

            Right, Starfish-and-Salamander Meditation was mentioned before, what does it do? Right, this is for treating crippling effects and other disfigurements or congenital shortcomings. Takes the form of a lengthy extended action, although its interval can be shortened… no, the roll interval can’t be shortened, just the amount of time the Lunar needs to spend working on the patient per interval, with Flesh-Sculpting Art. And Moonlit Cauldron Apothecary… oh, it partially bypasses the usual extended action rules and lets all of the successes on an interval roll count towards the goal. This is kind of one where a comparison to a Solar Charm feels very straightforward and ends up with a power that is less good (albeit in a slightly more elaborate way, mechanically, and one that still allows the Lunar to ultimately completely restore somebody). I don’t really mind that; it probably has to end up somewhere, and it doesn’t feel like a very big deal considering all of the gems surrounding it.

            Up to Essence 4, and What Lies in Darkness… models Lunars being able to deepen awareness and draw knowledge from altered states by making rolls to introduce facts without being limited by background. It can only be used once per story (albeit with a reset of the introduced fact greatly aiding the Lunar or somebody close), but I’d say this is a good way of using that system to represent Lunars being able to know things beyond their normal ken.



            I think Intelligence is off to a decent start, some nice effects and imagery in here.

            Tuesday has unfortunately caught up with me before I’m done, and I got a very late start today. Maybe I could spend the next few hours pushing through the next twenty pages, but… I think I’m better off, on several counts, of getting a nice earlier night in now, start up fresh and early in the morning, and see if I can’t polish them off quickly before going straight into some Lunar Dominions.

            I have approximate knowledge of many things.

                                                Ohhh I’m a little messed up. I’ll recalibrate eventually. Let’s see what some nice Mysticism Charms might do for me.


                                                Right, Crossroads Walker Entreaty. Basically like a somewhat weaker version of the Eclipse anima diplomatic power, with an additional quality of making hearing what they have to say mandatory, that the spirits and fae (which a sidebar provides definition for) can pay Willpower to be able to ignore the words and a bit more to initiate violence. But the Lunar also gets a bonus to their Resolve and Guile, and if they’re in a shape that the beings have an important Tie towards then overcoming the Charm requires Decision Points instead of just Willpower. Seems like a good way to get a foot in, and I do like the notion that it sometimes ends in a paw or a hoof.

                                                Ardent Prayers Fulfilled, lets one make influence rolls to get a spirit or fae to act for the benefit of their worshippers, or that conveys a message from those worshippers, gets double 9s and the unique benefit of adding their Cult rating as non-Charm dice. I find the idea that the Cult can’t be a source of Willpower to resist the influence to be particularly neat and insidious.

                                                And Devil-Pleasing Chiminage was seen in preview, but if there’s anything new to it… the Essence 2 benefit of getting to treat the offering as a Defining Intimacy to appeal to sounds unfamiliar. In any case, this fact introducing is a neat way to present the idea of appealing to spirits with unusual offerings, and I particularly like the idea that it could convey an element of knowing a way in that even the spirit doesn’t know yet.

                                                Raiton’s Dark Auspice, already previewed as well, although I had missed that it was a permanent enhancement to interacting with ghosts at the time. And then there’s Graveyard Hound Vigil, which upgrades Charms that work on spirits and fae to include physical undead beings excluding the Exalted; I do kind of wonder about that, since the general presentation of zombies is as something mindless, and I’m not even sure of the circumstances in which they would have worshippers… what sounds more valuable is gaining the capacity to gain Willpower and shed Limit for upholding an important Intimacy through a variety of achievements related to the Underworld and the dead. If there aren’t other Limit shedding Charms, I could get an interesting picture from this of characters being able to centre themselves against the pull of their Urges by standing on the liminal space between the living and dead worlds, so that would be a vivid way of pulling in that world walker element.

                                                Hmm, Penumbra Witch Mastery looks like a good example of one of those multi-faceted Charms; extra dice and 1 rerolling for sorcery and thaumaturgy rolls, introducing facts about or understanding a variety of supernatural beings and phenomena, and character insights into spirits and fae. I think the manner in which it is both broad and specific helps it feel more interesting than the usual dice enhancements, and it’s still effectively working out the image of Lunars having unusual sources of knowledge.

                                                Hmm, Sharing Luna’s Gifts, that sounds promising; has a health level cost and all. Yes, Charm for granting mutations (with an experience debt cost; I’m going to assume that it treats the usually Innate/Story Merits as though they could be purchased for that purpose), that seems reasonable for Essence 1, and up to five dots is fairly generous. The capacity for the Lunar to target themselves with the Charm (not needing to spill their own blood to anoint themselves, which I would have found a neat image, but the picture of just harnessing their blood directly kind of matches it anyway) initially felt like it resolved a question for me; five dot Wings would be a particularly useful mutation, but they’re beyond anything but Hybrid Body, which would both require a winged spirit shape and takes up most of that Charm’s benefit. But the fact that the Charm bestows the mutations permanently… would it be possible to add the Merit for making those wings retractable on top of this once the experience debt has been paid off? Mind, that would mind up making those wings a bit more convenient than what those shapeshifting Charms could provide… I’ll think about it.

                                                Ooh Swarming Locust Punishment looks like a neat kind of curse; enhance the Blood Geas Charm so that oathbreakers not only forfeit their shape, but become targeted by vermin in ways that cause things like pain and difficulty to getting through the wilderness, prevent them from effectively sleeping, and potentially induce an Obsession with eliminating the pests tormenting them. I do like how the Lunar has the power to deliberately revoke the curse before its natural duration of (Essence) weeks elapses or set a condition to end it; I, for one, would have a mind to often defaulting to a condition that stops it before it starts, in any case where the Lunar doesn’t think that the broken oath warrants that kind of punishment.

                                                Okay, Essence 2, beginning with Argent Guardian Yantra. Converts a bit of the anima into a ward that can’t be crossed at all by any spirit with lower Resolve than successes on a roll that the Lunar makes establishing it, unless their Essence exceeds the Lunar’s (which still costs them some Willpower). I’m not quite sure about the shape of the ward; the radial one is clear enough, but the line… the description about being bound against crossing it sounds like just projecting a section that can cover a particular path, but the description of the projection kind of sounds like it’s extending from the Lunar… I think the description of that one could be a bit clearer. Still, it sounds like it can have its uses; an image that stands out to me is a Lunar that has worked their way into a court of devils to negotiate, and when they go south surrounds themselves with a barrier to hold attackers off for a moment before getting to turn into something highly mobile to make good an escape. Maybe instead cast some kind of useful spell.

                                                Keeper of Midnight’s Gates provides a means of forcing spirits to materialize; even for the spirits that can resist that force, I’d say that there can be a benefit to inducing them to spend Willpower. Ghost-Banishing Howl allows one to exorcize possessing spirits, only needing a roll to beat their Resolve; it can also be used to make an Argent Guardian Ward into a cage for spirits (which frankly sounds more useful), and it has what looks like the most useful Protean benefit to an Intelligence Charm yet, of allowing a shape the target is afraid of to strip Willpower equal to rolled successes from any spirits of lower Essence.

                                                And finally Cage of Horn and Sinew allows one to ape one of Jesus’ less known feats, of forcing an exorcized spirit to inhabit the body of a nearby animal for months at a time. The process sounds particularly nasty for the spirit imprisoned; they’re stuck with the animal’s traits, can’t use any powers, even their mental capacity is diminished to be in line with that natural to the creature… they nominally have control of the body, but can’t even lead it deliberately into death to free themselves. And even when they do depart, it leaves a mark on the spirit as a Defining Intimacy related to the animal’s nature and/or experiences while imprisoned (the animal gains a similar thing in response, possibly even becoming god-blooded, assuming the animal isn’t immediately slain by the newly released spirit). It overall sounds like a rather fearsome thing that Lunars might do to unruly or offensive spirits. Ah jeez, and it gets even worse; if the Lunar has the Charm to permanently destroy the spirit, they get the option to imprison it for a whole year, during which the spirit is bound to a Defining Tie of gratitude for the Lunar’s mercy. That one can even apply to spirits of higher Essence so… yeah, might be some horror stories among gods and demons of people pissing off the wrong Lunar and getting the enjoy a year spent as a trout or something.

                                                Hmm, Font of Dark Wisdoms… oh wait, I get it; my first read was that the Thaumaturgist Merit it bestowed was limited, but I can see how it’s not just limited to those five dots of rituals, that’s just the amount you get for free… which I think might ultimately amount to getting to pay the cost of all those rituals as a single package… yeah, even if Intelligence isn’t Caste or Favoured, you’re getting between 13 and 15 XP worth of rituals for a discount of 3-5 points. And you get to take the Charm repeatedly to get even more rituals at a premium, so it’s still beneficial even if you get the Merit just from becoming a sorcerer. Actually, come to think of it… those five dots of rituals surely aren’t including what you got for getting the Merit the first time… do you get a new ritual every time you take the Charm? Overall, this seems like a decent path into that form of minor magic.

                                                A Lunar initiated into sorcery can learn the Spell-Rending Talon, through which decisive attacks by a high Initiative Lunar can help to distort spells that are buffing up the sorcerer that the Lunar is attacking, with the particularly good looking benefit of not counting towards the terminus for that disruption.

                                                Ah, finally getting to this one at Essence 3: Beast-Soul Awakening Crucible. Create a zone (that has to be situated in a demesne or Wyld spot; hadn’t expected that, but it seems reasonable) in which people who pass some appropriate trial acquire six dots worth of mutations modelled on the Lunar’s spirit shape, a transformation meant to convert them into beastfolk, that they become capable of passing down to their children to propagate lineages. If the Lunar is a chimera, their zone can bestow mutations reflecting one animal or the other, depending on how the trial is passed. I wonder if the mutations are determined by the Lunar upon creating the crucible, or apply differently for every petitioner. I like how the Lunar not only doesn’t fully control the nature of the trials, but that it’s also meant to include the nature of the demesne or Wyld. And the consequences for failure sound sufficiently repelling as to give a lot of people pause, making for an effectively evocative setting and story element even apart from the considerations for not empowering whole armies of people. Ah, the duration had been a bit of a concern, but it’s fine that it’s just how long the motes need to be committed before becoming permanent. And apart from functioning as an avenue to take such zones down, I like how a Lunar being committed to preventing a manse from being built or the Wyld from being shaped could be readily misinterpreted as them caring about preserving nature or the Wyld. Finally, the number of grounds that can be maintained sounds rather generous, even before considering how territories don’t count towards it.

                                                Okay, want to go through them a bit more quickly now when they don’t stand out especially…

                                                Crimson-and-Silver Rebirth: clean somebody in your blood to remove a mutation or shaping curse, although you only get one shot at it. But you do get to take away such things that you bestowed yourself without cost or roll… all right then, that resolves the Wings dilemma… possibly… do you still have to pay XP for a mutation after removing it from yourself? I might not be taking these in the spirit that they’re intended. Heaven-Darkening Eclipse allows one to… wow, that’s powerful, introduce facts about how to dispel the ongoing effects of spirit Charms (not for the Incarnae or Yozis or any theoretical Essence 10s) or sorcerous spells, allowing actual dispellation rather than mere distortion, although doing so gives the Lunar away to the one who cast it. And when repurchased at Essence 4, actually allows for Terrestrial workings to be destroyed entirely, and suppress higher ones for the length of some major task of the Lunar that the working would impede. This is a big one, and might speak a lot to the kinds of sabotage that Lunars have levelled throughout the Second Age.

                                                Ohoho, Unravelling the Tapestry looks like fun; the discordant element of Lunars allows them to determine loopholes even in the enduring prophecies of Sidereal and Solar Exalted. The opposition can’t be casual, and can’t mitigate the predicted effect entirely, but the Lunar, their Mate, anything they deeply care for and magically claimed territories will be protected by such success, and the Lunar also gets to be forewarned by prophecies that would target them or their cares to the extent that the Charm activates itself. I like the little example given and all. It’s an impressive way of having Lunars interact with such events, fitting with their nature, and also puts a bit of their relationship with Sidereals into context.

                                                Already saw Insidious Lunar Transformation… but Endless Masquerade Affliction lets one do a similar (but longer lasting) effect of turning one person into another (one of the Lunar’s shapes)… it’s described as a curse (and surely would be, if you turned a prince into a peasant or the like), but can surely be beneficial to some people, like disguising one’s agents or creating confusion about where and when certain people were and which ones might be Lunars. Selkie Mantle Enhancement extends from ILF to grant a person the capacity to change back and forth between human and animal (or human, if the Lunar also has EMA) at will, for as long as the Lunar commits motes for them (made cheaper if Lunar and beneficiary care strongly for one another).

                                                Spirit-Sealing Talisman allows exorcized spirits to instead be imprisoned within objects, from which they can never escape unless destroyed (made harder by their supernatural durability). The Lunar can commit motes for the talisman’s wielder to be able to use an Eclipse Charm the spirit either has, or a custom one that fits their nature, as long as they have the appropriate Essence (although the Lunar can spend Willpower to let another use the Charm on the Lunar’s behalf, of which the Lunar will always know), and while the power is lost if the commitment is dropped, the Lunar can freely recommit to acquire it again, while the spirit remains imprisoned. This one sounds both very useful and horrible.

                                                From Essence 4, Chains-of-Silver Geas provides yet another path for dealing with truculent spirits and fae; they just need to overcome the being (which seems to need to present a meaningful opposition) in some challenge, physical, spiritual (i.e. exorcism) or social, which then becomes irresistibly bound to perform an inconvenient or serious task, or even a life-defining one if the Lunar’s Essence is equal or greater. It’s even a very compelling binding, from which the being can only meaningfully deviate to attend to another threatened Major or greater Intimacy, or use them in Decision points to win short reprieves from pursuing the task. It can only made to last more than a year if it entails a task that the being could meaningfully conclude, but automatically prevents them from attempting to harm the binding Lunar in any capacity but self-defence. And it can only be used against a character once per story; you can’t readily put multiple bindings on the same figure, but you can hand out geasa left and right to many. This one looks really good on a number of counts, such as mystical binding rooted in a specifically Lunar method, engaging the function of guardianship, giving a bit more substance to how often the fiction has referred to them as bending gods and Raksha to their will. Good stuff all round.

                                                Hmm, pretty sure that Doom Brought to Heel was previewed… yeah, I’ve seen the power to evoke a cured poison or disease as a serving spirit before. Not much to say about it on its own, but some of its interactions with other Charms… hmm, not too sure about being able to remove a person’s mental afflictions altogether in the course of turning them into spirits… oh, but the idea of converting a defeated Charm or spell directly into a weird kind of spirit directly contrary to the power it was born from is particularly interesting. In most respects, the power comes off very well.

                                                And last of the Mysticism Charms, Witch-Among-Swine Spite; that’s a name with some real teeth on it. When a character the Lunar distinctly dislikes offers some kind of offence in their presence, it becomes possible to curse them into animal shape at range and without feeding blood. Most of the heavy lifting of this one is done by the prior Charm, but it adds on a nice bit of edge; to my eyes, more from the punishing offenders side than the doing it at range.


                                                So yeah, Mysticism acquits itself well, lots of distinct curses and blessings and a particular character to how they interact with other supernatural entities, a little bit more stick than carrot.

                                                Crafting shouldn’t take very long, and then I’ll get a starting look at… some of the new stuff.
