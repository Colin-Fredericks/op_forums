    I want them to be able to turn the weapons and skills of the enemy against them, by becoming them and more.

    In turn I want to be able to mix my shapes as I see fit, and have what I can turn into have tangible and awesome effects/powers.

    If I turn into a Tyrant Lizard/Furnace Rhino combo wreathed in spectral flame, I want to be able to mix and match powers from each of those to create this awesome form, not just aesthetics.

                                                        Yeah, the swarm of animals thing seems very Lunar to me.

                                                        ---

                                                        I kind of like drawing on the tales of shifting gods of various mythologies for Lunar inspiration, so that its not just about changing and animals but it isn't just coming out of left field either. One of the things I've noticed in those tales is that the figures often just do something and the story doesn't even try to give details. So they do things like open locks by touching them, make pieces of the world out of small objects, walk through walls or just appear, etc. We never really hear how, because obviously they are gods and we are not meant to know their ways, it is enough to know that they did this. Tellingly, their shapeshifting isn't treated as anything separate from this, its just another secret they know.

                                                        If you wanted to fit it back into transformational themes, you could say they know the secrets to transforming the world too (there's very little that couldn't be stated in those terms), but however you spin it I like the feel of that simple mystery. The Solars excel, the Sidereals exploit, the Lunars do.
