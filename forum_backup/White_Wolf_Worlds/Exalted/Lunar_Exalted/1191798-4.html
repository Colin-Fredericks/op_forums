                                                    Having so little engagement with the Ghosts of Editions Past, I don't know if my own feelings on the Lunars carry much weight. But I'd like to submit for consideration that there's a distinction between an archetype of killing or murder, and an archetype of predation. Murder is a net-loss for the universe: A victim is slain, and they will never be or do anything ever again. Predation is not necessarily a net loss: The blood of the stag becomes the strength of the pack. Sure, predation destroys something, perhaps as permanently as cold-blooded murder woiuld. But in a story about predation, something about the prey is preserved or passed on. Depending on one's perspective, you can talk about the stag becoming the wolf, or the wolf becoming the stag. You are what you eat, and so on.

                                                    Predation being a process, and a transformative process, is what appeals to me about the theme or predation and its relation to Lunars. So, non-lethal methods of taking Heart's Blood don't necessarily seem problematic to me, so long as they live up to those themes: destruction, consumption, transformation. If you can take Heart's Blood by, say, taking all of a target's memories away. It seems fair to say that the prey has been forever removed from the world, and that what has been taken from them is now the predator's to possess. That which has been taken now empowers the predator. Or if you can manipulate a fanatic into abandoning their faith entirely, or seduce somebody away from the love of their lives, steal a singer's voice, or any number of other things, really.

                                                    The mechanics of this edition seem to lend reasonable short-hands for establishing what you need to do to accomplish this kind of thing. What you do to them has to be dramatic enough to completely remove or replace one of their Defining Intimacies.

                                                    What tack is taken with non-lethal forms of mystical predation in Lunars in this edition remains to be seen. But I don't think the presence of non-lethal options has to weaken the Lunar identity as ultimate predators.
