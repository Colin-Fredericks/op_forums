                Other ideas:
                Creating a huge magical oasis teeming with life in the middle of the desert, protected by massive enchanted walls that make it invisible, to create a hidden kingdom for the Lunar's subjects, from which they can strike at the Realm.
                Create a flying island full of monsters that can be used as a mobile battlestation.
                Create a huge Wyld zone all the way around a country, stopping the Realm daring to attack it.
                Enchant a land so that those who eat its food while pregnant give birth to beastmen of the Lunar's totem (what, you think Lunar's give birth to all those beastmen by themselves?)
                Create an army of golems inhabited by the souls of the dead of the Lunar's tribe.
                Curse a Satrapy so that all children born there are hideous mutants.
                Curse a Satrapy so that it becomes a massive wyld zone.
                Bless (or curse) the Lunar's subjects so that when they die, their souls possess large animals (lions, horses, elephants, etc).
                Bless (or curse) the Lunar's subjects so that when they die, they transform into elementals of the local elements.
                Change time at the summit of a mountain so that students who go there can do awesome training montages under the Lunar's tutelage, and become (still mortal) kung-fu masters in a few days.
                Create a massive fortress which can think for itself, shoot ballista, use statues to defend itself, and move walls to confuse attackers (you don't live there, you just make the Realm think you do so they waste time trying to invade it).
                Bless the entire country's land so that one farmer can produce enough food for 100 people, allowing the people to move into other jobs and spark a massive economic boom.
                Create rings of stone that, when someone walks into them, lets them travel along a mysterious fog-shrouded, eerie path, leading to another stone circle, allowing someone to cross a kingdom in an hour. Multiple rings allow the inhabitants to travel all over the country, allowing troops and trade to move wherever they're needed incredibly quickly.
                Create a horde of terrifying flying monsters for your armies to ride.
                Turn thousands of people into were-wolves (or were-horses, or were-owls, or whatever).
                Enchant a land with a strange fog that drives the Lunar's enemies mad, but leaves her subjects and friends untouched.
                Mess with dragon lines, so that Satrapy's manses stop working (or blow up), while creating new demesnes far away in the wilderness.
                Create a radiation-breathing, super-strong, super-tough, super-stealthy, amphibious Kaiju, who really hates Immaculate temples, and who who lurks in the Inner Sea and periodically appears to destroy cities with Immaculate temples in.

                I think the main thing PCs are going to encounter, in terms of Solar workings made by Lunars, is magically enchanted forests/mountains/countries, or giant ravenous monsters created to attack the Realm (or whoever).
                And things to hide them from Sidereals.

                            I think that it's a mistake to go from "the rules technically allow this" to "everyone does this."

                            My feeling is that especially at the high end, the rules tend to be generous to PCs. My default assumption is that there has been maybe a dozen Ambition 3 Solar workings done ever. I don't mean ones done by Lunars, I mean ones done by anyone, including Solars. Maybe one or two of those has been since the Usurpation.

                            It just isn't something that you can really allow to happen more than a few times in history without the setting getting really, really weird (or unless you start to contort yourself into knots to explain why all these reality-shifts went unnoticed by almost everyone, or scaling down Ambition 3 workings to the point where they're no longer as impressive.)

                            Originally posted by Kyman201 View Post
                            Them's some broad assumptions on the number of Sorcerers.

                            That said, in the lead up to the release, Holden and Morke teased that a neat thing about the Caul was that the Lunars cut it away from the Loom of Fate. I'd buy THAT as being possible with an Ambition 3 Solar Working.
                            I think the Caul was "just" Ambition 2, since you're just altering one major metaphysical property of a region. Ambition 3 would be if you were moving it outside of time and space or to another dimension entirely.

                                                        Well, a Lunar sorcerer in my Dreaming Sea game bought a slave who had been blind from birth, in the hopes that she would provide him an extra Means for his Working. The Lunar sent a demon to go collect her (not knowing she had taken the Second Breath), but he's hedging his bets; if it doesn't come back with her soon, he'll just buy another slave.
