            Horses are fast but thats about it. They have really bad soak (3), and the prone infliction only works against enemies 'smaller than human size' which really limits its usefullness. Other than that and swift-hooved they have no merits or special attacks.

            Elk OTOH are just as fast as horses, with 6 soak, also have swift-hooved, plus the Gore, Trample, and Horn Toss abilities that are actually usefull.

            Quicksilver Steed Unity is a 1m reflexive charm that lets you do your choice of a bunch of things either for your mount or your rider, including reflexively Defend Other, reflexively make a distract gambit (which even if distract gambits are usually pretty bad is a free extra attack for 1m which is probably worth it just for the unslaught), or increase the difficulty of an unhorse gambit.

                                                            Do you have any of them?

                                                            I made a list the other day. HDNP's releases so far include:

                                                            Strix
                                                            Elephant/Mammoth
                                                            Metagalapan Riding Hawk (and Roc, which is an upgraded version)
                                                            Armoured Kraken
                                                            Giant Constrictor
                                                            Bat
                                                            Sky Titan
                                                            Eight-Tailed Molehound
                                                            Aurochs
                                                            Austrech
                                                            Forest Strider (giant stick insect)
                                                            Northern Ursidae (it's an aquatic bear)

                                                            The Kraken is the best form for Naval Combat. Because it has good stats, grappling powers, and... essentially is one of only two animals that can do Naval Combat at all.

                                                            The Elephant is a very strong form. It has good stats, can gore (add extra successes on decisive if you do no other actions), and has Trample, which IIRC there are a fair few Protean charms for. But I imagine that the Tyrant Lizard (gated behind the same charm) is a bit better? Elephants can spend a wp to flurry a withering and decisive attack against different targets, which combo'd with the Lunar charm for ignoring flurry penalties might be good.

                                                            The Sky Titan is the fastest form for long travel (80 miles an hour for up to 10 days), and its Legendary Size, and has some powers for resetting initiative to higher levels. But the stats aren't as good as land-dwelling Legendary Size creatures.

                                                            For the non-Legendary Size creatures, I'm not sure.
                                                            The Aurochs doesn't have brilliant stats (Soak 7, Damage 15, not terrible or amazing). But it has a lot of abilities: Gore, Deadly Charge, Horn Toss, Trample, Sturdy as Stone. As I said, I think there's some Trample Protean effects, but I'm not very familiar with them. Maybe it can be made good with Protean charms?
                                                            The Northern Ursidae are pretty much the same as bears except without the Grapple abilities. So still a berzerker, but not the best berzerker. However, they have this ability which looks really good to me, if you combo it with intimidation charms and that Latent where people lose initiative when you intimidate them?
                                                            "Roaring Charge (Latent): After moving at least one
                                                            range band toward a target on the ursidae’s current
                                                            turn, roll Intimidate against the target’s Resolve as a
                                                            miscellaneous action. If successful, the enemy reflexively
                                                            flees one range band in the opposite direction,
                                                            and the bear or its rider receives one automatic
                                                            success on the next attack roll against anyone who
                                                            witnessed the charge."
                                                            As it's a miscallaneous action, I guess it can be flurried with an attack?

                                                            The others are all meh. Not necessarily bad, but they're no Moose.
