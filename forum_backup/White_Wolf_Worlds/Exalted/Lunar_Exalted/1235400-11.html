                                            Originally posted by Blaque View Post

                                            My guess is 0 favored Abilities (including MAs) is the trade-off. It's harder for them to get Abilities to 5 out of chargen than Solar characters.
                                            Though I assume that they have an easier time raising favoured/caste attributes at char gen as well so... *shrug*

                                            I think of this more to balance the investment and training time it takes to raise an attribute (even a favoured one) vs an ability. Solars train attributes at new rating months. I assume this'll likely be more like new rating weeks for favored lunar attributes but that's still a hell of an investment of time to get there. In fast paced games, a lunar may be hitting a ceiling of attribute requirements a lot more often than solars would. In increasing the number of attribute points they can distribute at char gen, this mitigates the problem somewhat by just letting them always be higher on that scale without dumping all their time/xp into meeting requirements
