    Lunar Charms - shapeshifting, or monstrous power?

    05-25-2016, 11:29 AM
    Twin-Faced Hero seems like a proper Lunar Charm, to me. Luna's Chosen can emulate their goddess by blurring the line of "man or woman" as easily as she and they can blur the line of "man and beast"... or even "me and him".

    Likewise, Shaping the Ideal Form (the original First Edition Lunar Charm which included the male/female effect) seems Lunar to me. Since Lunar Exalted are to be identified across forms through their Tell, it doesn't matter as much if their true human face remains the same from day to day -- anyone who sees their basic form will see their Tell, and anyone who sees their Tell will recognize their basic form later, just as they could spot and recognize the Tell in any of their stolen forms.


    But outside of that one Charm, I don't feel that shapeshifting actually works for Lunar Charms so much as the aesthetic of "raw power, uncontrolled and psychotic". Bestial power. Not the power of animals, but the power that makes people think of wild beasts. Power that makes beasts of men.

    (Please note that Lunar Charms are entirely different from Heart's Blood shapeshifting, which very much does grant Lunars the powers of animals, by letting them assume the forms of those animals. But a Lunar using a Strength Charm in a lion form isn't adding the strength of the lion to the strength of the lion. He is adding the unfettered, monstrous strength of the Mad Moon Goddess to the form of the lion. He is becoming the monstrous Nemean Lion of myth and legend; a beast sent by the gods to carry out their wrath on Earth.)


    Consider this: A Lunar equivalent to Solar Strength-Increasing Exercise will probably make the Lunar twice as large from the swelling of his muscles and bones. But growing twice as massive could be explained as his body swelling to contain the divine, bestial strength now surging through his body, rather than his body shapeshifting into a stronger form. The growth becomes the side-effect rather than the method. The method becomes tapping into elemental strength. Psychotic, unrestrained strength.

    If a Liminal Exalt were to do something similar, adding more muscle and bone would certainly be the method rather than the side-effect, because the Liminals are the Exalted of the raw materials of the physical body. They're not strong because of divine, bestial power, but because they use dark magic to steal from the corpses of others to reinforce their frankenstein bodies.


    Consider this: An Intelligence equivalent to the Lunar Charm above wouldn't cause the Lunar's head to swell to twice its size to contain his mad brilliance. That sort of grotesque and ridiculous image inspires disgust rather than awe, and thus doesn't fit the divine, celestial nature of Lunar power. It is the sort of thing that Liminal Exalted do, because their entire aesthetic is about being repulsively wet and meaty and bulging in all the wrong places.

    Instead, the Lunar Charm might express the strain upon the Lunar by imposing a derangement. The Lunar's mind expands, his inner eyes opening up to the truths of the universe, but this short-cut to enlightenment drives him temporarily mad. To maintain his sanity, he might obsess over a singular goal, bending his mad genius to that pursuit until the task is done or the magic wears off and leaves him sane again. This would be far more fitting of Lunar imagery.


    Monkey Arm Technique isn't Lunar. It's a Liminal Exalt horrendously stretching and tearing their flesh and bones to reach farther than should be physical possible, likely making them spray blood everywhere. It's weird and gross and messy.

    Armor-Forming Technique isn't Lunar. It's a Liminal Exalt pushing their bones to the outside in order to form a disgusting exoskeleton for protection.

    Weapon-Trapping Body Dominion is a Liminal congealing their blood and wrapping their meat and bones around a sword to disarm someone who stabbed their frankenstein body.

                                                  My response to this is, and this is all pretty YMMV... I see where you're coming from with the idea of "Psychotic, unrestrained strength" - Lunars and the moon are associated with madness and tapping into imagery of lunar werewolf transformation links to that idea of an inner bestial might being unleashed and transformation and shapeshifting as relatively secondary to this, and the association of the attributes links to the idea of a raw magic.

                                                  The problem for me with this is I don't, fundamentally, associate Luna as a moon deity with unrestrained strength. She's not really the Incarna that is, if any of them are. Luna often feels more devilish crafty and flexible, ruling over the night by her cunning, than uncontrolled and mighty in native strength.

                                                  I wouldn't say she's in control exactly, more her thing is being changeable, unfixed, and that's where madness comes in to her, and the idea of being a monster inside (as that's linked to this idea of having hidden fundamental forms within yourself that are quite different from who you are on the surface).

                                                  Kind of a notion, this is actually what she is as a deity of the moon. The Moon can be defined as the celestial object that changes its form, and is casts its light from the night sky. So I guess to me this is why Lunars embody shapeshifting, and that shapeshifting is associated with the retreat of the rational, daylight world for the obscurity of night, and with passing from the human state to the more irrational and intuitive animal state, and with ruling over the night.

                                                  (Where by analogy, mythologically, as the sun rises and sets, the Solars represent the natural cycle of the zenith heights of brilliance and might and cyclic declines of power, to be reborn and resurgent again, and the Sidereals stand with the distant, fixed symbols of the heavens, hidden during the daylight hours that provide secret maps for human navigation (physical and spiritual) in which man can trust to light the way, if they are wise enough to bear witness.)
