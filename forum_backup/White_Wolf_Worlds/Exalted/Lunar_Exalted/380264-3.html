Even allowing Lunars to learn the charms that are "Eclipse" tagged should be fine, as Vance stated earlier that they were specifically balanced for PCs to take.

            Lunar shapeshifting into Gods/Elementals/Demons should be an option, but it needs to be balanced within the game. Eclipse's in 2E learning anyone's charms caused them to be massively unbalanced. We don't want to see Lunars go down that road.

                    Come to think of it, it really seems like Lunars have a big problem with patiance. It's like they can't stay concentrated long enough to make a long-term goal worthwhile. Like, their crafting charms and such are supposed to revolve around building simple but efficient things on the fly or while on the move, their shapeshifting is limited in how long they can maintain a ruse, meaning that indefinite infiltration is impossible, their tactics are very much about roaming around the place, either gathering up new hoards of barbarians and efing' up somebody's shit, or using guerilla tactics to hit the Realm where it hurts. Even when they do stay for a long time in one place, they don't really seem to... Do anything. Raksi and Leviathan come to mind.

                    Which, I don't think it's necessarily bad. If you ask me, there's a thematic space here, waiting to be explored. I want to see if this has something to do with their curse, or if it's further explored by their charms. Like, maybe their curse would make them fall into one of two extremes more and more- either a rampaging beast, or a hermit filled with ennui, that could be cool.

                    About the "assuming elemantal/god forms" thing, I just wanna chime in and say that if Lunars don't have easy access to the spirit's powers, they better have charms that make it easier to fool people into thinking that they DO have these powers, lest we repeat the "spy dillema" from past two editions. You know, the one in which you first need Larceny, Dodge, Athletics and so on to be an effective rogue and be able to disguise as your target, then you need Presence and Socialize to impersonate them aaaand then you need the rest of the skills your target has to impersonate them well. Yeah, that one. Can we not have it again? Because I like spies, I might want to play as one. Can it please not be a silly gimmick?

                                        The answer *could* well be "As a Lunar Exalt, you craft a fraudulent cover story to explain this away, then use Charisma and Manipulation Charms, stunted sorceries and whatnot to seal the deal".

                                        If you're doing a "The Talented Mr Ripley" bit, murdering people and stealing their identities, then that, I think, should be most interesting when it involves actual deceptive skill and choosing your victims carefully and working out an exit strategy rather than just assuming their entire skillset by magic in the most literal way. (That's sort of close to what people complained about with Havesh, in the last thread - he doesn't seem to be applying any real skill a lot of the time).

                                        It may be hard to try and impersonate specific gods with particular kinds of miracles and attributes which cannot be faked through sorcery and supernatural blagging - maybe Lunar games will have to get by with having Lunars not impersonate those gods, not really a problem as there are an infinity of gods available on tap.

                                        But that's just one option.

                                        I personally have interest in Lunars and no real interest in Lunars who drink of and then take on the essence of other supernatural beings, like a sort of vampire of supernaturalism, parasitically leeching off other beings' supernatural strengths - such a group feels lame to me. Has a "The Lunars is cosplaying another supernatural" wannabee vibe. No effort, no skill, nothing that comes from within, nothing unique from your own character, just drinking up someone's milkshake and becoming an impostor. It would take a lot of skilled writing to push me away from that feel.

                                        Whether this is more of a turnoff than turnon is something which the team should be playtesting through with a group of playtesters who do not have any tendencies to become obsessively committed to one option or the other as "rational" or overridingly important at the expense of a bunch of perverse consequences or feel, or whatever, or too much prior experience with the game and splat.

                                            I'd like it to be an option.

                                            Like, if you want to play sexy dartfrog Lunar who rarely changes shape and just uses lots of poison and social charms and if she's in combat relies on being super fast and strong and having paralytic venom on her weapons? A paragon of human strength and speed and beauty, who can eat scorpions and wrestle bears and dance the sun up in the morning and sing songs to drive a whole town to orgiastic revelry? That should be valid.

                                            If you want to play her as someone who shapeshifts constantly, hunting down and murdering people and animals and altering her own spirit shape with the best of each stolen form? A peerless hunter who studies his prey and takes a lesson from each hunt he survives: the sting of the scorpion, the fangs of the cobra, the eyes of the eagle, the strength of the elephant? An ever-evolving beast gradually evolving into a mighty dragon of brilliant colors and feathered wings, with toxic spines and claws like sabers, a shifting-hued avatar of nature's fury? That should be valid.

                                            If you want to play her as someone who studies the occult, who poisons herself and goes on vision quests, who shapes the bright markings on her skin into sigils of terrible power, a shaman who learns the secret ways of the spirits, who studies the ancient languages of gods and demons...and then hunts them? A dread sorcerer who summons demons and orders them to flee, then pursues them through the jungle and devours their hearts to ritually steal their fell powers? A predator who stalks the lands of the dead and the halls of Heaven, luring stray spirits away from their fellows and consuming them, and adding their strange magicks to her own...and, perhaps, becomes more and more spirit and less and less flesh herself as she does so? That should be valid.

                                            You shouldn't have to play any of these characters! But I'd like you to be able to play any of the three. Hell, I'd like to be able to play any of the three as members of any of the castes, though of course of of them does fit best into one of the traditional molds.
