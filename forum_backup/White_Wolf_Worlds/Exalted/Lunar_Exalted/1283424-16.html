                                                    I like Silver Ripple Mirage. "Fuck past Editions; the Lunar Exalted are the children of Luna herself, and their Charmset is going to reflect that".

                                                    I like Debt of Borrowed Skin, but don't feel it is clearly written.

                                                    How can the target feel the favor is worth their face AND whatever they're paying if they don't know their face is on the table?

                                                    Does the target need to know that the task is serious or life-defining for the Lunar?

                                                    Can the Lunar use this Charm in response to someone successfully influencing her with a bargain action?

                                                    What about this?

                                                    "Upon the conclusion of a successful bargain action by or against the Lunar, the Lunar may begin a sacred hunt to take the human shape of her target, who is the second party of the agreement. She and her target must believe that the Lunar is agreeing to perform what is, for her, a serious or life-defining task (Exalted, p. 210) on his behalf. Her target must consider it to be equal in value to his heart’s blood (along with any other compensation he provides for the task) at the time he does so, though he needn’t know of or consent to that aspect of the exchange, and can’t later renege on the deal."

                                                    Still doesn't answer the question of value. There are people who would sooner die than let a Lunar have their face, and refuse to agree to a bargain that is ostensibly about saving their life. There are people who offer vast riches in return for the deed done, and consider that more than worth the deal and would not consider the exchange of their face a fair price on top of that.

                                                    So if a person doesn't know they're giving up sole ownership of their face, then they ought to be thinking you're either bargaining to do a thing for no exchange at all, or for much less than they should be compensating you. So they should know the deal is too good to be true, or they should be thinking they're screwing you over.
