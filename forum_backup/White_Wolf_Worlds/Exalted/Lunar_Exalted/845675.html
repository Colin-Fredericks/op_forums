                  Originally posted by Raemus View Post
                  Long ago Lunars were considered great shape shifters and masters of Illusions... at least in the narrative. I don't think it ever panned out well in the system.
                  The system always supported them being badass shapeshifters. But neither the system nor, really, the narrative ever really went hard on them as masters of illusion.

                  What happened was that the 1e Core book had some very, very brief (like two or three sentences) about the Lunars as master illusionists, because that was written back when the Lunars were supposed to be, to quote Grabowski, "the insane inimical allies of the Fair Folk."

                  And people seized onto that really goddamn hard even though once the Lunars were developed as a splat and more materiel than a few lines about them in the Solars Core was published, that aspect of them had large been deep-sixed. It's been fifteen years and this still comes up. There's a huge back and forth about it over on the What We Know wiki, and this might just be my reading into it but Holden seemed really exhausted by the end.

                  But supposing you wanted to have a Lunar Exalt using Illusions to pass himself off as a recently slain Dragonblooded, thereby taking her place. How difficult should it be for a Lunar to cover his charms to impersonate the charms of a Dragonblooded? Or the Elemental Banner of a Dragonblooded?
                  If you'll permit, I think you're coming at this from the wrong angle.

                  The question isn't "impersonate the Charms of a Dragon-Blooded." It's "do enough of the same things the Dragon-Blooded did that it appears I am one."

                  Except for the most studious of savants, people who have studied the flow of essence all their lives and can probably detect its ebb and flow through the body of an Exalt just by observing them, most people can't tell what Charm someone just used, just the effect it had. So as long as the Lunar doesn't flare, people are going to assume that no matter what they do, they're using the essence and Charms of a Dragon-Blooded, because they think that Lunar is one.

                  Is the Dragon-Blooded renowned as a skilled artificer? Hey, great! Lunars probably have their own Charms for that, so you can impersonate her no problem; just finish all her projects. Nobody will be able to tell you used Lunar Charms and not DB ones. Are they a skilled bureaucrat and logistician? Lunars got Charms for that as well. Lunars can use their own essence to achieve a lot of the same output the Dragon-Blooded got, and it doesn't matter at all they aren't using the precise same Charms, because nobody will be able to tell.

                  It becomes trickier in some areas. If they're someone who loves to use Elemental Bolt a lot, her companions are going to wonder "why isn't she using Elemental Bolt anymore" not because they can tell the essence inside the Dragon-Blooded is forming itself into the patterns of the Elemental Bolt Attack, but because that particular Charm manifests in the form of hurling a giant bolt of whatever at someone and Lunars cannot actually do that. If they were a master martial artist, people are going to be like "why is a master of the Even Blade Style beating people to death like some thug in a tavern brawl?" if the Lunar isn't a master of that same style, because Martial Arts Charms are known as Charms and instantly recognizable. If they had a bunch of synergy charms, her friends may be like "why aren't you harmonizing your essence with ours anymore? Are you sick? What's going on?"

                  So the answer to this question is really "it depends on what people expect from the Dragon-Blooded they're replacing." It might be trivially simple to impersonate them. It might be nigh-impossible.

                  "SEX NOVA is the kind of person who, after being chosen as the divine champion of the god of heroes, decided to call himself SEX NOVA."
