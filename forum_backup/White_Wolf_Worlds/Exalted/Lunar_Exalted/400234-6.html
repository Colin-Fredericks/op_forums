                                    I am reposting this from the Ask Dev's one question thread, hoping to move the conversation here
                                    Originally posted by Sunder the Gold View Post
                                    Hide-Toughening Essence is the Lunar taking his flesh and making it harder. Claws of the Silver Moon is taking his natural weaponry and making them more vicious. Flowing Body Evasion is taking his body and making it more graceful, flexible, agile. In all ways, the Lunar is strengthening his raw power rather than amplifying the skillful results, as a Solar would.
                                    And if it's glowy and magical but has no physical change, then it is not shapeshifting, not physical, not visceral, and as it is none of the three, it is not lunar.

                                    claws of the Silver Moon should have a physical effect-you hands should well twist into claws, or the natural weapons of your animal form should be noticeably more viscous-a tiger's claws become longer, a squid's tentacles longer and with sharp barbs.

                                    That is more implied by how boring animal shape shifting becomes when you can always slap wings, fire-breathing and armor on whatever shape you happen to be wearing right now, as opposed to needing to shapeshift consecutively into something with wings, something with fire breath, and something with armor, or at least one creature which is supposed to already have all three.
                                    Chimera Exist. Chimera are combinations of traits from various animals. Theoretically, a Lunar would become a Chimera by grafting parts from one animal onto their spirit shape. beign able to temporarily manifest parts from an animal in human form or in the form of an other animal would be a logical prerequisite to such permanent alterations to the spirit shape.

                                    Alternatively, the ability to do mix and match parts could be an option chimera could have but other lunars do not, derived from their already mix and match spirit totem.

                                    I see no reason to drop such a thematic abillity-"manifest animal derived mutations" just because full changing ids going to be better. Especially since Lunars are supposed to be the most versatile Exalts

                                    And I don't see a strong need for the ability to manifest your totem's bear claws in your human form when you could just use your Deadly Bearman Transformation. Or Claws of the Silver Moon. Possibly a Tiger Style martial arts Charm, if claw-strikes in human form is very important to your character's image.
                                    Deadly beast man should be a broadsword, not a scalpel-turning into a hulking man-beast is really the kind of thing that's only good in a situation that requires physical action and and does not require subtlety.

                                    turning into a bear to get claws is also the kind of thing that will screw you if you need to be subtle.

                                    One option to manifest animal mutations offers option to have claws while maintaining subtlety.

                                    I'm an aspergic individual. This means that I can't imagine how others will react to situations. This has led to me accidentally offending someone, and has also led to me being less clear in statements than I thought I was. I'd like to apologize, and ask that others don't get mad, but just ask me to reword or clarify. Thank you
