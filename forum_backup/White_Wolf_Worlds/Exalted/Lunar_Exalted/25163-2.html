                                                    I feel like a broken record every time this topic comes up, but eh. I imagine some revised form of Lunar Blood Shaping Technique, Insidious Lunar Transformation (voluntary and involuntary polymorph respectively), Burgeoning Wyld Affliction (temporary/permanent mutations to others), and Flesh-Sculpting Art (combination healing/disguise) will be back, and hopefully expanded. Also made more easily accessible and more potent, in places (upgrades/repurchases for longer effect durations alone would have been nice).

                                                    Transforming other people just seems a solid fit for the kind of primal mythic space the devs have talked about for Lunars. I can just also see a case against, depending on where the splat is headed. If one of the big EX3 Lunar theses is a variant on, "No, Lunar powers don't directly magically change the world around them, but man can they change themselves in such impressive and multitudinous ways: they're not short on options for changing the world around them in huge and amazing ways," then I'm not sure those powers will fit.
