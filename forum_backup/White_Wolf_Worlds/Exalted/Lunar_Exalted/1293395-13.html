        As an example of how a build can develop over the course of a game here's my approx 150xp Full Moon from Wizard of Oz's 3rd ed Lunar game. Focuses primarily on strength-based fighting with a huge soak as backup and a final trump card of Mountainous Spirit expression if needed (yes I know as written its not that great, I just like the idea of a 20 ft tall angry walrus man smashing things).

        He's an icewalker from the coastal regions north of the Haslanti League who was exalted while trying to prevent the destruction of his village at the hands of dragon blooded led mercenaries. Defeated and left for dead, he was rescued by a group of silver pact lunars, tattooed, and spent ten years searching for the ones who wrecked his home before being sent to join the rest of the party at the court of a northern king. His moonsilver and ivory Grimcleaver incorporates the shattered pieces of his broken tusk into its blade, although it has yet to awaken any evocations due to only being forged a few years ago.

                    Originally posted by Keelbreaker View Post
                    So what sort of things would you change/modify if you were going for a similar concept?

                    Because I would be interested to see what someone elses take on the same idea would be.
                    I'd consider looking for ways to get light/medium artifact weapon stats that work with steel paw style or deadly claw blow. It's not a huge thing, but I'd worry that the current build has exceptional withering damage (I think 24 base), but merely good withering accuracy (looks like 12 base). It may be that natural weapons+brawl is helping there, but it doesn't look like you have access to artifact quality natural weapons, and don't have maxed-out brawl like you do melee. OTOH, your stats are good enough that your accuracy isn't bad, and sacrificing damage would be a drawback.

                    The splitting of combat style (having dots in melee, brawl and thrown, having an artifact weapon and a hybrid body transformation that grants natural weapons) is spend resources on a fair amount of stuff that doesn't aid your primary combat style. OTOH, it does help you if you end up in a situation where melee isn't ideal. I think lunars don't have any great charms for dealing with being disarmed or at a range as a Melee user, so grabbing a few dots is possibly an efficient way to deal with that.

                    This thread, and my thoughts, have focused more on optimizing shapeshifting than charms, and I think Lunar charms have less overpowering effects than solars and less useless-seeming effects than Dragonblooded, but it looks like you have most of my favorite strength and stamina combat charms. Little in the way of social and utility charms, but a few.

                    In general "Deadly Beastman Transformation, Strength 5, Stamina 5, either Dex 5 or the dex replacement charms, artifact weapon and armor or charms that grant these, attack ability 5+specialty, defense ability 5+ specialty" is a very strong starting point for basically any charms to be added to. I'm always tempted to use a martial art on these characters, since it isn't competing with native melee charms or animal form, but there aren't any martial arts that seem like a great fit for the character.

                            As Olaf stands now with Manipulation 3 Socialize 2 you should have Guile 3 reather than 2.

                            Originally posted by Keelbreaker View Post
                            So what sort of things would you change/modify if you were going for a similar concept?

                            Because I would be interested to see what someone elses take on the same idea would be.
                            Since he already has Cha 4 changing him to Cha 5 Man 2 Per(F) 5 Wits 3 and grabbing Arrogant Lion Bearing (use Cha for resolve) and Unwavering Predators Eye (Per for join-battle) could be a thing if you're not super interested in any more Cache charms than you already have, (walruses migrate https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walrus#Migration so you can grab the navigation charms as Per with totemic).

                            You could drop thrown or stealth from 2 to 1 to increase socialize to 3 to keep Guile at 3.

                            Beast-Form Empowerement or Chimera-Soul Expression are strictly superior to Terrifying Ogre Alteration (6 dots of mutations vs 4, whenever you are in Hybrid Body vs only when you are also in DBT). and would let you get Unusual Hide 5 and Carapace if you droped the piercing of the tusks.

                            With how many -2 and -4 HLs you have Relentless Lunar Fury would also be nice to have to still be a functional combatant at lower HLs, if you later adder Pain Tolerance with lunar xp you'd never take more than a -1 wound penalty.

                            That's 3 new charms so I'd drop all of Impenetrable Beast Armour, Durant Quicksilver Adaption, and Invulnerable Moonsilver Carapace, because they are not really very good charms. If you wanted to go Deadly Beastman + Mountainous Spirit + Invulnerable Moonsilver Carapace you'd be down 30 motes and 3wp before the fight even starts.

                                With their anima having been clarified to apply to normal attack rolls, No-Moon shapeshifting sorcerers are no joke in a fight.

                                I was making a character and with a fairly low investment in actual combat charms and abilities you're a serious combatant.

                                With a core of:
                                _______

                                Dex 5

                                Athletics 3 (Specialty: Animal Forms)
                                Brawl 5 (Specialty: Natural Weapons),
                                Hearts Blood 1
                                Medium Natural Weapon (Tell) 4

                                and

                                Quicksilver Second Face
                                Finding the Needle's Eye
                                Ox-Body Technique

                                any 4 mental charms (you're a no-moon you were 100% getting this anyway)
                                Terrestrial Circle Sorcery (ISoB control)
                                _______

                                You get parry 7, with -2 to enemy attack rolls at iconic (RAW only solar/DB archers and lunars can get around this penalty, plus potential enemies that dont rely on sight)

                                Turning into a Hippo with ISoB you have:

                                Combat Movement: 12 dice (from +3 Hippo speed bonus)

                                Soak/Hardness: 19/6 + essence

                                Hippo Bite 12 dice (19 Damage, Min 5, ignore 4 + extra success soak, inflict [extra success] crippling penalty with decisive attacks)
                                _______

                                All this for 4 commited motes, reflexive or day-long effects, which is amazingly mote and action efficient compared to what you have to commit to artifact weapons and spend to activate MA forms which require far more chargen/xp investment to access

                                With combat competency so easily taken care of you can dive into all the mysticism, knowledge, scrutiny charms and non-combat spells you want while still kicking butt in a fight.
