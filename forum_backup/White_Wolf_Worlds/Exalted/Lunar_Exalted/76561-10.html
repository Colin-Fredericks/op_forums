                The world is big. I dislike how Exalted is constantly reduced ; this is part of my major issue with splatism. I feel significantly more geographical variety should impact Exalted relations. Obviously, the Inner Sea and the Near Threshold are going to have significant cultural footprints.

                Beyond that, it's been more than a thousand years. It strains credibility to the point of absurdity to suggest that the Lunar Exalted have been soundly defeated for this long by a race of super-soldiers and the notion to... I dunno... ALSO use super-soldiers, has never occurred to them.

                It also involves accepting the idea that somehow, the Dragonblooded all declared allegiance as one big 'team'... which is absurd. Clearly, many Dragonblooded groups and organizations would have had powerful incentive to support the old system and to fear an accounting in the new ; yet, we accept as given in 2e that all of these people are now dead by and large, and only Realm-loyal Dragonblooded remain.

                A new Dynasty wouldn't be at all difficult to establish, but blood purity probably wouldn't factor very heavily into it. The truth is, that's not important, really... it's a nebulous game attribute in any case.

                The world is big, again. I will personally be pretty surprised if some Dragonblooded families aren't associated with the newly empowered Lunar faction in 3e. Don't take this to mean I think they ought to approach the sophistication and majesty of the Dynasty, of course... being the 'lackeys' of Lunars has powerful negatives as well as positives. But certainly, I fully expect that Dragonblooded will face Dragonblooded in the conflicts they have with the Realm ; in fact, DB vs Lunar-Loyal DB conflicts will probably outnumber actual Lunar vs DB conflicts by several orders of magnitude, just owing to simple numbers.

                                                        Originally posted by Fitter Happier View Post

                                                        Ultimately, I simply took issue with your characterization of the Wyld Hunt (as separate from the Realm itself) as the defining factor for the current place of Lunars within the setting.

                                                        Because it ignores a whole lot. Who originally drove the Lunars beyond the world? Well, the combined might of the Shogunate backed by Heaven, of which the Wyld Hunt was a part. Who keeps them out there? The Legions, the All-Seeing Eye, the Realm Navy, The Realm Defense Grid-- The Realm! And yes, the Wyld Hunt is part of that (including, as an addendum and a sidenote, random groups of Outcastes who band together to gank a Lunar who gets too close to their territory. And Lookshy).
                                                        Oh! Okay. Now I see why we're disagreeing.

                                                        The Wyld Hunt I have been discussing in previous posts isn't a "thing." Rather, it's an ancient tradition in which Dragon Blooded get together to go kill whatever they have labeled as Anathema. That pretty much always includes Lunars and Solars and can often include other things.

                                                        When the Realm's Legions go after a Solar, like the Tepet Legions did against the Bull of the North, that can be a Wyld Hunt. When Lookshy sends it's forces after a dangerous Lunar encroaching on their territory, that can be a Wyld Hunt. When a group of Immaculates get together to travel across the world in order to kill someone the stars say will Exalt, that can a Wyld Hunt. When a half-dozen Outcastes get together, get drunk and decide to go stab the nearest thing in the face while screaming, "ANATHEMA!" that can also be, technically, a Wyld Hunt (albeit a pretty shitty one and probably not a good representation).

                                                        Everything you listed above - the All Seeing Eye, the Navy, the Defense Grid and the subsection of the Immaculate Order called the Wyld Hunt... all of that can be a part of a Wyld Hunt. That's why you have small Wyld Hunts (just a few Dragon Blooded, or indeed on some occasions, just a single Dragon Blooded) and you have massive Wyld Hunts that involve dozens of Dragon Blooded or even entire Legions.

                                                        That's why I said that if there was no Wyld Hunt (and by Wyld Hunt I meant an ancient tradition of Dragon Blooded getting together to go kill Lunars/Solars and other stuff), and Lunars weren't hunted, harried and killed by Dragon Blooded for the crime of being Anathema, then Lunar society would be quite different. I can't really think of any other single thing that would change their society more than that, which is why I feel the Wyld Hunt (as a Creation-wide tradition that started during the Shogunate) has so heavily defined the way the Lunar Exalted exist today.

                                                        Due to the tradition of the Wyld Hunt, even Lunars who are far away from the power of the Realm still have to worry about Dragon Blooded creating Wyld Hunts killing them if they draw too much attention to themselves.

                                                        *EDIT* So in short, I'm discussing the Wyld Hunt as an ancient and wide-ranging tradition, institution and concept held among many of the Dragon Blooded of Creation - a tradition that crosses national boundaries, religious ideology and creeds, not as a group of individuals that you can point at.

                                                        Do you maybe see where I'm coming from now?
