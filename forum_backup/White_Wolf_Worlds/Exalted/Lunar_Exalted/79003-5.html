                            DBT is actually quite compatible with weapons in 2E/2.5E, and this is a big part of why it was a no-brainer whenever combat broke out. That said, I could definitely see 3E's DBT being less of a binary "Natural Weapons vs. Manufactured Weapons" since that'd just pigeonhole characters along different lines. In human form, you have access to Evocations and certain Techniques and the like; in War Form, you lose those but gain all the benefits you'd expect of claws like meat-cleavers or a sword the size of a surfboard backed by enough strength to chop an armored man in two like a head of cabbage. More of a stance system where you shift moment-to-moment depending on your immediate needs than a painfully-straitjackety charbuild dichotomy.
