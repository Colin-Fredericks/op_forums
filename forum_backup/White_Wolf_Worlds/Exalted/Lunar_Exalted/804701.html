    Uniquely Lunar Charms

    01-09-2016, 05:22 PM
    Okay so in the hierarchy thread the "Solars are the best at anything, but other exalted are the best at being other exalted." thing was brought up. I think Sidereals are really good at this. Solar dodge charms let you dodge an attack, Sidereal dodge charms let you move a city around. Solar ride charms let you make an epic and unstoppable mounted charge, Sidereal ride charms let you turn a mortal into a celestial dragon and bomb around on it.


    Solar charms are technically better at doing what their ability wants you to do, and Neighborhood Relocation Technique isn't exactly going to help you against an orichalcum daiklave to the face, but it's cool and powerful enough in it's own way to really sell people on Sids. Like if you had a friend who'd never even looked at a non-solar charmset and he wanted to know why you'd ever play a Sidreal archer when you could play a Solar archer, you could point to the fact that Sid archery lets you counterattack with a bow and fire things like rain and love and bountiful crops. Yeah, you're not a Solar archer, you'd lose in a fight against one, but you can do crazy awesome stuff that Solars would never dream of doing.


    Sadly, I haven't had the chance to play many Lunars, 2-3 at most I think, and at least one of those was for exactly one intro session. So basically what I want to know from people who've played tons of Lunars, what Lunar charms out there are like that? Or what Lunar charms would you like to see like that in the future? DB charms would also be welcome because I often find that unique DB mechanics are actually Solar mechanics with a bad, sometimes non-existant "No srsly guys, Dragonfly Finds Mate is totally a DB charm and not a Solar one. Honest." paint job.

          Lunar charms are meant to be more versatile than solar charms - at the same time they're not as cost effective. I like the charms from Glories: Luna as really good examples of this.

          Charms like River Reflects Desire and Dreams and Expectations Stance, which makes an onlooker think the lunar is... something else, or something he expected, even when the lunar isn't. It's not a disguise charm that will disguise you as someone specific, but it can get you into damn near any place if you use it wisely and intelligently. A solar disguise charm would let you disguise yourself as something specific, and do so very well - the lunar one can effectively change who you appear to be at the drop of a hat.

          Similarly, Flesh-Sculpting Art is a really iconic lunar charm.

          Do you need to interogate someone? Just begin molding one of their legs into the form of a kitten and say "I can undo this... if you tell me what I want to know"

          Do you need to fix a wound? Just patch it up

          Grow three feet of hair? Pull it out?

          Regrow an arm? A touch of the wyld and a bit of willpower.

          Its a charm with endless possibilities - or barely any, if you're not the creative sort.


          Argent Weaver Art is the same story: Officially it just lets you go all spiderman and make string spun of lunar essence.

          But you can make webs, rope, tie things up, tie people up, lash things together, snare people, lasso people, retrieve your weapon. Think of all the things spiderman can do with his webbing... this is now but one of your tricks as a lunar.


          Implausible Lunar Panoply. It might take an hour to stash something, but you can retrieve it in an instant. A grand pile of jade? An implosion bow ready to fire? A guild wagon? A house? A bomb about to explode?


          Solar charms are really good at what they do - but can't be used outside of what they're meant to let you do. There's a solar larceny charm that lets you walk through doors, only doors, not windows, not walls, only doors. A lunar would just turn into a fly and zip over the city wall, or as a flea go through a lock.

          IMO lunar charms don't need to be better - one just has to think differently when using lunars

              The most uniquely Lunar Charms are the ones that give you partial transformations. Hybrid Body Rearrangement is the wrong way to go in adding mutations; Lunar Charms should mostly resemble mutations but be much more effective, and normal mutations shouldn't be the things they mess around with. First edition had about half of this, with the other half being tied up in a botched execution that wanted to have a difference between tentacles that did bashing and tentacles that did lethal, among other deep issues. Illusion are generally to be avoided.

              I wouldn't count Flesh-Sculpting Art as the most iconic image of Lunars healing others. I think that should be Lick Wound instead, only with better hypothetical mechanics. FSA is really rather deep in Abyssal or Liminal territory.

                  I said this a long time ago, but I feel that a big part of the problem isn't just the lack of uniquely Lunar charms; the problem is that the basic Lunar narrative is very close to the Solar one, in terms of how they Exalt. Going over the types of Exalted and how they Exalt:

                  Solars are ordinary humans, wandering the world, who are uplifted by their patron out of the blue for excellence.
                  Dragon-Blooded inherit their power.
                  Sidereals are fated to Exalt from the moment of birth and are swept up to Heaven shortly after (or before) they Exalt.
                  Abyssals are chosen at the moment of death and have the option to accept or refuse.
                  Alchemicals are made (and therefore, generally, tightly bound to the city that made them, at least in terms of their backstory.)
                  Infernals have their failure thing, and have to choose to accept, but much more importantly they're swept off to Hell almost immediately.

                  All of those are very unique and lead to very different stories.

                  Then you have the Lunar one, which is basically "like the Solar one, but for a very, very subtly different kind of excellence." (And even that isn't really distinguished; Solars can be chosen because they're great survivors, too.) While Lunars have a bit more infrastructure than Solars, they're still the second-most infrastructure-light type of Exalt; at least as I've generally seen them used, the Lunar elders don't have the dramatic impact on them that the Realm, Heaven, Hell,or the Underworld has on DB, Sidereals, Infernals, or Abyssals (and let's not even get into Alchemicals.)

                  And while the game sometimes tried to make the Wyld the Lunar 'thing', it never really worked out, especially since Fair Folk are pushed fairly seriously as playable, probably more seriously than any other non-Exalt option.

                      Originally posted by webkilla View Post
                      Lunar charms are meant to be more versatile than solar charms - at the same time they're not as cost effective.

                      ...

                      Solar charms are really good at what they do - but can't be used outside of what they're meant to let you do. There's a solar larceny charm that lets you walk through doors, only doors, not windows, not walls, only doors. A lunar would just turn into a fly and zip over the city wall, or as a flea go through a lock.

                      IMO lunar charms don't need to be better - one just has to think differently when using lunars
                      In practice, at least half of all Lunar Charms aren't actually more versatile than Solar ones. The Stamina Attribute in particular is thematically indistinguishable from the Resistance Ability, enabling all of the same kind of Charms and very few different concepts.

                      However, the Lunar Charmset IS structured very differently from the Solar one because of the Attribute focus. A Solar has to learn Brawl AND Athletics Charms in order to pick up giants and hurl them across battlefields. A Lunar can find all of that in a SINGLE branch of Strength Charms.

                      A Solar who wants to be perceptive has to buy up various different Abilities (Socialize, Investigate, Medicine, Survival) to roll with his Perception, and the Charms for each of those different abilities, but a Lunar only needs to invest in the Perception Attribute, which likewise contains every single possible Lunar perception trick.

                      And that's before introducing limitations that Solars don't worry about. Lunar effects need to be more internal, with less outright transformation or conjuration of external objects, and Attribute Charm themes need to be limited in ways that Ability Charms don't.

                      For example, Clay-Wetting Practice can't return in its original form because the concept of "fuse Essence into an object, then mold it like putty" isn't limited by Dexterity in the way that Glorious Solar Saber is limited by Melee. Obviously, GSS can never be taken to the point of conjuring up armor or horses, because all following Charms still have to be Melee Charms. But what would stop Charms following CWP from exploring the concept of "the Lunar injects larger amounts of Essence into the world to reshape it according to her visions"? That concept doesn't contradict anything about the Dexterity Attribute, so the limitation needs to come from Lunar themes.


                      Now, Flesh-Sculpting Art and Clay-Wetting Practice can still can work, by playing to Lunar themes. For example, feeding your blood to a patient blurs the boundaries of "internal / external", and introduces the limitation that improving your effect upon the world requires you to work with the natural healing process of a living creature, to use actual Medicine actions and dice-rolling rather than performing completely magical blessings, and to bleed. That's a lot of barriers in the way of "wish really hard to turn a mountain into a palace". Likewise, I would have Clay-Wetting Practice require you to spill your blood on once-living materials [wood, bone, leather] in order to improve your Craft-based repair or manufacturing rolls... not because these materials instantly shape themselves according to your will, but because you're reanimating them and enabling them to "heal" themselves as you put them back together.

                      And notice how very similar the Lunar process of healing and crafting seems, in those suggestions? That's the nature of Lunar themes and Attribute-basing working together to create an entirely different Charm tree structure than the Solars have. Solar Craft Charms and Medicine Charms are very firmly separate, but for a Lunar, they're easily all just Intelligence Charms.

                                          One of the things that seemed to be touched on in 2nd ed DB charms is more cost effective charms in exchange for them being conditional. The best example off the top of my head is comparing DB's Spirit-Detecting Mirror Technique, with the Solar's Spirit-Detecting Glance. SDG at 3 motes lets you sense dematerialized beings in your vicinity unconditionally, while SDMT uses only 2 motes, but only allows such detection through reflected surfaces.

                                          I really like that distinction, that while DBs are powerful much of their capability has limitations that need to be worked around, whereas Solars just hit the "yes" button and if need be, throw motes at the problem to yes even harder.
