        Well, I found it a bit odd that...

        In playtesting, the devs (at that time) said they found that MA was really, really powerful when combined with multiple styles.

        So they made players buy each ability seperately, at great expense (I mean, to get all the charms it's 19xp if favoured for each second style, plus the cost of buying the charms themselves. That's a lot to not even get more dice). And you have to buy a 4pt merit (which you have to buy whether you're combining styles or not).

        Also, in playtesting, the devs (at that time) said they found that people didn't want to buy MA, because native charms were more attractive for the cost. So they gave people special XP which you could spend on MA (or sorcery), so you could buy native charms and MA. (And later let you use it to buy dots too.)

        This always seemed... a bit illogical to me, tbh.

        So, my solution would be to a)ditch the Merit, and b)give a steep discount on going into other styles (making it free, like it was in 2nd ed, might be a bit too much).

        I'd also suggest c)don't let people buy MA with Solar/Lunar/Dragon XP.

        Lunar XP, after all, was one of the arguments for MA being the One True Build for Lunars.

        And certainly, I bought up all of Tiger Style basically as an easy side-thing which didn't stop me buying charms as well. NIce, but maybe is a bit too good?

        I should say though, the Solar XP do currently help to mitigate the cost of being a Martial Arts master. But giving people special XP to mitigate a cost seems over-complicated.


        I imagine the above opinions are very unpopular, however.

                                                I guess how I would try to phrase it is:

                                                Solars absolutely have a relative advantage in being great sorcerous king heroes. (This is how it should be as well. For all that I totally get that people don't want to feel that when they play their Solar sole wandering yojimbo Jedi Knight type they're doing something "wrong" since Solars "should be" leaders. But they've still gotta have that relative advantage.).

                                                If anyone is going to build the Tower of Babel, metaphorically speaking, it's going to be them.

                                                In the bigger picture, then, keys might be whether the other Exalted have the tools to come at them obliquely to exploit the gaps in all the ways in which their grand projects Tower of Babel (to extend the metaphor for glorious and great works generally), are not a good idea. Further to exploit all the gaps that being a lord of powerful magical civilization and master of abilities does not give them an absolute advantage in. Further again have the tools to appropriate up or twist or neutralize the products of the Solar's power so that they do not serve his will so faithfully and even that actually they how they operate in the world add to your goals and detract from his.

                                                Those are all ways that they can compete if you can't challenge on a direct basis in the terms that your opponent is setting them, and without being directly superior in an ability. Which the Celestials and the Dragonblooded absolutely should have (albeit as an uphill struggle!). Or put another way, in a case by case expression of abilities they should probably not win against a Solar, but in their ability to employ a winning strategy against him that avoids competing on an ability for ability basis, much more should be possible. (Of course this is not really at all what the measure of their success as splats should be about, but ideally should totally be possible).

                                                I don't have a lot of trust in how much we can work this out in a kind of "white room" setup. If we're going to try and "white room" anything, to do it with more tractable elements like combat. (So big part of why we end up talking about that?).

                                                        Originally posted by Ghosthead View Post
                                                        That said, I thought the meta was that Solars were already supposed to be very viable and comparable with Celestial Martial Arts against a Brawl / Melee specialist already in 3e? If so, they don't really *need* a power boost from SMA, and also probably shouldn't get one (as Solar with SMA overpowering a Brawl Solar is at least as bad as underpowering them!).
                                                        They don't need (and shouldn't get) a power boost relative to similarly top-tier Solar Brawl; but they can't fall too far behind.

                                                        This is a problem if people want SMA-using Solars to be noticeably worse at SMA than Sidereals, who themselves are supposed to be slightly weaker than Brawl Solars - at that point, even accounting for the versatility of SMA, it becomes tricky to avoid a situation where the SMA using Solar is actually noticeably weaker in a fight than a Brawl one, which goes against the idea that SMA is this big valuable deal for Solar martial artists.

                                                        There are various ways around this, but most of them require sacrificing some ground on the three points I mentioned above. Historically... Epimetheus is sort of right in the sense that SMA was always "Solars are flatly worse than Sideraels at it and also pay bazillion XP, so if SMA was remotely balanced no Solar would seriously consider learning it except for flavor reasons." Of course, the corollary to this is that there has always been unbalanced SMA (so the answer to "why would a Solar want to learn this" has historically been "well, these are actually notably stronger than any published Solar charms.")

                                                        That latter part isn't going to carry over into 3e (obviously, since it was an error), but without it, if you want the story of a Solar finding an SMA tutor to make sense, you need to grapple with these questions more seriously.

                                                        (Although I suppose it bears mentioning that 3e has introduced another splat that supposedly uses SMA as well as Sidereals do - we'll see what that means. It's possible that there's widgets or options we're not seeing that allow Sidereals, Getimians, and Solars (plus any other hypothetical "secondary" users like Solars) to all use SMA in their own ways without stepping on each other's toes. We don't know if Getimians use SMA the same way Sidereals do, just that it's at least arguable that they're as good at it as Sidereals are.)
