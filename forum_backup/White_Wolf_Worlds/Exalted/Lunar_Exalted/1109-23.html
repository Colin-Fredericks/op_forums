                    Q: Will Lunar Bond/Lunar-Solar dynamics be revised in any way? (babyseal)

                    A: I would not assume the first statement to be true. The answer to your question, though, is "could be." (John Mørke)
                    Emphasis mine on that last one.


                    But yeah, the Lilith/Swan model is potentially okay as one possibility for how it might turn out. Not something I enjoy, but hey, I don't object to it at all if it's a huge outlier. That it is the default is just... Ugh. People who actually like it as the assumed default are vanishingly few, and thank gog for that.

                    In other Bond-related notes, it would have been nice to see more variety in representative manifestations. When reading about it in the Core and Lunars Manual, I had no positive feelings for the idea when it emphasized "love at first sight," up until I read that "incredibly awesome friendship" was an option. That immediately made it not merely okay, but awesome. I had been interested in Lunars before I got the Core and that just made them cooler to me. Cue disappointment that this was never really explored. "Romantic/sexual attraction" is the assumption from most people, while mentioning other possibilities seems to actually make them pause for a moment before maybe saying, "Oh, right, I guess that's an option."

                    For occasions the Bond came up in a story in the books, consider a circle for "The Bond is important to this story" and a circle for "The Bond's context is not of a romantic/sexual nature." I'm not sure there's any overlap in that Venn diagram. Nor much at all in the second circle, to be honest.

                                                        Alright, this thread ran out of topics. Let's talk about this:

                                                        [Idea] Lunars Let In Balor

                                                        Originally posted by God_of_Awesome View Post
                                                        So, the Contagion is happening, every asshole is dying and everyone is scrambling for a solution. So, right here around the edge of Creation, at the border of Pure Chaos where most of the Lunars park their summer homes, one Moon Elder looks into the infinite possibility of the Wyld and has himself a vision, as Lunars are want to do in their old age.

                                                        So, it basically spells out the Sidereal dilemma to him: 1. Do Nothing, aka Not An Option. 2. Try to fight the Contagion on yer own, aka Yeah Good Luck With That. 3. Let in the Fair Folk, aka When Did We Exchange Reason For Madness!? Yeah, Option 3 essentially establishes that the infinite possibilities of the Wyld Folk will cancel out with the necrotic stagnation of the Great Contagion and only dissolve a whole heaping big chunk of Creation, but it will be an ALIVE Creation, especially if you play your cards right. Option 2, well y'all have tried option two already, you've been trying it for a while. It hasn't been working. Then again, Option 2 is at least not 'Well, let's let in something JUST AS BAD'.

                                                        So the Lunar aren't the Sidereal, they don't gather up in one place and then duke it out to see who's gonna do what. They are ringed all over Creation. So, instead, this vision just leaks out through the Silver Pact and ever individual Lunar gets to decide what to do. Thing is, most of 'em could go with Option 2. All it takes is a couple of 'em to go look at Option 3, 'Eh, better than nothing.' And suddenly you're up to your eyeballs in Raksha.

                                                        So it gives Lunars something to do as a splat, something they did, something that utterly, literally, shaped Creation. Well, not quite as a splat, but individuals of the splat, emphasizing that the Lunars operate as roving packs of individuals bonded by circumstance and a desire to protect Creation. Or something.
