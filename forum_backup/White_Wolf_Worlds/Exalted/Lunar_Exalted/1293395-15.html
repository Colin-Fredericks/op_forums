                        Okay, that’s different. You’re wanting an actual crab shape not a puissant HBT/DBT crab person.

                        I gotta break it to you now, the extant crabs in the book are not the greatest, but I’ll do what I can.

                        1. You can Chimera Soul on 6 points of mutations to your crab body, so that’s Carapace 00 and Unusual Hide 0000. You can have Unusual hide 0 as a Tell mutation, but it’s only two points more of Soak. So this gives you 10 Armored soak (that stacks with armor) if you take the whole shebang. Large crabs natively have a Soak of 8, so that’s 18. If you were wearing Moonsilver Artifact armor, you have a base of around 28. (This is more than probably overkill)

                        *Maui Impression* You’re Welcome.

                        2.The Grapple side, there are a bunch of charms that enhance grapple, but I’ll let you sort them out. If you just want to *win grapple* all the time, I’d advise you be Crabzilla and take the Ess 2 charm Mountainous Spirit Expression which makes you Legendary size and you can auto win grapples against anything that isn’t Solar Supernal Brawl, another Lunar running the same suite or a Warstrider. (And even then good odds)

                        *Maui Encore* You’re welcome again.

                                        I am now building a Lunar Changing Moon that tries to be a hybrid of those two builds themes(but there are some nonbo's since m.a. cannot be used by the housecat)

                                        So the goal of the build is to:

                                        (at CC)Be social fighter/assassin.
                                        (at CC)Leverage human shapes for their ties

                                        Later I'd like to invest in:
                                        (future): Increase guile and stealth (Dex and Man charms)
                                        (future): increase/manipulate join battle (perception charms)
                                        (future): get instill love rolls upon being intention read
                                        (future) Copies and fakes (dex swarm and app mirror charms)
                                        (futrure) project blame (and anima) on other characters (man charms)
                                        (future) Make hunts of animals useful through poisons (stam poison charms)
                                        (future): get wood dragons claw if ST allows for it to be used in Cat shape and with WVS charms).(perhaps get the invocations of spring razor as suggested in the black claw maiden build).
                                        (future) Build out to full WVS and BCS (and maybe laughing monster style, M.A.'s are so expensive though).

                                        Code:
                                        <spoiler> Name: Alryss(a) Concept: Social Assassin Spirit Shape: Housecat (or butterfly, I'm tempted to get the butterfly if i'd also get the fluttering wings poison effect AND if somehow housecat does not bring enough utility with my build) Anima banner: Mothra wings (google mothra wings gif) [B]Attributes[/B] str 1 X dex 5 stam 2 cha 2 X man 5 X app 5 X per 2 int 3 wits 3 The idea is to max dex man app to get as high m.a. pools with guile bonusses appearance for the resolve penalty. Stam at 2 is to get closer to the poison charms requisites, favoured perception for later buying it up and getting charms. Wits only at 3 because Open palm caress kind of makes having amazing JB rolls a nonbo. [B]Abilities:[/B] I'm really struggling to get all the points i'd want. Ath 1 Aware 1 brawl 1 (I know, this is atrocious for fighting in animal (including cat) shape. But I really do not know where to take points away to have this higher, also i'm focussing on battling in human shape so there's that too). dodge 3 Integrity 1 Investig 1 Larceny 3 Presence 3 Resistance 1 Socialize 5 Stealth 3 M.A. (BCS) 5 M.A. (WVS) 5 The 1's are hygiene abilities, the 3 are the important abilities with the 5's being abilities that either give guile or open up the m.a. charms. [B]Merits[/B]: Tell: Unusual Hide 5, Imposing Features 2, pheromones 1 (love, humans). This represents beautiful fur that also smells amazing and is very strong (not sure how to represent the unusual hide 5, maybe it's made of white glass or porcelin or something.) The idea here is to enhance my social combat in all (human) shapes. I'm not just in someone's shape, i'm the slightly more awesome version. Mentor (for the m.a.'s) 1 Martial Artists 4 [B]Charms:[/B] Black Claw Style up to form charm (4) White Claw Style up to Alehouse Memory Stance (3) Subtle Silver Delcaration and Moon's hidden face (to hide the mutations when not in use) Memory Drinking Meditation (get info with shapes) Agile Beast Defense and Bending before the storm (additional defense package, also prerequisites for cunning anglerfish decoy, which i'd like to pick up early). Skulking Rat Spirit: extra bonusses to stealth, both good for cat form stealthing and WVS, also prereq for cunning anglerfish decoy). Many Faced Moon Transformation: to aid with disguising, maybe to help hide mutations when in use (like imposing features, i'd like to have the manes be just extremely beautiful hair in human shapes). Nest Raiding Slyness (I'm between this and heart rending alure for my non-lethal shape taking. This one seems easier to satisfy early on and also can take animal shapes easier then the other one). I will start out with just my human shape and my spirit shape (cat) because I think the journey of getting more shapes is more interesting than already having the full package. </spoiler>
                                        Questions:

                                        1 Any suggestions to increase the build at CC, also are there any tricks I missed for a social assassin?

                                        2 I do not have game experience with 3rd edition yet so I'm not sure if i'm going over dice-caps or if there are other things that are nonbo-ing tremendously.
                                        - One thing I feel is I'm extremely invested in defence but that's partly also because I want to get to cunning anglerfish decoy. I could drop the dex package in favour of the manipulation line that leads up to cunning anglerfish decoy, but the dex package works in animals shapes and seems really good so I would only do that if the BCS and WVS package makes the dex package useless.

                                        3 I could not get to terrestial circle sorcery with my charms (four mental charms, I took a lot of perc charms initially, but it does not do so much at CC). This means I will not have wood dragon claw
                                        at the beginning. Are there alternative packages for offence that would work in human shapes as well as animal shapes and work with the m.a. styles?

                                        4 one route I could see is to drop the mutation package and the hide mutations charm package. I could still get to Essential Mirror Nature through manipulation charms (which would incidentily avoid shifting penumbra stance, which i find lacking). Not sure if this is smart since unusual hide is going to be our main source of soak in the early game and the social mutations are nice bonuses. It does open up 5 points of merits, 3bp's and two charms (although I guess I might want subtle silver declaration at some point anyway?). I could take artefact armour but it conflicts with my m.a.'s and silk armor is not moonsilver.

                                        5 one other thing i'm worried about is that i'll be quite combat focussed at cc and it will take me a while to get more utility charms up and running (but all of those will make my combat suite better simultaniously).
