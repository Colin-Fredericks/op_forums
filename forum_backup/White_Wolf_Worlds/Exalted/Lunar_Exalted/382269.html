    3E Lunars facts and speculation

    01-26-2015, 04:57 PM
    Decided to start this thread after some discussions we've been having in another lunar thread (So, if Lunars can't use MA in animal forms...). It got me thinking about what we really know about 3E Lunars. While the book may not be out for a while, it's always fun to gather facts and speculate!

    I'm admitted not the best person to start this as I haven't been following Lunar's that closely, but I'll try and update this first post with any confirmed facts that get gathered together. So what have I seen from the Devs so far:

    - Lunar's are going to be a "viable" (don't shoot me John) option in 3E. For those who played 2E, that's already a huge bonus!
    - Lunar's no longer live on the borders of Creation... or at least most of them don't.
    - The Lunar concept has less to do with the Wyld then in previous editions. This will be interesting as it raises a lot of questions.
    - Lunar abilities and charms are being balanced against their shapeshifting, so you can play a Lunar that doesn't do a lot of shapeshifting and still have a lot to offer. This is a big one as in 2E, you were either a shapeshifter or you were useless (maybe a bit harsh)
    - Animals are being designed from the ground up with Lunar shapeshifting in mind. Another huge plus, because in 2E animals seemed really "tacked on" and not all that interesting. One type of bird was hardly different to another. The addition of unique abilities for each animal will really add to a shapechanging Lunar's toolbox. Hopefully the system/variety of animals continues to grow giving Lunar's a lot to play with.

    Well that's all I can think of off the top of my head. I'll do a little digging and see if I can't add to the "what we know" section. Let me know if anyone has seen other messages from the Devs, and I'll add those as well.

    I'm really excited about Lunar's, which honestly is surprising. I came into Exalted in early 2E, so Lunar's have always sounded interesting but had crap mechanics. I'm especially interested in some of their new back story. If they've lost a lot of that "Wyld tainted" background, it generates a dozen questions. Why are there only 3 castes instead of 5 now? What causes chimerism, or is that still a thing (hope it is because they make interesting enemies)? What happened to them after the Usurpation if they didn't flee into the Wyld like in previous editions? Is Raksi still bat-shit crazy, and what is suppose to be the cause if not the Wyld taint? Needless to say, I'm almost as excited by the Lunar's as I am by a revamp of my Abyssals!

              Other Lunar stuff:

              -Lunars in animal forms may not be able to use supernatural Martial Arts if the form doesn't make sense for it. A Lunar in python form can't use Snake Style, lacking the limbs needed to emulate a snake, while being a snake. lulz. In gorilla form, maybe they can still use Snake Style, being humanoid in shape?
              -Lunars in DBT form will lose some or all of their access to the Evocations they've unlocked on their Artifacts. This has to do with how much of your power you're "wearing on the outside." Possible comparisons to Sidereals being the "worst" at Evocations but also the splat with easiest access to SMA, which is another "externally-worn" power. Very well may lead to a lot more in-combat shifting in and out of DBT to gain DBT-specific benefits and Evocation-specific benefits as the tactical situation merits. Which would be fucking cool!
              -Lunars are no longer solely dependent on DBT as the keystone of an optimal combat build. Statements were made that most Lunars will probably learn DBT when newly-Exalted since it's a quick, cheap boost to their power, but a good percentage will largely abandon it as a main tactic once they start unlocking Evocations or other, deeper builds which are competitive with DBT but more expensive to build to. Probably also because it's easier to pass off as a god or other kind of Exalt when you're not in Crinos, without sacrificing potency for doing so.
              -Lunars can learn the "techniques" of animals they turn into, which allow them to fight even better as that animal. Ex. the Tyrant Lizard's terrifying signature bite attack.
              -Lunar themes are going to be more tied to Gaia, as animals are of Gaia. Whatever this means!
              -Knacks as a concept, if they return, will not be there solely to differentiate vs Charms to prevent Eclipse Charmshare from learning them. If they come back, it might be for reasons of mixed-Attribute-prereq effect trees, probably in shapeshifting (i.e. tier 1 is Perception based, tier 2 is Stamina, Tier 3 is Wits, etc). It's an organization that would be really screwy in "true" native Charms and remind people of 1e Charmcloud but might make sense for shapeshifting powers.
              -I think it was confirmed that formlock is dead. Or at least enough authors hate it such that it probably won't be as terrible and crippling as it was before.

                          -Chimerism is now an accepted state, and simply refers to Lunars customizing their spirit shape. It is no longer a horrible malignancy that melts a Lunar into a big boring blob of crazy. It is mentioned this was to enable hybrid beast shapes for those who find a single animal too limiting. It has basically nothing in common with what it used to be.
                          -Lunars still have spirit shape, which may now play bigger roles than in past editions. The specifics aren't known, and haven't been mentioned in ages, but John once mused about a system of associated keywords that would allow a Lunar to rearrange where some Charms fall in the set and make them accessible at favored costs.
                          Expanding on the bullet point about Lunars no longer being tied down in the Wyld:
                          -More Lunars now have dominions within Creation, the largest of which is a big island known as the Caul. The Caul is warred over with the Realm -- it's an important trading spot -- and events have led to it being taken "off the grid" of the Loom of Fate. Lunars have managed to install a sort of Fate-analogue in its place. It is suggested this doesn't operate quite so well as the Loom, but specifics remain to be seen.

                                                          -Illusions are no longer a Lunar thing, nor do they have any sort of Druid-like powers for commanding/controlling reality. Their power is rooted more in command of the self and in shaping perceptions of themselves. The latter lends itself to taking on some effects that used to be illusions for Lunars, and the former doesn't necessarily preclude changes-to-oneself that can impact things beyond oneself (pheromones were a given example*).

                                                          * Not necessarily for use in neutralizing knock-out gas, but y'never know!

                                                          -Lunars will be able to learn multiple options for shapestealing.When last mentioned, hunting someone for heart's blade is still the default, but the devs want to give a lot of options. One of the old means, sleeping with someone, was explicitly noted; intimacy-related shape-taking (of some sort) is already in the plans; and hatewheel agreed with someone who noted that "physicality is a part of form taking, not so much observation."

                                                          -"[The Solar/Lunar Bond is] a whole big complicated thing with huge bearing on what the Lunars are doing, rather than a very simple brain lasso." Direct quote from Holden (okay, okay, not counting the brackets >.> ). The Bond is looking to be very different than it was. They've mentioned not planning to make it a mechanics-heavy thing.
