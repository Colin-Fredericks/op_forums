                                                            The only time we did have a romantic PC-PC bond... it didn't work. The guy playing the Solar wasn't interested, and he left the game because there were too many players (and his character married another Solar, an NPC) and the Lunar was very depressed that the man she felt a deep mythic romantic bond with had just not been interested in her.

                                                            Essentially, the romantic bonds, between PCs at least, feel a bit forced. With NPCs romantic bonds work better, but we still don't do all of them as romantic bonds. The other Twilight, Cid Hawkwind, is married to a Fox Spirit who slowly wooed him, while his Lunar Mate is a lesbian and has no sexual or romantic interest in him at all.
