    Lunar Charms you feel are missing?

    03-11-2019, 10:50 AM
    Are there any Charm ideas you feel should have been in the manuscripts, or are there some obvious areas of growth that you hope to see covered in Many-Faced Strangers?


    I think there may be room behind Dark Premise Duality for swapping into your Dark Mind quickly for battle (especially when activating Hybrid Body Transformation or Deadly Beastman Transformation), but being forced out of it if you are crashed or incapacitated.

    (Thus can the Witch of “Blind Prince and Liar Princess” completely change from a smooth-talking sorceress into a howling engine of terror and destruction. Absolutely no Manipulation and possibly reduced Intelligence in favor of increased Charisma and roaring-type Charms.)


    Robert admitted there's room after Radiant Basilisc Jewel for a Lunar to use their body-grown Hearthstone to power artifacts they're touching (or at least armor they're wearing / Warstriders they're piloting), though he feels that there needs to be more to such a Charm than just that.

                              Originally posted by Sunder the Gold View Post
                              The only thing that seems obvious to me is a charm for being able to talk while in animal form.
                              In addition to Many-Voiced Mockingbird Attitude & Glance-Oration Technique, you can also select Musical Voice as a one dot mutation when picking Perfected Hybrid Interaction, specify that it's the voice of an animal that can speak like a parrot/raven. You can then speak in any form by committing a single mote indefinitely.

                              I'd hope for more body horror charms. I'd like to be able to
                              1. Turn into a squirming chimeric monster like 2e's Chimeras, or the Thing from "The Thing".
                              2. Have body parts detach and move on their own, like the hand from the Addams family. Avoid an amputation by having that body part detach.
                              3. Offensive charms that melt/warp opponent's flesh like Flesh-Sculpting Art did for medicine.

                              While it's appropriate that Lunars can no longer transform into demons and the like with their native charmset, I was hoping for artifacts that would expand the scope of shapeshifting into this direction, a little bit like what Sozen did for Solars.

                                              I was glad to see that there were charms allowing the Lunars to attune to artifacts of their mates but thought it would have been cool if they had the ability to be resonant with any artifacts crafted specifically for them by their mates as well. Maybe it would require Moonsilver be a part of the construction somehow.

                                              I also had thought there should be some abilities based on some of the weirder things animals can do, especially insects. One idea in particular I had was to use magic to create more powerful illusions through sound or pheromones.

                                              Lastly, given the focus on how 'malleable' the lunar exaltation is, and the influence of their solar mates, it strikes me as plausible that their mates should be able to grant some abilities, or influence the expression of the Lunar's abilities (with their consent). For instance, a lunar who is close with an Abyssal mate might be able to have some additional benefit for using animal that has some association with death such as wolves or bats, perhaps being able to manifest some of the Abyssal's necrotic powers in their own way.

                                              Lunars with an infernal mate might be able to gain Malfean mutations or demonic forms.
