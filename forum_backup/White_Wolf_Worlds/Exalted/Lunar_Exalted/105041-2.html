                                                        I brought this up in the other Lunars thread, though it got buried under a fresh wave of lapidary minutiae.

                                                        Lunars have the core conceptual issue that their theme cannot be applied reasonably to all areas of character competence. By this I mean, Solars have the theme of "superhuman excellence". All of their Charms are built by saying "In what ways could you apply superhuman excellence to task X?".

                                                        Sidereals have the theme of "manipulating destinies both of events and of things". All of their Charms are built by saying "In what ways could you apply manipulating destinies to task X?".

                                                        Abyssals have the theme of "power over death and the dead". All of their Charms are built by sticking the Mirror keyword onto Solar ones. But going forward, their Charms are built by saying "In what ways could you apply power over death and the dead to task X?".

                                                        Infernals have the theme of "exotic powers derived from the creators of the universe". All of their Charms are built by saying "In what ways could you apply exotic powers derived from the creators of the universe to task X?".

                                                        Dragonblooded have the theme of "control over the elements, both the physical ones and their spiritual abstractions". All of their Charms are built by saying "In what ways could you apply control of physical or spiritual abstractions of the elements to task X?".

                                                        Lunars have the theme of "shapeshifting into animals and creatures of legend". Shapeshifting is an extremely broad tool, to be certain, but it also completely lacks ways to apply itself to things like crafting and lore, while in other areas like medicine its applications are limited to maybe one thing. It's also absurdly frontloaded, with pretty much everything you could do with shapeshifting given to you either at chargen or within a very small number of Charms and Knacks to unlock types of forms. Lunars end up having to make up for the missing areas of competence, and the need to have more than five Charms in their entire Exaltation, by being Silver Solars.

                                                        Shapeshifting is not a theme, it's a tool, and one that cannot carry the weight of an entire Exaltation on its back. Lunars need to have a theme in which shapeshifting is a thematically appropriate tool sometimes, rather than making it the crux of their existence. As it stands, though, I'm not sure I can even say Lunars should exist as Exalted at all, if their theme can't support everything an Exalt needs to be capable of doing.
