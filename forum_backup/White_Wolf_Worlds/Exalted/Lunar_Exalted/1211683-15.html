            Originally posted by Sunder the Gold
            I’d like to see them allowed to have the emotional strength to overcome their psychological challenges as well.

            ​Hmm, that's a fair point, although one that I'm still inclined to try and find reconciliation with some of my own interests.

            ​One thing that occurs to me is a subject that I brought up with the Dragon Blooded; how their Medicine Charm for dealing with Flaws modelling mental illness don't go in for the idea of removing such conditions all together, but of reducing severity and relieving symptoms. In the case of the most ancient Lunars, the ones that remember their old Castes, I'm feeling that there may be a place for them having managed to develop means of dealing with the trauma to their Essence but where the injury is still a part of them, and always will be; managed, treated, but present, and still lending an element of difference to them.

            ​That and the idea that having lived in the First Age can make it somewhat difficult for their younger peers to relate to them.

            ​Mostly, I'm just interested in the line of thought that came with the idea that the First Age Lunars are not necessarily at the top of the Silver Pact. Possibly in the form of the attention they have to give to their own issues meaning that they're better off deferring those responsibilities to others, maybe with people that have only ever known the Second Age having the stronger perspective on how and why to fight being more suited as the driving force in the group. Maybe it's just a numbers game, and the smaller number of First Age elders creates a higher probability that younger ones will include the more driven or capable.

            ​After all, I expect the number of ancient Lunars with Essence over 5 to drop off sharply; past a certain point, the difference between being a thousand years old and coming up on 3,000 might not be very significant in terms of what you're capable of doing and how much respect you command.

            ​I want to go through a couple of other things...

            Originally posted by Sunder the Gold View Post
            But for this Edition, Ma-Ha-Suchi’s depressive sulk might be as much because of some relatively recent setback or defeat or loss, rather than one long, unbroken pity-party stretching back to the Usurpation.
            ​On the one hand, I disagree with characterising his attitude as a depressive sulk, but do think there's potential in the idea that the onset of his weariness is a more recent occurrence.

            ​
            Originally posted by Sunder the Gold
            Magnificent Jaguar might be given a different reason for his long sleep, or maybe a different time-frame.
            ​I have thoughts on his prior backstory, but would need time to rally them.

            Originally posted by Sunder the Gold
            Leviathan might be actively fighting the Realm rather than just hiding away, or bullying a sunken city of mortals and Dragon-Blooded. He might have better reasons to be with-holding powerful First Age weapons from other Lunars besides “I don’t feel like actually exerting myself yet”.
            ​Yes, I definitely think that if Leviathan still has Luthe, he needs to relate to it a bit differently. What I was saying before about what I liked about him was primarily in the manner of thinking that he's adopted a lifestyle that makes him virtually a behemoth; a terror who haunts the shipping lanes of the West, and boe betide and Dynast vessels that cross paths with him. I had a sense that among Lunars, he's admired and venerable and somebody that they're quite happy to leave off on his own doing his own thing, because it's achieving a worthwhile result, and nobody is exactly strongly motivated to find out exactly how deliberate his current lifestyle is. That there are Lunars who are uncertain whether or not his living in the manner that he does constitutes something being the matter with him, and are not sure which possibility is more concerning; best just to leave him to himself.

            ​
            Originally posted by Sunder the Gold
            Lilith might have gotten over her abuse centuries ago and just decided she doesn’t give a shit about the Realm, choosing to remain a retired hermit out of preference, but still remaining connected enough to the world that she can hear about the return of the Solars, so maybe she might accept Lunar students who seek her out for training in return for favors.
            ​I have mixed thoughts on the question of whether or not Lilith's pains are something that should come from abuse (whatever form it might have taken). On the one hand, I wouldn't want to diminish the idea that it's perfectly acceptable for an Exalted character to have faced such, that this doesn't lessen them, or that abuse is something that is somehow beneath them. On the other, I would also see problematic connotations in things such as a highly prominent woman Exalt being one whose life story is strongly characterised by abuse, and possible unfortunate implications in the idea of a reaction to that in the form of a social withdrawal (as well as the matter of pigeonholing the answer for exactly what went on with her that prompted her retreat).

            ​It's a fine line, and one that would need to be given significant consideration.


            I have approximate knowledge of many things.

                                                        Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post
                                                        ​I would suspect an alternative method of venomous attacks from Snake Style.
                                                        Based on scenes from the movie and videos of real centipedes, I’m guessing Centipede Style will look like a venomous Tiger Style. A brutal, rush-down, head-on approach in contrast to Snake’s graceful evasion and quick strikes.

                                                        Centipedes use their many legs to rush and tackle their prey with great speed and force. After they latch on with their poisonous claws, they use the leverage of their long bodies and many legs to hold, lift, slam, and flip their prey to render them helpless to escape or counter-grapple, all while continuing to tear at them with venomous claws.

                                                        The Centipede Stylist in the movie attacked with confusing and fast movements that were hard to evade, and would clinch his victims with one arm to deliver deadly strikes with the other. He could also render a victim prone and barrage them with kicks to keep them down.

                                                        But...

                                                        We already have Tiger and Mantis for rush-down grappling that puts opponents on the floor and keeps them there.

                                                        We also have Snake, White Veil, and Black Claw Styles: Three venomous Styles already. Four, if you count Wood Dragon Style, which is venomous in spirit with its soul-striking attacks.

                                                        Poison is a weapon, and it is already well-represented with published Martial Arts Styles. I would like to see other weapons get at least as much attention as slashing swords and polearms and firewands got. Especially weapons that never got attention in previous Editions.

                                                        Such as axes.

                                                        Swords have had Fire Dragon and Violet Bier of Sorrows Styles, and now have Single Point Shining into the Void and Steel Devil Style, and they also had Even Blade and Five Dragon Style in a previous Edition.

                                                        Spears have had White Reaper and Golden Janissary Styles, and they also had Crimson Pentacle Blade, Lightning Hoof, and Five Dragon Styles in a previous Edition.

                                                        We currently and traditionally have more Styles that use hook swords, seven-section staves, and war fans than Styles that use hammers, and none that have ever used axes.

                                                        Conversely, what do you suppose was the most popular weapon in the world? A weapon which doubled as an indispensable tool of labor, and which was far simpler and cheaper to make than swords.

                                                        The humble ax.

                                                        If Swaying Grass Style is being included because Luna famously chooses slaves in bondage and because Lunars foment rebellion among slaves, then axes deserve Martial Arts representation as the weapon of choice for poor peasants and farmers and militias defending their homes from imperial conquerors, and also iconically used by raiding bands of barbarians and “sub-human” monster races like goblins, orcs, trolls, ogres, minotaurs...

                                                        formerly Tornado Wolf, formerly Inugami
