        Originally posted by The Revenge of TV Head View Post
        Yeah, I guess that is true. It's not likes a prominent mythological figure that inspired a number of Lunar characteristics that changed his size as well as his shape. Oh..wait.
        Changing his size wasn't really his core power, though. (I mean, I guess he changed the size of his staff a lot, but that was more of an artifact.)

        And like I said, there's some room for wriggle room or to build on your themes in ways that branch out. If a Lunar is a master of turning into a huge ape, turning into an even huger ape is a reasonable extension of that, in the same sense that eg. creating things from nowhere via Words as Workshop Method isn't particularly central to being a Solar but can be a reasonable extension of Solar Craft.

        So I wouldn't want to say Lunars can never shrink -- you might be able to squeeze it somewhere into their powerset -- it's just not a core Lunar thing. If Lunars have eg. a clone-myself charm, they might get an upgrade that creates a lot of really small clones to rush about in order to complete a task quickly, but it would be a peripheral sort of upgrade. But it would probably be designed in a way that would reflect the fact that it's peripheral, making it at least a bit hard to play "the guy who shrinks" as your Lunar, in the same way that even if Solars get WaWM, it's hard to be "the guy who conjures stuff from nowhere" as your main concept when playing a Solar.

            Hmmm... couple of things.

            ​My questions were intended as basically components of a larger question; answering "what do they offer players" in isolation to all of the rest was not answering my question, and does not produce a result I would find useful to the game.

            The other thing is repeating my assertion that the thing to offer players in writing setting locations is something to play within.

            At that last White Elephant, when I was asked to write up a setting, that was something I struggled to realise; how to write a place to set games, rather than one that might just be interesting to read about because interesting things happened there are long time ago.

            The idea of gifting somebody with a setting that provides readily accessible assets did not even occur to me, because it's not how I conceive of what should be offered to players.

            I assume that, first and foremost, players want interesting stories about people, so I was asking what Liliputians offer to fulfil that.
