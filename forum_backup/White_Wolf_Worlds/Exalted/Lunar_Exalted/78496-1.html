    Originally posted by SuddenThunder View Post

    Which would be great, if the advice on how to handle shape-shifting wasn't 'give some bonus successes'.
    I imagine that it will certainly help when animals are given more comprehensive features.

    Although I'll admit, comprehensive mechanical representation of a rat being "sneaky" strikes me as a tad excessive. I mean, unless people are actively looking for rats, you need systemic representation of that about as much as you need it for going about the street, or dressing as a servant. Or for a rat to get into a place, as much as you need the system for walking through a door (although a special Dexterity check to slip through a tiny hole would be in order).

    Originally posted by hippokrene View Post
    I'm going to disagree that turning into a rat is just as good as having actual stealth and larceny charms.
    Well, probably, but those are the breaks if Lunars don't get Stealth and Larceny Charms.

    Turning into a rat probably isn't as good as what a Solar could do, but it's better than somebody with no Charms at all, and you generally won't have to pay experience for it.

    Shapeshifting might not be as powerful as Charms, and a Lunar who doesn't use much shapeshifting is probably not necessarily less powerful than one who does, but it does go some ways to helping individual Lunars be versatile. A Lunar whose character concept precludes them placing much emphasis on combat Charms or Abilities can still turn into a tyrant lizard.

    Originally posted by hippokrene
    Turning into a rat doesn't let me open locks with a touch, walk through closed doors, make it so no one sees me, or allow me to vanish immediately while removing the memory of my being there from other's mind. It doesn't make sure that I leave no trace of physical evidence of my passing, or let me hear through walls (and no, rat ears don't let you do this), pick a lock even if I have no tools whatsoever. It doesn't let me see perfectly in the dark, suppress all sound of my movement, or automatically distract guards/servants/marks.
    Should Lunars do all of these? I mean, sure, there are some that feel suited for them, but others are already Solar tricks.

    In particular, are you still talking about Dexterity when you suggest removing the memory of your presence from somebody's mind?

    I have approximate knowledge of many things.

                                                        Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post
                                                        Should Lunars do all of these? I mean, sure, there are some that feel suited for them, but others are already Solar tricks.
                                                        In particular, are you still talking about Dexterity when you suggest removing the memory of your presence from somebody's mind?
                                                        I think they should be able to do most of those, but I think the reasons should be a bit more oblique. I like to think of Lunars as creatures of stories. Not creatures that tell themselves into stories, like the Fair Folk, but creatures that others tell stories about. They are urban legends, myths, and creepy campfire stories (and often more than one of each). Lunars are the monsters you never see; even in broad daylight what you see may be no more than the fin above the surface of the water. So yeah, they should be capable of almost anything (in theory), because even the most obvious Full Moon should have hidden depths that keep his enemies guessing.

                                                        The challenge is not finding the things they can do, but finding the thematic space to build those things so that they aren't just the hated "Silver Solars" with the same charms only worse/more expensive/more limited etc. Just because Solars have a trick does not mean that Lunars can't do the same thing, they are just going to need a different method of getting there.
