                                        so around one-half of Lunars probably possess Terrestrial and/or Celestial Circle Sorcery
                                        Okay, so there's two things here. First, I don't think we should assume all No Moons are sorcerers. It's pretty lame when No Moon players are supposed to be all about something that isn't part of the Lunar charmset anyway. And there's plenty of mental concepts that don't require being a sorcerer. You could be a genius hunter with a perfect awareness of your environment, a quick-witted trickster, a genius polymath who can turn their mind to any problem... being a sorcerer is something quite character-significant, and I don't think should be the default.
                                        It's also, in this edition, not something that's so easy to teach (I should know, my Owl-totem celestial sorcerer tried to teach his lazy, unfocused Fox-totem daughter a few times, and she just couldn't get it. Teenagers have no attention spans), so it's not like all No Moons can be forced through the five stations by their teachers.

                                        On the other hand, obviously they can get it (both the Full Moon and Casteless in the game I ran learnt it, the Full Moon had Celestial), which is why you said half might have it.

                                        But then we come to, well... just how many Exalts are sorcerers? Which is quite a good question.
                                        I mean, PC-wise, in my experience it's not common at the beginning (maybe 1 in 4?) but becomes more common as characters go on, and learn it from various sources. By the end of my game, 4 out of 7 characters had it.
                                        But is that just a PC thing, or something you could say more generally? Becoming a sorcerer is quite a significant character-building event... but obviously, for Lunars who've been around for a hundred years, that may well have happened to them.


                                        All of the existing First Age Lunar Exalted should probably possess both
                                        I don't think so. I think there'll be First Age Lunars who are all about brutal combat, or charm, and not about magical knowledge. I think it'll be common amongst Elders, but not every single one. And some will have Terrestrial but not Celestial, because it's just a small part of who they are.

                                        I've seen some very experienced Lunar PCs (I mean, essence 5, or 600+xp, etc) with Celestial sorcery and loads of spells, and others with just 1 or 2 spells. I imagine it'd be like that. They are individuals, after all. Some might have 100 combat charms and like 3 spells. Others, of course, will have loads of spells.

                                        I don't really see Tammuz or Lilith or Admiral Leviathan as having tons of celestial spells. It's just not their thing. But Lilith probably knows tons and tons of martial arts instead, because that's her thing IIRC (I might not, I never was that interested in her), not sorcery.

                                        dozens of spells for each, and have created hundreds of surviving workings since the Usurpation.
                                        Of those that are committed sorcerers (like Raksi), this seems pretty reasonable. And yeah, I'm sure there'll be tons and tons of workings. Maybe thousands, though that includes ones that aren't that important. Big significant ones (Celestial or Solar)... yeah, across the entirety of Creation, probably hundreds.
                                        Which gives STs plenty to work with, there's lots of interesting plots and societies you can do with left-over workings created by Lunar sorcerers in the past 1500 years, even before you get to the smaller number of ancient Lunar sorcerers who are still actually around.

                                        Oh, any word on the No Moon power yet?                                         I hope not. Believe me, as someone who played a No Moon celestial sorcerer in both editions, it is actually a rubbish, rubbish, rubbish power.
                                        The +1 DV is way better.
                                        I must have played him in... oh, 70 games? Not sure. Well, let me tell you, I used the mote-reduction power in precisely one game. One. And it wasn't that useful, it just saved me 4 motes.
                                        The edition change, incidentally, has made it marginally better, but not good enough to be a good power. I guess it's not total weaksauce, it's probably about as good as a Night's ability to ignore Essence dots of penalties to stealth attempts. But it's certainly nowhere near the level of a caste's headline anima power (like, say, the Changing Moon's amazing ability to disguise themselves as whoever for a scene, which is so good that I hope they get something else instead so people don't try to find excuses to make their mental character a Changing Moon).

                                        I'd like good powers this edition. I'd also like to not have anima powers that say "You are a combat sorcerer! Be a combat sorcerer! Why is your mental character not throwing multiple spells in a scene? What do you mean this is a social game?" It in no way helps me with the spells I like casting, like Floral Ferry, Travel Without Distance, Shadows of the Ancient Past, Celestial Banishment, etc.
                                        (I mean, in theory it could help with Celestial Banishment, I guess, but it never actually did in practice. If I wanted to banish multiple first circles, I just used Celestial Banishment to banish the whole group with a roll, and if I was fighting multiple Second-Circles by myself, I'd probably just leg it. Celestial spells are too expensive to be throwing out 2 or 3 in one combat, even with a 4-mote reduction.)

                                        Ideally, I'd like 3 powers unrelated to sorcery (I heard everyone will get 3 powers, though that might be wrong), as I feel it dictates that all No Moons ought to be sorcerers, and if you're not then you're a rubbish No Moon (charms that affect sorcery are fine; if your No Moon isn't a sorcerer, you can not take them. Also I thought the sorcery charms for the example Lunar in the Core looked cool), but if I do get a sorcery-related anima power, I'd rather have something like "You get Essence (minimum 3) sorcerous motes when you see the Moon, which last until the next Moonrise". You know, something I might use more than once in 80 games.
