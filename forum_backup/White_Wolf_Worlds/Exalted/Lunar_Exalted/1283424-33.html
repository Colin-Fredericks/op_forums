                                                            03-11-2019, 02:53 PM
                                                            Originally posted by YeOfLittleFaith View Post
                                                            The new write-up for Lilith is pretty good. Between returning to her original concept, and painting her retreat from the world as the ramifications of a demigod dealing with long and deep grief over an even longer-lasting genuine relationship with her Solar bondmate, her backstory is very emotionally palpable.
                                                            Could not agree more.

                                                            Folks tend to forget this but we have historical had next to no actual canon example of a healthy Solar Bond, and books are in part supposed to create and inspiration framework. Second edition doubled down on this so much that it made no pair but two in my memory anything remotely resembling something to be mourned (in large part due to their not being heavily built on), meanwhile I can think of at least a dozen First Age Solars who were rape-hungry monsters (which was weirdly enough not tied to the Flaw of any of the examples that got a stat block, making you wonder if Sol was purposely picking rapists). It made one splat monsters and the other victims as a default.

                                                            This new version of Lilith is a good indicator of the new direction for Lunars as a whole, she is very much the exception among the elders we have access to in her mourning while also telling the reader that at least some Lunars remember their time with their supposed soul-bound comrades well. The framing is also very different, with it being in the context of being worn-down by her grief after a long time with different agendas, lovers and causes before finally choosing to rest, which is a stark contrast to the beaten victim that fled Creation and cared little for her kin of past editions.

                                                            I'm sort of inclined to refer to the Lost Lenore trope from the way the recollections are referred to. Actually, depending on how you read her time as an Owl, I'd be sort of inclined to almost start looking towards a Don Quixote character (its reaching but its also fun).

                                                            I'd also point out that a good book sort of gives you a good reference for a wide array of things. Most of the Shahan-yas are thankfully disconnected from Solars altogether, some historical Solars had outright antagonistic relationships and the bond is really only shown in present terms in three Shahan-yas. Sublime from her line gives you a good Shounen-rival angle, Leviathan gives you a 'moved on' angle, while Lilith gives a 'this was a good thing I want back in my life' angle.

                                                            Which is a site better than past editions wherein the Solar Bond was shown to Lunar Players as rape, abuse and literal mind-wiping (recall that one Lunar that got turned into breeding octopus when his mate went extra-crazy?).
