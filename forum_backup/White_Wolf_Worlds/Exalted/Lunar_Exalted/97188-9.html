        The position that you're championing is one that I formerly found myself in back when this material was new and shiny and everyone was just getting out of the first edition Lunar torture chamber. After a number of years of digging deeper into everything, however, all the flaws began to reveal themselves. The organization was ungainly, with pointless prerequisites, fillers and of course, not much in the way of shapeshifting. There was no celebration of the game that Lunars had been born of. The meaning of the Spirit Shape and Tell was still left unstated. And, of course, there was Relentless Lunar Fury, which you had to take in order to get your "real" dice pool. Which was also so brief in duration that it hobbled the capabilities of the Lunars beyond belief.

        We all perfectly well understand what the book was trying to accomplish. And most everyone now realizes that it was far from the best way to go about accomplishing the task of making Lunars fun. It was an exercise in tick counting and using a repainted God Hand to temporarily be important. It was the system shrugging its shoulders about other potential forms and just not caring about how fauna was statted out, even though turning into animals is a rather noticeable feature of being a Lunar. And it also chained the Lunars to the Wyld even more viciously than first edition did, since most of their interesting, non-copied Charms had the rider of "must be used outside of where everything important and interesting is happening."

        Which is why many people in this thread are rejecting most of the theses of your posts. They simply just don't work as a comparison between Solars and Lunars. Even if there are certain small numbers of Charms that can just be shifted around a bit, like Righteous Lion Defense and Ox-Body Technique, by no means does it make it okay to make all Charms according to this scheme.
        I never said Lunars weren't a mess. For instance, your point about spirit shapes and Tells - on the headcanon thread, I've said that when I'm running, they don't even have spirit shapes and their Tells are very different. I know the legitimate issues people have with the written material.

        All the same. if you're trying to say that's the case for everyone who throws words like "Silver Solars" around, forget it. A lot of them have never even played a Lunar character, and it shows.

A Lunar is generally stronger than a Solar, - he has more strength, and his charms are based on strength - even though the Solar can actually apply his strength to martial arts and get better results.                                 No, because there's a difference between potential and application. This isn't my own idea; it's how 2nd Edition explicitly defines these concepts:
                                STEP TWO: ATTRIBUTES
                                Once you’ve defined your concept and selected your caste and Motivation, it’s time to assign numbers to various traits. First, assign scores to each Attribute. The Attributes define your character’s innate capabilities and aptitudes.
                                [...]
                                Physical Attributes define your character’s physical potential—how fast she runs, how much she can lift and how healthy she is.
                                [...]
                                Mental Attributes define your character’s intellectual capacity—how quickly she thinks, how keen her senses are and how smart she is.

                                STEP THREE: ABILITIES
                                Abilities describe what your character knows and can do, what he’s learned rather than his natural potential.
                                I'm using the word because I'm trying to stick to the vocabulary of the system. To say Lunars embody potential isn't saying they're the best at everything, because Solars by their nature embody peerless application of power and skill. If Solars' potential isn't technically as great as Lunars', it's at least immense; and in their application, they achieve things impossible for Lunars or anyone else to achieve. What Lunars are "best" at is simply raw innate power, possessing monstrous levels of intelligence, charisma, strength, etc. to draw upon. Thematically, that's because because those traits are all related to your innate characteristics, the things that define not what you've learned or can do, but what you are - and no one is better at defining what they are than Lunars, because that's basically the whole point of Lunars, isn't it? Lunars don't have the peerless excellence of Solars, but what they do have is an infinite capacity to redefine themselves. In that sense, they're about potential.
