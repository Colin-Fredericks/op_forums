                                            Beat to death, but I still want to comment on this.
                                            How does a Wyld Hunt corner Lunars?
                                            Depends on what the Wyld Hunt is supposed to be doing in the first place.
                                            New Lunar? (And there has to be some success in hunting new Lunars, otherwise there would only be old Lunars.) Since it is a race between the Silver Pact and the Wyld Hunt as to who gets to the new lunar first, I would guess the Hunt has a pretty good record of nailing newbies. (Also look at the 3e released fiction of Seven Devil Clever to see how the Hunt might operate.)
                                            Older Lunar? The Lunar must be doing something to attract the Realm's attention. Which means something is going on that the Realm wants stopped. Which means stopping whatever it is is the Hunt's goal, and bagging the Lunar is gravy. Older Lunars will more than likely escape, but at the cost of the Realm showing that it has power to control this area. Unless the Lunar's idea is to drag the Wyld Hunt all over Creation (and then they have to just get away, and still be able to be found, or the Hunt goes home), the Hunt has foiled whatever plot the Lunar was cooking up.
                                            And, in fact, a 1e story had a Dragonblooded looking at the opening of yet another battle with the Lunar Anathema, Sleeves of War (with whom he has personally crossed blades with at least once). Meaning that there has been several battles, and since the DB has survived them, I would speculate that most were Realm victories, with Sleeves of War using Lunar tricks to escape and raise another army. So yes, Lunars can escape after losing. But the Realm keeps winning battles.
