            Some thoughts on Lunar Cloning:

            1. Lunar battle group forming charms ought to be "I turn into a swarm of bats," or "I turn into a pack of wolves," or "I turn my hairs into a small army of angry monkeys." They shouldn't be "I make twelve more of me."

            2. To the extent that Lunars are able to clone themselves, we shouldn't be looking to the Solar "The Flash" charms for inspiration. We should be looking to the Mirror Flag charms! A Solar can create a multitude of strangers within herself; a Lunar ought to be able to divide herself into multiple people with different capabilities. Rather than inventing fake personalities from whole cloth, though, a Lunar should be using what she's already got-one clone might have her intimacy of love for her children and her Stamina charms, one clone might have her hatred for the Realm and know her Martial Arts style, etc. The difference between this and Yozi soul-heirarchy stuff would be that the Lunar loses the things she gives to her Clones, and gets them back when the clones are destroyed. I like the idea of a powerful lunar having trouble making a decision splitting into multiple people to argue about it. I like the idea of a Lunar clone who only has one or two charms and just one extra animal shape to change into as a low level antagonist for a mortal or DB party who can foreshadow the greater enemy of which he is part. I like the idea that a PC who is going to have to miss game for a session or two could say, "I'll split off a clone and give her three of my Medicine dots and my healing-your-allies charms to back you guys up without you needing to worry about my combat charms, and the rest of me will go do an offscreen sidequest while I'm gone." Committing parts of yourself to different character sheets ought to be a bit dangerous-I'd make PCs divide their Health Levels up between the clones as well, and the ones without the Stamina charms don't get the benefits of those charms, and a clone getting killed should give you those levels back but full of damage.

            3. I like the idea of clones left on their own too long developing their own intimacies and being reluctant to return to the main Lunar when their mission is done, and I like the idea of the main Lunar's player gaining the option of absorbing those memories and intimacies when they reabsorb the clone (because that generates more plot potential than just devouring them). I also like the idea of clones who don't know they're clones, so you can have someone turn in terror to someone they thought was their friend and be like, "Wait...Chen hates spicy foods. You're the Anathema, aren't you?!" and have the Lunar smile and say, "No, Shin. YOU are the Anathema." Lunar sleeper agents could be fun and add interesting dynamics to their cold war with the Sidereals. Sidereals having to murder relative innocents for the greater good is always a good idea, and Lunar games involving questions about what identity means in the absence of a fixed shape are also good ideas.

            4. I like the idea that you have a limited number of shapes to share between your clones. You don't make three identical copies of yourself, you have one that looks like you and one that looks like that guy you ate yesterday and one that looks like his dog which you also ate yesterday. Playing "which is the real me," is like playing "which cup is the bean under-" it's a con, and the real you is the fourth shape that's an ant slowly crawling away hoping not to get noticed in the kung-fu fray. Why disguise yourself as yourself when you have so many better disguises immediately available?

            4. Lunars shouldn't be able to create duplicate items. If your combat style is based on your cool artifact axe, the cloning tree shouldn't give you five guys and five cool artifact axes. I'm cool with clones not arriving naked, because while hilarious the first time that gets old quick, so I'm alright with the cloning charm equipping the clone at creation with suitable clothing and any items you have stored in Elsewhere that you wanna share. Maybe you give the Clone your two dots in performance and that artifact Lute you have sitting in Elsewhere and your Manipulation and Appearance charms: keep your cool artifact axe for yourself and send him off to seduce the local princess.

            5. Killing the original should kill all the Lunar's clones. Wyld Hunts should remain possible and credible threats against Lunar PCs who have the Clone Myself Tree, which means that Wyld Hunt becomes a traditional "hunt down Dracula because if we kill him it kills all his offspring," plot. Bonus points if a clone has turned against the Lunar and is your friend and you know that killing the Lunar will kill your new friend.

            6. THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT ONE: You know the whole, "extra actions are unfair," thing? The solution to that is that Lunar clones are NPCs. They're not bound to your will like a demon. You create them, and they have the intimacies and knowledge you give them at their creation, so they're usually on your side...but the longer they're separated from you the more they become their own people. You're weaker while they exist, and while they have a vested interest in your continued survival so they probably won't try to kill you...they also have a tendency to not want to get reabsorbed after they start building their own lives. Clones should be costly to create compared to swarm/animal battle group charms to discourage pumping out a ton of them in combat, and using the charm should be understood by all as trading some of your own power for some dots in Ally. I don't want people to never take these charms, so I'd say the Clone requires a committed mote cost and dies when you decommit the motes, returning your charms/HLs to you...but if only you actually physically reabsorb the clone in person do you also get their memories/intimacies. I'd be open to reabsorbing clones who have gone off and learned new things being a way to make an XP engine or get around training times kinda like Solar Teaching charms, which gives busy Lunars a reason to give up some of their power for long term clones.

                    Originally posted by Sunder the Gold View Post
                    So you disagree with Robert Vance that Lunar Exalted can or should have a Charm to become a battle-group?
                    I'm fine with them becoming a battle group (though I would prefer swarmform for that)! But if we let you make a dozen short term copies of yourself (which, Sun Wukong or no, I am not about, I don't like the feel), only one of 'em oughta have your fancy magic axe.

                    This axe thing cuts to the heart of why I prefer personae style clone charms or none at all. Anytime you duplicate yourself in a game that includes a semblance of balance, you run into the need for the duplicates to be weaker than you to balance them, with a sort of inverse ninja principle. A battle group of you isn't fifty full powered Lunar warriors, it's you plus a bunch of watered down mini-yous. If you're wearing the Crown of Thunders, you don't get to make a bunch of mini Crowns of Thunders, it cheapens the feel of an irreproducable legendary wonder to make a bunch of cheap knockoffs. And that's before the "I make fifty copies of the Soulbreaker Orb and fly in a directions" shenanigans. There's something off about the very feeling of duplicating gear that I think is the reason we're having a discussion about it. That feeling goes away for me when the duplicates are using claws and teeth.

                    I feel that if you want a Battle Group of You, the original you with your magic axe oughta be acting as a Commander for that Battle Group-Lunars don't usually get telepathy, after all, instant wordless communication between human forms requires charms for a reason...but I expect you'll find that idea unsatisfying because it plays out as a "make a battle group to command" charm instead of a "turn into a battle group" charm. It's cool to have a swarm of bats or a cloud of wasps or maybe even at the far end a pack of wolves or clawstriders acting with a single purpose because that fits into the Lunar combination of monster and nature tropes. With human forms, though? Nah, I am not into it.

                    I would much rather keep the number of clones small and require investment in each of them.
