        They forgot memories or something, IIRC. Kind of like Ananasi in crawlerling form in WoD. That said, D'ivers were never explained that well as to how they actually worked. Lunars are definitely like Soletaken though.

        I think Lunars should probably be able to have Crawlerling Swarm Mode, but there's no point using the battle group mechanics if said mechanics aren't modeling the desired effect: battle groups are mechanical shortcuts to model a specific thing, and if you remove elements that need specific modeling (like the idea that soldiers will break and flee as their fellows are killed, or that a peasant levy will be less effective at fighting en masse than a regiment of drilled professional soldiers) then you're probably better off not using those mechanics if using them means leaving a lot of their system hooks (i.e. the whole purpose for using that subsystem) empty.
