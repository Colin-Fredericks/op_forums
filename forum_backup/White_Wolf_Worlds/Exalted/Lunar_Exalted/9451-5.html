            The way I see it, and I know I've said this before, Lunar craft goes beyond the sharpness of a sword or the balance of an arrow, Lunar crafting is about seeing beyond the purpose of an item to its potential. Lunars are the Wardens at the edge of Creation - a foot in every world and an eye on every horizon. They are about making that sword into something that slices a ghost as well as a man - about an arrow that only feels the heart of its target an ignores all else (wind plows through it without touching it and rain falls around it). Lunar craft is about paintings that crawl into the targets mind and whisper in the back of their brain and about taming the elements beyond the world (Glamor, Vitriol and anything beyond Creation should be their plaything). The sword of the Solar is a *Better* sword, just as the solar is a Better man - the sword of a Lunar is as Other as they are.

                                            Lunars are indeed the Anathema with which people are most likely to find familiar. They are also the Anathema for which the Wyld Hunt does not typically bring its full might to bear nor always pursue to the death. This is in part because the threat one poses isn't as great (at least not in the same way) and in part because a given Lunar is more likely to be able to finagle her way to a place where the cost and difficulty of pursuit exceed the benefit (what I wouldn't give to find that dev/writer quote >.> ). For the Solar Anathema, that place does not exist: a particularly well-stocked, skilled, armed, and supported Wyld Hunt will move out with all speed and not stop until the Solar is dead.

                                            No one -- at least no one who's right -- has claimed that Lunars aren't terrifying. They are. They've actually claimed good chunks of Creation among themselves, for crying out loud, and the impression the devs have given is that their brutal, long-term strategy is successfully, gradually grinding the Realm down. But think about what that says about the legends told of the Solar Anathema: as terrifying as Lunars demonstrably are, Solars are still prioritized above them.
