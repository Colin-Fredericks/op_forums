            Haven't we had this discussion before?

            There's more to being smart than the Intelligence stat on your sheet, and more to an Exalt's themes than what their prerequisites are. My opinion is that the broad thematic of being the most intelligent person around is simply more important to Twilights than to No Moons. (In fact, I might even argue that it's more important to Sidereals than to No Moons.) Historically, Twilights have always gotten many more charms that express aspects of intelligence, and I don't expect that to change in 3e. Lunar charm themes are also a bit vague in general, but I feel comfortable saying that expressing the legend of their abilities (including ones like Lore and Investigation, which imply intelligence) are more important to Solars than expressing abstract legends for Attributes is to Lunars.

            (Generally the Lunar charmset has gone to great pains to find other things to focus on, probably because the authors recognized that since almost everything is Ability + Attribute, and even the things that are just an Attribute often still fall under the thematic of an Ability, trying to structure Lunar thematics too heavily around straightforward applications of attributes dooms them to being Silver Solars. I mean, straightforwards applications of your attributes are there, but I don't feel they're a good thing to use as a central column of design for Lunars the way abilities are for Solars. Compounding this is the fact that attributes, unlike abilities, tend to be poorly-distributed in terms of what they do -- as we can see from Dexterity, for instance.)

            If you were to ask a typical historian in the Exalted Universe who the smartest or most charismatic person in history was, they'd name a DB (or even, possibly, a mortal) because everyone else has been erased from history or cast as monstrous outsiders. But if you asked some sort of Celestial Deity of History who had reasonably accurate knowledge going back to the First Age, they'd probably name a Solar, because the big memorable achievements that get you written down in the books as the "smartest person in history" or the "most charismatic person who ever lived" would generally be more likely to have been done by Solars.

            It wouldn't be, like, 100% -- intelligence is not quantifiable and can refer to many different things, regardless of the fact that we have a stat for it out-of-character, and there are a lot of achievements by all sorts of different people. There would be other historians who might name a Lunar, Sidereal, or spirit as a dark-horse candidate for those positions, say -- but it'd be like people who say they think the Eagles are the greatest band of the 20th century. Anyone who says that is probably, for one reason or another, going out of their way to ignore more obvious candidates.

            (Also I do want to reiterate that Sidereals have a major claim on the general concepts of wisdom and intelligence themselves, since even with their ability to predict the future, an adviser needs to usually be able to at least make a credible claim at being one of the smartest people in the room. But in the ask-a-historian test I mentioned above they are disadvantaged because they've erased themselves from history so well that even our Celestial Deity of History probably won't know about some of their achievements; and even beyond that, an adviser or behind-the-scenes manipulator is less likely to get written down in the history books -- which probably suits Sidereals fine, generally.)

                            Aside from sheer intelligence, though, philosophy is largely about examining life from different perspectives.                             Philosophy itself is not a single unified thing, nor is there any strict measure of who is a "better" philosopher. But in terms of everything measurable about it, Solars are probably going to have the best charms. Who is going to write most of the setting's philosophical treatises? Solars. Who has the best charms for attracting adherents to their philosophies? Solars.

                            Who is going to be able to produce a philosophy that will have the most beneficial impact on a society? To a great extent this isn't a thing -- the game doesn't give anyone an "axiomatically make correct choices" power. But to the extent that anyone has the ability to do it, it falls under Solar Socialize. Who's going to be able to learn the most about natural philosophy and the underlying truth of the universe? Again, not necessarily a thing in the larger sense, but to the extent that anyone does (and to the extent that people can show that understanding), it falls under Solar Occult, Solar Lore, and Solar Craft -- Solars will write better books on the underlying philosophy of the occult or the underlying philosophy of society or the underlying philosophy of anything they're good at, because they're good at those things. Additionally, philosophy beyond freshman navel-gazing requires knowledge of the great thinkers who come before, which falls under Linguistics and Lore.

                            (Obviously, the actual 'best philosophers' remembered in the modern world are generally not Solars for the reason I alluded to above; in the modern world, DB and Sidereal philosophies win out because they murdered all the Solars and currently rule the world, and there's probably a lot of famous mortal ones, too. In most of the world, people don't read Anathema philosophy outside of, say, the Heptagram, which probably studies the philosophical treatises of First Age sorcerers in hopes that it helps them get better at Sorcery -- most of which will be written by Solars. But Solars have the best individual charms for doing the things I mentioned, in general.)

                            What does Lunar Intelligence do? In general, it hasn't done much to help you with any of those things. Its thematic is more cunning trickery and strange magic. Excluding their excellencies, there is in fact only a single Lunar intelligence charm that directly has the thematic of "think hard about new things", and it does so under the thematic of "Lunar minds are strange and obsessive", not "Lunars are just smarter." There are far more Lunar Intelligence charms for manipulating the wyld, for instance, or for producing unnatural wyld-themed effects, than there are for doing anything that expresses smartness.

                            Philosophy isn't just "make a philosophy roll to determine the effectiveness of your philosophy", that's dumb. To the extent that it's reflected in mechanics at all, it's the gestalt combination of your knowledge-gathering, knowledge-producing, and knowledge-expressing charms in the areas you want to focus on as a philosopher; and Solars, generally, have the best charms in all three categories in every field we'd consider a "major" realm of philosophy.

                            How would you build a Lunar philosopher? Can you name the charms you'd take? I can name lots of charms for a Twilight, Zenith, or Eclipse philosopher-prince, with many obvious routes of expansion based on exactly what sort of philosopher I want to play.

                            Now, that doesn't mean "Lunars suck at philosophy, lolz." Lunars are doubtless good at writing about what I might call Lunar philosophies -- that is, philosophy approached from the unique perspective that their powers give them. You're totally right that shapeshifting does give them an insight into certain things, and they'd have a lot to tell you about that; they probably also have a lot of thoughts informed by their knowledge of the Wyld. But, you know -- while I still think it's silly to ask the question, and that, mostly, philosophy isn't a mechanical thing -- if you want to talk about which splat is more designed to support playing philosophers, in general? Or anything like that? Well, look, the knowledge you get from shapeshifting or the Wyld is excellent and all, but I don't think it can compare to insights on socialize, occult, or lore; and you're not going to be as good at expressing it to anyone else as a master of linguistics charms.

                                        In a followup to what I said above -- I think that if you followed the progress of philosophy from the dawn of the First Age to the fall of the Solars, what you'd probably see is several decent Lunar philosophers at the beginning, who eventually get increasingly overshadowed. This is not just because Solars grow faster; it's also because as time passes and more gets written, maintaining your credibility as a philosopher would require not just being able to publish yourself, but being able to keep up with the output of every other philosopher by rapidly reading and understanding what they've written. Without Linguistics and Lore charms, even if we granted that Lunar intelligence gives you an edge in white-room thinking, Solars will still win out because they'll be better at building on the ideas and thoughts from the past.

                                        (Lunars do have charms to preserve knowledge, mind, but not ones that are as useful in a wide-spread academic world. As a general rule the Lunar charmset is very good at operating in the wilderness with little support, but less good at taking advantage of established institutions -- which is a problem even for philosophers, since ultimately, contributing to philosophy in a developed society requires constantly working to keep up other people's thoughts.)

                                        But I do want to reiterate that this doesn't mean there are no Lunar philosophers, or even that Lunar philosophers are bad, by Exalted standards. I'm sure some of the books those scholars in the First Age read were written by Lunars, especially the ones from early on; and I'm sure that even the greatest Twilight scholars had something to learn by reading books by Lunars (and Sidereals and DBs and even mortals.) "Lunar philosopher" is a totally cool concept. But "Solar philosopher-prince" is also a totally cool concept, and IMHO it's more important to them (and more clearly visible in their charms, as a result).
