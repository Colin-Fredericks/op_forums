        Originally posted by Sanctaphrax View Post
        Here's what I wrote in the rewrite thread:
        I really like, in general, the "I can fake lack of infrastructure" Charms - which makes sense, because I think tying Craft more closely to sorcerous Means is a really good move.

        Instant Construction Technique (4m)
        Type: Supplemental ; Duration: Instant ; Keywords: –
        Prereqs: Dexterity 3, Essence 1; Tool-Emulating Transformation

        This Charm supplements a roll to craft something mundane, making it blazingly fast. Most human-scale projects can be completed in a few seconds or minutes.
        Is this strictly better than CNNT?

        Lunar Blade Reconfiguration (5m, 1wp)
        Type: Simple ; Duration: Instant ; Keywords: –
        Prereqs: Dexterity 5, Essence 3; Wyld-As-Workshop Method

        This Charm allows the Lunar to attempt to transform one non-Legendary Artifact into another Artifact of the same or lower rating made from the same materials. If the Artifact is not made from moonsilver, the Lunar must first break it. The breaking must be done carefully; an already-broken Artifact is of no use to the Lunar unless repaired.

        Transforming an Artifact uses the same rules as building a new Artifact. However, the difficulty is reduced to 0. The Lunar may roll once immediately, and may roll again twice a session or every two weeks. The old artifact is worth 5 rolls as materials.

        If the new Artifact is extremely similar to the old one, like two melee weapons with similar enchantments, divide the goal number by 3. If the new Artifact is somewhat similar to the old one, like a bird-themed powerbow to a suit of winged artifact armour, reduce the goal number by one third instead.

        This Charm must be activated once for each roll. The characteristics of a half-transformed Artifact are up to the ST.
        I like this quite a bit; in particular, it seems to solve the "What do we do with all these Dragon-Blooded weapons?" problem.

        Weaver To Weaponsmith Transformation (3m, 1wp)
        Type: Simple ; Duration: Instant ; Keywords: –
        Prereqs: Intelligence 2, Essence 1; –

        The Lunar meditates for twenty minutes or so, and in the process re-assigns their Craft specialties. The new specialties need not be similar to the old ones, and this transformation is permanent. Many Lunars prefer to return to their original specialties eventually, though; it's disorienting to remember being a blacksmith without knowing how to shoe a horse.
        ... Eh. For the cost of a Charm, I could buy another 2+ specialties. Reading those very narrowly, I can see where I'd favor the Charm over having ~4 specialties, but I don't love the idea of reading them quite that narrowly.

        This is less about the Charm than about the underlying revised system, I guess, and it may just be a sticker-shock thing.

        Material Substitution Technique (5m)
        Type: Supplemental ; Duration: Indefinite ; Keywords: Wyld, Stackable
        Prereqs: Intelligence 4, Essence 2; Weaver To Weaponsmith Transformation

        This Charm supplements an attempt to build or repair something, allowing the Lunar to use ludicrously unsuitable materials. Making a set of cutlery out of paper or repairing a cracked wall with rotting meat are both completely possible. For as long as the Lunar keeps their motes committed, the substituted materials are just as effective as the proper materials would be. They don't become more or less edible than they were, though, and they don't change their appearance or chemical properties.

        One activation of this Charm allows the Lunar to substitute about fifty pounds of material. Multiple activations may be required for large projects.

        In the Wyld, the Lunar need not commit motes to this Charm. Substituted materials remain effective until the object comes into contact with reality.
        I like this - I think it's close to how I would have fluffed your "Solar miracles up an object from nowhere" Charm, minus the Wyld clauses.

        Temporary Artifact Technique (10m, 1wp)
        Type: Supplemental ; Duration: Instant ; Keywords: –
        Prereqs: Intelligence 4, Essence 2; Material Substitution Technique

        This Charm supplements a roll to create a non-Legendary Artifact, reducing the difficulty to 0, doubling the successes after rolling, dividing the interval by 3, and allowing the Lunar to work without magical materials. If this Charm was used on any of the rolls to create an Artifact, it falls apart eventually; after (Essence x 2) days if it was made without magical materials, after (Essence x 2) weeks if it was made with moonsilver, and after (Essence) weeks if it was made with other magical materials. Working without materials gives the Lunar no benefit to the Terminus from materials.
        Can you talk about what you see as the use for this Charm? I have a hard time thinking people are regularly going to build Artifacts that fall apart in time comparable to the time to create 'em. It's a big success-enhancer, but that's a pretty steep tradeoff.

                                So i'm considering giving these a whirl for a game a friend is running in the north. However I noticed there aren't any chargen rules.

                                I wanted to help out with that.

                                So I was thinking it might start similar to the 2e rules, with a few adjustments to make the generalists more capable generalists.



                                Establish Caste/Castelessness

                                Attributes:
                                If you have a caste, 8/6/4 and +1 floating to a caste or favored, pick 2 in caste, pick 2 favored keeping in mind you can't have an ability that is both caste, and favored (as in no doubling up, can't be a full moon with Strength and Stamina caste AND strength and stamina favored)
                                If you are casteless, 8/6/4 and +2 floating to favored. Pick 2 favored

                                Abilities: Select a Favored ability and place 1 Free dot in it, and mark survival as favored and place 1 dot for free in it.
                                All Lunars know how to fight. Assign one Free dot into Brawl, Melee, Archery, or Thrown.
                                Assign 25 more dots, keeping in mind nothing can be higher than 3 without BP
                                Assign 4 free Specialties

                                OK

                                Now for where things deviate

                                I always found it odd that Lunars despite having had a structure for a lot longer (assuming you have a caste) had not only just as limited background selection as solars, but also paid MORE for them.

                                So my change is this...

                                Merits:
                                Lunars with a Caste get 13 points to spend on merits representing their having been found, tattooed and given the lowdown.
                                Lunars without a caste get 10 points to spend on merits. Lacking access to the structure of the Silver Pact



                                NEW MERITS:
                                Silver Pact: ( * - *** ) (Story)
                                Requirements: Caste Lunar
                                Being a part of the Silver Pact,comes already with a bit of benefits, but some lunars take it further.
                                Based upon the rating a lunar can call upon collected resources the pact has to offer. Along with adding the merit value in social situations when dealing with other members of the pact, a lunar may, a number of times per story, equal to the rating in this merit, a lunar can gain temporary access to Resources, Mentor, Contacts, Ally, Retainer, Followers, Command, Hearthstone, Demesne, or Artifact equal to the rating in this background.
                                Such access isn’t free however, and the pact expects the lunar to provide results and similar support to others who ask of it in the pact.

                                Taboo (LET ME EXPLAIN!) *-***
                                Prerequisites: Castless Lunar
                                Casteless Lunars seem by their peers to be more prone to madness they assume is caused by rejecting the argent Madonna. Castless merely assume its just another part of being chosen and the madness is a blessing.
                                Regardless, some take steps to help prevent it. A lunar selects a taboo based on the rating purchased. A number of times per story equal to the rating in the taboo, they may remove a single success from any roll in which they gain limit (they may explicitely NOT stack multiple removals on the same roll)
                                Should a castles break their taboo after having used its benefits in a story, they lose the points in the merit until they buy it anew next story or make efforts to attone. In addition, any lost points of limit from the story IMMEDIATELY come back

                                (I’d like to work out a mechanic that isn’t horrifying unplayable chimerism for this. Though I do like the thought of becoming a scary body horror so babysteps…)

                                Tattoo Artifact: **-*****
                                Prerequisite: Lunar with Caste
                                A lunars tattoos can be crafted with the powers of an artifact in mind. The artifact has to make sense, a series of swirls across a lunars chest granting the benefits of artifact breastplate makes sense, turning your hand into an artifact bow does not.
                                This is permanent, and they must attune to it. The benefit of this is that while in human form, they may learn and master evocations for their artifact if it is at least 3 dots or higher. IT should also be noted that no magic can forcibly deattune a lunar from their tattoos (I mean… I suppose you could literally skin the lunar to remove them….and THEN they could be deattuned).
                                Artifact armor has the benefit of not counting as armor for MA styles, BUT, cannot be stacked with other armor.

                                (Thoughts on this would be nice, again just pitching)

                                Solar Bond: (*-***)
                                (alright heres the big one)
                                The Lunars and Solars were brothers, sisters, lovers, friends, rivals, but most importantly, partners. With the return of the lawgivers, the pull to work with them again has also returned. A lunar wit hthis background has a bond in the form of an intimacy toward a solar (DOES NOT HAVE TO BE POSITIVE). The strength is based on the rating in bond. As an incidental, The solar in question is considered to have RATING less resolve and guile to their lunar mate. (Minimum 1)
                                Along with the above, as a benefit that goes both ways. Regardless of rating in the background, any charms enacted in behalf of the solar that grant dice or successes , grant non charm dice or successes, and any charms enacted upon the lunar by the solar that grant dice or successes to the lunar, grant non charm dice or successes.


                                (I still like the emotional connection, but allowing to to be more than just a positive context is something I like. I do feel however having a tangible benefit the works both ways is nice.


                                OK feel free to tear those apart….

                                CHARMS:
                                15 charms, keeping in mind minimums.
                                Martial Arts charms may be bought in place of lunar charms if they have the ability and meet the requirements
                                Evocations may also be bought in place
                                As may spells if the lunar knows Terrestrial Circle Sorcery

                                Caste and Favored attributes get their excellencies for free, any attribute you don't have caste or favored that you buy a charm in gets an excellency

                                INTIMACIES:
                                Must have 4
                                One positive, one negative, one defining, one tie, one principle.

                                Bonus Points:
                                15 which may be spent at any time
                                Caste and Favored attributes cost 3 (unless tertiary in which case they cost 2)
                                Everything else is the same as Solars

                                Calculate final stats
                                7 health levels (+ any from Ox Body)
                                Essence 1
                                16 Personal 38 Peripheral motes
                                Willpower 5 + any from BP





                                Anyway I wouldn’t mind feedback, especially on the limit idea for taboo. But overall any feedback on this would be great. Most of my custom stuff was for my own groups in 2E so I’m just trying my hand and bringing some things over.

                                I was considering as a sort of pseudo supernal, the lunar selects one caste attribute and treats essence as one point higher for charm purposes (Max 5). But not sure how that would balance.
