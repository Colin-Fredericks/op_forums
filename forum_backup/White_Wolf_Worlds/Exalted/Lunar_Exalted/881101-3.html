                                        Originally posted by Segev View Post
                                        It sounds like it boils down to what the vision for Lunars is. Are they limited to being "the animal Exalted," or is that merely their starting point?
                                        I feel framing the subject like this contains some loaded language, whether inadvertently or not.

                                        For one thing, I wouldn't think of Lunars not turning into many things beyond animals, and having a lot of Charms that reference mythic beast concepts, to render them as animal Exalted. Really, it would be a lot like reductively referring to Solars as "the human Exalted"

                                        I think with Lunars, it's a matter of having a theme or image to which the animal stuff is a component or subordinate, similar to how Lawgivers place the sun first while being largely expressed in culture and folk hero skills and having basically no sun powers.

                                        That's the argument for keeping them focused on the therianthropy. The argument against some of the others is when they're framed in terms of "animals as a starting point", since I think treating something as a starting point that you graduate from... I don't find it to be the best as game design, because if people are chomping at the bit to move on from it, then why bother including it, or if it gets fully elaborated on then it clashes with anything else that might rely on it as a starting point. As a matter of purely personal taste, I don't care for it, because I think the therinathropy stuff is interesting and fitting and deserves care and attention.

                                        On that subject:

                                        Originally posted by Drascin View Post
                                        When someone wants to narrow down Solars a bit from basically being like 90% of every single possible character type, the forum cries foul. When someone wants to expand Lunars a little bit beyond dudes what turn into animals, the forum cries foul.


                                        Yes? Because one of those is at the core of the game, both in terms of gameplay function and setting, and so is designed to be more widely serviceable, while the other is designed for a more precise and focused result.

                                        I get why people would become attached to the focused thing and want it to be expanded, but I don't think it's a very good idea as a game design thing.

                                        There are also other qualities to Lunars specifically, such as the values of maintaining a measure of image consistency, both with themselves and with the genre they're contained in. That's a reason why even for Solars, with their versatility, there are things that are fundamentally out of bounds. I think that settings that cast their net too wide ultimately become rather shallow, and have a harder time being compelling to people. That's probably not an issue for the person who wants their pet issue attached to it, but it's an issue if the necessary customer base for something consists of more then that person.
