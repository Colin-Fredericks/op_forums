    02-20-2015, 08:17 PM
    Originally posted by hatewheel
    I have a query for anybody who feels like participating.

    List if you can, every problem you can think of with a shapeshifting power that allows a Lunar to change their features, but only their human features. With this power, the Lunar may change their entire appearance, even their gender, but they may not change themselves to impersonate another character. Describe all the problems with this.       "Problems", hmm. Problems.

      Presumably, this Charm cannot change the Lunar's Tell (or the pattern of their Tattoos, which will be assumed as included in "the Tell" for the rest of this post). So long as the Lunar's Tell remains unchanged, anyone who knows the Tell can recognize the Lunar regardless of any number of permanent changes to her true human shape. Though continuously changing her true human face can make it harder for someone to attach a specific identity and history of association with the Lunar, if they failed to notice the Tell in previous encounters or fail to see it in later ones.

      Essentially, this Charm is just a really good disguise Charm -- like Solar Flawlessly Impenetrable Disguise, only without the mote-commitment or any need for make-up or props.

      A clever Lunar can, of course, assume an appearance plausibly close to that of someone another character has heard of but has never personally met. For example, a Dynastic mortal comes to Nexus looking to make a deal with a slave-owner known to have dark skin, red-hair and a thick Nexus accent. The Lunar can become a dark-skinned, red-headed man and speak with a thick Nexus accent, and even though the slave-owners associates would be able to spot the fake instantly, the Dynastic costumer can be fooled into thinking the Lunar is the man he's looking for.

      Especially if the Lunar uses social influence Charms to affect how others see her (the Lunar).

      I'm not seeing any "problems". Rather, I do hope we see a return of Shaping the Ideal Form!

              Oh, I forgot something obvious!

              Shaping the Ideal Form doesn't prevent the Lunar from layering a MUNDANE disguise over their altered appearance, and the mundane disguise rules probably allow for the possibility of looking exactly like a specific person. It's legendarily difficult, but theoretically possible in some limited cases.

              With Shaping the Ideal Form UNlimiting the cases where mundane make-up could approximate a specific person's exact appearance, a Lunar with a sufficient Larceny rating and make-up kit wouldn't need to take Heart's Blood in order to look enough like someone to fool even their friends... under casual, distant scrutiny.

                                  I don't have an easy answer myself.

                                  As he points out, that kind of arbitrary identity-lock makes sense for a Second Edition Ebon Dragon Charm, which alters reality according to a concept rather than operating purely through physical transformation.

                                  On the other hand, what stops a Solar using Second Edition Flawless Impenetrable Disguise from mimicking the appearance of a specific other person? The Solar isn't working on reality-altering conceptual levels, but is using physical make-up and props and reality-bending skill. If the Solar can look like someone else, she can look like ANYONE in particular. (At least, within the limited range of changes that Charm allows. It can only alter her apparent age, size and build so far, and not actually change her gender at all.)


                                  Wait, I think I do have an easy answer!

                                  Okay, assuming you will NOT impose a magical limitation of "Shaping the Ideal From cannot mimic someone else's exact appearance", then I would allow a Lunar to use it to appear like someone even without their Heart's Blood, but the Lunar's Tell is REALLY OBVIOUS. That way the Lunar can appear exactly like someone, but in a way that the disguise is still very imperfect, and in a way that encourages the Lunar to take Heart's Blood for a properly flawless disguise.
