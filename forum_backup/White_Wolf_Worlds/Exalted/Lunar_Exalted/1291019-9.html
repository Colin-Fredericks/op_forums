    Originally posted by Aquillion View Post
    Solars have to choose between MA and Brawl. Is your intent that MA should be a strictly weaker option for them, in combat?

    What about if a Solar martial artist has a Sidereal tutor? The Sidereal trains them diligently, even passing on the forbidden techniques of Sidereal Martial Arts; then the Solar has a duel with an equally-experienced master of Solar Brawl. Do you believe the Solar Martial Artist (with a Sidereal tutor, which is supposed to be a huge deal!) should be at a disadvantage against the master of Brawl?

    There isn't an easy answer to this! It's haunted Martial Arts, SMA, Sidereals, and Solars since 1e. There's a lot of contradictory requirements, and it seems like you're only considering some of them:
    In previous editions, solars still got hosed for getting SMA. Yes, they could use them but without sutra benefits they were impractical and too costly to use out of a few charms. More motes didn't matter when you were afflicted by willpower and mote loss. I don't know if it'll be this way but there's a distinct difference between sidereals having to jump through hoops and solars just not getting sutra effects. Even with native charms. Brawl and Martial arts are also two different beasts and I don't know where SMA factors into this. Almost no Solar is going to get SMA, they can't pass it on nor can the create them themselves they can only learn it from sidereals, so it'd be better not to treat it as Solar MA's end all be all.

                                                        Originally posted by RosePlancton View Post

                                                        I don't know. Most of the answer to Clownpiece seems off topic to me.
                                                        I think it's probably worth saying in context to the thread. Clownpiece was saying that one of the narrative purpose of the Solar Exalted is to return and tip the balance of the world to either salvation or destruction. They do this because their power is so much greater than anyone else's in the setting at the moment. Just look at Solar Circle Sorcery as an example. Since it is totally unfair that sorcerers are the only people who get to have that kind of impact and everyone else is a chump, Dawns must be similarly more powerful, and the combat rules should support this. That's why Solars need to be as much better at fighting as Ligier is more powerful than Octavian.


                                                        Except that's all built off of a series of shaky setting conceits that are mostly propped up by bad 2e ideas. Firstly, Sorcery actually IS a very special thing, that's why it's the only thing in the whole game that a Supernal ability does not give you access to. You need to be Essence 5 to summon Ligier, no shortcuts. Judging by the essence values of characters we have seen, it will take decades at least, or, more likely, centuries for a Solar to reach that point. So if Solars are going to tip the balance NOW they need to do it without even knowledge of celestial circle sorcery.

                                                        Not to mention that summoning Ligier is an insanely dangrous task that shouldn't even be attempted by an Essence 1 Solar if they did have access to it. You need to make a (Wits + Occult) roll against his Resolve, and Occult does not have tricky non-charm dice adders or ways to lower resolve for this like the social trees do. If you fail this roll on a third circle summoning, you do not get the chance to banish the demon back to Hell like you do with lesser spirits. If you flub that very difficult roll, Ligier has been unleashed unbound upon the world.

                                                        So, you see, the ability to summon Ligier should not be the measuring stick of Solar power. The rest of Solar Sorcery isn't even that much more powerful than Celestial. Rain of Doom can destroy a city but Cantana of Empty Voices can kill everyone inside it so it's not like an insane step up in power.


                                                        But that's actually okay, because Solars don't NEED to be able to summon Ligier to tip the scales. They are more powerful fighters than Full Moons, better sorcerers than No Moons, etcetera, but not by an insane degree, and certainly not more powerful than a lot of current Lunars. Leviathan would roll any Essence 1 Dawn. That's okay though, the introduction of 60 Dawn/Dusk/Slayers into the world is a huge deal anyway. The scale is ripe to be tipped in any direction, and even the introduction of 60 new Sidereals or Lunars would do it, but Solars are even more powerful than that!

                                                        So if Lunars end up being almost as good as Solars at using MA, especially if it's in their own unique ways, then that's totally fine. Lunars do not need to suck at MA compared to Solars. What we should be looking at is does the combination of Lunar charms and MA break the game, or become too powerful. We've accepted that comboing MAs and native charms with Evocations and sorcery can work, so maybe this is okay. Or maybe there's some effects that things like dicecaps and Simple charms just make easy to balance. Or maybe neither of those things and we should petition the developers to maybe change it? I'm still undecided, I'll make a chapter about it in my personal review thread at the end of the KS.

                                                        I would also like to offer that Strength charms might be more slanted towards melee fighting than dexterity, if for no other reason than the incorrect-but-genre-accepted notion that dexterity is for range and strength is for melee.
