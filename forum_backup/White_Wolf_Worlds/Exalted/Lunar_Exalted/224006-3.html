                            Evocations are a very broad class of power.

                            If certain evocations stacked with transformation magic in a game breaking way, the general approach of the gameline would lead me to think that the approach would favor blocking combinations with those evocations, or even just imposing a hard stacking limit, not something crude like "You can't use Level 3s, even when they're not broken" or whatever.

                            So it seems like that's the kind of tack they'd take, for the same kind of reasons they trust people to make sensible custom Charms rather than just blanket prevent customs. Of course, maybe it is just a circles thing, but that would just seem a little crude.

                            There's no real sense in designing things to prevent tactically interesting combinations that aren't broken.

                                            Originally posted by notthepenguins View Post
                                            Except, well, it is playing favorites. As with all design decisions, that idea that native Lunar Charms take up nearly all of their "transformative power" (which is a bizarre concept in and of itself - I mean, it's not like there'll be a point at which a Solar won't be able to learn more Lore Charms because they've used up all of their 'contemplative power') is a dev-level decision. You can't just sort of chalk it up to "Oh this is how the setting works there's just no choice!" Hell, even if that was some kind of core setting statement that you can only have so much "transformative power" it would be trivial to explain why Lunars can say "fuck you" to that rule in one way or another.
                                            Where do you get "playing favourites" out of "making high-level design decisions based on aesthetics and motifs"? Design decisions are going to affect different splats differently, but attributing that to simple favoritism seems crass.

                                            I tend to agree that DBT + Evocations is kind of a gross thematic clash. I would, for what it's worth, say the same thing about shintai + Evocations. The way Solar power manifests makes them less prone to this issue, but it also means that their native charmset tends to be less awesome, thematically, and forces them to go outside their splat for iconic "flash". Solar charms and outside competencies are the strongest overall, but Solars are also the most strait-laced splat, and tend to suffer from being printed first. There's a lot going on here! Distilling it to "playing favourites" is a blunt rhetorical oversimplification.
