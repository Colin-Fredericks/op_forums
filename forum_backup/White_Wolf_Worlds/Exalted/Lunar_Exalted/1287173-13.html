    Okay, can afford a little bit of time to look at the Charm previews.

    All right, show me a lovely Manipulation Charm… Trash-is-Treasure Misdirection. An outright Psyche effect, the Lunar’s successful Performance or Presence roll doesn’t merely convince somebody of faulty value, they actually perceive it as whatever higher value (exact depending on the successes) object of about the same dimensions as it is presented as, and treat that as the basis for whether or not the bargain qualifies. That the perception doesn’t change their sensory input, but rather instil in them the sense that such as merely incorrect in the face of the presentation, is an interesting way to imagine it being experienced and what it might say about this aspect of Lunar magic. Effect holds for hours based on the Lunar’s Essence, though three Willpower can be paid to shake it off if the discrepancy is distinctly brought to a target’s attention, and their Integrity preserves them against it being used again for that many days. Essence 4 lets it target multiple people.

    Yes, it’s a higher Essence, but this is a nice one for a good bit of Lunar trickery and sabotage, maybe even a unique sense of distorting ideas of value.

    Labyrinth of the Beast: Sounds like a Guile Charm… yep, the Lunar can bring their own animalistic qualities to the surface and let somebody who couldn’t get past their Guile see that instead, perceiving only base drives, and then ignoring the character thereafter. It’s a neat picture of how Lunars might harness that part of themselves, employ Guile, the nature of how they could be perceived in social scenarios (particularly interesting to contrast with Appearance and Charisma), and maybe hint at some more off-kilter stuff the Attribute might do. Also has a cute Protean effect of letting a Lunar seemingly confirm themselves as just being a normal animal.

    Devil’s Eye Brand: Ah, and here of course was what some of us were talking about, place a characteristic mark upon a person to make that person indelibly mistake for the Lunar. Right down to modelling the mark (applied with a gambit in combat) off of the Tell, for a number of hours. It’s a lot more obvious then an actual Tell, even making regular disguise attempt’s harder, and indeed needs a difficulty 7 action to realise that it isn’t what it seems. Seeing Lunar and falsie together makes it harder to effectively read either. It otherwise has no benefits or drawbacks, regardless of appearance. Certain anti-shaping magic can resist it, and at higher Essence it can be made to last for days with mote commitment.

    (I had said it earlier in RPG.Net, because there had been conversation about misdirection where I suggested something like this, but I'll repeat it for here: I like the idea of being able to ape Bugs Bunny by wrapping a cloth on another's head that resembles rabbit ears, and pointing the hunters in their direction.)

    Now, what of Perception (another I have some excitement about)?

    Soul-Piercing Predator Gaze: Let’s a read intentions or profile character action uncover somebody’s greatest weakness, for whatever that means for them, and possibly even narrowing it to particular fields within a greater whole. It has to be something that might be apparent on the character’s person at the time, and functions as a specialty to exploit it for whichever Ability is best suited. Doesn’t need commitment to sustain indefinitely, but can only have one at a time. Protean allows a Lunar in a feared shape to use another Charm at no cost to use the Tie to penalize Guile. It’s a lot like the kind of thing I once suggested for Intelligence, and in retrospect… yeah, obviously Perception.

    Dream-Delving Voyage: Psychically invade a character’s dreams, using a read intentions action with a variety of possible Abilities and penalized Guile to see individual dreams that all reveal useful information. Can only be used once per day on the same person. Mostly I just like this for the sake of Perception as a whole, although it does have fun qualities of association with dreams in particular and a general quality of transgression.

    God-Body Consumption: Lunars can’t turn into spirits anymore, but they can still partially consume them when they’re truly killed to be able to internalize any of their Eclipse Charms that are qualified for, although doing so requires a mote commitment, at least before they spend the appropriate XP (at a fairly generous rate). While committing, limited to (Essence) Charms, can learn as many as they want, the first Charm acquired gets to be free. Oh, now this is interesting, getting to take Eclipse Charms from humans, including an actual Eclipse Caste Solar, for performing a non-lethal sacred hunt to get their shape. Presumably that can’t be done repeatedly on somebody whose shape you already have. I overall find this to be a decent compromise for no longer getting spirit shapes, particularly since I think some of the powers were what people generally wanted anyway.

        All right, round out my beautiful Changing Moon Caste with some of those sweet, sweet Manipulation Charms.



        I think Argent Trickster’s Rook is one of the few Heart’s Blood Charms that had not been seen ahead of time. Allows a Lunar to get somebody to stake their face on a game of chance, whether knowingly, or through some misleading technicality or obscure language, that requires the Lunar to put up something they would consider of equal value. If the Lunar wins (without getting caught if they cheated), they get the shape. I think the concept is fitting for Lunars in general and this Attribute in particular, but I do have some notes. Namely, I think a little bit of guideline for resolving a game of chance in character would not go amiss, if only because I personally have often not been sure how to distinguish such a thing from general game mechanics in which everything consists of rolling dice. Furthermore, I feel as though the magic ought to enforce the Lunar that loses fulfilling their bet. But overall, it’s a fine sacred hunt alternative.

        Now, for the Influence Charms, starting off with the nicely named Fox-Tailed Trickster’s Grace. In stark contrast to all of the Charisma Charms based on aligning with or enforcing customs, this one allows one to make an instil roll against anybody who sees the Lunar violating a custom, in order to make them come up with their own rationalizations for not having a negative reaction to it (which mechanically entails being unable to form or change Ties from a negative inclination), and they need a Decision Point with a higher Intimacy to resist it, although it won’t go so far as preventing the consequences of actually breaking laws. As an introduction, this not only gives a good feeler for what Manipulation might do, but also helps to give the Changing Moons a strong sense of distinctive contrasts, being able to develop the talent to dance back and forth along the lines of propriety as they please. Oh, and to be especially subversive, an Essence 2 repurchase lets the Lunar grant some extra dice to any observers who opt not to apply Resolve to the influence, which they can add to rolls to convince others against the custom.

        Forsaking-the-Blood Posture also allows them to harness some of the power of taboo by making an instil roll that doesn’t experience multiple target penalties to create or weaken Ties as appropriate by accusing a character of having violated a custom of their own culture, with members of the same culture having weaker Resolve and being unable to unable to strengthen it with any Ties that would concern cultural solidarity. On the face of it, apart from the stuff with penalties, this would feel like a normal instil action, but it seems to me that it’s one where the Lunar’s power can override the need for evidence or complementary Intimacies where those would be necessary, which sounds like it can be distinctly useful.

        Going further, Tarnished Silver Imprecation allows one to spread harmful rumours by making an enhanced instil action to create negative belief about somebody else around in the scene, that simultaneously functions as a persuade roll to get them to spend the next several days to try going around spread the belief (which I presume generally consists of trying to make more instil actions) in the form of various social innuendos. It can also target the person who is the intended target of those negative ideas, which seems to kind of imbue the rumour into them, given how it makes other characters that read the victim’s intentions are also exposed to the Charm’s instil action (with their success at the read action being the limit of how much they can resist it), for a number of days equal to the Lunar’s extra successes. I’m thinking that some of these Charms will be ones I want to compare most directly to Solar Socialize Charms, to see how their capacity to sabotage a person’s social standing match up.

        I also want to take this moment to note that Lunars have had a lot of effects that can endure for extended lengths of time without needing to commit motes, which I think may be more than what a lot of Solar Charms can do. Apart from being something that might offset the drawbacks of some comparable Charms that have slightly weaker effects, I also think it could lend itself to an image of Lunar flexibility, being capable of keeping more plates spinning at once. But again, been a while since I examined the Solar Charms in detail, might be way off base with this.

        Oh, I saw the name Generosity of the Cuckoo before, really interested to see what Lunars can get from those bastards. Lessee, make an instil roll benefiting from double 8s that, in lieu of an Intimacy, obligates those affected by it to accept a gift offered from the Lunar, that they can’t wilfully throw away or abandon (although they can put it in storage, exchange it, gift it, or eat it as appropriate), and can only spend Willpower to resist this influence if the gift is clearly dangerous, or accepting would violate a higher Intimacy (might be hard to get somebody invested in a vow of poverty to accept precious jewellery). This Charm strikes me as interesting in that it doesn’t strictly designate a purpose to something as simple as “offer an object that can’t be refused”, but that’s a premise that can have a lot of plasticity: give somebody stolen goods so that they take the blame for the theft; give somebody something that is covertly poisoned, or otherwise dangerous; give somebody an object that might be seen by another as indicating some relationship or affiliation (the target didn’t know it was the handkerchief of another person’s spouse); or hell, give something that is sincerely valuable or helpful to another, either to endear oneself to them or just improve their lives (under circumstances where, for whatever reason, they might not accept such a thing otherwise).

        Hmm, Laughing Raccoon Audacity; I presume this is going to be a Charm to enhance attempts to tell somebody that most of the other people living in their home are soooo stupid? No, it’s an enhancement to the first Influence Charm, allowing a Lunar to reduce their own social standing by letting the Charm’s effect instill Ties of scorn or disgust in addition to getting people to rationalize their taboos (I guess those things would intertwine a bit)… yes, interesting, it makes people kind of perversely accommodating to your actually performing the violations by finding them typical of somebody of your low quality, as well as making them all very evasive about doing something about you. That certainly feels very different to what many other Exalted Charms might do with one’s social standing, and I’m interested to see where the Attribute might go with it.

        Glib Tongue Technique is a much simpler Charm, doubling 9s on instil rolls and slightly reducing the penalty for implausible claims, to model the idea that the Lunar is talented at making deceptions sound like something an audience wants to hear. Following that concept is Heart-Snaring Deception, hooking into the desires of others to believe, in the form of instil actions that utilize an existing Intimacy getting to ignore the Intimacy’s strength in penalties for implausible claims, rolling over into non-Charm dice if the difference favours the Intimacy. That’s an interesting notion, being able to exploit prejudice to get somebody to believe in more absurd crimes (or even positive feelings to think something unlikely but desirable has happened). I think these might be the roots of the idea I had of Manipulation turning a person’s Intimacies into weapons against them.

        I think I can guess a bit from the name Uncertain Sky Ambiguity. Right, special instil roll with bonus dice, yeah, succeeding causes the target to doubt the factuality of something. Going by the examples, seems to need to not be something too extreme; if you’re in the right place, if something is as valuable as you thought, if you were really robbed… it seems vaguely familiar, but not enough to override assessing it here. They’re relatively subtle doubts, but a Lunar can harness them as though they were Major Intimacies; you might get some surprisingly extreme things out of “did I leave the stove on”. The drawback is that the influence can be resisted without Willpower if directly contrary evidence is presented, and it can’t disrupt belief in something that any level of Intimacy encompasses; I do, however, appreciate the clarification of the wiggle room for that, getting somebody to doubt things that are kind of within the Intimacy. This is another one that feels very versatile, with a bit of extra oomph to it from that virtual Major Intimacy; apart from the influence it can give you over an individual, it feels as though it would be very effective at spreading general confusion. For the fact that it’s Essence 1… I’m getting the image of some audacious young Lunar who pops over briefly to the Blessed Isle, heads for somewhere like a large town or the main city of a minor prefecture, and just goes around breaking down anybody’s certainty that they’re where they’re supposed to be or doing what they’re supposed to be doing, causing normal society to grind to a halt, before the Lunar quickly bugs out (perhaps literally).

        Something a bit more overtly sinister is found in The Shadow Who Wasn’t There, allowing a Lunar to interject in a conversation about some unknown entity or person who has such on their mind and insinuate that the person was definitely not the Lunar (whatever the person’s current take on who the Lunar is should be). I’d say that, on the face of it, this is minor enough to warrant not requiring a roll (although it does require mote commitment for a scene). However, I think the thing that really makes this Charm is the capacity to leverage the certainty as a virtual Minor Intimacy; it’s more than just evading investigation or blame, it’s that being suspicious of just about anything can create an avenue for a Lunar to establish unwarranted trust, that they can then take advantage of (even if the target can resist the power by spending Willpower, which probably mostly happens on occasions where the Lunar seems kind of suspicious or they’re committed to trusting nobody). I like this one for both the subtle touch, the kinds of social doors a Lunar can slip into, and the admittedly absurd image of people discussing their suspicions in the presence of a really obvious Lunar with whom they’re entirely cavalier. Appearance can get in on this act, for those Lunars with mimicry spirit shapes.

        (Heh, the end of that paragraph constitutes a solid 66,000 words written about Lunars)

        Of course they need to have it both ways, as Ever-Present Shadow Insinuation takes the opposite tack, allowing the Lunar to create a certainty that the unknown person under discussion definitely is the Lunar (‘s shape). On the face of it, that can be used for intimidation or misdirection (go in the shape of somebody you want people hostile towards, make Manipulation actions to persuade your targets to go after the person without saying it outright), although it should also be noted that people can have such discussions about actions they like, and the Lunar can take unearned credit. It also has the strange capacity to be compatible with the preceding Charm, said to create a general minor psychological penalty from trying to resolve the confusion, while the Lunar remains free to exploit either virtual Intimacy; overall that just creates a fun image of how Lunars might mess with people, and come across as wholly inexplicable.

        Heh, I keep thinking about that idea of Manipulating people into going after you in another person’s shape… you take the shape of some traveller you know is heading to a town where there’s been a murder, get in ahead of them and walk past people wondering who it could have been…

        “Hey, stop; you look kind of suspicious, newcomer.”
        “What, who, me?! Nonsense! I was never even near the alley.”
        “Who said anything about an alley?”
        “What alley? I didn’t bring up an alley, that must have been somebody behind me. Besides, I don’t even own any knives, and I never saw that man before in my life.”
        “I think you’d better come with us.”
        “Okay, the thing about that is-“ {runs off}
        {as a mob chases them, leads them out the front gate just as the actual traveller is arriving, rapidly shifts into something discrete, lets that person deal with it}

        Heh, outrageous.

        For something coming at a society from a slightly different direction, there’s Wayward Pariah Affliction, by which one can make a special instil roll to convince a character that they’re secretly despised within their society; the flavour text addresses princes and elders, but the Charm can clearly be used on anybody. The affected character treats any of their Ties towards the culture or people therein as a step lower, and their suspicion and insecurity penalizes rolls for influencing or reading people in that culture, effects that persist until somebody from the culture assures them that they’re welcome, in a manner using rules for overturning influence. It’s not inherent to the mechanics of the Charm, but I’d say it’s reasonable for some characters to proactively engage in actions consistent with their new suspicions, particularly if other circumstances might make them seem more acute; if nothing else, if might affect the parameters by which their Intimacies adjust naturally. As a Protean effect, the target’s Resolve is weaker is the Lunar is wearing the shape of somebody who actually comes from the culture.

        To help the Lunar function with subtlety, Inexplicable Mystery Incarnation is an Instant Charm that can be added to any action related to any form of influence, reading intentions, or applying one’s own Resolve or Guile, to mute motes spent for those purposes. I think there might have been another Charm that did a similar thing for a slightly different process, but on the face of it I can see this belonging to Manipulation a bit more than Appearance, with the stronger focus at being discrete within an operation over general control of how one is perceived.

        With Subtle Speech Method, a Lunar is able to make their words have different meaning to two different listeners, i.e. a message (including influence) encoded for one listener is hidden within normal speech to another. Among these Charms, this might be among the most identical to a Solar Charm, but that’s not really an issue here; the Charm set as a whole gets traction for having more distinct stuff packed in every direction, and even the basic fact of this being placed alongside other Manipulation stuff creates a very different tone to a Solar with Linguistics who may or may not also have stuff like Socialize.

        And finally there’s Voice-Hurling Method, allowing a Lunar to imperceptibly throw their voice for the length of one influence roll or a few seconds of normal speech, which also allows them to speak without breaking concealment, and even imposing a slight penalty on rolls to figure out where they really are. This is another I could see with a variety of uses; leading somebody around, creating an impression that there are more speakers than there seems to be, Lunars should use their imagination with it.



        That’s only the Essence 1 Charms, but I’m going to put a break here because I think there were a bit more of them than usual, and I had a lot to say about many of them. I’ll see how the rest of Influence goes for deciding how to break it down.

            Okay, at least the Essence 2 and 3 Charms.


            Hyena-Grinning Playfulness invokes an image for Lunars that I’ve often found a compelling possibility for them and the effect… ah, interesting, I was thinking from the flavour text it would be a matter of hiding threat behind seemingly benign expression, but it’s actually the other way around, being able to make threaten actions that gets the benefits of effecting action, but lacks the negative drawbacks of being able to negatively incline their Intimacies, or even feel general offence or hostility; so they take the threat seriously, but don’t fully feel the violation of it (the flavour text refers to confusion and fear, but it can probably be played off in a number of other ways, such as the person laughing you off as not serious, but still with an underlying feeling that they really ought to do what you say). It’s a pretty interesting take on Lunar applications of fear and ways in which they might generally conduct themselves, even if it can only be used once per scene (which can still really be fairly frequent). Still, I can see how it could be powerful enough for it to be reasonable to give targets a fairly accommodating means of resisting the Charm’s effect to be able to fully feel the way that they’ve been threatened.

            Now, Wolves-in-the-Mist Incitement, there’s another fear-based effect, albeit a more direct one; the Lunar gets to make a fearful inspire action that can effect multiple people without penalty, doesn’t need to be directly addressed to targets, and specifically excludes the Lunar as the source of their resulting fear. Instead, those affected will feel as though they’re in immediate danger from something, and in addition to the usual effects of an inspire action, causes penalties to Resolve and Guile, and a separate, slightly larger penalty for any action that wouldn’t serve the purpose of protecting from that danger, and is at its worst upon Join Battle and Awareness rolls to detect enemies; I particularly like that idea that fixation on a nebulous, and non-existent threat, distracts them from focusing on or noticing something genuinely dangerous, both as a described experience and suggestion of the potency of the Lunar’s words. That makes up for the manner in which engagement in an actual fight won’t be penalised.

            Something like this is really… not strictly broadening, but adding facets to the kinds of things that a Lunar might engage in publically. Appearance can do fear, but it emanates from the Lunar. Charisma can provide a sense of ecstatic liberation, and now this to just give people a general sense that they’re under threat. It all weaves together to create a rich and vivid sense of what a Lunar at large in the world might do.

            Innocuous Rumormonger Remark has an interesting picture behind why a Lunar can make an instil action without target Resolve being improved by any negative feelings towards them specifically, that idea of being to pass the buck for their arguments away from themselves to the grapevine (even if I imagine that there are times in which the “rumours” are just originating in the conversation). The fact that the target can’t even recognise that the Lunar was influencing if they don’t read or profile them is a nice bit on top. The whole thing gives me an image of a Lunar who openly instils some detrimental Tie in a Dynast, who comes under the impression that the Lunar wasn’t making some argument themselves, just circulating some distressing information from back in the homeland.

            And then it gets an upgrade in Chattering Magpie Inflection, allowing Lunars the very strong image of obscuring themselves as the source of a statement, when standing amidst a crowd, and creating the impression that it just arose from the group as a whole… ah, and it plays delightfully off of the voice throwing Charm, to let the Lunar do it when slightly further away from the group. This all feels like a very effective tool in the arsenal of a Lunar shit stirrer.

            Heh, after the Appearance and Charisma Charms that let one get motes back from the sense of self-satisfaction for being themselves, there’s something strangely humorous in the idea that Lying to the World brings motes from successful Manipulation influence and assertions of Guile because those function as a kind of prayer to oneself. The unusual kind of spiritual elevation of innuendo, misdirection and deception.

            So, Brother-or-Duty Dilemma: Lunar gets to weight influence in favour of the former of those, by disrupting the capacity of Ties to groups, or Principles in general, to be used against influence leveraged against a Tie to an individual, an amusing kind of genre enforcement. That they can’t bolster Resolve is fair enough, but… it says that it prevents them from being employed in Decision Points if their intensity is lower than the exploited Intimacy. That’s… already how Decision Points work? Fair enough that the Protean effect says that, if the Tie is towards the Lunar (in human or animal shape) they can’t use such Intimacies at all, but for the base effect the benefit to Decision Points is either unclearly written, or based on misremembering the base rules and ultimately useless. I like the flavour of the power, but it definitely needs to be reworded a bit.

            Okay, moving on from that, errr, Faithless Wolf Strays, starting to provide a bit of social defence, and invoking that intriguing old image of Lunars doing so through a kind of manipulation directed at the self… and has an interesting element of Luna, of passions and relationships being prioritized over rarefied ideals. The Lunar thus gets to prevent Ties towards groups and Principles from weakening a Resolve that has been bolstered by a Tie to an individual that is stronger than them (although it’s not enough to prevent them from being leveraged for persuasion), and if the Tie is to the Solar mate, requires those Intimacies to be even stronger. So a Defining Tie to a person would only let Defining Principles or group Ties to penalize Resolve, or to a Solar, none at all.
            Heh, although now I’m considering the title, and how a more prejudicial view would see this less as a powerful loyalty to individuals, and more a sign of being unreliable.

            Countless Shadow-Puppets Dance enhances Shadow Insinuation and Shadow Who Wasn’t There, to allow a Lunar to insinuate blame or blamelessness onto any human shape in the Lunar’s library without needing to wear it. Doesn’t really change the story premises inherent to those Charms, just gives the Lunar some more flexibility within them. There’s also a helpful clarification that the cognitive dissonance of using both Charms simultaneously only applies if the provided assumptions are specifically contradictory; for the example given, the Lunar would need to make somebody think that the killer both is and is not the satrap. And mimic spirit shapes get to take it for Appearance again.

            Let’s see… Empress-Grifting Confidences: enhances instil actions to provide Ties or Principles based around giving a character an inclination to believe the Lunar specifically, by preventing a character whose Resolve is penalised by an existing Intimacy from spending Willpower to stop its formation until at least the next scene, barring being shown direct evidence… okay, I’m going to be honest, the wording of this one is throwing me a bit. I can’t quite tell if this is about instilling an Intimacy, or aiding a lie with a virtual Intimacy… hang on, start over, break it down. A Lunar tells a lie; while lying, instils an Intimacy to make the listener believe them. Intimacy can’t be resistant until next scene if the listener has an Intimacy that penalizes Resolve (representing the idea that they want to be lied to), unless shown evidence that the Lunar is a liar. If a scene goes by with nobody given such an Intimacy dispelling it, Lunar gets a Willpower point. It’s an odd Charm, and feels a little bit niche, but I suppose that it can be fairly potent under the right circumstances. I guess the player just needs to know that they aren’t creating an enduring belief, just a momentary certainty that they have to make the most of.

            Hmm, a longer Charm, hopefully it will be a bit less challenging to read… Gnawing Mouse Malaise: Lunar spends a scene engaging with an organization that they or their assumed shape-identity have authority over, consisting of various kinds of sabotage, then gets to make a roll using either Bureaucracy, Larceny or Socialize, with auto-successes equal to Essence, and opposed by the highest presumed roll of whomever is in the group, using Attribute and Ability suitable to contesting the Lunar’s activity. If the Lunar succeeds, the sabotage gains a momentum that wracks the group with confusion for the rest of the story, represented with the idea that any 1s rolled while engaging in its business subtracts a character’s successes, as well as doubling the time of any activity the group engages in, while the Lunar gets the chance of a Willpower point per day for when penalized rolls fail, and greatly enhanced Guile against attempts to figure out their roll in things. That’s important, because the cursed group is given a potential out by uncovering the Lunar’s roll in things, and then performing a project that counteracts their activity (although there are also Charms that can do it more outright). It can only be used on a group once per story, so they can’t be subjected again if they shake it off.

            I’d been thinking of whether Lunars would get very much to interact with Bureaucracy; I feel they should get hardly any, and if they do, it should really come mostly in stuff like this. Not much else to say on it, just generally another nice picture of how they might spread discord.

            Ohhh, and a really nice portent for Grows-in-the-Telling Technique. Yes of course, Lunar gets to reset a failed instil roll for a lie, weakens the effect on Resolve of a competing Intimacy already used, and dubious claim penalties are halved. Basically, give the impression that, if a person is repeating a lie so emphatically, maybe it really is true. As a social reset Charm, it’s certainly reasonable to limit it to scene. It’s funny, a lot of this should be standard trickster fare, but it keeps giving me a much more sinister, even sleazy, impression.

            Next is Herd Subversion Approach which is fairly straightforward, make an influence roll to get somebody to act against a custom, with members of the culture practicing the culture getting a slight Resolve penalty, and it’s not possible to use Ties to the culture to bolster Resolve or for Decision Points. It’s not quite as sexy as a lot of other Charms to interact with cultural mores, but still seems fairly potent, and yeah, good to add a means of breaking down cultural respect in their repertoire.

            Secret Devil’s Presence is another lengthy one aaaand ahahaha yes, Lunar generates fear by spreading stories about their own selves… after a manner of speaking. Lunar makes a contested roll of Manipulation + one of a number of Abilities, against a character’s Charisma + Ability to accuse them f being an imposter, with the Lunar rerolling failed dice for all of the target’s 1s (being able to accuse absent people at a penalty). With success, the extra successes… are they rolled against other characters in the scene, or just applied immediately? Either way, they function as an instil roll against those characters to create a Tie of suspicion, making people believe the target is anywhere from a confidence trickster in disguise to somebody possessed by a spirit; if they’re already suspicious of imposters, it costs them more Willpower to resist. While characters have the Tie, read intentions actions against the suspected character can only ever give a sense that there’s something off or false about the character, needing to beat the Lunar’s Guile to see through the deception. Being used in combination with one of the previous rumourmongering Charms waives this one’s Willpower cost, and it can also be used reflexively along with Wolves-in-the-Mist Incitement (normally being a Simple Charm). It seems a little bit convoluted, but looking at the full thing, I guess it can be potent enough to warrant it. I think I can see what it’s going for, the idea of a Lunar who has been operating for an extended period somewhere that would be on the lookout for them, and takes some of the heat off by drumming up suspicion against somebody else. I’m not sure why I never would have considered before a specialty of making people looking for shapeshifters think that somebody else was suspicious, as a means of keeping up their own ruses, but there it is.

That last one in particular seems very good for helping a Lunar generate a subversive or anti-authority element, rolling some of the core Lunar theme into their general perfidy. A reader only gets the opportunity to realize it’s fake if they have cause to doubt the authenticity, and even then are facing a difficulty of the Lunar’s Essence + Manipulation (meaning no less than 4).
