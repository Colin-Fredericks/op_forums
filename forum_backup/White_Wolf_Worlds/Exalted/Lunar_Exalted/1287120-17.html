                                                    Wizard, the city didn't need to be built around the Wood Shrine. The founders could have built it a safe distance away.

                                                    At some point, someone had to go to the effort and risk of clearing out a part of the jungle and building a dwelling there.                                                     But no one did that, even though the pressure of avoiding murder or disappearance would have created a natural demand for housing as FAR away from the Shrine as possible, leading to city planning favoring distance from the shrine, until any buildings around the shrine are abandoned or inhabited only by those who cannot afford safer housing.
