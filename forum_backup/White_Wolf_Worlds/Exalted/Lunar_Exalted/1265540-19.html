                Yes, but they got the name because "witch" was a term that those developers frequently used for the Lunar Exalted.

                And we're having this argument because "witch" is a term that these developers are using for the Lunar Exalted.

                So calling it an eleventh hour addition is completely ludicrous.

                Just as one last reference to the argument; a major difference between the Solar Exalted and the Lunars is that, to the extent that the former has Charms that might kind of touch on that kind of element, they're almost entirely confined to the Abilities that are actually about that thing.

                As opposed to Lunars, who will have a Charm to haunt people's nightmares in Strength.

                Part of the thing with the Sidereal Exalted having been kung-fu sage bureaucrats is that they have things that invoke martial arts imagery, sorcerous imagery and bureaucracy imagery throughout their Charms, rather than just confined to Abilities conventionally about those things. It distinguishes them significantly from the Solar Exalted by being a lot more focused, but not in a manner that means that they only do a few things.

                Same with references to an Exigent of some healing god, who could be written as focusing medical imagery through any of the Abilities, rather than only having Medicine Charms.

                So Lunars have it as a reference to them as a whole because it's a lot easier for more of them to have the image, since it is spread across their Charms, and because they have fewer stats they need to build up to be able to go for those Charms if they want them; a Solar that is a really powerful fighter and has no Occult has to go through a lot more to get stuff to mess with dreams than a Lunar that is really good at fighting.

                                        Originally posted by BrilliantRain View Post
                                        Given how successful the Lunars have been at bleeding the Realm and running it to ground, I find I really have to question the Sidereal’s decision to embark on the Usurpation back then. Or, at least, I have to wonder how Chejop is still managing to say “Everything is fine. This is good.” even several centuries ago when it should have become fairly clear that the Lunars are slowly winning.
                                        I think there's a couple ways to address this without making the Bronze Faction Sidereals look like total idiots (the explanations still involve some willful self-delusion, but hopefully not more than any other type of character could get into).

                                        First, you can assume that the Vision of Bronze was very high-level, showing the whole "Creation is greatly diminished but survives", but without showing much exact details, and the Sidereals just assumed that they'd always be in charge. The Vision could still be correct, but the Sids' assumption about who'd eventually be in command is simply wrong - Creation will still survive, even if it won't regain the heights of the First Age, under Lunar stewardship.

                                        Second, you can assume that the Sidereals did predict the eventual fall of the Dragonbloods to the Lunars, and simply assumed that they'd be able to transition from being the power behind the Dragonbloods' throne to being the power behind the Lunar throne. This might not even be too grossly arrogant an assumption. The Lunars' feud appears to mostly be with the Realm and the Dragonbloods, not the Sidereals per se. Sure, the Lunar eldars seem to know that the Sids were neck-deep in the Usurpation, and they've no doubt explained to the youngsters that Sidereal fate-manipulation is very helpful to Wyld Hunts, but for most younger Lunars, Dragonbloods are still likely the face of their enemy, not Sidereals. If, when the Realm looks like it's finally going to fall in the next century or so, the Sidereals stop helping the Dragonbloods altogether, they might stand a chance of making an offer to assist the Lunars once they become the new power in Creation, counting on the pragmatism of the younger Lunars to ensure they're not just turned down flat (possibly literally).
