        Oh, something I've considered before, about shapeshifting:
        For some concepts, having a ton of animal shapes doesn't make sense. For others, it does. But the thing is, those shapes are free, xp-wise. So, if, for example, you're playing a vegeterian Monk (as one of my players did) you're stuck with just your true form. Now, that player loved turning into an elephant (his totem) but didn't seem that bothered by not being able to turn into a ton of other things, because they'd fit his character concept less anyway. But he was essentially depowering his character just for fluff.
        Consider if, say, you play a vampire-themed Lunar. Your totem is vampire bat, you come from a Carpathia-esque region, you can turn into this bat, a wolf, a big black dog maybe... it doesn't really fit the concept that you turn into, say, a parrot. Or a monkey. Or an old woman.
        One poster earlier said he didn't want Lunars to be "pressured into shapeshifting".
        Another thing is that if you start with a bunch of forms, you have to buy the Heart's Blood Library background, but if you get them in game you don't have to pay (unless you have an ST that makes you pay XP for all backgrounds, which is an optional rule that most people don't use).
        At the same time, the sacred hunt itself can be awkward, in terms of the time, which can end up with you, say, spending 6 hours hunting the dog that lives next door because that's the dog you want.

        My answer: scrap the sacred hunt. You still have to eat your target (or sleep with them with the upgrade charm, and I imagine the new book will add some new options: I kind of like the idea one poster had about wearing the skins). But you don't have to hunt them for hours an hours, unless that is actually required to find your target (which it generally, in practice, actually isn't).
        But! To get the form you have to pay 1xp.
        After all, more forms is really useful. But it doesn't fit every character. As I said, this would also mean that the rules for getting the forms could be relaxed without the worry that Lunars would just go into a pet shop and grab 30 new forms.

        STing Bronze Age Exalted

            What do I want from Lunars... I know this way seem weird and counter to what they've said? But I kind of want martial arts, or charms in general that come from mimicking animals. Y'know, the idea of emulating animals to learn martial arts is a dang classic idea. I mean sure they don't want, 'Everything is based on animals with lunar charms' but at the same time? I had this idea for a Lunars rewrite before I heard that 3e was nigh (See: After New Year's, before winter ends estimation). Basically, two facets to Lunars for learning tricks. 'The Gaian Dance' for learning anything shapeshifting/animal related (Walking among, learning from the mystic qualities people attributed to animals like an owl's status as a harbinger of doom and a symbol of wisdom both, maybe like omen reading from owls) and, 'The Lunar Dance' for anything self-related like most traditional charms. The basic idea was to have aspects you could equip, but learning too many would trigger their brand of the Great Curse: Losing themselves in all the forms and qualities they collected, and chimerism being the ultimate and final form of the Curse itself. Not to say that Moonsilver tattoos stopped the Great Curse, just that it prevented it from accelerating crazy fast. Also helped solve the weirdness that was, 'Limit, despite being a function of the Great Curse, defined chimerism' in the Lunars book.

                                        I think there is a Knack that allow you to only beat your target into unconsciousness so you can gain their form without eating anything.
                                        There is now, though not at the time this was an issue (as it's in one of the 2.5 things, much later). But beating the shit out of a man to get his form is still not very monk-like (and honestly, I don't think it fits. The point of both eating people's hearts, or the upgrade for getting it through sex, is that you've taken part of them inside yourself and thus internalised their form. Beating them up doesn't do that.
                                        But anyway, that's off the point. My point was, my Lunar has about 50 forms, but that's not thematically appropriate for many Lunars. Think of mythic shapeshifters. Most switch between 1 human and 1 animal form. They might sometimes have 3 or 4. They generally do not have 50. Unless they can literally turn into animal at all, or something.
                                        Now, of course, if you play a Lunar based on, say, a werewolf (which is the most stereotypical example I can think of, and I doubt many would play it because it's so incredibly stereotypical, but it serves to illustrate this point), you can turn into a human, a wolf, and perhaps a half-wolf monster man (or lady). You could just not learn any other forms. But, since those forms are free, you are clearly limiting your power for your backstory. Now, normally (assuming charms are balanced, which honestly they're not always) you don't lose much from not buying stuff that doesn't fit your character, because you can spend xp on other things. But because learning new forms costs no xp whatsoever, you have literally gained nothing at all by not picking up a ton of different animal forms.

                                        Also, since apparently many of the animal forms are going to come with unique skills that make them even more mechanically useful for Lunars (which is of course a good thing, because in the old rules it was rather odd the way that, RAW, turning into a horse didn't actually make you any faster), it would be even more appropriate for Lunars to pay a small amount of xp for each form.

                                                That is, perhaps a Lunar buys a Charm, and the Charm gains additional effects (or any effect at all only) when a Lunar has formed some kind of bond with a spirit.                                                     However Lunar crafting Charms compare to those of other Exalted, I want Lunars to have superhuman facility with turning the trophies of their hunts into functioning, durable weapons and armor. Trophies from slain demons, elementals and gods should also be valid targets, especially for crafting artifact gear.
