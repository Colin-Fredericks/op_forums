                                    Hmm, I'm not sold on this. For starters, most of the MA-using Solars I've seen pick a MA do so to have a short, self-contained charm set which fits their concept. They also usually combo it with native defenses and the odd offense which allows comboing. Solar Stealth, Dodge, Melee, Athletics, Brawl and Resistance all offer combo's which quite frankly blow away anything shown so far for Lunars, and which also have to be balanced with all Martial Arts. Also, the increased cost for MA charms and MA ability dots is far from trivial when you wish to combine two or three Martial Arts. Something which will also become more and more prevalent as more will be released. Martial Arts is after all the ever-expanding Charm Tree...

                                    Finally, the Dawns I've seen until now have all gone for the Solar's native offense charms, not MA. But that is due to the current lack of high end options which leverage supernal and the aforementioned lack of easily combined MA's in order for that choice to truly shine and compete with the native charms. The Dawn focusing on Martial Arts is not in a good place compared to their other options due to lack of content. Not due to Lunars. That, however, will change in due time and is no way indicative of Lunars being better MA's.

                                    They dabble and combine MA charms from likely a single style with their native charms, and, yes, they will really be good at it, a true master. The Dawn Solar Master of the Blade, to pick one example, will be able to combine Single Point, Violet Bier, Steel Devil, some swordy SMA and his native Melee, Dodge or Resistance, the multipliers and other goodies from Athletics and so on. I'm sorry, but I have no doubt whatsoever which character will play and feel like the undisputed, transcendental master of Martial Arts, and which will not.

                                    Looking at the Lunar combat charms revealed so far, well, they obviously lack the sheer depth, breath and power of the Solar options. Ok, we've only seen Dexterity, but this is still a core part of their dodge and the accuracy part of their offensive trees, as well as pulling duty as their stealth tree. Taken as self-contained, and lacking supernal, I don't think anyone here can argue what we've seen is more powerful than Solars, right?

                                    It is obviously meant to be filled out with Martial Arts and/or shapeshifting, and those two are mutually exclusive! Yes, the Lunar will be flexible and have an answer for most situations, but they won't ever have the sheer power or focus of a Solar. Will they have powerful combos between MA and native charms? I'm sure they will! Same as with their animal forms and their native charms. Solars still have more, if only because of how laser focused and powerful their trees are. Add Supernal and no other splat has a larger selection to synergize and combine ridiculously powerful effects. They will also be able to do this with MA, even if perhaps to a lesser extent right now (and that is debatable).

                                                In terms of talking about power levels and the Usurpation, I think it's important to note that:

                                                A)The Usurpation-era Solars included a ton of elders who'd lived for thousands of years. Not hundreds, but I'm sure dozens. And probably most of them had lived hundreds of years. Whereas most of the Dragonblood had only lived a hundred or so years. There was a massive difference in essence and experience.

                                                B)Today, your average Dynastic adventurer in the threshold has been exalted 10-20 years. Your average Legion officer or Shikari maybe 40? A Satrap may well have been Exalted for well over a century.
                                                Your average Solar has been exalted for... about a year. An exceptionally old Solar has been exalted five years. Canonically, the only Solars with Essence 3 are going to be those who predate the return, like Yurgen Kaneko, Samea and Filial Wisdom.
                                                There's a massive difference in essence and experience.

                                                So, a group of Essence 2-3 Dragonblood has to be a beatable but challenging encounter for a group of Essence 1 Solars. A group of Essence 4-5 Dragonblood has to be a beatable but challenging encounter for a group of Essence 3 Solars.
                                                Because that's what they're fighting, and it needs to not feel like a cakewalk.

                                                But just because an Essence 4 Dragonblood is a tricky fight for an Essence 3 Solar, doesn't mean that 10 Essence 6 Solars didn't require an ambush by 100 Essence 2-3 Dragonblood, most of whom died fighting.

                                                Tldr; Dragonblood being a challenge for Solars in the Time of Tumult, doesn't mean the Usurpation was a cakewalk.
