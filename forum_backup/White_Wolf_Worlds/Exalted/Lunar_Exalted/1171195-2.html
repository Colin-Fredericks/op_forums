                                    This is third edition. If you are making assumptions about the (canon) setting not a) backed by a statement in 3rd edition materials or b) backed by a statement from a dev, you're already building on a foundation of ice, not stone. And given the amount of "back to First Edition" involved in the setting development, if you are citing something from 2e alone, the ice is quite thin.

                                    As far as the war against the Primordials, not only that but the whole First Age is explicitly being put behind a veil of ignorance and legend for 3rd. How many DBs were there for the war, and how were they created? This is not known, and (barring a change of heart on the part of the dev team) will not be answered. The war was thousands of years ago; the game line is focused on the current day.

                                    And whether breeding camps were ever realistic or not, they are now, as a practical matter, impossible. You could, from the information we currently have, at best infer that you could manage to make a mortal population as likely to produce Dragon-Blooded as the Realm's patrician class. But even that assumes that mass breeding of DBs to mortals automatically upgrades the kids' chances regardless of the state of the DB's progenerative essence, which is not a given.
