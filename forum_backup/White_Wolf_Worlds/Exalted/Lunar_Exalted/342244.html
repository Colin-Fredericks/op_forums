    Prototype as Lunar Inspiration...

    12-16-2014, 05:24 PM
    ...Why don't people like it?

    Let's look, not at the cancerous viral body horror, but at the mechanics of how the powers of these characters work.

    They have superhuman strength,m and speed, and toughness, and endurance, and regeneration. They can take the shapes of creatures by eating them, and also absorb their memeories and perfectly imitate their mannerisms. In combat, they use the natural weapons of creatures they've devoured. Preferred methods of learning information about the conspiracy? Hunt down people who know, eat them,and absorb their memories. James Heller himself has the power to easily track down anycreature who he declares his prey, and can sense when it's an apropriate time to go in for the kill.

    Incidentally, Absorbing memories from creatures in your hearts blood library is something being mentioned as examples of Lunar mental attribute charms.

    James Heller, from the sequel, works even better as a Lunar. His Xp goes into a level system, and once he gets enough he doesn't get new powers, he gets more super strength, better regen, or can jump higher.

    New Natural Weapons and such powers? He has to go out, hunt down a creature with that power or natural weapon, and eat it. IF he wants better claws, he has to find something with stronger or slightly different claws, or maybe on other attribute that matches well with claws, and eat that.

    James isn't like Alex. He's the same person before and after infection.

    He gaind his powers, becuase, after his family was killed by the infected, he decided he was going wipe out the infection and Alex Mercer or die trying, and if he died he was taking as many of those fuckers with him as possible.

    He proves to stubborn to die, as in the prologue of his game, he fights off or escapes from some of the more advnaced infected creatures, survives a tank explosion, and attacked Alex Mercer himself. He gets infected for his troubles, and h survives that too, and that's how he gets his powers.

    Isn't surviving a fuck ton of bad stuff, physical danger, life altering crises, and and emotional turmoil supposed to be how Lunars are judged worthy of their powers?

    So I'm confused. I look at it and I see the parallels, but people seem to be shutting it down based solely on Aesthetics or the protagonists being "shoggoths" or "rubbermen" when they're not.

    I'm an aspergic individual. This means that I can't imagine how others will react to situations. This has led to me accidentally offending someone, and has also led to me being less clear in statements than I thought I was. I'd like to apologize, and ask that others don't get mad, but just ask me to reword or clarify. Thank you

      I think the main problem is actually that Heller and Mercer aren't particularly tied into the other Lunar themes of shamanism, primal forces, and adaptability. You can pull some cool combat tricks off them on occasion, but then most of those tricks were easy to come up with already. Claws, super-strength, regeneration, speed, and so on are things Lunars already do. What more can be pulled from Prototype that hasn't already been realized? Mostly "shoggoth" type powers, like lashing out with flesh tentacles, devouring people instantly, or reforming from a drop of blood.

      To use them as inspiration for new Lunar powers, they'd need to offer up something that Lunars don't currently have but that they should possess. A Lunar really shouldn't be shapeshifting his arm into a sword or a whipfist, he should be manifesting Claws of the Silver Moon or drawing his moonsilver grimcleaver.

                                  Because Prototype & its ilk are a Science Fiction Action story. Exalted is a Pulp Fantasy story. They intersect in some places, but biology as a property of knowledge isn't one of them.

                                  That is, generally if a character is tapping into forces beyond themselves, those forces have a mind and will of their own. Mercer / The Thing / etc tend to work of the cold/alien anti-will of biology.

                                  There may be some places for Mad Science in Exalted (sort of, of a fantasy sort) but the Lunar are the opposite of this.

                                  Alex Mercer exhibits his powers in an Unnatural way. Lunars express their powers in a mythic, "Hyper-natural" way.

                                  So the Hydra, Cerberus, Ladon the 100 Headed Dragon, Peng, the Basilisk, the Qilin or Xiezhi or Gramar... there are thousands of combinations, powers, narratives to draw on. All without slumming around in Science Fiction / Resident Evil genere.


                                  Lunars and the characters from Prototype do . . .generally, sort-of the same things, but they do them in completely different ways, for completely different reasons.

                                                  Originally posted by Rater202

                                                  Other than the bolded part, you're not far off. If Lunar Charms are attribute based, then "Super Strength", "Super Speed", and "Incredible Toughness" all fit somewhere into the low level charms of the strength, dexterity, and stamina trees.                                                   Okay, this 'super normal attribute" thing is where you cross the line. Superman is very much not Lunar, regardless of his exaggerated physical capabilities, because Lunars don't just use exaggerated physical capabilities. Their strength increases, way back when, were associated with their muscles looking like they would burst through the skin.
