    Originally posted by Gigaton-Falcon-Emu View Post
    I think the issue that many of us are pointing to is that previous works have explicitly pointed to almost all Lunars who have such arsenals doing nothing of worth with them. To use Leviathan as an example almost every time his arsenal is mentioned we are flat out informed that he does nothing with them.

    I don't mind them being vague moving forward (it might even be helpful at times) but it is unfair to say that this concern is unfounded when there is much evidence to the contrary in the past.
    No one in the setting really does much with stockpiles of weapons unless they are attacked or they want to project force, and even then they're super-valuable resources so they tend to be used sparingly. Most of the firepower of the Realm is held in reserve as weapons-of-last-resort and/or deterrence, which I think describes the Lunar elders as well. Lookshy is the only group that I think is really portrayed as regularly totting out their warstriders, ashigaru and similar.

    Also keep in mind that we seem to be comparing the arsenals of the militaries of the largest power bloc in the world to the personal stockpiles of individual Exalted. Leviathan is powerful, sure, but he's not the entire Realm Military, or even the ruler of a similarly big wealthy empire. Thing about the sexy magitech artifacts like 'striders and airships is that they need big military-industrial operations to keep them running, because they're remnants of a time when that was the norm. Leviathan doesn't really have one of those.

                                        Originally posted by Erinys View Post
                                        Good ideas!

                                        Huh, somehow I didn't see this post before.

                                        So there isn't new info on whether Lunars own any artifacts in 3E, but they are doing something.
                                        The theme seems to be, "artifacts are fewer and more unique in general", and there isn't nearly as much focus on artifacts non-Lunars own, either. An artifact is more of a special thing a particular person has, and collectively artifacts are much less a type of commodity governments stockpile for national defense. And finally, Exalted are being treated more as individuals and less as groups; I recall seeing fewer collective references to Exalt-types and more mentions of specific named heroes (who are also Exalts) doing specific things. (that might have been a personal bias in how I read the book, feel free to disagree!!)

                                        Taking all that together, I think we can infer something about Lunars and artifacts, which is that any given Lunar's access to artifacts, just like any other character, is primarily a function of that Lunar's personal circumstances, and only relates to them being a Lunar insofar as being a Lunar affects their personal circumstances. This Lunar at the edge of Creation is going to have a different story about where he got his daiklave than that Lookshayan Dragon-blooded, and yeah, maybe in some abstract way the latter is "more likely" to have one than the former, but Exalted are so rare and so idiosyncratic that talking about them in generalities misses the point. Each and every one of them is an outlier so no concept of central tendency is terribly useful when you're talking about a specific character.

                                        I think that's a really significant change over the way previous editions and writers approached both the Exalted and Artifacts.
