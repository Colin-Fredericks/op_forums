    Originally posted by JayTee View Post

    I also think Knacks might still have a place. Unless you meant something else?

    and B: that they'll somehow be silver solar charms again.
    So do I. What's your point?

    Look, if people put together a wish list for Solars (and they probably did, a year ago) you would see just as much "crazy shit" on that list as this one. No one is going to ask specifically for all the little tricks like Dipping Swallow Defense or Bulwark Stance, or even waste time summing up such Charms with a wish like "I want Solars to be Good At Swords". That tamer sort of shit is just assumed; taken for granted.

    People will instead ask for Wyld Shaping Technique, Eagle Wing Style, Blazing Solar Bolt, Perfected Celestial Array... Stuff like that.

    Why should you expect any different for a Lunar wish list?



    I fully expect a similar situation with Lunars, their charms will be expressions of attributes, filtered through the lens of the moon.
    Hence the Charisma Charm that lets you scratch or piss on something to create a terror effect. Or the Appearance/Manipulation Charms that let you fake wounds or conceal them. Or the Intelligence Charm that represents a skill-independent talent for working with materials that used to be living plants or creatures.
