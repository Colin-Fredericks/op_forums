    Limits on Lunar size and swarming

    10-06-2014, 01:04 PM
    So, I hope that Lunars will be able to transform into an entire swarm of bugs, in addition to just one individual bug.

    I'm fairly sure that Lunars will be able to shapeshift into a giant bug. Bugzilla.

    If they can achieve those two extremes, does it not seem acceptable that a Lunar could become a swarm of man-sized bugs? Or more modestly, a small battle group of a Lunar and his Kage Bunshin clones?


    What are the appropriate upper limits on Lunar's giant-ness, massive swarm-ness, and Kage Bunshin-ness?

    formerly Tornado Wolf, formerly Inugami

          Being able to turn into a group of things using native Lunar charms (as opposed to sorcery - wasn't there a spell that let you turn into a flock of birds and reassemble somewhere else?) opens up a can of worms I'd rather remained shut.

                                                      I don't think I'd generally allow Lunars to turn into a swarm of large animals, because a Lunar that takes the form of a swarm is embodying the sense of a swarm as being a coherent thing, and that sense is harder to preserve if it's a Lunar turning into a buffalo stampede instead.
