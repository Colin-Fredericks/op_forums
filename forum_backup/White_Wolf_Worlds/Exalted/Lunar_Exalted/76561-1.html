            Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post

            That's one fast bird.

            Wonder how well the Wyld Hunt could do with several packs of specially bred attack raptors.

            Or their bows.
            Yeah, no.

            The idea is that the Lunar would fly off a couple of days, or even a week or two before the Wyld Hunt actually arrives on the scene. Not wait until they show up and initiate combat before leaving. That would be pretty dumb.

            Remember that it would probably take months for the Wyld Hunt to get from the Realm to parts of the Threshold. Even an ad-hoc Wyld Hunt composed of nearby Outcastes could take weeks to get together once news of an Anathema spread; even longer if the Dragon Blooded decided to bring along a large number of mortal warriors to serve as assistants/meatshields (which they probably would). That's a lot of time and money (and patience) that those Dragon Blooded would essentially be wasting on a glorified snipe hunt after they show up and find out the Lunar had left days or weeks earlier.

            A half-dozen Lunars who travel around the Threshold drawing Wyld Hunts and then leaving long before the Wyld Hunt arrives can, over time, cause significant problems for the various Wyld Hunts. Especially the "ad hoc" ones formed by Outcaste Dragon Blooded who probably have better things to do with their time than chase after Lunars who already packed up and left town five days before you got there. This sort of tactic would either seriously strain the Realm's Wyld Hunt as it sends teams after "non-threats" who leave before the Wyld Hunt arrives, or it will cause the Realm to sometimes ignore a Lunar, "Don't bother, he's just going to leave before any of our agents arrive," either of which is a pretty useful for the Lunar Exalted as a whole.

            In fact, I tend to imagine that's how most Wyld Hunts operate due to falling for that sort of thing over and over again. They only bother to show up if a Lunar looks like he has a real intention to stick around and build a powerbase. Which, in turn, means that Lunars who function as travellers can usually move about unmolested and without having to worry about a Wyld Hunt; and of course, those who do plan to set up bases of operation have more time to do so before the Realm realizes that the Lunar in question is actually going to stay put, rather than simply creating a ruse to force the Realm to spend time and resources on a prank.

            That's just one example of the sorts of things the Lunars can do, without a massive amount of effort beyond time that can cause problems for the Realm and other Dragon Blooded. I'm hoping to see similar tactics that the Lunars use so that it isn't just, "Raise barbarian army, get smashed by the Realm," or, "Hide and carefully guide a civilization to avoid Realm retribution," which generally are the only other two tactics we've seriously seen.

                    Yes. I would just like to see something like that in the books.

                    An example of a way in which the Lunars adapted to the Dragon Blooded dominance and found a way to "defeat" them, and the ways in which the Wyld Hunt evolved to avoid those obvious issues and then in turn, how that opened up niches and safety for the Lunar Exalted to abuse.

                    "Lunars who travel lightly rarely have to worry about the Wyld Hunt, due to the tactics that Lunars have employed over the centuries."

                    Or -

                    The Wyld Hunt in (insert section of the South) is often much, much stronger and heavily armed than is standard, though at the same time much, much more rare. This is due to the fact that a pack of Lunars known as the Earth Shattering Brotherhood occasionally frequent the <insert province> of the region. They were known to have a single member serve as a stalking horse in order to draw down a Realm Wyld Hunt, which arrives expecting a single Exalt, only to find themselves massacred by nearly a dozen Anathema. The Realm responded by sending larger, much better equipped Wyld Hunts, but when that happened the Earth Shattering Brotherhood simply chose to leave and proved impossible to chase down.

                    Since then, the Realm has learned that any attempt to deal with a Lunar Anathema in that province with a smaller Wyld Hunt will nearly always end the destruction of the Dragon Blooded, while larger forces inevitably find themselves chasing shadows. As such, the Realm has largely (though unofficially) written this province off. If the Lunar Anathema choose to dwell there then so be it, the Realm simply isn't willing to expend the resources to try and stop them until the day they prove themselves a serious threat. This makes the province something of a popular travel route for those who fear the Realm more than a brotherhood of Lunar Exalted.


                    I mean, there's lots of neat things that Lunars could have done over the centuries to work around and stymie the Wyld Hunt and the Realm. I'm hoping to see some of that in the books. It would also be nice to see some of the things the Wyld Hunt has done to stymie and work around Lunar tactics.

                            Yeah, that's where I got the idea. ^_^

                            In summery, before I go to sleep...

                            The Wyld Hunt is a thing Dragon Blooded do, an important thing, yes, but ultimately just one of a number of things they do. But, for the Lunar Exalted, the Wyld Hunt is the defining event of their existence. The Wyld Hunt is the reason why there are no empires, kingdoms or even cities openly ruled by Lunar god-kings. It's the reason why the Lunars have to hide along the borders of Creation. Nothing has defined the experience of the Lunar Exalted more than the Wyld Hunt. It is, almost entirely, the reason why the Lunar Exalted are what they are in the Second Age.

                            At the same time, having a "Wyld Hunt > Lunars" statement in underlined, bold 14 point font (yes I'm exaggerating a bit) isn't much fun for anyone so it would be nice to see some of the ways the Lunars have bested, manipulated, tricked and carried out victories over the centuries, and how the Wyld Hunt has been forced to change in response. I think something like that, even if it's just a page, would be very useful for bringing perspective to the Lunar Exalted in 3rd edition.

                                                        Originally posted by Crumplepunch View Post
                                                        Do you see Solars being defined entirely by the Wyld Hunt in the same way? If not, why not?
                                                        I'm not Anubis, obviously, but I would argue the Wyld Hunt defines Solars, just not in the same way.

                                                        With Lunars, it is pretty much the most direct threat a Lunar faces. By this I mean there may be more dangerous things than a Wyld Hunt (which is still very dangerous), but these are seldom actively pursuing the Lunar specifically because the Lunar is a Lunar. On most big actions they take, they must consider its danger, and they certainly wouldn't take other big actions if it wasn't a thing. Without the Wyld Hunt, the incentive for a Lunar to seek out other Lunars is probably much, much lower, so this promotes more of a group identity. The Wyld Hunt defines Lunars as The Oppressed.

                                                        In contrast, the Wyld Hunt defines Solars as The Dead. True, many of these points could have applied to Solars. The basic situation for Solars and Lunars is much more similar with the Solars returned en masse and the Wyld Hunt a shadow of its former self. However, the Age of Sorrows historical context for both groups is important. In that the latter has been able to make a historical context while the former is just now getting the chance.
