            Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post
            I'd stick to saying that it's so hyperbolic and extreme that it kind of hurts even any valid points it might make.
            A valid point remains valid, regardless of fluff (although I'll note your views on my presentation).

            That said, it's "extreme" in the same way as a Dragon-Blooded rejecting the Realm or being an Outcaste; or an Abyssal ronin (of which I saw plenty in Solar groups). To someone who wants to simply play independent, the Silver Pact is the extreme change (because on pain of mutation, turning into an NPC and then death, you're rail-roaded into an entire (and probably alien, if you're not a Barbarian) culture.

            Try it with other splats (yes there are similarities - none as serious).

            Outcaste Terrestrial ?
            Awesome! But as soon as you Exalt, you'll have a specialized murder-squad out to kill you -- and simultaneously, a group of Elder Dynastic Terrestrials who are being paid with artifacts & other backgrounds in order to retrieve you.

            You'll then spend a few months on the Blessed Isle (don't worry if you've never lived there before) where you will be educated in a Realm school, adopted by a House and have their jade sigil emblazoned on your skin. If you refuse, your saviors might kill you (it's not hyperbole, it's in the bloody text) -- and if not, every time your anima banner flares, YOU will be burned/frozen/drowned/poisoned/crushed by your Anima Banner.

            After all this, you can then return home to live your life as an Outcaste Terrestrial in an all-Outcaste game on the far reaches of the North.

            AND THIS IS A GENEROUS COMPARISON!

            Another point --- Terrestrials have an entire Dynasty and Realm full of patricians to support their culture. Abyssals have loose things in agreement -- but each Deathlord can have entire undead cities of dead cultures to keep the mood (think of Sijan). Sidereals have all of Yu Shan. Infernals have countless demons to sustain any nascent culture that a mere 50 Exaltations can't actually form.

            Lunars? You get 300 members --- at most. This is a culture? This establishes norms? Are the Beastmen doing wyld tattoos and reading claw speak and all engaging in shamanism, so when the Silver Pact convenes, each Lunar takes with them a few hundred mortals? I mean, a culture should be larger than 10 high school classes worth of demi-gods who might not even speak the same language.

            Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post
            I mean... one is free to interpret the stuff in those terms as much as they're free to interpret anything in any way, but it seems so far afield from what they're actually trying to go for that it mostly amounts to shadow boxing.
            As aforementioned, it's a rant against 2nd Edition not a criticism of 3E -- I'm eagerly awaiting any updates (on Lunars and Infernals).

            My main criticism is "why make something mandatory when there are Exalts for which it will be irrelevant? " That's what I'm criticizing. Not the existence of culture or aesthetics --- but making something mandatory. Every other splat has both ronin/outcaste/rebel/etc AND multiple sub-variants to pick-and-choose from (making them all super cool).

            Saying "these are things I can pick-and-choose from to inspire my Lunar" is one thing.

            Saying "these are things all Lunars must have in common, and hey yo, I'm throwing a bonfire moon-gazing orgy next week and us Lunars thought you'd like to come along.... oh wait, I thought I recognized your Tell.... you mean you DON'T have cat ears?" is something else.
