                        To be honest, one of the biggest concerns I have for Lunars in 3E is the idea that the Chimera will be an accepted part of Lunar society, instead of something to be avoided, like it has been for the last thirteen years.
                        I'm of two minds on this:

                        The first is starting to be won over by the idea that there's way too much baggage assigned to the idea of a Lunar with a syncretic spirit shape (which is to say, one informed by a coherent blend of animals features). Somewhere along the line, I feel as though too much was put on the idea of Lunars who resemble the actual Chimera. What the drive behind doing so might be, I could not say (possibly something to do with how spirit shapes inform Charm cascades).

                        The other side is still attached to the idea that a Lunar might be consumed by their own power, which might be found in the basic concept and accompanying motifs of the chimera, as well as a basis for the moonsilver tattoos.

                        Mostly though, I want the latter to be more firmly rooted to the actual Lunar concept and context. I want it to still leave something that has an actual character. I kind of want it to happen mostly on the inside.

                        Because I'm still won over by this:
                        And at first it was this heady rush where the might of a thousand beasts and a hundred gods and devils was coursing through you, and it was totally awesome? And then a bunch of guys showed up and did their level best to murder you? And then while you were on the run your power started to feel more and more like a thousand beasts and a hundred gods and devils running riot in your mind and spirit, and you had trouble commanding it? And then the other Lunars found you, and you had to go through that harrowing process of visions and the agony of having mystic silver tattooed into your skin to fix your Exaltation?
                        Hmm, I wonder at the possibility that a Lunar who wants to shift their Totem might need to remove or disable their tattoos, and the stigma might come from the fear of what crept into you while they were gone...
