            Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post
            I feel like the premise that already assumes that means that present the physicality of the Lunars is a premise that won't really match well with Lunars who use glorious god-bodies rather than illusions.

            Not that turning into something kind of insubstantial necessarily strays too far (it at least reminds me of the Captain from Hellsing), but still worth keeping in mind.
            I do not think that the Lunar charmset should provide any more support than is absolutely necessary for Lunars whose desire is simply "I want to use my glorious human physical stats in human form in the most straightforward way imaginable." The problem is that that thematic ("straightforward application of [attribute]") is, the vast majority of the time, going to be completely indistinguishable from "straightforward application of [ability]" for any ability / attribute pairing, and we already have massive numbers of people mining that extremely narrow and relatively boring territory. Obviously Lunars can't avoid touching on it occasionally, but if there is any way at all that something can be covered by another Lunar thematic, they should get that approach instead -- the Lunar thematic should be heavily about master cunning shapeshifter, trickster lord of illusions; and very little about Strongdude the Super-Strong Dude. The fact is that we got the charmset of Strongdude the Super-Strong Dude as a big part of the (usable) Lunar charmset in the past two editions, and it was mostly a boring regurgitation of charms that had already been published elsewhere with the power level mildly tweaked.

            Obviously that doesn't mean I don't think they should get charms that play up their physical attributes at times, but given the choice between a Lunar charm for escaping bonds by being extra-nimble, or having them do something with a more obvious shapeshifting-trickster theme, I would always prefer to go for the latter.

            But also, thinking about it, I take issue with your assertion that a Lunar shouldn't rely on shapeshifting to escape bonds because someone who knows they're a shapeshifter will use special anti-shapeshifting measures. If you're talking about specific magical bonds that defeat shapeshifting automatically, those constraints shouldn't exist. If they do -- well, what on earth is their purpose? They're special shackles that explicitly only exist to bind Lunars; clearly it makes no sense to allow those special anti-Lunar shackles and then give Lunars a way to defeat them. Trying to imprison a Lunar is probably going to involve more esoteric things like sealing them in a room with no entrances or exits, or dropping a mountain on them that always weighs a bit more than they can lift, or casting them into an infinite abyss or the like. And if you're talking about that, there shouldn't be any one charm that will solve the situation -- obviously using an "I escape anyway!" charm is not going to be satisfactory. In general, weird hyper-specific prisons like that seem like they're still probably best approached with shapeshifting, since the best way to get out is by having a power your jailor didn't anticipate, and shapeshifting is far better at that than charms simply because you can have many more shapes than charms.

            More generally, it seems to me that imprisoning a Lunar in a prison that is completely impervious to shapeshifting seems to me like it would be at least as hard as imprisoning any other type of Exalt in a prison that completely thwarts their charms.

            (Lunars are in general among the hardest Exalted to imprison because of this, of course.)

                                                Originally posted by Lord Sia View Post
                                                I can understand people wanting Lunars to be physical; wild, ravenous beasts that tear through everything in their path and devour their prey.                                                 What I don't get is people demanding that Lunars be one or the other. It's almost as if people have forgotten that Luna is whatever the feck she wants to be - and so too are her Chosen.
                                                We need to divide actual physical-ness from violence. I mean physical in that it's very raw, and very real. Lunars have an animalistic feeling, a primal or barbaric theme to them. This does not preclude them from sophistiction or high society at all. It makes them pragmatic or very visceral. They can still be tricksters by actually changing their body, not an illusion that is required to be believed. I maintain Lunar shapeshifting powers should have no UMI effects or tests to overcome, because the Lunar is literally what you see before you. There is no illusion being forced into your mind, or magical disguise, the Lunar is very much what she appears to be.

                                                With Solars its divine power, this magical thing that they manipulate for effect. It's an energy within them they use to improve their skills and do great feats, but they don't physically change. A Lunar does, a Lunar combines their divine power into physical form. It isn't an energy or magic, it's all within their body, their potential and power is as physical as they are.
