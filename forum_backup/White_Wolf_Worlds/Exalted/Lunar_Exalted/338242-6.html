                                                    Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz View Post
                                                    Indeed, most of them probably just say "I know 33 different forms".
                                                    I'm thinking it's more natural to their image to say that they have multiple forms rather than know multiple forms.

                                                    Even if it's implicit to the Lunar condition that the forms you have, outside the one you were born with and the one that is iconic of you, were "stolen" for some value of that term.

                                                    On a related note, even if they'll go for some novelties in possibilities of rearrangement of Charm cascades to reference the spirit shape, my main attachment to that idea is in the feeling that Lunars are based around a cultural and literary milieu that is more focused on the idea of turning into/having a dual nature with a particular thing, rather than the somewhat more modern idea of being a more amorphous shapeshifter. Lunars are more than werewolves, but they capture some of what is supposed to be evocative about such figures in how they turn into a wolf, rather than turn into a variety of things that happens to include a wolf. Such non-specific shapeshifters aren't necessarily inferior, but they wouldn't feel so "Lunar" to me.
