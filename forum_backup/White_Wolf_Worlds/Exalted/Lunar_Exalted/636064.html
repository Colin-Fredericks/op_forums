    [Lunars] A question on mutations

    08-22-2015, 02:20 PM
    ...Please don't end badly, please don't end badly, please don't end badly

    Hey guys! A question. Considering the devs have talked a lot about Lunars having highly physical magic, having god bodies and being god monsters, how do you feel about overtly supernatural mutations for warforms and true forms? A snake that can put you to sleep with eye contact for example, or a hawk with blades for feathers, or a tiger made of flames? I realise they're not hugely appropriate for low essence games, but maybe they might work for your truly ancient lunar elder figure, or your lunar sorcerer?

    Conversely, why do you think this kind of thing might be innapropriate?

    Thanks!

    Please don't end badly, please don't end badly, please don't end badly...
