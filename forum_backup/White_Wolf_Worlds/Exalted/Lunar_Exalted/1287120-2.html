    Hi, folks, Just here to explain the Rei thing. I'm in the Exalted Discord and in the lead-up hype for Lunars a lot of people were joking about #MakeReiCanon, as the backer intended to back at a level to canonize their character in the art.     He decided to jokingly put it in the hashtags, and no harm was meant, no lifting up one person more than others, etc. Just having some silly fun with our fans. It could have been anyone who got the hashtag, and if there's any history/bad blood, I was unaware. Sorry it caused some weirdness in here, as I generally find the community to be super positive and fun, and don't want to take away from that. <3

                                                            I admit that one line in the overall thing there made it feel a bit like the DBs are stuck as "inevitably" losing.

                                                            These strategies also played on the Empress’ conservatism and obsession with perpetuating her rule. Knowing that overreach would play into Lunar hands, she slowed the Realm’s expansion to a crawl, tolerated greater independence in troublesome satrapies, and grew more reluctant to embark upon grand endeavors — subjugating the Scavenger Lands, reclaiming Prasad,
                                                            expanding Westward.
                                                            Now, the situation presented here isn't wrong. It's the keystone of how the Lunar strategy works. But the wording basically presents the Empress' overall response as holing up in the Realm's existing territories and not doing much of anything for fear that Lunar bogeymen will jump out of the shadows and wreck shit the minute she makes a risky play. Which is absolutely the attitude that some members of the Realm likely have, given the psychological warfare going on, but attributing the same to the Empress (versus, say "biding her time until she could find a way to effectively engage them" or something more active and befitting the kind of person that founded something like the Realm in the first place) makes it seem less like a contest between equal players rather than the current situation that doesn't seem to give the Realm any scenario in which their being able to substantially threaten the Pact is even an option.

                                                            Now, the Realm is the Realm. We don't WANT them to win under current circumstances, obviously. But it does make it kinda painfully obvious that Raksi and Ma Ha Suchi did more to damage the Pact via in-fighting than the Realm seems likely to ever do even on a good century.
