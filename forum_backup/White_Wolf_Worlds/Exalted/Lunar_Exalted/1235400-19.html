    Originally posted by TalosX View Post
    I'll admit, I'm a bit bothered by Lunar's having sorcery-enhancing charms. While it's true Lunar's can't access Solar Circle Sorcery, it means that Lunar's are better then Solar's at Terrestrial and Celestial Sorcery. Solar's are stated repeatedly to be the masters of sorcery in general, not just the masters of Solar Circle Sorcery. In the end, as a ST, I only have 2 options. Either invalidate both the Lunar Sorcery Charms, or create a few for Solar's!
    Where can you point to find the phrase "sorcery in general", or anything like it?

    Lunar Exalted have been hailed as the best shapeshifters among the Exalted, but that shouldn't be taken to mean they can perform every imaginable form of shapeshifting, much less every form better than others.

    For example, we have been told not to expect rubber-man stretching Charms from Lunar Exalted, but we know for a fact that grotesque limb-extension is something that Liminal Exalted do.

    We know that Lunar Exalted can't take the Heart's Blood of demons (though they can the appearance), and that Infernals are slated to have Shintai transformations.

    So long as Solar Exalted can learn sorcery sooner than anyone else (no prerequisite Charms), as fast as the fastest learners (ie: Favoring costs), acquire three shaping rituals of any combination their player desires (without any native Charms filling in for flavorful but splat-universal rituals), and uniquely cast Solar Circle Sorcery, then how can they be anything but the best sorcerers in the world?
