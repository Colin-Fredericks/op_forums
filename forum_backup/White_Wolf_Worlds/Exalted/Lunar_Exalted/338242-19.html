                            Originally posted by Gayo View Post
                            Honestly, I'd prefer them to toss the Hunt and just make it "anything, anytime", maybe with purchases to gate certain classes of forms as with 2e. It is a bit too strong, but whatever, it's flash.
                            The Sacred Hunt was a thing even in First Edition, before Second Edition imposed the time-limit. You could pass someone on the street, turn around on a whim, plunge your dagger into their back, take one lick of the blood and wipe the rest off on their sleeve as they fall dead to the street, then turn back around and walk on like nothing happened, and THAT would have been a Sacred Hunt.

                            In fact, let me just quote the first manual (Page 118):

                            In order for the heart's blood to count as tasted, the beast must die, the Lunar must kill it, and the Lunar must drink its blood as it dies. Several Lunars can take part in the hunt, but only one can strike the killing blow and drink the blood.

                            The blood must be drunk on the spot, from the throat or the heart of the dying beast, and cannot be decanted, stored, transported, preserved, or otherwise meddled with. Immediately eating the creature's heart also counts, and some large-jawed Lunars simply crunch down the entire small beasts in one mouthful.
                            Ugh. Be grateful for that, because I had to go back through Exalted: The Lunars and looked in the wrong places first. "Heart's Blood" and "Sacred Hunt" weren't in the index at all, and "ritual hunt" just lead to that First Edition, head-bangingly terrible "Lunars as unadulterated Garou" writing.


                            Originally posted by Godjaw View Post
                            The Sacred Hunt is indeed a time spent "hunting" that which you seek to take the shape of, by understanding them. With loose interpretations of "hunt" it could be observing and pursuing another participant at a party, hours spent exploring a lover's body, diligently attending a scholars lectures, or a observing the petty machinations of nobles at court. In some cases the hunted acts as a lure to their target to better understand them, appearing as a person of their target's sexual tastes, or a clever and observant student who attends the scholar's classes diligently. The hunt isn't necessarily a creepy stalking thing, but it is a pursuit of understanding the target.
                            Originally posted by kindledRadial View Post
                            THIS. This is what I want on the top of my shapeshifting wishlist, more methods for form acquisition that keep the theme of "focused pursuit" while opening the floor to academic and social Lunars to excel.
                            There can and will and should be academic Lunar Exalt characters, but why should that mean they get ANY direct support towards being academic?

                            You're still a No Moon if you spy on a target's habits, learn when he's going to be eating alone, poison his food beforehand, and then kill him when he's helpless.

                            The killing involved in the basic Sacred Hunt doesn't make it a Full Moon activity. A Full Moon charging at his prey openly with fangs bared, chasing the prey down, and then beating it to death with his bare hands is what makes his hunt a Full Moon activity. A Changing Moon and No Moon should and do still need to kill their target; they just use different, less forceful methods. Typically, with tricks and traps.

                            Heck, in First Edition, a Changing Moon could seduce a team of mortal hunters to capture a beast for her and it doesn't matter; so long as she is the one who puts the final arrow in the heart of the totally restrained, blinded, dismembered, poisoned, nearly-bleed-out beast, it counts! THAT is a Changing Moon using her strengths as a Changing Moon, which was convincing others to use their strengths for her.


                            Now remember, I also want the Sacred Hunt to take on many other forms. Many of those forms lend themselves very well to Mental and Social trickery.

                            But I do not want people to start thinking that the basic Hunt is "just a Full Moon thing". It's a LUNAR thing, and you can't get away from the basic nature of hunting without also getting away from the basic nature of Lunars. You can't say that the first kind of hunt can only be a physical thing without saying that ALL hunts must be purely physical.

                            Yes, there's a very physical element to the basic Hunt. But remember that all Lunars are very physical; they all shapeshift into animals! The weakest academic could turn into a hawk or bear or elephant!


                            Also, the problem with merely observing someone as a method to stealing their Heart's Blood is that there's no confrontation. If you deny the target a chance to defend against your attempt to steal, then it's not HEROIC enough to be a proper Hunt.
                            Last edited by Sunder the Gold; 12-23-2014, 10:12 AM.
