    I may not have everything down to the details so there may be some faults in the details of my grasp on the setting, but I'll go out on a limb and write down my view of it here.

    1. Aspect comes from drawing power of solid, graspable ("physical") things.
    Dragon-Blooded channel the powers of the elements (of creation), the solid stuff that creation is built from/made up of. These are real, raw-materials. The same goes for Liminals who get/channel the power of what bodies are made up (the elements of the body); blood, flesh, sinew etc..
    In both these cases the different elements are symbiotic but different, and thus the host of that elemental group's Exalted will be divided into subgroups determined by which Aspect of the Head Group, the individual character has the strongest affinity for channeling (Be it Earth, Blood, Flesh etc.).
    The different Aspects of each Elemental Group all have different qualities and "behaviours", resulting in them (the elements, but also the powers and charms based on them) materializing, acting and solving problems in different ways. Usually this means one individual element will be more effective in some things than others (usually stone will be harder to pass through than wind), and therefore mastering some skills/abilities will come easier to characters from one Aspect than to characters from another Aspect of the same Elemental group. In other words: the power you channel determine what your skills are (most) suited for.

    Castes comes from the intangible personality and power of a deity
    This power is not physical and are One and the same for all the members of the Host drawing power from it. However, since generalised growth without focus can lead to jack-of-all-trades-master-of-none-sydrome, the Gods have found it more efficient to divid up their followers into a caste system, to make them all focus their power (and growth) into a specialized Niche, that (mostly) complement eachother, to create a better/more "powerful" whole.

    tl;dr
    To Sum Up: (my point of view) Aspects channel tangible aspects of the world. What the character is most suited to do comes from which power s/he has more affinity for/uses. (Restrictions are natural)
    Castes all channel the same, divine power, with much less finite possible applications. What the character has affinity for stems from a mold forced upon the character's exaltation. (Restrictions are synthetic).


    2.
    Coming from this view of what Caste contra Aspect is, I would have to say that I am torn..
    First Thought; Lunars should probably be forced into a caste system, simply in the name of symmetry, since they are Celestial Exalteds, However, If any Celestial Deity would defy, or break, that mould it would fit Luna.
    Second Thought; Since they draw upon the powers of Luna, they should have a caste-system to ensure synergy. But thinking over their powers and charms they seem more focused on the tangible things than magic feats. (making ones body strong enough to jump ten feet, rather than just "magically" doing it (the way Solars does it for instance).
    Thinking about how much of the Lunar-idea relates to and deals (almost exclusively) with the tangible world, albeit in a magical fashion, (that is to say, they deal, almost soley, with the physical but in the Way that their Patron Luna would do it?) makes me think that, maybe Aspect Lunars Would be a really nice idea. If there was to be a Hybrid of the two, they would Certainly fit the bill the best out of the current line-up of Hosts. But:
    Third Thought: What would those Aspects be?
    I really love the idea of them having Attributes as the source of their charms (but unfortunatley I don't think it has been implemented very well yet), but what aspects of bodies could be found that are as tangible as needed while still being ethereal enough to resonate with the Idea of Luna? If none can be found I'd say that'd be a problem for them having Aspects, but I've grown fond of the idea. It seems like Celestial Aspects could be such an Awesome and Powerful idea (fun fact; take the "A" from "Awesome" and "P" from "Powerful", mix them together and spell them out backwards and you get "Lunars")

    Well, I guess Lunar-Aspects could be something along the lines of Mind, Body, Heart and Soul. Of which only the Body is one we really associate wit hsomething tangible, while the others have tangible counterparts, that we do not associate fully with implications of them (the actual heart is not our emoions, and the Mind is not neccisarily reducable to the brain. The soul, of course, is actually a fact in the Setting). And then by mixing up which attributes are associated with which aspect you could get away from the Caste-yness of current Lunar Specification:
    Body could have Strength, Stamina and Appearance,
    Mind, Dexterity, Manipulation and Intelligence
    Heart, Charisma, Perception, Wits
    for instance. (soul I don't know, but of course there could be shared, or more limited aspect-attributes, depending on what one might think on).

    To Sum Up: I love the idea of Lunars, and their focus on the material world/bodies. Problem: I have been dissapointed of their implementations in the past, since I've not felt that they live up to my thoughts of their potential. Solution: Go nuts! Don't hold back. there is no reason to feel restricted to maintaining what the Lunars is or has been up until now, since we can't know for sure what it is that's holding them back. After all, there is nothing that is less Lunar, than to adhere to restrictions, right?

                                        More than anything, I'd love it if the Lunars got five Castes/Aspects in 3E. The three Castes idea was, frankly, a poor design choice from 1E that got carried over into 2E because . . . well, I don't know the because. Having only three Castes ironically pigeonholed Lunar characters, despite the broadness of the abilities they were given. While the Exalted Castes/Aspects could have just about any character concept applied to them and still work, the Lunar Castes were given limitations by being split between Physical, Mental, and Social. Even in 2E with Favored Attributes, it became difficult to flesh out a Lunar character beyond their general purpose. If you wanted to play a warrior, it was sub-optimal to not be a Full Moon. If you wanted to player a diplomat or trickster, it was sub-optimal not to play a Changing Moon. If you wanted to play a sorcerer or scholar, it was sub-optimal to not play a No Moon. Sure, the Solars & DB's & Sidereals have their warrior and diplomat and scholar classes delineated, you could still be a diplomatic Dawn or a scholarly Fire or a warrior Serenity. You were never told "this is what you are" because, despite a Caste/Aspect's leanings, you had enough wiggle room to play against type if you wanted to.

                                        The only redeeming factor with the Lunars in this regard is their shapeshifting. A full-on warrior could still have a mouse in his repertoire, allowing for stealth when needed. A sorcerous No Moon could still turn into a raging bull if it was time to smash or stampede. That's the only area of their capabilities that allowed them to stretch their legs a bit without being told "that's not what you do."

                                        Whether Lunars get Aspects or Castes, they need better definition and less limitation. Give them five, open the Physical/Social/Mental Castes up a bit to concepts beyond Warrior/Socialite/Sorceror, and then you'll see a lot more characters who are a blend of ideas instead of a singular one pronounced in a different way.
