        The way I'd like to see it is "Low level charms are using your essence to enhance your attributes, mid level charms are essence+Shape-shifting to enhance your atributes, and high level charms are blatantly supernatural-there's no way you aren't a Lunar."

        Using strength as an example, the low level charms would just be using your essenc to increase your raw strength to carry more or hit harder, possibly at the same time(As opposed to the Solar equivelent which would be spending essence to make you better at carrying or hitting, but not both)

        Mid level strength charms would be things like bulking up to make yourself even stronger or adjusting your musculature to be more efficient or growing fangs and claws to make you even better at hand to hand combat(Or making the natural weapons of your animal form better.)

                Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post
                Okay, I've used terminology without considering what it means to others... when I said "meat and potatoes" what I meant was... Lunars are probably going to have Charms that, whatever their actual mechanics, just plain would not look like supernatural powers to an external observer. Say... a bunch of Dexterity Charms that could be used to enhance sword fighting in a manner and with precepts distinct from Solar Melee Charms, and to a person looking would "just" seem like an unusually fkexible sword fighter. Those are the potatoes. The stuff that is very apparently, overtly the expression of the furious, bestial might of a Lunar, whether it be shifting to a war form or ripping a bear in half accompanied by the spectral image of a rending animal, that is the meat. What I am suggesting is that you can have the potatoes, and actually make them significant, even iconic Lunar Charms, the kinds of Charms you could build a character around, and dodge a lot of the Silver Solar problems by surrounding such Charms in incentives and conditions that make the character feel like a Lunar.
                I think that would work fine.

                                                            I haven't had a chance to really play with 3rd Edition as of yet, so this is primarily running off of just speculation at this point, but here's some random thoughts I have.
                                                            1. Charms that function differently when used with different attributes used. Basically swiss army knife charms...a bit odd, but just a thought.
                                                            2. Charms that have a variety of sub effects. I do recall a charm in Ride that gives a number of minor effects you can choose from.
                                                            3. Charms that passively change or alter other charms, giving them bonus effects or options available to them
                                                            4. Charms that can alter the tags associated with other charms within a specific category.
                                                            5. Charms that can be activated in response to another of your charms failing...okay that's not really "Meta", but it fits the adaptability theme.

                                                            I just don't really want to all of their charms to be solely to be about transformation, mostly because that seems to weaken a lot of the other options, especially if one doesn't want to be a Were-bear to be able to hit things as a Lunar, as it feels like it's conceptually limiting.
