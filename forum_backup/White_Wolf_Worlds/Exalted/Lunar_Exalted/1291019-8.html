                        One of the themes of Exalted is that the Exalted Host has the power to solve the world's problems... If they work together.

                        But of course, they don't.

                        If all 400 Lunars and 150 Solars worked in unison, they could defeat the Realm, though it'd be bloody.

                        If 400 Lunars, 150 Solars, 80 Sidereals and 25000 Dragonblood worked together, as they did in the High First Age, they could build a great society.

                        But the Lunars barely work together as it is (look at Ma-Ha-Suchi and Raksi), while the Solars aren't even organised at all. Solars may find Lunars horrifying, Lunars may be suspicious of Solars.

                        In the game I play, the party is Essence 5. We're facing an invasion by 2 Realm Legions. We've got 4 Solar PCs (Essence 5) including a Solar Sorceress and a general with every published war charm. We've got armies of Tiger Warriors. We've got 2 Lunar PCs (Essence 5). We have the support of the common people. We've got 4 more Solar NPCs staying at our base, and 3 Lunar NPCs. We've got a warstrider.

                        All of this is easily enough to defeat two Realm Legions.
                        But I think we will either fail, or take heavy losses before winning. And there's no way we'll liberate the Realm satrapies.
                        Why? Because all these Solars and Lunars just won't work together!
                        The general and sorceress hate each other. The spy master and priestess hate both of them. The priest hates the general. No one believes the prophetess. The sorceress considers everyone else useless. A lot of others think she murdered the Night Caste recently. The rabbit-Lunar hates the sorceress and the 3 Lunar NPCs. No one trusts the gang boss, or the Lunar lieutenant. Another Lunar said that he'd join us if the rabbit-Lunar asked, but she doesn't trust him.

                        If all the Solars and Lunars worked together, we'd beat the Realm. The problem is getting very different people with strong beliefs and a ton of ego to work together.


                        To put it another way: Imagine the Lunars as the USSR, Britain, and various guerilla resistances. We'd been fighting the Reich for years (2 years, to be exact), it was bloody, and brutal, and devastating. They'd conquered most of central Europe, but we'd managed to keep them out of the periphery at great cost. Their internal problems (bad economy, a mad leader) may have meant that, eventually, we'd have beaten them, or at least kicked them back to their core territories. Or not. Maybe we'd have just fought them to a stalemate. Maybe they'd have conquered us eventually. Very difficult to tell.
                        What we really needed was the USA to help us. As powerful as Britain and the USSR were, we could not land a decisive blow on the Reich. Defending the periphery, and causing economic damage with raids, was about all we could manage.
                        But would the USA help us? We didn't have similar ideologies. The USA had strongly opposed the USSR during its formation.
                        But then, well, the Reich declared war on the USA, they declared war on the Reich, all the Reich's enemies decided to put aside their differences and work together. And together, we won.

                        Obviously, in this example, the Solars are the USA.

                                a huge part of the reason why other splats don't get to combine MA with their native charms is because if they could, then absolutely every combat-oriented character would 100% want Martial Arts. Splitting them off ensures that you have to actually specialize in Martial Arts to get full benefit from it, and discourages a Melee character from grabbing one strong early MA charm purely for power reasons (because if you allow that, everyone will do it and MA will lose its unique meaning.)
                                I mean... maybe that's part of it, but that's not the main reason.

                                The main reason is that they're just different abilities.

                                When you roll Intelligence+Sail, you can't use Charisma or Archery charms. You can use Intelligence charms or Sail charms.

                                When you roll Stamina+Resistance, you can't use Manipulation or Bureaucracy charms. You can use Stamina or Resistance charms.

                                When you roll Dexterity+Brawl, you can't use Appearance or Melee charms. You can use Dexterity or Brawl.

                                When you roll Dexterity+Martial Arts, you can't use Perception or Brawl charms. You can use Dexterity or Martial Arts.

                                The special rule of Martial Arts isn't that you can't throw in other Abilities (why would you be able to? You can't use Melee on Brawl rolls).
                                It's that some Martial Arts can be used to enhance Evasion, and can be comboed with Dodge.

                                Other than that, it's rules as normal.

                                Now, obviously I recognise that Lunars (and Alchemicals I guess, and some Exigents) are the only ones who have both Attribute and Ability charms, which gives them more options on a Dex+Martial Arts roll than a Solar, who only has Martial Arts charms.
                                But I'm just saying, there is a mechanical reason that makes sense.

                                Originally posted by Aquillion View Post
                                Part of the problem of "Martial Arts are ridiculously good for Lunars" isn't how it compares to other splats - part of the problem becomes "is it ever reasonable to play a combat-oriented Lunar and not take Martial Arts?" To me, that's a far more serious problem than dick-measuring contests between splats.
                                Yeah, that's a very reasonable point.

                                And it's kind of a point I made elsewhere, though in the opposite way.

                                What I'm worried about, is that if you don't let Martial Arts work with native charms, then Martial Arts are a rubbish choice for Lunars.

                                Native charms are cheaper, have the same overall power level (I think they include less really powerful effects, but less weak charms too; obviously this is a bit difficult to work out as we only have Dex, plus it's just difficult generally), and are just as combo-able (I mean you can combine most of the Dex tree with most of the Strength tree, just like you can combine all those unarmed styles).

                                They also can be used with any weapon style. This means a)you have more tactical options, b)you can take Brawl, which you can also use in animal style, and c)you have a lot more options for evocations.

                                Currently, I think that Martial Arts is not an auto-pick for Lunars.

                                I think the combat builds are:
                                Native charms including DBT for general accuracy/damage/defence
                                Native charms for inflicting poison
                                Native charms for ranged (some of these charms are the same as above, some are different)
                                Native charms including DBT+Martial Arts
                                Native charms focused on shapeshifting
                                Native charms+Evocations
                                Native charms+Martial Arts+Evocations (but it really only works in very specific combos. So far, the only way is Frost-Thorn Knuckles and Tiger, but there'll probably be a handful more when we get the artefacts from Fangs at the Gate)
                                Native charms+Combat sorcery

                                Looking at these, I don't think I would definitely choose Martial Arts. Admittedly, I did make a Lunar martial artist, but just because my concept of "Snake-totem doctor who uses snake style" is one I've had for a while.
                                But I think there's plenty of other good options for combat. If I hadn't gone for Martial Arts, I think I'd have invested big in grappling and poison. Actually, I kind of want to invest in poison, but it doesn't really combo well with Snake's Martial Arts-produced poison, I think (we'll see; but Adder Fang Method doesn't. Nor do the Mutations). The ranged attack charms seem interesting (we'll see if Perception has more of them), and the Moonsilver Bow from the Core is just... well, OP, honestly. And there's always the traditional regenerating soak berzerker, though we'll see how good that is (I suspect... still good).
                                Not to mention turning into a giant snake! Or a tiny, stealthy snake, to sneak up and bite people, though that's really just for the first round. (I can still do that, but I've only got Brawl 3, so it's not that great. If I wasn't into Snake Style, I'd have Brawl 5).


                                Now, if, hypothetically, Martial Arts was the One True Build, then I would agree with you. But I don't think the Martial Arts charms are more powerful than Lunar native charms (which are cheaper), so there's no need to combine them when you can just take more native charms.

                                Or, if you want to take Martial Arts because you can buy it with Lunar XP, well you could also spend that on all those mediocre Abilities you have, or Evocations.
                                Because if you're not running DBT, and you're not into shapeshifting (and if you're into shapeshifting, you can't use MA anyway; spend your Lunar XP on latent abilities), then Evocations are a strong choice; the Resonant effects are, after all, kind of Solar-tier.

                                In fact, there's a whole thread on this forum on shapeshifting and the powerful, efficient builds you can go for with it.

                                Basically, as it is, I don't think Martial Arts is the One True Build for Lunars.
                                And if they can't combo Native charms and Martial Arts, I think it will be the One Rubbish Build. You'll get the occasional Lunar using the more powerful offensive styles (Single Point, Righteous Devil) and just defending with Lunar charms anyway (the same way Single Point stylists parry with Melee, and Righteous Devils use Dodge), but not much in the way of Tiger, Snake, Crane... Monkey, Mantis, Centipede, Falcon, White Reaper...
                                Ie the styles lots of Lunar characters should, genre-wise, be using.

                                I mean, fluff players are going to play Tiger-totem Tiger-Stylist, it's just that they'll then find it's really not that good.


                                As I said, I can accept that there may be certain combos of native charms+MA are broken. Snake Strikes the Heel with accuracy and re-roll charms is one Sandact6 brought up. Perhaps there are others. I suspect comboing Black Claw with Manipulation and Appearance charms is going to get brutal, though I'm not sure.

                                But if we nerf Lunars by taking away all the combos (adding an extra layer of hassle to combat, incidentally), then we need to do something to make Martial Arts a viable choice for Lunars.

                                Would you choose Martial Arts over those tasty Lunar charms?
                                Which styles would you choose? Which styles wouldn't you choose?

                                                        Originally posted by Epimetheus View Post
                                                        And they are and it's represented as Solar Brawl. An non-formalized set of abilities which give solars powerful unarmed techniques. Which isn't Martial Arts but I guess that distinction only matters to the person.
                                                        Solars have to choose between MA and Brawl. Is your intent that MA should be a strictly weaker option for them, in combat?

                                                        What about if a Solar martial artist has a Sidereal tutor? The Sidereal trains them diligently, even passing on the forbidden techniques of Sidereal Martial Arts; then the Solar has a duel with an equally-experienced master of Solar Brawl. Do you believe the Solar Martial Artist (with a Sidereal tutor, which is supposed to be a huge deal!) should be at a disadvantage against the master of Brawl?

                                                        There isn't an easy answer to this! It's haunted Martial Arts, SMA, Sidereals, and Solars since 1e. There's a lot of contradictory requirements, and it seems like you're only considering some of them:

                                                        1. Sidereal Martial Arts is the distinctive top-shelf combat ability of the Sidereal Exalted. They need to be (in some way) uniquely good at it. Having a Solar come along and (even with a Sidereal tutor) use your very best combat powers exactly the way you do is lame.

                                                        2. At the same time, Sidereals are also peerless teachers of martial arts, and their ability to pass on Sidereal Martial Arts to Solars exemplifies this. Learning SMA from a Sidereal should be a Big Deal for a Solar's development. I don't think it's sufficient for it to just add more options (Evocations, Sorcery, and CMA already add options), at least if those options are going to be noticeably weaker than Solar Brawl. Having a Sidereal tutor should be a character-defining thing for a Solar.

                                                        3. Solars are, on average, a bit better at combat than Sidereals, even when the Sidereal is using SMA (although in the modern day, Sidereal SMA masters tend to be massively more experienced than any Solar and will have a sharp advantage over them due to that.) SMA allows Sidereals to cheat in various ways - it's like one of the more cheaty Jojo's Bizarre Adventure stands - so "advantage" is a nebulous term here, but it's always been part of the game's design.

                                                        4. The combination of 1 + 3 means that a Solar who practices Sidereal Martial Arts really shouldn't be better at it than a Sidereal (or even use it identically to a Sidereal.) The combination of 2+3 means that a Solar who uses Sidereal Martial Arts has to be at least comparable in power to a Solar whose combat skill took a different path, though... and that "typical" Solar is a bit stronger than an equally experienced Sidereal. These things contradict each other.

                                                        Of course there's some ways around this. A Solar's power can be represented by more motes, larger dice pools, better "engine" charms from their native charmset, and so on. For example, if the defenses offered by Sidereal Martial Arts tend to, overall, not be quite as good as its offense or utility - ie. more on par with native Sidereal charms, while the other charms in SMA tend to be better than native Sidereal charms - a Sidereal using SMA will remain somewhat fragile compared to a Solar. Then you can even give Sidereals flavorful Sutra effects that emphasize attack or utility while keeping their glass cannon theme.

                                                        Another consideration is how Sidereals access Mastery benefits - I suspect that they'll get them in some way, but they may have to jump through some hoops rather than just getting them the way a Solar does. This could create a gap to give Sidereals those unique Sutra benefits, while keeping SMA as a reasonable option for combat oriented Solars. (This doesn't even require Mastery benefits in SMA, which could lead to issues with requirement 1 - Solars can benefit from easier access to normal MA Mastery benefits, and combine these stronger baseline MAs with SMA. But this requires some caution, because you still want SMA to be something that Solars want per point 2.)
