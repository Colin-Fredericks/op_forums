    [Ex3] Lunar Size, Strength, Limits, and Charms

    06-28-2016, 10:06 PM
    SHAPESHIFTING (page 545)
    A Lunar may pay four motes to assume any form he has taken. This transformation lasts indefinitely, until the Lunar reverts to his true human form (which is free and reflexive) or shifts into a different shape. Shapeshifting can be flurried, but not with an attack.

    Animal forms grant the Lunar access to all physical dice pools, as well as the animal’s special attacks and Merits. Latent or magical abilities are not granted by shapeshifting, but can be unlocked with training or Charms. Animals with Legendary Size are beyond the skill of most young Lunar shapeshifters, though this limitation can be overcome.



    LUNAR EXCELLENCIES (545)
    The Lunar Exalted tap into the raw potential of their Attributes to bolster their heroic feats, and can add up to (Attribute) dice to any action. However, with a stunt, they may add a second Attribute to the limit as long as the action is described in a way that fits the combination. For example, a Full Moon rolling (Dexterity + Brawl) to smash a Dragon-Blooded shikari would normally have a dice limit of (Dexterity), but could increase this to (Dexterity + Strength) if he described the attack in a way that evoked his raw brawn and mighty thews.

    When raising static values, Lunars can add up to (Attribute)/2, rounded down to the static value. With a stunt, they can draw upon a second Excellency, allowing them to add up to (Attribute + Second Attribute)/2, round down. Thus, a Dexterity 5, Dodge 5, and Intelligence 5 Lunar who attempts to evade an attack and describes how his keen mind discerns the rhythm of his opponent’s blows, helping him to avoid them, could raise his Evasion by up to 5.



    The Corebook does not specify how these two rules intersect, but I have a proposal.

    When the Lunar wears a form with a dice-pool larger than his own, each extra die counts against his dice-adding limit. When the Lunar stunts to add a second Excellency to his action, it is limited by his own natural Attribute score, even if it would be greater or lesser than that of the creature whose form he wears.

    Thus, a Strength 5, Athletics 5, and Stamina 5 Lunar who is currently a yeddim can only add one more die to his action with just his Strength Excellency alone. But stunting to include his Stamina Excellency will allow him to add a total of six more dice.

    If that same character is the form of a clawstrider and wants to apply his Stamina Excellency to improve his soak against a Withering attack, he cannot increase the static value of 7 at all. But stunting to include his Strength Excellency will allow him to add a total of 3 more points.

    These limitations should apply even if the form is the Lunar's own spirit shape, so as to remove mechanical incentives towards certain Totems and also avoid disincentives regarding any other sorts of Totems.


    Further, I wonder if the Charm which allows Lunars to assume the forms of creatures with Legendary Size should be more limited than the Towering Beast Form of past Editions; perhaps more like God-Beast Transcendence.

    Perhaps assuming the form of a creature with Legendary Size should not be allowed to flurry with any action, should cost at least one Willpower point, and perhaps should cost a certain number of motes per turn.

    In this way, a Lunar is still rewarded for having a gigantic, battle-worthy beast form, but encouraged to use humbler forms more often. A Lunar with a Tyrant Lizard Totem would usually prefer to use his Deadly Beastman shape for most of a fight, reserving his Spirit Shape for specific moments.

    For those Lunar fans inspired by characters such as Beast Boy, consider how little time he ever spends in the form of elephants, giant dinosaurs, and other megafauna. It should also be especially easy for fans of Steven Universe to imagine that "a power that big, comes with a bigger expense".


    That said, let's examine some ideas for Lunar Strength Charms!



    Undeniable Might - If your own Strength-based dice pool is larger than that provided by your stolen form, you may use that instead, but your Tell becomes more obvious. Even if another character has never seen your Tell before, they may make the disguise-checking roll to notice it.

    Devastating Ogre Enhancement - for the rest of the scene, the Lunar swells to twice her mass, her bones thickening and her muscles doubling. Her Strength and Stamina are increased, but the bonuses count against her Excellency dice-adding limits.

    Emperor Ox Expansion - for the duration of one attack or feat of strength, the Lunar explodes in size, striking with far greater mass, lifting with superior leverage, or absorbing damage with thicker flesh.

    Lightning Flash Might Methodology - for a single feat of strength, the Lunar can add a number of successes equal to the lesser of his Essence or Strength ratings, and does not need a stunt to justify adding his Stamina Excellency to his Strength Excellency.

    Mountainous Spirit Expression - the Lunar may use Heart's Blood to reflexively shapeshift into a creature larger than a man, but not so large as to have the Legendary Size merit.

    Hyena Jaw Technique -- This Charm allows a Lunar who crashes an opponent in a grapple to reflexively shapeshift into a form big enough to swallow her victim whole... or at least enclose them entirely within her jaws. The Lunar must have the Heart's Blood of a creature big enough for the job.

    Towering Beast Form - the Lunar may use the stolen Heart's Blood of a creature with the Legendary Size merit. Shapeshifting into such a form requires an action which cannot be flurried, costs one Willpower and two more motes to start, and costs two more motes each turn until the Lunar assumes a form without Legendary Size.

    God-Beast Transcendence - the Lunar may now flurry another action -- including an attack -- with an action to shapeshift into a creature with the Legendary Size merit.

    Body-Breaking Kata - the Lunar enjoys bonuses to damage and grappling control rolls against an opponent who is only half her size or smaller

      BIRTH OF FLIGHT
      3m, 1i; Supplemental, Instant; Decisive-only; Strength 3, Essence 1 – ???

      This supplements a decisive attack at any range. An enemy damaged by this Charm is knocked prone. This force is sufficient to end a rush against any target.

      If this Charm supplements a Decisive smashing attack, this Charm waives its Initiative cost, and doubles 10s on the Initiative damage roll.

      If this Charm supplements the special attack of a stolen form which can mimic the effects of the Heaven Thunder Hammer Solar Charm, this Charm waives its Initiative cost, and doubles 9s on the Initiative roll.


      But what happens when the Lunar learns an advanced Strength Charm which allows him to perform a trick like Heaven Thunder Hammer regardless of his form... and then he uses it with the HTH-like special attack of an animal form?

                  Given that Lunar Exalted have ways of increasing their effective Strength score just by using an Excellency or assuming an animal form (and given that these can be used for attack damage in ways that Solar Athletics lifting Charms cannot), I think the Lunar version of Strength Increasing Exercise could offer only a FLAT increase, as opposed to the Solar's Charm's variable increase.

                  The bonus Strength dot should also count against the Lunar's dice-adding limit, just like the higher dice-pools of a stronger animal form.


                  I might also suggest the possibility that "Devasting Ogre Enhancement" be canceled if the Lunar shapeshifts into anything but his human or DBT Form; thus a Lunar chooses to fight as a really beefy tiger, or he conserves his motes and fights more flexibly as any number of beasts.

                  This might be the pattern for various other Charms, like Armor-Forming Technique, Grasping Pseudopod Method (tentacles), Snake Head Defense (snake hair), and "Grow Wings Prana".
