    Just to be able to say that I got a start on the Perception Charms before sleeping, I’ll take a glance at the first few of them.

    The Heart’s Blood one, for instance, of which I do not believe I have any prior inkling. Face-Taker’s Gaze. Oh, now this is very interesting, having a certain call back to the portrayal of getting shape’s by killing in the last Edition, with the process of learning about the subject’s nature before the final blow. But it goes so much deeper here… that idea of becoming the person by a gradual transition of yourself to becoming akin to them in one of the more significant ways, knowing them and opening your heart to encompass and embody that knowledge. In some ways, I feel as though it might be one of the easiest sacred hunts to achieve, but it demands a lot of the Lunar, and can’t be something they’re casual about. Even if it doesn’t end up portending a lot about what Perception might do, it’s an intriguing process in its own right.

    (For the benefit of clarity: spend at least a week observing somebody to learn a Defining Intimacy, then form that same Intimacy and raise it to Defining; you get the person’s shape, and can’t change the Intimacy until the story ends. If the Intimacy in the target was not what you thought it was, hunt fails outright, and can’t be tried again that way for the rest of the story.)
    Oh wow, I thought that another Charm was going to be in the same boat as Intelligence, but Whispering Heart Revelation actually constitutes Perception getting an additional sacred hunt. Let’s see… right, acquire a person’s shape by determining extremely important secret of theirs. Not really much to say about this besides how incredibly invasive it must be. This one actually feels like it would often be so much effort that it might be the kind of thing you go for mainly because you’re really into acquiring somebody’s shape by prying really deeply into their lives. Hell, it might be the Heart’s Blood Charm of the technically narrowest application, if there happen to be a lot of people who don’t actually have such important secrets… although they’re tied directly to the scale of persuaded tasks, so it might be best to think of them less in terms of being actually, objectively important, and more things that they won’t casually reveal about themselves. Although even then… eh, it varies. Still neat.

    Oh, although it's the unusual Essence 2 Heart's Blood Charm, so... yeah, got to kind of dedicate yourself into it.

    Those will do for now, I think.

        I wanna say this was a thing they did on Wild Wild West a bunch and it's probably been done on lots of other series. I mean, I can't imagine why they wouldn't have done it on Mission Impossible, but I don't remember any specific instances.

        Anyway, the Wild Wild West had two main characters, James "Jim" West, a rough and tumble action hero, and Artemis Gordon, a master of disguise and gadgeteer genius. A good chunk of the episodes were "We've discovered that [Bad Guy] is plotting something in [Location]. Go to [Location] and find out what [Bad Guy] is doing and stop it." Jim would usually take the direct approach and get captured. This would usually entail him being brought before the villain and then, when he needed help, it would turn out that Artie had been disguised as one of the guards the whole time.

        As such, the setup for the charm is "Lunar with the charm goes off to pursue an objective off screen, while we follow the rest of the party." Play proceeds normally until something happens and a Big Damn Heroes moment is needed, at which point the "disguised Lunar" launches an Ambush attack or otherwise reveals themselves to save the day.

                    I’ve been interested in what Perception might do for a while with some of the ideas put forwards by the prior developers, which might not be completely defunct. That and how I think Second Edition Perception was one of the better cascades. It’s of course starting with Senses Charms.

                    Beginning with Ever-Wary Fox Technique, providing a non-Charm success to any Perception rolls opposing Stealth or detecting any hidden dangers, which can also be performed while asleep or incapacitated without penalty, which can awaken them or revive them from unconsciousness if their roll succeeds; it says that one still suffers the effects of incapacitation, but I’d have to check back to see what those are. Distant Thunder Anticipation is something a bit more specific, being a Charm to predict the weather within the next few hours (but not magical changes), but in addition to establishing that Perception might include some Survival stuff, it would fit for the situation a lot of Lunars might be in, particularly because a lot of times they might use such prediction to make plans to harass or escape enemies and prey. It also goes further at Essence 2, in which a Lunar gets to respond to a mundane change in the weather by introducing a fact about having made some preparation or contingency in anticipation, so yeah, the writing is actually capitalising on that image of weather prediction as an avenue for achieving bigger things.

                    Moonlit Sentinel Vigilance is a permanent enhancement to the Excellency and several other Charms for detecting threats, allowing a two mote surcharge to be made to let them last for a turn, applying those Charm benefits to any rolls to detect threats in that time, and only needing to use the surcharge once even for a combo of multiple Charms. Worry the Bone starts their means of opposing the Larceny of others, letting them add non-Charm dice to rolls to detect theft and becoming capable of contesting normally unnoticeable magic; I wonder if that’s limited to theft, or can also apply to some Stealth effects that might otherwise be absolute. To get in some of the Rage image, successful detection allows for immediately rolling Join Battle, with threshold successes functioning as bonus dice, and even getting to launch a surprise attack if their Initiative beats the thief’s and the first turn is used to attack. The Charm is themed about being protective of possessions, so it even gets to improve Defence and gambit difficulty against attempts to disarm them, allowing failed attempts to be hit with a decisive counterattack. Jeez, with benefits like that, it almost seems worth going around in a manner intended to invite attempted theft. It at least seems like a good way to draw a hidden Solar out into the open. Hell, use some of the Manipulation Charms to flare the Solar’s anima, exposing them to all.

                    Observed Prey Instinct lets their alertness safeguard them from hidden attacks, as they get to roll Perception + Awareness against unexpected attacks, removing their Defence penalty, and even against Ambushes, allowing them to apply a Defence at all; it starts off at the bare minimum, but more successes allow more Defence to be retained. Lunars get to take it with Dexterity if their spirit shape is a small prey animal.

                    Sense-Sharpening Change conveys the idea that a Lunar can recalibrate their senses to fit a situation, in the form of selecting one sense (being able to stack for more than one, with costs discounted down to one mote) for which they get to reroll 1s until they’re gone and ignore their Essence in penalty points, so long as those penalties are for overstimulation or understimulation; the given examples are sight being able to overcome too much or too little light, but not obscuring fog or things being far away. That penalty relief does get to apply to any rolls that would be messed up so, not just Awareness things.

                    Heh, Stricken Calf Sense has a very quirky and novel basis for being able to reroll 5s and 6s on rolls to diagnose hurt and sickness and make the examination instant; applying a predatory instinct for detecting weaknesses. Has to make for a poor bedside manner, the sense that your doctor looking you over is pulling in an impulse to eat you. It’s also one of the more unusual examples of a Charm applying to different processes, as the dice trick can also be applied to rolls to detect concealment or follow tracks, in addition to a non-Charm success if the quarry is afflicted with something that the Lunar has diagnosed in the past. So yeah, be careful not to piss your Lunar doctor off, or else they might actually chase you down and eat you.

                    The name Spider-Amid-Roses Discernment puts me in mind of how being in that role might be something well suited to Lunars, creating an image that familiarity allows a Lunar to gird themselves against it, lending more to the whole apex predator mythos; the Charm itself doubles 8s for scenting rolls to detect poison or contamination, whether it be to protect oneself against a tainted foodstuff or diagnosing somebody else, in addition to sniffing out a trap or enemy that might try to poison or infect them. At Essence 3, they can even detect completely odourless substances, on the logic that they deduce them from surrounding smells being displaced or diluted, and when in an animal shape with a good sense of smell, they instead get to double 7s.
                    Prey’s Scent Discernment touches a bit on some of those ideas I alluded to around Intelligence, as it grants a permanent enhancement that lets a Lunar log the scent of somebody detected or examined in that way with Awareness, in addition to reading intentions or profiling, to be able to spend two motes automatically when in that character’s presence to recognise them, either instantaneously or with a non-Charm success to see through magical disguises or scent concealment. The log only lasts for a day for most characters, but a Solar mate or somebody to whom there is a higher Tie can be retained indefinitely. A repurchase at Essence 2 even allows for the scent to be picked up and logged after the fact from casing a scene or tracking, so long as they’re not too old, and being in the right animal shape allows the recognition to be made without motes.

                    Heightened Sense Method expands a bit on Sense-Sharpening, although not in the form of a Permanent Charm; it’s able to cover penalties that the other one wouldn’t, and able to do it at a higher number while still at lower Essence, but it doesn’t reroll 1s, and it’s only an Instant Charm. However, if it’s used while the other Charm is in effect for the same sense, the two together are able to fully eliminate penalties. It’s a curious approach, but it doesn’t fall into being a speed bump, and I think it’s good for Lunars to be able to expand their senses so much even at the start.
                    Echo-Drinking Awareness was previewed, straight up echolocation (albeit now with the context of being able to see how Sense-Sharpening Change gets to apply hearing benefits to vision-based penalties), and being able to double 9s for rolls in a shape with Echolocation. One thing that I don’t think the preview version had was something unique among Protean Charms, being able to enhance a mutation in human form. I guess there’s a question of it not being too powerful, and just the general feel of how echolocation should work for a character. Mind, the statement on how that can benefit the true human shape makes me wonder if my assumption that social Protean Charms can work for connections to your true identity has been in error. I’ll try and find a clarification for that at some point.

                    Into a lengthier one with Prowling Stalker Concentration. Hmm, make a roll following a lengthy examination of a discrete location like a building, and with bonus non-Charm successes if examining somewhere within a claimed territory, to store successes that can be naked for a day. They can then be applied as non-Charm dice up to Essence or 3 to actions within the location related to deep familiarity with it; concealed danger, Larceny actions to infiltrate it, Stealth within, Athletics around, Lore for facts about the place, Influence to get in or calm down confrontation, and resisting environmental hazards or traps. It’s a real casing a location out kind of Charm, and I think the bonus to Lore especially can be very beneficial; do things like introduce ventilation shafts that a Tiny shape could sneak into or out of. Sure it can only be used once per day, but this feels like something in which you would want to put it on a focus for a session.

                    Sense-Borrowing Method is an old mainstay, although I think the need to make a roll against the animal’s Resolve to establish the connection through which their senses can be perceived is new. Plus the manner in which it designates being able to remotely get the use of the animal’s sensory Merits. I’m interested in the detail of being able to notice details an animal would overlook, and vice versa. But what’s really interesting is the use on familiars; Lunars haven’t had too much to do with them so far, but being able to make the Charm, that normally lasts only days, both Indefinite and have no mote commitment seems like it’s a very significant incentive for a Lunar to have one. And then they get to go and also apply it to humans and magical animals, and then spirits or fae (who don’t even need to be animalistic) as they make repurchases with growing Essence, which probably consolidates a few things that were spread out before. Mind, none of this allows one to actually direct the subject of your target, but I think that might be part of the fun, having to be discerning about who you connect to.

                    Unwavering Predator’s Eye is another straightforward Attribute substitution, being able to use Perception in rolling Join Battle, with the option to spend some motes to give the roll double 9s. That line continues with Wolf-Eye Advantage, to allow allow 1s on that roll, or in opposition to Stealth in combat, to be rerolled, with the special benefit of beating Stealth with the Charm while under Sense-Sharpening affording some Initiative. And then it’s into Essence 2, starting off with the Permanent Charm Argent Goddess Eye, another instance of Lunar self-edification as they can draw motes equal to rolled 9s and 10s on Perception rolls that unveil hidden dangers, as well as to reading and profiling if they have the later Charm Understanding the Prey.

                    Blood on the Wind provides some particularly good enhancements to tracking, to represent a Lunar’s relentless pursuit; they get to add non-Charm dice for every rolled 10, reroll 1s, apply tracking to characters who have covered themselves magically, and in an animal with a good nose also get non-Charm dice equal to wound penalty. I can see this one synergizing very nicely with the power to pursue a person that smells of poison or disease, especially if you put it there. Mind, it’s concerned with tracking, but now I’m put in mind of the question of whether scent rules ought to account for things like wind direction; if a Wyld Hunt might give itself away by being upwind from an alert Lunar. Perhaps that’s too much intricacy.

                    Anyway, for something more focused on preternatural battle awareness, Instinctive Supremacy Approach allows the Lunar who wins in Join Battle to spend some Initiative to give their first action in a fight, whatever the Attribute, a free Excellency, and they don’t even need to pay the Initiative if they had a Sharpened-Sense at the time; it really pays to be a Lunar who continuously keeps at least one of their senses at its best. And Shadow-Scouring Persistence, through which one gets to make a special gambit against characters in concealment of whom the Lunar is aware; probably often people who became concealed partway through the fight. The attack and Initiative rolls gets extra successes, rolled against a difficulty of the Stealth action’s successes; if the gambit wins, the other character gets dragged out of concealment, leaving them exposed again to all combatants, so it’s probably only really useful when in battle against somebody when the Lunar is alongside allies whose capacity to see through that Stealth isn’t as good (might be helpful for when alongside battle groups). It’s a Simple Charm, but can be used reflexively and without using up an attack if in a shape with improved senses.

                    Already it’s up to Essence 3, beginning again with a Permanent Charm, Fivefold Transcendent Insight to enhance Sense-Sharpening Change to allow it to function indefinitely with a surcharge of one-Willpower, which suffices to extend any number of activations for different senses. To further represent their growing divine sensory acuity, the Lunar gets all Perception Charms discounted, and Argent Goddess Eye doubles its reward, if all five senses are enhanced simultaneously. This feels like a certain subtle kind of growing transformation, or perhaps just a greater depth of instinctual alignment.

                    Hundred-Eyed Watcher is another Permanent enhancement, this time for sense borrowing; if the Lunar pays another three motes and one Willpower to activate that Charm, it gets to establish a connection to all instances of a particular type of animal within long range. It’s not just being able to switch between different individuals to look through, the Lunar is able to take in their information simultaneously, as their Perception rolls gets to add dice equal to the Size representing the group; it’s a lesser benefit if they’re a Miniscule animal, but it still amounts to being able to absorb and process information fed in by a large group of creatures, and get to ignore a sensory penalty if most of the connected animals wouldn’t be blocked by it. In addition to the surcharge, the cost of it is that the connection will be broken with any animal that strays out of range, and the Lunar is obligated to remain in meditation, ending fully if they do otherwise; there’s also not provision for this to work on anything besides animals. Still, even those might be countermanded a bit when the Lunar gets to repurchase it at Essence 4, and extend the range to several miles, or everything within a claimed territory; choose the right animal in the right place, and it might be just about impossible to approach a Lunar unannounced. Alternatively, place oneself in the middle of a city and link with all of the rats or spiders, and you can get insight into a lot of people’s secrets (which, with the right other Charm, can also acquire their shapes). There’s even a provision with a certain later Charm by which a Lunar can activate the Charm reflexively, waive the Willpower surcharge, and make it Indefinite (although I do wonder if that means that it will keep going while not meditating).

                    The name Scent of Bygone Ages alludes a bit to something that prior developers would often suggest as a more cerebral Lunar power, although the realisation is looking a bit more grounded, as it’s basically a casing action that can apply on something that happened in the recent past, occurs instantly, and has its roll take penalties for the number of days gone by, which the sense improving Charms cannot mitigate. If successful, they get to reconstruct the events that are being cased in terms of revelation conveyed by scents, such as recognition for familiar people, a general layout of strangers, and where and how the characters were; that bars them from information that would leave no such marker, barring very creative stunting; perhaps somebody with a strong smell on their breath would leave subtle variations from which a Lunar would get a vague sense of spoken words. The conditions to increase the interval of time represented by each point of penalty are actually more generous that I would have assumed; things like having a log of the scene of somebody present, stuff to do with high Intimacies, and being at least Essence 5. It’s another one that, compared directly to a Solar equivalent, ends up a bit weaker, but is sufficiently characteristic to Lunars that I think it hardly matters.

                    Okay, the much vaunted Watchful Spider Stance: a bit costly to activate, but significantly expands the range of a Lunar’s senses while meditating. Eyesight goes in every direction and once and sees out to a mile at the least, hearing and scent extends to Essence miles, or fives times that for anything that would make a big impression, taste and touch gets double 7s (I guess that can be useful for examination of something that is brought directly to the Lunar). It can be further enhanced while within a claimed territory, being able to extend throughout the reaches of that boundary, and perceive from whichever angle as to ignore distance or obstruction; imagine putting a rock between yourself and the Lunar’s citadel, unaware that the Lunar will be able to see (and smell and hear) you directly through the rock. There’ll be another Charm, probably in Stamina, allowing for hibernation, and the Charm can be used at a greatly reduced (although still fairly long) range to provide information even while in the static form. And as one last special benefit, an Essence 4 repurchase allows one to enjoy the benefits of the Charm while retaining mobility if at least ten motes are committed to Sense-Sharpening Change, albeit at the reduced duration of a day (and I guess the benefit of combining this with sense borrowing is being able to add the Size of a group of animals to your rolls). Yes, this is a nice one both for its tactical value and giving a sense of what the general experience of being a Lunar might be, a certain spirituality to it.
                    Continuing the means of tracking a person down, Inevitable Spoor Discovery is an Instant Charm allowing one to make a tracking roll with bonus dice, unimpeded by terrain or age of the tracks, and following somebody from up to half the Lunar’s Essence miles away even if that should be impossible. If a tracking roll is successful, the Lunar can then choose to commit those motes to grant themselves a pool of non-Charm dice equal to rolled 9s and 10s, which they can then apply to further rolls to track, profile their quarry or examine their actions, or read intentions, dice which can be retained indefinitely until all are used up or the Charm ends. An appropriate animal shape extends the range by which somebody can initially be pursued to the Lunar’s full Essence in miles. Just overall another nice addition to the Charms for pursuing undeterred.

                    Similarly, Mystery-Stalking Pursuit applies much the same benefits to casing a scene, albeit with rerolling failures instead of bonus dice and only banking rolled 10s, although those get to be added as successes for rolls to pursue clues uncovered by the initial casing (also not needing the motes to be committed to holding them, although using the Charm again resets the bank, that otherwise lasts for the story). It can only be used once a day, but upholding a higher Intimacy by following up on the leads will reset it.

                    Darn it, my mind won’t stop going to Detective Pikachu.

                    Not many left now… Red Visions Realized: make two Join Battle rolls, applying any benefits provided by Charms to both, keep the better one, and get Willpower if that wins the Initiative order. Dread Beast’s Eye: get to roll Join Battle again after crashing an enemy who was at higher Initiative, getting to apply a free Excellency to the roll (can only be used once per scene, is reset by incapacitating an enemy with at least 30 Initiative). Both seem like good solid ways to harness that roll.

                    And last of the Sense Charms, skipping straight to Essence 5, is Omniscient Instinct Concentration, which… was seen ages ago in preview. To recap, Lunar becomes permanently endowed with being able to spend one Willpower to preternaturally sense something that threatens or importantly affects the subject of a Defining Intimacy or the Solar mate, from up to 500 miles away, or the entire realm of existence if meditating in Watchful Spider Stance (as well as being inherently aware of the death and rebirth of one’s Solar mate, with no cost or limit). Probably very useful for the Silver Pact leadership to keep on top of a number of important things, as well as general major sacred guardian beast stuff.




                    Sense Charms seem a bit fewer and the cascades a bit more direct than some of the prior Attributes, but they’re still wringing a lot out of the concept, including a few that are immensely powerful, while generally things to effectively cover the basic idea of not having your sensory input and examination impeded.

                    Normally I would call it a night here, but… who am I kidding, I wasn’t going to be sleeping anyway, and I’m already behind, I might as well push through the remaining Perception Charms.

                    I have approximate knowledge of many things.

                        Okay, finishing off the Perception Charms by looking at the Scrutiny category. I’ll admit, I’m not quite sure to what to expect here that would be distinct from Senses.

                        First Charm is Understanding the Prey, providing double 9s on rolls for reading intentions, character profiling and seeing through disguises, followed by Serpent-Revealing Savvy, which is used following a successful read intentions to discover a motive the Lunar opposes, allowing threshold successes on that roll to provide bonus Resolve against the character attempting to influence them in a manner that would serve that motive.

                        So it’s about figuring people out? Maybe situations as well? I won’t nitpick that, given what the overall value of these subcategories is. For the Charms themselves, I think that the latter is giving this sense of being innately contrary and adaptation to that effect, which is cute.

                        Hmm, Forked Tongue Discernment… might be that this is should be viewed as helpful for opposing things encompasses by Manipulation in particular, both in seeing through their dissembling and not being taken in by lies. That actually reminds me that I had thought Perception would be the one to help figure people out when assessing Raksi, but somehow I felt the subject had already come up… I think some of the topics might be bleeding together. As for this Charm, it’s the Lunar method to judge whether or not a character’s statement is true or not, in the form of a reflexive special read intentions roll with bonus successes, which I honestly like more than even the Third Edition version of Judge’s Ear Technique. Mind, this one actually goes a bit further than even the Solar Charm does… wait, no it doesn’t, it’s just that continued invocations across the scene against a person that has already been judged by the Lunar work automatically without roll, representing having gotten a measure of their telltale signs. Even if a character employs an effect that lets Guile automatically win, the Lunar gets to oppose it with a dice roll again, this time with bonus dice instead of successes. Overall, though, it’s a slightly more involved process for being able to get into a person’s head enough to assess their honesty, so it’s compelling on that front.

                        Something a bit longer with Understanding the Herd… Lunar gets to supplement a regular read intentions or profile character action to get additional information on where the target’s home culture is, and what their Ties to that culture might be. It’s actually a Charm that can receive false information, as it will provide insight into the cultural background of a disguised identity, if the initial roll wasn’t also good enough to exceed the disguise successes. There’s good advice on how to convey that information in cases where the Lunar character might not be expected to be directly familiar with the culture in question, and even the notion that the player might be permitted to introduce a fact describing it, using the read or profile roll’s successes to determine the extent of that. Furthermore, an Essence 2 repurchase allows one to not merely determine the culture, but use the Charm again to assess the character’s role and status within it, plus hierarchical relationships to other characters from the culture in the same scene and the target character’s Ties to those people. So the Charm can be very useful even if you’re actually going around within the society that you’ve connected people to.

                        I’ve got to say, Perception is looking like a very useful backup to Changing Moons; not exactly unfitting to No Moons, but… actually, my first impression has changed, I can read this side of Perception as being more fitting to that Caste if viewed in terms of them also having more of a social intelligence side, which effectively rounds out that Caste in its own right, which might also bridge Intelligence to Wits (Perception being helped by also serving as a means of expanding avenues for absorbing raw information). A bit distinct from the Changing Moon thesis of insinuation, navigation and personal inscrutability.

                        Fellow Hound Insight helps a Lunar assess the value of their human shape library, as it forms a special read intentions action with bonus successes, that will assess whether the target knows anybody that the Lunar can turn into, as well as the Ties to that identity (yet more that’s valuable to the social Attributes). It apparently takes magic to apply any kind of Guile against that roll, so it’ll just succeed straight up in the majority of cases. Ah, there’s a bit of a mini-upgrade that comes automatically from reaching Perception 4, and I had misread the earlier part; the basic Charm requires the Lunar to try and find if the target has a connection to a specifically designated shape in the library, and the mini-upgrade is the one to get the measure of if a person knows anybody that the Lunar can turn into, although only one at a time if the person might know several. Still, even the basic version has an amusing connotation; it gives me the image of a Lunar opportunistically hunting a person who happens to be doing something of which the Lunar didn’t approve (having done due diligence of assessing where they came from first), later deciding to visit the place for whatever reason, and then going around reading random people to try and find somebody who knows the shape, and work their way back into that life (or possibly know somebody to avoid). It takes three Charms and at least Perception 3 to get this stuff, but it feels like a worthwhile sideline for a majority of Changing Moons.

                        Okay, this tree tops out its Perception min with the Permanent Expected Guest Insight, which enhances the prior Charm to allow it to instead assess whether or not an observed target has positive or negative Ties to any category which includes person or being that is within the Lunar’s library. That is to say, if a target hates the Realm and the Lunar can turn into a bunch of officials or soldiers, or has a great fondness for wolfhounds, the Lunar will be able to know of it. They can still only get one insight at a time, although the Lunar is allowed to either ask after feelings towards a group that they specify, or just freestyle it to learn of any connection to a randomly chosen one. Hmm, okay, four Charms and a maximized Attribute takes it a little bit from a sideline into a dedication.

                        Hmm, with Taboo-Observing Reverence, I’m getting an impression that a lot of the general purpose of these Essence 1 Scrutiny Charms is a means of providing some of the information that many Charms in a bunch of the other Attributes are dependent on. Not that there’s anything wrong with that, and the realisation is actually a lot more invested when it could have likely gotten away with being more workmanlike. The minutiae of the Charm are still a bit different; rather than being automatic, the read intentions roll has a difficulty dependent on the obscurity of the custom that the Lunar is trying to gain an insight into. There’s also a note on how it’s a read roll that gets to use Lore instead of Socialize, but only if the Ability’s background might encompass the culture in question. Success is described as giving a sense of the custom’s nature and purpose, in a manner permitting flawless replication of it, to the point that performing it within the scene grants Willpower. The read intentions roll also gets double duty if it beats the Guile of participants in a practice that the Lunar had observed for the Charm, allowing them to know one Intimacy from each valid target related to it. The Charm only gets one use per scene, so the education might be a bit slow.

Honestly, I feel rather remiss from having never once considered that mechanic myself as something helpful for Lunar cerebral powers in a hunting or harassing fashion.

It can only be used once per scene, but considering the nature of scenes in which it might be used, once probably makes an effective prelude before throwing oneself around the movers and shakers as deemed necessary (if not cautious).

Hmm, in the end a bit more finely tuned detective than prophet, but still neat.                                     Right, Mysticism Charms. Funny thing, these were a topic that, when looking back over the Second Edition Perception Charms the other night, I paid especial attention to, and they were prominent in a thought I had on those Charms that’s been growing through the last few weeks of comparing them, which I might talk about later. Spirit-Rending Fury is… oh, so is basically all of the spirit attacking stuff going to be here? Mmmm I can get on board with that; does basically fit the Attribute’s element of sensory enhancement on every axis. But yeah, Charm gives a tick-long enhancement to physical interaction and attack to get at the immaterial, although this one comes with a slight drawback of affording that same potential back to other beings.

                                    I like the imagery behind Demon-Drinking Fang as their spirit killing Charm, the extra damage dice to something lower on the Initiative order to convey the sense of slaughtering prey, feels very Werewolf the Forsaken Second Edition. Ohh, and Permanent Charms to keep enhancing that in even more proprietary directions… Shed Divinity’s Nectar lets the Lunar spend Willpower with the death blow to internalise the spirit’s Essence, acquiring mutations reflecting the spirit’s nature and capabilities and nature, and the value of its worshippers for the duration of a story, with the mild cost of taking on a Major Intimacy to carry on some of the spirit’s legacy, and an option to either digest it or spit it back out to reincarnate when the Charm runs out. Does it quite make up for not being able to shapeshift into them? Jeez, it’s an Essence 1 Charm, and I already think it’s more than some people deserve. And hey, my ongoing quest to let a Lunar give their human shape functional wings with impunity continues (even if you’d need to take down an Essence 4 winged spirit early on to manage this).

                                    From Essence 2, Devil-Restraining Grip allows a grapple gambit against an immaterial being that can use Perception and Occult for the control roll, and if successful lets the spirit be dragged into physical form, with additional power while in a shape with special grappling abilities or Legendary Size. I’ve got to imagine the occasional No Moon who dealt with a troublesome local god by making a big show of grabbing their ephemeral form by the throat, pulling them into the world, and leaving them surrounded and unarmed by angry villagers, flying away Iron Man style.

                                    Scent of Midnight Dweomers (I’m pretty sure that when I saw this name earlier, I assumed it was a typo) is their ticket into other worlds that people have often wanted Lunars to be roaming in; they get to make a roll to detect the presence of entrances to places such as sanctums or the Wyld, Malfeas or Underworld, and which one they go to (I presume the entrance needs to be in proximity), pulling double duty as a general weirdness detector as they get to try and sense the presence of ongoing sorcery or necromancy processes and identify their functions, and determine the nature and powers of hearthstones, demesnes and manses. To top it all off, they can determine the nature of spirits and fae by smelling them, although the roll to do so is a bit harder if they’ve disguised themselves as a different manner of being.

                                    Hmm, Divinity-Stealing Whisper, eh? In the presence of a god or a place sacred to them, get to roll Perception + Occult against a difficulty based on the spirit’s own strength and the size of their Cult, but with the benefit of doubles 8s and any magic that can aid hearing, in order to eavesdrop on prayers directed at the god; rather than taking them in individually or being able to process the many parts of the whole, Lunar is able to discern the basic gist of what worshippers are asking after. The Lunar is then afforded the means of co-opting the god’s position if it’s determined that they have no intention of answering the prayers, by providing the desired result in a manner that most worshippers can recognise; the reward for this is acquiring the entire Cult of lower Essence spirits, or taking one dot away from those stronger than the Lunar, as well as a capacity to gain the god’s Eclipse Charms if they’ve bought into another Charm. That’s certainly one way for No Moons to make gods behave, or else Lunars to gird themselves for other battles, bring down the unfeeling and comfortable mighty, or set themselves up as divinities. It does feel rooted in the sense that many Lunars are objects of worship in their own domains.

                                    Claw That Rends the Veil at Essence 3 is a scene long enhancement to make some general spirit fighting a bit easier, bestowing a kind of foxfire aura to their regular attacks, a natural weapon, or one bonded to their body, to allow that means of attack to get decent discounts on the Charms for aggravated damage and grappling into the material, although wielding that aura so revulses spirits that they experience the Lunar as Hideous (also getting to use their Perception instead of Appearance to determining dice added to threaten actions, which they get to reflexively make against spirits upon activating the Charm). It adds a nice bit of flair to Lunar activity in that world, and I’m getting an amusing image from picturing a Lunar applying their sensory acuity and discernment as a means of intimidation; perhaps making show of looking at the spirit through the flame, combining both its distressing aura and the implication of being watched very hungrily.

                                    I think Silver Curtain Parted (slip through sanctum entrances, cross realm of existence boundaries or get past sorcerous barriers) was previewed before. Not much more to say on it here beyond liking a Totem effect recognising migratory animals (which can be pretty damn broad) and admittedly becoming intrigued at the prospect of a new Wyld Migration Formation Charm.

                                    Devil’s Hidden Footprints functions as an enhancement to Scent of Midnight Dweomers by allowing it to sniff out the traces of a magic that has elapsed or was more temporary, right down to a flaring anima, although the difficulty to do so grows as each month goes by. It’s a bit more of a restricted Charm than usual, as the basic success only tells that something happened and how long ago, and getting more specific and useful information requires dedicating extra successes; from minor things like what the Essence rating of the user was or if it was one’s Solar mate, up through things like the specific nature of the power, an Artifact it might have belonged to in general terms, and an overall character nature and profile, with one of the effects in between letting the scent be added to the library for later use.

                                    God-Body Consumption basically functions identically to one of the Intelligence Charms for acquiring Eclipse powers, the commitment and limit, the option to but them permanently, right down to being able to get them from appropriate human characters when their shapes are taken non-lethally. The Lunar just needs to permanently kill a spirit to internalise these powers. Not much else to say about it, despite its heavy implications.

                                    Two Worlds Penumbra helps out with the whole emissary to or for the spirit world angle, as the illumination of the Lunar’s anima can become capable of rendering immaterial beings visible, or even tangible when close enough, although ones stronger than the Lunar can spend Willpower to become immune (although doing so requires a Decision Point if they fear the Lunar’s current shape, as a Protean effect).

                                    And last of all, once again skipping straight to Essence 5, is Omen-Beast’s Evil Eye, a Permanent Charm that denotes a whopping 30 motes (plus one Willpower) as a surcharge cost. The Charm enhances Far-Seeing Storm Crow (i.e. the Charm to introduce facts about somebody’s weakness), with the additional cost allowing something between a prediction and a curse to make that weakness into something that will doom the character in a coming challenge. The difficulty for the fact introducing roll is the target’s Essence rating, in addition to a modifier determined by how bad the doom will be; something that will only cause serious personal difficulties such as loans being called in or having a political challenger provide no modifier, a distinct danger such as being set upon by hired goons or hidden poisoners increases it by three, and a debtor becoming enslaves or a rebellion of breakout or something else that can alter the course of a life adds a full five to it. Jeez, and here was me thinking it would just be some kind of sabotage in a fight like a reserved botch, not the Lunar flavour of being a terrible prophet of doom. And part of that Lunar flavour is not being able to merely invoke it from afar; the target has to be forewarned of what’s coming for them, and given a way out (although they only get five to ten days to achieve that). It doesn’t seem to necessarily have to be something logically or thematically connected to the doom, making it a bit more uncanny and mystical; in basic terms it can’t even be anything beyond merely inconvenient (I’m guessing beyond time it might take to bring it about, even if within the realms of possibility), with more significant escape clauses requiring a decent number of threshold successes. The Charm also requires those motes to be committed, and the Lunar needs to reiterate the nature of the doom and the flaw resulting in it on a daily basis, lest they forget what lesson there may be. It’s also helpfully noted that the nature of the achieved doom is not absolute; it can be adjusted a bit by the intervening circumstances (break all the spinning wheels said to be capable of pricking you and driving into deep sleep, and destroying all the wheels will probably just switch it to knitting needles or something, or even a fork). More than even that, the character and player who are facing the curse are afforded an opportunity to fight back in the moment it unfolds, with the right applications having a fighting chance to oppose or prevent it (also now considering how, unlike the Solar Charms, can only target individals). A once per story Charm that gets reset by accomplishing a legendary goal exploiting a character’s flaw or helping them overcome their weakness.
