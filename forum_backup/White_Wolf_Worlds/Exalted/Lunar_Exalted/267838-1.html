                    Originally posted by While My Guitar View Post
                    Or one that you CAN use in a lot of forms, but it's not always useful.
                    I'm more fine with this.

                    I was thinking of the example of one of your forms being an emaciated lion that just looks crazed because when you took it's Heart's Blood, it was half-starved and going mad from hunger. If this is the form that a group of locals finds you in, you don't necessarily want to be forced to shift into a cute lil bunny form in order to do some kinds of social-fu - maybe you're trying to get them to care for the local lions, maybe you don't want to reveal yourself as an Exalt (and shapeshifting would, at the very least, reveal you as not a normal thing). So they find you in this form and you unleash some non-verbal Charms, which you're using to...I dunno, create an Intimacy tied to emaciated lions and evoke feelings of pity and protectiveness of them. Since this isn't a cute and cuddly form, maybe they get a bonus to resist or you take a penalty. Either way you still get to try and accomplish your goal in the form you choose to be in, without being forced to change forms to accomplish that goal with your Charms

                    And you should be able to use these same Charms in yeddim shape to maybe warm the hearts of caravan-drivers who beat yeddim, or in stag shape to awe hunters into no longer hunting trophy bucks, or in salmon shape to convince them not to overfish, or whatever.
