                            Originally posted by Blaque View Post
                            Just because something can be the case doesn't mean it's obligated to be so. The authors and devs probably have an idea of where they want to go with them and part of that seems to be "tightening-up" the Lunar aesthetic. Part of this seems to be less Mercer or The THING and more classical and primative sources.

                            Part of an annoyance some folks have about Lunar fans is how much of everyone's hopes and dreams, despite the divergence, got mixed into them. Such that for some people there is this entire "alternate Lunar" idea out there that can subsist on its own if we were to delete everything originally printed about Lunar Exalted. The Venn diagram of what folks wnat from the splat is complicated as shit and the team seems to be in the mindset of putting their foot odwn and picking a cross section to stick to rather than please everyone.

                            And stuff.
                            Indeed. The way 2e Lunars were written is particularly unhelpful for this transition. They seemed to have a bit of just about everything, but all very inconsistently. It's like they were using a really weird version of River Reflects Desire where almost nobody* is happy with what they see, but they see whatever combination of deletions and emphasis they individually favor as the obvious fixes to make.

                            * Of those who pipe up on these boards, anyway.

                            This is why I find it particularly important to get the idea of what EX3 Lunars are to be clear as crystal. And for this to happen as far out from their book as possible. Those who go into the Lunars book really looking forward to a radically different idea of Lunars than the book will give them will find some level of unnecessary disappointment. If they know clearly what Lunars are well in advance, they won't be expecting something they're not going to get. Some may have an amount of disappointment, anyway, but it will at least be further removed; they'll be able to better appreciate EX3 Lunars for what they actually are.
