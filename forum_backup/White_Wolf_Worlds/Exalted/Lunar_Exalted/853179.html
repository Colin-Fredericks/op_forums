                  Frog totem - an ugly witch-girl alchemist (it makes a change from the normal bikini witches) who is a master of poison-making and hallucinogens.

                  Raven - a Northern warrior, valkeyrie-esque, one-eyed, but also a loremaster.

                  Black dog - Indian, Graeco-Roman or Celtic-themed protector of the dead, uses necromancy to keep the dead where they belong

                  Hedgehog - prickly but cute

                  Salamander - pyromancer (Ifrit Lord pact sorceress) with regeneration and poison charms

                  Cuckoo - spy or undercover agent

                  Shrike - a butcher. I mean, literally that was her job before she exalted. Brutal, makes art of her enemies' entrails (perhaps she pushes people away because of issues?)

                  Snake - a (No Moon) doctor. Who also uses snake style, obviously (which saves the ST having to work out how to do her combat charms).

                  Cat - stealthy burglar. Loves the finer things in life, luxury, jewels, etc. If it's a black cat, also a sorceress.

                  Pig - fat but friendly. Very intelligent.

                  Goat - You can do a satanic wizard vibe with this, with goat horns or hooves as the tell. But I feel it works better for men than women (beard, randy old goat)

                  Otter - crafter (of mundane stuff, not artefacts, because Lunars aren't that great at crafting compared to Solars)

                  Octopus - Cthulu-girl! Mad prophet sorceress.

                  Shark - berzerker warrior

                  Monkey - mischeivous prankster

                  Vampire bat - basically, everything you associate with Dracula except being undead. Charming but creepy northern aristocrat.

                  Is there something the group is bad at, that isn't combat? That it'd be useful to have someone who's good at it?
                  (I say not combat, because frankly, having extra NPCs to help the team in combat is kind of annoying for the ST, easier just to have lower-powered encounters)

                                                      Armadillo-Lunar seems kind of obvious too.

                                                      A good source of ideas is finding the mythic associations of animals in different cultures.

                                                      For example, Chinese astrology is easy enough to research, and you can see the associations for each of the 12 animals. You probably think that something like a Snake is rather common, but what about a Chicken, Ox or Sheep?

                                                      Or myths like the African trickster myths which generally feature animals like spiders or hares.

                                                      Birds are good too, because a lot of birds have different associations. For example:

                                                      A wise, bespectacled scholar - Owl
                                                      A keen hunter - Eagle
                                                      A shameless clotheshorse hot guy - Peacock (doesn't work for girls unfortunately)
                                                      An infiltrator - Cuckoo
                                                      A necromancer or war looter - Crow
                                                      A sailor - Seagull
                                                      A singer - Nightingale, Robin, tons of others
                                                      A pacifist - Dove
                                                      A thief - Magpie

                                                      Etc. Perhaps not very exciting though.
