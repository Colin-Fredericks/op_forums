                            I'd like Lunars to favor five Abilities, one of which absolutely does not have to be Survival. The way it was back in First Edition (when you couldn't favor any out-of-Caste Attributes, but let's ignore that).

                            With the exception of Martial Arts Style Charms, Lunars couldn't learn any Charms at favored costs because of Abilities. Second Edition did give you one favored Ability to pick (in addition to Survival), but the only mechanically sensible choice was Martial Arts.

                            Even spells are favored through Intelligence rather than Occult... though given how difficult picking an out-of-Caste Attribute to favor is, perhaps Lunars should be able to enjoy favored Sorcery costs through favoring Occult.

                            Though if Martial Arts Style Charms are going to be based on abilities other than Brawl/Martial Arts -- if the purely sword-based Styles are going to be at least partially Melee-based, or even totally Melee-based! -- then the pressure to always choose just one particular Ability to favor is off.


                            As for Attributes, I'd like Lunars to be able to trade one Caste Attribute to favor an additional out-of-Caste Attribute. I seldom see a case in which I would personally want to do this, but it's a little bit more flexibility at the cost of basically nothing.
