It reminds me of a question I asked Vance about if A really worthy mortal was cursed into the shape of a beast like a Falcon when the Exaltations were released how would that work. Vance said they wouldn’t be passed over and the Exaltation of the Sun would likely break the curse or at least return the ability to take the shape of a human again. This is just clarification of how Exaltation works, by giving more in depth rules for Lunars.

                                                It was always explicit that Lunars often were NonBinary in their outlook and many shifted their genders to the point some couldn’t remember their original forms. This was an accepted and acknowledged thing before we got this Lunar book for 3rd ed. Nothing led me to believe it would be anything but open and accepting to this type of character.
