                                            Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post
                                            I'm thinking about some of the possible implications of this. Would it represent a limitation on what Lunar Charms can be, or does it just mean that Lunars get a dispensation that nobody else does. For instance, would there be Dexterity Charms to split attacks between multiple targets that could work with Martial Arts?

                                            ​Still, I imagine that if playtesting reveals it to be broken, the first solution is to use a Keyword to set aside the ones that are compatible.
                                            Hm, this also touches on another possible mild issue. Why use brawl? I imagine that this is yet another reason why the martial artist merit would prove a mild balancing feature, but honestly, brawl doesn't provide charms, but martial arts do.

                                            The worst case scenario I can see is that DBT weakens your mastery level and certain combat charms have upgrades when in DBT. Of course I'd be pushing for being able to use martial arts in the iconic lunar combat mode but at the same time...

                                            Hm. I have no idea how to avoid martial arts being too good but also worthwhile to have. The combo capability is just really potent, like crazy potent. Add in that if Lunars have Lunar XP, then they can double dip into combat charms and martial arts charms...

                                            And since martial arts charms are balanced around other exalted, we're more likely to see Lunar charms have to be designed around martial arts, which might end up having martial arts being a missing piece to the lunar charmset.

                                            That said, I already mentioned a possible solution: Deadly Beastman Transformation might have Lunar practitioners use charms at Terrestrial level instead but many charms have bonuses when in DBT. Now this doesn't remove the option of karate bug monsterman, but it does discourage it enough to give clear intended combat styles and modes.

                                            I don't know. I'm rambling. I will say I am mildly concerned that Lunar charmsets might feel incomplete because they have to be designed around working with martial arts. My issue isn't that martial arts is good, its that I don't want the only way to make a combat lunar end up being "take martial arts".

                                            So outside of the merit cost, why should a lunar use brawl?
