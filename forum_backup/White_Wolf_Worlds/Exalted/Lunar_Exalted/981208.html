    Do Lunars have any ancient Artifacts?

    10-03-2016, 10:27 PM
    When I read descriptions of Artifacts, those from the First Age are invariably either lost, or owned and made solely by the Sidereals, Realm, and Lookshy.

    I find this rather strange. The implication is that Lunars took no artifacts with them into exile, and remembered nothing about how to make them. The very few unique Luanar weapons that I know of were given away to a Raksha (Death at the Root) or abandoned (Islebreaker).

    This makes no sense to me at all. Lunars should absolutely have their own ancient artifacts and knowledge that is preserved nowhere else. They should of course have things they took with them and kept from the First Age. They were not universally murdered, nor were they stopped by American airport security on their way to the Wyld. They are the heirs of the First Age just as much as the Sidereals. There is a distinct imbalance in power, but the idea that the Lunars have retained nothing at all bothers me.


    Is there some book that I haven't read that details all the cool First Age artifacts the Lunars kept for themselves or still know how to make?

    She/Her. I am literal-minded and write literally. If I don't say something explicitly, please never assume I implied it. The only exception is if I try to make a joke.
    My point of view may be different from yours but is equally valid.

              Overall i don't worry overly much about it.               Yes, those wonders most certainly exist among the eldest, but the Lunars have lost just as many through the centuries, some (but not all recovered) and the degree of honesty about which of the chosen has which thought long lost artifacts can vary considerably, i would guess.

                          There are several things to keep in mind here:

                          1. The Lunars lost the Usurpation. They were forced to the edges of Creation, and the bulk of the treasures of the First Age were claimed by the DBs and Sidereals. So the Lunars had far, far fewer artifacts to begin with.

                          2. Lunars have less structure than the Sidereals and DBs. Therefore, while there are a few powerful artifacts held by Lunars, most people would see those as eg. "an artifact held by Raksi" rather than "an artifact held by the Lunars."

                          3. This lack of structure, combined with the pressure of the Wyld Hunt, also makes it far harder for artifacts to stay in the hands of Lunars as a whole. Immediately after the Usurpation, many Lunars would have had personal panoplies of powerful First Age artifacts; however, as they were hunted down and killed, these were taken by the DBs and Sidereals or lost, and didn't get passed to the next generation.

                          4. Often the things that did get saved and remained in Lunar hands ended up in the possession of Lunar elders. While the elders would sometimes reward these to younger Lunars (or use them as incentives to control them), they lacked the rigid structure of inheritance or military order the DBs had, or the more coherent fellowship of the Sidereals. A DB or Sidereal could reasonably expect to inherit artifacts from the previous generation if they existed; assuming a Lunar artifact remained in Lunar hands at all, a new Lunar would have to negotiate with the elder who likely had it on an individual basis. Individual Lunar elders might respect your claim as the successor to the Exaltation, but they wouldn't face the structural pressure Sidereals and DBs do.

                          5. It's very hard for even the most powerful and established Lunars to handle a direct, overwhelming assault by the Sidereals and the Realm (or the Shogunate, earlier.) That kind of assault is expensive and risky, so it doesn't happen lightly, but what this means is that the most powerful world-shaking artifacts held by the Lunars would have been taken (or destroyed by the Lunars themselves, who would realize they couldn't defend them.) This is especially true for structures, manses, and anything else that a Lunar can't easily run off with - many powerful artifacts that were once in the hands of the Lunars were likely destroyed by the Lunars themselves when they were forced to retreat in the face of an overwhelming Dragon-Blooded assault.

                          6. In terms of making artifacts... Lunars aren't great at it to begin with, relatively speaking. They're probably the worst Celestials (it might be a toss-up between them and Sidereals, but Sidereals are ability-based and at least have a dedicated Craft tree.) And if you account for the sheer number of DB crafters, they might even fall behind the DB host as a whole. And that's before accounting for the fact that after the Usurpation, Lunars have lacked the resources available to DBs and Sidereals and have often been on the run, making it hard to establish a dedicated workshop or to focus on long-term projects. The good news is that those crafting charms that they do have are probably pretty good at crafting in poor circumstances, but... it's not gonna make up the difference, it just means they're not utterly unable to make stuff. The artifacts they make now are probably mostly simple, utilitarian things, at least in comparison to what they were able to craft when they had the resources of the First Age available to them (let alone what their Solar mates once crafted.) Of course this is a matter of degrees, and their artifacts are still strange and wonderful, but nobody's crafting the Five Metal Shrike or a Thousand-Forged Dragon from a box of scraps while on the run.


                          There are more Lunars then Sidereals and they have more free time to Create.
                          I'm not sure I agree with the latter point. Sidereals have a day job, so to speak... but aside from the most powerful elders, Lunars often have to struggle to survive, and Sidereals (comparatively speaking) don't. A lot of Lunar time and energy is spent on hiding from, evading, or dealing with the Wyld Hunt. Even the really powerful ones generally have to either live far from civilization (which means a lot of their time and effort is spent on day-to-day things because they lack the broad infrastructure of civilization to draw on) or in secret (which means everything is harder for them because they have to hide.)

                          A Sidereal can take a sabbatical, relax in a manse in Yu-Shan, and generally have nothing to worry about while they craft for a while. They probably can't do it too often, but they can do it. A Lunar doesn't generally have that luxury - you can't ask the Wyld Hunt for a time-out (and, in fact, working on some big project is probably an easy way to draw their attention.)

                          Of course, this is relatively speaking again. Neither Lunars nor Sidereals have been able to craft too much over the past 1500 years - this is partially for setting reasons, since having piles of artifacts everywhere is bad for the game. But I'm dubious that Lunars actually have more time to craft than Sidereals do; a big part of the purpose of the Wyld Hunt, when it can't kill them outright, is specifically to ensure they don't have the time or resources for big projects like that by keeping them on the run.

                                  Unfortunately, the Lunar indolence problem is kinda well known and artifacts, as one of the biggest Solar THINGS, are probably going to come out of the books with ninety five percent "First Age Solar Workmanship" stamped on them. The intent of this is of course building up the Solar image in the books; grand builders of wondrous armaments and devices whose secrets are lost to the ages, only now to be rediscovered in the fallen age.

                                  In 2e I raged against this similarly. I made a Lunar craftswoman and banged my head against their terrible charms and made homebrew ones to craft at half the pace and equal power of a Solar with extra difficulty, since speed and minimums were the only things to affect in the 2e system.

                                  3e, I've turned my sights to something more Lunar in nature, in my eyes. Artifacts can be stolen but workings in the deep wilderness, too remote for the dragon blooded to hold and too difficult to remove, feel more in the Lunar style to me, and indeed do what I had in mind with my craftswoman better anyway.

                                  Unfortunately, the answer to the question is no; a grand total of two things in wonders of the first age were accredited to Lunar Exalted, and there was no book with much else aggrandizing them; it was an extremely low priority in 2e.

                                  Holden, in the what we know wiki, started previously that while artifact creating is not part of the planned 3e Lunar paradigm, "you will be potentially able to have your lunar Tony Stark putting together armor in a cave from a box of scraps", or something to that effect.

                                                  The moonsilver tattoos are moderately troublesome because in 2e at least they're kind of an air-breathing mermaid. "We wanted Lunars to have cool silver tattoos because we felt like cool silver tattoos were a cool unifying marker, so we invented a reason for them to all get those tattoos, they fucked up so bad in the wyld they broke their castes and needed tattoos to not fuck them up. What do the tattoos do that is cool? Mostly they just prevent you from degenerating into a shoggoth, either from your own powers or wyld exposure. If you pay for it you can have some artifacts built into your tattoos, but they're only the baseline artifacts of 2e and none of the cool magitech gizmos we're gonna drown the setting in later on."

                                                  Most people, when they think 'artifact', think 'cool device that would be awesome to use' rather than 'plot thing that protects me from a problem that was pretty clearly invented so I'd have the plot thing'.
