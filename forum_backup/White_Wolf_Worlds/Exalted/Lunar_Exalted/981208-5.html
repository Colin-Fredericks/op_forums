            The separate question is, if Lunar elders do own First Age artifacts (which a poster said they do in 2E), why they never make these available and never even use them themselves. I think that given the high casualty rate for very young Lunars, that a starting character is not going to have access to any more than a Solar does -- which is to say they should be able to raid tombs and ruins, including the tombs of Lunar elders, or try to kill DBs and Sidereals and Solars and take their stuff.

            But I do have some problem with the more experienced non-elders being blocked from using ancient artifacts. The Silver Pact has an overall agenda, and Pact elders aren't going to finance your random personal crusade.                                                     Originally posted by Erinys View Post
                                                    When I read descriptions of Artifacts, those from the First Age are invariably either lost, or owned and made solely by the Sidereals, Realm, and Lookshy.

                                                    I find this rather strange. The implication is that Lunars took no artifacts with them into exile, and remembered nothing about how to make them. The very few unique Luanar weapons that I know of were given away to a Raksha (Death at the Root) or abandoned (Islebreaker).

                                                    This makes no sense to me at all. Lunars should absolutely have their own ancient artifacts and knowledge that is preserved nowhere else. They should of course have things they took with them and kept from the First Age. They were not universally murdered, nor were they stopped by American airport security on their way to the Wyld. They are the heirs of the First Age just as much as the Sidereals. There is a distinct imbalance in power, but the idea that the Lunars have retained nothing at all bothers me.


                                                    Is there some book that I haven't read that details all the cool First Age artifacts the Lunars kept for themselves or still know how to make?
                                                    Of course the Lunars took Artifacts with them into exile. They certainly didn't drop their personal panoplies on the battlefield or hand it over to the Usurpers. For me, this is just a function of the game books having limited space, and none of the lead devs have the time/inclination to count up references to make sure every angle of proper representation is covered. Individual writers are paid little, and are on deadlines. They write what they are inspired to write.

                                                    Western culture is at a particularly sensitive time right now when it comes to things like diversity in media. That perspective has utility in the real world, but I don't feel it's helpful when analyzing the books. The books, even taken as a whole, are just a starting point, a small seed intended to birth nearly infinite stories. If something ought to exist, it's up to us as STs and players to write it. If the question is "Why doesn't this exist?" then the answer is "Because I haven't written it, yet."

                                                    Particularly now that we have Ex3, I just read the book, draw my conclusions, and move on. The book is just a starting point. It can't account for even 1% of the tales of the Exalted alive in RY 768. By the time the entire Ex3 line is published, we still won't know the names of 1% of the Exalted, much less their histories and what their ancestors/previous incarnations stashed away for another day.

                                                    In my Creation, Lunars have many secrets and treasures unknown to the Sidereals and the Realm. Unlike both, however, they generally don't have the infrastructure or organization to make more. Leviathan doesn't deploy his remaining First Age warships because he knows the Realm will make them a target, overwhelm them with sheer numbers, and he will lose. It's better strategy for him to prey on their shipping here, assist the Realm's enemies over there, and wait. He has time, after all.
