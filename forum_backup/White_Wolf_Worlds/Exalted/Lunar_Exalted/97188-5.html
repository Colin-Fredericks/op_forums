        Originally posted by Exthalion View Post
        The problem is that these intelligence+ability+Excellency rolls are going to have as their end result a number of successes. With their better dice caps Solars will general have more of those. Since in this context the result of the roll as measured by successes is going to be something like "Useful philosophical insight" the Solars will have better ones even if they are not as Intelligent in the raw sense because their skill and magic translates into results in the same way intelligence and magic translates into results.
        There are charms to get around that, though. For instance, a Lunar with Inevitable Genius Insight (available at Essence 2) increases her dice cap to (Attribute + Essence), and also adds one success to all rolls regarding research.

        So for example, if a Twilight and a No Moon each had identical stats (Essence 4, Intelligence 5 and Lore 3) and were making an (Intelligence + Lore) roll to do metaphysical research, and each had 3 dots of applicable specialties (Lore specialty for the Solar, Intelligence specialty for the Lunar), and the Lunar was increasing her dice cap with the above charm...

        With those stats, the Twilight's normal dice pool would be (Intelligence + Lore + Lore Specialty), 11 dice. On top of that, he could add (Intelligence + Lore) from his Lore excellency, for a total of 19.

        The Lunar's normal dice pool would be (Intelligence + Intelligence Specialty + Lore), also 11 dice. She could add (Intelligence + Essence) from her Intelligence excellency, for a total of 20, plus one success.

        True that the Lunar is using an additional Charm, but it's one that can last as long as needed. And the example assumes that the Lunar has no attribute advantage, and that the Solar's Lore specialty applies, when realistically, ability specialties tend to be less applicable and Lunars tend to have more dots in attributes.

            Originally posted by TSk View Post
            I think their dramatic motif is more like "the greatest fighter, the greatest socialite, the greatest scholar," strongest/smartest/most charismatic being measurements based on attributes, not abilities.

            I see this argument come up whenever someone suggests another Exalt is better than a Solar at something, because people feel it infringes on Solars' theme of unequalled greatness. But that's ignoring the mechanical basis of where Solar greatness comes from. Mechanically, a Twilight may be less intelligent than a No Moon, but she's still a better occultist and a better artificer. For the same reason that a Full Moon can be stronger, faster and more resilient than his Dawn mate, while still losing more of their sparring matches because the Dawn is the better fighter. Attributes (and attribute excellencies and attribute specialties) aren't the whole story. But they are pretty much the whole story insofar as innate characteristics like intelligence are concerned, and it is a fact in mechanical terms that when it comes to raw intelligence, Lunars have more of it.
            This is all so much hair splitting, honing on in technicalities and vocabulary minutia, and for what?

            I can sympathize with the desire to frame Lunars in motifs of "world's greatest X as approached from a different angle to Solars" if only because their own motifs have traditionally been vague and underdeveloped, but the fact remains that, while the Solars are still core (and considering the mythology space they emulate), it's the kind of thing that will only ever make Lunars look like slightly less impressive Solars, and it's so unnecessary when they've got their own distinct myth space waiting in the wings for them.

            It's not about preserving the image of Solars, it's about rejecting an idea for Lunars that lacks credibility on several grounds, including that it's only relevant in the abstract.

            A person says "Lunars can be more Intelligent than Solars" than struggles desperately to find things in the system and setting that are relevant as functions of pure intellectual exercise. What's the point?
