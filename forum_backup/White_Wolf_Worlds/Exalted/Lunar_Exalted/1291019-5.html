Imagine for a moment that, say, 1/10 of them are sorcerers of enough might to reach the third circle, in this alternate world where lunars get solar circle sorcery. Now, the lunars are all part of the Silver Pact, which is an organisation united around the destruction of the Realm. Actually around the destruction of the Shogunate, but they got beat to the punch there. Let's ignore the Scarlet Empress establishing an empire over the course of 100 years with none of them killing her or stopping this. Let's also say that only 1/4 of these sorcerers want to fight the Realm, the other 3/4 are... I mean, they're solar circle sorcerers, they're erecting wonders only seen since the First Age, the entire setting is so radically altered by this change to mechanics as to be unrecognisable. But let's just ignore this too. These 10 remaining lunars who are both ready and willing get together and say "We're going to fight the Realm". All 10 of them conjure up a 3rd circle demon from the pits of Malfeas. How does the Realm still exist after this as anything but a smouldering ruin?

                                To put it another way, there is a very good reason why the strongest things in the setting are locked behind a group of ~10 people who have spent 1.5 millenia being spawn-camped out of terror over what they'll do if left alone.
