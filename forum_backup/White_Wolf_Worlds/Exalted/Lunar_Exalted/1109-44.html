                That there were 300 original Solar Exaltations in 2e is indeed definite. That there were exactly 50 Infernal Exaltations in 2e is also definite. But the 2e numbers of Abyssals was presented as the Deathlords grabbing around half of the Solars, and then giving 50 to the Yozis, leaving about 150 Solars and 100 Abyssals. I'm sure there are places in 2e where the numbers are said to be exact. I'm inclined to discount those in favor of the ones where they're approximate. At least in 2e, the seizing of the Exaltations from the Jade Prison has never been presented as a precision affair.

                As for EX3, I'm not discounting the possibility that it's 300 Solars today out of however many originals. But yeah, "300 original Solars, roughly 150 now, roughly 100 Abyssals, exactly/roughly 50 Infernals" still strikes me as most likely. We'll see!
