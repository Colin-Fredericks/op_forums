                    Originally posted by Sunder the Gold View Post
                    Each had some distinct advantages compared to the others, yes.

                    But you still had Chosen of Battles as well as Dawn Castes. Sidereals don't JUST work the Loom, they'll also step into arenas where Solar Exalted are king. And there are few things that Sidereals can achieve with Astrology that Solars cannot eventually replicate through Solar Sorcery.

                    The very fact of each Host having multiple Castes or Aspects makes it hard to pidgeonhole them into particular roles where one host does one thing that another host cannot do.
                    One of the big things I love about exalted is the sheer breadth of character concepts you can embody. More than that, I love that splat is not the same thing as a character class. You can make pretty much any splat cover a massive range of character concepts.

                    However, in 2nd Edition I always found lunars to be more defined by shapeshifting than other exalted were defined by their special powers. Sidereals are able to do amazing things with astrology and Sid martial arts, but you don't hear Sidereals described as "the loom exalted" nearly as much as lunars were defined by being the shapeshifting exalted. However, that's all baggage from the previous edition. we don't need that. We have new descriptions of lunars.

                    Just as Solars are described as "masters of *****" (MA, Sorcery, Evocations, and creation), the lunars are "Walkers in Ten Thousand Forms," "Terrors in the Night," "Gods of the Untamed," "Armored in Silver," and... "Broken Bonds..."

                    Okay, what do these mean? What can they mean? If we accept that these blurbs are representative of the general thrust of Lunar concepts (which seems fair) we can likely make some guesses as to the direction Lunars will be taking.

                    Walkers in Ten Thousand Forms

                    Because Shapeshifting... like obviously. Still a big part of lunar concept to no one's surprise. The exact mechanics of this remain to be seen, but I remain optimistic that it'll be more useful than in 2nd Edition.


                    Terrors in the Night

                    From the blurb:
                    it looks like Lunars are gonna be spies, spooks, guerrillas, etc. I like the terrifying miracles from beyond the edges of the world bit. I'm interested to see if the lunars will simply be immune to the wyld as in 2E or if they'll actually be able to use the wyld as no other exalt can. I want to be clear: I am 100% fine with the concept of lunars being the wyld exalted so long as they're not the corrupted by the wyld exalted.

                    what I'd like this to mean:
                    Lunars are bloody hard to pin down and predict. I kinda like imagining the realm dynasts scaring their kids to bed with warnings that the lunars will get you if you don't. Especially since this is totally a thing that could happen. The lunars could be everywhere. It could easily be a lunar in the form of the parent sending the kid to bed so he's out of the way :-) I'd also like to see the lunars double down on the scare tactics thing. Make them really good at demorallizing an enemy. The monster in a horror movie is so much more frightening when it remains unseen. the reality is never as terrifying as the things your brain conjures up.

                    Gods of the Untamed

                    From the blurb:
                    The lunars are kinda the thousand stream river by default? Barbarian tribes worship the lunars? The name is so much cooler than the blurb i think. Ya, the communities outside realm control are likely going to be following the lunars. They're celestial exalted and you're living outside the power/protection of the realm. Still plenty of baddies ready to take out a tribe/ city/ civilization if they aren't powerful enough to resist. Lunars are gonna play into that regardless. Still, it's hard to say precisely what this blurb means for the lunars

                    What I'd like this to mean:
                    Lunars are able to utilize things as they are. No need to convince a tribe to give up their lifestyle to follow a lunar's way of doing things. Lunar simply points them and gives them a push. No need to reshape a landscape to grow massive crops to feed a starving city. Lunar can come in and improve the city's ability to fend for itself? Maybe improve the fertility of the area? I dunno. Even I'm not sold on everything I've said so far, but one thing I would love to see is rather than use wyld cauldron tech to shape the wyld into whatever you want, I'd love to see the lunar be able to use the wyld as it is to gain massive power out of it. Not for free obviously and certainly with just as much interference from the Raksha as the solars get. If the lunars live in and around the wyld all the time, maybe it's because that's where they're the strongest exalt? I don't want the lunars to become the wyld exalts exclusively, but why can't that be a character option? Same as Solars can be a master of Sorcery, or a master of evocations or a master of anything, why can't 'wyld exalt' be a valid character concept for a lunar? Not all of em, but a faction? Call them wyld seekers or wyld riders or something.

                    Armored in Silver

                    Moonsilver tattoos are back. They're cool, they're visually distinct, the lunars are the only ones who can have em. Nice.

                    Just please, don't make them mandatory for a playable lunar. By all means give them a hell of a benefit, but make it more than a false choice whether to have them or not. Hell, in my original post I mentioned I'd like to see un-tattood lunars able to change their caste over time (but not forced to change with the moon cycle). I'd like to see the silver pact as a political faction rather than every lunar who doesn't want to go insane and get permanently mangled by the wyld.

                    Broken Bonds

                    Okay, the solar lunar bond is still a defining part of their relationship. Or at least it was before the usurpation. From the direction of things it looks like the bond is gonna be optional so that means there's design space for benefits/costs on both sides of the bond. Perhaps in the first age everybody had a bond because it was accepted that the benefits outweigh the downside for that age. Maybe right now, in this age, there is still some benefit to bonding a solar but the downsides would make most lunars pause.

                    Perhaps the nascent bond is always there but requires something like a shape sorcery action to bring it to life. IE, there is a bond potential but mechanically no bond exists until brought into being by the actions of both solar and lunar. Above all, I cannot stress enough how much this bond should not be mandatory, nor able to be put in to place without explicit permission from both lunar and solar.

                    So... thoughts anyone?

                            Ohhh I can see that this thread is going to be testing my current attempt at a laid back attitude...

                            Okay, something relatively neutral...

                            The book says "rage", but in the context of everything around it I see that as basically meaning "Lunars have a constant itch in their soul where the Solars and the Realm used to be, and it makes them antsy". Most Lunars have responded to this by working towards tearing down what remains of the Realm and the people who usurped it; it's not even about revenge or caring about a civilization they never experienced for most of them, it's basically having memories and protective instincts for a thing that doesn't exist anymore and wanting to be done with it. A young Lunar fights the Realm so that they can be free of the impulse that tells them they're supposed to care about something called the Realm constantly knawing at the back of their mind while they're just trying to enjoy this new freedom to break any chains people might put around them and explore the myriad shapes.

                            Some other Lunars have this same maddening impulse, but they use it to more directly power their personal initiatives; they occasionally seek aid from other Lunars in this, as a tit-for-tat kind of thing.

                            The Lunars are set up as a Celestial Exalted who can be apart from the expected roles of the Sidereals to a greater extent and the Solars to a barely existing one*; they are that same scope of power in contrast to the others. Different Exalted are different; most Lunars would rather be able to do as they please with nobody to stop them.

                            The return of the Solars complicates this; it seems blindingly evident to me that this complication is generally going to resolve in the direction of Lunars attempting to destroy their bonded Solars because they'd very much like to leave a past that means nothing to them behind please.

                            I read that write-up in the Exalted chapter, and it so thoroughly beguiled me; it reminded me of this thing I read once, I can't quite remember the details, it was something like some person who is part of a society in which everybody gets bonded to a special spirit animal which they have a certain mystical emotional connection to, and she's a bit of a rebel, but at some point she encounters her animal and she feels the bond forming and it's so right and it makes so much feel clear and safe in her soul, and she reaches out to the innocent thing that trusts her entirely and she breaks its neck.

                            Incidentally, I got an impression from the antagonists section that totems may not even be a thing at all.

                            * the idea that Exalted in general are hardwired to social roles is pretty terrible, but never mind.
