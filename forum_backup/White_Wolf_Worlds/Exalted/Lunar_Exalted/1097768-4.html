                            Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz View Post
                            I wonder if Lunars being able to turn into a horde of clones to fight (ie a battle group), would detract from their (very important and thematic) ability to turn into a swarm of tiny-to-small(-to-perhaps-medium) animals to fight (ie a battle group). Which, certainly, is going to be quite mechanically distinct from Faster than Self.
                            I see them as two completely different tricks, thematically and mechanically.

                            A Lunar who clones himself occupies more space and multiplies his effective power.

                            A Lunar who turns into a swarm instead divides himself; he doesn't multiply his power, but rather trades one form of power for a completely different form of power. He loses the ability to make feats of strength (except to chew through things), he cannot use any of his weapons, and he cannot parry attacks, but he becomes better able to evade attacks and perform distraction gambits for his allies.

                            This is why I think the power to become a swarm grows from the power to become a small creature, whereas I think the power to multiple oneself is not inherently tied to becoming small or large.

                            But even if they are different, they are not incompatible; a Lunar can combine the power to subdivide with the power to multiply. Instead of becoming a swarm only as big as a man in total, he can use both tricks together to instead become a swarm as large as an army.
