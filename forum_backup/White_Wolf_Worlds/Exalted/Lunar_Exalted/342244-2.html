                                    Prototype can certainly be a fine source of inspiration for a Lunar character. Hunting enemies, taking their forms or just parts of their form and/or their memories are definately things Lunars can do, and which will be in theme. If you have the knacks for hunting demons/elementals/fae you can even go for the aesthetics. Even becoming more monstrous and inhuman is in theme, looking at Raksi, Ma-Ha-Sushi and other elder Lunars.

                                    When re-imagining any character from another property to Exalted there will of course always be certain concessions in theme and/or aestethics. However, when compared to other sources of inspiration for Lunars quoted in this or other threads (such as spiderman), Heller or Mercer will be much easier to create as a Lunar, at least in methods used and even looks.

                                    In the theme department you will have to build in some reluctance, or loathing towards his or her Lunar exaltation to reflect that in the original property the source of his power is a creepy body-horror virus and not something which the character considers a blessing. Take a Spirit Shape that the character dislikes for some reason, or better yet, some permanent limit and a chimera knack or two. Perhaps the character has a personal hatred for Luna because the event he survived that earned his exaltation (or the subsequent Wyld Hunt) resulted in the death of his loved ones. Season with some burning desire for revenge and the willingness to shed rivers of blood, and voilà, a Lunar Prototype expy in Creation.

                                    I suspect the fact that Mercer and Heller's powers are not a blessing, but a curse, and at best a tool to exact revenge is why people are reluctant to describe Prototype as general Lunar inspiration (as opposed to inspriration for a particular Lunar), but lean more towards Abyssals. This is after all a defining aspect of Prototype's narrative. However with the caveats that in Exalted being a Lunar is a gift from above, and is usually more animal oriented in the shapeshifting department, you can certainly use Prototype as a way to describe Lunars to new players.

                                                    Let me preface this by warning you that I haven't played Prototype. Based on my limited understanding of it, I would say it's OK as an inspiration for Lunars. Not good, and not terrible - just OK.

                                                    My reasoning for this is that, as you say, once you get over the visual effects of the Blacklight characters' powers, the basic mechanics of how they work are a reasonably good match to Lunar shapeshifting shticks. However, this is a very narrow lens to look at Lunars through. Lunars are supposed to have a lot of things going for them besides shapeshifting - a unique social context (Anathema to some, folk heroes to others), a divine connection (Luna personally visits every Lunar at least once, allegedly), a range of charms that have little or nothing to do with Shapeshifting, plus the usual set of baggage that comes just from being a character in Creation.

                                                    It's about as valid an inspiration as Sun Wukong (Journey to the West - who was never human and is crazy-OP), Bigby Wolf (Fables - also not really a human), or Elfman Strauss (Fairy Tail - another Lunar-y guy with none of the Lunar baggage.)

                                                    That said, I wouldn't recommend Prototype to most people as a Lunar example, specifically because they're going to think of the flesh-tentacles first and the mechanics second, and not get any of the other themes. If nothing else, this thread has proved that much.
