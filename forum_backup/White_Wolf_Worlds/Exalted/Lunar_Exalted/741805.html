    Lunar 2e to 3e Conversion

    10-30-2015, 11:15 AM
    I was looking at this topic and thought I should probably share my notes.

    This conversion assumes the user has access to 2e Lunars, 3e Core and the Scroll of Errata. It is written in Errata style, where only the changes are highlighted. Whenever a Charm has a significant wordcount, just assume it has the words "Replace the Charm text with the following". It also includes a few Charms of my own creation to attempt to match the sheer size of the Solar Charm trees, even just the lower Essence effects. Also, apologies if anybody else's homebrew made their way here through my headcannon - I would cite you if I remembered where it came from. If you see something sufficiently similar, I'd be happy to credit you.

    The content is in the next few posts so that I can edit them as I keep working. I know I have a ton of keywords to put in, for example, and I haven't gone through the Scroll of Errata or even Strength/Dexterity/Stamina Charms.

    Again, this is some homebrew for a 'play right now' purpose not a 'dev's please do this' plea. It is a crafting project, not an instill action.

      Changing Moon

      Charisma:
      Animal and Training Charms:

      Dog-Tongue Method - unchanged

      Sharing the Gifts of Luna - Essence 1, Charisma 3 - The target's Essence rating is unchanged. Otherwise, same Charm

      Wolf Pack Training Technique - Essence 1, Charisma 4 - pre-req: Sharing the Gifts of Luna - Instead of the Mandate of Heaven part, this allows the Lunar to have Essence non-Charm successes on issuing an order to that group. The Lunar adds an additional success if that group contains a commander sharing the gifts of Luna from a commitment the Lunar controls. This may be used on Animals with the effects of Pack Instinct Affirmation if you do not currently have any familiars.

      Lesson of the Winter Wolf - Essence 2, Charisma 4, pre-req: Wolf Pack Training Technique - Instead of Conviction applications of Wolf Pack Training Technique it requires Size applications. Instead of Valor checks, this grants the unit perfect morale.

      Holy Charms:

      Outworld-Forsaking Stance - Ess 1, Charisma 3 - permanent - You do not need to use willpower to reject a change of an Intimacy initiated by Creatures of Darkness. The Lunar may still be inspired or reach a decision point as a result of a creature of darkness' meddling. The Lunar gains the benefit of a spent willpower when attacking or defending against Creatures of Darkness in physical combat that threatens one of the Lunar's Intimacies.

      Foe-Turning Rebuke - Ess 1, Charisma 4, Simple, Instant, Psyche, pre-req Outworld - Roll Charisma+(Social Ability) against one or several creature of darkness' Resolve with penalties for multiple targets as normal. Success means a rebuked creature of darkness must spend either 3 Willpower or her next [Lunar's Essence] actions must be either Disengage or Withdraw actions. In social combat, success typically means that creature of darkness leaves the scene immediately.

      Scathing Lunar Condemnation - Ess 2, Charisma 4, Psyche - Charisma+(Social Ability) may be used for both single and multiple targets.

      Fascination Charms:

      All Ess 2+, ask me then if Charisma isn't your Supernal.

      Interaction Charms

      Rapid Speech Technique - Ess 1, Charisma 2, 5m, Simple, Psyche - The friendly Lunar approaches her target for a Read Intentions action and asks so many questions so fast the target has a hard time keeping her composure. The Lunar may make two separate Read Intentions actions with this Charm with Charisma+(Social Ability), the first one against Guile as normal but the second one against Guile-1 as if an onslaught penalty. If either of these Read Intentions actions fail then like a normal read intentions action failure read intentions actions (including those produced by this Charm) can't be retried for the rest of the scene.

      Secret Speech Method/Falling Leaf Distraction - Ess 2, Charisma 3 or Manip 3, Mute - Charisma+(Social Ability). Similar to the Solar Linguistics Charm Letter within a Letter Technique on 3e Core page 323.

      Instinct Memory Insertion - Ess 1, Charisma 2, Mute - Supplements an inspire action to remember the message. This auto-succeeds and grants Charisma bonus dice to recall permanently.

      Perfect Fear Scent - Ess 1, Charisma 3, Psyche - With this Charm even death-cultists can be made to fear the Lunar's punishment. Supplements either a read intentions, threaten or instill action regarding discovering the target's fears, compel action, strengthen or create a fear of the Lunar respectively. When using this charm a normally unpersuasive threat can be made to seem persuasive (Give me all your silver or I'll tickle you!). Alternatively this Charm always meets the condition for retrying these actions with normal criteria. In either case the Charm cannot be used against the same target again in the story.

      Seductive Lust Infliction (previously Terrifying Lust Infliction) - Ess 1, Charisma 3, Psyche - With this Charm the Lunar can sell snow to Icewalkers or learn the hidden loves of Dynasts. Supplements either a read intentions, bribe or instill action regarding discovering the target's desires/hopes/dreams, compel action, strengthen or create a desire for the Lunar's friendship/respect/love respectively. When using this charm a normally unpersuasive reward can be made to seem persuasive (I'll give you an apple to let me use your house for a month). Alternatively, this Charm always meets the condition for retrying these actions with normal criteria. In either case the Charm cannot be used against the same target again in the story.

      Hard-Nosed Denial Style - Ess 1, Charisma 3, Mute, 5m - The Lunar is true to herself and will simply not be moved if she is willing to tap into her divine reserves. Whenever the Lunar would spend a Willpower to resist an influence roll she may instead spend 5m. Using this Charm in a decision point can allow you both pay motes instead of willpower and also re-use an intimacy that has already been used to justify a resisting the influence of a decision point. This Charm cannot be used more than once in the same scene.

      Military Unit Charms:

      Maintaining the Pack - Ess 1, Charisma 3 - When a battle group under the Lunar's command would normally roll for rout due to lost Magnitude it instead keeps its last point of Magnitude and the Lunar's next rally for numbers action on that battlegroup gains +2 non-Charm successes. This Charm can be used once per scene per battlegroup. Maintaining the Pack refreshes for a specific battlegroup if that battlegroup has both already benefited from this Charm this scene and later loses a point of Size.

      Social Group Charms:

      Herd Reinforcement Stance - Ess 1, Charisma 3 - If new Intimacy conflicts with an existing Defining Intimacy it automatically fails with no roll. It can also be used to instill a positive intimacy of duty towards a legitimate leader.

      Forsaking the Blood Posture - Ess 1, Charisma 3, no pre-req - Same changes as Herd Reinforcement Stance. This Charm is sometimes called Culling the Pride Leader when used to create a negative intimacy against a specific leader instead of the society at large.

      Territory Charms:

      Boundary Marking Meditation - Ess 1, Charisma 3 - In addition to the normal effects, the Lunar is considered the leader of any native people (which can interact with Herd Reinforcement Stance and Forsaking the Blood Posture).

      Ranging Wolf Marking - Ess 3

      Sidebar: Owning Land

      Boundary Marking Meditation only supernaturally enhances a Lunar's ability and timescale to claim territory and punish those who displease her within it. Any Lunar with a Major or higher Tie to a territory that is not challenged for its ownership can be written as the owner by a few years of patternspiders' weaving and little god's reporting. This process can be sped up to a few months by claiming the highest rated Demense or Manse in the area. In the Wyld ownership can be achieved instantly by owning the Arcane Redoubt of a the highest rated Freehold or a Waypoint or set of Waypoints. Keep in mind that while a Lunar may be mighty in the territory they own they can always be dragged bodily from it by a higher power, though few such powers stoop to tossing Lunars about in the dirt.

      Man-Tongue Method - Ess 1, Charisma 3, Perception 3 pre-req: Dog-Tongue Method, 5m, Scene - The Lunar can speak any language spoken by the humanoid forms in her shapes library at Charisma 3+. At Perception 3+ she can understand any language spoken by the humanoid forms in her shapes library.

      Only the Strong Atemi - Ess 1, Charisma 2, Reflexive, 5m Indefinite - During and after a bloody battle the surviving veterans can be infused with Luna's Essence to be more than they could ever hope to be before. When a battle group you control loses a Magnitude in battle it gains 1 Level of Drill. At Essence 2+ this Charm allows Elite units to gain perfect morale. At Essence 3+ Elite, perfect morale units can gain +1 Might, and +2 might at Essence 4+. This Charm may be stacked up to the Lunar's Essence on the same unit, So a Size 4 Average Drill unit with normal morale could be changed to a Size 1 Elite Drill perfect morale +1 Might battlegroup after three applications of this Charm by an Essence 3 Lunar (thus committing 15 motes) over the course of one battle or several battles. Restoring the lost Size through later recruitment or dropping the mote commitment loses all effects of this Charm as the veterans are either diluted with green conscripts or lose the spark the Lunar has invested in them.

      Belief Charms:

      False Burrow Pursuit - Essence 1, Manipulation 2, Psyche, Mute - This Charm could cause a soldier on guard duty to check out the important disturbance or an investigator to chase either a dead end or the perfect lead as the Lunar desires.

      Mirror Sight Dismay - Essence 1, Manipulation 3, Psyche - With this Charm even devout worshipers can lose their faith and lead a secular life. Supplements any Instill Principle action so long as the Lunar believes the principle to be false. When using this charm a normally unpersuasive argument or emotional appeal can be made to seem persuasive (A patrician might be convinced that money has no power in this world by watching a relatively poor Dynast slight a rich patrician he knows despite all the other evidence he's seen of money's power - his Major Intimacy for Money (Covetous) could degraded to Minor). Alternatively this Charm always meets the condition for retrying an instill action with normal criteria. In either case the Charm cannot be used against the same target again in the story. Defense and Dissembling Charms:

      Mask of White Jade - Ess 1, Manipulation 2, 5m, 1wp, Reflexive, Round, Mute - The Lunar automatically wins any roll against their Guile for the rest of the round.

      Cat-Face Presentation - Ess 1, Manipulation 3, 6m or 6m 1wp, Supplemental, Withering-Only, Counterattack, Psyche - The physical or social attack supplemented by this Charm is unexpected as if benefiting from re-established surprise. Normal cost if surprise, add 1wp if ambush. This Charm can be used once per scene.

      Commanded to Fly - Ess 1, Manipulation 4 - When resisting social influence, the Lunar may counterattack herself to change that influence to unacceptable by targeting her own Resolve-1 +any normal modifiers such as Intimacies. This use of Intimacies to make the counterattack does not affect their availability for use via spending willpower and vice versa.

      Feint Charms:

      Butterfly Eyes Defense - Ess 1, Manipulation 2. 3m and 3i, Psyche, Mute - When you suffer Initiative Crash you may force your opponent to spend 1wp or they are denied both the Initiative Break bonus and the knowledge of your Initiative Crash via this Charm.

      Butterfly Eyes Fist - Ess 1, Manipulation 4 - pre-req: Butterfly Eyes Defense, Psyche - Double the initiative granted to your ally from a successful distract gambit you perform.

      Interaction Charms:

      Chattering Magpie Inflection - Ess 1, Manipulation 2 - 5m, Simple, Psyche - The Lunar bludgeons her target with so many commands so fast the target has a hard time denying her. The Lunar may make two separate Instill actions with this Charm with Manipulation+(Social Ability), the first one against Resolve as normal but the second one against Resolve-1 as if an onslaught penalty. If either of these Instill actions fail then like a normal Instill action failure further Instill actions (including those produced by this Charm) can't be retried for the rest of the scene without further, greater argument (though these two combined only count as one attempt for that purpose).

      Perfection of the Mockingbird - Ess 1, Manipulation 3, Mute - No other changes

      Military Unit Charms:

      School Becomes Shark Formation - Ess 1, Manipulation 3 - Instead of double Magnitude, increase Size by the Lunar's Essence. This can increase Size beyond 5.

      Creating the Wolf Attitude - Ess 1, Manip 3, Psyche - For social actions instilling fear or benefiting from an Intimacy of fear the Lunar does not take the penalty for multiple targets when supplemented by this Charm.

      Feeding the Bear Progression - Ess 1, Manip 3, Psyche - For social actions instilling craving/desire or benefiting from an Intimacy of craving/desire the Lunar does not take the penalty for multiple targets when supplemented by this Charm.

      Face of the Moon Concealment/School in the Reeds Technique/Rats in the Basement Style - Ess 1, Charisma 2 or Manip 2 or Wits 2, 6m, Mute - A Lunar may pass along the tricks of concealment they know with supernatural effectiveness, even the hopelessly obvious can be lead to a position where they can ambush their foes with the Lunar's help. This Charm enhances an order regarding stealth to a battle group, but may be given to any willing allies even if they do not normally comprise a battle group. The Lunar rolls [Charisma or Manipulation or Wits]+[Stealth or Survival or Socialize], with the Attribute she used to purchase this Charm used and whichever Ability is most appropriate for the type of concealment she is instructing the battle group to take, instead of War. Successes are added to the average dice pool of the battle group, as per normal orders. If used to plan an ambush stratagem the Lunar may use Charisma+(Survival or Stealth or Socialize) instead of the commander's Intelligence+War if the commander allows the Lunar to plan the stratagem.

      Dissonant Birdsong Order - Ess 2, Manip 3, Psyche, Simple, 5m, pre-req: School in the Reeds Technique (or its mirrors) - The Lunar hears or sees the command a rival commander is giving their battlegroup and disrupts it - some soldiers are psychically prevented from perceiving the order while others see a different order entirely. The Lunar takes this action on the same tick as a commander she wishes to interfere with and rolls Manipulation+War where the successes the Lunar earns are subtracted from those the rightful commander's order making it weaker. If no successes remain (the Lunar's successes are the same as her targets) then the order is ignored but the battlegroup may act as normal. If the Lunar gains more successes than the rightful commander, the battlegroup skips their next action as the soldiers attempt to sort out the miscommunication. The Lunar typically has to delay their action to activate this Charm.

      Anglerfish Distracts the School Technique - Ess 3, Manip 5, Psyche, Simple, 7m, 3i, pre-req: Dissonant Birdsong Order - The Lunar can build a mirage in the minds of her opponent's troops so convincing they perceive it as an order from their own commanders. With this Charm the Lunar may send a non-attack command rolling Manipulation+War to any battlegroup on the field regardless of the Lunar's ownership of that group. If the rightful commander gives a command order between this Charm and the battlegroup's action, the rightful commander's command acts like Dissonant Birdsong Order for the purposes of cancelling the Anglerfish Distracts the School Technique order but need not be issued on the same tick.

      Snake Bites Tail Strategy - Ess 6, Manip 6, Permanent, pre-req: Anglerfish Distracts the School Technique - Once per scene per battlegroup the Lunar may give an attack order via Anglerfish Distracts the School Technique. This resets if the battlegroup loses a point of size.

      Betrayal of Self or Shapeshifter's Kin - Essence 1, Manipulation 3, Mute - A Lunar can see when another wants to make a change so badly it is an Intimacy. Using this Charm a Lunar perfectly tells if any sentient being in their sensory range has such an Intimacy and what it is. If a farmer has an intimacy to become a travelling singer, she learns of this. If a Solar desires to be the best swordsman in Creation, she learns of this.

      Trickster's Way - Ess 3, Manip or Dexterity or Intelligence 4, 1m, Reflexive, Instant - Lunars are Luna's children, as fickle as dice and unpredictable as the wind. Lunars who have learned this Charm have internalized Luna's ways and can mislead thier opponents minds, words and arms simultaneously. Raise your Evasion, Parry, Resolve and Guile by 1 per 1m spent on this Charm. The dice limit for this Charm is equal to the highest limit of the four derived stats and interacts with stunted secondary attributes normally with the exception that Manipulation is always valid for the stunt even if it was already used to calculate the value (use twice Manipulation for Charm limits in that case).

When using Recognizable Scars Removal Technique this also affects crippling injuries converted health levels.

      Military Charms:

      Diving Hawk Inspiration - Ess 1, App 2, 3m - Like the hawk diving before a thunderstorm, the Lunar can catalyze her troops to action. When using this Charm to supplement an action the Lunar can give an order to a battlegroup reflexively to do the same action on its next activation.

      Tadpole or Claw Strider Simulacrum
      Cost: 15m, 1wp, Xahlvl, X/2 sxp, X/3 gxp, X/4 wxp round down
      Mins: Ess 3, Appearance 2
      Type: Simple
      Keywords: Salient
      Duration: Indefinite
      Prerequisites: One of the Herd
      The Lunar makes an exact copy of herself and her mundane equipment, which briefly stands next to her true self, then her true self retreats to elsewhere as if yanked by the scruff. The Lunar sees through and commands the will-less copy as easily as any appendage. The copy has access to the same Charms and Knacks, with the same Attributes, Abilities, Virtues, Intimacies, Willpower, and equipment but the copy's mote pool is restricted to 5 personal motes and 10 peripheral motes, available immediately. The copy can only attune to artifacts this mote pool can sustain, though artifacts carried by the copy but attuned to the Lunar stay attuned to the Lunar and may be treated as mundane equivalents without the unattuned penalty. The copy can respire Essence and Willpower normally and heals at the same rate the Lunar does. The copy has as many health levels as the Lunar expended when activating the Charm, at those wound penalty levels (this means the copy may have indefinite wound penalties if no -0 health levels were used). Additionally, only craftsmen can infuse the copy with enough Essence to contain more than a single health level. Typically only one health level is used. That many are possible is a closely held secret of the Silver Pact. Tamuz once cut himself intentionally under the watch of a Wyld Hunt so that they would overcommit to hunting his copy down. He successfully faked his own death by being torn in half while falling down a waterfall while providing cover for a retrieval pack in Chariscuro. When the Charm ends or the copy loses its last health level the Lunar's Essence Copy and the mundane equipment she was copied wearing dissipate in an obvious display of Lunar Essence leaving no corpse. The Lunar's true self reappears safely where the Charm was activated. While in Elsewhere the Lunar respires no Essence, but does gain Willpower and heals any health levels not committed to the Essence Copy Charm as if sleeping even if the Lunar cannot naturally sleep.

          Spirit Maiming Essence Attack - Dual - After rolling the dice of damage on a withering attack, add Essence post-soak damage. On a Decisive attack, spirits destroyed by this Charm are destroyed permanently.

          God-Body Dissection - Decisive-Only, Ess 5, Perception 5 - The gods of the Terrestrial Bureaucracy and even Yu-Shan often overstep their place in the Lunar's eyes but this does not always warrant destruction, merely a lesson. Against the Fair Folk or demons sometimes their greatest powers need swept away before they fall like assaulting the trusses of a bridge. This Charm can target any being who is defaultly dematerial such as Spirits, most demons and the Fair Folk. This Charm enables the Lunar to eliminate powers from these beings as a gambit of difficulty equal to the Essence minimum to learn the power. See Lords 74.

          Instinctive Essence Prediction - Ess 1

          Sense Charms:

          Sense-Borrowing Method - Ess 1

          Sinister Strike Defense - Ess 4, Perception 5. Permanent, Cost –, Pre-requisite: Eye of the Cat - When struck by an ambush attack this Charm automatically activates for 0 motes. The damage of the attack cannot fill the Lunar's last health level. The Lunar cannot die without seeing the blow that kills her, but she might still be surprised as this Charm has no effect on surprise attacks. No less of a death can take the Lunar from Creation. The Lunar who has achieved such enhanced vigilance cannot sleep, be incapacitated or lose awareness in any way. Treat the Lunar's incapacitated health levels as -6 health levels should she still be alive with a filled Incapacitated health level. Other effects that incapacitate are instead applied as a -6 wound penalty. Willpower generation is difficult for these Lunars, as the main method of sleeping is no longer available. NPC Lunars who know this Charm are suggested to have half their maximum willpower available unless they also have a powerful Cult.
          At Essence 5, the damage from such attacks is deferred till the end of the scene, but when applied still cannot fill the Lunar's last health level.
          At Essence 6, the damage from such attacks is ignored. What the Lunar does not perceive can no longer hurt them.

          Military Charms:

          Bird Falls from Flock Targeting - Ess 1, Perception 4, 3m - When targeting the leader of a battlegroup or battlegroups, the Lunar's action supplemented by this Charm ignores any hindrance that battlegroup mounts such as Defend Other, making terrain rough, posting a guard or simply being in the way. She smashes through pike lines or walks among the group mistaken as an ally or dances between every watchful eye unseen as suits her temperament and skills but the result is the same - for this action the commander cannot hide behind her forces, the Lunar is always granted audience or faced in a challenge.

          Wolf Eye Advantage - Ess 1, Perception 3, Permanent - When on the hunt every second not chasing or killing your prey is a second wasted. When the Lunar takes an Aim action, it counts as two Aim actions.

          Eagle Eye Advantage - Ess 2, Perception 4, Permanent, pre-req: Wolf Eye Advantage - The Lunar can take another moment to hone in on her prey like the Eagle. When already at a +3 bonus from Aim, the Lunar can take another Aim action to increase that to +6. With Wolf-Eye advantage, short aiming will typically meet this criteria in 1 turn but Medium or longer aiming still typically takes two consecutive applications of Wolf Eye Advantage.

          Wolf Ear Advantage - Ess 1, Perception 3, 3m - The hare's footsteps in the forest bring a wolf to attention despite any distractions. This Charm negates all penalties to a read intentions action. The Lunar may re-attempt read intentions once per scene per target after failure if the re-attempt is supplemented by this Charm.

          Motive Dissecting Eye - Ess 1, Perception 5, Permanent, pre-req Wolf Ear Advantage - Once per scene the Lunar gets a free full Perception Excellency for a read intentions action.

          Leaf-Falling Scrutiny - Ess 1, Perception 3 - roll against the target's Guile for targets concealing skills.

          Fellow Hound Insight - Ess 1, Perception 4 - The Lunar may explicitly exclude known beings from the search, such as packmates. The detection always succeeds for yes/no on type. The roll only determines if the highest non-excluded being's Essence is of that type, failure reveals that the strongest uknown Essence is of a different type (and might spur the Lunar to search again using a different Supernatural type). The types can be as broad or as narrow as the Lunar desires such as 'undead' or 'demons of the hierarchy of She Who Lives in Her Name' or 'any god that reports to a Calendar God'.

          Wyld Charms:

          Resisting the Lure of Madness - Ess 1, Perception 2, no pre-req - Instead of Magnitude 1, the Lunar can protect a battlegroup of Size (Lunar's Essence) even if those beings are not normally considered a battlegroup.

          (also some crafting Charms, let me know if you want to get into Crafting)

          Moonsilver Flicker Sense - Ess 1, Perception 2 Supplemental, Instant, 3m - Any reroll of a failed Perception roll may be supplemented by this Charm and gains Double 9s. This generally doesn't work by the time a hidden foe launches an unexpected attack but would work to try to find hidden characters prior to that point. Storytellers are encouraged to have players roll perception frequently for little secrets - not every missed detail is important.

          All-Senses Expansion - Permanent - For non-battletime scenes, increase the duration of the Perception Excellency to scene. When using the Perception Excellency in battle increase the Duration to tick instead of Instant. At Essence 2+ increase that to round.

          Blood-Kin Echo - Ess 1, Perception 2, 8m, Simple, Scene - When the Lunar enters an area one of her ties has been, she instantly knows it with this Charm. To a Lunar surrounded by loved ones the very land feels welcoming even when they are not there. To the Lunar oppressed the land itself is a choking reminder of their oppressor. It is as if her Tie's scent is on the air even should she lack a nose to sniff with.
