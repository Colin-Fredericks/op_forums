                                                Let's see if we can put this into a little bit better framework for purposes of analysis. There are several 'groupings' of powerful magic aside from standard charm use:

                                                1) Personal transformation (DBT, Shintai, etc).
                                                2) Sorcery/Thaumaturgy (different scales of things, but generally similar theme)
                                                3) Evocations
                                                4) Martial Arts

                                                Now lets see if we can define these a little bit more.

                                                1) Personal transformation expounds on the theme of putting your personal growth and expressing your own Essence through yourself. You are the master of yourself, and push yourself so far beyond your own normal limits that you have nothing left for things that come outside of yourself. These things are part and parcel of your Exaltation, and no matter how hard you try, you will never be able to reach into someone else's style of mastery (Infernals never get DBT, Terrestrials never get Shintai).

                                                2) Sorcery/Thaumaturgy is using the rules and shortcuts built into Creation, manipulated through Essence, to create effects. This is explicitly.a cross-Exaltation type of power. If you cast Death of Obsidian Butterflies, it is the same as someone else casting Death of Obsidian Butterflies. So long as you have the raw power to force your will across these procedures, you can do so.

                                                3) Evocations are the mastery of tool use. Everyone can use tools, but some Exaltations resonate more strongly with tools than others. This also means pushing your essence outside of yourself and attuning yourself, changing yourself, to match your tools so that both you and your tool are the same. The tool becomes an extension of you.

                                                4) Martial Arts represent an alternative form of Personal Transformation. Instead of using your essence and Exaltation to master your physical form, you use your physical form to master your working of Essence. You then use your mastery of essence to refine your physical form, and repeat the process. The more pure you are spiritually and physically, and the more in sync you are with yourself, the further you can go in Martial Arts.

                                                If everyone accepts these definitions (if not perfect, at least close enough for horseshoes and hand-grenades), we can then ask what should be compatible and what should not?

                                                1 vs 2
                                                1 vs 3
                                                1 vs 4
                                                2 vs 3
                                                2 vs 4
                                                3 vs 4

                                                These six compose all possible combinations. We can go through these one by one and see what we get.

                                                1 vs 2 (DBT vs Sorcery). My argument would be yes, these work together. The rules of Sorcery are part and parcel with the rules of Creation, and you are part of that.

                                                1 vs 3 (DBT vs Evocations) I would say no. To push yourself this far, you must push your body to be in perfect attunement with your Essence. You cannot do this while also modifying your natural essence towards a tool that has its own essence structure.

                                                1 vs 4 (DBT vs Martial Arts). These two are very close in theme. With MA, you use your body to master your essence. DBT, the opposite. In this case, I would suggest that while not incompatible, they are restricted. You cannot push your Essence into alien configuration through your natural body while pushing your body into alien configurations by using your natural essence. I would argue that someone in DBT can use those forms within their normal reach, but not above it. For example, a Celestial that can learn SMA cannot use them while in DBT. A terrestrial cannot manifest Celestial Martial Arts while using some variation of this power, like transforming into an elemental or something.

                                                2 vs 3 (Sorcery vs Evocations). These two do not have any themes that would interfere with each other. In fact, certain evocations may even assist in Sorcery. Rather than being incompatible, I would put forward the idea that in some circumstances, these are synergistic, allowing a practitioner of both to reach a little further when using both than he could using only one or the other.

                                                2 vs 4 (Sorcery vs Martial Arts). I don't know. I can see it several ways. I have no suggestions that are any better than others. My first thought is that, like DBT vs MA, you probably shouldn't be accessing any levels beyond your normal ability while doing so. A Terrestrial who has somehow initiated into Celestial Martial Arts and Celestial Circle Sorcery cannot use both at the same time, but can probably use Terrestrial Circle and Terrestrial Martial Arts at the same time.

                                                3 vs 4 (Evocations vs Martial Arts). These do appear to have contradictory natures, but only if the Evocations are not in line with the particular MA. If you are using an MA that doesn't work with weapons, it doesn't work weapon Evocations. Same with MA and Armor. If you are using an MA that allows a daiklaive, then you can use its Evocations. If your evocation comes from a belt, and it isn't in line with your MA, then nope, can't use it. If it is, then sure.
