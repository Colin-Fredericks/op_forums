                                  Originally posted by Holden View Post

                                  I would love to know how you tried to approach that.
                                  ​I'm not sure I have the notes anymore, but I'll try and remember any interesting details. Mostly, it was an attempt at "this Ability can be clearly used for a Lunar-y thing; can we make a new Charm out of it?"

                                  The reason we ended up in the Attribute situation in the first place was that all of the players had initially said they wanted to play Solars, but one of them later flipped through the art book and decided Lunars were too cool for school. So I attempted to build a stealth-and-gnawing Lunar template based on the rules I'd already done for Solars.

                                  When I say "failure," I might be being too harsh. We actually had very few mechanical problems, it just always felt like the Solar Charms (which ran closer to the canonical ones) flowed more easily.
