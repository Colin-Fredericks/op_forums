    3e Lunar homebrew

    08-12-2016, 07:09 PM
    Over the past few days I've been gathering up Lunar charms I made for my NPCs and writing up some more, and I've got enough that I felt like sharing my work. This isn't necessarily going to end up as a full homebrew project that will allow for a full variety of PC Lunars (it might, and I have written up provisional chargen and advancement rules, but I'm not going to make any overly ambitious promises), but I figure that at the very least it might be useful for STs looking to add some charms to their Lunar NPCs. My general design philosophy for the charms (which are primarily combat-related so far) has been to
    - Evoke predatory imagery - overwhelming strength, preying on the weak, being terrifying and untouchable.
    - Nothing shapeshifting-related, for two reasons. First, I want to start with writing a Lunar charmset that can stand on its own and feel Lunary even before adding in the shapeshifting, and second because I haven't figured out how I want to work in shapeshifting yet.
    - Make sure it's possible for every physical attribute to stand on its own for a combatant. Having an at least average dexterity score and an excellency is always going to be mandatory for a serious fighter, but it should be possible for a Lunar to just take a whole bunch of strength or stamina charms and both be useful and feel distinct, in the same way that a Solar with melee feels different to a Solar with brawl.

    With that out of the way, here are the charms I've written so far. A lot of them lack flavour text, since this is a work-in-progress, but the mechanics should be fairly sound. Any critique is welcome.

    Strength Charms
    Burrowing Devil Strike
    Cost: 1m; Mins: Strength 2, Essence 1
    Type: Supplemental
    Keywords: Withering-only
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: None
    No defence can hold for long against the tremendous force of the Lunar’s blows. When the Lunar makes a withering attack, she may use this Charm to raise her attack’s Overwhelming value by 1 or increase it to equal her Strength, whichever would be higher.

    Ferocious Biting Tooth
    Cost: 1m per die; Mins: Strength 2, Essence 1
    Type: Supplemental
    Keywords: Dual
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Burrowing Devil Strike
    When the Lunar makes a withering or decisive attack, she may add extra dice of damage at a rate of one mote per die. She may add up to (Strength) post-soak damage dice to a withering attack, or (Essence) dice to a decisive attack.

    Lion-Stalks-Gazelle Methodology
    Cost: -; Mins: Strength 2, Essence 1
    Type: Permanent
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Permanent
    Prerequisite Charms: Ferocious Biting Tooth
    When the Lunar makes a withering or decisive attack against an enemy who is in Initiative Crash or suffering from a -2 or worse wound penalty, the number of damage dice she may add with Ferocious Biting Tooth increases by half her Strength, rounded up.

    Undeniable Might
    Cost: - (1m per die); Mins: Strength 3, Essence 1
    Type: Permanent
    Keywords: Decisive-only
    Duration: Permanent
    Prerequisite Charms: None
    When making a decisive attack, the Lunar may substitute her Strength for her Dexterity on her attack roll. If she does, she may reroll up to (her Essence) 1s on her damage roll, at a cost of one mote per die.

    Claws of the Silver Moon
    Cost: 5m; Mins: Strength 1, Essence 1
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: None
    Duration: One scene
    Prerequisite Charms: None
    The Lunar shapes her anima into fierce silver claws. While this Charm is active, her unarmed attack is considered a medium weapon, deals lethal damage and gains the piercing tag. Additionally, her claws can damage immaterial spirits, though she gains no special ability to see the immaterial. If she wields a weapon, she may extend her anima across it to confer to it the ability to strike dematerialised spirits.

    Thundering Bull Charge
    Cost: 2m, 2i; Mins: Strength 2, Essence 1
    Type: Supplemental
    Keywords: Uniform
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: None
    The charging Lunar crashes into her foe with tremendous force, sending him flying. When she moves into close range of an enemy and attacks him in the same turn, she may activate this Charm. If the supplemented attack hits, its target is knocked backwards one range band and prone.

    Blade-Rending Claw
    Cost: 3m; Mins: Strength 3, Essence 1
    Type: Supplemental
    Keywords: Decisive-only
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Undeniable Might
    When the Lunar makes a decisive attack while on 12+ Initiative, she may strike with such force that any weapon used to block her blow shatters. If her foe successfully parries her attack, his weapon is destroyed outright, or disarmed instead if it is an artifact weapon. This counts as a successful decisive attack, and resets her Initiative to base.

    Legion-Scattering Might
    Cost: 3m; Mins: Strength 2, Essence 1
    Type: Supplemental
    Keywords: Withering-only
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: None
    When the Lunar makes a withering attack against a battle group, she ignores (Strength) points of soak. If her attack provokes a rout check, that check is made at +1 difficulty.

    Prey-Hobbling Bite
    Cost: 3m; Mins: Strength 2, Essence 1
    Type: Supplemental
    Keywords: Decisive-only
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: None
    A vicious blow from the Lunar cripples her foe or knocks him senseless, making him easy prey. As long as her decisive attack deals at least 3 levels of damage, her foe suffers a -2 penalty to all dice pools for the remainder of the scene, and an additional -2 penalty on all movement-related actions.

    Limb-Maiming Flourish
    Cost: 3m, 1wp; Mins: Strength 3, Essence 2
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: Decisive-only
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Prey-Hobbling Bite
    After making a successful decisive attack, the Lunar may exchange up to (Strength) levels of final damage she inflicted upon her foe for a crippling injury of equivalent severity.

    Lightning Stroke Attack
    Cost: 3m, 1wp; Mins: Strength 4, Essence 3
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: Decisive-only
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Blade-Rending Claw
    When attacked, the Lunar may respond with a reflexive decisive clash attack, which must benefit from Undeniable Might.


    Dexterity Charms
    Hunter-Becomes-Hunted Attitude
    Cost: 4m; Mins: Dexterity 3, Essence 1
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: None
    When the Lunar successfully spots one or more enemies attempting to hide from her, she may make a reflexive (Dexterity + Stealth) roll to enter concealment. Each character she detected in this way suffers a penalty on his (Perception + Awareness) rolls to detect her equal to the threshold successes she scored against him.

    Rabid Beast Attitude
    Cost: 2m; Mins: Dexterity 1, Essence 1
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: Uniform
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: None
    Being outnumbered only strengthens the Lunar’s warrior spirit. She may invoke this Charm to negate any onslaught penalties she suffers against a single attack. If she successfully defends, she gains Initiative equal to the onslaught penalty negated by this Charm.

    Savage Tiger Strike
    Cost: 3m; Mins: Dexterity 3, Essence 1
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: Counterattack, Decisive-only
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: None
    When an enemy successfully hits the Lunar, she may respond with a decisive counterattack that occurs before her foe rolls damage.

    Foot-Trapping Counter
    Cost: 2i per success; Mins: Dexterity 2, Essence 1
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: None
    The slightest stumble when fleeing from a Lunar can be fatal. When an enemy attempts to disengage from the Lunar, she may activate this Charm to subtract one success for his roll for each 1 in his result.

    Tiger Fades From Sight
    Cost: 1wp; Mins: Dexterity 3, Essence 2
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Hunter-Becomes-Hunted Attitude
    When the Lunar successfully enters stealth using this Charm’s prerequisite, she may deceive her foes by masking her disappearance. She might enter a building, pass behind a tree or simply lose herself in a crowd, tricking any observers into believing she has only briefly left their sight. Anybody trying to follow her will not notice for at least two rounds, or one minute outside of combat, that he has lost track of her. Using this Charm allows the Lunar to launch an ambush attack against anybody she has fooled, despite them being aware of her presence, as long as she meets the other requirements for an ambush.

    Thousand Claw Infliction
    Cost: 5m, 1wp; Mins: Dexterity 3, Essence 2
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: Decisive-only
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: None
    Striking with terrible speed, the Lunar makes up to (Essence or 3, whichever is greater) decisive attacks, dividing her Initiative as evenly as possible between them. Each successful attack deals bonus damage to its target equal to his current wound penalty.


    Stamina Charms
    Ox-Body Technique
    Cost: -; Mins: Stamina 1, Essence 1
    Type: Permanent
    Keywords: Stackable
    Duration: Permanent
    Prerequisite Charms: None
    A Lunar may purchase this Charm to permanently gain extra health levels. The number and type of health levels she gains is based on her Stamina.
    At Stamina 1 and 2: One -2 and two -4 health levels.
    3 and 4: Two -2 and two -4 health levels.
    5: One -1, two -2, and one -4 health level.
    The Lunar may purchase Ox-Body Technique (Stamina) times. If she increases her Stamina after purchasing Ox-Body Technique, her health levels automatically change to reflect the new rating.

    Armadillo-Shell Method
    Cost: 5m; Mins: Stamina 3, Essence 1
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Until next turn
    Prerequisite Charms: Impenetrable Beast Armour
    The Lunar ignores all penalties to Defence until her next turn.

    Den Mother Attitude
    Cost: 2m or 2m, 2i; Mins: Stamina 1, Essence 1
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: None
    When an ally within close range is attacked, the Lunar may reflexively take a defend other action to protect him for two motes. She may also activate this Charm for two motes, two Initiative when an ally at short range is attacked to dash into close range and defend him.

    Bruise-Relief Technique
    Cost: 5m, 1wp; Mins: Stamina 2, Essence 1
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: None
    Healing comes naturally to the Lunar Exalted. By spending an hour focusing on forcing her body to recover, the Lunar can heal (Essence or 2, whichever is greater) bashing or lethal health levels. She may walk, hold a conversation and perform light activity during this time, but any significant exertion will interrupt the process, forcing her to start over.

    Cornered Mongoose Attitude
    Cost: 2m; Mins: Stamina 2, Essence 1
    Type: Supplemental
    Keywords: Withering-only
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: None
    While in Initiative Crash, the Lunar may add one non-Charm success to the attack and damage rolls of a withering attack.

    Bloodied Beast Resurgent
    Cost: 1wp; Mins: Stamina 2, Essence 1
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Cornered Mongoose Attitude
    When the Lunar makes a withering attack that causes her to recover from crash, she may activate this Charm to immediately gain the benefits of an Initiative Break.

    Impenetrable Hide Demeanour
    Cost: -; Mins: Stamina 2, Essence 1
    Type: Permanent
    Keywords: Withering-only
    Duration: Permanent
    Prerequisite Charms: None
    Whenever the Lunar is struck by a withering attack that deals (Stamina) or less damage after her soak is applied, she gains one point of Initiative.

    Apex Predator Ascendant
    Cost: -; Mins: Stamina 3, Essence 2
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: Decisive-only
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Bloodied Beast Resurgent
    Whenever the Lunar receives an Initiative Shift and uses the extra action it grants her to make a decisive attack, that attack does not reset her to base Initiative if successful.

    Unstoppable God-Beast Might
    Cost: 3m; Mins: Stamina 3, Essence 2
    Type: Perilous, Reflexive
    Keywords: Decisive-only
    Duration: Reflexive
    Prerequisite Charms: Impenetrable Hide Demeanour
    Whenever an attacker deals (Lunar’s Essence or 3, whichever is higher) or less final levels of damage to her with a decisive attack, his Initiative is reset to 0 instead of its usual base value following that attack, immediately sending him into crash. The Lunar gains the usual Initiative Break bonus for crashing him.

    Halting the Scarlet Flow
    Cost: 6m, 1wp, 2a; Mins: Stamina 4, Essence 2
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: Perilous
    Duration: One scene
    Prerequisite Charms: Bruise-Relief Technique
    When the Lunar has taken damage to her -2 or -4 health levels, she may draw her anima into herself to close her wounds. At the end of the round in which she activates this Charm, and each subsequent round, she heals two levels of lethal or bashing damage. This Charm cannot heal -0 or -1 health levels, and ends if the Lunar is crashed.


    Appearance Charms
    Radiant Joy Stance (Puppy Eyes Prana)
    Cost: 4m; Mins: Appearance 2, Essence 1
    Type: Supplemental
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: None
    A Lunar with this Charm exudes an infectious exuberance. When she makes an inspire action to evoke feelings of happiness or similar positive emotions, any character who wishes to spend Willpower to reject her influence must cite a Major or Defining Intimacy in a Decision Point to do so.


    Perception Charms
    Inescapable Hunter Attitude
    Cost: 5m; Mins: Perception 2, Essence 1
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: None
    As long as a Lunar with this Charm knows a person’s scent, either from having met him in the past or having access to his clothes or other similar belongings, she may track him across any distance and ignores any penalties to do so. This allows her to contest magic that would normally make tracking impossible.

              Originally posted by Lanaya View Post
              The strength excellency can't be used on damage rolls
              Why not? The book does not forbid this.

              The Lunar Exalted tap into the raw potential of their Attributes
              to bolster their heroic feats, and can add up to
              (Attribute) dice to any action. However, with a stunt, they
              may add a second Attribute to the limit as long as the
              action is described in a way that fits the combination. For
              example, a Full Moon rolling (Dexterity + Brawl) to smash
              a Dragon-Blooded shikari would normally have a dice
              limit of (Dexterity), but could increase this to (Dexterity
              + Strength) if he described the attack in a way that evoked
              his raw brawn and mighty thews.
