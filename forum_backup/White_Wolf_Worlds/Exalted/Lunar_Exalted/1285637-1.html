                    Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post
                    Consistency with the previews?
                    Yeah, the previews have generally been good. If the book is exactly what I expect based on the previews, I'll likely consider it 9/10 (and 10/10 is very subjective of course).


                    Really, the most important thing I want to see is something I don't know that I want until I see it.
                    This is one of the reasons I like Minton's writing of locations; he doesn't just rehash old stuff but fixed, the way forumites demand.
                    He gives you entirely new cool societies and locations.
                    That's why I liked Compass: the North more than any other compass. Scavenger Lands was mostly stuff from 1st ed, but in Compass: North, while the stuff from Bastions of the North was still there, there was a whole chapter of interesting new locations.

                    The other thing is with charms. I want better, more interesting No Moon charms because I felt that my No Moon basically could mostly just take Sorcery, Tracking and Spirit-related stuff.
                    But I don't really actually have a lot of ideas for cool, interesting No Moon charms.
                    What I'm hoping is to see a whole bunch of Intelligence and Wits charms I'd never have thought of.

                    Some of the No Moon charms shown so far are ones I'd expected; stuff like Echo-Drinking Awareness, History-Divining Scent, Moon Follows Sun Assurance.
                    But others I'd never have thought of... yet in retrospect, they seem obvious. Of course there should be a charm to introduce a fact about a spirit. Of course there should be a charm to whip out an item and declare retrospectively you bought/stole/crafted* it earlier. But I didn't think of it until I saw it.

                    *I like how "Lunar" it is too. The Solar equivalent is just for crafting, but Lunars are Wits Exalts, not Craft Exalts.

                    STing Bronze Age Exalted
