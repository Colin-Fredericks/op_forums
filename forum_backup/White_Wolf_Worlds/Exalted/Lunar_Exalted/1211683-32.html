                    Type: Supplemental
                    Keywords: Perilous, Delicious

                    The Lunar's quarry has momentarily evaded his doom, and her hunger knows no limit. She pauses for a lunch, becoming a whirlwind of comestible destruction. This Charm augments an attempt to persist in the Lunar's endeavors: Add one success to any attempt to perform a feat of endurance, and reroll any "1" appearing on this attempt, up to a number equal to Resources spent on the Lunar's lunch break. Additionally, this Charm has the side effect of instilling an emotion of terror (watching a hungry Lunar eat) or disgust (condiments splattering in all directions) on anyone watching who does not already have a positive Intimacy toward the Lunar herself. Use of this Charm doubles a Stunt's Willpower reward (if it is eligible for one) if the player gets the Storyteller something from the kitchen while they're up. If the player actually brought food for the group this session, use of this Charm triples the Willpower reward instead.

                        08-07-2018, 09:57 AM
                        Originally posted by TheCountAlucard View Post
                        I'm also kinda interested in the prospect of the Lunar intentionally fostering an Intimacy in their intended prey so that the Lunar can then access the related memories when they eventually steal the human's shape.
                        I don't know... if they didn't have an Intimacy before, how many memories do they have of the subject, and are they even worth stealing? It seems kind of circuitous to me.

                        Hmmm...

                        Okay, looking at your follow up example, I guess there's something in the idea of a sufficiently long-lived person gaining and dropping Intimacies, but... it feels like a bit of an edge case. For one thing, it raises the question of whether or not the desired memories might be associated with another Intimacy that is still fresh (if the character has a Principle connected to the Wyld Hunt in any way, that would cover those interactions). Also the matter of whether or not its necessary to acquire memory of something incidental in a character's life to pass off as them. Or there's something assumed to be in the memories themselves that the Lunar wants to learn, and needs to cultivate an avenue to access them (which is still not covered by a more fresh Intimacy).

                        {checks again}

                        Wait, no, the forgotten Dynast herself is the target, and it's about finding a way to access her while also wanting to ensure that you get actual information... and I guess that anybody that is part of her life at the moment is not a convenient target? Mind, it seems to me that when the connection is that lapsed, it's not very difficult to use the same kind of trickery that con artists regularly use to convince strangers that they attended school together, where any gaps can be attributed to having forgotten in the intervening time. Not unless Dynast Alice (or Bob, technically) has a reputation for having the infallible memory that some Dragon Blooded powers can afford.

                        I suppose if one wants to play a Lunar that is being especially meticulous, but it does feel like a very specific circumstance, and a bit of a solution looking for a problem.

                        {reflects}

                        Hmm, that might be a bit of pedantic nitpickery on my own part, there. If I'm thinking more objectively, I can see that part of the fun of magic can be coming up with curious or novel applications and reverse engineering them into scenarios that call for it.

                        I don't ultimately have a point here, it's just my train of thought.

                        I have approximate knowledge of many things.
                        Write up as I play Xenoblade Chronicles.
