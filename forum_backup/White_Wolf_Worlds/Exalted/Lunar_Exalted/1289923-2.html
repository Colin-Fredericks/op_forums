        In general, it seems like strength and stamina aren't great investments for the animal-form-combatant, since you can't use them for attack/damage. They still affect excellency (thought stunting can give you a decent cap anyways), and probably have good charms.

        Looking through corebook animals for things with good all-around stats and not legendary size, hoping for forms useable by someone without much investment in combat:

        Bear (12 die attack at 11 damage, parry 3, soak 10), claw strider (12 die attack at 11 damage, evasion 4, soak 7), eagle (10 die attack at 11 damage, evasion 4, soak 2, flight), Gorilla (10 die attack at 13, parry 4, soak 6), Great Cat (13 die attack at 12 damage, evasion and parry 4, soak 6), or Quoll-Lion (11 die attack at 10 damage, evasion 4, soak 5).

        The biggest weakness here might be defense more than attack - Most of these have 10-13 die attack pulls and some way to get more from merits, but only 3-4 defense with few merit boosts.
        Turning into an animal might be the good option for a primarily ranged fighter - you want a good dex for ranged attacks, and a good dodge for defending at range, so if you get stuck in close range you can rely on your animal form for attacks while using your own Defense.

                            Bear (12 die attack at 11 damage, parry 3, soak 10), claw strider (12 die attack at 11 damage, evasion 4, soak 7), eagle (10 die attack at 11 damage, evasion 4, soak 2, flight), Gorilla (10 die attack at 13, parry 4, soak 6), Great Cat (13 die attack at 12 damage, evasion and parry 4, soak 6), or Quoll-Lion (11 die attack at 10 damage, evasion 4, soak 5).

                            I thought I’d reply to this too after some noodling. Stamina probably still helps as an animal, as you can use your own native soak (which might be higher than your form, see: Housecat), but Strength is completely useless, since you have to use animal damage, independent of Str.

                            So based on my clearer understanding, with 5 Dex, 5 Brawl and both the latent talents, if you want to fight as anything other than a cat and you want better combat stats:

                            Your Animal Form Accuracy has to be 14+ thanks to Ambush Hunter and Underfoot Menace (Latent)

                            Your Animal Form Damage has to be 12+ thanks to Stalking Shadow Hunter (Latent) and Underfoot Menace (Latent) (a couple of non kaiju animals beat this, but not the accuracy)

                            For safety, you’d better have an animal form merit that adds +2 to your EV and eats 2+ successes from any attempts to notice you.

                            Y’see, the bummer about having 10+ but not 14+ Accuracy as an animal shape (lots of “combat shapes” fall in this range) is that you can’t apply the free +1 Accuracy bonus from having a higher pool in Human form than animal form (you can as a Housecat).

                            Thus I submit the following contentious statement:

                            If you have 5 Dex and are willing to take both the latent Cat talents, the cat probably beats out all the non-Kaiju forms for the combination of accuracy/damage and Mote Efficiency, with Eagle coming a close second for first round ambush shenanigans (then you can shift into a cat afterwards).

                            If you are a no moon shaman and just want to maul something in a pinch, just pick one of the Combat shapes mentioned previously.

                            All hail Death Kitty!

                            Disclaimer: There is a high possibility that I am wrong or have missed something as this seems whimsically crazy even to me.

                            Edit: If your animal is non Kaiju and has a... 15-16+ Damage Attack, you’ve probably beaten the Cat in the attack/damage department (your Raw Damage is 4+ higher than a cat’s, which means your Accuracy matters less)

                            Nothing I’ve seen so far beats the cat on defense (not considering soak) yet...
