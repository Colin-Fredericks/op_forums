    I am really new to Exalted (as of 3E). I got the impression from the core book that the Lunars from the way I see it would have a fair number of artifacts of the 1st age. Less the ones taken from ones killed by the Wyld Hunt. I imagined that Lunars would have artifacts that were more person or ideal to them. Gifted to them by Solars when they were around. Warstriders? Prolly not a lot. A ring that lets you know when those hunting you are in 10 miles of you, or a scarf that lets you blink in short teleports for a short while to quickly use terrain to your advantage. I think the Artifacts they would have are geared more to them or would be highly personal as gifts given to them.

    I believe their artifacts are things that help them personally immensely but may not have their value recognized by others who capture or come across them. If they were chosen servants or partners of Solars I can't believe that they would not have received numerous gifts from their betrothed or master. As for crafting things on their own I don't see why they couldn't. I just don't get the vibe that making things is something that is a big deal or desire for them.

                    For fun, let's picture Creation as a reality show.

                    Some of the people on the show have been hanging out in the First Age for like... -3000 years-. During this time, they've lived in the lap of luxury essentially, afforded every convenience that you can imagine. They do lots of military stuff, as a general rule, so they fight lotsa battles and wars too. During this time, they enjoy training in the most sophisticated and advanced technology of that already impressive era. Over thousands of years they master and rely upon these tools to dispatch serious foes, many of which test them to the edge of ability... but good thing, they have all the wonderful toys the First Age has to offer!

                    Now in the new season of our show, post-Usurpation, everything has changed! It's off to the Wyld for the entire cast!

                    Imagine the hijinks that will ensue as our beloved tech-addicted cultural children of the modern first age try to wear animal skins for the first time! Can they make fire without essence lasers? How will they learn to play tribal drums without automaton servants?

                    (The real answer, of course, is that they wouldn't. The Usurpation essentially involves taking a group of people who are the 1% of Creation... the rich and powerful... who have been natives of a highly sophisticated technology-based society... and having them just give up all that to go live in the woods because they're 'really angry/scared'. Never mind that both of those things would seem to motivate them to rely on artifacts MORE, not less...)

                    (And let's not forget they abandon any Dragonblooded spouse/children/bestfriends... of which they likely had FAR MORE personal contact with than the Sidereal Exalted did, who were reclusive and not popular generals/warlords generally. Also because they're 'really angry/scared'.)

                    It really is just a nonsensical story.

                                        Originally posted by Elfive View Post
                                        I think in previous editions this was one of the things the old Solar Bond was supposed to explain.
                                        Nah, this all predates Solar Bond.

                                        Look, the real reason is frustratingly simple - not a lot of thought has been given to the Lunar storyline, and it just doesn't make any sense. Lunar Exalted were added late in the development process (they were originally intended to be a purely antagonist splat) and weaving them into the history of the game has always been an afterthought. The narrative focuses primarily on Solar/Sidereal/Dragonblooded interaction, and most of those interactions are fairly well thought out and flow from one event into the other.

                                        But Lunars? The Lunars have always just been kinda... mashed in. They're lazily described and poorly thought through. They were conceived from the 'top down', as opposed to 'bottom up'... and what I mean by that is that it was determined Lunar Exalted should be a playable splat, what that playable splat should more or less be, and then an explanation was thrown together post-hoc about how all that came to be.

                                        One of the most important things the game line could to to codify the perception of Lunars is examine these issues. They need a compelling, tragic history instead of just... random plot jumps, loosely held together with a shrug.
