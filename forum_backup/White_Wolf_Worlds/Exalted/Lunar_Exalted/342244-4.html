                                    I doubt that the limb-attachment is going to be too permanent, particularly if Claymore is playing into it. Characters tend to aim for cutting off limbs and then dicing them up because they know that limb reattachment is easy... if it isn't interrupted by dicing up the severed member with a rapid speed sword attack.

                                    Prototype 2 sounds slightly more structured for Lunars in potential, but it's important to note that "survive something that should have killed you" isn't solely the Lunar bag. Armattan survived being stabbed by raiders and had to wander the desert; he became a Zenith. What's more, some Lunars, according to 1e, ended up with their Exaltation because they or their actions were pleasing to Luna.

                                    RE: Roach as a totem, that's not actually possible unless it's a relatively large roach, which the species we're familiar with loitering around trash and invading homes typically aren't. The vaunted survival capacities of the roach are also based more on their numbers; most people don't usually speak of an individual roach as being unstoppably hardy. They're talking about how there are usually more hiding in unseen locations. In any case, there's another obstacle to totemic beasts for Heller, and that's the absolute rejection of a totem concept by the narrative and the underlying assumptions of its genre. Looking at the Let's Play called to mind Aberrant more than it did Exalted. It's quite firmly in the realm of sci-fi superheroics, or superantiheroics, alongside its rival game, Infamous. It's actually super-easy to break tanks in Aberrant, too; if you get even one dot in Mega-Strength, you no longer have to worry about the armor-add on a tank, which means that it only has six points of soak. A punch from someone with Strength 5 (trivial to achieve, since one nova point can get you three Attribute dots) does 7 dice, a kick 9. Add on the Claws power, and you can easily get a few extra dice of damage.

                                    Contrast with Exalted, where punching a tank up can be much, much more time-consuming, even if it suffers from the maladies of being an inanimate object in terms of how it takes damage. Perhaps it might not strike you as a genre barometer, but it was something that struck me as "hey, this guy totally fits in a game where the lowest level of enhanced strength pretty much pulps people in one hit". In which case Exalted isn't going to be super-accomodating to the concept no matter what splat you use.

                                            I don't really know or care about Heller. Everything I've heard about his character arc and the story of the game have been bad, so I've refrained from learning even as much about him as I did Alex. (I've never played either game, though I've seen plenty of gameplay.)

                                            Maybe Heller is closer to proper Lunar inspiration than Mercer. But that doesn't matter as much as Rater thinks.

                                            Absolutely accurate conversions from one medium or another into Exalted is not the goal. Superman is a prime Solar inspiration, but you're never going to see Solar Charms that shoot heat-rays from your eyes or let you breath hurricanes or maintain constant, effortless levels of superhuman strength, speed and durability.

                                            There's also the facts that Superman is supposed to be an alien, and his mote-pool is refilled by exposure to sunlight with no actual upper limit to the number of motes he can hold, and he's got an allergy to green space rocks.

                                            Basically, there are limits to which Superman is a valid influence over Solar Charms. Superman's powers are innate biological features; Solar Charms are learned and practiced skills. That's huge on a foundational level, even if there are superficial, surface-level similarities.

                                            Likewise, there are some superficial similarities between what Heller can do and what a Lunar could do, but the fundamental justification and genre for them is completely different. Heller is sci-fi, infected by a transformative virus that gives him the ability to improve his flesh and provoke it into cancerous growth and rapid calcification. He absorbs concepts like "mass" and "DNA". His body ultimately conserves mass, even though it cheats by keeping most of it Elsewhere so that he's merely as heavy as a car instead of a main battle tank (or ten).

                                            Lunars have never cared about mass. They make new flesh directly from their Essence without needing to eat the whole of what they kill, or to maintain a pocket dimension of spare mass. They can assume forms smaller than human, reducing their weight to whatever is natural to those smaller forms... and this has somehow never made them any less tough than they were at human size.

                                            Lunars have never cared about DNA. It has always been a successful Hunt which lets them steal the spiritual Essence of their prey, and from there it is their shapeshifting power which lets them express that spirit in a physical form. Blood may mark the end of a successful hunt, but the Hunt is a vast, multifaceted thing, and so blood is not always the end.


                                            Can you make a Lunar based on Heller's story and inclinations? Yes. But you cannot make the Lunar Exalted based on Heller's powers. There's enough overlap for tangential inspiration, but it's ultimately a distraction and one is better served looking elsewhere, at the things that get you inspiration not only for those tangential elements, but also far, far more..

                                            Can you make a Liminal based on Heller's story? Doesn't sound like it. But you CAN (probably) make the Liminal Exalted based on Heller's powers, which belong to the same root source.

                                                Originally posted by Rater202
                                                You see, these are just those Aesthetic differences that don't matter anywhere near as much as you people seem to think they do.
                                                Actually, they do matter. Because Exalted is an exception-based system rather than an effects-based system. You don't build powers up according to what they do, you start with aesthetics to determine how to differentiate them from other powers and make them look unique. Just as having Charms that do things in a manner that looks Solar is not good for Lunars, so, too, is having Charms that do things in a way that isn't focused on being animalistic shapeshifters, and Prototype tap-dances out of that territory with wild abandon, so it's not really eligible for inspiration-mining except in very peripheral ways that most of the anti-totem fanatics that formerly inhabited the board would have complained bitterly about before opining about their desire to fly "like an emo Peter Pan".

                                                I mean, you can make a Lunar very loosely based on Mercer or Heller, but you're going to have to change so very much of the character that people might need to squint and look very, very hard, since instead of a whipfist he grows a tail with a thagomizer, and proceeds to introduce his enemies to the late Thag Simmons. And he lacks a number of other defining traits, like the devastator, in favor of being able to turn into a sauropod and stomp very heavily on the ground. Also, he doesn't make the ground collapse under him spectacularly from the freakish "extra body mass" he's carrying around (because he isn't in this case, it increases or decreases based on what form he's in), or simply just absorb people like he's absorbing pudding right through his skin, even when wearing clothes.

                                                And there would be way more flying around as a bird, pouncing from height as a big cat, fighting tanks as a tyrannosaur. Or perhaps he prefers a half-human, half-totem form for kicking ass. And instead of plot-convenient NPCs doing most of the work, he goes into dream trances and sees a confusing series of images that hints at the next mission's objectives.
