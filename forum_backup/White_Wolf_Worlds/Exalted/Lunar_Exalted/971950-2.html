            Lunar Exalted seem to embody the pursuit of freedom and self-identity.

            They are chosen from those who know what it is like to live in captivity, trapped by one thing or another. Unable to live as they would chose. Luna gives them the power to escape and find their own place in the world.

            Further, when they are reborn, she gives each of them a Totem form to reveal their true self. Or perhaps all Lunars now choose their own Totems?

            For sure, Lunar Exalted are the only Host that gets to choose their Caste.


            Luna isn't the Unconquered Sun; she probably didn't envision her Chosen leading and uniting and elevating humanity into something greater. Luna may just not even care about humanity aside from the individuals she falls in love with and Exalts, which would be a neat reason why the Exalted of the Moon call themselves "Luna's Beloved". Luna wants to make her loved ones happy, but perhaps doesn't care what they do to find happiness.

            She would certainly prefer that they do something to defend Creation from Fair Folk, the Underworld, and demons, but that's something that most Lunar Exalted -- as human beings who were born and raised in Creation -- are naturally inclined to do, so that works well enough for her.

                                Originally posted by Thesaurasaurus View Post
                                See, it's one thing for the Lunars to take this approach, but another when the players and ST can't figure out what the hell a Lunar campaign is supposed to be. And even then, Solars already occupy the playspace of "anything you could possibly want to do" - as a matter of personal taste, I wish they didn't, but c'est la vie - and whatever a Solar chooses to do, they will do better than anyone else. Meanwhile, under the setting dictate that the Age of Sorrows be the Age of Sorrows, giving Lunars the role of "I dunno, whatever" relegates them to doing 'whatever' badly, or else there's no reason for the Solars to come in and fix everything. This makes the Lunars look like a bunch of stump-fucking asshole idiots, and likewise tarnishes any of the groups and peoples associated with them in the lore.

                                My preferred solution would be to emphasize that meaningful achievement is really fucking hard when very nearly the whole world is against you, and that the Lunars could accomplish a lot more and in a much more positive fashion if they had some breathing room from the Deebs and Sidereals trying to murder them nonstop. This is one reason I keep asking what Lunars do when they're not murdering Sids or Deebs, and what the less-hostile side of their powerset will look like - it's hard to portray a faction that routinely eats people as a matter of course sympathetically, and New Friend Aroma from the sample Changing Moon's list is preeeeeetty skeevy, which might well be the point, but still...
                                This is what my old group and I had issue with, when sitting around our gaming pit and discussing what campaigns are about, we ran in circles. The fact that Lunars were so highly adaptable and "monster heroes" wasn't the issue, it was what's their theme within the setting? With Solar it was fairly easy, we called it the "Phoenix Effect", which in brief was rising from the ashes of Creation's tottering on the edge, the Solar rebuild it to a new and glorious 3rd Age with their chief opponent being the Abyssals and Death Lords. (Note: This was my group and I's theme for Solar, not meant to imply this is what the authors of the books were claiming it was or should be).

                                Running a campaign where Solar and Lunar worked together towards that end was fine, as the Lunar were able to cover 2 or more weak sides of their Solar mate and were their ultimate Lts and ultimately that's what we wound up doing and it was a fairly enjoyable campaign. We had discussed beforehand how in a "Phoenix Effect" campaign, the Lunar was the one individual a Solar could truly trust above all others, even more so than their Circle mates whom they had faced against so much with. But a Lunar only campaign left us feeling that it was too much like a Werewolf campaign, not due to mechanical systems but due to the society the splat painted, what with the Elders, Silver Path, etc. We also discussed how Lunar don't have their version of Abyssals and why that was, but that is a different rabbit hole altogether and again, showed plenty of "crossing the proton streams" (obscure Ghostbuster's reference) of W;tA and EX.

                                Now part of the blame rests with us as gamers, who were devoted fans of the ST system (even though I often had to spend 100s of hours creating houseruled rewrites) as we had a lot of material stuck in our heads from other WW products as well as time played. Thus while the staff who wrote the Lunar book may not have contributed to any W:tA material, they (the Lunar authors) were likely affected to a certain extent just like we were, given how so many people who purchased/read the Lunar book drew many of the same comparisons my group and I did.

                                Later, back when my days didn't shrink to next to no free time, I did a Lunar rewrite that I shared with some other STs whom I met with online and did a bit of collaborating with. Unfortunately, several computer upgrades and hard-drive shreddings later, that rewrite is no longer to be found in either my archives or those I shared it with. Its summed up behind the spoiler below sparing casual readers my meandering babble.

                                (Disclaimer: Below is my solution that suited my intentions and goals for Lunar, it is not meant to imply it was superior to canon text or other people's ideas).

                                I had decided that what had me stumped as a ST was the thematic pairing of Sun and Moon as "a couple" when the text clearly suggested that Luna adored Gaia, but deferred to the US as It's lord that It had to obey (as did every other deity in creation) and was reinforced by the mating of Lunars to Solar. This is where I decided my rewrite needed to take place, so instead I said that Luna was an ally that the US courted and convinced to join the cause by his agreeing that Gaia was to be not only spared, but protected and nurtured.

                                I felt that the US ruled the day, and Luna the night, and much as in several classic myths the two "shared dominion and duties of the sky". Thus Luna is treated as a equal had It's own chosen, who would also commit to being allies to the US's chosen. This alliance was a bond the two parties would simply be able to sense and each would shore up the other's weaknesses much as their patrons did. There was no inherit slavery or obedience in this bond, it was instinctual level of "understanding/knowing" of each other with both generally sharing the greater goal of protecting and nurturing of Creation aka Gaia (note that Solar and Lunar differed in opinion as to what that meant when it came time to discuss details, but they were of the same mind when it came to the larger picture).

                                Then I decided that the Solar and Lunar each had a "impeccable foe" that they were ideally suited to fight, Creation had two primary enemies: The Primordials and The Wyld. These were two separate threats that required different kinds of champions, so the Solar were more suited to combat the Primordials and their ilk, and the Lunars were more suited to fight the Wyld. The word "more" is deliberate, while Lunars could fight Yozi and what not, their natural enemy was The Wyld and were innately more suited dealing with its chaotic energies than the Solar were, but that didn't mean Solar couldn't/didn't fight Fae or Lunar didn't fight Yozi, it just meant one was natural assets within easier reach to combat their primary foes with.

                                I had always cast my EX games as a "Flat Earth" style universe, where Creation was a disk floating in a sea of chaos, the edge of Creation came into contact with The Wyld where due to that contact, the Wyld had the most amount of defined form while still being "of chaos", but the deeper into "the disk" elements of the Wyld reached, the more beholden to the rules of existence such creatures became and the more defined. Thus a Fae well within Creation became a unique, but fixed, entity of finite abilities and (often strange) ritual, manners, alliances, etc. The "edges" of Creation were in a state of constant flux, the greater the empires of the Solar became, the larger Creation became. The Solar brought order and stability, as their domains grew, Creation expanded. In other words, the world disk got bigger in direct proportion to the efforts of the Solar as they grew the height of the first age.

                                The Lunar patrolled/guarded against the Wyld, though the greater share of this activity was at the outer third of Creation's borders, their duties would frequently draw them well within the inner 2/3rds of Creation (as "measured" out from the Earth pole). They would assist their Solar allies as needed, and frequently worked together towards common goals as they would lend each other's might against one another's "natural" enemies. So in a way, they were 'co-rulers' of Creation, in manner similar to how Luna and the US ruled the sky.

                                When the Uprising took place, it was a act of betrayal against the Lunar as much as it was the Solar, it wasn't a case of the Lunars saying "The Sidereal and Terrestrials betrayed my Lord and Master" as it was a case of "The Sidereal and Terrestrials betrayed Creation and us". The Lunar still have their natural foe of the Wyld, they are the ones who keep the Wyld from consuming Creation but they have lost much ground (as the decline of the Shogunate shows) over the years and were stretched too thin to combat the likes of the Death Lords, Yozi, Fae, and the general disarray of Heaven, at least until the Solar started to come back. So if anything, the Lunar are letting out a (cautious) sigh of relief and feel like their allies and companions of times past, have finally managed to find their way back.

                                Thus the elder Lunar don't have this entire outlook of former slaves having seen in the aftermath the reality of their collars, because that was never the case. However they did notice that the Solar had at the height of their abilities, enslaved lives of lesser power which led to the Usurpation. This makes the elders regard the new Solar with caution, while they may not know of the Great Curse per say, they do know that the Solar are not without flaws and can fall prey to excess, much like civilized nations can justify excesses in the name of a good cause. That doesn't mean they forgive those who endorsed the Usurpation, far from it, for to them all of the Exalted were part of a sacred oath that had been broken. Those who hold to the ideals behind the Usurpation are their enemies only slightly less than the Wyld/Primordials are. But those who fight against the Usurpation are seen as allies, be they DB or Sidereal.

                                So a all Lunar's theme was about assisting the Solar from time to time, delivering a unholy smackdown on those who "believe in the Usurpation" (that belief loosely stated says both the US and Luna are not to be worshiped/respected and are replaced with the DBs and little gods as the lords of Creation), keep the Wyld and its minions at bay, and fight against anything that "threatens Gaia" (i.e. Creation as a concept of ordered reality, of which the Natural Order is part of).

                                The snag I hit was with power levels. Conceptually I felt Solar and Lunars were equal in power, but the charms were already enough of a head ache and each had plenty of abusive loop holes they could exploit to where the only thing that I really had to debate was whether or not Lunar could do Solar level Sorcery. For my games i decided that they could, but their Sorcery was only capable of such heights with the proper phase of the moon. Loosely put, you want to do "War Solar Sorcery" it had to be on the nights of the full moon, etc. Otherwise I figured the differences in their charms didn't point at one innately being able to beat the other if they had the same degree of development, but I might've been wrong on that as I never had a chance to experiment with it.

                                I also did away with the 5 castes to mirror 5 castes things, but I don't recall at this moment all the things I did there, only that I had simplified it to the Full/Half/No moons and the Casteless were a type of Lunar who had no Solar ally and could venture deeper into the Wyld than other Lunar. I had also created a type of Solar that ironically enough, Onyx represented better as Exigents, but who at the time had no Lunar ally and didn't quite fall into any of the 5 Solar Castes.
