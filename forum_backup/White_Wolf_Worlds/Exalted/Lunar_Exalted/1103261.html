    Mario is a Lunar? Translation and evolution and type of inspirational source material

    06-13-2017, 05:02 PM
    People have noted translating a character to the system is always challenging.

    e3 is in swing and is inspiring me in weird ways. Mario has showed up with a new gimmick. This time he's meeting in the world of woman he shares an ambiguous connection to that can help him on his journey to power the means to track the rescue of his regular cake baker and go karting and golf partner from Bowser as he uses similar stolen power to force Peach to marry him and rule the world.
    ...
    Okay seriously!!!

    Actually the "sameness" helps lead to games LIKE exalted and the idea of a being being an incarnation of role/spirit. The Eternal Champion. Or The King Sent to Unite Britain. Etc.

    Still you often have to choose how you want to engage in what with your character from their inspiration. This is especially tough when you want them to function not just in spirit but actual effectiveness in system. For a bit Dawn Cast was a suckers game. Solars are powered by specializing and unless you chose poorly you didn't need five abilities being favored in pure combat capability. But what else would you call a wandering warrior and master of all combat and skills sent to fight the enemies of light and humanity

    In the latest Mario Odyssey trail the portabello plumber shows the ability to possess...damn near anything. People, stone statues, traffic cones, vehicles! Now as typical this enhancement is granted by a helper, Cappy, a wispy spirit who has possessed Mario's cap to help aid in ability and give him this skill, along with a ranged attack and double jumping ability.

    My subject line, now that I think about it, is rather wrong. From his overall freeness of posession and loss of self and need to insert in stories and collect ambient things that are common and important but for him souls-essence he's really more of a Rakhasa than a Lunar. Still with his introduction as a man taking tools against a beast who's taken a girl AND causing disaster he seemed, to me the Brawl Master (with a few dips into Melee and other things for hammers and such though he's never a MASTER of these just a user) Solar Exalted (hilariously Eclipse or Night he just lacks versatility and combat badassery to be a Dawn and is no prophet and definitely not a Doctor, Engineer, or Sage so not a Twilight). I suppose if you think "lesser power or underdog" is more important Fire Aspect. hehehehehehehehe. I remember on the ooooold forums, no even OLDER, but not the oldest, said Kenshin Homura couldn't be a Solar but a Wood Aspect, maybe with a hax martial art.
    So basically you see one aspect of the translation issue. Even if you are not dealing with a character who's constantly changing they will have multiple overlapping representatives or possible roles and you can't get everything out the gate OR have to lock out the potential.

    Basically just reacting and wonder if other boardfen are inspired by recent things to translate living or ongoing characters and what is best to emphasize and focus on.

    Another Example.
    Spiderman Classic. Seems perfectly suited for a Night Caste. Its his villains who are part Other. If he shapeshifts or leaves a 'pure' human existence, its a sign of him mutating or being corrupted. Even as he's evolved to be more organic as to "it just works" acrobat meets powerlifter meets minor gadgeteer with great danger/combat instincts...I don't think being part bug has been his thing so much as a curse or complication that flares up. I think JMS tried to idea of "Spider Totem man" but it didn't quite stick. aside from needing to hide his identity on the job he never leaves the human sphere and originally he often geared up with webshooters, tracers, spotlights, and so on. Not feeling intheme for a Lunar to make and equip advanced personal supertools to *disguise* or appear as an animal. The Movies emphasize the puberty or biological aspect and this is adapted into modern depictions. However I am sure Sunder the Gold would feel Peter Parker isn't THAT out of place for a Lunar. He is the original motormouthed trickster. But he wouldn't work for the Getiminian Exalted either, right?

    Finally I'm thinking gear. I love translating Zelda's ability expanding tools into Exalted use. Exalted seemed to not mind either having Azrael's extendo claws in the Night Castebook and other devices reminiscent of comics, games, and pulps/fantasy. Even an Excalibur's scabbard expy. Or a take off the holy Grail being a blood and thus health provider. One thing I'm thinking of is Zelda's Gust Jar or Luigi's Poltergust 6000.

                          With Batman (or any other similar character) you have to first decide which iteration you're looking at. Sometimes Batman in the comics Batman is basically just a street level hero, a smart, well trained guy who puts on a mask and punches robbers in the face. Other times he's the smartest guy in the universe who builds super-suits so he can fly through space and punch demigods in the face.

                          Ultimately Batman tends to get by on narrative power rather than super powers. He's not nearly anywhere near as fast or strong as other superheroes or villains, but the story's narrative always introduces some way for him to keep up with those super strong, super fast people and to allow him to contribute when they fight enemies who would normally be well beyond a "street level hero" when they show up in his comics (or when he shows up in theirs).

                          Which, of course, makes it impossible to perfectly translate Batman to a game like Exalted.

                          Also Possession Mario seems like a Fair Folk or a Ghost (or rather his hat is a Fair Folk or a Ghost who is somehow lending him some of its power).
