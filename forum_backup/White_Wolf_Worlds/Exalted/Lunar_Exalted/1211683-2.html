                                            Anyway, of the three Styles I said I'd narrowed it down to, I dissected and dismissed Terrible Ascent-Driven Beast Style above, and had the fundamental problem of First Pulse Style pointed out to me in another thread.

                                            The last one was Orgiastic Fugitive Style. I clearly recall Robert Vance saying he wasn't happy with this one; it didn't properly capture the nature of Drunken Boxing.

                                            Drunken Kung Fu Master seems like more of a Sidereal thing. Lunars have something of the oni about them, and the oni's love of alcohol is legendary, but not so much fighting effectively in a drunken stupor.


                                            So, that's about it for the old styles. It really does seem like the writers would have to create whole new Martial Arts Styles for the Lunar book, especially if Celestial Monkey is excluded.


                                            Ah, there was one I'd forgotten. Lightning Hoof Style.

                                            If Lunars are barbarians, then Lunars can be Mongols, and the Mongol ticket to victory was mounted archery. Though the Marukan nation would no longer be able to practice this Style at all.

                                            Still, within a Pack of Lunar Exalted, you might have one that prefers to fight in the forms of beasts large enough to ride, and one who prefers to fight from the saddle. A Style for fighting from the back of any mounted creature might therefore be extremely appropriate for Fangs at the Gate.

                                            Lunar children who inherited enough of Luna's glory might even be able to learn these Charms for themselves, to strike their mother's enemies from astride her shoulders, like San the mononoke princess and her wolf-mother Moro.


                                            Other than that, Styles for axes, hammers, and two-handed swords. Fantasy barbarians are all about wielding frighteningly huge kill-sticks.

                                            But I definitely wouldn't turn up my nose at a Spider Style based around restraining with projectiles like bolas, nets, and lassos. That's also good hunting.
