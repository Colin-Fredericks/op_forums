                                    There's a world of difference between hitting harder and hitting better - watch some MMA videos if you don't believe me. A harder hit will probably be more likely to stun or even kill if it lands in a half-decent spot, but a precise enough strike can incapacitate with a tenth or even a hundredth the force. A harder hit will probably power through the opponent's defense to damage them anyway, but a "better" one will waste far less energy to the same effect.

                                    The primary (but far from only!) difference between "punch harder" and "punch better" is that of economy - the Solar can do what the Lunar can, more cheaply, or exert himself similarly to greater effect.
