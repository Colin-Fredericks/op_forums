    Before I look at anything else, I would comment that the main reason why I think any comparisons to Alchemicals are going down the wrong path is that a big reason why those Exalted are able to get away with the fact that the actual substance of their Charms is often direct and covers pretty much all bases is that the element of resembling prosthetics or plug ins isn't just aesthetic, it's integrated into the very concept that Alchemicals can only access a limited number of their Charms at once, and need to go through a reformatting process to do things differently.

    I don't think there's anything Lunars could do that would be comparable that would not end up just trying to chase after something established for other Exalted (or even the Fair Folk) that is less theme cohesive.

    A sense of versatility and adaptability is not integral to the Lunar concept, but it's got a lot of establishment, enough to validate a decision to largely not design them towards an idea that use of their powers across the board requires shifting into a particular form and not being able to readily assume others.

    And if they didn't have that limitation, then its just giving them a large suite of Charms whose only sense of character is cosmetic, which I think is out of place for the general design philosophy of Exalted. For all Exalted, I think the strongest way to express personality and the kind of narrative they empower is in the functions of the Charms themselves, where the cosmetic elements are secondary (hence why there are many Terrestrial Charms with no overt elemental manifestation). I think the guy who wrote Keychain of Creation put it best in how this is largely a matter of that being most of the level at which things are engaged with and feel most tactile, because there's no genuine visual element to any of it.

    I think the game has a sturdy and well-established foundation of how most of what makes any of the Exalted evocative is in the stuff that takes place under the hood. Like how the Solar Exalted have a lot of things to represent their brand of being a superlative weapons fighter that function solely through dice mechanics that are not apparent to any onlookers in character.

    I regard that as the ultimate failing of Lunar Charms in past Editions; one way or another, they were too function based, rather than finding something comparable to how a Dragon Blooded can be athletic in a way that is evocative of fire even when they're not actually using flame jets to propel themselves. Third Edition was successful both at capturing the sense of what the Lunar would be evocative of and mechanics that then do that (while still creating positive feedback for fully shapeshifting into forms that align with what is being evoked).

    I have approximate knowledge of many things.

            You know, I'm looking back over some of the Charms that more directly involve bodily modifications. Things like the Charm for breathing underwater entailing the Lunar developing gills or growing additional lungs. The Charm for climbing sheer surfaces by moulding their body to adhere to even the smallest forms of purchase. Adder Fang Method is described specifically in terms of growing skin glands or venom ducts to convey their poison. And Kraken-Arm Lash is something to extend a limb out to medium range to grapple somebody. Plus some others that describe the mechanism for action in terms of some change but leave the specifics to the taste and imagination of the player.

            It's my preference that not all Charms be that by default, but I also see that as the kind of thing that creates a firm basis for stunting Charms that aren't like that by default to entail some additional physical element.

            It wouldn't cover everything. It's still not in the Lunar wheelhouse to have a Charm to express some power just because it's a novel trait possessed by an animal. On the other hand, as I said back in the Electric Lunar thread, something as specific as wanting eel electrocution already strikes me as more of a Supernatural Merit by way of mutation anyway, and Perfected Hybrid Interaction is an Essence 2 Charm (no Attribute) that allows a Lunar to acquire mutations from outside of their spirit shape. It's more limited than Hybrid Body Transformation, only grants four dots per purchase and can only be purchased (Essence) times, but it's still an inroad provided for them to possess such capabilities at will that is not shared by other Exalted (where you either have to purchase them at character creation or use external sources of magic that are potentially hazardous to acquire such Merits).

            That's also where Lunar creation of spider silk goes; Spinnerets is a three-dot Merit for generating lines of silk that function as exceptional equipment for just about everything somebody could think to use such for (including swinging around like Spider-Man). These things weren't eliminated entirely, there was just a revaluation of what the most cohesive way for them to be accessed by Lunars was.

            Oh, actually, the Charm Sharing Luna's Gifts also allows a Lunar to grant themselves mutations based on shapes they possess outside of the spirit shape for only three motes a pop (each use of the Charm can grant up to five Merit dots), plus experience debt. It differs from Perfected Hybrid Interaction in that while that Charm allows one to shapeshift in and out of the mutations for one mote per Merit dot, Sharing Luna's Gifts is permanent. In any case, it is an Essence 1 Charm.

            While being Merits puts some things below the level of what Charms are capable of, I think the Third Edition approach to Merits in general and Supernatural Merits in particular makes the Lunar capacity to harness them a lot more freely than other Exalted thus far a worthwhile part of their repertoire.

            I'm wondering about Lunar Charms that key specifically off of mutations... I don't think they would be suitable for publication, because accounting for the range of Merits that might be a prerequisite would be really messy and I think they'd have awkward interactions for powers to remove mutations, but I think they can be viable as a space for Lunar homebrew.

                My advocacy for the Protean Keyword is straightforward and twofold.

                One is that it creates an incentive for a particular kind of Lunar aesthetic, in which continuous shifting through numerous full animal shapes is emphasized. It also contributes to a particular tone to their social interactions; there are Protean Charms for human shapes to provide bonuses towards people with Intimacies to the original identities. Indeed, there are incentives to perform interaction in animal shapes as well, for when people have Intimacies (particular cultural) towards those. The Lunar presence in such scenes is distinguished if they do it in the shape of a locally venerated beast.

                The other reason is that Second Edition's introduction of the idea of an innate capacity to acquire and assume shapes, unrelated to Charms, is apparently something Third Edition developers saw value in to contribute for their distinct character and provide for unique mechanical advantages. The problem is that it can ultimately be superceded by their Charms; it's all well and good to turn into a tyrant lizard for extra strength, but why do that once you have a Charm to provide mythic feats of strength? By making it that such mythic feats become enhanced when performed in a complementary shape, the innate shapeshifting is able to keep its relevance as the Lunar grows, rather than being a quaint thing that they outgrow.

                It may not be to a given person's taste, and they may even think the execution leaves something to be desired. But I think to dismiss it as shallow does a disservice to the people who directed it from the top.
