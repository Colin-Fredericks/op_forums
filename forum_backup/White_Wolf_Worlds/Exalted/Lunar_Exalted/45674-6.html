            I'm very conflicted about whether there should be plant-aspected Lunars*. On one hand, I think it'd be very interesting: Treebeard, Poison Ivy, Swamp-Thing are all pretty great characters. On the other, how do you fit them into Lunars without it feeling like "oh yeah, and there are some plant-Lunars too, but we aren't really going to put much effort into them" because Lunar charms are going to channel animals de facto (if not de jure).

            On the other hand, Lunars have problems. While the devs have sold me on the fact that they can fix their fluff (see: Holden's spiel on barbarians, among other gems from the old forums), I haven't seen much about fixing the mechanical aspects (including but not limited to the Silver Solars bit that everyone is tired of talking about and which I do not wish to re-open).

            In my mind, expanding Lunars to include plants could theoretically add flavor/definition to the Lunars, but it could also weaken their schtick significantly as well. It'd be a really tough thing to accomplish. The Lunar identity is already weak: the deliberate downplaying of body horror is understandable but also very limiting. I was playing around with this in my head a few days ago... the closest I came to anything half-decent was Lunars being in complete control of their own body. This includes everything from myosin-strengthened bound to steady heart-rate insinuation to feline-tapetum stare. Lots of permanent charms that improve the body, with 'burst' charms that could produce a related effect coming off of those permanents. Giving them very relaxed dice-limits would be interesting too. Solars get Attribute+Ability as max, Lunars get Attribute*2+Essence? Anything to make them more interesting...

            * Don't read into me saying 'aspect.' It was the first word that came into my head and has nothing to do with the caste vs. aspect debate.

                        I think that if you want to play a Lunar who spends a long time studying oaks, who then produces children with leaves and flowers in their hair or rough barklike skin, nobody is going to look at you strangely.

                        That's just not a "typical" example of how Lunars work -- it's a strange edge-case that they can twist into by combining several powers, as evidenced by the fact that you needed two fairly obscure and situational knacks to even approach it. (You can produce beastmen right out of the box, but to produce children with even subtle plant features, you need both Laurel-and-Ivy Technique and then, since you can't reproduce with humans in plant form, you need another knack to let you imprint features and mutations without having to take plant form.)

                        So it's not like it's not allowed, but you're never going to see Lunars doing it in the backstory, or canon charms specifically devoted to it or anything -- given that both the knacks are on the obscure and situational side, and there's no actual reason to want to do it in general, I wouldn't be surprised if there's maybe one or two Lunars who have produced meaningful numbers of kids with plantlike mutations, ever.
