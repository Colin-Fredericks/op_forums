    I actually fully expect Moonlight Hunt to survive in an Essence 5 appropriate way. It's simply too interesting a hook for what are essentially the mobility exalts to give it up simply because it's previously been problematic. I think explaining it as time manipulation is kind of silly. You can easily just say "they become so much faster" and leave it at that.

    I don't think most of the existing Lunar perfect effect Charms will return, just because most of them were so poorly written they produced a lot of grumbling and griping. Which is somewhat disappointing, bluntly. Most of them are interesting and unique in ways that many other perfect Charms simply were not.

    I don't expect to see any of the lairing Charms return for Lunars, with the possible exception of one that simply carves out a temporary safe place anywhere for the Lunar to hide in while they rest. I think Wyld-keyworded Charms in general will see the axe.

        Originally posted by Sunder the Gold View Post
        Why?
        It was a JoJo's Bizarre Adventure reference (Series 5)

        I can see a single alternate form being one whole evocation, at least. We're probably not going to see any easy innate artifact power or evocation which allows it to assume any and all possible forms, no.

        But if a moonsilver sword cannot shapeshift into a different weapon form at all, what's the point?
        The point is that it's a legendary sword made to be a legendary sword.

        I think physicality is going to be more of a recurring theme than a universal requirement. If all Lunar Charms required some blatant personal transformation, how would they be different from the organ-based Liminals or cybernetic-dependent Alchemicals?
        Sleek and natural instead of a grotesque patchwork or obviously mechanical implants.

        Perhaps physicality is also going to be emphasized through the effects that Lunars don't get, like the power to conjure external sensory illusions. To make people see things falsely, they'll probably have to rely on words and physical props. Or, rather the scalpel approach, the hammer method of driving others mad enough to hallucinate wildly.
        Well, you could make people hallucinate, but it would involve turning into a particular type of amphibian and eating a whole lot of a specific type of ant. And getting licked.

        I suppose if one wants a butterfly for a spirit shape, having a giant butterfly form to start with isn't all that bad, especially if you can acquire the ability to shrink it whenever you want.

        But who wants to be a pygmy elephant or tyrant lizard?
        Mouse-sized butterflies wouldn't necessarily be very giant. As for the opposite side, I could see reasons for either, but I do generally advocate that the totem should be exempt from Knack requirements. There should be no expansion of choice, just offer the whole wide variety up front and require the expansions to get other shapes. You also avoid the ambiguity of what you can choose as a totem, and don't have to write in which ones, specifically, expand the choices.

                    I don't see why you would get rid of illusion effects.
                    Lunars whole Man and Monster dichotomy thing meshes nicely with "the moon is associated with insanity" powers, and inducing hallucinations, nightmares, and madness is definitely a thing I want my lunar shamans and tricksters (hell, maybe even warriors) to do. I can definitely see tying those powers more closely to the self, though: making oneself an object of nightmares or of obsession or of hallucinations. I like the idea of a Lunar being able to walk up to you in the shape of your beloved grandmother, carrying a pie that smells just like the pie she used to make even though Granny died twenty years ago. I'm not so interested in a Lunar being able to conjure a palace out of moonlight and gossamer...
