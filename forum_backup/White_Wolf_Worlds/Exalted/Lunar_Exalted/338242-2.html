                                                            One thing that I find interesting is that there's quite a lot of things that people say they want from Lunars which I think "uh, no, definitely not, that ruins the whole point of them." And other people do seem to argue about it too.
                                                            I think people can't agree what they want from Lunars, and that's possibly why it's difficult to come up with strong themes for them. I mean, they have two strong themes: shapeshifting, and animals. And some people seem to dislike that? If you take that away, they have very little left.

                                                            Anyway, I'll try and keep this list from getting too long:

                                                            I absolutely do not want Lunars to lose their animal theme. Reskin it, move it around, change how it works, whatever, but I absolutely should still be able to take an animal, think about it's mythic tropes and physical abilities, and build a Lunar off it, because in my experience more than half of the most iconic, memorable characters I've seen played in Celestial games are built this way.

                                                            In that vein, I want them to have at least the option of having a true form/totem. Some people say they don't want it. I wouldn't be massively upset if it's not compulsory, but it absolutely must be there as an option.

                                                            I want to be able to gain the forms of powerful creatures I defeat in combat and eat the hearts of, but didn't actually hunt for hours and hours beforehand. For example, if I run into a behemoth or a second-circle demon and get into a really difficult fight but win.
                                                            Honestly, the whole Sacred Hunt needs rethinking. I don't mean the concept of getting shapes by eating hearts, I mean what happens before it. The issue is that it seems mostly written for hunting down a kind of animal, ie "I want a dog shape" rather than "I want that dog right there". Which gets very awkward with the shapes of demons, elementals, behemoths, etc. I mean, you're not going to hunt down a superlative example of a behemoth because there's only one.

                                                            I want there to be a lot more Int and Wits charms, and the Wits charm not to be a copy of Dex charms.

                                                            I want something similar to Chimerism to be there, as in the horrific mutated monster kind of Lunar. It could be like 1st ed with having multiple true forms that mix together, or something new. I don't want all Lunars to be hideous body-horror abominations, but I'd like some to be able to (what I mean is, I want the mutated Lunar I'm playing to be able to go down this route, while the hot fox girl that's also in the party to not have to).

                                                            'I'd like the theme of savage bestial horror (insanity, eating people's hearts, fucking animals) to still be there with some Lunars, but it doesn't have to be there for all Lunars.

                                                            I'd like for there to be very, very few Lunar charms that are just Solar charms that have been nerfed a bit. I mean, excellencies is okay, but not much more beyond that.

                                                            I'd like for not every Lunar to have to get silver tats. I mean, silver magic tattoos is cool (though I wish they didn't always have to look the same) but it's not so cool when 99% of Lunars have them, it becomes rather bland.

                                                            STing Bronze Age Exalted
