                    The 'problem' is that the Lunar Bond needs to be flexible. It's a karmic connection which should be able to encompass everything from BFF's to star-crossed eternal lovers to fated nemesis.

                    Whether the bond is mutual or not, at the start, is moot. In fact, lets face it, the option of it being mutual is actually already on the table. See the sidebar 'Roleplaying, A Reminder', Exalted Core p. 218. Intimacies don't solely come from someone employing the social system. If you are going for the 'Eternal Lovers' option, by all means, let the Solar gain an intimacy representing a tie of love upon meeting his fated love, eyes widening as memories of their True Love across the ages flood him, because it makes sense in the story you are telling. And if you are telling a different story, well, do something else, up to and including the Solar going 'Huh? Who exactly are you supposed to be?'. No need to shoehorn this tool into something so restrictive, and I am fine with the only one being that the Lunar definitely feels something upon meeting her bondmate and the rest being up in the air.

                    Instead, lets focus on how this could play out in actual play! I myself have fond memories of playing out the asymmetric version, starting out with bond 5 and the solar (who was a PC) actively disliking my lunar and having to work hard, really hard, for this to finally blossom into an epic romance. But I've also seen most of the other variations over the years, and that's exactly what we want right? A system that supports the telling of many different stories as possible?

                    Perhaps a reminder of all these options and the wide range of possibilities suffices?
