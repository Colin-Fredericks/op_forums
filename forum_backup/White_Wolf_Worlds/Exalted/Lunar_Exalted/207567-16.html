                                        I assume that, when it comes to those Dynasts who are adventuring around the Threshold and beyond, a lot survive based off of the basic math; there are lots of them and only a few Lunars, the odds of them running into each other are pretty low, and not all Lunars necessarily have the best means to uncover Dynast travel plans. And even if they do intersect, even adventuring Dynasts often travel with sizable entourages, including a protective detail. Even considering that, I'm all for the idea that the number of Dynasts who don't come back because they encountered Anathema is disturbing to the Realm, even if it's a minority of the overall.

                                        Even so, that hardly seems like where the story ends; I'd say a fair number of the time, investigators (often Found Eggs) are sent out to find where the last signs of the lost Dynast's movements are end, and a lot of the time they pick up the trail of the Lunar and call in the Wyld Hunt. I can imagine that there are Lunars who spend years dodging the Hunts sent for them, big prolonged campaigns as they all run a merry circuit through some of the more obscure corners of the world. On that note, I'd also say there are Lunars who pick up on lone Dynasts while they're in the middle of other, sensitive projects that can't afford to lose their attention or have heat brought down on them at the moment, and so make the calculated decision to let the Dynast go, unless they're worth the cost.

                                        Not to mention ones whose strategies against the Realm aren't exactly concerned with killing people in the first place, or who are at least ready to let their fellows take up that side of the campaign. I'd say there are times where a Lunar who sees a Dynast getting too close to something they don't want the Realm knowing about might head a few miles off, do something in an alternate form that catches the Dynast's attention, and redirecting them for a bit, before trailing off.

                                        And I'd say plenty of Dynasts while they're living on the Isle trust in the defences of their borders and interior, and the difficulties an aggressive Lunar would bring on themselves in the heart of their enemy's power, and the statistical unlikelihood based on the limited number of Lunars, that they can feel secure in their own homeland.

                                        I think these are conditions that allow for occasions where Dynasts might get killed by Lunars while still being plausible as a thing that is not endlessly happening.

                                                    In the current game, the Wyld Hunt finds Lunars by astrology, spirits, charms and other magical means. When the dice are on their side, they know where and when a Lunar is going to appear well ahead of her actual arrival. When a Lunar Exaltation is going to happen somewhere in the Threshold, both the Wyld Hunt and the Silver Pact usually predict it with enough time to dispatch a party before the Exaltation itself actually happens.

                                                    It's not just a case of Dragon-Bloods physically chasing Lunars around the threshold, following a trail of sightings and rumors. They know where a Lunar is going, often, before than the Lunar himself does.
