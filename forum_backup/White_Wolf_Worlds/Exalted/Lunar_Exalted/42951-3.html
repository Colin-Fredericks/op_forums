    Castes and Aspects approach the causality of their talents in opposite way.

    You become a Dawn Caste because you have certain qualities; you have certain qualities because you are a Fire Aspect. Obviously there is nuance to this, but I feel this has a grain of truth. There is that feeling as a Dragon-Blooded that using the powers of a different Aspect is partaking of a completely different nature than your own, both in the departure from your usual aesthetics and elements and from the Essence surcharge you suffer for it.

    Dividing Lunars into Aspects would suggest this, to my mind - that a Lunar enters an Aspect to become. Rather than having elders scrutinize your life and deeds and deducing which Caste would best fit you, you choose which qualities you desire, which powers you seek. The power of the Lunar Exaltation is raw and protean, and the Aspects of the moon are ways of channeling it towards a more specific purpose, and you make that purpose, instead of it being an extrapolation of who you are.

    So, a scholar who must find the power to go at the heart of the Realm to rescue his daughter sold in slavery might ask to become a Changing Moon; it is not who he is, but it is who he needs to be.

    And in turn, once taken, the Aspect informs your nature and shapes you. There is a spark of lunacy in you, and its form is moulded by the Aspect you have taken.

    Bits and pieces of Exalted setting.

Elder Terrestrials are much more prone to showing their elemental aspect through small unconscious habits than are recently Exalted Dragon-Bloods, who have not yet had the years of experience with their respective element to color their interactions with the world around them. So extreme are the differences between the elemental aspects that philosophers

                Hm, on further thoughts:

                Castes are based on your deeds: you are chosen as a Dawn because of your combat prowess or tactical acumen. The diligent study of unknown horrors has proven to the UCS that you are worthy of the title of Twilight Caste Solar.

                Aspects are more along the line of your origins: Elemental Exaltation and its Aspects are hereditery (I am not completely usre if it's something totally random, or do you still have to prove 'worthy' of it) while Liminal's Aspects are decided through the method of their 'birth'.

                So Caste is a reflection of who you are as a person, and the deeds to your name, while Aspect is more what you are, almost independant of who you are.


                For Lunas this becomes extra fun, due to 'Caste fixing'. Not sure if that will be a thing still in 3E, but the quirk of Caste fixing is that it isn't your patron who decides your Caste: it's other Lunars, who decide your Caste based on.... your aptitudes and your nature, if I understand it correctly? I can see Aspects instead of Caste if you just say that 'Aspect fixing' is not simply attaching a Caste template to your Lunar, but rather it is the refining of your essence with the being of who you are, what you are, and how you do things: a warrior who spent his life fighting could have the aspect of the Full Moon, as he views every fight as a challenge, while one who is tired of fighting would look at alternate routs, and be a No or Changing Moon instead.

                Though looking at that explanation, it seems Castes have more staying power. Though much hinges on Caste fixing.
