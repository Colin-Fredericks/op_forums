                            Though I'm coming around on a few fronts I stand by my statement that Ex2 Lunars could not be good crafters or bureaucrats and that this was a failing of the MoEP: Lunars book. To answer Isator Levi's question I think there are several answers that all could be distinctly Lunar in theme, if we had charm support for them. The anarchistic economist who beggers the rich and drops piles of money on the poor while in Strix form would be a great trader Lunar. The village chieftain who leads his people through the difficult process of Realm "relocation" ala Trail of Tears but who poisons the process so that the Realm garrison forces assimilate to his culture ala Dances With Wolves would be a great Lunar bureaucrat. Neither of these ideas are possible using the pretty kitty technique that was the entire backbone of Ex2 Lunar social combat.

                            Have had time to think about and having heard people's arguments, here is what I'd like: more generally useful charms. The solar splat is well rounded and allows a multitude of concepts because their charms apply in a wide variety of situations. There are a multitude of character concepts that use Speed the Wheels, not just magistrates. Lunars should bring something unique to the table, yes, but in Ex2 all of their charms reinforced a narrow character concept. This was also a big problem for Abyssals. And this should be easier to do when their magic is already attribute-based! I'd like more charms in Intelligence that make you "think better" and fewer that are unnecessarily limited to "thinking better while you hunt and trap game animals."

                                    What I'm saying is that there was no charm support and without charm support you could not do those things at exalted tier. Literally no one wants to play a celestial exalt who's only magical ability in their chosen field is an excellency. Charms are how Exalted models the ability to be supernaturally gifted at a thing and Lunars had a wide range of potential goals that they could not be supernatural gifted at because they had no charms to supplement those actions. All pretty kitty allowed you to do was be so pretty people gave you what you wanted. Yes it was powerful but saying that's enough to satisfy the needs of social players is like saying that the Solar melee charms by themselves were everything the combat player needed. We all want to feel like our characters are unique and that is what charmsets allow us to do, if they have variety.

                                    So while it was true that the core book did not provide good rules for bureaucratic rolls the fact that no charms even attempted to support that character concept is a failure. Solars had charms that allowed them to advance that character concept long before we had Masters of Jade. If you include absolutely no charms that interact with a focus then it may as well not exist to your exaltation type because no one is going to be able to play that and feel useful in a party where everyone else is taking concepts that are supported by the charmset. So it is very important that in Ex3 the developers provide charms for the Lunars that are varied enough that they can support many playstyles that are not just barbarian beastman, not just one or two.

                                                            Fine fine fine, I'll parse this out differently then:

                                                            In Ex3 I want more non-violent options for the Lunars that are supported with general-use charms.

There's nothing in the crafting rules leaks that needs to be Solar-specific that i see and gimping them on that front was just perpetuating the Ex2 narrative that they'd built and done basically nothing since the Usurption which is a narrative the Devs have claimed they want to eliminate anyway.
