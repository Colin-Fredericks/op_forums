    But all of this is moot if the Lunars cannot transform anything but themselves. Of course, if they can't do that, they can't curse others with the forms of beasts or others, as with Wondrous Lunar Transformation.

    And would Lunars NOT have the ability to make men into women and humans into animals?
    Confirmation would make me incredibly happy. Minimal shaping of the world around them can be fine, but stuff like Wondrous/Insidious Luna Transformation (hopefully new things, maybe to the tune of infectious lycanthropy?) feel like it's right in their mythic wheelhouse. Leaving it out would feel like a huge missed opportunity, and I'd be deeply curious about what the devs feel would justify that.

        Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post
        I'm personally not too fond of this, partially because I can't see many instances where it looks meaningfully different from what a more practically-engaged Exalt would do, and partially because I find ideas of "Sidereals do stuff by seeing the future" to be a really shallow pool to draw ideas from, at least in comparison to their actual published Charmset.
        Hmm, yeah, Sidereal magic is surprisingly not *just* about seeing the future.

        Now, when people play Sidereals, they play them for characters who can see the future. And that's a big part of their appeal, being visionary.

        And they do actually have a lot of Charms that do work on this basis, seeing the weave of events and using this information to avoid problems, find the best solution, etc. They have a lot of powers like this in the "knowing" abilities (awareness, lore, Funnily enough it seems like seeing the future shows up most in their combat Charms - Jedi warriors are cool!

        Still, seeing the future, is far from the sole expression of how their Charms work. They're not simple precognitives. Their Charms tend to at least as much and probably more be about manipulating and declaring fate, according to their own passions and vision - exchanging fates, drawing auspicious and useful fates and their essences to themselves, dealing with spirits and making bargains (with fate and spirits), etc. with little actual basis in trying to see what the future is *supposed* to be. Actually a lot of people find these to be the more evocative and attention grabbing parts of their Charmset (but again, they're not the only part and Sidereals without that visionary aspect would be missing something).

        There's room in their craft magic for both.

        Originally posted by Gaius View Post
        Holden mentioned in the quotes I posted that Lunar craftsmanship will typically take the form of some piece of personal kit for himself or another. That's mainly a lead-in, and certainly not an affirmation that Lunars are in any way gifted at making stuff for themselves and particular individuals. To take it that way would completely blow off all the context around it. Still, it sounds like a cool idea, so I'm running with it as a fun direction anyway.
        Looking at those quotes, they seem to boil down to -

        1. Shapeshifting generally doesn't help with crafting. (this is not a ban on shapeshifting magic that does, just a statement)
        2. Intelligence Charms, on the other hand, clearly can. (although from context and what else the writers have said these will have to not take the form of "giant brain" Charms).
        3. Lunars don't tend towards large scale "engineering" projects because their current style (and perhaps this is a bit of a product of a persisting nature) trends them towards being on the run, travelling light and high mobility, "off the grid".
        4. This said, Lunars don't necessarily make quite as much personal gear for themselves either, because of higher tool independence (just notably more than large scale projects).
        5. They clearly aren't going to outperform Solars at any kind of crafting, even if they wanted to focus the same way, which they wouldn't for reasons. This is not to say that they are bad - they are very good, just that Solars are better at it.

        Hard to argue with any of that. Phrasing Lunar crafting in terms of the restrictions from their present situation and their strengths making certain kinds of gear unnecessary is a lot more useful than saying that it would take away from other splats to have them be good at certain abilities (or Attribute magic doesn't support crafting like it does combat etc.).

        I like the ideas ITT about Lunar crafting power involving personal, authentic, uncontrolled passion and organic qualities (e.g. Gaius's post).

That Solars could shape the Wyld in 2E was a great mistake in blurring that boundary. Crafting glamour, forging dreams, designing the intelligence behind an A.I - all of these should be areas Lunar excel over all others in. The cut and dry is old and dead 2E stuff. Sidereals can simply declare they had some fail safe incorporated when issues arise - retroactively) . Solars are really just about the strongest bridge, the tallest building, the fastest airship - its about excellence and performance and that's all they should be about.                                     The falcastra of Zatesh whose edge pulls the Wyld is just an external embodiment of the power of that god over the Wyld, just as the Godspear of All-Searing Noon or the Blade of Saturn represent qualities and powers of their Incarnae. Luna occasionally wields it because he left a big impact on her personality, but Luna's overall symbolism doesn't really conduct her embodying her powers in a panoply of tools.
