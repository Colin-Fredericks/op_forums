    Originally posted by Robert Vance View Post
    "Turn into a fly, go up someone's nostril, shapeshift into a tyrant lizard" is not a combat strategy that produces fun gameplay. A Storyteller's job is to produce fun gameplay. Sometimes, rules are unfortunately not helpful to them in this job.
    If a Lunar in whatever form is already inside your body, it's pretty much all over for you whether the Lunar can shapeshift or not. Claws of the Silver Moon is all that would be needed to give you a gruesome death. It's an easily exploitable scenario, but you're not going to fix it by saying there's no shapeshifting inside a cramped space. Better to just agree with your players not to exploit their abilities that way because it's not fun.

                                        You know, what's funny to me is that I don't think anyone on this thread was actually aware, until I pointed it out, that no known force in Creation, Hell or Heaven can prevent a Lunar from changing shape. Not orichalcum chains, not sorcery, not the Yozi Isidoros straining every muscle in his body until his veins go pop. That's kind of awesome IMO. And it's a pretty basic and important fact about Lunar shapeshifting, and you'd think people would be aware of it, but no one mentioned it until I did, and I don't think anyone was going to because no one actually knew the rules.
