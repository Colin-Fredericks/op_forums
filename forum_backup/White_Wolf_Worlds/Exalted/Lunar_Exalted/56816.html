    [Old Forum Repost] Lunar Fan Errata

    01-24-2014, 12:25 PM
    MoEP: Lunars

    Character Creation

    Attributes and Favored Traits
    Choose three non-Caste Attributes as Favored Attributes. Select these Traits before assigning dots to Attributes, which receive an allotment of 9/7/5 for Lunars with Castes. Prioritization is now along Caste, Favored and non-Favored Attribute lines for these Lunars, respectively being primary, secondary and tertiary in assignment.
    Casteless Lunars choose three Favored Attributes, which need not conform to any pattern other than personal qualities. Such Lunars use the same prioritization method as non-Alchemicals, but gain one dot to add on to a single Favored Attribute.

    Choose only one Ability as Favored; it need not be Survival. Regardless of whether Martial Arts if favored or not, all Lunars pay a flat 6 bonus points or 11 experience points for Martial Arts Charms; similarly, universal expertise in Knacks allows their acquisition for 5 bonus points or 10 experience points apiece.

    Abilities and Silver Pact Training
    Lunars who have received training from their fellows gain two free dots in Survival and one free dot in Archery, Martial Arts, Melee or Thrown, plus twenty-five more dots to place where they will. Moonchildren also have a bonus dot in their single Favored Ability.

    Backgrounds
    Any Lunar may start with the Taboo Background. Lunars who begin play with a fixed Caste gain one free dot in the Mentor Background and may ignore restrictions on social Backgrounds such as Influence.

    Knacks and Charms
    Lunars who have been trained by their more experienced compatriots begin with a combination of Charms and Knacks totaling ten, which must include at least two Knacks and at least four Caste or Favored Charms.


    Sorcery and Necromancy

    The Chosen of Luna know the dark as well as the light – the depths as well as the heights. Accordingly, they can initiate into the Labyrinth Circle of Onyx, learning and using spells of the second circle of necromancy.

    Castes

    Save for the Anima Banner of the Full Moon Caste and Changing Moon Caste, as well as the Anathema entries, replace the descriptions of the Castes with the following:

    Full Moon Caste

    Revealed in full, Luna’s face shines down on the night as an anomaly in its patterns – brighter than the stars, out of place in a time of darkness, seemingly awake while the world slumbers. Accordingly, the Full Moons are possessed of the foremost bodily power of the Moonchildren, able to push their bodies further against physical hardship, their might, speed and coordination all things of awe
    .
    While it is a common mistake, the Children of the Vigilant Moon are not the perfect warriors and soldiers that the Dawn Caste produces. In their corporeal refinement, they excel in certain areas that would be considered useful by a disciplined military organization, they do not have the inherent talent for other forms of excellence that an armed force would typically demand. Like most Lunar Exalted, they cut across the boundaries of socially-mandated callings in their great potential; able to keep open lines of communication and travel through personal action; the same methods can be used to inspire others, facilitate exploration and acquisition of knowledge, break in where the powerful do not want them, and, from time to time, act as a champion or a highly specialized variety of soldier. Tasks that require superhuman energy, power and precision are where the Full Moons are at their best.

    The ranks of the Silver Tempests are drawn from those who have survived past their bodily limits. While many survived by obstinately continuing a journey beyond the point of exhaustion or endured terrible wounds for a protracted period of time, Full Moons have often ended up finding their identities by asking so much might of their frames that their muscles would have been irrevocably damaged afterward, or raced in bizarre or undignified ways simply to keep up hope while being chased by a raksha’s wind-swift behemoth.

    When a subject catches the eye of one of Luna’s Monsters, it is often when the Full Moon in question witnesses someone with a great deal of pluck, often outstripping their capabilities or ambitions. When the relentless living dynamo lends her capabilities to such an individual’s concern, their most direct enemies often end up beaten or utterly annihilated, and as long as they stay true to themselves, need not fear from treachery involving poison or something so crude as an assassination attempt. Those who stray from the path and earn the Lunar’s ire, however, often end up facing the same conclusion of life that their foes faced.

    Scaled up to larger organizations, Night-arms are less at ease. While some may have a gift for interaction that makes presentation and administration easier, a Full Moon tends to have little to do other than pull extra shifts of work when not in the field, personally taking care of an outside matter. Should an organization end up with a disenchanted Night-arm, however, they can expect to be taken apart piece by piece, and in the long war against the Dragon-Blooded and their treacherous backers, the Full Moons are the ones who spring up to make every shot from an Essence cannon or implosion bow one wasted against a foe who shrugs it off, as well as divesting sky chariots, warbirds or warstriders of their pilots and not a small number of gears and springs.

    Anima Effects: For five motes, the Full Moon may instill her body with Essence for the scene, making her better able to resist injury and exhaustion. Halve any fatigue modifiers that accrue, rounding down, and allow her to soak lethal damage with her full Stamina rating; when paired with effects that provide similar resilience to the Exalt, add her (Essence) to her natural soak ratings. At the 11+ mote level, this effect begins automatically.
    Caste Attributes: Living engines of lasting power, the Full Moon Caste has an affinity for every aspect of bodily talent and direct, physical action. They favor Strength, Dexterity, and Stamina.
    Associations: The season of winter, the color white, the element of earth, and the Maiden of Endings
    Sobriquets: Children of the Vigilant Moon, Silver Tempests, Luna’s Monsters, Night-arms, the Frenzied (derogatory)
    Concepts: Message-runner, indefatigable ditch-digger, dancer, cat-burglar, village brute, internal alchemy specialist, Terror of the Wood


    Changing Moon Caste

    Ever-shifting, Luna’s face never remains quite the same from night to night. This is her need, to foment alteration so that the world will not become frozen in stasis. Changing Moon Caste Lunars feel the need of the Argent Madonna, and through it, find influence over others, changing opinions or inspirations and taking on whatever faces they find useful or interesting.

    Within their day to day lives and long-term goals, the Children of the Shifting Moon play others as if they were puzzle games or musical instruments. Unions are consummated or broken up, pending their designs or whims. Peasants can be fomented into an uprising with sudden knowledge of combat seared into their attention through insights powered by their feelings, and rulers convinced to abdicate in favor of following their dreams of industry or beauty. Chaos seems to follow in their wake, but one can never say that it fatigues those it takes hold for very long. Volatile situations release their energy more efficiently under the watch of a Changing Moon, not that most of the caste would use such phrasing. Their dance of intricate dreams and nightmares is, after all, often concerned with efficiency only in presenting the most lovely or terrifying of visions and letting things go from there, or providing a point of hatred that unites the people only to vanish before their eyes, leaving them had.

    Silver Whirlwinds are born from among those heroes who run out the limits of their appeal and talent for interaction. Meeting these limits, the Lunar continues to run through the gauntlet of challenges involved with surviving the treachery of the court by pulling the strings or in convincing uncommunicative monsters with barely any capacity for sociability to engage in or abstain from an activity. Some even find that their poise is hard-pressed to deal with the blind, or some other situation where their natural talents simply don’t seem to apply. Still, they find a way, as life tends to.

    Luna’s Marvels make an individual’s life brighter and grander in every possible way. While they may be less inclined to offer direct protection from snarling hobgoblins, a Changing Moon can inspire those that he takes under his wind, filling a cherished companion with the confidence to think of a way to take on challenges that might be outside the Lunar’s own capacity and, perhaps, surprising him in the approach. If the time comes for parting or vengeance, simply leaving can be terrible enough a consequence, leading the abandoned to know that their life will never be as vibrant or exciting as it was with the Exalt around. Should an unfortunate truly earn a shellacking, however, shacking up with a rival to the former beloved is a sure way to get the point across.

    Within the bounds of a larger group, a Night-face juggles between getting members fired up and imposing bizarre divisions amongst them. While a village might not have the best warriors, or a company gifted with the most adept financial minds, the winds of change can put the right perspective in the right place and time to happen upon knowledge that its discoverer has little idea of the technicalities of. Not that such a situation has ever stopped capitalization on such a phenomenon. In terms of the long war on the pretender Realm, the Changing Moons made the Shogunate jump at shadows, and they continue to do so to the Scarlet Dynasty, fouling up its intelligence network with false positives and well-timed true strikes at the underbelly exposed by the tangles in the All-Seeing Eye’s web.

    Anima Effects: For ten motes, the Lunar may assume the shape of a character that he is familiar with, having spent at least one scene interacting with him. Should the Moonchild spend (target’s Essence) days masquerading in this shape without any visible anima display, this grants the Exalt the subject’s shape permanently. Note that this capability may only be used to assume a shape if the type of being is one that the Lunar could assume the shape of. At the 11+ mote level, the Lunar either assumes the shape of a familiar character or becomes indistinct in shape, defeating any attempt to identify him until his anima dies down.
    Caste Attributes: Pulling at the heart from the first moment of meeting to every subsequent word and gesture, the Changing Moons know well the ways of inspiration, coercion and guises. As such, they favor the Attributes of Charisma, Manipulation and Appearance.
    Associations: The season of summer, the colors violet and yellow, the element of water, the Maiden of Journeys
    Sobriquets: Children of the Shifting Moon, Silver Whirlwinds, Luna’s Marvels, Night-faces, the Tricksters (derogatory)
    Concepts: Well-liked officer, enchanting courtier, rebel leader, village’s sacred beast, eccentric ambassador, alluring musician, mysterious martial artist


    No Moon Caste

    Hidden from the world, Luna’s face is turned to places outside of Creation. It is from these voids that strange secrets pass into her keeping, things often forbidden to the world she protects. So it is that the No Moons peer into the darkness for insights, always ready to learn something new and use it against their enemies, even if the enemy happens to be boredom.

    Children of the Unseen Moon approach all their problems with what they know, and how they can deviate from the established patterns. This affords them as much flexibility as other Lunars in what they can do, contributing calmly measured targeting to acts of war with the same eyes that see the desires of others, and sift through the memories of others with the same minds that conspire to construct wonders thought lost to the world and organizations to use them, including military orders. Where some saw the No Moons as imitations of the Twilight Caste, these Lunars proved them wrong by using their innate gifts to not only consider what a Copper Spider might, but also how to produce and market it. Operating alone, Lunars of this type are still capable of other significant tasks even before taking personal inclinations into account.

    Silver Mists are elevated from among those who have encountered the end of their cleverness’s limits. At the precipice of mental strain, they can only look to concepts that do not fit into their formerly-held preconceptions. Faced with bandits breathing down her neck, the Lunar might begin her existence as one of the Chosen through noting a pattern in which the gang moves without its knowing and stay within its blind spots while cutting them down one by one, adjusting her motions as the composition of the group, and thus the pattern, changes. Letting some measure of concentration for a precise shot lapse into pure feeling, she might gain a sense of time’s passage that makes the world seem to slow to a crawl. Blindfolded, she might make her way out of a cave based purely on scents, sounds and the sensation of air passing over her skin.

    Those who gain the favor of one of Luna’s Portents often find themselves involved with bizarre and elaborate mind games or outright bizarre or even frightening happenstances. Zombies escort the object of interest to and from potentially dangerous locales. Demons end up bound to serve the cherished individual’s commands. A solution to an isolated problem foreseen from long ago lies in wait under the companion’s pillow. Woe unto any who would turn on the giver of such aid, however; all their patterns will come back to crush them into the dirt.

    Amidst collections of personages working together as a whole, the Night-witches are want to meticulously investigate every potential resource in the group. After completing this measure, the organization is likely to end up in a tumultuous shakedown as it ends up being altered more to the No Moon’s feel for who can do what and where. Other societies or groups, who do not share the Exalt’s concern for their well-being, are also potential resources… for consumption. No purview is so outside of a given assemblage’s point of interest that the No Moons couldn’t consider a place for it as an organ of a larger group. In the context of fighting against Terrestrial Hegemony, No Moons purloin material whenever the enemy isn’t paying attention and construct raiding parties in such a manner that few would question their roles as ignorant savages. Under the veil of another form, the Silver Pact as a whole prepares to annihilate the traitors, all thanks to the care of the Night-witches.

    Anima Banner: Seeming to illumine in the darkness in a manner similar to the Abyssal anima, strange forms of energy play within the No Moon anima, passing bent through space to reveal the Silver Mist that radiates them in some facsimile of visible light.
    Anima Effects: In the presence of a corpse no more than a month old, which the No Moon did not kill, she may touch the cadaver and spend one mote to collapse the body into a crimson orb that she absorbs. This grants her the shape of the slain creature as it was in life. She cannot gain the shapes of beings that are closed to her using this method.
    For ten motes, she may obscure herself from the knowledge of others; this acts similarly to the Night anima effect, but targets rolls involving recalling information about her and attempts to discern her thoughts or feelings, rather than to detect her presence. At the 11+ mote level, this effect comes into play automatically, and the Exalt may simply touch an appropriate corpse as a miscellaneous action in order to absorb it at no mote cost.
    While it seems incredibly strange, the No Moons are the priests of Luna. Ever since the passion of the Waxing Moons was lost, only a cold vision has lit the way of divine guidance for the Moonchildren.
    Associations: Calibration, the color black and those colors beyond human sight, the element of air, and the Maiden of Secrets
    Sobriquets: Children of the Unseen Moon, Silver Mists, Luna’s Portents, Night-witches, the Ogres (derogatory)
    Concepts: Studious general, weapons production magnate, shadowy merchant, insurgent strategist, Black Nadir Concordat heir


    Casteless

    Unfixed and indistinct, a face of Luna that has not been decided upon could be anything. In that, it has great potential, but has yet to realize such potential. The Casteless share this state, unrealized in a primary field of expertise and only managing by their personal qualities – if you can call that managing at all.

    Little can be said of the Casteless as a whole; they are all individuals without a common thread of realized potential. They flit about the world, often finding themselves falling in with some power or another – if not the Silver Pact and fellow Lunars, then the Cult of the Illuminated, independent Solars or their darker counterparts, or even the Guild. Individuals and organizations become exceedingly important, as the Wyld Hunt is eager to move in on a target before it can build its strength.

    Anima Banner: By the time of the month, as per the displays fixed Castes.
    Anima Effects: By the time of the month, as per other Castes’ powers
    Associations: Hints and days of every season, the color of crimson, the Autochthonian element of oil, and all manner of alien monsters
    Sobriquets: Children of the Wandering Moon, Silver Shrouds, Luna’s Daydreams, Night-children, the Moon Mad (derogatory)
    Concepts: Mercenary commander, Blessed Isle holdout, Illuminated troublemaker, Beloved of the Dead, Warlock’s Most Trusted Lieutenant


    Backgrounds

    Solar Bond
    Remove the increasing penalty to DV that this Background mentions. Lunars may raise this Background to 5 without the need to use bonus points, paying the same cost for the fourth and fifth dot as for the first three.

    Taboo
    This Background reduces Limit when it is gained. Each level of the Background indicates one occasion where the Limit the Lunar would gain is reduced by one point. This can reduce Limit gain for an event to no points.

    Tattoo Artifact
    Replace this Background in its entirety with the following:
    New Lunar Background:
    Inscribed Aegis

    Carved into Lunars by their fellows, the designs and scarring, first and foremost, secure their Caste, and only show up as a part of anima display. More elaborate rituals can grant the Moonchild some extra measure of power, though each addition typically restricts the Moonchild from some action, such as an inability to use foreign Charms to alter her shape. However, because these advanced measures do bring advantages, they still have some adherents, no matter the drawbacks they endure; just what level of this treatment a Lunar experiences is largely up to her own desires and attitude. Bonuses granted by this Background are cumulative where they do not simply supplant the effects of a lower rating. Casteless Characters cannot start with this Background as a matter of conceptual exclusion.

    X: While not Casteless, the character has only had the minimum amount of stabilizing influences cut into her flesh. Only the most minor moonsilver patterns are apparent on her body, and even then only then when her anima banner shines brightly enough to make hiding impossible.
    •: Noticeable designs on one limb or faint traces on a pair of limbs mark more of the Lunar’s life. This affords the character a greater resistance to Wyld mutation, Fair Folk Shaping attacks and other dangers that normally see the Moonchild gaining dice on account of her Exaltation, raising the number from two to three.
    ••: Marking the chest and midsection noticeably or two limbs extensively, the protective designs also add two to the difficulty of affecting the Lunar with general Shaping effects, such as by the infectious destinies of Sidereal astrology. Mortal astrology and the Autochthonian methodology of probabilistics suffer twice this increase.
    •••: Covering space equal to the arms and body of the character noticeably, or as faint traces across her entire body, the inscribed aegis rises to great prominence with a similarly noticeably drawback. All effects other than those that are natural to Lunar Exalted (including Charms) that would attempt to alter her shape through transformative magic fail to do so. However, this blocks off a great number of potential benefits, such as those sorcery or many supernatural martial arts styles.
    ••••: Covering space over the Lunar’s entire body with extensive patterns, these ward markings also block off transformative Lunar powers that can be used on others. While an errant chimera’s flesh warping will not harm the Moonchild, she cannot also be healed by the ministrations of sane, helpful Lunars. At this level, the tattoos automatically arouse suspicion surrounding the Lunar’s Tell to all observers.
    •••••: Heavily marked across every old wound ever received, every touch of a lover and every sign of an accolade bestowed upon her all at once, it can be difficult to resolve the patterns on the character’s flesh. Nonetheless, the world knows, and as a result, it is kept at a safe distance. Environmental hazards blunt themselves on the power of the markings, granting the Lunar three bonus successes against all such inconveniences. The difficulty to note her Tell decreases by one as well.
    Legendary Inscribed Aegis: At this degree, even the Lunar’s eyes, mouthparts and internal organs end up marked. Such a level of protection is nearly unheard of, and while it decreases the difficulty to note her Tell by one more if not suppressed by magic, such an inscribed aegis affords protection against physical damage, doubling the base natural soak that the character gains from her Stamina.


    The Great Curse
    Replace this section in its entirety with the following:

    Much hinges on the Lunar capacity to change shape. Monstrous shapes have their uses aside the less imposing, the miniscule venturing where the gigantic cannot. Accordingly, when the dying breath of the first Neverborn took root in the Lunar Exalted, the flexibility of the Chosen of the Argent Madonna began to suffer, prompting them to stay in certain shapes even when it was not well-suited to the situation, or to wildly and uncontrollably blink between forms, with an advantage given up simply because. Purpose and whimsy of mood both ended up subverted, with their equally-addled fellow Exalted either blinded by their association with a god of madness or occupied with similar problems, and thus typically quite self-absorbed.
    Resisting unnatural mental influence inflicts Limit on the Lunar Exalted just as it does for the Solars. Denying a Virtue for the sake of an individual toward whom the Lunar holds a positive Intimacy inflicts two points of Limit on the first time in any given scene where the Moonchild deigns to do so; such a suppression of heroism requires no expenditure of Willpower. Triggers related to a given Virtue, which the Great Curse has sunken its fangs into and marked as Flawed, prompt a roll in the same manner as for Solars. Lunars who have not gained the Caste-fixing tattoos increase the Limit gained on any particular episode of rolling by (1 + local degree of separation from Creation).
    Unlike Solars, Lunar Exalted do not immediately gain Willpower back from a Limit Break, and cannot engage in partial control. Instead, for the duration of the episode, the Lunar doubles the return gained from regaining Willpower due to sleep, and may use the Flaw Virtue in lieu of Conviction to make this roll. While in the throes of an episode of Limit Break, there will be some stricture regarding the use of shapeshifting to assume different shapes, often proving disruptive to their effective use. As with Solars, all Limit is eliminated when Limit Break occurs.


    Lunar Virtue Flaws

    Compassion

    Tear of Hearts

    With the weight of so many lives inside, the Moonchild cannot help but feel overwhelmed by them and what they represent. All of the violence, the murder, the duplicity, the simplest of off circumstances that others could typically excuse and shrug off or the simple pressure of anxiety over who he really is begin to crush him, and to that end, he refuses to utilize the fruits of a false heart any further. While suffering through this Flaw, the Lunar may only utilize his true shapes. Under no circumstances will he enter a false form, even if it was acquired by a willing donor or a harmless method. If he has no false shapes, he will flat out refuse to gain any instead.
    Duration: (Compassion) days
    Trigger Condition: The character is confronted with harm, confusion or misunderstandings caused through his use of shapeshifting, no matter how minor.


    Faces of Sorrow

    The world can be immeasurably callous, the rich and fortunate crushing underneath the poor and disadvantaged. When the Moonchild tends to the problem, however, the faceless masses will confront their tormentors, and those cruel folk will know only the likenesses of those they have wronged. While in the throes of this Flaw, the Exalt will seek to acquire and solely assume shapes from those that she has seen suffer at the hands of others. As this in no way inhibits Compassion, most Exalted of this bent will tend toward nonlethal methods to accomplish this, thought the possibility of an already-dying unfortunate offering himself up is one that might occur, and No Moons may use their anima power to great effect if the crime against the disadvantaged was murder. While restricting her shapes in this manner, she will also seek to redress any wrongs against those whose faces she wears made by their oppressors.
    Duration: (Compassion) days
    Trigger Condition: The character sees innocents suffering through no fault of their own, or comes across significant evidence of such an incident.


    Conviction

    Lost Cub Overwhelmed

    With all of the potential of the Lunar Exaltation, one can easily get lost in a sea of information. Which shapes can do what, the most appropriate shape for a situation, and all of the many minute alternatives create a tempest of conflicting thoughts that leaves the Exalt paralyzled with regard to altering her shape unless someone can help to snap them out of the partial fugue state that they are in. Such an individual must be both someone that the Lunar regards with a positive Intimacy and who knows the capabilities of the Exalt well enough to communicate them to her in a manner that she can clearly understand. Given such a partner or selection of partners, she can deal with the chaos of her changing faces; otherwise, she cannot shapeshift out of the form that she was in when the Limit Break occurs.
    Duration: (Conviction) days
    Trigger Condition: The character is placed under severe stress or backed into a situation that she cannot escape from.


    Suited Tiger Dereliction

    Seething inside, the Moonchild sheds notions of other parts of himself. For as long as this Flaw persists, he is human, and specifically, the human he was prior to his Exaltation. No other shapes will be taken other than the Moonchild’s natural human form, without any mutation-based alterations upon it, though he is not restricted with regard to what Charms he can use. Communication with non-human beings suffers a -2 internal penalty for the duration of the Limit Break episode as well.
    Duration: (Conviction) days
    Trigger Condition: When the character is faced with a flaw of his spirit shape or the mortal kin of the beast that provides that shape.


    Temperance

    Thousand Faces Paragon

    While Solars who feel the world to be a wayward den of sin engage in devotions of spiritual purity common to some ideal, the Lunar who suffers from this Flaw fixates on any shape that is not her natural human shape or her war form and concentrates solely on living in that shape as its kin do in the wild. While the Limit Break persists, she cannot assume a different shape, and efforts outside of those tasks related to the daily routine typical of the shape or her Intimacies suffer a -1 die internal penalty.
    Duration: (Temperance) days
    Trigger Condition: Others around the Lunar engage in petty acts, abuse of trusts and other actions that violate Temperance.


    Writhing Squamus Nightmare

    Pure in purpose and Essence both, the Lunar sees a need to continually exercise the array of forms that she has acquired. To that end, when under the influence of this Flaw, she must change into a different shape with each scene, and may not change back into the form that saw her descent into the throes of Limit Break until the episode is over. Most disturbingly, perhaps, is that the Exalt sees no reason not to engage in life normally, leaving to many awkward moments where her shape makes that normal life anything but.
    Duration: (Temperance) days
    Trigger Condition: The character is offered some form of temptation.


    Valor

    Body-Congealing Anger

    Raging out of control, the Lunar simply forgets about the changing gift, acting with aggressive communication or violence as appropriate to the situation and paying no heed to any notion of changing the form that he was in when the Limit Break occurred. This makes no guarantee of the capability to communicate in ways that others would understand, given the potential limitations of shapes that typically fall into the libraries of the Chosen of the Moon. Coupled with the fact that his behavior will be aggressive and temperamental, penalties are the least of such an Exalt’s worries, as he berates and destroys whatever catches his ire.
    Duration: (Valor) days
    Trigger Condition: The character is belittled or otherwise suffers from impugned dignity.


    Fascinating Prey Chase

    Catching sight of the unique and the stand-out, the Lunar feels an undeniable need to take the shape of such wondrous beings for her own, and then to use it as often as possible while it is still novel. The prey is set at the moment of the Limit Break; lethal means need not be used to acquire the shape, but the Lunar is at a -3 internal penalty to make a comfortable and pleasing presentation of her plans to the target, and the single-minded pursuit that the Exalt applies is likely to be extremely disconcerting. Any shape may be assumed until the moment that the Moonchild acquires the desired form, at which point she must use it and explore every avenue of its use until the Limit Break episode passes.
    Duration: (Valor) days
    Trigger Condition: The character encounters a being with a capacity that relates to the Lunar’s present circumstances and needs.

      Shapeshifting Rules


      Dice Bonus Limits: Do not count spirit shape Attribute values as natural. Excellencies for true forms add only up to the number of dice that they could for a Lunar’s natural human shape, treating any higher value on the part of the spirit shape as a bonus that does not count against, but does not add to, the limit of dice added by Charms.

      Strength and Clinches: Even for fantastically mighty beings, treat Strength above 5 as only 5 for the purposes of targeting clinches.

      Spirit Shape: Any natural animal native to Creation at least large enough for a human to see with the naked eye can be the Lunar’s spirit shape. As a reflection of the Lunar’s life experience or iconic anima, this shape has the same significance as an anima banner manifestation.

      Gaining New Shapes: Lunars do not need to engage in the Sacred Hunt to gain shapes. Without other means of gaining shapes, however, they must kill the target and eat part of it (typically the heart). Additionally, natural animals (including humans) require no Knacks in order to take in their shapes, and stalking a target constantly for (target’s Essence) days can allow the Lunar to take a shape without this measure, so long as the Moonchild manages to stay in the presence of this individual the entire time.

      Anima Banner: Shape lock no longer applies. Lunars may continue to change into false forms at any level of anima banner and are not forced back into true forms because of its radiance, nor do high levels of peripheral Essence use reveal the Lunar’s Tell, though the glare of silver light that the text says would accomplish this end does always make someone suspicious.

      Cost of Assuming Shapes: The cost to change into a form is no longer fixed at one, three or five motes, nor is the Lunar required to pay Willpower to change into a human shape. Cost is instead based on size, or the Essence of a spirit; only the greater of the two applies. It takes only one mote to change into another human shape. For smaller shapes, shrinking down to a form described in the size range of Humble Mouse Shape requires three motes; to one down to the limits of Emerald Grasshopper Form, five motes; anything smaller requires seven motes. Larger forms up to the mid-way description of Towering Beast Form – to a hippo – require five motes, with elephants and tyrant lizards requiring seven motes; anything larger requires ten motes. The war form is slightly unusual, in that it requires motes equal to the Lunar’s Essence in order for a Lunar to transform into it.
