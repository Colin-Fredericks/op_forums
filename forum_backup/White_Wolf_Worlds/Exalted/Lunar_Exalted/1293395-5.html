        Kylina Sele (Final Draft) Once more from the top. This one will be the cat's meow, promise.
        Spirit Shape: Cat/Scarab
        Tell: Silent Movement OOO





        Attributes

        Physical (Primary        ) (3bp spent)

        Strength OOOOO
        Dex OOOOO (Favored)
        Sta OOO

        Social (Secondary)

        Charisma OO
        Manipulation OOOOO (Favored)
        Appearance OOO (Favored)

        Mental (Tertiary)

        Perception OO
        Intelligence OOO
        Wits OOO (Favored)

        Abilities (8bp spent)

        Athletics OO
        Awareness OOO
        Brawl OOOOO (Specialty: Claw)
        Dodge OOOOO (Specialty: Tiny/Miniscule Forms)
        Integrity O
        Larceny OOO
        Linguistics O
        Occult OOO
        Presence OOO
        Socialize OOO (Specialty: Cat Got your Tongue?)
        Stealth OOO (Specialty: Tiny/Miniscule Forms)

        Merits (4 bp spent)

        Artifact (Belt of Shadow Walking) OOO
        Hearthstone (Stone of Resilient Bamboo) OO
        Heart's Blood O
        Stolen Faces O
        Silent Movement OOO (Tell)
        Latent: Underfoot Menace

        Charms

        Excellencies: (Yes, she has Excellencies in all attributes. She is literally omni-capable. No, it wasn't easy, as you'll soon see)

        Strength
        Dexterity
        Stamina
        Charisma
        Manipulation
        Appearance
        Perception
        Intelligence
        Wits

        Universal:

        Quicksilver Second Face: Never be left without your battle form.

        Chimera Soul Expression: Kylina Sele is not merely a thorn in the side of the rich and cruel, she is an agent provocateur and a bringer of change, and so the Scarab, symbol of Death and Rebirth also becomes her. In battle, this gives her Unusual Hide OOOOO (as her skin takes on an almost onyx like hardness) and Mighty Thew O (as her compact form surges with strength). (Burrower OO would be perfect but I can't afford to take Unusual Hide OOOO can I?)

        Stamina:

        Impenetrable Beast Armor/Durant Quicksilver Adaptation: The wonder combination is the basis of Lunar Soak. In this case, we are hoping we don't get hit (9-10 Evasion), but if we do, this should take some of the edge off.

        So something I didn't realize, Soak enhancement isn't actually limited by your Excellency limit, its just that there are rules about how you can apply your Excellency, but it all stacks with Impenetrable Beast Armor.

        So Base 3 + Unusual Hide 5 + Impenetrable Beast Armor 3 (for 3 motes) + Stamina Excellency 4 (for 4 motes) + Resilient Bamboo Hearthstone 2 = 17 Ha! That's almost respectable! And of course you can pick up ISOB for another +6 if you know things are going to get dicey, bringing the total to 23, now that's decent Lunar Soak.

        Note: The Stamina Excellency you don't have to activate until after you see how many successes your opponent rolled.

        Dexterity:

        Agile Beast Defense: Our 1m workhorse will always give us +1 to Evasion and we can apply it after we see what our foe rolls (if it isn't 9, don't bother)

        Bending Before the Storm: Our Onslaught Negator.

        Charisma:

        Argent Songbird Voice: Our omni-applicable success-on-social actions generator.

        Debt of Borrowed Skin: Our altruistic method of stealing other people's faces. A thief with a heart of gold, indeed.

        Perception:

        Perceiving the Hidden World: Not only does this let us see immaterial, as a Cat we auto-detect out to Long Range!

        Spirit Rending Fury: For 1 mote per attack, this lets us attack with impunity with the Belt of Shadow Walking if the enemy can't strike Immaterial.

        Wits:

        Desert Basilisc Diadem: Gives us a handy slot for our Resilient Bamboo Hearthstone (hopefully the ST will allow it to either be Stable or Wyldborn) and is also the pre-req for the always great Radiant Basilic Jewel.

        Intelligence:

        Memory Drinking Meditation: If you ever replace someone and don't want their spouse to suspect, accept no substitutes. Also super good at collecting information on others (just steal the faces of those around them and reap the intel).

        Terrestrial Circle Sorcery: No need to belabour the usefulness here.

        Wood Dragon's Claw: A really key part of our combat - an artifact weapon on demand allows us to attack at a resting Withering Accuracy of 21 (!!!) (Dex 5 + Brawl 5 + Specialty 1 + Ambush Hunter 3 + Accuracy 5 + Stunt 2).

        Combined with Divine Predator Strike and Ferocious Biting Tooth (you'll need to buy these non-favored I'm afraid, but still not overly onerous), you are basically always adding 3 dice to your post-soak damage (you'll get 2 10s on 21 dice most of the time, statistically) and your overwhelming is going to be at least 4 so that's a 7 dice overwhelming damage even if their soak eats everything, which is nice.

        Appearance:

        Subtle Silver Declaration / Moons Hidden Face: Since our Mutations are now obvious, these are necessary investments to not reveal ourselves. Generally useful too for an infiltrator.

        So here are some numbers: (Not including stunting +2 dice)

        Withering Attack (Wood Dragon's Claw): 19 (+7 for 7m) / 15 damage (+5 for 5m)
        Decisive Attack (Wood Dragon's Claw): 14 (+7 for 7m)
        Soak (As Cat): 10 (+3 for 3m, +4 for 4m)
        Evasion: 8 (+1 for 1m, +2 for 4m)
        Parry: 6 (+1 for 1m, +4 for 8m)
        Guile: 6 (+5 for 10m)
        Resolve: 2 (+4 for 8m)

        Phew, I think thats mostly done, the obvious things to expand into with XP are the following:

        - Pick up Divine Predator Strike and Ferocious Biting Tooth, for low accuracy characters, these are white elephants, but for you the skys the limit. You will be rolling at least 2 10s and possibly/probably more on a regular basis with a resting accuracy of 21 (!!). That really boosts your post-soak damage.

        - Pick up ISOB, your emergency Soak puts you on par with legit Lunar Soakbeasts.

        - Pick up Radiant Basilisc Jewel - Your Free Greater Hearthstone awaits, lots of choices here.

        - Max your Appearance, why not? Just melt hearts wherever you go.

        I leave it to you, valued reader and fellow sojourner on this particular character's drafting to decide whether the character accomplishes what it sets out to.

        For me, as Death Kitty is, I am content.

            Here's another go at it.

            Death Kitty Revision

            Totem: Cat/Scarab

            Tell: Enhanced Sense (Smell)
            In the Ask the Devs thread, it was clarified that mutations can qualify you for Protean benefits as long as you aren’t in base form. This is great for a lot of Perception charms.

            Physical [Secondary] (3 bp spent):
            Strength 3
            Dexterity 5
            Stamina 3
            Social [Primary]:
            Appearance 2
            Charisma 5
            Manipulation 5
            Mental [Tertiary]
            Intelligence 3
            Perception 3 (Favoured)
            Wits 2

            Merits:
            Enhanced Sense (Smell) 3
            Artifact 3 (Moonsilver Chainshirt) 3
            Hearthstone (Stone of Resilient Bamboo) 2
            Heart’s Blood 1
            Stolen Faces 1
            Resources 1 (1 bp)

            Bonus Points:
            Dexterity 4-5 (3 points)
            Dodge 3-5 (4 points)
            Brawl 3-5 (4 points)
            Latent Ability (Underfoot Menace) 3
            Merit (Resources 1) 1

            Relevant Abilities:
            Brawl 5 (Claws)
            Dodge 5 (Tiny/Miniscule Forms)
            Stealth 3 (Tiny/Miniscule Forms)

            Charms:
            Excellencies:
            All of them

            Universal:
            Quicksilver Second Face
            Chimera-Soul Expression (Unusual Hide 5, Antennae)

            Appearance:
            Subtle Silver Declaration
            Moon’s Hidden Face

            Strength:
            Ferocious Biting Tooth
            Divine Predator’s Strike

            Stamina:
            Impenetrable Beast Armor
            Durant Quicksilver Adaptation

            Dexterity:
            Agile Beast Defense
            Bending Before the Storm

            Intelligence:
            Inevitable Genius Insight
            Memory-Drinking Meditation
            Terrestrial Circle Sorcery (Wood Dragon’s Claw)

            Wits:
            Squirrel’s Cunning Technique
            Magpie’s Nest Resourcefulness

            Relevant Numbers (Stunts not included):
            Withering Attack: 19 dice (+7 for 7m), 13 damage (+4 for 4m), Overwhelming 8ish
            Decisive Attack: 14 dice (+7 for 7m)
            Soak: 3 Stamina + 5 Armor + 5 Unusual Hide + 2 Hearthstone + 4 excellency = 19 soak

            While Impenetrable Beast Armor does add to soak, it has to be applied before the attack is rolled. Given how often attacks will miss, and that a great strength of Death Kitty is that she can increase her defense after she is hit until the attack misses, this is inefficient.
            5m is gained per turn, and if 2m is spent on FBT, 2m on DPS, and 1m on Agile Beast Defense, then Death Kitty is mote neutral.
            She will most likely hit with a withering attack and successful dodge at least one attack on her so she will gain about (8 overwhelming dice/2) + 1i (Agile Beast Defense)= 5i per round.

            Suggested Buys:
            Post-hit Defense:
            • Invulnerable Skin of Bronze

            Raise your soak by 6, your hardness to 6, and gain a disarming counterattack when hit.
            • Immovable Aurochs Defense

            Have you ever tried to move a cat?
            • Ox-Body Technique

            Taking health damage is inevitable.

            Mobility:
            • Graceful Crane Stance

            This might be necessary if you’re running around while bronze.
            • Instinct-Driven Beast Movement

            This is a prereq for some Dexterity subterfuge charms and also awards initiative.
            • Shifting Octopus Trick

            This is a solid answer for being grappled.
            Defense:
            • Fleeting Gazelle Exercise

            Not only does this raise your Evasion for the rest of the scene, it also provides initiative.
            Cunning Prey Reversal
            It raises Evasion after you’ve been hit and provides initiative.
            Flowing Body Evasion
            Technically the previous two charms have brought you to the dice cap, and this charm has to be declared before the attack roll. It’s still a good answer to uncountable damage and a way to mitigate decisive damage.

            Attack
            • Sinuous Striking Grace

            Lunar Defense charms often rely on your attacker having lower initiative than you, so lower theirs before they can attack you.
            Subterfuge:
            • Many

            With both Tiny size and the Ambush Hunter ability, Death Kitty gains protean benefits from most charms in this tree. The craziest one is probably Shadow Talon Execution letting you Double 9s on a decisive attack roll.

                                            So a small coda on Death Kitty as I like to keep all my noodling in one place.

                                            - Was going to make a full moon version of Death Kitty (Black Tom) but realized it’s not enough changes for me to make another post, just:

                                            1.)Avail yourself of all possible Soak Options (Sta 5 + Unusual Hide 5 + Artifact Light 5 + ISOB 6 + Bamboo Hearthstone 2) There, 23 Soak, no motes spent, no enemy can do more than overwhelming to you now.

                                            2.) Max Stamina for above if you want, or don’t, you still have 21 resting Soak.

                                            3. Definitely invest in Divine Predator Strike. I know I’ve said it’s a gambling charm before, but with a 21 dice attack pool, you are going to roll at least one 10. Congrats, the Charm has paid for itself. More than likely you are going to roll 2-3 10s and potentially up to 4 10s which is massive value for the cost and a great way to boost damage.

                                            4. Black Tom should keep the tail mutation and ambidextrous like I proposed. Then you totally get to Style on enemies with Weapon Snatching Coils.

                                            5. Sneaky Edit: It’s been pointed out to me on Discord by Drifting Soul that wearing a Frost Thorn Knuckle and merging with it with Last Warrior’s Unity is also great value as it gives you a Thrown Weapon that Death Kitty can use out to Long Range without an Aim action (With Boulders as Pebbles Attitude) and Death Kitty is super accurate with it as, guess what, it also benefits from the 3 extra dice of Ambush Hunter. You can conceal the knuckles while in Stealth Kitty mode with Squirrel’s Cunning Technique and nothing short of an Exalt with charms is going to see it.

                                            You probably can give up on the cute trick of Omni Excellency with this, but boy howdy is it a scary combatant.
