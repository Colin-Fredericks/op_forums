                            My desire for something like the suggested Five Faces of Luna isn't simply a mechanical one. Some of us Lunar-centric folks are NEVER going to love the core take on Lunars as in-opposition-to Exalts. The tribal stuff gets equally tiresome, and primal howls in their souls plays right into the Lunars-as-savages that seems the inescapable fate of Lunars from a setting-point perspective. Fine, do that with your general design stuff, but give us dissenters some relics from the more civilized times, because some of us really DON'T want to have anything to do with the Ma-ha-suchi model of Lunar.

                            In seven years playing Exalted, leaving aside persona-details, I can tell you that over ninety percent of actual Lunar PCs I've been Storyteller for (admittedly only among fourteen people, fifteen including myself) have had something in common. Prey's Skin Disguise, Flickering Star Infusion, and Evading the Spider's Eye. Why? Sidereal-repellent, so one could actually play a Lunar somewhere besides the wilderness of the deep (insert Direction). Why? Because mixed groups that are primarily Solar PCs don't want to be focused at the edges of Creation, or in some specific territory to the exclusion of widely traveling.

                            Don't mistake me, I love the concept of the Lunar Dominions. I've been saying their like should've existed since 1E. There's just always been this Lunars = Wilderness calculation, when in my experience, many of the people most interested in Lunars AREN'T interested in wilderness. I am NOT saying the focus of the splat should be altered to tastes of what's likely a minority, but I believe a few nods to those of us not interested in Primal Scream Lunars won't kill things for others.

                            Relics of the civilized past don't threaten the present of Lunar focus. They can exist side by side with the contemporary elements without threatening them, and at the same time, give those of us who want to roleplay more urban, civilized Lunars something to anchor characters to.

                                Originally posted by Wyldwraith View Post
                                Eh I disagree with the fact that no general culture means no native Style.
                                I certainly disagree with any idea that it means one couldn't or shouldn't have chosen/developed Martial Arts Styles that would be particularly evocative of the character archetypes that people buy the Lunars book to play.

                                People want to play Sun Wukong? Give them Monkey Style. But that's not happening for some reason.

                                People want to play Conan and other Hulking Barbarian Warriors? Or at least provide their Dragon-Blooded games with antagonists straight out of Warcraft's Horde of Shaman Orcs and Shapeshifting Tauren Druids? Give Lunars one of those rare, rare Martial Arts Styles that uses two-handed heavy-weight weapons, especially axes and hammers.

                                People want to play the Drunken Oni? Well, we might be getting Seven Drunken Gods Style.

                                People want to play a former slave who escaped or rose up against her captors? We will be getting Swaying Grass Style, and while Blanka's connection to capoeira has always been thin, someone out there might have always wanted to play an Elena who can turn into animals. And we can't forget Magi's Morgana.

                                I suppose Centipede Style is for assassins, if the Single Point Shining into the Void, Tiger, Snake, Ebon Shadow, Black Claw, White Veil, and Dreaming Pearl Courtesan Styles didn't already cover some particular flavor of assassin. (Air Dragon, too, and to some extent Wood Dragon.)


                                Other than the fully-official debut of Swaying Grass and Centipede Styles, and some "new but not entirely" Style that might be Seven Drunken Gods (as the descendant of the defunct Orgiastic Fugitive Style), we know there's going to be one totally new animal-based Style.

                                Maybe it's Wolf Style, that emulates the ripping teeth of a wolf pack with throwing hatchets?

                                Maybe it's Tyrant Lizard Style, that uses a two-handed axe or hammer to emulate the weighty head and horrifying jaws of the Tyrant Lizard while maybe incorporating kicks and stomps as the Tyrant Lizard does with its talons and tail?

                                Maybe it's Bear or Elephant or Rhinoceros Style, or whatever animal it takes to use a weapon more weighty and also more widespread than hook swords and seven-section staves.

                                Whatever it is, it will be at least somewhat compatible with Centipede Style. Which, in Second Edition, used... hook swords and seven-section staves. The later of which I could see, but the former of which made no fucking sense.

                                But hey, I don't see why Snake Style uses hook swords instead of spears, either. I mean, the Style doesn't emphasize or support disarming gambits -- it would rather paralyze your arm than go directly for your weapon. Also, an awkward hook cannot possibly be as natural to the Style's extreme focus on extremely fast, accurate, soak-piercing, jabbing attacks. A spear would also fit with "striking from outside of your opponent's reach", which is what Uncoiling Serpent Prana does. Javelins and spears could be thrown, and the Style distinct encourages an investment in Dodge (and thus Disengaging) through Serpentine Evasion and the Terrestrial version of Crippling Pressure-Point Strike.

                                formerly Tornado Wolf, formerly Inugami
