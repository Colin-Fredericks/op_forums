    3E: Any hints about Lunar shapechanging/warforms?

    08-10-2014, 06:19 AM
    I love lunars. Next to Alchemical's they're the most likely critter I play and if we're doing an Alchemical's campaign, I often say: hey, can I reskin a lunar as an Alchemical? Sadly, the mechanics do not always aid me in my love for the happy homicidal shapeshifters.

    So Has there been any hints, any comments from the developers before they pass out after a binge of drinking in how lunar shapechanging and warforms will be handled in 3E? Even the smallest spoiler? WE are legion. We demand it. We also have fleas, so we're a tad annoyed right now.

      Originally posted by NewK View Post
      So Has there been any hints, any comments from the developers before they pass out after a binge of drinking in how lunar shapechanging and warforms will be handled in 3E? Even the smallest spoiler? WE are legion. We demand it. We also have fleas, so we're a tad annoyed right now.
      Well, there are some things we know.
      For one, there'll be Charms that give you new ways to aquire a new form, beyond drinking a person's heartblood (an given example was sleeping with them).
      With the new combat system, different animals will have different moves and abilities that a human does not have. A tiger can gut a man with a claw-swipe. When a Lunar changes into the shape of an animal, they gain the abilities and moves of those animals.
      DBT will remain, but it will also be possible to make a Lunar who 'grows out' of that ability, and instead uses charms that only work when he is in his human form.
      Chimera-ism is less about being a Shoggoth this time around and instead about giving you interesting animal-hybrid-monsters as an option for your character.
      Shapeshifting in general is more about mythical shapeshifters and tricksters than about being the T-1000 or Mr. Fantastic.

      That's what I could find right now.
