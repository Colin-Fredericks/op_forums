        Still waiting for an answer.

        Solars and Dragon-Blooded get to favor the cost of buying Occult dots, sorcerous initiation Charms, and Spells.

        Dragon-Blooded need four prerequisite Occult Charms before they can buy the only sorcerous initiation Charm available to them: Terrestrial Circle Sorcery. They can only learn one shaping ritual.

        A Solar can learn Terrestrial Circle Sorcery immediately, with no prerequisites. When an Essence 3 Dragon-Blood is trying to squeeze as much power as possible out of Terrestrial Circle Spells, the Solar is casting magic on an entirely different scale with Celestial Circle Sorcery, and fueling those spells with a second shaping ritual. When a Solar reaches Essence 5, the Dragon-Blood has absolutely nothing which can compare to Solar Circle Sorcery fueled by three shaping rituals.

        There might not be a Solar Charm exactly like Five Winds Raiment, but Twilight Caste Solars have something similar as an anima power. Solars can also use Spirit-Draining Mandala to fuel shaping costs, and Lore's Heaven-Turning Calculations to boost the effects of spells.

        If the Lunar Exalted work like the Dragon-Blooded (save for being incapable of favoring Sorcery Initiations or Spell purchase costs), but on a higher level, then when they're Essence 5 and trying to squeeze as much utility out Terrestrial and Celestial Circle Spells as possible, the Solar Exalted are changing the face of the world within a matter of hours using Solar Circle Spells.

        It doesn't matter how easily I can summon Octavian on the night of the new moon if the Solar summoned Ligier during the last Calibration. My Incomparable Body Arsenal won't protect me from a Death Ray.
