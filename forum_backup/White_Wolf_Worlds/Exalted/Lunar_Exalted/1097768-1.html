                                I would personally not have any issue with a Lunar Charm like this having all of the copies being equipped in the same manner, and also looking like the Lunar.

                                ​I'm all for the shapeshifting, but I think Lunars still need a heavy does of clever trickster and elevated prowess Charms. Not to prove any kind of point about them not being zoo Exalted or anything, just because I think it's a cosmetic that is natural to Lunars. I think a big part of distinguishing Lunars from Solars is going to need to be about how their Charms interact rather than just what they do individually.

                                I mean, we've got more elemental kinds of Dragon Blooded Charms now, but we've still got Charms that are basically just doing a thing while referencing the element metaphorically, but they still play into differences in how the Dragon Blooded function because of the elemental Keywords and the aura mechanic.

                                ​Hell, I would not consider some things that function off of illusions to be out of place, while no more making the Lunars illusionist Exalted than the Solars having Phantom-Conjuring Performance makes them illusionists.

                                ​Back to the cloning, I think their weapons would be evenly distributed regardless of an inability to conjure a new weapon individually, because I don't think the Charms of anybody should be constructed according to that logic. It's the same as how a Solar being able to evoke a magic weapon from their spirit doesn't equate to being able to shape objects in general from sunlight and soul stuff, because the magic doesn't have a reductive element; there isn't an element that you can break down and reorganize to do something different, it's a capability that arises from a singular purpose informed by a narrative and erroneous human perception and categorization.

                                ​So a Lunar trickster power of making a hoard of copies can also copy one's weapon because it's going with a narrative of making the copies, while they lack a narrative of evoking a weapon from their spirits like that, and there's no contradiction because these things aren't connected in the first place.

                                ​(I'm not saying that this would be terminology that would be recognised or used in character, I'm just using it as a basis to describe how one might write magic here, and how it tends to end up being written almost everywhere)

                                ​I don't know if this answers any specific questions that have been asked, but it's my perspective on a broad read of this subject. I hope that it's useful, or at least relevant.

                                    I certainly agree with you Isator. A couple of things to note:
                                    Originally posted by Isator Levi
                                    ​I'm all for the shapeshifting, but I think Lunars still need a heavy does of clever trickster and elevated prowess Charms.
                                    The 2nd ed Lunar charmset was quite dull; very Silver Solar (though with a few particular charms that players enjoyed as being more distinct). My Lunar was very shapeshifting-focused, because it was more fun than their actual charms; but I wouldn't want everything they had to be about shapeshifting. For one thing, there's a limit to what you can do with shapeshifting (I found, for example, that once I could turn into a tyrant lizard, it seemed a waste of time to get more combat shapeshifting), but also, you just want to be able to do more than that.
                                    Shapeshifting is good for some stuff, but not so much for others. The other Lunar in the game (and this was a pretty high essence game, so we had about 40+ charms each) was a social Lunar; shapeshifting was certainly not the solution to everything that character wanted to do. Her player had a lot of fun using the charms that inflicted lust or fear on people, or bound them to their social unit/turned them against it.
                                    So yeah, clever trickster charms.
                                    Elevated prowess is fine too; I think the issue was that half (well, more than half) of the Lunars charms in 2nd ed were that. I don't think they should be entirely eliminated, or all forced to become shapeshifting charms as if Lunars can't just be really charismatic or strong or whatever; they just need to be a supporting part of the Lunar charmset, rather than the bulk of it.
