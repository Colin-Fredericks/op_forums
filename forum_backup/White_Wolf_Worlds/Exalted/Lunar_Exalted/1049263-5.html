                ​Fine, for the sake of closing all of the possible loopholes against which some "Um, actually"s might slip through:

                ​Martial Arts are not Exalted Ability Charms, and exist in the setting as codified magical extrapolations from martial techniques, largely irrespective of the kind of Exalted (and other beings) who are using them. They use the Martial Arts Ability, which functions differently from every other Ability in that you need to buy a special Merit to even use it, it needs to be bought separately for every Martial Art, and Exalted who use Ability Charms cannot develop their own Martial Arts Charms (with the exception of Excellencies, which are barely Charms). Hence, the difference between being Attribute and Ability Exalted should not be relevant for learning the Martial Arts.

                                Originally posted by Sunder the Gold View Post
                                Anyone else want to discuss the possibility that Lunar Exalted might get the ability to combo their native Charms freely with Martial Arts Charms? And the possible balance-point that Lunar Exalted might be stuck at the Terrestrial-level of Martial Arts Charms?

                                I think that would be really neat. All Martial Arts traits are Abilities, and Lunar Exalted don't learn magic based on Abilities. Being able to learn Martial Arts Charms at all seems like a stretch for them.

                                But being able to combo those Charms with their native Attribute Charms would really help represent the archetype of "unskilled, but so strong it doesn't matter".

                                I think that thematic point already going to be covered by the fact that Lunars don't get favored abilities. This means raising ability scores is going to be hideously expensive in character generation (4 points just to max out a single ability! that's more than a quarter of your total BPs for a single ability!), and both hideously expensive and time-consuming out of it (five weeks to get to ability 5 alone - if you decide to go for a second MA, your campaign might be over by the time you'd train up just the ability, nevermind picking Charms to go with it). You will also have to pay more for the Charms themselves.

                                I think the cumulative effect of this will be in typical play (ie. outside of heavy min-maxing environments) that most Lunars will fulfill the "unskilled but so strong it doesn't matter" niche simply by not having as many MA Charms (and sometimes even MA dots) as an equivalent-xp ability exalt. (I, at least, would certainly not bother with getting MA 5 on anyone but a dedicated martial artist if I was playing a Lunar - consequently, this would lock me out of about half of every tree in the game because grumble grumble stupid overinflated ability prerequisites grumble.)
