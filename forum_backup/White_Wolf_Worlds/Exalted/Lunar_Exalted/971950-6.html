                                                Thinking about this topic lately, I've taken a liking to a different approach.

                                                Solars are the Big Damn Heroes.

                                                Sidereals are the seers and powers behind the throne.

                                                Lunars are the intrinsic opposition, for both heroes and villains. They're the monster that stalks the woods, but they're also the beast who terrorizes the Wyld. They're the threat that stimulates growth and preparation, and the outsider who visits but never quite belongs. They wander, bringing trouble to hubris and ruin to vanity and war to the passive. Sometimes they're the Robin who's got Batman's back, and sometimes the Joker who makes Bats what he has to be.

                                                Ask a young Lunar, and he will explain that the Lunar Exaltation comes to those who guard their own truths and challenge orthodoxies.

                                                Ask an elder, and she will laugh and explain Moonsilver Philosophy: embody the Orichalcum of Excellence in the face of complacency; rend the wicked with the Soulsteel of Endings; choose your own destiny with Starmetal of your own will; burn away all that is false with the Vitriol of rage; carve the love of Creation in grand monuments of Jade.

                                                Even in the First Age, the Lunars first warned of the dangerous excesses and unchecked tyrannies. At the Usurpation, they howled in rage and tried to defend what was left.

                                                In the Age of Sorrows, some believe that the Lunar Anathema make unholy bargains with the Raksha. They are wrong. It is the Raksha who must (ever so elegantly) bow and beg bargains with the Lunars, for Lunar will challenges even the mightiest stories of the Unshaped.
