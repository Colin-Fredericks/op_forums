    Ma-Ha-Suchi's new version is actually interesting. I know I should have, but I kinda didn't expect that.

    His old portrayal, while it certainly has it's place, was so over-the-top cartoonish that I never managed to get any use out of him, that wasn't "generic lunar warlord, but worse and ancient".
    Everything that I found even remotely interesting (even if only because of how shocking all of it came together) about old Ma-Ha-Suchi is easy to transfer to any generic new NPC. I think nothing at all was lost and a lot was gained with this awesome version.

    I haven't read Raksi myself yet, but I really hope I get the same thing out of her. I had her as a villain in two games, but people just couldn't care about anything about her once the baby-eating was known about her.

                        Raksi is absolutely a terrible person, but she's also a better character for having coherent and meaningful motivations, ideology and engaging in methods of pursuing them, as well as a political presence that can range beyond her. She can have a greater variety of impacts in games that way.

                        I wouldn't look on serving babies as an affectation to offend as a better motive, or even a deeper one, but as a more Lunar operation (in the sense of that manner and tone of manipulation).

                        That being said, part of my point before is that the framing allows the babies to be one choice among many, adjusted to suit exactly how far one wants her to go.

                        Two other considerations:

                        If she primarily does it to unwanted guests, they don't necessarily actually eat the baby.

                        If the net result is that fewer of those meet her, then fewer such outrages might occur.

                        These can at least be rationalisations employed by Lunars who get the red carpet rolled out.

                        I have approximate knowledge of many things.

                                                            I have to say, I liked Ma-Ha-Suchi more than Rakshi, but I'm not sure why.
                                                            Maybe it's just because he's a sympathetic character and she's not...
                                                            Rakshi is actually perfect for her role in the setting (the baby-eating monster to show that the Wyld Hunt isn't entirely wrong). But somehow I didn't find her compelling.
                                                            I didn't really dislike her write-up, but nor was I enthusiastic about it. I guess I liked it more than Leviathan, but less than Ma-Ha-Suchi and the other three (who I won't discuss yet as Isator hasn't got that far yet).
                                                            Perhaps it's because Rakshi is 1st/2nd edition Rakshi but written better, whereas Ma-Ha-Suchi is essentially a whole new character who just looks like and has the same name as an old character.

                                                            I also didn't find her ideology compelling as a Shan-Ya. I mean, I can see why a sorcerer would become her adherant, or a Lunar who wants her protection. But a Lunar who believes in her anarchist vision of independent Lunars isn't likely to want to serve another Lunar.
                                                            I mean, I can see why more Lunars agreed with her than Ma-Ha-Suchi. But not that they'd want to become her adherants, compared to Lunars who believe in Lunar unity becoming adherants of Ma-Ha-Suchi.
