    Oh don't get me wrong, I can fully support the ideas presented and embrace them as the step sideways and then two forward that is the progression of the lunar archetype as presented in this image, that is fully the ideal.

    I'm just not certain about using it as a cover image. You see I look at it and think that by the edge trimming needed to use it as a cover image it's going to lose the proper framing.
