                                        Too many Charms were devoted to doing stuff and making things in the Elsewhere Den which would never matter in Creation itself. There's no reason for having a Wyld-Shaping Technique in your Elsewhere Den because you couldn't take anything out of the Den.

                                        At most, you would want a more streamlined Charm that would let you create any sort of workshop for crafting with the raw materials you brought in, or any sort of medical facility for healing yourself or someone else you dragged in. So, something more like Craftsman Needs No Tools and Contagion-Curing Touch than Wyld-Shaping Technique.

                                        Giving a Lunar the ability to create a whole other, self-contained world apart from Creation that could never interact with Creation and which ceased to exist when the Lunar left it and within which the Lunar could not be affected or dragged out... it just sort of encouraged Lunar Exalted to leave Creation and never come back. And the Lunar Exalted already suffered from being relegated to the fringes of the setting and forgotten.

                                                            Originally posted by TheCountAlucard View Post
                                                            That reminds me... I wonder how set Lunars are for being able to carry stuff Elsewhere. Seems pretty handy for shapeshifting.
                                                            This is purely a feature of our Aeon Exalted setting hack, and entirely in absence of any manuscript.

                                                            But!

                                                            Our Lunars will have a Lair, inspired by but not directly lifted from Beast. The Lair can connect to any place where the Lunar has a territory, including places in other Aeons. (Aeons being a pocket realm, such as the dead corpus of a long ago nation or religion.) The Lair is attached directly as a feature of the Exaltation, and there are some story elements for Lunars going on experimenting with and building through them. Inside the Lair, all the Lunar's shapes are present, as is their panoply. So, they have a hideout, a thematically intrinsic way to travel around, and built-in storage equivalent to Elsewhere.

                                                            Whether this will require some Charms or be completely intrinsic from the start is yet to be determined - in our Chronicle we're still doing the initial set of reveals about the existence of Aeons (*) and dropping a few clues about how they work. By the time we have the Lunars manuscripts in our eager paws, my players will know pretty well what they're dealing with. In the meantime, the NPC Lunars have an unexplained talent for vanishing entirely out of Creation and popping up somewhere else in a matter of moments.

                                                            Presented purely to share the idea!


                                                            (*) Coming Saturday's session will have us going to our first semi-stable Aeon. I'm looking forward to sending the Solars into the dead husk of the Empire of Xaarr.
