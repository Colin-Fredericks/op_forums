                    07-23-2018, 12:31 AM
                    Undaunted Behemoth Avatar is how Essence 4 Lunars avoid the per-turn costs of Towering Beast Shape. Mountainous Spirit Expression is how you make smaller forms bigger rather than assuming naturally large forms.

                    Steel Paw Style makes a triumphant return from the pits of First Edition as the much-anticipated Stamina Charm that lets you parry with Stamina instead of Dexterity, when using medium, heavy, or natural weapons.

                    Invulnerable Moonsilver Carapace returns. Effectively a suit of heavy armor, with the usual -2 Mobility, but its soak cannot be reduced below your Stamina. It can also fuse with armor you're already wearing, freeing up motes attuned to it.

                    There's a Stamina Charm to increase your soak after you get hit but before damage is rolled.

                    A sorcerer can trap sorcerous motes in her anima, allowing her to stop shaping, do something else, and pick it back up again. Another Charm allows your shadow to continue shaping and casting a spell while you do anything else besides attacking.

                    A Lunar can cast possessing spirits out into nearby animals, or force spirits to respect her.

                    Creation-Spanning Passion allows you and someone else, with mutual Defining Intimacies, dream about each other every night to learn about each other's conditions/situations.

                    Lunar crafters can gain crafting points by healing people (yay, I anticipated this), navigating, using a tool you made to contest a fact, or eating another crafter.

                    Lunar crafters can enjoy a full free Excellency by including parts of a creature she killed.

                    Wits Animal Ken Charms have the "ride or be ridden" Charms. You can share your tattoos with your familiar, and turn them into "an Essence fountain".

                    formerly Tornado Wolf, formerly Inugami
