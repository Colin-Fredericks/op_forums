                                                I've always loved that rant of Holden's, but I do feel he's a bit off-base with the Sun-Kings.

                                                There really, really should be a Solar faction. It doesn't have to be all "let's be their second fiddles again, yeah!" or even all that friendly towards the Solars. But the Lunars and Solars are supposed to be complements to each other, and even with the Solars gone for 1500 years, there should still be that bond there. Moreover, the return of the Solars is going to be a really big deal to two groups of Lunars; those who actually remember the Solars, who are going to be the most powerful and ancient of their kind, and those who dedicate much of their lives to warring against the Realm and the Sidereals, who are going to see them as natural allies and resources.

                                                I do take Holden's point that this makes them kinda seem like a less sophisticated Gold Faction, but there really should be a not-inconsiderably-sized group of Lunars who are pushing hard to start aggressively recruiting, training, and allying with Solars. My take on this is that they should be young and hungry with a tiny handful of ancient Elders, with the larger mass of "established" Lunars somewhat leery about sharing power with these new guys.
