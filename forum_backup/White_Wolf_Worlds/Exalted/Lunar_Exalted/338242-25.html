Because we can at least be fairly sure that Lunars might have a Strength Increasing Exercise equivalent that justifies its effect through the Lunar experiencing a sudden and dramatic growth in muscle mass.

                                                            Conversely, no one is really sure about Graceful Crane Stance, so no one has mentioned it.
                                                            I doubt very much that A: Lunars will only have attribute charms, and B: that they'll somehow be silver solar charms again. Solar, Sidereal or Dragon Blooded charms are all expressions of their abilities, yet they are very distinct because they are abilities filtered through the lens of the sun, stars or the five elements, respectively. I fully expect a similar situation with Lunars, their charms will be expressions of attributes, filtered through the lens of the moon. What we know so far about Lunar powers is that they are highly physical and focused inward. Those two things in and of themselves make it very hard for it to just make silver solars, but it also makes it hard to justify all the stage and esoteric powers that people seem to want lunars to have.

                                                            So, while I will certainly encourage you and my own friends who are Lunar fans to be excited about their 3E version, I will caution that the enthusiasm I'm seeing be tempered, just a bit.
