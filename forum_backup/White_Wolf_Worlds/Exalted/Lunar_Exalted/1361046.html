                  Each of the PCs in my game have now met their Lunar bondmate, with wildly varying results. For context, my game takes place in a modern version of Creation.

                  The first Lunar mate to make an appearance was the Betahl's. The Dusk caste pit fighter and her circle were captured and held aboard a Wyld Hunt submarine. Among their interrogators was a mysterious man who helped them escape (seemingly only to keep them out of the Silver Prince's hands when he attacked the sub). They lost track of their saviour in the fight, but as they made their escape, they saw a vast black-and-white shape attacking the Skullstone sub (it's not who you're thinking).

                  Weeks later the group has made it to Whitewall and started new lives. Being Abyssals and also PCs, they started a band (Stiff Competition) solely for the purpose of being 'invited' to play within the city walls. For various reasons, including one of their friends having exalted as a Lunar and being in need of a mentor, they were chasing rumours of Lunars in the region. This led to them meeting a gentleman called Randall Munroe, an aging Whitewall socialite who was really Eighteen Bright Sorrows, Betahl's Lunar mate.

                  He used his influence to get the Syndics to secretly grant them asylum in Whitewall in exchange for helping to defend the city. They signed on with the Whitewall Guardians, who were lead by Commander Sorira Dukor. The commander handled their secret missions (they couldn't openly use their powers, after all) and became something of a mentor-come-father-figure for Betahl. This was a big thing for her, because her own father had been absent during the years she needed him most.

                  It eventually came out that Commander Dukor and the mysterious man from the submarine were also faces used by Eighteen Bright Sorrows. They had also seen his giant emperor penguin form taking on the Skullstone submarine to buy them time to escape (yes, my players knew about Leviathan so the giant black and white shape was a deliberate red herring). Bright Sorrows tends to express different aspects on himself in each of his established identities, even in front of people who know who he is. Betahl tends to think of Dukor as the 'real' him because that's the one she feels closest to.

                  Their history had been particularly fraught prior to Betahl's lifetime. Bright Sorrows was old. Very old. In the First Age he was known as Smile From The Heart, and his Solar spouse was Fist of the First Glory. Fist was an unusual case in that he had near-perfect recall of his past lives. This led to him having a very cavalier attitude towards death. As time went on he stopped caring about his mortal lives, thinking of himself as one continuous identity and discarding his mortal identity every time he reincarnated. The Great Curse blinded him to the toll this was taking on Smile From The Heart, who eventually left him, unable to stand seeing him treat his lives as shells to be discarded. The Fist that Smile had loved had been lost somewhere along the way. Not long after, Fist was killed in the Usurpation, and finally stopped being reborn. In his grief, sorrow, and no small amount of irrational guilt, Smile From The Heart renamed himself Eighteen Bright Sorrows. One bright sorrow for every Solar lover he had mourned.

                  Betahl was the first incarnation of Fist who wasn't .. well, Fist. It was both a relief and a loss for Eighteen Bright Sorrows, who speculated that the personality had finally come unstuck when her exaltation was corrupted.

                  Bright Sorrows was old and worn out, doggedly holding on out of a duty to protect the holy city and Creation in general, but struggling to find joy in life. Betahl has made it her mission to help him find it, and to curb his more self-destructive tendencies (such as his drinking problem). As of now, he's gone teetotal, reconciled with his adult son and his ex-lover (the son's mother), had another kid with her (though they're still not married or even going steady - they're both very old and independent and content to have separate lives for the most part, despite being in love. It's ... complicated). For her part, Betahl has gone from a Dusk caste delinquent youth to an officer, a Dawn caste, and a mother. They've been good for each other, you could say.

                  Well, I'd talk about the others but this post is long enough and it's 2AM here. I'll wrap things up by telling you Eighteen Bright Sorrow's crowning moment in the campaign: stealing Chejop Kejack's chair, and forcing him to abandon a cup of tea.

                  "Measure of Hope is right about everything." - Wise Old Guru

                  Currently running an Exalted 2.5 Abyssals game in a homebrew modern shard because I value neither my time or my sanity, and I'm loving almost every minute of it.

                      Sheherezzan is the daughter of Vizier Hazzan of Fajad, a trusted member of court who has spent his scholarly life tracking down a fabled white flower said to grant wishes found but only able to do so once a century. While her father spent his life in scrolls and books, Sheherezzan was immediately found to have her mother's grace and beauty and started dance lessons at an early age. Considered the most beautiful woman in Fajad, she eventually turned her attentions to the sprawling secretive political world of Fajad's court and quickly grew quite adept at scheming and spying. She learned much from a rich patron, Golden Fan, who runs a series of warehouses and shipping contracts throughout the West. Golden Fan taught Sheherezzan how to better lie, imply, allure, deceive, play mind games and word games. He also began to tutor her in the fundamentals of the Dreaming Pearl Courtesan style, because Fan is actually a Solar Eclipse (not that Shehezzaran knew what that was).

                      The Solar Heroes arrived, and she was deeply upset when her father offered them the comfort of his estate. As the two heroes got wrapped up in the shadowy plots of Fajad and worked to help Hazzan decipher the lost location of the flower, Sheherezzan remained aloof. She didn't care for these interlopers. That changed when one of them, a stuffy and humble exorcist performed a majestic sword dance for the Court which reduced the audience to tears. As an expert dancer herself, Sheherezzan was amazed by the man's ability and read his soul through his performance, almost instantly falling in love with him.

                      As the Hunt for the Ice Flower ramped up, and Sheherezzan fell more in love with the Exorcist, she decided to tell him her secret: she planned to steal the ice flower at the last moment. Her father's research had shown her that its petals, normally used to grant wishes, could be made into a Tattoo of Power. Bearing such a tattoo would allow her to rid Fajad of its corrupting elements and save the life of the Boy Emperor. Ultimately, she failed to sway the Exorcist from his promise to use the Ice Flower to save an elder Lunar, Aethelryd of Alencrom. Defeated but still in love, Sheherezzan made use of her renown and near-universal love in Fajad to help organize the soldiers into formation against a Fae Queen, who desired the flower for herself. After a massive, massive battle, she stood by her loved in victory, but the Fae are a malevolent lot, and the Fae Queen used her last breath to throw a death curse at the heroes, which Sheherazzan tried to protect the Exorcist from with her body. For this, she became a Lunar.

                      Now finally empowered to save her beloved Fajad, she went with Aethelryd to kill the evil spirits which poisoned the Boy Emperor. She stayed in the icy wilds north of fajad to study under Aethelryd and learn how to be a Lunar fighter.

                      As the ST (and without a Lunars pdf, sadly) I just stole the Death Kitty build for her, so she has excellencies in every Attribute and I gave her several utilitarian shapes that Aethelryd helped her hunt: Panther, Hawk, and Shark.

                          Okay, I'm going to try to be less wordy about the other Lunar mates.

                          Mary and Fox were dating before either of them exalted. Fox was a Nexan P.I. and a mortal sorcerer. He got blasted off a roof by an air aspect while pursuing some perps, and exalted as an Abyssal as he hit the ground. Mary was a nurse at a major metropolitan hospital in Nexus. Some time after Fox exalted, Mary's hospital was attacked by a deathknight of the Mask of Winters, who tore through the hospital like a whirlwind, raising zombies she went, before being killed by an unknown assailant on the fifth floor. Mary survived but was traumatised by the event.

                          After the attack, Mary started having black-outs and nightmares. She often had feelings of being followed, and some of her neighbours had reported seeing an indistinct, monstrously large shadowy figure around the building late at night. She called Fox in terror and he stayed overnight to protect her. He set wards and went to sleep. The wards woke him up after midnight and he was alarmed to realise Mary was gone. Rushing out, he saw a shaggy black figure, at least eight feet tall, in the hall near the fire escape. He confronted the creature, a black bear-folk woman, who seemed disoriented, but recognised him, calling him by name and saying that he was "the one who helped people and got in trouble a lot". She had Mary's eyes. As he processed what this meant, she declared that she "had to go kill a man now", and started lumbering down the fire escape. When he objected, she insisted that the man had been "poisoning children" and she "had to go kill him now". She seemed not to know much beyond that, and insisted that she wasn't Mary, but she was helping her. He narrowly talked her down from eviscerating a drug dealer that night. In the morning Mary had no recollection of the event.

                          Over the subsequent days, he pieced together what happened. Mary exalted as a Lunar in the hospital attack and went hunting the deathknight. She was forced to put down the zombies of many people she knew, including colleagues, friends, and patients. She had only killed two people apart from zombies that night: the deathknight, and a student nurse called Sammy. In the heat of battle, Mary had accidentally swiped her as she came around the corner. Mary had suppressed her memories of the event and forgotten she was a Lunar, unconsciously disavowing what she had done. But power isn't cast aside that easily. Mary did a lot of community work with disadvantaged youth. She knew of many people in the drug trade who had escaped prosecution, and saw the ruinous affect their products were having on her kids. For years her blood had quietly boiled over the fact that nothing more could be done. After her exaltation, that helpless rage took the form of her alter-ego, who would awake each night with another name, description, and likely location, and a simplistic drive to get rid of the problem.

                          It took a very patient intervention over a period of days to get Mary to remember being a Lunar, and her night-time alter-ego to remember ... anything, really. What finally finished healing her psyche was when Fox took a bullet for her and almost died. This was the event that redeemed him, sending a Solar anima flaring into the sky. In that moment, she needed her bear's ferocity to take down their assailant and and her human cunning to hide Fox from the authorities, so her fractured selves were forced to reconcile in order to save him.

                          Mary and Fox's First Age incarnations were married, but for her that was several lives ago, so she doesn't feel as strong a connection to that time as he does, but doesn't really feel the lack. They're still lovers and soulmates. Her most recent incarnation was Sigrid, a northern warrior woman with a polar bear spirit shape, who was a White Reaper master. Some time ago, Sigrid had contacted Fox with sorcery, saying she was eager to meet him and judge his worth as a hero, but she had never arrived, so realising that she now lived on in Mary was bittersweet. Despite her own proclivities for healing and nurturing, Mary has inherited a lot of Sigrid's warrior spirit and martial prowess, including a tendency to berserk when facing unnatural foes. She's now also a sorceress, specialising in elemental summoning.

                          That was not less wordy at all. Why am I like this?

                                  My Zenith has five ~( ･ัω･ั)~

                                  Hōken Kōtaishi: The Crown Prince of clan Shūten, as he's quick to remind people, the Shūten was one of the many warring clans in the Shogunate period who resides in Shōsui-kyō, the city takes its name comes from the nearby mountain range where the spring's water is rumored to have rejuvenating property. The city is mysteriously disappeared after the Great Contamination strikes, many strange gates can be seen erected around the area nowaday, bind with great ropes and paper talismans, it seems that they are meant to keep something away.

                                  Fast forward to the present, Theo and Ve Nguyet enters the haunted capital which has been shunned to another dimension and finds that the spring has flooded both the city and the mountain with strange blood-red water, the citizen has turned into monstrous abominations while the aristocrats are now a cabal of man-eating fish people. Hōken, meanwhile, is trapped as a mindless giant carp inside the palace "pond". He got better thanks to Theo, they have to leave the city right after since Ve Nguyet is hungry and there are nothing editable in the dimension. Having nowhere else to go, the prince decides to follow Theo, there is just something about that man but he can't quite put his fingers on it.

                                  Being raised from birth as one of the elite, Hōken is haughty toward those who he perceived as belonging to the lesser social classes. Life on the road with no servants to cater to a crown prince's every need is hard, he's trained to be an expert in painting, plucking the strings, managing the economy,...etc, not gathering woods to light a fire, or skinning a fish.

                                  He is skilled in the naginata battle art, mostly in the Southern branch of Cresent Weaver Style, though he has started to learn bare-hand combat from watching Theo fights. Both his arms and legs are clawed and covered in emerald scales due to his spirit shape being an Eastern dragon.


                                  Hidden Spear: A wandering killer-for-hire, it seems that he used to have a concrete goal of what he wants to achieve in life, a code of conduct that he's quite proud of, along with someone that he loyally served, enough to maybe even committing generous acts in the name of the great good even, but the whole thing either crumpled bit by bit into corruption or exploded spectacularly, and he is left a bitter man with neither a purpose nor honor, now he murders for money to spend in booze and sexual gratification, though sometimes, just for a little short while, the little honorably knight philosophy that he has suppressed for so long comes brushing out like a geyser.

                                  He has killed for the Silver Pact before, though he isn't really interested in all the philosophy, and especially the Pact's Elder for some unknown reasons. Some members believe that he can be persuaded to be an effective blade for the cause, they can sense that there is a fire buried deep within him yearning to be a part of something greater than himself. But unfortunately before any of that can happen, he meets Theo ͡° ͜ʖ ͡°

                                  With a trinket from a No Moon, he follows the source of the dream that has been plaguing his sleep these past few months and reaches the source of that dreadful calling. He doesn't know what is it about this person, but Hidden Spears feels like he has to follow them to the end of Creation if he has to.

                                  The spirit shape of Hidden Spear is a chameleon and his Tell is ringed eyes, his name comes from a moonsilver spear that is fused to his body, he can expand and retract it from any parts of his body, this lead to an orthodox fighting style that is based more on instinct than any rigid techniques.


                                  Kouryu: A dragon who constantly seeks to improve his mastery of the sword through battle and self-reflection. Though a long time ago, he was a much different man, it's pretty much just your typical power-hungry fighter who cut down both devils and saints to satisfy their bloodlust and ego. As he has no interest in the weak and the powerless, his images in some places become that of a hero from old legend as people witness him felling invincible monsters beyond this world and the next.

                                  Fast forward to now, either age or some other reasons have softened his taste for violence, and he wanders the Threshold still challenging the strong, but rarely takes the opponent's life, mad monsters crawling from the land of the dead and fairies are another matter however. The Silver Pact thus tends to point him to various agents who schemes against Creation, since leaving the Dynast general alive is so much less useful than a beheaded behemoth.

                                  Theo found him battling some servants of the Deathlord, as the whole circle ganging on the Abyssal, the Lunar can feel his blood boiling up just like when he's still young, he would have flay off the deathknight's face if the other hasn't stopped him. Kouryu has heard about the bond between the Chosen of the moon and sun, how a meeting between the two can ignite bursts of intense emotions. Having seen his own loss of control over his emotion, he decides to follow the group to test his resolve and mindfulness.

                                      I started back in first edition and honestly looking back that is nearly 20 years ago now as I got into it when it started. More than half my life has passed since I began and I think there is no surprise but the kinds of characters I play and the Lunar mates they ended up with have changed through the years. My first that I can think back to was a Changing Moon tribal Queen called Crimson Paws to my characters Zenith hundred kingdom's King. Exaltation when their two sides met in battle and recognition of who each other was. If the game had been longer more might have come of it. She was a fox totemed Lunar and they got along well. Surprisingly well given how they met. A little distant, a little aloof, and the sort that always seemed to know something you didn't think they would. The second would have been where I played the Lunar mate in question. Another short lived game but concept wise while he didn't make much effort to try to do anything with his mate he would do absolutely anything and everything for her if it was asked or needed. He was a Changing Moon master of shapechanging roguish sort out of Nexus named Situro. Scam artist sort and a little too civilized for that edition's view on Lunars.

                                      The next is from the game I have been in the longest so far. Anise Stormtamer, a No Moon icewalker NPC. My Solar at the time was a Cathak scion who didn't exalt as a DB but did Exalt while in the Scavenger Lands with Dragonblooded kin who were out exploring old tombs and the like. He was a womaniser and she had lost a prior love to war when he had gone to serve in the Bull of the North's armies. Her thing has been her incredible perceptiveness mixed with some issues about her place as after her exaltation she had relocated from the north to the PC groups base in the east. They had a very rocky and complicated relationship from being very different people from very different worlds and not helped by my Solar having a lot of First Age memories including having her killed in the past. Luckily they were able to get over the issues regarding past lives just in time for him to heroically give his life to stop someone from exploding Greyfalls.

                                      The next would be a full moon Lunar named Mnemon Juriall, an npc Lunar mate to my Dawn. This is from an alternate world setting where Arada gloriously led the Dynasty to victory to destroy the Bull of the North and ended up regent and in charge of the Realm. Before some meddling by different PCs helped Mnemon escape and start her rebellion against him leading to a short lived civil war where she took over. The game he is in is a successor of sort to a prior game. He is an honorable, kind hearted, warrior sort who doesn't really hold to a lot of the values the Dynasty has about themselves compared with regular folk or their divine right to do whatever they want. He and his mate get along fine though as she is a more brash Dawn type who has no probably picking fights or wars for simple glory and for the thrill of it all that has been some friction between them. However he has a high level of bond as it were so goes along with it to help her with what she wants or at least mitigate the damage. As he was beaten to the punch in trying to date her he turned his romantic attentions elsewhere.
