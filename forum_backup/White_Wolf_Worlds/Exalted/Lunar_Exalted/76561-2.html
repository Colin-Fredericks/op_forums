        While I don't see Lunars as being defined entirely by the Wyld Hunt, they certainly have a long and deeply intertwined history in a sense that other Exalted don't. Lunars are the poster-children of Anathema, and if they were as rare as Solars it's likely that the Hunt would have drifted considerably from its original mandate. The constant presence and pressure of the Lunar Exalted, as well as their specific nature and tactics, have done a lot to shape what the Wyld Hunt is, and as the greatest single thread to Lunars over the past 750 years, the Lunars have to some extent been shaped back. None of the other Anathema can claim that sort of relationship.

            Originally posted by Ersatz View Post
            Yes. Shapeshifting is the Lunars thing. Wanting a Lunar who doesn't shapeshift seems (no offence) against the point of wanting a Lunar.
            I don't think any one who wants to play a Lunar actually wants to ignore or avoid shapeshifting, so much as they want to be able to do something cool besides shapeshifting. Or rather, they don't want True Forms shapeshifting and Heart's Blood shapeshifting to be the only tricks they can look forward to using, especially if every Lunar does it because every Lunar needs to do it.

            Basic Lunar shapeshifting is sort of a limited hat. Turn into animals for hiding, traveling or fighting. Turn into a half-beast murder-monster. Wear someone's face. Then what?

            We know the Lunar Charmset is being written with an eye towards making one's Totem an important customization choice; perhaps the Lunar Charmset is also being designed around the idea of making Lunar shapeshifting more exciting.

            For example, wearing someone's face becomes more fun when there are social Charms specialized for using someone else's appearance against the people who knew him -- or know him, if you acquired his Heart's Blood non-lethally.

            Collecting Heart's Blood non-lethally becomes more exciting if that gives you social and perceptual bonuses against the the individual.; having stolen their Heart's Blood, you can more easily understand and manipulate their heart.

            With Charms like that, you can specialize in one area of Lunar shapeshifting while other Lunars specialize in other areas. It introduces depth and variety, and thus customization and personalization of characters.

                    To continue what I was talking about in my last post...

                    Consider the Sidereal Exalted. Their unique trick is Sidereal Astrology. Their other popular avenues of power are Sorcery and Martial Arts; notably, they are the only known Host that can both learn and create Sidereal Styles. The archetypical Sidereal is a sorcerer, martial artist and astrologer. Maybe he's just two of three. Maybe he specializes entirely in one of those fields instead.

                    That right there is a level of variety and customizability. Three different fields of power thematically appropriate to the Host, as opposed to just shapeshifting.

                    But assume that the Sidereal Charmset wasn't so limited as to make Martial Arts and Sorcery overwhelmingly popular options for the Sidereal Host. That makes Sidereal Astrology even more analogous to Lunar shapeshifting. Astrology becomes THE Thing That All Sidereal Exalted Are Expected To Do.

                    Except Sidereal Astrology has always contained a wide variety of tricks. Astrological blessings and curses and Resplendent Destines with various attached perks and powers, all in 25 different colors and directions (ie: the 25 Abilities). The blessings and curses and perks can be applied to social activities, personal activities, societal activities, creative activities, personal combat, large-scale warfare, stealth and espionage...

                    And that's before even looking at the awesome shit that Sidereal Charms can do. Seriously, Neighborhood Relocation Scheme. Ride Charms that turn your pets into gods; gods that serve you. On that note, effects through the whole Charmset geared for making spirits bow to your will.

                    No one complains that Sidereal Astrology is all Sidereals get because it's not the only thing they get. An individual Sidereal really don't need Sidereal Astrology to be cool and fun because his Charms can do that. So his player doesn't feel pressured to invest in Sidereal Astrology... on top of that, Sidereal Astrology is (or can be; should be) extremely cool and fun by itself as well.


                    I wandered far off my point, so let's return to Lunars now.

                    At the very least, the Lunar Charmset needs to be extremely cool and fun, so that Heart's Blood shapeshifting doesn't become overused. Anything cool and fun isn't so cool and fun when it's commonplace, routine and predictable; that's why you can't repeat the same Stunt twice for the same reward!

                    Artifact Evocations are another thing the Lunar Host can have going for them. They can't take Evocations as far as Solars, but they can take it farther than Sidereals.

                    There's also Necromancy; Lunars were getting better at it than Sidereals in the previous Edition. That may well continue into the next. Perhaps each Exalted Host will get its own unique Sorcerous Initiations (Solar, Lunar, Sidereal; etc) to further differentiate between splats. Or maybe Lunars will have the options of the Wyld Initiation and a Necromancy Initiation, unavailable to Sidereals?

                                    Most Dragon Blooded have better things to do with their time than spend years or decades chasing a Lunar from one end of Creation to another. This is especially true when it can take months of time to travel from one place to another. And even more true for "ad hoc" Wyld Hunts (which were traditionally the most common type of Wyld Hunt by far). A group of Dragon Blooded who live around Chiaroscuro aren't going to spend a year chasing an Anathema into the wilds of the North. They have important stuff to do in Chiaroscuro. Even "full time" Wyld Hunts (which are a fairly new thing) can't spend years trying to chase down and track a single Lunar who doesn't want to be caught. They have training, social and spiritual obligations that they need to fulfill, plus of course, other Anathema to go hunt.

                                    The Wyld Hunt won't get called down for just any Anathema. Generally speaking the only time that they will go after an Anathema is when the Anathema is first created (Fate can give them a general idea of when and where someone will be Exalted), so they can have a Wyld Hunt in the nearby vicinity. Once an Exalt has survived the initial Wyld Hunt, they probably won't face another one until they settle down and start building up a power base. That's mainly due to experiences involving "hit and run" Lunars. It's just not worth it to spend important resources going after a single Anathema who isn't doing anything beyond just being an Anathema. It gives a certain amount of safety and leeway to Celestial Exalted, which is useful for player characters.

                                        Originally posted by Crumplepunch View Post
                                        Do you see Solars being defined entirely by the Wyld Hunt in the same way? If not, why not?
                                        Solars are defined by their Absence. They are, for all intents and purposes, only just now returning to Creation. Even if the Wyld Hunt didn't exist, most Solars would still only just now be returning to Creation after a long absence, and the return of so many would still be shaking the foundations of Creation.

                                        The Lunars, in contrast... their entire society exists in the manner it does because of the Wyld Hunt. They are forced to hide because of the Wyld Hunt. They were driven to the edges of Creation because of the Wyld Hunt. They can't open rule cities in the Threshold because of the Wyld Hunt. They are forced to hide their powers when they travel among most civilizations because of the Wyld Hunt.

                                        If the Wyld Hunt didn't exist, Lunars would be vastly different; they would be god-kings ruling over large nation-states and great heroes walking proudly among the people. Instead they are the Monsters of the Forests who must often hide their true nature.
