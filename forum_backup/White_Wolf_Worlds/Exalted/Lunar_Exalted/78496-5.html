                    The story of a Lunar Exalt is the story of a man, who becomes a monster. At first, that monster is in the form of a beast, but then he progresses into truly monstrous forms like the chimera or hydra. But unlike those simple creatures, the Lunar can flee from Herakles by turning into a bird when the fight turns bad.

                    Hmm. If Herakles had a truly Lunar opponent then, perhaps all of the monsters he fought as part of his Labors were really the same Lunar? Rather than killing each one, Herakles only forced the Lunar to retreat to survive and fight another day. Each time, the Lunar tried a new form.

                    The Lunar might also have been the queen of the Amazons that I think Herakles had to serve for one Labor.


                    But as part of this story of the man who becomes a monster, how does a monster-man progress?

                    As the quintessential human heroes, Morke designed the Solars to gain the most competence at the start of a path (Essence 1), with slow progress (Essence 2, Essence 4) that is interspersed with massive spikes of epiphany (Essence 3, Essence 5).

                    But how does a monster-man grow?

                        One of the things I remember hearing people speculate a while ago that I really liked was the idea of Lunars having multiple personae like the Black Beast of the Eastern Marshes or the Wanderer of Forgotten Paths that most people would think were completely separate entities. I believe the idea was an outgrowth of another one about each of the True Forms being a legend in their own right. Whatever the case, the idea that each of the labors was the same Lunar reminded me of that and I like the idea a lot.

                        As for progression, we don't define monsters as much by their own capabilities as much as their danger to us. I think each significant step a Lunar takes (whatever Essence levels are chosen) should amplify and broaden how dangerous a Lunar can be and remove potential impediments that could stand in its way. I'd space that growth a little more evenly to provide more moments where the beast just got a whole lot more dangerous.

                                                    Originally posted by hippokrene View Post
                                                    A simple thought on Lunars:

                                                    Today I thought to myself, "I am going to make a sneaky rat lunar.'

                                                    For a Solar exalted, this would mean I'd need to put points in dexterity, stealth, and larceny, and buy stealth and larceny charms.

                                                    For a Lunar, it means putting points in dexterity, stealth, and larceny, and then realizing there are no dexterity charms that help with sneaking someplace, breaking into places, or opening locks. The best I could find was an appearance charm that might help me escape detection after people are already alert to my PC's presence and looking for her, and some wits charms that might help me pickpocket things.

                                                    I find it odd there are no charms that help you blend into your environment or muffle the noises as they're both very animalish.

                                                    Anyways, I ended up pulling out my copy of Werewolf: The Forsaken, and making a starting Irraka instead.
                                                    Hybrid Body Rearrangement. If you weren't aware of it before, it'll probably be your favorite Knack from now on. Use to manifest an unlimited number of mutations that last indefinitely. Some useful options while sneaking:
                                                    • Chameleon: Active camouflage, bonus to stealth (2e core).
                                                    • Night vision: See in the dark, okay but you can do better (COCD: Wyld).
                                                    • Sonar: The Night Caste thinks he's invisible. Stunt yourself a Predator heartbeat while using this (COTD: West).
                                                    • Shark sight: Identify visible and invisible creatures in a 360 degree radius out to a considerable range. Prevents a lot of unpleasantness. (COTD: West).

                                                    ...And various others. Also use it to enhance natural weapons and armor, grow wings or limbs, walk on ceilings, breathe underwater, gain acid blood or just look freakishly bizarre. There's really no upper limit to the potential usefulness of HBR.

                                                    EDIT: Note the improved errata version.
