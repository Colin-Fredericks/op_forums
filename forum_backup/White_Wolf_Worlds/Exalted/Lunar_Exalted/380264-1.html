                                            So, here's the deal about Lunars and getting other people's Charms.

                                            I dunno if they're gonna get it. It's not something I've talked with the devs about, because none of my preliminary drafts for Lunars involve that sort of stuff. I've got literally as much information as y'all do.

                                            The way that the Eclipse anima now works—a small subset of spirit/exotic Charms that have been explicitly flagged as being okay for PCs to take, as opposed to an all-inclusive list with exceptions—makes me, as a designer, feel about 1000x more comfortable letting non-Solars get access to those effects through appropriate ways. I can feel much more comfortable knowing that it's not going to unbalance anything, and if I need to check interactions, it's with the handful of Charms that have the Eclipse keyword, instead of the vast majority of Charms that have the Native keyword. The ultimate deciding factor is a creative one, whether Lunars should be able to internalize these Charms (I say yes, but my vote's irrelevant). If we do decide they should, actually implementing it would be great facilitated by Eclipse restructuring.

                                                        Originally posted by Robert Vance View Post

                                                        I dunno if they're gonna get it. It's not something I've talked with the devs about, because none of my preliminary drafts for Lunars involve that sort of stuff. I've got literally as much information as y'all do.

                                                        The way that the Eclipse anima now works—a small subset of spirit/exotic Charms that have been explicitly flagged as being okay for PCs to take, as opposed to an all-inclusive list with exceptions—makes me, as a designer, feel about 1000x more comfortable letting non-Solars get access to those effects through appropriate ways. I can feel much more comfortable knowing that it's not going to unbalance anything, and if I need to check interactions, it's with the handful of Charms that have the Eclipse keyword, instead of the vast majority of Charms that have the Native keyword. The ultimate deciding factor is a creative one, whether Lunars should be able to internalize these Charms (I say yes, but my vote's irrelevant).                                                         Makes sense. I also wouldn't take much issue with Lunars being able to learn Charms that have been approved for Eclipses, but "they can learn anything from the shape" feels quite a bit dicier to me. Not least because one issue with the old Eclipse Charmshare, pre-Native, was that every single spirit Charm needed to be written under the assumption that a PC could learn it. This is a problem because it a) makes statting up a spirit a more onerous process than it should be and b) prevents the writing of cool powers that would be A-OK in the limited context of just the spirit, but which could easily do brokenly dangerous things were they to get into PC hands.

                                                        As with Eclipses, this could be worked around by introducing another Keyword to define powers that are okay for Lunars to learn and use in that spirit's shape. However, the viability of a Lunar-OK keyword would kinda depend on whether it already appears in the core book; if not, that ship's probably already sailed. And it's questionable whether it would pop up there, as it would depend on knowing about its mechanical interactions with a splat that hasn't been written yet. Even if it was considered, there's also a question of whether it's too much of a bother, adding an extra "Is it okay for this second splat to have this?" overhead check to the process. Ultimately, I feel creatures and spirits should be written with their own merits as the writer's chief concern; the accessibility of their powers by PCs should be a distant third.*

                                                        * Interactions the written-up individual/creature is liable to have with other things in the setting should naturally come in at a close second.
