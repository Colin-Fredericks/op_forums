                                    Originally posted by TheCountAlucard View Post
                                    No, I'm really not. Your dots don't somehow mean more just because you're the returning heroes of the dawn.
                                    Look, mate, I'm going to try laying this out again.

                                    A Solar's power is excellence; applying his excellence to intellectual pursuits make him smarter than mortal men. That's what Charms of Lore, Investigation, Occult and the like represent -- being smarter than mortal men can be (especially because qualifying for those Charms means you have the requisite dots to be at least as good as a mortal of the same capability). If mortal men could be so smart, they would have those Charms, or those Charms would be merely universal features of the basic system. Either way, the Solar Exalted wouldn't even exist.

                                    A Terrestrial's power is not pure excellence, but still elevates them above humanity in all 25 Abilities, because they are Ability-based Exalted and being elevated above the powers of mortal humanity is what Exaltation is all about. Like a Solar, a Terrestrial with dots and Charms of Lore is smarter than any mortal with the same number of dots, and situationally smarter than one with more dots.

                                    A Lunar's power is "bestial monstrosity". Their most iconic ability is turning into animals, which is certainly "monstrously bestial". People in Creation (especially in the Realm) can underestimate a Lunar's basic human intelligence on the assumption that turning into an animal curses you to be as dumb as one, and Lunars can feed this misunderstanding for all the advantage it is worth.

                                    You and I know that a Lunar is not any less intelligent than any other mortal human. But this tends to surprise people in Creation, who lack our meta-knowledge. What further surprises these fictional, subjectively-perspective people is that the Anathemic powers of "beasts" and "monsters" can be applied as much to mental capabilities as physical.

                                    So let us say there is a balanced Sworn Brotherhood of Dragon-Blooded mobilized in a Wyld Hunt against an old, powerful Lunar who balances physical and mental power. The Terrestrials see the Anathema fleeing and stalking and fighting them in the forms of beasts and monsters. She frequently doesn't look human, and maybe she doesn't even speak an intelligible language to them. They are faced with obvious signs of her physical power, but can easily begin to underestimate her intelligence.

                                    Remember that this is a balanced brotherhood. At least one of the Dragon-Blooded is an intellectual. He knows he is a true Exalt, the only kind of Exalt, a Prince of the Earth smarter than most of his Chosen peers, let alone the teeming mortal masses of humanity. Surely no barbarian peasant foolish enough to fall prey to demons could match wits with him, especially after the demons twisted her into a monstrous beast.

                                    But like I said, you and I know -- thanks to the Corebook and Lunar manual -- that this is not true. The Lunar knows this isn't true. Her Second Breath did not debase her, it Exalted her, and she has become far more insightful and cunning than ever before. In some ways, definitely more so than the learned member of the Terrestrial brotherhood.

                                    But not in all ways, because tiers are being tossed under a speeding bus. Terrestrials are going to have individual Lore, Investigation and Occult Charms which make them better at particular "smart" tasks than Lunar Charms can represent. Perhaps especially Crafting Charms.

                                    So, the question then becomes, in what manners are Lunars smarter than mortal humans? In what manners are they smarter than Terrestrials? In what matters are they as smart as Solars?

                                    In all the ways that ought to terrify a horror survival protagonist. It's a slasher movie, the Lunar is the monster, and the Terrestrials are the prey. The Terrestrials start out thinking the Lunar is stupid, but their Charm-represented cleverness and craftiness cannot protect them from her predation, because her Charm-representation cunning is too much for them. She figures out their magical locks, she has flashes of insight warning her about their magical traps, she recognizes how their magical weapons and defenses work and how to break them (or use them for herself), and she can eat any one of them to learn all of their plans and quirks.

                                    There are definitely other ways in which a Lunar can be superhumanly Intelligent! But this thread isn't for exploring those paths.
