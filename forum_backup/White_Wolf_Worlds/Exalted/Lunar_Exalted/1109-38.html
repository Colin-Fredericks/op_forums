                                                        TSk, that's a reasonable response to the tell. Unfortunately, that is not how the tell works as written.

                                                        First, the tell makes a magical social attack against every single person in the area against their MDV with 12 automatic successes. If you are in a true form, their MDV is doubled. Remember also that MDV is increased if the individual has a higher Appearance. Also, as a note for magical mental influence: "Targets recognize the supernatural force behind the character’s actions." That means, walking around in your true human form, every single person with an MDV of seven or higher automatically notices that you are a Lunar and that you are magically trying to make them not look at your tell. That's an MDV after modifications for Appearance, etc.

                                                        If your character's motivation is something like "Destroy the Anathema," the order to ignore the tell becomes an Unacceptable Order, with all the effects that entails. Admittedly, this is only in rare cases, like fanatical Wyld Hunt Dragon-Blooded. Of course, these are the people you most want to hide from.

                                                        Finally, if you spend even one mote of Peripheral Essence, your tell becomes obvious. If someone has seen your tell before, that's a +2 to their MDV. If they know that the tell is a sign of Shapeshifters, that's an additional +1. If they know that the Tell is only a Lunar thing, that's an additional +2 (not stacking with the +1). Purchasing certain Knacks can also reduce the number of successes at the Tells attack, but we'll ignore those for now.

                                                        This means that if someone spots you after having spent even 1 mote of Peripheral Essence, and wants to find you again, and you just happen to be in your true human or animal forms, and they know enough about Lunars to know what a Tell is, the MDV they require to see you is:

                                                        12/2 - 4 = 2. That's right, if by some chance you encounter a Wyld Hunt, spend some essence on trying to kill them, fail, and escape, if they even glance in your direction at a later date while you are hiding out, they will automatically see you, because your tell will scream at them "I"m right here!"

                                                        I admit, this is not because the Tell is a bad idea. It's because the social mechanics have a real problem, and the Tell should NOT be using them. Especially to attack anyone who sees you! That's just god-awful thinking.

                                                        Oh, and the suggestions for the Tell are something like "a fine mantle of down upon your shoulders in human form. " So, many STs won't go for a scent. I think its reasonable, but even if they do let it go, that scent automatically triggers a "Don't notice me effect!" If they do notice it, they realize that the smell is creating a magical mental influence of 'Don't notice me!"

                                                        I like the Tell in concept, but I hate hate hate how it was implemented through the weakest part of the ruleset in Ex2. The Tell isn't even half the problem. The fact that Lunars have THREE things that screw over their shapeshifting is what bothers me. The Tell, the Tattoos, and the the inability to spend Essence while transformed. Add that to certain Knacks you just can't take (shapeshifting knacks that make your shapeshifting better but also screw over your shapeshifting), and it just... kinda sucks.

                                                        End rant.

                                                        Edit: TSk, I really, really wish that the Tell worked like you suggest. Most capable STs I know throw out the ENTIRE Tell system and homebrew something to take effect. There's just so much work to do to make Lunars what they should be, they don't do it.
