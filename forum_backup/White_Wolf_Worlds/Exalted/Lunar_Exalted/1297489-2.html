                    So to answer the initial question:

                    1. You kill the human and drink their heart's blood.

                    2. You use one of the many "vegetarian" charm options that allow you to take the shape non lethally.
                    - You do a favor for them that they consider to be worth their identity (they don't have to know you are taking it)
                    - You beat them in a game of chance for it, and you can use very misleading language here
                    - You wrestle them and win it (they don't have to know that you're about to take their shape)
                    - You make a deal with them/see them make a deal and if they break it, their face is yours
                    - You break into their house (in your shape or in anothers) and either vandalize it/steal something they care about
                    - You go on a life-changing journey together with them
                    - You learn a defining intimacy of theirs and develop the same intimacy
                    - You share a moment of physical intimacy (can be just touch) with them in any shape. (High Appearance Lunars, take note, running around town as the cutest kitty in Creation and literally forcing people to stroke your head with your 14+ Pretty Kitty pool counts.)
                    - Several others I'm sure I've missed.

                    So that being said, it is almost trivially easy for a Lunar to take human shapes. I think I've seen the word "Birthright" of the Lunar Exalted bandied around. So to answer the second question, heck yes, absolutely no question you could do that as a Lunar.

                    1. You could already have the moppet shape in your library, just change your clothes (with charms) and change into her, and boom, effective disguise.

                    2. You don't even need to shapeshift. If you are confident in your disguise check, you could use Charms to morph from the femme fatale into the moppet (all alterations described can be done with one charm) even if you don't have their shape.

                    3. The moment a Lunar rounds a corner from a chase, you've already lost. Lunars can also become all kinds of animals (and its as easy to get animal shapes as it is humans), why didn't said Lunar become one of the many rats around the corner they moment they turned as a Reflexive action? Save themselves getting arrested.

                        Many-Faced Moon Transformation can allow one to alter personal features sufficient to make for a highly effective disguise attempt, and Changing-Plumage Mastery allows one to add to that a capacity to alter clothing, as well as a few other possible benefits (such as becoming really plain and forgettable). There's also a Charm that lets you very temporarily acquire a shape on imbibing blood without needing the usual sacred hunt, or even killing people at a somewhat higher Essence. That won't cover clothes, though. Might help to have a Manipulation Charm to get people to think you're shabbier than you really are, if you lack the magic to outright transform them.
