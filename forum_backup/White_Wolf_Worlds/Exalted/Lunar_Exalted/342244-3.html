    Originally posted by Rater202
    I do believe I've provided a re-skinned Alex, thus proving that appearance means nothing to powers, and there's very little difference between one kind of "Human mind in the body of a pants soilingly terrifying protean monster" and an other, and chhances are you're going to just as terrified of the one that turns into a TRex, a 12 foot bear man, and a squid as you are the one that turns into a mass of teeth and tentacles, a 6 foot armored bodybuilder, and a clawed horror.
    So you made a Lunar who is inspired by Alex Mercer? Great! Good on you. That does not mean that Prototype as a whole should apply to Lunars as a whole. Lunars are very much Mythical Shapeshifters, not the Protean, Amorphous shapeshifter powers of Prototype.

    Let me make one thing very clear to you though, the appearance of a power DOES matter, in fact, I'd go so far as say that it's the most important thing about a power, at least in this medium.
    If there's an Infernal Presence charm that works because the Infernal SPEAKS WITH THE PRESENCE OF MALFEAS, THE DEMON CITY, and thus is capable of convincing the WEAK, PATHETIC MORTALS to KNEEL to him, and there's a Solar charm that convinces people to accept a Solar as their king, because he has the aura of a born leader, they will probably have similar mechanics, yet feel completely different to the player.

    It's all about presentation when it comes to powers, because, at the end of the day, what we're really doing here is adjusting some numbers on a sheet of paper and rerolling some die. That's what a Charm really is, and it's the way that it's presented that that makes it interesting. It's one of the reasons why I love that every Charm has an over-the-top kung fu name.

                                            Originally posted by Rater202
                                            They work better for the aesthetic, but are terrible models for the Mechanic-the how the powers work and what they're used for, and the actual "This is how it works, this is how you do it, and this is what it's good for" is whats important

                                            The themes of "hunting down a monster, defeating it in battle, killing it, and after doing so eating it in order to take it's power for my own" and the "Shapeshifting"

                                            That's what drew me to Prototype, and that's what drew me to the Lunars

                                            The other similarities I pointed out were things I noticed later-the similar nature of how the powers worked, similar limitations(gotta be something ya ate), and the similar ways the powers can be used were things I noticed later. The "wait a minute, most of their non-shifting powers are basically just super attributes and augmented sense" thing, which are a core Lunar thing and a thing they can easily do are things I noticed later and thought "hey maybe I can play a Lunar that's kinda like that".

                                            The Mechanics of the shapeshifting and the ability to take more than one extra form, and the ability to combine aspects of various forms to augment my already superhuman attributes, and the focus on super human skills(Ability Charms) rather than Super Human Attributes.
                                            Okay, I'm pretty sure this is something we can work with...

                                            As far as mechanics go, you're asking this in terms of 3e (citing Lunars as now being cast as Mythic God-Monsters rather than . . .whatever Lunar were supposed to be in 1e & 2e) so my first and most primary suggestion is to wait to see what the Dev's come up with for Infernals / Liminals / Abyssals. I imagine a particularly strange Exigent could work for this as well. Since no one is yet saying which splat is coming out when, aside from Dragonbloodeds next and then Exigents, the latter are probably your best bet.

                                            The Finding-Killing-Becoming thing is still a thing for Lunars. What appears to be less absolute is the ability for Lunars to combine those shapes in bizzare gesthalt creations.

                                            My suggestion is to wait to see what the Dev's come up with, it's possible that the mechanics you're looking for will be more available down the line in the future. At the very least, I imagine Exigents could play off Attributes just as easily as Lunar's could.

                                            - - - -

                                            As for the blow back this thread seems to be generating, I feel like it's largely because you're looking at the characters as collections of powers. All the things you're describing are things Lunars do, rather than things Lunars are.

                                            Ultimately, whether or not Prototype fits with your idea of a Lunars Mechanics is up to you and your storyteller. But the themes of a Lunar, their history and character and what their powers say about who they are isn't ever going to jive with Mercer/Heller, they're very different kinds of characters/stories.
