    Originally posted by Jens Ole Knudsen View Post


    I like this idea - To make it seem more magical than computer code, it would be easy to say that the Imperial Manse originally found her "worthy" and then after 7½ centuries of political bargaining or what have you, it no longer did. Same effect for each scenario, but different aesthetics depending on what you like
    That actually works better than my thought, especially with 3e's (excellent, in my opinion) move away from the 'sci-fi'-ey stuff.

    The important things, in my eyes, about the disappearance of the Scarlet Empress are that:
    1. She's not here to run the Realm.
    2. She didn't plan whatever caused her to be away (because otherwise she'd have left some provision for when she's gone.
    3. People can't be sure she's not coming back.
    I personally like her being dead, because I personally prefer the 'Realm collapses into feuding territories that you have to deal with individually' plotline over the 'Realm reunifies after a brief period of instability' plotline, but that's just me.

    I'm a fan of A Song of Ice and Fire. Sue me.

                In that game I mentioned earlier, it turned out that her provision for while she was gone was Tepet Fokuf.                 That is amusing. Also amusing is the fact that so many people seem to look at Fokuf and go He can't really be that stupid, surely. Surely he has to be some kind of master manipulator.

                I ran a game once where the players (Dragonblooded) were convinced that Fokuf was exactly that kind of person, and spent ages trying to hint to him that they knew, they could be trusted and so on, first via letters and then by other means (this is while doing other stuff, mind you, it wasn't their sole focus). I almost went along with it, but then eventually decided that no, it would be funnier for them to be simply wrong. I mean, they made so many Read Intentions rolls and such, and they were just so convinced that even when I came out and basically said "He's literally just an idiot" they were certain there was something more to him, and all because of an NPC's botch way back near the beginning (they didn't know it was a botch).

                That was a good game. Sure, the Realm fell into civil war, and then we had to move onto a different game for reasons, but it was great fun. Their faces at the end of it all were priceless.
