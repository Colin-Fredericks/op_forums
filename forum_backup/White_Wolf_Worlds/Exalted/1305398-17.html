        Originally posted by DrLoveMonkey View Post
        How easy do you find the reset condition to be?

        Initially I expected it'd be a pretty tough condition to achieve, but a few things have made it easier:

        1. Most fights involve at least one non-Exalt. Dragonblooded love to bring friends. Non-Exalts mean attacks without Overwhelming, and not buffed by excellencies and Charms (though relatively often Command dice are involved, evening it out a little). This makes it easier to soak hits outright. Would be even easier if heavy armor were an option, but sadly Single Point precludes that.

        2. Even when Overwhelming gets into the mix, I read Iron Skin Concentration as allowing the user to treat up to Stamina points of Overwhelming damage as ordinary soakable damage. Even at a 2m cost, this often ends up being worthwhile when fighting Dynasts and the like.

        3. God help them if Aegis of Invincible Might is triggered, somehow.
