    [White Elephant for EndlessKng] Expanded Sidereal Linguistics

    12-24-2014, 12:18 PM
    Hello EndlessKng!
    Originally posted by EndlessKng
    I'm up for anything, but if you're looking for suggestions, I'm always a hound for new mechanics (I will, of course take fluff, but I have no real talent for crunch, and always like to see new bits and pieces and wonder what I can do with them). My favorite splats are Infernal and Sidereal, though I accept all comers. Also, if you can work something cool up for Burn Legend, I'm especially stoked for that prospect!
    Since I don't like Infernals and I don't know Burn Legend in the slightest, and I like Sidereals and writing Sidereal Charms, that's what you're getting! But no haphazard menagerie of Sidereal Charms. No, I've decided to write up a few for one of the shortest trees in the book: Linguistics!

    I'll start with a quick summary of themes. For Linguistics, it's connections and bindings and the power of words. Its three base Charms represent three takes on it. Favorable Inflection Procedure invokes the power of someone's name to calm them or build a connection with them. A connection through a powerful word. Blue Vervain Binding binds two beings together, granting them the power to communicate. A binding that brings a connection through words. Abandoned Words Curse strips a phrase or language from someone. It's the flipside of the theme, breaking a connection through suppression of words. Then the prayer strip Charm, which often will invoke the theme much more strongly. It's an oath – words – that bind two people so strongly that they can share their body and soul.

    I'll be expanding on the main theme, the different takes of the base Charms, and providing new interpretations. Note that two of the Charms below are ones I've written previously, Always Coming Home and Righteous Verse of the Ages. I'm putting them here for reference and completeness' sake. (Also, I have this all in a googledoc if that's more to your liking, found here.)

    Always Coming Home
    Cost: 2m; Mins: Linguistics 2, Essence 1; Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: Combo-OK, Illusion, Training
    Duration: Indefinite
    Prerequisite Charms: None

    A Sidereal is at home wherever she may go. When this Charm is activated, the Seer gains a Linguistics specialty — the dialect of the place she is currently in, with a perfect local accent. The Sidereal may spend the required experience points while this Charm is active instantly learn the granted specialty, as a Training effect. This Charm does not give the character knowledge of the actual language of the region. Locals also treat the Sidereal as a local-born, even if as one returning from a long time ago and a long way away, forgiving her of not knowing proprieties or even the local language. This unnatural Illusion costs two Willpower to resist.

    At Essence 3+ this Charm may be used to provide whole languages as well, granted temporarily without an associated Linguistics dot. This use costs an additional one Willpower to activate.

    A Familiar Smile
    Cost: 4m, 1wp; Mins: Linguistics 3, Essence 3; Type: Simple
    Keywords: Combo-OK, Illusion
    Duration: One scene
    Prerequisite Charms: Always Coming Home

    Connections between people are the stitches that make up the Tapestry that the Loom of Fate weaves. A Sidereal with this Charm can undo a stitch and, for a time, replacing one party in a relationship by hooking it to herself. All the Sidereal must know to activate this Charm is that a connection exists. This can be a general one, such as the connection a person has to their hometown, culture, or nation; or closer connections between family, friends, and enemies, as represented by Intimacies. The former may require a successful Lore-based roll on the Sidereal's part to determine, while the latter may require Reading Motivation. When this Charm is then activated, Fate twists all perception to match its new alignment, considered an Illusion effect that costs four Willpower to resist. The Storyteller may lower the required Willpower to resist the more discordant the new connection; replacing a man's marriage to a young woman with a different young woman would likely cause no reduction, while to a very old woman may reduce it by one, or to a man by two. Once resisted, an individual is immune to this Charm for the rest of the story.

    If Always Coming Home is active, this Charm costs two less motes to activate for relationships based on the Sidereal's current location.

    Righteous Verse of the Ages
    Cost: 5m; Mins: Linguistics 3, Essence 2; Type: Simple or Reflexive (Step 2)
    Keywords: Combo-OK, Virtue (Valor)
    Duration: Until next action
    Prerequisite Charms: Favorable Inflection Procedure

    The Sidereal draws truths of future and past from the skeins of fate and forms them into devastating poetry. The Seer chooses a target within (Essence) yards that can hear him and rolls ([Charisma or Manipulation] + Linguistics), adding (Essence) automatic successes, against the target's MDV. If successful, the target is clinched, bound by words that shake her to her very soul. The Sidereal must maintain the clinch, focusing on continuing his epic poetry. He may use the normal clinch maneuvers, albeit modified:
    • Breaking Hold: The target flings herself backwards, unable to withstand exposure to the supreme truths of the Seer's words. She is always automatically knocked prone, and may be knocked back (Charisma) yards. Knockback damage is calculated like Heaven Thunder Hammer.
    • Crush: The poetry is so devastating to the target's mind that it manifests physical ailments — sweats, shortness of breath, heart palpitations, and worse. The target takes (remaining successes + Charisma) bashing damage which ignores all soak.
    • Hold: The target's mind simply reels. While she is held, any action she manages to take suffers an internal penalty of (the Sidereal's Essence). This does not include rolls to resist the clinch.
    Righteous Verse of the Ages may be used to renew the clinch, allowing the Sidereal to roll ([Charisma or Manipulation] + Linguistics). The target may oppose the clinch with mental fortitude, rolling (Wits + Integrity), and may only elect to break the hold by releasing the Exalt without violence if she gains control. Unless, of course, the target knows this Charm as well, in which case she can use it to respond with her own overwhelming sagas.

    Finally, the Sidereal may reflexively use this Charm to oppose a physical clinch, forcing his attacker to attempt to comprehend the eminence of his words and lose control of the grapple; the Seer may roll ([Charisma or Manipulation] + Linguistics) to resist the clinch. If he gains control, the above rules apply.

    This Charm's effects are unnatural mental influence; by spending five Willpower to resist, the target can break free of the clinch.

    Betrayal of Voices
    Cost: 6m, 1wp; Mins: Linguistics 4, Essence 3; Type: Supplemental
    Keywords: Combo-OK, Compulsion
    Duration: Indefinite
    Prerequisite Charms: Abandoned Words Curse

    This Charm enhances a social attack in which the Sidereal makes known the ire she has for the individual she's targeting. In addition to all other effects, this Charm lays a curse upon him, an unnatural Compulsion, which causes him to betray those he relies on with his own words. This could be an inflection in idle speech which others hear untrustworthiness, or slips of the tongue. However it happens, the people associated with the target's social Backgrounds (Allies, Contacts, Backing, etc) start to leave the character's confidence. One dot of the Storyteller's choice (usually dependant on circumstances and how often the character interacts with his social Backgrounds) is suppressed per week at most, though if the character takes steps to speak little to these people or take himself out of the public ear, he can stave off the reduction.

    Any time the victim interacts with someone associated with one of his social Backgrounds, he may spend three Willpower to stop the curse from spoiling that relationship for the rest of the scene. Once he does this (the Sidereal's Essence) times, he breaks the curse completely and the Charm ends.

    At Essence 4+, one dot of Backgrounds is suppressed per day at most, allowing the curse to work much more swiftly. At Essence 6+, this limitation is completely removed.

    Unabridged Text Recovery
    Cost: 7m; Mins: Linguistics 4, Essence 3; Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: Combo-OK
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Blue Vervain Binding

    An unfinished word begs for a conclusion. By extension, phrases, sentences, or entire texts and speeches desire completeness. With this Charm, the chosen of the Maidens can complete what is broken, drawing the remainder from the skeins of Fate. It is activated when he hears or reads something that is unfinished, including a person cut off mid-speech or a text partially burned. There must have been at least an intent to finish. This Charm can supply great quantities of words, finishing an oration or a single volume of text, but more than that requires multiple activations of this Charm. The Sidereal must produce the missing words in the medium it began as; the remainder of an oration must be spoken, while the rest of a text must be written, or the recovery is lost.

    Repurchasing this Charm at Essence 4+ expands the capabilities of this Charm to mundane objects, for all things desire wholeness. The Sidereal must have the required materials and tools and take the time to complete the object's construction, but no skill is needed; Fate provides. The object is completed just as the original creator would've completed it, or did complete in the case of repairing a broken thing. At Essence 6+, this extends to artifacts rated (Essence - 5) or lower.

    Length of an Unbroken Word
    Cost: 12m, 1wp, 1lhl; Mins: Linguistics 4, Essence 4; Type: Simple
    Keywords: Combo-OK, Prayer Strip
    Duration: Indefinite
    Prerequisite Charms: Favorable Inflection Procedure, Unabridged Text Recovery, Always Coming Home

    The calligraphy of the prayer strip leaves a central empty space. When the Sidereal uses this Charm, he rips the prayer strip in half in a flash of blue-white light like lightning. On one half, he writes half a word, and hangs the half-strip in space. The edge dimly glows. At another location within (Essence x 10) miles, he carefully writes the other half of the word on the other half-strip such that it matches perfectly with the writing on the first half-strip. This is difficult penmanship, requiring a (Dexterity + Linguistics) roll at difficulty 4. If successful, the Seer may hang the second half-strip in the air. Across space, the halves of the word reach out to each other, not wishing to be unfinished. They join, becoming whole, bridging the distance between them.

    As long as the Sidereal commits motes to this Charm, he or anyone else who whispers the word written on the prayer strip can step from one of its halves to the other in an instant, exploiting the bridge the word has made through space. Wise Seers try to pick words that are not easily guessed when one half is read, to keep knowledgeable strays from using the bridge without authorization.

    With Essence 5+, the distance the prayer strip halves can be separated expands to (Essence x 100) miles. The penmanship has to be all that more precise however, increasing the difficulty of the roll to 6. At Essence 6+ the possible distance can span half the world, (Essence x 1000) miles. The difficulty of the roll increases to 7. At Essence 7+, anywhere in Creation, at difficulty 8. At Essence 8+, the strength of the word is so strong it can bridge realms of existence, at difficulty 10.
