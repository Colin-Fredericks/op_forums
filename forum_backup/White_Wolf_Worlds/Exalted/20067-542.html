                                            Good answers above, but let me take a slightly different spin.

                                            Your Cataphract would become a Raksha whose story has a chapter of being captured and memory-changed. Every good story has low points. Good stories transition from low points to high points based on overcoming whatever it was that made the point low. Thus, you'd be empowering him to generate a tale of escape and revenge.

                                            Some Raksha might even "want" ... from an authorial perspective ... to be captured and mistreated. How boring it would be to be ever victorious and have no great struggle. How thrilling it would be to become a story of defying the wretched Exalted and surviving their wrath.
