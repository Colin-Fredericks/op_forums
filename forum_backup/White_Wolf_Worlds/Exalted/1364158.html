          There's a few ways to approach this, all of which will require some homebrewing.

          The first is native charms of some type of Exalt. I could see this being an effect that fit in with either Solar or Dragon-Blooded Craft. For Solars, the logic would be "I can paint something so well it literally comes to life", while for Dragon-Blooded it would be "I can imbue my paintings with elemental power that animate them". I could see Lunars getting a similar effect as well, though I'd probably put that in Charisma or Intelligence for them, and the effect would be a bit broader, along the lines of "things I describe or depict are so realistic that they become animated".

          Second, you could make an artifact - a magic paintbrush, for instance. I'd put an artifact that can animate the images of what it paints at Artifact 3, while one that can actually summon physical things would be Artifact 4.

          Finally, you could go for the Exigent option. This will require the most homebrewing, as it basically means you're creating a whole new type of Exalted, with their own custom charmset. But if the player wants this to be their whole thing, then being the Exigent of the God of Paintings or something like that is probably the best way to get a powerset that makes this central.

          So, which option do you think works best for your player?

                                      Originally posted by Epee102 View Post
                                      Sounds like sorcery, with some craft based shaping rituals, and some reskinning of spells to be paintings.
                                      That's probably the most by-the-book way to do it.
                                      • Sculpted Seafoam Eidolon (from the Dragonblooded book) is almost exactly what you want-- it lets you create a simulacrum of anything from objects to people, with lifelike traits and basic intelligences.
                                      • Demon of the First Circle and Summon Elemental would also be relevant. Infallible Messenger too, maybe.
                                      • Floral Ferry (the Dragonblooded book again) creates a little ship from essentially nothing; seems fitting to me.
                                      But more fitting, perhaps, would be some of the Charms from Sanctafrax's Craft overhaul. Most of them are still compatible with the default system, and there's lots of good stuff that seems utterly perfect for this character. The whole rewrite is built around the idea of using Craft mid-adventure, so there's some big stuff about creating things really really quickly.
                                      • Words as a Workshop Method literally lets you build stuff out of nothingness, replacing materials with raw Essence. A follow-up Charm (The Art Of Permanence) lets you spend willpower to make them real. That's... what you want. That's exactly what you want.
                                      • Living Statue Genesis and its follow-ups, Life From The Workshop and Stone Legion Construction, are technically about animating statues and clockwork soldiers and stuff, but they'd work just as well with life-sized artwork. Boom, bang, pow. Concept filled. But there's more!
                                      • Craft of War lets you use Craft for in-combat gambits. Say, painting a cage around a dude and trapping him.
                                      • Instantaneous Architecture Technique lets you use the former as a simple action to create walls, houses, and bridges even mid-battle.
                                      • Painted Truth Prana is literally written for super-painters; it lets you encode social influence into artwork.
                                      • Glorious Solar Arsenal and Glorious Solar Armory let you pull artifact weapons and armor out of nowhere. Or, say, a painting.
                                      Heck, you could go farther and flavor more normal, long-term "sit in a workshop and build" Craft as painting too. Just something incredibly large and intricate and perfect, such that it takes weeks to finish even for a Solar.

                                      EDIT:... now I want to play this character.
