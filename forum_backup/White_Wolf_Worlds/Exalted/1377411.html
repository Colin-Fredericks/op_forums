    How does your Creation vary?

    03-30-2020, 08:23 PM
    I'll get the ball rolling.

    In my Creation, in contrast to elementals. The majority of gods lack a way to readily materialize and instead resort to similar methods to ghosts where they speak through their priests or inhabit effigies of themselves and ride them into battle. The definition of effigy is fairly loose so a Field Guardian might jump into a sacrecrow if its people are in danger while a more powerful and successful one might have its worshipers contruct a wicker man for it to stomp around in something akin to a ramshackle warstrider.

    In what ways is your Creation different to canon? I'm talking significant changes where players might have their expectations of the setting challenged.

                  Okay...
                  So, thousands of years ago a group of priests of the Titans were helped by Autocthon to steal the power of the Unconquered Sun, becoming the Solar Anathema; immortal spirits who possess heroic mortals. They convinced Gaia, Luna, etc, to betray the Titans. Leading a host of Exalted, they overthrew the Titans.

                  However, eventually the Dragons could not stand the Solars anymore. With the help of their Dragonblood armies, the Immaculate Dragons trapped the Solars in Hell with the Titans. However, in this great war the Solars used their magic to utterly destroy the Sidereals (who'd joined the Dragon's cause). Sidereals no longer exist as even a memory.

                  As the Lunars had stayed loyal to the Solars, the Dragonblood battled them for 1500 years. But then the sorcerer-queen Raksi and her pack managed to free the Solars from Hell and unleash them on the Realm.

                  The Solars, wielding infernal powers learnt from 1500 years in Hell, soon destroyed the Realm. They besieged many of the Realm's greatest heroes on the Imperial Mountain, eventually defeating them, traveling into the Mountain, and killing Pasiap. This caused earthquakes across the world for decades, as well as more metaphysical changes, as Pasiap was Gaia's fetich soul.
                  Covens of Solars travelled to the other Elemental Poles to wreak vengeance on the other Dragons for their imprisonment. Hesiesh died to Solar infernalism.

                  With this, Mela decided to strike back, attempting to destroy humanity with an eternal winter that lasted decades. Some brave Lunar PCs managed to convince her to stop though, in return for helping her kill a Solar assassination team and capture one of the Solar leaders, Yurgen Kaneko.
                  So, for the last few decades things have been slowly getting warmer, but with several Immaculate Dragons dead, and after a short Ice Age, things look very different.
                  (But I will have to leave it there for now, as that's as much as the PCs know)

                  STing Bronze Age Exalted
