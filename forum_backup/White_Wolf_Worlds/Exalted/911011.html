                              I think the Lunars are at least weakly less likely to pull together compared to Dragonblooded - they don't have a "by our powers combined" feel and no need to arrange marriages together to maintain their power, so there's no Dynastic pressure to all pool together for one large Realm. Supposedly Lunars benefit less from having stuff also, so higher mobility inherently.
                              So possibly more decentralised and a patchwork.

                              A bigger facet may be whether there's still a winner-takes-all-dynamic with one Lunar or a small clique seizing the Sword of Creation and using it to maintain power.

                              If not then the least that happens is probably that the Lunar Realm's center of gravity from which it goes forth to hold dominion over other nations is probably the Scavenger Lands, as the major center of population and Old Realm artefacts, with no reason to cluster around the Blessed Isle. (The Blessed Isle's position in that configuration would I see end up being a little like An Teng in the present parallel, writ larger - a relatively peaceful, large, oppressed country).

                              Beastpeople could form a distinct high/low status element in the population.

                              Dragonblooded could be very secretive; if they're around at all as a major element, they would either be fairly non-aligned with Sidereals against Lunars and/or the Sidereals may have done some serious Mask work to keep them evading Lunar networks. I think of the hidden ninja clan and ranger tropes here, I guess, and the Forest Witches (maybe an interesting parallel world where a version of the Witches forms early and then swells in numbers massively due to an exile, and becomes an insidious major power in the world).

                                      Well since the OP asked about the realm specifically I'm going into this with the assumption that the Lunars used the chaos of the contagion and the wyld invasion to retake creation as the Shogunate crumbled.

                                      The Lunars got in and got the defense grid operating rathe than the empress Maybe? OR perhaps they pulled their tribes and forces out of the way of the fighting until the wild and the realm broke their teeth on each other and stomped them both. Anyway the assumption is that Instead of the Scarlet empress Lunars of some stripe rose to power.

                                      Odds are they would have sacked the immaculate order and basically allowed the humans and the various spirits to sort shit out on their own. God-blooded and blessed would probably be far more common, but rogue spirits on the other hand would also be able to get away with more since while Lunars would be more than willing to protect their tribe they aren't hyper organized.

                                      Basically creation would be divided up into various territories controlled by lunars or powerful beings that the lunars feel they can play nice with, so life for exigents, liminals, and to a certain extent powerful mortals is probably quite a bit better than under the realm. On the other hand for most things people are going to be expected to deal with their own problems. So if you can't get rid of troublesome ghost or warlord you're going to have to shell out something spiffy for outsiders to help you and even then it's a gamble.

                                      Sidereals would likely be one of the few things that would get the various leaders working together. If Gemitians become a thing in this timeline then they flourish since they can likely build an alliance with enough of the lunars to really make the Sidereal loyalists hurt.

                                      The lunars would likely keep some modicum of overall structure and communication going if only out of habit from the shogunate years, but the day to day would likely be smaller kingdoms running themselves.

                                      Trade and merchant rings would have it both harder and easier, they'd be responsible for more of their own safety, but they'd have less restrictions placed on them.

                                      D-born would have it rough during the early days, but unless the lunars go whole hog on stomping them out I'd wager they'd still be the most common exalt in creation, if by a smaller margin. Odds are they'd end up forming their own enclaves based on their surviving shogunate strongpoints, or being incorporated into the new society in one form or another possibly dependent on which power is in charge of the areas near them.

                                      Some enclaves would be shogunate hold outs, but they'd be small nations that are cut off from allies and support and eventually a lot of that will fade as the business of living sort of grinds down a lot of their direct ambitions, to the point that some enclaves might even sign on as recognized kingdoms of their own in the new lunar controlled creation.

                                      Now the tech-magic-infrastructure question will be a bit odd.

                                      Lunars are celestials and as such can build bigger and better workings more easily than terrestrials. So they'll have an advantage putting infrastructure and such into place, but that's going to require the resources and interest, either they need to support people who do care, or do it themselves, which won't happen everywhere.

                                      So you'll have some nations ruled by a powerful warrior, while another is lead by a sorcerer-sage, eventually one will likely end up encroaching on the other as they compete for resources and territory. So I expect that by the time the Solars are released 700 years in that Creation has taken on a sort of patchwork feel tech-magic wise with some areas being heavy on such, while others are using sticks and rocks, and yet others are sitting in the remains of past wonders mad max style, and some areas having lot son magic but little tech, and other areas the reverse.

                                      However I suspect there'd be a slow forward trend since the Lunars wouldn't be likely to act to inhibit growth, progress, and change simply as a matter of course the way the realm does.

                                              A Lunar Realm would look more like the Confederation of Rivers. Largely independent states that have a mechanism for mutual cooperation and the resolution of disagreements, but with limited war still on the table if negotiations go south.

                                              If this "had always been" to me, that means the Usurpation succeeded, but only just. Lunars rose up and kicked the Usurpers off the Blessed Isle, along with the few "loyal" DBs who remained. With the resources of the Isle at their command, and the First Age crumbling around them, The Confederation of the Moon simply waited. When the cataclysm was complete, they began a systematic conquest of Creation. Lunars would help each other in turn claim their own dominion, and Sidereals defect from the Bronze Faction to join them in actually running Creation. Everyone likes a winner.

                                              Dragon-Bloods are the lieutenants of the Lunars, but are closely watched for further signs of betrayal. The world is mostly stable for a time. I expect eventually Lunar excesses would cause their own difficulties, because that's the cycle of Exalted, but I'm not sure what that would look like.
