    Originally posted by Leetsepeak View Post
    How would Immaculates feel about Un-Exalted sorcerers?
    It might be changed or better discussed in the future but The Immaculate Philosophy is all about regulating control for a stable world. They want most mortals to be pious while expecting them to be illiterate. They do not approve of people reaching beyond their capabilities if they are mortal, so if they are naturally gifted it may be okay for them to become a Doctor or Mogul, but most mortals they would like them to stay in their social strata and work so that perhaps their children or grandchildren can be doctors while they stay farmers. The supernatural for humanity is the purview of the Dragonblooded, only heathens even have mortal Shamans.

    That said, maybe in third ed the heptagram trains mortal Thaumaturges or Mortal Sorcerers to act as aides, probably without teaching them spells. Or they can just train mortal occultists as assistants.
