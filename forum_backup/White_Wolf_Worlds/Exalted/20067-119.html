                                        Originally posted by Zeal'Dib View Post
                                        I have a quick question about the social hierarchy in the Realm.
                                        I'd say it is: Exalted Dynast > Exalted (former)Patrician > Lost Egg > Mortal Dynast > Mortal Patrician >> Peasant >< Thresholder > Slave )

                                        Mortal Dynasts may have rank and social prestige comparable to Exalts of weaker Houses, but within a House precedence goes to Exalts. ( Though see also House Nellens which is internally headed by mortal Dynasts, but must use Exalted figureheads to have the other houses take them seriously. )

                                        Among the Exalted, prestige is determined by familial pedigree, elemental markings, and personal accomplishment. Typically Exalted Patricians will be assumed to have slightly higher breeding prestige than Lost Eggs unless there are obvious signs of favour by the elemental dragons. Both Patricians and Lost Eggs are usually adopted into a House and will partake of the prestige of that House, though less than Exalted born of that House. They will also be expected to earn this through service to the House and Realm; though so will Exalted Dynasts. While there's more than a fair degree of afluenza among the Dynasts, the Exalted don't exactly rest on their laurels for long. (See also Essence Fever.)

                                                I'd say in practice; Exalted Dynast > Exalted (former)Patrician > Exalted (former) Peasant from the Realm > Lost Egg > Mortal Dynast > Mortal Patrician >> Peasant > Thresholder/Disenfranchised > Slave


                                                In theory, former patricians and peasants are generally adopted into a Realm House (possibly under the legal fiction that they're someone's long-lost or bastard son), and thus are now dynasts. In practice, of course, people know who is and isn't a real dynast.
                                                Also, in theory, a Dragonblood from House Cathak who becomes a Winglord and a Dragonblood who was a teenage barbarian and got grabbed by the Satrap after exalting, and told to choose the legion or the Order, and becomes a Winglord, are the same rank. In practice, the Dragonlord above them is most likely going to consider the dynast more prestigious, and the dynast will also consider themselves more prestigious. They'll also likely better fit what the common soldiers assume a Dragonblood will be like, and thus be treated as more prestigious.

                                                Then of course, there's always the issues that arise because few societies fit their own social hierarchy properly. There's a middle class that don't properly fit into the class system, and in practice a Dragonblood's slave butler who was born and bred in the Realm, is going to be able to boss peasants around with no real issues. Similarly, Bal Keraz is not a Dynast... but he's still the most powerful bureaucrat in the Realm government, so Dragonblooded dynasts of great breeding will still defer to him... though perhaps now the Empress is gone, they'll start deferring to him less.
