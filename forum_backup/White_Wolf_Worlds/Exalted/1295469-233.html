            Originally posted by Anaximander View Post
            Just how well-known and mapped is Creation to those within it? Like is it all hazily understood, or is there still room for "exploration"? (Obviously this pertains to the highly-educated, like Dynasts or elder Lunars.)
            There is absolutely room for exploration, because it would be boring if that wasn't an option. Cartography may be more advanced among certain circles than it was historically on Earth, but it's still largely a matter of peripluses and itineraria and crude (by modern standards) maps for most people. And Creation remains full of places that you can't easily map because your people's enemies are there, or it's too wild and full of unwholesome perils. Savants may have access to First Age maps that are no longer entirely accurate in the Age of Sorrows; sorcerers can gather geographical information through spells, summonings, and workings, but those may be limited in various ways or disrupted by localized supernatural phenomena.

            The long and short of it is that the highly educated will have access to detailed information on many places, but there will remain regions that they only hazily understand.

                                                            Originally posted by Astral View Post
                                                            How do evocations work for Underworld artifacts obtained as grave goods?
                                                            We haven't finalized how grave goods work in 3e, so I'm afraid that we don't have an answer for that as yet.

                                                            Originally posted by Astral View Post
                                                            Also, can jade still be mined in the Underworld and does it remain "real" in Creation like Soulsteel does?
                                                            Stuff from other realms of existence isn't inherently immaterial or evanescent. Underworld jade is real in Creation, but the same applies to any old Underworld rock. Ghosts, grave goods, and other things that come into being via spiritual mechanisms are the exception, not the rule.
