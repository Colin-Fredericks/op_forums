        Also known as "Pride And Prejudice with swords" - how apropos!


        In Nexus, a mysterious behemoth tears up from beneath the earth. It rampages through the city for hours before The Emissary corners it, fights it, and kills it... but The Emissary, too, is destroyed. A confused funeral is held by a city which never truly believed The Emissary was mortal, and then the Council of Entities immediately begins pondering how to keep Nexus' enemies and allies alike from immediately trying to seize control.

        This not the end of the story. It is the beginning.

        Four newcomers emerge, and each is hailed as The Emissary's successor. One is a powerful ghost, which appears in tatters of The Emissary's robes, with half its mask intact and the other shattered to reveal a grinning skull - it dispenses harsh justice, and says that from now on it shall rule for itself, rather than the Council or Guild. One is a silent figure - thin and wearing very different mask and clothing, it nonetheless behaves much as the Emissary might have, but its methods are different, and it never speaks. The third is a young boy: he claims that he, the Emissary, was a powerful Spirit, and he has reformed in his Sanctum after his "death" - his power is prodigious, but his attitudes very different from The Emissary, and more than once he has had public arguments with the Council. The last is a very different puzzle - a mortal Artificer (in truth, a Twilight) who makes no claims to be related to The Emissary, but to whom many flock as Nexus' new protector, as his artifice gives him power, and he is certainly much more stable and reliable than any of the other options.

        (Spoilers: the ghost ultimately snaps and levels Great Forks, the boy nearly dies trying to stop it, the Twilight succeeds in finding the real original Emissary and returns alongside it, and the silent one sort of disappears without a trace.)
