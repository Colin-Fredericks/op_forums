                                                Originally posted by JayTee View Post
                                                Another bit of headcanon: All of the Chosen instinctively speak, write and can understand Old Realm upon Exalting. It's much easier for me to picture the Exalted Host waging war against the Primordials if they all share a common language.
                                                Whatever works for you, of course, but I feel it's better when the Eclipse Caste and Chosen of Serenity have vital roles in keeping armies with different culture and languages working harmoniously together.

                                                More headcanon time! The House Tepet Coup. I forget how much of this is actual canon, but it came from a pair of contradictory setting elements. The House Tepet legions sent to die in the North were part of a plot by the Empress herself... but the Great Houses did not take control over the Legions until after the Empress disappeared!

                                                So here we go. House Tepet had incredible control over the Imperial Legions pre-763, enough to cause the Empress to want to see their influence purged. In part this comes from Tepet leadership philosophy; they eat with their men, rest in the same tents, and always fight at the front of the line. But it also comes from Tepet's earning loyalty among the Outcastes serving within the Legions, for House Tepet is the most likely to adopt or marry them into their family. This behavior predates Futile Blood, and is part of the Tepet breeding programs; the elder Taimyo Council care less for perfected breeding than they do keeping out the Empress' blood from their lines... blood is power after all, even if our Dynasty Charms are not as crazy. Oh yeah, Consort Tepet was a member of a Shogunate gens, and his children who currently run the House are not by him and the Empress. I have no idea if that's canon.

                                                So the Tepets had five Legions completely under their control, and who knows how much influence in the others. They were outside the Empress' Dynasty Charms, and rejecting marriage proposals from the Vneef to keep it that way. All they needed was to get those forces into the Imperial City's walls so they could strike for the Throne itself. It turns out that every Legion that is part of a major military victory gets to parade before the Empress... so the Bull in the North was allowed to grow strong to act as bait. You can even see hints that the Tepet leadership was a single step from staging their own little usurpation in the Aspect Books with moments like Arada musing on what he'd have done if he had not been on campaign when the Empress vanished.

                                                Lunar lifespans. Our Lunar age-cap increasing Charms requires you spend more and more of your time in your totemic animal shape. The process eats away at their humanity, but expands their lifespan near indefinitely. This came about as a cludge after we added a few thousand years to the era of the Shogunates, but actually explains stuff like Leviathan and Lilith spending so much time as a giant whale or owl. It moves First Age Lunars off the world-stage, too, giving more space for younger Lunars to be the ones at the forefront of fighting the Realm.

                                                Gem. The city is at the edge of the mountain ranges south and a bit east of The Lap (bottom mountain in K12 on the Stephenls map). Little about its fluff has changed, I believe. Most of it makes a bit more sense. There are mining camps all long the mountains down to the volcanos where Gem sits in canon, and their production all eventually ends up in the markets of Gem to the north thanks to Guild caravans and sandsea ships.
