            Ehh, why not. I'm gonna flesh these out while I'm at it.
            1)
            Ethnic Origins: Western (Coral, Wavecrest, Wu Jian)
            Family Background: Farmer
            Family Size: Small Extended Household (Cousins and siblings in same household)
            Family Status: Family Drama: Upset by recent Exaltation of Parent
            Childhood Events: Studied Abroad (Foreign Language , Social, and Lore skills possible)
            Adult Career: Local Leader
            Exaltation Trigger: Discovered important Lost Lore
            Exalted Template: Night Spymaster
            Exaltation Fallout: Hunted by Immaculates (Reputation/Stealth Charms)

            Within this small, extended farming family in the West, it's not so much a recent Exaltation, but one recently discovered. One parent Exalted as a Lunar years ago, but managed to keep it a secret with the help of the other parent. The Lunar exercised these powers with discretion, bringing the family wealth and opportunities they would otherwise not have had. As a result, the character was able to study abroad at an excellent school, which helped the character attain a position of leadership. Which was threatened when a Wyld Hunt arrived, as the parent's secret came to light. After speaking with the Immaculates, the character becomes convinced the monster long ago killed that parent and attempts to aid in hunting down the anathema. The character deftly uses the well-built web of contacts and influence pulled together over the years, Lunars the anathema was working on something big, and traces the most likely path for the Wyld Hunt. After shadowing them to these ancient ruins, the character pieces together something the Lunar had not and Exalts as a Night Caste. The Wyld Hunt shifts targets to the surprise Solar, who nearly eats it. The Lunar parent teaches the Wyld Hunt why this was a terrible idea. The Lunar parent commands the new Solar to flee. The Solar does so, leaving the island behind, and almost certain the Lunar is actually the real parent after all. What happens to the Lunar is unknown, but at least one member of the Hunt survived and now pursues the new Sun-Chosen. The Solar begins picking up the pieces on the run.

            2)
            Ethnic Origins: Northern (Gethamane, Haslanti Leage, Whitewall)
            Family Background: Unknown (Family lost, raised in monastery, etc.)
            Family Size: A family member in every city. (You just have one of those really huge, slightly inbred, but widely spread families. Consider buying merit to reflect this Influence.)
            Family Status: Both Parents are alive and well
            Childhood Events: Lost in Wilderness for Many Years (Survival, Animal Handling, Archery skills)
            Adult Career: Merchant
            Exaltation Trigger: Saved a village from attack
            Exalted Template: Eclipse Diplomat
            Exaltation Fallout: Stumbled upon Lunar mate (Ally)

            The character became lost in the wilderness at a relatively young age, after a mysterious incident involving the rest of the local family. Managing to survive out there and not really knowing what happened or what else to do, the character is eventually found by a group of travelers -- including a relative who had been quite close with the character's family. The character winds a path through several other bits of the expansive family tree, finally settling into a merchant's life. Some years later, a local village is attacked when the merchant company is in town. In part from remembering past dealing with the raiding tribe, the character manages to end things peacefully, saving the town and leaving all parties satisfied, thereby receiving the Sun's blessing. Some time after, helping the merchant company to prosper and smoothing over other disputes on the way, a Lunar approaches the character. The Solar feels a strong connection, and the Lunar reveals a little lead about that strange incident, so many years ago, that had impacted the Lunar as well. The Solar and Lunar begin trying to get to the bottom of it all.

            3)
            Ethnic Origins: Southwest (Cinder Isles)
            Family Background: Administrative/Minor Noble(Realm bureaucrat, mayor, guild factor)
            Family Size: A family member in every city. (You just have one of those really huge, slightly inbred, but widely spread families. Consider buying merit to reflect this Influence.)
            Family Status: Both Parents are alive and well
            Childhood Events: Long childhood illness that had you bedridden for many years
            Adult Career: Criminal
            Exaltation Trigger: Discovered important Lost Lore
            Exalted Template: Night Spymaster
            Exaltation Fallout: Supported by Deathlord (Artifacts)

            The character's illness would have left a dead child in the streets had one of the character's parents not been fabulously wealthy. They tried everything to help, but nothing worked. At last, they made an otherworldly bargain they likely came to regret. The character grew strong again, but had a responsibility to those who had brought about the recovery, an intermediary agent of a local Deathlord. The character underwent extensive training and became responsible for theft and reconnaissance in service to this agent. The character had misgivings about the purpose behind this. In time, when delivering a report, the character went snooping around, and found out the true nature of the one the intermediary served. Resolving to bring this Deathlord to its end, the character becomes a Solar of the Night Caste. Seizing some important secrets that had evidently been kept from the Deathlord, the new Sun-Chosen finds a path to this Deathlord and exposes the underling's plot. The Deathlord seems to accept this show of loyalty and begins employing the Solar, deciding the Solar's talents will fill different niches than a Deathknight would. The Solar begins carrying out some dirty work in as benign a fashion as possible, seeking to learn as much as possible of the Deathlords, until the opportune moment.
