    Losing Essence and a 500 Year Old Starting Character

    09-25-2020, 12:05 PM
    So I was thinking today about how you can take a Solar or Lunar and lock them up, and rather than endlessly gaining xp like a player character their bodies and minds can just atrophy and they can actually lose charms and dots as they forget things. Then somebody might come along 500 years later to break them out, thinking they’re getting a legendary warlord, and actually getting a shell of what they once were.

    THEN I thought, could that be a PC backstory? Could you lose Essence dots, going all the way from 5 back to 1, after being locked away for half a millennia? Forgetting almost all your charms and any abilities and being left with what you’d get at chargen? Or is Essence something that once you have it can never be lost outside of things like that spiteful sorcery from 2e?

                                              Originally posted by DrLoveMonkey View Post

                                              Well a couple of things. For one you can’t hide behind being another incarnation. Like I remember vividly playing Dead Space 3 on Ironman mode but I sure as hell cant do it anymore. I also think it’s a cool start to the story where the rest of the circle thinks they’re getting Queen Merela who’s going to waltz into the Mask of Winter’s palace, put him in a headlock and make him cry without breaking a sweat, and they find someone who’s basically no stronger than any of them already are. As an added bonus you don’t have any attachment to the world right now, your prior life was 1,000 years ago. You get to find meaning in a life that you’d wholly given up on for a dozen mortal lifetimes.
                                              Right. But (assuming you're skipping the Amnesia bit), absolutely none of that requires anything more than Essence 2 to start with. I mean Queen Merala, sure might not be doable. But especially in 3E, one can be a legendary unstoppable warrior with pretty much just the Supernal. Then add a few thousand years of misinformation, propaganda, and outright lies, and it's absolutely possible that Eyes of Iron, the legendary Invulnerable Sword Princess, defeater of directional wargods, Slayer of Shogunate Legions, is a Solar who really is an (optimized) starting character.

                                              The genre (drawing from everything from Star Wars to Kill Bill to Kung-Fu Hustle) is that the ancient master who lives in isolation is likely to be far /more/ in tune with the deeper secrets of the Art than normal people. Maybe rusty, less skill. But if I told you there was an ancient master on a hidden mountaintop, who hadn't left the mountain for a hundred years, the general assumption is that he's a wise and potent dude, with a better grasp of the Truths of Reality than someone else. Now obviously, Exalted has its own vibe, and is not actually just kung-fu movie-based. But they used many of the same tropes and thematic beats, which is why people who go raise their essence often, if not always, go into isolation to do so. It just seems very counter to the themes of Exalted, and the way the setting ties Essence to experience (and Experience) and (much looser in 3E, but still somewhat) to age.

                                              That being said, I totally support a character who remembers the ancient past because he lived it. Things like the Veil That Holds Back Time are built to let you tell these kinds of stories, IMO. I've seen a really interesting character built in previous editions who was Veiled into the future during the Usurpation (and had Savant) but they were a rookie solar, and hence a regular PC (except for background access). Especially if the Veil (or the working, or whatever) is broken when you wake up, it wouldn't even cost you anything. Or one who was sorta like Misho. (I had a PC with amnesia before her Exaltation, throwback and a lot of past life memories in a game I ran).

                                              TL;DR - I agree with the above posters. So long as everybody at the table is cool with it (particularly your ST and any Lore characters), have fun. But as a fundamental setting thing, I find losing Essence because you're old and isolated to be unthematic and counter to the canon examples (such as they are) of characters who are obviously faded glories, with diminished capacities, but who still have essence scores commensurate with their achievements.
