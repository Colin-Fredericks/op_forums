    It's not so much my headcanon as the canon of my currently running game, but here goes.

    I set the players up as recruits into the Bronze Tigers at the start of my game, hoping to give a bunch of really new players a good groundwork to work up from. Rather than eventually outgrowing the Tigers as they leveled up, the group almost unanimously decided they were perfectly happy being subordinates to Dace and the rest of the Tiger's higher-ups. When a new player joined that was more familiar with the setting started trying to push them more towards the whole "Glorious Destiny of the Exalted" the rest of the group kept directing the efforts towards the Bronze Tigers as a whole. As such, after the machinations of the Circle, Dace eventually became crowned God-King of a new nation.
    And he f*cking hates it.
    He's perfectly happy being the noble General, leading from the front and showing comraderie to all his soldiers. When it comes to actually taking the reigns of the new-found court, he's a petulant child, going to such lengths as doing everything possible to prevent his new citizens from building him a suitable manor and other obnoxious stall tactics, eventually leading to the Circle's Eclipse Caste to use his charms to expedite the process whether or not Dace likes it.

                        The Celestial Bureaucracy and it's offices, tasks, and positions, only resemble a mortal Bureaucracy in the slightest. An office could be a lavish room, adorned in gold and jade. or it could be a floating expanse in a starry cosmos, with no apparent entry or exit, but still clerks come and go with ease. Everything that could be conceivably considered mundane is so grand and divine it seems bizarre. A god could write it's reports through their breath manifesting as smoky characters bound in wind, or upon flayed skin, the calligraphy carved intricately and scarred into the flesh. A god could have a desk of rich wood, or his items of station borne aloft by twisted vines, his seat a tangle of a tree. The most mundane of offices and reports will likely be from the Sidereals of the Bureau of Destiny, who are still mortal despite their eccentricities.

                                    Orichalcum, Moonsilver, and Starmetal can be mistaken for their mundane counterparts gold, silver and iron under certain circumstances.

                                    Orichalcum may be seen as gold when it is not in sunlight, either at night or in a windowless interior

                                    Moonsilver appears to be normal silver unless it is seen under the light of the moon or brought into a Wyld zone.

                                    Starmetal always appears as mundane iron outside of Calibration.

                                    Attuning to an artifact of these materials brings out the obvious magical properties, as does channeling essence through them in any way(Using charms with a weapon, using an artifact as a magical focus, etc.).

                                    Additionally, Starmetal weapons and armor tend not to be exceedingly oversized, aren't exceptionally heavy and can be used by mortals as extremely durable mundane versions of themselves. More than one Mortal has survived a fateful battle due to the fact they unknowingly carried a starmetal weapon.
