        Not sure where this fixation of tragedy came from for Abyssals. You're the dark mirrors of Solars, infused with the power of death. You are glorious and dark, you are gothic and romantic, you are beautiful and terrifying horror. Is there an element of tragedy? Yes there is, but that's isn't the focus at all. Abyssals were born from having a "Vampire" splat, they're inspired by Vampire, but it isn't constrained by it. Look for the reasoning and designs behind Vampires in OWoD, and why people loved to play them, and you'll find a plethora of themes that can be used for Abyssals.

        As for rebelling and having a liege rating. You do know you can make them lose that rating yes? If your rebellion is known to the Deathlord, you can be sure you won't have the support of the liege rating. That's what Zelbinnean means when they say "the nature of your rebellion" if you openly decree yourself a rebel, you won't have support, but if you are subtle about it, you can still manage it. Doing this comes with consequences and story hooks of their own, having to weight your loyalties, and being clandestine in your plans so your master doesn't discover them.
