                    Originally posted by Ranger_Lord View Post
                    How long was the Shogunate?
                    First Edition actually gives us a pretty easy method to figure this out...

                    "The First Age" is considered to have begun when Autocthon decided the Exalted were too scary, and fled Creation with a payload of mortals and souls. Autocthonia's calendar begins on that date. The 'current day' of Exalted Second Edition is RY (Realm Year) 768, which coincides with DA (Dawn of Autocthonia) 4878.

                    Wind time back to the Beginning of The Realm, where Her Redness restarted the Calendar, and that gives us RY 1, DA 4110.

                    Likewise, after the Usurpation, the Dragonblooded restarted the calendar as well. We don't have anything absolutely 100% solid...but the First Edition book Exalted: The Outcaste lists 'RY 768' as occurring in the 'Year of the Mouse, Bronze Era of the 11th Epoch of the Dragon-Blooded Shogunate,' according to Lookshy

                    Shogunate Epochs were a span of 125 years. Which were made up of 5 25-year eras named the bronze, gold, copper, iron, and quicksilver Eras consecutively. Each Era is sliced into 5 5-year cycles named after the elements of creation. Within each Cycle, each of the 5 years has a name based on an animal relative to that cycle. Knowing it to be the Year of the Mouse, we know that we are in the First Year of the Fire Cycle.

                    So, we have...
                    11th Epoch (10 have passed): +1,250 years
                    1st Era of the Epoch: +0 years
                    Fire Cycle of the Era: +10 years
                    Mouse year of the Fire Cycle: +1 year.

                    So, by the count of the Shogunate Calendar, it is year number 1,261, modern day. Subtract the 768 years of the Realm, and that gives us 493 years between the dawn of the Shogunate, and the dawn of The Realm. So, by that count from the First Edition, the Shogunate lasted for 493 years.

                    Independently compared to this, is another account (can't remember where from) listing that RY1 occurred on Quicksilver Siaka in the 4th (or 5th) Epoch of the Shogunate. There is a little contradictory info floating about.

                    So, 4/5 Epoch: +375 or +500 years
                    5th Era of the Epoch: +100 years
                    Year Siaka takes place in the Water Cycle: +15 years
                    Siaka is the fourth year: +4 years

                    This gives us between 494 years and 619 years. If we stick with 4th Epoch, (and remember that this is equal to RY1), then the math works out splendidly for a Shogunate duration of 493 years.

                    The problem some perceive with this...is that it means that the First Age lasted for (roughly...darn Cascading Years) 3,617 years, the Shogunate lasted for 493 years, and The Realm has lasted for 768 years. The Dragonblooded look back at the Shogunate as their 'Shining Golden Age,' so it does seem a little odd that it was so short. But that's what the books say.
