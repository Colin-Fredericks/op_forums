            I’m going to note that, in 3e, connecting two minds to communicate across any distance is a Celestial 1 Working. As such, I could see these being the product of a Sorcerous Working, with some kind of weird activating ritual type thing going on. Similarly there are a number of terrestrial spells that allow communication over long distances.

            so, there’s a part of me that would say, based on that, these could be artifact 2 or 3.

            On the other hand, Creation does not have widespread long distance communication technology and all the methods it does have are limited in one way or another. If these things are in the artifact 2 range, then any artificer can make a set of them and that potentially changes the setting.

            So, I could see a few ways to go here. You say “well, it’s my game and I’m just gonna ignore that bit” in which case, I’d make them artifact 2. Or you go with the “standard method” of marking that “this stuff does weird things to the setting” and make them artifact 2 but give them the First Age Technology tag and some kind of regular maintenance ritual that needs to be done, lest the ghosts inside get unruly and cloud the mirror. Or, you say “weeell, I don’t wanna break the setting but First Age tech isn’t where I wanna go with this either,” in which case I’d make them Artifact 4.

            Does that help at all?

                                            I've got a custom Artifact for you all! this one is tied to my character, a Solar Weaponsmith. In and out of game its based on Alveua's Forge of Night.

                                            I had a problem with Artifact Creation rules, namely that anything you want to make takes weeks or months with the right charms, or years without (but you can just start with Artifact 5 so why even bother investing in Craft to make them urrrgh). So like a good crafter I crafted a solution to the problem: ​Disposable Artifacts! (gasp) The Twilight Salzan is never satisfied with any design and is always eager to move on to the next thing, and he learned from his master Alveua the technique of forging sapients into more useful things, like swords.

                                            The Unstable keyword (I couldn't think of a better name) lets Salzan say a word or phrase and then destroy his own artifact for power, something that seems like plagiarism familiar to all of you Fate Series fans. Then fueled by inspiration, he makes a new one one.

                                            Without further ado, behold the Black Anvil


                                            The Black Anvil Artifact 3
                                            Attunement: 5m

                                            Forged in a leyline of Malfeas, the Black Anvil is a Sorcerous Focus as much as it is a tool of Crafting. To it's maker Salzan, they're are practically the same thing.

                                            Unique Keyword: Unstable. This effect triggers on the Artifact's destruction.

                                            Evocations:

                                            At Beck and Call
                                            While the Black Anvil irrevocably a part of the realm of Malfeas, it neither exists there or in Creation: it belongs to soul of it's Master, and therein it resides. This evocation allows you to summon and dismiss the Black Anvil from your soul by attuning to it (5m). Additionally, the Black Anvil always counts as the relevant tools for any crafting project- hammer, tongs, whetstone, saw, vice, etc.

                                            Malfean Resources
                                            The realm of Malfeas is as bountiful as it is wicked. A demon of the First Circle may be sacrificed on the Black Anvil and turned into the hellish material known as Corrupted Brass. A single demon produces one large ingot of this metal, enough for a man-sized weapon or suit of armor. Any excess material dissolves back into essence after the projects completion.

                                            Experimental Design
                                            Owning to it's creator's insatiable lust to always move on to the next design, and the cavalier disregard he has for the lives of demons, Artifacts forged on the Black Anvil may bear the Keyword Unstable. An Unstable artifact can be willingly destroyed by invoking a secret means, and this action will produce an effect of incredible -usually destructive- power. The effects depend on the artifact.

                                            Replacing the Irreplaceable
                                            Destruction breeds Creation. After analyzing the strengths and weaknesses of a previous design, inspiration for the next one leaps to mind. If Salzan has destroyed an artifact by using the Unstable effect, he may build a new artifact of a similar type with only a single scene of downtime crafting. May switch weapon type, light/medium/heavy. May only be used to make another Unstable artifact.

                                            *****
                                            Ligier's Hatred- Artifact Spear, Corrupted Brass.
                                            Concept: Lazer Cannon + Short Spear
                                            Unstable: will detonate after being thrown, sending the Green Sun's scorching rays in all directions. Deals # Aggravated damage as an environmental effect to everyone up to short range at the beginning of Salzan's next turn.


                                            I thought it was a pretty elegant work around to the limit of how long Artifact creation rules are, and how seriously Exalted takes Artifacts as weapons of Legend. The idea for Salzan is that each artifact weapon is like a proof of concept prototype, not a relic of power to last for millennium. Ligier's Hatred is there just to give you an idea of what an Unstable effect might look like- in this case, a laser shooting spear would explode into a laser death blossom like a grenade. Replacing the Irreplaceable gives you the freedom to change the weapon type, so after the Spear explodes you might make a Fiery Axe or a Sword that eats darkness. Generally speaking, its Melee Weapon to any other Melee Weapon, Ranged to Ranged, Armor to Armor. Although I don't plan on making much armor.

                                            My goal was to have an master smith of artifact weapons able to make new and exciting artifacts all the time, but without destroying the setting expectation for what artifacts are.

                                                            One of my players requested a combination weapon, a spear/firewand/seven section staff:

                                                            The Lashing Dragon’s Tail (***)
                                                            It was the pinnacle creation of an order of warrior monks that trained extensively to use it. It was used previously as the weapon of enforcement by a corrupt king and this is the last remaining one, as the rest were destroyed when his reign ended. It is decorated with alternating red and green patterns, suggesting a dragon's fire and dragon's scales. When the blunt end is spun, it makes a roaring sound. When not in use, it is worn like a bandolier, shoulder to waist, with one end locked into the other. It's also known as an Ouroboros Spear for this appearance.

                                                            Attunement: 5m
                                                            Hearthstone Slot(s): 1

                                                            EVOCATIONS OF THE LASHING DRAGON’S TAIL
                                                            The Lashing Dragon’s Tail is a curious weapon. Superficially it is a serpent-sting staff made of green and red jade, but a closer inspection reveals its secret. One tip of the weapon boasts a lethally sharp spearhead; the other conceals the barrel and firing mechanisms of a firewand. By reflexively spending 1m, the wielder can cause the seven segments of the staff to fuse together in different ways, allowing it to be used as a spear, staff, or firewand. The different configurations are:

                                                            Lashing Tail
                                                            • Type: Medium (+12 damage, +1 Defense, overwhelming 4)
                                                            • Accuracy: +3
                                                            • Tags: Bashing, Martial Arts, Disarming, Flexible, Reaching

                                                            Ouroboros Spear
                                                            • Type: Medium (+12 damage, +1 Defense, overwhelming 4)
                                                            • Accuracy: +3
                                                            • Tags: Lethal, Melee, Thrown (Short), Piercing

                                                            Spine of Eternity
                                                            • Type: Medium (+12 damage, +1 Defense, overwhelming 4)
                                                            • Accuracy: +3
                                                            • Tags: Bashing, Melee, Reaching

                                                            Breath of the Dragon
                                                            • Type: Heavy (+14 damage, overwhelming 5)
                                                            • Accuracy: Close -1, Short +5, Medium +3, Long +1, Extreme -1
                                                            • Tags: Lethal, Archery (Short), Flame, Slow


                                                            ENCIRCLING TAIL
                                                            Cost: 4m; Mins: Essence 1
                                                            Type: Reflexive
                                                            Keywords: Counterattack, Decisive-only
                                                            Duration: Instant
                                                            Prerequisites: None
                                                            The Lashing Tail’s links are quick to catch at enemy limbs and blades. This Evocation allows the wielder to make a disarm or grapple gambit as a counterattack, after the attack is rolled but before damage is rolled. This doesn’t count as her combat action for the round, and every threshold success on the attack roll adds one bonus die on the Initiative roll for the gambit.
                                                            If the Lashing Dragon Tail’s is not in its Lashing Tail conformation, it transforms as part of the counterattack.

                                                            OUROBOROS STRIKE
                                                            Cost: 3m, 1wp; Mins: Essence 1
                                                            Type: Simple
                                                            Keywords: Decisive-only
                                                            Duration: Instant
                                                            Prerequisites: None
                                                            The flight of the Ouroboros Spear cannot be stopped. This Evocation allows you to pierce through one target and strike another. Make a decisive attack against a target at close range. If you hit, roll damage as normal and make a second attack against a second target in close range of the first one. To determine the damage of this second attack, reroll all non-successes from the first damage roll.
                                                            If the Lashing Dragon Tail’s is not in its Ouroboros Strike conformation, it transforms as part of the Simple action.

                                                            ENDURING STANCE TECHNIQUE
                                                            Cost: 2m, 1wp; Mins: Essence 1
                                                            Type: Reflexive
                                                            Keywords: None
                                                            Duration: Instant
                                                            Prerequisites: None
                                                            The Spine of Eternity can absorb and redirect even the strongest of blows. This Evocation grants +1 Parry against one attack. If you successfully block, you may cause your attacker to move one range band in any direction.
                                                            If the Lashing Dragon Tail’s is not in its Spine of Eternity conformation, it transforms as part of the parry.

                                                            BURNING INHALATION
                                                            Cost: 1m; Mins: Essence 1
                                                            Type: Reflexive
                                                            Keywords: Dissonant
                                                            Duration: Instant
                                                            Prerequisites: None
                                                            As the wielder fires the Breath of the Dragon, a breeze draws fresh grains of firedust from her ammunition pouch into its barrel. Activating this Evocation reloads the weapon without an action.
                                                            If the Lashing Dragon Tail’s is not in its Breath of the Dragon conformation, it transforms as well.
