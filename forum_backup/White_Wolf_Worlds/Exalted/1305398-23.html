                            10-28-2019, 09:15 PM
                            When Shining Topaz exalted, she exalted in front of a pair of DBs from Lookshy. When her father refused to disown her, Lookshy declared war with the goal of killing the Anathema that had inhabited Shining Topaz. Terek was in no way prepared to fight a war with Lookshy, and once their field force had made the river trip, the country folded in short order. Within months, the capital was under siege, after barely a week local adherents of the immaculate faith had thrown open the city gates.

                            The five immaculate shikari of the wyld hunt manage to corner Topaz in the palace during the final stages of the assault.


                            -
                            Note: I am assuming a 1pt stunt on everything Topaz does, every roll and every application of a static value.
                            -

                            Join Battle

                            Shining Topaz spends 5m on Sensory Acuity Prana and 10m on the Awareness Excellency. She rolls 24 dice for 16 sux, and starts with 19i.

                            Nefvarin Vita spends 5m on Precision Observation Method to add 2 sux and 1 die to her roll. She rolls 9 dice for 4+2 sux, and starts with 9i.

                            Bounteous Pumice spends 3m on Precision Observation Method to add 3 dice to his roll. He rolls 9 dice for 2 sux, he starts with 5i.

                            Yan Tu Ja spends 5m on Precision Observation Method to add 5 dice to his roll. He rolls 15 dice for 11 sux, he starts with 14i.

                            Terasu Linwei spends 5m on Precision Observation Method to add 5 dice to her roll. She rolls 15 dice for 4 sux, she starts with 7i.

                            Yushoto Nali spends 5m on Precision Observation Method to add 5 dice to her roll. She rolls 13 dice for 4 sux, she starts with 7i.


                            -
                            Round 1

                            Shining Topaz (20/30m, 6/6wp, -0): 19i
                            Yan Tu Ja (29/34m, 6/6wp, -0): 14i
                            Nefvarin Vita (30/35m, 7/7wp, -0): 9i
                            Terasu Linwei (29/34, 7/7wp, -0): 7i
                            Yushoto Nali (30/35m, 6/6wp, -0): 7i
                            Bounteous Pumice (30/33, 5/5wp, -0): 5i

                            Shining Topaz spends 6m, 1wp on Heaven Sword Flash. She supplements this with 4m, 1wp for Thunderbolt Attack Prana, 1m for Divine Executioner Stance to add 4 dice, 6m melee excellency for 6 dice, 3m for Fire and Stones Strike, and 1wp for an automatic sux.

                            Yan Tu Ja spends 6m on Become the Hammer to raise his parry to 9.
                            Nefvarin Vita spends 6m on Become the Hammer to raise her parry to 9.
                            Terasu Linwei spends 6m on Become the Hammer to raise her parry to 9.
                            Yushoto Nali spends 6m on Become the Hammer to raise her parry to 10 and 5m on Wood Dragon Vitality to impose a -1 penalty to the damage roll if Topaz Hits.
                            Bounteous Pumice spends 6m on Become the Hammer to raise his parry to 9 and spends 5m, 1i on Stone Dragon’s Skin to raise his hardness to 11.

                            Shining Topaz rolls 23 dice for 10+1 sux. She hits Ja, Vita, Linwei, and Pumice with 3 threshold sux, and hits Nali with 2 threshold sux.

                            Against Ja. 10 (base)+4(DES)+3(FSS)=17. Topaz rolls 17 dice and deals 8 lethal damage, doubled to 16 by Thunderbolt Attack Prana. Ja is dead.

                            Against Vita. 10 (base)+4(DES)+3(FSS)=17. Topaz rolls 17 dice and deals 9 lethal damage, doubled to 18 by Thunderbolt Attack Prana. Vita is dead.

                            Against Linwei. 10 (base)+4(DES)+3(FSS)=17. Topaz rolls 17 dice and deals 8 lethal damage, doubled to 16 by Thunderbolt Attack Prana. Linwei is dead.

                            Against Nali. 10 (base)+4(DES)+2(FSS)-1 (WDV)=15. Topaz rolls 15 dice and deals 6 damage, doubled to 12 by Thunderbolt Attack Prana. Nali is dead.

                            Against Pumice. 10 (base)+4(DES)+3(FSS)=17. Topaz rolls 17 dice and deals 7 damage, doubled to 14 by Thunderbolt Attack Prana. Pumice lives with 7hl remaining, at wound penalty -2.

                            Shining Topaz resets to 4i.

                            Pumice rolls a rout check against diff 2. He rolls 5 dice for 3 sux, passing.

                            Bounteous Pumice makes a smashing withering attack. He pays 6m for become the hammer and 3m for Force of the Mountain, Topaz pays 1wp for Perfect Strike Discipline to raise her parry to 13. Pumice rolls 16 dice for 8 sux, missing. Pumice enters Earth Aura


                            -
                            Round 2, +5m for all

                            Shining Topaz (5/30m, 2/6wp, -0): 4i
                            Bounteous Pumice (15/33, 5/5wp, -2, Earth): 2i

                            Shining Topaz delays her action to clash with Bounteous Pumice.

                            Topaz and Pumice Clash! Topaz makes a withering attack and spends 1m on Divine Executioner Stance and 3m on Excellent Strike, she also spends 1wp for an automatic sux. Pumice makes a withering attack and spends 6m on Become the Hammer and 3m on Force of the Mountain, he also spends 1wp for an automatic sux.

                            Topaz rolls 19 dice for 9+2 sux and rerolls 1s for 0 additional sux. Total 11 sux.
                            Pumice rolls 16 dice for 5+1 sux. Total 6 sux.
                            Topaz wins with 6 threshold sux.

                            12+6+4 (DES) -16=6. Topaz rolls 6 dice and deals 3+3(clash) withering damage. Putting her at 9i and Pumice at -4i. Topaz gains 5i for crashing Pumice, putting her at 14i.


                            -
                            Round 3, +5m for all

                            Shining Topaz (6/30m, 1/6wp, -0): 14i
                            Bounteous Pumice (11/33, 4/5wp, -2, Earth): -4i

                            Shining Topaz makes a decisive attack. She spends 1m on Divine Executioner Stance to add 4 dice, 3m on Excellent Strike, and 2m on Fire and Stones Strike. Pumice spends 6m on Become the Hammer to raise his parry to 5. Topaz rolls 19 dice for 15+1 sux, hitting with 12 threshold sux.

                            14+4(DES)+2(FSS)=20. Topaz rolls 20 dice and deals 8 damage. Pumice is dead.


                            Her last foe dead on her blade, Shining Topaz collapses to her knees in exhaustion. She has killed five Princes of the Earth in less than a minute and is utterly spent, should any enemies find her in her exhausted state she would be easy pickings. Lucky it is for her, then, that it is not an enemy that finds her.

                            "Sister." Her brother says from an archway.

                            Panting, Shining Topaz leverages herself to her feet. "Gleaming Emerald, what news?"

                            Emerald grimaces, "Father is dead."

                            Topaz sways on her feet for a moment, processing her loss. Then the moment passes, and she falls to one knee. "What are your orders my king?"

                            She feels her brothers hands on her shoulders, and looks up into his eyes.

                            "Topaz, you need to leave. As powerful as you are, you can only be in one place at a time. Terek cannot stand against Lookshy, and they only attack us because they are after you."

                            Topaz swallows, and then nods in understanding. Her brother pulls her into a hug.

                            "I will need to denounce you for Terek's sake, but know that you are my sister and that I love you."

                            King Gleaming Emerald of Terek released the Forsaken Anathema Shining Topaz.

                            "Goodbye brother. Keep Terek safe."

                            "Take the tunnels sister. And stay safe yourself."

                            And so Shining Topaz, the Invincible Sword Princess fled Terek.
