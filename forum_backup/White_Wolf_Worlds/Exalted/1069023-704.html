    See, this has always been one of the things about exalted I've found amusing - because powers are based on some form of personal capacity other than just magical puissance and flavor, you could have someone who can grow a tree into a bow, but who would need to invest a considerable amount of effort in a completely different avenue of skill to learn to just grow a tree, if they're capable of learning to do that at all.

    Have the little folk of the Blessed Isle ever questioned how it is that Terrestrials can learn to grow a tree-bow with their enlightened might, whilst having no idea how to just grow a tree with their enlightened might?
    "But I don't understand!" Burdock complained. "If Mnemon Vayal had to scale the walls of the Anathema's fortress, why didn't she just grow a tree?"

    The itinerant storyteller smiled patiently at the child. "The feats of the Princes of the Earth are mighty indeed, but I have not ever heard of one calling a tree from the ground—except, of course, by planting one and waiting."

    "But last week, you said she turned her bow into a tree of arrows when she fought the Bone-Flute Bandits!"

    "Well of course she did. Mnemon Vayal is a master archer, who's held a bow in her hands since she was even younger than you are now, child. She knows them so well that it'd be easy as anything for her to whisper a word that makes a bow grow into a tree with deep roots and strong branches. But calling up a tree out of nothing is an entirely different thing."

                                    To better combat the dreaded Count of Words, my Microsoft Word proofreader has a suggestion:

                                    Replace almost every instance of the phrase "as long as" with the single word "if". Most of the time, the phrase is used to refer to a single instant of effect rather than an ongoing effect which would warrant the words "for as long as".

                                    For example: "IF the user is not Dissonant with this material, this Evocation will unlock when such a thing happens".
