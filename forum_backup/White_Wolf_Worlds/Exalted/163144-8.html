    Gifts Ungiven Prana
    Solar Lore (prerequisites include Power-Awarding Prana)
    A wise Lawgiver knows how to gift a mortal a spark of the Sun's own light and thereby light a flame. A still wiser Lawgiver knows how to ignite the fires of heroism by denying it. Setting a hand on a mortal, she wills a flood of Solar Essence to torrent through him, but only for a few brief moments. With a smile, she conveys to him that he does not need that power to be a hero, only his own strength of heart and will, and that he may even someday turn the head of the Most High.

    Filled with certainty in the truth of it and still warm from the afterglow, he acquires a Major Intimacy of self-confidence. So long as it lasts, he receives a point of Willpower when facing adversity, and may channel Willpower for 2 successes in the scene in which he does so.

        Expanding Void Sun
        Cost: 77m, 3wp; Circle: Adamant; Type: Simple (Dramatic Action)
        Keywords: Area Effect, Component, Obvious
        Duration: Until the orb is consumed

        To begin this spell, the sorcerer first requires a orb of pure tin. Upon this orb he must inscribe the seventy-seven Runes of Eternity, which describe the wheel of ages and the cycle of birth and betrayal that rules all things. Each rune must have one mote imbedded within it, causing it to pulse rythmically with power.         Originally posted by Gaius View Post
        King Becomes Princeling Trick
        Sidereal Larceny, building off of Name-Pilfering Practice

        Threads of fate are intertwined, and by pulling one may another follow. While the Sidereal has stolen someone's name with Name-Pilfering Practice, he may continue to pull on the thread of their fate, dislodging their reputation and social standing. This effect is a gradual shift in the world's perception, a lowering of grade; a king might become a princeling, or a master tradesman a journeyman. A priest seems to become an acolyte, or a farmer becomes a farmhand. Each stacked use of this Charm upon the same person steps them down even further: the princeling becomes a noble, or a journeyman a novice. This Charm doesn't affect their skills, just what everyone perceives the person as.

                            A Quiver Full of Sorrows

                            CMA, Needles of the 7 Despairs Style (form weapons: Arrows, Javelins, Needles, String*)

                            The martial artist strikes his target with a flat palm over the heart and rolls what would otherwise be a normal social Investigation action to reveal intimacies. On a success, he pinpoints his adversary's deepest disappointments in himself and pulls them out, one per extra success, to form them into long thin needles that can be used as any mundane (albeit of exceptional quality) Form weapon for this style, adding a bonus of +2 accuracy and the piercing tag. Alternatively, the martial artist can enhance a weapon in her posession with the provided bonuses by reflexively coating it with the extracted sorrow.

                            Each successful attack with such a weapon that dealt at last 1 level of damage creates an inviolable sorcerous intimacy for the rest of the scene that restricts the target's actions unless she spends 1 point of willpower per such intimacy.
                            (e.g. The target might be reminded of his long-lost brother, whom he failed to rescue from slavery and seems to have a lot in common with the Martial Artist (though this need not actually be the case) and so is restricted from attacking the martial artist while looking at him)

                            * a pre-requisite charm allows string and string-like things (such as hair) to be made solid as needles.

                            But Sexually.
                            Terrestrial Fire-Aspected Presence.

                            The Dragonblooded are known for their heated passions and larger-than-life desires. This charm rouses such intensity even in the common mortal. By teasing a glimpse of any portion of their body that could be considered sexually inviting and making it appear as the perfect combination of warmth, tenderness and strength, the Dragonblood fans the flames of existing desire into spectacular bonfires. For as long as motes remain commited, any action the target takes in the pursuit of satisfying the Dragonblooded's desires receives a bonus equal to the Exalt's Presence + Relevant Specialty. Every time the target gets a threshold of at least 5 successes on a task, they regain a point of willpower which ignores normal limits. The limit for willpower points gained this way becomes [Target's Willpower Rating + Exalt's chosen Specialty].

                            However, this charm may not create desires where none exist, it can only enhance existing emotions.

                                            Originally posted by Fata-Ku View Post
                                            Nexus of Five Heartbreaks
                                            Sidereal Thrown/Resistance: A super prayer strip Charm that requires two prayer strip Charms from different Abilities as prereqs, this one, building off of Storm's Eye Stance and Unrelenting Torment Technique.

                                            The Sidereal activates this Charm with a prayer strip which has the Scripture of the Woeful Maiden written upon it. She rips it into five strips, stating a name each time she does; each tear flashes like a yellow lightning bolt. Then the Exalt ties these strips together, binding the fates of these five people into an inexorable knot of woe. Each victim gains an inviolate sorcerous intimacy toward another that will disrupt their life and cause heartbreak and strife, along with a destiny to bring these people into contact. The Storyteller is encouraged to mix up the intimacies so that the most drama and pain will occur by their own doings.

                                            Trouble brews for the Sidereal's victims, and the Sidereal holds the knot of fate that ties them all together. While she holds the bundle of prayer strips, the victims are treated as bound by Storm's Eye Stance, and may be attacked as if with Unrelenting Torment Technique without requiring the Sidereal to activate either of those Charms.

                                            Finally, the Sidereal may yank out one of the torn strips from her knot, climatically releasing that victim's woe; in the next scene, that victim suffers a botch relating to the sorcerous intimacy of the Exalt's choosing. At that point, the victim is released from the Charm, and the sorcerous intimacy becomes mundane.

                                            Bonus scripture text!

                                            The Scripture of the Woeful Maiden
                                            Once, there was a maiden…
                                            …who was full of sorrows.
                                            She tried to run away, only to stumble into more trouble.
                                            "Why do you cry?" the trouble asked.
                                            "Running is all I know," said she.

                                                        Solar Linguistics
                                                        The Lawgiver's prose and brushstrokes flow with gripping elegance and readers find themselves swept along by them, turning page after page. When a Solar supplements a piece of writing with this Charm, she rolls (Linguistics + X). If her successes exceed A, the work instills a Minor Intimacy to read the end of the piece in anyone who reads even a bit of it (a sentence or two suffices).
