                                Originally posted by Jen View Post
                                Can different people with Tiger Warrior Training Technique use the charm on the same unit at the same time ?
                                I would have to say no. It is not Stackable, which would allow the Solar to use the same charm again on the same "Unit," which to me precludes multiple uses for Stackability purposes. If it's not Stackable for the same Solar, in this case, I don't think it would be Stackable for another Solar. (My Opinion)

                                Of course, that is a "Game Mechanic" take.

                                From a strictly training point-of-view...I know the Charm says "requires five or more hours of effort in any given week to bear fruit," but it's duration is One Week - which, to me means that the Solar is spending time every day training with the unit, especialy if "The Solar can train with the unit or as a solo unit, increasing her own traits." Discounting the time it requires to train in a normal day - say 8 hours a day (Especially if the Solar is Training with them to increase his/her own traits...) - with Valor, Strength, Dexterity, Stamina, Archery, Dodge, Martial Arts or Melee, the unit is going to need time for logistics, to prepare the area, clean-up the area (and camp), travel to/from the area, eat, conduct any other "normal" training and relax/rest up -- say 6-8 hours. Count another 6-8 hours for sleep and you have 20-24 hours accounted for.

                                Again...that is my Opinion...and you do know what they say about Opinions, right?

                                        Originally posted by Citadel97501 View Post
                                        I had a question regarding the Perronele vs. holy attacks. If you attack a Creature of Darkness wearing a Perronele does he get the soak benefit of the Perronele's soak boosting Charm?
                                        That's a really interesting question!

                                        GI cannot think of any real precedent here, but given the crazy power and utility of Demon Summoning in general, I wold probably rule that it gets peeled off of you like the Venom Symbiote in a churchbell.

                                        That said, between Perroneles and Hell-Forged Artifacts and things of that nature, the writers have had ample chances to write about demons not providing other creatures Soak against Holy attacks, and they have never done so to my knowledge. So, by the rules as written, the Perronele works like always (unless the one wearing it is also a Creature of Darkness, in which case only Aggravated Soak will do).
