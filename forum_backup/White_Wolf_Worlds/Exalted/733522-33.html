                        Originally posted by Accelerator View Post
                        Woah. Tell me more.
                        Fielding this belatedly.

                        The Forest Witches are a bunch of supremely odd Outcaste DBs. Their little slice of Creation is a spot where a First Age Solar butchered her handmaiden in a fit of pique, stringing her entrails through the trees, setting her eyes in flowers, bracing roots with her bones and so on.

                        Because life and death function oddly in that place, that woman is still alive after a fashion. She's about as close to a sentient forest as one can reasonably get, and she manifests little miracles for her friends, which usually involve some combination of greenery and meat.

                        As to the actual working? I'd say a tree that grows organs would be Terrestrial 3 or Celestial 1, depending on how you interpret "create an entirely new but mundane form of life". I'd be inclined toward Terrestrial 3, since making weird chimerical animals is within the Terrestrial circle.

                        A tree that happens to grow hearts rather than fruit is just taking two things from the natural world and smacking them together in the same way that breeding an owlbear is.

                        Good luck actually finding a surgeon in the Age of Sorrows that can perform organ transplants without killing people, but if you're a Twilight who thinks Medicine and Occult are two great tastes that go great together, a grove of meat trees would be Exceptional Equipment for certain tasks.

                        Or, y'know, maybe it just happens to be medically useful (for extremely gifted surgeons) but it wasn't intended to be. How else are you going to teach your society of mutant tiger-men about the art of agriculture?
