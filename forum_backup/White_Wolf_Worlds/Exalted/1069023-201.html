                                Not a DEV:

                                1. No, you can't reuse something that is committed to something else. You can commit other experience to fuel the Charm. Though at the end of the story you roll all committed exp, so you will eventually recover all your exp if you stop using the Charm. But if you use it every story you create a circular pool of committed exp, which is not too shabby if you keep in mind that with the right Charms you are able to win back more exp than what you put in.

                                                            Originally posted by Iron Phoenix View Post
                                                            Hi Robert,

                                                            How does Initiative Shift work when it occurs outside of your turn order?

                                                            1) I go on tick 5 and my opponent goes on tick 2. I take my turn for the round. On his turn, he attacks me and crashes me. He then uses a reflexive Charm that allows him to attack again immediately upon crashing someone. He swings with a decisive and misses. Unbowed Willow Meditation triggers and crashes him, giving me Initiative Shift. Do I take a second turn on the following tick, based on the rules for changing Initiative on page 192?
                                                            Yeah, I think that's a reasonable way to handle it.

                                                            2) My opponent goes on tick 5 and I go on tick 2. He crashes me and then uses a Charm for a reflexive decisive. He misses, and I crash him via Unbowed Willow Meditation. This resets my turn, but I haven't yet taken my turn for the round, so I assume I lose all of those benefits and simply proceed with my turn as normal on the following tick, using the same rules on page 192.
                                                            In this case, I think you'd get a "free" turn you can use to attack him immediately, and then take your normal turn based on your (new) Initiative rating. You shouldn't miss out on the benefits of Initiative Shift just because you're using something funky to crash a guy.
