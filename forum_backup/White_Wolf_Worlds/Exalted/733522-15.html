                                    One thing to note is that in 2e, a walkaway talisman didn't absolutely guarantee survival - it simply removed one success on a potentially-lethal damage roll (or 2 or 3 successes for a particularly strong talisman). So completely negating, or even guaranteeing you your last -4 HL, is definitely more powerful.

                                    I'd argue that this power level is definitely working-level, not just thaumaturgy. In fact, I'd peg this at Celestial. It's really a form of immortality, just with some pretty sharp limits. I'd put this at Celestial Ambition 2, because it sounds like it's granting a useful supernatural power to yourself.
