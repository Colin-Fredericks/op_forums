    Grimoire of the Primal Song

    "It is said by the Eldar that in water there lives yet the echo of the Music of the Ainur more than in any substance else that is in this Earth; and many of the Children of Ilúvatar hearken still unsated to the voices of the Sea, and yet know not for what they listen." -J.R.R. Tolkien, Silmarillion

    It sang through Creation, and it sang of creation in refrains too faint for ears to hear, patterns too subtle to be seen, reverberations that carried traces of the first and foremost act of Creation. There was a man of great learning and wisdom - and possibly madness; through dark science he recovered a fragment of the universal song. He wrote down what he could determine of its strains, its stanzas, its choruses and its refrains, its shifts to red and its shifts to blue. Written upon hardened bandages and bound in behemoth-skin, the result looks absolutely nothing like a song, for it dips between aspects of reality at will. Why would one call the movement of a universe a song, anyway? Perhaps there was no song which birthed Creation and the man was just a crazy person, but if so the tangled web of his madness alone is sufficient to tell the laws of Being to sit down and giddy-up. Would that be more impressive or less?

    The Grimoire is badly damaged, pieces and parts of the song incomplete or missing. Pieces of the text continually go missing and reappear again years or decades later, a whole page becoming torn-out when the book is opened again and vice-versa, suggesting that its damage is much more than physical. Nonetheless, sufficient study of its ways and patterns is more than sufficient to initiate a man into the higher mysteries of sorcery.

    The sorcerer may draw upon the power of surrounding music, letting it flow across his spellwork and give definition to it. Whenever she takes a Shape Sorcery action, ambient music grants her up to (Essence) sorcerous motes for the rest of the scene. No more than (Essence)*5 sorcerous motes can be gathered thus during a scene.

    The solidity and attunement to the framework of Creation allows the sorcerer a keener grasp on what lies outside it. When she Joins Battle against a being with an origin outside Creation, she gains a number of sorcerous motes equal to her successes on the Join Battle roll, to a maximum of 3+(Essence).

    Merit 3: Fortissimo - The fragment of the song allows manipulation of the fine aspects of Creation in dynamic (but ephemeral) ways. Creation-born being as eminently practical as they are, the book's bearers have occasionally decided to shape these ephemeral shifts in reality into weapons. The sorcerer might manifest ice knives, wind-blades, rob the edge from a man's sword to strike him with it or turn a man's fear into a ravenous monster - for a moment. In concrete terms, the sorcerer can create light mundane throwing weapons with the Thrown (Medium), Lethal, Mounted characteristics. These do aggravated damage to faeries and other creatures of the Wyld, as it assails them with the raw Stuff of Reality.

    Merit 4: Composition - This shaping ritual benefit can only be purchased by a being with the Wyld-Shaping Technique charm or some equivalent. As the sorcerer shapes the raw unreality of the Wyld, it echoes with the essence of the First Creation. The sorcerer enjoys double 9s on all rolls to Wyld-shape lands or demesnes and adds one success to them, ensuring that whatever else happens the land of the Wyld will not turn on the sorcerer. The resulting land is rawer and fresher, with very large plants and animals (if appropriate) and an exceptional levity in the chest upon breathing its air.

    Merit 5: Bridge - They made doorways. Bridges, to other strains. To other parts. Did they know? Could they know? The sorcerer gains mastery of Distance, allowing them to perceive doorways wrought in the stuff of Creation. Most of these are too small to send a pin through, much less an animal, much less a person. Every now and then one is large enough to move through. The sorcerer may automatically perceive gates to Yu-Shan, to Malfeas, to Zen-Mu and to the Underworld, and once per story may teleport up to 1,000 miles with a specialized Rush action that uses Intelligence+Occult. He may specify a direction but not a specific destination, for the bridges are ever-shifting. Taking a doorway is never straightforwardly fatal, but the other end may not necessarily lie in Creation...

                                                During shape sorcery, spellcaster may recite the words of power that materialize in her mind.

                                                            Hello there! I am in the process of making my first Exalted character in the 3rd edition and I decided to homebrew some areas of the character (such as artifacts and Shaping Ritual). I did a small internet search to see what was out there (and I shamelessly borrowed heavily from one (enlightenment of the Imperfect lotus)) but none was close to what I envisioned from my char, so I combined many ideas and made a new one myself. Please critic and find any problems/solutions. Would you allow it in one of your games?

                                                            BATTLEFORGED ASCENSION

                                                            Life is a continuous battle, existence is a continuous battle. Furthermore it doesn’t matter if you won or lost; what matters is that you fought, learned and survived. This is the first truth one has to comprehend upon which his eyes open to new horizons and his initiation to sorcery can begin. Yours is a sorcery rooted on the enlightenment of the Dao of battle.

                                                            Shaping Rituals

                                                            The most challenging enemy is always oneself. The sorcerer knows that he would rather conquer his self that win a thousand battles. Once per day the Sorcerer may enter a deep brief meditation lasting 15 minutes. During this meditation he enters an imaginary battle with himself. The outcome isn’t important but the enlightenment gained is. He may roll (Wits + Occult) and gain one sorcerous mote per success. These motes last until the next time she sleeps, and can be spent towards any spell she casts. If the sorcerer spends only sorcerous motes granted by the meditation casting her control spell, each counts as two motes towards meeting the spell’s cost.

                                                            The sorcerer finds hidden meaning and a chance to improve himself in any battle as he struggles to bring success against all odds. Whenever the sorcerer strikes with a withering attack he may opt to covert the initiative he would have gained into half as many (round up) sorcerous motes instead. Sorcerous motes gained through this ritual fade at the battle’s end (when the combatants leave Initiative).

                                                            All Battleforged sorcerers know that when facing death it’s the moment they most feel alive. The pure instinct to survive, the adrenaline from evading a death blow, the surge of essence as they try to endure a strike, they all become a source of power for them. Whenever the sorcerer successfully dodge, block or completely soaks an attack from a non-trivial opponent he gains 1 sorcerous mote .If the sorcerer spends only sorcerous motes granted by avoiding or successfully absorbing an attack, casting her control spell, each counts as two motes towards meeting the spell’s cost. Sorcerous motes gained through this ritual fade at the battle’s end (when the combatants leave Initiative).

                                                            Other Benefits

                                                            Tame Ones Fears (Merit ••••): The sorcerer has learned to battle his fears and instead of numbing his thoughts and action it helps his fuel them. Rather that becoming immune to fear instill actions the sorcerer overcomes them despite being frighten. If an opponent has succeeded in a fear instill action, roll (Wits + Integrity) against a difficulty set by the opponents essence. If you succeed it counts as if you had spent willpower to negate the effect. Furthermore you gain the opponents success on the attempt as sorcerous motes or essence motes your choice.

                                                            Loyal Armament Endowment (Merit •••••): The sorcerer has fought many battles together with his loyal artifacts overcoming the boundaries of the self and becoming one with them. Whenever he wants to activate an evocation of an artifact he owns and has used for at least a story he may pay part or the whole essence cost with sorcerous motes instead.
