                                                    Originally posted by prototype00 View Post
                                                    For the purpose of Flawlessly Impenetrable Disguise and similar effects that can't be defeated by "mundane means" does application of an Excellency, in this case Awareness, say, count as a mundane or supernatural mean?
                                                    In the case of Flawlessly Impenetrable Disguise the rest of the paragraph clarifies the intention considerably, but in general you can probably assume that a run-of-the-mill roll remains "mundane" no matter how many extra dice and successes are added to it.
