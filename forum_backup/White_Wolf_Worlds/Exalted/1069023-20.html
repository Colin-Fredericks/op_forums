                                Originally posted by Robert Vance View Post
                                Currently, there are no plans for this (Abyssals and maybe Infernals being the only likely exception).
                                When I start running mixed Exalted game, I feel as though I would be obligated to either remove Supernal from Solar characters or introduce a similar mechanic for other Exalted. Solar Charms and powers already put them a step ahead of the other Exalted, and adding Supernal into the mix feels like it would let Solars move several steps ahead of other Exalted.

                                Having one Solar PC starting out able to access Essence 4 or 5 Charms, while other PCs are stuck with Essence 1 Charms brings back memories of 2nd edition where Solar PC's tended to make the players of other Exalted types feel like sidekicks tagging along with the "real" hero.

                                Hopefully, even if other Exalted don't get anything like Supernal, they're designed to get something that helps keep the gap between a Solar player character and a player character of a different splat from feeling impossibly wide.
