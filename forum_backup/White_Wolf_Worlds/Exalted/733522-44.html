        The mountain-tugging stallion (Celestial circle, Ambition 2)

        A bizarre looking horse, with a mane of shining silver and hooves that screech out lightning as it runs, whether on air, water, or stone. Average-sized but with a presence like that of lightning, the stallion was made by a lunar sorcerer for his dignitaries and ambassadors, as they complained of how their trips to other nations took a long time through hazardous conditions.

        Essence: 5, Essence pool: 80

        Attributes: Stamina 7, Strength 7, Appearance 3, Perception 3, Intelligence 2, Wits 3
        Abilities: Athletics 6 (+3 in a straight line), Awareness 3, Dodge 5, Integrity 2 (+3 to serve my master), Resistance 4, Survival 4

        Merits
        Home in any place: The stallion gains no damage from extreme environmental conditions. Note that this applies only to things like hail, rainstorms, or blizzards. The Stallion will die if struck by an avalanche, or hit by lava

        Dragging them along for a ride: The Stallion can carry people upon its back, or drag a carriage behind it. The structure and construction of the carriage is irrelevant, as long as there is some physical connection like a piece of rope or reigns.

        Fast as hell: The horse's travel speed is 100 miles per hour. Same as stormwind rider

        Charms:
        Anyplace, anytime (10m)
        The horse gets no difficulty from terrain or even the lack of it. It can travel upon water, snow, forest, or air, the same as solid stone. This charm lasts for

        Reinforcement (15m)
        The horse creates fortified spiritual armor about itself, and its passengers. Add 10 soak to bashing and lethal, and 5 hardness for bashing and lethal to both the horse and whatever is attached to it. If the carriage the horse pulls is open-aired, the armour manifests as shimmering transparent fields.

        Landscape travel (10m)
        The horse doubles movement speed for a scene, whether to escape pursuit or to capture a fleeing prisoner.

        A/N: This is probably terrible. A construct meant to carry moderately large amounts of cargo, a small entourage of dignitaries, or a circle of exalted across Creation without slow travel, harassment by bandits, or having to get your own sorcerer.

            I do like the idea. I had been considering ideas for a sorcerously-created super horse myself, so this is interesting to see. Perhaps I'll write one up a bit later.

            The Stallion really needs actual combat traits to be properly evaluated; even if it's not supposed to be able to fight by itself at all, it certainly needs a dice pool for combat movement and a speed bonus (and soak, Evasion, health levels etc.). The Landscape Travel charm should probably have an alternate function for how it works in combat or tests of speed, too.

            What does the "Dragging Them Along for a Ride" merit actually do? What can the Stallion drag that a normal horse couldn't?

            Anyway, I'm a little torn. I don't think the Mountain-Tugging Stallion is quite on par with a Second Circle demon, but its speed (200 mph with Landscape Travel!) and +10 soak bonus for itself and its passengers are downright amazing benefits. Honestly, this guy would be wasted on lugging a carriage around when he could be carrying an Exalted horseman into battle.

            I guess I'd agree with Celestial Ambition 2 if I had to make a decision, but if I were GMing for you I'd probably suggest downgrading that soak bonus, or coming up with an alternative mechanic for the Stallion to protect its passengers. (Maybe it can reflexively Defend Other for its passengers or something?)

            I think allowing sorcerously-created minions that can effectively buff the PCs themselves, like with this soak bonus, could get out of hand quickly.

                                    03-10-2019, 02:39 PM
                                    I like it. It's powerful, but I don't think it's any more powerful than Octavian overall.

                                    Her Charms should probably have Essence costs. I would say "Beautiful Idol" should cost Essence as well -- being able to create a Major intimacy all in one shot is a powerful effect. (Dragon-Blooded can do that once per story at the absolute apex of their Presence charm tree -- Solars don't have any way to do that at all, although perhaps they should.)

                                    I think "Connections to the Pawns" is a little excessive. I like the idea of her being able to communicate with her pawns at any distance, but total mind-reading is probably too much. Her ability to influence things at a distance should be limited by the agents she actually has in place -- she shouldn't be able to do all of their thinking for them.

                                    "Obey Your Queen" seems kind of redundant -- does she really need the extra -3 when she can casually give people Major Ties to herself, and has superhuman social dice pools besides?

                                    As for the actual wish-granting effects: my suggestion would be to keep it simple. How about this: her blessing can give the target up to (her Essence), i.e. 7 dots in improved Attributes, and one Attribute dot can be traded for two dots in Abilities and/or Merits. This could turn someone mediocre into someone fairly impressive in their field. (Someone who gains a bunch of innate Merits is probably going to be assumed to be God-Blooded by most casual observers.)

                                    Do her beneficiaries lose the benefit of their wishes if they turn against her, or are the upgrades permanent?

                                    For large-scale effects: the 3e rules for leadership and projects are really vague. Maybe her blessing can grant a leader double 8s on rolls aimed at overcoming the failure conditions of a major project?
