                                                        I think it'd be interesting to have a kind of especially enraged warform or beast form which was difficult to control, but unimaginably dangerous. Kind of like if you're a gigantic burly dude who's also a werewolf. You can fight off ten other guys in your human form, but when an army of fey goblins come swarming towards the town you can transform into a monster from the primordial wild of the world, the kind of fanged, clawed, furred beast that spawns legends around campfires and keeps people awake at night. The kind of predator that stalks you even in your dreams and makes you wake in a cold sweat, desperately hoping that low growl was just in your head and not prowling outside your door.

                                                        It shouldn't be all war forms or all transformations, but I definitely think there should be an option to become an unfettered hunter of all things. I'm not sure how you'd do it properly, obviously just putting in a clause to kill everything friend or foe is bad in a million ways, but it could maybe have some danger to it. Like if you had a peony blossom alike you couldn't exclude people from it, and you couldn't pull your blows at all, If you choose to unleash that thing inside you the only way the scene is going to end is you drenched in blood while kneeling on a giant pile of bodies, screaming your victory to the sky.

                                                        Originally posted by Sunder the Gold View Post
                                                        But just as war forms and Evocations can't be used together, traditionally SMA and lots of lesser Styles have been incompatible with plate armor. That's not likely to change. Yet at the same time, there are Styles which allow full armor, and Sidereal Melee, Brawl, Archery and Thrown don't care about armor at all.
                                                        There should definitely be an option to wear armor, but Sidereals more than anyone should be given pathways that allow them to get away with not wearing it. Kind of like how Dragonblooded don't have any resistance charms that are incompatible with armor or that have enough boosted soak to replace armor. DBs, in general, need armor. They don't always always have it, but it's a damn good idea and it helps give them a certain look, if somebody mentions a DB you can expect that if they're smart they'll be running around in some kind of armor, and if somebody mentions a Sidereal you can expect them to be wearing anything but...and still kick your ass harder for it.
