                                    Anyway, there are probably ways to finesse it without it being a huge problem. Most of your "nimble as a monkey" charms probably benefit from having high dexterity, which you will in monkey-form. If one or two charms are redundant with your monkey abilities, it isn't the end of the world as long as you have other charms that work well with that.

                                    I am, in fact, rather against being able to use Charms and such in forms where it would be or look utterly ridiculous. I don't want silver houseflies cartoonishly snapping an armored Dynast's neck by slamming into him hard enough. I don't want silver snails easily outpacing a goddamned racehorse. Certain options should, must face the limitations of the form you constrain them in!
                                    I would imagine that the charms that care about your attributes would work that way. If you have a charm that does something based on your dexterity, it probably won't work well in snail form.

                                    But it's not necessarily an either-or thing; there can be some charms that don't care about your form, especially when they're intended to be obviously unnatural. So there might be eg. some Lunar stealth charms that care about your size (or ones intended specifically for when you're tiny), and some that explicitly allow you to ignore it. The Exalted can do impossible things; if a Solar can leap over a mountain, a Lunar can sneak up on them even in the form of a tiger the size of a house. The important thing is doing it when it's cool, which means deciding on a charm-by-charm basis and writing the charms that allow it in a way that sells what you're trying to do.
