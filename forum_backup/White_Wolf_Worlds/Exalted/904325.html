                          Thrown has: An accuracy booster [permitting range benefits as if one step closer], an opening decisive attack against a slow opponent to injure or kill them, a JB enhancer, a penalty inflicter, a cover-negator, a range-extender, a full excellency boost that negates dodge and damages everything around the target, another JB enhancer which grants [Dexterity] attacks, a silencing attack, a stealthy attack, a couple distract or disarm boosters, a stealthy attack boost, an AOE attack, a situational base initiative booster, a reflexive attack after the stealthy attack, another distract booster, a decisive attack booster, a distract booster that adds a disarm, a reflexive attack after disarming, an ambush booster, a decisive attack that is boosted by anima and harms demons, ghosts, and other 'creatures of the night', and a multiple attacker charm.

                          Roughly 4/24 [silencing, stealthy, reflexive post-stealthy, and ambush booster], yeah, it's 100% only good for stealthy sneaky assassins and has no value whatsoever to someone focusing on Disarm and Distract gambits [trick shots] or just generally being the first to attack.

                          Survival is heavily pet-based, but there's still more non-pet charms in it than there were in 2e.

                          Lore is very heavy on the Wyld-Shaping, could use other stuff.

                          I also get the feeling that Brawl's deliberate lack of easy, routine defenses was a stylistic decision; you could create homebrew defensive charms for it but by raw it seems like it's heavily leaning towards all-out offense compared to Melee's balanced approach

                                                          Archery: Little support for non-bow Archery weapons.
                                                          Athletics: Not much in the flexibility/contortionist area of Athletics.
                                                          Awareness: is 90% random ways to add dice, rather than actual capability expansion (the fluffy descriptions don't really help for implementation.)
                                                          Brawl: Light on Defense (expected), light on crippling/maiming actions (unexpected).
                                                          Bureaucracy: Is all about markets/dealing and "leading." Can't say it really has a gap, because it doesn't have much of a system to plug into.
                                                          Craft: Charm set is fanatically devoted to clicking cookies, with very little utlity or "can-do" added on.
                                                          Dodge: Narrow, but uh...it's Dodge? Working as intended IMHO.
                                                          Integrity: Still a bit of a grab-bag, but surprisingly light on simple Resolve/Guile boosting, overall.
                                                          Investigation: You're Sherlock Holmes. No, really, that's your character's concept, stop having ideas.
                                                          Linguistics: Has very little to do with languages.
                                                          Lore: As others have said, pretty light on charms actually related to the subject at hand. Creation's greatest scholars are apparently mote batteries.
                                                          Medicine: "I'm House without the 40 minutes of being wrong every episode," and that's it. No neat powers, no new avenues of setting interaction.
                                                          Occult: Apparently the only use for Occult is beating/imprisoning/eating spirits. Who knew.
                                                          Performance: Assumes a highly limited definition of the word Performance, and pays for it.
                                                          Resistance: Few non-combat applications of Resistance.
                                                          Survival: alias Survival="Pokemaster"
                                                          Socialize: Little support for manipulating or changing cultural norms (which is something Solars do).
                                                          Thrown: There have already been multiple threads on the problem.
                                                          War: Train troops and be a superb strategist, but next to zero support outside of it. War got short-changed pretty hard.
