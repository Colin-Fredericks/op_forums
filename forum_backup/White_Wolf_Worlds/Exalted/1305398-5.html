            Barebones writeup of a murderkitty build:
            Attributes
            Dex 5/Strength 3+

            Abilities: Brawl 5, Dodge 5, Probably With Specialties

            Merits: Artifacts (Moonsilver Chain Shirt, Moonsilver Smashfist)

            Cat form (Underfoot Menace)

            Charms - This depends on how many you want to give, but my ordering would be:
            Very Good: Last Warrior's Unity, Agile Beast Defense, Golden Tiger Stance,
            Good: Ferocious Biting Tooth, Prey Hobbling Bite, Coiled Serpent Strikes, Finding the Needle's Eye, Striking Mospid Method, Mighty Ram Smash, Sinuous Striking Grace, Cunning Beast-Warrior Instinct, Wasp Sting Blur, Quicksilver Second Face
            Essence 2: Fleet Gazelle Exercise, Sensing the Deadly Flow, Unerring Fang Technique (Ambush Predator+Underfoot Menace+Not stunting for an attribute makes this easy to hit)

            I think that Last Warrior's Unity is better than Stalking Shadow Hunter+Claws of the Silver Moon (Damage is 10+str/3 vs. 15/1+str, but accuracy is 5 vs 2)

            The character ends up using nearly entirely their own stats, except taking advantage of ambush hunter, underfoot menace, and tiny creature for a constant +3 attack, +2 evasion. And protean benefits. And if they have stamina 1 they can replace their soak with the cat's 2.

                        Thank you, very much!

                        1. This is going to be a kind of crappy answer but it's worth it if you think it's worth it. It will give you an extra parry and 2 extra dice, which could make the difference especially at high power combat, on the other hand they might not make much of a difference at all, and if you can spend those 4bp doing really fun things instead, I'd definitely say do that.

                        2. Lack of health levels is a big one, but i think his defense is pretty good. The DBs were taking min damage just because they couldn't reliably boost their damage up to the level that it tanks through that level of soak, especially with the post attack soak booster, so they boosted their Overwhelming damage instead, which they have artifacts and several charms for. I actually wouldn't really say that Membe is weak at all, there is no shame in losing a 3 on 1 against anathema hunting Exalted. That loss was in no way a failure to make a good and powerful character.
