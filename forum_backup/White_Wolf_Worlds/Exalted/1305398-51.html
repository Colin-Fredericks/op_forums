                                                        Okay, this next post is for habitableexoplanet.

                                                        It is not my intent to insult or belittle you or your opinions. We have different opinions, and I'm going to explain why. I'm aware that the tone here could be read as a little harsh: this is not my intent, but I'm not eloquent enough to phrase my argument in a manner which isn't blunt. I really enjoyed reading your post, and you make a good case. I ask that you read what follows in the friendliest, most charitable way that you can imagine.

                                                        But long story short: I disagree with everything you just said. Here's why:

                                                        Originally posted by habitableexoplanet View Post

                                                        Is it, game-mechanically?
                                                        Yes. It's huge.

                                                        6 dice averages 3 successes, and hits 2 successes (challenging fare for heroes with a complication) 81% of the time. With a Full Excellency you hit 5 successes 73% of the time and hit Parry 8 (Dex 5+Melee 5+Specialty+Stunt+Medium Weapon) 48% of the time.
                                                        10 dice averages 5 successes, and hits 2 succcesses 96% of the time. It hits 5 successes 57% of the time, rising to 97% of the time with a Full Excellency. It hits Parry 8 (Dex 5+Melee 5+Specialty+Stunt+Medium Weapon) 89% of the time.

                                                        Scores of 3 make you obviously talented, and capable enough to dominate a kingdom. The rules of the game reflect this, both in terms of static difficulty and the difficulty of NPCs.
                                                        Scores of 5 make you the best in the world.

                                                        The hardest, Difficulty 5, Feats can be overcome by Exalted with scores of 3. But to do so, they need to spend Essence. So if you have to leap a bottomless pit, then shrug off the effect of poison fog, then heal your badly wounded ally, then (when you fight the Mini-Boss at the end of the dungeon) you enter combat down a bunch of motes. And there's a decent chance that if four(ish) PCs are each rolling against three(ish) challenges back-to-back then someone is going to fail, adding in real danger (and Wound Penalties during that Boss Fite). When you face one of the most dangerous foes in Creation, fully half your attacks miss when you go all-out.

                                                        With scores of 5... not so much. You breeze through the dungeon, not bothering to spend motes on the easier tasks and with no chance of failure on the hard tasks. Then when you get to the boss fight, you chunk them in 2 rounds, because even though they're supposed to be one of the most powerful combatants in the world you're hitting them 90% of the time.

                                                        Then you consider the Charms you might not qualify for if only running at 3 instead of 5, and how that limits your tactical choices.

                                                        Then you multiply that by all the characters in the Circle.

                                                        ~4 PCs rolling ~6 dice against an assassin's Stealth attempt is probably going to succeed.
                                                        ~4 PCs rolling ~10 dice against that isn't rolling to see if they spot her, but who spots her first.

                                                        And there's nothing wrong with that. Exalted is fun because it allows this. But a game where most PCs have most relevant stats at 5 is a very different game from the default, intended powerlevel.

                                                        Originally posted by habitableexoplanet View Post
                                                        For a lot of noncombat checks, 3 Attribute + 3 Ability + full Excellency is plenty, and mote efficiency isn't an issue.
                                                        If mote efficiency isn't an issue and failure isn't probable, I suggest not rolling. You succeed. Enjoy being awesome.

                                                        Originally posted by habitableexoplanet View Post
                                                        Also if you don't have a Session 0
                                                        Without ill-will or sarcasm: good luck playing Exalted.

                                                        Originally posted by habitableexoplanet View Post
                                                        Also if you don't have a Session 0 where players agree not to take a bunch of 5s
                                                        Exalted literallty walks you through, step-by-step, how to make a character. A properly terrifying Solar assassin, has a single Attribute at 5 and only two Abilities above 3 -- Stealth and Melee 4. Exalted also walks you through how to make NPCs: it says keep their dice pools below 11 "so that players do not feel forced to maximize their stats".

                                                        In Tomb of Dreams, they showcase 5 pregen Solar Characters. They have 8 Abilities at 5 between the five characters -- one of which not having any 5s.

                                                        By default, you shouldn't be taking a bunch of 5s.

                                                        That said, I like starting with 5s. The only character I play took nine 5s at character creation.

                                                        I took them wanting to cake-walk anything in my area of expertise and really struggle against anything outside my comfort-zone because I wanted to play a street-smart fish-out-of-water who never made it in their own world.

                                                        Originally posted by habitableexoplanet View Post
                                                        (though the ST may need to up the difficulty of rolls a bit.)
                                                        Bleuh. Why?

                                                        If climbing a wall is difficulty 1, climbing that wall shouldn't be harder because I'm good at climbing walls.

                                                        Originally posted by habitableexoplanet View Post
                                                        I think DES may be more likely to help you overshadow another player. Say, in a crucial fight for the story where multiple characters have a defining intimacy that is obviously relevant, wouldn't you be playing as intended to go all Divine Executioner Stance while the chips are down? But then the other character doesn't have it, or any charms of similar power level, and they are made less relevant.

                                                        OK I'm exaggerating a bit (you would probably have to already have a more-efficiently-built character to see a big effect (though with Melee that's pretty easy to accidentally achieve)) but it seems like an easy thing to fix by just banning the charm outright or houseruling the mote cost a bit higher.
                                                        If you're playing DES as intended, then yes, when the chips are down and the Intimacy counts, one PC will be markedly better than another.

                                                        But if you're playing as intended, then on every scene leading up to this point, the other player would have had an extra Charm helping them.

                                                        The problem with DES is when the latter doesn't happen, and one PC constantly overshadows the others in every combat. And that problem occurs when you let people pick an Intimacy which is broader than the example Intimacy given in the Charm. And that's what I suggest people "fix".

                                                        Originally posted by habitableexoplanet View Post
                                                        I would also support XP-based character generation as a not-completely-easy fix to make it still very possible to be a badass with multiple 5s at character creation but not possible to have an absurd amount of 5s and not pushed so hard in that direction by the system.
