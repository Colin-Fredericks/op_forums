                    Originally posted by limaxophobiac View Post
                    If it's really supposed to apply to regular attacks, what are full moons for?
                    Assuming you're talking about the -2 to hit No Moons, I think you're overstating the case here. Full Moons are still getting (Essence) non-charm dice on movement rolls and the higher of (Essence) or 3 natural soak, at the same anima level, plus getting to add (Attribute) non-charm dice to a Join Battle roll after landing a decisive, meaning they can very likely start at considerably higher initiative than just base, which No Moons have no comparable power for fighting.

                    Plus, of course, there's the general thing that Full Moons aren't necessarily "the fighty caste", the way Dawns are. Full Moons are "the physical Attribute caste". That's a broader category than just fighting, and several other non-physical Attributes are also important for fighting, meaning that there's even less reason for Full Moons to be axiomatically better at fighting than every other Lunar.

                                            On No Moons and their anima:
                                            I'd like to point out that in 2nd ed they had an anima power that gave all attackers a -1 success penalty (so equivalent to 2 dice) on attacks against them.
                                            So this is nothing new.
                                            Also, they had a lot of combat charms favoured in Wits.

                                            And... yeah, the only person I ever saw play a No Moon was me, playing a sorcerer. I saw like 7 or 8 Full Moons.
                                            Believe me, an effective +1 Defence at bonfire, while good, isn't enough to get people to play "combat No Moons", not when Full Moons get better access to combat charms, better access to starting physical attributes, soak bonus, combat movement bonus, and that amazing (if expensive) Join Battle power.

                                            It's not like 2.0 Twilights because a)the Twilight anima banner was amazing, and b)you had essentially as much access to combat abilities/powers as a Dawn.
                                            (I confess, I never saw a "Combat Twilight" but I did choose Twilight over Zenith for my first character partly because of the anima banner, and I regret it.)


                                            Also, I agree with Kelly about Castes. Full Moons are the physical caste, which isn't quite the same as the combat caste.
                                            In my game, there's two Full Moons.
                                            Olaf Keelbreaker, the Steel Iceberg, is an absolute combat beast. Strength and Stamina 5, Melee 5 with a specialty in axes and a Grand Grimcleaver made from his own tusk, a whole bunch of attack-boosting charms, DBT, Mountainous Spirit Expression (which is now good), 3 ox-bodies, a bunch of soak charms, etc, etc.
                                            The other, Krove the Bull-Tiger, isn't bad at fighting per-se... he's got 8 dice for decisives, Parry 4, soak 7, one ox-body? But for a 100xp physical primary character, he's not amazing. Because he's all about being the strongest in all the land... not at combat, but at lifting things. I've seen him 20 successes to lift and throw menhirs. He resisted being grappled by the Elemental Dragon Mela. But he is definitely not a combat character, he's just okay at it coincidentally. I don't think he's really any better than the Changing Moon with a powerbow, or much better than the Casteless trickster with rapier or No Moon shaman with poisoned blowpipe. I'd say the Casteless king of the apes with Moonsilver Carapace and flaming fists is probably better.

                                            So I'm not bothered by other Castes being good at combat if it fits (for example, quick-witted No Moons or scary Changing Moons or genius tactician No Moons), as long as Full Moons are the fastest, strongest, nimblest and toughest. Similarly, I've got no real issue with a Full Moon blacksmith being a better smith than a No Moon due to her great strength, or a Changing Moon Joan of Arc who inspires her troops to fight much harder than the Full Moon's.

                                            Whereas if you go "I want to play the best combatant/general" with a Solar, it should be a Dawn. But then, your Dawn general might be Intelligence and Charisma 5 and physical tertiary, and that's fine. Similarly, your blacksmith might be a Supernal Crafter Twilight with Strength 5 and mental tertiary.
