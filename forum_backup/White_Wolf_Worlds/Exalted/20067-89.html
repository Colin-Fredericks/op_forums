                        11-09-2015, 12:20 AM
                        Originally posted by kindledRadial View Post
                        Does Elusive Mount Technique use your movement action for the round or no?
                        Disengage is a movement action. It is also a combat action. Elusive Mount Technique turns it into a Reflexive, so that you can disengage when someone approaches you, for instance, or when you realize you're about to take a big hit. However, Elusive Mount Technique doesn't change the rule of movement actions that states "A character can only take one of the following actions per round..." (p. 197). So the answer to your question is yes, EMT uses your movement action for the round.
