                Hello there, long-time lurker both here and from the old forums before the move to Onyx Path years ago. I've played my share of the original WoD games, but my favorite games were from the more heroic and epic lines like Aberrant, Exalted and Scion. I've dipped into Exalted since 1st edition but the majority of my games ran with the 2nd edition. After years playing it, though, I felt a bit burnt out and shifted my attention elsewhere.

                Exalted 3e is pretty much an entirely different beast and got me hooked right back into the setting, which now feels familiar but new at the same time, and is the main reason why I'm leaving my old status as 'eternal lurker'. I really like the changes to some 'canon characters' like Mnemon and Lilith, and though I kind of miss Magitech, I understand and approve the decision to abandon it and make artifacts unique as they are now.

                So basically just an old time Exalted geek speaking up for the first time. Nothing new or special to see here. Thank you for your time!

                                                Hello! I'm Ignepkt. I've been consistently playing RPGs for about 7 or 8 years, possibly longer depending on how exactly one counts it. I started with D&D 3.5, then played a lot of Pathfinder 1 with my friends. We eventually moved on to D&D 5e. There's also a some not-quite-D&D that I like, such as Ultraviolet Grasslands and Stars Without Number.

                                                I first became aware of Exalted around, oh, 2013-ish, possibly earlier, when my brother got the first edition rulebook. I started actually playing it sometime in early to mid 2020, I want to say, when a friend of mine asked if I wanted to GM a solo campaign for him in 2nd edition. I said yes. The specifics of the solo campaign have changed over time, starting as a Northern Zenith Solar and taking a brief interlude for an Eastern No Moon Lunar. Both of those iterations of the campaign were eventually put to bed and we've settled on a Southern Malefactor Infernal intent on wresting Gem from the Despot, then later conquering Malfeas and the South.

                                                I look forward to my time here with everyone.
