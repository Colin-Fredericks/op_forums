                            In a 2e Game i ran, my Night Caste player stole a piece from the Board of the GoD and tossed it in the Mouth of the Void, rendering the game non functional.
                            I had the Incarnae lose their minds and rage thru Yu shan and randomly destroying things and the Unconquered Sun declared the player a Creature of Darkness and tried to smite him repeatedly as he fled.

                            The Zenith and the Eclipse managed to talk the Incarnae down, but the games were ruined. The Night Caste remained a Creature of Darkness for the rest of the campaign but thought it was totally worth it.

                            At the time at my table i had the Mandate of Creation be an actual Oath the UCS swore, that He and the rest of the Incarnae would never interfere in the ruling of Creation so long as the Exalted ruled it. But with no GoD he went back to his dayjob, running Heaven and the Daystar and that did have the potential to change Creation for the better since he would be rooting out the corruption that had become the norm in Heaven. I had little taste for souring the enormous multi year long effort the Night had put into this heist, but he did become the rest of the god's number one enemy ever for ruining the games and getting the Incarnae to pay attention again. He had to dodge heavenly assassins a lot. And the sheer backlog the UCS was saddled with and the heartbreak of losing the games and going thru withdrawal meant He was really not willing to undo the CoD tag or help my Night Caste player. Luna and the Maidens were more forgiving, Luna being amused by his audacity and the Maidens somewhat relieved that the UCS was working again.

                                                        Originally posted by Sequal View Post

                                                        2) I have seen references to Upper Souls, Lower Souls, Ghosts, the Underworld, the Lethe, Reincarnation, and Celestial Exalts being called reincarnations despite it seeming that they are getting the memories of someone unrelated to them. It all seems to be written with the assumption that the reader knows how this all worked in previous editions. Could someone explain the entire cycle of how souls work in Creation?
                                                        Humans have two conjoined souls, the hun and po. The hun is associated with higher reasoning and consciousness, the po with emotions and instincts. When a person dies, the souls separate. The hun is either collected by the appropriate gods and carried to Yu-Shan where it will be put through the process of Lethe, cleaning it of memories of prior life and preparing it for a new incarnation, or it descends to the Underworld to continue with a facsimile of its living self. The po remains with the corpse for a period, with a certain sense to ensuring it's properly respected, before gradually fading away; a particularly traumatic death or defilement of the corpse can cause the po to rise as a hungry ghost, a violent spectre that will range out of the corpse by night to inflict damage with little discrimination. Hungry ghosts tend not to last beyond the corpse decaying enough to not serve as shelter from the sun, although they can take refuge in close enough shadowlands.

                                                        The Exaltation is spiritual in nature, and the manner in which it embodies itself in a person, particularly when carrying impressions and even memories, is viewed as akin to reincarnation by people in Creation. Even if it lacks such specific markers from its prior life, it's a profound experience whose process and meaning is like being born again, hence being the Second Breath.
