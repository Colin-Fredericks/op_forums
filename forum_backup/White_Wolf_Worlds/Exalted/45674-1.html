        Exalted clearly has something resembling germ theory, because they have a far better understanding of sterilization than we did at their technology level, but I'm not sure actual individual germs exist in Creation, and if they do it's probably not something you'd want to lampshade. If you want to talk real-world science I'm not sure a virus would count as an organism in the first place, but even if you shift it to bacteria I don't think "prokaryote form" is really a place you want to go. IMO, the fact that Exalted tends to steer well clear of talking about microorganisms is evidence enough that if you go that way you're invoking Rule Zero. I think you could theoretically justify having a Lunar turn into a contagion or illness (in aggregate), because that lies somewhere in the confluence of the plant knack and Air Body, but I wouldn't allow it personally.

        I would actually probably houserule the "always inactive rule" as it stands, because I don't see why you shouldn't be able to perform any actions that don't require movement. That said, I think a followup power to let you move is reasonable. I'd probably require mote-spend and commitment for it, but I'd certainly allow it.

        I would absolutely allow a lunar to sire plantmen and fungusmen with the powers you describe, because it seems in-theme, and what's the harm? Those powers suck, so a player who takes them is clearly really in love with the gimmick. However, since beastmen are fundamentally human, I'd rule that they're still animals who have some of the properties and appearance of plants/fungus, rather than being Swamp Thing. I imagine there's an unwritten followup to that power that lets you just sire outright Weird Monsters, though.

        I always liked the idea of a Lunar who combined the plant-shape Knack and the Leviathan form to go into deep cover as a mycelium bed in a forest. They wouldn't be able to change form without spending a day doing it and destroying the forest in the process, but I figured they would have some "municipal charm" type effects for manipulating the forest, and could communicate with agents through spores and mushroom patterns. Even that's getting really abstract for a Lunar, but I like the idea on its own merits.
