    Here's the Initiation idea I thought of earlier, fleshed out and refined a little more. All three of the shaping rituals are either lifted directly from the book, or are are from the book with some changes.

    Seared by Sacred Fire
    You have trained with a nameless cult of metallurgist-monks who maintain the secrets the manse known as the Forge of the Sacred Fire. In the process of mastering their holy craft you burned yourself on the divine flames of the forge and came away scarred. The pain of the flame was the ultimate teacher that opened your mind to the deepest secrets of metalwork, and the sorcerous structure of Creation.

    Shaping Rituals
    Once per story, the sorcerer may engage in a basic craft project in which the use of fire is necessary. Completing the project grants her further insight into the fabric of reality, and allows her to roll (Intelligence + Craft). She gains sorcerous motes equal to the successes that last for that story's duration. The sorcerer cannot use Charms to decrease the time it takes to complete the basic project to benefit from this ritual, but can make use of them for anything else pertaining to it.

    Whenever the sorcerer takes a shape sorcery action, she may draw an additional (Essence) sorcerous motes from any fire within medium range, coaxing its power into her spell. This diminishes the flame—a hallway of torches will be extinguished by a single invocation, while a bonfire might die down to small flames after the first, then go out altogether after the second. However, if the sorcerer is fighting near (or in!) a burning building, she has a nearly endless source of sorcerous motes to draw on. She may draw from fire elementals or other spirits of flame as well, draining (Essence) motes from their pools, but only if her shape sorcery roll exceeds their Resolve, and no more than once per scene against any one spirit. When the sorcerer draws Essence to fuel her control spell, flames are not diminished, and spirits do not lose the motes drawn from them.

    The sorcerer may bathe in the geomantic fires that burn in her manse’s heart to gain (Essence + Willpower) sorcerous motes, at the price of taking a level of aggravated damage. These motes last for a fortnight, or until she next claims motes from the manse. While within the bounds of the manse, she also gains one additional sorcerous mote each turn while casting a spell.


    I figure it has The Burning Name as a merit, but I want at least one other and I'm not sure what it should be.

        I heard somewhere that someone wanted Bloodbourne's Insight as an Initiation.

        While I don't own the book and have no clue what sorcerous motes are, I'll take a crack at fluff:

        Dreamer of the Poison Revelation

        There are things known only to the Yozis. The nature of the world they once lived in before making Creation (though not its name), the place where the Bridge to Nowhere leads, and the true name of the Unconquered Sun. To learn-or more accurately, infer-these things is to invite catastrophe in the form of Orabilis, End of All Wisdom and Librarian of Malfeas, who is permitted to travel anywhere in and out of the Demon City to silence those who would pry deeply into the minds of the imprisoned Titans. He lights the errant sorcerer's very soul aflame, and places it within the celestial firmament of Hell, simultaneously sentencing them to a long, slow death and honoring them for their intellect, for their death is a painless one in the court and presence of Ligier, the living Green Sun himself.

        Very rarely, a scholar's force of will controls the flames, and they remain as they were, with only a silver scar to mark them. From there, by definition whatever they learned is one not private to the Primordials, though Orabilis considers the surviving scholar a worthy personal rival from then on out. For the most part, these things are mere relative tidbits-that the Primordials former world is named "Zen-Mu" is largely pointless to the Creation-Born, or that there is something called a Shining Answer that Gaia seeks. A few are wonders whose release leads to golden eras, held back by the Primordials out of fear and later spite of their creations, such as the secret of forging orichalcum.

        At least one, however, was held back because of what compassion the Primordials had and have. In the ending days of the First War, when the great Dragon Beyond The World was imprisoned within his own wings, She Who Lives In Her Name attempted to free him through use of her laws, redefining his wings as something he may escape from. To her eternal regret, she succeeded-partially, allowing all who had gathered to save their brother to see what lay within. For the next five days and nights, Isidoros fled across the skies, Cecelyne declared herself ground for rebels, and Adorjan's passage made life from the ground. When the three recovered their senses, and coaxed their sister from hiding within the King's heart, all four agreed that no one deserved to know what it is they saw within Oramus.

        Naturally, a Solar Exalt discovered that in time. Among all of Malfeas' stars, his is unique in that it is the only one that was set alight long after feeling Orabilis' touch-his soul being ablaze was the only way he could not see.

        However, such a terrible thing could not be completely forgotten, though the Solar told no one and destroyed all that led him to that horrible wisdom, the idea has gained life . For Oramus' wings are forever chipped, and whenever someone invokes the secret laws of darkness and solitude that the Principle did on that auspicious day, so shall the Dragon's Revelation make itself known:

        I'm not alone in here.

        No one's ever alone anywhere.

        And those who learn that see why.

        (anyone up for mechanics? I see this as manipulation and twisting of perception; shaping rituals are generally based around confronting-or revealing-unpleasant truths).

            a sorcerous initiation an ST wrote for my Twilight character:
            Pact With Ligier:
            There is power in artifice, and the Infernal Architect draws it forth, hammering the essence of the world into shape as he would beat out metal on a forge. Whenever he acquires crafting experience, he also accumulates Sorcerous motes which last until he next completes another project of that level. Silver experience provides 1 mote, gold experience provides 2 motes per point, and white experience four motes per point.

            And with the following as Sorcerous Merits she might buy.

            ((Same as for the other demon path))
            Infernal Nobility (Merit •): Demons recognize the sorcerer as one of Ligier's proteges. First Circle demons are treated as having a Minor Tie of terrified awe towards her, while Second and Third Circle demons have a positive Minor Tie of respect, or some variation suited to their nature.

            ((Same as the fire elemental path))
            The Burning Name (Merit ••): Speaking a syllable of her patron’s name, the sorcerer may call forth green fire, sending it streaming from her eyes, fingertips, or mouth. She might use this to light a campfire or incinerate ropes that bind her hands, and can also use it as an attack, rolling (Intelligence + Occult) to direct the flames, which are treated as a light mundane weapon with the lethal, thrown (short), and mounted tags.

            ((Adapted from another path based on Ligier's sense of superiority))
            Font of Disdain (Merit ••) A craftsman has no time for emotion, regret. There is only his work. Once per day, when his Resolve is overcome by influence that opposes an Intimacy based on conviction or determination, she may lower the Willpower cost to resist it by one point.

                I kitbashed an initiation together from the ones in the Core;

                Heir of the Palace of Clouds
                The character carries within her the blood of powerful spirits of the sky, or the air elementals which tend the clouds, which has kindled within her the rage of storms, as well as the benevolence of the merciful clouds that bring the relief of rain.
                At the moment of initiation she breathed her first real breath in her entire life, her life force and sorcerous power congealing into a Familiar *** spirit/elemental of Air, (which must be paid for seperately) through which she might channel her sorcerous powers, and which aids her in shaping sorcery.

                ((I have no idea for shaping rituals, any help there would be greatly appreciated. The familiar, and the power of clouds and fogs, as well as the nobility-association of the skies are supposed to be major themes))

                ((Ported over from Mara's initiation))
                * - Lofty Nobility - Minor positive tie (per storyteller discretion) with Air elementals, and gods and spirits assoicated with storms, clouds, or the sky, with spirits of increasing power being progressively less impressed.

                ((Stolen from the Ifrit Lord initiation)
                ** - Words as Storms - From the cauldron of storms within her soul, the Sorcerer exhales an eddy of air current, that carries with it a dense fog; this might be used to enable stunts in which the fog obscures sight or disorients enemies. More importantly, the air currents can develop surprising speeds and force, and be used to make attacks with a (Intelligence + Occult) roll, and the traits of a light mundane weapon, with the bashing, mounted, and thrown (medium) tags, attacks of which reduce the cost for a knockdown gambit by 1.

                ((Stolen from Mara's initiation again)
                Mark of Clouds - Tattoo-like markings of clouds, and air currents, manifest on the characters skin, allowing her to develop Evocations that deal with the controll of the winds, clouds, fog, and at higher Essence scores, entire weather patterns, such as summoning violent thunderstorms.

                OOC notation: The actual aesthetics of this initiation are supposed to be less of an outright imitation of Air Elemental powers, but also to contain themes of nobility, and the elevated nature of the skies and clouds; weather manipulation within this path is supposed to be less outright destructive, and rather provide means of utility, like conjuring a healing mist, or bringing fertilizing rains, not just calling down lightning storms or ragdolling fuckers with air bending, because that would be boring. So lofty sky-god shit in the sense of Tibetan Sky Burials, or "up there be the sun" holy effects are welcome, if you guys can come up with any.)

                                Pact with a Forge-God(dess)

                                A Forge-God or Goddess will only form a pact with a notable craftsman who practices at least one craft that deals in metal. Learning their sorcery through the ring of metal, the shaping of steel, and the heat of the forge, they temper themselves much like they would a fine blade.

                                Shaping Rituals

                                Initiation reveals to the Sorcerer the sorcerous essence in all items forged by their own hand. As part of a shaping action, the craftsman may sacrifice one item that they had made themselves and have at hand. An item created as a Basic Project grants them 1 sorcerous mote, Major 2, Superior 4, and Legendary 8. Items sacrificed in this way are destroyed, but may be repaired with appropriate time, resources, and skill, though any item that is unique and cannot be recreated (Daiklaves and other artifact weapons, Legendary artifacts) is permanently destroyed if it is sacrificed a second time.

                                As part of a crafting effort, the Sorcerer may dedicate their effort to their Pact. In doing so they do not gain crafting xp of the highest type they would gain (no Silver for Basic, no Gold for Major, no White for Superior or Legendary). Instead they are granted power based on their skill and the resolve to make the item. Make a crafting roll at a forge after finishing a Project, putting their strength into it (Strength + Craft, difficulty 5 minus the value of the Project Basic -1, Major -2, Superior -3, Legendary -4). Success on this roll grants motes equal to the sorcerer's (Essence + extra success on the roll). If the finished item either hit all three basic objectives or was completed exceptionally well add additional motes equal to the value of the project (Basic 1, Major 2, Superior 3, Legendary 4). These motes last until the end of the current Story and may be replaced no more then once per day.

                                Other Benefits

                                Ritual Crafting (Merit ••) As part of using a Charm with a Craft Roll that takes at least one hour, the Sorcerer may do a Shape Sorcery action to gather motes. These motes may then be spent on the Charms being used to aid in the Craft Roll. No project may benefit from this more then once per day.

                                The Forge Knows It's Master (Merit ••••) In a ritual lasting twenty minutes to an hour (ten minutes per dot, N/A count as six), the sorcerous artificer may roll a Shaping Sorcery roll with no aid from Charms when handling an artifact they have made. Every hit on this roll lowers the Attunement cost of the artifact by 1, to a maximum of it's Attunement cost, for one week. No artifact may undergo this ritual more then once a week.

                                    So a friend of mine has a concept for a Twilight who Exalted by inventing a new form of Sorcery independent of the Salinian Method. This was prior to Ex3's reveal, and of the Initiations.

                                    Basically, the idea was that there's magic in numbers and in calculations, so charting out Essence flow with runes, numbers, and graphs... As well as preparing runes in solid objects, such as carving a rune that fires Death of Obsidian Butterflies from the wall when activated.

                                    Saying "Exalted by inventing her own Initiation" is an easy enough port, but it's tough to come up with some Shaping Rituals. I'm thinking like... Spending a few minutes centering herself in the morning to do the needed math, gaining (Int + Lore) Sorcerous motes for the day. I did think of a Merit!

                                    Working Optimization (Merit •••) The understanding of the flow of Essence around her, the Sorcerer can improve their Workings through proper planning. When determining the total number of Means for a Sorcerous Working, the Sorcerer may attempt to lay a rune foundation for her Working. The Sorcerer makes an Intelligence + Lore check (Difficulty 3 for a Terrestrial Working, Difficulty 5 for a Celestial Working, Difficulty 7 for an Adamant Working). Upon a success, the Sorcerer has earned an additional Means. A failure means that the Sorcerer does not benefit from their plans. A botch on this roll can lead to a Sorcerer working from a flawed design, resulting in a Complication, as though she had botched one of the rolls on her working (even if she does not botch any rolls on the Working itself)

                                    Any suggestions for Spell Maths and Runes would be appreciated!
