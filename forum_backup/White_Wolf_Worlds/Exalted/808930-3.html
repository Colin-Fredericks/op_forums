                        Break that down for me; the steps between a single Defining Tie, and altering a person's beliefs wholesale.
                        First, you give them the Defining Tie.                         You can persuade them to do almost anything, and unless they've got opposed Defining Intimacies (think they might need two: one to boost defense, on for the decision point) they're gonna do it.                         But to make it stick once CBT's brainwashing wears off, you'll want to make more social influence attempts. Fortunately, with a Defining Intimacy you can weaken or strengthen whatever Intimacies you want with simple rolls. Most people have very little wp and it regenerates slowly. Plus they don't use it on every roll. So you can easily rewrite their whole list of Intimacies over a few scenes.
