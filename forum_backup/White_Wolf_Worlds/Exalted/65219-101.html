    Generally, the Exaltations produced by Exigence are not destroyed by the death of the Chosen. Instead, one of the missions of the Sidereals is securing such lost Exaltations as close as possible to the Exigent's death. In case the god which produced the Exalt has survived channeling the Exigence, the Exaltation is reclaimed and returned to the god so they choose a new champion of their own. In case that the god was too weak to withstand the diminishment- or if the Exigence was preformed illicitly- it is reclaimed and contained in the Vault of Miracles (which is technically under Lytek's supervision, yet after the Usurpation the Bronze Faction makes keeps a close watch over it as well). Due to the fragile and corruptible nature of those Exaltations, the Sidereals keep a strict record of the contained Exigence, and make sure that whoever god would next use them would be both loyal to Heaven and won't try to taint them with their own Essence. The highest pro for a god of using an already shaped Exigence is that they do not risk diminishment by doing so- yet the greatest con is that the new Chosen is not generally loyal to the goals of their new patron.

    Exigences which were tainted and corrupted by a number of gods before being secured, however, are kept separately than the other Exaltations- deep in the Chamber of Darken Flames. No one but the guardians of the Chamber knows what happens to those Exaltations- the official claim is that they are being ritually purified from the abhorrent Essence or, in the case it is not possible, either destroyed when possible and locked when not. Some, however, whisper that those Exigences are actually kept protected, both studied in order to recognize exactly which gods have contributed to the crime of creating the Exaltation and to be used in battle, if an emergency would require those powerful yet twisted Chosen.

                                                            There is a semi-secret cemetery in Marama's Fell called the "Thousand Pact Graveyard". After The Bishop and Fae lords agreed to the Thousand Year Pact with the Syndics; certain ghost warlords and others who disagreed with the pact set aside a plot of land capable of holding 24,000 dead. Neutral ground in the constant gang fights that went on between them. After the Fae have had their fun, and after the Bishop's servants are done desecrating the dead, those warlords transport the bodies (or what's left of them) to the the graveyard for internment. The warlords know what it is like to be slaughtered just on a government's say so, and as such pay them some respect.
