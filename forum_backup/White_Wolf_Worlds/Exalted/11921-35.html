                    A good one I thought of.

                    A powerful Night Caste thief has appeared out of nowhere in Creation. Some believe her to be a First Age queen who escaped the Ursurpation, while others believe her to have worked with the Sidereals for thousands of years before betraying them and leaving Yu-Shan with a variety of powerful artifacts. Whatever her true origin, the thief has numerous spirits and demons bound to her service, using them to help hersteal various wonders from all the corners of Creation. She's succeeded in stealing everything from the daiklave of Cathak Cainan himself to entire manses!

                    Fortunately, a special Department of Fate has been assigned to recover the stolen items and capture this Solar. By recruiting the best and brightest Dragon-Blooded from the Cloister of Wisdom, the House of Bells, Pasiap's Stair and the Heptagram, the Sidereals form a temporary Wyld Hunt. This Wyld Hunt investigate the hints left behind by the rightfully cocky Solar, recover the stolen wonder and attempt to capture the thief. While the Department of Fate has succeeded in capturing a bunch of the spirits, demons and even other Exalted who help in the heists, they have yet to capture the Night Caste thief, who always manages to slip from their grasp at the last moment.

                                1. A young temple minder in training, years after his father's death, tries to save his scholar friend and monk superior on his 18th birthday when a pair of powerful ghosts attack. He believes in himself, and then dies.
                                However, he is offered an Abyssal Exaltation by perhaps the silliest and least morbid Deathlord, but with a condition: he has 99 days to collect 15 artifacts shaped by the souls of past Exalts, and if he fails, he will die for real this time.

                                2. The deathlord Shrieking Eagle Leaving Creation In Shock has been building his powerbase. One young man is taken to be a mighty Dusk, but breaks free before he can be brainwashed to the Deathlord's values. This Renegade, bearing the name Rider in the Locust Mask, kicks to death a great number of Abyssal Beastmen.

                                3. Three Solars are taught by a group of Lunars to shape their anima, through martial arts, into warstriders. They are joined by one of the Solars' wolf Lunar brother and a brilliantly lazy Solar, but face a pair of Abyssals. The Abyssals lead an army of the undead while one of them uncovers three Deathlords to learn greater power from. All the while, a Third-Circle Soul of TED himself weaves a scheme.

                                4. A Chosen of Secrets schoolteacher and her sentient transforming artifact vehicle bring a class of children into contact with the many wonders of Creation, from the oceans to the human circulatory system.
