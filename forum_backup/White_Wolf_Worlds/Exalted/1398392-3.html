        I think it's fair to say that the Solars are the best tool available in Creation for fighting Abyssals and Infernals. If you're going up against an Abyssal or an Infernel, and you can seek the aid of a Solar, it's probably a good idea. (Especially a combat-focused Solar, but even the noncombat ones can contribute.) When, a hundred years from now, Creation is not destroyed, and not dominated by Abyssals and Deathlords, nor by Infernals and Yozis, the fact that Solars fought against them will likely be a big part of the reason why.

        But it's not fair to say that Solars are necessary to fight Abyssals and Infernals, no longer how big an army or whatever other support they might have. (In particular, it's not necessary if you have an even bigger army.) It's not fair to say that Creation is doomed if the Solars do not fight the Abyssals and Infernals for whatever reason.

        I think a good analogy might be a modern fighter aircraft. A modern fighter aircraft is the best tool available for destroying a modern fighter. If you're going up against an enemy that has a modern jet fighter, you would really like to have a fighter of your own, especially so if they have the army and everything else to back it up. But you can defeat a modern fighter with a less-advanced fighter, especially with some advantage like numbers, pilot experience, or surprise. You can defeat one, in certain situations, with anti-aircraft guns, or by destroying it on the ground, or by running away from it and waiting for the infrastructure that supports it to fall apart. You're never without hope.

        Exalted is a game about the Solars. But it's also a game about heroism, and never being without hope, not matter how dire the situation seems. The fact that Solars exemplify heroism does not mean that Dragonblooded and even mortals lack it.

                            But if, from now on, nobody ever stands up to or defeats Jeff Bezos, the world is going to become a very bad place. If nobody ever defeats Jeff Bezos in business competition, or using political and legal methods, or however, Amazon will gradually grow to take up a larger and larger percentage of the economy and will eventually use its market power to raise prices and funnel huge amounts of cash to itself, causing various negative consequences.

                            You personally don't have to stop Jeff Bezos to prevent this. But someone, somewhere, has to decide they're going to go up against Jeff Bezos, do it, and win.

                                                            Incoming ramble basically repeating my now standardized thoughts when this topic of apocalyptic threats comes about :

                                                            My feeling on apocalyptic threats is; presenting them as an inevitable consequence of the inertia of the world should the Solars not intervene, flattens out the trajectory of where stories go in Exalted and flattens out the dialogue between Exalted factions about who should rule and how they should interact.

                                                            It's absolutely fine and fun for individual games to introduce "Apocalyptic Battle of Darkness Against The Light" type of scenarios, but seems to me better for everything around the setting generally if these are million-to-one events that just happen to have happened in a given game (and will do in waaay more than one in a million games!), due to outside MacGuffinry. Not an inevitable outgrowth of its fundamental internal historical forces and its design.

                                                            Exalted's really well set up as a fantasy game for "Epic fantasy where the fate of nations and kingdoms lies in the balance!" (whether the sort that places agency with general and kings, or the sort that places agency with a small and plucky band of journeying heroes) and it's a shame to compress that towards "Epic Fantasy Against the Dark Lord, Lest The World Be Destroyed!" scenarios where it's "Solars or Bust!".

                                                            (This is roughly similar to some other takes by writers / devs in the past, I think?)

                                                            (As a further caveat, I totally get and have some sympathy to the objection to this that lots of fans bought into a vision of Exalted where Solars with "cosmic power" -power they alone have among the Exalted - fought against "cosmic threats" to preserve reality from destruction. That sounds fun! But at the end of the day I don't think this has priority of claim, or is making the best use of the way the world is set up!).
