    Integrity:

    Nightmare-Slaying Determination
    Whenever someone fails a Threaten action against the Solar without the Solar having to spend willpower to resist it, the Solar's resolve increases by one. If the Solar reaches her maximum Resolve cap because of this charm, she no longer needs to spend willpower to resist Threaten actions so long as she can call upon an Intimacy to defend herself.

        Sail:

        Daring Adventurer's Wind
        This charm supplement's a Solar's attempt to plot a journey through sea, land or sky. If the Solar rolls the required number of successes, nothing happens. However, if the Solar achieves threshold successes, she may plot a second course. This course is filled with a number of obstacles such as environmental hazards, natural disasters and hostile forces equal to the number of threshold successes rolled, up to a maximum of the Solar's Essence. So long as the Solar follows the more dangerous course, she may deduct the total number of obstacles from the cost of using the Sail Excellency. In addition, her total travel time is divided by the number of obstacles along her course.

            Larceny:

            Golden Bauble Distraction
            The Solar uses this misdirection as cover for her crimes. This charm supplements an attempt at social influence to imply that one of the target's beloved possessions is about to be damaged, stolen or is otherwise in need of focused attention. If the social influence is successful, the target takes a penalty equal to his intimacy towards the beloved possession to any attempt to notice that one of her other possessions has been pickpocketed or stolen.

                War:

                Banner of the Victorious Legion
                The Solar may erect a number of banners bearing her name or emblem equal to her War rating on the battle field. So long as all these banners are standings, all battle groups under the Solar's command possess a Principle towards victory and following the Solar's orders which may be used to resist intimidation and maintain morale. The Principle is minor if there are 1 or 2 banners up, major if there are 3 or 4 and defining if there are 5.

                    Holistic Athleticism Synergy

While the Solar is engaged in strenuous activity such as racing or weightlifting, she may substitute Resistance for Athletics when rolling to resist poison, disease or deprivation.                             I'm not too sure. My first instinct is that you'd only pay for the Excellencies once, but I'm not too sure how to handle dice caps.

                            Dodge:

                            Flesh and Shadow Phantom
                            The Solar is struck in half by her enemy's blow, but her defeat is but a phantom; her form has become an afterimage with mass. The Solar activates this charm in response to an enemy's attack. If the Solar fails to dodge the attack, whatever amount of momentum her enemy would gain from the attack is reduced by 1. If the Solar benefits from the effects of this charm again during her next action, the total momentum her enemy would gain further decreases by 1. This effect is cumulative up to the Solar's Essence. Using this charm against a different attacker does not break this chain and the Solar can maintain multiple chains at the same time, but successfully dodging or soaking all the damage of an attack does.

                                Archery:

                                Broken Arrow
                                The Solar supports her allies with her archery. This charm supports an attack against an enemy who is engaged in close-range combat against one of the Solar's allies and possesses a momentum advantage over that ally. If the attack is successful, the Solar's ally can immediately take a movement action to escape, even if it would normally be impossible for her to do so.

                                    Linguistics:

                                    Unknowable Poetic Harmony
                                    The Solar writes a poem of sublime rhythm and rhyme. However, her writing remain incomplete and no other can finish it. This charm is a written social influence towards finishing the Solar's poem and must be aimed at a specific target. If the original roll is successful, the target gains a minor intimacy towards finishing the poem. Every time the target returns to the poem, the Solar rolls to strengthen the target's intimacy towards finishing the poem. These subsequent rolls cannot benefit from the Solar's other charms, but the Solar automatically adds a number of successes equal to her Essence to the roll. The intimacy created by this charm is often used to convince the target to work on the poem and abandon their usual duties and responsibilities.

                                        Brawl:

                                        Face of the Crowd
                                        The Solar may activate this charm when she is fighting before a crowd of people who hold a positive intimacy towards her. It remains active as long as the Solar uses only her unarmed punch or kick. Whenever the Solar makes a successful attack against an opponent who is armed, that opponent loses momentum equal to the crowd's intimacy towards the Solar (1 for minor, 2 for major, 3 for defining).

                                                            I was inspired by that thing where the guy empties his flintlock and then throws it away before drawing his sword. Eloquent, I know.

                                                            Anyway, these aren't part of my normal quota, but I've already forgotten some charms that I wanted to write and I would rather not do that again.

                                                            Melee:

                                                            Steel River's Current
                                                            The Solar may activate this charm when her attack misses. She may redirect her attack to any structure or environmental feature within close range. The Solar retains her original attack roll and charm activations and resolves the attack again against her new target's defense.

                                                            Socialize:

                                                            Unity of Ten Thousand Wills
                                                            The Solar gathers into her entourage a crowd of people who share a specific intimacy. When the crowd is present to bear witness to the Solar's attempt at social influence, the Solar may activate this Charm and roll Charisma + Socialize against her target's Resolve. If the Solar is successful, her target may not benefit from any Intimacy that goes against the Intimacy that unites the crowd. This effect lasts until the end of the scene, or until the crowd disperses.

                                                            Craft:

                                                            Gifter's Unwavering Spirit
                                                            When she crafts an item, the Solar may dedicate it to an intended recipient. If anyone succesfully claims the item from its rightful owner through theft, force or purchase, the item is immediately destroyed. Mundane items turn to ash and rust and structures rot and collapse. Artifacts explode, dealing damage to anyone in medium range. The destruction of a manse anointed by this charm is a truly cataclysmic event.
