                                                Originally posted by GrayWalker View Post
                                                I have looked it up and there was a thread about it a fair bit ago but do you know whether the management of Exigences are under Lytek's purview?
                                                Lytek has bureaucratic jurisdiction over Exigents, but he's not actually involved in the process of creating them.

                                                I believe with their varied methods of transfer there may be some joined authority between Lytek and Parad (if he's canon), with of course the primary control being under the god whom it came from.
                                                Assuming Parad exists, I imagine him trying to stake his claim to any Exigent that could be arguably construed as inherited and grab jurisdiction over it away from Lytek.

                                                Also may multiple gods take invest their energy into an Exigence, when exalting someone with it?
                                                Yes, but that may not be a good idea.

                                                Beyond that change are "blackmarket Exaltations" under the same restrictions as Exigences?
                                                Black market Exigents are the same class of being as any other Exigents; the only difference is the chain of events between the bestowal of Exigence on a petitioning god and the creation of an Exigent.
