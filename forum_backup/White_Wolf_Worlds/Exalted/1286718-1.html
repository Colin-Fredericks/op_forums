                Wizard, you're right in that Socialize is better at determining Intimacies than Presence. That's its job. (And protecting your own, of course.) Even if the Zenith in your group didn't take Socialize, are you telling me that no one in your group took Socialize? I mean, yeah, of course you're going to have problems with social influence if your group has no way of determining Intimacies.

                I think you harp on this too much, frankly. If anything, the DB has misplaced Charms, not Solars. Presence shouldn't be about 'getting Intimacies', as that is the purview of Socialize. Also, I'd have to question any ST that would allow a social-focused character to that degree to make that sort of mistake. That's like taking Archery Supernal, and not taking Dodge, Resistance, or any other way to defend yourself. You're missing a vital part of the Social system if you can't determine Intimacies.

                Also, something else that should be noted here--and likely any ST Guide that is created--is that NPC Intimacies should exist at some level to support social interaction. I'm not saying make it easy; I'm not saying to make it simple. There is some onus on the ST to not completely block the Social system by saying, 'Yeah, no, none of these guys have any Intimacies that can help.' If the ST gives them Intimacies, but the Solars can't figure out how to exploit them, then the Solars fail. But if there are no handles, the ST has failed. There's also the fact that Social system is far more of a detective game or puzzle than the Combat system in Exalted 3E, and people wanting to circumvent it to make it more like Combat I think are short-changing the system.

                I just don't see the problem. From a 'stereotypical' Solar Circle POV, the Eclipse finds the right target, and the Zenith persuades that target.

                                    Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz View Post
                                    Incidentally, I'd be happy to hear from anyone who's used a Solar with Presence charms.

                                    I've found them not much good unless they're also good at Socialise, but I'd like to see others' experience.
                                    I played a Solar with moderate-heavy investment in Presence and Performance and minimal (ie. not even the excellency) Socialize. I expected a lot of trouble going in because of the lack of Socialize than I actually experienced. Just talking to NPCs for a while or observing them for a scene or two was enough to give a rough idea of at least one intimacy to work with so I could usually target something blind with good odds. And if that failed (or was resisted) following up with an Inspire and then playing off that emotion usually did the trick as well. You're not wrong that it takes more rolls, but it was effective enough to wear down erode the faith of an Immaculate monk before Essence 3 with no social abilities Supernal.

                                    It was also worth its weight in gold during combat. Harmonious Presence Methodology + Poised Lion Attitude meant that I could fire off a simple social action with 11 dice every round in addition to normal combat actions for 3m. They didn't all land, but as combat drags on Willpower and motes for Resolve become increasingly scarce. Enemies could choose between being Inspired with anger and easily manipulated/Instilled with some less than ideal feelings, or they could burn willpower they would rather spend on charms to resist.

                                    By the time we finished at Essence 3 I was also running around with Sun King Radiance from Integrity, so anyone already broken (or any new allies watching for the first time) sad, at the ST's option, the ability to manifest a new Major Intimacy of respect with nearly every social pass my idealistic Eclipse made.

                                    Socialize would have made the build frighteningly more powerful, but it never felt particularly weak or ineffective in the social arena despite the gaping blind spot. But I also went into things knowing that blind spot would be there and prepared for what it would mean for how effective he could be and how I would need to approach things. If I hadn't gone in with open eyes I can imagine that it would have been deeply frustrating.

                                        I guess my feeling has always been that Solar Presence should be good at 1) being glorious and 2) convincing others of the rightness of your cause. From this thread it sounds like it does pretty well at the first one, but falls down a bit at 2. Honestly, I could see Solar Presence having a charm which allows you to treat someone as having a minor intimacy towards one of your defining intimacies, because your charisma and enthusiasm for your subject leaks over into your target. Or a charm which lets you get away with instilling one of your own defining intimacies into your target with only one supporting intimacy.

                                        Basically, I think Wizard's earlier suggestions mostly work, but I'd tend to require them to reflect the Solar's own defining intimacies and maybe have them fall down a bit if an opponent is really dead set against you, on the basis that Zeniths get to define their own definition of "Righteousness" in 3e, instead of applying some version of the Sun's definition of it. This way, it works for getting followers to your cause and getting your flock to do things for you, but trying to convince people to do things you wouldn't do or things that are tangential to your main goals, is where Socialize can really help.

                                            I think that might be why socialize seems more powerful than perhaps it should be. With my experience in the system nobody used socialize to figure out that the sneering Satrap who treated his servants like David Lynch’s Baron Vladimir Harkonen had an intimacy of contempt for the local people. Where socialize is useful is to find out that he actually doesn’t and it’s all a show, or that he secretly loves one of them or something.

                                            It happens enough to still be useful, especially in really high class socialite settings where you can pretty much assume everyone is hiding something, but a whole lot of the time you can just guess based on actions, or speech. Like if you come across a guy, respected in his community, good person to try and get on your side, and he lives out on the edge of the forest, keeping it clear of deadfall and making sure it isn’t over hunted. Tell him the Realm is coming to slash and burn down this whole forest to get at your circle, and no, it won’t matter if you’ve already skipped town because they’ll do it anyway just to be sure. You’re not 100% sure he’s got an intimacy towards the woods, but he probably does.

                                            Presence could definitely do with a treatment for having more cool punchy effects, but also when you’re playing a Solar some of that has to come from you too. Like you don’t have a lot of cool feats of strength charms, but picking up a giant beer still and dousing a flaming house is more about how you applied your 30 dice double 7s.
