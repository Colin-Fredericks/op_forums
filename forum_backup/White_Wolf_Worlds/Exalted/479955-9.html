    Aquillion MInd, in theory if we had super-intelligent AIs to account for every-fuck up a human can introduce, it probably would work. I am skeptical it ever gets there because you still have not-perfect people implementing things and so you are, as you note, just as likely to fuck things up. Especially since no system is free from human biases and issues therein. Note I don't disagree we should make progress. I just think that human systems are complex enough that such progress is oging ot be hard, have a lot of hiccups and super-strong centralized authority wrested on individuals is kind of asking for it to not work. I think my skepticism of the possiblity of something liket he Culture is I think a society will blow itself up before the AI goes on trying to make everyone's lives better.

    I'm also not saying that we can't improve things. Just that there are systems that as we can gather, are not deterministic and so need to be approached as such. I'm noting that what is needed is probably different conceptions than typically is overly applied phsyics or material engineering solutions to a lot of problems. And that things probably will break somewhere, and that folks should expect to have contingencies when they do. A lot of issues I think have in human history, even with policies I agree with which have had downstream consequences, often happen to cause problems since there's a problem pointed out that the implementors think will just fix itself. Or they say they need the thing implemented first...and then don't get to the Part Two of actually fixing issues.

    You're mischarecterizing me as a skeptic of societal progress because I am skeptical that something like I understand the Culture is a way to get there or that it is ever possible due to well, it's hard to get there. My issue is that society isn't just an engineering problem, or at least one that short of AI is fixable, and I'm skeptical we can ever get to the AI without I dunno, Facebook or Chiense Social Credit first, because the values of a system's creators aren't extractable and so I'm not so clear how we get to soemthing like the stuff in the Culture.

    It's also, note, why I say that even granting the Culture being possible, Solars probably can't do it. Because while I can grant a detached AI that somehow manages to surpass it's creator's own biases for a greater good (defined as peaceful, prosperous, post-resources carcity I am guessing) probably, Solar Exlted emphatically aren't those and will despite their high competence often probably hit a lot of the "There's not an objective right way to do X without hurting someone" things we have to deal with in policy making today. And being people, the Solar's biases are going to affect who she decides is the cost in any cost-benefit analysis.

    (I'll also note I only know about the series through secondhand, as I have not personally read it. But I have seen it explianed and discussed and it kind of hits up against what I have been reading elsewhere of late.)

    Accelerator Solar Bureaucracy is really good at managing systems, finding people to break those systems, and knowing how you can implement them. But it's notable that actually as written, it doesn't let you perfectly implement any program you want. You are probably the best at wiedling bureacracies to be able to implement policies you want and such...as long as you also make sure the people below you implement them. I think it's notable a good chunk of the Solar Charmset is about rooting out corruption, lazy people, and all that. And that even then, you'll note there's no "Perfectly solve problem" Charms. Just getting things inhumanely good at it.

    An issue of why something like Brasilia is a shitty city to live in or that the Five Pests Campaign was a disastor wasn't because the folks inovlved weren't able to implement their visions. It's that hteir visions were shitty to begin with. And to my reading, Solar Bureacracy doesn't actually stop you from doing that. You have to do the research and legwork on your own to find out downstream effects, and it's notable that nothing in the Solar Charmset does that perfectly. Very good, sure. But that you know how to eliminate all sparrows ever doesn't mean you even considered what the downstream effects of that are. Those are different questions and often people implementing sweeping policies like that don't ask them all. And nothing about Solar might in that sense really like, gives them that for free.

    As noted, if their magic did, they'd of not had an Usurpation.

    And stuff.

            I have to say something about the idea that minds all somwhow all-knowing and all-adapting. First thing, the Culture Minds have been fooled and blindsided by non-hyperintelligent minds several times (Hidrogen Sonata, Use of Weapons, Matter), and have had a hard time facing other cultures with similar thecnological weapons but no minds (Consider Phlebas and such). In the world of the Culture, being hyperintelligent is great, but is not omniscience, even more so with the ethical restrictions minds have for completely simulating another intelligence's thought-processs (where is the point where a simulation is so complex and complete that it has rights?)

            Also, they cannot analyze even with normal universe situations instantly. A culture vessal took years to parse all of Earth circa 1970 (The State of the Art). And they cannot adapt instantly to knowledge outside the rules of what they know of physics (Excession), in fact they still didn't get their tech for the previewed future.

            Having said that, Creation is in a sense potentially post-scarcity. All is made of essence, and essence flows through all of creation. Minerals are replenished in mines due to the workings of the Loom of Fate and Gods and such, Elements are created on the poles constantly, and so on. And also there is the possibility of creating something out of the wyld, even something as big as Creation, and the wyld if infinite. It has been said that the machinery of Creation is made to create artificially limited resources to mortals to get better prayer-value for the Primordials, and now the Gods.

            Supplanting the gods in yu-shan and reworking the machinery to serve mankind, along with N/A marvels created by the solars would make a post-scarcity society. Making this happen, and making it correctly, would be hard as hell, though.
