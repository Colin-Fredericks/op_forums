        Originally posted by Neall View Post
        Being Exalted is the baseline in Exalted. You cannot ever ignore another Exalt as a threat, period.

        And if we assume only Exalts are PCs, rather than going from an assumption of mortals and then the Exalted systematically ignoring parts of the mechanical engine, certain things change and parity in the party becomes way easier to achieve. If you divorce NPCs from that power curve and from using PC mechanics, you can do a lot more to help Storytellers match conflicts to the party and reduce cognitive load.
        This seems like what a game named "Exalted" should have tried from the beginning. But setting that potential argument aside, I'm eager to see the attempt made now.

        Playing an unExalted mortal seems like it should be niche enough that the system shouldn't be built around unExalted mortals.

        It wouldn't even seem complicated to play a mortal in Essence. No motes, no Exalted Advantages, no Excellencies, no Charms, possibly lower dice pools, and as many penalties from the storyteller as seems necessary to reflect a mortal's greater mortality in the face of injury and disease.

        formerly Tornado Wolf, formerly Inugami
