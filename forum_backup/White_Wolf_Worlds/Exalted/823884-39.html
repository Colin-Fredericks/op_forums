            Stellar Hawk (Grand Daiklave ····)
            Stellar Hawk is a long and slender curved slashing sword. Its spine is made of moonsilver and its edge of starmetal. Its hilt is made of plain wood and wrapped in black-dyed spider silk. The Old Realm character for “honour” is inscribed upon the guard in orichalcum. It has a single hearthstone socket under the wrap of the handle.
            It was forged by an Eclipse caste and gifted to his Dragon-Blooded bodyguard, who would stand in his place for duels of honour at the court of the deliberative. This Terrestrial warrior died protecting her lord during the Usurpation, and the blade was claimed as a trophy and consigned to the vaults of the shogunate. It is believed it saw use during the Balorian Crusade. Its current location is unknown.
            The nature of Stellar Hawk favours precision over strength, and was made to defeat single opponents in formalized duels, often with diplomatic consequences.

            By increasing the attunement cost to 8m, the wielder of Stellar Hawk gains the following benefit: when she takes an aim action she also gains the benefits of a full defence action at no cost only against the opponent she’s aiming at.

            Evocations of Stellar Hawk:

            Gentle Kiss of Steel:
            Cost: 1w; Mins: Essence 1
            Type: Reflexive
            Keywords: Psyche
            Duration: Instant
            Prerequisite Charms: None
            The wielder may activate this power upon rolling a decisive attack that would lethally incapacitate her opponent. In an instant, the blade is seen to dematerialize as it passes through her opponent’s body, leaving their flesh unharmed, but slashing away something else.
            The attack deals no damage, but the recipient immediately loses all initiative and is crashed (unless they were already at less than zero) and enters a decision point where they must call upon a defining intimacy and spend 3w or surrender. Even if they manage to resist, they take a -4 wound penalty to all offensive actions. This is an emotion effect that lasts until the end of the scene.
            Additionally, if any conditions were agreed upon before the fight took place, they gain a defining intimacy towards keeping their word.
            Special: only characters who have an intimacy related to honour or keeping their word of at least major intensity can learn this Evocation.

            Humbling Victory Meditation:
            Cost: 5m 1w; Mins: Essence 3
            Type: Reflexive
            Keywords: None
            Duration: Instant
            Prerequisite Charms: Gentle Kiss of Steel
            All duellists know that instant just after the final blow is struck, a moment of infinite stillness, an ephemeral nirvana. The wisest learn the most of such moments, grasping the threads of their opponent’s life as they extinguish it.
            This effect can be activated upon incapacitating a significant opponent with a decisive attack, allowing the wielder to reflexively Join Battle. If used alongside the prerequisite, ignore the willpower cost.

            Momentous Reflection:
            Cost: 3m; Mins: Essence 1
            Type: Supplemental
            Keywords: None
            Duration: Instant
            Prerequisite Charms: None
            The duellist alights at the beginning of the encounter, assuming a perfect balance of stillness and motion.
            This supplements a Join Battle roll based on awareness, rerolling all 1s. The wielder may take a reflexive aim action against anyone who rolls lower than she does.

            Flowing Quicksilver Concentration:
            Cost: 3m; Mins: Essence 1
            Type: Supplemental
            Keywords: None
            Duration: Instant
            Prerequisite Charms: Momentous Reflection
            The duellist achieves perfect focus, concentrating on her opponent even as she keeps their pace.
            This supplements a rush action. If successful, the wielder may take a reflexive aim action against the rushed target.
            Additionally, upon unlocking this Evocation, the wielder may take aim actions on turns on which she has moved, and reflexive movement granted by rush or disengage actions, as well as movement granted by charms no longer disrupt her aim. Knockback and knockdown effects can still make her lose her aim, as can taking any actions whose target isn’t her aimed opponent.

            Approaching Disillusion:
            Cost: 3m; Mins: Essence 1
            Type: Supplemental
            Keywords: Uniform
            Duration: Until Released
            Prerequisite Charms: None
            The duellist slows her breath and visualizes the thread of her opponent’s life as she prepares to strike.
            This supplements an aim action. The next attack the wielder makes will benefit from double 10s on the damage roll, or double 9s if it’s withering. If this effect is cast several times on consecutive turns, each turn spent aiming allows to double an additional number, to a maximum of double 7s.

            Perfected Strike Methodology:
            Cost: 5m; Mins: Essence 2
            Type: Supplemental
            Keywords: Dual, Perilous
            Duration: Instant
            Prerequisite Charms: Approaching Disillusion
            The duellist relies on precise slashes, cutting her opponents down where they are weakest.
            This supplements an attack that benefits from an aim action, adding the aim bonus dice to the post-soak damage of a withering attack or to the raw damage of a decisive attack. Each turn of aiming beyond the first increases this bonus by +1.

            Infinite Eclipse Slash:
            Cost: 10m 1w; Mins: Essence 3
            Type: Supplemental
            Keywords: Uniform, Perilous
            Duration: Instant
            Prerequisite Charms: Flowing Quicksilver Concentration, Perfected Strike Methodology
            By virtue of endless repetition and devoted exercise, the duellist has mastered a technique that rivals the arts of the divine. Her blade traces an elegant arc and leaves behind an afterimage, which draws blood a second time as it catches up, striking twice with a single motion.
            This supplements an attack that benefits from an aim action, turning all aim dice into successes. The wielder then immediately makes a second attack that also benefits from any aim actions. All valid charms that supplemented the first attack except the Excellency are duplicated at no additional cost. If used as part of a clash, both attacks are considered successful as long as one of them beats the opponent’s roll.
            The wielder may make any combination of withering or decisive attacks as she wishes. Once per scene, reset by spending three turns aiming without getting hit.

                Submitted for peer review. One of my players is doing a Dawn-caste archer, and he was interested in a powerbow that offered tactical battlefield control -- and that was less wantonly-destructive than Volcano Cutter's can get. We'd determined that he'd be picking this up from his peers in the Wyld Hunt, and that it would subsequently be something jade; the Wood associations followed almost immediately, which ultimately led to my attempt to give Maleficent's conjuration of thorns to a PC.

                If anyone has any suggestions regarding balance or additional effects (though I think these 4 are probably enough), please let me know!



                Emerald Reach (Artifact 5 - Green Jade/Orichalcum Long Powerbow)
                Hidden texts of the Immaculate Order explains that this bow is how Sextes Jylis defended himself from the dead and the Wyldlings, while he returned life to the lands in the wake of the war against the Anathema. The true story is somewhat stranger -- once, an artisan wished to sojourn to the Elemental Poles, to bear witness to primal elemental flows. The journey, while tremendously dangerous in the days of old, was not without its reward, for the artisan came back inspired.
                This long powerbow was forged from green jadesteel; an Immaculate-friendly iconic image of Sextes Jylis the Dragon is coiled around its length, orichalcum flecking visible in its scales, with a hearthstone setting mounted in the Immaculate Dragon's eye. The bowstring is a single hair from the beard of Sextes Jylis himself.

                A Solar or Dragon-Blood who attunes Emerald Reach awakens Life Ever-Blooming for free. They may spend an additional 3m on attunement to cause any arrows fired to sprout thorns mid-flight, increasing Emerald Reach's overwhelming value by 1.


                Life Ever-Blooming
                Cost: 5m, 5i OR 10m Mins: Essence 1
                Type: Simple
                Keywords: Uniform, Perilous
                Duration: One scene
                Prerequisites: None
                A surge of verdant Essence coalesces along the the head of a nocked arrow, and the wielder launches it at a target up to Medium range away. Life Ever-Blooming is a [withering] or [decisive] attack. Regardless of whether or not the attack damages its opponent, the arrow drives inevitably into the ground, unleashing a torrent of geomantic fertility. Life Ever-Blooming cannot be used against targets who are not on the ground, or on at least the ground floor of a building, but may be used against a target location. If used in combat, this Evocation costs 5m, 5i. Outside of combat, its mote cost is 10m, and may be used only [Essence] times per scene.
                A [bramblesnarl] of thick, thorny, woody vines erupts from the affected site, forming a nigh-impassable barrier-net that obstructs and attempts to snap up everything within Close range. Any characters within Close range of the thorny 'explosion' may attempt to reflexively Disengage from the growth site, rolling Dexterity + Dodge against a difficulty of the Solar's [Essence+3]. Failure at this roll means that the character has been caught within the [bramblesnarl]. Until they escape, their actions are impaired as though they are being grappled (-2 Defense, unable to take Movement actions, unable to flurry, at -1 die to all attacks, not cumulative with actual grapples). On the entangled character's action, they may begin to attempt to escape the bramblesnarl.
                The agile may attempt to weave their way through the thorns without injuring themselves. This is an extended miscellaneous action, difficulty 2 with 5 cumulative successes, that may not be placed in a flurry. After an attempt roll, they must avoid the thorns with another Dexterity+Dodge roll; every success below 5 causes 1 die of lethal environmental damage.
                The strong and bold may attempt to force their way through. The bramblesnarl may be attacked as if it were a mass combat unit with Defense 0 and Soak 7 (13 against attacks with the Bashing tag); dealing 9 levels of damage is enough to allow one character to cut their way out, or to cut a path to another character caught inside. Cutting apart the bramblesnarl entirely requires superior area-effect firepower, such as Death of Obsidian Butterflies or the burning fury of Volcano Cutter.
                A [bramblesnarl], once placed, offers Heavy cover, even for those unfortunate enough to be caught inside. Its Essence-charged state lasts for one scene, after which the Essence fueling the growth fades away. After this time, the bramblesnarl is an ordinary if tough and fire-resistant plant, subject to the ravages of nature.

                Life Ever-Intoxicating
                Cost: -- (+3m) Mins: Essence 2
                Type: Permanent
                Keywords: --
                Duration: Permanent
                Prerequisites: Life Ever-Blooming
                The wielder of Emerald Reach may spend an additional 3m when using Life Ever-Blooming to cause small, purple-and-pink flowers to blossom along the lengths of a [bramblesnarl]. These flowers radiate a syrupy-sweet, narcotic scent, dulling the reflexes and will of anyone nearby. This is an ongoing Poison effect that radiates out to Close distance from the bramblesnarl, with a Stamina+Resistance difficulty of 3 and damage of 2i (1B in Crash). Damage from this poison does not roll over into lethal damage, but anyone who spends more than ten combat rounds unconscious within the radius of the bramblesnarl cannot be awakened for the next week. The duration of this effect can be treated like any other poison with an Intelligence+Medicine roll, difficulty 1, where each threshold success speeds awakening by one day.
                The narcotic poison is active for one hour after the bramblesnarl is created, though the sickly-sweet smell remains in the air for up to a day afterward.


                Life Ever-Grasping
                Cost: 4m+1m and 2i per active bramblesnarl Mins: Essence 2
                Type: Simple
                Keywords: Perilous
                Duration: Instant
                Prerequisites: Life Ever-Intoxicating
                With their free hand, the wielder of Emerald Reach forms the Mudra of Sextes Jylis, and all active [bramblesnarls] surge with preternatural life, lashing out with their thorny vines to draw prey into its reaches. Each bramblesnarl may make a [decisive] attack with a dice pool of (Wielder's Archery + Dexterity + Essence); this attack is always an automatically-successful grappling gambit if it connects, rolling (Wielder's Archery+Strength) for the control roll. If the bramblesnarl succeeds at this roll, its thorns inflict an environmental hazard of 3L, Difficulty 3 Stamina+Resistance to avoid damage, and the target is pulled bodily off their feet into the depths of the bramblesnarl before being released by the thorny grapple.
                If two bramblesnarls are at Short range from one another, they may alternately surge towards one another, intertwining their vines to effectively extend their reach. This merges the two bramblesnarls into a single, much-larger entity. Any character caught between the two merging bramblesnarls may attempt to Disengage as described in Life Ever-Blooming; failure means they are caught within the larger bramblesnarl as described in Life Ever-Blooming.

                Life Ever-Hungry
                Cost: -- (+1wp) Mins: Essence 3
                Type: Permanent
                Keywords: Uniform
                Duration: One scene
                The thorns of the Emerald Reach rip and tear even the fleshless. This Evocation enhances Life Ever-Blooming, allowing the character to pay a surcharge of +1wp when creating a bramblesnarl; the bramblesnarl's vines can now affect, entrap, injure, and poison dematerialized beings. If an Ever-Hungry bramblesnarl merges with another with Life Ever-Grasping, the resultant larger bramblesnarl is also Ever-Hungry.

                    Phoenix Heart Blade (Red Jade Reaper Daiklave ••••)
                    Phoenix Heart Blade allows her wielder to focus a battle, drawing a single opponent apart from his peers and wearing him down without concern. As the two draw closer, she becomes more effective at piercing her opponents' defenses, and eventually learns to reignite her blade, rejuvenating alongside it as she strikes down her chosen foe and returns to the broader battlefield.

                    Phoenix Heart Blade is an exquisitely well-designed and balanced red jade reaper daiklave. On close inspection of the blade's surface, one can see the faintest trace of the garda bird feathers that went into the forging. Two hearthstone sockets.

                    Evocations:
                    A Solar or Dragon-Blooded who attunes to Phoenix Heart Blade gains the following power:
                    When the wielder first draws Phoenix Heart Blade in battle, her surface flows smoothly out of the sheath, crimson and orange feathers of raw fire following the curve of the blade. Phoenix Heart Blade has seven feathers, which are included in the costs of certain Evocations as "f". When the plumage of Phoenix Heart Blade is all burned away, her Essence lies in a torpor, not quite dead, but not truly alive either. Her plumage is restored to full when the Solar rolls Join Battle anew from entering a fight, or from certain Evocations.

                    Additionally, the act of attuning to Phoenix Heart Blade automatically awakens Ring of Fire Descending for no cost.

                    Ring of Fire Descending (4m, 1i, 2f; or 1f)
                    Type: Reflexive; Duration: One turn; Keywords: Withering-only
                    Prereqs: Essence 1; -
                    This Evocation is activated reflexively upon successfully dealing Initiative damage with a withering attack. The Solar takes advantage of how close she is to her foe and flicks her wrist in the wake of the strike, letting two feathers from her blade fall to the earth, surrounding her and her foe and bursting into a ring of fire which isolates them from the rest of the battle. When this Evocation is activated, she and her foe are trapped together within the circle; other characters are only caught within it alongside them in exceptional circumstances, at the Storyteller’s discretion.

                    Until the end of her next turn, all attacks into or out of the circle suffer a -2 penalty. So long as he is within close range of the circle, any character may enter or exit the circle as a reflexive action on his turn, but moving through the circle forces him to succeed at a difficulty 5 (Dexterity + Athletics) roll or suffer 2 Lethal damage in the process. Although it takes place on his turn, moving in or out of the circle does not, by itself, consume the character’s move action. The Solar has no special immunity to any of these clauses - the flames trap her as much as they trap her foes.

                    This Evocation may only be activated a second time while the Solar remains within the circle against a target also within the circle - in this case, the circle’s duration is refreshed, keeping all currently trapped within it so held, and it only requires a single feather to refresh it. Otherwise, she must either leave its radius or wait for its flames to die down in order to activate it again.

                    Grazing Flame (4m, 1f)
                    Type: Supplemental; Duration: Instant; Keywords: Withering-only
                    Prereqs: Essence 2; Ring of Fire Descending
                    This Evocation can only be activated against a target that has entered Initiative crash. If the withering attack it is applied to deals at least three Initiative damage, the target’s hair, clothes, or other peripheral features are set ablaze as the Solar casts aside a single feather from Phoenix Heart Blade.

                    Each round he remains alight, the victim suffers one Initiative damage (dealt after his action for that round). Further, for every five points of Initiative he falls below zero, he suffers a -1 to Defense (round down, so -9i means a -1 to Defense). Being on fire in this manner can be put out by the character stopping, dropping, and rolling, which is a miscellaneous action that cannot be placed in a flurry. An ally may also put out the flames by taking a miscellaneous action that cannot be placed in a flurry to douse or pat down the character on fire. Recovering from Initiative crash ends this effect.

                    Burning Blade (1+m, 1+f)
                    Type: Supplemental; Duration: Instant; Keywords: Dual
                    Prereqs: Essence 2; Ring of Fire Descending
                    The feathers of the blade are consumed to cast it in an ever greater fire, making her attacks hideously bright and dangerous. When this Evocation supplements a withering attack, it increases its raw damage by two per feather and mote expended. If it instead supplements a decisive attack, she turns one dice of raw damage into an automatic success for each feather and mote expended. She may only spend (Essence) feathers and motes on a single attack.

                    Special activation rules: If this Evocation is used in conjunction with a multiattack Charm such as Iron Whirlwind Attack which ordinarily allows a given Charm to be applied to every attack at no extra cost, the user must still pay the mote and feather cost for each strike she wishes the bonus of Burning Blade to apply to. A character might spend 2m 2f on the first attack, 3m 3f on the third, and 1m 1f on the fourth, or any other order and number she pleases, but she must spend them individually, and each enhances an individual attack; she cannot spend them in a single allotment to enhance all the attacks generated by such a Charm.

                    Funeral Pyre (1m)
                    Type: Supplemental; Keywords: Decisive-only; Duration: Instant
                    Prereqs: Essence 3; Burning Blade
                    This Evocation may only be activated when Phoenix Heart Blade is in its torpor, or when the other Evocations activated alongside Funeral Pyre would spend enough feathers to send the blade into its torpor. If the decisive attack this Evocation supplements successfully kills its target, their body is immolated and the blade renewed to its full plumage. Additionally, if the opponent is a non-trivial foe, the Solar recovers four motes, as the life and power that pulses through her renewed blade reaches her as well.

                    If the Solar is currently within the effect of Ring of Fire Descending when she kills an opponent with this Evocation, she gains eight motes instead. In the process, the circle of flame is drawn into the renewed Phoenix Heart Blade, removing it from the field of battle.



                    Light-Eater (Orichalcum Chain Shirt ••••)
                    Light-Eater is a hungry thing, chewing away at all light in its reach, helping to cloak the Solar from sight. Though bits and pieces of its stolen power may be thrown away, the stolen light will inevitably fester and grow large within the armor, until it finally bursts forth, brilliant and uncontrolled, when Light-Eater can hold no more.

                    Light-Eater is a beautiful, close-fitting weave of chains of orichalcum. When attuned and worn, it passively eats away at light that hits it, giving it an inky black color and serving to blend in with the natural shadows cast by any clothing worn above it. Three hearthstone sockets.

                    EVOCATIONS:
                    On attunement, Light-Eater gives the Solar one non-Charm success to Larceny rolls made to conceal the armor and disguise its presence or nature.

                    By committing an additional three motes on attunement, the wearer adds two dice to all stealth rolls.

                    Special Mechanic: As it operates and absorbs light, Light-Eater may accrue “light charges,” stored luminescent energy held within the orichalcum of the armor. These can be spent for special effects, and are noted with “lc” in the costs section of its various Evocations. Charges fade at the rate of approximately one every five minutes, and disperse entirely in a single, extremely obvious, flash of light if the Solar removes Light-Eater. Light-Eater may only hold ten light charges at maximum, and when it gathers that many, it glows with light, removing the effects of its attunement bonus, creating a -3 penalty to Stealth actions, and preventing certain Evocations from functioning.

                    Design Note: building up a ton of light charges is actually bad. You want to keep the number of light charges down as much as possible, but running Darkness at Noon (which is a really good Evocation for stealth, and expensive to turn off/on) will rapidly tick up your number of light charges in a way that’s hard to slow and virtually impossible to really stop.

                    Darkness at Noon (8m, 1wp)
                    Type: Simple; Duration: Indefinite; Keywords: Mute
                    Prereqs: Essence 2; -
                    While this Evocation is active, Light-Eater passively consumes and eats at the light surrounding the Solar. It creates a supernatural darkness out to close range, obscuring the Solar as light is eaten in the space surrounding them. Awareness rolls based primarily on sight suffer a -3 penalty while Darkness at Noon remains active; the Solar ignores this penalty.

                    Additionally, at the end of each round that Darkness at Noon is active, it gathers two light charges. When Light-Eater reaches its maximum number of light charges, this Evocation immediately ends and cannot be reactivated until Light-Eater has fewer than its maximum number of light charges. This Evocation may only be ended voluntarily if the Solar spends an additional three motes.

                    Splendid Radiance Protection (5m, 1lc)
                    Type: Reflexive; Duration: One round; Keywords: None
                    Prereqs: Essence 2; Darkness at Noon
                    Activating this Evocation causes Light-Eater to glow with its consumed light as it strengthens itself against oncoming attacks. It increases the Solar’s soak by four until the end of the round, but gives her a -3 penalty to all Stealth rolls for that period. Against unExalted creatures of darkness, this increases her soak by six instead, and grants her an additional three hardness.

                    Hiding in Sunlight (5m, 1lc)
                    Type: Simple; Duration: Instant; Keywords: Decisive-only
                    Prereqs: Essence 2; Splendid Radiance Protection
                    When the Solar activates this Evocation, she surprises her opponents with a brilliant burst of light, briefly obscuring her in its incandescence and distracting them as she slips off. This a difficulty 3 gambit that rolls (Wits + Larceny) against the (Wits + Resistance) of all opponents within two range bands of the Solar, and adds the Solar’s Essence to the initiative roll for the gambit. Record each opponent successfully affected by the gambit.

                    Immediately after the sudden flare of light, she makes a (Dexterity + Stealth) roll to enter concealment. Any opponent successfully affected by Hiding in Sunlight’s gambit suffers a -(Essence or 3, whichever is higher) penalty to contest this roll, even if he uses a sense other than sight or an Ability other than Awareness. For unExalted creatures of darkness, this penalty also applies to any other rolls made to detect concealed targets until the end of the next round.

                        Fleeting Shadows
                        (Silken Armor, Artifact 5)
                        Woven from the a captured twilight mist and stitched with silk of bound Anuhles, Fleeting Shadows was made for protection in the not uncommon event that a formal party was crashed by assassins and their ilk, or when a summoning and binding fails. Passed from its original weaver to his son, to his lover to a passing group of cultists, Fleeting Shadows remains an unknowable useful and fashionable protective garb.
                        Light as a shadow and just as modest, a Solar, Abyssal or Sidereal Exalted may pay three extra motes when attuning this armor to tighten it, thinning the waist, accentuating features, and increasing the wearer's Evasion and Appearance by 1. This may increase the wearer's effective Appearance above five.
                        Fluttering Fog Dodge
                        Cost: 4m; Mins: Essence 1
                        Type: Reflexive
                        Keywords: Uniform, Perilous
                        Duration: Until Next Turn
                        When the evening fog rolls off of the hills, it is beyond the capabilities of man to proceed with clear sight. Attempts to stop or capture the fog falter as the swirling mist encompasses and penetrates the night sky. Until the wearer's next turn, negate up to (Guile) points of penalties to the wearer's evasion.
                        Kiss from the Murk
                        Cost: 3m, 2i; Mins: Essence 1
                        Type: Reflexive
                        Keywords: Withering Only, Perilous
                        Duration: Instant
                        Prerequisites: Fluttering Fog Dodge
                        Tales of love found in bittered enemies embroiled on the battlefield are favored by bards and storytellers throughout Creation. After a successful withering attack, instead of rolling damage, perform an instill action upon the target of the attack with bonus dice on the social roll equal to the net hits on the withering attack.                                             Type: Supplemental; Duration: Instant; Keywords: None
                                            Prereqs: Essence 1; -
                                            Using this Evocation expends the Solar's move action for the turn, allowing her to leap a single range band in a chosen direction; if this would ordinarily require a Disengage action, she must still roll, and if she fails she is forced to change trajectory and merely rise vertically.
