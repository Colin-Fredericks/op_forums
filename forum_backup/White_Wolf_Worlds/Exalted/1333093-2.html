            I've been waiting for a while to get more Second Circle Demons in the monthlies, and overall I like this. Makario's powers are pretty fun and concise, and while I'm of a mixed mind on the art, it works fine - though it conveys a very different and more straighforwardly suspicious seeming than what I remember of his past presentation. In that sense, I feel the same for some of the Demon art in the core, but I don't think it's in itself inaccurate to his character.

            The dream-crafted wares being de-emphasized and his presentation not going into more detail of how his priorities as a supernatural being run there or his merchantile empire, are a bit of a shame. I think many of the Second Circle Demons in past editions have genuinely interesting or unusual characters, and I'd love it if a bit more wordcount could be found to show that. Another paragraph for Makarios could have introduced some more context for him quite easily.
