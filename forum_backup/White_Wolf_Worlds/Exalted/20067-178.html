                Charms with the eclipse keyword:
                Curse of the Dead (504)
                Black Breath of the Abyss (505)
                Broken Earth's Anguish (511)
                Towering Wheat Blessing (513)
                Legendary Reaper (513)
                Storm-Stirring Lash (514)
                Wrath From the Sky (514)
                Immolating Pyre (518)
                Prismatic Sea-Spoor Streamers (520)
                Racing Sailfish Surge (520)
                Seductive Shapechange (527)
                Night-Black Carapace (528)

                They are all from spirit/demon/not exalt type of creature
                The reason for the restriction is to avoid the explosion of charm combinations that need to be considered (if Eclipses can learn any charm, any charm can potentially combo with any other charm, leading to an impossible amount of work for the designers)

                        It doesn't stack with the Solar Appearance raising charms, Solars also have a charm for disguising themselves, and another for seducing across gender lines.

                        It's not "useless", it just stacks poorly against the four Solar charms it's competing with.

                        "But JD, one charm is fewer than four!"

                        It sure is, but each one of those charms is more powerful individually because they do other things as well (and each is cheaper XP-wise, and you can buy them at char gen). If you want a seduction charm Thousand Courtesan Ways is better over-all. If you want a "shapechange" charm, Flawlessly Impenetrable Disguise is better. If you want to specifically seduce someone whilst in disguise then sure, Seductive Shapechange is great... But not better than TCW and FID put together.

                        Seductive Shapechange is ok. I can't think when I'd ever take it, but it's a solid charm.
                        Night-Black Carapace (528) is ok. Not as good as Solar Resistance charms (or Artefact Armor, which is only 1XP more), but pretty good.
                        Immolating Pyre (518) is pretty useful. It's actually good.
                        Towering Wheat Blessing (513) and Legendary Reaper (513) are actually good enough that I might take them in play: looks like a great way to feed Followers and make friends.

                        The rest... Are fine for NPC spirits. (Broken Earth's Anguish would be great if it worked for YOUR DOMAIN; Black Breath of the Abyss blinds your allies, not your enemies and disappears once animas pop; Tidemare charms would be fantasic if they worked on land -- or if you're playing a game in the West where you don't have a ship and have to swim everywhere; the Storm Mother charm takes half an hour making it useless for PCs.)
