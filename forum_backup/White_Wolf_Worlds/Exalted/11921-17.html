            I hope this one isn't either too obscure or too obvious...

            A dynastic wedding almost done with it's preparations when a strangely dressed traveling apothecary slips appears uninvited, after hearing about the upcoming marriage, the apothecary offers the bride to be some of his wares, including some "marriage aids".

            Later, when the bride crosses the threshold to be escorted to the wedding, she suddenly falls dead.

            At first, the house apprehends the apothecary for poisoning the bride, but after they find a bejeweled sword in his pack that stubbornly refuses to be drawn, the apothecary slips out of his bonds and reveals himself to be a thaumaturge, claiming the murder was caused by a spirit of some kind, and that he can slay it only is he can discover it's true form, it's motive for the killing, and the truth behind it's origin.

            Slowly his investigation leads to the clues he requires, and once the mystery is solved, he reveals himself as a changing moon caste lunar and confronts the rogue cat god responsible for it all.

                                        Team YOZI
                                        A group of particularly violent faunus, bounded together by a shared sense of loss and a hatred for the upstart humanity. Truthfully, they've all been mentally shattered, and are used as weapons of terror by the White Fang.
                                        Maleficent Yridian
                                        Race: Hawk
                                        Weapon: Brass Dancer, full body power armor with wrist mounted flame throwers, rocket elbow, rocket feet and a jetpack.
                                        Semblance: Green Sun, pyrokinesis. Every flame she controls turns green.
                                        Appearance: Two ugly scars mar her shoulders, her eyes are glowing neon green, tanned, her hair is short and green.
                                        History: She lived in a place where human-faunus violence was still high and brutal, and frontier justice was the only justice. Once, her family ruled quite a lot of land, herself the newly appointed head of the house, but in retaliation to one ancient slight she lost her home, her fortune, her husband and daughter, and her wings. Now she is quite mad. Angry and insane.
                                        Oriana Argent
                                        Race: Cat
                                        Weapon: Kagami, a very odd machine, a technological marvel, reflecting the shattered genius of its wielder. It is capable of being every weapon possible, but it has no means to become any on its own. It needs another nearby weapon to imitate, clicking and rearranging all the right shiny silver pieces into place.
                                        Semblance: Silver Forest, copies other's Semblances, with a silver sheen!
                                        Appearance: Long glossy silver hair, silver eyes, silver ears, pale skin.
                                        History: Once a child in the poor ghettos far outside proper civilzation, all she ever had was a mirror and a lot of imagination. She preen herself, imagining herself as someone else, follow along behind other people, pretending to be them, and looking at different machines, postulating how one might make them into one super machine. Then bad men came, rounding up faunus and tossing them into prisons. She was thrown into a dark hole where she cracked her mirror, and her head. When the White Fang liberated them, she came out of the dark changed.
                                        Sanguin Zephyr
                                        Race: Wolf
                                        Weapon: Crimson Scourge, a chainsaw that transforms into a chaingun, wrapped around him by a chain.
                                        Semblance: Silent Wind
                                        Appearance: Long red hair the billows in the wind, red eyes, red ears, tail, the works. Lightly tanned skin.
                                        History: Some very bad men killed his sister, his only family, in some very bad ways, right in front of him too and everything. They were about to do the same to him as well, but he managed to loosen his bonds and un away. He ran away and never looked back, leaving it all behind, love, empathy, restraint.
                                        Isabelle Coal
                                        Race: Boar
                                        Weapon: Sky Twister, a pair of gauntlets that absorb kinetic energy, releasing their stores at her command to terrible force.
                                        Semblance: Nomad Force, Super Strength and Super Toughness to make an Implacable Man and The Juggernaut.
                                        Appearance: Dark brown skin, black eyes, black hair in an afro and big under canines jutting out from her lower lip. Her gauntlets have chains hanging off of them.
                                        History: They chained her, they dared chain her. She once bound free, without restraint, careless of walls or people or property and they took exception to that. And they chained her, like an animal that could be brought to heel! When she was freed by the gracious White Fang, she vowed never to be bound again, that she would destroy all the boundaries of humanity and faunus would run free, laughing and killing without restraint!

                                                        Project Razor is not a new thing. Autochthonia made its bridge to Creation centuries, but it was too little, too late. The habitable parts of the pole of metal are largely gone, and most of the people who once inhabited the Maker are gone with it. But a single metropolis hurled itself toward the core--the least corrupted part of the Great Maker--and has remained there, waiting out the long death of Autochthon. It's been so long, in fact, that the people of this city no longer remember Creation, Now, though, the Maker's death is imminent. Thinking themselves having escaped the death of their world, the larger populace does nothing; only the youth of the city delve into ancient charms and structures to create a new Champion, the Moonsilver Caste Ark of the New Dawn. Charged with seeking out other survivors, others who might be able to save their dying city and dying world, Ark's journeys lead her to Creation, where she encounters a pair of mortals and a formerly enslaved Bronze Tiger. She's sure the people from this world can save hers, but the journey back is long, and others along the way won't make it any easier.

                                                            In the Modern Age of Creation, war breaks out in remote parts of the world with astonishing regularity. Certain merchants, especially those belonging to the Guild, see quite the profit in selling weapons to fuel these wars. As soldiers kill each other, innocents are often caught in the crossfire or made targets by unscrupulous commanders. It is only natural that these wars create orphans.

                                                            One such orphan, a boy who was trained to hold a weapon before he could speak, has been Chosen by Luna for his devotion to his fellow orphans. He attacked an arms dealer, trying to take revenge for a girl who had been forced to the front lines to test a minefield. Though the merchant prince's bodyguards stopped him and locked him in a shipping crate, he emerged stronger, and cut a deal with the prince for the lives of his fellow orphans.

                                                            Now, he serves as the child bodyguard to the prince's sister, an equally-devious weapons merchant who travels Creation with her elite team of bodyguards, including a former Haslanti commander who lost all her soldiers in a battle in the South, a former gunnery chief from An-Teng, a man who used to be a police sniper, and many others. But this mortal woman has a very devious plan, one that only she knows the details of, and she has grown very, very attached to the little boy soldier assigned to her. Each job, however far removed from each other, seems to be part of a grander scheme, one that may put even the Sidereals to shame... if anyone knew what the goal was.
