                                                    This is a pretty obscure story, but:

                                                    ***

                                                    An Island to the West of Thorns is fighting a desperate war against the invading armies of the Mask of Winters, and has unofficially allied with nearby Scavenger Land states to do so. On this island, a young Dawn, Eclipse, Night and Zenith who along with a Twilight formed a Circle in the First Age, and have all recently exalted.

                                                    The Dawn Caste knows he could be useful on the front lines, but his patrician family has other expectations and he feels obliged to go do as they expect. His tutor, an especially zealous Gold Faction Sidereal, considers it inappropriate for a mere Sidereal to question a Solar's judgement on such matters and so is training him in the ways of bureaucracy and in the Immaculate philosophy. He does, however, introduce the Dawn Caste to some of his friends, including a scholar slowly dying of malaria and a spymaster. These two both quickly realize the Dawn Caste is hiding something extraordinary. The scholar initially attempts to discover what exactly the Dawn Caste is hiding, but finds it is more interesting to simply discuss natural history- a passion of both, and something the scholar knows a lot about through extensive study and the Dawn Caste knows a lot about through extensive memories of talking with Lunars and nature spirits about it. The spymaster's investigations eventually lead him to meet the rest of the circle and become a disciple of the Zenith Caste and a valuable ally to the Eclipse Caste and the Night Caste, 'though he struggles to reconcile this service with his oaths to the Empress. The scholar also attempts and eventually succeeds in persuading the Dawn Caste to defy his family's expectations and go to war.

                                                    The teenaged Eclipse Caste, meanwhile, has managed to get permission to travel to Nexus with her parents (a spy and a diplomat), and there has used a false identity (secret even from the other spies) to join a top secret team dedicated to persuading powerful Nexans to lend support to the alliance against the Mask. She performs various acts of espionage for this team- planting documents, gathering information and spreading rumors. But perhaps her main role is to serve as the handler of a soldier who, on their commander's orders, is having sex with several influential businesswomen in return for political favors- a state of affairs neither the soldier nor his handler is happy about, but which both consider necessary. Meanwhile, the Eclipse Caste is also writing a series of whimsical stories Letter-Within-a-Letter Technique. To most, they simply seem to be whimsical fantasy set in an anachronistic long-ago time. But Solars of the Eclipse's circle will recognize obvious allusions to their own lives in the First Age, which further create other obvious allusions back to the present- and from this perspective the stories are a detailed report of what the Eclipse Caste is doing and learning in Nexus.

                                                        South of Nexus lies a valley connected to the city by the Grey River. As the river flows to the southeast, it carries with it the refuse of the city. Over time, this refuse has accumulated in the valley and defiled it, causing it to become a place of decay and corruption, where the forgotten desperately swarmed in hope of a place to live.

                                                        Recently, a pair of the Anathema have arrived in the Valley in the hope of bringing some solace to the valley’s inhabitants with their powers, irredeemable though they be. One is a former Immaculate priestess whose desire to aid others corrupted her with false golden light, which she uses to protect and offer some aid to her followers. The other is her loyal guardian, a hulking brute clad entirely in silver armor who fights with the ferocity of beasts. While both are completely benevolent, they are still demons in the eyes of the Immaculate Order and the Wyld Hunt.

                                                        Now a lone Outcaste, seeking to rise in power, desires to eliminate the two Anathema to acquire glory for themselves.
