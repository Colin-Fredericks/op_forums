    There are only 100 sidereals...so how are there so many factions?

    09-25-2015, 03:53 PM
    There are but 100 Sidereals at any one time. Never any more, never any less. However, there are a ridiculous amount of factions and pseudo-factions nonetheless:

    Gold Faction
    Bronze Faction
    Independents
    Silver Faction
    Ronin
    Rakan Thulio

    Adding to this, the Bronze Faction is said to comprise the vast majority of Sids. So, if there's, say, 70 Bronze Faction guys, then that leaves only 30 for everyone else. Taking out Rakan, then you have 29. The Gold Faction has to have at least, say, 15, so you're telling me that the Independents, Silver Faction, and Ronin make do with the remaining 14? Unless the Gold Faction isn't a plurality of the Sids total, it just has the most members of all of them.

                      The Silver Faction is apparently at most two guys who think Lunars are really cool. They keep their Lunar dealings quiet, so they probably fall under Independent.

                      Let's say there are about 80 Sidereals active in Yu Shan. I think the order of most Sidereals to least is Bronze (40), Independents (25) (who in my opinion individually lean Bronze or Gold, but don't have the patience for Faction politics.), and Gold (15). The other twenty are Ronin or too young to Exalt. Bronze trends towards the older Exalts while Gold Faction members tended to be newly exalted.

                      Gold Faction is picking up members quickly since the Solars have returned. So it is possible that the numbers are more like Bronze (35), Gold (30), Independents (15). Bronze faction will lose members who were only Bronze for political reasons (and want to ride the rising Golden Star) or those who lost hope in the vision of Bronze. Gold Leaning Independents will finally commit once they see that the Solars are back in action.

                      The actual numbers will vary based on story teller.

                                              I imagine that the total numbers are probably somewhere around 55 Bronze Faction Sidereal, 20 Gold Faction Sidereal and 25 Others (including Ronin, those working with Thulio, those who actually are independent, young Sidereal who haven't Exalted yet, possible members who have some other philosophy that doesn't want to see Solars or Dragon Blooded in control of Creation, etc).

                                              Prior to the release of the Solar Exalted, the vast majority of Sidereals were claimed as members of the Bronze Faction, there was no Gold Faction and only a small handful of Sidereal were Independent. Once the Solars came back, some of the older Independents chose to side with the Gold Faction. Meanwhile younger members of the Bronze Faction, who would have to centuries to get anywhere in the faction either decided to join the Gold Faction, or go Independent and functionally develop ties to both factions (sort of like a mercenary) so that they can play a pivotal role in the future of the Five-score Botherhood and turn that into power and influence down the road, regardless of which faction wins.

                                              While the Bronze Faction still contains a majority of the Sidereal, and it tries to paint itself as being the most powerful, the reality is that it's position has slipped quite a bit and could very quickly be chipped away depending on what happens.
