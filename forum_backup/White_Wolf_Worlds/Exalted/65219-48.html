    During the Primordial War, the Terrestrial host was not yet divided into 300 gentes each pledged to serve a Solar for successive incarnations. That would have been terribly impractical, and the Solars hadn't consolidated their rule that thoroughly yet.

    Instead, the host was roughly divided into clans, ranging in size from "medium army" to "numerous gentes." Experienced Solar and Lunar leaders secured the lifetime allegiances of these clans through reputation, politics, and persuasion both fair and foul. When a Celestial leader died, their former Terrestrial followers sometimes spent a long time under their own command before being attracted to a new lord. Clans were also in a state of flux: they could split up or merge together, splinter groups might leave one clan to join another, survivors of multiple decimated clans might band together to found a new one.

    The Solars began to develop the gentes system during the Ochre Fountain Era and solidified it under the First Deliberative.

        Firearms existed during the First Age. They were somewhat rare (due to their particular ammunition), but commonly used by Lunars, Sidereals, and Dragon-Blooded. Artifact versions, especially Jade ones, are still commonly traded by Patricians and the Great Houses, but only as art pieces. Ammunition is hard to find during the Second Age. The most common Artifact versions were "Magma Revolvers" (pistols that held 6 bullets in a revolving chamber) and "Magma Rifles" (which held three bullets and were breech-loaded).

        The Magma weapons used two kinds of ammunition. The cheaper and more common ones were Fire Pearls. All one had to do was pop them in, the Orichalcum hammers then smashing the pearl and sending it flying with a loud blast.

        The second, and much more rare kind (extremely rare during the Second Age) were called Magma Rounds. These were pointed bullets formed of one of the Magical Materials and filled with alchemical fuel. The hammer smashed the end of the bullet and ignited the fuel, sending the round flying out like a rocket. When it hit something, the bullet intentionally underwent a structual failure and exploded (basically making them rocket-propelled grenades #FuckingAwesome). Since this detonated precious magic metals into useless shards, Magma rounds were reserved for only the most important and critical purposes. The MM determined the bullet's properties:

        Jade: Red Jade was by far the most common, as its Magma Rounds exploded into massive blazes like napalm bombs. Other colors were used though: Blue jade would either encase its target in ice or paralyze them lightning, black jade would release calming mist that also snuffed fires (making it useful for extreme fire-fighting emergencies), white jade smashed through structures and people like a rockslide, and green jade exploded into snarling tendrils.

        Moonsilver: Magma Rounds of this protean metal would explode into quicksilver tendrils that lashed out and attacked anyone nearby the target, making them great for crowd-clearing.

        Starmetal: When rounds of this metal exploded, the victims caught in the prismatic blast suffered problems in their fate--random bouts of misfortune would often befall them, and strange events were drawn to them like a moth to flame.

        Soulsteel: Less than 20 Magma Rounds of this metal exist. Even the Deathlords possess an astounding..12. No round of Soulsteel has ever been fired. However, if one were, its shards would drain the life from its victims, encasing them in a crimson-and-onyx cloud of death.

        Orichalcum: Extremely rare since Solars didn't use firearms much and getting the round to explode was much more difficult, Orichalcum rounds exploded with earth-shakingly titanic blasts that could annihilate the entire side of a multi-story building. Use with caution.

        Some shotguns exist (Artifact versions were called "Magma Deathspitters"), their massive and extremely rare Magma Slugs doing the above on a much greater scale (Moonsilver slugs would explode from the barrel into horrifying tentacles that killed everything in their path, Red Jade ones would explode with such force their flames could turn even a Warstrider into molten slag, etc.)

        A few Twilights came up with plans for Warstrider-mounted cannons that fired massive versions of Magma Rounds. They were never implemented (mainly because wasting that much MM on rounds designed to explode was extremely wasteful) and still lie in their manses or were burned by the Dragon-Blooded following the Usurpation.

        Also Magma Rounds exist in Exalted Modern.

        (This was inspired by the fan supplement the Armorium--but I added the various MM bullets and changed the artifact firearms so they could use Fire Pearls as well as Magma Rounds).

            In the Primordial War, Sidereals fostered their newly-incarnated children with Dragon-Blooded clans. This kept young Sidereals safer than they could be with mortal families, ensured they would not end up raised by Primordial loyalists, and gave them the education on how to be a dutiful Exalt starting from birth. It also freed the Sidereals from having to raise their own children while fighting in the War.

            They dropped this practice early in the Ochre Fountain Era, when they disovered that being brought up entirely by Terrestrial warrior clans ill-prepared their young colleagues for life as Bureau of Destiny officeworkers.

                Autochthonia possesses markedly different strata each defined by the "aesthetics of technology". The miles thick outer shell is composed almost entirely of various metal oxides and lithic cores and "simple" fire pits are the standard. As you more toward the Pole of Crystal it transitions through various levels, mostly industrial revolution esc in the area the eight nations occupy, before reaching data crystals and paramagnetic structures at the polar boundary. Those sufficiently attuned to the Maker can use this feature to navigate by following the development patterns of particular technologies in or out.

                The Pole of Crystal itself show the opposite, with the landscape becoming increasingly elegant and conceptional as you approach the Godmind. Outside the Chamber of the Design, the original thought-forms from which all other technology originate can be found. Even the Ministers are exceptionally careful about using any of them.

                What it took the nations, and the ministers, a long time to realize was that complexity and potency are not strictly correlated. There are some artifacts that can only be created on the rune graven forges of the outer reaches, and for Autochthon there is and was really no difference. The increasingly complexity allows for finer control and makes up for limitations in material or execution which are not present in the thought-forms. As such the First Forge outside the gates of the Godmind may be simple, but all the excellence and functionality of every forge that was and might be is contained within it. How to make proper use of it is something no one save the Maker has discovered.

The games are semi-sentient, and used to a horde of fully functional primordials competing at any one time. Should even one of the incarna be eliminated, it might register their expenditure as close to none, determine it's not being operated, and shut itself down.

                                                    My Version of the Underworld
                                                    The Underworld is made up of Memorium, the memories and lives of the Dead shape the Memorium when their Han plunges into the Underworld. In modern times this rarely has a large effect as it is averaged out with the rest of the memories that have already made up the Underworld, occasionally an exceptionally powerful soul will make changes to the landscape. In the beginning when the Memorium was almost completely unshaped each ghost created their own realms directly out of their memories, and "lived" out portions of their life over and over again.

                                                    The Underworld isn't a single dark reflection of Creation. While there is a "Dark Mirror" world, it is one of many. The Underworld also includes many hanging shells of Memorium, each linked to a specific era in Creation's history. Each of these at one time were the "Dark Mirror" of Creation (or a portion thereof). When a great shift occurs in Creation or vast numbers die this greatly changing the shared perception of Creation, this Dark Mirror, no longer reflecting Creation, and falls away. This realm is suspended from the few points of contact that hold it to Creation, and other Shells. These points of contact with Creation are the Shadowlands that were created during its tenure as Dark Mirror. Additionally when certain locations are very important in multiple eras this can tie those shells together as well. These Shells are called "Eras" (or Shells or Era Shells) and often referred to by the history that they correspond to. Not all Era Shells encompass all of Creation, smaller Eras that only cover parts (from single directions to Cities, and rarely smaller). Between and beneath these Era Shells exists the Labyrinth. If an Era Shell has its Contacts between it and Creation (and other Era Shells) severed then the Era will fall into the Labyrinth where it immediately comes under attack by the forces of the Abyss/Labyrinth. Some of the Kingdoms of Nephwracks were once Era Shells.Eventually unsupported Era Shells will fall through the Labyrinth and be consumed by the Void.

                                                    Venturing into the Underworld: When entering a Historical Shadowlands with the purpose of entering the Underworld, a character may end up in either the Dark Mirror of the current Creation or he may end up in the Era Shell associated with that Shadowland. To purposely choose which one you are entering takes an Occult roll and some knowledge of how/why the shadowland was created.

                                                    Travel Between Era Shells: Travel between the Era shells can be done one of 3 ways.
                                                    1. Travel to one of the Era Shell's Shadowlands and use it to reach the current Dark Mirror world then travel to the destination Era Shell's shadowlands.
                                                    2. The Labyrinth rests between the Era Shells and allows travel if very dangerously.
                                                    3. A shared location in history. If the same area is very important in two different Eras then the location acts as a transfer conduit between the two if you invoke the proper ritual. (Sijan is the most potent conduit in all of the Underworld(s), as it has been an important place of power ever since the Primordial War - the entire "life span" of the Underworld. More than 50% of all the Era Shells can be accessed from Sijan.)
                                                    Also Large portions of the Underworld are literally colorless except for shades of white, black, gray, and crimson.

                                                        Terrestrial blood is actually far more common than the Realm would have one believe. Dragon-Blooded are guaranteed to Exalt when they hit a certain threshold of Terrestrial blood compared to mortal (I'm thinking about 40% right now), and cannot at a lower. The reason that DB exaltation is so unpredictable is that DB aspects are all seperate blood types. The threshold has to be hit by ONE of the five aspects, and all DB are at least somewhat mixed.
