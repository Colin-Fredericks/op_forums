    The idea that people have a lower, animal soul is right there in the First Edition core book. It has always been the premise of hungry ghosts. It wasn't introduced in Exalted: the Abyssals, and it certainly wasn't contrived to answer niche Internet questions. For the record, I don't think Autochthon devouring only the po has anything to do with making him sympathetic, not least because I don't find the fate of the left behind huns to be sympathetic (to the extent that I care about sympathizing with Authochthon, which isn't much); I think it's entirely about creating the plot hook of lost ghosts in the Realm of Brass and Shadows who can stick around to haunt the Octet or explorers of the Reaches after Autochthon fuels himself using their more vital, lively part.
