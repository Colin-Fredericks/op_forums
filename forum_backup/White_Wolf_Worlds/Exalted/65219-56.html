                                            Some of my assumptions as Storyteller:
                                            • The Neverborn were originally manifest concepts of permanence, immortality, and other phenomena that when inverted make up the mortal condition.
                                            • As a corollary, Starmetal and Soulsteel as naturally occurring metals did not exist prior to the death of the Neverborn that came to represent the principle of reincarnation.
                                            • Isidoros is the oldest sentience in existence, not that he cares much. Adrian, Szoreny and the Ebon Dragon are tied for second, with Oramus, Cecelyne, and She Who Lives on in Her Name a distant third. An indeterminate number of Primordials arise before we get Theion, Cytheria, Autocthon, and Gaia, who are the youngest.
                                            • Autocthon's illness has less to do with housing a population of mortals within his body and more to do with the Great Curse. As such, it is beyond the scope of the game to try to cure him, but it would make for an interesting endgame.
                                            • The Incarnae, Gods, and lesser Spirits were originally subsidiary Primordial souls, whereas the Elementals and Dragons were those of Gaia, until Autocthon invented a way to separate those subordinates.

                                            Crunch isn't a hobby; it's a calling.
