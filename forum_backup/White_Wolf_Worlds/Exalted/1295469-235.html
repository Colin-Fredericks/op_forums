                    Originally posted by Epee102 View Post
                    How does Ysyr feel about Necromancy?
                    It's just another weird occult discipline practiced by foreigners. Ysyr has no particular tradition of producing necromancers.

                    Originally posted by Epee102 View Post
                    Does the Heptagram sanction classes on the topic?
                    Likewise, the Heptagram may have classes discussing necromancy, but doesn't cater specifically to the needs of a necromancer's education.

                    Originally posted by Epee102 View Post
                    Can a dragonblooded learn both or is it solely the domain of dragons of another color?
                    "Dragons of another color" — which was never supposed to be a formal name, just a colorful sidebar title — are no longer a thing in 3e. If Dragon-Blooded can initiate into necromancy (a question not yet resolved by the dev team), she may be pale and sickly if her player desires it, but that won't be because she belonged to some special sickly subclass of Dragon-Blooded. And we certainly don't want to say that you can't play a sickly Elric-expy Dragon-Blooded sorcerer because such a character was predestined for necromancy.

                    Developer for Exalted.

                    Want to write for Exalted? Look at the freelancer submission guidelines.
