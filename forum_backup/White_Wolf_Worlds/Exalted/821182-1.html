                                            The first three Charms alone make Crane an incredibly robust group defence/counterattack set. I have no idea what you guys are complaining about.

                                            Fluttering Cry of Warning seems pretty straightforward to me. It lets you make a Decisive counterattack against an enemy that attacks you. Unless you are in Full Defence, this doesn't meet the conditions to counterattack with Crane Form.

                                            If you are being attacked and you have a healthy initiative track, use FCoW in response. Add all but one of the rolled successes to your Parry. If the defence is successful, apply counterattack. If the attack is unlikely to meet your regular Parry you can always bank the successes and use them to defend against additional attacks during the turn, but this is secondary.
