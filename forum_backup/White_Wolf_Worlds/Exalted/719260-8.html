            Can someone check this initiation for balance?

            Legacy of Wonders
            Artifacts are a legacy of wonders, a shining testament to the might of the Chosen, a burning glyph in the face of Creation that embodies all that is fundamentally Right and Just. A pentagram of five wondrous metals can be alloyed in any way to set right any problem of the world, with only ingenuity, determination and vision, any artificer may forge the wonders to determine the course of an age.
            Shaping Rituals
            • Whenever the sorcerer forges an artifact of at least 3 dots, she gains (Craft + [Essence]) Sorcerous Motes that last for the rest of the story. These may never be restored, only replaced with new iterations of this shaping ritual.
            • When the sorcerer takes the first shape sorcery action to begin casting a spell and stunts it with a description of how she casts the spell through the artifact, she gains (stunt rating + 2) sorcerous motes towards completing this spell. This benefit can only be received once per scene. Stunts to enhance the sorcerer’s control spell do not count against the once per scene limit.
            Other Benefits

            The Right Hand of Power (Merit •••••): The Sorcerer may attune to artifacts. This causes them to add the artifact’s attune cost as a difficulty to all shape sorcery actions, gaining 1 sorcerous mote per threshold success instead of 1 per success. This is cumulative, though many never attune more than one artifact due to the increased difficulty. They may also use the artifact’s Evocations as spells, replacing their cost in motes with sorcerous motes.

            Puissant Crown Unvanquished (Merit •••••): The Sorcerer may count as a Dragon-Blooded for the purposes of learning and using Evocations for a single artifact.

            "There is a remedy for everything but death, a hope for everything but wickedness, and everything will lapse except righteousness."

                            Well, you should have a third ritual. And the phrasing is a bit awkward for both. Try this:
                            • Whenever the sorcerer forges an artifact, she gains (Intelligence + Occult) Sorcerous Motes, plus two motes times the Artifact's dot rating. These motes last for the duration of a story. Motes gained from further performances of this ritual replace the motes gained by previous performances.
                            • When the sorcerer stunts a shape sorcery action with a description of how she casts the spell through the artifact, she gains (stunt rating + 2) sorcerous motes towards completing this spell. This benefit can only be received once per scene per spell. Stunts to enhance the sorcerer’s control spell can receive this benefit any number of times.
                            • Through study of an artifact, a sorceror can gains momentary insights and so gather wisps of power. By spending a scene in study of an Artifact, other than her own creations, the sorceror can gain sorcerous motes equal to the threshold successes on an Intelligence + Occult roll with a Difficulty equal to the Artifact's rating. These motes last for a day.

                                        The Merits:

                                        The Right Hand of Power (Merit ••• or •••••): The Sorcerer may attune to artifacts by use of lengthy, intricate rituals. By succeeding at an extended (Intelligence + Occult) roll with a difficulty equal to the Artifact's rating, a goal number equal to the Artifact's normal attunement cost, a terminus of 5, and a roll interval of one day, the character can attune an Artifact to herself. The three-dot version of this Merit allows attunement to a single Artifact at a time; the five dot allows any number to be attuned.

                                        Puissant Crown Unvanquished (Merit •••••): The Sorcerer may learn and use Evocations for attuned Artifacts; where applicable, the sorceror is considered on par with a Terrestrial Exalted, though Evocations that specifically depend on Charms or other abilities unique to the Exalted are still inaccessible. Evocations that cost motes can be used, but the character must use sorcerous motes to empower them.

                                            Hey,

                                            I wanted to share an initiation I put together for a player in my upcoming game. I actually named the shaping rituals because... frankly, it made things easier for me. The first (Storm's Bounty) could easily be exchanged with the example stormy ritual in the book, but I preferred the notion of stunting the storm into the shaping, as did my player.

                                            Bloodsworn to Tempests
                                            Awakened by mingling blood with that of a god of storms and swearing binding oaths of loyalty before Heaven, your mind has been set ablaze with new insight. In stillness, patterns reveal themselves to your inner eye. In fury, insight strikes swift and devastating. You may be adopted kin to cloud herders, but it is the storm that speaks to you as a sibling. The sorcerer’s blood brothers and sisters lend their aid, but also their wrath and sorrow. The Merit costs may bear some tweaking. The "start of any journeys" clause in Tempest Harvesting is specifically included under the assumption that journeys = exposure to weather and also to offset the pain of dragging that poor Crafter out of their workshop.
                                            Shaping Rituals
                                            Storm’s Bounty
                                            The sorcerer gains sorcerous motes when using a stunt to incorporate a storm into a shaping action. If the storm is mere rains and winds, gain motes equal to the stunt’s rating. If the storm represents an environmental hazard, the stunt garners (stunt+3) motes instead. She may only gain these motes once per scene. Her control spell is not subject to the scene limitation.
                                            Storm’s Fury
                                            Upon gaining or acting on an Intimacy of hostility, anger, or sorrow, the sorcerer may reflexively add sorcerous motes equal to the value of the Intimacy to a shaping action. This expressly allows the sorcerer to maintain an ongoing shaping action while engaging in other actions. She may only draw on one such Intimacy per day with this ritual. Her control spell is exempt from this limitation, but the ritual may only grant up to 10 motes per scene.
                                            Parting Clouds
                                            Once per day when the sorcerer takes time to meditate, appraise the artistry of the Cloud Herders, or otherwise enjoys some time in quiet contemplation under the open sky, she may roll (Wits+Integrity) at a default difficulty of 1. She gains sorcerous motes equal to the number of successes. These motes are lost at the end of the day.
                                            Merits:
                                            Weather Eye (⚫⚫⚫):
                                            Your kinship allows you to intuitively predict weather patterns. Roll Perception+[Lore, Sail, or Survival] at a difficulty determined by the Storyteller. Success allows you to predict the weather for the next 2-3 days. Every threshold success adds another day to that time, to a limit of a week and a day. Predictions may be upset by sorcerous or divine interference.

                                            Voice of Thunder (⚫⚫):
                                            When you will it, your voice booms with palpable force. The sorcerer gains double 9s on all rolls where both volume and majesty would prove valuable, such as issuing orders in battle or performing before a large audience. She may also stunt usage of this talent to startle or deafen foes within Short range, but allies will also be at risk. The sorcerer may make herself intelligibly heard by those at Long range, and even those farther away may hear battlecries or incoherent shouts. Use of this ability can prove taxing, especially if used repeatedly or for extended periods.

                                            Tempest Harvesting (⚫⚫⚫⚫): The sorcerer is able to draw creative inspiration from whispers and howls of the wind, the shapes of clouds, or the rhythms of falling rain.. She gains 2sxp at the start of any journeys or when stunting how the weather is inspiring her creation. The sorcerer may instead utilize especially forceful weather conditions (downpours, driving gales, thunderstorms) as a Means toward sorcerous workings.

                                            Feedback is welcome!
                                            Garg
