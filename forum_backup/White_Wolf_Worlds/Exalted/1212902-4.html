    Originally posted by SoulGambit View Post
    That is, can you use Socialize to Instill outside of a court context?
    You see, that is a very good question. In the groups I play, Presence and Socialize are kinda like Charisma and Manipulation, different but sometimes confusingly similar.

    Presence is in your face, magnetic, attention hogging, bigger than life, confidence.

    Socialize is studied, subtle, polite, measured, precise, charming.

    We always played like you could choose which ability you wanted to use for the instills, but the ability descriptor seems a bit hazy in that it is not clear if it can only be used to instill in formal situations, and the only combat use is to read intentions or not. This is a good one for Ask the Devs.
