                    I think that, generally speaking, reskinning evocation trees isn't a big problem; Nightmare Cutter works fine as a Daiklaive, but Vance realised there was already a bunch of daiklaives (including 2 moonsilver) and no artefact boomerangs, so it ended up being a Sky-Cutter, rather than a daiklaive. I don't think anything in the evocation tree is broken on a medium melee weapon.

                    But the exception is when they combine with MA. Certainly MAs are designed with compatibility or lack thereof in mind; I think the evocation trees for weapons somewhat consider what MAs they are compatible with. (The same applies for Brawl/Melee; I notice none of the Brawl weapons have much in the way of defensive evocations, which maintains the key weakness of the Brawl tree.)

                    Plus, when we talk about evocation trees that were cut from the Core; well, the evocation trees in the Core were changed substantially before publication. They weren't just cut for word-count. Obviously John and Holden felt they weren't good for the game; perhaps some of them were overpowered.

                        Evocations changed because they were bloated as hell. John and Morke had another system planned for Evocations but were fired and pulled all their material (Morke claiming that his Stormcaller was so much better as he spent over 100 XP on its stuff).

                        Its also not the stats that matter when it come to Dawn's Fangs, more of the tags. Swords have the Melee tag. Do you want to give Melee access to Falling Hammer Strike? Tiger Claws have the Brawl tag, so they're not as bad there.

                        Originally posted by BrilliantRain View Post
                        I feel like, if you're using material that was cut from the game with certain martial arts and are finding the results amazingly powerful, it might not be the martial arts that are the problem.
                        No I think its more of a case if one particular thing makes something hideously broken? It might be good to have a look at both. Remember the Adamant Skin Gemstone with Laughing Wounds? Fun times. The difference here is that rather than breaking the game over its knee it makes one Martial Art go from weakest in the game to one of the strongest. Literally no other martial art gets that kind of power boost, the only one that comes close is Tiger and not nearly as much. I mention the Dawn's Fangs because thematically they also match Steel Devil's style too, both in fluff and in crunch. A person who wants to use them could make a case to their ST and not sound crazy like wanting to make Stormcaller into claws.

                        The same can be said of Righteous Devil. It has the opposite problem in that its crazy strong, the five shooter just makes it stronger. Or hell just use Sirrush as a template for an idea and you have a 1m automatic reloading action. 1m per shot is hen's feed. Or you know literally everyone ever wanting to replicate their cowboy aspect and going really hard for six/five shooters the second they realize that is a thing.

                        No if one item makes an entire style break in two I think you should start with the style itself.
