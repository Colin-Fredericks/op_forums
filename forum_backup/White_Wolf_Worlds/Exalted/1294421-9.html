                    Ok, I think it would be useful to consider more concrete examples, take it out of the white room as it were!

                    From the top of my head a Wyld Hunt might be called to deal with either a newly exalted Lunar, one which is reported harassing a region (so, not a newby) or to assault an entrenched Lunar (likely an elder). For the first your team is whomever is closest, the second will be more prepared and the last, well, that's more a full-blown military operation.

                    In any case, the DB's will not be going in blind and clueless. They will investigate. Scenes will be cased, witnesses canvassed and the Lunar profiled. Very handy for figuring the Tell and more. They should have an idea of its capabilities, powers, objective and preferred tactics. In other words, they can prepare. If you can figure out what it's after, lure it out and lay traps. If it's prone to flying away, have Agatae ready to materialize and pounce on it. Birds aren't the best forms to slug it out, you see. If it relies on disappearing among other animals, prepare area attacks. If it's the scary type or reliant on messing with your mind, do team building exercises, get to know each other intimately and use your magic to bolster your courage. Prepare workings or spirits with the sole purpose of spotting the Lunar, marking it visibly, or tracking it. Use poison. If you can figure out if the Lunar has allies or people they care about, stake them out, or take them hostage and use them against it.

                    In other words, first gather intelligence so you can formulate a real plan to counter whatever tricks the filthy Anathema has up its sleeve. The Dragonblooded's toolbox of tricks of their own is just about as large as that of the Lunars, so don't just blunder blindly into a fight hoping it will somehow work out.
