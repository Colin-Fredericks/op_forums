                            Originally posted by Flare View Post
                            Can you explain to me how Dervish Blade Frenzy actually works mechanically? Particularly if she has already learned Triple Attack Technique

                            Like, how does the sequence of events play out. The character hits an opponent with Empty Mind Strike, dealing damage, then she adds 1 point of Charge, then checks the extra successes on her Empty Mind Strike, then if her extra successes are past her opponent's Defense she triggers Triple Attack Technique? This would then deal just the Triple Attack Technique damage of (Dexterity + Charge + Base Damage) and checks if her successes remaining on the Empty Mind Strike after Triple Attack Technique beat her opponent's new Defense? Then, if they are, she adds a second instance of Triple Attack Technique's damage bonus (Dexterity + Charge) plus (Essence or 3) automatic successes?
                            Step 1. You use Double Attack Technique and hit, rolling damage normally. All the damage modifiers from Double Attack Technique/Triple Attack Technique apply in this step.

                            Step 2. If Double Attack Technique's damage satisfies Steel Devil Strike's threshold, you can use it to make a reflexive decisive attack.

                            Step 3. If your Steel Devil Strike's damage satisfies Empty Mind Strike Technique's threshold, you can use it to make a reflexive decisive attack.

                            Step 4. If Empty Mind Strike deals damage, you can use Dervish Blade Frenzy to gain +1 Charge and then reflexively use Double Attack Technique. If you don't know Triple Attack Technique, its benefits still apply to this attack. If you do know it, the Quadruple Attack Technique benefits come into play instead of the Triple Attack Technique benefits.

                            It's a lengthy process with a lot of interactions, so let me know if that wasn't clear.

                            And to clarify the damage by the Triple and Quadruple Attacks are Withering, not Decisive, even though Empty Mind Strike is a Decisive?

                            Yeah.
