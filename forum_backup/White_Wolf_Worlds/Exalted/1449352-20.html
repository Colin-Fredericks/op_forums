                            Originally posted by Chausse View Post
                            I don't even what Lintha is ? What's this ?

                            In very broad terms: Lintha is a pirate clan/crime family in the West, well biggest and most dangerous pirate clan in the West. Lintha often refers to the family and also people who sign up (usually by paying tribute) to enter the clan. The grandfathers and grandmothers are the family bosses essentially. A good amount of Lintha uses supernatural powers either inherited from breeding with demons like gills or sorcerous powers to control sea monsters, weather, and elementals. Just like the mafia, usually joining the Lintha means that you can't get out (relatively popular background for some exalted and an excuse to break away from the Lintha)

                                            Yeah, the Lintha thing is super strange. Any Lintha superfans out there who would rather have multiple Lintha-themed additions than one for the Forest Witches or some Third Circle Demon stats, or what have you?

                                            Originally posted by Gareth3 View Post
                                            Good point. The Sidereal equivalent has the same problem.
                                            Not exactly, because the Sidereal version doesn't impose any penalties. If you want your target to get the bonus, you'd tell them. If you're subtly manipulating an NPC by making it so they're more successful when they act in a specific role, so over time they do that more, then so be it.

                                            But the Getimian one is totally different. The conditioning thing it off the table, because it only lasts one scene. It can apply to every role, rather than just one in that scene. It has no text saying that other Fate stuff displaces it, so if all 64 Getimians are in the same room, they can each pile one onto the same person. Oddly, it doesn't require touch, which throws the total lack of resistance mechanics into sharper focus. And, of course, there's a penalty for acting against it, making this a nasty debuff as well as a way to help allies.

                                            I think the intention must be that 1m for -1 to most actions a target takes for a scene is an aggressive but fair rate for one of your splat's main schticks. There's no way the writers never thought of "follower" or "slave" or any other kind of "do not defy me, and then I tell you not to act" interaction. (This seems pretty strong to be irresistable from where I'm sitting, but they're the ones with playtest time.) It is probably not their intention to have a circle of Getimians each drop those labels onto the same person (-4 successes to everything is a lot of penalty) - I am reporting this, so hopefully Getimian loom stuff will remove other Getimian loom stuff (and maybe also Sidereal Destinies, but that's a more complex question).

                                            "For me, there's no fundamental conflict between really loving something and also seeing it as very profoundly flawed." -- Jay Eddidin

                                                Alright, so the Liminal Charms are a pleasant surprise. They have a nice spread of powers within their themes, while generally managing to be distinct from the Abyssals' deathly shit.

                                                It's a little awkward that there are both Aspect Modes and Nature Modes named for the Aspects, but the actual execution kind of looks like DB Aura, but better (compared to 3e) and more relevant (compared to XS). The Nature Modes for Last Exhalation of Secrets are especially neat, though I'd like them more as Aspect Modes (so that you could buy them permanently).
                                                Blood Nature looks like it's a lot more accessible than the others, since its self-harm option is the only one that doesn't require any external circumstance to trigger.

                                                I'm a sucker for meaty unkillable characters, too, so all the options to heal, get extra health, and generally Have Meat are very tempting. If I'm ever playing XS, I'm playing a Liminal.

                                                More Universal Charm Modes would be nice.
