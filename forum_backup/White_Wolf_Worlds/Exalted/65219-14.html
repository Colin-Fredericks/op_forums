    I've long found that Lytek tends to come across as the god of Solar Exaltation and that moving the Dragon-Blooded to Parad's domain only deepened those feelings.

    So I broke down any kind of rivalry between them (as it happens they're actually a couple) they just focus on heroism in different ways.

    Lytek can't not respect someone for being audacious and holding to their convictions, he would be genuinely honoured to meet Peleps Deled knowing full well that the Immaculate Monk would never accept his version of events.

    Parad isn't just about magical lineage but the origin of heroism and how one's parents and upbringing shape a person. When he looks at an Exalt he doesn't see a demigod but the product of their parent's union, he sees that a Dawn Caste has his father's sword arm or that a Changing Moon has her mother's beauty.

    In the aftermath of the Usurpation, Lytek was furious at how the Jade Prison had stolen potential from the world while Parad wept openly over the loss of the Golden Children.

                The reason Sidereals generally learn some form of martial arts is because of the mental discipline martial arts provide, not so much for the combat. A Sidereal's more impressive/confusing feats (moving a city by hand, punching fate in the nose, etc) require that strict mental discipline. While a Sidereal does not necessarily exercise this discipline at all times, he does so when performing such techniques.

                In addition to horngeek's preference theory (the idea that exaltations tend to go for the same kind of people) for exalts that reincarnate, the Terrestrial exalted's powers are reflected in their own bloodlines. In other words, a terrestrial exalt who learns/masters a technique makes it easier for his or her descendants to learn/master the technique as well.

                Luna and UCS think more highly of each other than they do themselves. UCS admires that Luna's abilities are in constant flux, adapting to whatever the situation requires. While he could undoubtedly do pretty much anything she does, he admires that she literally changes her very nature to do so. Luna, on the other hand, admires Sol's unrelenting strength and power. He does not need to adapt, for he is always ready, and she thinks of this in high regard.

                Luna and Sol's close companionship was intended to be the case when the former was made - the Titans did not want the moon and sun gods fighting with each other. The sex, however, was not planned.

                (might've posted this one already) The Solar-Lunar bond was Sol and Luna's idea. They saw their long companionship as a positive influence on both of them, and wanted their followers to have something similar. The clear hierarchy between the two (with Solars on top) was Autocthon's idea, since he wanted to make sure there was no conflict in command structure.

                Luna and Sol are the very definition of bestest buddies. With benefits.

                The game pieces for the Games of Divinity are as tall as a human being.

                                    To expand on my headcanon about Exaltation 'preferences'.

                                    These are almost always expressed in general terms, not specific, like I said. A specific Exaltation might 'prefer'... let's say Crafted Races. It'll very rarely be a specific kind of Crafted Race, and that's usually a sign that it's gone to two Djala in consecutive incarnations (for example)- and that Lytek's been falling down on the job. It'll be 'someone from the North'. The Exaltation may have two separate tendencies which work out to 'a woman from the North', but that's normally a sign that the previous bearer of the Exaltation lived all the way from being a Primordial War veteran right up to the Usurpation, and certainly no Exaltation's developed tendencies in three separate areas.

                                    The tendencies can be things like 'crafted race', of course, but also stuff like social status... not, generally, things like 'middle of a warzone'. I mean, yes, but that's inherent to the Caste, not the individual preferences of the Exaltation.

                                    Two separate pieces of headcanon, one somewhat related and one not:

                                    Firstly, the anima banner, when it goes iconic, is specific to the Exaltation, and while it may vary slightly from bearer to bearer, it will always take the same general theme. The iconic anima banner of she who bears the Exaltation of Triumphant Rose Guardian (drawing on a character of mine who I want to get into a game- this is also the source of the 'woman from the North' example) is always going to be floral in nature, or at least evocative of plants in some way.

                                    Finally, that bit from... either Dreams or Scroll of Heroes (yes, yes, lolregardinganyofthatascanon, I know, but I actually like a lot of what they do regarding Crafted Races). I remember reading in one of them that the Slave Races were ineligible for Celestial Exaltation. Strike that. The Solar Exalted had to be at least a little careful in their treatment of the Slave Races, because, well...

                                    Not even the gods could control where an Exaltation went.
