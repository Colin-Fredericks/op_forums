                                    War Dragon, Epimetheus, Zelbinnean, Lioness, thank you for popping in here to share your thoughts.

                                    Jen, thank you for the artwork in your Sidereal threads. It really helped me to imagine how Sidereal players feel. Especially that picture with Asna Firstborn getting jumped by angry Sids...

                                    Coik, Omicron, Jack in the Box, you folks are awesome, and I'm loving the different perspectives you are sharing. Thank you so much. I've got some questions and ideas that follow from stuff you've said.

                                    Originally posted by Jack In The Box View Post
                                    haha, your message reminds me of a small exalted comic in the BD book I guess. It was like 2 immaculate monks face to face basically saying :

                                    Hah, yes, a classic example of Peleps Deled! Some of the old chapter comics were really good.

                                    My understanding of Paradox gain from Simple Astrology is that it produces less than equivalent Classic Astrology. I did some theorizing, and spoilered it right below. My conclusion (and please tell me if you disagree) is this: Classic Astrology requires more explicit preparation, and can give more Paradox, but has a more varied range of powerful effects (rapid Willpower gain/loss, OMG!). Simple Astrology loses versatility and power, but is a little faster and produces less Paradox.


                                    My best reading of Simple Astrology is that Ascending/Descending Destinies only give 1 point of Paradox when applied to an Essence-user (so, 3 points to buff my current 3-person Circle), and nothing more (unless you fail or botch the roll). Meanwhile, the only Paradox gained from Resplendent Destinies seems to come from straining the identity with Resplendency Powers or breaking character.

                                    I did a quick comparison with normal Astrology, and came up with something like 4 Paradox dice per Ascending/Descending Destiny, assuming it covered the same Trigger/Scope/Duration/Frequency. If you increased the Scope to hit the whole Circle at once, you don't gain any extra Paradox, but the Effect Points needed jump from 8 (4 Frequency, 3 Essence Burden, 1 Duration) to 14 (+6 Essence Burden). Considering the best (min-maxed) pool I can currently build for the Effect Roll would be 16 dice... my chances of success are pretty slim.

                                    On the other hand, my min-maxed dice pool for Simple Astrology would be something like 19 dice (which Simple Astrology does not explicitly prevent from being converted to Auspicious Successes), against a Difficulty of 7 (self or Circle-mate). I could well get 12 effect points there.


                                    While I hate to lose some of the power that comes with Classic Astrology, I think Simple Astrology gives me a better chance for high rates of success, particularly when it comes to Resplendent Destinies, which I think are the more interesting parts of Astrology as it currently exists.

                                    So, my next questions revolve around synergies and workarounds. For example, could I use Embracing Life Method in my personal library in Yu-Shan, then reasonably make use of my Google-Spider or Research Assistant Invocation to harvest the clues remotely, rather than have to go all the way back to Yu-Shan? What do you think about my idea of disguising Resplendent Destinies as "summoning/fusing" with spirits, to help explain away the sudden disappearance of the engineer, and the equally sudden appearance of someone completely different?
