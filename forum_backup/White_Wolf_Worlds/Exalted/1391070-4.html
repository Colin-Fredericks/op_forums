                                        Playing only online with dicebots and using LCA's combat tracker shaves down some delay factors in my experience. The dicebots cut out most of the delays in rolling (counting up your dice, applying dicetricks by hand, counting up successes), and LCA makes keeping track of all your bits and bobs take less time as well.

                                        That said, I wouldn't really go to the full system for quick skirmishes either, and quick combat is something I agree with. I'm in a game in which we had a fighting tournament that was all NPCs and one PC, and for each fight we did a simple roll-off---including the ones where the PC was in the ring.

                                        You could even use a quick roll-off system for fights where it's not really a question of whether the PCs win, but instead a question of "how cleanly" or "how quickly" or "how impressively" or whatever, depending on what's at stake in the narrative. That kind of makes more sense to me in some situations than actually having the roll-off determine whether the PCs win at all, if it's a case where you can reasonably project that they'd probably win one way or another. If your fightstick Dawn rolls off against a schlubby mortal guard to try to knock him out quickly and happens to lose that roll, it seems a little goofy if somehow that guard kicks the Dawn's ass---if you played that fight out for real, it is highly unlikely that the mortal would be able to win. Instead I'd have that failure mean the guard is able to alert others before he's KO'd, or he manages to scramble away, or even that the Dawn accidentally kills him rather than just knocking him out (though this is only for if the killing is a significantly suboptimal result in your scenario).

                                            Originally posted by DrLoveMonkey View Post

                                            Out of curiosity, how much actual time and how many rounds would you expect a fight against two Cataphracts and a battlegroup to go? With some groups I’ve run it would take two rounds and maybe a half hour out of a 3-5 hour session.
                                            With the 4-5 person group I had? Two hours, easy. We played ~3 hours once a week; the longest fight (four Solars against three war gods in a complex arena) took two straight sessions.

                                            For quick combat, I'm wondering about something along the lines of: "everyone rolls Dex+Combat skill, and each side adds their successes together."
                                            • If the players win, they're good--they kick their foes' asses with no more resource expenditure than they dumped into the initial roll. That represents a fight where they just steamroller everything.
                                            • If the players lose, they have to buy off the difference-- for every success the foe beats them by, the group (collectively) has to spend 1 health level or 1wp or 5m. (Or something of that nature, like a point of Size of an battle group or the death of a minor ally), That represents a fight significant enough that it involved some danger and resource drain. (They could also just accept the loss and take the consequences, of course).

                                            That gives you a fairly fast resolution that involves the whole group and focuses in on the actual question asked by chaff fights-- not "did anyone get hurt," but rather "how many resources did we burn?"
