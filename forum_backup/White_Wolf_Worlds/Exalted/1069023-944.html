                                                    I have a question about Charms that convert damage dice into automatic successes. More specifically the White Reaper Charm, Greatest Killer Attitude. It reads:
                                                    Supplemental, Dual, Instant - A withering attack converts up to (Strength) dice of post-soak damage to automatic successes, and ignores any soak bonuses a battle group receives from its Size. Decisive attacks convert (lower of Strength or Essence) dice of raw damage to successes, and also double their effective raw damage for calculating how many extra levels of damage they deal to a battle group.

                                                    My questions are these:

                                                    -Do you use this ability before rolling attack or damage?

                                                    -Since it says convert up (Strength) dice of post soak or convert (Strength or Essence lower) of raw damage to successes, when do these conversions take place, before rolling damage or after? So for example if your post soak withering damage is 6 on a withering attack and your Strength is 3, do you convert the 3 into successes before you roll leaving you to only roll the remaining 3 or do you roll all 6 and choose which 3 to convert (obviously only converting the failures).

                                                    -These converted automatic successes still follow the Charm Limitation rule, right?

                                                        #14174
                                                        10-06-2018, 02:06 AM
                                                        -Do you use this ability before rolling attack or damage?
                                                        Unless otherwise specified, you'd declare them before the attack roll.

                                                        -Since it says convert up (Strength) dice of post soak or convert (Strength or Essence lower) of raw damage to successes, when do these conversions take place, before rolling damage or after? So for example if your post soak withering damage is 6 on a withering attack and your Strength is 3, do you convert the 3 into successes before you roll leaving you to only roll the remaining 3 or do you roll all 6 and choose which 3 to convert (obviously only converting the failures).
                                                        The dice are converted to succeses before the damage roll.

                                                        -These converted automatic successes still follow the Charm Limitation rule, right?
                                                        No, converting dice to successes doesn't count as adding Charm dice unless otherwise specified. In theory, if you convert a Charm die to a success, it'd still count towards the dice limit, but most of the time you'll have non-Charm dice to convert before you'd need to convert Charm dice.
