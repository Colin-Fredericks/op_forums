        I have the base for a initiation for a player raised by ghosts in Sajin, still need to flesh it out and get a third ritual. Dead Remembrances
        Among the dead in Sajan there are thousands of years of sorcerous knowledge. Unfortunately the shades can not cast the spells they knew in life. The caster has listened to their stories and methods and has gleaned the intricacies of spell casting from the dead and how to fear those who use Necromancy and serve the Neverborn.
        Has the following effects
        -Once a day the caster can make a prayer and burned offering to an undead teacher. The ghost gives the Solar some arcane lost knowledge. The caster gains motes equal to the strength of the intimacy of the teacher plus the level of stunt. These motes stay with the Solar untill he uses this ability again. The motes count double for control spells.
        -The ancient dead try to deal with Necromancy and servants of the Void. They hone their senses to sense hatred and oblivion and teach it to their students. When joining battle with Undead or servants of oblivion (Deathknights, Specteres, Nephracks, Deathlords etc) the caster gains the opponents essence +2 motes to be used on sorcery. The caster does get a measure of the of the power they are up against, although a being using supernatural stealth does not ping this ability.
        -The caster has no negative modifiers for counterspelling or recognizing Necromancy. (kind of iffy right now because Necromancy does not have any rules)


        Merits:
        Deathtalker *: You are marked by death and the underworld, ghosts have a minor tie of respect to the player. Ghost see someone who could help them and someone they could trust. Unfortunately servants of Oblivion can see this as well.
        Living with Death***: You are used to living and studying the dead. Your body becomes accustomed to the feeling of death, you can respire motes in a Shadowland like it was normal creation.

            The Salinan Working

            In the First Age, the idealistic sorceress Salina and her students convinced the Solar Deliberative to permit the embedding of sorcerous knowledge in various “caches” around Creation, such as with spirits and elementals, or concealed in patterns of light and shadow on a forest floor, reflections of sunlight on a pond at certain times of day, and more beside. But Salina did more than this: she and her students embedded into the structure of Creation a guaranteed path to sorcerous initiation, open to anyone with the knowledge and determination instead of being guarded by corruptible academies or inimical creatures such as rakshasa or demons. But being an idealist, she wanted to be sure that those followed her path were at least decent people, not aspiring tyrants and warlords. So it was that her working included many trials intended to either eliminate or enlighten the unsuitable, and teaches shaping rituals based in the simple things Salina valued before all others: love, care, and respect for Creation and its inhabitants. You have followed the clues Salina and her students wove into Creation, and gained both insight and a measure of enlightenment along with sorcerous knowledge.

            Shaping Rituals

            A Salinan sorceror can draw strength from the bonds of family and friendship. Whenever she takes a shape sorcery action while within medium range of a character with a mutual Tie of love, friendship, or a similar emotion towards him, he may reap additional sorcerous motes equal to that Intimacy’s value (for example, four motes from a Defining Tie). A given Intimacy can only be tapped for motes in this fashion once per day, and the sorcerer cannot harvest power from unreciprocated feeling. Normally he may only draw upon one Intimacy to fuel the casting of a spell, but he may draw power from any available Intimacies when casting a control spell. He cannot draw more than ten sorcerous motes per scene with this ritual.

            As a steward of Creation, tending it and inhabitants strengthens the sorceror both emotionally and spiritually. A scene spent in tending to the wellbeing of an aspect of Creation allows the sorceror to roll (Intelligence+Occult) and harvest a number of sorcerous motes equal to the successes rolled, plus the value of any relevant Intimacy he holds toward it. These motes last for one day, and a given aspect or action can only be tended to once a day. No more than ten sorcerous motes can be harvested in any such scene. Valid examples of such tending include, but are not limited to, tending an herb garden, working on a mural to beautify a city, ministering to the sick or injured, or setting a controlled burn to keep a forest healthy.

            By singing certain songs, performing certain dances, or playing certain pieces of music, often accompanied by the burning of rare incenses, the scattering of exotic oils, or similar offerings, the sorceror can request spirits and elemental to give her some measure of power. The sorcerer may make a performance roll (Charisma + Performance, difficulty 5 minus the Resources value of any sacrifices offered along with the performance) seeking power from her supporters and allies in the spirit courts. Success grants her sorcerous motes equal to (her Essence + extra successes on the roll). These motes last for the duration of the story, and can be spent towards any spell she casts. She cannot undertake these performances more than once a day, and receiving new motes replaces any granted by past performances.

            Merits

            Creation is Your Classroom •• Those who have completed this initiation can locate fairly easily teachers or instructions for new spells they wish to learn, either in the form of spirits and elementals tasked with holding that knowledge, or in natural phenomena encoded with the relevant instructions, such as a species of butterfly whose swarms hold in the markings on their wings the instructions for casting Death of Obsidian Butterflies, or a lake in which the bronze-coloured light of the rising and setting sun shows rippled reflections which instruct the watcher in the casting of Invulnerable Skin of Bronze. Locating such a source is modelled as a sorcerous working of Ambition 1 and the same Circle as the spell desired. Failure of the working merely indicates that the sorceror must look elsewhere; a botch indicates that the source had been damaged in some way. Perhaps the butterflies are in their caterpillar state, or the lake has been polluted and must be cleaned to be read. A spirit or elemental might want or need something done before sharing its knowledge, as with a volcano god that wants to try octopus before it will consent to teach Magma Kraken. This working costs no XP; the learning of the spell does. Time and assistance can affect the terminus, but components and infrastructure do not normally apply.

                Originally posted by Skina Stonedottir View Post
                I wonder about this from a balance perspective; mara's power is difficult to leverage because you need to spend time building unrequited love in the people likely to be around you; it's much easier for you to build hate in yourself something that it is to instil feelings in others.
                Fair point, and one I wondered about myself. I'd like to point out however that Mara's ritual allows you to draw motes from multiple people present for your control spells AND they only need to be present, while my version only lets you tap one person (if multiple targets are affected by the spell, only the highest intimacy counts for motes) and can only draw from the actual target. The idea was to go for a Sith-esque "use your anger" type of thing, but maybe reversing it will work just as well - gain motes when casting spells at people that hate you? Would that be more balanced?

                Originally posted by Skina Stonedottir View Post
                I like this, although I wonder if it might end up being overpowered in play; I haven't tested the new combat rules much so I'm not sure how much lethal damage a sorcerer might be expected to deal over the course of combat, but if you're garnering twenty sorcerous motes over a battle I wonder if it might be unbalanced, at least without some kind of drawback or limit (eg: sorcerous motes may only be used for spells that directly deal violence).
                I'm worried this one is on the strong side as well, but not convinced. There are already lots of things keeping it in check though:
                - Only works on decisive attacks (so you'll need to spend at least some effort to build up initiative before you can start gathering motes)
                - Only works with this one weapon (so casting DoOB or BBQing some chumps with The Burning Name won't help me gather motes)
                - Only works if the target bleeds, and there are several categories of enemies that don't (automatons, elementals, ghosts and most other undead, probably some fair folk...)
                - Needs to be able to hold the daiklave to access the motes
                - Limited number of motes held at once, meaning even if you manage to gather a lot of motes over a combat scene, you'll have to spread them out on several spells rather than save up for a big one.
                It may still end up being too strong, in which case I'm leaning towards limiting it to Essence+WP motes/scene rather than held at once. I've started playing a game with this ritual now, will report how it feels after a while.

                This seems like it would be a merit - shaping rituals only grant sorcerous motes, not benefits like this one. No idea how many dots it should be - as you said, the rules on Necromancy aren't out yet.

                        I have one. Warning: this is extremely silly and should only rarely be used in serious games.

                        Visions of Bacchanalia
                        Congratulations, you've forged a pact with one or more of the gods of the Cerulean Lute of Harmony. Now, your revelries are for more than just their own benefit. In the thrumming of music, the laughter of friends, and the heady rush of wine you see visions of the inner workings of the world. As you peel away the world and grasp towards chemical joy, you are rewarded with assistance in the sorcerer's art.

                        When you begin a scene suffering a dice pool penalty due to intoxication, you receive 1 + Penalty sorcerous motes that can be spent at any point during the scene. These sorcerous motes vanish at the end of the scene or in the event that the character stops receiving a penalty from their revels.

                        Example: Drinky Crow is absolutely obliterated by the time the assassins burst into the palace. Because he has helped himself to the lions share of the Satrap's hard liquor, he is suffering a -3 penalty to all actions he takes; for such epic debauchery, fate rewards him with 4 sorcerous motes. As the assassins cluster together in an extremely 'Obsidian Butterfly Deathable' formation, he finishes the last of his drink.

                        As an added bonus, you no longer subtract penalties due being drunk or otherwise inebriated from any pool drawing on your occult skill.

                        Exalted is an aesthetic.

                                            I could use your input on an initiation I'm doing for a PC in my soon-to-Exalt mortals game.

                                            The sorcerer in question made contact with a powerful entity she calls "the Observer", a Lovecraftian mass of dark tentacles and disembodied inhuman eyes that is very knowledgeable when it comes to dark secrets of the universe and willing to share with her in exchange for every bit of First Age lore she can uncover.

                                            Since we didn't have time to homebrew it before the first game (crazy schedules), so far I gave her the choice from two existing rituals: the one from Scarred by Nightmares that lets you describe strange prophetic dreams and gain sorcerous motes instead of Willpower from sleeping, and the one from the Talisman of the Thousand eyes that lets gives motes for stunting the casting with the use of Talisman, except instead of the bauble she uses an occult symbol branded onto her right palm. I'm generally content with keeping these, as they fit the theme and the player likes them, but I wanted to give her something unique and to expand on the theme of knowledge, so I thought to myself: why not something that gives sorcerous motes for introducing facts? Here's what I came up with:

                                            Whenever the sorcerer introduces a fact that pertains to ancient secrets, magical places and/or beings, she gains a number of sorcerous motes equal to her Lore rating that last for the remainder of the scene. If she uses these motes to cast a spell affecting the being or place that was the subject of the introduced fact, each counts as two motes for the purpose of spell completion.

                                            What do you think? I'm not sure about giving a flat amount of motes, sounds kinda boring and might overly incentivize getting to Lore 5 ASAP. Maybe give motes equal to the number of successes on the Int+Lore roll? Half of that maybe?

                                                    The Path of the High and Low

                                                    This peculiar form of initiation is currently practiced only by a small group of a half-dozen women in the Wavecrest archipelago. The witches have varied in number between as few as two, at their founding, to as many as a dozen. The founders were explorers of sensation, and found though their experiments in pleasure and pain, of subjugation and domination, a path to sorcerous might, which they later taught to their initiates. They do not initiate men, though men could, in theory, learn and use these secrets. The rituals listed below are lengthy, requiring at least a full hour each. Spending a full day in performing a combined ritual adds three motes total. All rituals add motes equal to the rating of the lowest relevant Intimacy the participants share.

                                                    Through certain rituals, the initiate can harvest sorcerous motes from pain, whether inflicting or suffering it. Roll (Stamina+Occult); each success grants one mote. If both inflictor and sufferer are initiated in this tradition, both roll, and gain motes equal to the higher roll. The sufferer also takes at least one level of bashing damage, but also an additional mote. These motes last for the duration of a story, and motes from successive rituals replace existing motes.

                                                    Certain other rituals provide a similar benefit from sexual pleasure; this ritual’s success is determined by an (Appearance+Performance) roll, and no damage is inflicted.

                                                    The final ritual involves the domination of another, or being subjugated by another. This ritual is performed much as the rite of pain, but the roll is (Charisma + Occult), and no damage is inflicted.

                                                    Merits: I have no idea. All suggestions are welcome; currently, I'm thinking that a given pair should get some sort of benefit in partnered casting, or targeting each other. Perhaps they could develop something like a familiar bond with each other.

                                                    Yes, this is sect of lesbian BDSM sorceresses. I couldn’t resist, the idea was just so odd yet appropriate (and the image too appealing). And yes, I'm quite aware that this group would have a distinct advantage when it comes to sorcerous workings.
