                                    STAR CROSSED LOVERS
                                    (Orchulcrum warfan & hook sword, artifact ****)

                                    This is actually a pair of weapons that were forged together in the first age. They are an artifact war
                                    fan and a hooked daiklave. They were made by "Osep", a sidereal of serenity and talented artificer. Osep
                                    loved a solar called "Serene Tsunami". He created both weapons out of the same batch of orichalcum but
                                    both weapons have an inset jewel made of starmetal. Osep weaved in an enchantment through these orbs of
                                    starmetal which twined the fates of these weapons together. He gave the war fan to Tsunami and kept the
                                    hooked sword for himself so that the fate of the weapons would bring them together. However Osep died in
                                    the primordial war. Osep's hook sword, through some twist of fate, ended up finding its way to Tsunami
                                    who took it up and (wielding both weapons) invented the crane style of martial arts that is known today.
                                    It was said that while holding both weapons, Tsunami was untouchable. However she was killed during the
                                    Usurpation at a dinner where she did not have her weapons on her. The star crossed lovers were scattered
                                    and lost in creation. Every now and then throughout history one of the weapons has been found and attuned
                                    and then the other weapon will find its way to the user.

                                    These weapons are treated as a pair and only one 5m attune cost is paid for them. You must choose which
                                    is in your off hand and when attacking with its stats you get the offhand penalty of -1.
                                    Once attuned: these weapons were built to match Serene Tsunami's peaceful nature and as such
                                    incapacitating a foe with them is always non-fatal. When attuned and in hand, the war fan gives an
                                    athletics/swimming specialty, a war/signaling specialty, and a resistance/heat specialty.
                                    (These specialties are based on historical uses of war fans)

                                    Attunement: 5m
                                    Type: warfan Light (+5 ACC, +10 DMG, +0 DEF, OVW 3)
                                    Type: hook sword Medium (+3 ACC, +12 DMG, +1 DEF, OVW 4)
                                    Tags: Lethal, Martial Arts, Disarming
                                    Hearthstone slot(s): 1 in the pomel of the hook sword
                                    Era: First Age


                                    ENTWINED DESTINY
                                    Cost: 1m; Mins: Essence 1
                                    Type: reflexive
                                    Keywords: None
                                    Duration: instant/variable
                                    Prerequisites: none
                                    The star crossed lovers have a shared destiny and always end up together. While the exalt holds one of
                                    the pair she can call the other to her. This can instantly teleport the other weapon to the exalt's hand
                                    at short range. At longer ranges the weapon will always find its way back through some lucky twist of
                                    fate. This can take weeks if it is miles away and will take longer the further away it is.
                                    Prerequisites: none
                                    (based on Call the Blade)


                                    PEACEFUL PATH APPROACH
                                    Cost: 3m; Mins: Essence 1
                                    Type: supplemental
                                    Keywords: withering-only
                                    Duration: instant
                                    Prerequisites: Entwined Destiny
                                    Through conflict you can truly learn who somebody is. As the exalt fights her enemies wielding the star
                                    crossed lovers as a pair, they uncover truths about her enemies. On a withering attack that does at least
                                    five initiative damage the weapons will inform her of one intimacy the enemy has towards a group or
                                    principle representing a philosophical or ethical belief (if one exists). This allows you to use such an
                                    intimacy for purposes of Humbling Enlightenment Commentary and Kindly Sifu’s Quill. The story teller
                                    decides which intimacy is revealed.
                                    (based on Heart-Knowing Blade)


                                    WHIRLWIND DEFENSE PRANA
                                    Cost: 3m; Mins: Essence 2
                                    Type: reflexive
                                    Keywords: none
                                    Duration: one turn
                                    Prerequisites: The Peaceful Path
                                    The exalt dances through her opponents, surrounded by buffeting winds which distract them. She gracefully
                                    slips between her enemies and turns their numbers against them as they get in the way of each other.
                                    Until your next turn you may ignore all onslaught penalties.
                                    (based on brawl charm Iron Battle Focus, normally essence 1 but raised to essence 2)


                                    PEACEFUL FIST MEDITATION
                                    Cost: 1wp; Mins: Essence 3
                                    Type: reflexive
                                    Keywords: None
                                    Duration: instant
                                    Prerequisites: whirlwind defense prana, crane form.
                                    This charm upgrades crane form. When You win join battle you may spend 1wp to reflexively activate crane
                                    form.
                                    (based on Meteor Fist Meditation but costs extra wp because crane form is more powerful than Adamantine
                                    Fists of Battle upgrade)


                                    GRACEFUL WINGED FLIGHT
                                    Cost: 7m, 1wp; Mins: Essence 3
                                    Type: simple
                                    Keywords: None
                                    Duration: instant
                                    Prerequisites: peaceful fist meditation, monkey leap, Wings Spread to Sky.
                                    Without seeming to even use muscles, the exalt is effortlessly pulled into the air as if on strings of
                                    essence. She flies a long way through the air, clothes flapping in the wind, before landing gracefully.
                                    You can jump up to 4 range bands away. This counts as your move action and as such may require you to
                                    disengage. You get +3 (charm) dice to all disengage attempts and if the leap is used to close with
                                    another enemy then no initiative is lost from the disengage action.
                                    (based on Mountain-Crossing Leap Technique)
