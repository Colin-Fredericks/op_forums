    Originally posted by Eldagusto View Post
    Oh its meant to be Hubris, and like going out in the Wyld to create their own world it backfiring can work for the story too, or at least having unintended consequences.
    I think I'm against it in part because creating a world is more interesting than creating an Exalt. To me it kind of makes Exaltation feel like, as said elsewhere, the ends not the means. I don't think anything is added to Exalts by generating more Exalts. I think it actually kind of dilutes it since it makes it just part of a checklist of "Things power Exalts do because they're powerful" rather than something that feels thematically coherent or interesting. It's not just that it's hubris. It's kind of entitlement and htis assumption that Solar Exaltation is omnipotence that I don't like.

    Hell, I'd argue attempts to do it and failing are probably always going to be more itnersting than some assumption that enough Solarness = you get to do this too. But I also think folks over-sold the "Prove the impossible!" bit at times and that buying into this hype that everything can be done ever makes nothing actually feel challenging or narratively significant as constraints. I like things working in narrative boudnaries. I think sub-Exaltation is one that dilutes, rather than helps, and so shouldn't really be on the table. The reason they don't get to do it is simply put, it's just not good narrative that they can in my view.

    Originally posted by Eldagusto View Post
    But there are a lot of things you can want that by definition you can't do yourself. You can't birth your own Grandson for instance, well you could in a way but lets not.
    Or even just empowering your family, not something as simple as sorcery something genuine like creating a familial line of Guardians to ensure their prosperity. Or even ensure your culture survives, as many Exalted have discovered their birthcultures don't survive the test of Apocalypse as a sure thing.
    Why do those need to be Chosen? Lunars seem to get by making beastfolk without having them be Exalted. The Empress has her Legion of Silence. And Volivat has its Ten-Fathered folks running about. There are other ways to do this that probably just are going to be as useful and diverse rather than boiling it down to "A bootleg Exaltation will solve my issues!" None of this need be Exalted. And I think that saying they have to both undersells the effort of Exaltation and cheapens what it was origianlly you know, for. The Sun did it to shake the world order and overthrow the creators. Not to have someone deal with his immortal trust fund.

    Originally posted by Eldagusto View Post
    And you could be busy in another endeavor like war or discovery and say want someone to be a hero for a place you don't want to live, like say Malfeas, the Underworld, Rakshastan, Heaven, the Ocean, the Sky, or Underground.
    So you want Secretary Exalted? This doesn't actually, to me, help your case. A Chosen can't be everywhere at once. She should build a network of allies, and compatriots of diverse sorts to help where she can't be. Exaltation, especially in 3e, isn't meant to just be a power pellet. But I gather here it's basically you want to have Chosen fo Doing My Shit For Me, which isn't a strong case. And again, doesn't really get into what the Exaltation is besides "Something useful for the Exalt." It's theamtically hollow and wasteful in a world where there's hundreds of other ways to empower people that can fill those roles without having to be Exalted.

    Originally posted by Eldagusto View Post
    And there is the idea you want a legacy that extends beyond your lifetime. If half the Solars have been Compromised into not being Solars anymore and the Exigent's aren't reliably something you always want then you could want legacies that will survive you to fill in the missing spaces in the heroic tableau of creation.
    This goes a bit into my bit of "Working in constraints is better than just giving folks the whole palette". Deal with what you have and make that work, don't try to go around making knock-offs like that is somehow the Solution. And that you don't get your legacy to last forever, that eventually you too can be Ozymandius, is something I'm fine saying you can try to fix. But again, nothing of it requires an Exaltation. Non-Exalted stuff can't probably stand the task, but there's always an Ending remember. Even your legacy will fail.

    More Exalts does nothing to fix or fill this. It just turns Exaltation eventually something cheap. "Oh this area needs a new Exalt, better pop one out. Utopia forever!"

    Originally posted by Eldagusto View Post
    But two facet question. What is wrong with doing this as a capstone or Epilogue in a game, and what is wrong with doing it in the middle of a game. They should be two different discussions in a lot aspects. We mostly seem to have addressed only the latter. And a lot of good points have been made.
    Capstone I could care less, but I think that you haven't given a compelling reason why it would be interesting an end. Mostly since the "Exalted of an Exalt" sounds like, to not mince words, one of the least tangible and sensible things. What you describe to me sounds like Secretary Exalted, not something worthy of the title. If their whole point is just extend a Solar Exalt's presence and ability to be omnipotent, I don't really think I would like it since I don't like a "And they lived happily ever after." The goals you note that htey would be there to prevent, I think shouldn't be goals osmeone should reasonably ever feel are ever perfectly achieveable. The only thing you can be sure in time is there's always an Ending. Even to your legacy.

    As for mid-game, the big one is that unless you are taking the time to build-out an entire new Exalt type with Charms, themes and such, that is more than window dressing for characters to show How Cool They Are, then it doesn't to me add much in-game, distracts and wastes times if anything, and cheapens/dilutes Exaltation themes since youb asically are turning them into something your character gets to succeed at making and pat themselvs on the back, rather than you know,a ctually use their power to do things instead of delegating.

    And stuff.

                                            The things I'm not crazy about in the premises are:

                                            - Exalted themed off existing Exalted: It doesn't seem like the themes of the personalities of PCs as a particular Solar, etc. lend themselves as much to new Exalted as those of gods. I'm a bit suss of that sort of thing as it seems like the weak themes of the "Devil Tigers".
                                            - Suggestion (if I detect it right) that Exalted kind of "graduate" at high levels of power to their own Exalted is a bit rote and seems inconsistent with setting history.

                                            Those said, I wouldn't be against a character whose mission and thing is to be the handmaid of spreading the Exigency or finding new Exalted. Possibly more an NPC sort of thing though.
                                            In terms of totally creating totally new Exalted which are original, with a lot more investment of a character's personal power in doing so, I think you would need to find a strong rationale for why this is something a character would want to do.

                                            The Unconquered Sun created the Solars to be agents and champions of his will in the world and to fight his enemies, tasks that he was unsuitable to do directly (the Great Geas and the scale and restrictions of his power). The gods seek their own Exigent Exalted (when they do) for the same reasons.

                                            What is there that your PC can't do that she would want to go to the challenge of creating a new type of champion at huge personal expense of power to do? Exalted have a lot of capabilities already, though any particular Exalted is limited.

                                            If you can come up with an excellent rationale that works for your story, then I think it could work. But not without that. There's nothing un-thematic in Exalted to the story of a mighty Exalted sorcerer who sacrifices his life or magic to empower a human as a powerful champion of his will, with strange powers that no existing Exalted can exactly replicate.
