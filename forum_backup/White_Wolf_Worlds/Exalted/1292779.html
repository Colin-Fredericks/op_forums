    Shaping Rituals and Sorcerous Initiations

    02-28-2019, 11:46 AM
    Y'all should know by now I'm a big sorcery fan. After the release of the Lunar sorcery stuff this Tuesday I decided there's finally enough material out there between the Core book, the Dragon-blooded book, and the Lunars book to just compile everything into a single document... which I did. Since it's copy-pasted from the books, I'm pretty sure I can't post it here. BUT it got me looking more deeply at comparing, contrasting, and just generally thinking about Shaping Rituals and Sorcerous Initiations.

    Here's what we have so far:
    Core book
    • Bargain with Mara
    • Pact with an Ifrit Lord
    • Scarred by Nightmares
    • Soul-Perfecting Elixir
    • The Talisman of Ten Thousand Eyes
    Dragon-Blooded book
    • Student of the Heptagram
    • Wanasaan Exorcist
    Lunars book
    • Raksi's Tutelage
    • The Jasmine Gems of Mishiko
    • Root-Lore
    My favorite Initiation is easily the Pact with an Ifrit Lord, and my favorite Shaping Ritual is drawing motes from a source of fire. Its a great source of stunt fodder when shaping spells, and I've even used it strategically at the start of combat by snuffing out all the candles in a room so my spell casts in sudden darkness.

    As my fire-breaking Twilight approaches Essence 3, I am compelled to consider how he should initiate into the Celestial Circle. I'm leaning towards The Talisman of Ten Thousand Eyes (all around useful) or the Soul-Perfecting Elixir (mostly for the merits) but I find some fun stuff in all of them.

    So what are YOUR favorite Shaping rituals and Sorcerous Initiations? Why do you like them? Have they inspired some interesting new homebrew? Tell me about them! Constructive criticism about particular Initiations or Rituals is permitted, but this is largely a positive thread about Sorcery.

                                          I was happy to find that the Bargain with Mara initiation had themes and benefits that allowed me to simulate an Infernal sorcerer/swordsman (inspired by Yrkoon) using the core rules. That ritual that involves stealing the souls of people you seduce and exchanging them for power reminded me of the way Melniboneans were depicted sacrificing slaves to fuel sorcery in Michael Moorcock's Elric Vol. 1: The Ruby Throne, and dovetails with the character's background as a son of privilege from Ysyr. The Infernal Nobility merit in particular should come in quite handy for a sorcerer who interacts a lot with demons, and I love the aesthetics of demonic tattoos even though I wasn't able to do anything practical with them at chargen.
