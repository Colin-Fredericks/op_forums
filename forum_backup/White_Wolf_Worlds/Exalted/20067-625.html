                                        Originally posted by Beast of Bitter Oblivion View Post
                                        Assuming craft 5 and int 5 , can you guys tell me please : what’s the minimum possible set of charms an essence 3 solar need to semi reliably make 3 dot artifact?
                                        Well, it depends on what you mean by "semi-reliably", of course. For the sake of an answer, though, let's assume that means "more that 50% of such projects are successful". Artifact 3 has a goal number of 50, with a difficulty of 5 and a terminus of 6 rolls, so you'll need to net about 13 successes per roll to hit that.
                                        The Power charms in Craft are going to be your go-to ones here. Your base Excellency and (Attribute + Ability) gives you a pool of 20, so on average 10 successes on each roll. Lucky for you, the Power tree provides a bunch of stuff that will quickly bump you up there. Flawless Handiwork Method's mostly a prerequisite for the others, its effects aren't all that impressive on their own, but Supreme Masterwork Focus, Experiential Conjuring of True Void, and Unbroken Image Focus should be enough to take you over the top. You'll have to burn gold xp to fuel Unbroken Image Focus, but frankly, it's not hard at all for a Solar crafter to have all the gold and silver craft xp they'll ever need. If you want more reliability than "50% of the time", just buy a couple more charms into the Power tree, and you'll probably be good.
