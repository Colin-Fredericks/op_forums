    Originally posted by DrLoveMonkey View Post
    For example Solars might have an MA charm that’s a damage adder, adding (Strength) damage or (Strength + Stamina) with Mastery. Lunars on the other hand can just throw on a strength based damage adder and combo for a better effect at the costs more motes and requiring a purchase of those strength charms. As a baseline that’s something Solars can’t do, and might lead to a more powerful effect at the cost of purchasing more charms and spending more motes. Is that a problem? Because it might be used for styles and in ways that the Solar can’t also do by picking up another charm.
    I'm not quite following this?

    The thing is, not including lunar books, we current have 19 MA's of which all but 3 can used unarmed and therefore combined. If we include lunars we have another 8 confirmed which all will also be unarmed styles. That'll be 24 different charms set that can be mixed together and there are likely many more to come. The idea that there wont be some overpowered interactions on par with whatever mixing Lunar and MA charms can produce seems kinda unlikely
    With my starting Lunar I can punch people for 25 withering damage. Using... only Martial Arts charms (and not even spending WP, just some motes), not Strength charms. At that point, I'm not even sure I need Strength charms (I mean, I'll spend 2 motes for +2 damage with the Strength excellency, but it's hardly worth buying another charm).
    At Essence 3 I'll be able to punch them for 27... ignoring armour, and then stripping off another 3 initiative.
    And it could be higher, if I'd cheesed it more.

    Originally posted by Aquillion
    This is a logical consequence of Solars being heavily focused on the idea of their charms representing "skill", ie. you have to actually be highly skilled to use them, whereas DB charms are slightly more cheaty magic stuff. Thematically, you can have a DB who is only sort-of good with a sword but who fills the gaps with their magic, whereas that makes no sense at all for a Solar.
    Sure, sure.
    Still, it does make Solar character sheets boring. When I was running it, I could pretty much guarentee my players had 5 in every Ability they were rolling.

        To be honest, Ability prerequisites seem pretty loosely related to any Charm function and whether that Charm function seems to need skill or not - I'd scratch my head if trying to explain in detail why any of the Solar 'Essence Charged Arrow Attack' type Charms would actually *need* any particular level of skill. They just seem to be like that because that's the game design wants them to be restricted to characters who've met a certain level of investment, not because there's any obvious reason in setting why it should be so. (There's a rewrite of Exalted rules that does away with Ability pre-requisites for merely different dot based experience costs, and I'm hard pressed to think that even Solars on the page feel particularly different).

                    Some of these critiques are more valid than others, certainly. But I suspect that everyone in this thread is on-board with the idea that more cool-and-viable character concepts = better game.
