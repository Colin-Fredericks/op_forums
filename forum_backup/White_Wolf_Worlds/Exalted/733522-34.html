                Originally posted by Accelerator View Post

                Oh man. This sounds interesting tell me more.
                Look into how people lived before electric lighting, and then remember, that's how Creation is. People don't burn torches and candles all night every night, those are expendable resources and the cost would add up real quick. Practically everybody gets up early to maximize their daylight hours, and practically nobody does anything at night because.. Nobody can see goose shit.

                If indoor lighting were a commonplace thing? Well, people wouldn't need to worry about not having enough daylight. A lot more stuff would get done in winter/summer, whichever is the brutal non-farming season in your corner of creation. The South in particular would lap this stuff up, since nobody wants to be out doing things at noon in Resplendent Fire anyway. You just invented the concept of the nightlife. and made Charioscuro the City That Never Sleeps.

                The Guild's idea of a "work day" would change for the worse. If they can make slaves and the exploited underclass labor for twelve hours a day and extract more profit thereby, they will. And everyone else will have to keep up, won't they?

                With people now commonly doing commerce and labor at night. you're going to need a much beefier night watch if you don't want this all going straight to hell, so..That too, and all the knock-on effects thereof.

                You also just boned anybody whose livelihood depended on selling light. Anybody dealing in lamps, lamp oil, the harvesting of plants or animals from whence high-end lantern oil is derived, and so on? Is going to hate your guts. That stuff used to be highly profitable until you came along.

                        A plant that quickly regenerates leaves. The leaves, when crushed by a mortar and then scattered into a barrels worth of water, causes dirt and detritus to clump and sink to the bottom, as well as purifying the water of poison and disease.


                        Obvious question: is salt "dirt and detritus" for these purposes? Is saltwater a poison?

                        I'm going with Terrestrial 3 there

                        Create a completely new but mundane form of life, or breed a specimen of an existing species with a minor supernatural power that augments its strongest traits.

                        There are plants that filter water already., such as irises, bullrushes and water lilies. You could make a supernaturally effective one that acts as a much better filter against poison and disease if you want. I'm not sure if I'd let the powdered remains of the plant work, but I'd totally let a sorcerer put a potted lily in a barrel of diseased water and purify it.

                        \Or you could use a Terrestrial 3 working to just make a local water source remain clean and pure. Much handier for the locals, but less portable.

                                            I'd say that a new magical species of water lily could reproduce - those things are like invasive waterborne weeds. getting them to *stop* reproducing takes serious effort. They might actually feed on poison, filth and disease, though, (depending on your finesse and how much your GM cares about open-source water purification) meaning that once the area's purified, you'll have to transplant them into a new pit of fetid nastiness if you want to build their numbers.

                                            The knowledge-bushes? They make seeds, but if you want them to grow into other knowledge-bushes rather than mundane plants with pretty flowers, I'd probably require you to ritually prepare and purify a new site for them, which is in itself a Celestial working with a goal number of 20. So, way easier than making them in the first place. and easier than a Celestial working should be, but you still need proper initiation to quickly perform the geomantic shaping required to attune them to the constellations.
