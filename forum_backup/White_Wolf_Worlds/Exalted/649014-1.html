I can understand a Realm citizen who's used to getting fucked becoming disillusioned, but Dynasts get basically everything. However the Immaculate Philosophy's indoctrination is so deep and strong that he would probably rationalize it away. This is what happened to Ten Winds in Keychain of Creation, but he was an old monk who had seen this happen over and over again. The Fire-Aspect we're talking about here Exalted at 15-or-so less than a decade before he joins the circle.

            4: He's really fucking bored and just joins them 'cause why the fuck not

            I'm just wondering why he would do this. What would drive a DB to join this Solar and Lunar? I don't want to use Solar brainwash bullshit or Lunar deception, I want this choice to be his own. Sorry if this has been discussed endlessly on the forum and I'm just wasting space.

            1) Depends on how serious/dark you want this comedy to be. Maybe this DB had a close mortal friend who another DB decided needed to be dealt with in some manner? A family member who never Exalted? The Realm's got a lot of problems that a noble DB (going by the actual meaning of 'noble' here, not the bloodline version) could find abhorrent on a personal level.

                We had a thread about this before. There's a bunch of reasons.

                1. They could be a defector from decadence who decides that the Realm is simply unsalvageably corrupt and that they're better off siding with Anathema to purge the whole thing. An older DB might feel that this generation of Anathema, while dangerous, are still young and could be taught the right way to do things, whereas the Realm is too entrenched in its ways. (See eg. Barristan Selmy.)

                2. A younger DB could feel the same way, and simply come to believe that these particular Anathema have the potential to make things better, or that they're simply better people than the families currently in control of the Realm. (eg. Zuko.)

                3. They could be a total outcast in the Realm for any number of reasons -- or just have family issues or a personal grudge -- and side with Anathema for revenge. (eg. Tyrion Lannister, or Zuko again)

                4. They could have been forced out of positions of power in the Realm somehow, and decided that siding with Anathema would be a way to get back into power while, probably, getting revenge on everyone. (eg. Tyrion Lannister again.)

                5. They could side with the Anathema out of genuine bonds with them personally -- love, friendship, and so on. (See eg. Jorah Mormont.)

                (A lot of these depend on the DB having at least some inkling about Creation's true history, or at least having less-than-full confidence in the Immaculate dogma -- but I don't think that's uncommon. Few people know the FULL TRUTH, but I think just about any society has its skeptics, and any skeptic who thinks about it is eventually going to notice how self-serving Immaculate dogma on Anathema and the Dragon-Blooded is, which is at least a reason to approach it carefully. Even then, someone could side with Anathema for revenge, personal power, or even love while believing them to be soul-eating demons.)

                    I point to Cynis Denovah Avaku's background; detailed in Scroll of Exalts. Not all Realm Dynasts have to come from the main dynastic households, but can come from a comparatively poor patrician cadet households and be less firmly indoctrinated in Dynast exceptionalism.

                    Avaku is an young idealist who adheres to the morality of the Immaculate Philosophy; but Avaku once partook in a Wyld Hunt against an exalted child who was destroying Cynist drug shipments targeting that child's hometown. Avaku became all "Is this how it should be?" angsty over the matter (though quietly). [Re: the cartoon in the Second Edition handbook; pages 116-117.]

                    His story is meant to demonstrate how even a Realm DB can have doubts and, if not be immediately sympathetic towards every Anathema they met, at least be not fanatically hostile, and so perhaps open to persuasion.


                    tl;dr : There doesn't have to be a stark choice between fanatical Dynast DB and doubting Outcaste DB. There are middle grounds to explore.
