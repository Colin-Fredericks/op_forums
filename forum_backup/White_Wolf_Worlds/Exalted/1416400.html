                                      Here's a stab at an example nemissary, inspired by the one from the 2e core book.

                                      Nemissary
                                      Old and malevolent ghosts, twisted by the dark whispers in the far reaches of the Underworld, can become nemissaries. These creatures are rare, valued by the darker powers of the Lands Beneath as spies, assassins, and saboteurs. They have the ability to possess recently-dead corpses, allowing them to impersonate victims, and to interact with the physical realm without needing to materialize. While "riding" such a body, a nemissary is fearless, not worried about the state of the meat it possesses. Outside a corpse, it is no more than a hissing whisper.

                                      A nemissary-riden corpse can be distinguished from a common zombie by the greenish glow of malign intelligence in its eyes, and palpable sense of purpose. Those who come to Creation openly can also be known by their dress: warriors wear black-iron armor with silver sigils of honor and authority inlaid, while those serving as scribes or ambassadors have grey silk robes with silver bracers and dull garnet trim. Most travelling in Creation hide their rotting features behind expressionless silver masks.

                                      Essence: 3; Willpower: 6; Join Battle: 6 dice
                                      Personal Motes: 80
                                      Health Levels: -0x2/-1x3/-2x3/-4/Incap.
                                      Actions: Command: 5 dice; Feats of Strength: 8 dice (may attempt Strength 5 feats; only 4 dice, Strength 3 in ghost form); Impersonate Current Host: 7 dice; Read Motives: 7 dice; Senses: 7 dice; Social Influence: 6 dice; Stealth: 7 dice; Threaten: 8 dice;
                                      Appearance 3 (Hideous, but not when riding a fresh corpse that didn't have Hideous in life), Resolve 3, Guile 2

                                      Combat
                                      Attack (Melee weapon): 10 dice (Damage 12 for light weapon, 14 for medium, 16 for heavy)
                                      Attack (Unarmed): 9 dice (Damage 12; 10 in ghost form)
                                      Attack (Grapple): 10 dice (11 dice to control)
                                      Combat Movement: 5 dice
                                      Evasion 3, Parry 4
                                      Soak/Hardness: 4/0 (when possessing a host or corpse, use the body's statistics)

                                      Offensive Charms
                                      Cunning Soul Thief (1wp; Simple; Instant; Decisive-Only; Essence 3): Brushing its dead flesh gently against a victim's skin, the nemissary steals fragments of his soul. This is a special gambit that must be made from either ambush or a surprise attack. The nemissary rolls 10 dice to hit, then rolls its initiative against a difficulty of the target's Resolve. If successful, it steals 1m from the target, +1m for every net success on the gambit roll, stealing from peripheral motes first, then personal. If the target has neither, the nemissary steals 1wp for every 5m it would otherwise have stolen. In all cases, the stolen motes or willpower are added to the nemissary's own pools. Motes or willpower over its normal limits bleed off in a display of shadowy energy if not used by the nemissary's next turn.

                                      Clutching Corpse Grasp (5m; Supplemental; Instant; Decisive-Only; Essence 2): The nemissary wraps a victim with dead limbs that never slacken or tire, and leaves them restrained while it goes on to the next. This charm supplements a grapple gambit. The nemissary gets double 9s to confirm the gambit, and double 8s on the control roll. If the grapple is successful, the ghost may reflexively abandon its current body, leaving it to clutch the victim while going in search of a new form. The body maintains the grapple – it may only restrain the victim, but restraint only costs one round of control, not two.

                                      Infiltrate the Fallen (5m, 1wp; Reflexive; Instant; Essence 3): Upon felling a foe, a nemissary can abandon its current body and inhabit the new one. After incapacitating an enemy, the nemissary can use this charm to use either Mortal-Shadowing Technique (if the enemy is unconscious) or Nemissary's Ride (if the foe is dead) reflexively.

                                      Miscellaneous Charms
                                      Mortal-Shadowing Technique (15m, 1wp; Simple; Indefinite; Essence 2): Nemissaries sometimes inhabit living creatures, using their senses and occasionally "borrowing" the body when the mind is sleeping or unconscious for their own purposes. Upon activating this charm, the nemissary rolls 10 dice against the target's Resolve. If successful, it slips into the subject's body, and may share its senses. The target may expel the nemissary automatically by spending 2wp, but only if they are conscious of its presence (for this reason, a nemissary usually only uses this on a sleeping or otherwise unconscious victim, since possessing them while they are conscious is obvious). A subject warned of the possibility of a nemissary's possession may confirm it by rolling (Perception + Integrity) against the nemissary's Guile.

                                      Besides borrowing the subject's senses, the nemissary may control the body. Doing so while the victim is conscious immediately alerts them to the foreign presence within, allowing them to expel the ghost, so nemissaries only do this while the subject is sleeping or unconscious. Even then, any sort of strong or violent activity risks awakening the victim. Some nemissaries prepare for nocturnal actions by having their host take strong soporifics or other drugs that will keep them asleep.

                                      Like the possession of Nemissary's Ride, the nemissary uses its hosts' physical Attributes, health levels, and soak in place of its own, if it takes actions. Attacks that damage immaterial beings hurt both the host and the nemissary's health levels, but otherwise, attacks only harm the host.

                                      Nemissary's Ride (20m, 1wp; Simple; Indefinite; Essence 1): As per Exalted, p. 508. If the nemissary is using Mortal-Shadowing Technique and its host is killed, it may activate Nemissary's Ride reflexively, for only 5m, to possess the new corpse. The cost of Mortal-Shadowing Technique and the 5m surcharge remain committed.

                                      Interested in chatting with other Exalted fans online? Join us on the Exalted Discord server!

                                          Re: Demons

                                          I have worked up a version of the Firmin and Perronele. Let me know what you think!

                                          Firmin, The Needlemaker; Demons of the First Circle


                                          Firmin appear nearly human at first glance, except that their backs bristle with sharp spines and quills. Forged by Alveua from mortals with dull dreams and small ambitions, they are simple creatures of bestial intelligence. From beneath their fingernail drips a mucus-like ichor that quickly hardens into shiny black needles. It is from these needles that groups of build their nests. Firmin decorate these nests with the impaled bodies of small animals, eventually progressing more impressive decoration offered by the bodies of humans or other demons. Firmin are social creatures, gathering in groups from as few as a dozen to as many as a hundred and prefer to build their nests communally
                                          Akin to clever animals, firmin have few desires beyond hunting and nest building. Their use of language is limited; while capable of mimicking the words they hear, they only use a limited and simple vocabulary among themselves, expressing concepts such as “food” or “dangers”. A wise summoner must be certain to limit their commands such as “kill this person”, “place a needle here” or “sit”.

                                          Essence: 2; Willpower: 4; Join Battle: 5 dice
                                          Personal Motes: 70
                                          Health Levels: -0/-1x4/-2x4/-4/Incap.
                                          Actions: Climb 5 dice; Craft Ichor 7 dice; Senses 4 dice; Stealth 6 dice
                                          Appearance 2, Resolve 2, Guile 1

                                          COMBAT
                                          Attack (Needle Sword): 7 dice (Damage 12, minimum 2)
                                          Attack (Throwing Needle): 9 dice at short range(Damage 10)
                                          Attack (Grapple): 6 dice (6 dice to control)
                                          Combat Movement: 5 dice
                                          Evasion 3, Parry 4
                                          Soak/Hardness: 4/0

                                          SPECIAL ABILITIES

                                          Needle-Sharp Thrust: Withering attacks made with a needle weapon the firmin has crafted ignore three points of soak.

                                          MERITS

                                          Craft Ichor: The firmin can create needles of various sizes from the black ichor secreted from beneath their nails, which the firmin use to build their nests and create simple weapons such as knives and swords. With proper direction, this material can be extruded into molds to created more sophisticated items such as tools, weapons and armor. Once hardened, items made of firmin ichor are extremely strong and have a higher melting point than iron. Firmin ichor catches the light of the sun, gleaming brightly beneath the rays of Ligier or Sol Invictus. Firmin gain double 8’s on Craft rolls when working with ichor.
                                          Crafting a simple item such as a knife or sword is a basic project that only requires a simple action to complete and does not require a roll. Others tools or items are basic projects that grant 2 silver experience for each such project completed. Building a nest is a basic project that typically requires a day’s worth of work, with large communal nests often requiring a week’s worth of work or more. With direction, a firmin or group of firmin can be directed to complete a major project forging weapons, armor and tools.

                                          Gleaming in the Shadows: The spines of the firmin’s body and the needles crafted from their ichor capture and magnify the light of the sun, whether it be Ligier in Malfeas or the Unconquered Sun in Creation. If the light of the sun is present, Stealth rolls made by the firmin do not double 10’s.

                                          OFFENSE CHARMS

                                          Ichorous Binding (7m; Supplemental; Instant; Essence 1): The firmin may use its rapidly hardening ichor to bind a foe in steel-hard strands of the material. This Charm can supplement a grapple gambit, doubling 8s on the control roll, but the firmin may only perform a restrain/drag action.

                                          Prey-Impaling Barb (8m; Simple; Instant; Decisive-only; Essence 2): The firmin hurls a foot long needle at a target within medium range, as a decisive attack with 9 dice that ignores up to 3 Hardness. An enemy damaged by the needle suffers one lethal damage die per round until she removes the spike, which requires destroying it or a difficulty 3 feat of strength.

                                          DEFENSE CHARMS

                                          Bristling Spines (3m; Reflexive; Until next turn; Essence 1): By bristling the razor sharp spines that cover their backs, the firmin cuts and slashes anyone foolish enough to approach them. Until their next turn, any opponent that hits the firmin with an attack at close range or is grappling with them during this time takes one die of lethal damage (ignoring hardness) and loses a point of Initiative.

                                          Ichor-Hardened Bones (5m; Reflexive; Instant; Dual; Essence 2): The firmin’s bones are composed of the same durable ichor they craft their needles from. They add 3 natural soak against a withering attack or gain Hardness 2 against a decisive attack.

                                          MISCELLANEOUS CHARMS

                                          Hurry Home (10m; Simple; Instant; Essence 1): The demon returns to its summoner’s side; unavailable when unbound.

                                          Materialize (35m, 1wp; Simple; Instant; Essence 1): The firmin rises from a puddle of black ichor.

                                          Measure the Wind (5m; Simple; Instant; Essence 1): The firmin may discern the nature of anyone impaled upon it’s needles.

                                          Storyteller Tactics
                                          Firmin typically only attack when they have a clear advantage, either by ambush or by outnumbering their prey. They are creatures of shadow, and shun the light whenever possible. When left to their own devices they will quickly establish a nest and search out suitable prey to decorate it with. They are most commonly summoned to craft large amounts of weapons and equipment quickly. They occasionally slip into Creation without being summon when a community leaves one of their own impaled beneath the dark of the new moon.


                                          Perronele, The Living Armor; Demons of the First Circle


                                          When each perronele tears its way free from the stomach of Lucien, who gave rise to their race, he leans down and whispers into its ear. From that moment, no creature in Malfeas or Creation is more loyal than the perronele. It would suffer annihilation for Lucien, and it knows no lesser loyalty for those who both summon and bind it. The phrase Lucien whispers is “I have seen you in the dreams of Sacheverell, who knows the things to come.” The reason this should inspire such fanatic loyalty is unknown.
                                          The perronele resemble flesh-colored puddles, tinted from ebony to pale. They move viscously along any surface and have no real orientation: One end is as much the head of the demon as another. They manifest eyes, ears or mouths at need and survive with limited senses in the interim. Those demons or humans under Lucien’s protection — as well as some sorcerers — wear the perronele like an undergarment, pulling the creature on over their body before donning their armor or clothes. It can reshape itself slightly to match the creature it protects — and protect it does, for when it spots an incoming blade or hammer, the perronele makes itself as hard as stone. It protects with equal ease against an assassin’s knife or arrow, if its blurred senses can spot it without eyes.

                                          Essence: 2; Willpower: 7; Join Battle: 5 dice
                                          Personal Motes: 70
                                          Health Levels: -0/-1x4/-2x4/-4/Incap.
                                          Actions: Climbing: 5 dice; Linguistics: 7 dice; Senses 6 dice; Social Influence: 5 dice; Stealth 6 dice
                                          Appearance 1(Hideous), Resolve 3, Guile 2

                                          COMBAT
                                          Attack (Pseudopod): 6 dice (Damage 9B)
                                          Attack (Grapple): 5 dice (5 dice to control)
                                          Combat Movement: 6 dice
                                          Evasion 3, Parry 3
                                          Soak/Hardness: 6/0

                                          MERITS

                                          Creeping Flesh: The natural form of the perronele is a puddle of viscous ooze. While in this form, the perronele gains+2 Evasion, ignores difficult terrain and can fit through any space a liquid could squeeze through.

                                          Linguist: Perronele are well-versed in languages, both the modern languages currently spoken in Creation, and many thought lost or long dead. Only the most obscure or alien dialects escape the perronele’s linguistic acumen.

                                          Symbiotic Skin: The perronele may allow itself to be worn against the skin of another creature, flowing over the host and providing a subtle layer of protection. While worn in this manner, the demon fits tight against the host’s body, granting them+3 Soak and allowing the host to wear other garments or armor. It adopts a similar coloration and skin texture to the host; noticing that the host is wearing a perronele is a difficulty3 Perception+ (Awareness or Lore) roll.
                                          While worn by a host in combat, the perronele rolls its own Join Battle and acts independently. It uses the greater of the host’s or its own Defense against attacks that would target it directly. Removing the perronele from it’s host requires a grapple Gambit and uses up five rounds of control. While the perronele may abandon the host at any time, the demons are known for their zealous loyalty, and would only do so under exceptional circumstances.

                                          OFFENSE CHARMS

                                          Metamorphic Arsenal (3m; Simple; one scene; Essence 1): While worm by a host, the perronele may grow weaponry made of bone and sinew. When this Charm is used, the host gains a mundane melee weapon with the traits of a one of the following; katar, short sword, tiger claws or whip. Increase the difficulty of any disarm gambits against the host using the weapon by 2.

                                          DEFENSE CHARMS

                                          Scabrous Armor (5m (+2m); Simple; one scene; Essence 2): The perronele assumes a form that is hard as stone and prepared for violence. It covers clothing and lighter forms of armor with a carapace of scar tissue, callouses, and scales. This Charm increases the soak bonus gained from wearing the perronele to 6. This bonus is cumulative and compatible with soak bonuses gained from Charms and light armor. This Charm may not be activated if the host is wearing medium or heavy armor. Spending an additional two mote allows the perronele to activate Metamorphic Arsenal at the same time without paying its activation cost.

                                          Vigilant Second Skin (2m, 1i; Reflexive; Perilous; Instant; Essence 1): The perronele may shift its mass and alter its density to better deflect incoming blows, gaining a +1 bonus to Parry. If used as part of a defend other action (Exalted, p. 196) the bonus to Parry increases to +2 instead.

                                          SOCIAL CHARMS

                                          Whispers of Lucien (5m; Supplemental; Psyche; Instant; Essence 2): Those that wear a perronele must be wary, for the demon gains the power to whisper secrets into his mind. Social Influence rolls made by perronele against the host gain double 9’s and the host’s Resolve and Guile suffer a -1 penalty.

                                          MISCELLANEOUS CHARMS

                                          Hurry Home (10m; Simple; Instant; Essence 1): The demon returns to its summoner’s side; unavailable when unbound.

                                          Many-Angled Observation (6m; Supplemental; Until next turn; Essence 2): The perronele may shape whatever sensory organs it needs from it’s formless flesh. Until it’s next turn, the demon gains double 9’s on perception rolls involving a specific sense (such as sight, hearing, smell, touch or taste). A different sense may be chosen each time this charm is activated. If the perronele is being worn, the demon may grant the benefit of this charm to it’s host instead, causing new sensory organs to sprout along the hosts body and limbs.

                                          Materialize (35m, 1wp; Simple; Instant; Essence 1): The perronele bubbles up from cracks in ground as it takes physical form.

                                          Measure the Wind (5m; Simple; Instant; Essence 1): The perronele can read anyone that has worn it.

                                          Slithering Regalia (3m; Simple; Indefinite; Essence 1): When worn, the perronele may take the form of a mundane set of clothing. Once changed, the perronele may stay in that form as long as it likes, but it’s appearance to a different set of clothing requires a new activation of the charm. The perronele is only capable of imitating clothing made from fur, leather, silk, or wool; or jewelry made from ivory or bone. Such garments and adornments appear normal and an observer can only tell that something is amiss with a difficulty 4 Perception+ (Awareness or Lore) roll. Particularly impressive sets of clothing (anything that would require more than two dots of Resources) requires the perronele to roll 6 dice(or its host to make a Crafts roll) with a difficulty equal to the outfits resource cost.

                                          Storyteller Tactics
                                          The perronele is most commonly called into Creation to serve its summoner as a discrete protector. In combat they will dedicate their actions to protecting their wearer or other allies. They are also known for being capable linguists, and are summoned to serve as translators. All summoners would be wise to remember that a perronele is utterly devoted to will of the demon Lucien, and even those bound by the summoner’s pact seek to further his goals.
