            The limit is more you can't supplement attacks from both Abilities. If you use SPSitV to attack, you need to use its MA Charms. And if you make an attack using Melee, you can only use Melee Charms. The Form doesn't go anywhere, but normally they can only benefit their own Ability unless they say otherwise, such as how Snake helps in Evasion.

            Single Point doesn't say the attacks of the sword have to be Martial Arts ones, but I would lean that since they are attacks generated by a Martial Arts Charm, they are themselves Martial Arts attacks. But since it doesn't say either way there, that's left to interpretation. But the character can still use her own attacks wiht Melee or MA however she pleases, she just has to choose which for purposes of Charms each time.

                                                        Originally posted by prototype00 View Post

                                                        Non cross compatability of abilities only apply to "combat" abilities. Specifically Melee, Brawl, Archery, Thrown and Martial Arts as laid out by the Writers. All other charms from other abilities can be combined freely (such as Martial Arts and Dodge).
                                                        Ex3 Core pg426: - "Martial Arts cannot be combined with other combat Abilities, they can be freely combined with one another,..."'

                                                        emphasis mine

                                                        We know the definition of combat abilities here has as you have listed. However this doesn't say martial arts can be freely combined with any abilities. just freely combined with one another. As in martial art + martial art. The exception to the base rules here is not that martial arts cannot combine with brawl etc, by the base rules they already cannot do that unless a martial art charm was written in a way to say it could. The exception is that martial arts can combine with martial arts, constrained by style weapons and armour.

                                                        As the charm we are talking about here is a reflexive charm we refer to the rules on reflexive charms.

                                                        pg253: "Reflexive Charms which enhance static values may generally only enhance static values deriving from their governing Ability, unless otherwise indicated. For example, Dipping Swallow Defense will only remove penalties from a Melee-based Parry attempt. "
