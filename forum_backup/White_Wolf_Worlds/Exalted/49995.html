    Awesome Lunar Warforms/Hybrid Body Rearrangements

    01-15-2014, 03:46 PM
    Since I brought it up in the topic on Lunar Spirit Shapes, I thought it'd be fun to have a place for people to talk about their favorite warforms and/or uses of Hybrid Body Rearrangement. Cool, creepy, creative - I want to hear about it, because shapeshifting is an awesome power and it can be used in a lot of really fun ways.

    My personal favorite is the warform of a Lunar I designed a while back. When DBT was activated, he went from angry wolf-man to giant angry wolf-man made of molten moonsilver with blazing red eyes. This led to being utterly terrifying in a fight, since you couldn't escape him, couldn't defend yourself against him (ever try parrying liquid metal?), and his movements borrowed from Arakune and Pyron.

    Your turn, forums!

                                      Something like this should be possible with that.
                                      HBR can't alter your entire form, so you can't use it to just get bigger. That's why Mountainous Spirit Exercise, Emperor-Ox Expansion and God-Beast Transcendence are all distinct Knacks.

                                      That said, a starting Mokole in Werewolf, assuming Gnosis 10, can get close to that bulk, if not achieve it (five purchases of Tall, three of Behemoth, you'll have to talk to your ST about adjusting the mass increase per purchase so it triples instead of doubles with each purchase). So, that could form the core of an argument to ease up on the prerequisites to the size-increasing shapeshifting powers available to the Lunars.
