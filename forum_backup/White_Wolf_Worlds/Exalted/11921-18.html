                            06-11-2014, 09:37 AM
                            During the Age of Dreams, a number of Celestials founded a city-state in the West, the heart of a maritime empire. They built great ships and engines, and extended the scope of Creation further out into the Wyld.

                            Alas, it was not to last, and when the Aftershock War began, one of the early campaigns saw the destruction of the city and its lords beneath the overwhelming hordes of the Last Primordial. The Celestials were not idle, though, and loaded the bulk of the populace on board all the transports they could recall, and sent them fleeing into the West before the city was fully besieged. One of them, a Twilight, laid her will upon the Wyld and Shaped a great fog to hide their passage, which would later foil all attempts to return.

                            The tailmost squadron, carrying the refugees from the Thirteenth Precinct, became separated in a storm in the Bordermarches, and were not seen again.

                            The other ships continued, however, until after long voyaging they found a green and pleasant island chain, with a full dozen demesnes just waiting for intrepid Terrestrials to found new settlements. The scientists among the survivors used their knowledge of Essence to cap the demesnes, and to mount the Reality Engines from the largest wyldfaring transports within the manses they built.

                            Over the next centuries (time does not seem to match up with that in Creation, what with the Cascading Years event and the nonlinear speed-of-narrative that happens in much of the Wyld) the survivors' culture grew, as their Terrestrial champions used the base of High First Age infrastructure hastily loaded onto the transports to found a new civilization.

                            Eventually, a traders’ expedition made contact with a society of pelagothropes (not unusual in itself) and found them engaged in an ongoing war. A Terrestrial warrior went with a pelagothrope column, to find that the enemy were Dragon Kings, who surprisingly were most upset to see a Dragon-Blooded anima, shrieking about treason and usurpation and attacking without thought of their own survival.

                            The war has lasted a thousand years, with the Dragon Kings having seemingly been wiped out, and the conflict carried on mostly by their Brass Legionnaire servants, presumably built in automated factory-cathedrals by the automata themselves. Also a product of the High First Age, the Legionnaires mostly use three-crew skimmers (for Archery, Sail, and War/Awareness specialists respectively - but otherwise quite like the flying car in CoCD:Yu Shan with a pair of linked implosion bows) to do battle with the settlers’ own Air-Aspect-piloted motonic fightercraft.

                            Recently, not long after the Fourth Fleet centred on the battleship Hippopteryx was wiped out, a settler merchant brought word to the Council that the enemy finally sought an armistice. A temporary cease-fire has held, and now the President has joined the main fleet to go to sign a formal peace treaty. Hopefully this will usher in a new age of peace for the settlers, perhaps one in which the President's academy classmate, the near-retired commander of another battleship, could fulfil his dream of searching out the descendants of the lost Thirteenth.

                                        A young man (who has a subtle Night caste Essence inside of him) is forced to work for a criminal lord in his town until he picks the pocket of an Abyssal swordsman (with a Soulsteel arm) visiting town. This leads to a sequence of events where the young criminal's sister, his only family, is killed, before the Abyssal murders the criminal lord. The burgeoning Night caste follows the Abyssal on his quest to acquire 12 magical items that can be made into a potent elixir that can make the partaker invulnerable, or return life to the dead. Joining them is a Chosen of Saturn gunman and a heroic mortal reporter.

                                        -----

                                        An Exalt - none are sure if he is a Wood-Blood or a Gaian Exalt, though he also bears the blessing of Saturn - currently lives in a moderate-sized southern town. He holds a magical blade designed to cut any armor which can also bind others to tell the truth, and has held it against one of his home Gods for ages. His foe, meanwhile, has sought aid from the Yozi and the Fair Folk to hunt this blade, and is preparing to enter the field himself. Together with his Abyssal and Lunar associates, who act as his legal advisors, and a strange ghost of a Sidereal using her strange magics to inhabit the body of a mortal barmaid, the Exalt must meet his ancient foe in battle once and for all.

                                                    Once upon a time, there was a Yozi who slept. While he was awake, he had been foreseeing a story...

                                                    There is a town, off on the frayed edges of Creation, where Fate and the Wyld mingle and warp each other into a peculiar sort of amalgam. In this town, Beastfolk openly walk the streets, and strange automata walk the streets, trading in gems of crystallized feelings. Chief among these wonders is a youth of surpassing beauty, who rarely speaks a word and whose face never betrays emotion. Only a handful of people know that this youth is really a mighty raksha prince who once did battle with Erembour herself and came out victorious, though at a terrible cost. The price of that victory was the shattering of the Prince's Graces, leaving him in the sorry state he is in today. This is the story of the Prince and the Exalts who love him.

                                                    The first Exalt is a Sidereal, a Chosen of Battles. The odd pseudo-Fate that governs the town hid his destiny from Heaven, so he remains ignorant of his full potential. His love is a possessive love, tainted by fear, for he has foreseen a grave struggle in the Prince's life, as well as his own death in that struggle, and he now jealously guards the Prince against all who would, through their ignorance, bring these fates to pass.

                                                    The second is a Casteless Lunar who Exalted when she learned to survive in the Demon Realm as a child. Her experiences there have left her broken, and Erembour's shadows lie heavy on her heart. There is no love without pain in Malfeas, but the Prince offers her an escape clause. In him she finds a placid, safe love, a love that asks for nothing and carries no surprises. In him, she is able to suppress her memories of Malfeas and forget the terrible oaths she swore at Erembour's feet. And she will stop at nothing to ensure her Prince remains as he is.

                                                    The third is not truly an Exalt at all, but rather an odd sort of behemoth. Initially, she was just a bird whose lineage (if birds had ever cared about lineage) could be traced back to Beastmen. The blood of humanity is quite thin in her, but the strange metaphysics of the town amplified it to the point where she gained a human degree of sapience. She fell in love with the Prince as she watched him dance, unnoticed by all, and her feelings were so great, and so unusual, that the Wyld energies transformed her into a human girl. Her love is a naive sort of love, as could be expected from a creature born outside of humanity. She feels compelled to restore the Prince to what he once was, and she cares little for the status quo. She is the errant gust of wind that will blow this carefully-balanced house of cards to the ground and send the story spiraling into certain tragedy...

                                                    "This is wonderful!" said the Yozi who was supposed to be asleep!

She quickly finds herself opposed by the Bronze Tiger's friend and his lover. In time, she discovers the friend to be a Chosen of Battles, who knows his fate is to be destroyed painfully and without purpose if and when the seal on the Shadow of All Things is released. The lover, on the other hand, fears the changes in the Lawgiver, and that he may fall for the graceful Changing Moon who has recently appeared. As the Ebon Dragon begins to touch the world again, she Exalts as a Fiend, but finds her love for the Solar bringing her into conflict with her patron, while her powers hurt the man she loves in terrible ways and nearly destroys him.
