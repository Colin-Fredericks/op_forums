                            Originally posted by Elfive View Post
                            Do we even still need endowment-created god blooded any more?
                            No, we don't. Endowment took a permanent willpower out of the God, which was a permanent diminishment - some people assumed they would just recoup that with their passive XP gain, but that was never even the goal of 2e (which, you know, specifically states that NPCs do not use XP). It is a major, major sacrifice in their part, and meant to be as ridiculously rare... as the Exigence. It was not treated as THAT rare, in-play, because the benefits were minor enough that people wanted many of them. But that's the thing - it was always the spirit losing a part of himself to empower someone, who then... got pretty much no powers, just the potential to develop some, with difficulty. An Exalted fits MUCH better with how they were actually used in-play: Spirit spends stuff, gains a powerful champion.

                            As Alucard says, Divine Favor is something we will still have, but in potency and style, it's very different from 'I made you an ess-using powerhouse flavored by me.'
