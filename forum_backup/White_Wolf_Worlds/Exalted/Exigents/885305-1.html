                                                            I've always kind of liked Lytek as a god. Even if it is just that he does the day to day drudge work that the Incarne can't be bothered to worry about with the exaltations. Plus, without his cabinet the whole Usurption and sealing the Solar Exaltations away in the Jade Prison kind of falls apart.

                                                            He works as a good intermediary for high powered characters that can get into Yu-Shan, but can't get into the Jade Pleasure Dome to speak with the Unconquered Sun directly. Though I've also had him manipulate players for his own ends. But I've also done that with Little Beam, if anyone here gets that reference.

                                                            Though it would be interesting to see an Exigent of Lytek. The Chosen of Exaltations. That could be interesting.

                                                            Lytek knows about the Great Curse, but only as it pertains to Solars. The problem is that he hasn't been able to find a way to fix it and he's afraid to tell the Unconquered Sun because he's afraid it will make him look incompetent at his job. It is also pointed out in the 1st edition Cult of the Illuminated book that he is extremely interested in a rogue Abyssal in their ranks as he thinks that the fact that the curse has been removed from him it might lead him to a cure for all Solars.

                                                            So, I could see Lytek creating an Exigent to put in a party of Solars in an effort to find a way to either cure the Great Curse or at least study it up close and in action in active Solars. Though this does bring to mind another question that I think I'll start a new thread on.
