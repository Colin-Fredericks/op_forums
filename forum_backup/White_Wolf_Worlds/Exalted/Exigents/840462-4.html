    So, since the question of a canonical answer to the Exigents of the Incarna has been repeated ad nauseam... I'll offer my opinion on it instead. Yes. The Exigence of Mars is a Chosen of Mars. The Exigence of Luna is a Chosen of Luna. There is no identifiable difference unless there is a way for a character to detect and distinguish between Exaltation and Exigence.

    As for your other Premise, I love the idea of an Exigence of a concert of Gods. More interesting* to me would be the Exigent of the Incarna if you were to combine the two premises together.
    *Interesting in that I would enjoy the novelty and narrative of such a character, much in the same way I enjoy the Scarlet Empress.

    But then again, I'm wait for the Exigent Book so that I can create my Exigent of Lytek.

                                Originally posted by Ghosthead View Post
                                Conversely, is it actually that good if the Incarna made no meaningful choices in the nature of what their Chosen were actually like?

                                While people can go a bit extreme with the idea of "The Incarna designed their Chosen to do *exactly* this particular role", I wouldn't want to lose the idea that they each intentionally decided to have specifically a certain kind of Chosen.

                                Rather than, y'know, they chose to Exalt people and then what fell out of that, what kind of Exalted and the nature of those Exalted, was pretty much something they had no role in deciding or intending.

                                I assume people who play the game want to be the Chosen of Luna, etc. because Luna wanted those type of Chosen, not just, well, given here nature that's all she was ever going to get.
                                You don't have to go to either extreme of them making no choices in what their Chosen would be like, every absolute little thing and ever potential within each of the Chosen being a choice. (And if they did make some choices, possibly we can imagine they could've made other ones.)
                                It's an interesting thought. The UCS and Luna both kind of already did break up their purviews by the Castes. Though the Maidens have pretty focused purviews by design. From a lore standpoint, wouldn't any Exigent created by the Incarnae be weaker then the current Chosen!? The original Exalts were created to fight the war against the Primordials. The Incarnae wouldn't have held back in creating their ultimate weapons when you consider enemy they were facing. The original Exalts are very likely the upper limit of each Incarnae's power to create.

                                With all that in mind, why would any of the Incarnae create an Exigent that is very likely weaker with a smaller purview focus, then their current Chosen? This is one canon ruling that will likely stick at my table, because it simply doesn't make sense for the Incarnae to need an Exigent when they already have their own Exalts to call on... not to mention that being an Incarnae means they could order any number of gods and spirits about as well!
