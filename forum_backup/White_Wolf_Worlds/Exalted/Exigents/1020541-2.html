                        Originally posted by Raemus View Post
                        The title pretty much says it but if they do/will/should what would they be?
                        Will the Exigents have castes? If a god makes multiple Exigents instead of just one, I suppose they could be divided into castes/aspects/classes etc. So, sure, why not?

                        Originally posted by Raemus View Post
                        Additionally what powers do we think that all extigents should innately have?
                        I've wondered the same thing. I've heard that their power level will range from dragonblooded to Solar, so I have no idea. Right now any exigence created Exalt the players come across in my game have the basic traits all Exalted share; fast healing, immunity to sickness, soaking lethal damage, etc.

                        Originally posted by Raemus View Post
                        And lastly what is the title for exigents? (Like Lawgiver or Stewards)
                        My impression is that since all the exigents are unique and as different as Abyssals to dragonblooded, there isn't one catch all title for them. In fact, IIRC, the term "Exigent" isn't one that exists in Creation. The Exalted that are created by way of the exigence are generally referred to as "Exalted." That being the case, I suppose "Exigent" could be a catch all title if you wanted it to be?

                        Originally posted by Raemus View Post
                        I've been using Herald as their title, does that seem appropriate or have other people come up with other titles for them?
                        If it works for you go for it. For me, the title "Herald" conjures an idea as to what the Exalt's duties/obligations/reasons for being are. In other words, for me, a title such as "Herald" would be too confining a title for all the exigence created Exalted. I would use "Herald" as a type of Exalt, like Solar or Lunar. Or perhaps more specific such as the name of an aspect or caste of exigent Exalt when there are multiple Exalts of one god. Hope that makes sense.

                                I have several Exigents in a play-by-post that I'm running (the current arc is taking place in Great Forks) and I've pretty much settled on a model for typifying them: there are Solar Exalted, Lunar Exalted, etc. Swap the word Exalted with the word Exigent, and make the first word something to do with their empowering deity.

                                For instance, Shoreline's Harlequin, Chosen of Miseng, God of the Rolling River, is a River Exigent. Claret O'Saar, Chosen of Nepele, Goddess of Wayward Breezes, is a Gust Exigent.

                                    Speaking of Forbidden Gods, FrivYeti came up with an idea that I'm planning on stealing shamelessly when the Exigents book comes out, but it might be useful for you too, joshopotamus.

                                    One of the Forbidden Gods (and Han Tha would actually be a pretty good choice for this) has managed to get several portions of the Exigence and convinced a number of gods to create Exalted together with them. Each god spends a fair amount of power to flavor and charge the various Exigencies, and Han Tha has killed himself providing the final energies necessary to activate all of them. So, now you have a bunch of Exigents which all look different and have different powers, but they all have this deep need to be part of something bigger, almost a hunger for it. But that's it. There isn't any other real sign of Han Tha's influence.

                                    Until one of them murders another of this specific group of exigents and absorbs their powers. After that, you develop an increasingly strong need to absorb all the rest, as well as blackouts, voices in your head, and other scary urges. If some one managed to become the last of these Exigents, holding all the power, Han Tha awakens and takes over your body and soul, becoming an Exalted Spirit and attaining true excellence..

                                            I have two Exigents who share the same origination in my game (I'm working on writing up all the mechanics to make these playable as PCs too but that will take a while longer until it's done). Instead of going with the caste route, the story is that the god split his power in two. One Exigent got his "dark" side and the other got his "light" side. They are angel-themed too and have the power to "give the gift of wings". Because the two sisters are bonded to each other both in blood and in exigence, they are drawn to one another in a love-hate relationship. The "caste limitations" is that the Charm set is divided into four categories based around the god's themes, where one of the categories is locked away from one aspect of the exigence. If one of them would give her wings to the other, that other would be complete and gain access to the full exigence. That's also how they pass the exigence along (giving their wings away and effectively become mortal). The exigence can be as two split parts, as one broken part with another "lost" part (i.e. one part of the exigence can be gone forever, leaving the other broken), or as one complete whole---the latter is the strongest version when it comes to excellencies, mote pools and Charm access. So not really castes, but kind of a workaround to having two characters with similar powers.
