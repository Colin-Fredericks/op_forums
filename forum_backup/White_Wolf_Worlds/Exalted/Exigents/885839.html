    Are Exigents affected by the Great Curse

    05-08-2016, 09:07 PM
    If a god petitions the Unconquered Sun to create a new Exigent are they automatically affected by the Great Curse?

    It has been pointed out in the books that if you could turn an Abyssal back into a Solar they would be free of the Great Curse because the Neverborn lack the power to inflict the Curse again. So if a new Exalation is created is it clean or does the Curse have something built into to snare new Exigents?

              So the Exigence was created before the Primordial War? I really expected it to have been created during the First Age.


              My understanding is that the Exigence is a divine flame that the Sun has, or keeps, or controls. The entire flame itself could be Cursed. Or maybe it's not, and there is no Curse on a completely new Exigent who was just created recently (instead of reincarnating an exaltation or being born into a bloodline or something else that dates back to the War).

              My headcanon is that the Curse was aimed mainly at the Incarnae, and that it either 'bounced' off their Perfect Defenses or dripped down by contamination into the Exalted and little gods. If that were the case, the Exigence would get contaminated even if Sol created it after the War, because he's Cursed.


              The very idea of Exaltation can't be Cursed, or at least was not in 2E, since the Alchemicals and redeemed former-Abyssals aren't Cursed. Presumably, this time around, Liminals won't be either.

                      I don't see it as having a big effect on the themes or playability of Exigents, since on the one hand you can always gin up a type-specific mechanic that gives a curse-like effect if they don't have it and you want it, and on the other hand you can always make it a latent non-mechanical guideline like with Sidereals, if they have it and you don't want it.
