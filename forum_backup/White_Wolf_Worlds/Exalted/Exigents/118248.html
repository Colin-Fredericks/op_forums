    ​Pointless but fun Exigent Mechanics Speculation!

    04-24-2014, 12:20 AM
    So I am 100% for the Exigents. The whole idea seems ridiculously cool and I am excited to see how it will work out. However I have no idea how it will work out.

    One whole hard back to help you make your own type of unique exalt. I assume there has to be some underlying mechanic introduced in the book for people like me. That is people with lots of ideas but suck at balancing and executing those ideas; I am terrible at game design. Additionally we know it does not use point buy so that is interesting.
    So let’s pointlessly speculate on how Exigents work Mechanically.

    I guess I’ll get it started:

    I am going to guess that maybe all Exigents share the same excellency charms which might not be attribute or ability based but theme based like Infernals. The themes being those of the god that the exigency drives from.

    I am also going to guess that Exigents can be either ability or attribute based. Unless the designers really pull something out of their hat and create a new paradigm just for Exigents which might actually be an interesting idea. Most Exigents probably won’t have castes so instead of having favored skills or attributes they will have some other factor.

    Perhaps how it will work is there will be basic charm ‘skeletons’ with additional options that add in ‘meat’ both of which can be flavored appropriately with text. Then this base Skeleton can be added onto with custom charms.

    What are all of your ideas on how this might work?

                  Originally posted by John Mørke
                  [Two Exigents] would be two completely different Exalts, like joining a Solar and a Lunar.

                  They range as far afield as you can imagine.

                  ...

                  We have put a lot of thought into how to pull this off, and so a part of the Exigents hardback will be dedicated to building your own.

                  ...

                  Exigents are unique Exalts. The difference between the classics and the Exigents are that Exigents are based on a variety of gods and can produce really strange results, some of which are difficult to trace back to any kind of spirit. Their powers/natures/builds are as varied and crazy as you can imagine.

                  ...

                  I formulated the Exigents to cover any Exalt you could come up with, with any kind of crazy Charm or mechanical schema imaginable.

                  ...

                  Questions like "I wonder if the Exigents will have an apotheosis path like the Devil-Tigers?" and "How good will the Exigents be at Evocations?" tell me that you are thinking of them as one kind of Exalt. They're not.

                  Every Exigent is unique and works differently. You can have an Exigent who has an apotheosis path and you can have an Exigent who is all about Evocations.

                                  Anyone wanna speculate on possible unprecedented Charmsets? I'll start:

                                  - A charmset with no requisites at all. No charm trees, just a gigantic bag of tricks that don't really advance into more powerful tricks and further power comes from various synergies between different tricks

                                  - A Charmset in which you just pay experience to determine how many Charms you know, but which Charms you have at any given time are determined at random each day from a pool of all possible charms you could have from your Charmset (possibly per Ability or Attribute or whatever, so that you could at least trust to always have, say, 5 melee Charms or something)

                                  - A Charmset composed of minor effects, but all of them permanent. Only a few even have some possible uses that cost any Essence.

                                  - A Charmset that starts out with really weak charms, but each new charm you buy on the same tree unlocks a new effect or increases the effect of all other charms on that tree

                                  - A consumable Charmset. Charms can be "consumed" or "expended" to generate a much greater effect than usual, but then that Charm becomes unvailable for a long time. For a more extreme version, ultra-powerful Charms that can only be used once (generally one scene) then you lose that Charm. You get your XP refunded and with training time can learn another Charm, but you can't relearn a charm you already used.

                                  -A living/sentient/aware/whatever Charmset that can activate itself and has its own ideas and opinions. You better make sure it has good intimacies toward you!

                                      Ooh, those are awesome, danelsan! ^__^ Very creative! Especially the last two, kind of stretching the idea of what Charms can be.

                                      Here are three you inspired in me, much as the first plays off your last one quite a bit.

                                      - A Charmset in which every Charm (or every root Charm) is a living creature/person. Each follow-up Charm can advance these other entities' powers and capabilities. They may or may not also provide some passive bonus to the Exalt (e.g. Not-Charizard learned a passive flame aura, so his Trainer Exalt is now super-resistant to fire!). It may be that only some number of Charm familiars may be "active" at once to prevent numerical abuse. And/Or maybe some other factors to consider in the case of Charm familiars getting killed. Depending on particulars, can represent an Exalt who's all about familiars, Pokemon/Digimon, Fate/StayNight, a Devil-Tiger's Soul hierarchy, and plenty else.

                                      - A Charmset in which every Charm is, or allows one to easily make, an object. Instead of being about becoming a great user of weapons, Melee would be all about creating melee weapons with interesting and distinct qualities. Higher levels might even produce (limited) artifact gear, possibly even with Evocation-like powers of their own. While dangerous enough if the Exalt uses this stuff personally (and perhaps with special bonuses if the Exalt does), the true power is in giving this stuff to other people. Be it equipping an army or another Exalt with special equipment or offering tomes of difficult knowledge conveyed very clearly to educate the populace or help another Exalt master something difficult, this Exigent is a clear force multiplier.

                                      - The Exigent develops an amazing tie to the land of his or her patron, and the Charmset reflects this. Charms define less personal capacities of the Exalt and more the qualities and ways of manipulating the locale, exerting influence over inhabitants (primarily through geographical features and local wildlife), and maybe even drawing off the land for personal power. Some Charms might create geographic features, or help the rivers grow pristine and filled with fish. A mountain might stand so majestic that those who behold it begin developing an Intimacy toward the land, while other Charms might make the animals desire to protect/serve the land and make them unusually powerful when doing so. Charms in turn for allowing use of Intimacies toward the land as though they were Intimacies toward oneself would be natural outgrowths, as would commanding beasts or geographical features to act or attack as one wishes.

                                      At the extreme end, Charms for great personal power (unusual for this set) might not cost motes (or at least not just motes), but would allow one to draw on the power of parts of the land at the cost of diminishing them (e.g. "I am swift as the coursing river!" for a scene might also mean the mighty river becomes less mighty; time and effort can mend it, but an especially tough situation can leave the land suffering at least as much as the Exalt). The Exaltation may ensure its bearer reincarnates as a person (or even animal?) born on the land, though this blessing may be thwarted if the land is sufficiently brought to ruin. There may be interesting possibilities if the land becomes, encompasses, or comes to encompass -- no reason it can't grow! -- a Shadowland, Wyld Zone, or other oddity.

                                      EDIT: One who wanted to play Sim City as an Alchemical Petropolis or Metropolis, or an Infernal/other entity with some sort of world body, could also find value in this idea.
