    Who can grant Exigent Exaltations? Who can be granted one?

    12-13-2013, 11:30 AM
    We know with a fairly high degree of certainty, that gods can grant an Exigent Exaltation to a mortal, assuming they've been granted the authority / ability by the Sun.

    Could an Elemental? In particular, I'm looking at some of the Heavenly Censors, whom are technically Elementals. (Amusing follow on question; could the Kukla?)

    We know that there are black market Exaltations, Exigence that has escaped into the world of spirits.
    Could a demon which acquired such make use of it?
    A ghost? A Deathlord?

    Can an Exigent Exaltation be granted to anyone other than a living, mortal human? I can easily imagine a pecular ghost Exalted- not the ghost of an Exalt, but a ghost that is Exalted. What about unelevated animals- Could the Lord of Tyrant Lizards grant their blessing to a single, spectacular Tyrant Lizard, granting it intelligence, long life, nigh invulernability and candy apple red skin?

              Who can be granted one: That's pretty easy, it's only creatures with human souls. The same mechanisms work for Exigence soul-pairing as it does for the other Celestial Exaltations. If you can't exalt as a Solar (or Lunar,) you can't Exalt as an Exigent. So no Tyrant Lizard Exalts, sorry.

              Who can grant is much more open. It just seems that you need a certain level of power (no Lesser Gods), access to the Exigence from one way or another, and not be specifically barred from it by the Unconquered Sun, like demons. Other than that, it looks pretty unclear, probably on purpose so it can vary from ST to ST.
