        My impression was that there were only about 100-200 exigencies and not all are in an Exalt at any one time.

        Originally posted by semicasual View Post
        My biggest concern is that I'm going to run into some player who won't be interested in any of the default splats and isn't mechanically savvy enough to build their own exigent, thereby forcing me to do all the work of character creation beyond the concept phase.
        I think it's too early to be sure Exigent charms will be so diffiult to make. One of their primary uses is to be NPCs. So their "charm creation matrix" will probably be designed to let STs create charm-trees as easily as possible. Me, I'm hoping it also can be used for good guidelines for making balanced Devil-Tigers as well.

        Devil-Tigers are pretty different from Exigents, though. They were so popular partly because the Infernals were given a crummy deal as a splat with the stuff about Yozis being able to spy on them and inflict Torment on a whim all the way from Hell. As long as the Celestial and Terrestrial Exalted in 3E aren't mechanically hamstrung, I don't predict everybody ditching them for Exigents. I'd be more concerned about everyone ditching Abyssals for Liminals, unless Resonance is successfully fixed to be less unfun.

        I also think that a good ST will require any Exigent (or Devil-Tiger) to have a theme and limits. The Exalted with the broadest breadth of what they can do are the Solars, whose theme includes "excellence at using Abilities and Sorcery." Nearly anyone else is likely to be more restricted. Remember that although Exigents as a whole can be nearly whatever, any one particular Exigent will be limited to the theme of their god, who won't be an Incarna with a huge purview like Sol Invictus has. I also think Mechalich has a good point about what kind of god would make an Exigent in the first place: the wider their purview, and the greater their power, the less likely they are to be existentially threatened enough to need an Exigent.

                                                            Originally posted by nalak42 View Post
                                                            Exigents are, as has been pointed out, theme bound and created for a specific purpose.                                                             ...
                                                            Even then you need to justify why would this exigent be involved with the rest of the players? I mean I don't think exigents are getting made for nebulous threats like the Deathlords, so what is the cause your character was empowered for that this being believed in strongly enough to potentially kill it if not rip off one of its limbs?
                                                            This may sometimes present an obstacle, but perhaps not as large a one as that makes it sound. They're still Exalted: one of the principles of that is the power they've been given can't be taken back. If an Exigent isn't interested in fulfilling the reason for being Chosen, or even just wants to do something else for a while, there's only so much the god can do about it -- particularly if that god already gave its life. While this does mean that most gods are going to select their Exalt very, very carefully, there are still going to be some in desperate straits who did the best they could at the time. Never mind the plentiful stories where a powerful entity makes a very careless or foolish choice in empowering someone and proceeds to pay for that in spades.

                                                            Even then, there's the nature of the purpose: some are more transient than others. Look at Janest and Ten Sheaves. He Chose her because of an imminent Fair Folk attack. Should she rout them all, the story of what she does next -- and of what response the other disparate forces of the world have to this new Exalt -- becomes pretty wide-open. It's been mentioned that Sidereals in the Bureau of Destiny have working with Exigence- and Exigent-related matters under their portfolio in the new edition. That'd be straightforward enough of an 'in' by itself.

                                                            I just don't think getting an Exigent to join up with a group of other Exalts is all that hard. I mean, to put it in perspective, remember that the "assumed default" group is a perfect Solar Circle. That assumes 5 out of ~150 reborn Solars all happened to encounter one another and decide to work closely together. Given that as the baseline difficulty, incorporating an Exigent into a group doesn't feel like much of a challenge to me.
