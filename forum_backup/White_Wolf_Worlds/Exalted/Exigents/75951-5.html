                        Generally, I'm inclined to think that for high essence gods in general, creating Exigents is not necessarily high on their list of emergency options. Since creating a legitimate exigent is supposed to be in alignment with your actual duties somehow in many cases its probably easier for a powerful god to engage the problem personally (or through a powerful subordinate) rather than sacrifice a portion of their being to create a servant to do it. Tien Yu, for example, has any number of dragon-blooded she can ask to do just about anything she considers important.

                        Utilizing the exigence just seems like a desperate (or in the case of black-market exaltations super high risk gamble) type of move. To again use Tien Yu as an example, there is nothing she lacks the power to beat up that an exigent would be able to stop without decades of development.

                                            MA'II, THE LAUGHING TUMBLEWEED VAGABOND-CHIEF VAGRANT, KEEPER OF COYOTES, AND TENDER OF TUMBLEWEEDS
                                            Ma'ii is a strange figure. The Laughing Tumbleweed Vagabond cares little for the politicking of his fellows up in Yu-Shan. Instead, the god of wanders, coyotes, and tumbleweeds travel around the South(primarily the South-East), watching over his charges first hand. The Chief Vagrant is a charismatic, carefree individual, whose natural charm often makes up for the fact he prefers to appear as a mangy, starving looking Southern man with the head of a coyote, though he will take a more mundane shape if the need arrives. In the mind of the Tender of Tumbleweeds, nothing is greater than freedom. Society builds chains of expectations and obligations that crush the spirit and stifles the soul; only by limiting one's attachments and traveling the world may one know their true self. He is quite friendly, caring nothing for status or rank, though he is crass and prone to cruel pranks to those he finds obnoxious. Often in areas he travels, the locals treat all guests with kindness and courtesy, for he is quick to reward good treatment with blessings, and bad treatments with horrific curses. Likely abilities for his Chosen include Survival(wanderers, coyotes, and tumbleweeds alike travel through the wilderness more often than they'd prefer), Resistance(those who wander through the desert, be they man or beast, must often go without food, water, or rest), Performance(the ability that covers the traveler's wild stories and the coyote's mournful howls), Linguistics and Socialize(knowing the local tongue can mean life or death), and Athletics(wether you've angered the local authorities or need to get to shelter quick, athletic talent is a must).
                                            REASONS FOR EXIGENCE
                                            • The Laughing Tumbleweed Vagabond is concerned. Zombies and War Ghosts have become common, attacking travelers in order to raise their numbers. People have begun hiding in cities, submitting themselves to tyrants and obligations to avoid death. This cannot stand! So now a Chosen of Ma'ii wanders Creation, dispatching the undead to make the roads and wilderness safe for travelers once again.
                                            • The Keeper of Coyotes is horrified. A wicked Exalt, allied to the souls of the Principal of Hierarchy, has staked out a territory in Creation , and plans to spread his rule throughout the world. His goal? Turn all of Creation into a single, massive city, its inhabitants turned into mindless drones who are defined by their obligations and duties. Ma'ii seeks to employ a Chosen post haste, lest freedom and travel be excised from the world!
                                            • While Ma'ii was traveling Creation, he logged in the home of a young mortal.He was impressed by their willingness to put up with his antics and to give the wandering god whatever he asked. But what really tugged at his heart was their sorry state. They were a living pawn, so bound up in obligations and duties and pre-arranged events that they had no control over their own life. When he returned to Yu-Shan, Ma'ii knew what he had to do. And so the young mortal received a gift, the greatest the Laughing Tumbleweed Vagabond could conceive: Freedom, and with it, the power to control their own destiny.

                                                        I dunno how on-topic this is, but I had an idea for an Exigent. Their patron was a God of Treasure and Artiface, who had grown weaker with the decline of Creation's economy. So the God wanted a champion to bring the treasures of the First Age back to the fore, which might rekindle his Essence in ways that would even offset the Law of Diminishment somewhat. (Note: In a 'net gain' sense. The God would still be much weaker than if he had not granted the Exigence AND Creation started mass archeological digs, but was weak due to so much of the past being lost that he engaged in a desperate gamble)

                                                        The resulting Exigent would be a metaphorical god among Scavenger Lords. 99% non-combat charms, for things like appraisal, mapping, and dungeon diving. Any combat skill would come from an affinity for artifact weapons, including the ability to unlock an artifact's Evocations. I don't know how viable or balanced that would be in Ex3, but I just love the idea.
