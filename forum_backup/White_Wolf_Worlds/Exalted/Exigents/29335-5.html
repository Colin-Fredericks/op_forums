                        The Affliction

                        During the the five days of Calibration, or following a particularly inauspicious series of circumstances, a desperate mortals throws his prayers across the sands of Cecelyne and into the alleys of the Demon City. The denizens of Malfeas pay heed to the mortal's desires and reach out with a fist of burning emerald flames to bestow upon him the power of the Desolate Exaltation.

                        A demon of the second or third circle uses the power of the Affliction to sheer away a portion of their own power and bestow it upon the mortal, where it festers and grows. The mortal gains the power to fulfill his cursed desires, while the demon dreams of his Chosen's glories.

                        Demons most often bestow this power upon mortals with whom they share a Defining Intimacy.
                        There's an equivalent of the Exigence that's thematically appropriate to demons. I came up with it in five minutes.
