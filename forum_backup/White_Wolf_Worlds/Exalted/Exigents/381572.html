          Never really thought too much about this before. But taking a few minutes, this is what I came up with.

          Such a hypothetical Exalt would represent the concept of Exaltation itself. They wouldn't necessarily be more powerful than any other Exalt, but their charmset would be inspired by the Exalted, the Exaltations, and the actions that the Exalted have undertaken - specifically the Celestial Exalted more so than Dragon Blooded, as Celestial Exaltation seems to be more Lytek's purview.

          So this charmset would be inspired by both by the greatest wonders that the Exalted host was able to bring into existence, the shining beacon of promise that was the early First Age, as well as the most abject horrors of the First Age so terrible that there was no choice but to forever diminish Creation itself to save it from those same Exalted.

          So charm-wise, I might take some inspiration from Gentimians, whom we have been told have 2 different Essence pools, and the way a given charm functions can change depending on which type of Essence they use to activate the charm.

          I don't know that I would necessarily give this "Chosen of Lytek" two Essence pools, but I would like to have this Exalted's charms dual-function as both "Hero" and "Horror" so that a given charm may be activated in either method. A Hero charm would be representative of the glorious Solars/Lunars/Sidereal heroes and be inspired by the seven virtues, whereas a Horror charm would tend to be representative of the depictions of the negative traits of the Exalted - Solar Tyrants, Lunars Monsters, Traitorous Sidereals, and draw inspiration from the seven deadly sins.

          For a Limit Break system, I would look towards Alchemicals and create a sliding scale for these guys, where they would move between the Hero and Horror aspect, their personality changing as it goes, with one side bringing out the best in them while the other brings out the worst. In either case, they would gain different bonuses and penalties depending on where they are on the scale, so sometimes it might be more advantageous to be a selfish-brigand and other times you'd be better off as a shining knight. Importantly, it would be fairly easy to move (even unintentionally) from one side to the other, so the character would be moving between those two extremes fairly often.

          Well, this idea could probably use some serious ironing out, but that's probably where I would get started if I were to try and whip out a charmset for this Exigent.

              He emerged in the East, but his army soon became known across Creation.
              In his presence, mere peasants can crush stone with their hands. The frail become tough as oxen, and the dumb may charm kings with their wit. It is said that a light- the scattering, fluid Exaltation within him- enhances their raw Attributes. To be near him is to be near a fountain of energy.
              He is never seen on the front lines, and this is well, for his gift is not a personal one. The many folk whose power relies on him protect him loyally, even valiantly. They love him as flowers love the rain.
              His will is carried out only by servants, never the man himself. He glowed, my lord, yet beneath that he was only a weak mortal.
              In the evenings, he sits around a campfire with his minions, telling them heretical tales of Anathema- and, supposedly, the Heavenly City itself. I...I almost found myself believing his lies, but logic has not failed me. It is impossible for any human, even a Dragon-Blooded, to know what he knows about the past.
              Worst of all, he insists that our Chosen are flawed. That a great darkness corrupted the incorruptible Elements long ago, and it makes itself known in terrible sins.
              He calls it the 'Great Curse.'

                  I'd probably spin it similar to Fabian Cortez from Marvel Comics. As a mutant, his only "power" was to enhance other mutants.

                  He could use this benignly -- when Wolverine stabbed Magneto in the stomach, he "enhanced" Magneto's healing (such that Magneto survived the wounds -- but his body was weak from the unnaturally rapid healing response).

                  He could use this malignantly -- when fighting the X-Men, he "enhanced" Bishop's energy-absorption powers (such that he began absorbing all kinds of ambient sources nearby, burning himself out).

                  .

                  Can't see how it'd fit with a regular troop of characters.

                  But possible powers could include....
                  ....lending motes to others.
                  ....temporarily lending Essence levels to others (so your Essence 2 Exalted is now Essence 3 for the scene - but you suffer some damage at the end of the scene from burn-out).
                  ....a mini-suite of defense (defend-other) charms.
                  ....a mini-suite of social combat charms (for prying in their heads and making you remember/forget things).

                  Could also grant a sort of Caliban-style (another mutant) ability to track/spot Exalts (at least those who aren't magically obfuscating their nature).

                  .

                  And as an NPC, I'd take even more Marvel inspiration with Apocalypse and his Horsemen. Apocalypse seeks out powerful mutants as his heralds -- and using (cosmic-divine-tech-tools) to empower them, just as he was empowered. An Exigent of Lytek might have a specific plot -- and use her or his abilities to hunt down specific Exalts (who he can then provide with artifacts and aim them at whatever cause he's pushing).

                          It's an interesting thought. First, we'd need 3E to really tell us what it is that Lytek specifically does. As it's changed from each edition so far. He isn't actually the God of Exaltation, as his official title is something like God of Power Bestowed Unto Mortals (or something equally innocuous like that). So his Exigent would probably have to bestow certain powers onto others. He could also have charms that bolster other powers used by different people. He might even have some high level charms that grant "power" in a different sense, like declaring some random guy a King of a new kingdom. It's a rather broad, and yet niche category that he controls.

                          Not to derail, but I seriously doubt Lytek would ever invest in an exigent. For one, he can't risk disappearing like Ten Sheaves did. From what we know, he's the only one that can do the "pruning" of exaltations for the celestials. I seriously doubt he's taught anyone else to do it, as he was said to take a lot of pride in his personal work with the celestial exalts. Also, most Exigents were claimed to be taken up by gods that really need them to deal with a major threat, or because they feel like their worshippers have forgotten them. Lytek isn't in danger of either of those two reasons. That might help with the direction of your exigent, "why" would Lytek need one to begin with? Just some food for thought.
