        Originally posted by Fata-Ku View Post
        This might be covered in the future Exigents hardback, but, who gets to say (in-setting) what Charms the Exigent gets? Are they derived directly from the god's themes and are somewhat set in stone like a closed charm-set? Is it the Exigent that makes them up as it goes along, constrained only by theme but otherwise free to make up whatever works down to any arbitrary depth?

        As in, is it up to the Exigents to figure out how to apply their power (e.g. volcanoes) to Craft and create appropriate Charms, or is there a point where the Exaltation just shrugs and tells the character "sorry man, I got nothing"?
        The dev's have mentioned that charms are now just aspects of a character's experience, will, imagination, etc and not things hard-wired into an exalation. (I don't think it was that way in 2nd either unless you were an infernal.) So the hard wired aspect isn't gong to show up and it will probably be closer to "making it up as you go" for everyone when it comes to charms. As for what exigents are limited to I would say they are limited in to theme of their patron god. Though like other exalts this gets tricky since we've had mention of a hypothetical healing exigent. The example there was that while the exigent would be able to heal injuries and wounds, they would also be able to do stuff like heal relationships. So think of it as sticking to your theme.

What comes out is a matter of instinct with most gods, like an oyster making a pearl. Very powerful and meticulous gods known for extreme control and delicate craftsmanship might be able to design Charms. The answer is basically, it takes the shape of whatever makes sense, just like how they are chosen or how the Exaltation passes on (or whether it passes on at all).
