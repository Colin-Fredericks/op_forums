                        Hmm...How would you all feel if 3E retconned Ahlat's daughter, Blood-on-the-Horn, into being an Exigent instead? It would show how they have shaped the world...but would it take away from God-Bloods?
                        I hope they don't.

                        After rereading Houses of the Bull God this is now my preferred way to add Exigence to that narrative.
                        • Ahlat applies for Exigence so that his Chosen could unite Harborhead, he is denied.
                        • Ahlat sires Blood-on-the-Horn who goes onto do what his Exigent would've done.
                        • The Realm unable to ignore the situation send 6 legions and Blood-on-the-Horn is killed in single combat.
                        • Enraged Ahlat confronts Taichi-Kun in Yu-Shan it's a tense moment as the two war gods square off until the Unconquered Sun intervenes.
                        • Once they're alone he speaks to Ahlat about why his earlier request for Exigence was denied, it's very much a conversation between old friends and during this he talks about his own experiences as a father and mentions the Solar Exalted. Ahlat understands.
                        • He offers him the Exigence that was declined him 60 years earlier knowing he could easily use it to get revenge on Tachi-Kun.

                        346 years later Ahlat still keeps the Exigence in a safe place. He's been tempted to use it several times over the centuries before ultimately deciding it's unnecessary. But as the Time of Tumult worsens he may soon have no choice but to Exalt a champion.

                                        I've been thinking of doing a Zhuzhiao exigent, because it seems like a strong palette to work with, and my current thinking for that is that the default justification would be that someone powerful stole some sort of game-specific MacGuffin from Zhuzhiao's horde, and then ran off with it to a place where Zhuzhiao couldn't reach. It's a little pedestrian, but it gives you an in to play the character in any part of the world, and you can easily wire it into an ongoing plot, like you're in a game about fighting the Dowager and your character's goal is to track down the Dowager, punch her in the face, take the Collar of Broken Dreams back from her, and huck it in the ocean.

                                                MUCALINDA- SHOGUN OF SHAMANS, DAIYMO OF SERPENTS, AND SUB-ADMINISTRATOR OF HARVESTS, HEALTH, AND INTOXICANTS.
                                                Mucalinda was not always so grand as he is today. No, before the Primordial war, he was little but a lesser god of a eastern species of snakes. But, by hook and by crook, he has raised himself into one of the more influential spirits in Yu-Shan, using his political acumen, skills at deception, and weak prophetic abilities to outmaneuver his opponents. A good portion of his power comes from being the direct subordinate of(and ambassador between) three rather potent spirits in the court of Yu-Shan. In terms of appearance, he usually takes the form of a Snakeman of some kind, often related to some Eastern species, to reflect the fondness for the direction in which he "grew up". Since his recent adoption of the Naga, a group of patronless rain gods, he has started taking a form in homage of their own, an 7 ft upright, four armed snake whose tail is a tangle of venomous serpents. He can take a more human form, but no matter what shape he takes, his eyes are always the cold slivers of his serpent charges. He tends to the cold and calculating, often seeing his fellow gods(such as his yes-man and crony, the god of eastern dragonflies) as nothing more than tools, to be discarded at a whim. Never the less, he does try to do what he thinks is best for his worshipers and charges, even if he is prone to think of them more as pets than people. He also bears genuine respect for those who seek to rise above their station, as he once did, and those who are truly devout, no matter what they believe(that is not to say he will tolerate the servants of Oblivion or the Yozi; rather, he will ensure the poison that melts away their insides suppresses their pain rather than enhancing it). He is relatively skilled at Martial Arts, though he is no great master. He is notable for teaching the art of hand-to-hand combat to the Solar who would invent Snake style. He often appears somewhat mystical to outsiders, prone to keeping secrets and using hallucinogenics alongside his panoply charms to gain vague insights into the future. Likely Abilities for his Chosen include Brawl and Dodge(the snake is swift and deadly, striking fast and escaping gracefully as its venom finishes its foe ), Socialize(The Daimyo of Serpents is a skilled manipulator, and snakes are well-known for deceit and desire), Survival and Medicine(Serpents are symbols of health and fertility the world over), and Occult(Serpents often have mystical and deathly associations, not to mention the prophetic might of the Shogun of Shamans himself).
                                                REASONS FOR EXIGENCE
                                                • Mucalinda is seething with rage. A growing number of his eastern followers have been tortured, killed, and enslaved by House Cynis, who have been using them to harvest the sacred plants used by the tribe's shamans in many important rituals. While he might have shown them mercy if they wished to partake in the wisdom brought by the magical intoxicant, they have merely grown tired of the current drug de jour and only want to find a novel way to get high. Thus, he has sought out a Heroic enemy of the Dynasts, and has offered him power,so long as he frees the worshipers of the Snake Daimyo in the course of destroying all Cynis holds dear.
                                                •Mucalinda has one responsibility he does not speak of: he is guardian of the Root-Eater Key, the only thing that can open the prison holding the Behemoth Nilbog, created by the Primordials to destroy Creation should they even grow irredeemably tired of it, the key to the prison stolen by a Night with the aid of the Great Maker, who forged the prison, just before the war. Except now the Key has been stolen! He knows not how, for he cannot remember the incident, and so he needs a champion who can look for it discretely, lest a panic be caused.
                                                • The Shogun of Serpents has sent a missive to a randomly select set of Heroic mortals in Creation, offering a mysterious prize to whichever rises highest above their current station in a year. He will then promptly Exalt the winner with no obligations, save the knowledge that to act against Creation or threaten his charges will invite the wrath of the Lord of Nagas.
                                                •Mucalinda has grow annoyed with the Immaculate Faith. While he tolerates those who are truly devout, he can not tolerate how this false religion leaves mortals content with their disgusting lowliness. He therefore has scouted out a strong-willed, rebellious mortal, whom he offers an Exaltation and a mission: Do as you please, and, in doing so, spread the doctrine of ambition and desire to all of Creation.

                                                    Ten Thousand Spears, God of Large Armies

                                                    Ten Thousand Spears is the God if Large Armies and the patron of the Realm's legions. Possible threats to his purview include:
                                                    - Ten Thousand Spears has struck a deal with the Bronze faction. He will provide an Exalted champion to serve as a neutral party to command the Realm's armies against Solar-led armies in the threshold. In exchange, the Sidereals will support his attempt to usurp Tachi-Kun's position. He need only find a suitable candidate.
                                                    - The battles and duels between the Dragon-Blooded warriors of the Realm Civil War are strengthening the influence of Tachi-Kun as the God of Exalted Warfare. In order to undermine his superior's position, Ten Thousand Spears has Exalted a champion to lead a populist revolt against the Dragon-Blooded and introduce a new faction into the war.
                                                    - The Realm's withdrawal from the threshold is undermining its influence. Ten Thousand Spears has chosen to find a champion to unite the abandoned satrapies under a single banner to repel Lunar raids and enforce their loyalty to the Realm.
