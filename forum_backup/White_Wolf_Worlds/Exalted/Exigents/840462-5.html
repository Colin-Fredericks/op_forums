                                                            I would tend to portray the Exigence as almost solely the province of the Sun, as I would see his whole King of Heaven thing coming from him being the more public and sociable of them, with the others having a more introverted and mysterious character. I would think of the Flame as something that has to be asked for, and Luna and the Maidens not exactly being figure whom you can petition. That's just my sense of how to maintain a consistency in their distinct characteristics and position in the Celestial Order. This is a schema in which it still might be fitting for one of the other Incarnae to endorse a small god's petition before the Unconquered Sun, which may or may not have been solicited, but the buck still stops with him.
