        Originally posted by Rater202
        Even if the Anathema do not start as evil but become evil, the fact that they are, according to immaculate doctrine, body stealing sorcerers, means that an Anathema not acting evil is merely pretending to be who they once were. It makes absolutely no logical sense for the Anathema in question to think that they're the person they body jacked, and if the anathema in question does not actually steal the persons body but merely grants them power and influences them-then they can both be resisted and therefore are not, in fact, inevitably going to turn evil or body jacking demons, and it means that regardless of why the Doctrine is wrong on the subject of Anathema, they were wrong on the subject of anathema.
        "Slowly replaces your soul" is not the same as "body-snatcher".
        Since, in Exalted, people have 2 souls (y'know, Hun and Po), and on top of that there's a huge difference between "soul" and "mind". Having one or both of your souls replaced by a thing that makes you want to exert your power and slowly twists the rest of you doesn't mean your brain or thoughts are altered.

        Also, you're grossly underestimating the degree to which people want to cling to their beliefs, no matter how mistaken they are. People don't generally want to accept that the thing they've believed their etire life is a lie, and will search for (and find, through confirmation bias) ways to rationalize away any discrepencies.

        Adding to that, Solars have augmented powers of self-delusion (Integrity can just as easily serve to strengthen existing and erroneous beliefs and prejudices, There are examples of Charisma + Presence rolls being used to convince yourself of things (e.g. to build intimacies of "I have been turned into a demon"), a botched Perception + Investigation (introspection) roll for which you have no excellency could easily make you think you feel things that aren't there).

        Lastly, "transcendent religious experiences" are something the fairfolk can inflict upon you simply by feeding on you, as well as through other charms. Just because it "feels right" doesn't mean it's not what the demon wants you to think.

        So no, not every single solar ever is going to figure out their life has been a lie at the exact moment of their exaltation, even though some might.

        Straw Nhilism fallacy. Someone who discovers or decides that there is no reward or punnishment after death and that life has no inherant meaning is just as likely to decide to give his own life meaning through great deeds of good as he is through great deeds of evil.
        It can be seen as confirmation that you are indeed posessed, as the demon inside you is trying to convince you that your life has been a lie, by giving you visions of false truths and fake transcendence that will only lead you onto the path of sin.

        The amount of things people can make up to hurt themselves out of self-loathing can be staggering. Exalted, especially Solars, really aren't exempt from that.


        Edit: Another example of the powers of delusion: Back in the colonial days of Quebec, anyone who hadn't attended Confession in seven years was thought to be a werewolf, and people believed themselves to actually be werewolves despite there being no such Void-damned thing.
