    Fuel for Exigents: the homebrew God List

    11-04-2013, 09:20 PM
    Let's make a list of all the custom gods from people's campaigns, and prep them for Exigence.

    -Name: Han Dei Fang
    -Purview: Knowledge and texts burned
    -Description: A bald man of middle-height, skin composed of pages in various stages of burning. Clothes of ash. Red-hot cinders for eyes and teeth.
    -Reasons why s/he needs an Exigent: As the Solars return, memories and texts of the past once lost forever are now resurfacing in the minds of the Lawgivers. Some of them are even rewriting them into physical copies and digital memory banks of manses. Han Dei Fang wants an Exigent of his own, stating that such knowledge is his purview and that, if the Solars wish to truly regain these ancient texts, gifted to him by Shogunate Wyld Hunts and centuries of Sidereal historical editing, they must prove themselves against his champion. Realistically, he just wants to keep the theorms and power of the First Age to himself, where he can gift it out piecemeal instead of losing entire tomes of knowledge from his control with every reborn breath. He's willing to 'share' his Exigent with others, provided the chosen serves his interests....

    I used to know things. Forbidden things.

          Name: Plaguefather Tsamaki Purview: Oversees plague in Nexus Description: A hunched, emaciated old man garbed in a flea-bitten ratskin cloak and nothing else, his features are ravaged by plague. Open sores cover his body, weeping pus and other ichors; patches of his gray hair are falling out; his neck and armpits are swollen and splotched purple. Whenever sickness spreads in Nexus, the people pray to Plaguefather Tsamaki: the healthy pray that his sickness does not touch them; the sick pray that he won't let them suffer. It is to the latter that his heart goes out. Why would he want an Exigent? Okay, I'm not sure about that one; he's just a god I've used in my games before.

              Name: Dutiful Nhan

              Domain: Shadows, dust, and forgotten corners

              Description: A short and slender figure, mostly humanoid, whose body and gauzy robes are both comprised of a homogenous tangle of dust fluff and cobwebs. His head and features have a vaguely feline cast to them, but he has no eyes nor mouth nor nose, merely the rough shape of a face. despite this, he can and does speak, but his voice will always, always come as a soft whisper behind the shoulder of the one he addresses.

              Why would he need an exigent? Nhan's duties frequently place him in contact with the Forbidding Manse of Ivy. The secret and lost places of Creation frequently fall under his gaze, and he may well feel it in the world's interest that what is lost and buried should perhaps remain lost and buried.

                  Name: Shah-Rajiesh, Goddess of Oasis, Southern Queen of the Dunes
                  Purviews: The twin-aspects of Oasis: sand and water, death by starvation, nourishment in hostile environments, isolation, desolation, and hope
                  Description: A young woman, by appearance, with skin the colour of bronzened olive, big, green eyes, long black hair, and garments of flowing black silk, interwoven with fine golden threads. She carries on bands attached to her dress and limbs small lanterns that flicker as if exposed to wind, yet never go out. The air around is always hot and dry, and smells of Southern herbs and spices, her steps always leave behind grains of sand, and if she walks the desert, small flowers bloom in her wake, only to dry up within the same day.
                  All during the South Shah-Rajiesh is venerated as the matron of the desert tribes, and the protector of caravans. She creates, and guards over, the oases of the vast Southern deserts, as such her charms and abilities deal with creating and destroying sources of food and drink, especially in the desert. She can manipulate the flow of water (not salt water), direct the flow of dunes, and show caravans the quickest path from oasis to oasis, or lead her enemies to a slow and dry death in the heat of the Sun's gaze.
                  She has fast travel charms to jump from oasis to oasis, can create and destroy sources of life within the desert, and manipulate (perceived?) distance while within the desert. She is the Immaculate Beuraucracy's attempt to make the former domain of Cecelyne something more... Just, and hopeful. She can be merciless if angered, though, and should be considered one of the major players in the Southern world of spirits, and probably mortals and exalts as well.

                  Why she would need an Exigent:
                  The Southern desert is vast, and borders the Wyld at the Pole of Fire. Shah-Rajiesh is a powerful goddess in her own right and well capable of keeping the odd band of Fair Folk off her oases, but the Wyld is ever encroaching on her domain, and the biggest blasphemy yet is just now surfacing - the Green Sun Princes, amongst them champions of the Primordial Cecelyne, whose former domain is now under the god's control. With the Realm satrapies taken up by strife, the protection of the desert peoples falls to the goddess, who cannot possibly be everywhere at once. Surely a Champion is needed, to unite the desert tribe in defence against the Fair Folk and even worse blasphemies, leaving the goddess to guard the land, having her Champion lead her people.

                  This is an example of a rather powerful god with an encompassing purview that would surely be able to sustain at least one Exigent without much diminishing herself, lending her exigents powers over life and death through control over the duality of desert sand and fresh water, as well as derived powers of isolation to keep within the theme of oases, like taking away a person's ability to understand and communicate with others for a limited time, or warp travel distances/speeds for individuals or small units with a low magnitude, as well as sorcerous effects controlling sandstorms and sinkholes.

                          Name: Yava Yochanan
                          Purview: Serendipity; Good Fortune
                          Description: An old and bearded man in the robes of a wanderer, with an almost perpetually amused expression and a mischievous gleam in the eye. Specifics change over time, but invariably he carries one or two items of great value, often quite incongruous with the rest of his appearance. Frequently smokes an almost comically long pipe.
                          Reason he needs an Exigent: Frankly, he doesn't. But he happened to win a black market exaltation from Plentimon in a card game, and shortly thereafter bestowed it upon a poor traveler he came across who offered to share with him some bread and water - it's in Yava Yochanan's nature to reward those who he deems deserve it, whether by their cleverness, tenacity or (as in this case) simple kindness. Besides, he wanted to see what the Traveler would do with it...

                                  Name: Kanra, King of Masks
                                  Domain: False identities. In addition to the predictable spies, assassins, and con men, actors, writers, and costumers also fall under his portfolio.
                                  Appearance: His default mode is eerily reminiscent of the Fair Folk, with sharp, inhumanly beautiful features. However, it isn't always so- his personality is quite mercurial, and a change in personality accompanied by small alterations to his appearance and wardrobe can have him seem to be someone (or something) else entirely. When not playing a role, Kanra has a strange, exaggerated manner, as if he's playing to a back row that isn't there- his speech is too enunciated, his movements to dramatic for any sort of conventional tone.
                                  Why he needs an Exigent: Nobody trusts a god with such a deceptive domain. Distrusted by gods and mortals alike, he created an exigence so that he could have a steward: a face people could recognize, a voice they could trust, and, if need be, a vector for social charms to smooth over the rough patches. Unfortunately, Kanra has never been able to escape his own nature- the exigence gives masterful powers of manipulation, and he favors confidence tricksters above all others with it.

                                                      Name: Azure Peacock

                                                      Purview: Beauty | Cosmetics | Fashion | Style

                                                      Appearance: He appears to be a fully grown, lean-built man with green and blue wing tattoos covering his hands and forearms. Dark blue feathers grow over his flanks without covering his abdomen and pectorals. He has blue hair with shoulder-length strands framing both sides of his face with the rest cascading down the back reaching his waist. He typically changes his dress every hour and is never seen wearing the same thing twice.

                                                      Reason He Needs An Exigent: It's quite simple, really. It's so that he can be better than you. "Oh, you're the War God for an entire Direction?
