    I can see the argument to reimagine Lytek for 3e.
    He's traditionally rooted in a very dehumanising view of Exaltation that might be worth taking the time to dismantle since he could still be interesting as a bureaucrat who has been recording the exploits of the Exalted since the First Age and probably knows any Exalt who gets to see him well enough to qualify as their unofficial biographer.

    It lends itself to more than "no! unpack your 2e baggage! bad! baaaad!"

        I like Lytek as the God of Exaltation- not of the Exalted or of Exaltations note pluralization of.
        A lot of the 'Lytek maintains, trims and grooms the Exalted Essences' stuff (which, to be far, was always present) had, I feel, an unexpectedly mechanisticizing (is that a word?) of the act of Exaltation; it moved away from being a miracle and become more firmly Sufficiently Advanced Magic.

        I like the idea of him act as the unofficial biographer of the Exalted, but that should be, like, a hobby. His *job* should be rubberstamping reports that read like 'Today, three people Exalted. They are Bob the Baker, Chompy the Lunar and Ragara Alice. Their Exaltations occured without incident.'

        And very occusionally, passing a report to the Sidereals of 'Today, Bob Exalted. Sort of. See, Han-Tha got his hands on an Exigent Exaltation, and it was already pretty screwed up before that, and... yeah, if you could get someone down to Rathess by Saturnday, that'd be just super.'

                            Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post

                            ​You know that even in Second Edition, Abyssal and Infernal Exaltation were granted by the direct will and choice of the Deathlords and Yozis respectively?

                            The significance of the "Exaltation as self-directed thing" was retained purely in a rationale for why those entities didn't have complete latitude in who they could choose, and what would happen if their control mechanisms were broken.
                            The omission of a "n't" aside, I have been imagining Exaltations in 3E as being more like relationships with the patron than artifact N/A glowing motes. It seems a lot harder to pervert the miracle of the Sun's noticing and falling in love with someone than it is to slap some yozi essence on a magic ball and have at it. I suppose this will just invite another round (I don't expect them from you) of accusations that I fail to understand the concept of something intangible being affected by other things, but perhaps it has more to do with no longer thinking of Exaltations as having an existence beyond the patron's choice. (Such that there is no Cabinet where Exaltations hang out between mortals taking second breath, they don't exist until Luna, or the Maidens, or whomever sees someone and their nature moves them to make champions of them at which point suddenly there is an Exalt and the Exaltation is the event.)

                            Though I don't seem to recall the Yozi having much say in the matter since they literally had to stuff the Exaltation into a demon who went around following the things own guidance system and then did the bargaining.
