                                                        Folks seem to be falling a bit into a weird thing where lots of numbers are closer than they actually are.

                                                        From things we know, based on the use of 1e Exalt numbers there are:
                                                        c. 150 Solars
                                                        c. 100 Abyssals
                                                        c. 50 Infernals
                                                        c. 400 Lunars
                                                        100 Sidereals
                                                        10,000 Dragon-Blooded in the Realm, probably twice that worldwide.

                                                        From other dev. coments we have seen implicationst aht there are:
                                                        c. 60 Liminals
                                                        No more than 1,000 Exigents at any given time in history and currently no more than probably around 100.

                                                        I'm going ot guestimate 60ish Getimians.

                                                        This totals to a total Exalted population of about 22,000 Exalted worldwide. Maybe a few hundred more after others are itnroduced. There's probably a simlar amount of god-blooded around. We've been told there's no such thing as enlightened mortals anymore, so toss that out.

                                                        That's about 35,000 supernatural beings. Which is a big number. It's a lot out there! But then remember that Creation on a lot of estimates of prior editions hasl 500 million people. This means that there are four orders of magnitude more total humans in Creation than there are Chosen. In fact, there are nearly 20,000 more total people than there are Exalted. Even if we're extra generous and say there's enough Terrestrials and god-blooded to make it 50,000 supernatural humans that's still one in 10,000.

                                                        Those are actually generally terrible odds for anything. For a vast majority of Creation, meeting any given Exalt is very unlikely unless they live in major settlements tha tend to draw them. I think the idea that we have worries about most Exalts save Terrestrials being counted in the hundreds or dozens as something overfilling the setting is bizrre to me. Excluding Dragon-Blooded, there are less than a thousand Exalts world-wide currently. That's vanishingly rare. One thousand people out of half a billion. In the modern world you're more likely to be a billionaire than any particular kind of Exalt.

                                                        And this is all with an assumption taht Creation doesn't pick up more people in the new edition due to increased area. And Exalt numbers will remain the same as I Note, meaning they're even more dispersed about.

                                                        Whether this is still to many for some folks is one thing, but I am somewhat surprised at the impressions on numbers given about here.
