    Monster of the Week Exigency

    06-10-2014, 07:59 PM
    You know what I kinda want to see?

    Exigencies from Forbidden Gods and/or Gods of Atrocity; with all their charms focused on making you a big honkin' monster.

    I want them to have the following traits:
    • They give you a big ol' boost of power.
    • They make you really inhuman.
    • They pare away at your options until killing everybody seems like the only reasonable, sane solution.

    I don't want this for players, exactly; I want this as something I can have Gods of Atrocity just toss out there, secure in the knowledge that they will inevitably decay into a self-sustaining cycle of violence and despair.

    Because... it just feels like something that would exist, ya know?

    I have decided, after some thought, that I don't really feel happy on these forums. I might decide to come back to post. Who knows - but right now, I'm gone.

    So good bye, good luck, and have a nice day.

                  Yeah, I'd really like to see this. A lot of people complained that Infernals (especially pre-Crane) were locked into a specific arc on the basis of their primary Yozi, but with Exigents that's actually totally fine, since they don't need to support a wide range of concepts.


                  I have an idea for a thermonuclear death-spiral Exigent of Adamant (probably a black-market attempt to gin up the Ultimate Most Powerful Exalt) who is basically Phoenix from X-men. The character arc is around building rapidly in power but also losing control as you do so. Some of my loose notions so far:
                  • The character's Essence increases by one dot every time she Limit Breaks, and she has some charms that can accelerate this process in order to lend her power when acting passionately. Anything that would cause her permanent Essence to go down fails. At Essence 10, either she goes permanently out of control and becomes an adversary NPC, or she explodes violently and transmutes a huge area to glass, or she evaporates into pure energy and leaves her heart behind as a five-dot Hearthstone that does not require a manse or demesne (ST's choice).
                  • Her powers kind of suck and her mote pool is low compared to others of equal Essence, so she's really underwhelming early on, but can outpace anyone else in the long haul just because she can easily end up 4-5 Essence above every other PC.
                  • She uses Attribute charms, sort of -- the Trait prerequisites are of the form (Attribute + Essence), ranging from 3 to 10. Dice-adder cap is also Attribute + Essence, maximum 10. The upshot of this is that as you reach the upper echelons of power, your abilities become less distinctive and less about who you are as a person, because you start bumping into the cap. Powers near the prereq cap tend to be a bit creepy and over-the-top and express the nature of the Exaltation rather than the nature of the character.
                  • On any turn in which she spends motes (or any 5-second period, outside of combat), she must spend at least (Essence) motes or the difference is spent automatically as a surcharge.
                  • In general, her higher-essence powers are harder to control, and she has a lot of effects that are harder to control specifically as Essence increases
                  • Her bonfire anima tends to coat things with a brittle, paper-thin layer of glass, and causes thin needles of glass to sprout randomly from surfaces.

                  It's a hilariously disruptive concept, so I can't imagine it'd work well as a PC in most groups, but it's kind of implicit that at some point you'll melt down and retire the PC, and the focus shifts on the other PCs' efforts to clean up the mess.
