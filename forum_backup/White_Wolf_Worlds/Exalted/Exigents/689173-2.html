                One of the big differences between the Realm and Lookshy is that Lookshy is merely a regional power and lacks a lot of the wealth, manpower, Exalted and supernatural backing that the Realm possesses.                         A group of Outcasts is hardly going to put their lives on hold for months or years in order to chase an Anathema across Creation. Once an Anathema leaves their territory, most of the time they will just say, "Mission accomplished," and leave it at that. They have lives to live, families to take care of, jobs to perform, kingdoms to run, etc. And most of the time they aren't going to drop everything to chase down Anathema foe thousands of miles when it's clear he has no intention of sticking around in their territory. They're people with lives, not video game enemies.

                        It's why the Realm's Wild Hunt is in a different league. Many Wild Hunts recruit monks for a reason. Monks generally don't have lives or major obligations outside of their religious duties. They are much better positioned to drop everything at a moments notice and chase the Anathema to the far ends of Creation. But that what makes the Realm's Wyld Hunt an exception and more dangerous and tenacious than local or regional Wyld Hunts. You probably shouldn't assume that Outcasts Wyld Hunts have the same scope as the Realm's Wild Hunts.

                        If some Anathema is causing problems in the Scavenger Lands and he escapes from a Lookshyan Wild Hunt into the Linnowan lands and then keeps going North, it's very possible that the Lookshayan Wild Hunt will disengage and cease pursuit. Lookshy just doesn't typically have the manpower to spare on sending Wild Hunts across Creation in the same way that the Realm does.

                                        While in theory the Dragon Blooded are kindred whom are unceasingly devoted to hunting down and killing the Anathema wherever they are found, the reality is probably a bit different. For most Dragon Blooded, facing off against one of the Anathema is likely going to be the most dangerous thing that Dragon Blooded ever does in his entire life. If they faced an Anathema, and the Anathema managed to survive and escape across a great distance, I can't imagine that most Dragon Blooded are so fanatical that they would willingly sacrifice their own lives, leaving their territories undefended and their families unprotected in order to chase an Anathema across Creation when that that Anathema no longer poses a realistic threat.

                                        The real threats to that Terrestrial's territory will be local Raksha, bandits, Realm incursions, etc, not some Anathema who buggered off to Coral. There is likely no shortage of stories in Creation about Dragon Blooded who spent years chasing some hated foe or wicked Anathema across Creation, only to eventually return home and find their family dead and everything they worked for in ruins.

                                        While non-Realm Dragon Blooded chasing an Anathema across Creation makes for a good setup for a chronicle, and reason for a group of Dragon Blooded to work together, it's generally going to be fairly unusual and atypical.

                                        Most Wyld Hunts probably end when an Anathema is dead or fled to the point that chasing them down is no longer realistic, and the Wyld Hunt is called off with the understanding that wherever that Anathema goes, a new Wild Hunt will be formed and track him down. He will be killed eventually. It will be the rare Wyld Hunt (likely fueled by a desire for revenge against that particular Anathema) in which the Wyld Hunt roams far beyond the territorial boundaries of its members.

                                                            Originally posted by DrLoveMonkey View Post

                                                            On the other hand most Solars probably aren't taken to simply leaving their home, they're chosen heroes of the rising sun! Especially if it's only an outcast Wyld Hunt, which are way more ragtag and unsupported.
                                                            Indeed, I don't imagine a lot of Celestials will just run away (they might run away from the Hunt, but probably only to strike back later).
                                                            Some will. But one issue is that when a Celestial runs away, it may well be to hide. Consider, for example, if you attack a Lunar and she uses Travel Without Distance. Where's she gone? No idea. You stay in the area for 3 weeks, and she doesn't reappear. Did she take the form of a peasant and is just living a normal life? Is she watching you as a literal fly on the wall? Did she turn into an albatross and fly off to Coral? Did she go to the Wyld? No idea. So, you look for her for a while, but without leads, you may as well go home. If she turns up again, you'll have to go back and try something else, if she doesn't, well, congratulate yourselves and driving her off and stopping her evil plans.

                                                            (This is another thing that less fanatical Dragonblood probably consider: the purpose of killing Anathema isn't actually killing Anathema. You can't kill them, all you can do is kill their current host, they'll just find another one somewhere. But what you can do is stop them building a power base, stop them training and developing their powers, and keep them running and scared. At the end of the day, the Anathema aren't a threat that can be defeated, merely one that can be kept down. Like plague, or ill-health. So, while killing them is best, ruining their plans, smashing their armies, and forcing them to flee to the wilderness to live like a dog is a solid second-best)
