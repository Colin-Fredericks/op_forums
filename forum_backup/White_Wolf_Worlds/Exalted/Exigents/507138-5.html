                                                Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz View Post

                                                Also, I wonder: how much does the UCS know when he's choosing people? It's not like he's omnipotent. How much does he know about then when he grants them Exaltation?
                                                I've had this idea before... I can't quite remember the precise way I articulated it when it first came to me, but it was something like the Unconquered Sun possessing a depth of senses and breadth of mind that is capable of encompassing your entire life story and a whole heap of details of your inner life with a single glance, but that doesn't necessarily mean he understands you or can't be surprised by you.

                                                I think it came to me when I was considering points such as, unless a historical personage left behind a diary or autobiography (and even then, sometimes) we can only ever really speculate on their motivations and the reasoning behind their actions. When studying history, we're looking for context more than we are answers; we can never really tell why Gaius Julius Caesar gathered all of the power he did or was so lenient with his enemies or any of the rest, even if we can factually establish the environment and background in which he did those things.

                                                And also when I was thinking of the idea that many or most or all of us contain a rich inner life of imagination and reflections and responses and motives that constantly underlies us as people but is probably never really seen even by our closest loved ones and that we couldn't really tell even if we had the words to convey it because it's just too big.

                                                So from there I have this idea that a god like the Sun could know those things, could see the total of what you are and... perhaps "assess" is better a word than "judge", and he makes decisions based off of that*.

                                                But still with this idea that he doesn't necessarily understand why you have these desires or qualities (not as a matter of him being distant or alien, just being enough of a person to be unable to fully integrate the personality of somebody else). So he could look at somebody like Caesar and get what his motivation or inclination is, but not personally understand why that should matter to him.

                                                But it's still in terms of him being able to see something worthy and grand in you, and wanting to see what you'll make of the power to chase it (even if he can be surprised and even unnerved or angered by the result, although I would say provoking the latter would be more difficult than in a human).

                                                That and the idea that there may be certain consequences he is less concerned about; I would say that the Unconquered Sun wouldn't really Choose a Hernan Cortes who was deliberately setting out to enslave and brutalise hundreds of thousands of people, but he would Choose one who was going on an adventure to Mexico to raid for treasure and stand astride a great civilization and pursue the riches of the land, even if that will logically and perhaps inevitably have consequences leading to the enslavement and brutalisation of hundreds of thousands of people (not because that was the point or that the people doing it are terrible, but because there's a complex chain of circumstances in which personal debts + untapped minerals + large numbers of readily exploited people is an equation that seems to solve itself).

                                                I wouldn't say that the Unconquered Sun is indifferent so much as... that's not necessarily looking far beyond you (although there will be a bit of assessment when the widespread enslavement ultimately comes around, even if he might still be taking a kind of big picture perspective on it).

                                                As for how he can know who to look at without being omniscient... I'd say it's a bit like Makarios finding people who'll trade with him without needing to randomly cycle through everybody in the world. It's just something kind of essential to the world and the god; his gaze naturally and unconsciously falling upon the things and the people that warrant it, because those are just how certain things are connected and intersect on an intrinsic and ineffable level. It's like... when you're the god of certain acts and personalities of heroism, you can be incarnate in those places because for a being of Essence things that are categorical can relate to you and your senses the way light and matter interact with a normal human.

                                                * I've also got this idea that even in the Bureau of Destiny, these things aren't really part of your thread or the general records of your life, or at least not all of them are, and although there are gods like Jupiter who might know them if they should look by and large they disappear from the world when you die, except for some impressions they leave on your next life.
