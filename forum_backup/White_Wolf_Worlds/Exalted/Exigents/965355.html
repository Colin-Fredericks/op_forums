          Yep, 2E Infernal SWLiHN Charms include a psionic blast, mind-reading, mind-controlling people whose minds you destroyed with psionic blasting, and lots of telekinesis. And what could maybe be called telekinetic healing if you squint.
          Infernal Adorjan Charms also let you get telepathy, for the tiny price of becoming mute.

          But outside of SWLiHN's large telekinetic Charm tree, there haven't been any canon Exalted types with dedicated psionics-focused Charmsets.

          I have no clue whether any of the new Exalted types will have such a thing in 3E, but what we last saw about Infernals suggests that they will have far, far fewer psionics in 3E.


          I suspect that mind-reading is going to remain very rare and difficult in Exalted. In 2E both Solars and Infernals have easy-to-use, flawless lie-detection Charms, and the Solar version got a nerf for 3E so that it doesn't cut off whole avenues of storytelling. While 2E Infernals and Sidereals each have one limited mind-reading Charm, I imagine that any 3E mind-reading would have to remain both weak and rare to avoid it becoming the next Judge's Ear Technique.

                                          Do I detect Eleven and Mob somewhere behind this topic thread?

                                          (because they've got me thinking more than I have for a while about how psionic heroes are actually cool.).

                                          Originally posted by Erinys View Post
                                          Would a psionics-focused Exigent make more sense as Attribute-based, or Ability-based? I feel like it makes more aesthetic sense to start with each Attribute and expand psionic applications from them.
                                          I think in either case they could work. Like, if you have Abilities, then you can have psy-Medicine and psy-Archery and psy-Stealth, etc, and there aren't any particular areas of the Ability map that I think can't be convincingly psyd up (behold: psy-Bureuacracy!).

                                          On the other hand, either Ability or Attribute also feels a bit weird because I don't usually think of psychic powers like, "There's a guy who's really good at moving skillfully, therefore it's an extension of his power to move things dexterously by his mind". Psychic powers in fiction seem usually a gift that happens without reference to normal Abilities or Attributes, not a complement or intensifier of them.

                                                      Honestly I find making charm ideas for psionics easy as hell. I mean if you go out and brainstorm, you'll find a TON of different ideas you can experiment with psychic powers alone.                                                       2) Figuring out their proper power tier. This would help explain balance their charm power. I'd place them at Celestial/Sidereal level, but I'm biased as I know Celestials very well compared to Dragonblooded.
