                                          Originally posted by Queshire View Post
                                          So if I recall correctly, it's been established already that only gods can muck around with Exigence, but does it break anything if I wanted, say, a chosen of Ligier without any of the other baggage that comes from being an Infernal? Or what about the story possibilities that come from a first age twilight who got his hands on a handful of black market exigencies and decided to do SCIENCE to them?

                                          Thoughts?

                                          The Exigency derives from the Unconquered Sun, and it's flat out impossible for demons to create Exigents. I think it's impossible for elementals to create Exigents as well, at least I haven't seen anything suggesting they can. Exigents are specifically something that gods create through the assistance of the Unconquered Sun.

                                          That said, it sounds entirely reasonable for demons to have discovering some way of improving or bestowing power on mortals. In fact, with Akuma going away as a thing, I think this could be a very reasonable way of replacing/redefining the original Akuma concept.

                                          Just say, a demon can have exactly one Akuma servant, and it bestows a lot of its power on him. This weakens the demon quite a bit, and more importantly causes some kind of drawback to the demon's Yozi (maybe it gets a -1 on all rolls for every Akuma that serves one of its souls). This creates a "soft cap" in how many Akuma you can have running around. So that way you can have cool antagonists with powersets based off of neato demons, while at the same time not running into the problem that Akuma had in 2nd edition (which was a theoretically unlimited number of them running around with Yozi charms).

                                          I imagine that, even if these guys weren't actual Exigents, the Exigent rule set would still be a wonderful base for creating them and building/balancing their charmsets.

The Yozi could take a mortal and turn them into one of their Akuma - and keep in mind that mortal-Akuma being slightly more powerful than a Dragon Blooded (they had the same Essence pool but were using Solar-tier Charms). This idea though is probably worth exploring in some form, though ensuring that there is some kind of a "cap" on how many Akuma there can be, and probably creating their own custom powers (likely determined by what sort of pact they have formed and what demon they have formed it with).
