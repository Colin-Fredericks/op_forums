                                                          Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz View Post

                                                          I didn't realise, but I recently looked through the older Ask the Devs thread, and Minton said there that Eight Directions is supposed to be out before Exigents.
                                                          That was the original plan, but the order is no longer definite. We're currently prioritizing development on Exigents in order to keep splat books roughly on an annual schedule despite the scheduling slump that set in during Heirs development. But because I'll be able to work on Eight Directions with another developer while Vance is working on Exigents mechanics, it's not clear which book will get through development first.
