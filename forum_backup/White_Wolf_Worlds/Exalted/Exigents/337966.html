      When a God is in severe need it seeks access to the Exigence, a spiritual mecahnisism set up by the Sun which allows them to produce an Exalt at the great cost. Where 'great cost' can include the entire dissolution of the God in question. It's not something they do for giggles, which is where it gets it's name

                  The Flame of Exigence is handed down from the Unconcquered Sun from on high to a god that has petitioned for it and found worthy. It allows for gods to create their own Exigents. They are not known as the Exigent Exalted, just Exigents, or specifically their own kind of Exalted. Strawmaiden Janest is a Harvest Exalted, which is an Exigent.

                  The Exigence is something of a last resort, as it requires great sacrifice from the god/gods that use it.

They're going to hear rumors, myths, and legend. They'll listen to stories about a harvest god blessing a farm girl with the power to fend off a shambolic legion of Fae, about a deadly warrior-poet who draw divine might from a sword forged by one of the gods, about a thief who stole a god's most treasured possession and wagered it back for unimaginable power.
