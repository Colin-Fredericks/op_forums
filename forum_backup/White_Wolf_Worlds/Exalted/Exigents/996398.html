      Not to put down anyone's creative efforts...but I never really got the notion of playing a splat before its rules come out. I was honestly a bit baffled when people where so taken by Liminals that they were posting their Liminal PCs, and this was a couple of years ago! We still know sooo little about the new Exalts, even with the Dev's comments...far less than we knew about the original Exalted when the 1E Core came out, way back then.

      I guess I'm just afraid of a fan backlash when the mechanics are finally printed, and it turns out that the PCs they previously conceived of (and in some cases spent actual years invested in) are actually not possible. So now they have to either ignore the rules and go with their original concepts or play something unlike what they wanted.

      And the earliest we can expect to see any solid data on them is, what, still a year or so away, at least?

      Personally, I'm mostly just ignoring them, until we get some real info on them. I've come up with a few Exigent ideas, but I wouldn't try to play one yet. Having said all this, Exigents are probably the most interesting of the new ones I've heard about so far, so I'm also interested in hearing about people's actual play experiences...and if they will change their PCs when/if the actual book comes out with unexpected mechanics!

                          Anyone tap into playing Exigents yet?

                          I'm the GM for my group and we haven't tried hacking into any splats like this yet so I don't have much in the way of practical experience here. Because all the exigents at my table have been NPCs I haven't had to worry about player experience and balance. That being said, I'll give you my two cents.

                          The first thing I would want to know first is what splat are the other characters? I assume Solar but I don't know if it's a situation where you've made up rules for other splats that they're using. That would effect the power level we were balancing towards.

                          The second thing I would want to know is what's his planned play style going to be? Is he going to have a single sword/weapon that he's going to call many evocations from? Or is he going to demonstrate his proficiency at evocations by calling out evocations from lots of different artifacts that he his going to use interchangeably? That might effect what sorts of charms you draw up for him.

                          As it stands I have a couple of very rough ideas for charms he can buy:

                          1) Switch weapon as a reflexive action.
                          2) Switch armor as a reflexive action.
                          3) Evocation Slot (Type: Simple; Duration: Indefinite): this charm lets the exalt awaken a free evocation from any artifact in his possession. It may only be used on one artifact at a time. Repurchase upgrades this charm to awaken two evocations from the same artifact.
                          4) You could also have a series of charms with effects that differ based on the material of certain artifacts. This could be especially useful for charms trees that don't tie nicely to artifacts that are known for evocations (for instance, most social interactions aren't benefited by swords and medicine doesn't really benefit from armor).
                          5) Another series of charms that differs based on the tags of the weapon/armor being used.


                          I supposed one thing you could also say is that Evocations are this character's Supernal meaning that he won't be subject to essence limitations on the charms.

                          My Exalted Character Creation Tool may be downloaded here. The thread talking about the tool can be found here.
                          Exalted: Seats of Power is a homebrewed manse creation system.
                          I have a rewrite of the craft system which can be downloaded here.
                          I like the Mesoamerican look.
