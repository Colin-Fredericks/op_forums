    The Champion Cat - A quick non-human Exigent [PEACH]

    02-27-2016, 03:52 PM
    This is a quick-and-dirty cat exigent I wrote this evening, as a possible concept for an upcoming Solar game. The plan is to rely mostly on already written material and maybe expand it with some custom charms much later if it makes it into the game.

    The Champion of Felines
    Sometimes, when the need is desperate, call for divine fire to bless a champion. Sometimes, that champion is someone unexpected.
    When the Master of Seven Plagues and his vermin threatened the nation, his end came not from the Dragon-Blooded warrior who sought to make a name for herself, but from a cat. This feline defender had been given the given the gift of exigence, at the cost of eight of their patron's immortal lives. With cunning and ferocity, the Death Knight was brought low, and a new hero joined the tale of the Exalted.

    Character Creation
    Creating the Champion Cat is done in a similar fashion to a Solar Exalted with some exceptions:
    Step 1 - Instead of picking a caste, decide if your concept is best expressed as a normal cat, or a great cat.
    Step 2 - Only great cats can pick Physical Attributes as their primary and start with strength or stamina higher than 2.
    Step 3 - The Champion Cat has no supernal or caste abilities. Instead, pick 5 of the following: Athletics, Awareness, Brawl, Dodge, Investigation, Lore, Medicine, Occult, Presence, Stealth, Socialize and Survival. Then choose 3 additional abilities. These 8 are your favoured abilities. Divide only 20 points among these abilities.
    Note that some abilities have limited use in a feline form. Specifically, you can not learn Martial Arts.
    Step 4 - Cats and Great Cats already start with some merits. Pick only 5
    Step 5 - Pick only 10 charms. Please note that the Champion Cat have access to a limited selection of charms (See below)
    Step 6 - The Champion Cat suffers not from the Great Curse, but her original feline psychology combined with Essence Fever sometimes drive her to extremes. Design your limit break and trigger to reflect this.
    Step 7 - Additionally, you may spend spend 3 bonus points to learn a latent or magical ability (See Cat Traits as well as p 555)

    Excellencies
    The Champion Cat uses Attribute-based Excellencies instead of Ability-based. She starts with Excellencies in Dexterity, Wits and Manipulation and may learn the other six as if they were favoured charms. Her dice cap for these excellencies and all other charms is Attribute + Essence, but if she is pursuit of an intimacy and spends a willpower, in addition to the normal bonus, the cap is now Attribute + Intimacy level if higher.

    Cat Traits
    The Champion Cat knows the abilities and merits of either a Cat or a Great Cat. She can learn latent and magical abilities (spending their own essence) for a cost of 6 exp, if they make sense for her physique. She may also learn abilities that require a larger or stronger form, if they can assume one using Survival Charms (see Master of the Wilds). Also, she is cunning enough that she does not need to learn the Gambit ability.

    The physical form of the Champion Cat of course limits what actions can be taken, though some of those restricions can be overcome with stunts.

    Eyes of the Hunter
    The eyes of the cats see what hides in the shadow, and the Champion Cat sees even more. She may learn Awareness Charms but only those with minimum Essence of 1. She may ignore the ability minimums to learn these charms if she has equivalent Perception.

    Emissary of Cats
    The Champion Cat is tasked to speak on behalf of all felines. As such she is able to speak like a human, quickly learning the local tongue and those others she takes the time to master normally.
    All feline beasts and spirits understand the Champion Cat, even if she speaks a human tongue or communicate normally and she understands them. Non-spirit felines are always relatively friendly, as if she was using Friendship-With-Animals Approach and spirits are treated as if they had a minor intimacy of respect towards her.

    She may also learn Presence and Socialize charms (only with minimum Essence of 1) and may ignore the ability minimums to learn these charms if she has equivalent Manipulation (for Socialize) or Charisma (for Presence)

    Feline Grace and Ferocity
    Cats great and small move with grace and strike with ferocity when they hunt their prey. The Campion Cat is an exemplar with few peers. She may learn:
    Athletics charms (only with minimum Essence of 1)
    Dodge charms (only with minimum Essence of 1)
    Resistance charms (only Ox-Body Technique* and Body-Mending Meditation)
    *In addition to the normal bonus, she also gains the optional size-increase from Life of the Aurochs)
    She may also chose a single Martial Art which epitomises their feline fighting style, most likely Tiger Style (the ST might approve a different style instead). She learns the charms of this style as equivalent native Brawl Charms, with access to the Mastery Keyword.

    The Champion Cat may ignore the ability minimums to learn these charms if she has equivalent Dexterity (for Athletics and Dodge), Strength (for Tiger Style Brawl, ST will judge if a different style is also Strength-based) or Stamina (for Resistance) instead.

    Master of the Wilds
    The lions’ pride rule the savanna and lesser beast tremble as the tiger stalks the jungle. As a one who walks the wild places of Creation, the Champion Cat is at home in primal nature. She may learn Survival charms, with the exception of Element-Resisting Prana.
    In addition, if she has reached Essence 3 and either Survival or Stamina 5, she may learn a special version of Saga Beast Virtue, with no charm prerequisites, which affects herself instead and a similar special version of Deadly Predator Method with Saga Beast Virtue as it’s only prerequisite. If she fulfills the normal charm prerequisites, she need not repurchase these charms and can use them on a familiar as well.

    The Champion Cat may ignore the ability minimums to learn Survival charms if she has equivalent Charisma (for beastmaster charms), Perception (for tracking), Wits (for hiding tracks) or Stamina (for remaining charms) instead.

    Nine-lives Wisdom
    There are many secrets know to cats and the Champion Cat is no exception. She may learn:
    Investigation charms (only with minimum Essence of 1),
    Lore charms (but not Wyld-Dispelling Prana, Order-Affirming Blow or Sacred Relic Understanding)
    Medicine charms (only with minimum Essence of 2 or lower),
    Occult charms (only with minimum Essence of 1 and not Ghost-Eating Technique)

    The Champion Cat may ignore the ability minimums to learn these charms if she has equivalent Intelligence (for Lore, Medicine and Occult) or Wits (for Investigation) instead.
    She may also initiate into the Terrestrial Circle of Sorcery and the Shadowlands Circle of Necromancy, as well as learn charms with the Eclipse-keyword.

    tl;dr
    The Champion Cats works a lot like a Solar, but with a little fewer starting Ability points, merits and charms. The charm selection is also limited to Athletics, Awareness, Dodge, Investigation, Lore, Medicine, Occult, Presence, Stealth, Socialize and Survival Charms. In addition, most of these are limitied to Essence 1, with exceptions for Lore, Survival and Brawl/Martial Arts. The Champion Cat can however learn fitting animal Abilities (3 BP/ 6 EXP) as well as TCS, SCN and Eclipse-tagged Charms.

          Neat idea, but I'm very against the notion that some Exalts end chargen with a greater Charm baseline that others. Why the limits to Essence? My concern would be the character's capability would cap out very early.
          It's my way of trying to adjust the flavour of the charmset. In general, I was going for "super-cat" and Keen Sight Technique is great for that, in a way Eyes of the Unconquered Sun isn't. Solars and their charms tend to start in "superhuman" and end in Exemplar of [Ability] and that's a bit further than what I was going for. I might have been a bit too restrictive on some of them though.

          I think/hope that as long as the player is interested in one or more of Lore, Survival, Brawl/Martial Arts and Sorcery, there's enough to keep them going for a while at least. It might be my experience speaking, where games have not lasted that long.
