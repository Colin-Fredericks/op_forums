    Exigents for you (and me)!

    12-05-2013, 04:16 AM
    Exalted 3rd edition is just around the corner (fingers crossed), but even being generous we're not likely to see Exalted: The Exigents until the 4th quarter of 2014, and possibly not until 2015. That is kind of a bummer since, of the new material that we're getting in 3rd edition, Exigents are by far the thing I am most excited about.

    Still, I figure, why wait? Once the 3rd edition corebook is out, and we have the full ruleset for Exalted 3rd edition, I imagine it won't be impossible to start creating charms and designing Exigent Exalted.

    Unfortunately, creating an entire Exalted splat is difficult; designing and balancing charms isn't exactly easy. And with Exigents, you're looking at dozens, if not hundreds of different "splats" each with their own charms. Writing up a dozen Exigents, even if you just have a very basic charmset for each, to include in a chronicle is going to be a pretty major undertaking, one that I don't imagine most people want to bother with.

    That's where this thread comes in. I imagine that fan-made Exigents will be fairly popular in many Exalted games, even just as NPCs, simply because it will be a useful way to include Exigents without having to spend the time to write up charms or concept yourself.

    So this thread is designed for people to post their Exigent plans, so that other forum goers will know what to look forward to.

    What sort of Exigent do you plan to create? What sort of charms are you looking at? How many charms do you imagine writing? Even if you're just thinking of writing a half-dozen or so charms to give an idea of what the Exigent does, that's perfectly cool.

    I am currently working on an Exigent of Arilak, the goddess of the Eastern jungles and dinosaurs(!). Obviously without the mechanics here's not a lot I can do, but I have a fair number of charms laid out and prepped. I'm drawing inspiration from things like the Mokole, Jurassic Park and even that old fighting game, Primal Rage. Hopefully within a couple of months after the 3rd edition corebook gets released I'll have a functional Exigent with charmset to post on the forums.

    How about other people?

    AnubisXy

          The one I've done the most work for is the chosen of Alidrisi (original god. Domain: maps and cartographers) and I've discovered that writing Charm trees is HARD. I wanted to draw off the Infernal trees, how the curve around and interconnect, but that turned into too much work for too little reward, so I broke it down into a set of your basic branching trees and went from there. Some ideas I like:

          Gnat Dance: Depending on how the Onslaught mechanic works, this could go a couple different ways. 1) bleed off your Onslaught to pump up your Dodge temporarily. 2) Dodge while maintaining Onslaught.

          Frog Deceives Scorpion: Counter-unexpected. Burn some motes and willpower on an Unexpected attack to attempt to dodge. If you succeed, you get a counterattack.

          Perfected Pictograph Form: Allows drawing maps with a legend that's (magically) self-explanatory. Anyone can read this map, regardless of literacy or lack thereof. Leads to Elusive Context Interpolation.

          Elusive Context Interpolation: Makes a map legible by some target (specified on activation) and no one else. It's essentially Wind-Carried Words Technique in written form.

          Inscribing Anima: I got this idea from the label maker at work. The Exalt uses his anima to heat paper and singe it in specific patters, letting him put words and line drawings on it in a matter of moments.

          One Found, One Kept: I wanted this as a variation on Dread Panapoly of the Silent Wind and pals- that whole school of charms that lets you make stuff disappear and draw it back when you need it. This one can only banish things of a set Resources value (decided when you activate it the first time) for dirt-cheap. The trick, is, though, that you always need to leave something banished- pulling out the last item in your stockpile costs a ton of motes and some ahl.

                  I've been trying to cobble together a few bare bones frames to drape once 3E comes out. Most are probably going to be Quick NPCs with some utility charms and maybe a neat trick or two.

                  Talk about how the various Exigents can/can't pass on their Exaltation prompted me to start thinking of different lineage avenues. I'm trying to work up something for what I'm tentatively calling The Son of Dunes*, an Exigent of a long gone lieutenant of Ahlat. The Son would basically have a term limit of [x] years, after which a tournament is held. Winner of the tournament gets the Exigency passed on to them.

                  *Like Princes of the Earth, the Son can be anywhere on the gender spectrum.

                  For Charm structure, I'm going to steal the idea from (either one of the freelancers or a comment made on the old forums) and have a limited number of permanent charms, with the ability to spend XP to upgrade those permanent capabilities. Probably make it relatively simple and say that becoming The Son gets you a permanent Charm for each Attribute, which you then spend your time refining/upgrading.
