                                              1) Celestial Exaltation takes a human body and human soul and powers it with the power of the Incarne.

                                              3) Alchemical exaltation takes a lump a clay lined with magical materials, the soul of a hero, and a spark of power.
                                              4) Liminal exaltation takes a collection of dead corpses, some unknown parts (presumably some kind of human soul), and some unknown power of the Dark Mother

                                                  One of the core ideas is that the process of Exaltation requires a human soul. This is why Dragon Kings, animals and the like can't actually Exalt. They don't possess human souls. I would say the same is true of the Exigency - it is a specific process of Exaltation and to my understanding you couldn't use it to "Exalt" an animal, or a Dragon King or anything else that the other, more traditional Exaltations would not normally work on.


                                                  That said, it's possible that the Ata-beasts in Halta have human souls - it's never been made clear, but it seems like a reasonable thing for an ST to introduce if you want to make a cool animal Exalt. It seems rather unlikely however that san-beasts do as they are only partially sentient.

                                                  Further, it's very easy to imagine "Exalted-ish" methods by which a spirit could "empower" an animal in a way that makes it very similar to the process of Exaltation (giving it an Essence pool, access to Charms, and perhaps human-level intelligence). This would not be Exaltation but rather a different process that leads to a result which is nearly identical for all intents and purposes.

                                                  Of course, at the end of the day your ST is the one in charge and Exalted is his or her game. If she or he wants to allow you to play a Solar sloth then you can do that.
