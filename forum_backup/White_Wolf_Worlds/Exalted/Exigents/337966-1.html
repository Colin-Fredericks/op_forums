                            I'd say that there are individuals like Mnemon who are able to see past the same sorts of logical incongruities behind excluding Exigents as they do for Solars and Lunars, even if they still think the ultimate reason for that exclusion is sound. I'd also say that even for ones who don't recognise other Exalted, somebody who has the sort of power that an Exigent would, they're recognised as a measure above more "typical" kinds of blessing, even if they're not all seen as the same kinds of people.

                            There is that line about how Exigents who are in active rebellion against the Immaculate Order are viewed as Anathema, but since that also refers to various other beings, that appears to be a categorization for the sake of our recognition, rather than an internal one. To them, it's just people empowered by gods towards nefarious ends. Maybe it's even a matter of theological debate as to whether or not the ones who are opposing them are ontologically distinct from ones who don't, rather than having a moral or ideological difference as guided by personal agency.
