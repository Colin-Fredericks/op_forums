    Solar-Circle Exigent Sorcerer Patrons

    04-22-2023, 04:25 AM
    Solar-Circle Sorcerers are supposed to be vanishingly rare. For the published Exigent types, most are Terrestrial-Circle with a few tricks or exceptions to allow them access to Celestial-Circle, and no published ones can go higher. What would be a good patron for an Exigent who can natively access the Solar-Circle at the appropriate Essence-tier?

    I like the idea of the God of Geomancy, on the grounds of being in-tune with the Essence of all Creation, but it's far from perfect.

    Five Days Darkness would also be an interesting option, since he's Sol Invictus' shadow.

                      Originally posted by KymmetheSeventh View Post
                      Right, but the Chosen of one of the Incarnae totally have Solar Circle Sorcery. I suppose that's a good argument for severely broadening the limits of what kinds of Exigents can attain the Circle of Adamant, since it gaining it seems unrelated to the exact capabilities of their patron.
                      I wouldn't quite call it unrelated. The Unconquered Sun embodies a kind of boundless excellence, but still expressed in the manner of a god, where there's a necessary focus and structure to it. When that becomes a quality for Exalted humans, who are less immediately powerful but have a certain broader flexibility, that becomes something that can specifically reach certain heights or break certain boundaries.

                      There are Exigents who can uncommonly and unpredictably come out with power comparable to that of a Solar, but they're still tied to their own themes; Strawmaiden Janest brings a lot of force, but it's force backing up the legend of the empowered farm girl.

                      I think it would make the most sense for a comparable Exigent who can get in on Solar Circle Sorcery to come from a god whose purview is weird and esoteric, something kind of ethereal so that if they turn out to be the strongest type of Exigent it's pointed at something that can touch off of the strangest magic.

                      Although still ultimately a bit of a theoretical idea; there's always the issue that it would take Essence 5 to get.

                      Originally posted by KymmetheSeventh
                      We'll see if Paths of Brigid (if it ever gets written) changes the story of how Brigid learned sorcery at the feet of a demoness.
                      Mara is in the core book, she's still a sorcerous and can teach sorcery to others.

                      That in itself does not need to have anything to do with the power of the Yozis, and indeed, I think later Second Edition opting to recontextualize Brigid's narrative into an implication that sorcery was a trap laid for her by Yozis actually did the character of Mara a disservice. It took the heat of her own persona and qualities and handed it over to the Yozis, who had plenty enough as it was.

                      It also really weakened the origin of sorcery narrative because when people got it into their heads that it was something handed over by the Yozis through a proxy, by virtue of being a specifically Primordial thing, it made them forget the fact that there were several other important supernatural figures in that narrative teaching Brigid as well.

                                          My offerings would be (of varying quality and justification):
                                          • God of Geomancy, on the grounds of being in-tune with the Essence of all Creation
                                          • Five Days Darkness, on the grounds of being the Sun's shadow. For that matter, any of his children, be it Little Beam, Nysela, or Ardis-Iara.
                                          • A god of boundaries and/or transgressions, on the grounds of breaking established limitations (though that steps on Autocthon's toes a bit as a purview)
                                          • The god of Thaumaturgy (can't remember his name) on the grounds of Thaumaturgy being the weird (and underpowered for cost) glitches in the fabric of Creation
                                          • Wyld-tainted gods, on the grounds that the Wyld is the raw fabric ito which Creation is woven
                                          • Gods of creativity/creation, on the grounds that Sorcery is often a way to make something whole new (and a generous helping of 2e's "Sorcery is the source code of Creation)
                                          • Gods of turning points or paradigm shifts, on the grounds of Sorcery often pulling a rabbit out of the hat.
