    •"Basilisk Fractal" style art that cripples those who look at it.

    •Creating your own psychosis-sanctum that you can manipulate on the fly using your art skills.

    •Art that warps people's memories and perceptions through targeted delusions and hallucinations.

    •Art that turns the insane but normal into the sane but mutated, and vice-versa.

    •A charm which rides off another art charm and compels everyone who sees it to attempt to copy it as best they are able, with the better made ones replicating all the original enchantments.

    •Art that sucks heath levels from those in its presence, and which may transfer the stolen levels to its maker with a touch.

    •Art that temporarily inverts the intimacies of its audience, compelling them to act against all they love and aid everyone they hate.

    •Art that can control hungry ghosts, making them love/obey the user.

    •Art that destroys the basic coherency of Raksha and other Wyld Things, killing or mutilating them.

    •Art that opens gateways between worlds, allowing demons and ghosts and fey to walk through the cracks.

    Hope that helps!

            Lan-Darha's still taking shape in my mind.

            Currently my mental image of her resembles common depictions of Hel with her left side giving way to something that's invariably creepy yet undeniably creative that probably changes from appearance to appearance. So maybe one time she has exposed bone that's been lovingly carved with poetry only to later replace it with a golden wire sculpture of exposed musculature.

            Personality wise she seems to be shaping up as something of a dark muse who’ll instigate terrible things by telling the wrong people that their goals are just within their reach. I don’t think she considers madness as inherently different from any other kind of suffering an artist might undergo for the sake of pursuing their craft.

            It’s also worth noting that she sees value in all art hence why she’s willing to aid exploited artists and I could easily envision see her seeking retribution upon Immaculate Monks for destroying a graven image.

            Finally, I’m trying to resist drawing obvious connections to Translucent Alabaster of RoGD1 besides the fact that they’re both art goddesses who may or may not be aware that the other exists.

                This strikes me as pretty important.
