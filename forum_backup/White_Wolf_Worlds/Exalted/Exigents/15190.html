    Weird Exigent Ideas

    11-21-2013, 03:11 AM
    So last night Hucksters came up in conversation on IRC (credit to Flare for getting the ball rolling with this).
    For those who've aren't familiar with the RPG Deadlands they're an interesting flavour of spellcasters who draw their power from playing games of Poker with evil spirits. If they win a hand then they get to use the spirit's essence to power a spell and the rules use a deck of playing cards. It's certainly stylistic enough to warrant it's own rules and charmset. However, having to crack out some playing cards when everyone else is just spending essence and rolling dice might be a little too convoluted while simply using poker hands as an aesthetic doesn't seem to be making the most of the idea.

    Thoughts? Anyone else have weird ideas they want to share?

      Well... first of all, I would not make them Exigents I think.Not if you want to stay true to the complete concept that is. Hucksters could fit the Exalted setting very well as mortals who make deals with malevolent spirits, literally the way they do in Deadlands. You have these spirits granting them the use of powers through blessings etc.but it comes at a price. The characters make deals with these spirits through gambles, and every time they lose the gamble the spirit takes a bit of their life or sanity. Hucksters are an archetype that doesn't really befit the Exalted, it is power at a great personal risk/cost for the user, where Exaltation is power at a great cost for the benefactor.

      Since you're in houserule land with this one, I would simply go with putting the deck of cards on the table and convert the Deal with the Devil table for appropriate use in Exalted. The card mechanic is cool enough.

                                      Originally posted by Gonzo View Post
                                      And that's exactly my point. Given that they can't "appropiately" be there. I was imagining a way to include "what makes them good" by other means.
                                      It's likely an attempt to stick firmly to the definition of "Exigent." I've seen the misconception flying around that "Exigent" is a synonym for "Custom Exalt," when the former is a subset of the latter created through a particular method. Both should be seen as just as legitimate and valid. I don't even personally have a problem if people want to use "Exigent" as an informal term for custom Exalts, as long as they know the distinction. It's just that, particularly at present with the only clarifications existing in EX3 Team comments scattered hither and yon (or compiled in the What We Know Wiki and Nishkriya, neither of which is as known as it is valuable), I think it's a worthwhile clarification to make, just in case.

                                      As for your idea, it could be cool! I'm not sure it would necessarily appear in a book,* but it could be fertile ground for homebrew. If there's a good idea for a niche that the other 6 castes don't presently fill, could be very neat indeed.
