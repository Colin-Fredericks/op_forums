                What danelsan and JimB said certainly flies. Additionally or as an alternative, he could consider doing so and take a different view. Depending on the perspective he's adopted, I could actually see a case for the Sun believing that denying other gods the creation of their own Exalted would be a kindness. Incredible arrogance and egotism, go!

                "I am the highest of the high. I picked only the greatest of heroes to be my champions. They were the best that humanity had to offer. In the end, they bitterly disappointed me beyond all measure. Were it within me to call back this divine blaze, I would have it done in a heartbeat. At best, I can withhold sparks. I can save those who would damn themselves in fires of their own. And so I shall. If the finest of humankind I Chose are to fall as mine have, then what chance do those less immaculate than myself stand of Choosing better? No. If gods are to create new Exalted champions, I will raise no hand in aid. I shall have no part in repeating these mistakes."

                The Sun's got his gaze back on Creation -- with more than implication that the Solars' return owes something to that.* It would hew together that something changed his mind, made him trust and try again, both with his own Chosen and granting the Exigence to gods he finds worthy. Again, just theorizing, here. But if so, I'd be interested in whatever turned his head. Should we ever find out.

                * This bit from the RPG.net thread:
                Originally posted by "Ask the Developers" RPG.Net thread
                (on the significance of the Sun's returned attention to Creation)
                Ardynor:
                Oh really.
                Did he godspear Thorns yet? Did he erase any other shadowlands? Did he beat back the Wyld?
                No. He is sitting as impassively in heaven as he was. The solars are cycling and doing what they want without asking him. Their massed re-emergence has nothing at all to do with him.

                How at all is he different from back when he turned away from the world?

                John:
                The Exalted rule Creation. The Unconquered Sun sent back the Solars. The answer is right in front of you. The return of the Solars means more than everything you just suggested.

                “Their massed re-emergence has nothing at all to do with him.” 2e was outlined to specifically contradict this assertion, and every single writer on the line until Glories ignored it. Many did the 180 degree opposite, which is why you see some dissonance in what we are saying as to what you have seen.
