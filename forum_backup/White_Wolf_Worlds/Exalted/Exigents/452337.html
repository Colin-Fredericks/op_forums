    Exigents, Martial Arts, and Sorcery

    03-22-2015, 03:29 AM
    We know that the build-your-own Exalted will have variable power levels, and some might even match the Celestial Exalted in puissance, so will they have access to the highest levels of martial arts or sorcery?

    Do you think Exigents should be able to summon Third Circle Demons? Master the March of Charcoal Spiders? Could there be pockets of civilization with working First Age wonders due to an Exigent maintaining them?

    This seems to break an fundamental assumption of the setting: that the returning Solars are bring back with them miracles and wonders that have been mostly absent since the Usurpation. At the same time, we know that there have been a handful of Solars popping up since then. Is "for the last thousand years, a handful of Exigents have been able to do X" change the setting any more than "for the last thousand years, a handful of Solars have been able to do X?"

    As good as the Wyld Hunt was under the Empress, I can't believe that not one Solar lived long enough to cast an Adamant Circle spell. Creation is a big place, so maybe the existence of a dozen Exigents who can reach these heights is fine. After all, while they might have Celestial/Solar/Sidereal tier abilities in one area, I doubt they'll have it in all of them.

    What do you think?

    What should the limits of Exigent charms/sorcery/MA/crafting be... if any?

      Well, like you said, if a single Exigent is occasional able to make it, in a single avenue. to Solar tier power (or sidereal MA), then you've got a dangerous creature, but they'll likely only be able to reach such puissance within a single, defined avenue.           I was under the impression that Exigents are pretty damn rare. Further each Exigent is totally different - they're more of a "wild card" Exalt in that any Exigent could possibly do anything.

          It's certainly possible that there are Exigents who are capable of casting Adamant Sorcery or using Sidereal Martial Arts, but they would probably be quite rare even among other Exigents.

          I think ultimately the best comparison for Exigents would be something like mutants in Marvel. Some mutants are incredibly powerful, others are really weak. Trying to classify what a Marvel Mutant should be able to do is kind of a lost cause because their power level and capabilities are so incredibly broad - for some their "power" is to have a third eye that doesn't work, while others have the power to completely reshape the universe.

          So I think, when the question comes up, "Should Exigents be able to do X," the answer should be, "Yes," but with the understanding that "Exigent" is sort of a catch-all term for ten million different potential types of Exalted, so while some could do X, many others might not be able to.

                                  From what I understand, there was a few Exigents, but not many. Recently there's been a big rise in the number of them.

                                  Anyway, I think that it perhaps could be conceptually possible, but only for really powerful ones within the particular limits.
                                  Like, perhaps the Exigent of Amoth City-Smiter could cast Rain of Doom, but only on cities.
                                  I don't really see much in the way of SMA being relevant, honestly, because they're just too esoteric.
                                  Like, you might say that the Exigent of the goddess of spiders should be able to use Charcoal March of Spiders, but actually that doesn't fit as well as straight spider powers.

                                  Thinking about it though, I wonder if this is looking at it the wrong way.
                                  Except for "Exigent of the God of Martial Arts" or "Exigent of the God of Sorcery" I think really martial arts and sorcery should be outside Exigent's themes.
                                  What I mean is, say you're an Exalt of the God of Drunkeness. You don't learn Drunken Master style, you have your own native charms powered by drunkenness. You learn Snake style in case you need to fight sober. You learn sorcery so you can turn your skin to bronze, conjure a cloud to carry you, summon and bind elementals to your will, and so on, because those are useful powers that you cannot learn as native charms because they're nothing to do with being drunk.
                                  Just like a Lunar or Abyssal might learn martial arts or sorcery to get things outside of their theme.

                                  So, except for the "Exigent of the God of Martial Arts" and the "Exigent of the God of Sorcery", maybe Exigents should just be stuck with regular martial arts and terrestrial or celestial sorcery (depending on their power level). Because the point of these things is that they're not really related to your themes that much.

                                      I feel like the flexibility of the sorcerous project system means that, say you have an Exigent who it would be cool for them to have once performed magic beyond Celestial Circle Sorcery, they don't necessarily face the quandary of having to have Solar Circle Sorcery on tap just so that they can perform that singular miracle.                                       So it would be a really extreme circumstance in which an Exigent really *needs* SCS as part of their character.

                                      Having a system of actions to support sorcerous projects / workings also allows some differentiation for Exalted in their sorcery beyond the simple hierarchy - maybe the Exigent of the Spider Goddess builds a sorcery through actions that are akin in some ways to a spider building a web or she can build webs (of a metaphorical sort) of connections and social influence which capture what she needs to fuel her sorcerous workings (without forcing the analogy too much)?
