    Exigence grabs

    08-28-2017, 02:26 AM
    We know exigence can be granted to various gods, and that it can be stolen, or ownership of a particular exigence transferred, once in circulation.

    So would demons and or the agents of the death lords make an effort to gain control of such things, or the gods who hold them? Would the Sidereals? Or maybe even some of the dragon blooded? Could the Yozi use stolen exigence to create new infernals, or something akin to them?

    It seems to me that the existence of the Exigence creates a whole new battlefield in creation.

                              Originally posted by John Morke
                              As some people have begun to speculate, the Black Market Exaltations refer to Exigents made illicitly, somehow bypassing the Unconquered Sun's erm firewall, so to speak.

                              In the most literal terms, there were raw Exaltations granted by the Unconquered Sun which were then not used by the petitioner, but instead passed into the world of spirits, where it cycled the realms of gods and men until a bidder was finally able to trigger it.

                              A "dirty bomb" (not actual game term) Exaltation is a similar concept, only the Exaltation has passed through the hands of multiple spirits who have modified the power inside. What would be the point of foils to a splat that has the unifying feature of incredible diversity?

                                                  A Not Quite Simple Soul: I went back and reread your posts in this thread, and as far as I can tell, you have two positions:

                                                  A: "Exigents are a potent tool, and powerful entities might attempt to suborn lesser gods, or wait and suborn the Exigents themselves after Exaltation, in order to use them for their own purposes." I don't think anyone disagrees with this. This sort of thing is true throughout the setting. Heck, out in Skullstone there is a Solar working loyally for a Deathlord. Everyone wants them some Exalted servants.

                                                  B: "Exigents are a potent tool, and there exist other messed-with Exaltations in the game, so powerful entities might mess with a granted Exigence, so we might see something like the Yozis using stolen Exigence." This is what people are jumping on and don't want to see, because the freeform nature of Exigents means that this can thematically crowd out all of those entities' own champions. That's what Tiresias meant about reducing other Exalts to the Exigence: the metanarrative reason Exigents are restricted to gods is so that Exigents can reflect the themes of the thousands of little gods in Creation and open the door to having them and their portfolios matter to the game and setting, and if Deathlords or demons or whoever metaphysically mess with the Exigence itself, it shoves the little gods out of the spotlight designed for them and also makes the eventual Exalted servants of whoever messed with it (Abyssals, Infernals, etc.) less special and unique.
