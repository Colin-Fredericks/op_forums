              Sounds like fun, let's do it.

              Thousand Petals on the Water, Exigent Scavenger Lord

              Aspiring Scavanger Lords were never rare in the East, but those that succeeded and survived the perils of buried monsters and deathtrap tombs were remarkable individuals. It was these few that drew the gaze of Kemrok, God of Things Forgotten and Remembered. The Scavanger Lords recovered treasures lost to time and secrets thought buried for good, but for all the advances they made in the name of avarice and bounty, more precious lore slipped away every year. Kemrok resolved to empower a champion to uncover the greatest treasures of the lost age.

              Thousand Petals on the Water was not the most successful of the Scavanger Lords, nor the most clever, nor the strongest, but Kemrok saw a potential in the fisherman’s daughter, a love for her work that extended beyond the desire for reward or fame. He appeared before her in a dream and made his offer, and she accepted without hesitation.

              Since then, she has braved the dread tomb of Agnath the Silverhorn, returned the five Hearthstones of Ix the Conquerer to still the rage of their volcano manse and narrowly escaped run-ins with the Outcaste bandit prince Grey Wave and the sorceress Xaiotur. Though Petals has been Exalted barely a year, her deeds are fast becoming legend.

              Essence:2; Willpower:6; Join Battle:5 dice (+4 for 4m, +6 for 6m)
              Personal Motes:14; Peripheral Motes:33
              Health Levels:-0/-1x3/-2x4/-4/Incap.

              Attributes
              Strength 2, Dexterity 4, Stamina 3, Charisma 2, Manipulation 3, Appearance 3, Perception 4, Intelligence 2, Wits 4

              Abilities
              Athletics 3, Awareness 4, Brawl 2, Bureaucracy 3 (Haggling), Craft 4 (Artifact Repairs), Dodge 2, Investigation 3, Larceny 4 (Escaping Trouble), Linguistics 4 (Translations), Lore 5, Melee 4 (Whip), Occult 3, Stealth 2, Survival 3, Thrown 3

              Combat
              Attack (Raintontail): 14 dice (+6 for 6m; damage 10/2)
              Attack (Grapple): 9 dice (+6 for 6m; 4 dice to control, +4 for 4m)
              Combat Movement: 7 dice (+4 for 4m, +6 for 6m)
              Evasion 3 (+2 for 4m), Parry 6 (+3 for 6m)
              Soak/Hardness: 6/0 (Stylish jacket)

              Powers
              Excellencies: Petals may add up to (Attribute) dice to an action with her Excellency. If she is in a place of forgotten knowledge like an ancient tomb, manse or library, or if the roll is made involving an object recovered from such a place, such as studying rubbings recovered from a sunken temple or attacking with Raitontail, she can add (Attribute + Essence) dice.

              Offensive Charms
              Flying Raiton Strike (3m, 1i or 6m, 2i; Supplemental; Instant; Dual): Petals strikes with her prized moonsilver dire lash Raitontail, which uncoils well beyond its normal reach. For 3m, 1i it strikes out to Short Range, and for 6m, 2i it strikes out to Medium Range. If Petals successfully grapples at range she must drag her opponents to Close range using Restrain and Drag actions before performing other grapple actions. Outside of attacking, Petals can use her extending weapon to scale walls or swing across chasms.

              Defensive Charms
              Escalating Withdrawal Stratagem (1wp; Reflexive; One Scene): Petals can use this Charm upon unexpectedly joining battle or otherwise facing an unexpected threat like a surprise deathtrap. She rolls her (Wits + Athletics) with Essence bonus successes. She gains a pool of bonus dice equal to the successes on the roll that she may apply to any roll in the following scene that is used to withdraw from combat or escape unharmed. If she is in a situation where she can apply her expanded excellency, the pool instead grants bonus successes. Once per scene, but can be reset if the scene fundamentally changes with another surprise threat, such as being ambushed by Grey Wave's thugs while dodging poisoned spikes shooting from the walls.

              Knowledge Charms:
              Buried Tablet Panglossia (5m; Reflexive; One Scene; Mute): For the remainder of the scene, Petals intuitively understands and can speak any strange and obscure tongue no longer in common usage, provided she comes into contact with some source of it, such as an inscription or inquisitive guardian spirit.

              Ten Fathom Mind Enlightenment (5m, 1wp; Simple; Instant): Petals may make a read intentions roll against an ancient structure or object by spending an action analysing it carefully. A successful roll reveals some relevant fact about the object or its history. Once per scene, she can make a stunt that gains some advantage from this information for (Essence) additional non-Charm bonus dice. Once per story, Petals can use this Charm on a newly unearthed artifact that she has attuned to unlock an Evocation.

              Utility Charms:
              Bottomless Pockets Treasury (6m, 1wp; Simple; Instant; Mute): The wonders that have run through Petals’ hands would shame the galleries of many great princes. At any time, she usually has some small wonder or part-functioning artifact about her person. Using this Charm, she can reveal her possession of a magical treasure useful to the current situation, with a power no greater than Artifact 2 or a Standard Hearthstone. The treasure remains usable until the end of the current story, after which Petals loses it, gambles it away or sells it, or else it breaks or runs out of power. Once per story.

                              Crumplepunch has some great ideas there. I'd like to toss in a few more theoretically in line with the same character:

                              Perception:
                              Thousand-Generation Eyes(5m (+1wp). Simple, One Scene)
                              Her eyes clouding over and turning pure jade-white, Petals broadens her gaze from the Now to the Then; figures appear in shadows and outlines all around her, showing people and buildings and objects that are not there now, but might have been, once. This charm supplements Investigation and Awareness actions with double 9's, and allows her to see dematerialized beings, for whom time has no meaning. If she is in a location that would enhance her excellency, or her awareness is directed towards an object that would do the same, she may increase the benefits of this charm to double 8's for a willpower point, but she suffers from a -2 penalty to her pools to interact with the present for the rest of the scene, as well as a -1 to her static values.

                              Combat:
                              Unearthing the Skin(No Cost, Permanent)
                              This charm grants Petals access to the Expose gambit, with a difficulty of 3. Successfully executing this gambit reduces the opponent's soak from armor by 2 for the rest of the scene; armor reduced to 0 is completely destroyed (if mundane) and grants no Hardness bonus. Artifact armor is not destroyed, but requires a scene to be reassembled. This gambit may also be employed against garments with a negligible soak rating, with predictable results. Petals has done exactly this, both for purposes of seduction and humiliation.

                              Social:
                              Exhumed Memories Interview(6m, Simple, One Scene, Mute)
                              The exalt must know the name of the dead to whom she speaks, and addresses them by it with an Instill action. If she holds or presents an object to which the deceased holds an intimacy (such as a fetter), then the shade takes an appropriate level of penalty to their Resolve regardless of context. If the roll is successful, the ghost is treated as having a minor Principle towards maintaining a peaceful conversation with the Exalt until the end of the scene or until they part company, whichever comes first. Even Hungry Ghosts may be held at bay with this charm; although they remain dangerously easy to provoke and are likely to resume hostilities as soon as they are able, they will speak with Petals to the best of their cognition. During such a time the targets of this charm are likely to forget that they are in fact dead, and respond as though they were still alive.

                                                  This is an Exigent of a God of Archaeology? For an Exigent of Archeology, off the top of my head I'd imagine him to have Charms that could be based around...

                                                  - Sensing and liberating stolen wealth ("This belongs in a museum"... not that Creation has those, but, well, perhaps it has the ideal or at least "This belongs in a savant's collection"!).

                                                  - Inspiring others through knowledge and evocation of the past (Presence) and through your commitment to the ideal that archeology represents.

                                                  - Knowing about who people are, and how they lived through their material culture (sort of Investigation, but also Anthropology and thus Socialise).

                                                  - Taking on the essence of archaeological artefacts into himself (akin to reverse Evocation, in a sense, where rather than him inspiring the artifact, it inspires qualities in him).

                                                  - Charms themed around understanding and avoiding deadfalls and traps... while setting hostile rivals up to get caught by them.

                                                  - Stealth Charms based on taking the essence of hidden ruins and lost wonders onto yourself (you become hidden, like the lost treasures you seek and guard)

                                                  - More tenuous, but casting others into the darkness and obscurity of ruins (socialise for social ruin, physical attack abilities for physical ruin?).

                                                  - Medicine Charms that can restore the function / appearance of youth, for the old.

                                                  - Posing baffling, confusing or maddening riddles, codes and plays of speech, drawing on the essence of uninterpretable archaeological enigma and mystery ("What song the sirens sang, or what name Achilles assumed when hid himself among women, though puzzling questions, are not beyond all conjecture. What time the persons of these ossuaries entered the famous nations of the dead, and slept with princes and counsellors, might admit a wide solution. But who were the proprietaries of these bones, or what bodies these ashes made up, were a question above antiquarianism".)

                                                  - Inventing or improvising technologies based on direct knowledge of the past, or to aid excavation.

                                                  - At high essence, bringing the past literally alive into a scene around him, and in a literal sense casting people around him into the personas and styles of ancient nations. Making the past live again. At lower essence, he can assume the mind and persona of some ancient king or dignitary or notable person, and their skills, through knowledge of their artefacts.

                                                  This is sort of pulp archeologist biased though - so sort of more Exigent of Doctor Jones. You'd change it if you wanted to be the Exigent of Time Team (You'd probably get a lot more Charms about setting up your "site", being able to protect it for damage and environmental insult and then understanding it through careful excavation, both for literal archeology sites and metaphorical ones).
