        I'm not a fan of the decision to use the array system for Attributes, without a called-out option to assign dots freely. It's fine for Abilities, simply because there are more of those.
        If I run an XS game, I'm going to houserule the shit out of that.

        Also, the different Liminal milestones don't really seem like they have parity.

        Soil and Blood Aspects get unique, flavourful ones, while the Marrow Aspect gets a dry-but functional one. The Breath milestone, though, doesn't seem like it has anything to do with their fluff (contemplative, passive = punish the wicked?), and the Flesh milestone is just a more restrictive Dusk one (the Dusk Caste only has to engage in combat, while the Flesh has to win).

        Decomposition reduces the Liminal to a shambling corpse or hungry ghost within one lunar month. They lose another Intimacy per week, and can no longer use Undying. They also decompose cosmetically, per the player’s aesthetic choice. The Liminal can still anchor her Thread of Life to a mortal within this time. If she does, decomposition stops and she heals. Liminals of Essence 3+ may anchor themselves to an Exalt within their Circle, or to another powerful entity.
        It's kind of unclear, but I think this says you can come back from decomposition even after one month has passed.
        Or, at least, I hope it does.

                I have to say, overall, I'm disappointed.
                Character Creation I am mostly very happy with, but I guess that's the easiest part to house-rule anyway.
                Some of the powers I like and some I don't, but that's kind of subjective. I can play something I like, others can play something they like.
                But for a system that's supposed to be simpler and more elegant, well tbh a lot of the anima powers don't seem much simpler than the ones in 3rd ed. Actually some are more complex.
                The Milestone system also ends up being more complex than a normal XP system. There's 4 types of milestones that you get at different, variable times, some are in a pool you fill up, two are swappable anyway. It feels more like an alternate system for standard Ex3 than a pared-down, simpler system.

                I thought Essence might be too simple for me, I didn't want something like FATE, but so far it doesn't really seem simpler or more elegant. It is in parts of course, but others are not.

                Hopefully the rules and charms will do the heavy lifting of simplification.

                                                Its a different ruleset, though it's the 3rd edition setting, so all 3rd edition books present and future will continue to be useful if you're running on the Essence rules. As far as we know there isn't currently a formal translation guide for characters, but it really shouldn't be hard to write up an equivalent character with the other ruleset.

                                                Currently we don't know about anything that will add more Essence rules beyond the Essence core, and the Companion that is being built with stretchgoals - hence it being good that we are getting all 10 canon Exalted types up front in Essence. The answer when it was asked about future cool charms in 3E books without Essence equivalents getting Essence translations was "Essence Charms are so easy to write up, you'll be able to do it yourself". But it would not surprise me, if we see some future sidebars and such in later 3E books with "here's this thing's Essence stats" or "Here's how to use it in Essence".
