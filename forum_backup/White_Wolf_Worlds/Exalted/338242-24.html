                        Originally posted by Sunder the Gold View Post
                        After what happened? Alchemicals treated Attributes the same way in Second Edition as they did in First. This impacted Lunars insofar as Lunars got even ONE out-of-Caste Attribute to favor.

                        The reasoning behind "just one" is that Lunars have a totally different relationship with their Charms than Alchemicals do, what with multiple-Attribute progression trees (especially considering submodules) and the fact that they can't have all Charms loaded at once.
                        The mechanical execution of Alchemical Charms was the smoothest functioning Charm set of them all, largely because you didn't have to hyper-calculate which Attributes you wanted to favor for maximum efficiency. You could almost pick any random Caste and three random Favored Attributes and still run the exact same Charms. I usually had at least one Favored Attribute to spend on something entirely concept related.

                        Lunars may not learn Charms the same way, but just looking at why the Alchemical set worked well and the Lunar set worked poorly would point at the Lunar set having too many constraints, too little flexibility, and too little support for anything other than "pretty kitties" and "RAWR! *SMASH*" builds. Adding one more Favored Attribute would help with at least two of the three problems.

                                            01-12-2015, 07:16 PM
                                            -Get rid of the Lunar's inferiority complex. SERIOUSLY. The entire Lunar splatbook felt like a diatribe about how much the Lunars suck and everyone else was cooler. That should NEVER be the case for the exalt's OWN SPLATBOOK. Give us a good reason to play a Lunar over another Exalt type, don't spend the whole time talking about all these cooler exalts we could be playing.

                                            -Most importantly, mechanics that don't suck. Yeah, I know, should be obvious. But it's apparently not.

                                            -Give Lunars the ability to alter their own strengths on the fly. This is something that makes sense for Lunars since they're shapeshifters, so I'm kind of surprised it's not there: charms that allow the Lunar to switch up their attribute points. Maybe make it so they can only alter their caste and favored abilities?

                                            -Lunar's blood as a charm component. In particular, I'm thinking about creating beastmen from the Lunar's blood. Picture it: under the full moon in a ruined temple, a cult meets to pray to their patron diety. At the height of their ceremony, the candles suddenly blow out, leaving only a shaft of moonlight from the ruined roof. From the moonlight itself steps a massive beast, with the figure of a woman but the mask and tail of a raccoon. Before her faithful she holds out a cup crafted from the moonlight, filled to the brim with her blood. Each member of the congregation takes a sip, and they are transformed.

                                            ... it's a work in progress, I'm just trying to reconcile how any Celestial with a brain decided needing an entire year of bestiality to create an army was the most efficient solution.

                                            -If any Lunar at any point says some variation of "Everywhere you are weak, I am strong." or "If I cannot outfight them, I will outsmart them. If I cannot outsmart them, I will outfight them!" I will be happy.

                                            -Sublime Danger's perspective on the Sword Sage comic's events. Seriously, so many implications from that story.

                                            -One of the few things I liked about the 2e book was the idea that the Lunars were Creation's shield wall against the Wyld, its defense against everything that didn't exist. Yes, I know the Fae aren't as big of a threat anymore and there's not as much need for someone to defend the borders of creation, but it was still a cool concept that I was thoroughly intrigued by. I'd like it to be a bigger thing in the Lunar splatbook, even if only as a fluff thing rather than as a player thing. Maybe an endgame where the Lunar gains enough power to fight alone at the edge of the Wyld, or offering this as an explanation for why so many lunars were away from their solar mates at the time of the usurpation.
