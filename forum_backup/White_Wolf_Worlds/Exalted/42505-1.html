                        I would probably just make Ma-Ha-Suchi a mink totem at this point and laugh maniacally at the forum reaction it generates.

                        That aside, I read the OP yesterday during game time and was like "that's terrible, but I don't have time to write a scathing rebuke."

                        So I ended up sleeping on it and now I'm more confused than displeased. What could you possibly hope to gain from dissolving the Lunar castes?

                        Is it really a big damn deal that your charming sex machine happens to be a Full Moon? (It is not.) Does it matter that your badass exorcist-monk monster hunter is actually a No Moon? (No.) What about the geeky crafter who makes moonsilver roses he secretly delivers to his love interest at night being a Changing Moon? (Still not actually a problem.)

                        So do you have a problem with the mechanical limitations inherent to the current Caste paradigm? I think so. Which further perplexes me because you could have simply and more cogently presented your argument with "Hey can we please have something that closely mirrors Peter Schaefer's original Lunar chargen be the official rules for Ex3 so there's a bit more flexibility in Lunar archetypes?" Which, by the way, do want. 2 + 2 is pretty ideal, and nothing stops people who want all three from getting them.

                        Trying to tie down mechanics to something as fungible as what your totem animal happens to be is, in a word, anserine. I don't even think totem spirits are something that need to make the transition to Ex3, bluntly. Does it significantly change your character concept if your hero's favored alternate form is no longer totemic to their being? It shouldn't.
