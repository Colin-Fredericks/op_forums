        Originally posted by Robert Vance View Post
        Essence can't be measured as an energy.
        Yeah the thing to consider is that essence and motes don't need to be a scientific measurement or unit on which Creation operates, the only reason they need to have a numerical value applied to them is that they are one of the resourced based balances of the game not the world (along with resources things like willpower and health). A Dawn doesn't think "I only have 10 motes left that means I can Iron Whirlwind once and apply Hungry Tiger to each attack", the actual though process is "I'm being pushed to my limits, I need to make this attack count, it's the last chance I have".

        By the same token, sure there may be immense applications of power in Creation that could use a "Kilomote" of essence, but a Sorcerer or Primordial isn't measuring out the the exact number of motes with a Orichalcum Multimeter, they are going to approach the situation with a much more abstract view, going with the amount of essence that FEELS right.

                            This isn't a "God is not the River" issue.

                            Fate is important, and it can have an effect on people. If someone is fated to not fall in love, while it is possible that they will (because Fate can always be glitched and other gods/Exalted, etc can use their powers to change things), it's terribly unlikely. Likewise if someone is fated to fall in love with another person, then they probably will (barring some outside interference).

                            Otherwise, if none of that matters, and it's just "descriptive" rather than "prescriptive" then the entire Bureau of Fate is pointless, and that isn't much fun for Sidereal players. Why should Players be too worried about planning Fate and running around fixing things when they go wrong, if the only issue is that someone's paperwork ended up being a little off?

                            Fate does matter, it has an effect on things, and when it gets messed up, it's important for Creation that things be put back on track, not just because it messed up some gods paperwork.

                            *EDIT*

                            I mean, when Prince Joe peeks out of his carriage and falls in love with the commoner walking across the street, that isn't really spiritual or a mystery or anything like that. To him it probably is, yes. But we, as readers, know it's something that was probably approved by a planning committee up in Yu-Shan several years ago, and only after they had weighed out the pro's and con's to determine if it's better for the future of Creation or not.

                            And then, when all is said and done, maybe they had to send a Sidereal agent to make sure that the prince fell in love with that commoner and not the Fair Folk next to her that was trying to tempt him.

                            Some of the things that other settings treat a mysterious, Exalted treats differently. And I have always found that to be something that makes Exalted kind of cool.

                                    I was thinking more along the lines of Soulfire Crystals, or Lightning Boxes or other artifacts that can store "X" number of motes.                                     I will agree though that charms are a very different beast, especially from different Exalted. Some Exalted are "more efficient" at using Essence than others, and some charms are more efficient than other charms, which is why you can have a similar effect cost radically different amounts of energy.
