        So, one minor piece of feedback; the description of Kalara's crucifixion leans perhaps a little too heavily on the audience's knowledge of what crucifixion is, and in particular the Christian image of crucifixion. A paragraph of Karala reflecting on her fate, and/or some earlier descriptions in the story of crucified criminals, would go a long ways towards addressing this.

        I mean, there's a lot to be said for leaving things to the audience's imagination, but I actually had to read the section twice to be certain that Kalara *was* being crucified- the term is never event explicitly used in the chapter about her crucifixion, and

        Originally posted by Chapter 7
        I shook my head again, no. I had no more tears left. No. I would spend my last moments satisfied in my final, tiny rebellion. I think I smiled then as the executioner drew his arm back to make the fateful strike…
        made me think Kalara was literally on the verge of being killed, whereas crucifixion is an especially horrifying punishment for the time it takes to kill the victims.

        (Note: Critique coming from a place of admiration for the overall work!)

            Hey there! Thanks for the feedback, which I totally encourage any and all readers to leave here!

            My intention with that particular description was that she knew when that 2nd stake went in, that she was in for a long and gruesome experience whose one and only outcome was a painful death. Even if it happens later, it was inevitable. The 'fateful strike' was referring to the driving of the final stake that would prevent her from being mercy killed if she confessed, an act whose finality meant she really and truly was going to suffer and die awfully.

            I kept the experience more focused on her internalization of it, so she wouldn't be analyzing the entire process (nailed to wood beams, hoisted up to suffer, eventually killed by broken legs that cause asphyxiation), BUT I think a clever way to address this that doesn't throw people out of the turmoil might be to have her remember having seen other crucifixion victims in the same square upon her first arrival (when she first realizes where her execution is taking place) and go into more description there.

            Thanks again! I think you're right this scene needs some clarification and I will be editing it when I get the chance.

                                            So it's National Novel Writing Month and I've decided to participate this month by writing a sequel to The Uncrucified! Feel free to keep up with the unedited NaNoWriMo excerpts over at my writing tumblr. You can also add me as a writing buddy over at my NaNoWriMo profile. I'm excited to get back to writing about this character, as she's really captured my muse! Here's a little tidbit to tease you guys for the sequel:
                                            The Sun Prince tilted his head in acknowledgement, a golden sigil burned into the air in front of his forehead, a circle within a corona, the mark of the Eclipse. At that, all creatures, mortal and immortal, bowed to him, honoring his glory and the reign of prosperity that would shine favor upon the city for centuries to come now that the East was his. Before Kalara would ever learn the name of Calymm, she would know his face in dreams, the face of the one whose power had passed to her upon her choosing.

                                            His purpose, his ambition, was now hers. Seeing through his eyes, she gazed across the prostrate figures basking in her splendor, feeling his grandiose aura sweeping over all in the hall. The patterns of creation wove themselves unseen, all entangled at his fingers. The slightest tug could cause a shortage of food in Varsi. A little pull could cause a rebellion in Matetha. The East would be her foothold into a great empire where not even The Guild could stop her. This power was hers now…

                                            …and then the uncomfortable pillow of a rock woke her from her reverie and reminded her that even those with great destinies could get kinks in their necks when they slept in ditches under bridges.

                                                Now that I've written about 3/4ths of a sequel to this story, I'm finding myself wanting to rewrite this story completely! I'm thinking of expanding on what's there by making it 3rd person and adding a little more flesh on the bones of what's already there.

                                                This is where I need your help! If you've read this story, how would you like to see it expanded?

                                                Are there any specific characters or scenes you'd like to see more of? Any other feedback? Personally, I'd love to expand upon Ahrun, as well as explore the idea of a Guild academy even further. There's so much potential for expansion in that bit alone! Plus, there are a few areas where certain people who have a certain impact on Kalara's life who come in towards the end with little warning. That certain someone is definitely deserving of more foreshadowing!

                                                So this is IT! Your crazy opportunity to give me a wishlist of what YOU'D like to see! I'm going to tag a few people I know who read this story or seemed interested in it, wastevens, Argonometra, VolatileSublime, Darkon, and adimar. I offer you the sneak peek of a work in progress rough draft of the sequel in your inbox as 'payment', if you can find the time to provide any feedback. It would be ever so helpful!
