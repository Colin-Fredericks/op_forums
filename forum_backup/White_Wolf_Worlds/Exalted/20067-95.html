        Originally posted by Xanandithras View Post
        On the subject of sorcery, if you have one of the rituals that allow for long-duration sorcerous mote storage, how does that interact with casting a spell using those motes? Do you still have to take a shape sorcery action, or its like a reflexive &quot;boom, wood dragon claws now&quot; type of situation?
        That's a tricky one Page 465 says:
        "If she uses a ritual that allows her to reflectively gather sorcerous motes for a turn, this counts as if she had used a shape sorcery action [...] allowing them to fluidly switch between fighting and shaping spells."
        "Once she has reach the total [of sorcerous motes needed] she unleashes it [the spell] immediately and reflexively."

        Reading the rituals, very few reflexively gather motes and most specifically work with a shape sorcery (or miscellaneous) action. But you're specifically asking about long term storage of motes... So...

        The rules don't say. You could argue that it's reflexive: you've gathered the motes poof! I would argue that you still need to take a single shape sorcery action, then it fires automatically.
