    I may be telling you something you know (apologies if so), but if you haven’t already then I’d suggest checking out page 554 of Core, it lists examples of Latent and Magical Abilities that you can train and might get you started, while the profiles of each individual animal further along in the chapter also give you merits and abilities specific to that animal.

    Depending on whether you mean a Cat (p. 560) or Great Cat (p. 565), at the most basic you could train the former to perform gambits or always attack from stealth against larger targets; while with the latter you could teach them improved Evasion via Battle Cat or maybe Midnight Claw Prana from the example list, which doubles 10s on decisive surprise attacks and allows a master to transfer initiative to said familiar.

    Cats of both sizes are already pretty well served by the example abilites, there’s lots of attacking from stealth or dealing massive damage to opponents you pounced on. If you’re looking to homebrew further stuff, I’d personally look to build on those themes.
