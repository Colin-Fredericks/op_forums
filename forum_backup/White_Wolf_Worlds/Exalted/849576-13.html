    It's not that I don't want them to not save and strike (as a mechanic), it's more that I feel that there should be a back-and-forth initiative exchange which eventually pushes someone back. At that point, I don't care if there's a single decisive attack or not. The problem is that from the very start, from join battle, there's a death spiral, where a bad JB is basically a death sentence, because the first mover will initiative crash them, and then likely one-shot them the next round. I don't feel like it's particularly cinematic and players don't get the opportunity to do cool things, because they're either incapped immediately, or win immediately. An example given is Luke vs Vader as a good emulation of what Exalted 3e's combat should be. Instead, even against tough enemies, it always feels like "Anime Protag vs Mook." It's just that sometimes the PC is the mook.

    There's a lot of cool stuff in the combat system: weapon tags, gambits, specific-use evocations, certain charms -- that will possibly never be realized because you never need them, because combat is basically a 1 to 2 round affair that is not really that strategic.

    Likewise, initiative shift never happens because the +5 from the crash bonus is such a complete buffer that in 1v1 or paired off scenarios, that once you're crashed, it feels to me like combat becomes a foregone conclusion instead of "I'm on my back foot."

                                            But what breaks if you remove SHH's restriction of "first you must use Increasing-Strength Exercise to the maximum"?

                                            The more dots of Essence or Strength you have, the more you have to pay for Increasing-Strength Exercise before you can use Strength-Hying Heave. It's not even proportional to the target you're lifting! A Solar with Strength 2, Essence 1 can pay for one bonus Strength dot and throw the same guy that a Strength 5, Essence 5 Solar needs to pay FIVE dots to throw. That's crazy.

                                            Also, I don't understand the point of having such a restriction at all. You don't need to use Increasing-Strength Exercise before you use Monkey Leap Technique or Mountain-Crossing Leap Technique, no matter how big or heavily-armored you are. You likewise don't need to use Increasing-Strength Exercise before you use Crashing Wave Throw or Heaven-Thunder Hammer -- not for man-sized targets.
