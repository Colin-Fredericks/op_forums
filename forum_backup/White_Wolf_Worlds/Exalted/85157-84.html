                                                        Seeing Mammon like that has caused me to revise some of my presumptions about the nature of the Bank of the Grand Dragon, and the worlds that it presides over. After all, it occurred to me that we know precious little of Mammon's domain, compared to several of the others.

                                                        ​It's probably still not especially good, but it might be less severely exploitative than I had supposed.

                                                        ​Mammon has also been referred to as the least powerful of the Seven, and on top of his physical capabilities, it may be that his functional empire is the smallest and does not expand (keeping in mind that, while each of them nominally has authority over 111,111 universes, many of those have not yet had their gates opened or been subjugated).

                                                        ​Before, I had wondered if the Bank might have been something that was developed out of his need to have assistance in counting his hoard. Now I'm wondering if it might actually be something that evolved out of all the people who journeyed to rob or slay him, and between the inability to do so, how pitiful he was, and a possible difficulty in getting out again, stayed behind, their descendants becoming a society of his devotees.
