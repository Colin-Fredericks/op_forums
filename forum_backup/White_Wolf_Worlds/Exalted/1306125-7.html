                                        Being completely honest, these are several interlocked issues, not just one. Aura, as an idea, is pretty awesome; you give up flexibility in order to gain power, or you lose power and gain in flexibility.

                                        The issue then comes around when the DB Charmset makes about no sense in where it puts elemental tags. The Entombed Mind tree, which has nothing to do with combat and is in fact actively counter to combat, and which consists of three Earth Charms? It comes after the DB Fire version of Poised Lion Attitude, Eternally Argumentative Flame. The Melee capstones? Water, Air, literally anything but fire. Besides having a Wood Charm in between two Fire Charms and other similar shenanigans.

                                        But then you go "Oh that just means I get more chances to juggle Auras around and enter different ones!" Wrong. With how absurdly costly the DB Charms are as a whole, you're hitting bonfire by your first or second turn, if not straight out of Join Battle, which means no Aura juggling for you. Which is the third component of this problem. The fourth is that many Charms are next to useless without Aura, and that other many ones would seem useless without each other but they're different elements. These issues sort of soften themselves out by Essence 3+ since you're getting to the actually decent part of the Charmset, but by then you're picking Signatures, many of which have the Aura keyword, and many of which do not support the Aura it requires at all (Water Melee Signature). And THEN there's the part where defensive Charms only disrupt and end and prevent your Aura, but do not create it, which is one of the dumbest parts of it.

                                        All in all, with how the DB set is, Aura translates to the feeling of getting choked out of fun and options the moment you try to use your powers, despite Aura itself being fine in a vacuum.

                                                    Originally posted by Epimetheus View Post
                                                    Except this isn't good because basically you're telling dragon blooded that it's okay to get screwed for not being the right element. This not good design when you have 3 elements struggling to get defensive charms while 2 others hog them.
                                                    They kind of do. Before Essence 4 in dodge the only defensive charm Fire gets that isn't also balanced is Flickering Candle, which isn't bad but the excellency lets you ignore environmental penalties anyway. Technically Ember Emits Smoke also raises evasion but it's more expensive than the excellency so you're really just paying for the counterattack. Once you get to Essence 3 fire's sig is another counter attack, where Woods actually gives you better defence and Water lets you cancel damage. Air gets Elusive Crosswind and fire gets Bonfire Shadow Evasion for actual defence boosts, so that's pretty even. Bonfire Shadow is actually probably the worst one until you get Coiling Dragon at essence 4, at which point I'd say Fire does pull ahead slightly, and then the essence 5 charm is omni-elemental and even retroactively reassigns elements to prior charms. What Fire gets by going hard into dodge isn't defence as much as it is Evasion based counterattacks and like pseudo disengage rushes and stuff.

                                                    For melee the only dedicated defensive charms in the tree is balanced in FBI and Water in graceful flowing defense, even the clash accuracy booster which can be kind of defensive/offensive is balanced. Then we get to the signatures and air gets mobility, earth gets debilitating effects, Fire and water get straight damage, and wood is left with the only one that actually boosts defensive capabilities. Then as we move through Essence 3 wood again is the only one with a defense boost, and it's probably the best in the tree. The closest Fire has is Portenteous Comet Deflection, which lets them try and reflexively clash with a decisive, or Cloud Riot, which is also water and probably better for keeping yourself safe since it's a withering clash. Water also gets another kind of defensive charm at the pinnacle. What Fire gets by being so melee focused is way more damage and defend other charms and stuff.

                                                    Okay, now resistance. ISoS is balanced, then the next two charms are water and wood, and the wood one is actually a stealth Evasion charm anyway because it lowers mobility penalties from armour. Unfeeling Earth is a good charm for Earth aspects who have a lot of ox bodies, eventually if they can stay in aura they just done give a fuck about wounds. For the signatures air and earth are both good, water and wood are decent, but are both really better suited to be used out of combat, and fire actually makes you less durable. Earth Bears Witness is also pretty good for Earth, Water and Wood, immovable mountain technique for Earth is very situational...and then we get into the complete assness that is late DB resistance with two good defensive Water AURA charms that lead into a defensive Air AURA charm. That sucks monkey totem Lunar balls. What earth gets out of resistance more than defense is ignoring food/water/sleep etc. As well as some decent protection charms.

                                                    Then we have even more context too, because thrown and archery and all sorts of other trees have charms that can help defend as well, AND we have the anima powers. Air gets ranged defense Earth gets soak, hardness, and penalty ignoring, Fire gets ...kind of a shitty flame defense, water gets a lot of mobility stuff, and wood gets to ignore penalties to evasion and resist poison.


                                                    So if your concern is that you hate that Fire gets all the charms to avoid getting hit, and Earth gets all the charms to tank damage, that really isn't the case. On the other hand if you really didn't like how much Fire gets to explosively counterattack with dodge, or defend other a ton, or that the last three charms in resistance have stupid stupid requirements, that's totally valid in my opinion. I don't agree with the former points because I think it's cool that Fire gets to do that as a really unique thing, but I can understand that you might wish you could do that while being hard into Water.

                                                    I also hope that my really long answer isn't coming off as aggressive, it's just really hard to explain in so few words.
