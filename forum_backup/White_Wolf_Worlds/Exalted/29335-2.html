    Originally posted by SuddenThunder View Post

    I kind of hope they don't, to be honest.

    Although it's the goal of the new edition to make Exalts more widely varied in all their forms, I'd like to see Exaltation remain special and catacylsmic in it's own right. It kills at least some of those who attempt it. The Yozis decided that their own army of super-soldier just wasn't worth the consequences ... and they managed to stick to that as a group. It's possibly the single thing they've all agreed on for the longest. I guess I'd like to see that they Gods who could, politically, mostly don't because their existance is pretty good, really, and they don't want to go through the metaphysical lung-and-kidney transplant that seems to be required. As for the Gods (or other beings) that do use it ... they have to. They don't see they have any other choice. They don't want Exalts, they need them.

    Black market Exaltations are talked about more in the hushed and uncertain terms black market nukes are, that the sort of legal and financial hubbub about black market heroin.

    Which is not to say that I'm not sympathetic to the making of TykeBombs, just that I don't think this is a good way to do it. Ochre's point is a good one, Creation is better off filled with half-breeds, Imbued Amalgams, Genesis-products, Tiger-Warriors (and subjects of other, similar Charms), olichike, and Wyld-forged prodigies than having everyone who wants to empower a mortal think 'well, it's off to the Excigence again'.
    I think that exalted created exalts isn't something that can't work-- so long as it doesn't ever get to "production line." My take on it, that I'm going to use for any game where this comes up, is that every exalt is, thematically and even mechanically, not a part of hte setting, it's something that to a greater or lesser degree *drives* the setting-- and a big prt of the setting is "foromer underlings sometimes exceed their creators vision or just outright gank him." Whenever you create an exalt you create a hero (or villain) who cannot be controlled. So sure-- go on a glorious quest (because there is no instruction manual) to claim the fire of heaven for your follower orboon companion-- but it will always be different, it will always be unusual, it will always be earthsaking...and sometimes, when you grab for heaven's fire, you get burned.

                        Originally posted by Holden View Post
                        The Exigence is for gods only. That said, you paid for the book, tell the story you want to tell.

                        The point of the Exigents wasn't to create a new box all new Exalts had to fit in, though, but rather to communicate that you can add whatever you like to the game (and to remove most of the mental heavy lifting from the most obvious avenue people would want to pursue).
                        This leads me to a question. What happens if the Unconquered Sun creates an Exigent of his own. Would it, essentially, simply be another Solar Exalted? And if Luna made an Exigent, would you end up with a Lunar? Or would you end up with something different?

                        Exigents basically take the concepts and themes of their patron and run with that. So, if someone really liked say, Ligier and decided to make an Exalt based around him, it probably wouldn't be much different, at its core, from Exigents who draws heavily from from a single patron's themes.

                        There are plenty of Exalted who don't work that way (such as Gentimians, Liminals and Alchemicals) and Exalts who draw from multiple patrons/themes (Sidereal and Dragon Blooded). So there's plenty of room for Exalted who aren't Exigents.

                        But, from what little we know about them, it seems that if you wanted to make an Exalt designed to be empowered by a single patron (say an ancient ghost or a powerful demon) then Exigents would be a very good place to start.
