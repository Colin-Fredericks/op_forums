        One might as well ask why a Dragon-Blood would bother being a sorcerer. Especially when the Realm despises sorcery. Yet they have a school that teaches it.

        Why do Dragon-Blooded bother learning Martial Arts? Well, the five Immaculate Styles which synergize with their Elemental Auras helps, but Martial Arts are useful even beyond that.

        Lunar Exalted can’t attain Mastery effects with Martial Arts, but they get Deadly Beastmen Transformation, and may possibly get to combo their Attribute Charms with MA Charms.

        I would be surprised and disappointed if Dragon-Blooded don’t get Essence 3 and Essence 5 Charms following Terrestrial Circle Initiation which allow them to learn other Shaping Rituals and further enhance or interact with elemental spells.

        Likewise, Lunar Exalted might get an Essence 5 Charm which helps with transformative spells and workings.

                    Well, Celestial Circle Sorcery is pretty badass on itself, Solars are far from common, Sidereals even less so, we don't know if either Abyssals or Infernals access Solar Circle spells (possibly not), so for most matters one might as well call it Lunar Circle, because they are the guys and gals people will see trumping whole circles of DB or mortal sorcerers nine times out of ten.

                    "Ohhh, you think you are mighty because one day in the elusive future you might get the capacity to access ultra-badass spells nearly nobody has seen since the turning of the ages?                                                     Originally posted by Sorcerous Overlord View Post
                                                    This is something that has been on my mind recently. 3rd edition is moving away from Lunars as "basically werewolves" into a bigger group of, well, Primal Heroes I guess. Shape Changers, Forest Witches, etc. But since only Solars can access the highest tier of Sorcery, what benefits do Lunars get that differentiate them?

                                                    To be clear, I'm NOT asking "why would you make a Lunar Sorcerer", because people have all kinds of reasons to play characters they like, rather, my question is "What advantages do Lunars get when learning Sorcery".
                                                    ​First I don't generally choose Sorcery to differentiate my Lunar from a Solar. Its more of a common toolbox open to just about anyone with a need and the aptitude to use it. Uniqueness is the province of native charmsets.

                                                    ​Secondly I don't justify a Lunar's need for Sorcery based on what Solars can do. Lunars may not reach the pinnacles of Solar Circle but Terrestrial and Celestial Circles are extremely useful on their own merits. Hell...most of your sorcerer's doings are going to involve the lower tiers anyway, regardless if you are a Solar or Lunar. To the extent this matters Lunars are entirely as capable as any Solar sorcerer.
