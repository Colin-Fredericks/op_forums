    A Deathlord as Mentor/Teacher for a Solar

    09-26-2017, 01:20 PM
    So, earlier today, I was reading Tanith Lee's Death's Master. It got me thinking about the Deathlords in a very different context than I had ever done: not as antagonists, but as possible allies or sources of power, even for a Solar that does not betray Creation.

    The Deathlords are, in essence, the most powerful dead things the player characters will encounter, unless they want to go talk to the Neverborn.
    However, they are also remnants of the First Age. They have knowledge of many powerful spells, artifacts and other things a young Solar would love to know.
    ​
    A comparison that I consider adequate is to the Third Circle Demons. They are equally dangerous (some more than others), both groups have access to stuff new Solars need to build a power base, and with the right persuasion, both are probably willing to offer such aid.

    There is, of course, a major difference: the Demon Princes have not all sworn to destroy Creation. While there are plenty of Demons who would love nothing more than to turn Creation into a hellscape, not all necessary enemies of the Solars (take Amalion, for example). However, most young Solars will not know that the Deathlords are out to destroy the world. A clever Deathlord might trick the Solar to believe they have amiable goals.

    Now, such a teacher-student relationship would be campaign changing. And I fully expect that once the full scope of a Deathlord's ambitions is revealed, the Solar will rethink their allegiances.
    However, I think such a story could be very cool too, without immediately having to resort to the Deathlord forcing the Solar to become an Abyssal, or trying to murder them because they might one day become a powerful enemy.

    So, now that I've gotten all that out of my system: what do you think? Is there a place in Exalted for Deathlords beside antagonists? Or am I just crazy and is this a stupid idea?

                                              Isn't the premise of the OP basically the canon situation with that Solar Admiral from Skullstone?
                                              The Deathlords acting as mentors would probably be no worse in the long run than some powerful but ambiguous diety like Amoth Citysmiter. While they are nominally supposed to destroy Creation they have so consistently drug their feet on the whole project and their current plans are so oblique to that goal that it can probably be dismissed as a pressing issue for all but the longest running campaigns.

                                              As for the specifics, it is important to remember that all of the Deathlords were raised with a First Age worldview. While they are no longer Solars, the notion that an alliance of semi-independent Exalted monarchies where the individual Chosen use their dominions as a means to pursue their individual goals and aggrandizement probably makes a great deal of sense to them. Its effectively how they behave now. The Second Age might strike them as wrong or disturbing on a visceral level that is hard for most people to understand.

                                              The melodrama of death might mean that the Deathlords cannot bring themselves to "take the win" while Creation is in the state it is in and that they seek to elevate Exalted, especially Abyssals and Solars, back into their "proper" place so that when the Deathlords do finally move into the end game of their goals the situation is the appropriate epic struggle they need it to be.
