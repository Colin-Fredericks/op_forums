            Originally posted by Braininthejar View Post
            Accord of the Unbreakable Spirit - What is its intended use? It seems to only work for an hour, and the people it protects are busy maintaining it during that time, so outside of being stranded in Wyld and waiting for outside help, what is it good for?
            You know, I feel as though, unless it's one of the really well-known or otherwise obvious Charm names, it would be good practice for people asking questions such as this to maybe refer to where the Charm is, and perhaps their Ability, as even with only three books there are so many Charms and similar powers that simply referring to their flowery names out of context means that a lot of the work of actually finding them lies on the reader.

            It's not as bad as everything being in abbreviations, but it still bugs me a bit.

            Anyway, the main value that I can see for it is providing protection under circumstances in which the Wyld has come to people, rather than the people going to the Wyld. That, and the somewhat more common circumstances of people being exposed to disease or the elements (by which I mean dangerous weather and the like). The latter two are already phenomena that are not uncommon, and even the Wyld one will probably feel more useful when more setting material and antagonists help to emphasize the idea that the Wyld is capable of drifting far inland, springing up out of nowhere, or being inflicted by Raksha.

            It can be the kind of thing to help people weather a temporary storm, or guard them until one of the Solar's circlemates removes the cause, I think.

                Originally posted by Braininthejar View Post

                Bear in mind that nothing in the charm says the group can't be moving while doing their chanting, prayers, etc. I wouldn't let them run at full speed or anything, but a steady walking pace should be absolutely doable. (Hitting yourself in the face with a board while chanting is probably not necessary, but is funny.)

                Since there's no size limit on the group that can be protected, a Zenith priest-leader can take a whole bunch of her followers through hostile terrain this way. For example, if you come to a city that has a plague going on, your followers just need to break out the hymnal as they pass through, and be reasonably sure of protection. And, of course, since you can pay 6m, 1wp after the first hour and have the effect keep going indefinitely, you don't even have to be there to protect them.
