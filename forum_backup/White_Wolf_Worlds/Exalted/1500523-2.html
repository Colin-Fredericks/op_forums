        Originally posted by gribblethemunchkin View Post
        Is it feasible to explain roughly how long the development process is from any of these stages? I'm not sure how wildly it might vary between products, nor how common slipage and rearranging of timetables is and whether this might be something that can't really be quantified.
        I've tried to do exactly that, in a historic sense, anyway. I'll link a spreadsheet below that tracks the dates that every Onyx Path product enters each stage, from the week be that At8D first entered the MMN list. It's a few weeks behind the current MMN event horizon at present, and I'm certainly not immune from error, but what's there has gone through multiple rounds of error checking by me. The cards products and Trinity Assassin's are the only ones where I know there's something weird going on my end for sure at this point.

        The "Analysis" tab will be what you're after. That gives average number of weeks in each development stage, both for all OPP products overall and various splits. These are by game line, IP owner, and product type (eg core rulebook, splatbook, bestiary, adventure, sourcebook etc). Some of those numbers may not totally add up, because Art Direction can run alongside other stages in some cases, and is used as a "stop date" for editing, so some truncation may happen if that's the case.

        Also worth noting that, because Art Direction and subsequent stages are notated slightly differently from prior stages, that I've often waited until release to trawl through those stages in detail. That means that Art Direction and beyond may not be as complete as they could be in some cases.

        With all that said, here's the link: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets...t?usp=drivesdk
        I'm also absolutely gagging for At8D. The podcast I cohost is quite setting-dependent, and once we're through the Great Houses there'll only be a handful of places we can cover with full 3e detail before the book comes out.



        Wondrous Atlas of Creation's Destiny - I cohost an Exalted podcast focusing on examining the setting's lore and how to develop them into elements to use in games.
        Ex3 Reference Materials - currently includes an ST screen, common actions sheet, weapons reference sheet, character creation summary and mortal QCs reference sheet.
