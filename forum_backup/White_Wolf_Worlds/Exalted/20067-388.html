                                01-12-2017, 01:02 PM
                                Originally posted by RustedAir View Post
                                Question about some Integrity charms:

                                Mind-Cleansing Prana has as a Bridge prerequisite "any 6 Martial Arts Charms", yet some of the charms further down the tree (like Energy Restoration Prana) have "Mind-Cleansing Prana or any 3 Martial Arts Charms" but read "When using the prerequisite" in their text.
                                Does this mean I can activate them just by meditating in the same way as with MCP (one hour's worth of meditation) instead of exclusively when activating it? And if so, could I potentially pick those charms up BEFORE I get MCP?
                                For those wanting to check the book, this happens with Energy Restoration Prana, Clear Mind Discipline, Barque of Transcendent Vision and Body-Restoring Benison.

                                Thanks in advance, and big thanks to all the people who take the time to answer in this thread.
                                So, first, none of the Charms function at all without Mind-Cleansing Prana. They do nothing. This is both clear RAI from their design, and also a pretty convincing reading of RAW. They refer to "prerequisite", singular; the only time any of them could have a singular prerequisite that is not Mind-Cleansing Prana is the corner case of a Supernal Integrity character.

                                Second, the "or" in the prerequisite is there because of a small detail in the way the Bridge keyword works. The Bridge keyword gives you access to the Charm at caste / favored prices if "all" the prerequisites used to buy the Charm are caste / favored. The intent is for you to still buy them in order (since they don't function otherwise). The idea is that MA-focused characters can progressively unlock these alongside their progress in their Martial Art

                                Third, the clear intent of the design of each of these Charms is, through the Bridge keyword, to permit MA-focused characters to progressively unlock these alongside their progress in the MA.

                                            Third, the clear intent of the design of each of these Charms is, through the Bridge keyword, to permit MA-focused characters to progressively unlock these alongside their progress in the MA.
                                            My only issue with this is that, assuming you have all the charms whether you got them through bridge or not, for some reason you can only relax into your meditation to restore energy, willpower and heal when...dealing with complex logistical or abstract problems in your head?

                                            Mind-Cleansing Prana fairly explicitly states that (despite being a simple charm) it involves a roll on a different skill so it can provide a bonus or relieve a penalty - if there isn't a roll it can't do anything and as a simple charm it must create an action. As an instant charm the only thing it does is initiate a process, there is no commitment to the charm and if we assume they can meditate and then walk away after the hour and get their bonuses still then we have to deal with the fact that there is no expiry date on the bonus/penalty negation. It would literally be a plop down for an hour and then walk around with a persistent dice bonus forever at no cost (instant simple charm activates at beginning of action and you regain back the 5m in the hour you are meditating be basic rules anyway) and if they DO take any of the actions like say... reading intentions... then free willpower! Each and every time!

                                            Moreover, as an instant charm any reference to 'when using that charm' can only possibly refer to the instant in which it activates and creates a roll on an ability unrelated to integrity in which case "each hour of using the prerequisite" makes no sense at all unless you're expecting a player to spend 5m every instant for multiple hours. And if we then allow it to say, "yes yes not just that instant", we have to deal again with the fact that there is no expiry date on the effects of the charm so unless the new charms specifically mention meditation... (energy restoring does, clear mind does, body-restoring does, barque kinda does but that leaves this whole other weird hole - meditate for an hour and then wander around immune to social influence so long as you don't "react" to anything. Oh look, in the single instant when Mind-Cleansing Prana is active you can reflexively activate a survival charm to react to a sudden and inexplicable change in weather, sure hope it doesn't shift back in the next instant otherwise that's another 5m on top of Hardship-Surviving Spirit just to not break meditation.

                                            For this to really work and not be ridiculous, we need to make some assumptions about the way it works; namely the charm 'lasts' as long as an hour's meditation despite everything else it says and the same assumptions need to be extended to the charms following it in the cascade otherwise you can only meditate as long as you have complex problems to solve... so...yeah I don't think clear is a valid descriptor for the intention of way these charms work, let alone how they are written.

                                            It certainly makes sense to follow the direction of the cascade charms requiring Mind-Cleansing Prana to be active and used before they take effect - taking a deeper look at how they all work makes it all stop making sense

                                                        Originally posted by Argonot View Post
                                                        1. What happens to a sanctum when the god associated to it dies? (Im not tied to any particular edition on this one)
                                                        Roll of Glorious Divinity I states that slain gods with sanctums normally resurrect inside them within weeks of months, but if the god is slain in a way that denies such resurrection (Ghost Eating Technique, Terminal Sanction, et cetera) then the sanctum usually fades away immediately, or within weeks.
