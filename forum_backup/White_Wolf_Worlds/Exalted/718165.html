    3rd ed Lunar fan conversion

    10-23-2015, 05:02 AM
    Here's my 3rd ed Lunar conversion.

    Obviously this isn't finished: not like there's been all that much time so far, I did most of this today and yesterday, though some stuff before. I'll post up what I've done so far.
    ​Now, while I have made some tweaks, this is based on 2nd ed Lunars (it's a conversion, not a rewrite) which I'm sure will infuriate many, but there it is.

    So far, I've done the basic rules (character gen, anima banners, etc), knacks (which are now charms) and Perception charms (Perception charms are the easiest I think, because some are copy-pastes of Solar charms anyhow).

    I encourage comments, but please give constructive criticism, not rants about the Thousand Streams River or how Lunars shouldn't turn into animals.

    Basic changes:
    Character creation is roughly equivalent to Solar char gen, with 3 less abilities and 1 more attribute. They have 2 favoured and 2 caste attributes, plus Survival and 1 other as favoured.

    Casteless Lunars have the same char gen, but don't get Anima banners (or turn into Chimera: you can take charms to get more true forms and become a Chimera like 1st ed).

    Lunars use the same XP costs as Solars. However, learning other forms costs XP (normally 2, but 4 for huge creatures, and 1 per dot of Essence for humans).

    I copied the Antagonists' section stuff about Tells (which I really like, incidentally), shapechanging, some new charms, etc.

    Character Creation

    Lunar characters have 8/6/4 dots for their primary/secondary/tertiary attributes respectively. They also add 1 additional dot to one of their favoured attributes.

    Each Lunar has 2 caste attributes from his caste's list, and 2 favoured attributes of his choice. They may choose one of their caste attributes to become a Supernal Attribute. This allows them to buy charms as if they had Essence 5 for that attribute.

    Before they are tattooed with mystic sigils made of moonsilver (which is normally done before character creation), Lunars do not actually have a caste. If, for some reason, the player does not want to take Moonsilver Tattoos at character creation, they simply have 4 favoured attributes and no supernal attribute. Their caste mark will change with the phase of the moon, but they gain no powers from their Anima Banner.

    Lunars have 25 dots of abilities. They cannot put more than 3 dots in any ability before spending freebies.
    Lunars may choose 1 favoured ability. They also automatically favour Survival; spending time in animal form makes it easier for them to understand how to survive in the wilderness.
    Unlike other Exalt types, Lunars who favour Brawl do not also favour Martial Arts. However, Lunars who favour Dexterity or Wits also favour Martial Arts for free, in addition to the other ability they chose to favour. However, Martial Arts is never a supernal ability, even if Dexterity of Wits are supernal attributes.
    Like Solars, Lunars get 15 charms, 10 points of merits, Essence 1, Willpower 5, and 15 bonus points. Their bonus point costs are the same as for Solars, but dots of favoured attributes always cost 3 points, regardless of whether they are primary, secondary or tertiary.

    Lunars have a personal mote pool equal to (Essence + 15), and a peripheral pool equal to ([Essence x 4] + 34).

    All Lunars start with a single true animal form, without having to hunt it. This in some way represents their personality, legend or spirit. Without charms, there is no difference between this true animal form and any form the Lunar learns by consuming heart's blood.

    When a Lunar exalts, their true human form sometimes develops animal features based on their true animal form. These are normally purely cosmetic, but could also include one of the following supernatural merits if the Lunar buys them with her merit dots: 1-dot Claws/Fangs/Hooves/Horns, Gills, Enhanced Sense, Tail (1 or 2 dots) or Unusual Hide (1 or 2 dots).
    This animal feature becomes the Lunar's Tell, and expresses itself in minor, subtle ways when the Lunar has shapeshifted (though if it is a supernatural merit, it won't give any mechanical benefits).
    Not all Lunars have such an animal feature; their tell might be a birthmark, scar, or any other feature the player wants.


    New Merits:
    Solar Mate: In a previous life, the Lunar was the sworn companion to a Solar Exalt, and this bond endures down the ages. He has an automatic Minor, Major or Defining intimacy to one particular Solar, Abyssal or Infernal, as chosen by the Lunar's player. This intimacy can be eroded as normal, but will increase by one step at the end of each story until it has returned to its original level. Some charms have additional effects when targeting a Lunar's Solar Mate.
    If the Solar Mate is another PC, this Merit costs 2 dots. This should only be done if both players agree.
    If the Solar Mate is a helpful NPC, the cost of this Merit is the same as the Ally merit.
    If the Solar Mate is a non-allied NPC, antagonist or left as a mystery to be determined by the ST during the game, this Merit is free.
    Note that “Solar Mate” in this case can actually refer to a Solar, Abyssal or Infernal. Whether the Solar Mate is a Solar, Abyssal or Infernal has no effect on how the Merit works.

    Tattoo Artefact:
    It is possible to incorporate an artefact into either a Lunar's moonsilver tattoos, or part of the tattoos. For example, the tattoos could be thickened around the Lunar's torso to form Light Moonsilver Armour, or one of his scars could be carefully reinforced with auspicious designs to become a Hearthstone Amulet. This costs the same amount as the artefact would normally.

    Advancement

    Trait Experience Cost
    Non-Caste/Favoured Attribute increase current rating x 4
    Caste/Favoured Attribute increase current rating x 3
    Non-Caste/Favoured Ability increase current rating x 2
    Caste/Favoured Ability increase (current rating x 2) - 1
    New Ability 3
    Speciality 3
    Purchased Merit new rating x 3
    Willpower 8
    Lunar Charm 10 (8 if in a Caste or Favored Attribute)
    Martial Arts Charm 10 (8 if Wits or Dexterity are Caste or Favoured Attributes)
    Spell 10 (8 if Intelligence is a Caste or Favored Ability)
    New Evocation 8

      Castes

      ​
      Lunar castes

      Full Moons:
      Caste Attributes: Strength, Dexterity, Stamina.
      Anima Banner:
      For 5 motes, the Lunar may add half her essence (rounded up) in dice to all movement pools and feats of strength (both the dice pool and the minimum strength for particular feats) for one scene. At bonfire/iconic level, this ability is activated automatically at no cost.
      Once per scene, when the Full Moon is at bonfire or iconic anima display, she may add half her Essence score (rounded up) to her base Initiative upon resetting to base after a successful decisive attack.
      For 5 motes, the Full Moon may reset one Strength, Dexterity or Stamina charm with an outstanding reset condition. This ability can only be used once per day, and is reset when the moon rises in the evening. If this ability is used on a night of the Full Moon, all Strength, Dexterity and Stamina charms with outstanding reset conditions are reset.

      Changing Moon:
      Caste Attributes: Charisma, Manipulation, Appearance
      Anima Banner:
      For 5 motes, the Changing Moon adds half her essence (rounded up) to her Guile and the difficulty to spot her tell for a scene. If her anima reaches the burning/iconic level, she gets this bonus automatically, as she is surrounded by an ever-shifting aura of light and shadows, concealing her identity. This also requires watchers her to make a Perception+Awareness roll, difficulty of her Essence, to identify her.
      As a simple action costing 5 motes, the Lunar can change the physical appearance of her true human form. She can change her height and weight by up to 50%, half or double her apparent age, and also change her facial features, ethnicity, colouration, and other minor physical features. She can also change her voice. No roll is required to look different, but if she attempts to take a form specific enough to impersonate a particular person, she must make a disguise roll as normal, though ignoring all penalties for ethnicity and body shape, and with a dice bonus equal to her Essence or 3, whichever is higher. If she knows the charm Twin-Faced Hero she can ignore the penalty for gender, and if she has the charm Hybrid Body Rearrangement and an appropriate animal form (such as a lion for a lion-man, a wolf for a wolf-girl, etc), she can roll to disguise herself as a beastman with no penalty.
      For 5 motes, the Changing Moon may reset one Charisma, Manipulation or Appearance charm with an outstanding reset condition. This ability can only be used once per day, and is reset when the moon rises in the evening.

      No Moon:
      Caste Attributes: Perception, Intelligence, Wits
      Anima Banner:
      No Moons are master shamans, in tune with the worlds of spirit and nature. But more than that, they are steeped in forbidden knowledge and the dark secrets of the Witching Moon, making them adept at negotiating with spirits both holy and unholy. For 5 motes, for one scene all social influence attempts by the No Moon against gods, elementals, Fair Folk and demons, cost one additional willpower to resist. In addition, the No Moon's Resolve is raised by (half her Essence rounded up, or 3, whichever is lower) against their social influence and charms. Note that this ability does not apply to ghosts; even Luna fears to learn the secrets of the dead Titans.
      As the Lunars who are aligned with the darkest of nights, No Moons can wrap themselves in shadow. For 3 motes, they can get (Essence or 3, whichever is higher) additional dice on a stealth attempt in shadow, darkness, or dim light.
      For 5 motes, the No Moon may reset one Perception, Intelligence or Wits charm with an outstanding reset condition. This ability can only be used once per day, and is reset when the moon rises in the evening. If this ability is used on a night of the New Moon, all Perception, Intelligence and Wits charms with outstanding reset conditions are reset.

          Miscellaneous powers

          Miscellaneous Lunar Powers

          Moonsilver Tattoos

          Soon after exalting, most Lunars are found by elder Lunars, who inscribe them with tattoos made from Moonsilver, the magical material that resonates most closely with Lunar essence. This gives them a Caste, and also makes them completely immune to all external magic and effects that would alter their form. A tattooed Lunar can have his arm cut off with a crippling attack, but the mutating powers of the Wyld will not turn his arms into tentacles. This does, however, also mean that a first-age artefact could not make a Lunar twenty foot tall even if he wanted to. He would have to use his own spell, working or charm to achieve this effect.
          The tattoos also make a Lunar immune to madness caused solely by being exposed to the Wyld. A faerie lord could use a charm to cause hallucinations, and being trapped in a forest of eternally screaming faces might erode the Lunar's sanity, but simply standing in the Wyld for a long time will not cause him to believe he is a tree. Nor will he become addicted to the Wyld.
          The designs of each Lunar's tattoos are unique to them, and in some way express the legend of his very soul. Players are encouraged to give their character whatever tattoo design they think is evocative, but the tattoos always cover a large enough amount of the Lunar's body, including hands and head, that wearing regular clothes is not enough to hide them.

          If a Lunar is tattooed in play, the ritual to tattoo him is an Ambition 1 Celestial Working and requires approximately half the Moonsilver necessary to make a Daiklaive. It can only be performed by a Lunar who themselves has moonsilver tattoos. Failing destroys the moonsilver, but doesn't stop the sorcerer from trying again once they acquire more material.
          Botching the working is extremely rare for powerful Lunar sorcerers, but it has occasionally happened. Generally, this will give the newly-tattooed Lunar some strange mutation.

          If the Lunar who will perform the tattooing wishes to incorporate an artefact into it, this artefact must be created before the sorcerous working is attempted, using the normal rules for making an artefact of that level. Failing the working does not destroy the artefact, though it won't work until a successful working

          It is possible to use alternate methods to fix a Lunar's caste as an Ambition 1 Solar Working, based upon ritually modifying the Lunar's body in some way, and this can theoretically be performed even by the casteless Lunar himself. When the Lunars first begin to experiment with fixing their castes, they experimented with a variety of techniques, and so some ancient Lunars have ritual scars, gemstones embedded under their skin, multiple piercings made from the bones of behemoths, or other stranger things. It was eventually discovered that the moonsilver tattoos are the most efficient method, however, so the vast majority of Lunars use that method.

          Minor Lunar Powers:
          All Lunars may spend 1 mote to flare their caste mark for as long as they wish.
          They can spend 1 mote to know the exact day of the Lunar month and phase of the moon.
          They may spend 1 mote to make their tell obvious and unmistakeable for one scene.
          They may take a simple action to concentrate and spend 1 mote to make their Moonsilver Tattoos sink into their skin, hiding them. This is done automatically, reflexively and at no cost whenever the Lunar takes a form other than their true form(s) or Deadly Beastman form. However, as soon as a Lunar's caste mark begins to shine, the tattoos will appear once more.

          Anima Level Effects
          Dim: The Lunar’s anima is invisible to all senses, masking her Exalted nature. This is the default level at which a Lunar’s anima resides when not spending Essence.

          Glowing: The character’s anima glows and pulses, outlining him with strange silvery essence. His Caste mark appears on his brow, shining through anything placed over it, and his moonsilver tattoos reappear if hidden, even when he is in alternate forms. Any attempts at stealth or disguise suffer a -3 penalty.

          Burning: The character’s anima blazes brightly, subsuming his Caste mark into its radiance, though the mark may reappear when he uses powerful charms. Wisps of bright power coil off his body and rise into the air. Anything subjected to his anima may become chill and damp to the touch afterwards, or perhaps slightly warped. Stealth becomes impossible.

          Bonfire/Iconic: The Lunar’s anima ignites in a grand display of iconic splendor, depicting personal iconography chosen by the player, often though not always related to his true animal form: one Lunar warrior’s anima may depict a howling wolf, while a No Moon sorcerer might be surrounded by an ominous flock of bats. This phantasmal display then collapses into a raging bonfire of spiritual power stretching high into the air, visible for miles. This level of display illuminates the area around the Lunar out to short range, but does not entirely banish shadows, which flicker and dance disturbingly. His Caste Mark behaves as in the previous progression, and the iconic display may also reappear at dramatic moments.

              Shapeshifting rules

              Shapeshifting:

              The Lunar Exalted may take the heart’s blood of humans and animals, stealing forms to wear at their pleasure.

              This Sacred Hunt requires the Lunar to hunt down and kill the target animal, consume its heart, and spend 1wp and 2xp. The Lunar's companions and followers can help, but he must deal the killing blow himself. The heart must be consumed within five minutes of the animal's death. Traditionally, Lunars begin the Hunt by dedicating it to Luna in prayer, but this is not strictly necessary.
              To find and hunt an animal that lives locally normally takes a number of hours equal to 6 minus the successes on a Wits+Survival roll, minimum one, though this might be modified by environmental factors.
              Note that if a Lunar is attacked, he can “hunt his enemy” by striking the killing blow in self-defence and then consume the heart, for who may dare hunt the greatest of predators and think they can escape retribution?
              He cannot, however, buy an animal from a shop and eat its heart to gain its form: this violates the bond of understanding between hunter and hunted, and will only lead to a dead animal.

              Without charms, the Lunar cannot take the form of any animal with the Legendary Size merit or any animal smaller than a housecat. Some charms extend the range of animals that a Lunar can learn the form of.

              A Lunar may pay five motes and take a miscellaneous action to assume any form he has taken. This transformation lasts indefinitely, until the Lunar reverts to his true human form (which is free and reflexive) or shifts into a different shape. Shapeshifting can be flurried, but not with an attack.
              When in animal form, the Lunar uses the animal's physical dice pools, such as combat movement, attack rolls, and senses. He also uses physical derived stats such as Evasion and Soak. However, health levels and non-physical dice pools do not change, and excellency caps are still based on the Lunar's true human form. A Strength 4 Lunar that turns into a Tyrant Lizard can still only buy 4 extra dice on strength rolls.
              Latent or magical abilities are not granted by shapeshifting, but can be unlocked with certain Charms.
              Martial Arts charms cannot be used in animal form. Such charms are not automatically deactivated, simply useless for the duration of the transformation; a Lunar could activate Tiger Form, turn into a bird and receive no benefits from it, then turn back into a human in the same scene and get the benefits of Tiger Form without needing to activate it a second time.
              The one exception is Deadly Beastman Form: as a roughly human form that resonates with the Lunar's own soul, it is possible to use Martial Arts in this form.

              All Lunars have a unique Tell: a physical give-away, which subtly translates in some way into his other shapes, which makes it possible to recognise them in another form. Spotting a shapeshifted Lunar for something other than what it appears to be is treated as a roll to recognize a disguised character, with a flat difficulty of 7. This can’t be attempted unless the observer is already aware of the Lunar's tell.

                  Basic charm rules

                  Charms

                  Unlike Solar charms, Lunar charms can always be combined with charms from other attributes when they apply to the same kind of action. However, they cannot be combined with Martial Arts charms on the same action.

                  Excellencies:
                  The Lunar Exalted tap into the raw potential of their Attributes to bolster their heroic feats, and can add up to (Attribute) dice to any action at 1 mote per dice. However, with a stunt, they may add a second Attribute to the limit as long as the action is described in a way that fits the combination. For example, a Full Moon rolling (Dexterity + Brawl) to smash a Dragon-Blooded shikari would normally have a dice limit of (Dexterity), but could increase this to (Dexterity + Strength) if he described the attack in a way that evoked his raw brawn and mighty thews.

                  When raising static values, Lunars can add up to (Attribute)/2, round down, to the static value, at 2 motes per +1 bonus.
                  However, when increasing soak, the Lunar may add up to (Stamina) to his soak, at 1 mote per +1 soak.
                  A Lunar can increase the static value of his Appearance when compared to other's Resolve for bonus dice, but this does not actually increase his Appearance, just the derived static value.

                  With a stunt, a Lunar can draw upon a second Attribute, allowing them to add up to (Attribute + Second Attribute)/2, round down. Thus, a Dexterity 5, Dodge 5, and Intelligence 5 Lunar who attempts to evade an attack and describes how his keen mind discerns the rhythm of his opponent’s blows, helping him to avoid them, could raise his Evasion by up to 5.

                  Motes spent via the Strength and Stamina Excellencies must, of course, obey the usual cap for Charms added via dice. However, effects which just state that they add to the Lunar’s withering damage or soak, do not. For example, a Stamina 4 Lunar could spend 4 motes to increase his soak from 4 to 8, and then use Armour-Forming Technique to increase it to 16, even though this is past the usual cap on dice added by charms.

                  New Keywords:
                  Gift: These charms can be activated in a special way with a changed duration whenever the Lunar enters his Deadly Beastman Form, if he can take this form. See Deadly Beastman Transformation for details.

                      Strength charms

                      Shapeshifting

                      Deadly Beastman Transformation
                      Cost: - (5m or 5m, 1wp); Mins: Strength 3, Essence 1; Type: Permanent
                      Keywords: None; Duration: Indefinite
                      Prerequisite Charms: None
                      The Lunar may shapeshift into a ferocious battle form that is half-man, half-animal. This adds a new Deadly Beastman form to her Heart's Blood library. Shapeshifting into it follows the normal rules and cost of 5 motes.
                      Unlike other forms, in this form she uses her own dice pools, but with a +2 bonus to the raw damage of all withering attacks she makes and to her combat movement pool.
                      In addition, while in this form she heals a single level of damage whenever she recovers from being crashed.
                      When the Lunar purchases this charm, she may select (Essence) dots of Merits whose benefits she will automatically receive when using Deadly Beastman Transformation. These cannot be changed until the Lunar's Essence increases.
                      These Merits must be chosen from the following list, and with the exception of Hideous, should reflect the Lunar's true animal form in some way:
                      Chameleon; Claws/Fangs/Hooves/Horns; Enhanced Sense; Extra Limbs; Gills; Hideous; Mighty Thew; Quills; Tail; Toxin Resistance; Venomous; Wall Walking; Wings.
                      Deadly Beastman Transformation does, however, come with a disadvantage, for the raw transformative energy of Lunar magic must be focused inside to activate it. Thus, while a Lunar is in her Deadly Beastman form, she may not use any Evocations.
                      Finally, when the Lunar spends the 5 motes to take this form, she may also spend a point of Willpower to simultaneously activate all charms with the Gift keyword for no additional cost. These charms last until she changes form out of Deadly Beastman Transformation, rather than their normal duration.

                      Devastating Ogre Enhancement
                      Cost: - ; Mins: Strength 4, Essence 2; Type: Permanent
                      Keywords: None; Duration: Permanent
                      Prerequisite Charms: Deadly Beastman Transformation

                      The Lunar’s battle form grows vast and terrible. This Charm upgrades its prerequisite. Increase the bonus to the Lunar’s withering attacks and movement pool to (Strength or 5, whichever is lower).
                      The Lunar also halves wound penalties to attack and movement rolls when in Deadly Beastman form.
                      Finally, in this form, the Lunar now also heals a single level of damage whenever she resets to base Initiative after making a decisive attack with 11+ Initiative.

                      Combat

                      Spine-Breaking Technique
                      Cost: 3m, 1wp; Mins: Strength 3, Essence 2; Type: Simple
                      Keywords: Decisive-only; Duration: Instant
                      Prerequisite Charms:

                      The Lunar may make a decisive savaging attack against a grappled enemy without resetting to base Initiative. This charm may only be used once per scene, but may be reset by doing damage with decisive savaging attacks twice more in the combat.

                          Dexterity charms

                          Dexterity Charms

                          Shapeshifting

                          Humble Mouse Shape
                          Cost: -; Mins: Dexterity 2, Essence 1; Type: Permanent
                          Keywords: None; Duration: Permanent
                          Prerequisite Charms: None
                          This charm teaches a character the proper ritual to begin a sacred hunt for smaller animals. Normally, a Lunar cannot master a shape smaller than a housecat. Now, creatures as small as a mouse become valid targets for the blood hunt. The hunt typically takes about half as long for creatures of this size. This charm expands the range of possible spirit shapes for a starting character.

                          Diminutive Monster Mastery
                          Cost: -; Mins: Dexterity 4, Essence 3; Type: Permanent
                          Keywords: None; Duration: Permanent
                          Prerequisite Charms: Humble Mouse Shape
                          Whenever the Lunar takes the form of an animal, he may make it half the normal size. This gives the animal form -2 on all its attacks, combat movement and soak, but +1 to its Evasion score and +2 on Stealth rolls.
                          If he knows Mountainous Spirit Expression as well as this charm, when taking his true animal form this charm instead allows him to choose whether or not to apply Mountainous Spirit Expression, rather than have it apply automatically.
                          This charm cannot be used to enhance the forms the Lunar takes with Deadly Beastman Transformation or Becoming the Swarm, or human form.

                          Emerald Grasshopper Form
                          Cost: -; Mins: Dexterity 3, Essence 2; Type: Permanent
                          Keywords: None; Duration: Permanent
                          Prerequisite Charms: Humble Mouse Shape

                          A character with this charm can become the literal ﬂy on the wall. Creatures as small as the typical houseﬂy become valid targets for the blood hunt.

                          Becoming the Swarm
                          Cost: -; Mins: Dexterity 4, Essence 4; Type: Permanent
                          Keywords: None; Duration: Permanent
                          Prerequisite Charms: Emerald Grasshopper Form

                          This Knack enables the Lunar to subdivide his body into a swarm of lesser creatures. These creatures individually may be no larger than a rat, and must all be forms that the Lunar has in his Heart's Blood library. The Lunar is free to mix and match different types of creatures in the swarm, but if he wishes to use unconventional forms of locomotion (such as flight or crawling up walls) all creatures comprising the swarm must be capable of such movement.
                          The Lunar takes a shapeshifting action to become a Battle Group with a Size of 2 and Perfect Morale. This swarm covers a roughly spherical area equal to one range band per point of Size. He cannot benefit from any kind of Command actions or equipment. His attacks are made as a Light weapon with the Brawl, Flexible, Lethal, Natural and Reach tags. He cannot make Grapple attacks or Parry incoming attacks, but must rely on his Evasion. He cannot take any actions that a swarm of tiny creatures would not be able to make, as adjudicated by the ST.
                          If the Lunar is reduced to Size 0, he immediately is forced to shapeshift to a different form. Since this is likely not on his turn, he will most likely need to reflexively return to his true human form, but other charms might allow him to reflexively shift to other forms.
                          The Lunar's own health levels are set aside while he is a Battle Group. When he shapeshifts to a different, non-Battle Group form, his health levels return, but he takes 4 levels of unsoakable damage for each point of Size he lost while in Battle Group form. This may kill him.

                          Hungry All-Consuming Swarm
                          Cost: -; Mins: Dexterity 5, Essence 5; Type: Permanent
                          Keywords: None; Duration: Permanent
                          Prerequisite Charms: Becoming the Swarm
                          This charm upgrades its prerequisite, allowing the Lunar to become a Battle Group with a Size up to his Essence. He may take a form with less than his maximum Size if he wishes.

                          Quicksilver Second Face
                          Cost: -; Mins: Dexterity 3, Essence 1; Type: Permanent
                          Keywords: None; Duration: Permanent
                          Prerequisite Charms: None
                          The Lunar can shift form more seamlessly. He can now place a shapeshifting action in a flurry with an attack action.

                          Lightning-Change Style
                          Cost: - (+3m); Mins: Dexterity 4, Essence 2; Type: Permanent
                          Keywords: None; Duration: Permanent
                          Prerequisite Charms: Quicksilver Second Face

                          Sometimes a Lunar simply must change shape now. Perhaps battle is imminent or someone is about to discover the character’s true identity. This charm enables a Lunar to shapeshift as a completely reﬂexive action once on her action, by adding three motes to the cost. She can even do this in response to an attack in order to gain better soak or other dice pools (if that attack was on her turn).

                          Constant Quicksilver Rearrangement
                          Cost: -; Mins: Dexterity 5, Essence 3; Type: Permanent
                          Keywords: None; Duration: Permanent
                          Prerequisite Charms: Lightning-Change Style

                          This charm upgrades its prerequisite, allowing it to be used to reflexively shapeshift at any time, rather than just on the Lunar's action. However, the Lunar may still only shapeshift once per initiative count.

                          Monkey Arm Style
                          Cost: 3m; Mins: Dexterity 2, Essence 1; Type: Reflexive

                                      If she wishes, she may immediately and permanently gain one of the following Supernatural Merits for free in her true human form: Chameleon, Claws/Fangs/Horns/Hooves, Gills, Enhanced Sense, Extra Limbs, Hideous, Quills, Tail, Unusual Hide (1, 2 or 3 dots), Wall Walking, Wings (3 dots). This must reflect some part of the new animal form. For example, if the Lunar's new animal form is a Bat, he could gain Enhanced Sense (Hearing), or 3pt Wings, but not Quills or Gills.
                                      Her Tell and anima banner may change as appropriate, to reflect a combination of both forms, though normally it primarily reflects the new form.
                                      This charm can be purchased as many times as the Lunar's Essence, gaining several different animal true forms.

                                      Many traditionalist Lunars consider learning this charm an insult to Luna and the totem gods of the Lunar's original true animal form. They also believe that it is a path to madness and the mutation of the Wyld, though this has never been proven. Both they and the totem gods themselves often react with hostility on meeting a Lunar whom they realise knows this charm. Luna herself has stayed silent on the matter, inscrutable as always.

                                      Mastering the Totem Form
                                      Cost: - ; Mins: Wits 2, Essence 1; Type: Permanent
                                      Keywords: None; Duration: Permanent
                                      Prerequisite Charms: None

                                      The Lunar chooses one true animal form he knows. He may now take the form of this true animal form as a reflexive action that costs no motes, like he can with his true human form.
                                      However, a Lunar cannot choose a form with Legendary Size for this charm; turning into a gigantic beast is never a trivial task.
                                      This charm may be purchased once for each true animal form (that doesn't have Legendary Size) that the Lunar has.

                                      Intimate Training Recollection
                                      Cost: -; Mins: Wits 3, Essence 1; Type: Permanent
                                      Keywords: None; Duration: Permanent
                                      Prerequisite Charms: None

                                      It is difficult for a Lunar to translate his skills into animal form: a human's brawling skill is completely different from the bite of a dog, and jumping with two legs is different from jumping with four. Lunars normally make up with this by animal instinct, but this charm allows them to combine this with their human skill.
                                      When making rolling a physical dice pool or using a physical derived value, such as Evasion or Soak, the Lunar may use their own dice pool or value, or that of the animal form, whichever is better.

                                      Twin-Faced Hero
                                      Cost: -; Mins: Wits 3, Essence 1; Type: Permanent
                                      Keywords: None; Duration: Permanent
                                      Prerequisite Charms: None

                                      The Lunar may choose to change the gender of any of his forms. As well as male and female, the Lunar may take an androgynous neuter form. This is chosen whenever he takes a shapeshifting action.

                                      Hybrid Body Rearrangement
                                      Cost: 1m per merit dot; Mins: Wits 2, Essence 1 Type: Permanent
                                      Keywords: None; Duration: Indefinite
                                      Prerequisite Charms: None

                                      A dedicated Lunar can learn to shift just part of his body instead of all of it. On his turn, he may reflexively gain any of the following supernatural merits: Chameleon, Claws/Fangs/Hooves/Horns, Enhanced Sense, Gills, Quills, and Tail. These last until he decommits the essence or shapeshifts.
                                      However, he may only gain these merits if this expresses a part of one the forms from his Heart's Blood library. For example, a Lunar who knows the forms of a bat, a wolf, a bear and a hawk could gain appropriate Enhanced Senses, Tail and Claws, but could not gain Quills or Gills.

                                      Perfected Hybrid Interaction
                                      Cost: -; Mins: Wits 3, Essence 2; Type: Permanent
                                      Keywords: None; Duration: Permanent
                                      Prerequisite Charms: Hybrid Body Rearrangement

                                      This charm upgrades its prerequisite, allowing the Lunar to make more substantial changes to his body. It adds Extra Limbs, Unusual Hide and Wings to the supernatural merits he may buy with Hybrid Body Rearrangement. As with the prerequisite, these must still express one of his Heart's Blood forms.
