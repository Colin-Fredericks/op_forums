    Okay, you’re using more homebrew. One thing you should probably take into account, though, is that the more unseen assumptions you fold into a work, the more confusion is going to be experienced by your audience.

    Also, just as a reminder, Magnitude as a system term now refers to the health track of a battle group, not any specific quantity of people.

                                                    Huh. A thing I just realized. Something really funny. Or really dark.

                                                    So let's say that Pacifica wants a sister. Maybe its a thing that she heard from her parents, how they wish for another child but couldn't afford the 9 month pregnancy and the resulting costs. Maybe its because she watches someone else play, and thinks it'll be cool to have a younger sibling she can be the big boss of. Maybe it was simply an idle thought.

                                                    But she wouldn't be a 15 year old if she wasn't impulsive. So she grabs a CRISPR kit, a DIY thing that's really only meant to interfere with yeast or E. coli. But its now in the hands of a solar, which means bad things for everyone. The CRISPR is enough to act as a tool for craft (genesis), along with petri dishes, microscopes, and numerous other things. What comes out is basically a cell taken from her own eggs, the DNA is interfered with, and she goes to town on altering the genomes and casting sorcerous workings, pushing her excellency and sorcery charms and medicine supernal to reduce anything she viewed as 'flaws' and added a few new things. Like several dozen new enzymes, removal of the appendix, a revamp of the nervous system, regenerative powers for both cartilage, connective tissue, and telomeres, the power to make her own vitamin C, constant neuroplasticity, enhanced multitasking, and, well, someone who is basically captain america +

                                                    And then, she cultured the cells. And then gave it to a Neomah. Which, in case you didn't know, is basically a flesh vat.

                                                    Its a thought that came to me, because solars and teenagers are never that great at considering the ramifications of their actions. And, well, in a world where there is already scientific debate over genetic engineering, and there was already an outcry over modifying human embryos to be resistant to HIV, what's the reaction going to be to someone who not only crossed over the line, but rushed over it in a burning motorcycle, leaving behind a trail of fire?
