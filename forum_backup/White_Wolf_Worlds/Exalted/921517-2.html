                        I would like warstriders to be very cool, but very expensive. Not only in motes/evocations, but in support and maintenance costs. Like, a warstrider burns 1 Gold Xp per turn of combat. This way, the Warstrider can be very much worth it when used wisely, but not a fix-all and replacement for everything else. Also, it makes useful to get a sense that 'it takes a village' to make a warstrider work. The Dawn who rides it dependes on the twilight who will keep it running, depends on the eclipse to get the materials, the night to steal them, the Zenith to comandeer them, and so on. It also makes it so that the pilot cannot be on the armor all the time, and has a reason to fight on his own.

                        Even then evocations could make it so that the maintenance costs are mitigated or outright removed, for a large investiment, of course.

                                            Originally posted by NuSoardGraphite View Post
                                            I want warstriders to feel and play like this:

                                            https://youtu.be/YK38WzlNvfk
                                            ​Hmm, I need to go back and re-watch Escaflowne some time, and continue it to the end; I had forgotten how thrilling it is.

                                            Thinking more on it, I think Totentanz expressed the right point about warstriders having powers that warrant having them, but I still think what those actual powers are is the mouse that is not given much thought to tying the bell on the cat.

                                            I think warstriders exist in an odd space in Exalted, in that they're things that emulate genre fiction of giant robots or magic armour or what have you, but that fiction tends not to attribute superhuman powers to the actual pilot (excepting things that actually aid the piloting such as improved reflexes or some kind of prescience). Part of the issue with the Exalted is that they can already be mighty in a manner that most inspirational fiction requires you to don the suit to be able to get at. That's why I think that the prior generic warstrider enhancement of greater strength doesn't cut it; it would be like giving Superman a giant mech.

                                            At the same time, while I want its powers to have something more, I think them varying too wildly makes the actual aesthetic of the warstrider itself feel irrelevant and perfunctory; you've got a set of generic weapons and powers hanging off of a platform that incidentally resembles a human. It's similar to issues I've had with manses, and even some Artifacts.

                                            So I'm trying to think of what would really make the warstrider; what its image would be, so that one could devise powers that would be evocative of that image.

                                            For the moment, I'm thinking of something like the warstrider as being a bit like a monument, in terms of the oversized image of a human as something to inspire awe. The warstrider as a massive icon of authority, of martial valour, the icon of a god of war striding majestically across the battlefield. It's not just a collection of weapons hanging off of a large suit of armour that an Exalt must command from the inside, it is the Exalted writing themselves large. Within a single warstrider, you can get an image with more variety and nuance than the most specific characters of daiklaves and similar, so a red jade warstrider as a fiery icon of battle an Exalt can elevate themselves into would have more avenues of expression than something like Volcano Cutter's direct violent eruptions, so there's a measure of balance between them even if the warstrider is technically more powerful (and may have a heavier fiery apocalypse type of attack).

                                            Think Doctor Manhatten walking as a giant through the burning fields of Vietnam.
