                            Horned to Hornless, Hornless to Horned

                            Cost: 6m Mins: Larceny 4, Essence 2
                            Type: Simple
                            Keyword: None
                            Duration: Until the sapient subject sleeps, or indefinite for non-sapient subject
                            Prerequisite Charms: Flawless Impenetrable Disguise

                            The Solar may apply the effects of Flawless Impenetrable Disguise to willing subjects, or docile/trained non-sapient subjects (making one animal look entirely different, disguising its color, adding or hiding horns, giving it a thick coat, scales, etc). The Solar must commit the 6 motes for the duration of the disguise's effect, and they may only have as many active disguises beyond their own equal to their Essence. A disguise on a non-sapient animal will last indefinitely, but will collapse once someone sees through the disguise or the Solar uncommites the motes. The animal's noises, stance, etc are covered by the illusion, but the Charm cannot compel an animal's behavior. A cow disguised as a large ferocious lion may start grazing when it is hungry, threatening the disguise to onlookers.

                            This Charm may be repurchased an unlimited number of times starting at Essence 3. Each repurchase allows for [Essence] more disguises to be maintained at a time.

                            The subjects of a Solar Master of Disguise are not capable of the true mastery of the art of impersonation she is capable of. The upgrade effects of Perfect Mirror do not apply to disguises upon others.

                                        Given Vance's comment on Peacock Shadow Eyes, it's interesting to compare the current version to the 2e version.

                                        2e Peacock Shadow Eyes indeed totally ignored social Abilities and replaced them with a Wits+Occult roll; the 3e version still requires actual social skills to make good use of the spell, although the spell certainly makes things easier in several ways.
