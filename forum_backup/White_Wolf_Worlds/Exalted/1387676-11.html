                        Full Moons
                        • Get two physical attributes favoured, being the only caste that can favour all three, or alternatively favour two and have flexibility favouring a mental and a social
                        • Gain a bonfire power of bonus (Essence or 3) soak, which stacks with armor, or for a turn for 5 motes.
                        • Gain a bonus (Essence) non-charm dice to all rolls to Rush, Disengage, vault a wall, tear down a tower, oppose an enemy rush, oppose an enemy disengage, etc.
                        • 1/day when you make a decisive attack you can pay 10m 1wp to instead roll join battle with 5 non-charm bonus dice, which is killer.

                        No Moons
                        • For 5 motes impose a -2 to hit penalty against most enemies for the turn, or free at bonfire.



                        You might as well be saying that Full Moons are the OTB social build because they can add 3 non-charm resolve to any threaten rolls or fear based influence, for free, forever. Lunars are the most flexible of all the exalted anyway, if there's anyone you'd expect to be able to crossover a bit from one caste to another, it's Lunars.

                            Originally posted by limaxophobiac View Post
                            I can't think of any charms that let you ignore generalized attack penalties, and while DBs and Solars have some Awareness charms that let you ignore visual penalties on Awareness rolls you are generally not rolling attacks with Awareness.
                            The Lunar anima power specifies that it's a visual penalty, and plenty of attack Abilities have charms to ignore those. Solars have Sight Without Eyes in Archery, Lunars have Sense-Sharpening Change and Heightened Sense Method in Perception, and Dragon-Blooded have Unobstructed Hunter's Aim in Archery (which is their Excellency, so it's quite basic). Also, the only restriction about using charms from other Abilities is that you can't generally activate them when you're making a roll with a given Ability. If a Solar has already activated Keen Sight Technique before making her attack, I'd certainly allow the bit about "reduce the difficulty of seeing" from visual hindrances to apply to the penalty. And I would allow Unsurpassed Taste and Smell Discipline and Unsurpassed Hearing and Touch Discipline to both function as "targeting senses", within Close range at least, allowing someone who had them active to target enemies non-visually without penalty, getting around the Lunar anima power completely.

                            Originally posted by limaxophobiac
                            Even if you rule that they do apply on account of the rules for when charms can or can't apply to rolls with different abilities being a mess, that still leaves it a strong combat power against the vast majority of enemies who either arent' exalts or exalts that don't have those specific charms.
                            Sure, it's a decent anima power, no argument there. But, again, the Full Moons aren't necessarily supposed to be the "oops, all warriors!" caste anyway, and they still have more, and better anima powers relating to combat. In 3e Exalted, having a good defense is nice in a fight, certainly, but it won't win you the fight either - you've got to be able to land hits of your own. Good defense alone is, at best, good for making it a no-score draw. If you took two Lunars, one a Full Moon and the other a No Moon, gave them no combat charms whatsoever, and equal investment in combat Abilities, I'd bet on the Full Moon every time, just based on anima abilities.

                                I think the once-per-day power to roll Join Battle with non-Charm extra dice rather than reset to base power alone is a huge deal for Full Moon killing power, even with that cost.

                                Thought about the Tell:

                                Even with the notion that it has its own natural concealment, I feel as though the Tell goes along a bit with visual media in which somebody is shapeshifted, possessed or even just undergoing some personal issues which carry a visual element of ambiguous diegesis.

                                Just as a way of picturing a scene in which a character is going around with sharper features and sideburns when they had none before, and nobody seems to notice until that key moment when somebody does (and puts it together that it indicates a shapeshifter). It's more satisfying to me than picturing it as something outright illusionary that melts away.

                                Does it 100% make sense? No, but I feel as though modern readers have drifted away from the idea of something supernatural having qualities that don't quite flow logically. Like, don't even break down some specific mechanism to it, just embrace the idea that a shapeshifted Lunar can have a slightly compromised disguise that isn't actually compromised 90% of the time just because.

                                Or if one must, think of it as just one little instance in which dealing with a Lunar is like engaging with something in a dream.
