                            Originally posted by Ranger_Lord View Post
                            If you don't find an NPC in a setting entertaining you are under no obligation to utilize that NPC.
                            Well, first, I think that powerful NPCs in a setting like Creation, in a system with the themes of Exalted, should be written to maximize use across as many hypothetical games as possible. Not just assume that every ST will use Ma-Ha-Suchi (for example) as a insane-body-horror boss fight. Some STs will use him for a boss fight and in such a circumstance a detailed stat block is useful. Some groups will want to enter into alliances or bargains or try to trick and subvert his goals, and for them a combat-oriented statblock or a one-dimensional write-up of "this guy is an Obvious Villain, note the obvious villainy he has done" is a lot less useful than a clear statement of what fires this guy has his irons in. The game should orient to provide maximum value to both of those extremes and anything in between.

                            For a game like Exalted, where Big Bads typically are assumed to have more depth and deeper goals than "evil wizard hiding in a dungeon sending zombies and skeletons up to kill people to have more bodies with which to make skeletons and zombies, because evil wizards are dicks, something something Orcus" then I think it's really valuable for the written game books to be written broadly enough that there can be all kinds of plots written for Raksi other than "wow she sucks and is a baby-eating psycho, murder Raksi kthx." It doesn't matter if you ultimately use her for a boss fight. You're basically suggesting "I did this and this was fun ergo this is what the default should be." And then you're calling other people arrogant and presumptive?

                            It's easy enough for an ST to look at a hypothetical long and nuanced depiction of Raksi, her past, and her goals (or Ma-Ha, or Chejop, or for that matter Dace or Iron Siaka or whoever) and say "nahhhh I don't need any of this, I need a boss fight to cap off this arc, and Raksi fits well enough to be behind the Bad Shit the PCs have been preventing." Please note that nobody is saying that using an NPC like that is BadWrongFun.

                            However it's less easy for an ST to look at a statblock and a weak paragraph of one-dimensional motivations that are all focused on "this person is Eeeeeevil with a capital E, see look at her eating babies, look at her daddy issues, that's all there is that we care to say about it" and determine how else they can use this person except as a boss fight?

                            Hell, in settings where there is evil more objective than baby-eating (see: Glorantha, with entities/groups like Ralzakark, Delecti, Sheng Seleris, the God Learners...) the setting presumes that other entities in-setting have reasons to truck with them (if only to keep them busy or redirect them, or be the dog on your leash instead of the wolf at your door). The inherent assumption is that PCs can be involved in those devils' deals and further them or work to kill these monsters, and the monsters' write-ups plausibly support either, depending on what faction your group is affiliated with. But the default is there (the Lunar Empire says "sure that guy is the greatest and most powerful and objectively-evil monsters ever to exist, who sucked at the teat of one of the greatest evil gods to ever walk the earth, and we could divert our resources to kill him, buuuut he keeps the other 40,000 broos in Dorastor in line and he actually abides by his contracts...sure, we can kill him at our leisure if we choose to send an army into that hellhole, but right now he's not near as high on the priority list as stamping out rebellions on the route to our newly-conquered giant port").

                            You have moral choice, action, and consequence all in there together. Kill Ralzakark and suddenly you have 40,000 chaos monsters with no terribly-feared leader to keep them in line and you've well and proper fucked every region that borders Dorastor. Leave him be and he's literally a cancer on the world but you effectively have all those broos in check just hanging out, posing less of an immediate danger to you than the numerous, successful barbarian insurgencies along the trade routes. Hell, negotiate better terms to that agreement and he might even help you with your barbarian problem since he fucking hates their gods.

                            That's the kind of shit Exalted should have, especially with its Big Name NPCs. Having many options to pick from is better than "let's just write Raksi as a stereotypical evil thing to Boss Fight," even if that's what you wind up using her for and have fun doing it. How do you even argue that providing more options to make an NPC more useful to more groups is not an inherently better state? Having Raksi be evil in a vacuum is just pretty dumb and doesn't provide help for any STs except those who decide to use her as a boss fight. A write up only conducive to use as a dungeon crawl is pretty much worthless unless you intend to do a dungeon crawl, and that sucks for anyone hoping for tips on how to run Raksi as anything other than someone you can unrepentantly murder without feeling bad about it.
