                Celestial 2: Hiding from the eyes of heaven

                Made by a savant that had become paranoid after several attempted assasinations. Especially one which nearly killed him, were it not for a sacrifice for one of his friends. Towards the end of his life, he became convinced that someone was watching him through scrying. And he decided to do something about it.

                At baseline level, the one with this power becomes immune to scrying and other esoteric and weird ways of attacking and viewing. Attempts to attack him via sympathetic magic or dreams fail. Remote viewing shows a blur. Attempting to divine his fate through the stars show nonsense. Upon interacting with Fate and Sidereals, the sorcerer slots seamlessly with the loom... at first glance. Only in close inspection does the sidereal realize that something is wrong.

                With the expenditure of one willpower, the sorcerer can activate one of these following effects:
                • Extend this effect to (Occult X 2) beings
                • Ward a room with this effect

                                        After weeks of genealogical research and editing, the sorcerer blesses his mortal followers (a group that would, if they formed a battle group, possess a Magnitude no greater than the sorcerer's Essence) in a grand ceremony, formally adopting them in the eyes of Heaven. Alternatively, the sorcerer may incorporate a willing spirit or other supernatural being into the ritual, allowing their ally to adopt the mortals instead. (If the sorcerer himself is mortal, the inclusion of a different parent is essential for the ritual to have any substantial effect.)

                                        In any case, the mortals become, essentially, "God-Blooded" of the sort that they would have been had the sorcerer (or other adoptive parent) been one of their actual parents. They can be expected to gain a small number of Merits and/or Charms appropriate to both their new "parent's" nature and their own abilities or inclinations, making them above-average as God-Blooded go (even if the adoptive parent isn't powerful enough to normally sire such potent God-Blooded).

                                        If the sorcerer is Dragon-Blooded...I guess the mortals become elemental God-Blooded or something? I didn't really think that part through.

                                        Here's an example of an elite soldier adopted by a Solar sorcerer. I admittedly wrote this up in a bit of a hurry -- I would have liked to come up with something more interesting for the Charms.

                                        Sun-Blessed Soldier

                                        Essence: 2; Willpower: 6; Join Battle: 10 dice
                                        Personal Motes: 20
                                        Health Levels: -0/-1x3/-2x3/-4/Incap.
                                        Actions: Read Intentions: 6 dice; Senses: 9 dice; Threaten: 8 dice
                                        Appearance 3, Resolve 4, Guile 2

                                        Combat
                                        Attack (Slashing sword): 12 dice (Damage 13, minimum 2)
                                        Attack (Self bow): 11 dice at short range (Damage 10)
                                        Combat Movement: 7 dice
                                        Evasion 3, Parry 5
                                        Soak/Hardness: 10/0 (heavy armor and augmented toughness)

                                        Merits:

                                        Filial Piety: As long as the sun-blessed soldier maintains a positive Tie to the sorcerer who "adopted" him, that Tie cannot be changed or eliminated by non-magical means.

                                        Radiant Martial Splendor: A battle group composed of sun-blessed soldiers has Might 1, and adds their Essence (normally 2) in dice on all rout checks.

                                        Charms:

                                        Brilliant Flashing Blade (3m or 5m; Supplemental; Instant; Uniform; Essence 1): For three motes, the sun-blessed soldier adds two dice to a physical attack roll with any weapon, or +1 Evasion or Parry against a single attack. If attacking or being attacked by a creature of darkness, the soldier may instead add four dice or +2 Defense for five motes.

                                        Luminous Veteran's Toughness (5m; Reflexive; Instant; Uniform; Essence 1): After being struck with a withering or decisive attack, the sun-blessed soldier may reduce the raw damage of the attack by two.

                                        I'm thinking this would be an Ambition 3 Celestial working? A large battle group of these soldiers wouldn't be any match for a combat-focused Second Circle demon, but if you have a variety of followers, they might have a fair number of interesting (albeit minor) abilities between them.
