                                    Sea Bishop(Aboleth)
                                    Demon of the First Cirlce of Piscaethces
                                    Creature of the great depths of Kimbery, the aboleths are reculsive and feared among demonkind for their habit of adopting any demons they find into their vast undersea kingdoms.. Slime covered three eyed whales lurk here, feeding on those foolish enough to draw close to their borders and be more interesting than is for their own good.
                                    The most feared and famed feature of the aboleths is their mucus. The mucus produced by the aboleths has multiple uses to alchemists, but a few famous ones for the aboleths themselves. First, it acts as a preservative against the harm of the acid sea and allows their favored servants to breath water, both from creation and Kimbery. The process also weakens those who are struck by it, their skin becoming amorphous and more suited for undersea life. And lastly, it keeps the aboleth itself alive and concealed as it goes about its buisness.
                                    Aboleths maintain an empire by swimming through the canals and rivers and near the islands of Kimbery, grasping and capturing other demons for aquatic use. The gaze of the aboleth is haunting and peircing, driving those who meet it made or into a fugue state of subservience. Those who brave the depths of their kingdom find it covered in spiraled and domed structures, full of holes designed for swimming creatures. The brave may also learn that aboleths do not cease growing—truly prodigous beasts can be found in the deepest of depths.
                                    The purpose the Blood Queen made the Aboleth’s for is long lost. They themselves assert it is to bring order to the depths of Kimbery, to fill the sea floor with both knowledge and function of vast cities. The aboleth’s capacity to recall events by past relatives and of its prey indicate that there is some truth to this, and their obssessive searching for knowledge and power knows few rivals.
                                    Sorcerers summon aboleths for all manner of reasons: Some for their knowledge of long forgotten relics and power, some for their capacity as slavers and builders, some for their fighting prowess on the waves or as exotic transport. Aboleths resent their bounds, however, and often seek to destroy records of their existence from memory. Once in a long while, when a coral reef fades from life and is covered in molds, an aboleth may enter creation unbound.

                                    Essence: 3 Willpower: 7 Join Battle: 9
                                    Personal Motes: 80
                                    Health Levels: 2x0/8x-1/8x-2/2x-4/Incap
                                    Speed Bonus:+3 (-1 out of water)
                                    Actions: Senses 10, Intimidate 12, Read Intentions 12, Instill 8, Stealth 9, Demonic or Aquatic Lore 12, Directing Slaves 10(12 if Skum).
                                    Resolve 6 Guile 3(See Alien Mind)
                                    Combat:
                                    Attack (Bite): 8 dice (10 damage)
                                    Attack(Tentacle) 12 dice (14 damage)
                                    Attack(Grapple) 10 dice (12 control)
                                    Combat Movement: 10 dice (3 dice out of water)
                                    Soak:13 / Hardness: 5
                                    Parry 4 Evasion 3(5 in water)
                                    Combat Charms:
                                    Tendril of Muck (7m, Supplemental, Instant, Essence 2): Attacks by aboleth have a contagion slime attached, that makes even hardened shells weak. On a successful attack supplemented by this charm, the target must make a (Stamina+Resistance) roll against Difficulty 4. If the target fails, reduce their targets natural soak by 1. If this reduces the targets soak to 0, the target takes a 1 hl of damage every turn unless they are full immersed. This effect ceases after one hour.
                                    Social Charms
                                    Dominating Crimson Gaze(4m(+4m), 1wp, Supplemental, Instant, Essence 3): The Sea Bishop can communicate the most primeval of ocean born thoughts with but a gaze. This charm allows the Sea Bishop to preform a social action with just a gaze. After the first success in a scene, the Sea Bishop enjoys double 9s on all influence relying on its gaze for 4 additional motes.
                                    Miscellaneous Charms:
                                    Mucus Cloud(6m, Simple, Instant, Eclipse, Essence 2): By spreading its cloud a bit farther out, the sea bishop allows everyone out to close range to breath underwater, at the cost of no longer being able to breath on land. This process can be reversed with a second activation of this charm. Those who wish to avoid the mucus cloud can do so by making a Dexterity+Dodge check, difficulty 2.
                                    Skum of Conversion(8m, 1wp; Ritual;Essence 3): The aboleth can coat a restrained or willing target in it’s slime for 5 hours+Essence, converting them into a ulat-kini. Ulat-kini are mechanically identical to their earlier forms, except they gain 2 points of natural soak, a major intimacy of loyatly to the sea bishop, and the ability to breath underwater or on land. For purposes of diesease and posions resistance, ulat-kini are treated as Exalted.
                                    Consulting the Depths of the Mind(6m, Simple, Instant, Essence 3): The sea bishop can consult the memories of its ancestors and prey, entering a meditative trance for one hour. It gains double 8s to establish fact with any speciality it has, and may attempt to establish or contest facts outside its expertise at a -2 penalty.
                                    Materialize:
                                    Hurry Home:
                                    Measure of the Wind: An aboleth can determine the supernatural nature of any being in contact with its slime.
                                    Merits:
                                    Alien Mind: Aboleth’s are particular difficult to understand and probe. When dealing with read intentions efforts that seek to understand their intimacies, the Aboleth’s guile rises to 5.
                                    Legendary Size: The Aboleth suffers no onslaught penalties from attacks from smaller opponents, unless they’re magically inflicted. Withering attacks from smaller enemies cannot crash it unless they have at least 10 post-soak damagedie. Decisive attacks from smaller opponents cannot deal more than (3+Attacker’s Strength) levels of damage to it, not counting levels added by charms or other magic.
                                    Depth Lurker: Any character attempting to notice the aboleth while it is deep below them subtracts three success from any Perception-based rolls they make to do so. In combat, this applies as long as the aboleth is at long range under the water relative to the character, or if it is moving up from long range while already hidden. It may also rush enemies from long range beneath them underwater, as long as the horizontal distance between the two is no more than one range band.
                                    Four Tendrils: The Sea Bishop may flurry two attacks in a row, at a -3 penalty. These attacks maybe launched at different opponents.

                                    Mindflayer (Ilithid)
                                    First Circle Demon of the Orderly Cerebellum
                                    In the depths of Malfean buildings, in woven structures of broken crystal, dwell the ilithid. In cities that bear a passing resemblance to a broken skull of stone, the illithids lurk in darkness searching for their ever rare prey.
                                    All ilithids begin their life as loose thoughts of the Orderly Cerebellum, a pusling brain that is part cyrstal part secreted thoughts. These looser thoughts become strange tadpole like entities, which the Orderly Cerebellum feasts on. Those that escape the cannibal instincts of the higher soul survive and are eventually taken up by their older cousins. At this point, an illitihid is little more than an empheral substance. The existing ilithids, however, place the strange creature in a body that has been hollowed out for it. The tadpole takes the place of an upper soul in the victims body, and gradually replaces the mortal flesh with demon skin, until only the hungry soul of their victim remains for the tadpole to feast on. The head of the victim gains eyes as black as the malfean sky and a set of four tendrils around a beaked mouth.
                                    Th newly finished ilithid lurks in the cities and spaces of the Demon City looking for prey. Their hunger is for souls more than all else, even if demon’s essence will do just as well in a pinch. Sorcerers who call upon Mindflayers are warned to observe them carefully, for to the ilithid creation is one great safari.
                                    Ilithids are often called upon for their role as hunters, their capacity as socialites, and their talent for coordination. Even then, rare is the sorcerer who conjures one for long—the feast of an ilithid or two can become both noticable and tiresome. Occasionally they are summoned as overseers of projects where the sacfrice of hundreds of such prey is acceptable.
                                    Essence:2 Willpower:6 Join Battle:8
                                    Personal Motes: 70
                                    Health Levels: 2x0/3x-1/3x-2/1x-4/Incap
                                    Actions: Senses 9; Stealth 12(14 in dark); Social Influence 10; Occult Lore 6; Tracking 10; Directing slaves 9(14 if lower souls); Read Intentions 10.
                                    Resolve 4 Guile 4
                                    Combat Actions
                                    Attack(Unarmed) 8 dice (10 damage)
                                    Attack(Bite): 6 dice (15 damage)
                                    Attack(Grapple): 7 dice (10 control)
                                    Combat Movement: 8 dice
                                    Soak 8 Hardness 0
                                    Parry 3 Evasion 3
                                    Merits:
                                    Night Vision: Ilithids take no penalties to awareness for darkness or lowlight.
                                    Combat Charms
                                    Send Forth The Captive Soul(6m;Simple;Instant;Essence 2): The mindlfayer sends forth the startling screams of its hosts lower soul, paralyzing beings nearby with fear of their own mortality as lambs to the cosmic slaughter.
                                    The Mindflayer targets one opponent out to short range and rolls 10 dice against the Resolve of all enemies within close range of the target. Those who’s resolve is overcome take a -3 penalty to all actions for rounds equally to the successes the mindflayer had. This charm maybe used once per scene.

                                    Feast On the Cerebellum(5m;Supplemental; Decisive only; Instant;Essence 2): The tendrils of the ilithid are especially suited for digging into the heads of their opponent, tearing away at flesh and bone to reach their food. This charm supplements a decisive savage action, giving it double 10s. If the opponent is killed with this charm, their upper soul is devoured, leaving their lower soul inhabiting their body.

                                    Iron Grip(2m;Supplemental;Instant;Essence 1): The illithid grips fast to it’s prey, difficult to dislodge no matter the tools. This charm supplements a control roll, giving the ilithid double 10s on the control roll.

                                    Social Charm

                                    Command the Lowly Beast (2m;Supplemental;Instant;Essence 2): The ilithid can command lower souls easily, its tendrils stirring the hungers of those below.
                                    This charm supplements any persuade action by the mindflayer that uses an intimacy of desire or want, increasing the bonus die for using the intimacy by 2. The mindflayer may also use this charm to command beings that lack an upper soul without opposition.

                                    Sifting the Mind(4m;Supplemental;Instant;Essence 2): The mindflayer’s soul is only lightly tethered to its host, and it is easy enough for such a faint connection to wander into still living bodies for its purposes. This charm allows a mindflayer to preform a social influence action on a target out to long range silently. The target is aware of the influnce, but may not be aware of th source if the mindflayer is properly hidden (Say, by being immaterial).

                                    Misc. Charms

                                    Predatory Convulence(3m;Supplemental; Ritual; One Scene;Essence 2): A group of three or more mindflayers is necessary to preform this charm. Preforming a ritual dance recorded in the Sargassoan Archive that sits around the Orderly Cerebellum, the illithids weave a pseudo-Cerebellum. Doing so allows them to combine their efforts and coordinate their actions for a number of weeks equal to the number of participants. Over that period, the mindflayers gain 3 bonus die on all actions and share knowledge perfectly, regardless of distance.

                                    Materialize:
                                    Hurry Home:
                                    Measure of the Wind: An illitid can determine the supernatural nature of any being that comes into contact with it’s tendrils.

                                    The Tyrannical Witnesses (Geosthian)
                                    Demon of the First Cirlce, of the Blazing Eye
                                    The geosthian are strange race even among demons. Resmebling floating cyclopean heads with a number of eyestalks jutting out of its sides, each Geosthian is distinct from its peers in some form or another. All geosthian further claim to be the original, from which all others are failed derivatives. When two of the demons meet, an astonishing amount of violence ensues.
                                    When not attacking each other, the Tyrannical Witnesses serve as bullies of other members of demon kind and sculptors of strange structures by destroying sections with their gaze. They are generally egoists, and each believes its mission of shaping and destruction to be a direct will of their progenitor.
                                    Sorcerers call upon the demon as a guardian for the many curses inlaid behind the beholders—and among those aware of it, the gaze of the Tyrannical Witness is fearsome. Those sorcerers worried about rivals may conjure the strange demon for it’s uncanny abillity to decay the very essence of the world while nascent. Of course, the Tyrannical Witnesses attitude and bearing is still a detraction, and rumors abound that geosthian plan the down fall of sorcerers who bind them from Malfeas.
                                    Essence: 3 Willpower: 6 Join Battle: 10
                                    Personal Motes: 80
                                    Health Levels: x0/x-1/x-2/x-4/Incap
                                    Speed Bonus: +4
                                    Actions: Intimidate 10, Feats of Strength 4(with mouth), Sense 10 (see All Eyes), Sense Motive 8,
                                    Resolve 4 Guile 5
                                    Combat Actions
                                    Attack(Bite): 8 dice
                                    Attack (Eyebeams) 10 dice at Short (14 damage, Thrown)
                                    Attack(Grapple): 6 dice (8 dice for control)
                                    Parry 2 Evasion 5
                                    Soak:10 Hardness: 2
                                    Combat Movement: 8 dice
                                    Merits:
                                    All Eyes: The many eyes of the tyrannical witness give it double 8s on all awareness checks related to vision. The geosthian does not benefit from double 10s on non-vision checks, lacking many refined senses.
                                    Flight: The Tyrannical Witness can fly as easily as others can walk.

                                    Combat Charms:
                                    Eye of Fright(4m, Supplemental, Instant, Essence 1): Opening one of it’s floating eyes to send forth a fearful gaze along with it’s firey bolt, the Geosthian may reflexively roll an intimidate action after the attack. If the attack was successful, the Geosthian gains bonus die on the intimidate action equal to the amount it beat the target’s defense.
                                    Eye of Flame(6m, Supplmental, Dual, Instant, Essence 2): The third eye of the tyranical witness is one of wasting. Supplementing a decisive attack, this grants the geosthian double 10s. On a withering attack, the geosthian gains double 9s to damage.
                                    Eye of Slumber(7m, Supplemental, Decsive Only, Instant, Essence 3): The fourth eye of the tyranical witness gazes into the houses of slumber. This charm allows the Geosthian to attempt a custom gambit at difficulty 4. On a success, the Geosthian has poisoned the body of the victim with a poison identical to snake venom. On crash, however, the target falls unconscious instead of taking lethal damage.
                                    Eye of Stone(6m, Supplemental, Uniform, Instant, Essence 2): The fifth eye of the Tyrannical witness contains the curse of stone. A successful attack supplemented by this charm inflicts a -2 penalty to the targets movement actions and evasion until the target makes a feat of strength difficulty 3 or 5 turns past.
                                    Eye of the Sorcerers Doom(8m, Reflexive, Indefinite, Essence 3): The centeral eye of the tyrannical witness is its most feared among sorcerers and scholars. For the gaze of that great center eye calcifies the flow of essence in the sorcerers hands.
                                    Every turn while this charm is active, the Tyrannical Witness may select on sorcerer to target with its central eye. It drains 8 dice of sorcerous motes a turn. This does not count as an action for the turn. Alternatively, the Tyrannical Witness may opt to attempt to disrupt the spell with the same dice pool.
                                    Array of Doom(10m, 1wp; Simple, Instant Essence 3): A tyranical witness has the advantage of its gaze being deadly and a plethora of eyes. With a use of this charm, it puts that to use.
                                    After activating this charm, the Tyrannical witness can make an additional attack this turn, as long as it activates a new eye charms for each attack. All attacks past the first suffer a -3 penalty.

                                    Misc. Charms
                                    Eye of Age(4m, Supplemental, Instant, Essence 2): The second eye of the tyrannical witness bears the marks of misery and decay. The Geosthian may preform a feat of demolition with it’s intimidate pool instead of its feat of strength pool.

                                        Javito, the cleansing muck
                                        Progeny of the living tower

                                        Hear that clang? Hear that wet, sucking noise. The lost demon within the hallway of Octavian shudders in fear. He had been injured while working, and had fled rather than face the overseers. Now he was lost.

                                        He turned around, looking for the source of the noise.

                                        Then he died.

                                        The Javito is a strange creature, composed out of shining liquid, its transparent form allowing people to see through it. It is the opposite of the metody. It does not corrode. It repairs and restores. The Javito eats away at corruption, damage, and entropy. As it slides through the great war-factories of Octavian, it fixes machinery worn out by hard work and rust. As it consumes swords and spits them out, it changes them from rusty, chipped, and broken pieces into pristine shining blades. Broken rubble and cracked walls become whole again.

                                        Of course, this is not without drawbacks, for the javito have certain bad habits. One of these, is how they attempt to repair living things. Upon contact with a living creature that has 1 or 2 levels of lethal damage and an open wound, the Javito leaps in to heal and repair them. But since the charm that lets them phase through solid pieces to let them repair the inner workings only work on unliving matter. So when the Javito attempts to heal living creatures, it simply kills them.
