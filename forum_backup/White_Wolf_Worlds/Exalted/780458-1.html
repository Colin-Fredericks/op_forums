                The most interesting thing about this particular part of the Sidereals job is that you can't pummel it into submission with Essence expenditure.

                I do not mean to imply that the other Exalt types are without depth, to forestall that accusation. Far from it! But the sort of problems their powersets are designed to deal with are, you know, comprehensible and often resolvable via direct action and application of their puissance. Even the sort of head-aching political imbroglios you can end up with in, say, a Dragon-Blooded game with a heavy Game of Thrones style focus on the vacant throne generally present problems where, mechanically speaking, you can just apply the appropriate amount of Essence via the appropriate Charms with a reasonable degree of success. A lot of problems Solars have are damned interesting and fun and have a lot of depth, but Solars are designed to be super crazy awesome and can usually overcome most problems by just hitting it with their Solarness until it stops being a problem.

                The Sidereals can do this as well. The parts of their job that have heavy overlap with the sort of problems other Exalt types routinely encounter (needing to solve a political dispute, someone has to die, how can I solve the mystery of the Jade Monkey by the next full moon, etc) they have their own avenues they can use to Essence them into not being problems anymore. When Iron Siaka needs to prevent a demon from slipping into Creation she can pick up her mace, beat it into a fine pink mist, and then have makeouts. When they need to make a political enemy go away they can use subtle magics to dismay their targets allies, that sort of thing. A lot of what they do is very standard...

                ... except when they're wearing their Fate Troubleshooter hats. How the hell do you solve a problem like "there are now three Sesus Rafara's each apparently running on their own individualized destiny track" or "this guy dies every evening and then in the morning wakes up normally in bed. Nobody remembers him dying except himself and this one other weird guy we suspect might be the lobotomized remains of a crippled god from the time-before-time" or any of the other examples extant in this thread with direct action? You can't.

                The problems are clearly solvable but they're real headaches both in-universe and at the table, which is what makes them interesting and fun.
