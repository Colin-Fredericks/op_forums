                                05-04-2018, 09:54 PM
                                Okay well either way lets take a look at the immaculate martial arts!

                                First of all just a few quick things that help, one the styles have elemental charms. Not only do the style use them themselves, but that means they can generate aura for the other charms as well, like say if you needed earth aura for a resistance charm you could use Earth Dragon the previous turn. Next is they lack both the terrestrial and mastery keywords, meaning that not only are they not worse for DBs but they aren't better for Solars either. Speaking of, DBs are the only terrestrials who can learn them. There's also a cool ability for DBs who've learned the form charm to ignore the terrestrial keyword for any MA by expending their aura. It's cool and definitely allows for a relatively easy route to power without making it just ignore the keyword all together.

                                BIG thing to remember before I get into it though, and that's the MA excellency. When I did the short ability previews I almost made a section on each for their excellency ability but it ended up too long. The MA one allows you to block/make lethal attacks with any bare handed attack, and also reroll damage 6s until they fail to appear. That means basically every single MA attack that I''m about to talk about also has "reroll 6s on damage until they fail to appear" for free.

                                Anyway onto the actual styles

                                Air Dragon

                                Air Dragon style is pretty cool, it's the only style with a ranged weapon (kinda) and it makes good use of it. It's all about positioning. You might look at the style and initially think that Evasion is your main form of defense here, but it's not, it's distance. You can flurry a disengage with an attack, knock back opponents to long range, make your unarmed attacks ranged, and have multiple ranged simple charms.

                                It also has a few charms to see in the dark, reestablish stealth in plain sight, and some mobility charms. So yeah it gets around and it's hard to pin down. How's it for the actual power of the attacks though? Pretty good! Lightning Strike Style lets you get overflow successes on decisive attacks and ignore hardness and cover. It even gets the ability with Hurricane Combat Method to roll a reflexive attack that doesn't count as her attack, and can be a clash. So it's offensive potential is decently high.

                                The image of an Air Dragon master is one standing in a wind swept field using chakrams and blasts of wind and lightning to turn back all attackers before they can even step within sword range.

                                Earth Dragon

                                Where Air Dragon's defense was range, Earth Dragon is all about soak. It's compatible with heavy armor, and it's got soak built into it's form charm, which upgrades later, and with a bonus soak adder. Something to notice though is that it's only defense against decisive attacks is hardness, which it can have a lot of. That also means though that if somebody gets a lot of initiative from somewhere else basically your only defense is not getting hit. or a reflexive clash which it also gets.

                                How is it on the offense? It centers around smashing attacks and disarming, which is just fun. It's also disabling, with attacks to stack on wound penalties, trap your opponent beneath the earth, and then exploit those penalties with multiple smashing attacks, breaking their weapon along the way. This is a solid style to use if you don't plan on going anywhere, and smashing your opponents prone or taking advantage of Air Dragon knocking them prone is good teamwork.

                                The image of an Earth Dragon master is one standing unmoved while catching an opponent's Goremaul, twisting the head off, and then burying his opponent in stone.

                                Fire Dragon

                                Fire Dragon is aggressive as hell, to the point where it's defense is basically how offensive it is. She can burn away her opponent's essence and initiative to ensure they never feel confidant making a decisive attack. You also really don't want to get behind her in initiative because she can stack up the onslaught penalties while dodging around them herself, and then even when she is hit she deals damage back automatically.

                                It also seems to have the most straight damage potential of all the styles, and has good synergy with charms from other MAs that also rely on initiative differences. Espeically since it just has the ability to kill enemy initiative while racking it up from multiple sources itself. It's also got a kind of neat trick for an alpha strike where you can roll 2 join battles and pick the highest, but you need to pay for all enhancement charms for each, and then you deal bonus damage if you start things off with a decisive. It's soooo damn expensive but it has the power to just end a fight before it begins.

                                The image of a Fire Dragon master is one who flickers around his opponent, burning them to ash and never letting them get their footing.

                                Water Dragon

                                Water Dragon is all about momentum and control. It's got charms to increase grapple, defend while attacking, and building off attacks to actually steal, not just burn opponent's essence along with their initiative. You can also control your opponent with grapple escapes and the ability to actually deactivate enemy charms that they have active. This ability alone can be a game changer as you deactivate that Solar's scenelong penalty negator, thereby meaning they basically wasted the turn they spent getting it up and are also now vulnerable.

                                It's also got some pretty solid damage adders and AoE attack charms, which actually most of the styles except Wood seem to pick up. I definitely like it and it does a good job ad what it's trying to do.

                                The image of a Water Dragon master is one who gently flows past her opponents attacks and then binds them in their own strength and relentless attacks.

                                Wood Dragon

                                The defense offered up by Wood Dragon is pretty unique. It's got more of that wood aspected instant healing, for both yourself and others, and it adds bonus health levels on top of that. It also borrows a bit from performance in that it can enchant opponents, and then send their soul right to the afterlife. It's also got a really super cool power to use a bow as a staff, it's form weapon, while being able to reflexively swap back to using it as a bow, which means that if you use this style and have archery charms you can instantly snap back to having a ranged attack whenever you want!

                                Out of all the styles this one seems the most focused on teamwork. It can heal your allies, it can control and tie up multiple enemies at once, letting your circle focus them down at their leisure, it's just a great support to have. It's also got some surprisingly good damage adding with Eyes of the Wood Dragon, adding probably 5 damage and letting you make better piercing attacks. The cake here though is Soul Mastery, which just negates all the ox-bodies and stuff your opponent took and lets you kill them outright with less effort, but more setup. It's just about the perfect one to round off the previous 4.

                                The image of a Wood Dragon master is one who gives strength to her sisters and brothers, gracefully saving them from their foes both during and after the battle.


                                Summary

                                These are great! They all offer something really unique in how they play, and they complement eachother well. Also for those who know Ragara Myruun from previous editions, he's a high essence DB who's mastered all 5 styles, and in this edition he's even more scary I think. Just picture the soak from Earth Dragon, combined with the mobility of Air, the damage of Fire, the duality of Water and the resilience of Wood all at once. Terrifying.

                                These all work really well and play off the aspect bias that we've seen previously in the ability section. The power also seems to be perhaps slightly above the rest of the DB charmset, but with the caveat that flexibility within an ability is reduced compared to, for example, the myriad of things Brawl is capable of. Combine that with the merit tax and I think it's a decent tradeoff, especially with the armor restrictions.

                                Now somebody before asked how these compare to other styles with Mastery, and in general it's both good and bad. As far as effects that are directly comparable like damage adders and stuff the Immaculate Styles tend to lose out, and their big flashy effects generally have a high initiative threshold to use. Also if you can't enter a state of Aura many of the charms become much less good. As a Solar I wouldn't take these if you're looking for a mechanically superior character. I think part of the reason, maybe, to be cynical, that there were so many immaculate Solars in the previous edition is because the immaculate styles were some of the best CMA out there, and if you wanted to make a martial artist they were a great grab.

                                As it is now though the effects that other styles DON'T directly emulate are still great. The ability to break an opponent's artifact weapon, or to steal their motes, or instantly healing are all great effects that Solars have great difficulty accessing otherwise. There's also stuff like Heaven Thunder Hammer being better than the Air Dragon knockback, but that needs to be taken in context. Air Dragon can kill you well before you close to melee range, where as Solar Brawl just has a hard time with that. Against an Air Dragon getting knocked back to long range is enough of a death sentence believe me.

                                Either way, the effects themselves are still good enough that it's far far from useless to take as a Solar. In fact the Earth Dragon soak adder is BETTER than the Solar one, albeit at a much higher essence requirement for the final charm. I'd still take them if they fit the character, and as I said if you combine multiple of them together they complement eachother really well.

                                Anyway that's it for now, let me know if you have any other specific questions, that's it for now.

                                                        Originally posted by danelsan View Post
                                                        Personally, I plan to house-rule Mastery and Terrestrial effects into them, probably with DBs having means of evading the Terrestrial keyword
                                                        I might avoid the terrestrial keyword there. I mean do what you want of course, it's your table, but these charms are not CMA strength that DBs get inexplicable access to despite being much more powerful. I mean you've got like, Tsunami Force Shout, for example. it's 10m, 10i, 1wp, and deals what will amount to threshold successes times the wound penalty, which the style only has one way of raising by past what it normally is, and even then only by 1 except against spirits.

                                                        By contrast Dragon Vortex Attack, same essence requirement, costs 20m, 2wp, but no initiative, close to the same initiative trigger requirements, but has 4 times the area of effect, has generally a harder attack condition to avoid, being an environmental attack, and has a similar effect with more damage and less setup, as well as being able to be combined with allies.

                                                        If we look at the closest analogue in the corebook, I think Dancing Devil Trigger Finger, we see that it has a similar area to Tsunami Force Shout, except that a celestial using it gets to roll their full damage against everyone in the blast, while DBs have to divide up their damage like with Dragon Vortex. Also it's essence requirement is 3 not 5, and it costs 7 less initiative, which is huge as the payment for a decisive attack. To do much damage to a bunch of targets with Tsunami Force Shout you have to stack up quite the penalty on all of them, and then roll decently over their defense threshold using a stamina based attack pool, whereas with Dancing Devil Trigger all you need is initiative, which the game is full of ways to get.

                                                        Now, Tsunami Force Shout does get a lot better when you have a bunch of allies who stack on the wound penalties, teamwork being the DB way after all, but now that's requiring even more setup of decisive attacks from multiple people on multiple targets.


                                                        Basically what I'm saying here is that these immaculate charms don't really feel like base celestial ones, maybe just DB times 1.25 or something. They get a tiny little kick in some places, but the overall costs, restrictions and power are generally much closer to Terrestrial. Unless maybe the way to avoid the terrestrial keyword is to just be a DB, in which case they'll probably be bad enough for all the other terrestrial exalted to not bother with.


                                                        EDIT: Actually wow Tsunami Force Shout when used solo requires even more effort than I thought. You need to build up initiative, attack, get a wound penalty, reset to base initiative, and then build up to 20 again, and that's if you just want to hit one target. This is one you should definitely avoid sticking a terrestrial keyword on at least.
