                                    Okay then;

                                    The Underworld has changed in the past few Ages. The Yozis have reconciled with their Neverborn brethren, pooled resources, and now generally menace Creation together, just as much so it rises to oppose them as to actually fight it. Unfortunately, in time the Shadowlands dried up thanks to a very old ghost who seems to have come by Deathlord powers without actually binding himself to the dead Primordials, and in time, the fear and awe that Creation once had for it's ancestors and demons has dried up. Even the Underworld itself starves, with even one of the last true Deathlords close to losing his grasp on existence from Essence starvation.

                                    All hope is not lost for the Hell-Undead empire though. A former Deathlord, his bond broken by a combination of his inability to break a promise to never menace a living mortal soul again until he can show a long-dead woman true terror again and his accepted lowly position as a middle manager and instructor for the Timeless Order of Manacle and Coin has finally been moved out of his self-imposed exile to retake his title and role, thus reviving the dying (...again) Underworld. With his loyal and cunning Lunar retainer, an odd Abyssal who seems to believe she's a Solar despite all evidence otherwise, the Ghost-Blooded son of the current Deathlord, and others, perhaps they will bring both worlds the supply of terror they both so desperately need...

                                    So long as nobody thinks of poisoning his supply of fish. He seems addicted to the stuff.
