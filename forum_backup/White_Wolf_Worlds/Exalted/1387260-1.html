        Another one - on the Anime-adjacent side because it's actually a donghua - a Chinese Anime, essentially.

        Mo Dao Zu Shi - The main character is basically a kung fu necromancer. There's a scene at the end of the first season that's just RIDICULOUSLY awesome inspiration for what I could see a powerful Abyssal looking like. Creepy, zombies, descending while playing a flute as green fires glow. Frankly, if this show doesn't end up being some good inspiration for Abyssals going forward, there is no justice in the world!

        Even better, it's legit available on Youtube because the company put it there! Plus it's a beautiful almost ink painting style of backgrounds animated. And the fight scenes are just amazingly well done.
