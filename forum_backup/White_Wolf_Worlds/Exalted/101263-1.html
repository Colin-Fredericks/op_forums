        Rather than evincing a single, barbarian aesthetic, I believe Lunars should wear garments that blend personal tastes with the constraints of their physical and cultural environments. Some elders may go sans clothing, but speaking from an anthropological view, clothing has a variety of purposes, not the least of which is insulating the body in climates that are not tropical.

        There are a number of fashions seen as barbaric by historical peoples, usually related to association with cultural otherness or violation of that people's cultural norms. These can vary in the extreme, encompassing everything from fur robes, sarongs and body wraps, or even trousers depending on the culture in question. Since all of these are barbaric from a certain perspective, we need to consider both which perspectives are dominant (likely the Realm / major cities) and what elements they might find alien or barbarous.

        In short, while any Exalt can wear whatever clothing fits their personal expression, the barbarian aesthetic should make sense within the constraints of culture and climate. YMMV
