            I feel as though Lunars should inflict dreams and nightmares largely as an after effect of seeing them. Not so much a psychic dream projection thing as being so beautiful or horrifying that they torment the sleeping moments of witnesses for days or weeks afterwards. Or possibly any sensory interaction with them; the sound of their voice, the feel of their fingers running along the back of your neck, the scent of them wafting along the wind.

            Maybe with an idea that a Lunar can put subtle nuances into whatever they're projecting themselves by that code particular responses or even messages into the resulting dreams.

            I've only just remembered, back when it was talked about in terms of errata, there was a sense of giving Lunars a fair number of Merged Charms. Like, that scarring thing, that was going to be available in Strength and Charisma, wasn't it? I presume that would have helped somewhat at getting further in to trees that weren't part of a favoured Attribute, as a way of easing the burden of the limited available ones to a Lunar; like, if there was a cool trick up in Charisma, but you didn't favor it, your Strength or Dexterity Charms could get you a fair way into the prerequisites.

            I wonder if that, or something similar, might be on the table. Perhaps it could inform this apparent idea of spirit shapes helping to inform the shape or progression of Charm cascades; like, one way it could work is by having Charms that are basically merged if the symbolism of the spirit shape suggests a measure of relatedness of an unconventional Attribute to a certain Charm.

            Like, I don't know, owls are erroneously considered wise, so maybe some Dexterity based attack Charms could also serve as prerequisites for Intelligence Charms and also let you buy them at favoured costs. Or for Perception Charms, since owls actually do have impressive senses. Or quite likely the other way around; an owl totem No Moon who is able to use their Intelligence or Perception Charms as prerequisites for certain attack Charms, without having to favor Strength or Dexterity.

                        Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post
                        Are people disappointed that the best Sidereal healing Charm is in Archery?
                        They have a dodging Charm in performance, an attack Charm in Lore, Medicine is for fighting the undead, Bureaucracy can turn gods into artifacts... As I said, Sidereals get away with that because it's not the sole exception, but rather part of a running theme.

                        I don't particularly like it, but I have always taken that to mean that Sidereals are not to my tastes, rather than there being anything wrong with how Sidereals do things.

                        If I have any serious problem against that Sidereal Archery Charm, it is that it is the only Sidereal Charm that allows them to cheat out of their limited Charmset by buying new submodules. I would think it preferable for each Sidereal Ability (or at least one per Caste) to have one Charm that allows submodules.


                        Originally posted by The MG View Post
                        It's the medicine, not the treatment. If anything, it would supplement all Medicine rolls directed towards the recipient, not just your own.
                        I wouldn't mind if Lunar Stamina allows you to share regenerative blood with someone to speed up their basic powers of recovery. Indeed, that would be cool.

                        I just don't want Lunar Stamina Charms to be so good at healing what ails people that it becomes a mandatory choice for a Lunar physician, just as I do not want Deadly Beastman Transformation to be a mandatory choice for Lunar combatants.

                        However, I may just have to deal with that, because Scorpion-and-Toad Assumption was cool, and still had undeveloped space in the realm of "defeat a disease within your own immune system; produce the antibodies which can spread out and cure the plague". Assuming that a concept like "antibodies" can be acceptably mythic for Lunar Charmspace.


                        It would also solve the Stealth-in-Charisma thing.
                        If this is meant to be a refutation of my claim that Lunar medicine is the only misplaced perk, I have some counter-arguments. Even if you did not mean to refute me, these points are important for clarifying the purpose of the "Stealth-in-Charisma" feature.

                        First of all, I'm pretty sure that there will be at least one Lunar Dexterity Charm which helps with sneaking around unnoticed, if only as a side-benefit to its general purpose. (Because if you can use a crane's stance to balance on the leaf of a tree, I'm pretty sure you can walk across gravel without making a sound, or over a nest of fallen twigs without leaving a trail.)

                        Second, Lunars are like Sidereals in that their legendary powers are expressed in more forms than Charms. The Solar padfoot who relied on (Dexterity + Stealth) needs to improve both Traits and buy Stealth Charms. The Lunar padfoot can freely acquire the form of a cat (if it was not already his Spirit Shape) to become literally padfooted (and small, and inconspicuous), on top of learning the aforementioned Dexterity Charm which provides benefits to sneaking around. Plus, the Solar can pay favored costs only for Stealth dots, but the Lunar could favor both Stealth and Dexterity.

                        Third, we are told over and over that Lunars are all about being seen. The Stealth-in-Charisma is not for avoiding the notice of people as per (Dexterity + Stealth); it is for dealing with the attention of people who have already noticed you. In short, the Lunar who uses that Social Charm has either already failed his (Dexterity + Stealth) contest, or he didn't bother trying to hide in the first place. His legend is not of the sneak-thief who slips by unseen, but as the confidence man who blusters his way past the guards with his silver tongue; (Manipulation + Presence = "I'm totally allowed to be here, officer. Honest.")


                        If that thing where your totem rearranges your Charm trees pans out, Medicine Charms being spread out all over wouldn't be so much of a problem.
                        I have some reservations about that concept.

                        Let's say a Solar had a totem of "swashbuckling pirate", which involves Sail and Melee. But for the purposes of this example, he's only bought Sail Charms and no Melee Charms thus far.

                        As one possibility for totem tree-remapping, the Solar's totem allows him to append Solar Melee Charms to his personal progression of Solar Sail Charms.

                        This does him absolutely no good. If he wants Solar Melee Charms, he wants to start at the beginning, because that is how Charm progression should be designed -- such that the starting Charms are desirable if not essential to the purposes for which you would put the Charms that follow it. Recall Arete-Driven Athletic Stride, the Essence 2 Solar Charm with the modest effect that requires the powers of previous Athletics running-Charms to become synergistically powerful.

                        If the Charm trees are not designed such that you want to progress from beginning to end, I question the integrity of the Charmset's basic arrangement. It would seem designed to punish anyone who isn't using their totem to sequence-break. Worse, it would seem like a blanket invitation to return to First and Second Edition's problem of unnecessarily long lists of prerequisite Charms which are only tangentially related to the Charm you want at the end.

                        On top of that, remapping Charms from other traits into the tree of which you have already invested doesn't help you progress through the tree in which you already invested. That is, the Solar swashbuckler-pirate can nab the Melee Charms he wants and avoid the undesirable prerequisites because of how many Sail Charms he's bought, but that didn't save him from having to buy low-end Sail Charms which were only tangentially related to the high-end Sail Charms he actually wanted. Given that his greater priority on Sail Charms means sailing is more central to his concept than swashbuckling, it doesn't make sense that he should be forced to progress less efficiently through Solar Sail than Solar Melee.

                        The other possibility for totem tree-remapping is allowing the Solar swashbuckler-pirate to learn Melee Charms with his Sail Ability rating rather than his Melee Ability rating. I think we can agree that's a bad idea on the face of it. But further, if Melee is part of the Solar's totem, it's important enough to his character concept that he should not only favor the Ability, but already have dots in it. So there would be no need to append Melee Charms into his Sail trees.

                        Note: I'm not saying that the developers and writers can't find a way to make this work. What I am saying is that the "totem tree-remapping" idea is a statement John Morke made a while ago, at a time when he made it clear that many of his ideas for mechanically implementing the Lunar Exalted were not set in stone. I am also saying that I imagine that he and the others have seen the very problems I have described above, and this might have by now persuaded them to find some other benefit for Lunar totems.

                        Or else they found a solution based on possibilities which do not occur to me; and if they occur to anyone else, I would appreciate hearing them.

                        formerly Tornado Wolf, formerly Inugami
