                    So, I have a couple of questions related to power levels.

                    First off as background third edition is the first edition of exalted I have ever played/Dmed and one of the things that does interest/confuse me is the idea that the different types of exalted are not on the same power level/level of competency. From my understanding there is going to be three broad power “Areas”. You will have Solar/abyssal/infernal at the top, a competency gap then Lunars/Sidereals other guys that fit here, another gap and finally dragonblooded etc.

                    The first question then is going from second to third edition what is the benefit of keeping the power gap between the first and second group from a gameplay perspective?

                    *note tradition/didn’t want to change how it has worked for two editions is totally a valid answer I am just curious if there is something else I am missing as a newcomer to the game line.

                    Like as a DM I am just imagining showing off the game in a couple years when all the splats are out and it going as follows:
                    1. You can play as the Solar exalted, legendary Golden heroes returned to a world in crisis. Possessing unparalleled skill and power. Also, the most powerful thing you can play and will most likely wreck everybody’s shit as you see please
                    2. You can play as the Abyssal exalted, powerful death knights and masters of the dead and death. Also, the most powerful thing you can play and will most likely wreck everybody’s shit as you see please
                    3. The infernal exalted, demon tainted exalted with all types of crazy esoteric powers and they can just straight up turn into a final fantasy boss. Also, the most fucking thing you can play and will most likely wreck everybody’s shit as you see please
                    4. OR you can play a fate ninja, werewolf, Frankenstein monster etc. but they are noticeably weaker than the first three options.

                    So, my second question is if the game line was fine with having three equally powerful groups, each with their own very different themes, powers etc, tied together loosely by the idea of “is at its core a solar” why not just make all the playable splats different variety of solar?

                    As in vanilla solar, death knight solar, demon solar, shapeshifting monster solar, fate manipulating solar etc.

                    The idea being from my perspective why is it someone has to be worse at sword fighting/talking/crafting etc just because they chose animal based shape shifting/fate manipulation as the thing that interested them versus the guy who chose all the death or demon stuff?
