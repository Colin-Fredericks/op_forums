        Originally posted by JohnDoe244 View Post
        Timid, bumbling, mild mannered Clark Kent has the same fearless, front-line personality as Superman.

        Kent, who faints and runs away from trouble to go get help, has the same apparent Intimacies as Superman?
        I'm sure that in the very long history of Superman there has been at least one wrong-headed version in which Clark Kent habitually forgets that he's invulnerable when he's not dressed in that leotard, but it's not the typical or popularly recognised form of the character.

        So Clark Kent is not actually timid in the face of things that he knows can't hurt him, and when he runs away it's not because he's afraid but because he wants to get out of sight so that he can put on the leotard.

        One might note the Christopher Reeves movie, one of the most famous incarnations of the character, in which he artfully uses his phony bumbling as a way of throwing his invulnerable body in front of a bullet going for another person.

        Playing pretend when he's not wearing a costume is a bit different from genuine social adaptations.

        There's also those depictions of the character in which he acts a good deal more Superman when dressed as Clark, and a good deal more Clark when dressed as Superman, in the presence of people that are in on his secret.

        All in all, I think persona Charms aren't best suited to model superhero narratives, and that the emphasis on that or similar things is missing out on a lot of potential.

                I'm ambivalent about Lunar Shadow-Mind Meditation.

                It's like Solar Heart-Eclipsing Shroud, but you can only have one alternate persona, it's not even fully alternate since it must begin with the same Defining Intimacies and Tie for a Solar Mate, and it is always unrelentingly negative, bitter, cynical, skeptical, fearful, or hateful to the point that it cannot voluntarily cultivate a positive opinion.

                As a disguise against understanding and persuasion, it is lackluster.

                As a mental defense against seduction, the four hours needed to invoke it is really awkward.

                As a Hulk to your Bruce Banner (especially when using Dark Premise Duality with a theoretical upgrade so you can go DBT to immediately exchange your Intelligence for another dot in Wits and more Perception, and any dots in Manipulation for more in Charisma or Appearance) it takes a lot of investment for dubious returns outside of that specific application.


                Plus, I was kind of hoping that a Lunar could craft alternative personae that took advantage of the fact that Lunar Exalted can have any of their potential Castes, by giving their personae a completely different Caste than theirs.

                                    Originally posted by TheCountAlucard View Post
                                    You’re not wrong, Isator; there’s a lot of value in using these Charms (and their prereqs) to play the double-agent (hence why I’m in the process of working up a second persona for Knight Raiton when I want to engage in shadier behavior).
                                    Oh no, being a double agent is just the person's cover story so that none of the groups will to heavily question why the person is clearly meeting with rivals. Their actual purpose is far more sinister.

                                    Being a meeting point between various seemingly irreconcilable factions with the ultimate purpose of bringing them together as a force of mutual cooperation, by means of having each convinced that the Solar is not only in favour of their particular views and agenda, and is manipulating the others to fall in line with it!

                                    WoooOoohhh {spooky hand waving}

                                    I think the big thing about the time it takes to switch personas is that it prevents somebody from changing their Intimacies on the fly in a manner that turns it into a perfect defence against social influence.

                                    I think the point is that you're supposed to spend some time scouting out a social situation and deciding ahead of time what configuration of Intimacies will be suitable for heading into it. If you expect to be seduced and manipulated, you ought to spare the time to adopt a mindset that will effectively weather such overtures.

                                    If you don't expect to be seduced and go about with passions and priorities that leave you susceptible to such appeals... Yes, obviously you should be vulnerable to them. Why on Earth wouldn't you be?

                                    It certainly sounds to me like the statement of "you should be able to change personas immediately, lest somebody catch you out by seducing you" becomes a lot worse when one of those words is substituted for "persuade at all", which seduction is really just a very narrow subset of.
