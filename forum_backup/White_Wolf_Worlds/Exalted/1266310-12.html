                                                            Originally posted by Elfive View Post
                                                            Ember Celicia are arguably bracers and not smashfists.
                                                            Interesting proposal.

                                                            They certainly do not cover Yang's fists at all. The protruding end is more about extending the gun barrel far enough that shots hit what she punches, rather than enveloping her fists in the blast, but the protruding part does not, itself, hit the target, because it falls just short of her knuckles.

                                                            She can strike someone with it on a backhand strike, and she can use them to parry like bracers, but that alone doesn't necessarily make it a smashfist. Especially if, mechanically, the backhand is still a barehanded strike.

                                                            If silken armor can be given special dispensation to work with martial arts Form Charms, it seems that bracers could be given special dispensation to work with martial arts parries.

                                                            So, in effect, what we're looking at is a combat artifact that is not technically a weapon in the usual mechanical sense. It enhances attacks through its Evocations, without giving Yang's punches the traits of an artifact weapon.

                                                            Therefore, it should be totally compatible with most martial arts that involve bare-handed attacks, such as Fire Dragon Style.
