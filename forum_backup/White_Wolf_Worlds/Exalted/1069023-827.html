                Originally posted by GrayWalker View Post
                Ah, from that comment would it be safe to assume you are keeping some charms from the previous edition?
                Some, but not all.

                In the previous edition the charms were the Yozis own. Are Infernal charms now just inspired by them, and drawing power from them?
                By that I mean attempts at emulating their power instead of their actual powers.
                Infernal Charms are uniquely their own. They evoke the Yozis in the same way that Solar Charms evoke the Unconquered Sun and Abyssal Charms evoke the Deathlords, but they aren't limited purely to that.

                Would some charms perhaps be inspired by the demons themselves as well as the Yozi?

                If not could there be an increased affinity to learning Demon Charms for Infernals?
                I think the latter's more likely.

                I have heard that there will be emphasis on crafting Shintai transformations. Will we be allowed multiple transformations or will the Infernals only have an individual transformation?
                Not far along enough in the design process to say.
