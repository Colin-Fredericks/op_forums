    When it comes to Craft, there are Craft: Air, Craft: Earth, Craft: Fire, Craft: Wood, and Craft: Water (and many more, if you count all of the other types of Craft), however, there are no Charms meant for these Crafts...just "Generic" Charms meant to be shared between all of the Crafts. So, if you have Craft: Air 5 and you have a Charm like Craft-Mending Technique (Craft 5, Essence 3), then you can be used with any Craft skill...even if you don't have have that particular Craft (ie: using a skill as unskilled).

    Does it make sense that there are no Charms for the different types of Craft's?

    Or that you can use the "Generic Craft Charms" with each other?

    Why have different Craft's, but not specific Craft Charms for each one?

    Or am I just being a nitpicker?

        Check out the sidebar in the Exalted core p. 211. It says that you can fulfill mins with any type of Craft, but Supplemental Craft Charms only work for the type of Craft(s) that meet the mins. That is, if you have Air 4, Earth 5, Fire 5, Wood 2, and Water 1, you could only use Crack-Mending Technique with Earth and Fire crafts. Other Charms apply to all Crafts, though, so even in the above case you could use Shattering Grasp with any of them.

        Now, as to if it makes sense that there are no Charms for the different types of Crafts? Yes, since there are so many crafts that making cool unique individual Craft Charms would be a huge bloat. That being said, it would've been awesome to have some more flavorful Craft Charms. On the other hand, it would exacerbate the problem of there being lots of types of Craft.

            Yep...my bad. "Sigh"...that's why I dislike the way that Craft has been split up. Another Special Rule to add to all the other Special Rules.

            Let me "Compare & Contrast" Craft among the different Exalted.

            For this comparison, I am sticking to the Main Books. I have every book, including the Scroll of Errata and Ink Monkey, but I'm not going to dig through 400+ pages...so, just the main books:
            • Solars (Exalted Core 2e): Out of 6 Craft Charms (+1 for "Any Craft Excellency"), 2 of which are Supplemental. The two Supplemental Charms are Craftsman Needs No Tools (Craft 4, Essence 3) and Crack-Mending Technique (Craft 5, Essence 3) -- the former of which is not a prerequisite of the other.
              • Note: MEP-Ab adds a new Charm (Mirror) called World-Slaying Arsenal Epiphany, which is not Supplemental (It's Permanent).
            • Dragon-Blooded (MEP-DB): They have it better, as all 6 of their Craft Charms (Not counting the required Excellency) are Simple. In MEP-DB.
            • Sidereals (MEP-S): Out of 7 Craft Charms, 2 are Supplemental, 1 is Special. The Special Charm, Elemental Vision, requires a minimum of 2 in each Craft that it is purchased for. The two supplemental Charms are Excellent Implementation of Objectives (Craft 3, Essence 2) and Implicit Construction Methodology (Craft 5, Essence 4).
            • Abyssal (MEP-Ab): 9 Craft Charms (+1 for "Any Craft Exxcellency"), 2 of which are Supplemental. The Supplemental Charms are Fault-Finding Scrutiny (Craft 3, Essence 2) and Frenzied Forge Within (Craft 5, Essence 3)
            • Alchemicals (MEP-Al): Use Attributes
            • Infernals (MEP-I): Use Essence
            • Lunars (MEP-L): Use Attributes

            The Alchemicals, Infernals and Lunars can be discounted as they don't use Abilities for their minimum requirements. As for the others, the Dragon-Blooded come out the best, as they don't even have any Supplemental Craft Charms and thus they can "Mix and Match," as it were. As for the others, well they all have some pretty good Craft Charms that require a Craft of 5 (Insert Whatever that Craft may be). So, if they want to use their Supplemental Craft Charms with those particular "Crafts" then they have to spend experience and get them to 5. The other Craft Charms can be used, no matter what the rating, just not the Supplemental.


            Let me work something out here: We have a Solar Character who want's to be great at Craft. We'll assume, for the sake of argument that Craft is either a Caste or Favored Ability. We'll approach it from two different directions:
            • Character Creation: The Solar purchases all 5 Crafts as high as possible. Out of 28 Ability Dots, 15 are spent getting each Craft to 3. Of the 15 Bonus Points, 10 are spent raising all 5 Crafts to 5. that leaves 13 Ability Dots and 5 Bonus Points.
            • Experience Points: We'll assume that the Solar put 1 Dot in each Craft Ability at Character Creation. To raise each of the Craft Abilities to 5, the Solar is going to have to spend (((2x2)-1) + ((3x2)-1) + ((4x2)-1) + ((5x2)-1)) = 24 experience per Craft Ability. To raise all 5 Craft Abilities to 5 it's going to cost (24 x 5) = 120 experience.

            I think both are too expensive to achieve something that could easily be done by having just one Craft Ability. (Shrug) In 2e, I would have preferred that Craft had been kept a Unified Ability, with Air, Earth, Fire, Wood, and Water all rolled up into the main Craft Ability. For 2e, you could make Magitech and the like seperate Abilities, but the others feel...forced...a leftover...something like that. None of the other Abilities were split up like that, and I can make a darn good argument for several of them to be split like Craft. But I just don't think that it's worth it, and it just doesn't "Feel Exalted" -- it feels more like a numbers game.

            This is one of the things that I hope isn't carried over into 3e. Keep all the Abilities Unified. As for things like "Craft: Fate," "Craft: Magitech." et. al -- they could be handled with a Merit or as an inborn nature of your Exaltation (ie: All Sidereals can use Craft to affect Fate...if such a Merit is even allowed). Things like "Articifer," "Life Crafter,' "Fate Weaver," "Necromonger," etc, that would allow a character to expand the uses of Craft, without having to dump tons of experience into high levels of an Ability that doesn't even have a Charm set of it's own.
