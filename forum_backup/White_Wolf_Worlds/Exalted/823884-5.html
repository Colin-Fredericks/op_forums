                                        This is a quick write-up as all my full notes and fluff are on my PC at home.

                                        Alabaster Calcine

                                        Attuning to Alabaster Calcine allows a Dragonblooded or Solar to sense the darkness within others. If a character the attuned Exalt meets possesses at least one negative intimacy tie, or a principle that is built on the suffering of others, the Exalt becomes aware though they must make Read Intentions actions to reveal what that darkness is. This effect does not reveal the presence of Pronciples that are entirely selfish or self centered in nature. "Subjugate the Realm" would trigger this power, "Take the Scarlet Throne" would not.

                                        Evocations of Alabaster Calcine:

                                        Impartation of the Luminous Curtana
                                        A successful Withering attack acts as a read intentions action that reveals the weakest dark intimacy in the target to every character in the scene. An intimacy revealed in this manner cloaks the target in dark miasma that prevents them from gaining any appearance based bonus to social actions.

                                        Injunction of the Coruscating Partisan
                                        Successful withering attack allows the attuned exalt to sacrifice initiative that would have been gained from the attack to seal revealed intimacies. The initiative cost to seal an intimacy is equal to its strength. A sealed intimacy cannot be used in social actions for the rest of the scene.

                                        Evocation 3
                                        Activating this evocation reduces the difficulty of revealing dark intimacies with Impartation of the Luminous Curtana equal to the number of intimacies already revealed. This effect lasts for one scene but can only be used on any given target once per scene.

                                        Absolution of the Sublime Mirabelle
                                        Once the Exalt has revealed and sealed all of the dark intimacies in their target, activating this evocation permanently erases them from the afflicted character. this does not prevent the character from forming more dark intimacies or replacing the ones they have lost, but they may not do so for at least the Exalt's essence in days.

                                                        Just got back from my honeymoon in Europe so I'm still playing catch-up on the forums. But while on the plane I finished an artifact writeup.

                                                        So I was inspired to rewrite some old artifacts from my very first Exalted game, back in 1st edition. Our GM gave us some cool weapons and it occurred to me that they would be well-served by Evocations. What I'm getting at is these weapons aren't my creations, they're my old GMs from 12 years ago, but the evocations are mine. I wrote up the evocations, but I'm going to use stories my GM wrote for my flavor bit. So I didn't write that part.

                                                        First up, is the Zenith's staff!

                                                        Harmonious Savant (Orichalcum Wrackstaff ****)


                                                        Backstory written by my old GM hidden for length

                                                        “I was the air. I was lighter than the sea, and as such I rose above it. Filling my mind with the joy of such lightness, I opened my eyes and gazed out upon the great Ocean of the West. At my back, the golden spires of Quius rose from the sea, a strikingly beautiful image that hid the monstrous truth of the metropolis' function from prying eyes. I cared not. Instead I took in the Ocean, which was filled with turbulence in many places, but always appeared calm when witnessed as a whole. I accepted its lesson, and felt my tension bleed away.

                                                        My mind turned over what had troubled me a moment ago. Yet another demonic summons had gone awry, and many sorcerers and priests had paid the price for our pride in demanding obedience from the imprisoned princes of Malfeas. Exquisite Feathers, a priest of Luna and my most promising student in the Aquamarine Pillar Style, had his head ripped from his shoulders by a brass scythe-limb before the demon was contained again behind his bonds in Malfeas. Of those present, only the head Solar priestess had lived, and she still was shaken by the incident.

                                                        Were we right to continue to torment those who we had fought so hard to lock away? Why did we keep opening their door?

                                                        Sensing my doubts, Harmonious Savant of Enlightenment blew a pulse of calm purpose through my soul. I lowered my eyes to my faithful weapon and allowed a smile to cross my face. An orichalcum staff of the most exacting design, Savant's length was precisely that of my height while standing. Glittering in the slowly rising sun's light, it was exquisitely carved with prayers to the Unconquered Sun and principles of truth and enlightenment. In battles that I had wielded the staff I had found myself blessed with a sudden lack of enemies as my opponents, upon witnessing such a perfect principle in solid form, had found enlightenment and begged the opportunity to learn from me.

                                                        Reassured, I stood from my lotus position. To an outside observer, it seemed as if I stood upon the sea. An enlightened mind or sharper eye would discern the truth. I stood upon the air, and the sea lapped beneath me. Gazing upon the Ocean one last time, I turned to walk back to Quius when a shockwave roared across the air and sea, sending waves splashing past me.

                                                        Elite ships of the Deliberative roared across the sky towards the spires of my city. Spilling from them in the hundreds were terrestrial troops, resplendent in their jade Dragon armor. I briefly debated the possible reasons for such a performance when the implosion bows of the vessels began to fire. The five largest domes of Quius exploded in golden light, and the armor-clad warriors dropped into the smoking holes. A second wave of troops begin to fall, but mixed among them were demons and the occasional tall warstrider.

                                                        Rebellion. I began to run, analyzing what part of the city I could best aid in defending, when Harmonious Savant roared a negative into my mind. I stumbled, shocked at his rebuke. A moment of consideration brought the staff's wisdom to my mind. There were too many of them, and too few of us to defend the city. There would not be such an overt sign of disobedience from the weak dragon-bloods unless they had all risen up together, possibly with more powerful backers. Quius would fall, and its defenders would die.

                                                        Filled with regret, I turned northwest and began to run across the sky, the sea beneath me and chaos behind. Set within Harmonious Savant were two hearthstones, one from the far West and the other the far East, a balanced and appropriate combination. I would hide myself in the western island manse, and prepare a way for my kind to regain the strength we'd lose today.

                                                        A keening whine approached, and I turned my gaze to see a sleek, narrow skycraft turning to follow me. I had been spotted. Leaping hard at the tingling warning of my Essence, I avoided a spray of explosive blasts from my pursuer's implosion bows. Although the Sun lent me great speed, I would not be able to outrun the flying vessel. If I paused to disable it, others would see my flight and overwhelm me. One option presented itself.

                                                        “My friend, we must part ways here,” I spoke out loud to reinforce my thoughts and strengthen my resolve, holding my glittering staff in front of me. I felt acceptance flow from the weapon, as it accepted its task. “I'll need this for a future day,” I said, withdrawing the Western hearthstone from its setting.

                                                        “May the Sun reunite us in another life,” I intoned a prayer to a god that I'd recently suspected had grown tired of his children, and infused Harmonious Savant of Enlightenment with my Essence. It flew from my hand with a trail of golden fire. Calling upon my gifts of speed, I began to cross leagues with my leaps, leaving Quius behind me. As it faded from my view, I saw the explosion of Essence as Savant struck true, sending my pursuer tumbling from the sky into the sea. Holding my hearthstone close to my chest, I concentrated on my far away goal as I ran. The Ocean waited beneath for me to tire.

                                                        It would be disappointed.”


                                                        Harmonious Savant is an Orichalcum Wrackstaff, engraved with depictions of various martial arts techniques, prayers to the Unconquered Sun, and parables meant to inspire wisdom. It has 2 hearthstone sockets along its length and when it strikes solid objects it chimes deeply like a prayer bell.

                                                        Evocations of Harmonious Savant
                                                        A Solar who spends 2 additional motes to attune to Harmonious Savant gains several benefits.
                                                        • Attacks with Harmonious Savant may deal Bashing damage instead of Lethal at the exalt’s choice for no penalty
                                                        • Gambits that are stunted as showing off the exalt’s superior martial skill (disarm, distract, etc) cost 1 less initiative to attempt
                                                        Additional benefits are gained against foes who witness the exalt wielding the wrackstaff in combat with the Martial Arts ability for at least 1 round. Additionally, the exalt must maintain a higher initiative value than their foes to benefit from these bonuses:
                                                        • The exalt’s Appearance is treated as 2 higher when compared against Resolve for purposes of bonuses in social influence
                                                        • Instill and Inspire actions that the exalt takes gain 1 automatic success
                                                        Font of Instruction
                                                        Cost: -- (2m) Mins: Essence 1
                                                        Type: Permanent
                                                        Keywords:
                                                        Duration: Permanent
                                                        Prerequisites: None

                                                        In time long lost, Harmonious Savant was a teaching tool, an instrument of learning. Wielded by a master martial artist, the wrackstaff gained enlightenment and learned the movements of its master. When Font of Instruction is unlocked, Harmonious Savant is awakened and will communicate with its wielder through wordless pulses of sensation that will automatically translate in the mind of the exalt. Font of Instruction has two effects.

                                                        First, Harmonious Savant can detect the threatening presence of those who would do the exalt harm. For 2 motes reflexively spent, the wrackstaff will grant rerolling 1’s to any awareness roll to detect hidden enemies, traps, or any source of harm not readily apparent.

                                                        Secondly, Harmonious Savant is a master martial artist and can instruct its wielder in the performance of many different styles. Consider Harmonious Savant a 2 dot Mentor with complete knowledge of several martial arts for the purposes of training.

                                                        Personification of Mastery
                                                        Cost: 2m Mins: Essence 2
                                                        Type: Reflexive
                                                        Keywords: None
                                                        Duration: Instant
                                                        Prerequisites: Font of Instruction

                                                        The prowess of the martial artist is a testament to the potential of humankind. It inspires veneration as much as fear. Personification of Mastery creates a reflexive Inspire or Instill action when used in combat. These actions suffer no penalties for multiple targets, but can only be applied against targets that are actively engaging the exalt in combat, and can only be activated once per round.

                                                        If the exalt chooses to make an Inspire action, they attempt to inspire Awe in the unbridled mastery of Martial Arts that the wielder of Harmonious Savant possesses. This Inspire action gains double 9’s and on success causes any foes who still wish to continue combat to pay a point of initiative each time they wish to attack the exalt. Some foes may surrender their arms to the superior combatant.

                                                        If the exalt chooses to make an Instill action, they attempt to create a positive intimacy towards the wielder of Harmonious Savant, often of awe, respect, or admiration. This action counts as leveraging a defining intimacy if the target is a martial artist themselves. On a success, the target is at a die penalty equal to the level of intimacy for all actions in opposition to the martial artist. (Minor is 1, Major is 2, and Defining is 3).

                                                        Special Activation Rules: This evocation cannot be unlocked for the Solar until they have learned at least 1 martial art through the form charm.

                                                        Embodiment of Perfection
                                                        Cost: 5m, 1wp Mins: Essence 3
                                                        Type: Simple
                                                        Keywords: None
                                                        Duration: Instant
                                                        Prerequisites: Personification of Mastery

                                                        Through skill and dedication, the martial artist is the master of his art, the perfect embodiment of power, focus, and wisdom. Those who have witnessed this magnificent presence first-hand see the futility in their actions, and the truth in their own weakness. Only learning from the master can bring them to the truth.

                                                        Embodiment of Perfection requires a foe to hold a positive Intimacy towards the martial artist, likely created by Personification of Mastery. It is a Persuade action, and can be used against multiple targets without penalty as long as they are all actively engaged with the exalt in combat. If the action is successful, the target will throw down their weapons and prostrate themselves before the martial artist, begging to be taken as a student. If the exalt accepts them, the targets will follow their new sifu in learning their wondrous martial arts skills. However, if the exalt rejects these students or if the exalt attacks them, the soaring emotions of awe and respect the targets have will turn to hate and rage. The targets may immediately roll join battle and take a combat action.

                                                        Special Activation Rules: Only a Solar who has completely mastered a martial art may unlock this evocation.

                                                            I felt like making an artifact inspired by Sidereal Thrown Charms, which were some of my favorite in earlier editions.

                                                            I'll note here that The Gathering Ire is basically a freebie Evocation representing the ability for most artifact Thrown weapons to return to their wielder. I could've made it baked in but it feels better as its own thing, I guess. As always, feedback welcome. I'm totally unsure of a lot of the Evocations' costs. I've also slowly added a bunch of effects that key off of Spite charges. Not sure if that's the greatest idea.

                                                            Shards of Hate
                                                            (Soulsteel and Starmetal Rainspikes)

                                                            Forgotten in a long-lost forge deep in the central mountains of the Caul, these ragged pieces of metal wait. The shards all came from one attempted weapon, fueled by the hate and betrayal its maker felt toward their fate and the fate of the world. Even the magical metal couldn't take the strain, and shattered. Each shard is unique, each balanced perfectly for throwing. Each holds a piece of that original hatred: Enmity, Malice, Rancor, Loathing, Antipathy, Acrimony, Animus, and so on. The shards desire nothing but the pain and suffering of their wielder's foes.

                                                            Evocations of the Shards of Hate
                                                            If a Solar, Abyssal, or Sidereal attunes these knives, every subsequent attack after the first with a shard of hate against same target gains one cumulative reroll of a non-success attack die or damage die, in any combination, except those that show 1s. These are called Spite charges, which reset at the end of the scene.

                                                            The Gathering Ire
                                                            Cost: —; Mins: Essence 1; Type: Permanent
                                                            Keywords: None
                                                            Duration: Permanent
                                                            Prerequisites: None
                                                            Each shard of hate was originally part of a greater whole; they are still bound to one another on a fundamental level. By awakening this Evocation, the Exalt taps into that link, inexorably drawing his blades back to him. Unless the Exalt desires otherwise, the knives slink back to him after being thrown, keeping to the shadows and staying out of sight if they can. This will even happen if he is unconscious. They return at a speed of one range band per turn, no matter the distance. This will take a few minutes if the shard is a few miles away, and proportionally longer if further out.

                                                            Special Activation Rules: This Evocation awakens at no cost when the Exal fails an ammunition check with the shards of hate. All the knives, no matter where they are, fold away like a shadow against the light. They appear hovering in front of the Exalt in the shape of the single weapon they were originally forged to be. The moment it is touched, it shatters back into the shards of hate.

                                                            Life Gets Worse
                                                            Cost: 5m, 1wp (2m or 1m); Mins: Essence 1; Type: Supplemental
                                                            Keywords: Decisive-Only
                                                            Duration: Instant
                                                            Prerequisites: None
                                                            Each shard of hate calls out when it tastes pain, drawing stray weapons in like a whirlpool. This Evocation supplements a decisive Thrown attack. Gain three bonus Spite charges for the damage roll; if at least one success results, the weapon lodges itself into its victim, unable to be removed. For the rest of the scene, the Exalt may spend two motes to redirect a ranged attack that missed its target to the victim so long as the missed projectile traveled within close range of the victim. If redirecting another shard of hate, this costs only one mote. Any redirected attack produces a Spite charge, not just shards of hate. The attack uses the same roll as the original, but gains (target's current Spite charges ÷ 2, rounded up) Spite charges, just for this roll. If a withering attack is redirected into the victim, no initiative is gained from the damage. If a decisive attack is redirected, the attacker's initiative does not reset.

                                                            Once the victim is crashed, incapacitated, or killed, the knife's lust for pain is satiated and the effect ends.

                                                            Special Activation Rules: This Evocation can only be awakened once the Exalt has built up at least four Spite charges against a single target.

                                                            The Stalker Waits
                                                            Cost: 3m (1wp); Mins: Essence 1; Type: Simple
                                                            Keywords: Uniform
                                                            Duration: Indefinite
                                                            Prerequisites: The Gathering Ire
                                                            Retribution is fullest when the timing is right. Pain is greatest in moments of weakness. The shards of hate will wait until those moments to strike. The Exalt casts a knife at a target within range. It veers away, waiting for the proper moment to strike. It can be mentally directed to stay as close to the target as the Exalt desires or to hide. If so, it follows the normal stealth rules, the Exalt rolling (Wits + Stealth) with (Essence) automatic successes. Stealth Charms may be used to enhance the knife. Spotting it is difficult; the Awareness roll suffers a -3 penalty due to the knife's size and each rolled 1 subtracts a success from the result.

                                                            When the Exalt desires the knife to strike, she may take a combat action to make Thrown attack. If needed, an aim action still needs to be taken first. The Exalt doesn't need to be near the target, or even have line of sight. If the shard wasn't spotted, it's an ambush attack. Any subsequent attacks from stalking shards within the same scene are surprise attacks.

                                                            At Essence 2+, the Exalt can spend one Willpower to make the Thrown attack reflexive. If multiple knives are hidden and they all attack at the same instant, they are all ambushes. If the shard causes a new level of wound penalties, the Willpower is refunded.

                                                            Special Activation Rules: This Evocation can only be awakened once the Exalt has successfully wounded a target with an ambush or surprise attack with a shard of hate.

                                                            Unrelenting Torment
                                                            Cost: 3m +1m per knife (1wp); Mins: Essence 2; Type: Simple
                                                            Keywords: Dual
                                                            Duration: Indefinite
                                                            Prerequisites: The Stalker Waits
                                                            This Evocation functions like the Stalker Waits except for the following: Multiple knives can be cast at once, each knife after the first simply adding one mote to this Evocation's cost. If the knives hide, only one Stealth roll needs to be made for all the shards, and any Charms used to enhance their stealth benefits all of them.

                                                            For one Willpower, this Evocation also allows the Exalt to take an unflurriable combat action to make decisive attacks against a single target with up to (Essence + 1 or Spite charges, max 5) stalking knives in one instant. She must spread her Initiative as evenly as possible between all the attacks, each getting at least two Initiative. Initiative doesn't reset until after all attacks are complete, and none require aim actions. If a knife deals damage, the next attack gains +1 damage. If it increased wound penalties, the next gains additional bonus damage equal to the new wound penalty.

                                                            Pain Amplification
                                                            Cost: 5m, 3i; Mins: Essence 3; Type: Supplemental
                                                            Keywords: Withering-Only, Perilous
                                                            Duration: Instant
                                                            Prerequisites: Life Gets Worse
                                                            This Evocation supplements a Thrown attack, the shards exhilarated by their victim's pain. The damage rolls gain double 9s if the target is in -1 wound penalties, double 8s if in -2 wound penalties, and double 7s if in -4 wound penalties.

                                                            This Evocation can be used once per scene. It's reset when the Exalt reaches a new wound penalty.

                                                            Special Activation Rules: This Evocation can only be awakened once the Exalt has used a shard of hate to inflict a -4 wound penalty or kill a target.

                                                            Final Retribution
                                                            Cost: 6m, 1wp; Mins: Essence 3; Type: Simple
                                                            Keywords: Decisive-Only
                                                            Duration: Instant
                                                            Prerequisites: The Stalker Waits, Pain Amplification
                                                            The shards swirl into the air, coming together into their original form, the singular weapon of retribution it once was: a great bladed javelin, black as midnight, swirling with iron lines that glistens like oil. The Exalt lifts it and hurls it the target of his ire. This decisive Thrown attack needs no aim action at medium range and requires one for long range. It can only be made against someone who the Exalt has at least five Spite charges against, unless the Exalt holds a negative Intimacy towards them: reduce the number of Spite charges required by one per Intimacy level. Spite charges are doubled for this attack. Spite charges used on the attack roll covert dice to automatic successes instead of rerolls. The attack deals ([1 + target's current wound penalty] x 3) lethal damage, ignoring hardness. The Exalt's initiative does not reset from this attack.

                                                            At the end of the round, the spear of vengeance explodes back into the shards of hate as the force of will the Exalt has exerted to hold the weapon together can no longer sustain it as a singular weapon. That explosion deals (Spite charges + 1) dice of lethal damage to everyone within close range of the weapon. This is treated as an environmental hazard requiring a (Dexterity + Dodge) roll at difficulty (Essence + 1) to evade. If the original attack struck and dealt at least one damage to the target, it pierces through her, unable to be removed; she takes aggravated damage by the explosion and cannot roll to evade.

                                                            Spite charges against the target are reset after using this Evocation.

                                                            Special Activation Rules: Rather than being bought with experience, this Evocation is awakened after the Exalt gains a Major of Defining Intimacy of hatred toward someone, or if someone has directly offended or defied one of the Exalt's Major of Defining Principles. This Evocation can only be first activated against that individual.
