              [So this should be approximately around the time Heracles and completed his first two labors (which his Artifacts are trophies from), establishing himself a great slayer of monsters and breaking into Essence 2. On the quest for the Golden Hind now that he's essence 2 he'll learn Trackless Region Navigation and Unshakeable Bloodhound Technique, as well as continue to build up his Athletics feats-of-strength Charms. By the time he's on Labor Eight (the Mares of Diomedes) he'll meet up with Orpheus in Not-Thrace who will educate him in the Occult enough for Heracles to learn to wrestle gods, and then the two will travel with Jason toward the Dreaming Sea as Heracles seeks the Girdle of Hippolyta. By the time Heracles is holding up the Dome of Heaven, he'll be Essence 5.]

              [I'm pretty proud of giving him Craft Earthworks and Craftsman Needs No Tool, as together with his Strength Enhancing Athletics Charms it will allow for things like the cleaning of the Aegean Stables]

                              Willpower: *****

                              Limit Trigger: Achieving a pursued goal (when the hunt/quest/etc is over, he gets bored - when he gets bored, he gets stupid)

                              Intimacies: Kingdom of his Past Life [Not-Thessaly] (righteous desire - Defining), Argo (Pride - Major), Crew (Loyalty - Major), Dragon-Blooded Social Order (Betrayal - Major), Venus (gratitude - Minor)

                              Charms
                              Sail: Salty Dog Method, Safe Bearing Technique, Ship Claiming Stance, Weather Anticipating Intuition, Hull-Preserving Technique, Hull-Taming Transfusion, Wind Defying Course Technique, Current Cutting Technique, Tide Carried Omens, Chaos-Cutting Galley, Blood & Salt Bondage

                              Melee: Excellent Strike, One Weapon Two Blows, Dipping Swallow Defense, Bulwark Stance, Solar Counterattack

                              Presence: Listener-Swaying Argument, Harmonious Presence Method, Excellent Friend Approach

                              Resistance: Ox-Body Technique (x3)

                              [So Jason's narrative unlike my other 4 Solar Argonauts, is specifically about reclaiming a crown that is his birthright. That's not unique in Greek Myth of course, but it is on this team, so I leaned into that and tied it to the Usurpation narrative rather than him being a literal lost prince. Furthermore, since Hera doesn't have a close equivalent in the Exalted Pantheon, I decided that the divine female figure who he carried on his back and acts as his adviser is his PREVIOUS SELF. He had a vision of her during his Exaltation. Venus is instead his patron goddess he directs his personal worship toward, for reasons that should be obvious in the source legend.]

                              [As he already needed to be a sailor for that to be his Supernal Ability in his Exaltation, I decided he had background a tradesman for the Guild before he Exalted (since without the pre-existing identity as a lost prince, he wouldn't be raised in protected isolation by Chiron). This fits better I think for the suave charming seductive swashbuckler he turns out to be than isolated pupil of a centaur]

                              [                              If it isn't obvious, I imagine Jason as a smirky asshole who you can't help but like. If you're attracted to men, you'd probably want to sleep with him and be annoyed by that]
