                                        Originally posted by ale137 View Post
                                        Are the peasant families in the realm also matriarchal?
                                        As above, so below.

                                        Am i playing the rules if i task a demon to enter a Yasal Crystal and give me its powers?
                                        The (tenuous) balance of yasal crystals is built around requiring you to bargain with the bound spirit for access to its powers. If you've bound a demon to serve you loyally for a year and a day, and you order it into a crystal, then sure, I guess you get to short circuit that. If you task bind a demon to stay in the crystal forever, then you're still going to have to bargain with it separately each time you want to access its power.
