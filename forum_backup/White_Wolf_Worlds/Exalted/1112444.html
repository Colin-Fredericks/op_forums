    Thinking about "off-Aspect" Dragon-Blooded Charms

    07-06-2017, 09:29 PM
    It must be such a great time to be a Dragon-Blooded fan, because I'm becoming more of one each day I think about what they're going to be like in Third Edition. Specialized Excellencies, Signature Charms, the Aura State system, and Charms of every elemental Aspect in each of the 25 Abilities.

    That last item is what I want to talk about, here. I want to share some ideas I have for Charm concepts we might see, and that I would like to see.

    I'd also like to see your ideas.


    ARCHERY (Wood)

    The original Wood Aspect Charmset for Archery Charms was cool, and I'm hoping to see tricks like Harvest of the Hunter expanding into the promised trick of creating an entire Womping Willow tree from your bow.

    Air-aspect Charms can control air currents to provide additional range, cut through inclement weather, and defeat cover. Hunter's Unobstructed Aim is going to become even less obstructed. There's also the ideas of shooting lightning bolts as arrows, or shooting clouds to produce rain and lightning.

    Earth-aspect Charms might work upon metal arrowheads as Wood-magic works upon shafts. Otherwise, I could see dragon-arrows creating sinkholes and earthquakes as they strike the earth like meteorites.

    Fire-magic means that Solar Exalted aren't the only ones shooting flaming, exploding arrows around. But here's hoping that we see some honest-to-goodness FIREWAND Charms. Maybe they'll have grafted in the Terrestrial Charms from Golden Exhalation Style?

    I'm not too clear on Water tricks.


    ATHLETICS (Fire)

    Effortlessly Rising Flame might become a Dodge Charm, now that rising from prone is rolled with Dodge when rolled at all. It would be right at home with Hopping Firecracker Evasion.

    Fire-magic has always provided the "blazing speed" that Dragon-Blooded Athletics needs, but now Air-magic can provide the aerial grace that it has previously lacked. They may have even better floating tricks than Solars.

    Earth-magic will finally let Dragon-Blooded stand upside-down on ceilings. Perfect Climbing Attitude could have found a new home in this Ability if not for the fact that climbing is sort of pointless next to running or flying up walls.

    Water-magic will, of course, finally make Fire Aspects great swimmers.

    Wood-magic, Vance has said, will contribute the vital lifting power of a tree that steadily raises up the great weight of its growing branches and fruit. It might also include the creeping, tearing strength of roots that break apart soil and stone.

    Earth and Wood magic together will likely allow Dragon-Blooded to lift great masses of stone, wood, or living creatures, and hurl them great distances -- possibly with the aid of Air for more distance.

    Earth might further contribute power for breaking apart stone targets, or bearing up great weights for long periods of time.


    AWARENESS (Earth)

    Air-magic for hearing sounds on the wind, and catching scents on the breeze.

    Fire-magic for seeing in the dark with the smallest amount of light, producing enough light to illuminate or even blind, and also protection against being blinded. Sense-Destroying Method and Essence Disruption Attack also fit the violent Essence of Fire.

    Wood-magic might help the Dragon-Blooded become more aware of the particular scents of specific people, beasts, and plants.


    CRAFT (Earth)

    Air-magic might include Charms that greatly assist the repair and construction of Artifacts, owing to how Occult and Lore are Air-aspected Abilities vital to those actions. In a Modernized Creation, this would also seem like a good Aspect for fixing and making electronic devices, which also resonates with how computer science is based on Lore.

    Fire, of course, is natural for assisting in metalworking, ceramics, and glassblowing, as well as any object-destruction Charms you might find in Dragon-Blooded Craft. Fire has a habit of finding and consuming any flaw.

    Water might finally give us Charms specifically for cooking food, as well as preparing medicines, poisons, perfumes, and make-up.

    Wood would be carpentry, obviously. Possibly even the power to shape living trees into the forms the Prince of the Earth desires.


    DODGE (Fire)

    Again, Air provides some of the grace that Fire lacked.

    Beyond that, I don't have much. But I kind of hope we see the elemental-based Clone Jutsus seen in Naruto -- Mud Clones, Sand Clones, Water Clones, Smoke Clones, or simple substitution logs.

      INTEGRITY (Earth)

      Air-magic is perfect for meditation Charms, as well as refuting social influence with rational, fact-based logic, and perhaps also protecting Principles that take the form of high-minded ideals.

      Fire-magic might be better for refuting social influence with passionate feelings, and protecting Ties.

      Water-magic is natural for refuting social influence through slippery logic, legal-ese, twisting the letter of an agreement, and finding tortured justifications.

      Wood-magic offers the perfect metaphor for treating your values like a garden -- pruning unwanted Intimacies while nursing passions and grudges into lusty flowers or weeds. Combined with Earth and Air's meditation, it can also help restore the body.


      INVESTIGATION (Water)

      Not really sure about this one, beyond Fire's ability to illuminate the dark of the underworld and threaten anyone whom an investigator needs to confess.


      LINGUISTICS (Air)

      I don't know. Fire might give you the power to scorch words into surfaces with a burning fingertip, or write messages of shining fire in the empty air?


      LORE (Air)

      Earth-magic could help a Dragon-Blooded Lore specialist understand the purpose and function of First Age artifacts. In a Modernized Creation, it could also lend itself to writing computer code.

      Water-magic would excel at computer hacking.

      Fire-magic might help with illuminating minds and inspiring a passion to learn.

      Wood-magic might also lend metaphors of planting seeds in the minds of others.

      The Elemental Bolt/Burst Charms might become more varied.

      I think either Lore or Integrity might contain Charms to provide new ways to use or control the Aura State mechanic.


      MEDICINE (Wood)

      Air... I'm not sure. Perhaps dealing with mental disorders, a la Madness-Analyzing Stare?

      Earth might offer ways to surgically remove foreign objects, as well as surgically graft on replacement prosthetic limbs. It could also include massage, chiropractic adjustment, and possibly acupuncture.

      Fire would treat diseases through fevers, as well as working to sterilize and cauterize in general. There's also moxibustion and heat-treatment therapies, like those seen from Jackie Chan's character in the remake of Karate Kid.

      Water... treating patients with medicine and food?
