    How common are Solar marriages?

    04-28-2016, 05:48 PM
    So I realized an interesting pattern among fluff Solars recently, that being that it is very rare to see romantic relationships between Solars. The only two examples that I could find were Desus & Bright (not exactly a healthy relationship) , Alkadi & Luthe's admin (clearly stated as a marriage of convenience) & Gold-Shadowed Arrow and his lover (who dont seem to be wed either). So do you think that Solars rarely bonded romantically or that they were merely not mentioned frequently? Also do you think that they were predisposed towards unhealthy relationships (of those only Jades predecessor seemed to be remotely healthy)?

                                                          Leviathans Solar mate in the first age, Admiral Arkade I believe was his name, was married to another Solar. Who Leviathan was having an affair with.

                                                          Now if you think about it. A Solar doesn't have a lot of options in the dating pool.                                                           If you think about it watching everyone around you grow old and die, while you remain young and strong, can be very isolating.
