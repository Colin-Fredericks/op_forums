            Accelerator, it's an error to treat the Oadenol point-buy system as some real statement about how manses are actually built, or as describing the actual tradeoffs involved. It's there to provide a rough framework for players to use for homebrewed manses, and isn't supposed to be gimmicked with stuff like "I'll take a bunch of points of maintenance, and then separately summon a demon to perform that maintenance". In particular, if you are summoning guardians or maintainers for your manse, that ought to be included in the point balance of the manse, even if you're like "haha syke actually I'll do it afterwards".

                        You can get some basic stats: For instance, It's feats of strength pool is 9 dice and it can do strength 5 feats so it's got strength 5 or 6 and athletics of 3-4. The fact that it has a grapple pool of 9 (dex + brawl) and a control pool of 10 (str + brawl) suggests that if it's got strength 5 then it's got brawl of 5 and dex of 4. This doesn't quite jive with evasion of 1, but these stat blocks are never rigorously tested for cohesion. He might have an internal mobility penalty from being crystal though. Next we know stealth is 2 from the dice pool. Next up: Perception, wits and awareness. We know that join battle (wits + awareness) is 6 dice and senses (perception+ awareness) is 8 dice. So we know that wits is at least 2 less than perception. I'd peg them at Wits 3, Perception 5, and awareness at 3. Based on what stats you'd use for navigating underground you might be able to pin down survival. If it uses int+ survival then you're just guessing. Finally, the social stats we can pin down fairly accurately: Guile (Manip+socialize)/2 is 6, resolve (wits + integrity)/2 is 4, read intentions (perception + socialize) is 6 and social influence is 8. So we know wits and perception so integrity must be 5, socialize is 1 so therefore manipulation must be... 11. Yeah again these stat blocks are mostly just a grab bag. There is no possible way for this stat block to jive properly based on these randomly generated numbers. I'd say that his manip is 5 but he has a huge boost to guile based on him being made of crystal (hard to read you know). Which would make presence 3 based on his social influence pool.

                        So the breakdown would be:
                        STR-5
                        DEX-4
                        STA-?
                        CHA-?
                        MAN-5
                        APP-5
                        INT-?
                        WIT-3
                        PER-5

                        Athletics-4, Awareness-3, Brawl-5, Dodge-4, Integrity-5, Presence-3, Socialize-1, Survival-2

                        You can take a guess at the remaining attributes, but abilities not listed here are prob 0. His natural combat movement pool of 5 is lower than it should be, but again, mobility penalties might have some influence there. The penalty to combat movement (3 less than it should be from dex+athletics) gave me an idea of the mobility penalty it's working under which lead to figuring out dodge skill there.

                        Hope this helps!

                                    Originally posted by Jen View Post

                                    In third edition, those are called the Cycles of Air/water/et cetera. Problem solved!

                                    In more words: the Cycles of Air and Water are wintry/prevernal, the Cycle of Wood is akin to spring, Fire maps to summer, and Earth is autumnal. Distribute the seasons as you see fit, depending on the local climate.
