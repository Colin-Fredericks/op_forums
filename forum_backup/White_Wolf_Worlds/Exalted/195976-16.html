                Originally posted by The MG View Post
                I've never seen a Solar PC with SCS.

                Why would anyone want it, anyway? All the cool spells are Terrestrial.

                I can answer this question actually. My character concept was one where my second age incarnation was actually hunted by his first age incarnation. He left traps and snares and hints around that would lead me to events that would shape me to be like him, leaving memory crystals and vast armories to give me his memories, and prepare me for a war of revenge with the Dragon-Blooded.

                I used the sacrifice stages to purge myself of him, such as releasing my familiar at Terrestrial (freeing it from many of the mind-warping charms it was under to influence me), breaking my weapons (which were a key to unlocking vast armories hidden in the Wyld, waiting for me to reach them, with their soldier-servant mind-altered inhabitants. For my Solar Circle, I gave up all memories of the First Age that were a part of my incarnation. I had to use an NA artifact, a Wyld zone, and a sidereal who had become half Fair folk to do it, and it left me with a serious long-term problem of progressive amnesia, where I could remember thelast few years, but that's it, and as time went by, I lost those memories as well. Was pretty bad at the point where I met one of my sons and didn't remember his mother, despite her being the love of my life.

                Still, that's what I use Solar Circle Sorcery for. It completes the transformation of a character. It's a mechanism for storytelling, not really a power grab.

                        Well, its going to be more complex than this, in any case. Or at least I hope so. Everything I say here is SPECULATIVE at best. I see it as: everyone who has an associated material gets access to that material. Solars get top access to the six magical materials (Orichalcum, Moonsilver, Starmetal, Soulsteel, Jade, and Adamant). Most people get access to the second tier of materials other than their own, or only first tier if it 'opposes.' I don't know what that means, or if they will do something, but I wouldn't mind seeing Moonsilver oppose Jade, and Soulsteel oppose Starmetal, and Adamant oppose Orichalcum.

                        I also want to see workarounds, and not some lame-ass five-fold universal adaptor. You want access to the top tier of a weapon type you normally don't get?
                        Go talk to the Infernals. Get them to make you a replica out of hell-tainted brass that burns with green fire instead of gold.
                        Go talk to the Yozis and sign up for one of those Akuma contracts.
                        Go talk to the Fair Folk about forging you a replica, or shaping you into something that can use it. Better get that checkbook out! It's gonna cost.
                        If you are a Lunar, go hunt down and eat a Solar, a la the Golden Widow society. Or Terrestrial, or Sidereal, or whatever. I really hope you don't pick soulsteel, because unless you have some Stamina charms, that's going to give you some bad rumbly-tumbly-tummy.
                        If you are a Martial Artist, go master your essence to the point where you can mimic another splat's essence through a form-type kata.
                        Go make a deal with the Alchemicals where they carve open your chest and put a big gold ring around your heart like Tony Stark. Oh, and don't forget the Clarity while you are there.

                        Talk to your ST about it. And remember, you aren't playing Exalted right if you aren't breaking the rules! That's why I love it when the authors say 'Here's something you cannot do. Absolutely not, under any circumstances, can you have this cookie. That just means I have to write a damn good story about why I get this and you don't.
