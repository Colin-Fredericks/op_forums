                                                I'm trying to figure out how many Lore charms i need to take to start with God-Kings Shrike, which has led me to study the prerequisite rules and now I'm wondering how non-specific prerequisite charms are supposed to work.

                                                Now reading it completely literally, the rules on p. 254 only stop you from claiming charms that are prerequisites for the specific prerequisite charms as the non-specific prerequisites, so in the case of God-Kings Shrike technically you'd be able to claim Harmonious Academic Methodology, Bottomless Wellsprings Approach, and Lore-Inducing Concentration as the non-specific prerequisites, since none of them are prerequisites for Prophet of Seventeen Cycles (the only specific prerequisite) as there's nothing in the book saying this rule or the definition of 'prerequisite' is recursive. Obviously this is not what's intended though as it'd make it all fairly meaningless, so how is it supposed to work?

                                                Say I want Heaven-Turning Calculations, Wake the Sleeper, and God-Kings Shrike. I can clearly take everything up to Sacred Relic Understanting + Prophet of Seventeen Cycles without running into this problem, and then claim both of those as the non-specific charms to take Heaven-Turning Calculations. So far its easy to figure out how it's supposed to work.

                                                But could I then count Heaven-Turning Calculations + Prophet of Seventeen Cycles as the non-specifics to take Wake the Sleeper, since neither of those lead up to Sacred Relic Understanding (the only specific prerequisite), then use Sacred Relic Understanding + Heaven-Turning Calculation + Wake the Sleeper as the non-specific prerequisites for God-Kings Shrike, since again none of those were required to take Prophet of Seventeen Cycles?
