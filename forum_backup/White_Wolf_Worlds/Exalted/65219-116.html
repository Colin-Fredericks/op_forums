                When the Solar Deliberative was formed the choice of the 1st initiative was crucial. A Zenith proposed to make the reduction of infant mortality in Creation by 99% their 1st mission, spawning 15 years. It was an impartial project, simple in its formulation, and was unanimously adopted.

                It laid the foundation to deploy health institutions across the world, improve education, and drastically improved overall happiness everywhere as well as set into Jade the support for the Deliberative in the peoples mind.

                Also while it was done within half the timeline it sprouted so many local challenges that a welfare committee was soon created and ran the greatest projects of the first hundred years of the Deliberative.

                        Jefepato created something nice, like a sorcerous working for making dwarf wheat. You know what? I think in Creation, there was a lot of this kind of shit. The crazier kinds of alterations died out, but by and by, Creation has a lot of things that were leftovers from sorcerous experiments, the first age, or people who granted mutations to crops and animals and other stuff to better themselves. So in other words, all the stuff that's already there? Someone's probably touched it sorcerously. The contagion did a real number on it. Those grapes that release sweet wine and whose leaves act as insect repellent? Not natural. Those flowers which glow like lamps after you pluck them from the vine? Made by a sorcerer for his sweetheart. That wheat which an agriculturalist would recognise as dwarf wheat? A remnant from the Shogunate, when the Dragonblooded realised that there were no more blessings from the gods.
