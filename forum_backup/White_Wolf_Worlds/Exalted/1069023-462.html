                                                            Originally posted by Phoenix_Kensai View Post

                                                            I was expecting Dragon Blooded character creation to be roughly the same as it is for Solars, so this is interesting. Are you willing to say more yet about your reasons for starting them at Essence 2, or any other differences in character creation?
                                                            As he’s commented on it elsewhere: default Solar PCs are recently Exalted adults. Default DB PCs are adults who Exalted in their adolescence and (if Dynasts, Lookshyan, etc) have had years of training of how to use their abilities until they reached maturity. Hence the whole Secondary School thing in the Realm.

                                                            You can play a newly Exalted Essence 1 DB, but as that generally means playing an adolescent, it isn’t the default.
