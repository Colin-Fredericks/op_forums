            I had Alchemicals in a Shard that took place in the 1920s.

            Basically the setting was Dragon-Blooded and Sidereals only until 1916 when the Solars and Lunars entered play (and unbeknownst to everyone else, the Abyssals and Infernals). The Alchemical Exalted came a little later after that as the brainchild of Communist Lunars who'd sought a form of exaltation that would be compatible with their values and ultimately forced to co-exist with mortals rather than rule over them. Working in conjunction with a Daybreak Caste necromancer and a renagade Jadeborn scientist one of their first creations was Stalin so by all accounts they failed spectacularly.

            Lenin isn't dead in the glass coffin but instead had sacrificed much of his essence to make this doomed vision a reality. The Alchemicals themselves were initially created by melting down a lot of the artifact panopolies of the Russian nobility but they were already starting to run out of other materials particularly Starmetal which saw undercover teams being scrambled to every falling meteor in the USSR in the hopes of scoring some, often contested by Sidereal teams who needed it too.

            Their colossi forms were going to resemble military vehicles with some stylistic liberties for the fact they're sentient beings. For example Stalin will as he gains power eventually have taken on a colossi form of a Soviet armoured train (not unlike the one in Golden Eye that's affectionately known as the "Ugly Face Train" by my fiancée) trying to hold onto power via drone body as he wandered Russia's railways like if Thomas the Tank Engine was a mad dictator before ultimately merging with Stalingrad to achieve his final form.

            It was fun, helped me unwind while working on my MA.

                                In Shards of the Exalted Dream, the Exalted Modern shard had Alchemicals as a secret government black project that essentially fused Lunar exaltations with Autochthonian fragments, mixed them with the Magical Materials, and ended up creating a bunch of gemstones that were SUPPOSED to turn people into cyborg slave operatives, but they failed on the slave part.

                                They way they work is that you put the gem on a Mortal’s forehead, it extends filaments through the mortal’s body (and soul) turning them into an Alchemical Exalt for the rest of their lives (once they die, it retracts and can be reused). Power-wise, the main difference between them and normal Alchemicals is that, instead of going to a Vat chamber, they can make their Exaltation go Dormant for a similar amount of time to reconfigure itself. They can also grow new charms and slots in their Exaltations.
