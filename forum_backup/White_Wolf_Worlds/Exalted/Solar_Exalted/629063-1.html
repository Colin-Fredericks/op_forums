    I like both major possibilities raised in the thread; that is,

    A) All Primordials bound as Yozi other than Gaia and Autochthon, with the nascent Underworld (and maybe Deathlords) the result of Exalted hubris sans Great Curse,

    or

    B) Primordial War several centuries or millennia in the making, with hot and cold phases.

    Both are very fertile ground for Shards, to second what others have said. Both also allow for Alchemicals in Creation in ways that don't require shoehorning the Locust Crusade out of the way (assuming that shows back up in however many years, anyway).

                                                    Originally posted by Zelbinnean View Post
                                                    Then I posit that if there had been no permakilling there would be no threat to force the Primordials to surrender. The Exalted likely wouldn't know that they could even do so, having no experience to show otherwise, or at the very least wouldn't know the result. If they hadn't implemented permakilling it implies they were not willing to deal with whatever hypothetical consequences it could cause and were unwilling to roll the dice and see.

                                                    I don't think the war would be winnable in that situation because the Exalted would lack leverage beyond "we will change your forms and annoy you."
                                                    I admit I haven't read the material in ages, but I don't recall the Primordials regarding fetich death as no big deal. It would just be hard for me to swallow if they did. Looking at Adorjan next to Adrian and Sacheverell next to Lidless Eye That Sees... I have trouble conceiving of the Primordials interpreting that in any way other than, "Holy shit!" It is a cessation of self in just about every way that's likely to matter to them. They cease to be, and some other jerk walks off with their Essence, having reshuffled their themes and motifs around. If they have for comparison the complete existential agony that is becoming a Neverborn on the table, sure, I could maybe see it coming off as favorable, but, well... It's not, for the purposes of this thread.

                                                    Hell, do we even have any reason to think fetich death ever even happened before the Primordial War? There's no mention. And the Primordials aren't merely presented as unkillable but as more-or-less unassailable before the Exalted came along. I could imagine that fetich death alone would be a pretty sobering discovery. The Exalted could inflict it on them and in a rather distressing quantity. Fighting a foe that just keeps coming, and proves very capable of bringing on an awful degeneration in more and more of your siblings, and it becomes increasingly clear that it could be just a matter of chance and time until that could happen to you...

                                                    I expect the Primordial War would have dragged on longer, some more Primordials would have become other than we know them before it ended, and the terms might have been less unpleasant for them. But I don't have difficulty believing the Primordials might have ultimately surrendered. It's not necessarily what would have happened, but I find it very conceivable based on what we know.
