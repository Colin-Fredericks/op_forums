    Wanna play a solar crafter? Dislike the charmset? Good news!

    08-03-2020, 09:47 AM
    So did I.
    So I just made a new craft charmset from scratch.

    It still uses the default craft system as-is, as I feel it is good on the lower end and adequate on the higher end.
    I had 3 major goals in mind:
    1) crafting artifacts is a huge deal and its own thing, and so crafters not interested in artifacts should get more love.
    2) minimize convolution. You won't see 5 charms that are numerical expansions on the same thing all the time. You want oodles of xp? that's 4 charms already including "buy dots with craft xp" and "transform craft xp".
    3) encourage different playstyles and ways to mix crafting with the rest of the game. Solar crafters are miracle workers even before they touch their first magical material.

    I balanced the roll boosters around "you want to use progressively more of them as you craft bigger stuff" and tried to keep the conservative odds at "if you don't push for external aides like exceptional tools and stunts you will be taking a sensible risk of failure". I also tried to keep the math simple (the most complex it gets is the capstone doubling accrued successes at the expense of craft xp) and unconditional.

                          This is intended.

                          Here's the rough success chances for a legendary project presuming you are going full excellency + all potency charms on every roll, and then adding 2 dice (stunt 1), 3 dice (stunt 1 + exceptional tools), 4 dice (stunt 1 + exceptional tools with whatever shenanigans give you +2).


                          excellency + all power charms 43.62%
                          excellency + all power charms + stunt 1 77.39%
                          excellency + all power charms + stunt 1 + exceptional workshop 88.19%
                          excellency + all power charms + stunt 1 + exceptional workshop++ 94.56%
                          A stunt 2 on the rolls has a similar effect, as does spending wp, although at this point you'll be easily drained 5-8 wp and have to take time off the rolls (which usually isn't important, but it is going to take a couple days more to finish the project).

                          The idea that even a solar has to go that extra mile to secure the greatest undertakings appeals to me, and was one of the better general results I got fiddling with the numbers. Better than non-charm dice charms by a mile.
