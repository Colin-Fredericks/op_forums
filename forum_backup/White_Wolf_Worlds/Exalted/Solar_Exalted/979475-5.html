                                                            I only just realised that my last character is an orphan, but I think orphan works because people with decent, whole families and regular dayjobs don't necessarily seek out adventure, which is something of a barrier to exaltation (although one of my current circle has a half-blood ape wife and went back to his office job after exalting and just hoped no-one would notice). Generally, if I don't specify parents/siblings/aunts and uncles/grandparents then my ST is free, within reason, to do what they like with them, and so am I. For instance, if we roll into a town and I've got a dot in Contacts, I'll weave Aunt Ciocia into the town and we'll go visit her, rather than approaching Contact #3346, but if we do, my ST is more than welcome to tell us that Uncle Wujek hasn't come back from his trip one village over, and start a little adventure.

                                                            P.S I realised while writing this that we don't have a joint-noun for aunts and uncles like we do for most other familial connections. Apparently nieces and nephews are "niblings".
