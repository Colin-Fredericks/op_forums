    Of course Havesh isn't a nice-guy "Robin Hood" type character. That was intentional, he was one of the Solars expressly written to cleave closely to the tales of the Anathema that the Dragon Blooded tell so he was supposed to fall a bit more towards the villain slot than the typical hero slot. Still, there's quite a bit more to him than just being some kind of two dimensional sadistic serial killer.
    Rather than chose to make something better of himself or the city, he did what he always did and took advantage of his powers just to kill people and make money. Havesh had plenty of options and he took the one that was easy and fun for himself. I don't know why you think someone who was poor can't just be a shitty person. I used to live in one of the worst places in miami for 14 years of my life. Shitty people would be shitty regardless of how poor or rich they were. That's just a poorly rationalized excuse.

        Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post
        You know, I realised that if I was going to look for reference points for Havesh in other fiction, one of the better choices would be just about any major character from the Guardians of the Galaxy movies. Maybe it would help his image a bit if he quipped occasionally, or was portrayed by a charismatic and attractive man. That's not really something that I would want to fault him for, considering that I don't think he's ever had a positive relationship or any kind of reinforcement in his life.

        Seriously, I can't really take the idea that Havesh was intended as a depiction of a Solar that goes down the wrong path and abuses the power bestowed upon him, when he's given the backstory of having been a homeless orphan. That's kind of the worst thing that it's possible to be, consistently at any point throughout human history. Kind of an endless tirade of mind-numbing boredom, deprivation, necessary violence, and if you manage to never actually be abused, it's probably a spectre that you live under constantly. If he was supposed to be straightforwardly bad, he probably should have come from a reasonably comfortable background.

        Certainly, if the development of his empathy was greatly stunted from having probably barely ever experienced it, he's at least doing well enough to direct his ire towards the people that benefit from and perpetuate the social hierarchy that resulted in his entire life up until he was Exalted being a miserable slog, rather than taking their cue and going for the more vulnerable people in his immediate vicinity.

        I often think about how the cruellest murder that Havesh ever committed, the one that entailed a bit of torture and was not prompted by payment or self-preservation, was directed at a man that he had earlier seen go out of his way to abuse a homeless person, because he was so very certain of the rightness of his privileged social station as a result of having been born at a certain time of the day, and because he didn't think that anybody would stop him.

        Havesh might improve a bit if he ever had a friend, preferably one whose draw to him is not predicated on a desire to exploit his talent for murder.

        Havash bothered me more because he's a rapist then because he's a murderer.Maybe I missed it in this thread, but that's the mark of the wrong path to me. "I murder rich folks and take there stuff" is fine, all things considered. It's not a special kind of bad. Rape is.
        I'd be less bothered if Havash living up life as the wealthy didn't include sleeping with his victims wives.

        As for the great curse...I'm fine with cartoonish evil. I'm fine with First Age Solars traping political prisoners in war golems to fight on the fields of battle, of chaining rivers to starve dissedents, of being all varieties of nightmarish awful there ever was. My own games featured First Age Solars that:
        -that established an cult of ethnic cleansing and genocide,crafting sorcerous creatures and leading legions in a fascist death cult to establish a perfect order before his Lunar Mate 10 Plauges his empire.
        -Preformed invasive alchemical experiments on his martial arts students and himself, eventually creating a being out of his inner id that murdered him
        -Built a vast forge that employed demons from malfeas in an effort to make a perfect metal idol to replace the sun, ulimately kidnapping and enslaving dissedents and locking them in suits of iron that forced them to work before Sidearals killed her for attempting to recreate a prehuman race of demon-priests
        -Trapped five behemoths in his crown and used their power to puruse a number of failed and bloody wars, before finally seeling them ina vast yasal crystal beneath ysyr
        -Trapped an army of fae in bodies of brass to create a legion of djinn and enslave the local population
        -Traversed the realms of existence and collected people he liked in his vast manse, before going to the ruin-land of Zen Mu and being devoured by a dragon there.

        My point of reference for the Usurpation, I guess, is mythic extinction events: Floods, wraths of God, sinkings of continents, and so on. Which means evil of a scope, scale, and depth that it's rather ...silly.
        My only preference is that it's not 'just' solars who were decadent, catastrophic monsters that really needed to be stopped from their vast and self consuming hubris. At minimum Lunars I prefer to implicate as well.
