        It seems like everyone who is arguing against me is trying to trap me in concepts of "good" that they themselves are bringing to the table. At no point have I expressed that I think every Solar is "good" in the sense that we might call an altruistic, even-tempered, upstanding citizen in a Western democratic republic "good", and I now explicitly disclaim that belief.

        When I say every Solar is "good" at the moment of Exaltation, what I mean is that every person who receives Solar Exaltation is a person who believes in some state of affairs as ideal beyond their own involvement in it, and who endeavors to bring that state of affairs about. Freedom. Democracy. Literacy. Longevity. Piety. Justice. Any number of things, many of which may come into conflict with each other. A Solar is never just a person who is really good at what they do, nor a person who has great personal ambitions. A Solar is a person who endeavors to create the world they think is best.

        If Solar Exaltation were simply about the potential to be personally great, simply about having an impact on the world, regardless of why, then you wouldn't have Yurgen Kaneko wandering out into the frozen wastes to die and coming back Exalted instead, nor Panther getting Exalted for noticing he's living a live of debauchery. You wouldn't have every Zenith getting told to spread righteousness in their own idiomatic way.

        I love Exalted for not nailing down what individual behaviors and perspectives and methods constitute "good", nor requiring a Storyteller or Player to do so. I think that's precisely why the Solars can all be "good" and also all be different and interesting and at odds with each other.

                                                        To a large extent, Good is a "Point of View." So is "Evil." If you are on one side, then you are doing "Good;" if you are on the receiving end then the other side is "Evil." Yes...this is a "Simplistic" explanation, but that is how things have been broken down throughout History; even today.

                                                        As has been mentioned, many things that we view as "Evil" today were, at one time-or-another, viewed as being "Good."
                                                        - Forcing people to Convert to a Particular Religion in order to Save their Souls. If they didn't, then execute them as Heretics. Not too long ago that was considered "Good." Nowadays, we see that as "Evil" and call it Terrorism.

                                                        - Sacrificing People, pulling out and burning their Hearts: The Aztecs saw this as "Good" because they were keeping the world Alive. The Europeans saw it as "Evil" and did everything they could to put a stop to it. Of course, the fact that the Aztecs had lots of Gold had nothing to do with it.

                                                        In the 2nd Age, a Solar may oppose the above because they see them as "Evil." Or, they may go forth and Vanquish their enemies in the name of the Unconquered Sun, putting to the sword or sacrificing any who fail to venerate Ignis Divine. Meanwhile, other Solars may oppose this desecration of the worship to the Unconquered Sun, defending the Mortals and rallying them against this Heresy. Both sides would see themselves as "Good" and the other as "Evil."

                                                        Being a Hero does not mean that others see you as "Good," even if you see yourself as "Good."
