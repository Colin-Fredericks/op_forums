    A solution for invincible Solar supremacy

    04-15-2017, 05:43 AM
    I was sorting recyclable trash earlier today and pondered how to make Exalted better with my fan contributions. And then a problem that has not even been hinted yet has occurred to me -- how can I prevent power creep in 3e and maintain that Solars remain best at everything? And then this idea dawned on me.


    DESIGN PARADIGM - ABSOLUTE SOLAR SUPREMACY

    Thesis: Solars are humans taken to superhuman limits, whose excellence is without peer. Other Exalted are also humans. Therefore, Solars must be peerless in specialties of other Exalted. FACT.

    Solution: Solars are best at everything. Whenever a new set of powers is introduced in a Exalted Third Edition splatbook, Solars do it better by using the following rules.


    The Exigents
    Solars gain access to the following Charm -

    I'm So Exalted, Even My Minions Are Exalted
    Cost: 2xp; Mins: Presence 5, Essence 5
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Favor-Conferring Prana

    This Charm is literally Exigence, except that a Solar can do it. This Charm can be used upon the recipient of the prerequisite Charm's effect. They are Exalted by the Solar's supreme supremacy, becoming an Exigent. They can develop their powers and grow to become a peer of any Exalted (except the Solars, and especially their Solar master).


    The Lunars
    Shapeshifting is not actually unique to the Lunars. I mean, like, really. Even kids play pretend at being a tiger and goes all rawr! Therefore Solars can change their shape also, 'cuz they are certainly better than mortal kids. Therefore, if a Solar can even imagine an animal and it has stats, she can transform into that animal. No need to eat their heart, because gross! Parasites!

    Solars also gain access to the following Charm -

    Deadly Man-Man Transformation
    Cost: 1wp; Mins: Socialize 5, Essence 5
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: None
    Duration: One scene
    Prerequisite Charms: Draw the Curtain

    The Solar transforms into a terrifying yet completely acceptable figure, melding the form of the Solar's person with the ideal person. Everything a human can do, the Solar receives an automatic non-Charm success to the related roll. Unrolled values increase by one.


    The Sidereals
    Solars can do astrology even better than the Exalted defined by their power over it. Solars have access to Solar Astrology, the system of which is perfectly identical to Sidereal Astrology, except the Solar fate-crafter receives an automatic non-Charm success to all relevant rolls. They don't even need to learn a Charm to receive this effect.


    The Abyssals
    When an Abyssal shows up and a Solar discovers that it's possible to make others be terrible with Exalted power, he transforms the idea to a more sublime form. Instead of making others worse at what they do, the Solar becomes simply so awe-some that others are awe-struck and forget to resist the Solar.

    The system is identical.


    The Dragon-Blooded
    Solars can manipulate the powerful and primordial elements of Creation by shouting at them. Roll (Charisma + Presence) with Difficulty 1. Success means the Solar has access to all Terrestrial Charms for the rest of the scene. Also, animal flux happens to the Solar as well, but everything glows golden.


    The Liminals
    When suffering from a crippling injury, a Solar can use a spare body part and the following Charm to heal it.

    Immunity to Myself Technique
    Cost: 6m, 1wp; Mins: Resistance 5, Essence 4
    Type: Supplemental
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Immunity to Everything Technique

    The Solar hero will not be defeated -- even by her own self. This Charm can be used to resist autoimmune disease. The roll is (Stamina + Resistance) with Difficulty equal to (the Solar's own Essence), but this Charm grants automatic successes equal to the Solar's Essence. Success means that the Exalt is immune to their own immune system.

    A possible creative application of the Charm is to receive a replacement organ or body part. The Solar can rub the replacement body part on their missing wound and activate this Charm, along with any magic that speeds healing. Success means the Exalt's body accepts the body part as their own.


    All Others
    Solars can do the same thing, but with an automatic non-Charm success.


    [end shitpost]
