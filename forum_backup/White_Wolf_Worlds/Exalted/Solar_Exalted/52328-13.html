                                            I love Performance, conceptually, but frankly most of the use my players have got from it has been from Excellencies. Many of the effects that they thought they could/should pull off with Performance seemed to turn up in Presence or Socialize, or in another Exalt's ability set altogether. So they either reskinned those Charms, stunted, or ran with the Excellency and called it good.

                                            Some charms that have been great: Respect-commanding Attitude, Phantom-Conjuring Performance, Demon-Wracking Glory, the Demon-wracking Shout. I say great because my players have loved and used these to good effect on occasion, though not as their core capability. Heart-Compelling Method, Memory Reweaving Discipline, and Husband Seducing Demon Dance have tended to not work out quite so well for them, though I am not sure if that was due to bad rolls, bad situations, or bad ideas.

                                            My group also loves to use prayers - they see rectifying the local divine situation as a way to help almost any plan, where rectify means get the gods working for them. So, prayer-oriented charms would be cool, but I would prefer them to be about the Solar rather than the god - because otherwise, the charms take the focus away from the character. I suggest some specific ways I think would be cool to handle it in a follow up post. However, here is the general idea framework.

                                            Generally, Exalted's prayer system has historically been a modification of the social system, for dealing with a fairly indifferent entity that is obliged to at least sort of pay attention if you yell at a particular volume...and nothing more. Prayer has never been about summoning or getting special bonuses beyond attention, and I sort of think it should stay that way - I prefer the focus to stay on PC power, rather than NPC power.

                                            Therefore, if you had a Charm that was a 'prayer' charm, it does things pretty much regardless of what the god in question thinks, either drawing on the nature of the Exalt, the congregation involved, or the Creation Ruling Mandate, for example. If the prayer roll also happens to hit the 6+ successes needed to get the god's attention, well, bonus, and maybe it comes to see what the Exalt is doing in its name, but otherwise, gods don't really care.

                                            The other type of prayer Charm might be god-focused, and be a prayer with hooks - the Exalt rolls a good prayer roll, and maybe there is a system where the god gets bonus bennies if it decides to care (develops an intimacy).

                                                    In a previous post, I gave sort of a thought-ramble on Performance, and how it has been used in my games.

                                                    Here is my Performance wish list:

                                                    Low-end charms
                                                    • Flavor Examples
                                                      • Tell a story to inspire a fearful village to rise against tyranny, or to break to will of an invader
                                                      • Use music to calm an angry crowd, inspire a king to mercy, or gather followers
                                                      • Use dance to inspire lust to convince a magistrate to execute a dissident, express grief to allow a community to heal after mourning their dead
                                                      • Give a speech that moves a nation to war, or that forms an idea that lasts a thousand years
                                                      • Lead a prayer that unites the congregants in the principles espoused in the prayer, or otherwise helps those doing the praying, regardless of the result's impact on the god in question
                                                    • Mechanically*, they might be something like:
                                                      • Allow Performances to shape emotions
                                                      • Use Performances to convey meanings in unusual ways
                                                      • Make people remember or share a message for a long time
                                                      • Improving the range of the Performance
                                                      • Allow Performances to make arguments or debate points, without the use of words
                                                    Higher end powers
                                                    • Flavor Examples
                                                      • Songs that calm storms, shatter stones, and call forth light and hope in the darkest places
                                                      • Stories that empower their hearers with the virtues or vices of the characters therein (like the Ancestor sashes from An-Teng), or change their understanding of events - rewriting history
                                                      • Dances that enslave their viewers minds or set them free of their addictions
                                                      • Music that heals the sick or inspires such passion that it kills
                                                      • Prayers that call upon the Creation Ruling Mandate to channel some portion of the god's domain or nature into the congregation - or the world, again, regardless of the god's will.
                                                      • Prayers that are so supremely skilled that they provide an extra hook for the god to respond, perhaps by creating/reinforcing an intimacy of the supplicant's choice.
                                                      • "Glories stream from heaven afar, Heavenly hosts sing Alleluia"
                                                    • Mechanically*, they might be something like:
                                                      • Allow Performances to become weapons or tools of healing
                                                      • Turn Performances into acts of creation
                                                      • Performances as vehicles for temporary training or similar effects
                                                      • Use Performances to control the world around the performer
                                                      • Use Performances to create or break long-term intimacies
                                                    In between these I see things like "the-song-you-can't-get-out-of-your-head and it drives you crazy", the talking-point everyone remembers - and shares, viral memes, phantasmal performance aids. I would love a charm that allows for tool-dependent performers to spin one from their soul, but in a way that felt more flavorful that the weapon Charms have in previous editions.

                                                    *Obviously, the mechanical breakdowns are speculative, based on previous editions and spoilers, and don't cover the full range of effects in the flavor examples.

                                                            Apologies for the Wall of Posts (TM) - I started to write my response to this earlier today, and kept tinkering in a text editor while dealing with an angry toddler, and when I copied it over, I realized it was too long for a single post, and so split it - and I still feel they are too long, but at least you can feel better for ignoring multiple posts

                                                            I notice from some comments up thread some interesting interpretations of what 'prayer' is, or is not. Protestant Europeans have a fairly narrow view of what is real prayer - part of the whole protest issue that led to the movement. It would be worthwhile to point out that prayers in the real world have encompassed everything from quickly murmured invocations and silent pleas to the carefully articulated exortations written in advance for public addresses that many of us have heard, but also can include the above mentioned week-long Mesoamerican rites involving many captives, lots of priests, pyramids, knives, etc, or spinning Tibetan prayer wheels carved with mantras, or hundreds of carefully choreographed dancers, or a choir of chanting monks, or any number of other methods - all of which would fall under Performance. An exception might be the games dedicated to gods in various Mediterranean and Mesoamerican cultures - I would probably rule those were opened with a normal prayer, but the subsequent Athletics rolls would add to the extended test, just to let the Dawns and Nights feel useful

                                                            Suffice it to say, humans are very creative in their devotional activities, and by the standards of Western Europeans, many of them are quite naughty. I kept those particular prayer activities out of this post - as I said, writing with a toddler, and some things, I am just not ready to explain for a few more years.

                                                            Exalted should have a prayer system that can handle all of what we humans came up with in reality, and more. After all - we probably do not have chthonic entities cruising around using us as prayer batteries solely for their own amusement.
