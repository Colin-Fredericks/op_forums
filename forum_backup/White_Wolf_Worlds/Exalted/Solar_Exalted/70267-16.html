                                                        I don't believe "Talent" exists. Talent is merely a skill for which there is no easy way to describe the factors that went into acquiring the skill. There may be many, even dozens of factors for why someone is skilled. What tends to rub people the wrong way about talent, I feel, is that it implies some people are just fated/destined to be better at something than another person is, and that bugs people's sense of egalatarianism. The fact remains that different circumstances can limit or aid someone in subtle ways which might as well be undetectable unless you have an extremely detailed life portrait of a person which would take you almost as much time to analyze as it did for them to live it, so it's all moot anyways.

                                                        All this being said, attempting to model that level of granularity in a game is ridiculous, and "Talent/No Talent" is just as suitable explanation as any.
