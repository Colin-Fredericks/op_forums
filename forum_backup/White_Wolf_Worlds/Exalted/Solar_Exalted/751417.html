    Are people born solar?

    11-03-2015, 10:50 AM
    In another topic I was discussing The exalted and I went to look through the Ex3 pdf to find a description of what an exaltation is... and I can't find anything. No mention of Lytek at all that I can find. No discussion of an exalted shard. I did find this in the Exigent description:

    Originally posted by Ex3 pg52
    The blood of the Terrestrial Exalted spreads
    far across Creation, while the Celestial Exalted reincarnate
    endlessly.
    So the celestial exalted reincarnate... though that could be interpretting that each reincarnation is the same person and the power simply lies dormant in them until they need it.

    Then again, the Age of Exalted portion of the history shows:

    Originally posted by Ex3 pg20
    Most of the Solars were murdered and their Essences
    sealed away in a jade prison—for such was their power
    that they could reincarnate upon death.
    Which from my perspective is somewhat clumsy wording unless you've read 2E stuff. I could easily interpret that sentence that it was the solars themselves that could reincarnate... I hope this doesn't cause issues if someone is coming into exalted fresh on Ex3 and expects their solar to be able to be resurrected with 500GP worth of diamonds... but that's another issue.

    Of course then they read this and possibly get confused:

    Originally posted by Ex3 pg26
    The Solars were overthrown by the Dragon-
    Blooded and their Exaltations imprisoned by the Sidereal
    Exalted, but now they have returned to try to save
    Creation from doom.
    Slightly aside:

    Originally posted by Ex3 pg25
    While memories of past incarnations
    sometimes guide reincarnated Solars back into the company
    of their fellows from previous lives
    So we know that there are still memories of past lives and this is discussed many times in the charms section and even in the blurb about performance... but is it actually described why the solars get these past lives? Hell, from that passage on pg 20 it sounds like the solar himself is reincarnated and remember the last time he was a solar?

    Then I found this:

    Originally posted by Ex3 pg2
    had done some great unrecorded deed
    and won Exaltation: drawn down into her body a portion
    of the essence of the divine Unconquered Sun.
    Right in the first Chapter story. So a solar exaltation is a chunk of TUS essence...

    and you have memories of past lives... No mention of who's past lives those are that I can find. Could be from the previous owners of the exaltation (as in previous editions), could be from your own personal past lives as a solar (this raises interesting questions about the exaltation process)

    Originally posted by Ex3 pg40
    The Unconquered Sun selected his champions
    to reflect all aspects of his majesty
    From this it sounds like TUS now guides exaltations personally. Hell, from the description in the lunars write up it definitely sounds like Luna chooses her own champions:

    Originally posted by Ex3 pg46
    The Chosen of the moon were forged in the chains of bondage but Luna only chooses those
    with the power to free themselves
    But then:

    Originally posted by Ex3 pg60
    Luna is the most active Incarna—she
    still attends the Exaltation of each of
    her Chosen
    Which implies that none of the incarnae need to be there when exaltation happens, like in 2E...

    It can certainly be interpreted that the exaltations function as they did in 2E. Far as I can tell there's nothing gainsaying that, but if someone comes fresh into the game, how are they supposed to come to this conclusion? Unless I missed the side bar explaining what an exaltation (shard?) is.

    Can anybody find anything definitively explaining what an exaltation is or how it works? Or did the writers simply decide to let players come up with their explanations? Because it seems like it's written as if it works the same as it did in 2E but how the heck are you supposed to understand that from the context of these quotes? The exaltation shards aren't exactly an intuitive explanation and as far as I can tell it's still the only thing that makes sense based on how the book is written...

    Am I missing a chunk of exposition? A pivotal side bar that describes what the shards are? anybody?

    Because I'm scared that suddenly people are born as solars and the power simply lies dormant until they perform a great deed. Please find me something that says this isn't the case.

    Check out my homebrew exalt: The Fabulists - Chosen of the Raksha here

      I wonder if the seeming contradictions are deliberate...

      Anyway, the answer is no. There is nothing definitively explaining Exaltation - the writers have made it clear that they're not doing that for this edition. "It's a miracle!" is as much of an explanation as we're going to get.

      As for your theory... the quotes you provided say that Exaltation is awarded, not embedded from birth. I believe "Reincarnation" in this context refers to how there are a finite number of Solar exaltations that pass from person to person, similar to (but not exactly like) how a soul gets scrubbed of its memories when someone dies and put into a new body when they are reborn.
