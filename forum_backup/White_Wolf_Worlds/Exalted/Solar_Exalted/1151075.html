    I have no idea how to use Solar Performance and Bureaucracy

    10-19-2017, 02:38 PM
    Since I can't really find anything substantial on the Internet about the subject so figure that I should ask here. Basically I have some trouble at creating plothooks where characters with Performance and Bureaucracy charms feels really Solar-ly.

    For Performance, I feel a bit like a one-trick pony since the only plot that I can think of is those stereotypical scene in ancient drama shows where some hot girl dances/sings, some guy falls in love with her but he has a wife/girlfriends and they start fighting. But this is Exalted and I'm Jen, the hot girl is a burly Hellenic man and he steals the heart of the woman and her husband. There, that's the only thing that I can think of for Solar Performance....

    I'm more interested in the kind of plot that has various cultural things sprinkles in, so I tried to read about how various cultures treat music and dance, but reading about how music is a gift from gods or how it enhances the fabric of society still doesn't spark my imagination at all. I guess that the problem is that I'm still uncertain about how various characters/places in Creation think about music? Like, if Dai Sifu does decide to sing in a public place in city X, does everybody around him starts to whisper about such a good singer much surely being a messenger of misfortune since the local deity of death has the form of a bird-body woman?

    With Bureaucracy, I don't really care for the merchant aspect so what left is the bureaucrat side, which is a problem since this circle of Exalted constantly travels around. The only plothooks in my head right now for such an Eclipse is him being a kind of troubleshooter, basically when the Circle arrives at a new place, the Eclipse starts to observe and proceed to fix various social/legal/bureaucratic problems. Though that's a little bare bone (and seems more like a modus operandi than a plothook ...), so I would love it if people can help by giving some examples of your characters solving Bureaucracy problems.

          I don't think building plots around Performance or Bureaucracy (or any Ability, really) is how it's supposed to work.

          You can use Performance to cause distraction, gain access to entertainment venues, teach valuable life lessons, make pocket change, motivate armies, incite mobs, garner support for whatever your political cause is, etc. It's a tool you use to solve problems. You can very easily play a character who's just in it for the art (in which case the ST should build a plot around that, because it's the player's major goal for their character), but it's very easy to make it a means to an end rather than an end in and of itself.

          Bureaucracy is a little different. If you're heavily investing into it, it's assumed you're either a merchant or running your own organization or both. However, characters who travel a lot will benefit greatly from Speed the Wheels and Measuring Glance to run roughshod over any organization they encounter. You can walk into a city and immediately meet with with most important people around -- administrators, generals, merchants, criminals, gods, whoever -- and you can use Measuring Glance to see who is and isn't loyal to the organizations they belong to, which means you know who would be willing to betray their organization to you for a bribe or favour.

          A lot of it comes down to the ST and the players and the kind of game they want. If it's understood that the campaign will involve a lot of traveling, a character with Supernal Bureaucracy is not a good fit. That's true no matter what RPG you're playing though.

              Possibly slightly overthought (and pretentious) ideas on Performance spurred off a little by trying to think about what you can do with them and about "heroes of performance":

              1) Performance is cool, because you get to roleplay being all the inspirational figures who wrote performed great poetry (Homer), or music (Mozart), or acting (er... Edward Alleyne?), and so on. And coming with that is the ability to influence culture and groups of people, and to reveal people's true feelings. (Think of every play-within-the-play that does this in media).

              2) At the same time, I do think all the above can go to a slightly cynical place at times - you perhaps default to Mirror Flag, the poet-playwright who is really the secret 4D chessmaster, working to change the world politically. That's cool, but at the same time, focused on too much sells short art as a genuine act of communication and inspiration, and a genuinely human act, if we are defaulting to these characters who are *just* creating forms of manipulative propaganda or satire that doesn't touch anything deep and true and genuinely moving. In short terms, characters who *use* art, rather than characters who *express themselves* through art, or *create* art.

              Of course, this is not to say that, art isn't often made by astute creatives, who understand the politics of their time, and are aware enough to use them in their art to increase their standing, or avoid censure. But that is just one aspect.

              3) The whole aspect of Solars as revolutionary upenders of world order, and their hunted status in the world order, seems like it can exacerbate the degree we go straight to 2) and limit our perspectives on Solars as artists. It *makes* *sense* for us to immediately start thinking of them as performers who are stuck in a situation where they have to use art slightly cynically.

              So I don't know if I can sum up, but think about characters who are have genuinely interesting convictions and beliefs and passions about the world, and want to express them, and are capable of doing so movingly and powerfully, and let the consequences play out in without having to tame all this within the bounds of "intrigue" and "politics", strictly. I guess specifically for you Jen, if you're reading this (possibly not, because it is a big ramble), you are an artist, so maybe you can draw on your own experiences as an artist?
