    Unlock the inner secret skills of your weapons! (Solar Melee charms)

    07-29-2017, 06:01 AM
    A lot of these charms have been rotting in my google documents for some time now, and given that I've been in a bit of a rut recently I decided to add a 'few' more and throw them out here. First we have some standard charms and then we'll move into some charms which I think helps breath some life into weapon tags a bit more.

    GRASS-CUTTING STRIKE
    Cost​: 5m; Mins​: Melee 3 Essence 1; Type:​ Simple
    Keywords​: Dual
    Duration​: Instant
    Prerequisite charms​: Iron Raptor Technique, One Weapon Two Blows

    The unskilled masses beneath the Solar’s blade are cut down like wheat to a scythe. This charm enhances an attack against an enemy battle group. Enemy size does not factor into their soak calculations but rather give the Solar swordsman additional damage based on their size. Damage rolls against battle groups have double 9s on withering attacks or double 10s on decisive.

    I liked this charm in 2.Xe, but AoE was heavily restricted in 3e. So I took the concept of Grass Cutting Scythe, allowing one to mow down tons of lesser foes, and applied it to Battle groups. White Reaper is still better at it, but this should help your Sword Princess become a better blender against battle groups.

    SUN-SWORD CONCENTRATION
    Cost​: 1m; Mins​: Melee 3 Essence 1; Type:​ Supplemental
    Keywords​: Uniform
    Duration​: Instant
    Prerequisite charms​: Fire and Stones Strike

    The Solar’s blade glows in a deep red cherry as his essence makes his blade hotter than an furance. An attack supplemented by this charm gains the Piercing tag. If a weapon already has the piercing tag then the wielder does not need to pay a point of initiative or defense when using this charm.

    At Essence 2+ the user may pay an additional mote when activating this charm, allowing them to cut through magical or natural soak as if it were armoured soak with this charm.

    Gaining the Piercing tag should help you take on armour if you're having difficulty beating it, but if already have a naturally piercing weapon then this becomes a great charm for you. The fact that you can use it to get rid of natural soak later on should improve it's effectiveness. I tried to make this charm balanced around the Brawl soak reduction charm, so while Brawl is stronger this applies as a supplemental, which works in tandum with the next charm.

    STEEL VAPORIZING EDGE
    Cost​: (+1m); Mins​: Melee 5 Essence 2; Type:​ Permanent
    Keywords​: None
    Duration​: Permanent
    Prerequisite charms​: Sun-Sword Concentration

    The Solar superheats his blade to a temperature that can only be found on the surface of the sun. This charm enhances Sun-Sword Concentration. For a surcharge of +1m the Solar may damage the armour of the opponent, reducing it’s soak by 1 for the rest of the scene if any damage was dealt. Mundane armour is permanently damaged by this charm, but artifact armour regains all lost soak at the end of the scene.

    If a Solar does more than (Stamina + Armour tier) withering damage on the opponent or any decisive damage then the armour is reduced by 1 point per threshold success on a one for one basis to a maximum of 4 per strike.

    This charm becomes very powerful very fast when paired with Iron Whirlwind. Armoured soak is torn apart in an instant for a very cheap price and allows everyone in the scene to benefit from its effect.


    MOLTEN SUN BLADE
    Cost​: 1m 1WP; Mins​: Melee 4 Essence 2; Type:​ Supplemental
    Keywords​: Uniform
    Duration​: Instant
    Prerequisite charms​: Sun-Sword Concentration

    This charm replicates the Glorious Solar Saber evocation on page 350.


    The following charms below are weapon-tag based charms, using the skill of the Solar Exalted to bring out excellence in weapons with specific tags attached to theme. Note that these charms can only be performed on weapons which naturally have the tags themselves. Charms that give temporary tags, such as Sun-Sword Concentration, then the weapon cannot make use of Piercing weapon charms. Artifact weapons that give an additional tag has some sort of permanent effect however are allowed as an exception of the rule.

    These weapon based charms are mainly an attempt to also breath more life into the weapon tag system. In where a Solar spearman feels much more different than say a Solar Greatsword user. I'd make some more charms for things like the Balanced tag, but I couldn't think of ideas. Well, let's enjoy what we got.

    SHIELD CHARMS

    This charm tree makes references to Light and Heavy Shields. Light shields are the ones currently listed in the Exalted book, Heavy Shields listed below.

    Heavy Shield

    Less of a shield and more akin to a portable wall of wood and metal carried around. The sheer weight of this weapon allows it to strike someone fairly hard but gain additional modifiers when used with a total defense action. For one the weapon’s defense modifier changes to +1 and counts the user inside of light cover for any attack against him. The downside is that these shields are very heavy and limit mobility, imposing a -2 mobility penalty on anyone who attempts to wield them (-1 for those already in heavy armour).

    Tags: Bashing, Melee (Heavy), Shield, Special

    ARROW PENNANT AEGIS
    Cost​: -; Mins​: Melee 3 Essence 1; Type:​ Permanent
    Keywords​: None
    Duration​: Permanent
    Prerequisite charms​: Dipping Swallow Defense

    When a Lawgiver wields a shield the only mark an enemy sniper will hit is the Lawgiver’s shield. Whenever Dipping Swallow Defense, Bulwark Stance, or Fivefold Bulwark Stance is active than the Lawgiver’s shield provides him with a cover bonus against any ranged attacked coming from short or longer range. Light Shields provide Light cover while Heavy Shields provide Heavy cover. When used with a Heavy Shield the defense modifier also changes to +1 against any attack made against it.

    Every wall has it’s flaws however. Any form of unexpected attack renders any additional defense anulled. Less stealth inclined and braver archers can instead attack at close range where this charm gives no benefit. This also doesn’t stack with the mounted bonus, I’m sorry but the bonuses you get from that are insane enough, I’m not allowing an 11 defense PASSIVE build. I had enough of that in 2.0e.

    If the user knows Heavenly Guardian Defense, then this charm may be repurchased. Any effect that calls for some roll involving Dodge or Resistance to avoid danger or some other negative effect may instead use Melee so long as the Solar has his shield drawn and gives a +1 bonus for using a Light shield or a +2 bonus when using a Heavy Shield.

    A Solar with a Heavy Shield and this charm becomes far more problematic for any sort of ranged based character, forcing them to either have anti-cover charms, roll really well, or engage the Solar blademaster on his own terms.

    IMPENETRABLE ORICHALCUM FORTRESS
    Cost​: 0m; Mins​: Melee 4 Essence 2; Type:​ Reflexive
    Keywords​: None
    Duration​: Until Released
    Prerequisite charms​: Arrow Pennant Aegis

    One would have better luck attempting to bypass a fortress wall than a Solar’s shield. Upon activating this charm the Solar takes a special stance with his shield against a specific opponent, slowing his movements to a crawl, reducing his speed by (5 - Strength, minimum 2) and ensuring he can only travel one range brand per turn maximum. In exchange he gains many benefits from his trusty shield. The first is that his shield gains the Smashing tag while in this stance, allowing him to knock back anyone who carelessly approaches him. Secondly is that Arrow Pennant Aegis applies to all attacks made against him at any range. Third is that any opponent who fails to beat the Solar's Melee based defense loses 1i which the Solar does not gain. Lastly the Solar reduces the damage by the amount of cover he has. Withering attacks are reduced by (Cover bonus +1) post soak while Decisive attacks have their raw damage reduced by a likewise amount. Lastly the Solar is completely immune to any knockdown or knockback effects when this charm is active. This charm applies itself in a roughly 180 degree arc in front of the Solar himself, in which even if he moves in a direction away from where his shield is facing he moves backwards.

    No matter how strong, a Fortress with invincible walls can do nothing against those crafty enough to find ways to bypass such walls. The effects of this charm can be countered in the following ways:
    • Any form of unexpected attack.
    • Taking a successful rush action against the Solar in order to move behind them. If the rush is successful then the Solar cannot target that character on his next turn so long as his charm is active and so long as a target is behind the Solar then the Solar cannot use the benefits of this charm.
    • Any weapon with the Flexible tag bypasses the protection this charm gives.

    Despite being reflexive the Solar may only activate or deactivate this charm on the beginning of his turn.

    If the Solar knows War Lion Stance, then the benefits of this charm and Arrow Pennant Aegis also applies to whomever he is guarding.

    Yea, this charm has a lot of very powerful effects, but they're somewhat balanced around the fact that your movement is going to be utter trash. Seriously even with Strength 5 a heavy armour user is going to have a -4 penalty to speed with a Heavy shield. So while this charm costs no motes, you're forced to go on a slow but steady advance or entrench yourself in your current position. If this charm proves to be too powerful I can just make the withering attack only work on pre soak instead.


    Piercing Charms

    SEAM SEEKING METHODOLOGY
    Cost​: 6m; Mins​: Melee 3 Essence 2; Type:​ Supplemental
    Keywords​: Withering-Only
    Duration​: Instant
    Prerequisite charms​: Fire and Stones Strike

    With the keen eye of the sun, the Solars are able to drive their weapons directly into the places that would hurt their opponent the most. This charm supplements a withering piercing attack, adding an additional post soak damage die for each threshold success he managed to hit his opponent, up to a maximum of (Perception).

    Using Fire and Stones Strike with this charm does not add any additional damage, but rather allows the Solar to turn one post soak die granted by this charm into an automatic success. At Essence 3+ the Solar may use Fire and Stones Strike reflexively in conjunction with this charm, activating when a hit has been confirmed.

    At Essence 3+, the Solar may add (Perception) damage regardless of the threshold successes so long as it is made against a crashed opponent or when he has spent a turn aiming at an opponent.

    At this point I’m kinda stealing from Tiger I know, but I think this charm is still fine. For one it is more limiting than Tiger, requiring a good perception to use effectively or eventual conditions in order to be used with a bigger effect. Plus I think it makes some good thematic sense in order to do something like this, I mean alternatively it could do something like lower soak by 1 for each threshold but I’ve already made Sun Sword Concentration.

    STEEL BECOMES RICE PAPER PRACTICE
    Cost​: 4m 1WP; Mins​: Melee 5 Essence 2; Type:​ Supplemental
    Keywords​: Uniform
    Duration​: Instant
    Prerequisite charms​: Seam Seeking Methodology

    With a single thrust of his weapon the Solar is able to pierce even the thickest of steel armours as if they were ricepaper walls. This charm supplements a piercing attack, allowing them to ignore any and all bonuses the opponent may have from armour. If the Solar is making an attack against a crashed target or one that he has spent an action aiming against, then he may ignore all soak and hardness.

    This charm can only be used once per encounter. It may be reset when the Solar incapacitates an opponent with a total of 11 or more soak and/or hardness.


    Smashing Charms

    HAMMER BECOMES BOLDER STANCE
    Cost​: 0m, 2m; Mins​: Melee 4 Essence 2; Type:​ Reflexive
    Keywords​: None
    Duration​: Until Released
    Prerequisite charms​: One Weapon Two Blows

    When men rush towards the Solar, his weapon sends them flying back. This charm may only be activated at the beginning of the Solar’s turn and so long as it is active they are unable to move more than one range brand per turn under any circumstances. So long as this charm is up than anyone who comes within close range of the Solar than the Solar may pay 2m to immediately make a Reflexive withering Smashing attack against that opponent. This attack can only inflict a maximum of (Strength) damage which the Solar does not gain and may only knock an opponent back a range brand rather than prone.

    If the Solar knows Impenetrable Orichalcum Fortress, then he may also use this charm with his shield as an exception to the weapon tag charms.

    The intent of this charm is that the Solar basically controls the battlefield, picking and choosing who gets to fight him at the cost of a few motes, 1i, and one point of defense (The last of which is a moot point with defense charms).

    SLEDGEHAMMER AGAINST FLY PRANA
    Cost​: -; Mins​: Melee 5 Essence 3; Type:​ Permanent
    Keywords​: None
    Duration​: Permanent
    Prerequisite charms​: Hammer Becomes the Bolder Stance, Hungry Tiger Attack

    I’m not cleaning that up.

    Even when a foe is helpless the Solar shows no mercy. This charm allows the Solar to use Hungry Tiger Technique against a fallen opponent so long as their weapon has the Smashing tag. When Hungry Tiger Technique is used in this method it can only be used once per scene, but may be reset upon incapacitating a strong or noteworthy opponent whom is also prone.


    Reaching Charms

    VICTORIOUS SHOCKTROOPER STANCE
    Cost​: 5m; Mins​: Melee 4 Essence 2; Type:​ Reflexive
    Keywords​: Perilous
    Duration​: Until Released
    Prerequisite charms​: Bulwark Stance

    The Solar Hero assumes the stance of the Victorious Shocktrooper, a fearless warrior known for charging the frontlines without fear or hesitation no matter the odds. When this charm is active then the Solar is counted as being mounted for all intents and purposes so long as he’s wielding a weapon with the Reaching tag, exploiting every advantage his range gives him to the fullest. Any attack made against an opponent who has moved into the range of the Solar’s attacks since last turn or if the Solar has moved into range of them affords a +2 raw damage boost for withering attacks and a +1 bonus damage for decisive attacks. Lastly he gains a Resolve bonus equal to (Melee/2) against all attempts to inflict fear, including supernatural horror. Unhorse gambits do not work against the Solar while in this stance, but a prone Solar cannot activate this charm.

    If the Solar knows Fivefold Bulwark Stance, than his charm may be activated alongside it reflexively.

    I was tempted to impose the movement penalty the other charms had, but I felt that mounted combat should feel more fluid and dynamic. A Shocktrooper was known for rapidly charging ahead into enemy lines. With this in mind I instead allowed the charm to work as normal with regards to movement.

    SPEARDANCER’S VAULT
    Cost​: 3m; Mins​: Melee 5 Essence 2; Type:​ Supplemental
    Keywords​: Stackable, Uniform
    Duration​: Instant
    Prerequisite charms​: Victorious Shocktrooper Stance

    With a skilled flourish of their own weapon, the Solar is able to use their weapon to both attack and move away from their opponent at the same time with breathtaking ease. This charm may only be activated if the Solar has moved towards their opponent before using it or if an opponent has moved towards the Solar since his last turn and if the Solar’s weapon has the Reaching tag. If the attack is successful than the Solar moves one range brand behind the opponent (If the Solar advanced) or one range brand behind (if the enemy advanced) without the need for any sort of disengage rolls. Successful use of this charm increases the raw damage bonus of Victorious Shocktrooper Stance by (Melee/2) against the target it was used on. This bonus is reset if the Solar is unable to attack or does not attack his chosen target for one action.

    At Essence 3+ this charm may be repurchased, allowing the Solar a chance to Unhorse a mounted enemy with it. If a withering attack supplemented by this attack crashes the opponent then the Solar may immediately make an Unhorse gambit against the opponent (No need to roll to hit, just roll initiative to see if the gambit is successful). When making a decisive attack than any attack dealing 3 or more levels automatically unhorses the opponent.


    Chopping Charms


    ORKZ CHOPPA’S ‘RE DA CHOPPIEST!
    Cost​: 3m 1WP; Mins​: Melee 3 Essence 1; Type:​ Supplemental
    Keywords​: Uniform
    Duration​: Instant
    Prerequisite charms​: Excellent Strike

    ‘Dose humie gits tink dere weapons ‘re betta than an orkz, but nuthin’ is choppier den a Choppa! ...Cept maybe da boss’s power klaw. Whens a git tries da defend against a Choppin’ attack, den any humie weapon is cut right up! Tough’er weapons, such as dose sparkly weapons dose Space Marines use, ignore dis tactic. But usin’ dis still gives two suxxusses on de disarm gambit!

    If you’re a spoilsport, call it Steel Sundering Chop instead.

    TREE SPLITTING CHOP
    Cost​: - (1m); Mins​: Melee 3 Essence 1; Type:​ Permanent
    Keywords​: Uniform
    Duration​: Permanent
    Prerequisite charms​: Fire and Stones Strike

    With a swing of an axe a mortal man can split a log, with a swing of the Solar’s axe an entire ancient tree can be felled in one fell swoop. This charm permanently upgrades the use of Chopping weapons for the Solar, increasing the effects done when using the tag. Withering chopping attacks have their damage increased by +1 per dot of strength the Solar has to a maximum of +3 (For a total damage bonus of +6) and ignores half that value in additional hardness. By paying a surcharge of 1m when making a chopping attack, the Solar no longer needs to pay initiative when making the attack.

    If the Solar knows Excellent Strike, then any Chopping attack cascades all 1s on withering damage rolls and increases damage by 2 on decisive attack rolls so long as Excellent Strike is used in conjunction with a Chopping attack.

    Yes, reroll 1s. This is specifically made in mind to screw over Resistance users. A Giant Axe is supposed to represent power, and this certainly gives it.

    MOUNTAIN SUNDERING CLEAVE
    Cost​: 4m 1WP; Mins​: Melee 5 Essence 3; Type:​ Supplemental
    Keywords​: Dual
    Duration​: Instant
    Prerequisite charms​: Hungry Tiger Technique

    Mountains will be split in twain with the Solar’s technique. This charm may only be used against a crashed, prone, restrained, or unaware opponent. On a withering attack it turns every single damage die into an automatic success. On a decisive attack turns an amount of damage dice into automatic successes based on the threshold successes, up to a maximum of Strength. When used on a feat of Strength to destroy something with a Chopping weapon then (Melee) failed dice are turned into automatic successes. This charm cannot be used in conjunction with multiattacking charms (Such as Iron Whirlwind Attack).

    This charm may only be used once per scene when used offensively, but may be reset by incapacitating an opponent with this charm then building initiative back up to 20+. If using Strong type enemies homebrew, than this charm can be used against such opponents without needing to reset.


    Two Handed Charms

    DANCE OF THE GREAT SWORD
    Cost​: 0m; Mins​: Melee 4 Essence 1; Type:​ Reflexive
    Keywords​: Dual, Stackable
    Duration​: Until Released
    Prerequisite charms​: Excellent Strike, Dipping Swallow Defense

    The skilled Solar Swordsman handles even the largest of blades like a skilled dancing partner, each swing of the powerful weapon battering the opponent senseless as the giant mass of metal flows through the air like water. This charm may only be activated or deactivated at the beginning of the Solar’s turn and so long as it’s active may only travel a total of one range brand per turn in exchange for several benefits. First is the sheer impact of two handed weapons makes it difficult for the opponent to mount a lasting defense, arms rattled when attempting to parry such a massive weapon. Anytime an opponent successfully blocks one of the Solar’s Melee attacks with a two handed weapon, their defense is reduced by 1 while all non-reflexive non-offensive actions take a -1 penalty, both of which stack up to (Strength or Charisma). This continues so long as the Solar continues to focus on the opponent and resets the moment when he fails to make an attack on them. Lastly when the Solar makes an attack against an opponent that hasn’t attacked you since your last turn and/or hasn’t tried to move away from the Solar then each 10 that appears on the attack roll of withering attacks turns one die into an automatic success and adds +1 raw damage to decisive attacks. Lastly the defense modifier of a Two-Handed weapon turns into +1 if it wasn’t that value already.

    Yea, you get a lot of bonuses here for this stance, but this is counteracted by the slower movement speed you’ll be forced to take. The penalty to defense and other rolls is potentially big but it can only be stacked if the opponent uses parry to defend. Lastly if an opponent actually does manage to get away from you then they’ll most likely be keeping that distance.

    FORCE WITHOUT EFFORT STANCE
    Cost​: -; Mins​: Melee 4 Essence 2; Type:​ Permanent
    Keywords​: None
    Duration​: Permanent
    Prerequisite charms​: Dance of the Great Sword, Fire and Stones Strike

    Each and every move of the Greatsword dancer is filled with purpose and poise, each lunge and step containing equal parts beauty and death. This charm enhances Fire and Stones Strike when Dance of the Great Sword is active. Fire and Stones Strike now costs 1m per 2 dice added instead. Additionally if the maximum amount of dice are added by Fire and Stones Strike, the user may also cascade any and all 10s on the damage roll. No partial dice may be added, so if the Solar has Strength 5 and wants to add 5 dice, then the cost one would need to pay is 3m.

    I tried to make this better than just a generic efficiency enhancer for Fire and Stones Strike, so I added the cascading 10s.
