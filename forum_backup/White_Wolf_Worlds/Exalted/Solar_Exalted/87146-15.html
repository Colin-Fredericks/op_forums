                            While I may not agree with the central thesis of the original post in every detail, I find myself supporting it regardless, as I find that hewing to a general impression of the setting where Solars are seen as generally good people stops my central complaint about exalted as a thing.

                            That being, when I go into a game playing a solar, and construct a solar who is actually a good dude, who feels guilty about having to kill people (does it anyway because there isn't a reasonable alternative), tries to help people, and is in general the kind of warrior-philosopher who focuses on self-awareness and finding root causes to problems BEFORE he starts swinging his sword (as opposed to after, if ever), you know, a GOOD PERSON. I get told that I'm doing it wrong, and need to make my character a callous killer, or support slavery, or a narcissistic asshole, because EPIC TRAGEDY and DECONSTRUCTION OF TROPES and REALISM.

                            @#$%! that noise. If I want to play that kind of mythic hero, screaming into the uncaring tide of violence and injustice of Creation, I am not having bad-wrong-fun, and I'm rather sick of being told by everyone I bring my characters up to that I am.

                            The idea that Solars are (secretly) the true villains of the setting, and/or that the setting is irrevocably doomed to degradation and/or destruction and nothing anyone does really matters in the long run is the single most toxic meme this fandom has ever seized on.

                                    "Solars are Heroes"

                                    I agree with this statement

                                    "Heroes are people who want to change the world for what they see as the better"

                                    I do not agree with this

                                    For me, a Hero is not defined by his motives, but by his actions. They do things that ordinary people cannot/dare not. They confront opponents that cause lesser people to quake in their boots. And yes, culture heroes usually are doing these impossible things and confronting these terrible foes for the betterment of the cultures they represent. But they don't have to be good, or even nice. They just have to be willing to dare what others will not.

                                        I’m really agree with you on this. I’m not sure why there is such an affinity to the darker potential of a Solar. I’m just not sure I see the appeal of playing a villainous or morally lacking Solar, and I am certainly surprised that so many people think that this style of character should be if not the de-facto state of a Solar, at the very least as common as the “good” variety.

                                        I know there are all sorts of canon examples of nihilism and abuse of power throughout the lore of the game in regards to Solars but I really feel this is more of an outlier element. If you read the core book in its description of Solars, and the backgrounds and stories of the signature Solar circle, they all reflect the Solar drive for greatness and to some degree a motivation to do good by the world. I think someone who is a sociopathic cult leader, a skilled assassin who will take any job for the right price, or a merciless magnate of a slave caravan, should be precluded from receiving an exaltation unless they will also receive a new direction in life in the process (ie. Panther’s revelation of his debauchery and vice). That’s not to say that they can’t lapse back into old form or find themselves pulled towards an even darker path, just that the beginning of their journey as a Solar should be virtuous in some way.
