                                            To do a working affecting a road, you have to do rituals and mystic stuff along that road. To do a working affecting all of Creation… you may not have to go to every last part of it, but you'd need to be doing big ritual effects in places all over Creation. And something on that level is going to get noticed, by the gods whose purviews are being affected, by the Sidereals as it resonates through the Loom of Fate, and others. Especially given it's a difficulty 2 IntOcc roll for a Sorcerer to notice 'the encroaching effects of a working on a region'. And the region for this is the whole world.

                                            If you're Salina, and you have the backing (or at least acceptance) of the other Exalts of your time, and can go all across the Creation to do your rituals, then yes, you can write the ability to learn sorcery into the very world itself.

                                            If you're a bunch of PCs who want to cripple the Guild by using a working to taint the nature of silver (one of their nightmares and why they still hold onto a fair bit of Jade), then it's going to be damn hard, but doing rituals along the Guild trade routes, and at hordes of silver, then yeah, you might be able to do it. It'll be damn hard, you'll want distractions to keep the Guild busy (as mortals they're likely at least somewhat less understanding of workings), and work out how you stop the Sidereals, Lunars, and Realm interfering. But it could be possible, because it's "Change the world and hurt the Guild" not "Destroy the world"
                                            Specific, targeted workings may well be a weapon the Deathlords and their minions use. But it's not going to be a "I sit at home, I make some rolls, the world is fucked."
