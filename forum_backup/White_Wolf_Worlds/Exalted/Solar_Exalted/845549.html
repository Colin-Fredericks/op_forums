                          Given the wyld does mutations and weirder things, and given that via directed sorcery there's no limit on the mutations you can grant yourself, I would believe there's no limit on the mutations that a Solar can have from the wyld. Which leaves weirder things.

                          I would have no problem with things like "the solar got turned into a kitten" "the solar got turned into a serial killer that kills people every time they hear the word shubbery" or even "the solar got turned into an animated pillar of salt". Because all those things are still actors able to interact with the environment in a meaningful way

                          I would declare them dead if they got turned (long term) into something like a salt statue of themselves (immobile) or a boat, or a forest glen, because at that point they can't meaningfully interact with others or the setting. They are still capable of being seasoning for food, a thing to ride or provide fruit but no more than the perfectly mundane version of those objects.

                          Where this gets a slight wrinkle for some people, is that THIS DOES NOT APPLY TO PCs. if a PC gets turned into a salt statue of themselves in the place of the Raksha Ad'oy and her circlemates are running around to seek a cure or treatment, there is still hope and they might be saved. This is my opinion YMMV etc etc.



                          The key is less "What is the line at which the Exaltation falls off" and more "Is it dramatically appropriate in this specific situation that the exaltation falls off?"

                                              Any hypothetical speculation like this really needs to be approached from "what serves the needs of a gaming group" to have any value, because any answer that doesn't make for a fun gaming session is not useful.

                                              If you want an exalted level-monster to hit home to your players what can happen if you wander the Wyld without protection, the answer is that things can get pretty freaky and still be an Exalt.

                                              f you have a player that wants to weaponize the Wyld to de-exalt people the answer is probably that the circumstances that can cause it are too difficult and complex to arrange for it to be a practical tool, but maybe possible to arrange as a one-off tactic for an otherwise impossible-to-kill enemy.

                                              If you're planning on using it on a player, that probably depends somewhat on wether you're using it as just a dramatic way of killing a character off, or as part of some sort of redemption arc, where the player loses their power and now has to find some way to regain it (or learn that they don't really need it to make a difference and accomplish their goals). Definitely keep an eye on how to keep things fun for the player you use it on.

                                                      I agree with those who've said that it needs to be addressed on a case by case basis depending on what fits your game. Especially since, as I see it, either side of the line has unfortunate implications. If you say the Exaltation sticks around, then you've got people trying to lock Exaltations out of the cycle of reincarnation via shenanigans that are probably harder than simply killing the person. If you say it leaves, then people are going to try to build some kind of De-Exaltation Weapon. The case by case basis thing seems like the best way to go to me.
