                                It also feels like you're getting a bit caught up in the abstractions here. Clash-cheesing is a vital and primordial survival tactic. It's older than humans. It's happening all the time. Almost every human culture has an idiom or adage about it. The problem is that those tend to assume we're the Tyrant Lizard closing on what looks like a light snack: don't back your opponent into a corner.

                                When you're fighting to survive, and your only option is win or die because running is off the table? You fight. You fight hard, and you fight cheap. You might be at the disadvantage, but you keep going because they might decide it's not worth it, and if you do nothing you die.

                                If you think about any video of someone backing a feral animal into a corner... you're going to see that animal try to clash-cheese: they sit there, puff up, and wait. When the person goes in to grab them? That's when they attack. That's a clash: to offensive moves being pitted against each other.

                                Whether the corner is literal or not, that's what the Tyrant Lizard vs. no-so-great-Melee Solar is: the Solar is not in a great tactical situation. The Solar is facing that same situation: make it clear that any time that Tyrant Lizard goes in for a bite, it's going to get stabbed. And it's going to keep getting stabbed until one side falls down, or the Tyrant Lizard goes away.

                                So this is a very basic survival situation. The only real oddity is dual-wielding light weapons is better than using a spear because the weapon stat blocks are a major source of iffy design decisions.

                                MA and sorcery as "go tos" for characters not planning on leaning into the main combat abilities seems stupid as they are definitely on par with the other abilities.
                                I don't think that plays out. MA is diverse, but very resource (esp. The core Charms are, generally, just going to work. MAs might be better for very specific strategies, but they don't pan out as situations get more diverse. They're great for having one style so you have some decent combat utility, but having to buy a 4 dot Merit, and each MA as its own 5 dot Ability, and then Charms? Mastering a few styles gets really expensive really fast and not really that great compared to other options.

                                Sorcery is hard to compare because it operates on such a different part of the game. Combat sorcery is a thing, but it's not throwing ki blasts instead of slashing with your sword. "Basic" attack spells like Death of Obsidian Butterflies are massive area attacks that can create problems for your own side if you're not really careful with them. Things like Wood Dragon Claw are great but... you still need to be able to fight with them with other Abilities to get much use out of them.

                                Evocations are not on par with MA charms but okay.
                                Based on what?

                                But, making every 'non-traditional combat ability' character a MAist or a Sorcerer feels like a bad game.

                                The whole point is that Solar XP helps you branch out in games where branching out it going to be useful.
