    Investigation:

    Transcendent Memory of Evidence
    This charm allows the Solar to recreate evidence that is damaged or inaccurate, such as an inaccurate witness testimony. The Solar can infer the factual evidence based on what she is presented with and investigate the charm from there. This charm alone is not enough to reconstruct evidence that was deliberately destroyed, tampered within or fabricated.

                                                Battlefield-Dominating Entrance

                                                    Socialize:

                                                    Life of the Party
                                                    The Solar can easily walk into any celebration or public gathering, even if she does not have an invitation. The guests will accept her presence and they will at least pretend to know her in order to avoid awkwardness. So long as this charm is active, anyone the Solar is interacting with is considered to have a minor tie of polite acquaintance to the Solar. This charm immediately ends if the Solar does anything to disrupt the gathering.
