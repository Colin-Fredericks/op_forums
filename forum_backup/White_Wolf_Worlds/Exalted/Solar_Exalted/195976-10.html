                                        Pretty sure that Solars are supposed to get the most from their evocations because they are the ones that drive themselves and those around them to excellence. Is it at all surprising that they get the most out of their tools? Also, without having seen what everyone is getting, here is the way I am reading it: Solar's get top level evocations out of every magical material, because they can push everything to the maximum ability the thing is capable of, whether human followers, tools, or their voice. Others get the top level evocations out of their own magical material because they are attuned to it. Dragon-Blooded are awesome at it because they are also very closely tied to the concepts of making and using tools. Lunars, less so. They adapt themselves to the tools they have, or don't have, as needed. They just aren't the dedicated tool-using splat. Sidereals? They master themselves, and work with destiny. They should get max out of Starmetal, which is deeply associated with that. Should they get giant golden glowing swords? Sure, but they don't express that way, so they can't get the most out of it. Soulsteel? Jade? Basically the same deal.

                                        We also don't know exactly what is going to happen. May we will see that moonsilver evocations don't resonate well with what the Solar wants, so even if he can get to the uppermost evocation, they don't really help him in anyway. A sword that attaches itself to you while you swing it is great for not being disarmed, but its even better when you don't have hands to hold it.

                                        Also, what if Solar's get top tier access to every magical material type, but lots of artifacts made of say, moonsilver, build off of Lunar charms? Solar can get to top tier, but can't get to any charms with that Lunar pre-req.

                                        There's a million ways to handle this, some of which could be very bad (Sidereals suck, so they only get lowest tier access to Starmetal, and access to nothing else: that would be pretty crappy), or generally everyone gets access to the top of their own, 1st or 2nd in others, but still can't get anything with a prereq? Does that make Eclipse too good, because they can take a Jade artifact, learn the prereqs, and get the most out of it? Does that screw the Dawn caste over? Not really, he still has stuff to do, and he's not really hindered in any way. He just doesn't fill that particular niche that the Eclipse can.

                                        Is the Sidereal screwed because he's not going to be as good using Jade evocations, or soulsteel? Those are outliers in the concept of their Exaltation. That is not the case for Solars. Solars are more broad, and represent the potential of humanity, good or bad, as big damn heroes, or soul-eating villains. If that's what you hate about Exalted, don't think 3rd is going to help. If your problem is just that 'That Solar wields a Soulsteel daiklaive better than my Sidereal!' then Ex3 is probably going to annoy you as well. If that Solar uses that Soulsteel daiklaive as well as my Deathknight, but my Deathknight has a couple of tricks because the evocations build off of my charm set, I think that's fantastic. If the Solar uses that Soulsteel daiklaive exactly the same why my Deathknight does, I think that's kinda boring.

                                        I think I see what the developers are trying to do. At least, I hope they do, because the idea seems fantastic. It needs to be implemented carefully though, and with more subtlety than I can manage.
