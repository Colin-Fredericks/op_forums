    Going by the text you quoted, it says "...after the murder..." so that may be a very long time. Even today (in Creation) it is still "after the murder". And even today we still have standing, albeit abandoned, temples to the UCS. Which suggests the purge wasn't as complete, violent or symmetric as one would think.

    Since the process of said dismantling is not discussed in detail, we sholudn't take these genarilities to be too limiting or let them impose on our suspension of disbelief. If them going balls to the walls on the UCS Faith irks you, nothing you quoted contradicts assuming things were more gradual.

    I personally envision it as a "The UCS is very angry with the state of the world. He doesn't want to be bothered by your prayers. We're closing this temple and you better start praying to gods who maybe will listen instead of one who said that he will smite those who bother him." Spectacularly kill some stubborn fools. Rinse and repeat through centuries. Introduce a new state religion with tremendous secular backing, and let the UCS fade into non existence.

    In this magical world people would notice that their god stopped answering. In this magical world you have the Princes of the Earth, nigh immortal super people, telling you whats up. Your beloved UCS is not raining down beams of searing light as they board up His temples and arrests unyielding believers. Some shit is certainly up with your God, so your best bet is get with the new program if you don't wanna go the same way.

            Originally posted by Piff View Post
            Well, we're not talking about Immaculates initially in any case (who knows what the early Shogunate faith was), but that distinction is kinda nitpicky and not that important to make. Your points are well taken.

            Answering the ideas put forward, I actually completely agree - I could see a faith vanishing in a century or two, sure. It's easy enough to create incentives that will move people from one way of thinking to another. In fact, I think if one is enamored with the idea of a 'Forgotten God' for pure story reasons or coolness, that's a pretty good way to do it. Just suggest that over time, sun worship became less and less important, until it more or less vanished into obscurity under the weight of an immense amount of theological focus and energy being devoted to separate areas.

            But the text describes actually PULLING DOWN THE TEMPLES - like, immediately as the Solar Exalted were killed.

            That's where I kinda go 'Waitaminute'.

            In order for that to really work, I find myself verging more into the 'Age of Nightmares' interpretation than I like.
            I think the key thing is that, even with temples pulled down, probably the religion didn't vanish within 10 years, 100 years, maybe even 1000 years.
            But it's 1500 years later.


            Think of it this way - Let's say the Pope and all of his Cardinals are proven to be guilty of widespread and monstrous abuses. Let's even suggest the most favorable situation possible to the Usurpers and assume that knowledge of these crimes is widespread and commonly known. So not only is the Pope and all these high church officials guilty, everybody KNOWS they are guilty and is sick and tired of them.

            Under these circumstances it's perfectly reasonable and even perhaps desirable for an insurgent faction to remove these individuals and punish them on behalf of the people! But... can anybody honestly envision a situation where based on this, CHRISTIANITY AS A WHOLE would be dismantled? Keeping in mind that 99% of the population is devout Christian?
            Consider that, in Western Europe, a mere 500 years after attempts to reform and revitalise, sparked by some corruption and social change... Christianity has gone from 99% to under 50%. And that's without (unlike Eastern Europe) overt attempts to suppress it. It's likely to fall to somewhere between 20-30% in the next 50 years.


            With 1500 years of social changes, deliberate attempts to suppress the religion by the DBs and Sidereals, and no answered prayers compared to all the Gods that do answer prayers... well, I wouldn't expect there to be no worship of the UCS, but not a lot.

            Though I could see some of this going on:
            I wonder if there ever have existed a farmer that haven't obsessed with things like sun and rain.
            ...
            So while they might not pray to the entity "The Uncured Sun", there are probably a lot of people sending prayers and offerings to that great ball of fire in the sky, maybe with a "god" someone made up a few generations back.
            That's a fair point.

                                                As I see it, least gods both are and are not a thing, mostly not. Everything in Creation has a spiritual presence, sometimes called a "least god," but this term is misleading, in that a "god" must have at least some slight awareness to actually be a god, and the overwhelmingly majority of things simply do not have awareness. A beach might have a god, a mountain or a field might have one, but an individual grain, or even a large sack, of rice will not.
