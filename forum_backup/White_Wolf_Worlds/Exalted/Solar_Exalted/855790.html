    In what ways are Solar the most powerful among the Exalted ?

    03-30-2016, 01:50 PM

    Solar: Why are you so surprise when you're the one who is puking out rainbow ?


    The book and the fan often said what in the title, but it's always declared in a vague and kinda fluffy way. Now that we have the charms for Solar, are these the parts of Solar powerset that'll make a Sidereal go "It's a good thing we tried to kill them in the past" and a DB go "It's NOT fair !":

    I'm not sure if Supernal is what make the Solar the most powerful, but their charms sure does, some of the effects are just crazy strong, like, Eye of Conky bullshit-ly strong (I'm not salty about Resplendent Destiny being dispel-able, I swear). Death Ray is a Scene-long AoE Decisive attack that don't reset you to base Initiative, a Tyrant Lizard getting buffed with Deadly Predator Method is horrifying, and of course, the infamous Doom-Bot and Meteor Shitting Shriek (though I don't understand why they name it after a bird). And some of the effects are perfect, like the Night's anima or some of the Larceny charms.

    Being the only one who can do a Solar 3 Working which is unspammable Shinmaic Communion (too bad I have no idea what qualify as "subtle alterations")

    Mastery effect for MA, maybe ? Since everyone will get them if they practice the appropriate MA so it's probably not.

    I'm not sure if this is the intent, but........shitting out N/A Artifact with the right Craft build.

    Errhh...Does being the only splat with powerful Bureaucracy charms count ?

    Edit: Wait, Deadly Predator Method is only Essence 3 (ಠ╭╮ಠ)........This have to be a typo, right ? I just can't imagine what an Essence 5 Survival charm look like if essence 3 can turn your Tyrant Lizard into a gigantic tornado of death-puking dinosaur who can shoot eye beams and force choke people with its tiny arms.

                      Yeah, old 2E Solars could rewrite the entirety of a society's social mores by having a chat with a dude in a cupboard. If anything, Solars were reined back from the kind of unfun bullshit they tossed around.

                      In any case, to answer Jen's question, historically it's been a combination of things. Solars got the most encompassing charms, the most efficient mote costs, the best XP costs, access to the best sorcery, incredible long-lasting dice advantages like Infinite Mastery, and so on. Basically, they held a superiority in all individual aspects that, when taken as a whole, meant Solars (and their offshoots) were playing in an entirely different league from everyone else.

                                      Bigger dice pools and dice tricks that squeeze more successes out of those dice pools. Sure, everything else they get helps a lot, but when it comes down to it, the basic advantage is how much effort Solars can put towards accomplishing a task.

                                      The Dragon-Blooded Excellency allows them to succeed at most mundane tasks, but they can't get as much insurance against bad rolls as Solars do.

                                      I think Sidereals have Solars beat at lower difficulties in terms of efficiency, but they won't have the sheer power needed to make high-end Artifacts. The upside is that they can get a lot of mileage out of their circumstantial bonuses.

                                      Lunar dice pools are on par with Solars, but the nature of Attribute-based Charms means that they can apply themselves to certain tasks the way Solars can.

                                      Liminals are at their best when they're burning enough motes to flare their anima, which suggests that they're equipped to deal with tough challenges when they're backed into a corner.

                                                      re: Martial Arts Mastery

                                                      Here's who gets benefits of the Mastery Keyword that we know for certain: The Solars and the Abyssals, since they're both Solaroids. The book also mentions that Sidereals have "their own esoteric methods for accessing these effects" (pg 427).

                                                      We can assume that unless things get changed more significantly that the Infernals will probably also get the benefits for the Mastery Keyword. Plus, in the layout leak, the explanation of the Mastery Keyword did in fact namedrop Infernals. ("The Solar Exalted, and their dark reflections, the Abyssal and Infernal Exalted").

                                                      Most Terrestrials are bound by the effects of the Terrestrial keyword in their Martial Arts, but it's rumored that there's ways around this for the Immaculates. Possibly through some kind of merit. We don't know if the Immaculate Styles will have Mastery benefits, or which groups can access them, and we won't know until the DB book drops.
