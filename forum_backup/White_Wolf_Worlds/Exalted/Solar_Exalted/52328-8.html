    Originally posted by John Mørke View Post
    You don't have a problem with manifestly different methods all ending in the same response?
    Within the limits of such the abstractions this system embraces, where anyone with a given rank of Performance is equally good at singing and dancing and acting and impromptu rap battles and stand-up comedy despite all those things being distinctly different disciplines that require different skills in the real world...yeah. I'm pretty much okay with it. If we're going to be basing Charms on Abilities, then I'd prefer to base them on Abilities rather than Specialties. I think doing otherwise would be a tacit admission that Performance just doesn't have much to do, mechanically, and we need to extend the Charm tree by coming up with ways to do the same things in different disciplines. I doubt anyone is arguing that we need different Craft Charms for the different kinds of elemental Crafts, or different Lunar Strength Charms for lifting with your legs or with your back.

    Of course, all this is operating from a perhaps mistaken belief Performance will have a similar role in third edition to what it did in second, and that social influence will be comparable to physical combat in that both systems will have goals of causing others to do things they otherwise wouldn't, whether it's signing a treaty or bleeding profusely on the floor. You probably know something I don't.

            Originally posted by JimB View Post
            How so? I mean, Hungry Tiger Technique working for every weapon instead of just spears doesn't mean the Solar Melee Charms make it impossible for me to play a dragoon.
            It is easy to agree that weapons all have the same function, even if they operate a little differently. It is easier to gloss over the difference in operation, because the difference is much smaller, and can be covered by stunts.

            Oration, dancing, singing, playing an instrument and acting all have somewhat different objectives. It is harder to gloss over the differences and yet feel like the character has done anything, or that your choice of method, and ergo the identification of your character as a great speaker or a great dancer, means something significant. Weapons at least have stats to make them different. They suggest the stunts by which they operate differently. Different performances all literally have the same end, making the choice of dancer or speaker completely arbitrary. Stunts don't change this, but rather they highlight the problem even more intensely. No matter how amazing your speech, or great your dance, it has the exact same result. This is appropriate when you stunt swinging an ax or dropping a hammer, but not when you are dancing or speaking. IE, dancing and speaking are sufficiently different as to desire something more in the result, something that affirms your method and makes you feel like one choice is valid and different from another.

                            Originally posted by John Mørke View Post
                            Some of you are mentioning prayer Charms. What do you imagine prayer doing?

                            Well, now that's not like three in the morning in my country I can actually muster the brainpower to elaborate.

                            - I want my prayer to be not only heard, but noticed. Ensuring a method of at least communication with the object of my worship. This should be able to be defended, so a player can't just nag the hell of the UCS even if he ultimately ignores his prayers.

                            - I want my prayers to empower the gods that answer them. Not only giving them essence, but making the more able to help. For example, a minor god of war may be able to bless a few of a tribe's warriors, a minor god of war compelled to act by my prayers can bless the whole tribe.

                            - I want to lead congregations in prayers of such beauty that gods not only feel compelled to answer but would actually feel heart-broken should they ignore them.

                            - I want to be able to withhold prayer from gods that have been dishonest with me in such a way that any other prayer they receive is hollow and "tasteless".

                            Overall I want to encourage gods to answer my prayers without forcing them and to make advantageous for them to work with me, not only because I'm a potent creature of my own right, but because I can help them squeeze so much more form their cult and their own domain that they wouldn't have been able to dream before.
