                                                            The same reason the Sun is both a burning chariot that is also an incandescent ball of plasma traveling through the skies, and the King of Heaven. Divine concepts are rarely easily constrained by a singular nature. The Solar Exaltation can be all of the following, at the same time: the Sun's deliberate and conscious divine gift to a few select mortals, a subconscious power of his working to empower the Solars so his glory is ever-present, an automated process that works based on the Sun's innermost nature, imperishable objects made of Essence that attach themselves to your soul, memories and power colored by the Sun's Essence delivered to you in one neat package.

                                                            Also, there were a number of Solars during the Second Age, continually being reborn across Creation. Even ignoring that, just because the Sun can make Exaltations doesn't mean it's trivial for him to do so.

                                                            In fact, that is usually the answer to: "if X can be done, why hasn't it been done a million times?". It isn't trivial to do X. Just because you can do one push up, or even ten, or hundred doesn't mean you can do a thousand, or a million.
