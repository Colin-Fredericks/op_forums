                        If everyone remaining is Elder that's not so bad.

                        Without homebrew, Solars wyld shape armies with mutations, add additional mutations (presumably more situational) with Solar Circle Sorcery (ie Fountain of Fathomless Dreams). Eclipses (and optionally all castes) can wield Grace Magic to turn mortals into 1-dot behemoths and add even more mutations/mutation keyword fey powers, but that's somewhat XP intensive and it's better to simply enslave your own Fey to do it for you.

                        Relying on mutations has some major benefits over relying on charms in games featuring serious combat as they cannot be shut down by Solar Circle Sorcery or the Greater Sign of Venus. For instance, if you protect an area with Abjuration of the Maidens and have high-statted behemoth servants guard it, attacking it won't be a cakewalk. This is not to say you cannot bestow your creations charms, it can be done in numerous ways.
