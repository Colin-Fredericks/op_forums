                                    Originally posted by Jen View Post

                                    The book and the fan often said what in the title, but it's always declared in a vague and kinda fluffy way. Now that we have the charms for Solar, are these the parts of Solar powerset that'll make a Sidereal go "It's a good thing we tried to kill them in the past" and a DB go "It's NOT fair !":
                                    ======= General Thoughts =====
                                    To bypass some of the sniping in the last several pages, Solars are the most broadly and directly powerful of the Exalted. At any given Essence/XP level, a given Solar is going to perform better in a general area of competence than another Exalt with a similar specialty. The only away around that is to define the area of competence in a narrow way, such as "manipulating the elements." Other Exalts can do things the Solar can't do, but generally the application of those things will be less effective for a given task.

                                    A Solar outside their specialty is not going to be better at most things than other Exalts, spirits, or even mortals. ISP may have an Integrity Excellency, but she can't function in a court nearly as well as her Lunar Mate, who only has a Manipulation Excellency.

                                    This is all necessary for the intended Solar play experience. Solars are like action heroes, or heroes from ancient epics. They are better/faster/stronger/smarter, but they are outnumbered, with few resources, and they lack grounding and infrastructure. The lower overall power of the other splats is intrinsic to that play experience.

                                    Similarly, the greater power of the Solars is intrinsic to the intended play experience of the other splats. For example, the Dragon-Blooded would have no reason to fear the Solars if the Anathema didn't have more power. From the DB perspective, the Solars are the Great Monsters, and only by bringing to bear all their advantages and numbers in unison can they defeat them. A group of PC Dragon-Blood who destroy one vile Solar Anathema have beat the boss monster of their story arc, if not their entire campaign. That boss fight would be pretty anti-climatic if they could have done it easily at char-gen.

                                    What's changed in E3 is how the gap manifests itself. Previously, a Solar who managed to get Infinite Melee Mastery up could go through n average Dragon-Bloods, and with a 2/7 filter it almost required ST fiat. Now a newly Exalted Dawn Caste can be expected, assuming average rolls, to defeat an experienced DB warrior, maybe even two. BUT, the Dawn will likely be wounded, and will definitely be running low on Essence, if not tapped. The Dawn won't be able to handle another two.

                                    This is a better mechanical model for the intended play experience. Solars are powerful and terrifying, but not arbitrarily so, and thus have to treat DBs and the Wyld Hunt as life-threatening. On the flip side, the DBs have every reason to kill the Solars young, because if they put down roots and rack up XP, it will cost them dearly to put the Anathema down. if the Dawn Caste trains a few Legions with TWTT and hits E4, the Realm will be forced to spend lots of lives to save the region from him.

                                    ===== Specific Parts =====
                                    The basic parts of Solar superiority haven't changed much.
                                    • Larger mote pools, over the long term
                                    • Larger dice pools, or easier access to the same dice pool
                                    • Charms with broad applicability and slightly better numbers here and there.
                                    • Charms that break/subvert dice caps.
                                    • Direct access to Mastery effects in MA.
                                    • Unique access to Solar Sorcery.
                                    • A few unique charms, such as WST.
                                    • Crazy Craft potential (even if it costs all the XP to E5 and beyond).
                                    But really, we don't know for sure, because we haven't seen the final rules for the other Exalts, yet.
