                      I think it's essentially the question of:
                      Is he an action hero?
                      Or is he a spy?

                      If he's an action hero, Dawn, if he's a spy, Night.
                      Either way, he'll also favour Presence and Socialise, but I wouldn't put him as a social class.

                      I'd put him as a Night (but possibly that's because I like the films where he's more a spy):
                      Caste abilities: Athletics, Awareness, Investigation, Socialise, Stealth
                      Favoured abilities: Drive(Ride?), Firearms(Archery?), Brawl, Integrity, Presence
                      He also has Larceny 5 (but not many charms) and a high Melee rating. But he does prefer brawl or firearms.

                      To be fair, you could also make him a Dawn with those abilities (and his Supernal would I guess be Firearms).

                      Luckily, he no longer needs a very high rating in Linguistics for his vast number of languages.

                          I'd divide this up by the different Bond actors:

                          Sean Connery - Night. This Bond usually succeeds by trickery. He's suave and not bad in a fight, but in the end his success comes from outwitting his enemies - not outshooting them or being more persuasive. Focus on the deception-oriented Larceny charms rather than the ones that involve actual physical theft. Probably has Presence, Firearms, and Socialize favored.

                          Roger Moore - Night again. More trickery, but also a little more emphasis on gadgets than on fighting - favor Lore or Occult. Max out Larceny - Moore-Bond was great at sucker-punching people.

                          Pierce Brosnan - More like an Eclipse, really. Brosnan-Bond relied very heavily on superior technology, the odd temporary ally, and being able to talk his way close to targets. Favor stealth and firearms. Find or make up a charm that lets you have perfect hair no matter what happens to you.

                          Daniel Craig - Full-on Dawn. Craig-Bond's approach to problems was pretty much always to go in shooting, or engage in dangerous chases. I will also point out that he rarely succeeded in deceiving anyone and managed to survive failing most of his missions. Favor Resistance and Integrity, because Craig-Bond never dies or gives up even when things actually are hopeless.

                          I can't speak for David Niven, George Lazenby, or Timothy Dalton - I didn't watch any of their movies.
