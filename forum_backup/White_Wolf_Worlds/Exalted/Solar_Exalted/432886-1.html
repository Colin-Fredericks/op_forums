        JAHN SUNSTRIDER, the Spacefaring-Adventurer

        Jahn Krem had fought, killed, and bled for His Divine Lunar Presence's Thousanth Dynasty, and the people of the Central Empire, but in the end, all his conscience had earned him was a dishonorable discharge for refusing to carry out his orders during a routine "pacification" action. Disgusted with the Imperial legions, the ex-soldier defected to the Frontier, where he could at least live on his own terms.

        Since soldiering was all he knew and was good at, he made a living at it, aside from that short stint as a Sky-Taxi pilot. There was no shortage for hired guns, and he had a good enough reputation that high-placed organizations started asking for him by name. One such was an exploratory mission, that had obtained supposed maps and codes to Rael, the legendary lost planet of the Solars, at exorbitant price, and needed equally high-priced bodyguards. Despite misgivings, Jahn signed on...

        The mission went well, at first: The system was found, and the defensive perimeters let them in. Once they landed, however, it all went very bad. No code could save them from the engineered creatures, mechanicals and behemoths that had all been programmed to slay any non-Solar outsider, and Jahn could barely save himself, let alone those who had accompanied him. He was the last survivor, forced to watch helplessly as they all died, and set himself to go down fighting...when a golden miracle descended from the sky.

        The legends were true; at least one ancient Solar still lived here, and it was the famous Contentious Sword, no less! He had been watching, but had only been moved to act by Jahn's willingness to die fighting. Taking Jahn under his protection, he treated him as a lowly servant at first, but warmed up to him over the weeks and months, finally regaling the mercenary with long-unheard tales of the ancient days of the galaxy. Eventually, he started sparring with him, quickly training Jahn in the forms of superior swordplay long since forgotten.

        It was when he made Jahn demonstrate his mastery of the final striking technique of this style that the point of all this became plain. Contentious Sword dropped all his defenses, allowing Jahn to strike home deeply, and fatally. As he died, he passed his Exaltation to the stricken soldier, along with instructions as to what to do next, chief among them, not to waste his life and power as he had done.

        Jahn first proved his mettle by interring his masters' body in the golden tomb complex that he had long ago prepared for himself, which was a dangerous task in itself. Once past all the traps, a holo gave him two rewards. The first was a treasure of a lost age, which even Contentious Sword barely understood and none today could replicate: An Orichalcum Beamklave, one of the few to ever exist.

        The other waited hidden in an underground hanger: An Orichalcum Artifact starship, the Invincible Sword Princess, as sleek and beautiful as she was fast and deadly, and co-piloted by a spirit-program of the same name. Activating her eager engines and Anima Circuits, Jahn finally bade farewell to the only world he'd felt at home at, and ventured out into the canals between the stars to make a new name for himself...which would come to be "Sunstrider".

        Notes: Jahn Sunstrider is a young Dawn-Caste Solar, who specializes in Melee, Firearms, and Pilot. He always carries his trusty old Starcaster as his primary weapon, but has several others to fall back on. Perhaps his most dangerous weapon is his Beamklave, which he has the Favored Weapon Merit for. As he learns to apply his Essence to the sword techniques he learned on Rael, he has been teaching himself the Charms for Single Point Shining Into The Void Style Martial Arts, of which he has learned up to the Form Charm.

        Invincible Sword Princess is a unique starship about the size of a space yacht, able to carry a crew of about 5-10, at most. Her Heavenly Guardian Defense shields make her tougher than her delicate build would suggest, and few ships are faster and more manueverable in direct combat. ISP herself often speaks as a disembodied voice, but can manifest as an image, either on the view screen or as a small figure on the controls. She recognizes Jahn as her master and will only take his orders, although she sometimes refers to him by her former masters' name by "mistake", when she's peeved at him.

        As one of the first Solars in action in recent years, Jahn's more flamboyant activities have attracted the attention of various powers, seen and unseen, in the galaxy, many of which have begun to seek him out. Perhaps the most interested of these is the Seven-Devil-Killer, one of His Divine Lunar Presence's top assassins...and the bearer of the Moonsilver sibling to his Beamklave.

                "BAD" PENNY

                Penelope was born to a peasant family, but her dreaming heart led her to run away from the simple fisherman's life to join a passing troupe of wanderers at an early age. In their care, she learned the arts of dazzling and entertaining bored townspeople, and how to dissemble convincingly, skillfully inventing new pasts for herself to everyone she met. Penny learned how to avoid the Guardians of the Realm and how to bribe her way out of trouble when that was unavoidable. Most of all, she learned how to gain and exploit the trust of others...

                Penny realizes that it was one of her more daring schemes that led to a Magistrate and the Black Helms cracking down on her caravan of wanderers, and she's pretty sorry about that, but omelettes require broken eggs, and she'd learned about all she could from them, anyway, so it was time to move on. Besides, they really weren't doing much with all that money they'd gathered, when she could put it to much better use!

                So, Penny went to the larger cities of the Realm where she reinvented herself. During her wanderings, she had put her talent for observation and mimicry to good use, and in short order (and many new clothes and accessories), she found that she could pass herself off as a member of any strata of Realm society, from peasant to citizen to patrician.

                Swiftly making contacts in the Realm's underworld, Penny soon made a name for herself as a conwoman extraordinaire, dancing through society in a dizzying array of disguises, successfully grifting wealthy commoners and patricians who had more jade than common sense. Eventually, she overreached again, this time by fleecing House Ragara while in the guise of a minor patrician noblewoman from a Satrapy in desperate need of a loan.

                This broke one of the cardinal rules of the Realm's criminal element, who survive by staying under the Dynasts' radar. Drawing that kind of heat down on them will make them give up any sister criminal in an instant, and the Dragon-Blooded are quick to make an example of any that dare their wrath.

                What happened next depends on Penny's mood when telling her story: Sometimes, she claims to have been Chosen by the Unconquered Sun in the very act of pulling off her audacious scheme, which both necessitated her quick escape and enabled it, as well; other times, she tells of being exposed and captured, only her exaltation enabling her to escape her chains and prison right before her torture and execution. Or, she successfully slipped out of gaol on her own, and was chosen on board the ship taking her off the Isle, as though in reward for her resourcefulness.

                Whatever the truth, the newborn Solar took her act on the road.

                Penny traveled the Vermillion Road across the South, conning and grifting with increasing supernatural skill as she went: A sizable shipment of precious stones went missing from Gem, several nobles of the Lap lost their shirts in a pyramid scheme, many magistrates in Paragon lost their heads when the Perfect learned they had been swindled, the Tri-Khan won't reveal what he lost to her, but suffice to say, she can't show her face in Chiaroscuro ever again; Harborhead's economy almost crashed when flooded the market with fake jade, and her meddling in Varangia caused the permanent shut-down of many of its famous gambling parlors. As for An-Teng...she doesn't talk about what went down there. That place is just crawling with Bad Craziness...

                Deciding that the South had become too hot (), Penelope set out for the East, eventually setting up shop in Nexus, a city after her own greedy heart! A place where, so long as you mind the Dogma and Civilities, no one can complain about you ripping them off because there are no laws? Hells, yes! Of course, nothing prevents your victims from taking their anger out on you, either, so you'd better be smarter, faster and sharper than they are, and avoid the notice of the Council of Entities and their Mercenary Companies...

                Penny maintains a few aliases in Nexus, including a wealthy socialite in Bastion, an eccentric artist in Cinnabar, a humble student in Sentinel's Hill, and a beggar in Nighthammer. She is greedily eyeing the Guild, having spent enough time studying its operations in her journeys across the Threshold to identify its patterns and weaknesses; successfully fleecing it would make her name across Creation, but it would also make her enemies as powerful as the Ragaras. It also galls her that she never got a shot at cracking either the impregnable Bank of the Scarlet Throne or the Vault of the Imperial Treasury; if such an opportunity feasibly arose, she'd find it hard to resist returning to the Blessed Isle...

                Notes: Penelope has amassed a significant amount of experience in her adventures, much of which went into her Mental and Social traits (Specialties in High Society Disguise, Gaining Confidence and Talking Her Way Out of Trouble), as well as Backgrounds reflecting her Criminal Connections and Resources (easily 5; at this point, she's clearly in it for the thrill rather than the wealth). Her Merits include Silver Tongue, Jack of All Trades, and Cache; her Flaws are Vice (Stealing) and Wanted (House Ragara).

                Penny's Charm focus is on Stealth, Larceny, Presence, Performance, Socialize and Dodge. As of yet, she has no Familiars or Exalted Allies, although the Gold Faction is aware of her activities and is awaiting the right time to approach her. Her only Artifact is a set of Everyman Armor she stole in the South, but after an encounter with Shalrina in her shop, she is eager to earn one of the Goddess' Mask Artifacts (as well as her other services, if her enemies ever catch up to her...).

                PS: I can't decide on Penny's Caste: Is she a Social Night or a Larcenous Eclipse? Which would you make her?

                                YUKI, THE SWORD-BRIDE
                                Not all of the sword-clans of Vaneha excel in swordsmanship, but the most prosperous still revolve around the sword in some way. This is true of the minor aristocratic Clan Kiji, whose hard-earned fame involves a secret formula they have perfected over generations, that allows their family to forge uniquely beautiful, as well as deadly, swords.

                                Unfortunately, the fortunes of the family fell when they fell into the hands of the young Count Yuji, whose extravagance and out-of-control gambling threatened to empty their coffers and ruin their reputation. When he began covering his debts by selling off ancestral weapons, and even bargaining with the secret formula of the Clan, his little sister Yuki decided to act.

                                Although competant with the sword as all of Vaneha's daughters are, Yuki was no great swordswoman, but she refused to stand by doing nothing; by law, only a blood-sibling could challenge the Head of the Family. However, she hedged her bets, in the likely chance that she would lose, by undergoing a ancient ritual wherein she symbolically married the Honor Sword of the Clan itself, ensuring that even if her brother cut her down, he would still ruin his standing in Vanehan society.

                                Elaborately dressed, painted and coiffured, Yuki publicly challenged Yuji, and met with him as the sun arose on a dueling courtyard in Jibei. Furious, her insulted brother spared her no quarter, and as the resolute girl, who could barely defend against him, prepared herself to pass on to her next incarnation, she decided to at least give him a significant wound to remember her by. The bystanders gasped as her last-ditch effort surged with power and skill, guided by the hand of the rising sun itself, cutting the Count's sword-arm entirely off!

                                Having suffered one of the worst injuries a Vanehan can bear, and faced with a blazing Anathema where his sister once stood, the Count had no choice but to surrender, and yield his status as Head of the Clan. Yuki, marvelling at quickly her swordmanship improved, spent the next week visiting the households who had gained her family's ancestral weapons and formula through the wasteful ways of her brother, challenging them for their return. Most saw no shame in capitulating, although some wished to test her steel, but all agreed to an Honor-Bond against using what they learned of her Clan's secrets.

                                Yuki's true passion, however, had always been working the forge of her family, working and treating the molten metal in their unique way, and shaping some of the most eye-catching weapons the nation had yet seen. She had always dreamed of her name being added to the Honor Roll of the Kiji's most illustrious swordsmiths, and now, empowered by the sun's own industry, that dream was within her grasp. With a couple of weeks of practice, she found herself forging swords easily the match of any her family had ever made, and even expanding upon the formula to craft swords even more durable and sharp than any yet seen in Vaneha!

                                But this would have to wait. Yuki was shortly visited by a delegation from the Sword Prince himself. In a polite, flowery manner, the ambassador hinted that, while Vaneha had no official policy regarding the Immaculate Order as no known citizen had yet become Anathema, it was perhaps best for all concerned if the young Lady of the Clan should delegate her duties and take an extended vacation, until such matters became clearer. Yuki agreed and set her affairs in order.

                                In truth, this turn of events suits her quite well. While travelling distant lands has never been a dream of hers, Yuki is curious to learn how weapons are forged in other lands. And she has lately been plagued by recurring dreams of her last incarnation, a mighty Lawgiver sword smith who was the prize pupil of the fabled Sword Priest himself, and who created many legendary weapons that might yet endure in this fallen world, many in unworthy hands. Yuki, the Sword-Bride, has taken it upon herself to free these lost children, whether they be lost, buried or held in unworthy hands, and to restore them to righteous heroes who may use them to carve new legends, as well as to birth new wonders of her own, that this fallen, shadowy age may yet be redeemed and brightened through the shining steel of the sword.

                                Image: Yuki is a young woman of average appearance, well-schooled in aristocratic dress, speaking and manners. Currently, she foregoes her formal wear, dressing in comfortable, but durable, traveller's clothes, never parted from the large sword-case that she carries her husband in, along with a small retinue of retainers. Few have seen her true aspect, a well-toned physique fashioned from many hours of handling the tools of the forge, complete with many scars and burns, marks of her trade that she takes great pride in.

                                Notes: Yuki is a Dawn Caste Solar, with the anima banner of a phoenix and the Supernal Ability of Craft. She has Essence 1, with Craft 4 and Melee 3, but will soon raise all of those traits with all the practice she is getting. While she doesn't mind getting injured in the process of forging new swords, she is quickly learning the benefits of Resistance Charms, esp. as she favors taking blows over dodging them. She is also finding the application of her Essence to her studies of Linguistics and Socializing to be quite useful, and every swordfighter benefits from having a powerful Presence.
                                Yuki specializes in crafting beautifully decorated Exceptional weapons, and is gaining enough experience through doing so to begin the next step of her journey, the crafting of magical blades. While her dreams are a guide, however, she still needs much mastery of ancient Occult Lore to be successful, to say nothing of access to exotic ingredients.

                                Yuki's most treasured possession and companion is her "husband", the Honor Sword of Clan Kiji. The most prestigious Vanehan families keep ancient Daiklaves in their sacred family shrines, the older and more storied, the better. Yuki's is a Black Jade and Moonsilver Daiklave named Kagehoshi, which, like all such swords, has long slept without a user to attune to it. Kagehoshi has finally awakened in Yuki's handling, and is somewhat bemused to find itself married, but plays its ancestral role well, and intends to hold Yuki to her vows. The constant practice the newlyweds have had has so far activated his most basic Evocations, which manipulate light and shadow.

                                Yuki has the Merits: Artifact 2, Followers 1 (a small group who cooks and cleans for her, as well as carries her things), Influence 2 (Sword Clans of Vaneha), Language 1 (Riverspeak, necessary for arranging sales of supplies, materials and swords), Pain Tolerance (earned at the fires of the forge), and Resources 2 (befitting the head of a Clan, albeit a small one).

                                Intimacies
                                Defining Principle: I make great weapons for great heroes.
                                Defining Principle: I am responsible for the fortunes of my family.
                                Major Tie: Kagehoshi (Respect)
                                Major Tie: Clan Fiji (Loyalty)
                                Minor Tie: Count Yuji (Disappointment)

                                Limit Trigger: Seeing beautiful weapons being used for unjust ends.

                                    XANTRA, THE DREAMING PROPHET

                                    Deep in the Southern desert, notorious Dejaz is reknowned for its never-ending revelry, and visitors are always astounded at the flow of sumptous food, drugs, music and pleasure provided for the asking. But for a time, one wonder that the natives steered away from, was Xantha.

                                    One of innumerable children born from the ceaseless trysting in the city, and as carelessly abandoned, he was raised as all such are, trained to cater to visitors and to provide whatever entertainments they wished. However, his masters soon learned that none would touch the boy, as he was already clearly touched by some higher power that spoke through the child, and even the excesses of Dejaz could not tempt revelers to defile a holy child.

                                    Indeed, the Benefactors soon ascertained that the boy was blessed, or cursed, with a gift, of slipping into a dream-like trance at any moment, and pronouncing dire fates upon all nearby. The augeries were clear but not good; they dared not kill or silence the whelp, nor could they exile or bury him, lest some greater ill befall the city. And so, if they could not entomb him in some dungeon, they went in the opposite direction, and erected a tall, thin tower of stone, and placed the boy atop it, that he could prophesy to his hearts' content up where none could hear.

                                    But even they, the masters of the forbidden, underestimated people's desire to taste that which was forbidden. And so, even as they used mechanical contrivances to feed the growing youth, who sat still atop his stone tower around which the surge of merriment swarmed, there were those who climbed or swung near or arranged to talk to Xantha, seeking some news of their future. None went away disappointed, though few were gladdened.

                                    In those days, there dwelt in Dejaz a notable Raksha Lady, of exquisite form and motion, who sought to build her Story on the foundation that she could entice any, however holy and reserved, to lust after her. Behold, saith she, I shall dance my dance, and e'en yon holy child shall scamper down from his tower of purity to lie with me! And she began her dance, and all the city paused in its revelry to watch.

                                    It was said that her dance was so sensuous, that even those besotted with drug and drink were cured of their addictions instantly, for they only lusted after her thereafter; but the young man was moved not. The Fair Lady increased her efforts, actually deigning to strip off her luminous veils, which caused blind beggars to pleasure themselves to madness and animals to rut wildly in the streets, regardless of species, but still the boy was unmoved. Undettered, the Fae Madame, desperate at losing her legend, danced a spiraling dance up the side of the tower itself to reach the youth, for none could resist her once she laid her fingers upon them. But just beyond she laid her burning touch upon Xantha, his brow burst with a brightness more burning still, that cast the temptress down like a screaming meteor.

                                    Then did Xanthas finally speak to the entire city, in a sunborn voice that merely repeated words he was given: Hive of Fools and Revelers! Thou iniquity has not gone unseen! Take thy sport while thou art still able, for the final hour draws nearer than thou knowest! Soon, these palaces shall fall upon thee and make for thine eternal tombs! Quit this place, and live! Or stay, and surely die.

                                    So saying, the newborn Lawgiver leapt from his tower to the outer boundary of the city, not setting one foot upon Dejaz's stones, for he knew that when he next did so, it would be either the Doom of the city or himself. As for the revelers of the city, lured and lulled by the Benefactors', they nervously laughed to themselves, dismissing his warnings as sun-madness, and rejoined their endless party.

                                    Xantha wandered aimlessly through the deep desert, finally coming upon one of the places where the world was weak, and tainted from the madness that lay beneath it. Here, the sands flowed as water, and Xantha swam through the rolling, cresting sand, until he came to a temple, the long-lost Floating Mosque, whose opalescent step he climbed to the doors. These were guarded by two great, glinting lions, a golden one from Heaven on the right, and a brazen one from Hell on the left, both of whom presented him with a riddle in two contradictory parts, the failure of which would entitle them to divide him as a feast between them.

                                    Fortunately for Xantha, his life had already given him the answer, and so the hungry lions were forced to bow and admit him. Within the Mosque, Xantha studied, and learned and grew in wisdom and power, and they say his tests and trials within were of a number only the Incarnae could count. Soon enough he departed, now able to speak but a word for the tempest-tossed sands to settle into a path as straight and solid as any in Creation, while ruby-eyed sandsharks with the voices of children sang hosannas as he strode forth back to the world.

                                    Now Xantha travels amongst the courts of the spirits and the fae, rescuing those luckless mortals who can be saved from their depredations, while reminding all of their proper place in the great chain of being, even if that means ruling over them for a time to set things right. He lets the Sun guide him across the South, ever aware that eventually, though not even he knows the hour, his steps will take him back to his birthplace for a final reckoning.

                                    Image: Xantha is an attractive young Southern man (although he has never noticed) and his constant walking across the burning wastes have given him a fit, though very lean, look. He allows his hair to grow wild, in accordance with the Southern custom that this designates one as holy. He pays little mind to his clothing, and when his inevitably wears away, he humbly accepts new clothes that townspeople gift him with, as he does gifts of food and drink.

                                    Notes: Xantha is a Zenith Caste Solar, whose Anima Banner is a golden four-armed statue sitting in a meditative pose. His Essence rating is 2, and his Supernal Ability is Integrity, with which he sometimes masters the powers of spirits while setting their courts aright. Resistance, Survival and Occult are his other major areas of focus, and he has learned Tiger Style martial arts from the gently harsh tutelege of his lion masters.

                                    Xantha has the Merits: Followers 2 (various mortals he has helped), Influence 3(Southern Spirit Courts), Language 1 (Old Realm), Manse 3 (Floating Mosque), Martial Artist, and Thaumaturgist (Inborn gift of prophesy, refunded upon Exaltation).

                                    Intimacies
                                    Defining Principle: Mortals and Spirits must hew to their place in the order of things.
                                    Major Tie: Mortals (Protective)
                                    Major Tie: Spirits (Wary)
                                    Major Tie: Fair Folk (Antagonistic)
                                    Minor Tie: Lion Tutors (Respect)

                                    Limit Trigger: Being unable to help mortals suffering from supernatural abuse.

                                        ELDER GUAN, THE TRAVELING HEALER


                                        Guan was born and raised in Wu-Jian, where, like many youths, he joined the gangs fighting for their turf to distinguish himself. Unlike many, he survived and settled down, graduating to the neighborhood associations who really ran Wu-Jian's communities, and who unofficially used the gangs as muscle. Guan married, had many children, and buried too many of them. These losses, along with the ones he had participated in on the rooftops of the city, motivated him to pour all his efforts into the poorly equipped clinic that serviced the people of his neighborhood.

                                        Guan turned the place around, inspiring the weary workers there, convincing those with wealth to channel more into better quality supplies and equipment, and even treating the sick and injured of rival neighborhoods, which strengthened relations in the long run. He also educated himself, becoming a self-taught physician of some skill, even teaching himself to read Old Realm so that he could make use of the ancient scrolls still within the clinic.

                                        The day finally came when Guan ventured below the populated layers of the city, in search of more ancient wisdom; there was little more he could do for his people, so either he came back with more to aid them with, or he vanished into urban legend, either outcome suiting him just fine. Instead, he came back with something else, due to a hazily-remembered encounter with an odd behemoth in the weirdly-warped shadowed alleys: Exaltation.

                                        From that day on, Guan's newly increased medical prowess became the stuff of legend, attracting even the wealthiest to risk coming to his little clinic for his aid. Soon, the place had more than enough money, and better still, the staff were trained to be as good healers as any hospital could boast. Guan decided to take his leave, to see the wonders of the wide world that he had only heard about before, to learn from the varied peoples, to bring his gift of healing to them and also to bring back what they had to teach on his visits back home.

                                        Earning room and board aboard the Denzik city-ship with free use of his skills, Guan soon earned a respected place within their hospice-barge. Whenever the small fleet of merchant ships disembarks from the Denzik craft to trade in various ports around the Inland Sea and in the Western Ocean, Guan went with them, drinking in the new languages, customs and games of people across Creation. Denzik traders spread tales of his acumen, and often came to drag him away to help some unfortunate with his skills.

                                        This led the Elder to encounter a wide variety of illnesses, both mundane and supernatural. Many of which were caused by curses of a wide variety, which has since driven him to increase his understanding of the supernatural world and its denizens. Guan has begun to broaden his reputation as a great medicine man into one as a puissant occultist and exorcist, as well.

                                        Elder Guan has yet to visit the Dreaming Sea area of Creation, which is well, as there is a domain there ruled by an exceptionally powerful spirit known only as The Maharani. What few outside of Heaven know is that this violet-skinned, six-armed woman is actually a creation of Guan's previous Solar incarnation, via his Occult mastery. She was his slave for centuries, serving him in all things with the hope of freedom constantly dangled before her. It took the Usurpation to actually free her, and she has traveled the world and grown very strong since, eventually settling to rule her own court of spirits and mortals according to her tastes.

                                        Now, the Maharani's Omen-Birds sing that her worst fear has come true, her former master has finally been reborn. She is uncertain if he can still establish any claim over her, especially as she has taken great pains to hide her true name from all in Creation. She ponders the wisdom of sending spirit spies to watch him, or even assassins to dispatch him, knowing that this will likely draw him to her that much sooner...

                                        Image: Guan is the very image of the wise old elder, a small, shrivelled Western man with a long, wispy beard and bushy eyebrows. He habitually wears dark, round glasses and smokes a long, clay pipe. He dresses in simple robes of particular shades of blue and violet, that his memories tell him physicians wore in the First Age to honor the Maidens of Serenity and Endings. Despite his obvious age, however, there is nothing frail about him, and he practically bursts with vitality and good cheer.

                                        Notes: Elder Guan is a Twilight Caste Solar, with a Crane anima banner. His Essence is 3, and his Supernal Ability is Medicine. Occult is his second highest Ability. As a young man, he ran with the Wild Heron gang, and learned their variant of Crane Style martial arts; he has since learned to apply Essence to those teachings, and has taught himself most of its Charms, which he often uses to deliver Wound-Banishing Strikes in order to heal and set his allies' injuries in the midst of battle.
                                        His Merits include Backing 2 (Denzik Hospital), Language 2 (Old Realm, Riverspeak), Martial Artist, Resources 2.

                                        Defining Principle: I am the greatest physician!
                                        Major Principle: I will see all the lands and spread my healing there.
                                        Major Tie: Spirits that possess mortals (Anger)
                                        Major Tie: Wu-Jian Clinic (Pride)
                                        Major Tie: First Age Lore (Possessive)
                                        Minor Tie: Denzik (Gratitude)

                                        Limit Trigger: Losing patients.

                                            THE RAITON KID
                                            Born into the prison-city of Fortitude, the boy who would grow up to become the Raiton Kid was determined not to let the systematic abuse of being born with the stigma of criminality, the mandatory gang-initiations and wars, nor even the worship of the Prison-God, brand him for life.

                                            Upon reaching his teens, he embarked on the Rite-of-Passage every young person went through, the obligatory escape attempt. But rather than half-hearting it, being captured as expected, and accepting one's natural place in Fortitude, he seriously made an effort, researching the city's layout and guard patrols, and laid his plans well in advance, such as by bribing certain guard dogs with choice (stolen) meat. His efforts paid off, and, somewhere along the path to freedom, he Exalted.

                                            He's not actually sure *when*, however, which bothers him. Some part of him can't help but wonder if he could really have done it without the Unconquered Sun's empowerment. But usually he puts such worries out of his head, for he has other worries now.

                                            Adapting to life on the outside has been difficult, for he only had the stories he grew up with as his guide, and he soon learned that they were hilariously wrong. Soon enough (and definitely with the help of his new nature, which he accepts begrudingly), he picked up on how things work, especially among the criminal classes of society, which his upbringing has uniquely qualified him for. Still, the practices and beliefs of "polite society", including honest, hard-working folk of all classes, frankly baffles him. He wants to understand them, and is a little worried that he fits in better with Guildsmen and the like.

                                            He earned the name "Raiton Kid" due to an incident in the Guild Border Town of Raiton's Teeth, where an innocent game of Boneyard escalated into a three-day riot wherein a prosperous caravan just happened to be fleeced of its many earnings. The Guild has put a huge bounty on the Kid's head, despite his protestations of innocence and coincidence. As if the bounty that fortitude put out on him, for being one of the very few to ever escape, wasn't enough...

                                            Image: The Raiton Kid is an average-looking Northern young man, although his rakish grin gives him a charm that makes him more attractive than he really is. He has gained enough money to dress in whatever style he wishes, and is still young and impulsive enough to want to show off, although he is learning the necessity of blending in. He is especially vulnerable to the wiles of beautiful people of either sex, as he had never seen anyone able to really make themselves so attractive in the dingy dungeons he grew up in. He is (slowly) learning that such folks can be as cut-throat as any other, although the appearance of true innocence and virtue gets to him every time...

                                            Notes: The Raiton Kid is an Essence 2 Night Caste Solar Exalt, with a Raiton anima banner. Larceny is his Supernal Ability, and Dodge and Thrown are his main recourse in a fight, although he prefers to avoid such with Stealth. Survival charms help him to make it through the harsh weather of the North, evade tracking from those who would bring him in for a reward and empower his Familiar, an Omen Dog that escaped his home city with him that he calls Frostbite. Although not yet a supernatural martial artists, the Kid is quite skilled with fighting chains from his time in his neighborhood's Chain Gang, and is seeking out a Dire Chain.

                                            His Merits are Danger Sense, Familiar 2, Language 2 (Riverspeak, Guild Cant), and Quick Draw 1. He also has the Wanted Flaw.

                                            Intimacies
                                            Defining Principle: I must make a huge legend of myself!
                                            Defining Principle: I will never go back to Fortitude!
                                            Major Tie: Beauty (Awe)
                                            Major Tie: The Guild (Comfort)
                                            Major Tie: Frostbite (Pal for life)
                                            Major Tie: Fortitude (Hate)
                                            Minor Tie: Chain Gang (Sadness)
                                            Minor Principle: Theft (Resigned)

                                            Limit Trigger: Being reminded of Fortitude.

                                                CAPTAIN SUNSTORM

                                                The boy who would grow up to become Capt. Sunstorm was born in the floating city of Gloam, a prosperous Realm port to the East, constantly surrounded by the sails of sea-craft, as testament to its popularity. With a natural head for figures, and a talent for smoothing over difficulties between peoples, the boy was groomed for a position with the Merchant Marine fleet, but it was the sheer act of sailing that made his soul soar.

                                                Until one day, when the ship he was on wandered too close to Threshold waters and was captured by pirates, who escaped pursuing Peleps pirate-hunters by fleeing up the rivers of the Scavenger Lands, where they were forbidden to follow. At the city of Nexus, perhaps the largest pirate port in the East, the ship and all aboard were sold to the Guild, who proceeded to dispense with the former Realm citizens as slaves as they wished.

                                                Fortunately, the boy was able to impress with his quick wits and agile words, and was retained by a Guildsman who had a need of a young mind to help him keep his accounts. The next ten years were a blur of disciplinary beatings, delirious rewards, lots of traveling and calculations, along with finding creative ways to help his master skim from the top. But however bad it got, and however much he missed his home and the sea, he was always well aware that there were others much worse off than himself; indeed, he often helped care for them in his masters' caravan before selling them to new masters, or worse still, the Fair Folk.

                                                Deliverance came when he was discovered using the same creativity he used to cook his master's books to arrange things so that some slaves were delivered to the wrong places, where they naturally escaped. Just as he was about to pay with his life, the Unconquered Sun set his mark upon the young man's brow, which stayed the Guildsmans' hand. Following Guild protocol for when a slave is Chosen, he promptly gave the newborn Solar his freedom, a generous amont of silver for his pains, and politely asked him to leave. The young man could have taken revenge right then and there, but he had learned patience...

                                                With the Sun enhancing his own gifts, expanding his seed money was trivial, and soon the boy was able to reinvent himself. Purchasing a ship and attracting a like-minded crew, he set out to become the River Province's most successful pirate hunter! It was a disaster. He'd underestimated how much of the Scavenger Lands' economy depended on piracy, few offered their help, and rivers constrained their mobility too much, unlike a wide open sea.

                                                While reevaluting his plans, he laid low at a Guild border station, where he knew how to blend in quite well. Everyone was marveling at a First Age wonder that a Merchant Prince had spent decades and considerable resources restoring: a ship that could said the very earth itself like water! With this miracle, he would have the most imposing caravans ever! Or he would, if he hadn't sold it to that non-descript young man for two dinar.

                                                Now with a ship that could treat the open land as his long-lost wide-open sea, the former pirate-hunter recreated himself yet again, this time as Capt. Sunstorm, scourge of the Guild and other slavers! Gathering his crew to him once more, they set sail, attacking caravans and border stations, raiding the Guild's coffers of slaves, silver and drugs, and freeing the lot (often destroying the latter), while still keeping enough coin to keep themselves well-off, of course. The Guild is not amused, of course, and spends increasing amounts to sic mercenaries and bounty hunters onto them, but so far, Sunstorm's speed, manueverability and First Age guns have given him the edge.

                                                Still, Sunstorm knows that his luck and skill can't last forever, and so he has negotiated safe haven with various spirit courts and even, ironically enough, Fae freeholds, for the inevitable time when it is time to hang up his colors for good and reinvent himself yet again. But for now, Captain Sunstorm sails proudly across the land of the River Province, raiding the wicked and freeing the helpless!

                                                Image: Captain Sunstorm presents himself as the very romantic ideal of the pirate that those who have never seen such might dream about, cultivating a rakish air while clad in colorful attire, weathered, yet still well-kept, always ready with a wink and a laugh. Only his chosen few in his crew see his true self, a haggered man, driven by nightmares of the miseries he contributed to, who longs to assuage the guilt that brings, and who knows that his very nature prevents him from returning to his long-lost family.

                                                Notes: Sunstorm is an Eclipse Caste Solar, with the anima banner of an Ox-Dragon breaking chains around itself with tosses of its mighty head. His Supernal Abiliy is Sail, with which he has enhanced both his ship and crew. Bureaucracy and Larceny also see a lot of use, as do Presence and Socialtize. Sunstorm is actually quite weak in fighting skills, never being able to find the time to shore those up. He prefers to Dematerialize when faced with real trouble, a trick he picked up from spirit acquaintances.
                                                Sunstorm's Artifact 4 represents his Land Ship, a large yacht that he has refitted as a pirate corsair, along with its built-in Lighting Ballista. He has rechristened this ship The Ox-Dragon, and crewed it with well trained Sea Devils (Followers 1), some of whom have learned Sea-Faring Hero Style martial arts. His First Mate is a mortal that he has enhanced with Underling Promoting Touch and Power Awarding Prana (Retainer 2). Their raidings have given him Resources 3, though sharing it with the crew and giving much to freed slaves reduces that to 2. Contacts 2 helps keep them ahead of bounty hunters hired by the Guild, which gives him the Flaw Wanted. Language 2 gives him Riverspeak and Guild Cant.

                                                Intimacies
                                                Defining Principle: I will balance the accounts of my life, by freeing at least as many as I have exploited!
                                                Defining Tie: The Guild (Hate)
                                                Major Principle: I will escape myself by becoming someone better.
                                                Minor Tie: Ship and Crew (Pride)

                                                Limit Trigger: Failing to free someone.
