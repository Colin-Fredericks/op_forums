        Resistance:

        Vengeful Essence Eruption
        The Solar may activate this charm after suffering health damage greater than her Essence. Her Essence explodes outwards, sending her opponents back. The Solar rolls Stamina + Resistance, treating the eruption as a medium weapon with whichever tags a goremaul has. The attack does not deal any kind of damage, but can knock opponents back. By spending an additional 2XP, the Solar can upgrade the eruption to an artifact medium weapon.

            Sweet, I finally rolled something interesting. I was just brainstorming a system for Lore, too.

            Lore:

            Astute Scholar Intuition
            The Solar can use this charm when attempting to solve a problem caused by something which is currently being hidden from her. The Solar uses her own common knowledge to devise a solution which, if executed properly, will prevent the problem from getting worse until the Solar can identify the root cause. If the cause of the problem somehow evolves to counter the Solar's efforts, the Solar may activate this charm again for one mote in order to learn which adjustments to make to her plan. This charm does not help against problems caused by completely lost knowledge, such as the Great Curse.

                        Craft:

                        Awaken the Sleeping Treasure
                        The Solar's acknowledgement transforms the mundane into the wondrous. The Solar spends a scene interacting with a mundane object; she can trade blows against her rival's steel, admire a statue, or walk the walls of a fortress. At the end of the scene, the Solar may activate this charm and turn it into a 1-dot Artifact with evocations determined by the Solar based on the object's form, materials and relationship to the Solar. If it would be more appropriate, the Solar can turn the object into a 1-dot Solar Manse with powers chosen by the Solar instead. If the Manse is ever destroyed, its demesne does not linger.

                            Linguistics:

                            Assured Solar Authority
                            The Solar applies her seal or signature to a document and activates this charm. Anyone who looks at the document automatically knows that it has been approved by the Solar. The document's holder gains a number of automatic successes equal to the Solar's Essence to any Threaten action against someone who denies the legitimacy of the Solar's signature.

                                                            Integrity:

                                                            Casting the Iron Hook
                                                            The Solar makes a social influencing against one of the Fair Folk to make herself the target of its ire, rolling Appearance + Integrity and adding a number of automatic successes equal to her Essence. If the influence is successful and the Raksha later chooses to attack the Solar, it loses momentum equal to the Solar's Essence every time it attempts to use its charms on the Solar and fails.
