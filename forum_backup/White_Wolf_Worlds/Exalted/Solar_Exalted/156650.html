    Solar Sifu?

    06-02-2014, 01:15 PM
    Teaching and fighting both fall into the purview of Solar excellence, yet there aren't a whole lot of training charms that a Solar martial artist could use to run his own dojo with. Tiger Warrior Training is clearly meant more for drilling formations of soldiers than teaching a class of students, and the relevant lore charms only teach support skills like Medicine and such. There is the essence enlightenment charm from Scroll of the Monk (walled off behind Essence-Lending Method, which I've never had a use for ever), but that's pretty much it. As far as I can see, you have to go into Crane Style to get Humbling Enlightenment Commentary and Kindly Sifu's Quill to pull off this character type.

    Am I missing something? Is there homebrew that fixes this problem anywhere? Or am I going to be forced to play a Sidereal if I want to teach people how to kick ass with TMAs/CMAs?

          Honestly, Tiger Warrior Training works pretty well. It's broad enough to do other stuff, yes, but you can have a Solar running a dojo with a bunch of students just fine. You would also want their enlightenment charm, and maybe a few other training charms. It's worth pointing out that part of being a sifu is also "overwhelming strength", and Solar charms support that all over the place -- a Solar who Exalted as an old man (or, in a game where you have elder Solars for whatever reason, one who is just legitimately that old) is positioned just fine to do the old "he's just an old man!" thing. Resistance and Athletics provide a lot of stuff that martial arts masters do. You probably want Lore to express ancient wisdom (and to also be a better teacher.)

          Part of the way the Solar charmset works is that it depends on you to flavor your theme, to an extent. It doesn't hand you 'Sifu' as a boxed theme, but "Wise old teacher who has mastered and can teach a lot of martial arts, and who is secretly incredibly tough and can run across a cobweb to the top of a mountain and then punch it in half" is pretty much all you need to play that concept, isn't it?

          Beyond that... you use MA for a lot of the Sifu thematics, yeah, but, well, you're trying to play an MA concept, right? What else would you use? Even Sidereals, who have Sifu as a core theme, rely heavily on MA for a lot of what it does, because, hey, if you're playing an ancient master of martial arts, you're probably going to want to be good at marital arts.

          (What Solars don't get, generally, is charms that make the statement "I can do this because I am a mysterious enlightened martial arts sifu" the way some Sidereal charms work their thematics. But that's not actually necessary to play a character like that; it's just a different sort of design.)

                      Originally posted by JimB View Post
                      Really? I'd have sworn I read something saying learning the form-type Charm makes one a master of a style.
                      I think both things have been said in different sections of the books, leaving the matter of "appropriate degree of mastery for teaching" fuzzy enough for STs and PCs to tinker with. Some games might enhance martial strife, questing, mystery and stuff by turning different post-form branches of a style into variant schools, making things such as pinnacle charms more elusive and mysterious, among other things.

                      As others have said quite well, Tiger-Warrior takes care of the mundane parts of martial training all too well, that you may teach a troop doesn't mean you can't teach individual students. Not to mention some of the tricks of Tiger-Warrior open room for young martial artists doing team combos in the opposition - coordinated does not have to mean "everybody doing the same move, at the same time", something a bunch of soldier squads & fireteams all over the world could explain quite well.

                      About the essence enlightenment charm from Scroll of the Monk, two things to say.

                      1) Essence-Lending Method feels like a speed bump to you, talk with your ST about house-ruling it into [permanent-no cost] or straight to Oblivion, or do it yourself, if you are the ST.
                      2) This, from Scroll of the Monk, pg. 19, in the end of that charms description.

                      All types of Celestial (and Abyssal and Infernal) Exalts possess a variation of Soul-Enlightening Beneficence. The recipient gains an affinity for the source of the Exalt’s power, like that granted by Touch of Divinity, which other Essence-sensing creatures can perceive.
                      Depending on how one works it out, Alchemicals and some of the new kids coming with 3e might have their own peculiar charm variants of it. Also, all those charms seem to "color" the Essence of the martial artist somewhat, meaning an exalt's mortal apprentices may show something of their teacher's essence in their charm use, making them not exactly popular with Blessed Isle natives and the Wyld Hunt.

                      And while not celestials, nothing there stops an enterprising PC of making up its own version, that is closer in effects to Touch of Divinity than Soul-Enlightening Beneficence's "enjoy your brand new Essence 2 now mortal, Haha!".

                      As an aside, few things could be as insanely awesome as some crazy exalt activating Soul-Enlightening Beneficence and then hitting an army's general or dojo's master with Empress Lives for All. Probably not kosher as both are simple charms of fuzzy duration, but certainly worth it for the overkill.

                      On a final note and before i forget, "Terrestrial Edification Program", Lord of Creation, pg. 61. If a charm to teach spells to mortals, DBs, DKs or spirits exists, no reason for one to teach MA charms not to.

                                      My advice - start a brainstorming thread looking for new Solar Charms about teaching and inspiring, that can then be applied to a sifu type character. Looking for MA specific Charms that sprout out of MA isn't going to work very well for Solars.

                                      Like, who are the great teachers from history? What can they do that Solars don't already have good Charms for?

                                      Like, for a sifu, I'd want a teacher who actually teaches his students ideas and values, motivates them, teaches them to make the right decisions and helps them become more experienced. Not really experience debt, training time reductions, bonus dice and random buffs. When I think of Mr Miyagi type characters, genuine teachers and educators, they teach and change the person, encourage what comes from within and reveal character. And as applied to martial arts, this makes the student more formidable in combat. They don't just make people more accurate, aggressive and stronger (unlike shouty dojo nemesis Cobra Kai guy). A Charm like Tiger Warrior Training doesn't really *feel* focused on imparting ideas, experiences, values and approaches, more on turning peasants with low fight-y dots into peasants with high fight-y dots (which it does with aplomb).

                                      I think in 3e, where social attacks and smart decisions (supposedly) matter in combat, might be well placed to enable this kind of character.

                                                      This is the Charm I'm using in my games to simulate this.

                                                      Bronze Tiger Litter Technique
                                                      Cost: —; Mins: War 5, Essence 5; Type: Permanent
                                                      Keywords: Training
                                                      Duration: Permanent
                                                      Prerequisite Charms: Legendary Warrior Curriculum
                                                      The armies of the Solar Exalted are as terrible a weapon as the daiklaves the Chosen wield. This Charm expands the capacity of the Exalt’s Legendary Warrior Curriculum to teach his troops special techniques. In the case of Essence-users the Solar may now teach them Charms that they could normally learn from the Exalt (Solar Charms if they are Solars, Terrestrial martial arts Charms if they are nearly any Essence-user). This only speeds the training of Charms that fall under those Abilities the Exalt can train with Tiger Warrior Training Technique.
