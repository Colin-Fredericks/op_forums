        I think that Exalted rightly distinguishes between "prophet" and "seer" (which is quite firmly Sidereal turf). A prophet is one who carries the word of god, an intermediary between mortal and divine. "Prophecy" originally simply meant the divine message, whether that be a vision of the End of Days or a commandment to care for the less fortunate; it's simply that, in our world, so many prophets proclaimed visions of the future that the concepts became colloquially linked. In and of itself, being a prophet implies no special ability to foretell the future -- especially in Exalted, where the gods are often as ignorant as mortals about what is to come.

        (As for prayer against the undead, to what should the Solar pray that is more potent against creatures of darkness than herself? The cleansing fire is within her and at her command; she needs no god to draw it forth, though as a stunt she might call upon the judgement of the Sun as she smites her enemies.)

While there's a threshold of cynicism on somethings, it doesn't mean that some topics are free from cynical takes. Creation is meant to run on real politik and since early Exalted 1e has generally gone pretty harsh on religions in general. 2e had a lot of other stupid to it in addition to this (mostly through petty malice and incompetence more than realpolitik).

                                                            The Cult of the Illumianted, Immaculate Order, the ancestor cult, and the Hundred Gods Heresy have always been varying range of control mechanism, conspiracy, political exploit or racket. Gods are generally assholes and not to be trusted to do their job for the sake of their job, because gods are people and people can be like that. WHen you're a powerful person and no one is about to tell you no, that becomes more likely the case.

                                                            Prayer has been a boring effect, as a note, so that you don't go around bullying gods for the effect you need as you need it. It would otherwise turn prayer effectively into a form of wishcasting against STs who don't know how to say "no" and kind of undermine what you're supposed to do, whcih is do stuff your damned stuff because you're an Exalt.
