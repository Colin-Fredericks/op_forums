            Larceny:

            Orichalcum Salt Deceit
            The Solar creates a forgery of an Artifact she possesses or has stolen. So long as this charm is active, the forgery acts exactly like the original, including its appearance, mote atunement cost and evocations. Once the charm ends, the forgery loses all its power and is exposed as a mundane item.
