        I decided to write up that "encode intimacy" charm I was thinking about. What do people think?

        Passion-Reifing Artistry
        Cost: 4m, 1gxp; Mins: Craft 5, Essence 3
        Type: Supplemental
        Keywords: None
        Duration: One crafting project
        Prerequisite Charms: Object-Strengthening Touch

        A Lawgiver artisan puts something of themselves into everything they craft. With this charm, this becomes vastly more pronounced - the fruits of their labor uphold their passions and motivations even in the hands of others.

        This charm is activated at the beginning of a major or greater project, and the motes remained committed until the project is completed. The Solar must declare one of their intimacies they are embedding within the object - this can be any level for a major project, while a superior project requires major or better intensity, and a legendary project must be defining. Once the project is successfully completed, the nature of the intimacy, both its target and its context, will be obvious to anyone who rolls (Perception + either Craft or Socialize) while scrutinising it.

        Anyone who using the object for its intended purpose in a manner that would uphold or support the encoded intimacy gains 1 non-charm die on any rolls required. This stacks with bonuses for exceptional equipment, if the object is such. If a user shares the intimacy, at the same level as the original creator, they receive additional bonuses: for a major project, or a mundane superior project, if they spend willpower on a roll that support's the object's intimacy, the user adds two successes, rather than just one, to their result. For an artifact superior project, or a legendary project, the item automatically gains an evocation that unlocks for free for any user who shares the intimacy at the same level. The GM and the player should collaborate to determine the details of this evocation - it should be appropriate for Essence 1 characters, and relate to the intimacy in some way.

                                    While I don't really feel like getting into the whole Charms that give you XP/things that cost XP thing, that's not really a good view on it.

                                    One of the explicit design goals for 3e is the removal of any Ability as a downtime only trait. Craft and Lore both received new systems that heavily emphasize using them in active play.

                                    Since this is a thread about Craft and all, sticking to that: The Expression Solar XP bonus and the crafting basic objectives actually overlap significantly if a Craft focused character has an Intimacy (or multiples) related to their craft that's Major or Defining. It doesn't have to be some big flashing act of crafting, you can do it with a low-key Basic project if you tie it into your Intimacy strongly enough/entertainingly enough. The Role Solar XP bonus is really simple to get for Twilight craftspeople, and not that rough for everyone else but Dawns; but you can always get the Role bonus by ceding spotlight time.

                                    So when it comes to what seems to be the original dev's design theories, it seems a bit more operative to assume that they were kind of acknowledging that Craft is an XP sink. This isn't even getting into the size of the Craft Charm trees, but things like Supreme Celestial Focus. The whole point of Supreme Celestial Focus isn't about whether or not you're going to hit the Solar XP bonuses with Craft, but making the need to buy each craft skill set your character has as an additional 0-5 Ability less taxing.
