    Originally posted by Elfive View Post
    It just wouldn't look right for the solars to not be decked head to foot in golden bling though.
    Yes, that's often how it feels when something has historically been only one way. I'd certainly prefer that being an approach that's still supported -- and encouraged depending on what you're after -- but not mandated as such. The writers seem to be going in this direction.

    Originally posted by Stephenls
    We're working to ensure there's reason to want to stick with your own Magical Material, and there's definitely going to be reasons in place for Solars to not particularly favor Starmetal over any other MM while Sidereals drop everything and chase fallen stars because that shit is so useful to them, and we definitely want to ensure that Dragon-Blooded are incentivized towards jade, but Solars get to use everything with equal facility for a number of reasons ranging from "John wants Solars to be especially proficient with Evocations, not just Evocations form orichalcum weapons" to "Because Solars are the primary player-character-type and it's more fun if everyone playing Solars can further differentiate their characters aesthetically via a wide range of weapon appearances."

    We are aware this is a change, and believe we are doing it for the right reasons, and are working to ensure it doesn't come off as gratuitous Solar favoritism. It'll make sense. And I am not cleared to provide details.

    (Also this edition will tend to have a lot more hybrid material artifacts -- Prince Diamond wields a sword made of orichalcum and Chiaroscuro glass, for example.)

                    Originally posted by John Morke
                    Evocations depend on how much power you draw from within; by contrast, if you wear more of your power on the outside (Sidereals, Lunars, Abyssals, Infernals) you aren't as good at them . . . It's about how far your Essence can carry you, and where it goes. Lunars put all of their power into their Charms, and their Charms represent all of their creative, motive energy transitioning into a visible expression of power (like DBT). In essence, Lunars aren't as good at Evocations because their Charms already evoke most of their creative, transformative potential . . . It is also why, when a Lunar goes DBT, his Evocations stop working.
                    Hmm. Just pondering this and its implications. Maybe Sidereals are so bad at Evocations because of how much they have to rely on Martial Arts (closed Charmset and all). Maybe activating a Form-type Charm takes a lot of "motive energy" and shows "wearing power on the outside" and might reduce Evocation power for anyone wearing a Form-tyle Charm from an MA, much like how DBT would shut down Evocation use for Lunars.

                    ​So maybe for Sidereals, starmetal is super important because it allows them to keep accessing (more/higher level) Evocations even while using "external" MA Charms and Forms, where if they were using a non-starmetal weapon, they could not do so.

                                    This'd be more my guess (and preference), too. The devs have spoken a lot about taking holistic views on splat balance, and I have no doubt that includes their associated Artifacts. Whatever benefits and Evocations moonsilver Artifacts can give, I expect they will generally be very attractive for those with a Lunar's capabilities and typical methods. The same should go for every splat with an associated Magical Material. Thus, absent other factors, individual characters of a splat may thus favor Artifacts of other Magical Materials for their purposes, even while most will gravitate to their associated ones, and none are necessarily making dumb decisions.

                                    I could see cases where a Solar could theoretically get greater Evocations with an artifact than a member of its associated splat, but the Evocations and other benefits the non-Solar does get just synergize so well with native abilities. On balance, the non-Solar might come out nearly, just as, or more impressive than a hypothetical Solar would (albeit maybe in different ways). I mean, the Sidereal toolkit has some rather peculiar and unique powers in it. I could see some Starmetal Evocations that are helpful if unwieldy for most, but downright devastating in the hands of a Vizier with the right Charms (and/or presumably functional Astrology).
