        It would be more accurate to say that Solars are meant to adhere to an inequal balance.

        As a Solar, you are supposed to be able to defeat any other splat in an area of shared specialty. If you are of equal experience and specialization it should not be trivial, and there is a reasonable chance that you might lose even though the odds are weighted in your favor.

        As a non-Solar, a Solar should be something you find yourself having to be wary around, and if they share an area of specialization with you you may have to ask for help where you wouldn't otherwise. It should not be a good idea to kill any newborn Solar you see on sight because the moment they get some XP they'll be able to tell you to get on your knees and make you like it.

                                    Let's not forget something important : not all Solars have all Solar Charms. Because Solars have the potential to be an head ahead everyone else (because one of the elements of their thematics is to be "Prime among pairs"), this doesn't mean that any individual Solar would be better than any given non-Solar character.

                                    If a Solar doesn't invest in defence Charms, even mortal humans can be dangerous to him. If he doesn't get the "I pown opponent class XYZ forever" Charms, then he can't dismiss opponent class XYZ... And even if he does get it, then he might not have enough XP to get the other Creation-shattering Charms. Or to extend his influence to protect all that he cares about while he's busy kung-fu fighting people elsewhere.
