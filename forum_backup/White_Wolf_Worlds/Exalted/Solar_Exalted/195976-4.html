                                                07-16-2014, 03:39 AM
                                                Originally posted by Coik View Post
                                                I'm not trying to say that artifacts that can do W can never be made. I'm trying to express that I don't want to see, six months to a year down the line, that the best way to build a Lunar is to make a Solar and take evocations for artifacts X, Y and Z. And why wouldn't you? Artifact Y has evocations that are better than Lunar shapeshifting, even if that wasn't the intent, and X and Z are almost as good; getting Solar mote pools and Charms out of it as well more than makes up for it.

                                                And yeah, I'm talking from a purely mechanical standpoint there.
                                                I think that that falls into the same category as "won't people just make an Exigent that is just like Lunars, but better?" Artifacts are meant to be unique. They might overlap with certain tricks various splats have, but their overarching direction is likely to be their own. You can make the Sword of the Sun, but it probably won't give you Solar Stealth. If an artifact is directly overlapping with the core of an Exalt type (rather than just overlapping with some of what they can do), that's probably not an artifact that most games would allow by default.

                                                Remember -- artifacts still have attunement costs. Therefore, if your plan is to attune to three powerful artifacts which, collectively, cover large portions of what's important to you from the Lunar charmset, you're probably going to end up with most of your mote pool locked up in them. If you swap between them, then you're not really a Lunar, in the same way that Alchemicals might have some individual charms that can compete with Solars at Solar stuff in certain areas, but can't equip them all at once -- that's already a clear way in which you're functioning distinctly from a regular Lunar. Not to mention you're also spending a bunch of background dots (or craft-efforts) that could have been spent elsewhere.

                                                Finally, artifacts, by definition, can be taken away from you. It's not easy, sure, but there's a fundamental difference between "I am the mighty shapeshifter, moon-blessed master of the wilds" and "I am a mighty Solar Exalt with the legendary blade that lets me assume ten-thousand shapes." It seems great until you run into someone with epic larceny or disarming charms or something and the next thing you know you're standing there empty-handed looking stupid and unable to shapeshift.



                                                With all that said, remember that Exalted are defined by what they cannot do as much as by what they can. Part of the reason to play a Lunar Exalt is because you like the Lunar story; and part of that story means not being a Solar. That means that worrying that a hypothetical Solar Exalt with a cloak of shapeshifting might, in certain ways and under certain circumstances, be better than you is silly -- if you care so much about Solar charms, play a Solar; if someone wants to play a shapeshifter who uses Solar charms, I don't feel that the game needs to kick them in the nads by telling them that artifacts and sorcery don't do that or by trying to keep those charms from 'overshadowing' Lunars.

                                                If someone is trying to play that character in a game where you're the one Lunar in a Solar group, hit them with a book. If the mere thought that that character might exist somewhere and might sometimes be better than you while having abilities that overlap with yours is intolerable to you, well, so what. I don't feel that they'll ever be a better way to play a Lunar, which is what really matters.

                                                (Remembering that as I said, relying on artifacts to shapeshift already has a significant built-in thematic limitation in that you, well, are reliant on those artifacts; and that has mechanical / balance implications. The story of the Solar with a daiklave that lets him take on ten-thousand shapes is cool, but it is not a Lunar story -- no Lunar has to worry about someone disarming or stealing their ability to shapeshift. If you want to play someone who has shapeshifting as the intrinsic, inseparable core of their being, you still have to play a Lunar, even before you get to the fact that Lunars probably have a different take on shapeshifting, probably have other additional themes, etc.)


                                                Also I'll step back and point out again that the issue here continues to be Lunars specifically (in that the books have failed to really make them distinct enough on their own) -- you wouldn't have the same problem with Sidereals, say, because even a Solar with the sword of prophecy in one hand, the Fate-Cutting Blade in the other, and mastery of SMA from a Sidereal tutor is still going to have a very very different storyline from a Sidereal Exalt due to the different background their Exaltation implies.
