                        In Honor of Mother's Day!

                        MITO, THE WANDERING LIONESS AND CUB

                        Mito's strident voice and inspiring charisma were so effective in raising resistance to the local Duke's oppressive policies (which took the life of her husband), even using her own home as a center to organize it, that the Unconquered Sun uplifted her into the Zenith Caste despite her late-term pregnancy. Her eventual delivery was thereby relatively quick and easy, and she carried her newborn babe close to her breast through the riotus streets, an iconic image that soon spread throughout the region as a symbol of heroic uprising.

                        Her work done, Mito decided to follow her calling by the Sun by protecting the rights of others throughout the provinces, but saw no reason to give up her blessed status of motherhood to do so. Now she carries her child throughout the land, helping the people she comes across to right wrongs done to them in the Sun's name, while fending off those who would claim her and her child's lives, either to stop her protests or simply because of her Chosen nature. It remains to be seen how well she will do once the Wyld Hunt comes calling...

                        Mito is a Zenith who excels in Performance (her Supernal Ability), especially Oratory, Presence and Socialize. She is quite good at Craft, which is good for making and mending clothes, and has constructed an innovative form of baby carriage, that appears normal, but is armored but quite lightweight. It can be quickly broken down in an instant, the carrying portion turned into a papoose, and the remaining parts becoming staves and wheel-based shields, which she wields expertly with Melee Charms.

                        Mito's anima banner is a proudly waving flag whose pattern constantly changes; her Defining Intimacies are towards Freedom and Equality (Principle) and her child (Tie); her Limit Trigger is when her work requires her to neglect her child, however temporarily.

                            MEGO, THE HOT-BLOODED SPORTSMAN

                            There is a confederation in the Threshold whose member nations spilled such massive amounts of blood in their history together, that they resolved to solve all future disputes amongst themselves as (relatively) bloodlessly as possible. They found their solution in a contact sport, where teams use strategy and martial skill to move a ball through zones on a field. Teams represent entire nations and political factions within each state, and the confederations' charter places strict sanctions on any member-states who fail to abide by the games' outcomes, however badly they may lose. This violent but honorable sport is called Fistball.

                            Mego was a legend among patrons, players and fanatical spectators. The Headman of his team, he rose quickly from his local league to the juniors, to the professional level, and was bidded and traded with lavish sums. While he wasn't the biggest, strongest or toughest player, nor the smartest strategist, his true strength lay in his enormous heart and driving passion, which inspired his fellow players to win, often against seemingly impossible odds.

                            One such game, after Mego's team had battled back from the jaws of certain defeat, hinged on his daring a nearly impossible shot. As he let fly with the ball, he heard an unbelievably heroic voice in his head telling him to "win one for the Sun!", and the ball flew with a precision and force clearly unnatural...Sadly, the game was called on account of Exaltation, and the referees conferred as to how to proceed.

                            This had happened before, but rarely, and always with Terrestrial Exaltation. The rules were clear that said games must be suspended and replayed, and the formerly-mortal players moved into "early retirement". Indeed, by this time, there were almost enough Outcaste players (or would-be players interested in the sport) to form their own league, if the Confederation weren't blocking those efforts! But none had ever exalted as a Celestial before, and although the member-states weren't Immaculate in faith, that propaganda reached far enough that they decided to play it safe and give Mego a more-than-generous severance package along with strong encouragement to seek his good fortune elsewhere.

                            Quickly divesting himself of his earnings to those much needier than himself, Mego set out with the barest minimum of belongings, wandering the wilderness of the Threshold in search of a new purpose. He found it in helping people plagued by all sorts of problems, from common bandits to the strangeness of the Wyld. He had always been proud of serving as a symbol and icon of his nation, but now he realized that he could help people in a much more direct way.

                            His latest adventure led him to a ruined castle-manse that all the villages nearby claimed held a terrifying apparition that regularly strode down the hill every full moon to lay waste to all he came across. Mego went there as the sun set, waiting for moon and ghost to appear. It did, wearing an elaborate golden and scarlet armor that it had possessed. Mego challenged it, exacting an oath from it that, if prevented from setting foot outside the manse throughout the night, it would give up its terrorizing ways; otherwise, he would willingly serve as its living vessel during the day.

                            With its assent, the contest began; the two grappled and wrestled throughout the waning hours of the night, smashing what little was left of the long-dead manse's structure, the powerful ghost-armor driving Mego closer and closer to the gates...but, pouring sweat and gasping for air, knowing the people depended on him, Mego held firm. As the sun dawned, the armor straightened and opened its helm to reveal a face Mego had constantly seen in his dreams, thanking him before melting away in the purifying rays of the morn.

                            Mego took the armor, his again with this cleansing, and donned it's eerily familiar weight. This encounter confirmed certain things for him: He now realized that what he'd been missing wasn't so much a purpose, as it was a team to share that purpose with. Inspiring others had always been when he'd felt most truly alive, after all! So now he sets out in search of others like himself...

                            Notes: Mego is a Zenith, whose Anima Banner is a cheering crowd, and whose Supernal Ability is Resistence, reflecting his ability to absorb the worst punishment possible and Never...Give...Up!! Athletics, Brawl, Dodge and War are well-developed in him, from playing Fistball his entire life and improvising daring tactics to win, but he really shines in Presence. He has a Defining Principle of "I always seize every opportunity to live life to the fullest!", a Major Principle of "I always play fair and to win!" and Minor Ties to Sports (Obsession) and Cheaters (Contempt).

                            Mego's sole Artifact is the Corona Carapace, an Artifact 4 suit of Orichalcum and Red Jade armor. Interestingly, as he becomes increasingly attuned to its awakening essence, he perceives it as having the demeanor of a relentlessly driving coach, always pressuring him to go onward and upward, especially when it comes to awakening its Evocations.
                            Interestingly, Mego own increasing prowess in Resistance has led to him manifesting his own spirit as armor, which he did for the first time at the climax of his duel with his previous incarnations' Po spirit...he's still unsure if he hallucinated this due to his extreme exhaustion, but in times to come he will refine this power still more. In time of his direst need, he will even learn how to enhance the Corona Carapace with his Glorious Solar Plate, wielding both sets of Evocations to devastating effect...

                            While reflecting on all the other N/PCs I've made and read about, it occurred to me that I'd never seen a Hot-Blooded Shounen Sports Star type before! This character owes more than a little to the main character of Akame Ga Kill (which is definitely NOT Akame!), and his evolving super-armor. The basic details came about while watching the Superbowl with my Dad, and maybe that would have been the best Sunday to post this, but I was otherwise busy. Hope you liked it!...Man, I really wanna make the rules for Fistball now...

                                    JUBILANT HARP

                                    Jubilant Harp grew up in one of the many petty kingdoms of the River Province. He showed an early talent for music, but his family would have preferred he focus on his chores. When a traveling show came to his village he ran away to it and earned his keep as a musician for the show. He threw himself into music with a passion and dedication that would have greatly surprised his parents.

                                    When he was grown to adulthood the traveling show came to a principality whose interpretation of the Immaculate Philosophy banned excesses such as music. While the traveling show moved through the principality quietly, Jubilant Harp stayed, unable to countenance a place that actually banned music. Finding some like minded youths, he trained them in music and went to the princess to argue his case. Their performance so moved the princess that she immediately lifted the ban on music.

                                    Emboldened by this success, Harp and his band made a living as traveling musicians, as he had inherited a wanderlust from the show he had joined as a youth. He played in the worst dives, and the grandest palaces. He participated in his share of bar brawls, and became addicted to LSD when he heard of it's purported creativity enhancing properties. His reputation and his fortunes grew, and soon he was giving command performances wherever he went. He has given concerts for the gods of Great Forks and for the General Staff of Lookshy, he even played for the Scarlet Empress herself a year before she disappeared. Through it all he never stopped playing for the poor and destitute, believing strongly that music was for everyone.

                                    His legend grew to reach even into hell itself. One day a few years after Empress had disappeared, his band was approached with an offer to give a command performance for the Yozi Malfeas, the King of Hell. He discussed it with his band, and eventually they decided to agree. They made the five day trek across the Endless Desert, and gave a concert to the Brass Dancer with all His souls in attendance.

                                    In the middle of this concert time seemed to slow, and a golden figure with for arms appeared before him, charging him with using his music to inspire the world to greatness. Then the figure looked at the Brass Dancer, and advised Jubilant Harp to play like his life depended on it, because it really really did.

                                    Pouring everything he had into the concert, Malfeas rose to dance, so pleased was He with the performance. Jubilant Harp gave an Encore at Malfeas' demand, then attempted to get gone before the Ruler of Hell remembered why he hated Solars. Yet Ligier had other plans, and intercepted the band before they could finish packing their essentials. He complimented Harp and his band, saying that Malfeas and all His Souls were well pleased with the performance, and that he would like to forge a proper instrument for Harp.

                                    Not really able to say no, Harp and his band stayed a little longer, and Ligier took Harp under his wing, making for him not just an Artifact Guitar, but also arms and armor fit for one of Harp's new status. He also taught Harp sorcery. Ligier's aim was to secure the young Solar as an ally, and in this he succeeded, Harp feels strongly indebted to the Fetich Soul of Malfeas.

                                    Nevertheless, after Ligier had finished forging Jubilant Harp's Panoply and he had learned of the Musical styles current in Hell, Harp ventured back into creation to fulfill the task given to him by the Unconquered Sun, to inspire the world to greatness with his music.


                                    Notes: Jubilant Harp is a Zenith with supernal Performance, his anima banner is rocknroll concert pyrotechnics. He has the Infernal Nobility merit for Liger's sponsorship of him, and his control spell is Invulnerable Skin of Bronze. He has 3 artifacts, a 4-dot Guitar, a 3-dot Chain Shirt, and a 3-dot set of Razor Claws. He has extensive contacts among musicians (3 dots), and speaks Old and High Realm in addition to his native Riverspeak. He has resources 2 as a phenomenally successful musician, and his 1 dot of followers represents his band.

                                                        KALVALA

                                                        The most fortunate of those born in mystic Ysyr are those with an active talent for sorcery, which guarantees them entry into its ruling class. But of those born into the rest of its society, perhaps the most fortunate are those whose Essence-created mutations give them a chance at a better life. Such was arguably the fate of Kalvala, an already pretty girl, whose feline appearance only added to her exotic appeal...at least, so claimed her parents, when they sold her to the Child-Hawkers.

                                                        The girl Kalvala persued her lessons with feverish abandon, allowing her tutors to shape her into the perfect pleasure slave, especially skilled at dance, story-telling, and pain-relieving massage. And so she caught the eye and coin of Sorcerer Umir, who brought her to his Tower Surreal. As masters went, he was decent enough, not punishing her mistakes harshly, waiting till she was of age to take her into his bed, and not asking anything too demanding of her therein.

                                                        In addition to her usual activities, Kalvala also entertained her master with her comedic antics; she was so good at this, that Umir began to bring her along to his meetings with other sorcerers, ostensibly to show off her beauty, but really so that she could afterwards regale him with exaggerated impressions of them. Little did Umir guess that she also performed the same function for the other slaves of his household, with himself as the subject matter!

                                                        To complete these mockeries, Kalvala even learned some of the terminology she'd overheard from her master and his fellows. In his amusement, Urim even broke the law of his land, by teaching her the secret cant of the rulers, so that she could better ape his rivals. Noticing her facility with these terms, he didn't stop there, and before she knew it, Kalvala had learned enough about the materials and functions of his sacred sanctum to assist her master during his rituals.

                                                        Finally, the hubris of Urim overreached his wisdom, and he called up that which he could not put down. Incensed by his temerity, the statuesque demoness danced through the air and performed a salterello over him that twisted his body and bones into a bloody puzzle. The horrified Kalvala knew that flight was pointless, and that her meager occult knowledge could not save her ersatz family from suffering the demon's wrath, and so she fell back on the only skill she possessed that might.

                                                        Recognizing even in the demon's most casual movements that of a master dancer, she moved before her with a sultry gait, and let her body deliver an unspoken challenge. Whether from ancient nightmare or mortal slave, the language of dance was universal, and the demon, anger lessened by amusement, took up the challenge. Into the night the two danced at each other, even though the outcome was never truly in doubt. As her breath grew short and her limbs heavy, Kalvala knew all was lost, but pinned all her hopes on one thrilling acrobatic display. As she landed in a dramatic pose, all were astouded by the golden light that burst forth from her, as was she by the empowering, encouraging voice within that told her: "You've GOT this, Kalvala. Now let's show this infernal bitch how we do things in Creation!"

                                                        Her opponent sized her up with shock and delight, seeing a truly worthy challenge suddenly appearing before her: it was ON.

                                                        Neither dancer held anything back, their movements, improvisations and combinations dazzling the slaves who fearfully watched, and who would never be able to express the wonder blazed into their brains for the rest of their lives. By the time the sun peeked over the horizon, both dancers came to a halt. Kalvala was drenched in sweat, her pounding feet bleeding all over the floor; a newborn Solar is an impressive thing, but even they have their limits, and both knew without speaking who had won the contest.

                                                        Still, the demon found herself impressed and fascinated. She finally spoke, offering the young Lawgiver a choice to grow with her help. Seeing little option, and curious despite herself, Kalvala agreed, and drew close for one last dance, an intimate tango that left her spinning alone. As she recouperated that day, she conferred with the chief steward of the household, who explained that once the other overlords of Ysyr learned of their master's death, they would weep openly, while proceeding to divine all of his belongings amongst themselves, including the slaves.

                                                        Not wishing to lose her family, and unsure of her status in Ys society due to her new condition, Kalvala hit upon a solution: Umir was one of those few eccentric sorcerers who had revived an out-of-date custom of always wearing heavy robes, guantlets and masks in public, to hide his physical perfection from unworthy eyes. If she wore his vestments, she could perfect her already-good impersonation of her former master, and keep the household together, at least until they could figure else something out...

                                                        That was a year ago. Kalvala has perfected her imposture of her master, whose increased reclusiveness isn't all that unusual for a master sorcerer of Ysyr. The slaves of the Tower Surreal have all accepted her as their new mistress, although she asks little of them beyond maintaining the place and keeping their shared secret. Most of her days and nights she spends in her sacred sanctum, honing her increased skills of both magic and dance.

                                                        She has no wish to keep up this deception forever. Indeed, Kalvala has come to realize that, in time, she can grow far more powerful than any sorcerer of Ysyr. She plans to wait until she attains this greater power, then publicly reveal her true identity and turn Ysyrian society on its head, as a mutant slave takes the long-vacant but undeniable position of Archmage...

Her Supernal Ability is Performance, and she has developed most of the Dancing and Voice Charms (she hasn't had much time or opportunity to learn the Sex Charms, but she is likely to be quite good at them!); she also has a good grasp of Presence, and Larceny helps her to flawlessly impersonate Urim. Occult is her most developed Favored Ability, giving her access to Terrestrial Circle Sorcery.

                                                        Kalvala's Control Spell is Stormwind Rider, and she has a Shaping Ritual that gives her sorcerous motes through dancing. Her pact with Stanewald gives her the Infernal Nobility Merit, and she hones her skills through dancing with a Gilmyne named Sattriya, who brings her gifts and advice from Stanewald each time she is summoned. Her impersonation of Urim has given her the Merits of Manse 3, (Tower Surreal), Resources 2, Influence 2 (Ysyr ruling class), Followers 1 (slaves of the Tower Surreal), as well as his sorcerous infrastructure, including many exotic components; her personal Merits include Mentor 2 (Stanewald), Unusual Hide (Fur), Tail and Cat Ears.

                                                        Her most developed combat ability is Dodge, which she supplements with Battle-Dancer Method. Despite the urgings of her bodyguard, Kalvala shows little interest in learning the arts of self-defense; after all, what are bodyguards for? However, she has recently come across something in her readings which has gained her interest, something called the Dreaming Pearl Courtesan...
