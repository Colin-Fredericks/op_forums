                                    Originally posted by Greyman View Post
                                    No. The concept is barely mentioned in passing. However, it is detailed in Miracles of the Solar Exalted.

                                    Exaltation unbinds from the soul upon death, and the divine spark flows back to the Unconquored Sun ready for him to choose a replacement.

                                    The exaltation remains touched by the legacy of its previous bearers and fragments of memories manifest as strange dreams, inexplicable emotions or waking dreams when triggered by locations or such. Also, artefacts, manse defenses, sorcerous workings, or various spirits and such might recognise the inheritor as though the were the same person.

                                    Basically. The idea to allow your character to have excuses for being drawn to artefacts or manses, stunt picking up on clues, or to form unexpected connections or inexplicable enemities with spirits (see Tale of Visiting Flare), and such without characterisation being taken over by the passed life.
