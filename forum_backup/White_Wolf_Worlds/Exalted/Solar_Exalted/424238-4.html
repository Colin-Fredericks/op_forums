                                Solars have TK when it implies mastery. It would unlikely be a function of any one particular Ability. Call the Blade, for instance, causes a weapon to fly to one's hand.

                                Athletics may, indeed, have the ability to lift something without the proper leverage to hold it together.

                                Larceny could allow lockpicking without having to reach inside the lock, or pickpocketing without having to actually touch the pocket-bearer.

                                Stealth may have loud, noisy objects move out of the way, and obscuring objects move to interpose themselves.

                                Presence could have dramatic environmental flourishes - doors flying open, dance partners being pulled in a slide into one's arms, sheer force of personality literally pressing against onlookers - accompany the absolute, well, Presence of the Solar.

                                Though this creeps into Abyssal themes, Resistance might allow telekinetic control of severed body parts, as the Exalt's will resists even disconnection.
