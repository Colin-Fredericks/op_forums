                                    Originally posted by Imrix View Post
                                    On the one hand, yay backer Charms.

                                    On the other hand... Power concerns aside, given that charm bloat was already understood to be a problem in 2e back when the Solar charmset was just over a third the size of the current one, I am mystified why you seem to consider Even Moar Charms to be a good thing.
                                    "Charm bloat" in 2e was not really about there being too manyCharms. Charm bloat was the perverse way in which, from a relatively small corebook, the Charmset of certain splats grew by addition after addition in every single supplement, inducing a constant power creep to mechanics that weren't stable to begin with. In the end, the power-set of Exalts was scattered around a dozen different books that veered wildly in nature, theme and power from their original basis, taking the game in bizarre directions and forcing you to constantly cross-reference everything. That was bloat. "Having a lot of Charms" is not bloat, and it's not inherently bad. Neither is "adding new Charms," depending on how you handle it.

                                    Bits and pieces of Exalted setting.
