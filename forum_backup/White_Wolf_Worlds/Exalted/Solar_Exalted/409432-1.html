    The thing about Abyssal 'redemption' is that is it not actually about good and evil. It's about changing from being Oblivion oriented to Creation oriented. It is possible to make a philosophical argument that the death and final obliteration of all things is not only inevitable, but desirable right now - it's just a grim argument and most people aren't going to be convinced because human beings aren't very good at viewing their own existences through a rationalist lens (and there are evolutionary imperative not to).

    Being an Abyssal, irrespective of any actions a character takes, means being aligned towards oblivion. Your very essence rebels against the essence of Creation - you are quite literally not a part of the living world anymore. Even if - to borrow the Reclamation plotline for a second - a circle of heroic Abyssals were to throw down the Ebon Dragon and save Creation from demon rule, they would be unable to claim that victory. Creation can never, ever welcome them. They cannot have a home among the living, or even truly among the dead.

    'Redemption' for Abyssals is about turning away from Oblivion and embracing living existence - returning to the state of a forceful expression of Creation, a Lawgiver whose very existence solidifies, supports, and expands the world out of formless chaos. Redemption doesn't mean becoming good again, it means returning to champion life over the end of all.

    Most of the examples we pick as actions along that path follow a traditional moral conception of 'good' while most pro-Oblivion actions follow a moral conception of 'evil' but this has to do with he origin of human moral schema - no one wants to be killed so killing is outlawed. Theoretically an abyssal could return to status as a champion of Creation through actions most observers would consider utterly horrible - serving as a champion of Ma-Ha-Suchi's cause for instance.

    In the inverse, an Abyssal can absolutely do 'good' in the eyes of most people and still be promoting Oblivion.

(as written by bad authors), and we get the endless "how to cure the tainted corruption of these!?!".         Hey, here's an idea: Abyssals that don't flip back to Solars. They transform into a third thing: the Chosen of Lethe. Glowing gold if you insist, but still inherently tied to the dead - but as protector and shepherd, not killer.

        Or heck, this is a good place to stick "the Chosen of ME!" - they get to rebuild themselves into a form they (in that moment) approve of, breaking the rules laid down by Sun and Oblivion alike.


        And moving the world *onwards* to something *different*, rather than rolling back the clock.

                                                Abyssal Redemption & Vampire the Masquerade:

                                                Vampire had a lot of "end of the world" worries.
                                                • First, each generation was weaker than the previous (think of it like Dragon-Blooded Breeding.... each generation, the purity diminishes).
                                                • Second, there was a prophesy about "the end times" being marked by "Thin-Blooded".
                                                  • Imagine if Abyssal Exalts heard a whisper that the world will end when Abyssal Half-Castes arrive. Then, in modern times, there are a bunch of Half-Castes.
                                                  • Of if we can use the Dragon-Blooded Breeding example, if they had Half-Castes, the Dragon-Blooded begin freaking the fuck out to see children born weird. Terrestrials with no anima banners. Or Terrestrials without a clear Aspect. Or Terrestrials who can't master anything above Essence 2 or 3.
                                                • Lastly, each Vampire bloodline had a founder - who was still intimately connected to their clan. They defined their clans, as the first of their lineages, and the Eldest of these lineages was tied to all their progeny.
                                                  • Again, in Exalted terms, imagine each Deathlord being the founder of their "Abyssal" clans -- anything you can do, they can do better. They are gods compared to you.
                                                  • Or for Dragon-Blooded, imagine each House founder was immortal and had those Dynasty keyword charms -- so they could wreak horrors upon and through their progeny.

                                                So imagine Vampires as a hybrid between Abyssals & Dragon-Blooded. All of the Abyssal powers and curses --- but they reproduce via blood, passing on a Breeding score like Dragon-Blooded.

                                                When the end of the world finally came?
                                                • In Exalted terms, imagine all the "Vampires" begin seeing their maximum Essence cap reduced. All Essence 10 were reduced to Essence 9. A few weeks later, 9 were reduced to 8. A few weeks more, 8 was reduced to 7. Then 7 to 6. This had many wonderful ramifications.
                                                  • First, the Essence 10 Vampires are reduced to Essence 9. The eldest & most powerful -- are also paranoid and isolated. They had no way of seeing this was a species-wide thing (since so few exist at the highest Essence cap), so they thought the curse was a personal thing. Being paranoid & disempowered, it eliminated the very "apocalypse" everyone feared because none of them would dare go apeshit with their powers, too afraid they were the only weak one (and that their enemies were waiting for them to show their faces, to finally one-up them).
                                                • Second, some discover that cannibalism restores their powers. Essentially, you either grew weaker -- or killed each other. Which is it? Are you prey or predator?
                                                  • Imagine if Abyssals found their Necromantic spells waning and had to cannibalize other Abyssals to restore their mastery over the dead.
                                                  • Or if Dragon-Blooded could temporarily improve their Breeding again by eating other Terrestrials.
                                                • Third, that part about the Thin-Blood? They may be weak - but they're still Vampires - so some Elders begin feeding on them. Others, seeing them as an omen of the end-times, simply murder them en masse whenever they find them.
                                                  • .....and that part I mentioned about every Elder having "Dynast" style charms? Now those paranoid Elders, who are losing power, also detect that their progeny are being mass-murdered. More paranoia. So they begin braving the world and feeding on their rivals (and their progeny) to even the score.

                                                All the while, this is all kept fairly discrete - for the same reason, in Exalted terms, that a bunch of Abyssals or Dragon-Blooded wouldn't want to go public (and let Sidereals & Solars discover their weakness).

                                                *
                                                *

                                                By the time this new "curse" reaches Essence 6 -- and Essence 6 becomes Essence 5 - now we're talking things accelerating to days, each transition coming faster and faster. This is where most characters fall.

                                                The Scenario for characters was written with them taking shelter in a church. Even if you're non-religious, the idea was that those prowling the night were preying on each other or falling weaker - but the church served as a sanctuary of sorts, so you all decide to hide indoors.

                                                So a bunch of Abyssals begin hiding in Solar Manses -- simply because it's a good hiding place. Inside, there are other Abyssals too -- all trying to avoid the chaos of what's happening outside.

                                                *
                                                *
                                                *

                                                Surviving (and "Winning") the scenario...... required doing nothing at all.

                                                *
                                                *
                                                *

                                                What the end-game scenario reminded us was that Vampirism was the curse -- and it had been weakening. Each generation was "weaker" than the previous because it was "less damned". The Thin-Blood are proof that it had reached a point that many of the strongest "curses" were relenting --- hence, some who could eat, others who could resist the sun, some get pregnant, etc. They were human.

                                                Did you spend the end-times cannibalizing your peers for power? You're a monster.
                                                Did you kill your rivals when they grew weak, for whatever reason? You're a monster.
                                                Did you (anything normally considered horrific), etc.....
                                                When the "curse" ends, you hit Essence 1 and your body aged to it's true-age, leaving piles of dust and bones in many cases.

                                                Characters who do no such thing...... hit Essence 1.....and are human once more. No curse. No damnation. People die every day - but you're given a new chance. You had power and some horrific curse - but if you cleaved to what made you human, despite it all, you return that way again.

                                                *
                                                *
                                                *
                                                *

                                                So yeah, Abyssals needing to run around like goth bish boys have a strong "aura" of Mary Sue when "Redemption" means turning into golden god-kings as opposed to.... I dunno..... simply wanting a second chance at life (which was cut short, in all their cases).

                                                Maybe if they weren't at the top of the power scale. Or maybe if they could somehow downgrade (an Abyssal shrugging off the curse - and Exaltation), yet maybe running around as a Heroic mortal or Half-Caste or something. I dunno.

                                                Maybe if they actually gave something up to prove their allegiance, rather than merely wanting to "swap for better charms".
