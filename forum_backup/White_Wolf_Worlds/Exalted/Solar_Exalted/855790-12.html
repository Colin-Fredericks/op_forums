        Like I said previously I'm not fond of the idea of designing a MA that a Solar can't excel at. Its like telling me you're going to design a logic test for dogs that chimps aren't able to excel at, while I may be surprised and find out that it exists, its just against the widely known paradigm.

                                                        The thing for me is that I expect Solars to be the best (or tied for best) with any given Ability, when taken as a whole. Now, I don't mind if some splat can surpass Solars in a specific niche within that ability. Maybe Alchemicals can mass craft identical mundane items better/faster than a solar or something.

                                                        So, why not be fine with DBs being better than Solars at 5 out of all Martial Arts? Well, there are two things going on here:

                                                        First, the system partially treating each MA as a separate Ability kind of leads me to consider that Solars should be able to do their thing at each of them. The Dragon Styles don't feel like niches within an Ability, but closer to 5 distinct Abilities to me.

                                                        Second, I wouldn't actually mind if the Terrestrials were better at it. The proposed idea for implementation is what feels wrong to me. If they worked better for DBs due to synergies with their Anima Banners, Aspect Powers, Native Charms or something like that, I'd be applauding it as great design. Instead, we get Solars unable to fully master the styles to the same extent they can with every other MA Ability, and that doesn't sit right with me.

                                                        Like, If the Solars could show off their unsurpassed skill by mastering the techniques better than DBs, like in every other MA, but DBs still did better when using it because they were made for DBs and thus take advantage of things only DBs have? I would actually really like that dynamic (as long as there are balance checks in place for the difficulty and resources spent to acquire them so that DBs picking any other MA don't feel like chumps)

                                                            The Solar excellency isn't that awe-inspiringly powerful any more. Lunars can easily match or even exceed it with their nifty stunt-excellency, Sidereals can exceed it pretty easily with rolled actions (though they lag behind a lot for static values), Liminals and Dragon-Blooded both start out weaker but end up with comparable or even superior exellencies by high essence values. An essence 5 DB who rolls 6 extra dice to begin with and then rerolls 5 failures is effectively adding 11 dice to their roll, which is better than what Solars can do. It costs a willpower and is obviously worse at lower essence values, so the Solar excellency is still obviously better overall, but it's not head and shoulders above what everyone else gets any more.
