                        Rather than putting it down to specific effects (and attempting to ignore the more base claims that radiate from this sort of ... Philosophy of entity I guess) I'd say the thematic Solar power are the effects that radiate out of their inherit authority. More than any of the other Exalted, the Solar are what they do, rather than being are who they are. The will to control thing they have sort of radiates out from this idea space. They conquer, they mold, their power increases to the degree it is challenged (esp in 3rd Edition). Glorious Solar Saber indicates this, as does Dogstar Ruminitions, or the channeling Buddha charm from Integrity; their charms shift the focus of the story to being centered around themselves and the (seemingly) inherit power they tune. I think the iconic nature of the Solar is that where other Exalted "flex" the power, the Solar Exalted must "grasp" it, they reach from the self towards the goal they pursue.

                        I acknowledge this is a flawed understanding, but it helps me reckon STing my exalted game and who the Solar are as a "group".

                            For me, if we were doing away with the assumption that the Solars are the baseline heroes who do anything you want and do it well, and we are repackaging them as something more specific along the lines of other Celestials, then I would focus on: Anti-demon and anti-undead attack and defense abilities, their "Arthurian" (for immediate want of a better term) themes and specifically leadership, charisma, trust and ruling powers (including diplomacy), and then achieving grand magic with specifically utopian and anti-monster and human heroism enhancing Solar themes (while being less exceptional at grand magic which is just as powerful but isn't specifically immediately that helpful for a role as kings of a new golden age for humanity, without some lateral thinking).

                            Rather than detail specifics, the most iconic powers should be found under anima powers, then for Charms, under War, Socialize, Presence, Performance, Integrity, Occult and probably more the attack abilities than dodge or resistance.

                            Kind of just think super-Paladin wizard kings, without the Arthurian knightly iconography, and think more grand than lawful good.

                            If we're looking to keep them broad and default heroes, then go under each ability and pick the tricks that seem the most memorable or which immediately give you the image of "I've definitely seen this in a film / book" before.

                                        In a lot of ways, Solars are normal protagonists, but with the volume cranked up. Solars do relatively little by charm volume that you can't already sort of do in many other systems, they just do it LOUDER.

                                        What little they have that is extremely specific to them seems to involve light-theming. Shooting sun lasers, shining with the light of the sun, holy fire, that kind of thing. So if you wannt to make something that evokes the feel of Solars in other games and settings, giving them mechanics to do the normal mechanical things in the system but BIGGER and BETTER, and also go around being all radiant as shit will get the feel across decently well with relatively small mechanical difficulty.

                                            Cool discussion! It's really interesting to read.

                                            However, I feel like there's some confusion on what I mean by "iconic effects". For example, there's little doubt that the Solar theme of excellence is an overarching one and that, for example, a melee-focused Solar fulfils that theme if her Charms allow her to be really good at swording things. However, to achieve that, there's still a few specific effects that Solars typically have at their disposal. For instance, in order to block an attack, you often don't specifically need a perfect defense. Having a high enough defense will, in most cases, serve just as good and therefore support the Solar excellence theme. However, if there was a Solar Charmset printed that didn't include Heavenly Guardian Defense, that would make them feel just a little different, right? No longer can a melee-focused character go into battle with their player knowing that, if they need to, they can turn away any attack. If the exclusion of an effect would change the the "feeling" of the Solars to the point where they start feeling different as a Host, that's when I'd call the effect iconic.
