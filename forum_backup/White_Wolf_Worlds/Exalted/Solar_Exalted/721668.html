    Solar XP

    10-23-2015, 11:56 PM
    Having run and played games for many years, I don't really like giving out XP for roleplaying (it makes the players who don't get it annoyed, and requires me to constantly be thinking about whether the players should be getting it for whatever they're doing, rather than enjoying the game).
    I also don't like, generally, giving players different amounts of XP (there are some exceptions: I'm willing to give out extra XP to support characters whose powers are all about buffing other players rather than being awesome at combat or socialising themselves, as it benefits the whole party, and I don't mind people with suboptimal builds getting more XP). A small amount doesn't matter, but I've played games where, say, two or three Qin characters had 70xp and two or three had 150, or even worse a Vamp Larp where new characters had 0xp and old characters had hundreds, and what tends to happen is the low XP characters die over and over again.

    So, I'm a bit wary of Solar XP. But on the other hand, from the way Holden was talking, it sounds like the balance of the game expects you to give out Solar XP: he mentioned that one of the important things with Martial Arts is that you get XP to spend on it as well as your Solar charms, hence the high cost of it (merit, buying each ability separately).
    This makes me think that not giving out Solar XP would be problematic, and make Martial Artists (and sorcerers I suppose) perhaps weaker compared to characters who are focused on normal charms.

    So, my first thought of a solution is to give out 5 or 10 Solar XP at the end of each story as a story reward, and just give out 5 regular XP per session.

    But would that be enough? Due to its nature, I can't really tell how much Solar XP it's expected the characters will earn. Maybe about 2 a session, -ish?
    (Also, I don't imagine this is an issue that would become apparent one way or the other for about a year or so, at which point it might be awkward to change)

    This also applies to making high XP characters.
    The game I play will hopefully convert to 3rd ed (the ST said he wanted to read the book before deciding, and I doubt he's had time yet), and we have more than 700xp. Obviously with free essence and excellencies that translates into less 3rd ed XP, but we'd still be effectively 500xp characters, or something like that.
    If he wants to give us the same amount of XP as he did before, would us having, say, 500 regular XP (rather than 400 regular XP and 100 Solar XP or something) be unbalanced?
    On the other hand, if he wants to keep us at our current Essence of 4 (there's an argument that converting characters should have 1 less essence, as Essence 1 is the new Essence 2, but they probably shouldn't have more Essence), would giving us 200xp and 300 gold XP work or be horribly unbalanced?

    What do you all think? Obviously I'm most interested in the thoughts of the developers, but I'm interested in anyone's opinion.

    STing Bronze Age Exalted

          Solar XP is basically free. If you take two minutes to talk about one of your major or defining intimacies, you get 2. If you ask one of the other players to do something in line with their Caste role, you get 2. Basically, the only way you'll miss out on solar XP is if the circle is split up and people can't get in contact with each other and even then, you'll still get the Expression bonus.

                                      I would start by giving out Solar xp liberally. At end if every session, ask the players to justify why they should get the xp. Accept any reasonable justification. Accept any funny or smart one. Accept anything that the player did that made playing the game more fun for everyone. Most players should get all the bonus xp each session. This incentivizes player involvement and reflection on the game. It's also a great way for players to boast and let off steam. He'll, share on last beer at the table and give xp for talking about the awesome session you just had.

                                      But let the players know that this is a trial run. You might find that the power level increases too quick for you. That's fine. If you think that us happening then let them know, talk to them and hear their thoughts, and then stop the award for a while to let the campaign catch up to the Pcs. You can always go back to giving it out again if you choose.

                                      You could also slow down the award to half the max value. I don't recommend slowing to a variable amount because the competition can become an issue. Instead, expect to give out all, half, or none of the xp to players that provide a justification for it.

                                      Also, if someone is struggling to come up a cool thing to justify the xp award, let the other players step up and suggest it. Make it a team effort to show that everyone was in it together.

                                      Giving out solar xp is also a good way to deal with age old question if what xp to give to folk who miss the game. Give then the regular basic award and the lose out on the solar xp.
