                                                    Originally posted by Murcushio View Post
                                                    This isn't true. There are plenty of beings of immense power handing down diktats from on high in Exalted that are used as a justification for various moral codes. The Unconquered Sun does that! So does Malfeas.
                                                    The distinction I make here is that those beings are not omnipotent [only exceedingly powerful], not omniscient, and not considered infallible. Yes, a god might give a dictate, but I don't accumulate "evil points" for rejecting that dictate that eventually "forces me to change my alignment". I don't have an alignment per se, and no amount of being good to the poor and downtrodden or, contrarily, murdering orphans to bathe in their blood has metaphysical ramifications such as 'you are now more vulnerable to specific classes of magical power', ala being Evil alignment and suffering extra effects from a Holy Word spell or whatever.

                                                    Now, there are probably ramifications of other sorts, such as people objecting to my murdering of orphans and aiming to stop me by force, but this is not the impact of an Omniscient Alignment, this is the work of a mortal will taking umbrage at my actions and attempting to stop them.

                                                    The important thing relevant to this is that there's nothing inherently wrong with fostering a cult; a given person may find it offensive that another is worshipped for their deeds and receives power as a result, but that is entirely that given person's opinion, not a reflection of some universal principle. Which is a lot of words to say "Is the conflict you're brewing really that important, and maybe it would have been good to present your character's opinion on cult worship before the game started rather than springing it now and causing trouble?"

                                                            Originally posted by Murcushio View Post
                                                            Worth noting that in both previous editions this wasn't entirely true; you could do things or undergo state changes that would get your classed as a Creature of Darkness, which did in fact render you increasingly vulnerable to certain kinds of magic. And that this effect operated omnisciently.
                                                            Creature of Darkness isn't really much like alignment though. Your alignment in D&D is determined by your actions. If you do evil things you get an evil alignment. If you do good things you get a good alignment.

                                                            The Creature of Darkness tag is something you get slapped with regardless of your actions. For example, all ghosts are Creatures of Darkness, even if they're really nice and generous ghosts. Sure it's theoretically possible that some nice ghost might be able to get the Unconquered Sun to make an exception for her if she somehow gets his attention, but it's safe to say most nice ghosts get stuck as Creatures of Darkness. Same with demons and most other "Creatures of Darkness." The specific actions don't matter, all that matters is that they were born (or became) a member of a type of race the Unconquered Sun hates.

                                                            So to continue the D&D comparison, the Creature of Darkness tag would be a bit more similar to a racial tag in D&D than an alignment tag. Imagine a god in D&D who grants his followers Bane Blessing that let them deal +2d6 damage vs goblinoids. The tag is determined by what you are rather than what you do - if you're born as a goblin, you're going to get smote, regardless of whether you are a good or evil goblin. Yes, there might be certain actions you can perform to overcome this such as using a permanent polymorph spell or reincarnating into something that isn't a goblin, but for the most part your own actions have no baring on whether you are considered a goblin (or Creature of Darkness in Exalted).
