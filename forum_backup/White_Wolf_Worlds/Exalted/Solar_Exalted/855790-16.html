                Originally posted by Zeesun View Post
                I'd be curious to hear your thoughts on this.
                I'd also like to hear Zelbinnean's thoughts, but I can tell you why I agree.

                Storytellers and players will always try to find a natural equilibrium in gameplay. Characters who fight will find fights, crafters will find things to craft, diplomats will find diplomatic crises to resolve. This is the cornerstone of an enjoyable game for everyone involved. Sure, the storyteller will throw a curveball now and then, but fundamentally if you are going to run a group that has written up illiterate Dawn combat monsters through a courtly intrigue nobody is going to have a good time.

                With this equilibrium in mind, the threat that the Anathema are hunted and face an omnipresent military force chasing them down wherever they go looks less like a problem and more like a feature from a game perspective. A Dragon-Blood warrior and a Solar warrior are going to spend roughly the same amount of their stories threatened with deadly force, because that's just how games work out.

                            Originally posted by danelsan View Post
                            OK, here is an exercise.

                            Your MA-focused Solar spent multiple stories challenging the 3rd Circle Demon Sifu, Suntarankal, the Crucible of Brass and Iron, passing every grueling test and has finally earned the right to learn the Demon Prince's secret style.

                            Would you be ok with this style being inferior for the Solar than other styles, because it lacks Mastery effects due to being made by a demon, for himself, and developed by only himself and for himself for four thousands years?
                            Depends.

                            Look above to my last post. Note how the Lunar Exalted made two different canonical Styles. Note that while both creators likely made these Styles for themselves, they did not choose to create Styles centered around things exclusively natural to Lunar Exaltation. So they incidentally created Styles where Solar Exalted would just outright perform better than them.

                            Suntarankal might create a boxing or kicking-focused Style, just because that is a thing he wanted to do. Since nothing about punching and kicking is less natural to humans than to demons, these Styles would have Mastery-level effects for Solars.*

                            But if Suntarankal developed something like Throne Shadow Style -- except instead of working through Shadow Fingers, the Stylist works through his Second Circle Souls -- then Solar Exalted are out of luck. Because Exalted don't HAVE Second Circle Souls.

                            Why should a Solar player regret not being the best with a Martial Art Style designed by a Third Circle Demons for the express purpose of weaponizing one of the unique features of being a Third Circle Demon?


                            *Suntarankal also wouldn't enjoy Mastery effects for these Charms as Mara does for her Style, because he's not designing these Styles to reflect his demonic essence. (He MIGHT enjoy Mastery effects simply because he's a living embodiment of Martial Arts mastery, though.)
