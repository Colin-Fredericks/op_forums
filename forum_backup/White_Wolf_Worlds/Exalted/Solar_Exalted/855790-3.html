        Originally posted by Kell_Tamer View Post
        This also needs to go. "Oh you cute little not actually exalts... here you can't be trusted to break your limitations on your own.... we'll do it for you..."
        So, that's what you think Immaculate Martial Arts should be? An inherent limitation-breaker? That's... not really in line with the Martial Arts system presented in Third Edition as I understand it, and a huge change to the setting.

        The Martial Arts system allows an Exalted of any type to express their Essence in similar ways, representative of their philosophy and their training instead of their nature as a Lunar, Abyssal, Dragon-Blooded, or whatever. The Mastery and Terrestrial keywords exist as they do to try to balance Martial Arts Charms against the user's native Charms. This means that the Terrestrial keyword and inability to use the Mastery keyword is inherently limiting to the Dragon-Blooded under the existing system.

        Turning the Immaculate Martial Arts into a limit-breaker for Dragon-Blooded that other Exalted can't use as well as they can... that's not the Martial Arts system. That's just simply not what that system does. That's some kind of native Charm, and not really inherently affiliated with the Immaculate Philosophy.

        If it's a Martial Art, then I don't particularly see why it's a terrible thing if it is taught to them by the setting's foremost martial arts experts.

        Because of the way the system is structured, the Immaculate styles as used by the Dragon-Blooded need to be balanced in some form against Terrestrial Charms. Similarly, the way that the Immaculate styles are used by Solars needs to be balanced against Solar Charms. It's not about who is the Bigger Protagonist with the Bigger Dick. It's about how the system and the setting work. For all the complaining, I'm not really seeing a mechanically balanced, setting consistent way around this being proposed. Instead, there's accusations of juvenile power fantasy, phrased in terms of Whose Dick Is Bigger. Real mature.
