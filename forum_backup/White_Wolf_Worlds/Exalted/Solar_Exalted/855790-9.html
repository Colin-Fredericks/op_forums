                    I don't think you're likely to see Immaculate styles balanced by XP / chargen taxes (of the "take these pointless prerequisites purely to be able to access Immaculate MA" or "you must build your character in exactly this way to access Immaculate MA" style.) There's already the Martial Arts merit and the individual abilities for each Martial Art; if you take it too much further, you end up with anyone who wants to start as an Immaculate having like one charm in their style and sucking at everything else. (This was a problem in earlier versions which, IIRC, they want to fix.)

                    At the end of the day the balance-point for the Immaculate styles should be "how can this be as fun as possible for everyone who wants to tell stories with it?" And the best way to do that is to make it clearly "Dragon-Blooded" in character, somehow, while still leaving it open as an option for other people without making it pants-on-head idiotic for them; and to aim for a balance point where playing an Immaculate doesn't require sacrificing most of the rest of your character sheet on some sort of grim altar to fun.

                    I mean, Immaculate MA shouldn't be a dominating option for DBs; but it's a big deal for them conceptually, so it should be a strong option, and one that is intrinsically appealing to players (just not so appealing that it encourages them to completely ignore Melee.) Excessive XP / chargen taxes aren't a good idea for something that, in a typical DB circle, you'd expect at least one or two people to take.

                    (The other problem with using high XP charges to balance stuff is that in my experience it rarely works. If you made Immaculate MA much more powerful than Melee but gated it behind some sort of high XP investment, what you'd see is people either never taking it at all or gritting their teeth and plowing through it and not really enjoying it, which is bad for everyone involved.)
