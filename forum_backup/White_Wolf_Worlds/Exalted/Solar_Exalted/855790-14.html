                                            Solars are already best at the styles as skill can do. The thing is, that is not something that they do better than DBs. DBs also do the styles as good as anyone can do.

                                            What bonuses they get in addition probably won't look like Mastery effects. And likely play on the fact that DBs you know, can as noted, do thing slike set themselves on fire.

                                            I think a thing to remember is that to an extent, Martial Arts aren't fighting by channeling any Essence in particular. THey're fighting like something. Tiger stylists fight like tigers. Ebon Shadow stylists fight as shadows. And a Fire Dragon stylist fights like the Fire Dragon. The base Ability is emulating that. Magic is emulating techniques of that Abiltiy, more orless. And because of it being Exalted, knowing a skill well will often let you know some weird shit when you get deep into it. Like how fighting as a shadow lets you teleport as a shadow.

                                            This is a skill. Solars are going to be good at that skill. It is just that when you fight like the Fire Dragon, you probably aren't going to do it with some extra bit of mastery of technique that an actual Chosen of the Fire Dragon is going to have. You're going to be still top at that skill. It just hs a different ceiling. I mean, note that Mara fights as good as you do at her style. Because, well, it's not fighting as Mara. It's fighting like her.
