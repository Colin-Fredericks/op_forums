    Originally posted by light-hero View Post
    Is actually the Lore charms I often find myself to want for Occult charms
    Some of them, yeah. The purview of Lore as an ability is barely related to Lore charms. Looking over Solars, I guess charms for teaching, shaping, and mote transfer have value, but it's a shame we don't get charms for *knowing actual lore*. Getting back to Occult, I could see Lore's mote transfer and shaping stuff fitting in better with an ability that deals with magic as a whole and the Fair Folk. Beyond that, 2e's spirit wrangling is a legit use of Occult, though there could be something social to complement all the violent charms. Necromancy needs to have a role, but Solar Occultists should be able to do more than punch ghosts without bringing in other charm sets. And rereading the uses of Occult, I'm struck by the emphasis on knowledge and spirit etiquette and so on that is lacking in the 2e charm trees. In general, I hope 3e brings us more knowledge-y and finesse-ish charms instead of just ways to nuke stuff.

            Mortal Occultist: I know about other realms, at least heard about them and know what may live there.
            Solar Occultist: I am comfortable with other realms and can explore them at my leisure with some preparations available to me.

            Mortal Occultist: I know that the world is not as it seems, that there is a deeper truth to it that I can gain an advantage on and use this for my own purposes.
            Solar Occultist: I can catch the mysteries as they flit by, trap the ineffable and pluck the threads of the loom itself if I can silence its guardians for a while.

            Mortal Occultist: When danger arises, I consult my knowledge and ward against dangers seen and unseen. I can aid my people and help preserve us through the dangers to safety.
            Solar Occultist: I can spot the approaching danger and counter it, stymie it, confound it, lead it astray into my own trap and thwart it until I can bargain for safe passage and future guarantees of neutrality.

            That's sort of what I'm hoping for.

                I've apparently got a fairly different understanding of Lore/Occult than the other people on this thread.

                The way I see the divide between them, past the obvious "Normal Science vs Magic Science" divide, I see Lore as fundamentally being about understanding the rules of how Creation works and Occult being about understanding the rules of how Essence works with a second theme of breaking the rules everyone else plays by.

                With Lore, you've got the charms to teach and educate, but you've also got Charms to move Essence and Willpower around from yourself and into others(knowing how those things move and operate in different vessels), you can assert Creation's laws anywhere, and you have such a deep understanding of how Creation works and how it is put together that given the proper resources(the Wyld) you can make more of it.

                Occult, though, you've got Charms that let you set up a field of Essence that repels the essence of Dematerialized spirits, and you got methods of reaching across the Chasm of the Material to bitchslap them if they get uppity, to detect Essence flows and to analyze them, and to smash apart active workings of Essence. But you've also got Charms that let you kill beings that cannot die, that let you tear power and knowledge that you shouldn't be able to access out of the world, to summon a powerful demon even during times when it should be impossible to summon them, and that you twist up Holy until it would count whoever you happen to be pissed at that day as a Creature of Darkness.

                    Hmm, I'm not sure we should infer the true meaning of Abilities from their Charms. I'd rather see Charms that amplify what the Abilities do. It's counter-intuitive to read an Ability description, decide that your character should or shouldn't be good at that, and then have to separately check out Charms to make sure the stuff you care about is in the same place.

                    Here's most of the 2e Lore write up:
                    Characters with the Lore Ability have some knowledge of current events, history, geography and the customs of other lands, as well as the magical secrets of First Age technology....
                    Trait Effects: Someone with Lore 1 is literate and can find most large nations on a map of Creation. Someone with Lore 3 knows details of the customs of lands thousands of miles away and can help operate, maintain and service many First Age devices. Someone with Lore 5 knows details of the history of the Shogunate as well as the truth about the mysterious Dragon Kings. In addition, she can maintain and repair the most complex First Age devices, including warstriders.
                    Very little of that is reflected in the Charm set. All the customs/etiquette stuff gets eaten by social Charm sets and the repair stuff ends up with Craft. Maybe knowledge of history and current events is too hard to translate into worthwhile charms? Still, I don't get mote transfer or fighting the Wyld as elements of Lore. Now here's Occult:
                    This Ability represents a character’s knowledge of and familiarity with all forms of magic, ranging from the Charms of the Exalted to the minor but ubiquitous workings of mortal thaumaturgy. In addition, it also covers familiarity with all manner of supernatural creatures, including ghosts, gods, elementals and the Fair Folk. Characters who are skilled in Occult know both the capabilities of such creatures and how to petition or appease them.
                    Again, I think charms should be amplifying what we see here, not going in a new direction.

                        I'd like a Solar occultist to be like a luminary geomancer, great exorcist, or Taoist/Buddhist sage like from Japanese and Chinese folklore. The sort of person the Emperor calls in when elementals keep manifesting at unusually high rates in the nation's capitol, or when a demon has possessed his firstborn child and none of the lesser spiritualists seem able to dislodge it. The sort of person that Pharaoh calls to interpret his strange dreams!

                        Heaven and Earth should move in harmony with a Solar occultist, and the hidden currents of the world should be apparent to them. Solar occultists should be able to expel spirits possessing/haunting a person, object, or location with the power of their concentrated holiness, whether through masterful and efficacious ritual at lower levels or simply with a word shouted like thunder at higher levels. A Solar occultist should be able to, when the Devil appears to tempt her, take it by the nose with her furnace tongs and hammer it silly, then lock it in a (fragile, easily opened) box for a few centuries.

                        Occult-focused Solars should have a refined understanding of demesnes, manses, and dragon lines, not merely the currents of the Terrestrial energies but the strange death-haunted vales or the places holy to the Sun, Moon, and Stars; they should be able to use this understanding to make recommendations about how to effectively cope with or modify an area's geomancy - landscaping and architectural changes, for example, or ritual festivals that vent accumulated Essence-pressure, or changing governmental policies that grate on the sensibilities of the spirit world.

                        Correspondingly, communication and interaction with spirits is obviously a must, although spirit-communication Charms might also be available through Performance, Linguistics, Awareness, and so on. This could be expanded even beyond the simple "sense their presence/perceive them/touch them" progression of 2E - a Solar occultist might take a Charm that allows spirits to pass them messages in their dreams, even if the spirit couldn't normally speak without manifesting or send dreams to people. If there's any space in Solar themes for them to get prophetic or clairvoyant dreams or visions, Occult is where I'd want to put it; by the same token, Solars attuned to the subtle currents of Creation should be able to sense the approach or attention of powerful entities or forces, such as typhoons or a Dusk Caste.

                        A Charm or Charms to resist possession and/or illusions seems like a must, although that might better belong in Integrity.

                        That's my two cents, anyway ^^;. Playing characters like that (who are pretty common in media) wasn't really viable in 2E, sadly, and it seems like something that could enrich a game. Obviously, taking Occult isn't going to make you an omnicompetent toolbox just because there's spirits involved - being able to touch them won't do you a lot of good if you don't have the fighty Charms to wrestle with them, nor will being able to speak to them do you much good if you don't have the social mojo to bargain with them. But I think there's some space that other Abilities don't already fill in which Occult could have some fun, flavorful, and useful effects.

                            I'd also think it was cool if a Solar occultist could, sort of drawing on how the Sun's Exigence empowers lesser gods to select Chosen, send a surge of Essence to an object's least god, temporarily animating the object as a loyal tsukumogami for use as a general-purpose minion. I have an odd predilection for inanimate-objects-minion-commander characters - as you can imagine, I really enjoyed Shaman King. That's far from central to the Solar Occult concept I've espoused above, though, and more a matter of individual preference.

                            Drawing further inspiration from Shaman King: there might exist a Solar Occult Charm or tree of Charms that draws on the legacy of the Dragon King olchilike, warriors possessed by the spirits of the gods to serve as champions, that allows a Solar and a spirit riding the Solar to work together at greater efficiency than the spirit's own Charms would otherwise allow. It could be counterbalanced by making the Solar a more attractive target for unwilling possession, of course... Similar effects might grant the Solar additional benefit from being blessed by a spirit or acting as a champion on behalf of a spirit or group of spirits, mechanics obviously subject to whatever form such blessings end up taking. That would tie in neatly with the Usurpation, too - while the Solars ruled with the favor of Heaven and Earth and acted to preserve the good order of the world, they were invincible, but when the spirits withdrew their favor in fear of their might or displeasure at their hubris, the bright kings fell to the Terrestrials' blades.

                            Sort of like Craft and the social Abilities, I feel that Occult will be most compelling if 3E gives it a strong mechanical foundation to interact with, as the combat Abilities have historically benefited from. Fun, flavorful, and well-designed thaumaturgy (but not too crunchy and fiddly, because that way lies broken combos and system bloat) will work wonders, and take a lot of the weight of bearing the entire conceptual load of the exorcist-spiritualist-geomancer-psychic spectrum off of Occult Charms for all splats. If spirit mechanics can be designed so that they integrate well with those aspects of the thaumaturgy system that mess with spirits, even better! Although I believe the devs have stated that they were moving away from a unified toolbox-style "spirit Charmset", so that may be a less daunting task than it appears if each spirit can be tailored as appropriate.

                            An Occult-focused Martial Arts Style based around "blasto wizard"-style Essence manipulation, or around the sort of feats attributed to Buddhist and Daoist sages, could be cool, if done properly. Mortal Techniques might let you do things like boil water with your qi or knock objects over from across a room without touching them; with active Essence you could graduate to things like energy shields, levitation, tagging people or an area with sourceless phosphorescence that makes sneaking about unseen difficult, or shooting blasts of energy from your palms or eyes or nose or something like that. That all might be more appropriate to render as She Who Lives In Her Name native charms, though, or not be flavor-appropriate for the setting at all.

                            If only there was a way to make multiple Charms or abilities that gave you an energy blast not redundant, or somehow work with each other harmoniously! :T

                            N.B. I'm less excited about/committed to all of these ideas than I am to the ones in my previous post, so if (like Kukla) you have endorsed said post please do not feel that your words are being stretched to cover this one as well. This is moving more into the realm of "what I think would be cool" than it is "what would definitely work for everyone."

                                        I am a student of philosophy and I can say one thing- Logic is a strange creature. The knowledge about the rules themselves is pretty straightforward, but curiously knowing the rules is not enough, it requires skillful application of them to solve complex problems and establish a really sound reasoning. I say it not without a certain disdain, but it requires certain talent, skill that can be aquired with time, but which isn't inherent with knowing the rules.

                                        That's how I'd like to see Occult realised- as a skillful application of knowledge you have about the rules underlying the World. Just knowing the rules themselves can be a number a different abilites, including but not limited to: Lore, Medicine, Craft, Socialize and so on. But Occult is the way you're applying rules that you know- how to apply specific rites of purifaction, how to write a specific prayer, how to forsee the next Lunar/Solar eclipse, how to set an apropriate date for a ritual and so on and so on.

                                        And so I'd like to see Solar charms expand on it- allowing for fast and accurate calculations about astronomical events, hastening the process of finding the natural veins of Essence, making alchemy and desigining rituals, creating wards. Of course that's on top of what's already there- being able to phsycially interact with spirits and seeing patterns of Essence.
