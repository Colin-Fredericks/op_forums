                Originally posted by John Mørke View Post
                Any requests?

                Please try not to ask for things that are already in the 2e set.
                John,

                One of the major issues I had with 2e medicine was the distinction between MENTAL and PHYSICAL health.

                Exalted needs to kinda take a stance on an issue we haven't figured out entirely in the modern day, which is to say... how much does biology play into things like madness?

                Can you cure a derangement with medicine?

                If not, how do you cure a derangement? Via presence and Freudian analysis?

                Both methods make powerful arguments about the way Exalted treats neurosis and, well, people in general.

                In 1e (and 2e) the only real methods of interacting with mental issues that I recall are in the Dragonblooded charmset, which always struck me as very weird.

                It'd be nice to have some sort of system for quantifying mental strain / madness and methods of interacting with that mechanic that were more defined and direct.

                Thanks!

                                    Originally posted by Lurker View Post

                                    That's another option, certainly.

                                    My point is that Exalted has to decide if madness...

                                    A) ... is a biological issue caused by improper physical condition. This is actually how modern science more-or-less understand these things.
                                    B) ... is an emotional condition that cannot be treated physically, only through contemplative growth, healing and self-exploration... (the 'therapist' approach)...
                                    C) ... the consequence of evil spirits invading the body that must be removed (probably the most authentic).

                                    I don't really have a severe preference here just, we need to get off the fence about it, because mental illness DOES happen in games.
