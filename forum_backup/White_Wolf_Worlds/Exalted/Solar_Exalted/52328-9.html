            For the record, I agree with all JimB has said about this.

            I actually want all types of performances to have the same end result in most cases. I mean, sure, you generally use acting rather than dance to imitate someone (to go along with that disguise from Larceny, for instance)...Unless you are imitating the mannerisms and style of another dancer. So the Charm that helps in this should work with both, even if in most cases acting is gonna be more appropriate - the rest is stunts.

            I just have a hard time seeing what a method could accomplish that another couldn't in most cases. If I want to make a village distrustful of a neighboring village in preparation to get them into comflict, one might do it through song or a speech. But why not with a dance? Why not with a theatrical performance with no words? Isn't that the sort of thing a mythologically good, say, dancer, should be able to do?

            I'm sure there are some edge cases, though none come to mind right now, in which a specific kind of performance should be the only one to be able to achieve a given effect. I would be pretty happy if these are left to custom Charms, possibly with a couple in the book to serve as examples.


            I should clarify, though, that this is not something I care all that much about. I prefer it being the way I mention, but as long as the execution is well done, I'll be happy either way.

                            I'm of a mind with Hatewheel that prayer and religious ritual is too deeply rooted in society and culture for Charms that enhance it to just be a set of generic bonuses.

                            That said, some prayer Charms would be interesting! I could definitely see room for a prayer Charm that ensured that a Solar's heartfelt entreaty (and it has to be heartfelt!!) will stand out from the background mumble and place itself prominently in the foreground of the spirit's mind.

                            As far as what I want from Performance generally:
                            - Respect-Commanding Attitude, in some form or another.
                            - Possibly building off of that, I'd like my performances to endear me to people - witness Conan's reluctance to kill Rinaldo, even when Rinaldo directly and openly agitated against him. A Solar's audience, just like the audience of any master, can easily become so besotted with a performer's craft that they will forgive the performer themself a great deal.
                            - This could also easily branch into Orpheus-style "music soothes the savage beast" effects - I just really want my Solar musician to be so talented that even wild beasts will calmly listen to her play and not harm her.
                            - Even departing from the strict "your performances endear you to people" thing, you could move more generally into effects that make the Solar a larger-than-life showperson whose flashy moves and inimitable charm mean you can't help but grin. A Dawn with some Performance could be the epitome of the charismatic, show-off, crowd-pleasing superhero with legions of adoring fans. It'd be sort of like Black Claw Style in the way that even your battles, or your ordinary conversations, can be performances that will sway an audience.
                            - I'd like my music, or speech, or whatever, to penetrate my audience's consciousness even through adversity and distraction. If there are ten thousand rebel soldiers who haven't eaten well for a week or two, haven't bathed in a month, and have been camped out in the wilderness, they might not be in the mood to listen to a rousing rendition of their movement's anthem - but if a Performance-focused Solar is singing, it should still put a fire in their hearts.
                            - "Command of inanimate objects through the power of your music" may be a little weird for a Solar effect - but I like the idea of the trees and rocks turning to listen as a Solar plays, or of the least gods of furniture and tools and dishes being woken to action by a Solar's house-cleaning song. Go full Disney Princess on this.
                            - Maybe an effect, related to or an upgrade of Respect-Commanding Attitude, that makes a Solar's performances very noticeable. Even if you're distracted, or your mind is on something else, the distant sounds of a Solar orator's speech will draw you out of your house and wandering through town looking for it. At higher levels, even immaterial spirits may pause in their duties and listen, enchanted by your skill. A really skilled Solar musician might be able to still a sandstorm with a performance, the storm-spirits quieting their own winds so that they might better hear the song - and to Hell with the tongue-lashing they'll catch from their superiors for it!
                            - An effect to make my voice, or music, or whatever, carry further than it might be expected to, and be less diminished by background noise or distance than it otherwise would be. As a possible upgrade, maybe something that guarantees that people the Solar is close to will hear the Solar's music - the circle takes heart from the faint songs of one of their members, even as they are all confined to separate cells in the Deathlord's vast fortress.
                            - Solar Performance should set fire to the spirits of its audience! I don't know precisely how that would be represented, but I'd definitely enjoy some kind of effect that allows people to draw strength/determination/comfort from the Solar's performances (or the memories thereof) in times of doubt or hardship. Obviously, that sounds like it would mostly be used for good, but I will note that a Solar dictator could use it to harden the hearts of their secret police just as easily as another Solar could encourage dispossessed refugees not to despair and lose hope.

                                                        Originally posted by John Mørke View Post
                                                        Some of you are mentioning prayer Charms. What do you imagine prayer doing?
                                                        I rather want prayer to work in one of two ways. And for both of these two ways, I want it accessible to mortal priests too, if only in a lesser form. I want mortal prayer to be the low form of exalted prayer, like thaumaturgy is to sorcery in 2e, or like mundane martial arts systems are to supernatural martial arts.

                                                        1. The force of true faith. The priest turning back a vampire by brandishing a cross and reciting the lord's prayer with such force of personality and potent faith that the creature of darkness can't help but retreat. Holy effects that protect people praying or repel hostility from approaching people praying. I want my prayers to be able to invoke such fundamental faith and holiness that a deity doesn't even have to answer the prayer for creatures of darkness to flee or be unable to enter my temple for the prayers resonating among its halls.

                                                        I want true faith to be a Holy force that can be harnessed with prayer. Hell, I want stories of mortal priests able to hold off hungry ghosts from their temple with impassioned prayer, with Solar Performance just taking an existing effect to a whole new level.

                                                        2. Requests to deities that can be answered. I want there to be rules - actual rules, not the half-description of how Prayer works in 2e - for praying to a crafting god in order to gain boons and luck while making a daiklave. I want to be able to pray to a god of health and have disease cast out of someone without invoking a Medicine charm. I want to be able to pray for seas to part. As long as I contact the right deity, pay the right price, and roll high enough, I want to be able to access all kinds of effects with prayer.

                                                        That said, I want there to be a price to the versatility. It should be slower than a charm activation - slower than shaping up some Sorcery, even, unless Sorcery is already slower in 3e. I want it to be expensive to use charms that facilitate you reliably getting an answer. In other words, I want you to be able to pray for effects normally available with other charms and sorcery, but contingent on getting the appropriate god to actually pay attention to you, which costs more essence and more time than doing it yourself would have cost.

                                                        In other words, I want to see Zenith's getting help from gods and spirits using prayer in the same way that, say, an Eclipse might use contract negotiation and bureaucracy, or a Dawn might directly bully them into a task, or a Twilight might summon and bind them to a function, or a Night might blackmail, manipulate, and cajole them.

                                                        For both of the above items, I want them to be accessible in lesser measures to mortals. I want a priest with true faith to cause ghosts and other creatures of darkness to feel pain in his presence as he recites his heartfelt prayers. I want a sufficiently impassioned plea by a mortal supplicant over a moderate period of time to yield an actual miracle from on high. And I want there to be actual rules for making this happen, rather than it being a one-off ST houseruling, which get taken to Exalted levels by someone who's invested in Performance, not something that only becomes accessible at exalted levels in the first place.
