                                                    Jasmine is one of those Disney Princesses who is certainly compelling, but still not without her issues. She's given the trappings of agency, but very little in the way of actual capability or accomplishments. She's right in that she's not a prize to be won, but the story ultimately reflects this in terms of her having the power to select who gets to win her; this gets to be flexed a bit in her trying to tease out some honesty from somebody she's actually interested in, and distracting an antagonist with a show of reciprocating his lusts, but is otherwise fairly minimal.

                                                    ​If I was going to transplant the scenario into a game and do more than just a complete revision, I would have a mind to giving her a bit more agency in terms of the greater significance of any husband whom she might select, but that wouldn't have her as Exalted, but a mortal with great narrative significance based on a position of political importance.

                                                    ​That wouldn't necessarily mean subverting the narrative of marriage for love rather than duty, as something could still arise from development of a relationship based on personalities, but I would have a mind towards giving it some troubling consequences.

                                                        Lilo is a good fit, I think, for Zenith. She's all about Integrity and Brawl and Presence, and the thing where you take a creature of Darkness and redeem it by teaching it about Love and Family is solid Zenith material-an Eclipse would have tricked or enslaved Stitch, not taught him to dance.

                                                        I'd call Mulan a solid Night with War favored.

                                                        Merida I'd probably run as a Lunar, honestly. Her story is about rebellion against the status quo and choosing her own freedom over the demands of her station, and about the difference between a monster and a man-even if she's not the one shapechanging, the story is about the anchors of love and family keeping her from turning into a monster like the cursed prince who serves as the villain of the piece. Plus, post-movie, she's totally gonna end up an unstoppable barbarian warlord leading a screaming horde of bear-skin clad berserkers, you just know it.

                                                        Elsa is an interesting case. She's much more X Men and less Greek hero. I suppose you could call her an Air Aspect, especially as they're going to be stronger in 3e...but I think she's actually a good fit for a Defiler in 2e. She's a budding sorceress who hides her talents from her suspicious subjects, and after a major failure she gets a huge power boost, abandons her fetters, and goes off to build a giant fuckoff ice forts guarded by ice demons and terrorize the countryside with eternal winter entirely by accident. I mean, Twilights can do all that, but she's not a scholar-she's a rightful queen denied her rightful dominion. Her redemption by her love for her super-strength Lunar Mate sister is a nice arc.

                                                        So I'm making God-Kicking Boot, an Exalted webcomic, now. Updates on Sundays. Full-color, mediocre but slowly improving art. It's a thing.
