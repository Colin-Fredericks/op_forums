                                    Originally posted by Anasurimbor View Post
                                    You don't see the problems with giving Dragon-Blooded a full Solar-level combat suite?
                                    Of course I would, but that's not what is being suggested.

                                    A Solar Martial Artist uses more than just Martial Arts Charms; they can use Solar Resistance and Dodge for defence, Solar Awareness to boost their join battle pools and Solar Athletics to enhance their attacks and mobility. They also have Solar Excellency caps and mote pools.

                                    A Dragon-Blooded martial artist with identical Martial Arts Charms would be at a disadvantage in all these areas.
