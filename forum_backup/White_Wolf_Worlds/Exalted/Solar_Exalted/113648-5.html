            Brawl:

            Fort-Breaker Fist
            If the Solar is aware of a target behind cover, including complete cover, she may use this charm to attack and damage the cover. If this attack deals (10 - Solar's Essence) damage to the cover, the Solar may immediately roll to repeat her attack against her target. This second attack uses the same charms and merits as the first, so all powers used must be applicable to both the cover and the target. However, the target does not benefit from any cover bonuses to defend against the second attack. In addition, the Solar's first attack against the cover generates appropriate momentum.

                Performance:

                Festival of Hopes and Dreams
                The Solar sings a song which appeals to one of a specific listener's positive Intimacies and rolls Charisma + Performance. If the roll overcomes the target's Resolve, the strength of that Intimacy is increased by one rank so long as the Solar keeps singing. If that Intimacy is already defining, then the target cannot spend Willpower to resist social influences which are based on that Intimacy.

                            I'm starting to think my dice are loaded, which is odd, because I have ten of them and I don't always use the same ones. Anyway...

                            Resistance:
                            Inevitable Steel Tide Approach
                            Whenever the Solar would successfully dodge or parry an attack from medium range or farther, she can activate this charm to retroactively reduce her relevant defense value to 0 and take the full brunt of the attack. The Solar gains momentum equal to the damage she successfully soaked. In addition, if the Solar moves towards her attacker during her next action, she may apply the amount of damage she has suffered as a mote discount when activating an Excellency relevant to her combat movement dice.

                                            Integrity

                                            Vengeful Champion's Suspicions
                                            The Solar may activate this charm when she discovers that she was influenced into undertaking a given course of action or espousing a specific belief under false pretenses. So long as the Solar commits motes to this charm, she may reject any social influence based on the Intimacy that was used to deceive her without spending willpower, even if she is unable to call upon another intimacy to guide her actions. This charm automatically ends when the Solar makes reparation for any misdeeds she may have committed while under deception, or when she obtains appropriate vengeance against whoever deceived her.
