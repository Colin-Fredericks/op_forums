    How to tell if someone is a Solar (e.g. the Manse at the centre of Whitewall).

    12-04-2015, 05:03 AM
    There are many examples throughout 1st and 2nd ed, where a device was made for the Solars and it takes an act almost legendary for the age to get to the point where anyone but a Solar can use the item (the Empress and the Realm Defense Grid), but have we ever gotten a baseline for how that mechanically works? Is it legit to say - this device can tell if you are a Solar or not? How much effort would a god have to go to, to fool it (the Syndics seem to not have that trick and they are pretty powerful, although it isn't in their purview to impersonate people).

              Solar Exalts can be recognised by Caste Marks and Anima Banners. Lunar, Sidereal, Abyssal, and Infernal(GSP) too. Terrestrial Exalts only have Anima Banners. Alchemical Exalts also have Anima Banners but their Caste Markings are the design style of their charm modules.

              Mostly its Caste Marks; they were basically meant to be an unforgeable Badges of Office. Anima banners were a little more personalised heraldric device.

              Obviously "unforgeable" is a relative term, as at least one Dragonblooded fooled a Solar-Only Manse/Artefact.

              ---

              In 2ed, different magical materials attuned easier for different exalt types; orichalcum responded best to Solar Exalts. You could maybe key some form of type-recognition on that basis.

                                                          I've always just figured that the actual, functional Essence produced by different Exalt Types was subtly different. It explains why different MMs synthesize better with different Exalt Types (that material is just more compatible with the vagaries of your Essence). And, this is backed up by the books with how manses work. A Solar-aspected demense is a Solar-aspected demense, and you have to build a Solar-aspected Manse on top of it...and this is because the Essence that has pooled around the demense is Solar-aspected, and so behaves differently than other types of Essence. So, Solars generate Solar-aspected Essence. Just like how a Solar manse needs a Solar demense to sit on top of in order to function...and might explode if you build it on the wrong sort of demense...it should be possible to create other things that require Solar-aspected Essence in order to function.

                                                          Another source of 'Only Solars can do this' is to make it require Solar-Circle Sorcery to repair it exactly as it was. Sans the Mantle of Brigid in the hands of a Lunar or Siddie...that just isn't happening without a Solar.

                                                          Of course, 2E laid out methods by which the aspect of a demense could be changed...and this is described as a very slow and work-intensive process the time-period of which is measured in years. It also, apparently, ruins any connection to a manse sitting on it...because manse construction is a VERY specialized process. But they also talked about the ability to remodel a manse to accommodate for minor changes to the demense it sits upon...meaning that if you very gradually were to change the demense, you could possibly remodel the manse to keep up with the change in power-source. The real challenge is that doing this can change the effects and powers of the manse...which is not generally something you want to do.

                                                          So...using that as a mental template...here's the thinking. It is possible to remodel an artifact, manse, or other system to accept a different power source by several possible means.

                                                          With a complex artifact (pretty much required to get something that will only work for a Solar), it may be possible to take some long-running Craft (Artifice) rolls to first understand how the artifact works, and then remodel it to allow some other aspect of Essence to power it. Unfortunately, this may cause the Artifact's capabilities to change somewhat to be more in-line its new Aspect. The Artificer should be able determine what will change in advance...and possibly up the difficulty to reduce the impact of these changes. A 'simple' artifact like a Daiklave is not complicated enough to be successfully altered in this way.

                                                          With a manse...well, same idea, but with Geomancy instead. You make long-running roles to change the aspect of the Demense...and roles with similar rules to change the aspect of the Manse. The problem is that you have to convert both the demense and the manse to the new aspect at the same speed. If one finishes before the other, or you botch rolls...you've got serious problems...like boomy manse-explody problems.

                                                          And finally, with Sorcery...it is possible to conduct a Sorcerous Working against a target to 'hack it' into working. This, bolstered by the strongest fate the Sidereals could weave for her, coupled with the death of her entire squad (possibly as sacrifices, if you want to say so), and a good bit of plain-old luck, is how Her Redness got the Realm Defense Grid up and running. The catch is...'hacking' an artifact system into working in a way it is not designed to is a touchy prospect at best. I'd have to think about it a bit...but the higher rated the system you were trying to alter, the higher Circle/Ambition I would make trying to perform a seamless 'hack job.' By accepting limitations on your hack job, you could reduce the difficulty...so, basically, the 'weird behavior' that comes from messing up Working rolls...you can take ones intentionally to make the hack easier...though, like with messed up Working rolls, the ST has say on what exactly the problem is.

                                                          So, yes...the Scarlet Empress managed to get the Sword of Creation to work...but it won't work for any Terrestrial except her...it exacts a much greater toll on her (higher Essence charge? maybe inflicts damage of some sort?) to use it...and it doesn't really work right, setting off local disasters in addition to the carnage it unleashes on her targets and everything near them...as opposed to the terrifyingly lethal precision it operates with when in the hands of a Solar. In fact, these 'issues' it has are of sufficient concern that she didn't just hold back from using it for fear of wrecking her new empire...but also because she had no idea how bad the disasters would be each time...perhaps next time a disaster may be bad enough to cripple the RDG...and honestly...no one has any idea how to fix this thing if it breaks. And to address the 'drawbacks' from her working...the reason those drawbacks are so relatively minor are because of the absurdly good luck that the Sidereals gave her while weaving basically everything left in Creation into making her succeed.

                                                          (In my head...the RDG is composed of a ton of different manses, a big pile of N/A Artifacts all wired into a single system, and a heap of high ambition Solar Workings...probably assembled by basically every Solar (and possibly Lunar and Sidereal) Craftsman alive at the time. Makes it quite a bit more epic as the crown jewel of the weapons of the First Age.)
