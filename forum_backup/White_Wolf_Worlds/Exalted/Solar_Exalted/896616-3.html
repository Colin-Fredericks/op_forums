            Take it from someone who's run many mixed games: Balancing is often going to be a god damn nightmare with many different splat types. Its easier with Celestials, but *do not* use Terrestials mixed with Celestials. This is to only be done by a GM who is well aware of both or a player who REALLY knows the ins and outs of the system. For this example, Dragon Kings are even lower power than Terrestials. I mean they get some nice tricks, but they're a pain in the ass to balance around. 150 XP gives a few more grab bag of tricks and some high abilities/attributes... before you realize that Solars with 50 XP can often blow those out of the water. The only thing that would give them pause is something I toyed around with in something like my Exalted Modern game where Elder DK's turned into literal Western Dragons (Like out of Endless Legend, and even scarier to deal with).

            Gods as characters have never had fully fleshed out sets in exalted, seriously. Even in 2e the advice for making a PC god was basically "Do whatever you think looks cool." My best advice would be to wait until Exigents for Gods, or just treat Gods as Exigents for simplicity reasons. The only thing a God really swaps around is his anima banner for their spirit nature (Very hard to kill). The God charms in 2e can be ported over, and honestly I'd suggest doing so to save yourself some time in statting things, but for PC's you're making an entire charm set from scratch.

            My honest advice, if you're going to mix things up stick with Exalts. Solars are the only splat that are officially released right now. My Alchemical homebrew I can attest to being relatively balanced (Probably baised, but still). My Infernal homebrew still has a lot of work left, but I heard some crazy people were using that so.

            Read my shit at my homebrew topic, 2.5e and 3e material!

                                            Originally posted by kingcrackers View Post
                                            What about as a deity, like a god, or an elemental? The upside would be that you can maybe come up with new powers that relate to your domain or nature on the fly.
                                            Free-form anything is pretty hard in Exalted. The entire system is built on discrete units of power that are built to interact with each other. Workings and Artifact creation are somewhat free-form, but they are also deliberately segregated from the normal system, and are subservient to it(Workings make things that work within the normal system, for example).

                                            In my experience, flexibility equals power in RPGs. That's why Mages tend to be scary in oWoD mixed games, even though they are arguably the "weakest" of the supernaturals.

                                            I think any attempt to tack on a free-form power system to Exalted is doomed to failure. At best, it works, and screws up game balance. At worst, it's lackluster and boring.
