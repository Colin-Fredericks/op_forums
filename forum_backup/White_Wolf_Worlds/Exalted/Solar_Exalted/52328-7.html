    Originally posted by JimB View Post
    I'd like to put out a dissenting voice to all the calls for Performance Charms that focus on a specific type of performance, like dancing or painting or whatever. I think Charms ought to be divided according to what you're trying to do rather than how you're trying to do it, so dance-specific Performance Charms would rub me wrong in the same way sword-specific Melee Charms would. I think they'd come off as a very artificial attempt to length the trees.
    I don't know, most of the sail tree is targeted at ships on the water when we have ice-ships and air boats and subs and planes and sky skimmers and so on and so on. Kinda like how the DB charms are focused (in 2E) upon a specific element and there has been talk about the same charm having different effects for different elemental aspects (in 3E) - something like that could be done here. It's hard to apply the same charm to painting and doing a piano recital then a spoken performance (I mean painting and piano are both psychical then you do some oration?). Performance is a hard one just like linguistics - in both you are basically crafting. In the past the craft ability has been split between 5 sub categories and I think it would only add flavor to do the same for performance. It's the social craft to mirror the mental/physical crafts.

                Things I Wanna Do With Performance In-Game:
                1. Y'know in Victor/Victoria, where the main character, through practice and sheer mastery, can reliably and consistently hit a note that causes wine glasses to explode and champagne corks to pop out? I want to do that. I want to be able to shatter glass or crystal with my voice, or split asunder the glacier covering some First Age Ruin in the reaches of the North.

                2. "Play it again, Sam." "Luke, I am your father." I want the ability to perform something so perfectly that bits of my performance become a meme that just spreads on its own. I want NPCs to be quoting my performances in cities that I've never been to. I want my one-liners to spread like viruses, predisposing people to believe that I'm awesome even though they've never met me.

                3. I want to inspire passion and change. I want to break a Guild Slaver's heart with a short poem about human cruelty so that he rethinks his way of life. I want to whisper a sonnet from under a balcony and win the prince's heart. I want to sing up popular uprisings by singing a mournful song about how hard life is for whoever I'm singing for. I want the duke who sees me dance to order my companions imprisoned and me put in chains and brought immediately to his bedchamber, because he simply must own me. I want people who hear or see my performance to be driven to ACT, even if it's not always in the way I'd planned for them to act.

                4. Mythic Music: I want to pull out a flute and play a gentle lullaby that puts an army or a rampaging behemoth to sleep. I want to march into the Underworld and into the Thousand and sing of my love for a dead woman so powerfully that the fell lord of that place sees himself in me and releases his newest ghostly slave. I want the Green Sun of Malfeas to fear my pen, for if he offends me I have sworn to make him the object of a ribald tavern song and a joke throughout Creation. I want to walk up to a tornado and speak a haiku, and watch as the winds stop and the Storm Serpent thrashing within them quiets the thunder so she can hear my voice.

                5. This is My Whole Deal: I want to be able to invest in Performance as my primary ability, like you say we now can with most Abilities. I'm not so sure about improving the competency of my allies with my inspiration, but I definitely want to be able to give opponents second thoughts by singing about their folly or how terrible I am. I want investing in Performance to give me tools other than just persuading people: I want high-end Performance to start bordering on Sorcery.

                6. Celebrity: I want to turn my legend into a part of my power. I want Performance charms that play off my Cult or Followers or Reputation for greater potency. I want my performances to be better because I get my legions of screaming fans to sing along. I want my marching army to sing with me as we march and inspire dread in my enemies. I want the Brides of Ahlat as my backup dancer-bodyguards while I turn violence into a ballet. I want to lead my cult in songs of prayer that call down rains of fire from the heavens and split the seas asunder that we might walk across them. I want to sing and inspire others to join in and sing along, because together our voices are so much stronger than mine could ever be alone.

                Some Performance Inspirations:
                1. Bilbo Baggins. In the book, you know the silly "Attercop" song he sings, where he terrorizes his deadly opponents to the core by sing-song nonsense as he murders them? That. Also, I like the way the song at the beginning inspires Bilbo to leave his home and go out into danger. When I picture Husband Seducing Demon dance, at least used to recruit men to go to war, I always picture Bilbo listening to the dwarves' song and dreaming of fire and gold and distant lands as he drifts off to sleep, and awakening with a hankering for adventure.
                2. Evita. Like, the entire movie.
                3. Prince of Egypt. Again, the whole movie. Moses has always been great Solar inspiration.
                4. Orpheus. I wanna be able to sing Cerebus to sleep and warm the hearts of the gods of the Underworld.
                5. Constantine: It's a mediocre movie, but at the end when Gabriel sends him flying with a breath, and when Satan blows Gabriel's wings right off with a roar? I want to use my voice as a weapon like that. Might be more Abyssal territory?
                6. Victor/Victoria: Performance as disguise (less about physical disguise and more about acting and creating expectations: everybody's too busy saying "He's totally lying, he can't really be a Count" to question whether you're a "He"), and as a weapon of mass destruction (exploding glasses turned up to Exalted levels of power).
                7. Trigun. There's this scene where pacifist Vash the Stampede wants to clear out a place, so he just sings a creepy song over the intercom about how much he loves killing people and relies on his reputation and performance to scare his opponents away, and to unnerve whoever's left into being less of a problem. He pulls the same trick again later on a whole city by "rampaging" through the streets firing his gun in the air and roaring about the slaughter he's gonna bring to the place, just to make sure there aren't any innocent bystanders when shit goes down.
                8. Mary Poppins...but her songs might be more Raksha performance inspiration...
                9. Six-String Samurai. It's a ridiculous and terrible cult classic, but I want to see music in Exalted in the same sort of "bordering on sorcery" magical sense it appears in this movie. I want an Abyssal and a Solar doing a supernatural rock-off battle over the fate of modern music to be a thing. I want philosophical and artistic differences over genre and style to manifest themselves in Exalted-level violence.
                10. Hendrix smashing his guitar. I want my performances to be so iconic and memorable that people keep copying or satirizing it or ripping them off or misquoting it for decades...along with whatever message and philosophy went with them.
                Last edited by Wise Old Guru; 01-19-2014, 06:22 AM.

                                                I'm sorry, but that's not how Abilities work. The end mechanical result is not the dividing factor, but the means by which you get there. Compare Melee and Martial Arts. They both have the same mechanical outcome: they interact with the combat system, deal damage and such. The only difference between them is if you use a weapon or not, and that is entirely stylistic. That's a means, no an end. I can understand if you'd rather not see too narrow Charms (even if I myself think Charms in 2e were too broad if anything), but that's not the same thing.
