    Investigation:

    Counter-Ambush Insight
    By quickly analyzing the layout of her surroundings and the behaviour of the people around her, the Solar can learn the precariousness of her situation and the intentions of would-be enemies. The Solar rolls Wits + Investigation. If the roll is successful, she immediately knows if any of the people around her and of whom she is aware are planning on attacking her or causing her arm during the rest of the scene. In addition, the Solar can find the perfect spot to defend against the attack, if there is one. If the Solar chooses to stay and confront her attackers, she gains momentum equal to her Investigation upon rolling Join Battle.

        Melee:

        Iron Heart of the Crimson Storm

        This Charm supplements a close-range attack against a battle group. If the attack is successful, the battle group takes a penalty to its combat movement dice equal to the Solar's melee rating. If the battle group moves away from the Solar, all ongoing penalties directly imposed upon the Solar by the battle group's actions immediately end. This Charm lasts for one scene.

            Faith of the Sorcerer-King

                War:

                Unyielding Entrenchment Manoeuvre:

                Upon activating this Charm, the Solar immediately spots a suitable defensive position on the battlefield. If a battle group under the Solar's command moves to that position within (Solar's War rating) rounds of the charm's activation, the battle group increases its soak by (Solar's Essence / 2, rounded up). This charm lasts for one scene, but immediately ends if the battle group moves away from its strategic position.

                        Ride:

                        Dragon and Rider Are One
                        By activating this charm, the Solar automatically becomes used to the natural environment of her chosen mount. She does not suffer any penalties or damage due to environmental hazards and her own vital functions, such as breathing, become the same as her mount. For example, a Solar riding a whale would gain the ability to breathe underwater and to survive the immense pressure of the ocean depths.

                                That last one seems like it'd take a lot of bookkeeping. Not only do you need to keep track of a player's Intimacies, you also need to keep track of every Intimacy you've ever had in case it becomes relevant. Plus it's got the perverse incentive of encouraging specific types of backgrounds (opposing the Realm right now is nice, but if you were a devout Immaculate before you Exalted, you get a free Intimacy that only applies when you want it to!). Plus, as the Solar grows older, the Charm comes closer to "Pay X motes to act like you've got a relevant Intimacy," which kind of defeats the point of Intimacies in the first place.

                                    Peerless Infiltration Method:

                                    By analyzing the defenses around a building, site or encampment the Lawgiver can develop a plan that will allow herself and (essence X2) companions to infiltrate that site, and combine their disparate skill sets into an action worthy of the Unconquered Sun. While executing this plan, all of the companions:
                                    • Are immune to discovery by unenlightened mortals. Mere guards will always look in the wrong places for the Lawgiver and her companions as they hide in places mortals simply do not bother to look.
                                    • Are able to use the Lawgiver's Stealth skill (plus any relevant specialties) in place of their own .
                                    • Piece the outer most layer of the site's defenses. This effect is arbitrated by the Story teller, but means that all the affected characters are able to Perfectly access the area, though they can risk discovery later on (ex: Isana Two Nail and her companions are infiltrating Thorns. Careful examination of the guard schedule allows the group to exploit a gap and slip over the walls unseen by climbing some bamboo that grew to close to the city. Isana and her companions are on the roof of a building adjacent to the out wall)
                                    • All affect characters have memorized all available information about the site, and instinctively know the fastest (not safest) route to the target, and the fastest (not Safest) path to the nearest exit.

                                    Charm Concept: Any Shadowrun GMs? Know how an infiltration session can devolve into 2 hrs of planning, and then collapse? This Charm is meant to bypass that and get the sneakiness underway as quickly as possible. It also simulates how pulp heroes are never discovered by mere guards,even if they have to display cleverness in avoiding them. Call it the Ocean's Eleven Charm, and an Essence 5 solar can indeed field a party of up to 11 people.

                                                Archery:

                                                Unerring Memorial Arrow
                                                Having tested her accuracy against her target once, the Solar's aim becomes truer. After the Solar makes a successful attack against a target and deals damage, the Solar may repeat that exact attack during her next action, using the same charms, gambits, techniques and merits. All dice that come up as a 9 when rolling to attack count as two successes. The Solar can repeat the attack again to make 8s and 7s count as two successes as well. However, the Solar's successes on the attack and damage rolls cannot exceed the successes of the original attack being copied.
