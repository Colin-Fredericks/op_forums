                              I want Performance to make up its mind on whether it's supposed to be about music and dance or about long-winded speeches, because I have some trouble seeing certain Charms apply to both sides of that coin.

                              I like that one of the basic Performance Charms is "stay here until I'm done." Having spectators unable to turn their eyes away from your Dance of the Seven Veils is very yes. I would like that it not be brain-washing - why the hell is the memory-rewriting Charm in Performance? I want it to play on emotions - to rouse anger or to bring peace of mind, to spur love or hate, and to inspire people to be heroes. Performance should not so much convince people of things as it should draw their attentions, inspire feelings, make you beloved or feared. It should also still summon ephemeral images assisting your art.

                              Performance in 1e was pretty big, but that's because it contained most of what later became War; 2e split that into two Abilities without giving Performance new toys, and as a result it is pretty barren. It really needs more stuff to play with, I think.

                              In The Phoenix on the Sword, one of the conspirators who plot to assassinate Conan is a bard, a poet and singer, who almost single-handedly turned the city's opinion on their new king from "rightful savior of the people" to "hated usurper." And yet still, as he has done that and now is throwing himself at Conan trying to kill him, Conan is reluctant to hurt him until he has no other choice. "A great poet is greater than any king. His songs are mightier than my scepter; for he has near ripped the heart from my breast when he chose to sing for me. I shall die and be forgotten, but Rinaldo's songs will live for ever.""

                              When you are a master of Solar Performance, even your sworn enemies should weep at the thought of killing you.

                              Bits and pieces of Exalted setting.

                                                      The whole awe inspiring, speech giving, army leading is really the core I think and I liked the previous edition's system very much.
                                                      On our table "War" is not actually that good for leading, but is great for the logistics, tactics and strategies (and countering them). So a good leader relied more on Performance and a good second-in-command, or military advisor, relied on War. People leading with War only worked well with highly disciplined units.


                                                      What I really want to see in Performance is some sort of illusion magic: Setting the correct mood of a scene with light, sound and scents. And on higher levels not only as mind trick, but as physical emanation (allowing for stunts next to bonuses for social rolls).
                                                      That could even go so far as illusions of great beast controlled by the Solar's flute which only incapacitates on a kill, but for everyone it looked like the victim was shredded to pieces.
