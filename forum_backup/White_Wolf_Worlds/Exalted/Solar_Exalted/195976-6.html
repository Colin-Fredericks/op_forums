    Originally posted by While My Guitar View Post

    Then why would I want my Sidereal to have anything but a Starmetal Weapon? It gives me the coolest stuff.
    Honestly, I hope the connection between MM's and Specific types of Exalted gets seperated as much as possible. In 1e, it made sense, five types of exalted, five magic Materials, but Now we're at 3rd, and we now have at least 10 different types of Exalted (counting Exigents as one group) and the number of MM's has not increased. It just seems like making material type A better for Exalted type A pigeonholes Exalted type A into picking Material A, since it's objectively the best choice.

    As per Holden, this is the goal. Sidereals get Starmetal weapons, because it gives you the best stuff. As for 10 Exalt types, and no new MMs, see my above posts. 6 MMs, six base exalt types as near as I can tell (Liminals, Getimen, Dragon-Blooded, Sidereals, Lunars, Solars). Alchemicals are also composed of the six types, so they don't get their own MM. It's been hinted multiple times that Abyssals aren't naturally linked to Soulsteel, and that there is a sixth Exalt type, hidden.

                                            Soulsteel was something that bugged me in the past because it required something that couldn't exist before the war because the universe its self could not conceive of a titan dying and staying dead so it had to make a place for that. The ore to make soulsteel came from the universe understanding a stagnation of death, a terminal end to things that should last forever, that nothing is that exists is without a way to pass from existance.

                                            That made me wonder if the first Soulsteel, was in truth a more primitive soul steel made from a less effective ore and souls. It was less effective at it's job, more time consuming and difficult to make because the forces that it needed to reach it's final state did not yet exist. After the primordial war, the powers of death became stronger and death had a true sting and the Labyrinth Dark Ore became manifest.

                                            The new artifact systems actually make sense with pre-existing lore because prior to this why would solars or lunar bother with the stuff when you could mine oricalcum, atune easier to it, and get more benefit. from using a none matching material. A Dragon Blood might want a Jade and Soul Steel Daiklave to hunt solars with because it makes sense to him. Yes the Dragon Blood might not get as much out of the Soulsteel as a Abyssal but he can still use it to bring out some amazing power in his weapon.

                                                        Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post
                                                        Personally, I wouldn't mind if they loosened the associations between specific Exalts and Magical Materials.
                                                        This, a thousand times this. I honestly think that the caste / magical material associations were a bad idea and should be massively downplayed or even removed entirely.

                                                        It encourages people to make more boring, generic characters (you play the DEATH EXALT and use weapons with the DEATH METAL; you play the SUN EXALT and use weapons with the SUN METAL etc). When you look at heroes with legendary weapons, their weapons are generally entirely their own thing -- that is to say, a five-dot or N/A artifact should feel almost like a second Exaltation (like, if you want to use game-terms, a secondary class or something) -- not like a duplicate of your main Exaltation. Using artifacts to just let you do what you can already do but better is incredibly boring.
