                        Okay, so...a few thoughts here.

                        1: DBs should continue to be, alone, weaker than an equivalent Solar. However...if the Wyld Hunt comes for a Solar, they won't be equivalent and they won't be fighting one on one. They know how scary Anathema can be, and you are going to get hit by a significant number of DBs, many of whom are older and more experienced than you. That's kinda always been their whole thing...not a huge threat to a Celestial when alone, but absolutely terrifying enmasse. (And I hope we see a decent number of DB charms that synergize with the charms of other DBs...kinda tying together the fact that their greatest power is when they work together). One of my most memorable moments as a ST was the first time my players had a run in with the Wyld Hunt. They were like 'Oh, they are just Dragonblooded' and joined battle. It did not end well for my players...2 ended up incapacitated, and the other 3 ended up burning Essence like crazy trying to get away...they managed, but all invested in escape charms immediately thereafter and resolved to be a lot more careful about bringing the Shaolin Death Squads down on their heads. Even rumors of the Wyld Hunt were enough to send them scurrying away.

                        2: As has been mentioned, the Immaculate Styles were specifically designed for the Terrestrials, to give them enough umph to take on rogue gods (really, that was the original intent...they were created during a time when the Solars were mostly locked away and the Lunars were in hiding). I'm not sure how to handle the initiation into the styles...but the simplest solution is that those styles simply do not have Terrestrial limitations on them...and some charms may work better for a terrestrial as it makes use of their elemental anima. I mean, if you are designing a martial art for the Terrestrials, why would you design an art for them that they couldn't actually wield freely?

                        3: So, back to the OP...

                        Solars are generally rated as the 'most powerful' because of Mastery, because of their Supernal, because they get access to the best Sorcery, because they have lower XP costs (and thus develop faster), and because they have a bigger Essence Pool and more efficient/effective Charms. In short, Solars are generally considered the most powerful because they can just face a problem and bulldoze their way through it with raw power.

                        This doesn't mean that they will always be the ideal choice for a situation though. Solars tend to be the 'brute force solutions' type of powerhouse, and have a tendency to specialize towards being really good at only a few roles...their ability spread is a good indication of this. The other splats tend to be good at other sorts of solutions. (Note: what follows is more going off of flavor/lore, and thus how I hope the splats will be written for 3E...rather than how they have necessarily been written in the past.) Lunars are ridiculously adaptable and can change themselves to suit a situation as it develops...they are generally the most 'multi-purpose' of the splats. Siddies are the best at long term thinking and planning, because they can literally look ahead, and modify destinies to make their plans happen...and solve most of their problems by cheating. (An actually good example of Siddie cheating is how their Perfect Dodge worked. A Solar's perfect is either a flawless parry, or turning themselves invulnerable, or impossibly dodging...a Siddie's perfect was basically them just saying "Nope, you missed. No, I don't care that you saw your sword hit me in the throat...you missed.")
