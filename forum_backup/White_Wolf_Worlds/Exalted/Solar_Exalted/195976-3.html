                    Originally posted by Zelbinnean View Post

                    Well, I think of it in a different way. Take the kickstarter example, Volcano Cutter. Volcano Cutter seems to be kinda like "my sword strikes evoke the fury of lava," which is not necessarily the same as "my sword strikes are effective fire Elemental Bolt or draw from DB themes." Ditto with the one that's like "when it is drawn, a literal hurricane starts above me." I just hope it branches into non-elemental things, which is probably likely when you're not talking about elementalized jade weapons. And it even seems like a moon silverweapon's Evocations are't all going to be about moonsilver or Lunar themes. Some might be some (like, maybe Evocations relating to mutability and fluidness, but specifically NOT shapeshifting), but some are probably also going to be about the themes of the weapon, its crafter, and its wielder, even if it just so happens to be made of moonsilver.

                    It'd be a bad idea to let Artifact Evocations just steal wholesale from other splats' themes, let alone replicate their Charms - that was such a criticized part of Eclipse charmshare the last 2 eds, where every Charm had to be balaced against Solars getting it, where Knacks existed because they needed to be kinda Charms but also definitively outside of the reach of Eclipses...it was a huge mess, and if the Eclipse anima is getting revamped to prevent that issue, I doubt the debs are just going to reintroduce it with Evocations.
                    I fully agree with you. I would love to see exactly how the evocation system plays and how weapon style effects it. Do Grim Cleavers have different types of Evocations available than Daiklaves? I have said that, and it sounds like, the way Evocations will work is they will give us template evocations (Like Powers in Savage worlds) we fill in the details and that creates the final evocation.

                    Either that or I hope they do what they did with Shadowrun and it's custom created spells system and give the formulas for determining mote costs, fair durations, ect. (Or at least I'm hoping they have one.) That way GM's have less to worry about balancing because so long as the player used the formula the Evocation will be balanced. It can be as unique as they like, but it will still be balanced by having them follow some clearly spelled out rules for evocation creation.

                    Though I do wonder if Plunge a blade into the ground and raise a small army of trees as my warriors is even possible as an evocation. I think that would be cool for a Saphire level evocation for Iron Heartwood.

Shapeshifting weapons sounds like something common enough to let solars have (particualrly if it only has 2 or 3 forms), but I'd certainly give lunars a discount on it.
                                            I don't really agree with hardline splat-specific Evocations for the following reason. Evocations are built into the weapon, not in the user (unlike Charms); the user just accesses the weapon's Evocations. It's feasible for a Solar to be using moonsilver, or a DB to be using soulsteel, and doesn't make much sense if some of those Evocations are unavailable just because the character is a DB and not an Abyssal.

                                            ​However, Volcano Cutter has at least one Evocations in its tree that has a Solar Charm as a prereq. Possibly implying that the Evocation builds off of or adds functionality to uses of that Charm. I think that's evidence of an effective splat-specific Evocation since nobody else can get that Solar Charm (barring like BMR-type stuff for Solars existing in, say, the 3e Abyssal charm set or whatever...which may happen, but I kinda doubt it). But that still sits better with me than the idea that a lot of powers on an Artifact will be splat-specific, versus a couple low-hanging-fruit Evocations that have a native Charm as a prereq.

                                                    Originally posted by Aquillion View Post
                                                    Even more amusingly, Abyssals got their version, Unconquered Hero's Faith, long before BMR was printed (although it referenced a hypothetical Solar version, it didn't confirm that it was possible.)

                                                    But people complained way more abut BMR... admittedly, probably because it was for a splat that people actually played.
                                                    My single biggest problem with Black Mirror Revelation vs. Unconquered Hero's Faith was that it treated Limit and Resonance costs as interchangeable and ignored the difference between the two mechanics.

                                                    Resonance is something you're supposed to manage on a meta level, the entire Abyssal playstyle assumes you're aware of how close you are to killing loved ones on the other side of the world, alerting the Aerial Legion to your presence or being posessed by the Neverborn and measuring your rebellion against your dark fate carefully otherwise it'll be crushed before you can make any real progress meanwhile Limit is intended to be a sword of damocles that you never quite know when it'll fall.

                                                    Charms that cause Limit gain are potentially beneficial in a way that charms that cause Resonance aren't because they allow you to have a bout of murderous rage, calculating cruelty (or what have you) at a moment where it can seem appropriate or even justified rather than blowing up at the wrong people.

                                                    This is just confounded by the fact that the Holy keyword means that several Solar charms are unusable to Abyssals anyway.
