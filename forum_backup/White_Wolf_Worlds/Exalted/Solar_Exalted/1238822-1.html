                                        Mhnnn I've changed my mind; I will talk about some things.

                                        The Lunars' bond to the Solars has been a few things over the Editions. I've still not yet read the First Edition book (I might actually see about getting around to that this weekend), but I understand that to have characterized that as a matter of how in the First Age, they would engage in ritualistic political marriages, that would tend to recur over incarnations, and had left a lot of Lunars in the Time of Tumult with very deep senses of connection to newly incarnated Solars.

                                        Second Edition upped this to an outright metaphysical connection, each Lunar Essence bonded to a Solar one at the time of its inception, with an attached mythology of the Unconquered Sun having demanded it to ensure the hierarchy in Exalted ranks (with it having a certain tricky twist from Luna). It came through mechanically in the idea that when a Lunar would meet their bond mate, they'd automatically form an unbreakable Intimacy (not necessarily a positive one), and there was an overall sense of partnership. It also came with a Background to build on that, which as I recall was basically just bonuses and penalties to Mental Defence Value as it related to one's Solar. Glories added a few Charms that had some variations in the concept, but that was the core.

                                        Now, speaking personally, I actually have a preference for the idea of a substantial metaphysical element to the Bond, an idea of something in the Lunar soul that reaches out to and connects with the Solar that they have associated with for ages. It appeared that a number of people, including some writers, found something to stir imagination in that as well (for better or worse); in particular was ideas that Holden used to posit, in which the natural state of the Lunar was a kind of raging storm that the Bond could help to ground and focus. I will admit to having had concerns when it felt like the new developers were going to downplay that side of it (even if I was ready to accept such gracefully).

                                        What we have here appears to be something that blends the framework from First Edition with the most readily salvaged elements of Second, in the context of the new direction of Lunars in Third. I wouldn't fret too much over the matter of a background being given to it, especially if that should be given no more detail than ancient myth. The key thing, I think, is that it would be myth that characterizes the metaphysical Bond as a thing that evolved organically from the agency of the Lunars and the development of their relationship with Solars. I should think that will be particularly helpful in redeeming the concept in many eyes and validating that it is something primarily to the benefit of Lunars; in the absence of that, I fear that some people would default to an assumption that it was something imposed upon Lunars by the gods, and that just... has too much baggage.

                                        In that light, the primary significance I see in the idea that it's something that they opt into is that it drops the notion of a Lunar instantaneously forming a connection simply from meeting a Solar, and instead that there's a part of their Essence that is always ready to resume the relationship, which will have certain benefits and incentives, but that part is still ultimately leashed to the will of the Lunar.

                                        (Unless it simply means "you always have an option to play one of the quarter of Lunars that never had a mate")

                                        As for how it formed, I wouldn't expect a working to do that, as I think a general thoroughline is that Exaltation is a miracle beyond even their reach. I would instead suspect that it would be an application of the mutable nature of Lunar Essence. Same as changing the function of their Castes.
