                    I've always thought it would be cool for Medicine to some pressure-point style Charm(s) for messing people up - blindness, penalties, temporarily crippling a limb, that sort of thing - using their understanding of the body and the meridians.

                    Similarly, I have a long-standing dream to play a kung fu doctor who both attacks and heals people using thrown needles and acupuncture. Now, clearly, custom Thrown Charms would be part of that, but some Medicine Charm that lets you ignore penalties for improvised equipment might also be needed.

                    Chinese stories often seem to have expert doctors be able to tell all kinds of things about a person based on the disposition of elements as reflected in breath and so on. Broader information-gathering Medicine Charms sound cool to me.

                    More prosaically, it would certainly be useful to have a Medicine Charm which let you create tonics or elixirs which stuck around, even if they were less effective than what you did on your own, so that your allies and servants could have access to it while you're not around.

                    And though the potential for abuse must be watched, I have always liked the idea of using Medicine to clear out the chi of people (mortals or Exalts) to help make them more powerful, or better able to focus on a given task.

                    Of course, looking at that list... I don't know if any of that is Medicine. It might just be Martial Arts, Thrown, Investigation, Craft, and... lots of skills (War and Lore did it in 2E) respectively. Medicine Charms are tough!

                    Arcanus' idea of pre-emptive medice seems really hard to argue, though.

                            Originally posted by John Mørke View Post
                            Any requests?

                            Please try not to ask for things that are already in the 2e set.
                            A system for diseases that integrates well into both the general medical rules and the solar medicine charmset was the main thing I thought 2e's Solar Medicine tree was missing. It was one of the few non-combat trees that felt like it synergized with itself pretty well, since you could combo everything together into a "Lay on Hands" instant healing effect.

                            As long as poisons, diseases, crippling effects, and gross physical trauma are all things my Solar Doctor can take care of, I have no complaints.


                            ....That said, since you're asking, I'd like to see more inter-ability synergy. Medical charms that, say, give you bonuses to hitting vital organs with your physical attacks, ones that work well with whatever the poison/medicine making Craft charms are, ones that can help out with interrogation techniques in Investigation and Larceny, and so on.

                            (For the record, I expect all the other abilities to reciprocate. Combos and Synergy are big parts of the fun in CCGs and Exalted's charm system is designed much like one of them. Setting things up such that being good at, say, any of the Twilight abilities helps you out with the other four seems like a good way to make a twilight feel more like a twilight, as opposed to a dude who just happens to be good at the thinky stuff.)

                                                        I'd like to see a charm where a solar can reattach a limb and make it function again.

                                                        Overal though, what I hope happens is that medicine becomes less boring.
                                                        A lot of the medicine charms are too universal, so there is nothing interesting you can do with them. Why would I care if someone has a fever or is the first victim of Plaguefather Shen's new disease? The only difference is that I have to roll a few more dice.
                                                        Also, because medicine in Exalted can't work like our medicine, due to them essence instead of molecules, I'd like the medical procedures to show that.
                                                        Things like the Five Elements in acupuncture, and the way the ancient Greeks explained certain diseases by excesses of different fluids, which are in turn tied to different temperaments.
                                                        And I know that in 2e there were diseases of conviction and diseases of Valor, that sort of stuff, but those were really only about the symptoms. Having a disease that's stronger on a person who has a high Valor would make both the disease, and hopefully the way of treating it, much more interesting. It would also explain why some people suffer more from a disease than others.

                                                        I guess what I really want is there to be somewhat of a middleground between the semi-sciency medical things, since those could make actual sense in exalted, and more realistic, surgical procedures.
