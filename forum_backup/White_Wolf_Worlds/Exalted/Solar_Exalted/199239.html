    Othar Tryggvassen, Solar Exalted

    07-16-2014, 05:19 PM
    Let's hypothesize, for a moment, that you're a redeemed Abyssal who's just gone Solar, now freed of the Great Curse and likely one of the most powerful beings in Creation. And with the experiences you've had and your mind clear, you decide that even if the Curse were broken the Celestial Exalted would remain an unacceptable threat to Creation with their existence, that they give too much power to too few people. Solars, Lunars, Sidereals, Infernals, and Abyssals, yourself included, all have to go. Preferably, of course, in a way that allows Creation to survive. We can refer to this hypothetical Solar as Othar.

    Now, the Exaltations themselves, of course, are inassailable. They can be trapped and modified, but not destroyed, and as long as humans exist that meet their qualifications they will continue to latch on to them. Jade Prisons can break, and of course Othar can't trap his own Solar Exaltation in one, which he wants to do. There is, of course, also the matter of the Dragonblooded Exaltations, which while more numerous still give their power to the privileged few. However, they can in theory be the key to defeating the Celestial Exalted once and for all, for no being can have two Exaltations. If every human in the world were a Terrestrial Exalt, the Celestial Exaltations would continue to exist but be stripped of their danger, unable to find a host to possess.

    Othar's task is therefore threefold:
    • Find a method of permanently transforming every human in Creation into a Dragonblooded
    • Ensure the resulting race is up to snuff for dealing with the challenges that will face Creation
    • Eliminate any remaining threats that they will not be able to deal with on their own
    Transforming humanity is the one that takes the longest time but can be done in any order, as it requires creating a Dragonblooded line guaranteed to Exalt without being diluted. Possible ways to do this have come up before, such as sponsoring a Dragonblooded to assist them in taking every purchase of Transcendent Gaian Harmony. Once this is done, and a sufficient number of children are seeded, the rest will come naturally without Othar needing to do anything as the Dragonblooded population will slowly increase over millennia until it becomes the norm. In doing so the Immaculate Philosophy will naturally undergo reform, and while there will still be differences in social status based on wealth and political power, the ability of the oppressed to grow more physically and spiritually powerful than their oppressors will ensure that the social order remains mutable. Laden with more conflict than it might be with no Exalted at all, perhaps, but never quashed under one group's heel for long, and this is all that Othar desires.

    Of course, the current Dragonblooded are too weak to deal with many of Creation's needs, such as expanding the borders of Creation to ward off the Wyld, maintaining the Loom of Fate, or performing the highest levels of Sorcery. This requires expanding the Salinan Working to enable all beings regardless of level of Exaltation to be able to access all circles of Sorcery with sufficient training. It also requires creating some equivalent of Wyld-Shaping Technique. In this case the comparative weakness of Terrestrials is a boon, as it can be used to make the resulting Charm not create non-Dragonblooded humans who might accidentally Exalt as a Celestial. Maintaining Fate is a more tricky prospect, as the system requires restructuring to deal with the much higher concentration of Essence-users anyway. Clearly the operating system is in need of an upgrade!

    All this in mind, what else would our gentleman adventurer have to do in order to achieve his mad goal?

      There's only one real way to ensure that every living person Exalts as a Dragon-Blooded: Kill everyone who isn't. And keep the program up for a few generations to weed out anyone of weak blood.

      Then, develop transcendent charms for Dragon-Blooded. It's a thing that can happen in the rarest of rare cases.
      Or, create artifacts that mimic the necessary Charms/Spells, because allowing allowing DBs to transcend allows them to get to the same dangerous power level.
      Do not under any circumstances work to allow access to greater Circles of Sorcery, because otherwise one of those damn DBs will break through to Solar Circle somehow, eventually, and then all hell breaks loose again.
      Also, to protect creation, use the mass slaughter to run necrotic essence wards through the rim of Creation. That's pretty alien to the Wyld, and should hold it off.

      Of course, Othar is a psychopath who will slaughter just about the entirety of Creation. Meaning there is a simpler solution.

      Destroy Creation, make a new, better one in his own image. Fixing all of those flaws. Maybe even save a few, worthy Dragon-Blooded. Probably a few hundred, tops.
