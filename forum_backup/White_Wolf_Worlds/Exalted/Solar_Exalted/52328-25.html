                                        Originally posted by SuperG View Post
                                        In short, Occult is the "real magic" skill of the setting, and the priests are left with a "charlatan" badge, because... why? Why do sorcerors get real magic and priests get to be phonies?
                                        ...? Because this isn't D&D with arcane and divine magic? Being a priest isn't about magic, it's about your relationship with a god. This CAN mean you get some magic: maybe he gifts you with some kind of power, maybe he teaches you secret techniques. One might say... occult techniques...

                                        Plenty of mortal priests probably aren't even thaumaturges, nevermind being sorcerers or Exigents or having some kind of artifact or endowment or whatever. Being a priest is a social role. It's about being the link between the god and the population: keeping the god placated with prayers, transmitting the edicts of the god, etc. An Exalted version of the same will STILL be focused on the social role.

                                        Casting spells and exorcising is Occult. This is something a priest CAN do, but it is not a core competency for Zeniths. If you want to do it, you pick Occult as one of your favored Abilities. If you want to heal people, you pick Medicine. Beating people up is something a warrior-priest CAN do, but it is not a core competency for Zeniths. If you want it... You get the picture.

                                        As an aside, gods are usually considered very good sources of knowledge and wisdom and occult secrets, so of course you're going to take a bunch of Twilight Abilities if you priest concept is focused around being a learned man. In fact, there's nothing wrong with making a Twilight priest.

                                                    Correct me if I'm wrong but the issue is, I think, that a priest has no meaningful ability to do anything. They can pretend God(s) care what they do but they don't. God(s) do whatever they want regardless of what you do. Essentially making one of the descriptors of the Zenith cast non-protagonist.

                                                    Being the best priest ever and doing the things unique to priests that differentiate them from sorcerors and wordsmiths and so on achieves absolutely nothing as it's entirely dependant on God(s) doing what you ask...which they are under absolutely no obligation to do. So, the key unique selling point of being a priest type character (note, I did not say Zenith here, just the priest archetype) is bugger all. Because really, anyone who could speak to a God or interact with it (Exalts for example) could make it do something anyway and the priest part is utterly irrelevant.

                                                    So, we're left with the social role, how pepole react to a priest. But the net result of being treated as a divine link is easily replicated by any archetype with good group social/leadership skills anyway. And, arguably, the Eclipse as diplomat has the same protections and perks of access offered to a priest.

                                                    HOWEVER.

                                                    This is somewhat predicated on the idea that the populace in general know that Gods are under no obligation at all to answer prayers or act and that a priest is essentially a public figurehead with no actual power to enact the things the devotees want. Then you're left with God(s) saying 'Do X in my worship' and the priest basically just relaying that to the general populace and organising the things required for that to occur. Which is not particularly proactive and excellence in this area still does bugger all because the God(s) can still just go "meh, ain't doin' what you ask, but I'll punish you if you stop".

                                                    ---

                                                    If we're playing a game and a PC is playing up the Priest-King Zenith archetype, what actions to they take in a game session that is meaningfully different to the Priest-King archetype or the Philosopher-Moraliser or the Judge archetypes? What is unique about being a priest that doesn't boil down to claiming some divine link that is ultimately pointless as your divine link can't be used proactively.

                                                    ---

                                                    Alternatively: Why play a Priest-King rather than a King?

                                                            All I really want for prayer charms is the following:

                                                            1. Personal cult manipulation. Being able to set my own cult to intense prayer for a scene to give me some kind of bonus - enhanced mote regen, a discount on charm activation costs, whatever - for a climactic battle or something. I want to be able to sing a hymn to my glory, have my cult in the streets below sing along, and be empowered by the faith of my people as I battle in the sky against an abyssal threat - or whatever.

                                                            2. Deity resource manipulation. Being able to offer a deity a one-time intense spike in prayer in exchange for something. Being able to threaten turning his cult away from him in order to pray to a rival. Generally being able to manipulate - including enhance - the amount of prayer going somewhere by leading the actual rituals personally.

                                                            3. Being able to directly send a message to a deity using the prayer system. I don't mean begging or submissive please, necessarily. I mean opening a channel for one-way social influence in the exact same way as someone using Linguistics to send a social influence letter. I want to be able to communicate something to a deity - whether it's a whisper in his ear or a thundering rumble in his entire sanctum - through a properly-executed prayer. And I want to be able to use my normal Performance charms as social influence through that link.

                                                            So, in short, I want to be able to alter prayer revenue streams and be able to socially influence through prayer. Honestly, that could be handled by just two charms if you make the one that manipulates your own cult's prayer and the one that manipulate's another cult's prayer the same charm - being able to lead the cult of another exalt or deity in prayer to augment them just as well as augmenting yourself. But those are the two things related to 'prayer' that I definitely want included in the Performance charm trees.

                                                            Does anyone have any suggestions, criticisms, or points of clarification on this? Anything I've missed or failed to consider?
