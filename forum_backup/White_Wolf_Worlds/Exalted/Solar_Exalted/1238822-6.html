    Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz View Post
    Does Solars being chosen with morals in mind apply to other Celestials chosen by patrons? Ie Lunars and Sidereals? Or purely Solars?
    The Unconquered Sun is a war god so he's okay with killing in various contexts, but I don't know that he'd be able to fall in love with a sociopathic serial killer like BTK or John Gacy if he knew what sort of a person they were. The Unconquered Sun, of course, isn't omniscient so he might see a John Gacy type character doing something good or impressive and Exalt that person without ever realizing that the guy is a horrible serial killer with a basement full of dead children.

    Luna however has been presented as fickle but also a much darker being than the Unconquered Sun, so it seems reasonable to me that she could intentionally Exalt a mass murdering serial killer, viewing them as a "hunter of humans" in a a way that would make her deem that person worthy of Exaltation.

    As for the Maidens, it depends on how much control they have over their Exaltations. In some ways they've been presented as doing things because Samsara says they will do them, so if Samsara says they will Exalted person X that's what they're going to do. At the same time though, Sidereal are expected to work in a hierarchy alongside a lot of other people, so I imagine they would prefer not to Exalt true sociopaths who are have a difficult time feeling affinity for other people.

        Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz View Post
        Does Solars being chosen with morals in mind
        I still feel that we're not quite on the same page; that I'm not so much arguing that they're Chosen because they're moral, but that distinctly immoral people, people that could be reasonably assumed to go directly to using their power for their own benefit at the expense of people that are already at the bottom of the social ladder, or strongly infringe upon the lives of people at large, or simply express some venomous or antisocial hangups, would not be.

        At the same time as I'm arguing that the likes of Lyta and Havesh aren't quite as bad as they're made out to be (or at least aren't bad in the ways and for the reasons that many assume), with a small reference to Arianna hardly being bad at all.

        (One person whose write-up I would quite like to examine again is Ophiles Ses)

        I should also reiterate that my motive in assessing the criteria for selecting Solars and the manner in which they might conduct is constantly labouring under the need to distinguish them from what they are when fully corrupted by the Great Curse (in a manner more sophisticated than the corrupted Solars being ridiculously evil, and something that actually connects to how it works mechanically).

        Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz
        apply to other Celestials chosen by patrons? Ie Lunars and Sidereals?
        Sidereals have a duty to preserve the existence of the world while personally existing somewhat apart from it, that is bestowed upon them at birth, and that they're conditioned to for their entire lives up until Exaltation. I think that's a firm foundation for the majority of them consistently being... well, dedicated to an authority and interests outside of themselves, at least, and generally beneficial, even if the principles that they serve can be somewhat amoral.

        I currently think that the imperative behind Choosing Lunars is to bestow them with freedom, or at least a greater capacity for the pursuit of their own freedom. I do think that can say a lot about their possible backgrounds and how those might inform character, but overall feel that they should contain the greatest diversity in personality and aspirations among the Celestial Exalted, and be more accommodating to people that are primarily concerned with pursuit of their own interests. Even if I imagine that their powers will generally afford them a means of simply acquiring what they want directly, rather than generating systems for it.

        I think that there are legitimate implications to this that would make it a good thing that they cleaved to the Solars and adopted a role as guardians, and for the Sidereals to be nervous about the implications of a world in which they are unfettered and hold the mastery.

        EDIT: Oh, I'll also note that if I'm being led by example in assessment of the various kinds of Exalted, many of the Lunars are up to their necks in creating and benefiting from systems designed to exploit vulnerable people. They're not doing it in precisely the manner of the Scarlet Dynasty, of exploiting their labour to enrich themselves, but there is definitely a widespread trend among the Lunars for radicalising people that have been harmed by the Realm's policies and then expending those people to secure incremental advantages. I would definitely think that there are many Lunars, and possibly not exactly old Lunars, that have done more harm to more people that had enough to deal with already for their own benefit than Havesh or Lyta have.

        I don't condemn them for that, partially because I can sufficiently distance myself and partially because I sympathise with their position a little bit, but it would inform my perspective of the criteria of them being Chosen.

                        Anyway, the little subargument about the nature of the Sun: he is not a perfectly moral being. Never has been. He may be a perfectly virtuous being, but they're the virtues of Socrates and Aristotle, not the seven virtues of Catholic philosophy. There should be a little moral ambiguity at every step, even as the options for, say, continued human existence are probably those favored.

                        The Sun led a slave revolt, true. But on the other hand, the Primordials were cosmic beings to whom the Sun and his kin were, like, their working dogs. It's not good to mistreat such an animal, but you wouldn't let it have a vote.

                        The Incarna play the Games of Divinity and neglect their duties, but on the other hand they never had a say in taking those duties, see aforementioned slavery.

                        The Usurpation was probably necessary to keep Solars from wrecking the world, but it forever lessened it. The point here is there are often no objectively correct answers, just ones that happened to be the answers chosen.

                            Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz View Post
                            But as long as the baseline is low enough that I can have Solar thieves, Solar assassins, Solar tyrants, and Solar fanatics, it isn't really a problem, practically speaking. I don't really want to run Solar NPCs, or many NPCs generally, who are simply moustache-twirling evil.                             I've always been of the opinion that none of the Incarnae will choose someone of evil character. Rather they choose individuals who have something to offer Creation. None of the Incarnae are going to exalt a psychopathic serial killer, or rapist, or a genocidal tyrant. This is reinforced by the fact that we have dozens of examples of people exalted by the Incarnae, and none of them started out as "moustache-twirling evil" individuals. However, it is entirely possible that their power and circumstances turn them evil after exaltation (example Filial Wisdom). As for your list of wanting Solar's that are assassins and thieves, this is literally the archetype of the Night caste. Tyrants and fanatics are more of the gray area, because it depends on a lot of different factors. For instance, I don't see any of the Incarnae exalting a Tyrant despot who takes joy in oppressing and torturing his subject. However, a tyrant that cracks down on his kingdom to enforce stability and conquers surrounding kingdoms in brutal warfare, is someone the Incarnae might find useful.
