                                                Watched the 5 episodes whcih are subbed, yesterday (and props to this thread for that). Pretty cool. Really creative animation and vibrant designs, comedy gore, it really will parody anything, and it zips along at a fair old pace with absolutely no filler at all.

                                                About his Solarness, Saitama seems a hero who just trained and trained and unlocked his potential himself and is *just* a human ("Humans don't fly" when asked why he doesn't fly!). So that's like Solars... At the same time, he kind of seems to playfully parody with skepticism the idea of people just unlocking their potential by effort (100 push ups, 100 sit ups, 100 squats, 10 km run!) in a way that Solars just play with more unironic sincerity. And he's so socially apathetic, not a character whose fits burn with rage for social justice! But then yeah, it is seem like, partly, a parody of shounen. Different genres do not translate 100% (or even 50%, 20%...)!
