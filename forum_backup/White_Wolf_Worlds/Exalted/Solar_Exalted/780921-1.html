                                If you're ST is fine with it, then they're fine with it. Survival is as good a place to put it as anything else.

                                If you want to try and do it in a way that is at least somewhat "canonically" acceptable, it's harder. The most basic options are:

                                1. Sorcery or Sorcerous Workings. You said you didn't want Sorcery, yes, but I'd suggest you consider Sorcerous Workings. You don't even need to perform them yourself; you can find a Sorcerer somewhere who performs a big ritual where you wear a wolf-pelt under the light of the full moon while he chants arcane runes from the forging of the world, and bam, after that you can turn into a wolf. This is probably the simplest way to get shapeshifting "inherently". Sorcerous Workings can do just about anything, and "the old man of the mountain imbued me with the spirit of an animal so I can take its shape" is a natural concept for a Sorcerous Working.

                                2. Evocations. You mentioned armor evocations; cloaks of animal feathers or fur that allow you to take on their shapes are also mythologically appropriate and appear in a lot of settings. (If you had to do it as a Solar charm, perhaps you could base it off of this -- a Craft or Occult charm that lets you bring out the power of such garments to assume their form. It somewhat fits within Craft and Occult in that respect, although usually you'd do it by actually making an artifact or performing a sorcerous working.)

                                3. There's a Solar charm in Integrity that lets you gain the charms of a god you champion (or defeat.) Be the champion of a wolf-god. Gain the charm he uses to turn into a wolf as part of his blessings. Or defeat a wolf-god in order to gain his power, either way. An Eclipse could also do it without this.
