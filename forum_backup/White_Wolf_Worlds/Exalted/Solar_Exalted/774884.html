    Solar XP, how much are you getting?

    11-26-2015, 01:04 PM
    I have not yet been able to start properly planning a game, let alone get my groups schedule sorted out to play it (heck, I still have a ton of material I want to translate for them to read first). So, I'm curious about how easy is Solar XP to get in practice.

    Those of you who managed to get a game going, how is it working in terms of getting the SXP rewards? Has it been easy to get both rewards? Or is it more common to get only one of them? Is it common for a few player to get no SXP in a session?

    In short, I want any information you care to share about how much SXP people are getting in your games.
