                        There is a big problem with this discussion. Everyone is using their own definition of prayer to justify their position, most aren't even close to what prayer is in Exalted.

                        In Abyssals it makes references to prayers used to threaten and intimidate both gods and ghosts including at least one charm that actually makes use of the Prayer Action. This clearly indicated that prayer is not an act of supplication or even begging by definition. Prayer is exactly one thing a method of communicating with a being capable of hearing prayers that provides a Cult rating. Any other definition is not at all useful to this discussion.

                        Under this definition the most obvious charm is in fact, the being I'm praying to cannot ignore me if capable of hearing my prayer. I fact Abyssals currently get exactly that as part of a charm.

                        So long as we get clear guidelines of what spirits can and can't do right out of the core book, I'm cool with prayer providing no direct benefit because ST's would have enough to make it up on their own. In which case keep prayer charms about manipulating the communication with spirits.

                                        It seems to be the old "faith versus knowledge" thing I see in other areas. Where faith is belief in something, regardless of evidence. Healing with Medicine is healing because of how the Solar doctor knows how a person can be put together right (their Ability in Medicine) and how to arrange their Solar-flavored Essence to achieve that via their skills (Solar Exalted expression through knowledge and implementation of skill).

                                        Faith healing is healing despite knowing what goes on. It's as I gather defined by using hope mojo to get stuff going an being ignorant of the process, just that divine will made it happen.

                                        The issue with this for Solars is a fewfold:

                                        - It means the Solar is getting things done by having someone else do it for them. Solar Exalted the Exalts who are peerless at acts of skill. Healing people is an act of Medical skill and the Solar who wants to do that already has a blazing miracle burning in their soul to go and do that without having to go ask a god to do it for them.

                                        - It turns Performance and prayer into a catch-all power, since apparently you can just beg the right god hard enough and they'll do shit for you, rather than use your god-given supreme agency to do it your damned self better.

                                        - A bit with above, the god you got power from and represent is the boss. You don't go about asking gods to heal things because of faith in them. You tell them to do them because you represent their boss.

                                        - It assumes "faith" on itself is menaingful at all. That it has some nice mojo to it that there's something valuable in it inherently. Which has never been the case in Exalted. Cults are described oftentimes as more or less protection rackets or acts of corruption. The Immaculate calendar exists as a bargaining chip. Prayer isn't soemthing that's valuable other then the sense it grants gods power and genereates stuff they want. Just in the same way a steady flow of money does for mortals. Prayer is a resource which gods use to get what they want. It's currency, not some magic compulsion. And gods, like any other potential bully or politican, are free often to just pocket the money and do nothing anyhow in response. It's no power over gods anymore than bribe money is to politicans.

                                        So for me, that "faith healing" isn't anything more than a fluff is an "of course that's what it is." It's either you as a Solar using your inherent knowledge to make people pray to you more, or it is you using your bribes with gods who do that sort of thing to maybe do it for you. But ambiguous "faith heals" things are just not how Exalted's metaphysics appear to work. Faith is nothing but a tool, not some special mojo fairy that exists as long as you believe in it real hard.

                                                            I am not using my own definition of prayer. My own definition of prayer is not the Christian definition and therefore would be foreign to many of the participants. They would not understand things like meaningful sacrifice and geasa in return for favor. I am therefore using the Christian definition, which is to beseech action from a being greater than yourself to intercede on your behalf offering nothing but your devotion.

                                                            There is functionally no difference in Exalted if the Charm includes an elaborate performance or if it is delivered clinically. But that's where players get to make choices about their characters. Taking away those choices by trying to pin down Charms as this OR that is stupid when they can easily function as this AND that.

                                                            The Abilities themselves need to be tightly defined. The Charms should follow. Granted, I realize this means Lunars and Alchemicals will provide their own set of headaches. Which is what it is, but it's not a reason to try to straightjacket Charm thematics. Realistically, Lunars and Alchemicals should have Charms that traipse across Ability boundaries when and where appropriate.
