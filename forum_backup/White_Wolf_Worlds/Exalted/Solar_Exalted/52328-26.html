    Originally posted by John Mørke View Post

    It's not an argument. The more powerful the god, the more someone would want to pray to it, and the more likely that it's going to ignore that prayer, because there are hundreds or thousands more that it is going to receive.
    In actual play, I've found this to be pretty inaccurate, at least so far as it describes the way PCs interact with the gods. Players aren't interested in trying to petition the big gods very often, but they're immensely interested in trying to get the attention of local gods for things like, say:

    -"God of this mountain, are there any troops lurking in ambush on you?"
    -"God of the local roads, confound the people trying to flee from me!"
    -"God of these fields, bless my people's harvest and help them flourish."
    -"God of the local smallpox outbreak, quit being a jerk, dude."
    -"God of the nearby waterways, so, I'm looking for this boat that went down..."

    And so on - and for almost all of these, it's the local gods (the ones connected to the phenomenon in question), who have relatively sparse resources, who are being reached out to. Honestly, it's difficult to explain in many cases why the gods wouldn't jump to answer these calls, when the Solars could offer what is (for them) a pretty trivial expenditure that is (for the gods) a pretty huge boost in how much prayer they get - selfishness and greed seem like they'd be pretty powerful motivators to respond immediately.

    ***

    There's a lot of "Who does the Unconquered Sun pray to?" conversation in the past few pages, which is weird to me, because, like... do any of the gods pray to other gods? Is that a thing? That seems kind of counter to the whole "We need humans as prayer batteries" deal.

                All I really want for prayer charms is the following:

I mean opening a channel for one-way social influence in the exact same way as someone using Linguistics to send a social influence letter. I want to be able to communicate something to a deity - whether it's a whisper in his ear or a thundering rumble in his entire sanctum - through a properly-executed prayer. And I want to be able to use my normal Performance charms as social influence through that link.

Honestly, that could be handled by just two charms if you make the one that manipulates your own cult's prayer and the one that manipulate's another cult's prayer the same charm - being able to lead the cult of another exalt or deity in prayer to augment them just as well as augmenting yourself.                         The thing is that religion has not really ever presented as anything but the opiate of the masses, in a sense.

                        The Immaculate Philosophy is a social control program created to elevate the Terrestrial Exalted, retard the will of the populace towards potential Celestial Exaltation, keep gods in the pockets of the Sidereals and to enforce order upon the world.

                        The Cult of the Illuminated is the result of a drug-addled hippy's doomsday cult being co-opted into a Solar traiing conspiracy and indoctrination programme.

                        The Hundred Gods Heresy is in the end a series of ponzi schemes, protection rackets or bribery chains. Gods normally only do the jobs they do because they have to or get paid to do it and corruption with no consequence results in cults.

                        The cult of Autochthon is pretty utilitarian. Gods and humans do get along there, there is actual reverence of the Great Maker and his subroutines, but note that in the end, it's still an expectation somewha to reciprocity. Humans worship the pantheon, the pantheon does its job. It is part of the great system, a fuel. Faith itself doesn't bouie things up, prayer's generative properties do.

                        The ancestor cult is more or less the above thing with the Hundred Gods Heresy, but even more cynical.

                        Faith in Exalted isn't some special source of power in itself. It is not a praiseworthy source of anything in itself. Believing really hard in stuff doens't go and make it real. Prayer is a source of resources and power. It is the currency of the divine. Zeniths as priests help to mediate this. They create situations that use this resoruce to their advantage. As priests of the Sun, they already have the highest authority to call on to get what they want and are given Exaltations to make that manifest. Other gods are in theory deferrent to the authority vested into the Zenith as the voice fo their boss. No matter how much folks decide they really believe in it, the world works how it works and any outcome of prayer is convincing the gods that it's worth their time to act on those prayers.

                        I guess it might just be me thinking religion in the real world is not so much better, but Exalted investigates most politcal organizations through a lense of real politk. Religions don't get some special pass because "faith is important." It never has been stated as such in Exalted as a special place that needs protection or power, anymore than love, morality, humilty or niceness would (as in it doens't).

                                        Faith in Exalted isn't some special source of power in itself. It is not a praiseworthy source of anything in itself.
                                        In itself is the important bit. The solidity of human belief was real useful in maintaining the existence of world against the onslaught of the Wyld and Fair Folk. Apparently.

                                        Faith is real powerful in this game's world. Because that's real. The setting tends to call belief by more morally multihued terms like Conviction and Willpower rather than faith because they lead to more morally interesting places and a world more in more colors than black, white and grey.

                                        Faith as a supplement to real knowledge and real know how is a force. Faith as a substitute, not so much. That's where any power that edges into the territory "Oh, well, I don't know anything, I just believe real hard" has problems (for any Exalted, but especially the heroes of being skillful and knowing stuff).
