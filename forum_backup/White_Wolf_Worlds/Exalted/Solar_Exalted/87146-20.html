                    Originally posted by hippokrene View Post
                    That said (because, I think you're suggesting this) if introducing Solars as heroes is off-putting to the majority of player, then it's probably bad for the game line as they're the core splat.
                    Solars as heroes is perfectly acceptable. Narrowing down the definition of heroism the way you want it... not so much off-putting as a needless restriction that may actually harm the thesis of the game.

                    Just so we're on the same page: I oppose making this a certain and definite thing not because I don't want to see Solars played that way*, but because throughout the years I've grown fond of many different, often conflicting points of view espoused by different factions and types of beings in the setting, and am wary of anything that could make those viewpoints seem invalid by default.

                    *In fact, the most fun I've ever had with Exalted was when I played as straight a good guy as humanly possible, though he was an Orichalcum Caste Alchemical rather than a Solar. I had a blast with him, and would play him again, or reuse his personality for a Solar in 3e, in a blink of an eye. Still, I don't think my experience would be any richer if Alchies were penned as straight good guys by default, rather than champions of morally ambiguous totalitarian states, and I don't think playing a Solar expy of him would be made any better by a definite statement to this effect.
