                Originally posted by Sakii View Post

                Ah, so its about scale not effect. Gona have to remember that.
                Its a bit of both. When you read the Ambition section it says that it is about power and scope. It then scales by Circle. You then balance out the area you want to cover with the extent of the change you want to cause. It mentions, for example, that Terrestrial Circle Workings should deal with the natural world somehow, not with "supernatural" stuff (its a bit weird on that part), it then goes on to say that if you want to do the most radical change allowed by this Circle it would be restricted to a chamber of a structure, or one single being.

                Lets say you want to change people into Übermensch.

                With Terrestrial Circle you can change one person

                With Celestial Circle you can change an entire village, or a neighborhood of a city and they can fly.

                With Solar Circle you can change the the whole country and besides flying they can shoot laser from their eyes.

                (Something like that, didn't really consider specifics)

                Its a balance act. The less drastic the effect the more area it affects. And then the values just escalate upwards as you ascend the Circles.

                Like you can bless a village and its surroundings to have bountiful crops at the Terrestrial Circle. You can probably do a whole Direction with Solar Circle, if that is all that you want.

                                    Originally posted by SoulGambit View Post
                                    But seriously though. Lunars banding together to get someone to do a Solar 2 working to curse the blessed isle feels like a much more productive use of time than "send wave after wave of barbarians at their satrapies."
                                    I'm going to have to recheck the rules to see how close you need to be to the thing you're trying to affect, even considering the point that a single working having overt effects on a continental landmass is out of sorts.

                                    The idea of certain Lunars strategically and surgically doing that kind of thing is probably valid, but kind of the whole point is that you don't have free ride over an empire run by thousands of hostile Exalted.

                                    Likewise, it would be pretty darn terrible if workings just turned into the new form of cruise missiles that somebody could just sit in their fortress building and lobbing out across the entire setting.

                                    I'd say that's really a basic answer to the original question; because the setting is not intended to be something over which sorcerers run roughshod, and to whatever hopefully minor extent that the rules for workings could be extended into being able to do so, the Orichalcum Rule applies.

                                    Sorcery is great, but I feel as though, at the very least, there's a point at which trying to use it to do every little thing means you're distracted from the cabal of kung-fu masters who come up to punch your heart out.
