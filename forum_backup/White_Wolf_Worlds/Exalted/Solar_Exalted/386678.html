    Solars, what foreign magic are you most excited for?

    01-30-2015, 05:33 PM
    One of the unique things about the Solar Exalted is the ability to take the commonly available sources of external magical power (Sorcery, Martial Arts, Evocations) and take them up to the highest level possible. With 3E coming around the corner, what are you going to bee-line towards with your first character? This is, of course, assuming your ST doesn't just drown you in XP and you have to be choosy.

    Personally, I am most excited about the new Sorcery system. Sorcery in Exalted is how I always wanted a magic system to be, but could never find it: Slow but extraordinarily powerful, a strategic resource as well as a tactical weapon or tool, and what makes me even more excited is the concept of Sorcerous workings that let me place powerful, potentially permanent spells on places, people or things.

          For me...Sorcery and Evocations. I have a Twilight concept in mind: a big burly Lookshyan smith/engineer who is primarily going to be using Solar Brawl as his combat ability, so MA is not at the top of my priority list (though at some far-off point, his interest in natural Essence patterns is going to push him towards Prismatic Arrangement of Creation Style). He's likely going to start as a sorcerer, having been trained in certain applications of the art in his mortal life, and I want to play with Evocations he'd unlock on his smashfists.

          I've thought of him being the sort of person to be riding a Cloud Trapeze above the battlefield as his Circle engages a foe, relaying strategic information to them before casting Invulnerable Skin of Bronze or Incomparable Body Arsenal and swan-diving from a great height into the fray.

          Part of my relative disinterest in MA is this concept, and part is due to the brand-newness of Evocations and the total Sorcery execution revamp (I guess I don't think MA will be paradigmatically changing as much as Sorcery is).
