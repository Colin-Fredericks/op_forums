    What are the iconic Solar effects?

    12-26-2017, 07:16 PM
    In the thread If you could change the Solar Charmset, there's a lot of interesting discussion on what kinds of alterations people would like to do to the Solar Charmset. The purpose of the thread is to streamline the set for inclusion in a homebrew setting that still uses the Exalted 3e mechanical engine (at least I think so?).

    However, I think the opposite question is also interesting. If you were to try to recreate the Solar Exalted in another setting, or in another system, or just trying to get to the core of their themes, what effects do you think need to be there? This could be individual Charms, or an effect that several Charms create together. It could also be something covered by Backgrounds or Merits, like the propensity to recover their rightful Artifacts from their previous incarnations' graves.

    Off the top of my head, I personally think that some kind of perfect defense (Heavenly Guardian Defense), some kind of unarmed knockback effect (Heaven Thunder Hammer), Craftsman Needs No Tools, Worshipful Lackey Acquisition and Tiger-Warrior Training Technique are important aspects of the Solar idea and need to have some sort of representation in any translation of them.

                  I don't think adaptability is particularly Solar. They're not bad at being adaptable, but Lunars are the masters of adaptation.

                  I'd say that Solars are the best at a finite range of skills, at which they shine brighter than any other. Which skills those are vary from Solar to Solar, but probably some two to five Abilities would become a particular Solar's domain of excellence.

                  Layer that specific excellence with some supernatural toughness. Add a bit of holy might against beings of darkness. Add a bit of predestiny and divine authority, and you have a Solar.


                  To make a Solar-ish in another system...

                  I think I could do something (in my mind) similar in World of Darkness, using the Arete rules in Mirrors, with a small number of Simple Charms brought forward. Sunder's list is pretty good, I think. That would give the amazing skill talent, at the very least. Probably some extra dots of Merits for a signature fighting style (noting that they're only up to five dots worth of "moves" rather than whole Martial Arts Charm trees, but still signature moves for the character)

                  I think I could do something (in my mind) similar in D20 by using one of the variant rules meant for tiny parties: Every level-up, you gain levels in two classes, taking the better skills, saves, HP, and BAB between the two, plus the special powers of both class levels.

                  I think I could do something (in my mind) similar in D6 by giving extra starting skills dice/pips, one or two supernatural attribute dice for free, and a house rule that selected skills are half as expensive to level up, chosen per character.

                  None of those would be an exact fit, but I think they'd fill the same role, in the contexts of their own universes.

                                      I don't think "much more flexibility is resting on the shapeshifting" is necessary to make them adaptable, because shapeshifting as is, is extremely adaptable. Most Lunars are at least decent at combat (because they can turn into a big animal that's decent at combat), decent at stealth (because they can turn into small, sneaky animals. Or just turn into an animal because it's a good disguise), and the majority of them are also good at getting around because they can turn into birds.

                                      I never felt, in 2nd ed, that Lunar charms made my character adaptable. So unless shapeshifting is suddenly much more expensive or difficult or something (which, other than the totem form, I sincerely doubt), they're still going to be more adaptable than Solars, regardless of how their charms work.

                                      In fact, shapeshifting is going to make them better at combat than it used to, just because the animal stats are much buffer. I used to frequently turn into a bear for combats, and bears just got much better.


                                      But anyway.
                                      I find Blazing Solar Bolt to be among selections of what is iconic.
                                      Yeah, this kind of thing.

                                      Solars are two main things; 1)superhuman, 2)infused with the power of the sun. There are other lesser themes (like kingship, or the ability to create order out of chaos), but those are the two primary ones.

                                      The first can easily be represented in a different system by a completely different mechanic. Giving them high base attack, mega-charisma, 80% stats in athletics, whatever is appropriate for the system. A superhero system's rules would likely be fine.

                                      (It should probably be noted that Exalted is a specific genre, and things like Graceful Crane Stance represent superhuman ability in that genre. So, a system that represents a different genre would give Solars rather different abilities)

                                      The second is a bit more specific. Things like generating bolts of blazing light isn't just Blazing Solar Bolt. Light-themed effects are found in lots of charms, especially but not exclusively in combat.
                                      So having Solars shoot bolts of light out of their bows/surround their sword in blazing light/surround themselves in light as they power up etc, would be pretty important.
