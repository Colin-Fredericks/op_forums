    Investigation:

    Reconstruction of Lost Memories
    This charm allows a Solar to use telltale clues to imagine a the exact layout of a crime scene that was erased by the passage of time. After using this charm, the Solar knows exactly what the scene looked like exactly after the crime and can investigate the imagined room as if it were real.

        Brawl:

        Hundred Flowing Rivers Defense
        The Solar takes a defensive posture and expands her awareness. Whenever the Solar parries a close-range attack, she gains one momentum. Every attack she parries after the first grants an additional point of momentum, cumulative up to the Solar's Essence. In addition, if the Solar performs a decisive attack on an opponent whose attack she has parried during her previous action, she treats her opponent's momentum as if it were the lowest of all the close-range opponents surrounding her, regardless of its actual value.

            Thrown:

            Thousand Leaves Misdirection
            This charm supplements a Thrown surprise attack. If the attack fails, it still manages to distract the target. Until the target's next action, all surprise attacks against him gain a number of bonus dice equal to the Solar's Essence, and any successful attack against the target grants one additional point of momentum.

                    Performance:

                    Lonely Night Cantata
                    The Solar's voice drowns out all others. So long as this charm remains active, all actions that take place within earshot of the Solar and require auditory stimuli suffer from an external penalty equal to the Solar's Essence. This includes all verbal social influences, as well as supernatural powers, such as the charms of the Silver-Voiced Nightingale style and the powers of the angyalka.

                        Thrown:

                        Mundane Arsenal Refinement
                        The Solar can gain munitions from whatever she has on hand. Whenever the Solar needs a thrown weapon, she may grab a random nearby object that qualifies as a light weapon. The weapon has the Short Range and Blunt tags. It also gains a number of additional weapon tags equal to the Solar's Essence. The Solar chooses which tags to add when learning this charm and when her Essence increases. The choice is permanent.

                            Stealth:

                            Sunset Shadow Venom
                            This charm supplements a surprise attack and numbs its damage so that the target does not notice the attack or its effect until it is too late. If the attack is successful, the target loses one momentum at the start of her action. If the charm is used to supplement a decisive attack, the target instead takes an additional -1 wound penalty. This charm lasts for a number of the target's actions equal to the Solar's Essence.

                                Performance:

                                Infinite Cornucopia Sonata
                                The Solar organizes a great festival to the harvest, trade, crafting, fertility, health, mining, fishing or any other local activity. This festival may last for a number of weeks equal to the Solar's Essence rating. So long as the Solar personally performs at least once a day, all those participating in the festival add the Solar's Essence as automatic successes to their relevant rolls. This charm also ensures that competitions of any kind between participants of the festival will not lead to any form of negative intimacies. The festival has lingering effects that last for the rest of the season and make things slightly easier or more convenient for the locals. Harvests are more bountiful, merchants are more likely to visit the village, children are born healthier and so forth. This charm can only be used once per season in any given town.

                                    Presence:

                                    Enduring God-King Order
                                    This charm can be used to supplement an action to instill an intimacy into someone. Whenever someone attempts to erode the target's intimacy or get them to act against that intimacy, the Solar may reflexively roll Charisma + Presence, adding a number of automatic successes equal to the Solar's Essence, to counter the social influence. The Solar's roll cannot benefit from the Presence Excellency or other charms.

                                                Investigation:

                                                Astute Watchman Methodology
                                                This charm allows the Solar to appoint a number of assistants up to her Essence rating. The Solar may use her Investigation charms through these assistants when they investigate a crime scene on the Solar's behalf. This allows the Solar to effectively investigate multiple crime scenes at the same time, but she does not know the results of any of these investigations until her assistants report back.

                                                        Sail:

                                                        Fallen Seas Navigation
                                                        The Lawgiver knows all the currents of Creations, including those who that flow beyond the world. At the dawn or dusk of Creation, the Solar can activate this charm to sail her vessel into the oceans of the Wyld, the Underworld, Yu-Shan, or Malfeas, regardless of her current position. If she is in another realm already, she can use this charm to return to Creation. This charm can be used without restriction during Calibration, including to travel from one outworld Realm to another.
