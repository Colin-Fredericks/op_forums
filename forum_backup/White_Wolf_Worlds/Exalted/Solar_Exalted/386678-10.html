        I get the idea that, with Charms representing the kind of hero you are, each tree is home to several archetypes within that ability. Like, you might want to be "the best captain at sea" without necessarily being "the fastest boat pilot"

        So, not only you might not be able to get all the dozens of published Charms for a given Ability (especially since you likely want at least a few Charms in other Abilities too) but you might not want all the Charms in there, simply because not all of them are appropriate building blocks for your concept. This edition is supposed to very encouraging of homebrew, after all, so even if you get tons of XP over time to expend on Charms, you might want to have custom Charms that go well with you concept, instead of having your ultimate strongman become also a runner because you got all the strongman Charms already.

        This allows a circle to have, say, 4 people focusing on melee, yet having different playstyles and feel in combat.You could have one fencing with a rapier, another dual-wielding axes, another with a sword and shied, and a another with a huge two-handed hammer. And they could be different in play via multiple things - different stats and tags on their weapons, different Evocations, and different selections of melee Charms, resulting in 4 characters with quite distinct approaches to combat, despite all going deep into melee.

                                            I have a sneaking suspicion that one of the differences between this edition and second edition in regards to why there are so many charms has to do with developing characters organically. As weird as it sounds I imagine writing Charms under the idea that they are representations of what your character does(internal) vs what your character can do(external) means you're already tapping into a hell of a lot more narrative space to play with mechanically. That in and of itself potentially leads you to simply not exploring any given charm tree of an Ability because the focus wont be on make sure I have mechanical effect X,Y,Z available in my Charm list.
