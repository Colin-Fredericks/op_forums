                        I build my personal Immaculate Order and its dogma around a few core ideas.
                        1. It's a completely artificial faith, created from the ground up by incredibly long-lived, patient, and skilled Exalts, and immortal gods.
                        2. The faith exists to legitimize and reinforce Dragon-Blooded rule.
                        3. It also exists to turn the world against the Lunars and those few Solars still out there.

                        With that in mind, I imagine that the "signs" of the Anathema are taught to all, in the same way Western culture tends inculcate imagery of the devil. Millions of children all over Creation know about Caste marks (brands of their treachery and evil!) anima banners (perversions of the pure expressions of elemental anima flux!), and so on.

                        Such is the success of the Order that even in places that aren't controlled by them, mortals have heard of the terrifying skin-stealers, and the danger of those who stole the power of the Sun.

                        When the farmer in a loyal Realm satrapy is helped by the young girl with a glowing gold circle on her forehead, he's more likely to run in fear than thank her. That's the challenge the returning Lawgivers are up against, and that's the challenge the Lunars face every day.
