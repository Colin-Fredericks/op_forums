                                            ​It's an interesting question. It also puts me in mind of ancient Rome, where people would have often liked theatre, but had a very low opinion of actual actors.

                                            ​Your example of Asian cultures also has me wondering about variations depending on who is playing the instrument and why; the difference between the monarch playing the bells in ceremony to generate a tone harmonious with the universe, and the person who plays an instrument full time, whom people will denigrate even while they listen in.

                                            ​So I wonder if it's possible to reconcile what the books might say with the notion that some of those people might love the performance, but not the performer.

                                            ​Still, I don't think that your idea would be a major contradiction of anything fundamental to the setting, so even if the books never portray it, if it's something you know of and want to portray, you know best what to do with it.
