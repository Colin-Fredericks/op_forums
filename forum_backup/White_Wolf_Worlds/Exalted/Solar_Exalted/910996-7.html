            Wasn't referring to that particular niche. I was referring to the general 'Exalt type to Magical Material' correspondence. The fact that Abyssals were the group most fundamentally comfortable with wearing armor and wielding weapons made from screaming tormented souls said something meaningful about their themes. The policy of no longer allowing mass produced artifacts to be a thing should assist with that a great deal.

                    __________________________________________________ __________

                    The Mask of Winters sat on his massive throne, carved from the blackest basalt and decorated with the countless skulls of his fallen enemies. There, in his fortress atop the Juggernaut, surrounded by a legion of his finest soldiers and most trusted advisers, his power was at its peak. In front of him knelt one of his Deathknights.

                    "Ahh, Unending Darkness of the Final Day. Of all of my followers, you are among the most loyal. You repeatedly demonstrate initiative and wisdom, engaging in tasks for the benefit of Thorns and myself. You have even repeatedly helped your fellow Deathknights. Indeed, you are one of my finest and darkest gems. You may rise and explain to me, for what reason have you come before me this day."

                    Unending Darkness slowly stood. His gaze turned, taking in the vast array of ghostly soldiers and the gallery of onlookers whose only task, it seemed, was to make the Mask of Winters more imposing and fearsome. Finally his gaze turned to the Deathlord. "My master. It is with great regret that I come to ask you for a new daiklave."

                    The Mask of Winters froze, and as he did, so too did all of the gathered watchers in the vast chambers. "A new daiklave?" The ancient and terrible witch-king asked slowly. "And just what happened to the one I gave you?"

                    The deathknight furtively looked around and then pulled out a sheathed sword. "It is here my lord."

                    "Yes. Ten Thousand Thieves of Life and Honor. Did you know I forged that weapon when the world was young? I spent nearly a century seeking out the most wicked thieves in all of Heaven and Earth, those whose thefts and lies brought an end to loved ones, to cities, to empires. For ten years did I stand at the heart of Black Volcano, smelting those souls down that they might become the base for my greatest weapon. And now you say you seek a new sword? Pray tell, what mistake did I make when I created this one?"

                    Unending Darkness' gaze slowly slipped to the ground where it remained. He let out a small sigh and then looked up into the frozen rictus-mask of his master. "It... it screams at me, my lord."

                    The Mask didn't move for several moments. "It... pardon, what? It screams?"

                    "Yes," Unending Darkness stated. "It screams at me," he repeated, emphasizing the word. "Honestly, it's rather aggravating. So, if it's not too troublesome, could I perhaps get a different sword?"

                    "It screams?" The Mask said, seemingly incredulous. "Well of course it bloody well screams. It's made out of ten thousand souls trapped in eternal torment after all. I mean, I'd certainly be screaming if that happened to me."

                    "Well, it might sound obvious, but I'll have you know, I haven't had a good nights rest in months. Every time I try to fall asleep, this thing," he said, shaking the sheathed sword, "starts screaming at me. And that lack of sleep is starting to make me rather cross."

                    The Mask of Winters seemed to be searching for something and then finally asked, "Well, have you maybe tried telling it to stop screaming?"

                    "Of course!" Unending Darkness responded, exasperated. "But when I tell it to stop screaming, that just makes it scream at me even more! I understand ten thousand souls trapped in eternal torment wouldn't exactly be happy, but even still I think it has a very terrible attitude for a sword. I mean..."

                    He stopped speaking for a moment and the Mask of Winters urged him on. "Yes...?"

                    "Well, like last week, for instance. I was trying to sneak into the palace of the Walker in Darkness. I was thinking that maybe I could find some dirt or useful information on him." He looked up to see the Mask of Winters nodding.

                    "Oh yes, that would certainly make me quite pleased. So what happened?"

                    "Well, in the middle of my sneaking around, this thing," he shook the sheathed sword again, "started screaming for no reason. It blew my cover and I ended up getting my arm chopped off. I mean, yes, Seven-Degrees Physician put a new one me later, but I swear this sword was laughing at me the whole time."

                    As if to confirm it, the sword made a moan that really did sound rather like laughter if you cocked your ear just right.

                    "See? Really, I think it hates me."

                    "Oh, nonsense," The Mask of Winters said soothingly. "I mean, of course it hates you, but it hates everyone. And even if it does prove to be a slight bother when your sneaking around, you have to admit, it is a very good sword. I spent ten years making it after all."

                    "Well it certainly is good if you ignore the screaming but even then, that's not the only problem..." Unending Darkness paused and looked around at the gallery of ghosts, all of whom were doing their best to pretend they weren't listening. He sighed and then leaned in closer and whispered, "The truth is, I haven't been able to have sex with my wife in almost three months."

                    The Mask of Winters was glad that his facial features were obscured by his mask. "Y-you don't say."

                    Unending Darkness continued, "Every time my wife and I start, well you know, doing anything, this sword starts moaning like it's trying to join in. At first we thought it was a little amusing, but now it just completely ruins the mood. It's definitely doing it on purpose." He roughly shook the sword again for good measure.

                    The Mask of Winters sat, silent and still as a statue.

                    "You know, last night I chucked it off the side of the Juggernaut."

                    "Y-you chucked Ten Thousand Thieves of Life and Honor, one of my finest creations off the Juggernaut? I spent ten years forging it!" The Mask of Winters was shocked.

                    "And then," Unending Darkness stubbornly continued, "I immediately went to my quarters and started engaging in affairs with my wife. But wouldn't you know it, not even five minutes later this thing," he shook the sword again as it suddenly began making lascivious moans, "suddenly popped back into my room, screaming and moaning worse than ever. The Maiden of the Mirthless Smile came and knocked on our door and told us to keep it down. It was quite embarrassing for everyone."

                    "Oh, oh yes. I remember I enchanted it so it would always return to its owner when lost," the Mask mumbled. "Well..."

                    "Can't I please get a new sword?"

                    The Mask of Winter sighed and slowly stood up. "Alright, alright. Come back here." He walked behind his massive throne and there was a series of crates and barrels. Out of one of them poked the handles of a half-dozen different daiklaves. The Mask of Winters reached down and grabbed one seemingly at random.

                    "This is Enshadowed by the Darkness, forged from jade which has never seen the light of the Unconquered Sun and combined wi-"

                    "Does it scream?" Unending Darkness suddenly interrupted.

                    "Wha-? Umm, not that I'm aware of," the Mask of Winters responded.

                    "Does it make moaning sounds?"

                    "I don't believe so."

                    "Will I finally be able to have sex with my wife while that sword is in the room?"

                    The Mask of Winters, obviously flustered, flailed his arms up and down. "Like, I guess?"

                    "Good, I'll take it." Unending Darkness reached out and grabbed the jade daiklave while placing Ten Thousand Thieves of Life and Honor in the Mask of Winters hands. Then he turned and quickly stomped out of the throne room.

                    The Mask of Winters stood there and stared down at the sword in his hand.

                    "Urr hurr hurr," it moaned. It definitely sounded like it was laughing.

                    The ancient and feared Deathlord sighed and then walked out from behind his throne. He slowly looked around at the countless ghosts and advisors. "Umm," he started to speak. Every eye in the place locked onto him immediately.

                    "Any murder-hobos here interested in a slightly used daiklave? I guess I should let you know beforehand, it screams. I did spend 10 years making it though."

                                Something I'll note about the 5-dot soulsteel Artifact we have in the core that was made by a Solar and was wielded by them and some Dragon Blooded is that it comes across as a fairly palatable example of the thing. I mean, it's powered by the souls of murderers and is used to punish the wicked.

                                ​I feel as though references to soulsteel being rarely used in the First Age was a matter of it being difficult for many Exalted to develop Artifacts that would have characteristics that they would be happy with; I could look on Black Wind as being a bit of a stretch to apply the powers natural to that material to a purpose that would be suitable for those kinds of heroes.

                                So I think Abyssals can naturally get more out of soulsteel if one imagines them as vastly more likely to explore its possibilities.

                                That and I feel that Volcano Cutter sets a strong precedent for the most powerful Artifact weapons often requiring associated manses to power them, especially their greatest Evocations, and I'd say that most of the good Abyssal manses in the Underworld have fallen under the power of the Deathlords.

                                I think for Evocations to give Artifacts some character is a much stronger way of distinguishing them and marking their associations then there ever was before. Hell, it might be stronger than bonestriders and necrotech; I'm pretty sure that Solars could cast those spells.

                                        Originally posted by Erinys View Post
                                        Except if Necromancy is just a different initation into otherwise mechanically identical Sorcery, without a separate spell-list then Solars and Abyssals are equally good at both Sorcery and Necromancy, which would be nonsensical IMO. Solars and Infernals should be the masters of Sorcery. Abyssals should be unequaled masters of Necromancy. Lunars and Sidereals should be unable to initiate past the first circle, while most Terrestrials and Infernals should have at least severe difficulty using it, if they can at all. That doesn't work if all spellcasting is the same, just with slightly different window-dressing.
                                        Here's how I'm guessing it'll work.

                                        Necromancy spells will have their own tag - Necromancy. They'll be split up into 3 spells and be right there alongside Sorcery. If you have a Necromancy Initiation, you'll be able to learn the highest level of spells you would normally be able to learn, but only if they have the Necromancy tag. At the same time, you wouldn't be able to cast spells of that highest level that don't have the Necromancy tag.

                                        Meanwhile, you would be able to cast all spells from lower Circles, regardless of whether they have the Necromancy tag or not.

                                        So, for example, take two Lunars. One has a Necromancy Initiation, the other has a Sorcery Initiation. Both have access to Second Circle Sorcery.

                                        The first Lunar, the Necromancer, can cast any Terrestrial level spell, regardless of whether it has the Necromancy tag or not. However, for Second Circle spells, he can only cast spells with the Necromancy tag. Meanwhile, the second Lunar can cast any spell of the Terrestrial Circle, regardless of its tags, but he can only cast spells that don't have the Necromancy tag.

                                        This means that you still make a choice, whether to focus on Necromancy or Sorcery, but it doesn't make Necromancy it's own style of not-Sorcery, and anybody can learn a few lower end Necromancy spells without having to dedicate themselves and picking up special Charms (Shadowland Circle Necromancy, Labyrinth Circle, Void Circle, as was the case in 1st and 2nd edition).

                                        This does run into the fact that it would be possible to have a Solar or Infernal capable of learning Third Circle Necromancy spells (if they have a Necromancy Intitiation) and likewise Abyssals could cast Solar Circle Sorcery (if they had a Sorcery Initiation), but I don't really see that as a huge issue. I don't think the setting is going to fall apart if there isn't hard-coded niche protection. Just because there was in prior editions doesn't make it the "best way" or necessary, though I'm open for reasons why it should remain in Third Edition.
