                    I see Dagger Wing Style as something that needs a tool to be used, like Archery or Single Point Shining Into the Void Style.                     So what if the same thing applies to the Immaculate Styles?

                    What if a Solar needs a fire-producing/controlling/immunity-granting tool to use Fire Dragon Style as easily as a Fire Aspect Terrestrial who can generate, control, and survive fire without a tool?

                    It would be just like how a Solar needs a winged-backpack Artifact in order to practice Dagger Wing Style.


                    But to be fair, I'm not saying that the Immaculate Styles should lack the Mastery keyword.

                    I think I heard that the devs/writers specifically WANT to leave the Mastery keyword out of the Styles? And I'm willing to wait and see how they do that. But I'm right there with everyone else when I say I have NO IDEA how they intend to balance the Styles without the Mastery keyword.

                    On the other hand... I don't care.

                    Someone was saying that if the Immaculate Styles are simply, straight-up denied any Mastery effects, that the Immaculate Styles would be the weakest in Creation... but, that's only true for the Solar Exalted. It's very much NOT true for the Dragon-Blooded.

                    And WHO are the Styles being made for, both in and out of the setting? The Dragon Blooded.

                    The game has ALWAYS (or at least since Second Edition) gone out of its way to reward an Immaculate stylist, in little ways, for being a Terrestrial Exalt rather than a Solar. There were some perks I don't remember, regarding the style weapons.

                    The Solar Exalted have EVERY OTHER Martial Arts Style in the world to enjoy Mastery. Plus, it's not like the Immaculate Styles would be alone -- there are also these weird SIDEREAL Martial Arts that play by different rules which don't favor the Solar Exalted the most.

                    Funnily enough, both the Immaculate and Sidereal Styles were made by the same Exalted Host! If the Sidereals could invent the Sidereal Martial Arts where they reign supreme over Solars, I'm willing to believe they can invent Immaculate Styles where Dragon-Blooded can achieve better results than Solars.

                    That's not the same thing as "Styles which allow Dragon-Blooded to fight Solars as superiors or even equals", mind you, because dice-pools, mote-pools, native Charms, XP-costs, and the Supernal Ability all remain a thing.

                                    You see, we've been presented with a new way to treat Martial Arts. They are Abilities that any "mortal" can practice, but only Essence users can extrapolate to supwrnatural power.

                                    I don't like the theme that someone can design an Ability that a Solar can't master. They might try and believe they can do it, but the greatness of the Solars' Essence will show they are wrong.
