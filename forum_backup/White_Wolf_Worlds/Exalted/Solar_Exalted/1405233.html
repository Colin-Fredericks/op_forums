    Solar Presence rewrite

    07-29-2020, 10:45 PM
    Do you like Solars but hate how their social charms were less than stellar shortly after the corebook launch, let alone after Lunars? Would you love it if the charms had a little more punch while maintaining some resemblance of balance? Well I got news for you: I got bored. Which means nearly 40 or so charms appeared out of the ether.

    What can you expect with this rewrite?
    • Rewriting of the Solar Presence tree from the ground up. Trust me: It needed it.
    • Oodles of charms that do brand new things, while Exalted vets enjoy a trip down memory lane with a few official and fan favorites.
    • Dedicated support for Manipulation focused Solars. No longer does Presence feel like Charisma: The Ability. You have plenty of support for your social actions and some very neat tricks of your own. Easily convince others to hand over the shoes, or tell them that the sky is purple they'll actually buy it.
    • Minimal charm copying. I only needed to copy two Lunar charms. Technically 3, but the third existed in a charm I made 10 years ago so hell with it that doesn't count.
    • Absolutely no play testing, as I just finished making these like an hour ago.

    Want to amaze those peasants? Then click here to find out in this one simple ability!

                                                  Personally I wouldn't use it because to me Presence is "Charisma: The Ability". I prefer the Charms with the explicit flavors of conning people, lying to them and manipulating them to stay in the realms of Larceny and Socialize.

                                                  Here's a critique of the first few Charm, might do more later if I have time:

                                                  Exemplar of Virtue Demonstration - should probably limit either the intensity of the Principle that can trigger this, or the intensity that can be instilled in others. For instance, should the Charm allow someone to take their Minor Principle and strengthen it to the Defining level in someone else who already has it at Major?

                                                  Begruging Respect Acceptance and Shameful Action Retort both feel properly Solar, good stuff.

                                                  Impassioned Discourse Technique & Shrouded Night's Tongue - There seems to be nothing preventing me from using Shrouded Night's Tongue without doing anything that would provoke penalties, so the Charm is basically E1, 3m for 3-5 non-Charm dice. Besides being way OP as dice adders go, it also makes Impassioned Discourse Technique worthless since they're both mutually exclusive and the latter does the exact same thing, except with 3-5 Charm dice instead of non-Charm dice.

                                                  Overlooking Innocent Transgressions - preventing someone from being offended by things that go against their defining without a chance to resist sounds roleplay-breaking, to be honest. Maybe increase the cost to more than 1wp or something if the target doesn't hate the Solar?

                                                  Faithful Words Prana - the wording was not clear to me, had to read multiple times to understand what it does. The Charm sounds more Lunar than Solar but otherwise fine.

                                                  Duplicitous Wordsmith Obfuscation - I don't like that it can suppress a Defining as easily as a Minor, otherwise fine.

                                                  Tripping the Bumbling Fool - Cool flavor, I'm just not sure about the "cannot face the Solar" effect, feels like on some characters that will be, well, out of character.
