                This seems massively inaccurate, Stephen.

                I've always loved Karal Fire Orchid, but her writeup in Zenith doesn't bear a lot of resemblance to what you just described. The Lookshy General Staff doesn't actually know Fire Orchid is a Solar yet. That's a big plot point in her backstory, because it ties into her other big plot point, which is that her mother and brothers are, in fact, keeping an open mind about things; her brothers have been sent to find her, not to just murder her on sight, but because her family was like "Okay, we know Fire Orchid, and she doesn't seem like the sort of person to make some sort of evil pact and steal power from the heavens, plus from what we can ascertain she's still being all heroic and shit. We're hella suspicious, and if it seems at all like she's become possessed by evil we're going to fucking end her, but first we're going to look her in the eye and hear what she has to say before we charge in with daiklaves swinging."

                I mean, people who aren't part of Gens Karal are probably not going to be that open-minded. Indeed, other members of Gens Karal who aren't specifically the progeny of Karal Linwei probably won't either. It's implied heavily that Fire Orchid's Exaltation is going to put a pin in Linwei's career no matter how this works out, and that the rest of the General Staff isn't going to be that pleased with her. But Linwei at least didn't just default to "let's murder the Anathema," although I'll concede she probably would have if Fire Orchid weren't her kid.

                It seems like when it comes to Solars and Lookshy, the situation is going to be very contextual. As things stand normally, they're not going to want anything to do with the Solar Exalted, because they are strongly ideologically opposed to the Solars even existing, let alone entering into alliances or even non-aggression pacts with them.

                However, Lookshy is also a lot more practical about that than the Realm is. Nexus is only about, what, a week upriver from Lookshy, but Lookshy isn't going to try and invade and crush them for harboring large numbers of Anathema the way the Realm would any city-state in its own sphere of influence. If the Mask of Winters saddles up Dreadnought and heads for Lookshy surrounded by a massive corpse-army and his entire Abyssal honor guard in kickass bonestriders, the General Staff may stroke their chins and seriously consider the offer if Dace shows up with his army and is like "You guys want to team up against the terrible unlife abominations?"

                It's all contextual. But as a matter of the "status quo" you probably don't have an in, because the Lookshyans still take their faith very seriously and their faith says "if it glows gold, kill it."
