    Solar Non-Lethality Charm (PEACH)

    01-02-2014, 02:13 AM
    So this is a bit of charm work to aid along those Martial Pacifists who don't feel like kung-fu. Because you should be able to make Kenshin using just melee charms, dammit. Or if you just want a duelist who doesn't kill his opponents all the time.

    To Battle Under the Laurel
    Cost: 1m; Mins: Melee 4, Essence 2;
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: Combo-OK, Merged
    Duration: One scene
    Prerequisite Charms: None
    To defeat without killing, to destroy without slaying. Such is the greatness of the Solars skill. So long as this charm is active, whenever a Solar reduces a foes to their final Incapacitated health level, that health level is always bashing, and no further levels of damage are done. This applies even to any poisons, diseases, environmental, or ongoing damage effects that originate from the Solar. Even bleeding wounds halt their flow as the opponent falls unconscious, and the there is no risk of infection even on the flithiest battlefields. Furthermore, they may recover from crippling wounds inflicted by an Exalt without any special measures, recovering at the normal rate for such an injury (if no time is given or the effect would be permanent, use mortal -4 health level healing times).

    Solars with this Charm and Compassion 3+ can choose to reflexively improve it at any time, transforming it into a costless Permanent effect. By suppressing their Compassion, they may deal damage as normal for a scene.

    Merged: Archery, Firearms, Martial Arts, Thrown

    Thoughts and comments?
