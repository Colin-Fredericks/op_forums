    Solar/bonus exp while still mortals.

    10-15-2016, 10:58 PM
    So my game has the players start out as mortal. In 2nd ed the bonus exp was more a house rule to give to players if they did something extra like maybe a journal or illustrations ect. In 3rd end we have a nice built in system with solar xp, that you can't spend on solar charms. So I know it's kind of ironic, but what are your thoughts on giving Solar exp out to mortals for things like intimacy adherance? Or for things like detailing a backstory? I'm thinking my group will only be mortals for 2 stories maybe. Or since its minuscule should I just make it exp? 3rd level stunt exp are no longer a thing right?

    Any thoughts on the subject?

          Based on developer comments, we know that Solar XP isn't called such "because only Solars get it" but because it is "the highest form" of XP -- and that others get it, with their own means rather than Solar Roles.

          As such I would say it is totally appropriate to permit your mortals an Expression Bonus for their intimacies and flaws, and a Role bonus for either ceding the scene or supporting someone else doing the thing they specialize at, or pursuing Roles that most match their focuses and abilities. So the socially-oriented mortal merchant might pick up a Role bonus for either Zenith intimacy-driven inspiration, hardship, or great deeds, or for Eclipse-style accords, diplomacy, and social maneuvering. Or if he's more mental, maybe he gets it for Night-style trickery through commerce and underhanded social dealings. Regardless,a +2 "That was cool and very in style and clever" for that is cool, as is "That was awesome pursuit of intimacy or demonstration of flaw".

              If they're only going to be mortals for 2 sessions, it doesn't really matter, tbh. So yeah, if you want to, just give them Solar XP. If you/they know what castes they're going to be, even give them their caste's role bonus, if you'd like to.

              The point of Solar XP is so that players don't feel they have to save all their XP for cool charms and it's a "waste" to spend it on anything else. For mortal characters, this isn't a big issue. If you think your players are going to want to hoard all their xp to spend on charms when they exalt, then I can see the point of giving them Solar xp.

                  In my game, which had an indeterminably long prologue ("you guys will Exalt when we're finished going over the basics of the system or whenever you do something particularly noteworthy, whichever comes later"), I decided that all xp earned by mortals counts as solar xp (in order to incentivize spending it instead of saving it up for Charms later). They could gain expression bonuses and the role bonus for ceding the spotlight, but no other role bonus.

                                          In the game where I had players start out as mortals and Exalt at the climax of the first story arc, I elected not to hand out Solar XP while they were still mortal, as I wanted them to feel they're not just much more powerful as Exalted, but also growing in power and gaining new tricks faster than they had as mortals.

                                          If I had a mortal character playing alongside the Exalted in a mixed game for any reason, I'd certainly not do it the same way, the guy would be fighting an uphill battle anyway without me hamstringing his exp gains.
