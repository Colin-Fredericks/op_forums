                                                Okay, looking at the Integrity charms, I think there are some that genuinely do help you resist powerful mind-altering effects made by things with fat dice pools (like Abyssals or Infernals or Sidereals... well, Sidereals have small dice pools, but, you know, Target Number 4). I mean, they're not like the old Elusive Dreams Defence, but that made social combat essentially a waste of time.

                                                Destiny-Manifesting Method downgrades (or makes you immune to, with the upgrade) "a reality-shaping or fate-weaving attack that would permanently alter her mind". So wacky Sidereal psyche effects may well be covered by that.
                                                Transcendant Hero's Meditation should also help against most wacky psyche effects.

                                                Steel-Heart Stance means, once per story (with a reset condition that might well come up occasionally), when persuaded to do something significant, you can just ignore the successful social influence.

                                                Righteous Lion Defence perfectly defends against any social effect that would cause you to act against a Defining Principle. One of the PCs in my game has this for her intimacy of "No Gods and no masters" which has made her immune to the Zenith's common huge-dice-pool social influence attempts to get people to join his religion and worship the UCS.

                                                Unhesitating Dedication is, essentially, the cap-breaking boost Irked was arguing for (on behalf of Sanctaphrax? Lack of avatars makes it hard for me to keep track of who's saying what), so it does actually exist. It only works to stop people eroding your Defining Intimacies though, once per intimacy per story.

                                                Invincible Solar Aegis protects (as long as you properly uphold a Defining Intimacy) against "Fiat-level alterations to her destiny. • Sorcerous curses such as the spell Corrupted Words. • Psychic assaults of any kind. • Magical attacks that would transform her body in some unwanted way." So that definitely protects against Psyche effects.

                                                To be honest, it looks to me like the wacky psyche effects of other splats is exactly what Solars are good at defending against. Regular, excellency-boosted social influence that doesn't go against their Defining Intimacies is what they have less defences against.

                                                Next I'll have a look at Psyche effects and whether they do indeed have ways to avoid them built-in, like Isator said.

                                                    Okay, I had a look at the Psyche effects, all of them explain how they can be defended against (generally by spending a bunch of wp, though in some cases it's only by using Transcendant Hero's Meditation... which is explicitly mentioned*) except for Essence-Laden Missive, and, as Sanctaphrax has said, Corrupted Words. Though I think that Corrupted Words is probably covered by some of the Integrity charms.
                                                    Some of them you can only resist if they go against Defining intimacies, or people present evidence as to why you're wrong, and if you spend WP to ignore Hypnotic Tongue you forget that they hypnotised you.

                                                    So, it seems to me like Solars don't really have much in the way of perfectly defending against stuff (except for outright magic curses), but do have various narrative ways to defend.
                                                    So, when faced with a mind-control throwing huge-dice-pool Celestial, I think that the Integrity Solar will break free after some narrative drama. This may work well, or may not, it depends.

                                                    It's certainly more interesting than the old "use Integrity charms, ignore". But it does probably rely on the devs only writing social effect charms that are interesting in of themselves, and written in the way that influences the narrative in interesting ways rather than just "you are my mind-slave now" or "you attack your friends instead now".

                                                    *This, and the general way they're written, implies to me that the Solar Integrity and social influence/psyche charms were in fact, written under the assumption that they would be playing off against each other. Possibly as a model to ensure they still work later when other splat's charms go up against them, but still.

                                                            Originally posted by Irked View Post
                                                            It's only a little over 50%, yeah, but it's still more-likely-than-not that you lose when hit with a big mental effect.

                                                            Unhesitating Dedication is a good catch! A lot of the other stuff is either very narrow (Righteous Lion - very potent, for exactly one Intimacy) or doesn't kick in until after you've stabbed your wife (Transcendent Hero's Meditation is built for going "What have I done?"), though. And, unfortunately, Unhesitating Dedication isn't going to be applicable in most of the really nasty stuff, because it only resists Instill actions - which very few of the big effects are. There's no general-purpose cap-booster that would apply against more general social magics.

                                                            And so what do you do against, say, Memory-Reweaving Discipline, or Mind-Wiping Gaze?
                                                            Or, and I cannot emphasize this enough, Twisted Words Technique. I know keep throwing this out there, but that's because it's one stop shopping for beating every defense which gets brought up:
                                                            • It gets to target your strongest Intimacy, meaning that attack beats defense well over 50% of the time, since the attacker will get a big bonus from the strong Intimacy, and you may not have opposing Intimacies at all.
                                                            • Likewise, it'll never have to rely on Instill actions, Inspire actions, or eroding your (Righteous Lion-able) Intimacies. It does rely on Read Intentions, but you've got forever to study up on someone and guess that, hey, she really seems to hate slavery. Only then do you actually walk up to her and make her sell her family.
                                                            • Well, "walk up to her". It's in Linguistics, so you don't actually have to be physically present at all when it goes off.
                                                            • You can't just spend Willpower to resist. You need someone else to dissuade you, and they're opposing your Resolve +2 (granted, you're probably not gonna Excellency this one). If you drop this awful thing on a group's social specialist (who is still helpless as anyone else to resist it), the less-erudite characters might legitimately have a hard time breaking them free. When they do, of course, it'll cost multiple Willpower to shake, so hopefully you've kept that on hand.
                                                            • For the same reason, I'm not sure whether you're allowed to fire off Transcendent Hero's Meditation, save as an alternative to the WP cost.

                                                            In short, the only reliable defense is anti-Psyche tech (which is a pretty vague category, because the book never uses that Keyword in the descriptions of Charms which appear to be referencing it). And that's a little sad, when so many people were so keen on an edition without "either have an explicit 'nope out of this' Charm or auto-lose" as a concept.

                                                            P.S.: Seven Omens Shot can have similar issues, in the sense of "surprise negator or die," but at least that deigns to interact with the system at multiple points on the way.
                                                            P.P.S.: Yes, if your opponent tags you with TWT targeting an Intimacy which you've got Righteous Lion Defense of, you agree to what they're saying, run off to betray everything that you hold dear, then are unable to do it (but don't understand why). Yes, that's awesome. But you only get one RLD, and I'd rather not turn the social interaction system into "guess the RLD" Battleship.
