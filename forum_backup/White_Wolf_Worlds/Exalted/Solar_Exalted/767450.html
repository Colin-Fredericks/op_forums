                      Some possibilities...

                      1) Deathlords don't have access to sorcery, and necromancy doesn't work the same way.
                      2) Deathlords don't have access to any effects that would require XP expenditure, in a special rule that they might share with other ghosts.
                      3) Deathlords haven't been around for centuries in this edition. Who knows?
                      4) Deathlords are not motivated, either personally or by any external power, to simply kill off the world (also known as 'The End of The World is Not the Deathlord's Win Condition Anymore' theory)
                      5) Deathlords can only perform Creation-affecting workings in Creation, and other forces have been present to foil the attempts each time so far (hooray for Sidereals!)
                      6) The lack of stats beyond five keeps such workings from being a sure thing, and Deathlords are unlikely to automatically have all the needed stats at 5 just for being a Deathlord.
                      7) Deathlords, like most ghosts, don't have excellencies, making these workings much more difficult.

                      Take your pick!
