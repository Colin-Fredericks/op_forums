                    The best way I've found to confer this is that Solars are neither Batman or Wizards, but Jedi. So maybe a power like force lightning can be looked at and go "Okay that's a unique skill that's very obvious and discrete." but a Jedi also has powers like enhanced reflexes and greater stamina, which are absolutely force based and also utterly indistinguishable from their innate strength, except by magnitude.

                    So in this case I really think that this charm is more a case of the latter. Player 2 has as much right to judge Player 1 for taking that charm as a pod-racer does for calling Anakin Skywalker a cheater for using his force enhanced reflexes to compete. It's not like he can just consciously turn it off and sever his connection to the luminous heartbeat of the universe!

                                I would like to say that being aware of your essence doesn't mean you are aware of specific charms. From the moment of exaltation, you are filled with essence and you may or may not be aware of exactly what that means. In sports, sometimes you are just better than you thought you could be. Your muscles move just right, your breath goes at the perfect pace and your intentions work out perfectly. Basically the stars align because you try hard. I imagine that's what essence feels like only grander. An exalt would most likely be aware that he is channeling his essence into what he is doing if the is paying attention. However, the presence of essence has become as constant as breathing and how many people pay attention to that.

                                I very much doubt there is a distinct difference between pumping essence into an excellency and using excellent strike unless the exalt actively tries to find that difference. To him, he is simply fighting, trying to find an opening and making unconcious decisions about how to flex which muscle. I always found my best results in sports came about when I was not focusing on what I was doing and simply did.

                                Furthermore, from a gameplay point of view, playing a Jessica Jones-esque Killgrave is much more interessting than playing yet another mind_dominator_03. I think player 2 needs to take a step and look at the big picture of what makes the game fun for each individual rather than insisting that mechanics work a certain way. Character's aren't aware of the difference between withering and decisive atttacks either and the world works just fine without that mechanical distinction. It's an integral part of the gameplay but it has zero bearing on the world.
