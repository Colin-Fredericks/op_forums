    Originally posted by Tiresias View Post
    Corpse-Hiding Technique
    This Charm can be used to help the Solar hide a dead body. People will not find the body unless they are specifically looking for a corpse, nor will they notice the victim's disappearance unless they have a specific need for her.
    This seems more Abyssal to me. Solars might have an "Hide stuff perfectly, even from people's memory"-charm which also allows them to hide bodies, but a specific "Hide corpses"-charm doesn't seem to fit their thematics.

    Oh, and a charm I'd love to see?
    Sun Blends Out The Sky Method (Or "Pay no attention to the man behind the curtain Prana"... something like that)
    Ability: Stealth, or maybe Larceny, or maybe even Presence.
    I... can't actually make up any specific rules for it, but this charm would allow a Solar to hide his allies by drawing all the attention to himself. Ideally, even in an otherwise completely empty room, his enemy would only see him, and no one else.

        Demon-Hunter's Instinct
        Whenever a spirit uses one of its Charms to harm a mortal within (Essence) miles of the Solar, the Solar may roll Perception + Occult to learn the precise location of both the spirit and its victim.

        Otherworldly Banquet's Etiquette
        When the Solar attempts to leverage the customs of the Fair Folk, spirits or the undead against them, she may reroll a number of unsuccessful dice equal to her Occult rating.

                Manna of Flesh and Will
                Whenever the Solar would gain a point of Willpower, she may instead satiate herself as if she had eaten and slept enough to last a number of days equal to her Essence without requiring further rest or sustenance.

                If the Solar is hit by a close-range Melee or Brawl Attack, but takes no damage, she may initiate a counterattack against her opponent using nothing but her armour. The Solar rolls Stamina + Resistance to hit and adds Strength to her damage. Treat the Solar's armour as a weapon of its size and give it the Blunt damage tag. This attack may be enhanced by Brawl charms.

                        Linguistics:

                        Undisputed Authorial Word
                        The Solar becomes the absolute authority on anything she writes. This charm supplements an attempt to erode a minor or major principle through written communication. If the attempt succeeds, the principle is not weakened, but the target cannot apply its benefits to resist the Solar's social influence. The principle's drawbacks always apply in full.

                        Discourse of False Logic
                        This charm allows the Solar to interfere with another character's attempts to influence a third party's intimacies. If the other character's roll is successful, the Solar may roll Manipulation + Linguistics. If the Solar obtains more successes on the roll, she may change which of the third party's intimacies is being influence, although its strength must be equal to that of the original intimacy.

                                    I've decided to think up one new charm per day, every day, until Ex3 is in my hands. Abilities will be chosen randomly.

                                    Investigation:

                                    Lawman's Sudden Realization
                                    If the Solar fails an Investigation roll to identify the culprit of a crime and the criminal begins to plan a second crime in the Solar's general vicinity, the Solar may reroll the Investigation roll in order to gain sudden insight into the culprit's behaviour and stop her before the second crime is committed. In addition, if the Solar uses the Investigation Excellency to enhance the roll, the excellency's cost is reduce by one mote for each of the culprit's crime scenes that the Solar has investigated, successfully or not.

                                    Lawman's Sudden Realization 2.0
                                    A successful Wits + Investigation roll automatically allows the Lawgiver to know if two crimes were committed by the same person. In addition, when investigating serial criminals, the Solar reduces the cost of the Investigation Excellency by one mote per crime scene she has investigated. Finally, if the Solar has investigated (6 - Investigation) of the culprit's crime scenes, she may roll Wits + Investigation against the culprits Manipulation + Larceny in order to predict the culprit's next crime in the Solar's general area, if the criminal is planning one. If the roll fails, the Solar may try again after investigating the culprit's next crime scene.

                                        War:

                                        Steel Wall Encirclement
                                        When the Solar commands a combat group against another, she may user her troops to seal the enemy's movements. The Solar and the enemy commander make an opposed Wits + War roll. Each of the Solar's net successes applies an external penalty of 1 to the enemy force's combat movement. This charm lasts for a number of actions equal to the Solar's war rating, but ends immediately if the Solar's troops move.

                                                Reef-Crushing Wave

                                                        Medicine:

                                                        Remedy of Exalted Resilience
                                                        The Solar's supernatural healing skills persist in her absence. When treating a wounded mortal, the Solar may commit a number of motes equal to the lesser of her Intelligence + Medicine or the patient's Stamina + Resistance. So long as the motes remain committed, the patient may apply the benefit of the Resistance Excellency to all attempts to resist poison, disease or death from grievous wounds, adding a number of dice equal to the number of motes committed.
