    Originally posted by hippokrene View Post

    We know that Solar charms reward dabbling. Grabbing level 1 charms will give you cool toys to play with while level 2 ones will be more focused and powerful.

    I imagine that if you want to play the guy who can shoot 9 suns out of the sky, you'd want to invest heavily in archery charms as well as go deep into the evocation tree for your bow.     I assume you only feel spread thin if you're attempting to become good at all three, as opposed to great with one and rounding out your character with low-level powers/abilities from anothers.
    Also with free excellencies and stuff we're going to have a lot more charms to play with anyway, way easier to take a little of everything that you want to do. If you wanted, it won't be "optimal" but you could use a lot of your regular xp to get evocations and stuff and really invest in being a Kung-fu artifact wizard.

                                                            I am also a completionist with rpgs, but the point is that you get everything by the end of the game. And pen-and-paper RPGs don't tend to end the same way. So I might think "I want this character to be an awesome swordsman who will get better and better at being a swordsman" but I don't actually want to be able to buy all the Melee charms because, well, what will I buy then? How will he get better at being a swordsman? Being the world's greatest swordsman may be his goal, but once he's achieved that the character's less interesting. It's the development and struggle to become the world's greatest swordsman.
                                                            One thing I disliked about 2nd ed is that with a lot of the charm trees, you could very quickly get everything in them (especially the Night caste charm trees) and then there was no more room to develop that part of your character.
                                                            So this makes me pleased.
