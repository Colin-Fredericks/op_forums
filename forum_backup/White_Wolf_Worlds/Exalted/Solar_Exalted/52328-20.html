                Coming late to this party...

                I'd like to see Solar Performance being honest. It should be about being able to powerfully portray the truth, not about spinning illusions to beguile the masses. When Micheal Jackson does his possessed dance in Creation, he's showing off the power and majesty of his performance (and his patron if we're running with MJ as a Solar). I've got nothing against Solar MJ having a light show to accentuate his dance, but the charm should be about enhancing the dance, not providing the light show.

                Solars are the shining heroes of the Sun. I feel they should be honest about their legacy. I don't like the idea of putting charms to do with lying and fake memories in their Performance tree. Or, to put it another way, David Tenant and Hugh Laurie use Lunar Performance Charms: when they act they completely change their accent. Sean Connery is using Solar Performance charms: whether he's playing a Russian, an American, a Roman, an Egyptian, an Alien, an Englishman or what-have-you he always speaks with his own Scottish accent. Brian Blessed also uses Solar Performance charms. A child dressed up as an Ewok is clearly not an Ewok, but is still as adorable. A Solar acting like something else should still have his greatness shine through.

                And, because Performance is huge, going back to comments made at the start of the thread, the Charm tree should have a clear idea of what it's doing. It's OK that Performance covers a broad range of tasks, but the charm trees shouldn't bounce around. The Charms that effect every type of Performance should be accessible to everyone and I shouldn't have to buy a Charm that's only effect is to let me play mad riffs on a Saxian to get to the charm that makes me a better public speaker.
