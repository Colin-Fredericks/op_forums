        Even more irritatingly, well...

        Prayers are a real thing in the setting. If Solar-Jesus is being prayed to to help, there is actually already-in-the-setting way for the message to reach them. Actually...


        Sleep of the Deathless
        (costs and stuff)
        Even when the Solar cannot provide a cure immediately, they can buy time to seek a solution. This Charm can only be used on a willing or unconscious target, and immediately puts the recipient into a deathlike coma state as long as the motes remain committed. While comatose, the subject can be mistaken for a corpse, but a flicker of life still clings to them. Whatever affliction or injury is killing them is put into abeyance, increasing the length of time between checks to see if the subject dies by a factor of ten times the Solar's Essence.

        Whispers of the Dying
        (costs and stuff)
        Prereq: Flawless Diagnosis Technique
        The Solar's awareness of sickness, disease and injury is transcendent, able to sense subtle changes in the life of people at vast ranges. The range of Flawless Diagnosis Technique increased by a factor of the Solar's Essence, or becomes infinite if the afflicted individual or someone is physical contact with them is praying to the Solar (specifically) to come and cure them. If the Solar knows Sleep of the Deathless, it's range is also increased.


        There we go. I want THOSE two Charms.

            I'm on the fence about this.

            I think Exalted really has to decide whether it wants to play the mythic part straight, or whether it wants to subvert it. Because the same thing happaned back in Performance thread and it's still unresolved. It seems everybody has different ideas on what should be mythological and make sense in a parable sort of way and what parts of mythic narrative should be subverted.

            It seems that the best way would be to subvert those parts of mythology that try to exploit atrocities and play straight the parts that just try to explain the World with a clever narrative that lacks a lot of depth, but is very entertaining. But mythology being so murky and not having a clear divide between the two parts, is really hard to work with!

            It seems that people will trip over this forever.

                    I see people asking for a "Doctor Needs No Tools" Charm and, well, 2e already has one. Contagion Curing Touch (and Instant Treatment Methodology if you want the extremely-quick part of it). Actually, here's a quick reference to what 2e already has:

                    -Negate penalties from Sickness/Poison/Crippling/wound.
                    -Treat in unfavorable conditions, without medicines/penalties/stunt (aka equipment).
                    -Heal damage straight-up.
                    -Grant bonus health levels.
                    -Convert agg to lethal.
                    -Diagnose perfectly.
                    -Treat derangements.
                    -Cure any Sickness.
                    -Cure any Poison.
                    -Cure any Crippling.
                    -Cure a specific disease within miles, make it into a plague god.
                    -Add/remove mutations.
                    -Gain motes/reduce Limit from doing medicine.
                    -Preserve youth.
                    -Do an hour of medicine in seconds.
