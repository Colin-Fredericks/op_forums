                        Originally posted by EX3 What We Know Wiki
                        Q: (AliphanG)
                        I'm curious, Holden: how important do you feel "morale", in the D&D sense, is to Exalted 3E burly-brawls? Is it mostly confined to magickal effects, is Legion of Silence Guy so terrifying that the last 900 bandits scatter after he kills the first 100, or what?

                        A: (Holden)
                        It's baked in to the point where damage to a battle group is considered to consist partly of incapacitated/dead opponents and partly of people losing their nerve and no longer contributing meaningfully to the fight, and defeating a BG is usually achieved through rout. There's a "slaughter" action you can take in some circumstances to just wreak massive carnage on a defeated BG that is lighting the hell out of there.

                        The traditional Abyssal zombie army is scary because you can't break it-- you have to beat your way through it until they're all dead.
                        I don't know if what applies to direct combat Abilities should necessarily apply to other Abilities like this. Not that I have a horse in this race: so long as it's balanced by whatever reasonable, decided standard and someone can potentially mainline Medicine alone (like Holden's example of someone building around Awareness), I should be happy with whatever we get.
