                                            I guess the point I was sort of obliquely trying to make was that the difference between 275 solars and 300 solars and 325 solars is immaterial so go with whatever you like. (I really like one for every day of the year, though.)

                                            The point at which it actually makes a difference lies on orders of magnitude. At 5, the PCs are It. At 50, they're ridiculously rare and each one has to acquire and leverage massive resources instead of relying on their own (albeit considerable) power to change things and a circle is still this region-shattering confluence of incredible powers. At 500, there's room for circles aplenty and running into Solars throughout Creation but each one is a singular event. At 5000 they'll make a huge and devastating splash world-wide and running into them in big cities or important events isn't even weird (this is the place I honestly think most sandbox games with turnover are at; Celestial Exalts come and go and nobody's wondering if there's a Creation-wide depletion of them to fill Nexus). At 50,000 you live in a World of Ham.

                                            A few hundred is probably ideal though. A few dozen involves Solars in a world that may well not need them any more. A few thousand involves Solars in a world that probably doesn't want them. (Just a few involves Solars in a world that desperately needs them and doesn't have any.) Those are all completely valid play modes, but a good "default" is in the middle.
