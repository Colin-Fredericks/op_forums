    My retort is - why should they have Occult 3? Why not just a high enough Craft? Why not Craft Charms that allows you to imbue your own Essence into the Magical Metal/ Materials you are working with to make a badass artifact? Why is Occult required beyond the point of "because thats how it is in the rule books?"

    The way I'd see it is that a Mortal with Craft 5 can make a Big sword. They can even make a Big sword out of Jade. And it "may" be an Artifact weapon, but for the most part its just a large item made of a particular metal (real world comparison). Its the limit of what a Mortal could produce.

    An Exalt with the same Craft score could imbue mundane material (steel, wood) with their Essence in its creation to create a 'mundane' Artifact weapon which may have unique properties, but still not be at "Artifact level".

    Or the same Exalt could do the same object, but working with Magical Materials, make a powerful weapon/ armor/ warstrider etc. The fact the Exalted can use Essence to empower an object should be magic enough - Occult isn't required for it to happen. Being able to inject Essence into an object should be mystical. Occult should be something seperate to it.

    I can see the argument being "for a weapon to be enchanted, one must understand enchantment" but for me - it feels like more complexity for little overall purpose. You want to build something? Use Craft. Simple. You want to cast Sorcery? Use Occult. Simple.

    I know some Artifacts have high magical qualities, but I've seen that more as a comination of the craftsman skill to mold the material to a result - not from casting Enchantment INTO the material once its complete. Sorcery is large and visable. Craft is subtle and precise. The two do cross paths, but they are unique qualities.

    Thats what my mind is on. My proposal doesn't prevent you being both - just makes things simple to be more inviting to casual players; less confusion creates less conflict.

                            1) The overall goal is to simplify the selection of abilities down - reducing it (currently) from 25/26 abilities, down to 16 abilities, to make things simpler and more streamlined
                            2) Each Exalt should have both Exclusively Favored Abilities, and Shared Favored Abilities, to again make things simpler (less abilities means less book keeping and less research needed)
                            3) If possible, the Icon/ Symbol of the Caste should dictate their role - not each Caste should have a Role and their Icon be assigned to represent said role (aka - it should be Sunset represents Sorcery, not Sorcery is represented by a Sunset/ Eclipse).
                            4) Due to this being a fan-made suggestion with no offical weight - the idea should be free roaming and not constrained by pre-existing mechanics (Charms, Anima Effects) or game history (Twilight have always been the archetype Sorcerers).

                            So this isn't a challenge of saying Exalted is bad. Its a proposal that it could be something different, while still similar, taking what are good qualities, but editing the rest to try and build it towards something better - but equally making it simpler and more accessable.

                            So if we go through this one at a time, maybe you can see where I'm comming from. And then maybe you can give insight in a way that encourages me to see your view, so we can finally achieve clarity:

                            1) Many skills have been Merged Down. Brawl is now merged into Melee (basically close-combat fighting), Dodge and "part" of Athletics are merged into Acrobatics, Resilience and "part" of Atheletics are Merged into Vigor, etc... pulling 25 abilities down by 9 into 16 abilities, so that with less Abilities to choose from, the Storyteller won't need overthink things to figure out which ability is needed for what test attempt.

                            2) My chart has designed this "2 Exclusive Favored, 3 Shared Favored" setup. So far the major resistance I've had has been on the basis of Zenith getting Occult, and Twilight and Eclipse losing it.                             3) I've reviewed the Icons of the Castes and looked at what they could represent, and this in turn encourages the abilities each Caste should get for what they represent. My view in this variation, to try to match their icons, are as follows:
                            • Dawn - They push back the Night/ Darkness/ Evil/ Enemy. People Run from them. They are Dangerous. They are Killers. They are power. They make things Go Away - through active Execution or threat of doing so.
                            • Zenith - They Stand Tall and are seen by all. They Command and are Obeyed. They are the closest to Heaven then any other in their Group, and are connected to the Gods the Govern all by their height. Enemies are humbled by them. Mortals bask in their Glory and feel no fear as their world is illuminated. Nothing is hidden from their height.
                            • Twilight - They Watch for Incomming Threats (the approaching Darkness that ends the Light), and find solutions to survive the attack.                             • Night - They maintain Hope when Darkness has engulfed all. They are present even if unseen. They manipulate the world, but are ignored when doing so.                             • Eclipse - They unite forces, make enemies into friends, make deals between parties, resolve conflicts with words and discussion, they appear and do their part and then leave just as suddenly leaving a strong emotional memory and a longing for it to return. Something that could meet what I feel are some of its flaws - specifically being too convoluted for casuals to join in.
