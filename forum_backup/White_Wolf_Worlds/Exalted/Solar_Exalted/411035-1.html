                                            Originally posted by Robert Vance View Post
                                            You don't think the Eclipse Caste who decides to go all Illuminati Master would end up being famous or celebrated, would he? :P
                                            Probably not, but Eclipse Caste Metternich probably would be, at least among their peers.

                                            More seriously, I completely understand that signature First Age Solars more or less had to be representations of what happens when a high-essence elder Solar really wallows in the Great Curse and their own hubris, but at least the Hierophant makes sense as a Zenith qua Zenith. Desus honestly never seemed like much of a deal-maker or diplomat, and his most famous action was just... punching someone's lights out.

                                            I stand by my statement that the Night Caste aren't blunt instruments, tho.

                                            I'm going to be the annoying one in this thread and take a more grossly mechanical, rather than narrative, look at these questions, mostly because people who are far more clever than I am have already give all the best koan-like answers and why chase success?

                                            Also too, to define terms: there's a difference between "this what $EXALT_TYPE is best at," and "$EXALT_TYPE is the best at this." I'm gonna assume hippocreme was asking the former and not the latter, although in the specific case of Solar-equivalents they're rather intertwined. So with that in mind...

                                            Solars are the best at anything, although any individual Solar cannot be the best at everything. They have a large resource bank and cost-efficient access to applications of Essence that are just straight-up superior to everyone else. There are exceptions to this, I remember a lot of 1e Sidereal chicanery involving Blade of the Battle Maiden and Joy in Adversity Stance for example, but for the most part if a Solar stakes out a competency and pursues it, it won't just be the thing they personally are best at, they will be the best at it period everything else being equal.

                                            Lunars are, as has been said, the best at adaptability. In actual practice on a real table, what this boils down to is that shapeshifting is the swiss army knife of powers. Assuming other forms is very cheap and very useful and very powerful, and a creative, adaptive player can use that hammer to drive in every nail they see, especially because that hammer is also a wrench and a screwdriver. They're also the best at remaining themselves; their immunity to Shaping effects is not always relevant but it is complete; no force in the setting can force a Lunar to stop being a Lunar short of, well, killing them.

                                            Sidereals are tricky; I'm probably going to spend more words on them than the other three combined. First, I'm going to say that in this context, I don't think Sidereals are best being martial artists. That's the obvious first answer for their mechanical table strength, right, martial arts? And I don't think it is so. They may be the best martial artists, but I don't think that's what they themselves are best at.

                                            So what are they best at? Giving it some though, I would have to say that Sidereals are equally best at two things; defining or ignoring the terms of reality, and remotely managing all manner of... stuff. Sidereal mechanical effects tend to function around "Sidereal decides that X is true, not Y, and behold, it is so." These usually happen through a sheer act of will, or occasionally (Neighborhood Relocation Scheme) require action that one suspects is a metaphor for what's actually happening. Any other Exalt who wants Y to be untrue and X to be true has to engage in what we'd recognize as logical, observable steps to get there. A Sidereal just thinks about it, expends motes, and it happens.

                                            Their ability to do this is heavily circumscribed by both the narrowness of their Charms and the closed charmset, but it is definitely what they are best at. Their other major competency is the ability to lay ongoing effects on the world via Astrology and then walk away and leave those effects running without needing to either commit essence or constantly monitor and tweak things. They can just slap penalties and bonuses (as well as other stuff) onto people, places, and things, and then stroll away and leave it running. This can be wickedly hard to use at the table (Sidereal Astrology rules have broken strong men) but I would have to say it is definitely a "best at" thing.

                                            Dragon-Blooded are also slightly tricky. I don't actually think that what they themselves are best at is synergy, although they're clearly better at that than any other Exalt type. What I have to say is that they're best at being basically competent.

                                            No, really. Best at competence. Hear me out! Dragon-Blooded have dirt cheap basic dice-adders/Excellencies. They can leverage specialties into their dice pools in an efficient manner as well. Your "default" Dragon-Blooded is going to have access to a lot of equipment, artifact and otherwise, to help them do whatever it is they like, and they're probably plugged into a support network of some sort as well. As befits their status as soldiers, what Dragon-Blooded are best at is just throwing lots and lots of dice at a problem until the problem goes away. They can do that very cheaply and very well, better than anything else they can do, really. So that's what I'm going to say they're best at.
