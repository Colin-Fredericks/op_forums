                                                    It's helpful to read the actual rules here.

                                                    Mentors and similarly skilled trainers should also reduce training times, proportionate to their skill at teaching.
                                                    So, by default, training times given represent the time to self-develop your own skills.

                                                    Finally, a word on advancing mystical traits. While it is possible to give instruction in Charms, most Chosen, and certainly most Solars, develop their own Charms through instinctive cultivation of skill rather than through rigorous practice. As such, while Charms can certainly be trained like any other skill, many characters 'train' their Charms by exercising the Ability in question under duress. Evocations usually require extensive training with the Artifact they're attached to, but also demand that the Exalt establish a rapport with his equipment, often on the field of battle. Martial Arts Charms always require rigorous training, and learning spells requires a mentor or a text copy of the spell in question and many hours of intense study.
                                                    So, look: only spells are called out as requiring external instruction. Martial arts charms are explicitly listed without that constraint - self-training is entirely possible, it's just that you can't say "poof I know new charm" you have to claim you have been explicitly working on learning a new charm. But very very clearly you do not require external instruction for it.

                                                    If I were to, as a house-rule, apply any kind of training requirements to Martial Arts, it would solely apply to learning the first dot of a new style, and I'd permit that to be learned from text rather than from a sifu. A sifu will surely be helpful, but once you "know" a style, even at the one-dot level, there is no good reason that a Solar Exalt especially couldn't train by himself to pefect his own mastery of it.
