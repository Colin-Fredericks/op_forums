    Originally posted by TGUEIROS View Post

    As cool as your description is (and I truly mean it) unless you are a dev and are spoilering this part of IMAs it is not making the case clear for why IMAs don't have Mastery.

    If I gave White Reaper some Lunar fancy text people would just say it is not written that way.

    And IMAs aren't, for any practical purposes, written yet.
    Okay, let me unpack that a bit.

    For a start, there is nothing here to spoiler, the origin of the styles is setting information that has been around since the Immaculate styles were first detailed in Exalted: The Dragon-Blooded.

    Second, White Reaper is a red herring because it has never actually been a specifically Lunar style, it just happened to be released in Glories Luna.

    Third, yes, of course they have been written, they have been in previous editions, and guess what? Dragon-Bloods have always been better at using them. They granted small, specific benefits for using the style weapons. 2.5 (an errata that was written by the current dev team) clarified that these benefits where Terrestrial only, making them the only thing in previous editions in any way analogous to Mastery Charms.

    Obviously this didn't stop Solar martial artists with Immaculate Charms being generally better fighters, because their martial arts were backed up by superior native Charms, mote pools and dice pools. And this is still the case.

    Finally, if you want something to dig into from the current edition, here's a little teaser from the core book:

    "The power of Terrestrial Exalted who undergo the Order’s martial art training is beyond question. Immaculates spend their lives developing their Essence, and use the natural Charms of their kind mixed freely with the proprietary Immaculate martial arts styles to perform feats of martial prowess notable even among the Exalted." p72

    Writer for Exalted.
