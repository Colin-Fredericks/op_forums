                              Here, what the corebook has to say for us on solar animas color palettes...

                              Dawn Caste Solars' animas tend to be bright white and pale gold, sometimes tinged with red or light violet.

                              Zenith Caste Solars' animas are a brilliant, burning white or a majestic gold.

                              Twilight Caste Solars' animas tend to the most spectacular colors, containing bright golds as well as bright and dark reds, purples and even blues.

                              Night Caste Solars' animas are ghostly whites and golds, tinged with purple and purple-gray.

                              Eclipse Caste Solars' animas are brilliant whites and golds, almost sparkling, but filmy and fluctuating, like the corona of the sun during an eclipse.
                              No pink or rose outright mentioned, but between reds, purples, golds and blues, there might be some wiggle room to be found, i think.

                                      Or recognize that since anima color has literally zero mechanical effect, players should feel free customize it as much as they want (within reason, I mean, a solar is not going to have a black and green anima, but a zenith whose anima is a bright white-gold rose tinged in reds, or a giant golden cactus with filmy white blossoms reminiscent of heat haze isn't hurting anything).

                                      Another example, I have a Dawn whose anima is a pale gold double-headed eagle with rosy-tipped talons and beaks (as in, looks flecked with blood) and burning blue-violet eyes, with blue-violent along the trailing edges of its wing pinions as well.
