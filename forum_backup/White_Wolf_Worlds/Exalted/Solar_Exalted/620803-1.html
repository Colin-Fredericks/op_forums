        Originally posted by Jutlander View Post
        You say that the ideology of the Walker in Darkness offends you. Would you care to explain that in further details from your point of view? Do you know what the ideology of the Walker in Darkness is or are you going off of assumptions?
        And when I exalted, I had a moment of extreme clarity in which saw the beauty of what the world was, what it is now, and most importantly what it could be. From what I've heard from the servants of the Walker i have encountered, his goal appears to be the destruction of that world, though I may be grasping at straws here. You'll understand if I'm not too keen on that.

        Originally posted by Zeal
        How did you develop your working vocabulary about supernatural beings? Solar, Zenith, Dusk, etc.?
        Shara, The leader of our circle is about 250 years old, her mentor was even older. Their knowledge of that got passed down to us in turn. For the Abyssal caste names we simply found opposites to the Solar names.

        Originally posted by Brasstreader
        Do you know the names of your Charms, or do you make up nicknames for them? Or are they simply 'things you can do'?
        My what? I suppose it's the latter, though I do get the urge to shout "HEAVEN THUNDER HAMMER" every time I punch someone across a room.

        Given the the near-ubitquitousness of the Immaculate Faith/Philosophy, most people panic when they see a gold anima. How much do you try to hide your Solarness?
        I hide it most when I deal with Realm Satraps or Lookshy. I play it a bit looser elsewhere if I need to. the goal is to become powerful enough before it gets widely public such that when it does, Killing me would be inconvenient.

        -Are you one of those Solars that glows gold near-constantly, trying to drum up old world values of Solar righteousness and dominion? Or do you hide the ever-loving crap out of your status?
        As above, it kind of varies. when I want to drum up old world values, I'm not afraid to flare. But since I'm still to close to the Realm and Lookshy for comfort, I mostly hide it.

        What kinds of Exalts have you seen so far? Do you have a preference or dislike for a particular set of Chosen, or is it all about the individual?
        Lunars, Abyssals, Sidereals and say one or two Dragonbloods. I largely judge on an individual basis, as I've met good and bad people among all of the above.

        Which do you think has more value: a man's kingdom, his principles, his honor, his loved ones, or his life? Which would you trade first?
        Hmm... I... genuinely don't know. the only thing I can say is that my honor is nothing compared to the safety of my people.

        Originally posted by Zelbinnean
        Tell us about the vision given by the Unconquered Sun when you Exalted. Was it a very concrete conversation or an abstract moment of connection? Metaphorical? Like a hallucination?
        A bit of both, the whole thing feels like it was a hallucination, but the words of the Unconquered Sun still ring through my head. The Idea that we both made mistakes in the past, but now was the time to begin making up for them. The call to take light into the darkness, and make the world righteous as i saw fit.

        "Life before Death, Strength before Weakness, Journey before Destination."

        "I will protect those who cannot protect themselves"

If we can get Lookshy to mobilize, I'll call it a win. If the Realm decides to rear it's head, then the enemies of Creation should be very afraid, I know I will be, should that come to pass. And while we avoid discussing the possibility with the Lunars, Skylas and I hope we may turn Heaven's eye toward the Deathlords, as that kind of help would be invaluable to our efforts.

                                Originally posted by Mr.Jaxus View Post
                                Do you hold anything of the Immaculate faith's morality to be noble in of itself? I understand not putting faith in the whole Solars-bad, Dragon bloods - awesome, but the basic ideals of compassion and giving mortals hope in doing good deeds will lead to a greater reward.
                                I do believe that the Immaculate Order has the right of it on living a good life, doing right by others so that others will do right by you. I just believe the call to live a righteous life should include everyone, mortal or Exalt.

                                Do you associate more with the idea that Exalts exist to protect and inspire mortals or that mortals should obey exalted rulers above all things?
                                I believe our purpose is to inspire mortals. We show them that the dark is less fearsome than they thought, that the monsters can be beaten, and that the sun always rises after the long night.

                                If you had the chance to defeat the Walker in the Darkness in an instant, but could release something terrifying and dangerous to Creation, like a Primordial, a Yozi or a Shinma, would you take the risk?
                                If my defeating one threat to Creation releases something even more threatening, I'm doing it wrong.

                                And Finally, the deepest and most important question of all, what sort of shinies (artefacts) do you possess, if any?
                                I possess two. The orichalcum daiklave used to kill my first age incarnation, which is tainted by betrayal. It occasionally urges me to assault people that depend about me when I use it. I intend to change it's nature by using it for a more noble purpose. The other artifact is a buff jacket made of moonsilver and starmetal, wrapped in the skin of a behemoth. I can it's change color and shape at will, so long as it remains roughly an overcoat. It even changes subtly on it's own based on the situation I'm in, such as the collar growing higher when I threaten people, or taking on a more military appearance with my troops.

                                "Life before Death, Strength before Weakness, Journey before Destination."

                                "I will protect those who cannot protect themselves"
