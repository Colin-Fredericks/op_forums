    Interview with a Solar

    08-08-2015, 10:15 PM
    From the desk of Strider of the Northern Sky, Joybringer and Head of the Division of Serenity:
    Contained herein is the transcript of my interview with the Zenith Caste Solar, Sesus Rai.

    Strider: Alright, shall we begin?

    Rai: It’s as good a time as any.

    S: Alright then, You are Rai of House Sesus, son of Sesus Soral and Zenith caste Solar. Correct?

    R: I am, yes. That hasn’t changed in the past ten minutes.

    S: (laughs) Sorry, standard questions. So if you’re a Dynast, why did I find you in Great Forks?

    R: Well I’m a Solar, It shouldn’t be surprising I’m not sitting pretty on the Blessed Isle. That said, I’d been outside the Realm for some time before that… it’s a long story.

    S: Right. So I was at a loss of questions to ask, so I discreetly allowed some others to submit some. If you don’t mind answering, I’ll put them to you.

    R: Works for me.

    So then, I'm working on this Character for an EX3 game I'm planning to run and while I have a good sense of what he's like, I want to nail down his place in and views on the world. Instead of just sitting down and thinking real hard about what questions, I decided it might be more fun to put it here and answer your questions. Meta questions are fine and will be answered in a self-aware, fourth-wall breaking manner, but all other questions will be answered without Meta knowledge. So... yeah, fire away. And thank you for reading!

    "Life before Death, Strength before Weakness, Journey before Destination."

    "I will protect those who cannot protect themselves"

                  Originally posted by Tiresias View Post
                  How do you decide who to save and who to kill?
                  I thought you started with the easy questions. To be honest it's mostly a case by case basis. I protect the people I care about, but I try to avoid killing needlessly. I have my goals, though, and will kill to realize them.

                  Originally posted by Wardragon
                  How did you Exalt?
                  I exalted when I was traveling back to Great Forks from a personal trip. One day on the roads I encountered a pair of Immaculate Monks, one an elder and the other a trainee. They were both wounded and the elder was a dead woman walking. I offered them some help and inquired what exactly they were doing that far from the Isle. They told me they were part of a Wyld hunt called to respond to some Anathema activity. Before they could explain much more, "Anathema Activity" found us, It was a group of undead, somehow able to survive the sunlight. Part of the forces of the Walker in Darkness, according to the monks. We squared off with them, the three of us versus some dozen odd corpses. The younger monk and I killed two in the time it took for the elder to cut through six. We may have won, had the leader of the undead not shown up. He was one of the Forsaken, or as I know them know, a Dusk Caste Abyssal. He made his entrance by plunging his sword into the elder monk's heart. As she lay bleeding out on the ground, he took the time to look us over. It was like staring death itself in the face. every instinct told me to run, the young monk lost his nerve and collapsed in a heap next to me. The horrified look on his face was one I'd seen before, and I realized I had no choice but to face our enemy both for him and the last person i saw with that look. As i made that decision, the moon rose to block out the sun, and I was exalted by the Unconquered Sun. I cut down the remaining undead, and dueled the Abyssal to the death. He lost, and the monk and I managed to escape.

                  Does your family know that you're a Solar? If so, how did you survive them? Are they still hunting you?
                  My family does not know, or at least I don't know if they do. They had been chasing me for two years before then for different reasons, but I survived with a lot of luck and some supernatural help. They haven't sent an assassin in a while, which if I had to guess is because the secrets I may have had on them are very out of date by now. or they just need a good exuse to send a Dragonblood this time.

                  Do you worship the Unconquered Sun? Why or why not?
                  I don't worship the Unconquered Sun, because I find it hard to worship someone I could feasibly meet. Also, the closest thing to a 'God' in a religious sense I had growing up was my Dragonblooded relatives, which have never really been my Idols.


                  Are you, on the whole, glad that you Exalted as a Solar?
                  Yes, it gives me a chance to make the world a more righteous place, and to atone for the things I've done in the past.

                  "Life before Death, Strength before Weakness, Journey before Destination."

                  "I will protect those who cannot protect themselves"

                      Originally posted by Exthalion View Post
                      What do you view as the most important factors in need of change in Creation?
                      The fact that there are undead and the fair folk just waiting to prey on us. Important to fixing that is the fact that most of the exalts in the world need to get their heads out of their asses.

                      Do you intend to do anything to change these factors, and if so how?
                      I do, with the help of the circle I'm part of, we intend to wake up some other Celestials and Dragonbloods to the outside threat of the Deathlords. We intend to do this by showing we can beat them, and are currently working to destroy the Walker in Darkness.

                      How do you view your relationship with your Patron, the Unconquered Sun?
                      I owe him for the chance to make a difference in the world, and a reason to fight on in life, but beyond that... I'm not sure.

                      What do you think is the proper role of heaven and the gods in Creation?
                      Hmm... I think if heaven and the Celestial Bureaucracy got their shit together, it would be easier for the Exalted Host to work against outside threats and improve the quality of life in both heaven and Creation. And for the record, I do know that us Exalts all need to get out shit together too, perhaps even more than heaven does.

                      "Life before Death, Strength before Weakness, Journey before Destination."

                      "I will protect those who cannot protect themselves"

                                      Originally posted by Aretii View Post
                                      What's your favorite Ability, and why is it Resistance?
                                      *Laughs* Because I had some issues with a young Lunar once, and seeing the look on his face when I took a punch and stayed standing is only second to the look he had when he realized I'd exalted as a Solar. He got his ass kicked after that.

                                      Originally posted by Owill0w
                                      Do you have any vices?
                                      Nothing major, some bad experiences with... unruly family members tend to turn you off those kinds of things. That and a very insistent woman *laughs*.

                                      Is there a family member you would like to meet again?
                                      My mother and my sister, to apologize for everything. Among the living, my cousin Shu, who I used to play strategy games with as a kid.

                                      If yes, how do you think that meeting would go?
                                      Poorly, seeing as he's a Legionnaire these days. It could only go well if I never tell him I'm a Solar.

                                      How did you meet your circle?
                                      This one's complicated... the abridged version is that I was supposed to marry a patrician girl from House Nellens for political reasons. However, during a trip back to my family's estate we were attacked by assassins. At the end of the confusion I found her kneeling over the body of one of my guards who had turned traitor. Standing over her was a shining silver woman. They both looked to me when I approached. One looked panicked, the other amused. I eventually managed to tell the Nellens girl that we needed to run, and we fled the Isle, somehow. We traveled east and managed to find a pair of other Lunars who would take her in. She took the name Crimson Lotus, and was made a Full Moon. The pair of Lunars were the Full Moon Shara leader and elder of about 250 years, and Invisible Courtier (who we call Corty), a Changing Moon who exalted three years prior. I was an informant for them in the beginning as I wandered the Scavenger Lands. Two years later, a Solar Eclipse happened and I exalted I joined them myself. a few weeks after that Corty, who had been away at the time, returned to us with his Solar mate, an Night Caste by the name of Skylas in tow. And now the five of us work together against the local Deathlords.

                                      Do you have a best friend? Who is it, and what do you like about them?
                                      I do. That would be Skylas, the circle's Night caste. It's nice to have someone around to give a different perspective on things. He was a gutter rat growing up and understand the needs of the common folk far better than I ever did, and as such isn't afraid to call me out when I do something stupid. We've reached a surprisingly deep respect considering that our first instinct on meeting was to try and kill each other. We realized that it was because the last 'him' was the one who killed the last 'me' in the past, but we managed to get over it as that was 'their' problem and 'we' refused to be tied down by it.

                                      "Life before Death, Strength before Weakness, Journey before Destination."

                                      "I will protect those who cannot protect themselves"
