        Bureaucracy

        Honest Greed's Omission
        The Solar may use this charm when negotiating the price of her goods. So long as her buyer possesses a positive intimacy towards the Solar and the Solar makes no direct or indirect mention of the value of her goods in any given market, the buyer may not use his own knowledge of the goods' resale value to resist the Solar's social influence. In addition, if the Solar sells her goods above their worth, this does not erode the buyer's intimacy towards her. The buyer will simply rationalize that they are at fault for negotiating poorly, or that the high price of the goods is a necessary loss leader to maintain their relationship with the Solar.

                            Brawl:

                            Hundred Fists Nova
                            This charm can be used as a decisive attack. The Solar's fist becomes wreathed in sunfire and explodes upon contact with his opponent. The Solar first resolve this attack against every enemy within medium range of the Solar. These enemies take the attack to their momentum rather than their health. The Solar then resolves the attack against her initial target. If the attack hits, the Solar gains a number of dice of damage equal to the number of opponents who lost momentum, up to a maximum of the Solar's Essence.

                                Ride:

                                Ascendant Horselord's Crown
                                The majesty of the Solar's steed cows lesser beasts into submission. When the Solar activates this charm, any rider whose mount is lesser than the Solar's, either because of a difference between their mounts' natural abilities or because the Solar's own mount is bolstered by other charms, must succeed an opposed Charisma + Ride roll versus the Solar's Appearance + Ride. If the Solar succeeds, her opponent's mount gains an minor tie of fear towards the Solar's steed, which the Solar can exploit with further intimidation. If the Solar's mount is a blatantly supernatural being and the enemy's mount is mundane, the intimacy becomes major.

                                        Linguistics:

                                        Essence of Words and Wind
                                        Upon learning this charm, the Solar invents a new language of absolute precision and clarity. The language is universal and can be understood by anyone who possesses at least one iteration of the Language Merit, but it cannot be used in casual conversation - it can only be used during negotiations. Those who speak in the language cannot knowingly deceive, either through lies, omission, or willful ignorance on their part, nor can they be misunderstood. They add a number of automatic successes equal to the Solar's Essence rating to all their social influence rolls during negotiation and can always leverage their own trustworthiness as a minor tie if the party they are negotiating with has no other intimacy to exploit. The Solar can teach this language to as many people as she wishes.

                                                    Larceny:

                                                    Box-Cracking Foresight
                                                    The Solar prepares for her crime while scouting out the area. While casing a joint, the Solar can roll to pick locks or deactivate traps normally. Success allows her to make slight modifications to the trap or lock. The Solar can then activate this charm to "suspend" her success, delaying its effects until she returns to the area to commit her crime. The traps and lock work normally until the Solar returns to "cash in" her success. The Solar can keep a number of this charm's activations equal to her Essence at any given time, allowing her to prepare for multiple traps or locks. The Solar can voluntarily turn off this charm without returning to the scene of the desired crime, which causes the traps and locks to function normally once again. Traces of the Solar's work can be detected with a Perception + Investigation or Larceny roll with a difficulty equal to the Solar's Larceny rating.

                                                        Bureaucracy:

                                                        Courageous Quill Discipline
                                                        The Solar bolsters the hearts of her subordinates so that they do not stray from their duties. The Solar announces a policy to protect her employees from violence and retaliation. So long as this charm remains active, the Solar's subordinates increase by one rank the strength of any intimacy they can use to resist social influences meant to intimidate them into taking bureaucratic actions within the Solar's organization. If the Solar's subordinates can call upon an intimacy that is already defining, they add the Solar's Essence to their Resolve. In addition, if the Solar fights someone who has attempted to threaten or intimidate her employees, she adds the size rating of the relevant organization to her momentum after rolling join battle.
