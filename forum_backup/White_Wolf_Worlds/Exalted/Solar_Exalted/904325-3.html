                                                    Something I saw touched on briefly but not really discussed: raising social defenses.

                                                    I was flabbergasted when I realized that there are no Integrity Charms for pushing your Resolve beyond what an Excellency can do. None. No cap-breaking bonus in the vein of Reed In The Wind, no math tricks a la Drifting Leaf Elusion, none of the "subtract enemy successes" tech which show up all over the Solar charmset. If another supernatural being tries to persuade you of something, they will succeed. Don't waste motes - plan to spend Willpower.

                                                    P.S.: Yes, there are effects to increase your mote economy for Resolve, or which ignore penalties. No, that's not the same thing.
                                                    P.P.S.: Also, I will allow it is possible that the intent of Charms such as Stubborn Boar Defense and Temptation-Resisting Stance is that they should count as non-Charm bonuses, but I am legitimately unsure, and when in doubt, I assume the intent is to use the rules as written. I will point out that Undying Solar Resolve looks suspiciously like a Charm written as "subtract enemy successes," then changed later, so I suspect that the lack of social defenses is deliberate, for whatever reason.
