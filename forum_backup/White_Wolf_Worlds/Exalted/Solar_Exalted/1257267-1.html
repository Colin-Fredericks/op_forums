    Originally posted by RadioFreeDeath View Post
    Quite frankly I don't know ow why they think a loom that has strands for all mortals in creation would be hand sized...
    See, when you say this, it's a warning sign to me. I think that an important skill, when you're running a game, is, when your players are doing something you think is obviously stupid, to stop and ask yourself "why are they doing something stupid, and is it because I haven't given them enough information?". It's important to remember that the Storyteller will pretty much always have more information than the players do - even if they've read all the setting material extensively, your version of the setting may well be different. Furthermore, you're the channel that all in-character knowledge flows through. You get to decide what their characters would know. So, you should know why the characters think the Loom could possibly be that small - is it because they're all ignorant peasants or just completely uneducated in the larger world of Creation? Or have you not given out information that they should logically have?

    Originally posted by RadioFreeDeath
    But you know. I think it will be a great moment in campaign.
    That's possible, but it's also possible it won't, and will, in fact, be a major source of irritation and aggravation to your players. As a player, one of the things I find most irritating is when I make decisions that turn out to be based on wrong information when my character would have logically have known the right information, or even had a chance to work it out that I never got. It creates the feeling that my character is just an idiot, which isn't very fun. This is only reinforced if the GM then goes on to say how ridiculous they found my actions, because they were the one who withheld information from me. We find anecdotes like the Gazebo Story amusing, but bear in mind they probably weren't that funny to the player they happened to.

    Remember, if any of the PCs have any dots in relevant Abilities, like Lore (to know general history and the nature of things), Craft (to know how artifacts generally work), or Occult (general practical magical knowledge), it's okay to feed the players a hint or two - you can call for a (Wits + Lore) roll and, if it gets any successes, say "You know, thinking about this, something that dictates the fate of a whole world might not be that small. Maybe you should do a bit of research before we get there."

                                            Sorry! Its been a week. I need to catch everyone up on where we are at.


                                            So my PCs were attacked by a sea witch as they crossed the sea. The sea Witch had been dispatched by Raksi to bring them back as she was unhappy that they had fled Mahalanka after getting into the library sessions ago. They convinced the sea witch to remove the curse from them and let them go.

                                            Upon arrival in the Imperial City, one of the players ran into Tepet Ajava (A 4 point ally) a dragon blooded they had known from their time in Llinowan at the very beginning of the campaign. He was unaware that the PCs were exalted and he was in town in an attempt to convince the realm to help the Llinowan against the Bull of the North who was massing forces in Halta to invade Llinowan. He invited them to stay with him in the Tepet wing of the Palace. He is not having much luck as parties jockey for power. They learned about the imperial palace and the disappearance of the empress a few years before.

                                            The Night Caste also received attention from the all seeing eye but showed the letter he recieved from them to the Zenith Caste so both are now marked for death.

                                            The players did some research and determined that they believed the loom to be located in a temple.

                                            Now I have to take a quick break to explain about a legendary artifact the PCs have. Its one main doorknob and 5 secondary doorknobs. Each of the secondary doorknobs can be placed somewhere in creation on a door and after attuning for one day that door now opens to the main doorknob wherever it is anywhere in creation. The players have one doorknob in Chanta. So they devised a plan. The eclipse caste crossed to Chanta and visited the Bull of the North. He convinced him that he had found a cave that would give a small number of soldiers access directly to the heart of the realm. The bull decided to use the "cave" (really just the artifact created door to the imperial city) to send across spies and saboteurs into the imperial city. However, one of the other members of the Bull's circle began to figure out that the "cave" was really something else and that the eclipse was playing them. The PCs panicked and the Night cast snuck in and stole the doorknob keeping the cave open after a small group of the Bull's soldiers and made it through and shut the gate down. However the eclipse caste was trapped on the Bull's side. The bull angry at the double cross sent soldiers to capture the eclipse who at the beginning of this session is about to fight his way out of the Bull's ambush.

                                            Meanwhile The other 4 PCs waited until the Bull's soldiers in the imperial city had started a fire in the outer slums. As the monks rushed towards the slum to help with the spreading conflagaration they PCs snuck by the few remaining guards into the temple. The Dawn caste was spotted but quickly and quietly defeated the guards before they could raise the alarm. The PCs made their way to a room at the center of the temple where they discovered a portal to Yu- Shan. ( I handwaved opening the portal for narrative purposes) Two Golden Lions stepped through the gate and informed the PCs that they would not be allowed entry.

                                            Thats where we ended the session. So one PC is trapped halfway across creation at the mercy of the Bull. The other PCs are deciding if they can convince the Lions to let them pass or whether they will try to storm the gate.

                                            So for tonights session:

                                            I'll start with the Bull of the North and the Eclipse. The eclipse will either escape and be pursued or will be captured and have to find a way to escape or convince the bull not to kill him. He knows that if he can get to Chanta he can get back to the rest of the circle. And the circle will quickly learn what trouble he is in.

                                            The other PCs will have to find a way into heaven where they will have to find a way to find the loom. Upon realizing how big the loom is, things will get creative. If they get too stuck I like the idea of a gentimian who can fit the entire loom inside itself. I may create an opening there. I need to remember to allow the players to realize the destroying or damaging the loom would be a really bad idea.

                                            I also ended up with a split group which will be a serious problem. I didn't expect them to panic and create the massive division.

                                            Meanwhile I think realm agents will quickly capture or kill most of the Bull's agents. Once they realize what the Bull did I imagine the hammer will come down quickly on the PCs if they stick around.
