        I've had to deal with things like this before in other systems.

        First of all, it only really becomes problematic in PvP combat; any situation in which I, the GM, am controlling the opponents means that I can give accurate predictions as to what they're going to do to the players if they have ways of knowing that.

        In PvP, the easiest way of resolving this I've found is to force the player of the character being predicted to lock in their action privately, with me, before moving on to the player of the character doing the predicting. Then I can weigh how much information the mechanical method of prediction will convey through my filter, and give that over. The first player can't do backsies.

        It's always worked relatively well. There have been edge cases, of course. In an Amber DRPG game I had one memorable moment of a character who as a tactical god (which gives you insight into what your opponent is going to do next, but only based on knowledge you actually have) fighting a character who had mentally compromised them and so could read their thoughts as they happened and react accordingly. I had to figure out how and to what extent they cancelled each other out and what information to give both players.

        That's a super edge case, tho.

                PraxisCat How about Battlefield Calamity Vision
                Cost: 4i, 1wp Requirements: Awareness 4, Essence 3 Prerequisite Charms: Sensory Acuity Prana (Instant, Reflexive) Keywords: Decisive-only

                A rotted tree, a thick bramble, a hidden sinkhole, a crushing boulder; the solar's acute eye notices all these things. With subtle cues that the opponent reads "with ease" she tricks her them into a precarious situation. This charm creates a gambit where the solar rolls their Manipulation + Awareness against the higher of the targets Resolve or Guile. Success tricks the target into maneuvering themselves into an environmental hazard with a base damage of (Essence)L bypassing hardness, lasting 1 round. Successes above the targets defense can be split between additional rounds or increasing the damage by 1L on a one for one bases. The target may take an (Dexterity + Awareness) action, which may not be flurried, against a difficulty of the initiators guile, each success reduces the duration of the effect by 1 round to a minimum of 1 round.

                Keen Predictive Insight Technique
                Cost: 5m, 1wp Requirements: Awareness 3, Essence 2 Prerequisite Charms: Surprise Anticipation Method (Instant, Reflexive) Keyword: Clash, Withering-only

                Whenever you are attacked, instead of applying defense, roll Dexterity + Awareness. Subtract your successes from the attack roll's successes and treat the attack roll's successes as a difficulty for any of your other awareness charms. If you would automatically succeed the check using your ability, then you successfully dodge the attack. If you do not, then treat the remaining successes as roll-over successes.

                This charm does not work if the opponent is using a supernatural method of stealth or suppression which you are using to pass the check; for example, a demon with no smell cannot be noticed with smelling, and a supernaturally silent assassin couldn't be noticed through hearing.

                        BadassOverlord , I like the idea of using something different than Dex to help with defense; it certainly helps with Dex being the godstat in Exalted! Maudova has the right idea when it comes to helping balance it; if you make it perception / awareness, you get rid of making Wits the new godstat, since it also contributes to resolve and join battle.

                        Maudova, good suggestions! I like the thought that went into Battlefield Calamity Vision, but my worry is that it's a quite complex charm. That being said, it's a "free damage" charm, so I don't see why not. As someone who both plays and STs, my general disposition is to avoid as many "check what values each person has" as possible, in order to keep things smooth. That being said, I like the thought that went into this, and it definitely captures the spirit of the act.

                        On Keen Predictive Insight Technique, I think the cost is a little high. Ultimately, we want to compare any defense booster to just using an excellency to boost your defense. Assuming you're using keen hearing, we can assume a baseline elimination of 3. If someone's rolling 15 attack dice, let's assume 7 successes. So we need to boost our defense by 3 to get there, assuming we start with a defense of 5. We're at a 2 defense deficit between this charm and base right now. Using just an excellency and a stunt, it costs 4m to get into the "safe zone" and successfully parry. With our dice roll, the results are variable. Assuming we're rolling 10 dice, that's 5 successes. We definitely succeed there. But dice are tricksy, and it could end up being 2 or 3 successes. Then, we're fucked, and we spent an extra 1m, 1wp.

                        On the other hand, with an excellency and a stunt, this charm becomes very powerful. I think gating it behind WP is smart, but it might be worth it to gate it behind a battle limit. Maybe "Twice per Scene," and decreasing the cost from 5m , 1wp to 3m, 1wp.

                        Ren9077 , here's an idea for an awareness / dodge hybrid charm:

                        Battle Ending Intuition
                        Cost: 2m, 1wp Requirements: Awareness 4, Dodge 5, Essence 2, Prerequisite Charms: Safety Between Heartbeats, Any Keen Sense Charm, Keywords: Uniform, Counterattack, Perilous

                        The Solar's senses are deeply attuned to the clamor and chaos around her, and all movements are laid bare before her. Whenever the solar dodges and attack which causes her opponent to lose any initiative, she may make an immediate disarm gambit. She may add any initiative lost by her attacker to make up the cost for this gambit.

                        Fool Catching Prana
                        Cost -, Requirements: Awareness 5, Dodge 5, Essence 2. Prerequisite Charm: Battle Ending Intuition. Keywords: Uniform, Counterattack, Perilous
                        This permanently upgrades the prerequisite, allowing the solar to make any gambit counter attack in response to a successful dodge. Once per scene, unless she successfully dodges 3 attacks in a row.

                        Heavy Arms , I've been thinking about what sorts of interesting mechanical stuff could be gotten out of delaying actions. I've also thought of breaking up clashes for the Solar's benefit. Here's one for clashes.
                        Time Stopping Praxis
                        Cost: 5m, 1i, Requirements: Awareness 5, Essence 2, Prerequisite Charms: Any Keen Sense, Surprise Anticipation Method, Keyword: Clash

                        Whenever a solar would be in a clash, she may instead opt to split the clash into a normal combat action for both members of the clash, and may choose who goes first. If she chooses to go second, she may take a free full defense or a free aim action before her opponent makes their attack.

                        I was also thinking about one that combines Heavy Arms and Ren9077 ideas. Here's one that keys off of solar melee.

                        Blade Anticipation Method
                        Cost 1m, 1i Requirements: Awareness 5, Melee 5, Essence 2, Prerequisite Charms: Any Two Keen Senses, One Weapon Two Blows, Keyword: Uniform, Perilous

                        The Solar marks her target and girds herself, tracing the flow of her enemies hand until it reaches its zenith, misses, and leaves an opening. Upon using this charm, she immediately moves to one tick below her intended target. If she takes no damage from either a decisive or withering attack until her turn, she may activate One Weapon, Two Blows as though she successfully met its requirements, even if she has not.

                        At Essence 4+, this charm may repurchased. Repurchasing this charm allows her to chain One Weapon, Two Blows twice if she successfully meets its requirements in the first or second attack.

                            A few charms I came up with based on the idea of predicting your enemy's actions. The problem is neither of this actually call for an Awareness roll, so they don't fit the second requirement of "reason to have all those long-term Charms up".


                            Stilled Pond Understanding
                            Cost: 2m; Mins: Awareness 4, Essence 1
                            Type: Reflexive
                            Keywords: None
                            Duration: One turn
                            Prerequisite Charms: Surprise Anticipation Method

                            The Lawgiver's insight into action and reaction is unparalleled. There are only so many places a person can go, so many actions each person can take. She places herself at the nexus of these choices, reducing them all to one: strike me.

                            The Solar selects an enemy within close range when activating this Charm. Until her next turn she may reflexively Defend Other the target of any of his attacks. She may only have one instance of this Charm active at a time.



                            Rippling Interference Method
                            Cost: 1m; Mins: Awareness 5, Essence 1
                            Type: Reflexive
                            Keywords: None
                            Duration: Instant
                            Prerequisite Charms: Stilled Pond Understanding

                            The Solar's foes move as one to strike her down, their planned blows etching out a delicate pattern of bright-edged steel. She moves once, and the pattern turns against itself.

                            Whenever the Solar is attacked while suffering an onslaught penalty she may activate this Charm to apply that penalty to her opponent's attack dice pool. She still suffers the penalty as normal unless using other Charms to negate it (which does not interfere with this Charm).

                                    PraxisCat How do you feel about this rewrite?
                                    Predictive (Sense) Technique
                                    Cost: 5m, 1wp Requirements: Awareness 3, Essence 2 Prerequisite Charms: Surprise Anticipation Method, Keen (Sense) Technique (Instant, Reflexive) Keyword: Clash, decisive-only

                                    The Solars senses are preternaturally sharped to the point that even the slightest sensory queue forecasts her foes intentions. Whenever she is attacked, instead of applying defense, roll Dexterity + Awareness as a clash action. Compare successes between the attack roll and the awareness roll. If her successes are equal to or greater than her enemies not only is she unharmed by the attack but also allows her to to create a reflexive disengage action against the target, adding her net successes as non-charm dice to the roll. This charm can be used once per combat, reset by returning to base initiative and reaching 11+.

                                    Special activate rules: This charm does not work if the opponent is using a supernatural method of stealth or suppression which the solar does not overcome with awareness or charms; for example, a demon with no smell cannot be noticed with smelling, and a supernaturally silent assassin couldn't be noticed through hearing.
