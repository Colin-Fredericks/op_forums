    My Alternate Solar Favored Abilities

    10-30-2017, 03:17 AM
    This isn't a functional rule set. Its just a personal view of how I personally felt Exalted could be in the mechanics of its design. I love the world of Exalted, the Lore, the ideas - but the game rules are too convoluted for me and my group. So we're basically sticking to DnD and the like.

    That said, I am a stubborn person, and I need to vent my creativity in unusual ways :P So I came up with a version of how I feel the Solars should favor their abilities, which:
    - merged some of the pre-existing abilities for mechanical streamlining
    - shared favored abilities around more (aka no more "you can only choose Archery as your Supernal ability if you are Dawn Caste")
    - still kept certain abilities in each Solar Group being Supernal abilities

    So, before getting into it, I want to cover what abilities got Merged:
    - Dodge and Athletics got merged into "Acrobatics" (though the more 'strength based' athletics got merged into a different ability; see next)
    - Athletics and Resistance got merged into "Vigor" (basically streamlining mobility effects VS power effects)
    - Investigation got merged into "Perception" (both are based on observational skills; though some Investigation rolls would probably use Lore)
    - Presence got merged into single "Presence" (Performance is basically a variation of 'look at me' - so it made sense that both abilities designed to draw a crowd should be a single trait)
    - Ride and Survival got merged into "Wildlife" (with exception of only a few charms; the majority of Survival Charms was about dealing with a familiar. And survival rolls can be suplemented for a Lore roll for how well you KNOW how to SURVIVE or something similar).

    With that covered, let's get into my Caste/ Ability assignment:
    Dawn Brawl Melee Archery Thrown War Vigor
    Zenith Occult Presence Lore War Wildlife Vigor
    Twilight Craft Medicine Lore Perception Sail Acrobatics
    Night Larceny Stealth Archery Thrown Perception Acrobatics
    Eclipse Bureaucracy Socialize Sail Wildlife Acrobatics Vigor
    This spread gives each Caste two exclusively favored Abilities (see the first two emboldened columns), and gives each Caste at least either Vigor or Acrobatics for a defensive ability (Vigor for toughening out damage, Acrobatics for avoiding it).

    There is also an ability bridge into each Caste - Dawn and Zenith both have War, Zenith and Twilight both have Lore, Twilight and Night both have Perception, and Night and Dawn have Archery AND Thrown. This is slightly for flavor, giving the feel that each Caste Flows into the next (Eclipse is the exception, though they share Sail with Twilight, and Wildlife with the Zenith).

    This also means, with exception of two exclusive Abilities per cast, EVERY other ability is favored by two Castes (.

    This DOES change the roles of each Caste member of course (e.g.: why does Zenith have Occult instead of Twilight?), but I've looked into that as well:
    • Dawn are the Warriors who charge the Darkness and make it Run (pretty standard)
    • Zenith are the Leaders who bask in the Light and are noticed - through either drawing and addressing Crowds or Armies, Educating the Masses to make them 'Enlightened', or casting Epic Sorceries that reshape the landscape. Everything about a Zenith is about being seen and noticed (the opposite of a Night caste - see below)
    • Twilight are the Problem Solvers, who reflect on a newly approaching problem (i.e.: the darkness) and find a solution. They build devices and sail on their creations, and find new ways to heal new afflictions.
    • Night are the Assassins and Spies. They fight with misdirection and through espionage. They are the secondary Warriors (to the Dawn) who attack from a distance with Arrows and Throwing blades; killing without being seen.
    • Eclipse are the Deal Makers. They travel on Horseback or Ship to their meetings, overcoming any obstruction through strength or agility, to bartner agreements through seductive interaction or logistical bureaucracy. Their role is to not to be seen or heard (like a Zenith) but rather negotiate and find social solutions.
    For me, I feel this works, though a lot of the rebalancing would come from the Caste Abilities (which I think do deserve some attention and not just be "tacked on" abilities).

    __________________________________________________ ________________________

    Regardless it an unofficial idea which is probably not going to be considered. But its an idea I've had. And I figured instead of letting it rot in my brain, I'd share it on the grounds maybe someone gets inpsired by it for their own work.

                  Nice feedback! Ty.

                  1) I agree character creation will need be adjusted with this. My idea is to go with characters choosing a Primary Supernal Ability, AND a Secondary Supernal Ability. The Primary must be from their Favored Ability (as listed), and they treat the purchasing of Charms for that Ability as if their Essence was +2 Higher (aka they can have up to Essence 3 Charms in that ability).

                  The Secondary Ability can be ANY ability (not just the favored) and they treat charm purchase as if their Essence was +1 Higher (so they can have Essence 2 Charms for that Ability). It spreads the character creation options around for more interesting combos, while still encouraging theme of what your character is "destined" to be good at.

                  2) I also like the merging of the BS I was toying with it for a while myself, creating the "Bargen" ability (the term gives enough leeway to be making deals - socially or fomally), and think I will go with it. It DOES cause the Eclipse to have only 4 Favored Abilities (instead of everyone else having 5) but I may tweak their Caste Abilities to compensate - sorta like how they already have the ability to learn non-Solar Charms.

                  3) The way its going, its probably going to drop to 16. Can't quite break it to the 15th level, but I think its pretty solid where it is now. The reviewed Chart (mering "Bureacracy and Socialize", "Brawl with Melee" [you can specialize in Unarmed or a Weapon for either effect], and discarding Sail - its Charms will probably find its place in Craft, War, Occult and Presence depending on their function) now looks like this:
                  Dawn Melee Archery Thrown War Vigor
                  Zenith Occult Presence Lore War Vigor
                  Twilight Craft Medicine Lore Perception Acrobatics
                  Night Larceny Stealth Perception Thrown Acrobatic
                  Eclipse Bargen Wildlife n/a Acrobatics Vigor
                  16 Abilities in Total, 10 Exclusive to their Caste, 6 shared between at least 2 Castes.

                  You wanna break Dawns? Because that's how you break Dawns. A monopoly on supernal combat abilities is the heavy lifter in their "actually best at combat this edition" thing.

                  The Dawn are still the Combat Engines they were designed to be. They retain exclusivity for Supernal Melee and Archery, sharing Thrown with the Night Assassins and War with the Leadership of the Zenith. If needed their Caste Abilities will be improved for more Combative Power, so they are still the Brutes and Champions they are destined to be - though I'd like them to have an innate ability to be Intimidating (which they kinda do have) so they have some involvement OFF the battlefield.

                  As for the Zenith getting Occult, I think its fine. And your statement of "seriously" doesn't give me any grounds to reply to your objection. So to respond to you and Hakkonen, my thinking behind it was that the Zenith are "BIG". They are designed to be SEEN. Like the Sun at its peak, people notice them. So their actions are BIG. Nation shaking. They rally crowds into riots, or organize them into Armies. They manipulate energy that reshapes landscapes, devistates nations, changes the history books, etc... They are BIG. Their actions are ground shaking. It ties into their connection to their God - and as living champions of the sun, they use this power to bring change. Sometimes even directed by the UCS itself.

                  As for why the Twilight and Eclipse loose this, its because thematically these two work in a different direction. The Twilight are still big, but their epic creations are physical. They create weapons, warstriders, fortresses, temples, Manses, armors, tools. Their magic exists in the Forge and the Pen. Their structure is more defensive, which comes from the theme of the Light Fading, and them fortifying their homes to survive the darkness until the Light comes. Sorcery is almost typically offensive, so it worked against the Twilight Theme.

                  And the Eclipse are Deal Makers and Negotiatiors. Sorcery CAN come from making these bargen's, but their magic should be in the words and the friends they make. Their most basic Caste ability - to solidify a deal between two parties - is VERY exploitable. Just as the symbol and what they represent, they are about unifying forces - not casting spells to demand change. They are tricksters and manipulators. Not Sorcerers.

                  Keep in mind these are FAVORED abilities. Not exclusive. Any Caste (even a Thuggish Dawn) can learn Occult and become a Spell Caster. This is desined to assign personality and purpose to each caste, and to match what they represent. If you can explain how the image of a Sunset and an Overlapping-of-Celestial-Bodies symbolize "magical power" in the way Sorcery behaves in Exalted, please do.

                  But regardless, thats my opinion on where I'd like Exalted 3.5 to go.

                  Originally posted by Hakkonen View Post
                  Maybe you could just tell us which bits are giving you difficulty, and we can help you.
                  The difficulty is the effort required to play the game and explain things to my group. Exalted takes an EFFORT to learn the rules and, in our current society, unless you are a complete geek - Exalted will intimidate many players away once they hit the Charm selection of the book.

                  To learn how many Charms work, you need to learn how Combat works. And given many RPG players have come from DnD, where each fight round is typically 2-3 dice rolls at most (one roll to hit, one roll to wound), its a MASSIVE change when you have to understand the difference between Withering and Decisive, book keeping initiative order, book keeping motes spent, book keeping anima banner level, book keeping location, making the choice between Dodge or Parry, know the rules about grapples, clashes, etc...

                  And thats at its simple. THEN if someone wants to go into Craft or Sorcery, you have to re-read the ENTIRE chapter on each of these to understand what Gold Points are, Silver Points, Sorcery Motes, rituals, Sorcerous Workings, etc... which again, unless you are a nerd who WANTS to learn this, you'll shut down fast.

                  In the game we attempted, one player asked if they could take the Wyld Shaping Charms at the start of the game. By my calculations, they were allowed to. And that single charm required 3 pages to explain ONE ability!

                  And THEN, even if you get through all the rules, you still need to understand the backstory of Exalted, what is a Deathlord, what is a Rashaka, how a God is a Spirit but how a Spirit is not an Elemental, etc...

                  AND THEN, even if you get past ALLLL of that for character creation, when a player says they want to persuade a Guard to let them in the gate, the Gamemaster is confronted with the ability choice of "do they use Bureacracy, Socialize, Presence, or Performance?"

                  This is comming from someone who has read the rules 3+ times, and understands it enough to make sense of it, but its just unneseccary headache as far as I can see. Even one of the most complicated Character Creation systems, the HERO system, still keeps combat fairly simple - "roll X dice to hit, roll Y dice to wound, deal Z damage to target minus their resistance level".

                  I respect Exalted is not a casual game, but it goes from Complicated to almost requiring a University Lecture to explain its basics. And the disapointment is that its world is great, but the mechanics prevent people getting in.

                  Like other groups I've heard of, I've been tempted to just have my group explore the world of Creation using DnD rules or even the FATE Core system. Those mechanics are easy enough to use, though in doing so it would also drop a lot of what Exalted's characters could do.

                  So if you can offer a way to streamline the entire mechanical sprawl of Exalted, make the whole thing less intimidating to players, accelerate the process that combat occurs, and give complete clarity on all the abilities so no one has any room to second guess "why can't I use Athletics to Throw this Knife as an attack?", then I'd appreciate it.

                                                      For me I see Occult as the opposite of Lore. Lore deals with the Academics of the world (history, literature, human culture, maths, science, mundane knowledge). Occult deals with the supernatural (knowledge of gods, daemons, spirits, elementals, rituals, sorcery). For me I see it as an Academic VS a Priest.
