                    As a ST, I having the characters being from seconds after they exalted, up to a few weeks since they exalted, as the the game started. The idea is to let the players get used to the system (2.5-ish) as the characters get used to their powers. During char-gen, I have informed them that we either have to come up with some explanation of why they have what they have; or they will get it somehow during one of the early game sessions (this isn't limited to artifacts, but also to resources, allies, familiars, etc).

                    So when it comes to artifacts picked during char-gen, there has been...
                    From before the game started:
                    * Family heirloom the character nabbed while running away.
                    * From a heist a couple of days before exalting.
                    * An item given by the character's tribe after exalting, as the character was pushed into a position/rank of the tribe (well, that one was more an important piece for a manse not already having access to).

                    After the game started:
                    * Placed in a show-case, in a hidden manse guarded by a huge demonic guardian, during the first session. The character being able to deactivate and reactivate the defense system, and the creature welcoming the character back, and informing the contract is now fulfilled, before going poof (beside the two artifacts, the player had put some points into having a manse).

                                                            Originally posted by Coik View Post
                                                            Artifacts, particularly weapons, are weird because the game wants them to be fabled weapons of legend all, with storied histories and pedigrees similar to champion racehorses, while simultaneously wanting every Solar to be able to trip over a half dozen of them within seconds of Exalting so they can pick out the exact one that’s absolutely perfect for them, personally.
                                                            ​Hmm, now I'm curious to go back over example Solars who were never meant to be playable and seeing how many of them have Artifacts, because the whole time with this thread I've been thinking about the point that the idea of people getting their weapons from the tombs of their past lives being a common thing for players to do doesn't mean that it is a common thing in the setting.

                                                            ​No matter how many groups describe that as part of the background for their own characters in their own game, it doesn't need to mean that every one of those characters is not the first person to do it in the world, because these games are not taking place in a shared universe.

                                                            ​How many games involve people defeating or taking over the Realm? That doesn't make the Realm a kind of ultra concentrated ghost kingdom.

                                                            ​Even accounting for that, there are still some faults in the logic, from either end. On the one hand, something being commonly associated with people who are themselves rarer than hens teeth doesn't exactly make those things common. Quite a few monarchs have been in possession of jewel-encrusted gold crowns, and yet these things are not cheap trinkets. Crowns that, incidentally, often have distinct appearances and names and stories to them. Rare people and rare things going hand in hand doesn't exactly make them less rare.

                                                            ​The other side to it is the fallacy of assuming that a thing being merely uncommon (as opposed to extremely rare) doesn't mean that it can't be unique with an interesting story to it.

                                                            I mean, champion racehorses? How many of those do you think there are?
