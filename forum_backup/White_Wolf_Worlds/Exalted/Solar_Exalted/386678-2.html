                        I freely admit a childish fondness for big, impressive, flashy things, which regrettably puts Martial Arts as a distant third out of the Evocations-Sorcery-MA group. Not that there aren't flashy things to be found in Martial Arts, but -- outside SMAs and the more unusual styles -- it's not what comes quickest to mind. Still cool, and I can see some characters of mine digging it, but they're definitely not where I expect I'll be gravitating. Though who knows! There are some neat styles in the Core, and Vance's write-ups may well knock my socks off.

                        Evocations are probably a close second of the three. Like Weimann above, I'm usually not that keen on items and equipment in RPGs. With Evocations, though, I dunno. Something about the way they've been described has my interest and I can see a lot of potential there. The idea that multiple bearers of a given artifact can each draw different sets of Evocations from it really sells me on the idea that it is about a personal connection the character has forged with the artifact, not just a bunch of powers. I dig it, and it makes me want to create a character where the artifact and its Evocations do make up a good swath of his capabilities.

                        Sorcery's topping the list at the moment, and just about everyone who's mentioned it has already iterated why. I really, really want to see the initiation paths, as they're probably what's most recently captivated me; some of the teasers Vance has dropped really have my interest. And that's on top of sorcerous workings sounding just ridiculously flush with possibilities, and spells sounding less suicidal in most forms of combat. The idea of different mote-gathering methods for shaping spells also sounds like it could add some tactical depth and incentives to play in certain interesting ways. So, MA and Evocations are cool, but they just don't feel like they can compete with Sorcery. We'll see how things shape out in the end, but it's gonna be exciting. ^_^

                        Ah. And Eclipse-Only Charms also sound neat. Just... >.> Not sure where to put them. Maybe a bit spiffier than Martial Arts...? I dunno. Looking forward to them, too, though!
