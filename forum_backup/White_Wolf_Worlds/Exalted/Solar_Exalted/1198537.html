      I could imagine...

      A Dawn champion, who always spares his foes. He preaches by showing how mighty virtue is and how powerful solar faith is. He strikes his enemies down, only so that he can lift them up again. He tells them doubt and cynicism were their weakness and he has cut them out.

      A Twilight scholar, who studies and explores the infinite vastness of Creation and its secrets. She seeks to understand the beautiful mystery of Creation that the Unconquered Sun gave everything to protect. The more she learns, the more she understands the Sun.

      A Night magistrate, who punishes the wicked from the dark. Though he is a thing of shadows, he understands that even the Sun's shadow is righteous. He does not preach with mercy or lead with compassion. Instead he shocks the unbelievers into faith with fear.

      And an Eclipse politician, who seeks to bring everything into harmony. To her understanding, the Unconquered Sun's wisdom is infinite and only a perfect world can even begin to imagine it. To that goal, she wishes to reconnect and rebuild Creation so everyone is more ready to witness the Sun.

      ...though, I concede that the Zenith Caste is most likely to be the Sun's chief priests, since that's their shtick. Perhaps those above non-Zenith Priest of the Sun concepts don't have the same connection as the Zenith Caste has with the Sun.

                  I mean, there's still a working thaumaturgical ritual that the sun hears the prayer from, which if successful prompts him to instantly review the entirety of who you are as a person and every decision you've ever made. If he thinks you're a truly virtuous person with no other way out of the quandry you're petitioning him about, he'll send a Mouse of the Sun to advise you or subtly aid you. If he doesn't, he'll send a Mouse of the Sun to give you a disease which you can only keep from killing you through regular prayer, and which can only be permanently cured by begging the sun for mercy in person.

                  If you were going to set up a priesthood of just enlightened terrestrials, or dragon kings, or something, meanwhile, you could use Eye of Heaven Style to ensure successful prayers - but I don't think the sun would be chill with that many disembodied hearts.

                  Really, though, I think proper Priesthood to the sun is still a viable thing, if you're willing to deal with Immaculates. It's just, your prayers will be handled by the sun's secretary's secretary's secretary, instead.
