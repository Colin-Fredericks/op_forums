    *only recently realized the degree to which people know more about what's in Ex3 than I do...*

    I bought some custom charm slots on the kickstarter and I need to pick what I want to do with it.

    - One possibility is a performance Charm to influence nature. The official position is that Orpheus-like control of nature would have to come from Sorcery, but it's so iconically tied to his music, and he's such an iconic "Solar Hero" in mythology, it feels like it needs some representation.

    - I also need to see the degree to which using insults/repartee in combat is mechanically represented. If it isn't, some socialize Charms to do that would be appropriate.

    - Another possibility is something tied directly to the fertility of the land. Again this is something that the official position leaves to Sorcery, but is deeply tied to the iconic role of "Solar Heroes" in myth, who are primarily worshiped as fertility figures, particularly as related to descent into the Underworld and return, corresponds to the rising of the sun, and the rebirth of the land. Perhaps a Survival Charm, but one that requires you be the recognized ruler of the land effected (and doesn't impact supernatural blights, which you still need to confront head on).

    - A secret keeping Charm tied to the "false identity" stuff I've heard about, that conceals your actual intentions even from yourself so any truth-detection powers turn up the false truth that you yourself temporarily believe? Is that too Sidereal?

                            I don't think there's any inkling of fertility associated with Orpheus, Hercules or Gilgamesh themselves in their individual descents to the Underworld. Especially when one of those excursions had nothing whatsoever to do with death paving the way for life, and at least one other was made with the intent to violate the order of things. The idea is represented much better by someone like Osiris, whose death and ressurection as the lord of the underworld is in fact supposed to represent the cycle of wilting and renewal, and by extension fertility as is evident by the worship he received.

                            But I'm not sure he counts as a Solar Hero given his feats amount to "dying", and I stand by my ground that Solars are not intended to be representative of the concept.
