                              I think effects that seem like telekinesis are completely out of theme for the Solar, except for those Solar who plunge into the esoteric or other sorcererous arts.

                              Solar flight shouldn't seem like telekinesis. It also shouldn't seem like Superman flight either (ye whimsical gods am I tired of hearing the Solar being compared to Superman), it's an expression of human might ascending to meet the challenge with which it contends.

                              What I basically mean, is that when a Solar does something amazing it does not do it casually. A Lunar can casually turn into a bird and fly. If telekinesis were a spell any sorcerer could cast it and then casually lift whatever with their mind.

                              The Solar does not casually do any of the amazing things they do. They expend effort, will and being; through these things they ascend.

                              It's a human skill, in the sense what humanity (or at least some humans) do/does is rise up to meet a challenge.

                              As for energy bolts or artifacts from nowhere, I'd go with Ki/Chi/Essence whatever being a true and existing thing in the setting as part of the reason. The Solar expresses their judgment and swordsmanship in the form of essence born trappings because that's who they are as an individual, the way a gods panoply expresses who they are as divine beings.

                              The Solar with appropriate melee charms isn't the best mortal swordsman ever (though they could be) they are divine and incomparable expression of what it means to be a swordsman, the way myth and imagination writs it large.

                              TLDR; these things that Solars do that seem to clash with their identity as the most human of Exalted, clash because the Solar expresses human identity in a divine and transcendent (not excellent or perfect, but specifically transcendent) way.

                              ​Not necessarily the best swordsman, the divine swordsman.

                                              Telekinesis really only works for Solars in very specific contexts. Calling a sword to your hand isn't really telekinesis, as the sword in question is a weapon that you've bonded with and practiced with to the extent where it is considered metaphysically apart of you, you're actually just calling a piece of yourself back to yourself.

                                              Likewise with Athletics flight, it's not that you are psychically propelling yourself though the air, it's that your ability to jump and balance on impossible surfaces is such that you can do that with air currents itself. With lifting and throwing objects, the best you could get is actually grappling with a large object and throwing it without the object collapsing under it's own weight, which I think was mentioned above. Even then, that isn't proper telekinesis, it's an extension of your supernatural strength and ability to distribute weight.
