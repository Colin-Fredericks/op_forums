                                I always find it funny/weird how mad people get about Solars being the best at every subsystem when they don't actually have a native subsystem of their own. Lunars had shapeshifting, Sidereals had Astrology and Dragon-Blooded had elemental teamwork synergy.

                                The only thing Solars have is to be really really good at the things that everyone has access to.

                                I actually found that it really helps drive home their nature as the "Exemplary human skill" Exalted. They don't have an esoteric subsytem because normal humans don't have any esoteric magic to use natively, and any magic they do have is the result of mastering a particular skill, which is exactly what Martial Arts/Sorcery/Evocations are meant to be expressions of, Mastery of Facepunching, Occult Forces and your favored weapon.
