    When it comes to Caste and Favored Abilities, an Exalt will always be able to master those, barring extremely unusual circumstances (Brigid). It's just the magical benefit of Exaltation. Even if a Solar was completely illiterate prior to Exaltation, if Linguistics were one of his Caste Abilities, he'd be able to master writing and languages.

    But non-Caste, non-Favored Abilities, a Solar is no better at picking those up than any mortal and could find himself struggling to get good at that Ability.

        A lot of these arguments sound like "I'm choosing for my character not to do this," or "I'm choosing not to spend XP on this," which is different from "they're actually physically or mentally unable to do this."

        It's weird mashing up the narrative aspect ("he's incapable of doing so") with the mechanical ("I choose to spend XP elsewhere"). A Solar swordsman PC has no obstacle preventing her from raining to Resistance 5, but that still requires a lot of effort (and OOC, a lot of CP that the player might blanch at), so the player stops at a rating of 3 where they can buy all the Resistance Charms they really want, fluffs it as "I really have no interest in going even harder at Yu-Sham CrossFit" and maybe the player goes back to it later or maybe not.

        For a pure NPC, even then it flows more from "this is the role of the NPC in the story and these are her personality and goals. It would take something monumentally disruptive to her life for her to get into Survival training, let alone become puissant at at...in other words, she could dabble in learning it if it was literally a matter of life or death, but that's just exceptionally unlikely, and it's even less likely she would ever achieve the kind of mastery represented by a 5."

                                                    I don't think human beings have the ability to develop the level of universal aptitude in real life that would be represented by having five dots in every Ability. Even the best and the brightest polymaths are only able to achieve so much in the spread of Abilities they're able to master—eventually, you're going to not be good at something, and moreover, you're going to be bad at learning it.

                                                    It'd be easy enough to say that Exaltation removes that limitation. I don't think that's a good idea. The "dehumanizing" effect, where suddenly every Exalt in Creation is a potential omnicompetent master without any of the human personality traits or flaws that intrinsically limit that, detracts from verisimilitude—plus, it basically never comes up. How many characters, player or NPC, have you seen with Charms from all twenty-five abilities?

                                                        Originally posted by hippokrene View Post
                                                        Given what we're told of Solars, however, is it possible that Unfurling Steel Blossom simply can't become that hearty and resistant? It's just a limit she'll have to live with outside of external magical forces or the like.
                                                        You're asking if it is possible that a particular Solar might suffer from a kind of metaphysical limitation not otherwise experienced by other Solars, which makes it objectively impossible to learn something that ought to fall within their realm of capability? My instinct is to say "no". But this comes with several caveats:

                                                        1. It's your game. You can always make an exception, if you think it makes the story better.
                                                        2. There is a difference between difficulty and impossibility. It can absolutely be more difficult for one Solar to master a particular set of abilities than it is for another Solar to master the same set, and it's absolutely believable that if s/he simply finds it very difficult and time consuming to learn, s/he may opt to stop trying entirely and then justify giving up by deciding that the difficulty = impossibility. People do this all the time, and Solars are people (see: Luke and Yoda in the swamp with the X-Wing).

                                                        In other words: no, I do not think that there are flawed Solar Exaltations floating around which are metaphysically incapable of imparting mastery of things within their realm of potential. But I do think that there are Solars running around who might be psychologically unwilling to push past the difficulty of mastering things which are harder for them than others.
