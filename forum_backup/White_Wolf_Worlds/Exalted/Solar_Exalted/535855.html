    Stealing Charms - Using Solar Charms for other Splats

    06-01-2015, 04:01 PM
    In one of my games, my Storyteller had his Sidereal NPC use Letter within Letter technique (or a custom Sidereal version of it). I though having a charm that allows Sidereals to secretly send messages fits pretty well for the Splat.

    Since everyone and their mother seems to have access to Ox Body and Lunars have access to Keen Sense Technique, so there is precedent for other splats having access to some Solar Charms.

    Here are some ideas I had:

    Sidereals - Harmonious Academic Methology (Lore), Legendary Scholar Curriculum (Lore), Letter within a Letter Technique (Linguistics)
    Lunars - Chaos Repelling Pattern (Lore), Wyld-Shaping Technique (Lore), Wyld Cauldron Technology (Lore)

    I also considered running a DB Dynast game with building the Dragonblooded characters with Solar Charms (replace sun imagery with appropriate Elemental Imagery).

    Has anyone else does anything like this in their own games? How did it work out?

      Everybody has a Charm called Ox-Body. What they don't have is the same Charm.

      You have to be super, super careful when exporting Solar Charms onto other Exalt types, because Solar Charms are usually a lot more badass, especially at the high end, than the Charms other Exalt types get, and also are often not really entirely appropriate for those Exalt types themes.

      Like, I would never export the 1e or 2e Solar charmtree into the Dragon-Blooded, even if they are probably the Exalt type best suited to receive it. Giving the Dragon-Blooded access to easy perfects of all sorts, easy flurries, the true jaw-dropping insanity of the Solar Athletics and Survival trees? Wyld-Shaping Technique and Wyld Cauldron Technology in the hands of the Realm and Lookshy? I mean, wow. That would be one hell of a gonzo game, for the very brief amount of time it would take for your the Realm and it's thousands upon thousands of Solar charm-users to just completely roll over everyone and everything in their way.

      There's nothing wrong with looking at the occasional Solar Charm and going "yoink!". I would advise heavily against exporting any charmtree wholesale. Your game has a decent chance of just breaking.

          I've always toyed with the idea of creating 'ability styles' that aren't martial arts based. The idea there is if you want to make certain charms available to everyone, take part of the tree and make it into a style, maybe add a few extra prereqs, and have it be equivalent to CMA in who can learn it.

          Beyond that, if you only want to take one or two charms, that's likely much safer and easier to do, just make sure those specific charms suit the Exalt type you're giving them to.

          I fully agree with Murcushio though, in that letting Dragonbloods have access to all Solar charms (or even a handful) is a very bad idea and gives you an entirely different setting just by definition of what dragonblood can normally do.

                          Well, I feel like it is all about how well the Charm fits with the splat. And whether it is necessary.

                          Particularly, if they're already things you can do by stunting Sidereal or Lunar Charms, with an attitude of ST generosity towards stunts and using the Storyteller system, I'd honestly be tempted just to let them do that. Like if your Sidereal wants to teach some NPC and they're have some Charm that makes them amazingly persuasive or able to communicate (Blue Vervain Binding?), I'd just say they can stunt that and the NPC is really inspired and goes off and trains and is now amazingly better at that ability. It's not like it's game breaking or anything and it fits as a capability the Sidereals should have as characters.

                          If they want to cook up an whole army, then it should be within Sidereal Astrological capability and general craftiness to weave destiny such that a force of great warriors arises among the people and comes together. What kind of version of Sidereals is acceptable where they *can't* secretly shape fate such that the Outlaws of the Marsh learn their skills and come together, and eventually become the champions of the people? If the Sidereal Astrology and Charm system as it exists can handle this capability, just let the players roll play and stunt their way to it. If it can't, then I don't think adapting the Solar Charm plus asking the player to stunt it with Sidereal flavour is a big deal. But seriously think through whether the players can do it without adapting any Solar Charms first.

                          Looking at the other example, Letter Within Letter Technique, does it make sense that the Sidereals *shouldn't* be able to conceal a message cleverly within another message such that only a particular listener can understand it? Given everything we know about the sort of characters Sidereals are meant to be (subtle encoding of messages and recognition of messages not exactly being a weak point of theirs). If so, why not let them have the Charm, or even let them do it without the Charm, just as a stunt (albeit without some of the special qualities granted by the Charm)?

                          Or with Lunars and Wyld Shaping Technique, what is it that they need the Charm to do? If it's something which makes sense for them they can achieve it by some other means, they don't really need the Charm. Like, if it makes sense for them to retreive treasure from the Wyld by exploring the Wyld and claiming it from there, just let them do that. If it's making new land, do they really *need* to make new land (Creation has plenty)? And so on.
