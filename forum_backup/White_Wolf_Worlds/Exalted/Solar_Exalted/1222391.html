    Not Necromancy, but Solars raising the dead

    05-27-2018, 09:14 AM
    I am trying to write a game to play soon, just need to find the time, but an idea hit me and I wanted to share it. As Abyssals gain strength from the simple act of bloody combat, at the least corpses are left and ghosts rise. What if, a Solar could bind the life of a fallen person to their body? Think of it as a rally to stay alive till the job is done. I haven't read through all of 3Ed to see all new stuff so I may stick to 2Ed. But, I want to see what others thought about this idea, whether this would be a charm, spell or even an artifact. I can see a field of freshly fallen soldiers rising to complete their Lords edicts and burning in a Holy fire at the conclusion.

    I would really like to see if others see this as an interesting story tool.

                          Yes, it is from the 3e Core page 423

                          "The Solar represents a new dawn on the last day of Creation.
                          Her coming foretells the battle’s end, a day of blood for the
                          wicked and light to vanquish darkness from the world once
                          more. When her army is routed, the Lawgiver may call upon
                          glories past—in this life and those that came before—to create
                          a rallying tactic that miraculously reforms her army. This
                          Charm is an automatically successful rally action (p. 210).
                          It can be used once per scene, but it can be reset if the Solar
                          succeeds at vanquishing a terrible foe or conquering a powerful
                          enemy through the might of her army."

                                  In the game I ran, the Twilight had a guilty conscience after her Limit Break resulted in significant casualties among the Dawn's mercenary company. We were spitballing sorcerous workings she might undertake to make up for it, and one of the ideas was:

                                  Unblinking Vanguard of the Sun would be an Ambition 2 Celestial working that safeguards the lives of the affected battle group. Searing heat automatically cauterizes wounds, blinding flashes interrupt deathblows, and if the unit is completely shattered, the spirits of the fallen tarry upon the battlefield as ghostly legionnaires of living sunlight to negate the defense penalty suffered during slaughter actions.

                                  Save for the last bit, it largely served limit losses suffered after battle, which is not something comprehensively represented, mechanically.

                                                      ​Sudden idea: the Charm would build off of Tiger Warrior Training Technique and only function on people that had been trained to have perfect morale. It both removes ambiguity from the scenarios in which the Charm can be used (a battle group with perfect morale reduced to nothing can be handily assumed to basically have been killed in its entirety), and creates a stronger thematic basis for the effect both in terms of the troops' bond with their commander, and in the strength of spirit that the Solar is able to kindle to get them to rise up for one last crack at it.

                                                      ​Maybe also limited to only being used for troops that fell in pursuit of a Defining Intimacy that is shared with the Solar.
