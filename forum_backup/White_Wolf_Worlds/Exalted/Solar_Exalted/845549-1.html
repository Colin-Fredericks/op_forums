                    Originally posted by Wandering View Post
                    Any hypothetical speculation like this really needs to be approached from &quot;what serves the needs of a gaming group&quot; to have any value, because any answer that doesn't make for a fun gaming session is not useful.

                    If you want an exalted level-monster to hit home to your players what can happen if you wander the Wyld without protection, the answer is that things can get pretty freaky and still be an Exalt.

                    f you have a player that wants to weaponize the Wyld to de-exalt people the answer is probably that the circumstances that can cause it are too difficult and complex to arrange for it to be a practical tool, but maybe possible to arrange as a one-off tactic for an otherwise impossible-to-kill enemy.

                    If you're planning on using it on a player, that probably depends somewhat on wether you're using it as just a dramatic way of killing a character off, or as part of some sort of redemption arc, where the player loses their power and now has to find some way to regain it (or learn that they don't really need it to make a difference and accomplish their goals). Definitely keep an eye on how to keep things fun for the player you use it on.

                                                    Originally posted by CapitanTypo View Post

                                                    I was trying to think of ways that a PC might get to encounter the consciousness of their past incarnation.

                                                    Cool. There's probably lots of ways to pull this off, actually. The most commonly used one is typically to have them run into the ghost of their former exaltation who's still kicking around the underworld. Or you could have the players run into an artifact (humanoid and/or animate, or not) that their former self imbued with a perfect copy of their conciousness. With the Wyld, being a source of strangeness that it is, you could just have the characters meet someone who seemingly is their past-life self, still living! Is he from an alternate Creation somewhere out there in the Wyld where the Usurpation never happened? Does he just believe he is, but was simply created from the chaos ex nihilo with memories to fit? Is it actually some strange Wyld spirit that simply disguises itself by reading and mimicing the past life memories of the Exalted it encounters? The reality is, you wouldn't even need to have an answer, if it's not something the players have any practical way of finding out.
