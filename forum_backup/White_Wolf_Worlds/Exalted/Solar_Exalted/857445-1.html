                                    Building off of what Raziere said, but sometimes I feel it might be healthier for the game as a whole if we were to cut out the other splats and allow their special abilities to be optional avenues of power for Solars, similar to Sorcery or Martial arts. I feel it would really diversify the Solar's options and allow for more varied characters, as well as smooth over the setting's rather messy history and sociopolitical situation. That way you could have a cyborg Solar, a fate ninja Solar, a shapeshifter Solar, and an elemental Solar all in the same circle without worrying about having to justify it.

                                    This would be a natural evolution of the game line, and would work well for a wider verity of games.
