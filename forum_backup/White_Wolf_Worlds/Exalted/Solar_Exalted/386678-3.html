                        Originally posted by Jen View Post
                        I'm pretty sure that the other way around, here a quote by John from the Ask the Dev thread:
                        From the same thread:
                        The Solar Charm set does indeed lack any Charm (that I can remember off the top of my head) as singularly jaw-dropping as Volcano Cutter’s final published Evocation, All Creation Turns to Ash[1]. (Yes, we extended the cascade by one more effect.) ACTtA does… not quite what it says on the tin, but change the name to “Hey, Where’d Nighthammer District Go?” and you’re pretty close.

                        But if you don’t have fundamental combat skills, you will never, ever, ever manage to actually perform the necessary set-up for that Evocation’s deployment, much less use it without killing yourself and your whole Circle in the process.

                        [1] Except maybe Wyld-Shaping Technique.
                        and
                        Trying to rock a character with nothing but Evocations is a bit like playing Street Fighter, picking Ryu, and trying to win using no moves other than the dragon punch. Unlessyour opponent forgot to plug his controller in, that is not going to work out for you.
                        That suggests to me that yes, evocations without the required base skills are weaker then the "pure" base skills, but if you mix these base skills with evocation, you come out ahead - So, given an equal level of XP, daiclave evocations alone are weaker (because they can't be used) then melee charms alone, but either of these are weaker than a proper balance between daiclave evocations and melee charms.

                        'Course, "Stronger" in this case also depends on what kind of situation you're in. If you don't want to burn down everything around you, "All Creation Turns to Ash" might not be the effect you want to use. So, "Situational to some degree" is another factor for evocations.
