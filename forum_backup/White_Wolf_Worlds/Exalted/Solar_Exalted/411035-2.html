                            Originally posted by HalfTangible View Post
                            Yes, I know, we covered that - I was addressing the argument that having your anima flare is terrible for stealth by pointing out that if your anima is flaring, stealth is already out the window as an option for you.
                            Except, you quoted me.
                            And I never said it was terrible for stealth.

                            That's kind of like swinging your fists at a guy whose three blocks and fourteen years away from you.
                            1. OP asked "what are Solars" (and Sidereals, and Lunars, and Terrestrials) "best at".
                            2. I responded (with typical, obvious, grandiose Solar god-king imagery) for what Solars excel at.
                            3. Murcushio (probably in jest) responded by saying that the Night Caste "would like to have a word with me" (and then admitted to wanting to include Eclipse, only to realize that the most famous Eclipse in the setting literally solved a negotiation with a sucker-punch).
                            4. I followed on the Night Caste example with their anima banner - I like (and was laughing at the absurd genius of it) that they continue to be stealthy, while glowing like a small explosion, a blackened silhouette against blinding light. It wasn't a criticism of Solar stealth - I admired the fact that they found a "brute force" way to depict a traditionally "subtle, delicate" thing (stealth).
                            5. Thereafter there sprung up people trying to argue that a Night Caste could use their anima power to hide the banner, to quibble.
                            6. My response to that was that Sidereals and Lunars can be stealthy -- regardless of caste -- for a much reduced cost -- and with much less effort -- even with their animas flaring.
                            Instead of addressing OP (with a broad, all-inclusive discussion and comparison of different splats and castes), some people have mistakenly confused this for a Solar Night-Caste Stealth thread.
