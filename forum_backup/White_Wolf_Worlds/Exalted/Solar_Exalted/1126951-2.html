                                    Hmm, thinking about it... if one focused on her character beats rather than the precise scope and form of her power, sure, Elsa fits fairly well with a Dragon Blood in general and a Dynast in particular. That whole thing where you spend the early part of your life repressed and fearful and then it all explodes out in a moment of catharsis and wild abandon, accompanied by possibly dangerous elemental expressions, that's the basic life story of a Dynast and fits with a lot of Outcastes as well. You don't really have a straightforward equivalent in anybody to the part in which you grow up with an anxiety about your powers being a danger to yourself and others that needs to be held back at all costs... unless you had a story in which somebody was the child with the Dragon Blood who was raised by parents who would really rather prefer that she never Exalted, and had the impression that this could be done by preventing her from experiencing strong emotions.
