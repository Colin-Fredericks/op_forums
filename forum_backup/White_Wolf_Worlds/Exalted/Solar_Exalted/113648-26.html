    Bureaucracy:

    Immediate Measure of Correction
    The Solar may activate this charm when one of her subordinates fails a bureaucracy roll and informs the Solar of her failure. This charm allows the Solar to come up with a plan to fix the mistake and make a bureaucracy roll in her subordinates stead. If the Solar is successful, the mistake is immediatel rectified according to the Solar's instructions. This charm also allows the Solar to immediately rectify any deliberate error or forgery which she notices within an organization under her control.

            Thrown:

            Thorn Amidst the Barren Trees
            The Solar may activate this charm to protect herself from disarmament. As long as this charm is active, even if the Solar's thrown weapons run, are disarmed, are stolen or are confiscated, the Solar will still have one weapons of her choice in reserve to perform one more thrown attack. The charm immediately ends once the Solar uses this attack and cannot be reactivated until the Solar acquires more weapons.

                        Craft:

                        Universal Alchemy of [Magical Material]
                        Upon learning this charm, the Solar selects a magical material which she has used in the past to construct an artifact. Each colour of jade counts as its own magical material. The Solar can include the characteristics of this magical material in any item she crafts, even if she has none of the material on hand. Even if the Solar would normally be required to use this magical material to construct or repair an artifact, she may replace it with any other. The Solar must always provide a sufficient total quantity of magical materials when crafting artifacts. This charm is often used to replace magical materials which the Solar finds difficult to acquire, or to add the desirable characteristics of a magical material which the Solar finds aesthetically displeasing.

                                    I'm moving over the weekend, so I'm not sure if I'll have Internet access. Posting three days worth of charms just in case.

                                    Athletics:

                                    Indomitable Bull Spirit
                                    The Solar may activate this charm during a contest of strength. In order to clearly win the contest, the Solar's opponent must obtain a margin of success greater than the Solar's Essence rating.

                                    Bureaucracy:

                                    Stilling the Paper River
                                    This charm supplements a project carried out within an organization which the Solar is a member of. The extended roll interval of any competing or opposing projects are increased by a number of time units equal to the Solar's Essence rating. If the roll interval takes place every few minutes, this charm adds (Essence) minutes to the interval, if the roll takes place every day, the charm adds (Essence) days and so forth.

                                    Thrown:

                                    Shattered Earth and Sky Rain
                                    The Solar makes a thrown attack against all targets within short range of an area. Her weapons fly and ricochet off each other. Any target who is hit by the attack and who does not leave the affected area before the Solar's next action takes damage again at the start of the Solar's next action. This second damage roll cannot be enhanced by charms or any other means, and it uses the natural dice pool of the previous damage roll.

                                        Occult:

                                        Devouring the Voice of God
                                        This charm supplements a decisive attack against a mortal who leads a god's cult. If the cultist dies, the Solar uses the link between priest and god to devour a portion of the god's power. For a number of months equal to the Solar's Essence rating, the god's own Essence rating is reduced by half the Solar's Essence rating, to a minimum of one. A god may only be affected by one iteration of this charm at any given time.
