                                            I think that the more robust and elegantly-designed the system for disease and injuries is (or how robust and elegantly-designed the basic task resolution mechanics are) will have a big effect on how fun Medicine Charms are to use. </statement of the obvious>

                                            Looking at the 2E corebook Medicine tree, it seems basically adequate. I think that Contagion-Curing Touch was probably not the best way to model "Solars can do their work even in difficult situations" - there's definitely room for that sort of effect, but "you can do even more stuff with a stunt" might have been better than "you don't need to stunt anymore." IDK.

                                            So, I guess, some things it might be cool to see from 3E (a lot of these are probably not universal enough to warrant a slot in the Corebook, admittedly ^^
                                            • Charm to aid in my diagnoses (or at least to prevent misdiagnoses).
                                            • Charm building off of it that makes serious health problems stand out to my Solar like they're lit up with neon: they'd be like a cancer-sniffing dog, or able to tell that someone was healing from a stab wound just based on their body language, or able to detect the signs of a "social disease" as soon as the next morning. Assessment of peoples' health would become almost automatic and reflexive, so perceptive would be the Solar doctor.
                                            • I'll also second Chejop Kejak's request for more general information-gathering Medicine Charms that let my Solar tell things like what position an athlete plays based on which of his muscles are the most developed, or someone's entire medical history based on the taste of their sweat or the smell of their breath, or similarly wild stuff.
                                            • Charm to increase the effectiveness with which the Solar can formulate their own medicines - maybe that's more appropriate in Craft, IDK, or could be cross-prereq'd or something, but "Hermit Joe's Miracle Swamp Weed Tea" should totally be a thing my Twilight can formulate.
                                            • Maybe not some sort of effect that just straight-up cures diseases or removes poison, at least not early on in a tree, but something that buffs up a Solar doctor's ability to fortify their patient's own strength for the purposes of rolls to fight off infection/poison/the influence of malevolent spirits/etc. or battle their way back from the brink of death.
                                            • Some sort of "in the zone" Charm that allows a Solar to push through hunger, fatigue, pain, their own illness, distractions, and so on as long as they're devoting their full attention to caring for those who need their medical help. "The only time the legendary dragon-chaser Huo Sixth-Beard could resist the call of the opium pipe was when some poor soul was bleeding on his operating table..." - y'know?
                                            • Acupressure!! Obviously actually messing with other characters' Essence respiration is probably detrimental to fun, so this would require some careful design, but Solar Medicine should totally let you suss out the Essence flows along the meridians of living creatures - even spirits. Might aid in the diagnosis of any detrimental supernatural effects that are afflicting a character? Possibly build into some sort of "sense the Essence of living creatures as glowy silhouettes on your HUD" effect, if that's appropriate?
                                            • I think it was in Fringe that I saw this - at one point a shapeshifting character had to break the bones of their face so they could reknit it into a new appearance. There was also a wuxia film (I think it was "Judge Dee and the Phantom Flame"?) where a few of the characters did similar face-changing shenanigans with acupuncture. I don't know if that would be a Charm on its own, but it'd be cool for Solars to be able to do something like that, if only as part of a broader cosmetic-surgery Charm. Gotta disguise Jim Kirk as a Romulan!
                                            • Maybe some sort of "independent automatic treatment" effect - like, you give someone a prescription or a diet/exercise regimen and if they follow it, it's like they have you assisting their treatment rolls for whatever malady afflicts them except you don't actually have to do anything. This could obviously upgrade into Medicine-based Training effects based on healthy living and internal alchemy.
                                            • Someone suggested a "Don't worry, I'm a doctor!" effect that allowed a Solar to confidently seize command of a medical emergency, and I think that's really cool. It could also build into an effect that allows a Solar doctor to dispel a patient's fear and panic with their masterful bedside manner.

                                                Originally posted by MissMaddy View Post

                                                That's another option, certainly.

                                                My point is that Exalted has to decide if madness...

                                                A) ... is a biological issue caused by improper physical condition. This is actually how modern science more-or-less understand these things..
                                                Errr. My understanding is that modern science (in as far as there is a consensus on these things) holds that it's a lot more complicated than a simple biological cause in quite a few situations. There are cases where there is something biologically wrong and that's mostly it, but there are also cases where it's a complex interaction of biology, personal history and social surroundings (this isn't an argument in favour of psychoanalytic approaches, mind, as they tend not to work) -- this is why you can sometimes control an illness with drugs combined with an approach like cognitive behavioural therapy, or (in mild cases, speaking from personal experience) with CBT-type approaches alone.

                                                Some statistics via a psychology grad student friend of mine regarding schizophrenia, which is one of the illnesses most generally held to be strongly biological:

                                                1) Identical twins have only a 50% chance of developing schizophrenia if one of them does. They share the same biology, so environment and social factors are just as important as biology.

                                                2) In treating schizophrenia, drugs and other purely biological approaches are actually some of the least efficient if used alone. Family therapy, where the family of the patient undergo therapy together with the patient, reduces the relapse rate from 75% (if using drugs alone) to 50%, and CBT even more so.

                                                Modern science does not understand mental illness as having purely biological roots. (I can get citations if you want.)

                                                Basically, Solar medicine should cover therapy as well -- it shouldn't be conflated with psychoanalysis; it isn't a scam. It's one part of the full suite of approaches to tackling a very difficult problem even in cases when its grounded in biology, and an approach to mental illness that ignores it in favour of a purely mechanistic 'brain fix' also isn't playing up to the notion of a Solar healer as a healer, rather than a neurosurgical technician or exorcist. (And it also fails to do justice to the experiences of mentally ill people.)

                                                EDIT: There are also issues arising in cross-cultural psychiatry regarding this kind of thing, but this post is complicated enough. There's an interesting and tragic case study of this sort of issue in Anne Fadiman's The Spirit Catches You and You Fall Down, about the interaction of an epileptic child of Hmong refugees with the US medical system.

                                                        I should add that my thinking about how medicine should work in games has been strongly influenced by Legends of the Wulin. In that system, recovering from injuries (or any number of other conditions) is actually a series of die rolls. Anyone trained in Medicine can give you an assistance bonus to your rolls to recover from injury, and someone actually trained in the Secret Art of Medicine (i.e. wielding Charms) gives you the assistance bonus and allows you to use the better result of your roll to heal and their roll to treat your injuries on recovery checks.

                                                        I think it would be cool if Exalted represented that sort of cooperative effort between doctor and patient a little. Adds drama, imo.
