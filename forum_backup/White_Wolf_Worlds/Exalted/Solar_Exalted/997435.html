    Solar Awareness: Combat Prediction?

    11-02-2016, 07:48 PM
    Hey everyone,
    I'm in a little bit of a design rut on a new path for solar awareness. As it stands, Solar Awareness is good at three things.

    1. Anti-Stealth
    2. Join Battle.
    3. Tasting / Hearing / Seeing / Feeling all the things, all at once.

    That's cool, but I'm really only interested in #3 for a character I would like to invest more in Awareness. I was thinking about some other paths awareness could take, and something that came to mind, that I already stunt, is using awareness charms for a character to figure out how their opponent is going to move in combat, or get a few seconds of precognition before something disastrous happens. As far as I can tell, there's no real reason to activate keen sight technique in combat, but I do it anyway for the ~flavour~.

    What if I didn't have to? What if there was a branch of the Solar Awareness charm tree that let the Solar figure out what their opponents are about to do at each step via careful analysis? How could this be made mechanically interesting in a way that isn't "+1 dodge" or "you hit with your sword better"? What types of charms this have? What would you like to see? Any ideas, suggestions, criticisms, or sample charms would be greatly appreciated!

                  The mechanics seem the biggest sticking point here, because precognition of combat actions requires either "rewinding" combat, or forcing people to do things somehow.

                  The one thing I can think of is a small tree that improves on and builds off Delaying Actions. There's an initiative cost that could be waved, giving you the choice of acting before other characters in that tick instead of instigating a Clash so there isn't a need to rewind/etc., granting the more boring bonuses in a more flavorful/unique way by having them hinge on how you delay into the action and so on.

                  I think that could get some of the flavor of reading your opponents.

                                                  I think the problem is that combat is too mechanically strenuous for modelling something after a Lore Charm that's meant to do things like influence military campaigns; which is already a very loose part of the system.

                                                  You'd have to be extremely restrictive on what you can "predict" people will do for a combat Charm. It's not hard to come up with actions nobody would want to take in a fight ("I will kill yourself for daring to raise arms against the Chosen of the Unconquered Sun and die with honor.") for a mechanical combat bonus, turning it into a debuffer Charm that runs into the concern hippokrene stated.

                                                  Before even getting into bonuses and penalties, there needs to be a thorough consideration of the limitations of the Charm. And then the bonuses/penalties have to somehow be universal and/or varied enough to cover multiple options.

                                                          Hey everyone! I'm really loving this feedback, and I'm excited to see people are engaged with this! While I hypothetically like the idea of the player predicting the other character's sheet or what they'll do, my worry is that it'll create a subsystem within the combat subsystem and will significantly slow down the pacing of the game. I think it works well with things like ship combat or the lore charm because those aren't "all hands on deck, entire circle is doing THE THING" time. I'd prefer something less mechanically and extra-layer-y than me personally predicting what this other character would do. More interesting is abstracting that into a set of benefits.

                                                          In that vein, I'm interested in both Ren9077 's idea of having it interact with other trees, like dodge or melee, and Heavy Arms idea of delaying your action for some bonus. I'll write a sample charm for both of these later!. Here's two charm ideas I had that are related to this. They're not eloquently written, balanced (balance wasn't really thought of at all) or filled with fluff: these are merely illustrative of the types of things I was thinking about.

                                                          Battlefield Seeing Fancy Word
                                                          Cost: ??? Requirements ??? Prerequisite Charms ??? (Instant, Reflexive)

                                                          A rotted tree, a hidden sinkhole, a loose boulder; the solar's acute eye notices all these things. The solar rolls Perception+Awareness as a reflexive action to notice any environmental factors they might use against a difficulty of 4. The story teller creates an environmental hazard which makes sense given this roll; every 2 additional successes on the awareness roll results in an increase of 1 damage die from the environmental hazard.

                                                          Fancy Predictive Use of Your Other Awareness Charms
                                                          Cost: ??? Requirements ??? Prerequisite Charms ??? (Instant, Reflexive)

                                                          Whenever you are attacked, instead of applying defense, roll Awareness + Essence. Subtract your successes from the attack roll's successes and treat the attack roll's successes as a difficulty for any of your other awareness charms. If you would automatically succeed the check using your ability, then you successfully dodge the attack. If you do not, then treat the remaining successes as roll-over successes.

                                                          This charm does not work if the opponent is using a supernatural method of stealth or suppression which you are using to pass the check; for example, a demon with no smell cannot be noticed with smelling, and a supernaturally silent assassin couldn't be noticed through hearing.\

                                                          I'm excited to use the idea of delaying your action and dual tree charms too! I'll write some of those up in a few hours
