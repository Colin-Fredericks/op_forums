    Ex3 Soak Overhaul and Solar Resistance rework

    09-18-2020, 12:17 PM
    How soak works in Ex3 has bothered me so I decided to give it an overhaul. Soak now allows people to have a significant amount of damage reduction that is geared towards conserving your resources but does so in a way that no longer bogs down the game into 20 turn crawls through the mud where tears of joy are wept if you gain more than 2 initiative on a single attack. Likewise, the rules should be simple enough that you won’t need to rewrite 95% of currently written material (So soak charms from NPCs, other exalts, Martial Arts, sorcery, and all that other jazz should neatly co-exist with this without much issue). It’s overall far less drastic to some of the other fixes I’ve seen, and I feel it’s the best I can do without making Ex4 or rewriting vast swaths of NPCs or other bits of the system.

    Solar Resistance has also been bothering me as well. It suffers from a lack of clarity on its charms, charms that are too effective and bog down gameplay, and some charms that are just don’t impress me. I think each charm a person purchases should be both entertaining to use in-game and serve a useful purpose or niche. Charms that were broken (EX: Essence Gathering Temper) have been nerfed and a small smattering of new Resistance charms. The old Resistance tree worked, but it felt like a rattling bumpy car to me. Hopefully, this new version should be much smoother.

    But enough rambling here's what you came for.

              15 soak is just shy the maximum a person with artifact heavy armour and Stamina 5 can achieve. Heavy weapons with Strength 5 will have a damage of 19 before charms, so even 1 threshold success will end up with 5 damage. If you want a more fair comparison for mortals look at mundane heavy armour and stamina 5, which is a soak of 12 and compare this to a heavy weapon which will have a damage of 16 when used with Strength 5. Artifact weapons and armour are nearly a different tier, and often much more effective to those things below it by virtue of math alone before adding in other bonuses.

              If you’re worried about how high 15 soak is, then yes you typically will have to invest in damage charms in order to damage those people. More of often than not those people will be likely be using magic or other investments in order to increase their soak that high.

              Also flip the thought you had in your head. The idea wasn’t making a person lose initiative with this rework, the idea was to make it much easier for the other party to gain initiative. It’s much easier to gain initiative from someone with 15 soak 5 burner init than it is against someone who just has 25 soak. Typically the only way past 25 soak are very lucky rolls, post soak builds, or Solar Brawl (Which becomes much less useful when soak goes beyond that). I’d like large amounts of raw damage to be scary again rather than make people go asleep. Likewise Storyteller’s won’t need to balloon antagonist damage values so that the thing that challenges the 25 soak character won’t instantly paste the 10 soak character. There’s some wriggle room there at least.

              Soak reduction remains the same, it’s essentially a form of damage increase anyways. As mentioned in the document I haven’t tested this extensively yet.
