                So my major take aways based on what I have heard from people here about this fight:

                1. I just flat-out got the rules wrong about clashes, disarming artifacts and TTSS, and the fight would have been very different if I had used the rules correctly. In my defense, Exalted has always been a system that requires STs to memorize a tremendous amount of rules and information, and maybe that makes it a bad fit for me.

                2. I shouldn't have given my Dawn something that would permanently increase his soak up to 20. As a side note I also ruled that his armor was environmentally sealed and I can't tell you what a mistake that has been. I could have been dousing him with environmental effects like poison and drowning this whole time if not for that. Lunars get these kind of soak numbers, a Solar with these plus their insane Defense ratings is just too much.

                3. War Lion Stance is probably the best melee charm. Seriously, if your Dawn is already impossible to hit, and they have Bulwark Stance on, then allowing every ally within short range that same protection just starves out the antagonists while also adding extra attacks each round that at least do ping damage with no real drawback.

                4. I paired the wrong personality to this charmset. War Pig is a big and rowdy berserker type that uses a full physical excellency when he attacks because that makes the fight fun for him. If I had built the exact some Lunar as a turtle totem or something I would probably have just sat back each round using Impenetrable Beastman Hide until my Dawn mote tapped himself, and I would have won.

                5. Gambits are better than 1-2 ping damage. I could have easily disarmed my Dawn on round two with that Trickster since we ruled that she had ambush. I could have also distracted him, or gotten him in grapple which would have been a real bad time for him. But instead I was focused on crashing him because he had had an initiative reset, and the result was extremely underwhelming.

                So apparently the consensus is that if I don't switch to being a player in this Ex3 game then there won't be enough people to justify continuing it, but I'm pretty well and truly done with this system. Everyone here was a real help, and I wouldn't have gotten half as much done without your ideas and support. I hope this thread has been of some use, and maybe it'll continue to be helpful to you in your games. I certainly hope so.
