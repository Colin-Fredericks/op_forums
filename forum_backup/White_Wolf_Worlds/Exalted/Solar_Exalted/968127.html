    Conan the Solar

    09-12-2016, 04:55 PM
    Conan the Cimmerian is cited as inspiration for Exalted. To quote the book: “Solars owe a lot of their DNA to Conan, an ostensibly mortal man who yet demonstrates superhuman might, quickness, and resistance to injury.” I agree with the assessment, but whether Conan himself is a Solar is an interesting question. Most of his abilities are within mortal bounds, which is what makes him so interesting. The following is a tour of Conan’s abilities, and where I believe he needs to step beyond mortal bounds into the realm of the Exalted.

    First, let’s talk about Conan’s strength. The man can throw a bench made of silver. He can yank out crucifix nails driven into his own legs. He’s strong! But… is he supernaturally strong? It’s tempting to give him Increasing Strength Exercise to represent his fantastic feats, but he really doesn’t need that: in the books, he never demonstrates anything that cannot be accomplished with Strength 5. Just give him Strength 5, Athletics 5, a specialty in Feats of Strength, and three dots in the Mighty Thew Merit. Now with stunting (and Conan always stunts), he is tossing sixteen dice at Feats of Strength. Add in a point of Willpower, aim for a level 2 stunt, and he can manage even a Difficulty 10 Strength 5 Feat in a pinch.

    What about actual combat? Conan has no problem dealing with large groups of foes. This is easily explained: battle groups! The most surprising thing here is that Conan’s quickness is often more important than his strength or resilience. In single combat, he typically draws his weapon and lands a killing blow right out the gate, which is best represented by possessing a brutal Join Battle roll. Perception 5, Awareness 5, with a Join Battle specialty and Fast Reflexes will take him pretty far (especially if supplemented with a point of Willpower). If you’re on the edge, then giving him an Awareness Excellency is enough to widen the gap against mere mortals.

    I’d say Conan is Supernal Resistance. You can duplicate most of his enduring prowess by giving him pretty much every Merit that has Stamina or Resistance as a prerequisite. Set his Stamina and Resistance both to five, and he’ll clear most of the obstacles listed in the book. However, there are occasions where I feel Conan must be pushed past mortal endurance. The stand-out moment is in one story where Conan is beaten up by a group of soldiers, then crucified. He still possesses the strength, after two days in the sun, to kill a vulture with his teeth, survive falling face-forward onto the ground with the weight of the crucifix behind him, yank out two of his own crucifix nails, and then ride ten miles unassisted without water. If a person can survive all of that and recover after only a few days, they’re beyond mortal.

    How deep Conan needs to go into the Resistance tree is a different question. I’m sure he possesses Armored Scout’s Invigoration (his stealthiness in armor is a key trait) and – coming back around to combat – I expect he draws upon Battle Fury Focus, or even Bloodthirsty Sword-Dancer Spirit, when faced with supernatural threats and endless hordes of foes. His ability to parry with a sword is impressive (and he might possess Dipping Swallow Defense), but it’s really his ability to withstand blows and keep going that stands out: I’d give him maxed out Ox-Body Technique for sure, and he likely possesses a few other Resistance Charms beside. If you're this deep into Resistance, you might take a few others to round it out, but Stamina/Resistance Merits accomplish much the same thing. It's really his ability to not die from repeated injuries that is the focus here.

    Here’s a few other points of note.

    Conan might possess Sensory Acuity Prana: he has almost preternatural hearing and sight. His abilities never get to the point where it seems downright impossible, but it definitely resides on the border of possibility.

    Stealth is another key Ability. Conan can move silently at a full run, and it’s tempting to say he possesses a Charm which removes any bonus that enemies might get for listening to him moving. His opponents rarely hear him coming, and surprise attacks are fairly common.

    He possesses dots in most Abilities, but here’s some notable ones. His War ability is rated at least at four dots. He has Larceny, but you know that already. Brawl and Melee are his highest-rated Abilities; both are likely at 5. Give him a dot in Occult to represent his knowledge of the supernatural and (though he loathes it) a couple dots in Bureaucracy; he’s accustomed to haggling with merchants and, later in life, he makes for a good king. His social stats (Attributes and Abilities) are all pretty decent all around, and he’s often drawing upon Intimacies of fear or awe toward himself to weaken his target’s Resolve. He possesses Linguistics, and is talented enough that his style is as unmistakable as it is bold. He also speaks a variety of languages, including ancient and dead tongues.

    Do not forget Lore. Conan must possess Lore at 3+. In most of his adventures, he establishes at least one fact relating to cultural practices, religion, politics, geography, or history. His ability to both establish facts and contradict others is a key facet of his character, providing him with the upper hand against his opponents, and guidance in dangerous situations.

    Conan’s social Merits vary over time and fluctuate wildly. Sometimes he’s a penniless vagabond. Other times he has commanded hundreds of men for a warlord. And, at one time, he was a king. The only thing which remains stable is his Influence, which grows over time. Later in his career, Conan becomes a figure of awe and a legend even in his own time.

    On a final note, he possesses a minor Intimacy of fear toward Sorcery. It’s strong enough that he will avoid dealing with sorcerers if reasonably possible, but other concerns often override it. He certainly won’t stay behind to finish off a sorcerer if he’s got what he’s come for.

    So, in the end, here’s what we have…
    Conan is the perfect example of a Solar whose essence-enhanced prowess is just subtle enough that mortals don’t question it. Conan might be a Heroic Mortal who possesses maxed out stats in several Attributes and Abilities, along with armloads of Merits, whose regularly doubling the pools of his rivals. However, he most likely is a Dawn Caste Solar, with Supernal Resistance, but a relatively light Charm investment. When pressed to mortal limits, he draws upon Excellencies from his Favored Abilities.

                              I don't know; I'd honestly say that Conan is just a Heroic Mortal. You have to remember that for mortals, 2 is considered "average" for an Attribute and "professionally trained" for a Skill. Most mortals won't have traits that exceed 2 or dice pools that exceed 5. Conan's ratings of 5 in all Physical Attributes and remarkably high ratings in Social and Mental Attributes are already putting him on par or just about with your average professionally trained human being. Then you add his own Skill ratings and he's leaving them high and dry, outclassing them in nearly every category you can care to imagine. He's very much an exceptional mortal, but he's not going to be chucking elephants or nearly flying any time soon. He's putting all of his Experience into his Traits and Willpower instead of Charms.

                              If he's any kind of Exalt, he's probably an Exigent of Crom. In 2e, Crom was another name for the Unconquered Sun, but that may not be true in 3e, or he may be a minor aspect god of the UC focused on physical prowess. I can imagine him to be a minor War God with a focused purview on physical prowess, and thus his Exigent would have subtle powers of physical superiority. That his Exigent is also pretty darn smart and sociable is just a happy bonus.

                                                      Here is an expression of Conan's Intimacies, Limit Trigger, and Limit Break, in accordance with Robert E Howard's work, adjusted to fit within the context of Creation.

                                                      Minor: Sorcery (Fear)
                                                      Defining: Civilization corrupts and weakens
                                                      Minor: Homeland (Affection)
                                                      Defining: Better a peaceful king than a tyrannical emperor
                                                      Major: I will not leave anyone - friend or foe - to the mercies of monsters
                                                      Defining: I will never betray afriend or companion
                                                      Minor: I must accept that glory and wealth are fleeting
                                                      Defining: The fine arts must be glorified and preserved
                                                      Defining: Seek the affections of beautiful companions
                                                      Minor: I must be honorable in my dealings
                                                      Minor: I owe neither honesty or loyalty to betrayers
                                                      Major: If I am a stronger leader than my liege, I must replace him
                                                      Minor: Deception and stealth are the preferred approach against a foe or rival
                                                      Minor: When you don't know what to do, rush the enemy
                                                      Defining: Violence is a good solution
                                                      Major: Live a wanderer's life
                                                      Minor: Worship is pointless
                                                      Major: Seek wealth and personal gain
                                                      Defining: Experience all the wonders of life

                                                      Limit Trigger: Failure to protect a friend or companion, whether by action or inaction
                                                      Limit Break: Seek vengeance against whomever or whatever caused the harm. Conan will take a strategic advantage if one is available, but he will abandon all other concerns - even personal safety - to mete vengeance.
