    Originally posted by Eric Minton View Post
    I'm unhappy with previous Exalted material that portrays the First Age Dragon-Blooded as nothing more than subservient adjuncts to the Solar Exalted. In that context, I feel that taking the Dragon-Blooded/Lunar conflict over the Caul and turning that into a Solar/Lunar conflict over the Caul is a bad look.
    I get that, but part of the image I'm developing of the backstory for the Caul is that, having been a place that was mutually sacred to the Lunars and Dragon Blooded, they were often staunch allies in its defence during the First Age. That would often have been against threats from the Wyld or that emerged from its own nature, but occasionally from Solars and Sidereals who found its existence threatening, regardless of whose side Gaia was on.

    I didn't really picture Dragon Blooded being strongly involved in an original conflict leading to the formation of the Silver Pact, since that seemed especially personal to the Lunars, but that would have hardly been the last time that the place came under threat.

    I formed all of this around the idea that there's a tragic irony to how the Lunar and Terrestrial Exalted are now fighting tooth and nail over control of the Caul. That a sufficiently old (or immersed in past life memory) Lunar might look out over Faxai, with its patrols of the Wyld Hunt, from a high place, and have a bit of melancholy from a sharp flash to a time in which those groups fought side by side in defence of the place.

    To be clear, it wasn't an idea of Solars trying to take control of the Caul because they could get anything out of it; it was about Solars wanting to destroy or purge the place because they found any remnant of something beatified by a Primordial to be too threatening, to which Lunars vehemently disagreed. I would at least find that to be sufficiently distinct from the modern conflict over the place, and throw its contemporary stakes into sharp relief.

                                                        Originally posted by Sunder the Gold
                                                        I’m concerned about how often one would be able or need to use Taboo-Enforcing Beast, but I love how the Lunar could use it as the king with their own face, or as the stolen or borrowed face of a king or their Solar Mate as the king. Also the Lunar could use it as the American bald eagle to affect an American. Would the Realm count dragons as their animal?
                                                        I think it's something you can use a lot if running a society, I've just been having a discussion with one of my friends about how Herd-Reinforcement Stance and Taboo-Enforcing Beast can be used.

                                                        So, for example, our Casteless Lunar, Timid Silk Blossom, has a Defining Tie to the Cult of the Illuminated (I think).

                                                        So, say she's hanging around on Hen Ling, the theocratic righteous bandit village that serves as the centre of the Cult's power in the South (Kether Rock got destroyed by a Raksha invasion).
                                                        Whenever a mortal from Hen Ling tries to criticise a Shining One* in front of her, she can use this charm.
                                                        Whenever a mortal from Hen Ling refuses to do what a Shining One* tells them in front of her, she can use this charm.
                                                        Whenever anyone from Hen Ling tries to commit a crime (other than, you know, righteous banditing) in front of her, she can use this charm.
                                                        Whenever she sees a local eating on a fast day, she can use this charm.
                                                        If she's ambushed by local thugs who've been paid off by the Realm, she can use this charm.
                                                        If she's ambushed by local thugs who just want to beat and rob her, she can use this charm (it's a custom to rob outsiders, righteously, but not locals).
                                                        If anyone says "I don't want to be a righteous bandit, can't we make peace with our traditional enemies instead?" she can use this charm.

                                                        *Though here Shining One is defined by the local customs, not by her or the Cult's leadership. So King Ses, snakeman leader of the Eastern Cult and supreme gang-boss, probably wouldn't count because he doesn't fit the locals' view of a Shining One.

                                                        And, very importantly, it's reflexive. It would be okay but not great if it was supplemental (it's not that difficult to resist), but it's reflexive. You could use it when ambushers are using Join Battle.

                                                        It's probably not very useful in a game where you travel around a ton though, because you probably don't have Major intimacies to the cultures you interact with.

                                                        The Changing Moon in the other game I play can take the shape of Prince Meek of Sukhmet and the Emperor of the Southern Dynasty, but even if she's swanning around as the Emperor she can't actually use this charm at all because she doesn't have a Major intimacy to the culture of the Southern Dynasty, she only went there for a couple of months. My own Lunar had a Major intimacy to a particular province of his homeland of Medo, but on our adventures he only ever met one other guy from there, who was just a minor NPC.
