    Originally posted by Revan058 View Post

    And how does that alivate the point of being a bit vexed when someone can do something better then you in every way, and when you point it out, the return is "Oh, but they can't do this useless subsystem"? Because it doesn't seem to in the least.
    First, let's start working under the assumption that the Devs don't want to create useless subsystems, and that they plan on making shapeshifting, astrology, elemental teamwork charms, robotic charm swapouts, and...whatever the Getimians and the Liminals do...as cool as they possibly can. I mean, they're designing a game, they want that game to be fun to play, they're gonna put all the effort they can into making those exalt-type-specific tricks cool.

    If your character concept is "I want to be the very best at X," then you want to play a Solar, unless X is something weird that solar themes of "paragon of mortal skill" don't let you do, like shapeshifting or altering probability through the use of judicious red ink. Solar magic is all about "I am a peerless paragon who takes this thing mortals can do and then does it orders of magnitude better, to the point where I outclass gods." Nobody is gonna be a better swordsman than Invincible Sword Princess...but that doesn't mean you can't beat her in a fight, if the game's well-designed. It just means you can't beat her in a swordfight, unless you play dirty.

    If you're playing a Lunar or a Sidereal or whatever, your concept is probably not "I am the best swordsman there is." It's probably "I am a swordsman who can fly like Peter Pan," or "I am a swordsman who can cut you so fast your grandpa falls over dead," or "I am a swordsman who grows six arms and has swords in ALL of them and did I mention the arms are long and super-stretchy because I have an octopus totem and that I change color rapidly to hypnotize my opponents with color and weirdly beautiful movements while we fight?"

    At the end of the day, though, these other character concepts are about making fun use of interesting tricks not available to Solars. What Solars do is just practice swordfighting a while bunch, and develop a deeper bond with the very soul of their sword because they're spending all their time and energy on mastering it rather than mastering Dancing Octopus Style. A Solar is busy meditating and sharpening his blade while the Sidereal is busy planning fate to arrange who he's going to swordfight over the next week and the Lunar is busy developing a trick to secrete blinding poison from his suckers to spray on his enemies. These guys might beat Invincible Sword Princess in a fight! They're not strictly worse at combat! It's just that she's better at bonding with her sword, and they're better at other things.

    EDIT: Please note I picked swords because they're an easier example than other things, but this holds true of most areas of the game. Solars do a human thing (like get really good with their favorite tools) with divine skill. Other Exalts can do human things really good, but their true power lies in inhuman powers.

                        "2: Solars aren't "Better than you in every way", they are very very good in areas that they make an effort to be very very good in, but they are not unbeatable."

                        The thing is, thematically this includes pretty much anything. I get that Solars are meant to be a big deal and the most powerful overall, no problem there, but sometimes it feels like no one else can outdo them in any one thing because they are Solars and have to the best in every single thing - which essentially constrains the other themes. There are exceptions, but not that many - shapeshifting (which a solar can definitely tap into with bio-augmentations and artefacts, which they are the best at), and astrology. Maybe martial arts too, but once they have learned an SMA style I am not sure if their higher mote pools don't cancel out any sidereal bonuses.I'd love it if 3E solars weren't automatically the favourites in pretty much any single thing they care to do, but we'll see.

                        Also, I'm not so sold on the whole "solars are defined by their humanity" theme. Many charms, especially non-starting ones, go way beyond human perfection and limitations - conjuring a magical golden sword out of thin air, for example, Being defined by humanity giving you access to the broadest and best powers makes no more sense than getting them through the theme of other exalted - Lunars experience the world through myriads of forms that give them unparalleled insight into its working, Dragonblooded are infused by the elements that created and define Creation, etc. These two splats in particular can also have archetypes and charms play on the humanity them (they don't have to be that, but it's definitely a possibility in their theme). They just seem to be humans exposed to something else than what solars got.

                        Originally posted by Wise Old Guru View Post
                        Solar magic is all about "I am a peerless paragon who takes this thing mortals can do and then does it orders of magnitude better, to the point where I outclass gods." Nobody is gonna be a better swordsman than Invincible Sword Princess...but that doesn't mean you can't beat her in a fight, if the game's well-designed. It just means you can't beat her in a swordfight, unless you play dirty.

                        If you're playing a Lunar or a Sidereal or whatever, your concept is probably not "I am the best swordsman there is."
                        /headdesk. By Cthulhu, how I hate this argument. You are an exalted, a great hero, but you can never be as great at "Insert heroism here" as the other type of greater hero, because that is how heroes work - by not overcoming the odds and not becoming something greater than they were. You could try to cheat to win, but actually, the greater hero can be even better at cheating, because it is a thing and thus not something you as not-greater hero can be better at (if they care to do it at all). "Everything humans do, better than everyone else" can really be a bitch to build around when there are 5-6 other splats who are also great heroes with incredible powers and potential (a.k.a. Exalted)

                        To me, the concept - if it is to be the cornerstone of Solar design philosophy, though I prefer "Returned god-kings of old" as the more definite and thematic one - it involved solars being the most accomplished at the pure implementation of a skill. However, this need not and should not equal automatically being the better at everything involving this skill. For example, you are the best guitar player because you are best at actually playing the guitar, but when it comes to an overall performance, others can compensate with on-stage presence or a better designed song. The best swordsman may be the one with the most pure technique, but in a fight, this could be offset by the enemy being stronger and more agile. They can be the better swordsman - as in, fighter who uses a sword - even if they aren't the best at their sword technique. This is okay, what I don't want to see (but fear is possible) is something that boils down to "You are a solar, this is something a solar can do, you win."

                        Anyway, back to evocations - the idea of having special abilities based on your weapon and stuff only you can do with it is cool enough, and has been in some games I've played with. Though I'm not sure it is so good if evocations allow something that impinges on the theme of another splat, like a solar throwing off waves of flame and molten earth that rival or outdo the mastery of a Dragon-blooded who uses their innate powers to do the same.

                        edit: Wise Old Guru, I saw your clarification - that I can definitely agree with.

                                            Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post
                                            You know how the majority of mythological and literary heroes are basically human in form and function, and consistently overcome all the monsters and things?
                                            Which is sort of expected in a human mythology, on a world where humans are the sole intelligent life. Creation is a world created by inhuman primorial beings, run by spirits and gods, with intelligent elemental spirits, and populated by all matter of mystical creatures. If their link to "humanity" is the biggest or one of the biggest defining traits of Solars, there must be a reason why that matters so much - what about Humanity justifies these abilities in that world. Perhaps solars are defined by heroic virtues, and sure, humans can be heroes - but in a world where there are other intelligent creatures, there is no reason why heroism should be limited to humanity. Just because we are the sole heroes in our culture doesn't make it a universally valid archetype for every possible situation. Plus, heroism is defined by its opposition to the mundane and mass traits as a whole. Heroes are different to most humans - in a sense, they are outsiders.

                                            On the other hand, this connection to humanity should mean solars are the most human exalts, and I'm not sure this makes much sense - the association of the Solar Exalted with humanity seems to be more about the lack of association with anything else (at least anything concrete). After all, all exalted start as humans, and become been empowered by an exaltation related to one of the incarna (or the sub-soul of a Primordial, in case of Dragon-blooded), none of whom is particularly human in itself. Sol Invictus favors humans, sure, but that in itself does make them any more important. Every exalt has begun their existance as a human and as the High First Age taught us, at the peak of their power the chosen of the Sun were no more human than their mates and viziers... if not less.

                                            I would argue that if anything, it is the link to (classical) heroism that makes solars so special, but to me beyond their gimmicks all exalts are heroes. This is why I prefer to stress the other Solar defining traits - that they are the chosen of the Sun (with all the influences that entails), the God-Kings of old, the leaders and bearers of the original mandate of Creation. They give them a much better place and legitimacy within the setting.

                                            A somewhat narrower theme will in turn determine what someone is good at. Perhaps solars could have evocations that stress their mastery and power - a gleaming armor that make everyone who wishes to strike the bearer avert their gaze or go blind, a bow whose arrows disappear and instead the rays of the sun fall as orihalcum spikes, or a banner which, unfurled, will restore make routing troops turn back or summon guardian spirits to battle (though the latter could work for sidereals as well,with a minor twist). Tremors of earth or waves of fire seem like something more in tune with what I would expect from a Dragon-blooded. It is not so much about which does the most damage but what sort of things fit someone's type.
