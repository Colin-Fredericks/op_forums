        Originally posted by Oriares View Post
        Do brass and Malfean Iron count as Magical Materials?
        In 3E... I have no idea. In 2E several Infernal artifacts were made out of them, though I'm not sure if that means they were classified as magical materials (don't have my books in front of me to check). It's more that demons seem to have a preference of working with brass and malfean iron. While Orichalcum, the material associated closely with UCS created with the power of the Sun and gold, tends to have strong "bane to creatures of darkness" themes. Creatures of darkness include all demons, the dead/undead, and fey. Once again I'm not saying no Orichalcum artifacts will have some demon themes. Just that it's probably far and away the preferred material for demon themes.

        Admittedly I could very well be completely wrong for 3E. Though it would require a rewrite to Orichalcum as a whole to make it the preferred material.

                                                            *attempts to shield himself with his own pedantry*
