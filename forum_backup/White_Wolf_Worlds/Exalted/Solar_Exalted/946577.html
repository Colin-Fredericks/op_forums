Is the assumption that you're evading large groups or what? How does it work? What charms do you need?

                          Dodge Mote Generation...

                          Charms of importance:
                          Flow Like Blood
                          -This removes all the penalties you could ever take, and gives you 1 free initiative point if staying close to the enemy. It also reduces the cost of Reed in the Wind to 1 initiative.

                          Quick Silver Feint + Force Stealing Feint
                          -This steals 1 point of initiative each time you are missed.

                          Thousand Step's Stillness
                          -This lets you gain 1 point of initiative per 1 or 2 they rolled, since they are likely trying to throw as many dice at you as possible, although you probably should combo this with Hundred Shadow Ways to have become immune to Excellent Strike like effects.

                          Full Effect:
                          Basically if you are willing to be careful on offense motes since you will be regenerating none due to expenses, you will be likely gaining 5 or more initiative just from dodging.

                              So after some looking at the Dodge charms, I've come up with the following list of all the Init modding Solar Dodge Charms:

                              Shadow Dancer Method (D5, E1, Permanent) - for 1m you can ignore the 2i cost when Disengaging. If an opponent approaches you and you move away, but opt to close with them on your next move, you gain 2i.

                              Searing Quicksilver Flight (D4, E1, Permanent) and Force-Stealing Feint (D5, E2, Permanent) - Whenever you successfully dodge an attack, your opponent loses one additional init. With Force-Stealing Feint you gain the lost init.

                              Safety Between Heartbeats (D5, E2, Reflexive) - After you successfully dodge an attack, you can pay 5m to cause your opponent to lose 1i for every 1 they rolled on the attack.

                              Flow Like Blood (D4, E2, Reflexive) - For 5m, 1w you gain several benefits for the scene, including +1i each round you remain within close range of an enemy without being successfully attacked.

                              Thousand Steps' Stillness (D5, E3, Reflexive) - After successfully dodging an attack, you may pay 5m to gain 1i per 1 or 2 rolled on the attack roll.

                              Unbowed Willow Meditation (D5, E3, Permanent) - If you successfully dodge a Decisive Attack without using a charm, you steal all the attackers init and crash them. Way of Whispers Technique synergizes well with this charm.

                              Unbridled Shade Attitude (D5, E3, Permanent) - Gain 1i for every -0 HL damaged by a Decisive Attack. This one is really just there to help recoup the losses from Living Bonds Unburdened, but you might be rocking 3x Ox Bodies, I dunno.

                              Refinement of Flowing Shadows (D5, E5, Permanent) - After you use Seven Shadow Evasion to dodge something, you gain 1i and 1i every round until the next time you get hit.


                              From looking at all of these, I've come to a few conclusions:

                              1) All of these charms are either Permanent or specifically say that they can only be used after you successfully dodge. As such, none of them need to be declared when you're declaring your defense charms beforehand. So, there's no real downside from having them, except for the points you spent to get the charms.

                              2) Shadow Dancer Method is the only charm that gains you init at E1 and only 2i per turn, so, if you want to have the "Evade Opponent until you Strike!" thing going on, you'll need to take Dodge Supernal, or build up to it over the course of the game. The charms in Bold above are the ones I'd consider best for this style of fighting (though keep in mind I've never tried playing this type of character, so I might well be mistaken).

                              Those charms together mean you get 1i every round, 1i every time you dodge successfully and you can pay 5m to get additional init equal to the 1&2s on the attack roll or, if you didn't need a charm to defend, you get all the opponent's init and crash them. While you'd probably get more init with a weapon, these don't have to be mutually exclusive options. With prereqs, it costs 9 of your starting 15 charms, and you might want a couple more just to ensure you can evade everything, but it's doable at chargen.

                              Note that, to gain anything but the 1i from FLB each action, you need to be attacked, so these charms don't combine well with Stealth charms. As several of them key off you remaining in close range to an opponent, they don't really combine well with Archery either. Thrown, Melee, or MA would seem to be your best options for other charms to use with this set up. MA especially, as several of them combine well with Dodge like Snake (gives bonuses to Evasion, needs high init), Black Claw (Evasion Bonus, steal init, attack penalties), and Dreaming Pearl (Evasion bonuses, can steal init).
