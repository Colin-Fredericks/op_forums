                            Originally posted by BrilliantRain View Post

                            But, since the poison penalties also subtract directly from other dice pools, doesn't that effectively double the penalty for static values? That seems like it could be problematic.

                            I think I can help with this one. Static values are not based on dice pools but on attribute+ability. No matter what penalties (or bonuses) your actions might have, your attribute/ability dots remain the same. Same applies when counting the max dice you can benefit from when it comes to excelencies (or other charm dice). If you got 3 brawl and 4 dexterity with a -2 penalty, be it from poison or wounds, the maximum charm dice you can benefit from is still 7 for a total of 12 dice (3+4-2+7) as the penalty only lowers your dice pool. Not the attribute/ability.

                            So no, there is no double dipping in the penalty. Penalty is simply applied to the static value straight.
