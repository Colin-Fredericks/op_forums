    Not in detail, but I did a quick sketch.

    5 attribute + 5 ability + 1 specialty + 10 excellency + 1 tool, with double 7s and cascading rerolls on 6s, is 22*8/9 = about 19.5 successes on average. You can expect 2 or 3 10s in there, so after rerolling those we'll say you get 22 successes. So you roll 7 extra dice from Divine Inspiration Technique, earning you about 6 successes. So you roll 2 more dice, and get another 2 successes. Then you roll another die and get nothing.

    So you've got 30 successes before looking at First Movement Of The Demiurge. You've rolled 15 7-10s. Let's say that's 3 sets of 3 7s, 8s, and 9s. So you set 3 dice to 10, and reroll them for 2 more successes. So 38 successes. Two more dice from Divine Inspiration Technique. We'll call that two more successes. One more die, which we'll say isn't a success.

    So the peak Solar gets ~40 successes per roll. One roll for an Artifact 2, two for an Artifact 3 or 4, three for an Artifact 5. So they can do any normal Artifact with ease, and are ready to make a serious attempt at Legendary Artifacts.

    It's not even close to what canon allows, but it's more than enough if you ask me.
    I concur. So, hm, about half of this comes from adding Divine Inspiriation + First Movement of the Demiurge, but even without those you have enough to make a solid go on an Art 5. That... seems... okay.

    (I really hate the repeated cycling between DIT and FMotD, but that's certainly not a problem you've introduced - I just like having an unambiguous order of operations that doesn't allow, "Do A, then check B, then check A again, then...")

                Originally posted by Irked View Post
                (I really hate the repeated cycling between DIT and FMotD, but that's certainly not a problem you've introduced - I just like having an unambiguous order of operations that doesn't allow, "Do A, then check B, then check A again, then...")
                Agreed. In my work on the charmset I've introduced the following, to both order and clarify dice effects:

                Charms can modify dice rolls in a variety of ways. Some of these are also mentioned in the glossary, but repeated here for ease of reference. If multiple effects modify a single roll, apply them in this order.
                • Add X dice: Roll X more dice than you normally would. Remember that the Solar Exalted cannot add more dice to a roll than the relevant (Attribute + Ability).
                • Exploding Xs: For each die that comes up, X, roll an additional die (this isn't limited by the normal Attribute + Ability cap).
                • Disappearing Xs: When any die come up X, reroll it, and keep rerolling it until until X stops coming up (ie, there should be no Xs showing in the final dice pool).
                • Double Xs: A result of X or greater on any rolled die is counted as two successes rather than one. Most rolls benefit from Double 10s, except for Decisive damage rolls. Multiple Doubles are redundant - a roll with Double 8s already gets two successes for roll a 9, so Double 9s adds nothing more.

                                Actually I'm thinking about a Painted Truth Prana tree some more, and I think it should basically look like one of the Performance trees (e.g. dance, voice, oratory).

                                2. Analogue of Respect-Commanding Attitude: folks automatically notice your art (applies to graffiti, applies to an item in a museum, etc.)
                                3. Upgrade to #2: folks must pay 1wp to tear down the art
                                4. Upgrade to #3: if the art is taken down, those who saw it get an (automatic?) negative intimacy towards the person who took it down
                                5. Actually #2-#3 should be only two charms I think, but not sure how to split the effects between them.
                                6. Can embed a secondary persuade action encouraging folks to replicate the art (including its message). (This can be combined with Flawless Example!)
                                7. You can embed a "read intentions" action in art depicting a person's face, where if the action beats the onlooker's guile then they feel like the depicted person has seen through them. (OK this is actually kind of useless but I am amused by the idea.)

                                Oh and the Respect-Commanding Attitude types should also apply to architecture. So if your Sphinx has its nose blown off everyone will then be mad at whoever did it.

                                            On one hand, there are enough Charms here.

                                            On the other hand, I was struck by inspiration and I didn't see any real reason to deny it.

                                            These are kinda weird. Not really sure how good they are.

                                            Sublime Cartography Technique
                                            Cost: -; Mins: Craft 4, Essence 3
                                            Type: Permanent
                                            Keywords: Agnostic
                                            Duration: Permanent
                                            Prerequisites: Miraculous Mapmaker Method

                                            This Charm enhances its prerequisite. Whenever the Solar produces a map with Miraculous Mapmaker Method, that map is enchanted in the following ways:

                                            -As long as the map is in the territory that it depicts, a small mark appears on it showing the map's location. A "you are here" mark.
                                            -As long as the map is in the territory that it depicts, it shows the local weather. This ability is limited by distance: a map of Creation, if held in Lookshy, will not show rain on the Blessed Isle.
                                            -The map is so perfectly clear that absolutely anyone can understand it. No matter how many complex concepts the Solar depicts, the map is always clearly understandable.
                                            -The map may depict an innately unplottable area like the Wyld or the Labyrinth or the world-body of a Primordial. Somehow, whatever spatial inconsistencies the area possesses are perfectly explained by the map.

                                            Omniscient Surveyor Meditation
                                            Cost: 15m; Mins: Craft 5, Essence 4
                                            Type: Supplemental
                                            Keywords: Agnostic
                                            Duration: Until the map is finished
                                            Prerequisites: Sublime Cartography Technique

                                            This Charm allows the Solar to draw a map of an area they know nothing about. The Solar rolls Perception + Craft, and obtains information based on the result. One success gives the knowledge of an ordinary person from the area, three successes gives the knowledge of an educated and well-traveled person from the area, and five successes gives the knowledge of an extremely competent professional cartographer from the area. Further successes reveal forgotten secrets, or expands the map to include more information than any single mortal could know about the area.

                                            The effectiveness of this Charm depends on how mysterious the area is. Five successes can tell a Solar everything they need to know about an ordinary city, but if used on the Labyrinth it will only reveal a small area. This Charm never gives false information, and the information it leaves out is never particularly misleading. Knowledge acquired by this Charm flows directly from the universe into the map the Solar is drawing, and never touches the Solar's conscious mind.

                                            The Map Is The Territory
                                            Cost: 10m, 1wp; Mins: Craft 5, Essence 5
                                            Type: Simple
                                            Keywords: Agnostic
                                            Duration: Indefinite
                                            Prerequisites: Omniscient Surveyor Meditation

                                            The Solar makes or edits a map, and the world changes to match. The little gods of mountain, river, forest and city become aware of the Solar's work, and are embarrassed to discover that they are not where they should be. With all the speed they can manage, they drag themselves across the world until the imperfect world reflects the perfect truth of the Solar's map.

                                            Geography does not move quickly, and its larger features move especially slowly. A mountain might only move a single meter each day. But as long as the Solar keeps their motes committed, the world will always gradually move towards the map.

                                            This Charm also affects living things, but strong-willed creatures like humans can easily ignore the nagging feeling that their city is in the wrong place if they wish to. Particularly stubborn people might even abandon or demolish their homes if they find them moving away from where they want them to be.
