                                                        Originally posted by Nervaqus987 View Post
                                                        Does anyone see anything mechanically iffy about Merging Spirit Weapons into the other combat Abilities, or would you consider that kosher?
                                                        After comparing it to the existing Archery and Melee conjuration charms: it's iffy and not kosher.

                                                        The thing about the Throw conjuration charm, Spirit Weapons, is that you are meant to throw them away. You then either recover them with Call The Blade, replace them with another conjuration, or through more mundane effort. It's similar to the Archery ammunition conjuration, Phantom Arrow Technique. The cost is spread out over repeated usage.

                                                        The weapons of the Archery and Melee conjuration charms, Immaculate Golden Bow and Glorious Solar Blade aren't intended to be tossed and recovered over and again. Though in return you gain some superlative qualities. This is similar to Phantom Arrow Technique's successor charm, Inexhaustable Bolts of Solar Fire. The conjuring cost is front loaded, rather than spread over the scene.
