                Originally posted by Lumi View Post
                My Twilight has been tutoring the Dawn in her Craft to get herself an apprentice to help her out, but I soon realized that two Solars working together on a craft project doesn't actually yield any benefits. Beyond creating a custom charm that lets Solars share craft XP, what do you think would be a good way of handling this?
                I don't think there'd be anything wrong about letting two players share the cost and reward of a craft project without requiring a Charm. That and/or circumstantial bonuses.

                On the same topic, the Twilight is capable of using Wake The Sleeper to unlock Evocations. If she rolls well enough to unlock five Evocations from an artifact, but her Essence is only two and the artifact only has two applicable Evocations (one Essence 1 and one Essence 2, with two evocations at Essence 3), what happens? Can you awaken new (unlisted in the book) Evocations at Essence 1 or 2? Do you unlock the Essence 3 Evocations but can't use them yet? Do you just lose the extra Evocations you would've gotten if you had used the charm at a higher Essence? The last option seems backwards for the fantasy of unlocking the true potential of artifacts in the style of Eternal Nova.
                You can customize the Evocation trees given with each artifact and add new, custom Evocations in. Arms of the Chosen will contain some guidance on the details, but for the purposes of your question, Wake the Sleeper can unlock custom Evocations just as easily as canon ones. Just make sure you're talking with your Storyteller so you don't have to take a break mid-session to design half a dozen new Evocations.

                                            Ok, speaking as a self-interested Lore supernal Twilight with a bunch of teaching charms, I've gone through the book three times on this particular count, and found... very little.

                                            So, can you explain the intended teaching system for me? What does it look like? How is it supposed to interact?

                                            What I can infer from what the book says basically lines up like this, and I want to see if I have the right of it.

                                            Normally a teacher has no effect on a student except to speed training times. Anything else is roleplay and social influence; a teacher attempting to instill their values would be a socialize check typically, not a lore one, if they were doing it intentionally. Unintentionally, it'd just be a student deciding to pick up a teacher's intimacy because roleplaying is the reason we're here.

                                            Willing students you successfully teach are assumed to have sufficient EXP to pick up charms, spells, merits and appropriate abilities if they're NPCs and the training time is reduced by... some amount dependent on your teaching skill, effectively making you their mentor.

                                            A mentor's teaching skills are independent of their Mentor rating, probably, you could have a common schoolteacher who nobody cares about, who is a great teacher but still Mentor •, while conversely you could have a sorcerer-Mentor whose rating is •••, but a genuinely terrible teacher, who is more valuable because of his political associations. But at the same time that Mentor ••• could also be a Solar Exalted of the Twilight Caste whose concept is "Greatest teacher ever" so it's up to player and ST definition, just like Allies and Followers.

                                            Using Flowing Mind Prana, you can confer your own unspent EXP to a student as part of the lesson, cutting the training time to ONE lesson of appropriate time according to training time, instilling them with your intimacies, and getting the EXP refunded at the end of the story dependent on their dice roll.

                                            So my first question then, is assuming this is right, how much time does a mentor usually reduce training times by? Is it a roll? Or is it only a roll when it's a PC? What does a set of 6 successes mean? How much time does that reduce training of a charm by? An attribute? A spell? What about an ability?
