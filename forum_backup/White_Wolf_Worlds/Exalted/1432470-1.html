                        Okay, moving on.

                        If it was me, other Exalted undertaking the Caul pilgrimage wouldn't bestow any form of powers, but would have a very good chance of bestowing that person with a deep insight into the Lunars or Terrestrials that could be used to relate to them in unique ways.

                        I think I'd represent that as a Merit with at least some particular social bonus; perhaps something like the equivalent of a virtual Intimacy that can be extended to any of the respective Exalted type, but one with a relatively narrow and esoteric scope. Say, something that can appeal to them on grounds of the spiritual and metaphysical. Possibly leveraging into things like the role bonuses of their Castes and Aspects.

                        So like, you could appeal to a Dragon Blood to conduct themselves in some manner fitting to what could be the overall mission statement of the Terrestrial Exalted, or their place in the world. Conversely, the person's insight of that kind means that they themselves can be appealed to on similar grounds. It's not the direct transformation that comes from having the appropriately compatible Essence, but there's something profound about it.

                                        At the risk of sounding trite: Because it's there? Think about it from the perspective of a Solar with, for the sake of argument, no investment in any specific belief system in which the Caul plays a central role. You've heard the Realm really badly wants this strange, foreign land. You've heard the Lunars regard it as sacred. And you've heard weird, but nonlethal things happen to you just to complete the journey.

                                        Even if Solar Joe doesn't actually get anything out of the Caul, he might take some interest in the burning landscapes and the city that can only be traversed in dreams and all the other mystic scenery as he wanders from scenic vista to scenic vista. Depending on how you play it this could be a chance for the Solar's player to reflect on the legacy left behind by previous civilisations and wonder how their own character's goals compare. Or for the sorcerously inclined, even if the place has little to do with their own understanding of magic it might be a good place to get some artistic inspiration for their own magic. Or it can be an actual vacation.

                                        For your Abyssal, it could well be that despite having no direct connection with your patron the Caul is intrinsically wondrous and soothing in a weird way that it brings you to a calming state of mind in which redemption is easier than it would normally be. I could also see an Infernal just having a fun time trying to play around with the phenomena of the Caul using her powers.

                                                    Originally posted by Hakkonen View Post
                                                    What ideas (motivations and/or effects) do you have for non-Terrestrial Exalts following the Caul pilgrimage road? I introduced an Abyssal NPC in tonight's session, and I'm thinking about making the Caul pilgrimage the way to redeem her into a Solar.
                                                    This may be a bit simplistic, but you could always lean into the old 'past life' trope? Make it more personal rather than fret about creating a splat-wide mechanic?

                                                    Maybe your Abyssal escorted their Lunar mate on the pilgrimage in days gone by and the Abyssal is being pulled to relive that experience and remember who they were? Or maybe their Lunar mate asked to be buried on the Caul long ago and, grief-stricken, the Abyssal elected to bury their own treasured daiklave with them. Now the tomb, the location of which can only be found via visions granted to someone who has completed the pilgrimage, has just been disturbed and the daiklave is in the hands of another – flooding your Abyssal with flashes of rememberance of the person who they used to be.

                                                    Or perhaps your Abyssal led a particularly valiant and pious gens of DBs in a mighty battle battle against a Fae incursion. The DBs sacrificed themselves and the site of their heroic last stand has just been desecrated by Lunar forces. Compelled by forces they do not quite understand, the Abyssal undertakes to complete the pilgrimage in order to cleanse the site, in the process taking another faltering step towards redemption.

                                                    Etc etc...
