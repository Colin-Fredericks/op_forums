                CoCD3 says...

                Benadamaru, Censor of the North, is a lesser elemental dragon of air. The North is currently quite beyond his control; he cannot manage to bring an audit against the Syndics of Whitewall, and he dares not try to prove divine collusion in the Bull of the North’s activities. He contents himself with auditing minor cases of divine malfeasance that are brought to his attention while sending reports to Yu-Shan of constant success and progress

                Adiubande, Censor of the East, is a lesser elemental dragon of wood. She is utterly corrupt and will take bribes to overlook anything except direct Fair Folk invasion. Personally she has become quite sympathetic toward gods involving themselves in cities, such as Great Forks, and she may overlook or conceal crimes in return for future favors as well as current payments.

                The three censors on the Blessed Isle are Profamo, Danqueret, and Dyballe, all lesser celestial dragons of earth. The other two defer to Profamo, who is several centuries their elder, and all three of them are faithful to the Bronze Faction. The Immaculate Order regularly notifies them about any deities trying to establish their own cults, and they are quick to stamp them out. Dyballe wishes to take Profamo’s place, as she rightly views him as lazy and time-serving and has been investigating rumors of Solar activity; she hopes that if she can demonstrate her efficiency at capturing or removing Solars, the Bronze Faction will reward her with promotion.

                The same book also says that there's 125 Censors in the Buerau of Heaven as well as the seven in Creation One of the BoH's Celestial Censors is named - Aligept (he's also called out as one of the more corrupt Censors). He has a collection of corrupt Lion Dogs on his staff, he invades various cities in Creation, frames the local deities for whatever he can come up with and then audits them. He then appoints one of his corrupt Lion Dogs to "keeping the area under surveillance while ensuring a return to proper divine-human interaction", which means he's the new area's despot.

                Finally CoCD3 says that all the Heavenly Censors are lazy and most are corrupt

                When one is accustomed to privilege, equality seems like oppression.

                        Originally posted by Jen View Post
                        Are there any ways to accelerate the speed that Dragon King regain their sapience and past life memories ? Locking a bunch of stalker inside a den and wait for a hundred years sound boring, but I have no creative way to make it more interesting.
                        Scroll of Fallen Races has it that it takes five or six years to mature physically, then a decade of special training through hunting games in enclosed game preserves to awaken their reasoning ability, just like most teenagers. Only then were they moved into dwellings to train in the arts of civilisation. Of course, without that training this will take more than two decades, like most teenagers. Then a few years after their intelligence develops, their past life memories start to emerge.

                        It's totally a cross between Jurasic Park and Dinotopia; only they live to kill, not just kill to live.

                        tl;dr Anyway, it doesn't take centuries, but you can't be an absentee parent. If you're rearing a pack with the proper facilities and staff, its about as fast as raising babies through high-school to early college. Probably about as difficult; just less changing nappies and more ...uhm... teething troubles.

                        There are a couple of Lore charms that enable a Solar to raise various traits of social groups, but I'm not sure is a hunting pack of ravenous dinocubs qualify, and rational thought isn't one of the traits listed. But you could custom build a branch of the Lore tree to enable it.

                        Edit: No wait, there is indeed a solar Lore charm in Debris of the Fallen Races called: Dragon Soul Enlightening Method, that does just that; once learned and at no-cost(per use), it permanently enables Harmonious Academic Methodology to awaken a Dragon King to rational thought as a training method. (Explicitly only raising essence from one dot to two.)
