I don't necessarily love everything Borgstromantic, but Dr. Moran did her graduate work in computation - that's a lot of background with systems that need to handle discrete packets in an unambiguous way with built-in conflict resolution.

                        Some of the assumptions the rules made in 2e were far more ambiguous, mind - I don't want to act like everything was clarity, all the time, because we still had whatever the heck "Duration: Instant (Story)" was supposed to mean, and the weird corner-cases in the definition of "attack," and so on. But I wonder whether Ink Monkeys etc. was clearer because it was playing within this strict timing/vocabulary framework, instead of 3e's wide open spaces.

                                                    Presence

                                                    Number of Charms: 24
                                                    By Ability requirement: 0/1/2/3/18
                                                    By Essence requirement: 4/6/11/3/0
                                                    Nonstandard dice tricks: Free full Excellency, about ten different situation bonus dice things.

                                                    Themes: More social dice. Fearless minions. Minions who stand in your place. Supporting allies with extra dice. Terrifying anyone who sees you.

                                                    And back down to a 24-Charmer. So far, 20-30 feels like about the right number – the trees that go larger than that are the ones that tend to feel bloated or unimpressive. (Or else they’re Awareness.)

                                                    Charms:
                                                    1. Gain bonuses to Instill or Persuade – bigger bonuses, if the target boosts his Resolve.
                                                    2. Gain bonus dice to social rolls for a scene; social Charms are 1m cheaper.
                                                    3. Anyone with Defining Ties to you will perform inconvenient tasks without a roll.
                                                    4. Permanent bonus to Presence and intimidate. With #7, auto-intimidate anyone who tries to ambush you.
                                                    5. Gain bonuses to persuasion in line with your Intimacies.
                                                    6. Grant an ally +1 to one Attribute and one Ability.
                                                    7. Enemies must pay 1wp the first time they speak or act against you.
                                                    8. Anyone with Intimacies for the Solar also has Intimacies for the target; the target can act with the Solar’s authority.
                                                    9. Turn your high-Appearance bonus dice into bonus successes.
                                                    10. Increase your Appearance by 1; lower the target’s Resolve if this is seduction.
                                                    11. A Decisive attack on creatures of darkness does agg, or a social influence attempt lowers their resolve. Treat people you hate as CoDs, for this one attack.
                                                    12. Increase the cost of a Decision Point (i.e., reject successful Persuade) by 1wp.
                                                    13. Allies to Medium range are immune to fear penalties. Have we seen fear penalties, so far?
                                                    14. Threaten everyone out to Long range. Also, inflict a fear penalty on battle groups – well, that answers that.
                                                    15. Bonus dice to threaten, persuade, or instill.
                                                    16. Target forgets one piece of social influence he was about to carry out.
                                                    17. Program the target with a series of short commands. Really wishing I hadn’t made a Code Geass joke already.
                                                    18. On succeeding at a major task, your enemies must grovel before you for the next few days.
                                                    19. Grant a follower motes and a Resolve bonus to act as your prophet.
                                                    20. Grant three bonus successes to a follower.
                                                    21. Double-9s on seduction. Seduce even people who can’t be seduced.
                                                    22. 1/scene, free Presence, Performance, or Socialize Excellency.
                                                    23. As per #6, but stacking with that bonus and of Indefinite duration.
                                                    24. Burn your anima for extra Intimidation dice. Enemies who don’t attack you lose 1i/turn.

                                                    Harmonious Presence Meditation (#2) is by far the best pseudo-Infinite Mastery so far; it’s cheap, applies to any social Ability, and – above all – Reflexive. Which makes the 7m version a little weird: why would I pay extra motes for a version that’s Indefinite, if I can commit the 5m version instantly whenever I want? This would make better sense if it, say, cost a WP, but for just a mote cost that feels pretty strictly inferior.

                                                    Excellent Friend Approach (#3) makes it so that people who will literally die for you will also agree to do inconvenient things without a roll. I mean, yes, I’m sure they will, but in most cases shouldn’t that fall under the rules for trivial actions? The target’s Resolve is no higher than 6, minus three for a Defining Intimacy – nine dice would be enough to do that without a roll anyway, and we know the Solar has Presence 5… Again, this feels like a waste of a Charm.

                                                    Empowering Shout (#6) notes that it can grant +1 to one Att and one Ability, or +1 Defense – but the +1 Defense has a limitation when used on Defenses over 7. Why not just add +1 to Dex/Melee, or whatever? That’d grant +1 Defense, as well, with no caveat. Also, how does this Charm interact with Excellencies? It says a +1 bonus to the stats, not to rolls based on the stats. (The same question could be asked of Increasing Strength Exercise – or, for that matter, Awakened Carnal Demiurge (#10).) This would feel a lot more potent if it wasn’t a one-turn bonus – if a Solar is going to give up his entire turn, a single +2 bonus is pretty limited.

                                                    I don’t know if it’s coming through in the Charm summaries, but as of #9, there are a lot of powers here that are just “MOAR DICE” - #1, 2, 4, 5, and 9 all fit that description. That’s a shame, because this is probably the least interesting thing a Solar Charm can do – well. Other than maybe imitate animal sounds (great fun at parties!). Unfortunately, it may also be one of the most potent things, as 2e demonstrated amply – but I don’t want five different variations on “Save some motes on your Excellency.”

                                                    Enemy-Castigating Solar Judgment (#11) is so very much worse than Sharp-Light of Judgment Stance – 3m, 1wp for one attack? There’s also a system limitation here – there’s no way to express “Decisive-only or social influence” in keywords, and yet that’s what we have.

                                                    My impression is that Presence has a lot of Reflexive Charms that would be Simple in most other Abilities. That’s not a complaint, but it makes the Presence effects considerably more useful in a number of cases. (It also violates the original description of Reflexive, of course - +X dice neither enhances an unrolled action nor creates a reflexive one.)

                                                    Mind-Wiping Gaze (#16) has again the problem of catching people just before they inflict some kind of influence. It feels like there should be an “influence radar” Charm, in the same sense that there’s, say, “Larceny Radar” – otherwise, these effects seem like they’re going to lead to some awkward question exchanges (“Hey, is he just about to try to say something?” “Um, you don’t know, dude.”). This is not as bad as “He was going to say something, but he’s decided to put it off a little,” though – but I’d like the Charm much more if it was a broader “I was just about to do something…” That feels both a little easier to predict, and a little higher utility – “Someone has to tell the king about this!” says the guard; “Lovely weather we’re having,” says the Solar.

                                                    Hypnotic Tongue Technique (#17) is just nasty. I especially like the clause that rejecting the influence causes the target to forget the influence – this was a major limitation in 2e’s version of the power.

                                                    Worshipful Lackey Acquisition (#18) is kind of fun, but I’m not really sure what it’s for – I initially read it as actually turning minor enemies into allies, which is a fun twist on some narrative tropes, but it looks like that’s more of an edge case.


                                                    Favor-Conferring Prana (#23) has the duration I wanted Empowering Shout to have – but can’t raise things over 5. I mean, that’s a sensible result, but it deep-sixes what seems like the most frequent use case (i.e., combat). Possibly this is for enabling otherwise unspecced people to be secondary combatants/socialites/etc.?

                                                    I’m really not feeling either social ability so far – too many “cheaper Excellency” effects, or things that feel like they should just happen for free (“Sure, Beloved Solar, I’ll take out your trash for you.”). Between them, there’s a good spread of powers here, but it doesn’t feel like enough good stuff to support either Ability on its own – there’s just less interesting stuff to do that isn’t “dicewhomp hard, all day.”

                                                    Yes: Hypnotic Tongue Technique looks very useful. I had a player get a lot of use out of the free Excellency.
                                                    No: The lack of any description of how Charm-based bonuses to stats, as opposed to bonuses to stat rolls, interacts with Excellencies.
                                                    Just Plain Weird: The prophet Charms – Prophet-Uplifting Evocation (#19) and Shedding Infinite Radiance (#20) – are baffling. In what circumstances am I going to want to first buy Charms, and then spend further XP or motes sustaining Charms, to grant a small (and potentially temporary) bonus to a mortal follower? What concept is this supporting that has – to steal a line from Root – more than purely narrative benefit? (Who’s going to go off and roll a bunch of dice for my three uplifted mortal followers, anyway?)
