Maybe he whispered to you or maybe his essence robbed off on you, but a part of him now resides in you. All the knowledge and power of the first shadow now flow trough you. You have made a pact with five days night.
        Shaping ritual:
        Since five days night is never present during the day, he could not pass on knowledge of how to harness mystical energy during that period. The only thing he could say is “stick to the shadows so that the divines in the sky don’t see you.”
        If you start the scene expose to the sun, moon or one of the five maiden, you only receive {(intelligence + occult)/2} sorcerous motes. If you start the scene in the shadows or unexposed to one of the divine, you gain the {(intelligence + occult) + essence rating} each time you use shaping ritual. Since five days night is the only truly active god during calibration, all shaping ritual wield double sorcerous mote during that period, following the normal light/shadow rule.
        Five days night has a lot of accumulated knowledge and hungers for more. Has you ingest page from old tomes, the ink character appear on your skin and flow in direction of your eyes. The more you eat the more your eye become pitch black sphere from the ink that it absorbs. Eating pages from a book will confer 1 to 4 sorcerous motes. 1 for a quarter too 4 for the complete book, the blackness of your eyes fill from center in the same way. If you eat more then one book you start to cry ink from you eyes and gain no more motes.
        Rules of the season (merit 2)
        You gain knowledge of the intricate ways season spirit affect the world during each season, you can now tap directly in that energy:
        During the season of air: you tap in the air flows of your lung to reduce your weight, gain +1 on movement rolls. When falling reduce distance by one range band
        During the season of wood: you tap in the primal essence in your body. When hiding in vegetation gain +1 stealth and covers is one step further.
        During the season of earth: you tap in your bone to harden them. All craft are less strenuous gain -1 difficulty. You gain natural 1 soak and hardness has long has you touch the ground
        During the season of fire: you tap in you’re the emotion of joy and hatred. All major and defining intimacy gets +1 to align and opposed dice pool. +1 to damage versus hated intimacy.
        During the season of water: you tap in the vast amount of water in your body. You can breath underwater. Gain +1 evasion

        The shadows knows all (merit 3)
        Your eyes become two sphere of pitch black darkness. When you eat a book, you skin starts to become pitch black, starting from the area around your eyes and outward; you no longer cry ink and can now absorb a number of books equal to your essence per scene. When making a lore or occult roll, add +2 to your dice pool. Gain another +1 per tome you have eaten this scene, doing so consumes one book from your body. You will need to eat more books to use the bonus again
        Kiss from the shadows (merit 5)
        You are blesse by a kiss on the forehead by five days night. You accept a part of him in you, becoming a creature of darkness in exchange to a direct access to his essence. You permanently gain +1 essence and become a creature of darkness for game purpose. Your blood becomes pitch black and can be seen thought your skin; your eyes are now always black spheres. Consuming book now makes your whole body look like a living shadow. When confronted with the light from one of the celestial exalted, make a willpower roll diff 2 to be able to stand in close range or reflexively move to short range of him. If you are a celestial exlted roll ( stamina + resistance) the # of success represent the number of round you can maintain solar charm or spell of the celestial 1st and second circle.

                        02-16-2018, 08:22 PM
                        Originally posted by Gigaton-Falcon-Emu View Post
                        Regarding Shadow Replacement Technique:

                        -If a Solar is in the shadow of a target without exerting control, how would the host percieve the possession? Is it in any way uncomfortable or do they more or less feel normal? Can they control themselves freely until the Solar exerts its control?
                        The Solar is free not to exert control. I don't imagine doing so is particularly stealthy, since your victim presumably saw you dive into their shadow and is aware that's not a normal thing, but ultimately, Storyteller call.

                        -If a solar controlled individual tries to attune to artifact armor or weapons, can the solar attune/force them to attune and wield them?
                        The Solar can attempt to compel them to attune the same way they can compel them to take any other action. The Solar can't use her own motes to attune an artifact for her victim (or, more broadly speaking, use her own Charms to enhance the victim's actions).
