                                                        Generally, the category "awakened mortals" doesn't exist in 3e the way it did in 2e. What you're going to see, going by Adversaries of the Righteous, are mortals with a particular set of tricks - they're a sorcerer, they're a thaumaturge, they're God-Blooded, they have a mortal-usable artifact (or an artifact specific to them), they can use the Charms of one or two martial arts, etc. If you have an Essence pool, you have it as part of your thing, rather than it being a thing in itself.
