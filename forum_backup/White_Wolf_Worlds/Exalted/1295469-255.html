                                    Originally posted by Organ Boy View Post
                                    1. The topic about using Ambition 3 Solar Workings to achieve transhumanist effects came up with some friends, with regards about how Infernals have that as an avenue for transhumanism (by targeting themselves with the "Create a supernatural being of a singular nature and considerable power" aspect, Perfect Cell-style) even though Devil-Tigers as a discrete category of being no longer exist. My question is what is the appropriate "powerlevel" for an entity created by this feat-would an E5 Solar doing this be akin to a Third Circle Demon, an Incarna, an Elemental Dragon (whatever they actually are in 3e now they apparently aren't 3CDs) or "merely" a very powerful and exotic behemoth like Karvara? The description for it seemed rather open-ended and we were just wondering what would fit the bill here.
                                    I want to note that you're actually asking three different questions here.

                                    1) "Can I use an effect that says 'create' to modify an existing thing?" — No, "create" means "create." A sorcerer who wants to change her nature needs to look at effects that talk about modifying an existing being, not effects that create new beings.

                                    2) "Do 3e Infernals retain 2e's transhumanist focus?" — This isn't really a theme in 3e. Infernals will have devil-body transformations, but that isn't really about forever casting off their humanity to become something new. (Though of course you can do whatever you enjoy at your table.)

                                    3) "What is the appropriate power level for a creature created by an Ambition 3 working?" — Lesser elemental dragon, Third Circle demon, and cool behemoth all seem to be within the broad span of power appropriate to such workings. This is ideally something that will be covered in an upcoming book on sorcery if we can get it onto the schedule.
