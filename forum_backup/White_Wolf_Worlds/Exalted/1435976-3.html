                                                I don't have an answer for this one. Just wanted to see what other people's take on it is:

                                                Originally posted by Sith_Happens View Post
                                                What's the Dynasty's etiquette on where you do and don't bring your daiklave?
                                                The age old debate.

                                                I have always, since 1E, ruled that Dragon-Blooded are expected to be holy warriors first and... whatever else second. As such, it's always appropriate to be ready for battle. Always. Always.

                                                "You are on trial for murder."
                                                "Okay, lemme grab my daiklave real quick."

                                                "Bath time."
                                                "Okay, lemme grab my daiklave real quick."

                                                "An audience with the Scarlet Empress."
                                                "Okay, lemme grab my daiklave real quick."

                                                "Your grandmother's funeral."
                                                "What did she die of?"
                                                "She accidently stabbed herself in the chest with her daiklave whilst eating her breakfast in bed."
                                                "Okay, lemme grab my daiklave real quick."

                                                Even in the first age:

                                                "I'm sorry Heirophant, but you know the rules, no weapons on the Deliberative floor."
                                                "Very well, hold this for me Jeeves."
                                                "Very good, sir."
                                                "Your holiness, there are no exceptions. I'm afraid your man-servant-"
                                                "He's a Dragon-Blooded."
                                                "Oh, very good then! Go on through."

                                                You'd think that'd get silly... but 2E let you sleep in jade armor so... And L5R lets you carry a katana everywhere.

                                                On the other hand, all the games I've played in have been very big on formal etiquette dictating that you not bring massive oversized weapons into formal situations. In my experience, this just leads to everyone taking Elemental Sheath or Elemental Bolt Attack (or Sorcery or Martial Arts) so they can always be armed anyway.

                                                    If for nothing but variety I'd say that you need to be in a special position to carry weapons in certain social situations. Like if you're the garrison commander, or the leader of an imperial legion you can show up to a fancy party in armor with your daiklave hanging by your side. It's not like as soon as you're off duty you're expected to lock it up or anything. Which might change too, a house Tepet party might be a lot more lenient than a house Cynis one, for example.

                                                    I think a good metric is just how much, socially, do people trust you to be disciplined with your weapon. The main reason seeing a weapon on somebody makes them uncomfortable is that people are unpredictable, and no matter how well you know somebody something dangerous might happen, or somebody might escalate something way further and faster than it needs to be. I don't like seeing you with a sword, because even if I have a sword I don't want our argument to turn into a swordfight because your dumb ass can't control yourself.

                                                    If the person is like, a monk, or a more senior soldier or something, hopefully one without dangerous PTSD, or somebody sworn utterly to defend your life in some way, that gives you more leeway to carry arms in your presence.

                                                    Part if it too is just to get milage out of all the charms that conceal weapons. Not just elemental sheath but Hidden Chakram and Hundred Thorns Blosom and stuff too. Also to get milage out of the benefit in brawl and martial arts to never be disarmed.
