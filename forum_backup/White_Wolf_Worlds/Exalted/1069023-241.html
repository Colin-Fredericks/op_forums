                            Originally posted by Growls View Post
                            First; Why is the Last City called "The Last City"?

                            Second; What is Feng-Yi? Some sort of parallel dimension offering succor to the Dragonblooded?
                            Feng-Yi is the Last City, so called because it it is where Dragon-Blooded complete their pilgrimage after venturing through the five elemental shrines.

                            EDIT: Earlier, I'd said Feng-Yi was one of the shrine cities, which isn't true. I shouldn't answer before I've had coffee!

                            Third; what the hell is the deal with Asura? Is that a shrine city like the ones mentioned in regards to the Five Elemental Dragons? It's one of those locations that is on the map, but is never really mentioned by the text.
                            There are many more locales on the map than the text covers. Asura may be described in later books.

                            On a sidenote, is Moon-Touched just another fancy word for beastman, or a specific kind of creature bearing the mark of the Lunar Exalted?
                            Moon-Touched is a term for children of the Lunar Exalted that carry a measure of their power. They are what were called Lunar Half-Castes in previous editions, although we're dropping that term because of its real-word derogatory usage. Not all Moon-Touched are beastfolk, and not all beastfolk are Moon-Touched.

                                                            Rather than answering your questions directly, I'll try to give you something that'll help you run it.

                                                            The Caul is a strange land with an otherworldly nature. The Dragon-Blooded maintain a desperate defense of Faxai against the Lunar Exalted and their armies of Moon-Touched beastfolk, and the faithful, the idealistic, or the foolish might seek to reclaim the other four shrine cities that the Realm has lost to Lunar control. But the Caul is much larger than the war, and the wilderness beyond the cities holds things stranger and more deadly than Lunars. Your players might encounter impossible geographical features, tribes of Caulfolk that hold strange-seeming customs and superstitions, primeval spirits or other beings as old as Creation, horrors and miracles hidden away by Luna herself at the dawn of time, or similar strangeness.

                                                            If your players are already strongly invested one way or another with regards to the Realm or the Silver Pact, I recommend focusing on the conflict over the shrine cities - let them hold the walls of Faxai against besieging Lunars force, or lead the siege themselves. If you don't think they'd be interested in that, there's the allure of exploring a mysterious land that might as well be another world entirely.
