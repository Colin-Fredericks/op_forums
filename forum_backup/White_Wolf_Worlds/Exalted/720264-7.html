                So, 3rd Edition seems to be getting back to the 1st Edition setting, more or less, which had a good premise the books seemed intent on not getting through.

                See, the most terrifyingly powerful thing in Exalted is the Realm. Nothing else comes close. It is Ten Thousand Dragons. Ten Thousand EXALTED. The Realm has people like Cathak Cainan, Peleps Aramida, and Tepet Arada. Even though Arada left, the other two did not, and it is noted that he is one of the best, but not THE only one. It has people like Ragara Bagwei, Mnemon and Cynis Mond, and even though the first and third are away from Realm politics, they do believe in the Realm, they did work for its betterment, and you can be damn sure they will show up like Gandalf in Helm's Deep if your anathema army is crossing the sea. It has immaculate monks with multiple anathema kills like Peleps Deled, Imminent Thunder, and Ragara Myrrun. Each one a beacon of celestial-defying might.

                And all of them are decked from head to toe in the panoply of ages gone by and a world bled dry.

                Remember the special caveat of Artifact 5, in previous editions? How you have access to their arsenal of wonders? Grand airships flying the sky and raining lightning death upon the ground below. Immense statues of obsidian that breathe out molten light. Thousand-Forged Dragons. Grand Warstriders whose Evocations can glass entire cities. All that is within the vaults of the Dynasty. All that can be pulled out if the need is great enough, if you can just convince them, at that level, to give you the keys to chariots pulled by palace-trampling bronze bulls and armor of brass that turns men into gods of war.

                And all of that does not take the Sidereals into account.

                We are talking about sixty-plus of them. The majority faction. The HEGEMONY. The Bronze Faction. Including the single most powerful Exalted alive, in political and personal terms, Chejop Kejack. Including the most terrifying martial artist alive, Anys Syn. We are talking about the people currently ruling Heaven. The people whom the politics are working FOR. The people who get to have the lion's share of the resources, of the projects, of the allies. The ones who get the first pick at Starmetal, the ones who get all the best toys. The ones under the wings of the powers. And they are aware. Their entire plan IS The Realm. Every day, they look at it, they strengthen it, they fight for it. Sixty-plus Celestial Exalted with Heaven on their pocket, and they are dedicated into preserving, fighting for, and fighting with, this might.

                And with them, there's the Gods

                You know, the Imperial City has a God. He is incredibly powerful, wealthy, and loves the Empire. The Empire itself. All the gods of things the Empire loves, and furthers, such as Imperial Might, Rulership, Order, Cities, Trade, Dominance. They are the hegemony, and those thriving under this hegemony are powerful Gods indeed. Those who decided to play with the Immaculate Order rather than to raise grand cults may not receive power like the Great Forks and Whitewall Lords... but those who did it since the beginning and are tied to the machinery of it have quite a bit of power, and they will protect that which empowers them.

                And then, there's the weird.

                We know there is a Legion made out of Mountain Folk. And while they are being entirely reworked, their status as a Realm Tributary is not something noted as changing. There are Exigents noted as being on the side of the Realm, and in the what we know, it is noted they are part of the makeup of Wyld Hunts. There might be a Faerie-Queen enamored of a Dynastic lord, her hobgoblins serving the Empire (despite the stigma against that extant after what Cevis Ghandarva did); and... likely much more strange things like the Mountain Folk. They rule Creation, and Creation is strange. Above and beyond the automatons mentioned above, weird folks with strange powers live in Creation, and the majority of them will give the Realm some token of their power.

                All that is the Realm. When united, it is the single most powerful thing extant. Return of the Scarlet Empress is a terrible book that worfs the entire rest of the setting, even THAT atrocity had to kill all Sidereals and destabilize the Realm before the Yozi came out, because, that sword described above? It would cleave the Ebon Dragon in half if its edge hadn't been dulled. It is the closest thing to the mighty Exalted Host of old to currently exist. It is terrifying.

                And the existence of the Caul means it FAILED.

                Quoth the old man:

                Originally posted by Chejop Kejack, Exalted Third Edition Core
                Lunars—the most numerous; one of the most dangerous
                as well. He was keenly aware of the Full Moons out
                there doing who-knows-what, maneuvering for some
                plan hidden even from Kejak’s eyes. They were hidden
                in the West, too, there on the Caul. The Caul had held so
                much promise in the wake of the Usurpation, but all those
                halcyon dreams had come to ash and blood along with
                the rest of the age, and now they were at the brink of a
                war that would send even more cracks through the fabric
                of his Creation.
                He wanted nothing more than to have The Caul. All that sword described above came down upon it.

                They failed.

                Ma-Ha-Suchi took over his Nameless Lair. You know what this means?

                It means Lookshy and The Realm, both at the height of their power, utterly failed to stop him. And they have to have tried. They have to have done their best.

                They lost.

                Raksi took over Sperimim. SPERIMIM! Capital of all learning, the place that holds the Book of Three Circles. It is priceless. It is something all those powers would wish. And she took it. And nobody will take from her. Nobody dares to approach her.

                The Lunars were driven from the world, when they were Usurped with the Solars. And each step they took was fighting the most powerful empire in Creation, and winning, step by step. That? Is something that the hunted, reincarnating Solars utterly failed at. Joachim, if he was indeed a Solar, may have made quite a splash, but he was still brought down. No Solars created dominions, none were ever able to survive its onslaught of power.

                That? Is not something a Solar in the Time of Tumult will ever be able to replicate. Because everything is now falling down.

                It was asked many times in this thread what is their victory, and what they did that is worthy of being Celestial. They looked upon the greatest might in the setting, and slowly, but inexorably, pushed it back. Something the reincarnating Solars weren't able to do. If you don't think that's impressive, or worthy of Celestial Might, you weren't paying attention.
