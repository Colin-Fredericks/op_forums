    “In some parts they know the master of Throne as the god Zayus Patrom, father of lightning. In others they know him as the great warrior called Angel-Breaker, who fights with a spear dipped in molten silver. Only one name is bestowed upon him in common across all the wretched cosmos: King.”
    -Sayophraxes, Silent Sister of the Wax Covenant

    Dude. Fighting with a spear dipped in molten silver is precisely the sort of thing my Ysr sorcerer (or any Resources 4+ Solar) would do. Summon some fire elementals to carry and heat the raw materials, dip your Longfang in molten silver, and transform your enemies into (briefly) living artwork of silver kintsugi as you slay them.

    How would you run this, not only as a gimmick for a stunt, but as a tactic that could be repeated over and over again? It seems like you'd need Craft(Combat Taxidermy) or something else really ridiculous in order to make true artwork out of their remains, and that would also help with the preservation of their corpses. Does this warrant custom charms? A weapon specific to the task? It seems like leaving a trail of molten silver wherever your weapon strikes would do aggravated damage instead of lethal, but this is definitely not a subject directly addressed in the rules. Opinions?

    Actually, forget the sorcerer, I'm turning this guy into a Deathknight for my campaign.
