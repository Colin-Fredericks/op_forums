                                                        Based on earlier dev comments, I suspect that Warstrider will simply be a term for any sort of giant armor that people pilot around rather than for a specific type of giant mobile armor as was the case in 2nd edition.

                                                        Some warstriders might be magical golems composed of rock and stone or wood and metal. Others might be like large automatons, crafted from complex clockwork and gears. Yet others could be formed from still-living demons, or made from necromantically animated bone and sinew with plenty of bone-jacking possibilities, or even unique wyld-behemoths you can don like armor.

                                                        Ultimately all of these Warstriders will share similar statistics and any differences will largely be secondary - for example, a warstrider made out of rock might repair itself by sinking into the earth for 5 days, or one made out of demons might have a unique ability that allows its screams to instill fear into those who hear it. But the core capabilities and statistics of Warstriders will be fairly similar regardless of how or what they are constructed from.

                                                        I imagine that the most "common" type of Warstrider will continue to be the Escaflowne-inspired ones that we saw back in First Edition, but likely the gameline will be clear that Warstriders can take almost any form and be created from almost any type of material.
