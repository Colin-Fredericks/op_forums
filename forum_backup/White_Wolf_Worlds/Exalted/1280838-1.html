        The main advantages of the warstrider are as follows:

        ​1) Truly awesome Evocations.
        ​2) Legendary Size bonus/mitigating the bonuses of a Legendary Size opponent.
        3) The free Devastate action each turn.
        ​4) Having a whole second health track, plus the warstrider's Soak and Hardness.
        5) The warstrider's weapons are artifact weapons.
        ​6) Sheer awesome factor.

        The main disadvantages of the warstrider are as follows:

        ​1) Huge Merit cost. The lowest-costed ones are still five dots, plus you need to power it with a hearthstone.
        ​2) Huge mote cost that could go toward powering combat Charms.
        ​3) Fighting human-scale opponents gets a -4 dice penalty. There's a further -3 penalty if you're not specifically trained to fight in a warstrider.
        ​4) It's vulnerable to gambits that disable its limbs or block its vision or breach its fuselage in a way that leaves the pilot vulnerable.
        ​5) There's not a single warstrider Evocation available at Essence 1.
        6) Maintenance and repair.

        So again, you can't just toss any warstrider at any Dawn and say, "There, now he wins."

                My feeling about how it should work is that it depends entirely on their age, experience, Caste, specialization of strengths chosen, circumstances of the battle, and whether the Lunar is rocking any significant artefact of their own of a comparable grandeur and puissance to the Warstrider! (Plus luck).

                I'd say Warstriders should be certainly be great for offsetting the disadvantages faced by being a small scale fighter trying to battle something big...

                So if you're playing a Solar who totally doesn't have any other way of fighting something big, and is really focused on human scale opponents, a warstrider might be an efficient way to scale up and offset your disadvantage.

                But Solars should have loads of other ways they can draw to tackle big monsters on even if they don't have a Warstrider - ranged, athletics (lots of leaping and clambering), sorcery, evocations, and so on.

In fact unless you're trying to pull off some 40 mote instant kill Solar doom combo on turn one it probably is strictly the best thing to invest motes in.

                                        The real key to feeling like a powerful exalt is don't compare yourself to a similarly invested Solar, lol.

                                                        Sure, some warstriders might have Iron Man-esque internal countermeasures, and less hardware in the interface. See my first response.

Sure sure, the XP and BP costs shake out a bit differently (although if Strength and Dexterity are both in caste...), but all things being equal, your Lunar can probably consistently hit a Solar (or threaten to hit enough to pull in costly defence), and even a warstrider's soak can't stop all of that (what kinds of armour piercing Charms will Lunars have?).

                                                        There are probably things within the Solar combat suite that come out stronger than what Lunars can get. Free excellencies, say. Defenses that can go a bit further. Things that maintain the concept of Solars as the mightiest overall.
