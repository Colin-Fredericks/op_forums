    Originally posted by aluminiumtrioxid View Post

    And critique I shall!



    Converting 1 die to a success is, on average, the equivalent of adding +1 non-charm die to the roll (you turn 0.5 successes into 1). Increasing Parry by 1 is the equivalent of a 2m Excellency. That's a pretty hefty passive bonus, so I'd bump up the attunement cost to +2m.



    Conceptually, a good first Evocation, but the wording is unclear. Does "allied ranged attackers that are currently aiming at other targets" mean "allied ranged attackers who took the Aim action on their last turn"? If yes, does "may spontaneously switch their target to the wielder's opponent at no expense" include "retain bonus dice gained from aiming", or does it just allow them to make an attack out to medium+ range without aiming? Moreover, does the last effect last until the beginning, or the end of the wielder's next turn?

    In any case, effectively reducing the target's defense by 2 is an incredibly potent effect (+2 non-charm automatic successes on all incoming ranged attack rolls!), and a smart party can reap massive benefits from this if they're built around doing so. I'd either add a WP to the cost, or bump it up to 4 motes and give it a reset condition, with the assumption that you're modelling the opposition on the corebook's Antagonists session, most of whom have no convenient way to negate defense penalties. If your table regularly features opponents who can do so, the Evocation is fine as-is.



    I like how this expands on the native niche of Thrown as the tricky, gambit-oriented fighting style. On the other hand, I think using mean Evasion is quite unwieldy (are you rounding up or down? doesn't the math get annoying to deal with when there's 3+ opponents hanging around?). Just use the highest rating. Otherwise, it seems okay (cost is pretty hard to figure out - it lets you break initiative order, but that's basically Thunderclap Rush Attack, which costs 3 motes, but it also adds 1-3 non-charm successes to a disengage, but it's also tied to succeeding on an attack roll and has an Initiative cost, but it also moves people around and knocks them down, which is basically Mournful Crane's Cry, which costs 3m, but this is AoE, but MCC flings people away for 2 range bands - in any case, if you sum up the effects, 6 motes and the inherent costs of making a Distract gambit sound about right).



    Seems like a pretty fun effect. Mechanically, it's a bit all over the place (summary says it lets you surprise attack a target, but it isn't actually a surprise attack, and the way it interacts with hardness is just... bwuh?). If you assume artifact armor to be rare, the damage boost can get out of hand quite quickly, but then again, the cost is incredibly high (about 15 motes if you full Excellency it, assuming Awareness 4 on part of the enemy, which strikes me as reasonable for most combatants important enough to warrant using a capstone on). And, of course, it runs into the problem of artifact armor and a decent Awareness score taking quite a lot out of its teeth, at which point you're stuck with an incredibly pricey effect that's of little benefit above Initiative 10 (and why would you launch your big decisive doomcombo while below Initiative 10?).

    So, umm, I'd basically make it an attempt to re-establish surprise, forget about the Hardness interaction, and reduce the cost.
    • I've upped the attunement cost, as suggested.
    • Follow the Wind's Lead now clarifies who gains the benefit and I lowered the defense penalty to a -1.
    • Whistle Down the Wind now uses the highest evasion.
    • Out of the Blue is now 4m and now simply provides a surprise attack or an ambush, depending how well the contested roll to establish stealth goes.

        Sorry, inspiration struck! It needs some mechanical help, though.

        The Adder's Fang (Green Jade Throwing Knife - Artifact ***)

        Forged during the height of the Shogunate for one of Shogun Shuoqing's favored daimyos, the Adder's Fang is a perfectly balanced throwing knife of green jade with a trio of pearls set into the hilt. The Fang has a long and storied history of murder, including the assassination of Shogun Shuoqing by his daimyo. In fact, everyone who has ever given the Adder's Fang as a gift has, thus far, been killed by it. Whether this is a true curse or merely ill fortune is hotly debated by those who discuss such things.

        Evocations of the Adder's Fang

        A Solar or Dragon-Blood who attunes to the Adder's Fang grants the weapon the Balanced and Special tags. The Special tag indicates that the Fang, once thrown, returns to its wielder's hand like an Infinite Chakram. The Fang otherwise behaves as a Light Artifact Thrown weapon with the Thrown (Short) tag.

        Adder's Venom
        Cost: 3m; Mins: Essence 1
        Type: Supplemental
        Keywords: Decisive-only
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisites: None

        The least and most obvious power of the Adder's Fang is to produce a dose of a highly-potent nerve toxin, injected into a victim's bloodstream by even the smallest scratch. The toxin goes to work on the victim's chakras in seconds, slowing his reactions and causing his muscles to spasm and twitch uncontrollably. A successful decisive attack made with this Evocation injects the target with a dose of Snake Venom.


        Toxic Bond
        Cost: 1m; Mins: Essence 1
        Type: Reflexive
        Keywords: Perilous
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisites: Adder's Venom

        The power of the Adder's Fang is not merely in the wound it deals, but in the venom that courses through the target's veins thereafter. By activating this Evocation, the wielder can forge an instantaneous bond between herself and any target currently suffering from at least one dose of Snake Venom applied through this Evocation's prerequisite. This bond funnels the strength sapped by the venom from the victim into the wielder. Any Initiative lost due to Adder's Venom this turn is immediately gained by the wielder.


        Venomous Whisper
        Cost: 5m,1W; Mins: Essence 2
        Type: Reflexive
        Keywords: Psyche
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisites: Toxic Bond

        The magic of the Adder's Fang is such that it can poison the mind as well as the body. This Evocation can be activated only when a target is suffering from Adder's Venom and has been the target of Toxic Bond in the same scene. While the poison courses through his veins, he begins to hallucinate, and the wielder of the blade gets a single, tortured glimpse of his psyche, functioning as a reflexive Read Intentions action to determine a Minor Tie. In that instant, she can metaphysically twist and cause that existing Tie of Minor strength to invert its emotional context, from hate to love, for example, or from pleasure to disgust. She must know the Tie she seeks to twist, and she cannot change the emotional context in any way but reversing it - she could not turn a man's love of his wife into lust, for example - only hatred.

        If used against the same target, and the same Tie, in the same scene, reduce the Evocation's cost to 3m and the Evocation can intensify the Tie to Major strength.

                    Sky-Bender (Black & White Jade and Starmetal Aegis Shield, Artifact 4)


                    The Base

                    An artifact Heavy Shield (Accuracy +1 Damage +12 Defense +0 Overwhelming 5 Attunement 7 Tags: Chopping, Lethal, Melee, Shield, Smashing)

                    Concept

                    It was forged by the Solar that captured Isidoros single-handedly, at great personal sacrifice (their entire Legion), and echoes the legendary powers of that particular Yozi.

                    Description

                    A great matte disc of Black Jade, covered in a coarse but intricate engraving in Old Realm script, rendered in wire-fine White Jade and Starmetal, describing a lament for a worthy opponent at the cessation of hostility. Has a single Hearthstone Setting, on the rear face, off-center, in line with the hand-grip.

                    Evocations of Skybender

                    This Heavy Shield finds its greatest potential in the hands of those who are self-reliant, driven, and relentless. Exalts of any kind that attune to Skybender add their Anima level to any Parry Defense they make with it, at a cost of reducing their Evasion by an equal amount, as the Black Boar was never one to flee from a fight, but rather to allow His foes to bask in His singularity.

                    Solars that attune to Skybender are drawn to greater acts of bravado and heroism, often heedless of risks to life, limb, and welcome company; they add their Anima level to Resolve when defending an Intimacy relating to fighting on, be it against a worthy adversary or in the face of terrible odds.

                    Sidereals, in contrast, find the solidity of Skybender’s fate allows them to ground their own; while attuned, witnesses add the Vizier’s Anima level to rolls to recall his presence.

                    Dragon-Blooded, whose forebearers paid the greatest cost in bringing Skybender into existance, add their Anima level to their Hardness and Soak while attuned.

                    Veil of Personal Magnetism

                    Cost: 2m, 1a per level of Cover, optionally 1wp (+1wp) Mins: Essence 1

                    Type: Supplemental

                    Keywords: None

                    Duration: One turn

                    Prerequisites: None

                    Skybender, living up to its name, diffuses its Bearer’s Anima into a gauzy haze that throws mirages and deflects attacks.
                    This Evocation supplements a Take Cover action, allowing the Bearer to channel his Anima to bolster his Defense. The Bearer’s Anima must, at a minimum, be at Glowing to use this ability. For every level of Anima committed, the Bearer may treat his Cover as one degree better, to a maximum of Full. At the start of the Bearer’s next turn, his Anima fades to dim. Optionally, if no Cover is available, the Bearer may spend a point of temporary Willpower to summon viable Cover from his own Anima with this Evocation. This cover may move with him, and there are no additional limitations on available actions he can take while this power is active.
                    At Essence 2 and above, the Bearer may expend an additional Willpower to extend the Cover granted or improved by this Evocation to a Battle Group of a Size no greater than his Essence.

                    Lacuna of Crushing Eminence

                    Cost: 3m, 1a Mins: Essence 1

                    Type: Reflexive

                    Keywords: Psyche, Uniform, Clash

                    Duration: indefinite

                    Prerequisites: Veil of Personal Magnetism

                    In blocking his adversary’s strikes, Skybender draws its bearer’s quarry into his orbit with the strength of a collapsing star.
                    The Bearer may clash a withering or decisive attack with such singular skill that it stokes the enemy’s fear and respect. This clash attack does no damage, but, in addition to the penalty to Defense, allows the Bearer to make a reflexive Instill action (rolling Charisma + Melee), adding threshold successes from the Clash to the roll, and creating or improving an Intimacy of Awe toward the Bearer. This Intimacy lasts until a week passes with the Bearer not being within Essence miles of the Target or the Bearer is Crashed by the Target. The Bearer’s Anima must be at Glowing before using this Charm, resets to Dim at the beginning of his next turn, and counts as the Bearer’s attack action.

                    All Falls to Force

                    Cost: 1m, 1wp Mins: Essence 2

                    Type: Reflexive

                    Keywords: None

                    Duration: One Turn

                    Prerequisites: Lacuna of Crushing Eminence

                    Marked as worthy opponents, the Bearer’s foes are drawn to the hunt.
                    When using Skybender to defend himself, the Bearer may use this Evocation to make those they cherish inapplicable targets. All incoming attacks must target him until his next turn. During this time, if his defense is penetrated, those whom he holds an Intimacy towards, or who hold an Intimacy toward him, cannot be attacked. His Anima must be at Burning to use this Evocation.

                    Magnanimous Adversary Ansible
                    Cost: 2m, 1+a Mins: Essence 3

                    Type: Reflexive

                    Keywords: Uniform

                    Duration: Instant

                    Prerequisites: All Falls to Force, Lacuna of Crushing Eminence

                    Distance means little to those who are the center, and Skybender was born in the shadow of the center.
                    While the Bearer's Anima is at least at Glowing, those that recognize his greatness, and are bathed in it, are always readily at hand. The Bearer may invoke this Evocation to instantaneously close the distance between himself and one that either bears an Intimacy toward him or that he bears an Intimacy toward, appearing at their side as if he had been there all along. The distance he may cross is proportional to his Anima level; at Glowing, he may cross Short distances, at Burning, Medium, at Iconic, Long. He may hold the activation of this Charm to Clash a Withering or Decisive Attack made against or by the subject of the Tie with Skybender. This counts as the Bearer's Attack Action, and causes his Anima to fade back to Dim.

                    Unassailable Whim-Driven Behemoth

                    Cost: 5m, 3a Mins: Essence 3

                    Type: Simple

                    Keywords: None

                    Duration: One Scene

                    Prerequisites: All Falls to Force, Lacuna of Crushing Eminence, Magnanimous Adversary Ansible

                    At the heights of power, those who’ve truly bonded with Skybender are like unto the great beasts of ages uncounted.
                    When the Bearer’s Anima becomes Iconic, Skybender’s magic makes it extraordinarily difficult for Trivial enemies to engage him in combat, regardless of their numbers. He does not take onslaught penalties from any attack made by Trivial opponents or Battle Groups, although magically inflicted onslaught penalties still apply against him. Withering attacks made by such enemies cannot drop him below 1 Initiative unless they have a post-soak damage of 10 dice (although attackers can still gain the full amount of Initiative damage dealt). Decisive attacks made by such enemies cannot deal more than (3 + attacker’s Strength) levels of damage to the Bearer with a single attack, not counting any levels of damage added by Charms or other magic. This Evocation ends prematurely if the Bearer is Crashed, causing their Anima to fade to Dim.



                    Feedback welcomed.

                            EDIT: Says I posted at 9:59. sure. I started posting at 9:59. I finished posting about 16:00.

                            I've never create an artefact or evocations before, but I had this idea while pondering an alternative character for the game I'm in, and I want to get some feedback before I go in supplication to my ST. Formatting stolen wholesale from Jen's Lunar Eclipse (since it's also a mask, and I like the colours Jen used). I've included a few additional options for charms, because I had so many ideas and didn't know which ones would work best. Only one of the capstones is actually included in the final evocation set, so the capstone A is really jsut an extension of the previous ability, but one i'm not comfortable keeping if the other capstone is also in. This is all based largely on fluff and what I want the artefact to feel like, but I'm under no illusions that this is mechanically fine as it is - if you guys could set me straight, that'd be great.

                            Eyes of the Raksha (Moonsilver and Black Jade Mask ****)

                            The Eyes of the Raksha is a new artefact, crafted only months ago for the disfigured artist, Silent Song. It enhances and projects the ever-growing insanity of the wearer, helping others see the world as he does. The mask is also associated with scalding steam and burning water - the tools used to torture Silent Song moments prior to his Exaltation.

                            The first evocation of Eyes of the Raksha is unlocked if the wearer has a Derangement. If the wearer loses their Derangement, they lose access to all the evocations of Eyes of the Raksha until they regain their Derangement, although evocations with special activation rules must be unlocked again. The wearer of the Eyes of the Raksha beholds the world in an entirely alien way, and may automatically dismiss any attempt or situation that would remove their Derangement. (These rules are in place to allow for a character to regain their sanity if they should choose, but not require them to do so, and supports the heroic image of a character throwing away their source of power, only to return to it, contrite, when times are dire and the world needs them once more). The Black Jade is there because I want the mask to have steam associations, but the Black Jade isn't represented in this set of evocations.

                            Keyword: Maddening Insight. Maddening Insights represent the.. well, the maddening insights, accrued by those who face the Eyes of the Raksha. The Eyes feeds off of this power to tip the balance of situations towards true, deep madness, and those who wield its strength.


                            Seeing Through the Eyes of the Raksha
                            Cost: 3m; Mins: Essence 1
                            Type: Reflexive
                            Keywords: Psyche
                            Duration: Instant
                            Prerequisite: None

                            When the wearer is Seeing Through the Eyes of the Raksha, they can impart their understanding to the people around them, gracing everyone with the unwelcome insights she has gleaned from the union, and filling her foes with uncertainty.

                            As a reflexive action, when the wearer of the Eyes of the Raksha makes a successful social action, or someone makes a successful social action against her, her opponent gains one Maddening Insight. A character may only have as many points of Maddening Insight as the wearer has Essence, and must reroll successes on their first action each round equal to the number of Maddening Insight they possess, starting with non-doubled dice. At the end of a scene, any Maddening Insights are lost.


                            Lunatic's Alternate Truth
                            Cost: 2m per threshold success; Mins: Essence 1
                            Type: Reflexive
                            Keywords: Psyche
                            Duration: Instant
                            Prerequisite: Seeing Through the Eyes of the Raksha

                            Lunatic's Alternate Truth upgrades its prerequisite and costs 2 mote per threshold success scored against her on a social roll.

                            What is madness to the mad? Anyone can see meaning where there is none, but only the insight of lunacy allows one to see nothing where there is something. Sharing this true understanding, the wearer of the Eyes hides what is and presents a much more palatable "truth" to those who enquire.

                            When using Seeing Through the Eyes of the Raksha to add a point of Maddening Insight (if they cannot or do not gain another point for any reason, this evocation does not apply) when she is the target of social influence, the wearer of the Eyes pays 2 motes per threshold success her opponent scored, and simply lies about the result. A Profile action provides a false profile, a Read Intentions gives a false intimacy, a Persuade gives the impression of success but the wearer is simply unimpressed. Unless the wearer of the Eyes does something that clearly violates the falsehood she has provided, this alternative truth is as real to the other character as a success would normally be, and violating the alternative truth does not provide insight into the actual truth.

                            Special Activation Rules: Lunatic's Alternate Truth cannot be unlocked with XP. Instead, it automatically unlocks after someone with Maddening Insight makes a successful social action against the wearer of the Eyes of the Raksha, and cannot gain a point of Maddening Insight because they are already at the limit.


                            Ultimate Refuge of the Wise
                            Cost: 1m +2m per Maddening Insight; Mins: Essence 2
                            Type: Reflexive
                            Keywords: Psyche, Perilous
                            Duration: Instant
                            Prerequisite: Seeing Through the Eyes of the Raksha

                            The wise man knows that he knows nothing, and everyone else knows even less. When a foe is blinded by sight or deafened by hearing, the wearer of the Eyes of the Raksha releases them from their burden, freeing them to partake of the sheer intention of their actions without heed to the falsehood of "result".

                            The wearer of the Eyes of the Raksha may turn successes on a roll into points of initiative at a 1:1 ratio. This effect may be used on the wearer, or anyone with Maddening Insight, by spending at least 1 point of their Maddening Insight and 1 mote, plus 2 more motes per point of Maddening Insight spent. The ratio of Successes to Initiative is Maddening Insight spent to 1 initiative. When using it on themselves, the wearer of the Eyes transfers successes to initiative 1:1.

                            (In pure maths - The cost is 1 mote + 2X motes, and yields a ratio of X Successes to 1 initiative, where X is the Maddening Insights spent, or a flat 1 Success per initiative for herself. Although I feel like the effect is probably too much hassle if I have to use algebra to express it)


                            Teachings Absent of Meaning
                            Cost: -; Mins: Essence 3
                            Type: Permanent
                            Keywords: Psyche
                            Duration: Permanent
                            Prerequisite: Ultimate Refuge of the Wise

                            Teachings Absent of Meaning upgrades its prerequisite.

                            Imparting an absence of knowledge is a difficult task, even for those who know nothing, but it is always worthwhile. By completely opening the mind of an opponent gifted with true understanding, the wearer of the Eyes of the Raksha can transform all action into resultless intention.

                            When the wearer of the Eyes of the Raksha spends all of the Maddening Insight on an opponent with this evocation's prerequisite, the number of successes required to turn into a single point of initiative is doubled. Conversely, by opening her own mind, she can convert her own successes to initiative at a rate of 1 success to 2 initiative.

                            (In pure maths - This changes the prerequisite to cost 1 mote + 2X motes, and yield a ratio of 2X Successes to 1 initiative, where X is the Maddening Insights spent, or 1 Success to 2 initiative for herself)

                            Special Activation Rules: Teachings Absent of Meaning cannot be unlocked with XP. Instead it automatically unlocks after the wearer of the Eyes of Raksha gains Essence 3 and uses its prerequisite to expend the maximum number of Maddening Insights on an opponent.


                            The Philosophy of Nothing - Capstone A
                            Cost: 1m per Maddening Insight; Mins: Essence 3
                            Type: Reflexive
                            Keywords: Psyche
                            Duration: One Scene
                            Prerequisite: Ultimate Refuge of the Mind

                            The Philosophy of Nothing upgrades its prerequisite.

                            The student of the Eyes of the Raksha comes to a sudden realisation - The false dichotomy they have constructed between intent and result is just that, a falsehood. Imparting this knowledge, even for a fleeting moment, brings the world to a halt.

                            When the wearer of the Eyes uses Ultimate Refuge of the Wise or Teachings Absent of Meaning, the effect can be applied to Threshold successes or successes after soak.

                            Special Activation Rules: Teachings Absent of Meaning cannot be unlocked with XP. Instead it automatically unlocks after the wearer of the Eyes of Raksha reduces the total number of successes on a roll to 0, while increasing the target's initiative to 16 or more.


                            Opportunities to Learn - Capstone B
                            Cost: 1i per Maddening Insight; Mins: Essence 3
                            Type: Reflexive
                            Keywords: Psyche, Decisive, Perilous
