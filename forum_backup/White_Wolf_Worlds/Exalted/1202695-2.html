                That Sobriquet Merit, though...

                Two women faced each other beneath the boughs of a peach tree. Each wore a sword, but neither had yet drawn. Cathak Yelena gripped her scabbard in her left hand, the hilt of her blade in her right. Her skin was stony grey, and her hair the deep brown of rich loam. Her eyes were clear and hard as diamonds.

                Tepet Araun's hands rested, seemingly casually, on her own hilt and scabbard. Her hair, the pale violet-white of a lightning bolt, was bound up and held in place with two sticks. Her skin was as white and unblemished as newfallen snow; her eyes, the cloudy grey of solid ice.

                Both women stood straight, apparently relaxed. Araun noted the slight tightness around Yelena's mouth, and the tension in her shoulders. Her discipline is not as firm as she thinks, Araun thought. Yelena shifted her grip fractionally. Here it comes.

                Tepet Araun inhaled.

                No one, not any of the onlookers, not even Cathak Yelena, saw the strike. In one instant, Tepet Araun stood with her blade sheathed; in the next, she held it high, adding a flick of her wrist to the end of the motion to clean the sword of blood. Yelena crumpled to the ground, her eyes already empty.

                Tepet Araun exhaled.

                "NOOOOOOOO!" Cathak Khalid, Yelena's husband, burst from the crowd of onlookers and flung himself across his wife's body, wailing with grief. A single tear ran down Araun's cheek, leaving a trail of frost flowers in its wake: another challenger proven unworthy. She turned to go.

                "Monster!" Khalid hurled the familiar imprecation at Araun's back. Tepet Araun, known throughout the Realm and Threshold as the Winter Razor, kept walking.

                                    Cynis Erina, who was originally my first Dragon-Blood character and first Exalted character. I never got to properly update her to 2e because the 2e dragon-blood book was cancerously terrible.

                                    She's (probably - have to consider the new charmset in full first) an Air aspect, like her closest Exalted relatives, her aunt Cynis Beryl and her maternal grandmother*. While her own mother came from well -husbanded Essence, she never Exalted, and she was paired of with a boy from a rising patrician house suffering from an unfortunate lack of female heirs. Erina's mother had a dangerous habit of needling her sister about her family being more successful in business, and one night her parents died in a tragic accident. Her aunt adopted her -though mainly for the sake of her inheritance - and treated her as little more than a spare child in comparison to her spoiled, thus-far only son Dorim, who was expected to Exalt while she of course was not.

                                    *Also her blood father, Tepet Palim, her aunt's late husband. Fortunately for Erina, her aunt doesn't know. His mother, Tepet Mailin, is who eventually told Erina.

                                    She Exalted fairly ahead of the normal curve in primary school, so that flipped the relationship. It was surprising, but less so given she actually got her unfaithful uncle's progenerative Essence instead of Dorim. Suddenly she was Beryl's favored daughter, and her aunt revealed that she was actually a spy and assassin for the house rather than having much direct participation in business ventures. In addition to her normal training at the Spiral Academy, Erina learned violence and tradecraft which she took to enthusiastically.

                                    Then her shit of a cousin finally Exalted - unusually late, already in secondary school, but still Beryl wasted no time in favoring him again despite him being a boy (and a dumbass). When she graduates, it's clear that Beryl intends to get her stuck managing her parents' business instead of anything exciting. So while her aunt is away negotiating marriage, she slips into her aunt's secret rooms and steals her skycutter and a mask of disguise (I think that used to be a somewhat standardized artifact) and, while there, finds Beryl's ledger with a note about killing her sister and brother-in-law. So she sets out into the Threshold, seeking to make her own fortune and hopefully a Hearth that she can convince to come home and kill her aunt eventually.

                                    In play, she's mainly sneaky and talky, a Jane Bond sort with a similar taste for luxuries (and women).

                                    She might use White Veil instead of or with Thrown, depending on how that is.

                                    When I'll actually get to play her, I haven't a clue.
