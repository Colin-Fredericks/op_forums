        1st edition made no comment to that nature that I'm aware of. Second edition did as kind of a post-hoc justification, I think. But it isn't strictly necessary, I don't think. You could go either way.

        Personally: I think it's more thematically interesting to say that The Unconquered Sun looks upon creation and decides on exalts, but the exalts are now mortals who can surprise you for better or worse. It adds a certain degree of ambiguity to the incarnae to have them reflect creation in certain regards. Was the jade prison broken because The Unconquered Sun wished to look back on creation, and the exalted are now his intense gaze? Or did he start looking back because he felt the exaltations now free? Which is the cause, which is the effect? Leaving gods ambiguous this way I feel gives them a touch of mysticism and makes them feel less mechanical.

                                                01-07-2016, 04:52 PM
                                                Yeah, the Melee and Brawl tags are strongly symmetrical, in that each means "This weapon can be wielded to make close-ranged attacks with Melee/Brawl" (respectively). The Martial Arts tag is different -- it's only on weapons that cannot be wielded with Melee, Brawl, Archery, or Thrown. Whether or not a weapon has the MA tag, it can be used to make attacks using any MA style for which it's a style weapon.
