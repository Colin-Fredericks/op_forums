                              Originally posted by Epitome View Post
                              I can't help but imagine mortal toys as having a horror slant to them, what with mortals being surrounded by deadly danger on all sides.
                              I think this might be an overly-grim take on it. I'd compare it to real-world practices from Earth. In the real world, before the modern day and outside the rich First World economies, the average person was often surrounded by deadly danger, to much the same degree that someone in Creation is. Plague, war, abusive authority figures, and so forth all existed. But most cultures didn't develop toys that involved them. Rather, I'd expect that most toys in Creation follow similar patterns to those on Earth, being some combination of representations of domestic people and objects (things like dolls that represent average people or common farm animals and miniature versions of common tools or vehicles), representations of heroic or aspirational figures (dolls that represent standard iconography of heroic or legendary figures, toy soldiers, toy weapons, and so on), and objects that combine ease of creation and teaching basic life skills like hand-eye coordination (balls, sticks, jacks, etc.).

                              I think in areas where the Immaculate Faith is dominant, you'd be less likely to see dolls, particularly of the "aspirational" or "heroic" type. That's due to both the Immaculates' aniconic stance, and because I think it would go against general Immaculate doctrine that people shouldn't try to rise above their station. An Immaculate monk who saw a child playing with a doll that looked like a Dragon-Blood and pretending to be such a person would probably deliver a lecture, if not a more severe response.

                              Outside of Immaculate-controlled areas, dolls that represent Dragon-Blooded would be much more common, I'd think. Even in Lunar Dominions, unless the Lunar was particularly set against Dragon-Blooded in general rather than the Realm in particular, the Dragon-Blooded are probably the most visible and obvious heroic figures in most of Creation.
