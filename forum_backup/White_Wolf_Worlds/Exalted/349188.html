    Shamanism - the Lunar style of sorcery?

    12-24-2014, 03:04 PM
    http://avatarcomic.net/ExaltedWiki/m...2F_Thaumaturgy

    I'm going back through the first Lunar manual; it spent its storytelling chapter on "barbarians", so the lexicon and chapter explain the terms of "shaman", "chiminage", "taboo" and "god". Basically, a shaman is a client who provides worship or performs work ("chiminage") for a supernatural figure in exchange for knowledge, protection or blessings. Worship often takes the form of prohibited behaviors ("taboo").

    The Shaping Actions required to gather power for necromancy-style spells likely involves blood-sacrifice; necessarily, this makes playing a necromancer extremely different from playing a vanilla sorceror even if they're using the same spells.

    Perhaps Lunars or anyone who pursues shamanism-style sorcery can gather power through performing worship or acts of service for spirits appropriate to the kind of spell one wishes to cast?

    Perhaps this might have a benefit in allowing a shaman to set up multiple spells ahead of time, as "favors" already "owed" to him by various "gods" which he can cash in whenever this or that spell becomes suitable to his situation?

    Maybe there might be taboos which prohibit certain actions that would void the "favor" and erase the power gathered for the spell?

                          My issue with this is that it becomes vey much a relationship to be managed, whereas channeling rituals as I understand them are very much of the 'action in, motes out' variety. You might gather ambient Essence, or perform blood sacrifices, or channel energy from an object but they're what we would have thought of as Simple actions, things you do n9w for immediate result ... even if you might have to stack a few ti charge your spell up.

                          The 'remember when I tended your shrine last week' model lacks this immediacy and introduces a whole bunch of complications. Does it allow shamans to bank a large amount of power from months of shrine-tending? If you really need to cast a spell now can you go into shrine-tending debt? What if either side reneges? Can they even? Does providing this pover effect the God, negatively or otherwise?

                          I've played a lot of spirit-speakers and priests and I tend to think that the relationship between spirits and mortals is rich and dynamic and best played out over the long term in reference to the specific purview and powers of the Gods in question. This is especially true when thauturmagy and the Occult ability are used well (which, certainly, isn't always the case).

                          I don't think the relationship is particularly helped by adding 'generic battery for spells' to the boons Gods can provide.
