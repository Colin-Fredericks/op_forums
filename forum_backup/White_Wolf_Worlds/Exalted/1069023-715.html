                                                And now for a more serious question. Is it possible for a character to change their favored abilities? After the first few sessions as they are working out the kinks in their character, I know I wouldn't allow a player to do so lightly, requiring what I believe psychology calls a Significant Emotional Event where their entire worldview changes. Something like a character has spent their life perfecting their skills with a weapon only to have it stolen and used to kill a loved one. They can't even bear to look at it again, and then they devote their lives to learning the medical skill that might have saved their life. I can imagine half a dozen traumatic experiences off the top of my head that would cause that kind of flip.

                                                How would this be represented mechanically though? Is the player stuck fighting an uphill battle of higher experience costs for non-caste/favored because their exaltation took form based on who they were at that point in time? I don't want to penalize a player for an amazing character moment, but from a strictly rules standpoint they are essentially gaining a 6th favored discount unless I retroactively make them pay the xp difference for all the ranks/charms in whatever skill they are abandoning.

                                                How would you handle it?
