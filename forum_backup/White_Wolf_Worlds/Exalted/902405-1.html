                        They don't necessarily count as 'cool' by any count, but for a possible upcoming game I created an uptight and somewhat unhinged minor Road God whose domain winds through several small villages in the East. He's come to value keeping his road in pristine condition more than letting people use it for its intended purpose, to the point where he nearly throws a tantrum every time someone walks on it in a way he dislikes, and is currently having a petty feud with the little god of a sacred old cherry tree in one of the villages, whose tree constantly sheds petals onto his road and who refuses to apologize for this. Their fight and the inconveniences it is causing the local mortals is intended to serve as a simple and humorous little sidequest between the actual challenges the pcs face and/or just be exists to add a bit of lighthearted flavor.

                                    I've run a couple of campaigns where characters stumble across the little town of Amoree, benevolently ruled by Dayless Dawn, aspect-god of the Unconquered Sun. Dayless comes across a little weird - he doesn't have four arms, for characters who know about that sort of thing, and he's kind of pale and thin for a guy who claims to be a manifestation of the Sun. Still, he and his heavily-robed priest-children rule the town peacefully and well from the old stone temple on the top of the hill, so what's the harm?

                                    A little digging reveals that Amoree was founded after the Contagion, when a wandering band of survivors stumbled onto the temple and found the statue of the Sun inside. No, not a big humanoid Sol like in the corebook - a statue of the Sun. You know, a big crystal orb? Glows somewhere deep inside? It's up there in the temple, the one surrounded by all those priests with what looks suspiciously like radiation burns - the one that, if someone actually competent at Old Realm gets a look at it, clearly says Armory just inside the front door.

                                    ... he's a soulbreaker orb - or, well, the god of soulbreaker orb, who through some combination of focused devotion and lots of time is evolving into something more. Trouble is, whatever else he becomes, he's still the god of a fantasy nuke, and sooner or later that sucker's going to go boom.

                                    This usually leads to some fun discussions. In my current game, Dayless Dawn has begrudgingly worked with "My beloved Chosen" (i.e., the party) to fight off a fae army; as a result, he's now the focus of a growing cult in the area. That's troubling for the party; what's troubling for him, sneaky jerk that he is, is that the more people worship him as a font of virtue, the more he feels the urge to be exactly that.
