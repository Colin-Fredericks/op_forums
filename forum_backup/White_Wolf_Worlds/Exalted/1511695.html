    Artifact: Anima-Weaver

    05-15-2023, 01:11 AM
    New "Minor" Artifact - Infinite Respendence Amulet •••
    Functions as a Collar of Dawn's Cleansing Light. ​By spending 1 mote, the wearer spins a full outfit for themselves. This lasts as long as the Artifact remains attuned, but can be rewoven or dismissed entirely by spending another mote. The clothes are counted as exceptional equipment (Exalted, p. 580) for social rolls where the Exalt’s garb is relevant. When removed from the owner's person, the clothing denatures at the end of the scene.

    Different Magical Materials result in different kinds of clothes - Jade amulets create clothes nearly identical Dragon Graced Raiment (Heirs To The Shogunate p143), while Moonsilver creates seamless silvery garments, for example. Each material often confers minor benefits as well; Orichalcum clothing embroidery might be able to glow at will, Moonsilver might automatically reshape itself around Lunar shapeshifting or Wyld mutations, while Jade clothing might be immune to Anima Flux. These benefits should be minor and/or cosmetic, but each amulet can incorporate the effects of multiple Magical Materials.


    Anima-Weaver
    Adamant Silken Armor, Artifact ••••

    When Aurora's Chosen were put to the sword, Sabok Asna-Touched wept. The Lunar had loved the Aurorals, attributing his survival of the Divine Revolution to their ministrations. Though he accepted that the Muses he had loved were gone, he could not help but create some small memorial to them.

    From the shards of Adamant Hearteater armaments and moonsilver spun beneath the final aurora, Sabok wrought a simple torc. Into it he poured what memories he had been able to scavenge from his Heart's Blood of the Muse's creativity and splendor, filtered through the skittering cunning of his Spirit Shape. When the work was done, he set the Artifact in the entryway of Remembrance, Veiled In Light, and walked away.

    Anima-Weaver has passed to many owners over the years - mainly the Silver Pact, but occasionally falling into Sidereal hands. Though Sabok is long dead, Asna Firstborn is known to show a great deal of interest in current holders - those who "abuse her grand-nieces and nephews" are know to suffer from atrocious bad luck. Pattern Spiders who encounter Anima-Weaver often ask after their "little cousins", and are delighted on the rare occasions that the mote-spiders can respond in kind.

    This torc is comprised of two chestnut-sized spheres of Adamant, connected by a cable of Moonsilver threads. The transition between the two materials is blurred, a gradient bleeding into one-another. When not being worn, the cable is slack and flexible, but stiffens and contracts when donned to rest comfortably against the wearer's throat.

    When spinning new clothing, Anima-Weaver's spheres come alive with dozens -if not hundreds- of tiny flecks of light. Those of especially keen eye can make out the shapes of featureless spiders, casting out innumerable threads of Anima-woven silk.

    Attunement: 4m
    Type: Light (Soak +5, Hardness 4, Mobility Penalty −0)
    Tags: Special, Silent
    Hearthstone Slot(s): 0
    Era: The Winnowing of the Heart-Eaters

    Evocations of Anima-Weaver
    By spending 1 mote, the wearer of Anima-Weaver spins a full outfit for themselves. This lasts as long as the Artifact remains attuned, but can be rewoven or dismissed entirely by spending another mote. These garments are woven from the owner's own Anima, and are limited to the colors normally present in the Exalt's banner. The clothes are counted as exceptional equipment (Exalted, p. 580) for social rolls where the Exalt’s garb is relevant, although the limitation on color choices may conversely lead to penalties (such as wearing black to a wedding). When removed from the owner's person, the clothing denatures in a puff of light at the end of the scene.

    Anima-Weaver's clothing always has the statistics of Silken Armor, and doesn't count as armor for determining their compatibility with Martial Arts.

    Raiment of The Soul
    Cost: —; Mins: Essence 1
    Type: Permanent
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Permanent
    Prerequisites: None

    Anima-Weaver is a fastidious thing, concerned with beauty and unmarred perfection above all. It strives to keep its owner to that same standard, acting at all times like an attuned Collar of Dawn's Cleansing Light.

    Special Activation Rules: As long as the owner is not Dissonant with Adamant, this Evocation awakens at no cost when first attuned.


    Trifles And Fancies
    Cost: 1m; Mins: Essence 1
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: Mute
    Duration: Scene
    Prerequisites: None

    The mote-spiders create a small object of glowing silk that could easily be pulled from a pocket -ribbons, short or thin lengths of rope, handkerchiefs- that lasts until the scene ends. While limited to simple objects composed entirely of silk, each one is as durable as real silk. These items are not as refined as Anima-Weaver's clothing, and as such cast a light comparable to a candle, in a hue matching the owner's Anima Banner. At the end of the scene, these baubles vanish in a puff.

    A repurchase at Essence 2 allows this Evocation to create more durable constructs. Creations can be much larger -50 feet of sturdy rope, for example- or more complex -a puppet or doll- but not both. All items are treated as if they were made from Hell-silk, and are exceptional equipment for relevant tasks. They still unravel at the end of the scene.


    Helping Hands Weave
    Cost: 5m, 1wp; Mins: Essence 2
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: Mute
    Duration: Scene
    Prerequisites: Trifles And Fancies (x2)

    The helpful mote-spiders of Anima-Weaver surround two of their number and hurl them into Creation on a bridge of their owner's will, weaving soft-yet-firm flesh of glowing silk around each as they do. The two become an extra pair of hands -each with eight unsettling fingers- that float gracefully around their owner, emulating the "Extra Limbs" mutation. They are dexterous but weak, and cannot be used for combat actions (punching, stabbing, grappling, etc). However, they reduce the flurry penalty for one action in a given flurry to 0 rather than -1, and can even undertake esoteric actions like gathering Sorcerous Motes.

    Like creations of Trifles and Fancies, these hands emit a constant glow on-par with a brignt candle. When the scene ends the silk hands unravel, and two tiny sparks of light leap back into Anima-Weaver. Asna Firstborn is known to brook great umbrage towards any foe who destroys the gloves before the Charm ends... and even greater towards the owner that allowed them to be harmed so.


    Deft Weaver Tricks
    Cost: 5m, 1wp; Mins: Essence 2
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Scene
    Prerequisites: Raiment of The Soul

    By murmuring a reverant prayer to the mote-spiders dwelling within Anima-Weaver, the wearer convinces them to spin motes into novel designs. The wearer chooses an Exalt type they and the mote-spiders have seen before - for the rest of the scene, their Anima Banner appears to be of the same Caste. This only covers visual manifestations - while they might be surrounded by flames reminiscent of a Fire Aspect, they do not have Anima Markings or Anima Flux. Any Iconic manifestations the Exalt has appear as normal, simply modified through the lense of their chosen Caste.

    Aurorals are a valid choice, should anyone think to try, but this carries justifiable risk of being mistaken for a Hearteater.

    Additionally, Anima-Weaver's cloths are no longer limited to the colors of the owner's Anima Banner.


    Auroral Wave Unfurling
    Cost: 1a; Mins: Essence 2 Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisites: Deft Weaver Tricks

    Anima-Weaver dispenses with subtlety, thrusting out a burst of kaleidoscopic light. All Trival characters are blinded for the rest of the Scene, while Non-Trival characters must succeed on a contested (Resistance + Awareness) roll against the Exalt's (Essence + Presence) to avoid blinding.


    Ripples Across The Heavens
    Cost: 7m, 1wp; Mins: Essence 2
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Indefinite
    Prerequisites: Auroral Wave Unfurling

    This Evocation allows the Exalt to make a Stealth attempt even if there is no cover available—so long as she is standing still or moving slowly, she blends perfectly into her surroundings. If used in conjunction with Auroral Wave Unfurling, her initial Stealth attempt is automatically successful. While this Evocation is in effect, her Stealth attempts garner (Essence) automatic successes.

    Extreme movement renders her temporarily visible. Any time she attacks or changes range bands, she can be seen and targeted by opponents until her next turn in which she doesn’t. If she is struck by a withering or decisive attack, the Evocation ends.


    Clothed In The Light
    Cost: 10m, 1wp; Mins: Essence 3
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Scene
    Prerequisites: Deft Weaver Tricks

    Anima-Weaver draws the entirely of the Exalt's Anima Banner through itself, refracting and scattering the soul's radiance. Their clothing seems to bleed into the banner, fraying into waves of light at the edges. For the rest of the scene, the wearer's mundane features, Exaltation type, and all distinctive Anima Banner manifestations are obscured by a kaleidoscopic aurora. Its obvious that they are *an* Exalt, but not what kind or who they are. Track Anima gain and loss normally for the purposes of Charm costs, but otherwise treat the wearer's Anima as being at Bonfire-level for the rest of the scene


    Masterworks of Thread and Light
    Cost: 1/5/10m, 1wp; Mins: Essence 3
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: Mute
    Duration: Indefinite
    Prerequisites: Clothed In The Light, Helping Hands Weave

    Urging the mote-spiders to a fever-pitch of activity with a burst of Essence, Anima-Weaver spills forth a dense construct of silk. This quickly resolves itself into a scintilating spider, which happily nestles itself on its owner's shoulder. Through gaps in the silk hundred of mote-spiders can be seen scurrying about, pulling at strings to explore the wider world. This construct assists its owner in any way it can, but is non-violent by nature and fares poorly in combat. Treat it as a three-dot Familiar

    The size of the mote-spider construct depends on how many motes are committed - 1 mote creates a Miniscule spider, 5 motes creates a Tiny spider, and 10 motes creates a dog-sized spider. This commitment can be changed to any level as a Reflexive action, shrinking or growing the spider accordingly.

    The construct acts as an aggregate of the mote-spiders within it - they are knowledgeable about artwork, weaving, and idealized memories of pre-corruption Hearteaters, but naively curious about most everything else. While individual construct instances have become quite intelligent over time, any progress seems to be lost when a new owner takes possession of the Artifact.

    If Asna Firstborn's ire is roused by the harming of Helping Hands Weave gloves, her fury at the destruction of a Mote-spider Mobile Exploration Platform is nearly incandescent.

    Mote-spider Mobile Exploration Platform
    Essence: 2; Willpower: 6; Join Battle: 7 dice Personal: 70
    Health Levels: −0/−1/−2/−4/Incap.

    Actions:
    Climb: 10 dice;
    Debate Art: 9 dice;
    Feats of Strength: 7 dice (can attempt Strength 3 feats);
    Read Intentions: 4 dice;
    Senses: 9 dice;
    Social Influence: 4 dice;
    Stealth: 6 dice;
    Auroral Lore: 7 dice;
    Weaving: 12 dice
    Spin Silk: 12 Dice

    Appearance 4, Resolve 3, Guile 1

    Combat
    Attack (Bite): 1 dice (Damage 3B)
    Attack (Grapple): 6 dice (4 dice to control)
    Combat Movement: 10 dice
    Evasion: 5; Parry: 2
    Soak/Hardness: 3/2

    Intimacies
    Defining Principle: The World Is Beautiful, It Just Needs Mending Now And Then
    Defining Tie: Our Owner (Loyal Affection)
    Major Tie: Woven Art (Appreciation)
    Major Tie: Hearteaters (Fearful Confusion)
    Major Tie: Aurorals (Whistful Nostalgia)
    Minor Tie: Pattern Spiders (Filial Worship)

    Merits
    Ever-Present Memory: The mote-spiders within the Platform are always aware of the presence of a Hearteater, Hearteater bones, an Auroral, or Auroral Demesne present in the Scene, though not their exact location. Pawns currently being possessed by a Hearteater count as the Hearteater for this purpose.

    Spun of Light: the Platform and its constituent explorers are immune to poison and disease, as well as crippling effects that target organs, nerves, or the like. They don’t need to eat, drink, sleep, or breathe.

    Wallcrawling: the Platform can climb up walls and ceilings.

    Woven Into The World: while individual mote-spider weavings are still temporary, some anomaly in the existence of the Platfom allows it to spin lasting threads of light. Treat this as normal silk for all purposes, save that it glows with aurora-patterns.

    Offensive Charms
    Silken Light Snare (5m; Supplemental; Until the clinch ends; Essence 2): When the Platform restrains a grappled enemy, it wraps him in webbing. The Platform can take other non-reflexive actions while grappling and suffer no Defense penalties for grappling. The opponent’s penalties to attacks increase by one. This can’t be used on the turn the Platform makes the grapple gambit.

    Web of Warning Lights (10m; Simple; One scene, Essence 2): The Platform spins a web out to short range, which is difficult terrain for other characters. While within the web, the Platform doubles 9s on movement actions and on hearing-based Senses rolls against others within the web.

    Miscellaneous Charms
    Hurry Home (10m, 1wp; Simple; Instant): The Platform collapses in on itself on its next turn, reweaving at Anima-Weaver.

    Lanterns Floating Aloft (5m, 1wp; Simple; Instant): By directly touching an Exalt, the Platform can convert any Anima levels the target has into fist-sized orbs. These orbs float merrily along behind the construct, shedding source-appropriate light like a lantern. Anyone who grabs one of these lanterns can consume it in place of an Anima Level cost for their Charms, regardless of the original source. Lanterns fade after around a day if not used.

    Auroral Silk (7m; Supplemental; Instant; Essence 1): For seven motes, the Platform may supplement a Spin Silk roll to create Auroral-silk instead, a lightweight material with the strength of a steel cable. Each supplemented Spin Silk action creates enough Auroral-silk to make one square yard of fabric. Treat this as Hell-Silk for all mechanical purposes.
