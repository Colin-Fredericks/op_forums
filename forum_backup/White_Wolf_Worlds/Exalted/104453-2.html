                    Originally posted by Godjaw View Post

                    He means physicality in a real visceral way, just in a violence capacity. No illusions happening, that fear is very real as if that monster coming to eat your head than was once your mistress.
                    The problem is that this doesn't really work outside of physical Charms, which we have already established are the predominant Charm type for Lunar Exalted. Cool, I can reorganize my brain to be smarter-er!... But how does that actually get applied in the context of design? Similarly, are we just supposed to turn Lunar social Charms into flesh-sculpting and pheromones? Because that's interesting for maybe five Charms, and then you need to think up something else.

                    Lunars have a lot more space to grow in if you take it from outside the physical space- illusion, mystery, madness, rage, chaos, insight and prophecy are all things traditionally associated with the moon, most of which don't fit neatly into a "physical" theme and most of which I'd like to see Lunars explore more.

                        Hmmm, primal gods...

                        Sekhmet, lioness-headed Egyptian god of war, fire and vengeance, Before Whom Evil Trembles, Mistress of Dread, Lady of Slaughter, She Who Mauls, Ra's selected source of retribution against treacherous humanity who then had to be rendered stone drink when her blood lust raged out of control and threatened to destroy all humans (although she's a friendly and placid drunk).

                        I've also got something of a mind towards the Morrigan, shapeshifting haunter of battlefields, crafty and a bitter fighter, contributes pursuit, subduing and destruction to war, who sought to torment Cú Chulainn in the shape of eel and crow and wolf and cow, a sexual creature who fought the enemy of gods by taking the blood of his heart and the kidneys of his valour and depositing handfuls of them in the river.

                        I kind of think a god might be "primal" when it appears to be a rather visceral, if not violent, creature, ones immersed in flesh and the moment, ones who seem to lack a lot of the pretensions of godlyness, possibly even to the point of being subversions of their native mythology, viewed as fearsome beings.
