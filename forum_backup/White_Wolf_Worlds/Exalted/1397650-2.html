Forms that I'd rather not use over Crane until my charge is high and cheese out the opponent, an RNG based attack that Snake has far more reliably covered, a psuedo-decisive that is good but relies on RNG from the previous charm to fire off, a rush charm that feeds off the dice blown if I lost (Vs snake which already attacks out to medium range already), an AoE that relies on rush, and Iron Lotus which is useful but vs Snake which can paralyze an arm for the scene then it's somewhat questionable. You get some things for investing in Steel Devil, but ultimately little compared to other areas.
