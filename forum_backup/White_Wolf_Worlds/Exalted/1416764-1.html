                                        Okay, so the House of Black Water. How do you get black water? Well either the water itself has to be really murky, or inked somehow, or it needs to be really deep, or in a really dark place. So let's mix those last two here.



                                        The demense which was to become the House of Black Waters was a massive rocky outcropping, cleft nearly in two, in the Incas Valley. In the middle of this crevasse lay a lake, a lake so deep and so dark that many still affirm that it has truly no bottom at all. This opinion is reenforced by the unique manse that caps it. Starting at the shore, there is a small cluster of buildings that house minor guards, some groundskeeping staff, and unwanted-guest rooms.

                                        Coming out into the lake from this small village is bridge, long, wide, and made of immaculately fitted wood. Thousands of planks long, and on each side flanked by rows of dim lanterns it represents the only terrestrial path to the House of Black Waters. Either constructed on a foundation deep in the dark lake, or else entirely floating on the surface of the water, rises a mansion of imperial stature and proportion.

                                        The water-level square on the perimeter of the manse is covered with greenery, gardens, lawns, and gazebos. Many of which are connected to their own little dock where sleek and beautiful boats are moored. The house uses them for pleasure and training out further in open water. Terraced up from this are several other levels replete with various verandas and balconies, and at the very top some high towers.

                                        This is only half of the manse, however, the mask over the face. The true depths of its character lie below the water's surface. Through hidden passages in the manse, past guards and wards, there lies the hidden heart of Iselsi. They're far too experienced to keep all of their most sensitive information there, far more of it is distributed across the globe, but in hidden vaults in the depths lie weapons, and coded information that form the dagger in the cloak.

                                        Of course the number of people in the manse who even know if the existence of those places is small indeed, and House Iselsi has spent decades of work and hundreds of talents of jade making special modifications. Much of that spent on ironwood.


                                        Scene 1 Foyer to Elder

                                        This may not necessarily be the foyer, but it's whatever room the players show up at first. This is under the assumption that they get to the manse without incident. If they passed through the gatehouse area the defenses there are not enough to warrant fighting. The players burst into the manse to find nothing but fleeing servants and terrified functionaries. Some with more stern resolve have the presence of mind to surrender to this assault.

                                        One has been conditioned to offer to bring the group to the elder of the house in exchange for her life, which she will attempt to bargain for with a roll of 8 dice if she's not taken on her offer. This is not a lie, or a witting plant, it's a legitimate offer, she simply has had her intimacies manipulated to value her life more than the household she serves, and given false information about where the elder is. She leads them deeper into the manse, through the narrow wood-paneled servant backways, quieting those she comes across as part of the deal.

                                        She says that the elder is behind one final door, one which is made of an ironwood laminant and she knows it's locked. A Dex + Larceny roll of difficulty 4, or a Feat of Demolition Strength 5 Difficulty 8 will also open it.

                                        Behind the door sits the figure of the house elder. In reality this is a Sculpted Seafoam Eidolon, and a trap. She nods to the players and invites them in to discuss terms of surrender, and once everyone is safely inside the room hits a lever with her foot. This causes the whole room to disconnect from the superstructure of the manse and plummet onto a hard stone floor two range bands down. Appropriate athletics rolls and stunts can reduce this damage.

                                        Even if they manage to spot the trap and avoid it, they know they have not found their elder, and throughout the manse above/around them they here the bustling of movement.


                                        Scene 2 Getting Out From Behind the Mask

                                        It's become obvious at this point that not all here will be as it seems, and nobody can be trusted. Even worse, if the players attempt to go back the way they came, they find that the walls have shifted, the path no longer exists. Attempting to smash through the wall where once there was a door proves difficult, as just under the surface of any wood panel is yet more ironwood, same difficulty to smash through as the door, and doing so isn't much help as the hallway that was there now goes perpendicular to the way it was.

                                        Navigating out of this maze is either an extended Perception + Investigation roll, difficulty 3, goal number 10, terminus 3 task, or three consecutive wall bashes must be made. Failure at either of these indicates the manse has rearranged itself and placed the characters at the start of a new maze. Freedom lets the players break back out into the public areas of the manse.


                                        Scene 3 Apartments Down Below

                                        The players may now proceed to investigate the manse proper, but find only unhelpful servants just as confused as ever. At some point though they find something, either some discarded scrap of information or see somebody just in time to watch them slip into a hidden door in the wall, behind which is a long spiral staircase downward. Whatever information it is, the players are lead to believe that the real treasure is far down below.

                                        Getting down to the bottom of the public-facing manse is easy, and bashing through the floor another ironwood difficulty. Either that or a difficulty 4 Perception + Investigation roll to find a hidden passage opening.

                                        Once down there they find the structure they're in changed. Now granite stone replaces wood paneling, and darkness pervades every hallway and room. They pass some rooms with simple but comfortable living quarters, as well as some storage rooms for food, first aid supplies, and weapons. However the only way forward seems to be a deep pool of dark water on the other side of the apartment level. A difficulty 2 Perception + Investigation check reveals that indeed this way does have a lot of foot traffic.


                                        Any attempt to breach the under-manse from the outside is a difficulty 15 Strength 10+ feat of strength, and will lead the characters to a random room except the final one.


                                        Scene 4 Teeth of the Deep

                                        With no other option than to press forward, the characters descend into the water, which glows faintly. Still dim enough to impose a -2 penalty on sight based actions. The pool leads deep down, for a long time, and then opening into a larger cavern. Three range bands forward is a moon pool, leading back up to the surface at the lower level. Unfortunately, they're not the only ones down here. They are ambushed as they cross by a group of 4 Benthic Knifetooth's, but that's not all. In the second round of combat a light flickers into view, glowing runes covering an armored carapace. It is a captured legendary Armored Terror. This terror has a spiritual heritage and access to the latent abilities and charms of its statline. It also blockades the moon pool exit.

                                        The players may either attempt to escape or battle the creatures, but keep in mind that under duress the characters can only stay under water for Stamina x 2 rounds before drowning. The fish are instinctively aware of this facet of prey, and often attempt grapples to keep underwater.


                                        Scene 5 Rainbow Stairs

                                        The players then exit the moon pool to find themselves in front of two doors, one Red, the other Violet. The inscription above reads that essence is required to open these doors. A suitably epic feat of demolition, or a mote of essence may do the trick. Also a small riddle, "Water turning hard to soft, and bread from soft to hard, this is how we go" as a hint to start cold and go warm on the colour spectrum.

                                        Whichever one the characters attempt to open, they find a staircase leading down, and to another landing with another set of doors. If they picked red, this one has a red and orange door, if they picked violet a violet and blue one.

                                        Going down the same coloured door as before leads them back to the landing they first ended up on. If they progress through the correct doors in the colours of the rainbow, either backwards or forwards, they end up in a new hallway. If they didn't take the riddles advice of going cold to hot the final door breaks its hinges and flings itself towards them. Hazard of Dex + Dodge difficulty 4, damage 4B.


                                        Scene 6 Riddle Door

                                        The hallway next leads curving back on itself as it slopes down, and ends in a door with a face. It stops the players and says it doesn't recognize them, but no matter, for it was designed to let clever people past. It has four riddles, which it says answering them will let them trough. Pick any four from this website.

                                        https://parade.com/947956/parade/riddles/

                                        He asks them one at a time, humming at their answers, but not accepting them until they confirm it is their final answer. A Read Intentions roll against his guile of 5 allows a player to see if their guess is correct, with a fail telling them that it is. Regardless when he splits open to admit them he also unleashes a cloud of noxious gas, difficulty (1 + Number of failed riddes) damage (2 + Number of failed riddles)L.


                                        Scene 7 Nightmare Maze

                                        Another moon pool, and more tracks leading into it. This time though the water is murky, black even with the light of arcane magic or anima, full of some inky pollutant. The reverse side of the face door has another riddle, "Mountains but no trees, rivers but no fish, cities but no people, what am I?" if they guess a map correctly it produces one of the maze they are about to encounter.

                                        The pool doesn't just lead to an underwater room, but an underwater maze. An extended Perception + Survival roll of difficulty 3, goal number 10 to get through. There is no terminus, but the interval is thirty seconds and people drown after Stamina minutes under water. Having the map reduces the difficulty by 1, as do charms that allow sight without seeing.


                                        Scene 8 The Final Battle

                                        At last at the end of the long chain of guarding puzzles and traps, the players face off against the house Elder, having had plenty of time and warning to prepare with her dragonblooded in the hearthroom. The players exit into another moon pool in the center of a round room. This room has all manner of devices, books, scrolls, and even some creatures in cages and tanks adorned around it. On another level, a gallery above are more treasures. Also stationed there are a pair of archers, having both Short range from the pool from above and Heavy Cover due to the granite railings.

                                        On the ground outside the pool is the House Elder and one of her best shikari fighters. They mean to end the players there. Or if they want to they can attempt to flee and escape. Successfully beating them gives them access to the hearthstone room, and many Iselsi secrets, although, of course, not all, and not even most.







                                        Okay, that was a little janky but normally I have more time to come up with these and think about them.

                                                        Thinking about it more I can suggest some other things for my version of the House of Black Waters.

                                                        Originally I didn't put a scene at the gatehouse because I figured the PCs would just Stormwind Rider over the lake if they're assaulting it. Then I thought that in a world with sorcerers, air elementals, flying carpets, Lunar Exalted, airships, agata, and flying whales from the Wyld, there's little chance that a major faction stronghold HQ is just defenseless from air attacks.

                                                        So something like this:


                                                        Tornadoes and Sharks <- WIP name, may be a better way to fuse them.

                                                        If an assailant attempts to fly over the black water lake, it starts to swirl and foam beneath them, quickly sending twisting jets of water into the air. Fliers must then engage in an extended roll, using whatever stats are appropriate, for example Sail + Wits if flying an airship. The roll has a difficulty of 3, and a goal number of 10, advancement allowing the characters to move across the lake. Failure ends in them being dashed down to the water by one of the water spouts.

                                                        Once in the water the characters are assaulted by a school of sharks. If the sharks are defeated the characters may either retake to the air or attempt to swim to the house, although depending on how far along the extended roll they were this may take some time. Simple sharks may also be too mundane for a water manse's watery defenses.


                                                        Suspicious Gateguard

                                                        If the players attempt to just walk in they are stopped at the gate by a mortal with the stats of an Elite Bodyguard. He's got the following intimacies

                                                        Defining Fellow Guards (Loyalty)
                                                        Major House Iselsi (Loyalty)
                                                        Major Husband (Love)
                                                        Major Immaculate Order (Faith)
                                                        Minor Things that live in the black waters (Fear)


                                                        If he can be convinced that the PCs have legit business there in these times of open war then they will be let through, otherwise he assaults them with his fellow guards. He commands a size 1 battlegroup of elite bodyguards with elite drill while a sniper takes shots from an elevated position in cover. For the sniper use the stats of a horse archer with +2 bow damage representing a longbow and in heavy cover.

                                                        Note that this is only really worth busting out the dice for if the players have a tendency to gleefully dunk on mortals with much more effort than is strictly necessary.
