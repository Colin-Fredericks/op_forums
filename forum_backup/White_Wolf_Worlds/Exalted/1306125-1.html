            Originally posted by Epimetheus View Post

            The problem with that basically means that a non-fire dragon dodger has a subpar build and is screwed out of effects not just within the defense tree but also the attack tree.             I'm not actually sure if that's true, in all cases anyway. Again though, I haven't used it in a full campaign so I'm not 100% sure. For example it's true that if you're in Fire Aura you have access to Flickering Candle Meditation and Ember and Smoke Misdirection, which are both decent charms. You don't have access to Swaying Grass Elusion though, which is the only dodge charm that boosts your evasion cap. If you want to shed your Wood Aura for it it actually becomes twice as cheap as the Solar version of the same charm.

            So yes it is true that if you're a wood dragon dodger who likes to sit in wood aura you can't use Swaying Grass Elusion, and then counter with Unassailable Body of Fire. That's just, in theory anyway, the difference between how a wood dragon dodges and a fire dragon dodges. Or if you didn't want Swaying Grass you could still use Heat of Battle, Ebbing Tide, Flickering Candle, Hopping Firecracker, Virtious Negation and Bonfire Shadow Evasion. It's not strictly worse, but it is different, and it's a lot different from Solars and Lunars and stuff who can just combo anything they have.

            Also not that it matters for most games but at Essence 5 Elusive Dragon God Dispersion works sfor any element, and changes Unassailable Body of Fire and Hopping Firecracker to whatever element you want.
