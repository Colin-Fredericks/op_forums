          Joyous Lark is a Night Caste sorceress. Not scholarly by nature, she came by her affinity for fire magic the same way she comes by most of her valuables - she stole it. Deep within the manse of an Ifrit Lord, she found something that opened her eyes to the numinous power of flame - and her daring infiltration won her Solar exaltation.

          Joyous Lark is not very tall and has a slim, athletic build, with pale olive skin, long black hair, and blue eyes. Her expression is usually impish, gleeful, or sly. She was raised as a performer, and even before her exaltation she was a practitioner of the art of fire twirling. She therefore favours clothing that she can move freely in, and when she is not prowling rooftops or stealing whatever isn't nailed down, she prefers festive colours, particularly red, with accents of black and yellow/gold. She usually wears one or more sashes in which she conceals lockpicks, throwing knives, and other essentials for a girl's night out. Her hair is usually in a high ponytail or pigtails, all the better to whip around dramatically when she is twirling fire-sticks all over the place or jumping from rooftop to rooftop. While her "day job" as a dancer is a cover for her rampant criminality, she does find a genuine joy and fulfillment in her performances.
