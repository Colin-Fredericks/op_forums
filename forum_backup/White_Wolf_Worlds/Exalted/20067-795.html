                    Originally posted by Jefepato View Post
                    (I'm still a little shocked he doesn't get Immaculate holidays though.)
                    The Immaculate calendar is a system by which the Sidereal Exalted utilize the religious system they manage to bargain with god's for cooperation with their endeavours in and out of the immediate domain of that religion and to keep them in line.

                    Bargaining with the Sun is unnecessary. Sure didn't we have a Usurpation in the first place when we noticed that he'd stopped being very engaged with the world and had to figure out why? Days on the calendar given to him are days we're not giving to a god who is an actual concern. The believers can easily rationalize this as him being so lofty and grand and distant that he doesn't require or much notice worship.

                    As a reason behind the writing of it, I think it's simply a matter of Sun worship having never really been a part of the Immaculates, and it's kind of aesthetically weird, especially if a point is being made of him having withdrawn from the world. Sun worship being lapsed gives another thing to change in play as a signifier that the Solar Exalted have returned.
