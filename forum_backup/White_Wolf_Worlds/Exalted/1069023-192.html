                                            Originally posted by The Lidless Eye That Sees View Post
                                            Question about the liminals:

                                            Assuming that they have the terrestrial keyword with MA and are the worst at evocations, what are the benefits of playing one?
                                            I disagree with the premise of this question, that the value of playing a certain type of character is tied to their power. Otherwise, why would anyone bother playing a Dragon-Blood, the weakest of the Exalted, when they could play a Solar?

                                            Why might you want to play a Liminal? To play out the unique character arc of a created being, wrestling with the fundamental question of your humanity and identity and discovering, or deciding, exactly who you are. To hunt the dead in service to a mysterious patron. To draw power from sources no other Chosen can, whether it's the severed limb of a Dynast still clutching the hilt of his daiklave or an eye plucked from a seer's socket. To pursue your foes implacably, even as they deliver killing blow after killing blow, rising literally from the ashes. To stitch half a dozen severed arms onto your body and take up a weapon in each of them. You know. That kind of stuff.
