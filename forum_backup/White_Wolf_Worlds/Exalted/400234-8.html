        Originally posted by Fata-Ku View Post
        because Snake-Head Defence is charm-shapeshifting... I'm guessing that its temporary nature offsets that, but why is that different for Hybrid Body Rearrangement?

        Is it because SHD is goal-focused while HBR is an all-purpose "have this for a while"?
        That may very well be!

        Snake Head Defense as I envision it is very goal/effect oriented; it creates an environmental hazard for any enemies who get too close to you, but the specific form the hazard takes (while always bestial) isn't important. Because it's not important, it can be fluffed as anything appropriate (or alien) to your form.

        Hybrid Body Rearrangement focuses more on the means; you increase soak by growing armor, you fly by growing wings, you strike more often by growing arms. It places the emphasis on the form it provides, which detracts from the importance of the form you are wearing.


        There's also the fact that Hybrid Body Rearrangement is a toolkit Charm that steals focus from other Charms.

        We've already mentioned Armor-Forming Technique as an alternative to HBR's ability to grow armor. That effect is ALL Armor-Forming Technique does.

        Let us say that Lunars have a Charm that allow them to fly without wings, by standing and running on thin air or something. That's ALL the Charm does.

        Snake Head Defense only surrounds the Lunar with a protective hazard, and doesn't do anything else.


        You could use forms that fly, forms that bear natural armor, or forms that have natural protective hazards (always-on-fire, electrified, clinging vapors of poisonous breath)... But the Charms are enhancing these forms, rather than making them something they're not.

        That's how it feels to me.
