    Originally posted by Accelerator View Post
    During the First Age when there was a lot, lot more information hanging around, and there was an impromptu messaging boards, there were entire internet forums where people discussed sorcery. People threw around ideas for sorcerous workings, concepts for spells, links to tutorial videos laden with training charms, There was a monthly competition where people tried to think up new spell designs. There was also a forum where people talked about demons, what they could do, their various predilections, and also made new demon designs. The forums were backed up in an archive, somewhere within the Threshold.

    The archive contains a snapshot into the thoughts and culture of the First age... and is also a priceless archive on the knowledge of sorcery. Anyone within it not only gains extensive knowledge of sorcerous, geomantic, and magitech principles of the first age, as well as extensive knowledge of demons, but they also gain many working demon and golem designs that can be created and used in the first age.
    The problem with unregulated information to the extent of what we have in modern times is that this will contain a lot of garbage information that will be regarded as the wisdom of the ancients. This could be part of the fun, but I am unsure of your intent.

                        All Exalted have the lifespan of Dragon-Blooded or somesuch, ranging from 200 to 350 years at most. However, just as many powerful Dragonblooded in the Age of Sorrows have found means to extend their lifespans, so too have many Lunars and Sidereals (and Solars of the First Age).

                        Back in the day it was pretty easy to just like, eat some Peaches of Immortality every once in a while. There are still a handful of Lunars and Sidereals from the First Age still kicking after like two thousand years, and they probably use similar methods. I'm sure there are rumors that Raksi bathes in pools of virgin's blood or that Ma Ha Suchi steals the lifespans of any mortals he devours, but these are the fictions of superstitious fools. Probably.

                        My reasoning for this change is that it creates less of a weird dichotomy between Celestial and Terrestrial Exalts, but it leaves open the possibility for really long-lived Exalts to still be kicking.

                            Personal headcanon: You can build manses and reroute geomancy using mortal efforts alone. It's expensive, but doable. What this means is that in more complex cultures where there's lots of division of labour, the local god-king/ council of elders/ shaman can do some pretty impressive geomancy and manse construction on their own (including Sandact's own manse rules). That means that when the local pharaoph creates gigantic monuments, statues, and works, sometimes he's rerouting geomancy and constructing manses as a public work program. This means that low-level, blink and you'll miss it magic is something that can be seen. A local D&D adventurer who throws away canteens of infinite water won't notice, but anyone who's focused and careful to look can find places where he can buy Lembdas bread.

                                        Re: Mortals and Geomancy

                                        If I may chime in -

                                        I don't think it would be a stretch to say that Creation's essence flows across the world and gathers in places of power, similar to the concept ley lines, nexuses, and fonts of power. While mortals cannot perceive of such things directly, the effects and influence of such power becomes plain to those who know the signs. People in our world have been building things on occult principles for millennium, I don't see how that would be different in a world where the magic actually works.

                                        As for 3e Thaumaturgy, I don't see a conflict with this headcannon, but maybe with the terminology. 3e Thaumaturgy seems to be closer to "miracles" - unique blessing that are difficult to replicate. Perhaps calling them as such and leaving the term "Thaumaturgy" to represent manipulating the inherent mystic principles of Creation (a function of Occult and occasionally Craft or Lore) would resolve the conflict.

                                                Your headcanon and my headcanon line up pretty well. I see no reason why this wouldn't be possible. Essence permeates everything, so the world should be teaming with magical plants, animals, locations, and weather. Manipulating and using that essence can include "strange folk magic", but it also includes knowing where to place a shrine to bring fertility to your village, knowing which berries to brew into a balm against the cold, and knowing how to mix a kohl that wards against the evil eye when worn. Mechanically I make use of Talismans (page 580, Ex3) for many of these minor magics.
