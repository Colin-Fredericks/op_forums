    [Ex3] Lunars moving forward

    10-23-2015, 03:57 PM
    I like what the lunars have become from the description in Ex3 book. I love it! They're big! They're strong! They're angry!

    Big difference is that they've removed the Solar bond. Not just removed it, but torn it free! Unsurprisingly the lunars do not want it back, and nor do I!

    However, the wrirteup left some things unsaid (as well it should not being the lunar book) and I'd like to take the opportunity to discuss them and the possibilities we now have.

    First, they're the shapshifting masters. Let's take that to the logical extreme. Why should a master of his or her own form be limited to only that of the animal they kill? Why can a lunar who has killed multiple individuals of a species not be able to mix and match traits from individuals? Why can't they add wings to a shape if they want? Oh wait, they totally can with the right knacks. Okay, why then can't they remove these features? Like from a wyld mutant form? or... from their own form? What I'm proposing is that the lunars were meant to be the perfect foil to the wyld and wyld-born threats from inception. When the gods were creating the chosen in the first place surely they knew that the wyld was a legitimate threat and that shape shifting or wyld-like attacks were a thing. It makes sense that they would plan for these sorts of threats with an exalt that deals with that stuff. So basically I'm saying that the lunars should not need tattoos to be immune to wyld mutation and shaping. This makes sense in game. Like I said before, the lunars are masters of modifying their own forms. It is absolutely inconceivable that the wyld can simply override that ability. therefore, make it so that the lunar can buy charms and knacks that make them immune to shaping stuff, or make it bend to their will. Someone tries to turn you into a statue? Surprise! Instead all you've done is give me an armored carapace! Or nothing happens! or I turn into a statue and back again, only this time angrier and with more power! The options are endless when you consider lunars as an exalt made to master the wyld and its energies rather than an exalt that's being poisoned by it and slowly going insane in mind and form.

    Second, moonsilver tattoos. What to do with them? They're a really cool thematic effect and I'd hate to lose them. On the other hand I'd hate to see them retain the 'get these or die' aspect that lunars have right now. Make moonsilver tattoos additive rather than mandatory! My first point feeds into this. If you allow lunars to build charms or knacks that allow them to be masters of the wyld and immune to the harmful effects of travelling it rather than building the protection into their skin, you make things a lot better. It also frees up the thematics of the tattoos for other stuff. Traditionally the tattoos fix the caste of the lunar in question and that makes a certain amount of sense. However, I'm not convinced we need this mechanic. If they don't have the tattoos they're at the mercy of the moon cycle rather than their own agency. I'd far prefer to have a changing caste as a viable, character driven option, but I'll talk about that in a bit. Moonsilver tattoos could be used as a silver pact power-boost type thing. If you have the silver pact as a faction focused on the destruction of the realm and the usurping dragon-blood and traitorous Sidereal then perhaps the moonsilver tattoos are primarily to make a lunar more resistant to charms use. Make the silver pact into a force that has reshaped themselves so that they can deal unrelenting destruction against other exalts! I envision the lunars as a little bit like Batman. You defeat him with surprise but if he has a chance to shape himself to the task then there will be no stopping him.

    Third, lunar castes and fixing them in place. I feel that the idea of a lunar trading between castes has a lot of merit. Being forced to change castes is not terribly good for an exalt with their own agency. However you can't just have a lunar change at will as that would certainly end up as game-breaking. I propose this: by default the lunar caste is set by the dot total in their caste attributes/# of charms. This allows a lunar to shift their focus over time while not being game breaking. Make a lunar choose a caste at character creation to represent their current aptitudes. If the player wants to shift caste they'd still have to buy non-caste charms and attributes at more expensive levels until they swap over to the new caste. Allows the lunar to be flexible. Hell, you could have a character ride on the raggety edge bewtween castes, but they'd be spending a ton more xp on keeping their non-caste stuff level with their caste stuff. In addition you could add in that moonsilver tattoos do fix your caste if you want the caste power while still specializing elsewhere. This even explains Ma-Ha-Suchi a bit. Perhaps he started as a changing moon caste all roses and charm and then ended up a full moon to reflect his nature after the usurpation. Lunars are the most flexible of the exalts after all.

    Fourth, Chimersim. Still kinda feels like a dirty word right? I don't think it has to be! What if, instead of being poisoned by the wyld, chimeras are what you get when you give a person unbelievable power and shapeshifting ability then exile him to the borders of reality, have everyone hunt him as a monster, has his entire family turn against him, and then add the realities of the unprepared wandering through the wyld. Most exalted would be able to deal with this. most wouldn't worry about it. But would they all? In short, chimeras are lunar exalts who went mad and simply realized that there really wasn't any limit to shapeshifting. They're something all lunars could be if they strove for it, but who in their right mind would want to? this fits with Ma-Ha-Suchi very well. Mr. goat-wolf himself simply went over the edge from the usurpation and everything else. Hell, his actions sure seem like those of a madman. This would create a rife storytelling opportunity as the threat of chimerism is still there, but it's not from the wyld. It's you. What would you do to avoid becoming a chimera? What would you stoop to to save your family? What would you do if you lost them anyway? Classic faustian type thing. great power at a cost of losing your humanity and ultimately your sanity. This has the upshot of never making a PC unplayable as well. After all, the Solars went mad in the first age. If the lunars went mad as well, what form would it take? I think this is a viable one. Imagine a lunar pushing their shapeshiting powers in an effort to transcend limits on them. Until the shape part of shapeshifting gives way entirely to make room for the shifting.

    tl/dr: Lunars should be able to be masters of the wyld as exalted. IE They have charms that allow them to use the wyld energies for their benefit. Not Wyld-shaping, but rather wyld-using charms. Make Moonsilver tattoos a thing that the silver pact uses to make them more effective against exalted opponents rather than an absolute requirement for non-crazy lunars. Could be something else, but remove the mandatory aspect of them. Allow lunars to reshape themselves over time even up to the point of letting them shift their caste if they work towards it. Out of all exalts it makes sense for lunars to have this ability. Finally, make chimerism a function of a lunar simply giving in to insanity and letting go of their humanity and logic entirely to embrace pure chaos. There is no need for external forces to force a lunar to this point, it's far more chilling for it to be something that every lunar watches out for in themselves.

    What do you guys think? Anything here that would be utter crap? Anything I missed?
