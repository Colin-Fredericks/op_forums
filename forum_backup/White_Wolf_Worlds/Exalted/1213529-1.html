                                                    I'm not completely done with the episode (20 mins to go), but I can't overstate how important it is to make players see their own Limit Breaks not as a failure state, but as their moment to shine in a horribly cool way.

                                                    My own trick is to present it as "You may treat all NPCs as furniture, and do whatever the hell you want.". (May, not must. That's important.) Everyone has a least a little bit of psychopathy that we keep under control. Limit Break is THE moment. To. Let. It. Out.
