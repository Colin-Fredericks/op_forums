            An excellent way of dealing the biggest point of confusion generated by the endless going back and forth on what are Imperial Legions as opposed to House Legions. I expect to steal this one.
            You are welcome to it! Our House Tepet is somewhat different from the one you'll find in most people's headcanon. Many assume that because Tepet Arada is devout that all of the family lines share his fervor for the Immaculate Philosophy, but there's little evidence for it. Tepet Ejava is openly athiest in Aspect Book: Wood, and the House's main interests are two secular pursuits, the Legions and commerce. We added a lesser focus on maintaining the artistic practices of the Shogunate too. House Tepet is what happens if the Daidoji, Kakita and Yasuki are in charge of the Crane Clan from Legend of the Five Rings, basically.

            My ST fleshed out all the Great Houses somewhat from their 1st edition write-ups, actually. House Vneef's foundingis another area with some contradictions that needed to be settled. Vneef is Imperial Cupbearer to the Empress in her youth. Her exceptional fertility makes itself known after she gives birth to quintuplets. Few in the Realm know that the sire is actually her own father, one of the Empress' consorts from a minor Cynis bloodline. After all five children Exalt and maintain the excellent breeding of their parents, the Empress grants her favored daughter a new Great House and an Imperial monopoly on grape wine; every vineyard within the Empire is transferred to House Vneef. Some of the former vintners, such as Tepet Jegan, petition for adoption by Vneef; they betray their own Houses to keep some measure of control over sources of wealth. This happens 60 years before the present setting, so Vneef is actually somewhat older than 60 even if she still has not passed her first century. In the intervening years before RY763 the Empress carves the Merchant Marine out from House Peleps to add to Vneef, just as in canon, but also folds most of the minor houses of the Dynasty into the Vneefs to bolster their numbers. These are the minor households of Imperial children who were not granted Great House status, and between them and the turncoats like Jegan House Vneef has a small stable of elders who advise the much younger Imperial Princess. (Since the minor Houses are never detailed anyhow, we sweeped them all into the Vneefs to explain how a 60 year old House gets big enough to seat Senators, run every vineyard in the Empire and manage the Merchant Marine)

            Although it is not terribly important to our games the Sword of Creationis an exotic component within the Realm Defense Grid. It was an enormous spike of jade found within the Wyld used by Autochthon during the construction of Creation itself. It is the Sword grace of Creation, imposing the story of world on the Wyld that surrounds it; this is why it is so effective when used against any enemy of Creation, and why the earth suffers when it is turned against any other. The Ewer of Souls is the Cup grace, granting the "mercy" of forgetting oneself before being birthed anew. We left the others as mysteries to expand on if we ever found a great idea for them.

            TheEwer does remind me of some theories I have about reincarnation and Exaltation after reading the Alchemical's book. I don't like Lytek or Taru-Han having much say at all in who is Exalted, even if they claim credit all the time. I believe the process for selecting worthy souls for insertion into an Alchemical Exalt is a manual version of an automated procedure that happens within Creation. Those heroes who are selected by an Exaltation are not just people who have met appropriate criteria in this life, but ones whose souls have been heroic in previous lives as well. This explains why a Sidereal Exaltations work the way they do; they enter a new life with the first breath when the body gains its soul, and the Ewer always assigns a heroic soul to match it. Those to attain Dragon-Blooded Exaltation must have a similar process, judging from Rising Butterfly Prayer's ability to recommend a soul for reincarnation as a Terrestrial Exalt. Oddly this means the Immaculate Philosophy's teachings on the path to Enlightenment are only partially wrong; all of the behavior they encourage for peasants removes them from being worthy of Exaltation, for instance.

                I'm stealing a bunch of things that have been posted here.
                Also, more of my headcanon:

                The Museum of Splendrous Wonders
                An ancient, out of the way war museum of the first age, it lost it's significance when the Exalted host moved it's government away from the region it was close to.
                Trying to make it matter again, the administrator tried to rekindle it's attraction by adding things you would expect of a high class spa and retreat rather than from a museum.
                The museum is filled with more ancient artifacts than your average group of starting exalts can use, with some of the pieces being merely good replicas (usually made out of 5MM) and others being in a bad shape of disrepair but most are intact.

                Russian Skynet:
                A socialistic Russia themed land exists in my setting. It is called Dokeschia and also known as the Union of the Socialistic Dokeschian Republic, or USDR. It is a totalitarian regime that tries hard to leave no privacy to it's citizens, while also empowering them so long as they don't go against their country. The country is filled with primitive, barely artifact 1 drones that act as cameras and populistic statues decrying monarchies and democracies.
                It is secretly ruled by a versions of IAM that was made to rule a country in the case it's exalted caretaker died. It was never planned to do this for more than 10-20 years. S.T.A.L.I.M. aka Silent Totalitarian Autocrat Left In Mastership has, of course, gone crazy in the meantime. While it is trying very hard to create a society in which normal people have a good life, it is insanely afraid of the Exalted, as they are a great threat to every mortal within the borders of it's land.
                So, obviously, it secretly had to build a ridiculously large amount of skeleton-shaped (psychological warfare) brass soldiers. If it can't find a solution to get the Exalted to stay out of it's republic, long-term, it will have kill off humanity, so humanity is no longer endangered by Exalted.
                It doesn't actually have a chance, but it will endanger many lives and bring destruction to a large area. Or it may be reasoned with, using it's own warped logic.

                                                Originally posted by Chejop Kejak View Post

                                                Pardon if I'm being dense, but what are "the Hanya"?
                                                I forget where they were introduced, in an Ink Monkeys article or in the GWM errata, but they're a weird new breed of Unshaped that rose up after the Balorian Crusade failed. Once Balor's hold on the Raksha collective narrative was broken, the Hannya as a group grabbed it and changed the default story of the Wyld into a place where monsters eat your damn face. Now most New Unshaped are Hannya from the start, and the only old school Unshaped out there are the rare few who are sturdy enough to withstand these younger ones trying to take bites out of them.

                                                The Raksha we know best are the remnants and descendants of the Crusade trapped in the places that are too Wyld for Creation's defenders to harry them, but not Wyld enough for the Hannya to hunt. Imagine a civilization of mermaids trapped in a shallow harbor, watching shark fins circle in deeper waters.

                                                It's been suggested that in 3E, Hannya will be their own distinct breed of Wyld Prodigy, rather than an exotic divergence of regular Raksha.
