                                        Your List is Appreciated, But I Was Only Looking For Your Most Immediate Priority: Stuff Is Happening!
                                        Thank The Dragons: 6
                                        Get Your Stuff: 3

                                        Still panting from adrenaline, you stand tall on the roof in victory. Then, you bow your head in prayer.

                                        “So, uh. Hey, Dragons…there’s no way I’m this lucky,” you say quietly. “Um…thanks. For looking out for me, I guess. It’s…not like much of anybody else really is.”

                                        Praying out loud is a bit awkward, but it feels appropriate.

                                        After a few moments of staring at the setting sun and being really grateful not to be dead, you close your eyes and shake your head a little to clear your thoughts. You have things to do. You should grab your things-and, now that you think about it, you ought to untangle that raiton. You can’t really stomach the idea of eating it-it did sort of help you, after all, and raiton is pretty greasy meat anyway…and saving an innocent creature is the sort of thing Sextes Jylis would do, right?

                                        You turn and crouch to start gathering your things…only to find yourself suddenly surrounded by black feathers and claws and beaks and harsh croaking. The flock of raitons overhead descends upon you, and upon the entire rooftop, with very little warning! Ignoring you, they are tearing into the bundle of river dragon parts, squabbling over them with each other! Twine tears, meat tears, and valuable bits of River Dragon are dragged and thrown all over the place! The tangled-up raiton has the misfortune to be tied to the big bundle at the center of it all, and is getting dragged all over the roof as its fellows fight each other to try and make off with it!

                                        Frustrated and enraged, you wade into the middle of the flock, yelling and kicking and waving your knife around. The horrible feathered reptiles screech angrily and flutter away when you get close, only to land again a few feet away and creep aggressively back toward morsels of River Dragon, watching you warily. The roof is slippery and it’s getting dark and they seem to realize your mobility is much more limited than theirs. There’s a big circle of them around you, and you’ve grabbed the biggest bundle (and dangling from it, an angrily squawking feathered reptile who, up this close, you realize smells pretty strongly of smoked fish) for yourself, but you’ve lost more than half your prize.

                                        Growing bold, one of the raitons darts in to try and snatch a bite from your bundle. You swat at with your knife, and it veers off and flaps away clumsily.

                                        The rest of them seem more wary now, and watch you curiously but stop trying to get closer. Maybe it’s just your imagination, but…their eyes seem oddly intelligent. Now that you think about it…Nexus is full of people clambering about on the roofways, and they’re not all as graceful as you. The local raitons probably know that people falling off roofs are a decent source of food…most of these ones seem to be incapable of working cooperatively, they’re just squabbling over scraps…but there are a few dozen who are just sitting still and…watching you. Are they smart enough to work together?

                                        What now?

                                        Flee: Stuff your bundle, and the attached raiton if cutting it away takes more than a few seconds, into your bag to prevent further flyby attacks. Then back away slowly, and climb down to street level. Don’t risk your life gathering up what you’ve already lost on a slippery roof in the dark fighting flying rats.

                                        Free the Raiton: Disentangling it from the twine and letting it go might let the others know you’re not a threat, and you’ve heard stories about people who take care of a raiton with a broken wing and have black-feathered friends bringing them coins and buttons and other shiny objects for months afterward. You could stand to make some new friends…and you don’t much relish the idea of trying to climb down with that thing struggling and wriggling right next to you.

                                        Go On The Offense: Put your stinky little bundle in your bag to protect it, then go gather up the bits scattered over the roof. Keep the knife in one hand, swing the heavy bag to keep the birds off with the other, and go after the ones that are sitting and watching you first to intimidate them out of any thoughts of cooperation. Get your stuff back!

                                        Make An Example: Hold up the tied-up raiton in front of them and snap its neck. You’re pretty sure raitons are clever enough to understand the interspecies sign for “Fuck with me and you end up like this guy.”

                                        Something Else: Do something else!

                                        Combat Resources:
                                        • Rough twine (1 roll)
                                        • Your Lucky Pebble
                                        • Twine Armband
                                        • Knife (Currently Wielding in your Left Hand)
                                        • Carrying Bag
                                        • Two Heavy Clay Jugs (In the Bag)
                                        • 7/7 Health Levels
                                        • 3/4 Willpower
                                        • A River Dragon Spleen? You think it’s a spleen? Tied in a bundle, but ripped up and leaking smaller pieces of meat.
                                        • A Spleenful of River Dragon Parts
                                        • A tangled-up Raiton

                                        Potentially Useful Scenery Scattered About the Roof?
                                        • Fishhooks (2)
                                        • A medium sized smoked fish-currently being eaten
                                        • Three River Dragon Fangs (one big one, four smaller ones-count as Knives)
                                        • A four-by-four swatch of thick, scaly River Dragon hide-currently being pecked at
                                        • Assorted chunks of River Dragon organ meat-currently being eaten
                                        • A Brick Chimney
                                        • A Flock of Raitons
