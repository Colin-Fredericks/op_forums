    GLORIOUS SOLAR BUILDING
    Costs: 15m, 1wp Mins: Craft 5, Essence 3
    Type: Simple
    Keyword: Obvious
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite: Thousand-Forge Hands

    Good craftsmen can make a sword in half the time. Very good craftsmen can make swords in mere hours. Supernatural craftsmen can create a sword out of haematite with a mere wring of their hands. Some craftsmen can make things from nothing.

    The solar completes a single mundane superior project in a single moment of pure excellence, the world obeying her command, reshaping itself. With materials at hand, this charm can be used once a week, and the materials leap into the air to spontaneously form the project. Without materials, the light from the anima flares to coalescing into a solid building, and can be used once a year. This can also be used to create large numbers of major projects, ranging from having a thousand swords burst out of the sand, or have dust coalesce around your soldier's clothes to form impervious armour.

    Stunts are recommended.

    METAMORPHIC PRAYER

    Sorcerous working: Terrestrial 3/ Celestial 1

    "I wish to transform, into a newer, better, me!"

    This working was created at the behest of someone who was truly miserable with the conflict between the talents he had, the talents he wanted, and the duties he wanted. A single shrine with an alter, with a single unquenchable flame within. Toss a sacrifice within the flames. It will not harm you. Whether it is pot brownies, coins, gold, silver, or a small wooden horse carved by your son. Spend a willpower, and the sorcerous working will continue. This lets you convert abilities, attributes, and merits on a dot by dot basis. A wandering swordsman an become a social butterfly. A strongman wrestler can become a scholar. This can only be done willingly.
