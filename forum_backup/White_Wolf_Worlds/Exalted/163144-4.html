            Five-Hundred Arhats Murderous Pilgrimage
            Raksha Sword
            Despite their appetites, the Fair Folk can recognize some in greater need of Valor than they. In moments of pity, they may grant this. When feeding on a Creation-born's Valor, the raksha gains no motes, Willpower or gossamer. The Prince of Chaos instead takes this Valor into the kiln of her Sword, alloys it with gossamer, and returns it. In this way can the Creation-born become enlightened beyond the heights of mortal Valor.

            The Creation-born's Valor is treated as though it is 5 dots higher, ignoring the 5-dot limit, for all purposes other than channeling his Valor to enhance an action or any other effects for which it presents a clear benefit (higher mote pools, a Charm granting bonus dice equal to it, etc.), all of which use his original Valor. The first exception is a Valor roll to resist fleeing combat. The second is that the character may use this inflated Valor to retain Willpower when resting, but only in any day the character faces at least one Valor roll and spends no Willpower to resist successes on any such rolls. The character's skill in war and battle become more resilient, and she may reduce any internal penalties to such action by 1, to a minimum of 0.

            All social rolls from others meant to curb violent action are automatically resisted by the afflicted with a bonus of half his inflated Valor, while all social rolls to promote violent action may be resisted only with an equal penalty. No raksha may feed on this tainted Valor. The effect ends only when other effects reduce the target's 5 "artificial" Valor. Many in the threshold have lived in fear of raksha lords with bands of these rage-kissed, mortal warriors with souls drunk empty of all but fey-touched Valor.

            Similar Charms exist for the other Virtues.

                New Charm: Wheels Within Wheels, Worlds Within Worlds
                Sidereal Bureacracy

                There is no greater trap than the illusion of progress. When any character is interacting with a organization of which the Sidereal is an active member, they can be targeted with this charm. All of their social attacks on members of the organization will be redirected in such a way as to make it appear that they have succeeded, but without actually having any effect. Use cases of this charm include:
                * A member of the organization is trying to gain a promotion. Their "successful" performance or socialize checks get them transferred to another position with the same amount of influence and pay.
                * An investigator is trying to get information about the organization. Their "successful" investigation checks lead them in a circle, with responsibility for providing information or making decisions constantly being passed from person to person, department to department.
                * The leader of the organization wants to restructure it. Their "successful" bureaucracy check changes everyone's titles and positions, but everyone still answers to everyone else in the same hierarchy they had and performs the same duties they had before the shift.

                    Ignoble Varlet's Promise
                    Sidereal Larceny/Astrology

                    The Sidereal, taking the role of a minor functionary or servant of a target, borrows an object from somebody under a vaguely worded oath, such as 'I'll take care of it while you're away' or 'It'll be in the parking lot when you wish to leave," or even 'Not a scratch.' The target must roll P+Investigation versus the Sidereal's Essence+Larceny, or else willingly part with the object. Note that, while the Sidereal is under no real obligation to keep his word, any attempt to actually adhere to the terms of the promise yield a 3-die bonus to the use of the 'borrowed' object.

                        More Wicked Than Thou

                        Infernal Presence

                        The servants of the Yozis laugh at your attempts to sway them with pain and fear. The Green Sun Prince doubles his successes on a roll made against an attempt at intimidation, torture or similar acts of violence meant to prey on the infernal's fears. Note that the Infernal's actual fears don't necessarily need to come into play, there simply needs to be an attempt. For instance, an Infernal who is locked in a cage with a swarm of wasps doesn't need to fear wasps to use this charm.

                                                            Falling Star Renewal - Sidereal Bureaucracy

                                                            Sometimes a Sidereal must execute a god, but still requires a being to fulfill its role temporarily. In such cases, the Sidereal may put a replacement together from the remains. The Sidereal reforms a god that has been struck down in his presence within the last day. The god has 1 Essence for each point of willpower or for each 15 motes put into this charm, plus half of the user's dots in Bureaucracy (rounded down). The god remembers none of its previous existence, and bears a new intimacy for the Sidereal who performs the charm of absolute loyalty and obedience that remains for a year and a day. The god may lose the intimacy after this point, but should they lose the intimacy, the god dies once again.

                                                            Lift the Dragon High - Solar Occult

                                                            On rare occasion, a Solar in the armies of the Primordials would have to gift a Dragon-Blood, Lunar or Sidereal with his own power to accomplish a task. The Solar gifts another Exalted all of his non-sorcery charms for one scene. The Exalted must pay at least 1 wp per charm used. (charms that already require willpower do not need to spend additional willpower. The Exalted also gains all permanent and reflexive charms, but does not require aforementioned spent willpower to use them. This charm can be extended to a full day, but requires all of the Solar's remaining Willpower and Motes to do so and must pay the minimum cost for a scene. As long as this charm is active, the Solar cannot use any granted charms. The Exalted targeted by this charm is treated like its own Exalt type unless the used charm requires the user to be a Solar.
