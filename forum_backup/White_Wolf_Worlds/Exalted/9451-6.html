                        It's not that they can't do what the others can, it's that each splat has a particular style which they lend themselves to. Solars are good at power and perfection, so their magical weapons tend to be very powerful and a perfect example of its kind. Sidereals do the fate-thing; their most powerful works of artifice are created when they imbue it with a Destiny - a magical sword can still be powerful against other foes, but against that one foe it was destined to fell, it can equal or even outmatch the greatest of Solar blades. And Lunars, instead, tie their weapons to people, like how they are bound, how their patron is bound; depending on the Lunar and the weapon, a sword might be perfect for an Exaltation - frequently, the Lunar's mate - or it might be tied to the soul of the person the Lunar cares about. To others, it is still a powerful artefact, but when wielded by the one it was made for, it is greater still.

                        Aretii: "As one of the players described it, 'After initiation, it was actually a relief seeing who had eyepatches or bandaged hands - you know what they sacrificed, instead of wondering what bits people cut out of their own souls to gain power.'"

                            One idea I had for Lunars is a cross between MacGyver and the "make stuff up from Wyld" of the Fair Folk: the Lunars can make short term items out of anything. An axe made of silk that cuts as well as steel, a flute of grass, a shield of air.... things that are not craftable for even a Solar without serious sorcery backing them up or a Fair Folk drawing on its impossible power are at the tip of the fingers for a Lunar. Just as their shape can be matched for the occasion, so can they shape and mold their environment for their benefit, literally. At higher levels I can see cloaks of shadow and fortresses of fur and wood and bone... as long as it's something of an elemental material, something from Gaia and Creation, it can be shaped for a purpose. Maybe play it up as Luna's mercurial teaching meeting Gaia's stalwart substance or something?

                                                            My favorite Lunar craft charm from previous editions allowed them to restructure materials simply by passing their hands over it, making any substance like soft clay in thier hands. That always seemed to be the essence of lunar crafting to me. Where a solar can make the absolutely most amazing wonders when they amass enough infrastructure and support (and whatever else is deemed necessary), a Lunar can take nearly any item at hand and adapt it to be just what he/she needs for the situation.

                                                            SO a solar can craft several weapons of unparallelled power to be ready for any battle that may arise. A lunar, on the other hand, only brings a walking stick to any fight... and has a damn fine daiklaive, bow, shield, axe or what have you from that one item alone.
