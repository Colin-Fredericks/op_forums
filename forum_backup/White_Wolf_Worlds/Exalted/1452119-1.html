                                Okay, new Charms! I'm going to eat my dessert first, and start with the Getimian ones.

                                (I don't really understand what's going on with the Flowing/Stilled stuff, but that's probably a conversation for the main Essence thread, since it's a rule, not a Charm.)

                                I wonder how many sessions it would be before I could convince another PC to drink the magical elixir I spat up with Alchemic Power Reserve? I was hoping that Getimians wouldn't just do weird glitch stuff, but also weird Taoist stuff.

                                Fate-Unwinding Denial's effect doesn't do it for me, but the idea of a Charm powered by banked stunts is a surprise. Not sure how I feel about it.

                                Summoned From Time is a Charm with big cosmological implications - it looks like a mid-Essence Getimian can make thousands of people from her Origin real in Creation. To me, that says something different from "these Exaltations take certain specific discarded destines and create a person around them."

                                (I know this is likely left up to individual groups by design as well as necessity, but I really want to know how these people remember the Origin timeline - does it stop at the moment of Exaltation, or are they full of stories about things falling apart without the great hero's guidance?)

                                Devil-Queen's Court is full of spooky manipulative overlord stuff, but beneath all of that, it's a cap-breaking dice adder. This concerns me.

                                After grumping about Memetic Dogma Discipline, Forgetting the Lie (Flowing) gets my approval - it doesn't mess with NPCs' social rolls, but just describes what ends up happening. And what an effect it is! "Why aren't you searching the compound, Cheng? The Satrap's orders were clear-" "No, Li, the Satrap got overthrown five years ago. What are you talking about?" "Oh, right, so they did. Hey, why is there some fraud using some kind of magic to make us act like the Satrap is back? We've got to warn everyone!" (Li is in for a bad time if they decide to split up with Cheng, but either way, this should sow enough infighting among the guards for an easy getaway.)

                                Also, a Charm prerequisite! So those do still exist. That's fair, so long as they're used sparingly.

                                Icon-Obliterating Passion wins the award for "most likely to become a mainstay in long-running internet arguments." So, the God is not the river, eh? (Cue several dozen posts debating whether the Charm itself created the God-river connection, or it already existed for the Charm to exploit.)

                                Getimian Charms still have their theme of "there are no rules to oppose this." There are LOTS of effects which appear to just happen - mostly it involves Intimacies, but definitely not all of it. Most notably, Culture-Hero Usurpation means that if Sha'a Oka blows one Integrity roll, the entire Silver Pact sees the Getimian as a respected luminary. They cannot resist, their memories change to accommodate this, and I don't even think the Getimian ever needs to go to The Caul or so much as see The Black Lion in person. In fairness, this one does pretty squarely fall under Destiny-Manifesting Method, but wow. Throne-Smashing Ambition has a similar impact - if you punch Amarel, there's nothing Fakharu can do to keep from hating her.

                                (Oh, you don't think a Lunar elder would ever lose a rolloff with an Essence 5 Celestial? Okay, come up with the biggest mover and shaker you can imagine who has surprisingly low Integrity. Is it the head of a Great House? The sometimes-portrayed-as-totally-brainwashed Mouth of Peace? Regent Fokuf may not have any real power, but I bet that if every Dynast and Sidereal treated me as him, I could make the Realm Civil War go hot by noon tomorrow...)

                                I hope that somebody is still using social influence in Essence, so that someday I can use (Flowing) The Filial Chain: you have to call the target in advance, but you can "tank" for less-social allies and the payoff is pure comedy. "Renounce my suppression of the old religion? No, can't do that, but tell you what - I'll pay penance by having you cast off your worldly possessions and become a wandering hermit."

                                The Flowing mode is okay, but Wall-Denying Fury mostly makes me happy as the first time I've thought a Still mode was way cooler. That's kind of a relief, since Autumn is the Timmy Caste I'm most likely to play. "In my world, there's not a castle here, but a carnival!"

                                Oh, hey, Constant Problem Tactic also has a way more entertaining Still mode. Things are looking up! In an unmoving Yin kind of way. CPT is also interesting as a fairly rare case of a Charm which has a totally different role (one in combat and one outside of it) depending on the mode, which is rad.

                                One-on-One Imprisonment is oddly lacking Flowing/Still modes, but remains incredibly versatile. Good for styling on a weaker enemy (to keep them from escaping), dramatic social scenes (for showing that Sidereal exactly why your world was better), and even the occasional heroic self sacrifice (alright, Mask of Winters, let's you and I step into another universe while my allies lick their wounds or run away).

                                It seems fitting to end on Unforged Masterworks, since I had been meaning to close by saying that the Getimian Charms do help me to understand the old statement that they tend to skew "antagonistic." A lot of their powers involve destroying this world's versions of things - including people - to bring in their Origin counterparts, and a magic sword of "kill you forever to spend five minutes with a version of you who likes me" is about the perfect summation of that.

                                They're still at the top of my "if I ever get to be a PC in this game" list, but it's still worth reflecting upon.

                                "For me, there's no fundamental conflict between really loving something and also seeing it as very profoundly flawed." -- Jay Eddidin

                                                            My big thought about Summoned From Time is simply that you must be Essence 3 take it. That means 3-dot Merits. Command 3 is enough for a Size 5 Battlegroup, which (at least in 3E) is 1,000 people. And "the character may use an Exalt Milestone to purchase it permanently," meaning there are now 1,000 people in Creation with a memory of a world that never was.

                                                            If they all really have their own pasts and memories and souls, and if you could interview each of them and find that their world is internally consistent on details that the Getimian doesn't know (and Creation doesn't share)... to me, that's a much stronger argument for discarded destinies being "real" worlds than the terrible fire of Exaltation bringing 64 people who never were into existence at any given time.

                                                            To be clear, I like the idea that the ultimate reality or unreality of Origins is something that nobody in-universe has a definitive answer to. Even gods and sorcerers can sometimes discuss philosophy, rather than objective knowledge. But from my point of view, that's a compelling argument.
