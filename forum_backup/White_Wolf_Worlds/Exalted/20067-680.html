                                    Like many parts of life, teaching isn't something that can be put down to simply one attribute. Charisma, Perception, Intelligence, Wits, I'd say all of these are important. It depends on all sorts of things. First, you've got to lower students' affective filters. Which could be Charisma, but could be Wits to think of a creative lesson, Perception to notice external factors that are stopping students learning, etc. Maybe even Manipulation under some circumstances! (Not Appearance though). Then you've got to get students to realise what they don't know, then you've got to explain it to them; which does require understanding from you (you can't explain well what you don't understand), but it's not just that. Just because you get something doesn't mean the students will. Then they've got to actually practice it to help them remember. And you also need Perception and Wits to notice when the lesson isn't working and needs to be changed.

                                    You can't be stupid, but universities are full of genius researchers who really know their stuff and are terrible teachers.

                                    My boss always says the first thing a good teacher needs is presence and a rapport with the students. Some people have it, some don't. Once you've got that, the main difference between good and bad teachers is pedagogy, creativity and adaptability. Well, and resources and students, but not much you can do about that.

                                    Tbh, I'd probably allow Charisma+Lore, Intelligence+Lore, Perception+Lore, or Wits+Lore. If I had to pick one, I'd go for Wits. You've got to be on the ball. But I could see arguments for any of them.

                                    Saying this... the Lunar teaching charms are in Intelligence. So, sure, go with that.

                                    (Obviously we've said Lore here, but if you're teaching Melee it's going to be Melee, etc.)

                                    STing Bronze Age Exalted
