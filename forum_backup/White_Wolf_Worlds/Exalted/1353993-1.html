                                                            Listening to all of this, I think I will add a little additional information.

                                                            The campaign I hope to run will be a Dragon-Blooded focused one... specifically on the survivors of a successful but costly Wyld Hunt. The players at this time will be fresh from graduation at their respective Secondary Schools (or the equivalent there of) and will have joined this Wyld Hunt to gain some experience for their future careers (looks great on a resume, so to speak). They will be going after a small but growing cult that seems to have fallen under the sway of an Anathema (presumed to be a Solar). What they find however is that the Cult worships a Solar and Lunar pair... with the Lunar being a powerful Witch/Sorceress (Essence 5 No Moon Caste) while the Solar is a competent Warrior-Priest (Essence 2 Zenith Caste). While the Wyld Hunt easily decimates the mortal followers of the Anathema (with the Players taking the lead in this part), the power of the two Anathema is such that it more than compensates and decimates the strength of the Wyld Hunt. By the end of the first encounter, the Cult is shattered, the forces of the Wyld Hunt are diminished significantly, the two Anathema escape (but are drained and wounded) and the players are the only surviving Exalts from the Hunt (though there is secretly a wounded and drained Sidereal survivor too). The players will have to decide if the fails and the go to get reinforcements (allowing the Anathema time to recover) or press on to get the job done (this may be their best and only chance to finish the Anathema off). Hopefully, the players will charge in to finish the job... which will see the Solar captured and the Lunar killed. The Solar will be brought to the Scarlet Empress (or other authority that might be able to save the soul of the person possessed by the Anathema) for imprisonment.

                                                            After this, there will be a 50 year time skip (which may be played out in short one-shot adventures over a few sessions, with each taking place years apart as events bring the Sworn Brotherhood back together... or test their bonds to each other). I am thinking of granting players a small EXP reward for this period to show how their characters have progressed in this period... depending on how much they have gained through their first Hunt and the small adventures between. Ideally, when we resume the main story, they should be close to reaching Essence 4 (if they already haven't).

                                                            When the main story resumes, the players will be in the present setting, with the Empress having vanished about 3 years ago and the Realm inching ever closer to Civil War while more and more Threshold States rebel against the oppressive taxation of the Great Houses. Into this mess comes rumors of a barbaric Warlord raising an army to reunite the nation the players decimated 50 years ago... suggesting that the Lunar has reincarnated and reinvented themselves as a Full Moon Caste (Essence 4 due to constant activity) with a massive and growing army. The also learn that the Solar they imprisoned (and likely thought long dead) has managed to escape its imprisonment and was able to flee to the Threshold (and is likely looking to join up with the Lunar... once the learn of each other). Like the Lunar, the Solar has become more powerful than before, but due to its imprisonment, it isn't as powerful as the Lunar (Solar is only Essence 3). Knowing that their best chance for dealing with these threats is to move quickly and hunt them down before they can join up again, the only resources the players will likely be able to call upon is that the are personally responsible for (troops and such loyal to them) as any nation they are part of is likely gearing up to deal with the impending Realm civil war.

                                                            In short, the Second Hunt will more or less reverse the roles of the players. Where they were well backed and supplied by the Wyld Hunt the first time, their resources in this one is more limited and finite. Where before they were going up against mostly untrained combatants, this time they are fighting a trained (though not an elite) army. The only thing really in their favor is that they are more experienced than before, but the Anathema have also learned to fight more effectively than they did before. The Lunar may not have access to the same level of Sorcery as before... which is where the Zenith comes in (it was able to recover enough confiscated occult material from where it was imprisoned to help it master Celestial Circle Sorcery... it learned Terrestrial Circle from the Lunar 50 years prior). This should make the rematch a lot more interesting and uncertain. While it is very possible for the players to triumph and put these threats down for a time (they will reincarnate again and again), it is also possible that this will be their final battle... their final stand against the chaos and darkness engulfing Creation. There is also the possibility that they come up with another option that I haven't thought of yet (such as maybe discovering an even greater threat that can only be defeated if they join forces with the Anathema... though what could cause this outcome?).

                                                            To be honest, I just wanted to be sure that 50 years is enough time for a Lunar that is reasonably active (as a warlord) could attain Essence 4 in 50 years. I think that I might be pushing it to raise the Solar to Essence 3 in the same time frame, given its imprisonment (likely with devices to drain it of Essence to prevent escape). As for the players, I think having them close to (or having just reached Essence 4) puts them in a reasonable place for this final part of the campaign.

                                                            The general rule I have been going with is that Dragon-Bloods reach Essence 2 by the time they finish Secondary School, Essence 3 by the time they finish their first tour of duty, Essence 4 around the time of their first century... and Essence 5 around their 2nd century. For Solars, they usually reach Essence 2 within a year of Exaltation, Essence 3 within a decade, Essence 4 within a mortal's lifespan and Essence 5 after a century or more (Essence 6 requires at least a few centuries to a millennium). Lunars are roughly the same, as would Abyssals and Infernals (though these two have access to resources that can speed this up). Sidereal likely retain teaching techniques that allow them to reach Essence two by the end of their training (a year or two) and can reach Essence 3 or 4 within the first century after Exaltation... Essence 5+ likely requires centuries to attain under normal conditions. Alchemicals likely follow a similar pattern. I don't know enough about the newer Exalts (Exigents, Liminals and even Getimians) to be sure of their tendencies and initial power levels to know their Essence growth with any reasonable reliability (Exigents alone are so varied in power levels and even lifespans to be hard to generalize in any fashion). These generalities of course assume a reasonable level of activity without world-shaking events that could accelerate this (or reset them if killed).

                                                            Players of course defy these generalities as a mater of course, since they tend to be the main focus of their stories and thus accomplish more in a reasonable short period of time.
