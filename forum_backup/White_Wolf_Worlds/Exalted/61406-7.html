                                I've been on a bit of an Asimov kick today, so I'd like to recommend Caves of Steel for Autochthonia inspiration and End of Eternity for Sids.

                                Caves of Steel is a murder mystery set in the far future, where the cities of earth have become Cities - massive enclosed complexes designed to make the feeding and housing of Earth's many billions efficient enough to be sustainable. Between that, the retro-future elements, and the form of social hierarchy used in the story, it gets close enough to the Eight Nations that I'd have few qualms about importing it to be a 9th Nation.

                                End of Eternity is a novel about the people who, via time travel, watch over all of human history and, when necesary, make changes to it to ensure a safer and more prosperous future. They live outside the usual flow of time, in a tightly controlled and bureaucratic organization (with the resulting level of corruption), which handles making all these changes. Ultimately, they're trying to ensure that human history is safe and comfortable (and boring) for everyone. Sound familiar?

                                I'm fairly certain you can pick either one up for the Kindle.

                                                        I've ranted about Zelazny enough, but it's obligatory so...go read some Zelazny. Go for the short story anthologies, the other stuff is good but it's not his best work. Anyway.

                                                        Here's a list of things I sometimes watch to get in the mood for running Exalted. Please note that my taste is highly erratic, and I make no pretense to be a judge of quality, only what inspires me. Entries marked with an Asterisk are things I sometimes cajole my players into watching in order to get them in the mood for playing Exalted:

                                                        1.The Flight, because I love works of beauty.
                                                        2. The Protector- Because pretty martial arts is pretty.
                                                        3. Ong Bak 2*- I made the players in my An-Teng game watch this movie, for the vibes.
                                                        4. Ong Bak 3- Not as good as 2, so I don't make others watch it, but good enough for me.
                                                        5. Kung Fu Hustle* -Because Toad is best First Age Lunar.
                                                        6.Ironic T-Shirt Sketch: Grant Proposal- In my private headcanon, this Deliberative funding application was the straw that finally broke Kejak's back and led to the Usurpation.
                                                        7.DyE Fantasy - I use this to get in the mood for WoD too, but when I'm looking to play Exalted Modern, or when I'm looking to get my Yozi on, I watch this video first.
                                                        8. Hero* -This is the story of an Eclipse defeating a Dawn and a Chosen of Endings through social-fu.
                                                        9. Detective Dee and the Mystery of Phantom Flame*- Because murder mysteries are awesome, and kung fu murder mysteries doubly so!
                                                        10. Avatar, the Last Airbender* -Seriously. Y'all know why.
                                                        11. The Neverending Story -The book is better, but in my game Luranume appears as a silver-white dragon of adorable fluffiness on occasion. Also, good Oblivion vibes here.
                                                        12. Anything by Hayao Miyazaki* - Because Chuubo's isn't out yet. Mononoke's the best Exalted inspiration (gender-swap all references to the distant Emperor and the movie might as well be set in Creation), but they're all good.
                                                        13. Harry Potter* -But only for Heptagram games.
                                                        14. A Wizard of Earthsea, by Ursula K. LeGuin-For Heptagram games. Let's get some books u[p in here.
                                                        15. Cowboy Bebop- For West games, and for good one-session travelogue adventure ideas.
                                                        16. The Twelve Kingdoms-Look, Labyrinth of Magic is pretty good, but The Twelve Kingdoms is more Exalted. Don't...don't expect a satisfactory ending or anything, though? Just mine it like the gold vein it is.
                                                        17. Thor 1 and 2: They're not that good, really. But that funky Kirby-style ancient looking magitech is exactly the sort of vibe I want First Age artifacts to have.
                                                        18. The Mission: Robert deNiro and Jeremy Irons costar in this movie. Why haven't you seen it?! Also, it's good inspiration for anywhere Immaculate Monks are faced with a conflict between the benevolent teachings of their order and the worldly interests of their Empire. I watch this movie for inspiration if any of the PCs have plans to do something about slavery.
                                                        19. Sinbad: Legend of the Seven Seas: This one I watch for West games. Also, in my prive headcanon? Sinbad is a young Desus, and his love interest a young Lillith. Personally, I feel like the Primordial War may have been a number of simultanous world-shaking MacGuffiny quests like this one before it became the Primordial War.
                                                        20. Firefly: Nobody does an ensemble cast better than Whedon, and I watch this show just as much for ideas on how to build solid group cohesion as for Exalted inspiration.
                                                        21. Equilibrium: Gun Katas. 'nuff said.
                                                        22. Lo: I watch this one when I need demon inspiration. "No love without pain," is one of the laws of Malfeas. Right up there with "No silence without death."
                                                        23. WakFu: Cuz it's purdy.
                                                        24. Monty Python's The Life of Brian: ...okay, my Cult of the Illuminated might be a little too heavily inspired by this movie...
                                                        25. Beowulf: Look, this movie looks ABSOLUTELY TERRIBLE, and the acting is mediocre, and it kinda shits the bed. But the plotline and the themes at its core are Gaiman looking at heroism and aging and kingship, and they're great inspiration for Exalted, particularly for Bull of the North ideas. I mean, read the original, it's better, and get some linguistics professor to gargle it to you in the Olde Tongue, because it's hilarious unless you're around a campfire with booze in which case it's awesome, but the movie's worth a look.

                                                        So I'm making God-Kicking Boot, an Exalted webcomic, now. Updates on Sundays. Full-color, mediocre but slowly improving art. It's a thing.

                                                        The absence of a monument can, in its own way, be something of a monument also.                                                         -Roger Zelazny
