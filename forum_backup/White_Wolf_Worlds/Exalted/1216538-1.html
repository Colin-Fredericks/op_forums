        Originally posted by Delta View Post
        The basics: The campaign is set in the middle of Northern Nowhere, somewhere off to the west of the road which leads all the way up to Ascension, so there's a whole lot of snow, mountains and more snow there. The basic hook was "A bit more civilized than the Icewalkers, less advanced than the Haslanti, a people descended from 'Frost Giants'", so what we have is more or less a people of big, "landlocked vikings" if you will, where six foot is considered small and people with the Giant quality are considered especially "pure" heirs of their mythical forefathers, where most people live in small villages in more or less fertile valleys between snowy peaks and mountain ranges, with only two small cities, one of which is all the way over to the east on the road connecting Ascension to the rest of the world, pretty much the only part of their culture which has any regular contact with outsiders.

        My one character so far is actually not from there, a girl who was brought here over a decade ago by her "father" (not her biological father), who fled here from somewhere south and settled down in a little settlement to become a blacksmith (living in such a region, the people are rather pragmatic, they won't turn away an outsider if he can help the community) and she's now his apprentice, hoping to take over his spot one day which is kind of difficult since it's a male dominated culture, so there's setup for early conflict while she's still a mortal right there.

        One big thing I'm still undecided on and would like some input is the past of my so far only PC, who is she and why did the blacksmith bring her here of all places? She's a mortal, but will sooner or later turn Solar, I'd like for her past to be a hook to be revealed later and still be relevant after her exaltation. The player intentionally left that part of her background blank.
        Well, this initial setup, the particular combination of land, myth and characters described got me really thinking of this.

        So, how about making both girl and father holders of "the Secret of Steel" (thaumaturgy rite or somesuch), that he's hiding in this distant land for fear of the vicious cultist marauders that destroyed their people, that the child may never know. And the cult, while now focused in other interests, is still on the lookout for them?

        Ah, back on the "landlocked vikings" thing, how do you feel about taking it a step further with raiding bands on sled-ships pulled by large animals like yeddim, mamooths and/or "mortal dragons" (dinosaurs)?

        Originally posted by Delta View Post
        Their religion I imagined mostly built around wandering priests with a heavy focus on stories and prophecy, basically every religious story ends with "And one day, when <obscure condition>, he/she/it will return and <thing> will happen!", so they're big on "A man makes his own destiny!" on the one hand, yet on the other hand constantly on the lookout for which of their hundreds of prophecies might fit a given set of circumstances. One idea I had was that centuries ago, their culture may have been influenced by a Sidereal or other powerful force in a way similar to the Bene Gesserit do in Dune, setting up a certain "canon" of prophecies to be used if needed later, but not sure about that yet.
        Take a look into the Sobriquet merit on the Dragon-Blooded book. Titles and the making of one's personal legend as a source of stunts & other benefits could give some fun bite to this whole "one person making one's destiny out of purloined prophecy" conceit.

        Originally posted by Delta View Post
        I can go into more detail if needed, but what I need most help with is actually "grounding" the setting a bit more in Creation. What exactly could those "Frost Giants" be the people descended from? I so far considered them to be quasi-mythical, most people never get to see one, some holy men claim to maybe have met one in a snowstorm on a high mountain, stuff like that. Not sure if they're spirits, something out of the Wyld or something altogether different. I've already hinted that there are tribes of Wyld barbarians to the north which sometimes raid into their territory, but I haven't decided yet on if and how Undead or Fair Folk will play a role here.
        World of Warcraft has devised some interest lore about how many of the multiple races of the planet of Draenor are all differently dilluted/diminished descendants of one original race of giants, on itself originated from broken fragments of one titanic primordial giant made in a long lost past by a cosmic power.

        The lineage of Breakers, so to speak. Do check it out for some creative fodder possibly quite relevant to your ideas.


        Also, not everything spins around the First Age or the Exalted. Unexalted sorcerers, human or non-human, living or dead, from Creation or outside, could use sorcerous workings to craft a band of superhuman warriors, elemental colossi of frost, wind and fog might carve their households and palaces from mountain, glaciers and/or stormclouds, their ghosts or those of their first mortal children shaping a cold domain of their own amidst the northern wastes of the Underworld, strange gods or demons to play games of cult-making, intrigue and power among varied tribes, towns and warbands, exiled fae to purloin already existing myths to craft their own peculiar spin of epic narrative amidst local powers mundane and supernatural, along with many other possibilities.

        Any of those tales/myths/facts - or all of them - could be true and might have come to be in any of a number of past ages, either before, after, during the days Solar Deliberative's glory. And if none are, those with ambition and power might decide to attempt to make it so. Usurpation was about 1500 years ago, that's a lot of time to have "nothing really important happened" as base assumption, a terrible, terrible tendency of 2E that i feel we are all much better of without.

            So, I've had the chance to GM a couple of times, only for said single PC so far. Sorry if what follows is a bit long, but a lot happened...

            The game started with a wandering priest visiting the village, and the PC basically immediately trying to get him to allow her to formally join the local religion (since she's lived there her whole life yet always felt like a complete outsider it seemed like a logical motivation for a teenager), there's some stuff between her and the other youth in the village that doesn't really matter in the grand scheme of things, in the end she does complete the proper rituals and formally joins the people she's lived with.

            At the same time, her father gets some disturbing news about outsiders visiting the main city of the region far too the east, he doesn't tell her yet why but it troubles him enough to leave her alone for a while to travel east to talk to some people and see what's going on (basically trying to check whether it's the Wyld Hunt or someone else looking for them, I decided to take him out of the picture in light of her joining so she does get a picture of the pros and cons of living among them on her own)

            First problem is that the culture is rather male-dominated, even though she's already a very good smith, most people simply can't really accept a teenage girl being the village's blacksmith. She tries to counter this by accepting a farmboy from the village who always wanted to learn from her father but her father never took up another apprentice except for her, it's only later that she understands that by accepting a teenage male into her house like that, they're basically considered betrothed now by the villagers.

            One night, the other youth of the village put her through some light hazing ritual in a old barn outside the village's walls, which is cut short, when suddenly, some monsters attack (basically some wyld mutated wolfs with icicles instead of fur and teeth, giving them some extra soak and damage but still manageable for mortals in small numbers) and she manages to save the daughter of the village chief which had been ribbing hard on her until this point, gaining some acceptance in the process.

            After some hunters report seeing more of those beasts as well as traces of barbarian tribes in the mountains to the north, the chief decides not to allow anyone outside the walls without express permission and sends out more scouts and hunters. They find more traces of barbarians coming closer to the village and more of those wolves they seem to be using as hunting animals and beasts of war, so people get rather nervous.

            One of the hunters, a good friend of the PCs new "betrothed", doesn't get home from his last scouting trip, but with the enemies apparently closing in on the village, the chief is reluctant to send out anyone looking for them. So in the end, he decides to sneak out and go look for him himself, she finds him and doesn't let him go alone (she doesn't know yet that the hunter and her new "roommate" are secretly an item which is something teenagers are usually supposed to grow out of once they get older and marry and so on). So they sneak out and climb up the mountain where he expects his friend to have scouted and thought he saw a light the night before, and they indeed find them, but a snowstorm has come up and he's surrounded by a couple of wolves who have already wounded him.

            They try to fight back but quickly realize it's too many, trying to pull back the wounded hunter into a nearby cave, it's rather hopeless when suddenly a huge shadow swoops an and throws two of the wolves off the mountain, but at the same time, a massive horn sounds throughout the mountain range causing an avalanche just as they get into the cave.

            (side note: The "swooper" is supposed to be a Lunar, not 100% sure yet whether he's been around the area keeping watch for a while or if he's part of the "big secret" somehow and got word from her father when he got to the next village, he'll show up more later)

            The cave entrance gets completely buried by the avalanche, but the PC notices another exit opened by all the shaking and rumbling towards the back, and they find the ruins of an old Wood Manse under the mountain, they have no idea yet what it is but they find hot water, tons of mushrooms growing there (some glowing) and ruins of weird stuff, it's supposed to once have been a "genetics lab" where the Twilight did some experiment on the people she created, but most of it is buried and they're kind of in a hurry to get their wounded companion back home. They manage to find a second exit further down the mountain and see their village being attacked by a small force of barbarians and wolves, with a huge "ice bear" being used as a kind of siege weapon to break down the village walls.

            To get down the mountain quickly, the PC has the great idea of basically using a metal table they found inside the ruins together which the snow shoes, walking sticks etc. they're carrying to create a makeshift sled, so they slide down the mountain at massive speed and due to some excellent rolls, she manages to steer it right into the middle of the battle and basically take down the huge bear just as it smashed through the barricades by ramming it with the sled and jumping off into the snow at the last second.

            Even with the bear taken out though, the battle looks bleak since they villagers are outnumbered, but then a cavalry literally arrives in the form of around a dozen soldiers on the backs off massive elks sent by the local "chief of chiefs" to investigate (one of the scouts the village chief sent out got to the next village in time) led by an eight foot tall woman who smashes through enemies laughing with a massive axe (she's kind of notorious and stories had already been around about her, the daughter of said chief, and while women usually aren't accepted as warriors in their cuture, there's an exception for "trueblooded" giants like her, I mean who's gonna tell her she can't?)

            The barbarians are thrown back and the village is saved, no one's too hard on the PC and her friend because of the way they took down the bear, I try to make Malgra (the giant woman) a bit more unsympathetic by showing her "might makes right" mentality, she doesn't really care about the opinion of the villagers and does and takes whatever she wants, doesn't really care too much about consent as well, but it doesn't really work as intended because player as well as PC swoon about her from the moment she shows up and pretty much worships the ground she walks on, and with her having a new megacrush and the village being in damage assessment mode the latest session ended.


            Okay, this has gotten much longer than I initially intended, sorry you had to read through all of that.

            Where I imagine it would go from here:

            -Malgra will be out for glory, trying to get everyon she can armed and follow up on the retreating barbarians to get them before they can reform, while the village chief wants to send for more reinforcements, the PC may play a role in deciding which route to take since right now, she's one of the two heroes who saved the day
            -She will probably do something about the manse she found, I'm not completely sure what she can find up there, I thought repairing the manse might be a good project for after she Exalts
            -The Lunar will at some point show up again, not sure where and when
            -Other than that, I'll try to take things as they come and wait for a good time for an Exaltation (if she decides to follow Malgra I imagine a fight where they face a much stronger opposition than expected would be a nice opportunity for her to "return the favor" and save the day just as the giant warrior saved her village)
            -Still not completely sure on which route I take for her "secret background", one idea I really liked is that the Twilight somehow managed to create "backup clones" of herself in some kind of stasis, which she knew would inherit her shard after her original body died, so since the fall of the Solars, the Wyld Hunt managed to hunt down most of those clones, and she had been hidden for centuries until her "father" found her, but I'll have to think more on that

                        I think what you need is two simultaneous catastrophes. One to give your PC her moment to shine (with anima, but also metaphorically), and one happening elsewhere to keep your Lunar distracted. Then it becomes a plot point and furthers the mystery - they finish dealing with their battle, and on their way home they come upon a scene where, for example, even more enemies have been slaughtered, but with no signs of the victorious side's dead or indeed evidence of a second large force of men at all (no trail or campsites except the invader's).

                        If you're thinking raksha for your boss battle, be cautious about throwing the Cataphract in the core book up against a freshly exalted Twilight with no circle to back her up. Maybe customise a raksha with abilities that are fearsome to mortals but that your new Twilight will be well-equipped to handle. Perhaps he destroys Malgra's massive axe with a touch, but the Twilight can build new weapons in an instant or imbue them with resilience so the fae's power can no longer affect them? Perhaps he beguiles the giant woman with his hypnotic tongue, but the PC is too stubborn to fall for it? Perhaps instead of honeyed words he traps the warriors' minds with a riddle, but she has the intellect to find her way through the mental maze?
