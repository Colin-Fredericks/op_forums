            Originally posted by Epee102 View Post

            What larceny charm? The one that lets you bank successes? I thought there were some really hard limits on that.
            No, it's Fate-Shifting Solar Arete. It lets you choose a number from 2 to 5 to treat as a 10 for one non-extended roll per full night rest. 2 numbers with a repurchase at essence 4.

            So it lowers the odds of failure by removing possible failure numbers, and raises the success count by turning those failures into double successes.

            It basically lets you dramatically raise your odds/success count for one roll per day, regardless of what Ability is part of that roll.
