                    Originally posted by Timbo View Post
                    Okay, so my plan was to try to use fire aura for Melee pretty much all of the time and dump anima with Smoldering Essence Attack but I'm pretty sure they 5m cost is actually going to cause be to stay essence neutral, so I'll have to use it everytime I want to spend more than 5 essence once I hit burning anima. Is that right? They seems incredibly expensive.
                    I'm not actually 100% sure this works. If the 5m cost for Smorldering Essence Attack is coming out of your peripheral mote pool, I think you might just be spending a level of anima only to get it right back. It's not useless though, it means if you have a level of anima you can use it and 4 other motes and not pop up a level.

                    Managing anima can be frustrating, and while it's not impossible it does stop you from doing anything really explosive more than once or twice. You CAN do it by carefully metering out your peripheral essence pool, like only spend 4m out of it for join battle, only raise your parry through peripheral by 2, ect. You can also do things like use Portenteous Comet Deflection as long as you don't excellency the crap out of the clash attack. Good news though! If you're only fighting one opponent the Melee excellency discount will stack up to make full exellency attacks possible! Also if you don't excellency a lot of attacks, there are very few melee charms that cost more than 4 motes, so activating any one of those on its own is fine.

                    Then after a turn or two of that, especially if you haven't punched up your anima at all, you can cut completely loose and blow all of your personal motes as well as anything left in the tank from your peripherals on a series of overpowered attacks. It can also fit a certain type of samurai aesthetic where you're holding back, holding back, holding back and then controlled release to end your opponents. There's even a neat trick you can do at Essence 4, if you ever get there, where you do all that, explode into bonfire with your last attack, which swaps you instantly to Air Aura, and then next turn you can use Mela's Flashing Tongue to make a bunch of withering attacks and one decisive in a single turn.


                    There are some problems with that style though. For one if you're facing a truly deadly opponent in a duel then your restraint in the first half of combat could get you killed. In that case I say go as hard as you can with personal, then when you get to peripheral if you're not ready to end the fight try shaving off enough motes that you only spend 4-9-14 in a single instant so that you don't peak more anima than you have to, and then when you're backed into a corner trigger Harnessed Firestorm Assault and try to end the fight, or at least put them in their -4s or something.

                    After that you lose Burning Pinnacle Strike, Smoldering Essence Attack, and the really good benefit of Portenteous Comet Deflection,, and Harnessed Firestorm Assault, which are the only ones that really matter in that kind of instance. That won't necessarily spell a death sentence, but it sucks, and you need to make that buildup count. Also that only works if you start the fight with full personal motes anyway.


                    Another thing to consider is your ST, because there's a lot out there, my former self included, who don't like putting in fights against opponents that aren't super skilled and really deadly. I don't think it's necessarily the wrong way to play the game if everyone is having fun, but I don't like it anymore personally. In which case managing your motes by using charms without full excellencies is going to feel really bad. It's just hard to hit somebody with a parry of 11 and onslaught penalty negators without going full attack, and that doesn't fit your character.

                    On the other hand if you're facing more reasonable things with a parry of like, 4-5 without penalty negators, and you maxed out your stats then you can easily just use 1 mote or 0 mote excellencies combined with the fun charms like Burning Pinnacle Strike, or Smoldering Essence. Remember though to use Smoldering Essence you need at least one anima level, so maybe full excellency your join battle or first attack or something.

                    Originally posted by Timbo View Post
                    DrLoveMonkey, your post is amazing and it makes me feel like my concept isn't hopeless. Though, I feel like I'm going to feel like I'm working too hard when my Earth Aspected friend is just effortlessly whomping everything with Fist of the Titans and Earth Dragon Style.
                    Ah, yeah Earth Dragon is one of the most straightforwardly powerful options for DBs. As an Earth Aspect using it you CAN get a lot more out of it by being clever, but if all you do is stand there and hit things you'll do just fine. The only real advantage you have over him by doing the Fire Aura style of fighting is that because you're really throttling your motes back, over successive fights or even long fights you're going to have a lot left for the final battle.

                    If there's 2-3 action scenes in one, I dunno, palace assault or something lets say, and your friend full excellencies every join battle, every attack, every defense, and boosts them all with as many charms and evocations as he can, then he'll end fights fast and be out halfway through the second one.

                            You would've been happier if you'd chosen to join your mother in her world.

                            Here's my unsoliticted advice.

                            Swordplay is not "in Aspect" for Airs. Neither is Larceny and Investigation. You can do both, but splitting focus that much means you're not going to be good at both. If you want to be good at both you need to be looking at Water or Fire Aspects. I've dropped Larceny and Investigation from this sample build.

                            When you hold a Hattori Hanzo sword, you compare it to every sword ever made, that was not made by Hattori Hanzo.

                            Itto wields a practical battle-sword in an era where decorative, stylized weapons are the norm. Now Shining Ice Mirror is a simple, practical katana, but I don't think it's a good fit. In the manga (and films) his sword attracts everything from condescending curiosity to pants-wetting-terror: it's an ugly weapon built to kill, a weapon of war in a time of peace. For me, I think Gorgon translates this well.

                            I threw on a warboomerang. Javelins, short spears or hatchets work just as well.

                            At night, we make a fire, and have our tea, and we listen for the ninja, who never make a sound.

                            Tactics:
                            The idea is to stay Balanced and use Evocations as your combat workhorse. You're good at Stealth, Awareness, Athletics. You've got a ranged option if you need it. You don't completely suck at intellectual pursuits (especially if you can stunt thinking on your feet). You are a straightforward and forthright warrior... which plays socially as you'd expect.

                            You suffer from a limited mote pool and a lack of Onslaught negation.

                            You want to spend WP to wake Gorgon. Wind Rider's Swiftness to close in. Then Excellency and Stone-sinew to lock your target down. The Belt stops you getting mobbed by mortals. You want to pick on battle groups first to build Initiative and Wakefulness.
