                                                            Originally posted by Shaddar View Post

                                                            You'd need to both be able to take advantage of a supporting Intimacy to raise it from Minor to higher, and you'd need to be holding back some evidence you could use to justify strengthening it that's significantly greater than what you used in your initial roll.
