                                            I am not trolling you, I am seriously concerned because prioritizing a nuanced, mature, complex setting over a setting that's actually fun to play in ruined 2e as much as it's clunky combat and diplomacy mechanics did.

                                            This kind of prioritizing is the reason the Bronze Faction seems unambiguously right about the Solars in 2e, why the Realm's "White Man's Burden"-type excuses for it's behavior are actually taken seriously by the writing, it's the reason there's a brothel in the 2e Underworld filled with the polymorphed ghosts of rapists who, as punishment, are themselves raped and the text implicitly treats this as a form of just punishment.

                                            So, you'll forgive me if I'm a bit crass when it comes to this mindset. Shit ain't always gotta be complex. Sometimes the returning heroes from an Age long past are actually heroes and not the inevitable bringers of the apocolypse. Sometimes the evil empire is actually an evil empire and not a benevolent bringer of infrastructure, education, and medicine. Fucking rape is always bad using it as a punishment for rape isn't a point in favor for the complexity and maturity of the setting, it's just something else I have to awkwardly explain to potential players that it exists in Exalted.

                                            Complexity is stupid and unhelpful when implies the baby-eating, civilization-destroying, Wyld-tainted evil witch is actually what the setting considers a positive force in Creation so your PCs shouldn't try to defeat her; in fact, according to some text, she exists primarily to potential mentor for a PC sorcerer instead of an evil to be vanquished.
