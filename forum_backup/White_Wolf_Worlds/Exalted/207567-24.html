        Neither of those statements is necessarily true. You’re just not using your imagination here, Alucard.

        Take, for instance, the Myriarchate of Tethys, an autonomous province in the far north. In centuries past, the region was a tributary of the Realm, and over time, the blood of dragons found its way into the local aristocratic families, many of whom received the Grace of the Air Dragon. When the Great House to which the Myriarch paid tribute fell out of favor with the Empress, their wealth and power was broken, and their legions and garrisons withdrew, leaving their former tributaries to fend for themselves.

        Into this tableau, in RY 522, came Masuda Storm-Thrush, an outcaste from the coastal states. The bastard daughter of an exceptionally well-bred Mnemon, Storm-Thrush was not permitted adoption into the house of her Exalted kin because of her relatively low birth to a serving woman, and because, in their judgment, she represented only contamination, being, unusually, an Aspect of Air. Filled with scorn for her dynastic relatives, she led a tempestuous and bloody life as a mercenary, hacking her way around the battlefields of Creation before one day finding herself a dragonlord, leading the forces of then-Myriarch Tarsios IV Tzymenides in defense against the fae.

        Rewards and honors followed her victories, and it was around this time that she met, or rather became re-introduced, to the man who would in later years be her husband, a wealthy townsman who was, in fact, the Chosen of Luna called Rain Falls in Silence.

        The two were a perfect match, and in centuries to come rarely left each other’s side. Rain’s wealth and Masuda’s rising prominence ensured that their children (of whom several Exalted) married well, integrating their blood into that of the local Dragon-Blooded aristocracy. Two centuries later, their children, grandchildren and great-great grandchildren have become a minor dynasty in the region. And with no slavery or rape required.
