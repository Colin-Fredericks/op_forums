                                    You're a better judge than I on if you've only ever met people between 2 and 5 on the Kinsey scale, but there's a bigger world out there, it consists of plenty of 1s, 6s and asexual people, and their sexual feelings aren't really mutable.

                                    Originally posted by Hark
                                    The setting is loaded with characters that are into pretty much everything.
                                    It has a few. More than many settings would give consideration to. The exact number has always been greatly exaggerated within the fandom. I find a lot of the ways in which people have talked about the whole "everybody in Creation is bi" to be informed by wider attitudes that are not respectful to bisexual people.

                                    Originally posted by Hark
                                    The setting is incredibly cynical about people and power. Take that away and you just have shiney people hitting eachother with surfboard sized swords.
                                    There are a few things to unpack in this.

                                    One is that this Edition is the least cynical about that. I referred to Ma-Ha-Suchi earlier, and he's not the only Lunar in that book who interacts in a less absolute manner with people. Fangs at the Gate depicts a number of rich, engaging social scenarios that wouldn't exist in any other setting while still being predicated on images of power being employed responsibly and respectfully.

                                    You still brought presumptions about people and power that were framed not merely as things present in the game, but how you would expect people to act and things that ought to be in the game. As well as just general ideas of how sexual interplay works out; framing seduction in terms of something that a person has to and fails to resist because of a weak fortitude that is inflicted upon them by another is not merely weird and outputting, it's extremely narrow. It should not be at the core of anybody's argument about how these things work in the game.

                                    That's not to say that nobody can ever play a bastard character who acts like that with people, but it shouldn't be considered the default and it has issues that need to be managed carefully.

                                    Consent is something that is essential to sexual interaction; if not more than many other kinds of social activity, then at least something that has a long ugly history of being treated as less than. Even the best game rules will have difficulty making it feel as though consent is being portrayed credibly when the mechanics are still predicated on an actor leveraging resources against a passive target, finding the precise sequence of actions and phrases that unlocks a sexual response. Even if this is not what it is in the fiction, it's close enough to reasonably feel murky to people that have good cause to be really cautious about the matter of consent. That's not just people with direct or indirect traumas, who are undeniably a part of the hobby (an increasingly vocal part); we all benefit from continuously reinforcing that consent is paramount.

                                    So the Red Rule is there to serve as the final word on it, the final line to establish or deny consent, with the statement that players and Storytellers respect that.

                                    The concerns that somebody might playact withholding consent for mechanical advantage, or to deny another player the same? Well, I suppose that somewhere out there in the hobby there's somebody who's weird enough about playing games to do that, but they shouldn't really be the primary concern in discussing the idea. They're an outlier that should be dealt with in a case by case basis, not a basis for arguing the removal of a system of giving project to establishing consent.

                                    And I want to say outright that I find prioritizing the optimization of one's build over the value of that boundary is not a good look.
