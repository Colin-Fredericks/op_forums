                    EA gets 9 successes, attack hits. Damage pool of 22. Volfer repeats his use of Spirit Strengthens the Skin (4m) to boost his soak to 13.

                    EA: 17i (13p/1ph, 2 WP, 3a)
                    Volfer: 3i (4p/0ph, 2 WP, prone)

                    Volfer flurries rise from prone, spending 1 willpower to make sure he makes it, with an unenhanced withering attack, rolling 9 dice after the flurry penalty.

                    EA’s parry is 7.

                    Mote regen
                    Anima flux does nothing

                    EA: 28i (4p/0ph, 0 WP, 3a)
                    Volfer: -4i (0p/1ph, 0 WP, prone)

                    Volfer flurries rise from prone with a withering brawl attack. He makes the rise from prone and rolls 10 dice. EA uses a 1m Excellency and Blinding Spark Distraction (1m, 1i) for a parry of 8. Volfer rolls 3 successes, misses and is blinded.

                    TURN 5:
                    Mote regen
                    Anima flux inflicts 1 HL and knocks Volfer prone
                    EA: 25i (2p/5ph, 0 WP, 3a)
                    Volfer: -4i (0p/6ph, 0 WP, blinded, prone)

                    EA makes a decisive attack using a full Excellency (4m) for 19 dice. Volfer uses 6m Excellency to boost his parry to 4 (Parry of 4 + 3 Excellency + 1 Stunt – 1 prone – 3 blinded).

                    6 successes, attack hits. 25 initiative give 13 successes, Volfer is down.



                    Notes



                    - I may have screwed up here in turn 2 on the melee excellency when Volfer hit – if I don’t use the charm (having already used it that turn) but fail to parry does that attack count in terms of resetting the discount?

                    - Dragon-Scale Shield giving heavy weapons a boost was the difference in turn 1.

                    - Rumbling Earth Rebuke worked here and is powerful if held back for when motes are low, but obviously situational – Dragon-Graced Weapon or Call the Blade make it an inconvenience. Still, not having to give up an attack but still being able to use a disarm and turn your typical white room Melee 5, Dex 5 nightmare sword fighter into a Dex 5, Brawl 0 target is nice. Perversely, Volfer, as non-optimised as he is, actually has some brawl and was only knocked back a couple of pegs in effectiveness. I ‘declared’ Rumbling Earth Rebuke in the initial attack – I probably need to clarify if that’s necessary with devs.

                    - Speaking of white rooms, one-on-one duels in particular, I think DB melee probably does ‘better’ in these than it would in an actual game with extras, battle groups, archers or the like – the excellency discount and the clash enhancing charms thrive in this environment.

                    - I really like Pasiap Preempts Haste – charms that help you do two things (win JB and improve your first attack) are okay with me.

                    - I’ve thought this before, but earth’s anima power seems OP to me. Leaving aside the soak, being able to knock people prone is a most massive debuff, no?

                    - Benchmarking builds against Volfer – as has been noted elsewhere he doesn’t have all his charms/merits etc so I should probably not do that any more as that way lies non-fun ‘If you can’t beat Volfer are you even Exalted?’ thinking. Also, while this fight was straightforward, as maligned as he is as a Dawn, Volfer has a good shot at landing that opening attack on a pretty-near optimised DB and then going into a One Weapon, Two Blows combo to suck defensive motes and potentially inflict a wound penalty. The clash could have gone his way too with a little luck. Still think the DB would win if either of those had happened, he has too many health levels and artefacts, but Volfer’s not that bad – y’know, like Scroll of Exalts Solars bad. He’s incomplete.

                    - I've got to get better at writing these up if I'm going to do any more of them...
