    I'd like to see a medicine charm or two that benefits from friendship ( can't remember if intimacies are still a thing or not). Like being able to treat a circlemate while in combat, because you two have worked together so much, you have an instinct for where to be to not get in the way. Removing wound penalties or committing x motes/willpower to the injured person,not assigning temporary health levels like in 2nd edition, but actually sliding the damage back.
    (piss poor example but it's the best way I can try to explain.)

    Battle Hardened Patient Bond
    cost: 2m/5m
    duration: one scene
    The alliances and friendships forged upon the field of battle overcome the strongest foes and bolster through the most grievous wounds. A Solar may activate this charm and direct the effects at an ally whom she has an intimacy towards. for 2 motes she can give up to her Intimacy+Essence in temporary basing health levels. These levels only hold damage that has already occurred, thereby clearing the target's normal health track. Any health levels over the damage taken are converted to bonus dice to the target's next roll to stop bleeding, maintain consciousness, resist poison. For 5 motes per dot, the Exalt can give lethal health levels. The motes spent on this charm are committed until the scene ends, at which point the motes and the damage returns to the normal health track.

            Well, to get the 2e-ism out of the way first, it's not a medicine charm but rather a part of medicine. I liked and would like to see again is the ability for the player to define how they do medicine. acupuncture, herbalism, using leeches for everything... anything was fair game. I liked that. It allowed you to do everything from the Solar British Sergeant-Major style that shouts at his men until they get better, to the classics like acupuncture all the way to doing drum circles to align spiritual energies.



            That out of the way, a Solar medical master should have a robust understanding of how people work, be it mentally, physical, emotionally. Indeed, this should spill over to animals, spirits and anything else. They should have an understanding of how organic group systems work, leading them to an understanding of the spread of diseases of the mind body and soul, the natural spread of life in general, be it modeling the growth of a village for the next few decades or the growth of moss on a tree.

            From these concepts, we get into effects. A Solar should be able to restore ability to an old crippled limb, but by the same token they should know exactly where to strike in combat to disable a limb or function. Improve health and condition, the classic healing and medical treatment.

            They should be able to talk with a man or a spirit and in time dig out the causes anger or fears and work with them to sooth them. By the reverse token they should be able to study people and gain insight into their mental and emotional triggers.... such as a gang member's blind hatred of the gang leader and the deep seated desire of the former for the latter's position. Thus giving you the insight that if you take out the leader this other one will step up and his hate will likely keep him from doing what the previous leader thought was a good idea*.

            They should be able to guide the growth of individuals and groups. The whole raise 30 generations of selectively bred plants/animals in an instant kind of thing where you bring forth specific desired traits. There should be the ability to encourage or prevent disease in an area or in a population. To analyse needs and requirements for a desired effect now and in the future... For example, knowing that the animals are doing poorly due to a lack of a particular substance or mineral, and have an idea how it could be addressed. Or what diet to feed them to make the goats far more productive of milk.

            And just as Solars are leaders of men, so too should their medical skills in some case be able to treat collective groups. Curing an individual of a plague is something a skilled mortal can do. Curing a city of the plague is something a Solar should do.




            Going up against a Solar with a medical focus in a physical conflict should have people quaking in fear. Not because they hit hard, not because they'll convince you to stop with clever words, but because when they do hit you, they know exactly where to hit and in what order to do everything from disable your leg or liver, all the way up to stopping your heart.

            Going up against a Solar with a medical focus in a social conflict should have people quaking in fear. Not because their words are honey'd traps, not because they'll convince you to sell your own grandmother, but because they know every injury done to you by yourself, others or just time. Mental, physical or emotional they can offer you a cure for any or all of it... for a price. Worse it can go in the other direction. Your enemies can be made to suffer plagues, gain addictions to substances you alone control... or worse.

            Going up against a Solar with a medical focus in a kingdom vs kingdom conflict should have people quaking in fear. His people are always at the peak of physical fitness. They have no untreated vices or additions to exploit. They eat better and live without the periodic rampages of terrible disease that cripple other armies. His people are content, have a fantastic quality of life... and are willing to fight anyone that tries to take that from them and their families.


            *-yes I'm aware this is edging into socialize.

            Odd_Canuck is not a topical medication or food product and is not to be taken internally or seriously.

                    More Medicine Charm Ideas

                    * The Solar become extremely adept at handling poisons and diseases you have treated before. Any mundane disease or poison you have successfully treated may be banished from the target with but a touch and a few motes.

                    * Guided by her will and intuition, the Solar uses local resources to create a powerful medicine that can be applied to food, drink, crops and so on. Roll Intelligence + Medicine and re-roll X until there are no more X dice. If the roll meets or exceeds the highest (Virulence/Poison) difficulty, all hazardous effects are removed. This is a single application good enough for one person. This Charm can also created enough medicine to effect a large population by adding +Y motes and 1 Willpower. When using the Charm in this fashion, it creates enough medicine to effect up to (meaningful number) people/miles/places.

Solar Charms thrive on a logos of action and dramatic effect. This is what allows them to be so powerful. Taking away the need for logos does make them more powerful, but it also makes them into miscellaneous wish-powers, which by turns makes them uninteresting, boring, and disappointing. A large part of this is going to be on you, and the Storyteller. Making things interesting requires a little elbow grease and creative use of the system and Abilities at large. No Charm, no matter how well written, will ever suffice.
