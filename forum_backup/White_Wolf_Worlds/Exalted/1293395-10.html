        Originally posted by Kelly Pedersen View Post


        It's definitely a tricky question! Given that great cats are canonically examples of a 2-dot familiar, it's tricky to find creatures that have better stats that aren't Legendary Size. Given that Lunars can take the form of mutated animals, you might end up having to draw from that well a fair bit - claim that you went into the Bordermarches and Sacred Hunted a tiger that was perpetually on fire or something. That said, there's a couple of creatures in the books that I'd peg as 3-dot Familiars without being Legendary Size.
        • The pestletail doesn't have the dice pools for combat that a great cat does, but its damage is very nice, and its Smashing Tail Hammer latent ability is enough by itself to push it up to 3-dot Familiar for me.
        • A hippopotamus, for similar reasons to the pestletail: high damage if it lands a hit, and an impressive natural ability in Bone-Crunching Bite.
        • Northern ursidae (from Hundred Devils Night Parade) strike me as 3-dot familiars, since they're both very impressive bears and can swim underwater indefinitely.
        Those are all I can find that I'd personally rate as 3-dot Familiars, though.
        Just to note Kelly, all the animals you have mentioned are Familiar 2 creatures by word of Dev.

        It’s been confirmed that to be Familiar 3, you either need Legendary Size or be a Familiar 2 creature with a magical ability.

        Familiar 1 creatures are anything you expect a Disney Princess to be cavorting with.

        To answer the OP: For Lunar Shapechange there is nothing canonical for a 3 dot. I’d go with what Wizard says (forgive me it’s early)

                                    Unusual Hide is good for everything obviously (though of the above list Cat and Strix want it the most), as are four dot medium natural weapon like some horns or antlers for +1 brawl parry (though cats defend with evasion).

                                    For a pestletail maybe moose/elk for some big medium antlers with natural shield so you can flurry full defense without the flurry defense penalty or initiative cost.

                                    Vance has said Deadly Weaponry mutation can be applied to any natural weapon in the final product so that will be good for any animal. You could make your Hippo tusks piercing or chopping weapons on top of crushing bite for complete overkill withering attacks. Or make your Pestletail or Siege Lizard tail-slam Flexible to dunk on some Crane stylists.

                                    Imposing Features and/or Frighting Voice works well for any predatory form thats going to use Predators Menace and instill ties of fear to itself. So Sky Tyrant/Tyrant Lizard/Hellboar could use it.

                                    Chameleon is good for kitty to be even stealthier.

                                    Extra Limbs is good for anything that might want to flurry.

                                    Tempered By the Elements if an animal is chimeric with an animal native to a different environment is going to be really good if you find yourself in that environment a lot.

                                    Fast Reflexes if a nice merit that works as a mutation for some animals but requires you to start combat in your spirit shape which works fine for kitty but less so for spirit shapes that are inconvenient to stay most of the time.

                                    Centaur is a really good mutation but it's going to be a stretch to justify on anything but say a Gorilla.

                                        Its a trick I pulled for the build I just posted but Enhanced Senses (X) OOO gives you two extra dots to every Perception based roll using (X) sense.

                                        You can make Join Battle a Perception Based roll with the charm Unwavering Predator's eye. So if you can stunt it so that your keen eyesight gives you the edge when violence starts, its an automatic 2 free dice on every join battle. (More bang for your buck than the similarly priced Danger Awareness merit from the Ex3 core)

                                            So continuing on for G'intora na Yas, the derived Statistics (pre-stunt)!

                                            Defenses:
                                            Parry: 6 (7 in HBT due to Medium weapon Claw, which she can be in all the time due to Moon's Hidden Face, 6 in DBT)
                                            Evasion: 4
                                            Soak: 12 (17 in HBT, 22 in DBT) (5 Stamina + 2 Stone of Resilient Bamboo + 5 Silken Armor + 5 Unusual Hide in HBT + 5 Strength in DBT)
                                            Resolve: 4 (5 if social partner has Resolve <5)
                                            Guile: 2
                                            Health Levels: 0, 3x(-1), 3x(-2), 3x(-4), Inc

                                            Movement:
                                            Rush: 9
                                            Disengage: 7

                                            Offense:
                                            Join Battle: 9 (11 if it can be stunted that keen eyesight contributes)
                                            Unarmed Attack: Accuracy +15, Base Damage +12, Overwhelming 1 (Bashing, Brawl, Grapple, Natural)
                                            Claw: Accuracy +13, Base Damage +14, Overwhelming 1 (Bashing, Brawl, Grapple, Natural)

                                            General:
                                            Essence: Personal (16/16), Peripheral (32/38), 6 committed (4 Silken Armor +1 Stone of Resilient Bamboo +1 Gem of Crushing Might)
                                            Willpower: 5

                                            Intimacies:
                                            Defining Tie: Martial Arts (Deep and Abiding Passion)
                                            Defining Principle: I will seek the guidance of my learned Silver Pact Elders and treasure their advice. (This was from her duel with Vanamaithri's Crane Style)
                                            Defining Principle: I cannot hate anyone who fights me with their whole soul!
                                            Major Tie: Luna (Gratitude and Reverence)
                                            Major Tie: The Slumbering Populace of the city in the Deep Wyld, Lost Yasal. (Protective Steadfastness)
                                            Major Tie: Lilith (Immense Respect and Admiration)
                                            Major Principle: The Strong defend the Weak.
                                            Minor Tie: Dragonblooded (Ambivalent Affection *The Royal Line of Yasal was historically Earth Aspected, as were both of her parents, doesn't change the fact that they are currently trying to kill her*)
                                            Minor Tie: The First Age (Nostalgic Longing)
                                            Minor Principle: I hate being called and treated as a Princess, even though that is technically what I am.

                                            All right I think that just about rounds her out. G'intora na Yas is a frightening combatant who is always at least in HBT (she can hide all her mutations with Moon's Hidden Face and just look like a, admittedly physically perfect, normal young adult), and who hits like a truck even at this stage with not even half of the martial arts charms that contribute to big withering damage.

                                            Her maximum withering damage output in DBT is: 12 (Base) + 5 (DBT) + 5 (Str Excellency) + 4 (DPC) + 5 (Snake) = 31 base withering damage for 11 motes expended

                                            In the short term she should pick up 3 points of MA: Wood Dragon so she can get Eyes of the Wood dragon and add her 5 perception onto the above,as well as rounding out her soak suite in Stamina and pick up the counterattack charm Coiled Serpent Strikes in Dexterity, so when she gets a massive bank of initiative with the above, she can turn around on the same turn and deal it back as a massive Decisive counterattack if anyone misses her.

                                            Of course she isn't completely optimized, I did make some concessions to backstory (Yasal Crystal) but overall, decently pleased. Simple concept, well executed.

                                            Also reasonably pleased that my basic guiding principle of Shonen Manga Heroine worked out quite well as a backbone to build her up.

                                            Anything I should have done differently?
