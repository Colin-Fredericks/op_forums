                        I played an Essence 5 Solar with Godspeed Vanguard and most of its evocations (and compatible Solar powers like some Melee, Athletics and Stealth charms).

                        I used it in one fight; one where it wasn't really necessary, I just wanted to show off.

                        The problem is they're just do damn awkward to get around. I had a charm+evocation that meant I was able to go at 100 mph, and specifically chose Godspeed Vanguard because it can do so (most Warstriders are slow; it's not like your sorcerer friend can carry you in his Stormwind Rider, or your Eclipse friend can carry you on his super-fast elephant), though I still had to invest in Athletics charms.
                        100 mph probabably doesn't seem so bad. But travelling can cause damage, so you don't want to travel much. And if you're sneaking somewhere, or going on a diplomatic mission, you can't take it with you. At least with artefact armour you can get a charm to keep it in elsewhere, but that's not possible with a Warstrider.

                        Then there's the awkwardness of fixing them. To even be able to do so practically, you need a lot of investment in Craft. Even with that, I rarely had the time to fix it up, and it ended up covered in lava and thus needing a full repair, which I never had time for.
                        And all that Craft really cut into my other abilities' charms.

                        And then of course you need to buy the Warstrider (a level 5 artefact) and have a Greater Hearthstone (4 dots, 5 with a Manse) to power it.

                        A Lunar though? They can fly as a small bird into the middle of the enemy camp and go full Kaiju. They can attend a decadent soiree at the Queen's palace, and then when demonic assassins attack break out their Ultimate Form. They can take on a Mothra form and carry the rest of their party on their back across the ocean without taking ever-increasing damage.

                        So, to get to the level of a Solar warstrider pilot with 10 evocations, I expect you'll need 15 charms, because you've got the advantage of convenience and not having to invest in a bunch of Craft charms and 9 Merit dots.


                        But I do expect that, even it requires more charms, you will be able to create a high-Essence Lunar that can challenge a Warstrider in Kaiju vs Mecha combat, if that's what you want to build. Not just for the look of it but because there's no way an Essence 5 Full Moon with 30 combat charms isn't a combatant of earth-shaking power. Considering how many charms were in the Solar and Dragonblood books, Lunars are going to have a ton of charms, and if, say, a quarter of them are combat charms, that'll be a hell of a lot of combat charms. And, like Solar combat charms and Warstriders, not all of them are going to be relevant to Kaiju combat (DBT upgrade charms aren't useful) but most of them will.
                        It's not like they're going to put a "Legendary Size - No" keyword on.

                                                            Originally posted by Calboon View Post

                                                            Presumably it would just limit how much your can increase your natural soak up to the animals, like what dexterity does for the dice pools
                                                            I dunno, maybe, but considering you don't get your armor while transformed and QC pools aren't broken into 'natural' and 'armor' soak, you'd end up capping your soak at really low values while transformed. For an example, even a bear has soak 10 which would be capped at 5 normally or 10 with a stunt. Which means you need two traits at maximum and you need to stunt every time you want your soak above 5. Without stunting, someone with light armor is usually going to match or beat that, while someone with light artifact armor will always beat that.

                                                            It also gives rise to the oddity that a lunar who transforms into an Armored Terror doesn't get much in the way of soak.

                                                            Hell, consider a combat-focused lunar who hasn't maxed out stamina. It's hard to consider someone 'combat focused' when their soak is three.

                                                            Edit: I later realized that I'd been borking the math here.the lunar's dice cap for natural soak would be their staminaX2. So a Stamina 2 lunar cap would be 4, or a stamina 3 lunar would be 6. So a stamina focused lunar still couldn't get full benefits from something like the Armored Terror (Soak 17) but with appropriate stats and a stunt could get pretty close (soak 15).
