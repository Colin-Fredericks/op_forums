                There are a great many ways to ruin a reputation, how would you like to go about it?

                Flawlessly Impenetrable Disguise (Larceny) lets you become Peleps Deled and do something terrible.

                Letter-Within-A-Letter Technique plus Essence-Laden Missive can make somebody inexplicably shout "Minemon is a pretender to the throne!" after looking at a drink menu. Add Voice-Caging Caligraphy and the words "I didn't mean to impugn Minemon's good name" for extra fun. Then there's, of course, forging unflattering letters and distributing pro-Anathema tracts in Peleps Deled's name. You don't need to beat your Exalted victim's Integrity, just the audience's.

                Regardless, you probably want some Socialize - Motive Discerning Technique, Shadow Over Day and Intent-Tracing Stare are good for figuring other people out while not being figured out yourself.

                Pick up Black Claw Style to triple down on being a manipulative prick, and give yourself some much-needed combat utility.

                        Writing out a session as a series of flashbacks is an awful lot of fun. I strongly suggest talking it over with your players ahead of time, to get buy in on the semi-railroading that needs to happen.

                        As for your character, he might want to plan ahead for a potential brawl to break out at the party as a consequence of his actions. That would suggest enough Brawl or Martial Arts to make your getaway. Planning to grapple might not be a bad idea, because klobbering your victim to death is a bad way to ruin them for life.

                        Some Larceny would be good for acquiring or planting damaging evidence. Ditto Athletics and Stealth.

                        All those together would probably be good for tackling scenarios other than what you're built-to-do, too.

                            Ultimately, you want Venomous Rumors, but making a beeline there right now is horrendously expensive in the sheer number of charms required, so let's lay the foundations. For this to work out, you need to..

                            1: Figure out who the mark's friends really are.
                            2: Understand what makes them tick.
                            3: Persuade them to turn on the mark
                            4: Avoid being exposed as a manipulative bastard.

                            For priority #1 and #2, you probably want Mastery of Small Manners, Motive Discerning Technique , Humble Servant Approach, Wise-Eyed Courtier Method, Unimpeachable Discourse Technique and Cunning Insight Method.

                            For #3, just having knowledge of your target's intimacies and a 10-die Manipiulation+Socialize pool goes a long way, but I'd also pick up Harmonious Presence Meditation from the Presence tree. because why wouldn't you want that?

                            For #4, I'd revisit Socialize for Shadow Over Day, at least, because 1m for +1 Guile is pretty great.

                            Leaving you 7 charms for other things. I'd consider grabbing a little Dodge and a Martial Art like Dreaming Pearl Courtesan or Black Claw, but Snake or even Tiger work fine too I suppose. The point is mostly to have a potent unarmed option in case fights break out while you're trying to look harmless.

                                I looked through the various martial arts and noted four which work for Shining Crimson Oath: Snake, Crane, Black Claw, and Dreaming Pearl Courtesan. They all have a general push for defense to start and a heaping helping of "Surprise, bitches!" when the inevitable fight breaks out, sometimes possibly in front of witnesses. Those witnesses are best mollified with either of Black Claw or Dreaming Pearl Courtesan, but it could be just as easy to say something to the effect of "One has to be able to defend oneself, right?" as an excuse for knowing either of Snake or Crane style. That said, I do have to profess a certain love for the Black Claw and Dreaming Pearl Courtesan styles with respect to how I envision Shining Crimson Oath.

                                Speaking of, it's (back)story time: Shining Crimson Oath was a slave. She grew up a child of slaves, learning the arts of servitude, style, and socialization, though the latter pair were as a result of observation rather than experience. She threw off her shackles and ditched the house she served, liberating herself one night through a bit of guile and luck. Not long thereafter, she was lucky enough to run into a Presence supernal Exalt who took Shining Crimson Oath under her wing, teaching [insert martial arts style here] to her while bringing a finer point to the social skills Shining Crimson Oath possesses. The Wyld Hunt rolled into town on unrelated business and her mentor couldn't resist messing with the group. It ended... poorly... Shining Crimson Oath went on the run as her mentor was captured after an unfortunate exchange that revealed her identity. Shining Crimson Oath has since honed her skills for social situations and vowed not to be so overconfident in her abilities.

                                Finally, thank you Amayad for the relative progression I'm looking for in Socialize and Presence. I have a couple of Performance and Larceny charms I'd like to pick up for Shining Crimson Oath, and I'm considering how Integrity might work out with her, too.
