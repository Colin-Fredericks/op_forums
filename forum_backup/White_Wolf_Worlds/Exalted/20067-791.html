    Originally posted by Jefepato View Post
    Has anyone combined Steel Devil Style with Fire Dragon Style? I'm not seeing a lot of obvious synergy, but have I missed anything?

    If nothing else, you can wear medium armor with both and use paired short swords with both. But that's probably of little use if they don't work well together.
    They’ve got great synergy. Steel Devil’s Triple Attack Technique needs 12/9/6/3 successes to hit an enemy with defense 5/4/3/2. In Fire Dragon Form that drops to 9/6/3/1 because the onslaught penalty gets applied before your attack not after.

    Also the join battle roll, for a hefty 15m you get to roll Join Battle twice, with 2-5 auto successes on each roll and pick the highest. If you win you gain an automatic point of steel devil charge and can reflexively enter Fire Dragon Form at the same time. Also because the wording on Flash Fire Technique says “if you make a decisive attack on your first turn.” you can attack with triple attack technique and then smash a Steel Devil Strike with 5 extra decisive damage.

                            Originally posted by prototype00 View Post
                            What would a Lunar look like if in Hybrid Body Transformation form but with the charm Moon's Hidden Face activated? A wild looking human? Or a beastfolk lacking any of the usual identifiers of beastfolk-hood?

                            I think it's going to depend on which mutations, exactly, are being given the Subtlety tag. If it's just a bear-totem Lunar with Unusual Hide and Fangs, she might just look like an unusually hairy human with a big smile. But one thing I, at least, allow for Subtlety is to allow the mutations to simply be very easily concealed. So a spider-totem Lunar with a bunch of extra spider-eyes might find that they all fit easily underneath a headband or hat, and that conveniently doesn't get taken off. Or a Lunar with Wings could stuff them underneath long clothing and not really create any suspicious bulges.
