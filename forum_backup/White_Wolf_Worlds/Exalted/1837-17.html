    While it's not as cool as a character portrait, I did find a medieval mapmaker who specializes in fantasy maps and custom work. His take on Creation is outstanding, and I'm happy to share his contact info with anyone who messages me.



    This one's not exactly a commission, as it was designed and produced totally in-party. Over the course of our campaign, our circle's picked up a few dots of Cult after saving a city or two. More than the willpower benefit was that of the in-game merch; all the temples of the area started carrying little idols of Grayson (Night-y Twilight hiding behind the wall) and Sil (Twilight building the base) and I (Dawn praising the sun). We figured it was a neat little trinket to make as "Party" favors for everyone in the game. I 3D-Printed and painted them, but I've our Twilight Sil to thank for all the awesome design work!
