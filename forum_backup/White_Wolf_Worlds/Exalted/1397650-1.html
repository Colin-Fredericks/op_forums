                                        06-21-2020, 08:08 PM
                                        Steel Devil was a last-minute addition and it's generally recognized as being Not Good, with the primary value of the entire style being the permanent +2 parry from Dual-Slaying Stance. That being said: someone with Parry 5 and an onslaught negator is not at all the intended use case of the style; that's specifically choosing the case where it's least likely to be effective, so it's not surprising that it's ineffective there! The style is intimately connected with onslaught both in that it relies on it for effectiveness and in that slapping tons of onslaught on enemies is one of its primary benefits, so if you omit that element it goes without saying it's a lot weaker. Yes, mundane characters often have defenses of 3-4 (the fact that they can spend WP or stunt to raise it isn't that important as this rarely happens in practice), but they usually have no way to prevent defense penalties, so you need to apply those for the multihit powers to be any good.

                                        All that being said: it is a pretty bad style. Double Attack Technique would be weak if it were free; at 3m it's only worth using against an opponent with very low Defense, or when you want to trigger one of the stronger follow-up effects like Steel Devil Strike. Practically speaking, the main point of Steel Devil Style is that you take Dual Slaying Stance and Twin-Blade Defense and parry like a fiend, which is very unintuitive but not that bad a gimmick assuming you buy your way there with Solar XP.
