                        I think the "granting mutations" bit is probably supposed to involve like, physically grafting wings onto the subject or something. It's not just "poof, you can fly now".

                        As such, I don't think it can alter individuals on the genetic level. The upper bound is breeding in new traits. Think selective breeding vs genetic engineering. Terrestrial sorcery can do the former (for magic stuff) but altering someone's reproductive capabilities would involve the latter.

                        It's not just a mechanical alteration, you're changing how a person's cells divide. That's something that occurs at the DNA level. So yes, it would require that the person be fundamentally restructured to be "biologically female", as you put it. Or at least, their new uterus would need to be.

                        You could probably do a uterus and ovary transplant, but then the kid would technically be the donor's child and that might mess up inheritance or some shit.

                            Having followed all of this, it seems a bit overly heated for what amounts to either nothing if it's part of your character's backstory that this was done for them, or something that's not particularly hard to pull off in-character as long as you have time.
