        The Nihilist Exalted are the broken, corrupted remnants of what were once Luna's Chosen. But gone are the days of their sacred hunts, of their grand rituals of spirit calling, of their culture, their poise, and their love. They have been twisted by their Neverborn masters, wretched remnants, mockeries of what once was. Where a Lunar would hunt a great beast for the thrill and joy of the act of hunting, the Nihilist kills the greats simply because it can. Where the Lunars entreated with gods as allies, the Nihilist perverts nature to call upon the dead. Where the Lunar once shapeshifted into the natural shapes of the world to gain their strengths, the Nihilist transforms into mockeries of the living, creatures no man should ever be forced to see. Where the Lunar once loved Creation, the Nihilist seeks to destroy it from within. Perhaps it is due to the madness the Lunars already hold in them, held over from Luna's madness - perhaps their exaltations were simply less resistant to the corruption. Regardless, these Exalted fully feel the madness of their masters.

        This Exalted type represents the insanity of the undying dead. Where the Abyssal Exalted represent the poise of the dead, their Nihilist siblings represent only the madness of death itself. This does not mean every one is a madman and unable to function - they simply hear the whispers of their masters in a way that would drive weaker men to the depths of madness. Where the Abyssal Exalted drag the dead back to life and turn the world into Shadowlands that mimic the Underworld, the Nihilist transforms themselves into monstrosities of the underworld that twist the mind of those who witness it.

                                I like to think a lot of "Why did the Sidereals do/not do X" could be answered by "because that is(n't) what the Vision of Bronze demanded."

                                The guaranteed survival of Creation demanded, per Fate, the murder of all the Solar Exalted and the prevention of their reincarnation. It also needed the Dragon-Blooded to sit in charge afterward.

                                If the Vision required the Lunars to be likewise locked away, the Sidereals likely would have found a way. As it stands, it didn't. This probably came as a relief to the Viziers; killing all the Solars was the hard part, sure, but needing to be thorough about both the Lawgivers and their God-Beast spouses would have been even more of a nightmare.

                                But let's say Fate did demand the imprisoning of the Lunar Exalted. They are hunted and killed and largely gone from the setting in between point A (the Usurpation), and point B (the Present Day). This is honestly not terrible in and of itself since the majority of this history is "Eh, things happen and go to shit, and the Empress rises to power in there somewhere." We'll also assume that something majorly setting-wrecking like "the Fair Folk have nothing to hold them back and just curbstomp Creation" happens, too.

                                Where it starts to get tricky is they actually step on one of the Solar's narrative shtick now, that of the returning champion of antiquity. You could argue that Exigents are doing this now, but while the Exigence itself is a sort of old thing making its return, these new divine champions don't have the same sense of legacy.

                                Without a mass exodus of the Lunar Exalted to the edge of the world, they don't have any elders anymore; the Sidereal Exalted become the only surviving host of Celestials with significant numbers who remember the First Age. Maybe--maybe--to highlight their ability to hide and blend in, they have one Elder somewhere, cagey and paranoid as hell, a skin-stealing horror no less messed up than Filial Wisdom, albeit better about hiding it. The Wyld Hunt is likely more aggressive, since they have a duty to hunt down the Lunars not just when they Exalt in their lands, or make themselves known. Driving them off is not an option.

                                We probably drop Wyld Taint as a thing. I doubt many people miss it. The moonsilver tattoos are completely gone as a splat-wide thing, just because there's no reason for them to be everywhere if there was no lunar culture to create or maintain them. Lunars may also drop the Casteless/shifting Caste thing, since
                                they no longer have the deep-rooted ties to the sorts of cultures where there's a sort of parallel between fixing of the caste and the rites of adulthood.

                                In short, a lot of what does exist to separate the Solars from the Lunars and give the latter their narrative niche disappears. The Lunar Exalted maintain a little difference in flavor from their shapeshifting charms, and maybe Luna still tends to Exalt people from barbarian cultures, but the sense of real tradition is gone. Second Edition Lunars were occasionally accused of being Silver Solars; this just aggravates the problem.
