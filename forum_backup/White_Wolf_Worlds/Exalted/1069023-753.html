                    Originally posted by Sunder the Gold View Post
                    Is it reasonable to usually (where it makes sense) require warstriders and sea monsters of Legendary Size to engage in Sail-based Pursuit actions to engage boats in combat, and then to require these large antagonists to succeed at broadside and ramming or boarding actions to attack the boats or the crews, respectively?
                    In some places, yes, although I think in other places the Storyteller might decide that regular combat is the more appropriate/fun way of handling things. I wouldn't require the pilot to roll Sail, though; straight-up Athletics should work for a warstrider in a naval pursuit.

                    If such an opponent attacks a small group of boats, it can only choose one to oppose with a naval maneuver. Must the other ships oppose it along with the target, or can they choose to “oppose” no one that round and abandon their ally so that they automatically win their rolls and potentially escape in the next round or two while the monster is busy?

                    I like that the RAW allow for cooperating boats to confound a single pursuer, delaying their own escape to attempt to escape together. But it’s more meaningful if there’s a cowardly alternative or the option for one boat to sacrifice itself for the good of the others.
                    As written, I don't think you'd get a benefit from opting not to oppose a roll against an allied ship.
