    Who were your first characters?

    12-12-2019, 04:03 PM
    In my quest to finally learn how Third Edition works, I'm updating my first two Exalted characters for the new system. I have a lot of fond memories of these two, so it got me curious about what everyone else's early experiences with the game were like. Tell me a bit out your first characters.

    My very first character, back in 2005 during the waning days of 1st Edition, was Ellario Last-of-Faith. He was a Zenith, who exalted when the local authorities purged the little cult he belonged to. This cult believed that pain was necessary to purge impurities from the soul, and it was Ellario's duty to perform the purification rituals. He wielded a dagger that inflicted more pain than damage. He had become so desensitized to pain that only his own dagger could inflict any on him (all his health boxes were -0's unless someone turned the dagger against them, then they became -4's). I maxed out on Ox Body Techniques during character creation so I wouldn't have to learn a bunch of Charms immediately. The rest were mostly Melee Charms. Ellario didn't last long in the game, as he soon discovered that founder of his cult was a Deathlord who promised to aid him in avenging them. So he went over to the other side and became an NPC antagonist.

    My second character was Heart-Melts-As-Snow, a demonlogist from the southern fringe of the Scavenger Lands. As a mortal, he conjured minor demons the lord of his homeland. One day, a summoning went wrong. The rampaging demon drained all the color from Snow, and left his wife pregnant. She died giving birth to a demonblooded daughter. Snow raised the child as his own, and she continued to follow him after he exalted as a Twilight. While this character primarily focused on Sorcery (this is where I fell in love with summoning), he also was quite proficient at Snake Style. He joined up with the Circle some time after Ellario vanished and continued with them to the end of their adventure. Along the way there were a few complications, notably when a censor of Yu-Shan called in a favor with the group's Eclipse to have Snow's daughter killed. The assassination when wrong, and she survived. When Snow learned of the party's betrayal and how extensive the conspiracy had been, he attempted to summon the Heart of Darkness to swallow recently reconquered Thorns. Luckily, he hadn't quite mastered that Spell yet so the city was spared his wrath until cooler heads could prevail. They worked out a new deal with the powers than be in Yu-Shan. Through some rigorous work, the daughter's essence was purified of the demon-taint and she transformed into a minor god rather than the demon they feared she would become. She got a gig as the Goddess of Exorcisms after that.

                                          My first character was a Dawn Caste named Chen Minxia, way back in the summer of 2001 (Good God).

                                          She was one of the Hundred Riders of the Demon, a mysterious supernatural master whose nature I never did quite find out. Taken into service as a child, she trained as a warrior, plundered villages and made off with slaves for the Demon...until one day the Riders attacked a caravan and a Dragon-Blooded Sorcerer among them fought back. Chen charged the Sorcerer as her fellows fell beneath his Death of Obsidian Butterflies. A peerless swordmaster, she matched the Exalt blow by blow until the Sun came, caught her up in a suspended moment of time and told her "Preserve the innocent by cutting out the wicked".

                                          Newly Exalted, she didn't kill the Dragon-Blooded and ended up saving him from the other surviving Riders, leading to a memorable chase scene where she eventually met up with a Solar Circle made up of a Night Caste...and three other Dawn Castes. Yes, four Dawns and one Night in one Circle.

                                          Our campaign rode harder than the Lord of the Rings and included such memorable moments as us needing to board an airship so we put one of our Dawns in a catapault, shot him up to it so he could knock the ship out of the sky, so we could get onto it. Chen's finest moment came when a corrupt Elemental Dragon Censor came after our circle with a surprise attack of spirits. As the only one with any Occult, Chen had learned Ghost-Eating and I'd just assembled my first Combo with it. And instead of fighting a holding action to keep the dragon at bay while the other Dawns rampaged through the dragon's forces, she single-handedly ganked it in the first round while burning 60 motes of essence in an all-or-nothing attack that paid off with buckets of successes.

                                          I've played many rpgs in my life but I've never loved a game like I love Exalted.
