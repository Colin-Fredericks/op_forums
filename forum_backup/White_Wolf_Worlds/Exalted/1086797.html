    Demac's Exalted Character Creation Tool

    05-02-2017, 05:33 PM
    So version 2.21 of my character creation tool is released. To those of you who are new to the tool, Demac's Exalted Character Creation Tool is specifically designed with the idea that you can go into the sheet and easily customize it to your game needs with just a little knowledge of Excel/Google Sheets.

    Version 2.21of my character creation tool is now available. I've made some major updates to it from version 2.2:
    • First and foremost: IT SUPPORTS DRAGON-BLOODED CHARACTER CREATION (I'll pretend I hear some applause)
    • You can now shift easily between creating a new exalt and an experienced Exalt at character creation.
    • Caste and Aspect abilities now automatically update when the Caste/Aspect field is changed. That means with Solars, the list of available options for each ability (Caste, Favored, or Unfavored) automatically updates. For Dragon-Blooded, Aspect Abilities are automatically marked as such. Contrast this with version 2.2 and before where you had to update your template after changing your caste and you'll see that the workflow is much smoother.
    • I corrected a mistake with the printable sheet that made it not update the Excellency/Specialty markers next to the ability name after character creation.
    You should also notice that some charm names and all merits appear in a list in the appropriate field. The charm names are only those charm names that require an exact spelling for certain calculations to work correctly in the sheet (i.e. sorcery/necromancy charms, Ox-Body Technique, and all the Dragon-Blooded Excellency charms). If the charm you want isn't in this list, then you shouldn't worry because you wont' get an error message for populating the field with a value not in the list.

    As of today (4/17/18), we don't have the sorcery merits that will be offered in Dragon-Blooded: What Fire has Wrought so they obviously aren't on the merit list yet. I'll do a quick update of that list when we get them. Otherwise, enjoy version 2.21



    DECCT is capable of handling these functions:
    • Easy to use menus that automatically displays and hides sheets based on whether or not you are using them.
    • Printable character sheets.
    • Martial Arts attacks are calculated using the highest Martial Arts ability.
    • Attacks made with a Martial Arts ability rated lower than your Highest Martial Arts ability.
    • Attacks made with non-combat abilities.
      • Example: The Burning Name
    • Soak and Health Levels calculations that are effected by Charms and Merits.
    • Charm calculations that automatically take into account free spells from sorcery charms.
    • Weapon Tags that automatically effect the appropriate attack and damage pools.
    • Automatic Bonus Point Calculation for New Characters.
    • Automatic XP Calculation
      • supports both regular and solar XP awards and calculations.
    • Custom Character Creation Rules
    • Custom Splat Creation (Found in the Engage the Exigence) menu option
    • Siege Weapons (weapon calculation and the pools needed to use them)
    • Customizing the Decisive attack pools that appear on the printable sheet.

                      Update (v 1.1)

                      Problem: Parry calculation would not calculate correctly when the same ability was used for two weapons of different weapon types. This most commonly manifest itself when I martial artist wanted to list their unarmed attack as well as a "Close: Medium" weapon and/or "Close: Heavy" mundane weapon. The vlookup formula would always use the the top weapon wielded by the duplicated ability.

                      Resolution: Added section in the Parry Calculation sheet to separate the weapon's Parry bonuses by ability and then changed the Parry calculation formula to take the highest bonus for that ability.


                      Problem: Parry did not calculated on Special attacks. This was a problem for those who wanted to use the Special attack feature to represent a second martial arts style instead of a ranged attack like The Burning Name or Death of Obsidian Butterflies.

                      Resolution: Added Special attacks to the Parry Calculation.

                                      12-03-2017, 12:51 PM
                                      Version 2.2 is out!

                                      This version added in the use of siege weapons to the equipment page. I also added the (Intelligence + War) and (Intelligence + Sail) dice pools into the decisive attack list (which automatically makes them available for withering attacks). This means you won't need to use the Special attack slot to get correct calculations for siege weapons. Be sure to add the "Siege" tag to your siege weapons in order to get the correct calculation.

                                      I've added a way to customize the decisive attack pools that appear on the printable character sheet. I did this because the addition of two extra decisive attack pools would have messed with the formatting of the printable sheet so instead of altering the formatting, I chose in increase the user's ability to customize their sheet. There are 6 slots on the printable sheet for decisive attack pools. If you place the number of the slot you want the pool to appear in next to the decisive attack pool on the "Character Traits - Attributes and Abilities" page (which can be accessed once you've converted your character to an experienced character), that pool will appear in the appropriate slot on the printable sheet.

                                      Changed the formula's I'm using to calculate damage. I'm doing this because I'm playing with ideas on how to create the ability to add customized weapons tags in the system.

                                      Additionally, people might notice that I now only have one splat for Dragon-Blooded instead of 3. I did this because the developers have made comments in the past about not having "tiered" character generation within a splat. Dragon-Blooded have 6 specialties available instead of 4. These changes were made in preparation for the Dragon-Blooded book (which is still a long way out).

                                      As always, I welcome feedback on the sheet and ideas about what can go into it in the future.
