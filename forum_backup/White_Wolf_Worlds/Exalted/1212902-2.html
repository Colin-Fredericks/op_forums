                THE MANTIS MATING DANCE
                Open Palm Caress
                Torn Lotus Defense
                Flexing the Emerald Claw
                Black Claw Form
                Storm Calming Embrace
                Iron Arm Block
                Crushing Claw Technique
                Mantis Form
                Joint-Locking Technique
                Birdsong Over Blades
                (Optional) Doe Eyes Defense
                (Optional) Grasping Claw Method
                (Optional) Grasping Claw Defense
                Total = 10 to 13 Charms

                Use Birdsong Over Blades with Flexing the Emerald Claw to poison the target, retain your initiative for the followup grapple, and give them -5 to resist the poison. Then establish a Grapple with Storm Calming Embrace. If you open with Open Palm Caress you can make this an ambush instead of spending a ton of motes to hit. If you then hit with the Grapple and they are not immune to poison/grapple, then they are probably dead.

                Total Cost:
                Hit with two decisives
                DECISIVE 1: Flexing the Emerald Claw + Birdsong Over Blades + Open Palm Caress Ambush = 9m, 1wp
                GRAPPLE GAMBIT: Storm-Calling Embrace + Crushing Claw Technique + Excellencies =5m, 1wp + Excellencies (max +8 on the attack roll, +5 on the Control Roll)

                EVERY TURN AFTER: 5m per turn for Joint-Locking

                This combo is fairly reliable as well. Its difficult to avoid ambushes, and the Grapple attack roll is going to average AROUND 14.3 Successes if you go all in and have maximun dex/ma.

                To improve on this combo, I would add the rest of White Veil. The Dragon Dies Screaming is a *far* better Poison... and, yanno, they stack and you can deliver both on the same attack. Thats also a lot more charms, so ymmv.

                Maxed, these three styles combine well offensively. Mantis' capstone works amidst Storm-Calling Embrace and the resulting -(A Lot) Onslaught can, via Tickling the Dragon's Throat, be persisted basically for the rest of the fight, assuming they somehow survive.

                        You know whats really slow? Poisoning and grappling. You know whats efficient? 20 HLs of damage in addition to actually rolling damage. Lets make that happen.

                        THE JUMPSCARE
                        Birdsong Over Blades
                        Owl Clutches the Night
                        Alehouse Memory Stance
                        White Veil Form
                        Blithe Unruffled Plumage
                        Blinded By Laughter
                        Nothing But Shadows
                        Seven Points of Weakness Strike
                        Ebon Shadow Form
                        Shadow Stepping Motion
                        Throat-Slitting Shadow Atemi
                        Monkey Leap Technique
                        Increasing Strength Exorcise
                        Thunderbolt Attack Prana
                        Total = 14 Charms

                        Its simple, you're enjoying a party over in a corner. Then, all of a sudden, you are somewhere within short range and someone is very dead.

                        Combining Blinded by Laughter and Throat-Slitting Shadow Atemi and Thunderbolt Attack Prana means that you make an Ambush (vs 0 Defense) where up to 11 Successes above defense are added to the damage roll. Every successes above defense converts a die of raw damage into an automatic success. Then, after all of that plus your actual initiative, you double your damage.

                        That said, this may be too many charms to be worth rushing.

                                                            Alright, take two!

                                                            Abilities:

                                                            C                                                             Brawl O
                                                            C Martial Arts (White Veil) OOOOO
                                                            C Martial Arts (Black Claw) OOOOO (Razor Sharp Nails)

                                                            Did you know that dots 4 and 5 in abilities is the best use of your BP during character creation? It’s true!

                                                            So a brief rundown:

                                                            Combat:

                                                            Martial Arts (White Veil/Black Claw)- it’s efficient to buy a
                                                            MA up to 5 at character creation if you are going to be getting high level Charms for it.
                                                            Dodge - Dodge is the main defence for all of these styles

                                                            Sideboard: Snake Style 3 dots. Replace Occult with this. Snake Styles first two Charms are great and synergistic for this character but not worth Deep investment.

                                                            Social:

                                                            Socialize - You need to max this for Guile.
                                                            Presence - You might as well max this to instill with Black Claw
                                                            Performance - A value purchase of thousand Courtesan Ways boosts both your Appearance Instills and your Instill attempts count for double. If you need to strip willpower quickly in Social combat, this is the charm for you.

                                                            Others:

                                                            Occult- value purchase for Sorcery easy access, as well as the charms that let you strike Immaterial Foes. With the Belt of Shadow Walking it lets you strike Material foes while Immaterial, which is as good as perfect Defense against certain foes.

                                                            Stealth- A lot of effects from White Ceil benefit Stealth, so you might as well be world renowned in it.

                                                            Merits:

                                                            Martial Arts OOOO
                                                            Artifact (Silken Robes) OOOO
                                                            Resources O
                                                            Artifact (Belt of Shadow Walking) OOO
                                                            Martial Arts and the Silken Armor are non negotiable. Since White Veil gets you one of the most mote efficient Stealth Enhancers, Belt of Shadow Walking gets you 19 Dice for Stealt Checks for a 4m expenditure. Not necessary but nice, as is Resources.
