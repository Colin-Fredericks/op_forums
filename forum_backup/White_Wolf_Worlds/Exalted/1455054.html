    Essence of Dynasty - Play Report and Shameless Fan-Fic

    07-16-2021, 07:47 PM
    Trigger Warning: This is a (mostly PG13, occassionally light R for language) summary of an X-rated play which deals with mature themes that some may find distressing. Violence, sex, and discrimination are central themes: if you think you might be distressed by this story then I urge you to read no further.

    ****

    Some time ago, I started a write up of my first 3E game Dynasty of Dovak.

    That game has now finished.

    The story continues in this game: my first Exalted Essence game.

    If anyone was following Dynasty of Dovak, it started as a one-on-one game with my partner, with us alternating the ST role, following our characters Ganan and Reya, and grew into a party of three as we suckered our siblings into playing Exalted with us. Ganan and Reya have now been semi-retired for actual gameplay (as we basically made them as eRPG characters rather than "play a tabletop game with people we're related to" characters), but I still want to write about their story. As such, there will be awful ReyGan fanfic chapters mixed in with the actual play report chapters. Reya's player has a new character, the Alchemical known as Champion 314. Gracious Pealing Melody's player and Cynis Falen Jinabar's players reprise their old characters from Dynasty of Dovak.

    If you haven't read Dynasty of Dovak, fear not...

    The Story Thus Far

    The Realm of the Dragon-Blooded has endured for 770 years. The first Empress, the Scarlet Empress, also known as the Old Empress, seized control of the Sword of Creation – an ancient weapon of a bygone age – and with it she saved the world from the brink of annihilation, and carved her empire into the world. The Scarlet Empress and her kin are Dragon-Blooded: mortals who have been Exalted with the power of the Five Elemental Dragons. For centuries, the Dragon-Blooded have ruled Creation as the Princes of the Earth: great champions who protect the world from the nightmarish, cannibalistic shapeshifters known as the Lunar Anathema, and other horrors, such as the soul-sucking Raksha, the shambling undead, and the vicious demons of Malfeas.

    And then, seven years ago, she vanished.

    In the ensuing civil war, her grand Dynasty tore itself apart, before her eldest surviving daughter, Mnemon, took the throne. Seven Great Houses make up the pillars of the Realm’s nobility, each presided over by a mighty Great House Matriarch. The Great Houses rule over their own kingdoms, in the Empress’s name, and send their “advisors” to the kingdoms of the Threshold – the barbarian lands that lay beyond the shores of the Realm’s Blessed Isle. Wise Threshold rulers accept these Satraps, and willingly turn over tribute to the Realm. But increasingly bold barbarians reject the Realm’s overtures and declare war. Mnemon’s Realm is surrounded, and slowly realizing that it is outnumbered.

    Worse, it may be outgunned.

    Five years ago, the Solar Anathema returned. These dread lords, god-kings driven mad with power, once presided over Creation. Their reign of terror was ended only at a great cost, and only by the intercession of the Five Immaculate Dragons, or so the stories go. The Five locked the Solars away, imprisoning their foulness in the very depths of the Underworld, far beneath the deepest depths of the Western Ocean – for if a Solar is slain, the essence of their power may pass anew to a mortal foolish enough to try to rise above their station and usurp the righteous rule of the Dragon-Blooded. But now the Solars are free. Some of them seem to be twisted by the powers of the Underworld: these Deathknights seemingly stand against life itself.

    And stranger beings exist.

    In defiance of the natural order, little gods Exalt their own champions. These mockeries of the Dragon-Blooded’s power are not always evil and are rarely to blame for their new condition – often they can be made into allies – but their numbers are growing, despite the terrible toll Exaltation takes upon its divine benefactor. The Demon Princes of Malfeas have somehow anointed their own champions and sent them force to do mischief in the world. The Exalted of Heaven, the secretive Sidereal Exalted, increasingly meddle in Creations affairs. And these are perhaps the least strange of those “stranger beings” that wander Creation.

    Our story follows three champions of Mnemon’s Realm: Alinos Gracious Pealing Melody, an admiral in the Realm’s Merchant Navy, responsible for collecting the tribute gathered from the Threshold kingdoms on behalf of the Great Houses, and ferrying that wealth across the water to the Blessed Isle; Cynis Falen Jinabar, once a Magistrate, handpicked by the Old Empress to dispense the Realm’s justice, now a fugitive from the Realm’s law, exiled from the home she loves; and 314, a being out of time, one of the Alchemical Exalted, a prototype for the Dragon-Blooded, created to fight in a war that ended thousands of years ago.

    The Great Houses

    As of Realm Year 770, there are seven Great Houses of The Realm, ranked in approximate order of power, they are as follows:

    Rulinsei – Earth that Forms the Bedrock of Empire
    Matriarch Rulinsei, the Empress’s sister, presides over the Great House that claims the most direct descendants of Empress Mnemon. House Rulinsei has a strong association with the element of Earth – the majority of its scions holding the same Elemental Aspect as the Empress herself. It is a House of builders and architects, that cleaves firmly to tradition and propriety. This nigh on fanatical adherence to the tried and true methods of the past helps to maintain the purity of the Rulinsei bloodline – it is here that perhaps the Blood of the Dragons flows the strongest.

    Berit – Water that Drowns the Dragon’s Foes
    Matriarch Berit is also sister to the Empress. Further she holds the title of Sei-i Taishōgun, or Shogun, meaning she commands the Imperial Legions – the Realm’s principle fighting force. House Berit is a new House, which lacks the storied legacy of some of the older Houses. A significant minority belong to the Aspect of Water, that of the House’s Matriarch, but the majority of those who bear the Berit name are adoptees from the Patrician classes.

    V’neef – Wood that Parts the Spray
    V’neef is the third sister of the Empress who commands her own Great House. House V’neef was founded toward the end of the Old Empress’s reign and once commanded the Merchant Fleet. The House now controls the Imperial Navy: the Realm’s fighting force upon the seas. Whilst House V’neef possesses great military power, the soft-power it wields by leading the Realm in culture and fashion is the true focus of the House. Only a decade ago, V’neef was principally a House of adoptees – but V’neef had her pick from the cream of the Realm’s Patricians, carefully cultivated, and this combined with her own potent bloodline, and no small amount of politicking, has seen a great many children born with the V’neef name. V’neef herself (a consummate explorer in her youth) is a Wood Aspect, as are many of her direct descendants, but Water Aspects are also particularly valued by the Realm’s naval House.

    Nellens – Fire that Stokes the Engine of Commerce
    Under the Old Empress, House Nellens was not a House of prestige. Named for a long departed mortal consort of the Scarlet Empress, and oft overshadowed by their rivals House Sesus, House Nellens managed to acquire a great deal of influence amongst the Realm’s patrician class, and with the wealthier of the Realm’s merchants. When House Ragara – the so-called “Imperial Bank” – set itself against Mnemon in the Realm Civil War, it was only natural that House Nellens step in to fill the void in the new order once Mnemon was seated upon the throne. This caused a small rise in House Nellens fortunes – though, thanks to debt forgiveness mandated by the new Empress for her allies, coupled with her enemies defaulting on their loans, this rise was not as large as some might have hoped. What caused a meteoric rise in House Nellens fortunes, however, was the absorption of House Sesus. Long had Sesus agents worked in the shadows to decrease the fortunes of House Nellens, now not only was the House’s biggest obstacle removed, but it was actively working in favor of the House. Traditionally, House Nellens is ruled by the Most August Conclave: an assembly of mortals and Dragon-Blooded. In recent years, House Matriarch Nellens Gazal, and her husband Oban (brother to the Empress), has assumed more direct control. Until the wedding of House Sesus to House Nellens, the Great House possessed no elemental aspect – now the great preponderance of Sesus blood in their ranks has shifted the House firmly to a majority of Fire Aspects.

    Ledaal – Air that Raised the Bones of Giants
    House Ledaal is another legacy House, founded under the reign of the Old Empress. Ledaal, Empress Mnemon’s niece, earned her House by exposing a treacherous plot hatched by the shapeshifting Lunar Anathema, and the House has stood as a shield against the darkness ever since. Though Ledaal died centuries ago, her House follows her example in courage, curiosity, and integrity. Ledaal Yasmet leads the House with the aid of her Flashing Tempest Council. Though foreign trade and unearthing mysteries are important to the House, nothing comes before their Shadow Crusade: an unending war against the Anathema. The Air Aspect is the most common in House Ledaal.

    Tepet – Wood that Grows into a New Age
    The origins of House Tepet predate not only Empress Mnemon, but the Realm itself – the House draws on traditions dating back to the Dragon-Blooded Shogunate. It is a martial house, named for a warrior who opposed the Scarlet Empress in the earliest days of her reign, who would fail to overthrow her and come to be her lover. It is a House built on exacting traditions of excellence, seeking enlightenment by walking the warrior’s path. It is a House that nearly fell from greatness. Once Aspected toward Air, and commanding the mightiest army of all the Great Houses under the Scarlet Empress, House Tepet was broken by one of the Solar Anathema – the Bull of the North. Its power shattered, the House was saved by its current Matriarch, Tepet Ejava, who committed the House’s troops to the support of Mnemon during the civil war and later marched the House Legions upon the Deathlord known as the Mask of Winters in the war’s aftermath. Members of the Fallen House Cynis were given the option of exile or joining Ejava’s House. Ejava declined renaming the House after herself, but welcomed the infusion of new blood. The House is now Aspected toward Wood (though Mela, the Elemental Dragon of Air, remains an important figure in the House’s traditions).

    Alinos – Air that Rises to Rarified Heights
    Matriarch Alinos is the Empress’s granddaughter, and House Alinos consists of her own daughters, granddaughters, and great granddaughters, as well as her mother’s daughters and their descendants, and all those who were once members of Great House Mnemon but who did not wish to join Great House Rulinsei. Alinos is Aspected toward Air, as is the majority of the House: where House Rulinsei are great builders of tradition, Alinos is a House of innovation which builds to reach greater heights. It is the smallest, and youngest, of the Great Houses. House Alinos commands the Merchant Navy, collecting the tribute gathered by the other Houses and ferrying that vast wealth into the coffers of the empire.

    House Rules

    The Exalted can just spend a point of Essence to raise their Anima by 1 if using a Charm to do so would not make sense.

    The Soulsteel Anima power is not cap-breaking.

    Anima flux doesn’t hit other Dragon-Blooded.

    Chimera Soul Expression doesn't let you Double Down.
