    Dance Fighting as Performance Charms, not a Martial Art Style

    04-22-2018, 09:35 AM
    Solar Exalted have Battle-Dancer Method, an instant Charm which allows them to boost their Defense if they're dancing.

    Dragon-Blooded have Petal-Strewn Pavane, an instant Charm which allows them to boost their movement action dice-pool with their Performance and reflexively take a Performance action without penalty.

    There is technically nothing preventing each of them from having a version of the other's Charm.

    There is currently only one Martial Arts Charm that works like this: Hearing the Heart's Song, in Silver-Voiced Nightingale Style, which only works with Evasion. It was printed within the same book as Solar Performance's Battle-Dancer Method, and remains fully compatible with it.

    But despite having their own Charms which use Performance rolls, Dreaming Pearl Courtesan and Black Claw Styles do not use the Performance Ability to enhance Defense, and none of the three Styles use it to enhance movement dice-pools.

    Native Performance Charms such Battle-Dancer Method and Petal-Strewn Pavane therefore constitute a natural and effective complement to these three Styles, as well as any others you might want to characterize as "dancing".

    No single Martial Art Style could possibly cover all imaginable forms of dance-battling, since no Martial Art can be weapons-agnostic, and one can dance with just about any weapon imaginable. Sword dances, spear dances, pole dances, ribbon dances, whip dances, fire wand dances, bare-handed battle dances...

    Further Performance Charms could enhance a few other combat actions.
    • Rules as written, it is possible to flurry a Full Defense action with a dancing-based Performance action without needing a Shield-tagged weapon. A Charm could explicitly allow this flurry while removing the usual flurry penalties as per Petal-Strewn Pavane.
    • Another Charm could allow you to reflexively perform an appropriate dance-based Performance following an attack. It would need to be an aggressive and intimidating dance if aimed at the target of the attack, or a galvanizing, encouraging dance aimed at an ally who saw you attack the mutual enemy. In that, it would be very much like Voice of the Nightbird, save that the Performance Charm does not in and of itself provide a weapon, much less a ranged weapon with infinite ammunition.
    • Perhaps the final possibility is a Charm to reflexively take a Performance action together with Defend Other; you take your ward's hands in your own as you lead them into a dance where you interpose yourself between them and any danger.
    • I am skeptical of a Charm which would flurry an Aim action with a Performance action.
    There would be nothing like a Martial Arts Form Charm. Dancing would remain as compatible with any Form Charm as before.

    Currently, the only source of magical dances which one might want to maintain, unbroken, for the length of an entire battle are found in Dragon-Blooded Performance; Charms with a duration of "one performance", such as Heart-Strengthening Defense, Harmonious Life-Affirming Song, Irresistible Whirlpool Diversion, Mesmerizing Siren Call, Life-Spirit Symphony, and Battle Anthem of Ten Thousand Dragons (which is technically a song, but you can sing and dance with the same Performance action).

    Combat dance Charms might help with maintaining such dances, though those Charms are a little confusing in their clauses. Either it's not actually possible to flurry them with anything but Miscellaneous actions, or the Charm writer thought that social influence actions in combat are supposed to be Miscellaneous actions. In any case, it's not clear how they're supposed to interact with Petal-Strewn Pavane, though it seems to me that the intent is to allow them to work together.

    Of course, I'd like to see prolonged, magical, Solar dances which have useful effects in battle and thus benefit from dance-battling Charms.

    Battle Anthem of the Solar Exalted is a song, rather than a dance, but you can dance and sing with a single Performance action. The Solar Anthem was written with more stringent action restrictions than the Terrestrial Anthem, but perhaps it would be errata'd (or house-ruled) to allow similar combat performance?

          Robert confirms that dance-based Performance actions are still just as valid to be placed in a flurry with Full Defense actions as any other social influence action, even without using a Shield-tagged weapon.


          And, as evidence to support the claim that social influence actions are not Miscellaneous actions:

          On page 196, Miscellaneous actions are explicitly defined as a combat action for taking "some non-combat action". (emphasis mine)

          The list of combat actions on page 195 of the corebook does not include movement actions, yet movement actions are not considered Miscellaneous. "All movement actions may only be taken on the character’s turn, regardless of whether they are reflexive or a combat action." (emphasis mine)

          Also, the line on page 223: "All social actions are considered combat actions, and may be placed in a flurry." (emphasis mine)

              Originally posted by Lioness View Post
              It's worth highlighting that the thing that makes Battle Dancer Method terrifying is Respect-Commanding Attitude.
              Sorry, had to pull my initial response from the previous post, since I misread the rule.

              Respect-Commanding Performance
              "This Charm does not function after combat has started, but if activated beforehand, it remains active after Join Battle has been rolled, up until the Solar is forced to stop her performance."

              Battle Anthem of the Solar Exalted
              "While playing this song in combat" (emphasis mine)


              If a Solar starts up her Battle Anthem, then Join Battle must be rolled before the Solar takes the turn in which she begins her performance. It's purely a song of battle; the enemy knows that means hostilities have begun.

              Since Join Battle is rolled before the turn in which Battle Anthem of the Solar Exalted takes effect, it can never benefit from Respect-Commanding Performance.

              But what about other performances?


              Respect-Commanding Performance
              "Characters may pay one Willpower to leave the performance, but if they wish to interrupt or attack the Lawgiver, they must pay two Willpower, and may only approach her by blending into her demonstration, capping the actions of each affected character by their individual (Charisma + Performance) ratings until one of them is able to successfully stop her act."


              Yeah, that's pretty powerful for a Solar who has other Performance Charms which allow her to continue a performance while also taking unpenalized Full Defense, movement, Defend Other, and/or attack actions, while also occasionally boosting her Defense with Battle-Dancer Method and/or Hearing the Heart's Song.

              If the combination of these Charms would be too powerful even for the Solar Exalted, then the solution is to design the Charms so that they cannot fully interact.

              For example, the Charm that allows one to flurry an attack with a Performance action might explicitly break the thrall of Respect-Commanding Performance, at least in regards to the character you attack. Or at the very least, it waives the Willpower cost for them to attack you.

                  Robert makes it clear that, even considering how much stronger Solars are than Terrestrials, Solar Battle Anthem is simply too strong to loose its restrictions. No dance-battling with that one, except to use Battle-Dancer Method to survive.

                  But for a Solar not committed to using Solar Battle Anthem, a Charm like Petal-Strewn Pavane is okay. As long as it's not as thoughtless as "PSP, but stronger".


                  Robert also clarifies that the Charm-born performances of Dragon-Blooded Performance Charms are not social influence actions; they are not effects dependent on dice-rolls. This is why they are treated as Miscellaneous actions.

                  They're still Performance actions, and so valid for the special flurry of Petal-Strewn Pavane. PSP ignores the usual penalty for flurries, but not the usual penalties of Miscellaneous actions, so you're still suffering a -1 Defense penalty, and PSP costs a point of Initiative.

                  Robert said that, with Solar Presence's Poised Lion Attitude (and presumably Terrestrial Presence's Eternally Argumentative Flame) as precedents and balance points, Solars and Dragon-Blooded can both have Charms that allow them to attack and ignore the usual penalties for flurrying that attack with a Performance action.

                  I'm not sure I agree with him on this score, since PLA and PEA help you to flurry ANY combat action with a Presence or Socialize action, not just attacks. Petal-Strewn Pavane is more powerful but much more specific, so I figure any other battle-battling Performance Charms would be the same. Also, PSP is not Balanced-keyworded like the Presence Charm.


                  Just as Petal-Strewn Pavane needs to be considered within the context of Effortless Rising Flame and Threshold Warding Stance, any Performance-based attack enhancer/flurry-enhancer needs to be considered in light of Bonfire Stoking Style, Seeking Weapon Technique, Hunter's Obstructed Aim, and Become the Hammer.

                  Likewise any Terrestrial version of Battle-Dancer method.

                  It's worth noting here that Silver-Voiced Nightingale's Hearing the Heart's Song does not have a Terrestrial debuff, though it does have a Mastery effect.


                  Attempting to flurry a Full Defense action with one of a Terrestrial Charm-borne performances would be completely pointless. The +2 Defense bonus of a Full Defense action would suffer a -1 penalty from the flurry itself and then lose the last bonus point to the Miscellaneous action.

                  You'd need a Full Defense-specific Performance Charm to make that flurry worthwhile.

                      Since Respect-Commanding Performance and Solar Battle Anthem are so powerful, and because Solar Exalted aren't as good at turning non-combat Abilities towards combat, I think it's fine to have Battle-Dancer Method serve as the gateway to all other Solar battle-dance Charm, rather than have those others be just as accessible.
