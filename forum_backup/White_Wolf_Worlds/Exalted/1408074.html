      Looks fun. For Cynis Ganan:

      1. Are you a morning person or more of a night owl?

      "Duty knows not a time of day! A scion of the Realm stands ready at all times!"

      Ganan is not a morning person. He rises early to perform his daily ablutions, but is taciturn and sluggish doing it. He's also not a night owl: he likes his sleep. He's more of a "noon" person.

      2. What’s the first thing you notice about a person when you meet them?

      "Warriors carry weapons. As do assassins. A person's choice of blade or bow tells one all one needs to know."

      3. You see a huge spider in your room. What do you do?

      "Usually, in my room, I'm studying?"

      Ganan is unbothered by spiders.

      4. If you could go back and change one decision you made in the past, what would you change?

      "This conversation is over."

      Ganan is haunted by his first love, Amilar Shavok, who betrayed him, murdered their Sworn Kinship and left Ganan with his legs broken in a Manse that was about to explode.

      5. Tell me about your first kiss.

      "Is that a euphemism?"

      Ganan was raised by one of the most successful madames on the Blessed Isle and schooled in the arts from a young age. His first kiss was an instructive lesson with a slave, not a romantic endeavour.

      6. Do you give people second chances?

      "No."

      7. Are you a cat person or a dog person?

      "Cat is too stringy, but the odour of roasted dog is unpalatable. Mouse, horse, chicken -- all preferable."

      8. Do you think you’re attractive?

      "Yes."

      9. What’s your worst habit?

      "There are... expectations of Dynasts: I don't meet them. I am too coarse. Unrefined, like a Lost Egg. But Creation is so vast, its secrets so deep... There isn't time for niceties and games!"

      10. When was the last time you cried?

      "The mountain does not weep."

      Crossing Cecelyn after the death of his companion Whims-of-the-Flesh.

      11. Are you a good liar?

      "No."

      12. What’s your biggest pet peeve?

      "The mountain is not moved by triviality."

      His sister, Cynis Falen Jinabar, irritates him in every way imaginable. They're currently on a month long journey together, camping in the wilderness.

      13. Have you ever had your heart broken?

      "... Yes."

      His boyfriends Amilar Shavok and Whims-of-the-Flesh. Also his wife is conducting an affair with a mortal princess. Ganan isn't in touch with his feelings enough to articulate why this bothers him (his wife has taken many lovers, often with his enthusiastic encouragement and even by his recommendation), but it's because she seems to genuinely love this woman and he is jealous. Growing up in a Cynis brothel has kinda stunted Ganan's emotional feeling.

      14. Are you more likely to use your fists or your words in an argument?

      "I don't argue."

      It's true. Cynis Ganan is loyal to his wife's house. The women make the decisions and do the talking, then Ganan follows through. Which usually means beating the problem to death with his fists.

      15. What’s something you’re naturally good at?

      "The lore of Creation past yields naturally to me. As do the hidden secrets of the now. I inducted myself into sorcery as a boy."

      16. What’s something you had to work hard to be good at?

      "As a child, martial arts came easy to me. Perhaps I would have done well in the Cloister of Wisdom. Mastering Earth Dragon Style without an Immaculate tutor was the hardest thing I've ever done. But it's worth it."

      17. Can you tell when someone is flirting with you?

      "... of course."

      He can't. Not even a little bit.

      18. Do you think money can buy happiness?

      "Jade is a means. Only filling one's duty in the Perfected Heirachy can bring happiness."

      19. Do you believe in destiny?

      "Of course. But every human, great and small, has the power to pervert destiny."

      20. Are you a good cook?

      "No."

      Actually he is. But he judges himself by the Cynis slaves who created the lavish meals of his childhood.

      21. What do you think happens after you die?

      "We are reincarnated. The goal of life is to fulfil the duties of one's station to move closer to the Elemental Dragons. Ghosts are a perversion of the natural order."

      22. Did you have to grow up fast?

      "I was indulged the same wet nurse until my second birthday. And I was beaten only rarely for failing my lessons from then until attending the Heptagram. In retrospect, such a soft and pampered childhood may have weakened me somewhat. Certainly my own children have received harsher discipline."

      He's a Dynast raised in a brothel. He might not have grown up on the streets but he didn't have a childhood.

      23. Who do you look up to?

      "My wife, Mnemon Alinos Danireya, is the model Dynast. Ragara Bhaghwei, my old master, is the perfect scholar. Pasiap, of course, is the model Dragon-Blooded."

      24. When you go to a tavern, what do you order?

      "Food, drink, perhaps lodging?"

      25. What do you like most about yourself?

      "I don't understand the question."

      His mind.

      26. What do you like least about yourself?

      "You know I am Exalted, correct?"

      He feels clumsy. Being crippled by an exploding Manse has taken decades of physical therapy including rare, mystic, treatments, to get Ganan to the point where he can walk without a cane. Even though he's fully recovered, he still feels broken. Part of that is mental trauma, part is emotional anguish.

      27. Do you want kids someday?

      "I have two fine children *pause, guilty smile* and another on the way."

      28. Are you a planner, or more spontaneous?

      "The mountain is."

      Ganan believes in plans. He thinks they should be made and stuck to. Ganan... doesn't stick to plans. He'll follow orders, but he'll do what he thinks is right in the heat of the moment, then berate others for not following the plan.

      29. Can you keep a secret?

      "Of course."

      He can't.

      30. Do you like being the center of attention?

      "That is a burdeon that must sometimes be endured."

      He doesn't.

      31. If you knew you were going to die tomorrow, what would you do today?

      "My duty."

      32. Do you enjoy getting all dressed up for a special occasion?

      "I enjoy not being invited to such vacuous wastes of time!"

      No. But he dresses in finery to brawl people in the street, so...

      33. Where do you feel safe?

      "In Creation."

      Mostly this is a pompous way of saying he feels safe everywhere. But he was recently in the Boardermarches and Malfeas and he did not feel safe in either place.

      34. Do you love or hate being alone?

      "They say Humans and Dragons are social animals, but I prefer my own company."

      35. What’s the last nightmare you remember having?

      "The mountain does not dream."

      Every night he dreams of Shavok betraying him. Every night for forty two years.

      36. Do you admit to mistakes when you make them?

      "It is more efficient to simply not make mistakes."

      He does not admit his mistakes.

      37. Do you want to grow up to be like your parents?

      "All Dragons have their place: mine is not within House Cynis Falen."

      38. How do you deal with being sick? Are you stoic, or super whiny?

      "The mountain is not 'super whiny'!"

      SUUUUUUPER whiny.

      39. What did your parents expect from you when you were born?

      "My father expected me to become Immaculate. My mother wanted a successor. Fortunately the birth of my sister eleviated the latter expectation.

      40. Do you have a strong sense of style?

      "Adornments are baubles."

      Ganan dresses in the height of fashion... for forty years ago when he still had Cynis slaves to dress him. He doesn't seem to realise that no-one else in the Dynasty wears house colours and capes anymore.

      41. Would you rather camp outdoors or stay the night in an inn?

      "The mountain endures all discomforts."

      42. Is there a food that most people like that you absolutely hate?

      "I just said I don't like cat or dog."

      Ganan doesn't like tea. Which, in the Dynasty, is kinda a big thing.

      43. Are you more of a hoarder or a minimalist?

      He's a hoarder.

      44. Are you superstitious?

      He's a sorcerer. The superstitions he believes in are mostly true.

      45. Are you the kind of person who remembers people’s birthdays and pets’ names and stuff?

      Hahaha! No!

      46. What do you do to feel better when you’re sad?

      "Mediation cleanses the mind. Exercise cleanses the body."

      Ever watch/read Fight Club? Fight Club.

      47. Is it hard for you to trust someone?

      "Fools trust. The mountain endures."

      48. Are you susceptible to peer pressure?

      "No."

      49. If you decided to stop adventuring and settle down, what kind of job would you take?

      "At my core, I am a teacher. Though travel teaches me much, I long to return to the Isle of Voices. Perhaps in retirement."

      50. As a kid, what did you want to be when you grew up?

      "Don't laugh... I wanted... ... it doesn't matter now."

      To marry the Scarlet Empress and become Emperor.

          Answers for Poena Hanim

          Are you a morning person or more of a night owl?
          I’m more of an evening person to be sure.
          What’s the first thing you notice about a person when you meet them?
          Probably their attire, clothes make the man or woman and I like to offer critique.
          You see a huge spider in your room. What do you do?
          Find somewhere else to live.
          If you could go back and change one decision you made in the past, what would you change?
          If I could do it without changing the person I am now? I would’ve found a better way to end my first marriage.
          Tell me about your first kiss.
          It was okay. The first time I kissed my now wife Driana I knew why.
          Do you give people second chances?
          Hurt me? Yes. Hurt someone I love, and I will make you suffer.
          Are you a cat person or a dog person?
          I adore cats. It is a little-known fact that in Chiaroscuro a great number of them roam the streets at night and control the vermin population. My grandmother respected this symbiosis and would have me leave treats to our nocturnal protectors.
          Do you think you’re attractive?
          Darling, I know I’m attractive!
          What’s your worst habit?
          I have extremely poor impulse control at times.
          When was the last time you cried?
          I cried with relief when I discovered that someone who I care deeply for was alive.
          Are you a good liar?
          When the situation calls for it.
          What’s your biggest pet peeve?
          People who don’t respect soft power.
          Have you ever had your heart broken?
          Yes.
          Are you more likely to use your fists or your words in an argument?
          Words are the recourse of the civilised, but I’m mindful of the fact they can cut deeper than a knife.
          What’s something you’re naturally good at?
          I’ve got a good head for numbers and see profitable ventures in areas others might overlook.
          What’s something you had to work hard to be good at?
          Empathy. I try to be a better person but it’s so easy to lapse into constantly looking over my shoulder for potential threats.
          Can you tell when someone is flirting with you?
          If she’s pretty I’m normally flirting right back.
          Do you think money can buy happiness?
          To an extent, yes. People who argue that there’s no correlation between money and happiness have access to everything they need. I do believe that pursuit of money can become a cage though.
          Do you believe in destiny?
          I do.
          Are you a good cook?
          I am an exquisite cook.
          What do you think happens after you die?
          I suppose you stop living, does continuation of the soul really matter without the experiences that shaped us?
          Did you have to grow up fast?
          Not compared to some, but I look back on my first marriage and I still felt like a child.
          Who do you look up to?
          I always looked up to my grandmother and lost an important part of myself when she passed. I respect a lot of people but attempts to replace her with a new mentor have met with failure… my mother-in-law doesn’t much care for me.
          When you go to a tavern, what do you order?
          It really depends on where I am and if I trust them to get anything more complicated than water right.
          What do you like most about yourself?
          My wits. While I’m not always the smartest person in the room I will often approach problems in a way that others wouldn’t consider.
          What do you like least about yourself?
          My sometimes-poor impulse control, particularly when it leads me to lash out.
          Do you want kids someday?
          Absolutely not.
          Are you a planner, or more spontaneous?
          I wish I could be more spontaneous, but if I’m in charge of something I have a need for control.
          Can you keep a secret?
          Of course.
          Do you like being the center of attention?
          Far too much for my own good.
          If you knew you were going to die tomorrow, what would you do today?
          Make the most of the time with my wife. I am certain you can use your imagination without me painting a picture.
          Do you enjoy getting all dressed up for a special occasion?
          Why a special occasion? Can one not simply claim a moment to celebrate themselves and their success.
          Where do you feel safe?
          Home. Wherever that might be of course.
          Do you love or hate being alone?
          I don’t like to be alone.
          What’s the last nightmare you remember having?
          I dream of becoming the sort of person who’d hurt the people I care most about.
          Do you admit to mistakes when you make them?
          Yes, within reason.
          Do you want to grow up to be like your parents?
          I’ve already surpassed their merge life goals.
          How do you deal with being sick? Are you stoic, or super whiny?
          I make quite a drama of it so my wife will take care of me.
          What did your parents expect from you when you were born?
          A dowry from a rich husband, I suspect. I don’t owe them anything else.
          Do you have a strong sense of style?
          I am often emulated and usually flattered by the effort.
          Would you rather camp outdoors or stay the night in an inn?
          Almost always the inn. Even if I might not trust what they consider a beverage.
          Is there a food that most people like that you absolutely hate?
          I am not a lover of onions.
          Are you more of a hoarder or a minimalist?
          I want to be a minimalist, but my wife and I occasionally get sentimental over items. I fear that this is how many hoarders begin.
          Are you superstitious?
          I’m aware of and respectful of tradition and folklore because of my grandmother but I don’t let it limit my actions.
          Are you the kind of person who remembers people’s birthdays and pets’ names and stuff?
          Yes, I’m also the kind of person to drop hints to someone that they’ve forgotten something.
          What do you do to feel better when you’re sad?
          Find someone to hold me.
          Is it hard for you to trust someone?
          Yes.
          Are you susceptible to peer pressure?
          Sometimes.
          If you decided to stop adventuring and settle down, what kind of job would you take?
          I always like to mentor people of promise, likely something in that capacity.
          As a kid, what did you want to be when you grew up?
          As a child my horizons were limited by my society’s view of womanhood. I wanted to be happy and successful but was content to enjoy that by proxy through a husband and sons.
