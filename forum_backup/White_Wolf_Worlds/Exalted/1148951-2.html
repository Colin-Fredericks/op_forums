                                Originally posted by Kunoichi View Post
                                I dunno, I kind of want a martial art like Weiss's fighting style. Frozen Ballerina Style?
                                Graceful Hummingbird, actually. A style that Holden homebrewed before and that we can ask the devs to bring back officially. Someone on this forum has already taken a "stab" at updating it for Third Edition.


                                Dust magic could be homebrewed into something uniquely its own thing in Creation. We already have Fire Dust in Creation, and the way it is used in firewands is very similar to how Fire Dust is used in Remnant.

                                Dust spells would be limited by ammunition rules like firewand firedust, and also generally require you to have (and protect) a financially solvent background to continue affording the supply.

                                The "spells" would be structured like Evocations, with one trick leading to other tricks.


                                Semblances are a different mix of elegance and complexity than Charmsets or Naruto Jutsus. By giving a character only one magical trick, it's easier to continue providing challenges that fall outside of their strengths or even continue challenging their strengths. They can learn how to adapt their Semblance to perform new tricks, but it all comes back to a single, mostly-well-defined ability.

                                But it essentially makes everyone an Exigent; every "Charmset" needs to be homebrewed. What's more, Creation is a place where mind-altering powers are more commonplace than Remnant, so lacking the "well-roundedness" of a Charmset that must include mental defenses is a problem.
