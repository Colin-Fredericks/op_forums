                                                    Cost: 5m, 1wp; Mins: Cat 4, Essence 1
                                                    Type: Supplemental
                                                    Keyword: Psyche
                                                    Duration: Scene
                                                    Prerequisites: Attention-Seeking Mewling
                                                    The feline form is adorable and fluffy, and must be petted at once. This charm supplements an Instill social influence to view the cat as fluffy and adorable, compelling all who hold the cat to either spend 1wp, or immediately fall next to the cat and pet it for (Cat) turns. The recipient of this charm may roll (Intelligence)+(Integrity) vs the half of cats (Manipulate) + (Performance) to realize they have more important things to do on each turn.

                                                    Goddamn Cat Stance.
                                                    Cost: 8m; Mins: Cat 4, Essence 2
                                                    Type: Supplemental
                                                    Keyword: None
                                                    Duration: Scene
                                                    Prerequisites: Aww Kitty Prana
                                                    Struck by the cats adorable nature, those enthralled by it's spell area easy victim for trickery. This charm may be used to supplement any Athletics or Brawl-based Gambits applied against a victim of the prerequisite charm. The cat may add (Essence) automatic non-charm successes, to the attempt, as well as impose a -3 penalty to the dice pool to resist it.
