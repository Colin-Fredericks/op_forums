    Hi everyone,

    Thought I should post an introduction here as well.

    I got introduced to roleplaying in my early teens. A friend at school mention he had been trying a cool kind of game, talked about it. I actually don't know if it was Dungeons and Dragons, Drakar och Demoner (a Swedish RuneQuest derivate), or something else. It was a sanbboxy game, and it was without any mechanics and we just free-formed it (so now you know where my disrespect for RAW comes from ). I loved it, and later that year, around Christmas, I bough the Drakar and Demoner Box.

    While impovising a lot, and just seeing mechanics as support or things to disregard, I still have a fascination of them, and prefer the mechanics to be in sync with the game. So I can switch between using them and just freeform, without it being an obvious switch.

    During my teens, I GM'ed a lot and played some. I also spent quite a bit of my money back then on more roleplaying stuff. So I have quite a few different settings and systems lying around at home, not counting my own homebrews.

    Adult life have definitely made gaming being less common. Being really picky of how I want to roleplay, and apparently not being in the main stream, isn't helping either. I haven't tried much voice or video chat yet, but hopefully that suits me; and as it would really increase the area to find fellow gamers...

    I first heard about Exalted from a coworker, as I was talking about if a gaming idea I had for a "god level" game. I bought both first and second edition core rule PDFs, ST companions and a few other books. Then I bought both the PDF bundles for first and second edition, and a while later starting to get some hardcovers (well, some for first editon, and almost all for second edition). So yes, I have bought the Second Edition core book three times...

    Came for the mechanics. Stayed for the setting. I don't really like the over the top kung-fu stuff, which seems to be a common appeal. But because I love exploring different cultures and mindset, while also being bored to death by the standard medieval Tolkienesque fantasy, Exalted hits almost all the notes for me.

    While second edition isn't a perfect fit for me, it is the one closest to what I want. Now that I have Ex3, I plan to mod 2ed with stuff from 1ed and Ex3. What I really would want to do is a complete overhaul, which I tend to refer to as "Edition L". However, even if I ever get that down into a written format, comprehensible to others, that would be way off into the future.

    I have noticed that my comfort zone might be a bit wider than most, and even if I try to censor myself, I might voice opinions or ideas that squick others out. I guess hanging out in BDSM circles, being an agnostic (with a hard atheistic leaning), and being a Scandinavian leftish liberal might be part of it. Or I'm just a sick dude...

    Some other notes... Work with computers and been an IT-consultant for most of my career. Was in an urban warfare specialist battalion in the army. Did a lot of martial arts in my late teen (logged my hours one of the years and realized I did slightly above 500 hours that year) and kind of still having an itch to get back into doing it. Trying to learn dancing Salsa and Bachata, and finally feel I'm starting to grasp the basics.

    Oh... and I tend to do long-winded posts at times.

                                                I know a lot of details about far more game systems than I have had the opportunity to play, mostly due to time constraints and difficulty in finding people to play with...currently DMing a D&D 5e game and putting together pieces for a Shadowrun 5E game that will, honestly, probably never actually get played.

It's why I like DM/GM/STing so much.

                                                In terms of Exalted, I've mucked around with 2 and 3E a bit, though nothing for very long...but am blessed (cursed?) with a bizarre memory that absorbs RPG rulebooks (and other technical things) like a sponge, even though I can't remember what I had for dinner three days ago, and often struggle to remember someone's name. So I do actually know quite a lot about how the game works. My favorite splats are Siddies and Solars, but I certainly have appreciation for all the other splats as well. My love for worldbuilding and (sometimes annoyingly) accurate memory for rulebooks does make me tend towards homebrewing as a solution to issues within a system, and I put together an Exalted Modern setting a while back...around the same time Shards came out.
