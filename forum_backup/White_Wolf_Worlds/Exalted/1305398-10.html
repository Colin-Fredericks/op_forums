    Okay, well I rolled out another fight against 3 DBs. It took five hours. It wasn't against a Lunar though, it was against an Essence 2 Dawn, Supernal Melee, with 5 in every stat and an assumed specialty always applicable int all abilities, and all 48 melee charms. He had artifact fullplate and a daiklave. Everyone had 2 ox-bodies and thank god for that because if they had more I would have killed myself.

    I did not build the DBs to kill him. They did not have max in every stat, they had two 5s and a 3 in their physical, 4 wits and no awareness specialty applicable. The Fire Aspect melee fighter was wielding a Grand Daiklave. The Water Aspect Brawler was wielding Smashfists with medium armor, and the Wood Aspect Archer had a powerbow and light armor. In the spoiler below is a play-by-play of my thought process trying to get the Solar to win.

    There's a TLDR; after the spoiler if you don't feel like wading through a thousand numbers.


    Okay so first off, I need to spend everything I have into this join battle roll because for one it gives me decisive damage I can play with, and for another if I don't, and I roll bad, and one of those DBs goes before me, it could start a huge chain reaction where withering attack after withering attack puts me to the bottom or crashes me. Besides I get 5m back at the start of my turn.

    Solar spends 10m for 21 dice, gets 11 for 14 initiative
    Fire spends 4m for 2 successes, gets 5 for 8
    Water spends 4m for 2 successes gets 6 for 9
    Wood spends 4m for 2 successes, gets 5 for 8

    ==============
    Round 1

    Okay, so now I've gone first, none of the DBs rolled exceptionally well, so that's good. Now what do I do? If I don't activate Fivefold Bulwark right now, I'm vulnerable to onslaught penalties, and with it Dipping Swallow costs 1m and raises Parry by 1.

    I could try to do Ready in Eight Directions Stance, but all that'll happen then is one of the DBs will attack to provoke my reset, and the rest will just aim.

    I could try Heaven Sword Flash but my odds of incapping a DB with one blow are low on 7 initiative.

    On the other hand I could fire off an Incincible Fury of the Dawn, putting 3/3/3/4/4 into the attacks and relying on damage boosters to kill her, using Invincible Fury's move ment to get out from between these two melee fighters. I'd start running away afterwards to start regenerating motes, but then I'd have to start spending on shutting down rush attempts and that would be counterproductive.


    So that's what I'll do, 5m 1wp Invincible Fury of the Dawn, 3m Fire and Stones Strike, 3m Excellent Strike, 3m 2i Hungry Tiger Technique, 1wp Perfect Strike Discipline, 4m 1wp Thunderbolt Attack Prana.

    So a base cost of 18m, 2i, 3wp. Really good. What do I do about the excellency thouigh? I need about 16 dice of damage, with TAP active, to kill her 13 health levels, and I'm doubling a lot of the overflow damage. Her Evasion is 9/8/7/6/5, and I've got 11 dice, rerolling 1s, plus an autosuccess at base, and 1 free full one with Perfect Strike. So I use Perfect Strike on the last one and full excellency the previous 2 and I should be good. If I do that then I'll have 12 motes left, but only 2 opponants.


    Rolling 11 dice for 4 total successes, no hit
    Rolling 13 dice for total of 3 successes, no hit
    Note that if I dont excellency this next attack, the Wood Aspect uses her Anima power and I decently risk missing again.
    Rolling 23 dice for a total of 13, dealing 9 damage rolling for 5, doubling for 10
    Rolling 23 dice for a total of 13, dealing 10 damage rolling for 1, doubling for 2
    Rolling 22 dice for a total of 15, dealing 13 damage, rolling for 5, doubling for 10

    ----------
    The Wood aspect is SLAIN. Perfect Strike Discipline resets! Supernal is a hell of a thing. Okay, next the Water Aspect goes. Walking over to me he withers me for 6m full excellency, 5m Hammering Wave Technique.

    If this hits me, I am in some deep shit. I have 12m left, and I need 5 for Bulwark Stance or else the onslaught penalties are going to kill me, I'm going to try and get through this with Hail Shattering Practice, which will let me spend motes 1/success to remove an attackers successes post attack roll.

    He rolls 20 dice to hit, and gets it with 12. At Essnce 2 I can't remove enough successes to deal with this. Damage 8, rolling it for 5. I'm crashed and he gets initiative break. Okay, so now I'm targetting him, I need initiative shift to bring this fight back my way.
    ----------
    The fire aspect attacks, 6m full excellency, 3m 1wp Crimson Fang Bite, an attack with 18 dice to hit and 24 base damage. Activating Bulwark Stance I have 7m left now.

    She rolls 18 dice for 10 against my Parry of 7, rolling 11 damage for 7

    He enters Fire Aura
    ==============
    Round 2 starts

    Water goes first, activating 10m 1wp 1a Embracing the Violent Flow
    ----------
    Fire goes next, activating 3m 2i Flame Warden Stance, targetting Water. Note here that if I hadn't used Bulwark stance they would have been taking advantage of my onslaught penalties to farm some more initiative, or land crippling decisive attacks.
    ----------
    I go, I activate 5m Fivefold Bulwark Stance. Seems stupid aginst 2 opponants but it also discounts my first parry excellency by a mote each time, so it's saving me 4m this turn alone. I still have 7m for Hail Shattering Practice if I have to.
    ==============
    Round 3 starts

    Water goes, he attempts a grapple with 6m full excellency, 5m Inescapable Whirlpool Hold. If this hits me it's over, so I spend 1wp and full excellency my defence with Perfect Strike Discipline.

    He rolls 15 dice against my parry of 13 and fails with 7
    ----------
    Fire goes next, Striking with 5m full excellency, 3m 1wp Crimson Wolf Bite. I could respond with a reflexive clash, but if I lose I'm taking even more initiative damage due to the clash rules.

    Fire rolls 18 to attack for 11, rolling 12 damage for 3
    ----------
    My turn again, now with 12 motes I need to try and stop Water. But Fire is constantly using Defend Other on him. I'm stuck. I could sit here frugally using motes on defense, but their animas will cut me down even as I block every decisive blow. Assuming they don't just get lucky and kill me

    At this point, it's worth my time to wait for reset. It's free initiative. I simply attack Fire without using motes. He doesn't defend with motes either

    Rolling 14 against 7 I fail with 3
    ==============
    Round 4 starts

    Water goes and rolls no anima damage. Attacks me with a 6m full excellency I defend with 1m Dipping Swallow. He rolls 20 dice for 7, becoming 8 with EtVF, and I use 1m Hail Shattering Practice to cancel the attack.
    ----------
    Fire rolls no anima damage. Attacks with 6m Full excellency. I defend with 1m dipping swallow. Rolling 18 dice for 9 I spend 2m and cancel it with Hail Shattering Practice.
    ----------
    I go reset to 4 initiative. I attack Fire without spending motes. He defends without spending motes.

    I roll 14 dice against 7, getting it with 8. Rolling minimum damage of 5, for 5
    ==============
    Round 5 starts
    ----------
    Water uses an Aim
    ----------
    Fire uses Full Defense
    ----------
    Water has 20i, fire 12, and me 10. Water stands a very good chance of hitting me next round if I don't bring my full defenses to bear, and right now he's if I don't crash him, but behind. I take a full defense. I could try Ready in Eight Directions Stance, but Fire would just counter clash me with Cloud Riot.
    ==============
    Round 6 starts
    ----------
    Water attacks with 6m full excellency, I defend with 1m dipping swallow, 6m Excellency

    Water rolls 20 dice against a parry of 14, geting 7
    ----------
    Fire attacks with 6m full excellency. I defend with 1m Dipping Swallow, 4m excellency

    Fire rolls 18 dice agasint a parry of 13, missing with 6
    ----------
    If I attack Fire will Cloud Riot me and I don't have the motes to beat him in a clash. I take a full defense.
    ==============
    Round 7 starts
    Water attacks with 6m full excellency, I defend with 1m dipping swallow, 2m Excellency

    Water rolls 20 dice against a parry of 12, geting 12, I pay a mote to cancel with Hail Shattering
    ----------
    Fire attacks with 6m full excellency. I defend with 1m Dipping Swallow, 4m excellency

    Fire rolls 18 dice agasint a parry of 11, missing with 9
    ----------
    If I attack Fire will Cloud Riot me and I don't have the motes to beat him in a clash. I take a full defense.
    ==============
    Round 8 starts
    Water attacks me with a 6m full excellency, I defend with 1m dipping swallow and 2m excellency.

    Water rolls 20 dice against 11, hits with 14. I can not Hail Shattering Practice down to a miss.

    Water rolls minimum damage, which is 8, getting 4. I am now at 3 initiative
    ----------
    Fire attacks with 6m full excellency. I defend with 1m Dipping Swallow

    Fire rolls 18 dice agasint a parry of 10, missing with 7
    ----------
    If I attack Fire will Cloud Riot me and I don't have the motes to beat him in a clash. I take a full defense. This keeps happening because I keep needing to feed almost all the motes I get back each turn into defending myself, and the Dragonblooded can't afford to stop forcing me to do that or else I might be able to pull some kind of combo on them.
    ==============
    Round 9 starts
    Water attacks me with a 6m full excellency, I defend with 1m dipping swallow and 2m excellency.

    Water rolls 20 dice against Parry of 11, missing with 10
    ----------
    Fire attacks with 6m full excellency. I defend with 1m Dipping Swallow

    Fire rolls 18 dice agasint a parry of 10, getting 11, I pay 2m to Hail Shattering Practice it away.
    ----------
    I have 10m now, slowly creeping up turn after turn but I need to try and avoid being crashed. I attack Fire with 3m Excellent Strike, 7m full excellency. He responds with 6m full excellency, 5m Crossfire Slash, 4m 3i Cloud Riot.

    I roll 25 dice total for 11 successes, he rolls 23 dice for 13, beating me by 2. I have no motes left to Hail Shattering Practice.

    Fire rolls 6 damage for 0. I then take 3 initiative damage for losing a clash, and am crashed, hardness set to 0, and with no motes remaining to dipping swallow.

    I reflexively spend 1m 1wp 3a to peony blossom with 3m excellent strike. Fire defends with 4m excellency.

    I roll 17 dice total against a parry of 8 and miss with 7
    ==============
    Round 10 starts

    Water rolls 1 lethal and attempts a grapple gambit against me. 6m full excellency 5m inescapable whirlpool hold. I am out of motes to defend

    Water rolls 18 dice against parry of 7, getting 8

    Water easily makes the roll to lock in.

    Water rolls 6m full excellency for control roll, rolling 17 dice against my 11, getting 8 against my 11. I win control of the grapple but he still gets one action, which will be a decisive slam attack, 1m for exellency rerolling 6s

    Rolling 25 initiative damage for 10 damage
    ----------
    Fire rolls 0 damage with anima and attacks with 6m full excellency decisive.

    Fire rolls 17 dice agasint a parry of 7, getting 3 and missing
    ----------
    I flurry a rise from prone with an attack against Water

    Rolling 11 dice and getting the rise with 12

    I attack water with 3m excellent strike, 2m excellency, Fire intervenes and does not spend motes to defend

    I roll 16 dice, getting 10 and hitting, rolling 5 damage for 4, pulling out of crash
    ==============
    Round 11 starts
    Fire attacks with 6m full excellency against my parry of 7, getting 8

    Rolling 6 damage for 3
    ----------
    Water Spends 5m on full excellency attack, rolling 19 and getting 12 against my 7 parry.

    Rolling 8 damage for 5, re-crashing me
    ----------
    I attack water with 2m excellency, 3m excellent strike, fire intervenes

    I roll 17 dice for 11 against Parry 6, rolling 7 damage for 4, pulling out of crash.
    ==============
    Round 12 starts

    Fire spends 5m on full exellency decisive attack against my parry of 7, rolling 17 dice and getting 12. Rolling 16 decisive damage for 7, killing me


    Basically in the first turn I used a doomcombo to kill the Archer right away. Then 11 rounds of slogging back and forth, with two poor sods trying to penetrate a Parry DV of base 7 while I had Fivefold Bulwark up and Hail Shattering Practice available. Between the two of those I can effectively raise my parry DV up to 10 for 3m. I gave it my absolute best attempt. I even went back and did some sections over again because I figured out a better way to do them.

    This isn't even remotely realistic for a game anyway. When would this ever happen? Want to see if your Lunar is really good at fighting? Fight 3 Bloodapes at once. If you think that's meaningless because obviously any remotely competent Essence 2 celestial exalt should be able to rip through 3 bloodapes without even thinking, rather than a demonstration of the gut-wrenchingly terrifying power of the Exalted, as far as I'm concerned, that's your problem.

    If you see something that you think I missed, or mistakes I made, or some crazy awesome combo that will definitely work, please, please keep it to yourself. I don't even care if I'm totally wrong and I have no idea how Lunars/Solars are supposed to fight and if I knew I'd have no trouble at all. I am done with this topic.

                I don't think I've ever played a 12 round fight, we thank you for your sacrifice.

                Actually I just remembered something I asked Vance about ages ago thats a huge advantage for any group of DBs fighting a lone celestial if they have the charms for it.

                You can defend other in a clash, and it really dicks the person clashing who doesn't have someone defending other for them.

                So as long as 2 or more DBs survive round 1, and know Flame Warden Stance, Portentous Comet Deflection, and Fire Incites Water to a Riot of Clouds, any lunar or solar who doesn't have some way to work around it is going eat penalties for losing a clash every time (s)he attacks even though he hits the DB defending.
