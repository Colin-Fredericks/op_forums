            Actually, the supreme choice for a shirtless Earth Dragon build is to combo it with White Reaper: with Impenetrable White Shroud, you get +6 soak straight out of the gate as long as there's a single battle group within striking distance, and potentially +9 if you're willing to make two attacks before swapping to Earth Dragon Form. Additionally, you get top tier penalty negation to your Parry (3m to ignore up to 2 points of wound penalties, 4m 1i to convert onslaught penalties to a bonus), absolutely ungodly withering damage with the combination of Falling Scythe Flash + Force of the Mountain, and you can make use of Bleeding Crescent Strike for extra accuracy on your decisives. With the amount of motes you save on offensive and defensive excellencies, not even the mote-hungry nature of the style can slow you down.

            Evocations for the demonic tattoos gained from the Pact with Mara sorcerous initiation || Pyre-Kindler (Soulsteel and Red Jade Grimscythe, Artifact 3) || Tenebrous Descent (Stormcaller's Black Jade Reaver Daiklave cousin, Artifact 5)

                But, you know, Air and Fire Dragon are totally effective combat styles and absolutely comparable to Earth and Water, right?
                So what happens if we have an Air Immaculate fight an Earth Immaculate?

                Earth Immaculate:

                Attributes: Str 5 Dex 3 Sta 3 | Wits 4
                Abilities: Earth Dragon 5, Athletics 4, Awareness 4
                Charms:
                Earth Dragon: Stone Dragon's Skin, Force of the Mountain, Stillness-of-Stone Atemi, Unmoving Mountain Stance, Earth Dragon Form
                Awareness: Precision Observation Method
                Brawl: Become the Hammer
                Resistance: Ox-Body Technique *2, Impervious Skin of Stone

                Specialties MA Earth Dragon, Athletics Feats of Strength, Resistance poisons.


                STATS
                Tetsubo 10 (Damage 19/5)
                Parry 5
                Soak 14


                Air Immaculate:

                Attributes: Str 3 Dex 5 Sta 3 | Perception 3, Wits 4
                Abilities: Air Dragon 5, Awareness 4, Dodge 5, Stealth 4
                Charms:
                Air Dragon Style: Air Dragon's Sight, Cloud-Treading Method, Wind Dragon's Speed, Breath-Seizing Technique, Air Dragon Form, Shroud of Unseen Winds
                Resistance: Ox-Body Technique
                Awareness: Precision Observation Method
                Dodge: Threshold Warding Stance
                Brawl: Become the Hammer

                Specialties MA Air Dragon and Dodge/Athletics, while in Air Dragon Form

                STATS
                Chakram 16 (Damage 13/3)
                Evasion 5
                Soak 8/4


                FIGHT

                Both Dragons use 4m to full excellency their Join Battles

                Earth Dragon rolls 8 dice for 2, for 7 initiative
                Air Dragon rolls 8 dice for 3, for 8 initiative


                =========
                Round 1

                Air Dragon uses 5m Wind Dragon Speed to flurry an attack and disengage

                Earth defends with 5m 1i Stone Dragon's Skin

                Air rolls 1m attack for 17 dice against parry 5, scoring 4 and missing.

                Air rolls 10 dice to disengage, spending 4m for 2 autosuccesses against Earth's 5 dice, getting 6 against 1 and disengaging
                --------
                Earth moves toward Air who reflexively moves back, and rushes, rolling 5 dice against 10, failing with 2 against 4
                =========
                Round 2

                Air Dragon spends 8m to activate Air Dragon Form and moves to medium range
                --------
                Earth dragon closes to short range and activates 9m Earth Dragon Style
                =========
                Round 3

                Air Dragon attacks, spending 4m on excellency for 20 dice. Earth spends 6m to raise parry to 8, rolling 20 dice and missing with 6

                Air Dragon retreats to medium range
                --------
                Earth dragon closes to short range and rushes, rolling 5 dice against 11, scoring 2 against 6 and failing
                =========
                Round 4

                Air Dragon attacks, spending 4m on excellency for 20 dice. Earth spends 6m to raise parry to 8, rolling 20 dice and missing with 5

                Air Dragon retreats to medium range
                --------
                Earth dragon closes to short range and rushes, rolling 5 dice against 11, scoring 5 against 6 and failing
                =========
                Round 5

                Air Dragon attacks, spending 4m on excellency for 20 dice. Earth spends 6m to raise parry to 8, rolling 20 dice and getting it with 11, rolling 3 dice of damage for 2

                Air Dragon retreats to medium range
                --------
                Earth dragon closes to short range and rushes, rolling 5 dice against 11, scoring 2 against 3 and failing
                =========
                Round 6

                Air Dragon attacks, spending 4m on excellency for 20 dice. Earth spends 6m to raise parry to 8, rolling 20 dice and getting it with 12, rolling 3 dice of damage for 3

                Air Dragon retreats to medium range
                --------
                Earth dragon closes to short range and rushes, rolling 5 dice against 11, scoring 3 against 2 and suceeding!
                =========
                Round 7

                Air Dragon takes a 1i full defense action, getting ready to spring away again
                --------
                Earth Dragon moves into close and attacks with 6m full excellency, 3m Force of the Mountain, and 2i smashing attack, trying to knock the air aspect to the ground. Paying for this causes the Earth Immaculate to crash itself, losing an extra 5i, but at least it denies it to the air aspect as a crash bonus.

                Air defends with 4m full excellency

                Earth rolls 16 dice against an evasion of 11, scoring 10 and missing
                =========
                Round 8

                Air Dragon uses 5m Wind Dragon Speed to flurry a decisive attack with a disengage.

                Air Dragon uses 6m full excellency, Earth Dragon defends with 6m full excellency. Rolling 17 dice against a parry of 7, getting 10 and hitting. Rolling 14 decisive damage for 5, putting Earth Dragon into his last -1 health level

                Air Dragon disengages with 11 dice and 1 success from Air Dragon, against 4 dice from Earth Dragon rolling 1 total of 10 successes against 5 and disengaging
                --------
                Earth moves into short range and rushes, rolling 4 dice against 11, scoring 1 against 5 and failing
                =========
                Round 9

                Air Dragon decisive attacks, spending 6m to full excellency, Earth dragon defends with 6m full excellency. Rolling 17 dice against parry 8, getting it with 12, rolling 3 decisive damage for 0

                Air Dragon retreats to medium range
                --------
                Earth dragon closes to short range and rushes, rolling 4 dice against 11, scoring 3 against 5 and failing
                =========
                Round 10

                Air Dragon decisive attacks, spending 6m to full excellency, Earth dragon defends with 6m full excellency. Rolling 17 dice against parry 8, getting it with 9, rolling 3 decisive damage for 1, putting Earth into -2s

                Air Dragon retreats to medium range
                --------
                Earth dragon resets to base initiative and closes to short range and rushes, rolling 3 dice against 11, scoring 2 against 6 and failing
                =========
                Round 11

                Air Dragon decisive attacks, spending 6m to full excellency, Earth dragon defends with 6m full excellency. Rolling 17 dice against parry 7, failing with 5

                Air Dragon retreats to medium range
                --------
                Earth dragon closes to short range and rushes, rolling 3 dice against 11, scoring 0 against 4 and failing
                =========
                Round 12

                Air Dragon withering attacks, spending 6m to full excellency, Eath Dragon spends 6m to full excellency defense. Rolling 20 dice agasint parry 7, scoring 8 and hitting, rolling 3 dice for 1 damage

                Air Dragon retreats to medium range
                --------
                Earth dragon closes to short range and rushes, rolling 3 dice against 11, scoring 0 against 5 and failing
                =========
                Round 13

                Air Dragon withering attacks, spending 6m to full excellency, Eath Dragon spends 6m to full excellency defense. Rolling 20 dice agasint parry 7, scoring 9 and hitting, rolling 3 dice for 1 damage

                Air Dragon retreats to medium range
                --------
                Earth dragon closes to short range and rushes, rolling 3 dice against 11, scoring 1 against 5 and failing
                =========
                Round 14

                Air Dragon withering attacks, spending 6m to full excellency, Eath Dragon spends 6m to full excellency defense. Rolling 20 dice agasint parry 7, scoring 9 and hitting, rolling 3 dice for 4 damage, crashing the Earth again

                Air Dragon retreats to medium range
                --------
                Earth dragon closes to short range and rushes, rolling 3 dice against 11, scoring 0 against 11 and failing
                =========
                Round 15

                Air Dragon decisive attacks, with 6m full excellency, rolling 17 dice against Earths 6m full excellency parry ot 7, hitting with 8. Scoring 8 damage off of 17 dice, the Air Dragon slays the Earth

                                Originally posted by aluminiumtrioxid View Post
                                I feel like somebody was putting their thumb on the scale there a bit, between giving the Air Immaculate more artifacts, better skills and specialties, and explicitly ignoring my advice to ditch an Ox-Body in favor of the Athletics excellency :P
                                I gave BOTH sides artifacts, and if your style is giving you an Evasion bonus you either invest as much into Dodge as you do your MA ability or else waste those bonuses, since otherwise your parry won’t even keep up with your dodge.

                                Not doing that is like running a Silver Voiced Nightingale stylist who decided that Performance was cool to leave at 4 with no excellency, or a Black claw stylist who didn’t feel the need to invest into Manipulation or Presence.

                                Earth Dragon doesn’t really care, it doesn’t dodge, or disengage, or use performance attacks so you can afford to just leave it. I could have given the Earth Dragon a bunch of rush charms, that could have helped, but now I’m building to counter air specifically, and you can’t build to counter every enemy. Where as giving air evasion is just building to complement your own MA, which is good in every scenario.

                                Air Dragon loves disengaging and keeping away from its enemy so it can abuse the fact that it’s one of the very few ranged Martial Arts, if you want to use it, use it for what its good at.


                                Also if this battle could have taken place in difficult terrain, or in the dark, or both, if it did it would have been even more one sided.
