                                                            Minor question about Lunars and Order Affirming Blow. In 2nd edition (grown) Order Affirming Blow (here after referred to as OAB) specifically had the keyword "Shaping" on it, which meant that Tattooed Lunars could not be affected by it. Untattooed lunars however could, which mean that the untattooed lunars could be "cured" of the wyld taint that afflicts them. Presenting a very interesting delima for the Lunar Exalted as a whole as well as a chance to make drama for any Goody-two-shoes solar looking to gain allies by doing so. Removing the tattoos would be an epic quest in of itself (probably painful as well), some lunars would not like this idea, others may surprise you, and then there is the broken trust between solar and lunar as it is to play with.

                                                            My question; is there any reason why a solar could not wield OAB to these ends? Is there some other blocker (beside the GM said so) that could be deployed or will be deployed? Was this meant to be so or simply an oversight? Or will Lunars simply be vastly different than they where in the previous editions in regard to their social and physical problems of dwelling in the Wyld for centuries?
