                I have to say, it’s definitely a way way less useful effect for a DB. Even if it was as fast as the Solar version that’s still not very helpful just because, why? The only reasons I can see for a DB having artifact armor and not wearing it are to appear less threatening in social situations, or if they want to be sneaky and pass themselves off as a mortal. Which I guess are somewhat similar things. Or maybe just as transport if you’re a monk or something? Typically monks travel in just their robes with a bowl for food and begging, so if they have it banished and find themselves in a spot of trouble they don’t have to head back to their monastery to get it.

                That spot of trouble needs to come with at least a little warning though. You can rush light armor to put it on in 30 seconds, but that’s still too long to use when combat starts. You’d need the kind of advance warning that either has you launching the attack yourself or seeing enemies approaching from afar, possibly by way of scouts or magic or something.

                I think the real reason is that vanishing weapons and armor to elsewhere is considered by many people to be a base competency for exalted, the charm shouldn’t be more effective than the solar version, so making it just a few rounds is out, and there’s enough niche uses to justify not cutting it.

                                                            Originally posted by Chausse View Post

                                                            I'll try to give a more concrete example : Suppose the lunar has the Spinnerets mutation (Lunar manuscript) and he wants to use it while in the shape of the cloud-folk descendant. Can he produce lines of cloud-adhesive stuff that allows him to stay in the theme of the shape he stole, or does he produce his silkery-spidery stuff he would usually produce when he is in his Spider Spirit Shape form and some people may pick-up clues that there is something off about him ?
                                                            Hmmm... On the face of it, being God-Blooded wouldn't prevent somebody from having a mutation that functions normally because it came from some other source. But mutations are also not objective things of the setting, so one could reasonably represent the divine heritage with something aesthetically modified but mechanically identical to Spinnerets.

                                                            Likewise, I feel as though it's reasonable for an externally inflicted mutation to interact with a pre-existing condition to produce something unique, so a spider god might give a human descended from those elementals the Merit and it produces a kind of cloud stuff.

                                                            A Lunar who acquires the shape of a person with that mutation will have the look of it by default. Bringing their own power to create a mutation doesn't necessarily cause that change, but I don't think there's much wrong with letting it do so from time to time.
