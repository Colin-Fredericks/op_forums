    Good point.

    If the PCs are Solars invested into combat, especially if they are combat-optimized, then it is very hard to create an enemy that will give them a challenge 3 on 1. There are few such enemies in the books, narratively speaking there are not supposed to be many creatures this powerful in the world, and it is not easy to design your own (at least not one that creates a fight that is both challenging and fun).

    If the PCs are DBs, there do exist enemies which give them a challenge 3 on 1 - I would think for any given DB builds there should always be a Solar build that can give them a challenge without killing them.

    But people who have posted heavily in the Arena thread probably know more.
    Actually, it is kind of a sliding scale.

    There was a combat run in the Exalted:Arena thread of Ess 2 Solar Invincible Sword Princess with all the Melee charms (so pretty much the strongest Ess 2 supernal combatant you could make) vs 3 immaculates at Ess 3, the 3 Immaculates won (one died due to 1st round alpha strike).

    I like to use this as a convenient benchmark as here we can see that even if you build an absolute cleaver of a character (the strongest Ess 2 Melee combatant in the game, arguably), 3 v 1 is still going to beat you until higher essence, it would seem.

        Ah, that being the case, that will depend enormously on the PCs involved.             I've run combats with PCs against a Single Baddie and it's, pardon my Old Realm here, fucking terrible. I've got a Single Point/War/Sail Eclipse with a steel-willed battlegroup, a Twilight Melee Sorcerer with a Full Survival Tree Familiar, and a Presence Supernal Zenith. It is a goddamn nightmare to run anything short of Octavian at them. I had to install a custom charm/rule that the Sole Baddie only loses 1 initiative when withered (Solars gain full initiative, the baddie just only drops 1) and negate Onslaught penalties or there is no way to dig yourself out of the hole of having all your initiative constantly stolen from you. Even high soak doesn't help that much, because you are constantly bleeding from a being attacked 4 times a turn, and your motes are basically at a 4-to-1 disadvantage.

            The other problem that comes up in actual play is you invariably have a spectrum of "The Jesus of Murder" to "Super Tax Accountant" heroes, and if you do throw Octavian, Ahlat, Fakharu, or whoever at them then you the ST have to walk a fine line of "Instantly Splat the Socialite" or "Use Razor Nightmare Hurricane on the Dawn, use Gentle Pillow Toss on the Twilight". It can also break immersion if not done carefully.

            A PC Solar 1 v 3ing some Dynasts is going to nova one down instantly almost every time because its the best tactic, a 3 Dynast PCs against an NPC Solar are probably not going to have one player draw the short straw and get decapitated before they can even move.

            Please be warned: this is not champagne, this is most likely a duck.

                Originally posted by DrLoveMonkey View Post

                Charms like Fivefold Bulwark Stance, Hail Shattering Practice and Against The World Stance make defending yourself against even fairly powerful opponents almost free. With Fivefold Bulwark Stance you not only ignore almost all penalties to your defense, but it makes Dipping Swallow cost only 1m to activate, and ignore literally all penalties to defense, AND raise your parry by 1. So if you're eating 3 attacks a turn, you spend 3m per turn to raise your parry to 8, no matter what. Then past that there's charms like Hail Shattering Practice, that let you retroactively raise your parry if you get hit, and Against the World Stance, which gives you motes back every time an enemy misses you.

                So with all that it's mega easy to go even 5v1 against most enemies.

                You may run into a bit of a hitch if 5 Essence 5 Immaculate Grandmasters all gang up on you at once or something though.

                I wouldn't say it's "easy". It's a very specific build.

                                                    I'd take what Sorcerous Overlord said with a grain of salt.

                                                    That's not really a core problem with 3e as a system, but with Solars having access to as much power as they do; and has always been an issue with Exalted.

                                                    Hell, it's a problem that goes well past Exalted to most point-buy systems where PCs can vary wildly in their proficiency in any given situation.

                                                    3e's general combat mechanics give plenty of tools for a group of PCs fighting a single enemy to be fun and exciting as there's enough defense in the game to avoid numbers being an automatic win.

                                                    But as I said off the bat, what's fighting matters. Getting ganged up on by a bunch of Solars is bad news for almost anyone in the setting by design. There are very few things in Creation that are supposed to be able to stand up to five Solars at once.

                                                    I also think the "Octavian splats everyone else," problem is a bit overstated. 3e Solars have a surprisingly broad number of ways to be relevant in combat without being the PCs' combat heavy. A lot of non-combat abilities still have some combat utility Charms, there's a lot of good low investment combat ability Charms, and ways to help take down the epic big bads meant to challenge a group of Solars. Sure you can't force the players to take anything, but the investment to not being a liability in a fight against such enemies is small enough that they should at least have enough defenses to ensure something that can be a challenge for a combat maxed out Dawn isn't going to insta-kill everyone else.
