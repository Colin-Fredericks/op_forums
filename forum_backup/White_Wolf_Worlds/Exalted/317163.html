    Tel'aran'rhiod, the world of dreams in exalted. Lunars and dreams theme idea.

    11-20-2014, 05:37 AM
    In Robert Jordans wheel of time series there is a setting element called Tel'aran'rhiod, the world of dreams. This world is a dream-reflection of the waking world. All things in the world of dreams are geographically dependent on the real world. things which are sedentary in the real world such as mountains, forests, buildings and furniture tend to stay put in the world of dreams while things that move or see use, such as utensils, correspondence, tent or wagon encampments or tracks tend to pop in and out of existence since their passing fails to imprints itself on the dreamworld. Normal people may enter the dreamworld while asleep and usually remember this as a very vivid dream. The dreams and nightmares created by normal humans that find their way into the world of dreams sometimes stay put, adding to the weirdness of the world. Tel'aran'rhiod is thus filled with echoes of things that exist and things people wish existed and things people fear. There are artifacts that allow people to enter Tel'aran'rhiod with a higher lever of control and awareness, allowing the user to exploit the opportunities of the world of dreams. There are however some that can enter Tel'aran'rhiod consciously and purposefully on their own - dreamwalkers.

    People who find themselves in Tel'aran'rhiod can control the world of dreams by controlling their subconscious. A skilled dreamwalker can change their appearance, create scenery, levitate, instantly travel to any location in the dreamworld and so forth. Injuries in the world of dreams affect to bodies of dreamers back in the real world, so breaking your leg in a landslide imagined in a nightmare by some random sleeping human is a very real danger, not to mention if you come across another dreamwalker who might conjure up anything nasty they can imagine at you.

    So what are the benefits of the world of dreams? Well, a dreamer can for example take a birds eye view of a battlefield to plan a strategy, visit their enemies headquarters in hopes of finding some evidence of their next move pop into existance briefly, communicate with other dreamwalker allies across the world and evaluate a population or an individuals hopes and fears by observing and drawing conclusions from dream constructs they come across.

    Now why am I talking about this on the exalted forum then? Because this would be a great setting element for No Moon lunars. It can be a way to implement the vision quest and the shamanistic spirit world. Lunars could have a charmbranch based on dreamworld manipulation. It's a quirky, fun and thematically appropriate angle for lunars to be useful. It can be used as alternative to the sidereal heavenly google charm, the sorcery infalliable messenger and a way for lunars to do investigation in a way that is definatley not silver solar. In the exalted setting you could also have sidereals roam around in there when normal astrology or real-world investigation fails them and letting the place be tainted by the nightmares of the Neverborn allows the Abyssals a thematic way in as well.


    What do you folks think?

                      This notion of a dream world bothers me a lot, and I think the reason for that is that I don't want there to be a non-physical space where some splats or specialized characters can go and get an overwhelming advantage over other characters.

                      Currently, ever other "Realm" besides creation is a physical space where you physically have to go to physically do stuff. Mind and body are not separated, and the effects of damage are the same everywhere. If there is some space where that principle isn't true, then you have to put protective measures in place to stop mages and other professional planeswalkers from going there and killing everyone in their sleep.

                      Other games that include otherworld-like spaces at all have such preventative mechanisms, you will note. InShadowrun you can't attack anyone that isn't also Astral while you're on the Astral plane, or do much of anything besides scout. In Changling it is expensive to enter people's dreams on a whim, and you can't do anything in there except mess with their minds. In Unknown Armies you might wander into an otherspace while you sleep, but for all intents and purposes you are physically there, and how imaginative you are won't impact how strong you are or what other abilities you might have.

                      A place like Tel'aran'rhiod is cool as a concept, but as a gameplay space it would be deeply problematic because some characters would be entirely unable to access it while others would be disproportionately powerful there. You might find you've got something like the Decker problem in Shadowrun, in which characters who can effectively use the Matrix a) can basically leave the whole party behind while they go off to play in their special world that only they can really see or interact with and b) can utterly screw up anyone who is dependent on Matrix-using technology but is not personally a Decker, which includes most people who aren't magic.

                          While I do understand semicasual's point, I feel as if an Exalted take on the dreamworld would probably involve preventative mechanisms as well- something uniquely exalted, like "you are physically a part of the sleeping world, and while other people can be present mentally in their sleep, you can only hurt other people who are physically present". Or something, I'm not a dev.

                          I do feel it raises another problem when you start tying it into Lunars, though, because it's really just adding to the thematic incoherency that's dogged the splat through the last two editions. Lunars are already animalistic-shapeshifter-barbarians-who-defend-against-the-wyld-and-serve-luna-but-also-gaia-kind-of-because-of-the-animal-thing-and-are-stewards-of-creation-and-social-engineers*. Do we really to further dilute that with -and-have-special-dream-magic?

                          Don't get me wrong, I think there's something worth looking at here. We've already talked about how the yozis are free to enter the dreaming realm, and I could see the dead and the wyld being able to access it too, maybe. It'd certainly provide an interesting battleground. And kind of explain why the Lunars have been sitting on their asses since the usurpation. And it fits with Luna a bit better then the animal stuff does currently...

                          ...and somehow I'm talking myself around to this idea. Hmm. This, I think I shall ponder.

                          *Or at least that's what they've been in the past. We'll see what the devs do with them this time around.
