When an attacker strikes the wielder, the blade attempts to parry almost of its own accord, striking the attacker's hand and weakening his blow.                 Sounds about right. In most cases, this is going to be equivalent to 1 extra soak gained from Spirit Strengthens the Skin, which costs 1m/die. The ability to make non-artifact weapon hits irrelevant doesn't justify the cost by itself, in my eyes (you're basically spending three times the normal amount in order to eliminate the possibility of taking damage, that's a good deal pretty much only if you're at initiative 1-2 and that damage can crash you), but the synergies with Resistance charms that reset after not taking damage from X attacks make this a very potent and desirable effect.

                Cost: 4m,1W; Mins: Essence 1

                That's... really pricey for what basically amounts to conjuring up +2 Initiative from thin air. Drop the WP cost at least. I'd say 3m would be a reasonable price for the effect as written, but I'd actually rather give it a bonus than reduce the cost. It's a starmetal alloy, how about fiddling with target numbers a bit, like granting reroll 6s on the attack roll in addition to the difficulty reduction, for 4 motes?

                Originally posted by Octopoid View Post
                Aegis of Compassion
                Cost: 3m; Mins: Essence 2
                Type: Reflexive
                Keywords: Perilous
                Duration: Instant
                Prerequisites: Pacify the Hurtful Arms

                With this final Evocation, for which the blade itself was named, the wielder can produce cover from any ambient terrain - or even, if no such terrain is present, from a blurring afterimage of the sword. This Evocation functions as a reflexive Take Cover action, which lasts for only an instant, enough to assist against a single attack and no more. The cover provided depends on the stunt used to describe creating it - a level one stunt provides light cover, level two provides heavy, and a level three stunt provides full cover. This Evocation functions by using the blade to chop cover from the surrounding scenery - hacking down a tree to fall in the way of an incoming attack, churning up a clod of dirt and clay to intercept a blow, or even simply utilizing the blade's blurring afterimage to provide an instant's cover. This last option is always available and always counts as a level one stunt. Remember that repetitive stunts rapidly lose interest and thus only the afterimage can be used repeatedly to create cover. Any other cover so created must be original and imaginative.
                And we're already at the capstone. Short and to the point, I like it.

                However, I'm not entirely convinced that tying its effectiveness to stunt rating is the way to go. It basically means a player is tremendously unlikely to get full cover out of it (3 point stunts are supposed to be highlights of the entire session, with only one or two awarded to the entire group!), and even medium cover won't happen all that often (the guideline says players are supposed to get two or three per session, so even if they get one or two during combat, those aren't very likely to be actions enhanced by this charm).On the other hand, the pricing looks appropriate with that assumption in mind (3 motes for +1 non-charm defense is fairly generous, but balanced by the fact that it's basically useless if the battlefield already has plenty of cover).

                Also, the wording is slightly unclear - it's said to function as a reflexive Take Cover action (which is rolled with Dex + Dodge), yet it has the wielder using the blade to create cover from the scenery. Does it count as a Take Cover action that's automatically successful? Is it a regular Take Cover action? Maybe a Take Cover action that's rolled with Melee instead of Dodge? Hard to tell.

                        Hello here is another artifact in need of a peer review. I'm a little worried the capstone might be too powerful but I was unsure of where else to go with it. I tried to make sure that the player had plenty of reasons to expend charges, so every charm after essence 1 requires at least one charge, All but 1 evocation benefit from expending a charge.

                        Ravaging Flame
                        Artifact 4 Soul steel and Red Jade Reaper Daiklave

                        Forged for a Dragon Blooded agent of the First and Forsaken Lion, Ravaging Flame has earned it's name quickly as it hacked through armies, torn through defenses and burned a bloody swath through history. When the shogunate came to an end the Daiklave was glutted on battle and it's legacy was considered long and graced in slaughter. Eventually though that Dragon Blooded traitor was put down and is blade with thrust deep into river Styx without a realization that it had been given back to it's master. This malevolent blade passed from one servant to another until finally it awoke in a blaze of bloodshed that would have made the Neverborn weep tears of joy.

                        Eventually the blade wound up in the hands of an abyssal who died at the hands of a newly returned Solar who claimed the blade for himself. Breaking the pattern of it being returned to the underworld by those who did not understand it's nature. For this blade was forged from the vindictive souls of those who burned to death. The red jade in the blade channels the rage they feel and the need to burn with the same fires they were burnt to death in. The soul steel channels their need to bring suffering and brings it to blazing upon the fools who stand against this blade.

                        Evocations of Ravaging Flame
                        When a tuned by a Solar, Dragon Blood, or Abyssal this blade burns brighter and hotter than at any other time. When ever it successfully deals lethal damage with a decisive attack it generates a flame charge.
                        Each flame charge adds +1 withering damage. Ravaging Flame can only hold 4 charges.

                        Shield Shattering Blow
                        Cost 3m, 3i ; Mins essence 1
                        type: Reflexive
                        Keywords: Uniform
                        Duration: Instant
                        The burning vengeance of the vengeful ghosts traded within the steel of this sword will not be denied just because someone happens to be quick with a shield. This Evocation brings the cleaving blade against the foolish thing that attempts to block it and rends and burns though it. Against mundane shields and weapons this evocation destroys what ever was used to parry the attack shattering it. Against an artifact it is thrown 1 range band away.
                        Special Activation Rules: If a flame charge is expended as a part of activating this charm, than the blast of shattering flame burn the person holding the shield and they take an amount of damage equal to the overpower value of the weapon, this Initiative is gained by the weirder but not the +1 for a successful hit. If this causes initiative crash this bonus is also not gained.

                        Pyre Flame Radiance
                        Cost -; Mins Essence 1
                        Type: Permanet
                        Keywords: Decsiive - Only
                        Duration: Permanent
                        Prerequisite Charms: None
                        Gorging on the fire of life Ravaging Flame glows brighter than it would after just tasting blood. Generate 2 flame charges when a foe is slain with the weapon.

                        Burning Armor Touch
                        Cost 3m, 1+ FC; Mins: Essence 2
                        Type: Supplemental
                        Keywords: Decisive Only
                        Duration: Instant
                        Prerequisite Charms: Pyre Flame Radiance, Shield Shattering Blow
                        The burning spirits traded within the blade set alight the flesh of another making their armor, burning hot, and setting clothing on fire. This charm supplements a decisive attack and causes the subject if hit to be lit on fire taking environmental damage every round for a number of rounds equal to essence + Flame Charges expended. The difficulty to avoid this damage is increased by 1 for every flame charge expended.

                        Pyre Burns
                        Cost 4m, 1+ FC; Mins: Essence 2
                        Type: Reflexive
                        Keywords: Withering-Only
                        Prerequisite Charms: Burning Armor Touch
                        The Roar of the funeral pyre brings such a strong heat that it can slowly cook the audience if they are not careful to stand back far enough. Thus does life fear fire, injuries from such are sever and fester easily, and worse yet cause pain that is so terrible those who die from this method of execution prey to suffocate before the flames finish with them as a final mercy. So it is with the flames of rage this weapon channels. The spirits burned to death seek to visit that pain and fate on others and they will stop at nothing to do so. For every Flame charge expended up to 1/2 the initiative gained apply a -2 penalty to dice pools, and a -1 penalty to defense for essence rounds as the painful burn from contact with the blade make movement and though more difficult.

                        Burning Ghostfire Torrent
                        Cost 5m, 1wp, 1+FC; Mins: Essence 3
                        Type: Simple
                        Keywords: Decisive-only
                        Prerequisites: Pyre Burns
                        It doesn't take long for those who have mastered the Ravaging Flame to to realize that the power to project a holocaust of it's fire exists but it takes mastery of self to be able to draw out that power and deliver it to the field of battle without being immolated one's self. This charm creates a ranged decisive attack out to short range which adds Essence automatic success, this attack is unblockable. This attack Consumes the users Initiative, they we be left with 0 initiative regardless if it hits or misses, and all Flame Charges on the blade are likewise consumed. Decrease the target number for damage success by 1 for each Flame Charge charge consumed to a minimum of 4. Any excess charges add +2 damage.

                                            The Captain’s Bulwark – Orichalcum Shield (***)

                                            The Captain’s Bulwark dates back to the Primordial War, when an aspiring Twilight forged the discus of unbreakable orichalcum to protect his circlemate from the worst ravages the enemies of the gods could offer. It was carried into battle by one of the finest warriors of the Solar host, who won victory after victory against the spawn of the Primordials. In the end, the warrior himself was lost in a reality flux in battle with one of the Primordials themselves, and neither his Exaltation nor the shield have been seen since.

                                            The Captain’s Bulwark was a concave disk of orichalcum with straps of behemoth-leather. Its design was deliberately subdued, providing a greater focus on the Captain than the shield. Its outer surface was most famously lacquered with a series of concentric circles around a central image of the sun, though its current appearance could be wildly different, if it even still exists.

                                            Evocations of the Captain’s Bulwark:

                                            A Solar who attunes to the Captain’s Bulwark gives the weapon the Thrown (Short) tag.

                                            Righteous Ricochet
                                            Cost: 5m; Mins: Essence 1
                                            Type: Reflexive
                                            Keywords: Decisive-only
                                            Duration: Instant
                                            Prerequisites: None

                                            The least of the Bulwark’s powers is a simple ricochet trick, guided by its wielder’s Essence. This Evocation can only be activated when the wielder succeeds at a withering Thrown attack made with the Captain’s Bulwark. If the attack is successful, immediately before gaining any Initiative, the wielder can make one reflexive decisive Thrown attack with the Captain’s Bulwark at any one target within Close range of the first target.


                                            Righteous Recall
                                            Cost: None; Mins: Essence 1
                                            Type: Permanent
                                            Keywords: None
                                            Duration: Permanent
                                            Prerequisites: Call the Blade

                                            The Captain’s Bulwark always returns to its wielder’s hand, sometimes by thoroughly circuitous routes. This Evocation is a permanent enhancement to its prerequisite, reducing the Charm’s mote cost to zero if it is being used to recall the Captain’s Bulwark.


                                            Righteous Recoil
                                            Cost: 6m; Mins: Essence 1
                                            Type: Reflexive
                                            Keywords: None
                                            Duration: Instant
                                            Prerequisites: None

                                            The nature of the Bulwark is to absorb the force of any incoming attack, and so it does. This Evocation may be used in response to any attack that overcame its wielder’s Parry Defense, if that Defense was calculated using the Captain’s Bulwark. Any knockdown or knockback effects caused by that attack are wholly nullified, as the Bulwark absorbs and disperses the force of the blow without consequence to its wielder.

                                                Originally posted by Octopoid View Post
                                                Righteous Ricochet
                                                Cost: 5m; Mins: Essence 1
                                                Type: Reflexive
                                                Keywords: Withering-only
                                                Duration: Instant
                                                Prerequisites: None

                                                The least of the Bulwark’s powers is a simple ricochet trick, guided by its wielder’s Essence. This Evocation can only be activated when the wielder succeeds at a decisive Thrown attack made with the Captain’s Bulwark. If the attack is successful, immediately after resetting to base Initiative, the wielder can make one reflexive withering Thrown attack with the Captain’s Bulwark at any one target within Close range of the first target.
                                                For a mere 5 motes, you can almost completely nullify the downside of a Decisive attack by immediately attempting a Withering! Obviously, it's not a guarrantee, but I'm not sure I actually like this effect's impact on the game mechanics, because it means you're not likely to often end up in the danger zone. If it was reversed, letting you do a Decisive after a Withering, or even 2 Decisive's back to back (with the second one only using Base Initiative) I'd be more favorable to it.

                                                Originally posted by Octopoid View Post
                                                Righteous Recall
                                                Cost: None; Mins: Essence 1
                                                Type: Permanent
                                                Keywords: None
                                                Duration: Permanent
                                                Prerequisites: Call the Blade

                                                The Captain’s Bulwark always returns to its wielder’s hand, sometimes by thoroughly circuitous routes. This Evocation is a permanent enhancement to its prerequisite, reducing the Charm’s mote cost to zero if it is being used to recall the Captain’s Bulwark.
                                                Nothing wrong with this one, aside from I feel it's odd to fork it off of a melee charm instead of just creating an evocation that says you can reflexively call it back to you. Otherwise, it seems like the sort of thing the Captain should be able to do.

                                                Originally posted by Octopoid View Post
                                                Righteous Recoil
                                                Cost: 1m; Mins: Essence 1
                                                Type: Reflexive
                                                Keywords: None
                                                Duration: Instant
                                                Prerequisites: None

                                                The nature of the Bulwark is to absorb the force of any incoming attack, and so it does. This Evocation may be used in response to any attack that overcame its wielder’s Parry Defense, if that Defense was calculated using the Captain’s Bulwark.                                                 I'm reluctant to approve of anything that perfectly nullifies an effect like knockback, especially at Essence 1, and especially for 1 mote. It could be bonus dice to it, or it could be like 6m in cost. But I've seen (and personally designed) a lot of knockback affects, and to negate them for a one-time evocation purchase and 1 mote is... dismissive.

                                                But conceptually, this is all in the right space! Could go upwards from here with adding ricochets, deflecting attacks, absorbing falling damage, etc.

                                                    Well, here it is. My Artifact Grimoire. It is enormous by my standards. It clocks in at 7 evocations, in 2 trees which meet at the top. Some of it is solid. Some of it is shakey. I think one evocation is broken. I KNOW it's a slog, but since I made this on crowd recommendation, I'd appreciate a little feedback. Thanks guys!

                                                    Opus of Antiquity (Starmetal Grimoire/Dire Chains *****)

                                                    Orphene Sagastorm was one of the first twilights to ever grace Creation. Receiving her second breath at the end of the great war of the gods, she showed no talent for combat, and yet she loved war. She displayed no skill at politics, yet she never missed a debate. She claimed no skill in the Occult, yet she studied books and scrolls from every corner of the globe. Orphene loved one thing before all others: history. She wanted to witness and record every major battle, every sweeping decree, every fact of history so that it was never lost to the ravages of time.

                                                    It was to this end that she set down to forging her masterwork. Together with her Sidereal partner, a woman of limitless patience, who loved Orphene despite being forever second to her thirst for knowledge, the twilight set to creating a single central tome to record her work. She wove the pages from the silk of pattern spiders, the binding from thin filiments of Starmetal, and the cover bound in the leather of a rare god-blooded elk blessed with immortality by the moon and the stars. The book was then wrapped with chains of solid Starmetal and fixed with a lock of pure Orichalcum which bared no keyhole.

                                                    The pages would keep only the events recorded by fate in the purest form through the eyes and words of its author. The binding and cover would keep the words eternal and unravaged by time, while also allowing an infinite number of pages to exist within its confines. The chains would keep the knowledge and power of infinite history written within contained and safely channeled. The lock would ensure none but those worthy of the book could ever release its potent power.

                                                    The Opus of Antiquity is a Starmetal Grimoire wrapped in Dire Chains. It functions as a weapon when it needs to, but is primarily designed as a record of history. The book may be carried with one hand, but when the chains are active, it must be wielded with two hands to contain the powers of the book in the absence of its restraints. When not in use, the chains will retract to bind the book closed and the lock will snap shut, once again containing the power of the Opus.

                                                    Evocations of Opus of Antiquity
                                                    When a Solar or Sidereal spends the cost of 7 motes to attune to the Opus of Antiquity, the exalt can feel through the chains that bind the ancient tome. With but a thought, the unbreakable lock on these ancient restraints snaps open and the chains come alive. The Starmetal chains move in accordance to the will of the wielder and can extend beyond their apparent length to accomplish their goal within Close range. For all intents and purposes, these function as Dire Chains with the usual tags under the control of their wielder.

                                                    The exalt may substitute their Lore rating for Melee or Martial Arts to control the chains, but then the cap for dice added from charms is half the normal pool rounded up. Attacks with the chains may be enhanced as normal with Martial Arts or Melee charms, but must use the appropriate ability rating to do so.

                                                    Unbound Knowledge
                                                    Cost: 2m Mins: Essence 1
                                                    Type: Supplemental
                                                    Keywords: Uniform
                                                    Duration: Instant
                                                    Prerequisites: None

                                                    The chains that hold the Opus of Antiquity are attuned to knowledge, and can reveal it as easily as withhold it. An successful attack supplemented by Unbound Knowledge reveals a single piece of information about the target chosen by the exalt upon activating this evocation.
                                                    • The highest rated combat ability and its strength (number of dots). If used multiple times, this evocation reveals the next highest combat ability, or the highest non-combat ability if none remain
                                                    • An Intimacy that is actively being pursued or defended at the moment and its intensity. If used multiple times, this reveals another related Intimacy and so on until no applicable Intimacies remain
                                                    • The target’s permanent Essence or Willpower score
                                                    Only 1 piece of knowledge may be revealed per attack. If the exalt knows at least 2 pieces of knowledge about a foe, they increase all defenses against that target by 1. This increase is a non-charm bonus. Facts discovered through means other than this power cannot benefit Opus of Antiquity or its evocations and must still be revealed through this Unbound Knowledge. All discovered facts fade from the Opus’ knowledge at the end of the scene.
                                                    Authors notes
                                                    I wanted a hook to hang future evocations off of with the chains, and with Starmetal as the selected material, I decided to have it reveal hidden truths about the target. And, while it is building up for future powers, it also needed an immediate benefit to this knowledge, hence the defense. Basically, it's translating one kind of power for another.


                                                    Primer of Yesteryear
                                                    Cost: 5m Mins: Essence 1
                                                    Type: Simple
                                                    Keywords: None
                                                    Duration: One Scene
                                                    Prerequisites: None

                                                    The Opus is a history book without peer and the words within it are a living document of the past. When the exalt faces a problem to be solved through Bureaucracy, Investigation, Lore, or Occult, they may attempt to use the Opus of Antiquity to help aid them.

                                                    When the exalt successfully introduces an original fact that explains a past event in history and how it tangentially relates to a current subject or problem, the wielder may open the Opus and channel the event through it. The historical event referenced then appears as a ghostly image before the exalt and any gathered. The display shows the event accurately, but the only sound that plays is the narration of the event as recorded by the original author. By spending a scene analysing this projection, the exalt gains a flash of insight into the current predicament and gains the lower of 3 or Essence non-charm automatic successes on a roll to learn or apply such knowledge. This event cannot be referenced for the same purpose more than once.

                                                    Special Activation Rules: Primer of Yesteryear only unlocks to an exalt who has spent either a season or a story studying the lessons and stories of the Opus of Antiquity. This means at least 8 hours of study a week for the duration.
                                                    Authors notes:
                                                    This is the beginning of my other tree, where it was suggested that I "weaponize history". It needed to start small, so I figured... this is a history book, it should benefit Lore somehow. So this was an attempt to reveal hidden truths combined with exuding God-miracles. This was also my first attempt at something I saw the devs mention recently. Evocations are supposed to be a story and/or representation of the relationship one has with their artifact. It's not just a power-stick. This is why Special Activation Rules exist and why some weapons are designed with restrictions for learning, unlocking, and using their evocations. It wants you to have a relationship with your artifact. SO, the Opus has 2 trees. The chains which you can learn regardless and just be a chain book guy, and then the pages which require effort and study, giving you time to really connect with the book. That was my thinking anyways.


                                                    Art of War
                                                    Cost: 4m, 1+A Mins: Essence 2
                                                    Type: Reflexive
                                                    Keywords: Counterattack
                                                    Duration: Instant
                                                    Prerequisites: Unbound Knowledge

                                                    The Opus of Antiquity contains knowledge of strategums and maneuvers from dozens of histories famous and infamous warlords. By channeling this knowledge, the exalt gains insight into how to counter the actions of their foes.

                                                    Once the exalt has gathered at least 3 pieces of knowledge about a foe revealed via Unbound Knowledge, they may reflexively activate Art of War in response to a gambit from that target. Declared after a successful attack roll but before the gambit is rolled, the exalt may spend the cost of the evocation and shed levels of their anima to increase the difficulty of the gambits being attempted by 1 per Anima level.

                                                    If the attacking gambit is unsuccessful after using Art of War, the wielder may reflexively attempt to counter with an identical gambit if they haven’t used their combat action yet this round.
                                                    Authors notes:
                                                    Back to the chains. I had a different effect in mind for this one until I came upon the name Art of War. Brilliant for a comprehensive book of history right? Of course this book will contain strategies of famous warlords, so I figured it should be useful in countering such strategies. If I were more knowledgable in mass combat rules, I'd include stuff for actual Strategems. So if you're savvy in that area, this is where you'd add that ability to this artifact. Anyways this falls into unraveling tactics.


                                                    Adversary Composition
                                                    Cost: 10m, 1wp Mins: Essence 3
                                                    Type: Simple
                                                    Keywords: Decisive-Only
                                                    Duration: One Scene
                                                    Prerequisites: Art of War


                                                    Through knowledge of a thing, one can begin to understand its nature. Through knowledge of nature, one can transfigure the understanding of a thing. When the exalt knows at least 5 pieces of knowledge revealed via Unbound Knowledge, they may unleash a special gambit attack against foe that warps a quality of their nature. This attack is a shaping effect and is subject to any rules that interact with such.

                                                    On a successful gambit at difficulty equal to the target’s Essence, the exalt chooses an aspect to alter in their target. For every 2 threshold successes on the gambit roll, the exalt may select an additional effect to inflict upon their foe.
                                                    • Alter the emotional context of a Tie or swap the context (positive/negative) of a Principle
                                                    • Swap the intensities of two revealed Intimacies
                                                    • Swap the rating of a revealed combat ability with that of a revealed non-combat ability
                                                    • Swap the rating of any two Attributes from different categories (Physical/Mental/Social). Use of this power does not confer knowledge of those Attribute ratings
                                                    • Swap Personal/Peripheral essence pools
                                                    • Swap Essence and Willpower ratings. Temporary Willpower above the new permanent rating is reduced by an equal number
                                                    • Other effects may be negotiated with Storyteller. All effects must be a swapping of current states, never created or eliminated ones
                                                    All changes last for the rest of the scene before fading away and reverting to normal.
                                                    Author's notes
                                                    This... is the evocation I feel pretty shaky about. I tried to come up with a long list of interesting powers of translating one kind of power into another and striking or harnessing ephemeral concepts but I admit to struggling with this the most. I'm very open to other suggestions, but I didn't want it to interact with Initiative or Defense scores at all, because that quickly unbalances things. It's supposed to be a weird one and I think I succeeded in that regard.


                                                    Doctrine of History
                                                    Cost: 10m Mins: Essence 3
                                                    Type: Simple
                                                    Keywords: None
                                                    Duration: 8 hours
                                                    Prerequisites: Primer of Yesteryear

                                                    For an exalt that has learned to truly harness their Essence, the Opus of Antiquity lies waiting for their contribution. The lorekeeper activates the evocation and then spends at least 8 uninterrupted hours recording every conceivable detail of a significant event that they have personally witnessed. The cost of this evocation is committed for the duration of the recording, and if the session is interrupted, the power deactivates and the words the exalt recorded evaporate from the pages as dust in a breeze.

                                                    At the end of this session, the Opus of Antiquity glows brightly with starry light as the words are committed to the tome forever. The recorded event is forever bound to the Opus and may be recalled in the future via Primer of Yesteryear. Additionally, the exalt who recorded it is granted a free permanent specialty pertaining to the subject of which they wrote. Finally, spending time recording events in the Opus is considered a magically accelerated training session. Training times for raising a single knowledge-based non-combat Ability, or Mental-based Attribute may be completed entirely during the duration of Doctrine of History.

                                                    This evocation cannot be used more than once per story unless reset by using Primer of Yesteryear to resolve another significant situation.

                                                    Special Activation Rules: Doctrine of History may only be learned after the exalt resolves at least 3 problems or situations thanks to the evocation Primer of Yesteryear, as learning to understand the function and flow of the recorded history and its powers is crucial to properly contributing to the volume.
                                                    Author's notes
                                                    Back over to the history tree, this evocation is meant to be the one that keys it's predicessor. Yes, it is weird that it takes a higher essence evocation to create things for a lesser one to use, but I wanted it to represent a higher mastery of Essence to be able to interact with the book on such a level. Anyone can pull information from it, but it takes a master to write to it. I got to the end of it though and found it didn't have... much bite. Much mechanical benefit to it. So I tacked on the increased training time as an afterthought. I don't know how it translated in the end, but if it feels weird, that's why.


                                                    Yarn of Battle
                                                    Cost: 10m, 1wp, +(5+m, 1+wp) Mins: Essence 4
                                                    Type: Simple
                                                    Keywords: None
                                                    Duration: One Scene
                                                    Prerequisites: Doctrine of History

                                                    The stories of many great and terrible battles are recorded in the pages of the Opus of Antiquity. At the decree of the lorekeeper, the armies of these battles stand ready to be commanded. Activating Yarn of Battle causes misty images of armies long forgotten to appear before the exalt. This army is not made of spirits, they are simply reflections of history and are very real to the foes of the exalt.

                                                    This evocation creates a Battlegroup of Battle Ready Troops. The context of their appearance is left at the behest of the wielder based on which battle they were summoned from, but their stats remain the same. Yarn of Battle creates a Size 1 group with Poor Drill, and Might 0. However, for every 5 additional motes spent on the activation, the exalt may increase the Size by 1. For every additional Willpower spent, they may increase the Drill by 1. By increasing the reset of this evocation to one Season, the exalt may Increase the Might to 1.

                                                    This Battlegroup is loyal to the wielder of the Opus and vanishes at the end of the scene. This evocation may only be used once per day unless extended to 1 season as written above.

                                                    Special Activation Rules: Yarn of Battle may only be learned after the exalt has contributed the story of at least 3 significant events to the Opus of Antiquity via the evocation Doctrine of History.
                                                    Author's notes
                                                    This evocation I knew would be in there from the first moment it was suggested to me. It was the first thought I had. Summon an army from history. I like this one a lot. I think I have the power-up balanced. I could just as easily be convinced that it should take 10m per increase in Size, but I was trying to compare it to similar abilities (of which there aren't many). This is yet another example of requiring the bond between wielder and artifact to improve.


                                                    Saga Convocation
                                                    Cost: 15m, 1wp Mins: Essence 5
                                                    Type: Reflexive
                                                    Keywords: Decisive-Only
                                                    Duration: Instant
                                                    Prerequisites: Yarn of Battle, Adversary Composition

                                                    The greatest power of the Opus of Antiquity is its terrifying ability to absorb the heroes of past generations. Those upon death’s door who willingly submit to the will of the Opus may choose to become a champion of the book instead of dying. This power effectively removes the will of the subject (and removing their ability to be a PC) making them a slave to the book and records them as an echo within the pages of the tome.

                                                    Alternately, when a Decisive attack with the Opus of Antiquity successfully deals a foe’s last health level of damage, the exalt may reflexively activate Saga Convocation attempt to force the foe into the book instead of killing them. This functions as a contested permanent Willpower roll, with a bonus or difficulty of the difference between (exalt’s Resolve - target’s Resolve). On a failure, the target dies normally instead.

                                                    All such characters absorbed into the Opus are pulled in via the Starmetal chains and are considered Retainers. Yarn of Battle upgrades upon learning Saga Convocation to allow the summoning of these champions for the base evocation cost with no ability to upgrade them. These champions are much more potent than the echoes of armies long past, retaining their charms, abilities, and other powers. However, the Opus struggles to contain such power, and using a champion in this manner releases it at the end of the scene. The tome may contain no more than 3 champions at a time.
                                                    Author's notes
                                                    The capstone. I must have rewritten this one the most. I think it ended up working out as balanced. It was hard coming up with a difficulty to drag something into it. I couldn't think of all the possible situations that could be in here, but at least Willpower rolls aren't modifiable. But I'm happy with how it came out. Believe it or not, the willing subject was the afterthought on this one. I also loved the idea of the chains pulling a foe into the book to be trapped as a servant. hehe
