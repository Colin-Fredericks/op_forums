    Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post
    The Usurpation is improbable mechanically because the mechanics aren't built to model dozens of Celestial and hundreds of Terrestrial Exalted all mashed together in a horrific scrum.
    I very much agree with this vis a vis the Calibration feast. To add a different phrasing of I think the same idea: mechanically, a battle including dozens to hundreds of exalts on both sides will hinge heavily on effects which can effect an indefinite number of people without concern for friendly fire. This is probably not an accurate measure of the setting, so should probably be taken as meaning the mechanics are designed around the middle, and reach odd conclusions at the extremes. Because of this, it probably isn't helpful to consider the precise unreasonable outcomes of the mechanics.

    But that leaves me nothing to do, so I'll instead switch from considering AoE attacks to friendly AoEs.

    Battle Anthem of the Ten Thousand Dragons: Possibly Team DB only, but a single loyalist DB knowing the charm could grant it to the Solars. Grants 1 free die to attack rolls, movement, and command actions. Allied battlegroups get a die to resist rout. Successful withering attacks grant 1 bonus point of initiative.
    Battle Anthem of the Lunar Exalted: Probably Team Solar only. Bonus die to attack rolls. Rage based intimacies count as 1 level higher. Battlegroups get lower of (essence or performance) dice to attacks and rout checks.
    Battle Anthem of the Solar Exalted: Probably Team Solar only. Grants 1i per round, allied battlegroups gain (essence) dice to attacks. Soul Voice increases this to 2i, and (essence) successes
    Soul-Stirring Cantata: Probably Team Solar only. Grants 1m/round, doubled by Soul Voice.
    Heroism-Encouraging Ballad: Probably Team Solar only. Double intimacy bonuses against fear. Allied battlegroups gain (essence) successes to rally for numbers.
    Inspiring Battle Hymn: Both teams can probably have this. Reroll 1s on join battle, +1 resolve against fear.
    Aria of Victory: Both teams can probably have this, Team DB may be inhibited by the Terrestrial keyword. Grants 1 willpower each turn, which lasts for that turn and possibly cannot be spent on charms. Terrestrial forces you to commit 4m/turn, but who cares, one terrestrial losing a bunch of motes is pretty small compared to the melee supernal killing 5 people/turn over there.

    All of these rely on you being heard, which possibly means they don't actually affect everyone in the battle.
    Maybe you can use noise-enhancing charms, but presumably those don't work if everyone is using them.
    Actually, how many of the survivors of the Usurpation had hearing loss from it?

    Any social influence charms could qualify, really. Spend a bunch of motes on your roll, force every enemy you can communicate with to either spend motes resisting, or willpower.

    Even if shouting as loudly as you can is useless because any influence is drowned out by everyone else's shouting, it's still useful to deny your enemy coordination/willpower removal by drowning them out. Telepathy charms are great for this.

    Seems like the solar wide area of charms will beat out the dragon blooded battle anthem, except I'm probably missing a handful of charms. I would worry that Team DB can more afford to have 10 people stand to the side singing than Team Solar can.

    From this I (sarcastically) conclude that the Usurpation included several dozen Dragonblooded shouting encouragements, threats, requests for surrender, tactical advice, etc at superhuman volumes and/or inside people's minds, as well as a handful of Solars doing the same for the other side.

                                                    The wonderful thing about 3e is it doesn't try to nail down the Usurpation for us, instead telling us to try and imagine what happened for ourselves. The fact that we have no hard facts means we're free to assume that things happened that history never recorded. Perhaps in Nara-O's Big Ol' Book of Secrets-n-Stuff is a tale of the greatest Night Caste to ever have lived. A night caste who disgusted with the decadence and madness he saw around him decided to do something about it. Maybe he worked with the Sidereals even agreeing to have his own essence sealed away when things were over to remove the Solar menace from existence forever. Or maybe he pulled a Rakan Thulio and manipulated his fellow Solars while never letting the Sidereals know he was on their side. Numinous asked how a caste of assassins could have been assassinated and others wondered how the secret could have been kept. Maybe the individual most trusted to ferret out the enemies of the Solars manipulated them into never seeing the threat until it was too late. This is all apart of my personal head cannon of course, but I think traitors amongst the Solars and Lunars is a feasible possibilty that we ignore because it's never been stated. Well much about the Usurpation is a mystery and if no one discovered the traitors or it's being held as a super tightly guarded secret we wouldn't know. I still think it's another possibility to consider when you're trying to simply wrap your head around how the Usurpation could have possibly happened.
