                                                            So, my thoughts on the matter...not all of this is canon, a bunch of it is my thoughts on how it should be, and how I represent them.

                                                            The Lunar 'Miracle.'
                                                            I think they are off to an okay start with Shapechanging (and the nature associated with it) being the 'core' of the Lunar Exalted, I just don't think they took it far enough. Beyond just changing form...a Lunar should be fully capable of 'becoming' something else. When they become an animal, it comes with the full kit of instincts and reflexes that the animal would have. But that's just the tip of the iceberg. As they grow in power, the extent to which they 'become' a form is a choice. At the most basic, they simply adopt the shape, but as they delve deeper into becoming that form, the more like it they are. Eventually, at the height of their power, they can truly become anything or anyone. For example, if a Lunar took the Heart's Blood of a guardsman and chose to fully 'become' that form, they would gain access to the former guardsman's memories, access to his intimacies (and the ability to temporarily adopt them as your own), and would fully adopt the guardsman's personality as their own. And, as their power grows beyond the need to take Heart's Blood, and can make due temporarily with just a sample, allowing them to temporarily become someone else, without having to kill that person. And a powerful enough Lunar is not even limited to mortal things.

                                                            In Social terms, the same concept of 'change' would apply. A Lunar could rewire their mentality at will in order to become the best person possible to win this argument. This was often used to great effect in the First Age, where the Lunars could play the perfect Devil's Advocate for their mates by literally changing themselves into a person who believed what they needed to argue. Furthermore, if a Lunar was losing an argument, they could simply become someone to whom that argument would slide off of them. But that's only when a Lunar decides to argue with you to your face...

                                                            On a grand scheme, regarding their powers beyond "I can turn into other things," I think they should have abilities that primarily work on an instinctive (sub-conscious), animalistic, and group-based mechanic. While a Solar would look at a project that doesn't have enough skilled laborers to work on it, shrug, and do it all himself...a Lunar may grant a number of mortals an instinctive understanding of how to assist with the project. Solars are great at inspiring people to follow them, Lunars would instead bind people into a tight-knit pack that pursues a common goal, rather than a person. If a Solar wants to provide housing for a group of people, they'll slap it together overnight, or just conjure it up with Sorcery...a Lunar would influence the people to operate with the instinctive efficiency of an ant colony to construct their own housing.

                                                            And, to build on the commentary made about Lunars and being Slaves...I do think that Lunars should have the widest variety of ways by which they can go 'you can't control me,' whether physical or social...

                                                            The Role of the Lunars
                                                            I see the Lunars as the counterpoint to the Solars. Each are complete on their own, but the two compliment one another so well that they may wonder how they functioned before. A Solar is implacable, a Lunar is flexible. A Solar leads from the top, a Lunar builds up the people. A Solar is resolute and unchanging, a Lunar adapts on the fly. The actions of the Lunars may be eclipsed by those of the Solars, because what a Lunar doesn't isn't as in your face and obvious as what a Solar does. The Solars dreamed of an Empire and built it with their own hands...but the Lunars built the society into something that could support what the Solars had wrought. In fact, by this standard...it is possible that the Shogunate managed to exist only because of how tightly woven the Lunars had made the people of the Solar Empire. The world was shattered with titanic battles, and the Dragonborn fought with the Solars and Lunars for years...and yet, when they returned, they found that society had been chugging along without them as best as they could, rather than dissolving into all out anarchy that would have completely shattered the Blessed Isle as a base of power.

                                                            The Silver Pact
                                                            Unfortunately, this is easily explained as being the result of the Elder Lunars. Of all the mortals in all of Creation...the Lunars are the only ones still alive who lost all the glories that were theirs. The Terrestrials of the Usurpation are long dead. No Solars survived it. And the Sidereals still have Yu Shan. No one is more keenly aware of how far the world has fallen than the Lunar Elders who lived through it, and no one has lost nearly as much. That, coupled with the Breaking of the Bond (whatever that is), along with a probably dose of untreated PTSD had probably led the Lunar Elders into hating the Dragonblooded with an unrivaled passion...and possibly drove some of them at least a little bit mad.

                                                            So, along comes a newly Exalted Lunar, who just got rescued from a bunch of angry Dragonblooded screaming "Die, foul Anathema!" and brought out to one of the domains of the Lunars. They are new to their power, and an Elder Lunar speaks to them and offers to protect them and train them until they are ready to go back out to make their own way. Often, such a Lunar may accept such a generous offer...and then spends the rest of their time training with this Lunar hearing of the atrocities of the Dragonblooded and the Sidereals...and tales of the First Age...and takes part in the Elder's schemes to cast down the Realm because, well, the Elder is their mentor.

                                                            Then, when the day comes that it is time for them to go their own way...well, they have been so well taught on the evils of the Dragonblooded and the necessity of destroying the Realm, that they now believe it to be true. And if the time comes that they take on a mentee, they too will tell stories passed down to them from their mentor, and speak of the evils of the Dragonblooded.

                                                            So, perhaps the 'chains' that the Lunars need to throw off are actually the Elders, and the passed-down hate the Elders have for the ones who took everything from them. Perhaps the Silver Pact is actually Silver Chains binding the Chosen of Luna into a path of destruction. Perhaps it is time to stop fighting their ancestors' war. Perhaps it is time to do something better.
