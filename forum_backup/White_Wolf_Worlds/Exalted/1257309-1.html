                            ST: Okay, you ready for some solo gaming?
                            PC: Excited to start. So I'm playing a scavenger lord, right?
                            ST: Yeah, expect a lot of combat though. There will be some puzzles, but you'll definitely be punching some legionnaires.
                            PC: Hm. I'll still take Mental primary, though. I played a combat monster last time, so I want the fighting to be challenging this time. Also, I was dirt poor, so this time I want to have some dots in Resources.
                            ST: Can you justify that in game?
                            PC: Uh, hm... ah! I have it. He's a professor at a university! They have those in Creation, right?
                            ST: Yeah, that will work. Could be they send him out on adventures to get artifacts and relics and stuff.
                            PC: I like that, it sounds cool. Still needs some character, though. Do you mind if I take a Flaw?
                            ST: Sure, you'll get Solar XP when I bring it up. What did you have in mind?
                            PC: Ophidiophobia! Snakes have always creeped me out.
                            ST: Nice. Alright, I'm thinking I'll start you off first with a simple dungeon crawl, maybe introduce your nemesis. See how you like the way your character plays.
                            PC: Sounds good. Then what?
                            ST: Well, I'm thinking the local government will come to you. I haven't decided if they know you're a Solar yet, but they're definitely at odds with the Realm. They've got word that a Great House is looking for some incredible artifact, something made for a god that predates the Primordials. Spooky army-crushing stuff.
                            PC: Awesome. Just one last thing: my character has a whip. And a hand crossbow! Those are cool.

                                                Originally posted by LeTipex View Post
                                                A young farmer receives a missive from a king rebelling against the Mask of Winter, asking for the help of a great heroine. He stumbles upon the heroine in question, an old gold faction sidereal hiding in the desert, exalts when her family is killed by the Mask's troops, and with the help of a night caste pirate and her trusty beastman sidekick, they go rescue the king, now captured by a terrible Dusk Caste clad in Soulsteel armor.

                                                Plot twist : the Dusk Caste is the young Solar's mother.
                                                ...the Force is strong with this one.

                                                The PCs: An Eclipse from the Realm currently running a teahouse in Thorns, an Abyssal Day caste serving the Mask of Winters as an enforcer, the Eclipse's Lunar Mate (a resident of Thorns who Exalted rescuing some of her loved ones during the invasion), and her lover- a Dragonblooded who survived the occupation, and has figured out the Mask's secret weakness and is trying to get safely back to House Cathak with it.

                                                The Plot: the Lunar and Dragonblood are trying to smuggle the secret weakness MacGuffin out of town to get it to the Realm. Eclipse is trying to gather intel on the Deathlord for the Gold faction. Abyssal is secretly looking for a way out from under the Mask of Winter's thumb. Nobody knows that anyone else is Exalted or anybody else's motivations at the start of game-the ST has lied to each of them and told them they are the secret exception being allowed in a "we all start as mortals" game. Shenanigans ensue.

                                                So I'm making God-Kicking Boot, an Exalted webcomic, now. Updates on Sundays. Full-color, mediocre but slowly improving art. It's a thing.

                                                        and a third:
                                                        Young and hopeful DB arrives in the Imperial city, fresh from the House of the Bells - first of her otherwise lowly family to ever exalt and make anything of herself. She gets a job with The Guardians of the Realm, but due to her extremely low-born background she's largely ignored and initially sent to police incorrectly parked carriages and mounts. Later she meets a street-wise hustler who infuriates her endlessly, but after getting herself attached to an impossible to solve case, with her job on the line, she manages to force the hustler to help her solve the case. What ensues is a deep dive down a rabbit-hole of conspiracy, demonic poison that maddens those afflicted, and corruption at the highest tier of the imperial city's government. Will the young guardian be able to keep the peace and solve the mystery, or will she accidentally finger the wrong magistrate and plunge the city into chaos?
