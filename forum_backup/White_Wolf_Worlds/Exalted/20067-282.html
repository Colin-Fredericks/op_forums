                    Further the 4th range band is a significant increase in distance as the 4th is extreme range right? You start with close because you can't jump to close range band you are already at close so 1st) Short, 2nd) Medium, 3rd) Long, and 4th) Extreme vs short, medium, and long, right? Extreme range is described as barely able to see targets, so you can leap a several miles at a time possibly. On earth our visual plane ends at roughly 3 miles or 5 km due to curvature. So you can leap about half a mile per jump. You can keep jumping until your done jumping as per the charms description. I am not so sure about the fatigue of jumping like this repeatedly but, if a jump lands within the realm of the round in which it was initiated and the duration of the standard combat round 3 seconds (most games go with 3 seconds but later I will amend that time because whoa) I've seen in many other games. So you can cross about 10 miles a minute or 600 miles an hour. That's stupid fast.

                    MATH?!?!?

                    Round = 3 Seconds
                    1 jump per round = .5 Mile
                    1 minute = 20 rounds
                    Speed per minute calculates out to 10 miles a minute over the course on an hour thats...
                    .5*20*60 = 600 mph so yeah its fast as hell.

                    As an ST I would error on the side of "WTF is this bullshit" and say a round is 9 seconds as that is a more cinematic (the flavor of exalted combat) time frame and limit the jumps to 200 mph and roughly 3.3 miles a minute because that's bananas even for an exalted.

                    Revised Math!

                    Round = 9 seconds
                    1 jump per round = .5 mile
                    1 minute = 6.6 rounds
                    Speed per minute calculates out to 3.3ish miles per minute and you multiply that by 60 minutes and you are traveling at 200 miles per hour. Looking at this map even a skinny mountain range on this map would take you a couple hours to pass working with these rules.

                    As a side note I would describe long range as about 250 meters (750 ft) if we are working off archery ranges here. You could run at about 60 miles and hour with Racing Hare Method.

                    This all still feels too much.

                    If we are talking about Earth sized mountain ranges sure. Exalted/creations mountain ranges are preposterously huge.
