    Man, I love Dragon-Blooded

    09-29-2015, 06:43 PM
    Posters may remember that I love Solars. I love them as unironic heroes there to bring goodness and light to a world filled with myriad shades of darkness. They’re the mighty saviors of the world; the Supermen, Thors, and Wonder Women whose very passing causes the world to shake.

    I also love (Imperial) Dragon-Blooded, and I love them because they’re the antithesis of the Solars. I see them as far more of a foil/counterpart than the Abyssals or Infernals ever were.

    What do I mean? (This is going to be obvious to many of you.)

    The Solars are the new-kid on the block. The Dragon-Blooded and the Realm are the established power.

    While a Solar is often a wanderer with no built in organization, Dragon-Blooded are born and raised in Creation’s premiere powerblock. At the same time, Solars are likely to create their own organizations – becoming warlords, merchant princes, or the like – while Dragon-Blooded are typically constrained within the institutions that birthed them.

    Solars are defined as the strongest while the Dragon-Blooded are the weakest. Yes, they’re doing away with the tiers, but Solars are always going to stand at the pinnacle of Creation. Heck, they *define* what that pinnacle is. Dragon-Blooded are near the bottom in Creation, and are the weakest of the PC splats.

    And this creates an interesting paradox of power. The DB are the weakest, but they overthrew the strongest. The DB can simultaneously be the hegemonic authority and the scrappy underdog. The existence of the Realm means Solars can both be the God-Kings of Creation and the rebels fighting against the overwhelming might of the Empire.

    The Solars are the shining heroes while the Dragon-Blooded are always shades-of-grey. Even the most noble and good-hearted Dragon-Blooded are supporting a social system of exploitation, slavery, ruthless conquest, corrupt bureaucracy, and a false belief. Even the most decadent and cruel Dragon-Blooded is supporting the greatest civilization in the Second Age. one that’s been instrumental in safe-guarding all of Creation for a couple thousand years.

    Also, I find Solars and Dragon-Blooded to be the most human of the splats, which I like, but that doesn’t support my ‘Dragon-Blooded are the antithesis of Solars,’ so ignore that.

    Footnote 1: Please don’t turn this into a thread about how my conception of Solars is bad and wrong.
    Footnote 2: I am specifically talking about Dragon-Blooded of the Blessed Realm who are part of a House. Outcastes are okay, but not half as interesting.

    Come and rock me Amadeus.

                              Responding to Hippokrene's original post.

                              Terrestrials are my favorite splat. I love all Exalts, but something about them strikes me as awesome. It's partly the whole Romance of the Three Kingdoms vibe they have, its also partly that they absolutely require the infrastructure they have built up. It makes them feel more attached to the setting and I love that.

                              I do love outcastes as well but thats more cause winning the genetic lottery suddenly opening the entire world up for you is always a fun thing. (I play outcastes in that regard like a Solar, but without the fear of being an Anathema, and have managed to make a low bred outcaste the most useful member of an all celestials (1 solar, 2 abyssals and 1 lunar) party.

                              I tend to make my outcastes young kids who get thrust into a story Link style. Growing up, suddenly no longer a kid, but expected to be a hero because you glow now. Always kinda fun.

                              I don't like the idea "they are weakest so they are useless" mentality. I had a player in the exact game i mention who played a solar actively tell me OOC "hey, yeah your DB is dragging us down and we need you to learn CMA so you can stay relevant..."

                              I love the whole political intrigue, the whole no matter how much you may be a big shot, there are a bunch of people pulling strings behind you. It feels good.

                              So ,Yay to this thread. (And while Solars are my least favorite, I've had a lot of fun with them as well)
