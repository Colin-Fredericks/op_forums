    Expanding Elemental Summoning (Homebrew - 3 spells, 1 charm)

    05-22-2018, 12:54 PM
    This is just some stuff I wanted to get written down, but again I'm always happy to get feedback.

    Summon Greater Elemental
    Celestial Circle Spell
    Ritual, 2WP

    Weaving together the essence of her surroundings, the sorcerer conjures an Elemental of tremendous power. This spell works as Demon of the Second Circle, except that it summons an Elemental with an essence rating of 4-6. This spell cannot summon a Lesser (or Greater) Elemental Dragon. As with Summon Elemental, this spell creates a new Elemental rather than summoning an existing one. However, unlike their lesser peers, these Elementals never dissolve away once their bindings end, free to leave and perhaps to repay their former master’s kindness or cruelty.

    The ritual to cast the spell lasts six hours and must be completed at midday on the 14th (ie. middle) day of any month. The sorcerer may only summon elementals whose aspect matches the current season, unless she casts it within the boundaries of a Demesne aspected to her preferred element. The sorcerer need not own the demesne, but must be able to complete the ritual uninterrupted.

    - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
    Summon Elemental Dragon
    Solar Circle Spell
    Ritual, 3WP

    The sorcerer makes the Sign of Gaia and channels the primordial power of the elemental poles to her through Creation’s invisible web of dragonlines, forging it into an avatar of raw elemental might. This spell Demon of the Third Circle, except that it summons a Lesser Elemental Dragon with an Essence Rating of 6-9. As with Greater Elementals, such Dragons always remain once their binding ends. In addition, they are immensely cunning and blessed with self-interest; they will obey their summoner but also plan for the bigger picture of their existence.

    The ritual takes 12 hours and must be cast within the boundaries of a Demesne during Calibration, and then only once during the five days. The less rigid nature the essence of Creation takes on during this period allows the Dragonlines to handle such monumental power, but without a season to draw on, the demesne used determines the Dragon’s elemental aspect.

    A closely guarded secret, even in the high first age, is that the spell CAN function outside of Calibration. But doing so extracts a terrible price for the sorcerer’s ambition. Completing the ritual at midday on the 14th day of a resplendent month, within a Greater (4 dot) demesne of a matching element, the sorcerer can invoke the Sign of Gaia and summon a Lesser Elemental Dragon. However, channelling the undiluted energy of the poles during the year proper causes unimaginable devastation. The Demesne is annihilated, permanently lessening Creation’s geomantic composition, with any connected Manse or Hearthstone also ceasing to function. But more than that for (Essence)x50 miles in every direction the regional Dragonlines warp and shatter. The essence of the former Demesne’s element overwhelms the existing balance; temperate locations become severe and already strongly-aligned climes become exceptionally wild and natural disasters abound for months if not years afterwards. Spirit courts and mortal populations alike are thrown into chaos and the forces of Heaven and the Directional censor will surely come seeking answers.

    - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
    In the ancient scriptures Gaia represented Creation. She was a sister to the fallen Yozis, but when the Incarnae rose up against their enemies and blessed mortals with the exigence and their own divinity, Gaia stood with them. Such was her primordial might however that no human could take on her blessing and survive. Instead her gift was filtered through the first Elemental Dragons, and each mortal would instead take on only one aspect of her true power. Thus, the Dragonblooded were the least of the Exalted host, but working in unison they can transcend the limits of even the returning Solars.
    Invocation of Elements and Anima
    Terrestrial Circle Spell
    Ritual, 2WP

    In a 6-hour ritual culminating at midday on the 14th day of the resplendent month of the season which matches her anima aspect, a Dragonblood sorcerer may call forth a matching Greater Elemental of essence 4-6. This spell cannot summon a Lesser (or Greater) Elemental Dragon.

    The Elemental must be bound with the same rolls as the spell Summon Elemental. However, if the sorcerer is successful she immediately reduces her Essence score by an amount equal to the Elemental’s essence for the purposes of effect calculations. This does not actually lower her Permanent Essence, so she does not lose access to charms or alter her mote pools, but the effects of charms, spells, evocations, anima powers and merits which are based on her permanent essence are diminished. This can turn bonuses into penalties where applicable. This penalty reduces by one at the end of each story, or can be removed completely if the Elemental allows the Sorcerer to achieve a goal or protect a defining intimacy which she could not have done so otherwise.

    A sorcerer in a sworn kinship however may share this heavy burden with her bonded family. The reduction of essence for relevant effects may be distributed amongst herself and her willing sworn kin however they choose; ie after summoning a Garda Bird, the summoner might accept her essence effects being reduced by 2, with her two sworn siblings each accepting a reduction of 1. Further, the sorcerer may summon elementals of aspects and in seasons that match one of her sworn kin’s anima rather than her own, at the cost of an additional 1WP to cast the spell. The kinship decide in what order the penalty is reduced over time.

    Hidden within the Imperial Manse there is a cache of sorcerous research personally penned by The Scarlet Empress. In it she theorises that with her anima and transcendent sorcery she could forge a superior version of this spell, which would allow her to bind Elemental Dragons as the anathema once did. Though the Bronze Faction have no evidence that she ever attempted such a feat, the regents who sit on the Scarlet Throne sometimes secretly feel that the five jade dragons upon it are more than mere sculptures…...

    - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
    Mandate-Invoking Call
    Cost:- (1 lhl)
    Mins: Occult 4, Performance 4, Essence 3
    Type: Permanent
    Keywords: -
    Duration: Permanent
    Pre-Reqs:Celestial Circle Sorcery, Respect-Commanding Attitude (other Exalts use a similar charm as a pre-requisite)

    The surrender oaths sworn by the God’s enemies require that every Demon must answer when a sorcerer calls them into Creation. Elementals have no such obligation, yet the mandate of rulership handed down to the Exalted after the war is no mere formality.

    When a sorcerer with this charm casts Summon Elemental or Summon Greater Elemental she may speak aloud the name of a particular Elemental and cut their hand, inflicting one level of lethal damage. If it is within Creation then the named Elemental smells the scent of the sorcerer’s blood, hears her voice and is pulled immediately into her summoning circle, ready to fight the battle of wills. Even should the Elemental win and remain unbound, it must make the journey home itself, the magic of the charm having passed.

    Elementals whose permanent essence matches or exceeds the sorcerer’s may pay 2WP to resist and cause the spell to fail, the sorcerer does not recover her spent Willpower. Elementals who exceed her essence may instead send a spirit servant as a proxy in their place.
