              In no particular order...
              Ox-Body Technique is generally good to have at least one or two purchases of by default.

              Wind-Carried-Words Technique, aka Glorious Terrestrial Cellphone, is one of my favorite powers in the entire Exalted canon. It's not huge, it's just a really nice utility Charm that you can use in fun ways.

              Elemental Concentration Trance is the gate to Elemental Bolt, but even before that it's a rather fun Charm if you like introducing facts. It lets you use various Abilities instead of Lore, which is nice if you want to introduce War or Bureaucracy or Survival facts.

              Elemental Bolt Attack is a cool Charm, especially if you want to hit those good good Bending notes.

              Oath of the Ten Thousand Dragons is an evocative, cool Integrity Charm that lets you stay true to your pals real good, and is the gate to some stuff that builds on that.

              Honestly, the whole Earth Awareness line is a big draw for me, because that Toph-esque tremorsense is just really cool.

              Dragon-Mariner Attitude is a neat Sail charm that lets you wear various social "modes", sort of like Seasoned Criminal Method for Solars but more nautical. It's also the gate to Deck-Striding Prana, which lets you use Sail to boost your combat mobility. Likewise, Performance has Petal-Strewn Pavane, which uses Performance to boost combat mobility.

              I don't know about must-have, but cool-to-have, definitely, depending on what you want your DB to do. One fun thing about DBs is that compared to Solars, there's a bit more in terms of Abilities reaching over to other Abilities, Charm-wise (such as the Sail and Performance mobility Charms I mentioned, a Bureaucracy Charm that enhances a specific kind of Larceny action, and others).

                          Originally posted by Tytalus View Post

                          Whichever. I'm curious which charms have big bang for their buck and wide utility, with low requirements.
                          A bit hard to say generally, partly because a lot of charms are elementally themed so that they don't always mix well with other powerful charms. Notably a lot of the Essence 3 Signature charms are, as you might expect, standout awesomeness, like the Water Larceny sig that turns you bodily into water, letting her automatically escape a non-magical grapple, giving bonuses to Hardness, Evasion and Stealth rolls, which become non-charm if under water, and, of course, the ability to simply move through any obstruction that's not water tight.

                          For stuff you can get right off the hop Virtuous Negation Defense in dodge is pretty cool, it lets you reflexively Defend Other using Evasion instead of a parry to push your ally out of the way of an attack, even out to Short range.

                          There's Invisible Street Performer Technique in Performance which lets somebody hide in plain sight by playing music gently in the background, never to be noticed. Oh also they can play that music by picking up a tree branch and forming it into a sanxian, or causing a flower blossom to grow and stretch its petals over like a drum. They can then use that sanxian as a weapon by plucking the strings and hurling bolts of force or phantasmal beasts at her opponents.

                              Wound-Denying Dragon Faith is a standout for me. 3 charms deep, can be taken at chargen and it essentially makes you ignore wound penalties for a scene so long as you have a fellow DB nearby, which should be always, for a low cost and, most importantly, element-agnostic.                                   Of the three DB PCs in my game (Earth combat monster, Wood archer/socialite, & Air socialite/sorcerer), I'm pretty sure the only Charm we all have is Ox-body Technique (and even the primary Socialite has 2 OBTs now).

                                  My personal stand-outs are:

                                  Become the Hammer (Brawl/MA Excellency, Uniform reroll 6s on damage)
                                  Force of the Mountain (Earth Dragon Style, massive bonus damage -- I use it on every attack)
                                  Earth Dragon Form (Infinite (Martial Arts) Mastery)
                                  Granite Curtain of Serenity (Integrity Excellency)
                                  Wind-Carried Words Technique (Linguistics, when regular play starts again I think we all might buy this as it's soooo good for letting the party communicate)
                                  Wound Closing Touch (Medicine, by the Dragons this has far and away been the most welcome Charm at the table -- YMMV)
                                  Terrestrial Circle Sorcery
                                  Harmonious Life-Affirming Song (Performance, who doesn't want to heal seven times faster? Some wonky book-keeping but this is an excellent combo with Wound Closing Touch. Healing Lethal from your second -2 takes 11 days* of bedrest. Or eleven hours with both these Charms. I don't mind spending a day in bed after a hard fight, but I'd rather not be down for 11 days. Plus you could go full Witcher and flurry Life-Affirming Song as you go about your daily business as normal, carrying your wounds with you and still heal over the course of a single day.)
                                  Loquacious Courtier Technique (Socialize Excellency, Mute)

                                  None of us have Dragon Mariner Attitude but I think it looks really cool. Likewise Wound-Denying Dragon Faith is high on my list to pick up.

                                  [Edit]

                                  Agree with Croakamancer below that Auspicious First-Meeting Attitude is really cool.

                                  *Mortals take 24 days (basically a month) to heal, reduced down to 2 days. If you have folks without Exalted healing, you REALLY want these Charms.

                                              Wind-Carried Words is honestly overrated - its effect is trivially replicated at basically no cost (two merit points and 1 committed mote) by a Clockword Bird. However, it is a prerequisite for Speech Without Words, an awesome utility power that allows you to insult people to their faces at social functions with no-one being the wiser and to communicate with your entire Hearth silently. The ability to say "I don't trust this guy, for these specific reasons..." while said guy is standing right next to you is invaluable.

                                              Friend-To-All-Nations is another gem, allowing you to operate at maximum dice cap anywhere, without having to blow xp on specialties. It's also a prerequisite for Seizing-the-Tongue Technique, a Charm that allows you to reroll dice on social rolls after you've seen the result - combined with the exploding 10s of the Presence excellency, this can give you considerable reach.

                                              I agree with the consensus that Wound-Denying Dragon Faith is great (if you're a team player and can reliably proc it off the back of its prerequisite, buuuuuuuut if you're not that keen on loading up with positive Intimacies towards the bunch of unstable murderhobos you're travelling with, it might easily end up as a dead Charm) and Elemental Concentration Trance is a must have (not only because there's basically no introduce fact roll you couldn't justify with it, but also because it's a prerequisite to Elemental Empowerment Meditation, the single best way you have of alleviating your teensy-tiny mote pools).

                                              Finally, while excellencies are almost always the best use of your Charm slots pre-E3, Hidden Secrets Whisper stands out even among them. It's a dice adder, so you can wring maximum value out of it even with an odd score, it gives you rerolls for free, and best of all, you can apply it to so many situations. Outside the wheelhouse of Occult, you can apply it to tests of speed and combat movement with a travel spell of your choice, you can use it on withering attacks with the Burning Name merit, and best of all, it gives you the cheapest and most reliable decisives of the entire Dragon-Blooded combat suite. (Admittedly, with Heirs out, Melee can finally bring its offense to par... if the attacker doesn't mind losing Fire Aura, has aimed on the turn before, and she's able to afford to blow 6m on top of the cost of the excellency.)
