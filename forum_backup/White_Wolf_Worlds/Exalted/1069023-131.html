                        Will Lunar Charms have a stronger bias towards pursuit than retreat, as the Solar Exalted do?

                        Solars have all sorts of Charms that help them to cover greater distances in order to attack (Thunderclap Rush Attack, Ascendant Battle Visage, Thunderbolt Attack Prana, Leaping Tiger Technique, Eagle Wing Style, Godspeed Steps, Invincible Fury of the Dawn) because of the aggressive nature of the Brawl, Melee, and Athletics Abilities. (Athletics being aggressive by dint of its responsibility for Rushing and lack of involvement with Disengaging.)

                        But the Attributes don't have quite the same aggressive focus. Strength-based jumps could lead away from a fight as easily as towards one, and Dexterity trees could have an equal focus on Disengaging, Withdrawing, and defending as it does on Rushing and attacking.

                        I know you said that the Lunar Exalted have strong themes of hunting and predation, but would there also be an undercurrent of "hit-and-run", where the Lunar retreats from an unfavorable battle in order to fight another day, at a time of her choosing?
