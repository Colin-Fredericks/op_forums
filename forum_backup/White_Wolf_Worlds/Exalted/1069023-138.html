                Originally posted by Robert Vance View Post
                In terms of mechanics, I've got one specific idea up my sleeve, but I'd want to actually get Lunar Charms written before I start discussing it.

                Oh, and, like Solars and Dragon-Blooded, each Lunar caste will have three anima powers apiece.
                What are your thoughts on the old Changing Moon illusion anima power?

                I'm hoping it won't return. Not just because illusions apparently are expressly not a Lunar thing anymore, but because it was kind of shitty to deny that power to the other Shapeshifting Skinwalkers instead of making a Charm.

                I wrote at least two threads to cover different ways in which that anima effect was a bad idea, and to explore alternatives.
                http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/m...e-anima-powers
                http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/m...-shapeshifting

                Hatewheel/Morke also seemed to think such a power was rife with problems, though I thought it could be mitigated:
                Hatewheel has a Lunar question! - Onyx Path Forums
                http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/main-category/exalted/413424-hatewheel-has-a-lunar-question
                Link to RPG.net post: http://forum.rpg.net/showthread.php?749241-Lunar-Exalted-What-Are-They-to-You&amp;p=18759676#post18759676




                On a related note, I had this idea that each of the three Lunar Castes might have one of the three Dawn Caste powers.

                * No Moons get the higher base Initiative after Decisive attacks, because they favor Wits (Join Battle).

                * Full Moons get the once-per-day combat/movement reset, because they favor Strength and Dexterity for mobility, and their physical Attributes are the most combat-focused of the nine

                * Changing Moons get the intimidation effect, because all of the social Attributes can be applied to intimidation, and soft power over hard power is their entire theme


                Full Moons might get their old Feats of Strength booster (because Strength) and either the Night Caste stealth-penalty reducer (because Dexterity) or the Twilight soak effect (Stamina). In fact, if they just had those three, they don't need the Dawn power.

                Changing Moons might also get the familiar-catcha of the Twilight's, and either the identity-masking of the Night Caste (assuming that also shouldn't be a Lunar Charm) or the diplomatic immunity of the Eclipse Caste (because Changing Moons are the social Caste)

                No Moons could have the Twilight teleport and the Night Caste anima-deccelerator.

                formerly Tornado Wolf, formerly Inugami
