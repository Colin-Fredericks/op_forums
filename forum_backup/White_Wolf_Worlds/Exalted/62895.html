    Abbandon all hope all ye who enter here- I'm doing an Abyssal game!

    02-01-2014, 10:22 AM
    So, I'm getting tired of waiting for 3e and knowing that even when it comes I'm gonna need to wait probably a year more untill Abyssal expansion comes out. And since I'm filled with ideas and anxiety which will go unrealised for another couple of years if I don't act, I decided to do this now.

    Some basic information: I am on a short break from school due to exams (I know, sounds weird) so I have a lot of free time on my hands right now. what this means, is that practically any hour and any days of the weak fit my schedule. I live in GMT +1 timezone though, so I understand that it will probably mean playing during nights for me. 6 hour bridge does that. The game can take place on Skype or any other similar program I might not be aware of (courtesy of Totalbuiscuit, I was made aware of roll20, which I am eager to try out). It can hopefully be realised as video confference game, but I will not push for it (as I would have to buy a mic myself, but at least I'd have an incentive to do so).

    As for the game itself: we'll be playing 2.5 with a few minor changes, such as widely accepted rule about Excellencies imported from 3e, and under the rules of appropriate splat expansions. Any additional material suggested by players will be reviewed on case-by-case basis. I want the majority of the party to be Abyssal Exalted, but I'll accept Necromancy-wielding Dragonblooded, Lunars and Sidereals of Endings also on case-by-case basis.

    Where the events of our story take place is largely a matter for an open discussion. It largely depends on your preferences- if you guys want to play a game in the West, Pirates of the Caribbean style, I'm game. If you want to take part in the events surrounding the conquest of Thorns in the East, I'm game as well. I'd like to hear you out on where do you want to play and what specific stories are you interested in telling.

    So write here if you want to participate and ask away about anything that interests you!

    (and then I make two mistakes in the title of the thread... That's a great start, bravo, I aplaud myself that's how fucking good that was...)
