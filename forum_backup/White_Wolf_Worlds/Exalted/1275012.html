                                          Originally posted by Ekorren View Post
                                          Has anyone gotten started with any DB games yet since the Kickstarter?
                                          I've been playing a Water Aspect in a game with two Solars (well, one's a homebrewed Abyssal, but similar power level). I feel like the power of the Dragon-blooded is very specific, but when you find that niche they can really shine. They can get really impressive rolls using auto-success, reroll, and exploding dice Excellencies, but only in two or three applications of an Ability, depending on what elemental aspect the Charms are. For instance, my Water Aspect has been scarier on Intimidate rolls than the Dawn thanks to Burning Dragon Mien, Dragon Mariner Attitude, and the Presence Excellency. However, the Dawn is making broader uses of her Presence, rather than just big Intimidate actions. I've had a lot of fun finding the ways to make my Charms work for the situation that we are in, rather than just having a huge power answer to most things like a Solar (which is fun in its own right, don't get me wrong). Also, the universal DB anima power of manipulating your element for a mote is incredible stunt fodder. Need higher Defense? Sure is hard for these attacking ocean critters to swarm me when I swirl the water into a whirlpool around myself!
