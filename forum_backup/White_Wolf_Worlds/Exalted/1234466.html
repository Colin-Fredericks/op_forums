    Don't Understand the Calculation for the Inheritance Background (2E)

    07-12-2018, 09:00 PM
    OK,
    So first, pardon me: I only have access to the Background as it's explained in MoEP: Infernals, which only gives the Background a brief treatment. As near as I understand it (and searching on here keeps getting me the Permanent Essence Scores of both parents added together and divided by two), I must be missing something.

    Otherwise, every Half-Caste whose Exalted parent has an Essence 5, who has a divine or Demon parent with an Essence of 5 would be Inheritance 5...and it doesn't seem to make sense if nearly every Half-Caste God/Demon-Blooded is Inheritance 4-5. Have I missed a step in the equation which determines their Inheritance rating?

    Apologies if this is an archaic question/issue, but I mainly approach Exalted from a fan-fiction writing standpoint, but I try to maintain at least moderate mechanical fidelity. Moving over to 3E isn't something I can afford as yet, so here I am.

      The key is that Essence 5 is not particularly common. Mortals have an Essence of 1, a starting 2e Exalt usually has an Essence of 2, most 2e First Circle Demons/Terrestrial Gods/Elementals have an Essence of 2 or 3. So most God-Blooded are Inheritance 1 (Essence 1 mortal with Essence 2 demon/god/elemental, (1+2)/2 = 1.5, round down to 1).

      Cases of powerful Exalts mating with Second-or-Third Circle Demons/Celestial Gods/Elemental Dragons are rare; there are going to be only a handful of God-Blooded of such powerful lineage, and thus Inheritance 4 or 5.

              The thing to remember about Inheritance is that its generational.
              Its pretty easy for an Exalt with similar means to a PC to have kids with Inheritance 4-5, but the grandchildren will probably have a lower rating unless they find partners of a similar supernatural lineage or start in-breeding.

                  In our game, the Solar and Lunar had an Inheritance 3 baby; that's the child of two Exalts.
                  The "average" demon-blood is likely Inheritance 1 or 2; even the half-demon child of a second-circle demon probably has a mortal parent, so even if their mum is Essence 6, but their dad is Essence 1, that's Inheritance 3.

                  Of course, you could have the child of an Exalt and a second-circle demon or elemental dragon, but how many Lunar elders, Sidereal elders or Dragon-blood elders have kids with second-circle demons, high-ranked gods or elemental dragons?

                  I'm sure there's a handful of Inheritance 4-5 half-divine star-children, and a few half-Infernal demon-blood babies, but it's certainly not average.

                                  Thanks all,
                                  I *thought* that the logistics of dual-supernatural-lineage was what I was missing, but I wanted to be sure. No, I didn't believe two Essence 5 beings having kids was common, but the Inheritance rating is the same whatever the parents' Essence scores are if they both match. So, an Essence 3 Exalt and an Essence 3 God, leads to Inheritance 3 offspring. That seemed like it might be more powerful than intended, but again a) I realize from reading here prior to getting registered that the general consensus is the Inheritance mechanism isn't particularly well-loved, and b) My own personal experience with it comes from the truncated explanation it gets in MoEP: Infernals.
