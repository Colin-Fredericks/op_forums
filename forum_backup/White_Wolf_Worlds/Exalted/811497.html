    Lunar 3e conversion: Anima banners

    01-18-2016, 11:11 PM
    So I've been working, on and off, on a Lunar conversion (as between 2 games our group has 4 Lunars). It's about half-finished, and I posted up some what I'd done before, but I think people were still absorbing the backer pdf. Also, it was very much tl;dr.

    So, I thought I'd try posting in short chunks to get more feedback.

    So first, as it's short and easy to argue about, here's what I have for the anima banners. Tell me what you think!

    A couple of notes: First, there's the always contraversial Changing Moon anima. On the one hand, it's really good and makes people think "why am I not a Changing Moon?" On the other hand, it's iconic enough that I don't really want to abandon it. So essentially I just weakened it and made their other powers weaker than the ones the other castes get.
    Second, there's the No Moons. I wanted to make playing a non-sorcerer No Moon just as good as a sorcerer No Moon. So took away their sorcery effect, and gave them a bonus to dealing with spirits instad, to fulfill their role as Shamans. But giving the mental Lunars a bonus on social rolls feels weird. Any suggestions are welcome.

    (Oh, also, for character gen, the idea is that Lunars choose 2 caste abilities from the list, and then 2 favoured abilities. I considered having them choose 3 of 4 caste attributes, like Solars choose 5 out of 8, but it was difficult to decide what they should have.)

    Also... do bonuses from anima banners count as bonuses from charms? I'd assume they don't, but someone on these forums said they do. That'd affect the balance.
    Full Moons:
    Caste Attributes: Strength, Dexterity, Stamina.
    Anima Banner:
    For 5 motes, the Lunar may add half her essence (rounded up) in dice to all movement pools and feats of strength (both the dice pool and the minimum strength for particular feats) for one scene. At bonfire/iconic level, this ability is activated automatically at no cost.
    Once per scene, when the Full Moon is at bonfire or iconic anima display, she may add half her Essence score (rounded up) to her base Initiative upon resetting to base after a successful decisive attack.
    For 5 motes, the Full Moon may reset one Strength, Dexterity or Stamina charm with an outstanding reset condition. This ability can only be used once per day, and is reset when the moon rises in the evening. If this ability is used on a night of the Full Moon, all Strength, Dexterity and Stamina charms with outstanding reset conditions are reset.

    Changing Moon:
    Caste Attributes: Charisma, Manipulation, Appearance
    Anima Banner:
    For 5 motes, the Changing Moon adds half her essence (rounded up) to her Guile and the difficulty to spot her tell for a scene. If her anima reaches the burning/iconic level, she gets this bonus automatically, as she is surrounded by an ever-shifting aura of light and shadows, concealing her identity. This also requires watchers her to make a Perception+Awareness roll, difficulty of her Essence, to identify her.
    As a simple action costing 5 motes, the Lunar can change the physical appearance of her true human form. She can change her height and weight by up to 50%, half or double her apparent age, and also change her facial features, ethnicity, colouration, and other minor physical features. She can also change her voice. No roll is required to look different, but if she attempts to take a form specific enough to impersonate a particular person, she must make a disguise roll as normal, though ignoring all penalties for a different ethnicity and body shape, and with a dice bonus equal to her Essence or 3, whichever is higher. If she knows the charm Twin-Faced Hero she can ignore the penalty for gender, and if she has the charm Hybrid Body Rearrangement and an appropriate animal form (such as a lion for a lion-man, a wolf for a wolf-girl, etc), she can roll to disguise herself as a beastman with no penalty.
    For 5 motes, the Changing Moon may reset one Charisma, Manipulation or Appearance charm with an outstanding reset condition. This ability can only be used once per day, and is reset when the moon rises in the evening.

    No Moon:
    Caste Attributes: Perception, Intelligence, Wits
    Anima Banner:
    No Moons are master shamans, in tune with the worlds of spirit and nature. But more than that, they are steeped in forbidden knowledge and the dark secrets of the Witching Moon, making them adept at negotiating with spirits both holy and unholy. For 5 motes, for one scene all social influence attempts by the No Moon against gods, elementals, Fair Folk and demons gain (Essence or 5, whichever is lower) extra dice. In addition, the No Moon's Resolve is raised by (half her Essence rounded up, or 3, whichever is lower) against their social influence and charms. Note that this ability does not apply to ghosts; even Luna fears to learn the secrets of the dead Titans.
    As the Lunars who are aligned with the darkest of nights, No Moons can wrap themselves in shadow. For 3 motes, they can get (Essence or 3, whichever is higher) additional dice on a stealth attempt in shadow, darkness, or dim light.
    For 5 motes, the No Moon may reset one Perception, Intelligence or Wits charm with an outstanding reset condition. This ability can only be used once per day, and is reset when the moon rises in the evening. If this ability is used on a night of the New Moon, all Perception, Intelligence and Wits charms with outstanding reset conditions are reset.

    STing Bronze Age Exalted

          Thanks for your advice!

          Originally posted by Sunder the Gold View Post
          Making one Caste better at shapeshifting than the others in any way would be like making one Aspect of Dragon-Blooded better at elemental conjuration than the other Aspects.
          This is kind of an issue, yes. On the other hand, I don't want to say to two of my players "nope, your characters suddenly can't use one of their most important shapeshifting powers that you use all the time".
          I'm wondering about making it an Appearance charm so anyone can potentially take it (but Changing Moons are most likely to, as it's probably favoured). Probably with the charm for changing your hair and stuff as the prerequisite.

          If Full Moons get bonuses to physical tasks without shapeshifting, why not give Changing Moons bonuses to social tasks? You start with something good regarding Guile, why not continue in that vein with bonuses to Threaten and Inspire actions?
          Like Solars, the castes ought to have powers that are useful for them. But I'm a bit hesitant to just say "you get bonuses to the stats you use most" because that's what, well, better stats are for. Or excellencies.
          Like how Zeniths get 3 powers that are useful for them, but don't just get "moar dice". Actually, the only people who do get "moar dice" really are Dawns, and they don't get it to attack rolls.
          So, raising the difficulty of the Tell definitely works (not like you can raise it with excellencies), but I was a little hesitant with the Guile. Still, I decided in the end to go for it to big up their role as Tricksters. If anyone has an idea for another ability that'd be useful for tricksters that isn't just a bonus, I'd be interested in hearing it. (For the same reason, I'm unsure of the dice bonus that No Moons get on stealth, but I think that's marginal enough that it's fine).
