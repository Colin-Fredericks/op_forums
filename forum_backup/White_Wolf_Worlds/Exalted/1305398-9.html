                                                        I can’t speak for every dragon style but I mean, the answer (to the double question of ‘should non DBs take them’ and ‘are they as good as other styles’) is threefold.

                                                        A) The cap charms. These supplement the Form, and while they’re expensive, they absolutely make the forms superior to other MA forms. Earth Dragon is a flat +10 to nat soak and Hardness assuming you have 5 stamina. More if you boosted WP. These are lunar numbers and they last the scene.
                                                        Consuming Might of the Fire Dragon is +3 overwhelming. If you have an artifact weapon that adds up a lot, and if you don’t have one well congratulations because now you have a pseudo one. You’re also punishing the enemy for hitting you, regardless of how much Hardness they have. Again you don’t have to do Anything. Assuming you won JB you didnt even use up an action. Motes, yes. But your form is providing the effect of several excellent charms every turn for the rest of the combat.
                                                        These are significant effects that are matched by few MAs in terms of scene-long effects.

                                                        B) MAs aren’t in competition. If you took every MA and entered optimised splats into a swisse/elimination tournament, like I guess Shining Point would do well? White Reaper would seem kinda bad, actually, without multiple opponents.
                                                        Except Righteous Devil and Shining Point would be freaking D e m o l i s h e d by Black Claw. Specifically by table-turning reversal.
                                                        The other thing that would happen is you would stop because it take forever and actually not be very fun.
                                                        The Dragon styles aren’t great against other MAs because they’re not well suited to countering them. And that’s ok. No one is out here saying Golden Janissary is useless because Solars/DBs/Lunars aren’t Creatures of Darkness.

                                                        C) I mean, they’re really good if you want to fight Rogue Gods or Demons or Elementals? Like really good. Way better than pretty much any other MA. Maybe Golden Janissary against Demons and ghosts, but even then I’m not certain.


                                                        Lastly. The thread is pulling double duty between cross-comparing combat styles and writing fights that are of interest to us. Running and balancing a combat by yourself is time consuming and difficult, I wouldn’t think that having the fights be narratively compelling is too much to ask.

                                                        It’s not pulling a third duty of advocating for any splat or MAs superiority. Like we’re talking about what does and doesn’t work in practice... that’s not the same as arguing for or against builds.
