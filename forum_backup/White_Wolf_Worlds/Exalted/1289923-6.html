    Ok hear me out: Wasps.

    This requires essence 3 or essence 2 with Radiant Basilisc Jewel, because it needs the repurchase of Mamba-and-Cobra Mastery to overcome the wasps poisons low damage, but the wasp does have a big advantage in having a naturally long duration (6 turns) which becomes (9 turns + essence) with Adder Fang, Mamba-and-Cobra, and the Enhanced Toxin merit, and then Mamba-and-Cobra repurchase lets you get 1i+(10s on attack & damage roll) (max 5) aggravated damage on it.

    This is in many ways superior to The Dragon Dies Screaming (in comparison wasp lunar gets +4 duration, on average equal damage attacking with 10i, and lower penalty) from White Veil but instead of 6m 6i 1WP you just pay 3m 2i and you get to do it with Miniscule Size bonuses and flight. Pretty brutal for a lunar assassin.

    Edit: I remembered Vance has said they are going to explicitly allow enhanced toxin mutations to enhance creatures natural venoms so numbers went up.

    Edit 2: This works at E1 with octopi since they dont need the damage boost and only the duration from the first purchase of Mamba-and-Cobra, but if you want to do it on land you need to be some sort of chimeric flying octopus abomination. Being a wasp is just cleaner, though I wish we had like black-widow stats.
