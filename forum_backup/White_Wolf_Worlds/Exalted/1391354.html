                                  The best description and interpretation of Hell in Exalted is all from 1e, where it was weird an interesting and different, with psychedelic art depicting strange alien beings, and where the Yozis (weren't really called that back then) were more like places and natural hazards. Anyway! In that book Hell is pretty inimical to unprepared or unguided mortals on account of it 1) killing you within precisely seven days, and 2) lot of Blood Apes around etc. However. With proper preparation, one can survive the natural environment, and with a guide or patron or safe place to stay, one could easily spend extended periods of time there and do quite well. It's not like Neomah, for example, are naturally hostile or even unsympathetic to the plight of others.

                                  Even by accident, one could survive there. There's definitely scope for, as an example, a slave escaping into the desert, wandering into Malfeas accidentally, being found by an Agatae who develops a bizarre attachment to them (because they do that) and taking them to a convent of Neomah who then take them in, feed and clothe them as best they can, and take care of them. Perhaps they die after a week anyway, at which point the Neomah are confused and grieve, or perhaps they know of such things and bargain for an antidote to protect this mortal, who then lives with them. That is not particularly likely but it's entirely believable.

                                  As are many stories. Perhaps the slave encounters Ligier who has had a strange dream and wandered out to ponder it, encountered the slave, took a liking to them and blessed them with relief from the air of Malfeas, telling them that if they can survive by their wits and strength, the Demon City will bring them the freedom they never had.

                                  Like it's an alien world. It's often a very scary world. It's got some incredibly dangerous and hostile entities. But like, so is wandering the desert, lost and alone. So is getting shipwrecked. But it's survivable if you're lucky and come across the right locals/have a bit of will and grit to hang on. It's not Christian Hell. The things there aren't there just to torture you (although some will. You also might encounter an Agatae jealous of the colour of your eyes, which it then carefully gouges out, though it may then feel impossibly upset when you bleed out in front of it, and spend ages building a cairn above your body in memory) but rather they are... very alien.

                                  And if you are a Sorcerer who can find a way in, well, there's lots of possibility for power. It's not just a "you're going to get betrayed and screwed," Demons can be pretty reasonable!
