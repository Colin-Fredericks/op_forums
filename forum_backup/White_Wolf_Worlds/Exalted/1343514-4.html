        So Poland got invaded by Germany.

        So I had a thought. There was definitely Jewish resistance to the Nazis. And there was most definitely sympathy for the nazis. And there was the death camps. And nobody gave a shit.

        So here's the idea. Some time before the nazis got the americans involved, but some time after the death camps, a band of freedom fighters sabotaged a bunch of nazi soldiers that were hunting down fleeing jews/ undesirables. The people fled, and the local cell decided to serve as a distraction, maintaining that no one should be sent to the death camps if they could help it. They flee with enemy soldiers in hot pursuit. They try to flee... but with enemies so close, they'll just get caught. There's no way to break contact. Someone must keep them away.

        That someone, was the cell leader. In comes our protag. A jewish woman who went to university, she is well educated, well taught, and well trained, having gone hunting and trained extensively while in the resistance cell. An owner of a chain of businesses, her income was wrecked when people boycotted and torched her businesses, and her husband and two sons were killed in a riot. Furious and angry, she saw her neighbours disappear one by one, and decided to act.

        Fun fact of being a resistance fighter. Its shitty. You get no pay, you get no support, you get no supply line, and your enemies are trained, motivated, and have better weapons than you. The woman has a rusty machine gun, a few hundred bullets, and one pistol with a single bullet for herself.

        She runs out to the last one, and has pulled out a knife, when the Unconquered Sun, seeing this woman's courage and selflessness in the face of adversity, graced her with his power. Dawn Caste, War supernal.

        Suffice it to say those men are not going back to their families.

        The next few months are slow for the German government. And did I say slow? I mean very slow. Our lady here had favoured in war, lore, firearms, bureaucracy, occult, athletics, subterfuge, and various other things. Paperwork is constantly lost, and has to be replaced. The bureaucracy grinds itself into gridlock, red tape and bureaucratic finanglings causing their response to move at a snail's pace. And all the while, the rebels are fighting back. Entire offices of the Gestapo go dark. Entire fleets of tanks and planes go silent. A city burns, and the one gibbering survivors screams of how they came out of the sewers and that everyone was dying. Skilled men and women of the Third Reich disappear, or just get found in their beds with their throat slit. Fires, explosions, and broken machinery in the factories, mean that Germany's already unfortunate supply problems begin to get even worse.

        And then shit happens. All this while, our Dawn caste has been stalking the shadows, stabbing people to death and grabbing vital documentation. And now, things change. The rampage guts Germany's army, shattering their core. Sent reeling, they pull back. At the same time, numerous guerilla fighters burst from what can be described as nowhere, leaping out of a sudden. Previous stormtroopers and soldiers find themselves with their pants down against supremely trained tiger warriors that have been trained via custom war charms to fight on the level of first circle demons. They find themselves torn to pieces, demolished, every ambush predicted and ever desperate attempt to counterattack effortlessly parried, once proud formations decimated by rebels who should by all means be cut down like scythe before a wheat.

        Britain pushes back, and old armies who either fled or deserted or in hiding join up with the main front, appearing suddenly and marching onto Berlin. The armies of the Third Reich pull back, wishing to protect their capital and their Fuhrer, while supply lines are cut off and the German lines in Russia find themselves overrun.

        The germans fight like demons, but they are faced down by Tiger Warriors led by a War supernal, who has a hand-held mortar and Accuracy Without Distance combined with Arrow Storm technique. In the end, the capital is broken to pieces, its defenses torn apart.

        Then the dawn caste strides into the bunker, and beheads half the nazi elite. The other half, is taken prisoner. Anyone who resists, and anyone with a gun in their hands and not part of the liberation army? Shot.

        Here's where things get hairy. Remember all the propaganda, of all the suberversives were out to destroy nazi germany, and so they had to be put down? Remember how majority of people in germany agreed with nazism, knew of the holocaust, and held auctions for people who had been sent to the gas chamber? Remember how people were perfectly willing to look the other way and ignore the death camps, or even send them back to Europe even when it was a death sentence?

        Remember how our dear dawn caste is someone who mass trained infiltrators to destroy and sabotage factories and vital infrastructure? Remember how our dawn caste mass trained a guerilla army to wipe out the Nazi Core? Remember how our dear dawn caste crushed an entire city which was devoted to the nazi war machine, killing them to the last man, woman, and child? Remember how she managed to pull a Creed, and pull an entire army out of seemingly nowhere?

        Think of it from the perspective of the Nazis. And I don't just mean the jackbooted thugs. I mean the civilians.

        Our Dawn Caste has won the war, stopping the holocaust before it could truly begin, putting in tens of thousands of documents on the heinous shit the nazis have done. But the appearance of a dawn caste who has shattered the nazi third reich may change the way history look upon eugenics and nazism.
