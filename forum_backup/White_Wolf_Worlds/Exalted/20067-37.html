                            Originally posted by Crowley View Post
                            And in page 41 interbreeding is mention, im still curious about this and alchies (alchemycals)
                            To be clear, that passage wasn't about Alchemicals. It was about how breaking the Seal of Eight Divinities would introduce the Autochthonian population to Exaltation types they have never known. In the part about interbreeding, it was talking about mortals from Creation, where at least trace amounts of Dragonblooded ancestry somewhere back in the family tree is basically ubiquitous, would introduce that possibility to Autochthonian bloodlines by having children with Autochthonian mortals. Again, this has no impact of any kind on the Alchemical Exalted. An Alchemical can have been an Exalt in one of her past lives -- indeed, there's an NPC who was a Solar in a past life -- and may then have some past life memories of Exalted existence, but it doesn't make any difference beyond that.
