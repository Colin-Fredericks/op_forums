                                                    Originally posted by The Dude View Post
                                                    I didn't see anything about it in the book, so have they done away with the 5 Magical Material Bonuses and the penalty cost associated with attuning to the wrong Magical Material?
                                                    Magical material bonuses now take the form of evocations. The primary material now affects what magic the item gains. Jade gaining elemental powers, orichalcum gaining powers showing greatness and sun themed stuff, etc. Base weapon stats are unchanged between types, attunement costs for all artifacts materials remains the same. However, only solars can gain the evocations off of every artifact, and there's are different from the evocations a dragon blooded may gain from the same jade artifact. Though it's implied that it's possible for other exalted to get evocations off of orichalcum, it's also stated to be heavily diminished. We don't have set in stone evocation rules yet, beyond 'only 3+ dot artifacts get them', but we're given enough samples to get an idea for how to build them ourselves, at least for solars.
