            Back in First Edition, Lunars didn't just have Armor-Forming Technique and Claws of the Silver Moon, they also had things like Flying Squirrel Method for growing gliding skin flaps between your arms and legs, and Panther Stride Style, for making whatever form you were using slimmer and more aerodynamic for better running speed.

            But they also had Hide-Toughening Essence, and other glowy, non-physical, "purely magical" effects.

            During one out of my hundreds of attempts to fix the Lunar Charmset, I thought that it would be good to allow Lunars to buy two Charms for any effect that could be justified with physical shapeshifting; one for the physical transformation, and one for the "purely magical" effect, just like the pair of Armor-Forming Technique and Hide-Toughening Essence. The purely magical effect would be less obvious, but also less effective.

            But that was back in the days when animals weren't worth shit for turning into. You turned into a bird when you needed to fly or a fish to swim, but cheetahs and horses had poor traits for running, so why bother using them except for off-screen travel? If there was any on-screen action, you wanted Deadly Beastman Transformation.

            Well now animal shapeshifting is finally being made competitive with DBT by making animals awesome. Now Lunar players WILL want to turn into horses and cats for groundspeed.

            Which is why I hope that Panther Stride Stance and its kind will not return. Perhaps not even Armor-Forming Technique.

            If a Lunar wants living armor, should he be able to grow it on any form he pleases? Hybrid Body Rearrangement let you slap armor and wings and fire-breathing on anything you became, and it really stopped mattering what form you had.

            Hide-Toughening Essence, on the other hand, improves the soak that the Lunar already has without a physical transformation, and thus enhances rather than muddles any form the Lunar might wear. Yes, his octopus form becomes hardier, but it's still an octopus rather than an armor-plated cephalopod.

            Claws of the Silver Moon, as the last Edition, likewise merely improved the traits of the Lunar's existing natural weaponry rather than growing new bits. You don't grow horns or claws on the octopus, but its beak bites more sharply and its tentacles lash like razor whips. The form is very much still distinctly an octopus, but it is now an octopus of legend; a beast of mythical power.

            Let Heart's Blood shapeshifting handle Lunar shapeshifting. Let the legendary power come from the Charms. Say "No" to Hybrid Body Rearrangement, Armor-Forming Technique and Flying Squirrel Method.

            formerly Tornado Wolf, formerly Inugami
