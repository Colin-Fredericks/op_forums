    Lack of Swimming Charm

    11-19-2015, 10:24 AM
    So, this is something that has bothered me quite a bit. Especially since pretty much every GM I've ever had has been like "Yeah, I'd let your movement speed affecting charms help with swimming... if there were swimming charms." The lack seems strange since it's not like there's going to be any direction other than South where it'll be near useless. The West is going to favor it, but North and East are going to have a lot of use for it as well (note the huge rivers in the East). The Blessed Isle of course has similar use.

    So... other than the past editions of Exalted ignoring it, why don't we have any in the core? And that said, does anyone have any good ideas/charms to fill the gap?
