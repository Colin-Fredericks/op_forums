            A lot of fueling abilities off of initiative is very not good. Especially defensive ones. If they were mostly offensive, like “instead of dealing 5 damage with that 5i, create a 5L environmental hazard against all enemies in range.” That’s okay, but on the defence, especially the DECISIVE defence, it’s just something they’ll basically never use.
