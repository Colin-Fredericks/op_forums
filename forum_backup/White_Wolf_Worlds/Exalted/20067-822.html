                So, back in the 1e days, Castebook: Night had a trio of Lore Charms based around the creation and application of poison:
                - Brewing Venom Technique: spend half an hour boiling water and infusing Essence into it, turning it into poison (can be either deadly or narcotic, weapon-based or ingested at the Solar's option)
                - Envenomed Weapon Prana: Simple Charm, can envenom a weapon, plate of food, or beverage with a touch
                - Mastery-of-Poison Technique: Reflexive Charm, can envenom a weapon (or food or whatever) on the same turn it's being used, at a distance of (Essence) yards

                ...Kinda sounds more like Glorious Solar Wizardry and less like supernatural levels of skill, there. I figure that a Solar might be able to distill more potent poisons than a mortal could, and might even be able to whip up poisons from a series of unlikely ingredients that no mortal alchemist could have used properly, but just boiling water into poison with no ingredients to speak of sounds like sorcery (or thaumaturgy, really) to me.

                So here's my actual question! Which Ability suits Charms for brewing and distilling venom? I'm a little torn between Craft and Medicine here. (Survival might be important for gathering ingredients, but probably not for improving the result, I think.)

                I'm slightly leaning toward Medicine, because honestly, characters practicing medicine are probably brewing concoctions for patients sometimes and I wouldn't expect them to roll Craft for that either. And...I just noticed that DBs get Poisoner's Deft Hand under Medicine, which is another point in Medicine's favor, although that Charm is more "deliver the poison in an especially effective manner" rather than "create an especially effective poison."

                Although, on a slightly-related note, I mentioned Survival. Something that occurred to me a while back -- what happens in the following scenario of PCs working together on a problem?
                - The Solar circle (or other PC group) arrives in town to discover that a rare, terrible disease is spreading
                - The Twilight introduces a fact that certain rare herbs growing in the region were discovered to be effective on a very similar disease back during the Shogunate era
                - The Zenith woodsman trots off into the forest, rolls a bunch of 10s, and returns triumphantly with a basket of rare medical herbs
                - The Twilight doctor makes his Medicine roll with...a +1 for exceptional equipment? That seems to be all the rules actually have for this kind of scenario. There aren't any real guidelines for Glorious Solar Teamwork, which is a shame because I'dvery much like opportunities for PCs to work together on situations like this.

                I can see why the writers wouldn't want to make it easy for players to stack massive bonuses onto each other's rolls, but a +1 bonus seems kinda weaksauce for a potentially dramatic success by one's assistant. Has anyone come up with any general guidelines for this kind of situation? (I suppose one option would just be to say "Roll at difficulty 5, your net successes become bonus dice on your teammate's roll.")

                EDIT: I just noticed the DB Medicine/Survival Charm, Nature's Healing Bounty (WFHW p. 221) has one example of improving the exceptional equipment bonus, in pretty much exactly the kind of scenario I was just discussing. Something to consider.

                    Originally posted by Jefepato View Post
                    So here's my actual question! Which Ability suits Charms for brewing and distilling venom? I'm a little torn between Craft and Medicine here.
                    One thing to note is that Exalted isn't really exclusive for a lot of tasks - multiple Abilities can probably do a job (albeit sometimes at different difficulties). In this case, I'd say either Craft or Medicine could be used to brew up a poison. Craft will be a bit better if you're going for a bulk lot, enough poison to coat a hundred arrowheads or something like that, whereas Medicine would be better for cooking up a new poison, something no-one had ever seen before (though once that was done, it would go back to Craft to make it in big batches).

                    And just like basic Abilities, I don't think there's really any reason to absolutely prohibit charms that have overlapping effects, as long as the activity reasonably falls into all of them. The book doesn't generally do it, of course, but I think that's more a question of saving wordcount - it's generally more interesting to see ten ways to do different things, rather than five ways to do basically the same thing, and only five unique tasks. But if someone has an Ability that would logically involve being good at an activity, I'm fine with homebrewing up a charm to help them with that in the Ability they have, rather than saying "no, you must take this other ability you aren't interested in, because it's the one the corebook happened to stick charms for the thing you want to do in".

                    For poisoning, I think brewing batches of it is adequately covered by the existing Craft charms. Under Medicine, you'd need to come up with a new charm. I'd make something that can brew doses of either poison or curatives, using the principle that "the dosage makes the poison". And I'd put administering poison under both Medicine and Larceny, with the Medicine angle being about hiding poison by concealing symptoms, or mixing poisons that were precisely tailored to the victim, while the Larceny stuff would be about using sleight of hand to conceal poisoned vectors, possibly even doing the retcon thing of "you know the wine you've been drinking all night, from your private stash? Yeah, I poisoned the grapes that were used to press that, months ago."

                    Originally posted by Jefepato
                    Although, on a slightly-related note, I mentioned Survival. Something that occurred to me a while back -- what happens in the following scenario of PCs working together on a problem?
                    - The Solar circle (or other PC group) arrives in town to discover that a rare, terrible disease is spreading

                    - The Zenith woodsman trots off into the forest, rolls a bunch of 10s, and returns triumphantly with a basket of rare medical herbs
                    - The Twilight doctor makes his Medicine roll with...a +1 for exceptional equipment?
                    If someone's declaring a fact, I would be giving a considerably larger bonus than just +1 for exceptional equipment. Not dice bonuses, perhaps (Solars or any Exalt rarely need too many more dice than their charms already give them). But I'd be allowing people to make recovery rolls much faster, or even just automatically doing so, at least if there were large enough doses of the herb. And collecting a bunch of the cure for a disease means that the doctor can explain how to prepare it, and then a whole bunch of people can be treated at once, instead of the lone doctor getting run off their feet trying to treat everyone individually.

                            Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz View Post
                            I have found the ketogenic diet quite successful.

                            It also occurred to me that, if you had excellency-less DBs, you could balance it with Solars by reducing Solar's excellency caps to (Ability). That's just for balance though, it doesn't solve your issue for PCs, just saying if you play excellency-less DBs.
                            I've been toying with an idea for Excellencies.

                            First, dice-caps stay the same (Solars are Attribute+Ability, Lunars Attribute+Stunted Attribute, DBs Ability+Specialty).
                            Second, Lunars and DBs get their Excellency free when they buy one Charm in the Attribute/Ability in question (and Excellencies don't count for prereqs).

                            Now the meat. Excellencies have their own dice-caps.

                            DB Excellencies can be used once. You may buy one die (or success depending on the Excellency) or raise a static value by one.
                            Lunar Excellencies may also only be used once, unless you stunt a second Attribute then they can be used upto a dice-cap of that (Stunted Attribute) or (Stunted Attribute/2 rounded down) for static values (Soak capped by Stamina/2 as normal).
                            Solar Excellencies are capped by (Ability), but if they spend Willpower to add a success to the roll, this rises to the full (Attribute+Ability).

                            Charms which grant a "Full Excellency" still give the full, dice-cap bonus (so, say, Awakening Eye still gives Perception+Awareness, capped by Wits+Awareness as normal). Charms which require a full Excellency only require you to hit your Excellency cap (and are thus significantly cheaper). Charms which previously served as "like an Excellency but cheaper" now become the main way of hitting your dice-cap. But, of course, most folks don't hit their dice-cap.

                            I think this pretty effectively shuts down Solar "thousand success Craft rolls", and makes pseudo-Excellency opponents like Octavian pretty terrifying.

                            But I don't want to run a "low-power" Exalted, or present a straight nerf, so I'm still juggling it.

                                                    Sorry.

                                                    Excellencies have their own, lower, dice-cap than the splat dice-cap.

                                                    So a Solar with Manipulation 4 and Socialize 2 has Guile 3. They can add upto +3 Guile with Charms, then they hit their dice cap. BUT the Solar Excellent Socialize can only add +1 Guile: to hit the dice-cap you need to use other Charms such as Shadow Over Day.* A Solar with Manipulation 4 and Socialize 4 has Guile 4, and may add +4 with Charms, but only +2 with Excellent Socialize.

                                                    A DB with Awareness 4 may use Precious Observation Method (the Awareness Excellency) to spend 1m to add a single die to her roll, or 2m to add a single succcess. (Instead of being able to spend upto 4m for four dice or two successes under RAW.) However, the DBs dice-cap is still 4: even though she can only add a single die using the Excellency, if she has other Charms she can still add another 3 dice. For example, if she has Occult 3 and Secret Wind Revelation she could add one die from the Excellency and three further dice from Secret Wind Revelation.

                                                    A Lunar with Dexterity 3, Strength 5 and Melee 3, rolls 6 dice on her Decisive Melee attacks. So long as she has at least one Dexterity Charm, she may add 1 die for 1m to this roll using her Excellent Dexterity. If she stunts her attack as using her great Strength, then she may instead add upto 5 dice for 1m each. In the former example, she can add upto two further Charm dice to her attack (perhaps from using an animal form); in the latter example she may add upto three further Charm dice: but in neither case may she get these extra dice from Excellencies.

                                                    Sidereal Excellencies have a dice-cap of (Essence) or can raise static values by (Essence). Sids can lower target numbers as described on page 548, but this adds a 1 WP surcharge to the Excellency.

                                                    *If the Solar spends a point of Willpower to raise her Guild from 3 to 4, then they may buy all three additional points with the Excellency, no further Charms required. (Though, of course, if they do know Shadow Over Day, it'd be cheaper for the Solar to use that.)
