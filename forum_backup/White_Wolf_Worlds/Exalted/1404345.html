    Master's Stone

    07-23-2020, 11:10 PM
    Simple question... would allowing a Hearthstone to be used to allow a character to use the Mastery Keyword for Martial Arts (assuming that they were not an Exalt type that could normally use the Mastery Keyword) be over or under-powered? Would one consider it a Standard or Greater Hearthstone? What would it be Aspected towards? Would such Hearthstones be limited to a single style or could they allow Mastery of multiple styles?

    Honestly, I don't see it being potent enough to warrant being limited to Greater Hearthstones, unless of course one of such potency would also nullify being subject to the Terrestrial Keyword (though even here it is questionable). As for what such Stones would be aspected towards... I honestly think they could be aspected towards any element or celestial (though given that Solars and Sidereal can already aspect these charms normally, Solar and Sidereal aspected hearthstones of this nature are likely redundant). Not sure about the number of styles one could master with such a Hearthstone... perhaps the potency of the Hearthstone would be important in this aspect.

    Just curious if anyone thinks this idea has potential.

                      I agree, that what Sith_Happens suggests, requiring a Greater Hearthstone or a Dedicated Standard Hearthstone to use the Mastery Keyword of a single Charm, is way too weak of an effect for any Hearthstone, especially one of this nature. That would mean one would need multiple Greater Hearthstones (and even more Standard ones) to access the Mastery Keyword of a single style... which is kind of ridiculous given that most styles have a half-dozen or more charms with the Mastery Keyword. It is simply too weak of an effect for a hearthstone... and that is saying something given how weak some of the given effects of Standard hearthstones are.

                      I could see a Standard Hearthstone being only powerful enough for a single Martial Art style... while a Greater Hearthstone allowing for multiple styles, especially if they resonate with the style. This brings me to my next point... what Aspect would a Master's Stone have. The obvious choice would be a Solar or Sidereal aspect to them, as these Exalt types are able to access the Mastery Keyword naturally. The problem is that because Solars and Sidereal would not need a Hearthstone to access the Mastery Keyword of Martial Art charms, it is unlikely that they would build a manse that would focus essence into a Hearthstone to allow one to access the full potential of Martial Art charms. It would be like creating a Lunar Hearthstone to access Protean or Totemic Keywords in Lunar Charms... completely redundant. I could see a case that Solars could decide to make these hearthstones for their Lunar mates... and Sidereal could be persuaded to do something similar for Dragon-Blooded... but over all, it would likely be seen as frivolous and a waste of a good Manse.

                      The Exalts that would most need these hearthstones would be those who can not normally access the Mastery Keyword... such as Lunars, Dragon-Blooded, Liminals and some Exigents (assuming some are able to access Mastery effects). If we assume that Solar and Sidereal manses are unlikely to be used to create these hearthstones (since most if these would be controlled by Solars or Sidereal who have no need for them), the most likely aspect to produce them would be Water (due to its connection to Brawl/Martial Arts) and/or Lunar aspects. To be honest... given that many Martial Arts tend to have at least superficial connections to the elements and such (or are at least elementally neutral) I would argue that this kind of Hearthstone could be produced by almost any Manse. All the Manse would really need is to be a good place to focus and meditate on the Martial Arts overall... which most likely could do. In fact, I would dare say that Master Stones could be one of the easier and relatively common stones that could be produced... with only the relatively limited scope of the effect being the counter balance to doing so. In short, given the variety of Hearthstones that a Manse could generate, creating a Master's Stone seems fairly limited. For an organization like the Immaculate Order who likely have hundreds of Temple Manses throughout Creation, this might not be a bad idea as it keeps the Manses from becoming the target of greedy Dynasts who may want to claim a given site while still giving a Monk with a non-Dragon style a small boost of power. Greater Manses are the least likely to have their potential to be wasted like this... though such Manses could possibly produce multiple Standard hearthstones of this nature (again, it would be a matter of balancing the power one could gain with a different hearthstone vs. what one gains with Mastery).

                      As one has no doubt noticed, I am of the opinion that a Master's Stone would be one only produced in a Manse. Given that Martial Arts, regardless of whatever they are trying to emulate, are an artificial creation (something that has to be learned rather than being a natural extension of one's abilities), so to would a Hearthstone designed to enhance such fighting styles. Now, whether such a Hearthstone would be Linked (only remains powered so long as the Manse remains untouched) or Steady (will remain empowered even if the originating Manse is destroyed) is another question. I would determine this by the power of the originating Manse in most cases... with Standard Master's Stones being more likely to be Linked and Greater Master's Stones having a greater chance of being Steady due to the amount of Essence focused into the hearthstones. It is because of this, creating a hearthstone that can survive if its Manse is altered or destroyed, that might persuade some to use a Greater Manse for such a hearthstone with the intention of altering the Manse at a later date without loosing the hearthstone in the process... though the formation of Steady Hearthstones likely takes much longer than Linked ones (likely taking years, decades or even centuries to form as opposed to weeks, months or a year).

                      Finally, when it comes to the Terrestrial Keyword... I would agree that a Master's Stone is most likely to allow one that is subject to it to ignore the Keyword... especially if the Charm also has the Mastery Keyword as well.

                      Over all, I don't see a Master's Stone being an overly powerful stone just for allowing access to the Mastery Keyword of Martial Art charms as the increased effect is fairly limited and wholly dependent on knowing such charms to begin with. Given the hoops one needs to jump through to even learn a Martial Art (4 dot Merit, each Martial Art learned as an Independent Skill, etc.), putting increased restrictions on a Master's Stone could seem as being overly harsh, especially compared to everything else the bonus points/merit dots could have been spent on (assuming that one is getting this at Character Generation). I mean, we are talking about not giving too much power to characters that are by their very nature some of the most powerful beings in the setting. If one doesn't see the irony in that... then you really haven't been paying attention to the setting.

                      With all that said, I present:

                      THE MASTER'S STONE (Varies)
                      Keyword: Manse-born (varies)

                      Hearthstones of this type allow an Exalt to focus their understanding of Martial Arts to a fine point, allowing them to access the effects linked to the Mastery Keyword. Unlike other Hearthstones, the geomancy used to form a Master's Stone can be applied to the construction of any manse. When applied to the most common Manses, it produces a Hearthstone of Standard potency that is Linked to the Manse (adds the Linked Keyward). When applied to a rare and powerful Manse, the Hearthstone formed sustains itself (adds the Steady Keyword). Regardless of the power level of the Manse that formed the Hearthstone, the Master's Stone, when socketed into an attuned artifact, allows the user to access the Mastery Keyword of Martial Arts charms and supersedes the effects of the Terrestrial Keyword. A Standard Master's Stone is limited to providing this benefit only to a single Martial Art Style while Greater Master's Stones will allow the user to access the Mastery Keyword from all Martial Art Charms they know.

                      There are three types of people in the world... those who can count and those who can't.
                      I reject your reality and substitute my own!

                                          On pure theoretical balance considerations, I would either not allow this effect as an ST, like, at all, or have it on a lvl 5 or N/A artifact (possibly silken armor?) where it's gated behind an Evocation that has the Style's Form as a prerequisite.

                                          If I was asked by the guy playing a DB in a mixed Celestial Circle, I'd probably just allow it, because I don't see it as inherently wrongbadfun to have the DB be kind of on par with the rest of the Circle as long as they're all Player Characters.

                                          I would just never allow this to casually exist in-setting because I don't want to have to think about the possibility of more than one DB running around with Mastery.

                                          In general I think if it's not upsetting the flow of play, it's okay to have one of the PCs be singular in some way. I don't mind a Lunar PC finding a way to cast Solar Circle Sorcery, or unlocking Mastery. Or a Solar PC having access to DB Aura solely while practicing an Elemental Dragon Style, if that gels well with the story and feels like it's cool for the concept.

                                          Just, you know, don't have people running around rolling DBs because they can flare everywhere and not have to hide, and then have them Celestial level power without drawbacks. If your table likes it that way, sure do it, but I feel like it's upsetting the balance of things.
