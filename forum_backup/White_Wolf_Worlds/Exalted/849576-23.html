                Originally posted by Chausse View Post
                What do you think of Lizard-Tyrant as familiars ?
                They are definitely very, very powerful. Anything with Legendary Size is going to be, and tyrant lizards are pretty much the top end of those.

                You're certainly within your rights as an ST to disallow them if you're just not comfortable with it, though I'd definitely say the player was owed some kind of replacement creature, rather than just having it removed by fiat. However, before you do that, consider a couple of things.

                First, I'm not sure it's as powerful as you're making it out to be. Its Terrifying Dragon Roar ability, for example, counts as a decisive attack, resetting it to base initiative. That means that a) it's basically just used up all the initiative its attacks have gained on doing that rather than actually damaging anybody, and b) at base initiative, it's now more vulnerable to being crashed, or at least reduced to 1 initiative. Also, I would personally say that once you spend WP to resist the effect, you're immune for the rest of the scene. I think that's consistent with how other emotional influence effects are treated in the system.

                Second, remember that walking around with something like a tyrant lizard in tow should have consequences. Basically everybody who meets them has reasonable grounds for instantly developing an intimacy of fear, for example. And cities and other settlements will almost certainly automatically forbid it entry, and probably fight if the character presses the matter. While the PC will probably win those fights, it will still make doing business in any settled area much, much harder. When a tyrant lizard comes to town, everybody who can move out probably does so. And if the player chooses to leave their familiar out in the wilderness in order to avoid these kind of hassles, well, those are times when they won't have it available for a fight. And there are places that a tyrant lizard just can't go, no matter how much the player would like to have them handy. Try taking one into a cave where the human-scale characters have to crawl on hands and knees, for example.

                Basically, a tyrant lizard, as a familiar, exchanges raw potency in a fight for all the other things a familiar can be useful for - spying on people, appealing to people's love of pets, simply being around to help out in most circumstances, and so forth. And it's not unreasonable for enemies to take steps to deal with it either! I wouldn't recommend too much of "while you're off shopping, your tyrant lizard was ambushed and badly injured", but if people know they're going to be fighting the PC, they can do things like prepare pit traps, set up huge piles of meat to lure it away, and so on, meaning the PC with the familiar will at least have to spend time commanding and controlling it, if not losing it for the fight.

                    Originally posted by Chausse View Post
                    What do you think of Lizard-Tyrant as familiars ?

                    I'm not really having fun as a ST when one of my player has one, but I don't want to private them of a legitimate aspect of the game. What do you think ?
                    The Exalted wield the powers of the greatest gods in Creation. In play-testing, a starting Dawn killed a Tyrant Lizard without taking a single level of damage. I would say it's perfectly reasonable to not roll the Tyrant Lizard into fights unless using Seasoned Beast-Rider’s Approach.

                    If your approach to STing is going to be to ban anything that's powerful then you are not going to have fun running Exalted.

                    It is perfectly reasonable to disallow something that doesn't fit with the tone of the game. If you're running a game of politics and intrigue then a Tyrant Lizard is not appropriate.

                    It's perfectly reasonable (and your job as ST) to show the concequences of actions. Tyrant Lizards need a warm environment. They need to be fed hunt. They're basically impossible to domesticate (marking the character as Anathema as surely as a giant golden sword). They're enormous -- they're not going to fit through city gates or into sailing ships or ancient tombs. The PCs are going to be easy to track. People are going to react with fear. Imagine trying to walk a pet tiger around your hometown, now multiply that by a thousand in a world where you are guilty until proven innocent.

                    Terrifying Dragon Roar is a Latent ability that needs to be unlocked with XP (on top of a three-dot merit). It's resisted by Resolve. A Tyrant Lizard has a Base Initiative of 5 and a 10 dice Intimidation pool -- which is about 8 successes. A normal mortal solider (before Initimacies) has a Resolve of 2 and so is only losing 6i (which the Tyrant Lizard isn't gaining). An Elite Solider with an appropriate Initimacy loses 0i (zero, zilch, nadda, nil, nix, nought, nothing). Essence users with Resolve scores lower than 4 can always spend motes to make up the shortfall. Fearless adversaries also exist: Tiger Warriors, The Dead, Automatons.

                    Lastly, whilst it's an Intimidation pool not a Threaten check, I personally would use the rules for retrying Threaten checks on p223. I also agree with Kelly that once WP is spent it should protect you from fleeing for the scene (and even if you don't play it that way, at the low end, Conscripts have 3WP which is giving you three turns of fighting).

                    If you really want to ban it then talk to the player and refund the XP so they can buy something else. But Tyrant Lizards are far less powerful than Solar Charms: if you can't balance this you are going to struggle running this game.
