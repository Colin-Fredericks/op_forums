                I doubt that Exigents will step on the toes of God-Bloods much. God-Blooded are going to have their own sets of not-broken charms, which won't be Exalted or spirit charms. They will be much easier to create or endow than Exigents, but probably remain significantly weaker than Exalted.

                Meanwhile, are not the "lesser Exalted" occupying a power level between Dragon-Bloods and mortals. Some can be that, but they can also be scary enough to challenge a Solar circle. They will have at least as much range of "power levels" as existing Exalted types, and I expect that practically speaking, any god who goes to the trouble and risk to Choose one will make darn sure their Exalt more powerful and impressive than a mere God-Blood. Otherwise, what was the point of seeking the Exigence? The only time I'd expect a god to bother creating an Exigent who's no mightier than a mere God-Blood is if they're put into a position like Ten Sheaves was, where they're going to die no matter what, but with so little power than they just can't create a very impressive Exalt. Even then, every Exalt is impressive so I don't expect any to be pushovers.

                Note that in talking about power I mean "overall capacity to influence and change the world," not just "who would win in a white room fight."
