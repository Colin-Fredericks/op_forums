        Linguistics:

        Speaking the Mother Tongue
        People love to hear the sound of their native language. When the Solar speaks in someone's native language and activates this charm, all tied social influence rolls automatically go in the Solar's favour regardless of who had the better stunt. In addition, while the Solar keeps the conversation the going, the person she is speaking with does not need to spend willpower in order to resist social influences based on minor intimacies that would force her to leave the Solar's side.

            Sail:

            Jade-Forged Admiralty Code
            When training the captains of her fleet, the Solar may select a number of intimacies equal to her Essence rating. When performing sail actions indirect support of at least one of these intimacies, the Solar's subordinates gain one of the following benefits depending on the strength of the intimacy:
            - If the intimacy is minor, the character may spend Willpower to enhance the action. If the action is successful, she regains the spent Willpower immediately.
            - If the intimacy is major, the character may reroll all 6s once.
            - If the intimacy is defining, the character may reroll all failed dice once.

            These benefits never applies if the subordinate's actions are in opposition to another intimacy chosen by the Solar as part of this charm.

                    Presence:

                    Aura of Supreme Excellence
                    The Solar may activate this charm after succeeding a task involving one of her abilities. The cost to activate this charm is significantly reduced if the Solar achieves five or more threshold successes on her task. So long as this charm is active, the Solar's reputation for success will spread among interested parties. Those who require the Solar's services will seek her out and the Solar will gain additional benefits during negotiations. First, the Solar will always be given priority so long as she and her competitors are calling upon the same intimacies during the negotiations, regardless of who obtains more threshold successes. Second, when negotiating price, the Solar may ignore normal restrictions on intimacies. The Solar's client will agree to any payment that does not directly contradict one of their intimacies. This charm automatically deactivates if the Solar leaves her general area and goes some place where her reputation has not spread.

                                    Medicine:

                                    Rice Paper Plague
                                    The Solar uses her knowledge of drugs and pressure points to inflict the symptoms of any disease she is familiar with upon someone. The target of the charm is not significantly impaired or endangered, but barring supernatural means, anyone who examines her will believe she is sick. The Solar has complete control over how long the disease lasts and how it can be cured. For an additional 2XP, the Solar may invent entirely new symptoms and diseases to inflict upon people, within the bounds of what normal diseases can accomplish. For an additional 6XP, the Solar can choose to make the symptoms contagious through any normal vector she chooses.
