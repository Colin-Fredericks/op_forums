            Originally posted by TalosX View Post
            There needs to be a reason why the Loom can't accurately predict Exalts every actions.
            I see two issues inherent with this statement; one is the assumption that the Loom of Fate can be used to predict actions to an intolerable degree, and the other is a wording that limits it to something as simple as "accuracy". A read intentions can accurately predict somebody's actions. I would warrant that from a sufficiently informed and removed standpoint, most characters in the setting are eminently predictable, regardless of how many Charms they have.

            ​That being said, my specific problem is with the notion that any such difficulties take the form of "spending Essence causes problems with the Loom". It makes Charms too much like magic, and it skewed the perspective on what the issues with the Solar Exalted were, more like they were emitting ontological toxic waste than being dangerous as a result of being large personalities with enormous strength. It was a thing that bled into future writing; twee statements on how the Sidereals pled with Solars to stop spending so much Essence, resulting in curious Twilight Castes spending even more just to see what would happen.

            Originally posted by TalosX
            it wouldn't have been possible for the Exalts to defeat the Primordials, or for a Solar/Lunar to escape a Wyld Hunt, or for anyone to outwit the Sidereals.
            You know, foresight is not necessarily the benefit that a lot of people seem to think it is. If I tie you up and leave you in the middle of a busy train tracks at night (sufficiently camouflaged), you can very accurately predict what will happen, and the only advantage that this will confer upon you is that when you are obliterated, it won't have been a surprise.

            ​But again, it leads back into an assumption that the Loom of Fate can be used as a kind of everything search engine in the first place.

            Originally posted by TalosX
            From a ST/player perspective, you can never accurately predict what another player/ST might do.
            ​Perhaps not, but both of them can cheat; a player activates a power to predict the future, and the Storyteller is obligated to give them information about future events. A Stroyteller is running a character that is supposed to have powers to predict the future, they don't actually require foresight, or even to get the players to tell things; the player characters arrive at a spot, and the Storyteller adjudicates then and there that a soothsayer who predicted them is already waiting.

            ​But that's tangential. The whole idea of prediction is tangential. To reiterate, the Essence of Exalted should not be treated as a solvent for reality.
