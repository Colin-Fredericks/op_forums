    I am Monkipi and like Keichiokami and Ferryman I live in San Diego. I'm a student, a researcher, and someone who does general miscellany for a charter school development company. I also perform regularly at San Diego's National comedy theatre, where I met the majority of the cast for my actual play podcast.

    Been playing games since I was about 9 years old, with the WotC Star Wars RPG, so yeah, I'm young. I got the Exalted 2e core and Chageling: The Lost right before I entered high school and fell in love. I lost the books not 4 months later in one of San Diego's many wildfires. My favorite Exalts are Sidereals, and were I chosen I reckon I'd be a Chosen of Serenity or an Eclipse.

                            Hi all, I'm Aaron, and no, I don't expect you to share your given name just because I (and Colin) have done so. I won't be offended if you don't remember it, either.

                            I go by Drake here, but some of you might be more familiar with the name Drake Noble (and corresponding Dracula avatar), or may even known me from other parts of the internet as AaronOfBarbaria.

                            I've lived a bunch of places in the U.S.A. over the years, but Idaho has been home since I settled in after a lady named Katrina suggested I move out of the New Orleans area about 11 years ago.

                            I don't work anymore (I had some serious health issues that got in the way, but I've since gotten them in control - now I am just reveling in not currently needing to work to live comfortably); but I used to be a cook, and I've done loads of customer service - I just keep the house in order and keep a steady flow of games going for my wife (who I'll probably never actually get married to for financial reasons) and our friends.

                            Also, as if it wasn't already obvious, I'm prone to extreme openness - do please let me known if that ever makes any of you uncomfortable.

                            I'm interested in a wide array of games, and it was almost completely accidental that I ever got into Exalted - I was picking up some other book at a shop and saw a 1st edition Exalted book sitting on the shelf, and while I was intrigued by it at the time but couldn't get my D&D group interested in trying a different shade of fantasy or my World of Darkness group interested in trying out a different flavor of White Wolf. So I didn't even buy the book, and barely even thought about the game at all for years. Then, once I had found myself in Idaho and found a FLGS to get in with a new group of players at, they were sitting down one day to play 2nd edition Exalted - I sat in on a session to see how the game looked in play, went home after and bought the PDF, stayed up the whole night studying it and building my first character, and then met up with the ST at the FLGS the next day after work to show him the character and ask if I could get in on the game.

                            I was hooked so bad that it didn't even stop me when the rules system kept crushing my mind every time I tried to ST my own chronicle, and now with EX3 out Exalted has become my go-to game out of the whole collection of White Wolf/Onyx Path work.

                            Not so noble anymore.
