    You seem to be having a dick measuring contest while wanting to establish a category for "blue dicks" and saying that no matter how big the other guy is since this other one is blue he has to win. I just don't see why that must be so, why is there a dick measuring contest in the first place?
    Definitely. All this dick-measuring crap is totally missing the point. Yes, Solars are the strongest, and there's nothing wrong with that. To continue with the superhero analogies, Superman is flat-out stronger than Batman, but that doesn't make Batman less interesting as a hero. Many people would argue the reverse, in fact - trying to give Batman superstrength, flight and laser vision because it's not fair that Superman gets to be the strongest isn't just missing the point of Batman, it's actively making him worse as a character because a large part of his appeal comes from being the one normal guy in a world full of gods. Yes, DBs are the weakest of the Exalted, and that's why they're my favourite Exalted. Trying to give them Solar-level power does them a tremendous disservice, and comes with this weird assumption that the only way a character can be worth playing is if they can beat everyone else in a dick-waving contest.

                        I find it odd that people are discussing the Immaculate Martial Arts as if they were some kind of trump card against the Solars.They were created by the Sidereals to help the Dragonblooded bring wayward gods to heel. The Dragonblooded had already proved themselves very capable of killing Solars with their native charms by that point. The reason Wyld Hunts are assumed to have a whole bunch of Immaculate masters also has nothing to do with any kind of perceived superiority of the martial arts for fighting Anathema over the other abilities. All Dragonblooded warriors were expected to participate in Wyld Hunts, but in these tumultuous time many neglect their spiritual duty in favor of helping their House prepare for civil war. Those that remain are the most dedicated to the Immaculate faith, which for the most part are old, experienced monks and their young pupils. And old monks tend to have the highest essence, the most combat charms, and they have seen all your tricks already. That's what makes them scary for a Solar to face, not the Immaculate styles somehow being better than other Dragonblooded charms.

                                            Okay, so I think I figured out part of the problem here. There isn't a worse comparison to make regarding power than the absolute limit of Exalted power (Solars) and the absolute weakest Exalted (DBs) especially in an area which is specifically designed to allow exalt splats to break out of their themes. So yes, a Solar using Breath of the Fire Dragon deals more damage than a terrestrial, and you can say that a Solar is "better" at breathing fire than a DB is, in this one particular worst case scenario.

                                            Really though, a DB can be a lot more versatile with fire than a Solar can. It's part of their anima and their native charms. They can do cool things like instantly rise from prone like a firecracker and stuff like that, while Solars are relegated to just the charms in Fire Dragon Style, and any evocations they unlock.

                                            So lets look at something else, Sidereal native charms. This is a way better way to compare Solar power. So Guancyto brouight up Getimian resistance charms. Those are not going to be able to soak damage better than a Solar, for sure. But look at what Sid resistance charms can do...well for one they can bind an elemental to your will! Yeah, there's NO logical connection to make for a Solar charm to be able to bind elementals because you shrug off damage and hardship. It's also powerful, it's like an instant summon elemental spell, it's great! A Solar just can't do that, they can't ever use a resistance charm to instantly bind an elemental.

                                            If a Sidereal did that in front of them they'd ask: "How in the holy mother of fuck did you do that?"
                                            To which the Sidereal replies: "I don't know, you just have to be really tough and the elementals of the world obey you, they respect that I guess?"

                                            And no matter how hard they try, no matter what they do, they never will be able to do that in the same way.


                                            The thing is, when you make it a martial art, that brings it right back into the Solar wheelhouse. It's not a native effect that only your exaltation can power, it's specifically something any exalt and many spirits. You're not taking the essence of fire within your soul and extruding it through your weapon, you're performing specific spiritual motions that anyone qualified can use to bring forth fire. That, combined with the mechanical effect, means Solars are the best at it. Yeah, it sucks for the DB PC who's facing off against a Solar who's laughing in his face because he can't do Air Dragon Style as well as the Solar can...but then that DB just screams a torrent of lightning at him from his native charms and proves that while the Solar merely adopted the wind, he was born to it.

                                            Of course things being equal that DB is going to get his ass handed to him, but at least he'll have proven that mastering Solar Air Dragon Style doesn't make you a chosen of Mela.
