                        Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz View Post
                        In terms of balance, my suggestion is that an artefact 3 firewand comes with a regular bayonet, while an artefact 4 one (such as Sekhem) has an artefact bayonet.
                        I wouldn't treat this in such a mechanistic way. An artifact bayonet is additional base functionality, and every artifact has some sort of additional base functionality that's appropriate to its theme and mechanical design. If an Artifact 4 dragon sigh wand is already highly tuned or is thematically designed for avoiding close combat, I wouldn't add a bayonet for free because "any Artifact 4 dragon sigh wand can have a bayonet." On the other hand, a dragon sigh wand designed specifically for melee use could easily be Artifact 3, with the bayonet (and some associated mechanical flourish) serving as the core of its additional base functionality.

                                            Originally posted by Jefepato View Post
                                            Still, there's no law that says everything made out of a magical material has Artifact stats. I wouldn't have been surprised (although this turned out not to be the case) if Sekhem's bayonet had mundane stats and its only special trait was being able to handle Sekhem's heat output.

                                            Yes, this. Traditionally, artifact weapons get their high stats from their abnormally high mass (eg, daiklaves the size of surfboards). Magical material composition does not in and of itself provide artifact weapon stats.

                                            (This is one of the things that gets house ruled away a lot, and which the game line itself — especially in earlier editions — hasn't always consistently adhered to. I'm just spelling out canonical principles here for those who are interested in such things.)
