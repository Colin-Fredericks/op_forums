                                            In thinking back over some of the logistics, I'm reminded of the fact that in addition to the enormous Scarlet Dynasty, the Realm also has a number of native outcastes that also outnumbers the entire Dragon Blooded population of Lookshy. Sure, Lookshy's population of Exalted also consists of a mix of old blood natives and naturalised new blood, but they don't draw so much of a distinction.

                                            So for the Realm, you probably get a lot of Dynasts who have the luxury of being able to have some personal country estate where they spend a lot of their time drawing on the profits of family business interests or being a noted attendee at all the regional society events or doing something artistic, because the state is able to project its power over vast distances without needing to call upon your service in particular, owing to a vast pool of peasant Exalted for whom military service from the outset is kind of mandatory.

                                            And sure, part of the reason why the Blessed Isle has such a large number of native outcastes is probably the long term result of their aristocratic setup in the first place. Like, your mother and matriarch will probably gripe and tut over the intemperance of men any time you sow your oats far afield, but at least the Empress would know that doing so is helping to ensure that the mortal population has enough Exalted blood to reliably generate large numbers of people who are so socially disadvantaged that they can't really resist when you say that they've either got to be monks or soldiers, all to the ultimate profit of the Realm and the Dynasty (although the latter complicated a bit by any Dynasts who don't like the idea that they're not as powerful in the military as they think they should be). Or even if it's not as direct as Dragon Blooded men going around having sex with peasant women, there are enough mortal Dynasts with mortal children that eventually they'll descend out of the more privileged strata and carry that bloodline among the plebians, and it's happening all over and has been going for centuries.

                                            The Scavenger Lands probably has at least as many people overall as the Blessed Isle, but proportionally fewer of them are meaningfully descended from Exalted, and can't become so because Lookshy lacks that reach and rank. And that means that the Dragon Blooded of Lookshy are expected to do more for less.

                                            Thinking about that brings me to the consideration that, where the Realm is often written as having consistently had ten thousand Dynasts for a reasonably long time, it seems logical that Lookshy has only gotten to 3000 Dragon Blooded fairly recently. I really doubt they had that when they were a Shogunate army left as a garrison force (having taken some losses fighting the Fair Folk? There are later wars, against the Realm and major Eastern invaders, that certainly took their toll), and that would have also created the environment in which they survive primarily by holing up in their fort city and lack the muscle or street cred to turn the mortal population of the region into an engine for continually producing large numbers of Dragon Blooded. At least proportional to the Realm.

                                            That and how, if proportionally more Gentes Exalted go into military service, their population growth is probably much weaker than the Dynasty's. Sure, most Dynasts don't live past one hundred, but I'll bet you anything that a minority who even make it that far are the ones who dedicated their lives to continuously getting involved in situations with people unreservedly trying to kill them. Lookshy's primary business is not only spreading its Exalted beyond the confines of its walls, it's probably ensuring that a sizable minority of them never come home and procreate.

                                            One could read Lookshy's state of growing national crisis as being a result of having, after almost eight hundred years, finally started accumulating an Exalted population large enough that they both have the option to expand beyond the traditional model, and might be obligated to if that model is buckling under the strain of them.

                                            Still, that leaves them with the awkward (and intriguing) question of where their expanding Exalted population is going to go.

                                            Now, having said all of that... I'm really interested to learn more about Prasad's military. Their Exalted population makes sense for their situation and history, but considering that they're a more actively expansionistic empire neighbouring territorial rivals that can actually put up a decent fight against their mystical strength, it raises a few questions. How large is their military? What proportion of their military are Exalted (and what does having so many of their Exalted out in the wars mean for their society)? Or, in contrast to the Realm and Lookshy, does Prasad need to adapt to its situation by putting less of its Exalted might out in the field, and more into the logistical and organizational side of things? Or is it even just a matter of being uncontested Exalted who reside in splendour and dominate so thoroughly that they can command vast armies of mortals to fight on their behalf, while feeling comfortable making little direct contribution to their success?

                                            I have approximate knowledge of many things.

My point was really just that city-states can still be major regional powers, and that their limited nominal land holdings can be inherently misleading to a moden observer. Most of a larger Bronze/Iron Age country is likely going to be wilderness anyway; somewhere like Lookshy, while nominally small, can be just as influential, powerful, and populated as one with considerably more territory.
