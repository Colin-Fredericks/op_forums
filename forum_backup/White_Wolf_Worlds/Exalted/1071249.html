    Exalted Tunes

    03-31-2017, 10:48 AM
    So, something I've been lamenting greatly; the absence anywhere I can see, of any really good Exalted music playlists. And usually when I do find a few, they're basically a pick-and-mix bag of songs with wildly different moods and tones, which, being the lazy sort I am, I have to babysit during a game and can't really just sit back and let it run in the background and count on having appropriate music to the scene in progress.

    So I got sick of it and made my own.

    Wonderous Music: https://www.youtube.com/playlist?lis...vwdQfVUFSs5C3M

    Fierce Music: https://www.youtube.com/playlist?lis...kBBFRKI2Te_q_M

    Sorrowful Music: https://www.youtube.com/playlist?lis...CWxKt5PPAKK2bb

    Cheerful Music: https://www.youtube.com/playlist?lis...8wGujWjzF3CZLE

    Romantic Music: https://www.youtube.com/playlist?lis...ikV3nOAqcVK8zj

    Adventuresome Music: https://www.youtube.com/playlist?lis...6Smi8WY25qMIgy

    Villainous Music: https://www.youtube.com/playlist?lis...yiZc7G50mWKcIK

    Victorious Music: https://www.youtube.com/playlist?lis...7pk_CoVetR4CFd

    Pensive Music: https://www.youtube.com/playlist?lis...YlpBnsIURbcU3-

    Suspenseful Music: https://www.youtube.com/playlist?lis...CjT456W9dgPCEP

    Stirring Music: https://www.youtube.com/playlist?lis...2KR5_xpCcsShxu

    They're all sorted by mood! This is very much a work in progress kind of project that will likely be constantly expanding as I scour the depths of youtube. So in other words, I'm always looking for songs to add. Just FYI, I try to stay away with songs that have English lyrics, if any at all, since they tend to distract from dialogue between players. I also tend to go for more traditional flavored stuff than straight up modern stuff; a japanese folk song with electric guitar in it? Cool. A straight heavy metal song? That isn't even like, Viking metal? Less cool. I find it tends to distract from the feel of the playlist, and by extension the game.

    ​Anyway, hope you guys enjoy.

                          Listen to this: https://austinwintory.bandcamp.com/a...oth-and-tail-2

                          Wintory uses several leitmotifs in this album in different songs. The specific leitmotif I would suggest to you develops through "The Food of Beasts", "The Old South Distillery", "Who Becomes the Meat?", "The Ivories of Beasts", and "The Victors will Feast".

                          If I had to put them in order of least to most refined, I'd go with "The Old South Distillery", "The Ivories of Beasts", "The Food of Beasts", "Who Becomes the Meat?" and finally "The Victors will Feast".

                          Of course, this is all assuming you're okay with early 20th-century Russian waltzy stylings. If you're looking for a different genre, please specify.
