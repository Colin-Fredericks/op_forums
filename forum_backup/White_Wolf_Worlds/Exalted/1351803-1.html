                I took all the great advice in this thread and made my first proper Ex3 character, a Night Caste names Smiling Tiger and had a Session 0 with my future ST.

                I started with Ebon Shadow, Stealth, and Larceny at 5. Dodge, Athletics, Tiger Style and some other stuff were at 3. I have the Artifact Vengeant
                ●●●
                and Belt of Shadow Walking
                ●●●.
                I didn't realize how much I was gonna miss those 10 committed motes, not going to lie. E1 is 13/33 so it's literally 20% of my essence pool to keep those things on. I also kept forgetting to add the +3 Stealth dice from the Belt, but its very useful.

                Stalking Shadow Spirit from Miracles is the coolest charm, I used it twice. It made me feel like I was really in control of the places I snuck in to, and i went from Resources 0 to Resources 2 by casing a Jeweler's and then using SSS with Flawless Pickpocket to steal a wide array of jewels and gold. Living Shadow Preparation is another incredible charm. I have two separate pools of successes to use whenever I need them. LSP can be used anytime anywhere, but i have to use all of it at once. SSS can be rationed out as needed, but only in a single location that I've cased out. Both of these pools were 6 or 7 successes big.

                Larceny's Seasoned Criminal Method was also amazingly useful both for RP and for dealing with criminals. I looked like a desperate mark to one greedy jeweler, I looked like a sailor on shore leave ready to blow a months pay on dice booze and girls to the door guards of a teahouse crime front, and I convinced the inner guard that I was a prospective member to get access to the Big Boss. All from one charm with no mote cost. Its great. I have Unshakable Rogue Spirit but I didn't use it and I'm not sure if I like it. I don't think I need the extra success it offers but the idea of stealing from yourself is hysterical.

                Graceful Crane Stance and Monkey Leap were ok for some thematic stuff, Naruto roof hopping and stunts, but I didn't feel like I needed them. They unlock a lot of cool stuff though, so they'll stay.

                Sensory Acuity Prana helped a bit (double 9s on its own isn't great with a dicepool of 6) but I fell in love with the Keen Sense line of charms and I'm going to by all of them. Especially Hearing and Touch, but I don't have them yet.

                I bought Reed in the Wind but didn't need it yet, its there as a safety feature. It's my only defense charm besides the bonus from Ebon Shadow Style. I had only one fight in my Session 0 and it was me ambushing them to almost instant death.

                So, on to the fightin bits. Vengeant with the first Evocation unlocked (but unused), it's a weird ability where you slowly accumulate initiative against what I assume is supposed to be unaware targets. I didn't have a chance to use it yet.

                I have the first three charms of Ebon Shadow, and they were each useful. I have Striking Fury Claw as well.

                So I watched a Shikari try to shake down a Lost Egg Crime Lord for my location (we'd spoken earlier that night), and when words turned to blows I took action. I was already in Shadow Form from the Belt (holy shit, 10 motes on top of 10 committed cuts deep), and I attacked from complete stealth. I used Blinding Battle Feint to roll JB and start in stealth, blew my Living Shadow Prep and Stalking Shadow Spirit pools to get a starting Initiative of 15 (to the delight of my ST, a current Single Point Stylist) to the Shikari's 6. Automatic Ebon Shadow Form (giving me even more Initiative, 18 to 5). Seven Points of Weakness and Striking Fury Claw made the attack shockingly brutal and I went from 18 Initiative to FORTY TWO, the highest our game has ever reached. I crashed the shit of the Shikari and because of stunts put her head into a hotpot and left her windless and dazed. She spends her turn just standing up and trying to get a read on where I am.

                So I Decisived for 42 dice and did 14 damage. Instantly dead.

                I did a little RP stuff from there and it was all great. Smiling Tiger is now headed to the Main Plot on a ship owned by one of the PCs.

                He left the body of the Shikari (sans white jade armor) in a dingy pushed out of port with a letter in the boat that simply says "The Dragon Eater Ate Well" (my crime alias is the Dragon Eater, a killer who takes down Dynasts who deal in the criminal world).

                I had a lot of fun. I went through motes a lot quicker than I thought I would. 15 charms is a thin budget when youre the one making characters, I only took one charm in my supernal ability that wasn't E1, and there's always another 10 charms you want to complete your build. You have to make big sacrifices in Abilities to get what you want from the start too.

                I really felt like a character with a specific theme who had a niche carved for himself and I didn't feel like I spread myself too thin.

                I can't wait to start playing! Special thanks to JohnDoe244 for the charm advice.

                Please be warned: this is not champagne, this is most likely a duck.
