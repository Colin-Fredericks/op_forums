    [White Elephant for Dietaku] Carrion Gorge

    12-25-2019, 07:07 PM
    Dietaku's request afforded Lunars, so I took it as an opportunity to bring out this fellow who I thought up while imagining something else entirely.

    Carrion Gorg

    Background: Gorg’s life began as a squire tribute by his family to the Knights of the Black Dragon, that martial order that reigned over the iron-rich hill lands nestled at the connecting point between the Yellow and Maruto rivers. His diminutive stature might have prevented him from ascending to full knighthood, but the point was rendered moot when the order was destroyed by an alliance between an army from the River Province and the forces of neighbouring Vaneha as a response to the century-long war between them and their northern neighbours. The sight of the once formidable citadel of the Knights being reduced to slag by the ancient siege engines of Lookshy would haunt Gorg for the rest of his life, before the Sword Prince dealt with the few survivors (mostly fellow squires and other support staff) by stripping their arms and exile from his newly annexed territory. It was not long before the wanderers, who had become dubbed the Dian, vanished, and most assumed that they had merely died in the wastes of the Scavenger Lands.

    The truth was that they entered a region riven with entrances to tunnels left behind from the First Age, in which the exiles would seek shelter. Gorg found new purpose there, as he proved to be well suited to squeezing through tight ducts or around debris to scout ahead of some tunnels or find his way towards the mechanisms that could open their doors. When the Dian located a tunnel that appeared to be wide and deep enough to accommodate all of their people, Gorg had to employ all of his ingenuity to find his way to the stiffened levers for the entrance, twisting his hands to pry them loose, resulting in doors throughout the complex unlocking. A great cloud of silver mist was freed from the depths and washed over him, before rushing out into the open night and drifting to the northeast. Compelled to follow, Gorg assumed the shape of a ragged crow and flew after it.

    The mist led Gorg to a city of wonders the likes of which he had never imagined before, and he found himself the honoured guest of a beautiful and terrifying devil queen. He learned many things in that court, and while much was of the sheer power of the Lunar Exalted whose ranks he had joined or the secrets of sorcery that his new mistress seemed to have an endless library to share, what he regarded as the most valuable was the history of the Dragon Blooded Shogunate and how their tyranny had been opposed by the righteous Silver Pact. Gorg recognised the shadow of that Shogunate in the mighty soldiers of Lookshy who had led the armies of the west to destroy all that he had known, and what was once terrible fear transformed into furious hatred, and then a cold obsession. The Queen of Fangs found this development quite pleasing, and with her encouragement and blessing Gorg returned to the tunnels where he had left his people in security and some minimum of comfort. He convinced them to abandon that safety in pursuit of vengeance and a return to their old dignity, and led them deeper into the darkness of those tunnels than they had ever ventured before.

    In the centuries since, the ragged scavenger Carrion Gorg has cultivated his Dominion into a subtle poison worming its way through the Scavenger Lands. The tunnels turned out to be far more extensive than first realised, and his excavations have developed a network providing rapid access across the region. Strange creatures were found to reside in the depths; Gorg tamed them and they serve now as hunting hounds and guides for the Dian so they can travel without the burden of fuel or torches, while the few intruders never leave. With these assets, Gorg endeavours to continue the legacy of his shahan-ya, although where she wielded grand sorceries and vast monsters his preference is for spying to identify weaknesses and lightning raids to make off with key supplies, often aided by the misdirection of his own appearance far from the true target. The intelligence communities of the River Province have started to recognise that these incidents are connected, but they remain at a loss as to how they appear and vanish without a trace or the use of bulky transportation. The Dian use more than just access to tunnel networks for this, as Gorg was well instructed in the value of flexibility. One of his favourite tricks is to send infiltrators in the guise of itinerant workers whom he has endowed with the power to assume the shape of horses so that they can hitch themselves up with wagons and disappear at great speed; the heart’s blood for the animals came from one of his earliest and most renowned feats, when in a single night he personally hunted down and slew all remaining steeds of the Marukani that had clashed against the Black Dragon Knights in their final battle.

    Lookshy remains at the centre of Gorg’s world. Almost all of his operations are intended to come back to hurt the city in some manner, and while the Dian are widely distributed most remain clustered in tunnels that keep them within striking distance of the city or its redoubts. While Gorg usually keeps his head while evading or drawing away Lookshyan Wyld Hunts, few things can make him lose his reason like the sight of lone and vulnerable Dragon Blooded of the Seventh Legion, and attacks targeting pieces of the First Age arsenal take the biggest risks. Gorg dreams of a day on which the Thousand Fangs Army will issue forth from the east to destroy the River Province, and when the Legion goes to man its defences find that they collapse beneath them, the foundations of their walls undermined, the dragon lines fuelling their weapons severed, and assassins will spring from the ground to strike at their flanks before the ranks of apemen obliterate them. He even envisions tearing down the proud Lookshy Manse and on its ruins raising a new citadel of a revived Order of the Black Dragon.

    Image: In his human appearance, Gorg is a short man (who looks even smaller from his tendency to hunch), pale, bony and hairless, and still favours the simple tunic of a squire. In honour of the fallen order he wields no weapon or armour, but does possess a pair of moonsilver armbands with power to summon and direct obscuring mists, wrought for him by Raksi herself and are his most treasured possession. In his hybrid form, scraggly black quills grown from every part of his body but his face, his hands become gnarled claws and a pair of wings spring from his shoulders; they cannot fly, but he wraps them around himself like a cloak. His totemic anima displays a series of disembodied claws that alternately twine around one another like cogs or stretch out until they resemble tunnels.

    Intimacies:

    Defining Principle: I will see the last remnants of the Shogunate swept away.

    Defining Tie: Raksi (Aromantic infatuation)

    Defining Tie: Lookshy (Hatred)

    Major Principle: The Knights of the Black Dragon will rise again.

    Major Principle: Retreat always gives you a shot at the target tomorrow.

    Major Tie: Dian Raiders (Protective Pride)

    Minor Tie: Luna (Curiosity)

    Minor Tie: The tunnel beasts (Fondness)

    Minor Principle: The secrets of the Old Realm’s tunnels beckon me.

    Secrets: Gorg is among those who have Raksi’s confidence, and regularly corresponds with her; as such, he is privy to many of her secrets, both personal and mystical, and has extensive knowledge of Thousand Fangs Army operations. He has committed the extent of the tunnel systems that he has excavated to memory, and knows the movements of his people around them; he also possesses full knowledge of their infiltrators around the Scavenger Lands. A secret he keeps to himself, having not even shared it with Raksi, is a strong draw to manifestations of the Underworld, that he suspects implies a capacity for necromancy if he ever opted to seek out an instructor. He hopes this is the reason he feels an uncanny yearning towards Thorns.

    Roleplaying Hints: Gorg has a preference for attacking his opponents sideways, employing curses, traps and attacks from the shadows after careful observation to go after important supplies or cut off mystical power sources. When he presents himself openly in battle, it is with savage fury and throwing himself against opponents with wild abandon, but this is merely to be loud and attention grabbing so his operatives can pursue his true objectives elsewhere. While he’s not risk averse, he’ll generally opt to pull out and attack again from a different angle if he judges a situation to pose very much danger to himself or his raiders.

    Caste: No Moon

    Spirit Shape: A sickly looking, partially featherless crow

    Tell: A lingering stench of decay.

    Attributes: Strength 2, Dexterity 4, Stamina 4, Charisma 3, Manipulation 2, Appearance 3 (Hideous), Perception 4, Intelligence 5, Wits 5
    Essence: 4
    Willpower: 8
    Join Battle: 9 dice
    Personal Motes: 19
    Peripheral Motes: 50
    Health Levels: -0/-1x2/-2x10/-4x5/Incap
    Abilities: Athletics 2, Awareness 4 (Specialty: Vulnerable assets), Brawl 4, Craft 2 (Specialty: Traps), Dodge 4, Integrity 3, Investigation 3 (Specialty: Key resources), Larceny 3 (Specialty: Lightning raids), Lore 4, Occult 4, Performance 3 (Specialty: Stories about the Knights), Presence 2, Resistance 3, Stealth 5, Survival 3, Thrown 2, War 3
    Merits: Allies 5 (Two purchases, denoting ties with fellow students of Raksi), Artifact 3, Backing 4 (The Silver Pact), Command 4 (Dian Raiders), Contacts 5 (Dian infiltrators), Danger Sense, Fleet of Foot, Followers 3 (The Dian), Heart’s Blood 3 (The Scavenger Lands), Hideous, Mentor 3 (Raksi), Stolen Faces 5
