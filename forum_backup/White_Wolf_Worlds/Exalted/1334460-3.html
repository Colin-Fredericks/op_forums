        Personal thoughts:

        Arc 1: The beginning
        • Exploration of powers
        • First usage of craft charms to make good food and supplies
        • Medicine charms for the unhealthier people of the apartment - massages and basic medication, in return for some money
        • Usage of craft charms to create clothes, repair damaged property, etc
        • First usage of Food from the Aerial table
        • Payment of the homeless to serve as stand-holders; no need for license for selling art
        • Suits, tables, and other equipment are created via craft charms (basic and major projects, taken from the scrapyard)
        • Artwork involves poetry, paintings, charcoal and ballpoint drawings, embroidery, and anything that requires low level of starting materials. Embroidery is taken from old clothes recycled.
        • Money comes in. Money is used to get better material - equipment bonus ensures better quality
        • More money comes in
        • Laptop is bought. First usage of Godmind alogrithms. A fake name is produced, as well as a bank account. Finalization of relationship, causing ally rating to increase
        • Art projects now occur at high speed. Once it was 60 dollars or so a day. Now it is hundreds (internet really streamlines shit).
        • Meanwhile, she works at a vet shelter, using her medicine charms and testing them out. Access to internet means access to online publications at her own time. Medicine rises from 1 to 5 (supernal)
        • Usage of subterfuge charm to enter vet clinic after-hours.
        • Meanwhile, in school a friend's relative is injured (car crash? Cancer? HIV?). She goes to heal them. First usage. Then she goes and heals others.
        • Subterfuge charms to sneak in after hours to heal people
        • People begin to notice
        • Close call with 'Destination Truth' crew.
        • Computers wiped, and hijacked with a virus to prevent recording.
        • This goes on for awhile, gaining more money and helping to pay off bills.
        • Until there's a car crash

        Arc 2: The discovery
        • Massive solar bonfire as mote reactor is activated and she begins healing a 20-car pileup
        • No body dies, but in the age of smartphones, this is the end of anonymity for her
        • Police intervention, bring her in for questioning
        • Mass news network and social media outcry. Most dismiss it as a hoax, but the event was large and at an intersection, and so eyewitness accounts, multiple videos from various smartphones and CCTV, all converge. It gets more and more believable
        • Outcry is enough such that trained doctors are brought in, to verify the powers the girl possesses
        • Verification indicates that, yes, this is a thing. Yes, mass healing had occurred, and so the girl's abilities are verified by experts. Most scoff at this
        • Some remember the massive bonfire anima released several months ago, in the dead of winter. Most had dismissed it as a stunt or prank, but now...
        • Mass confusion and talking.
        • First requests for healing come in; first a stream, then a raging river
        • Healing occurs regularly, with full on experts. Derogatory comparisms to 'faith healing' is made, but they are wrong. For one thing, she's atheist. Also, she doesn't bother with speeches or theatrics. She simply separates them into two lines and flares her mote reactor to do work. And thirdly, she's perfectly fine with people coming in to watch her.
        • As it becomes clearer and clearer that she's a true McCoy, more people come. People from overseas. Rich people. Powerful people. Cancer cures, repair of destroyed limbs and brains, fixing muscular dystrophy
        • This comes to a head where the US government comes in and takes her into see what's happening.

            Originally posted by Accelerator View Post
            Well, that's awkward. If you're a 15 year old kid, you aren't going to have anything above 3, or hell, even a 2 is surprising. Perhaps I should chalk it up to past life? Or do a short training montage for grabbing information and things?
            Exaltation chooses exceptional people. Now "exceptional" is what you choose to do, not how many dots are on your sheet, but if we're talking about the one person in all the world who deserves to be Exalted by the Sun, a girl who studies medicine despite all odds and expectations, then this deserving and worthy mortal is probably not a 1-dot amature in her Supernal ability.

            I don't know if you're familiar with the legal drama "Suits", but Mike Ross is a drug dealer who never went to law school who all happens to be the greatest lawyer of all time -- worth watching the pilot episode. It's great inspiration for Exalted Modern.

            Training montage works. I guess. Taking an ability from 1 to 5 takes two weeks in training time (9 days for 3 to 5). It's a lot of XP though.

            Personally, I'd build the character mechanically and fit the story around that. I can already hear the "boo hiss" from the gallery, but as I see it you're either writing your own fan-fic which is loosely inspired by Exalted (in which case do what you want and who cares) or you're genuinely exploring the concept of a lone Exalt in a modern setting (in which case you should constrain yourself to what that would actually mean). And as there aren't other players and you're effectively the ST, and you have a strong character concept in mind, I see no particular disadvantage from working backwards from the mechanics.

            A Solar Exalt can do X. How does X effect a modern world.

            You want to tell a story of evolving power. How much medicine does she need to start with to tell the story you want to tell? If you want her to start with Medicine 5 Charms, maybe she should be a gifted healer since before her Exaltation, reading medical textbooks in Elementary School. If she only needs Medicine 2 Charms to start and grows into her power over weeks and months, then start her at Medicine 2. Your story outline for 1 to 5 via online journals also works.

            We don't have anything concrete on Past Lives in 3E, but they never increased your raw ability scores before.

                                Personal thought.

                                Faith Healers. Yes, those guys. The ones with stadiums. The ones with 'miracle crusades' and 'not enough faith' and 'seed money'. The ones with megachurches and entire tv channels for them. The ones that sell miracle water and have entire beachfront houses and private jets, bought with cash.

                                So their reaction to a real life healer? At first, disbelief. And then, as she gets more and more powerful, and the reports come streaming in, they'll realize something. That something funky is going on. Someone at there has real life healing powers. At this point, they have two choices. Attack or co-opt.

                                They'll either try to discredit her, and when that fails, they'll try to deal with her. Either by decrying her as a witch, calling her evil, or just saying that she's the antichrist trying to lead people astray with false miracles and such. Others, will try to co-opt her. Either to try and become a 'mentor', or by claiming her as proof of god's intervention in the world (she's an atheist) and then use her as proof that magic healing can exist (she hates faith healers).

                                On the religious part, I'm not quite sure. This is set in America, and obviously, christianity is going to play a huge part. Catholic church? Majority of christian denominations say that no more miracles this age, which is going to be awkward with someone pulling off miracle healings left and right. She's also an atheist. This is important, because I'm quite sure that I will write about the christian portion trying to get her to sign on with or endorse them. Which... she would probably not do.
