    What are the iconic Dragon-Blooded Charms?

    02-13-2016, 05:31 PM
    Exactly what it says on the tin. I'm converting Exalted to a different system to run for my group, but it's a long time since I played it for any length of time and I don't really have the sense of the Charm sets any more that I used to have. So I'm asking everyone here -what Dragon-Blooded charms do you think are the most iconic and recognizable ones, the ones that are "signature" powers of the Terrestrials? I'm after both general and Elemental effects and the more Aspect-spesific ones.

    Elemental Bolt Attack, Dragon-Graced Weapon and its equivalents in the other combat abilities are what spring to mind for me as bread-and-butter combat effects, as well as the anima flux itself, even though it's not actually a Charm. There's the Immaculate Styles, in theory, but I don't really have a good picture of what each of them actually does for you in a fight, beyond Fire Dragon letting you make super-fast attacks and Water Dragon letting you control people's blood. There's Unassailable Body of Element, which isn't really a staple the way the dodge tree is built, but has the imagery of one, if that makes sense.

    There's Oath of the Ten Thousand Dragons and Ten Thousand Dragons Fight As One, but other than that I'm coming up empty on social magic. Not to say that they don't have it, but it's mostly rather generic stuff, or if interesing then in a way that doesn't evoke their themes much to me, like the "fight under cover of a performance"-Charms.

    Utility-wise there's Terrestrial Ability Reinforcement which lets you share skills with others, All-Encompassing Earth Sense and Feeling the Air Technique which I always loved, there's some Air charm whose name I don't remember which lets you turn invisible... and there's Wind-Carried Words, which I've always felt should have a pretty profound effect on social life in the Realm -it's not a phone, but it's still very convenient!

    What effects have I missed which you think are iconic of the Dragon-Blooded? How do you see things differently? Please help me out!

                          I know we already hit this one, but every single Dragon-Blooded can and should learn Wind-Carried Words. So phenomenally important.

                          Beyond that, there are two areas that stand out for me that folks haven't already mentioned.

                          The first are the teamwork charms, like letting more of your friends pile on one person, or doing stealth for a whole group. Dragon-Blooded were always billed as being good at teamwork (when they could stop stabbing each other in the back), and I wish we had seen more of these.

                          The second are the Investigate charms that let you find out who someone knows and where they live just by talking to them. That's incredibly powerful, and it helps to explain why the Wyld Hunt is so effective.

                                                  Tricky to think of many as iconic, like "This is what Dragonblooded are all about" or "Without this, they're not Dragonblooded".

                                                  Talking about general themes, just taking the Air Charms for example the themes of the Charm trees in 1e Dragonblooded book were:

                                                  Linguistics - Words carried through air: Universal communication, meaning twisting and amplification, distant speech and hearing, all so long as the communication is speech through air, and then With One Mind, which is enhanced non-verbal communication and wordless coordination, through infused Air essence.

                                                  Lore - Meditation and willpower to master and draw of elemental energy (kind of strange but where else do you put meditation and will, I guess?). Only Elemental Concentration helps the character learn; other Charms allow drawing on elemental energy, or channeling it.

                                                  Occult - Kind of a mish mash. Spirit Mirror allows Dragonblooded to see dematerialized spirits in mirrors, Harmonious Wind-Luring Song summoning and controlling wind spirits via song, then utility spirit stopping and attacking.

                                                  Stealth - Using control of air to detect surroundings without light (Feeling The Air Technique), to make no sound, to use air to call and create minor distractions, and to summon breezes to obscure traces of movement (eliminating "Whose footprints are these?"), Wind-Walking Technique is literal walking on air and so being able to cross flimsy beams, mud, etc with no trace, Dragon Shroud Technique is "Bird of Prey" / Predator style blur invisibility through command of air essence.

                                                  Thrown - is mostly what you'd think. Plus using air control to turn aside weapons and make an weapons and armor from roiling winds.
                                                  (I kind of think Air ended up getting the best stuff that was truly elemental in feel, maybe in part because of how omnipresent and subtle Air can be. It was kind of a contrast to me how Air Linguistics or Stealth just feels like the Dragonblooded is commanding Air, while for contrast Wood Medicine didn't really even involve plants or wood at all, which seems like a missed opportunity, when you consider how much Medicine could draw on that. Likewise not much in Ride about using plant covered terrain, or nourishing your mount on elemental plant essence, etc.).

                                                  For Charms in the Dragonblooded set that are strong and useful, regardless of whether they're that in theme, I thought Testing the Waters (Water Bureaucracy) was pretty good - the Exalt just knows how a vote would fall, if a matter were put to a vote, for groups of seven or more. But it's not exactly something you can only imagine Dragonblooded knowing. Dragonblooded Investigation is all pretty useful really, although it all does ride on "How difficult would a roll for this be, anyway?" (in which case why buy the Charm?).
