                                        Is it unusual for me to write the significance of various Backgrounds and Merits/Flaws in my character sheets? Usually, I write something like this when building my Exalted characters:

                                        Artifact 3 (grand goremaul): While other Abyssals receive their weapons as gifts from their Deathlord patrons, the Custodian seized his from a fellow deathknight. The Dusk underestimated his intellect, and the First and Forsaken Lion now has an extra Exaltation to give out. The Custodian took the Dusk’s weapon, a soulsteel grand goremaul, as his own, and can wield it one-handed thanks to his mutations.
