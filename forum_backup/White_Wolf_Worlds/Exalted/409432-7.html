                                            I'll point out that in my example, the goal is not to kill Malfeas.

                                            I was merely using the targeting of a charm (which AoEs around the victim) with the one target who happens to be within range of everyone (by defining the entire realm). And this would have been irrelevant in the Primordial War, because Malfeas was not a prison.

                                            Essentially, less "my Solar can single-handedly slaughter all of Malfeas" and more "my Solar can be a massive dick and brutalize innocent demon species, while the Primordials just get annoyed".

                                            Also, Insignificant Embers Intuition says it "discerns the strength and aspect of the target's Essence relative to his ow it is "not a means of bypassing stealth" and "costs no motes" (for Essence 4+) but "still requires a Charm activation". And it's vastly inferior to it's predecessor, Theion's Burning Gaze of the Firmament, which adds (Essence) successes to the perception roll, which lets him focus his gaze into wrath (creating an instant Intimacy of righteous anger or hatred), and which then compliments many other Theion charms to punish those Intimacies.

                                            So it's much easier to curbstomp Malfeas - avoid being seen - even as his subjects burn in holy fire. Malfeas can probably just soak or take the aggravated damage without flinching - the idea is to torment the others.

                                            *

                                            And even if this gets broken, well, again - the goal wasn't to play a "Holy Solar killing demons".

                                            The example was "a complete asshole demonologist Solar" who "abuses Holy keywords to reign over demonkind".

                                            Hence my Infernals are often Batman (heroes born from tragedy) and my Solars are Superman in Injustice (a horrid god-king).
