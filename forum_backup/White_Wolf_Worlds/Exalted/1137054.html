    The Devil Tigers Lament

    09-03-2017, 06:01 PM
    In 2nd edition I fell in love with Infernals, I run a long-running Infernals game with all 50 Green Sun Princes so my players would be familiar with their peers rather than NPCs appearing out of nowhere with me insisting their characters know them by reputation. When Devil Tigers became a thing I embraced the mental exercise and thought about what their legends might look like.

    As time has gone on I have come to see the Devil Tiger as a false promise. The difficulties inherent in writing an interesting charmset coupled with the community having a high bar on who/what was worthy of titanhood lead us nowhere.

    Thus I present to you the Devil Tigers of mine who never were and shall never be.


    Speaker to the Mighty, The Priest Queen

    About the character:
    Speaker to the Mighty stemmed from my desire to portray the Green Sun Princes as having an almost Solar Deliberative like structure where they bicker and scheme and conspire to upstage one another but never attack directly because that would be rude.
    The Malefactor does not like to be reminded that she was once a slave having styled herself as a Priestess Queen of Hell. Decadence is second nature to Speaker, her once skeletal frame is now slightly pump due to a diet of only the finest food and drink as well as her insistence to travel by sedan chair. In Malfeas terms she is also extremely rich and well-connected among the Unquestionables and has a lot of access to infernal prayer strips to the point that she occasionally wears skirts comprised of dozens of prayer strips each documenting favours owed to her by various demons that contain a measure of their power.
    Speaker had previously objected to the Green Sun Princes forming permanent Covens viewing it as lionising their Solar Exalted heritage rather than embracing that they were something new.
    Once a true believer in the Reclamation she has started to find herself in agreement with old enemies and now suspects that the Ebon Dragon and maybe the Yozis as a whole have no long-term plan for their Chosen but for now she works within the system until her doubts are confirmed.

    Devil Tiger:
    As a Devil Tiger Speaker of the Mighty wears a theme of decadence like a fine coat, with ‘ride’ charms that enhance her ability to fight from the back of a sedan chair and the ability to readily delegate tasks to demonic peons.
    Her prayer strips really help her lazy fighting style, I imagine if she had to get out of Malfeas due to relations with the Yozis finally breaking down she would start making similar contracts with gods and elementals in exchange for portions of their power in a way that would not readily be confused with the Sidereals.
    Another origin story Imperfection for Speaker. She has cast out onto the scorching hot daytime streets of Gem for failing to convince her mistress that she had not been stealing from her. Her imperfection does not tie to that confrontation in particular but several instances leading up to it where her pride was wounded and she could do nothing. She has done everything she can to bury the slave girl she once was, even her name but it was her weakness that made her the woman she is today.
    Speaker of the Mighty is weak against anything that would remind her of her enslavement by constraining her physically or socially.

          I attempted the signature Coven too or more specifically the version of them I made before Scroll of Exalts. Still, I am curious to see other versions of them.

          Cearr, the Savage Barbarian

          About the character:
          This is my rendition of one of the Signature Coven that I have named The Shattered Thrones due to a vague common interest in trashing the Scarlet Empire from all directions.

          Cearr once ruled a coalition of eastern tribes through fear and intimidation until they were subjugated by the Realm. He lead a resistance movement against his oppressors but ultimately agreed to a situation where the Dragon-Blooded provided him with the illusion of control, he was free to lord over others and pillage as he pleased provided that certain quotas were met, namely slaves and they would attack any group that threatened the interests of the Realm.

          Unfortunately, one of these enemies was the Bull of the North and the military fiasco there cost Cearr both his army and his crown as the tribes under his control scattered to the four winds. He realised too late that he had been used as a pawn and it was then that a Blood Ape gave him an offer that was far more interesting. His first act as a Slayer was to liberate every slave and prisoner he could find, House Sesus recall a particularly infamous incident where a barbarian liberated a prison camp by constructing a ramp of the bodies of the guards and several prisoners.

          Shortly after this he met Sulumor and concluded she talked too much, then Manosque Cyan who talked too little, then Bitter Copal who talked too muffled and then he met Captain Gryfalcon who talked just the right amount, and understood muffled… perfect! As he grows in strength he has started to let Dragon-Blooded he could easily slay go. The common belief is that they offer him no further sport but the truth is that Cearr is not blind to the excesses of his fellow Infernals and is starting to doubt if he is on the right side.

          Devil Tiger:
          The charms Cearr uses reflect a black and white world view in which he is a savage hero fighting against a tyrant with impossible odds. It really makes no difference if it’s the Scarlet Empress, the Perfect of Paragon or Malfeas himself.

          The fact he prefers losing odds is important, he finds little satisfaction in crushing a weak opponent with overwhelming force and instead holds himself back, using only as much force as is necessary to fully test his abilities. Slavers however are regularly met with overwhelming force.

          Even in social combat he lets his strength and stamina do the talking, it is not unknown for him to attempt to impress new people by lifting them up above his head, flex his muscles to impress the ladies or show how much alcohol he can drink and remain conscious.

          If Cearr has one great weakness it would be his narrow world view. He styles himself as a larger than life culture her so his cause must always just and that of his enemies is always wrong if he has to consider that things might not be as they seem then he is vulnerable.

              Rolain Sikh, the man with many wives

              About the character:
              Rolain draws his origins from a discussion about Infernal Acts of Villainy and how they were not necessarily evil. A Fiend who uses Exquisite Bride Obsession not to play Bluebeard but to collect wives like other men would hats. Not out of a desire to own them, but rather as an expression of his own insecurities and need to know people love him.

              Some point along the way I did the cliche thing and envision him Desus’ successor because I liked the contrast of going from a villain with good publicity to a man you can make a lot of damning judgements about from the fact he is Infernal, a Fiend or his tendency to surround himself with a small harem (it has never been a factor in any game he is in but the notion of him attempting to court Lilith amuses me). Meeting him is going to be a bit like encountering an orientalist depiction of a sheik. I generally like to make my Fiends a little off-putting given that most of them will know Witness to Darkness and not be the best with Charisma.

              The character is something of a deconstruction of patriarchal power, despite being from Coral a male dominated society he has been shaped the most by the woman in his life. His dear mother who poisoned his father when he wanted to be rid of such a sickly looking infant, to his first wife who bled him dry and all the women who have come since. Even his current appearance owes a lot to the women in his life helping him to look the part of a prince.

              Devil Tiger:
              It could be easy to base his charmset around marriage or the role of women in his life but I attribute that more to the world view he gained from growing up in Coral and will become increasingly less important to him during the prolonged lifespan of an Exalt. Instead its going to focus on the acquisition of goods and admirers seemingly for its own sake.

              Though his serial polygamy is a manifestation of some deep seated issues he is not incompetent with it. His various wives and lovers provide him a spy network that would be the envy of many and I see him with social charms that revolve around having an entourage. The important thing is that he makes no pretense of owning his wives. So a charm to speak through them as though they were vassals or borrow their senses would be inappropriate though going the other way and being able to invest his power in them would be reasonable.

              Its here that we get to his Imperfection, If its still appropriate I like to base my Devil Tiger Imperfections upon the circumstances that lead to them becoming Green Sun Princes in the first place and Rolain is no exception. He fell in love with a con artist and lost the substantial wealth his late mother had left to him… there is something rather evocative about the notion that after all this time he has not really learnt his lesson from that and she could still screw him over again and that his own wives are actually the greatest threat to him.

                  The Crimson Student, the Secret Keeper

                  About the character:
                  The Crimson Student was my response to the popular notion that “giggle murder zoom” summed up the entire Scourge Caste. The Scourge PC in my game is a speedster first and foremost so I wanted to create an antithesis (really more of a sitcom arc nemesis/stalker) for her who remained true to the themes of Adorjan with the absolute minimal investment in super speed. I like the notion that Adorjan is wise despite her clear insanity and came up with a concept called “The Game” in which she tasks each of her Ten Scourges the task of tormenting another while they are in turn tormented by another Scourge.

                  Crimson Student has the dubious honour of being the only Scourge who knows this and Adorjan has stitched her mouth shut to ensure that she does not spoil the surprise. Even Student does not know what, if anything The Game is supposed to accomplish but the notion that the Silent Wind has something resembling a plan would be terrifying to the Unquestionables. In her previous life she used her cunning to capture creatures for the Guild in the Eastern Wyld, at least until her mind was broken by a beast whose laughter drove everyone who heard it to madness. She attempted to kill the demon that was sent to offer her exaltation but Adorjan saw potential in this broken mind.

                  Thus the Crimson Student is probably one of the few people ever whose mental state improved after becoming one of Adorjan’s chosen. She still lived for the hunt but now she could identify friend from foe. The Game intrigues her and while other Scourges acted on a whim she puts a truly elaborate amount of effort into her traps because how else would her beloved prey know that she cares?

                  Her theory is that The Game has a Darwinian purpose in which each of the Scourges will adapt or die when they fail to expect the unexpected.

                  Devil Tiger:
                  Crimson Student has a love of death traps worthy of the Joker or Wile E Coyote and never uses the same trick twice out of a matter of personal pride. Her combat tree should be a death-trap to your typical Scourge tactics with lots of tricks to blockade movement and generally turn their surroundings against them. She also lacks the aversion to ranged combat, the ability to bring unexpected misfortune is much more important than the personal touch.

                  There are elements of the Adorjani stalker tree here and her own “survival of the fittest” interpretation of the Silent Wind. Her traps are never unbeatable because they are intended to help those she loves become better. Ultimately however she is a bit of a hypocrite who has failed to see that she has not adapted herself and is still very much the mistress of traps she was as a mortal. Take her toys away and what is she?

                  How far she can move away from Adorjan ultimately depends on if she musters up the courage to pull those stitches out of her mouth.

                      Starry Wisdom, The Augur in Robes of Crystal

                      About the character:
                      Starry Wisdom is the first of many references to the writings of HP Lovecraft among my Infernal Exalted. I had previously thought about non-Sidereal astrologers who monitor the stars in the skies of Malfeas, the Calendar of Setesh as well as stars in the Wyld and other stranger places to gain a more complete picture of the future.

                      A Varangian astrologer priest obsessed with a rigid obsession with timing and order. It was a momentary lapse from her tight schedule at the request of her lover that saw control of Starry Wisdom’s life spiral away from her through one small event after culminating in her disgracing her caste by accidentally placing a child into the wrong caste.

                      For the first time in her life she faced an uncertain future when a servant of She Who Lives in Her Name told her that she would be able to reach for the stars and need never experience human imperfection again.

                      As an Infernal Starry Wisdom leads a Coven who call themselves the Inquisitors, a like-minded group of Green Sun Princes dedicated to the acquisition of knowledge through various sources with her specialty being the stars. Her obsession with fate and predestination has some demons troubled that she may attempt to contact the Sidereal Exalted directly or awaken Sacheverell.

                      The Inquisitors have become aware of the Ebon Dragons previous escape attempts and the fate of his pawns each time, they are now thinking of their own future even if it is one without the Yozis.

                      Devil Tiger:
                      While the Sidereal Exalted use their Astrology to manipulate events via the Loom of Fate, the Devil Tiger Starry Wisdom views the stars of every realm of existence available to her to become aware of all the possible vectors in a situation and acting upon it with clockwork precision.

                      Her charms work towards acquisition of knowledge and exploiting all available information to provide herself an advantage. She has leadership charms that work by precise timing, socially she is better at learning and exploiting intimacies than a direct attack through raw force of personality and finally in combat she works through lengthy aim actions where she studies the motion of her target and attacks when they are at their most vulnerable.

                      If she decides that its time to impose her own fate its probably going to take a very different form to that of the Sidereal Exalted, I envisioned the creation of 'omen stars' being created that radiate possibilities through the night sky such as making an area a haven for criminals that are drawn in like a moth to flame.

                      Starry Wisdom demands nothing less than perfection from herself errors and anomalous readings confound and infuriate her. These are not in itself her weakness but her inability to accept a situation beyond her control can lead her into making serious mistakes that are entirely her own.
