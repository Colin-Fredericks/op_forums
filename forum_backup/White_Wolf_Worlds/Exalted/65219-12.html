                            The Sidereal charmset was closed for security reasons - the human mind, even one so painstakingly prepared as that of a sidereal, cannot quite grasp all nuances of Samsara, resulting in charms prone to causality failure/cascading paradox. A few high level smart-ass Eclipses of the First Age, ignorant of this fact (to be fair, very few even among First Age sidereals knew the actual reason) tried to "make up their own custom sidereal charms" and paid the disastrous price for their hubris. If any of those "defective" sidereal charms was actually taught to a sidereal that still lives, no one is telling.The Maidens are the only ones capaple of (safely) making sidereal charms, what they have done from time to time for reasons their own.
                            • That said, they understand the power (and value) of human will & initiative and accept proposals in petition, the more signatures the better. Something apparently no one knew because a group of young sidereals in the current time of Tumult were the first to actually think of taking such an approach. This new revelation has generated something of a subtle but deep crisis in the fivescore fellowship as a number of younger chosen of all the factions have started to secretly - or not so secretly - question the wisdom of many their elders.

                            Amidst the mayhem of the Primordial War, a trickster Incarna whose name has been striken and forgotten, fashioned five companions out of the Maidens' reflections, with whom he sought to turn the conflict to its own benefit. He failed and met his doom along with them but two survive still, one remade as one of the chained titans souls, the other as the Maiden even she forgot she ended and replaced.
                            This false Maiden turned demon, the Red Star, lives in a damaged but functioning manse amidst the ruins of Meru and once joined forces with other entities restricted to the fallen city in a clique of sorts for a ploject most bold: to use their geomantic connection to the ruins of Meru and through it to the Omphalos itself, a peculiar failure in causality that happens in a number of centuries and an artifact soulforged from a soul of the pre-war Ebon Dragon to remake themselves into a wholenew primordial and its soul hierarchy, to rule Creation and remake it. The plot failed due to sidereal intervention, as she knew it would, but now a few siderealsknow of her and made accords in payment for her good behavior. One she touched and now ocasionally visits in his dreams, to (safely) teach things...
                            • Defeated they may have been, but yet unbowed the conspirators stand still: the ghost of a first age necromancer, discoverer of a dark mirror of solar exaltation before there were Deathlords, tells tales of his dark glory and trades necrotic secrets with those ready to listen. A mad dragon of 5 elements crafted by lost solar sorcery and geomantic devastation loses itself once more into cycling between them, uncaring. The unshaped amidst mad dreams and phantams seethes masterless and frustrated and shackled to its lunar manse. The goddess of the ruin of Meru abandons foolish alliances & ambitions and returns to her personal obsession with killing he who should have died when she was born. And Regil, glorious Regil that prides and yet broods at the glory of being a perfect duplicate of the Green Sun's majesty, for the first time in millenia has enemies and wardens, his own, not Ligier's, and smiles at the tought of what he will make of them - and himself - now that he has exalts to play with.

                                            Ligier is "ashamed" of the Brass Dancer in a very similar way to how some U.S. citizens are ashamed of how much taxpayer money goes to national defense - maintaining a jouten is expensive and energy-intensive, and while there's nothing technically wrong with having a jouten whose main purpose is to feel and spread joy, the Green Sun thinks that there are probably better uses that the resources supporting the Brass Dancer could be put to.

                                            All the third-circle souls of a primordial put together make up something like the conscious mind of that primordial. When all or at least most of them agree on a course of action, the primordial will probably do that thing. As a corollary, the third-circle souls are not above surrendering to the primordial equivalent of biological urges and doing things that are against their nature if the superbeing they are tied to demands it.

                                            Ligier specifically is something like a superego to Malfeas, and has something like a Protestant Work Ethic. He only allows himself leisure activities when he can justify them to himself as advancing some other goal (e.g. dancing for fun only is wasteful, but dancing to relieve stress and/or build relationships with potential allies is acceptable)

                                            Nothing in Creation or the worlds attached to it is infinite - anything described as "infinitely deep" or "infinitely wide" or "infinitely strong" is using hyperbole. There are, however, a lot of things that are indefinite - they can be as great or small as their narratives demand that they be.

                                            The mechanism of the Loom of Fate contains what might be called an Emergency Reset. At the end of time and/or when some apocalyptic event threatens to annihilate Creation entirely (whichever comes first), Saturn will activate it, and the state of Creation will return to what it was at instant it was made, before the Primordial War or any of the events afterwards that reshaped it.

                                            There is absolutely no relationship between Creation and the World of Darkness, except by coincidence.

                                            On the frontier of the Wild South, there's only one woman with the grit to take on its most dangerous outlaws and bring them Back Alive, or Maybe Dead.

                                                EDIT: I hadn't read the whole thread but how did my post NOT appear at the end of the thread? I don't understaaaannnnddd!!!

                                                My own ideas:

                                                * The Incarnae are actually mostly decent people, although not particularly human-like. That does not mean they will or even can save Creation for you.

                                                * Sol's original shape was a dragon, not a humanoid or even Dragon King.

                                                * The Five Elemental Dragons are among Gaia's 3rd Circle souls, but the other elementals aren't.

                                                * Lintha were always a non-Human species, but both they and Humans were separately created based on the vivisection of the First Jadeborn. The Humans were finished second because it took a while to perfect their utter wretcheness while making them actually intelligent (early, rejected attempts produced great apes). This is why Lintha and Humans have such similar soul structures and can interbreed.

                                                * A Sidereal who doesn't work with Yu-Shan is a rogue. An Abyssal who betrays and leaves the Deathlords is a ronin. A Green Sun Prince who uses heresy or betrays and leaves the Yozis is a heretic.

                                                * The original 13 Deathlords are extremely powerful and difficult to destroy, but they are not indestructible or invulnerable. They don’t know every Solar and Abyssal charm. They can only teach, not use, Sorcery and Sidereal Martial Arts they knew in life. They aren’t already on the verge of destroying Creation yet.

                                                * The Neverborn didn't get their hands on exactly even numbers of Solar exaltations of each caste. They gave exactly 10 of each caste to the Yozis, leaving the Abyssals with somewhat unequal numbers of their castes.

                                                * The Ebon Dragon's fetich soul and some 3rd circle souls of Malfeas, Cecelyne, the Principle of Hierarchy, and (just for lulz) Adorjan started the Reclamation and Green Sun Princes, and provide castes. 3rd circle demons of Hegra, Isidoros, Kimbery, Oramus, and Szoreny later joined. Each 3rd circle demon has opened its Yozi's charms and added Urges to the collective pool. Urges are assigned randomly, and don't depend on caste. Ebon Dragon and Szoreny each intend to later betray the Reclamation and free only themselves. (The Reclamation can't actually free any Yozi, but the Ebon Dragon’s plan may work for him.)



                                                Adopted from other people on other threads:

                                                Originally posted by Revlid
                                                I basically ignore the idea that gods are forged into starmetal, and (though I wasn't aware of this when I came up with it, having entered the game in 2E) take it back to its origins.

                                                Every star in Creation's night sky is representative of a convergence of destiny-strands on the Loom of Fate; it is essentially a giant display screen for the Loom, which the Maidens used before the Primordial War, when they were still barred from Yu-Shan. This is why astronomers can use thaumaturgy to glean insights into Fate's workings from it.

                                                When a set of stars is no longer necessary (i.e. that destiny has ended) the pattern spiders send a notification and the star is question is shut off and removed from the sky by the argentim, dropped down to Creation. Their delicate luminous mechanisms burn up and fuse together in the atmosphere, consuming the dangling strands that once held them to the sky. This reaction produces a hunk of raw starmetal, which can then be refined into a usable magical material through the thaumaturgical process detailed in Oadenol's Codex.
                                                * Green Sun Prince caste marks and animas are different. Each has a color and symbol more appropriate to the Caste Yozi. Slayers green with sunburst, Malefactors silver with azure hourglass, Defilers white with a flame, Scourges crimson with a vague swirly thing that never stops moving, Fiends shadowy black with an ouroboros-shaped hole in the forehead.

                                                *Other 3rd circle demons have unrelated escape plans and other agendas:
                                                • Disassociation: Sever all ties and interaction with Creation, end all summons, making it possible to unfold infinitely "into" Elsewhere. Led by Qaf and souls of Oramus and Adorjan. Souls of Cytherea are involved, but her overall plan is actually to inextricably entwine all Yozis together via soul-hierarchies and geomancy, make Creation and Hell into exact metaphysical opposites in all Essence, then mass-summon demons to collide and annihilate everything, birthing all-new Primordials and cosmos. (Both of these plans would work, more or less.)
                                                • Elloge is processing and examining the Surrender Oaths inside herself to look for loopholes. Souls of Oramus might be assisting. (This won't work.)
                                                • At least one of Sacheverell's souls believes he will reveal the way to freedom and must be awakened. Lucien monitors them, but Cecelyne forbade their death because the effect on Sacheverell's soul hierarchy can't be predicted.


                                                She/Her. I am literal-minded and write literally. If I don't say something explicitly, please never assume I implied it. The only exception is if I try to make a joke.
                                                My point of view may be different from yours but is equally valid.
