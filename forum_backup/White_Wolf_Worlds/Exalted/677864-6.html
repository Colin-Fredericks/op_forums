    Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post
    Part of my ideas for how Destiny works do have the idea that during the First Age there were various factors, such as the management of the world and its ontological issues by the Exalted, the relative restraint of the Celestial gods, operating budgets that were a lot higher (you had billions of people and a lot of holidays sending constant streams of Essence and cash in huge quantities to Heaven) and the Sidereals being aided by all kinds of wondrous tools they could get the Realm to produce for them, it was a lot easier both to make plans for unfolding history that could be adhered to a lot more smoothly, and kind of make the world... nicer. People would still have to put some of their own work in, but the world could be a bit less arbitrary about some things, so people who were really ideal for each other didn't need to have their happiness prevented by little things like being on opposite sides of the world from one another.

    When I say tools made by Solars, I'm thinking of things like thinking automatons optimised to helping manage the unintuitive workload of destiny management, engines capable of modelling all kinds of life intersections and alternate futures, and even things like writing kits made to allow Sidereals to write out all of the necessary orders and directives to precisely manage enormous numbers of destinies per day in a manner that transcended the physical limits of how much paperwork a human shaped thing can do in that time. Not things that replaced the powers of Sidereals, but complemented them; the kinds of tools that would be useful to somebody with World-Shaping Artistic Vision in a similar way to how a daiklave is useful to somebody with Hungry Tiger Technique.

    I don't see Chejop Kejak as having been a very sentimental man even in the First Age (I've got this image in which Chejop has incidentally had numerous children over the centuries, and isn't very interested in any of them beyond doing a bit to give them destinies to insulate them from some arbitrary disasters; I don't see this as necessarily being a flaw), but I can imagine some old Sidereals who occasionally look at the work they have to do now (not in terms of "we must make Hard Choices for the Greater Good" so much as it just being a bit... grubbier), and look back with nostalgia on the times when being an agent of Fate meant you did a more meaningful equivalent of ensuring that it never rained unless everybody had an umbrella.
    I've had something relating to this thought as well...with some extra stuff rolled in to boot.

    We know that the Solars and Lunars were awesome at securing the borders of the Wyld. Massive Terraforming engines rolled out to make new Creation for people to live in, and the combined operation of those, the Order-conferring Trade Pattern, the large populations, and the Order-generating Obelisks meant that Wyld Incursions were few and far between. High-end Solar Sorcerers can stroll in and slap the undo button on a Shadowland, and the Deathlords didn't exist yet. These things combined mean that there was a lot less Outside-Fate intervention to deal with. This means that when Fate was laid out, it tended to not get screwed up by something they couldn't see coming.

    In addition, the Sidereals operated more openly...no Arcane Fate. They cooperated with the rest of the Exalted to seek the best of the world. As it is now, if there is a demon-summoning cult that the Siddies see messing with their plans, they either have to manipulate up a Wyld Hunt, or they have to deal with it themselves. In the First Age, they could send a note to the office of whichever Solar ruled over those lands, informing him that he had a Yozi-cult infestation, and information on where to find it. The Solar himself may not even see the memo, but it would move through the bureaucracy (that was actually efficient, because Solars were running it) and make it back to the Dragonblooded manager of that particular territory, and he'd send troops out to deal with it.

    Really...in the First Age, the Sidereals largely operated as advisers and planners. If the Solars had an idea that they wanted to Fate-check, they called up a few Sidereals and had them cast prophecies to see how it would go. If a Lunar was hunting a Wyld Incursion, they could just Infallible Messenger a Siddie and say 'Hey, could you look for Fate disruptions in this general area? I'm trying to locate where they are coming out of the Wyld.' Yeah, they'd still go do field work...some stuff needs the touch of a Chosen of Fate...but they were much less overworked.
