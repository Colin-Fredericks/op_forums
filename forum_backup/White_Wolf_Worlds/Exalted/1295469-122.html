        Hi all! I know y'all want answers to a lot of questions in here, but as Eric and Vance are both incredibly busy developing books, they may not have time to answer involved posts about specific power interactions. They/we aren't ignoring you, but in some cases where a post would require a several-hundred-word answer, it may not be something we can get to in a timely manner/at all. We're making so many books at once right now (12 at last count!), and all of them require some amount of Eric and Vance's oversight.

        Sorry if we can't get to a question, but we're working hard to make the best books we can for everyone! If you can, try to keep queries to a few sentences, and Vance or Eric will answer them when they get a chance. Thank you for understanding.
