    Solar Survival at Essence 4&5?

    12-23-2015, 06:36 PM
    So, the Solar Survival Charms in the Core only go up to Essence 3. Note that I am not complaining about this. Deadly Predator Method scares the crap out of me and (especially once you can apply such things to an Elemental/Demonic Familiar) and there are plenty of charms to grab before you can get it.

    What I AM wondering about is... Well, where do you go from here? Deadly Predator Method is pretty crazy and, while I didn't read the charms in detail, it looks like all the bases are covered. It doesn't seem like we necessarily even need higher essence charms here. I suppose that granting your familiar Legendary Size might be E4, but I dunno.

    What do you guys think? Am I way off base? Is there some stuff you want that is very obviously E4 or E5? Vance, do you have any thoughts about where you'd like to go?

      Essence 3 is, as you noticed, where a lot of the realultimatepower of Solar Charms lives.

      Once you reach Essence 4, the Charms you find tend more towards a couple of things:
      • Raw power tends to be channeled towards into a specific condition, circumstance, or restriction, as opposed to the more broadly applicable power you find in Essence 3 Charms., e.g. Flashing Nocturne Prana, Heavens Crash Down, Inviolable Essence-Merging, Subject-Hailing Ideology
      • Providing upgrades or extensions the Charms you've been using so far, e.g. Bonfire Anima Wings, Dust and Ash Sleight, Seal of Infinite Wisdom.

      Essence 5 opens the gates to new worlds of possibility through powerful apex Charms that define and reinforce archetypes that exist in the Ability. They tend to provide the finishing touches for some character concepts fresh out of chargen through Supernal Abilities. Nine Aeons Thew, for instance, increases the scale of what you can achieve with feats of strength to bounds of conceptual possibility, defining the mythic strength of Herakles; Living Wind Approach lets Hermes/Atlanta/Hiawatha outspeed Mercury (uh, the Maiden) in a foot race.

      (usual caveats: not a dev, didn't write the Solar Charms)

                  Originally posted by BrilliantRain View Post
                  Hmmm... If we're going for mythic resonance at E5, then would that perhaps turn a familiar into a Legendary Animal? Legend Beast Saga and Deadly Predator Method are both very awesome, true, but neither is always on. Would E5 be for things like "My Housecat is now the Nemean Lion" at all times?
                  The best answer I could come up with means it depends on how you define legendary." Taking a powerful Charm with a limited duration, and give it an Essence 5 upgrade that makes it essentially constant is enough to catch my mental red flags for "I should test this a bunch before I send it to the devs."

                  A useful perspective is that of a player at character creation. If you've already sunk the majority of your initial Charm choices into picking up Deadly Predator Method, being able to use your last pick or a couple of BP to become just plain better than using Deadly Predator Method on its own tends to warp progression towards conformity (i.e. one true builds), rather than rewarding specific choices that player makes. This isn't necessarily broken (and homebrew is so much more forgiving of broken stuff), but that's something that would push me towards Essence 3 when I decide the Charm's minimum.

                  tl;dr: Essence minimums are a much easier place to finish your Charms than to begin them.
