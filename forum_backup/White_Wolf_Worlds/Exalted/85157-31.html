            Spoilers for the last couple of pages:

            Note, this is specifically Juggernaut's point about being part of a cycle.

            In the beginning, YISUN/the gods made Heaven, grew weary, and died, and their dominion fell into corruption.

            Then, a king came, destroyed the old regime, and received instruction from Metatron.

            Then Zoss' dominion grew corrupt and he abandoned it, to eventually seek a successor. Metatron wants that successor to destroy the old regime, and then be brought before him for instruction. The Thorn Knights partially exist to fulfill this purpose, including killing Zoss to make his successor more pliant (Juggernaut jumped the gun by trying to kill Zoss early and bringing his successor directly to Metatron, and views the failure as being inevitable, and delaying the fulfillment of the cycle).

            Comments about cycles being clean and infertile suggest to me that this is Metatron's attempt to curtail the power of Will, making the Wheel repetitive and controlled, predictable. Royalism for the sake of preventing change.

            Juggernaut also seems to believe that Zaid is the successor, and that Allison having the Key is something Zoss did as a result of being interrupted. That Zoss appeared to her might suggest that they are mistaken in this.

            I have approximate knowledge of many things.
