                    TLD + TCW (Since it is Presence based no EIT, and not intensifying, but creating)

                    +Minor Tie (Admiration Black Claw Stylist)

                                Had a great deal of fun with a Black Claw MA dancer. Start your dancing with Respect-Commanding Attitude before attacking and with Black Claw you become basically unhittable. Sure, you have to flurry a dance action every round, but you use it for instilling and compelling of course.

                                Between Respect Commanding Attitude, Torn Lotus Defense, Thousand Courtesan Ways, Doe-eyed Defense and Poised Lion Attitude you are set. Your opponents will burn through their willpower at a ridiculous rate and before long they are your loyal and loving minions. Mix in Charms like Wise-Eyed Courtier and Energic Influence Technique to truly disgust your ST. I would look there before going deep in stealth, which imho, is not what Black Claw does best.

                                In fact, you should almost never have to resort to damaging decisives and killing opponents. Why would you when you can turn opponents into 'ally' or 'contact' dots?

                                    Originally posted by TheCountAlucard View Post
                                    So aside from having Mastery*, which Sidereals can also access, what is it that Solars, Abyssals, and Green Sun Princes have in common? Not sorcery (as Abyssals don't have access to the Adamant Circle), not Evocations (as Abyssals are only Resonant with soulsteel), and not Charm design (with Abyssals and Green Sun Princes branching off into Deathlord/shintai directions respectively). It sure isn't in how they're Chosen. Sure, you could say they're all monstrously powerful, but the same is true of Exalted in general.

                                    Simply put, "Solaroid" is not a good term because the thing it's trying to convey - that you can just lump them all together under the same umbrella - is not a good idea.
                                    Even in 3e, Solars are PRETTY CLEARLY intended to have, in general, the strongest charm effects, with Abyssals and Infernals [both of which draw on a stolen Solar exaltation, still in 3e] being comparably 'superior'. It's no longer the "Solar is captain america next to Lunar who is an ordinary dude with four broken limbs and DB who is literally in a frickin' iron lung" of 2e but to imply that the Solar 'standing above' in most raw charm power is nonexistent is disingenuous. Mastery and Resonance primarily exist to grant Solars more reason to use MA and artifacts [with the flavor benefit of Sidereals being as good as Solars at MA and non-Exalts being able to draw as much from their magical material as Solars, generally speaking, being secondary benefits creating incentives]; this incentive wouldn't be necessary if MA and Evocations were without those keywords comparable to Solar power [but if they were, then there would need to be disincentives to prevent MA/Evoc being OTB for everyone not-Solar].
