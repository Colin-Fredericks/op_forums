            Originally posted by Childofthesun1 View Post
            So how does Elsewhere connect to Creation? Is it directional, like go far enough up and you'll end up in Elsewhere? There's a general sense that Underworld=down, Wyld=around, and Yu-Shan and Malfeas are ???
            For me, Elsewhere is all around Creation. The Underworld isn't in my mind downwards, but overlapping (as a parallel dimension of sort).

            But unlike the Underworld, I don't see Elsewhere overlapping. The lack of distance in Elsewhere is what makes it possible to reach something put there from wherever, and why Elsewhere can fit something as large as Malfeas without problem. If you're familiar with low level programming, the access to something in Elsewhere has more in common with memory pointers than a three (or two) dimensional coordination system. In that comparison, Elsewhere would be an enormous (or infinite) memory bank.

            This is why it is shorter to use two Yu-shan gates, than go the same distance through Creation. Both Creation and Yu-shan follows the logic of a Cartesian coordination system, but Elsewhere do not. For example, I see no reason for the placements of the gates in Yu-shan, other than it feels more "logical" to have a gate in the eastern part of Yu-shan connected to an eastern part of Creation. They could as well have been placed at random (which is also why getting in and out of Malfeas tend to be a bit ... wonky).

                Justice is not a physical variable you can interact with. It's an idea or emotion. Location is a very tangible thing I interact with constantly.                 If it makes sense is beside the point. It is a fact of the setting (at least in 2E) that things with such properties as make no sense are real.
