                                    Confirming something I hadn't yet realized:

                                    Artifact crafters don't actually need more than three dots each of Lore and Occult, anymore. Not for anything less than N/A-rated Artifacts or First Age Artifice Artifacts.

                                    Your crafting roll is limited to the lesser of your applicable mundane Craft rating and your Artifact Crafting dots, and Lore and Occult no longer factor in at all.

                                    So, most Artifact crafters don't face nearly as much pressure to favor Lore and Occult as they did in previous Editions.

                                    formerly Tornado Wolf, formerly Inugami
