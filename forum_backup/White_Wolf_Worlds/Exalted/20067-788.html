        Originally posted by Lanic View Post

        Death Ray ussually also creates a lava hazard. It's not strictly implied by description, but by analogy with Flight of the Brlliant Raptor, I'd personally make a ruling that whenever it makes some sense, you automatically have to avoid attack and lava each round.

        Another thing is that the spell bypasses most of the combat system in a unique way.

        If you have decent Join Battle or some other ways to gather Initiative, then you have access to the attack that:
        - does not reset your Initiative, so it allows you to keep repeting a decisive attack each turn;
        - uses the result of your original roll (!) If you rolled 15 successes (quite possible with dice tricks), you keep it and keep attacking, knowing that each round you simply will hit. If you don't - you just repeat the spell.

        If in the melee tree you could find a charm that lets you make decisive each turn without resetting your Initiative and keeping the result of the last roll, it would be dramatically overpowered.

        I agree that the original developers made clear mistakes when gauging power levels of various things, but I think that Death Ray is not really flashy, it's just deadly efficient if you are able to gather decet initiative in the first place.

        What are some good dice tricks to use with Death Ray?

                                                        Yeah totally, it's not about the epic high essence melee charms, a Solar with supernal Melee could get those from essence 1. It comes down to 3 basic things.

                                                        1. More motes of essence, an essence 5 Solar has 86 motes before any charms that regenerate motes or expand his motepool or what have you. The Solar could probably take down Fakharu and the Nephwrack both in the first round of combat before they even act and still have more than half his motes left.

                                                        2. Bigger (Essence) calculations. From melee alone there's lots of charms that use (Essence) in their calculations, and they'll all be amazing.

                                                        3. It's not just the melee charms, it's combining Essence 5 Melee with Resistance, Occult, Dodge, and Awareness. The Occult is totally unnecessary, but considering all four of them are spirits it just makes it so much easier. Stuff like Ghost Eating Technique and Breath Drinker Method enhancing Invincible Fury of the Dawn.


                                                        It is hard in that you need a somewhat specific set of charms, but it isn't actually that hard if you do have them.
