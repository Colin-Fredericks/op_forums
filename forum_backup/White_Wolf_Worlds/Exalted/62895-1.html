                                            Well, I'm not really interested, but have some custom charms from the Abyssal Game I'm running!

                                            Peace of the Grave
                                            Permanent Charm, Resistance 3, Essence 2
                                            Prerequisite: Cadaverous Torpor Technique
                                            This charm enhances it's prerequisite, multiplying healing rates while in torpor by (essence). When the user has also been given a proper burial, the factor is instead (Essence + resistance), and it accelerates the healing rate of aggravated damage as well. The DM is encouraged to be liberal with the definition of 'proper burial' to facilitate stunts.

                                            This charm counts as Wound-Absorbing Discipline for the purposes of qualifying for other charms, as it is a variant charm.

                                            (One time, the character with this charm was sent blasting off by the guy he accidentally set up as the next Scarlet Emperor, put an obol in under his tongue and prepared to punch the ground as he landed so that he was buried, with a headstone from a boulder he broke with his ranged punch charm. Seeing as this guy was a Solar in disguise, he had a lot of agg damage to heal.)

                                            Body-Mutilating Bluff
                                            2m Simple, Larceny 3 Essence 2
                                            Indefinite, Keywords Combo-OK, Crippling
                                            This charm allows the Abyssal to impose any crippling injury on himself deliberately. He actually has the injury, but can reflexively end the charm. A Manipulation+Larceny roll vs. a Wits+Awareness roll determines how off-guard people would be from the sudden unmaimed status of the Abyssal. The Abyssal must be able to survive the Crippling effect, so nothing that hurts the neck, brain, or heart. However, while the Injury may be at any stage of healing cosmetically, it is always treated as sufficiently healed to bestow no complications beyond the obvious. This charm may bestow scars, amputations, missing non-vital organs, and all sorts of other survivable nastiness. This is capable of reducing his Physical Attributes or Appearance if desired.

                                            As a side-note, while this charm usually banishes any missing organ or limb Elsewhere, the Abyssal can forgo this benefit and merely detach them. This is harder to reverse, and the organ or limb must be place back in his body/held to the stump to release the charm without complication. Otherwise, dismissal of the charm imposes the amputation effect for real.

                                            Mockery of Insanity Defense
                                            5m Reflexive(2), Integrity 5 Essence 3
                                            Indefinite, Keywords Combo-OK, Illusion
                                            Prerequisete: Body-Mutilating Bluff
                                            This charm allows the Abyssal to impose "crippling" effects on his own brain and psyche, imposing upon himself a derangement, illusion, or compulsion, lower his Virtues, mental attributes, manipulation or charisma, or even his Abilities, specialties, and it is even capable of making him forget his non-permanent charms. This charm can be used as a Perfect Dodge against mental attacks by changing the Abyssal's perspective enough to void the influence. This charm is also capable of making the Abyssal believe anything, acting as a roundabout lying charm by making him believe whatever he was lying about is the unmitigated truth. The Abyssal's player has full control over the activation and release of this charm, although the Abyssal himself doesn't necessarily have such control, as would usually be the case when giving himself a delusion to lie about something(awful hard to believe something's the truth when you remember that you made yourself believe that, after all).

                                            Simulacrum Attendance Preperation
                                            5m, Simple, Larceny 4, Essence 3
                                            Indefinite, Keywords Combo-OK, Illusion
                                            Prerequisite: Face-Drinking Bite
                                            This useful charm allows the Abyssal to keep a long-term disguise while still being able to skip off to meet with his leige or kill babies or whatever gets his rocks off. The Absysal takes a corpse, fashions it with some basic props, and animates it with his essence. The Simulacrum then proceeds to fully act out whatever role the Abyssal desires of it, using the Abyssal's attributes and abilities when needed, although it is an extra. The Simulacrum cannot break character, but the Abyssal can seamlessly switch places with the body if it is within line of sight with a miscellaneous action. The Simulacrum is then disguised as the Abyssal or whatever the disguise the Abyssal was wearing at the time. The Disguise may be enhanced by committing additional motes to Face-Drinking Bite and expending Willpower to False Heart Mein, only the Simulacrum receives the benefits.

                                            As a point of clarification, Abyssal Disguise charms are activated per disguise, no matter if the Abyssal or a simulacrum is disguised as it. If the Abyssal had used Face-Drinking Bite and then prepared a Simulacrum of that disguise to attend a secret meeting, the Simulacrum would have the benefits of Face-drinking Bite, and the Abyssal would have to re-activate the charms for his new disguise to attend the meeting in if necessary. As long as the motes remain committed, the Abyssal could re-enter the original disguise and not need to re-expend the motes and willpower.
