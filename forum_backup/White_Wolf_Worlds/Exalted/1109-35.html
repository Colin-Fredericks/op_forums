                                    The main reason to put Charm-based barriers on the forms a Lunar can assume, is to help mitigate how frontloaded Lunars are. The Lunar Charmset having to be balanced against how much power and flexibility Lunars get for no XP investment means that even an ideal Lunar charmset has to look somewhat lackluster relative to other Celestials who don't get such an advantage. There are multiple ways to handle this, for instance sticking an XP cost on properly internalizing a shape gained by the hunt to make it usable, but a less restrictive method is just to gate off certain categories of form behind a Charm purchase. This hinders shapeshifting, but in return means that everything else about Lunars can be made stronger than would be allowable if unregulated shapeshifting was allowed at chargen.

He was the strongest man in the camp, possibly the strongest mortal in that part of the south, and when he used his great strength and fortitude to stand up for others, he received the blessing of Luna. The Lunar Exaltation didn't transform him into someone totally different, it empowered him because of who he already was.
