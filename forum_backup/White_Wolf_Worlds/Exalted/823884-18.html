                For evaluation and critique:

                Imperial Mandate of Flames

                This is one of Ysyr's great treasures, captured during a war with Prasad some centuries ago. Forged in the depths of the South by a Solar during the early First Age, of pure red jade and had an illustriuous history, seeing much battle against the invaders of Creation and a less illustrious history in squables between the Dragon-Blooded. Eventually, this moved a Sidereal to remove it from its current owner after he used it in a most dishonourable fashion. Then, it gathered dust for a while, until it was given to the Dragon-Blooded during the Usurpation, during which it was shattered and the shards captured by a Solar craftsman, who reforged it for his only remaining circle mate, piecing it back together and binding it in Orichalcum. More powerful than before, the armour and the Dawn within it were said to strike the Dragon-Blooded with the force of a falling star, laying waste to several armies before the Solar finally fell to the strange arts of the Sidereals, who once more captured the armour. However, the Solar's ghost began to pursue them across Creation, and eventually even struck the gates of Heaven. Rather than placate the furious ghost, a Sidereal reworked the armour with Starmetal characters, allowing the power of the armour to affect the ghost. Then it was given to a Dragon-Blooded champion who battled and defeated the ghost. This Dragon-Blooded Exalt then travelled to the Southeast, where Prasad would one day be, and settled there. And the rest, as they say, is history.

                Heavy Artifact Armour of Red Jade, Orichalcum and Starmetal with a single Hearthstone socket on the inside. A Solar, Sidereal or Dragon-Blood who attunes to the Artifact doubles his soak against flame based attacks, gains an additional 3 hardness against them and doubles 8s against environmental hazards based on fire or heat.

                Blessed Flames: Radiance
                Cost: -(1m); Mins: Ess 1
                Type: Permanent
                Keywords: None
                Duration: Permanent
                Prerequisites: None
                When casting a fire-based spell, for every sorcerous mote by which the caster exceeds the amount required for the spell, a small, sourceless flame flares into existence around the attuned. These flames provide a source of light and may be directed at a target with a wits + occult roll, by paying 1m. They have the traits of a light artifact weapon with the Thrown(Short), Mounted, Lethal and Flame tags.

                Blessed Flames: Demonlight
                Cost: 1m ; Mins: Ess 2
                Type: Simple
                Keywords: None
                Duration: Instant
                Prerequisites: Blessed Flames: Radiance
                Gathering 5 flames gained through the previous Evocation and binding them together with his own essence, the attuned may create a larger, pale blue flame. This flame renders all immaterial creatures within short range visible for one instant before forcing its way down the throat of the attuned.

                Blessed Flames: Starfire
                Cost: 5m, 2wp ; Mins: Ess 3
                Type: Simple
                Keywords: Aggravated, Decisive-Only
                Duration: Instant
                Prerequisites: Blessed Flames: Demonlight
                Having swallowed the demonlight, the Attuned may over the course of one turn gather all of the remaining flames within himself, before releasing them with a horrific scream, unleashing a beam of red-silver energy that vaporizes metal and stone and burns flesh. The beam is aimed with Wits + Occult, and may not strike any target beyond medium range. This attack is very exhausting and as such may be used only once per scene, and inflicts a -1 wound penalty which may only be healed by one night of rest and is cumulative with other wound penalties.

                Blessed Flames: Emperor Blaze
                Cost: 5m ; Mins: Ess 4
                Type: Reflexive
                Keywords: Psyche
                Duration: Scene
                Prerequisite: Blessed Flames: Starfire
                Having unleashed the blast of starfire, the Attuned is vulnerable. Armour, however, is meant for protection and draws on the bearer's remaining essence automatically, phasing it through the armour and creating a blaze of multi-hued, beautiful flames around the bearer. Any fire based attacks or hazards are absorbed by the flames, and any attacks from close range suffer a -3 penalty from the bright light. Furthermore, anyone within close range of the bearer suffers the effects of a Bonefire Hazard. The wounds and penalty are all an illusion, however, albeit a very realistic one and may be resisted by paying 1wp every turn one wishes to ignore the flames. After the first activation, the attuned may use this without using the prerequisite
                Special Activation Rules: This Evocation awakens for free and activates automatically after the use of the prerequisite once the attuned meets the minimums.

                Blessed Flames: Pyroclastic Judgement
                Cost: 1m ; Mins: Ess 5
                Type: Simple
                Keywords: Decisive-Only
                Duration: Scene
                Prerequisites: Blessed Flames: Emperor Blaze
                This evocation requires the attuned to use Blessed Flames: Starfire and then build up 10 more flames, turn these into Demonlights and swallow both of them, all while surrounded by the Emperor Blaze. He may then activate this final evocation. A pillar of flame surrounds him, and carries him to medium range above the ground into the eye of a tornado of flame. From there he may unleash large blasts of fire which strike everything within short range of their point of impact. The attack may not be dodged though the centre of the flames is solid enough to be deflected or even returned with a successful dex + melee/brawl/MA against the bearers attack roll of wits+occult. The bearer may further use Blessed Flames: Radiance as if he had at least one flame.

                        Hey guys, this is the first artifact I've come up with and its for black orchids post (Im the player mentioned in it) from a few pages ago.

                        The weapon itself, in its base form, is a separating chakram that the wielder forges a primal bond with an unlocks some innate connection to some beast spirits kept locked away in it.

                        I present to you:

                        Primal Fangs Wheel (Artifact 4; Moonsilver Infinite Chakram)
                        Viewing her future husband as unwilling to do the things he must when the time arises due to his morals, Grim Ebony set out to forge a weapon to assist and influence him when the need comes to do as he must. The pack does as it must, but her wolf was hesitant.

                        She hunted down ten thousand beasts, and took a claw or fang, barb or spine, to use in the forging of the weapon. Each animal's natural arsenal was used as the core of the blades and she used the carcasses to heat her forge. Each blade was quenched in the blood of prey animals to enhance the predators spirit of the weapon. After the sickles were quenched, they were put onto a band of orichalcum and blessed by gods of predator animals and the gods of hunts.
                        The weapon proved a valuable gift for her bondmate, who carried it with him for centuries. After he was murdered in the Usurpation she tried to use the weapon in retaliation but to no avail and the weapon has been used as a beautiful gift of the Dynasts ever since; be it for weddings or forging alliances, this weapon promotes unity. Its last owner was a Sesus patriarch who wished to form such an alliance.
                        Primal Fangs Wheel’s surface is a shimmer of silver that only becomes more magnificent to view when the weapon is being used at night or during the use of Evocations. Stalking is about 2 foot in diameter with a deceptively thin but wide blade, made up of hundreds of interlocking sickle shaped moonsilver blades with delicate starmetal filigree. Four much larger “claws” sit at equal spacing, all interwoven on a thin band of orichalcum etched with moonsilver and starmetal decoration. Due to the weapons design and function, it is impossible for Grim Ebony to have been able to produce it with a hearthstone socket.
                        The spirit of the weapon is unruly, like a pack of undisciplined hounds, and takes a harsh master to keep in line; to this date no Dragonblooded has kept the spirit of Primal Fang Wheel in check and it yearns for its true master.

                        Evocations of Primal Fangs Wheel
                        A Solar or Lunar who attunes to Primal Fangs Wheel gains an uncanny insight into the rush of combat, the weapon ushering its wielder into the hunt, gaining an additional +1 on Join Battle, stealth and awareness rolls to spot danger.

                        Savage Wolves Assail
                        Cost: 4m Mins: Essence 1
                        Type: Supplemental
                        Keywords: Uniform
                        Duration: Instant
                        Prerequisites: None

                        The master commands and the pack obeys. When this Evocation is used four distinct grouping of sickle blades gather around the main claws and sail through the air on a path towards the wielders target on a trail of silvery white essence.

                        The roll gains (Perception) double 9’s. If used against a battlegroup, the battlegroup’s Size is added to the number of doubles rolled and the roll gains double 8’s as well.

                        Diving Raptor’s Descent
                        Cost: 5m Mins: Essence 1
                        Type: Supplemental
                        Keywords: Dual
                        Duration: Instant
                        Prerequisites: None

                        As the wielder unleashes the spirits of the hunting raptors, trails of white essence follows as the blades gather into flocks, taking flight to assail the victim with talons and beak leaving a wake of blood and terror.

                        The Shrieking Wheel splits into numerous smaller sections and fans out to attack an enemy or battlegroup within range. If used as withering the attack converts (Perception) dice of post soak into automatic successes and if used as decisive it converts the lesser of (Perception or Essence) dice of raw damage into automatic successes. If used against a battle group, the withering attack removes bonuses to soak from size and the decisive doubles raw damage for calculating extra damage levels.

                        Defiant Matriarch Stance
                        Cost: 6m, 2i, 1wp Mins: Essence 2
                        Type: Simple
                        Keywords: Perilous
                        Duration: One Scene
                        Prerequisites: Savage Wolves Assail, Diving Raptor’s Descent

                        As the two begin to feel the ebb and flow of each other, so to does the bond of faithful beast grow stronger towards its master. With a staunch stance, the wielder takes the weapon in both hands and flares of silver white essence explode out on the trail of the blades as they begin to orbit the wielder, like a lioness protecting her cubs.

                        Using this Evocation gives the wielder full cover from the orbiting blades while removing the advantage any assailants get from engaging the wielder in melee. Against a battlegroup they do not gain any benefits of might or superior damage due to magnitude as the individual blades parry their attacks away from hitting the wielder.

                        Pack Retaliation Technique
                        Cost: 4m, 1i Mins: Essence 3
                        Type: Reflexive
                        Keywords: Decisive-only, Counterattack
                        Duration: Instant
                        Prerequisites: Mother Wolf Approach

                        The pack retaliates, attacking with fury and grim determination against those who to seek to injure or kill their master, throwing themselves into the fray. The blades follow the attack to slay the aggressor pulsing with silver-gold light, to punish him for such an act.
                        Pack Retaliation Technique is a counter attack ability that targets using the normal counter-attack rules but allows the attack to reach out to short range, as the blades don't want to leave the wielders side. The Evocation creates a counter attack using the wielders Thrown as a decisive attack.

                        Special Activation Rules:
                        Using Pack Retaliation Technique ends the activation of Defiant Matriarch stance for the rest of the scene. It can be reset if the wielder crashes a non-trivial opponent after unleashing a savage attack; be it through the use of Charms or a well described stunt.

                        Primeval Spirits Merge
                        Cost: 10m, 2wp Mins: Essence 4
                        Type: Simple
                        Keywords: None
                        Duration: One Scene
                        Prerequisites: Pack Retaliation Technique

                        Meditating on the beast spirits that reside in the blade, the wielder release a loud call; a growl or shrill shriek of some kind and summons a sleeping beast from the shards. The Essence of both mingle, causing the wielders anima to take on a brilliant combination of their natural essence plus the silvery red contours, lying over them like a ghostly cape and hood of the ancient beasts they have released back into Creation. The blades gather in a large circling flock, the centre of the wheel in line with the root chakra of the wielder.

                        When the wielder activates Call Forth the Primeval Beast they gain numerous static benefits for the remainder of the scene. The first is that they gain an additional 8 hardness that can stack with other sources, as well as +2 dice on all combat related actions and gaining an additional 1 point of Initiative every turn. On top of this the wielder also converts 2 dice of withering or decisive post soak dice into automatic successes and gain a +1 to their Resolve when a situation arises of someone or something attempting to intimidate, threaten, plead with or lie to. Finally, due to the innate and powerful connection of the myriad of ancient beasts, the wielder also receives a Defining level Intimacy for the scene of “I must shed my prey’s blood”.

                        Special Activation Rules:
                        Call Forth the Primeval Beast is a tiring Evocation, and takes its toll on the wielder's body and the connection between warrior and weapon. To be able to reuse Primeval Spirits Merge the wielder must find a worthy prey animal and kill it, bathing the weapon in its blood and feasting upon the raw meat.

                            Hello people and Exalted community once again. And you The Unsung Hero, love most of your posts.

                            Anyway, despite being proud game master for numerous RPGs and long time Exalted fan (and storyteller), I find myself in difficult situation.
                            I've home brewed several artifacts and evocations for my players for the 3rd edition, some with their help. However, my newest group has a player whose wish for a sword and resulting thought process has created me a challenge for creating an artifact that I have not been able to overcome yet.

                            Maybe a little background information of the situation is in order first.
                            The player is new to Exalted, though not beginner role player. He wanted to have a Masamune kind of swordsmith Twilight. Initial concept was him being a 100 year old swordsmithing hermit, but since it wouldn't make sense for having a Solar that old (...or you could, but then it would be good that all player characters be 100 year old surviving solar exaltations that weren't locked down) we came to a compromise.
                            His character hails from the end of the First Age. He was present when the Usurpation happened. However, he was young and had exalted quite recently (details matter not, as one will notice soon). He got away and traveled with a pile of magical materials far away into the eastern wyld, attempting to forge a weapon that would smite the dragon blooded for the Usurpation. Since creating potent enough weapon would have taken lot of time (don't start with the Thousand Forge Hands, he bought it only after the game started) and he wanted to get to the fight as soon as possible, he made a deal with a Raksha Noble named "Prince Laavdhi". The Raksha allowed him to use a special location deep in the eastern Wyld known as "the Valley of Times". Not a time travelling device (I dislike time travelling too) but a certain kind of stasis area, where time slows down, allowing for really long tasks or projects to be done without barely any time passing for the user. As an inexperienced Solar, he made a fateful deal with Prince Laavdhi. He forged his artifact with his own hands, but only after he left the Valley, he noticed how he had been tricked. Time did move slower related to him, but it moved normally for everything else outside the valley. Creating the artifact had taken millenia. Moreover, Prince Laavdhi mockingly informed the solar that even memories not related directly to the task at hand in the Valley will lose track of character and vanish to the Wyld. If the Twilight wanted even some of those memories back, he'd have to come back one day and either beg Prince Laavdhi to fish them out of chaos or beat him to do it. Twilight can remember barely anything from the First Age, only that things were...different then, but cannot specify why or how they were different.
                            And so it is that the player's Twilight wanders Creation with a sword created in a metaphysical time capsule to fight in the Usurpation that ended before the weapon was even finished.
                            I call it "Ekidnon, Sword of Retribution".

                            The problem is: What kind of evocations I'd give for something like that? I've thought numerous approaches: better defenses against people that have already hit him (defense doesn't really have thematic link to "Retribution"), then I thought of "Retribution points" he can spend to fuel effects and he gains them by getting hit or having negative intimacy towards his target (too much book-keeping) and finally I gave the sword ONE evocation based on the first evocation of Beloved Adorei, except it reveals only directly opposing intimacies to the wielder or his intimacies.
                            I could hit my head to the table long enough to figure something out, but at least I thought I could ask for advice. Not asking anyone to create it for me though.

You'll need to look through the examples in the book and model it after one of them.

I'm HOPING that the +2 dice on all combat related actions counts as charm-added dice, in which case it's fine. A mote drip is VERY powerful. I would give this charm the Perilous keyword at least, and end it when crashed.

Not all of the evocations add to that theme (like the cover one), but some of them get it spot on (like the first few). I think this is a good addition to our forum collection.
