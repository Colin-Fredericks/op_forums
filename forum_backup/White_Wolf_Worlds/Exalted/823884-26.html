                    Originally posted by Binary Phantom View Post
                    Lights the Night
                    ●●● Artifact: Orichalcum Chain Shirt

                    Attunement - non-night caste Solar and Fire-Aspected Dragonblooded Exalted may pay 1 additional motes to lessen the cost of raising from dim to glowing anima and glowing to burning anima to 4 peripheral motes each. This will reduces the amount of essence respired from 5 to 4 motes/(turn or hour, as applicable)
                    - Upon attunement, Guiding Candles Through Rough Waters is learned at no experience cost

                    Evocations

                    Guiding Candles Through Rough Waters
                    Cost: 6m
                    Mins: Essence 2
                    Type: Simple
                    Keywords: Mute
                    Duration: One Scene
                    Prerequisite Charms: None
                    One Line Summary: Rally armies or stop panicking crowds with your literal light

                    The warm glow of Lights the Night is a beacon that the masses can rally behind, lead the lost home, bring calm where there is frightful darkness. Upon activation, the wearer and all allies are more resilient against panic. When the target of a social influence action to instill fear, the target may add (wearer’s current anima rating) to their resolve. If the wearer is commanding a battle group, the group can reroll (current anima rating) dice once, taking the better of the rolls, to resist rout and to rally around the wearer. Finally, the ability for the wearer to guide others through treacherous terrain is significantly improved, negating penalties from poor visibility and adding 1 success to all others in long range to safely navigate around dangerous obstacles.

                    Special Activation Rules: Usable once per scene. This evocation can only be activated when the wearer is already at the glowing anima level or higher and will end prematurely if it should fall to the dim anima level.


                    Like a Beacon of Indomitable Brilliance
                    Cost: 3m 1a 1i
                    Mins: Essence 3
                    Type: Reflexive
                    Keywords: Withering-Only, Perilous
                    Duration: One attack against the wearer
                    Prerequisite Charms: Guiding a Candle Upon Rough Waters
                    One Line Summary: Creatures of darkness must spend additional motes to attack the wearer.

                    Darkness, no matter its form, is simply a lack of light, in one way or another. It is this lacking that the Light’s the Night’s inner light taxes shades, the soulless and the damned. Whenever a creature of darkness attempts a withering attack against the wearer, the attacker must spend an additional (wearer’s current anima rating before expending it) in motes. If the attacker was already spending essence on the attack, they must pay this tax on top of the essence already spent.

                    If the attacker cannot fully pay the surcharge in essence, the attacker must pay what they can and then the rest are paid in successes removed from the attack roll. If the loss of successes is enough that the attack misses or if the attack would hit but is fully absorbed by soak before minimum damage is applied, the wearer is recouped the anima loss.


                    Spreading the Eternal Light
                    Cost: 5m 3a 1W
                    Mins: Essence 3
                    Type: Simple
                    Keywords: None
                    Duration: Instant
                    Prerequisite Charms: Like a Beacon of Indomitable Brilliance
                    One Line Summary: Release one’s anima to ignite the anima banners of allied exalts and a bonus for mortals

                    Even in the purest void, a single candle can start a brilliant bonfire and thus even the smallest light can flare into a new dawn. By letting go of their anima and letting it wash over those around them, the wearer can charge the anima of any other exalt that the wearer has a positive tie with. The targeted exalt must be within line-of-sight. The exalt gains the wearer’s intimacy rating in anima levels and gain initiative equal to the strongest positive tie they have for the wearer.

                    Benevolent mortals in line-of-sight also benefit from this act, gaining the initiative as an exalt does and gaining the following:
                    - Ignore up to (wearer’s intimacy rating for mortal) of dice lost to penalties
                    - On their next action, they can reroll all 1’s until 1’s disappear.

                    Useable only once per scene.
                    I like this one. My only hesitation is how much of the rules revolve around all participants having mote pools, but you do account for that in the end. The attunement bonus is interesting and unique (and frankly almost punishing) and the rest of it seems to work out pretty well. I'm not sure if Mortals get the bigger benefit or Exalts on that last evocation, but I guess if I'm not sure, then it's relatively balanced (someone else may say otherwise).

                    I love artifacts that use Anima levels and this seems to do it pretty well. Nice jorb!

                            Sky-Bender


                            The Base

                            An artifact Heavy Shield (Accuracy +1 Damage +12 Defense +0 Overwhelming 5 Attunement 7 Tags: Chopping, Lethal, Melee, Shield, Smashing)

                            Concept

                            It was forged by the Solar that captured Isidoros single-handedly, at great personal sacrifice (their entire Legion), and echoes the legendary powers of that particular Yozi.

                            Description

                            A great matte disc of Black Jade, covered in a coarse but intricate engraving in Old Realm script, rendered in wire-fine White Jade and Starmetal, describing a lament for a worthy opponent at the cessation of hostility.

                            Evocations of Skybender

                            This Heavy Shield finds its greatest potential in the hands of those who are self-reliant, driven, and relentless. Exalts of any kind that attune to Skybender add their Anima level to any Parry Defense they make with it, at a cost of reducing their Evasion by an equal amount, as the Black Boar was never one to flee from a fight, but rather to allow His foes to bask in His singularity.

                            Solars that attune to Skybender are drawn to greater acts of bravado and heroism, often heedless of risks to life, limb, and welcome company; they add their Anima level to Resolve when defending an Intimacy relating to fighting on, be it against a worthy adversary or in the face of terrible odds.

                            Sidereals, in contrast, find the solidity of Skybender’s fate allows them to ground their own; while attuned, witnesses add the Vizier’s Anima level to rolls to recall his presence.

                            Dragon-Blooded, whose forebearers paid the greatest cost in bringing Skybender into existance, add their Anima level to their Hardness and Soak while attuned.

                            Veil of Personal Magnetism

                            Cost: 2m, 1a per level of Cover, optionally 1wp (+1wp) Mins: Essence 1

                            Type: Supplemental

                            Keywords: None

                            Duration: One turn

                            Prerequisites: None

                            Skybender, living up to its name, diffuses its Bearer’s Anima into a gauzy haze that throws mirages and deflects attacks.
                            This Evocation supplements a Take Cover action, allowing the Bearer to channel his Anima to bolster his Defense. The Bearer’s Anima must, at a minimum, be at Glowing to use this ability. For every level of Anima committed, the Bearer may treat his Cover as one degree better, to a maximum of Full. At the start of the Bearer’s next turn, his Anima fades to dim. Optionally, if no Cover is available, the Bearer may spend a point of temporary Willpower to summon viable Cover from his own Anima with this Evocation. This cover may move with him, and there are no additional limitations on available actions he can take while this power is active.
                            At Essence 2 and above, the Bearer may expend an additional Willpower to extend the Cover granted or improved by this Evocation to a Battle Group of a Size no greater than his Essence.

                            Lacuna of Crushing Eminence

                            Cost: 3m, 1a Mins: Essence 1

                            Type: Reflexive

                            Keywords: Psyche, Uniform, Clash

                            Duration: indefinite

                            Prerequisites: Veil of Personal Magnetism

                            In blocking his adversary’s strikes, Skybender draws its bearer’s quarry into his orbit with the strength of a collapsing star.
                            The Bearer may clash a withering or decisive attack with such singular skill that it stokes the enemy’s fear and respect. This clash attack does no damage, but, in addition to the penalty to Defense, allows the Bearer to make a reflexive Instill action (rolling Charisma + Melee), adding threshold successes from the Clash to the roll, and creating or improving an Intimacy of Awe toward the Bearer. This Intimacy lasts until a week passes with the Bearer not being within Essence miles of the Target or the Bearer is Crashed by the Target. The Bearer’s Anima must be at Glowing before using this Charm, resets to Dim at the beginning of his next turn, and counts as the Bearer’s attack action.

                            All Falls to Force

                            Cost: 1m, 1wp Mins: Essence 2

                            Type: Reflexive

                            Keywords: None

                            Duration: One Turn

                            Prerequisites: Lacuna of Crushing Eminence

                            Marked as worthy opponents, the Bearer’s foes are drawn to the hunt.
                            When using Skybender to defend himself, the Bearer may use this Evocation to make those they cherish inapplicable targets. All incoming attacks must target him until his next turn. During this time, if his defense is penetrated, those whom he holds an Intimacy towards, or who hold an Intimacy toward him, cannot be attacked. His Anima must be at Burning to use this Evocation.

                            Magnanimous Adversary Ansible
                            Cost: 2m, 1+a Mins: Essence 3

                            Type: Reflexive

                            Keywords: Uniform

                            Duration: Instant

                            Prerequisites: All Falls to Force, Lacuna of Crushing Eminence

                            Distance means little to those who are the center, and Skybender was born in the shadow of the center.
                            While the Bearer's Anima is at least at Glowing, those that recognize his greatness, and are bathed in it, are always readily at hand. The Bearer may invoke this Evocation to instantaneously close the distance between himself and one that either bears an Intimacy toward him or that he bears an Intimacy toward, appearing at their side as if he had been there all along. The distance he may cross is proportional to his Anima level; at Glowing, he may cross Short distances, at Burning, Medium, at Iconic, Long. He may hold the activation of this Charm to Clash a Withering or Decisive Attack made against or by the subject of the Tie with Skybender. This counts as the Bearer's Attack Action, and causes his Anima to fade back to Dim.

                            Unassailable Whim-Driven Behemoth

                            Cost: 5m, 3a Mins: Essence 3

                            Type: Simple

                            Keywords: None

                            Duration: One Scene

                            Prerequisites: All Falls to Force, Lacuna of Crushing Eminence, Magnanimous Adversary Ansible

                            At the heights of power, those who’ve truly bonded with Skybender are like unto the great beasts of ages uncounted.
                            When the Bearer’s Anima becomes Iconic, Skybender’s magic makes it extraordinarily difficult for Trivial enemies to engage him in combat, regardless of their numbers. He does not take onslaught penalties from any attack made by Trivial opponents or Battle Groups, although magically inflicted onslaught penalties still apply against him. Withering attacks made by such enemies cannot drop him below 1 Initiative unless they have a post-soak damage of 10 dice (although attackers can still gain the full amount of Initiative damage dealt). Decisive attacks made by such enemies cannot deal more than (3 + attacker’s Strength) levels of damage to the Bearer with a single attack, not counting any levels of damage added by Charms or other magic. This Evocation ends prematurely if the Bearer is Crashed, causing their Anima to fade to Dim.



                            Feedback welcomed.

                                I was hoping to get some assistance with a Grimscythe that I am putting together for a character. When I was talking with the other players about using a scythe, there were several jokes about it being like the one from RWBY. Thinking about the scenes of Ruby Rose slicing through hordes of Grimm, I got the image of a Solar carving their way through a horde of Fair Folk, and decided that this Grimscythe had been crafted to hunt down incursions from the Wyld.

                                I would like to have 5 or 6 evocations in it, but I am not quite sure what to do for the other 2-3. I don't have a description, but there would be a hearthstone socket, probably on the base of the blade. For the design, I figured White Jade would be appropriate for the great force delivered in the capstone evocation. My Storyteller felt that Soulsteel would be an appropriate second material.

                                The Kill Must Be Clean was actually suggested by my Storyteller for the weapon. I see it as the capstone of the evocation list, and would not have any objection to making an additional evocation or two a prereq. I wanted to have some sort of fast movement evocation, to emulate the way Ruby moves around in the show that inspired this, but wasn't quite sure what to do that's not just a copy of an existing charm. I would also like to have one or two more combat evocations, possibly designed around dealing with battle groups, like the packs that hobgoblins and silverwights typically form.

                                I am looking forward to any suggestions you might have.


                                Erufukira, the Death of Fair Folk (Soulsteel/White Jade Grimscythe Artifact 3)

                                Evocations of Erufukira
                                When a Solar, Abyssal, or Dragon-Blood is attuned to Erufukira, it guides their hand in all fights against its primary foes, giving 1 automatic attack success vs Fair Folk.

                                The Hunter Is Never Prey
                                Cost: -
                                Mins: Essence 1
                                Type: Reflexive
                                Keywords: None
                                Duration: Instant
                                Prerequisite Charms: None
                                Once the Exalted is attuned to Erufukira, the weapon makes sure that he is aware when forces from the Wyld are in the area. If there are Fair Folk or other beings purely of the Wyld within medium range of the wielder, he will automatically sense their presence. This does not give him a location or even a direction, it only lets him know they are present. He will also detect the presence of Wyld mutants, but only within short range.
                                When the wielder becomes aware of their presence, he can try to locate/track them. He takes an action to make a Perception+Awareness check, applying any Awareness charms normally. For each 9 rolled, he gains a single mote of Essence, and for each 10 rolled, two motes, which can only be used to offset the costs of the Awareness charms used for this check.


                                The Pack Is No Protection
                                Cost: 4m, 1 wp
                                Mins: Essence 2
                                Type: Supplemental
                                Keywords: Decisive-only
                                Duration: Instant
                                Prerequisite Charms: None
                                When the Fair Folk invade, they often come in large numbers. This is no protection against the wielder of Erufukira. When facing a battle group and making a decisive attack, the damage bonus is doubled, to 2 levels of automatic damage per 4 damage dice. If a charm is being used that lets him make multiple decisive attacks in a round, he pays the wp cost only for the first attack, paying only 4m for each additional attack.


                                The Kill Must Be Clean
                                Cost: 6m, 1wp
                                Mins: Essence 2
                                Type: Supplemental
                                Keywords: Decisive-only
                                Duration: Instant
                                Prerequisite Charms: The Pack Is No Protection
                                Every hunter knows that the prey should be taken out as quickly and cleanly as possible. When making a decisive attack against any creature of the Wyld, he may add the weapon's attack and damage bonus. If a charm is being used that lets him make multiple decisive attacks in a round, he pays the wp cost only for the first attack, paying only 6m for each additional attack.
