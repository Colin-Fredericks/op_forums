          Rose Dame, Moonsilver Sword, Artifact ••••



          Evocations of Rose Dame

          The mirror shards of Rose Dame is exceptional at abosrbing energy and essence-based attacks, granting its master a non-charm Defense when blocking such attacks. Her allies in short range also benefit from this bonus as light and flame and all things that burn can't help but be drawn toward their Dame.

          Shattering-Roses Beginning
          Cost: 5m, 3a, 1wp; Mins: Essence 1
          Type: Reflexive
          Keywords: Dissonant, Resonant
          Duration: One Scene
          Prerequisite: None

          The Exalted hangs high Rose Dame into the air as her anima is absorbed into the sword, she then smashes it into the ground, shattering it into pieces. For the rest of the scene, she can attack out to Medium range as she directs the flying shard to attack her enemies, Rose's previous owner uses its hilt like a composer, moving the shattering blade into a symphony of cutting death.

          Dissonant: Rose Dame can only attack enemy out to Short range.

          Resonant: The Exalted can pay 3m to attack enemy out to Long range but lose the benefit from the sword's Defense bonus for this round.

                      Medeia, Starmetal Knife, Artifact •••••



                      Evocations of Medeia
                      To his friend, the witch is a fountain of knowledge and empowerment, allowing him to Introduce Fact even if he has no relevant specialty or backstory at an increased +2 Difficulty, a character will receive one on-charm dice on their roll if they employ a witch's fact, they must accept a Minor positive Intimacy toward the witch the action is succeed, this rush of positive feeling toward the witch can be resist by paying one Willpower.

                      One Harmless Obedient Witch
                      Cost: 5m; Mins: Essence 1
                      Type: Reflexive
                      Keywords: Dissonant, Resonant
                      Duration: Indefinite
                      Prerequisite: None

                      To any who would slander the witch's good name, they will find the hero's mind an impassible sanctuary. A character's Resolve is increased by one against any attempts to degrade his Intimacy toward the witch.

                      At Essence 3, this Evocation automatically upgraded, allowing the character to store one Witch point whenever they benefit from any Fact introduced by the witch, they can spend these points to increase both their Resolve and the Resolve's dice cap. A character can only have 5 Witch points at any given times.

                      Dissonant: Characters dissonant with Starmetal can not access the upgrade at essence 3.

                      Resonant: The Resolve added by Witch points are counted as non-charm dice.


                      Letting The Wrong One In
                      Cost: 5m; Mins: Essence 1
                      Type: Reflexive
                      Keywords: Dissonant
                      Duration: One Scene
                      Prerequisite: None

                      A thousand Luna can't even compare to the fickleness of a man's heart, men are wicked beast that can't help but betray those who love them and the witch will be the divine retribution comes crashing down like a torrent of needles. A character who benefited from a Fact introduced by the witch but develops a negative Intimacy toward him instead (whenever in their first meeting or later) are despised. The witch's attacks against such characters received several benefits depend on the strength of their Tie toward him as long as this Evocation is active:
                      • Minor: +1 Overwhelming
                      • Major: Ignore 5 Soak and 1 Hardness
                      • Defining: Wounds inflicted by the witch can not be healed naturally and required specialized magical effects to do so.


                      Dissonant: Only the first two benefits are available to those who can't resonant with Starmetal and a mote surcharge is required every rounds to keep the Evocation actives.


                      Tamed Witch Kills Savage Wife
                      Cost: -; Mins: Essence 2
                      Type: Permanent
                      Keywords: Resonant
                      Duration: Permanent
                      Prerequisite: Letting The Wrong One In

                      Vile weed always decorate the path to a man's heart , with sickle in hand a witch clears the path to his true love. This evocation expand the definition of despised to include characters that has positive intimacies toward the same character as the witch does.


                      Scarlet Web of Straw Dolls
                      Cost                      : -; Mins: Essence 3
                      Type: Permanent
                      Keywords: Resonant
                      Duration: Permanent
                      Prerequisite: Celestial Circe Sorcery

                      Once a character benefit from a Fact introduced by the witch on 5 separate roll, he becomes an effigy, a symbolic link that the witch can use to cast effects that require it such as Silent Words of Dreams and Nightmares (Exalted, p.475). This effect can only be used once per week and will only target characters whom the effigy has a positive intimacy toward and vice versa.

                                                  Dreaming Lamp, Artifact •••



                                                  Made from stained-glass, wyld corals and black jade, this lamp contains a dreamworld within it. An owner can change how the world look, but it's always remained an endless ocean with no shore in sight where physics holds no place, night can change into day in a blink of an eye, water rises up into columns of shimmering liquid, and all manner of strange creatures swim beneath the surface.

                                                  The coral's branch can be broken off (which will quickly restore itself after a few minutes), these pieces can be given to other characters which allow them to visit this world if they sleep while having the coral piece in near proximity.

                                                  The pleasing experience in the dreamworld grants the character an additional point of Willpower when they wake up.


                                                  Ia Ia Cthulhu Fhtagn
                                                  Cost                                                  : 10m,1wp; Mins: Essence 5
                                                  Type: Reflexive
                                                  Keywords: None
                                                  Duration: Instant
                                                  Prerequisite: Being Theo

                                                  The witch's fiancee protects his lover's dream from unwanted suitors. Any powers that would disturb Theo's dreams must roll at an increased +1 difficulty, another 10m can be pay to increase the difficulty by one to a maximum of 5, failing will inflict on the suitor an unsoakable level of bashing damage for each die that is a non-success as their brain melt from the psychic backlash. A botch will inflict lethal level of damage instead as the lamp tries to transport the suitor directly to the Great Dreamer, their body starts to be ripped apart by unseen force and spiralling into a single point before disappearing.

                                                          Vọng Phu, Starmetal Bell, Artifact •••



                                                          Evocations of Vọng Phu
                                                          Characters without any Intimacies relate to love or romance suffers a -1 Resolve against Performance-based social actions made by Vọng Phu. If such an actions success, the target also subjected to a feeling of melancholy toward Theo, this emotion only counts as Minor intensity however.


                                                          Love Shines Like a Beacon Amidst the Rolling Storm and Rising Tide
                                                          Cost: 5m, 1wp; Mins: Essence 1
                                                          Type: Simple
                                                          Keywords: Resonance
                                                          Duration: Indefinite
                                                          Prerequisite: None

                                                          Standing at the pier, a maiden sounds Vọng Phu hoping that his love can find their way back from the storming sea. Those that Theo has a positive Defining Tie toward and vice versa always aware of his general location, they reroll 1s until none appears on any navigation roll to find their way to Theo as the world nudge them toward the maiden. A Lunar mate (if Theo even has one) can always choose to benefit from this effect even if they and Theo have never met each other.

                                                          Resonance: The mote cost is reduced every time a character finds their way to Theo for the first time while benefiting from this Evocation, this can potentially reduce the committed cost to 0m.


                                                          Overflowing with Tears, He Sings a Song of Love
                                                          Cost: 4m; Mins: Essence 2
                                                          Type: Supplement
                                                          Keywords: None
                                                          Duration: Instant
                                                          Prerequisite: None

                                                          Those with a heart, ask not the maiden if he still keeps his promise. After a successful song-based Performance roll using Vọng Phu, Theo's Intimacy toward the subject of the song is immediately revealed to the audience as if they had succeeded at a Read Intention roll.


                                                          Lady Crying on the Street For Lost Love
                                                          Cost: 5m; Mins: Essence 2
                                                          Type: Supplement
                                                          Keywords: None
                                                          Duration: Instant
                                                          Prerequisite: None

                                                          Instead of drawing people to him, the maiden instead repels them away. A successful song-based Performance roll causes people to disregard Theo completely as their mind is filled with bystander apathy, the audience can't attempt to resist this charm with wp unless Theo performs obvious supernatural action. The cost to resist this effect depend on the number of audiences, 3wp is needed for a large crowd in a busy street, 2wp for a small group of individual, a character who is alone with Theo can ignore this Evocation completely, they are instead afflicted with a feeling of sadness toward Theo.


                                                          O Purest Snow, Blanket Every Inch of this Land In Sadness
                                                          Cost: 10m, 1wp; Mins: Essence 3
                                                          Type: Supplement
                                                          Keywords: Resonance
                                                          Duration: Until ended
                                                          Prerequisite: None

                                                          Theo make an inspire roll with a free full Excellency, and for every 10 rolled, one non-success is converted into a success, up to a maximum of (Essence) success can be converted this way.

                                                          As long as Theo continues to play the song, characters who fail to resist will be overwhelmed with a tidal wave of sorrow, whether toward Theo or themselves, and are unable to perform any actions save for weeping or lamenting their lost love. They can defend themselves but will resume their mourning after successfully dodge or parry the attack, being damaged by a decisive attack cancel the effect but the emotion of melancholy will still last for the scene.

                                                          Those who resist this Evocation is automatically subjected to (Essence) level of bashing damage as their heart and mind rebels, if they can't cry after such a sorrowful melody then why even live?

                                                          Resonance: When this Evocation is used in combat, all affected characters lose 1i per turn.


                                                          If Heaven was to be Merciful, This Love will Endure
                                                          Cost: -; Mins: Essence 4
                                                          Type: Permanent
                                                          Keywords: Resonance
                                                          Duration: Permanent
                                                          Prerequisite: None

                                                          This Evocation upgrades Righteous Lion Defense, allowing Ties to be affected by the charm. Any characters who attempt to decay the Tie and fail to beat his Resolve is affected by an emotion of melancholy for the rest of the scene. The power of this Evocation reaches even remote magical effects such as those of the Sidereal Exalted.

                                                          Resonance: A botch attempt at decaying the enchanted Tie resulting in the world punishing the target in subtle ways, bugs eat away their cloth on a first date, horse tries to run them over in a busy street,...etc.
