      The Knight Raiton, as a Getimian.

      ​Thomas was an affluent Guild medicine-brewer who grew to admire the factor Martin Brus, that eventually the two were wed. A generous bribe was made to a sorcerer known to the Guild, who summoned a neomah to create a new life for them to have as their own. They spent a sizable sum on teaching young Carter, as the child was called, the ways of finance, persuasion, and etiquette. Unfortunately for Carter, while he was still a youth, his parents were tragically killed, and the Guild seized most of their assets while the youth mourned.

      Comforted only by his manservant Avery, Carter spent a decade of seclusion in his late parents' mansion. In this time, he trained and honed his body and mind, and began to emerge at nights to investigate those murders that went ignored by the Hooded Executioners, cases where the poor and powerless lacked the means to get the justice they deserved. Donning a silver beaked mask, bolos and steel gauntlets, he was The Knight Raiton.

      ​Eventually Carter eased himself into the public eye as himself as well, and began to take over what remained of his family's holdings; often he had to travel with the caravans himself, to ensure the success of the ventures. These efforts begun to pay off, and recently, in open defiance of the Council of Entities, he hired a circle of Exalts to brave one of the Tombs of Nexus. Though he had to tithe a number of the treasures to the Council to mollify them, he was able to ensure that a number of the artifacts went to good use providing safety and shelter to the lowest class of the city.

      ​Then Carter Brus awoke into a world in which he had never been born, a world in which Nexus had never heard the name of the Knight Raiton. He was devastated to learn that having never been born did nothing to prevent the deaths of his parents. He sought out Avery. The Brus family manservant was unconvinced that the dark-haired man was the son of his late employers, but the righteousness of Carter's cause and the passion in his words led Avery to throw in with him. Late one night the two broke into Guild offices and forged records for Carter, creating a paper trail of receipts and forms sufficient to convince the Guild of his existence.

      ​Following that, Avery "revealed" to the Guild that Carter had been raised in secret according to the wishes of the late Thomas and Martin Brus, for fear of other members of the Guild using him as leverage against them, and now that Carter had come into his own as an adult, he came seeking his inheritance. The Guild reluctantly restored the family mansion to Carter and Avery, and a fraction of his family's fortune; seeing the plight that still hangs about the lower class of Nexus, the Knight Raiton now seeks out those Exalts who were daring enough to delve into the Tomb of Keening Spirits in his world, to see if they can do the same in this one...

      He/him

              So my 3E Character Cynis Ganan, is already a kind of re-imagined version of one of my 2E Dragon-Blooded, Cynis Aneda. I don’t have much interest in reproducing him as another kind of Exalt like-for-like, but I am interested in a “What If...” style “What if he never Exalted as a Dragon-Blooded”.

              As such, the Solar character who follows is not “as faithful a reproduction as possible”, either in terms of equivalent power or equivalent powers. But he's pretty close, and I enjoyed myself doing this, so… enjoy!

              Cynis Ganan – A Dragon-Blooded Hero (Default)

              Born in Realm Year 705 to Cynis Falen Lorana (a renowned scholar and socialite, who received the blessing of Exaltation at the tender age of eleven) and Ledaal Vidad (an official in the Thousand Scales, and a double Immaculate Master and lay monk), Cynis Ganan seemed destined for great things in the Scarlet Dynasty.

              A scion of two renowned bloodlines, Ganan’s mother was only slightly disappointed to note that she’d birthed a boy, and she drove Ganan with the relentless expectation that he should break her record for early Exaltation. A prodigy in the martial arts, Ganan mastered the mortal forms of Earth Dragon Style as a mortal child. Whilst Ganan’s eleventh birthday came and went with nothing but disappointment from his mother, it was to be his rigorous study of The White Treatise in the lead up to his twelfth birthday, that saw Ganan both self-initiate as a sorcerer and Exalt with the blood of Pasiap.

              Ganan’s initiation into sorcery changed the direction of his life. Where study at the Cloister of Wisdom had been all but guaranteed at this point of his life, becoming a sorcerer had profoundly changed the young man, perhaps even more-so than Exaltation. He was now driven to attend the Heptagram, and was able to secure a place thanks to the fortuitous position of his father’s sister on the staff.

              Ganan excelled at the academic, but failed to properly grasp the intricacies of Dynastic society. This, otherwise fatal flaw, was mitigated both by the poor reputation of sorcerers as misfits and social outcasts. Ganan also did not neglect his physical body, learning the deeper mysteries of Earth Dragon Style from half-forgotten Shogunate records whilst at the Heptagram and building himself into a physical powerhouse.

              Disaster would strike when Ganan opted to complete his training at the Valkhawsen Sorcery Academy. Lured into the thrall of the Mad Sorcerer, Amilar Shavok, Ganan would join the Lookshyan terrorist cell, the Seekers of Eternal Truth, and uncover the dread Lost Manse – site of the Shavok Incident (which slew the other Seekers to a man, including the dread Anathema Crashes-the-Waves). The Incident would grant Ganan the mighty white jade tetsubo, Thunder’s Crash, but leave him crippled for decades and haunted by the possibility that Shavok survived...

              Whilst barely able to walk, and weakened beyond measure, Ganan’s mind remained sharp. When his mother wished to establish a foothold near Greyfalls, it was Ganan who scouted the Dragon-lines to find an untapped demesne to site The Palace of Dragon’s Repose, her now-infamous pleasure house. Lorana gifted her son the manse’s hearthstone, a Stone of the Ram’s Horn, in recognition of his service. Ganan’s long convalescence in his mother’s manse allowed him to begin to rebuild his sorcerously shattered body.

              Returning to the Heptagram as an instructor, in Realm Year 736, Ganan would meet the love of his life and future wife, Mnemon Alinos Danireya. In due course, he would sire the twins Muli and Nula, both of whom would go on to Exalt.

              Something of a blunt instrument, Ganan is ill-suited for Dynastic politics – straight-forward and direct, in his 63 years of life he has yet to find a problem he can’t club to death. He has traveled to the Threshold on his wife’s behest to act as… I don’t want to say “her muscular slave”… actually… I kinda do. Ganan sees his wife’s venture as a chance to smash an uncivilized beastman horde, and perhaps even stave in the skull of an Anathema. Those who don’t know him well might assume that this is but a carefully crafted façade to hide his true ambitions, but rare for a Dynast and rarer for a Cynis, he really does want nothing more than to prove his might.

              Cynis Ganan – The Unclean Anathema (Solar)

              Born in Realm Year 705 to Cynis Falen Lorena (a renowned scholar and socialite, who received the blessing of Exaltation at the tender age of eleven) and Ledaal Vidad (an official in the Thousand Scales, and a double Immaculate Master and lay monk), Cynis Ganan seemed destined for great things in the Scarlet Dynasty.

              A scion of two renowned bloodlines, Ganan’s mother was only slightly disappointed to note that she’d birthed a boy, and she drove Ganan with the relentless expectation that he should break her record for early Exaltation. A prodigy in the martial arts, Ganan mastered the mortal forms of Earth Dragon Style as a mortal child. Ganan’s eleventh birthday came and went with nothing but disappointment from his mother. Every birthday until this point had been a milestone, a celebration of life. Every birthday following was to be a condemnation.

              At fourteen, Ganan attended the Cloister of Wisdom. His mother was glad to be rid of him, and promptly divorced her husband on the grounds of infertility. (Ledaal Vidad would petition the Empress to take a pilgrimage to the Caul.) Ganan prayed fervently, lost in blind religious devotion, but whilst skilled in the martial arts, his lack of social ability and combined with his mortal station saw him flunk out of the Cloister by age 16. Ganan would complete his secondary school education at Flowering Vestibule, alongside other mortal Dynasts and Patricians. By age 21 his was disowned in all but name: his boorish nature unsuited for Dynastic politics, he was practically exiled to Greyfalls where he would live his life in quiet resentment of his Dragon-Blooded betters.

              For forty years, Ganan lived on the Threshold in a state of quasi-exile as the Realm’s fortunes ebbed and waned. Every day he would consider running away into the Hundred Kingdoms and starting a new life, free of the shame of his failure. Every day he would report to his family to see to his duties. Studying the Dragon-lines around his home, Ganan discovered a confluence, an untapped demesne. Not being a Heptagram trained sorcerer, but a mortal drop-out of a military school, Ganan had great trouble securing funds from his house to scout and construct the manse. It took decades, and required that Ganan design the manse almost single-handedly.

              In Realm Year 765, things came to a head. The half-constructed Palace of Dragon’s Repose was a project too large and too complicated for the mortal Dynast. Ganan took up a knife, ready to plunge it into his stomach. Amalion trembled in Hell, ready to complete the commission herself. At the last moment, however, Ganan turned the knife from his body and plunged it into his designs. Cutting away the failed parts of the design, Ganan worked in a daze, Essence Fever burning as he redesigned, and even physically completed, the manse single-handedly. It was only as the manse’s hearthstone began to form that Ganan even noticed the mark of the Unclean gracing his brow.

              Ganan sent word for his sister, Cynis Jinabar, to take ownership of the manse as he fled south, driven by dreams of a half-remembered Realm Before. Over the next three years, Ganan would claim his powerful gore-maul from a the tomb of his prior incarnation, Bright Forge Heldenhammer, and join forces with the sensuous Deceiver, Crashes-the-Waves, and her terrorist organization The Seekers of Eternal Truth. Ganan has devoured what First Age lore the Seekers have been able to uncover through their infiltration of the Valkhawsen Sorcery Academy.

              Exaltation has restored the 63 year old Ganan to prime physical condition, though he’s not as powerful as he was as a teenager he is certainly formidable. Ganan and Crashes-the-Waves now search for the Lost Manse, a relic of the Shogunate said to be able to materialize artifacts from the user’s very soul…

                      Oooh, I like that challenge.

                      Chinoe Smoke-in-the-Rain, Night Caste (default)

                      (Long story short)
                      Chinoe is one of the personas of Night Caste Chekai, investigator and prosecutor of the Realm, whose Exaltation went to her head until she got herself assassinated... but having prepared for that contingency, she was reborn as Chinoe, excellent thief and master assassin.
                      Thing is, Chinoe has a lot of gaps in "her" memory, and can only tentatively retrace the events leading to her rebirth as the person she now is. She came to the conclusion that Chekai had dangerous, megalomanic plans in motion, and whoever stopped her did very well. Deeply sobered up by the evidences, she took it upon herself to find, in turn, other Exalts who might show themselves to be similarly tyrannic and dangerous, and insert herself into their entourage to either subtly guide them towards a better path, or murder them once there was no hope left.
                      The third of Chinoe's talents, you see, is passing for a complete moron, and letting others believe she's just a tool who needs to be pointed in the right direction in exchange of simple riches...


                      Smoke Rising in a Rain of Knives, Chosen of Endings


                      Actually a lot more straightforward background...
                      Smoke found herself Exalted as an agent of Fate while studying and training hard to fulfill what she thought was her destiny as an ambitious, but mortal agent of the law of the Realm. She witnessed her old identity fade away slowly, and only then started realizing the delicate webs of power building up. Her real role in the world is to set the threads, especially this that happen to be golden or fated to become so, just right, and if not, which happened more often than not... cut them cleanly, and let them be forgotten just like her unenlightened previous self was.



                      Chinoe Smoke-in-the-Rain, Getimian


                      In a Creation that differs very little from the one everyone else knows, Chinoe, charming young rogue excelling at concealing her smarts, witnessed a comrade Exalt very unexpectedly, and became his sidekick. He slowly descended towards madness and tyranny, however, cruelly taking advantage of many innocents. After several attempts to reason him - all very subtle, lest he would declare her a traitor and execute her as well - he made it clear that redemption was beyond him as he killed a whole family for the example. While he was still drenched in blood and celebrating his revenge, she betrayed his absolute trust and murdered him without a warning. From then on, she took it upon herself to repeat the end of that scenario as many times as necessary, becoming a boogeyman whose extended survival relied on nothing more than her harmless façade. Eventually, Chinoe found her greatest target: Chekai, a master investigator of the Realm, secretly a Solar using her work to gather as many resources as necessary to enact a grand and terrifying plan that may not only lead to great bloodshed, but also start a war. Pretending for years to be a simple-minded informant, Chinoe too prepared for an assassination, perfecting her mastery of dark martial arts and plotting with other hidden figures... until the culmination of their plan. After a terrifying night, her victim, finally dead! Of course, Chekai had many minions and allies, and they're all after Chinoe now, but she accomplished her goal, and maybe she'll manage to escape certain doom for longer...

                      ... but she woke up one day, and she was never there, and her old "friend", without her influence, slipped much faster into homicidal obsession before the Wyld Hunt got him. Without even the urban legend going around, many tyrants simply don't worry. Oddly enough, megalomanic Chekai does not seem to be present in this reality, either... but that is little comfort.

                          Coruscating Eo, Eclipse Caste Solar (Default)
                          Left on a brothel doorstep, Eo grew up immersed in the seedy side of society. She became a natural part of the criminal world, always running two or three schemes to keep herself and her people afloat. When the relatively laid-back and grandfatherly old crimelord of her area was replaced by his brutish son, she saw he was going to bring heat from all sides and ended up having him killed and fed to pigs for the good of her community. This earned her the Sun on her brow.


                          Vermilion Smile Eo, Getimian
                          This is similar to her default---she was part of a criminal underworld and when a power shift brought an unfit leader into play, she saw how bad it would get and had him taken out. She took a leading role in his place and expanded the syndicate, digging its roots deeper and building up its safety net. Her charm and cool head earned her the respect of other crimelords and even of certain lawkeepers, who found her at worst the lesser evil and at best a friend on the other side of the books, a community-minded leader with the sense to know when not to push...

                          And then... none of that happened. She found herself in a world that, til now, had lacked her presence. The unfit crimelord had not been taken out quietly, but had brought ruin to the people Eo loved in his hamfisted clawing for power. But now she is here, and if she is to be denied the fruits of her earlier labors, she will simply have to recreate them here.


                          Perfected Radiance Eo, Infernal
                          Eo ran herself ragged for her community, for her family. She did good work and smoothed things over with the law when need be, and under the old don it was all fine. When his son took over, he tore it out from under her, perhaps fearing her as a rival to power. He ensured nobody would listen to her even as she worked herself nearly to death trying to counter his short-sighted gambles. In the end, when hope seemed lost, something listened and echoed her grief and frustration.

                          And now, it's time for everyone to listen. She is reborn as Perfected Radiance, and with her wits and brilliance now hellfire-tempered, she is going to make everything better again, and everyone can either stand behind her, get out of her way, or be taken apart.
