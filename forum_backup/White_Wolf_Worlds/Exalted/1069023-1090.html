                        I don't know if this is really the correct place to ask this kind of question, but I have a concern regarding a charm in the preview. It isn't a power question so much as a question on intent.

                        I feel like Chain-Breaking Fury's presentation as the Lunar becoming enraged when confronted with having to take actions to support an influence done to them could be turning ever so slightly back to 2E's fandom 'Any use of Essence to convince someone to do something is brainwashing'.

                        I don't necessarily think this one charm makes it so, and I don't even think the charm is too powerful, or even unthematic; Lunars are defined by their Rage in this edition, it seems, and the effect of only breaking Influence when it comes into conflict with a Major or Defining Intimacy means the charm isn't really that out there in power. But I worry that thematically, or due to how it's worded, it's presenting the idea that if someone convinces you to do something through the social influence system, even if it's all above board, that's still them (in a negative way), manipulating you, tricking you, or otherwise crippling your ability to decide so that you take the action in a negative way.

                        I really like the way the current Social System works, and I like that there are clear, defined Charms that say 'this is when you're brainwashing someone or forcibly mentally disabling them rather then trying to interact with them as a person', so I worry the fact this charm immediately makes the Lunar 'Rage'- and at a Major Tie- when they reject Social Influence with it could be leading to further charms where the Social System continues to drift back into bad assumptions.

                        Do you think it's valid to maybe change the fluff on this charm or similar, without changing the actual effect being done, or do you think that this isn't necessarily a problem since it's just a single charm, and is something you're still being wary and watching out for?
