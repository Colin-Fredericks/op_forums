    Originally posted by habitableexoplanet View Post
    Does Essence-Gathering Temper reset at the end of the scene if you don't meet the reset condition? Can you simply never use it again until you soak a large enough withering attack?

    By a strict reading of the charm, it's the latter. Mind you, the reset condition isn't all that hard to achieve - get attacked by any heavy weapon (or a medium with a single threshold success), have soak reduce it to minimum damage, and have that come up with no successes, and you're good.

    That said, the charm's effects aren't so impressive that it should be the only charm that doesn't reset in a timeframe, in my opinion. I'd put it at one use per combat scene, personally.

                                Exalted Core, Page 230, Environmental Hazards -- Emphasis added

                                "They can generally be mitigated to some degree with a resistance roll [...] up to the Storyteller. (Stamina + Resistance) and (Dexterity + Dodge) are the most common"

                                If your players have a wave of lava coming towards them, you don't have to let them have a roll to avoid that at all. If you choose to let them roll (Dexterity + Athletics) to jump out of the way, then that is the resistance roll. They don't get to roll (Stamina + Resistance) to tank the lava - you choose the appropriate method of resistance.

                                Lava does 6L/round at Difficulty 5.

                                So you're in a cave and lava is coming towards you. You get one roll to mitagate the hazard -- by jumping to safety. If you flub the roll then the lava gets close enough to damage you and 6L bypassing Soak and Hardness, is rolled against you. If that rolls 0 damage, then the fumes and heat have yet to affect you, and you're probably not yet burried under the lava.

                                On your next go, at ST discretion, you get another roll to try to jump to safety again. Lets say you fail that, and lava washes over you doing 3L. It burns! It burns!

                                Next round, you fail to jump to safety again, but the lava does 0 damage (the dice gods love you). Obviously you've been able to pull yourself up to the side, out from under the lava, but still in a position where you're in danger.

                                Six dice of Lethal Damage is unlikely to kill anyone outright. So I'd say it represents the heat and fumes more than buried under tons of molten rock. If you want to bury a character under tons of molten rock, then I simply wouldn't allow any roll to mitigate the damage: you're stuck under the lava taking 6L each round until you die. But at that point, it's pretty much an arbitrary "rocks fall, everybody dies".

                                I'd say to keep the Storyteller's Rule in mind, and try not to write yourself into a corner with your descriptions.

                                                            Originally posted by Jefepato View Post
                                                            Has anyone gotten any good use out of the spell Death Ray? It seems really tricky to me to use Solar Circle sorcery effectively in combat, but it's just so awesome.

                                                            But I guess the out-of-combat utility might be worth the price of admission for some characters. Carve messages into mountains and fortresses, tear through pretty much every mundane object, etc.
                                                            Death Ray ussually also creates a lava hazard. It's not strictly implied by description, but by analogy with Flight of the Brlliant Raptor, I'd personally make a ruling that whenever it makes some sense, you automatically have to avoid attack and lava each round.

                                                            Another thing is that the spell bypasses most of the combat system in a unique way.

                                                            If you have decent Join Battle or some other ways to gather Initiative, then you have access to the attack that:
                                                            - does not reset your Initiative, so it allows you to keep repeting a decisive attack each turn;
                                                            - uses the result of your original roll (!) If you rolled 15 successes (quite possible with dice tricks), you keep it and keep attacking, knowing that each round you simply will hit. If you don't - you just repeat the spell.

                                                            If in the melee tree you could find a charm that lets you make decisive each turn without resetting your Initiative and keeping the result of the last roll, it would be dramatically overpowered.

                                                            I agree that the original developers made clear mistakes when gauging power levels of various things, but I think that Death Ray is not really flashy, it's just deadly efficient if you are able to gather decet initiative in the first place.
