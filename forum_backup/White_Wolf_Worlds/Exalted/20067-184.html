                    You would do exactly that. Any character who acts before the immaterial demons or whatever don't get a turn but cannot be ambushed. They know somethings up even if they can't see it. They would be subject to surprise attacks. Everyone else can be ambushed until such time as the attacks have revealed themselves or they have had a 'turn'.

                            Originally posted by SamuraiMujuru View Post
                            I'm working my way through the final PDF, but there's something that's been kind of confusing me and led to some frustration at a few sessions I've run.

                            When dealing with Stealth in combat, how exactly do you resolve things like surprise rounds? For example, the Circle is confronted by something no one has the means of detecting (IE: Immaterial demons), and no charms to notice the threat (Surprise Anticipation Method, Reflex Sidestep).

                            Would you roll initiative as normal, and then just skip any characters that have no method of reacting, applying either an Ambush or a Surprise Attack as the situation requires, then the following round everyone's initiative resolves as normal?
                            A sneaky Sidereal ninja stealths up to the party. The Storyteller menacingly calls for Perception + Awareness rolls, which we'll say all the players fail. Time for sneaky ninja to do some killin'.

                            Time for everyone to Join Battle, everyone rolls Wits + Awareness. If a Solar beats the Sidereal's Join Battle, they act before him in combat. He's still concealed, but they're aware that someone is sneaking up on them and can take appropriate countermeasures.

                            Once the Sidereal's turn comes up, he can make his surprise attack. If he attacks a Solar who didn't beat his Join Battle, he gets to make an ambush. If he uses it to attack a Solar who beat his Join Battle, it would only be an unexpected attack.
