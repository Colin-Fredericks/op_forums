                                            Hello, Discounted Rent…I Mean, New Roomie: Unanimous

                                            “Yeah, for now,” you tell her. “Who are you?”

                                            “I am the Transcendent Princess of Umbral Infini-whoops.”

                                            In attempting to dramatically exit her closet, she gets caught on a button knocking down a couple hangers with outfits on them and tripping to land on the bedroll. She lies on her back on the bedroll, staring up at the ceiling with an expression of abject despair.

                                            “So…this is your mess, then?”

                                            “The world is my mess,” she moans.

                                            You look down at her, not entirely sure what to make of the situation. She really reminds you of some of Dwi’s girls who have spent too much time in poppy dreams and too little time in the real world.

                                            “Listen, um…Princess. This is-“

                                            “Transcendent Princess. Of the Umbral Infinitudes.”

                                            “…okay, that cannot be your real name.”

                                            Again, she groans. “You sound just like my father. Cultures all over Creation have coming of age ceremonies where they choose their own names! One’s name should speak of one’s rank, or one’s accomplishments, or one’s aspirations! A name is a thing of power, and to let another choose that name for you is to give up a vital-“

                                            “Lady. I am…I am not calling you Trancing Princess of the Umbral…Infinisims?”

                                            “Infinitudes.”

                                            “What’s an infinitude?”

                                            She rolls onto her side and rests her head on her hand, looking up at you. “It’s an uncountably vast number of something. Like if your armies are unending, you have an infinitude of soldiers.”

                                            “So your name means you’re a princess of unending…what, exactly?”

                                            “Shadows!” She rolls her eyes at you as thought you’re an idiot. “Umbral, it’s an adjective modifying infinitudes. It means ‘shadowy,’ but it has connotations as well of ghostly or phantom phenomena, or a silent but ever-present companion similar in that respect to one's shadow.”

                                            “So…you picked a name for yourself that means…’I’m royalty and command a vast army of ghosts?’ then?”

                                            She rolls her eyes. “Shadows. Ghostly is just a connotation.”

                                            “So this vast army of…shadows…can they, um, do dishes?”

                                            “Gods, you sound exactly like my father! I don’t have a shadow army yet, the name is aspirational! It represents my commitment to achieve my-”

                                            You close your hand on the handle of your knife, briefly considering actual violence...and then release it, taking a deep breath.This woman is particularly annoying.

                                            “Listen. Princess.”

                                            “Of the-“

                                            “Shut your mouth or I swear to the Dragons I will slit your throat and see how many months’ rent I can get by selling your organs to a back-alley alchemist!”

                                            She shuts her mouth, and stares up at you, obviously and abjectly terrified. You’ve never actually experienced anyone being…scared of you. It feels odd. But not actually bad…

                                            “You currently owe Gleaming Smile a bunch of money. Nod your head yes or shake it no.”

                                            She nods her head. You can see tears welling in her eyes.

                                            “Do you have enough money to pay him back, or a way to GET enough money to pay him back?”

                                            She shakes her head no.

                                            “Okay. Is all this your mess?”

                                            She nods her head yes.

                                            “And you have NO source of income.”

                                            She shakes her head again.

                                            “Shit…listen, what’s your real name?”

                                            “W-White M-Moth.”

                                            “Okay. Moth. What do you do? How were you paying your rent back when you were paying it?”

                                            “I, um. I’m a student. At the University of Nexus. I’m studying funerary traditions and warding rites under the Master of Nine Black Chains. He's from Sijan. I was…I was living on the stipend Mother gave me to cover food and living expenses, but I used it up faster than I meant to.”

                                            “…this stipend. Was it a one-time thing, or does your Mother give you money regularly?”

                                            “She, um. She gives me money at the start of each Season.”

                                            “It’s Resplendent Water now…so you’ll get more money at the start of Ascending Earth?”

                                            She nods, sniffling a little.

                                            “How much money does your Mother usually give you?”

                                            “I’m not really sure…usually ten or twelve Dinar?”

                                            You open your mouth in shock. Then you close it again.

                                            “You…you spent twelve Dinar…since the start of last month?”

                                            She nods glumly.

                                            “But. But you…twelve…” You find yourself spluttering slightly. “How?”

                                            “Books, mostly. And, um. Wine. And clothes. And makeup? And supplies.”

                                            “Supplies.”

                                            “For the Master of Nine Black Chains. We needed chalk, and incense, and lots and lots of candles, and molten lead, and herbs, and a white goat that had never seen-“

                                            “Shut it.”

                                            She nods.

                                            “Okay. Here’s the deal. This is my apartment now, Moth. I just signed the lease, I’m renting it out. And I do NOT want your filth all over my apartment. The way I can see it, I can either kick you out…or I can let you stay and just not tell Gleaming Smile about it.”

                                            White Moth’s eyes widen with hope.

                                            “If you agree to pay me for half the rent for your time living here. And if you agree to pay Gleaming Smile back for the back rent you owe him. As soon as you get the money from your Mother.”

                                            She nods, smiling.

                                            “Also! If you’re living in my apartment, you will clean up after yourself or I turn you over to Gleaming Smile and he will probably sell you to some whorehouse someplace to get his money back. You will clean your dishes, clean the chamberpots, clean your clothes…and clean yourself, because I swear, lady, I cut up a giant lizard last night and walked past piles of garbage on my way here and we both smell better than you do right now.”

                                            White Moth is no longer smiling.

                                            “Also…you’re going to teach me to read.”

                                            “You...can’t read?”

                                            “Not yet. But every day you’re gonna teach me. That way if Gleaming Smile catches you before you pay me what you owe me…at least I’ve gotten something in exchange for letting you stay here.”

                                            “…very well, I can use it as a chance to review. It’s a deal...but don't get any creepy ideas about sharing a bedroll or anything!”

                                            “No objections. I can smell your bedroll from all the way over here. Now help me clean this place up. I’m seriously worried about catching the plague.”

                                            “Okay, okay…don’t throw out all the empty wine bottles, though. I need a few for Master of Nine Black Chains, so we can make spirit-traps.”

                                            “…spirit-traps?”

                                            “Traps for evil spirits.”

                                            “…okay, another rule. No bringing evil spirits into my apartment.”

                                            “…deal. Um. Thanks. For letting me stay here, I mean.”

                                            “Don’t thank me. It’s just cheaper to split rent, Moth.”

                                            “If we're going to be living together, could you…not call me Moth?”

                                            “Well I’m not calling you Tapdancing Princess of the Umbral Infinitudes.”

                                            “Transcendent! And why not?”

                                            “Because you’re not actually a princess, for one? I can’t believe you picked your own name and that was the best you could come up with.”

                                            “Yeah? What’s your name, then?”
