                        Originally posted by Epitome View Post
                        Here's an idea based on Guardian Fog :

                        Epiphany-Inducing Workshop
                        Cost: 6m, 1wp; Mins: Craft 3, Essence 1
                        Type: Simple
                        Keywords: None
                        Duration: One Scene

                        Create a flawless specimen of an object that will grant insight and inspiration to people trying to do the same. Perform a Basic Craft Project in front of other crafters, then upon completion note your total successes and the highest stunt you achieved. Until the end of the Scene, all observers gain both your stunt and half your total successes (round up) as dice on their first interval to craft the same object.
                        I like this a lot. I'd like to suggest an additional effect:

                        A repurchase (requiring Craft 4, Essence 2) allows the Solar to do the same thing with a Major project (increasing the duration of the Charm to "one project" as appropriate), but observers must have at least one dot in a relevant Craft ability to take advantage of the effect. (The Solar can definitely lead a group of amateur blacksmiths in forging good-quality weapons, but someone who has no idea how to use a forge can't join the fun.) A Solar with Craft 5 may apply this effect to non-magical superior projects as well, but observers require two dots in a pertinent Craft to join in. This Charm can't be used to assist in the creation of artifacts under any circumstances.

                        (I dunno, maybe the bit about superior projects should require a second repurchase? A Solar who can take a crew of modestly-capable shipwrights or masons and turn out a whole bunch of ships or houses could be a very big deal in the right circumstances.)

                                            Originally posted by Accelerator View Post
                                            Does anyone have good ideas for justifying or narrating wound cleansing meditation, or stunting healing without medicines or tools in a modern context?
                                            Stunting Wound Cleansing can probably be done by stunting extreme speed. Like have you ever seen those video compilations where people work insanely fast because they've practiced the same motions for like 15 years? It's like that except absolutely non-stop for 15-60 minutes.

                                            Asforstunting without tools...I'm not sure that's allowed. The charms specifically call out thatyou need proper medicine and bandages and stuff. Instant Treatment Methodology doesn't require medical tools if you stunt it, and it gives a pretty good example of striking pressure points, but it also costs a willpower, so it's not super sustainable to do it all the time.

                                            Wound Banishing Strike is a bit more sustainable, it also lets you instantly fix somebody's crippling problems by striking them with your fingers or throwing acupuncture needles into them, but it doesn't let you fix problems that aren't temporary. If you want to fix wounds instantly and without tools Anointment of Miraculous Health let's you do that.

                                            There's a bit of a tradeoff as you go up though. Anointment of Miraculous Health let's you instantly treat a number of health levels equal to your Essence, which starts at 1 and rises kind of slowly. If you want to treat really bad wounds very quickly you can combo Wound Mending Care with Instant Treatment Methodology, which lets you nearly instantly cure someone of terrible grevious wounds, sometimes without needing tools, but that's two willpower and a bunch of motes. You have to trade off efficiency for speed and power.

                                                    Originally posted by Accelerator View Post

                                                    Contagion curing touch and instant treatment methodology.

                                                    That costs Willpower?

                                                    I thought it costs motes only?
                                                    Instant Treatment costs either 5 motes or 5 initiative in combat, but it always costs a willpower. Contagion Curing Touch is slightly different, it lets you cure infectious disease without the proper tools or medicine, just as long as your methods are still logical. So keeping somebody quarrentined in a clean environment, controlling their temperature with water and blankets, ensuring they get exactly the nutrients they need in their food.

                                                    Contagion Curing Touch also doesn't work instantly, it just lets you treat somebody normally without proper medicine and tools, and with bonus successes. If you want to cure somebody instantly that costs a willpower, and it's Plague Banishing Incantation. Otherwise Ailment Rectifying Method is where you want to go. It lets you give a really solid bonus to the people you're treating, and you can do as many in a day as you have hours to work.

                                                    If you're treating really difficult diseases you might want some charms later in the tree that do things like let you sap your own health for motes, or make the medicine excellency twice as efficient.
