                                        Originally posted by habitableexoplanet View Post
                                        "the aurochs-dragon of Hellbender Mesa": There's dragon-animal hybrids?
                                        Bear in mind that adding -dragon to an animal's name in Creation doesn't necessarily mean they're a hybrid between an animal and a powerful elemental. -dragon is used as an adjective to mean "really huge and tough, and probably ferocious" quite often. See the ox-dragon and river dragon in the Ex 3e core book for examples. Since there's already an ox-dragon in Creation, I'd personally guess that an aurochs-dragon was probably a related creature, but a more desert-adapted one than the ox-dragon. The ox-dragon is a triceratops, so I'd base the aurochs-dragon on another ceratopsid, maybe diabloceratops.

                                        Originally posted by habitableexoplanet
                                        Fivefold Dragons’ Grasp: Has there been an artifact before that is basically only usable by one Exalt type? Probably, I guess.
                                        There are several artifacts in the core book and Arms of the Chosen have Solar charms as prereqs for some or all of their evocations. But the devs have generally said that those aren't exclusively for use by Solars, just that other Exalt types who picked them up would unlock different evocations based on other charms from their charmset. Likewise, I would think that if a Solar or someone got Fivefold Dragons' Grasp, she'd be able to develop evocations for it that built on her own stuff. Note that even the basic attunement bonus refers to boosting "elemental spells" as well as Elemental Bolt Attack, and a Solar, Lunar, etc. sorcerer could certainly cast those and thus use that bit as-is.

                                        Originally posted by habitableexoplanet
                                        Ragara: If you're immortal, isn't someone asking to be named as your heir very suspicious?
                                        I don't know if the Scarlet Empress has ever claimed to be immortal. I mean, she's obviously much more long-lived than any Dragon-blood in the 2nd Age, but I don't think she's out-and-out told anyone "Oh, BTW, I can't actually die". Claiming to be actively immortal would probably have political consequences for her (people are going to ask "how do you get immortality?", and if she doesn't answer, at least some of them are going to speculate that the method is unsavory), and as long as she doesn't openly claim it, she can't really object to people asking her to clarify the succession.

                                        Originally posted by habitableexoplanet
                                        "As Western routes progressed from being accessible only through rare feats of Dragon-Blooded naval prowess to being open to regular mortal mercantile traffic, House Peleps — now fully invested in the Imperial Navy — established footholds there. " - What change over time is this in reference to?
                                        It's referenced in a bit more detail in the Fajad writeup in The Realm - apparently, the route to the West via Wu Jian didn't open up until less than a century ago. Why it was that Wu Jian wasn't accessible before then is unknown, though.
