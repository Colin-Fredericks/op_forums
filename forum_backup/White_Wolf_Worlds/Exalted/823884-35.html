                                In case anyone wants to take a crack at it while I try to think something up: Hope's Last Gasp is a Night Caste Solar who found the Chain Shirt in a safe house manse belonging to one of the previous holders of her Exaltation and was guided there by past life memories after escaping from the Dowager. The Chain Shirt is a normal 3 dot artifact, and it originally belonged to a First Age Night Caste who I have done zilch to develop because they aren't important beyond their memories guiding Hope to the safehouse manse.
                                I was actually just musing on what would be useful light artifact armor for a Night with a Dodge/Stealth focus who gets hit very rarely (and thus won't benefit much from soak), and came up with this:

                                Eye of the Storm (White Jade Artifact Breastplate ***)
                                A simple artifact breastplate composed of white jade, with a single hearthstone socket at the center of its chest.

                                Attunement:
                                A Solar who attunes to Eye of the Storm automatically awakens Sandstorm at no cost.

                                Sandstorm
                                Cost: 4m, 1wp, Mins: Essence 1
                                Type: Reflexive
                                Keywords: None
                                Duration: Indefinite
                                Prerequisite Charms: None
                                With a small push of her Essence, the Solar causes the white jade of the armor to resonate with the local earth. It grinds at the surface of hard marbles and stones, sending flecks into the air, and kicks up sand and dirt. This Evocation may only be used so long as there is a reasonable quantity of elementally-appropriate materials in the surrounding area; if there is not, the Solar will realize it before she spends her motes.

                                Once the various materials have been clawed at by the power of her armor, they're thrown into a whirlwind of sand, dust, dirt, and other particulates. As they spin through the air, they inflict a -3 penalty to Awareness rolls to see into or within the whirlwind, as the materials obscure everything within close range of the Solar. This Evocation lasts so long as the Solar wishes it to continue, but she can allow her Essence to lax at any time, causing the whirlwind to quickly end and leaving the various materials strewn around. If the Solar moves range bands, the effect follows her, becoming refreshed by new materials as she moves, other bits being left in her wake.

                                Note that this Evocation does not manipulate the air within the storm, only the earth. While it can knock over objects, irritate enemies, and serve as fodder for stunts, it cannot be used to, for example, protect the Solar from a toxic gas.

                                Tremor Discernment Technique
                                Cost: 3m, Mins: Essence 1
                                Type: Supplemental
                                Keywords: None
                                Duration: Instant
                                Prerequisite Charms: Sandstorm
                                This Evocation enhances an Awareness roll made within the effect of Sandstorm. The Solar reaches out through the resonant white jade, sensing through the maelstrom she stands at the center of. She ignores the normal penalty to Awareness rolls inflicted by Sandstorm, and further, adds a single non-Charm success to her roll. This boost represents a tactile sense for the maelstrom that surrounds her, and as such cannot be applied to objects beyond close range, Awareness rolls relying on differentiating light or color, or similar effects. Awareness Charms which boost the Solar's sense of touch also boost Awareness rolls made using Tremor Discernment Technique.

                                Turbulent Strike
                                Cost: 3m, Mins: Essence 2
                                Type: Supplemental
                                Keywords: Uniform
                                Duration: Instant
                                Prerequisite Charms: Sandstorm
                                This Evocation enhances an attack made from within the raging chaos of Sandstorm. The kicked up particulates seem to start to die down, coalescing near one another, before the Solar makes her sudden strike. Whether the Solar is making a withering or decisive attack, this Evocation creates a withering attack just before it.

                                This withering attack is made with (Dex + combat ability used for the attack it supplements), has a base damage of 10 dice, and Overwhelming 3. It has no inherent accuracy boosts and does not gain a damage bonus from Strength. This bonus attack has its Initiative damage applied before the Solar rolls her subsequent attack. Any Defense penalty for being subject to surprise applies to both attacks.

                                Once this Evocation is used, the effect of Sandstorm ends.

                                Mother Earth's Caress
                                Cost: -, Mins: Essence 3
                                Type: Permanent
                                Keywords: None
                                Duration: Permanent
                                Prerequisite Charms: Turbulent Strike
                                This Evocation enhances its prerequisite. After the chaotic whirl of rock and earth strikes her target, it returns to the Solar, covering her from head to toe in a cake of mud and stone as it resonates with Eye of the Storm, enhanced by the Earth-flavored Essence of her armor. After making an attack enhanced by Turbulent Strike, the Solar increases her soak by six until the end of her next turn. Activating Sandstorm again immediately ends this effect.


                                It's pretty much a first draft and I don't have much experience making artifacts, though. (I wrote it thinking breastplate but you could scratch that out and replace it with Chain Shirt without any problems, I think.)
