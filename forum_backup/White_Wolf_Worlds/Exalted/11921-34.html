                                                    A post to get this moving again:

                                                    Two young Dynast twins of a minor House in the Realm are sent to a tiny satrapy in the Northeastern Threshold. There they live with their disgraced relative, who maintains his meager estate by creating fake Wonders of the First Age and displaying them to gullible mortals. Their lives change forever when one of the twins stumbles upon an ancient ruin with a dusty old tome inside. Reading over the tome, the boy learns that the tiny satrapy was built upon the ruins of a First Age city, and many of the strange wonders produced by those ancient Solar kings still exist in the forests surrounding the Satrapy. Together with his twin sister, the First Age tome, their miserly relative and his servants, the Dynast twins face all sorts of dangers, including a horde of Fair Folk seeking a new queen, the power mad Dragon-Blooded boy attempting to steal the ancient tome, numerous Sidereals drawn to the strange satrapy, a trapped prince of an undersea kingdom and a Soul of She Who Lives Within Her Name constantly manipulating behind the scenes in order to kickstart the Reclamation.

                                                        Let's see... I've got three. I'm a little new to this (and Exalted in general, though I backed 3E), so bear with me.

                                                        In a small town on the coast, a mighty weather-beaten Manse sits, modeled in the image of a great warrior. Inhabited by a young Solar half-caste and the three Exalts who love him, they strive together to protect the town from the horrible Behemoths that occasionally pop up to threaten it. However, from the dark machine realm of Autochthonia, old enemies have at last turned their eyes back to Creation, and plot to finish what they started.

                                                        Long ago, four incredibly powerful Exalts were united together by a secret Council to preserve the Balance of Creation. In doing so, they aided the Usurpation in slaughtering all of the raging, decadent Solars and locking their Exaltations away. Now, one of the four has been charged with sparking a war between Yu-Shan and Malfeas which has almost decimated the entire population of Creation. His brother, adamant in his belief that the warrior would never purposefully do such a thing, descends into the Underworld to speak with the Lords of Death in an attempt to prove his brother's innocence by undoing the very crime itself.

                                                        Ordinarily, Yu-Shan turns a blind eye to the sinful behaviors of those who know how to hide them well enough. Unfortunately for these two God-blooded sisters, they didn't, and now they have been banished to Creation. In order to return to Yu-Shan, they must work together and use their heavenly powers to defeat demons who have escaped from Malfeas and in doing so, earn Ambrosia Coins to buy their way back in. Along the way, they are aided by a massive bear-totem Full Moon Lunar priest, a nerdy thaumaturge inventor kid, and a small, strange green First Circle demon who can only speak one word.
