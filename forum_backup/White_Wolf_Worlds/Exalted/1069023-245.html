        Something that's come up in some of my games:
        One of the Celestial Workings (level 2) is giving yourself powers. But these all seem like quite permanent changes: a silver tongue, scorpion blood, a hypnotic gaze. Rather than a charm or spell, which is something you do as an action.

        So can you use workings to give yourself an at-will power, rather than change yourself permanently? For example, give yourself (or one of your minions) the power to change into a horse at will, or the ability to shoot fire, or have a hypnotic gaze when you activate it (but not when you don't), or fly (not with wings, just the power to fly), or something like that.

        Also, you can use workings to change a building. Can you use a working to give an object a power? Like enchant a sword (not a daiklaive) so it's on fire (or can burst into fire when the wearer wields it). To what extent does that run up against "this should be an artefact, not a working?"

                Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz View Post
                So can you use workings to give yourself an at-will power, rather than change yourself permanently?
                Yeah.

                Also, you can use workings to change a building. Can you use a working to give an object a power? Like enchant a sword (not a daiklaive) so it's on fire (or can burst into fire when the wearer wields it). To what extent does that run up against "this should be an artefact, not a working?"
                Ultimately it's up to the Storyteller to draw that line. My personal leaning is that most anything that could be an artifact, should be an artifact, and workings shouldn't be able to swallow up that concept space.
