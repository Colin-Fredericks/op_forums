    Most of your post is very agreeable (like animal focus = good)!

    However, I've read through the it multiple times, and I'm still not seeing an actual argument for why Full Moon's shouldn't be the ones who use the ol' heart-eating method as their default, whilst the others do not, merely some vague reasons as to why you think they might be able to have everyone use it.

    You seem to think the main objection is that other castes might not be able to eat hearts, and you argument is predicated on this narrow thinking. It took me a while to work out where you were coming from because it was such an odd assumption. So let's look at your reasons:

    First reason - Anyone can turn into a big mean animal, and it's easier to be basically okay at combat. Only relevant if you assume capability is the issue (and somewhat overstated).

    Second reason - Well, someone else/magic/theoretical traps for which the game has never had any rules can do it for you! Again, entirely focused on capability, and seemingly unable to see the pretty huge problem with "Just get someone to do it for you!" when others do not have to do that. So you've focused on capability, but even there you've fallen down - taking shapes is a core, perhaps the core Lunar ability, but you seem to think only Full Moons should be able to do it easily, reliably, and without burning Charm slots to do so. That's not very good reasoning, I would suggest.

    Third reason - It should be easiest for Full Moons, and harder for everyone else, because that's most dramatic! That's the start of an argument, but unfortunately that's as far as you go. You never really present good reasoning as to why everyone should have to use a method that fits one Caste perfectly, and is trivially easy for them, beyond that you like the specific, narrow, and dare I say it, repetitive drama of "I have to kill this being to take it's form!", which is always going to boil down to "Oh, well fuck that guy! *CHOMP*" or "Nope!". It's not exactly taxing or involving after literally the third or fourth time (esp. as you support alternate methods).

    Some of your "alternative methods" presented are also pretty strange, and seem focused entirely around taking forms from humans. I literally do not see a single suggestion from you for taking forms from animals. Why is this? You agree that animal forms are a big part of Lunars, but then you're all about suing or shagging or foot races or whatever for taking forms from others, none of which apply to animals (I hope! Otherwise it's all a bit Jerry Springer).

    Finally you focus on the difficulty issue - you claim being able to KO as well as kill is a pure upgrade. It is, of course, but you fail to recognise that the other forms could have default methods as difficulty and potentially even as disturbing as the heart-eating, and that no-one, that I'm aware of is demanding they get to simply KO people and take their faces. Obviously such a method should be a bit more involved and risky, like having to shown their own true to face to the target or something, or having to keep the target alive for say, a month or a year after they last use his form, or else they suffer some kind of curse/backlash (which would make you think pretty hard about whether to use it or not on a lot of people or creatures).

    Thankfully you avoid the obvious nonsense of the vampire feeding comparison.

    In the end, though, you present nothing beyond opinion as to why we shouldn't have three castes with three different default methods of taking forms (esp. if they can expand them later). It's pretty dull if every Lunar works exactly the same way at their core, though, and doesn't really support the Caste distinction. In fact, contrary to what you were saying, the very centrality of this issue makes a good place to distinguish the Castes, rather than to make them all the same.

    All that said, again, I agree with the rest of what you're saying.

    Oh except one thing from a later post - Hunter is not Trickster. One does not fit into the other. They are very different archetypes, and when you mix them together, you weaken both.
