    Originally posted by Lanaya View Post
    You don't get to claim a martial art as yours like that. By their very nature, all non-SMA martial arts are generic and universal; you can't block Solars from using or being supremely skilled at a certain martial art just by declaring it to be important to your culture. The Dragon-Bloodeds' designated toys are their charms, not the martial arts styles they happen to favour, and Solars are indeed completely unable to use DB charms.
    Perhaps I could have phrased that slightly better. But to claim that the 5 Immaculate Dragon Styles are not aligned to the Terrestrial Exalted above other known Exalted requires a certain degree of deliberate obtuseness. As martial arts, they're in the public domain, so to speak, and nobody's saying (at least I don't think anyone's saying) that Solars shouldn't be able to benefit from a Mastery keyword, were one to exist. The objection comes from the Solars being to use those charms being able to use them better than the Exalted host to whom they are iconic. A solar wielding Immaculate MA could very well defeat a Terrestrial Immaculate because:

    1.) He has better stats.
    2.) He has better luck
    3.) He's supplementing his attacks with the power of the Solar charmset.
    4.) He has Martial Arts as his Supernal, so he's able to reach the pinnacle of that style far, far faster than a Dragon-Blooded monk.

    There is plenty of room for a Solar to step in and which her natural prowess unlock secrets that have stymied and/or eluded Terrestrial savants of all stripes for centuries. This particular incident is not one of them.

            A bit of a false equivalence. Immaculate Martial Arts are Sidereal-created disciplines, designed to a) be effective element-themed martial arts, which by definition any of the Exalted can learn, that happens to b) coincide with and reinforce the elemental natures of the Dragon-Blooded. In past editions, there's never been anything to suggest that the Dragon-Blooded are inherently superior practitioners of Immaculate Martial Arts, just that mastering them conveys on them an additional benefit, potentially negating an innate weakness.

            What you're describing is an interesting heritage artifact that may provide additional benefits when attuned by a member of a specific lineage. Seems like perfectly valid and intriguing design space. Because of the way Artifacts work, it seems likely that a generic Solar would be at least as good with that Artifact as a generic Dragon-Blooded, but a true heir could very well gain some real benefit.

            I love the concept, but I'm not sure how it would be interpreted as equivalent to the actual topic.

                                                Anyway, I see it like this.

                                                The aristocratic themes of the Dragon-Blooded square really well with their martial artists being, for want of a better term, excessively orthodox. There's probably a specific term for "your kung fu is too mired in tradition and you are afraid to innovate too much" that comes with the genre but I don't know what it is. But if you spend a hundred years mastering a martial art that was specifically designed for you and you are adamant in your orthodoxy with it, shouldn't you be really fucking good at it? Is the giant-dicked power fantasy that is (apparently?) the default Solar tale really worth that much worfing that you have to be better than everyone at everything in all circumstances or else your tent will fall down?

                                                But then Wind starts bringing in powers from, I dunno, Crane or Single Point and Peleps Deled is like oshit what do

                                                Doesn't that make a much better fight, a much better narrative than "Wind is inherently better at Water Dragon than Deled because reasons"?

                                                What's better? That the rebel martial artist uses his giant protagonist-cock to perform the orthodox-art better than the orthodox-user, or that he masters different arts that the orthodox-kung-fu-man was afraid to touch and uses not giant-cock-powers but versatility, strategy and open-mindedness to humble him?
