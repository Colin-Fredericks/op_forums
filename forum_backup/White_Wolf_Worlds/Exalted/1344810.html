    Dark Rivers Style [PEACH]

    10-30-2019, 10:51 PM

    Those who swim in the waters of the underworld often find their memories have been swept away by the current. Some take this as freeing, the loss of their old identity leading them to a new life. Some are left empty, wandering across a world that has lost its meaning. A few are stripped of all morals, becoming ravening beasts that destroy all they see.
    The Monks of Dark Rivers take this as their founding creed – No-one knows who they may become, or who they may have been. The monks spend most of their time administering charity to the poor, in hopes that when in some future life the monks will receive the same charity. On festival days the monks hold grand feasts, debauchery clouded from future reproach by drink and herbs, leaving only vague feelings that joy was had. When necessary, the monks fight in battles they judge righteous, always offering mercy before killing lest they one day be killed without similar mercy.
    To become a Monk of Dark Rivers is to abandon one’s past life. The monks adamantly refuse to refer to a monk by their past name, and will protect their members to the death from judgment for crimes committed prior to joining. Likewise, they will permit no support given to or from past causes, families and offices. Many members were once criminals and soldiers fought by the monks, offered redemption in forgetfulness. Others were heirs to noble titles, whose rivals could not afford to have them killed, nor permit them to roam free.

    Dark Rivers Style
    Weapons: Dark Rivers Style can be practiced unarmed, with a staff, or with a fighting chain.
    Armor: Dark Rivers Style can be practiced in light armor.

    Distant Currents Defense
    Cost:1m; Mins:Martial Arts 2, Essence 1
    Type:Reflexive
    Keywords:Mastery, Terrestrial, Uniform
    Duration:Instant
    Prerequisites:
    The past is a foreign land, connected to the present only by easily-forgotten memories. The martial artist follows this truth selectively, unchanging no matter how much their enemies strive.
    Use after successfully parrying an attack. You do not gain onslaught from the attack.
    Terrestrial: This may only be used (Essence) times per round.
    Mastery: If you already had onslaught, you may remove any onslaught for a 2m surcharge.

    Blocking the Flow
    Cost:5m; Mins:Martial Arts 3, Essence 1
    Type:Reflexive
    Keywords:Uniform, Terrestrial
    Duration:One Turn
    Prerequisites:Distant Currents Defense
    This constitutes a defend other action on a target in close range. If the beneficiary has a wound penalty, add the wound penalty to your Parry for the turn.
    Terrestrial: This instead is a simple charm that may be flurried with other actions, the usual defense penalty for flurries.

    Full Life Exhaustion
    Cost:2m, 1wp; Mins:Martial Arts 4, Essence 1
    Type:Reflexive
    Keywords:Mastery, Withering-Only
    Duration:Instant
    Prerequisites:
    Make a withering attack with double 8s on the damage roll. You gain no initiative for dealing damage, though you still gain initiative for hitting and initiative break. If this crashes them, they take a -3 penalty to all actions, and a -1 penalty to defenses, until they leave crash.
    Mastery: Double 7s instead

    Dark Rivers Form
    Cost:8m; Mins:Martial Arts 4, Essence 1
    Type:Simple
    Keywords:Form
    Duration:Scene-Long
    Prerequisites:Blocking the Flow, Full Life Exhaustion
    The martial artist assume a stance of measured apathy – they know not who they are, so why should their failures weigh upon them?
    Gain +1 Parry. Missing with a decisive attack costs 1 less initiative. Reduce all wounds penalties you suffer by 2, minimum 0.
    Special Activation Conditions: Dark Rivers Form may be activated reflexively when your wound penalty increases.

    Old Pains Forgotten
    Cost:1wp; Mins:Martial Arts 4, Essence 2
    Type:Reflexive
    Keywords:Mastery
    Duration:Instant
    Prerequisites:Dark Rivers Form
    Leave crash, resetting to base initiative. Until your next turn, enemies don’t gain initiative break for crashing you. Use this only after a having spent a turn in crash.
    Mastery: This refreshes your turn as with Initiative Shift, though it does not allow you to make a join battle roll. This version may only be used once per scene, though the martial artist may continue to use this charm merely to escape crash.

    Empty-Mind Bliss
    Cost:2m; Mins:Martial Arts 5, Essence 2
    Type:Supplemental
    Keywords:Decisive-Only
    Duration:Instant
    Prerequisites:Dark Rivers Form
    This supplements an attack against an enemy with wound penalties. As long as it deals at least 1 level of damage, they suffer a poison with damage (their wound penalty+1)I, B in crash, duration (martial arts+their wound penalty), penalty (-1). For the duration of the poison, they ignore their wound penalties.

    Live for the Moment
    Cost:1lhl, 3m; Mins:Martial Arts 5, Essence 3
    Type:Reflexive
    Keywords:Mastery, Uniform
    Duration:Instant
    Prerequisites:Old Pains Forgotten
    Use after being hit by an attack. Reduce the damage, post soak in the case of a withering attack, by (your wound penalty). If they do not deal any damage, you may make a reflexive withering counterattack with damage equal to (raw damage they dealt)+extra successes.
    Mastery: You may pay an extra 5m to replace the counterattack with a decisive counterattack with damage equal to (raw damage they dealt), which does not include your initiative or reset you to base initiative on a hit.

    Treasured Flotsam Strike
    Cost:3m; Mins:Martial Arts 5, Essence 3
    Type:Supplemental
    Keywords:Decisive-Only, Terrestrial
    Duration:Instant
    Prerequisites:Empty Mind Bliss
    What one man throws away, another may pick up as their heart’s desire. In time, a single person may even play both roles.
    Supplements a decisive attack. When you reset to base initiative, reset to (Essence) more.
    Terrestrial: This can only be used against enemies with more initiative than you.

    Second Life Mercy
    Cost:8m, 2wp; Mins:Martial Arts 5, Essence 3
    Type:Simple
    Keywords:Decisive-Only, Mastery
    Duration:Instant
    Prerequisites:Treasured Flotsam Strike, Live for the Moment
    The Monks of the Dark River offer their mercy to everyone they fight, granting those who wish the blessing of a new life. For some recalcitrant sinners, it may be necessary to wash away their past life more directly.
    Make a decisive attack. Rather than deal health levels, this attack removes intimacies. The target must choose ties they have, each tie removing 1 level for minor, 2 for major, or 3 for defining, until they have counteracting all the damage. The target of any lost tie is forgotten completely. If they do not have enough ties, they lose willpower equal to the difference, then take anything left as levels of aggravated damage.
    Mastery: The martial artist may replace lost memories with new ones. They pick lost ties whose value total to half the damage dealt (i.e., 8 damage would be enough to replace 4 minor intimacies, 2 major intimacies, or 1 minor and 1 defining intimacy). The chosen ties, rather than being removed, have their target replaced with the person of the attacker’s choice. This similarly replaces the old target with the new in all of the target’s memories.

    Use after being hit by an attack. A withering attack doesn’t add extra successes to damage, and a decisive attack subtracts (essence) dice of damage.

      Love the flavour, but the power is a bit too strong. A spammable Charm where you only lose only 40% of your initiative on a Decisive is an Essence 3 effect. Negating all threshold successes on a withering is at least E2.

      Edit :
      After rereading it's not as overpowered as I thought on first impression, though it's still quite strong. Some thoughts :

      -The main issue is that the martial arts has a lot of universally powerful Charms on the cheap. Martial Arts tend to follow a theme, and usually you won't want an MA if you don't share that theme, but this MA's Charms will supplement any style really well. Basically this means this Martial Arts is always the one you want to take as a secondary if your primary Martial Arts is compatible (and this Martial Arts is compatible with 98% of all Martial Arts since it allows Unarmed).

      -Something this strong might work as a dedicated weapon martial arts, in which case you'd need to remove unarmed compatibility

      -The Charm that lets you keep your initiative when you Decisive needs a willpower cost or a per-Scene limit.

              I like the fluff. It's a very fun organization backed by an interesting set of ideals.

              But I don't see the mechanical theme. Most MAs have a "You use me to do this!" that's obvious to them. Single Point wants you to go first and kill people in one super-decisive each. Water Dragon wants you to wear foes down with a stream of small decisives. Ebon Shadow wants you to jump in and out of hiding. I can't see that here. What are these charms supposed to channel the user into doing?
              For example, you've got an Onslaught negator that works when you parry, and charms that soften the blow when you don't parry. Are you supposed to be taking hits or not?

              I also don't get the Mastery effect of the capstone. I understand what it does, but not the narrative way it does it. It feels like Mastery pushes it to an entirely different style, and I don't like that.
