                Originally posted by Tribalgeek View Post
                Does a usage of Melee Excellency to attack also raise Parry Defense Value until his next action? Or do you have to use it for attack or defense.
                Excellencies only enhance a single roll/static value each time they are used.
                ​So you would have to pay separately for every attack you make and every attack you wish to have a higher PDV against.
                ​You don't have to activate it for all attacks in a given flurry through nor do you have to add the same number of dice/successes to every instance.

                Originally posted by Korekore
                If I have a charm that adds dice and a charm that doubles dice, which comes first. For example if I have a charm to add to post soak damage dice and a charm to double post soak damage dice which comes first?
                The charm with the lowest "step" always comes first, non-reflexive charms normally happens during step 1, while reflexive charms specify which step they are activated in.
                As far as I know, there's no official rule either way on what to do if the two charms are activated during the same step.
