            Originally posted by Croakamancer View Post

            Yeah, I know. It makes sense to have multiple things in the queue. If it weren't for Exigents I'd probably be less bitter. Without being able to look inside the Devs heads or actively follow what specifically they're currently working on, it feels like Heirs was the backburner project - especially since an entire splat book will seemingly make it all the way through the process before it arrives (Lunars)
            Something to consider is lead-in.

            The Realm was started around the same time as WFHW. It actually has a lot of the same authors and I think material even might have moved between them. In effect it was written as additional chapters for Dragon-Blooded. That said, note that it didn't come out until after the KS'd book. Because in the end, they were on parralel tracks...one started a bit after the other. ANd one came out after the other.

            No work on Heirs to the Shogunate was started before the KS came out. The devs needed an oultine, final count of rewards, and feedback from backers before things could even get started. So keep this in mind. The Realm and WFHW were more or less bieng worked on for about 18 months when they came out. Heirs didn't really start being written until June (according to MMNs). Since you know, working on finsihing DBs probably took priority still. In this context, we're still kind of well in, but still a ways away. Mind, it looks like it'll take more than 18 months, (no clue where art/alyout is). And in addition, note that when Heirs was being outlined, Lunars probably was already contracted and outlined. Going over the Monday Meeting Notes, FatG first-shows up in April as First Drafts. Note that at this time, Heirs is still probably being outlined. And while Heirs is likely not as big a book asFatG, it is not enough to make it's lead-time much different. See WFHW and The Realm.

            All and all, it's just a matter of first in, first out. Lunars had work starting up pretty much just as DBs was wrapping up. Heirs didn't get booted up until a couple months after that. My guess is that with this timing in mind, Heirs is likely due a bit after Lunars is out. Note that it's the most ahead of the other books too (CoL and At8D's current stages won't affect Lunars much).

            Looking at things we also have Redlines right now for Exigents and Many-Faced Strangers. Which yeah, might slow down Heirs. But note that MFS is also paid-for and something they proably want moving along. And Exigents is something they have a clear temporal deadline theyw anna hit (Q1). It is gonna be annoying that the companion isn't out before the first KS, but in the end, they ahve what looks to be an 18-20 month start-to-finish cycle. THey can't quite do the 9-month turnarounds of 2e anymore due to manpower, resources and other bottlenecks (layout being the big one). It just happens that they overlap in a way that looks this way, mostly.
