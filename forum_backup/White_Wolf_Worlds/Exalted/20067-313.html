                                        Originally posted by Thanatos View Post
                                        How, exactly, does Immortal Soul Vigil work (Occult charm, page 359)? It appears that the user of the charm enters a patient's psyche using Soul Projection Method (meaning the Solar's body falls into a trance), looking for memories of spiritual influence. Upon finding such influence, the Solar's persona in the patient's mind falls into a trance, opens a door in her (the Solar's) mind which allows her avatar to dematerialize to confront the spirit (how the hell the Solar's projected persona inside someone else's mind is considered "materialized," I don't know, but let's go with it). Is this the present-day version of the spirit, or some echo of the spirit in the person's memory? Like, is the Solar altering the memory (which the charm explicitly says she cannot do)? Like, if the Solar is pulling the present-day version of the spirit into the dreamscape of someone they messed with a hundred years ago, that would explain a lot of spirits' dislike for the Solars.

                                        Also, what happens if you're a Twilight and you summon a spirit familiar while you're doing this?
                                        I think how it's supposed to work is once you've identified that there's a spirit preying on your subject by going through their memories, you commit the motes, and then the next time the subject encounters that spirit again, you fall into a trance wherever you are and your dematerialized form appears near the subject so that you can protect them. It basically lets you play invisible guardian angel to someone that's being haunted or fed upon by fae or demons or whatever.

                                        I would think you'd be able to call your spirit familiar as normal when confronting the enemy spirit, although it would probably be dematerialized to everyone else in the scene, just like the character.

                                                    "In addition, whenever the martial artist is hit by an attack, she may spend up to her (Appearance) in motes, adding one point to her armored soak against that attack for each mote spent this way."

                                                    So it seems pretty clear that you can declare this Charm after being hit and before the damage is rolled.                                                     Releasing it's commitment after every use may not be a wise strategy, if you're spending more than 5 motes per turn you won't get a chance to regen that released commitment and will only be adding a one mote cost to every use of the charm.

                                                        Originally posted by Lionmanguy View Post
                                                        So let's say two people are fighting and third person think's they shouldn't be, or is trying to intervene in some way. He could flurry two defend other actions and defend both of them from the other's attack to stop them from hurting each other right? My gut tells me yes if only for cool points
                                                        The situation is uncommon enough that I'd rather just say that you could defend both characters from attacks made by the other than to mess with the implications of being able to flurry two defend other actions. It's probably worthwhile to flurry it with a social influence action to "stand down!" or "stop this foolishness at once!" if you want to bring the fight to an end.

                                                        If your targets are making a clash attack against each other, I could see permitting a third character to intrude (assuming they also had the ability to act on that tick either naturally, by holding their action, or a charm that permitted a reflexive clash) with some kind of custom gambit that split their attack successes evenly between the two opponents as a penalty to their attacks against each other, and permitted the intruding character to take any left over successes from the targets attacks as damage to themselves, as long as they beat the attack rolls of the other two with their initial attack roll. It would make for a great I'm in ur fight, blocking ur attacks moment.
