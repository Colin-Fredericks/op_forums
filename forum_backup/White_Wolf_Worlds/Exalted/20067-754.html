        Originally posted by DrLoveMonkey View Post
        I’m kind of curious about how far Worshipful Lackey Acquisition goes. If you make a big scene at a party during a diplomatic meeting, accomplishing a goal as you do, then the next day can you just order the envoy from Cherak to agree to whatever terms you want?

        I don't think it's quite that powerful. It says that the target "[has] a Principle defining them as one of her acolytes". I'd say that the Solar still has to use the normal social influence system to get the envoy from Cherak to agree to terms, but she's got an Intimacy she can use now, one which is pretty much automatically going to be applicable to "do what I say". Annoyingly, the charm doesn't specify the strength of that Principle. I'd rule that it's Major, mostly because making it Defining is just a bit too powerful for my tastes.

        So, if you make a big scene (remembering, that scene has to be based on some sort of performance, which I take to mean one of the formal styles of rhetoric defined in the Performance Ability. So you have to make a formal speech, engage in structured debate, or thrown down in a rap battle, you can't just make a really cutting remark next to the canapes) at the diplomatic meeting, then next morning you can talk to the envoy from Cherak and ask her to agree to your treaty between your nation and hers. You'll have a major Intimacy to use, so you can get her to agree to a serious task, but her existing Intimacies still apply, so if the treaty is seriously lopsided against Cherak, her positive intimacy towards her own nation will boost her Resolve. And if agreeing to the treaty would lead to the destruction or serious damage to Cherak (as agreeing to a treaty with an Anathema might, to a Realm satrapy), agreeing to it would probably be a life-changing task, and thus not possible with just a Major intimacy. But even if the envoy declines, she'll be very deferential in the process of saying "no".

                                                        Originally posted by Grod_the_giant View Post
                                                        Question: if I built a combat-oriented character with only excellencies and a full set of Evocations from one of the example artifacts, how viable would that be?

                                                        Depends on which example artifact you're talking about - Moonlit Huntress, from the core book, for example, only has a single evocation, and I don't think that's going to make you combat competent. Even of the more fully fleshed-out artifacts, I think you'd probably want a higher-dot one, a 4- or 5-dot artifact, due to the way evocations are generally designed. Remember, a 3-dot artifact's evocations generally supplement an existing skillset or approach, whereas 4-dot ones are more likely to provide a whole style of doing things.

                                                        Even with a 4- or 5-dot artifact, though, you're also going to run into issues where the higher power effects aren't available at low Essence. There's no Supernal ability to take evocations. Of course, you can make combat-capable characters at Essence 1 for splats who don't get Supernal Abilities, so that might be less of a concern for you.
