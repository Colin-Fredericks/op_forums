        HEART EATER (Soulsteel/Moonsilver Reaver Daiklave, Artifact 3 probably)

        This is my first attempt at an artifact (made with Unsung's process)---well, I have two more in the works already (I had a long wait at work and it was raining very hard outside) but this is the one I finished. At least, finished my first pass on---I'm sure this will come out of the thread rather changed for the better.

        Image (a little large):

        Heart Eater was originally just a random reaver daiklave I made for my first homebrew Abyssal and I didn't intend to put Evocations to it. However, looking at this thread, I decided I wanted to try. Since I already named it, I thought about what Heart Eater could be a good name for, and settled on what I have here: a sword designed to facilitate hunting down and slaughtering entire families or groups of boon companions.

        Backstory:
        Degara of the Twilight Caste was noted both as a brilliant sorcerer and a terribly strong swordsman. His Lunar bondmate was not his lover but his deepest friend, a masterful weaponsmith named Eight Shining Eyes; the spider-woman was planning on constructing Degara a wedding present in the form of a great chopping sword of orichalcum and moonsilver (perhaps that she might not be totally forgotten once he was settled in with his husband---Eight Shining Eyes was said to be a little jealous, after all). She had the moonsilver edge ready to be enfolded with sun-metal when things, as they do, went bad.

        Raincloud Breath, a woman of ghostly blood from a family of death-royalty, killed Degara’s husband-to-be, but her gauntlets of soulsteel were wrested from her in the struggle and she had to flee before she could reclaim them. Mad with grief, Degara brought the gauntlets to Eight Shining Eyes and demanded she use this terrible metal for his sword. It could not be his wedding present, so it would be his instrument of revenge. The Lunar hesitated, but set to work reforging the strange, horrid material. Degara’s brilliance came in handy, as she needed his help to unravel the necromantic processes necessary to get soulsteel to behave properly. The metal drank in his grief and fury as he stood over the smith, and she shuddered when it was done---the thing may not have been quite evil, but it was certainly rank with the taint of wretched intent.

        Degara killed the son Raincloud Breath sent after him, and the next two. Every time, it got easier---the sword seemingly leapt straight into their flesh to cleave and tear. When Raincloud Breath sent her children and husbands into hiding, he hunted them down. Finally he found her---he, drunk on the empty high of ten-thousand-eyes-for-an-eye; she, stripped of her family and power. When he finished cleaning her gore from the blade, he walked into the land of the dead to find his husband and never came back. Perhaps they had their union. Perhaps his husband saw what Degara had become and could not face him any more.


        ATTUNEMENT: For +1 extra mote in attunement, Heart Eater allows its wielder to add one non-charm die to attack rolls (withering or decisive) against a target who the wielder has, through Read Intentions or some other means, determined to be the subject of a Tie Intimacy of love (familial, platonic, romantic, whatever), deep friendship, or true admiration. These Intimacies are hence referred to as Bonds within the context of this Artifact and its Evocations.

        TASTING THE RED STRING
        Cost: 1m; Mins: Essence 1
        Type: Supplemental
        Keywords: Decisive-Only
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisites: None
        Heart Eater tastes, in blood and in steel, the ties that bind. It is always looking for these vulnerabilities.
        On landing a successful decisive attack or parrying a decisive attack (including in a Clash), the wielder may make a reflexive Read Intentions roll with the intent of discovering Bonds. Damage exceeding target Guile is added to the Read Intentions roll as dice.
        ((Is this too narrow? Should Withering attacks also be included?))

        CUTTING GROOVES IN THE HEART
        Cost: 5m 1wp (+3m/1m); Mins: Essence 1
        Type: Supplemental
        Keywords: Decisive-Only
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisites: Tasting the Red String
        Heart Eater’s moonsilver edge is a living, hating thing; when it has felt the ties within somebody it makes itself ever sharper in order to tear them apart. When it snuffs out their life, it reaches in and steals the love from their heart, carefully scribing a hit list in their cooling blood.
        After discovering a Bond in a target, the wielder may use this to enhance a decisive attack made at 15+ Initiative. It converts damage dice to successes equal to the positive value of the highest Bond known in the target.
        This Evocation has a secondary effect. On a decisive attack that kills (whether enhanced by this Evocation or not), the wielder may pay 3m to absorb (number) Bonds from the target. If the primary effect of this Evocation was used to enhance the killing blow, it instead costs only 1m.

        SCENTING TIES IN BLOOD
        Cost: 3m; Mins: Essence 2
        Type: Simple
        Keywords: None
        Duration: Indefinite
        Prerequisites: Cutting Grooves in the Heart
        In wracking, red-tinged heartbeats of visions, Heart Eater tells its wielder where to find the next target. When it tastes them in the air, it nearly shivers to get at their flesh.
        Having discovered a Bond, the wielder may commit motes to this Evocation, gaining the Bond’s value plus 1 in non-Charm bonus dice to all rolls to discover the location of the Bond’s subject. In addition, whenever in the target's presence, they are alerted to this fact.

        HEART EATER TRIUMPHANT
        Cost: 5m, 1wp; Mins: Essence 3
        Type: Supplemental
        Keywords: Decisive-Only
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisites: Scenting Ties in Blood
        The satisfaction of having murdered one person drives Heart Eater to bloodthirsty glee when it is about to taste the blood of the first victim’s son, lover, friend---its edge becomes a narrow sea of ripping teeth.
        This Evocation may be used when knowingly attacking a character who is the subject of a Bond held by someone the wielder killed with this weapon. It adds the Bond value to the damage dicepool of a decisive attack, whose damage becomes Aggravated; if the target reciprocated the Bond, the value on their end (which may or may not be the same as the Bond’s value from the dead person’s end) is converted to automatic successes.
        ((Is this too much, or too little? I really don’t know. I thought it was too little, so I added the automatic success bit; if it’s too much and I should only keep one of those, which should I keep?))

                Phalange of Omnibu’Shen
                Relic Wrackstaff (Longfang or Direlance works too), Artifact (·····)
                This staff carved from the blackened glass finger bone of Omnibu’Shen, The Keeper of Words of Power, Fallen Fetch Soul of He Who Bleeds the Unknown Word. The base of the stave is shod in starmetal etched with a single sigil of power, and capped with a pair of orchalcum prods, and a floating adamant sphere suspended between them. Though impressive as the staff is the most catching thing is the moonsilver filagree along the shaft telling a story in old realm of a clever lunar that tricked Omnibu’Shen with a riddle so complex and arcane that it drove him mad in the process of solving it. When he finally solved the riddle he spoke aloud the answer, that was the moment he realized it was a trap. The answer was an inversion curse to reverse the fated outcome of the speakers next action. Some say the curse was the cause of his speech, that it twisted his essence inverting its soul hierarchy, turning every aspect of himself upon each other. Those that wield the stave claim it speaks in furious screams heard as if from a great distance and offers them hallucinations from deep within it’s primordial mind. Sometimes from the moment of death where all the words of power escaped from his skull at once, other times sigils, glyphs, and symbols overwhelmly poured across their vision and minds. These visions often come without warning and instill a an unspeakable rage within the wielder. However each image, word, and glyph imparts primordial power to the wielder reshaping their understanding of the foundations of creation.

                Sidebar - Thematics and Function

                Evocations of the Phalange of Omnibu’Shen follow several themes including words and language, rage, madness, power, curses, deep understanding and contemplative enlightenment. The Shaping Rituals, Mysteries, and Absorptions tap into both his wisdom and insight as well as the insanity of Omnibu’Shen. A few Evocations and Shaping Rituals tap into themes and concepts from He Who Bleeds the Unknown Word, as well as hint at his reincarnated as Elloge the Sphere of Speech.

                She may spend 3 additional motes while attuning to the Omnibu’shen grants 1 point of syntax on a successful withering or decisive attack. In addition she gains 1 non-charm die to all forms of silent communication, powers that depict symbols, glyphs, sigils, per tier of sorcery mastered. Evocations of Omnibu’Shen

                Transubstantiation of the Furious Soul

                Cost: 6m, 1wp; Mins: Essence 1
                Type: Simple
                Keywords: Perilous
                Duration: One Scene
                Prerequisites: Terrestrial Circle Sorcery Bleeding the unbound potential of their own exalted soul into the interface of Omnibu’Shen it swells with arcane syntax. On any turn in which the Solar wields Phalange of Omnibu’Shen in combat she may convert 1 point of initiative into 1 sorcerous mote, upto a maximum of her essence+1 per turn these motes are referred to as syntax(nx). If the wielder joins battle with a higher initiative than all opponents this evocation may be activated reflexively. Special Activation Rules: Rather than being bought with experience, Transubstantiation of the Furious Soul is learned after first time the wielder gains a crash break in combat.
                Sidebar - Syntax(nx)
                Sorcerers wielding Omnibu’Shen in combat have a number of ways of generating and storing energy into the interface, the adamant sphere capping the stave, this energy is called Syntax(nx). The staff can store up to the attuned sorcerers Intelligence + Occult in syntax a time and absorbing into the interface after a scene. Syntax can be converted into sorcerous motes reflexively on a 2:1 bases or be spent to power its evocations as a 1:1.
                Burning Sigil Strike
                Cost: 2m, 2nx; Mins: Essence 1
                Type: Supplemental
                Keywords: Uniform
                Duration: Instant
                Prerequisites: Transubstantiation of the Furios Soul By channeling syntax through the core of the Phalange the sigils on the shaft burn with a brilliant crimson light pulsing at the striking plane. This evocation is activated after the attack is rolled but before damage is applied, increasing the minimum damage by the sorcerers tier of sorcerous initiation. Oeuvre of Inversion

                Cost: 4m, 4nx; Mins: Essence 1
                Type: Simple
                Keywords: None
                Duration: One Scene
                Prerequisites: Transubstantiation of the Furious Soul Blinding the shinma of separation and the principle of identity into a blurred glyph within the interface she breaks the lines of reason and comprehension to all who would act upon her. Anyone attempting to take action on the wielder or staff suffers a shaping effect that reduces their potential, lowering their maximum natural dice pools by the sorcerers tier of sorcerous initiation +1. This does not affect static numbers such as defense or resolve only rolled effects. Trembling Lip Curse

                Cost: 1nx per attack; Mins: Essence 1
                Type: Permanent
                Keywords: Uniform
                Duration: Instant
                Prerequisites: Transubstantiation of the Furious Soul Her mind erupts in a cascade of forgotten pain as fragment of Omnibu’Shens death curse escapes from her lips. The words explode from her throat in a horrifying death scream, allowing her to make attacks against against enemies with the stats of the Phalange removing the default accuracy bonus and adding the thrown (short) tag. Shaping Rituals

                The relic of Omnibu’Shen resonates with world shaping power. Those that attune to it and gaze into the sphere experience a terrible enlightenment as the fragment of Omnibu’Shen’s soul that still survives in its heart, speaks primordial words of power blasting open the doors of her soul. To the wielder, the world is redefined as an arbitrary system of of interacting symbols that she can manipulate through its syntax. These sorcerers tend to see everything in terms of symbols and communication: map and territory, call and response, rhetoric and games. Sitting in silent contemplation of the complexity of the laws that govern creation, sorcery, and its primordial creator. Her will is eroded away as she loses herself in the endless diagrams, sigils, and equations burning a willpower, she rolls (Intelligence + Occult) gaining one syntax per success. Her caste mark acts as the sorcerers own artificial interface storing the syntax until she casts them in a spell or her anima lights up to the bonfire level. These syntax can be spent for spellcasting at 1:1. Writing a sutra about Omnibu’Shen and the fate of the written word the sorcerer expands the mythos of the fallen primordial soul. This takes no less than one hour and at the culmination of the ritual she consumes the writing and internalizes the story tuning the words into a prayer to herself and the staff. She rolls ((Charisma or Manipulation) + Linguistics) each success grants 1 sorcerous mote that lasts for up to a week. When the sorcerer takes the first shape sorcery action to begin casting a spell and stunts it with a description of how she casts the spell through the Phalange or draws on its power, she gains (stunt rating + 2) sorcerous motes towards completing this spell. This benefit can only be received once per scene. Stunts to enhance spells with spoken or written components do not count against the once per scene limit. Mysteries (Other Benefits)

                Inculcate the Unknown Word Merit (··)

She may teach this language to any that gaze into the interface of Omnibu’Shen willingly. Semantics of Sorcery Merit (····)

                Initiates of Omnibu’Shen intuitively understand the syntax of creations schinmatic underpinnings and the sorceries derived from it. By studying sorceries she may invent new words of power based on the root meaning though changing the overall structure of their manifestation. Allowing the sorcerer to invent new version of the spells she already knows. She may changing one aspect of its manifestation such as the shape of its area, manifested substance or elemental nature, or point of origin. The spell costs the same amount of sorcerous motes and cannot be more powerful than the base spell. The experience cost and training time to invent and learn the new spell is half the normal amount. Absorbing Shinmatic Syntax Merit (···)

                Those that witness the mind of the Omnibu’Shen and absorb his maddening vision also touch upon his enlightened understanding of the complexity of sorceries. By witnessing a sorcerous spell of working of a circle in which she can normally attain she gains an immediate understanding of what she witnessed. This grants the sorcerer the ability to learn any spell she has witnessed by parsing out the structure of the spell costing the normal amount but taking twice the training time. In addition the difficulty to detect and discern the location of any sorcerous working is lowered by 1 to a minimum of 1.

                    So I worked on this for a player of mine in a game that's stalled out a little bit - either way, I figure it doesn't hurt anything to post it and see if there are any tweaks that need to be made. I liked the idea a fair bit, but tunnel vision and all that.

                    What She Carried (Artifact 5 Soulsteel/Red Jade Dragon Sigh Wand)

                    Once there was a woman…
                    …who lost everything in the fires.
                    Then she found her breath again and went on a journey.
                    There she became strong - undeniably.
                    She practiced kindness - in a manner of speaking.
                    She even became wise - from a certain point of view.
                    But she never stopped being angry.

                    One day, she learned who had started that old fire.
                    So she went to meet him again.
                    (But not before she took some things with her.)
                    A little jade from a burnt-up forest, for warmth.
                    A piece of steel, cold and black as the wicked thing’s heart.
                    And inside that piece of steel?
                    Forty-three people, just as angry as she was.
                    (And only a little more dead.)

                    She put a little piece of herself into the thing she made.
                    Every artist does, of course.
                    But only some of them cut it off and burn it first.
                    So when she showed up and said to that old wyrm -
                    - “Old wyrm, do you know me?”
                    It didn’t know her.
                    But it knew she meant business.
                    (That’s why it breathed flame all over her.)

                    She just laughed at that,
                    right through her blackened lips.
                    “You can’t kill me that way,” she said,
                    “I’ve been burning my whole life.”
                    And it was true.
                    And she killed the wyrm, then and there.
                    But not before it killed her dead.
                    (Only a little more dead than it had already killed her,
                    but that was enough.)

                    She didn’t say anything when it was done.
                    (You know, being a little more dead and all.)
                    She just blew away on the wind -
                    - with the rest of the ashes.
                    But first she set that thing she’d made down.
                    “Someone else might need it,” she didn’t say.
                    (“There’s always an ending,” she also didn’t say.
                    Because sometimes -

                    What She Carried is a thick, angular thing of soulsteel, half again the length of a human’s forearm. The jade used in its construction was crushed into flecks and worked into an alloy with the metal, catching the light as red and orange gleams. Sculpted to resemble a dragon like similar weapons of its caliber, the weapon’s twin barrels sit side by side in the dragon’s empty eye sockets, allowing its wielder to fire both before the weapon’s firedust reservoir is exhausted.

                    Statistics
                    Accuracy +1/+5/+3 (close/short/medium range; the last only through magic); Damage 18; Overwhelming 5; Attunement 5
                    Tags: Lethal, Archery (Short), Flame, Slow (two shots before reloading)

                    Evocations of What She Carried
                    A Solar, Abyssal, or Dragon-Blood who attunes to What She Carried adds one bonus die to all threaten actions, as well as any attempts to inspire fear, despair, or similar emotions. This bonus increases by one for every ashen health level she possesses.


                    Flesh of Ash
                    Cost: 2m or 1m; Mins: Essence 1
                    Type: Reflexive
                    Keywords: Decisive-only, Stackable
                    Duration: One scene or until healed
                    Prerequisite Charms: None

                    What She Carried drinks in the agony its fires cause, inflicting that same pain on its wielder to give them the vitality of a thing already burned. Upon making a successful decisive attack, the character may pay this Evocation’s cost to create an ashen health level, the ash and lingering smoke from her shot clinging to her and cinders burning within her injuries. Ashen health levels are temporary -0 health levels, which take damage before any other health levels and may be created even if the character has taken damage to their -1 health levels or further. The character may stack Flesh of Ash up to (Essence) times, more and more ash and smoke gathering about her, although she may only create one ashen health level per decisive attack. This Evocation costs 2 motes, or 1 mote if the attack killed or destroyed a non-trivial opponent. Ashen health levels not filled with damage expire at the end of the scene; damaged ashen health levels must be healed before the Charm’s commitment can be lifted and the ash fades.

                    White-Hot Hateful Heart
                    Cost: 6m 2i; Mins: Essence 2
                    Type: Reflexive
                    Keywords: Counterattack, Perilous
                    Duration: Until next turn
                    Prerequisite Charms: Flesh of Ash

                    Rage bursts forth in a thick heat haze as What She Carried glows like a forge-fresh iron. Until the start of the character’s next turn, anyone who hits her with an attack at close range takes one die of lethal damage, ignoring hardness. This damage is also applied to anyone grappling with her while the heat flare lasts. When this damage is applied, she may optionally make a special disarm gambit as a counterattack, which does not require an attack roll and receives bonus dice on the Initiative roll equal to the number of ashen health levels she possesses. A successful gambit superheats the attacker’s weapon, forcing them to choose between suffering a second die of lethal damage or dropping the weapon at their feet. Against natural weapons, it instead forces the choice of a second die of lethal damage or taking a -2 penalty to all their dice rolls from the intense pain, which lasts for the remainder of the scene and does not stack with subsequent uses of this Charm.

                    Special activation rules: The character must have at least one ashen health level to use this Evocation.

                    Self As Kindling
                    Cost: 1 ahl; Mins: Essence 2
                    Type: Reflexive
                    Keywords: None
                    Duration: Until next turn
                    Prerequisite Charms: Flesh of Ash

                    The pain of self-immolation drives all lesser frailties from the mind. Until the start of the character’s next turn, she ignores all her wound penalties. In order to use this Evocation, she must have an ashen health level which is either empty or filled with bashing or lethal damage, which becomes filled with aggravated damage as the glowing cinders filling her wounds roar into full flame. If she burns an uninjured ashen health level in this way, the surge of power as her body is consumed restores three motes to her.

                    Fuel for the Pyre
                    Cost: 5m, 1wp; Mins: Essence 3
                    Type: Reflexive
                    Keywords: Counterattack, Decisive-only, Perilous
                    Duration: Instant
                    Prerequisite Charms: Self as Kindling, White-Hot Hateful Heart

                    The wraith of the ashes drags its enemies down with it into Oblivion - or claws its way out over their corpses. If the character would be incapacitated or killed by a decisive attack, she may make a decisive counterattack before the result of the damage roll is applied to her health track. If her counterattack inflicts damage, she may use Flesh of Ash as many times as she wishes to add ashen health levels before the damage from the triggering attack is dealt to her, potentially saving her from death. Should she kill or destroy her opponent with the counterattack, she still takes the damage from the triggering attack, but the number of ashen health levels she may have at once is raised by one for the scene, and she receives an ashen health level at no cost.

                    Candle-Soul Conflagration
                    Cost: 10m 1wp; Mins: Essence 4
                    Type: Simple
                    Keywords: None
                    Duration: One scene
                    Prerequisite Charms: Fuel for the Pyre

                    At the heart of What She Carried are fourscore and four souls who have known what it is to burn utterly. The weapon’s master may turn it on herself to partake of those deaths, drowning in its roaring flame. When she emerges, she has become a creature of cinder and ash, burning brighter and hotter with each life she takes.

                    On the turn she uses Candle-Soul Conflagration, White-Hot Hateful Heart activates for free as the heat of her transformation scorches all who dare approach. At the start of her next turn, the flames explode outward to reveal her new form - all her flesh burnt to ash, falling away in the hot winds that dance around her, and a dreadful firelight glowing within the cracks in her immolated skin. The earth scorches where her feet fall, and if her anima flares, its light is the dancing orange of a wildfire, shot through with rising smoke.

                    While she bears this dreadful curse, the other Evocations of What She Carried are enhanced:
                    • Flesh of Ash: This Charm may now be stacked a number of times equal to the character’s total health levels. Temporary health levels added by Charms such as Iron Skin Concentration do not raise this limit, but those from Permanent Charms such as Ox-Body Technique do.
                    • White-Hot Hateful Heart: Mundane weapons disarmed by this Charm are instead destroyed, burning away or melting into slag. The penalty applied to an attacker using a natural weapon increases to -3 and does not automatically heal at the end of the scene; special care may be required to restore the injury.
                    • Self as Kindling: Wound penalties negated by this Charm are applied as bonus dice to the character’s attack rolls while it lasts, as well as any social influence rolls to which the attunement bonus applies.
                    • Fuel for the Pyre: This Charm loses the Perilous keyword and may be used in Initiative Crash, explicitly allowing a Crashed character to make a decisive counterattack and giving her counterattack a raw damage equal to her base Initiative. A character in Crash who misses on the counterattack loses 2 Initiative as though she had missed a normal decisive attack, while a successful counterattack does not cause her to reset to base Initiative.

                    Should she die while so transformed, her body crumbles into cold, grey ash, only to be carried off by the wind. All that remains of her is What She Carried and any other similarly durable belongings.

                    Special activation rules: The character must possess at least four ashen health levels, at least one of which is damaged, to use this Evocation.

                        THIN-SKINNED COURTESAN (Soulsteel/Black Jade Grand Goremaul, Artifact... •••? Maybe ••••?)

                        Backstory:
                        All-Appraising Dayu was a Zenith Caste, a songstress and courtesan above all others. She had charmed godly crafters into constructing for her an instrument like an overlarge sanxian taller than she was; this instrument became popular for its soulful tones and spawned many imitators, and the oversized class of instrument is still called a dayu to this day. It has gone through cycles of obscurity and popularity; the Empress herself was not known to like them very much, but following her disappearance they may be coming back into vogue.

                        All-Appraising Dayu was also known---though never to her perfect face---as Thin-Skinned Dayu. She took insults badly, and was well-known to bring retribution upon those who slighted her. When the man she desired was known to love another woman, she was driven to madness with jealousy and spite. Making a gracious offer to play at the man’s wedding feast, she brought her well-tuned instrument and played a song that set the guests to murderous frenzy; the walls dripped blood as the ceiling licked with fire and Dayu laughed. Seeing her desired man try to protect his wife only drove Dayu to further fury, and she slew them both by cracking their heads with the instrument.

                        Her dayu broken, the still-fuming Solar was not done. She called up darkly-muttering gods of the forge and journeyed to the Underworld itself to exact favors from ghostly smith-lords. The souls of the wedding party she had gathered up and pounded agonizingly, combined with strange dark ore, into soulsteel. Along with fine black jade, this was used to build her an instrument that would never break from being used to crack a few skulls. Its strings were behemothgut wrapped in fine strands of soulsteel; across its great resonator-box was stretched the skin of a demon-snake.

                        After Dayu was strangled in bed with her weapon’s own string, the accursed instrument inherited her moniker. Thin-Skinned Courtesan popped up a lot of places over the centuries; a No Moon once won it from a Deathlord, a god of assassins once had it played while a whole room of Air Immaculates utterly failed to assassinate him, and the Scarlet Empress herself was said to have chucked it out a window (she was heard to have said she found soulsteel to be a revolting material befitting Anathema and the foul lords of the dead, but a rumor persists that she found the instrument frustratingly difficult to play)


                        Thin-Skinned Courtesan is a sort of oversized sanxian about six feet tall. Its body is constructed primarily of soulsteel and its strings are soulsteel-wrapped behemoth-gut; its resonator is clad in the darkly iridescent skin of a demon-snake. Its fingerboard and tuning-pegs are black jade, and black jade panels are set into its resonator with inlaid mother-of-pearl designs. The whole thing combines fearful instrument and massive warhammer in one terribly lovely whole.

                        When attuned for one extra mote, attacks on foes who have been socially influenced toward negative emotion in the current scene reroll one non-successful Withering damage die. It also counts as an Exceptional instrument and grants one bonus die on Performance rolls that make use of its throaty tones.
                        However, Thin-Skinned Courtesan is a fussy artifact and will not accept attunement from any character who does not meet the following requirements: Performance ••, Presence or Linguistics •, Socialize ••, Appearance ••. Curiously, it will happily accept the attunement of a character with the Hideous merit; perhaps it feels that being strikingly awful is as good as being strikingly lovely.
                        TAGS: Melee (Heavy), Bashing, Reaching, Smashing, Two-Handed

                        EVOCATIONS

                        SAFETY IN SORROW
                        Cost: 2m or 4m; Mins: Essence 1
                        Type: Reflexive
                        Keywords: Psyche
                        Duration: Instant
                        Prerequisites: None
                        As they turn aside the slingstones and spearpoints of foes with their instrument of murder, the wielder may strike a doleful chord and utter a few words that cut to the heart. The sudden wash of despair may cause foes’ weapons to feel a little heavier and clumsier in their hands.
                        When defending against an attack, this Evocation allows the user to reflexively make a Performance-based social influence attempt against the attacker (so long as they are within Short range); the attempt must seek to instill a Principle of melancholy and futility. For an additional cost of 2 more motes, the user may add the threshold successes of the Performance roll over Resolve to their Defense, to a maximum addition of (Essence + 2).
                        [[Reflexively make a Performance attempt to make people sad when you Defend against their attacks, and if you spend more Essence you increase your Defense by how well your Performance roll exceeded their Resolve.]]


                        ARISE, O WRETCH
                        Cost: 6m; Mins: Essence 2
                        Type: Supplemental
                        Keywords: Decisive-Only, Psyche
                        Duration: Instant
                        Prerequisites: Safety In Sorrow
                        Thin-Skinned Courtesan is not done with you once it bashes your brains out---its wielder may pluck a stream of notes to wrest you, wailing, from your battered carcass. You had best hope your loved ones are not on the battlefield, lest they join you.
                        Upon killing a target, this Evocation may be used to wrench their Hun soul into ghostly and visible form, where they are termed a Mournful Ghost. They have a magical Defining Principle of futility and melancholy. This creates a Performance-based social attempt (out to Medium range) to instill melancholy; characters who held a Major positive Tie toward the deceased reduce Resolve by 1. For Defining ties, 2. The Performance roll rerolls away all 1s.

                        Mournful Ghosts are bound to the wielder for a scene; they may have (Essence) ghosts bound at a time. They can be used in the two following Evocations, but can be used in other ways. Sorcerous or Necromantic Shaping Rituals that involve sacrificing bound souls for sorcerous/necromantic motes can make use of Mournful Ghosts. Additionally, the Twilight and Daybreak Anima Power to bind spirits to oneself may be used at the end of the scene by the wielder or another with the wielder’s permission; the Mournful Ghosts return to their natural ghost state, downgrading their magical Defining Principle to a Minor one, as well as having a Major Tie of fear toward the wielder. Mournful Ghosts who are not consumed or bound return to their senses at the end of the scene, retaining the Minor Principle of melancholy and Major Tie of fear as stated above.
                        [[When you kill someone, turn them into a mournful ghost and bind them to you; you can use them to power later Evocations or for other stuff, and just seeing it happen bums everyone out, especially if they liked the person you killed.]]


                        TWICE BEREAVED, ONCE BITTEN
                        Cost: 5m, 1 Mournful Ghost; Mins: Essence 2
                        Type: Supplemental
                        Keywords: Withering-Only
                        Duration: Instant
                        Prerequisites: Arise O Wretch
                        A wailing wraith may be torn apart before his comrade’s eyes, slowing his limbs against the crushing swings of Thin-Skinned Courtesan.
                        This Evocation enhances a Withering attack by spending motes and consuming a Mournful Ghost. The wielder rolls their Performance and converts Withering damage dice equal to the Performance successes into automatic damage successes. If the target had a Major or Defining Tie to the now-consumed Mournful Ghost, the wielder rerolls away all 1s and 4s on the accuracy roll.
                        [[Consume a Mournful Ghost and convert a few Withering damage dice to autosuccesses; if the target liked the Ghost you just consumed you get to reroll some of your accuracy dice.]]


                        ACCEPTANCE OF DESPAIR
                        Cost: 5m, 1 Mournful Ghost; Mins: Essence 3
                        Type: Supplemental
                        Keywords: Decisive-Only
                        Duration: Instant
                        Prerequisites: Twice Bereaved Once Bitten
                        Seeing their friends or loved ones killed and then destroyed as ghosts, along with hearing the soul-throttling tones of the great sanxian’s lugubrious voice, is enough to make a warrior want to lay down and accept death---even if the urge lasts only a moment, that moment is a fatal hesitation when Thin-Skinned Courtesan’s wielder wishes you to fall.
                        This Evocation enhances a Decisive attack by spending motes and consuming a Mournful Ghost. The wielder reflexively rolls a Performance-based influence attempt to instill melancholy; if the target had a Tie to the now-consumed Mournful Ghost, the influence attempt gains bonus dice equal to the value of the Intimacy. Threshold successes over Resolve are subtracted from the target’s Defense, and the damage roll adds three dice.
                        [[Consume a Mournful Ghost and make a Performance attempt against the target of your Decisive attack, which gets bonus dice depending on how much they liked the Ghost; the better you do, the more their Defense gets reduced, and your damage roll has three bonus dice.]]


                        As with Heart Eater before it, I'm sure I've got cost or effect scaling problems. I actually realized early on that my original direction was too similar to Pyre-Kindler (Soulsteel/Red Jade Grimscythe like 20+ pages back) and then remembered that Black Jade has the capability to do stuff with spirits. Thus we got the Mournful Ghost mechanic!

                        I hope I was able to make each Evocation useful without needing the following Evocations---I'll admit that Arise, O Wretch might be a little less useful if you don't have the followups yet and can't make use of the Mournful Ghosts for a Shaping Ritual or the Twilight Anima, since at that point it basically just makes more people subject to the modest attunement benefit of getting to reroll one damage nonsuccess on a Withering attack. With the full four Evocations the idea's basically to bum people out so you can a little more easily build up Initiative, use that Initiative on Decisives to kill people and turn them into Mournful Ghosts (thus bumming more people out and making it easier to Wither on them a little), who you then spend to better kill more people and turn them into ghosts until you run out of people to beat into foie gras.
                        Needs toning down evidently. Check back later.

                                    Beans

                                    These effects are much much too strong for Artifact 3, and maybe too good for artifact 4. The first evocation alone is wildly powerful compared to any of the first evocations that you'll see in the core book for artifact 3 or 4 weapons. Along with the ability to deal pretty massive withering damage using the mournful souls + additional intimacies, I'd be inclined to say that this is artifact 4 / 5.

                                    Something to consider about adding defense or subtracting defense is that, functionally, they act as an automatic success, reducing your barrier to hitting as well as increasing your total damage, or preventing damage to you all together. That is extremely, extremely powerful, and it seems to be thrown around in evocations sets without a lot of thought for their mechanics. Artifact 3 weapons do things like "you can roll a read intentions when you hit:" and "add one die of damage." They are not "Decrease their defense by 3, grants a number of withering autosuccesses". Something to note is that a withering autosuccess isn't mentioned in the melee charmset until Immortal Blade Triumphant. it is an extremely, extremely powerful effect.
