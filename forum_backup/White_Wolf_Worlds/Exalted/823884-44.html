        I just finished writing up a pair of artifacts, both meant for the same character, and I would love some feedback on the first one here. I'm not currently in any Exalted games, but I'm still hoping to use them some time. The original concept for the first artifact was an improvised artifact weapon. I put some commentary after each evocation in a spoiler. I hope that clarifies some of my design decisions.

        I want to keep this first one at artifact 3. I think it may be too powerful, so I'd definitely appreciate any help toning it down where appropriate. Keep in mind that, as an improvised weapon, this is used with Brawl.

        The Kraken's Nail (Black Jade and Orichalcum Anchor, artifact 3)
        Originally the anchor for an artifact ship from an age past, he Kraken's Nail has long since broken off from the ship proper. Once, it manipulated gravity to become incomparably heavy, anchoring its ship to the seabed without fail. Now, its terrible powers lend itself to its new purpose as a weapon, cumbersome but powerful.

        Legend has it the patron god of the ship on which the Kraken's Nail originally served was given power and independence, and was the one who decided to separate anchor from ship. Sometimes called Captain Cegus, the formerly-patron god went on his own adventures, wielding the anchor as a weapon against the monsters of the deep.

        Yet there are more legends, saying the ship once attached to the Kraken's Nail sunk at sea, picked at by scavengers until only the anchor remained, its gravitic powers preventing anyone from moving it...until the right woman came along. Stories give her many different names, but most agree she was a shining golden champion. The anchor responded to her touch as she was diving in the waters, allowing itself to be wielded as a weapon...of sorts.

        One final legend says the ship meant for the Kraken's Nail was actually never finished, leaving the anchor adrift in the world, used as a weapon since it lacks any other purpose. Scholars agree this is the most likely out of all the legends surrounding the Kraken's Nail, if only out of fear of imagining the kind of ship that would go along with something so terribly powerful.

        The Kraken's Nail is a stockless anchor over six feet long, and more than a match for a grand daiklave in weight. Around the arms and lower shank of the anchor is an emblem of an octopus, made from orichalcum filigree. Most of the rest is made of black jade. It still has the ring, along with a small length of orichalcum chain. It has no hearthstone sockets.

        The Kraken's Nail is a heavy artifact weapon, so heavy in fact that attuning it requires a feat of strength (and thus Strength 3+). Its tags are: Bashing, Improvised, Reaching, Smashing, Two-Handed.

        Evocations of the Kraken's Nail
        The Kraken's Nail is about as merciful as the rip current. A decisive attack against a crashed target gains bonus dice equal to the initiative spent on the tags (i.e., improvised and smashing) and evocations of the Kraken's Nail, up to three.

        This has the potential to be a very powerful and efficient bonus, hopefully balanced out by the fact it requires spending some initiative. Trading initiative for mote efficiency will be a running theme with this artifact. For reference, the maximum initiative that can be spent on one attack with this artifact is 9, and it is balanced with that in mind.


        Vandal's Tide
        Cost: 4m, 1i; Mins: Essence 1; Type: Supplemental
        Keywords: Decisive-Only
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisites: None

        The Kraken's Nail shatters all who oppose it. When the wielder makes a decisive attack with the Kraken's Nail, she may roll all initiative spent on the tags (i.e., improvised and smashing) and evocations of the Kraken's Nail as a special disarm gambit if she hits. This gambit does not require another attack, costs no further initiative, and is made at difficulty 2 as if it had the Disarm tag. If successful, the wielder may choose to destroy a non-artifact weapon or shield instead of disarming it. The attack enhancing function of this evocation may be used once per fight, reset by gaining initiative break.

        Alternatively, this charm may be used in a feat of strength to break an object using the Kraken's Nail, adding two non-charm successes as if the Kraken's Nail was appropriate for breaking the object in question.

        The power of disarming someone is hopefully balanced out by the reset condition and its reliance on spending lots of initiative. I want to keep the current reset condition so it can synch up with the final evocation.



        Crashing Metal Tsunami
        Cost: 3m, 1i; Mins: Essence 1; Type: Supplemental
        Keywords: Dual
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisites: None

        The Kraken's Nail crashes through defenses like a tsunami through a sand castle. On a withering attack, for each initiative spent on the tags (i.e., improvised and smashing) and evocations of the Kraken's Nail the wielder may transfer one extra success to the post-soak damage instead of the raw damage. On a decisive attack, for every three such initiative, round down, the wielder may convert one die of damage into an automatic success, and the damage is made lethal.

        Designed to make the Kraken's Nail more useful against the likes of Octavian or artifact heavy armor users. Getting hit by an anchor should always hurt! Like all other Kraken's Nail evocations, it is heavily dependent on spending initiative, so it is mote efficient.



        Gravity Squall
        Cost: 5m, 1i; Mins: Essence 1; Type: Supplemental
        Keywords: Uniform
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisites: None

        A swing of the Kraken's Nail changes the direction of gravity, sending the target tumbling through the world. For every three initiative spent on the tags (i.e., improvised and smashing) and evocations of the Kraken's Nail, round down, the target is knocked back one range band in any direction, landing prone. If they collide with an obstacle that could reasonably stop them, they take falling damage appropriate for the remaining distance. When knocking a target straight up, reduce the distance by one range band.

        This evocation overrides other knockback effects, including the smashing tag and Heaven Thunder Hammer. Normally, this evocation cannot do more than knock down an opponent significantly larger than the wielder. Combining this evocation with Heaven Thunder Hammer or similar magic, however, allows the wielder to knock back prodigiously large targets such as tyrant lizards.

        Special Activation Rules: Gravity Squall may only be awakened after incapacitating a non-trivial opponent with a smashing attack using the Kraken's Nail. The target must not have had any damage in their health track beforehand.

        I don't like Heaven Thunder Hammer. I don't like how powerful it is, and I think it takes too many paragraphs to explain its effects. I designed this charm as a hopefully more reasonable alternative for my character, without obsoleting Heaven Thunder Hammer completely. It requires some shenanigans to unlock, as it's at least a little bit more powerful than the other two essence 1 evocations.



        Looming Iron Surge
        Cost: 5m, 1i; Mins: Essence 2; Type: Supplemental
        Keywords: Dual
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisites: Vandal's Tide, Crashing Metal Tsunami, Gravity Squall

        With an ominous hum, the Kraken's Nail uses its gravitic powers to magnify its weight, becoming simply too heavy for anyone to counter its momentum. If the initiative spent on the tags (i.e., improvised and smashing) and evocations of the Kraken's Nail is equal to or greater than the parry defense of the target of a withering or decisive attack the attack becomes unblockable. Otherwise, their parry suffers a -2 penalty.

        The momentum of one attack carries through to the next. If the attack was decisive and either the target was incapacitated or accepted a crippling injury, the wielder gains two initiative if she dealt 1-10 decisive damage, or three if she dealt 11+, including damage prevented by accepting a crippling injury. This also includes any damage added by Gravity Squall.

        It should be hard to parry something so heavy. Unblockable is a very rare property for a good reason, so I hope this evocation costs enough and requires enough initiative spent to compensate. The second paragraph is there so it's not totally useless against people who use dodge, or have low defenses in the first place, and so the wielder can use its evocations after a decisive attack without crashing.



        Inward Vortex
        Cost: 7m, 2i, 1wp; Mins: Essence 3; Type: Simple
        Keywords: Decisive-Only
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisites: Looming Iron Surge

        A hit from the Kraken's Nail turns the relentless forces of gravity onto the victim, crushing them from all angles instead of just one. This evocation creates a decisive attack. If it hits, the target is forced to accept a crippling injury (which does not count against the once per story limit), negating as much damage as she can, or an amount equal to half the initiative spent on the tags (i.e., improvised and smashing) and evocations of the Kraken's Nail, round up, whichever is less.

        Once per scene, reset by crashing a non-trivial target who originally had 11+ initiative with a single withering attack. Inward Vortex may then be used on that target on the wielder's next attack.

        I revised this one so many times. Originally inspired by Crimson Spray Blur from Octavian, it's meant to be able to end a fight instantly. An earlier version dealt lots of bonus damage if the target refused to accept an injury, but I decided it was too much for an artifact 3 capstone.

            Vicious Censurer of the Liminal Fugitive (Artifact 3, Orichalcum Grand Daiklaive)

            It is said that in another age a man called Huqod was Exalted by the Sun while he hunted down and slaughtered the brigands that murdered his wife, Saeema. Later, Saeema’s ghost returned to Huqod, and the two tried to resume their relationship. It seemed that the two loved one-another again, until one fateful day when the Dawn snapped and rent the echo of his wife apart forever. Ghosts, he had determined, were not the people whom they resemble, but malicious copies that had no business existing.

            As the years passed, Huqod’s hatred of ghosts only grew, as well as the terrible destructions he would put them through. He sought a tool that would help him tear apart the denizens of the Underworld, and, dissatisfied with anything else, delved into artifice in order to see his vision come to being. The result was Vicious Censurer of the Liminal Fugitive, a weapon seething with so much malice towards the dead that any wielder cannot help but feel it. As for Huqod, it is said that he died violently less than a dozen years after crafting his blade as he took it deep into the unknown heart of the Underworld in an attempt to destroy whatever strange forces bring ghosts into existence.

            Vicious Censurer of the Liminal Fugitive hates the fact that ghosts can cross over into Creation and after any scene where any of its evocations are used, the wielder must spend one willpower (and roll a die for limit if applicable) or gain or strengthen a principle of, “I will destroy those who cross the border of death.”

            Evocations of Vicious Censurer of the Liminal Fugitive

            As a strange quirk of its being, though Vicious Censurer of the Liminal Fugitive is made from Orichalcum, its evocations function perfectly for Abyssals and Liminals as well as Solars, and they receive Your
            Corpus is Brittle for free. Any of the aforementioned Exalted who commit an extra two motes to the weapon when they attune to it find themselves able to harm dematerialized ghosts (or other death-essenced beings).

            Your Corpus is Brittle
            Cost: 2m Mins: Essence 1
            Type: Supplemental
            Keywords: Dual
            Duration: Instant
            Prerequisite Charms: None

            The first lesson that Vicious Censurer of the Liminal Fugitive teaches is that hatred can be used to destroy. This functions only against death-essenced beings (see sidebar below), and the wielder finds himself momentarily feeling the same hatred that the sword feels.

            If used as part of a successful withering attack, add post-soak damage dice equal to the intensity of the wielder’s intimacy of “I will destroy those who cross the border of death.” If the wielder has no such intimacy, count it as one. When dealing magnitude damage to a battlegroup, these post-soak dice are converted to successes. Note that in neither case does the wielder receive the extra initiative; it is all absorbed by the sword. On a decisive attack, ignore an equal amount of hardness.

            Your Soul Hides Nothing
            Cost: 3m Mins: Essence 2
            Type: Supplemental
            Keywords: Decisive-only
            Duration: Instant
            Prerequisite Charms: Your Corpus is Brittle

            Vicious Censurer of the Liminal Fugitive’s second lesson is that a quick burst of violence can reveal a victim’s vulnerabilities to the aggressor. A successful decisive attack supplemented by this evocation acts as a successful read intention against a death-essenced foe, and reveals a strong intimacy to the attacker. Alternatively, against a foe that has a method of escaping destruction (a spirit with a sanctum, or a Deathlord with an immortality clause, for example), it reveals the existence and nature of one method of revival or protection.

            By committing a single mote in the instant after learning of an intimacy or immortality clause, the wielder of Vicious Censurer of the Liminal Fugitive may hold on to a fragment of the victim’s soul and use it against them. Immaterial chains of hazy essence as black and gold as the corona of an eclipse snake off from the sword into the distance, visible only to the wielder and anyone that can see dematerialized spirits, which leads towards the subject of the intimacy. If a ghost cares for her daughter, the chains will lead to her. If a Deathlord may only be destroyed by throwing his original bones into the Elemental Pole of Fire, the chain will lead to the bones or perhaps to an ancient library detailing such a ritual. Regardless, as long as the mote is committed, the wielder receives three charmless dice on any attempt to find the subject of the intimacy such as tracking, investigating, researching, or anything else appropriate. As long as these motes are committed by the wielder of Vicious Censurer of the Liminal Fugitive, the victim is inherently aware that their secrets are in danger and are likely interested in safeguarding their intimacies or immortality.

            Your Purpose is Extinguished
            Cost: 10m, 1 wp Mins: Essence 3
            Type: Simple
            Keywords: Decisive-only
            Duration: Instant
            Prerequisite Charms: Your Soul Hides Nothing

            The final lesson of hatred taught by Vicious Censurer of the Liminal Fugitive is that to truly defeat an opponent, you must destroy everything that they care about. Strike at their greatest loves, and you strike at them as well. Use of this evocation causes Vicious Censurer of the Liminal Fugitive to glow oppressively golden like the hateful noon sun over the Burning Sands.

            This evocation requires the use of its prerequisite, notably the committed mote that links sword and intimacy. It is used when the wielder destroys an intimacy that the sword is linked to, and can be activated reflexively should that instant of destruction be mid-combat. If a ghost cares for her daughter, the wielder would slay that daughter to activate this charm, but if a ghost cared about the library that she maintained in life, it may be activated when the library is burned down and the sword swung across its ashes. For intimacies to abstract principles or cosmic beings (devotion to the Immaculate Faith, or to a Neverborn, for example) the destruction can be of a significant symbol of that principle or being, as the Storyteller sees fit. In all situations there must be destruction involved.

            This takes the form of a special automatically successful decisive attack that strikes the being from whom the intimacy was taken no matter where they are, even across dimensional boundaries. The damage pool for this attack is Essence + the level of the intimacy “I will destroy those who cross the border of death” + double the level of the intimacy that the victim had for what was destroyed. Hardness does not apply against this aggravated damage, and 10s are doubled. If the intimacy was destroyed in front of the victim, double 8s. Add the dice from any stunt for a grisly or harrowing destruction to the raw damage.


            Sidebar: On Death-Essence
            Vicious Censurer of the Liminal Fugitive hates those who cross the border of death. Normally that includes many creatures hailing from or suffused with the essence of the Underworld. Being such as ghosts, zombies, hekatonkhires, Deathlords, Abyssals, Liminals, some necromancers, and Neverborn comprise a non-exhaustive list. Those are the beings that the evocations of Vicious Censurer of the Liminal Fugitive work against.

            Well, those being s are those that cross the border if you’re in Creation, Yu-Shan, Malfeas, or the Wyld. However if you’re in the Underworld, those beings are native and Vicious Censurer of the Liminal Fugitive has no ill-feeling towards them. Instead, while in the Underworld, all the evocations work against beings native to Creation. Humans and animals, some behemoths, most Exalted, Elementals, and Gods are all strangers in the Underworld and there the sword only functions against such beings. In a Shadowland, the sword works equally well against both Creation-natives and Death-essenced beings, making it very formidable.

            The bearer of Vicious Censurer of the Liminal Fugitive does not automatically have knowledge of this, potentially leading to some dangerous situations.

                I had a quick look through the thread and couldn't see anyone tackling a sorcerous phylactery artifact in the style of the Talisman of Ten Thousand Eyes, so here's my attempt.


                The Coil of Ahiahep
                (Starmetal sorcerous phylactery, artifact ••••)

                The Shogunate was an era of upheaval and destruction. The Dragon-Blooded attempted to assert their new dominion over the world they had claimed, its wonders fell to ruin and wars were waged over those that survived. Many petty tyrants carved out empires in this period, unchecked by a Shogunate burdened with internecine strife and corruption.

                One such tyrant was the sorcerer-prince Ahiahep, a mysterious figure who appeared out of the forest wilds and claimed to be the last inheritor of the lost Devonian Library, and he bore a black ring that he claimed granted him dominion over the powers of Hell and the spirits of the East. Whatever the provenance of his sorceries they were real enough; using them he subjugated mortal kingdoms, spirit courts and rival Exalts alike and forged them into a nascent empire, razing forests from horizon to horizon and fashioning mighty ziggurats in his own name.

                Before long, this nascent empire seemed poised to strike at the heartlands of the Eastern daimyos of the Shogunate, but almost overnight it collapsed and fragmented as Ahiahep vanished. The story goes that a daring mortal thief charmed the black ring from his finger and left him to be torn apart by his resentful minions. Whatever the truth, the ring continued to have a bloody history independent of its master until it was lost during the Great Contagion.

                The Coil of Ahaiep is a ring of blackened starmetal in the form of an intricately carved dragon swallowing its own tail. The way light glitters across its finely worked scales makes it look uncannily lifelike.


                Evocations of the Black Ring

                When attuned for three motes by a sorcerer and worn, the Coil of Ahiahep reduces by one the Willpower cost of the first spell the sorcerer casts during the day.

                The ring’s evocations can be paid for with motes or sorcerous motes, which can be gathered with a Shape Sorcery action for this purpose. Sorcerers without a mote pool can wear the ring and learn and use its first two evocations in this way, though they cannot gain its attunement bonus.


                Knowledge Comes from Within
                Cost: 3m; Mins:Essence 1
                Type:Simple
                Keywords:None
                Duration:One scene
                Prerequisite Charms:None

                The first lesson of Ahiahep’s black ring teaches of what can be known by sufficient magical intuition. For the remainder of the scene, the sorcerer gains two bonus dice on all rolls on Occult rolls to examine spirits, fair folk and other non-human magical beings. She also gains this bonus any Shape Sorcery rolls to cast sorcery against them. Should she Join Battle against such beings, she gains a number of sorcerous motes equal to her successes, which last for the duration of the scene.


                Balance in Emptiness
                Cost: 1wp; Mins:Essence 1
                Type:Simple
                Keywords:None
                Duration:Instant
                Prerequisite Charms:Knowledge Comes from Within

                The master of the black ring can use this Evocation to trap a minor spirit in the empty void within, compelling it to use its powers on her behalf. Balance in Emptiness allows the ring to function exactly as a Yasal Crystal, save that it can imprison up to (sorcerer’s Essence) spirits of Essence 2 or lower.


                The End is the Beginning
                Cost: 10m; Mins:Essence 2
                Type:Simple
                Keywords:None
                Duration:Instant
                Prerequisite Charms:Balance in Emptiness

                Precious seeds of magic fall still in the Age of Sorrows, never to come to fruition. Better to nurture them, and give them a chance to fulfil their purpose. If the sorcerer finds a way to counter a magical curse that should have been fatal or extremely debilitating, she may use this Charm to trap the magic in the structure of the ring. The ring can sustain the curses of sorcery, the Charms of spirits, the death-curses of ghosts, the bans of the Fair Folk and even stranger and more exotic magics. At any time thereafter, upon seeing the originator of the curse, she can use this Charm again to inflict an identical curse upon them.


                The Unbroken Coil
                Cost: 1wp; Mins:Essence 2
                Type:Supplemental
                Keywords:None
                Duration:Instant
                Prerequisite Charms:Knowledge Comes from Within

                Few in this fallen age understand more of the nature of sorcery than the force that slumbers within the black ring, a beginningless legacy stretching back through time. It whispers guidance to its master, warning her against pretenders to this power. A sorcerer can use this Evocation to attempt to counter or distort a spell of one circle greater than that into which she is initiated (Celestial for a Terrestrial sorcerer, Solar for a Celestial). A Solar sorcerer can use this Evocation to gain her Occult in non-Charm bonus dice to a roll to counter or distort a spell.


                Beyond the Black Rainbow
                Cost: 5m, 1wp;Mins:Essence 3
                Type:Simple
                Keywords:None
                Duration:Instant
                Prerequisite Charms:The End is the Beginning, The Unbroken Coil

                The ring remembers a Time Before, through it flows a river of sorcerous power long since etched into the very fabric of the world, fact made mystery by the passage of ages. Once per story, the sorcerer can reach beyond the veil of the material world and grasp at the sorcerous power that courses beneath. Doing so grants her knowledge of a single spell of the Emerald circle. She can only cast this spell once or incorporate it in as means in a single sorcerous working, after which the power recedes once more from her grasp. She can counter and distort the spell for the remainder of the story as though she knew it, however.

                Alternatively, the sorcerer can reach for knowledge of an even greater power. She understands the fundamental truth of a spell of the Sapphire or Adamant circle. She cannot cast this spell, but can counter and distort is as though she can. She must still use The Unbroken Coil to counter a spell of a circle she cannot cast.

                            Hello everyone. After reading through this thread, I found the inspiration to make some artifact of my own. My first few are just small attempts at creating a simple artifact, but after getting some practice in I decided to try something a bit bigger.

                            This artifact is meant to be wielded by a warrior-scholar type. The idea is for the wielder to be able to absorb the knowledge and wisdom of those he defeats, even if he slays them. It certainly needs some more work, both in the wording and the balancing, but I was hoping I could get some feedback on my first draft. The last evocation in particular feels a bit clunky as it is written right now, and I feel a bit unsure about the evocation bonus

                            Ematril, The Library of the Passed
                            Starmetal Wrackstaff, Artifact •••••

                            Brilliant Sorrow was a Chosen of the Maiden of Secrets. Both a warrior and a scholar, he found sadness in the knowledge that was lost with each life on the battlefield. After a year of meditation in the libraries of Heaven, he found the inspiration to forge Ematril.

                            Ematril would be a solid staff of starmetal, 6 feet long, on which everything that was known to Brilliant Sorrow would be engraved with orichalcum. The staff greedily took in every secret he gave it, the runes shifting and shrinking to make space when there seemed to be none left. Finally, after decades of restless work, the staff knew everything Brillant Sorrow did, even the knowledge he was not aware he possessed.

                            Brilliant Sorrow was not satisfied however. His own knowledge preserved for all time, he began the next step in Ematril`s creation. Through sorcery, he created a spirit of knowledge and bound it to the staff, to curate the impressive library the staff would one day be. To this spirit he gave the power to read the minds of those whom the staff struck, so it could copy their knowledge for the future.

                            Safe knowing that he would no longer rob Creation of knowledge and wisdom carrying out his duties to Heaven, Brilliant Sorrow spent the rest of his life serving the Maidens, perishing when he defined heavens will and saved one who was fated to die, binding himself to the staff in his final moments to aid those who would carry his legacy.
                            Ematril has three hearthstone sockets, placed such that they divide the staff into four sections of equal length.

                            Evocations of Ematril, The Library of the Passed
                            A Solar or Sidereal that attunes to Ematril finds their minds filled with knowledge. They receive +2 to any Lore or Occult rolls based on recalling information or knowledge while holding the staff. Other beings find the staff impossible to attune to, as the spirit within judges them unable to comprehend the knowledge within, and refuses them. Such being can attune to the staff if they have a combined Lore, Occult and Intelligence of at least 10, although they will be unable to learn Ematrils final evocation.

                            Student Reading Strike
                            Cost: 2m Mins: Essence 1
                            Type: Supplemental
                            Keywords: Uniform
                            Duration: Instant
                            Prerequisites: None
                            If an attack boosted by this evocation hits an opponent, the wielder learns their Lore rating, as well as any specialties they have in Lore. If the successes on the damage roll is enough to beat the targets guile, the wielder also learns of any knowledge based intimacies the target may have.
                            This evocation can also be used out of combat, as a Simple charm, in which case it involves a simple tap of the staff against the target. If the target wishes, such a use of this evocation reveals all relevant intimacies without a roll. Otherwise, make a Read Intentions roll, using (Lore + Perception) instead of the successes on the damage roll.

                            Note: Unless there is a special reason not to, it is usually safe for the Storyteller to assume that most characters have between zero or two dots in Lore, with perhaps one specialty relevant to their occupation.

                            Enigmatic Tutor Lecture
                            Cost: 3m Mins: Essence 2
                            Type: Supplemental
                            Keywords: Withering-Only
                            Duration: Instant
                            Prerequisites: Student Reading Strike

                            This evocation supplements a withering attack against an opponent struck by its prerequisite earlier in the scene. It adds the difference in the characters Lore ratings to the raw damage of the attack if that difference is positive. Specialties add to each of the characters Lore ratings for this purpose.

                            Mentor Enlightening Student Method
                            Cost: 3m 1wp Mins: Essence 2
                            Type: Reflexive
                            Keywords: Decisive-Only, Stackable
                            Duration: Indefinite
                            Prerequisites: Student Reading Strike

                            Upon landing a decisive attack on a foe that has been struck at least once with the prerequisite, the exalt can activate this charm to learn from their foe. They learn one of the Lore specialties for as long as the motes remain committed, and it is considered a known specialty for all purposes. When the commitment is dropped, the knowledge fades from their minds. The specialty can be added permanently at any point by paying for it as normal, with no training time required.
                            If the target is willing, this charm can be used without the need of a decisive attack, in which case it is treated as a Simple charm.

                            Heavenly Cage Invitation
                            Cost: 4m 1wp Mins: Essence 3
                            Type: Reflexive
                            Keywords: Decisive-Only, Psyche
                            Duration: Indefinite
                            Prerequisites: Enigmatic Tutor Lecture, Mentor Enlightening Student Method

                            A crippled warrior can still serve as a teacher and give council. This evocation allows the saying to apply to those slain by the exalt.
                            Upon striking a killing blow, the exalt can activate the charm and offer the target a chance to remain a little longer to guide those who remain. Treat this as an instill action, targeting a Major intimacy, unless the exalt knows of any stronger intimacies to exploit. Wound penalties do not apply to the targets Resolve for this action. The exalt may substitute Lore for a social ability, if desired.

                            If the instill attempt is successful, a phantom copy of the target is put into the staff. This phantom knows everything it did in life, but cannot meaningfully do anything but talk to the holder of the staff. The phantom keeps any intimacies it may have had in life, but also gains a Defining intimacy towards aiding the staff`s holder in scholarly pursuits and in training the holder.
                            The phantom is not a ghost, nor is it made of the person’s soul. It is still possible for the enemy to rise as a ghost in the future, or for their soul to be reincarnated. While inside the staff, the phantom enjoys a heavenly life, where all their needs and desires are met, though they may never leave the staff. The holder may revoke or grant privileges if the phantom turns out to be unruly.

                            Once commitment to the evocation ends, or a new phantom is created, the phantom fades into the infinite library of the staff. He is still there, and still lives in the heavenly world, but he is no longer reachable to the holder. The wielders ability to influence the phantoms world also ends at this point, allowing it to enjoy eternity without restrictions placed upon it by the wielder.

                            Opening the Grand Library
                            Cost: 15m 1wp (1wp) Mins: Essence 5
                            Type: Simple
                            Keywords: Perilous
                            Duration: One Day
                            Prerequisites: Heavenly Cage Invitation

                            Having shown himself a worthy scholar, Ematril opens its doors fully to its wielder. Meditating for a moment on the true purpose of knowledge, he then taps the staff into the ground creating a brilliant flash of essence. Once it passes, the exalt, and anyone he wishes to bring with him, disappears.

                            This evocation brings the exalt and anyone he wishes into the great library of Ematril, located in Elsewhere. Anyone can resist this pull with a (Willpower + Essence) roll at a difficulty of the holder`s Essence.

                            The library is a massive collection of the memories and knowledge of anyone the staff has ever touched. The attunement bonus from the staff increases to +4, and anyone else gains +2 to applicable rolls. If Heavenly Cage Invitation is active, the phantom can be summoned or banished by a gesture from the holder of the staff. This action is a miscellaneous action in combat. The amount of motes a character recovers each round of combat is limited to the highest of their Occult or Lore while inside the library. While inside the library, the holder can spend a few minutes concentrating to make changes to its layout, such as creating reading rooms, living areas, rearranging shelves and so on, though this is rarely useful in combat.

                            Anyone forced inside the library can leave by paying one willpower and willing themselves to do so as a miscellaneous action, rolling (Intelligence + [Lore or Occult]) against a difficulty of (The holders Essence - 2). If the holder is conscious, he can oppose this roll reflexively without paying any willpower. Those who entered willingly, or where invited, can leave without a roll, unless the holder wishes to prevent them from doing so. He can also force someone to leave paying on willpower and making the same opposed roll against the targets (Essence – 2). In this case the target does not need to pay any willpower.

                            While inside the library, the holder can spend a few minutes concentrating to make changes to its layout, such as creating reading rooms, living areas, rearranging shelves and so on. This ease of access makes it easy to research subjects while inside the library. The interval of any extended action related to researching something using Lore or Occult becomes one hour, unless it would normally be shorter.

                            Ematril is hesitant to open its doors, even to a worthy wielder. This evocation can only be used once a season, unless reset by gaining a dot of Lore or Occult, or a new specialty in one of those abilities towards which the wielder pays experience.

                            Special: This evocation can only be learned after discovering a great secret of Creation unknown to Ematril, such as the Great Curse or the weakness of one of the Deathlords. After doing so, this evocation is learned for free when all its prerequisites are met.
