                                        Originally posted by KittyEmpress View Post
                                        Are abyssals still exalted before the moment of death and saved, or are they exalted after the moment of death and a form of undead?
                                        Not 100% sure yet; we'll probably go with whatever First Edition did unless there's good reason not to. It doesn't matter a whole lot either way - I'd classify them as undead regardless.

                                        Do their hearts still beat?
                                        Probably, I guess?

                                        Can they still have kids?
                                        Yeah.

                                        Are liminals completely undead (no body processes, no beating heart, etc) or are they a form of 'resurrecting' a living being, and thus being living themselves, despite their monstrous appearances while using essence?
                                        Liminals are definitely undead, but have an active metabolism like Joe Alive Human. Notable exception is that the likely can't have children naturally.
