    Silent Reef Style (WIP)

    01-08-2020, 12:33 PM
    I dusted off one of my old martial arts styles and attempted to redesign it for 3rd Edition. At the moment, this is crude first draft and a first attempt at any 3rd Edition mechanics. So any critiques, suggestions, and other commentary are welcome. In particular, proper costs are eluding me. I don't have a good feel for how to value various effects yet, so any opinions on that front would be helpful.

    By way of a short introduction, this style was designed for a society of bounty hunting retiarii in the West. Once governed by a strict code, they've become increasingly fractured, with several renegade members engaging in more unscrupulous methods to apprehend their prey. Among traditionalists, it is preferred to bring their targets in alive.

    Silent Reef Weapons: Silent Reef style is rarely practiced unarmed, but its barehanded strikes are strong-armed grapples and strangling choke holds. Preferentially, the Silent Reef fights with the armaments of the fisherman - the net and the trident. The net is used to distract and unbalance as well as to bind, while the trident finds use when grim necessity demands a wounding blow.

    Armor: Silent Reef style is compatible with light armor.

    NEW WEAPON: TRIDENT
    Heavy (Accuracy +0, Damage +11, Defense -1, Overwhelming 1)
    Tags: Lethal, Melee, Disarming, Reaching, Two-Handed

    Note: If I were keeping true to 2nd Edition rules, this weapon would also have the Piercing tag. At the moment, I dropped it, thinking that might be too many tags for one weapon.


    NEW WEAPON: NET
    Medium (Damage +9, Overwhelming 1)
    Accuracy: Close +4; Short +3; Medium +2; Long -1; Extreme -3
    Tags: Thrown (Short), Grappling, Special

    Nets are often cast on lines, allowing the angler to more easily retrieve his catch. Once thrown, a net with such as casting line can be retrieved with a ready weapon action instead of moving to reclaim it. Nets do not inflict bashing or lethal damage, and therefore cannot normally be used as part of a decisive attack. They can be used to perform a grapple gambit out to their maximum range, usually employing Thrown as the relevant combat ability. Once the control roll has been made, the caster need not be involved with the grapple. If so, the victim suffers only the base effects of the grapple. If the caster wishes to make a savage, restrain/drag, or slam/throw action, they must be an active participate in the clinch, though they may stunt this through a casting line.

    Fisher of Men Stance
    Cost: 3m;Mins: Martial Arts 2, Essence 1
    Type: Supplemental
    Keywords: Dual
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: None

    The martial artist adopts the stance of the prepared angler, ready to strike with net or trident. The martial artist may reflexively perform a ready weapon action before her attack. If she attacks a target she successfully attacked with a different weapon on her last turn or more recently, her withering attack gains (higher of Essence or 3) bonus dice. If she performs a disarm or grapple gambit, her decisive attack doubles 9s.

    The Clownfish Smiles
    Cost: 4m, 1i, 1wp; Mins: Martial Arts 3, Essence 1
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: Mastery, Perilous, Terrestrial, Uniform
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: None

    As the clownfish is safe among the stinging arms of the anemone, the martial artist is safe within the grasp of his foes. When the martial artist is targeted by an attack while they are grappling with another enemy, she gains +2 Defense. If the attack misses the martial artist, her grappling opponent must defend against its rolled successes as though he were the original target, passing the onslaught penalty along as well. This Charm may be used once per grapple and resets once the current grapple ends.
    Mastery: If the martial artist controls the grapple and redirects an attack with this Charm, she regains 1 willpower.
    Terrestrial: The martial artist must control the grapple to use this Charm.

    Entangled Krait Courtship
    Cost: 6m; Mins: Martial Arts 3, Essence 1
    Type: Supplemental
    Keywords: Decisive-Only, Mastery
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Fisher of Men Stance

    The sea krait entwines its mate, and the two snakes cannot be parted. The martial artist binds her foe in similarly inescapable embrace. This charm supplements a control roll during a grapple, doubling her 9s. Each 1 her opponent rolls subtracts one from his successes. If the martial artist wins control, she may not savage, slam or throw her target so long as she maintains the clinch. Restraining and dragging expend only 1 round of control.
    Mastery: Restraining and dragging expend no rounds of control.

    Polyp Proximity Strike
    Cost: 5m; Mins: Martial Arts 3, Essence 1
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: Decisive-Only, Mastery
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: The Clownfish Smiles

    The reef is a massive predator, but an immobile one. The martial artist stands ready to grasp her foe as he approaches, rather than pursuing him. Until the martial artist’s next action, if an enemy moves within close range, the martial artist may perform a reflexive grapple gambit. If she is armed with a net, she may do so if an enemy moves within her weapon’s maximum range. Additionally, she may perform a reflexive grapple if she opposes a disengage roll and wins. If armed with a net, characters within short range must roll to disengage from her. Once the martial artist has successfully won control of a clinch, the effects of this Charm end.
    Mastery: This Charm’s type is Reflexive, and may be activated in response to an enemy moving into range or attempting to disengage.

    Silent Reef Form
    Cost: 8m; Mins: Martial Arts 4, Essence 1
    Type: Simple, Decisive-Only
    Keywords: Form
    Duration: Permanent
    Prerequisite Charms: Entangled Krait Courtship, Polyp Proximity Strike

    The reef thrives on a thousand entangled lives, yet hardly makes a sound. The martial artist lowers the difficulty of disarm and grapple gambits by 1. While grappling or being grappled, she does not suffer the usual -2 Defense penalty. She restrains her victims with a powerful stranglehold, preventing them from speaking or breathing until her next turn. If she performs enough consecutive restraining actions to cause her foe to perish from lack of air, typically (their Stamina x2) rounds (Exalted, pg. 232), they are instead incapacitated but alive. Once her enemy is incapacitated in this way, she must release him, leaving him unconscious but once again able able to breath.
    Special activation rules: When the stylist initiates a grapple and wins the control roll, she may expend two rounds of control to reflexively enter this form.

    Coral Body Mediation
    Cost: 4m; Mins: Martial Arts 4, Essence 2
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: Dual, Mastery
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Silent Reef Form

    The martial artist tangles her own essence around herself, twisting and hardening her flesh against an attack. She gains +(higher of Essence or 3) Soak against withering attacks and (higher of Essence or 3) Hardness against decisive attacks. If she has not taken a movement action this round, she adds +2 to either her Soak or her Hardness. Against an opponent who controls a grapple against her or attacking barehanded, she also inflicts 1 level of decisive lethal damage, ignoring Hardness. So long as she has not performed a movement action since last activating this Charm, each subsequent activation reduces its cost by a cumulative 1 mote, to a minimum of 1 mote. Moving as part of a dragging action does not interfere with this Charm’s effects.
    Mastery: If the martial artist has not performed a movement action, except as part of a dragging action, since her last activation of this Charm, its cost is reduced to 0 motes.

    Hull-Breaching Atemi
    Cost: 6m, 3i; Mins: Martial Arts 5, Essence 2
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: Mastery, Terrestrial, Withering-Only
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Coral Body Meditation

    For every ship sunk by acts of war, two more have had their hulls torn asunder by the grasping reef. The martial artist performs a restraining action against a grappled enemy, reducing his Soak by -(higher of Essence or 3) until her next action. She may expend any number of rounds of control, with each round reducing the target’s Soak by an additional -1. The martial artist also gains any initiative her opponent loses to withering attacks while under the influence of this Charm. If he crashes, the martial artist counts as the character responsible for crashing him for the purposes of future Initiative Shifts. On her next turn, the martial artist immediately releases her opponent. This charm can only be used once per scene, unless reset by crashing a grappled opponent. Even after resetting, this Charm cannot be used against the same enemy more than once in a scene.
    Mastery: The stylist does not need to release her opponent on her next action.
    Terrestrial: The Terrestrial cannot expend more than 5 rounds with this Charm.

    Reef Captures Frigate
    Cost: 4m, 2i; Mins: Martial Arts 5, Essence 2
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: Counterattack, Decisive-Only, Terrestrial
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Coral Body Meditation

    When a vessel skirts to close to the reef, it should count itself lucky to only run aground. Against an attack made from close range, the martial artist gains +1 Parry. If the attack misses, she may counter attack with either a grapple gambit or a disarm gambit, adding her Parry as bonus dice to the attack roll. On a successful disarm gambit, she may keep hold of the weapon and use it as though it were compatible with this style until it leaves her hand.
    Terrestrial: This Charm counts as the Terrestrial’s action for the turn and cannot be used if she has already acted.

    Anemone Makes a Friend
    Cost: 8m, 4i, 1wp; Mins: Martial Arts 5, Essence 3
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: Decisive-Only
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Hull-Breaching Atemi, Reef Captures Frigate

    Mastering the Silent Reef, the martial artist reexamines its earlier lessons and learns to restrain her foe with merely a touch. She makes a decisive attack that exposes her victim to the Silent Reef Toxin. This poison has Damage 6i/turn (bashing in crash), duration (attack’s threshold successes) rounds. Its penalty prevents its victim from speaking or breathing, potentially causing him to pass out if deprived of air long enough. In such cases, the victim is merely incapacitated rather than killed as with Silent Reef Form and beings breathing normally once unconscious. However, so long as the victim does not move away from the martial artist, the poison’s onset is delayed. Once it has begun, it can be suspended again by moving back into close range. If the martial artist takes a move action away from her victim, the poison does not activate. The difficulty to diagnose the Silent Reef Toxin is +3 while active or +5 otherwise. After a day, the poison leaves his system if the victim has not yet triggered it.

      This is cool, I've already noticed there's an interesting synergy between the form charm and the pinnacle.

      Ordinarily, Ex3 combats will end well before Silent Reef Form can make anyone suffocate but poison that deals initiative damage is a useful meta way to extend a fight. Someone who might launch their doom combo or surrender might hold off in favour of keeping moving so the poison keeps burns through initiative rather than vital health levels.

          Happy to hear you found this interesting.

          In the 2e version, the Form Charm also had a weaker variant of the poison tied to it. This time around, I made it a more mundane choke hold for the form and saved the supernatural poison for the pinnacle charm.

          As far as the duration of a fight is concerned, restraining an opponent prevents them from acting at all, so you don't have to worry about them building up their initiative for any big attacks too much. With Entangled Krait Courtship, you're minimizing (or negating) the number of rounds of control you have to sacrifice to maintain the restraint, so that helps extend things too. If he's got buddies trying to free him, then you've got The Clownfish Smiles to use him as a human shield against their attacks. Though that still consumes rounds of control for being attacked - I didn't give the Silent Reef any means of negating that issue.

          I did realized after posting this that the reflexive activation of the form is significantly weaker than Mantis Form, so I may have to reconsider that. Mantis Form just needs to control a clinch, and doesn't need to sacrifice any rounds of control.

              First off: very cool Martial Art, with a clear thematic feel. I wanted to actually read it from your description, and the Charms didn't disappoint. Your costs feel roughly correct, in general, although I'll have a look again to make direct comparisons.

              Specific thoughts:
              There are no effects that reduce the penalty to round of control for being attacked when grappling, that I could see. That's probably intentional, but I thought I'd note it.

              Entangled Krait Courtship is.... Possibly going to end up as either really boring or completely overpowered in practice. It's Mastery abilities allows a Master to indefinitely restrain a target, which is boring for both participants. It also combines with the above problem to make it not a brilliant strategy if the victim has friends, but combines with the Form Charm to make it an unstoppable single-hit power if the target is weak to strangulation.

              Polyp Proximity Strike would probably benefit from having a clearly-stated duration, and the "when used, stops working" line, just for clarity. it's a cool ability, and looks like it would be fun to use.

              Coral Body Meditation is a brilliant ability, it's a charm I would really enjoy using, but I think it's another Charm that needs some tightening. It can be used "reflexively" and inflicts 1L against someone controlling a grapple against the practitioner. As written, I can't see why they can't just re-use it; it should probably say that it inflicts the damage after the attack.
              In terms of costing, 4m is a good price for 3-5 soak, and 4m for a turn of 5-7 soak at mastery is very attractive. I might bump the price a little, but it's probably fine as-is.

              Hull-Breaching Atemi kind of confuses me. It reduces an opponent's soak, potentially duplicates initiative gain, and the martial artist can't actually capitalize on the penalised soak because it wears off on their next action. It also had the withering-only tag on a restraining action. Is it supposed to be a savaging attack, so the martial artist definitely gets a benefit? Could it reduce soak to a negative value?. I'd say it's potentially a little weak, potential initiative duplication aside, as 6m for +3-5 damage is probably a better Charm for earlier in the tree.

              Having gone through this again, I realise that this style really benefits from being Strong and Dexteritous. Is that the archetype you have in mind for people who use this martial art?
