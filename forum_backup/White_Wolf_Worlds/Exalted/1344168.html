    Help me stat Mela the Elemental Dragon of Air

    10-28-2019, 07:53 AM
    Fimbulwinter grips the land, and Ragnarok has begun; it seems the Second Age is ending, the Realm has (apparently) been destroyed, and Mela (apparently) now attempts to choke the entire world in ice... for somewhat unclear reasons. (Possibly Hesiesh is dead. And/or Pasiap. One PC thinks Pasiap has killed Mela and replaced her and is trying to destroy the world because he's angry humans keep pooping on his head. The other PCs are unconvinced. Possibly this is all Raksha lies or something.)

    So my PCs have travelled from their homeland north of Iscomay all the way to the Haslanti city of Crystal (via wooden-wyld-whale-tentacled-ship), where they're just in the process of hiring an airship. Next session (assuming they don't get into some random trouble or something) they'll be leaving Crystal to fly across the Wasting Tundra, and then to the Elemental Pole of Air to Mela's floating ice palace beyond the edge of the world.

    Then they will attempt to talk Mela down/rescue Mela from her evil brother/punch her in the face.

    And if they try and punch Mela, or just social-fu her, she'll need stats.


    My thought is to start with Fakharu (an Essence 6 Dragon), but with these changes:
    All water-aspected effects become air-aspected. For instance, River-Drinking Renewal becomes Storm-Inhaling Renewal, consuming gale-force winds, downpours, and/or heavy fog instead of water; Drowning-in-Coils Constriction asphyxiates by pulling air out of the target’s lungs; and Flowing Body Dispersal turns the dragon’s body gaseous. Seven Venoms Exhalation could breathe lightning instead.
    I can also give her the charms of the Storm Serpent: Fulminating Blast, Thunderous Rebuke, Evaporating Defence (which is actually really good), and Live Wire.

    Also, of course I can just increase her dice pools, soak, etc. I think maybe Essence 9 would be suitable for one of the five Elemental Dragons?

    But the numbers are easy, the hard thing is the charms. Maybe I should give her an excellency-like charm, like Octavian and Ahlat have. And should she have charms for, say, social influence, or just giant dice pools? Maybe charms to defend her main intimacies, so it's difficult to make her betray Gaia or stop all winds from moving or something fundamentally opposed to being the Elemental Dragon of Air.

    Any ideas?

    (A small note; my aim is to make her really powerful. It's not specifically for her to be able to wipe the floor with PCs. I mean, obviously the first might lead to the second, but I don't think she needs, say, an Overwhelming Negator, which a lack of is a big defence hole against a pack of Exalts. But that's fine, obviously groups of Exalts defeated Third-Circle Devas all over the place in the Divine Revolution. If they fight her, I want them to go "oh shit, she's really powerful" but she doesn't need to be amazing at exploiting the combat system itself, if you see what I mean. She's an Air Dragon, not an Invincible Sword Princess.)

              Idk.

              As the Children of Gaia, stating them is like trying to stat the Arrow Rain of Malfeas. 2E had the Dragons as a significant source of that element's Essence, and pegged them above the Greater Elemental Dragons (who were plot devices to be endured).

              I would certainly make Mela at 3CD level. At least 1/scene attack that does uncountable damage.

              The Elemental Dragons aren't potrayed as being socially adroit, but I certainly think a Defining Intimacy along the lines of "I have earned this furlow" is appropriate. I'd handle the social aspect as dice-light as possible. Just guide it based on stunts and Mela's Intimacies (and I like the idea of Righteous Lion Defence style protection of key Intimacies).

              For the sake of argument, I'd lean towards big dice pools over Charms, simply so I had less to keep track of. But I think fighting Mela is best done through negotiating environmental hazards -- like a Legendary Size Battle Group that spits out Bonfire Hazards instead of Withering attacks.

              I'm not saying "don't do it", but I'm saying how do you stat the 23rd Crystal Orb that orbits SWLIHN, or the specific feeling of melancholy that strikes the citizens of Malfeas, or a being which is literally a constant rain of arrows from the sky, or the raging force of fire and destruction at the heart of the sun. Because these are the beings which are peers to Mela -- not second circle demons and lesser elemental dragons.

                      A question I have is if they fight her or engage socially, are they just going to throw down, or do they have some kind of larger plan?

                      I’ve always felt that beings on the level of third circle demons or elemental dragons shouldn’t be something that just gets fought straight up. More that you have to go on a journey to either dismantle some of their powers or collect specific knowledge and/or weapons that make it possible. For one there’s a good chance Mela is going to have some serious backup in the forms of air based spirits, that’s something your players may want to consider dealing with first.

                      Looking through the Air aspected social charms, in social scenes Mela should be cold, calculating, but also idealistic. She is highly dedicated to principles over personal ties for her intimacies. She’s also steeped in understanding, both able to make other understand with clarity and understand others herself.

                      She should absolutely have legendary size, and I also agree with John that for the sake of running her she should probably have less charms which are more straightforward and powerful. Like she might have one that costs initiative and gives her an uncontested disengage. There’s also just something about being a dragon that makes me want to give her some really good grapple abilities.

                      Some of this also depends on how badass your players are. If you actually want them to be able to win reasonably or not.
