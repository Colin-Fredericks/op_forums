    Jupiter is physically incapable of explaining her motives for any given action. Ironically, this has made her the most trusted of the five Maidens.

    How, you may ask? Shut up, I'm talking.

    Let me give you an example.

    Jupiter contacts one of her chosen in Examport. She tells him to go to the market and ask the apple merchant for a bag of oranges as many times as he can. He's confused, since there isn't a single merchant in the city who sells oranges (and she asked him to go the apple merchant anyway) but he does so. Naturally, he gets no oranges. She tells him to do this every day. As he does so (switching between Resplendent Destinies), oranges flow into the city because of inflated demand.

    Just before people start to realize the oranges aren't actually selling, a boat arrives in port. Many of the sailors are suffering from intense scurvy, so they buy up all the oranges they can. They just barely manage to get enough for everybody, and so the sailors live.

    Jupiter tells her chosen to stop asking for oranges. Now she wants him to ask for bows.

    Over the millennia, incidents like this have taught the gods and the chosen one very important thing: If Jupiter tells you to do something, then do it, even if she can't tell you why. It's important.

                            3. If any being is freed from the Great Curse, they experience a long moment of profound mental and emotional clarity in which they remember each of their limit breaks and other actions strongly influenced by the Curse. The veil is torn away from their inner eye and they see in stark hindsight their actions and the consequences, and understand that their mental state was influenced by some outside force. They don’t gain any insight into where that influence came from, however. The certainty that they are now free from that invasive influence allows them to regain 1wp. (How the revelations about their actions affect them is a matter for roleplaying.)

                            Every Cursed Exalt experiences this upon their death, no matter how much they suffered as they died. But when a Solar becomes an Abyssal, the violation of their body, mind, and souls, and the screams of the Neverborn, completely drown out this revelation.

None of the eight heroes were Exalted, but the oldest cities are survivors of the Niobraran.

                                        A few mortals find their way to Yu Shan once in each generation. Of these, a very small fraction will seek out one of the five Maidens of Fate to ask for a favor. Of those, a still smaller fraction will be granted that favor, in exchange for pledge to give their lives to their Maiden. "The Bound" is the name unofficially given these mortals who have pledged themselves to the service of one of the five fates. Only a few of the most elder sidereals and the most senior gods are aware that they exist. They are not acknowledged in any of Heaven's records.

                                        There have only been twenty-three Bound in all of Creation's history. Some were ordinary humans, some were godblooded of some kind, and a few were Exalted. Every one died accomplishing a suicide mission on behalf of their Maiden, for reasons known only to the Maidens themselves.

                                        Each of the five Maidens possesses a singular ability to manipulate the fates of other beings in a very specific way.

                                        Mercury can compel others to travel to a specified location of her choosing by their own means. They may go against their will, but they will go. Only death will deter them, and sometimes that only means a ghost will try to complete the journey.

                                        Venus can overwhelm a mind with desire, forcing a person to seek out whatever they believe they must have to be happy. For some, this means an insatiable craving for sex, food, narcotics, or adrenaline-soaked exercises. For others, this means obsessive pursuit of some object of love or ambition. Whatever the source of their contentment might be, the victim will not know any joy until they have what they seek.

                                        Mars can instill a desire to fight even in the most timid of individuals. Rage, righteous indigation, cold determination, or other motivators become too overwhelming to ignore. The victim will be urged to act on those feelings against whatever the source of their ire is, and they will not stop until the source is destroyed or they are.

                                        Jupiter can make someone forget any piece of knowledge they possess. It could be a name, a body of information about a subject, or the ability to perform a certain task. The victim may someday re-learn whatever they forgot, and get curious feelings of deja vu in the process.

                                        Saturn can end a life prematurely. On its face this ability may seem trivial compared to her sisters' talents, especially given that all of them have the ability to kill with more mundane means. However, the fact that it can be used even on creatures that are nominally immortal makes it a powerful weapon in Saturn's hands.

                                        Of course, any being immune to the pull of fate is immune to these powers, but such creatures are very rare, and even more rarely find themselves the subject of a Maiden's full attention.

                                        South of the Threshold, beyond the Lap but well before the fiery edge of the world, lies an expanse of land officially called the Southern Frontier by the Realm and the Wild South by romantics. During the last century there has been a growing push from the Realm to expand into this region. Settlers from the Blessed Isle and threshold have gone south, fighting the natives and reshaping the land as they went. Some are motivated by profit gleaned from arable landed not owned by any House, minable minerals, or commerce without taxes. Others are motivated by more intangible things - freedom from the laws of the center, the prospect of adventure, or some ideal of independence.

                                        The Scarlet Empress personally never took much interest in the Southern Frontier, and gave only minimal support to colonization efforts. This meant freedom-seekers could be satisfied, but it also meant that order was rarely upheld and banditry was common. These issues were mitigated, to an extent, by the presence of traveling Dragonblooded marshals - adventurous House scions, for the most part, who were granted carte blanche by the Realm to enforce the law as they wished. But since the Empress disappeared and the struggle for the throne began, the majority of marshals have found reasons to go back north and there has been little new blood to replace them.

                                        The Frontier has become increasingly characterized by small, tribe-like townships that aggressively defend their territory against any interlopers. Outside the towns there is little hope for protection against a growing bandit problem. The displaced natives, particularly the Dunefolk, are starting to return in force. Few of the settlers can see it, but the continued existence of the Frontier as they know it is very much in question.

                                        On the frontier of the Wild South, there's only one woman with the grit to take on its most dangerous outlaws and bring them Back Alive, or Maybe Dead.

                                            Not really headcanon, just a thought I had that stuck...

                                            Before each Incarna made their own Exalted, they tried to work together, thinking that Exalted created by their combined powers would be more powerful. However, the Unconquered Sun being as he is, he outshined the other Incarna, and the resulting Exalted were nearly indistinguishable from the later Solars. Since this was a waste of both Luna's and the Maiden's powers, they abandoned the idea after the first batch of 60 Exalted, who are today considered members of the Solar Exalted. Still, they carry just a spark of Luna and the five Maidens. From Luna, they got the ability to barter with Spirits for power. From the Maidens, they got the ability to bind the words of beings into fate.
                                            Since they're so similar to "proper" Solars, they are called such, but they are a fifth caste next to the four Solar castes.

                                            tl;dr: Eclipses are the product of a collaboration of all the Incarna, not just TUC.

                                                        Libations poured out to the Dead become a special sort of Grave Good. They materialize next to the ghost they honor in an ornate drinking cup of his favorite design, and the luminescent, foggy drink within tastes like their favorite drink, only much tastier. It can manifest as tea, coffee, liquor or any other drink they've enjoyed in life, but it *only* tastes like that, even to other ghosts who have never had said drink. Some ghosts make a fortune pooling their libations by opening bars or drink stands that offer a wide variety of tastes.
