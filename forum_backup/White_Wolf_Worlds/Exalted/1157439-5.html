    Premise: I never played Infernals, I started with 3E and I know the still not published splats only by osmosis and the description given until now.

    I think -and that's only my personal preference, but I think it fits the Infernal pretty well- that at least some part of the characterization of the Infernals should be... Solar, but weird. Sorcerously weird. They are, after all, Exalted by the corruption of a Solar Exaltation by the infernal powers of the creators of the world... creators of the world that, by what I gathered, are the original owner of what's called 'Solar Sorcery'.

    Now, that could depend on the fact that I don't know what each Yozi represents in detail and so I can't think of specific charms for all of them, but I think that having each Charm be directly linked at one Yozi is... kind of limiting in a way. A little like how Dragon-blooded Charms are linked to a particular element and that mess up the ability of the Dragon-blooded to mix-and-match them. It would a lot more simple if the charms were neutral and then acquired specific bonus depending on the patron Yozi of the Infernal (which, incidentally, is how I would home-rule the Dragon-blooded, because I hate how Aura work for the normal charms: I like much more the approach used for the Immaculate Martial Arts).

    For me, the base Infernal Charms should be, in a way, sorcerous Solar Power: they accomplish -mostly- the same thing as the Solar do, but instead of being fueled by superhuman perfection, they do it in an unnatural way. Athletics Charms don't make the Infernal faster: they slow down everyone else or distort the space so he gets there first. Lore Charms don't find secret information by virtue of illuminated knowledge: they force the information out of the dark by the dictate of the Green Sun Princes. Etc etc.

    You could build the various varieties of the Charms depending on the Yozi after that, like there are various variety of some Dragon-blooded charms. It probably is a concept more fit for Essence that for the main splat, but I would like the Infernal put in that way: it makes them more like a variation of the Solar than... whatever they were before.

                Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz View Post
                Eh, I don't think Vance and Minton should write Infernals based on Essence.
                That's not what I meant to suggest.

                I was merely suggesting that while Infernal Brawl Charms would be noticeably distinct from Solar Brawl Charms, at the end of the day, the Brawl Ability has a similar character for all Ability-based Exalted.

                Under the traditional model of printing entire books for each distinct splat, it makes sense to have an entirely separate Solar Brawl Charm tree and an entirely separate Infernal Brawl Charm tree.

                But if one wanted to simplify things, it would theoretically be possible to write a single Brawl Charm tree with each Charm having little caveats so that the Charms work a little differently for each splat.

                It would certainly be superior to how the Second Edition Abyssal manual tried to save on word-count by using "This works just like the Solar Charm, but" so often.

                                        2E Infernal charmsets seem to have been made with the intent that they should be largely self-contained and that an individual Infernal focuses on one or two with perhaps a little dabbling outside those lines. It also had each Infernal Caste tied to a specific Yozi.

                                        It seems like that's not so much going to be the case in 3E. The Ascendant Caste writeup we saw on the Discord mentioned the Yozi in collective but no singular Yozi as the Caste's patron, and combined with the plan for Ability-based Charms, I figure we're headed for Infernals who draw on the combined glory and power of the Yozi as a group overall first and foremost. I suspect we might be getting something at least vaguely akin to Lanaya's Infernal brew, in which a single Ability might draw on different Yozi for its different aspects---Adorjan is reflected in Athletics charms about speed and mobility, while Malfeas or Isidoros might be the face behind feats of strength and demolition. I think I'd like that to be the case, certainly.

                                        I also think that, a little like the stated Abyssal model, the specific Yozi nuances might be less specific at the start of the tree and get more overt as you go deeper in. Those mobility-centric Athletics Charms might start just as general expressions of hellish excellence, but then get more and more specifically reflective of Adorjan's themes as they go along.
