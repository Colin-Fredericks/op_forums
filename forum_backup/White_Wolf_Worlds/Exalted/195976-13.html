                Those would be very cool ideas for Evocations on orichalcum gear. I just wonder how much some of those effects you mentioned might be better served as native Charms. Then again we haven't seen an actual Evocation spelled out in detail yet so it's hard to say what kinds of effects are "normal" for them. I'd just be a little disappointed if the Artifact legion banner threaded through with orichalcum wire had the same mechanical effect as, say, the War Charm Rout-Stemming Gesture or whatever.

                To the second point: the only Evocation tree we've seen is for a red jade daiklave. We've heard about other ones, sure (Stormcaller, which spawns a real hurricane above you when drawn - that effect implies it may be blue jade but we don't have that detail yet), but the only cascade publicized is Volcano Cutter. So it makes sense that the Evocations on Volcano Cutter have names evoking fire and magma (it's supposed be a sword that cut a volcano, right?) and probably also playing in to volcanic effects. We haven't seen any Evocations specifically designed for an orichalcum weapon, but it stands to reason that orichalcum won't involve throwing magma at people or creating an earthquake. Probably.

                On this note I feel like jade Artifacts would be the easiest to guess Evocation effects for. They'll likely build off of colored jade elemental themes, at least loosely. The other MM have very different themes and feels, so I'd expect fewer overt elemental effects, but possibly obtaining those effects through a different method than directly doing it with elemental jade.

Or is freaky strong and snaps their sword with heavy hammering blows. This is how a Lunar swordsman beats a Solar swordsman. Sometimes the more skilled swordsman is up against an opponent who has mastered some esoteric sword style they've never heard of, like an epee fencer faced with some Shao Lin monk coming after her with paired scimitars and what the bleeding hell he just kicked me in the face I thought this was a goddam swordfight! You're not gonna be more skilled than she is, but at the end of the day her skill and her supernal connection with the soul of her sword might not save her when you're coming at her with the grace of a gazelle and the strength of a bear and almost as much skill as she's got, or if Fate demands that she slip on a banana peel right as you thrust.

                                                    Sometimes, the less skilled competitor wins, especially if the less skilled competitor has other talents that can make up or overcome the difference in skill.
