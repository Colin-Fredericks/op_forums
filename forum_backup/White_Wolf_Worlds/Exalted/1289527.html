    Sorcerous Martial Arts and Empty Mind Style

    02-19-2019, 10:50 AM
    Sorcerous Martial Arts

    Many are the esoteric paths of both the martial artist and the sorcerer; for the enlightened mind, the two are one and the same. The mind shapes the body, and the body shapes the mind. Through a combination of sacred movements, rigorous study, and careful cultivation of internal power, you have unlocked the secrets at the intersection of sorcery and martial arts.

    Initiating into a Sorcerous Martial Art requires the Martial Arts merit; mortals doing so must a Martial Art 3, in addition to Occult 3 and a five-dot merit for Sorcery.

    Shaping Rituals
    When entering combat, the sorcerer learns to harness the thrumming energy of Creation, wrapping it about her like a cloak. When making an Awareness roll to Join Battle, she may spend 1 Willpower; if she does, she gains one sorcerous mote per two successes on the roll. Sorcerous motes gained in this fashion last for the duration of the scene, and can be spent towards any spell she casts.

    The sorcerer's attacks are meant to both empower herself as well as to bring defeat to those who oppose her. She may flurry a shape sorcery action with withering martial arts attacks; when she does so, she does not roll (Intelligence + Occult) to gain sorcerous motes, and does not add her excess successes on the attack to withering damage. Instead, she gains one sorcerous mote per two excess successes on the attack roll.

    The true master sorcerer can transform the aggression of her opponents into tranquility. Each turn, the first time she is attacked, the sorcerer may spend 1 Willpower to use her opponent's power against him, gaining one sorcerous mote per success on the attack. If she is struck, she gains no sorcerous motes from the attack. Sorcerous motes gained in this fashion fade at the end of the next turn.

    Other Benefits

    Martial Sorcery (Merit ••)
    The sorcerer may spend sorcerous motes on the Martial Arts charm for a specific style. This merit may be purchased multiple times, adding an additional Style with each purchase as the sorcerer unlocks deeper unity between them.

    Sorcerous motes spent on these Charms can be consumed from those already gathered as part of a previous shape sorcery action; if this reduces the gathered motes to zero, then the spell is lost as normal. Sorcerers may also use shape sorcery actions to gather motes specifically for sorcerous martial arts; motes gathered in this fashion fade at a rate of three per action not spent on shape sorcery actions, and may only be spent to pay for Martial Arts charms for styles purchased with this merit.

    Mortals who purchase this merit may purchase Martial Arts charms as spells. Mortals using Martial Arts charms are treated as Dragon-Blooded martial artists for the purposes of the Mastery and Terrestrial keywords, and increase in Essence at the same rate as a Solar. However, they do not gain personal or peripheral essence pools, and cannot learn Charms or other Essence-driven powers other than sorcerous spells and Martial Sorcery Styles.

    Burning Radiance (Merit •••)
    When gathering sorcerous motes, the sorcerer may manifest the gathering power as a blazing light that burns the eyes of those who raise arms against her.

    You may reflexively activate Burning Radiance at any time you have gathered one or more sorcerous motes. This creates a blazing aura, equivilent in intensity to an anima at bonfire, and which imposes a penalty of -(Essence or 3, whichever is less), consuming one sorcerous mote per turn. Once activated, Burning Radiance cannot be extinguished as long as you have sorcerous motes. Although this aura resembles an Exalt's anima banner, it cannot be spent to fuel charms which consume anima levels.

    Exalted who know this technique will typically manifest an aura which resembles their anima; the appearance for mortal sorcerers is more varied.

    All Things Presently (Merit ••••)
    Masters of Sorcerous Martial Arts know the body and mind are one, and that the Dance of Twelve Trigrams, the Eight Secret Breaths, and the One-Hundred-Twenty-Eight Gestures are fundamentally the same as the katas and movements that underlay combat. Sorcerers with this Merit may spend one Willpower to flurry shape sorcery actions with withering Martial Arts attacks. They gain the Willpower back if the attack is successful.

    ---

    Empty Mind Style
    What was your face before you were born? What is the name of the wind? When you do nothing, are you doing anything? How do you describe blue to a blind woman?

    Students of the Empty Mind Style will spend hours meditating upon paradoxical koans and sayings; masters of the Style can seem unusual, sometimes even deranged. But to those of enlightened mind, all things are possible, and the flow of the universe becomes obvious. Practitioners will often seem to fight in a weak, even passive stance; but their light touches carry surprising force, and their lazy dodges place them always a hairsbreadth out of reach.

    While every Martial Arts Style can be learned as a Sorcerous Martial Art, Empty Mind Style is perhaps the most common of those rarefied techniques.

    Empty Mind Weapons: You cannot practice the Empty Mind by filling your hands; Empty Mind Style may only be used bare-handed.

    Armor: Empty Mind Style is incompatible with armor.

    ---

    Calm Heart Skill
    Cost: 3m; Mins: Martial Arts 1, Essence 1
    Type: Supplemental
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: None

    Adherents of the Empty Mind learn to clear their thoughts and remain present during raging storms, in sweltering heat, and while being beaten with bamboo reeds. With but a moment, she make herself ready for all things, and re-rolls up to (Martial Arts) 1s or 2s on an Awareness roll.

    All-Seeing Eye of God
    Cost: 3m, 3i; Mins: Martial Arts 2, Essence 1
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: Mastery, Perilous
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Calm Heart Skill

    Going further, the students of the Empty Mind become attuned to the smallest disturbances of breath and tremor. Even when blindfolded or struck in total darkness, she deftly weaves away as if seeing with the eyes of God. The martial artist ignores the Defense penalty for defending against a surprise attack. (Exalted p. 203)
    Mastery: The martial artist retains her Defense against ambush attacks (Exalted p. 203), to a maximum of 2.

    Continuation of Motion
    Cost: 7m; Mins: Martial Arts 2, Essence 1
    Type: Supplemental
    Keywords: Terrestrial
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Calm Heart Skill

    The illusion of control chains and trips people, but the martial artist has learned to unbind herself from those chains. She walks unimpeded through overgrown forests, and dances atop boggy marshes or heavy drifts of snow. The martial artist ignores the penalties for moving through difficult terrain. (Exalted p. 199)
    Terrestrial: She still incurs the -3 penalty when attempting to rush, disengage, or withdraw across difficult terrain, but does not require two move actions to move through such.

    Empty Mind Form
    Cost: 7m; Mins: Martial Arts 3, Essence 1
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: Form
    Duration: One scene
    Prerequisite Charms: All-Seeing Eye of God, Continuation of Motion

    Swaying to unheard music, the martial artist dances with the cosmos. Observers may believe she is drunk or mad, but the enlightened can see that she sways in time with the universal beat. She increases her base initiative by 1, usually to 4 instead of 3.
    Special activation rules: Whenever the martial artist resets to base initiative, she may reflexively activate Empty Mind Form and gain this benefit.

    Spontaneous Effort
    Cost: 1m; Mins: Martial Arts 4, Essence 2
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Empty Mind Form

    Too much thinking can make you stupid; too much looking leaves you blind. The martial artist is relaxed and ready for any opportunities which present themselves to her. She eliminates the initiative cost for delaying action (Exalted p. 196)

    The Instant Now
    Cost: 1m+; Mins: Martial Arts 5, Essence 3
    Type: Supplemental
    Keywords: Decisive-only, Terrestrial
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Spontaneous Effort

    There is no time but the present; the past is beyond our reach and the future does not yet exist. Internalizing this truth enables the martial artist to take seemingly trivial actions with profound effects. For each mote spent on this charm, she may reduce the initiative cost or the difficulty of the Disarm, Distract, or Unhorse gambits (Exalted p. 200), to a minimum of 1. At the Storyteller's discretion, the Instant Now may also be used with other gambits, but cannot cause direct harm or result in prolonged contact with the target, such as a Grapple.
    Terrestrial: The martial artist may not reduce the difficulty or initiative cost to less than 2.

    Yielding To Adversity
    Cost: 3m, 2i; Mins: Martial Arts 4, Essence 2
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: Mastery, Perilous
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Empty Mind Form

    Time is long, and even the Imperial Mountain will one day be worn away to nothing more than a small hill by the endless repetition of wind and tide. Foolish men will stand like strong oaks against a storm only to be battered down; the martial artist instead yields to the tide and the storm, turning its power to her own. Each 1 on an opponent's attack roll cancels one success, to a maximum of (Martial Arts).
    Mastery: Each 1 or 2 on the attack roll cancels one success.

    Impassive Existence
    Cost: 3m, 1i; Mins: Martial Arts 5, Essence 3
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: Perilous, Terrestrial
    Duration: One turn
    Prerequisite Charms: Yielding to Adversity

    The wind does not care if it blows, and the tides do not concern themselves if they are rising or falling; because they do not care, they will last forever. The martial artist emulates their lack of concern, walking heedless through conflict. She ignores onslaught penalties to her Defense for the remainder of the turn. If she used Impassive Existence the previous turn, she does not need to pay the initiative cost again.
    Terrestrial: The martial artist must always pay the initiative cost when using Impassive Existence.

    The Force of Lightness
    Cost: 3m, 1wp; Mins: Martial Arts 4, Essence 2
    Type: Supplemental
    Keywords: Withering-only, Mastery
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Empty Mind Form

    The martial artist bears no weapon, but she is not unarmed, for she bears the universe against her foes. With deceptively gentle movements, she can bring enormous forces to bear that will buckle the strongest armor or send the hardiest warriors sprawling. This charm allows her to make a Chopping, Piercing, or Smashing attack (Exalted p. 585-586), as if her unarmed attack had the appropriate tags.
    Mastery: Clash attacks enhanced with the Force of Lightness also treat the martial artist as dual wielding.

    Existence Without Definition
    Cost: 7m, 3i; Mins: Martial Arts 5, Essence 3
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: Perilous, Dual
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: The Force of Lightness

    Injury and death are illusions the body casts upon the mind; the unfettered mind frees itself of such limits. Those who appear to successfully strike the martial artist often find themselves mistaken, as she increases her natural soak by (Martial Arts) and gains (Martial Arts) Hardness against a single attack. If the attack inflicts no damage, it is considered to have missed. Existence Without Definition may be used after the attack has hit but before damage is rolled.

    Erasing The Concept of Emptiness
    Cost: 7m; Mins: Martial Arts 5, Essence 4
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Spontaneous Effort, Yielding to Adversity, Existence Without Definition

    Life and death, love and hate, peace and war; all dualities reinforce the lie of separation which the enlightened mind transcends. The martial artist exits Initiative Crash, resetting to base initiative (Exalted p. 194). Erasing the Concept of Emptiness may only be used once per combat.
