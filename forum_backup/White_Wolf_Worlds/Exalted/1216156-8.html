                        So I guess this is the thread for custom sorcery spells? I was going to just post in the Sorcerous Workings thread, but if nobody minds me bumping an older thread then here's an idea I had earlier.

                        Honestly, I was originally thinking of more interesting ways to produce food than Food From the Aerial Table, and I ended up with this weird concept. The original idea was something like "what if you used a sorcerous working to give someone an ability similar to the Second Bread thaumaturgy ritual," but I decided that something too similar to an existing power wasn't a great choice. Hatching animals out of plants was probably vaguely inspired by Odin Sphere.

                        Wood Dragon's Clutch (Terrestrial Circle Spell)
                        Cost: Ritual, 1wp
                        Keywords: None
                        Duration: Instant

                        The sorcerer plants a fist-sized "seed" in the earth consisting of one or more body parts from an animal. Any part(s) of the animal will do -- meat, bones, organs, whatever else -- as long as all the material is from one species of creature; the earth need not be suitable for growing normal crops, but it must be at least moist -- even desert sand will do as long as the sorcerer pours water on it. Over the course of an hour, the sorcerer covers the "seed" with earth while reciting a ritual giving of thanks; sorcerers of the First Age gave praise to Gaia for nature's gifts, but Dragon-Blooded sorcerers in the Age of Sorrows give acknowledgement to Sextes Jylis instead.

                        During the next five hours, a shrub-like plant grows, eventually producing "fruit" in the form of a clutch of eggs. Each egg hatches into a healthy juvenile (not newborn) member of the species that originally gave rise to the new plant; they are generally typical examples of the species, and have enough instinctive knowledge to take care of themselves if left to their own devices, but no special training. The new animals hatch with a Minor Tie of affection towards the sorcerer that grew them, and are unlikely to attack the sorcerer or his associates in the short term, but the Tie is not guaranteed to last -- an unprepared sorcerer who is not skilled in animal husbandry might very well see his creations disperse into the wild depending on their temperament.

                        The number of eggs produced depends on the size of the animal in question:
                        - An animal the size of a quail, dove, or other small bird yields (sorcerer's Essence * 4) eggs
                        - An animal the size of a chicken, duck, turkey, or large rat yields (sorcerer's Essence * 2) eggs
                        - An animal the size of a sheep, goat, or small pony yields (sorcerer's Essence) eggs
                        - An animal the size of a horse, cow, bison, or camel yields (sorcerer's Essence)/2 rounded up eggs
                        - This spell isn't capable of producing creatures with Legendary Size.
                        - This spell also doesn't work on humans, or on spirits of any sort.

                        This spell is not an effective method for breeding high-quality animals. While the hatched animals are not all identical and display some degree of variation, they are not the children of the particular animal(s) who donated the "seed" -- burying the bones of a fine Marukani horse will yield only horses that are more or less average. The spell is more often used to produce animals for food or other farming purposes.

                        A sorcerer who knows Wood Dragon's Clutch as her control spell can use the spell for selective breeding -- if she so chooses, the newly hatched animals will be the genetic offspring of the seed's donor(s). This effect is always optional.


                        Titan-Hatching Seedling (Celestial Circle Spell)
                        Cost: Ritual, 1wp
                        Keywords: None
                        Duration: Instant

                        This spell functions as Wood Dragon's Clutch, but may produce a single creature with Legendary Size (or at least, a single juvenile creature that will have Legendary Size when fully grown). The plant takes seven hours instead of five to grow in this case. A sorcerer with Essence 6+ can hatch a pair of such animals instead.
