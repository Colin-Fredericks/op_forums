                                    Playing Malfeas's advocate for a moment, they may disagree. Holden noted their frustration that the leak(s) robbed them of the ability to control hype development. I have no idea if releasing rather a lot of content in very short order is the best way to do that, but I can see a case for it being more valuable than it looks. IF that's their plan, they've got between them a hell of a lot more experience than my little nil; I'd not really be in a legitimate position to criticize.
