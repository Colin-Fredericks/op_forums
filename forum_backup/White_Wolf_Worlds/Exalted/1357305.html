    Two Secret Organizations, a White Elephant for Measure of Hope

    12-28-2019, 12:10 AM
    "I am partial to art, particularly cool creature art, or character portraits - PM me for details if you like. For writerly types, I'd love an interesting location or organisation with a backstory and some story hooks."



    The Dragon Eaters and the 5 Secret Colors

    The Dragon Eaters are similar to a Crime Syndicate “super group”. Informally lead by the Night Caste Solar Smiling Tiger, they are assassins and crime lords of various competing or affiliated gangs putting aside their normal squabbles for a single shared purpose: keeping the Realm out of their world, the criminal underbelly of society. Smiling Tiger has made it his mission to punish and kill any Dynasts who want to dip their toes in the world of crime, and he has a special hatred for the Great Houses. He sees them as exploitative and subversive, looking to turn the crime world against itself and become yet another pawn in the political games of the Great Houses. Smiling Tiger has thus made a deal with the various criminal factions he encounters: they get him info on the Dynastic Meddlers and he kills them. And the crime bosses who choose to work for them. Now in their third year, the various crime syndicates such as the Ghost Haired Girls (lead by the Lunar Te'Te, Queen of Toxins) and the Flower Street Boys (under the rule of the massive mortal King Hippo) are enjoying soaring profits and much lower interference from the strings of the Scarlet Court. The Dragon Eaters are thus an informal alliance with no regular meetings. Any member may send a spokesperson to the other gangs and call a meeting when the need arises. Of course, they wouldn't be crime lords if they didn't use this as yet another opportunity to back stab and steamroll other gangs, but as long as they keep it from interfering with the main mission, Smiling Tiger is usually content to leave them to it. Unless they push too far, as the Lintha backed Sea Stabbers found out the hard way when the body of their beastkin boss Eight Arms Ring was delivered to them in a series of small packages.

    Smiling Tiger- Night Caste. Larceny Supernal. Ebon Shadow Style.
    Defining Tie: Great Houses (Hatred); Crime Syndicates (True Brotherhood)

    Te'Te- Lunar. Poison master and Gang Leader. Snake Style Expert.
    Defining Tie: My Gang, the Ghost Hair Girls (protect at all costs)
    Defining Principle: Indirect Means are always the best course

    * * * * *

    The 5 Secret Colors
    Weeds grow in the garden of Justice

    The 5 Secret Colors are one of Creation's longest serving groups and the oldest to have never been discovered by outsiders. Despite the name, there are 6 members, 5 assassins and 1 leader, called the Grand Master. The 5 Secret colors are all Exalts of various backgrounds who share a single defining hatred of injustice and abuse of power, and the group exists to take down those who are shielded from justice by their power or position, such as corrupt judges, sadistic royalty, and would-be tyrants. The group is secretive even from their own members, each assassin takes a mask and a code name, and the 5 only seldom meet when their secretive Grand Master calls them in assembly. They each pursue their own brand of justice and choose their own targets, but may petition the Grand Master to have another member carry out an assassination on someone out of their reach or too close to avoid suspicion. Each member is carefully selected among the potential candidates to have a unique approach to assassination , both in technique and philosophy. In this way, the group is never suspected of even existing and no one assassin can be recognized by signature style.

    The members are as follows:
    Grand Master: identity unknown. Exaltation or Divinity type unkown. Has lead the group since the First Age and whose mission is always some form of hidden but necessary justice. Wears plain grey robes and a simple kabuki mask of a grand father.

    Cloud over the August Moon
    Bio: Female, Lunar, No Moon caste. 2nd in command.
    Appearance: A beautiful woman with some exotic features
    Color: Wears all white and a Kitsune mask. She wears white to challenge herself.
    Day Job: Courtesan or Noblewoman's Maid
    Style: Pacifist Ghost Run- never seen, no guards killed.
    Trigger Injustice: Abuse of Women

    Blood of Oni
    Bio: Male, newest member. Seldom speaks. Solar, Night Caste (Athletics Supernal)
    Appearance: A muscular giant, near 7 feet tall.
    Color: Wears a red gi and an Oni mask. Wears red to invoke fear of killers.
    Day Job: traveling laborer
    Style: Speed Run- Start to finish as quick and direct as possible, kill anyone in the way
    Trigger Injustice: Abuse of the Poor

    Black Scorpion
    Bio: Male, Exigent of the Scorpion God of Assassins
    Appearance: Handsome man of middle age, average build
    Color: Wears black finery and a cruel Scorpion motif mask. Black to inspire terror.
    Day Job: Arms Merchant
    Style: Methodical- Carefully kills every guard first, then the mark. Hides and waits in shadows
    Trigger Injustice: Abuse of Merchants

    The Falling of Spring Leaves
    Bio: Woman, Wood Aspect Dragon Blood
    Appearance: Young noblewoman, disarmingly baby-faced
    Color: Light Green with a mask of a popular forest spirit- wears green to remember connection to life
    Day Job: Medicine maker and herbalist, also a low ranked Dynast
    Style: Poisoner- poisons target a mix of undetectable drugs that produce tailored, “natural” deaths
    Trigger Injustice: Abuse of nature and the spirits

    There group is currently searching for a 5th member. Cloud over the August Moon makes the choice.

          First of all, thank you for your kind words!

          Originally posted by Measure of Hope View Post

Do they ever take commissions?
          Have the colours been consistent throughout their history?

          You decide! But to me, no. The colors and their color-theory baggage are part of the identity of the Assassins and a way to communicate to the others how they operate.
          Black Scorpion, for example, kills everyone, and then he goes on to the main mark. It also references his "stick to shadows" theme. So Black is heavily coded as evil, dark, etc. Blood of Oni, however, is a massive man who fights unarmed and takes down guards head on before they can even react. He wears Red to show how "direct" and "bloody" his style is. He wasn't given a specific role as the "red ranger" a la Power Rangers. He's just a red-type, which his outfit reflects. You could think of it as colors in MtG or types of Jade.

          It sounds like each assassin's colour is a personal choice, so is it a case of "pick one that isn't taken that's meaningful to you" when a new member joins, and then they get a personalised mask and outfit?​

          It's chosen for them. I cut them from the write up to not bias you, but Cloud over the August Moon is the longest serving member currently and she she is the one who picks new assassins. She decides what mask and color to give them. Blood of Oni, for example, has the Hideous Merit, and is both powerful and direct, so Oni fits his persona.

          One thing I will say is that I view them as a sort of well-rounded team. There will always be more potentials than members so if say, Falling of Spring Leaves gets offed than Cloud isn't going to pick another Brawler type like Oni because that would unbalance them. Assassin's strike where the enemy is weakest, and diversification opens up new avenues. Or to quote Major Kusanagi "When you overspecialize you breed in weakness".

          Have the injustices been consistent or are prospective members assessed on their chosen causes as well as their methods?

          The Latter. Only people who turn to assassination when justice has failed them otherwise are eligible to be members. In that sense, each one is a reaction to a certain way that people suffer. Black Scorpion, for example, punishes those who prey on merchants, because he is one and he saw many good business men ruined when lords voided debts owed or seized merchandise. This is a pretty rare backstory for Assassins, who don't usually count "merchants" among the people who need protecting. Cloud Over the August Moon was impressed by Scorpion's views and commitment, so he was offered a mask and color.

          Does the Grand Master finance them or do they have their own revenue streams?

          Almost exclusively the latter. Grand Master gives new members what s/he thinks they need for their new life as an assassin and will occasionally come through with special items- passports, keys, etc. Black Scorpion is quite wealthy and lives as a rich socialite and therefore doesn't need anything. Blood of Oni is a poor wanderer (Resources 0. dude can barely keep fed), so he has occasionally been given money to get him from A to B. Spring Leaves gets rare medicines and plants sent to her when she may need them, but everything from Grand Master is always hands off. Even August Moon doesn't know anything about him/her and could not backtrack who Grand Master is.

          Do they ever take commissions?

          NEVER. Not once in their history have they taken a commission. To do so would mean an automatic expulsion from the 5 colors (and if they try to out heir former comrades, death). Have individual members ever killed for less than ideal reasons? yes. Exalted are still people and sometimes even people make mistakes. Spring Leaves killed a man she really shouldn't have because he was two-timing her and stole some of her expensive medicines to share as party drugs. Blood of Oni didn't kill a man once because he was a good father to his young daughter. Black Scorpion was once tricked into killing the wrong man. It happens. Grand Master met with each one afterwards and had a discussion with them about their failings. The will to try to do better is probably GM's most valued virtue.

          You didn't ask, but here's some likely questions:

          Can they quit?

          Yes. It's not a death-cult. When Black Scorpion feels that his city and his region are free of the abuse of tyrants, he'll hang up his mask. He kills for a cause, and that cause has an end. Blood of Oni will only quit when the poor are no longer abused, so never. Spring Leaves will quit if she decides that killing is doing more harm than good. Some members in the past have joined just to get a chance to kill a single, specific mark and then walk away. The Solar known as Snow Secret was one of these members.

          Who is Grand Master?

          Even I don't know. But I think it's a god of some kind. maybe a functionary of heaven. Somebody that the Bureaucracy would never suspect. Could be a Sidereal. Maybe. Grand Master calls meetings and will hand out assassination marks. No one knows what the big picture here is, and no one wants to.

          Ideals Over Everything

          Honest-to-God members of the Wyld Hunt have been active members along side Lunar Anathema that they are hunting in their "day job". A core part of the 5 Secret Colors is that members put aside their personal lives to punish those who abuse justice, and that loyalty is higher than any other. August Moon has killed a Lunar before. Spring Leaves regularly kills Dynasts in her own House. Black Scorpion had to kill someone he liked in his day-life. That's the price of having an ideal.

          No Wild Cards

          No thrill killers allowed. No one who decides to step "out of the shadows". No calling cards. No warnings. Assassination is a solemn thing, and anyone who starts enjoying it too much risks compromising their ideals. It's happened before. It will happen again. It always leads to...

          The Erasure

          Sometimes necessary. Blue Waves let power go to his head and started killing pirates and people he thought might be pirates. He got a warning from Grey Mists, but Blue Waves didn't care at that point. Killing people he didn't like was his religion. He awoke, miles outside of his own home, next to grandmaster in an empty disused temple. There was just a knife on a rice paper sheet in front of him. After that, Grey Mists replaced him with a young Lunar- White Cloud.

          Spotting the Theme

          This is just me, but I designed each member first around how they killed, and everything about them came after that. On top of that, each one is designed around common Stealth game strategies. Cloud over the August Moon is the player who memorizes the guards loop and tricks and makes it to the end of the level without being seen. A perfect stealth pacifist run. Black Scorpion is the "kill em all" style of stealth player- patiently wait for an opening, kill guard, hide body, advance to next guard. Blood of Oni is the speed runner. He beelines for the boss and kills the guards he would have to spend time avoiding and leaves before the bodies are found. Spring Leaves is the indirect Hitman style of kills, where you poison someone's medicine cabinet and then wait calmly in public for them to die because no one even suspects its a murder.

          There's a lot of design room for unique styles. You hit upon Betrayal already, motivate someone else to do it. An Exalt who makes the target kill themselves. Someone who arranges "accidents". Someone who commits open murder but wearing another's face. Be creative.

          And feel free to ask questions, anyone! I pour a lot of effort into Exalted stuff. I love sharing it.

                      I'm gonna flesh out more about the 5 Secret Colors and Smiling Tiger. There will be rolling updates to this thread even if its just not space for myself. Like I said, originally I didn't want my creator bias to tell people what to do with them which is why they are location and plot generic, and also I have played a few of these characters before.

                      The 5 Secret Colors

                      The first ever assembly of these assassins came about in the First Age, after the Primeval war but before the Usurpation. The one known as Grand Master appeared in a humble grey robe, wearing a mask of featureless grey wood, which look inexpertly whittled. Grand Master was seen, or rather, let themselves be seen, by a few of the greatest Stealth experts in creation: Three Songs Unsung, Twin-Moons Jaguar, Destruction on Silver Wings, Falling Knife, and A Silence Between Lovers. The 5 experts, united under the common cause of defeating Injustice, put aside their differences and delayed some of their personal goals to achieve what each thought impossible- the eradication of untouchable villainy. Exalts, Gods, Demons- none were beyond their blade. Three Songs Unsung sang so beautifully that many were seduced into their own death. Twin-Moons Jaguar dispatched many by moving through the smallest cracks and narrowest patches of darkness as a common and unseen spider, and so on. Each one eventually came to their own end of tenure- by choice or by untimely death. Grand Master after the first member left (Falling Knife, from his own death) the Grand Master gave each assassin a mask, so that a new initiate - who had not sworn the same sacred vow to each other in equality- would not know her fellow's real identities. One by one they were replaced and the tradition held. There has never been a time when the group was disbanded, even after the Usurpation, Contagion, Balorian Crusade, and into the Time of Sorrows.

                      Details on the current members:

                      Cloud Over the August Moon- No Moon Lunar in her fourth century. She was originally a target of the 5 Secret Colors, and not a prospective member. As a Lunar out of East, she viewed "mere humans" as worthless and valued beastkin above them. Her cruel reign under the name The Queen Beyond Sight saw the killing of many human leaders and the expanse of a beastman horde. Strict social castes were enforced, and the humans were worked near to death and used as fodder. When the assassin Grey Mists came for her, he interrupted her own mission to kill a captain of the Wyld Hunt. She was soundly defeated, but before the killing blow was struck she pleaded with Grey Mists to at least warn the beastkin of her death and the attack of the Wyld Horde so they might flee into the Eastern Forest. He did not. He hit her with a paralyzing strike, as was his art, and left her to watch the devastation. Suffering greater than ever before, she was amazed when the humans she had so cruelly abused during her reign broke rank and joined the Wyld Hunt- The Queen had assumed that her rule over their minds was total and absolute. The beastkin were defeated in a rout, and The Queen was left to watch it in helpless horror. Suffering a type of ego death, she spent an entire day and night staring at the battlefield. When she moved at last, Grey Mists was there with a white robe and a white fox mask. The Queen Beyond Sight was dead, and Cloud Over the August Moon was born to atone for her sins.

                      Blood of Oni- Night Caste, Athletics Supernal. Living as the wandering day-laborer Stray Dog, he went where the work was and spent his days laboring and his nights alone. He had no interest in women, and he seldom found a man who would look at him with affection. Stray Dog is almost seven feet tall, and he is a mishmash of faded scars and sunburnt skin. He was one of the strongest mortals in Creation by virtue of his sheer muscular bulk but he was made ugly, as if each part of him was a sculpture only partially finished. He possesses the Hideous (**) Merit. At they age of 44, he settled down in a small but prosperous town and began apprenticing in medicine studies under the Apothecary master Li Shun. The town they lived in, Red Gate, is the site of a Moon-and-Mountain festival once a year, and many noblemen come. One of them, terrible and sadistic, stole away Li Shun's daughter for rape and mocked her afterwards by shaving her hair off as a keepsake. Stray dog found the daughter, Li Zhara, and brought her home in the early hours before sunrise. Filled with the same simple resolve he had used as a laborer, Stray Dog moved like an arrow through the city and over the walls of the Nobleman's District, he went through any guards he encountered lethally and dispassionately. The nobleman was not so lucky. Every bony in his body, every muscle, every joint that was not critical to supporting his life was broken cleanly. He was tied to the Red Gate with the hair keepsakes he had collected. He was given powerful stimulants to keep him awake and insure he remained breathing until he was found. A powerful Dynast came with a personal guard in the following weeks, and burned the entire city to the ground after what was done to his grandson, the broken noble. Stray Dog exalted upon living through a night of torture and killed the old Terrestrial with his bare hands. As he stood in the ashes of his once-new home, he became aware of a woman in white wearing a fox mask. She held out a mask for him- an Oni with a dispassionate yet fierce grimace. He accepted her offer.
