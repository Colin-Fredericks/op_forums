    What artefacts have you taken? What fun did you have with their evocations?

    10-05-2019, 04:57 PM
    (This is about Evocation sets, not "I took a jade breastplate" or whatever)

    Despite me and several of my players owning Arms of the Chosen, I've rarely seen anyone actually use any evocations. Many of the PCs have artefact weapons, but they're pretty generic orichalcum daiklaives or staffs or whatever they took when the game started, before these books came out with more options, so I haven't seen Evocations used much. There's a few with cool backstories (like the Walrus-totem's grimcleaver made of his own tusk), but as they're not from the books, they don't have a fully-realised set of evocations.
    (There's also some stuff they took from dead Dragonblood, that as ST I picked from the books, like Rainwalker, Shining Ice Mirror, etc. But they traded them back for peace/threw down the back of the sofa and forgot about them. But they didn't use them.)

    My friend's theo-fascist Dawn warlord has Five Edicts Dominion, which mostly seems to involve people attacking him and then falling over prone while he laughs contemptuously.
    I had Godspeed Vanguard, which was pretty cool; you could sneak around invisibly which is quite entertaining as a 30ft golden golem. Though sadly I didn't get to use it much.
    That's pretty much it.

    So I'd like to hear from you; what evocation sets have you taken? What fun have you had with the Evocations?

    STing Bronze Age Exalted

                  I used Sun's Brush on my Lore Supernal, which was a lot of fun flavor-wise. You haven't experienced true Solar Hubris until you've written "IGNORANCE" in golden letters on a giant flaming demon with the effect of lowering all their dice proportionally to how much dumber they are than you.

                  I also had Asphodel, which basically ended up being a neat excuse for the ST to give us hints and tips in return for doing small favors for the ruler of the city inside Asphodel.


                  A Circle mate used the Heaven and Earth Gauntlets, which are absolutely amazing power-wise. He never got to the capstone which lets you divert the course of mountains with a single punch, but the Evocations he did get saved his life more than once.

                  That Circle mate also had the Ultimately Useful Tube, which with its 1000 uses was a perfect fit for his comic relief type character.


                  Another Circle mate was the most unsubtle Night ever, a building-lifting Athletics Supernal who expressed her pyromania with the Sekhem flamethrower. That thing is extremely potent for Jade, and it single-handedly turned the tide of battle from a total party wipe to a total victory against Mahicara the Volcanic Earthwalker. I came out of that fight with the feeling that that Behemoth was designed for the explicit purpose of being utterly destroyed by Sehkem.


                  A third Circle mate had Stormcaller. His player struggles with roleplaying, so we were worried he would have trouble playing with a cursed 5 dot artifact. We warned him it would have a huge impact on his character but he insisted on taking it. And then as we feared he started disliking his character when his story became centered on his relationship with the cursed blade and whether he would manage to fight its evil influence. He ended up dropping his character altogether and switching for a new one.

                          Well, my first solar character, Modest Verse, was a wandering sorcerer-exorcist partly inspired in Miroku from Inu-Yasha, so his first "artifact" was actually his cursed right arm, represented by Wood Dragon's Claws as control spell.

                          Used "green jade/wood magic make things grow or wither and siphon Essence from living things" as an excuse to steal evocations from soulsteel artifacts like Asphodel and Soulfarer both to make my own weird set, where the arm's vague setience awareness that occasionally messed with his mind in eerie prophetic mad dreams (“Scarred by Nightmares” archetype) would eventually evolve into full bloom (sorry) conscience, a creature living and ruling a strange dream-world of its own for which the PC's arm acted as a gate of sorts.

                          Later on the ST pointed out those might be too powerful a set of evocations for just a terrestrial circle spell, so we made a custom occult charm or two to boost the artifact level of my control spell into 5 and leave me free rein with the crazy.

                          (yeah, the charms did some other cool things related to thaumaturgy and occult actions, but running amok with the evocations as i alreaady did was my point all along)



                          For a Dragon-blooded game, i had considered making a kid that exalted in the middle of some podunk in the Isle, was turned into a sort of feral, Mowgli-esque figure and self-taught sorcerer & "wood gods king" through a mix of essence fever, inexperience and luck, until a group of monks looking for an unruly spirit got quite a surprise but managed to restrain him and take the young feral dragon to be properly civilized through immaculate education and indoctrination with, mixed, ressults. My idea was to see him eventually turning into a literal dragon, snatching Mela's Coils set of evocations as reference for that.

                          But then someone pointed out that "bad holy man with a proclivilty for summoning and crazy shenanigans with Wood Dragon's Claws" was pretty much a summary of my Solar too, so i decided to try something else.

                          (I might have also decided that my follow-up idea - using Mela's Coil "become a lesser elemental dragon" capstone charm as a springboard for buying Warstrider charms might be a little too much abuse for just the control spell of a self-taught young DB. Maybe....)


                          Instead at present i'm divided between two concepts:
                          - a geomancer with a passion for jewelry, hearthstones, the "spirit of stones" and understand why some of them could manifest evocations and others not.
                          - a young dynast so hilariously obsessed with riches he's experimenting with "jade is magic - jade is money - money is magic" associations to create a arcane art he calls mammonism (Resources Evocations).

                          I'm almost tempted to combine them in one, if i didn't know trying to toy with two sets evocation-based magics would be a ridiculous XP drain to actually explore ingame.

                                  I make basically bespoke artifacts for all of my players, both weapon and armor.

                                  The reason for this is primarily for theming issues. As I have particular things and ideas for stories and how I want to present them, Evocations enhance the told story and offer avenues of interpretation.

                                  For instance all of my Dragonblooded PCs in my DB game have an Evocation on either their weapon or armor that gives them the ability to launch a free Elemental Bolt attack if they execute a certain condition. This both allows the player to be rewarded for playing to their concept and let's me include themes in the artifact and how it processes. Similarly, their weapons are designed to tell a story about the DB as an individual, and strengthens those stories.

                                  Only one of my players has really gone all in on their artifact so far but I'm thinking the others will soon.

                                  I actually love Artifacts and often make them for my group's pcs and NPCs, though they aren't often invested in as much as I do.

                                  My Dawn also has a cursed sword he bargins evocations from but those are more rough draft powers that I don't know much about and he's not willing to let it off the leash.

                                                      Originally posted by JohnDoe244 View Post
                                                      The sample Evocations in the Corebook for Shining Ice Mirror are explicitly not for use by Dragon-Blooded.

                                                      "Below are a number of sample artifact weapons and armor, and their associated Evocations. Unless otherwise specified, these are designed for use by Solar characters, and will yield up fewer Evocations, Evocations of reduced power, or even nothing whatsoever in the hands of other sorts of characters. [...]
                                                      I'll be honest, I assumed that was essentially the early form of Resonant/Dissonant. That's how I run it.

                                                      Ie a Dragonblood gets Evocations of the same power (mostly the same, but obviously they'll have something different to the ones that have Solar charms as prerequisites) as a Solar when wielding Shining Ice Mirror, Spring Razor or Volcano Cutter; while a Lunar gets slightly weaker charms, and a Sidereal weaker still, and they may not be able to access the final charm. Similarly, a Lunar gets the same evocations with Moonlit Huntress as a Solar and everyone else less, a Sidereal gets the same evocations with Freedom's Cadence as a Solar but everyone else less, and an Abyssal gets the same evocations with Black Wind as a Solar and everyone else less.

                                                      Otherwise it just doesn't fit with the rules in all the rest of the books. (Obviously that's because it was written before those had been fully fleshed out, but that seems the obvious way to square that circle).
