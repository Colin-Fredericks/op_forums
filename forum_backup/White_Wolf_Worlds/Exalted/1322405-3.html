            Tactics:

            So Flame-Borne Interception goes up on turn 1 to protect Rexy.

            Rexy then launches bites to build Initiative followed by unopposed grapples backed by Raging Devil Beast Empowerment. Throat-Ripping Execution plus Devouring Legion Maw on the resulting decisive savage attack backed by Deadly Thrash or Ferocious Bite and you're instagibbing pretty much anything. Burning Fang Strike should be insane overkill, but I've just come from the thread about fighting Ahlat so...

            (I would allow you to start play with Rexy's Latent abilities unlocked, and I would roll you into play each on your own Initative Track. But this build does not depend on either concession.)

            Otherwise it plays not dissimilarly to the Fire Melee build or Invincible Sword Dynast I posted earlier in the thread. If Rexy has his own turn, it's 5m on the Excellency, Withering, Smash attack. That's 18 dice to hit. Or forego the Excellency for a Withering Colossal Whirling Strike Smash Attack if you can hit with only 13 dice.

            Crimson Fang Bite plus Windrider Swiftness costs 8m 1WP for +10 Withering damage, or +5 Decisive with double 10s. Aim and throw in another 8m on Dragon Soul Burst and you're rolling 10 dice of Hardness ignoring Decisive damage with double 10s... and you're not resetting to base after... AND you're building Charge which is upping your Withering damage. If you can afford the total of 21m 2WP to tack on Colossal Whirling Strike that's 15 Decisive Damage (ignoring Hardness) with double-10s that doesn't reset you to Base and increases your Withering damage by 1 for the scene.

            Heck, if we're going big, throw on the Excellency and make it a Smash enhanced by Bone Crushing Strike (which costs 1 Charge so no increased Withering damage until you can afford to repurchase Chambered Fist Charge): Accuracy 20, Decisive Damage 15B (double 10s, do not reset to Base), Sets enemies on Fire for 3L/turn if you deal 3+ levels of damage, Knocks foes Prone or back two Range Bands, trade 1 level of damage to impose a -3 penalty on all target's attack and movement rolls for the scene. 30m 2WP 1i. Straight to Bonfire (including Fire Anima Flux for a second set of 3i/L/turn), Fire Aura. If you knocked your foe Prone that puts them at -6 to attacks, -1 Parry, -2 Evasion, -1 Onslaught, unable to take movement actions (including contesting Rushes and Disengages) except a Difficulty 2 (Dexterity+Dodge-3) roll to rise from Prone. And they're on Fire and probably deep into their -2s for Wound Penalties (-8 to attack, and they can't even attempt to attack you unless they have a Ranged or Reaching weapon, -5 to the difficulty 2 Dodge roll to stand up).

            Main benefit is you can't be attacked unless your opponent has ranged/Reaching or climbs up your T-rex. And your Smash attacks are now basically free.

            Flying up to Long range above the ground and peppering your foes with arrows is a cheesy peasant technique. But if your ST comes at you with flying enemies or kills off Rexy then that's your back-up tactic. It's a real fun killer for everyone involved (as is ordering Rexy to backpeddle whilst you pepper foes) because it makes combats drag and STs fight dirty (anything you can do, the NPCs sent against you can do better). But it's there as an option if there's absolutely no other alternative.

            You don't quite have the Soak and Health Levels of the alternative builds. But nothing stops you grabbing a couple more Occult charms for that sweet +Essence to soak, or buying more Ox-bodies/Pain Tolerance. I'd probably nab the Ox-bodies then fill in Melee charms (Dragon-Graced Weapon for the 6 OVR especially), but if I sold having 19 soak to you then go for it.

            You're a full on combat-womble murder-monkey with this build. The other builds gave you some rounding out and useful functions beyond stabbing things. This is all hammer, all the time. Awareness/Survival makes you a fair scout, but you have no Stealth (the T-rex is better at hiding than you). Survival/Occult paired with your good mental stats means you have some knowledge of Creation's flora and fauna... but with no Lore you are not going to be introducing any facts and no Linguistics means you're illiterate. You're a world class weightlifter, I guess. You shouldn't neccessarily play as a murder-hobo, but you're going to be solving problems through the judicious application of violence.

            This is a look at what a DB can really do at 175XP. It is a peak optimal build that I honestly don't think could be improved on.

                                Sample Combat The Invincible vs Volfer

                                Combatants start at Long range.

                                Volfer Join Battle: 12 dice for 6m. Result of 9. (7m Personal/33m Peripheral)
                                Invi Join Battle: 17 dice for 6m. Result 11. (14/18m)

                                Invi turn 1. Flame-Warden Stance goes up on Rexy. (14/15m). 9i.
                                Volfer reflexively moves to Medium and bellows a threatening war cry to instill a Tie of Fear (13 dice for 6m, 3 rerolls, 8 successes whiffs against Invi's Immaculate backed Resolve). (1/33m)

                                End of round
                                Invi 9i (14/20m) Fire Aura 1a
                                Volfer 9i (6/33m)

                                Turn 2.

                                Invi's Windrider Swiftness allows a Reflexive move and Rush from Medium range (14/15m). 13 dice to 5, Volfer getting Rushed and attacked this tick.

                                Volfer moves to Short. Invi's Rush triggers, Volfer kicks off Thunderclap Rush (3/33m). Volfer closes to Close and attacks with an Unclashable attack. He doesn't have Reach so has to attack Rexy. 16 dice, 1 automatic success, rerolling 1s and carrying over upto 4 Threshold successes (0/23m). Invi's Defend Other kicks in and Volfer is all too happy to hit the mistress not the beast. 22 damage is soaked down to 3i. Volfer Withers Invi down to 6i vs his 13i and trips of One Weapon Two Blows (0/11m), 3i vs 17i. Feel the fury of the Dawn.

                                Invi's actual action then ressumes having been so rudely interrupted. Stoking Bonfire Style, Crimson Fang Bite, Colossal Whirling Strike (14/1m) Withering Smash. Volfer does not want to get hit and boosts his Parry to a massive 9 (0/3m) and flips off Durability of Oak Meditation (0/0m). It helps but not enough. 9 successes means Volfer is hit for 29 damage, Soaks 9 thanks to Durability of Oak Meditation. He gets a Withering 10i rolled against him. Twice. Invi goes up to 28i, burying Volfer into a -3i Crash and knocking him Prone. Invi has a higher i than all other combatants so trips off Burning Pinacle Strike (12/0m) with Crimson Fang Bite (9m/0) on a second Withering attack. 6 success vs Volfer's new Parry of 3, leads to another 6i Withered putting Invi at 35i to -9i.
                                Volfer's Durability of Oak can't stop Anima Flux in Crash, which puts 1L on his Health Track. *Rotrough.*

                                Invi 36i (14/0m) 4a Fire Aura
                                Volfer -9i (5/0m) 4a Prone -0 Wound Penality

                                Turn 3

                                There can be only one. Invi unleashes her Aura to throw out Dragon Soul Burst backed with a full Excellency (1/0m). Volfer knows it's do or die and drains his last remaining motes to boost his Parry to 5. Invi rolls an average 8, hitting easily, followed by a monstrous (5+36i)B. Volfer eats a mondo 14B, knocking him out and spilling over into L.

                                Invi gives Rexy a ruffle behind the ears. Her anima will take 15 minutes to recede, during which time the Crashed and Unconcious Volfer will take a further 45L dice from her anima flux which is more than sufficent to kill him as Invi rides away into the sunset. Rexy is hungry and doesn't like his meat overcooked. *Gulp*

                                With 6m in her personal pool and not a single level of damage, Invi has dropped a Dawn Caste Solar in two moves.
