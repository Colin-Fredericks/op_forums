    I know kung fu (OR Let's talk about martial arts!)

    05-06-2018, 04:22 PM
    Put simply, this thread is for a discussion of all things martial arts in Exalted.

    Which are your favorite styles? Your favorite MA charms? Your favorite MA combos? Which styles pair best? What works and what doesn't work? Best practices for building and playing a martial artist?

    Let's leave discussion of interaction with non-MA charms (Solar, DB, etc.) to another thread; this thread is for the love of MA.

    Challenge: your exalt isn't really a martial artist until she knows the form charms of at least two styles and has reflexively switched from one to the other in a battle. Come at me, bro.

      For my part, Snake Style is probably my favorite 3E style thus far, closely followed by Mantis Style; I very much enjoy the design of both.

      When it comes to MA in Exalted, my preference is always to have an unarmed fighter (it's a handicap, I know, but the aesthetics appeal to me), so I tend to focus on the styles that can be used unarmed. My first priority with a martial artist character is, as I suggested above, to get to the form charms of two styles. While going deeper in one style is usually mechanically superior, I contend that you're not really about that MA life until you can mix styles

      My martial artists almost always take a lore speciality in MA, as you need to be a student of the game. In a shoutout to a nice bit of creativity in 2E's Scroll of Exalts, I will sometimes take a medicine specialty in 'wounds I inflict.'

      I may, at some point in the near future, make a thread about my favorite RPG character of my gaming career, a 2E lunar martial artist.

                                  prototype00's martial Arts Principles.

                                  1. Martial Arts is a major merit investment. No one ever dips in martial arts, it always is to accomplish something concrete.

                                  2. Martial Arts trees are short, and with the sunk cost, it makes sense to get more than one (caveat if you are taking a weapon style)

                                  3. Choose synergistic styles based on the following 3 considerations: Share a Form Weapon (you can use artifact versions of said weapon), Share One or more Attributes or Abilities that are used in Charm calculations (more bang for your character creation BP), Share attack System focus (Gambits/Gambits, Grapple/Grapple, Decisive/Decisive e.t.c.)

                                  4. If you want some early Charms, just buy 3 points in an MA style, calculations for attacks always use your highest existing MA value (should be at 5 points).

                                  5. Be like Water.

                                                  Originally posted by BrilliantRain View Post
                                                  Can someone here explain Steel Devil to me? How does Double Attack Technique even work? What are you trying to do with the style and how do the charms synergize? Can you activate the successive effect combo without running out of Essence?
                                                  The most common thing I've heard being said about Steel Devil is that it is good against things that you would already have an easy time beating, because they are easy to hit.

                                                  Double Attack Technique lets you add more damage to an attack that scored at least [(Enemy Defense x 2) -1], so if your enemy's Defense is 3and you score 5 successes you trigger it, or like in the books example; The enemy has Parry 5, so if you score 9 successes you trigger DAT.

                                                  When DAT is triggered you add [Dex + (OCHARGE/2 round down)] to that withering attack's base damage. Note that this does not spend the charges (which can be spent on later charms like Empty Mind Strike).

                                                  Triple Attack Technique basically changes DAT's formula to Required Successes: [(Enemy Defense x 3) -2] and damage bonus to (DEX+OCHARGE).

                                                  The basic chain is DAT(TAT)+Steel Devil Strike+Empty Mind Strike costs 12m+2wp (-3m if in form and 1wp is recoverable if you damage the opponent with SDS).

                                                  A nifty thing is that while you roll only one damage pool, for the purpose of Onslaught penalties, these attacks count as separate attacks, TAT gives a -2 onslaught penalty, SDS lays another one and EMS another, for a total of -4.

                                                  Then you can pay 12m, 1wp to activate Dervish Blade Frenzy and initiate a new cycle, but instead of having TAT you now have Quadruple Attack Technique. which is like TAT but with more damage, and presumably (not clear) more onslaught.

                                                  But if you can pull this off things will probably go splat on the first chain, and not many things can survive a 2nd go round. But yes, it gets very expensive and Willpower intensive.

                                                  Thing is, to pull DAT/TAT/QAT off you have to beat your foe's Defense very handily, and here is where most people dismiss it.

                                                  But I see it as a good support style to stack onslaught, or a good finisher if you have people laying on the onslaught that lets you more easily trigger XAT, it is also good for fighting safely away from crash, since SDS only uses initiative from the XAT that triggered it, and EMS unloads OCHARGE as decisive damage, leaving you at the initiative you were before the current chain, not reset to base.

                                                  It also has a very good decisive counter attack in Dual Slaying Stance, that doesn't reset you to base, but takes the place of your Form (later they combine in Whirling Guillotine Dance, the capstone).

                                                  System wise, it is very good against low defense high health opponents. If you get a TAT in with DEX5 at ESS3 at max OCHARGE (10 if you haven't crashed anyone yet) you will add +15 dmg to that withering attack. That is in addition to your normal withering damage, you can easily get 13-18 (+5 if you crash the enemy) initiative out of this, which you can then unload with SDS to easily get 5-7 damage to the opponent and the EMS for another 11 damage decisive, (3-4 damage). In one round you can crash and lay 8-11 damage into an opponent, while keepin your previous round initiative, not resetting to base.

                                                  And if they don't go down at first, you restart and blender them away.

                                                  Hard to pull off, but very good if you do.

                                                  PS It jives very well with Stealth, if you pull off an ambush, the target is very probably dead.

                                                  PPS An easy dip was Striking Cobra Technique for an easy withering damage enhancer, dual wielding two hooked swords.

                                                  But now that we have Fire Dragon Form, how is that with Steel Devil huh? Letting you benefit from all those onslaught penalties before they hit is awesome for Steel Devil, making it that much easier to trigger your XAT. Though the interaction is not 100% clear.

                                                  Might just be the thing that makes people look at Steel Devil again.

                                                  But in my mind it changes the formula as below;

                                                  DAT Trigger Requirement: [(Enemy Defense x 2) -2]

                                                  TAT Trigger Requirement: [(Enemy Defense x 3) -3]
