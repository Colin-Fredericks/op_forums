        Ride:
        Charging the Bloody Fort
        The Solar may activate this charm after performing a decisive attack while mounted. She may reflexively take a move action to close in on another enemy.


        And a Larceny charm I thought about that I don't want to forget:
        Blinding the Fellowship
        The Solar may use this charm to supplement a social influence to misdirect someone investigating a crime that the Solar has committed. So long as that person has a major or defining positive intimacy towards the Solar, the next roll they make to investigate a crime committed by the Solar will fail. They will still collect evidence according to their roll, but they will not connect the evidence to the Solar.

                    Sail:

                    Silver-Laced Wind Compass
                    When the Solar is in search of a specific item, the winds conspire to guide her towards it. The Solar gains a number of automatic successes equal to her Essence rating to all rolls to sail towards this item, including by tracking other ships. In addition, when the Solar lays hands on the item, she immediately claims ownership of it and any attempts to steal it from her incur an external penalty equal to half the Solar's Essence rating.

                            Presence:

                            Deicide Domain Manifestation
                            The Solar unleashes her titan-slaying wrath, cowing all those who would raise a hand agaisnt her. The Solar makes a threaten action against all opponents within short range of her. All those who are affected lose momentum equal to the Solar's Essence. Afterwards, the Solar may activate this charm reflexively at a reduced cost at the start of her action. All opponents within short range of the Solar who were affected by the charm's initial activation lose 1 momentum. Anyone who successfully hits the Solar or moves away from short range of her becomes immune to this charm's effect until it is reactivated.
