    Who are the Sidereals without the Setting?

    06-03-2014, 09:18 PM
    This concept got brought up in a different thread and I wanted to explore it more fully without derailing the previous discussion.

    The concept is what happens to the sidereal splat if you remove Yu-Shan, contact with the Pattern Spiders and all of the Heaven based business and guidelines from the sidereal concept. Essentially not much is left for a Sidereal to be a Sidereal (as opposed to any other kind of exalt).

    If you look at Solars with "setting support" then you have the First Age, without that support structure and their tendency to rule, you have the classic second age solar. Both are recognizable as Solar Exalts, with solar powers and doing solar things.

    Lunars have pretty much the same result. They are transhumanist shape shifters of animalistic qualities regardless if they are city socialites, building a private society away from civilization, being shape shifting elite scouts for a first age solar army or just being the Exalted analog to the shifters of Werewolf the Apocalypse game. Alchemicals fall under similar transhumanist genre concepts.

    Dragon-Blooded follow the same path in that their powers are inherited, which makes any story permutation based around family automatically in their domain. With setting support you have the Realm or Lookshy or the First Age Gentes. Without setting support you have the classic lost egg games where they play the wandering heroes trying to carve out a niche for themselves which is found in almost every fantasy rpg.

    While occasionally poorly implemented, even Abyssals function regardless of setup. They can have setting support by being loyalists who follow their patron deathlords orders or they can for go it and try to be tragic heroes who constantly struggles to overcome their condition and attempt redemption. Regardless which path they choose death in all its forms and permutations are never far behind them.

    Now lets look at the Sidereals...... without their plot device setup as running a chunk of heaven, without benefit of there being a comparatively large number of elder essence Sidereals running around, without access to the pattern spiders to wield astrology, without benefit of advanced martial arts.... what exactly are the sidereals?

    If say you swap the sidereal character generation with say a Solar or Lunar and restrict the backgrounds accordingly, there isn't very much "sidereal-ness" left in a sidereal. In fact if you consider just how much of their charm set would not be worth taking since they wouldn't be working for heaven or running related missions, one could be forgiven for considering their charm set under developed.

    If you think about it, Sidereals don't serve a clear function in an Army of any sort (unlike Solars, Lunars or Dragon-Blooded), they don't have the forced character oversight that say the Infernals do despite being clearly chosen by very powerful beings that are active in the running of Creation, they are not allowed to be active heroes who are known for their exploits even if they are in service to others like what the Alchemicals do.

              Sidereals without the whole heavenly aspect of Yu-Shan and Loom of Fate? This is where they get a little bit weak. The reason is that they were designed with that in mind. Without it, they are still mysterious ninja and assassins who show up at the the exact worst time and disappear at will. They still make great advisors, and they make a great conspiracy of elders who never take direct action. Part of the problem is I never really saw them as the front line hero, but the movers and shakers in the background.

              I think what you are doing is a little unfair, as their first age and second age rolls are very different, and you are removing the second age roll from them in many ways. Without access to heaven, Martial Arts, Astrology, Pattern Spiders, and heaven, well yeah, there's not much left. Even many (at least some) of their charms reference heaven or pattern spiders, like Efficient Secretary Technique. So yes, without most of their powers, they aren't very interesting.

              You can do them as Ronin, agents of heaven who never go there, secret identity superhero ninjas (that guy who runs that shop that isn't very interesting who is never around when monsters attack, MI-6 type adventures, old school Mission Impossible stuff, but they do lose a lot of character.

                  This is where a long view on the line is beneficial: all the stuff you're saying Sidereals are incoherent and redundant without is stuff that was introduced more than two years into the game line. Without that, you're left with the things that led people to think Sidereals were cool in the first place: they're mysterious, esoteric exalted with uncanny foresight and an intimate connection to spirits and destiny (not the Loom of Fate, which didn't exist, but "destiny" in a general sense), who practice powerful martial arts: heroes destined from birth to be peerless advisors and teachers, but who seized autonomy for themselves long ago in the doubt that others could be trusted to lead.

                  Before the Sidereals book came out, the main concern was "how the hell are they going to make these guys a playable faction?", not "I don't see why these guys are in the game." On the contrary, it's always been the Lunars who didn't really have a clear role in the history of the setting -- they were largely redundant with the Solar Night Caste in the Primordial War and First Age. And while the Sidereals book threw in a ton of new shit out of left field, it left their core themes undisturbed, which is to say that their niche -- which was very firmly established straight out of the core -- was not changed, just greatly embellished upon.

                  That said, there is one thing from the hardback that's a huge part of the modern "Sidereal thing", but which you're passing over, and that's their charmset. The Sidereal charmset encodes a very large amount of the Sidereal thematics, in ways both overt and subtle. Some of their charms are wired into the Heaven/Loom motif, but that's just a loose rationalization, so they'd probably still have those charms if you removed Sidereal Astrology and all the hardback fluff. It's not an exaggeration to say that, until Infernals was published near the very end of the line, there had never been anything in the history of Exalted whose essence was as deeply encoded into its charmset as the Sidereals. If you don't make an attempt to understand the information about them contained in the charms, you aren't really going to get what their deal is.
