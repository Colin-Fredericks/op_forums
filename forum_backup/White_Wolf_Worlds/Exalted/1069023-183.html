    Originally posted by Colapso View Post
    And so, I have to ask, in what I imagine will be the only quoted part: is this concerted effort to re-establish the primacy, importance and mystique of the Core Five still a major part of your agenda? Or is it something you do not feel is as important as your predecessors did?

    And can you tell me how you view the five and their place in the setting and game?

    The way I put it, the answer seems like a no-brainer, but Infernals fans will likely be quite disappointed at no longer being the greatest masters of Sorcery, greater existences than the Yozi, and greater in freedom, agency and metaphysical grandeur than anyone else in the setting; Alchemical fans will also likely be quite disappointed that they can no longer faceroll Creation and no longer hold the true allegiance of most of Creation's apparatus; and I have met too many people who thought the Core Five should not be viewed as any way special, and that their primacy over the setting should not be treated as anything other than a historical accident. Certainly several very influential writers who worked on Exalted to this point believed that.
    I don't think it's possible to do Exalted right without emphasizing the importance of the "core five," if you want to call them that, and their place in Creation. The Solars are the mightiest of the Exalted, whose rebirth into the world shakes it to its very pillars. The Dragon-Blooded rule the greatest empire in Creation, Princes of the Earth and premier antagonists of the returned Solars. The Lunars are the fangs against the windpipe of the Realm, the monstrous Anathema who haunt the nightmares of Dragon-Blooded and Sidereals alike. The Sidereals are the agents of Heaven itself and the secret masters who have orchestrated the rise and fall of empires from behind the scenes of history. The Abyssals are demigods of death arisen from the dark places of the world, as dangerous and as revolutionary as the Solars they hold a mirror to. I don't see how you could take away from them, diminish their importance or dispel their mystique, without completely distorting the heart and soul of Exalted.

    However, I don't buy in to the premise that emphasizing the original Exalted as pillars of the setting has to come at the cost of the newer Exalted. I want the Infernals and Alchemicals (and for that matter, the Exigents and Liminals and Getimians) to have the absolute best play experience we can give them, to be fun and cool and awesome. Whatever changes are made from their past presentations are going to be in order to make them as great as they can be, not to subtract from them in order to benefit other types of Exalted.

    Developer for Exalted
