                  Also its cool someone else out there realized how good Inevitable Victory Meditation is - big thumbs up from me.

                  Heaven Fury Smite is a great charm no doubt, but I've found in actual play I frequently want to follow up a big withering hit with a Simple-type charm, which HFS does not let you do. A small mark against it.

                  The only charm whose position I strongly disagree with is Burning Fist Burial - that thing is way more niche than it might seem, and usually is a complete waste of motes. Brawl is so good at generating initiative (and damage in general) that adding raw decisive dice, even for 1m for 1d, is a pretty questionable exchange rate. Paying a 4m surcharge on top of that for the benefit of post-roll activation and a soft-cap on the number of damage dice you can add is not worth it. This may seem mindblowing considering how good post-roll activation generally is, but it's true.

                  It's really bad if you only want to add a couple damage dice (6m for +2 dice?? In brawl??) and doesn't scale that well in efficiency for normal case uses. 10m for +6 dice is not good. Burning Fist Burial sucks and should be moved from blue to orange.

                                                      Originally posted by realitycheque View Post
                                                      Could someone please list the purple charms for each type, so I can make a colourblind version?
                                                      Sure thing:
                                                      PUNCH: Fists of Iron Technique, Ferocious Jab, Thunderclap Rush Attack, Falling Hammer Strike, One With Violence, Knockout Blow, Adamantine Fists of Battle, Fivefold Fury Onslaught, Striving Aftershock Method, Inevitable Victory Meditation, Heaven Fury Smite, Ascendant Battle Visage

                                                      GRAB: Unbreakable Grasp, Devil Strangling Attitude, Crashing Wave Throw, Thunderclap Rush Attack, Oak Curling Clinch, One With Violence, Dragon Coil Technique, Shockwave Technique, Striving Aftershock Method, Superior Violent Knowledge, Inevitable Victory Meditation, Raging Wrath Repeated

                                                      Also note a few mentions that charms should go purple in special cases:
                                                      Iron Battle Focus: If you are fighting other Solar (or Abyssal) Brawlers for any reason whatsoever this charm is super good and you’ll thank the heavens for having it.
                                                      Wind and Stones Defense : It goes from decent to crazy if used in combination with Reed in the Wind [Dodge] at higher essence.
                                                      Dancing With Strife Technique: The reset condition is pretty rare, however, so I recommend this charm more strongly to Dawn castes.
                                                      Adamantine Fists of Battle: The repurchase is the second episode, by the way.
                                                      Titan Straightening Method: This charm is pretty good on its own, but it becomes truly amazing alongside Raging Wrath Repeated (see below).
                                                      Apocalypse Flare Attack: It is worth noting that the text defines the range of this charm as “a foe the Solar can perceive” which can become very extreme with the help of charms such as Unsurpassed Sight Discipline [Awareness].
                                                      Heart Eating Fist: But I digress, really, being able to activate Ascendant Battle Visage without commiting motes is a scary thought and something worthwhile to pursue for the supernal brawler that blundered his way into this branch of the charm tree before reaching Essence 3.
