                                    04-27-2018, 06:33 PM
                                    Originally posted by Zardoz View Post
                                    The Aughdeighe's Hold Fast does (1+rounds stuck) damage if you succesfully use a pry-yourself-off gambit. Is this levels or dice?
                                    Dice; Hardness is compared to damage dice pools.

                                    Originally posted by Zardoz View Post
                                    Hold Fast has a duration of Instant, and the only way given to end the effect is a difficulty 3 gambit that does (1+rounds stuck) damage to you. I assume either party dying ends the effect as well? Is it sufficient to knock-out the Aughdeighe to end the effect? Can the Aughdeighe conciously end the effect?
                                    No, it's essentially a walking environmental hazard. So once you're stuck, you're stuck until you pry yourself free, unless your ST rules that another relevant action does the trick. Like if you decide to chop off the hand that's stuck rather than face the gambit, I'd rule that would automatically end the effect (and count as a crippling injury); or your buddy could perform a feat of strength to pull you free, which I'd rule would end the effect (but still inflict the gambit's damage anyway). If you die, it's really up to your ST what happens. Knocking out or killing the horse doesn't do anything, it's already frozen your hand (or whatever) to it with magical ice. Even if it's dead, it's still basically a pole you licked.

                                    Meghan Fitzgerald | Onyx Path freelance writer & developer
