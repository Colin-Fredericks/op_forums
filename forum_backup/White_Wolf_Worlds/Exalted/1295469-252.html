    Originally posted by Iron Phoenix View Post
    1a) When I savage someone with Iron Whirlwind Attack (which is valid as described by http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/m...55#post1076188 and http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/m...53#post1410153), is their defense set to 0 against all attacks, or only the first one?
    All of them.

    1b) Invincible Fury of the Dawn allows me to move a single range band to make each attack (which does not consume my movement action), but the grapple gambit says "She can take no other movement or combat actions so long as the grapple persists." Can I still move while using Invincible Fury of the Dawn as a savaging attack, and if so, does my victim come with me?
    No. In order for something to let you move in a grapple, it's gonna have to specify that.

    2) Lightning Strikes Twice uses the Exalt's movement action, but states that "it’s possible to move several range bands while chaining multiple uses of this Charm." Does this mean that, as long as I can pay the cost of Lightning Strikes Twice and have met the reset condition, I can chain Lightning Strikes Twice indefinitely?
    Yes.

    3) When I use a gambit such as grapple, disarm, or distract, and Clash with my opponent (Clashing gambits appears to be expressly permitted based on Grasping Mantis Defense), what happens if I win the gambit? The Clash rules state "A successful decisive Clash attack adds one additional automatic point of Health Track damage after damage is rolled.", but some gambits (such as disarm or distract) don't roll damage. Does my opponent take an automatic health level of damage anyway?
    Gambits don't receive any specific bonus from winning a clash. If the Storyteller wants to add one, that makes sense.

    4) If I Clash a savaging attack with Fervent Blow or a similar Charm while grappled, how does that interact with savage's text that "this attack hits the opponent automatically, even if the character generates 0 successes on the attack roll." Does the contested roll of the Clash supersede the savage rules, or do the savage rules supersede the Clash rules, or can a savage not be Clashed?
    I wouldn't let someone clash a savaging attack, based on that.

    [quote]5a) Since savaging attacks "don't care what Ability the initial roll was made with" (http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/m...49#post1224449) and "you can generally use Simple Charms when you make savaging attacks", can I use Injury-Forcing Technique from Lore as a savage? Do I simply skip to the Initiative roll for the gambit (since the attack roll is automatically successful in a savage)? Note that gambits being allowed as savages is based on this response: http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/m...01#post1341701
    Sure.

    [quote]5b) Could Charms such as Craftsman Needs No Tools or Wound-Mending Care Technique (with Instant Treatment Methodology) be used as savaging attacks?
    Simple Charms that don't have you make an attack can't be used as a savaging attack.

    5c) Is grapple recursion allowed? Since savages can apply gambits, and grapple itself is a gambit, can I use a grapple gambit on my savaging attack against my grappled opponent to add more rounds of control?
    No.

        09-05-2020, 11:47 AM
        1) Normally, the initiative cost for a disarm gambit is paid after the Initiative roll, but since IWG replaces the cost of the gambit with the cost of the charm does that mean the 4i is paid before rolling Initiative? For example, if I use IWG while at 10i, do I roll only 6 dice on the Initiative roll?
        Yes.

        2) If the target is wielding paired weapons, does IWG steal both of them or just one?
        Only one.

        3) Can IWG be used to steal Artifact weapons that the target isn't currently wielding? For example, if my opponent is using both hands to wield a grand daiklave, but has a short daiklave sheathed at his waist, can I steal the short daiklave?
        I'd allow it.

        4) As far as I can tell, IWG is the only Solar charm capable of stealing attuned Artifacts. Can it be used to steal Artifacts outside of combat? Or would that require a custom charm building off of IWG?
        You don't need a Charm to steal an attuned artifact - you can still just grab it and run, and wait for the attunement to wear off after a day.

        5) Since IWG turns a disarm gambit into a reflexive action instead of a combat action, does that mean it can be used multiple times in the same round?
        As written, there's no limitation on how often you can use it, but I think the intention was just once, and Storytellers are well within their rights to shut down spamming a bunch of disarm rolls if it's detrimental to the playgroup's experience.

        6) Does the disarm gambit still inflict an onslaught penalty?
        Gambits inflict onslaught penalties as usual, as do reflexive attacks.

            I'm having a little bit of confusion imagining how characters perceive their mote pools. I know that Charms and mote pools are abstract representations of a character's prowess, I'm just trying to understand how that abstraction is viewed in-character.
