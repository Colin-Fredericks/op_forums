As for art, there's a two-page piece on pp. 88-89 of Roll of Glorious Divinity 2, the leftmost figure in which is probably supposed to be a person wearing a perronele. The piece is by "Kiyo", and displays a lot of the 2e art tendency to have gratuitous female sexualization, though, with the person wearing not much else besides the perronele. Whether or not the picture is "good" or not is thus up to you. I don't know of any others.

                                                            This exchange happened in the Ask the Devs thread a while back:

                                                            Originally posted by Jefepato View Post
                                                            What is the Immaculate view of the Unconquered Sun, generally speaking?

                                                            On one hand, he doesn't do the things that gods normally do to offend the Immaculate Order. He stays in Heaven and makes the sun cross the sky every day, and isn't out there trying to force or cajole mortals into worshiping him directly.

                                                            On the other hand, advanced Immaculates know that those damn Solar Anathema are his Chosen, right? Surely they can't be happy about that.

                                                            So what's the deal there? Do they see the Unconquered Sun as a largely noble god who mostly does his job well -- but he once made the terrible mistake of trying to turn men into heroes, only to discover that too much celestial power drove mortals mad?
                                                            Originally posted by Robert Vance View Post
                                                            The Unconquered Sun is seen as a prominent and significant god—one who stands perhaps just below the Elemental Dragons themselves among gods—though not one who merits or asks for worship. His connection to the Solar Exalted is known, but the circumstances that led to the creation of the Solar Exalted are not precisely defined in Immaculate orthodoxy, leaving room for what I'd imagine are some interesting theology debates.
                                                            So, I'm curious what the folks here think. (I seem to recall that in older editions, "the Anathema stole power from the sun and moon" was the general belief, but it sounds like it's not that simple anymore.) If a young Immaculate asks his sifu what the deal is with the sun god, which of these is more likely?

                                                            1) "The Unconquered Sun is one of the Dragons' foremost lieutenants -- as you'd imagine, since the sun is second only to the elemental poles themselves as a pillar of Creation. The foul Anathema waylaid him for his power long ago, but he managed to escape with enough of his power to continue illuminating the world. He has long stood as an exemplar of virtue, and has never shirked his duty to seek worship from the mortals (although some fools have been known to pray to him regardless)."

                                                            2) "The sun god is a fool whose power was stolen long ago. If he'd been more vigilant, we wouldn't have to deal with Solar Anathema! Still, he performs his actual duty well enough, so I suppose the Dragons must have forgiven his lapse eventually."

                                                            3) "The Unconquered Sun is a damnable traitor. The Solar Anathema are his so-called 'Chosen!' Someday he will pay the price for his perfidy. Still, the Dragons must have their reasons for tolerating him now."

                                                            4) Something else (obviously there could be a lot more nuance to any of these angles)

                                                            (Eventually Immaculates do learn that the Solar and Lunar Anathema are Exalted, but I'm not sure how much context they get for that information...)
