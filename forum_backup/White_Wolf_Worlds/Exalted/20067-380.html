                                    Originally posted by Citadel97501 View Post
                                    Hello all,
                                    Well as I still had a question I might as well ask it.

                                    -If you use a counter-attack charm such as Solar Counter Attack to initiate a Grapple gambit with a Dire Lash, do you get a free action such as a Savaging attack or Throw, with that grapple as well like you would normally?

                                    Example:
                                    Bob has Solar Counter-Attack, and uses it in response to an attack choosing to make a Grapple attack with his Dire Lash, he succeeds with his grapple gaining control, does he immediately get to Throw the enemy into a bonfire across the room?

                                    -What about Peony Blossom Attack to trigger an additional Savaging attack or using One Weapon Two Blows to immediately trigger a Throw after the initial savaging attack?
                                    First question yes. You apply one of the four grappling moves every turn, including the one in which you initiate the grapple.

                                    Second question no, because you choose to apply one of the four possible grappling effects each turn. You do not get a grappling attack while in a grapple, nor do you ever make a withering or decisive attack while grappling. You apply withering or decisive -damage- and roll -as if- it were, but it is not. The grappling section on p201 goes on an on about how it's not a proper attack, and anything that looks like an attack is not one.

                                    Apologies Citadel, but I'm going to have to ask you to RTFM here
