    Abyssals in Yu-Shan

    07-04-2015, 01:25 AM
    In a solo game I'm running, the PC went to the Deep Wyld looking for her Lunar Mate only to find that a Raksha turned them into a mask. Her Sidereal friend took the Lunar to Lytek so he can try to turn the Lunar back into a person and is planning on using an Abyssal she's keeping as a prisoner as a bribe since Lytek wants to research their Exaltations. Other than Bronze Faction being mad, would there be a significant reaction to a Sidereal taking an Abyssal to Yu-Shan?

                  I am kind of curious about this too. One of the core concepts of Lunars (on page 127) is that nothing can constrain or prevent them from changing shape. Even if the Lunar is subject to magic that would do so, he can ignore it can still change his shape. So even if the Lunar were shaped into a mask by a Fair Folk, he could just turn back into his normal form whenever he wanted. So what sort affect is on this Lunar, or is the OP's game well into houserule territory?

                  As to an Abyssal walking around Yu-Shan, expect to most likely be detained by Celestial Lions if you decide to start going iconic with your anima, and for the Sidereal to be questioned extensively by his superiors as to why he didn't bother to inform anyone he was bringing an Abyssal to heaven. But, provided the AByssal keeps a low profile, people will just assume he's a regular person (unless he looks like a skeleton or zombie, and then they might just assume he's another spirit with an exotic appearance).
