    [PEACH] The Medal of the Broken King, an artifact armor

    05-08-2018, 08:31 AM
    The Medal of the Broken King (Black Jade Medium Armor, Artifact •••)

    The Broken King is a figure of legend along the Dreaming Sea, an ancient trickster who travelled and drew chaos around him by turning local populations against the might of the Shogunate during its early years. Many believe that he was a Solar or a Lunar, but some believe him to be the ghost of a powerful lord or even a mortal sorcerer who used the powerful magics left to him by his solar master to wreak havoc and carve out his own kingdom out of the chaos. While little is known about his life and even less about his death, he left behind a veritable panoply of precious objects, mostly made of jade, glass and black iron, all broken in some way.
    The Medal of the Broken King is used as a protective talisman over the Pearly Coast or in Dir-Jal, with replicas sold on the street or given out to friends and family.

    Attunement: 5m; Type: Medium (SOAK +8, HARDNESS 7, MOBILITY -1)
    Tags: Buoyant
    Hearthstone Slot(s): 1
    Era: Shadows of the Usurpation

    EVOCATIONS OF THE MEDAL OF THE BROKEN KING
    The Medal of the Broken King feeds on people’s devotion to its holder. As long as the medal was gifted to her by someone with a positive intimacy towards her, or by someone who was part of her cult, she gains (lowest of Intimacy’s Strength or Cult Rating) natural Soak as long as she wears the medal. People that either have a positive intimacy towards the bearer of the Medal of the Broken King, or are part of a cult venerating her, are referred to in the subsequent evocations as her wards. Foes of the bearer cannot be wards, even if they fall under these conditions.


    Unburdened by Blood
    Cost:     5m; Mins: Essence 1
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: Dissonant, Decisive-only
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisites: None

    No hidden blade might pierce the skin of one blessed by the Broken King. Against the first decisive attack to target her during a fight, the Exalt gains Hardness 10, even if this attack is made by surprise.
    Special activation rules: This Evocation is awakened at no cost when the wielder first attunes to the Medal of the Broken King.
    Dissonant: The Exalt cannot use Unburdened by Blood against an ambush.

    Flowing Steel Blossoming
    Cost:     3m, 1wp; Mins: Essence 1
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: Resonant, Dissonant
    Duration: One scene
    Prerequisites: Unburdened by Blood

    Black Jade flows like water over the Exalt’s skin, iron and glass foaming to form an articulated armor centered on the medal. This armor has the stats of Medium Artifact Armor (SOAK +8, HARDNESS 7, MOBILITY -1), and lasts until the end of the scene or until the Exalt concentrates to draw it back inside the medal.
    Resonant: When she uses Unburdened by Blood, an Exalt resonant with Jade can activate Flowing Steel Blossoming reflexively.
    Dissonant: Dissonant users are only able to coax a Light Artifact Armor out of the medallion.

    Barrier-Unfurling Icon Technique
    Cost:     5m; Mins: Essence 2
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: Resonant
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisites: Flowing Steel Blossoming

    When protecting others who look up to her, the medallion’s holder flows through a fight like a river, intercepting blows meant for her charge. Using this charm allows the Exalt to apply the bonus of a Full Defense action (+2 Defense) to a Defend Other action when protecting one of her wards, without losing initiative. Each subsequent activations of Barrier-Unfurling Icon Technique during her turn cost a mote less, down to a minimum of a single mote
    Resonant: Users resonant with the Medal of the Broken King can also apply Barrier-Unfurling Icon Technique up to short range.

    Armor of the Faithful
    Cost: 7m, 1wp; Mins: Essence 2
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: Dissonant, Resonant
    Duration: One Scene
    Prerequisites: Barrier-Unfurling Icon Technique

    The followers of the Broken King are protected by his icon, even if he is not at their side. By using this charm, the bearer of the Medal add (her Essence) to the natural Soak of all her wards present during the scene.
    Resonant: When her wards carry a replica of the Medal of the Broken King, or openly display a sigil of loyalty to the bearer (painted on their shield, carved on their forehead, etc…), they gain two additional points of natural Soak.
    Dissonant: Dissonant users can only add (lowest of her Essence or Cult rating) to the natural Soak of her wards

    Shielding the Single Soul
    Cost:     5m; Mins: Essence 2
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: Resonant
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisites: Barrier-Unfurling Icon Technique

    As she protects those who follow her, the resolution of the Exalt makes even her fiercest foes hesitate to strike. When protecting one of her ward from a decisive blow, the bearer can use Shield the Single Soul to impress those who see her determination. She rolls (Charisma + Presence), adding (her ward’s Essence, maximum 5) in non-charm dice. All characters witnessing this action whose Resolve is beaten gain a positive Minor intimacy towards her (usually of respect, but other positive intimacies are possible depending on the circumstances). This influence cannot be resisted except by spending three willpower. A character doing so is immunized against this charm for the rest of the scene.
    Resonant: If they do not already have a negative intimacy towards the bearer, targets whose Resolve is beaten gain a Major intimacy towards her.
    Dissonant: Resisting the influence of this charm only costs 1 willpower.

    Unfallen Protector
    Cost:     7m; Mins: Essence 2
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: Decisive-only, Resonant
    Duration: One turn
    Prerequisites: Flowing Steel Blossoming

    The bearer of the Medal of the Broken King stands undaunted, impossible to stop or to lay down, the protective essence of the armor snaking through her veins to strengthen bones and reflexes. When she is fighting to protect something or someone that she has a positive tie towards (excluding herself), she can use Unfallen Protector to escape from any effect that would hamper her movement. Until her next turn, she ignores difficult terrain, rises from prone refllexively, cancels any crippling effects that would keep her from moving, and if held in a grapple, can force a new control roll, adding (Essence) automatic successes.
    Resonant: When calling on a specific tie to use Unfallen Protector, the Exalt treats that intimacy as one level higher for the rest of the scene. Defining ties instead become inviolable.

    The Hand That Caught Death
    Cost:     6m, 1hl ; Mins: Essence 3
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: Dissonant, Counterattack, Uniform
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisites: Unfallen Protector

    As she is struck by a killing blow, the bearer of the Medal catches it, shattering her armor and sending shards of black iron and green glass back at her attacker. This evocation can only be used when wearing the armor granted by Flowing Steel Blossoming: the armor immediately gains +3 Soak and +3 Hardness against this attack, and explodes in a rain of shards. The bearer makes a (Dexterity + Resistance) decisive counterattack that occurs after the opponent’s attack results, but before damage has been rolled, and doubles 10s on her damage roll. This evocation can only be used once per scene, rendering the Exalt unable to use Flowing Steel Blossoming during that scene, unless reset by saving the life of one of her ward against a non-trivial opponent.
    Dissonant: This evocation can only be used once per story.

    Righteous Oblation of Self
    Cost:     10m, 2i; Mins: Essence 3
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: Resonant, Decisive-only, Psyche, Perilous
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisites: The Hand That Caught Death, Shielding the Single Soul
    The life of one who protects other is too precious to loose, leading others to sacrifice themselves for her should the need be great enough. Should the bearer of the Medal of the Broken King be facing certain death and have at least one of her wards at close range, she can use Righteous Oblation of Self after damage is rolled. One of the wards at close range will throw himself on the attack meant for her protector, giving his life if needs be so that she does not die. The ward will take the decisive damage and other associated effects carried by the attack, be they curses, poisons, or anything else meant for the Exalt.
    Resonant: When the conditions for Righteous Oblation of Self are present, and at least one of the wards at short range of the bearer has a Defining Intimacy towards her or her cult, the bearer has to activate Righteous Oblation of Self, or enter a Decision Point to avoid sacrificing her would-be savior.
