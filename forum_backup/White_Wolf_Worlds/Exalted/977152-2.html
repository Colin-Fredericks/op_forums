                            Celestial Exaltations are the person receving an Exaltation, which was held by some previous hero, which empowers them and gives themt heir Second Breath. Nothing has ever been said that the spiritual mumbo jumbo being grafted to you is how Exaltation occurs. This is also the language used for things like the Deathlords in relation to the Neverborn and Akuma.

                            However, since early 1e, the fact that DBs don't have some external mechanism save lineage, folks have over the years had tried to find one. Thsi begs the question that Exaltation = something stuck to your soul.

                            Alchemicals break this as well, note, since they have perfectly normal souls just in extraordinary bodies. And 3e is introducing other kinds of Exalted with non-Celestial origins like Liminals to further hammer home the idea that Exaltation is not grafting random spiritual mumbo jumbo to people.
