    What Fire Has Wrought, The Realm, and Heirs of the Shogunate all have sections on Prasad, which is in the South-East. I'd say the Realm's section is most informative about the area, as it mentions various places Prasad has conquered or is at war with. The Realm also has a bit on Greyfalls, which I guess you could say is in the East.
    Lunars has good bits on Mahalanka, the city of ape-men ruled by Raksi, and the area influenced by Ma-Ha-Suchi (between the South-East and Scavenger Lands). Both of them I thought were really good.

    But for direct "East and South-East" sections, rather than looking at particular places in those areas, I think you'll have to wait for Across the Eight Directions.

                                This has always been intentional.

                                Raksha are dead if killed by someone from Creation. No Charm required.

                                First Circles are dead if killed by anyone. Same for Elementals. Usually. No Charm required.

                                Second Circles/Third Circles/Primordials/Dragon Kings/Gods are immortal eternal beings which are impossible to kill. Dragon-Blooded can permenantly weaken them and/or seal them away for five centuries with their native Charms. That the Celestial Exalted can do the impossible and kill these beings is a sign of how monumentally scary they are. Any other being who wants to kill the unkillable is going to have to go on some sort of epic quest (or at least out of their way) to harness some kind of immortal slaying magic.
