            Originally posted by TheCountAlucard View Post
            Just like with your proposed War Supernal Japanese soldier in World War 2, Accelerator, there's no single straightforward ultimate answer. What do you want to have happen?
            Let's see now.

            I want things to start out slow first. First she starts at an animal shelter (after exaltation). And then interns in a hospital (much to the disapproval of her parents). And then shit happens. That long-term patient who everyone said could never get better? Just did. That comatose man who lost half his brain? Just regained consciousness. Burns, broken bones, and lacerations heal suspiciously fast. Patients that were on bad level, just... got better. They get suspicious. Guards are posted once or twice. And with them being alert, they nearly catch her. Almost. Solar power adds a burst of speed to lose them.

            People notice. Of course, its very coincidental and very subtle, so other than the coincidence of some strange perpetrator wandering around, it all seems like just a statistical fluke (I have a custom charm that acts like Touch of Grace/ Eternity). Nothing Obvious is happening, but anyone with a medical profession knows that something funky is up.

            Reporters come in, and after a few weeks of seeing people lay about in hospitals and talking to doctors, they just... leave. It just doesn't work for the ratings. Things go on as per normal, until the staff just accept that someone out there is somehow helping them.

            Until there's a massive car pile up, and the girl's got a choice. Walk away, and keep the secret, or walk in, and help out, and out herself? There's only one answer, and she didn't intern in a hospital to walk away from people who needed medical help. So the combo is unleashed, and she flares up to bonfire level. And when I say that, I meant 'pillar of light'. In the age of smartphones, photos and videos are already taken, with the cops and ambulances arriving to a multi-car pileup with no serious injuries.

            Of course, most people call it a hoax. Most people in the city saw the gigantic beam of light pumping through the atmosphere and turning night into day. Things come in, investigations are done, and... the girl just cooperates. So yes, the world knows about the superhealer. Offers come in, and people start going to her. First comes the terminal cases. Those desperate. The ones who would usually go to a faith healer and get their money fleeced. Only this time, it doesn't happen. Again and again, the healings continue. Even in controlled settings like labs and other somesuch.

            The wealth gained is giddying, and she starts donating it. Fast. More and more people come. Multibillionaires with blackened lungs and liver cirrhosis. The waiting list is several years long, and you can wait for a new organ and live a life on immunosuppressants, or you can go to the girl.

            This means power. People. Everywhere. Including unsavory ones.

            So she begins to do things like 'ask for favors'. And 'request' things. Fallout of this is yet undecided.

            So there's also the question of how her parents handle the sudden change. From lower class to quite possibly billionaires in less than a few days. Its like lottery winning, only a lot bigger. I'm not sure how to write them yet. Misguided and good people who simply believe in things like alternative medicine or assorted woo? People who see their child as a projection and a chance to do things they never had the chance to. Or are they going to be like the parents of those child stars who took advantage of their kid?

            Still not sure how to handle the school part.

            Then, of course, there's the part about the nutcases. I'm not sure how the nutcases differ from America and Britain, but I think I've seen enough religious fundamentalists to know them well enough. They'll either praise her as miraculous, or curse her as a devil and the anti-christ. Probably the anti-christ one. It can range from pastors preaching against her, protestors, or full on crazies trying to gun her down.

            The climax will probably be someone kidnapping her, and holding her hostage, threatening to blow her head off and themselves if their demands aren't met. The police/ SWAT will have to stop and listen, cause, well, its the miracle healer there, and losing her will mean the end of their careers.

            The girl just looks at her captors, and requests things for arts and crafts, because, well, she's bored. And they look at the small, scrawny little girl who's only 15 years old, and agrees to it.

            Bad mistake. The girl's got dots in stealth and craft, and she's a medicine supernal. After several weeks of training time, she puts on her disguise, activates a charm, and begins walking around, releasing sleeping gasses and firing the dartgun she fashioned from office supplies. In the end, all the kidnappers lie unconscious, and she walks out, unharmed.

            The next part of this arc, is people wondering 'how did a 15 year old girl get out of that situation', and the even more pertinent questions of 'What else can she do' or 'Is there anything she can't do'?
