                                    Originally posted by Voyd211 View Post
                                    How does one play an Abyssal without being either "HURR DURR KILL ALL DA TINGS" or a mopey Edward Cullen type of character?

                                    I think several options exist, depending on exactly how stringent your requirements are:
                                    • Confine your killing to people who deserve it. There's several sub-types within this, ranging from "Grim avenger of the night" (The Shadow or The Spectre are both good inspirations here) to a more personal "I focus on killing people who wronged me personally" (just watch The Crow, basically).
                                    • Be a promoter of the interests of the dead, rather than against the interests of the living. This can involve boosting ancestor cults, making sure people are treating the dead respectfully, finding ways for ghosts to live well, and other things that don't require you to spend all your time in the Underworld.
                                    • The "struggling against their own curse" type of Abyssal is actually pretty viable. We don't know how Resonance will work exactly in 3e, but even in 2e, there were ways to guide how Resonance got vented. You'll probably end up with a bunch of effects like death auras and obvious supernatural manifestations of entropy about you, but those are good reasons to have a scene or two where you bemoan the terrible curse you're under, before going out and doing some more good.
