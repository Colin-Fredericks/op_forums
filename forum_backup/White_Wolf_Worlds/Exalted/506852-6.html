                                                            I believe, Erinyes, that the best way to view the Sidereals--and mind you, this is my perception which may not be shared by anyone--is people who are way too smart and clever for their own good, by and large.

                                                            They are the classic image of the smartest guy in the room, who thinks that he should run everything just because he's smart, and that attitude breeds a certain arrogance that starts to feed itself. You start assuming "I'm smarter than you, so my ideas have more merit than yours, and that means your ideas have less merit than mine, so I must be right, and since I'm right, I'm smart, and I'm smart so I'm right..." and it just goes in a circle that doesn't allow for dissenting opinions.

                                                            It's worth noting that the Sidereals, when choosing how to save the world, consulted no one outside themselves, because they considered no one else capable of understanding the burden they bore, or capable of making the choices they made, or of comprehending the enormity of the choice itself.

                                                            They truly felt that they AND ONLY THEY understood enough to make the "right" choice, and that even the wisest beings in creation outside the Sidereal Host could not possibly be trusted to act in the best interests of Creation.

                                                            It was not until the decision was made and events were set in motion that they even involved the dragon-blooded, and they used them as catspaws and weapons, not as partners in a struggle for the fate of the world.

                                                            If the Solars were tyrants, the Sidereals were the wise men whose very wisdom led them to foolish action. That they needed to act, one way or another, is clear.

                                                            That they assumed the rest of the world too incompetent or too tainted to trust, also clear.

                                                            That this directly led to the lessening of the world, and that they consulted no one but themselves, that they never truly considered viewpoints other than their own, and that they entirely neglected to consider threats from outside fate--the very threats the Solars themselves were so exceptionally good at holding back--is simply a series of examples of the Sidereals' flaws coming to light.

                                                            Sidereals are absolutely wise, powerful, mystical masters in their way.

                                                            They're just categorically incapable of trusting anyone to do anything unless they manipulate the crap out of the situation until there's no other choice first.

                                                            ...And to be fair, they're not entirely unjustified in that.

                                                            Still makes them kinda douchey though.
