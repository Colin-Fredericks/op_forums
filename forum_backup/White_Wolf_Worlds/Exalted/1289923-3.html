    Originally posted by autXautY View Post

    For some reason I misread the rules on shapeshifting soak. Stamina is still useful for shapeshifters who turn into low-soak animals.

    Venomous snake can compete with cat on defense, but has no way to get good accuracy or withering damage. Poison gives a nice decisive though. Possibly worth using with Quicksilver Second Face just for a decisive attack?
    Indeed, the ability to pick and choose shapes on the fly is a good reason to argue for the strength of the Pure-Shapeshift combatant. It is probably hell on the book-keeping though.

    I would add clawstrider and pestetail to the list of optimal combat forms alongside cat, with claw strider being useful if you have allies in melee and pestetail focusing on soak+damage over accuracy.
    I agree that most of the straightforwardly good at fighting forms are good for lunars with little-to-no combat investment as an emergency option, but not for lunars with a good dex+brawl pool of their own.
    You know, I have to say, mea-culpa, I shouldn't have bad-mouthed the Pestletail at all, and you are 100% right, here's why.

    For animals with a <10 attack pool for any attack, with Lunar shapeshifting, that is automatically (given optimization) a 11 pool (based on max Dex + 5 Brawl + 1 Accuracy), 14 with stunting and an applicable Speciality.

    14 Dice (18 for the 4 mote, below anima flaring boost) is a whole heck of lot of dice to throw if your base damage is 17 (!!) and you aren't Legendary Size (so it's as cheap to turn into a Pestletail as it is to turn into a bear.) For reference, that's almost as much damage as the Earth Shikari and his Artifact Tetsubo with +2 more accuracy for a mere 4 mote commitment.

    Tack on the 17 soak and 10 hardness and the ability to wear Light Armor (because of course you are a Pestletail with high Evasion) and you are basically a lumbering Silver Tank.

    So I'd like to say, if you want to screw stealth and let it all hang out, Pestletail is a damn good form to kill things in. (But of course highly noticeable).

    If people are interested, by the by, I could walk through the rest of the animals and grade them for aspiring Lunar Shapeshifters.

    Also, I'd like to note that a cat's low soak can be mitigated with moonsilver armor (probably light to keep your evasion high). Just for the mental image of a cat in a suit of silver armor.


    Moonsilver armor is going to be great for the high Soak critters. For the Pestletail it's a resting soak of 22 (!!) with Artifact Light. Cat soak is less impressive.

    A lot of animals have merits+special attacks interacting with grapples. I wonder if there's any pattern of shifting forms with Quicksilver Second Face to take advantage of a bunch of these.

    Edit: the best of these are on River Dragons and Tyrant Lizards, who are legendary size. Unless shapeshifting resets mauling bite, the best I found is be a Death Moa/Angler Lizard, grapple someone at short range, and then turn into a bear or great cat for mauling bite. Maybe there's some value to switching between River Dragon and Tyrant Lizard using Behemoth-and-Flea Mastery to use a death roll followed by a ferocious bite followed by a deadly thrash?
    Hmmm, you've clearly lapped me here, I hadn't even started to consider grappling (mostly because as you said the best forms are the Legendary Sized ones, whereas I've just been thinking about day to day DB 'killin). Good point through, cycling through forms might be a necessity for the consumate animal form combatant.

                                I'll be honest, I'm not convinced by the tiny creatures for combat.

                                My totem form is a viper.
                                If I do a withering attack with my hands, I have +4 accuracy and do 11 damage.
                                If I take viper form and do a withering attack, I have +1 accuracy and do 3 damage.

                                Sure, I'm not that bad at hitting (well, 9 dice vs 15 dice, but that's because I can't use Snake Style as a snake; anyway, theoretically it's only 3 less), but with 3 damage, I'm just going to be pinging. And other small animals are the same.

                                Now, there is some value in sneaking (with double 9s for Camouflage, and the enemy losing 2 successes) up to someone and biting them with the initiative from my Join Battle (7 dice, +3 successes, so let's say 7 initiative). And I'll do a bit of damage. But more importantly, poison them.

                                And yes, then they are attacking me and I get +2 Evasion, but Dodge is expensive compared to Brawl (as Brawl gives you Parry and attack) so my Evasion isn't that good anyway, and I could always spend 4m to get the same effect.

                                But then... how am I going to get more Initiative with a Withering damage of 3?

                                All in all, the snake form seems good for sneaking up and biting people, but not after that. Which seems... fair, tbh?

                                A housecat can't even poison people. It's bite has a Withering of 4. Your accuracy is 3 less than a punch; yeah, you make it up on a surprise attack, but that's charm dice anyway.
                                Yeah, they're hot shit at grappling... mice. Rats.
                                They can't grapple people.

                                I suppose if you get the two Latent abilities, they're good, but that's 12xp, you could have bought a charm and a half for that and not needed latent abilities just to do attacks.

                                All-in-all, if you're really keen on killing people as a housecat, you can. It's not a very powerful choice though.

                                Easier to do it as a Tiger.
