                                In my Creation, monster hunters are a thing. The assumption of mortals being basically helpless without Exalted aid against the supernatural and the beasts of Creation never sat well with me. So small bands of hunters form with relative frequency around the Threshold, people who make a living traveling around and taking out the threats that standing armies are too lazy, too far away, or too slow to take out. Wandering Raksha, first circle demons, and all sorts of rogue spirits and strange unique beasts have been laid low (frequently while shouting some variant of "THIS CANNOT BE!") by sword, cunning, grit, and thaumaturgy.

                                This adds a new dynamic to most gods' relations with humans. A great many spirits have learned the hard way that stomping boots on necks has to be done in a calculated manner, because if you step *too* hard, you might find yourself at the business end of a roving band of expert hunters looking to win your people's hearts and purses through your demise - and soon after discovering that it takes you ten painful years to reform and by the time you do your territory has been muscled in by spirits with a better vision sense, or how it feels to get outright sealed inside a teapot and thrown into the bottom of a lake for the next two hundred years.

                                Plenty of Lunars and Sidereals like to claim being behind the monster hunter emergence, but in truth, it's a purely human construct - a reaction to the extremely high degree of bullshit that life on the Threshold in the Age of Sorrows can sometimes imply. Which doesn't mean they don't get used by those same Exalts with relative frequency, of course.
