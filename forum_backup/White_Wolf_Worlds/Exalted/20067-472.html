                                If you want extra occult dice, I think there's more interesting (and genre-appropriate) workings than "you have an Occult excellency". (I mean, I would probably not allow it because I think a working to give you extra dice is both dull and, as Isator says, potentially broken. But I could see why other STs might allow it. Probably should be charm-dice though.)
                                Like enchanting your mighty sanctum so your magic is more powerful when within it, for example.

                                I'll be honest, I think the "give yourself powers" Celestial 2 working could do with a bit more guidance, about what powers might not be appropriate.

                                But generally, I don't think mortal sorcerers need an occult excellency to be, well, mortal sorcerers. They're kind of supposed to be weaker at magic than Dragonblood sorcerers. Having much bigger dice pools than mortal sorcerers is supposed to be what seperates Dragonblood sorcerers.

                                (Also, having an excellency isn't much good for a mortal sorcerer: they don't have any motes to pay for it.)

                                Originally posted by accelerator

                                An ambition 3 Celestial working gives you a supernatural being on par with a second circle demon. What about a 1st circle demon, one that can reproduce?
                                It depends how powerful we're talking (bottom or top end of the "on par with a 1st circle demon" scale), and how fast they reproduce (if it's one generation every 20 years, that's not that useful for most games). And how loyal they are.

                                But I went with Celestial 2 or 3 in my game, when this came up (the creatures the sorceress wanted were pretty powerful, so it was Celestial 3 I think, but it'd have been 2 if they'd been weaker).

                                                Some quick questions:

                                                1.) Some spells make reference to essence rating in their rolls, especially as control spells, do I assume mortal sorcerers have essence 1 or what?
                                                2.) Invulnerable skin of bronze, and I've probably asked this before and forgotten the answer, it says it counts as medium armor, does that restrict the use of normal armor? or martial arts styles?

                                                On the idea of mortal sorcerers trying to get excellencies: I'd avoid it. The excellency is tied up in the nature of the exalted and it's just not worth the headache of trying to exalt yourself. If you want to get better at rolls with a mortal sorcerer there are a few tricks: specialties (shaping essence, workings), 2-dot artifacts (They have no special powers just a bonus dice on whatever skill or type of roll the artifact is designed to work on, but they don't need to be attuned.), self improvement (via workings a mortal sorcerer can give themselves merits and boosts that might be useful).

                                                In fact... how much of a working would it take to raise an attribute to 6 is that a thing you can do? If you can raise your intelligence you can get a bonus on a lot of sorcery rolls, and maybe repeat the process to get larger dice pools. I mean some animals can get an effective 7 dots in strength so a mortal might be able to enhance their thinking bits to a similar level.
