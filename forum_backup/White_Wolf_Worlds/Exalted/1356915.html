          The Witcher is an odd one, because Geralt is actually a mutant created by magic, and so one could argue that he's a mortal turned into a Witcher by a sorcerous working.
          The other thing of course is, while overall he tends to be rather Dawn-ish (Melee-supernal, Dodge, Thrown, Resistance), although with good ratings in Twilight abilities, he does have various spells that really don't fit Exalted's style, it's more DnD.
          I guess you could have him as a Dawn with Hypnotic Tongue Technique, and a ranged magical attack (Aard for Withering, Igni for Decisive), but I'm not sure how you would do Quen or Yrden.
