        Reading this thread and thinking about the many ways in which Sidereal charms encourage them to be jerks makes me think that Rakan Thulio's personal crusade is an in-setting catharsis/guideline for everyone that doesn't like stuff like Shun the Smiling Lady, Ride the Dragon, and general fishboning. Maybe the 3E understanding is that as a Sidereal player, sooner or later you have to come to terms with the fact that you work for the Bureau of Callous Dickery, and you need to decide whether you're okay with that or not.

        Earlier editions gave us plenty of example NPCs that somehow deal- Chejop, Ayesha, etc. have come to terms with working through the Bureau of Callous Dickery, and being willing to accept varying degrees of compromise regarding how much the world sucks in exchange for some scrap of stability. Alongside them, 3E gives us the PC who halfway through the campaign gives the Maidens the finger and sets off on his own, to bring true justice to the cosmos with his own two fists. The Getimians are, in part, an option for players who want to make that rebellion the whole focus of their campaign, rather than a midway point of character development.

        Of course, 3E may also downplay the emphasis on callousness as a component of Sidereal power.

            I honestly think that we are going to see a massive revamp on how Siddy charms work, come 3rd Edition. They will still have a closed charm tree, because that's kinda their schtick...but there are some things about them that are getting changed. It has been explicitly said that a Sidereal is not required to branch off into Martial Arts to remain viable in high-xp combat.

            First off, they are no longer pariahs in Yu Shan, with everyone trying to screw them over. The devs have been pretty clear that the Siddies are well respected people doing an important job. (Possibly because most of the Siddies who were around during the Usurpation are dead now)

            Secondly, it's been acknowledged that the Siddy charm tree was rubbish in many places...and they have said that they would 'be shocked' if the Sidereal Charm Tree didn't see the same sort of expansion as the Solar tree has seen.

            As to the character concepts... John Morke says:
            there have been problems with the way Sidereals have been depicted, especially in how they operate mindlessly and without question, and especially in how the gods seethe with hatred and resentment for them. This is shit that will be corrected.
            Honestly, I think the 2E writers made a bit of a mistake with that 'choke on a fishbone' mission thing that they dropped into the MoEP. It made it look like that's how they always operated. Because the thing is...a Sidereal's job is to make sure that Fate goes the direction it needs to go. Sure, a Chosen of Endings may have a tendency to push Fate along their chosen path by stabbing anyone who might interfere...but there are plenty of other options.

            Say...you need to prevent the rising of a tyrant. A child has been born who, if untampered with, will rise up to be a horrible tyrant who colludes with the Fair Folk, and leads to a vast number of deaths.
            A Chosen of Endings may simply make sure the child doesn't survive to adulthood.
            A Chosen of Journeys may instead bring about events that relocate the child and their family to somewhere where they won't have the same Fate, or give the child experiences that pushes them towards a path as a traveling merchant
            A Chosen of Serenity may ensure that the child has such a happy life that they grow up as a well-adjusted member of society.
            A Chosen of Battles may ensure that as the child grows, they are pitted in conflict with the Fair Folk so that, while they still become a powerful ruler, their blade is turned against the forces of chaos, instead of towards seizing rulership
            A Chosen of Secrets may tamper with the child's education and upbringing so that, while they still come into a powerful ruling position, they are instead a fair and just ruler who leads his people well.

            Sometimes, Sidereals have to do dirty work. Sometimes you have to excise a tumor to protect the whole. But sometimes, you get to terminate a plague instead. Sometimes you get to arrange the meeting and romance of a destined couple. Sometimes you are the traveler who stops in a tavern and encourages a young man to set out on the road to seek his fortune.Sometimes you're the old man on the mountain who advises the hero in his quest.

            Some days, you have to do terrible things. But you aren't blind and you aren't stupid. You can look into the future and see the results of what you are about to do the same as your superiors can. You don't have to obey without question. You can find another way. You can expose the corruption of your superiors. You've got a hard job for sure....you often have to make tougher calls than any of the other Exalted because you know more about the shape of the world than they do...but it is worth it. Some days...one must die to save a thousand. Others days...one must be protected to saved a thousand.

                Originally posted by Amakawa Yuuto View Post
                Ok, I'm slightly off-topic here, but...
                Not headcanon, not "what if", it's right in the beginning of the charm section. The Maidens can and did create new charms. They don't do it often, but they do it.
                The charmset was never as hermetically "no new charms, ever" closed as it's often made out to be.
                The thing is, the charmset is hermetically sealed... from an in-universe perspective.

                Other Exalted who have been around for any period of time have either been taught or figured out that the "natural" applications of their essence that come to them relatively easily aren't the be-all and end-all of their potential. They can develop the way their essence interacts with the world and with themselves, their Charms, according to their own wishes. Some of them will do it through focused study and meditations, others through trial-and-error brute force, but they can actually forge new Charms to use in furtherance of whatever they decide their purpose as an Exalted is. They don't have carte blanche, of course, but there's a lot of flexibility.

                Sidereals can't do that. What they have is what they have. Sure, maybe the Maidens will show up tomorrow with a bunch of new ones. You can't count on that, and that's different from having control over your own essence.

                From the perspective of your average Five-Score Fellow, his charmset is hermetically sealed. That's always been part of the point.

                                    Notionally and semantically, I don't know how much it's like

                                    "The Maidens have designed these Charms; they have given these Charms to their Exalted; they could've designed others, but didn't because Fate or their wisdom"

                                    vs

                                    "Among the Maidens, for example, the Maiden of Battles has dominion over close combat; these Sidereal Melee Charms are just all the techniques channeling her essence and Sidereal essence that there can be" (no one is "designing" anything, it simply turns out that these are the Charms, that exist, from expressing the essence of the Maiden of Battles and the Sidereals with the certain economy of expression characteristic of the nature of Fate).

                                    I sort of have a preference for the latter, simply because it feels less like the Maidens are choosing the limits of the Chosen, limiting them from what they could be, so much as that is just a natural limit inherent to their essence. (trying not to leap into my typical spiel about how it's important for the Sidereals not to feel like stupid fools who do things for no reasons while facing arbitrary limits designed to frustrate them).

                                    Note this still actually allows for some expansion from the Maidens themselves, it's just that becomes more of "She is changing her Essence and the Sidereal essence, which is a big deal for Fate, and now it can be channeled in new ways" and not "She made a new Charm or deigned to gift it to her Chosen".

                                                            Originally posted by Murcushio View Post
                                                            The thing is, the charmset is hermetically sealed... from an in-universe perspective.

                                                            ...yeah, no. Introducing new charms does not break canon (not that "canon" is even a thing on the table - it's just a thing in the books, but even there, new charms are reasonable). Adding in new charms is perfectly possible. Heck, in Glories, the Maiden have a charm that allows them to give any sidereal praying to them a charm as a training effect, which is explicitaly called out to be combinable with their ability to create new charms - so, I sidereal in a bind can pray "How in Malfeas am I supposed to prevent these bandits from killing these few puppies?", and the Maidens can answer by directly handing him a charm that allows him to give these puppies the fate of an huge wolf pack.
