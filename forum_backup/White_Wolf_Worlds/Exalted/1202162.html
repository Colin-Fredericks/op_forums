    Has WFHW Changed Our Understanding of DB Exaltations?

    04-02-2018, 05:00 AM
    One thing that I've noticed in the WFHW preview that we have so far is that two alternative Dragon-blooded traditions (namely Prasad and the Forest Witches) have the expectations that Dragon-blooded will reincarnate as Dragon-blooded, and be able to remember previous lives.

    As I understand it, Dragon-blooded sparks don't reincarnate. Are Prasad and the rest mistaken and/or delusional about the nature of DB exaltations, or has WFHW induced a fundamental change to how the Chosen of the Dragons work?

                                          Right?

                                          The whole karmic reincarnation thing the Immaculate Philosophy has going on was developed as a means of social control by the Sidereals and quite likely by the Dragon-Blooded themselves. (This has never been stated explicitly anywhere, but I have always believed that the post-Usurpation Dragon-Blooded were deeply involved in crafting the tenets of the Immaculate Philosophy, at least as much as the Sidereals were.) It says to the peasantry that if they hew to the correct way of living, they'll be rewarded by earning an Exaltation in their next life, and it says to the Dragon-Blooded that if they don't hew to the correct way of living, they will be denied union with the dragons and be reincarnated into much less comfortable circumstances than being a Prince of the Earth.

                                          The Prasadi view DEEPLY undercuts that. It establishes a perpetual overcaste that persists through death and reincarnation regardless of your deeds in any specific life. That's going to have pretty big knock-on effects for, say, how the Prasadi Dragon-Blooded interact with the unExalted, and especially how their Immaculate offshoot does as well. And that's just for starters.

                                                          (My response does not speak for the developers.)

                                                          Reincarnation is a form of social mobility in the Immaculate Philosophy, but that mobility isn't only upward. According to this philosophy, Dragon-Blooded who fail to uphold their place as sacred pillars of society may fall from grace in later lives. Dragon-Blooded status in the next life is earned, over and over, not assured. I'd hazard a guess that some Dynasts assume that Lost Eggs are on the cusp of falling back to mortality in later incarnations.

                                                          ​I don't believe there's an emphasis in Prasad on remembering​ former lives, so much as inheriting and being recognized for them. The Dragon Caste knows of Lethe, after all. The Pure Way itself doesn't teach that reincarnation follows clan and jati, but it is a common belief. This belief also allows for downward social mobility--someone who behaves like a member of a lower caste may fall beneath the family they've known, and be all the more scorned and tragic for it.

                                                          ​The Forest Witches are weird, and have been since 1E.
