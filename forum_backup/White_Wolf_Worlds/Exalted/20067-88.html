                                                    Originally posted by iamrpgdm View Post
                                                    Does this mean that you can be at short range, perform TRA and then move back to short range? Also, if you went on your initiative, could you be at Medium range, move to short, then TRA?
                                                    No. The attack counts as your turn, so you never get to take your turn, therefore you never get a chance to use your movement action. Likewise, you couldn't take it partway through your own turn, since you can't replace a turn you're already taking. I'm not sure what the not using your movement clause is intended to do, but it doesn't seem to accomplish anything by the rules. Might be something to send to Rich.
