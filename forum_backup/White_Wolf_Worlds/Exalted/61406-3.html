                                Here's one that just occurred to me:

                                Anyone who wants inspiration for The West is well advised to watch one of my favorite movies, Captain Blood. It has a man sold into slavery for a crime he didn't commit (though he was guilty of disliking the unjust laws crushing his society), who is forced into difficult labor but still takes pains upon himself to spare his fellow slaves. He manages to arrange an escape, and then uses a combination of manipulation, medical skills, and sheer swordplay to both become a dread pirate and protect the defenseless - including his former tormentors - from slaughter at the hands of less ethical outlaws. Good stuff.

                                    Been doing some rewatching of Disney and other animated movies lately. And have to say, for those of us who are absolutely fed up with the serious business approach of large sections of the fanbase, Rapunzel is actually surprisingly apt as an inspiration for a Solar. Everywhere she goes she rekindles the given up dreams of people with but a word and a smile, and basically all who meet her all but become her minions because she just has a light inside that fires up everyone. People are willing to go against their own establishment or risk death and imprisonment for her sake not because anything she offers or because of any grand power - but because she makes them feel like they matter, and so they want to do so.

                                    Not appropriate for your average Creation, though. A Rapunzel in a story where Gothel is closer to right than wrong would probably be a rather different thing.

                                    Also watched Kung Fu Panda. Oogway is so Sidereal he shits starlight. So is Shifu, really, only in the other facet. One is the sage seer who knows you better than you do. The other is the put-upon sourpuss teacher whose failures come back to haunt him. Oogway is what being a masterful Sidereal is supposed to work, Shifu is how most average Sidereals often end up feeling. And well, can't go wrong with Kung Fu Panda as inspiration for martal arts, really. The choreography is just wonderful.
