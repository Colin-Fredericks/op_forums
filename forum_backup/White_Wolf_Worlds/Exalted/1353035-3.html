            Generally, I'm not a fan of rules-lite variants. A lot of them are jumpstart/quickstart kits that only contain some of the rules, which is totally fine for a jumpstart kick to do to make things easier for totally new players, but doesn't interest me because it feels like I'm buying an incomplete version of the game when I could just wait and buy the real version when it comes out instead. Looking at you, Cyberpunk Red.

            The ones that are meant to be a complete ruleset have a tendency to streamline or cut out the parts of the system that I like or that I appreciate for what they are. Looking at you, Shadowrun Anarchy.

            For me, there's just a lot of... disinterest by association? Not sure what to call it. I like the product in principle for people who need it, but previous experiences with similar products have turned me off to the idea.

            There's a good chance I'd buy it just for the lore on the Exalted though.

            [Ex3] Why Gods Need the Exigence - Plot hooks for Exigent characters of various gods.
            [Ex3] Homebrew Solar Charms - I can see the future, and it is glorious.
