                            Originally posted by Chausse View Post
                            Take travel distance and time. It's good to have a Charm that says &quot;You can travel in one day instead of one week&quot;, but I think it's not really different than &quot;You can travel in 2 days instead of 1 week&quot;, because most ST do not account for the specific of time and distance, and are more usually working with time periods (days, weeks, months, years, decades, centuries ...). I don't think I've ever seen any situation in my gaming life where the ST was like &quot;Well thanks to your speed superpower you made it in specifically 4 days and I set my timer at 4 days and a half and no more so you succeeded, but you wouldn't if your superpower allowed you to do this in at best 5 days&quot;.
                            This is an issue that can be solved with extended rolls representing time sensitive travel, which I use all the time.

                            You have three days to make the journey, the extended roll is difficulty 0, with a goal number of 15 and an interval of one day. Get 15 successes in 3 rolls or fail. Any sail charms that add speed add to this, and so do any athletics charms that affect tests of speed.

                            Charms like Wind-Racing Essence Infusion give you a dice bonus to tests of speed, but also give you a speed multiplier in “narrative time”. I’m 90% sure what that means is that when time is not a factor and you aren’t busting out the extended rolls, you can still use the charm to move blazingly fast, but I’m fairly sure there are no charms in the game that just give you a speed bonus without mentioning how they affect tests of speed.

                            As far as the speed reading goes, it doesn’t provide a bonus to extended rolls but it’s so absurdly fast that you can pretty much just handwave the thing anyway. It’s like Lock Opening Touch, just instead of “oh there’s a lock in our way? *poke* it’s open, let’s go” it’s “oh this is a record of this kingdoms historical legends? *flip flip flip* okay, got it, there were a bunch of pointless stories about their old city god who’s dead now, but one veeery interesting one about an air spirit who once lived here and now doesn’t, we need to track her down.”

                            Originally posted by Alistair View Post
                            For an effect as niche as &quot;read fast&quot; without any other uses, no, it should at best be Linguistics 1 Essence 1 no prereqs. Literally no game I've been in has involved reading something as part of the plot, let alone reading something super fast due to some time limit, and I've been in a ton of them.
                            I’ve also been in a ton and I’ve never seen strategic warfare used even once, and I’ve probably only seen cover mechanics used maybe twice. I honestly can’t remember a single time cover has been used in any of the games I’ve been in, I’m just not as 100% certain that I’ve never seen it as strategic warfare. That doesn’t mean that Battle Visionary’s Foresight isn’t a stupid OP charm, just that I’ve never played in or ran a game where any of my players were great generals or involved that mechanic.

                            It might be slightly over pre-req at Essence 3, but I wouldn’t put it under Essence 2. You have to balance charms for the kinds of campaigns they’ll be used in, not how often those situations come up over all campaigns. Otherwise Sail would be a very different charm tree.

                                                            Originally posted by Alistair View Post

                                                            I can see that you feel strongly about this. Golden Rule it to whatever you like at your table.

                                                            We're not in a game together, you don't have to convince us.

                                                            I think everyone here agrees Linguistics 5, Essence 3 and 3 prepress is too high. We'd don't need to agree a consensus on what it should be.

                                                            For my part, I see Solar Charms like this:

                                                            Essence 1 -- normal but better. "Excellent Strike" style Charms. Things that give an edge over mortals.
                                                            Essence 3 -- the power of the Sun. Bend reality to your will with Wyld Shaping and Celestial Sorcery. Fight with the Invincible Fury of the Dawn. Seduce monks and husbands with your demon dance. Things that let you dominate gods.
                                                            Essence 5 -- peak Exalted power. The Aegis of Invincible might that leaves you invulnerable to harm. Proclaim doom upon a city with Dogstar Ruminations. Slay all foes before you in a Circle of Bright Reaving. Rapters in an F16.

                                                            Reading fast? Essence 1. Reading as fast as you can turn the page? For me, that kind of Flash speed, super reading isn't on a par with "rebuild[ing] a ruined body and stand it upright in the time it takes to lace on boots" (Essence 3) but seems about the same as slashing a shockwave through the air to hit a distant foe (Essence 2).

                                                            It's not about how useful the Charm is, it's about how overtly magical it is (with some concessions for genre).

                                                            Johnny-5ing a library is not (in my opinion) an Essence 1 effect. But it's not like Exalted is perfectly balanced game. If you want it at Essence 1, then go for it. Houserule it however you like.

That doesn't mean an Essence 1 speed reading charm can't exist: just that it can't be as powerful as three pages a second for a single mote.)
