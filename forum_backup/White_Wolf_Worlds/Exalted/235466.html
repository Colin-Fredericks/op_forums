    Ragara Bhagwei is the Best Character in this Goddamned Game

    08-20-2014, 12:32 PM


    Ragara Bhagwei is the son of Cynis and Ragara. No, not the houses; the founders. Which means he is the son of two half-siblings, but instead of giving him genetic disorders it gave him genetic awesomeness.

    Because of his origins, Bhagwei might be the one person with the most solid claim to inheriting the Scarlet Throne in the whole Dynasty, but he just doesn't give a shit and pretends to be a simple Ragara.

    Ragara Bhagwei was taught medicine by a Wood Aspect doctor known to be "one of the finest physicians in Creation" while he was a child, and he became better at medicine than his teacher while he was still a mortal child. He also self-taught himself Old Realm before he was 11. Then he Exalted while curing someone of an unknown, uncurable poison at the Empress's personal demand.

    Ragara Bhagwei is the only other surviving student of Versino, the Realm's school of sorcery previous to the founding of the Heptagram. The other survivor? Mnemon, who blew it all up while Ragara was off on a vacation. Cursing Mnemon's damn shenanigans, he swore to sow life and order wherever she would spread destruction, making him Mnemon's Good Twin. Getting to an auspicious start, he immediately founded the Heptagram, which he still directs.

    Ragara Bhagwei knows the ways to sex so powerful, it makes his Dragon-Blooded partners explode. He usually refrains.

    Once, Ragara Banoba and his council were considering the seemingly impossible matter of allocating resources to the 11 satrapies of his House. Bhagwei passed by while eating a peach, caught a glance of the papers on the table, grabbed them, and scribbled a complete solution to one of the Realm's major economic crisis in fifteen minutes. Then he ate the rest of his peach.

    House Cynis is currently considering whether to assassinate before he decides to get into that whole "war for the throne" thing and make his legacy public. Little do they know that not only Bhagwei gives not a single shit about that, he is too busy investigating the Abyssal Exalted. Which he assumes to be the dark mirrors of the Dragon-Blooded, because come on, if you were as awesome as he is you wouldn't be looking for a more powerful host for Abyssals to mirror.

    When the Dowager's Abyssal child assassin came after him because he stole a valuable book, Bhagwei's bodyguards killed her. Baghwei then flayed her corpse and made a hood out of her skin so he could see death stuff, because he is that hardcore. And also creepy. Did I mention the exploding sex already?

    Ragara Bhagwei's plans for the future are mainly centered around curing the Great Contagion, therefore retroactively proving the entire Shogunate to have been a bunch of half-assed slackers.



    Anyway, Aspect Book: Wood is the most insane of Aspect Books, but it's also just plain great. Read it.

          It is!

          RAGARA BANOBA ON BHAGWEI

          Save for Heral, there is no man in Creation who is closer to my heart than my brother. His mind is like no other I have encountered; at once a perfectly still and unwavering pillar and, simultaneously, a darting flight of raptors, far-reaching and inescapable. I remember on one occasion when Heral and I had called our council to chambers in order to decide the allocation of resources for 11 distant satrapy holdings of House Ragara. This was something I thought impossible at the time, and the entirety of my council felt so as well.

          Along comes my brother, an old man even then. He was eating a peach at the time, rivulets of juice dripping from his chin and moustache. Doddering and humble, he made his way to the planning table and casually greeted the satraps and heads of lines that surrounded him as his eyes darted over the piles of charts and figures, even being so bold as to seize personal records from the hands of those still reading over them. He turned to me, smiled like a madman and then took my chair, scribbling in a frenzy while talking to himself. After no more than a quarter of an hour, Bhagwei sighed and handed me the culmination of his dabbling. A major crisis of the Realm’s economy had been resolved in less than a half an hour by a doddering old man with peach juice on his face.

          My brother is a deeply compassionate being with an undying love for all life and life in and of itself. He is considerate, sensitive, patient and wise. For this reason, Ragara Bhagwei should never rule the Realm.

                                  Cynis has been specifically mentioned as having a dragon-blooded daughter of the Empress as founder and that she had at least 5 daughters of her own, 3 of whom are house Cynis current leaders (and apparently much like Banoba, younger than Bhagwei too).

                                  As an aside, i always had the impression from the fluff in Aspectbook Earth that, at least up to her exaltation, Mnemon had something of a secret crush on Ragara - and things might have gone very differently for both if not for his hastily-thought attempt on her life disguised as a gift.

                                  Speaking of the Windtamer, in Aspectbook: Air we are first given the Crosswind Techniques, a group of combat (Thrown) charms predicated on emulating and channeling essence in the form of the very wind, taught centuries ago by the Windtamer. The most basic of these Crosswind Offense, would later become the opening charm of Night Breeze Style in 2e, while Crosswind Feint might have a relation to either Shepherd the Leaf or Thousand-Hailstone Morning and Wind Needle Training, Night Breeze's pinnacle charm is pretty much Crosswind Empowerment Technique.

                                  While it's not a whole port - Crosswind Feint is not quite either of the two charms i mentioned and has even more in common with Essence union darts (Oadenol's Codex, pg.34) and Crosswind Gale has no 2e equivalent that i know of - Night Breeze does show some interesting connections that would point to its creator being someone familiar with Cynis Mond early work, if not the Windtamer itself.
