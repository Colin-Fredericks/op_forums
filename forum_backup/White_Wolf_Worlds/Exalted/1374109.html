    The Anatomy of a Catgirl

    03-11-2020, 09:07 AM



    As you all know, other species have different anatomy from humans which range from having four pairs of nipples to inserting sperms through pseudo-needle genitals. Which leads to the question of what happen to a Lunar's anatomy when they use Hybrid Body Transformation? Would a Shark Lunar have his junk turn into claspers? Do they grow additional organs to produce things like poison or fire-breath? There are so many biological mysteries about Lunar that are still answered, so how do you deal with this question in your story?
