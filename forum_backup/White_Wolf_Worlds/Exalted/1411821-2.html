    I can't believe this slipped past me for as long as it did; mostly finished reading the main book, have peeked a bit at the follow-up Companion book. As someone who was a huge fan of classic WoD and got into Exalted from its connections to it, this is the sort of thing that would have made me squirm in ecstacy a decade ago! These days, I'm more of the opinion that the more distance between the two gamelines, the better, and I openly wish that the few vestiges of WoD in Exalted be removed. But I still had a TON of fun with this!

    The Charms were a particular high point, as has been mentioned. Part of the draw of Vampire to me has always been the straight-forward simplicity...I can introduce that game to people who don't know what roleplaying is, and have them rolling in a half-hour! That sort of quick and easy simplicity was always an appeal of White Wolf and tbh, I feel like that's getting lost. As though some working for WW are kinda embarrassed by it, and run on the idea that "real" gamers like heavy crunch...Storypath seems geared towards that in a way; it's actually not that complex, but it strives to give the *illusion* of complexity. But, yeah, the mild headache of having to learn *yet another* alternate version of the charm sets was offset by how cool and easy it all was TO learn. I'll be surprised if not a few players go, "Can we...just use this for ALL our games now?"

    Really appreciated the humor, too! One of my favorite bits was Holden warning against have mixed PCs including various Exalts and obscure Supernaturals for thematic reasons...only to immediately reverse that, and *encourage* such a campaign, where they all ride around in a car beating up antediluvians...and then suggests detailing this game session to the folks at the White Wolf booth at a gaming convention, just so you can watch the light slowly die in their eyes in real time!

    While on the subject of WoD: afaic, it ended with Time of Judgement. I completely bought into their stated reasons for ending it, and embraced the New WoD, which in many ways, I actually like better! I was frankly shocked when they brought OWoD back, and rebranded NWoD, as if to tacitly admit that it wasn't that good or selling that great, and now we're in some sort of Marvel/Ultimate Marvel situation. I wonder if this is sustainable, or if it's just a matter of time until they cancel CoD, or merge it with WoD (if only by introducing things unique to it like Prometheans and Deviants).

    I'll be honest, I haven't looked at any of the new, revived stuff. I'm sure it's good, but how similar is it to the OWoD stuff? Times and culture have changed, so I guess it's not as In Your Face as it was in the 90s? Is it still Metaplot heavy? Does it reference the ToJ at all, or is this like an alternate setting where none of that happened? Are there any plans to introduce the newer games, like Promethean and Beast?

    If I could ask Holden any one thing, it would be on the feasibility of adapting this book to CoD, and whether he'd be up to the task...

                        I’ve played it. In fact, though things are about a month and a half into a break, the game is still ongoing.
                        ExWoD is fun! At its base, it’s just plain fun. We’ve had a Sidereal (Journeys), a Solar (Zenith), an Abyssal (Day), and an Infernal (no castes). We’ve just added a Lunar and a 7th Gen Kiasyd, but I haven’t seen them in action yet.

                        The game has lots of clunky bits—Infernals are particularly unbalanced, getting the best of a couple things by a large margin, and getting straight screwed on some others, but pretty much every part works like it’s supposed to work. Solars are a holy terror for Vampires, Abyssals are the S-class of killing things, etc.

                        Seriously, the only part that really seems to be an issue is the fact that it was designed for the OWoD, and not the newer Chronicles. And if you’re a dyed-in-the-wool WoD partisan, like me, that’s not really an issue at all.

                        Bottom line: If you’ve ever wanted to just wreck the careful balance between Vampire factions, or stomp on some Wan-Kuei, play this game!

                                "Have you played it? "

                                Nope

                                Read it?

                                Yup

                                "What do you think of it? What's your opinion of it?"

                                I liked it, but it kinda leaves out my favorite part of Exalted, which is that in Exalted the abilities and powers of the Chosen grow in all dimensions of possible power. For example, a Dawn and a Twighlight and an Eclipse all have radically different but radically potent abilities to change the world. Someone can be a super-diplomat and that translates into meaningful powers and meaningful reactions within the fiction. One of Exalted's core strengths, to me, is that it makes (or at least tries to make) those aspects of plays as meaningful, important and accessible as the climactic battle scene.

                                Exalted vs WoD is all about the climactic battle scene. That's what the author was writing the book for. That's the story he was interested in allowing play for. I'm just not as interested in that story. I can get that from other systems without worrying about the baggage of the OWoD system that I dislike anyway.

                                " Any suggestions for improvements?"

                                There are certainly things I would do differently, and things I dislike about the game; but these ae things that would fundamentally change what the game is about. It's like.. although I like Exalted and I like WoD and I like the idea of a mash-up, I don't really want a mash up that tries to be the game played in this way. If I wanted a game that played the way ExVsWoD plays, the version that's out there is perfect for it. If I tried to "improve" it, I wouldn't be improving it so much as changing it from the game it's trying to play to the game I'd rather be playing. So, it wouldn't be honest or appropriate of me to claim I could improve on it.

                                "Have you done any homebrew for it?"

                                Nah, though it has inspired me to do a lot more work on a homebrew game sort of like Exalted Vs WoD. And then I abandoned that possible game; as one does when one is a TTRP gamer =D
