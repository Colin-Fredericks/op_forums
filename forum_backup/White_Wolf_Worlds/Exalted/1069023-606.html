                                            For a QC write-up like Mara, where she's just got the one Martial Arts style, it's safe to assume that whatever she can use is within her dice limit.

                                            If you're making your own that can stack a bunch of bonuses to the same thing, the easiest method is to borrow the dice cap of an Exalt type of comparable strength.                                                         #9104
                                                        02-27-2018, 12:05 PM
                                                        Originally posted by HighPriest View Post
                                                        What is the mechanical effect of Prophet of Seventeen Cycles (Lore Charm, page 339)? It says that it gives people who follow the Solar's prediction get bonuses and characters who don't get penalties, but there are no numbers or rolls given for how large the bonuses/penalties are, or how many times they apply per use of the Charm.

                                                        I think the answer to this is ultimately "Storyteller's call". The intent of the charm, it seems to me, is that the Solar using it is just revealing already-existing environmental, social, or supernatural factors that will prevent the course of action they want to avoid. Note that it references the project system, and the example given mentions a bunch of things ("resource shortages, elemental
                                                        attacks, inclement weather...") that would be "penalties" to the project, but not mechanical dice penalties.

                                                        When it comes to dice penalties, which the charm mentions as well, I'd use, as a rule of thumb, -5 or larger penalties to any rolls that directly oppose the prediction, -3 for rolls that indirectly oppose the prediction, and -1 for rolls that don't directly or indirectly oppose it, but could set up other actions that do. So, using the example in the book, Memnon's generals would be at -5 to their rolls to directly invade Jiara during the summer, including War rolls, Bureaucracy rolls to organize such an invasion, Sail rolls to cross the sea from the Blessed Isle to Jiara, and so on. She'd be at -3 to make a Presence roll to convince another Great House to make the invasion in her place, and her generals would probably take that penalty if they tried to push for an autumn invasion instead of winter proper. And she and her forces would be at -1 to land an invasion force in Realm-controlled territory near Jiara during the summer, or to try to destablize the Jiaran rebels to weaken their resistance during the summer.
