                        03-07-2019, 06:01 PM
                        Originally posted by wern212 View Post
                        So, this might end up a bit too complicated of a question to ask in here, but I would really appreciate some feedback here.
                        As far as I can tell, the main thing that is stopping a social-focused Exalt from just loading everyone with Defining Intimacies of their choosing is that in order to strengthen an intimacy, you must present greater evidence (or arguments that are more compelling). (Core Rulebook, page 216). Getting someone a supporting intimacy can be achieved by throwing dice at their resolve, but this evidence must exist in-setting. It can, of course, be fabricated, for the more con artist style of play, but it must still exist in some fashion.

                        This all comes down to the question: what constitutes a big enough increase in evidence or arguments to allow one to upgrade an intimacy?
                        There's no objective measurement of evidentiary value, or differences in degree thereof; it's left to the Storyteller to determine whether a renewed claim is backed by sufficient proof to make success possible.

                        And does it matter what level it's going to, or does it just matter that it's more than what came before it?
                        In theory the latter, although in practice they'll tend to have the same effect.

                        And does instilling a minor intimacy require evidence of any significance?
                        Not beyond your own testimony!

                        Finally, when I wish for a context of an intimacy to be shifted slightly (from friends to lovers, for example), does this require building up an entirely new intimacy?
                        There's not a non-magical way to alter an Intimacy of X into an Intimacy of Y without completely eroding it and rebuilding it through influence (although you could skip the eroding step, and try to instill them with an Intimacy of Y in addition to their Intimacy of X).
