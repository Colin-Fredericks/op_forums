    She isn't designed to be a literal genie or a jackass genie. Her entire purpose is to give people knowledge, and she seems to go all-out in order to do so. She takes pride in it.

    On the Jinn topic, I doubt Jinn will try to screw a person over since that feels kind of counter to the point of the relics.                                         Thoughts about the divine curse:

                                        Who cursed Salem to live forever? The God of Light; the sympathetic, non-dickish brother.

                                        Why? Why did he think Salem was that important? Why doesn't he remove the curse? What is unique about Salem?

                                        Realization: She was the first person to go to the God of Darkness and ask for something, or at least something that people would normally go to his brother to get. Or maybe she was just the first to lie to him.

                                        One way or another, Salem is uniquely important to the Dark God. She seems to have gained his attention in the way that no other human did before. He actually did something for her to be nice, even if it was motivated by his own vanity.

                                        In short, Salem represents a turning point for the Dark God.

                                        Which god moved first to attack her when she was about to attack them at the pools of annihilation? The God of Light. He stopped her from provoking his brother, who would have killed her or worse. He saved her life from his brother, and then he went even further to protect her life from his brother and his destructive creations and anything else.

                                        WHY?

                                        Remember what the ENTIRE POINT of this story is. This entire story is the God of Light trying to change his brother's perspective. To change his mind and get him to like created things so that he'll stop destroying everything.

                                        Salem became an opportunity that the Light God COULD NOT ALLOW to vanish.

                                        If Salem can learn the lessons that the God of Light has been trying to impart to his brother, his brother who is watching her might finally change his own mind too. He might also accept the value of mortal life and the weight of mortal death. He might also stop thinking only of himself, as Salem does, and consider how his actions hurt other people.
