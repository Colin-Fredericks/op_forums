                    Death Kitty is certainly nice, not to mention fun, if you invested in those 2 latents, with a truly impressive base defense but it won't dominate the game. For that the damage is too low. It misses the cannon part of glass cannon. As for accuracy, a pack hunter who learns the latent to apply this to his human allies can add up to 3 successes to his attack roll (and after 1 wp, 3 on raw damage of decisives), so I must contest that death kitty is far from the most accurate of animals. Also, the pack hunters generally add distract gambit shenanigans which are supported by native charms, and some like the wolves/dogs even add in free defend others with complimentary counterattacks (though much less damage than claw striders).

                    Also, there are so many great grapple latents! For example a bear can open with 'Crushing Bear Hug' on a withering attack, allowing a follow-up reflexive grapple with an accuracy boost. Learn 'Crushing Embrace', giving that a follow-up decisive attack with raw damage equal to the extra successes on your control roll which ignores hardness and doesn't reset. Add 'Mauling Bite' for good measure. Now that's a solid way to open combat, with a thoroughly mauled opponent in your loving embrace! And we have yet to see the Strength Charms!


                    Also the flyers! A Death Diving, Eagle with Swooping Descent & Peck out the Eyes has +4 accuracy, +2 successes on that first attack roll from stealth, double 10's on damage & can forgo one damage to blind. That is also a really nice way to start (or finish) combat!

                    And with all their intimidation charms lunars have yet another way to do truly wicked stuff. Take the Gorilla: can do join battle with intimidation thanks to Howling Aggression, and you go first anyway if you beat their resolve. Add Predators Menace so all successes over resolve also reduce their intitiative and, well, you have entire charm trees dedicated to be scary, so, eh, solid odds for crashing them while rolling join battle. Oh, and much grappling goodness such as mauling bite and savaging strength, so see above. Merciful kami, gorillas will be very, very scary lunar opponents...

                    Ok, and that was just me looking till 'Gorilla'. I'm convinced Death Kitty will be one scary, awesome, deeply troubling house cat, yes, but then again it's a Lunar housecat, so yeah. It's still best suited for sneaking and infiltration and there are some much more effective animals for general mayhem and destruction.

                        I've thought about this and reviewed the stats and:

                        1. You are completely right and I was probably fixating on it a tad too much.

                        2. Don't know why my eyes glazed over big cat bite damage, dang!

                        Revised rules for decent shapeshift options for day to day use:

                        1. You probably want to avoid the Legendary Sized stuff except for emergencies as A. Super obvious and B. Not Mote Efficient.

                        2. You probably want something with an attack with crap accuracy (<10 dice for accuracy), but big damage (15+). Then you can sub in your own dice pool and pick up a +1 accuracy to it on top. Sans all else, you get a 14 accuracy pool pre-merits and mote expenditure if you count stunting and a relevant specialty. In this specific category (<10 accuracy 15+ damage) we have:

                        - Bear (Bite 15 damage)
                        - Benthic Knifetooth (Bite 15 damage)
                        - Boar Tusk Crocodile (Bite 16 damage)
                        - Bunyip (Headbutt 15 damage)
                        - Claw Strider (Bite 15 damage)
                        - Death Moa (Peck 15 damage)
                        - Gorilla (Bite 16 damage)
                        - Great Cat (Bite 16 damage)
                        - Pestletail (Tail Slam 17 damage, Minimum 4)

                        As you can see if you want to get the most out of your specialty (Vance's recommendation is that you can select one type of attack), you'd want a Brawl Specialty for Bite, which gets you the superior accuracy on the Boar Tusk Crocodile, the Great Cat and the Gorilla and you just miss the gold standard of the Pestletail (which you have to take a specific specialty for, I don't think any other animal has a Tail Slam.)

                        It's late and I don't have time to go through all the cases mentioned above, but lets look at the four I highlighted.

                        Boar Tusk Crocodile:

                        A very decent combat form thanks to high soak (13/0) and this merit:

                        Crushing Bite: The three elongated fangs that give the
                        boar-tusk crocodile its name have formidable biting pressure.
                        Any withering bite attack it makes ignores up to
                        four points of soak, plus an additional point of soak for
                        each extra success on the attack roll. This cannot reduce
                        an enemy’s soak below his (Stamina).
                        If you boost your accuracy on your attack, this is liable to reduce your opponent's soak by a considerable amount. They also seem to have a sideline in knocking prone (smashing snout) and dealing bonus Decisive damage but I'm not sure how useful this tactic would be given how easy it is to rise from prone, even with foes at close range (difficulty 2).

                        Killing Strike: The boar-tusk crocodile takes down prey
                        by ramming them to the ground, then biting at their
                        exposed vitals. Any decisive bite attack it makes against
                        a prone enemy adds +5 raw damage
                        I'd probably just stick to the damaging, soak bypassing bite and very decent soak to see me through fights.

                        The Boar Tusk Crocodile has no specific defense merit or latents, and you'll have to depend on base parry/dodge pool and whatever Lunar Charms you have, which I would advise investing in.

                        Gorilla:

                        As mentioned (not by me) Gorillas can roll Intimidate to Join Battle (so should be good for Cha Lunars),

                        Howling Aggression: A gorilla may Join Battle with its
                        intimidation dice pool, attempting to frighten enemies
                        into fleeing by shaking large tree branches, beating its
                        chest, or baring fangs. It may take its turn on the first
                        round before that of any enemy whose Resolve was overcome
                        by the roll, regardless of its actual Initiative rating.
                        I'm not sure how it interacts with this universal latent though:
                        Predator’s Menace: Animals with an intimidate pool can
                        be trained with this attack, causing an intimidated enemy
                        to lose Initiative equal to the extra successes over his
                        Resolve, even if he pays Willpower to resist. Once per scene.
                        If the ability to join battle using Intimidation pool is actually a Threaten attempt, then all well and good, if not, we'll have to find some other way to threaten, maybe Poised Lion Attitude?

                        Gorillas are also great grapplers (if you are going the pure Dex route, no worries, you can just use their native 10 dice, 10 dice to control pool and this Latent to guarantee yourself at least 5 successes on the control roll)
                        Savaging Strength (Latent): A gorilla that has been trained
                        for combat can put its strength to use in denying enemies
                        escape. It may pay a point of Willpower to convert up to five
                        dice on the control roll of a clinch to automatic successes.
                        This latent:

                        Crushing Embrace: Large predators that rely on clinching
                        can be trained to make a decisive savaging attack upon
                        grappling an enemy, with raw damage equal to the extra
                        successes on control roll. This attack ignores hardness
                        and does not reset the animal to base Initiative. Once
                        per scene.
                        and Mauling Bite:

                        Mauling Bite: When the gorilla makes a decisive bite
                        attack against a clinched enemy, it adds +4 to the raw
                        damage of this attack. It can receive this bonus only once
                        per clinch, unless reset by crashing the clinched enemy.
                        Will allow you to take two quite damaging decisives on any grappled target.

                        The Gorilla has no specific defense merit or latents, and you'll have to depend on base parry/dodge pool and whatever Lunar Charms you have, which I would advise investing in. Worse, it's soak is 6/0, so you will definitely need your Moonsilver armor for this form.

                        Great Cat:

                        Great Cats are all about attacking from Stealth and moving into close range, attacking with their weaker claw attack (12 damage as opposed to 16 damage on the bite) and hoping for 5+ levels of Withering Damage and a free grapple, otherwise quite similar to Gorilla, sans the Intimidate shennanigans. (They even have mauling bite).

                        I dunno, I kind of like the straight forward nature of the gorilla's Intimidate - grapple - Crush - bite repeat strategy better, but thats just me.

                        Great cats actually have a defensive merit:

                        Battle Cat (Latent): Great cats in the wild rely on ambush
                        tactics to take down weaker prey, and generally avoid
                        animals capable of fighting back against them. With training,
                        they can be taught to defend against the tactics of
                        trained combatants, adding +1 Evasion against any attack
                        made by an enemy with lower Initiative and +1 Evasion
                        against any attack made from medium range or further.
                        These bonuses stack.
                        So if you're winning, you're going to be harder to hit, still at Soak of 6/0 and a situational EV boost, I would recommend Moonsilver armor.

                        Pestletail:

                        I've made my feelings on the Pestletail really clear, it is one of my favorite forms because it has the most damaging attack of non Kaiju forms and the most soak too. It also has 4 (!!) defensive soak oriented merits/latents which makes it a much more survivable form (good merits too):

                        Bony Shell: Withdrawing into its heavy shell affords a
                        pestletail extraordinary defense. It may take Full Defense
                        actions without losing any Initiative. In addition, Full
                        Defense actions add four to the pestletail’s soak and hardness
                        as well as increasing its Defense.
                        Impenetrable Armor: The minimum damage of any
                        withering attack made against the pestletail is reduced
                        by one die, to a minimum of zero.
                        Sturdy as Stone: A pestletail cannot be knocked back or
                        prone except by magical effects and by creatures of extraordinary
                        size, such as tyrant lizards. Similarly, a grappled
                        pestletail cannot be thrown or slammed unless magic
                        such as Dragon Coil Technique is used, or if the grappling
                        creature is gigantic.
                        Hardened Shell Superiority (Latent): Whenever a withering
                        attack against the pestletail fails to deal any damage,
                        the attacker loses a point of Initiative which is granted to
                        the beast. Whenever a decisive attack fails to damage it,
                        the pestletail gains an Initiative Break of 5 points
                        And you can pseudo Heaven Thunder Hammer with it's tail during Decisives for even more damage (falling damage is no joke in Exalted. This potentially, with the base Decisive damage, could one hit TKO even most Exalts)

                        Smashing Tail Hammer (Latent): The pestletail may pay
                        a point of Willpower before making a decisive tail slam
                        attack to grant the attack the benefits of the Solar Charm
                        Heaven Thunder Hammer if it hits. Using Smashing Tail
                        Hammer counts as making a smashing attack, and incurs
                        the additional Defense penalty for such.
                        Man, I love the Pestletail.

                        Well, thats it, I'm sure I missed something, but I'm feeling more confident about playing a Dex focused Shifter Lunar who maybe splashes Str for Grapple charms (or not if going Pestletail). If you want it to be your main combat style though, Latent purchase is probably necessary.

                        I'll pick up with other animals next post.

                                    So Charms for the Consumate Shifter (i.e. someone who wants to fight with those sweet, sweet animal form bonuses all the time.) I'm just going to focus on E1 charms as they are whats important for Character Creation.

                                    Universal

                                    Chimera Soul Expression: Add 6 more mutation points from another animal to your base spirit shape form. Not bad, not bad at all, though based on what you choose, you could stand out just a smidge.

                                    Hybrid Body Transformation: Useful for your human form, of course, but I would choose defensive or utility mutations as most of your combat will be done in critter form.

                                    Shifting Beast Nature: Eh, it's a separate pool of mutations for your HBT and you can switch between the as a misc action for free. Its not bad, if you have a concrete use in mind for it (i.e. talky HBT and Utility HBT)

                                    Many-Faced Moon Transformation: Is useful, for disguising, which every Lunar should be good at, not just Shifters.

                                    Quicksilver Second Face: Pure gold, you definitely want to take this. For 2i you get to shift into another form (for no motes, it seems) at any point on your turn. Attack in your bitey form and then transform into your defensive form (Pestletail! Death Kitty!) for massive evasion/soak before you end your turn. A must have pick.

                                    Dexterity

                                    Shapeshifting:

                                    Heart's Blood, Nest Raiding Slyness: The only Heart's Blood Option that works on animals previewed thus far. For all you animal lovers out there, you don't have to kill them, you just have to be a massive jerk. Woohoo! (Probably pretty good)

                                    Emerald Grasshopper Form: To turn into a literal fly on the wall. You aren't going to kill anybody in this form, but if there was a charm for Perfect Spying, this would be it.

                                    Offense:

                                    Finding the Needle's Eye: A bit situational, but will be very nice if your foe is behind partial cover (or even full cover!)

                                    Coyote-And-Badger Technique: Depends, do you want to kick ass yourself, if so this is not so great, or do you want to set up your friend's Decisive? Even so, I'm not too fond for this build.

                                    Weapon Snatching Coils: Disarm is a pretty good tactic against certain Exalted foes, and if you are a Pestletail, this becomes super good to use (you basically get to toss the weapon really far away reflexively)

                                    Striking Mospid Method: A very good bread and butter to reroll 3 failed dice on a withering attack making your bite super accurate.

                                    Foe Driving Attack: Again another accuracy booster, this time by increasing onslaught penalty. Not too expensive either.

                                    Many-Armed Warrior Panoply, Needle Quill Technique, Bombardier Spittle Alchemy, Stinging Ichneumon Scourge: Actually super bad for you, animal forms don't use weapons.

                                    Sinuous Striking Grace: Pseudo +5 (based on max Dex) to your Init to determine who goes first, if you hit an enemy who hasn't gone yet +1 non charm dice to accuracy. Not bad, but take Striking Mospid Method first. Go-first is of course a powerful tactic.

                                    Cunning Beast Warrior Reflexes: The Flurry better charm (only -2 penalty on both actions, no defense penalty). I am trying to think what would be a good action to flurry with attacks in general and am drawing a blank, anyone want to enlighten me? Otherwise it is situational.

                                    Wasp Sting Blur: If your foe is suffering a bunch of Onslaught, you can really make them hurt with this charm. A bit situational as there haven't been any multi-attack charms as of yet.

                                    Defense:

                                    Agile Beast Defence: Bread and butter defensive charm and it's great! Especially if you become Death Kitty at the end of your turn. With this charm and a 4m expenditure to boost your Evasion, you can end up with a 12 EV (if you maxed Dodge).

                                    Bending Before the Storm: Here is your onslaught negator if you are Higher Init than your foes. Its not bad, and quite early on in the defense tree. If you don't have higher init, its still quite good.

                                    Ferocious Guardian Beast Defence: Makes you very good at defend another actions (which are reflexive now) Probably a must pick, especially if you have either 12 EV (Death Kitty) or 22 Soak (Pestletail with Moonsilver Armor).

                                    Golden Tiger Stance: Ooh, potentially 13 EV Death Kitty! I like it.

                                    Coiled Serpent Strikes: A reflexive Decisive counterattack on a miss is not bad. I'll have to think about how best to build up the Init quickly, Full Moons have that 1/day anima ability if you want to Decisive twice in a turn.

                                    Edit: Not done yet, but will probably put the rest in another post.

                                        These five charms and a good Heart's Blood library (I'd try at least to have the Pestletail, the Cat and the Boar toothed crocodile) will probably make you quite a fearsome combatant even with such a minimal investment in Dex charms.

                                        Str might have it's own awesome E1 charms, but it is not a given that Shifters have good strength as it doesn't really play into damage calculation. Sta might see more play.
