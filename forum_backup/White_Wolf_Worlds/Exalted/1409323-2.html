    I think the sidebar on page 58 of Lords of Creation.



    Charms running from Essence 1 to 5 are detailed
    on page 184 of Exalted.
    Charms having Essence minimums from 1 to 5
    encompass the complete array of effects for which the
    Solars were given purpose. Having conquered chaos
    and ascended to rulership of Creation, Solars expand
    to represent a purpose for which there was no design.
    Charms beyond the Essence minimum of 5 suggest
    a force for which there is no peer, and a measure for
    which there is no call. In short, these effects equip the
    Solars with a new array of effects of such magnitude and
    scope that there could be no known reason. Beyond
    this point, Solars transcend humanity, expand their
    notice beyond the core of existence, bargain with
    incomprehensible forces, and build new worlds—if
    only to have more to conquer.
    Charms with an Essence minimum of 6 leave be-
    hind worldly miracles—the Exalt now engages in the
    work of gods. Her power redefines her standing among
    the Exalted, and it is on the back of these wonders that
    an Age may be built.
    Charms with an Essence minimum of 7 depict
    the authorial primacy of one who knows no value she
    cannot change and no limit she cannot reach. These
    miracles leave marks on the world that last for untold
    generations, each application a legend unto itself. The
    holy might contained by these Charms evoke such awe
    and terror that the Celestial Exalted look upon them
    with shocked remove.
    Charms with an Essence minimum of 8 touch the
    very foundation of the universe. The Solar lays hands
    upon the forces of the very cosmos, and they recognize
    in her Essence a peer.
    Charms with an Essence minimum of 9 affect all
    the heavenly glory of the sun unbound. Solar power
    expands almost beyond the capacity of the world to
    contain it. What is the possible purpose of such great
    and terrible power?
    Charms with an Essence minimum of 10 denote
    powers beyond those which forged the universe. Not
    even the Primordials fully understand Essence 10; at
    this level of effect, the intellects which defined ex-
    istence find themselves in the position of discovery.
    What challenges even the Primordials, a Solar may
    come naturally to grasp and engender: power beyond
    all reason.


    Which to me is like this;

    DotFA: Essence 5 is the essence of the greatest heroes of the world. Essence 5 is what you need to do anything. Essence six is beyond that. As Essence increases past six the world itself and everything in it becomes nothing to you.

    Me: Okay so what that that mean?

    DotFA: Yeah what DOES it mean?

    Me: No, I mean like, how do I ST for a game with Essence 10 PCs that are beyond even Primordials?

    DotFA: Now THAT is an intriguing question, isn’t it?

    Me: You have even a hint of an answer?

    DotFA: What do you think I am?

    Me: An RPG rules/setting book- wait, hang on, aren’t the Deathlords Essence 10 with all solar charms that they meet the minimums for and more?

    DotFA: correct.

    Me: So in the second age, where most games are set, there’s thirteen dudes walking around who are greater than the primordials, and to whom the entire rest of the world is pathetically, meaninglessly weak?

    DotFA: Indeed, the Deathlords possess powers FAR beyond the capacity for anyone or anything to challenge them, even remotely. Except for people like Chejop Kejak, he can.

    Me: So when the 2e corebook promised that the returning Solars, who are Essence 2-5 at best, would either destroy the world or save it that was...?

    DotFA: A total lie, yes. Players like Kejak and the First and Forsaken Lion treat entire directions of Creation as pawns in their chess game.

    Me: Please tell me how you expect me to play this and use the setting info as a Storyteller.

    DotFA: Okay, okay, I’ll give you a little hint, because I’m nice not because it’s my job. You know those tens of thousands of words you’ve read about the setting? About the gods, elementals, spirits, kingdoms like Paragon, Whitewall, Nexus, and Coral?

    Me: Okay yeah?

    DotFA: Treat that like we told you the intricate social networks and habits of primary school children. If you want a place like Nexus or Whitewall to matter, you can say an Essence 10 Solar survivor from the first age lives there, that’ll make it important.

    Me: So a primordial is just hiding out in any city I want to mean anything.

    DotFA: Not a primordial, greater than a primordial. He or she is just hiding out there for some reason which you’ll have to think up yourself.

    Me: This is dumb.


    And ultimately that kind of thinking is what lead especially late 2e into being a game where nothing but Exalted mattered, and nothing but Solars REALLY mattered, especially ones who started at Essence 5 with Divine Transcendence of (Ability) and that other cap bursted from Glories. It made for a ton of frustration, I’m glad it’s over, and I hope it never comes back.
