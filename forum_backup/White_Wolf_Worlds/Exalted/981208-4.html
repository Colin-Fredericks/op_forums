    Originally posted by Erinys View Post
    Again, I'm not talking about Lunars making ancient artifact designs, I'm talking about them owning none at all.
    Lookshy and the Realm are both depicted as owning huge arsenals of powerful First Age artifacts, both warships and personal panoply, while the Lunars (depending on the book) either own absolutely zero, or own some but are never ever allowed to use them. That is, Lunars are in the exact same position as Solars, for no damn reason.

    I feel like people are talking past me and not seeing what I type, because the posts I see keep replying to a situation that... has no resemblance to what I've seen and read or asked about.
    Lookshy and the Realm had unified places to stash their stuff (and the size of their stashes is up for debate, since Lookshy is only a single city state and some redoubts, and anything they have has to fit in those limited spaces). The Lunars did not. The Lunars have been the #1 target of the world for thirteen to fifteen centuries. Whatever they brought along could not have lasted for very many years of that exile, so at present, they don't have massive, unified stores of First Age wonders.

    You don't get the benefits of squatting on the Solars' old empire if you're not squatting on the Solars' old empire. And once someone is squatting it, they can obliterate anyone who challenges them with relative ease until the lights go out.

                                                    I think they ought to have outright magical infrastructure centers, and I'll tell you why - if only to be critical of the 'rubbing rocks together and singing to trees' cliche again.

                                                    We all have heard the line - 'The Lunar Exalted don't build up a city filled with artisans because the Wyld Hunt will come crush it!'

                                                    Let's examine that.

                                                    The Wyld Hunt hasn't crushed Mahalanka. It hasn't crushed the Nameless Lair. It hasn't crushed Luthe. All of these locations are well known, even infamous.

                                                    So let's point out something that's already implicit in the text - the Wyld Hunt cannot simply be everywhere. Many, many places in the world are simply too much of a pain in the ass to make it worthwhile to project force there. And it's ALWAYS been this way, because several of the aforementioned locations existed even during the heyday of the Wyld Hunt! So that justification for why Lunar Exalted don't create artifacts of that type doesn't really hold water.

                                                    So we know that the Lunar Host in practical terms can, and does, secure territory and cities. Some were claimed post-usurpation and never unclaimed. And do you know what they do with them?

                                                    Let them fall into ruin, of course!

                                                    Every single one! Without fail!

                                                    Because, of COURSE that's what people who were culturally part of the first age for thousands of years... brought up in a technomagical utopia, and trained and educated in all manner of sciences... would do! Throw on the animal skins, start a buncha fires, and break all the artifacts! HOWL AT THE MOON! Right?

                                                    I propose that in fact, if enterprising Lunar Exalted were interested, it wouldn't be at all difficult to find a place in the MASSIVE LANDMASS of Creation to start an entire civilization dedicated to the construction of magical artifacts. It might not be oceanside Realm property, mind, but it would not have a problem existing. Wyld Hunt kill teams would not arrive. They're more concerned with the lower hanging fruit.

                                                    And further, I propose that this probably should have happened already. Somewhere in Creation, a Lunar Lookshy might/should exist, waiting for you to write it.
