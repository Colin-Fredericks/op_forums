                                                05-15-2018, 05:00 PM
                                                Originally posted by BrilliantRain View Post
                                                Do you guys ever define more specific "benchmarks" for the charms of a given Exalt in a given area? Like "Solar Melee, at Essence 3, can make X attacks, with Y bonus to Attack, with Z Bonus damage, for A motes, B Will, and C other costs." I get that there are lots of nuances, but I could imagine the benchmarks helping figure out what to shoot for.

                                                Or is it a more holistic approach?
                                                Doing that would be less useful, for two reasons:

                                                A) The benchmarks that apply to [Solar Combat Ability X] are not going to be the same as the ones that apply to [Solar Combat Ability Y].

                                                B) There are going to be places where, e.g., Lunars are going to dramatically outperform the equivalent Solar benchmarks, and that is not a bad or broken thing, because it's balanced out holistically.

                                                At the end of the day, any benchmark you can formulate with any particularity is going to be subject to so many exceptions as to approach uselessness.

                                                            One of the things that I've noticed about some artefacts, specifically Warstriders, is the sheer amount of Evocations gated behind certain Solar charms, which kind of force you into a certain build. On the other end of the spectrum, you've All Conquering Colossus (5 of its 10 charms require you invest in Solar Brawl) and Ascendant Nova Phoenix (8 of its 12 charms require investment in charms spread over three abilities).

Going forward, will Warstriders be more like to former end instead of the latter?
