                            Updated Charm of the Weasel based on feedback. Pinnacle evocation may still need some work. For clarification this is not armor, it was based on the idea of an utilitarian artifact based on a hearthstone bracer.
                            Charm of the Weasel
                            ●●● Artifact
                            Origin
                            Many years ago a lunar craftsman became enamored with the humble weasel and the way this unique and fascinating predator worked. From his fascination came the charm of the weasel a trinket inspired by the weasel and a gift for the lunar craftsman's night caste lover.

                            Appearance
                            This artifact takes the form of a moonsilver armband shaped as a coiled weasel wrapping around the wearers arm. The artifact has a single hearthstone socket between the front claws of the weasel making it appear as if the the artifact is holding onto the hearthstone. Additionally the artifacts eyes change color to mimic any hearthstone that is inserted into it.
                            Attunement
                            Cost: 2m
                            As with most hunters the weasel can see its prey long before the prey see them any exalt attuned to the charm of the weasel gain +1 on all visual awareness checks and have no difficulty seeing in anything other than pure darkness.
                            Evocations:
                            Prey of the Weasel
                            Cost: 2m
                            Mins: Essence 1
                            Type: Reflexive
                            Duration: One Scene
                            Prerequisite Evocation: None
                            One Line Summary: Once the Exalt locates her quarry she chases it until it is hers
                            The weasel is a predator that shuns the tactic of stalk and ambush in favor of flush and chase. The exalt may reflexively activate this evocation to mark her prey, be it a person, animal, or item. Once the prey has been mark the exalt will chase it down till it’s hers. The exalt gets +1 non-charm bonus on all tracking rolls. Should the exalt locate their target while this evocation is active they receive +3 initiative a join battle action to attack her target or +1 non-charm bonus to any larceny action to acquire her target.
                            Special rules: The exalt must see, hear, or smell her prey to activate this evocation and the sense used will determine the difficulty of tracking. Also the exalt must continue tracking until she finds her prey, if she loses the trail or gives up the evocation ends and the prey is no longer marked.
                            Bones of Rubber
                            Cost: 5m
                            Mins: Essence 2
                            Type: Reflexive
                            Duration: Instant
                            Prerequisite Evocation: Prey of the Weasel
                            One Line Summary: Allows the exalt to squeeze through any opening that they can get their head through
                            The weasel is a predator that’s path cannot easily be barred. This evocation allows the exalt to channel the ability of the weasel to fit through any opening which they can get their head through. One this evocation is active should the exalt be able to move her head through an opening her body flows and adjusts to make it through the space as well.
                            Changing one’s Coat
                            Cost: 5m, 1wp
                            Mins: Essence 3
                            Type: Simple
                            Duration: Indefinite
                            Prerequisite Evocation: Bones of Rubber
                            One Line Summary: Allows the Exalt to change her outward appearance to blend in with her surroundings
                            Just as the weasel changes its coat from brown to white so can the exalted. An exalt using this evocation can change their outward appearance to blend in with the scenery. This evocation allows the exalt to change the color, texture, and feel of their clothing. Additionally the exalt can affect their skin and hair as well changing them to blend with their surroundings. These effects provide for +1 non-charm bonus to all stealth checks when not moving Once the commitment to this evocations lapses the exalts appearance returns to normal

                            Special rules: Should the charm of the weasel have the chameleon stone socketed into it the following additional effects are available. The evocation allows access to the hearthstones innate ability and expands upon it. The exalt may now change not only their hair, eye, and skin color but also their clothing and equipment to blend into any situation. With the chameleon stone the exalt receives +1 non-charm bonus to all stealth checks and larceny checks for disguise.

                                So this might be cheating a bit as I am a player in Binary Phantoms Game and know the player and character he wants this for but here is my attempt at a 4 dot grand goremaul. If anyone has seen log horizon I took inspiration for two evocations form there and the last one is obviously a reference to x-men. I'd appreciate any feedback on balancing this or ideas on fixing them if they are broken.
                                Dissuaders Bulwark
                                ●●●● Artifact: Orialchum and White Jade Grand Goremaul
                                Origin
                                It is a known fact that when you start an ambitious project things don’t always turn out the way one would expect. Such is the case with Dissuaders Bulwark. In the high first age a Twilight artisan was commissioned by his circles Dawn to fashion him an impregnable shield. The twilight craftsmen at once started gathering materials from across creation, Shells from great sea beasts, Shards of the hardest crystal and white jade from the pole of earth itself. The craftsman tooled tireless, working day and night only taking a break during the hours of the early morning when the pure sunlight washed over his creation. When all was said and done the twilight was impressed with his work the finest he’d ever done and presented it to the dawn but there was one issue. In his creative stupor the Artifact he created was not the shield requested but a Grand Goremaul. While taken back on the item put before her the dawn was in awe at the majesty and strength emanating from the weapon. Taking it in hand she could feel this weapon would protect her better than any shield and it served her well in many battles. Had Dissuaders Bulwark been with her when the hands of fate closed in on her circle during the usurpation her attackers may not have faired as well, but alas now Dissuader sleeps awaiting another soul to protect
                                Appearance
                                Dissuaders Bulwark is a mighty Grand Goremaul Standing six feet high with a head two feet long and a foot in diameter. The weapon is an alloy of Orialchum and White Jade with a shaft wrapped in the hide of a long dead Behemoth and the pommel adorned in the shells of tortoises that call the pole of fire home. The head of Dissuaders Bulwark is fashioned of a castle atop a giant turtle with the head of the turtle agape with a giant rock in its mouth for the hammerhead and the tail as the spike. Where one would expect the eyes of the turtle two hearthstone sockets appear.
                                Attunement
                                Cost: 2m Any exalt who attunes to Dissuaders Bulwark for an additional two motes gain the defense benefit as if it was a medium Artifact Melee weapon and can add +1 to their parry
                                Cost 6m: Any exalt who attunes to Dissuaders Bulwark for an additional six motes may take Defend Other actions at short range
                                Evocations:
                                Anchor Howl
                                Cost: 4m, 2i
                                Mins: Essence 2
                                Keywords: Psyche
                                Type: Reflexive
                                Duration: 2 combat actions
                                Prerequisite Evocation: None
                                One Line Summary: The exalt who uses this evocation paints themselves as the target to anyone within long range and must be attacked or the attacker takes a penalty
                                To protect those around themselves the exalt must sometime draw all attention on herself. Upon activation the exalts anima flashes to burning level drawing all eyes to her. This charm interacts with the exalts anima making it impossible to ignore her must attack her. Choosing to ignore the exalt and attack someone else incurs a 4 initiative penalty to the attacker. This charm lasts for three combat rounds and cannot be used again for 4 combat actions.

                                Keystone of the Wall
                                Cost: 6m
                                Mins:Essence 3
                                Keywords: None
                                Type: Simple
                                Duration: One Scene
                                Prerequisite Evocation: Anchor Howl
                                One Line Summary: Slamming the ground the exalt sends ripples through the ground raising stone and earthen walls creating a funnel for all enemy combatants to the exalt
                                Sometimes drawing attention to yourself is not enough and you must defend your allies. Upon activating this charm the exalt channels her essence into Dissuaders Bulwark and brings it down upon the ground in front of her. From the Point of impact fissures expand out and two walls of earth and stone erupt from the ground. Each wall is approximately 10 ft tall of sharp ragged stone and earth and should be treated as an environmental hazard of 1L/round difficulty 5 to move through. Each wall extends for approximately 3 range bands at 60 degrees from the exalt and leaves a 10 ft wide gap between them where the exalt stands.

                                Castle of Stone
                                Cost: 3m, 6i
                                Mins: Essence 3
                                Keywords: None
                                Type: Reflexive
                                Duration: Until next action
                                Prerequisite Evocation: Keystone of the Wall
                                One Line Summary: Once activated this evocation makes the exalt impervious to most damage but roots the exalt in place until his next round of combat
                                In the heat of combat it is sometimes necessary to find a moment to catch one's breath. When Caste of Stone is activated the exalt is routed in place and the her anima wraps around her making a protective shell. The exalt cannot move or be moved until her next action. She is treated as having a hardness of 20 and a soak of 20 till her next turn.
                                Juggernaut
                                Cost: 10m, 1wp, 4i
                                Mins: Essence 3
                                Keywords: Perilous
                                Type: Simple
                                Duration: One Action
                                Prerequisite Evocation: Guardian Wall
                                One Line Summary: The exalt becomes a moving wall, nearly impervious to damage and unstoppable

                                Sometimes defense is not enough and you must show the world why they should have been dissuaded in the first place. Upon activation of this charm the exalts essence infuses directly into her skin giving it a reddish brown color. Until her next action the exalt has the following stats Soak 20, Hardness 20, Strength 6, and Athletics 6. This charm can only be used once per combat.

                                        So I figured out a concept, but I don't know how to finish it off. Any advice would be greatly appreciated.

                                        Ring of Power (Artifact 3-N/A)
                                        Any who take hold of one of these rings automatically gains an intimacy of a strength defined by the power of the ring which endures as long as the ring is in their keeping. Artifact 3 rings bestow a minor intimacy, 4 dot a moderate, 5 dot a major, and N/A a defining. These intimacies are selected from among those of the ring maker at the appropriate strength at the time of its creation and cannot be changed. In all respects they are treated as a natural intimacy, not as a form of mind control or external influence. To the bearer they seem natural, though those radically opposed to the character of the bearer or which clash with an existing intimacy of equal or greater strength may be resisted. This occurs even if the artifact is not attuned.

                                        Each ring contains a number of evocations equal to their artifact level, the first of which is always available to a bearer even if they are not attuned. Any evocations past the first require attunement.

                                        The primary power of a ring is to allow attuned bearers to perform a number of sorcerous workings without exhausting their own energies in the process. To that end, each contains a reservoir of experience points which may be used for no other purpose and which can only be used in ways appropriate to the intimacy the ring imposes. Thus a ring dedicated to amassing wealth could assist in workings which caused gold to literally multiply or traders to always give the bearer better deals but could not be used to cause a river to run clean despite defilement upstream. Each ring contains a number of experience points equal to its artifact rating times 10. When performing such a working, the attuned bearer may use the dicepool of the creator or their own, whichever is greater This power requires attunement.

                                        Finally, rings grant power according to the nature of their bearers. It allows users to perform workings up to the level of sorcery they can natively learn but only for workings which draw from the xp of the ring and motivated by its intimacy. Mortals are thus limited to terrestrial works, gods to celestial, etc. This power requires attunement.


                                        The workings performed with the power of a ring are somewhat more fragile than normal and can be undone. Should a ring be destroyed they will immediately end, and if a new user becomes attuned they may unravel the workings of previous bearers.

                                            This is my first time making stuff for Ex3, so I have no idea what I'm doing. The idea behind it is to have a weapon that follows Wuxing principles of Overcoming and Generating, but in Creation's terms. Inspired, partly, by the Sword of Gou Jian, which is surprisingly small.
                                            The Imperial Daiklave, the Five-Jade Blade (皇大劍,五玉刃)
                                            Jibei is known as the City of the Sword, and has been known that for ages. The title has always been ceremonial and metaphorical, used to denote the metropolis’ martial arts and culture. Always, that is, until the time of Sword Prince Gensuji and the Imperial Daiklave.

                                            Also known as the Five-Jade Blade, the Imperial Daiklave was forged at the behest of the Scarlet Empress, a commission of great care and meaning. As Jibei was one of the first cities that would bend knee to her glorious regime, the weapon is a symbol of unity, brotherhood, completion, wisdom, understanding, and universal harmony.

                                            Five feet long and four inches wide, the Five-Jade Blade is a simple no-frills straight-sword forged the style common in the Spring-And-Autumn Period of the Shogunate. The crossguard is shining steel, etched with a stylized dragon’s face. The hilt - also of steel - is wrapped in wire, and carved into the base of the pommel is a pentacle. The blade itself is an alloy of equal parts red, black, blue, green, black, and white jade. Each jade is an intricate trace of lines in the metal, leaving the blade a brilliant blaze of multicolor. Stamped into one side of the blade, near the base, are five characters, the names of the elements in Old Realm. On the other side are another eight words, also in Old Realm; “Sword Emperor, Five Jades, For Sword Prince’s Use.”

                                            Legend has it that the Imperial Daiklave can, in the right hands, awaken to any and all elemental evocations, possibly duplicating the powers of such storied weapons as Spring Razor, Shining Ice Mirror, or even the Volcano Cutter. But for now, the Imperial Daiklave’s only known traits are to use one element to encourage or destroy another, in accordance with the elemental cycles.

                                            The Imperial Daiklave has two hearthstone sockets, both in the crossguard’s “eyes.”

                                            Evocations of the Imperial Daiklave
                                            Solars and Dragon-Blooded who attune to the Imperial Daiklave awaken two evocations for free from both the Harmonious or Impeded trees, and may pay 1 willpower to reset all Imperial Daiklave evocations; Dragon-Blooded may also pay 5 motes to reset any one of the Imperial Daiklave’s evocations. Other exalts awaken one evocation for free.
                                            All free evocations are based on caste/aspect associations as a default, such as Air Aspects taking ‘Air Freezes Water’ or No Moon Lunars taking ‘Water Freezes Wood’. However, characters may take others if the Storyteller accepts player arguments to the contrary.

                                                MARVELOUS CLOCKWORK BRACERS
                                                The work of the crafting-god Vincible Coil, these paired armbands are a wonderland of interlocking gears and pulleys in alchemically reinforced brass and bronze. Designed to cover the entire forearm from wrist to elbow, and to be easily concealed beneath any but the tightest sleeves, their undersides hide razored blades within retractable armatures. Spring-loaded to deploy with lethal force in an instant, and with essence-triggered hinges that can turn them to be used as ordinary knives, they put the tools of murder instantly at their wearer's hand. Each bracer also has a hearthstone socket, built into its outer surfaces near the elbow.

                                                Evocations of the Bracers
                                                When attuned and worn, the bracers' wearer may spend 4m to activate their blades trigger mechanisms, gaining the ability to deploy and retract them reflexively for one scene. The blades are light artifact weapons with the Lethal, Worn, and Concealable tags, and the user may choose from one deployment to the next between the Brawl OR Melee tags. They may be used as style weapons for any Martial Arts style using knives or katars.

                                                Grapple Launcher
                                                Cost: 2m Mins: Essence 1
                                                Type: Simple
                                                Keywords: Archery
                                                Duration: Instant (Indefinite)
                                                Prerequisites: None

                                                This evocation adds a spring-loaded launcher to the Bracers which is capable of propelling a dart and line out to Medium range. The head of the dart is enchanted to bond with inanimate material and is incapable of interacting with living flesh, but will flawlessly anchor itself to a living tree or all common architectural substances, as well as to clothing or armor. The line may be severed by any lethal attack, but is otherwise sturdy enough to bear the weight of a single person.

                                                Switching Brackets
                                                Cost: 1m Mins: Essence 2
                                                Type: Reflexive
                                                Keywords: -
                                                Duration: Instant
                                                Prerequisites: Grapple Launcher

                                                This evocation modifies the mountings of the Bracers' grapples and blades to the same standard, and adds a mechanism to allow them to be swapped. Using it, their wearer may fire the blades as light artifact Archery weapons with the Crossbow tag in place of Grapple Launcher's usual anchoring effect, or anchor herself to a given surface for as long as she chooses without requiring the usual standards of athletics and endurance.

                                                Feathered Winch
                                                Cost: 3m Mins: Essence 2
                                                Type: Reflexive
                                                Keywords:
                                                Duration: Instant (Scene)
                                                Prerequisites: Switching Brackets

                                                This evocation gives the Bracers' master finer control over the powered winch that retracts the line fired by Grapple Launcher, allowing her to automatically set it at a certain desired length, and to play them in and out at will for the rest of the scene. A wielder trained in the use of the rope dart, fighting chain, garrote, sling, or other appropriately flexible weapon may use the resulting weighted lines as such.

                                                Automatic Magazine
                                                Cost: - Mins: Essence 2
                                                Type: Permanent
                                                Keywords:
                                                Duration: -
                                                Prerequisites: Switching Brackets

                                                This evocation adds a ring of additional mounting slots to the Bracers, allowing the Switching Brackets to load, and the Grapple Launcher and deployment gears to fire, any suitable small item, attachment, or alternate blade that the Bracers' owner can fit into the slots.

                                                Redundant Controls
                                                Cost: 6m Mins: Essence 3
                                                Type: Supplemental
                                                Keywords: Mute
                                                Duration: Instant
                                                Prerequisites: Feathered Winch, Automatic Magazine

                                                As originally designed, the Marvelous Clockwork Bracers could only accept one stream of essence from their wearer at a time, serving as both power source and control mechanism. This evocation modifies them so that each bracer can accept its own, separate stream, letting them be used in concert and allowing the wearer to take two actions involving the Bracers (Any of firing a grapple, making a withering attack, or making a decisive attack, resolved in that order). Double decisive attacks add an additional instance of the wielder's base initiative to their initiative pool, then divide their initiative evenly between the two simultaneous attacks.

                                                            Faekiller (Orichalcum and Starmetal Powerbow - Artifact ***)

                                                            The Fair Folk have ever been a threat to Creation, for it stands for all that they hate: stability, definition, reality. The Exalted of the First Age knew this and forged numerous weapons to do battle with the shapeless hordes beyond Creation's rim. One of the least of these weapons is Faekiller, an orichalcum powerbow forged into the shape of a roaring lion's mouth, with fangs of starmetal and starmetal filaments running through the bowstring. Faekiller was wielded by Night's Prurient Scythe, an Eclipse Caste of the high First Age, and was placed in his tomb during the Usurpation. When the Fair Folk invaded Creation, a daring Sidereal whose name has never been successfully recorded broke into the tomb, daring the anger of the Solar's hungry ghost, and retrieved the bow. Where it has gone since that time, no one knows.

                                                            Evocations of Faekiller
                                                            When a Solar or Sidereal attunes to Faekiller, its attacks deal aggravated damage to Fair Folk. Note that this does not include Wyld mutants, but does include hobgoblins. For an additional 2m commitment, the bow deals aggravated damage to anyone it strikes.

                                                            Subverting the Pretense
                                                            Cost: Varies; Mins: Essence 1
                                                            Type: Reflexive
                                                            Keywords: None
                                                            Duration: Instant
                                                            Prerequisites: None

                                                            The magic of the Fair Folk is nothing but dreams and whispers, no match for the harsh truth of reality. This Evocation may be activated when the wielder makes an attack against a target and that target uses Fair Folk defensive magic. The wielder may pay motes equal to the activation cost of the defensive magic to nullify that magic against this attack. If the magic has a duration greater than Instant, it functions normally against further attacks unless the wielder of Faekiller again pays the mote cost to nullify it.


                                                            Ignoring your Imagination
                                                            Cost: 6m; Mins: Essence 2
                                                            Type: Supplemental
                                                            Keywords: Dual
                                                            Duration: Instant
                                                            Prerequisites: Subverting the Pretense

                                                            It is not enough to pretend to have armor or weapons. Against Faekiller, you must actually have them. An attack supplemented by this Evocation ignores all soak and Hardness from gossamer armor. Against Fair Folk, it is also unblockable by gossamer weapons.


                                                            Fate Becomes You
                                                            Cost: 5m (+1W); Mins: Essence 2
                                                            Type: Supplemental
                                                            Keywords: Uniform
                                                            Duration: Instant (One Scene)
                                                            Prerequisites: Subverting the Pretense

                                                            This Evocation represents the ultimate weapon against Fair Folk: Fate itself. When an attack supplemented by this Evocation hits a Fair Folk, that Fair Folk is temporarily hauled inside Fate and given a destiny. That destiny is almost always death at the hands of Faekiller's wielder, and so is very short. For the remainder of the scene, the wielder of Faekiller re-rolls 6s on Archery attacks made with Faekiller against that Fair Folk until 6s no longer appear. For an additional Willpower cost, that same destiny (and its resultant re-rolls) can be applied to any target.
