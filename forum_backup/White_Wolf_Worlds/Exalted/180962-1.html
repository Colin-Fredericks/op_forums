            I don't know if the Infernal Charmset will,* but that's a common trend from what's been said of Abyssals.
            Originally posted by EX3 What We Know Wiki
            Q: Zakkeg
            From what I gather, it seems like a lot of [Abyssals'] foundational charmset will be functionally equivalent to what Solars get; it doesn't start really diverging until you go a few Charms up the tree.

            A: John Mørke
            Some of their Charm trees will start off crazy different. My hope is to provide a lot of Charms that feel like mirrors without being mirrors.
            There will be some exact copies, though.
            Originally posted by John Mørke
            1) Hungry Tiger Technique. Abyssals will have an identical mechanic. Some Charms just don't get any better.
            2) It is stylistically accurate to depict the Abyssals as having deep connections to the Solars. EX3 Abyssals are radically different than they have been in previous editions. Having some identical Charms to Solars is a benefit, rather than a subtraction, because it emphasizes the fact that they were / are Solars, while also making them something else.
            * It would certainly make sense, I just think it's too early to say. There can be different ways to build a Charmset, even if trying to be evocative of another.

            Off-topic Infernal design speculation, ho!
            From comments, EX3 Infernals seem meant to evoke the decadent sorcerer-king Solars of the First Age. For an alternative setup to Abyssals, I could see some Infernal roots looking like Charms that Solars get higher up in their trees, and then going off to odder places. These versions might be weaker, more constrained, less flexible or the like (or might not), but would evoke the sort of casual power and heights that Infernals get to fling around.

            Given what we've been told about Solar Charmtrees being generally designed in a sort of helix shape, it might also emphasize that simply having "more advanced" Charms doesn't mean as much on its own; in the Solar set, some such later Charms depend on being used with Charms earlier in the set to get the most out of them. Giving Infernals identical mechanics to those higher Solar Charms (albeit with lower prereqs) might work fine, and also subtly point to the idea of decadence (they're removed from more human fundamentals).

            Then again, the Infernals preview also showed mock-ups for a group of effects, of which the first (don't remember if it was prereq-free) amounted to "become the Silent Wind." I doubt they'll just straight-up diverge, but we'll see.

                            LARCENY

                            Borrowed Years Masque

                            Meditating on the timelessness of death, the Abyssal draws an advance on her limitless existence. While motes are committed to this Charm, the Abyssal may apply additional years to her natural appearance, and can become up to 50 years older than they normally are, allowing her to appear as an older mortal. This Charm automatically upgrades with each dot of Essence gained, allowing the Exalt to raise her age an additional 50 years per dot. After 100 applied years the Exalt may choose to appear as a walking corpse (gaining the Hideous tag(?)), and after 150 applied years the Exalt may appear as a living skeleton. This Charm does not affect physical attributes.
