    Lunars: Castes or Aspects

    01-05-2014, 01:06 PM
    Originally posted by BrilliantRain View Post
    I wonder if that means Lunars should have Aspects instead of Castes?
    Originally posted by John Mørke View Post

    I would like to see this discussed in its own thread. It sounds extremely promising.
    Per request of the Dev, this question has it's own thread.

    To give a bit of context, about here in the Create a Lunar Trio thread, there was some discussion as to whether the Lunar castes were at all useful. People were observing that most castes give you a general role and the capability to carry out the tasks of that role, be they mental, physical, or social. The Lunar castes however, just give you the ability to carry out any task associated with one type of attributes.

    This sounded a bit more Aspect-like to me, so I asked the question.


    Anyway, give us your thoughts on the following two questions in this thread:

    1) What's the difference between Castes and Aspects?

    and

    2) Should Lunars have Aspects instead of Castes?

          Castes are limited synergies of splat powers that are aimed towards a specific goal.
          Aspects are different expressions of power within the same splat. In the case of Dragon-Blooded, Aspect and Favoured Abilities are secondary to the elemental powers of individual charms. For the moment, I expect Liminals to work the same way.

          On a design level, you could have Lunar Aspects based on their Totems and have the totems apply differently to each charm. However, it would be impossible to accomplish in practice, as there would be far too many Totems to account for.

                  It is a good damn question.

                  1. Aspects are just what we called Terrestrial Castes, because functionally they are identical. This... should change. There is no reason to give Terrestrials castes and call them something else. Castes for Abilities, Aspects for Attributes? I like this, as it seems to relate to every Lunar being an aspect of the ever-changing Luna, if only rhetorically. Calling them Aspects might adapt our thinking. Others have proposed eliminating Lunar Castes- if instead folks were arranging their characters under Luna's aspects (Argent Madonna, Huntess, etc) it might break Lunars from Ass-Beater, Smart-Guy, Manipulator roles. Huntress might be Dexterity, Stamina, Perception, for example.

                  2. Hell if I know? I think that Lunars need to change. I think that Alchemicals demonstrate that Attribute-based exalts can operate very well. I think removing Lunars from three areas of over-specialization will only do them good. Castes/aspects can be viewed as shackles, they can also be archetypes. I think they need to be re-arranged a bit. A part of me thinks they should have no aspects/Castes. Another part thinks they should represent some archetype derived from Luna's many faces.

                              Castes, by contrast, tend to be archetypal. They present a task that an Exalt excels. Or in the case of Sidereals, the most "Aspect-like" due to soem of their Charms, favor a suite of Charms that allow them to accomplish certain roles.

                                              The way I think of it is that Aspects reflect something inborn and bodily, while Castes reflect something about your personality and the role your patron deity imagines you serving. In the case of Lunar Castes you can replace "patron deity" with "the Lunars who changed the castes", I guess. It feels to me like Aspect is something that bleeds into your nature unbidden, whereas Caste is more the result of an Incarna saying, "My Chosen should be people like this." (Caste also implies a social division during the Primordial War and First Age, but Lunars are special since they changed theirs, and since the division of labour was never as rigid as the term implies, I'm not sure how different it would really be from "We're posting you to monitor the pelagials because you're a Water Aspect".)

                                              Now, I do think you could draw a meaningful role division within the lines of the Lunar Caste Attributes, but I have to agree that the game hasn't done so to date. As a result, I don't think it would really hurt Lunars (relative to their current form) to give them Aspects, but it's hard to say which option is best overall. Caste implies a prescriptive framework and hints at a broader social structure and division of labour. Done right it emphasizes the Lunars as a host. Aspects are more like character quirks -- they say something about where the character comes from and emphasize the Exalt as individual. Lunars are the Exalted most associated with individuality, so that could be a good thing.

                                              While I don't have a strong sense of which option is better, I think I prefer castes, because I feel the Lunars need what sense of cohesion the game can give them, and I prefer how it interacts with un-tattooed Lunars' "flexible subsplat" gimmick: I prefer the idea of Lunars wearing many hats to the idea of Lunars being many hats (though the latter is legit since we're talking about Lunars).
