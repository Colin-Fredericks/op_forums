                                                    #5578
                                                    10-26-2017, 11:58 AM
                                                    Originally posted by serGregness View Post
                                                    -Seasoned beast rider's approach goes out of its way to say that ordering their mount to take a ride-based movement action doesn't use the Solar's own move action. What are the practical implications of this? Should the Solar get what amounts to two move actions (one on their turn, one on the horse's)? Move, then dismount, then move?
                                                    You can't take two mounted move actions on a turn, but if you're unhorsed, you could take a move action to come close enough to mount and then move, or you could take a mounted move, dismount, and move on foot.

                                                    -With Supernal Lash Discipline, Immortal Charger's Gallop, and Speed-Fury Focus, you get +8 automatic successes to join battle (assuming a horse or simhata), but do these remain non-charm dice since technically all those charms are simply modifying the speed bonus?
                                                    Speed bonus remains non-Charm, even if modified by Charms.

                                                    -If Flashing Thunderbolt Steed, and Wind-Racing Essence Infusion are active, and you use Grizzled Cataphract's Way to use your Ride to join battle, do you get the + Essence automatic successes from Wind-Racing Essence Infusion, or is Join Battle not a valid 'ride-based command' despite being rolled with ride in this instance?
                                                    They wouldn't add to a Ride-based Join Battle.

                                                    -Can the bonus reflexive movement from Invincible Fury of the Dawn enable Impaling attacks? Likewise if you got a reflexive movement from a rush action after having moved towards the target on your last turn, could you then impale that target?
                                                    Yep.

                                                    -Actually, the rush thing reminds me of another question: Difficult Terrain is noted to need two movement actions to cross, but how does this interact with the reflexive movement from rushes and disengages?
                                                    It'd only count as one of the two.

                                                    -If you have a way of rolling Join Battle again mid-combat (like with Battle Path Ascendant) if you use Seasoned Beast Rider's Approach with it, does the mount's initiative get replaced, or added to?
                                                    Eh. Based on the way the Charm's written, I think it'd get replaced with the Solar's new total Initiative, but if a Storyteller wants to just add the result of the Solar's Join Battle roll to the mount's Initiative, I think that'd also be fine.

                                                    -How do Warstriders interact with Ride charms? Can Phantom Steed summon me a Warstrider-sized horse?
                                                    Sure, why not.

                                                    -When summoning a specific demon (as in Demon of the Second Circle), what happens if the spirit has already been bound by another sorcerer, or spirit-killed by an exalt? Busy signal? What does Octavian's voicemail sound like?
                                                    Demons that are already bound or non-existent obviously can't be summoned, but we haven't really gotten into the weeds of it because that's really not fun in play. I'd assume that any given demon the player chooses is available to be summoned unless past events of the game make that utterly implausible. In the latter case, it's a Storyteller call - I'd probably have the spell summon a different-but-similar demon.
