                                    Hello! I'm ... a privacy fanatic so I won't share personal details here. I hope that's ok.

                                    But I can share my Exalted experience! I've been a player of Exalted since early 1st edition. Actually, I've almost exclusively played 1st edition. By far, my favourite books are Scavenger Sons, Exalted: the Sidereals, and Exalted: the Fair Folk, in that order (Fair Folk would have made 1st place, if not for the Mountain Folk chapter which I found painful). I also like Savant and Sorcerer, Games of Divinity, Manacle and Coin, and Exalted: the Lunars. I really, really dislike Book of Bone and Ebony, and Exalted: the Abyssals; to a lesser extent I also dislike Exalted: the Dragon Blooded and a number of Caste/Aspect books.

                                    When 2nd edition came out, my game group and I gave it a try and ... well, after a few sessions we decided it wasn't our cup of tea. Mostly because of mechanics we found uselessly convoluted and poorly balanced, but also because of the excessively "animesque" tone -- which admittedly, had been creeping up throughout the second half of the 1st edition line. I gave a glance at a few later supplements and they confirmed my initial opinion. So we went back to playing 1st edition, even though eventually we abandoned it in favour of other games (Blade of the Iron Throne, in particular).

                                    A few months ago we heard that Exalted 3rd edition had come out, after a loooooong development period -- which we took as a good sign: rushed jobs rarely produce good results. So we decided to give it a new try. We've run just one session with it and so far ... hmmm. It seems definitely much better than 2nd edition. It also seems to solve some, but not all, the problems of 1st edition. And it has a number of smart innovations -- some now almost standard in newer rpgs, some specific to Exalted. It's still a bit rough around the edges though, and has some ugly parts -- like the huge number of different resources one has to track (motes, sorcerous motes, willpower, Intimacy levels, limit, initiative, health levels, xp, solar xp, silver xp, gold xp, white xp ... are we joking?!?). I guess I'll have to play with it a bit more, and see how the rest of the line comes out.
