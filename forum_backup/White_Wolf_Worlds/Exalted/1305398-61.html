                Just finished the fight, I'm dumb and don't know how to use spoilers so here's the battle report:

                Bobby and Sally both roll join battle with a full excellency: Bobby rolls 21 dice for 12 successes and starts at 15, Sally rolls 22 dice for 8 successes and starts at 11. Both rise to Glowing anima.

                ROUND 1

                Bobby goes first, goes for the grapple gambit:
                Vicious Lunge + Devil Strangling Attitude + River Binding Wrath + Dragon Coil Technique + 10 excellency dice and 1 willpower for 21 dice rerolling 5s and 6s and 1 automatic success for 16 motes and 1 willpower. Rises to Burning anima.
                Sally defends with 10 excellency dice and 1 willpower + being mounted for 14 Parry for 10 motes and 1 willpower. Rises to Burning anima.
                Bobby rolls 10 successes so he misses. He loses 3 initiative and drops to 12.
                Sally swings a withering attack:
                Divine Executioner Stance + Fire and Stones Strike x5 for 17 dice and +5 post soak damage for 6m. Rises to Bonfire anima.
                Bobby defends with Essence Gathering Temper for 5 Parry for 1 initiative.
                Sally rolls 9 successes so she hits with 4 threshold successes, meaning her raw damage is 21. Bobby has 27 soak so he reduces it to overwhelming 5 plus Fire and Stones Strike for 10 post soak damage. Bobby gains 5 motes from Essence Gathering Temper.
                Sally rolls 10 dice for damage and rolls 2 successes. She raises to 14 initiative and Bobby drops to 9. She triggers One Weapon Two Blows and does the same withering attack for 9 motes.
                Bobby defends with 4 Parry.
                Sally rolls 4 successes so she hits with 0 threshold. Post soak damage is 10.
                Sally rolls 10 dice for damage and rolls 4 successes. She raises to 19 initiative and Bobby drops to 5.

                ROUND 2

                Sally delays her action, paying 2 initiative and dropping to 17.
                Bobby tries another grapple gambit:
                Vicious Lunge + Devil Strangling Attitude + River Binding Wrath + Dragon Coil Technique + 6 excellency dice and 1 willpower for 17 dice rerolling 5s and 6s and 3 automatic successes for 12 motes and 1 willpower. Rises to Bonfire anima.
                Sally clashes with a withering attack:
                Divine Executioner Stance + Excellent Strike + 4 excellency dice and 1 willpower for 21 dice rerolling 1s and 2 automatic successes for 8 motes and 1 willpower.
                Bobby rolls 14 successes, Sally rolls 22 successes; Sally wins the clash. Her post soak damage is 5.
                Sally rolls 5 dice for damage and rolls 1 success; she deals 3 automatic damage from wining the clash. She rises to 22 and Bobby drops to 1. She pays 1 mote, 1 willpower and 3 levels of anima to activate Peony Blossom Attack and makes a withering attack with no additional charms. Her anima drops to Dim.
                Bobby has 0 Parry.
                Sally rolls 13 dice for 5 successes so she hits. Her post soak damage is 5.
                She rolls 5 dice for 5 successes and crashes Bobby. She rises to 33 initiative and Bobby drops to -4.

                ROUND 3

                Sally activates Iron Whirlwind Attack + Gleaming Sever for 7m 1w 1i. Her anima increases to Glowing. She is at 0 motes now. She makes 5 decisive attacks.
                Bobby still has 0 Parry. Gleaming Sever gives 1 automatic success to the attack roll and extra successes don’t matter so I’m just gonna roll damage.
                First attack, 9 dice, Bobby uses Invincible God Metal Flash for 10 motes and 3 anima, shattering the Invincible Skin of Bronze to reduce the raw damage by 6. His anima drops to Dim. Sally rolls 3 dice for 0 damage.
                Second attack, 9 dice for 5 lethal damage. Bobby takes a crippling injury, losing an arm and negating all damage.
                Third attack, 8 dice for 4 lethal damage.
                Fourth attack, 8 dice for 2 lethal damage.
                Fifth attack, 8 dice for 4 lethal damage, exactly enough to incapacitate Bobby.

                End of fight status:
                Sally has 0 motes and 2/5 willpower left. Her anima is Dim. She is at full health.
                Bobby has 2 motes and 3/5 willpower left. His anima is Dim. He is dying.

                Soooo yeah, pretty much as expected. What surprised me but should have been obvious in hindsight is that at essence 1 neither had enough essence to use their Supernal wombo combos, so really only their basic sets of charms came into play. That said this was basically the perfect matchup for Sally, high overwhelming and high defense were pretty much the best counter for an infinite soak 1 hit kill monster. If Bobby was put up against a glacier-type boss like Ahlat or Octavian or a DBT build Lunar he'd tear them to pieces. I still think his build, the Solar tank grappler, works better with Resistance Supernal, since in general I've found its better to get your defensive stuff before your offense. If I were to build a Brawl supernal to counter Sally I'd probably have them go unarmed and mundane light armor and try and use Stealth to land a grapple from ambush and finish it quickly.

                Are you in the market for some Martial Arts? Perhaps some custom Artifacts for your campaign?

                                            I somewhat doubt it. Brawl definitely gets better with more Essence, but there’s a lot in Melee that gets crazy with it too. Like Rising Sun Slash or Hail Shattering, where effectively all your opponents 1s and 2s on an attack roll subtract from their successes. There’s also some pretty crazy defence in Heavenly Guardian Defence, especially with Celestial Bliss. Melee has the advantage of being both one of the best offensive AND defensive abilities for Solars. Brawl need to try and use Dodge to close that gap, but then you’re sacrificing heavy armor to do so.

                                            Also Solar melee has an infinite combo as long as motes/willpower last. Peony Blossom attack lets you reflexively launch Invincible Fury of the Dawn, and if you spend 15 motes in addition to it you have enough anima to reflexively trigger Peony blossom attack, which lets you reflexively trigger Invincible Fury of the Dawn again.

                                            As John says though, Solar Brawl Will absolutely destroy anything else.
