    • Sidereal are Martial Artist Exalted, Martial Artist in some Wuxia movie that I watch never got hit => Sidereal must be squishy.

                                                      Sidereals are comparatively fragile and brittle, but this tends to express itself in a variety of ways. Probably the easiest way to see it is to look at the idea behind SMA (even if the implementations have generally had issues): SMA is really powerful, but that power depends on a specific strategy or approach. You are really great at fighting -- as long as your "trick" keeps working. If you get knocked out of your game, you're often screwed.

                                                      Whereas, say, Solar or Lunar combat styles don't work that way; the best Lunar tricks are generally not going to have anything as strong as a SMA style fighting on its terms with ever cylinder firing, but they will tend to be more broadly applicable and reliable.

                                                      Sidereals aren't bad fighters, but they are best at fighting like a Sidereal, which usually means planning ahead, relying on tricks and manipulation -- even in a fight -- and so on. This tends to mean that if you screw up, you have less room for error. It also means that there tends to be rules and limitations you have to worry about, which will cause problems for you (including failures in combat) if you don't cover for them.

                                                      Sidereal combat styles also tend to be a bit risk-averse (as opposed to, say, Lunars, who can probably afford to take risks because they have more health levels and can get them back easier.) The idea is that Sidereals don't flip a coin unless they've weighted it in advance.

                                                      Whereas if you want to be able to drop everything with no warning or plan and lunge at someone's throat while howling for blood, or if you want combat styles that will almost always work reasonably well, Lunars or Solars are a better bet.

                                                      Sidereals having fewer motes and health levels in general is part of this because those things give you more margin for error, which allows you to play it by ear -- that's something that Sidereals are generally worse at than other Exalted. But that doesn't mean that they're bad at combat, it means that they're bad at a style of combat where you take risks and rely on your ability to soak up hits to cover for mistakes.

                                                      Of course this is just generalities -- Sidereals are still capable of fighting with no plan when they have to, and they have to sometimes be able to take a hit. But it's a bad strategy for them. The idea behind Avoidance Kata and their future-warning charms isn't to never fight ever, but to make sure you fight on your own terms.

                                                          Originally posted by Jen View Post
                                                          Ox-Body Technique is such a weak and horrible charm, and some people seem to think that Sidereal has the worst OBT among the splat, so they go make thread about how aweful Sid's OBT is
                                                          Maybe it's because I lack sufficient experience in Exalted combat, but I don't agree with any part of this. Exalted is not a JRPG or an MMO where you expect to get hit every time the enemy attacks. It's a game about heroes who fight cinematically, which means they don't often get hit, and when they do, they don't usually take serious damage. Ox-Body allows you to reflect the punishment you can endure, but Resistance Charms and armor are what allow you to not take serious punishment in the first place.

                                                          Ox-Body -0 is for characters who take a punch or a gash and don't even flinch. When your health levels come from Ox-Body -0, you end up looking like the Terminator. Bullet holes? Who cares.

                                                          Ox-Body 2x-1 is for characters who can take a serious beating and stay in the fight. Your character shows that they are human, they feel pain, and they can be slowed down, but they don't give up. The synergy with Battle Fury Focus is intentional.

                                                          Ox-Body -1, 2x-2 is for characters who just won't die. This is for Rocky characters. You see them get seriously messed up, but they stay in the fight against all odds or sense.

                                                          Sidereals only have access to Ox-Body -0 not because they are fragile, but because the way in which they are durable is the way the Terminator is durable: damage doesn't slow them down at all until it gets really serious. Lunars have access to 4x-2 because they are the monsters who keep fighting in spite of grievous injury. Solars have access to a broad set of Ox-Body options because some of them are Terminators and some of them are Rockys, and some of them are something in between.
