    Dragon Blood Charms Help!

    09-28-2014, 08:49 AM
    Hi, I am pretty new to exalted and have encountered a 'problem' in my game, and was hoping those more well-read than I could help me find what I need.

    I won't bore you with the current story details; other than that I am playing an Eclipse and I'm currently trying to stop an N/A manse from exploding. (Specifically, Lookshy)

    Now, I have 10 days in game to do this.

    I've been told that it can't be done in the time frame (Basically, we need to cap a demense) without a solar craftsman or sorcery. I disagree, as by the rules- the time to make something is reduced by the hands involved (many hands make light work, etc) and I can get the MANPOWER to construct it; source the materials fine, transportation is not an issue--

    My issue is the DESIGN rule. It says in the core book that to design a manse it takes a month MINIMUM (page 133). I have 10 days for it to be FINISHED.

    My math worked it out to 51,600 people building, can get it constructed in 10 days. If I employ the entire population of Lookshy helots (and dragonblooded thaumaturges etc ) that will by RAW get it done in 5 days or less.

    How can I, using Dragonblooded ONLY charms, or thaumaturgy rituals, push the design time down? I have access to a LOT of specialized help, it can be assumed someone in the characters social circles will have these charms.

    Are there any spirit charms?

    I have the Bureaucracy 2nd excellency only, otherwise I would attempt speed the wheels.

    Dragonbloods are known for the fact that they work best in groups. I know there is an excellency that adds successes to OTHER people (Dragon-Blooded have access to a special Charm, Terrestrial (Ability) Reinforcement, which allows a Dragon-Blood to temporarily boost the Ability traits of other characters with whom he is allied) but I don't know what else I'm looking for. Craft, Lore, Occult, Bureaucracy?

          To prevent a manse from exploding, as long as it's exploding due to geomantic reasons and not due to an inherent feature of the manse, all you need to do is place the appropriate capstones. Five of them, totally a Resources 5 cost, are placed at appropriate places, determined by a Perception + Lore roll at a difficulty of 1. That's it. No more explosion, you're free to work on the Manse, repairing it, for the months it requires, though proper repairs take far less time than actual construction.

                                  The problem with the design is different from the one of the construction.

                                  Design is about creativity, you need to imagine a Manse, study the Dragon Lines and flows of Essence etc., see how it can all harmonize with the Demesne and so on. I don't think the Dragon-Blooded have Charms to boost creativity which is exactly what you need and that's what takes one month.

                                  If you get the original design of the manse you don't need the one month rule, you already have it, or perhaps a similar one to work from. Imagine it like an architect, it's the calculus and creativity that takes time, not drawing on paper. And the charms seem to boost the time and how well you do the drawing, the thinking you'll have to do on your own...

                                  In your place I would try to boost Occult and Lore through Excellencies and other insight charms to get a better understanding and convince the ST your superhuman levels of the abilities necessary for the design are a good reason for allowing the One Month Rule to be bent. Sometimes rules must follow the game and not the other way around, especially if a players is putting serious effort into it and coming up with logical and/or cool solutions.

But I don't agree that "Magic" is the answer for everything, you have to have some rules and base logic, that's why the game has charms and explanation for each charm.

                                              Breaking it down to an example: Frank Lloyd Wright's "Hanna House"

                                              Imagining Hanna House and drawing it on paper is pure creativity, making the necessary calculations to make it real an inhabitable is the part of the design where you can apply Craft Charms. Craft Charms won't boost your Creativity, but in my humble opinion, will speed up your calculation capacity and everything you need to translate the concept you imagined into a real thing.

                                              Despite charms making stuff happen because it's "magic" is not enough, that's why Manse Building has Lore and Occult minimums, there is a sense of logic built into Exalted Magic. Besides, if you read the charm descriptions, they enhance your working abilities, they don't make stuff appear of thin air, you still need materials and working hours.
