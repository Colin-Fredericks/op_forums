                Originally posted by Jefepato View Post
                1) Are there any particular hard limits on what types of creatures can become an Exalt's familiar? Per the Familiar merit, it's possible to have a God-Blooded animal or something as a familiar, and a Twilight Caste at least can take Essence 1-3 elementals and demons of the first circle as familiars.

                I ask because I assume that sorcerers aren't supposed to be able to whip up some kind of super-animal and then use all those familiar-buffing Survival Charms on it. Is there a strict limit somewhere in the rules, or is it basically just recommended that STs not allow familiars dramatically more powerful than existing options?
                By default, they have to be animals, but magically-enhanced animals are kosher.

                2) Lunars have access to Charms that allow them to enhance a familiar's intelligence to human levels, and even teach their intelligent familiars sorcery. Is this kind of thing potentially within reach for a Solar (presuming sufficient mastery of both Occult and Survival), or is this type of animal shenanigans too far outside of Solar themes?
                I'm not sure I see Solar Charms elevating animals to human-level intelligence, although they could do so through other methods.

                3) If a Lunar has a familiar that already has human-level intelligence, can they use Witch-Beast Ascendancy on it without applying Erudite Beast-Mind Expansion first?
                You'd still have to use it; it'd just apply the benefit for already-intelligent familiars.

                WBA normally requires prior use of EBME, which normally requires prior use of Blessed Moon-Beast Empowerment, which explicitly applies to animal familiars. So I assume WBA isn't meant to be usable on non-animal familiars at all (correct?). But if the Lunar has an intelligent God-Blooded familiar or an ata-beast from Halta or something, is this doable?
                You're correct that the animal-only restriction still applies to Witch-Beast Ascendancy. God-Blooded animals and ata-beasts still count as animals.
