                                                            Originally posted by norraba View Post
                                                            [*] the charm starts by saying that the benefit is generated by the solars students passing on their knowledge. do these students need to be the solars followers or can they be anyone that they significantly trained
                                                            Anyone you've taught. You don't need to actually have the Followers Merit for them; the Charm just uses it as a way of representing the size of the total group you've studied.

                                                            [*]the charm makes it so that the teaching themselves become a capable of generating followers as if they themselves where a character, so are the teaching able to have multiple purchases of this merit, say having followers in multiple different cities throughout creation?
                                                            Nope, all your students would be aggregated into a single group of virtual Followers.

                                                            [*]how would you conceptualize the spreading of these teaching? as in can a solar have 100 highly trained students who each go to 100 different cities and start spreading their teaching in each city, and how would the solars students accrue more followers within their discipleship for the teaching? would this be a project the solar would have to do as a player, or is it something that the story teller manages in the background and thus be something that the player has no control over other than trying to train more students to spread the teachings further?
                                                            The project system would definitely be a useful tool for handling it.

                                                            [*] is it meant to be that the maximum benefit that the player can get for each purchase of followers be cult 2, since the mechanic is cult rating of half the teachings followers rounded down, or would the effect of the player having potentially hundreds of cult ratings at level 2 just be able to be lumped together into a higher cult rating?
                                                            The former.

                                                            [*]since the followers that the teachings amass belong to the teachings themselves and not the character, does this mean that the solar may gain the cult benefit without anyone ever knowing their true identity, such as the followers of the teachings simply knowing of them as simply the venerable teacher who created the ideology? also could the lore charm Hidden Wisdom Bestowal be used to amplify the secrecy in which the venerated teacher shrouds themselves in, in order to avoid censure from the immaculate faith.
                                                            Yep!

                                                            [*]also, and i think this is the most important for me honestly, how effective would you consider this charm to be in its ability to effect the group consciousness of creation, to me it seems like the ability it grants to treat all of your students as if they are trained disciples who can then inspire others into a cult like adherence to your ideology, denotes a much greater, and less character intensive ability to spread a certain way of thinking desirable to the player, and i would love to hear your thoughts on this.
                                                            It doesn't literally transform your students or those who've received your teachings into cultists - instead, the flow of your teachings through society becomes a source of power. It won't help you in changing the group consciousness of Creation, but it lets you draw power from doing so through the narrative.

                                                            [*]also one last thing i thought about, can this only effect mortal humans, or can different creatures including spirits be effected by the effects of this charm?
                                                            Anyone counts.

                                                            above board i want to use this charm to over time spread a defining belief in everyones inherent equality throughout creation and promote the existence of what might be called fully automated luxury socialism, but in a way that keeps with creations themes.
                                                            Soul-Light Spreading Discipline alone isn't going to be able to do that. You'll want to use Flowing Mind Prana and Legendary Scholar's Curriculum to spread that Intimacy to your students, and rely on social influence to strengthen and spread it. Teaming up with your circle's Zenith and/or Eclipse would go a long ways towards helping with that.
