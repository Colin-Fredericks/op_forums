            Originally posted by Sakii View Post
            when you do a working, if your successes match the difficulty, do you get 0 or 1 success?

            Workings are extended actions. According to the general rules for extended actions, "When determining a character’s current success total, count only those successes that meet or exceed the action’s difficulty" (emphasis in original, Exalted 3rd Edition p. 189). Since the working rules don't say anything about changing how extended actions work for them, I would assume it's the same - if you meet the difficulty exactly, you get one success. Yes, this does mean there really isn't any difference between meeting the difficulty exactly, or getting 1 success over it.

            Edited to add, because I failed to read further: Actually, the example for extended actions suggests that meeting the difficulty exactly gets you one success, and each success over the difficulty adds one. So if you meet your working exactly, you get one success, and if you make one more success, you get 2 successes towards the total.

                            Originally posted by Voyd211 View Post
                            What setting elements and character types are gone from EX3? Judging by an answer I got in the Ask the Devs thread, Green Sun Princes no longer exist.
                            This is question is a long answer and I hope others will fill in more aspects, but your specific reference is that Green Sun Princes (as in Infernal Exalted) definitely still exist, but they don't evolve into Devil Tigers anymore. The "build your own" exalt concept from Devil Tigers was folded into Exigents for Ex3.

                            The Yozi's being about to escape is gone, they are trapped forever with no hope of escape.

                            The Deathlords no longer enslave Abyssals by default, the assumption now is a master-apprentice relationship.

                            In general, any setting elements that were originally painted as "super evil, gross, and simply the worst thing ever" are in stead nuanced with reasons for why they do things. Personal motivations, intimacies, etc.

                            Less emphasis is put on detailing things from the past and leaving it more in question or open to interpretation.

                            I know there's more, but those are some of the biggins to me.
