                    To clarify : when you buy you'r 1st ox-body, you het a -0 HL and you convert a -1 HL into a -0.
                    The second purchase would convert the second -1 into a -0.
                    The third and fourh would respectively convert one fo your -2 into -1 and then -0.

                    The end result with 10 OBT (the max you can buy) is a sid with 16 -0 and then directly falls into incapacitated. Help them because they have few choices for ignoring wound penalties, which was quite limited (either constantly pump motes in excellencies, or costly charms like heartless maiden trance)

                    This was inspired by the fact that lunars have a "bonus" on their OBT that fits their style (dying HLs IIRC) that doesn't totally change the math of health level acquisition.                                             Sidereals are the most fragile type of Exalted for their "power level", but that doesn't really have anything to do with their Ox-Body except insofar as their Ox-Body is a a loose thematic nod to the convention. If you were trying to assess it on the basis of individual elements like that you might well end up mistakenly believing that Sidereals are the tankiest Exalted, thematically, because they can attune Starmetal armour for half what everyone else can and it has potent damage reduction. It's more that Sidereals are fragile for other reasons, and the Ox-Body thing is an easy thing to point to as an example, because the actual reasons are the result of complex interactions embedded in their charmset. (Talking about 1e here, obviously. 2e Sidereals weren't designed, they just happened.)

                                            The thing that makes Sidereals fragile is that Exalted has always been constructed on the basis that all offense is strong and thus you need to defend strongly against all offense, but although Sidereals do have strong defenses, they have big holes in their repertoire and need to scramble to cover their bases. There are a lot of important, fundamental parts of an "Exalted build" that Sidereals either don't have, or have in a single form that's saddled with awkward limitations, and when getting hit once is serious business, these are perilous, and they need to make the most of what they have while making it look awesome. That's what the "Sidereals are flimsy" deal is about.

                                            Obviously, in 3e, this won't work very well, since a lot of the changes 3e is making are designed to remove the pressures that make fighting as a Sid tricky, while at the same time requiring everyone to keep on their toes in combat. So I'm interested to see what they do with them there!

                                                        Well, I'm not sure my intent was clear so i'll clarify :

                                                        The idea is not about complaining about this or that, but to point out a fact and a (possible) solution if people think it's a problem.

                                                        Namely, having a charm work exactly the same way for different exalts but each has access to different options, just as solars have the same OBT than sids except they have access to more options.
                                                        The consequence is the solar version is STRICTLY better.

                                                        One solution is to add a bonus different for each splat, especially since OBT are considered "weak" for their XP cost. The consequence is, while solar OBT is still arguably better (because the splat bonus should be balanced and they still have access to more options) it's not strictly better anymore.


                                                        Now in terms of "tankiness" sidereals were in fact not so bad in 2E. You could still stack armor with the resplendency that gave you +9B/+9L soak, Optimistic security practice, sidereal shell-games (with the advantage of reducing your enemy's), possibly medicine's pinacle charm. All of that without counting as wearing an armor.

                                                        One of them main problems in this matter, just like in a lot of others, is that quite a few of their weaknesses were meant to be "fixed" by elements... that were not exclusive to them :
                                                        - They have almost no access to hardness, but this is made up by starmetal armor bonus... that everyone can use
                                                        - They have very few damage boosting charms, once again compensated by weapon MM bonus, and MA charms that raise minimum damage
                                                        - They have arguably weaker combat charms, but they get access to SMA and a very powerful CMA... but solars have access too (and are even better at it because of their higher dice caps)

                                                        The other elements that force them to "glass canon" style are :
                                                        - the lack of DV boosting charms, especially since their MA style is a full-offense assassination-centered one
                                                        - they are even more binary that other exalts.
                                                        Duck fate : either you roll enough successes and you perfectly dodge, or you wasted 10 motes for strictly no bonus (and that was still when other perfects were at 3/5 motes).
                                                        Walking outside fate and 2E optimistic security practice : both automatically succeed or fail depending on the enemy essence rating.
                                                        Someone else's destiny 2E : You can perfectly ignore and redirect any poison (roll is trivial) but you once you have one dose "stored", the charm is powerless until you hit and apply it to someone.
                                                        Impending the flow 2E : Either the attack is perfect or unblockable and the charm has strictly no effect, or he perfectly negate the attack

                                                        It's not always bad, but giving the option for different styles would be interesting.
