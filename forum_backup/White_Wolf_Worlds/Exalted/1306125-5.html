                Honestly the Immaculate Dragon Styles are another source of annoyance for me. Yes, they don't have Terrestrial keywords, but they are not very good styles. I'd say almost all of the other styles are stronger, even when limited by Terrestrial keywords. Earth Dragon is the best of the 5, Wood dragon is okay, Water and Air are meh, and Fire sucks ass (not gonna shed a single tear for fire dragons, they already have Melee and Dodge).

                But aside from them not being very good, the very big problem they have is that they are super stingy with their form weapons. Every DB who wants to become serious about MA will feel very pressured to eventually buy his aspect's IMA up to at least the form, but each style has only one weapon + unarmed, which really limits your options.

                Air Dragon has the chakram which no other style uses, so go unarmed or go home.
                Earth Dragon has the tetsubo which has the same problem, but at least it allows all types of armor.
                Fire Dragon has short swords, which combine with Steel Devil and MAYBE if your ST feels pity for you Single Point.
                Wood Dragon has staves, which are shared as form weapons by Golden Janissary, White Reaper and Laughinf Monster. We'll probably see a few more styles using staves in the future, but that's a pretty good spread.
                Water Dragon has tiger claws, which work for Tiger, Ebon Shadow, Centipede and Falcon, making it the most flexible atm

                A terrestrial who wants to practice Snake, Crane, Dreaming Pearl Courtesan, Black Claw, Mantis, White Veil or Swaying Grass Dance is forced to go unarmed-only.
                If they want to practice Single Point, Silver-Voiced Nightingale or Righteous Devil then you'll either be forced to stick with Terrestrial keywords or buy 5 dead charms.

                And going unarmed is not a small demerit, its in fact a bigger tradeoff for DBs than for other exalts because it means they can't use artifact weapons, which means they not only do they lose lots of free stats on all withering attacks but also lose out on Resonant Evocations, which is one of the greatest sources of power-parity for DBs.

                This all taken together means DB martial artists are very limited in their choice of styles as well; some styles are only viable with some aspects, and some styles become dramatically worse chioces for DBs as a whole. Also a lot of cool MA weapons like hook-swords, seven-section staves, fans, etc. are not all that useable.

                And that is Bad.

                Are you in the market for some Martial Arts? Perhaps some custom Artifacts for your campaign?

                                        As an adendum to my last post:

                                        Terrestrial keywords vary greatly in importance, but on some styles they are not so bad. For the type of character who for example is mostly a social guy but picks up some Silver-Voiced Nightingale to be able to contribute to combat encounters while taking advantage of his high social stats, things are fine. What I outlined on my previous post is only REALLY a problem for characters who want to be combat focused and/or want "being a master martial artist" to be an important part of their concept.

                                        For Serious Martial ArtistsTM Aspect becomes a really important choice:

                                        Water Aspects are probably still the overall best: MA is an Aspect ability for them, they can enter Water Aura with just the excellency, and Water Dragon has the best weapon compatibility and pretty good armor compatibility. Also worth noting is that they have easy access to Embracing The Violent Flow, the Brawl Water signature charm, which is very strong and compatible with all unarmed martial arts, and could be a solution to a "no artifacts" build.

                                        Earth Aspects are pretty good too: Earth Dragon is the strongest of the Immaculate Styles and is the only one compatible with all kinds of armor, and they have access to Earth Resistance. They are pretty mush locked to unarmed-only though if they want ot combine any other styles. White Reaper is a good accompaniment . If they do go unarmed, then Crater Making Impact (Earth Brawl signature) can be worth picking up, as can Earth Thrown.

                                        Fire Aspect is weird: their IMA is the worst by far, BUT Fire Dodge is great and is only really viable for Fire Aspects. So they are a good choice if you want to main a MA that likes to use Evasion mainly (Snake, Dreaming Pearl Courtesan, Centipede, Swaying Grass Dance and Laughing Monster off the top of my head) or if you want to do Steel Devil specifically. They also have Socialize and Presence as Aspect abilities, so you could pick them to go with social-heavy MA like White Veil, Black Claw and Dreaming Pearl. Good signature picks for them could be Unbinding the Inner Flame or Raging Fire-Dragon Spirit, as well as Aura of Invulnerability.

                                        Wood Aspects are okay: their MA is pretty good an combines well with others, White Reaper specially. Other than that there's not much to say about them. I guess you could combine their Wood Performance charms with Silver-Voiced Nightingale, I have a player in my game doing just that and he's doing okay. They have a scene long Appearance booster in Vivacious Dragon Beauty, so I guess thay would make good Dreaming Pearl Courtesans, or you could go with Snake/White Veil/Centipede if you want to play up the poison aspect.

                                        Air Aspects are the worst off: light armor + unarmed only, and Air Dragon Style is really not very good. The only really viable option I can think of is taking advantage of Air Stealth and using Air Dragon + Ebon Shadow for an assassin build. Their Brawl signature, Flying Whirlwind Razor, is really really good, but I feel like its wasted on them.

                                        Are you in the market for some Martial Arts? Perhaps some custom Artifacts for your campaign?
