    I mean, people are saying that the front cover looks blurry, but... it's pastel art. That's what pastel art looks like. It is the result that one achieves from drawing a picture using big sticks of pigment.

    I can get pastel art not being to every taste, but I just want to lay that out there for any statements that seem to be attributing its appearance to some kind of technical failing, rather than it being what that particular style is intended to look like.

    Personally, I find it to be gorgeous, but perhaps that's just because pastel art managed to somehow be a prominent feature of my childhood, and so there's a deeply engrained sense of endearment towards it. If ever there was a time that I wanted to be an artist, making something that looks like that picture of Kingfisher Swift is probably what I aspired to achieve.

                                        Sure, she can like purple. But from an artistic perspective, there's nothing at all in pictures of Mnemon to tell you she's a high-breeding, high-essence Earth Aspect. Ideally, she should have strong aspect markings and then wear whatever, but aspect markings can be difficult to show (for example, rock-hard skin doesn't necessarily have a visual element) so clothes are a subconscious shorthand.

                                        The sigs for example, all either have some appropriate colouration (the fire Aspect's red hair, for example, which while it can be natural, is common in the South, not on the Isle) or if not, they wear the colours (the Earth's white armour, the Water's blue-green clothes, etc), at least in their sig pictures (I do see the point mentioned earlier that on the cover page she's less Earthy-looking... But I still think it's better than the Core).

                                        I don't want to sound negative here. There's loads of good stuff in here. And you're never going to please everyone (I don't like pastels except for nature scenes, Isator does, it's pretty subjective). But since you asked.

                                            Mind, somehow I've associated her shade of purple with amethysts even before I had much awareness of prominent pop culture Amethysts (some of whom did not exist at the time).

                                            Overall, I don't think we need to redesign Mnemon for a largely hypothetical person who won't know who she is from proximity to text describing her, which in the majority of instances will make clear what she's about (text that will also describe Aspect markings such as skin texture that wouldn't really be coming across visually anyway). And even if that person was around... what are they losing from it?

                                            Like, Mnemon's been around for fifteen years, this is mostly a discussion for when she was way in the past.
