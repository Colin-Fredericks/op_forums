                                Also, something i wanted to clear up in the discussion about 1CD from dead Primordials living in Malfeas.

                                I'm not opposed to it at all and in fact in my own games there are both tales and examples of such "orphaned" beings, of all circles, existing both inside and outside of Malfeas.

                                My focus when i brought up the subject was in the development of specifics of why a breed of demons would follow the Yozis in exile:

                                - Some obscure breeds of masterless Anuhles joined the hosts of the spawn of the defeated out of a twisted sense of identification/camaraderie with all other spider demons, their "ronin" status leading to their becoming something of diplomats/secret networkers bringing a peculiar sense of fraternity/unity to the many races of hell-spiders beyond their separate origins.

                                Or:

                                - A singular 2CD, borne out of a peculiar act of love/union between the souls of two different Primordials, would outlive its "core" master by joining the ranks of the souls of its Oath-mate, in the process taking the many 1rst circle races it spawned in the time before the Divine Revolution along with it.

                                Or some other crazy narrative, involving demons descending of dead primordials and where they ended up, be it Malfeas, Underworld, under the earth or waters & above the clouds, custom prison-realms like wherever the Eyeless Nation rests, Wyld and beyond, the deeps of the Well of Udr or elsewhere, depending on one's particular preferences.

                                It's possible to take the thing in (m)any directions, even have more than one variant, as diferent races or Primordial souls that remained through singular circunstances each seeks their own particular path.

                                Explanations can be made up - and when you do so, you may have something more than you had at the start. Cooking up the reasons adds to the lore particular to one's table. But coming up with reasons, what separates a certain being or group of beings from others of similar circunstances and how they ended up where they now stand, is where stories and richer scenarios can take form, imho.

                                Sometimes you just don't have a definite answer - and you can make a plot out of looking for that.

                                        As described in the 3e Corebook, after the Great Contagion, the forefathers of Sijan used their princely wealth to found crusades across Creation.                                         In fact this bit of made up lore would be quite relevant to the first 3rd edition game i actually played, relating both to the origin of my Twilight - who was part from one of the many "wandering exorcist clans" that would cross the Hundred Kingdoms dealing with all kinds of supernatural troubles (not just ghosts, what lead into the loss of some of the funereal lore for the sake of other branches of the occult) - that of a kingdom whose Underworld ties would be subverted in terrible ways when a Deathknight joined the royal court and the interplay of those "sibling groups" with each other and "the old homeland" beside the Black Chase.
