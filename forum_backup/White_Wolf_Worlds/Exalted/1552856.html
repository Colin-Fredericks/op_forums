    Question about Yozis and charms 2e / 3e

    08-03-2025, 01:54 PM
    Greetings,


    Totally new to exalted, so if this is clearly stated anywhere, or if any misconceptions there, please correct me:


    I understand that the Yozis are defined by their charms, that in a sense they are the charms. Also, that every Yozi learns the charms that their exalted learn.
    1. IF so, then aren't the Yozis crosscontaminating each other when their exalted learn charms from a different Yozi?
    2. Isn't the Ebon Dragon becoming more a more different as the Fiends learn charms from other creatures?
    3. Are Devil-Tigers also defined by the charms they know?

      1. Think of the 2e Yozis as Charm trees. Malfeas is the set of Malfeas Charms, everything that defines him is in there and everything in there defines him. An Infernal Exalted who creates a new Malfeas Charm is adding to the definition of Malfeas, within the confines of Malfeas's preexisting definition (you can't make a Malfeas Charm that makes him less like what Malfeas already is). A Slayer who learns an Ebon Dragon Charm is not turning it into a Malfeas Charm, he just has Charms from multiple Yozis. Malfeas remains the same.

      2. In the same manner, Fiends aren't transforming foreign Charms into Ebon Dragon Charms. They know Ebon Dragon Charms, and they also know these Spirit Charms or whatever. The Ebon Dragon is only transformed by adding new Charms branching out of his Charm tree.

      3. The short answer is no. Infernal Exalted who've developed the Devil-Tiger/Rising Phoenix magic aren't Primordials (Yozis being a subset of Primordials). There's an actual human there, underneath all those Permanent Charms, with a Motivation of his own and a soul of his own. His self-definition comes from being a person and he retains all of the free will and capacity for self-contradiction and complicated feelings that any person has, minus whatever constraints are imposed on him by individual weird Permanent Charms he knows. To the extent that he has become like a Yozi, he is only like a Yozi in the specific ways his Charms (such as the Devil-Tiger tree) have made him like one, and not any other ways.

      This means that if, say, Chwen Screaming-Curses makes the Chwen Screaming-Curses Infernal Charm tree through Devil-Tiger/Rising Phoenix, and then teaches a Chwen Screaming-Curses Charm to another Infernal, and that other Infernal invents a new Chwen Screaming-Curses Charm, Chwen Screaming-Curses, who is an actual specific human, does not automatically learn that new Charm. Because the Chwen Screaming-Curses tree is a tree of Infernal Charms, though, it is likely to have a bunch of transformative Permanent Charms in it that will start to limit the nature of Chwen Screaming-Curses, the person, if he invents and learns them. That's just what Infernal Charm trees are like: they start with a few core thematic elements and build outward from there through thematic associations, often including a lot of Charms that transform what you are like and may limit the things you do in exchange for power, which you may have to route through as prerequisites to get to other Charms you might want. The Chwen Screaming-Curses tree, whatever form it takes, cannot escape following this model.

      Here's the long answer getting into the unnecessary weeds:

      When we say Malfeas is the set of Malfeas Charms, we mean that the Yozi Malfeas who exists as the Demon City is an entity that knows the full set of Malfeas Charms, that probably came fully-formed from the Wyld at the dawn of the birth of the Primordials (though back then he wasn't Malfeas yet, fetich-death transformed who he is and what his Charms are). At the very top of the Malfeas Charm tree is an Essence 10 Charm called Malfeas Cosmic Principle. Learning this Malfeas Charm effectively makes you the Yozi Malfeas. If an Infernal Exalt learned so many Malfeas Charms and gathered so much Essence that he was able to learn Malfeas Cosmic Principle, he would cease to be an Exalted human being and instead become a Yozi. Since Yozis can only know their own Charms, he would lose all of his non-Malfeas Charms. Any unique properties of the Yozis as beings, like instantly learning all the Charms added to your tree, being able to adapt the Charms of foreign Yozis as Charms of your own tree, or so on, are conferred either by this Charm or by other very high level Malfeas Charms branching out from there. The other Yozis have corresponding Charms at the top of their trees.

      Yes, this does mean that if a Slayer learned Malfeas Cosmic Principle, there would then be two Malfeases, the one that was birthed from the Wyld long ago and underwent fetich death, and the newborn Malfeas who used to be a Slayer.

                              I would say basically the same thing Yue Ryong said, but phrase it differently: (Yozi) Cosmic Principle isn't really a Charm that is "invented." Once an Infernal Charm tree exists, the potential for (Yozi) Cosmic Principle already exists, and it is just a matter of somebody eventually getting around to learning it.

                              So Ibeaumanior does not create Ibeaumanior Cosmic Principle. It's not a Charm anybody knows, because Ibeaumanior, as a Devil-Tiger, can't learn it, and nobody else has access to learning it. If Ibeaumanior dies and Yue Ryong inherits his Exaltation with his Ibeaumanior Caste, Yue Ryong can hypothetically eventually learn Ibeaumanior Cosmic Principle. So: who created or invented Ibeaumanior Cosmic Principle? Not really anybody, except in the sense that Ibeaumanior created the Charm tree. But Yue Ryong is the first person to ever manifest and learn it. And now he's not a person, now he's the exaggerated image of Ibeaumanior as viewed through the essentialist, transformative lens of Infernal magic.

                              I'd also object to calling Devil-Tiger Infernals "half-Exalted, half-Yozi." They're not true Yozis, and they're not any less Exalted than they were before. They're Infernal Exalted who have a subset of Yozi-like qualities.

                              It's just that what an Infernal Exalted can be turns out to encompass some really weird possibilities.

                                                  A devil-tiger (as in somebody who knows Triumphant Howl) can't learn (Yozi) Cosmic Principle. I assume you mean "the inheritor of a devil-tiger's custom caste."
                                                  The Infernal Exaltation moves on from an Infernal who learns (Yozi) Cosmic Principle in the same way that it would move on if the Infernal instead died.

                                                  There are, mind you, some trade-offs to becoming a Primordial. All your perfect defenses gain a Greater Imperfection on top of their normal Imperfection. You lose access to all Charms outside of your Primordial Charm tree (so becoming the artificial Yozi Ibeaumanior means you can only learn Ibeaumanior Charms, etc, and say, if you know any Malfeas Charms or Adorjan Charms, you lose them). Primordial Charm trees all deliberately have blind spots and situations they just aren't well prepared for.

                                                  More trade-offs await if you go further into the Primordial transfiguration. For instance, (Yozi) Glory Incarnate, which gives you big Primordial dice pools and Primordial-grade Shaping defenses, also severely cripples your ability to act outside the confines of your personal Excellency.
