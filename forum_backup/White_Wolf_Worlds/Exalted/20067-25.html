                                        Originally posted by Queshire View Post
                                        Do Exalted count as mortals when they talk about mortals in charms and stuff, or is it only unexalted people?
                                        When powers (charms, anima powers, artifacts, ect.) says mortals, they only means beings that cannot spend motes, so normal humans, animals and so on.
                                        Even Enlightened Mortals doesn't count as mortals. This is defined in a sidebar on page 218 of the Core.

                                        There is however one notable exception to this, for the sole purpose of Abyssal charms (and maybe anima powers) ghosts count as mortals.

                                                As ST, I've generally rulled that since the Charm's effect takes place in Step 7, the successes doubled are those remaining after external penalties and DV have reduced them in step 5 because this is the point that we 'determine damage'. Otherwise you're jumping about in the step order and that sorta just bothers me. Additionally, it would flow on to very massive dice spikes when comboed with Heaven Thunder Hammer.

                                                In the way I'd run it with your numbers above, SHF and HTH would net you ((7-6=1)x2)x3=6 Yards knockback. If we ran it the other way it'd be ((14-6=8)x2)x3= 48 yards knock back. Since you can just 'aim down' and thus apply all that as auto bashing damage on top of the damage you'd normally do it's just... Its too swingy for me.

                                                YMMV
