    Originally posted by SamuraiMujuru View Post
    This came up at my last session, and it occurred to me that I didn't know the official mechanical ruling on it.

    When spending for charms with duration, the motes are committed for the duration of the charm. If a charm has a willpower cost as well, is that willpower ALSO committed?

    My immediate thought was no, as Willpower functions differently, but figured it'd be worth getting a more official answer.
    The rules for Charm duration specifies that Essence spent on non-Instant Charms is committed. This isn't a general rule for resources spent on Charms, so it only applies to motes and thus doesn't apply to Willpower. (You don't commit levels of anima spent, either.)

                                    Originally posted by Voyd211 View Post
                                    I don't have EX3, so I have to ask: what are Evocations? Are they like Charms for artifacts?
                                    Yes. Instead of Artifacts simply having a set of innate powers, these powers unlock (via evocations, which function like charms that you buy with xp) as your bond with your artifact.

                                    It represents the relationship you have with your weapon or armor and how it's not just some cool thing you picked up, instead it's YOUR weapon or armor, that's been by your side through thick and thin. The Dragon-blooded take it a step further and place importance on generations of your family using that artifact. It was your mothers, and your grandfathers, and your great-grandmothers and your great-great-grandmothers, etc.

                                    The short answer is that they are indistinguishable from charms, except that they only have Essence requirements (not ability requirements).

                                        It's not uncommon to have a bunch of effects that unlock automatically, and often the first evocation will unlock for free if the artifact is made of the right magical material.

                                        See. instead of MM bonuses there's the Resonant and Dissonant keywords, which affect what evocations a character can unlock based on the material the artifact is made from. Dragonblooded are Resonant with Jade, so they can get full power out of Jade artifacts, where as Lunars aren't, so the evocations they get from the same artifact will be a bit weaker. With Moonsilver the positions are reversed. Solars are resonant with everything.
