                                                        Originally posted by Grod_the_giant View Post
                                                        ... If a successful attack against a battle group grants initiative equal to its size?
                                                        Fighting a large battlegroup becomes easier, since you're regaining initiative faster, and initiative determines how long it takes to kill you.

                                                        When fighting a battlegroup alongside exalted or similarly powerful entities, it becomes more initiative-profitable to attack the battlegroup. Given that battlegroups don't generally have defensive excellencies and charms (or any excellencies and charms), this risks making the battlegroup too easily the focus of attention, especially since a battlegroup's attacks are generally stronger than its defense (orders and multi-target attacks don't really have a defensive equivalent)

                                                        Enfolded in the Dragon's Wings becomes a bit stronger, since it's potentially denying your enemies more initiative.

                                                        If this includes successful decisive attacks, then it becomes harder to take advantage of the reset to crash someone, and more often the case that a decisive increases your initiative, which is weird. (This can happen in normal cases, but only if you make 1 or 2 initiative decisives)
