                                                  Reposting from an rpg.net post:

                                                  Core Manga Inspirations:
                                                  Fist of the North Star (which I like but actually don't love *so* much as a personal thing, but it is so obviously Formative Influences Canon, and Kenshiro so absolutely a Solar)
                                                  Trigun
                                                  Ninja Scroll
                                                  Vampire Hunter D: Bloodlust
                                                  Vision of Escaflowne

                                                  Less Core Inspirations (in rough order of potence):
                                                  Princess Mononoke, Nausicaa
                                                  Cowboy Bebop, Samurai Champloo
                                                  Laputa: Castle In The Sky
                                                  Slayers
                                                  Street Fighter II: The Animated Movie

                                                  ...

                                                  Pretty much the main things in an influence are some form of "Wandering guy(s) with stylish character design and awesome spiritual martial arts in a fantasy world with a post-apoc/war-torn/Wild West vibe, with lots of ruins of fallen civilization, where injustice and gritty violence are common". That's kind of "The Solars" after all, and "The Solars" is Exalted's heart. With a dash of JRPG style "Heroes of Light" chosen of the crystals / spirits / "the planet".

                                                  The less core inspirations have a similar gritty mileau but are more focused on "ordinary people" (though still 'heroic' and able to do some beyond real human stylish action but not supernatural, like Spike or Nausicaa), or supernatural people in a world that's either more "ordinary fantasy" or "ordinary modern" world (like Slayers, SFII). Mostly pulp adventure stories; dynastic and ruling stuff is not so tightly in focus.

                                                  Common traits are where character designs are often the focus and is are detailed and exaggerated; oversized weapons, huge and stylized attacks, strong silhouettes with exaggerated proportions, lots of detail in character design and detail in character design (possibly with too many belts and scars and such!) is more important than fluidity in animation. There's probably not *too* much animation either and it's more like a literal translation of a manga into animation (as in, you can see what the panels would be, rather that the shots are using the medium to do things that wouldn't translate well to a series of static panels).

                                                  All fairly old; if there's newer stuff that meets the same sort of criterion, then that's workable, I just don't know about it!
