        One of the features I loved about the new approach to MM and artifacts is the alloying idea. Mix properties to get new awesome effects. Like how Volcano cutter has Red and White jade to get molten earth, and Orichalcum to amp up the power. (And yes, it really does have 3 MM in it) Dragon-Blooded weapons can absolutely have Orichalcum in them, because it's a power amplifier among its other attributes.

        Also, I'm fairly confident that the Arms book will at LEAST detail how the different exalts get different things out of certain weapons. Like I imagine it'll discuss what the limits of Sidereal Evocations are compared to Dragon-Blooded. Or Lunars compared to Solars. I'd be VERY shocked if it didn't.
