    Occult:

    Light Without Darkness
    The Solar lays her hand on a ghost's brow and blazes her anima, burning away the ghost's darkness. From that moment onward, the ghost is no longer considered a creature of darkness. It can survive without penalty in either the Underworld, shadowlands, or Creation, but it does not respire motes in Creation unless it is within the area of influence of a manse attuned to the Solar whose blessing it received.

            Sail:

            Western Wind's Guidance
            The Solar places her flagship at the head of her fleet and pulls the tide and wind in her wake. This charm allows the Solar to command a number of unmanned ships equal to her Essence to follow her flagship. So long as flagship succeeds its sail actions, the unmanned ships will follow successfully. This charm does not allow the unmanned ships to fight.

                    Craft:

                    Eternal Art Contingency
                    If a mundane object crafted by the Solar is every destroyed, she may replace it with a backup copy. As the Solar was already prepared for this contingency, she does not need to spend any additional funds on materials and the reconstruction itself is accelerated. Small objects can be replaced instantly, while larger objects such as fortifications take up to a day to replace.

                        Survival:

                        Green and White Trapper's Noose
                        The Solar scouts out an area in preparation for a battle and activates this charm to set a trap. So long as this charm is active, the trap is "suspended." Upon deactivating the charm, the Solar selects a target and makes an opposed Wits + Survival roll against the target's Perception + Awareness. If the Solar succeeds, the target is affected by the trap.

                            Awareness:

                            Eastern Echo Mapping
                            She smashes her heel against the floor and uses the sound's echo to map out a building. Upon activating this charm, the Solar rolls Perception + Awareness. Every success allows her to learn one fact about the building's architecture or inhabitants. For a small apartment, two successes are enough to obtain both a map and learn the number of people inside, but such information costs more successes for considerably larger structures.
