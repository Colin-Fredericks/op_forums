    King between Rats Camouflage
    Lunar Charisma

    The Lunar spendsa majority of the scene among several people (in a bar, in a theater, in a crowd) and subtely infuses them with mercurial essence that resonates with the Lunar's personality. This adds an internal environmental penalty equal to the group's magnitude to anyone trying to pick the Lunar out of the crowd.

    If discovered (either as an impostor, as a thief, through a failed Stealth roll, etc.), this charm allows the Lunar to treat the crowd as having a Major or Defining intimacy of loyalty towards him. They will do whatever they can to help the Lunar make an escape or a stand. For example they could help by distracting his accusors, blocking their paths, throwing tomatoes at them or helping the Lunar hide; though this charm cannot force anyone to knowingly put themselves in mortal danger.

    Though the crowd doesn't actually develop a hive-mind, they are more prone to act in unison, such as everyone turning simultaneously to stare down the accusor, or arranging their clothes in a manner similar to the Lunar to create red-herrings.

    Commitment to this charm lapses as soon as the crowd disperses or the scene in which the Lunar was discovered ends (whichever comes first), but is otherwise indefinite.

            Wheat-as-Gold Craftsmanship

            Solar Craft

            The true craftsman knows that 'spinning wheat into gold' is just a metaphor - every masterpiece of art or craft is in truth a labour of many hands, who provide the materials and tools for the craftsman to create it, in a chain that might have dozens of links. No master's work or no man's wealth can ever come to pass without this, sometimes vast, network. One can only create true beauty and peerless function, and obtain true wealth, by recognizing it.

            The Charm allows the Solar to meditate upon the task before him and picture the most efficient ways of obtaining materials, resources, tools and labour required for it. He gains a supernatural insight into obtaining the best possible components of the project and all the efforts to obtain them. She cannot be fooled by shoddy materials or unskilled labour being passed as high-quality, and will be granted an intuition as to obstacles that may appear. However, this Charm does not prevent random, unforeseen events from interfering.

                The Riddle of Steel
                This artifact buckler has a scripture engraved on it in an unknown language.
                The scripture is a riddle addressed to the Titans and to Creation itself. When activated (as a simple action, or reflexively during a Defend or Defend Other action), its writing glows and creates an area of effect within which objects harden and slowly, over the course of [some time], gain the properties of iron or steel (weilder's choice).
                Generally this increases those items' hardness and soak. In the case of iron, the bonuses are smaller, but it has the useful property of keeping the fairie folk at bay.

                With [extra cost] the weilder can also target individuals, increasing their soak (if he does so, his own soak increases in the same way for no cost).

                The effect's AoE, speed and Soak/Hardness bonus scale with the weilder's essece, and works on items already made of steel or iron when its bonus would surpass their normal stats.

                            Wearing the Lion's Mane
                            Liminal Charisma
                            With this permanent Charm, one of the Chernozem may derive further benefit from pieces of any animals she has added to herself. She may enhance her social actions, if she treats any cultural or personal attitudes others hold toward any such animals as though they applied to her. If others can observe these animal parts, they may likewise benefit from this enhancement when applying such attitudes toward her. If an animal part is meaningfully incorporated as part of the Liminal's stunt, this enhancement improves.

                            ... Kidding.

                            Hunting Nightstalker Presence
                            Lunar Awareness
                            The Lunar opens his eyes and ears to the nightmares that prowl like hungry predatory beasts, and may interact with them as such. They are simple entities, viewing dreams and a good night's rest as their prey. The Lunar may attempt to engage in social actions with them or attack them as though they were physical. If he destroys or dissuades them from affecting himself or anyone else, such sleepers receive a bonus toward a good night's rest (for the purposes of Willpower and mote recovery, etc.). On the contrary, some Lunars find it a great advantage to drive the hunters toward less favored sleepers, giving their enemies penalties to the same.

                            Under most cases, nightmares are relatively few and trivial opponents, simple to drive off for easier prey. However, circumstance plays a heavy role. Such creatures are more numerous and fiercer in dangerous places, and sleepers experiencing fear, depression, or other mental anguish prove more alluring to them. They likewise become more dangerous if the Lunar himself is currently frightened. This Charm also allows the Lunar to perceive and interact with dream-influencing magic effects as though they were physical. This may include interrupting another dreamer's dealings with Makarios (and even harming the demon) or fending off some strange raksha oneirochorous disease.

                                        Soul-Wrecking Higanbana Field
                                        Abyssal Socialize

                                        It is said flowers that bloom near grave sites guide the dearly departed to their next incarnations. The deathknight's blossoms have somewhat different aims. Near a corpse or in a graveyard, the seeds of a flower associated with death begin to sprout shortly after the Abyssal sows them. All souls nearby that have not yet passed on have a significantly increased chance of lingering as ghosts. This chance increases if the Abyssal takes plains to plant them carefully and tend to them for at least a day. Onlookers who hold personal or cultural associations of death for the flower may find themselves moved by its beauty. Such onlookers lose some aversion and unease typically felt toward the dead and deathly things, and have an increased likelihood of leaving a ghost upon death. These effects fade after a number of days equal to the Abyssal's Essence, or linger as long as the individual possesses a positive Intimacy for the blossoms.

                                            Wolf Den Hospitality

                                            Lunar Socialize

                                            While it lacks the comforts of the city, there is a cozy warmth to the den of a mother wolf. For any matter in which the Lunar does not need to engage in social combat, she is looked on favorably by those she interacts with within her territory. During social combat with any guest within her lands, the Lunar may choose to add her essence to any roll that does not already incorporate it. She may also pay 1wp in order to create an intimacy of her choice in those she speaks with for one scene. This intimacy must be related to the territory the target is currently within. If the Lunar's territory is a forest, for example, the intimacy must be related to the forest. Its wildlife, a river within it, even the Lunar herself, but it must relate to the territory.

                                                            Evocation (Harmonious Storm - paired short daiklaves, each is one part Starmetal alloyed with two parts Blue Jade)

                                                            - clashing the blades brings a mist and resonant song to the area, obscuring others' views and subtly cuing the wielder on when to best move and act.
