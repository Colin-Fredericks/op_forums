    The thing I have always wanted to play since 3rd edition came out is a socialize supernal Zenith with the persona charms, to allow you to become those whose intimacies you have taken on, but alas Socialize is not caste for Zeniths. I think on balance that the caste-only probably makes for the best default rule to prevent things that could be abusive (twilight survival shenanigans) or otherwise problematic (Nobody cares about playing a dawn if other castes can get their stuff). However since this is a game with actual people we can chat with STs about it and hopefully work it out. Most of the concepts I have seen in this thread seem fine to me and I would allow as an ST, but I'm glad the rule is in place and deviating from it is optional rather than the other way around.
