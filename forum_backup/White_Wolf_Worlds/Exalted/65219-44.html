                    Crossposted from the Greater Elemental Dragons and why thread:

                    I've always felt that the reason Elementsl Dragons go full Kukla when they hit Essence: 10 was related to the Primordials' destruction of the original Great Elementals in the early part of the Primordial War.

                    Some background, not often mentioned since 1st Ed.: the Incarnae, inspired by Gaia's Five Elemental Dragon offspring, created the Great Elementals as one of their first autonomous acts after the Primordials gave them the keys to Creation and told them to start running it. Oceku, Urwl, Mindful Forest, the Wind Master, and the Great Garda were pretty much the only elementals in existence, and had commensurately great power; the Great Garda, for example, was powerful and organized enough to be in charge of all fires everywhere, both terrestrially and celestially.

                    When the gods first rebelled, the Primordials responded in predictable fashion: They looked at what the gods had made, and smashed it to bits. By annihilating the five super elementals, not only did they remove a powerful weapon that could be used against them, they put Creation into a serious tailspin, forcing the Incarnae and the gods to patch things up, instead of fighting. With the Exalted to press the war though, the gods were able to perform some emergency repairs.

                    The immense amounts of Essence the Incarnae had invested in the Great Elementals had been scattered, coalescing into lesser gods whose form and function reflected some aspect of the being it had previously belonged to. In damage control mode, Gaia sent the Five Elemental Dragons to wrangle these disparate lesser gods into line, creating the first Elemental Courts. Over time, dribs and drabs of OG Elemental Essence sprouted into lesser elementals, whose late arrival and often low intelligence contributed to the bigoted attitudes about them that still persist.

                    SO! I think that when a Lesser Elemental Dragon reaches the pinnacle of enlightenment (Essence: 10), it suddenly realizes the commonality of sentience that its element once had. When the Kukla hit Ess. 10, its insight evolved to the point where it understood and perceived all earth as one being, drawing upon the power of all earth at the same instant that it attempted to reassemble the mind common to all earth, the mind of Urwl. But since the Essence and duties of Urwl were now spread amongst nigh-infinite numbers of Gemlords, Terraserpents, Mercury Ant hives, Kri, Jokun, etc, attempting to encompass all those minds was impossible, even at the Kukla's vastly increased level of power. His sentience shattered, and he became Creation's doomsday device/reset button.

                    Tl; dr: When Lesser Elemental Dragons hit Ess: 10, they tap into the might and mind of the first Great Elementals. Since the Great Elementals only exist as all the lesser elementals nowadays, tapping into their minds drives them super-crazy, even as they become super-powerful.

                                    There is no Great Curse, and the "Curse" does not come from the Neverborn, or at least any Neverborn that yet exist. When the Exaltations were first forged, Autochthon excised a portion of his own diseased Essence, and infused it into the crucible. This is what granted the Exalted the power to kill the Primordials, but the Essence of the Engine of Extinction sits at the heart of all the Exalted, twisting them as it does Autochthon. However, those few Exalts who have discovered this, and researched the possibility of removing it, also discovered that if they could remove the curse, they would also remove the power to slay beings such as the Primordials, an eventuality that would surely embolden the Yozi. The Neverborn desire this power, and its destruction, for it was this that placed them in their current state. It is for this reason that they have absorbed the Essence of the Engine of Extinction from those Exalts they control into themselves, an act that has shifted their sarcophagi-temple bodies imperceptibly closer to Oblivion.
