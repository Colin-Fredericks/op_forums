                    I have a question about Reflexive Crash Attacks;

                    This happened in a little white-room fight I just rolled out with a player.

                    Melee Solar goes first in this round. Announces an 'Excellent Strike'+'Melee Excellency' Combo.
                    Brawl Solar announces 'Thunderclap Rush Attack'.
                    Melee Solar can't oppose the new reflexive attack without a charm with the clash keyword, so he adds 'Fervent Blow' to his list of charms.

                    'Fervent Blow' creates a reflexive clash that can oppose 'Thunderclap Rush', but also doesn't count as the Melee Solar's combat action, the two solars roll off, the Melee Solar wins the clash, delivering a decisive attack against the Brawl Solar, deals damage and then his Initiative resets.... and then his actual combat action for the round begins to resolve?

                    The Melee Solar then makes the Withering attack he initially decided on with 'Fervent Blow' and 'Melee Excellency' (paying the cost again) - his attack strikes, drops the Brawl Solar's Initiative while gaining the result for his own Initiative track, setting off the conditions for 'One Weapon, Two blows' - Another decisive attack ends the fight.

                    It's that bold bit there that has me puzzled. By a strict reading of the rules it seems totally legit, but it seems odd. Cool, but odd.

                        So the solution is don't attack a Solar with Fervent Blow while he has a weapon and Willpower. Because Fervent Blow is an Essence 3 Solar Charm that costs Willpower (and the Supernal Ability making it available at Essence 1 does not change that), it is allowed to be that powerful.
