                    Here's my initial draft of a replacement mechanic for the Tell that uses carrot instead of stick, tries to get you to actively buy into it despite its downsides a la Infernals, and also acts as a built-in low-key mechanic for chimera weirdness at high Essence.

                    Warp and Weft:

                    When using this optional rule, reduce bonus points at character creation to 12 and remove the Tell (Lunars, p. XX), including the ability to associate a mutation with the Tell to apply it to all forms. Instead, at character creation or upon Exalting, a Lunar may choose to gain up to (Essence × 2) dots of mutations. Each time the Lunar's Essence increases, they may choose to add or upgrade mutations up to this total (even if they had no mutations before). The chosen mutations must either reflect the Lunar's spirit shape in some way (or shapes, with Chimera-Soul Expression) or have been gained temporarily through magic at least once. Each of the chosen mutations automatically gains the Warp merit.

                    Warp (0)—Purchased

                    Prerequisite: Lunar Exalt, one or more permanent mutations.
                    Keywords: Stackable

                    Each time you take this merit, choose a different permanent mutation you have. That mutation applies to all of your forms, can't be removed even with potent magic like Wholeness-Restoring Meditation, and can't benefit from the Subtlety merit (Exalted, p. 166) or from magic that directly hides mutations (including Lunar Charms like Moon's Hidden Face).

                    Each time you change form, you can attempt to hide one or more of your Warp mutations. This is treated as a reflexive disguise attempt (Exalted, p. 224), with a bonus of (Willpower + Essence) and a penalty of (total mutation dots hidden), but you always roll the average number of successes. Charms can supplement this disguise attempt as normal. As long as you get at least one success, the chosen mutations are hidden similarly to the Subtlety merit, but observers who succeed against the disguise attempt notice or deduce the existence of the hidden mutations.

                    If a mutation you choose for this merit is one that you have also selected for an effect like Hybrid Body Transformation, you may immediately re-choose a different temporary mutation or mutations with the same dot total.

I guess any kind of scene in which a Lunar goes through a number of human forms in order to achieve something in short order is right out, or at least extremely awkward and difficult.
