        Originally posted by JohnDoe244 View Post
        Sure, the "problem" is finding the Exalted combat system boring.

        I don't have that problem, I think it's super-amazing cool. Easily the best combat system Exalted has ever had.

        But people say it's boring and repetative and they want epic battles and not to win with nova strikes.

        You can pick up the start of the conversation: here.
        And a saliant description of the "problem": here and here.
        So I'll give my personal perspectives that are pretty simple about my problem with this game concerning the fighting part. I'll try to cut it in different parts but some are linked to the others I guess. Some are inherent to the game, some not. None of these necessary turned me off the game, but with all of them I had at least one argument with my players because there were no simple middle-ground we could find that felt fair for everyone.

        1) Not enough tools for the ST. This one is pretty simple, everyone always talks about it, we don't have enough tools for the ST to create and customize fun, personal and challenging enemies for my characters. Hopefully Crucible will answer this. It took me 2 years before I did a challenge that I believe was really entertaining from the "fighting gameplay perspective", meaning the outcome of the fight was not obvious after the first 2 turns, they possessed a wide range of different abilities that sollicitated many of the characters different skills as well as the creativity of their players, there was different "objectives" and reversals during the fight, and they won as they should but ended up exhausted, with one character death on the last phase. I obviously did some fights that were interesting before this one, but I never found them interesting from the gameplay perspective.

        2) The system is overall too complex with too many exceptions and subsystems. You obviously get used to it with practice and I'm fine with that on the long term, and I don't consider the basic system is too complex (with Initiative and withering and stuff), but you quickly have a player asking "Can I grapple this guy ?", "How does mounted work already ?" "Can I use X Charms under Z circumstances ? I'm not sure" "I try to intimidate this Battle Group" and often the ways to handle this are not straightforward.

        3) Guess this could go into 1) as well but you will often have "I'd like to fight with an Ice elemental to protect me from the lava god, Bob would like to have his blood ape fight with him, and Alice has her battle group of normal human, but she as well trained and convinced a battle group of war ghost to help us so ..." and suddenly you are like 8v5 in a too complex fight to deal with properly. So I ended up applying your advice which is to ignore fighters too weak to participate but then people asked me "What's the use of summoning stuff if I can't use them in fights because they suck" - "Well ...". Now I'm regrouping all the players summons into a Battle group with stats depending of the quality of the summons but it's not such an easy decision to take.

        4) I like the Initiative system but there are still things I'm still trying to work out about how I feel about them :
        a) Starting initiative can go very high, and not even counting for Charms like TAP things can be very frustrating even for other player that took time to have combat abilities and stuff because someone will basically start the fight by destroying everything, so there was a lot of momentum and setup for everyone and only one person get to do anything. It's obviously linked to 1) because at first you don't have that much defensive tools for your NPC's and cheating doesn't bother me too much as a ST as long as it doesn't negate a player's impact (which it does in this case).

        b) We discuss already how I don't like that the system doesn't discourage going to high initiative because it just delays the inevitable but is a little pointless. I guess you can say "Maybe at this point just let the PC's win" and I'd answer "Yeah or maybe at this point just do a fucking decisive attack because that's the purpose of the system".

        c) There are some weird interactions with spells (could go into 2) I guess) where some spells consume Initiative, some not, some work on Willpower, some etc ...

        d) I find the game loses in dynamism at a certain level of Soak, and there are some too easy tools to acquire good Soak at start, which will create weird situations when if you try to adapt a bit as a ST to match the Soak of the Soak player you'll end up gaining 20 initiative on anyone else that you attack.

        I may (or not) come with some other points but I guess these are the major ones that bugged me at least once when playing the game, and I found no easy answer where everyone felt it was the fair and good solution.

            I agree with Chausse with a lot of stuff.

            1-ST tools. I don't have nearly enough monsters and enemies with just Core + WFHW. Yes HDNP is out there but i dont wanna buy 30 pdfs one by one to get monsters. There's one premade adventure that I'm aware of, and it's for not-even-starting strength characters. Compared to DnD which has, like, 8. There isn't anything about how to ST or common plots or how to play Dynasts politically. The game is very "here's 600 pages of rules, DIY"

            2- Same. I need a flowchart to figure out combat, and then we upgraded to another flowchart which I still use, after 2 years and about 25 sessions. Battlegroups especially are hard to use.

            3- Its so ridiculously easy to acquire battlegroups, followers, bodyguards, demons, etc that each player has effectively 2 characters minimum. My Eclipse has a pirate crew that get a massive Health bonus from sail and war charms, my Twilight has a Survival pumped Otter God (mechanically a River Dragon), My Zenith "just" has a Fire Caste Dynast bodyguard. The Dawn has 3 Tyrant Lizards. THREE. Don't even get me started on all their dumb artifacts which are weird changes to combat. I mean, I love them and I designed half of them, but each one has its own resource to track and shit.

            4- TAP and others are insanely, over the top good. My Night Caste can and does start combat with 20+ initiative from Stealth and Larceny, and it's impossible to even attack him without two different abilities, so he's free to get to Ini. 40 before nova-ing somebody right to hell. And that's without TAP. High Soak and Hardness also make fights an incredible slog. there's very little you can do to a Resistance/heavy artifact armor user. Brawl Grapple by itself is countered almost entirely by Brawl Grapple and when my Eclipse gets his hands on someone I basically just ask him how he's gonna kill them.

            Please be warned: this is not champagne, this is most likely a duck.
