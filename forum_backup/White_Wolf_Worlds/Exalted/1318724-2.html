            Hm, here's a brainstorm my own. My proposal and take for the Nocturnals history written up the style of splats intros, albeit roughly. Critiques and thoughts are welcome.

            Originally posted by History of the Nocturnal Exalted
            In the Time Before, long before the Incarnae raised up their champions for war. It has been said that there once existed a God, now lost. His name was Nox, God of Possibilities, Incarna of the Night Sky. Who was the God of Night, none can say. They say he was many things, brother of the Five Maidens, emissary of the world’s makers, traitor to the Incanae, a mad broken god, all and none of the above. Nox is defined by mystery, by contradiction, by paradox. He is a god long gone, broken and bound. Only remembered by the mark he left on Creation. Of his Chosen

            Like their patron, the origins of the nature of the Nocturnal Exalted is also a mystery, while they were uplifted from the ranks of mortals like the other champions of the gods, whether they rose to support the Gods or rose to tear them down, the answer has been lost to time. What can be certain is the during the War of the Gods the Nocturnal Host was split, fighting on both sides on conflict and that on the defeated was laid down a mighty curse to help bound the fluctuating nature of the Night's Chosen, to help support the Exalted Host rather then be against it.

            In the time of the First Age, the Harlequins drifted through the courts of the Exalted. Bound by pact, they never took power for themselves, but rather went on to serve the interests of others. They danced, fought, and entertained for the masses of Creation and its Exalted lords as marvelous and mysterious courtesans, gladiators, and jesters. They reveled in both in the whispers of the rumored and the glories of the spectacular, living as walking, breathing, laughing contradictions. The Chosen of the Night weren’t slaves however, bound as they might be. For they are as much capable in bringing revolution and coup d'etat against the wicked as they are in bringing about wonders and revelations to the joyful. Where the Nocturnals came, change quickly followed in their steps.

            On the eve of the Usurpation, from observations on the behaviors of their masters and patrons, as well as benefiting from some leaked correspondences from the Sidereal Exalted, who often served as partners, minders, and companions to the Nocturnals, the Nocturnal Exalted were far to aware of the coming tension between the Exalted Host. Seeing themselves trapped by chains of loyalty and oath that threatened to bring themselves against each other once again, in their desperation the Nocturnal Host opted to abandon the coming conflict and all their obligations with it. Through mighty magic and sorcery, the Nocturnals broke open the Gates of Night and in mass escaped to the broken form of the God of Night Sky. Thus they disappeared from the face of the world, leaving only legends and folktales.

            In coming centuries, few Nocturnals left the bounds of the Firmament. Once again becoming embroiled in the schemes and intrigues of the Exalted, of the wars of the Shogunate and Silver Pack. Others choose to ignore the kingdoms and realms of the other Chosen, instead wandering among mortals across the Threshold, becoming heroes and tyrants without abandoned as they tied their interests to the plights and loves of those that caught their eye. As a whole though, the Nocturnal Host secluded themselves among the broken stars of the Firmament, living in revelry and celebration in the Caelian City, the heart of the Firmament and a city that ever changes. In those days, they kept to themselves willfully abandoning Creation as they distracted themselves with pleasures and dreams unimagined, bound to no one.

            Now in the Times of Tumult, things are starting to change. Mysterious self-proclaimed Heralds have rose from among Nocturnals, their motives obscure and origins a mystery. They say they speak in enigmas, claiming to hear the voice of Nox, their natures frequently shifting. They speak out amongst the Night's Chosen, saying that the time has come to return to Creation, that the future is in flux and a new era comes. New possibilities have appeared they say, new chances, and its time for the Nocturnals to claim it. To make themselves a new future. The Heralds speak, and many listen.

                Hm, here's a brainstorm my own. My proposal and take for the Nocturnals history written up the style of splats intros, albeit roughly. Critiques and thoughts are welcome.
                So, some thoughts about the section-

                First, I do like the obscure approach to the Nocturnal history. It is well consistent with the other books, and it does fit 3e's writing style, so well done for that.

                As for the Curse- I would just want to note that while I do like the idea of it, as I've mentioned before, I think it would benefit much from not tying the servitude of the Nocturnals to Exalted only. The idea of the curse would be to insure loyalty, and the Solars would have utilized it by making themselves so much important in the life of the Nocturnals so that they would be compelled to obey them. I do like the way you portray the 1st age Harlequins and their function in the Old Realm.

                Some issue with the isolation of the Nocturnals- while it does gives an explanation for where the Nocturnals have been through all of this time, the outcome is that the vast majority of Nocturnals have done practically nothing in the time they spent on the Firmament. Spending all of their existence with celebrations is kinda boring after a few generations- the Nocturnals should be more active, either establishing their presence upon an alien world where their god is bound and whispering, or taking an active part in the life of Creation. Also, there is an issue that they have no reason to return to Creation, other than the Heralds telling them to. I suggest to both develop what the Nocturnals actually did in the Firmament, and to strength the ties between Creation and the Firmament- that would help to different them from the Alchemicals, which also live on a separate world until they suddenly return to Creation. Creation is a wide world, returning Nocturnals could do a lot of things in it without being noticed for something other than Exigents. Not to mention that as Harlequins, it kinda goes against their theme to stay outside of Creation without any "master to serve". I do like the distinction between a "servant" and a "slave" and the writeup, which is an important one.

                The writeup also lacks a very important point- connection to the Sidereal Exalted. The Nocturnals are the companion splat to the Sidereals, after all- they should be dominant in their history as much as the Solars are dominant in the Lunars. While we are still working on the exact relationships the two splats would have, removing the Sidereal- Nocturnal connection would harm the identity of the Nocturnals, IMO.

                Finally, we should establish what the Heralds are and where they come from. I think that a nice twist would be that instead of making the Heralds those Nocturnals which have sided with the gods in the Divine Revolution, they would be those which have sided with the Primordials. After the war was ended, the Exalted Hosts have decided that they could not trust those strange Exalts- those who turned against them were executed and locked back in the Firmament, while those who supported them were bound to serve, so to make sure they will not betray them (something which didn't quit worked, as Nox's nature twisted the curse from being bound to their Exalted, to be bound to their passions). Perhaps with the opening of the Gates of Night, the Heralds were freed and connected back to Creation, and so while the Harlequins were establishing their presence upon the Firmament the Heralds have traveled Creation, hiding their existence from their siblings while working on plans of their own.

                So in short, the main criticisms I have are that the Nocturnals need to do more (remember that one of the criticisms about the Lunars in 1e was that they just sat in the Wyld and were doing nothing, IIRC), they need a good connection to the Sidereals and they need a clarification about who are the Heralds and where they have been through all of this time. The writeup should also clarify the nature of the Curse, and how it has effected the life of the Nocturnals in the First Age.

                                Iceblade's idea of the Nocturnals exiling themselves into the Firmament gave me an idea for an alternate origin :
                                Whatever the Night King was, he was but one of many within the Firmament. He ruled over a court of fate-warping half-mad beings just like him, whose whims, if left unchecked, would warp the Firmament, which in turn would introduce fate anomalies into Creation. Their origins are just as unexplained as Nox's own, with some scholars even theorizing that they are all, in fact, different versions of Nox, but no proof exists that would confirm this theory.

                                Because every being in this Brotherhood of Night was equally mad and power hungry, none was ever able to rise above his siblings. Realizing he could not rule over his siblings through force, Nox instead created the Harlequins to infiltrate their courts and become their trusted servants, placing his Chosen in a position from which they could shepherd the unwitting beings of Night without them noticing.

                                All known records of Nox cease around the time the Nocturnals appeared. It is unclear whether creating the Nocturnals used up all of Nox's power or whether his brothers found out his trickery and exacted revenge, or something else entirely. His plan did work however, as the Harlequins slowly integrated themselves into the chaotic politics of the Firmaments and began stewarding the Night Sky from behind their mask of servitude.

                                To this day, most Nocturnals have their hands full keeping the Night Sky in one piece, making their visits upon Creation relatively rare.
                                And here's an idea for the Sidereal Bond :
                                The bond between Sidereal and Nocturnal forms silently over countless starry nights, without the two parties ever actually meeting. A compatibility between their Essence makes it so that Sidereals astrologists sometimes attune themselves to a Nocturnal over many readings and contemplations of the Night Sky, with either side only rarely actually noticing the bond's creation.

                                Once bonded, a Nocturnal acts as a Sidereal's guiding star, their Essence of possibilities bringing the Sidereal new insights and greater creativity. At the other end of this bond, the Nocturnal benefits from the patient and orderly Essence of their Sidereal mate, stabilizing their whimsical and chaotic urges when they would lead to terrible consequences.

                                Over many incarnations, a Sidereal/Nocturnal pair gains a sense of familiarity with this strange bond, cultivating the sense that someone out there is looking out for them even though they rarely if ever meet each other. The first meeting of a Sidereal/Nocturnal pair is said to be an especially transcendent experience, as though they had finally found a person they had been looking for not only their whole life but in fact for several lifetimes.

                                    So let's break apart what we have about the history of the Nocturnals-

                                    - The Divine Revolution, the gods win, out of honor and respect the Nocturnal Host which has supported the gods pledge loyalty to them, and with that their Essence twists and shifts into forming the Curse of Subservience. The Nocturnals which have fought against the gods, however, are judged to death by the Solars. I suggest that the Nocturnals which served the gods beg for mercy for their siblings, and the Sidereals which convinced the rest of the Exalted to choose choose exile over execution- for the war was bloody enough without forcing the Nocturnals to see their kin dies. As a result, the rogue Nocturnals are banished beyond the Gates of Night and into the Firmament. That was when the division between the Heralds and the Harlequins was formed.

                                    - Through the First Age, the Nocturnals worked in the background of the rest of the Host, using their talents in order to magnify and support the Exalts, just like the sky contain and serves as the background for the luminaries which shine through it. Due to the pledge, the Nocturnals have became servants of the courts of the Exalted- but never slaves, as they chose their own masters and helped them to show what possibilities the future had for them, and how great their light could shine. Some Nocturnals may have even made small kingdoms around them, becoming guardian spirits for whole nations as they worked to bring fourth the future only they could see. Dropping some names for the miracles and wonders which the Nocturnals have managed to establish would help to establish the greatness of the Nocturnals in the setting. That should also include details about how the hosts of the Sidereals and the Nocturnals have started to draw closer- as both Exalted have worked to shape the future, both from without (the Sidereals) and from within (Nocturnals). It should also include a number of events where the two hosts worked together and what they have achieved. Also, this section should name drop instances when the Nocturnals went against the wished of the Old Realm due to their unexpected nature.

                                    - The coming of the Usurpation. Instead of them predicting what is going to come due to "observing the behaviors" of the Solars, I would instead move it to be completely the result of their ties with the Sidereals. As the Harlequins are compelled to serve, we should also note that as the light of the First Age has started to fall and the Solars fell victim to their hubris, the Nocturnals were enforced to constantly fight over control against their mad patrons- twisting their own words against them and showing them not the future that they wanted, but the future that they needed, as a warning. When the Usurpation has been decided, the Sidereals have tried to keep the Nocturnals in the dark as they knew that if they couldn't decide between two possibilities, adding the endless possibilities that the Nocturnals saw in their dreams would not help. When the Bronze Faction assassinated the Gold Faction, the Nocturnals were their next targets- yet many of the Sidereals could break the bond and warned their bound partners to hide their presence. As the Nocturnal Host debated what to do, many have been is favor of finding escape beyond the Gates of Night, even though some have decided to stay by the side of their patrons- even if it meant to die beside them. As such, in the darkest night when even the stars didn't shine, the Nocturnals broke the seal upon the Gates of Night and left for the Firmament.

                                    - the time period known as the Exodus, when the Nocturnals of the Firmament have stumbled upon the strange civilizations of mutated humans and other beings which live beyond the Gates of Night. There, they have started to establish their own civilizations and cultures, build around the Caelian City. In the Firmament, they discovered desolated palaces and abandoned temples, all seem to have been built by the Heralds of ages past yet their creators were nowhere to be found. Fighting against the strange denizens of the Firmament and being so close to the touch of Nox for the first time, the Harlequins had to find a place of their own upon the desolated heaven- some have became jesters and servants of the so called "lords of the Firmament", twisting their wishes and turning them into puppet rulers, or perhaps even serving them truthfully just as they did in the First Age. On the same time, those Harlequins which have stayed in Creation had their own issues to deal with- being reincarnated all alone in the wilderness, the Sidereals have kept a close watch to try and find signs for new Nocturnals in order to guide them and make sure they would stay out of the way- or, if they were not willing to listen, to brand them as Anathema and send the Wyld Hunt after them. Mentioning a few cool things they could have done during this time could be awesome. The Great Contagion and the Balorian Crusade, of course, would destroy anything of value which the Nocturnals have made in Creation, and the beginning of the Realm would change everything.

                                    - During the rise of the Realm, the Nocturnals have established their own civilization upon the Firmament. Caelian City became the center of their culture, the only place where there were no masters and no servants. But around the Firmament, they served the local powers and brought them into greatness, while fighting the strange terrors which lurked beyond the Gates of Night. Around this time, they have started once more to maintain contact with their siblings in Creation, and discovered the harsh condition they had to deal with. Working together, the Nocturnals have started to tighten their relationship with Creation, reaching beyond the Gates of Night to introduce a new future to those who had no hope, and find possibilities in the heart of despair. As whole, the Nocturnals have tried to stay neutral in the Lunar- Realm conflict, but a few always found themselves being drawn to one of the sides, offering their talents under cover in order to bring fourth the future those people dreamed of. Meanwhile a few Nocturnals have even found themselves in Yu Shan, following the millennia old bond and try and help Heaven to see there is another future than the one they saw- yet the Bronze Faction has grew suspicious toward their companions, and only a few Nocturnals have returned to serve among them. Once and awhile, a Nocturnal may even throw their lot with a new Exalted Solar, but the Wyld Hunt have usually put an end to such companionship.

                                    - the Time of Tumult has began. A new Solar warlord, the Bull of the North, brought ruin upon the Realm. The Empress is gone, and no one knows to where she went. A civil war is brewing in the Imperial City, while the Lunars bar their fangs against their ancient enemies. A corpse mountain rose upon Thorns, bringing the dead from the Underworld. The Solars returned, and with them came their dark reflections- the strange Abyssals with all of their macabre glory. Ancient Sidereal has declared a war against Heaven, and brought with him strange Exalted- ones which are both familiar and strange to the Nocturnals, in many ways like kin and in others... something else. The status que has been breached, and Nox's laughter rings all across the Firmament and whispers in the minds of his Chosen. As the Harlequins debate what to do, strange entities come to the Firmament, claiming their ancient thrones and gathering their armies against Caelian City and Creation alike. The Heralds have returned, and with them a single message- join us to bring a future free from masters, or die among those you chose to serve.

                                    The future seems gloom- but who if not the Night's Chosen could find a new possibility which no one could expect?
