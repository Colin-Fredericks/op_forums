Something like, Breeding is a static property, set at your conception, that sets your exaltation chance as well as your progenitive essence "cap" and "recharge rate", and is based on the strength of your parent's progenitive essence at the time of conception. So, low-Breeding parents still have low-Breeding kids (because their essence only ever gets so strong), but high-Breeding parents can also have low-Breeding kids (if they make babies faster than their essence recharges).

                                                            Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post
                                                            If they have children when their Essence is spent, those children inherit barely anything, and what they pass down will be equally insignificant. Even if the parents are well-bred.
                                                            I believe that this specifically is not correct in 3E (note that I'm on the same page as you otherwise). On page 98 they specifically mention for having back to back kids, the 'leftover children', that "Even if she Exalts, the stigma remains to a lesser extent, with her bloodline baselesly considered inferior to that of her more esteemed kin." They then follow up saying that mortals always have the same chance, no matter what. From this information we can deduce that the Exalt's bloodline is the only variable factor going on here (it does say anything about if you say took two mortals from an otherwise peerless bloodline, and they had a child who Exalted and what that Exalts bloodline would look like).

                                                            A quick reading of the paragraph following that could also be a source of confusion, since it notes that the progenitive potency passes on at conception, but only completely drains the parents. Honestly re-reading the section a couple times now, it's a bit vague, but with that quote above about the bloodline being diminished as baseless, I lean towards what is passed down is always same, as long as you Exalt to enjoy the benefit. I'll bow my head if the devs have addressed it specifically elsewhere, although for my game I prefer this interpretation. Having large swaths of common sense/knowledge being dead wrong adds to the world for me!

                                                            As an aside, I worked out a calculator for chance to Exalt DB based on all the various information given and with the following assumptions you get a bit more than a doubling of DB potential every 2 generations (this is actually really slow population growth compared to say humans, and i say potential because some might not have exalted yet when that generation marker passes):

                                                            A generation happens every 12 years (pg 99, 12-20 years for maximum chance)
                                                            The progenitive curve is sigmoidal
                                                            Strong bloodline is 1.6x more likely than baseline to exalt and compose 25% of DBs
                                                            Weak bloodline is 0.4x less likely than baseline to exalt and compose 25% of DBs

                                                            The above analysis is kind of rosey, and I would personally whack it down to a 1.5x increase of the DB population ever 2 "generations" to take into account things like sleeping around too much, early deaths, etc etc. But even with that, within ~150 years you'd go from 10k DB to ~113k DB. It is not at all unreasonable that there were literally millions of DB when the great contagion came around and killed almost everyone, followed by the Fae Invasion.

                                                            As a final note, please only do math like this if its actually important to some point or part of your story. The best answer for how many DB there are is "as many as are needed for your group's story to work".
