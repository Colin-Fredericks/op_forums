                        Hmm... Lunars... vaguely romantic Lunar-y shaped notions (or phrases) which appeal to me are -

                        Being able to choose your body.

                        Being at one with nature.

                        (and getting strength from that, in a deeper and more complete sense than mere Survival Charms offer you).

                        Endure. Survive. Adapt. Outlast. Win.

                        Oh the night is my world... In the day, nothing matters. In the night, no control. I live among the creatures of the night (tldr; Lunars feel like they belong to the world of the night, in a way the other Exalts really feel like they belong to the day, even if they're Night, if you get my meaning).

                        Animal senses.

                        Having a mind which is faster, more enduring, more focused than the human in a way that is not the same as turning skill and knowledge up to 11.

                        Not being urbane, and if you are urban, you are something else which is intrusive, colonising and thriving there, playing "their" game better than "they" play it, wrong but right.

                        I like that the Lunars feel they have a kind of sense of brotherhood to them, that's more a fellowship than the Solars get, as a splat, which is not like the bureaucratic fellowship of the Sidereals or the blood ties and national bonds of the Dragonbloods. I like that this brotherhood has an natural animal like feel to it, a bloodied feel, beyond the artificiality and falseness of human society and its ideologies and convention. (At the same time, there is animal cunning).

                                Originally posted by TheBadWolf View Post
                                It's really sucks. I mean, what they will do in Creation? They need some idea.
                                But Lunars have a chance in one-to-one fight against abyssals. In exalted "werewolves" aren't forder for "vampires"
                                Well, like I said, it's not that Lunars won't sometimes influence nations from behind the scenes over generations as part of a social engineering thing. It's just no longer going to be their big thing. From what we've been told, there will be a lot more focus on Lunars as outsiders waging a guerilla (or gorilla) war against the Realm, but I suspect that there will be a lot of other stuff, too. Most simply, like some people have said above, Lunars are generally more scattered and disorganized than most of Creation's Exalted (bar Solars), which puts them in a weaker position in some respects but also leaves players and writers a lot more options with what to do with them.

                                And Lunars are one of the more fight-y Exalted, sure, but it makes me leery to have people say that what they like about them is how they kick ass, because... Sidereals are also fight-y Exalted. And so are DBs and Abyssals and Infernals and Solars and Alchemicals and, probably, Liminals and Getimans and so on. They fight in different ways, but "combat" is a bit broad for anyone to universally be the best at it -- people excel in specific types of combat (in the sense that Solars excel at talented swordsmanship and Sidereals are hidden masters who expose their true power at end of their elaborate plans, etc.)

                                One of the problems Lunars have had in the past is that their books have often had this chest-beating about how Lunars are THE BEST and MORE THAN A MATCH FOR ANYONE, beyond what you see anywhere else (even Solars, despite having a theme of raw power, are generally fairly low-key about making comparisons to other Exalted, really.) And this has caused problems, because Lunar mechanics can't really give them the kind of "fuck the other Exalted, LUNAR" combat prowess that some parts of their books implied they had. I mean, Lunars are awesome, but more than anyone else their writing sometimes seemed intended to encourage dick-measuring, which slammed headlong into the fact that other Exalted are awesome, too, and better at certain types of fighting.
