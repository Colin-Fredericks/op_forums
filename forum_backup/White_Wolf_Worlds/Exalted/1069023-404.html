                                                So after looking over most of the artifacts, the art direction, and such, I want to first say I love the hell out of the book so far. Thank you for this fantastic collection though I am woefully wounded at the lack of a magic transforming couch! But I'll forgive it. My curiosity is heavily upon the aesthetic atm, finding that the visual appearance of armors this time around (even on warstridres) definitely seems to be taking a lean towards what I am used to seeing. Hell, even the Warstriders visually seem to be shifting from Escaflone/Gundam to Pacifc Rim. None of which are bad! I want to say, just feels different.
