                            Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post
                            ​It's a lot more than was ever given in a previous core book.
                            The Black and White Treatise was published nine months later, although obviously that was part of 2e's ridiculously accelerated schedule, and that book itself was clearly an obvious victim of the drawbacks to that sort of approach. But more broadly, yes, obviously nobody could prefer 2e Sorcery.

                            That said, I do think that 3e provides much less support for being a spellcaster than it does for any of the other "primary" roles that people are likely to come to the corebook expecting. The issue isn't just the number of spells but the general lack of the concrete sort of progression at spellcasting outside of the three circles - charms build on each other, spells don't.

                            If I were revising the 3e core, one of the first changes I'd make would be to remove one or two artifacts and replace them with a full evocation tree for the Talisman of Eyes. It's important. Much, much more important than any one other artifact in the book. Honestly I would go so far as to say that it's more important than all of them - I mean that seriously; if I had to choose between every evocation tree in the 3e core, and the evocation tree for the Talisman of Eyes, I would pick the Talisman in a heartbeat, especially knowing that Arms of the Chosen was going to come out very shortly. Weapon-focused characters can get by on their charms until then; spellcasting-focused characters are essentially incomplete without evocations backing them up because they have no way to really progress at spellcasting outside of accumulating a pile of spells and hitting the limited breakpoints at E3 and E5.

                            I think that if the book was like "Yeah, if you want to make a spellcasting-focused character, you're basically Doctor Strange and need a talisman to focus your unique development as a spellcaster", that would be fine. But right now it just sort of vaguely alludes to this without giving us any real mechanics, and that's a pretty huge failing when it's something huge numbers of players are going to come to a fantasy game like this specifically to play.

                            And yes, as you've said, I know there are other facets to playing a Sorcerer than just being a spellcaster, just like there can be more to playing a warrior than just swordsmanship. But I think the fact that these threads come up constantly shows that, yes, the lack of a clear, concrete mechanical way to progress at spellcasting in a satisfying way is as severe of a problem as if the core had been published with an incomplete Solar Melee tree. The other aspects of being a Sorcerer are necessary but not sufficient; different people want different things out of the game, and a lot can be smoothed over having the right group and ST, sure. But "steadily becoming a better spellcaster over time" is still absolutely central to what many people want out of the experience of playing Twilight caste, and absolutely everything in the game's flavor, source material, and marketing says that that's supposed to be something it offers.

                            It's failing at it, at least for some people.

                                    There's a little bit of support in the Solar charmset. Specifically:

                                    Spirit-Manifesting Word is very valuable to a summoner, letting you keep a bunch of bound immaterial demons so they don't attract attention, then pull them out for 1m each. Material Exegesis Prana does the same thing on a larger scale.

                                    Ancient Tongue Understanding boosts your Shape Sorcery rolls, although the disadvantage to activating it is pretty steep.

                                    Supernal Control Method's full free excellency once per scene helps in the same way and is honestly probably the best Sorcery-boosting charm Solars get aside from Spirit Drawing Oculus.

                                    Spirit-Draining Mudra can be used to power your spells, although it's often a pain in the ass to set it up when you need it.

                                    Spirit Drawing Oculus can be used to power spells, too, and is probably the best Sorcery-boosting charm Solars have. A free mote reserve for shaping actions is a pretty big deal.


                                    There's a few Craft charms that could be relevant.

                                    Breach-Healing Method creates an aura that grants a non-charm dice bonus equal to your Essence to several things, including Occult rolls (and therefore Shape Sorcery rolls), as long as you are working on repairing an object or treating an injury. I do not believe this is the charm's intent, but technically you are inside your own aura and can benefit from the bonus yourself when shaping spells to accomplish those things - it says the Solar "establishes a field" and grants the bonuses to "anyone standing in this field", not "anyone ELSE standing in this field". The text at the very end mentioning how you inspire those around you could be interpreted as explaining why the bonus also affects others rather than limiting the bonus to others.

                                    Words-as-Workshop Method can be used to summon Occult-boosting artifacts like the Talisman of Eyes (and you explicitly get their evocations for free - seriously, that charm is INSANE. I don't get why people don't discuss it more.) Unfortunately we have no idea what those artifacts look like.

                                    But my problem with these is that they don't really provide a way to develop your spellcasting in any concrete direction, outside of maybe "generic summoner", which everyone who uses Sorcery already is because it's so good (ironically, Breach-Healing Method, using the "inside-my-own-field" interpretation I suggested above, is actually the closest we have, since it makes you better at using sorcery to heal or repair stuff specifically.)
