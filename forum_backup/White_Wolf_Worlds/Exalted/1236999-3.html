                                                        I vary a bit from canon on this. Same-sex marriages happen, but they’re exclusively participant forced love-marriages, and thus are seen as minor scandals. I get why the writers didn’t go with that option, since the Realm is the default arena of “social play” for the game and they didn’t want to do anything that would tell LGBT players that characters like them are less welcome on said arena - but being queer myself, I feel a bit freer to play with the politics at my own table, and I think “there’s zero taboo on same-sex affairs, but marriages are generally arranged to be reproductive, so same-sex marriages while legal are almost always scandalous love-marriages, and subject to identical scorn and jealous awe as male-female love marriages” reaches a happy medium for me.
