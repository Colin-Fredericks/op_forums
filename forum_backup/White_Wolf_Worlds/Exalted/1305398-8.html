                                                    Originally posted by prototype00 View Post
                                                    Sure, I agree, and was not specifically referring to that particular fight.

                                                    But Defend other is an Ess 1 Lunar trick that they are VERY good at. (Ferocious Guardian Beast Stance)

                                                    (I’m basically trying to understand how Ess1 Lunar warlord with battlegroup got punked by Ess 3 DB warlord with equivalent battlegroup)

                                                    Edit: I seem to recall it was you who said, all things being equal, Lunars beat DBs one on one? It seems that all things were equal here but one side was not using superior tactics that were by right available to both? That doesn’t sit well with me, apologies for the slight rancour)
                                                    A couple of things on this:
                                                    - I did give more charms to the DB than to the Lunar on account of him being Essence 3; I feel like the amount of charms available to each fighter was appropriate and the Earth Aspect wound up not using half of them.
                                                    - I tried to characterize the commanders somewhat through their choice of powers. The Earth Aspect was a defensive commander who cared about the lives of her men. The Lunar was an aggressive leader commanding fanatical troops. That's the big reason I chose Appearance warfare charms over Charisma ones: they include things like Diving Hawk Inspiration that felt more blitzkrieg-y to me. It's also why I went with medium armor and natural weapons despite it not being the strictly optimal choice.
                                                    - More specifically regarding the defend other: I don't think it would have helped the Lunar much; it might have made things worse for him. The wolfmen only lost effectiveness at the very end - the reason they lost was that the Lunar broke first, defending the BG would have only cost him more motes and got him crashed earlier. Meanwhile the DB could tank for days relying solely on his passive soak of 16 without losing motes. Now, if the Lunar had had defend other charms, a medium weapon and heavy moonsilver plate, THAT would have been a very different story. But that feels too much like a different character, and I think this outcome highlights the advantage that DBs have with their greater availability of artifacts.
                                                    - The thing that really won that fight was Ramparts of Obedient Earth. Holy shit. If the Lunar could have gotten the ambush he might have been able to put wound penalties on the commander and started a deathspiral of wasting actions rallying instead of giving attack orders. Also in this scenario I didn't give either party ranged attack because I specifically wanted to see an infantry clash. If the realm troops had been given bows, they could have taken 3-4 free shots with orders while the wolfmen were stuck wading through difficult terrain. That would have ended very quickly.
                                                    - Lastly, the wolfmen had an advantage from Might 1 and slightly better stats, but the greater size and the ability to Engage wound up counting more in the end, which I didn't expect.

                                                    Hopefully my thought process is a bit clearer. I certainly didn't intent to rig the match or anything.
