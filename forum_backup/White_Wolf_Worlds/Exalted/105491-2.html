                The Sidereals, in my view, are majorly the Exalted who are all about using cleverness and smart use of power to overcome sharp limits on the amount of force they can bring to bear.
                If you look at the Sidereal's signature literature inspirations (chiefly the Traveller in Black), that's the sort of characters they are.

                So they have powerful Charms that are heavily circumstantial (e.g. most of their Charms and major Astrology Charms), and weak powers that could nonetheless have broad applicability that allow for versatile use (e.g. minor Astrology), and their Charms are written in a way that encourages players to "think like a Sidereal" and get more out of what their Charms can do.

                And they have a capacity at martial arts to overcome their limits at being able to craft new magic. This also plays really well into their themes because martial arts, as styles in movies, is often all about smartly applying styles to counterpick against other styles.

                I think it's useful to note that they don't get martial arts as "the teacher guys" or "the weird guys" or the "we're the wuxia hero guys" (the last of which Solars are probably better placed to fill in). Instead, it's an extension of how the Sidereals get odd powers that nonetheless have useful applications in order that the players get to experience the fun of being a Sidereal, a hero who uses his fundamentally human quality of supreme insight, pragmatism, common sense and efficiency to overcome limits and restrictions to their force and power and manipulate Fate into a human shape (for better or worse).

                (This isn't to say they're "smarter" Exalted, just that they are placed to be smarter on a different, "meta" (ugh a term I usually hate) level, where they're smart, if not wise, about managing and using their powers, rather than their powers expressing genius and smartness, directly through the power.)
