    As an addendum to my previous post: I agree with Totentanz. I don't think the disparate power levels of the Exalted are a problem. It really isn't about "fairness" between them. It's about everything still being fun, and that goal is not incompatible with the premises of the setting. It does necessitate a certain consideration to design character types who are fun, cool and desirable to play in the presence of the others, and we know the writers and developers intend for that.

        Originally posted by Lionmanguy View Post
        This is never how the game has worked. There have always been certain play styles that simply fit better for certain exalts then for others. Abyssals do necromancy better than anyone else does, if you want to play a dark brooding necromancer you got more bang for your buck as an abyssal than anyone else. The thing that makes the different types so interesting when compared to eachother is that they have strengths and weakness's. They can do things that the others simply cannot do. If every exalt was able to do every thing every other exalt could do wouldnt that make them all more dull by comparison?
        You know what's never how the game has worked? Dragon-Blooded being individually more powerful than Solars. Dragon-Blooded can be terrifying opponents, because they're probably older and more experienced, have better support and equipment, and above all, have more Exalted than a Solar or Lunar.

        In previous editions, Lunars were the best with Attributes and shapeshifting, and because of mechanical execution, maybe that didn't mean a whole lot. Hopefully this time will be better. Dragon-Blooded were the rulers of the world and had a whole society's worth of infrastructure, as well as thousands of themselves. A whole society of elemental super-soldiers. Again, mechanics were pretty poor, but that was the idea. Abyssals have the support of the accumulated might of Death itself, etc.

        All Solars have is that their Abilities are above and beyond anyone else's. That explicitly includes Martial Arts.

        The Immaculate Martial Arts are elemental Martial Arts. Yes, they're elemental, but they're Martial Arts. By definition, Solars should be best at that, and the backer PDF doesn't shy away from that in the slightest. Dragon-Blooded will still be the best at elemental stuff, because they have native Charms, but as soon as you start choosing Abilities and saying "well, Solars don't have to be the best at THIS Ability," the entire idea is flushed and we should have had Dragon-Blooded in the Core instead.

Because the people who like these splats and want to play characters capable of cool things most certainly deserve consideration.

And (to put it in extremes that are not reflective of Exalted) I don't consider fun to be your favorite character type in a game being by design entirely outclassed by another in every possible way and their unique qualities nulled by the extended capabilities of the other type.

            When I say "Dragon-Bloods deserve better", I mean the players of the Dragon-Bloods deserve better because...

            I don't know about everyone else, but I roleplay my fictional character. So when people say a splat deserves better, they're thinking of their characters, and they want them to be cool and awesome, they want to be cool and awesome.
