                        11-07-2019, 06:02 PM
                        Originally posted by Sunder the Gold View Post
                        Unless we're miscommunicating.
                        We are.

                        When you're inside or right up against a battlegroup you're in Close range with them, and just like being in Close range with any enemy, you can't just move away freely. There's no just using your reflexive move to get away from a brawler when you're an archer, you need to take the Disengage action, where you roll your Dex + Dodge against their Dex + Athletics and pay some initiative to see if you can get away. Same holds true for battlegroups. If you're in a position where a battlegroup can hit you with their swords, and you want to move to a position where they can't, you need to Disengage.


                        Engage is an action that can be taken by battlegroups that replaces the Grapple gambit, and also has essentially nothing to do with the Disengage action. It's just the shorthand word for "Special battlegroup grapple." Instead of one character grabbing another by the arm, like a normal Grapple gambit, an Engage gambit has the battlegroup encircle the character with their weapons and totally blockade them. It acts very much like the Grapple gambit that regular individual characters can do though. One of the big things that it does is simply stop all movement. When you're the victim of a successful Engage action that a battlegroup takes against you, you can no longer move at all. You can't Disengage to get away from them, and you can't even reflexively move to get to another part of the battlegroup, you're stuck.

                        In addition to that, they get to make their savaging attacks against you, just like a regular grappled target.
