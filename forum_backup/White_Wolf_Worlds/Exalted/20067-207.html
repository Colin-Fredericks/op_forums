                                            Openly? no. Dynasts marry for political/financial reasons mostly and binding houses together.

                                            But adultery is very common, it is almost expected to happen all the time, since nobody marries for love and the likes. The thing is, you need to follow the rule of discretion, which means that female Dynast having a child by someone else than her husband is frowned upon. Usually women take a herb for miscarriage, or try to convince the husband it's his child or sometime...the DB just happen to take a leave of absence for a year or so somewhere to go drop the baby somewhere or give it away.

                                            Male dynasts on the other hand, can have a long string of bastards, as long as they support them discretely with their money and keep a long enough distance, that nobody asks questions. Of course, someone who has a lot of bastards will quickly see most of his money and taxes, go into paying his children support.
