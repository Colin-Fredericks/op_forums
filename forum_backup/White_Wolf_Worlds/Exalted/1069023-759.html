                                    Originally posted by ale137 View Post
                                    After reading a recent thread i started to think about something, what is the balance reason behind the different access to the resonant keyword between the exalts and magical materials? Is there something else beside theme consistency?
                                    It's twofold—there's a thematic component, making the Exalted strongest when they're using the associated material, and a balance component, letting us gate off access to effects that would be overpowered by placing them on non-jade or orichalcum artifacts.
