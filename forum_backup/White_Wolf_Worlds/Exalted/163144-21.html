    Pearlescent Anima Nova
    Dragonblooded Integrity
    5m 1wp
    elemental, Emotion, Shaping

    As water and wind steadily eat away stone and bark to leave behind only smooth flowing edges, so too the children of the Dragons erode their own social bindings.
    This charm allows the Dragonblood to direct her Anima Flux to erode Intimacies. For the rest of the scene her anima flux instead erodes intimacies that other characters hold towards the exalt or that the exalt holds towards other characters, with each damage success instead applying a level of intimacy erosion. The Exalt chooses whether this power erodes positive or negative intimacies during this charm's activation, and may not choose both. For every character in range, each level of erosion only applies to one appropriate intimacy chosen at random or by the storyteller.

    At storyteller's discretion, this charm may be used to do away with other forms of social or mental obligation, such as social backgrounds or mental merits. This charm can be comboed with other effects that extend the Exalt's elemental influence, such as [that war charm that creates a fire tornado]. At essence 5+ this charm can erode Wyld Mutations at a rate of one point for every 3 successes.

            Sidereal Ride
            5m, 1 wp

            The Chosen of Journeys, when pressed, may force all paths to collapse so they may end their travel. This Charm supplements an action to reach a given location. The Sidereal must already be on the journey when he invokes this Charm. Circumstances conspire to allow him to arrive at that location much faster, but a truly amazing and circuitous set of happenings allows it. For example, if the Sidereal desires to cross "to the other side of town," then a building might partially collapse on cart, causing it to see-saw violently upward, propelling the Chosen into the air. Then he might fall into the local aqueduct, which sluices him across most of the town, only to drop him into a fountain. When he steps out, some cows in the market stampede, pushing a set of barrels in front of him, that he then rights out of town.

            As Fate conspires to aid the Sidereal, other paths and journeys are delayed. For this reason, this Charm may only be used once in a given area per scene. Further, it is Obvious to any other Chosen of the Maidens that this Charm was used.

            The travel time for the Sidereal is reduced by a factor of (Essence+Ride). if any other Ability rolls are required (such as the Sidereal riding a horse, or piloting a ship), all difficulty penalties are negated. if this Charm is used to run away from an encounter, apply (Essence) automatic successes to any contested rolls.

                Auspicious Conclusive Time Marking Meditation
                Type: Lunar
                Attribute: Perception
                Time, like all linear things of Creation, begins to break down upon exposure to the Wyld. By spending 2m, a Lunar traveling in the Middlemarches or Deep Wyld can can create a small bubble of quicksilver essence the size of a small hut. This bubble perfectly reflects the current season and time of day in Creation, including phenomena such as eclipses and Calibration. This temporally linear bubble will only last for one hour of Creation-time before dissipating into the madness of the Wyld, but the Lunar who invoked this charm can invest an additional 1m/hr to keep the bubble intact against the ravages of atemporal madness outside.

                    Silver-and-Gold Attunement Matrix
                    Fairfolk Heart

                    The Noble Raksha has experienced the powerful stories of the chosen of the Sun and Moon, and were shaken, and were awed. Their hated enemy had shown them what possibilities they lacked; yet impossibility is nothing to the tides of chaos. Using this power while touching a Solar or Lunar exalt, one of the fair folk can forge a mutation of passion deep within themselves which inexorably ties them to Creation and to that chosen, replicating (though not replacing) the bond that Solars and Lunars share in a limited capacity - this new bond only goes one way, the chosen exalt is in no way affected.

                    In addition to the effects of Solar Bond, the Raksha becomes immune to calcification as long as they directly interact with or touch their chosen exalt (whether beneficially or antagonistically). At the end of any such scene, the Raksha may gain points of Gossamer equal to her highest stunt as long as that stunt interacted with their chosen exalt in some way.

                    Lastly, the Raksha gains the ability to awaken Adamant-level evocations in artifacts made of magical materials that correspond to their chosen exalt's bond-mate. (i.e. choosing a Lunar allows the fairfolk to awaken adamant evocation in Orichalcum artifacts; choosing a Solar allows the same for Moonsilver Artifacts).

                                Fuelling this charm with the Getimian's Yang pool allows the group to benefit from the positive effects of Fate, Shaping and Astrology as though they always met the appropriate conditions.

                                * this includes non-mechanically-enforced decisions made by the bureau of destiny.

                                    Codex of Eyes and Lips
                                    Celestial Circle Sorcery Spell

                                    Fashioned by a Sorcerer with a greater fondness for books than for people, this spell allows one to turn pieces of another's body into a record of all they learned. When cast, a faintly glowing circle inscribes itself on the ground or floor nearby. Into this, the Sorcerer then sets body parts from one with knowledge she seeks, and then concludes the spell. The circle and the matter placed within it vanish, and create a tome, a Codex of Eyes and Lips. Within is knowledge appropriate to the experiences of the body parts used. This takes the form of a firsthand account of all of the individual's experiences as though the individual had written them, and in whatever language the individual would have chosen. This is instead in a language the Sorcerer knows if the individual was unable to read, write, or even communicate in an easily understood manner. Regardless of whether one knows the language, a reader is aware of a deeper truth to be reached with focus. Doing so allows the reader to perfectly experience the scene exactly as the individual once did, seeing through their eyes, knowing the words they spoke, and feeling any tactile sensation, as appropriate to the body part.

                                    These words and experiences are limited to what the parts chosen could have experienced. Most obviously, eyes will give vision and lips will give the individual's words (as well as any tastes), but without ears, there will be no sound. Sorcerers with much obsession or caution, and little compassion, may prefer not to worry about missing subtle details (such as a smell, or a bit of pain, pleasure, or textural information) and instead choose to sacrifice the individual. So long as the body dies within the circle, it will vanish and its complete experiences will be recorded.

                                    As an alternative, a Sorcerer may instead inflict damage equal to 1L to a chosen piece of the body, though this will provide only about a scene's worth of time. If the Sorcerer knows what she seeks when she removes this piece and it exists to be found, she receives her wish. If her request is impossible or she makes no such request, the individual may choose the account or it will instead default to an experience prominent in the individual's mind at the time. This prominent memory is often of the painful injury just received, so Sorcerers tend to be careful about this.

                                    The book is surprisingly well laid out and makes its information intuitively simple to find, though some poring over it may be needed. The words can be transcribed, and remain as perfectly accurate as the written word can be, but the copy will lack the mystical experiential option. These experiences are only possible through the flesh, blood, and bone used to create the tome, traces of which can be found all throughout its leather, ink, and pages.

                                                Ousted Sultan Smiles
                                                Lunar Manipulation
                                                Duration: One full Lunar Cycle.

                                                When someone reveals the Lunar's nature or intentions (examples include outing them as a shapeshifter, revealing the Lunar's dishonesty or unmasking the Lunar's disguise), the Lunar may commit motes to this charm to slip a sliver of their essence into the target's mind. While this charm is in effect, the Lunar may use their Manipulation excellency to supplement the target's social rolls to appear like a righteous hero castignating a foul villain.

                                                The target of this charm always counts as a valid target for intention-reading and sense-riding Charms, such as Sense-Borrowing Method or Predator is Prey Mirror, despite any range considerations (such as touch or line of sight). At ST discretion, other dramatically appropriate charms may share this benefit, such as charms that affect dreams or inflict hallucinations.

                                                        Blinded Eye Panopticon
                                                        Alchemical Municipal Charm

                                                        There are those who cannot be allowed to roam free in Autochthonia, yet cannot be destroyed--whether as a matter of practicality or literal immortality. Fortunately, the Great Maker has an answer.

                                                        Taking the shape of a foreboding yet sleek soulsteel tower, the Panopticon is a scant five meters in diameter; while few can claim to have seen the inside, the glimpses from when these prisons open up suggest that the charm is capable of folding space upon itself to greatly expand its carrying capacity.

                                                        Prisoners kept inside the Panopticon are rapidly sedated, and the structure extends a Personality Override Spike into their nervous system before connecting them to other structures to maintain their relative health. Each point of Magnitude within the Panopticon requires an additional mote of Essence be committed to its activation.

                                                        While captive, prisoners will continue to dream as normal; the Metropolis with this charm installed may use Personality Override Spike or any of its expansions upon them while they do so.
