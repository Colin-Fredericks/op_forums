                                        Kind of for context, Onyx Path in general isn't very fast. Signs of Sorcery for Mage: the Awakening 2e was written alongside the 2e corebook and didn't come out until 2019. Scion 2e and Trinity Continuum were announced alongside Exalted an originally slated to KS that year. We didn't get them until later. And while there were issues with the previous devs holding things up, the manuscript was in layout and art direction for about a year.

                                        A big thing to remember is that unless you're WotC, it's kind of hard to actually do RPGs at any decent scale or pace. Especially when most everything you od or work with is freelancers who have, as noted, more often than not other jobs. A lot of bottlenecks can happen and things can change as things go along.

                                        It looks like things are on pace. A big help is some of the things learned from previous Kickstarters, like not putting all the extra material in the book but having a companion, and also having a manuscript done before you go to KS, were amongst htem. But I think i'ts worth noting that the whole "Half a decade withotu Abyssals" is being said in a very different decade for RPGs than the early 00s, and different expectations of quality to get there.

                                        As noted, the devs have a lotta books they wanna do. It's just that two hardcovers and six supplements or more a year is something they can't do and promise the quality we've been getting. Until RPG freelancers are a job worth doing over other shit, it's what it is.
