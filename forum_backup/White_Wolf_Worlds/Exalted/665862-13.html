    1. Admittedly, having a former prostitute be one of the signature Abyssals might not have been the best idea, but I've seen it played, in my rpg group, well (also, admittedly, with more of a geisha or courtesan origin than streetwalker).
    2. How would one such as yourself go about designing a former-prostitute Abyssal? Is it possible to 'play' Lady of Darkness in Bloodstained Robes with just a costume change, or is a more through cleansing required before it becomes palatable as something 'playable'? (This is not meant as a critique; this is pure curiosity, and as a way to analyze thoguht processes beyond the 4-5 people I usually play with in terms of character generation.     Well, to turn that uselessly around on you, it's kind of up to you and your table. You're ultimately the arbiters of what you find palatable, and frankly, what you have in your own games is none of our damn business. In that sense, she's "playable" as is.

    That said, since you're asking, I think the biggest problem is her history and description, particularly if you go by the Scroll of Exalts entry. Bare breasts are salvageable, particularly since it's a non-visual medium and you determine the context they come up in. As someone said earlier, her walking naked yet unscratched though a battlefield is undeniably a powerful image (particularly if armour is a big deal in 3e), and one I personally think there should be room for in Exalted (on the male side as well). However, some of the attitudes expressed in Scroll of Exalts cannot really be saved, and they colour her personality way more than her appearance does. I'd start there, personally.

                                                            I wonder, when one talks about a courtesan seducing somebody, are they thinking of all of that roleplay stuff that might be a preamble to put their client at ease prior to the actual sex? Kind of like in that episode of Firefly where Inara is hired for a rich man's virgin son, and she has the tea ceremony and pleasant conversation with him beforehand? I mean, other than that... it's a service that one gets commissioned for, you don't really get seduced for it. Incidentally, I've noticed that most historical accounts of some unlikely person winding up rising from lowly position to become the consort of a powerful ruler (Theodora to Justinian, Roxelana to Suleiman, Catherine to Peter the Great), or managing to stand out amidst a harem to usurp the position of most highly regarded wife (Zinat Mahal Begum to Bahadur Shah Zafar), those accounts tend to universally feature women who forged relationships through wit and shrewdness, attractive personalities and no small talent for government, rather than being "seductive"; a few of them actually wound up becoming co-rulers or successors. It's an interesting point on what it might take to actually stand out and form a lasting connection with somebody who possesses the wealth and power to get sex basically whenever they want, rather than being lured into it somehow. "Oh, you'd better watch out king, I might manage to manipulate you by being a pretty face and a good lay." "Person, there are entire industries based around getting that to me on-demand; how's about you try to hold an interesting conversation for a few hours, and I'll decide if association with you is anything I want to jeopardize my dominion for." "Drat, foiled again! Why is Amazon taking so long to deliver my copy of How to Make Friends and Influence People?"
