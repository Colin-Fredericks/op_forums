                                                        Originally posted by Jang View Post
                                                        What determines the difficulty of a Distract gambit?

                                                        Edit: Nevermind, I guess the attacker decides themselves. However, now that I comprehend it better, what is the purpose of a distract gambit? Why on earth would I grant another player 6 extra dice on an attack when I would have done 5 levels of damage were I not distracting? I get that there are charms that requires some characters benefiting from distracting, but other than that, it seems dumb to do this.
                                                        It's situational. If an enemy has lots of hardness, you may not be able to turn your initiative into any damage at all. Distracting lets you give more damage to your buddy who can get through hardness. If you have an ally who could kill the big bad but is going to act a few ticks too late to pull it off, distracting gives your ally the initiative they need to go first and splatter the enemy. An ally with big decisive-boosting charms might be able to do more than 5 levels of damage if you hand them 6 initiative. The ally might be the only person who can damage your enemy, such as if you're fighting an immaterial foe and they're the only one with Spirit-Cutting Attack. Stuff like that.

                                                        My homebrew: Abyssals, Infernals, Dragon Kings, martial arts.
