            Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz View Post
            It looks to me like the main issue in that fight was all the environmental damage. So I'm guessing (and not asking you to run this!) that long-range archery builds and people with survival charms for resisting environmental damage are the way to go when fighting this thing.
            I think the survival/resistance charms to resist the environmental damage is a must either way. Long range archery builds might be a way to go, especially if you can't get those charms, but the combat is going to be pretty slow. Solars have some ways to reflexively aim, but they cost a Willpower to use, and if you ever run out of willpower Mahicara gets a chance to catch up, which it's not awful at. It's a small thing but if you're out of motes to oppose it it automatically succeeds at Rushes, but even one excellency mote will negate that. It's definitely not invincible, but trying to take it on without Warstriders and without preparing specifically for fighting it is going to be really hard.

            Supposedly Dragonblooded can beat it, but I don't see it unless everyone has Earth Protection Technique active, with good Stamina and Resistance.

            Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz View Post
            Any advice?
            Run them as QCs first of all, so make no distinctions between withering/decisive attacks, or worry about their specific stats. Upon opening the difficulty 4 lock a spirit will materialize, bound as one of the final protectors of this place. It has stats similar to a fair folk Lorelei, and desperately tries to dissuade the characters from continuing. It must either be slain, ignored, or convinced to allow the party to pass.

                                                        Upon opening the difficulty 5 lock plugs break open and begin to flood the room with an acid bath as the exit slams shut. It may be stopped by breaking open the exit door Difficulty 10 Strength 5+ Feat of Strength

                                                        Upon opening the Difficulty 6 lock, nothing appears to happen except the door opens, revealing a stair upwards and out off the acid bath. It also, however, exposes all present to a disease. Use the stats of Plague.

                                                        The locks may be opened without triggering their effects at +3 difficulty.


                                                        Scene 7 Victory!

                                                        Here whatever treasures or knowledge or whatever that the players sought is there, along with means to deactivate the acid bath and leave the way they came. Hopefully in time to meet whatever time pressure was on them.



                                                        So, if that's the session of getting through that first age tomb, you can see that the Claw Strider attack right at the beginning is just the start of it. If those claw striders attack it's probably going to cost the party some motes to defend themselves. If those striders land decisive attacks though, those are wounds that are probably going to last for the whole day. Now you're fighting the walking statue battlegroup at -1 wound penalty. Then you take more damage from the poison door, and now you're fighting the Anuhle at -2. Finally when you achieve victory not only are you totally drained on motes, but also kind of beat up...which I think makes for more exciting and dramatic action.

                                                        Also anyone who bought medicine charms that help people recover wounds, or resistance charms that help them heal faster don't feel totally useless because the world they live in can't scratch them anyway.
