                            Originally posted by Iron Phoenix View Post
                            1) How is Unbowed Willow Meditation supposed to work? The qualifier is if the Exalt successfully dodges a decisive attack without using a Charm. Does that mean reflexive Charms? I'll give some scenarios...
                            It triggers when you dodge an attack without activating any defensive Charms in response to it. So...

                            1a) I have +1 Evasion from Silver-Voiced Nightingale Style as a scene-long effect. Someone attacks me with a decisive, and misses. Does Unbowed Willow Meditation trigger?
                            1b) I have +1 Evasion from Fourfold Shiver Binding as a scene-long effect. It does not count as dice added from Charms. Someone attacks me with a decisive, and misses. Does Unbowed Willow Meditation Trigger?
                            1c) I am using Flow Like Blood and have been attacked earlier in the turn and accrued onslaught penalties. An opponent with lower Initiative than me attacks me with a decisive. I ignore the penalty to my Evasion thanks to Flow Like Blood. They miss with their attack. Does Unbowed Willow Meditation trigger?
                            Yes on all of these.

                            1d) I use Drifting Shadow Focus, which lasts until my next turn. An opponent attacks me with a decisive and misses. Can Unbowed Willow Meditation trigger? If it can, do I get to decide whether their attack proceeds normally against another target within close range, or whether I use Unbowed Willow Meditation against the attack?
                            Yes, as long as you activated it before the attack you're redirecting. You both redirect the attack and crash them.

                            1e) I use Living Bonds Unburdened and gain -0 health levels. An opponent attacks me with a decisive and hits, but only does damage to my -0 health levels. According to Living Bonds Unburdened, I am considered to have dodged the attack and it does not count as the use of a Charm. Does Unbowed Willow Meditation trigger? If it does, when does it trigger? Does the attacker reset to base Initiative from damaging me, then have all of their Initiative stolen? Or does Unbowed Willow Meditation act before the attacker resets to base?
                            Yes, it triggers. I think it makes sense to crash them before they reset.

                            1f) Unbowed Willow Meditation triggers and I also have Force-Stealing Feint. What's the order of operations? Does Unbowed Willow Meditation crash them to 0, then Force-Stealing Feint puts them at -1? Or does Force-Stealing Feint steal 1 Initiative, then Unbowed Willow Meditation crashes them to 0?
                            The former makes more sense to me.

                            2) Hundred Shadow Ways says it lets you perfectly evade the effects of a Charm. However, the sidebar example says that an attack enhanced solely by that Charm automatically fails, which I assume means it automatically misses and isn't even rolled. Which is it? Let's say I lock out Excellent Strike. Someone attacks me with Excellent Strike. Do they roll their attack, minus the benefits of Excellent Strike? Or their attack simply fails with no dice rolled?
                            It works like the sidebar specifies.

                            3) Living Bonds Unburdened does not specify that the -0 health levels must come from the use of this Charm. So let's say I create three -0 health levels with Living Bonds Unburdened, and have an additional two -0 health levels from Ox-Body Technique. An attacker hits me with a decisive attack and does four health levels of damage. This depletes all of the -0 health levels gained from Living Bonds Unburdened, but I still have -0 health levels remaining from Ox-Body Technique. Am I considered to have successfully evaded the attack, per the rules of Living Bonds Unburdened?
                            Correct.
