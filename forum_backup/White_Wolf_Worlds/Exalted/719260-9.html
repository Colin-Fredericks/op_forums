        Have a Wood based one... vaguely.

        Draught of Eternity's Venom

        In the far east, near the Pole of Wood, there is a horribly old tree, 30 feet in height, ridden with disease and every kind of parasite. In its crown, there is a pool of perfectly black liquid, so utterly poisonous that nothing else grows within fifty feet of the tree. Treat this as environmental hazard 4A/Round, Difficulty 4. Damage takes the form of rapid aging. Actually touching the poison double both the damage and the difficulty, so most people use a branch from the tree itself, the only substance shown to have any resistance to poison except for diamond. If the poison can be retrieved in a vial of diamond and boiled in the fires of the earth(read: Lava), the poison becomes an elixir of incredible potence, able to restore youth and grant knowledge of the truths underlying Creation. However, this only ever works once, and drinking a second draught will cause the imbiber's immediate and painful death.(Well. 12A/Round, Difficulty 12, Duration 20 Rounds, Penalty -5. Have fun, and should you somehow survive, talk with your ST)

        Shaping Rituals:

        Having imbibed the greatest of Creation's Poisons, the Sorcerer learns to draw enlightenment from lesser poisons. While under the effects of a poison, the Sorcerer may choose to have the penalty become a bonus to all Shape Sorcery Actions and suffer no further damage from the poison. If, however, the Sorcerer is Crashed while using this ritual, it causes a temporary activation of the poison in his blood, causing him to suffer the effects of Eternity's Venom while he is Crashed.

        Once per Story, the Sorcerer may meditate for up to an hour to crystallize some of Eternity's Venom in his stomach, trapping a number of sorcerous motes within the stone. The Sorcerer may roll up to 3 Shape Sorcery Actions and choose the one with the most successes. Each roll requires 20 minutes of uninterrupted meditation, and once the Sorcerer has decided not to trap the motes in the poison, they are lost. Once the Sorcerer accepts a roll, the crystal appears and within the next ten minutes, enters the Sorcerer's bloodstream until it rises as a black mark on his forehead. At any point during the Story, the Sorcerer may cause this crystal to shatter, releasing the the Sorcerous motes into a spell he is Shaping at the time. However, if an enemy manages to strike the mark and the Sorcerer is not currently casting a Spell, burning poisonous essence spills down his face, rendering him blind if he fails a Stamina + Resistance Roll of Difficulty 7, and leaving him hideously scarred for (6-Essence) weeks. Striking the mark requires succeeding on a difficulty 5 gambit.

        Merits:

        Wings of Death(3):

        The Sorcerer may draw the poison out of his body to grow weak wings of black and green. By spending a point of willpower, these wings can have their strength increased, granting the full benefits of the 5-Dot Wings Merit OR granting the Sorcerer Unarmed Weapons with the Lethal and Flexible tags. What form these enhancements take are unique to the Sorcerer. These wings may also be dismissed or called reflexively, sinking or rising as black-green fog out of the Sorcerer's back

        Font of Venom(2):

        The Sorcerer may replicate any lesser poison, drawing from the archtypical Venom in his blood. Provided he has lost at least one health level to another poison, he can copy it and transfer it through his bodily liquids or unarmed attacks.

        Fear is the Greatest Poison(2):

        The Sorcerer's venomous presence seeps into the mind of those around, allowing a character who does not have the Hideous Merit to apply his appearance bonus as if he were Hideous, if he so desires. This only works on individuals he has had physical contact with in the last hour or so. If the character does have the Hideous Merit, he may instead use his poison to cloud other's vision, making it so that he appears normal. The effects of this merit wear off if the character fails to maintain physical contact with his targets every hour.

            Eh, not sure on balance, but this is my idea of what a blood tradition would look like.


            The Path of Blood
            Blood has held power since the dawn of mankind, when the hearts of mortals were sacrificed for the love of the Unconquered. It is perhaps the most primal of power, and so has ties to the most primal of all magics, sorcery.

            Shaping Rituals

            As blood is shed on the battlefield or in an alleyway, you can feel the dying life in it evanescing into the aether. You have learned to pull that energy away before it dissipates, and use it to fuel your spells. The spilled blood turns to ash. Each time an enemy gains or increases his wound penalty in a scene, gain sorcerous motes equal to the value of the wound penalty. You can gain up to 10 sorcerous motes per scene in this fashion. Motes spent on your control spell do not count towards this limit. Any unused sorcerous motes gained from this shaping ritual fade at the end of the scene.

            You may reap the essence from your foes as you cut into them directly, in its most honorable form, this is willing sacrifice, in almost all other situations, it is a brutal sheering of flesh in the heat of battle. When you inflict post-soak damage in a decisive attack you may reflexively activate this shaping ritual to gain sorcerous motes equal to the damage dealt (up to essence + willpower). If it is a willing sacrifice, there is no cap. This shaping ritual can be used once per scene.

            Your blood, however, is the most potent of all, steeped in sorcery as it is. Once per scene you can draw on your own blood for power. You may use blood that has already spilled from an open (lethal) wound, or inflict a fresh wound upon yourself. Roll Essence + Willpower + Wound Penalty (as a positive), gaining extra successes equal to the value of the health level filled if you inflicted the wound yourself.

                                                        Woo, didn't even realize this one existed when I posted my thread a few days back. I'll just repost this in here!

                                                        I'm helping a friend develop her own form of shaping rituals and merits--she's a demonologist and demon hunter, and while "Bargain with Mara" initially caught her eye, it was too inherently malicious for her concept. So I took some others and reworked/reflavored them for her character (save one from "Scarred by Nightmares" which remains identical), and was wondering if others could take a look to make sure the options are all balanced for working together. Note that I know you can only have 3, but I'm just writing out a list of possibilities for her to choose from.

                                                        ADDENDUM: She has decided on 2 (Trapped Pain-Essence), 4 (Meditation Tattoo Storage), and 5 (Self-Harm Ritual). Do these, in particular, look acceptable?


                                                        Infernal Draught of Enlightenment

                                                        You have spilled the blood of the Valethyn, the Fleshreaver, Demon of Pain and Brine. With his blood, you have brewed a draught of sorcerous power, and upon imbibing the searing fluid, your mind was made painfully aware of the essence flows surrounding you. Ominous markings now manifest on your flesh and, though minuscule, infernal blood now pulses through your veins. With focus, you can channel pain and fear into mystic strength.

                                                        ((The first one is adapted from “Bargain with Mara,” basically replacing “love” with “fear”.))
                                                        • 1) Much like the demon before her, the sorcerer draws power from those who fear her. The Essence of their terror is clay in her hands, taking form in her sorcery. Whenever she takes a shape sorcery action while within medium range of a character with an unrequited Tie of Fear (or a similar negative emotion) towards her, she may reap additional sorcerous motes equal to that Intimacy’s value (for example, four motes from a Defining Tie). Intimacies can only be tapped for motes in this fashion once per day, and the sorcerer cannot harvest power from fear she reciprocates. Normally she may only drain one Intimacy to fuel the casting of a spell, but she may draw power from any available Intimacies when casting her control spell. She cannot draw more than ten sorcerous motes per scene with this ritual.


                                                        ((This next one is another Mara-convert, the sex one. Admittedly, you are often more likely to kill than have sex in Exalted, though “consensual sex” could feasibly happen at least once a day if the seducer goes hunting specifically to stock up—so I used “once a day” as a reasonable balance.))
                                                        • 2) When the sorcerer kills someone with Essence through physical violence and bloodshed, she may choose to capture the victim’s suffering essence as it escapes in his death throes. The snared energy lingers invisibly around her, tethered to her by umbral chains. As a miscellaneous action, the sorcerer may burn one of these suffering coalescences in exchange for power once per scene, rolling (Essence + Willpower) and gaining that many sorcerous motes. She retains these motes until the end of the scene, and may spend them towards any spells she casts. Additionally, a bound coalescence may be reflexively expended in place of a point of Willpower when paying the cost of the sorcerer’s control spell. She cannot have more than (Essence + Occult) coalescences bound to her at one time, and she cannot collect more than one per day.


                                                        ((These two are super easy conversions from Talisman.))
                                                        • 3) When the sorcerer takes the first shape sorcery action to begin casting a spell and stunts it with a description of how she casts the spell through channeling the demon's blood within her, she gains (stunt rating + 2) sorcerous motes towards completing this spell. This benefit can only be received once per scene. Stunts to enhance the sorcerer’s control spell do not count against the once per scene limit.
                                                        • 4) A sorcerer with an Essence pool may spend a scene in meditation to commit ten motes to the infernal essence marking her flesh. Once she begins shaping a spell, she may draw on the power she’s invested, contributing up to (Essence + 2) sorcerous motes towards the spell until the full commitment of ten motes has been converted into ten sorcerous motes, or the spell is cast (or countered). Committed motes are converted to sorcerous motes on a one-to-one basis normally, but are converted into two sorcerous motes when casting the sorcerer’s control spell. The sorcerer must wait until she’s had a full night’s sleep before she can charge her demonic markings again.


                                                        (( This is one taken from “Pact with an Ifrit Lord”, but because of the new power source, I had to tinker with the mechanics and I’m not sure how to properly balance it. NOTE: This is the one I'm most unsure about, but it's also the most thematically fitting of them. I lowered the difficulty start from 5 to 3 since aggravated damage might be more difficult to compensate for compared to a traveling Exalted with crazy loot, but I then increased it to 4 because unlike the original, where you could burn something so small as to count for Resources 0 and just make a prayer roll at difficulty 5, you have to take at least one level of damage just to start (so effectively the max difficulty is still 3). All that said, should I have gone the other way?))
                                                        • 5) The sorcerer has learned that pain is power, and through a ritual of self-harm in which she spills her own blood, her markings may flush with the power of her sacrifice. The sorcerer focuses on the infernal power within and inflicts a chosen amount of aggravated damage to herself. At the moment of affliction, she rolls (Stamina + Occult, difficulty 4 minus the levels of self-inflicted damage), attempting to maintain ritualistic focus while converting her agony into sorcerous might. Success grants her sorcerous motes equal to (her Essence + extra successes on the roll). The motes last for the duration of the story, and can be spent towards any spell she casts. She must fully heal from one ritual’s damage to attempt the process again, and any new motes replaces motes obtained from previous rituals.


                                                        ((Then of course there’s always the dreams from "Scarred by Nightmares" which works identically.))
                                                        • 6) When the sorcerer sleeps, her player may describe the strange visions that haunt her. She recovers no Willpower from sleep, instead rolling (Wits + Occult) and gaining one sorcerous mote per success. These motes last until the next time she sleeps, and can be spent towards any spell she casts. If the sorcerer spends all of the sorcerous motes granted by a dream casting her control spell, each counts as two motes towards meeting the spell’s cost. Once per story, the sorcerer’s player may stunt to describe how the current situation was somehow symbolized by her dreams, essentially declaring that her past dreams retroactively foreshadow the present. Doing so awards her a number of sorcerous motes equal to (her Essence x [stunt level + 1]), which last for the duration of the scene.




                                                        And here we have the merits, taken almost word-for-word from others and reflavored for this initiation.

                                                        Merits

                                                        Infernal Threat (Merit •/••): Demons recognize the sorcerer as one who has forcefully taken the power of Valethyn. First Circle demons are treated as having a Minor Tie of Terror towards her, while Second and Third Circle demons have a positive Minor Tie of Grudging Distrust, or some variation suited to their nature. [NOTE: For balance, I figured this would be 2-dot if included with the “fear-absorbing” ritual, 1-dot otherwise]

                                                        Sorcerous Blood (Merit ••): The demonic essence within the sorcerer reacts when it comes within ten miles of dangerous sorcerous forces—a sorcerer wreaking havoc with spells, a demon bound to ill ends or unbound and rampaging, an ancient curse fallen upon the land. Any tracking rolls the wearer makes to pinpoint or chase down such perils enjoys double 9s.

                                                        Suffering Unhindered (Merit •••): The sorcerer draws her occult power from various forms of pain, such that her mind has grown resistant. Deprivation penalties and wound penalties do not apply to any mental actions that the sorcerer takes, including shape sorcery actions.

                                                        Demonic Tattoos: Valethyn’s blood manifests as ominous tattoo-like markings on the sorcerer’s flesh. The sorcerer may unlock Evocations from these tattoos that allow her to manipulate or gain power from pain or fear, alter her control spell to incorporate elements alluding to her infernal power source, or manipulate or empower summoned demons in similar allusions.

                                                            I posted this initially here: http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/m...-rituals-peach

                                                            Let me know what you think!


                                                            Bond of the Familiar
                                                            The sorcerer uses his bond with a familiar or spirit familiar to fuel his magic. There is a power in the bond between a sorcerer and his familiar as their souls almost seem to merge together. These merged souls transform the sorcerer and familiar on a spiritual level, turning them into something far more than human and beast. This allows the sorcerer to reach beyond his normal capabilities and command the Essence of the world.

                                                            This method of learning sorcery always requires the sorcerer to have the Familiar Merit or a spirit familiar. The sorcerer does not need to be within a certain range band of the familiar to cast spells, but some rituals have a range restriction.


                                                            Shaping Rituals

                                                            Drawing upon his bond with his familiar, their shared soul flares with power and life. Whenever the sorcerer takes a shape sorcery action, he may draw an additional (Essence) sorcerous motes from his familiar if they are in physical contact with each other (such as riding a horse or an eagle perched on his shoulder). Calling on the bond in this manner causes faint supernatural occurrences to manifest around the sorcerer and familiar, such as both of their eyes glowing or transparent flames rising around them.
                                                            When used to draw sorcerous motes for his control spell, the sorcerer draws additional (Essence + 2) motes instead.

                                                            Once per scene, the sorcerer surges with power in response to seeing his familiar harmed. If the sorcerer has a positive Intimacy towards his familiar, and witnesses the familiar being injured, he can reflexively roll (Occult), adding bonus dice equal to the intensity of the Intimacy. The sorcerer gains sorcerous motes for each success on the roll. These motes last until the end of the scene.
                                                            When these motes are used to cast his control spell, he gains additional sorcerous motes for the casting equal to twice the inverse of his familiar’s current wound penalty. For example, 2 sorcerous motes if the familiar is at a -1 health level, 4 sorcerous motes at a -2 health level, and 8 sorcerous motes at the -4 health level. If the familiar is at a -0 health level, the sorcerer only gains 1 additional sorcerous mote. If the familiar is incapacitated, the sorcerer instead casts his control spell without needing to spend Willpower.

                                                            Not all bonds are forged in war and strife, some of the strongest bonds are formed through friendship and happiness. Once per story, the sorcerer spends an hour or more playing or relaxing with his familiar, allowing him to roll (Essence + Willpower) with bonus dice equal to the intensity of a positive Intimacy towards the familiar. The sorcerer gains sorcerous motes equal to the number of successes rolled, which last for that story’s duration.


                                                            Other Benefits
                                                            Master Trainer (*): Your familiarity with animals has made it easier to teach them new tricks and practice their abilities. The training difficulty determined by the animal’s attitude is reduced by 1 to a minimum of 1.

                                                            Sense the Bond (*) - Prerequisites (Familiar Merit or a spirit familiar): Your familiar bond goes deeper than normal. You are always aware of your familiar’s location and your familiar can always sense where you are.
                                                            In addition, you and your familiar have a sense of each other’s emotional states across any distance. This allows you and your familiar to know when the other is scared, hurt, happy, and so on.
                                                            This Merit can be re-purchased any number of times, each time it applies to another familiar.

                                                            Shared Soul (***) - Prerequisites (Familiar Merit or a spirit familiar): The charged bond between master and familiar is so powerful, it starts to radiate power. For every hour that you remain in short range of your familiar, you regain 3 motes of Essence. This does not stack when you have multiple familiars nearby, but you only need one to remain within range to gain the benefit.
