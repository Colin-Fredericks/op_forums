                                            Not a dev, but:

                                            Originally posted by Altasaire View Post
                                            Are there any guidelines as to how much magical material you can cut with mundane material before it stops being able to support an artifact? Jade alloy cut with steel or bronze is suggested in the corebook, but how little jade per alloy still "works"? I don't think you can melt down a single obol in a daiklave's worth of steel and work with that, can you?

                                            For that matter, is there a guideline as to how much magical material can you use in an artifact so that you don't need to add additional mystical ingredients (drawing Evocation themes from (only) the basic nature of, say, orichalcum and the mood and approach used by the artifact's author)?
                                            It's a different edition Savant and Sorcerer actually answered this way back in 1E:

                                            "Creating artifacts is primarily a mystical activity — an Exalt can weave magic into a stick or a stone so long as there is a speck of a Magical Material to pin the Essence into place. [...] Most artifacts require nothing more than a single piece of one of the Five Magical Materials [...] Every artifact has, at its heart, one of the Five Magical Materials. For basic artifacts, these Materials are forged together with mundane components, but for more powerful wonders, exotic and rare items, often powerful in their own right, must be added to the mix. Different Magical Materials give a different focus to the object and display quite obviously what kind of Exalted they are intended for. [...] The Five Magical Materials are not enough on their own. The Laws of Creation state that while the five, either alone or alloyed, must form the core of an artifact, there must also be additional ingredients that inform its purpose and focus its Essence in a particular fashion. Items generally take their character from the patterns of their Essence, and various objects of occult significance are used as “seeds” for the artifact’s effects, to provide a sympathetically similar template for the effect the wonder will create."

                                            3E is different. You don't need any of the Five Magical Materials to make an artifact anymore: you can do it just off of exotic materials. But I think having a jade daiklave made from a single obel is probably fine -- but you still need the same "amount" of (small m) magical ingredients -- you don't save anything from making it from a single obel because now you need more demon blood, god bones, hardened sunlight, frozen lightning and the like.

                                            Arms of the Chosen has some interesting bits on page 12/13 and 15-17 that might help you come to an informed answer.

                                                        Originally posted by Altasaire View Post

                                                        It's all very &quot;soft&quot;, in general, that's why I asked for clarification. But the gist of my question was, if I am making a daiklave made of jade alloy -and only jade alloy-, deciding to forgo the additional evocation flavor and themes exotic ingredients would provide, how much jade do I actually need?

                                                        The book suggests a typical daiklave weighs about 40 pounds and you need 5 talents of MM to make a grand daiklave (and presumably, the other grand-weapons like a grand goremaul or grand grimcleaver), but it's unclear how much of those 40 pounds, for a medium-daiklave, should actually be stony, magical-material, jade.

                                                        Can I do it with 10 pounds jade, 30 pounds bronze? 5 pounds jade, 35 pounds bronze? Should it be half and half? and so on.
                                                        If you need to Wyld Shape 5 talents of Jade to make a Grand Daiklave then you need five talents of stone Jade. Period. You need five talents if you're carving it out of jade. You need five talents if you're alloying it with steel.

                                                        If you need five talents for a grand daiklave, you probably need three for a daiklave. The excess weight is lost to the mystical process of the forging.

                                                        Far more typical for artifact crafting would be to use other materials to help shape the magic instead of using ~164lb of waste jade. With exotic components, you could make a daiklave with only a "speck" of jade.

                                                        But there's no easy way to make a daiklave. You can make one with only a single Jade bit, alloyed with 40lb of bronze... but to do that you need rare, exotic, mystical components. If you want to make a 40lb daiklave with just jade you need 204lb of jade.
