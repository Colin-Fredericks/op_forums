        Another Initiation. Let me know if people think it's any good. I know someone wanted a blood magic type one...
        ------------------------------------------------------------------------
        Bargain with Baashael, the Apostle of Blood

        You have met with Baashael, the column of blood that seethes in the Market of Rage in Malfeas. The law of the market, and a being without mercy, he has the control of a rare substance in Malfeas, blood. For that reason he is willing to make a pact with one who can spill it for him, in Creation where it is more common. Somehow you have convinced him to spare you from the rage of the place he calls home, even as he teaches you secrets of his blood sorcery. Though be careful, for Baashael is one without mercy or favor, only the law of the Market tempers him.

        Shaping Rituals

        Blood is the price of power, or so you have learned. You may spill blood of a willing or helpless target that has blood to power your sorcery as part of a Shaping Action. This is not a decisive attack, instead a willing target dictates the maximum number of non-temporary health levels you can you take at no roll. Against an unwilling helpless target you instead use a Shaping roll to determine how many you can take, either separately or as part of the casting of a spell. Each success over their Resolve is one non-temporary health level, as your ritual beats their trying to fight against it. A target that is bound completely immobile or sleeping is treated as Resolve 0. Each non-temporary lethal health level dealt in this manner becomes one sorcerous mote, unless a willling target lets you kill them with it, in which case double the number of motes. These motes last for one week, and can be added to on a first in first out rate, though additional motes into a pool can only be gathered if you deal half the remaining health levels as lethal. If you choose to target yourself with this you are always considered willing. Unless the target is yourself, you must have or be able to stunt lethal damage to use this ritual, or otherwise be able to draw blood.

        The blood craved by the Apostle is there for the taking in some places. You may draw (Essence) motes from already spilled blood as part of a shaping action from any source of blood that is within medium range. No one that has been targeted by a blood ritual that scene may be targetted by this, regardless of if it was from a different ritual and regardless of if they are living or dead. A single body will be exhausted after a single use, whereas a battlefield can fuel your sorcery for a long long time. Living targets need to have their Resolve beaten by your shape sorcery roll in order to draw the motes from them, but the dead have no such protection. Demons with blood fear this power even uninjured, as it allows the sorcerer to draw blood directly from them in the form of motes from their pool. The bodies of the dead and demons can be targetted always when drawing Essence to fuel a control spell.

        (Third Ritual? Maybe base if off Mara's third ritual?)

        Other Benefits

        Infernal Nobility (Merit •): Demons recognize the sorcerer as one favored by Baashael. First Circle demons
        are treated as having a Minor Tie of terrified awe towards her, while Second and Third Circle demons have a positive Minor Tie of respect, or some variation suited to their nature.

        Demonic Tattoos: Baashael marks those he bargains with in blood, dark red tattoos the same color of blood as himself adorn their bodies in sworls and First Age runes, each person marked differently but always following the flow of blood in their bodies in some manner. Formed of a combination of his own blood and the sorerers, these tattoos are unable to replicated, and all of them grant control over blood. Evocations that the sorcerer unlocks from these affect blood or their own body in some way, allowing manipulation, gathering, and drawing extra power from blood. They may alter the control spells of the sorcerer to incorporate elements of blood or rage, or they can manipulate or change summoned demons into creatures of empowered blood. Evocations that empower the sorcerer's own body always require one of the following bee met to be able to be used: that they be wearing no armor other then that granted by spells or charms with a good amount of the tattoos visible, be covered in blood, or be bleeding.

                                            Originally posted by FallenEco View Post
                                            Any ideas for the 'normal' shaping ritual of Ysyr?

                                            A society where you are either a sorcerer or a slave has got to have some regulations on the matter.

                                            My ST made a set of rituals and merits for our Dreaming Sea game.


                                            Sorcerous Archetype: Sorcerer-King of Ysyr

                                            You were born and trained on Ysyr, gifted with the power of sorcery and welcomed into their ranks as a Sorcerer-King. Your body is perfectly formed, you may command legions of slaves and lesser thaumaturges according to your whims, and you cloak yourself in Wyld energies. None save the Magnus may place themselves above you, for your blood is Sorcery itself.
                                            Shaping Rituals:
                                            • Your fingernails are painted with a Wyld-born solution, one for every spell you have mastered, to a maximum of ten. Each fingernail painted in this way grants you a single sorcerous mote per day that can be applied to any spell. Motes applied to your control spell refresh at the end of the scene rather than the day.
                                            • Your slaves worship you alone, granting you exceptional magical power. You may add your Cult rating to all shape sorcery rolls. The first time you cast your control spell per scene, you add your Cult rating in successes rather than dice.
                                            • You have made a pact with the Fair Folk for greater power. Once per scene you may consume one of your Intimacies, gaining sorcerous motes equal to its rating, but reducing its rating by one step. Intimacies weakened in this way recover at a rate of one step per week. Casting your control spell does not count against the once per scene limit
                                            Other Benefits
                                            Perfect Body (Merit ****)
                                            Your body is shaped by sorcery, perfect in every way. You gain two successes on all rolls to resist disease and poison, and your Appearance is treated as one higher when comparing against Resolve. Finally, you become immune to Wyld mutation.
                                            The Lesser Fuel (Merit ****)
                                            You have learned how to divert the cost of Sorcerous Workings onto your lessers. By sacrificing sentient beings and draining their power, you can reduce the xp cost of Workings. For every 50 being sacrificed, you may reduce the xp cost by 1. Each circle above Terrestrial doubles the required number.

                                                            So let me try to come up with a few things, so you can tell me what I'm doing horribly (because when was the last time I did something right?).

                                                            Disciple of the Golden Exhalation

                                                            In the South, firewands and flame pieces are often viewed as premier weapons of war, owing to the relative ubiquity of firedust. For most people of the desert, the terrifying power of fire at their command is all they need. Some warriors, however, have a more mystical bent, and through intensive study of their weapon, their fuel and their bodies, they can unleash the might of sorcery itself. These occultist disciples often speak of an entity they call only the Golden Exhalation, which they say is the source of firedust -- Ingenious Flame, the god of firedust weapons, often reacts with bemusement at this claim. Regardless of the truth of their words, the spells they can coax out of the mingling of Wyld and elemental fire are undeniable, and most disciples prefer to let their firewands and spells do their talking for them.

                                                            Rituals

                                                            It is better to shoot last than it is to shoot first, but the true Disciple strives perfection in all moments of the battle. If the Disciple acts before all his opponents in battle, on his first turn, he can reflexively convert as many Initiative as he wants to gain sorcerous motes on a one-on-one basis. This does not affect his place in the acting order on the first round, but it does normally affect it in any subsequent rounds. Sorcerous motes received in this fashion have to be used towards the first spell the sorcerer casts, and cannot be divided between spells. Any sorcerous motes received in this fashion fade once the battle is over. If the Disciple uses the sorcerous motes to cast his control spell before he is crashed, he can add a bonus to the spell's damage equal to half the sorcerous motes he received in this fashion.

                                                            There is power in the purity of form when the flames of battle rage around, and the Disciple knows how to power his spells through his graceful movements. Whenever the Disciple receives a stunt bonus in combat when he makes an attack or uses a gambit with a weapon bearing the Flame tag, or uses the Rush or Disengage actions, he gains one sorcerous mote. In addition, he can convert bonus successes received from such stunts into sorcerous motes one a one-on-one basis. These sorcerous motes can be used to cast any spell The sorcerer can receive a maximum of (Wits + Occult) sorcerous motes per conflict in this fashion. If the sorcerer receives sufficient sorcerous motes to cast his control spell as a result of a stunt, he can immediately reset his combat action for the round and use it to cast his control spell, if applicable.

                                                            Firedust is more than just a source of ammunition. It is born of the union of the chaotic energies of the Wyld and the pure inferno of the Elemental Pole of Fire. The sorcerer who follows the Golden Exhalation can disperse such a volatile union into nothingness, feeding on the energies released from the act. Once per scene, as a part of a shape sorcery action, the Disciple can sacrifice any firedust he has loaded in a flame weapon to add (relevant combat ability, generally Archery or Martial Arts) non-Charm dice to his shape sorcery roll. If he is gathering sorcerous motes to cast his control spell, he can do this as many times as he wants as long as he has a weapon loaded with firedust available, but any such sacrifices after the first in a scene count as dice added by Charms.

                                                            Other Benefits

                                                            The Ascendant Flame (Merit 2): The Disciple is considered to have the one-dot version of the Quick Draw benefit with regards to any flame weapons she wields, regardless of the Ability used. In addition, the Disciple can use two sorcerous motes (either unaligned or gained for a specific spell) to fire any flame weapon she is wielding that is not already loaded with firedust or another fuel. This allows the sorcerer to shoot without needing a source of ammunition or spending a miscellaneous action to reload, but it does not remove the Slow tag or allow the Disciple to get sorcerous motes from ammunition created in this manner.

                                                            The Resplendent Flame (Merit 4): The Disciple reveals her true glory when surrounded by flames of conflict, whether it is to exacerbate it or to quench it. When the scene the sorcerer is in contains sufficient fire (a single candle does not count, but a hundred candles probably will), she gains (Occult / 2, rounded down) bonus dice to all attempts at social influence, and one bonus die to all physical or mental actions targeting another character (for clarification, these count as dice added by Charms).

                                                            The Descendent Flame (Merit 2): Having been purified through the ignition of battle and the conflagration of the Golden Exhalation, the Disciple has little to fear from the least flames of Creation. Use the rules for Unburnt Majesty (EX3, p. 468) to reflect this.

                                                            EDIT: For what it's worth, a mortal using this initiation would be considered to have a bonus dice cap of (Occult).
