    Warstrider That Needs A Name

    02-18-2019, 03:07 PM
    In one of my games, a Twilight has just fought and won for a mine's worth of Blue Jade, with the intent to form it into a Warstrider, before having an Air Aspect ally start using it (the story introducing the artefact is a mix of rumour and truth)

    I've statted up what I believe is a warstrider that makes sense, and combos into Dragon-Blooded Charms related to its operation and general oeuvre, but I'm not 100% convinced that I'm happy with all the Evocation. The big problem, however, is that none of our group can come up with a name that isn't "Stormcaller", "Stormbringer", or "The Teapot Tempest". I'd really appreciate suggestions for names, as well as feedback on the artefact and its mechanics.

      Thanks to my good friend Thesaurus.com I became a big fan of the world Fulmination, which is a strong work for Lightning and is a good end part of a name. This is where you get Righteous Fulmination, or Calamitous Fulmination, or Tenacious Fulmination.

      Or you can put it on the from and go with Fulminating as the descriptor. This gets you names like Fulminating Vigil, or Fulminating Sentry, or Fulminating Cataclysm.

      If you wanted to go with the Storm angle, you've got Cyclone, Gale, Monsoon, Squall, and Tempest as good words to work with as well.

      Just a few thoughts from 5 minutes on Thesaurus.com

                                      The lightning is the Hurled Lightning Weapon described in the stat line, I can only apologise that this wasn’t made more clear. It’s a Medium thrown weapon with medium range. Like all Warstrider weapons, it’s treated as an artefact. Without Evocations, it does nothing special; an Evocation for DB let’s them use Elemental Bolt Attack’s benefits with it.

                                      Trivial opponents can be “deafened” by bursts of thunder, was the intention. Which was a roundabout way of saying “it’s very loud, but not mechanically loud enough to do anything in a proper battle”.
