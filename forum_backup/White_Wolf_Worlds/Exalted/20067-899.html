            Originally posted by Grod_the_giant View Post
            In general-- I wasn't even sure if they could manage first circle spells. Besides Charms and Excellencies and the like, is there anything an E1 Exalted sorcerer can do that a mortal sorcerer can't?

            A mortal sorcerer can definitely manage Terrestrial Circle spells. Spells with sorcerous mote costs (as opposed to Ritual) might take them longer than an Exalted sorcerer, but they can do it. Let's assume the average sorcerer has a shape sorcery pool of 6 - that lines up with the quick character guidelines for someone who's skilled in the area. With 6 dice, it'll on average take 5 rounds to gather the motes for the classic battle spell, Death of Obsidian Butterflies. That's a while in a fight, of course, but a sensible sorcerer should have some bodyguards or allies, either mortal warriors she's employed, or spirits she's summoned and bound. And shaping rituals will generally speed things up too - if, for example, the sorcerer made a pact with an Ifrit lord, and took the first shaping ritual, the one about drawing power from fires, she could add 1 sorcerous mote on every shape sorcery roll, accomplishing the spell in only 4 rounds on average. If she burnt a wp on every roll, that pulls the time down to only 3 rounds. And shaping rituals can generally stack, and nothing actually prevents a mortal sorcerer from having more than one shaping ritual. So if she had all three of the Pact shaping rituals, she could have banked, say, 4 sorcerous motes from making a sacrifice, and 5 motes from upholding an Intimacy, leaving her with only 6 motes to gather, probably doable in just a couple of turns.

            And, outside of combat, all of that isn't even particularly necessary to cast spells. Even the spells that don't have a Ritual cost can still be assumed to be automatically cast if the sorcerer has time. A sorcerer who knows Invulnerable Skin of Bronze and Virtuous Guardian of Flame, for instance, can cast them at the start of the day, and the fact that it might take her a couple of minutes to gather the sorcerous motes to cast each doesn't really matter outside of a fight, as long as she's okay with walking around as a mobile bronze statue with a torch-bright will-o-wisp hovering over her all day. Sorcerers are supposed to be weird, remember!

            Summoning spirits is definitely one of the big benefits of being a sorcerer, and I'd suggest that it's an unusual mortal sorcerer who doesn't know how to summon at least one of elementals or demons, and many know how to do both. For the cautious sorcerer, summoning elementals is probably the better choice, since if you fail the binding roll, the elemental doesn't run amok, unlike a demon, it just vanishes back into raw essence. Assuming a binding pool of 6, and an average resolve for a summoned elemental of 3, a sorcerer can successfully bind one every 2.5 days (on average, they'll successfully bind 50% of the elementals they summon, and have to rest an extra day after the failures to recover the lost willpower). A dedicated mortal sorcerer thus could have a group of 225 elementals constantly bound to themselves, assuming they tried to summon one every day. Of course, most won't be that dedicated, but it shows what they can do.

            For sorcerous workings, a mortal sorcerer can pull them off, if she sticks to the Terrestrial circle. Finesse isn't really a concern with NPC sorcerers, so we can assume they're basically always rolling at difficulty 1. If they've got that average pool of 6 for workings as well, they're averaging 3 successes per interval. With no means at all, that allows her to succeed at Terrestrial Ambition 1 and 2 workings most of the time, and she only needs 2 means (or one 2-point means) to let her succeed on Terrestrial 3 workings on average.


            As for how an Essence 1 Exalted sorcerer can do better than all that, it's mostly a matter of being more successful on average and doing things quicker than the mortal. An average E1 Solar sorcerer could use the Excellency and throw 12 dice at each shape sorcery action to cast Death of Obsidian Butterflies, for example, finishing the spell in an average of 3 turns without spending any willpower or using shaping rituals at all, and even faster if she does take advantage of that. And when she's summoning spirits, she would succeed much more frequently on the rolls, thus having a larger force of spirits at her beck and call, or the same size with less work. And she'll be able to finish sorcerous workings much faster, with a better chance of success, including possibly pushing herself to accomplish Celestial Circle workings.

                                        12-23-2020, 08:26 AM
                                        1E Core explains it better.

                                        Personal Essence is your personal power. It's roughly equal to what oWoD characters have to fuel their powers (Blood Pool, Gnosis+Rage, Quintessence, etc.).

                                        Peripheral Essence is your Exalted ability to go beyond your personal power and rip ambient spiritual energy forcibly from the universe. You do this by using your anima -- the invisible energy field that surrounds all living beings. The energy you pull through your anima causes you to light-up.

                                        Gods and so forth can't rip extra energy from the universe. What they've got is what they've got. Peripheral Essence is an Exalted thing (barring a few niche effects).
