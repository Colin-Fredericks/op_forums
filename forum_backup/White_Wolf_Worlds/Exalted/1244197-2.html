                Originally posted by Cruzwindt View Post

                Well, first off, let me say I love that Drizzt reference lol.
                Sorry to sound insisting but, cliche is an overused word as of today. People use it as a disqualificative and as something inherently bad. I've seen many cliches like travel back in time to fix things been used in extremely original and fresh ways, that I haven't seen used before ( like for example Tales of Demons and Gods, also big part of the Wuxia genre goes around the cultivation cliche ) . So, just because something is a cliche, it doesn't necessarily makes it bad, nor unoriginal. I'm estimating that it will come the day when we have no more things that we cannot call cliche in some near future.
                What you're describing is called a trope. Tropes are story elements that get reused over and over because it turns out that stories often naturally gravitate towards using them. This can be good or bad. Cliches are story elements that get used over and over because it's easier for the author than thinking their work through and coming up with their own ideas instead. This can be neutral (if done to move the actual main plot along instead of bogging it down with side bits) or bad (when it diminishes the main themes).

                In this case, as we are dealing with a group of people intended to be used by STs and players to fill their story with interesting characters, using a blanket generalisation to define them all is using a cliche, not a trope. It was done to reduce the effort of fleshing out the first age, but as it was meant to be an idea pool for players to build characters out of, that was a bad move. If you want to encourage creativity, you don't start by offering a setting full of bland filler. The story didn't call for this, the readers didn't call for this, but the authors did it because it was easier. Thus, it's a cliche (and a bad one), not a trope.
