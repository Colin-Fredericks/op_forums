    When you guys get around to writing a new book about the Wyld, or the edges of the world, or both, do you see any potential for the return of Songlines?

    Songlines were an interesting seed of an idea in the original Lunars book, in First Edition. Printed on page 20 in a sidebar.

    Though the book credits the Lunar Exalted with their creation and maintenance, there's nothing inherently magical about them; they're technically just a mnemonic device. Any mortal passing through a land can compose a song of the journey focusing on the natural features they observe along the way, and then pass those songs down to future generations. It's basically just an oral map, and just as much a part of the Survival Ability (or Lore Ability) as knowing things about spirits is part of the Occult Ability.

    But I think Songlines can have an interesting relationship with the Wyld.

    Theoretically, songlines could allow human societies at the world's edge to impose order upon lands where they currently do not dwell and do not observe, by singing the songs of those lands to describe them exactly as as their ancestors observed them.

    Songlines would become even more effective when sung as humans take the journey in person, as they harness the power of all previous generations singing the song and traveling the road before them, while putting their own present power into the song. Even if the land had become Wyld, the Wyld itself would be forced to conform to the story of the songline, in the same way that the Wyld conforms to the will of those who venture into it with clear ideas of what they want.

    I think it would be interesting if Raksha couldn't fight the power of an old and faithfully-preserved songline. They would need to attack the people themselves, either killing them or tricking them into forsaking the tradition of singing the songlines, or getting them to change the song, or simply forget it in part or in whole.


    But others, like Darth Artemis, are of the opinion that songlines should have no affect upon the Wyld unless the songline was composed, or is being recited, by a Thaumaturge with a thaumaturgical power specifically for the purpose.

    That certainly would tie songlines back to Lunars, but also other Exalt who learns the thaumaturgy miracle, and perhaps some terrestrial gods.

    formerly Tornado Wolf, formerly Inugami
