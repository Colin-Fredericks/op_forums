            I hesitated to post this as a) I'm mainly a lurker and b) this place should mostly be about sharing positive stories and ideas.

            I've been GMing exalted for about 10 years now, on and off. I've never been good at it but "He who owns the books shall DM" and what not. Since Ex3 I feel like GMing has gotten worse, harder to manage, more numbers to keep track of in my head. While I don't think Ex2 was better (no social system, perfect defenses, etc) it certainly had less to keep track of and less "exceptions to the rule" type charms. That's just my opinion however, I haven't crunched the numbers on charms.

            Nowadays there is just so much work to be done prepping for a session that I've stopped thinking of Exalted as a game to play and more of a job I do. I still try because like most people here I love the setting and the themes of the game.

            Recently my group has been moonlighting with another RPG called Lancer. I bring this up because, much to my great frustration, Lancer has an App called COMP/CON for character creation and GM management. It frustrates me because I can see how the same sort of tool could benefit exalted and take away most of the complexity that I struggle with. The App has only been in development for 8 Months and Lancer isn't even officially out yet but it's already come so far.

            I don't know the story behind the App, I think they were just lucky to have a fan who was a developer and had the free time. However it makes the game easy, let's us play around with our builds, let's us focus on role playing and jokes.

            I guess the reason I bring this up is that I'm afraid the complexity of this game is going to turn off new players or old players who have become time poor. Neither is a good thing for the game line.

            My one hope is that Onyx Path's growing connections with a full on video game studio might evolve into a focus on computer assisted gaming. Maybe that goes against the spirit of TTRPG's but personally I'm all for it if it means I can focus less on math and more on my friends.

                            A note about apps to reduce complexity (because I do agree that Exalted has a lot going on; when running combat I frequently make small mistakes because there's Initiative, Overwhelming penalties, motes going up and down, etc).

                            There are a number of player-created macros I have copies of that I use to help, and I've found them very beneficial. I'd give credit to the creators... but I can't remember most of them. (Colin Fredericks?)

                            This is a combat spreadsheet one of the forumites made that sorts players/NPCs by turn, keeps track of their overwhelming, etc. I've only used it once, but it worked really well.
                            https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1SP9T31SAqo_vhTb4zsm6boRqZsnLJLn3H3wyHkGjtRk/edit?usp=sharing


                            This is a battle group generator where you choose the base unit (you can make your own or choose from a list), put in the drill/size/might, and it generates all the stats for you. One thing I find very useful is that when the size goes down, you can just move the size down and it reduces stats appropriately, so you don't have to work it out again.

                            https://colin-fredericks.github.io/ex3-battle-groups/


                            This is a dice roller that lets you put in doubles, re-rolls, etc.
                            https://colin-fredericks.github.io/ex3-dice-roller/


                            And last, here's a map that tells you travel times for spells, agatae, etc. It's not 100% perfect, but it's pretty good.
                            http://howsfamily.net/Exalted/map/
