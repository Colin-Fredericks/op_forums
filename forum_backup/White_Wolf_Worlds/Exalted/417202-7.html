                                            Mm, that wasn't a copout exactly, that was a deliberate decision (by EarthScorpion, and I agree with it) to kill a chunk of the Lunar Elders.

                                            It was felt that the number of Lunar Elders held too much sway over the identity of Lunars as a splat, and that they constituted a setting problem because they were too powerful to be free actors, but unlike the Deathlords or similar figures they couldn't easily be shackled to a particular place to keep them from being free actors. Tamuz really should have more than just control over Chiarascuro, but when you've got around a dozen of these bozos floating around, each of which needs to have similar levels of accomplishment*, the setting starts to groan uncomfortably.

                                            That's part of why TAW has all those Shogunate- and early Realm-era Elders running around, violently disagreeing with the Deliberative-era Elders. They're a lot easier to fit into the setting, and it means people like Ma-Ha-Suchi or Raksi aren't alone in the spotlight as the face of What Lunars Become.

                                            Some of the dead Elders were pretty cool, like Tamuz**, so I can certainly see how you'd disagree with that decision, but I think calling it a copout is unfair.

                                            * Else they look like chumps, and that's unacceptable given the sway they held over the identity of Lunars as a splat.

                                            ** Although you could definitely re-introduce him as a Shogunate- or early Realm-era Elder with less of a firm grip over Chiaroscuro.
