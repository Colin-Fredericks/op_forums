    "Hearts of the Grave" (3E Liminals Homebrew) v1.0

    05-17-2023, 11:34 PM
    Hello all! I've been a huge fan of this game for years now, and the Liminals quickly became my favorite Exalt type. The themes I see in them of self-actualization through the changing of one's body and the overcoming of being seen as monstrous by society really resonate with me. Unfortunately, rules for playing as them don't yet exist in 3e. To solve this problem, I spent about a year-and-a-half making my own.

    LIMINALS: Hearts of the Grave

    In my estimation, it's not even close to being "done", but the character creation, play rules, and Charms are all finished, so that's something.

    Please let me know what you think! Feedback of all sorts, especially criticism, are very important to me.

          I'll give you my thoughts as I'm reading through this.

          First up, Aspect/Impetus. I like the idea of bringing in the four humors. It seems thematically appropriate. I see that with the Aspect Attributes and Impetus Attributes idea you essentially split the Attributes into the most physical Attributes for use as the Impetus Attributes and the more mental/spiritual Attributes for use as the Aspect Attributes. Personally, I would have flipped this script, with the physically named Aspects getting the physical Attributes and the more mental Attributes going with the Impetus Attributes. I'd have moved Perception into the physical Attributes group to accomplish this.

          Next, the free Anchor merit seems awkward. Mechanically, you are incentivized to just always take an Ally 5 or Mentor 5 Anchor, but roleplay wise this isn't always the most interesting. I would suggest that you should instead assume a mortal sorcerer is the "basic" Anchor and make characters spend some of their Merit dots to "upgrade" to a more powerful model.

          It feels odd that they can't use even Ivory Circle Necromancy. Necromancy isn't just about making undead creatures. It is also mastery over the Underworld and the ghosts that inhabit it.

          Muscle Memory is a cool merit idea.

          Great work on the Aspect and Impetus descriptions. Same with the Anima Banner chart.

          Anima disquiet is going to involve a lot of rolling. You may want to come up with a less involved mechanic in order to speed things up at the table. Maybe look to the Lunar Tell to get ideas.

          Anima Effects needs work, both thematically and mechanically. I do like the basic anima effects and that each Impetus has an anima effect of its own, so in a way you have twenty Aspects instead of just five. However, the four Impetus Anima Effects are of vastly different scales and lack parallelism. The Choleric Impetus ability is probably the most powerful. The Sanguine Impetus is nearly useless. Mechanically, they are all over the place. Two are once per day abilities, one is always on, and one is at-will and free at Totemic. Then there's the Aspect effects. You've got some basic parallelism going in that each has an at-will and free at Totemic power and one once per X power, but again the mechanical scale is off and there's some weird burrs in the parallelism.

          The Blood Anima Effects are solid.

          The Breath Anima Effects are almost useless. Perception-based rolls are frequent, but they are most often for things like searching a room or spotting an assassin. Rarely is the motivation going to be regret or repentance. Meanwhile, once a day you can find out a few minor things about a dead body? The Zenith Caste can do something similar at will for a single mote.

          The at-will Flesh Anima Effect is out of line with the parallel effects for the other Aspects. I'd expect it to add dice to a Wits-based roll motivated by rage, anger, or madness. Of course, that would be a weak effect, but then being harder to calm down is a very niche effect anyway and not that much more powerful. The once-per-story effect is pretty dramatic, but flavorful.

          The Marrow Aspect at-will power is less useful than the Blood anima, but still serviceable. The once per day effect is going to vary wildly in power based on whether the target has an ally available to shout at them.

          The Soil at-will power is, like the Breath at-will, going to be almost useless. Intelligence-based rolls are common, but rarely are they about sorrow. The once per story effect is thematically great, but I'm not sure that one boosted roll per story is as good as being able to rebuild your body out of anima, especially if the Storyteller is able to control what event that roll can apply to.

          My advice here is that you should redo all of the at-will powers. Most of the abilities that activate automatically at bonfire anima are things which are useful during a combat scene. Being able to throw some more dice at a narrow selection of social and metal rolls isn't going to come in handy that often when swords are swinging. Also, the Breath once per day needs either buffed or replaced and the Marrow once per day needs some work to make its effect more stable.

          Humoral Nature seems like a more complicated version of the Dragon-Blooded Elemental Aura. That isn't bad, in and of itself, but if you were going to call it Humoral Nature I would have used the four humors again instead of the five Aspects. Basing it on the four Impetuses would make for more varied Liminal builds, where a Choleric Flesh Aspect might have different advantages than a Sanguine Flesh Aspect.

          In the Resurrection section, the "soul hanging out with other dead Liminals" is weird and going to be hard to use at the table.

          I find it strange that grafting takes longer with assistance than without. I also find it against the base of their inspiration (Frankenstein's monster) that stitching isn't required and that they can change the look of grafted body parts. I think elder Liminals should be a patchwork of stitching and scars, not an unblemished canvas. Replacing body parts shouldn't replenish Willpower.

          Thread of Life contradicts character creation. Character creation says that the Intimacy for the Anchor can be of any intensity, while the Thread of Life section says that it has to be Defining.

          If the Intimacy towards the Anchor is completely erased, what happens? Is that considered "breaking" the Thread of Life?

          I find it odd that you've decided to give creator and creation a psychic bond.

          The text about recovering from degradation implies that those suffering from degradation can't graft new parts, but this is not listed as one of the effects of degradation in the earlier paragraph.

          Can a Liminal choose a new Anchor while she retains an existing Anchor?

          Your list of appropriate anchors contradicts the origin of one of the canonical Liminals. She was supposed to be a perfect priestess, assembled from the body parts of all the other priestesses serving her god, by that arrogant god himself. He was her original Anchor, although she ended up killing him. Spirits need to be on the list of allowed Anchors. Honestly, I think the prohibition against undead anchors is also going too far, as the only thing required for a Liminal to arise is an attempt to bring the dead back to life. Intelligence is the only prerequisite for such an attempt.

          Corpus is an awkward term for a group of Liminals. It's already the term used for the manifestation of a ghost's form. You might just go with Body instead, as it already has as one of its definitions: "a group of people with a common purpose or function acting as an organized unit.".

          In the Gaining and Losing Limit section, you have one of the conditions being: "Once per story, when the Liminal’s Anchor comes to great harm or is killed, the Liminal rolls five dice, gaining Limit equal to the successes. If the Liminal acquires a new Anchor before the end of the current story, this effect may occur again.​" You should just simplify this to "Whenever the Liminal’s Anchor comes to great harm or is killed, the Liminal rolls five dice, gaining Limit equal to the successes".

          In the Undying Torment section, the phrase "unnatural nature" just looks wrong. I'd suggest using a synonym for "unnatural" instead, such as "aberrant", "abnormal", or "perverse".

          Can Undying Torment really be shut down by the right someone telling them "You are a good person"? That seems to be what the text implies.

          Bone Deep Musings is not a good idea. Having a limit break of "try to kill yourself" will result in a narrative end to the character.

          Ending this post here. More to come later.

              You need to include the text from the Lunar Excellencies about boosting damage and soak into the Liminal Excellency text.

              Stitch is an evocative keyword name, but I don't feel that what is evoked is what the text covers. To me, "Stich" implies that the Charm requires that the Liminal graft on some particular body part as part of activating the Charm and that the Charm would end when the body part failed, possibly because of the strain the Charm puts on the body part.

              Heart-Unifying Graft Technique can smash the social system over its knee. The strongest Intimacies are brought over, but can't be used against the Liminal and don't influence her behavior, allowing the Liminal to pick up all sorts of contradictory Intimacies to deploy whenever they are convenient defenses. The idea is great, but the execution needs limits. If the Intimacy is going to help her, it should also come with all the downsides as well.

              I'm noticing a lot of things that are going to require you to know a lot about the character sheets of anyone you take a graft from. That looks like it is going to be a lot of paperwork for anyone playing a Liminal.

              What stops a Liminal from grafting a thousand one inch squares of skin from different donors across his back? That would give him a lot of donors to pull Abilities from with Graveborn Savant Method.

              Embodied Resonance Transplant doesn't feel right. There's little point in Liminals being marked as neutral with soulsteel and dissonant with all others if they can get around that limitation with three Charm purchases.

              Union-of-Pieces Prowess is broken. This is five motes for four or five non-Charm dice to any roll. That's alright with Craft, where 80 dice is just expected, but non-Charm dice to a Melee roll is highly restricted.

              Two-Bodies-as-One Talent needs the Autotomic keyword. If the extra dots count as additional Attribute dots for the purposes of Liminal Excellencies, this Charm is broken. Liminals would then be able to reach a Melee dice pool of 5 Ability + 8 Dexterity + 1 Specialty + 5 Light Artifact Weapon + 13 Excellency (8 Dexterity + 5 Essence) or 32 dice. This is something only a Solar can reach periodically with Rising Sun Slash. This gets even worse if the Liminal can graft a limb from superhumanly dexterous creatures, such as a Fair Folk Cataphract, or if you leave Union-of-Pieces Prowess unchanged. I suggest you call out these additional Attribute dots as not increasing the Excellency's dice limit.

              Essence Assimilation Focus is broken. There's a reason that the Eclipse Caste anima power was heavily nerfed when the line went from 2nd to 3rd Edition. The combinatorial synthesis you could get up to is unlimited.

              Burgeoning Blood Vitality can be used to manufacture Willpower for 5m and two hours.
              Step 1. Cut off toe, taking damage to your -0 health level.
              Step 2. Graft toe back on using Burgeoning Blood Vitality, healing the damage caused and gaining 1 Willpower.
              Step 3. ???
              Step 4. Profit!
              Hands Quicker Than Blood speeds this Willpower manufacture up to two minutes instead of two hours.

              True Undeath is broken. It essentially doubles her social Attributes when making social rolls against ghosts, Liminals, and Abyssals.

              You've missed an opportunity with the Excellencies and your Modal keyword. You could have created Excellencies where the benefits change depending on which Humor the Liminal is currently attuned to. As is, the Modal benefit is always the one that is easiest for your character to attain (just go Totemic) and is repetitive across multiple other Excellencies. You've really only got two different Modal effects here.

              Cenotaph's Serenity of Will is strangely named for a Charm that boosts rolls about thinking quickly.

                  Looming Corpse Diversion's Breath modal option seems to think that Initiative is transferred from the target of the attack to the beneficiary of the distract gambit. This isn't the case. Meanwhile, the Flesh modal option would actually make the distract gambit worse, as the difficulty of the gambit is the number of Initiative points transferred to the beneficiary. I think what you want it to do is impose a penalty to the target's Defense against the decisive attack portion of the gambit.

                  Many-Faced Courtier Mien's flavor text doesn't fit. Liminals aren't shapeshifters.

                  Spark-of-Mystery Glance looks like it was lifted straight from a Lunar Charm.

                  Walking Nightmare Mien's Flesh modal option is broken.

                  Shifting Abomination Aspect feels like it's a Lunar Charm. The Blood modal option doesn't actually do anything special unless the Liminal is only trying to instill fear. All other uses of Threaten already would fall into the Decision Point system.

                  Without-a-Scratch Icon Stature doesn't feel very much like a Liminal Charm. Same with Honey-From-Carrion Beckoning and Porcelain Seduction Mask.

                  I like King Among Corpses.

                  When I read the title of Funerary Hysteria Presence, I get a feeling very different from what I get from reading the Charm's text. The title makes me think that the Liminal will be inspiring emotions felt around death, such as fear, grief, despair, or even relief at the passing of someone suffering greatly. Instead, the Charm is free to inspire any feeling whatsoever and is more about making people feel towards the Liminal a certain way and then urge them to act on it.

                  Trembling Sword Hand Prana is great.

                  Terrible Stranger Method once again feels like a Lunar Charm.

                  Beauty In Blood seems more like an Abyssal Charm than a Liminal Charm.

                  Crimson Warmth Accord feels like another Lunar Charm. Same with Black-Veiled Courtesan Rebuke and Scarred Hide Monster Bearing.

                  The Adulation effect of Passion-Stirring Grotesquerie feels out of place in a Liminal Charm set.

                  Unforgettable Horror Dredging is great.

                  Deathless Obsession feels like a Lunar Charm.

                  Looming Night Stalker Terror feels like an Abyssal Charm.

                  Plague-upon-Memory Incubation feels out of place. It's an interesting idea, but not a Liminal idea. It looks more like a Solar Charm than anything else.

                      Humor-Diluting Alembic Method has issues. First, the prerequisite Smooth Over the Stitches doesn't exist. Was this an old name for the Appearance Excellency?
