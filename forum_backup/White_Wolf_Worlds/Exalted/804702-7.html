            Destiny-Manifesting Method
            Cost: 3m, 1wp; Mins: Integrity 1, Essence 1
            Type: Supplemental
            Keywords: None
            Duration: Instant
            Prerequisite Charms: Ten Charms from any one Ability

            This charm supplements a defense against a reality or fate-shaping attack that would permanently alter the Solar's mind or body in a way that would render her incapable of representing any Ability in which she has ten or more charms. The exact effects of this charm depend on the effect it's defending against - it might slow the effect down, weaken it, or negate some aspect of it. The Storyteller should work with the player to ensure that the Solar is not immediately changed beyond the legend represented by the ten prerequisite charms, that there are conditions she can meet to shatter the curse, and that there is ample time in which to meet those conditions. The Solar may not initially know how to break her curse, but with help should be able to discover the conditions.

            For example, if a master swordswoman is struck by a curse that would change her into a cat, she may change into a feline beastwoman instead, slowly losing human traits until she can beg the Goddess of Cats for permission to return to human form. Or if a Solar courtesan's existence would be rewritten to remove her beauty, she might transform to an old crone from midnight until dawn every day until she can find true love's kiss to break the spell.

            A repurchase renders the Solar immune to any curse she has already broken with this charm, without need to activate it again or re-break the curse - the effects may manifest for a brief moment, but fade away quickly with no lasting effect.

                        Originally posted by Sanctaphrax View Post

                        If they don't know you can perceive the immaterial, they won't bother hiding. If they do bother hiding, they can do it just as effectively as though would were they material.

                        I'm just automatically bothered if it's possible to notice an immaterial being that you wouldn't have noticed a material version of. I guess you could also have the Charm let you detect material spirits, but that has its own problems.
                        "If you know about this effect, you can contest it, but if you don't you're screwed" is an area I try to avoid as much as possible - it's mismatched incentives between player (or ST) knowledge ("even though I'm invisible, I should still hide my aura") and character knowledge ("I'm invisible, why do I need to hide?"). Giving the spirit an automatic roll - no knowledge needed - makes it easier on the ST (no more asking "would this spirit know Solars or this particular character con do this?") and removes the mental twitch of intentionally doing something suboptimal because your character wouldn't know better.

                        In short, I'd much prefer opposition to be automatic rather than requiring knowledge to realize there's a contest to be had. Simplifies things.
