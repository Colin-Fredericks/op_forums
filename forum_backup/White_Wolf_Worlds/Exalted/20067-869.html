                                    Pretty rare?

                                    Aggravated damage is damage so serious that it can't be healed by magic. It either kills you outright, likely resulting in your complete destruction (like if you get sucked into a black hole generated by a five-dot Warstrider -- Arms of the Chosen, page 150), else it takes weeks for your divinely empowered superhero to recover from.

                                    That said, there are weapons that deal aggravated damage to a narrow field of targets (fire elementals, sorcerous constructs, Creatures of Darkness). Zelator is a four dot artifact that just gives you free agg dam as an attunement bonus -- but only to a very tightly defined, very narrow list of foes.

                                    And Dragon-Blooded have Agg Damage charms, like Dragon’s Sacred Talon (only works on undead, Essence 2), Sage of Iron Meditation (only affects crashed creatures of the Wyld, Essence 3) and God-Immolating Strike (only works on spirits, Essence 2).

                                    So... Aggravated Damage to any foe? That's probably an Essence 3 Solar effect that probably needs a lot of drawbacks on it. Blazing Solar Bolt is probably as staight-forward as it gets (six prereqs, Essence 3, once per combat, costs Willpower, Initiative, enough motes to flare, Simple-type) -- but I see no reason not to have a similiar affect in the combat ability of your choice. Possibly an Essence 5 DB effect (probably Fire Aspected).

                                    Aggravated Damage to a specific group of foes? Essence 1, knock yourself out for Solars (1-2 prereqs, ~3m): Solar themes lend themselves naturally to Creatures of Darkness (actually, Blazing Solar Bolt is only to Creatures of Darkness, but it's also packaged with a LOAD of other bonuses including unblockable, undodgable and bypasses Hardness), but you can justify just about anything under their paradigm (and if you can't, that's what Evocations are for). Essence 2-3 for DBs (Aspect dependent on what the group is -- Wood for undead, Earth for fae, Air for spirits, Wood for plants, something like that).

                                                    Originally posted by Prince of the Night View Post

                                                    One thing I Always just assumed was kasif from first edition would lose his faith in the immaculate order if he found out he was lied to

                                                    How would that work using the intimacys rules from 3rd edition
                                                    DrLoveMonkey has it right here - barring magic, a character can always simply choose to reduce or eliminate an Intimacy of their own. There's certainly no need to use social influence or anything on yourself to do so. Of course, it's also valid for a character to choose to change the context of the intimacy. An Immaculate who discovered the "truth" of the order could change their Defining Intimacy of "I respect the Immaculate Order and its precepts" to a Major Intimacy of "The Immaculate Philosophy (betrayal)", but keep a Major Intimacy of "My old Immaculate comrades (respect and well-wishing)".

                                                    Note that, in 3e, the Immaculate Philosophy doesn't make many claims that are easily disproven. A big one is that the Philosophy, at least to initiates, does not keep with the "Solars and Lunars are demons that replaced the human souls of the bodies" idea. That's basically something they let peasants and the ignorant believe, because it's easier to explain, but within the Order, it's known and acknowledged that Solars and Lunars are indeed a type of Exalted. They just believe that the power of the Sun and Moon is so great and terrible that it inevitably drives anyone who holds it mad, so they still need to be put down for the safety of Creation.
