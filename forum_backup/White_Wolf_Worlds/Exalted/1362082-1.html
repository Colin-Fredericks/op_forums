    Originally posted by DrLoveMonkey View Post

    Well, yeah, and every exalt type has charms for adding more dice, at the very least they have excellencies for every action it’s not totally exclusive. Even their charms that aren’t just flat more dice tend towards just making that action better or more efficient or enhancing the scope in some way. They have an athletics charm to double damage, but not one to create a wide range environmental hazard that knocks targets back and prone with a big fiery shockwave.

    Again, not exclusively true there’s some really out there charms, but considering the staggering size of the Solar charmset compared to the DB one the DBs have way fewer charms that enhance an action.

    Edit: if we’re considering things like One More Step and Living Wind Approach to be “do cool shit” charms then I can’t agree with Chausse that Solars don’t have a lot of them, they have literally hundreds of them.
    I think my stance is rather than they have Charms that do cool shit, but there are too many Charms that are boring dice tricks as well (or contain boring dice tricks that just changes the motes calculation for nothing), and some of them are pre requisite as well (Craft is of course the worst offender, but it's not the only one). My initial post was to ask wether Dragon-Blooded were more spared from that, which you confirmed they were.

    My philosophy about "efficiency" Charms would be that it's fine to have them, if there are few of them and their impact is direct and effective for the player : Gain a Free Full excellency on ability one per scene is cool and you easily remember it, but Umbral eye "You gain 1 automatic successes and 2 dices for 2 motes on a roll to specifically read intentions about a character for which you created or changed an intimacy" is way too specific. Gain +3 Guilde in permanence is cool and clear and written on your sheet in a memorable way, but Winning Stride discipline that gives you 2 motes or 2 initiative only for Athletics when you successfully win a test of speed but that is not the same Charm that Onrush Burst Method which gives you 3 motes (and not initiative) for Athletics Charms but only on a Rush action and not in a test of speed and bzzzz. I can assure you when you play someone with Athletics supernal these Charms are the worst to be paid, you just forget them because meanwhile you have 8 different other Charms with real effect on the game that you have to take into account when you rush and attack someone.

    So I might be slightly accentuating the problem of dice tricks (because they are not the majority of Charms, thanks God), but I was really glad when I played Dragon Blooded and felt much more ease to use their Charms, except for the excellency that I think they should have all unified to avoid checking "What's the special effect of Athletics Excellency already ?" with something like "Dragon Blooded always reroll 6" or something like that.

                    Originally posted by Sunder the Gold View Post
                    But Solar Athletics DOES have a Charm for substituting a normal rush action with "I am instantly at close range with you, and I get to make an enhanced attack as well", as well as a Charm for "I get to make a normal rush action against you from outside the usual range for a rush action, and also I get TWO reflexive advances on you instead of one".

                                                        It really depends. If you're regularly opposing Exalts with massive dice pools, they're worth the xp. Otherwise, your free excellency is fine. In fact, the excellency is a better charm than a lot of them (not all), and you should only use them if you've already maxed out your excellency.

                                                        There are others that are basically just discounted excellencies, and I suppose saving motes is worth a charm for some characters.

                                                        I do feel a lot of them are marginal enough that they should really not be at the base of trees though. If you're the sort of player who carefully keeps their essence expenditure down, then a charm that gives Diuble 9s for 4m is something you are not going to use (as you could instead buy 4 more dice, which on a 10 dice pool is better).
                                                        Similarly, charms to reduce mote costs of your excellencies are something that you don't need until later, because at character gen you may not have many charms to use.
