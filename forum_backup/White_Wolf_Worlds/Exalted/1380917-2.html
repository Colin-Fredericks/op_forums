                                                        Originally posted by TheCountAlucard View Post
                                                        How in Creation is a Hellstrider meaningfully different from a warstrider made by a Solar sorcerer who bound demons into the chassis? If it's not meaningfully different, why the distinction?
                                                        From what I understand, a Hellstrider actually is a demon, or rather several, remade into the living internal frame of a much larger armor; you don't pilot one so much as guide it. It's a mount, not a robot. They also seem a bit easier to repair, since a lot of their architecture heals on its own.

                                                        More importantly, they're much, much easier to make, so one showing up doesn't need its own backstory - it could be a brand new one, which fits the new Infernals as "heralds of revolution" (since this Infernal has created something that should be a relic of times long gone). Making one seems a lot more doable for a relatively young Exalt, and since much of the infrastructure exists in Malfeas already, it seems like making one is more of the start of a story than the climax of one.

                                                        Most importantly, relative ease = more of them, which means more potential stories wherein multiple sides have Hellstriders or equivalent forces, which really suits the more war-torn nature of Malfeas. Also, since Hellstriders are characters in their own right, that leads to even more potential stories about finding a balance between their wants and their pilots'.

                                                        EDIT: I posted this before seeing the edit. Karvara's existence is a point, but he's also N/A; not something you can get out of the gate. I do agree that, even if the core technique is repeatable, every Hellstrider should be a unique creation, same as any other warstrider. In fact, I say that the twice-unique nature should be emphasized, because not only is every Hellstrider a unique character, they're born at least partly from a unique set of demons blended together, and building one in the place of simply getting some neomah to make a really big unique demon or bribing a behemoth needs their own personal reason. Hell, even typing this, I have an idea for a demon lord that is a Hellstrider; she was born, in true Evangelion fashion, from a human woman letting Alveua reforge her soul into the core intellect of one to protect her family, and now she maintains her portion of the layer by guiding pilot-viceroys to unlock her Evocations.

                                                        EDIT2: To clarify; the idea is "lower barrier to entry" not "less special." Believe me, I'm just using "Hellstrider" for ease of reference; they are still warstriders, they just have Evocations based on their components. I doubt if many new ones were made before the return of the Solars, especially since demons don't generally pilot them. I think the analogy I'm going for is that a Hellstrider is very much a Second Age innovation on the basic idea of a warstrider; the old techniques and wonderworking is gone now, but demons are still bindable, and it's simpler in theory to put armor on a very big demon than it is to make pure armor walk.
