She's pretty normal. Sure, she's a little girl who can beat up dozens of grown soldiers, but hey, we've already seen the badass normal tropes and Black Widow.

            Things start to change when she starts using Essence 3 athletics charms. Like mountain crossing leap and racing hare method. It doesn't matter how much someone is called or insists on 'an ordinary person except that I have healing powers' when she can break the speed limit and leap over entire buildings in a single bound. Imagine that. Someone who can move at 50 miles per hour, and disappear in a burst of light and sound and reappear miles away. Someone who has taken resistance charms (to boost soak) and can ignore grenades exploding right next to her.


            They start asking inconvenient questions, like 'by what rhythm or reason does she gain her powers'. The answer is simple and easy for anyone to see. Barring medicine (she was a self-taught doctor when she exalted). All of her powers appear to come one after another, after training. She trains to use a computer? God-mind algorithms. She trains in arts and crafts? Crack-mending technique and impurity hammering blow and Craftsman needs no tools. She jogs and runs around? She gets racing hare method and mountain crossing leap.

            Which means that she can gain nearly any kind of conceivable power, as long as she decides to train for it and there's some way to logically extend her training to what she's doing. She can treat wounds, so she can have patients come back from injuries in a single day where it would normally take a decade. She's trained herself to ignore pain and become more durable, like martial arts training, so she can walk past explosions. And so on and so forth.

            Which means that she's a lot more dangerous than she should be, which means, well, bad shit for everyone.
