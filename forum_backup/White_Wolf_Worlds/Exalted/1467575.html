      Notes
      1. Just about every ShadownEssence project is unfinished and abandoned. And currently SnE is completely offline
      2. Many of my own documents are color-coded. Usually black = canon, violet = my headcanon, red = others’ homebrew; or my color code for each cWoD gameline. Some documents use glyphs from the Maya hieroglyph font, High Realm and Low Realm from Storyteller's Vault, WoD glyph and script fonts, and/or wingdings. None of the symbols are necessary (except for my font keys/guides) but they add some fun.
      3. For Archived copies of my Google Docs, if the listed url doesn’t work, change it to https://web.archive.org/web/*/https:...om/document/d/[Doc ID]/ or ask me for a new url (on this thread).
      4. Many of the urls in the Library have been Archived in the Wayback Machine, but the huge section in italics is stuff I haven't tried to Archive yet. Other exceptions are the Storyteller’s Vault products (which aren’t free); my Google Drive docs, the forums, Unofficial White Wolf Wiki, and Exalted Wikis (all of which I’d like to save eventually); Sacred Texts (which is HUGE); most images, OneDrive, Google Sites, Dropbox, Mediafire, Tumblr, Pinterest galleries, most name generators, and some images and RP Nation threads (which the Internet Archive can’t save).
      5. Most of the pages I have found are in English; most exceptions are noted.
