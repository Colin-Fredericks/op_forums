    [Lunars] Characters from other media who felt trapped

    06-18-2016, 11:34 PM
    Since it seems that Third Edition's Luna chooses her children from those who were willing to sacrifice anything - even their lives - for personal freedom, I think it would be useful to compile a list of such characters from other stories.

    This category is going to overlap with other possible Exalted. The idea is not "only those who were trapped and tried to escape can be Lunars" but rather "Lunars can only be those who were trapped and tried to escape". It's about who Lunars are rather than what other Exalted are not.

    I would never think of Tony Stark as a Lunar, but his origin story of being held captive by the Ten Rings and risking his life to escape is something that can lead to a Solar Exaltation. (Though one might argue he was less concerned with personal freedom and more with personal responsibilities beyond his imprisonment.)


    I once composed a list of how the game of Skyrim would play out if the Dragonborn had Exalted. As a Solar or Sidereal, it just happens. He cannot be a Terrestrial without the blood of the dragons, or a modern Alchemical without a Exaltation Core. As an Abyssal, he needs to die by an uninterrupted beheading or because of the dragon; as a classical Alchemical, he actually dies and stays dead a while before being brought back on the slab. A Liminal made from his corpse is someone new.

    Until the corebook came out, I didn't see a particular difference between the Solar and Lunar, but now I do.

    I think Luna doesn't choose the one who decides to give up and die with dignity on the chopping block. I think she chooses the young man who decided to actually try running away, even if it meant getting shot in the back. He breathes in her Second Breath and makes good on his escape.

    formerly Tornado Wolf, formerly Inugami

                                                  Reposting from here: http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/m...ned-3e-musings

                                                  Ever since I learned about the Lunar's new "Chosen for breaking their chains" narrative, I had this idea.

                                                  A princess (mortal or Dragon-Blood) is given a male slave taken from an oppressed local people-group. In actuality, he was chosen and trained by elements within his people with the intent that he become a slave in the castle to act as an inside agent and eventual assassin.

                                                  When the day comes that they finally storm the castle, the slave has decided to break free and make his own destiny. He Exalts as a Lunar and slaughters absolutely every one of... his own people.

                                                  He had grown to love the princess, and had felt trapped by the duties imposed upon him by his own people. He rejected slavery to his past and continues to build his own place and destiny as the secret protector of the woman he loves.
