      What's a POW ?

      I think it's a complicated matter. I try to approach the world of Exalted as some kind of (fantasized) mythical Antique time of earth, where slaves was just a political situation among others, and not a central political matter.

      More generally in RPGs, I try to consider that, as long as I know that the person I'm playing with is not truly defending offensive political views in real life, all philosophies are more or less equal morally, the question being more how they will handle personal dilemma rather than questioning universal modern moral problematics.

                                      There’s been a thread or two about “is it okay to enslave demons and/or elementals because that’s what summoning is”. Generally I think the topic is skipped over because most people don’t judge demons as being the same thing as people. They’re not human, have non-human drives and needs(some are close to human, some aren’t), and so are thought of differently. Most traditions exalted draws on for its occultism take similar views on the morality of compelling demons to your service.

                                                          There was loads of conversation about this during Exalted's original run. It got downgraded a little bit I think as change in conversation / previous devs looking to emphasise more the heroic nature of Primordial War and generally deemphasize these sorts of "Yozi apologism" and ideas that Creation was built on cycles of usurpation which didn't fundamentally have any more or less moral legitimacy (the new gods triumphing over the old gods and so on). Tldr basically something fanbase just kind of stopped discussing too much.
