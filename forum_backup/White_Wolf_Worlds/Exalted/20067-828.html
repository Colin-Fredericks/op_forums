        High Realm is the courtly language (not a language family) of Dynasts and patricians of the Realm, as it always has, and Low Realm is the collective names for the various regional variants used by the Blessed Isle's commoners. I've compared it to literary vs. vernacular Arabic before, but I think a better comparison would be courtly Ottoman Turkish, largely impenetrable to the lower classes, who spoke an earlier form of modern Turkish.

        The language family confusion comes due to the Language Merit being really inconsistent, initially calling the Directional languages "languages" before switching over to "language families." High Realm, however, is consistently presented as a language.

                                Celestial 2 is where I'd put most immortality workings, but I'd definitely say it needs a complication. That's because I feel pretty much all sorcery needs complications, to keep it interesting.

                                My headcanon is that one of the more common immortality workings essentially strips "you age and eventually die from this" out of your personal strand on the Loom of Fate. But Saturn always takes her due, and in exchange for losing that part of the Endings of your fate, another one of the ends in your fate is strengthened. This gives you a "bane", some kind of thing that becomes more likely to kill you. That could be as common as "fire", in which case you might take a bit more damage from fire and maybe fires are a bit more likely to go out of control around you, or as specific as "Brena the Blood-Ape", in which case fate will ensure that Brena is sufficiently powerful to kill you, and ensure it doesn't die prematurely. Note that this doesn't stop you from dying from any other causes, just makes one of them stronger and basically guaranteed to eventually kill you. The Finesse the working is done at decides whether the sorcerer's character or the Storyteller gets to choose exactly what the bane is going to be.

                                                    04-25-2020, 03:10 PM
                                                    Originally posted by Accelerator View Post
                                                    Any reason why there is a difference in soak and hardness?

                                                    It came up during a discussion of exwod. Apparently in their system soak can tank things down to zero. Here, its different, right? Soak reduces damage, but doesn't remove it entirely.
                                                    Soak can reduce damage down to a miniumum of the attack. So in Ex2 it was down to a minimum of your Essence, in Ex3 it's down to a minimum of 1, or whatever your weapon's minimum damage is.

                                                    Hardness applies to actually damaging Decisive attacks, and represents a minimum threshold that damage must exceed before it can be rolled. If your damage is greater than their hardness, it goes through at 100% strength, if it's less than hardness it's negated completely.
