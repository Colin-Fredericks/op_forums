            Originally posted by MoroseMorgan View Post
            Had to do some digging, but back in May TGUEIROS actually did a pretty good analysis of Steel Devil beyond what I had always heard of it being a "win moar" style.

            http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/m...92#post1216292
            I'm going to add a few things to that post actually, hopefully without going over the same material too much. There's a few points that I'll take a look at.

            Steel Devil is For Beating Opponents That You'd Beat Anyway

            Yes, that's true, that's one of the things it's good at, but that's not a bad thing. Let's say you're facing a foe with 4 defense and you've got an attack of 20 dice with a stunt and two motes using short daiklaves. Then you spend 3 motes into Triple Attack Technique putting you equal with your per round regen. You're doing 10 + (Strength) + (Dex) + (Charge) + (essence) + Overflow damage with that attack. With Naked Fang Draw it's not hard to start a fight with a full bar of charge, and assuming strength 3 that's a damage of +24 + (essence)*, with a light weapon. For 5 motes. 2 of which are boosting accuracy to help you hit anyway. If you're in Steel Devil Form it gets even cheaper.

            So why does that matter if you were going to win anyway? Firstly, if you're facing off against a big bad guy and a few of his lesser mook guys, then you've just driven one of them into the ground and gotten some crazy initiative off them while barely trying. Secondly though, even for fights that don't have a big bad guy right there, winning with little effort matters. For one if you're in a dodgy area not flaring your anima is great. For another your ally, who's using melee and blows half his mote pool crushing lesser, foes will be much less prepared for the final big boss. Lastly, although this matters somewhat less due to how badass most PCs are, even little enemies can be dangerous if you don't deal with them quickly. If you are trying to save motes and not flare, then that means greater risk.

            Steel Devil is For Group Fights Not Solo

            I wouldn't necessarily say that, allow me to suggest a stragegy. Absolutely agree with the other posters here that Steel Devil loves fighting in groups, either to apply an onslaught penalty for her allies, or greatly benefitting off it herself. If one of your allies MAKES your opponent have 3 defense, well that's just grand. On the other hand though, even in a duel, or a duel style portion of a larger fight, you have options, and one of them is to use an initiative flip like melee does with One Weapon Two Blows. If your opponent starts with higher initiative than you, which isn't hard since you dump all yours into Charge off Join Battle, and you attack them, sending them below you in initiative, then before their defense resets you get to attack again. This will allow you to onslaught with yourself, no allies needed.

            What would be nice, if you could, is to go absolutely all out, and try to get at least a double attack technique on the first hit, if you can do that, you've won. It's not so bad, Exalted favours attacking over defending, especially with light weapons. Going all out you've got an attack pool of 28 with a stunt, and up to 3 more auto successes from better stunts and willpower. This isn't really helpful against a Solar who's likewise going all out on defense. You'll never get a double attack off on them, sadly.

            If you're fighting somebody who's got like 7-8 defense though, that's actually doable. If it DOES happen, and you proceed to launch a Steel Devil Strike afterwards, then you've just lowered his initiative to lower than yours, given a -3 onslaught penalty, and done some damage. Then you get to go again, and his defense is at 4-5 to start out with. Go all out again for a triple attack technique and their defense will be down to ~0. That in turn lets you do your Steel Devil Strike into an Empty Mind Strike into a Dervish Blade Frenzy, into another one of the former. At this point their defense is so low you don't need to spend any motes on hitting. Which is good because all those charms cost motes and willpower, something you'll be out of afterwards.

            When it doesn't work, it sucks

            The above tactic also doesn't work on any Solar who's taken Bulwark Stance for themselves, obviously. It also combos well enough with Fire Dragon to get boosts to every attack in that second flurry that I outlined above, but that doesn't help if you can't get it off. This is going to be kind of campaign dependent, if your ST never throws enemies at you with less than 9-10 defense, you're just not going to have a good time. There's a few tricks you can still use like Cross-Blade Catch into Iron Lotus Unfurling, and Seconds Before Strife into Sonic Slash, but your main gimmick is hamstringed.

            You can look at this one of two ways. Well, assuming your campaign isn't just fighting Dawns, in which case you should look the way of another MA. First, you could just diversify your martial arts. Steel Devil can share form weapons with Snake, Single Point, Fire Dragon, and debatably Crane. All of these styles have ways to shore up your defenses against the kinds of targets you're bad at fighting, or in some cases apply a defense penalty that you can then take advantage of with Steel Devil.

            The other tactic is just to understand that your Steel Devil Style is not great at what it's not great at, and console yourself with the fact that no other style is as efficient at doing what you do either. Also, personally, I find that fights that don't involve the entire group are really rare, most STs like to avoid them because it usually sucks for the players not involved. So really it's just how often you run into dudes with casual onslaught negators.


            *I'm counting Triple Attack's "damage rerolls" here as just bonus damage, because for pools that big it basically is.


            EDIT: After a clarification by Vance Triple Attack Technique is not nearly as good as I thought it was.

                                Originally posted by DrLoveMonkey View Post

                                Oh really? I haven't really looked into it, what's so broken about RD?
                                1) Getting dual 5 shooters for the style pretty much removes the need to reload for most combats. I've rarely seen combat go above 3 turns.

                                2) The form applies aim actions on targets, making it so that you can fire out to medium range every turn without aiming. Yes it has an activation condition to go with it, but the dicepool for doing so is generous. Firewands are short, but the moment you find a way around this the style explodes. Firewand evocations have to be very careful with range extensions as a result.

                                3) Because you're aiming on someone always, Kiss of the Sun gives +3 automatic damage every turn as a Solar. Phoenix Flies on Golden Wings is also efficient to the point of insanity, giving additional automatic successes on damage. Total both of those for extremely punishing withering attacks combined with the form means you're not missing often.

                                4) Dancing Devil Trigger Finger + Caress of 1000 Hells = Pretty much instant encounter ended if you go with the rule of "AoE attacks need only to supplement one attack". Single point may do more damage in a single hit, but if Righteous Devil allows this combination you can snap entire encounters in half.

Many of them were not updated to match the current balance of the game and that needs to factor into how people evaluate them and other MA's in the future.
