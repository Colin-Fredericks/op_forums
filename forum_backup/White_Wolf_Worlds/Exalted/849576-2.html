    Dice-adding charms get much, much better, as do Defence-increasing charms.

    For example, Harmonious Presence Meditation becomes a really good dice adder that all social characters will want, rather than just a way to reduce your mote costs in scenes with a lot of social attacks.

    Arguably this is a good thing.

    It also gives MA a big advantage over Melee and Dodge, because Melee and Dodge don't have a lot of charms to boost your Defence (they don't need to, they have excellencies), whereas there are a fair few MA charms that do stuff like give you +2 Evasion or whatever.

    Also, all non-Exalts become more threatening against Exalts.

    Arguably this is also a good thing.

    Basically, it's another thing where I don't think the game breaks as such, but it definitely makes some stuff better and other stuff worse.

    There are a few charms that you'd need to fiddle with since they do stuff relating to excellencies, of course.

            Originally posted by Valles View Post
            What breaks if you just cut out excellencies, so that nobody gets them?
            You lose the baseline ability, as a player, to decide when a roll is important and when it's not; when you really want to succeed, and when you're okay with failure. A WP for success doesn't go far. When you start adding in Charms, they allow you to make that decision again, but it takes a lot more Charm investment to get to that point.

            I don't think it breaks anything besides maybe really high-level combat, since fighting a Solar-equivalent with a persistent Defense of 7 becomes very difficult to even hit without being able to throw an Excellency down every so often.

            Besides that it's fine, but as the Wizard of Oz said, it does vastly change the worth of dice-adder and dice-trick Charms. Especially those "add a full free excellency" once-per scene Charms.

                        Originally posted by JayTee View Post

                        Mostly the above.

                        I know that sounds weird that I think you'll simultaneously encourage and discourage diversity, but keep in mind that different players will react differently to the change.
