                                                            #375
                                                            04-24-2016, 11:21 AM
                                                            Originally posted by Chejop Kejak View Post
                                                            Mercury, your quips and banter are always a delight to read. But for the last time, it was my first command job, everyone TOLD me that the Thousandfold Alabaster Physician could regrow Exalts limbs instantly, the Southern Censor really WAS a Primordial sympathizer, I replaced those damn flowers, and eventually Dancing Mountain even came to like her Jade leg more than the original one. CAN WE PLEASE MOVE ON.

                                                            Really? Does that make much of a difference, when neither of the styles we've heard of her using are in the 3E Core?
                                                            In 2E, without Enlightenment, there was no way to mechanically differentiate mortal martial artists from one another without the use of specialties. When I was in a 2E mindset I was thinking about Boot as an Enlightened Mortal, just so she could do cool stuff.

                                                            In 3E, I don't need charms for that! Boot simply has dots in different Styles, and she uses lots of Gambits. Things like the Golden Janissary Style pressure point strikes that have disabling effects on creatures of darkness don't need for me to justify her attaining enlightenment, they only need her to have an understanding of how pressure points work on the types of monsters she's familiar with. Having a rough idea of how powerful heroic mortals are supposed to be in comparison to the Exalted storywise (from running a Mortals game for over ten sessions in 3E) has been really useful for me. I want the comic to remain if not canon then still possible according the game's rules. And 3E martial arts gives me a lot more interesting room to do really neat but not-magic things.

                                                            Also: Boot has taken a sub-optimal path. She's got dots in three different martial arts styles. I like that she's pouring her life into the XP sink: it fits her backstory and her motivations pretty well.

                                                            So I'm making God-Kicking Boot, an Exalted webcomic, now. Updates on Sundays. Full-color, mediocre but slowly improving art. It's a thing.
