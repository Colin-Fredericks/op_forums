                            Originally posted by JohnDoe244 View Post
                            Otherwise, yes, I agree with TheCountAlucard the "Elite Troops/Champion" profile is for the likes of Hector, Theseus, Castor, Leonidas I, Saladin, Ghengis Khan, Kal Drogo. You can make a battle group of them (that's literally what the Argonauts were -- it's very Exalted). But it's not something that a random peasant kingdom is lousy with.
                            I don't think this is what is intended exactly. First, most of these characters are most famous for commanding other troops, and if they appeared in a game would likely be making some Command rolls, but the Elite Troops don't have a Command pool. I'm not so familiar with what abilities the first three ones had off the top of my head, but the last four deserve their own stat blocks, although you could accomplish this by taking the Elite Troops block, modifying the weapons and armor a bit, and adding additional dice pools for other actions they would have, likely in the 6 to 9 range.

                            Second, after Elite Troops / Champion it says Elite Bodyguard. None of these characters are bodyguards. Some of them probably had bodyguards that are mostly forgotten by history. They still would have been Elite Troops. I agree that most forces in Creation won't have a standing army, but any ruler who does have the resources to form a standing army of substantial size has the resources to pick a couple top soldiers, give them special training and "enchanted brocades", and make them his bodyguards.

                            Third, "shock troops" and "janissaries", while rare, when you find them you will usually find them in substantial groups. There were tens of thousands of janissaries at one time in the Ottoman Empire, and the point of shock troops is to get enough powerful fighters together to break up the enemy formation. Running into a battlegroup of them should be much, much less surprising then running into the Argonauts, a legend even among legends.

                            If your characters have reason to fight an army of thousands, and decide to run / fly/ sneak around them and go straight for the leaders (an appropriate strategy for PCs in almost any game, but surely in Exalted), they shouldn't be surprised to encounter a battlegroup of Elite Troops when they arrive. They should probably be surprised if it's more than Size 1 or 2 though, sure.
