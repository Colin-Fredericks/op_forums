I've been assuming that for scene-length charms, the charm only ends if it says a crash ends it, but that the player doesn't get the benefits of the charm until they come out of crash again; I'm less sure about denying the benefits of tick-long or "until next turn" defenses if the player is crashed after they are activated. Is that correct?

                    And then there are a handful of specific oddities:

                    Aura of Invulnerability (WFHW pg. 245) grants additional temporary health levels and soak for a scene. Are they just unavailable until the character comes out of crash, retaining any damage they had already taken? Or unaffected by being in crash?

                    Unbinding the Inner Flame (WFHW pg. 222) grants a number of benefits including one mote and one initiative every turn, but notes that the initiative is not gained when in crash. Does that mean that all the other benefits besides the initiative are retained in crash?

                    Karvara's Final Fury Rampage (AotC pg. 173) presumably loses the +2 initiative per turn while in crash, but does it retain the ability to act without being attuned, or does crashing it end it's rampage?
