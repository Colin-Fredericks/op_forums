                        You know, I've pledged and all, but man, OPP kickstarters often frustrate me.

                        All I want is a pledge tier that gets me:

                        1) The standard, plain old physical copy of whatever they're kickstarting.
                        2) A PDF of same.

                        That's never, ever ever ever available, or at least not on anything I'm ever interested in. Tiers always go right from "PDF only plus a lot of ancillary stuff I don't usually care about" straight up to "premium hardcover."
