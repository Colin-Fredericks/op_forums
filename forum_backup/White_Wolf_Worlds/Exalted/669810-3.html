                                            Generally, I consider the differences between various Exalt animas to be obvious no matter how much you try to confuse someone.

                                            A Solar with a red anima cannot possibly be mistaken for a Fire Aspect, as the Solar's anima is TOO RED, its looks fiery but not like actual fire or anything, and something about it just seems more symbolic.

                                            the Fire Aspects anima......is actual freaking fire, with heat and the usual mix of orange, red and white and so on.

                                            The Chosen of Mars anima when it flares is not fire, and just a red glow or red strands of fate weaving itself around them.

                                            now for the difference between a Green Solar, a Wood Aspect, a Chosen of Secrets and an Infernal:
                                            Green Solar: it looks like a deeper softer green than an Infernal, a flame of hope
                                            Wood Aspect: their anima explicitly actual life and things growing around them.
                                            Chosen of Secret: soft green glow and green threads of the Loom as with the other example
                                            Infernal: their green fire burns like hate, it looks sickly and yellowish and there is a whole bunch of cracks or lightning running through it.

                                            .......perhaps it would be better to find a way to show the animas and how they look than to try to describe them.
