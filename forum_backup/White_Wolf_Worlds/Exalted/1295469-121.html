            Apologies if this has been answered elsewhere, as these threads have gotten huge. I'd like to get clarification on some specific interactions.

            1) If you beat someone's Resolve with social influence, and they spend Willpower to resist, is the social influence considered successful for the purposes of Charms such as Stillness-Drawing Meditation or Soul-Bracing Momentous Power (because you beat their Resolve)? Or is it unsuccessful because they rejected the influence (they spent Willpower to resist)?

            2) Soul-Bracing Momentous Power grants non-Charm successes on the Exalt's next social influence attempt. Can this grant non-Charm successes to social influence rolls from other abilities, such as Presence or Socialize? Or even non-standard social influence roles that use other abilities to make social influence rolls?

            3) Does Soul-Bracing Momentous Power replace the influence cost to resist if I beat the target's Resolve on the subsequent influence attempt? ie, I use Soul-Bracing Momentous Power which is successful. I then use Terrifying Battle Shriek, which beats my opponent's resolve. Do they spend 1 willpower to resist Terrifying Battle Shriek, causing Terrifying Battle Shriek to be unsuccessful, and thus completely ignore the effects of Soul-Bracing Momentous Power because the influence roll was not successful? Or is the influence roll successful because it beat their Resolve, and they now must pay three Willpower on (Solar's Essence) separate days if they wanted to resist the effects of Terrifying Battle Shriek?

            4) Soul-Bracing Momentous Power requires errata as written, anyway. It's a reflexive Charm with a variable cost. The Charm text says "Before taking a social influence action, roll the Exalt’s Willpower, rerolling non-successes once. Remaining non-successes determine the Charm’s activation cost in Willpower points." Since it rolls the Exalt's Willpower and not the Exalt's temporary Willpower, it is possible to arrive at a cost higher than the Exalt's temporary Willpower, ie, the Exalt cannot pay the cost. Chapter Six on Charms states "Players (including the Storyteller) must openly declare which Charms their characters are using, and all Charms (unless their text indicates otherwise) must be declared, and their costs spent, before any dice are rolled." In this scenario, the cost cannot be determined before the dice are rolled, therefore, I must assume that the Charm fails with no effect if the cost cannot be paid. However, the Charm is reflexive; the Exalt could simply reflexively reattempt the Charm as many times as necessary until they arrive at a cost they can pay. This would also allow an Exalt with 0 temporary Willpower to use Soul-Bracing Momentous Power infinitely; they would reflexively activate the Charm until they successfully roll all successes on their Willpower roll, reducing the cost in Willpower to 0. There is no reset condition on the Charm to prevent this.

                                                    Originally posted by Robert Vance View Post
                                                    If they spend Willpower to resist, you did not successfully influence them.


                                                    It's Performance only.


                                                    It replaces the normal Willpower cost to resist.
                                                    Thanks for the reply. Please help me out here with the timing, then, because the wording used in Performance is much different from the wording used elsewhere in the book. For example, let's take Enemy-Castigating Solar Judgment:

                                                    Originally posted by Enemy-Castigating Solar Judgment, Exalted 3e pg 371

                                                    If the influence is successful, it costs an additional Willpower to resist.
                                                    This implies that a social influence roll that overcomes Resolve is considered successful influence, although the opponent has the option to still resist by spending Willpower.

                                                    Originally posted by Infectious Zealotry Approach, Exalted 3e pg 364
                                                    When the Solar perceives a character who is about to put off making a social influence attempt that the Solar believes should be attempted now, she may use this Charm, rolling a ([Charisma or Manipulation] + Performance) based action against her target’s Resolve. The Solar inveighs against inaction and scorns all dereliction. If successful, her feverish contagion of will causes the target to forget all other concerns, goals, and ambitions, focusing socially on a single social objective for the rest of the scene. This effect can be resisted by paying one Willpower, but the affected character may only pay this Willpower if confronted by some circumstance that would force him to abandon a Defining Intimacy in order to carry out his social influence action.
                                                    This seems to support the Enemy-Castigating Solar Judgment interpretation. The roll is not explicitly stated to be an influence roll, but it is rolled against Resolve as if it is a social influence roll and can be resisted for 1 Willpower in a similar fashion.

                                                    Originally posted by Fulminating Word, Exalted 3e pg 371

                                                    Upon successfully beating a target’s Resolve with a bargain, threaten or persuade action, but before a Decision Point has been entered, she may activate this Charm to magnify the impact of her argument. Doing so raises the cost of the Decision Point to two Willpower.
                                                    This does not make the same implications that overcoming Resolve is considered successful influence, but does add a timing window after Resolve was beaten and before a Decision Point was entered.

                                                    Originally posted by Soul-Snaring Pitch, Exalted 3e pg 287

                                                    If the target’s Willpower is greater than the Lawgiver’s Essence, the Solar rolls a (Manipulation + Bureaucracy) persuade action with (Essence) automatic successes against the target’s Resolve. If successful, the target is not only convinced that he must attain whatever the Solar is selling, but its value in his eyes is multiplied by the number of extra successes on the roll. In order to resist this effect, a character must spend (the Solar’s Essence) in Willpower.
                                                    This states "if successful" in reference to the roll against the target's Resolve, and then modifies the Willpower cost to resist.

                                                    The core book makes specific reference to the idea of "successful influence" as something that should almost be a defined term.

                                                    Originally posted by Social Influence, Exalted 3e pg 213
                                                    Successful influence rolls allow you to alter a character’s feelings or beliefs or to convince him to do something for you, based on the social action or Charm used.
                                                    This idea is referenced later on as well.

                                                    Originally posted by Resisting Influence Rolls, Exalted 3e pg 218
                                                    The base difficulty for an influence roll is always the Resolve rating of the character targeted by the roll. On a failed roll, the character is unimpressed or unmoved by whatever argument or offer was made. On a successful roll, the target is persuaded or moved by your words.
                                                    This states that an influence roll is rolled against the Resolve rating of the target. Failure is (seemingly) defined as failing to hit the Resolve of the target, and success is (seemingly) defined as hitting the Resolve of the target, and this is stated before any options to resist with Willpower are given. The rules for spending Willpower to resist the influence, or entering a Decision Point, are outlined in subsequent pages.

                                                    Now let's look at how Performance Charms are worded.

                                                    Originally posted by Soul-Firing Performance, Exalted 3e pg 361
                                                    Roll (Charisma + Performance) with (Essence) automatic successes against the group’s Resolve. If successful, the group is struck with an emotion conveyed by the performance, and each affected target reconsiders a decision he made in his past, connected to that emotion.
                                                    This implies that success is based on beating Resolve as well, however, the target could still resist the influence by citing another intimacy in the subsequent Decision Point and spending Willpower to reject the influence.

                                                    Originally posted by Stillness-Drawing Meditation, Exalted 3e pg 361

                                                    Whenever she successfully influences a large group with a Performance-based action, she regains (Essence) motes.
                                                    If the wording of Enemy-Castigating Solar Judgment is correct, then beating a target's Resolve means the social influence was successful, regardless of whether they spent Willpower to resist the effects of successful influence. Therefore, Stillness-Drawing Meditation should restore motes regardless of whether the influence was ultimately rejected as long as Resolve was beaten.

                                                    Originally posted by Soul-Bracing Momentous Power, Exalted 3e pg 362

                                                    In addition, if this influence is successful, the affected character must pay three Willpower on (Solar’s Essence) different days before he can overcome the influence, and may only do so if confronted with how his actions might be damaging to his Major or Defining Intimacies.
                                                    Similarly, if we assume that "successful influence" means that the target's Resolve was beaten, then Soul-Bracing Momentous Power seemingly replaces the normal rules for resisting social influence outlined in pp. 218-219. However, if we go back to your clarifications:

                                                    Originally posted by Robert Vance View Post
                                                    If they spend Willpower to resist, you did not successfully influence them.


                                                    It's Performance only.


                                                    It replaces the normal Willpower cost to resist.
                                                    I am having difficulty in understanding how it can be simultaneously true that influence is unsuccessful if the target spends Willpower to resist, but that Soul-Bracing Momentous Power replaces the Willpower cost to resist. The text that replaces the standard rules and costs for resisting the social influence is contingent on the statement "if this influence is successful". In order for the influence to be successful, you state that the target cannot spend Willpower to resist. Therefore, the timing window would appear to be:

                                                    1) Soul-Bracing Momentous Power is used and rolled.
                                                    2) The Solar makes a subsequent social influence roll against the Resolve of a target.
                                                    3) The roll overcomes the Resolve of the target.
                                                    4) The target follows the standard rules for resisting social influence on pp. 218-219.
                                                    5) If the target resists the influence using those standard rules, Soul-Bracing Momentous Power does nothing. If the target does not resist the influence, then the influence is successful, and the rider kicks in that increases the Willpower cost in the event that another party attempts to overturn your social influence.

                                                    If we stick with the interpretation that social influence is successful if it overcomes Resolve, but can still be rejected, then the Charm seemingly works as you've clarified it's intended. However, that causes issues with the following Performance Charms:

                                                    Originally posted by Fury Inciting Speech, Exalted 3e pg 364
                                                    This acts as a ([Charisma or Manipulation] + Performance) inspire action against the average Resolve of the crowd. If successful, the speech ignites anger at a specific cause, but leaves the mob as a disorganized, riotous mass. However, for every three extra successes on the roll, the Solar’s player can direct the mob at a single target linked to the crowd’s anger. For example, with nine extra successes, the Solar could lead a mob in Nexus to attack the Guild Hall, set fire to the docks as a distraction, and free the slaves on auction row.
                                                    If social influence is successful based on overcoming Resolve, then this Charm, as worded, turns the mob into a riotous mass regardless of whether the inspire influence is ultimately rejected using Willpower.

                                                    Originally posted by Dogmatic Contagion Discipline, Exalted 3e pg 364
                                                    When the Solar uses oration to inspire a crowd, she may supplement the roll with this Charm. Extra successes on the roll are passed as bonus dice to those characters who she’s able to successfully influence, for the express purpose of passing on the Solar’s influence. Such characters will retain these bonus dice for (Essence) days. Anyone they are able to successfully influence with the Solar’s arguments gain the same bonus to do so to others, although not the impetus.
                                                    This Charm has similar issues as Fury Inciting Speech, if my understanding is correct. The effect occurs regardless of whether the social influence is rejected.

                                                    To me, it seems easier to errata or clarify Fury Inciting Speech and Dogmatic Contagion Discipline to state that the effects only occur if the target(s) do not reject the influence utilizing Willpower. A similar errata/clarification can be made for Stillness-Drawing Meditation if the intent is that the motes are only gained if the targets do not reject the influence roll. However, my interpretation of how the system presently works may be wrong, so if I've gone astray here, please help me understand how it should work instead.

                                                    For example, take the following scenario. Is this a correct interpretation?

                                                    1) I use Soul-Bracing Momentous Power.
                                                    2) I use Fury Inciting Speech.
                                                    3) My roll for Fury Inciting Speech beats the Resolve of the targets with sufficient threshold successes to direct the crowd toward a specific task.
                                                    4) I regain motes from Stillness-Drawing Meditation.
                                                    5) The targets now must spend three Willpower on (Solar's Essence) separate days to reject the influence. They choose to do so.
                                                    6) I now lead them to go burn down the Satrap's manor, since we're still on day one and I'm an Essence 3 Solar, so they have not had the opportunity to fully reject the influence.

                                                    If that isn't a correct interpretation, what happens? And does Soul-Bracing Momentous Power work the same way for both social influence that must be resisted via Decision Point, and social influence that can be resisted via simple Willpower expenditure?
