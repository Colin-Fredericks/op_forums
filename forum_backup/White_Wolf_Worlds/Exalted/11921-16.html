        For most mortals, she only brought out the regular scissors. They were nice scissors, to be sure, cold iron with handles of obsidian, but they were only special in so far as the entity that wielded them. This, however, was no mere mortal, but a Prince Of Earth. Saturn brought the jade scissors.

        Envanel Tepet's hun soul glowered down at his body, sorely disappointed in its performance.

        "It was my ankle," He told her.

        I'M SURE IT WAS.

        Envanel looked curiously as the Maiden Of Ending reached into him. Normally, he'd be frightened but at this point the worst had passed, as far as he was concerned. Honestly, this was more fascinating than anything else. Envanel was no sorcerer, but he had to admire how Saturn reached into all three of him, his body and both his souls, with just one grab and one hand and pulled out one thread. A thread that somehow came from all three of them but just one place at the same time. It was a bit geometrical nonsense that normally only sorcerers, demons or fae got to see.

        He still felt, however, the need to defend him, "I twisted my ankle, see? Jumping to the defense of my elders, of course." He twisted trying to outrun his elders, actually. He glared over at the shining glowing gold beacon of the Lawgiver, hastily retreating from his corpse.

        "If my ankle hadn't been in bad shape, I would've gotten him."

        His po soul growled in agreement.

        I'M CERTAIN YOU WOULD HAVE. That was a lie. The Night Caste had set everything up perfectly for that ambush. Envanel had only had time to turn around because the Solar had wanted to see the light go out of his eyes. However, there was no point in breaking the poor dear's illusions.

        "I'll get him eventually," Envanel said with certainty.

        YOU'RE NOT THINKING OF BECOMING A GHOST, ARE YOU?

        "What? No, don't be silly! I'll get him next time," He twirled his finger in demonstration, "You know, next time around."

        SPEAKING OF WHICH. Saturn took the jade scissors to the Fate of his life and cut it cleanly.

        GOOD BYE, ENVANEL TEPET.

        "Next time," Envanel muttered confidently as his hun soul slowly faded, "Maybe I'll be an Air Aspect next time. That way I'll-" The rest of his words were snatched away by the silent roar of Lethe.

        Saturn made her sign and left. There was more work to be done.

        There was no end to endings.

                                            You know, it occurs to me I haven't actually suggested one of since the new forums came up. Shame on me.

                                            Playing fast, loose, and with the caveat that I haven't touched any of these in years.

                                            ****************

                                            A disillusioned Immaculate Monk is imbued with the power of the Zenith Caste, and travels the length and breadth of the Realm trying to discover what it is he's become. While he has allies--his Lunar mate is a deadly archer, and he's attracted the company of a jovial-but-wicked day caste--he's picked up enemies, too. Along the way, his devil-may-care attitude garners the wrath of authorities mortal, divine, and--in the case of a sidereal scorned deeply by fate--in between. Before long, he even has a relentless, terrifying, and brutal Dusk caste on his heels.

                                            The deck's stacked against him, but he's got a mission: to confront the Unconquered Sun himself and take him to task for turning his face from his Chosen, and letting Creation fall as far as it has.

                                            ****************

                                            A glitch in fate draws a young Terrestrial to the East, where she is blessed to be the herald of Sextes Jylis himself. Accompanied by seven champions imbued with the dragon's souls, she seeks to defend her adopted land from Realm incursion, where a similar hiccup in the workings of the world have left her closest friend as the herald of Pasiap. But there is a greater price to pay than mere loss of a friend. For when she brings the dragon of Wood into the world, it may bring her her heart's desires, but it may well consume her in the process. And even though she has fallen in love with one of her champions, his existence is finite--even if she succeeds and survives, he cannot remain incarnate for long.

                                            ****************

                                            A young man discovers a daiklave and takes it, drawing the second breath as one of the Dawn Caste. Now, with a runaway Dynast and a mischievous Lunar, he must travel across Creation to protect a series of war manses. Along the way, he encounters mountain folk, benign raksha, the powerful elemental guardians of the manses, and a small team of entrepreneurial scavenger lords. Some will help. Some will most definitely not. But if the boy and his friends fail, the powers of Malfeas will have their hands on the Sword of Creation. And they discover all-too-late that the Dynast's lover--an officer in the Imperial Legions--has a dark destiny upon him. They do fail, and the world cowers.

                                            Perhaps more terrifying is Heaven's response: Unleash the Kukla.
