                                    Originally posted by Epimetheus
                                    is completely different than a weapon and much harder to control.

                                    How much harder to control? They can't learn spells without texts and tutors. The kinds of workings they can perform in the first place have limited scope, and workings take weeks to accomplish; it's not hard to think of ways in which people could be regulated to make it hard for them to devote their attention to it. They're being watched by the Exalted, including powerful warriors who can likely put them down without much trouble if they stray too far.

                                    And of course, there are the benefits of the soft power approach; positive reinforcement and material benefits. Being a monk might not be as good as you could get striking out on your own, but it's more than you had on your own and has the benefit of being socially acceptable and resonating with your pre-existing values, which most examples are liable to want to stick to.

                                    Sorcery is not like being Celestial Exalted, it doesn't present so severe a threat that a 1% chance of a sorcerer effectively going rogue has to be treated like an absolute certainty.
