                                                        If you kill a sorcerer, are her bound demons:
                                                        a) Free
                                                        b) Free for the rest of their remaining summoning time, then they go back to Hell
                                                        c) Still got a Defining Intimacy to their summoner, she's just not got any orders for them because she's dead, but they might avenge her or follow her last orders or something until the spell ends
