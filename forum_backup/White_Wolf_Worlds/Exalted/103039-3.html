                    Sorry, I just have to double-post here, because that is such a bizarre statement. I mean, what do you mean would be silly? You know that already happens, right? Have you by chance had a look at the Lunar charmset?
                    • Luna's Hidden Face: At Essence 3, take the form of any Wyld creature.
                    • Hearth-and-Flame Shell: At Essence 4, take the form of any elemental. If applicable, your body is now made of electricity or wind or fire, with all the expected consequences.
                    • Green Sun Child: At Essence 4, become any demon, any circle. Such as Ligier, provided you can kill and eat him first (edit: or taste his blood or have sex with him, if you have those Knacks).
                    • Hungry Dream Cloak: At E4, become a Fair Folk. Learn Fair Folk Charms beginning at E5.
                    • Heart-Theft of the Behemoth: At Essence 5, become any behemoth up to a certain size. At E7, become any behemoth of any size. Go to Thorns, drink Juggernaut's blood, become Juggernaut.
                    • Moon and Earth Song: At E7, shapeshift into a mountain, a river, a forest, a desert or any other geographic feature, at a cost of 10 motes. Become immune to virtually any damage and oh right, you can still move.


                    I mean I like you a lot, Isator, really. You seem like someone it'd be nice to play with someday. But in your comments about Lunars on this and other threads, I have to wonder sometimes if you've really ever looked at Lunars. Stuff like:

                    Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post
                    All I know is that, when I think of something that shapeshifts in the same basic paradigm of therianthrope legends, and something that would shapeshift into, say, Angels from Evangelion, the idea of them both being the same thing feels kind of silly.
                    The Lunars book actually references giant monster anime like Evangelion as a source of inspiration for Lunar games. You know the Knacks from DotFA/Glories explicitly allow your own spirit shape to be larger than a city, right? Larger than a city, and made of fire? You don't even need to eat anything's heart to do that. That's your own, true form.

                    And you know you can use core Lunar Knacks like Hybrid Body Rearrangement (Essence 3) to mix various features of different shapes, so that instead of merely being a wolf of flame five miles long, you are now a dragon of flame five miles long, with eyes of lightning and claws of death-essence? Even at E5, you can take that form at a size large enough to engage armies in mass combat.

                    It's just, it's not like these are obscure tricks... they're just the canon uses of Lunar powers. It's so weird to say it'd be silly to have Lunars shapeshift in ways like the Shintai charms allow. They do shapeshift like that. That's their power. There's nothing in the setting a Lunar can't already turn into.

                                    Yes TSk, you're pretty much right, originally the intention behind Lunars was to have them have powers that would make Evangelion-style change possible. It was done so haphazardly and so badly that many people didn't even realize. And then Infernals pretty much did the same, except they did it right. Everybody forgot that, yes, originally in 2e Lunars were supposed to be able to do that. But that's not saying much, because what 2e Lunars were and what weren't supposed to do is lost in time and nobody can ever know, because their book really gives us no insight in the matter.

                                    Skip a few years, John and Holden came, with glowing halos, speaking words so sweet and seemingly wise, they spoke so matter-a-factly about what Lunars aren't, without ever once actually saying what they are, they talked about barbarian aesthetics with great conviction showing us all how it absolutely demands from Lunars a limitted ability to shapeshift, how shapshifting isn't really even their thing, but just one of their thing, not even the most important or definitive one, certainly not the one they are masters of and since, remember, it has to work with the barbarian vibe (everything has to, barbarian aesthetics is, after all, the most important thing about Lunars and their definitive feature), they should be able to change into a limitted suit of beings, dervied from legends and folklore John and Holden are interested in incorporating into Exalted. After all, you don't want to tell a story of your own, do you? Noooo, you want to retell the legends you know, surely! That will never get boring!

                                    And everybody pretty much bought it. Which, as long as it's executed right, is ok. But so many jimmies were rustled in the process that I wonder if it was worth it in the end.

                                                    1. In some general way, using your shapeshifting powers tend to gradually add to your Limit.
                                                    2. At Limit Break, curse effects are modeled pretty much like they are now, except that instead of acting like your spirit shape, they compel you to act unlike your spirit shape. So if your spirit shape is a wolf, you might become disloyal or something.
                                                    3. Optionally, every Limit Break also adds one dot to your permanent Limit, which in addition to chimeric effects could compound the existing curse, so you are now disloyal and selfish, two curses instead of one.
                                                    4. In some general way, spending time in your spirit shape (or evoking it somehow) decreases your Limit and/or permanent Limit.
