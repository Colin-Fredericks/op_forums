    The Tri-Khan of Ciaroscuro has gone mad - each day, he marries a new wife, and each night, kills her. The city lives in fear. But there is hope - the daughter of one of his Dragon-Blooded advisers has Exalted as an Eclipse. Not wanting to invite the Wyld Hunt or force her father to oppose her, she cannot fight the Tri-Khan. Instead, she volunteers to marry him, and each night tells him a story.

    Of course, the stories tend to have cliffhangers at daybreak, and her skill is such, no man could let her die without knowing the ending. But how long can she keep it up, without having to flare her Anima, and making everything so much worse?
    One Thousand and One Nights


    In Whitehall, there is a girl with mismatched eyes, and a difficult home. Eventually having enough, she runs away, getting caught in a massive tide, and washed into a hidden Fae Freehold and shadowland. Twenty four islands, each locked into being a specific time, divided between the Day, associated with the Wyld, and Night, associated with the Abyss, with one more island hidden away at the center by a perpetual storm. Despite the odd Wyld-touched she finds and her own background, the girl manages to fit in well, and even resembles a former Fair Folk Noble, once though to possibly bring peace to the lands by marrying the Ghost-touched Ruler of the Night, before being killed. Unfortunately, the Ghost-touched knows this, and seeks this reminder of his lost love, while his dark, unnamed Grandmother looks on, and things stir deep in the Ocean.
