                    Originally posted by raaabr View Post
                    So, this came up when I was talking with a player for my upcoming game. They wanted to know if Brawl, which is described in the ability book as punching, kicking, etc in an instinctive way, can be used while also holding other weapons. In this particular example, they wanted to know if, while wielding two flame pieces (The pistol forms), they'd be able to use brawl to attack in cases where their pieces are out of ammunition, without having to put them away. Soon enough, I ruled that since they had quick draw, they could sheathe the guns and flurry a brawl attack action instead, but I'm still curious.
                    Brawl can be used to make attacks by kicking, headbutting etc, so you can make brawl attacks with your hands full at no penalty by simply not using your hands. If your player doesn't want to kick, the rules for wielding multiple weapons specifically allow you to take an attack mechanically made with one weapon and stunt it as being done with the other. Which means that you can make brawl attacks while wielding your flame pieces at no penalty and stunt them as being pistol whips, point-blank rapid reload flame piece shots, stylish moves where you toss the flame pieces into the air, punch someone and then catch them, etc, in order to blend both abilities seamlessly.

                    My homebrew: Abyssals, Infernals, Dragon Kings, martial arts.
