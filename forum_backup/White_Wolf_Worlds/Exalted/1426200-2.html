                                                    No matter which Ability-based Exalt you built Yang as, she would Favor Resistance.

                                                    If you were to try to build Yang Xiao-Long as a Solar, you would definitely use Brawl rather than Fire Dragon Style. It wouldn’t even be a question.

                                                    If you were to try building Yang Xiao-Long as a Dragon Blood, you absolutely could not use Brawl and would need to use Fire Dragon Style.

                                                    It’s not simply about preserving her visual image of Fire, but the reality that Resistance’s Signature Fire Charm is the closest to emulating her Semblance, and Brawl’s Signature Water Charm is far from it, and both Charms are completely incompatible.

                                                    Making her an Earth Aspect is a compromise. The Earth Signature Charms for Brawl and Resistance are adjacent to Yang Xiao-Long’s “play style”, but not the most evocative of it.

                                                    At least Ember Celica is a pair of bracers rather than knuckle-dusters, so they’re still compatible with Fire Dragon Style.
