    I find that the signature characters make a good reference point for a game that began in vanilla Creation and you want to give a potential new player an idea of what's changed globally.

    Edit: I once tried to join a long-running game where the ST told me all about the players and major NPCs and I figured I had enough to go on. So I made a member of a character who had some relation to the Guild only to be told that the PCs had casually destroyed the Guild while they were in Nexus. I ended up with a sense of continuity lockout and ultimately didn't join the game, but I think I learned how to keep my own games more accessible from that experience.

                                                    I put the signature circle under Walker in Darkness as I’d already fleshed out the Mask of Winters’ Abyssals as a rogue’s gallery of reoccurring antagonists for my PCs.

                                                    My version of Mirth changed the most but hasn't had much screen time yet; she's loosely based on Jubal Early from Firefly in that she's got this air of professionalism hiding the fact that she's a sadist with a hair trigger-temper. The Lunar PC almost bit her in half which she survived but is only capable of walking thanks to an implant from the Physician.

                                                    By extension Physician is someone you’d actually trust to be your team’s medic. He’s still not a nice man but he’d be quite horrified if people assumed he was no more than a talentless butcher with a knack for necromancy.

                                                    Lady is still a seductress but dresses like she does on the MoEP cover and actually has dignity. There’s more going on there but it’s rather involved and incidental to her immediate presentation.

                                                    Poet and Disciple haven’t changed much. The most notable thing there was that Disciple surrendered to the PCs to prevent Mirth from getting bitten in two, was their prisoner for a time before they were ransomed back to the Walker in Darkness.

                                                    Currently they’re trying to stick together in the Walker’s absence.
