    Chargen Workshop: Earth Immaculate Monk

    07-01-2018, 04:07 PM
    Hello everyone,

    In contrast to a lot of other RPG forums, one of the things that doesn't seem to happen a lot around here is the posting of character builds and advice; so I thought to post a build thread!

    In all honesty, I was somewhat disappointed with how Dragon-Blooded turned out in 3E. However, my actual play experience in 3E is limited, so I'm willing to entertain the possibility that I'm wrong about how they play mechanically (the fluff being a whole other banana). To that end, I'll be making a character in a mixed online game, and I've decided to make a terrestrial exalt to really try it out; I want to crowdsource some of the character build to give the experiment its best shot.

    For now, I'm not going to get into the character's background; I've got a pretty good idea of who he is, but I want to focus this thread on the mechanics, both chargen and thoughts on how to progress the character (subject, of course, to what actually happens in play). The key constrains of the character are that he is 1) an Earth-aspect Immaculate monk and 2) a sorcerer. Thats a lot to cram into one character at chargen, but that's how I roll Let's get to it with stats and some thoughts:

    Mnemon Kazuma, called Five Iron Bells - Earth Aspect Immaculate Monk

    Attributes:
    Strength 3, Dexterity 5, Stamina 3, Charisma 2, Manipulation 2, Appearance 3, Perception 2, Intelligence 5, Wits 2

    My thought is that he needs Dexterity 5 for all of the normal reasons and Intelligence 5 because he's a sorcerer. I quibbled about splitting Strength and Stamina 3/3 or 4/2, but ended up going with 3/3 since plenty of charms test off stamina (despite being "ability" exalts).

    Abilities:
    Athletics 5, Awareness 5, Brawl 1, Dodge 2, Integrity 5, Linguistics 1, Lore 2, Martial Arts (Earth Dragon Style) 5, Medicine 1, Occult 5, Performance 2, Resistance 5, Socialize 2, Survival 1, everything else zero.

    Awareness: Duels, Medicine: Wounds I Inflict*, Occult: Sorcery, Martial Arts: Testubo, Lore: Immaculate Philosophy

    All of my bonus points save for the Martial Arts merit went here. (I used BP to buy the Martial Arts merit at this point in chargen so that I could put three of the starting 28 ability points into it.) I really didn't know what to do with Dodge; this will actually be my first time in Exalted (I've played since 1E) building a character with heavy armor and that mobility penalty, so I left it at 2 but favored it. I favored Performance because he's an itinerant monk and needs some basic ability to speak Immaculate philosophy to the masses; also prayers. Medicine and Survival I took because it seemed to fit a traveling monk; survival for being able to walk the roads and medicine to treat people in the small towns and villages that he visits. I'm still very much up-in-the-air about specialties.

    *Shout out to Scroll of Exalts

    Merits:
    Backing: Immaculate Order 2, Backing: House Mnemon 2, Language: Low Realm 1

    Marital Arts 4, Language: Old Realm 1, Well-Bred 2, Pain Tolerance 4, Artifact: Fist of Titans 3, Artifact: Reinforced Breastplate 3

    The first set of merits are his Dynastic merits. The backing merits just represent his place in the world. While I feel like Dynasts should start with both high and low realm, I don't recall seeing anything in the manuscript to that effect, so I took low realm, and any self-respecting sorcerer should speak old realm. I took well-bred because I like the idea of a celibate character with excellent breeding potential (I'd like to eventually take appearance to 5 to keep playing that theme). I took heavy armor because I figure that you may as well go all the way with an Earth monk... which brings us to the Fist of Titans. I'm skeptical that the charge mechanic is going to work in practice (you just don't have that many turns to stroke your goremaul, but the artifact is just so damn cool and eventually has the passive evocation for smash attacks (to go with Earth Dragon Form). Pain tolerance came in because I had 4 merits left over, and it fits Earth well; I also don't expect him to be able to avoid taking damage.

    Finishing Touches:
    Willpower: 5 Essence: 2 Personal: 13 Peripheral: 20/31 Committed: 11
    Resolve: 4 Guile: 2
    Join Battle: 7 Parry: 6 Evade: 2
    Rush: 8 Disengage: 5
    Soak: 16 (Reinforced Breastplate, ISoB control spell) Hardness: 10
    Health: -0,-1,-1,-1,-2,-2,-2,-4, I

    I would have liked to buy more willpower, but I put substantially all of my BP in abilities; I'd definitely welcome thoughts on that. Other than his decent Parry and soak, his defenses are not so great. His HL tract includes one Ox-Body Technique, and his soak include +Essence from being his control spell; on days when he knows that battle will be at hand, he can cast ISoB in the morning to boost his soak for the day and drop into Earth Dragon form for some more. I'm not including his intimacies, but he'll start with quite a few of them, many related to the Realm and the Scarlet Dynasty as well as the Immaculate Order.

    Charms:
    Earth Dragon Style
    Become the Hammer - The excellency... a necessary purchase
    Stone Dragon's Skin - At 5m 1i, this charm is wildly underwhelming, but it is what it is.
    Force of the Mountain - A basic damage adder that keys off strength; I'll take it!
    Stillness-of-Stone Atemi - an interesting, very thematic setup charm... I'd like to see how this works in practice
    Unmoving Mountain Stance - The initiative denial is interesting, though seems a bit weak for the cost; the movement denial is thematic, but 6m is still a lot.
    ​Earth Dragon Form - More smash, more soak... can't go wrong with that.

    Occult
    Hidden Secrets Whisper - The excellency... a necessary purchase
    Spirit-Detecting Mirror - it's the best that DBs can do in terms of seeing spirits :/ the repurchase is cool, though
    Spirit-Grounding Shout - DBs just have a hard time seeing and hitting dematerialized, but a monk's gotta deal with those things
    Secret Wind Revelation - This one is actually quite fun
    Terrestrial Circle Sorcery - Now, we're in the money!
    Invulnerable Skin of Bronze - Control Spell. I took this for the earth themes, for the passive soak, and because it's a day-long defense boost that can be cast morning when he knows that there will be ish that day. He just isn't going to be able to generate enough SM to fire off one-turn spells at all reliably, so my first spell purchase in-game will be Virtuous Guardian Flame because it's cool, can help his defenses, and can be cast each morning; I like the idea of him being known for trailing a fiery will'o'wisp wherever he goes.

    Miscellaneous
    Granite Curtain of Serenity - More integrity when he needs it
    Precision Observation Method - More awareness when he needs it
    Effortlessly Rising Flame - More athletics when he needs it
    Ox-Body Technique - He's not great at not getting hit, so he's going to need more of these


    Please, I'd very much appreciate any insight or advice you'd be willing to offer to make him the best monk that he can be coming out of chargen! Thanks!

      You seem to have bought Martial Arts with merit points because you thought you needed to buy it at an earlier point? Char gen doesn't have any kind of order of operations. You can totally put 3 of your 28 ability dots into Martial Arts, no problem, and buy that merit with your normal selection of Merit dots.
      So, if you want to get rid of Pain Tolerance, you could spend 4bp somewhere else.

          Hmm, is that written somewhere? I don't mean to be lawyerly about it, but the writing of the rules would imply otherwise.                                   Originally posted by aluminiumtrioxid View Post

                                  I don't mean to come across as overly negative, but this kind of thinking is literally poisonous to the game line.
                                  I wouldn't necessarily go that far, however allow me to offer a counterpoint to really needing dex 5. First let me just say that dex 5 is a perfectly valid character concept and nobody should ever feel bad taking it if their idea is to have the most precise agile fighter in the world, or whatever you like.

                                  On the other hand dropping down to dex 4 or 3, for an Earth Dragon stylist, will net you a loss of 1-2 attack dice out of your pool of 19, and 1 defense. However if you boost strength and stamina by 1 will give you...


                                  1 bonus withering damage base
                                  1 bonus soak base
                                  1* bonus ignored wound penalty from Stone Dragon's Skin
                                  1 bonus withering damage from Force of the Mountain
                                  1 bonus leeched withering from Unmoving Mountain Stance
                                  1 higher threshold for getting knocked back from Unmoving Mountain Stance
                                  1* bonus die to smash attacks and attacks against prone enemies from Earth Dragon Style
                                  1 bonus soak from Earth Dragon Style
                                  1 bonus round for Ghost Grounding Blow
                                  1 bonus damage for Earthshaker Attack
                                  1 bonus to demolition rolls for Shattering Fist Strike
                                  1 bonus difficulty to breaking out of Hungry Earth Strike
                                  1 bonus goal number for breaking out of Hungry Earth Strike
                                  1 bonus withering damage from Perfection of Earth Body
                                  1 bonus decisive damage from Perfection of Earth Body
                                  1 bonus soak from Perfection of Earth Body
                                  2 bonus hardness from Perfection of Earth Body

                                  *These are only applicable when increasing the stat to an uneven number

                                  So it's actually not TOO bad to sacrifice dex for strength/stamina. It doesn't really kill your dicepool in the same way as it does a solar, losing 2 dex means losing 4 dice, and you get quite a slew of upgrades for your charms because Earth Dragon is so focused on Strength/Stamina. People will probably point out that you need to hit for any of those effects to trigger, and dex helps you hit, but if you're dropping your attack pool from 19 to 17, especially considering you can really stunt hard and spend willpower if you need to, I don't think you'll find hitting things to be too difficult.

                                      I can state from experience that the Dex of 3 doesn't hamper much. My current immaculate was built with 5 3 5 in Strength, Dexterity and Stamina respectively (BP spends). I gave her faith's pillar and she unlocked Dragon's Provide and has ended combats with single blows. (Battlegroup didn't stand a chance when 32 withering dice with rerolled 6s came at it). Stunting really helps as well. Managed to knock a sorceress out of her stormwind rider by being crazy enough to leap out of the back of our carriage into her tornado to hammerblow her with a withering smash. It's been quite fun.

                                      That being said, build how you want to build. It's very hard to make a character nonviable, even a terrestrial. A Dex 5 supremely graceful earth stylist can be just as fun.
