            Again, I'd really rather we placed bars as suitable based on utility and power rather than any of these other descriptors based on "type." Like if at starting Lunars could transform into "generic" versions of any non-magical species with physical stats not greater than their own plus one, and size equivalent to 1/2 or 1.5x their own, that would be fine with me. Then you would buy a separate knack for creatures of greater/lesser size, one for increased attribute flexibility, one for creatures with magical abilities and one for copying a specific creature rather than a generic version. Thus a burly starting Lunar might take a Blood Ape form at starting, but a tyrant lizard would require a little charm investment. The actual power of the form would be the balancing factor rather than kitsch factor.

                        Originally posted by Saur Ops Specialist View Post
                        But in terms of the capabilities of the shape itself, not really more effective than a gorilla or orangutan. For the Lunar's purposes, quite possibly less so, since the great ape shapes are likely to have special effects, as we saw with the tyrant lizard, and blood apes probably will not, since their power is tied up in spirit Charms which the Lunar cannot use.
                        I dunno if that last bit would be so extreme. There isn't a universal set of spirit Charms anymore, so there's no reason every single spirit's powers need to be represented by Charms. As far as Lunars are concerned, I kinda see this as one of the advantages of it: spirits might have some capabilities for Lunars to add to their toys. I mean, we're talking about blood apes, the go-to 1st circle demon for primal physical violence. I'd honestly find it odd if none of their capabilities simply resulted from the nature and use of their physically imposing bodies. As with ordinary animals, a Lunar should be able to use those. I mean, in the 2e core, because of how spirits were modeled, a blood ape had to spend motes on Landscape Travel to increase its movement rate when swinging from trees and vines. I see absolutely no reason why that shouldn't just be represented by its physical form being such that it's faster about swinging from trees and vines, as I imagine will be the case with most primates in their write-ups. It makes sense that a Lunar could still use that.

                        Not to say a Lunar would automatically prefer it to any other large, non-demon primate; if some of an Erymanthoi's intended power is bundled up in Charms or the like that a Lunar can't access, a gorilla might generally come out ahead. It still makes sense that a Lunar posing as a blood ape would benefit from that shape beyond appearance and Attributes. I presume the devs have thought about this and will have some rules for which capabilities Lunars can get and which they can't. One way or another, given the emphasis on Lunars' beast-god angle, I expect a given Lunar will not be particularly incentivized to ignore regular animal shapes for those of more exotic realms.
