    Dragonblooded of the "Corpse Elements" are still a thing, and tend to spring up among those born near shadowlands, or survivors of near-death experiences. They're unusually common around Linowa, and a major reason why Halta hasn't decided the war by now. There is a necromantic initiation ritual that involves burying shamanic prospects in the Field of Woe to commune with, and emerge victorious from, the spirits of the vengeful dead.

    Linowan necromancers seldom send the dead on raids, as the bounty of conquest is for the living, but they have called talons of dead men to the defense of their homeland, and crafted masks that allow ghostly veterans to "guide" young warriors directly.

        Some more for my stuff.

        Terrestrial Inheritance Stuff
        I straight-up ignore the textbox on Dragon-Blooded breeding stuff in the 3e corebook. The heritablity is based on whether either parent was an Exalt first, whether that parent cultivated their Progentive Essence, and it goes down from there. It's usually not better than 50/50, but just kind of due to the vageries of statistics, and correlation and causation, a lot of "strong blodlines" are lucky ones that folks think have something going. It probably looks likea bell curve more than anything.

        Kind of additionally in this is that there's means to pass one's Progentive Essence to an adopted child. The kid still needs to be young enough, and there's still the cultivation, but there's rituals to officially adopt someone as if they're their own kid. This is the same thing that lets sorcerously-conceived children count.

        Kind of in this vein, some Realm prejudices might seem odd, but fitting. Someone who's actually the result of a pillow pregnancy that a guy had with the two years after his last kid might still be Will-Bred if it's bene hidden enough and the family has the prestige. Someone who's a leftover child might be considered Thin-Blooded even though thier kids are as likely to Exalt as anyone else.

        Getimian Exaltation
        A big weirdness with Getimians is that their Exaltations were all Chosen when they were firs tcreated. Sacheverell and Oramus due to being a combination of "Sees everything in the future" and "realizing everything possible" more or less have "Exalted" everyone who ever will in all of history as a Getimian. This note, doens't mean they're limited. There's only the 64 and they do get inherited. It's just that it's predetermined hwen and how every single one was. And the two now-Yozi can't really stop it if they wanted to: The Exaltation is jus tpart of thier natur enow.

                            The most popular form of cooking in the First Age was with solar ovens partially as a gesture of piety to the Unconquered Sun but also because the carefully cultivated weather around major habitation centres made them rather practical.                                                             There's at least one Beastfolk kingdom in the East that resembles the Lizardman lands from Warhammer Fantasy. 3+ distinct lineages descended from a variety of reptilian Lunars, each lineage originating from -and perpetuated by, in emergencies- a set of "spawning pool" Beast-Soul Awakening Crucibles.
