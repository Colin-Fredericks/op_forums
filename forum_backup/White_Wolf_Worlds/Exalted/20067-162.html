        1)Since we don't have the DB book, it's a bit difficult to tell how easy it is for them to defend against, say, this hypothetical 40L Decisive Ambush. My guess is that it's not easy for them to negate it perfectly, because it's not that easy for Solars to do (at least, not without some downsides), and DBs are weaker than Solars. But that's just a guess.

        2)As Alucard says, it's going to vary. There isn't a standard training for Wyld Hunt members. You're going to have swordsmen, spies, assassins, Immaculate Martial Artists, snipers, berzerkers, etc. Dragonblood are pretty varied.
        I'd expect that, generally, the Wyld Hunt strategists will try to send the less experienced members after brand new Anathema, and their most experienced members against powerful Anathema. But it probably often just depends on what's available.

        So, bearing in mind the issues I've mentioned, my overall guess is that it might be possible for a Wyld Hunt member to defend against your 40L ambush, but I'd guess only the more experienced members would be able to, and probably only some of those.
