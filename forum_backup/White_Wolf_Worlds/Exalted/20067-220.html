                                            Originally posted by Exthalion View Post
                                            If there was a thread about this I have forgotten it.

                                            How evil are the deathlords exactly? If my Western Solar formed trade alliances with Skullstone if he propping up a world-ending regime or setting himself up to be murdered?

                                            In First Edition (and especially Second Edition) the Deathlords were magically bound to destroy all of existence. Those who refused or did a poor job would be punished (like the First and Forsaken Lion), or threatened by the Neverborn (such as Eye and Seven Despairs when the Neverborn threatened to take his Abyssals away). This forced all of the Deathlords to pursue the end goal of destroying the universe, even if they didn't want to.

                                            The developers for 3rd edition noted that this was bad, because it did mean that (among other things) forming alliances or treaties with them was pointless - they would break those later. Plus you would be helping them.

                                            We don't know exactly how the Deathlords will be in 3rd edition, but Dev comments have suggestion that they will be less under the Neverborn's thumb and they won't all be focused around destroying the world all the time.

                                            That said, if you're using First or Second edition talk it over with the ST out-of-character to see what his take on the Deathlords is.

                                                Originally posted by Exthalion View Post
                                                If there was a thread about this I have forgotten it.

                                                How evil are the deathlords exactly? If my Western Solar formed trade alliances with Skullstone if he propping up a world-ending regime or setting himself up to be murdered?

                                                As a general rule, forget the overarching theme that the Deathlords will kill the world. That's not the goal anymore. The Silver Prince is a conquering Deathlord who is building up a strong military but looks like he is gunning for a culture victory, to use Civ terms. I suspect we will see several Deathlords focusing on mundane conquest, whether military (First and Forsaken Lion), militarily/politically (Mask of Winters), or military/culture (Silver Prince). I suspect others will focus on spreading their own ideology throughout Creation, creating cults, and generally putting themselves at the head of the cult (Lover, Bishop). Others will probably still focusing on killing and suffering as much as possible (Dowager).

                                                Are all the Deathlords evil? Go define evil, and we'll talk.

                                                Are all the Deathlords bad? Well, if you are a big fan of life and freedom... you probably consider them bad. Even those Deathlords that are really good at organizing a society and making sure needs are met, and serving up philosophy and religion, are generally all about death and control. Death becomes a more ideal state, to be aspired to once you have matured enough... like moving from a child to an adult.

                                                Will your Solar be propping up a world-ending regime? Probably not. Will he be profiting from a regime that teaches that life is not important, and that death is the single most important event in a person's life? Yes, yes he will. Will he be setting himself up for assassination attempts? Well, how loyal is he to the death cult? Lots? Yes, people who oppose Skullstone and love life and freedom will probably try to assassinate you, or your subordinates. Do you oppose Skullstone and try to bleed them economically? Yes, Skullstone probably tries to do lots of bad things to you. Not because they are evil, or you are evil, but because you don't support the overarching goal of making the Silver Prince the undisputed ruler of the West. Most PCs will eventually run into trouble because at some point they will decide they don't want the Silver Prince to be the undisputed ruler of the Western direction.
