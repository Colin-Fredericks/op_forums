    There's an awful lot of creatures in the corebook and Hundred Devil Night Parade that serve as antagonists, but have abilities that are either really unsuited to battlegroup formation, or outright impossible to use.

    Pack Hunting, for example, just doesn't do anything for creatures all massed up in a battlegroup, so why have it? You might say "Well, because as a latent feature it can be unlocked for use with non-wolves when they're a familiar." but why not just make it a latent feature exclusively then? Sometimes you have a swarm of silverwights attacking because of the birthing cries of a pregnant woman, then you run them individually. Sometimes a massive army of the Wyld marches upon creation with hundreds of silverwights, and more, under the command of a noble fairy, then you run them as a battlegroup with elite drill.

From what I know of Dace and Volfer they basically just do that, but Demetheus actually seems like somebody who could punch almost anyone clean in half, but decides not to for not entirely selfish reasons.
                                                            This is one reason I don't like the Volfer fiction in the core book. Walk into a room to beat people up for no reason is not how I, personally, would enact my war against the Realm.


                                                            Although, as a Dawn, Topaz could just learn Fire Dragon Style out of nowhere just by wanting to.
                                                            Where is this written?
