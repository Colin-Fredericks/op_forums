                                    Originally posted by Elfive View Post
                                    Do warstrider evocations have to start at essence 2?
                                    It's not a hard rule. My reasoning was that I wanted an Essence 1 warstrider pilot to have a period where they could just appreciate the power of having a warstrider by itself (and their player has time to get used to the rules) before introducing Evocations into the mix.
