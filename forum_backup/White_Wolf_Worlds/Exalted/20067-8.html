                    Crossbows don't add your Strength to damage, which means you don't need to train in Strength to wield crossbows. In order to match a crossbow's damage, a bowman would have to get all the way to Strength 5 (unless they give up the Piercing tag), and not even Tiger Warrior Training Technique can do that. Just train your soldiers with a Crossbow specialty in Archery and you're good to go.

                    Also, the crossbow has a faster Speed than any of the bows and save for the siege crossbow they don't have the S tag, so bizarrely they can fire more often before accounting for flurries.

                                                    Originally posted by Jen View Post
                                                    Can someone explain this paragraph for me, it's from Jade Defense, a DB's Socialize charm.
                                                    If someone has a Charm that doesn't effect people with an Essence score higher than the User's Essence, the DBs Essence score is considered to be (Essence+1) for the purposes of that Charm.

                                                    The net effect is that if a charm doesn't work on people with a higher Essence score, when targeting the DB, it doesn't work if their essence is equal or higher, as opposed to just higher.
