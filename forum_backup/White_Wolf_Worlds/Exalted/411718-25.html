        Sadly, most naval-themed fantasy illustrations tend toward an Age of Sail aesthetics, so I don't have many of them, but I do have some!


        When I saw him, my mind immediately went "yeah, this guy's an Eclipse". I mean, the sails in the background are pretty tattered, and the crew is being relegated to blurry figures in the background, but he undeniably looks cool, and whether he's holding a map or a ledger, it certainly accentuates the caste's role as explorers and bureaucrats way more than yet another anime dude with a fancy sword would.




        This one is pretty much the exact opposite of the previous: just pure, unadulterated ship porn:
