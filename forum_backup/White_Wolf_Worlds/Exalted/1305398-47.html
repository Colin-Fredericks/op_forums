                                                        If we're talking Athletics as well as Melee Long Range isn't actually enough. A Solar can move once reflexively, move again with One More Step, move a third time with Invincible Fury of the Dawn, and then a fourth range band with Thunderbolt Attack Prana. They can close into melee distance in a single action from the first range band of Extreme if they really want to.

                                                        It also doesn't propagate to six range right away. You start at Short and then grow it successively, which gives them more rounds to rush in. Or alternatively just back away past six range bands and wait for you to get sick of channeling death ray, then charge in.

                                                        Originally posted by habitableexoplanet View Post
                                                        It might also be interesting to force the Melee type to only use Melee charms (or only Melee among the main combat abilitiies) so Thunderclap Rush Attack is not allowed.
                                                        Restricting to just melee charms might be enough. Solar melee though is really, really fucking good, in almost all situations. For example if you're facing a Dawn with Death Ray at Long range. So you're going to attack with your awesome 13 die attack with 5 auto-successes, and counting both 1s and 2s as 10s, that's pretty damn awesome. The defense you're going up against to hit that though is a parry of 12, and any 1s and 2s might count as 10s, but up to 5 of them also subtract 1 success due to Hail Shattering Practice.

                                                        Even with that though, you're still likely to hit. Maybe only just, but a hit is a hit. After that though, Heavenly Guardian Defense comes into play, stripping one flat success of damage for 1i each, and if the full damage is nullified then Protection of Celestial Bliss activates. Up to 5 1s and 2s on the damage roll get banked for the next Heavenly Guardian Defense, which can be doubled for 4m 1wp by Protection of Celestial Bliss.

                                                        If you can get to the point where they're out of initiative to Heavenly Guardian Defense before they reach Short range though, you're still not in the clear, because they might just decide to run if they're smart. So they're at medium range, and you blast them back to long, they can then get up and start withdrawing which gives you a short period of time to chew through their health levels before they're gone. If they don't think to or refuse to leave though, then at that point, basically you win, since they can't get closer to you, and your awesome to-hit roll will always hit.


                                                        On the other hand you could flub the attack roll, or the roll to generate 25 sm just by getting unlucky. If that happens and you can't muster at least 12 successes, then you're in mega trouble.


                                                        I know the instant gut reaction to seeing Death Ray with Solar Arete is "How could anything, how could ANYTHING stand up to that!?" but Essence 5 Dawn casts are the greatest warriors in all of the Exalted host bar absolutely none.

                                                            Also, I think the best defense might be solar charms, not distance. To pull this off you only really need 9 charms (FSSA and prerequisites, 3 sorceries), leaving you pretty free to take additional defensive charms like Adamant Skin Technique or Uncanny Shroud Defense or Hail-Shattering Practice.

                                                            Originally posted by DrLoveMonkey View Post
