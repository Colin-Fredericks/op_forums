    Originally posted by Lioness View Post
    How much of that is their actual willingness to compromise with other people though?
    It depends on your players- In the end, Exaltation doesn't give you a bigger ego, and it doesn't take away all of your flaws either. Solars aren't any more likely to be giant arrogant assholes than any other type of Exalted, (except maybe Infernals) even though they'll probably have an easier time of it. If your experience with Solars is that they tend to have big egos, then that's your group's fault. My group has more Solars who are humble (or simply aren't idiots) than they have Solars who can't compromise with others. And if a Solar is willing to compromise with other Exalts (And with other beings overall), then odds are he would be even more powerful than the the one who isn't.



    Originally posted by Lioness View Post
    Could you have achieved the same result as those awesome speeches with the social character instead simply shouting "I'M SO PRETTY!" or "KNEEL BEFORE ZOD!"
    Could we? Yeah, sure. We've done it before. Except you're going to have a real hard time make a working society just by being impossibly magically beautiful and only having people who are obsessed with your beauty and sexuality obeying you. For the "KNEEL BEFORE ZOD!" one, it also works, but it also means the people under you are going to obey you out of fear and because you dominated them forcefully. That makes it easy for you to be betrayed. Remember the Usurpation?

        Originally posted by Lioness View Post
        Or individuals that those kind of people value and trust.

        Much as being "the person this prominent NPC trusts" is a PC cliche there is a reason why it exists.         Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post

        ​Wealth and respect are things that you can take as 5-dot Merits at character creation.

        ​And while Dynasts older than a hundred might be a dot or two higher than Essence 3, they're distinctly assumed to not be very numerous this time around.

        ​So there's a question of whether an Exalt, particularly one whose powers have the kind of dependencies that the Dragon Blooded are bound to, can ever be outdone by a slightly less powerful Exalt.
        It's not really a matter of if a DB can be outdone by a slightly less powerful DB, that's obviously true. The problem is that even if you spent all your BP on merits and took every social merit you could on chargen, that doesn't make you the head of a dynastic house. You are QUITE impressive, with connections all over the place, and in a lot of those cases those connections put you in a position of authority, but that doesn't make a thousand DB beholden to you, like Mnemon has. So you're not even as politically powerful as Mnemon, and you're going 12v1 in the political realm against every other house.

        Remember here, the original question was reforming the Realm, the REALM, into a "beacon of justice and virtue". This isn't just as simple as ending slavery in the Realm, or maybe getting them to lay off the expansion and extraction gig they've got, as if that wouldn't be hard enough. This is about how plausible is it that you turn the whole realm completely upside-down on it's head all while the wolves of Celestial exalted are hammering down their gates, in an already toxic environment of fear and fierce tribalism.

        As a Solar, yeah that makes sense, you have unbridled power and force of personality that has not been seen in this world for a millennium or more, but as a DB you don't really have anything that those other people don't. Which again isn't to say it can't be done, you're just going to need some ST help with it to give you an avenue to do it. Like if they got a hold of the Imperial Manse, for example, that'd do it for sure. In contrast, for example, Solars don't need the ST to give them dick-diddly.

        It's not just social stuff, if you took a group of chargen DBs and pitted them up against Octavian, Siggereth and a legion of 500 blood apes, there's almost no way to win. Barring maybe some weird exploity white room thing like everyone taking 3 5-dot command merits and just drowning them in legions or something. Which isn't to say that the cause is hopeless either, you're just going to need some way to maybe separate them, like discover that Octavian has an ancient geas put on him by some anathema that prevents him from turning down an invitation to a summer fire festival or something. Or perhaps find some hellish intrigue that you can use to pit them against eachother.

        A Solar circle on the other hand just jumps in there and deals with the problem using the best solution, the Dawn solution.

                Originally posted by DrLoveMonkey View Post
                So you're not even as politically powerful as Mnemon, and you're going 12v1 in the political realm against every other house.
                ​There are approaches to the subject that are a bit more clever.

                ​For example, promote and provoke conflict between all of the Houses in a manner that weakens them and discredits their leadership, while working to set oneself up as a worthwhile candidate for a position of significant authority as various ranks start to look for alternative solutions to the problem than coming under the leadership of the people that have led them into that mess.

                ​Alternatively, cultivate power outside of the Great Houses; the patricians, the magistrates, the outcastes, maybe even the peasantry. Create a banner under which they can pull together into a force of approximately equivalent strength to a Great House, that can compete on their playing field.

                ​Heh, there's something in there that I myself have never considered before; the idea of a Dynast (or several of them) renouncing their House name. Hell, in the absence of the Scarlet Empress, and as the credibility of the Realm's institutions breaks down, there's not exactly anything strictly preventing a collection of disillusioned Dragon Blooded from across the Realm breaking away, joining together, and declaring themselves to be a new Great House.

                ​Or, taking inspiration from the Japanese warring states, sections of the Immaculate Order that are dissatisfied with being involved in the struggles of the Scarlet Dynasty (probably a schism that fractures along lines that leave out a lot of Mnemon scions) organise themselves as a separate polity under which other unhappy elements of Realm society pull together and hold themselves apart, fighting to defend their sovereignty.

                ​
                Originally posted by DrLoveMonkey
                Remember here, the original question was reforming the Realm, the REALM, into a "beacon of justice and virtue".
                That can only ever come at the far end of a long journey. It makes no sense to consider development of that narrative and look at every possible first step with an attitude of "this can't be done, because the current power structure won't allow it".

                ​A Dynast with a decent amount of mystical power combined with immense political capital and unfathomable riches may still be at a disadvantage, but I feel as though some people too often look at disadvantaged situations as something to resolve with head-on collisions that are liable to fail, rather than approaching problems sideways.

                ​And when you've got cash and friends and respect, there are more sideways from which to approach than if you lacked those things.

                ​Besides, sure Mnemon has political power. The basis of that power is in things represented through the social system; use that system, in combination with the transformative circumstances of the Time of Tumult, to break the foundations of that power down.

                ​Would one look at the fact that Mnemon, or any other character, is still alive and has a backstory involving several instances of having gotten into brutal fights, and use that as an argument against actually engaging with her through the combat system?

                Originally posted by DrLoveMonkey
                as a DB you don't really have anything that those other people don't.
                By this basis, how does anybody ever accomplish anything? Like, even within the context of evaluating the Dragon Blooded.

                ​People aren't interchangeable, and they're more than just their basic capabilities; they're their motivation, their circumstances, their relationships. All of these things mix together to create a unique instance that can generate unusual outcomes.

                ​Mnemon has immense political power, and her son went down among the Mountain Folk, fought their enemies and earned their respect. He didn't do that on the strength of having powers that no other Dragon Blood had. If he had been motivated, do you not think that he could have utilised that respect to achieve some significant results? Possibly ones that would have been contrary to Mnemon?

                ​What about Mnemon herself? How did she survive Ragara's assassins when so many other siblings did not? Could just any Terrestrial have tamed V'neef's ancient monster wolf? Is Sesus Nagezzer not in a position to utilise his army of reprobates to accomplish some things of significance in the Realm?

                Look at the stories of the Dragon Blooded and try to find ways in which one's particular drive, what they are compelled to use their powers for, creates stories in which they can compete against their superiors.

                After all, that's the other way to utilise the point that other people don't have anything that you don't; 20-year old Mnemon didn't have any powers that you're not capable of, and look what she accomplished.

                            Originally posted by Lioness View Post
                            My point was that your examples were more of Solars being good at influencing people, not necessarily cooperating with them.
                            This was what I was getting at. It's not that Solars are bad at social-fu, they're very good at it.
                            But often having such power makes it too tempting not to co-operate with people, but instead bulldoze them with social-fu (or kung-fu).

                            Originally posted by Isator Levi
                            While I wouldn't fully discount the significance of the personality of figures such as Muhammad, I wouldn't entirely attribute their success to them having something like a unique and overpowering force of personality. There have to have been social forces that were receptive to the message that he was preaching, and that were ready to start flowing when somebody with the right idea spoke up to give them a push.
                            This is a good point.
                            And I think a lot of what allows Dragonblood PCs to rise to power in the canon game is that there is a specific time of flux. With a civil war in the offing, Anathema returning, etc, many DBs may be looking for something different and new. For example, without the Empress, do you just get a new Empress (Mnemon says yes, her, but many DBs would prefer something more cooperative), or do something new? With the appearance of the Deathlords, is fighting Lunars the priority? With the satrapies getting restless and troops being withdrawn from them, can it still carry out such an oppressive imperial policy?

                            Also, related to that, "great men" are not just often the confluence of specific social changes, but also often the figureheads/representatives for a confluence of influence groups. Your PCs are not necessarily just 5 lone heroes battling the establishment, but may be the representatives of their Houses, interest groups, etc, who may want you in power because it suits their own interests.

                            For example, in the Dragonblood game I used to play, a retired PC had become Empress, and promptly gone into seclusion so as to not be assassinated.
                            Thus, real power was wielded by her consort, Hearth-mates, etc, most of which were from House Pelleps.

                            So, when our PCs overthrew the Prime Minister and replaced him as representatives of the Empress's will, it wasn't really just us vs him, it was House Cathak/Sesus/Mnemon/Ledaal/Tepet/Cynis/the All-Seeing Eye/the Sidereals/the liberal wing of the Immaculate Order/some Lunars who wanted to make peace with the Realm vs House Pelleps/the conservative faction of the Immaculate Order.

                            Dynastic games are very much about family, and that means your PC isn't necessarily fighting alone.
