                                                    03-03-2015, 12:03 AM
                                                    In order for a "telekinesis" effect to flow naturally from the Abilities of the Solar Exalted, it needs to make sense within the actual and symbolic context of that Ability. This is why Call the Blade works as a Solar Melee Charm. You can't treat Charms as scientific phenomena and derive new Charms from that—Eagle-Wing Style won't generalize into actual psychic telekinesis any more than Racing Hare Method will let the Solar do bullshit like stealing speed from people. That's how the Flash works. Solars aren't superheroes.
