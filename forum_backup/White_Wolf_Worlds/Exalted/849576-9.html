    Originally posted by Boston123 View Post
    Take the above with a grain of salt, as I run mortal campaigns were even a +1/-1 bonus to rolls are important.
    +1/-1 bonuses to rolls are important in Solar games. Arguably more important, since dice tricks mean it can translate to more than .5 successes for them.

    That's why I am DEEPLY hesitant to give such a bonus to anything. I'm also deeply hesitant to take a combat system built around being cinematic and unrealistic and start assigning granular bonuses/penalties based on real-world traits of weapons. My honest advice (especially if you're not using Charms) would be to find a system designed to support that style of play.

    But clearly you know what you and your group are enjoying. It's just sufficiently far-removed from what I see Exalted 3 as equipped to handle that I can no longer predict what will "break" - the status quo represents a massive break from my expectations already.
