    Originally posted by Shaddar View Post
    Would it be to much cheating to have an artifact that counts as both armor and weapon?

    If it can only do one or the other at any one time, then I would probably take the highest artifact cost between the two of them, add one to it and then call it a day - so an Artifact 3 weapon and an Artifact 4 armor, I would charge you for them as if it were an Artifact 5. You'd pay the attunement cost for whichever was great.

    However if you wanted one artifact that provided both a bonus to armor and a weapon at the same time (say, a suit of artifact armor with a huge horn you want to treat as a short daiklave for when you headbutt people) I would make you pay the price to purchase two artifacts both armor and weapon. I'd also make you attune to two artifacts. Then, we would just flavor it as one artifact.

                    You know how some of the corebook artifacts have a bonus attunement cost you can pay to get extra benefits?
