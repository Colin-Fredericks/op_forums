    Lunar Cloning - no items, fox only, final destination

    05-28-2017, 10:35 AM
    Even if the Lunar Exalted have an easier time multiplying themselves into a battle group than Solars do of approximating the Flash's "I'm everywhere at once" trick (Faster Than Self Technique), they naturally suffer their own limitations. Such as a possible inability to create copies of their weapons.

    Lunar Exalted have never had an effect quite like Glorious Solar Saber, because a Dexterity Charm tree doesn't impose the same limitations as a Melee Charm tree. It is not appropriate for the Lunar Exalted to have a Green Lantern power which allows them to conjure any object or tool they might need, the way that Infernal Exalted could with the Charms of She Who Lives In Her Name. This is also a big part of the reason the Lunar Exalted cannot have anything approximating Phantom-Conjuring Performance.

    A Solar using Faster Than Self Technique could run into an ammunition problem if using a single bow and quiver, but at least he could use a single bow. A Lunar with a single bow may be left with just one bow and a lot of empty hands.

    Both the Solar and Lunar have the option of scavenging the battlefield for enough munitions, but more often than not, the Lunar might simply resort to becoming a battle group of beasts, armed with tooth and claw.


    Can a Lunar clone fake a Lunar's clothing? The clones should dissolve into motes of Essence after they die or expire, so the Lunar can't run a textile business by copying a few really expensive dresses.


    But there's more. Since Lunar cloning could be so much easier for them than Faster Than Self Technique is for Solars, lesser forms of it can appear much earlier in the Lunar Charmset to help give them a very different experience than the Solar Exalted.

    For one thing, it wouldn't be found in their "Athletics" Charms, but at an intersection of their Evasion and Extra Attack Charms.

    Solar Exalted need to use False Image Feint and Ebon Shadow Style's Shadow-Body Dissolution to perform the phantom Clone / Replacement technique, where the enemy strikes only an afterimage or illusion as the Solar watches from concealment. But the Lunar Exalted could produce an actual doppleganger to take the blow for them while they use the opportunity to re-establish surprise, like RWBY's Blake Belladonna and her Shadow Semblance.

    Though if the Lunar cannot copy his clothes or weapons, he might not be able to pull off Blake's trick except when he starts in an animal form. Not unless he leaves his clothes and weapons with the clone while he himself tries to enter concealment as an animal small enough to evade being seen.


    Clones also spice up Lunar attacks! Invincible Fury of the Dawn allows a Solar to move one range band per attack in the Extra Attack flurry, leaping around with spear or dagger. But a Lunar might instead burst into a temporary group of clones that simultaneously leap in completely different directions to attack their own targets.

    Which of course leads to questions of what happens when a target successfully clashes a clone. It would be a major advantage for the Lunar to be effectively immune to the dangers of clashing opponents by sending clones to do it for him. But then, if killing a clone hurt a Lunar, there would be no point in using them for dodging distractions.



    FINAL DESTINATION

    Alchemical Exalted would approach the matter between the Lunar and Solar methods. Their Charm structures would work more like Lunars, with the "I'm an army of one" Charm being found in "Evasion and Extra Action" rather than "Athletics", but their "quantum super-positioning" trick would allow them to "copy" their weapons like Solars.

                                                      You're confusing "the limitations of a single Charm within a set" with "the limitations of ALL Charms within a set".

                                                      A single Solar Melee Charm generally only focuses on offense or defense, and only one trick thereof. Every Solar Melee Charm before and after it likewise must generally be strictly offensive or defensive. More importantly, all of these Solar Melee Charms must relate to the Melee Ability, which is why Glorious Solar Saber cannot serve as the basis for Immaculate Golden Bow or Words-as-Workshop Method.

                                                      A Dexterity Charm set has no Ability-related restrictions. If the Lunar Exalted could have Charms that fashioned artifact-quality tools out of Essence, then Glorious Lunar Saber, Immaculate Silver Bow, or Howls-as-Workshop Method could all be found in the same Attribute. Sooner or later, that broadness eliminates the restrictions that keep this capability from evolving into a "Green Lantern Ring" power that lets the Lunar Exalted act like Infernals with the Charms of She Who Lives In Her Name, creating walls and constructs of Essence to fight telekinetically at a distance without needing to use weapons or animal forms.

                                                      This is also why the free-form holographic powers of Phantom-Conjuring Technique are inappropriate for the Lunar Exalted. Such an external power renders their powers of internal transformation redundant and meaningless.

                                                      All of the other Exalted have already got "external manipulation of the world" well-covered, between Solar mastery, Terrestrial elementalism, Sidereal cheats, Getimian hacks, Abyssal blasphemies, and Infernal heresies.

                                                      The Lunar Exalted are being parred down to focus on an intensely personal sort of mastery, with some extraneous parts of that internal focus being cut off and given to the Liminal Exalted who can expand upon those pieces in ways that were never appropriate for the Lunar Exalted.


                                                      Alchemical Dexterity is different. For one thing, the Chosen of the Machine God should naturally be good with any kind of tools. But there's also the fact that they can only load so many Charms at a time, which limits how broad their powers can be at any given time.
