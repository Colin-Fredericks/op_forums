                        Originally posted by Zeesun View Post
                        From the testing I see thusfar, it looks like DB's are...surprisingly dangerous, actually! Essence-wielders in numbers seem like a real tough nut to crack short of Solar-level ubercombos; I wonder if there's some critical threshold combat-focused Solars beyond E1 (and especially at E3) are able to comfortably exceed accuracy/damage-wise that makes them so deadly.
                        It's not a certain amount of accuracy/damage that puts the Solars ahead so much, it's their access to ways to bypass or shortcut entire parts of the combat engine, and they have sooo many ways of doing so!

                        Example (for added difficulty, I excluded Dawns, and kept it under essence 3):
                        Supernal Survival up to DBT, from Ride take Master Horseman’s Techniques, Seasoned Beast-Rider’s Approach, Worthy Mount Technique. From Melee War Lion Stance.
                        Familiar Mount and Rider can defend other each other, and one of them is immune to decisive attacks, so funnel withering to the rider (who can't dismounted btw) and decisive to the mount. Becomes ridiculous with a Tyrant Lizard. Possible at essence 1 and becomes really ridiculous at essence 2 when you add Immortal Rider’s Advantage (shift initiative between rider and mount reflexively) and Rousing Backlash Assault (mount can do decisive counterattacks). The cherry on top would be including something high damage like Righteous Devil to the mix. Can definitely take on groups of DB's of, well, any size.

                        This is the worst but there are others, like the sexy, Black Claw using, dodgy battle-dancer who rolls into battle with Respect Commanding Attitude active, capping all of his or her opponents to their Charisma + Performance and flurries a dance action every round to keep that going. This makes most everyone utterly irrelevant. After getting Doe Eyes Defense at essence 2 even the few who had a pool left no longer have one. Turns all combats into Bollywood dance-off social combat which no one but you can win. Should be able to take on groups of DB's as long as the Solar initiates combat on their own terms and doesn't tackle, like, a DB traveling minstrel show.

                        Solar Stealth (up to Mind Shroud Meditation and Hidden Snake Recoil) + Single Point's Fatal Stroke Flash + Steel Devil Strike is a way to create an ever increasing initiative total which never resets. Probably the least reliable, as it relies on stealth, and is heavy on the WP use, but done right should be able to casually handle multiple DB's.

                        But yeah, Solars, you know, and all three examples hinge on using downright broken Charms and synergies on top.

                        Now, I wonder if adding some spell like the Virtuous Guardian of Flame spell to the Lunars we've seen performing in the Arena would put them over the top? Haven't had the opportunity if seeing that in play yet, but it should force them to waste some actions to deal with that first. That perhaps combined with Membe shifting to pestletail to thunder-hammer the earth DB away, making him waste turns to return to the fray, might buy the time to take at least one of them out of the equation, which would change everything.

                                        Essence 2-3 Dragonblooded is probably the biggest power jump in the game, by far maybe even. Not only are the signature charms some of the most unique and powerful charms in the DB charmset, but there’s also a bunch of other ones in there like Cloud Riot that are really powerful. Losing those is a big step down.

                                        That being said if the Lunar plans on fighting until she wins, I feel like she’s going to need to get a bit lucky still. If the DBs are being designed the way players would make them, not literally 5s in everything but if you have 3 charms that key off perception you’ll have a 5 there, it won’t be a cakewalk.

                                        The signature charms tend to end fights a lot earlier but the long game doesn’t really favour the Lunar. It basically boils down to this, you can win almost any 1v1 as a Lunar vs a DB. Your charms are better, more efficient, and shapeshifting is amazing, some combos probably being powerful to the point of being overpowered. BUT if every DB has 2 ox-bodies, and no that’s not unreasonable, they could have 5, or even 5.5 at E3, then to get to that 1v1 you need to farm up enough initiative to decisive 26 health levels out of 2 targets, while defending yourself from around three attacks every turn. Now factor in that your only sources to farm initiative are encased in jade armor which means you need to deal some serious damage to peak over Overwhelming, and even boosting your defences will only stop them from hitting you more than half the time. Also factor in that every time you regenerate 5m they regen 15, and that’s not even factoring in Willpower, which I’ve been intentionally avoiding including in these fights. They spend a willpower on every attack and Defense, so do you, and even if you have max willpower and they have base you’ll run out first.

                                        None of those factors change when you drop the DBs to e2. The funny thing is that Lunars are so god damned fucking strong that even with all those disadvantages you still might win, and even if you lose one of the DBs is probably going to die, but unless your Lunar is Essence 4-5 I wouldn’t ever rely on it. Like if you and your two Lunar friends are sitting in the market and get ambushed by 3 badass looking Dragonblooded, the tactically sound responses to that are either you all fight and probably win, or all run and probably get away, but not “You guys run...I’ll handle this.” And go charging in there on your own.

                                        Also be hyper aware of your surroundings. Drawing out a group of Lunars with underwhelming odds only to spring the OTHER half of the ambush halfway through the fight is exactly the kind of dirty trick the Wyld Hunt would pull...and remember that for when you decide to get retribution by ambushing THEM inside their own barracks. Nowhere is safe.


                                        All also see about running those solo fights, they tend to go a lot faster and I make less mistakes because they’re simpler.
