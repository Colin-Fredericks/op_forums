                    Originally posted by AtG View Post
                    Yeah, as a practical matter it is just a signpost for "here is a good place to homebrew your own charm tree; you have explicit permission to use it". Fluff-wise it just reflects the fact that you are using the spell a lot and developing some kind of shared magic to enhance it or whatever.
                    AtG has the gist of it. Keep in mind that, while sorcery is more about the world's Essence than the Solar's, Evocations in general depend on the personality, combat style, and other features of the wielder. In particular, your sorcerous initiation is very likely to play a part in the nature of a spell's Evocations. For instance, if Mara taught you Wood Dragon's Claws as your control spell, you'd probably develop different Evocations than a sorcerer who learned the same spell from Wyld fever dreams or from mystical alchemic asceticism.

                    Those differences might not have anything more than a visual manifestation, if that, but don't close off your design space to just what the spell itself could be expanded to do.

                            Originally posted by Leetsepeak View Post
                            How is demon-summoning actually viewed in most of Creation?
                            Depends on what you mean by "most of Creation."

                            Most communities in Creation are tiny things that have probably never even seen a sorcerer or demon. They may not have a law against it, but that doesn't mean folks won't freak out at the sight of your demonic entourage.

                            (That doesn't mean elementals are a better choice, by the way. Folks won't particularly applaud you leading a greenmaw on a leash, either.)

                            Meanwhile, bigger cities will probably have encountered sorcerers, demons, and the like just by dint of the amount of people that visit or live in it; whatever laws they have on the books are likely to vary based on local politics, past events, and prejudices.

                            The Realm actually has enough sorcerers (and enough history with sorcerers) that they pass legislation concerning not only the sorcerer's ability to summon demons, but how many they're allowed to summon.
