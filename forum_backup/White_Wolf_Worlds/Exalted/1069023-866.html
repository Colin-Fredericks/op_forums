                                #12998
                                07-28-2018, 07:38 PM
                                1. Spell Distortion - Are you able to distort a spell you don't know?
                                Yes, albeit at a penalty.

                                Can you distort spells a circle above like a Terrestrial circle distorting a Celestial circle spell?
                                Yes.

                                2. Spell Distortion 2 - Do you have to complete a distortion in one round or is there a terminus?
                                There's a terminus of 5.

                                If you fail to gave the needed successes can you just immediately try again?
                                Yes, but you have to start over from zero.

                                Do you have to use a willpower that gets refunded if you succeed like a spell or just constantly weave essence?
                                No; the only reward is that you distort the spell.
