                                                Suppose I should introduce myself as I've made a couple of posts already.

                                                Heya! I'm a Finnish roleplayer/ GM, who got into the Exalted at the twilight of the Second edition. Back then I tried to run a campaign (Solars, one Infernal), which crashed as my group did not like the game enough to learn the rules. I came back, when 3rd edition launched and am now a happy player in a Solar campaign - and soon in a DB campaign too! My favourite type of Exalted are the Sidereals (tho Infernals come close).

                                                I started playing RPG's around the time I got too old for make-believe, tho I've actively played Pen&Paper only since 2010. My first tabletop RPG's were GMing Call of Cthulhu and Rogue Trader. Nowadays I don't like to stick to one system so I play quite a lot of different games, like Savage Worlds, Blades in the Dark, ODnD, DnD4 and Night's Black Agents. And those are just the one's I actively play in addition to Exalted at the moment. I like homebrewing, but don't like numbers (which is kinda a bad match, I know). At the moment I have two artifacts and a martial art on the anvil, which I'll post here, when they're ready-ish. When playing a game, I tend to get interested in what the developers intended for the game - which I usually bend enough that they meet with what I want to run.
                                                I consider myself very lucky to live in a place, where I can generally choose to play and run games how I want.

                                                As a person I have MoA degree in Literature with a teacher's qualifications. I'm currently having a leap year (read: mandatory civil service), which I'm using to game and ponder, whether I should pursue a PhD or not.

                                                        Wow, occurs to me I've never actually introduced myself here before...

                                                        Hi, I go by Hand-of-Omega (Doctor Who reference if you're curious), and I've been into White Wolf since college in the 90s, when I was a huge fan of the original World of Darkness, esp. the Eastern-themed Year of the Lotus line. I was really excited to learn the folks behind that were starting a new game based on the pre-history of the WoD! Turned out that connection was highly exaggerated, but by that point I didn't care, I was hooked and never looked back.

                                                        Finding Exalted was probably the greatest joy of my gaming life; rarely having the time and opportunity to actually play it would be the saddest part of it. At this point, I'm pretty resigned to just collecting the books and enjoying the world of Creation in my own head...and this forum! I've been with Exalted from the start and have been through about three forum reboots on this site. Every now and then, I take a month or so off, but I think this last hiatus (which was due to family issues) was my longest yet.

                                                        I actually got tired of waiting for Third Edition books (beyond the two released of course), so when other stuff demanded my time, I just kinda let Exalted go for awhile. Recently, however, I found myself digging out my old collection, and realized just how much I missed reading through them and discussing stuff here with you good folks. So, I thought it time I circle back around, this time for the foreseeable future! I see there's a lot of new names here, so I hope we can all have fun with 3E!
