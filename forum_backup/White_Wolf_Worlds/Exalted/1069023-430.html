                    #6455
                    11-22-2017, 08:21 AM
                    Originally posted by Ryoki View Post
                    1. Do Exigence get "used up" and die with their Exigents?
                    Usually, but not always.

                    2. Does an Exigence have a physical form and if so what would be appropriate?
                    No. There might be some Exigents whose Exaltation has a physical form (imagine a daiklave that can bond to and Exalt its wielder), but with Exigents, there's always gonna be an exception.

                    3. Personally I think Exigence aren't fragile constructions so to create a corrupted Exigence one would need powerful magic probably equal to higher ambition Celestial or regular Solar Workings. Would that fit with your views?
                    If it's even possible for sorcery to affect an Exigent Exaltation, I think it'd have to be Solar Circle.

                    4. What happens to an Exigence when the Exigent dies? Does it die with her, or does it "drop" or does it return to Lytek so he can clean it from the remnants of the Exigents memory? Something else?
                    Most of the time, an Exigent's Exaltation dies with them. Some might have reincarnating Exaltations; Lytek would likely have stewardship of those. Others might have stranger methods of passing on.
