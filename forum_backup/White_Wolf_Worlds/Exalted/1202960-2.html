        So the pre-Primodial War has The Dawn Time as a new name now. I thought colossi is one of those fantasy version of a real word like wild=wild, but it's actually the plural form of colossus......My English vocabulary still need much much work.

        The paragraph also mentions elementals, so they are not created from the shattered essence of those five big elementals in this Edition I assume?

        There also a bunch of non-human races and their weird artifacts, now I feel as thought Ex3 really likes to.....eh....invent news things on the spots? As in there are a bunch of names and short descriptions in pretty much each paragraph. Which is very much different from Ex2 who, for some weird reasons, keeps inventing news fluffs about old stuff, for example: "Remember the Forgotten Blade that we mention on MoW's write-up, want to hear very detailed fluff on who craft it, when they craft it and why?". I think that I prefer Ex3 approach, since Ex2 just sucks the fun out of me with these long-ass chapter about the FA and Primordial War.

        Oh oh oh, the Chosen learns to use MM without any mention of Autochthon? Can it be true, that Exalted won't have to kiss that trash bin’s butthole anymore?????

        I'm not gonna be optimistic though, the chance of Autotincan being the inventor of Exaltation and being cramp into anything crafting related by the writers are still too high until I see the Alchemical book. For now, I’ll enjoy the fact that the Artifacts created in the war are the invention of the Exalted themselves instead handout from the tin can the Moutain Folk (urgh, why does anything Autochthon related always involve stealing heat from the Exalted and the Incarnae).

        Sorry about that, this is making some very unpleasant opinions of mine surfaced, I may just have to give this chapter a Nega-Je…….Wait, did the next paragraph just says COLOSSAL WEAPONS, not magical weapons, not legendary weapons, but COLOSSAL!!?? Scrap that, I’ll give this chapter a five Jen, even if everything else in the book are just fetallio on how great Autochthon is.

        Hmmmm, this Eternal Nova guy, being mentioned in the book as inventing a MA style and the first to discover Evocation, not to mention awakening Evocation with a touch all seems…..a bit much I think.

        I’m ridiculously impressed how this book can write about the First Age and there are not even a single mention of Autochthon, all these examples of the Exalted hosts crafting and exploring and meeting new challenges are so exciting. If this is written in Ex2, I’ll probably need a puke bucket since the book will go out of its way to avoid mention anything about the Exalted and instead will wax endlessly about Neverborn, Yozi and Primordial. I think I understand why Infernal have such a hate-boner toward Ex3 now.

        This sentence is devoted to the person who namedrop the orichaclum throne that transform into a giant murder robot. May the Sanxing blessed you with endless happiness and when troubles comes knocking on your door, a thousand arms Guanyin will descend from heaven and social-fu the trouble into oblivion.

        The Age of Sorrow part is kinda predictable to be honest, civilization crumbling, can’t reach the height of the First Age,…etc.

        Eeeh, the Scarred Land section is really overwhelming me with all these examples, like, all the previous section has nothing but examples with a few fluff sentences in between, now it’s even MORE examples. I just gonna skim them all, oh wait, it’s the trash bin’s eye in the last example, and the book still refuse to say what this thing actually can do except “It’s suuuuper powerful man”, this is already annoying enough, the fact that it’s a typical RPG cursed object is even more infuriating, if I do find it I’ll reforge it in a squat toilet.

        And now I reach the Magical Materials section, and WOW! That Mesoamerican art amazing. Gosh, it will be so good if each material having its own traditional artwork.

        Orichalcum: I wonder why it’s called like that instead Sungold like the other materials. Moon-silver, Star-metal, Soul-steel and then….. Orichalcum is a little strange, isn’t it?

        The first fours themes are pretty obviously Sun-themes so my eyes kinda skipped over them, but the contrast (or harmony) between light and shadow is very interesting, I hope one of the Artifact in this book explore this side of Orichalcum.

        I have no idea what channel Essence/Sorcerous energies better actually mean, like, if you feed the Artifact one mote, it will use that mote more….efficiently? I just not sure if this will ever come up when homebrewing Evocations.

        Ooohh, the whole bringing down disasters is new for this edition I think? So many ideas are bubbling in my head when I read that line.
        That last paragraph kinda comes out of nowhere for me, greenish tint, baleful sorcerous essence? Who is channeling those essence, why was its tint is green? I’m not really sure what this text is trying to tell me exactly.

        Moonsilver: Those sfx are really hard to imagine, ripple like troubled water? Oh dear, it still have an affinity for the Wyld, I hate Lunar as shoggoth or silver Fair Folk so I don’t like this bit at all.
        Okaaay, I write so much for Orichalcum so this will look super bad. But that’s it for Moonsilver, since I have no ideas what its theme are. I saw the words in the book, but my mind is a shamble trying to connect the dots, one moment it’s secrecy then another it’s fury and furry. I sense a dark time ahead for Lunar.

        Starmetal: The idea that starmetal are formed by Creation’s anima is so very evocative, way better than soulsteel-for-Sidereal in previous edition. Aside from that, it’s very much what I expect from starmetal so there are not much to talk about. I do wonder something though, if it’s possible for Orichaclum to have Evocation that influence gods too? Since Zenith’s anima and Charms has those as one of its themes.

        Soulsteel: I always thought the book use “Enemies of the gods” since the Exalted hosts and the gods fought a bunch of different enemies now instead of Primordial-all-the-time, but now I think it’s more like a synonym for Primordial, probably?

        Well, suffering, disease, death, silence, darkness,…etc. Pretty much in-theme for Soulsteel. Though looking at those keywords, I just find it’s too funny that there are the idea of “White Soulsteel”, oh my weapon don’t have tortured souls instead, but it can shoot maggots that burrow into your skull and lay eggs in it.

        Jade: is jade, red jade is hot, green jade is plant-ly, there’s not much to talk about to be honest. Though some of the non-elemental keywords are very interesting, especially white jade, I mean, what does sleep and history have to do with earth exactly?

        Oh there is a sidebar, unlocking your boyfriend super powers by going out with him, sounds straight forward enough. That arrogant FA breastplate sounds amazing though, my Zenith gonna guilt-trip it into oblivion with the power of Black Claw. And there is an aquatic giant vessel called Brass Leviathan and an aquatic giant Lunar called Leviathan, both live in the West???? They should wrestling shirtless to see who deserved the title. Orca moon man vs mecha brass man, oh my gay.

        If you want extra themes for your Artifact, throw in some exotic material. Hmmm, a staff whose theme is age/longevity and it has a peach on the tip, could it be…?



        Conclusion: This chapter is such a roller-coaster for me, there is pure rage and there is ultimate joy. But anything that mention COLOSSAL WEAPONS deserves a five Jen!


        The no.1 fan of Demetheus. I also draw Exalted things and is looking for commission works ~
