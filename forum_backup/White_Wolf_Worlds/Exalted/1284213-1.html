            The Yozi Cytherea, The Essence Of All Things

            (Author's Note: My first draft of Cytherea can be found at Cytherea (Friv Yeti).
            Cytherea is the Divine Ignition, and the Mother of Creation. In the time before time, her solitude and loneliness gave voice to the Primordials, helping them to form their shapes and design their worlds. Together with Gaia and Autochthon, she shaped the form of Creation, and she lent her power to her siblings so that they would craft great wonders to amuse her. She clung to her creations and to her siblings, and she helped the other Primordials people the world with their own children and whims alongside hers.
            Cytherea was proud of the world which had been shaped from the stuff she had granted the Primordials, and she imagined that it loved her for her selflessness. This may or may not have been true. Whether the gods invited Cytherea into their rebellion has been lost to time - the only ones who still know the truth are the Incarnae, and Cytherea herself. Did they beg for her aid, only to be rebuffed as she chose her siblings instead of them? Were they consumed by the need to surpass her, and struck her from surprise? Was Luna jealous of Cytherea, or Cytherea of her creation? Whatever happened, Cytherea has seen fit to conceal it, out of shame, embarrassment, or love.
            Cytherea avoided fetich death during the Primordial War, but she was badly scarred nonetheless. Many of her souls were slaughtered, transforming and shifting into new shapes, and the Mother who ended the war was not entirely like the one who had entered it. She believed that she had discovered her error - although she must give her power to others, to shape them correctly, they were ungrateful, obsessed with being free. Preserving this illusion was important, Cytherea thought, and so she wrapped herself in mystery and lies, fading away from her fellow Yozi and living in the background. Many of her souls likewise vanished, and the Mother of All became as secretive as she had once been open, without ever losing the divine spark at her core.
            In modern times, Cytherea lurks in the background of Yozi politics and of life in Malfeas. Her jouten are never seen walking the streets, and few can point to any action and say that it is her doing. Instead, she takes part in a long plan, an expansive plot to secure freedom that she does not share with her fellow Yozi. No one knows what she is planning, but everyone knows that she has not surrendered. Cytherea is one of the few Yozi who do not hate the gods, or even the Exalted - she instead regards them with a loving sort of frustration. They may have scorned her, spurned her, sent her away, but she knows that, deep down, they don't understand what they've done. And, ideally, they never will. Let the other Primordials seek to force the Sun to bow his knee. It is enough for Cytherea if she knows that she is needed, and the rest of the world carries on oblivious.
            Cytherea and the Reclamation

            Cytherea is not, to the best knowledge of its architects, a member of the Reclamation. And just as well. Malfeas scorns her, for she gives only the barest nod to his authority, as though indulging in a child's whim. Cecelyne fears her, for the ease with which she subverts and circumvents her authority. She Who Lives In Her Name is more accepting of Cytherea's nature, but has few points of interest with her, certainly not enough to bring her into this conspiracy. And Adorjan... some admit that it is possible that Adorjan secretly involved Cytherea. Adorjan walks the secret places, and if anyone has had a true conversation with the Mother of All, it is likely that the Silent Wind would be that person. When asked, however, Adorjan merely laughs and changes the subject, and in truth it is not likely. Cytherea and Adorjan are nearly opposite in nature, barely able to converse with one another.
            The Ebon Dragon, on the other hand, is perpetually frustrated by Cytherea. It is maddeningly difficult to corrupt someone when you have no concept of their wants, their hopes and fears. Alone among the Primordials, Cytherea rivals him in secrecy, but hers is unmarred by spite, without even the deceit at the core of Szoreny. The others assume that he would thus want to keep her at arms' length of his great plan - but who can judge the logic of two beings composed of secrets and lies?
            If Cytherea is a part of the Reclamation, she has not admitted it. If she has taught her Charms to Infernals, they have not boasted of it. Indeed, if she is a part of this, she has worked quite assiduously to keep her role in the coming events a secret. This is not a tremendously comforting train of logic for those who think about it.
            The Cytherean Urge: The Urge To Support

            If Cytherea is a part of the Reclamation, it is likely that a few Infernals are bearing her Urge, rather than those of their supposed patrons. If this is the case, those marked by Cytherea would be filled with the desire to help others achieve their potential - not their desires, for Cytherea recognizes that those are fleeting things, but their perfect place in the world. Most commonly, this involves raising someone to greatness, always in a manner that requires that others not achieve that greatness (for example, placing Tepet Fokuf on the Scarlet Throne would qualify, while making Ledaal Kes a perfect spy would not). Other times, it involves raising an organization or nation at the expense of another (for example, desiring to have Gem conquer Paragon).
            Cecelyne's Torment involves inflicting terrible lassitude on her servants, such that they can no longer bear to face their own goals or activities. Mechanically, this functions identically to the Torment of the Ebon Dragon, except that the imposed Limit is Heart of Tears (rather than weeping, the character will simply sit vaguely, unable to focus enough to take actions). As with the Ebon Dragon, those who attempt to interact with the character are likely to fall prey to the same melancholy.

            The Mother of All is subtle and stubborn. She pushes others ahead, sacrificing her own needs in order to make them successful, while quietly ignoring dissent against her ideas and plans. She is filled with deep love for all, even her enemies, and will support them regardless of their attitudes even as she alters their plans.             Indulgent Ear Attitude

            • Edition: Second
            • Exalt: Infernal
            • Yozi: Cytherea
            • Caste:
            • Source: FrivYeti
            Click Charms:Indulgent Ear Attitude to go this Charm page

            Cost: 1 mote; Mins: Essence 2; Type: Reflexive
            Keywords: Combo-OK
            Duration: Indefinite
            Prerequisite Charms: None
            The first step to gaining people's trust and love is to pretend that you care about the same things as them. Of course, with so many people, actually caring may prove difficult, and so the character finds a shortcut. Upon using this Charm, the character chooses any Intimacy she possesses, and shrouds it behind a false Intimacy that she creates, which must be positive. Any attempt to read her motivations, either mundane or supernatural, will return the false Intimacy as one belonging to the character, and no attempt may return the true one. If opposed by a perfect Charm, the character adds her Essence in automatic successes to the roll-off. If the character takes actions demonstrably against her supposed Intimacy, onlookers will assume that she is simply acting against a desire, which people do sometimes do.
            At Essence 3, the character may expand the scope of this effect. By spending 2 motes instead of 1, she may have the above effect apply to any Virtue, causing it to read as though it were a totally different rating than it is. At Essence 5, the character may spend 4 motes in order to do the same with her Motivation.
            This Charm may be purchased a second time at Essence 3. At this time, a character activating this Charm may commit an additional one mote to conceal up to (Essence) Intimacies at once (or a mix of Intimacies, Virtues, and Motivation if she has spent the additional motes required to conceal those). Alternately, she may spend an additional four motes to conceal all of those traits behind a false shell.

            Stepping Between Moments

            • Edition: Second
            • Exalt: Infernal
            • Yozi: Cytherea
            • Caste:
            • Source: FrivYeti
            Click Charms:Stepping Between Moments to go this Charm page

            Cost: 5 motes, 1 Willpower; Mins: Essence 2; Type: Simple
            Keywords: Combo-OK
            Duration: One Scene
            Prerequisite Charms: Indulgent Ear Attitude
            It is a small step from shrouding your beliefs to shrouding your body. By wrapping herself in a lack of desire, the Infernal steps out of sync with the rest of the world, fading from sight. As long as this Charm is active, any Perception or Awareness rolls made targeting the Infernal suffer an external penalty equal to the character's Essence rating, and the character suffers no penalties to her Stealth rolls for using Stealth in wildly inappropriate situations (for example, walking down the middle of a sunny street). The character's words fade, her feet leave no footprints, even her scent vanishes like smoke. However, stepping out of the world requires one to actually step out of the world. If the character takes any action that has an impact on the world, from attempting to open a door to speaking to someone, the Charm instantly ends, and cannot be activated again in the scene. If the Charm is cancelled, it also cannot be activated again in the scene. Actions taken that cause the Charm to end do not gain any benefits of either the character's mundane or magical stealth.

            When Cytherea meets her siblings and children, she speaks idly, but her words often serve to create new ideas and loves in her targets, who seem something in a new light. Upon activating that Charm, the character may spend an additional two motes to make an attempt at unnatural mental influence instead of her usual social attack. Only new concepts may be implanted by Cytherea's divine spark.
            At Essence 4+, the character may spend an additional two motes when activating this Charm to fortify her Intimacy. If she does so, it applies double the usual bonus and penalty to MDV for as long as it remains magically charged.

            Reality-Evading Defense

            • Edition: Second
            • Exalt: Infernal
            • Yozi: Cytherea
            • Caste:
            • Source: FrivYeti
            Click Charms:Reality-Evading Defense to go this Charm page

            Cost: 3 motes; Mins: Essence 2; Type: Reflexive
            Keywords: Combo-OK
            Duration: Instant
            Prerequisite Charms: Stepping Between Moments
            As harm approaches her, the character fades silently away, vanishing from all of reality for a fraction of a second as she effortlessly avoids harm. This Charm acts as a dodge, perfect evading any attack with a physical component. The character may use Reality-Evading Defense as a dodge against undodgeable attacks - however, it suffers from the Imperfection of the Mother of Creation. Imperfection of the Mother of Creation: At her core, Cytherea still believes that her children love and want her, even when presented with all evidence to the contrary. As such, any Charm bearing this Imperfection may not be used to defend against a character to which the user has a positive Intimacy, or one who is a positive focus of the Infernal's Motivation or Urge. If the Infernal has an Intimacy or Motivation towards an organization, only critical people are affected. This applies equally to mystically imposed Intimacies (such as the one created by the Solar Charm Husband-Seducing Demon Dance.
            In addition, if the character does not have any positive Intimacies or Motivation towards an individual or group, she may not activate this Charm. If she possesses only one or two such Intimacies or Motivations, she must spend an additional two motes to activate it.
            Note that this limitation is based on the Infernal's perceptions, not the truth. If the Infernal believes she is fighting a loved one, she cannot defend, even if the 'loved one' is a stranger in disguise. Similarly, if fighting a loved one disguised as a stranger, she may defend normally.

            Lost To The World

            • Edition: Second
            • Exalt: Infernal
            • Yozi: Cytherea
            • Caste:
            • Source: FrivYeti
            Click Charms:Lost To The World to go this Charm page

            Cost: 5 motes, 2 Willpower; Mins: Essence 4; Type: Simple
            Keywords: Shaping, Sorcerous
            Duration: Indefinite
            Prerequisite Charms: Stepping Between Moments, False Negotiation Approach
            There are truths that adults conceal from children, because they are not ready to know them. Cytherea conceals many of these truths. She has concealed so many, in fact, that most people hardly remember who she is anymore. The character chooses one fact about herself when activating this Charm. This can be something as simple as "I am skilled with swords", or as complex as "I was present at the Blue Lotus Inn in Gem on the 17th of Resplendent Wood, R.Y. 767." Whatever the fact that the character chooses to conceal, she reaches into the fabric of the world, and causes it to vanish.
            All beings with an Essence rating lower than that of the character forget the information in question. This is a mental Shaping effect. In addition, any physical evidence of the information is muddled, lost, or altered, with the exception of that contained within artifacts or similar magical devices with ratings equal to or greater than the character's Essence (for this purpose, consider Artifact N/As to be Artifact 8). Any attempt to learn the information fails automatically. If this effect is opposed by a perfect effect, the Infernal adds her Essence in automatic successes to conceal the effect.
            There is, however, a major weakness to this power. If the Infernal does anything to reveal this hidden fact (engaging in swordplay while claiming to be unskilled with a sword, expressing familiarity with a location she should not have been to), the effect shatters with regards to everyone who witnesses the revelation. These beings can spread the information in question without penalty, and anyone they spread it to also becomes immune. This immunity is permanent - the Infernal may never conceal this fact from those people again, even by using other applications of this Charm that overlap. Furthermore, this Charm cannot be casually dismissed. If the charm is dispelled, it is still considered to be in effect for one week, during which time there is no mystical protection over the knowledge, but the contagious immunity to reactivation of others learning it still linger. As such, those who learn this Charm are generally very, very careful about what they hide, and how they stop hiding it.

            What Doesn't Kill You

            • Edition: Second
            • Exalt: Infernal
            • Yozi: Cytherea
            • Caste:
            • Source: FrivYeti
            Click Charms:What Doesn't Kill You to go this Charm page

            Cost: 1 or 2 motes per level, 1 Willpower; Mins: Essence 2; Type: Simple
            Keywords: Combo-OK, Crippling
            Duration: Instant
            Prerequisite Charms: Gift Of Hope
            All injuries are simply life's way of teaching children a lesson, and thus Cytherea finds it a simple thing to change one sort of lesson into another. The character targets any injured character within (Essence x5) yards. She must then spend 1 mote per level of Bashing damage that she wishes to repair, or 2 motes per level of Lethal damage, as well as one point of Willpower. The affected injuries are healed completely. However, the target immediately develops a number of points of negative mutations equal to the number of levels of damage taken, chosen by the Infernal. These mutations typically reflect the circumstances under which the target was injured. These negative mutations heal as though they were levels of damage of the appropriate type and level healed, and heal after all true injuries, starting with the most severe mutation. Effects that cure Crippling effects may cure these mutations, as can effects that alter healing rates, but effects that instantly cure damage cannot. If used against a target that the character has a positive Intimacy towards, this Charm has no Willpower cost. If the Infernal inflicted the wounds herself, or if they were inflicted by her allies while she was in battle with the target, the Charm costs double the usual number of motes. The Infernal may not heal more than (Essence) levels of damage from a single target per day. A child can only learn so much.
            A second purchase of this Charm at Essence 4 allows the healing of aggravated damage at a cost of 5 motes per level.

            It seems the XOWA tool is capable of handling most of the wiki dump. It has some problems, such as the Charm lists not being automatically displayed yet, but the page content is there...

            Very short walkthrough (for XOWA 1.10.2):

            1. Grab the wiki archive (https://archive.org/download/wiki-wi...07-wikidump.7z)
            2. Install XOWA (http://xowa.sourceforge.net/) -- grab the archive and extract it to where you want XOWA installed
            3. Menu 'Tools' -> 'Import Offline...'
            4. Switch 'Wiki' to 'Other Wiki', switch 'Where to get the dump' to 'read from file'
            5. Enter the XML file as the file, leave all other settings as is.
            6. Hit the 'Import now' button (the process might take a while)
            7. When the process is done, you'll be taken to the Exalted Wiki main page

            I recommend immediately adding the Exalted Wiki front page as a bookmark through the 'Bookmarks' menu; navigating back after closing XOWA appears to be tricky.

            There's lots of things that don't work out perfectly, but at least you have the content in a more readable format. If you know the old page name of the charm/artifact/whatever you want to look up (such as http://wiki.white-wolf.com/exalted/i...Divine_Portion), you can simply go there directly by editting the adres bar in XOWA to be '<yourdir>/wiki/Charms:Appointment_of_Divine_Portion'. And up pops the charm.

                1. Grab the wiki archive (https://archive.org/download/wiki-wi...07-wikidump.7z)
                2. Install XOWA (http://xowa.sourceforge.net/) -- grab the archive and extract it to where you want XOWA installed
                3. Menu 'Tools' -> 'Import Offline...'
                4. Switch 'Wiki' to 'Other Wiki', switch 'Where to get the dump' to 'read from file'
                5. Enter the XML file as the file, leave all other settings as is.
                6. Hit the 'Import now' button (the process might take a while)
                7. When the process is done, you'll be taken to the Exalted Wiki main page

                I recommend immediately adding the Exalted Wiki front page as a bookmark through the 'Bookmarks' menu; navigating back after closing XOWA appears to be tricky.

                    The Yozi Cytherea, The Essence Of All Things

                    (Author's Note: My first draft of Cytherea can be found at Cytherea (Friv Yeti).
                    Cytherea is the Divine Ignition, and the Mother of Creation. In the time before time, her solitude and loneliness gave voice to the Primordials, helping them to form their shapes and design their worlds. Together with Gaia and Autochthon, she shaped the form of Creation, and she lent her power to her siblings so that they would craft great wonders to amuse her.                     1. Grab the wiki archive (https://archive.org/download/wiki-wi...07-wikidump.7z)
                    2. Install XOWA (http://xowa.sourceforge.net/) -- grab the archive and extract it to where you want XOWA installed
                    3. Menu 'Tools' -> 'Import Offline...'
                    4. Switch 'Wiki' to 'Other Wiki', switch 'Where to get the dump' to 'read from file'
                    5. Enter the XML file as the file, leave all other settings as is.
                    6. Hit the 'Import now' button (the process might take a while)
                    7. When the process is done, you'll be taken to the Exalted Wiki main page
