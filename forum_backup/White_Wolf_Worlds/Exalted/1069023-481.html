        Important clarifications - Pestletails are actually a Pleistocene mammal known as a glyptodont (specifically those like Doedicurus). Their stats are stated by developers as being usable for ankylosaurs until ankylosaurs are statted out. Also, crocodiles are crocodiles and not dinosaurs, and river dragons are somewhat mosasaur-ish, which places them as being closer to lizards, as mosasaurs were squamates, in other words lepidosaurs rather than archosaurs (two principle main branches of Reptilia* that split a long time ago).

        Edit: As Blaque reminded me, I forgot to add an asterisk to Reptilia to make it Reptilia*.

                                        Originally posted by GrayWalker View Post

                                        There are some things logically entailed by it that aren't represented by its mechanical bonuses. If you have four arms, it follows that you can hold four things. However, there aren't any mechanical bonuses from them other than the ones the Merit provides.

                                        Yes, but if you want a dozen arms, your single purchase of Extra Limbs can be a dozen arms.
