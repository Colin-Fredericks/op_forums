                                            Originally posted by Baaldam View Post

                                            As an aside, do Lunars still have a core beast shape/warform as in previous editions?
                                            They still start with one animal form without Heart's Blood, and right now that's the only advantage of the spirit shape (regarding shapeshifting, at least). As it should be, because we don't want people choosing totems based on what is most advantageous in a fight for cheaper or faster transformation.

                                            DBT is being split apart. The Hybrid form is the package of mutations, and you can have multiple hybrid shapes. The War Form is the massive stat-buff you can only use while in Hybrid form.

                                            Some part of those buffs is still useful in a warstrider - better meeting the strength minimums, boosts to movement actions... And some mutations are still useful inside a cockpit, like Enhanced Senses.
