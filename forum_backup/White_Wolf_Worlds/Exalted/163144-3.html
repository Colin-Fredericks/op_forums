    Winter is Coming
    Abyssal Awareness. Permanent. Can only be learned by a Deathknight who has survived a beheading (through the power of their Charms, at the moment of their Exaltation, by the timeless turns of Fate, etc.). It grants them a great and abiding clarity regarding a single great impending threat that targets one of their Intimacies. Visions of the future etch themselves into their mind while they sleep, allowing them to attempt to witness and investigate events to which they are not party so long as they closely relate to this threat.

    This vision cannot turn inward. If their hearts are cold and forsaken and have no remaining Intimacies to safeguard, or if the deathknight is the single greatest impending threat to their Intimacies, they become listless and unaware of the threat they themselves pose, taking a penalty on actions to notice and investigate the ruin they themselves have brought. People they once cared for may be hanged, betrayed or slaughtered, and lands they once ruled may fall into ruin, but what does that matter? Winter is Coming.

                New Charm: Cherry Blossoms Blooming
                Terrestrial Performance (Wood-focused, but general)
                Displaying grace, skill, and self-mastery, the Dragon-Blooded enhances a Performance roll by flaring her anima to its fullest for up to several seconds, before dropping it to its original level. Her anima can inflict no damage to anything during this time, becoming not a weapon for the flesh but a feast for the senses. This may take any pleasing form, such as the blossoming of a magnificently perfumed bouquet or a grand stone statue of Pasiap or the Imperial Mountain, the crack and still life of a lightning strike or bonfire, or a brief, sweet elegy on the wind. All who perceive it find the display breathtaking, granting the Terrestrial <benefit>. Though its greatest beauty is brief, any minor pleasant reminders it produces, such as the scent and sight of flower petals, a sweet smoke of incense or a faint echo, provide the Terrestrial <minor benefit> on social rolls with anyone who witnessed the initial display for the rest of the scene (and the next, if Cherry Blossoms Blooming was used near the end of the scene).

                Some Chosen of the Dragons sacrifice art for more practical ends. While the great display remains just as beautiful, the Terrestrial maximizes the remnants it leaves at the expense of graceful restraint. The great clouds of overbearingly scented blossom petals, dust, or smoke cost the performer any benefit, and may even earn penalties, with those whose tastes favor refinement or with art forms intrinsically dependent on such during and after the display, though those interested in spectacle may appreciate it just as much or more. However, these reminders are now substantial enough to provide <benefit> to Stealth actions in the area against the senses they affect. In particular, if these reminders target a sense of smell, all rolls dependent upon that sense automatically fail (or go to Charm roll off). Wyld Hunts have found this talent to be of particular use against a common advantage among the Lunar Anathema.

                                All Worlds Are One Hyperbole.
                                Lunar Manipulation
                                The wiles of Luna's Chosen render even the preposterous both credible and obvious. This Charm supplements an attempt to persuade someone of a ridiculous lie, even in the face of direct evidence. So long as the Lunar provides some form of rationale, even one that makes sense only through the logic of metaphor or dream, the lie weaves together a new world for the target to perceive. This may include helping a guard understand that the reason it's a little hot today is that the entire village is actually on fire, or that the ruling lady just needs to look a little closer at her right-hand man to see that the reason for his loyalty is that he's a literal dog.

                                The target will be baffled at the inability to see this obvious truth before, but will proceed to treat the world as though what is seen is perceived is the truth. If the guard shouts to people about fire, he may need to strain to hear their confused replies over the crackling blaze, while the ruling lady may note to her man that she never realized he was a dog, she will hear plaintive barks and whines instead of his flustered attempts to point out his humanity. When confronted with evidence of the categorical falsehood -- evidence that the target can perceive, at any rate -- the target may roll again with a bonus to disbelieve it against the original difficulty. The guard may find it odd that the fire does not burn him nor do the people walking through it seem bothered, and the lady may rethink herself if someone else points out the poor man's humanity to her. The target receives a bonus on this roll, which gradually increases as inconsistencies mount.

                                Originally posted by Lafing Cat View Post
                                New Charm: Never Before, Never Again
                                Infernal (Abyssal?) Integrity
                                Wrapping a hand around a chosen Tie, the Warlock wrenches it free from his heart and grinds it to nothing in his hand. It disappears like dust on the wind, and takes with it all memory of the Intimacy's subject. All the Green Sun Prince's memories rewrite and reorder themselves to account for this. Mark the Intimacy, as the Infernal no longer possesses it for normal purposes. Still, the hole in his heart remains, and seeks to protect itself from being filled. The Infernal functionally now possesses a Bond, at the same degree of the Tie, to prevent himself from reacquiring or being reminded of the old Intimacy. The Infernal may see or become aware of or be reminded of the subject of the Tie, so long as the original context is not referenced, and may form a new Bond to the subject, so long as the context differs meaningfully from the original context.
