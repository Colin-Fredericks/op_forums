        I also have two unfinished and unpublished fights, one against Fakharu and one against Octavian, and they also went the same way. Even with Fakharu starting at range and breathing poison on all three exalts facing him he still wasn’t doing well by turn 5. He’d wither somebody and then the simple overwhelming damage from three artifact weapons would take it all away again.

        They might be scary if there was only one or two people with artifact weapons and armor in the group, but otherwise not really. Thankfully there’s almost no powerful enemies in Exalted that aren’t also the leaders of other powerful things. Fakharu should have elemental backup, Octavian should have a host of demonic allies, ect.

        EDIT: Which might be a good thing. If a small group of people who aren’t THAT combat focused want to fight Octavian they can, and the ST just has to bend things a bit so that they’re facing him alone. If a group that has everyone with combat abilities at 5 and artifact weapons, then the ST can bring on the size 3 battlegroup of Blood apes, Octavians special Agata, and a demon-blooded akuma champion or something.

            Ma-Ha-Sachi vs Five Starting Solars:

            Solars:

            Dawn: Shining Topaz
            Zenith: Karal Fire Orchid, Tomb of Dreams Stats
            Twilight: Iay Sela-Amu, Tomb of Dreams Stats
            Night: Faka Kun, Tomb of Dreams Stats
            Eclipse: Modest Desert Mouse

            Ma-Ha-Sachi: Kickstarter Lunars Stats
            Wolf Bodyguards: Size 2, Magnitude 10, Elite Bodyguards, Elite Drill, Might 1, Defense 8, Soak 12, Bow 14 at Short 13/1


            Combatants start at Long Range to give Ma-Ha-Sachi’s archers a fighting chance.

            Shining Topaz spends 10ph (13/23m) and Willpower (4), for 13+1 successes 17i
            Karal: Spends 7ph (13/26m) and Willpower (5) for 8+1 successes 12i
            Iay: Spends 4ph (13/29m) and Willpower (6) for 5+1 successes 9i 4sm
            Faka: Spends 8ph (13/25m) and Willpower (5) for 8+1 successes 12i
            Mouse: Spends 8ph (13/10m) and Willpower (4) for 9+1 successes 13i

            Ma-Ha-Suchi: 21 dice for 10ph (21/34m) plus Willpower (9) for 13i (Hybrid Body Transformation is active)
            Guard: 7i

            Round 1

            Topaz closes to Medium
            Mouse closes to Medium
            Ma-Ha-Suchi uses uses Invincible Beast-Warrior Empowerment (21/19m, 3a, 8WP, 12i)
            Karal closes to Medium
            Faka closes to Medium
            Iay closes to Medium
            Guard Aims

            Round 2 – 5m regen on all

            Topaz closes to Short and Rushes Ma-Ha-Suchi. Ma rolls 16 dice (21/17m) for 4 successes to Topaz’s 10 (13/22m) 5 successes, and against the odds, Ma is Rushed.

            Mouse closes to Short and unleashes a Kiai! Targetting the Elder she backs her blast with Essence (13/0m) and Willpower (3) who attempts to Parry (21/7m). Mouse rolls 11+1 successes, scraping a hit, which is Soaked down to minimum damage. Mouse 15i. Ma 11i. Mouse also sings a song of woe Inspiring Ma with a Minor Intimacy of Fear and a second Intimacy of Love for Mouse: she uses a 1m Excellency for +1 die and Threefold Magnetic Ardor for +2 successes (9/0m). Ma can’t very well raise up a Lunar Realm if he’s dead, so applies his Defining Intimacy for +4 Resolve, taking him to 9 and giving Mouse 2 non-charm dice. Mouse rolls 11 successes Instilling both Intimacies, but Ma quashes them both with Willpower (6).

            Karal closes to Short.

            Faka closes to Short and launches a Full Excellency Withering Knife throw on the battlegroup, rolling 25 dice vs Defense 9... and missing.

            Ma-Ha-Suchi Commands his Battle Group to rain arrows on the Solars, rolling 21 dice (18/0m) and Spending Willpower (5) and using Raiton Banquet Assault to add to damage (16/0m, 4WP, 10i). He rolls 14+1 successes. Ouch.

            Iay Shapes Sorcery with a Full Excellency (13/25m) and gathers 9sm, plus 3sm from his nightmares. He spends 1WP and casts Death of Obsidian Butterflies, targetting the Battle Group (to avoid hitting his allies in the back) with a full Excellency (13/16m) for 9 successes vs Parry 5. 4 Magnitude damage.

            The Bear Guard fire arrows with Willpower (3). 11+3 dice gets 5 successes +1+3+15= 24 successes. All the Solars are hit. (10+Threshold Successes-Soak) dice +18 successes in Initiative damage. Yikes.

            Topaz: Dipping Swallow (13/20m), Excellency (13/10m), Hail Shattering Practice (13/9m) is hit by 11, Soaks 10 and takes 20 Initiative damage, CRASHED at -3i.


            And I’m going to stop there…

            Yup. Ma-Ha-Suchi with archers is an absolute beast.

            Five Starting Level Solars can ABSOLUTELY beat him hands down, no doubt. But they have to be five Solars that are custom built to take him down.

            Five normal Solars, even accounting for a relatively powerful Dawn build are going to die.

            Still at the end of Round 2, Ma-Ha-Suchi has burned half his Willpower and is down from 74m to 16m... and he's Rushed. But then the Solars are Crashed and about to take Decisive Battle Group Damage.

            Respect your (Lunar) Elders kids.
