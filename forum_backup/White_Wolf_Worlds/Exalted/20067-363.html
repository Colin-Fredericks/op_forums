                                    Originally posted by Eldagusto View Post
                                    If a Character level two stunts a defense for each attack in a round... what is stopping them from just racking up the Willpower? Anyone thought about a limit of Willpower gain of once per round?
                                    1. "Whenever a player describes a two-point stunt, her character gains a point of Willpower, up to a maximum of her permanent Willpower rating." (P. 187)
                                    2. "to be a two-point stunt, the action needs to stand out memorably as a highlight of the scene. [...] A player might get two or three of these in an evening of play." (P. 187)

                                    Even if the player can come up with something awesome for several defenses each round, and even if all of them are awesome and justify a two point stunt in themselves, they shouldn't all be because they can't all be the highlight. If you want to reward creative play, you could extend the first stunt to a second(+) defense and consider the various descriptions to be part of a greater whole instead of giving out single two point stunts.
