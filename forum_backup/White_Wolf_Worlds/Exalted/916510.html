    The Uncrucified, An Eclipse Caste Fanfiction (Text + Podfic/Audiobook)

    06-17-2016, 08:36 PM
    I've been wanting to write more for a long time now, but my day job always got in the way and I found the task very intimidating. Playing Exalted provided just the inspirational punch in the face I needed to create not only a short story about my Eclipse Caste's Exaltation, but an audiobook as well! I'm excited to share this creation where more Exalted fans might be able to see and enjoy it.

    I'll be sharing one chapter a week until it's been fully posted. Half of the story (4 of the 8 total chapters) have already been shared online. I'll post here when new chapters go live, for those who want to keep up. Give it a read/listen and let me know your thoughts!

    SUMMARY: Whispers of The Uncrucified lie on the lips of every slave in Gem, Chiaroscuro, Nexus and beyond. She is Kalara Vadras, Chosen of the Unconquered Sun and bringer of the Eclipse. Some say she is a murderer, an Anathema, a Deceiver. Others know the truth written in her own words and passed along to those who dare keep such a heretical tome in their possession. Read on, if you have the courage to see the world for what it really is.

    Reading and listening links can be found here.

    EDIT: I'm also working on a completely expanded and revised version of this short story.
    Check out the early Alpha draft at the reader feedback blog     HERE!
