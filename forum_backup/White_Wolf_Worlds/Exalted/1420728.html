    IRL Exaltation, as determined by random dice rolls

    10-28-2020, 07:59 PM
    So during a patch of insomnia I started listing out what would happen if Exalts suddenly appeared in the real world as it stands. For lack of a better method, I divided them out according to current population by country, as listed on this site. Took a little while and a lot of abuse of a dicebot, but for what it's worth here's what I came up with. If you're wondering why you should care, you absolutely shouldn't, but it's a lot of text, so I figured I'd post it in lieu of having anything interesting to share.

    Rules for countries with 0.5% of world population or more (Category A)
    Method of determining numbers
    Solars/Lunars (600 total): 6*population percentage, round outcome down. Split evenly between Solars and Lunars, where the number was odd the extra one is decided by d2 roll (1 Solar, 2 Lunar).

    Solars: 1d27 for Supernal (alphabetically, 13 is Martial Arts, also 27 is Evocations which was available to any caste). Then 1d6 for type (1-3 Solar, 4-5 Abyssal, 6 Infernal), 1d2 if multiple castes get ability as a Supernal choice, higher or lower of the two in traditional order (Dawn, Zenith, Twilight, Night, Eclipse). Abyssals and Infernals had the same ability options as Solars; just because I didn't have an alternative.

    Lunars: 1d4 for caste (1 Full, 2 Changing, 2 No, 4 Casteless). Then 1d3 pick the first of their caste attributes (eg 2 = Dexterity), 1d2 to pick from the remaining two for the other (eg 1 = Strength, 2 = Stamina). Not done for Casteless.

    Method of determining numbers
    Sidereals (100 total): 1*population percentage, round outcome down. Countries with less than 1% population but at least 0.5% got a d2 roll (1 they get a Siddie, 2 they don't).

    Sidereals: 1d25 to choose an ability, caste determined by which one had that ability in 2e lists.

    Method of determining numbers
    Dragonbloods (10,000 total): 100*Population Percentage
    DBs: 1d5 for aspect using traditional order (Air, Earth, Fire, Water, Wood). Weirdly, Water was almost always overrepresented.


    Rules for countries with less than 0.5% of world population or less (Category B)
    198 countries left with less than 0.5% population. 141 with more than 0.0% population percentage.

    Solars/Lunars/Sidereals: 155 remaining Celestial exalts lotteried off by dicebot. Any country could have up to 1 Solar, 1 Lunar and 1 Sidereal. Repeats of any given type were re-rolled.
    Solaroid ratios adhered to. Once a country was determined to get a Solar I rolled them in a dicebot. First 8 got the remaining 8 Infernals, who were then decided as above. Same for the remaining Abyssals. All those left were Solars.

    Giving proportional numbers of DBs to each country with more than 0.0% resulted in 9,997 DBs being issued. Last three were issued amongst the 0.0% countries by lottery (Turks & Caicos/Seychelles/Saint Martin).


    Notes
    • Celestial Exalts all appear within a week of E-Day at Essence 1, 0 XP. Celestial exaltation is totally at random according to the priorities of their Incarnae patron.
    • Dragonbloods Exalt at E2 if aged up to 40, E3 if aged 41-80 and E4 if aged 81+. DB exaltation is most often along family lines in countries with lots of DBs.
    • Countries with no Exalts at all get a single Exigent. Just because.
    • My tallies at the end SEEM to match up except that I have 301 Solars. No idea how that happened since I have 300 listed Supernal abilities. I'm not above admitting there are probably other maths errors along the way.
    • I make no judgement on what does or does not count as a country IRL, all names are taken from the website linked above. Categories A & B are based on population size only.
    • Good sleep hygiene is important, if you ignore it you might end up doing mad shit like this.
