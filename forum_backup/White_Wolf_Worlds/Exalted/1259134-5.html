Don't you want your children to have the best chance possible of being Princes of the Earth?). But if you don't want that, Progenerative Essence usefully avoids it, while at the same time shutting down ideas of breeding DBs like livestock, and a whole bunch of scenarios in-between.

                                            Originally posted by DrLoveMonkey View Post

                                            I think personally it might work better as a subtle thing. One of the balancing acts here is kind of like with that LGBT acceptance in the Realm. On the one hand in the realistic fallen socially regressive, racist, sexist, classist, nepotism rife, extractionist empire it seems an odd contrast that acceptance for genderfluid individuals is universal and never questioned, but on the other it’s really lame if you are or just want to play somebody who’s trans or bisexual and the game says you’re going to be ridiculed endlessly for it.

                                            Realistically the Realm could end up being shockingly sexist, where a female mortal would have a better chance of being promoted to manager/officer than a male exalt simply because men aren’t officer material and everyone knows it. Which kind of sucks for anyone who wants to play a man in the game unless they’re looking to smash the matriarchy as a personal goal. Rather I suggest being really subtle about it, let your players get 15 sessions into the game and realize only one of the officials they’ve talked to has been male, or have a scene where the male members of a house shut up and retreat from the conversation when a female representative from their house shows up, even if they’re technically not any more in charge.
                                            I'd say the LGBT is at least partly a result of the Solar rule history (exaltation didn't discriminate, and once someone was a Solar/Lunar their personal preferences were most likely not up to a debate or for judgement), late Shogunate debauchery, and, last but not least, the fact that the Empress herself is very much bisexual (and not trying to hide it). Add to that gods, and you have a situation where sexual preference discrimination might not be as common. Now, that might be a little bit different on the lowest level, but even then i'd think the social norms would be a little more relaxed.

                                            And as for balancing factors, there's a very big one that is often forgotten. Two first children of the Empress vere male, very powerful and very influential. And while we don't speak of Manosque anymore, Ragara is still very much alive, and still very much touchy about the subject. As such, i'd be surprised if House Ragara was anything but patriarchal in its internal politics. Other male-descended houses might have their own differences as well, that at the very least might soften the bias, or sometimes even go against it (House Cathak for example seems to lean towards having a male in the highest position, although we don't know if it's a result of some internal bias or there simbly being no other candidates of sufficient quality).

                                            Now, i'd expect House Ragara-Mnemon relations at the lower level be full on snide sexist remarks going both ways, seeing as those two houses are probably at the very opposite sides in this (and their founders don't like each other, to put it mildly).

                                            As for dynastic marriages, while the default is that the man moves to the woman's house, in most cases what dictates the end result would be business and politics, not tradition. Tradition would be at best used to strengthen one's negotiating positions.
