    What are Chimera in 3rd Edition?

    02-06-2020, 09:52 PM
    I've always felt conflicted about Chimera in 2nd Edition. The idea of some Lunars devolving into protean horrors due to Wyld exposure was tragically interesting, but it always felt weird that Lunars were supposed to be dealing with the Wyld quite a bit & had less immunity to it then their patron goddess.

    I've had many alternative ideas about how Chimera might come to pass for home-brew, but they all had their issues.

    How has 3rd Edition handled the concept & origin of Chimera? I heard it is quite different.

      Originally posted by Daredevil View Post
      How has 3rd Edition handled the concept & origin of Chimera? I heard it is quite different.
      Yeah, the whole idea of the Lunar Exaltations being warped by the Wyld, and the tattoos being necessary to correct that has been completely dropped. The moonsilver tattoos and the current set of Lunar castes that they create are presented as an active step that the Lunars took to adapt to the post-Usurpation landscape. They decided that their old castes didn't work anymore, so they came up with a new set, and the tattoos are the way they set them (and there's some hints that changing the castes happened before, possibly just after the war against the Primordials). Casteless Lunars now don't risk mutation or derangement when shapeshifting. They get access to some caste powers from all three of the current Castes, though on a shifting schedule, and they don't get Caste Attributes (but do get slightly cheaper attributes and charms that are non-favored).

      Chimeras, rather than being linked to Castelessness, are instead any Lunar who's spirit shape is a combination of two different animals rather than a single beast (which is represented by taking the charm Chimera-Soul Expression). So Ma-Ha-Suchi, for example, is a chimera, because his spirit shape is a hybrid of goat and wolf.

          Now, a Chimera is just any Lunar with the Essence 1 Universal Charm Chimera-Soul Expression, which lets them mix a second animal into their spirit-shape, applying some merits to represent the added traits; those traits are also present in Hybrid Body Transformation. Instead of being degenerate mutant monsters, it's just a representation of how some Lunars' spirit shapes are or become more than just natural beasts. It's very neat, because you can just be a Chimera if you want, and that just means your Lunar nature pushes at the limits of the animal kingdom.

                              Ah, my apologies, I myself had forgotten that the portion of Exalted: the Lunars I have read so far did actually clarify on the point.

                              Lunars that had cultivated hybrid totems were called chimera... in the sense of "we're pejoratively comparing you to or outright accusing you of being this otherwise separate stigmatized category".

                              A chimera was a Lunar who had lost their mind and in a state of almost constantly shapeshifting. A Lunar with a hybrid totem was supposed to be somebody who looked vaguely similar enough to that for other Lunars to be put on edge by the resemblance, or even think that the hybrid totems was actively seeking to become a chimera.

                              So there was chimera the being and chimera the slur.

                                                          I wouldn't say they're so much protean horrors, as Chimeric horrors (appropriately), with the characteristics of dozens of beasts large and small, mammalian and alien, grafted together to make a horror out of nightmare.
                                                          It's not... not protean, but it's not like a formless horror that can become anything.

                                                          For example, most of their mutation charms only deal with mutations based on your known forms. So you might walk into battle with the claws of a wolverine, the skin of a rhino, tentacles and spider-legs, antannae and hawk eyes and a beak full of fanged teeth that inject neuro-toxin, not to mention shoot strands of web or belch out a swarm of angry wasps... but you can't have iron skin, a bone-gun that shoots burrowing bugs, tentacles covered in glass shards, a shadow-maw of flint teeth, and four necrotic zombie-legs.

                                                          You're not a Tzimisce, or a Tyranid, or a Shoggoth.
