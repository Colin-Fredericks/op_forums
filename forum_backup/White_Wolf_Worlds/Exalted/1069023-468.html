                                                I suppose that's a good thing, but it was very amusing.

                                                I know it's still a long way off, so maybe you haven't decided for sure (and I won't hold you to it), but I was wondering about Sidereal charms.

                                                The 2nd ed Sidereal splat-book charms were, as far as I'm aware (I don't have a copy of 1st ed Sidereals), mostly just the 1st ed charms updated (badly) to 2nd ed.

                                                With 3rd ed, are you going to try and create a charmset broadly similar to 1st ed Sidereals, or start again from first principles, with just a few of the more evocative charms kept? Because the example in the Antagonist section seems to mostly have the same charms as Sidereals had before.

                                                The reason I ask is that I really like Sidereals, but not their charmset. That may seem odd, but I don't think the Sidereal charmset is that great at following the actual themes of Sidereals. Some parts are (Lore, Awareness and Ride come to mind), but others just feel like "give Sidereals wacky random powers" that don't relate that strongly to their themes (though not quite as much as I found Infernals). They're often decent or fun powers, they just don't always seem very appropriate for the secret ninja fate masters who live in Tian and can teach secret techniques.

                                                That's why I actually liked Throne Shadow so much; it felt much more appropriate to Sidereals (as a fighting style about being the power behind the throne, and also actually making you good at being a Sifu) than Violet Bier (which, tbh, always seemed to me to just be an MA about stabbing people really well that wouldn't seem out of place used by anyone else).
                                                Oddly, also, the Drive and Firearms charms in Shards. The feel of the powers largely felt to me like how Sidereal charms should work.
                                                Together, these made me think "hmmm, Exalted writers come up with much better charms when they're not trying to just copy 1st ed".

                                                So it made me think that you guys can probably come up with a bunch of new Sidereal charms that would make Sidereals much more thematically tight and fun to play.

                                                But I realise this might be a controversial view, because people (possibly including you) love 1st ed Sidereals.

                                                STing Bronze Age Exalted
