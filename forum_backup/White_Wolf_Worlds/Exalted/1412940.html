    Torrential Lead Downpour (Artifact 3 Black Jade Grand Reaper Shellcaster)

    Border skirmishes along the the Linowan and Haltan territories were nothing new, but the vicious and coordinated attacks brought on by the Haltan people were something on a level that the Linowan were not used to. A massive war effort coming from the Haltan jungles had them supported by soldiers equipped with modern day assault rifles, terrifying guerilla wood elementals that lurked in the forces, and potent Exigents from the pole of wood that led the charge. The Haltan Forces pushed past the demilitarized zone and directly into Linowan cities. News reports saw multiple bodies piled on top of one another while others vanished into the night, the Haltan advance making brutal conquest after brutal conquest as they sought to reclaim the land that was once theirs. The Linowan people pleaded for someone, anyone to help their nation in what they considered to be their darkest hour.

    The nation that answered their call was Meruvia, the strongest nation in the world. Their president bolding declaring in a speech to his nation that Meruvia would never tolerate those who use violence and genocide to accomplish their goals. Others who carefully read between the dotted lines claimed that this entire war was a proxy war between the rapidly declining Haslanti Union and Meruvia. However the initial Meruvian troops quickly found out they were poorly suited to deal with the situation. The enemy was forcing the Meruvians to fight on their grounds, in swamps where their vehicles could not reach and over dense jungles where their aircraft could not see. The fledgling United Eastern States (UES) offered the Guardian forces to help and assist Meruvian and Linowan armies, but Meruvia declined their services. The answer was soon made apparent to the baffled UES and Linowan forces.

    Project Codename: “Princes of the Earth” was completed. The first Dragonblooded ever known in Creation made landfall much to the relief and amazement of the beleaguered allied forces. With them came equipped with some of the most bleeding edge weapons and armour the superpower could muster. A water caste Dragonblooded known as Kenneth “Torrent” MacLeod was one such soldier. As a heavy support specialist he brought weapons to the battlefield that most men struggled to even lift. Pinning entire squads under an unceasing avalanche of lead and letting Mal sort them out. His brotherhood led an army through some of the thickest fighting through unrelenting storms in order to take out a storm elemental that was stopping supplies from coming in. Not wanting to stop there, Torrent took the sealed spirit before the Immaculate Faith could receive it and hammered its fury into a weapon. The storms continued for days while he hammered the spirit into the endless depths of the Black Jade at his disposal, locking the spirit inside the weapon forevermore. From that point on the Torrential Lead Downpour brought endless streams of lead down on any opponent unfortunate enough to stand in its way.

    Torrent passed away, sacrificing himself holding out at a hospital to stall for time while it was being evacuated. He is remembered as one of the many heroes made during the war. To this day the Torrential Lead Downpour remains in the Meruvian armoury.

    The Torrential Lead Downpour is a large heavy machine gun nearly the size of a small child, one's eyes playing tricks they they gaze upon its smooth surface as they see drops of rain race across it. A large house shaped box of ammo fits directly in the middle of the gun while a rail sits on top allowing for scopes (Torrent himself never did, he never fought enemies far away enough). The weapon only has a single barrel but the advanced liquid cooling allows it to sustain a high rate of fire for long durations. Normal underbarrel attachments, such as grenade launchers, can be attached to the side of the weapon with some slight modifications.

    Attunement: 6m
    Type: Heavy (18L DMG, OVW 5)
    Accuracy: Close -1; Short +5; Medium +3; Long +1; Extreme −1 (Alternatively, +3 with the Ranged Combat Overhaul.)
    Tags: Archery (Long), Ballistic (Like crossbow tags but for guns), Lethal, Mounted, Strafing, Slow (When only reloading)
    Hearthstone slot(s): 2
    Era: The Linowan War / The Dragons Take Flight (Modern)

    Attunement: Weathering a storm without seeking cover is a poor man’s choice. Anyone the Torrential Lead Downpour attacks out of cover gains an additional point of post soak damage on withering attacks. If the wielder is resonant with jade they instead turn one dice of withering damage into an automatic success.

    This weapon can also fire underwater without any penalties. The weapon however is very heavy, and anyone wishing to wield it requiring a strength of 3 or higher.

    Note: This weapon makes heavy use of the Strafing tag and the Covering Fire gambit. This information is posted below at the end of the document.

    LEAD STORM TECHNIQUE
    Cost​: 2m; Mins​: Essence 1; Type:​ Supplemental
    Keywords​: Decisive-Only
    Duration​: Instant
    Prerequisite charms​: None

    A storm of lead washes over the opponent suddenly and without mercy. This charm enhances a Covering Fire gambit, adding +1 success to hit and counting 10s as double on the gambit roll.

    Special Activation Rules: This evocation is automatically unlocked if the user is resonant with Jade.

    WAVE BATTERING MAYHEM
    Cost​: 6m; Mins​: Essence 2; Type:​ Reflexive
    Keywords​: Dual, Resonant
    Duration​: Instant
    Prerequisite charms​: Lead Storm Technique

    When one stands against the full fury of an ocean’s wave they are immediately battered senseless. This charm may only be activated when the reflexive attack of Covering Fire is triggered, giving (Essence or 3, higher) bonus dice to hit on the attack roll. Against opponents in cover withering damage gains an additional amount of post soak damage equal to the rounds of control left over to a maximum of (Essence or 3, higher) while decisive damage rerolls a similar amount of damage die. Against an opponent outside of cover the wrath is far more severe, the post soak damage die turning into automatic successes and decisive damage gaining double 10s.

    This charm is indeed very strong, but its balanced around the face that it cannot be voluntarily triggered by one’s self, only the pinned opponent can elect to trigger this.

    Resonant: The value for damage rolls is instead (Perception or 3, higher).

    MAELSTROM VORTEX POOL
    Cost​: 8m 1wp; Mins​: Essence 2; Type:​ Simple
    Keywords​: Resonant
    Duration​: Instant
    Prerequisite charms​: Wave Battering Mayhem

    By pulling down the trigger the Torrential Lead Downpour creates a whirling maelstrom of lead that instantly tears apart any impatient fool that decides to brave the storm. Select a target or a single point within the weapon’s range (with an aiming action needed for any point medium and beyond), the wielder may then may make a Covering fire gambit against everyone within close range of that point. This uses one attack roll for each target and the gambit roll only needs to be confirmed once with a difficulty of (3 + 1 for every target beyond the first). The person with the highest (Wits+Dodge) rolls for everyone in the zone to determine rounds of control.

    The rest of the charm plays out like a normal covering fire gambit with the exception of the reflexive automatic attack. If a target within the area provokes the covering fire reflexive attack the wielder may choose to immediately use the full reflexive attack, in which doing so makes the effects of this charm end the beginning of the wielder’s next turn. Alternatively he can choose to make an attack with all the rules of River Rushes Around Rock, even if he doesn’t know the charm. He may make as many of these reduced damage attacks as opponents trigger them for free (EX: They do not cost a turn nor any rounds of control). Making normal attacks still costs one round of control.

    Resonant: At Essence 3+ the wielder may pay an additional 1a surcharge to make this charm target everyone within short range of the target.

    PULLING TIDE ONSLAUGHT
    Cost​: 4m 1wp; Mins​: Essence 3; Type:​ Reflexive
    Keywords​: None
    Duration​: Instant
    Prerequisite charms​: Wave Battering Mayhem

    It only takes a brief moment of carelessness before one is dragged out by the tides and into the depths of the ocean. This charm may be activated upon crashing a target with an attack, allowing you to make an immediate Covering Fire gambit upon them.

    HOLOGRAPHIC RIPPLE TARGETING
    Cost​: -; Mins​: Essence 2; Type:​ Permanent
    Keywords​: None
    Duration​: Permanent
    Prerequisite charms​: Pulling Tide Onslaught, Blazing Bullseye Attack

    One can tell where a stone was dropped by looking at the direction of ripples in a pond. This charm upgrades Blazing Bullseye Attack, allowing you to apply its effects automatically to an enemy under a covering fire gambit and grants (Perception/2) aiming dice instead of the normal amount. As a permanent enhancement to Torrential Lead Downpour, upon attacking a target, the first of your allies to attack that target within the same round gains +1 aim die. This does not expand their normal limit on dice from taking Aim actions beyond the normal +3.

    CEASELESS POUNDING WAVES
    Cost​: 3m 1WP; Mins​: Essence 2; Type:​ Reflexive
    Keywords​: None
    Duration​: Instant
    Prerequisite charms​: Pulling Tide Onslaught, Hunter’s Swift Answer

    Waves in a storm batter an opponent without pause or mercy. Upon triggering a Covering Fire reflexive attack a Solar may pay the cost of this charm to have the attack not count as part of their next action.

    RIVER RUSHES AROUND ROCK
    Cost​: 3m; Mins​: Essence 1; Type:​ Reflexive
    Keywords​: Withering-Only
    Duration​: Instant
    Prerequisite charms​: None

    One cannot avoid getting wet if they’re directly in the path of a river. Water will either flow around an object or expand to meet any area. This charm may only be activated upon missing an opponent with any attack. The character may make a secondary withering attack at their full dicepool against the target with onslaught applied as normal. This withering attack yields no no initiative beyond the 1i gained for hitting a target and can only deal a maximum of (3 or Essence, higher) damage against a target.

    Resonant: The maximum damage is (3 or Perception, higher).

    Special Activation Rules: This evocation is automatically unlocked when the user hits a target with 0 threshold successes.

    OCEAN’S CRUSHING DEPTHS
    Cost​: 2m; Mins​: Essence 2; Type:​ Supplemental
    Keywords​: Resonant, Withering-Only
    Duration​: Instant
    Prerequisite charms​: River Rushes Around Rock

    The crushing depths of the ocean can cause all but the most fortified of armours to buckle and give way. This charm grants the weapon the benefits of the piecing tag without the need to pay initiative or defense. If an attack does more damage than an opponents Stamina (maximum 5) then their soak is lowered by one. Mundane armour is permanently damaged, artifact armour recovers at the end of the scene.

    Resonant: If this charm is used on opponents outside of cover OR triggering a covering fire attack their soak is reduced by one regardless of the damage.

    WAVES CRASH AGAINST THE MOUNTAIN
    Cost​: 3m; Mins​: Essence 2; Type:​ Supplemental
    Keywords​: Resonant, Uniform
    Duration​: Instant
    Prerequisite charms​: Ocean’s Crushing Depths

    Give the ocean enough time and its waves can beat down even the mightiest of mountains. The Torrential Lead Downpour just speeds that up a little. This charm is used on an opponent in cover, making a reflexive feat of Strength to destroy the cover. This is calculated using a pool of (5 + Firearms/Athletics) and is counted as having a strength of 5. If successful heavy cover is immediately degraded one step while light cover is shredded to pieces. This charm cannot work on imperishable objects, but Storytellers are encouraged to assign Feat of Strength rolls that make sense. (EX: If someone is taking cover in a window behind a house, you’re trying to destroy that section of the wall usually not the entire house.)

    Resonant: The Wielder may use (Strength + Firearms/Athletics) if that pool is higher and can use any valid Feat of Strength charm to batter down the wall. Otherwise the Feat of Strength roll gains double 9s.

    LEAD TSUNAMI
    Cost​: 10m 1WP; Mins​: Essence 3; Type: Simple
    Keywords​: Decisive-Only, Resonant
    Duration​: Instant
    Prerequisite charms​: Pulling Tide Onslaught, Waves Crash Against the Mountain

    The Torrential Lead Downpour brings to bear one of the ocean’s most powerful manifestations, a tsunami where which virtually no one escapes unscathed from its wrath. This charm allows the wielder to fire out in a 90 degree arc in a range out to medium, saturating the area in a tidal wave of lead. One attack roll is made against every target and everyone who is hit taking (Initiative/3) decisive damage, adding the wielder’s Strength for anyone not in cover. Battle groups take full damage. All cover within the firing zone is treated as being subjected to a free activation of Waves Crash Against the Mountain (Feel free to reduce it by one automatically if you don’t want to roll). Despite all of this destruction the firezone doesn’t let up, as everyone who takes any action that would trigger a covering fire attack immediately takes a withering attack with the rules of River Rushes Around Rock, this includes people entering the zone.

    This charm is very harsh on ammo reserves. The wielder must reload on the beginning of his next turn. If using ammo tracking, this charm cannot be used if the wielder is at a -3 or greater penalty. This charm can only be used once per scene.

    Resonant: This charm ignores any hardness the opponent may have. Your only hope is dodging the onslaught.


    APPENDIX INFORMATION

    This weapon heavily relies on the Strafing tag and the Covering Fire gambit, both detailed below.

    Strafing - These weapons are capable of firing in fully automatic modes, overwhelming foes in a hailstorm of bullets. When using a weapon with the Strafing tag, users may use automatic fire to saturate their opponent with a hailstorm of bullets, making it nearly incredibly different to block or dodge and when a shot hits, a few more are almost certain to follow in an effort to overload by sheer volume. A Strafing attack fills an area roughly one range band in size with a high volume of inaccurate fire. Strafing attacks are considered decisive, cannot be placed in a flurry, do not target any one specific character, and automatically reset the attacker to base. They create an environmental hazard for any character not under at least light cover within the afflicted area with Damage equal to 1/3rd the attacker's Initiative (round down) and a difficulty to avoid equal to half the successes rolled on the attack. This environmental hazard does not bypass Hardness. Wits+Dodge can be used in place of Stamina+Resistance on the trauma roll against all strafing attacks.

    Gambit

    Covering Fire (Difficulty 3, requires weapon with Strafing tag): This gambit makes use of an automatic fire weapon, pinning it’s victim in a hailstorm of suppressive fire that makes it incredibly dangerous to move away from their current position. The difficulty for this gambit is 3. Successful the shooter rolls (Dexterity+Firearms/Archery) in an opposed roll against the victim’s (Wits+Dodge). If the Defender wins or ties they too elusive to pin, escaping from gunners control on their next action. If the gunner is successful then they pin their victim to that location, excess successes give rounds of control at a rate of one per excess success that determines how long the covering fire lasts similar to grappling. The victim faces a -2 penalty to both his defense and faces a -1 penalty for attacking targets at close range and a -3 penalty for attempting to attack people at range (And any attempts to oppose disengage).

    One can attempt to move attack someone that isn’t the gunner but they face a severe punishment for attempting to do so. The target is subjected to an immediate attack, resolving before their action with an amount of bonus damage equal to the rounds of control that are left to a maximum of the gunner’s Perception rank. This is applied as raw damage for Withering and additional dice for decisive. This ends the Covering Fire gambit and counts as the gunner’s next attack for the turn. A pinned victim may attempt to move from her current spot safely and into another spot with cover by making a (Wits + Dodge) roll at -3 penalty vs the gunner’s (Dexterity/Perception + Firearms). Success means the target safely dives for cover, failure means they cannot proceed as the risk is too great.

    Like grappling, attacking a target you have under covering fire costs a single round of control (A gunner may attack no other targets). Being attacked and getting hit reduces the amount of control rounds you have left over by one. Unlike grappled there is no option to “Slam” a target unless they attempt to move or attack while under the covering fire gambit. Ammo count is a real concern during covering fire, as if a gunner runs out of ammo their gambit immediately ends. Rather than make this a whim of the Storyteller the player can make a (Wits + Firearms) roll with a penalty based on how much they have fired without reloading so far. A single success means they ammo, failure means they have run out or their gun has jammed.

    A gunner CAN attempt to pin at medium or even long range, but they’ll need to take aiming action beforehand (Or the Focus action in the ranged combat rework). Attacks against a pinned target at medium or long range need to be aimed as normal, even if you’re showering an opponent in lead you need to zero in order to get a better shot. If a target provokes the reflexive then there is no need to aim, you can immediately attack.
