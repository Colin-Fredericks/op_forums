                                        Originally posted by Rowanas View Post
                                        Spears. Do they reach, or do I get shunted into my own range band at some point?
                                        I would say that, at the level of abstraction Exalted combat uses, that by default spears reach. However, I'd allow training any creature with the Legendary Size merit with a latent ability that effectively did put you, the rider, at short range, rather than close, with anyone in close range of your creature. That would cost the use of one of its attacks, though - in the tyrant lizard's case, it couldn't use its bite, since it would have to hold its head up out of reach in order to keep you out of reach.

                                            I think I would allow such armour with a very high craft roll. Not easy, but I don't think it's utterly impossible.

                                            I do see Isator's point about mass though. The armour is always the same mass. So I think it might give less protection than regular armour.

                                            Spears... Rules-As-Written, your spear just reaches. And enemies can reach you with fists, knives, etc (at a penalty; Reach weapons obviously ignore the penalty). But personally I feel there ought to be a point at which you're in a different range band. And you'd need like a magic moonsilver extendo-spear to attack at range, or like a red jade spear that shoots fire, or something.*

                                            *Again, I guess you could have a mundane spear that's just really, really, really long. But it's going to be hard to hit anyone with it at that distance, and there's various other disadvantages, like how useless it is against people climbing up your dino. There's a reason long pikes are formation weapons.

                                                    The rules for mounted fighting go into this somewhat

                                                    Enormous mounts such as mammoths, yeddim,
                                                    or tyrant lizards prevent their rider from being attacked
                                                    at all by short-range weapons not possessing the reaching
                                                    tag, unless opponents first use a miscellaneous (Dexterity
                                                    + Athletics, difficulty 3) action to climb aboard the huge
                                                    mount (this counts as the character’s movement for the
                                                    round). However, riders atop such mounts similarly cannot
                                                    attack opponents on the ground with short-range weapons
                                                    which lack the reaching tag.
