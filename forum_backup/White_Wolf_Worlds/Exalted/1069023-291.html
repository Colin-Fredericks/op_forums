                                    A proud and unbeatable Solar Swordsman chases the Lernaean Hydra from its swampy domain to claim the manse it guarded. In his celebrations later, he lays in bed with a rather sinuous local woman.

                                    When the Hydra returns to reclaim its home, it knows exactly where he is, it knows his tricks, and its maddening hisses and roars shake him deeper within his Essence than they ever could before. As if, on some level, it now owns him.


                                    A very important prince travels with his honor guard from a tributary city back to his father's capital. His caravan comes across a merchant selling food whose smell tempts the prince to stop and eat.

                                    The prince demands the food as tribute, but the merchant begs for a contest. The merchant happens to have on hand a board game that happens to be a favorite of the prince's court, and the prince's in particular. Let the prince eat his fill first, and then amuse himself with a game. If the prince bests the lowly merchant at his favorite courtly game, the merchant will prepare him more food for the journey home, but if the merchant manages to win, the prince will pay the price of the meal.

                                    Enticed by the chance to enjoy an easy victory and prize, the prince agrees. With a full belly to soften his mood, and a lively, charming conversation with the merchant, the prince enjoys himself so much that he doesn't even mind just barely losing the game. He gladly pays the price of the meal and sets out again for home.

                                    The Lunar waits until the prince is long gone before assuming his face and returning to the tributary city. The capital would have responded quickly if the prince had failed to arrive as scheduled, but now it would take weeks for the capital to learn something was amiss in the borderlands of their kingdom.
