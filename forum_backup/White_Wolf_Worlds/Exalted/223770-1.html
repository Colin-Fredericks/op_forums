    Zippi, the Tailors of Sighs.

    Zippi, the Tailors of Sighs.
    Demons of the First Circle, Progeny of the Lake of Doubt The Lake of Sighs is a living kingdom of somnolence amongst the Pantheon of the Yozi Yestoc the Traveler above the Horizon. From the beaches of Lake of Doubt occasionally fish like shapes will rise and walk on land and then take the form of one of the Tailors of Sighs. Each Zippi takes the form of a lanky seven foot tall humanoid each quaffed with a stylized pompadour of cottony crystalline sugar of any coloration. They are of any gender, and each has large black ovals for eyes which appear to be large fish eggs from which they hatched in the Lake of Doubt. Each has high cheek bones, and little is it recognized in the Age of Sorrows but each Zippi possess the voice of a specific 1st Age Singing Idol found on IAM. Their flesh appears like warm light violet marble with a deeper violet blush.

    Despite their height the Tailors of Sighs are viewed as amongst the most effete of demons. Though their reputation as dandies works to their advantage as they are greatly sought after as superb tailors, and due to the nature of their required ingredients they have become top notch gigolos and courtesans. For a Zippi has utmost integrity for what they will fashion into cloth so they take to working producing sighs of utter bliss from their courtesan duties, as well as sighs of utter relief from workers of Creation given amnesty from execution, to the cherished sighs of fond memories from old doddering dynasts. Zippi cannot stand to see nudity due to poverty, for they believe only in purposeful exposure of flesh, and thus have made it their duty to cloth Creation and the Demon City alike. All this however starts with the Zippi pioneering a new style out of their first sigh of relief after leaving the Lake of doubt with which to make their own nudity a thing of the past.
    Zippi will work as a prostitute and a tailor probono for the folk of the Demon City and Creation but they find they can achieve greater heights if they accumulate employment and resources. Summoners have found that these are amongst the more amiable demons to bid the presence of with thaumaturgy for they have a strong sense of duty to garb those who need garbing and to relax the populace. The clothes and fabrics they create are often exceptional if not outright perfect in quality, and even in the Blessed Isle it is common for a Dynast to receive a cape or curtains expertly tailored just for their sensibilities by a demon summoned months ago to pleasure them at a soirée. Each Zippi possess a great pare of perfect silver shears with which to aid them in their works, but which they also put to good use upon individuals whom shirk the gift of clothing to lounge naked without the excuse of coitus. These victims are often found later lounging cold in a pool of their own blood having been snipped of their thumbs, if not other protrusions. Each Zippi will accumulate a point of limit for witnessing complete nudity with no signs of sexual congress, limit of one point a day. They also need to roll temperance to keep from assaulting the offenders with scissors. These Demons have been known to be commissioned to create simple Artifacts like Privacy Veils, Coats such as a Fur Merchant’s Gift, and Silken Armor. They have been known to torture or frighten individuals just for sighs sighs of relief. And they will often spend time in the dungeons or torture chambers of Malfeas just to collect the sighs of the relief of death.



    Summoning: (Obscurity 2/3)



    When a maiden weeps in the lambent light of a gibbous moon for a ruined or lacking dress a Tailor of Sighs may appear to craft for her a gown of exquisite beauty from the sigh of her relief.

    Once a year upon any of the nights of Calibration a Tailor of Sighs feels a unique emotion that if they desire will drive them to the nearest body of liquid, whether water or Kimbery, upon which they let out an inscrutable sigh which they cut on the spot forming a pillow unique to the occasion. Upon this pillow they remove one of their eyes, and two thumbs (each from a different being) and sew it into the Pillows seam. They place the pillow into the liquid and upon the first day of the New Year a new tailor is hatched. The parent may regrow their eye if they find the birth pillow and make an eye patch from it and wear it for at least one season, removing it upon the next Calibration revealing a new eye.

        Jaemi, the Harvesters Under the Moon.
        Demons of the First Circle, Progeny of the Ivory Idol of Salt and Flame The Frowning Idol of Salt and Flames created the first Harvester Under the Moon by incubating the blood of a sacrifice in a beast of Burden for a month. The method is now used by the Jaemi themselves who may produce new Harvesters by creating a pill of their own blood and using it to inseminate a beast of burden which will swell with a new Jaemi for one month and then burst open spawning a full grown Harvester.

        The Jaemi are often three or more yards tall with the head of an oxen and the snout of a boar complete with a brass ring etched with characters for Labor in Rocktongue. Their bodies are hunched lean and powerful with calluses and scars from a lifetime of labor and beige fur mixed with jet black thick hairs. Their arms are like a mix between badgers and an ape’s. with three brass clawed fingers and a thumb. They often garb themselves in overalls and they wear a wide brimmed hat made of silver or brass straw to keep the harsh shining of Liger from their eyes. Their horns are brass and thickly grooved.

        The Jaemi were made to farm the brass and stone rubble of the Demon City. They have taken to both keeping a farm by themselves, or taking a mate and farming together, or even living in a demonic commune with each demon taking their role. The Jaemi have varied labors that they expect of themselves, and a single Harvester may farm Brass bread, heard Hell Cattle, collect silver and brass timber, and mine the jeweled bowels of the bones of Malfeas all in the same season. Though they find certain labors take priority in certain seasons, all for the sake of diversity, and they an irrepressible urge to only harvest each crop for one season a year. So in the Season of Fire they may harvest Brassbread for the Pastry Smiths at market, and then in the Season of Wood they may plant Bonecorn for the great milltowers to grind for use in tortillas.

        The Jaemi are masters at husbanding animals in hell. They often take to breeding with their cattle to create bloodlines of Hellbeast for superior meat, labor, and milk. They see it as their duty to do this ever few generations, and even when summoned into Creation a Sorcerer must take great pains to not have their Harvester breed with local livestock. This is particularly difficult during full moons, when the Jaemi are filled with the urge to “labor” and tend their animals till the sun rises. Much crops may be harvested, and bastards sown during these events.

        The Jaemi are rough backbones of Demon culture, they reap the fields to bring food to the markets of the Demon City, and cut lumber and quarry stone for the Hopping Puppeteers to rearrange into tenements, and they provide mined jewels for the Heranhals to make into brooches and rings. They collect Hellcattle milk to made into cheese and wine, and they fish for Kimbery spawn to feed the slaves of their betters. They provide shelter for Neomah to work their craft, and housing for Anuhles so they may harvest their silk, and food for Firmin so they may collect fashion tools for them. The Jaemi support the Demons of Hell so that they may continue their lives of toil and torture, for that is their nature. Most sacred to them is the Blood Red Moon of the Demon City which they worship as a Goddess of the Harvest. Sometimes their Patron even rewards the greatest of them with sacred tools of Orichalcum, Moonsilver, and even Starmetal tainted with Vitriol.
        Summoners are drawn to summoning the Jaemi to act as a diverse labor force for a multitude of projects whether it is a Lumber worker, harsh mining, or simple field work, animal sheparding, and even cooking; the Harvesters are familiar with them all and they are excellent brutes and thugs. They make excellent support for any number of other demon slaves.

            Vlectics, the Mother Vines.

            Vlectics, the Mother Vines.
            Demons of the First Circle, Progeny of All Hell.
            Vlectics, the Mother Vines
            Much like the Anuhles the Mother Vines are less a specific species of Demon but are a common recurring archetype created by various demons throughout the Demon City. They serve the purpose of birthing broods of other demons to help proliferate the desirable populations for the various lords of Malfeas. Even the Yozis themselves will spawn the Mother Vines populate newly unfurled landscapes of their World Bodies.

            The Vlectics are created as seeds and placed into the ground and fed chalcanth which catalyzes their growth from a seed into an actual First Circle Demon. Each type of demon refined into chalcanth and fed to the growing seed modulates the fully grown Vlectic’s capabilities allowing them to birth these demons after collecting enough essence and gestating them in seedeggs. Some Mother Vines are fed only one draught of halcanth as a seed resulting in a fully grown Mother Vine from the spot after a year has passed. If fed another dose of chalcanth the Vlectic will take a season to mature, and another dose will cut this time to only a month, with each additional dose cutting the maturation time in half.

            Full grown Vlectics appear as a large knot of brass vines ranging from the size of a childs arm to that of the length and thickness of an Anaconda. Thicker older limbs have a mixture of scale like brass bark, and cutting them will cause them to bleed sweet smelling ichor that has potent alcoholic properties affecting those who consume it like two doses of strong liquor per dose. Their central mass appears much like a mixture between a pitcher plant, a Venus-flytrap and a clam. Appearing on the outside as tarnished brass with a green patina, and the inside of the mouth appears like a pile of meat from various demons and animals, preserved in various states ranging from fresh bloody kills, to rotten and maggot ridden. Foolish demons looking for an easy meal are lured in by the scent and the sweet music that drifts about, and the mouth finally snaps shut, grappling the demon and melting its essence with vitriol to be converted into nourishment for the eggfruit which ripens on the vine limps. The vine limbs often spread into the very ground, or wrap around any demon they can find to leech essence from, petrifying their corpse into brasswood lumps. Some limbs climb up or through the various buildings of the Demon City in their expansion of territory and search for nourishment. The central knot is the major consumer of the Demon’s anatomy with the limbs manly working as structure, sensory organs, auxillary feeding mouths, and most importantly as budding stages for the growth of egg fruit which hatch into demons they are programed to spawn.

            Each eggfruit grows from a tiny cherry sized greyish bulb to a bright scarlet corrugated pumpkin-like shape of the size necessary to hatch the particular demon. It normally takes a month from conception of the eggfruit for it to hatch. 10 motes are committed for each level of Essence the newborn of the Demon’s type typically possesses and a willpower point is also needed to initiate the grow. Everyday each eggfruit needs 3 motes of essence spent to feed it, which ideally will come from a grappled and petrified demon. During this month of growth mutations may occur if beings with and essence of 5+ are fed into the eggfruit, or if other specifically tamper with it to instigate mutation in the newborn. If the Vlectic is attuned to a manse or demesnes that is not aspected to Malfeas or Vitriol mutations often occur in the newborn if explainable by the foreign essence. For instance a Bloodape hatched from a Vlectic attuned to a Kimbery demesnes may have amphibious mutations, or a Neomah fed by a Solar Manse in Creation may be born with the extra limbs (arms) mutation in emulation of the Unconquered Sun. If the Vlectic has access to hearthstones or cults such essence respired from these sources may mutate some of their eggfruit as well, or alter the standard traits of the demons born from their essence, such as changing languages based on the prayers of the cult, and specialties tied to the cult or manses.

            The Mother Vines are amongst the oldest of the 1st Circle Demons, and they are very primal expressions of the urges of Hell to devour, grow, and spawn.

For this reason it is viewed as bad luck to bury women with brass in many places throughout creation, including such things as pets that died in childbirth buried with a brass bell.

            Sorcerers primarily summon Mother Vines as a method to produce other demons in Creation, and some rather enjoy growing one within their layer just simple for the sheer intimidation factor of having a man-eating demon factory in their dungeons. They make exquisite security systems. Wise sorcerers know not to summon more than one Mother Vine, because if one were ever to eat another they would gain the ability to spawn eggfruit containing other Mother Vines and may over run their domain with a demon plague beyond their capabilities to contain. Mother Vines gain one limit every day they do not have at least one victim petrified upon their vines. They also treat releasing any captured prey as an unacceptable order.

                Jyeontelleaveau, the Screaming Man Parade.
                Demons of the First Circle, Progeny of the Grinning Murdernaut Mydwryths, ghosts, and especially humans are long sought after, but often rare and protected prey. A prey the Grinning Murdernaut weeps melancholy just imagining his demon brethren never get to realize the joy of slaughtering such a child of the earth, even while he slaughters the same demon brethren he weeps for. When his tears mix with their gore and viscera out will sprouts the Screaming Man Parade. And they have spread throughout the Demon City with the very desperate desire the citizens of Malfeas have to duplicate the experience of a human victim, or consort or slave.
                The Jyeontelleaveau have the same range in height as a human ranging from pygmy height of barely under a yard off the ground to looming height of nearly three times that, but while humanity has stabilized their height to a more medium range that varies in populations, the Jyeontelleaveau often vary randomly with each generation. Each has a thick elephant like skin which ranges from pinkish grey hue of a hippo, to a pale corpse white, to a dull greyish blue of a frostbite sufferer near death. They have simple features with four rough digits and no noticeable articulation for wrists or hips. Their feet are simple and stump like without toes, and they only possess a single eye on their thumb like heads. A deep grove follows from the deep eye socket much like the shape of a thunderbolt or root all the way down to their pelvis. Roughly where their bellybutton would be they possess another larger eye. This fissure pulsates whenever they speak. They do not start with any method to chew food so they pour slurry into an opening along this fissure. Both eyes are large and oddly colored and appear more like the eye from a whale or squid.
                The Scream Man Parade are born believing themselves to be humans. Their knowledge however of the human condition is grossly inaccurate. For instance they desire to consume any organic substance from Creation believing everything from bear droppings to owl bones must be human foods and a delicacy somewhere in Creation. In Malfeas they take any rumors and news then can get modeling their looks after what they believe humans would wear and style themselves after. If they find humans they intensely ape their habits with a stern eye. They will start to form their features into the rough features of humans they witness, and when they can they will perform amateur surgery to remove human organs and insert it into themselves, they replace it with anything nearby at hand, whether a baby’s rattle, or a live mouse. Their mistaken belief is that more organs equal more humanity. The Screaming Man Parade with greater amount of organs are given the greater respect, and can often imitate human form and speech with greater accuracy and precision then their fellows. However they appear morbidly obese as they are literally stuffed with stolen organs, and any close physical examination (Perception + Medicine roll difficulty 1) may reveal medical oddities such as additional hearts or stomachs in the wrong area. They may only steal one organ from a human, and the theft is never fatal as long as they find any replacement. Though expect reduced traits and or deficiencies if for instance they replace a womans left hemisphere of the brain with a small wooden horse.
                Tradition has it that humans breed with other humans. So the most organ heavy Jyeontelleaveau will find one or more others heavy in organs. They will then each donate at least one stolen organ, with one member also donating enough skin to contain it, and the rest filling the sack of skin and organs with blood. They all may add essence, and at least one must spend a willpower until they have collected 20 motes and 1 Willpower point, and a new Screaming Man Parade is born. Those parents wealthy in organs may produce multiple children in one go. They believe this is how humans are made, totally missing the details of how families work besides the fact that they are congregations of humans. It is also worth noting the Jyeontelleaveau are hermaphroditic.
                The Screaming Man Parade make excellent apprentices and sorcerers often summon them with this in mind. They will start with very general knowledge of humans, but if exposed to what they believe are humans they will often latch onto a perceived template that is given to them, and thus apprenticeship is a natural calling for those who want to shape their own gullible apprentice. They often take information about humanity given to them as truths, with contradictions easily confusing them. They will dress as they believe their roll calls for. In Hell they make natural slaves and victims of violence or hunger as that is their first notion on what being a human is all about. They have an irresistible urge to steal organs from a human if they are left alone with one who is either sleeping, or injured with an open wound, they cannot be commanded not to do this and will treat such orders as unacceptable unless the their masters are in the same room as them. Proving to the Screaming Man Parade that they are not human will force them to roll their Willpower, and receive a point of limit for each success.

                                Cik, the Frantic Chefs of the Fallen.
                                Demons of the First Circle, Progeny of the Horned Oak War is the pastime of the Demon City, and afterwards the landscape is littered with the carnage of the fallen. Many are the demons who have no desire to let the corpses go to the Demon Winds, and hunger for their fallen brethren to satisfy and affirm their own merciful continued existence. Deblodiah the Horned Oak is taken aback by notion that the Demon Winds absconding with any of the resources of the Horned Oak’s Dominion, so the Horned Oak’s sulfurous seeds were planted and the Frantic Chefs of the Fallen were set force to prepare feasts of the fallen so that the survivors may no longer envy the dead and reap succulent repast from their remains! Their popularity in their home dominion resulted in them being smuggled and planted en mass throughout the Demon City by covetous lords.

                                The Cik have sharp angular features, and beet colored skin. They have thin limbs with flesh stretched taut against oddly jointed arms and legs. Their noses are like caricatures of the longest of human noses, taking up over half of their heads. They have pronounced hunches from constantly bending over to prepare their meals. They have four fingered hands, with each finger sharp like scalpels, to aid in cutting the meat from the bone, but possessing no nails the edge originates from the finger bones’ edges emerging from the skin. Their bandy legs end in feet thinner than any birds; they appear as though they are standing up flexible pitchforks. Their cuspate boned ears take up most of the remainder of their head, with beady little eyes with the square pupils of a goat but with unnatural coloring unique to each Cik. Their miniscule mouths are filled with three rows of black aciculate teeth, and they have a prominent overbite due to having a diminutive jaw. They possess a black forked tongue, with keen taste buds, that flicker to taste the dead to make sure they are properly deceased enough to be superb ingredients. Their ten jointed tails are skinless, appearing only as spiny brass vertebrae and copper cartilage, ending in a stone arrow shaped tip. They are less than a man in length, and their tails are one and one half the length of the rest of the body. Their only clothes are usually leather aprons they are awarded as gifts of appreciation, or as uniforms of employment.

                                Like carrion birds after massacre, the Frantic Chefs descend upon the plains of the dead in hordes, each singing in tune with their brethren as they cut and slice the dead and prepare flames for roasting and platters for display. Their youngest work to disperse the spices and prepare the serving mats, but the rest ceaselessly toils to make each feast grand. As soon as they ready one meal, the gathering assemblies of locals agree to first come first serve as law, they move to the next. They derive nourishment from the hunger of their guests, just as much as the fullness of the glutted.

                                Cik spawn after their own deaths, which are served just like any other corpse. The entity that devours the largest portion of a Cik’s tail incubates their young in their bellies, and eventually the tail grows into a beanstalk of copper and hair which usually kills the host demon/life form. The pods which grow upon the stalk are withered and within ten days of the tail being eaten the pods hatch. Normally two pods are the default for the stalk, but if the host died within the essence rich boundaries of a manse, or a pool of Vitriol, then five pods may form.
                                Cik gain limit everyday they cannot prepare a meal from the dead. They also receive one limit each scene they witness a demon being processed into chalcanth. They instinctually hate the chalcanth trade due to it removing corpses from which they may prepare a meal. If there is one thing the Frantic Chefs hate more than chalcanth, it is their own deep satisfaction upon imbibing chalcanth. Upon drinking Chalcanth the Frantic Chefs regain 5 Willpower, even raising it past their maximums, but at the end of the scene they also receive five limit points. Sorcerers often summon the Cik to serve as recyclers after their illness has struck a whole herd of cattle dead, as they are able to sanitize the dead as part of their preparation process. Some less scrupulous first Age sorcerers would be known to summon the Cik after famine wiped out half their kingdoms population, or after a massacre of their enemies.
                                An interesting discovery made by the Cik is that upon seeing a ghost they have a distinct urge to shred and prepare them for consumption. Cik have actually formed raiding parties into the Underworld via shadowlands, in order to make their denizens into meals either for the locals of the shadowlands or for accompanying bands of ravenous demons. This tradition has formed into an organization all its own with members from numerous demon races, including a sizeable Blood Ape and population to devour during the raids, Firmin to skewer the meals for transport, and Jaemi to transport the rest to markets for sale. They are known as the Bones into Bread Tradition.

                                    Havatie, the Drums of Fear.
                                    Demons of the First Circle, Progeny of the Obstinate Malikah of the Gilded Frown Havatie, the Drums of Fear, progeny of the Obstinate Malikah of the Gilded Frown
                                    Born from the seeds Vriggan spit into the stool of Melon Scourge, the Drums of Fear are grown on thick yellow green stalks. They ripen on the vine as melon colored eggs which crack after precisely three seasons of growth. By the time they hatch the eggs are the size of a Cherak Clydesdale, and they have been drained of their color. From their melon shells emerge horned beetles the size of a large horse which march forth hungry for raucous. They may appear as gigantic versions of any number of horned beetles, each with a lustrous carapace with wither stripes or spots of rich verde and ebony. After another season their first carapace molts off and they often grow into the size of a bull rhino, but with thick growths on either side. Their backs form grooves befitting of a natural saddle, and just above their side growths appear a fourth set of limbs. These limbs harshly strike the growths in rhythm with their marching.

                                    The Havatie often gather in herds as large as they can manage. Their drumming has unnatural echoing qualities around Brass, often causing the bones of Malfeas to hum and whole buildings to vibrate. When they are in herds, or alone they have an instinct to drum while marching into populated areas, either crowded hamlets of Malfeas and Creation, or confronting rival herds of Rhino and Zebra in creation. Their drumming stirs fear in those who hear, unless they also are riding a Havati themselves, or are also playing an instrument. The effects are amplified when surrounded by other forms of mobile life such as when in cities, or around densely populated ecosystems. The Havatie feed off of the fear and panic, respiring essence from it, and if they vacate an area completely they may stay for a time to nibble off of the grass and the dirt. During these times the Havatie will rut and form into threes. When they form into threes during these times they will first challenge each other with drum beats. These challenges are acoustic versions of the popular Shogunate game Earth, Wood, Fire, with the player choosing Earth losing to the player choosing Wood, but winning if the other player chooses Fire. After a series of rounds the losing Havatie will burrow into the ground and the two winners will copulate fiercely, to the point of cracking their shells and bleeding ichor upon the ground. Eventually the herd continues its endless migration, and three seasons later stalks with melon eggs (one for each Essence Dot of the buried parent) containing the next generation of the Drums of Fear will grow from the spot of the gravewomb and form into one or more herds themselves. Vriggan grows vast herds of the Drums of Fear in her Ranch of the Horned, and she delights in spreading their herds whenever she camps for the night with Melon Scourge.

                                    The Drums of Fear are popular mounts cattle in Malfeas. Since they double as transportation/beasts of burden as well as noisemakers to repel the Silent Wind they find easy employment in the legions and communities of Malfeas, and can be found in locals such as the Silver Forest, the Shores of Kimbery, and even within the carnivorous Swamps and Sargassos of Metagos. Havatie who have obtained employment have a habit of collecting any sort of jewelry, accessories, or baubles to hang around their drums and saddles, the gaudier the better. A popular drink sold throughout Malfeas comes from the bloodmilk of the Havatie, which naturally cools as it ferments and is sold as melon smoothies in stands and taverns of the Demon City. Each cup this drink works as a dose of hard liquor but with the side effect of negating a meals worth of nutrition, but does nothing to effect hydration. This was popular in High First Age as an option over the vomitoriums, where wealthy patrons would drink this cool spirit to make room in their stomach for more feasting. As famine became more common in Creation after the Usurpation most civilizations have forgotten this rare import, however some indulgent outcastes in the Far South still partake of this treat during feast days. Some degenerate Dynasts have discovered this drink and have a laugh when they offer it to starving thirsty peasant children only to see their joyous faces turn to horror and then fears as they starve at the Dynasts feet. More than one slave has sold his own freedom just for a loaf of bread after being offered one of these spirits, just so they wouldn’t painfully starve to death on the spot. Canny Guildsmen have taken to using thaumaturgy to harvest this milk for use as a dietary supplement for overweight city folk.

                                        Bromwheelers, the Catapult Turtles.

                                        Demons of the First Circle, Progeny of the Prolific Engineer of the Transmundane.

                                        These massive creations of the Prolific Engineer of the Transmundane are often mistaken for Behemoths by those who first encounter them. They resemble stout turtles of cyclopean stature. They possess shells the size of large wagons and each possesses a siege weapon upon or within its shell. The Prolific Engineer of the Transmundane hammers their shells and skeletons from the vitriol-fused bones of the fallen demons that litter the Demon City’s rubble, along with an undershelling of brass-bamboo varnishing. Due to their dense structure and their pressurized fluid-filled organs, Bromwheelers each weigh well over 5 tons and several of them have the large mutation or a greater variant of it. Their necks are wrinkled to accommodate expansion, and their heads are like a combination of snake and turtle heads with ears resembling those of a coyote. Their eyes are hemispheres of silver liquid hovering within their deep eye sockets. Their clawed feet seem to possess the qualities of a strong magnet and can tightly grip metals for bracing. Their scales appear as citrine colored brass, their underbellies are shine like haematite, and the tops of their shells are grooved with obsidian and plated with stone. The type of stone varies from Bromwheeler to Bromwheeler, with some having shells covered with polished marble and others having shells covered with lapis lazuli mixed with grey slate.

                                        Catapult Turtles are perhaps the most successful export from the territories of the Prolific Engineer of the Transmundane. They first spread throughout the Demon City as living siege engines that Warlords such as the Living Tower bartered ample resources for in order to sample populations to breed in their domains. Due to their success rival Warlords throughout the layers of Hell rustled and looted enough of the Catapult Turtles that after the Bromwheelers had been on the Markets for 108 years their progenitor no longer had a monopoly on them. Mother Vines and Neomah were all that was needed to bolster their swelling numbers. Recently their inventor Geezeirbo received the blessing of the Trout that Swims Through Time as the Unquestionable reanimated the bones of the first and largest Catapult Turtle, the gargantuan Proterrapin. With his living siege fortress operational for the first time since the Low First Age Geezeirbo has taken to bolstering the numbers of the Catapult Turtles beyond all precedent, in preparation for what he says is a turning of the ages that will put the Usurpation to shame.

                                        A Catapult Turtle may fire or launch anything it can lift at least 500 yards out of its shellborne apparatus, and some can manage a distance of miles with proper calculation. They still have the same limitations on firing as a normal siege engine, although they normally have about 45 degrees of tilt without having to move their entire forms. They have excellent aim, and enjoy hurling as a pastime when alone. When in groups they can be playfully competitive. Their tendency to fire without foresight is a significant problem for their masters, as Sorcerers need make sure that they summon Bromwheelers only into regions that they don’t mind having demolished the moment they leave the Catapult Turtles to themselves. Every period between the sun setting or rising in which a Catapult Turtle doesn’t get to use its siege engine forces the Bromwheeler's summoner to roll a die and to add the results plus one to their limit. When left to their own devices within the confines of the Demon City Catapult Turtles naturally attempt to socialize with each other by seeing who can project something closest to Ligier. This game involves hurling ammo or stray demons sunwards, and those who have their shots blasted out of the sky by the Green Sun or some other mighty being are treated as champions among their communities. However most come short usually resulting in a great deal of property damage throughout the surrounding area as boulders, shrapnel, and pedestrians rain down upon the wretched populace of Hell’s City.

                                        Catapult Turtles have a second limiting factor and that is their prodigious capacity for consumption. Catapult Turtles require their weight in meat or vegetation (Brass Bamboo works just as well as bark from an oak) as well as at least one ton of any liquid (hydraulics are an essential part of their musculature every day to support their siege engine metabolism, and they will gain 1 point of limit each day if not sufficiently fed. They will also loose the use of their siege weapons until nourished. Curiously, the pungent liquids that Bromwheelers disgorge through their mouths are perhaps the closest thing to potable water a human is likely to find in the sprawls of the Demon City. Some mortals in Malfeas have learned to convince Catapult Turtles to spit into basins, thereby filling the basins in question with water that can be boiled into potability.

                                            Ebidreul the Sky Drumming Imps.

                                            Ebidreul the Sky Drumming Imps.
                                            Demons of the First Circle, Progeny of the Effulgent Conductor Born from the phosphorescent blood of the Effulgent Conductor which pelts the ground during his frantic works zipping across the Demon City, the Sky Drumming Imps emerge from cracks in the streets of the Demon City ready to make their music but without a drum to play. The first thing a Sky Drumming Imp does is find something sharp to slowly flay the coarse pale skin from their newborn hides. When they are finished removing their skin in one clean piece they work it into leather with which they fashion into an elegant drum. These drums are their source of pride and their method of survival. They play these skin drums with a pair of brass pummel sticks which they wrap the tips of with the remaining bits of their drum leather.

                                            The Sky Drumming Imps appear as short ugly goblin like creatures with bandy legs. Many of them are potbellied and flabby though some are emaciated with taut muscles. After they removed their birth skins their hides are slick and with tones ranging from pink to brown and grey. They have large wide set eyes with two simple nostrils between their eye sockets. Their eyes appear as shiny translucent orbs filled with deep scarlet blood. Their heights range from the smallest being barely over two feet with their tallest members a head shorter than a grown man. Their massive ears are often heavily pierced for decorations to indicate employment or affiliations with different bands.


                                            The Ebidreul have poor eyesight and don’t distinguish colors. Most only recognize movement; instead they rely off of their excellent hearing with their heavy drumbeats providing something similar to echolocation when received by their massive ears. The bombastic music which they constantly drum has a unique resonance with the clouds in the sky and their drumbeats tug down a nimbus like a hungry fisherman catching a carp. A single Ebidreul can form a simple fog over the ground enough to obscure the inside of a barn or an entity as large as a yeddim, with two or more they can drum enough clouds to cover an entire farm. The clouds they summon have a unique echoing quality which not only enhances the Ebidreul’s senses but also reflect and refract Anima’s and other strong lights. The Ebidreul use this field of blindness to take advantage of their foes which they frantically beat with their pummel stick, and a band of Ebidreul can drum themselves into a frenzy clubbing their confused foes as they caper between each other, and then feeding upon their fallen forms eating their fill and lapping their blood and fluids from the dirt.
