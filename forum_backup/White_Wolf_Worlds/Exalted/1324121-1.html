                            Originally posted by Sticks View Post

                            When does your character outgrow their pre-exaltation sense of self and embrace their new role?
                            I guess the question is... do they have a new role? Because it's quite likely that what they wanted to do when they were a mortal is also what they want to do now; the difference is just that they have the power.

                            If it's not (maybe they're pulled by responsibility, or other Exalts try and convince them to do it), well, that's the plot of many ancient myths and modern Superhero films. When does Spiderman realise he can't just have a normal high school life and has to be a hero? It's never something he gets entirely comfortable with, that's a tension running all the way through. Odysseus never accepts his role; he's just forced to abandon his family and fight a stupid war, and then pisses off the gods and is cursed to take a decade to return home. But he never actually wants to be an epic hero.
                            And then there's plenty of heroes who get into it pretty quickly; but even they occasionally have the tension.



                            Our PCs still go to the pub/teahouse, try and get married, fall in love with the wrong people, and also are Essence 5 Solars and Lunars.

                            Actually, the 300xp Night Caste who grew up on the streets and now is the brother-in-law of the Pharoah still hasn't really accepted his role of Prince; it involves things he finds traumatic (like beating his other brother-in-law to death because he was trying to overthrow the Pharoah with an army of the dead), so he's currently pretending to be an ordinary human martial artist and staying far away from the palace.

                            It all depends on the character and circumstance, really.

Your powers are your own, formed from your abilities and skills. You're uplifted, and while there's still a human at your core, behind the wheel, you are different afterwards than before. Hence Second Breath. You aren't a person with an Exaltation, you are Exalted. That doesn't mean 'you're not human', but it means that the abilities and skill of a Chosen are very firmly inherent. Whatever you feel in that moment of change is your reaction to it.

                                        Essence Fever, as I read it, doesn't contradict that idea but undercuts it. The Essence is inside you! It wants to be used! It's making you feel excited.
