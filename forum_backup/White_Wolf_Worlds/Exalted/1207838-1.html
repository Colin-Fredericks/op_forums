                                Originally posted by Greyman View Post
                                Lunar: You are the Solar's pet monster who later learned to take human form. Shut up. Yes you are.
                                You broke pattern here by describing a secondary rather than main character.

                                Therefore I presume you haven’t read Re:Monster, which is a buffet of these trashy tropes. It’s a reincarnation isekai where a magically-genetically engineered super-soldier mercenary from earth is murdered in the street by a yandere stalker.

                                He’s reborn into a stereotypical Japanese DnD fantasy world as a rare black goblin born of one woman of several kept captive for the purpose by a band of goblins. He will go on to be a rapist himself when he’s powerful enough to conquer kingdoms and drug resisting women.

                                In order to get that far, he will use the skills he brought with him from his previous life, but especially the magical-genetic power he also had: eating corpses to gain their biological strengths. Which in this world includes magical talent.

                                It’s a garbage story, and very boring.

                                A much better story is Spirit Migration. It’s set in a similar world, but the protagonist might not be from another world. He has no memories at all, and is a disembodied spirit which can possess natural and magical beasts. Whenever confronted by a stronger beast, he simply jumps ship and (when necessary) kills the previous weaker one.

                                Instead of a meandering and pointless story about acquiring personal power, wealth, and sex slaves, Spirit Migration is about the mortal people living, dying, and scheming in the world, and how the Migrating Spirit tries to help them as an outsider predator of great power and compassion.

                                At one point, the hero leaves the dungeon entirely to help a little girl solve a family mystery in the bodies of her pet bird and dog.
