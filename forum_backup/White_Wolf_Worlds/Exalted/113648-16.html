            Presence:

            Whisper of the Binding Tie
            The Solar may activate this charm when competing against another character to influence someone. The Solar calls upon one of the character's ties to argue against her competition and creates a personal connection to the topic. The strength of the chosen tie increases by one rank. If the tie is already defining, the character does not need to spend willpower to use that tie to reject social influence.

                                    Bureaucracy:

                                    Balancing the Scales
                                    The Solar may activate this charm when offering to broker a deal between two organizations which she does not own. Barring other intimacies, the two organizations gain a minor tie of trust towards the Solar while she is negotiating the deal. The Solar automatically gains a number of automatic successes equal to her Essence rating to all relevant rolls to equally maximize the deal's benefits for both organizations. Once the deal is accepted, the Solar may choose to weaken any single negative intimacy each organization possesses towards the other, increase the strength of any such positive intimacy or create a minor tie of mutual trust between the two organizations.

                                        Performance:

                                        Stage within a Stage Technique
                                        The Solar chooses a signal to communicate with her companions which she can slip into one of her performances, such as a wrong step or an off-key note. The Solar may activate this charm to hide the signal from the rest of the audience; those who do notice it will dismiss it as an innocent mistake and think no more of it. At a higher Essence rating, the Solar no longer needs to determine the signal ahead of time and may use this charm to communicate simple ideas to any member of her audience she chooses.

                                                Medicine:

                                                Payment for Salvation
                                                The Solar knows how to leverage the value of her skills. When the Solar offers her services to someone who is likely to need medical assistance in the future, they automatically gain a major tie towards agreeing to the Solar's demands. If the target of this charm is currently suffering from disease, poison or wounds, they gain a defining tie instead.
