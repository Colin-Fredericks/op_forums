                                08-17-2017, 09:53 PM
                                Originally posted by ale137 View Post
                                What should be the difference between Allies and Retainer? There is an overlap between the merits.
                                Allies are more powerful than Retainers, but also more independent, expecting you to repay them for what they do for you. Retainers are less powerful, but are personally loyal to you without requiring any other compensation.

                                Is there a reason to pick combat capable Followers instead of Command?
                                Your Followers can have proficiency in skills outside of combat, while the troops you get with Command don't have much in the way of that.

                                How would Command handle an army that has Might?
                                Command can't give you a battle group with Might.

                                Developer for Exalted

                                Want to write for Exalted? Look at the freelancer submission guidelines.
