        Cathak Cainan is, per What Fire Has Wrought, as are Cevis Ghandavra (though not of the Realm, he's helpful for knowing what an essence 5 db can look like) and Ragara per Heirs of the Shogunate. Amon Mora, Tepet Arada, Peleps Deled and Cathak Setod are essence 4.

        That makes me think it likely that at least a few other heads of houses are essence 5, as well as probably some people associated with the Immaculate Order. My guess would be 10-100 dragonblooded of essence 5 in the Realm, but I don't think we're given a lot to go on.

        Assuming 100 dynasts exalt each year (average lifespan ~100, there are 10,000 dynasts), and since a few dozen are enrolled in the Heptagram at a time, they enroll for 7 years, and some don't graduate, probably something like 2-10% of Dynasts graduated from the Heptagram. Add in various who become sorcerers from another source, and you have probably around 10% of dynasts are sorcerers. (Compare to 20% of dynasts have occult as a caste ability, and if all abilities are favored at an equal rate 20% have occult as a favored ability. Assuming we should treat caste/favored as anything other than game mechanics, I'd expect nearly all sorcerers to have occult as caste/favored, and many people with occult caste/favored not to be sorcerers). For comparison, 20% of the known essence 5 dragonblooded are sorcerers, but 5 people is really not enough sample size for this.

        Assuming no correlation between these, I'm estimating 1-10 essence 5 dragonblooded dynasts are sorcerers. Plus maybe a few more outside the Realm.

        Though, it has been mentioned that part of the explanation of why there aren't many essence 5 dragonblooded is "Why bother, essence 3 is strong enough", and if it's known that sufficient enlightenment allows sorcerers to reach dramatic new heights it might encourage more of them to seek that out.

        Given how rough nearly all of these numbers are, it seems reasonable for the final result to be fairly different than I got (though, clearly at least 1, since Mnemon is an Essence 5 Sorcerer)

                                                    I would say that, besides Demon of the Third Circle, there is not much difference in Solar spells to make them so game-breaking. Really, the 3rd circle speels are good, but not that much. Workings are only harder to get, if they are solar, so that's a non-issue (*).

                                                    And then, Demon of the 3rd circle could be game-breaking or not, depending on how they statted. They have never had stats (in any edition - mind you), so it is the game master's choice. Are they 'you win' entities? Are their stats just more inflated? How much? Are they one-trick poneys?

                                                    Also, there would be a matter of economics for demons of the 3rd circle. How? Well, a demon cannot be invoked twice at the same time. This is no issue with demons of the first circle, they are races, and plentiful.

                                                    This starts to be a problem with demons of the second circle. Suppose you want to invoke Octavian, the Living Tower. For him to be available, you will need to beat every other invoker that would want to get him, if he was not invoked already. And if he is invoked, no luck this time, better luck next month (**). That will be more of a problem if there are (hundreds?) of DBs invoking.

                                                    This is much more of a case with demons of the Third Circle, who are not so numerous to begin with. In the setting as written, in the beginning, it will be easy for the solar to invoke any demon and say they are the 'first of the age', but then later the issue will raise its ugly head again. And worse, if it's a miss, better luck next year! If there would already dozens of celestials calling them, this would a fucking rat race! There would be rushes to be the first to end a ritual in the year and get first pick, and chances are big that in the end, you would choose poorly just to be beaten to it and get nothing (***).

                                                    And then there is an OOC issue: there aren't that many demons statted. If demon summoning is more prevalent, the storyteller would need to create more demons. This is a lot of work for 2nd circle demons, that have their unique things going on. This is a huge problem for demons of the third circle, for which we have nothing on the subject of stats.

                                                    * - Except Solar ambition 3, but who cares! Sure, they are creation-spanning, but oh so very subtle. Everybody wants to make exalted-lite supersoldiers.
                                                    ** - Really, going for broke on Summoning Octavian is a newbie mistake. It is a cutthroat market and you just won't get him! You should go for Joxer, the Obscure One.
                                                    ***- It would be much safer to go to the obscure ones, again. And there would not be that many obscure ones, with there being so few and existing a living culture of invoking them.
