        Originally posted by glamourweaver View Post

        My character is a Zenith Sorcerer with the Summon Elemental spell. Does a summoned Elemental or Demon count as a spirit familiar even without the Twilight anima effect? If not, are the relevant Occult Charms meant to be Twilight only?
        I think this is a case of unintentionally specific language occluding the intent, which is to better facilitate summoner builds by allowing them to keep bound spirits off camera until a dramatically appropriate moment. So, to answer your first question, you could have spirit familiars without being a Twilight, but if Occult is not available as a Supernal, it would take longer. I sincerely doubt that you'd need to designate a spirit familiar in order to use any of the spirit-manipulating charms as written, but a familiar does have the advantage of being bound more or less permanently.
