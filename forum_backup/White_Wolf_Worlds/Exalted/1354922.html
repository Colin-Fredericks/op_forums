              Let's think about what's in the few QC charms.

              Black Soil's Claim: an offensive charm whose damage-over-time effect keys off the target's wound penalty. For undead targets it instead adds dice of Aggravated damage that increase with wound penalty.

              Stitched-Flesh Juggernaut: The QC adds 2 STR, possibly keying off Essence given the QC is E2. It adds 4 with anima up. It can be reset by spending motes and absorbing muscle from a corpse.

              Weeping Crimson Gaze: Doubles up to three 9s on an attack roll, also being able to see and hit dematerialized undead (specifically undead, by the text) for the instant. The character's wound penalty reduces the cost.

              Patchwork Body Reconstruction: After killing a nontrivial living foe or a fleshy undead, heal up to 2 (Essence?) non-Agg HLs or a crippling injury.

              Skeletal Spike Embrace: Extrude bones to get the benefits of cover and also hurt grapple partners or people hitting you up close til next turn; at your -4 HLs, the effect is greater all around.

              Clambering Horror Chase: Enhance a rush, rushing from afar and moving up walls and such. This can be committed for the scene, but it burns out a limb and requires it to be replaced.

              So...
              We have stuff playing on your and others' wound penalties, rewarding you for targeting the hurt and giving you discounts or boosts for your injuries.
              We have stuff that's specifically keyed for undead.
              We interact with corpses.
              We can burn out body parts for big effects.

              That, and using the core's stated themes for the aspects, should help a little bit. I'm not sure how to divide up the Attributes.

                  Originally posted by Beans View Post
                  I'm not sure how to divide up the Attributes.
                  I'm going to be lazy (and, perhaps, kind of a tool) and quote myself. From another thread on the topic:

                  My personal setup leaves Blood with Charisma (passion, connections) and Wits (purity, but also navigation and guidance), Breath with Dexterity (grace and subtle action) and Perception (understanding and reflection), Flesh with your set of Strength (force, madness, savagery) and Appearance (metamorphosis and lust), Marrow with Manipulation (secrecy) and Intelligence (analysis, obsession), and Soil with Stamina (resilience, resurrection) and Intelligence (mysticism, creation).
                  Intelligence repeats mostly because Masters of Jade pitched Liminals as "sorcerer-exorcists," and both sorcery and exorcism slot readily into the Intelligence Attribute.

                  Of course, I maintain that Liminals should have a set of Universal Charms to fulfill the essential fantasy of undead ghost-hunter, just as Lunars have Universal Charms to support their role as shapeshifters regardless of build. This would include the typical anti-spirit tech, as well as Charms like Ox-Body Technique and other baseline survival effects, while Intelligence and Stamina Charms would build off these for more esoteric or specific powers, such as binding defeated ghosts into servants of clay or meat, or absorbing a mass of corpses to become a giant of bone and sinew.
