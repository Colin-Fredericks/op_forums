                            This is a problem generally encountered with almost any Exalted crossover, and is why I'm not a fan of sticking Exalted into other things. One of the primary principles of the setting is "the Exalted will always rise above any challenge that isn't purely moral or the Great Curse", and if you take that principle as default when you do the crossover, the answer is the Exalted win. They don't offer anything new to the other setting, they conquer it. It's more irritating than interesting, because it turns the other setting into little more than window dressing.

                            But oftentimes what people mean when they think about an Exalted crossover is the idea of people endowed with sun/moon/fate/elemental-themed powers being introduced into the other setting. That idea has some grounds for at least some fanart, or a story about existing characters from the setting being endowed with those new powers. But that's not really crossing over with Exalted, it's crossing over with a thing that has outward similarities to Exalted. Rarely does such a crossover include any of the realpolitik or serious assessment of the fantasy tropes involved, when that's foundational to what makes Exalted Exalted rather than just a game about having superpowers..
