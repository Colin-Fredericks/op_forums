                            Originally posted by Snailslayer View Post

                            A Terrestrial dragon-blooded or Terrestrial Exigent?
                            When used as a noun rather than adjective, "Terrestrial" is usually synonymous with Dragon-Blooded. Referring to a "Terrestrial level" Exigent is useful for getting a sense of the scope of their power, but it's not a distinction recognised in-character.

                            Originally posted by Snailslayer
                            And if the god misses the obvious hints and the sidereal sharpening a knife and just goes for it?
                            Keep in mind that reincarnating as a Sidereal entails being bestowed with their Exalted Essence, rather than either of their normal souls. A person destined to be a Sidereal has the actual Exaltation within them, it's just waiting for the right time to awaken.

                            I think it's fair to assume that the god's Exaltation just won't follow through because the prospective Chosen is thoroughly incompatible.

                                                        There's technically a surprising amount of plant life in Autochthon, though. Not, like, a lot, but more than you might think. The main populat-relevant one is that some hydroponic gardens are carefully maintained for medicinal herbs and such, and iirc enough edible crop is being produced amongst that that being able to eat vegetables is "merely" one of the greatest luxuries available to the upper echelons of the Tripartite rather than an unthinkable occurrence.

                                                        The Reaches have some organic flora, mostly lichens and algae with a smattering of other plants hardy enough to get by in the unwelcoming environment over the last thousands of years; the World-Engine of Steam is noted as being a special reservoir for the greatest abundance of plant life in Autochthon, with some variety but most heavily consisting of moss, protected and tended to by the subminister Kushkala. This makes a good source of food, weaving material and dyes for some tunnel folk bands, but largely is neither abundant enough nor close enough for the Nations to exploit directly.
