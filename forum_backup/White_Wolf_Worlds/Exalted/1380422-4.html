                Mighty Thew: Wow! I would probably houserule that, if it ever came up, to let my players become swole if they want to.

                Originally posted by DrLoveMonkey View Post

                It's cheap for Solars, 3 Occult, no pre-req charms. For Lunars they need 4 previous mental attribute charms, and DBs need four Occult charms. The Lunar has to be already quite the cunning individual, and the DB must be a very accomplished Occultist.
                If Occult were a random ability we would expect ~40% of Solars to have at least a 3 in Occult at character creation, and from a pure optimization perspective they all should take Terrestrial Circle Sorcery - it just offers so much for so little investment. (I'm partial to taking Stormwind Rider as a control spell, and then Travel Without Distance when you get Celestial, because they offer great long-range movement options, which are hard to get from other Charms in Exalted.) In practice I think people mostly only take significant Occult if they're planning to make their character a Sorcerer based on character concept.

                I mean, if the Terrestrial Circle Sorcery charm just got you Sorcerous Workings and the other incidental abilities, and you had to take another Charm to cast a spell, would people say that this is underpowered or doesn't reflect how difficult it is to acquire Sorcery in the fiction?

                This is basically what Martial Arts is - a Charm's worth of investment before you get to the good stuff - except the good stuff in Sorcery is more unique, and this first Charm would still get you totally unique abilities.

                Thus I think the character building system pushes you away from taking Martial Arts and towards taking Sorcery, whereas based on the fiction it should probably be the reverse.

                    How about "not dying from physical harm, ever" and "regenerating faster than a Lunar with a maxed investment in Stamina"? (The Gem of Incomparable Wellness was a mistake.)

                    Other standouts include the Ivy-Hilt Gemstone (Disarm gambits against you have +2 Difficulty, effectively making them far too risky and expensive to ever be worth it), the Key of Mastery (for the low low price of just one Evocation, gain the control effect on one of your spells, a bonus you can even swap between stories), the Stone of Aquatic Prowess (all the benefits of being a Water Aspect for just two Evocations! too bad you could do a sorcerous working to gain Gills 3 with Subtlety for a fraction of the cost) and the Seven Leaping Dragons Stone (gain 2i whenever you switch MA Forms reflexively; at this point, I think we have enough styles for this to be effectively +2i/round for free on average).

                    Evocations for the demonic tattoos gained from the Pact with Mara sorcerous initiation || Pyre-Kindler (Soulsteel and Red Jade Grimscythe, Artifact 3) || Tenebrous Descent (Stormcaller's Black Jade Reaver Daiklave cousin, Artifact 5)
