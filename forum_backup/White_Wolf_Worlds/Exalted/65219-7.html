        Wow, where to start...

        - The year starts with Wood (spring), then Fire (summer), then Water (monsoon), then Earth (autumn), then Air (winter), and then Calibration.

        - there are 24 hours in the day, the 25th hour is something believed lost in the Primordial War

        - While concepts, races, and aspects of Creation were lost in the Primordial War, including a desperate closing attack by She Who Lives In Her Name; there is not the sense that 90% of it is lost & beyond even imagining, beyond the claims of some Pyrian Cults.

        - Yu Shan is as massive as described, but rather than a domed city, it's structures float in a black void hanging over a sea of stars (if you're familiar with the "Sandman" or "Lucifer" comic series, imagine the Silver City, but with far more diverse magical material obviously). It's immense parks are hanging gardens, and spires reach as far down as they do up. There is no true day or night, rather the dominant luminescence of the city is defined by who ever is ahead in the Games. If one falls there is almost always more city to land on, but in the rare event that that is not the case, when you reach the sea of stars below there's a flash of starlight & the character is thrown from a random Yu Shan gateway in Creation.

        - Except the Calibration Gate, the Yu Shan Gateways are stable points in Creation, but they are not (for the most part) mystical stone circles. By the Age of Sorrow most are just locals in nature, where a splendid shaft of sunlight always falls perfectly beteween two trees, or a waterfall, or a sea cave. A few of the First Age Temples that erected the stone circles around the naturally existent gate points still stand, at least in ruins though.

        - There are no such thing as Half-Caste. The children of Exalted are almost certainly Heroic Mortals & prone to Enlightened Essence and even some unique mutations (from essence exposure), but Exalted Charms are right out.

        - Weather Working is not its own Art. It falls under God Beckoning and is subject to being ignored or considered Bribery.

        - Very rare Lunar or Sidereal Exalted can assume Death-tainted essence (such as if they're ghost-blooded, or if they attune to Calendar of Setesh instead of the Loom of Fate, or perform the Sacred Hunt upon the animals in shadowlands near-exclusively, etc etc etc) the result of which is the ability to wield Labyrinth Circle Necromancy, at the cost of being able to wield Celestial Circle Sorcery.

        - "Pure" mortals, even with Enlightened Essence can NOT wield Sorcery. Essence-fueled Thaumaturgy is as close as mortals come. Those desirous of Sorcerous power need an Endowment from a God or Demon (ala demon pacts of myth) making them effectively God/Demon-blooded for mechanics purposes.

        - At the edge of the Underworld things fade into a grey timeless mist that can be shaped by memory (but not imagination ala the Wyld); when Creation shrank, the fading was not instantaneous but it was enough to cause mass migration of ghosts. Some associate the mists with Lethe because it is said that those who go deep into them loose their memories possibly allowing them to enter lethe.

        - Ghosts do NOT get to psychically send single last messages to someone in Creation when they enter Lethe. Getting a last message to a loved one is the type of thing they need to deal with in order to move on, not a reward for doing so.

        - There are 13 Neverborn, the first of which created the River with its death, and all the following dragged the constellations out of the River with their passage. Individual stars were dragged over by significant ghosts. The Pole Star was anchored by the Calendar of Setesh. There are only 12 Deathlords, but there are Nephwrack-sread rumors of a 13th based on the fact that there are 13 Neverborn. Only 5 of the Neverborn can be said to wake and actively seek out minions with their whispers though (the five opened by the Black Nadir Concordat).

        - Saigoth does not “survive” in the Underworld; rather it’s a true realm of the dead beneath the waves, many in the West believe it to be the proper resting place of those who die at sea; a natural kingdom of the dead in contrast with the abomination of Skullstone. Drowned Clepsy is a Shadowland ruled by The Queen Caliginous Over the Hundred Depths (a Deathlord obviously)

        - Starmetal is made from fallen stars not dead gods. The death of god does cause that gods star to fall, but also major readjustments to fate (such as the intrusion of a Third Circle Demon into a predestined affair) can cause significant enough recalibration to cause a star to fall.

        - Mela, Pasiap, Hesiesh, Danaa'd, & Sextes Jylis are the names of the five Immaculate Dragons - who did in fact exist - not the five true Elemental Dragons (who are known simply by the Old Realm names of the Elements). The five Immaculate Dragons were the originators of Immaculate Martial Arts styles under Chejop's training & led the Usurpation forces. Mela died wielding a Martyrdom charm on the night of the Calibration Feast. Hesiesh & Danaa'd died in later closing battles against the survivors of that Calibration - also in Martyrdom-level spectacles. Pasiap & Sextes Jylis were instrumental in helping rebuild the world under the Shogunate after the war ended, but were mysteriously assassinated, officially by Anathema, but more likely by the increasingly paranoid Shogun.

        - Monstrance bound (and IME bound) Abyssals use the canon Resonance system. Those freed use the Doom system. Only the freed may learn Unconquered Hero's Faith.

        - Deathlords are high essence ghosts with Panoply Charms as powerful as those of Third Circle Demons. They may wield all 3 tiers of Necromancy, but no Sorcery or Exalted Charms. They may TEACH Sorcery, and the Abyssal mirrors of the Solar Charms they knew in life to their Deathknights. Many of their powerful Panoply Charms and defenses are restricted from working in Creation, especially in daylight - hence a dependence on Deathknights.

        - Deathlord essence is bound to the Neverborn and indeed can not be GETed or equivalented (though reforming may take years/decades/etc). Their secret weaknesses are all tied to their Passions or Fetters - thus the secret to defeating a Deathlord is to unravel the mystery of who they were and what fetters them to the Underworld.

        - There is no mandatory potluck for Infernal Exalted during Calibration. The Thing Infernal assembles only as needed, involving all required Infernals involved in the issue at hand, all Infernals stationed in Malfeas, and Reclamation supporting Third Circle Demons. Infernals are not ritually called from Creation when it is most vulnerable!

        - No rape requirements for GSP characters... obviously!

            Hmmm, what of my headcanon...

            Well, for a start:

            Every property and image attributed to the Daystar is parceled out and distributed amongst various other wonders of the First Age. For instance, some of its mightiest weapons are actually powers of the Sword of Creation, harnessing the Essence of the Sun (and thus unfortunately beyond the Scarlet Empress, even if the engines that gather and focus such power had not long since withered away), although some of its lesser weapons formed their own self-contained systems.

            The Chirmirajen in particular was commissioned by the Hierophant as a means to seek out and gather the disparate Solar Exalted and bring them to Meru to attend the Deliberative councils (although it developed something of a sensitivity for going out of its way to rescue stranded or distressed individuals).

            The Eschaton Lock was only ever a purely theoretical weapon, a thought experiment amongst certain particularly morbid engineers or paranoid warriors about how the arsenals of the Realm might be turned upon the task of utterly annihilating the world through the turnings of a singular central control Artifact. As far as the most discerning Sidereals and inquisitive Lunars could tell retrospectively, no Solar ever made a serious attempt at even beginning to construct such a thing, although some Sidereals were mildly worried at just how far a Solar could get at abstractly assembling it within their own imagination; mostly though, it was dismissed as, at best, severely impractical, some even saying that the efforts necessary to construct it in the first place (particularly the enormous economic strains) would be sufficient to destroy the world for all practical purposes in the first place. The Sidereals were much more worried at what they imagined such theoreticians saw in their peers to imagine that such a weapon could be controlled; nigh impossible though the weapon might be, no Solar involved ever seemed to doubt that there were some among their numbers worthy to wield it (and one could even deduce some specifically considered candidates from careful observations), and what are the prospects for a world inhabited by one or more individuals capable of bending the apocalypse to their will...

                        My Headcannon (Some of it at least):


                        A Creation Before This One: The Primordials lost a Civil War in another Creation and fled with what they could (Including the Games of Divinity) as far from that Creation. Several were severely injured, including Autochthon who never fully recovered. They hid their Essence flows by going through the deepest of the Wylds and eventually came to a suitable place, where they formed their own Creation. They put a Geas on the Gods in order to prevent them from rebelling as they had Rebelled against the rulers of the Creation they fled from. Even now, the Rulers of that other Creation are seeking the Rebels. Meanwhile, the only surviving Primordial from this Creation is fleeing back to it's former home, to beg forgiveness and aid. What happens in the future is unknown at this point. (Note: Autochthon has more in common with the Primordials of the other Creation than he ever did with his siblings who rebelled. That is one of the reasons that they mistreated him, and why he had no problem betraying them.)


                        The Empress Rules: The Empress still rules the Realm. She is not the "Ruler of Creation" -- the Creation Ruling mandate is still tied to the Exaltation of the former Queen Merela, whom the forces of the Yozi's (Specifically the Ebon Dragon), are seeking.


                        Our Daughter: Merela and the Unconquered Sun had a Daughter. She is now a Goddess in Yu-Shan, and with the reemergence of the Solars is looking for his Mother's Exaltation. She has an extreme dislike for the Sidereals, and is very concerned for Her Father's fascination with the Games of Divinity and believes that only her Mother's Exaltation can bring him out of the Jade Pleasure Dome.


                        The Salinan Working and Salina: When Salina's original Exaltation, an Ancient Zenith, died against the crystal-trapped fires of She Who Lives In Her Name, he heard her Whispers and comprehension blossomed into a Vision that would carry subtly forth into future Exaltations: what creation would be without She Who Lives In Her Name (See DotFA). Salina, recalling this Vision, worked at making the "Great Working" (Salinan Working) come to fruition, in order to imbed the Five Ordeals into the Hierarchy of Creation and preserve Sorcerous Lore forever. What is not known, is that the Great Working was subtly manipulated by She Who Lives In Her Name, allowing momentary pathways to form between Creation and Malfeas. In addition, the potential was woven into the Hierarchy of Creation that allowed for Enlightened Mortals to learn Terrestrial Sorcery and, with powerful enough Essence and enough knowledge, a Terrestrial or Celestial, could learn greater Sorceries than ever before. No Terrestrial had an opportunity to discover this before the Usurption and none have had a reason too since, while the Sidereal have yet to realize that this is even a potential. However Rakshi may be the first to discover this among the Lunars due to her age and control of Sperimin -- if she does so, then an ancient, insane, power hungry Lunar who can wield Solar Sorcery will be unleashed upon Creation. Salina, during the Usurption, was overwhelmed by the Great Curse and filled by the whispers of She Who Lives In Her Name. Fleeing to Malfeas, she pledged herself to the Principal of Hierarchy, where she has served ever since as a powerful Akuma.


                        The First Exalted: When Autochthon was designing the Exalted, he initially crafted a template for each Exalted Type. These First Born, were both more powerful and flawed. The Essence that flowed through them was purer and rawer, filling the First Born with exceptional Essence Pools, and they were vastly more powerful than those who would come later. Yet this power was difficult to contain, and was stressful upon the Templates and the area surrounding them. From the First Born Autochthon created all of the other Exalted. However, the Essence flows of the First Born were a weakness, for through them was the Great Curse imprinted upon all the Exalted, and ultimately a malignancy was laid upon the Incarna that eats at them today, in addition to their addiction to the Games of Divinity. Too late did Autochthon realize the error in his design, as the First Born were the most to suffer by the Great Curse. The First Born fouoght in the Primordial Wars, and disappeared during the the Usurption. Recently (since the breaking of Jade Prison) there have been sightings of extraordinary powerful Exalted, and elders among the Lunar's and Sidereal realize that the First Born have returned. (The Lunar First Born retreated to the Wyld and established a stable place somewhere in the Deep Wyld; the Solar First Born were imprisoned and upon being freed immediately sought hosts; the Sidereas First Born walked their own paths, hidden from all but the Maidens, even the Viziers themselves; the First Born of the Dragons each went to the appropriate Elemental Polls, where they learned much at the feet of the Elementals and the Elemental Dragons). Perhaps through the First Born, the Great Curse could be reversed.


                        What Was Destroyed Was Preserved In Flames: That which She Who Lives Within Her Name burned away in her rages was not truly destroyed. Instead they were preserved in her 99,997 spheres in anticipation of the time when she would once again be free upon the world. Then, those parts will be returned to the world, altered by the very Hierarchical nature that is She Who Lives Within Her Name. All free will burned away.


                        What Came BeforeWorld Before Ours: Exalted is the Prequel for the World of Darkness

                                There is no Immaterial because that's inappropraite for a heroic/mythic fantasy world[1]. So it's gone. Spirit Cutting Attack and similar charms let you hit spirits (and behemoths and mutants and the odd Exalt) who have physical forms it's infeasible you'd be able to meaningfully harm without investing in exotica, explosive weaponry, or a justifying stunt. (E.G. Doldrums and Need Fires.) Other charms which interact with the Immaterial have a similar mapping where appropriate (e.g. a Thunderbird's Material Tribulation Divestment lets them briefly transform into a thunderclap to avoid attacks).

                                Ghosts have bodies in the Underworld because it's a place of memory and will - the corpus is the stuff of the Underworld taking on a form the ghost is associated with by other ghosts and herself. In Creation and other realms, a ghost is little more than a single breath of air laced with the dread of the grave and the chill of the void.

                                [1] But it's totally appropriate for an urban fantasy game and White Wolf gonna White Wolf.

                                        I have never really been at peace with how Exalted's Immaterial works, as it (1) is piece of the game which interacts with relatively few other pieces of the game, and (2) is ripe for abuse as soon as someone who's willing to break with genre convention gets ahold of it, but I think it's hard to argue that something like that is not just approrpiate, but practically mandated by the source material - how can it not be in heroic fantasy when Tolkein used it?                                             Another bit from my games:

                                            The Five Elemental Dragons are Elementals in the same way that the Celestial Incarna are Gods. That is to say, they are a distinct order of being within the larger category, the ultimate manifestation thereof. All lesser Elementals are descended from the Five Dragons, although the precise method of creation is lost to history, leaving the possibility of the story of the primal super-elementals to still be true. When a lesser Elemental refines its essence sufficiently, it gains the potential of a powerful draconic shape, although not all pursue this option. There are no "Greater" Elemental Dragons. There are still absurdly powerful top-tier Lesser Elemental Dragons, but compared to the Five, even the Gardullis is distinctly "Lesser". Were one of the Five to perish, a Lesser Dragon of sufficient might could theoretically grow to replace them. While the Five persist, that potential is locked up inside of them. The current five may not be the original five, but ascending to that tier is a lot like taking upper-end Yozi charms. The role really winds up defining the entity who occupies it, so that even if the current Dragon of Air isn't the original Dragon of Air, they are mostly indistinguishable.

                                            It is also possible for a Dragon-Blooded of sufficient power to evolve into a replacement for one of the Five. One idea I've toyed with is that, during the Usurpation, the Solars murdered the original Five Elemental Dragons hoping that the loss of their patrons would de-claw the Dragon-Blooded. Instead, the Five Immaculate Dragon-Blooded ascended to become the new Elemental Dragons.

                                            None of the Five Elemental Dragons slumber. They all play the Games of Divinity regularly with the Incarna, a fact greatly resented by many lesser Gods, who are racist about Elementals in general, even if some of them are apocalyptic sky-kaiju. Their duties require them to leave Yu Shan and visit the Elemental Poles more frequently than the Incarna leave their own spots at the games, which is why their visits to Creation always have a rushed, urgent feel. Pasiap and Sextes Jylis walk the world in human shape more often than their siblings do, because they are more likely to encounter other entities at their poles, and they prefer to stay incognito.

                                            The Elemental Poles are all colonized, although the people who live in or near them tend to be either heavily mutated to survive, or wholly dependent on First Age technology that they can barely operate, much less repair. Only the gardens under the shade of the World Tree in the East approach the population density on, under and around Mt. Meru. The perils inherent in proximity to the Sovereign Flame, the Eternal Storm or the Boundless Deep are comparable to the handful of Autochthonian colonies outside the relatively hospitable Poles of Metal or Crystal. Each of these communities has a healthy Terrestrial bloodline of the appropriate element. The bloodline associated with the Imperial Mountain has been wholly absorbed by the Scarlet Dynasty, which is one reason why the ratio of Realm Dragon-Blooded just barely favors the Earth Aspect.

                                            The Kukla is not an Elemental Dragon at all. The Apocalypse Dragon is the fetich soul of Ramethus. When the Solars locked Ramethus up and made him the power source of the Sword of Creation, they locked away all his souls separately, to keep him from any more self-inflicted evolution.

                                                        Exalts should be able to burst into a god's sanctum and drag them out to cast down in front of their worshippers. Drunk peasants should stumble into a god's sanctum and bring back tales of splendour undreamt-of by their peers. Travellers should meet gods (or demons) riding on palanquins along the road. Demon summoning should lead to a physical being appearing in the midst of the summoning circle. These are all things that should happen in Creation. The Immaterial forbids or suggests against any of these happening because spirits and sanctums are hidden away in a coterminous otherworld where they are permanently, effortlessly, athematically intangible and invisible.

                                                        I don't reject intangible, invisible spiritual forces. A spirit of the air is free to use Landscape Hide to disperse into the wind (or a cloud, or a tornado) and another spirit might do the same to inhabit an idol (or a mountain, or a lamp, or a shrine). The important thing here is that they can do this in thematic ways. It's cool that you would be wary when the wind blows if you've made an enemy of a wind spirit. It's less cool that making an enemy of a silver mine's god means having to be worried about invisible, intangible stalkers at every moment. It's also nice to have the ability to foreshadow the arrival (or presence) of spirits the players know about, which is (sadly) undermined when the Immaterial exists and is the default state of being for most spirits because there are no ambiguous signs of a spirit's possible presence when they're Immaterial.

The important thing here is that they can do this in thematic ways. It's cool that you would be wary when the wind blows if you've made an enemy of a wind spirit. It's less cool that making an enemy of a silver mine's god means having to be worried about invisible, intangible stalkers at every moment. It's also nice to have the ability to foreshadow the arrival (or presence) of spirits the players know about, which is (sadly) undermined when the Immaterial exists and is the default state of being for most spirits because there are no ambiguous signs of a spirit's possible presence when they're Immaterial.
                                                            In the case of gods, their lairs occupy the same starry space Yu-Shan does. My headcanon states in every gods lair you can see Yu-Shan in the distance/horizon.
