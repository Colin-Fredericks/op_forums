                          My own estimate is that certain Essence levels represent natural plateaus for many Exalts, which they can stay at for decades or even centuries. I think Essence 3 and 5 are both plateaus of this sort, with 4 being somewhat less of one, but still something possible to stay at for quite a long time. The upshot of this is that there probably isn't a really meaningful "average time" that an Exalt spends at a given level. Some could only take a few years to break through Essence 3, while others could hover there for centuries.

                          I do think that Dragonbloods probably get to Essence 3 and higher Essence levels a bit faster than other Exalts do, statistically speaking. I think their shorter lifespans probably contribute a bit of sense of urgency to cultivating their power, and their larger population and more developed support network mean that it's more plausible for them to retreat from the world in an appropriate location for the weeks or months required.

                          So, to provide my own answer, I'd guess that about 50% of Lunars, Solars, Abyssals, and other "Celestial" Exalted who reach 100 years old are probably Essence 3, whereas more like 75% or more of Dragonbloods are.

I cant quote exact sources, but in past editions DBs didnt advance very quickly, not just for XP reasons, but cultural reasons as well. The typical dragon-blooded is much more down to earth than the celestial exalts. They have mundane worries like ruling creation and managing the family, and generally being nobility.
