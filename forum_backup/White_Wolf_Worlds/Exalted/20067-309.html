                    Originally posted by Rowanas View Post
                    As a follow up question for the noble forumites - I don't see anywhere that you actually break stealth when you attack, unless you run into the middle of a crowded fight to shank someone. You have to spend an acton to re-establish surprise, but it doesn't indicate that you've actually become noticeable. Is this the correct reading?
                    My understanding of your question is that it pertains primarily to ranged attacks. Once you've made your attack, that's grounds for the target (and any other witnesses) to roll (Perception + Awareness) against your (Dexterity + Stealth), including any modifiers. If you ran into the middle of a crowd to shank someone, you probably don't have any cover/concealment and your Stealth roll would fail automatically.

                    If you were attacking at range, say shooting your bow from around the edge of an ancient redwood tree (textbook example of heavy cover), then it's possible that you could make your attack and not be detected immediately thereafter. You'd still have to re-establish surprise, as you said, but you at least have the chance to do so.
