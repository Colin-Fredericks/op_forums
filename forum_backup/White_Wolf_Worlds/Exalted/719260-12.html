            Well, my crunch-fu is not strong yet, so this is probably a horrible idea. Lets give this a try anyway though.

            Elit Druin
            In North West Creation their lives an small group who have taken the mantle of defenders of Creation upon themselves. These shamans and druids originally began soon after the Great Contagion. Noticing that knowledge was being lost at an astounding rate, since people were burning books for warmth instead of reading them, the Elit Druin was formed by a former scholar named Walker in an attempt to reclaim and restore as much of the First Age knowledge as possible. From their home in the formally abandoned First Age fortress renamed to Cumhacht and partially converted into a library, the Elit Druin move freely though the area, being outside the tribal structure entirely. With Wyld mutants and Fair Folk incursions being a not unknown experience, these druids do occasionally have to answer the call to fight these monsters from beyond the border. However, since the loss of even one druid is seen as an enormous loss because of the knowledge and experience that she contains, more often the druid will attempt to move behind the scenes in order to produce similar results.

            Two areas embody the true core of the Elit Druin’s power. While their actual names are lost to history, these areas are just called the Well and the Lake. Because of magically binding oaths that are taken while still alive, after the death of a druid her Po soul is immediately sucked into the Well while her Hun soul is taken to the Lake. Receiving the blessings of the dwellers of both Well and Lake is required in order for a person to be considered a member.

            Shaping Rituals:
            A druid cannot make a normal Shape Sorcery Action. Instead she has a Sorcery Essence pool of Int + Lore x 3 that naturally recharges at one point per day. After the pool is depleted, casting spells ages the character at the rate of one year per 5 motes (round down). The only way to reverse this is by the Taste of Death ritual.

            Other Benefits:
            Living Fire (Merit 2): As The Burning Name but change to dice pool to (Intelligence + Lore). This fire is dark blue in color.

            The Breath of Life (Merit 2): As Astral Meditation.

            The Taste of Death (Merit 2): The Taste of Death is a twelve hour long ritual that must be conducted at the Lake. Upon the ritual’s completion a dark, robed figure emerges out of the waters. The druid and the figure then bother sink beneath the still waters. During the druid’s time in the Lake she begins to reverse the aging done to her by the casting of spells at a rate of one year of suspended animation for one year of spell induced aging. This will continue until the she is the same age as when The Taste of Death was bought. The Taste of Death also makes the character sterile.

                                If you're looking to simplify it, go with my earlier suggestion and peel off the abstinence bits since you didn't like those. For the refluffed Merits, maybe you'd instead need to maintain a lifestyle consistent with the imbibed demon's Essence.

                                Take for instance the sesseljae, the stomach bottle bugs who eat poison and other such taint. If yours is the kind of character who would rather internalize the Essence of a sesseljae than actually summon and host one, you could revise Living Spirit Cultivation so that it grants the bonus vs. poison but requires you to ingest poison on a regular basis in lieu of maintaining an ascetic lifestyle to maintain its effect. This emulates the need to keep a sesseljae fed, but requires that the sorcerer expose herself to somewhat greater risk.

                                You could go on and look through the other listed demons or make up your own to fit the other Merits. As a second example, maybe internalizing the songs of the angyalkae give clarity of mind analogous to Vital Focus Cultivation. Go with what you like and what you want, then come back and present concrete ideas. You'd be lucky to find someone willing to do all the work for you. ;-P

                                            Here is one me and my ST made together. Hope you enjoy.
                                            Tao Teh Ching Martial Sorcery
                                            Theme: The sorcerer learned their sorcery through rigorous training of martial arts and katas and hand seals. Through discipline, meditation, and determination the sorcerer delves into the corners of his mind and joins with the natural world to shape the world and essence around them.

                                            Description: In the Monastery of Dreams both martial arts and sorcerous skills are taught. All students learn to cultivate their minds in meditation and their bodies in learning the katas and forms that shape them into masters of their respective paths. Most students only choose one of the two paths but the ancient masters of the monastery occasionally have a student who sees two roads to the same end and masters both martial and sorcerous skills. The Monastery martial arts focuses on weapon based arts and in using hand seals and wards for sorcery. Students of both paths have achieved some notable reputations around the Dreaming Sea as formidable warriors and dangerously competent sorcerers.
                                            Shaping Ritual: Minds Inner Peace - Once per day, By spending an hour in a quiet area in meditation the sorcerer can gain (MA rating) motes of essence that last until they goes to sleep. Also Once per Month, the sorcerer may take a misc action to focus himself through martial katas and making the motes stored by this ritual count for double for his spells, or triple for his control spell.
                                            Shaping Ritual: Kata of the Formless Stance - Once per day, the sorcerer perform breathing exercises and katas for at least 15 minutes to center himself and gains (MA rating) motes till they next rest. Alternatively, Once per week, If the sorcerer performs these exercises while leading a group of at least 5 disciples he gains(MA+Occ rating) motes instead.
                                            Shaping Ritual: Sorcerous Reaping Prana - Through focused respiration and intense breathing techniques, the Martial Sorcerer may reap sorcerous motes from the shed blood of his enemies as he fights. Any attack against a non-trivial opponent that deals at least 5 points of initiative damage, or 1 level of decisive damage, he may gain and store 1sm. Additionally, any attack the Sorcerer successfully defends against with their martial arts derived parry, that is accompanied with a thematic stunt gains them an additional 1sm.These motes last til the end of scene and cap at (Ess+MA+Occ) motes.
                                            Merits:
                                            Mind’s Maze Defense (4) - The sorcerer’s mind is an inhospitable landscape for those that would try to manipulate or infiltrate it. The Martial Sorcerer gains +2 resolve against any magical effect that tries to sway/corrupt/confuse/mislead his mind. This works equally against charms like “mental invisibility technique” and against attempts of the fair folk to consume pieces of his soul, to sorcerous spells like “silent words of dreams and nightmares”
                                            Mind Over Body Resilience (2 or 4) - By drawing upon their inner strength the sorcerer can continue actions long after others grow to tired to carry on. For 2 points, the character may apply a -2 difficulty to all fatigue rolls, rolls to defer sleep, or any roll involving physical or mental endurance, including resisting the effects of torture and similar rolls.
                                            For 4 points, the character gains the above benefit, and on top of that, he can ignore the effects of all but the most grievous of wounds. By spending a point of willpower, the character can ignore -1 wound penalties for scene though -2, -4 and incap still affect him normally.

                                                        I wanted to create something which reflected a "vampirism" concept that embodied the elements of human sacrifice modeled in stories where sorcerers drain the "life force" of others to empower their dark arts, including where a sorcerer works a terrible ritual upon an entire city. I gave the thrust of it to the Shaping Ritual, then used the Merits to expand on it. The biggest challenge is creating a sense of temptation to purchase the (inherently horrifying) Merits.

                                                        Essence Vampirism
                                                        You have learned to harness the magic which sustains mortal life. Maybe you had an inborn talent which blossomed, or perhaps the revelation came from your own studies into human nature. You may tap into reservoirs of power within yourself which others can only dream of, but your talent extends beyond your own frame. Draining the life of others grants you a kind of power that inspires fascination and terror, both for the heights which you may achieve, and the horrors you must inflict to gain such strength.

                                                        Shaping Ritual

                                                        The sorcerer may draw upon the life force of lesser mortals to empower his own sorcery. The sorcerer simply touches the victim and rolls Essence + Willpower to draw power from their victim. Successes drain motes, converting them into sorcerous motes on a 1-for-1 basis, first from peripheral, then from personal. If the victim has been drained entirely of motes (or, as in the case of mortals, has no motes in the first place), successes inflict Bashing damage instead.

                                                        If in combat, treat this as a Gambit, with a Difficulty equal to the number of sorcerous motes you wish to drain. This represents reaching out, grabbing your opponent bare-handed in mid-combat, and sucking the life out of them.

                                                        Other Benefits

                                                        A Means to an End (5-dot Merit): You may drain the lives of mere mortals to empower wondrous workings beyond their imagining. By draining several dozen lives, you may use their collective energy to your own ends, adding one to the Terminus of a roll/Means of a sorcerous working. The experience is harrowing, and all victims must make a Stamina + Resistance roll (difficulty depending on the project they are empowering) in order to survive, with a bonus equal to the group's Size. A sorcerer may instead gain two Means if they use this Merit to willingly drain enough people to death that it constitutes an unspeakable horror (such as the death of an entire city). Performing this requires either physically restraining individual victims or using a sorcerous working to create a magic circle which drains the victims (such a circle can potentially surround an entire city).

                                                        Detect Life (2-dot Merit): You are attuned to the life force of others. As a simple action, the Sorcerer may detect whether there are any creatures nearby which possess an Essence rating. This costs 1WP and requires a successful Difficulty 1 Perception + Occult roll.

                                                        Essence Inspiration (5-dot Merit): The life of others inspires your genius. By draining a victim to death, the sorcerer may convert their life force into Crafting Experience. Each dot of Essence converts into either 10 Silver Experience or 1 Gold Experience. To gain this benefit, the Sorcerer must describe a project which will benefit from this Merit. A project may benefit from only one expenditure of this Merit.

                                                        Pyramid of Bones (4-dot Merit): You have learned how to twist the Essence of mortals into the stuff of legends. They shall join something greater. You may take a slain being's Essence and use it as a component part in crafting Artifacts. The more powerful or exotic the Essence, the more useful it is.
