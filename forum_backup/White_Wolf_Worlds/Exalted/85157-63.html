                        The needles Delicious hit her with are special things called Vacus Needles, which have the power to force angels and demons to the void. It's dangerous for them to even touch them (Delicious does it because she's crazy.) That's why Cio needed Allison to remove the needle from White Chain for her.

Vacus needles are more like Angel Kryptonite, letting you disable them with minimal damage and effort due to their special properties. Actually cutting an angel in half is something else entirely.

                            I believe it's a difference of the needle cutting off one of the channels in the armour through which the white flame flows to give it motion, rendering it suddenly no longer suitable as a body for an angel, so they just kind of step out of it, compared to actually rupturing the armour in a manner that causes the white flame to go spilling out of it and back to the Void.

                            ​It would appear that, strictly speaking, it's not the damage to their body that kills the angel, it's the trauma of being so suddenly and forcefully shunted out of reality.

                            One should bare in mind that, barring the intervention of Metatron, an angel's body is something unique and permanent; shoving a needle through White Chain's body that renders it inoperable for an angel constitutes banishment because that's the only body she can ever have. There are angels whose bodies have been broken who have reincarnated in the Void but cannot return to the world, because the Knights Peregrine (who are tasked with repairing bodies) have been conflicted about what to do with angels in centuries.

                            For the moment, I think it is unclarified whether or not providing a new body is something that Metatron can or will do repeatedly, or if the broken body of a Thorn also needs to be fixed before they can return to reality.
