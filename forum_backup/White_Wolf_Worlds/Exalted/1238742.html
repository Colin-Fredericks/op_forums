    Neophyte Campaign Method

    07-25-2018, 09:38 PM
    Hello everyone. I am posting here because I would like your help. I’m a Veteran GM of 15+ years and got introduced to exalted several years ago as a player. I fell in love with this crazy over the top setting. My friends and I talk every now and again about our “RPG white whales”. The games we love but have not been able to play a game that went for a satisfying amount of time, or that burned out or faded away. Well I am about to try and make a friend of mine’s rpg white whale game come true.

    We have one veteran and four neophytes. Since I have a group of five players for a perfect solar circle and I am going to try running an Exalted game in 3e that is turned up to 11 even by exalted standards. That being said I have no direct experience running exalted. I’ve run plenty of games but never one this big and weird before.

    Below is my campaign outline. If you would be so kind I would love some feedback on the general structure of it, and anyhting i might be able to do to make it feel very anchored into the world of Creation.

    I’d also find particularly valuable suggestions for iconic named NPCs, artifacts, and locations that are particularly iconic. This may be their only exalted game experience and I want to make sure it’s memorable and iconic.

    Spoiler Warning: On the off chance that any of my players find this:If you Live in NYC, commute to the monthly game with a little dog, and just finished a Nights Black Agents Game. Don't read this summary. You'll spoil the overarching plot.

    Campaign Outline – Exalted 3e

    Solar Creation Theory (Character Creation)
    • Inspiration / Mood: Dawning Power, Session 0
    • After modified character creation, running a small vignette with each player kicking off a storyline or theme they would be interested in. Also designed to give them an intro to system basics, combat, social influence, limit, intimacies, and excellences. Will end the session with each party member diving into a market square as the exaltation takes full effect.
    • Since I have 4 newbies and 1 veteran player from 2e who has not read 3e, for character creation I’m going to do everything normally except the players will have 5 points of merits, not pick charms, and not spend bonus points. They’ll play through these vignettes to see how the system works and then be able to tweak a bit and spend points to finalize creation.
    • Destiny Entanglement Reality: I’ve heard how difficult it can be to keep exalted games focused and so I’ve decided to link the main story missions/events with raising essence level. After the players hit the XP threshold for essence they’ll be able to play the story mission that will raise their essence. This way they can play out however much they want and spend as much time on a side quest or interest as they’d like but they’ll still have a tangible mechanical reason to come back to the main plot.
    Unexpected Exaltation Kata (Mortals -- > Exaltation)
    • Inspiration / Mood: Superhero origin story, High Speed Chase, Battlestar Galactica
    • The party exalts in the middle of the imperial city and must flee via pirate ship (or other means) to Chiaroscuro, taking shelter with an old friend as the realm sends a hastily assembled kill team after them while the full Wyld Hunt is mustered. They must navigate the criminal underworld of Chiaroscuro, escape the trap set for them, and elude or defeat the kill team to escape into the wilds of creation before the full might of the hunt arrives.
    Past Lives Technique (Essence 1--> 2)
    • Inspiration / Mood: Fractured Memories, Unreliable Narrators, and the temptations of power.
    • The Circle begin to receive vision/memories of their past lives. These visions will gradually darken and twist until it is revealed that their first age exalts were true monsters. (These visions are made more powerful as a demon / Yozi is responsible for sending them, slowly adding intimacies in support of the “Perhaps I am a monster” ideas and actively attempting to undermine the party’s confidence in itself). Only by confronting their past incarnations in a (dreamscape / Alternate dimension) can they make peace with what they have done and move forward with who they are. They need to do this quickly, as their bodies lie in state while the Wyld Hunt closes in.
    Sublime Court Assertion (Essence 2--> 3)
    • Inspiration / Mood: Courtroom Drama, Backstabbing & political maneuvering. Q Episodes of Star Trek.
    • The party will be contacted by a mysterious benefactor (see Sidereal Manipulation Proxy below), letting them know about a court case in which they have been indicted. They will have to find a way to Yu-Shan or be tried in absentia. Upon arrival they will discover that there is a trial being held to examine and pass ruling on the cycle of celestial exaltations. The circle will have to prepare an argument for continuing the cycle of celestial exaltations against the Scarlet Empress and Chejop Kejak. (I plan to research ROT5K era legal theory and codes to run this adventure, should put a very non-American justice system spin on it.) Whatever way the trial goes the party will likely depart Yu-Shan as a “last chance” and be closely observed for a period of time to prove the worth of the cycle of celestial exaltation.
    Unending Labors Form (Essence 3--> 4)
    • Inspiration / Mood: Epic of Gilgamesh / Trials of Hercules
    • The circle will be informed after a time that the bureaucracy of Yu-Shan has gathered almost enough information to make a final ruling. The time has come to perform their final deed that will be considered in the reckoning. This can be augmented by any idea that the players may have but the primary idea put forward by Yu-Shuan is to appeal to the Five elemental dragons at the elemental poles. If the lynchpin of the terrestrial exalted’s power backs the cycle of exaltation that would be a powerful argument for continuing it. Each dragon will request a service to prove the circle worthy of its support. After they have done so the earth dragon of the imperial mountain will suggest that they get gaia’s support. This will conclude the arc of heaven and Yu-Shuan will make a decision about the celestial exaltations. The wheels of bureaucracy of course will turn slowly and not reveal the final result until the end of the next arc.

    Creation Stabilization Method (Essence 4--> 5)
    • Inspiration / Mood: Game of Thrones, Fate/Stay Night
    • By now the party will have gathered enough information or had enough hints to suspect something sinister occurring behind the scenes. They’ll get the first tangible glimpses of the green sun prince’s plan.
    • With the court case finally resolved the Scarlet Empress will return to the realm. Depending on the players actions the Empress may return with a favorable view of the solar exalted, or she may double down on her hatred of them. Regardless, entrenched interests within the empire will oppose her, weakening the realm at a point it desperately needs to remain strong. They’ll either have to aid her, or tear the realm down and build something in its place fast enough to matter.
    • If Yu-Shan has continued the cycle of exaltations then the players will have to race across the land, finding the newly woken exalted and protecting them against predation, trying quickly to whip them into a fighting force.
    • If Yu-Shan has ended the cycle of exaltations then the players plight will be ever more desperate as they try to find and recruit the only remaining experienced celestial exalted for the coming war.
    Darkness Resistance Finalization (Endgame)
    • Inspiration/Mood: The Stormlight Archive, First Lord’s Fury
    • This will occur shortly after Realm Stabilization Method.
    • The pits of hell open and the Green Sun princes march to war. An all-out conflict erupts across creation for the very soul of Gaia herself. The players lead the armies and allies of creation into battle against the hell spawned armies of the Yozi. Desperate fight after desperate fight erupt as the players battle to determine what the prince’s goal is.

    Subplots
    • Celestial Recovery Theory: The party will begin to feel their focus pulled to geomantic sites of power, and gradually discover the manses and artifacts they possessed in the first age.
    • Sidereal Manipulation Proxy: There is a 6th player that may be able to join the in a few months’ time. I had an idea for the player to play the sidereal trying to help the circle achieve its goal. This sidereal would be part of the gold faction and only ever had contact by written word with their master. Thus minimizing the effect of the great curse. Basically tweaking small things for them from behind the scenes and eventually revealing themselves to help the party during Sublime Court Assertion (See Above).
    • Lunar Joining Methodology: I love the idea of a lunar mate without making that character subservient to the Solars. I’m planning on having 2-3 of the lunar mates having been around for a long time so they can provide context and history for the Solars, and having the remaining ones come from valued npcs of the other characters. No better drama than having your SO be the perfect soul bonded match for your Circle mate.
    • Veil Piercing Technique: The party will gradually realize that they should have run into other celestial exalted by now. They will eventually realize that something is blocking the celestial exalted this is a new sorcerous working or power of the imperial manse discovered by the Scarlet Empress shortly before she disappeared. Effectively they each have a “perception filter” over them that prevents them from seeing anyone but their fate bonded others. (So the circle, their lunar mates, their sidereal ally, and an opposing Abyssal Circle). Terrestrial exalted are unaffected by this and are slowly but surely killing the other celestial exalted one by one.
    • Primordial Wyld Duplication: The long term plot running behind the scenes of this game is going to be that the Green Sun princes have been slowly gobbling up space in the wyld, slowly replacing it and encircling creation. Eventually they will move quickly and claim the “near wyld”. Creation then finds itself flanked on all sides not by chaos but by unrelenting horror. The inferals will be solidly cast as bad guys and I plan to take a full Lovecraftian, chaos gods, and Body horror tack with them. If I do it right they’ll be cast as something that even the Abyssals would join with the Solars to fight. I might event double down and have several loved ones of the PCs disappear under mysterious circumstances to become infernals.
    • Player Input Method: Each player will provide me a brief outlined story of a subplot they’d like to see. My favorite way of putting this comes from an earlier game I was in: “Choose your apocalypse” For those familiar with 7th sea this is like a 5 step story from that. You give me the high concept and a sentence or two on each step of the way and I improvise from there. This has worked really well for other games and gives the outline of a story the player would like to play but saves the major twists for me to implement and take care of.


    So thats my first stab at a campaign outline. As I said any feedback you'd care to give would be most appreciated.

      Ex3 is a bit mechanically involved and has some counter-intuitive crunch elements (withering and decisive attacks, battle groups, massive charm choices).

      For newbies I may suggest that simplifying sessions initially could be helpful -- focus on one element of the game rules that at a time perhaps? Social influence, flat-out combat, environmental effects, travel, My game's GM started with us as mortals and exalted one of us at a time - a similar approach might help with your group?

      As far as charms go perhaps suggest that your players focus on a handful of charm-trees beyond those of critical importance in combat (attack, defence, resistance?. There is nothing wrong with loading up on their supernal ability too! Time for them to shine. I find it hard to not to be a bit of a dilettante with charms, so I have a wide base of them with a little top-end capability -- not a bad strategy but when compared to specialists in a Circle I'm les capable.

          Are the new players new to RPGs in general or just new to Exalted? Either way, the Exalted 301 on RPGnet or the 3e wiki is a good introduction to the game and combat. http://exalted3e.wikidot.com/ess:combat-301

          I will also note that the power level players can potentially employ in Exalted, especially if you’re planning to turn it to 11, can cause issues with preplanning. Never assume the players can’t do something. In fact, with Solar Exalted it’s better to assume that, if they want to do something, they’ll succeed. Thus the question is more “what do you want to do and are you willing to live with the consequences?” than “do you succeed?”

          Reading over your plan, I can see a couple of potential pitfalls. Your players might deduce that there is a person pulling strings behind everything as early as the Past Lives Technique, so you might need to be ready to toss out Chejop as the initial “string puller” and then reveal that he’s been corrupted by the demons or cut to the Green Sun Princes entering Creation early or something.

          Also, rather than having them go in search of Gaia or the Dragons, what about having them go in search of the Sun? Either braving the Jade Pleasure Dome or finding a way to travel to the actual Sun and stand before that nuclear furnace in judgement. They might consider that more fitting than finding the Dragons, I dunno.

          And, I don’t want to tell you to toss out your idea for a plot, but have you considered running a huge war between the Realm and the Lunars or something like that? I’m pretty sure you could turn that up to 11 if you wanted. The Dragon Blooded book and Realm book should be out relatively soon (and if you know someone who backed the DB Kickstarter you could get a draft copy of the DB rules now) and the Lunars book should, hopefully, be out next year. By then you’d probably have set up all the necessary groundwork for a huge war or whatever. And the rules for Warstriders are in Arms of the Chosen which is out right now.
