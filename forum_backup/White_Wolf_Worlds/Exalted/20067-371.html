                                    12-16-2016, 09:53 AM
                                    The charm elapses first. The motes will the return at the normal rate. This also applies to any other charm with a duration other than instant, or things like attuning artefacts.
                                    So, for example, the Red Fox, master thief, steals a daiklaive from his rival Elegant Blade at 3am, and commits it for 5m so he can escape with it. Over the day, he recovers all the motes he spent on stealth charms, but is 5m down from his maximum, because of the daiklaive. But later, at 6pm, the Red Fox gives it to his wife, the renowned warrior Pinnacle of War, and so decommits the 5m. As you regain 5m an hour, he will still be down 5m for the next hour, as that's how long it takes to recover them. However, if Elegant Blade bursts into the room just after the Red Fox decommitted the motes, and they all roll Join Battle, then it'll only take 1 round for him to get back those 5 missing motes.
