    In Rathess, in the previous editions, there was a major necropolis of Solar tombs. Thses go all the way back to the Primordal War. I assume that every celestial exalted who died before the usurpation was buried in a major tomb, and that their later incarnations would have the option to reclaim their gear. After the Usurpation, many Solars were buried in lavish tombs to try to keep their ghosts down, and any equipment that survived the fight was buried with them. As a storyteller, I have included things like half a warstrider in a Solar tomb.

            In my creation while mortals of the realm see humble but sufficient funerary services the dragon blooded themselves receive much grander funerals often with several days of mourning services and celebrations of life and deeds before a procession equal parts parade and mourning escorts the body to the families crypt-palace. These palaces are often large grand homes in the style of the family's ancestral manse. During the days of mourning a personal room is constructed for the deceased Nobel where he is played to rest among the treasures and luxuries he chose not to pass on in will. The crypts are tended by mortal family members trained in the arts of exorcism and care for the dead. Heroes whose bodies were lost are laid to rest in effigy at the least still receiving similar honor. Dragon blooded who died without honor or after being banished are often still laid to rest in modest "by dynasty standards" crypts close to their homes. These family palaces do in fact cast shadows into the underworld where the ghosts of dragon blooded past play their games of Nobel dead. Their constant drives to one up each other keep them divided and weak but should the Nobel houses of dead dragons ever unite they would make quite the notable force.

                    Originally posted by The Unsung Hero View Post
                    Here's a second set of rails for this topic. Have you ever had a tomb for a different exalt type in your game? Any Sidereal tombs? Lunar tombs? Dragon-Blooded tombs even? I'm not surprised by the lack of Abyssal tombs, but if Solars used to make their own tombs, why wouldn't there be others as well?
                    Oh, good point.

                    I have included Dragon-Blooded tombs for FA Chosen who were favored servants of Solars, often built around the Solar's tomb. I've also done a twin crypt thing for a Solar/Lunar couple.

                    If I had to headcanon some overall trends with a gun to my head, I would go with:

                    Dragon-Bloods have the most tombs of any Chosen, with a good chunk from the First Age, and a ton of them from the Shogunate. However, those tombs are more likely to contain "mundane," wealth and symbolic representations of their possessions, as DBs tend to inherit stuff, rather than bury it. There also plenty of family mausoleums, since DBs value family. In the Realm, I'm thinking the Immaculate Philosophy discourages over-attachment to the dead, reminding people they have finally ascended to unity with the Dragons. Monuments are more common, with tombs only for exceptional (or at least exceptionally powerful and wealthy) individuals.

                    Sidereals are fundamentally agents of Heaven. I think the closest offiicial thing they have is a Wall of Honor kinda thing, like the CIA has at their HQ. Unofficially, I'd say friends of the Sid (almost always fellow Sids) will often take time to bury or burn the body somewhere in Creation the deceased loved dearly, or they all pitch in and pay for a tomb in Sijan. In any case, Starmetal is rare, and departmental resources are always tight, so phat lewtz are going to be held by the Bureau, likely for the next incarnation.

                    Lunars are going to be the most split. First Age Lunar tombs are probably only slightly less rare than Solars (there are now far more Lunars than Solars, but they were still less likely to get tombs). However, those tombs are on the whole less ostentatious, with a few stand-out exceptions. They may or may not contain magical grave goods, as I headcanon most Lunars in the 2nd Age are pragmatic to a fault, and thus likely to rob tombs at every opportunity. Shogunate and Realm-Era Lunars who fall are likely to be interred in a place of natural beauty, such as under an artistically carved boulder on a mountain-top, or their ashes are scattered at such a place, and the modern tombs are crap for grave goods (what Lunar can afford leaving behind Artifacts?). Due to the ever-shifting lines of the war with the Realm, a tomb built today could be a bathhouse for Dynasts in a century or two, so I don't see Lunars putting much time and effort into "traditional" mausoleums. Exceptions might exist on the Caul or similar places.

                    On the whole, only the First Age really afforded the kind of raw weath and privilege required to erect and provision structures we think of when we say "tomb," because we are likely imagining those structures in Nexus that harbor great wealth and items of magical power. If you broaden the definition, they may become more common, but culture and simple pragmatism is against large, formal construction projects for this sort of thing.

                                        I'm thinking that most of the big dungeon tombs are Solar. Dragon-blooded pass their goods back to their family, and probably are interred in the family mausoleum. Great heroes probably get more than an average family member in terms of space, but that's probably about it. I suspect most Lunars don't like the idea of a permanent structure. I've always viewed them as more attuned to the cycle of reincarnation, and don't need the sort of immortality in death that a god-king needs. I also don't think Sidereals need much in terms of tombs. I do envision a 'heroe's graveyard' in Heaven where dead Sids are buried in reward for their service, with a memorial wall. Sids are probably pretty good about having their goods handed down to whom they want to have them, rather than be buried with them. Abyssals build them now and live in them. Infernals will very much build great tombs if they get the chance.

                                        As always, exceptions exist. There will be first age Lunar tombs built by Solars, for example, but I picture most Lunars considering them gauche. There will always be the DB general who conquered enough territory that he can rule it like a god-king of old and build a great tomb... but it probably becomes a mausoleum for his family after a few generations. Or it becomes the centrepiece of a Valley of Kings type thing, which is really the same thing, but on a bigger scale.
