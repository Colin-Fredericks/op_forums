By DotFA you have setting quotes saying the Legendary Breeding is basically gone because of interbreeding with non-DBs. The Shogunate and Contagion hit this even further.

        This narrative is partly reflected in the Breeding rules, which are supposed to show that the Realm cares a lot about marriages in the same way that Pure-bloods from Harry Potter do, that Lookshy cares less and is more focused on having a ton of okay-blooded DBs versus a few super-pure-blooded ones, and that outcastes are pretty much just Exalted out of sheer luck and a drop of Dragon's Blood from their great-grandfather who, like, nabbed a tavern wench while on campaign 400 years ago, or whose family contained DBs before the Contagion but they're all gone until a remote scion Exalts the better part of a millennium later. The mechanical effect of this narrative is that Breeding is cheap for Dynasts, more expensive for the Seventh Legion, and fuck-all ridiculous for outcastes (the outcaste chargen rules are really, really bad, but that's neither here nor there).

                        The Empire of Alligators isn't canon, but I do think it's an excellent example of this kind of project done wrong. That is to say, the Lunar did it wrong, not the writer did it wrong. Dzilbilchan is a cautionary tale in designing a vicious and unsustainable society that can't survive the death of its architect, to say nothing of being a distasteful exercise throughout. The basic idea that Dragon-Blooded beastmen are opposed to the Realm by default is a solid one, however. The prejudice of most of Creation against beastmen would not be lessened by their Exalted status.

                        There are two components of the plan. First, the Lunar needs a clan of loyal beastmen who can be culturally swayed towards accepting Exalted members of their number. This doesn't seem to be a problem for a lot of Lunars. Use of Fish or Fowl Gestation and Tiger and Sultan's Daughter removes the need for forced bestiality. The clan should be small and relatively isolated (aquatic or flying beastmen are ideal for this) to minimize cultural influence, migration and predation. They should be as stable and sustainable a society as is possible. Locating an out of the way jade mine or other important resources with the assistance of spirits is recommended. Given that this plan will take at least decades, now would be an excellent opportunity to task bind demons to amass resources for your new society's benefit.

                        Next, the Lunar needs beastman children with Dragon-Blooded exaltation potential. Using the above mentioned Charms, The Steward need only seduce an Outcaste or Dynast in female form (using Twin Faced Hero if necessary) and imprint the unborn children with beastman traits from any of the species of her heart's blood library. Seducing a dozen randy Dragon-bloods should be no obstacle for a Lunar suited to the task. With Tiger ans Seahorse Dynasty she can guarantee triplets with her clutch of eagle-eggs and repeat the process as many times as is necessary to grow a large stable with which to breed a future beastman gens. To avoid the problem of half-sibling-incest, she can simply wear different human forms during the pregnancy so that her children are born to different biological mothers.

                        The Lunar should raise and educate the first generation, her own children, teaching them, in addition to a robust general education, sorcery, the forging of arcane contracts and the working of jade. She should draw up cultural practices calculated to maximise the prosperity of her clan and its new ruling caste, appoint a strong successor, and then depart.

                        Within a few short generations, provided the new gens does not collapse to infighting, there should be a strong new faction of dragon-bloods in the world, armed with all a Lunar mastermind can grant them and friendly to the interests of the Children of the Moon.

                        Wait wait, I forgot you need to have sex in the Wyld to make beastmen without bestiality.

                        NEVER MIND, another plan defeated by really stupid Lunar mechanics. This plan only works for E6 elders who have access to Beast Lords of the Blessed Isle.
