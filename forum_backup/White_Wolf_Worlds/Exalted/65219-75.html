    Originally posted by glamourweaver View Post

    I think the juxtaposition is what makes it work. It's far better than the cheap-shock-value the Infernals book went for with her.

    And far more like one of the indulgences in kindness that a character like Azhrarn, Prince of Demons, Night's Master would allow himself to better refine the subtle beauty of his cruelties.
    Both of these, +1. Maleas is a terrible, horrible place still, and the juxtaposition of how wretched it is with how much idolatrous love she receives from GSP's and Demon's is supposed to be a big giant "Some Shit Aint Right Here" spotlight for the Players. I love the Cackling B-Movie horror villain cliche's the Infernals get saddled with, because in my games I really play up the facet of the Yozi that they are utterly alien in thinking. The whole thing, in total, comes back to the Lovecraftian nature of the Primordials and Malfeas in general.

    Plus, as Glamourweaver said, it highlights how evil some demons/Infernals are when they give a box of candied dreamfruit to a little girl with a pat on the head, and then turn around and discuss the best way to inflict the Brain Wasting Plague on Chiaroscuro. I try my best to sell the utterly unfeeling divide for maximum shock value. I actually got the inspiration from 80s big business movies, where Corporate Exec #1 will give his secretary chocolate on her birthday and then enter a meeting and discuss bulldozing an orphange.


    Plus I have a weak spot for little kids. I teach Elementary School.

                                                        Originally posted by Dark Lord Potato View Post
                                                        An Infernal with the memories of Merela or Brigid would be an incredible advantage.
                                                        Not as much as you'd think. Even the clearest of First Age memories don't do anything more than provide some die bonuses, at least according to 2e. The Exaltation has to adjust its Essence pattern to the soul of its new host; even the relatively subtle shifts going from one Solar host to the next render your old Charms and spells and things unusable until you relearn them, for instance. With the frankly massive Essence modifications transforming a Solar Exaltation into an Infernal one, such an Exalt would be starting pretty much from scratch no matter how well they remember the First Age, aside from possibly if it's enough to get them back to old labs and such.

                                                        (Also, Brigid's Exaltation probably got the good old Lytek-scrub sometime between inventing Sorcery and the Usurpation, but that's besides the point.)
