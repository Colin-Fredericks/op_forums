                                            Originally posted by Neall View Post

                                            Oh indeed! Though I think most people complained about other things than that. (Some, I'm sure, complained about how their 20th level wizards were severely nerfed.)

                                            5e at least acknowledges the asymmetric power curve compared to the level curve and builds upon that as a feature, not a bug. If you're aware of the Linear Fighter, Quadratic Wizard problem, you can put in compensation mechanics or elements to highlight the positive aspects of that.
                                            Does it? I'll be honest, I've not played above level 8 with 5th ed, or read the DMG.


                                            Anyway, Star Wars. So the thing of course is that yes, Jedi are better than non-Jedi. Some SW roleplaying games say "you can't play a Jedi". Some SW roleplaying games say "you can play a young inexperienced Jedi or a skilled bounty hunter/smuggler/scout/pilot/soldier with a bunch of skills in all sorts of areas." And in a small number of those, that's actually balanced!
                                            (In a lot of games, it's supposed to be balanced, but isn't.)
                                            With computer games, you generally either play a bounty hunter/smuggler/scout/pilot/soldier with a ton of equipment, a Jedi/Sith, or an inexperienced bounty hunter/smuggler/scout/pilot/soldier who then becomes a Jedi.
                                            Often you are a Jedi, and your companions are smugglers, Wookies, Droids, younger Jedi, etc. PC and NPC companions don't need to be balanced though.

                                            That is, of course, how the films work. Luke is a farmboy with a bit of force power who is a good pilot, Han is an experienced fast-talking smuggler and awesome shot and a great pilot. Chewbacca is really strong and tough, has a laser-crossbow, and is a great mechanic and decent pilot. C3PO speaks 6 billion languages, R2D2 is a brilliant hacker and mechanic. Kinobi is an OP DMPC, but the DM quickly kills him off so it's fine.
                                            So, the guy with the force is the weakest character in every other way (least money, least skills, also if he uses his powers he'll give himself away to the evil empire who hunts those like him), so it's kind of balanced.

                                            Anyway, you can use that as an example of how to balance a Solar with other splats. But certainly it's worth bearing in mind that while some SW game effectively balance this, most try and fail.
