        I can't honestly imagine this is something that is actually going to come up in your game*. But historically speaking it's going to be a rag of some kind with herbs to cover the smell. I imagine a Collar of Dawn's Cleansing Light would also take care of it.

        For neatness, I'm inclined to suggest that Maiden Tea prevents menstruation. Like modern oral contraceptives do (I don't envision the people of Creation drinking placebo tea seven days a month).

        *That said, I had a game where the Snapping Turtle Lunar would attack folks whilst they were pooping**... so...

        **Menstruation is a significant, oft overlooked, issue for half the population, but (almost) everybody poops. The point is, that there are perfectly normal bodily functions that do not normally get screen time in an RPG. My game has referenced the menstrual cycle only in so far as it pertains to characters getting pregnant: I imagine my players getting squicked out if we went into any more detail than that, certainly I would be made uncomfortable by the graphic description of primitive or inadequate hygiene products.

        Tl:dr

        PCs and the wealthy shouldn't have to worry about this. The poor are going to be weighed down in hygiene problems, but describing visceral, graphic privation is going to make people uncomfortable.

                            I know it's not exactly an Exalted thing, but a classic trope of the Eastern mythology is the 'monster/god/entity' sealed inside a character, and I was thinking how to best rule that.

                            I think something on the line of an Artifact is the easiest solution, using the Evocations as the powers granted by the entity, even if an Artifact that's internal to the character can be a little overpowered since it can't be removed... but apparently shadow tattoo can have Evocations, and you can't really remove a tattoo from someone. Considering that this artifact is not actually doing anything aside for giving Evocations, I think it's a fair trade.

                            The problem is... Artifacts are good for a mechanical standpoint, but creating such Artifacts using the actual rules gets you a very lengthy creation process, while usually, the sealing requires... days at most. So, some kind of Story Merit that works as Artifact but it's not would be more appropriate?

                            What do you think?
