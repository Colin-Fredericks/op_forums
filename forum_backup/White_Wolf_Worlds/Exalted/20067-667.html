    Originally posted by Beast of Bitter Oblivion View Post

    Does grappling manoeuvre specifically require two arms to perform though ? What about snakes who have no limbs or clinching someone by sitting on them or classic pinning someone to a wall with one arm by their throat etc?

    Snakes don't have the Amputee flaw - a snake, or other animal adapted for constriction that doesn't have gripping limbs, has other adaptations that make up for that. Whereas a human's main grappling tools are their hands and arms - lacking them puts them at a disadvantage. As for the "pinning someone by one hand to the wall" maneuver, that's usually done to a much weaker opponent, one for whom a bit of a penalty would be fairly trivial. And in any case, for a typical human, the other hand is still there, and still a potential threat that the victim has to keep in mind if they start struggling. So even if someone stunts such a grapple as using only one hand, I wouldn't penalize them. But if they have the Amputee flaw, I would impose the -3. If you want to be a one-armed grappler, be good enough to soak the penalty.
