                                        Speaking of, I loooove the new Ma-Ha-Suchi and Raksi.

                                        Ma-Ha-Suchi is completely different, but I like him as a grumpy old veteran who may or may not have an overly nostalgic view of the First Age even if he wants something rather different in theory and practice way better than a generic hentai villain. Meanwhile, Raksi has been changed little, other than that she's perfectly effing sane. This makes her both far more sympathetic (because while she's still a monster, she's a very human monster who desperately wants to avenge her people and friends while not making the same mistakes the Realm did and does), and far more terrifying (because her tyrannical hedonism is an entirely deliberate way to intimidate and entice; she's not a decadent, she's a ruthlessly competent politician and sorceress who simply parties as hard as she works, and with just as much concern for those in the way).

                                                    Oh yeah, and I'm guessing Essence Fever is what they're calling Lunar Limit now.

                                                    This is an unmitigated improvement. It even ties into how obsessive the Silver Pact is; there's a flaw in the very Exaltation that makes them prone to holding grudges and acting on them. A Lunar is very much an outsider and trickster hero; hurt her, and she remembers. Especially since that for the Lunars, the Great Curse was a mixed blessing. Yes, they do get intense about revenge, but the rage is fed by things that have truly hurt them in the past and is completely under their control; it can be focused, tamed, turned into something good, it just can't ever burn out. So, they don't see it as a problem, or if they do, it's one they've made peace with, shrugging and politely sweeping one of them having a bad day (read here: foaming at the mouth, declaring oaths of eternal hatred, screaming berserk fury, usually at the same time) under the rug to deal with the fallout later.

                                                    I am seriously hoping we get mechanics that build Essence Fever for benefits, in other words ("I AM TOO PISSED OFF TO LISTEN TO PALTRY DESTINY AND PROPHECY, PUNY SIDEREAL!").
