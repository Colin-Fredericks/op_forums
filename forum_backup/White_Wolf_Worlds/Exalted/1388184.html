    vampire hunter D has Fangs (at the Gate)

    05-13-2020, 07:49 PM
    So, I just got my first copy of Fangs at the gate (I skipped the earlier manuscript release figuring it'd be too rough to digest). So, I'm reading this for the first time and thought I'd share some thoughts on my first read through. up to Chapter 4 so far, but decided to start posting so my thoughts are fresh:

    Chapter One....nothing really new here to comment on.

    Chapter Two. The Silver Pact feels more like 1E than it did in 2. Also some stuff sounds familiar, but can't recall if it's stuff I'm recalling from 1E or W:tA (I uinfortunatly lost those books when I moved last to look). Like the newer Ma Ha Suchi, now he seems to have some goal to his rather violent actions (and is actually less violent and more organized). Rakshi comes off as less crazy. definite improvement. And I'm now thinking who out there would be a good Shahan-ya to some Lunars I want to make (hmm, who would teach the Lunar daughter of the Empress herself?). Sha'a Oka almost feels like Black Fang from W:tA Bastet book.

    Page 36, Thank ou to the artist for showing a real tyrant lizard, wth a cape of feathers down it's back

    Reading on Abyssals and Infernals though...I was under the impression they were going a different route this edition and making the origins and connects to the Solars for both vague and not set in canon. This reads a lot like they were in 2E. I hope I'm wrong in my reading though, esp with the Infernals (if we get bloated hantai-monster loli back, I swear....!)


    Chapter 3 I buzzed through. This kind of setting chapter I will read more indepth later since I love setting-dense stuff like this, but for now want to get a feel for the whole book. I did read over Luthe now though, since that was a 2E locatoion I really enjoyed. And now I miss the Traitorspawn


    on Chapter 4 now, which is all crunchy stuff. So I'll back when I have more to say.

    But till then I just wanna say... IÄ! SILENT PEARL FTAGN!
