    Nellens Lomin
    Water Aspect
    Nellens Lomin
    Description: Lomin has Aqua-touched hair, and a build that is far better built for speed than it is for strength, with his small frame and height of 5"1'. His veins show deep tints of ebony, he smells of fresh but light rainfall, his eyes are a soft turquoise and are typically focused on something he deems important, and it always seems a bit harder to breath if one is too close to him.
    Background: Lomin is a small ray of sunshine and hope for the Nellens family, being the pedigree son of a Water and Air aspect couple, and also the third of four kids. His blood was by far the most pure, and such was shown when he exalted at an early age of 11 after coming into brief but stable contact with an artifact that had slept innate for 150 years, awakening the artifact alongside himself.

    Lomin knows deeply of the responsibility he has to attempt to uplift House Nellens into better standings, and it does little to daunt him. He expects himself to be able to rise to the ocassions as they come, and to overcome or outdo most, if not all, of his competition. As such, he can only see failure as a bitter and heart-piercing moment before having to re-evaluate.
    Now 14, Lomin is awfully studious. Though such isn't uncommon in those touched by the Dragon of Water, he has even started to harness the understandings of Sorcery; a gift taught to him by the blade he awoke. As such, when one can't find him at The House of Bells training his skill with the sword itself, they can likely find him at the practice grounds of places not unlike the Heptagram, training his understanding of the sword's soul in his personal time.
    Lomin cares little for connections with others, having taken on the Nellens belief that you can't depend on any of the other houses awfully early, but he will not activly seek to get away from others so long as they aren't disruptive. Try to actively disrupt him though, and you'll find a diminutive short temper backed up by sorcery and skill. As a detriment of this, his social graces tend to be lacking, and he has very poor perception of his surroundings.
    His ultimate goal is to snatch the seat of the Throne from underneath all other contenders, and raise Nellens into the upper standings of the houses.
    Aspect: Water Anima: Black Rippling Pools reflect Lomin and all his actions like mirrors within the confines of his anima, every separate reflection showing a similar yet different sea serpent.
    Attributes: Str 2, Dex 5, Sta 2 : Cha 1, Man 2, App 4 : Per 1, Int 5, Wit 5
    Abilities: Archery 1, Athletics 3, Awareness 3, Integrity 3 (Stay On Topic), Investigation 1, Linguistics 1, Lore 4, Melee 4 (Samori), Occult 4 (Shape Sorcery), Presence 2, Resistance 2, Ride 1
    Merits: Influence (Nellens(2)), Artifact (Samori, Reflection's Edge(3)), Well-Bred (2), Resources (1), Language (Old Realm(1)), Language (High Realm(1))
    Intimacies: Defining Principle – "I shall see House Nellens rise to the top!" ; Defining Tie – Samori (Guarded Ownership, Collaborative Understanding) ; Major Principle – "For there to be competition, there must first be a threat." ; Minor Tie – Libraries (Safe-havens) ; Minor Principle – "Leave all that relationship junk alone until I can help House Nellens with it."
    Willpower: 6 Essence: 1 Peripheral: 20/27 Personal: 12/12 Committed: 7
    Resolve: 4(5) Guile: 1 Join Battle: 8 Parry: 5(6) Dodge: 3
    Soak: 2 Hardness: 0 Disengage: 5 Rush: 9
    Health Track: -0/-1/-1/-2/-2/-4/I
    Attacks:
    Unarmed: 9 dice (4 Decisive), Damage 7, Bashing/Brawl/Grappling/Natural
    Samori (Daiklaive): 13 dice (10 Decisive), Damage 12, OW 4, Attunment 7, Lethal/Melee/Balanced
    Charms:
    Occult: Hidden Secrets Whisper, Spirit-Detecting Mirror Technique, Secret Wind Revelation, Spirit
    Grounding Shout, Terrestrial Circle Sorcery (Control Spell: Impervious Sphere of Water)
    Melee: Stoking Bonfire Style, Flame-borne Interception, Graceful Flowing Defense
    Lore: Careful Insight-Gathering Study, Elemental Concentration Trance

    Lomin has spent significant time learning with help of his weapon, Samori. Samori, Reflection's Edge is a Black and Blue Jade Daiklaive that has been a slumbering weapon and has not attuned to any Dragon-Blooded in the past 150 years. In reality, the weapon refuses to bond to any Dragon-Blooded that does not have the Well-Bred Merit, or any Dragon-Blooded that is a Fire or Wood Aspect.
    The commitment bonus to the weapon is up in the air, and can be whatever is convenient for the storyteller.
    It has a slightly steeper commitment cost as 7m, and Lomin has unlocked the first two evocations of the blade(8 bonus points) These charms should both fall into the idea of playing with illusions possessing the Psyche keyword or controlling the battlefield immediately around Lomin with difficult terrain, and both should be powerful enough to charge a willpower for them. The blade if slightly vain, and loves nothing more than being able to make illusions of itself.
