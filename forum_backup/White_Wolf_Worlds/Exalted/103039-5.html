                                Originally posted by TSk View Post
                                Well, that's where a good storyteller comes in. I wouldn't approve tyrant lizard as a spirit shape, because I don't like to get the idea that players are treating their characters as pure statblocks, but I have nothing against PCs acquiring the shape ingame.
                                You don't put spirit shapes off. They're totemic to a character. You have them from the moment of Exaltation, like your anima banner.

                                That said, I'm not going to ignore the obvious problems of that shape. For one thing, good luck going anywhere indoors. For another, trying to win a clinch roll with arms that can't even reach the middle of your torso, when your opponent is composed of raw electricity for instance, is probably not a good idea. Especially since your opponent can just banish the tentacles reflexively if s/he loses.
                                It wouldn't be likely to come to that. The formless not-Lunar would die.

                                Also, I'm guessing you got your 6B/6L stats for diamond exoskeleton from the stone body mutation. Stone is generally a poor substitute for diamond.
                                I think that you need to read the text of the mutation again. It mentioned crystal lattices as an option, just like how flight can net you wings or a helium bladder for LTA static lift, or any number of other possibilities.

                                Also, given the use of a large, heavy hammer, diamond's vaunted hardness doesn't really pay off too well, as with extreme hardness comes extreme brittleness. In spite of the JJBA series 4 title, you can rest assured that diamond is not actually unbreakable.

                                Has anyone seriously ever chosen tyrant lizard for thematic reasons?
                                There are an awful lot of options for it. The name itself, for Lunars who were autocrats prior to their Second Breath. It might be the greatest terror of the character's homeland. The character could be a great hunter with keen eyesight and smell, and utilize ambushes to bring prey to her. You could have a mystic that chews on bones to gain insights. It's not really confining for any reason. Great terrors, aka northern titans, weren't, either. It was probably more common in 1e, when the only Strength-adder took 15 turns to warm up and lasted for maybe half that. And was based off of Charisma, with a Manipulation Charm as its prerequisite.
