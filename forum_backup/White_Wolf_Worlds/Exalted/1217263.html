      My campaign started out as an attempt to homage shounen anime, and thus opened with a PvP martial arts tournament. A Wyld Hunt crashed the tournament before the final round, and I dodged having to work out mechanics for the Sidereal-Martial-Arts-wielding BronzeSid leading the Hunt by having her and the SMA-wielding GoldSid who hosted the tournament cancel each other out. The rest of the Hunt was run with Core Quick Character stats.

      From there, the party sailed to Champoor, got tied up in a bunch of divine / criminal intrigue, and is currently in the process of fighting multiple gods. I'm using Fakharu's statblock from Core for Tenepeshu herself, and I also worked in a few other Core statblocks (Fogsharks, Greenmaws, a grab bag of mortals) during their time in the city, but I've had to cobble together stats for some of the gods myself.

      After Champoor... well, some of my players read these forums, so I can't get too detailed, but I am planning to use some more non-Solar, non-DB Exalts. Martial Arts and Evocations are useful for any types, you can sorta fake Abyssals with Solar Charms... right now I think using Lunar NPCs would be the biggest challenge, but thankfully they're next in line.

      Oh, I will say that I thiiiiiink the Sidereal Excellency in Core is broken. They're weak on defense since they can never add more than (Essence) to a static value, but the Target Number reduction is very efficient. Bending fate to work in their favor is core enough to their concept I don't think I can get rid of it entirely, but I I'm going to try capping it at TN 5, rather than 4, next time a Sidereal rolls against my players. We'll see if that's enough or if it needs further tweaking. (Maybe a cost adjustment.)

              My players are all new to Exalted, and we've only had our first session. We'll do one a month i think. We play in the West, and will do a lot of island hopping.

              So far, they are on tutorial island, learning mechanics. They were all at a festival when it was attacked by bandits looking for a relic, so now they're caught up in plot to secure a key to a long lost mausoleum, which has sunk into a swamp. The Mausoleum has a really powerful "call" to the Underworld so ghosts and nightmare beasts are flocking there, causing a shadowlands taint.

              There are 3 challenges ahead of them, related to Fire, Wind, and Wood (Mausoleum is Earth). After "fixing" all 4 challenges, the elemental restraints of an ancient Leviathan will be broken by a Deathlord who used the essence of them solving the elemental challenges as a geomantic lockbreaker. The freed leviathan will cause a massive rampage.

              Thats the main story for Arc 1. Arc 2 will deal with the fallout of that and the Realm using it as a casus belli to "pacify" the West

              Edit: antagonists. So far, bandits and zombies. An Abyssal made a big enterance at the last minute of play and is about to fight the Solars. He has a Liminal flunkie and a Golem undead construct. Maybe a 2nd abyssal. Lotta ghosts coming up, then depending on what they do, elementals, brass soldiers, Realm Navy, a god or two.

                  TBH, I did find the lack of books annoying, which was one of the reasons I stopped running it.

                  When I did run the game, the PCs started with a plot where their friend had been kidnapped by a coven of sorcerers, the Ascended. Mostly mortal sorcerers (with first circle demon lackeys), but one Dragonblood and one Lunar (though they never found out about the Lunar). So sorcery helped replace charms to some extent.

                  Then they essentially had a choice, with clues to three different groups of enemies. The one already mentioned (but they'd mostly smashed them), the Sons of Shadow (an Ebon-Dragon cult, so I could use demons like Mara and Alveua, or just make my own), or the Crimson Masters, a fanatical Cult of the Illuminated-schismatic sect leading a horde of barbarian horsemen, the Crimson Horde.

                  They ended up going after the Crimson Masters, so my antagonists were mostly Solars leading battle groups of Tiger Warriors.

                  I'd say the game went well, but honestly, other than the Solar NPCs themselves, I found it a bit frustrating how little there was to oppose players (I should say this game was Essence 4, as it was a continuation of our 2nd ed game; it might have been easier for me if they'd been Essence 1. I haven't played an 0xp of Exalted for like 7 years though).

                  So now I'm running Star Wars instead.


                  But my friend has just restarted my old game, and he's setting us up to fight the Realm (which I always had in the background for when the book came out), which should be good. For now (as the book hadn't come out when he started) we're hunting moonsilver tuplas. I guess he's just made up the rules himself. We also fought some elementals and a Hydra (well, the Dawn fought the Hydra) which bled hungry ghosts. That was cool.

                  So, basically, so far, sorcerers, custom monsters/spirits, and Solars.

                  You could also do a game with Abyssals as the main antagonists, and just use the rules for Solars. But my 2nd ed game had Walker in Darkness as the Big Bad, so I didn't want to use Abyssals again.


                  I also play another game, in which I play a monk (Alazar) from the Black Monastery of Leng, who came to the party to learn kung-fu so he could go back and defeat the Nephwrack known as He Who Shall Not Be Described, who took over his monastery and sent the other monks insane.
                  The STs run with it, so we've mostly been fighting/investigating ghosts, spectres, mortwrights, etc, and we're hunting a Nephwrack. We also fought some giant acid-spitting worm-monsters on our way to the monastery,

                  That group is not very combat-optimised (though they're not terrible) so we don't need really powerful foes. But we're still Essence 4 Celestials.

                  STing Bronze Age Exalted

                                  My game is 3 Solars who've mostly been wandering the West pursuing whatever minor issues come up in front of us.

Found out the demon was bound by our old First Age circle-mate who is alive and taunting us for some reason.

I tend to help him where I can with building foes because I have a better eye and patience for that kind of detail. We've only fought 1 DB and that was the Earth Immaculate straight from the back of the core book. No other types yet, but I expect my GM to just fudge stuff based on 2e as needed.

                                      We're about to finish a Solar campaign in the North. The characters haven't fought any other exalted cause we want to wait for their books to come out in the distant future. Nonetheless, there has been a lot of supernatural antagonists. We don't care about setting taxonomy, meaning that we don't need to define if a certain entity is an elemental, spirit or whatever to determine if it would or would not have enough power to face the main Solar characters. So overall we improvise stats and custom powers to make things fun for us, and don't aim to make them canon or compatible with everyone else's games or anything.

                                          I'm currently playing in a homebrew PBTA hack of Exalted 3E with full splat support. I'm also running/playtesting a more streamlined hack of EX3 which works on the same mechanical principles, just faster. The latter only has splat support for what the main line does. Prior to the DB book coming out we did what we did for years and just made stuff up/used the existing inspirations from previous editions (mostly 1E) to slot into the mechanical housing of QC's as a base. All I'm really getting out of the actual EX3 books are ideas and fluff, which justifies a purchase. I'm certainly not paying the writers for their mechanics.

                                          That being said, my PBTA group is running 2 Infernals, a Solar, a Sid, and a DB. We mostly bumble around and get into trouble and riff from there. Like an old-school adventuring game. My streamlined group is all Solars, one from each caste and the goal of that game is to overthrow a satrapy in the west to create an independent state able to negotiate with the realm on equal terms. Think of it like a polynesian revolution type thing. It's fun and essentially a byway for myself and my players to learn about ocean-bound cultures.

                                          Incentive is not permission or justification.
