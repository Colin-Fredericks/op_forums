                Originally posted by Lioness View Post
                If you view Dragon-Blooded as interchangeable worthless foes, either the Realm falls or you die trying and the fact it's Realm Year 768 is a pretty good clue of how that one tends to end.
                I'm not really suggesting that any Lunars would sincerely believe such a thing, rather than using it as a carefully cultivated insult that cuts right to the heart of Dragon Blooded pride, but I also feel as though this is a kind of simplified attitude to how people in life or death struggles, often persisting across generations, characterize their enemies.

                Still, even accounting for how irrational a Lunar that might sincerely believe in such a thing is*, there are ways in which the rhetoric can account for their victory taking a while. The degree to which they're outnumbered (which can itself be incorporated into a wider rhetoric of the Dragon Blooded being a kind of infesting vermin). Attributing their success to their happening to possess the legacy of the Solar Exalted, or the support of the Sidereals. All sorts.

                * And how writing off irrational beliefs as insignificant is kind of to discount the fact that they've actually resulted in countless deaths over the entire length of human history, right up to the modern day. There are quite a few people in the world today locked in struggle against the United States of America who are kind of impervious to any kind of logical argument about the futility of that, and they are still the cause of many many murders.

                                        I was kind of disappointed with the "police procedural" feel of the Investigation charms. I think it really clashes thematically with the rest of the setting and I also hate cop shows. I'd love to see Investigation charms focus more on spies, scavenger lords, blackmailers, courtiers, or basically anything else.

                                        Though I have to admit, the Dragon-Blooded charms were still better for this than the Solars'. There were a significant minority of non-cop Investigation charms. And I guess you could rationalise it to a degree because a lot of Terrestrials with Investigation will be the Empress' Magistrates, and it's plausible that they fulfilled a similar role in the First Age. Even then, I think I'd still rather that Investigation charms designed for other purposes have secondary applications that you could use to catch criminals if that's what your character is about. (Possibly that last part is my anti-cop show bias showing, but I stand by the rest.)
