    Deceitful Surgeon, a void-tainted mortal in Autochthonia, uses unnatural mental influence to control eight young Alchemicals, which he uses to attack the city and wins control through a murder of Emily and trial that discredits the her lover, the noble Luminiferious Physician, Deceitful Surgeon's one-time colleague. Luminiferious Physician builds the ultimate fighting machine, Null, to take back the city but he is overwhelmed by the opposition, unaided by the cowed populace.

    The Blue Bomber is a Blue Jade Alchemical created by Luminiferious Physician in his exile. Despite Luminiferious Physician's pleas not to take up his older late brother's hopeless one man crusade against Deceitful Surgeon's occupation of the city, The Blue Bomber strikes out on his own to win the city back for the people. Unexpectedly he meets his dead predecessor, Null, in battle, and when no one, even Luminiferious Physician, helps his battle he turns his back on humanity.

                I got a super easy one.

                During the Usurpation, a band of Sidereals and Dragon Blooded sailors caught a legendary Eclipse caste upon nothing more than a ragtag ship. At his public execution, he told the crowd that his vast fortune, First Age artifacts and countless secrets would belong to whoever found them first.

                Cut to the second age, and people are still sailing across the West trying to find the legendary belongings of this First Age Eclipse. Among them is a go-lucky but determined Solar (believed by some to be the reincarnation of the First Age Eclipse from legend) who suffers from a strange Wyld mutation and his crew of Exalted pirates. Traveling with this Wyld-mutated Solar include (to name a few) a Dawn Caste feared across the West for his swordsmanship, a Twilight Caste trickster and marksman, a Night Caste navigator and thief who is obsessed with treasure, a No Moon Lunar with exceptional Medicine skills and a polite (if sometimes goofy) Midnight Caste violinist.

                        Five mortal children growing up in various parts of Lookshy get caught outside during a raid by implacable Infernals. The dying Lunar man charges them with guarding creation from these beings and uses a long-lost artifact called Luna's Benevolence to grant them a limited version of the gift of the Lunars: Changing into any animal whose blood they taste. Should they remain in those forms longer than two hours, however, they would remain those animals until they died. Now, they must battle an akuma and find their own heroism without exposing who they are, lest they be silenced on their knowledge, their families killed or enslaved in Malfeas itself with them...

                                Since the First Age, the Ebon Dragon has had a strange fascination with a single soul's journey's through the Loom of Fate. While this soul has shown to be a paragon of the 'virtues' (or lack thereof) of the Ebon Dragon, the Yozi has never blessed the man with an Infernal Exaltation, most likely due to his incompetence in every sinister scheme he comes up with. Thus far, the Ebon Dragon has watched this soul in five lives: His first as the Half-Caste son of a mad, war-mongering Dawn Caste king in the First Age, the second as a Dynast and close adviser (and possibly lover) to the Scarlet Empress, the third as the personal servant of Regent Fokuf and most recently as a commanding officer for House Tepet in the Realm Civil War. Perhaps the Ebon Dragon merely enjoys to watch a mortal he finds so much like himself, or perhaps the Yozi has much larger plans for this particular soul. Perhaps Creation will never know...perhaps it will find out all too soon...

                                                Oh wow, I actually have one, now. Ahem:

                                                Great Forks is well known as a joyous city of temples and leisure. Among the many known revels in the city are the bands of energetic young performers whose dancing skills win them great fame and adoration. However, these bands are also aggressive, and sometimes bitter rivals with each other, competing for fame and the right to the very stages they dance upon. Things have changed lately, however, with the rise of several new Exigents to the leadership of many of these gangs. Simple dance contests and other games of skill between them are now giving way to much more physical and violent contests. These battles are rather intense, but to date, not fatal. Yet. And in the shadows, a group of unknown gods with ties to the Black Market of Exaltations continues to spread these powers around, waiting and seeing what will come next.

                                                Most strangely of all though, nearly all of the Exigents involved appear to be Chosen of the god of a specific type of fruit...

                                                    As the original poster of the original thread, the fact that this was resurrected makes me very happy indeed. Thank you, dreamking89, for bringing it back to life, and thank you to everyone who has posted so far!

                                                    Mmm... I should make a contribution... let's see...

                                                    ----

                                                    Several years before, the Realm attempted to go to war with a culture of new Exalts and their Godblooded progeny living in a distant corner of Creation, hidden in the Northeastern Bordermarches, due to a misunderstanding in cultural mores. Despite the power of the distant land, which almost annihilated the Blessed Isle (due to their ability to evade targeting by the Defense Grid), the distant culture discovered something about the Realm that made them halt their attack and sue for peace.

                                                    Now, a new Embassy has been created in the Bordermarches of Western Ocean, where the representatives of the Realm and the new culture are joined by reluctant agents the Guild, representatives from the Jade Folk (who have been able to get partially free of the Great Geas, and who have antagonized the Guild), and a strange representative of a culture from Deep within the Wyld. The head Realm ambassador is a Water-aspected naval commander who inexplicably survived the final battle of the earlier war; joining him are a mysterious representative of the strange culture (who secretly is a Zenith Caste priestess), a disaffected Guild factor with higher desires, and a vengeance-minded Jadefolk with a dozen schemes in mind (including trying to somehow acquire Exaltations for the Jadefolk). In addition, a man representing unusual clients also resides, offering powerful favors in oblique tones; a Sidereal agent keeps an eye on behalf of the Realm, searching for humans with non-Terrestrial Exaltations; and there are strange stirrings in the ruins of ancient cults, as Infernals and their demon servitors begin gathering hidden weapons out of the Wyld...
