              To my knowledge, that's a pretty exhaustive list, unfortunately. I didn't even know of Creation's Gambit, I'll admit.

              For completion, let me mention Two Circles. 16 pages in total, ran between 1st of March and 24th of May 2010. Kind of cute, but never got off the ground.

              Something I did have hopes for, however, was Tales of the Mastah, a Keychain spinoff that for a time was hosted on patternspider.net before they took it down when they changed names to rpdom.com. The DeviantArt archive still remains. This one, I honestly had high hopes for. The 16 pages that got produced are actually good. It doesn't take ages getting going, and some of the early characters are pretty interesting. Unfortunately, it started up twice and fell flat on the same god damn page both times. I can sympathise; sometimes something is more work than you think. Just wish it could have pulled through.

              Edit: Oh, and also! If we (that is, I) are doing the completionist thing, I'd be remiss if I didn't mention The Freedom Stone. It's the grandmother of the Exalted webcomics. By the time I even heard of it, it was long since abandoned, and by the current day I don't think you can find it anywhere on the internet in its complete form. The link above is a collection of pages the author posted on the old White Wolf forums a while ago. It's far from comprehensive, but it's what she had available without having to revive a couple fried hard-drives.
