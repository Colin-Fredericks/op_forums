                              The Storm This Way Comes has three story threads.

                              They're set up to cross sometimes and allow the Chosen to swap out depending on what they want to do. We haven't had a lot of swapping yet, but I think there's some interest.

                              Our first main story arc was back and forth with the established DBs and the newly-arriving Solars. This was mostly aimed at introducing the setting, discovering our characters, and so on. We're now into the second arc, which has our story threads pretty well set up.



                              The first thread is mostly Dragon-Blooded. It takes place in the far southwest, starting in a homebrew area called the Stormclaw Dominion. Stormclaw is inland from a coastal mountain formation. This protects it from the constant pounding of thunderstorms-to-hurricanes, drawn inland by the hot air going northward from the Pole of Fire.

                              This one was mostly focusing on the experience of being in a guild, with a well-established community around the PCs to support them in their endeavors. We explored backstory with flashbacks to build up on that feeling a bit. One of our Solars swapped in to this thread - the guild was drawn to the northeast into An Teng, to stop an incursion of Abyssal Anathema who were taking over.

                              The Solar had been nearly killed by the Satrap of An Teng in her Exaltation backstory, and went back to An Teng as a conqueror.

                              We finished up this bit over USA Labor Day weekend (a week ago as of this posting) with a decisive battle for the fate of An Teng. The Abyssals were repulsed at great cost. A conspiracy of Sidereals was hinted at. The Solar discovered that she'd been set up to be killed by her beloved sister. Her sister, secretly a Sidereal, had been convinced that she needed to learn Necromancy to battle the Abyssals and their Deathlord patron.... and that the best sacrifice for that attainment must be the dearest sacrifice.

                              The DBs lost their mentor - a well-respected and wise emeritus member of the Wyld Hunt. He'd carefully sheltered the guild through the touchy diplomacy of survival. He was assassinated during the battle by an Abyssal who had, as a mortal, once benefited from his target's mercy. Caught between his command to kill and his feeling of respect, he was in a shrieking limit break and fit of madness.




                              The second thread is taking off shortly. Our Solars recovered a skyship at the end of the first arc, and managed to get it to lift off. It desperately needs repairs and refitting to become the Cool Ship which will be the focus of this thread. They're joined by a DB - the swap-character of our Solar from the DB arc.

                              The skyship will be the main location, but I'm planning to have it sail north-ish from the Stormclaw area in the far southwest. Thence up to Wu-Jian and over toward Halta in search of repairs and adventure.

                              Depending how it plays out, they're likely to have some business to settle with the Deathlord behind the Abyssal incursion into An Teng, but that's far from certain yet.

                              Writing for this one, I'm trying to be consistent in playing up the flat shape of Creation. Depending on altitude and weather, you could potentially see from the edge of the world to Meru, or all the way into the mists of the Wyld.




                              The third thread has just gotten a bit of playtesting. This one is for Lunars, which we're just figuring out. So far, it's been just to knock around some characters during off-game nights when we're missing some players.

                              This one is starting off in the southeast, a bit northeast of Prasad. It will probably roam across the Scavenger Lands. Since our Lunars know some NPCs in the other two threads, already, they have ties pulling them west toward An Teng, and north to Nexus and beyond already.


                              Check out Momentum Exalted!

                                  We actually did something a little similar ages and ages ago:
                                  We started out in Medo (it wasn't called Medo then, but it was a land of nomadic horsemen that's pretty much where Medo is now), but fled the Wyld Hunt west on my Azure Chariot, then fled the Wyld Hunt again, and ended up in the Haslanti League (too far for them to reach us). (I should mention that our two combat characters died in the first few sessions, hence the fleeing.)

                                  In the Haslanti League, we got hired for an expedition on an Airship (well, a group of Airships). We travelled west across the North, then across a series of wacky islands (kind of "island-of-the-week", but with some overarching story threads), trying to beat a Ledaal fleet to an island of sleeping Dragon Kings in the far north-west (turned out the leader of our airship fleet, who we named Admiral Insano for his complete disregard for human life, was actually a Dragon King in disguise and he'd tricked the Haslanti into helping him reach and wake up this ancient city).
                                  Then we travelled south, and our airship was destroyed (always make sure your instructions to bound elementals aren't open to interpretation!). We made it south-east to Coral on a pieced-together wreck. The government of Coral wanted us to take an airship to the Neck on missions for them, but instead we helped overthrow them. Then there was some time-travel shenanigans, and we ended up in Harborhead...
                                  Anyway, travelling across the land by airship actually worked well as a plot. You just need to give people specific reasons to go to different places.

                                      Oh, I should say, we're actually doing a crossover right now. But I thought I'd say where my games are normally.

                                      So currently, as well as the game I run every few weeks, I'm playing 3 (each one is monthly) games:
                                      1) A pocket dimension of floating islands that slowly go from darkness up towards a sun covered in angry faces. They start freezing, as they rise they defrost as do the frozen talking animals on them, until eventually they burn up in the sun. This world was a practice world the Primordials made before Creation.
                                      2) A jungle land of talking snakes, monkeys and wolves, who were once ruled by humans, until the talking pigs, dogs and geese came across the sea with their trucks, TVs, phones, machine guns and rifles, and killed all humans in order to institute "True Animalism". Yes, their leader is a pig bureucrat who had his rival pig murdered with an ice pick. And their rivals are the cat-nation who practice Meowism and I assum are led by Chairman Meow.
                                      3) An island inhabited by frog-people, blue giants, eusocial lizardmen ruled by queens, talking trees, and underground craftsmen who only appear to others dressed in metal suits so no-one knows their true appearance. Instead of a sun, it has a massive central pillar that glows with the colours of one of the Maidens, which it cycles through every few years/decades. It was yellow, and there was an era of trade, but then a Dawn caste warlord arrived and conquered the frog-people, and it turned red. Also, the island is sinking, pushing the five nations to war over dwindling resources. Visions indicate we need to find an Eclipse to captain a giant boat to carry people from each tribe (and 2 of each animal) across the sea to a new land, and this will cause the pillar to turn yellow again and... maybe the pillar will turn yellow again.


                                      But these wacky realms probably aren't useful for my survey.
