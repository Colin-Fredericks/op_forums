    Abyssals are not doomed enough.

    04-01-2014, 02:34 PM
    I know that Abyssals have sometimes felt like the black sheep of 2e, and I think we all know the reason: They are not doomed enough. It was too easy to play a 2e Abyssal and, by simply never creating life or disobeying your Deathlord or falling in love or answering to your name or using your Unexpected-remover or resisting UMI, live a mostly-normal life as long as you don't mind never regenerating Essence in Creation and paying WP for several of your most important charms. You could fall in love and the person you loved might never get murdered by the Neverborn using a fiat-level power against which there is no defense!

    Obviously this drove people away from Abyssals, so I think we need to brainstorm some fixes. For example: Every time an Abyssal uses anyone's name, something they have an intimacy to dissolves into bitter dust and ashes. STs are advised to adjudicate this rule carefully to prevent abuse -- some players may choose their intimacies to target opponents, or to avoid having an intimacy to the things that actually make the game fun for them! Do not let them get away with this. Determine what they enjoy and have it turn to dust and ashes immediately. If the player persists in having fun, you may declare that their character dissolves into dust and ashes. But this is not an escape! They must continue to play a pile of dust and ashes for the remainder of the game; they may not create a new character.

    In 2e it was too easy to vent resonance. Therefore, 3e should fix this: In 3e your resonance can never go down ever. An Abyssal who reaches 10 resonance simply continues to experience a full-scale resonance eruption every tick forever.

    Other characters should intuitively understand that the Abyssal is evil; when they meet them, they should declare their hatred and disgust with the Abyssal in a loud tone of voice. This is unnatural mental influence costing 1 WP a scene to resist.

    Every action an Abyssal takes, without exception, brings the world closer to its doom. The ST should constantly keep private notes of every single thing Abyssal characters do in a session; then, at the end of the session, pull them aside and go over every single action, explaining how each one helped doom the world a little bit more. ("Twelve seconds into the session, you said 'hi'. These words were overheard by an invisible spirit in the corner of the room, reminding him of his dead love and driving him over the edge. He immediately rampaged out and killed...")

    Whispers also work differently now. They represent the irrevocable and irresistible taint of the Neverborn on your soul. All Abyssals must have at least Whispers 1, and it increases by 1 every scene, as well as every time they botch a roll or succeed a roll or fail a roll. It is not limited by the usual limit of 5 for backgrounds. Your whispers rating connects your mind to the Neverborn, constantly filling it with their gibbering madness and allowing them to take total control over you a number of times per scene equal to your Whispers rating, for as long as they want. Since the Deathlords have your monstrance, they may do so as well (their limit for controlling you is counted separately from the Neverborn.)

    I know this probably isn't enough to make Abyssals really, truly doomed, so let's hear if anyone else has any suggestions to enhance the Abyssal "doomed" experience.

          Well, in my games, you had to purchase all Charms as Taints. If a Charm didn't have a Taint, it now does for double the EXP of the Charm and providing a random mutation.                                           I've got some ideas!

                                          First off, Soulsteel's got to go. It's not really gruesome enough, y'know? It's practically just Starmetal, only made out of mortals rather than gods. Lame. Instead, I propose that we give them a new focus on the sadly-ignored necrotech. In fact, let's require all Abyssals to exclusively use necrotech artifacts!

                                          But necrotech's only an aesthetic. If we want that feeling of bleak despair to really hit home, we should require that necrotech artifacts be made of the Abyssal's family. Only blood relatives (maybe wives, but that's sort of stepping on the Ebon Dragon's stuff) can be forged into necrotech weapons and armor! This will highlight the dark, mature themes of the Abyssal Exalted and really connect players to the narrative significance of their attire.

                                          Next up, Blasphemy. This keyword was mostly introduced for the Infernal Exalted, but I'm seeing a real opportunity to step it up a notch with Super Blasphemy. This keyword not only alerts the Sidereals and all beings in the area that you've used a charm, it also marks your current location for the next week and sextuples the speed of all pursuers. This will create a palpable feeling of opposition, allowing Abyssals the chance to fight literally everything in Creation. To expedite this, I propose this keyword be attached to all Abyssal charms.

                                          Finally, let's spice things up a bit. Abyssals are meant to draw from vampiric themes, but they're lacking so many narratively interesting traits! I propose traditional weaknesses, such as an inability to cross running water, deathly fear of the sun and unhideable Transylvanian accent be grafted on to the splat.
