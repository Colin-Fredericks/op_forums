                                        Originally posted by Snakesandsuns View Post
                                        So the Dragon-Blooded book is being drafted up and considering that the core is really good I am excited. As a person who never actually got to play exalted but twice (both solars) in second edition I never got to experience the joys and wonders of the Dragon Blooded. However I always thought that they had a lot of cool things going for them and now that I have a gaming group more interested in exalted games I look forward to the chance to actually get to play one.

                                        So what do you all think is going to be in the book? What are you hoping to see in the Dragon-Blooded book?

                                        It is going to be interesting to see the Dragon Blooded book because it is going to give us a clear idea of how the mechanics look from a different angle both from power and theme. Things I am hoping for include: New Spells (pretty much a given), the elemental styles, an idea of how evocations work for other exalts, and additional antagonists as well as more demons and elementals for summoners. Something I would really like is some focus on other Dragon blooded other than the Dynasts and Lookshy. I know they will make up the bulk of the book but I would be delighted for more information on Prasad, forest witches, the blood-bats, and some new groups.
                                        I am looking forward to an immense joy of mine. Playing the outcaste DB who somehow gets more done than all the solars and celestials in the group.

                                        I am looking forward to storied lineages and storied items of inheritance. I am looking forward to a robust charmset where I can feel like I can make an air aspect swordsman who doesn't get shot in the foot every time they activate Dragon Graced Weapon. I am looking forward to seeing the immaculate styles. (would really hope that becomes one of those things where its better for a DB than for another exalt).

                                        Looking forward to playing my immaculate Little Mountain and my thief Slip.

                                        They are my favorite splat, I love the whole Romance of Three Kingdoms feel they have. I want that game. I want it so bad.

                                                    You know, this thread reminds me of something that caught my eye in the backer pdf regarding the Immaculate Styles:

                                                    Originally posted by Ex3 p.72
                                                    The power of Terrestrial Exalted who undergo the Order’s martial art training is beyond question. Immaculates spend their lives developing their Essence, and use the natural Charms of their kind mixed freely with the proprietary Immaculate martial arts styles to perform feats of martial prowess notable even among the Exalted.
                                                    Emphasis mine. This could admittedly mean absolutely nothing (Solars mix their Charms with Martial Arts too if you count supplementing your combat suite with Dodge, Resistance, Athletics, Awareness and such), but it could also mean the Dragon-Blooded can use their native combat abilities alongside Immaculate Styles in a way other Exalts can't, like supplementing a shortsword attack with both Fire Dragon and Terrestrial Melee Charms. That'd be pretty cool, don't you think?
