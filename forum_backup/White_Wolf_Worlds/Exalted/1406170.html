    Trying to understand ghosts

    08-03-2020, 07:27 PM
    I have some doubts on whether I understand the ghosts in Exalted correctly.

    1 PO ghosts - these seem simple. Perpetually angry. Usually walk in Creation, immaterial unless they materialise. Get killed by direct sunlight. Bound to their body.

    2 HUN ghosts - the actual 'mind' of the dead person. When in the realm of the dead, or in shadowlands, they are material and interact with stuff normally.

    Now what I want to make sure is how they interact with Creation outside shadowlands.
    I take it they are usually immaterial, and few of them have means to materialise.

    - Do they need to find a shadowland to enter creation? If yes, do they need to come back to it before sunrise or is sunlight not a problem for them?
    - Can they interact with mortals without directly entering Creation (show up in dreams and such)?

              Originally posted by Braininthejar View Post
              - Do they need to find a shadowland to enter creation? If yes, do they need to come back to it before sunrise or is sunlight not a problem for them?
              The best information we have at this time is still from 2nd edition. According to the Roll of Glorious Divinity 2 and CoCD: The Underworld, ghosts manifest in the Underworld, usually in the place equivalent to where they died or where they were buried, so to enter Creation they need to use a shadowland or some other magical means. Ghosts do not have a problem with sunlight directly, though their grave goods are destroyed by the light of the Unconquered Sun.

              - Can they interact with mortals without directly entering Creation (show up in dreams and such)?
              Yes. The Arcanos called Honored Ancestor's Ways contained a Charm called Courier In Dreams, which allowed a ghost to send 50 word messages to a descendant.

                      Originally posted by Braininthejar View Post

                      I would describe the Hun soul more as the seat of most of the dead person's memories and identity.

                      Originally posted by Braininthejar View Post
                      When in the realm of the dead, or in shadowlands, they are material and interact with stuff normally.
                      This is accurate, except they're only naturally material in shadowlands at night.

                      Originally posted by Braininthejar View Post
                      I take it they are usually immaterial, and few of them have means to materialise.
                      This is correct; for instance, honored ancestors and war-ghosts need other ways to interact with Creation.

                      Originally posted by Braininthejar View Post
                      Do they need to find a shadowland to enter creation?
                      Generally, yes.

                      Originally posted by Braininthejar View Post
                      If yes, do they need to come back to it before sunrise or is sunlight not a problem for them?
                      We don't know the full answer to this one yet; there's been no indication yet that ghosts suffer any negative effects in the sun (other than, in previous editions, its light destroying grave goods).
