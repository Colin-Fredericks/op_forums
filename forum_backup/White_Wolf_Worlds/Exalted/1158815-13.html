    I had a notion about five-dot Artifact weapons in which the thing in common between them isn't so much a power that corrupts, as a power that is great enough to require being handled with care.

    ​When thinking about their basic comparisons measured against the lower levels, the term that came to my mind is that five-dots are brimming with power. That can come in forms such as the corebook, where they accumulate and unleash energy just from being used in combat, or some of our new ones with things like an entry-level power that is quite strong, up to things like Stormcaller where just pulling the thing out causes a growing manifestation; all depending on the Artifact's temperament. You're holding a lightning bolt, something that twists around and vibrates in your hand, and while it's not necessarily dangerous to you, you've got to keep a tight grasp to actually direct it.

    ​That's how I would characterize the cascade of their Evocations; three-dots' flow into you, adding some colour to the edges of your actions, and four-dots' try to shape you, creating a channel for you to act within, while five-dots have their own thing going on and your part is to focus and refine that, bending the manifestations of its power to your own will and needs, whether that be applying that in ways that are somewhat novel, or at least pointing it in the direction you want before taking the latch off the door. Depending on its nature, that might sometimes make you open to some kind of influence, but it's mostly a subject of keeping a handle on this power as you use it (since, in most cases, the Artifact can hardly use itself).

    I have approximate knowledge of many things.

                                                On it's own, Eat the Heart of God is decent. But remember that you don't have to pay anything to get it, you get it for free when you attune to it, so it's functionally a baseline power of the artifact. From there, many of the other Evocations build off of it and do amazing effects using EtHoG as your trigger. This is the core of the design. A basic free power that you'd use all the time because it's easy, but later on you get big benefits from using it in unique situations.
