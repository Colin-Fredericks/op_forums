    Dragon-Blooded Power Level in 3e

    03-31-2014, 12:27 AM
    I was reading on another thread about how with the Exigents coming in that many of these new Exalts will be able to rival the Solars in power, even when created by Gods, and etc.

    I find that kinda baffling and it led me to question a few things.

    a) Are the Dragon-Blooded going to be more powerful now? In the lore aspect of things it would make no sense for the footsoldiers of the First Age to be as powerful as Solars, but I am willing to go with it.

    b) If they are not more powerful, why will there be Exigents created by not-Elemental Dragons that as powerful as Solars?

    c) I might have made this letter a), but... is there ANY truth in that Exigents might be as powerful as Solars in 3e?

          From what I can tell, Exigent power levels will fluctuate wildly from Exalt to Exalt, depending on how powerful the god they're made from was and how much of themselves they gave up.

          DBs were footsoldiers in the first age for two major reasons and a third that I can't remember right now:
          1) There were more of them than any other Exalt type. This still holds true, since the one bit of fiction we have on Exigents indicates that it is a laborous, difficult process to get approved for an Exigent, and it's possible the god that the Exigent is derived from can die.

          2) DBs worked best in teams, which makes sense given that their powers are derived from the highest parts of Gaia's titanic being. Exigents are created one at a time, and their powers are all derived from different beings who may very well have as much connection to each other as UCS does to my left foot's toenails. So it's unlikely they would complement each others' powers as well.

          Granted, they were BUILT that way, but you get the idea. And Exigents don't fill either of those niches in the Exalted host.

              A. As far as I know, no. I believe they ARE making the difference in power between Terrestrials and Celestials a bit less, but on the whole, Dragon-Bloods are the weakest of the main Exalted host.

              B. It's a matter of how they were made. The Terrestrial Exalted were created weaker so that they could pass along their powers by the blood... I would imagine that, if they were any more powerful, the Exaltation could not be passed on biologically. If they had any reason to, I would think that one of the Elemental Dragons could, theoretically, take an Exigence and create an Exalt more powerful that a Dragon-Blooded, but s/he likely wouldn't be able to make more of the same by breeding.

              C. Yes, but I believe it's only in potential. If you wanted to, you could create an Exigent that is every bit as powerful as a Solar*. However, he would likely be a single instance, his Exaltation probably wouldn't pass on to a new host, and we don't know how Exigents and Exalts might differ (if they do at all, of course). Setting-wise, it probably doesn't happen, if only because it's likely very rare for a god to be able to pass on that much power through the Exigence, even by sacrificing their own life. However, if you, as a player, can think of a good reason why it could happen, then the developers are merely giving you the tools to do so.

              I'll try to answer anything I may have been unclear or wrong about.

              Edit* Likewise, you could make an Exigent weaker than a Dragon-Blood, perhaps even much weaker, who passes on their powers by 'infecting' others blood with blood to blood contact. I think.

For example, Strawmaiden Janest was stated, I believe, to be about equal in combat to a Solar...assuming she was on the fields what were from the god who empowered her.

                                          Exigents aren't a specific class of being, so much as an umbrella to keep all the 'Sun gives power so a God can craft his own Exaltation', so they can be pretty much anything. Some of them will be as strong as a Solar, but then again, I expect those to be vanishingly rare, and for us to pretty much not see any canonical examples of that, left only as an option to STs and players. There are already several Hekatonkire, Gods, Third Circles and Crazy Unique Oddities that rival Solars in some way or another, and I expect the same would be true for the Exigents; they will probably still lack some of the Solar tools, and all of their niche, too. So it's not a threat to the Solar's niche, just one of those odd rare things that can stand up to them, no stranger than bumping into Five Days Darkness along the way.

                                          And as for DBs... devs play favorites, and Exigents are their new hotness. DBs won't be changed because of them, but will still be fine as what they used to be. Like most of the 'old Exalts', apart from maybe Solars (and certainly Sidereals), they shouldn't be expected to shine (Lunars and Abyssals will certainly suffer.)
