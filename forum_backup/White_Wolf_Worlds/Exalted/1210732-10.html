            Sorcery

            Sorcery! How on earth could sorcery be different for DBs and Solars? It's the same initiations, the same spells, the same rituals. Even the ones in the DB book like the Heptagram initiation aren't DB exclusive at all. So what's the diff? Well actually a lot. The difference here is mostly in the charm support for sorcerous spells.

            Let's start with Solars. Their biggest charm is Spirit-Drawing Oculus. It costs 1wp and all your anima, and it lets you roll a full, free, (int+occult) excellency to gather sorcerous motes reflexively. Which if you're optomized is 21 dice, and over 10 motes on average, stunt it for another 1-2. That, combined with another charm that lets you use your full excellency for free with a reset condition, PLUS shaping rituals, means that you can pull like 20-25 motes into a spell pretty easily. With spells that are Celestial circle or Solar circle this is a big deal, a spell that would be guaranteed for other exalted to take 2-3 turns with, Solars can do it in one.

            The thing is though, for spells of the terrestrial circle, particularly ones that you'd want to cast in combat, it's pretty overkill. Most of those are 10-15 motes, which a full excellency with a stunt and a shaping ritual gets really close to anyway. It's good if you absolutely must ensure that you get that spell of this turn, or if you're not actually a super optimized sorcerer and kind of need it to take less than 2 turns to shape something up.

            There's a few other charms but they're more minor.

            Now as to DBs, the first charm they have to help out all the time is Hidden Secrets Whisper, AKA the DB occult excellency! For each 10 on the roll you reroll a non-1 failed die. It's not much, and a lot of the time it won't help you at all, but there's a few times out there where it will explode your already great roll. Then there's two sorcery specific charms, the first is one that protects you while you shape. Solars have a similar effect with their Twilight anima, but instead of just adding hardness like the twilight anima, this one adds defense and hardness, and in air aura it's even non-charm.

            it's solid and it helps, but the big one here is Dragon Sorcerer Puissance. Whenever you shape an elemental spell you get a bonus mote, or 3 if you're in that element's aura. Rather than the Solar one, which is built to get off huge expensive spells fast and infrequently, this one is built to help you consistently smash out elemental spells. Since it's free and permanent, it makes it much easier to be a battle sorcerer, saving you motes and not wasting precious reset condition spells like the Solars do.

            Let's just say, for example, you're a fire aspect. You're dual wielding a sword and a hand that's trying to cast Flight of the Brilliant Raptor. You leap into battle using a fire aspected athletics charm, putting you in fire aura already. It's not super reliable, but with the bonus from your excellency, a stunt and a shaping ritual you can pretty reliably crack of a combat spell that's fire aspected every turn while using your defend other tree in melee to contribute even further to combat.

            I know it sounds like I'm making a huge deal out of nothing but on a full excellency roll you're actually getting more sorcerous motes than a Solar for slightly over half what they're spending on their excellency. I'd call that pretty impressive myself.


            So how does this affect how you play your sorcerer? Firstly you don't really have to worry about it until essence 3 anyway, so before that just do whatever you want, but maybe plan ahead. After that though you're going to want to pick spells that align with your elemental aura, or pick charms that let you easily slip into the aura you want for the spells you're going to cast. Like maybe you want to snag one of those shiny resistance charms that gives you bonus soak, always handy, so that you can slip into earth arua for Obsidian Butterflies.

            There's also one other key difference in how the two play, and that's because they get different things out of their charmsets. Solars for example don't really get effects like the Wind Carried Words that DBs get, so they're getting a slight benefit out of sorcery for being able to do that at all. Conversely there's stuff like Silent Words of Deams and Nightmares, that lets you instill something into a person without them even realizing what you've done, which is something Solar charms can do, but DBs have trouble with.

            Anyway, that's gone on long enough about sorcery, next time I'll be talking about evocations and how they are, I think, actually much better for DBs than Solars, and also DB chargen and what you want to look for.

                Evocations

                Evocations are another one where the difference is big and you might think it's zero, or just slightly lower for DBs because smaller dice caps and resonance list. Since, apart from the evocations locked behind Solar charms there doesn't really appear to be anything different. This one comes down to two things. One is heirloom items, which is pretty minor, but worth mentioning. Heirloom items are items from the DB corebook that have bonus unlocks for a particular bloodline. Like for example if attuning to an artifact grants you that artifact's first evocation, being of the right bloodline actually grants you the first two! Which is very helpful.

                The other is that DBs start at essence 2, which is massive. Most artifacts with evocations have like 1, maybe 2 at essence 1, but the ones I've seen really take off at essence 2. Spring Razor has 1-1-2-2-3 for it's essence requirements, Volcano Cutter goes 1-1-2-2-3-4, Faith's Pillar goes 1-1-2-2-2-3, you get the idea. When you start at essence 2 instead of 1, you go from just dipping your toe into the evocation tree, to usually grabbing more than half of it and really getting it's charm tree online. This means that you can basically take one of the more fighting focused evocations and almost replace your combat tree with what it has, or at least seriously augment it with it's special abilities.

                So like if you get Heavenly Typhoon, the chakram, you can take the first four evocations, the first you get for free, second for free if you're a Ledaal. That gives you a marking ability for bonus withering damage, an occult charm booster, a withering counter attack, and a decisive accuracy/damage booster. That's a foundation for, and almost a complete, little combat suite there. If you've got just a little Air Dragon Style, or Thrown, that's more than you need really.

                Yeah, so it's getting late, but just looking through the artifacts I have access to it really benefits a character being able to take these evocations off of chargen. The heirloom items that enhance DB charms specifically, and unlock even greater power with the correct bloodline means they are something that you're almost always going to want, if you can work it into your character, and makes it well well worth the artifact cost.

                Speaking of chargen, that's just the first thing that you're going to want to look at, artifacts can be a big part of your character and style, more on this in the next post about how you might want to pick charms and what you can do there!
