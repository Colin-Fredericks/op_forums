    We'll probably have to wait to see the shape of Sorcery in the core book before we can properly speculate about Necromancy. At the very least, I'd expect to still see the terms for the circles of Necromancy sticking around; those always felt distinctive and evocative to me. I'm curious whether the other Initiations alluded to will have their own circle names for spells/workings (e.g. the Wyld Initiation could borrow its names from raksha Oneiromancy, giving "Dreaming," "Waking," and "Shinma" circles) and will be roughly as distinct from "vanilla" Sorcery* as is Necromancy, or if Necromancy having its own reflects something distinct about it compared to other subsets of Sorcery.

    * For the record, I don't believe there's really such a thing as "vanilla" Sorcery, but the term tickled me.
