    [White Elephant for TheCountAlucard] : stories of Nexus

    12-29-2019, 11:20 AM
    From what I remember, you created Knight Raiton, which is some kind of Batman. I decided to take inspiration from The Joker to make my first enemy, a prince-merchant of The Guild in Nexus. I don't know if this is the same campaign, but here it is.

    The second antagonist, the Army of Nothing, is more to be used as some sort of background menace and recurrent enemy, and test to the characters will when the odds are against them.

    Hope you like them.

    The Laughing Man

    Slaves you say ? There are no slaves here, only happy folks! Who wouldn’t be happy to be relieved of their mortal worries ?

    Long ago he was a young serious man, dedicated to make fortune in one of the most profitable business, slavery. But now he has changed, and the only thing he spreads is happiness ; richness is but an unavoidable side-effect of his devotion.
    His slaves usually do not see things that way when he acquires them, but you can be sure soon enough the only thing they have on their face is a smile. For the most stubborn, he even has a special little room he uses to break them happy, rumors are it comes from the First Age. What is sure is that few can withstand its shock. Even Dragon-Blooded heroes, opposed to his reign of slavery, couldn’t stand proud when trying to rescue their loved ones imprisoned in that room.
    His lieutenants are much more pragmatic than him. They play his game, but deep down they only want two things : Money, and to run as far as possible from this man. Sadly for them, just when they have enough of what they desire, they usually become just like the slaves. Happy. But there are few that successfully escape his influence, only to live hidden in the shadows of Nexus. Maybe one of them know the location of The Laughing Man’s secret laboratory, where he keeps the recipes of his drugs, or maybe even darker secrets …

    Skills : A now happy man with a dark past. He has learned how to subtly evade direct violence.
    Solve a situation peacefully : 9
    Use dark magic : 8
    Empathically read the heart of someone : 8
    Prepare and administer drugs : 7
    Defend himself against intruders : 7

    Combat :
    Attack (staff) : 9 (damage 12)

    Defense :
    Parry 5 ; Evasion 2
    Resolve : 4 ; Guile 4

    Intimacies :
    There is no one I cannot make happy (Defining)
    The color grey that evokes sadness (Hatred) (Defining)
    Rashit, my most loyal lieutenant that is almost mine (Fatherly love) (Major)
    Toys are really wonders of the human soul (Major)
    Taking time to spend with my slaves to make sure they are alright (Major)
    Money doesn’t make you happy, but it still helps (Minor)
    Using twice the same word in a sentence (Disdain) (Minor)
    Immaculate Philosophy (Skepticism) (Minor)

    Merit :
    An army of 200 trained soldiers, it almost feels like they feed only on happiness.
    Servants of all kinds specialized in drugs, medicine, negotiation
    One strong ally in the Council of The Guild that can seriously back him up in exchange for a favor
    A network of spies in Nexus
    Enough money to maintain his fantasy of being a good man despite all those he enslaves
    3 strong lieutenants that could be mortal heroes, in less dire circumstances

    Charms & equivalent :
    First Circle Sorcery : The Laughing Man knows the spell Silent Words of Dreams and Nightmare.
    Perform Sorcerous working of Lunar Circle Ambition 1, given enough time (2 years). He already did it once, but no one knows what for.


    The Army of Nothing

    They are Nothing. They may not have always been Nothing, but it’s too far to remember now. They have no food to spare, no clothe to wear, no god to pray, no hero to follow, so they have Nothing to lose. They trained in the sunless part of Nexus, where no one dares to go. They trained until their feet and arms were covered in blood, they trained until the only thing they could see was their hatred, they trained until there was Nothing left of them other than vengeance. And now they are ready to strike, to fall upon Nexus’ corruption and evil. They will purge it of everything, by any means, because there are no innocent in this city. They will not go in blindly, though. They know the weak spot of their target. They want to make them suffer, as much as they have suffered. They want to make them fear, as much as they have feared in the dark. They are the army of Nothing, and Nothing can stop them now.

    Here are different ideas and objectives they might accomplish when the time is right :
    Poison or destroy an important part of the food of Nexus (the river, the stocks of passing merchants, the close agricultural harvest, etc …)
    Raid and destroy a calm neighborhood of Nexus when everyone is busy
    Kidnap an important member of a family during peace or commercial negotiations
    Sabotage an important cultural or symbolic ceremony that is taking place in Nexus

    Their means : How many are they ? Too many to count. The army of Nothing can usually have one Exalted at their disposition during an operation, to lead troops and make the difference in the field. This can be a young Lunar that wants to take revenge on Nexus, an enigmatic Sidereal following the schemes of the Loom of Fate, a young Solar that decided to take the power for himself, a traitorous god that lost his schedule in the Calendar of prayers in Nexus, or a broken Exigent that has nothing left in his life. In any case, they can usually count on 50 to 100 trained « monks » that will focus on a precise objective. They will always strike on the worst time, making life a hell for the heroes of the story.

    To dissipate the Army of Nothing, it would take courage and empathy. Undeserved love for those who has never known it. Infinite patience for those who see all as their enemy. Unbidden kindness for those that will show none. Endless mercy for those that can only make suffer. No amount of violence can stop them, for their numbers would only grow more. This, or put fire to Nexus slums. But at this point, you're playing their game.

              It's okay, I don't actually post about my Nexus game all that much.

              ​
              Knight Raiton is only one of the PCs in the gaming group; there's also Taroketu Kithri, a Fire Aspect Dragon-Blooded soldier from Lookshy; Robin Vaara, an Eclipse Caste Solar author and sorceress; Lyse of the Anvil and Pliers, a Casteless Lunar artisan; and Invisible Horse Princess, a Night Caste Solar assassin and martial artist.

              ​With the exception of Lyse, a recent addition to the party, our circle came together on the road, stopping a ghost from driving people into murderous frenzies, and then we journeyed together toward Nexus.

              Over Calibration we stopped a plot by a Lookshyan general to assassinate Robin to secure the capture of a First Age manse network. Then when we reached Nexus, the circle discovered that an Essence 5 Solar was trying to use Nexus as the focal point of a Working to restore his daughter's soul after she was fed on by Fair Folk; since it was threatening to drown countless poor people, we stopped the ritual and promised to help him find another way.

              We got some wealth and a number of artifacts after we looted one of the First Age tombs; we also got some pull in the city when we discovered the Emissary of Nexus' identity, and solved the murder of a member of the Council of Entities (an Abyssal working for the Walker in Darkness did it and blamed it on IHP).

              We then made attempts to ease the burden on the city's poor by working to produce means for the Nexus poor to fill their bellies free of charge, when a strange illness began spreading through the city. Robin found that an Anathema healer had been working on a cure for the disease when he got chased out of the city a lifetime ago, so we went to where he'd died in Great Forks for clues, only to find that it had all been a ruse so one of our friends could be murdered over something he had no involvement in. Bad times.

              ​Since then we've also tried to improve the lot in life of those in the city by working to disincentivize the practice of slavery in Nexus.

              ​Of course, in a recent turn, it appears that Invisible Horse Princess has been kidnapped, an event we'll investigate in our first January session.
