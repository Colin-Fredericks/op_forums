                                                Originally posted by TDS View Post
                                                So a strength 1 Lunar that turns into an Elephant to lift something heavy will turn into the weakest elephant that ever existed?
                                                Originally posted by Robert Vance View Post
                                                Part of the reason the Lunar Excellency works the way it does is to give you a way to access those higher pools by stunting to add a second Attribute to your limit.
                                                You take your native Attribute+Ability dice pool. If it's larger than the dice pool that animal would use for the action, you're just that awesome, use your native dice pool. If it's smaller, you take your native dice pool and add up to either (Attribute) charm dice or until your total dice pool is the shapeshifted form's dice pool. If you took the first option (so you have 2*Attribute + Ability dice in your pool but that's still smaller than your shapeshifted form's pool for that action), you can stunt a second Attribute into your dice cap and then add (Attribute2) charm dice, or until you reach the form's pool. You've done this without actually spending motes on your Excellency, mind, so essentially the form is a super efficient way to Excellency certain dice pools, depending on the form.
