                                        Originally posted by Erinys View Post
                                        How does martial arts initiation work in 2E? (other than initating Terrestrials into CMA) The 2E corebook doesn't talk about it, and the 1E Player's Guide isn't clear. P. 231 only says that it takes months or years and doesn't say whether the process is separate from learning the first charm of one's first style. The description of Solar initiation into SMA also doesn't say whether the initiation is separate from learning the first charm.

                                        So my Qs are:
                                        1. Is initiation just the way in which you learn the first charm of your first style, or a separate training you have to take before you can learn any MA charms?
                                        2. Does 2E clarify how long it takes?
                                        3. Also, what's the name of the thing Raksha can do to themselves to learn Martial Arts charms? The wiki charms-lists don't work right anymore so I can't find it there.
                                        Initiation is not necessary for anyone but Terrestrials trying to get into Celestial Martial Arts, or Mortals trying to get into Terrestrial Martial Arts. Initiation is only necessary because Terrestrials cannot normally use Celestial Styles, and it requires careful shaping of their Essence in order to allow it. This takes 'several years' though this is not specified mechanically...and a Terrestrial cannot dabble around between styles. When they start learning a Celestial Style, they must Master the style before they can start learning another one. (Also requires these two charms: Spirit Sight and Spirit Walking before they can learn any CMA)

                                        Lunars may learn any Terrestrial or Celestial Style freely.

                                        Solars/Abyssals/Infernals may learn any Terrestrial or Celestial Style freely, and may learn any Sidereal Style freely if they can find a Sidereal to teach them. There is no initiation involved here...it's just that only Sidereals can teach Sidereal MA.

                                        Siddies can do whatever they want. Magic Kung-Fu Fate Ninjas and all that.

                                        Elementals, Demons, and Spirits can learn TMA up through Essence 5. From Essence 6 and up, they can start learning CMA.

                                        Ghosts cannot learn new styles (unless they used to be Abyssals), but retain the skills of any Terrestrial Martial Art they knew the Form charm of, but cannot advance further. Former Abyssal Ghosts with the Relentless Revenant Technique can freely master TMA, and can learn CMA up to the Form charm.

                                        Mortals with Essence 3 (and Essence Awareness and Essence Mastery) and MA 5 can learn Terrestrial Martial Arts.

                                        Fair Folk who take the "Root of the Perfected Lotus" Mutation can learn Martial Arts like a Terrestrial
