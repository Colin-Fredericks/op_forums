                In First Edition, the only thing a Lunar needed to do to get someone's Heart's Blood was be personally responsible for marking off the victim's last health level, and then taste the blood.

                Second Edition added all sorts of new restrictions and limitations in the form of the Sacred Hunt, which required a length process of locating, stalking, and physically running your target down.

                This was not ideal for No Moons and Changing Moons, who previously had the ability to set traps or persuade hunters to do the trapping for them. The new and greater bias towards killing your prey in personal combat is very responsible for the idea that Heart's Blood was inappropriate for those two Castes and should be restricted to the Full Moons.


                Which Edition do you think did it better?

                formerly Tornado Wolf, formerly Inugami
