    Originally posted by Paradim View Post
    Well... "rely more on" is not an exclusionary statement, just an indicator of preference.
    The developers felt so strongly about limiting the transformative properties of moonsilver that they made it the only magical material with words devoting to talking about what it explicitly couldn't do, rather than relying on the implications formed by the things it could.

    The fear seems to be that if they give an inch (moonsilver can transform for some Evocations) then gamers would take a mile (moonsilver can trivially become any possible weapon).

    If the Lunar Exalted can produce copies of artifact armor and weaponry for a battle-group's worth of clones to use in battle, then what limits them from having Glorious Lunar Saber?

    If the Lunar can produce mere illusions of equipment for brief-lived distraction clones, then what limits the Lunar Exalted from having the illusion Charms that Holden and Morke insistently said they would not have?


    So, there has been established precedence for Lunars being able to use their powers regarding objects which are non-magical in nature.
    Not in this Edition.

    If you want to look at previous Editions, there's established precedent for Lunar Exalted being able to warp the laws of space and time, too. But now that's for the Getimian Exalted.


    I think there's a big question that has to be considered as to what the impact of these clones is meant to be. What's the purpose being accomplished? Are they distractions?
    Momentary clones could be used for distractions, whether for dodging and ducking behind cover to hide, or for making an attack from an unexpected angle.


    Can they use similar powers as the Lunar themself?
    Brief distractions wouldn't, because such brief clones are just fluff justifications for the effects of the true Lunar's action.

    More durable clones used to fill out a battle-group would be using the Lunar's own powers to keep up with him. If he's going to use a movement Charm, they're all going to use the same movement Charm.

    If the Lunar Exalted can produce copies of artifact armor and weaponry for a battle-group's worth of clones to use in battle, then what limits the Lunar Exalted from having Glorious Lunar Saber?

    If the Lunar can produce mere illusions of equipment for brief-lived distraction clones, then what limits the Lunar Exalted from having the illusion Charms that Holden and Morke insistently said they would not have?


    clones created as a distraction can be more numerous/weaker but serve their purpose less if they're incapable of being deceptive.
    They're extremely capable of being deceptive as animals if the Lunar is the same species of animal, or if all of them are a different species than the one the Lunar was just a moment ago. No clothes or weapons are needed.


    It's a poor shapeshifter/trickster that can't pull off a deception simply because they lack the props to sell it.
    You assume they need props to sell it.

    Holden and Morke said they'd be plenty tricksy and deceptive even without illusions.

                                    Yeah, I think for mythic warriors, there's a reliance on mythic themes; the Lunar can copy himself (via illusions or actual spawning; one is likely much higher level than the other) and the copies have copies of his weapons (which, I think, should only last as long as the clone, and maybe should fall apart if removed from the clone), because that's part of the theme, but that doesn't mean the Lunar should just be able to copy the weapon itself. I think that's okay.


                                    How many clones are we talking? How long do they last? Are they real or illusory? How does this work mechanically? I know these questions have come up already, but I think it depends a lot on that.

                                    I kind of feel like if the Lunar is making himself into a battle-group for one scene, then it's fine, but if he's growing a clone of himself for general purposes, and the clone is going to last a while (until decommited or killed, for example), then that guy/girl should be naked, or at most just wearing clothes and that's it, not armed. That may seem odd, but there's an element of what's practical for the game; it's awkward if 10 days later you've still got extra swords, but it's also awkward if you split up in the middle of combat and your clones are not armed.
                                    As you said, when they die the copies should definitely vanish, but I think that's still potentially too long, and starts getting into abusable territory. But one scene is fine.

                                    You know, thinking about it, I feel much, much more comfortable with the image of a Lunar splitting into 10 versions, and his moonsilver sword splitting into 10 copies, than I do with a jade or orichalcum sword splitting into 10 copies.

                                    The other thing I find myself thinking, with this conversation, is that the more we talk about it, the more I'm quite happy for Lunars to turn into swarms of rats or bees or whatever (which I absolutely want them to be able to do), than be able to turn into a swarm of clones to become a battle group. I don't hate the idea, but nor do I find myself that interested in it. I wouldn't hate Lunars being able to do it, but I'm not sure it's a charm I'd want; being able to turn into a swarm of animals is fine enough.
                                    Though I do like the idea of spawning 1 or 2 clones for general utility (probably as quite a high-essence charm though, like 4 or 5).
