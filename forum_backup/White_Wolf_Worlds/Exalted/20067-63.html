    I am an enormous DB fan, but I don't think the themes of the game would be served by giving them access to the Sapphire Circle. Part of the point of the Terrestrial Host is that they tend to be overmatched against any given supernatural threat, but make up for it with teamwork and discipline. Furthermore, they are meant to be political inheritors to a vast supernatural empire, but not spiritual inheritors - they cannot replicate the wonders of the First Age, or even maintain them without vastly inflated amounts of effort and resources. If only the Adamant Circle is closed to them, that ironically shifts even more focus and narrative weight on the Solars, because it implies that it was only through their puissance that the First Age was full of wonders, and the other Celestial Exalted had very little part in those wonders - if it were otherwise, the Dragonblooded would have the power to replicate and maintain those wonders through Celestial sorcery.

    Also, we've been told that the Terrestrial/Celestial Martial Art distinction is going away in Ex3, so an argument from parallelism is poorly timed.

    Glorious Solar Kitten
