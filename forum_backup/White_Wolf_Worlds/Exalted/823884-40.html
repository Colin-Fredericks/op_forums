    The following Artifact was created for a campaign of mine. You can read more details here.

    The Crystal Heart (Artifact ****)

    Attunement Cost: 5m


    The Crystal Heart is an accursed artifact, born of a lover’s sacrifice and twisted by the whims of the fairy queen who forged it.
    The Crystal Heart, true to its name, appears to be a stylized heart, forged from a single smooth piece of transparent crystal. While unattuned, the crystal is cold and clammy. Once attuned, a rose-colored liquid manifests in a reservoir within the heart and a comforting warmth emanates from it. The heart settles upon the wearer’s chest, where it silently beats in rhythm with the wearer’s own heart. When one of the Heart’s Evocations is activated, the Crystal Heart glows brightly enough to read by.

    In order to attune the Crystal Heart, the wearer focuses on one of her Major or Defining Ties. Typically this is a positive Tie of affection, friendship, or love, but other emotions may be fine. The Crystal Heart internalizes the Tie, drawing power from the wearer's connection. So long as the Crystal Heart is attuned, no magic can prevent the wearer from applying the Intimacy's rating to her Resolve (where normally applicable).

    The Intimacy cannot be weakened or strengthened by mundane means. If the wearer breaks the attunement, she feels a sickening ache as the Intimacy is weakened by one level. If the Intimacy is ever weakened to Minor, the Crystal Heart stops functioning, but remains attuned.

    If the wearer's Intimacy is ever erased, the attunement immediately ends as the Crystal Heart stops beating, growing cold once more. The wearer gains a Defining Principle that opposes any attempts to rekindle the original or anything like it: for example, a Tie of "My Wife (Love)" is replaced by “I will never love again”. Like the original Tie, this new Principle cannot be degraded by mundane means.

    Storytellers should note that the Crystal Heart is not intended to be a cheap path to power. Any character who would attune the Heart to a Tie “they can afford to lose” probably does not possess the Intimacy at a rating that would allow the attunement. There are stories to tell about tragic heroes who would voluntarily sacrifice something they hate about themselves, but be wary about allowing Ties that the character would be happy to be rid of.

    Upon attuning the Crystal Heart, the wearer gains access to the following Evocations for free.

    Heart's Fervor
    Cost: 1wp
    Type: Reflexive, Supplemental
    Keywords: Uniform
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisites: None
    The Crystal Heart offers power at a terrible cost. If the wearer taps into the Crystal Heart, she may draw power from her Tie at risk of destroying the Tie itself. The wearer may draw upon their Tie to enhance one rolled action or application of a static value. Rolled actions gain the Intimacy’s rating as non-Charm dice. Static values gain a non-Charm bonus equal to half the Intimacy’s rating (rounded up). After resolving the triggering action, apply the triggering action’s successes against the wearer’s Resolve, with a penalty equal to the Intimacy’s rating. If the triggering action meets or beats the wearer’s Resolve, weaken the Intimacy by one level. Note that if the wearer had activated the Crystal Heart to boost her Resolve, she does not gain that benefit against this, but her Resolve may otherwise be enhanced as normal.

    As a final measure, the wearer may tap even deeper into the Crystal Heart. For three Willpower, the duration extends to one scene; if the wearer has activated the normal version of Heart’s Fervor, decrease this activation cost by one point. At the end of the scene, the Intimacy is erased. The wearer may prematurely terminate this power: in doing so, she gains the Intimacy’s rating as automatic successes on her next rolled action or static value application, but she suffers the consequences immediately thereafter.

    Numb the Fire
    Cost: 1wp
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisites: None
    Is life more important than love? This is the question the wearer must ask himself when he feels his heart’s-blood slipping away. The Crystal Heart offers a trade. The wearer may tap the Intimacy held within the Crystal Heart to prevent harm. Preventing 1-2 Health Levels weakens the Intimacy by one level. Preventing 2-4 Health Levels weakens the Intimacy by two levels. Preventing 5+ Health Levels destroys the Intimacy altogether. The Crystal Heart cannot be used in this way if the rating is not high enough to support the harm it would prevent.

        A Pair of Reaper Daiklaves(Each Artifact 3)

        Principle of Heavenly Motion

        Forged in the North, with a hilt of Orichalcum and a blade of Red and Blue Jade around a core of meteoric iron, the Principle of Heavenly Motion is a blade of extreme elegance. It was first wielded by Star Shining Across the Heavens, a Solar who adored the sky and stars, and was inspired by a comet, a Fae weapon launched across the sky at the Silver Throne.

        Evocations
        A Solar or Dragon-Blooded the Principle of Heavenly Motion imbues its wielder with the speed of a falling star, granting them 2 dice to all tests of speed. While using these dice, the character trails brilliant blue light.

        Gathering the Stars
        Cost: 3m+ Mins: Ess 1
        Type: Reflexive
        Keywords: Perilous, Stackable
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisites: None

        The Exalt creates a sphere of blazing blue fire around her blade, and may create up to (Essence) or 3, whichever is greater such spheres. The sphere lasts until the end of the scene. When she defends herself from an attack later in the scene, she may expend 1 of spheres to inflict a -1 penalty on the attack roll.

        Make Stars Fall
        Cost: 5m Mins: Ess 2
        Type: Supplemental
        Keywords: Decisive-Only
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisites: Starshine

        By expending all spheres garnered through the use of the prerequisite, the Exalt may unleash an arc of flame at any target up to Medium Range that is not in contact with the ground. If it strikes, the Exalt may execute a gambit to ground the struck target, with a difficulty of 4. If the Gambit succeeds, the target is dragged to the ground in a corona of blue fire, and knocked prone. They take falling damage as normal.

        A Hundred Burning Stars
        Cost: 5m/10m Mins: Ess 3
        Type: Supplemental
        Keywords: Decisive-Only
        Duration: Scene
        Prerequisites: Make Stars Fall

        This charm supplements a Decisive attack, allowing for a special gambit. This gambit has a difficulty of 5. If the gambit is successful, the target is hurled skywards, and left hanging at medium range from the ground, surrounded by a corona of blue flame. By paying 10m, the gambit may be executed against a battlegroup of Size 3 or less. Larger Groups require multiple activations of this charm, at a cost of 5m per dot of Size. Anyone or any group struck by this gambit may later be struck by Make Stars Fall, which releases the commitment. Otherwise, they gently float back to the ground at the end of the scene, unharmed.

        Hammering Meteor Strikes
        Cost: +1m+ Mins: Ess 5
        Type: Simple
        Keywords: Decisive-Only
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisites: A Hundred Burning Stars

        This is a special, un-blockable, un-dodgeable version of Make Stars Fall, against all targets suspended by A Hundred Burning Stars, paying 1m per target or Size of Battlegroup in addition to the base cost of Make Stars Fall. They are hurled at any point within long range and take damage as if they had been thrown from Long Range above the ground. Upon striking the ground, blasts of blue fire smash craters into the ground, knocking prone anyone within close range of an impact.



        Wheel of Eternity

        This sword is forged of Starmetal, Moonsilver and Orichalcum, with a guard in the shape of a wheel and repeating moonsilver wheel motifs along the orichalcum blade that slowly turn. The blade is an example of Sidereal craftsmanship from the First Age, based on certain understanding of time. It was taken by a Solar into battle against the Raksha, and considered a kingly blade even then, even if it wasn't the most powerful. The Sidereals reclaimed the sword during the Usurpation, but it was lost during the Contagion.

        Evocations
        A Sidereal, Lunar or Solar who attunes to the blade for an additional 2m may reroll a single non-success on every damage roll.

        Burning Time
        Cost: 1hl Mins: Ess 1
        Type: Supplemental
        Keywords: Perilous, Aggravated
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisites: None

        Time cannot be controlled. It is well known that the required principles were destroyed by the 3 Spheres Cataclysm. And yet, a way has been found. By feeding one's own lifeforce into the gears of the Wheel of Eternity, one can momentarily take control of one's own time, striking with incredible precision in a perfect moment, with vicious speed and force. This allows for the completion of an aim action instantly and reflexively.

        Screaming Gears
        Cost: 2m Mins; Ess 3
        Type: Supplemental
        Keywords: Decisive-Only
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisite: Burning Time

        By turning the wheels with Essence rather than Lifeforce, the Exalt drives his unbreakable sword against time. The gears scream, and the world rebels against the blow. The Exalt may choose to reduce his attack pool, and for every dice by which he lowers his pool, he adds a dice to the eventual damage pool as the gears tear into the very fabric of his foes being and rip it apart.




        Song of the Spheres
        Cost: 5m Mins; Ess 4
        Type: Simple
        Keywords: Perilous
        Duration: Scene
        Prerequisite: Screaming Gears, A Hundred Burning Stars

        Crossing the Wheel of Eternity and the Principle of Heavenly Motion, the Exalt transcends the material world. He may reflexively materialize and dematerialize for the rest of the scene. Additionally, he may strike any target he can see, though he takes a -1 penalty for every range band between his location and his target, hurling burning blue stars. He may fly above the earth, suffering no penalty for movement within Creation.

        When using Meteor Hammer Strikes, he may simply pay a single mote to use it even if he has no target suspended, congealing time and essence into a sphere of heavenly chromatic light. This projectile explodes on impact, unleashing a decisive attack against all targets within short range with an accuracy of 10 dice and a damage pool of 10 dice+3 for every range band between the wielder of the two.

                I have to say the new system and how to build interesting and new artifacts is both intimidating and exciting. I've got a couple of artifacts that I would like to field at you to solicit opinions and constructive advice. I really had no idea how to price mote costs or even field mechanics. Hopefully people get where I was coming from and can clue me in where I need to be clearer.

                (Note: I have significantly changed the story of the Celestial Lions from that of 2nd edition, but I thought it the idea was cool. I stole liberally from the story of Akodo and the Kitsu from L5R for it. My PC is extremely fixated on the law and justice to some extent. Sometimes it’s a tyrannical drive in her. )


                Righteous Judgment
                (Level 4 Orichalcum & White Jade Grand Goremaul)

                Crafted for the mighty Dawn Caste, Walker in Glory and Victory, Lion of the Unconquered Sun, Righteous Judgment was used to mercilessly mete out punishments of death often at the head of an army as dictated by The Wrathful Onyx Tyrant before he became the Righteous Devil. Its head is crafted out of the purest of White Jade while the rest is built from Orichalcum.
                Upon attunement by a Dawn Caste at the cost of a 3 additional motes, Righteous Judgment grants the user a +1 Accuracy and a +2 Damage modifier in additional to the usual Heavy Artifact Weapon modifiers. Unlike other grand goremauls, its blows are so fierce that is causes Lethal damage.

                Order in the Court
                Cost: 5m; Mins: Essence 1
                Type: Simple
                Keywords: None
                Duration: Scene
                Prerequisites: None

                Sometimes it is necessary to call a punishment to order and this will demand silence before the words are spoken. Righteous Judgment can silence an entire battle group whose size does not exceed the wielder’s Essence. In effect this will give a +3 non-charm dice bonus to any Stealth-based stratagem or Stealth roll. Note that it does not make the wielder or his battle group invisible; merely silent. It also has the side effect of stifling any noise in the immediate vicinity including the death screams of people killed by the wielder or his forward units. This will work on the smaller scale too and give the wielder and people in his immediate vicinity complete silence. The Evocation does render verbal Orders or even speech impossible however while active.

                Righteous Army Prayer
                Cost: 10m; Mins: Essence 2
                Type: Simple
                Keywords: None
                Duration: Indefinite
                Prerequisites: Order in the Court

                Righteous Judgment grants a potent blessing to troops led by its wielder. A battle group led a Dawn Caste wielder will be granted +1 Might as long as the weapon is held by the unit leader. This stacks with any other similar blessings such as that granted by Tiger Warrior Training Technique’s training regimens.

                Attention before the Court Stance
                Cost: 8m Mins: Essence 2
                Type: Simple
                Keywords: Psyche
                Duration: Scene
                Prerequisites: Order in the Court

                When the judge stands all must do so as well. When holding Righteous Judgment, the wielder can command all enemies that see it or the wielder to stand up at attention or present themselves visually and verbally if able to the wielder for a moment. Resolve this as a Charisma + Presence roll versus the target’s Resolve. It is only momentary so it does not render them defenseless but it will force them to break stealth or invisibility if able. Should Righteous Judgment be dropped or concealed the magic breaks until it is once again held in the open.

                Kneeling for Judgment Strike
                Cost: 6m, 3i, 1wp; Mins: Essence 3
                Type: Simple
                Keywords: Withering, Perilous
                Duration: Scene
                Prerequisites: Righteous Army Payer, Attention before the Court Stance

                All must kneel so that judgment may be pronounced. The wielder strikes the ground and performs a single Withering attack upon all persons, friend or foe, within Essence yards of the wielder. While this does the normal amount of damage to the targets, the wielder only gains an amount up to his essence for each target. Additionally all targets must make a (Dexterity + Athletics) roll at difficulty 4 or be knocked prone/driven to their knees. When used against a battle group the value gained is multiplied by the group’s size signifying the large amount of people affected.

                Purifying Gavel Strike
                Cost: 5m, 1wp; Mins: Essence 4
                Type: Supplemental
                Keywords: Decisive-only, Aggravated
                Duration: Instant
                Prerequisites: Kneeling for Judgment Strike

                As a punisher of the guilty, all creatures of darkness or those have committed sins against Heaven or the Unconquered Sun shall meet their just punishment at the end of Righteous Judgment. This Evocation calls upon the unfailing purity of the Unconquered Sun to cleanse the target. The Evocation grants Double-10’s on damage rolls and turns this blow into Aggravated damage. Should the target survive he will have a Major intimacy of Fear towards the wielder and Righteous Judgment

                If the target is truly innocent and is hit anyway, then the wielder takes half the damage as aggravated and the target merely takes the rest as bashing anyway.

                Aegis of the First Pride
                (Level 5 Orichalcum Articulated Plate)

                Long ago after the men and women became the first exalted, one among them that would become known as the Walker in Glory and Victory hunted the first lions. No common lions were these, but the mighty spirits who would one day become celestial lions guarding the very gates of Yu-shan and enforcing heavenly laws. They called themselves the First Pride. At first the Walker hunted them because they served the Primordials, but he began to see the nobility in the beasts. With some effort and assistance from his circle the Walker in Glory and victory won them over to the side of the Gods and their servants the Exalted. A deal was struck as both sides were impressed with the nobility of the other. They began to join battles alongside the Solar Exalted and the leader of the First Pride was wounded mortally and so to save his friend the walker called upon his circle’s twilight for aid. The Pride’s leader was bound and saved. This pact forever changed the Anima of the Walker in Glory and Victory.
                The pact was embodied in the Aegis and the First Pride’s Leader was linked to it. Runes symbolizing this pact were hammered into the Orichalcum plate and it shined like the hide of the Celestial Lions once completed. To this day when the power of the Aegis is called upon it will defend the wearer to its utmost. When the wearer leads a group that is willing to uphold his ideals, her might grows and her ability to unshakably fulfill her mission.

                When attuning the the Aegis for an additional 2 motes, the wearer takes no mobility penalty as it conforms to the wearer as a 2nd skin.

                Unshakeable Pride Spirit
                Cost: 4m, 1wp Mins: Essence 1
                Type: Reflexive
                Keywords: none
                Duration: Scene
                Prerequisites: none

                The leader of the pride is strengthened by its belief in him. With this Evocation Aegis’ wearer adds an amount to the Exalt’s Resolve all attacks versus one intimacy. The amount depends on the Size of the unit she is with. The maximum bonus is up to the wearer’s Essence. If this is an actual Battle Group that the exalt leads then the maximum bonus is Essence+1. Note that a typical circle of Exalts would be at least size 1.

                Orichalcum Hide Spirit
                Cost: 6m Mins: Essence 2
                Type: Reflexive
                Keywords: Both
                Duration: Instant
                Prerequisites: Unbreakable Pride spirit

                The hide of the celestial lions is akin to solid Orichalcum. When invoked the wearer’s skin and hair turn golden and stiffens versus all attacks. It doubles the wearer’s bonus Soak from the armor versus Withering Attacks as well as Hardness versus Decisive Attacks. The protections may not be overcome or lessened in any way.

                Shard of the Pride Summons
                Cost: 10m, 3a, 1wp Mins: Essence 3
                Type: Simple
                Keywords: none
                Duration: one Scene
                Prerequisites: Orichalcum Hide spirit

                Bringing together a union of armor, Essence, will, and spirit, the Exalt reads the pact from her armor with a triumphant cry, and her anima bursts free as an enormous, brilliant golden lion, which joins the Exalt in battle. This avatar of the armor immediately rolls Join Battle and gains its own Initiative pool. It has the traits of a great cat (p. 565) with the addition that it gains a soak and hardness bonus as if it wore heavy artifact armor (this in no way impedes its movement and it suffers no mobility penalty), and is in all ways considered the Solar’s familiar—including being a valid subject for Survival Charms and Ride Charms, even those that permanently empower a familiar. At Essence 4+, the Solar may pay for Aegis of the First Pride’s instant-duration Evocations by spending points of the anima lion’s Initiative in place of motes on a two-for-one basis. If the anima lion is incapacitated, it vanishes and cannot be called forth again until the Solar successfully hunts and slays a deadly beast or monster that challenges her skills as a warrior. During that time, the cost of all of Aegis of the First Pride’s Evocations rise by one mote, as the armor’s wounded spirit strives to mend itself.

                The Pride Returns
                Cost: 15m, 3a, 1wp, 1lhl Mins: Essence 4
                Type: Simple
                Keywords: none
                Duration: one Scene or more
                Prerequisites: Shard of the Pride Summons

                This impressive evocation calls upon the pact and summons the slain spirits of the First Pride as well as the living to form an impressive unit of grand golden lions. They form a Size 5, Might 3 unit with unusually high statistics and Perfect Morale. Invoking this has long reaching consequences as it summons all the Celestial Lions and can only be performed once as essentially this ends the Pact with the Walker in Glory and Victory that the Celestial Lions share. They will stay until the battle is won or the wearer of the Aegis is slain

                It’s possible that the Pact may be restored, but such a feat as to impress all of the Celestial Lions with your nobility may be more difficult given their nature now. Such is the stuff the Solar Exalted are made of now though.

                                    Prismatic Beauty

                                    Prismatic Beauty was made in the first age. Few know exactly when, none wrote it down. But a single myth is told of its creation. A lunar shaman made it as a gift to their bethrothed. Every night for 1,000 nights, the lunar left her home in secret, and played a song to her patron, Luna as she collected wood spider's silk. Then, on the thousand and first night, as she sung her song near a lake to gather her thoughts, the moon's reflection showed the form of a gentle Rabbit, one of the many guises of Luna. The lunar bowed her head, as Luna spoke:
                                    "You sing for me though I am not your lover. And I have fallen in love with your voice. I know that my heart cannot take the pain of separating you from your love, nor can I bare these feelings... and so I will grant you a boon to end your song, so I may move on."
                                    And for her boon, she plucked exactly seven hairs from Luna's tail. These hairs seemed small at first, but as she spun them, it was like each one could wrap around the moon in the sky itself. And so, she spun them into a silken cloth, miles long yet only inches long, and used Luna's moonsilver to gain control of it.
                                    After nearly 100 years of spinning, she had crafted a masterpiece, a silken armor designed in love's name, given to one who sought justice above all. In time, the armor came to take on it's wearer's personality, showing a desire for style and grace at all times.


                                    Shimmering Guise
                                    Cost: 1m; Mins: Essence 1
                                    Type: Reflexive
                                    Keywords: None
                                    Duration: Scene
                                    Prerequisites: None
                                    Prismatic Beauty shimmers and shifts, altering itself to take the shape it's user desires. It can look like nearly any piece of clothing, though most often takes the form of a long dress, colors shifting to be appropriate. This evocation is granted for free to any who attune to it, and grants the user +1 appearance for the scene. It gets bored of looking the same way for too long, and always pushes the user to allow it to shift for each new event they attend.

                                    Moon Prism Power
                                    Cost: 1wp, 3a; Mins: Essence 2
                                    Type: Reflexive
                                    Keywords: None
                                    Duration: One Scene
                                    Prerequisites: None
                                    Prismatic Beauty feels the warmth of a solar's aura, and pulls it into herself, deepening their communion. The Solar's anima is vented into the armor, and it takes on a dramatic transformation, glowing equally bright, but in a prism of colors in the slivers of moonsilver woven into the silk. The outfit becomes one of impossible beauty, framing the solar's own beauty and giving them a bonus to their appearance equal to their essence for the remainder of the scene. Further, on activation, the solar becomes attuned to the dangerous situation they're in, all thoughts but victory (and style) leaving their mind effortlessly. They are granted 15 initiative. This initiative bonus may not be granted more than once per day, unless the solar spends an additional willpower on this evocation, and may only be done if it is in the name of love or justice.

                                                    Ok, I've been working on this for a week or two, but after seeing Sunder The Gold's post about throwing melee weapons, I decided to post my work-in-progress. Here's my first ever homebrew weapon, Crystalline Sliver of the Infinite.

Inspired by Fate/Stay Night, Dark Souls, and Soul Eater I wanted to make an artifact that gave the wielder an infinite amount of extremely sharp, extremely breakable weapons that blend utility and "cool factor", allowing them to rain crystal on foes in melee or at range with the tried and true method of "Blade Spam"

                                                    Crystaline Sliver of the Infinite
                                                    Artifact •••
                                                    Attunement: 5
                                                    Tags: Melee, Lethal, Thrown (Short), Special

                                                    Sometimes called the "Crystal Lightening Blade", "Frozen Moonlight", or the "Flashing Shard", legends don't agree on the source of this strange object which looks like a broken sliver of crystal glass: flat, jagged, and partially opaque. Whether it was found in a naturally occurring crystalline leyline or made by a glass-obsessed sorcerer, the strange shard has indisputable powers of reflection and crystal synthesis. At least two of these items are known to exist, but how they are related and what specific properties are intrinsic to them has been a source of debate among scholars for many centuries.

                                                    *Note: The Crystalline Sliver is small, about the size of a broken dagger's blade. Attuning to this weapon requires an hour's meditation in which the crystal is slowly, painlessly inserted into the user's heart. Once finished, there is no physical mark about the body and the Sliver can't be detected even by expert medical inspection. This makes especially useful to assassins, diplomats, and other who would prefer to appear unarmed.

                                                    This artifact grants the wielder the ability to generate crystalline blades instantly with a flash of their hand, but the blades are very brittle and short lived. At the wielder's discretion, they can Reflexively use a Draw Weapon Action1 to create one of the following weapons in hand: a Daiklave, Reaper Daiklave, Reaver Daiklave, or Short Daiklave. This may be included in a flurry.

                                                    1 the Join Battle action includes this use of Draw Weapon as though the Solar had been using a "regular" sword, and is considered armed with the weapon of their choice as normal. All ambush rules apply to starting a battle unaware.

                                                    Weapons created by the Sliverare as sharp as they are brittle, they deal an extra 3 (???) damage on Decisive attacks, which also causes them to shatter. The wielder of the Sliver will need to be cautious that they are not caught unarmed before they can create another weapon. All Weapons created by the Sliver will also shatter is they are further than Short Range from the Artifact bearer.

                                                    *Special: Despite not having a sheath, Reaper Daiklaves generated by the shard may be used with the Single Point Shining into the Void style as normal. Confusingly, they still mimic the sheathing and drawing action as normal, although no recording states if this act is explicitly mandatory.

                                                    Infinite Regress of Wielding
                                                    Cost: 2m Mins: Essence 1
                                                    Type: Supplemental
                                                    Duration: Instant

                                                    Special Activation Rule: The character must have shattered a weapon on a Melee Decisive Attack and then drawn a new one afterwards in the same battle.
                                                    SAR 2: After Essence 2, AND shattering a blade on a Thrown Decisive attack, the character may create up to [Essence +1] blades instead.

                                                    Cost: 3m Mins: Essence 1
                                                    Type: Movement
                                                    Duration: Instant
                                                    Prereq: Infinite Regress of Wielding

When the Character makes a successful Dodge or takes a Movement (such as a disengage, rush, or the like) they can choose to appear instantly at one of the blades scattered around the battlefield. This requires the character to have non shattered blades around them, within Short Range. This action may ignores any obstructions between the character and the target blade, as long as there is line of sight between the two. The character does not suffer any effects from hostile terrain during this instantaneous movement, such as burning ground, poisonous gasses, or acidic rain. The character suffers these effects as normal if they persist where the movement ends.

                                                    SAR: The character must have made a successful stunt to retrieve a Sliver blade while in combat, either as part of a move, dodge, parry, or the like.

                                                    Prereq: Infinite Regress of Wielding, Reflective Path of Manifold Mirrors

                                                    SAR: Must have used Reflective Path of Manifold Mirrors on a blade that was on a hard to stand location, such as a ceiling, high up a mast or tree trunk, the underside of the Behemoth of Thorns, etc. The location should have been reachable only by stunt or an appropriate charm.

                                                    Frost Blades of the Morning Mist
                                                    Cost: 5m Mins: Essence 3
                                                    Type: ???
                                                    Duration: Scene

                                                    Arguably the most famous Evocation of the Sliver of the Infinite, the character produces dozens of blades in a flash and throws them outward in every direction. Every surface within Short Distance is so packed with blades that the character is never more than arm's reach from one. The indiscriminate nature of this maneuver is such that all characters withing Short Range, friend and foe alike, must make an attempt to protect themselves or suffer damage as from a thrown attack. The attack's indiscriminate nature makes it easy enough to avoid, however, (Easy dodge or block or parry, I don't math here).                                                             Black Wind is great, but it's definitely not for everyone. I decided to play around with some concepts for other high-powered Thrown artifacts, and landed on something that is along the lines of "Spider-Man meets the Hulk", with a little Batman-style "jump off a cliff and figure out how not to die on the way down" thrown in for good measure. It's meant for everyone who treats the Feats of Strength chart as a bucket list. Comments welcome and criticism actively sought!

                                                            Shackles of the Steel Dragon (Moonsilver Hearthstone Bracers, Artifact 5)

                                                            Among the members of the Solar Deliberative, few were as widely respected as Taenn Mountain-born, a scholar and philosopher renown for his humility and tact. While many of his colleagues were, perhaps, more popular than him, and the majority may have been more accomplished in martial or arcane arts, Taenn was well-liked for his gentle manner and conscientiousness...

                                                            ... outside of battle, anyway. In a fight, Taenn was a terror, not only to his enemies, but to his allies and himself. Prone to destructive rages, he wielded every weapon available to him to destroy those persistent enough to draw his ire, including rocks, furniture, horses, trees, and hapless bystanders. Disturbed by his approach to combat, the sages of Heaven attempted to calm him through study of the martial arts, ritual intoxication, and finally peaceful seclusion. When at last Taenn collapsed a marble temple on himself, they turned to his wife, a gifted No Moon weaver of moonsilver threads. If anyone could mend the cracks in his temper, it would be her, and they beseeched her for assistance despite her curious disregard for her husband's temper.

                                                            She said that she would require the hearts blood of a species hunted to extinction, which the cruel among them spilled for her. She said that she would require the hide of an elemental dragon too bloated with power to be capable of reason, which the mighty among them skinned for her. She said that she would require a sample of the Wyld itself, so pure that even a Raksha could not look upon it, which the blind among them found for her. She said that she would require provisions stolen from the Sun's liquor cabinets, which the daring among them procured for her. Finally she said that she would need two hairs from Luna's own head, which were freely given. With all of these materials, she said that she might fashion shackles capable of binding even her husband's ill-hidden rage.

                                                            She lied. Instead, she made him these.

                                                            The Shackles of the Steel Dragon are moonsilver hearthstone bracers that whisper voiceless temptations of reckless action to their wearer. When a hearthstone is presented to them by their wearer, the moonsilver withdraws around the top of the forearms to reveal orichalcum settings, one in each bracer. Should one of the Solar or Lunar Exalted spend five additional motes (for a total of six) when attuning these bracers, they may ignore penalties for fighting with an improvised weapon, and treat mundane throwing weapons as if they were artifacts of the equivalent size. This improves accuracy and damage, but does not allow weapons which do not normally return to the wielder to do so. Additionally, the following evocations may be awakened:


                                                            Inertia-Preserving Focus
                                                            Cost: --; Mins: Essence 1
                                                            Type: Permanent
                                                            Keywords: Uniform
                                                            Duration: One Round
                                                            Prerequisite Charms: None
                                                            Every time the Exalt uses a thrown weapon to damage an opponent or succeed at a gambit, she gains a non-charm success to all movement actions and (Strength + Athletics) checks until the end of her next turn. If the weapon was improvised, she may reflexively ready another improvised weapon immediately, so long as the new weapon is heavier than the previous one, a condition which will eventually require feat of strength checks. If such a check fails, Inertia-Preserving Focus is disabled for the rest of the scene unless the Exalt takes a Miscellaneous Action to catch her breath.

                                                            Special Activation Rules: This Evocation awakens at no experience cost when the Solar voluntarily initiates combat against an opponent with a Strength score at least 3 higher than her own.
                                                            (If you ignore the imposition of escalation, the second half of this Evocation is flatly superior to Spitting Hand Technique when it comes to improvised weapons, but it doesn't apply to normal armaments. I'm of two minds about this; I feel a little guilty, but it IS an Artifact 5.)


                                                            Steel Souls Tethered
                                                            Cost: 2m; Mins: Essence 1
                                                            Type: Reflexive
                                                            Keywords: Uniform
                                                            Duration: Indefinite
                                                            Prerequisite Charms: None
                                                            Normally, the shackles empower thrown weapons with a nigh-imperceptible filigree wire of essence that vanishes soon after the weapon's flight concludes. This Evocation reinforces that connection, strengthening it into a visible cord of moonsilver that can retract and loose at the wielder's command. This allows the Exalt to retrieve the weapon without a roll, but that is only the simplest of this evocation's uses. When employed in a stunt that involves movement (perhaps through grappling, rappelling, or swinging), this tether contributes one non-charm success.

                                                            Special Activation Rules: This Evocation awakens at no experience cost when the Solar voluntarily leaps into a drop of at least three range bands.
                                                            (I really just want to play Spider-Man.)


                                                            Stoneheart Avalanche Impact
                                                            Cost: 2m; Mins: Essence 2
                                                            Type: Supplemental
                                                            Keywords: Withering-Only
                                                            Duration: Instant
                                                            Prerequisite Charms: Inertia-Preserving Focus
                                                            Setting aside her reservations to take up arms, the Chosen of the Sun stokes the fury in her breast to new heights in order to cast down an ever more hated foe. This Evocation may only be used when attacking or defending the subject of a Tie. The Solar adds damage dice to the attack equal to her (Essence + Tie's intensity rating). If this attack successfully deals more initiative damage than the Solar's Resolve, the Tie increases in intensity. If the Tie was already Defining, she gains a point of Limit instead. She may only gain Limit once per scene in this fashion. An intimacy escalated by this Evocation may not be voluntarily eroded until the next New Moon.

                                                            Special Activation Rules: This Evocation awakens at no experience cost when the Solar voluntarily enters combat for no material purpose other than to defend the subject of a negative Tie.
                                                            (Twice the cost of Fists of Iron, half the scope, but no worrying about the edge case of an opponent without soak to ignore. On top of that, it gives you probably-unwanted intimacy escalation and Limit! Isn't that great?!)


                                                            Selfsame Projectile Method
                                                            Cost: 6m, 1wp; Mins: Essence 3
                                                            Type: Reflexive
                                                            Keywords: Decisive-Only
                                                            Duration: Instant
                                                            Prerequisite Charms: Steel Souls Tethered
                                                            Immediately following a thrown withering attack that crashes its target, the Solar may use this Evocation to follow up with an immediate Brawl or Martial Arts decisive attack. The thread connecting her to her weapon shines with surging essence and pulls the Solar along the same path as her missile, bringing her into contact with her opponent in a blur of sudden motion. This counts as an automatically successful disengage check, if one is necessary, and may take the Solar up to two range bands. The target may not exercise reflexive movement of any kind until the follow-up attack is complete. If the follow-up attack is made unarmed, the Smashing tag is available for use.

                                                            Special Activation Rules: This Evocation awakens at no experience cost when the Solar accomplishes initiative shift against a significant opponent.
                                                            (This gives you a timing advantage as if you crashed someone from an initiative score below theirs, without requiring that low starting initiative. That's... significant, and worthy of an Essence 3 evocation for a game-defining artifact. On top of that, it's one hell of a ride! Weeee!)


                                                            Shattered Treasure Vengeance
                                                            Cost: 10m, 1wp; Mins: Essence 4
                                                            Type: Simple
                                                            Keywords: Decisive-Only
                                                            Duration: Instant
                                                            Prerequisite Charms: Stoneheart Avalanche Impact, Selfsame Projectile Method
                                                            This Evocation is a last resort reserved for moments of greatest triumph or greatest tragedy. A draconian claw of moonsilver will extend at up to medium range to snatch the weapon and return it to the Solar's hands. Overloading it with the essence of reckless violence, she casts it at her enemies and it explodes into a plume of white-hot essence and razor-sharp fragments of the invincible. This is a decisive Thrown attack which affects everyone and everything within long range of the target except the Solar herself. Roll the attack once, and deal normal damage to everyone hit, as well as additional dice of damage equaling the artifact rating of the now-lost weapon. If the Solar knows the charm Fiery Solar Chakram, it may be used as a Supplemental charm for this Evocation.

                                                            This Evocation may only be used once per Lunar Cycle. In the case that the owner of the shattered artifact weapon survives the battle, or that the wielder of the Shackles pays an experience cost suitable to purchase the weapon's Artifact background for herself, a curious miracle takes place on the next full moon. The owner--old or new--will discover the once-lost weapon planted in the earth under the light of Luna, whole but not un-scarred. The restored weapon will be mended with moonsilver kintsugi, and any who attune to it will feel a momentary, searing echo of the grief or satisfaction that shattered it.
