                    Apologies for my stretching the "Patrician" definition. You're absolutely correct, non-Terrestrial Nellens members are Dynasts. That said, the Immaculate Philosophy's whole "Dragon Blooded are spiritually more enlightened" might be a basis for the Immaculate Order to do something like "Only the Dragon Blooded possess sufficient spiritual enlightenment to resist the corrupting influence of the Spawn of Hell" ergo making mortal demonology either end up illegal, or at least something that is severely frowned upon.

                    What I was getting at more broadly with the talk of Nellens members was: In 1E/2E, demon-binding being the exclusive province of the Exalted meant that sorcery had far less utility for mortals. Summon Demon of the First Circle was explicitly stated in (In Character) terms, as the most powerful spell of the Terrestrial Circle, because it can be used to essentially replicate so many other spells. Want to go somewhere with Stormwind Rider? Summon and bind an Agatae, have it follow you around immaterially, and you can hop on and fly anywhere you want to go at the drop of a hat. Want to mess with someone's mind, ala Peacock Shadow Eyes? Call up a Todescu, and have it go look the victim in the eyes. You might lose a little specific refinement, but the trade-off was learn one spell, and you get vast utility. When learning individual spells was a commitment of years for mortals, that was huge.

                    By making the full utility of Sorcery available to mortals, it's no longer obvious to a House like Nellens that sending a mortal member to the Heptagram would be a waste of manpower, when you could send them to the House of Bells or prep for the Thousand Scales. Even if getting a mortal Dynast into the Heptagram becomes near-prohibitive, there's more utility to be gained in exchange for taking the risk of taking your sorcery-teaching "In House." Other Houses (like Ledaal) already had a history of doing it, after all. Mortal access to demonology could become something of an equalizer for a House like Nellens, OR (and I consider this more likely) sorcery gets a morally negative association attached to it by the Immaculate Philosophy, to justify keeping it to the Dragon Blooded.

                    If the Immaculates say something like the above "Only the Dragon Blooded possess sufficient spiritual enlightenment to resist the inherent spiritual contamination of the Spawn of Hell" they are still saying "The very presence of Demons is a spiritual corruption." That has negative social connotations for Terrestrial Sorcerers, since just because the Philosophy says the Dragon Blooded are the ones spiritually enlightened enough to resist, it still means sorcerers who traffick with demons are considered to be constantly exposed to spiritual corruption.

                    If the Immaculate Philosophy somehow doesn't condemn the practice of mortal demonology, then more mortal sorcerers enter Dynast society than previously existed, and the Dragon Blooded are bound to push back in some way against something that is empowering non-Dragon Blooded. Either way, it doesn't end up meaning anything socially positive for Dynast sorcerers.
