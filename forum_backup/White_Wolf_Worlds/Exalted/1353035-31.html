    Off-topic, but I want to share some life-wisdom with y'all.

    You never get older. The world gets younger.

    I can remember the days when I first started paying attention to Exalted like they were last week, but I just had to use the words "20 years ago".

    There are probably some of you today who weren't even BORN yet.

    I'm still me. I'm not "one of the old people", because the people I think are older got older along with me. Even if some of them are dead now, most of them are still around.

    But here you are! People younger than me. People like you didn't exist in my world, once. But now there are more people younger than me, and fewer people older.

    It's human nature to notice the changes in the world more than in yourself.

    And just the other day, I was telling a little second-cousin that if she wanted to use the trampoline, she needed to ask the adults, and she asked me, and I realized that from her point of view, I was indistinguishable from any other adult. (So I had to change tactics and inform her that it wasn't my home or trampoline; she had to ask the adults who owned those.)

    formerly Tornado Wolf, formerly Inugami

                                Originally posted by TheCountAlucard View Post
                                ​The Wyld as it's been described at times seems kind of pointless to interact with beyond scourging it out of existence via Lore Charms, because even when it's not in a state of flux, its details could be changed almost trivially by an interested raksha.
                                Exactly.

                                The deepest Wyld is defined by its lack of definition and meaning. Things are so formless that they don't actually exist (Greek Kaos was "nothingness"), and closer to Creation, things only take forms briefly before becoming else so quickly that there's no rhyme or reason; a riot of sound and fury, as told by an idiot, signifying nothing.

                                Creation is the most meaningful place in the universe of Exalted in every sense of the word, which is what makes it the most interesting and rewarding of investment by the people in the setting, by the people who write it, and the people who play in it.

                                The Wyld is only meaningful or interesting to the extent that it's just a region of vanilla Creation that was twisted just slightly weird by the Wyld, so that you can tell a story that would otherwise be impossible in normal Creation. But the region isn't still in flux; the change happened and then stopped, so that any further change is the result of characters or of natural phenomenon (even if that phenomenon is only natural to that region, it still follows self-consistent laws).

                                formerly Tornado Wolf, formerly Inugami

                                    Wacky fun and imaginative? Oh yes indeed. Just not something I care to experience for more than a few minutes. Rather, give me the 100,000 strong army of various fey creatures from the Wyld, and make them a rag-tag band with their own unique desires and cultures that I can interact with without them changing on me.

                                        While we're talking about baggage from the past, tangent, something I would consider if I were helping write a hypothetical Exalted 4th Edition (in 2040 after all the Ex3 books come out)?

                                        I don't think Exalted needs the Great Curse, really. Limit is said to be part of it, but Occasional Emotional Outbursts don't seem to fit the Slow But Steady Fall From Grace the Great Curse is described as.

                                        So, hypothetically, how would I describe Limit in an Exaltrd where it's not part of the Curse?

                                        "The Exalted are people, but greater. Their abilities, their power, their ambitions, and their emotions are far beyond than they were before their Second Breath. Sometimes, their control slips, and their emotions run rampant and wild. This process is called Limit Break."

                                        So yeah. Sometimes Exalts just have too many Feelings. And I'd probably try to mimic something like Strife in the newest edition of Legend of the Five Rings.

                                        Now, what of the other part of the Great Curse? The decadence and mania of the Solars of the High First Age that led to the Usurpation? Well this may just be me, but I personally don't see a need to blame an external curse for why some people who were blessed with an absurd amount of power, a life span in the thousands of years, and with growing ambitions and desires in a sociopolitical environment that gently approved of them following their whims could have produced enough bad eggs for the Sidereals to go "Right, we should fix this problem"
