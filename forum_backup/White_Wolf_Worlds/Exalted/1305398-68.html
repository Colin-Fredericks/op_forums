                        I think the combat balance is a little more complex than that, though solar do end up comming out on top in the end.

                        At character creation, lunars do have some real advantages over solars in a larger mote pool (which they lose at E3), and needing less investment to hit 'i only take overwhelming' soak levels thanks to shapeshifting or Hybrid Body, plus some decisive boosters from animal forms.

                        Solar advantage at E1 is that their charms are almost always stronger and that they have access to supernal.

                        The end result at E1 is that the lunars will often have raw stats that look more impressive, but once both start popping charms it swings in the solar favour.

                        It's far from a done deal though, a Solar TAP Single-Point stylist will more often than not cut down a big scary animal-form lunar, but if both are stunting the dicepools are close enough that there's considerable risk he'll miss and proceed to run out of motes before the lunar does.

                        As essence increases the solars start to pull away more as their mote pools get larger than the lunars and they get a more complete set of combat charms including Resistance charms etc.

                        There's also larger variance within the solar charms than there is within the lunar charm-set. So solars going for the outliers like stealth-join-battle will pull ahead of the lunars faster just as they pull ahead of other solars.

Cathak Urima is probably around Berit/Deled's level – her Evocations look pretty nuts. Saloy Hin and Linwei are probably similar to Ejava: weaker armour and fewer dice, more applicable charms.

Alpha strike is a losing strategy for DK as he simply won't do enough damage to go through all Arada's health levels in one turn, and in response Arada will use Fulminating Thunderclap Surge, hit (even if he's on his last legs – the DK's best Evasion is 5) and probably kill if he has, like, double digits of initiative thanks to Crimson Fang Bite and Unerring Lightning Razor. The DK basically has to win Join Battle (which 'on average' he shouldn't – Arada's max effort is 1 dice better than his) and constantly win wither exchanges until he can guarantee a kill. Clashes aren't a good idea, Arada's best effort clash is 24 + 1 with 6(!) re-rolls thanks to ULR so the DK is not going to keep winning those.

                                                Arada's probably better than even Ragara in this specific fight (albeit I suspect Ragara wins in a 1v1 between them). Air melee is reaaaaally good at killing people in armour.

                                                Ghandarva's cool but his lack of Overwhelming alone nixes him in this fight. He loses badly near as I can figure.

                                                EDIT 2: I forgot to include Mnemon, and while in other straight-up duels she's not as dangerous as the other guys (parry 5, can only boost it by 2, 12 die base attacks, best effort 19 die + 1 success clashes) she's got a ton of health levels and Unfeeling Earth Meditation (which means she'll ignore all wound penalties as long as she stays in Aura, which in a fight she'll always be). Her fight would just be one massive, massive slog of getting clashed by the DK though. Would be unbelievably boring I fear.
