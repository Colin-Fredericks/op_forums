                                                Originally posted by DrLoveMonkey View Post

                                                I'm curious about where this is coming from. Is it just a symptom of everyone being combat monsters so every enemy needs to have defense 7+ with penalty negation? The damage bonus of DAT and TAT work out like this:

                                                DAT

                                                Defense 0 = +9 damage needing 1 success
                                                Defense 1 = +9 damage needing 2 successes
                                                Defense 2 = +8 damage needing 3 successes
                                                Defense 3 = +7 damage needing 5 successes
                                                Defense 4 = +6 damage needing 7 successes

                                                And TAT does this

                                                Defense 0 = +13 damage needing 1 success
                                                Defense 1 = +13 damage needing 2 successes
                                                Defense 2 = +12 damage needing 4 successes
                                                Defense 3 = +10 damage needing 6 successes
                                                Defense 4 = +8 damage needing 9 successes
                                                Defense 5 = +6 damage needing 12 successes

                                                For either 2 or 3 motes, it does 8 bonus damage and triples your onslaught penalty against a foe with Defense 4 if you get TAT off. The enemy doesn't even have to have a base defense of 4, it could be like a Boar-Tusk Crocodile with a base parry of 5 but already hit with an onslaught or wound penalty.

                                                It only becomes useless or nearly useless against things like Fakharu, who's got Evasion 7 and doesn't suffer onslaught penalties unless you've got a warstrider. If it's just Fakharu though, you don't need any tricks, just facing 5 to 1 odds against combat Solars is going to be way more than enough to kill him easily. If he's not alone target the things that don't have legendary size.
                                                My 3 categories correspond roughly to defense 1-3 (if you have optimized stats and combat charms it doesn't really matter how strong those charms are) , defense 4-5 (3m for a conditional +5 or 6 to damage is decent - compare to Elegant Weapon Repertoire, Kiss of the Sun Concentration, Seven Points of Weakness Strike, Striking Cobra Technique - as is a charm that adds 1 or 2 conditional extra damage for 0 extra motes), and defense 6+ (you are very unlikely to frequently roll 11 successes, and doing so only adds 4 damage for 3m)
                                                Also, all of this seems to assume you have dex 5 charge 8 or similar, which costs 8i to get the charge

                                                On the topic of combat monsters: The attack roll successes needed to to trigger double or triple attack technique against defense x always exceed x. Unless everyone is at such low stats that the bonus dice for withering accuracy make up the difference, triggering double and triple attack technique require the optimization of the attacker exceed the of the defender. Triggering them reliably enough that the mote efficiency doesn't drop below similar charms require even more optimization - if you roll 14 dice against defense 4, you have a 57% chance of triggering DAT for +6 damage, and a 27% chance of triggering TAT for an extra +2, for a total of 3.96 extra damage for 3m and 2 charms. Very few characters roll 14 dice to attack and have defense 4.

                                                All of this seems to be assuming a dex 5, martial arts 5, essence 3 exalt, who has similarly high wits+awareness. Yes, this is a combat monster. Insofar as we pay attention to how well it can fight, the question should be how well it fights in battles that challenge a combat monster. "Can kill 2 greenmaws alongside a friend" is a low bar for a dex 5, martial arts 5, wits 5, awareness 5 essence 3 dragon blood. The greenmaws did not land an attack the entire fight, while spending willpower. Darla never missed, and Rag, the mortal, missed once.

                                                Also - the scorebook exalted quick characters, assuming full excellency, a 1-point stunt, and willpower, have defenses ranging from 8 to 13. Fakharu can enhances his 7 evasion with stunt, willpower, and causing 1s and 2s to subtract successes (which admittedly is weaker against Steel Devil Style than just a defense boost would be)

                                                I think I have been downplaying existing onslaught penalty, though it does play into the "if your exalts outnumber the greenmaws, you can win without charms, or with weak charms, or with strong charms" category. I also tend to allow NPCs to stunt their defense, which makes it slightly worse.
