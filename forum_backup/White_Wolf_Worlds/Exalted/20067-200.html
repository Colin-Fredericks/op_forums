                                                    Originally posted by Daerian View Post
                                                    Cascade of Cutting Terror refresh is: The Solar can reset Cascade of Cutting Terror by hurling away her last missile and then recovering at least a single throwing weapon with a stunt.
                                                    Artifact thrown weapons return to wielder after being thrown.
                                                    So how does it work - is character with artifact thrown weapon unable to ever reset his Cascade in fight, or can he reset it with stunt every time her Infinite Chakram/Skucutter returns after attack?
                                                    Because "never" makes having artifact weapons counterproductive (why should Glaive or Hiraikotsu wielder be penalized? Excalibur or Laevantein wielding Melee guys aren't penalized for their ultimate swords!) and "always and easily" kinda makes one of Thrown ultimate attacks spammable,
                                                    The refresh condition is meant to have the players create cool scenes of picking up projectiles to throw at the enemy; note that it doesn't specify that the weapon(s) you pick up have to be the ones you threw away.

                                                    So I'd go with a "no" for the auto-return, but you probably can throw away your artifact weapon (without having it come back) and then stunt picking it back up and throwing it in the same action next round. You can flurry a ready weapon action with an attack.
