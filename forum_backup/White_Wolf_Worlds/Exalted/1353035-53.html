                Originally posted by TheCountAlucard View Post
                Not all capability is combat capability. The absence of Solar circle sorcery, Wyld-Shaping Technique, and the highest tiers of Solar artifice is plenty to justify a fall from a glorious golden age.

                ​- The absence of Solar circle sorcery means the greatest workings can only be achieved haphazardly and at great cost.
                ​- The absence of Solar artifice means that the greatest manses and artifacts, once lost, will probably never be seen again.
                - The absence of Wyld-Shaping Technique means that any land lost to the Wyld is gone forever.
                Those are major factors for sure, but the absence of the Solar Exalted isn't the whole story.

                Another factor behind the fall of the First Age is that the Solar Exalted and Terrestrial Exalted now existed in a state of total war against each other. Previously, any conflict between these Hosts was specific and personal to the Chosen involved, and they were just as likely to gladly work together.

                The First Age wasn't built by the Solar Exalted alone. It was made by the Solars, Lunars, Sidereals, and Terrestrials all working together.

                The Usurpation brought that cooperation to an end. Now Terrestrial and Sidereal Exalted hunt Solars and Lunars like animals, and Solars and Lunars work to tear down everything that Terrestrials and Sidereals build up.

                The fall of the First Age wouldn't have been nearly so bad if all of the Solars and Lunars had just... disappeared. The Second Age has been greatly shaped by the Silver Pact's long guerrilla war against the Realm, and many First Age wonders must have been lost in final acts of vengeance from the Solar Exalted before they died (and when their next incarnations came back for revenge).

                                                            Originally posted by Neall View Post
                                                            Nah. Aspects indicates that Dragon-Blooded and Liminals are elemental in character - they all possess all of their elements within them, they just lean towards one more than the others. Castes indicates role and purpose. I realize that's a distinction without a difference - it's basically saying there's really only one type of Liminal or Dragon-Blooded, sort of similar to saying how Sidereals don't have castes since they're each Chosen by a different Incarna.
                                                            Originally posted by Sunder the Gold View Post
                                                            I think the easier criteria to understand and accept is that CHOICE is involved with Caste, and determinism is involved with Aspect.

                                                            For Solar Exalted, the Sun chose you to be a Dawn, rather than any other Exaltation that might have been available.

                                                            For Sidereals, a specific Maiden decided to Choose you.

                                                            For Lunars, they choose their own Castes.

                                                            For Alchemical Exalted, the people building them chose them and chose what Caste they would have.

                                                            But there's no such choice involved with Dragon-Blooded; whoever takes the Second Breath is reborn with the Aspect of one of their Dragon-Blooded ancestors. It's automatic and deterministic, even if the determining mechanisms are so minute and finicky that possibly only the god who monitors Terrestrial Exalted could predict it.

                                                            Likewise, the Dark Mother doesn't get to choose what sort of Liminal she breathes into. Their Aspect is determined by the creator of their physical body.
                                                            Revisiting this.

                                                            The other reason I offered an alternative explanation for the "Caste / Aspect" divide is because I didn't think "possess all of their elements within them" worked as an explanation.

                                                            Each Sidereal Exaltation comes from one specific goddess the same way each Terrestrial Aspect comes from one specific dragon, yet each Sidereal contains the Essence of the four other Maidens as well. A Chosen of Battles can still learn the Charms of Secrets, Journeys, Serenity, and Endings, and even Favor them as much as the Charms of Battles.

                                                            Alchemical Exalted are either made with a little of all six metals from the start, or they could end up incorporating all six through their Charms.

                                                            Certainly, Solars and Lunars possess all of their "elements" within them, to whatever extent the Caste Abilities of the Dawn Caste are associated to "Dawn", while a Dawn Caste could also learn and Favor areas of Solar magic associated with the other four Castes.


                                                            Ah, but CHOICE. Choice is what distinguishes the purposefulness of Castes against the serendipity of Aspect.

                                                            While each Caste is associated with certain personalities, mostly they're associated with duties and responsibilities, with the potential for personalities which would normally be at odds with such roles.

                                                            But Aspects reverse this. They are primarily about associated personalities, with roles and responsibilities arising organically from those.

                                                            If Caste is top-down and deliberate, then Aspect is bottom-up and emergent.


                                                            There was a faction of Raksha in Second Edition who were trying to become human -- or at least Creationborn -- by somehow plugging themselves into the reincarnation system. If they succeeded and ended up becoming a new form of Exalted in the process, they would be good candidates for Aspects rather than Castes. Perhaps the Aspects reflect the sort of Raksha they used to be, but while that Raksha was shaped by their choices, the reborn human didn't make those choices anymore than a Fire Aspect made choices for Hesiesh.


                                                            I forget if Getimians are supposed to have Castes or Aspects, but you could make a case for either. They chose how they changed the worlds-that-never-were, so their Castes could arise from their choices.
