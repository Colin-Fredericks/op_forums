                                      Originally posted by Erinys View Post
                                      Or you can murder thousands or millions of people with no consequences whatsoever. Which was the whole point of Arcane Fate after all. Never again being accountable for any actions, no matter how extreme, unless they anger the Bureau of Destiny higher ups.
                                      Uh. No.

                                      Arcane Fate is an unintended consequence. The Sidereals were not planning to break the Mask. That just happened because the need to avoid being purged in the wake of the Usurpation caused them to overstress it. It was supposed to be a one-off.

                                      Second of all, there are other organs of the Heavenly Bureaucracy aside from the Bureau of Destiny, and they have just as much power to hold Sidereals accountable for crimes against their specific society. It is true that at this point Heaven is so corrupt that that isn't much of a guarantee of justice being served... but the Sidereals didn't know that was going to happen (they don't see everything, after all) and they are not happy about it. At the time of the Usurpation Heaven was still functional and they had every expectation it would remain that way.

                                      In that case, no accountability unless you anger a more powerful Sidereal enough that they actually bother to take it out on you.
                                      Also not true. The Sidereals have political enemies who love to move against them, and having actual, for-real dirt on the Sids just makes that even easier.

                                      Example: in 2E, the head of the Convention of Air was executed for criminal activity. It is implied that they weren't actually guilty, although they were probably guilty of something, but a powerful, veteran Sidereal, in good standing with the Bronze Faction, with lots of hard-to-replace experience, was hauled in front of a censor and killed, and Kejak couldn't do shit about it.

                                      That's not precisely justice, but it is a form of accountability that comes outside of the Bureau of Destiny. If a Sidereal rolls around committing all kinds of crimes, well, there are interested parties in holding them accountable, not necessarily because of BURNING JUSTICE, but because they want the Sids taken down a peg.

                                      It is worth noting: when I speak of "crimes" I mean "crimes from the perspective of Heaven." Heaven doesn't necessarily care that you killed and raped your way across An-Teng; they care if you didn't fill out the proper forms to do that or trespassed in the rightful sphere of influence of a god or something.

                                      "SEX NOVA is the kind of person who, after being chosen as the divine champion of the god of heroes, decided to call himself SEX NOVA."

                                                  I think a comic in the Sidereal Corebook portrayed this beautifully. It was a woman and her two daughters arguing the details about a guy who came through and killed a monster in the forest.

                                                  "Yeah, she likes him but she can't even remember his name."

                                                  "... I'm drawing a blank."

                                                  "Remember? He had red hair?"

                                                  "No, his hair was gray, like his beard."

                                                  "Wait, you mean that soldier who killed that beast in the forest?"
