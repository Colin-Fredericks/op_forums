                    On Athletics: it's also important to note that Athletics isn't just the ninja jump and ninja run ability. It also contains superior strength through lifting or breaking. Solars (humor me) also express Athletics through various methods of improving their combat capabilities.

                    An Endings expression of Athletics could be an embodiment of eventuality and the forward march of time. It can represent how all things crumble to dust in the grasp of ages. It can call forth the swift end that all forms of motion face. It can also burn away the Chosen's own life force or consume their focus burning away other details for the chase.

                    I imagine a possible charm tree for Feats of Strength starting with a basic boost for counting strength higher for minimums. Then from there a charm that enhances suffering objects. Eventually I could see an essence 5 charm that could turn let you break an intimacy by tightly grasping a mortals hand. Maybe even end an abstract concept or break strange things normally not capable of being suffered by strength, like literally breaking enchantments. Though that's the Feats of Strength sub-tree I'm thinking about, and likely a sub-branch of that. Strength enhancing is pretty easy to think about when it comes to how to end things.

                    Charms that enhances chases are obvious. You're chasing them to end them. Maybe weird combat charms that let chases enhance a decisive attack? Nah, not too interesting but.... Hmmm...

                    Leaping lends well to flashy assassin ninjas of course.

                    I guess what I'm saying is "just from looking at the solar charmset in 3e, I expect the FoS and combat tricks to get plenty of love"

                                                        Yeah, the ones I posted were what Vance suggested; if he intended to give Sidereals more choice, I guess he'd say.

                                                        DBs don't really need a spread because a)no supernals, and b)each one of them comes with a pretty wide set of caste abilities. They all have at least 1 combat, 1 social, and 1 ability that's useful outside combat and socialising.
                                                        Not to mention they're not supposed to be as powerful as Solars anyway, and that Aspects, I think, are more limiting than castes in some ways.

                                                        That doesn't necessarily mean no other Exalt types will have it. Just that DBs, and it seems Sidereals, won't. I imagine it'll be decided on a case-by-case basis.


                                                        For example, the last Vance said about Lunars is that they will get a choice, just like Solars.

                                                        Solars get 20% of their abilities as caste, and get to choose these from 32% of the total number of abilities.
                                                        They then get to choose another 20% of their abilities, out of all the remaining ones.

                                                        Lunars will get 22.222...% of their attributes as caste, and get to choose these from 33.333...% of the total number of attributes.
                                                        Then they'll get to choose another 22.222...% of their attributes as favoured, out of all the remaining ones.

                                                        (If you didn't quite follow that, I mean Vance said he's currently going with Lunars choosing 2 caste attributes from a choice of 3, and then 2 favoured attributes. So very similar to how Solars do it.)

                                                        This is exactly how my group has ran it for years, and it works very well.


                                                        It's possible Vance has changed his mind, but I think it's a good way to do it.
