    Which Ability to put these Sidereal Charms?

    03-28-2016, 04:14 PM
    Yes, yes. I know Sidereals have a closed charm set, but since the Ex3 Sidereal book is a long time coming, I'm going to brainstorm some charms. I got a few charms in mind, but having trouble figuring out which ability to put them. With the Sidereal Charm set, the first ability that comes to mind isn't really the right answer.

    Atmospheric Analysis Deliberation

    This feels like a Secrets Charm, but the Serenity abilities seem to fit best. Linguistics? Socialize? Lore? Survival due to the "talking about the weather" aspect?

    Sometimes a Sidereal need to relay information to an individual without the rest of listeners knowing. The Sidereal must initiate physical contact, like a hug or a handshake, to activate the charm on an individual. Then the Sidereal can start discussion about an innocuous topic, like the weather or gardening. The target of the charm will understand the Sidereal’s words as a metaphor for a topic of the Sidereal’s choice.


    Alternate Destiny Accommodation

    Should probably be a Journey’s ability, but this charm fits none of their Ex2 abilities. Occult? Bureaucracy?

    It is not easy for even a Sidereal to be in two places at once. This charm allows a Sidereal to loan one of her inactive Resplendent Destinies to another Sidereal. The Sidereal with the loaned destiny can don the destiny as usual, but is unable to use any of its resplendency powers. (may add something about increase paradox, but who knows what is going to happen with paradox in Ex3?)

                      I've been reading though the Maiden Scriptures and now I am reconsidering what ability this charm matches. The Scripture of the Savory Maiden (Larceny) seems to fit better since it talks about serving the world. I also doubt the Sidereal acquired the location, supplies, and resources to start the business via legal means.

                      Pho Cart Methodology
                      Cost: 5 Motes, 1 WP; Mins: Bureaucracy 3, Essence 2; Type: Simple
                      Keywords: None
                      Duration: Seven Days
                      Prerequisite Charms: None
                      The Sidereal can set up a small established business in a town. This Business will neither make or lose money. Residents will patron the business as if it was always there. At the end of the charm's duration, the business will vanish and the town's inhabitants will not remember its existence.

                      Essence 4+: The duration of the business is extended to the end of the current story line.

                                      The Pho Cart Methodology is less the forgetting the business afterwards and more the quick set up of an established business. Through use of this charm, Fate will arrange for the Business to have patrons and maybe? employees. If someone asks about the business, a local will be able to tell that person about it. No need to advertise or fiddle around with paperwork or hire vendors for supplies. It's basically the Respledent Destiny version of a business. Yes, a Sidereal can spend some time actually doing the footwork to create a business, but that takes time to setup and build a reputation. Sidereals don't have Speed the Wheels or anything. So this charm allows them spend some motes and willpower and bam! - they have a business.

                                      Probably should edit the charm to make that clear or add "develop a business plan on a prayer scroll" to the charm.
