    Help Me Name My Sidereal

    07-07-2014, 08:21 AM
    So, my character's gonna be a trickster mentor-type Gold Faction Chosen of Secrets for our group of naive and inexperienced solars (and their less naive and inexperienced lunar mates). The question is: how should I name him? (I also need a name for the Resplendent Destiny I'm currently wearing.)

    Since I'm incapable of taking things seriously, I've decided to follow my usual practice of "when in doubt, use a Culture ship name". A few I've thought were semi-appropriate (some of them are, others still need to be exalt-ified):

    Credibility Problem: it's a very sidereal mentor-name.
    Prosthetic Conviction: 'cause I'm the high-conviction do-what-must-be-done kind of guy amidst a group of idealists. I'm somewhat iffy on using the word "prosthetic" in a fantasy game, though.
    Fallen Outside of Normal Moral Constraints, Victim of the Ambient Morality and Ethics Gradient also riff off this theme, kinda.
    Reasonable Excuse and Honest Mistake are perhaps more appropriate to Bronze Faction, but the latter could go either way, really. If we're talking factions, Recent Convert is a perfectly good goldie name.
    Wit Amidst Folly: mentor for a group of naive solars, again. Also, Chosen of Secrets, being smug is part of the job.
    Fairly good Chosen of Secrets names also include Unreliable Witness and Graceful Avoidance of Awkward Facts.

    I need to choose two, and convert them to the standard Adjective-plus-Noun-related-to-nature format of Exalted names. What do you think?

    Evocations for the demonic tattoos gained from the Pact with Mara sorcerous initiation || Pyre-Kindler (Soulsteel and Red Jade Grimscythe, Artifact 3) || Tenebrous Descent (Stormcaller's Black Jade Reaver Daiklave cousin, Artifact 5)

                                      Ooh, I like these... as for suggestions:

                                      Unfortunate Conflict of Evidence, maybe? Although, as a name, it might be a little contrived.
                                      Congenital Optimist doesn't have that problem. It'd be great for someone with that outlook - or hysterically ironic if he's more that other type.
                                      Stranger Here Myself is also appropriate for a Siddie (or their resplendent destiny cover), if perhaps a little on-the-nose.
                                      Wisdom Like Silence sounds seamlessly Exalted-y to me.
                                      Sober Counsel is also not too bad on that score, in addition to sounding mentor-like.
                                      Ditto Conventional Wisdom, or Lucid Nonsense for a more humorous spin.
                                      Inappropriate Response and Lapsed Pacifist also stick out has having potential, though maybe more for a RedSid - the latter especially.

                                      Less character-specific, but both plausible and hilarious when you consider that in most cultures it's usually the parents who do the naming:

                                      Youthful Indiscretion
                                      I Blame Your Mother
                                      Someone Else's Problem

                                      And those are just the ones that don't call for much adjusting. You've pointed me to a wonderful resource here.

                                      Also, because apparently I can't not see something like this without instantly relating it to Abyssals, I'm totally gonna try to do something with The End of Invention at some point.

                                      Also also, Irregular Apocalypse. Just because it sounds far too badass not to mention.
