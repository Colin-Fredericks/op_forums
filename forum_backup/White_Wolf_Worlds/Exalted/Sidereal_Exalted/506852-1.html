                        Originally posted by Erinys View Post
                        But I'm talking about personal accountability for personally committed acts, not being opposed by somebody who just hates Sidereals in general, and you happened to be one.

                        What are the actual circumstances under which a non-Sidereal can remember a Sidereal's face and name and deeds, and connect those three together, and also know they're a Sidereal? This isn't explained in the corebook, so my understanding is vague beyond "nobody inside Fate remembers them."
                        It's actually super simple, and the mechanics are very similar across editions. I'm going to use the 2E ones, but the 1E ones are pretty similar.

                        First of all, four categories of beings automatically are immune to Arcane Fate:
                        • Other Sidereals
                        • All Celestial gods and all beings outside of Fate
                        • Any official or functionary of the Bureau of Destiny
                        • Acquaintances and Familiars of the Sidereal in question

                        So damn near anyone in Yu-Shan a Sidereal is likely to meet doesn't give a shit about Arcane Fate.

                        For everyone else? Wits + Integrity roll, difficulty 1, -3 dice pool penalty. You have to make this roll every time you try and remember the Sidereal as a Sidereal until you fulfill one of three conditions:
                        • You succeed with more successes than the Sids Essence rating on a single roll. From then on you remember them and don't need to roll.
                        • You succeed on a number of rolls equal to the Sids Essence rating. From then on you remember them and don't need to roll.
                        • You fail. You forget them.

                        The thing is, it is not hard to do that for a lot of Exalts. A lot of Lunars are going to have a dice pool that lets them take an auto-success on that roll. Hell, it's possible for a mortal to have a dice pool that lets them take an auto-success on that roll, with appropriate specialties. The rules explicitly allow Excellencies to be invoked and Willpower to be spent on this roll.

                        Extras are gonna forget about a Sidereals en masse real real quick. Other people? Not so much. This is why they stooge around wearing Resplendencies and assumed identities.

                        Originally posted by Ghosthead View Post

                        Memory might be off on this, do you wanna chuck some First Edition Sidereals cites on that at me? I haven't got the books to hand either or the time to look through them either though, so no probs if that's not possible for you either.
                        Do you mechanically or fluff-wise?

                        Mechanically it's substantially similar to 2E. There are some differences (it's Wits + Occult, not Integrity, and the intervals for rolling are different) but basically the same roll.

                        Fluff-wise, the breaking of the Mask in 1e was very hand-wavey. There isn't an explicit reason given for it, it's just sort of stated "the Sidereals were withdrawing from the world after the Solar Purge, and they used the Mask so much in such a short period of time they busted it." That's on page 120-121 of the 1e Sids core. However, it is explicitly in 1e still an accident. They didn't mean for it to happen, and it is an inconvenience to them.
