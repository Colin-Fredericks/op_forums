                                        The assumption is that Sidereal Exaltation start at birth; the Fivescore Fellowship uses vaguely-defined astrological prediction magic to track the birth down and then shape the infant's life until adolescence and the realization of the Exaltation. This can involve meddling in the child's life (if its birth circumstancces are discovered to be fortuitous) or kidnapping the infant and planting it in a wholly manufactured community (more common). Rarely, things can go wrong and Heaven can either fail to find the Exaltation or can lose track of the growing child, but usually things proceed within acceptable tolerance of just-as-planned.

                                        This makes faking your death as a Sidereal really hard!

                                                            If you have to fake your death at all, one assumes you're dealing with people who can see through a Resplendent Destiny, because otherwise, why bother?

                                                            So, Stephenls' concept of finding someone who looks a lot like you and kidnapping them has merit. Especially if you need a corpse, and can't get by on explosions/falling off a cliff/being swallowed by a Tyrant Lizard/whatever. HINT: always try to not leave a body if possible. Much fewer things can potentially go wrong.

                                                            But if you DO need a body, and lack the time to find a body double, there's always Spirits! Get a shapechanging spirit, bind them, and have them assume your form, play dead for a while, and then Dematerialize and leave. Pretty straightforward, and even if your "killers" do saw off the head to make sure, at most, they'll uncover the spirit - you're out of there.

                                                            If you don't even have time for that, just lie really still (Ebon Shadow Style is helpful here, as in many things), and once they start to bury/cremate you, Avoidance Kata out. It's not a perfect solution (these days, it seems to get harder and harder using Avoidance Kata after accomplishing anything else...), but it'll do in a pinch.

                                                            All of that can be avoided if you simply use sorcery (at crudest, a Neomah - more sophisticated methods are better) to create an exact double of yourself, kill them cleanly, and then throw the body in an Elsewhere Egg. It won't decay, and if you ever need to die on short notice, just call the egg, drop the corpse, use the appropriate Someone Else's Destiny variant to give them any distinctive wounds/poisons/curses your would-be killers know to look for, and get out of there (Avoidance Kata optional, but preferred). It even works for escaping tedious social events with annoying co-workers (or any given Mouth Of Peace).
