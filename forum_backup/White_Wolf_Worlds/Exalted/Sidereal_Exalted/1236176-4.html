    First,
    I never thought that "The Bronze should have foreseen the Great Contagion/Balorian Crusade" arguments were compelling. I always took the stance that, logically, *anyone* should have not prognostically, but common-sense-wise foreseen that Some Sort of Vast, Creation-Totality-Threatening Grave Existential Threat was the inevitable result of pink-slipping 6/7ths of the Celestial Exalts. Trying to 20/20-hindsight-blame the Bronze for not foreseeing the specifics didn't hold a lot of water, to me.

    Second, I took it as a single coin-flip. Heads, Creation goes down in flames. Tails, problem solved, and Super-Utopia arrives. I draw the linkage that the Bronze's unwilling-to-flip the coin is the exact same thing as saying "In exchange for preventing total annihilation, we knowingly accept that things LIKE the Great Contagion and Balorian Crusade will happen." I've always felt like Bronze Faction proponents believe the level of "lessening" the Bronze thought they were buying was represented by the Shogunate, and the Contagion/Crusade came out of left field. I take the stance that logic, entirely independent of the ability to foretell the future, made it well understood such things would happen, and THAT was the price they chose to pay.

    Where do I get this from? The fact that, consistently, Chejop Kejak has ALWAYS been shown to never have reconsidered the Usurpation, not for a moment, even after the Contagion and Crusade. If those events were not cause to reconsider what they did, he considers them acceptable, anticipated, collateral damage.

    THAT, I consider more monstrous than one coin-flip for the fate of Creation.

    Edit: It goes deeper than that though. Part of the reason I find Nazi's Independents so compelling, is that they take the position It is not the place of Sidereals to decide Fate, it is their job to execute Fate. Basically like how the traditional conception of the Grim Reaper doesn't decide who dies, he makes sure that those who are "supposed" to die, do. That the Bronze and Gold have both become so caught up in Pro-Solar/Anti-Solar agendas, they consider the original job the Sidereal Exalted were created for the secondary focus, and their Solar-centric agendas their primary focus. Whereas Nazri proclaims that, IF there must be Pro-Solar/Anti-Solar agendas, they should clearly be something secondary to the Sidereal Mission Statement. NOT something that divides the Five Score Fellowship to the point of betraying that Mission Statement. Nazi looks at issues like the political war being waged over the Chair of the Convention of Air on purely Bronze/Gold lines, and fears for the Sidereal Exalted as a whole.

    I also took things like the 1E Cult book saying Ghataru was ready to go off the reservation and deploy the Aerial Legion in defense of the Cult's Solar-training camps as implying there was reason to believe the Bronze would attack such camps if they could find them, and throw down with the Gold Sidereals present if necessary to get at the Solars. The entire implication there is one of violence ready to kick off, minus the logistics of one side not knowing where the battle-to-be was yet.
