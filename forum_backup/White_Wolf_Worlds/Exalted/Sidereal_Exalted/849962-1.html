        Based on the volume of changes, I decided to create a new post rather than perform an edit in place.

        CHANGELOG:
        * In general, these changes are meant to emphasize the Mast more- which meant moving the soak-enhancing charms out. The Mast is stoic and strong, but it provides no protection- that is more the purview of the Shield, which is where such charms will be found.
        * In particular...
        *** Someone Else's Destiny will be moved to Athletics (The Rising Smoke, which governs murder. Nothing really explicitly covers poison that I could find)
        *** Optimistic Security Practice, Water and Fire Legion, and Storm's Eye Stance will all be moving to Presence (The Shield - Protection)
        * Broke the 'reshuffle ability points' element of Resplendent Destiny of the Mast into a repurchase, and limited the abilities it can fill in to limit the omni-competence problem.
        * There are several new charms. In part, this is because there are many more hooks, and Sidereals should have tools to interact with them. It is also to better represent the breadth of the constellation- The Mast governs Stoicism, Tools, Architecture, Manual Labor, Silence and Ignorance.

        Resplendent Destiny of the Mast
        Cost: - (10m); Mins: Resistance 1, Essence 1
        Type: Permanent
        Keywords: Mute
        Duration: Permanent
        Prerequisite Charms: None

        The Sidereal creates a new persona, a character of the Mast. The intimacies selected must be appropriate for an archtype of the Mast, relating to the appropriate associations.
        For example, the Resplendent Destiny may have a tie of admiration towards Ragara Yukio, a noted architect and geomancer; or perhaps a principle of disdain towards scholars and other individuals of book learning.

        An Essence 2+ repurchase of this charm allows you to more fully realize the persona. Redistribute all dots you have in Linguistics, Investigation, Socialize, Lore or Bureaucracy amongst Resistance, Ride, Sail, Survival, Thrown, Craft, Dodge, Athletics, and Brawl.
        You may raise Resistance to 6.

        ----

        Ox-Body Technique
        Cost: -; Mins: Resistance 1, Essence 1
        Type: Permanent
        Keywords: Stackable
        Duration: Permanent
        Prerequisite Charms: None

        As per the Solar Charm 'Ox-Body Technique', except:

        Stamina 1-2: An additional -1 health level
        Stamina 3-4: An additional -0 health level
        Stamina 5+: An additional -0 and -2 health level

        ----

        Forward Thinking Technique
        Cost: 1m; Mins: Resistance 3, Essence 1
        Type: Simple
        Keywords: None
        Duration: Until released
        Prerequisite Charms: None

        A laborer is nothing without their tools; by the virtue of the Mast, you are never so deprived. You may always call to hand any mundane tool you need for a task you are presently working on. The item vanishes once it is no longer being used for the job it was called for, and may never be either sold, traded or used in a fight.

        ----

        Two Thousand Hands And Two
        Cost: 5m; Mins: Resistance 4, Essence 1
        Type: Simple
        Keywords: Stackable, Design
        Duration: Indefinite
        Prerequisite Charms: Forward Thinking Technique

        The blessing of the Mast ensures that your labor is swift and easy- even the largest projects fall before you in short order. Each time you use this charm, you gain a Major Project slot for as long as you keep the motes committed. You may not purchase more Major Project slots than you have dots of Essence.
        If you work on the project exclusively in the destiny of the Mast, you may re-roll all 1s and 2s on your Craft rolls.

        At Essence 3, you may instead commit 15m to gain a Superior Project slot. Such a slot may only be used for large-scale mundane works, such as building a ship or a palace, and enable to Sidereal to work on the project alone- seemingly tirelessly accomplishing what should require teams of people. You may not purchase more than one such Superior Project slot at a time.
        Using this Charm makes the projects a matter of destiny; those interested in them may note the omens prophesying their successful completion.

        ----

        The Wisdom of Drudges
        Cost: -; Mins: Resistance 5, Essence 2
        Type: Permanent
        Keywords: None
        Duration: Permanent
        Prerequisite Charms: Two Thousand Hands and Two

        Hard work is it's own reward, and the dreamless sleep that accompanies a day's labor is sweeter than wine. You gain 1 silver point per 10 rolled on any Basic Project, 1 gold point per 10 rolled on any Major Project, and 3 gold points per 10 rolled on any Superior Project.
        If you work on the project exclusively in the destiny of the Mast, you may re-roll 10s as well, gaining successes and bonus points, until no 10s appear.

        ----

        Scry the Hearthstone
        Cost: 3m, 1lhl; Mins: Resistance 4, Essence 2
        Type: Simple
        Keywords: None
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisite Charms: Forward Thinking Technique

        All architects belong to the Mast, and the blood and sweat of builders sanctify every structure to the stars. By giving an offering of blood, you may gain an intuitive understanding of the layout of a building- including any secret doors, hidden rooms, or mechanical traps.
        You do not learn anything magical about the building- if an illusion conceals a door, you may know the door is there, but still not be able to see it. Nor do you learn anything of the building's occupants.

        At Essence 3, you may also spend 1 willpower when activating Scry the Hearthstone; if you do, you also learn where all of the building's occupants are at that moment.

        Scry the Hearthstone may not be used on buildings raised by sorcery or the glamours of the Fair Folk.

        ----

        Strength of the Mast
        Cost: 1m / dot; Mins: Resistance 4, Essence 1
        Type: Supplemental
        Keywords: None
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisite Charms: Resplendent Destiny of the Mast

        Under the blessing of the Mast, you can perform improbable feats of brute strength. For a single attack or feat of strength, you may temporarily increase your Strength score by 1 per mote of Essence. You may not more than double your natural Strength with this charm.

        ----

        A Place to Stand
        Cost: 3m / die; Mins: Resistance 5, Essence 2
        Type: Supplemental
        Keywords: None
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisite Charms: Strength of the Mast

        Beyond improbable, you can now do the impossible- engaging in feats of strength that defy reason or explanation. For every three motes spent, set aside one die on a Feat of Strength roll; that die is automatically a 10. You may set aside no more than your Resistance in dice this way.

        ----

        Ignorance is Bliss
        Cost: 3m; Mins: Resistance 3, Essence 1
        Type: Reflexive
        Keywords: None
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisite Charms: None

        The Mast knows nothing- neither of the wider world, nor of their own limits. For a moment, neither do you- all pain and fatigue fading into a fog of ignorance. You may ignore all penalties caused by pain, fatigue, poison, or any other purely internal affliction for one action.
        Additionally, you may forget yourself for a moment- concealing your true emotions beneath a mask of guileless ignorance. Add half your Resistance to your Guile or on any social action to convince someone of your simple-mindedness as non-charm successes.

        ----

        Invisible at the Center
        Cost: 5m; Mins: Resistance 5, Essence 2
        Type: Simple
        Keywords: Design
        Duration: Indefinite
        Prerequisite Charms: Ignorance is Blisss

        Laborers are everywhere, and yet they easily go unseen; one dullard drudge looking much like the next. You share in this unique invisibility; as long as you are performing hard manual labor with a group, you are invisible.
        Taking any overt actions or participating in activities other than manual labor ends this charm.
        Certain Charms may aid in detecting the Exalt. Roll the Sidereals’s ([Stamina or Strength] + Stealth) against her opponent’s relevant (Attribute + Ability) or Resolve, if the attacking Charm is based on the opponent’s Integrity

        Each week past the first, destiny strains a little more at the deception. Omens begin to declare the presence of the Sidereal amongst the workers, and the difficulty to understand these omens drops by 1 per week. Weeks in which you wear the Destiny of the Mast do not count; Creation expects laborers to go unnoticed.

        ----

        Unwavering Well-Being Meditation
        Cost: 1m / level; Mins: Resistance 5, Essence 2
        Type: Reflexive
        Keywords: Decisive-only
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisite Charms: Ignorance is Bliss

        Like the Mast, you bear punishment with stoic silence, drawing strength from the pain and victory seemingly from the jaws of defeat. When struck by a decisive blow, you may spend up to 1m per level of damage you take. You gain 1 initative per mote spent in this fashion. You may not spend more than your dots of Essence on this charm.

        ----

        Heartless Maiden Trance
        Cost: 10m; Mins: Resistance 5, Essence 3
        Type: Simple
        Keywords: None
        Duration: Indefinite
        Prerequisite Charms: Unwavering Well-Being Meditation, Invisible at the Center

        You refine the lessons of ignorance, and learn to swallow your heart- becoming immune to all distresses and distractions of the body. While under the effects of Heartless Maiden Trance, you may ignore all penalties caused by pain, fatigue, poison, or any other purely internal affliction. You will not fall unconscious when your Incapacitated health level is taken; dying only when you have accrued (Stamina) additional levels of damage.
        Furthermore, poison, starvation and dehydration hang in abeyance until the charm ends, whereupon they all take effect at once.
        Finally, while under the Heartless Maiden Trance, you draw no nourishment from the world, and respire no essence outside of combat. Even in combat, the numbing chill of heartlessness leaves you respiring only 3 motes per turn.

        ----

        Unswerving Juggernaut Principle
        Cost: 5m, 1wp; Mins: Resistance 3, Essence 1
        Type: Reflexive
        Keywords: Destiny
        Duration: Indefinite
        Prerequisite Charms: None

        You weave your arrival into the destiny of a location- as long as you do nothing but run towards that destination, you can continue to run towards that destination. While Unswerving Juggernaut Principle is active, you do not need to eat, drink or rest, and ignore damage or impairment from any natural environmental hazard.
        In order to activate Unswerving Juggernaut Principle, you must be able to physically reach your destination by running, although you can also ride or take other transportation to arrive sooner. You must always be moving closer to your destination, however, and cannot stop to involve yourself in festivals or fighting; doing so ends the effects of the charm.

        While active, omens and portents will herald the arrival of the Sidereal.

        ----

        One Direction Invocation
        Cost: 10m, 1wp; Mins: Resistance 5, Essence 4
        Type: Simple
        Keywords: Design
        Duration: Indefinite
        Prerequisite Charms: The Wisdom of Drudges, Scry the Hearthstone, A Place to Stand, Unswerving Juggernaut Principle, Heartless Maiden Trance

        You inscribe a prayer strip on one side with the scripture of the Eternal Maiden, and on the reverse, a pledge of your name to accomplish some goal. The strip bursts into golden flame, and your accomplishment is written into the Heavens for the wise to recognize.
        While under the effect of One Direction Invocation, you gain a Defining Principle to accomplish your stated goal; this Principle may also be called on an unlimited number of times in social influence. You also suffer a -3 situational penalty to all actions which do not directly facilitate achieving your goal.
        However, any non-aggravated damage you have suffered heals at a rate of one level of lethal or bashing per turn, while aggravated damage heals at a rate of 1 level per day.

        At any time, you may end this Charm; it will also end automatically if your goal becomes impossible. If the charm ends with your goal not having been accomplished, your name and identity are forfeit; you must requisition a new name from Heaven.

            Very rough cut of Sidereal's Ride - this tree is causing me all manner of heartburn.

            Ride: The Messanger
            Trappings: The Messenger has a mount and rides on urgent business. She is daring and armed. She carries a package, from which she cannot be separated.
            Correlations: Duty, bravery, arrogance, journeys, communication, selflessness, news

            The Scripture of the Desirable Maiden
            Once, there was a maiden…
            …whom many men wanted for their own.
            She stepped as softly as starlight.
            She shone with glory.
            And one by one, they came to claim her.
            She fled by the quickest route she found.
            “Why do you run?” the storm clouds said.
            She said, “I cannot help who drives me.”

            Ordained Bridle of Mercury (Get a familiar)
            Spirit-Shape Companion (Promote a familiar to demi-godhood)
            Godly Companion (Promote a familiar to full godhood)
            Breaking the Wild Mortal (Get an Acquintance)
            Yellow Path (Fastest route)
            Glory Path (Increase speed, ignore natural obsticles)
            Riding the Dragon (Turn an acquintance into a motherfucking Dragon)

            Iron Heart, Iron Horse: Perfect bravery while carrying a message
            Blow Wind Style: Ignore fatigue
            Field Mouse Rider: Ride your familiar, no matter what it is

            Spirit-Shape Companion and Godly Companion are moved (probably to the Sorcerer [Occult])
            Breaking the Wild Mortal is moved to either the Pillar or the Lovers (although the name may change)

            ---------

            Resplendent Destiny of the Messenger: Create a persona. With a (Essence++) repurchase, you gain a divine Familiar as a mount, usable only while you wear the Destiny. (Equivalent to Familiar 3)

            Ordained Bridle of Mercury: Get a horse! (Or other appropriate mount). Basically, whistle up Shadowfax or giant eagle rescue. (Getting a flying mount should probably be an Essence ++ benefit)

            Iron Heart: Immune to fear. Your mount is also immune to fear.

            Yellow Path: You know the fastest route to some destination. With a repurchase, you know the fastest route to achieve some goal. Fastest is not necessarily safest.

            Glory Path: While traveling the Yellow Path, you speed increases to a thing of legend. If mounted, your mount can gallop over any terrain, even water, up sheer cliffs, or across thin air without difficulty. Immune to fatigue while traveling the Glory Path.

            ~: You know a traveller’s current destination.

            ~: You can Defend Other without using your action. (Bonus) when you Defend Other your mount. At Essence ++, you can use ~ up to Ride times per turn.

            ~: For a short time, you can understand and be understood without linguistic barriers.

            ~: You can social influence someone to fulfil their duty as if using a Minor Intimacy.

            ~: (Bonus) to Disengage, Withdraw and any other test of speed. (Bonus++) if in the Destiny of the Messenger

            Ride the Dragon: Transform a mortal who has a major positive intimacy towards you into a Lesser Elemental Dragon. Said dragon will tirelessly carry you to anywhere in Creation within the next 25 hours, after which it fades away, leaving the broken husk of your former friend in it’s wake.

            ------

            Ideas I had and discarded:
            * Speak with the voice of god when conveying a message from Heaven; people may not like it, they may ignore it, but no one who hears you can deny the truth of your words. (Feels out of theme and too easy)
            * Enlist a mortal to bear a message for you (If a Sidereal wants to delegate, they should have to delegate directly)
            * Know the value of a piece of information (Feels more like a Secrets thing)
            * Able to gain audiences with people of rank (Again, too obvious and easy)
            * Know where you are needed ('Needed' is subjective- this feels like it's basically asking the GM for a plot cookie. Might keep it as a Journey's anima effect tho)

            Ideas that I like but can't find effects to capture:
            * Something about heralding something.
            * Something about traveling at the speed of rumor / bad news.
