    Sidereals and Children

    08-13-2015, 10:51 PM
    I don't know if the canonical material has ever addressed this, but I don't think it ever did. Arcane Fate blurs the memory of Sidereals in the minds of most beings governed by Fate. One has to imagine that the grim necessity of the Arcane Fate means that even a Sidereal's immediate family would be affected by it -- parents, siblings, pre-Exaltation lovers, et cetera. But how would it affect a Sidereal's children? We have to divide up these children into two categories: children produced pre-Exaltation and ones from post-Exaltation. While I can't think of any logical reason why pre-Exaltation children would be exempt from Arcane Fate, but it might be that the ones conceived after being chosen by the Maidens would be different. While the Sidereal Exaltation isn't hereditary, a fetus being exposed to said Exaltation (or more specifically, to their parent's Sidereal-resonant essence) during its formation might mean that the child produced by that Exalt "dimly reflects" the glory of their parent's connection to the Maidens and allows them to side-step Arcane Fate. If this is the case, would the "rules" be different if one parent were an Exalt and the other wasn't? Or if both parents were Sidereals? Or even more uncertain -- if one parent were a Sidereal, and the other parent were the Chosen of another?

    Also, if it were possible that the biological investment (or something like that) of Sidereal-resonant essence could sidestep Arcane Fate, could a Sidereal do that for some kind of construct or something even odder? I can't imagine that sex alone would be enough to bypass Arcane Fate, since if it were, the Chosen of Venus would be spending an aggravating amount of time filing paperwork with the Pattern Spiders. But a biological or overwhelmingly intimate tie with an entity -- would it be enough?
