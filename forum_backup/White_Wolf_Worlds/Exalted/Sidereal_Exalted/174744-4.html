                            In some ways, it's just an expression of the fundamentally different themes of each Incarna and the way those themes are expressed through the Exaltations that Incarna catalyzed. All Exalts can do similar things, their way of approaching those things is just different.

                            Compare Solars, Lunars, and Sidereals: the former two have a way to flawlessly disguise themselves as another specific person (Solars through disguise-y Larceny Charms, Lunars through Appearance Charms, but more to the point, through Knacks); Sidereals could probably do pretty well using disguise-y Charms and Astrology, but it works in a different way. A Solar who wants to impersonate Cathak Cainan disguises themselves as Cainan, mimics his clothing, his voice, his body language...and does so so convincingly that others think the Solar is Cainan for the duration of that Charm. The Lunar eats Cainan's heart and, in many ways, actually becomes him; if that disguised Solar sired a child on one of Cainan's concubines, that child wouldn't look anything like Cainan, but instead like the Solar and the mother, but the Lunar effectively is Cainan while he wears his shape with Heart's Blood, and a child probably would actually resemble Cainan (I'm trying not to use terms like genes and DNA, because Creation's biological and physical laws don't work as a perfect of the real world, being fundamentally composed of motes and not atoms, etc). A Sidereal under a certain Resplendent Destiny, like Prince of the Earth, could adopt the trappings of a Dragon-Blood and perhaps even trappings that Cainan would carry, but he won't look like Cainan unless he applies other magic, even if he's able to convince people that he is actually Cathak Cainan (such people probably would not be fooled if they had ever seen or heard an accurate description of Cainan though; RDs are much better at convincingly imitating archetypes than any real person that exists anywhere else).

                            Or with defenses: a Solar about to be hit with a sword dodges puissantly, perfectly. He is still there, in the path of the sword, but then he isn't. He's still in the area, or the scene (barring use of other Charms). Lunars, with their 2e "Silver Solar" flavor, pretty much work the same way: it's a puissant dodge, it's moving between the sunbeams to avoid the sword. Sidereals are just not there. They quickly write an order to the Loom and instead of being hit with a sword in Nexus, they're two streets over looking at silks, because they just altered reality. Mechanically, the effect is the same: you are perfectly not hit, but the way in which this is accomplished is very different. And that thematic difference in Charms applies to the splats as a whole. Solar and Lunar Charmsets express themselves naturally through a degree of imitation of the Incarna that catalyzed the Exaltation; by contrast, the Sidereal charm set is described as being "written" by the Maidens with each Ability tied to a constellation and a fundamental truth about Creation's laws, rather than expressing itself organically through the lens of the Exaltation like other Celestials' sets.

                            The following is maybe not the best analogy but I feel like it's somewhat apt: the Sidereal Exaltation is like a closed-source OS and their native Charms are closed-source programs that run on it. Sids don't have "source code" access for their native powers, thus they cannot create new expressions of power from whole-cloth and tend to expand their competence through universal applications like Martial Arts and Sorcery. Every other Exalt type's Exaltation and Charm technology is more like open-source, in that an individual Exalt can develop custom expressions of their Exalted power.

                                Originally posted by nalak42 View Post
                                Ok I get that the Sidereals can't make custom charms in universe because The Maidens won't let them, but I wanted to know how this works. I mean I don't exactly understand how The Maidens are able to limit them from making their own. Was that ever explained?
                                No, that's wrong.

                                Sidereals can't make custom charms because that's not how a Sidereal Exaltation works. The Sidereal Exaltation grants you the ability to use certain powers, and that's it; just like a Solar Exaltation doesn't grant you the ability to shapeshift, a Lunar Exaltation doesn't surround you with raw elemental fire when your anima is flared, and a DB Exaltation doesn't grant you access to SMA, a Sidereal Exaltation doesn't grant you the ability to invent your own charms. Different Exaltations grant different types of power based on the thematics of the being that empowered them; something about the nature of the Maidens means that their Exalted get a specific suite of charms and do not get the ability (common to most other Exalted) to create new native charms from there.

                                                Originally posted by Lioness View Post
                                                Predictably most people who take this charm make characters who are Manipulation 5 Charisma 1 and argue to the bitter end that everything should be achievable with Manipulation.
                                                In fairness this has always been a problem with the WW vaguely-defined-social/mental-attribute-applicability, particularly Cha/Man (and it's so good that Exalted (at least 2e, possibly 1e) ended the bullshit moral judgment placed on Manipulation uses that dated to the earliest days of OWoD).

                                                But even other Yozi Charms that didn't give the explicit penalty of WtD at least implied that there was one way to play with that Charm, and other ways were Doing It Wrong. This is implicit in the Excellencies, being that they represent a broad swath of how a Primordial thought process operates.

                                                I see this as totally different from how the Sidereal Charmset works: Yozi Charms put you down a certain mental and spiritual path and their Excellency applicability rules push you to try and achieve all goals through methods allowing the Excellency to be applicable (and thus boost rolls in furtherance of that goal); Sidereal Charms, on the other hand, may have more specific applicability or activation conditions than other Exalts, but promote a general "approach this problem sideways" mentality versus changing your approach to interacting with the world only through certain action-channels to achieve your desired results.

                                                TL;DR: GSPs suffered from being constrained by the Yozi's Charm themes and trying to make all actions fit a particular set of "If you do it X way, then you can apply this Excellency." Sidereals are not constrained by the Charm themes of the Maidens themselves per se, but rather by having a fixed toolkit of powers that must apply to all possible situations (until you branch out into the generalist powers of Sorcery/MA).
