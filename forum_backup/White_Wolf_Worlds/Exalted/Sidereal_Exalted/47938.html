    Help - Sidereal Swordsman (the trouble with Melee)

    01-13-2014, 01:38 AM
    I like Sidereal Melee - Jedi Charms, protective mentor, Fate-O-Vision, it's all pretty cool. So I was pondering a new character, a star-sworn knight - Sidereal swordsman. I was considering which Martial Arts Styles would go well to supplement such a concept, and found the combination of Violet Bier of Sorrows and Sidereal Melee to be pretty fun...

    Then I realized it wasn't possible. The errata made it so you didn't have to buy combos or spent Willpower to activate them, but the limits on combos remain - you cannot activate Charms of different Abilities as part of the same combo. This limits severely the compatibility of any MA Style with Melee, and as cool as Melee is, it has all of seven Charms.

    So I'm turning to the forums for help in how to design a character who can progress in combat skill past the basic Sidereal Melee Charm-set. I want to use Melee, so "just use MAs" is not the kind of answer I am looking for - rather what to spend my xp on once I'm past that stage. Some measure of Resistance Charms seems a given, but other than that, I'm not sure. Are there MA Styles that could work well in combination with it despite the combo restrictions(perhaps through heavy use of scene-long boosts)? Or should I turn my attention somewhere I haven't looked?

      Originally posted by Omicron View Post
      Then I realized it wasn't possible. The errata made it so you didn't have to buy combos or spent Willpower to activate them, but the limits on combos remain - you cannot activate Charms of different Abilities as part of the same combo.
      That's not entirely true. Look for the words "must" and "may" in the Timing guidelines for Combos (on page 245). "Must" is compulsory, "may" is optional. Also check the charms themselves for exceptions.

      For instance, Supplemental charms must benefit the Simple charm of the combo if there is one, or non-reflexive actions if there is not. This normally requires them to be of the same Ability, but not always (refer to Meditation on War for a Supplemental Melee charm that explicitly may combo with other Ability attack charms and supplement them).

      On the other hand, Supplemental charms may benefit Reflexive action, if it's valid to do so (this normally requires them to be of the same Ability, unless indicated otherwise). Thus Reflexive charms and Supplemental charms of different Abilities can be included in the same Combo, the Reflexive action just does not benefit from the Supplemental charm.

          You are being very confusing, mr Greyman. It's far simpler than that - and not entirely as you write.
          1) The ability restriction you mentioned is a Supplemental Charm thing(supp charms by default only enhance the ability they're in), rather than a combo thing.
          2) On Combos:
          Simple Charms: can only have one, and have to use it. If there's an extra action, you have to use the Simple on all extra actions.
          Supplemental Charms: As many as you want. You have to use all Supplementals on all actions contained in the combo.
          Extra Action charms: like Simple: can only have one, and have to use it.
          Reflexive Charms: no restrictions, use at your leisure.
          Charms have to have the Combo-Ok or Combo-Basic keyword to be used in a combo. The former is thoughtless. The latter may only combo with reflexive charms. IIRC Form-Type also means Combo-Basic.
          3) On top of it all you have overdrive: Charms entirely paid with overdrive do not count as charm use, and do not interact with combos at all, so you can use them pretty much freely.
          ----------------------------------------------------------------------
          Consider Throne Shadow Form + Meditation on War. Every time one of your Fingers Parries you gain 2m. Sure, this restricts your ability to use other Forms, but if you're fighting with a significant group your overdrive just went on super crack, and you can use it on them. And later there's always Prismatic Arrangements of Creation Form.

              I played a sidereal swordsman before, and this setup worked well enough. Make sure to pick up World-Shaping Artistic Vision with something like "Swords" as your category, so that you can subtract your stunt value from the target number of every attack. Once you get Perfection of the Visionary Warrior you'll be able to attack multiple times at low cost, while ignoring both action and onslaught penalties. Use heavy armor combined with the scene long medicine charm for absurd soak, and pick up Shield of Fate from resistance to remove ping while also returning the damage to the attacker. If you use a starmetal Grand Daiklaive you will get decent damage, and with World-Shaping Artistic Vision and Fateful Melee Excellency you will also hit just about anything. Impeding the Flow combined with Blinding the Boar should give you decent DV.

              This worked nicely in the early parts of the campaign, but if you plan on playing this character for a long time you will eventually run into trouble with the closed charm set. Sidereals not having any melee charms above essence 4 will make you fall behind at high xp. When I ran into this issue, I picked up Monkey Style. It works with all armor and weapons once you master it, and it has a few charms that are really good. The DV charm is ridiculously overpowered, and being able to throw an attack before combat even begins is quite nice. You can't combo the charms with melee, but they still have some uses.

              Basically, sidereal melee works decently on low levels, but you'll regret it if you choose to use this charm set in either a really long campaign or if the other players are combat optimized solars.

                      Alright, well first, VBoS only fails to function with Flight of Mercury, Life-Severing Blow, and Metal Storm. The rest work with Melee, although the Form isn't as useful if you're not doing MA. And on the flip side, as Ithle said, only Orchestration of Mirrored Fates and Smiling at the Damned doesn't work with MA.

                      As always I suggest talking to your ST about custom Charms. VBoS Style isn't stretching thematic boundaries very much, and Sidereal Melee is fairly straightforward -- you could probably replicate a good amount of VBoSS in Sidereal Melee straight-up, then continue to make straightforward custom Melee Charms from there. Or you could could make upgrades to Orchestration of Mirrored Fates and Smiling at the Damned to allow them to work with non-Melee Abilities; that wouldn't be out-of-theme for Sidereal Melee, seeing how some of them do allow for that already.

                      There are also other useful combat Charms scattered around the Sidereal set. Not only Resistance, but Athletics and Awareness as well, along with Craft, Dodge, Integrity, and Performance (depending how far you want to spread yourself).
