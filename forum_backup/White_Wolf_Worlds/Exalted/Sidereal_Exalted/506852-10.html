            I mean creating the Immaculate Philosophy and Wyld Hunt. They assumed the crazy was inherent to Solar exaltations, and not a result of Solar society. We know OOC it is, but they had no reason to think so. The Prophecy only showed the Solar Deliberative, in its First Age form, ruining the world.

            They destroyed Solar society. Great! But instead of building a better society and then giving new Solars a chance to grow better than their predecessors with guidance, they built a shittier (equally oppressive) society and locked the Solars away forever. They never made any attempt to find out if the Solars were capable of getting better. Didn't attempt the reform in the first place, didn't attempt reform later after their corrupt Deliberative was gone. They destroyed the most obvious source of the corruption, but didn't bother to stop afterwards and ask if that had been sufficient. They just kept on murdering innocent people, completely unnecessarily as far as any of them could know, and planned to keep doing that for eternity. They had no actual evidence that new Solars, separately raised in a completely different society from the Solar Deliberative, would go crazy at all. They built a worldwide society so single-mindedly obsessed with murdering those innocent people that most Dragon-Bloods are now unwilling to even consider the possibility that Deathlords or Fair Folk are a threat to Creation, even though everybody in Creation knows something resembling them almost destroyed the world.

            Real human beings do not suddenly become serial killers or gangsters or warmongers because they were born evil. The problems in real human society arise out of society and the culture and values people are raised with, not from congenital demon parasites or ancient eldritch curses or the voice of Satan. The Solars are human. The Sidereals knew nothing about the Curse, so they have no rational reason to think the new Solars are any different from the rest of humanity in this regard.

            Even cursed, the Solars don't wake up one day with their entire personality, social background, and cultural value system replaced whole-cloth by a brain upload of their Past Life. For example: Swan did not wake up the day after his Exaltation with a brain upload of Desus replacing his personality and set out to turn Creation into a hellhole. He continues to be a diplomat from Coral or Wavecrest or wherever he came from, who grew up in the Second Age. He's a little more emotionally unstable, but he didn't suddenly forget the society he came from overnight, or suddenly transmogrify into the product of an extinct society he's never seen and probably doesn't remember. Why would he? Past Life memories don't work that way even with the Throwback Flaw, and as Celestial Exalted the Sidereals know it doesn't work that way. Even when Swan eventually does go batshit insane, he'll be a batshit insane diplomat from Coral or Wavecrest, not a brain upload of somebody who grew up in the First Age -- not that the Sidereals have actual evidence he'll go insane at all.
