    Does Anybody have any....Complains about Sidereal Charms ?

    09-15-2014, 02:06 PM

    Iron Siaka:     Uuuuuu, guy, I'm seeing something, it's coming to me, I'm starting to see the futur...........ARRHHHHHHH!
    Surprise ! It's un-errata BotBM-chan ~ <3

    ~ Future Prediction of the Day: Sidereal is Broken (Again) in Third Edition ~
    Overpowered in First Edition, Broken in Second Edition. It's the truth that being a Sidereal is suffering ! I wanna complain about Sidereal charms in this thread.

    1/ Conclusive Wisdom/Transcendent Hatchet Of Fate: Eh, shouldn't only one of these exist since they basically do the same thing: sapping motes/WP and showing the opponent their death/doom. Not to mention these two charms seem like a plot device pretending to be charms, I suppose if you like free-forming power and have a nice ST then you will like these but I never really have any ideas for using them in a game aside from when I want to demonstrating my ~weird~ power.

    2/ Investigation only has........3 charms. It's nice that the Christmas tree capstone basically force the ST to tell me all the campaign's secrets, but I still like more charms you know. And who even want to learn Auspicious Prospects for (Caste) anyway.

    3/ Why does Absence kinda look a lot like Shadow Over Water, no....it's Shadow Over Water but is more expensive !

    4/ Isn't their Archery tree kinda....boring, aside from GAT and MMBT, we got a Speed-reductor, a Archery Counterattack, and a so-so cover-negator, at least Every Direction Arrow have nice fluff. Maybe that the reason people only talk about GAT/MMBT when they mention Sidereal Archery, since the other charms are so boring ?

    5/ I have no idea when to use Storm's Eye Stance and Unrelenting Torment Technique, they seem crazy expensive power that only useful in some niche situations. Do anybody want to use these charms even after errata ?

    6/ Five Ordeals Odyssey: Somebody say that the charm was pretty broken in 1st Edition, but turn into a useless plot device in the next Edition. I wonder what the dev have in mind for Third Edition regarding this charm since raising Essence is a pretty big deal now and "Defeating this monster to instantly gain 1 dot of Essence" is just not acceptable (I think).

    7/ Sidereal Ride only have ONE charm that have anything to do with Ride. I really think people focus way too much on the whole "metaphorically riding" aspect of the tree. Even though Sidereal Ride always get cited when people want to show newbie how ~weird~ Sidereal are, I really hope Third Edition Sidereal Ride have at least one or two charms that enhance your mount instead of a bunch of power that you only use once in the character life-time and then left in the dust (freaking Spirit-Shape Companion).

    8/ Shouldn't Name-Pilfering Practice be more.....um......useful ? I get that the stealing SWLiHN's name joke is funny and all, but I still try to figure out why I want to learn this charm.

    The no.1 fan of Demetheus. I also draw Exalted things and is looking for commission works ~

                                          (2) is based off of a particular design philosophy, the same design philosophy that had a Solar Dodge cascade of like 5 Charms total. Both of those sucked for the obvious reasons of how easy it was to just be done with that Ability, vis a vis Charms, relatively quickly. I doubt that philosophy will remain, as Solar Dodge has been confirmed to be much larger and the Core is supposed to have 700+ Charms, plus Spells and Evocations. I expect other splats to have similar expansion of their core cascades before supplements and homebrew increase their numbers even more.

                                          (3) is based off of old notions of balance. Old, misguided, shitty notions of balance. Many Lunar and some Sidereal Charms approached the issue of "basic Celestial Charmset competency" by taking effects printed in the Core (i.e. Solar Charms) and "balancing" them for Lunars/Sids/etc by increasing prereqs, stat-key mins or activation cost, or narrowing applicability compared to the Solar Charm. I doubt this paradigm will be carried forward.
