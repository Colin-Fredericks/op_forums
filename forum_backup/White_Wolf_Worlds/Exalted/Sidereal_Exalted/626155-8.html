            One of my favorite stories is when I ran a Lunars game, and the group, while traveling with a scholar, was accosted by some bandits with an earth elemental at their beck and call. The scholar botched his Occult roll, and thought that it was a Jade Lion, one of the guardians of the gates to the Underworld. The players didn't question it, didn't roll Occult of their own (and at least two out of the three probably would have succeeded), freaked out, ran away as fast as possible, leaving their wagon and stuff behind, and then proceeded to stalk the bandits for several days, waiting for them to go back to the underworld so the Lunars could block whatever the entrance was.

            I asked them later, and for some reason they thought that since the bandits had a 'Jade Lion' they thought that they were connected to an Abyssal in some way, if one of the bandits wasn't an Abyssal themselves. So basically, even educated mortals haven't heard of everything or can be as thick as a brick.
