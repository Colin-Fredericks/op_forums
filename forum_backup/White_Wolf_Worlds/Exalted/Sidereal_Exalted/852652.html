      Originally posted by wastevens View Post
      So, tinkering with Sidereals, and now I'm pondering- what is the reason to introduce the Acquintances background? What role in a story do Acquintances serve; why do Sidereals cultivate them?

      They are people who remember the Sidereal and that the Sidereal can have an actual Human Relationship with without lies or Resplendent Destinies. They are the Sidereal's touch stone with their humanity. Acquaintances (or lack there of) are the most important part of a Sidereal's backstory.

      The Ex2 Sidereal book never really got into how a Sidereal forms social connections with Acquaintances. There is that one Ride Charm, Breaking the Wild Mortal, but it only lasts for one story. I just head canon it that the Sidereal must spend time and effort visiting and spending time with the person. After the Sidereal has broken though Arcane Fate, they must regularly visit the social group to maintain that connection.

                              honestly, this part of Sidereals doesn't seem to be inspired by anything in particular. I doubt you could find any examples in all of media, except in a metaphorical rather than literal sense. A secret agent having relationships outside their job is the closest you could probably come to it: sure the secret agent (and we're talking like, fictional super-spy kind of secret agent) is incredibly competent and goes to places all over the world, kicks ass and makes sure bad things do not happen or accomplishes some other mission, but all that is still their JOB, what they do for a living, not them actually living, and work can stress you so you need something non-work related, and Acquaintances are like, the secret agents friends who don't even know what their job is and are just people they hang out with because man, being a secret agent requires you to lie, and be in disguise and work with demanding superiors who expect you to fix everyone's problems with your spy-ness,and sometimes you just got to get away from that, especially when your job involves ending the lives of people whether they deserve it or not, making sure certain secrets are kept so that they come out at a certain time and probably cause a lot of pain and suffering like whether or not someone's husband is cheating on somebody, causing battles to happen, or making people go on journeys across Creation which is full of dangers to survive.

                              and Sidereals have it hard, because their non-spy friends could forget them if they don't maintain relationships. I doubt there is anything that particularly matches aside from other WW stuff involving secret conspiracies or whatever.

                              but yeah, Acquaintances are basically the Sidereals "people that aren't involved with your job that know you" background, because imagine you get kidnapped to a continent sized city where you've been chosen to work 24-7 with a bunch of people whose jobs it is to make sure reality works right, and every single person there is a part of that job in some way, and your trained your entire rest of life there to eventually become your own agent, so that you work for 5000 years more there, and every single person there is playing politics in some way and all the humans you know there are scheming masterminds who are searching for every single bit of advantage they can get while not insulting the people they work with all for the sake of making sure everything runs smoothly and nothing goes wrong and reality works right, and that your expected to somehow carry on the job when the world is the worst it has ever been and all the people outside the city whose job it is to make sure reality works right are all corrupt mafias who are being attacked by the police your predecessors set up but that said police is currently starting to divide and fight itself....

                              yeah, wouldn't YOU find some way to escape all that for a bit? y'know just a break from that headache you get when the Dragon-Blooded go after the wrong thing, or when a Solar thwarts you carefully laid plans again, or when the Fair Folk suddenly decide to have fun with the next door nation while your trying to figure out what these Yozi cultists are up to, or when the freaking Empress goes missing and all the Houses are now focusing one each other rather than the new influx of Solars all of a sudden, or when two gods can't stop arguing about what the weather should be like today while the plant gods make all the crops die just as a form of protest until they can make up their mind thus causing all the mortals to start getting angry and raiding other people for food...its all a complicated pain. when sometimes what you need is just some simple relaxation with people you can talk to without needing to manipulate them.

                                              My impression is that making Acquaintances a background was meant to say that your connections to the mortal world were rare enough to be a precious commodity even for a god-king who lives in undreamed-of splendour -- to emphasize the lonely separation of the role and encourage you to value the connections you had, and to show an ironic contrast between the glories of Heaven and the humble pleasures of ordinary life. In practice, I'm not sure how well this worked. I think they needed an appropriate-feeling mechanical benefit or some Charm hooks that didn't involve sacrificing them for the greater good, because you're not likely to be RPing scenes with your acquaintances often in-play, and so not taking any "feels" like you're just playing the exact same character but with more background dots.
