    Sidereals, Sorcery and Sacrifices

    09-29-2015, 10:56 AM
    So I've been looking through Manual of Exalted Power Sidereals, as well as The White and Black Treatises, a thought up of this experienced Sidereal Chosen of Secrets who learned both Sorcery and Necromancy so that he could summon up both ghosts and demons for information on Malfeas and the Underworld that the Loom of Fate couldn't give him.

    Then a thought occurred to me: isn't this what a Sidereal, in a way, supposed to be doing?

    Let me elaborate: a big concept of Sidereals is their utilitarian approach to things. they do what will be better in the long run by their calculation given what information they have. Furthermore, they are about sacrificing what is needed to make sure the whole stays well- what parts of the body to cut so that the rest lives. Sidereals are very much the doctors of Creation, and they have a whole lot of patients. They have finite manpower, resources, time, everything, even if they have fate, Heaven and super-martial arts on their side. The entire point of Sidereals is that they can't be everywhere- thus they calculate where it is best for them to be and go there. If some other place goes to hell because of it, too bad. Why? Because people are always suffering. Every minute of every day. People will suffer no matter what they do. Thus they take steps to minimize that suffering for the greatest number they are capable of doing so, take steps to improve what are capable of doing within their power, no matter the cost.

    How does this relate to the first sentence? Well see, a lot of people complain that Sidereals can't get knowledge of places beyond the Loom of Fate, so isn't it kind of useless or whatever? Except no, they can. Sorcery and Necromancy are right there. You want information, initiate, summon up a ghost to get the info you need or the demon to inform you of whats up in Malfeas. The Loom of Fate can't do everything. thats the point. sure, some people won't trust you because you summon up ghosts and demons but you need the information and without it, you might not be able to stop whatever plan those guys are trying to do. its just a sacrifice you got to make, like anything else. Its simply whats necessary when the Loom of Fate can't do everything for you. a Sidereal is all about dirtying their hands so that everyone else doesn't have to. sure the gods might not like you if they find out, but you got the job done and that is what matters.

    Simply put: the Loom of Fate is limited to Creation, because it actually enhances the Sidereal's themes of sacrifice for a greater good. you can't get the info the normal way, so how're you going to get it? will you summon ghosts? will you go down and investigate it yourself? will you do a favor for someone you really don't like? strike a deal you'd rather wouldn't have to? those are questions you've got to ask, how far your willing to go for what you need, what your willing to sacrifice for the mission, for your goals. especially when you have limited resources. Sidereals don't interfere with something because they don't know about it- they don't interfere because they might look at it, see it as something concerning...then see something even MORE concerning over there that they have to deal with, and thus decide "this one area here? we can let this fall to ruin, but we can't let THAT one fall at all. we're taking care of that area." and that is just one more sacrifice you got to make, and if you want to improve things, well you probably have to decide which area is best to improve over all the others in the long run, because you don't have luxury of improving it all, even if you can see it all.

    people keep talking as if Sidereals are expected to somehow solve every single problem in Creation. they're not. they are expected to figure out what are the most important problems to solve and figure out how to solve them so that the rest benefits, even if not everyone benefits. Sidereals are all-knowing, not all-powerful. if they don't solve something, its because they think they have a more important problem to solve. they might be wrong, they might have misinterpreted the data but they have judgement calls to make, and humans don't always make those calls perfectly. But they do have to make the call of what to sacrifice and what to keep, what to improve and what to let fall to ruin, or they won't get anywhere. sure, if they can they will direct some other Exalt to the problem if they can arrange that, but generally its all about figuring out what the most important problem to solve is first, and therefore what the cost of solving those problems will be.

    and sorcery if I recall, is all about sacrifices in some manner yes? giving up something for power over the cosmos? how does that not tie into the Sidereal's themes then? Sidereals are MEANT to go into Sorcery, and probably summon up second circle demons just to interrogate them on what the Yozis are up to THIS time, or summon a certain Ghost that has strong ties to a certain Death Lord to get inside info. They need that info to judge whether or not there is a problem that they need to solve above all the others, or to solve the problem they need to solve above all the others, no matter what Yu-Shan says about the method they get the information. the rest is just figuring out how much they might lose from solving this problem. You might pick the right thing, you might pick the wrong thing, but you've got to pick something if anything is to be done.

    Course its not all doom and gloom. Some patients just require more simple remedies and more easily cured, sometimes you just need to advise someone on proper nutrition, sometimes they just have the common cold, and some places in Creation really does just need a light touch to get things back on track, and sometimes-maybe even a ot of the time- you get the opportunity to solve those simpler problems when the big ones aren't rearing their head and making you make those tougher decisions. Don't mistake this for "hard men making hard decisions" or whatever, its about resources, time and the amount of effort you can put it to solving the problems that you can solve at all, sometimes you can solve a lot, sometimes you have to bite the bullet and solve only some of them, but you got to solve them regardless. sometimes that means going a bit beyond the boundaries of what your job says you should do and you have to learn Necromancy so that you can summon up ghosts for important stuff, but if you don't do it....who will? Well aside from whatever Exalt you direct to do the job for you. assuming they will solve it without the information you have, or knowing the full capabilities of what they're up against or that it won't backfire horribly in some way....

    Really, the key element of portraying Sidereals well is understanding how their perspective is possibly the widest of all the Exalts, yet how that perspective can be just as limiting because of how big it is, and how that informs how they act and what they do to make sure things work. yet so many people are caught up in misconceptions from believing they are worfs to believing they are complete unbeatable badasses who know and do everything. Sure, they probably know more than anyone- but that doesn't mean they can do more than everyone, which means they've got to choose....I hope this post has been enlightening, or at least didn't say anything stupid.
