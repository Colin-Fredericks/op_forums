    Originally posted by Erinys View Post
    I think this depiction right here (or my impression that they're depicted this way) is why I hate the Sidereals, or at least the Bronze. They aren't honest about trying to destroy the world or make it into Hell, they're pointing at how horrifically atrocious the world is, how it's closer to apocalypse every single day, how many billions have died hideously for their plans, how many atrocities the Dragon-Blooded and Deathlords commit every week ... patting each other on the back for a Job Well Done.     Er.

    Erinys, I'm often wrong, so I'm a bit hesitant to wade in here... but I think you might be arguing against fanon here, not Sidereals-as-they-are.

    I'm a giant Sidereal fanboy and I've seen very little evidence in the actual books, in either editions, that the Bronze Faction in any way has a mindset of "everything is fine, there are no problems and we should never try to improve anything. This mess is exactly what we wanted." Quite the opposite, really. And that's certainly not the canonical Bronze party line.

    The members of the Bronze Faction aren't members of it because they think the current stat of Creation is awesome; they're members of it because they think that this is the best that can be done. Chejop Kejak and the Inner Circle are not portrayed, ever, as self-deluded idiots who think that everything is amazing, they're portrayed as people who are working themselves to death to try and make the world a better place than it currently is and to stop other people from making it worse... by their own standards of "better" and "worse." Kejak hasn't been propping up the Realm because he thinks all the slavery and racism is super sweet; he's doing it because he doesn't see a workable alternative that he considers to have an acceptable risk/reward tradeoff.

    It is true that the Bronzies often try to convince other factions that everything is cool, they're in control, it is all going according to &c., but that's, you know, politically motivated lying, which the golds are no less guilty of.
    Their response instead should be "No, our Vision was correct, these problems were caused by beings Outside of Fate. We have a better plan to fix these problems, as usual we're understaffed but we're working on it." and their plan should be presented as plausible and potentially sufficient.
    This is actually very close to what it is; it's both "these problems were caused by beings outside of fate" combined with "our Vision is pretty shitty, but we didn't adopt it because it was awesome, we adopted it because the other Visions were even shittier." They don't deny the existence of problems, and their response to problems is usually "we don't have the resources" or "we're working on
    it."

    Their default should not be horrible callousness
    It's not. People don't play Bronze Faction Sidereals to play people who are either horribly callous or dupes. The writers didn't write them that way, because they knew that would be shit.
    The Bronze's plan shouldn't be, with OOC knowledge, unambiguously unviable.
    And it isn't.

    In-universe, the Bronze Faction's plan has been working pretty fucking well. Creation endures. Most people are born, live out their lives, and die. Many people live and die in horrible conditions, but if the Bronze Faction had no acted everybody in Creation would be the violated mind-slave of the mad, slavering tyrants the Solars were well on their way to turning into. Jenna Moran's "there is no part of you they have not defiled" vision for "what happens if there isn't a Usurpation" would have come to pass. The Realm may not be awesome, but the Realm has millions of people sheltering under its aegis who eat three full meals a day and go to sleep with their family in a warm house. That's what the Bronze Faction has brought to Creation. It's not perfect, but it is better than the alternatives.

    Their response to the Gold Faction is "you want to hand power back to the people who turned into insane god-kings. You're crazy." That's a reasonable response!

    In fact, I would argue that even OOC, the Bronze Faction plan isn't unambiguously unviable. It is explicitly possible within the setting to play a gang of Bronze Faction PCs who place a successor to the Scarlet Empress on the throne, stabilize the Realm, clean up Heaven, find a way of sidelining or otherwise co-opting the Solars, and basically keep on as they've been keeping on. Their corebooks in both editions present this as an option!

Solar excesses in the First Age started to look like the rule of the Primordials, godly superbeings that used humanity and reality itself for their amusement. When projections say the Solars will become just as bad as the Primordials themselves, any alternative looks preferable.
