    If I were writing Sidereals...

    03-19-2016, 10:12 PM
    If I were writing Sidereals...

    GOALS:
    * Fold Astrology into their Charms; leave larger workings as a specialization of the Sorcerous Project system.
    * Emphasize the planning and preparation nature of Sidereals- their best effects require they don astrological masks and stay within an archtype, or generate omens and prophecies that can forewarn enemies.
    * Make being Outside of Fate more of a lateral move than straight benefit.
    * Keep as many of the 1e Ability <--> Constellation associations as possible.
    * Solve the 'Every Sidereal has to favor Martial Arts' problem.

    NOT GOALS:
    * Binding to the same Charm Trees exactly as in 1e or 2e. Although I'm striving for a 1e feel, that's as a guide, not a straightjacket.
    * Maintaining Prayer Strip capstones. Prayer Strip charms are powerful and useful- but they don't suit as the capstone to every ability.

    ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Some general notes regarding the Sidereal Exalted:

    **ARCANE FATE**
    Due to the shattering of the Mask, the Sidereals slip from the memory of the world.
    At the end of any scene, all mortals who do not have at least a trivial intimacy towards the Sidereal begin to forget about them- by the end of the next scene, they retain only the haziest of memories.
    At the end of each story, all characters who have an Intimacy, positive or negative, towards either a Sidereal in particular or towards Sidereals as a whole are subject to a 10 dice social influence action to weaken the Intimacy, supported by a Major Intimacy. This influence occurs once per appropriate Intimacy.
    A character who has their last Intimacy towards a Sidereal erased by this will forget the Sidereal entirely; likewise towards collective Intimacies.

    A character who holds no intimacies towards Sidereals, either singularly or collectively, is at a significant disadvantage in learning anything about them. Knowledge of the Sidereal Exalted is naturally hard to come by, and the world seeks to erase their presence.
    Such a character incurs a -3 situational penalty on any attempt to learn or reveal information about the Sidereal Exalted.

    **RESPLENDENT DESTINIES**
    When a Sidereal needs to interact with the world for an extended period, they will most often do so by weaving themselves into the story as an appropriate character- an act they call donning a Resplendent Destiny.
    Each Ability has a Charm, 'Resplendent Destiny of (Constellation)'
    With each purchase, the Sidereal crafts a new persona, much as the Solar Charm 'Heart-Eclipsing Shroud', although the specifics of what the new persona's design vary by constellation.
    These personas are not touched by Arcane Fate; mortals will remember interactions with them normally, and intimacies formed to them will not fade under the vagaries of Arcane Fate. This is true even when the Sidereal is not wearing the Destiny.

    Resplendent Destinies are fragile; they immediately end if the Sidereal grossly violates their role or if their anima flares to bonfire level. They can also always be ended as a simple act of will, requiring a Simple action and 1 Willpower.

    Donning a Resplendent Destiny can be done for free over the course of five hours spent wearing the trappings of the Constellation and without violating the associations, or as a Simple action for 10m.
    Resplendent Destinies can provide minor cosmetic changes to a character's appearance, but only to the extent of a mortal disguise. They do not overtly change a character's clothing or possessions- and a character who is dressed too far out of character for a given Destiny may have it shatter as soon as it's donned.

    **DESIGNS OF DESTINY**
    Several Sidereal Charms allow them to describe, within some limitations, some set of circumstances under which an individual or activity is especially propitious or baneful. Such Charms have the keyword 'Design'.
    Once a Design is undertaken, until either the Sidereal releases the committed motes or the Design is fulfilled, Creation will begin to bring forth omens and prophecies, letting those with the appropriate Lores or thaumaturgical gifts insight into the Designs.
    Many Sidereals, given the chance, will set up multiple favorable designs upon themselves and their allies, and then endeavor to ensure that they are fulfilled just when needed.
    Unless otherwise noted, recognizing and correctly interpreting the omens and portents that herald a Design is an Intelligence + Lore action, with a difficulty of (8 - Charm's Essence). If you have an intimacy towards the Sidereal who enacted the Design, you gain a +3 situational bonus. This does not tell you exactly what the Design's effect is, but gives you an educated guess and a heads up.

    **OUTSIDE OF FATE**
    Many of the Sidereal's most powerful charms relate to altering someone's role within the weave of the world upon the Loom of Fate.
    These powers also work similarly upon those beings who's story is told in the constellations of the Underworld, the broken stars of Malfaes, or the ever-changing story of the Wyld- although sometimes less effaciously.

    Especially potent individuals, however, have no part in any weave- they stand apart, and no destiny helps or hinders them. Such beings are said to be outside of Fate.
    Such beings are not subject to Arcane Fate, and always only perceive a Sidereal as themselves, even when they have donned a Resplendent Destiny.
    Likewise, they are secure in their mind, body, destiny and location- they ignore effects which would alter their TNs, cost or grant Willpower, add or remove Intimacies, or change their location.
    To their detriment, however, this can leave them out of step with everything else that is. When someone Outside of Fate directly acts against the designs of destiny, they suffer a -3 situational penalty to all actions for the remainder of the scene- having fallen out of step with the world, they find themselves improvising, like an actor that has forgotten their lines.
    Additionally, when such a violation occurs within Creation, the Bureau of Destiny is often notified. If such a violation occurs against a specific Sidereal's designs, then that Sidereal is alerted, regardless of the location.
    Individuals inside Fate can also act against the designs of fate, and do so without risk to the overall weave. Instead, they will have to contend with the powers of the agents of destiny directly.

    As a rule of thumb, only the following entities or individuals of similar power and scope are outside of Fate:
    * The Incarna
    * The Demon Princes and their Third Circle Souls
    * The Neverborn and the Deathlords
    * Fair Folk of the Ishvara
    * Rakan Thulio

    Certain Charms, powers, spells or sorcerous working can also remove an individual from Fate, either temporarily or permanently. Likewise, some of these individuals can be drawn into the weave of Fate again, although usually for only a limited time.

    **MASTERS OF THE MARTIAL ARTS**
    All Sidereals gain the Martial Arts merit for free, and always purchase dots of the Martial Arts abilities and charms as favored even if they do not favor Brawl.

    **FAVORED AND FATED ABILITIES**
    Sidereals have 9 Favored Abilities; at least 3 of them must be from Caste Abilities.
    Additionally, one of their Favored Caste Abilities is their Fated Ability.
    Much as the Solar Exalted are noted to excel with a particular supernal ability, each of the Sidereals has an ability with which they are Fated to master.
    Sidereals gain the Resplendent Destiny of their Fated Ability for free. When they don this Resplendent Destiny, they may treat their Essence and Ability as 5 for all Charms of the Fated Ability.
    They may also treat their Essence and Ability rating as 5 for the purposes of learning the Charms of their Fated Ability- but they may only use those Charms for which they do not naturally qualify when they don the Resplendent Destiny.

    **ANIMA EFFECTS**
    (I am honestly drawing a blank on most of these)

    **CASTE ABILITIES AND CONSTELLATIONS**
    The House of Journeys
    Resistance (The Mast), Ride (The Messenger), Sail (The Captain), Survival (The Ship's Wheel), Thrown (The Gull)

    The House of Serenity
    Craft (The Peacock), Dodge (The Ewer), Linguistics (The Pillar), Performance (The Musician), Socialize (The Lovers)

    The House of Battles
    Archery (The Quiver), Brawl (The Gauntlet), Melee (The Spear), Presence (The Shield), War (The Banner)

    The House of Secrets
    Investigation (The Key), Larceny (The Guardians), Lore (The Treasure Trove), Occult (The Sorcerer), Stealth (The Mask)

    The House of Endings
    Athletics (The Rising Smoke), Awareness (The Crow), Bureaucracy (The Haywain), Integrity (The Sword), Medicine (The Corpse)

    Integrity replaces Martial Arts under the Sword.

      Resistance: THE MAST
      Trappings: The Mast is drably dressed and unspeaking. They are strong and honest but ignorant.
      Associations: Stoicism, tools, architecture, manual labor, silence, ignorance, phallus

      The Scripture of the Eternal Maiden
      Once, there was a maiden...
      ... made from a lump of iron, shaped by wind, sea and fire
      Fearing how wood might shape her, she ran, and did not look back
      One day she forgot what she ran from
      So, she pulled out her heart to ask it.
      "Why don't you look back and see?" it said.
      So, she sighed and threw her heart away.
      "I have no use for beginnings" said she.

      Resplendent Destiny of the Mast
      Cost: - (10m); Mins: Resistance 1, Essence 1
      Type: Permanent
      Keywords: Mute
      Duration: Permanent
      Prerequisite Charms: None

      The Sidereal creates a new persona, a character of the Mast. The intimacies selected must be appropriate for an archtype of the Mast, relating to the appropriate associations.
      For example, the Resplendent Destiny may have a tie of admiration towards Ragara Yukio, a noted architect and geomancer; or perhaps a principle of disdain towards scholars and other individuals of book learning.

      While wearing this Resplendent Destiny, you must redistribute any dots you have in Linguistics, Investigation, Socialize, Lore or Bureaucracy amongst your other abilities.
      You may not have more than 1 dot in any of those Abilities while in this Destiny.
      You may raise Resistance to 6 while under the Resplendent Destiny of the Mast. Additionally, in any populated area, they can find a menial job that will ensure they are fed, clothed and sheltered from the elements.

      ---

      Ox-Body Technique
      Cost: -; Mins: Resistance 1, Essence 1
      Type: Permanent
      Keywords: Stackable
      Duration: Permanent
      Prerequisite Charms: None

      As per the Solar Charm 'Ox-Body Technique', except:

      Stamina 1-2: An additional -1 health level
      Stamina 3-4: An additional -0 health level
      Stamina 5+: An additional -0 and -2 health levels

      ---

      Strength of the Mast
      Cost: 3m / dot; Mins: Resistance 3, Essence 1
      Type: Supplemental
      Keywords: None
      Duration: Instant
      Prerequisite Charms: Resplendent Destiny of the Mast

      The Mast is always strong enough to do whatever job they have. When performing a feat of strength, they may increase their Strength up to the strongest person in the area, plus their Essence. This increase only enables their ability to perform the feat- it does not add to their dicepool for succeeding.
      When wearing the Destiny of the Mast, Strength of the Mast costs 2m per dot.

      ---

      Someone Else's Destiny
      Cost: (Intervals) m, 1wp; Mins: Resistance 4, Essence 1
      Type: Reflexive
      Keywords: Perilous
      Duration: One Scene
      Prerequisite Charms: None

      Poison and toxins cannot slow the Chosen of Journeys- mostly because they were always destined for another. When exposed to a toxin, you may activate Someone Else's Destiny; it costs one Willpower and a number of motes equal to the total duration of the effect.
      The poison ceases to exist, save as a memo attached to the Sidereal's destiny. They may unleash it as a Gambit (difficulty 3) against a target within the scene, or allow it to disperse without effect.
      Someone Else's Destiny may not be used while in Initative Crash.

      ---

      Unswerving Juggernaut Principle
      Cost: 5m; Mins: Resistance 4, Essence 2
      Type: Reflexive
      Keywords: Design
      Duration: Indefinite
      Prerequisite Charm: None

      The Sidereal writes their intention and weaves their arrival into the pattern of destiny, as well as when they will arrive. Omens and signs begin to appear at the destination, heralding the arrival of the Chosen.
      The destination must be, as far as the Sidereal knows, reachable through pure physical movement (walking, climbing or sailing) within the given timeframe.
      As long as they do nothing but travel towards their destination, re-roll 1s on any contested roll which could prevent you from arriving.

      ---

      Optimistic Security Practice
      Cost: 4m; Mins: Resistance 2, Essence 2
      Type: Reflexive
      Keywords: Dual
      Duration: Instant
      Prerequisite Charms: Someone Else's Destiny

      Some people do not know how to stay down; some people are too stupid and stubborn to die. Such ignorance can be awe-inspiring.
      When activated against a withering attack, Optimistic Security Practice increases your bashing, lethal and aggravated soak by Essence + Resistance. If you are wearing armor, it only increases your soak by Essence.
      When activated against a decisive attack, Optimistic Security Practice provides a number of -0 health levels equal to your Essence. (As per Iron Skin Concentration)

      ---

      Water and Fire Designs
      Cost: 3m, 1wp, 1lhl; Mins: Resistance 5, Essence 2
      Type: Simple
      Keywords: Design
      Duration: Until Calibration
      Prerequisite Charms: None

      A sacrifice of blood, either given to a bonfire or a large body of water, secures you from being harmed by the designated element for the remainder of the year.
      If the design is enacted upon fire, you suffer no damage or impairment from natural flames, although may still be burned by magical fire- such as a bolt from a Dragonblooded, or a sorcerous attack.
      If the design is enacted upon water, you cannot drown, and may move freely in water.
      Water and Fire Designs cannot be used during Calibration, and cannot be in effect for both fire and water at the same time.
      After being enacted, until it is released, omens will surround you, informing those who look into your destiny of the bargain you have struck.

      At Essence 3, Water and Fire Designs can be purchased a second time. If they do, then by touching an appropriately aspected spirit (which may require a Brawl or Martial Arts gambit, difficulty 1), you may attempt to bind them into protecting a place until Calibration.
      Make a Charisma + Resistance roll, against a difficulty of the spirit's Resolve. If successful, the spirit gains an unbreakable Defining Principle towards the task you describe until Calibration.
      The omens shift to indicate this protection over the location, rather than the Sidereal's person.
      Doing so ends the protection provided by Water and Fire Designs. Re-enacting the protection renders the principle no longer unbreakable and reduces it to a Major Principle.

      ---

      Shield of Destiny
      Cost: 5m; Mins: Resistance 5, Essence 2
      Type: Reflexive
      Keywords: Counterattack, Decisive-only, Perilous
      Duration: Instant
      Prerequisite Charms: None

      Those who try to tear down the Mast often find the task is beyond their ken or expectation- even when they succeed, the fall is likely to ruin them as well.
      When subject to a decisive attack, you may use Shield of Destiny. Roll Stamina + Resistance; each success reduces the initiative pool of the attacker by 1. If your successes are greater than their initiative, then you crash them.
      After their attack is resolved, you may make a decisive counterattack.
      When wearing the Destiny of the Mast, double 8s on both the Stamina + Resistance roll and the counterattack.

      ---

      Heartless Maiden Trance
      Cost: 10m, 1wp; Mins: Resistance 5, Essence 3
      Type: Simple
      Keywords: None
      Duration: Indefinite
      Prerequisite Charms: Unswerving Juggernaut Principle

      You swallow your heart and still your breath. While active, you ignore the mundane needs of your body for food, water and rest, as well as any penalties you suffer from injury or exposure. If you are exposed to an illness or poison, it is automatically held in abeyance.
      You cannot be knocked unconcious; instead, you will only be killed if you take a number of levels of damage equal to your Stamina.
      However, you also cease to respire motes; until Heartless Maiden Trance is released, you do not gain motes outside of combat, and only gain 3 motes per round instead of 5 during combat.

      ---

      Storm's Eye Stance
      Cost: 10m, 1wp; Mins: Resistance 5, Essence 4
      Type: Simple
      Keywords: Design
      Duration: One Scene
      Prerequisite Charms: Strength of the Mast, Optimistic Security Practice, Water and Fire Designs, Shield of Destiny, Heartless Maiden Trance

      You inscribe a time and place upon a prayer strip inscribed with The Scripture of the Eternal Maiden, and then cast it either into fire or water, whereupon it vanishes in a golden light.
      It appears again in the hands of whomever you wish. Make a Charisma + Resistance roll against their Resolve to persuade them to seek you out at the named place and time, as if backed by a Major intimacy.
      The time and place must be somewhere you can reach within one lunar month, and must be at least one week from the time you use Storm's Eye Stance. The time may be ambigious- for example, 'When the courtyard shines bright as noon' could be satisfied at noon, or at midnight- if you have taken pains to ensure that enough bonfires have been lit to make it as bright as noon.
      At the time and place you describe, you may fulfil this design, placing yourself at the center of the storm.
      Until the end of the scene, you ignore onslaught penalties. When enemies attack you with withering attacks, you steal 1 point of initiative for each 1 or 2 they roll, and reduce all withering damage to minimum. Add your Resistance in bonus dice to any roll made to rush or disengage.
      If you wear the Destiny of the Mast from the time you begin the design until it is fulfilled, you also re-roll 7s, 8s, 9s and 10s on any actions you take for the scene.

          Positively, keeps it nice and simple by rolling together astro and charms, looking functional and engaging and await to see how Celestial Astrology, the kind that affects the destinies of others, is worked in through Sorcerous Workings (and Charms).

          Critically:

          - Omens seem like an interesting idea; as who doesn't want to be the mover of fates whose arrival is heralded by signs and portents, and it would shut down some of the "Bah. Sidereals everybody just gettin' Si-dereals leaping on up at them out damn teacups up in hiyah!" grumbles.

          On the other hand, Sidereal are supposed to be secret and act secretly. I feel also omens might be more practical with guidance on how they actually affect things, like difficulties on Lore and Occult, if used against quantified NPCs etc. An omen an average DB can read is different from one a No Moon with specialist training at understanding fate can read, is different from one an experienced Sidereal only can read. e.g. If a character could already use their Lore to see your coming, how much easier (if at all) do omens (like those with Unswerving Juggernaut Principle) make it? I'm a bit leery of it being "as much as your group reckons", opposed to having at least some defined baseline that Sidereal players can try to game to their advantage / disadvantage. Although perhaps that's the most practical solution (avoiding heavy blocks of text etc.).

          - I'm kind of into a Sidereal design that allows spontaneity if you're able to, and encourages planning if not. More than a "this must be done in advance" design. Just as a general point, in favour against trying to design *too* much obligate planning steps into Sidereal magic.

              I love the Arcane Fate part and it's interaction with Intimacies. I have a rough draft of my thoughts on the Loom of Fate and I have a section on how it ties in to Intimacies. I also have immunity to Arcane Fate is based on access to the Loom of Fate. For example, gods of the Bureau of Destiny, Celestial Lions, Auditors, and Sidereal Half Castes that work for the Bureau have access to the Loom of Fate and thus are immune to Arcane Fate. Creatures outside of Fate are also immune until they gain a thread on the Loom.

              Yes to folding Astrology Resplendency powers into charms! I rarely see Resplendency powers used in games.

              About planning and Sidereals. I also prefer more Spontaneity in Sidereal design. Yes, planning and foresight does play into the Sidereal themes, but sometimes we need to set that aside for what makes an interesting play experience. I would prefer Sidereals to have more options for when things don't go according to play beyond Avoidance Kata.

              Also, the less Destiny Taxes the better. Let's make Sidereal paperwork a flavor joke or stunt fodder instead of having a sidereal make six rolls for one Astrology effect. (I should probably note, I have played a lot of Fate in my day, so I will always side on "the Less Rules the Better" and "Only Roll if Failure is Interesting")

              I don't see a problem with all Sidereals favoring Martial Arts. Martial Arts, especially Sidereal Martial Arts, encompass more than just smacking people with your fist (which is why I disagree with needing a point of brawl for MA, but that is another argument). I hope in 3rd Edition, Sidereals get that merit for free.
