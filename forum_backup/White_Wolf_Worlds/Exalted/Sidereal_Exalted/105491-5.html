            Originally posted by Hand-of-Omega View Post
            Or does it have to? What if the two systems could be combined? What if Sidereal Martial Arts in 3E became some form of Astrological Martial Arts? What might that look like?
            I don't think it's a great idea, but here's how I'd do it:

            - Remove College dots, and/or otherwise keep them while linking the cost to the respective Ability
            - Tie the Scripture of each Ability to the respective College and fluff the whole thing as the '25 fundamental truths' or something similar
            - Merge SMA and Greater Astrology, so that you've got suites of combat charms and also Sorcery-like simple large-scale charm effects
            - Re-work Paradox, but keep it as something really horrible to experience. Use that to counter-balance the strongest effects.
            - Tie SMA/Astrology styles into the Colleges, and make having the relevant Ability/College the pre-req for each
            - You could even separate each College into a 'Martial Style' and an 'Esoteric Style'

            So at that point, when you bought, say, Integrity, you'd also gain points in The Rising Smoke. You'd be able to make an RD from that after a point or two of Integrity, but you'd also be able to purchase Rising Smoke Martial and Esoteric charms. Charcoal March of the Spiders works pretty well for the martial charms, and then Esoteric charms would cover both effects like a charm which granted you bonuses for changing your motivation and An End to Darkness. The second effect would give you Paradox for using it, so you'd do so sparingly.

            (Obviously there's no way in hell anyone's writing 25 SMA styles just to fit there, but SMA is hard to write no matter where you stick the pre-reqs, so eh.)

            ~

            Here's the thing about taking Sidereals and making them 'the Astrology Exalted'- what does that mean? What is it you want Astrology to do?

            Remember that the primary Sidereal competencies and powers come from the charm set. You get yer prophecy from Lore, you get powers to bind gods from Bureaucracy and War, you get dodges and teleporting and the starting tools for pretty much any kit a Sidereal is likely to need in one of those trees. After you're done there, Sorcery serves as the grab-bag of overtly magical powers which aren't really within your themes and Martial Arts serves as the combat-intensive grab-bag of powers which really aren't in anyone's theme. So that stuff is covered- Astrology doesn't need to touch it.

            Astrology, as-is, is just a weird potpurri of things that let you stick TN reductions places, costumes with weirdly unbalanced powers attached and a secondary system of things which are pretty much Astrological-themed sorceries. It's not a coherent power at all, so if you propose to expand it, you've got to figure out which way you think it should be expanded.

                Hmm. How about this, then:

                Sidereals are not so much the "astrology" Exalted as they are the "enlightenment" Exalted. As many have pointed out in this very thread, it is their Understanding of Esoterica that defines their approach to their powers and capabilities.

                So what if Astrology, through the Colleges, becomes the gateway to that Enlightenment? What if that Enlightenment isn't restricted to any one ability or power, but can be applied to whatever the particular character specializes in?

                This way, we can have the Chosen of Endings who applies her Enlightenment to her Martial Arts, thereby gaining SMA. But we can also have a Chosen of Journeys who applies his Enlightenment to his role as a Teacher, making him a supernal instructor to mortals and Exalts; or a Chosen of Serentiy whose Enlightenment helps her to become a legendary Vizier, guiding the leaders of nations and armies; or a Chosen of Secrets whose Enlightenment aids her in Sorcery, enabling her to reach the Adamant Circle.

                This would be a good way to avoid the "one, true build" problem Sids face, wouldn't it?

                (and thanks, Mercury, for actually taking the time to think out how "Sidereal Circle Sorcery" and "Astrological MA" might actually work!)
