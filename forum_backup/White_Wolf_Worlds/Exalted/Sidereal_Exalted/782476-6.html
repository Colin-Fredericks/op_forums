    Originally posted by Sanctaphrax View Post
    PS: Might also be a good idea to change World-Shaping Artistic Vision. -1 TN feels less useful now that it's so easily available to Sidereals.
    True, but a permanent -1TN that applies across abilities and colleges still feels worth a charm to me. I realise that it didn't actually say that it applied across all abilities before, but that was the intention, so I've fixed the text. If you made the speciality Swords then you'd get that bonus when ever you use a sword, craft a sword and in the college of the Sword (plus other thing I can't think of just now). If you were to chose a specific area of creation then you would be significantly better off whilst doing anything in that area, pretty much regardless of context. It also works well IMO with the Sidereal theme of working behind the scenes as using this they could easily get away with spending less essence to achieve their goals. Maybe I could add a way to rearrange the specialities, so that it becomes more like The Methodology of Secrets? Then it would allow Sidereals to ultra focus on their objective.


    PS: Oh and I like the craft charms. I've read your thread on Craft and like the look of it, but I'm not in a game as a crafter at the moment so I have no opportunity to test them or compare outside of theory-crafting.

                Originally posted by Grifftofer View Post
                Craft
                Elemental Vision
                Cost: -; Mins: Craft 2, Essence 1; Type: Permanent
                Keywords: None
                Duration: Permanent
                Prerequisite Charms: None
                There are 5 versions of this charm, one for each of the elements. Different crafts resonate with different elements, so each element requires a different craft ability to buy the apprpriate charm:
                Air: calligraphy, jewelery making, glassblowing
                Earth: masonry, stone cutting, creating earthworks
                Fire: blacksmithing, creating ceramics
                Wood: carpentry, weaving, flower arranging
                Water: cooking, brewing, leather working
                The charm gives a Minor Tie of Respect for the Sidereal in any elementals, dragon-bloods or god of the corresponding element.
                I think all splats need a version of Supreme Celestial Focus, as an Exalt Fix for the Crafting XP sink. So I would have this charm also let you spend gxp to raise the associated craft from 1-5, with a limit of (Essence) crafts.

                Possible additional changes are:
                1. It can be retroactive - if you buy it for a craft you already have rated above 1, you can also use gxp to refund the ranks you've already purchased. (This is a minor change but seems fairest.)
                2. Rather than having to purchasing it five times (which is pretty expensive!), you purchase it once, and it applies to at most (Essence) different elements.
                Originally posted by Sanctaphrax View Post
                Elemental Vision

                There are five crafts which represent the elements to the Sidereal Exalted. They are

                Air: Painting
                Earth: Architecture
                Fire: Weapon-smithing
                Wood: Weaving
                Water: Cooking

                Each costs only 1xp or 1/3 of a bonus point to a Sidereal with this Charm. A Sidereal who already has such specialties is refunded 2xp for each upon buying this Charm. In addition, any elemental or Terrestrial Exalt of an element that the Sidereal has the associated Craft for is treated as though they had a Minor Tie of respect for the Sidereal. Elementals and Terrestrials with Major or Defining Ties for the Sidereal ignore this effect.

                                                    Ok. So I've been very remiss in keeping up with this project. Honestly, the Resplendency powers just crushed me. I got through the one I could, but then just hit a brick wall in terms of creativity and enthusiasm.

                                                    Been drifting back through things in the last couple of weeks though and have started adding some expansions to the earlier charm trees, which I've had written up for a while. The expansions will vary in size quite a lot as I end up with more ideas for some things than others.

                                                    I'm carefully avoiding Resplendencies for now until I can come up with enough willpower to dive into them again or an alternative, simpler, approach. A thought I had was that I could turn the effects I have now into 'proper' charms and instead give resplendent destinies a small 'generic' power. I know its not a hugely exciting proposition, but I think something along the lines of: "While in a resplendent destiny the Sidereal's dice cap for the related ability is increased by one." might work.

                                                    Anyway I'm hoping to keep going with this. If only to provide an alternative to wasteven's works. (I really like what he's doing, but his plan is different enough that I think its worth me continuing.)
