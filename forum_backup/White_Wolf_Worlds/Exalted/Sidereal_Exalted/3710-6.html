                                                        I feel like there are two things to this:
                                                        1) Okay, so a god who isn't very good at fighting won't be much of a challenge in a fight. That says absolutely nothing useful in comparing the might of gods and Exalted.
                                                        2) Let's take the Jorst example; how is Jorst fighting you? Or more importantly, where? Because if it's in the middle of the relevant kind of forest, it seems like he'd be more about getting the trees to swing around at you or uprooting themselves and going crazy, or overall go "last battle in the movie version of Prince Caspian" on you.
