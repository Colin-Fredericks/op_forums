    Sidereal Caste Abilities

    12-03-2017, 05:18 AM
    Quite a long time ago, Vance posted his current thoughts on Sidereal caste abilities, and said he was interested to see what people thought. But there was never any real discussion about it, so I thought I'd start a thread about it.
    Journeys: Resistance, Ride, Sail, Survival, Thrown
    Serenity: Craft, Dodge, Linguistics, Performance, Socialize
    Battles: Archery, Brawl, Melee, Presence, War
    Secrets: Investigation, Larceny, Lore, Occult, Stealth
    Endings: Athletics, Awareness, Bureaucracy, Integrity, Medicine





    And also, all Sidereals have Martial Arts as favoured.

    First, I think that giving them all Martial Arts as favoured is a great idea, since 90% of Sidereal PCs would just end up wanting it anyway. Though I wonder if they'll still have to get the Martial Arts merit.
    I kind of think maybe they should get it for free: part of the point of the Martial Arts merit, as I understood it, was that Martial Arts was just so good that it needed gating. But Sidereals are actually worse at martial arts than Solars because they (I assume) don't get Mastery. And its thematic.
    Still, that's slightly off-topic.

    So back to the main abilities.

    Journeys, Serenity and Secrets are all the same as in 2nd ed.

    What's been moved is that Battles have got Brawl, and lost Athletics to Endings (since their Martial Arts is now shared by everyone).

    I don't think this is terrible: Brawl does, I think, fit better with Battles than Endings.

    But I don't really see Athletics as fitting with Endings. I'm not really seeing much connection there: what does running, jumping and lifting have to do with ending stuff? Whereas Medicine obviously does.

    It fits better with Battles. Also, consider that now with Brawl and no Athletics, Battles has 4/5 of the same abilities as Dawn Castes (which I always assumed was something the designers were deliberately ignoring).

    But thinking about it more... should it really be in Battles either? I mean, it's Athletics. It's previous versions included charms like "run without tiring". Shouldn't it be in Journeys?

    I'd therefore suggest that maybe Thrown should be Endings one combat ability. I think it's fitting for fate ninjas whose theme is ending stuff to throw shuriken or knives at people, and, you know, end them.

    That does mean Journeys no longer has one of the main combat abilities, but they'll have Athletics and Resistance (whereas Serenity just has Dodge and Secrets doesn't really have any combat stuff).

    For a more out-there alternative, Athletics could stay in Battles and Endings could get Melee. It might seem odd for Battles to not have Melee, but the executioner's axe, the assassin's blade, the sword that cuts the Gordian knot, could be in Endings. And with Archery, Athletics, Brawl, Presence and War, the Chosen of Mars still have plenty to do in fights while not having the same abilities as Dawn Castes (which I've always assumed is the reason they have Presence, frankly).


    I'm not really saying "I think this selection of Abilities is awful for Sidereals, and must change!" Just giving possible suggestions. What do the rest of you think?

                      Honestly, I think there is no way to map 3E's abilities to 1E constellations. The Mast has nothing to do with Resistance (if I had to pair it with a 3E ability, it would be Integrity, but even that is sort of an awkward fit). War should obviously be The Banner, but that leaves us with no constellation for Presence. The Sword can't really be anything but Martial Arts (I mean, it could be Melee, but then you have nothing for The Spear and you have to rewrite the entirety of Sidereal Melee from the ground up). You could maybe say that The Gauntlet now corresponds to Presence (it's not a terrible fit thematically) and The Sword is Brawl, but let's face it, mapping an ability that can't use swords at all to that constellation would be... strange, to say the least. You could possibly make it work, because sids are supposed to be strange, but it still feels wrong.

                      So I guess the least terrible solution I could come up with would be the following:

                      - everybody has MA as favored
                      - Journeys: Integrity, Ride, Sail, Survival, Thrown
                      - Serenity: Craft, Dodge, Linguistics, Performance, Socialize
                      - Battles: Archery, Melee, Presence, Resistance, War
                      - Secrets: Investigation, Larceny, Lore, Occult, Stealth
                      - Endings: Athletics, Awareness, Brawl, Bureaucracy, Medicine

                      (My preferred solution, of course, would be hiring Jenna Moran on the Sid book and letting her sort the whole mess out.)

                                          Exalted: the Sidereals
                                          Journeys: Endurance, Ride, Sail, Survival, Thrown
                                          Serenity: Craft, Dodge, Linguistics, Performance, Socialize
                                          Battles: Archery, Brawl, Melee, Presence, Resistance
                                          Secrets: Investigation, Larceny, Lore, Occult, Stealth
                                          Endings: Athletics, Awareness, Bureaucracy, Martial Arts, Medicine

                                          Manual of Exalted Power: Sidereals
                                          Journeys: Resistance, Ride, Sail, Survival, Thrown
                                          Serenity: Craft, Dodge, Linguistics, Performance, Socialize
                                          Battles: Archery, Athletics, Melee, Presence, War
                                          Secrets: Investigation, Larceny, Lore, Occult, Stealth
                                          Endings: Awareness, Bureaucracy, Integrity, Martial Arts, Medicine

                                          As a bonus, in early 2e Jenna Moran (who wrote the original Sidereal Charmset) had her own ideas on what they should have in 2e. And for that, she was of an opinion that the Sidereal set should basically be the case that the merged Abilities went to Journeys and Endings, and Battles picked up both the Abilities intorduced that edition. This would result in the following for Mercury, Mars, and Saturn's Chosen; Integrity would pick up the Scripture of the Drowning Maiden and apparently she was just fine with re-jiggering and rewriting the Presence tree to fit the Shield's scripture if need be. 2e was isntead written as usual 2e copy-paste, but that's another story to bitch aobut.

                                          Jenna Moran's Proposed 2e Set
                                          Journeys: Resistance, Ride, Sail, Survival, Thrown
                                          Battles: Archery, Integrity, Melee, Presence, War
                                          Endings: Athletics Awareness, Bureaucracy, Martial Arts, Medicine

                                          Proposed 3e Set
                                          Journeys: Resistance, Ride, Sail, Survival, Thrown
                                          Serenity: Craft, Dodge, Linguistics, Performance, Socialize
                                          Battles: Archery, Brawl, Melee, Presence, War
                                          Secrets: Investigation, Larceny, Lore, Occult, Stealth
                                          Endings: Athletics Awareness, Bureaucracy, Integrity, Medicine
                                          All: Martial Art

                                          Okay, so what's the 3e set look like? IN a way it's kind of a split difference. This time Martial Arts is kind of pulled from anyone having it special, and Brawl is back. So Mars keeps Brawl, but doesn't get Integrity, it instead filling in for its place in Ending's gap.

                                          I think a few things need to be considered as the devs write things. One is that to some extent, the Charm structure for Sidereals in 1e can't really be held in contxt of current Charms. That is to say, I don't think there being a Charm per scripture line is something we'll see again, if only because 3e has a general feel of avoiding "This Ability has four Charms and you're done." This is why a lot of Sidreals burned a favored on MAs or Occult no matter what, afterall. So I think we'll porobably see bigger Sidereal Charm trees. Not necssarily Solar or DB big, but bigger enough that there might be more to get to those ultimates, or something. This in itself probably opens some space to incorerate older effects and also expand Abilities for Sidsereals.

                                          I think a big thing that might end up is some Charmsets just get tossed out and written froms cratch, with maybe some extras tossed in to help. Here's what I mean on that. Take Resistance. A big issue wiht it in 2e is that it was kind of jus tthe 1e Endurance and Reistance trees glued together, with not a lot done to the former Battles Charms to make them fit in the Journeys flavor. I think here, And some of the Endurance Charms got transported over to Athletics, which made it even messier. And so on.

                                          Likely what'll happen is we see Resistance kind of just ripped apart and reevaluated. Many Charms might not reappear at all, replaced with ones that fit it better. Effects that are iconic, It also probably will just mean a shuffle on what Abilitty is associatedw ith what constellation. But I think if one was willing ot accept hey, maybe we end up with Integrity Charms based on the Drwning Maiden as something that could of happened, I don't see how it's not possible to make Integrity Charms based on the Scripture of the Expectant Maiden

                                          Sidereals are weird in that they have some very specific things based on the mechancis of the game and their homologues in the setting that other Exalts I don't think really feel in the same way. But I think it's also worth remembering is thes relationships are arbitrary, and while they were well tuned in 1e, doens't mean that changes are bad unless they're done in a lazy (or as at times I feel, hostile) fashion as the 2e adaptation did things.
