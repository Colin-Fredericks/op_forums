                                        I think that, as has already been mentioned, the Acquaintances are designed with the idea that they are people with whom a Sidereal can just relax and be themselves - they're mortals, and thus unlikely to have any agendas with regards to Heavenly politics, they're normal folks, so they don't have much when it comes to mortal politics (necessarily), and the Sidereals can build relationships with them.

                                        They are there to humanize the Sidereals, to remind them what it is like to live in Creation. To a Bronze Sidereal, they might be a representation of why it is so important to ensure that Solars do not get control of the world again - mortals like their friends were slain in great numbers when the Solars went mad. To a Gold, they are reminders of why it is important to have the Solars in charge again - because their friends' lives suck, and the Solars made things better. To an Independent, it may simply be a deep connection as to why it is important to continue doing their job - the regulation of Fate makes the lives of others like their friends less likely to end because of reasons (whatever those may be).

                                        Acquaintances are the mundane friends of a Sidereal - outside the Heavenly politics of Yu-Shan or the Bureaucratic rivalries of their fellows, these are people to whom the Sidereal can show their true face, and just complain about how crappy their job is, or the like, over tea or alcohol, without fear that their superiors will hear and offer them some sort of punishment. The life of a Sidereal is full of incredible tension even as it's a series of mundane bureaucratic jobs - is that god trying to force you into showing off your opinion on a matter that will harm the interests of your Bureau? Is this being a spy for Rakan Thulio, or is he just addicted to Celestial Cocaine and trying to get his next hit? Etc. Acquaintances are those with whom the Sidereal can forget about such things and just... be themselves.

                                        Whether this is something that they should be charged bonus points for is another question, but it seems a premature one to me, given how in 3e the exact nature of Sidereal Arcane Fate is not clear, and whether Acquaintances will be making a return as a merit is unknown.

                                        All that I write but don't cite is simply my perspective, colored by my experiences and beliefs. I extrapolate a lot, too, so don't take it too seriously. :P
