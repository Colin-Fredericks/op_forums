                This probably falls under Headcanon really, but since we're discussing Suntarankal here...

                *The Crucible of Brass and Iron manifests as a vast complex across many miles of the Demon City, built around a seven-leveled pagoda, within which is the punishing labyrinth of the Forty-Nine Chambers. Most of the Unquestionable allow their component souls their own way in life, but Suntarankal is not so giving. Each floor of his central tower is full of mechanical and environmental hazards, and guarded by one of his Second Circle souls, who pose the final test for each student to ascend to the next level; passing these trials within the Chambers can instantly teach a Charm in a single Style the student is training in. Those who fail any of the tests (which range from physical duels to games of skill or chance to riddle or koan contests) are Banished from the pagoda and must start again, facing new challenges, from the bottom floor.

                Those who pass the final test of Suntarankal's Warden Soul may claim their prize, which always varies with each student. Sometimes it is the final Charm technique of the Style they study, making them masters; other times, it is a demonic Endowment, enabling the student to practice a level of supernatural martial arts beyond that which they normally can. Once, there was a manual which contained the means to reconstruct a Sidereal Martial Art that was otherwise lost to the ages.

                It was this last soul that would brand graduated Masters with the Mark of the Crucible. However, recently (as demons reckon such things), a Sidereal graduate had wise words with the Warden, and put to it a question, that vexed it greatly. This soul soon vanished, beyond the ability of even its Principal to tell where it went or to call it back. Suntarankal is most displeased at this, and has vowed to personally punish the Vizier for their temerity. In the meantime, he has replaced this demon with a powerful Akuma.

                *The most common First Circle Demon created by one of Suntarankal's lesser souls are the Thousand Brassmen. Roughly humanoid figures made of brazen wood, they fulfill their purpose as the primary sparring partners of the Demon City. Showing little initiative, they make poor soldiers, but have an uncanny ability to detect the patterns in an opponents' style, and can even mimic certain charms. The more they spar with a partner, the more they begin to resemble them, until a student is effectively fighting a brass version of herself, even down to her movements and utterances.

                *The Crucible of Brass and Iron can slip through to Creation without being summoned on the occasion when a parent and child fight a formal duel to the death (though they need not know of their relation). One one such occasion, a mortal warrior so impressed him that he allowed her to seduce him, and fathered a powerful Demon-Blooded child. She has since become a master of multiple styles and a destroyer of many dojos, and carves out her legend along the Fighting Tea Road of the Southern Coast (Scroll of Heroes, pg 22). Her father appears to her in her dreams, and she seeks a way to come to him, and claim her ascendancy into true Demon-hood by climbing his tower...

                *Few of the Mortals eking out a poor existence in Malfeas have attained any success or notoriety. Perhaps the greatest is Daron Mason, who was brought to Hell to entertain his masters in gladitorial combat against other Mortal slaves. Tiring of this lifestyle, he escaped, but found life on the Brass City's streets little better. But the demon realm has its own unofficial fighting world, where one may challenge one's limits...

                Demon promoters and trainers liked what they saw in the hardy human, and set him on a harsh regimen of training and treatments that Awakened his Essence and focused his will to survive and win to almost inhuman levels. When they thought he was ready, they sent him to Suntarankal, whose gates welcomed him in, and released him years later, with the brand of Mastery upon him.

                Daron is now a complete Master of First Pulse style, and has gained from the Crucible the ability to combo the charms of that Style. He has since made a name for himself by fighting and winning against First Circle Demon in underground rings, and has become a hero to the desperate Mortals just barely surviving in the cracks of the Demon Realm. He knows this can't last; it is only a matter of time until enforcers of Cecelyne's Law make an example out of him; the best he can hope for is to be made an Akuma for one of the Yozis. He intends to escape to Creation before either happens.

                Maybe those new Green Sun Prince guys can help...
