        I think you're on to something Sunder, and I remember asking Holden something similar about this back when we were discussing how Solar Charms are extensions of themselves, how Martial Arts Charms were distinct entities, etc, but I never really got a response. Sidereal Charms are essentially external powers that you learn how to use, rather than internal power like a Solar's Charm. A Solar simply channels Essence through his arm while swinging a sword and, voila, he now has a new Charm. If a Solar encounters something entirely new, they can still channel their Essence while approaching the task. So for example, if a car suddenly appeared in Creation,a Solar could channel Essence while driving it and that Solar would now have car driving technique once he had been able to attain basic mastery over the task.

        Meanwhile, Sidereal Charms are external miracles granted to them by the Maidens. Sidereal can't simply make up a new Charm. Rather, they can only channel their Essence in certain proscribed ways. Now of course we can get into meta ideas, where if a Sidereal player wants to make a new Charm then the ST can simply say that Charm always existed. But from an in-character view, if the Charm doesn't exist, then it's not something that can exist. So if a Sidereal finds a car, and the ST decides that this uses a new Ability (Drive) and that there are no Sidereal Drive Charms in Creation (because he hates Sidereal and refuses to look at Shards of the Exalted Dream), then the Sidereal character simply cannot use Essence in conjunction with car driving, no matter how high the character increases his or her Drive score. So in general, if a Sidereal Charm does not exist in-setting then the Sidereal cannot learn or create such a Charm. This is one of the core differences between Sidereal and other Exalted.

        Of course, all of that pre-supposes that the Sidereal Charmset is still closed and given to them by the Maidens like it was in 1st and 2nd edition. So far I haven't seen anything from the dev's (new or old) that would suggest that's changed. So if that's the case, then your comparison of Getimians to hackers seems somewhat reasonable. In this case the Sidereal are like database administrators who are limited to only using the programs and systems that have been proscribed to them by the business owner. They can't start creating their own programs to interact with the database (the Loom of Fate). Meanwhile Getimians (again presupposing that they can create their own Charms that manipulate Fate) they are more like hackers who use their own systems to manipulate and interact with the database in unconventional and unplanned ways.

        So I don't know i that is necessarily the case, but it seems like a reasonable comparison to come to given the data we currently have.

            I think the idea here is that when the Sidereals use their Charms, they are drawing the mastery of the Essence of destiny into their skilled actions, in a complete integrated and fluid motion.

            They're not some "buttons" that exist outside them which for some reason the Sidereals have to be sufficiently good at Ability X to be allowed to press the button. It is a skilled action born from inner essence and mastery of the Ability.

            Neither are they something which are unnatural or a cheat in the universe - the essence of Fate is a natural part of the universe, and Sidereal Charms are a natural consequence of using and understanding that essence.

            There's a fascinating question here in a way about what could happen if the Maidens decided that all Sidereal Charms were not accessible to Sidereals...

            But... I suspect they basically couldn't. It would be an action on the order of the Sun deciding that the essence of Solar light and excellence simply wouldn't flow, and therefore the Solars could not act, or perform Charms, or for the Elemental Dragons halting the flow of elemental essence through Creation to prevent the Dragonblooded accessing Charms. It's probably not in the nature of these beings to be able to do these things.

            The Maidens are more bound by their nature, by necessity and by Fate than any Incarna. They are bound to act in accordance with how Things Must Be and once they've set themselves in motion and set a Fate in motion, they cannot reverse themselves.

            Personal canon, there's something inherent to the Charms of the Sidereal Exalted present in the patterns of fate Essence flowing through Creation; there's not something that can be done about them which wouldn't tend to degrade the structure and nature of Fate.

            If it were possible to remove the Charms of the Sidereal Exalted, I suspect that rather than be diminished they would become something else. Power wouldn't be dammed, but flow differently.

                Anyway, to help centre this discussion in text, how about we go back to how Sidereal Charms were first described by Jenna in Sidereals 1e:

                "Manipulating Fate

                Sidereal Charms are personal abilities, and in one sense, the affect the world in the same fashion as any other natural or supernatural ability - as part of the workings of fate, not as a manipulation thereof. Yet the Sidereals remain destiny's creatures, like the constellations that order their Charms and the Maidens that built those constellations. Using these Charsm requires an active awareness of Essence influences the weave of fate, and of the machinations by which the Maidens bestow their power on their Chosen.

                Sidereals and those Eclipse or Moonshadow Castes arrogant enough to dabble in the magic of the stars, must apply their Charms with versatility, wisdom and skill. An elder Sidereal must often master at least 20 ways to play with fate, none of them easy. She catches glimpses of the future. She uses her connection to her Maiden to implement spot changes in destiny planning. She twists impending fate with her own two hands. She learns to appreciate and manipulate the overall patterns of fate that make up each individual and thing in Creation - or at least, what those things are about to be. The list continues.

                Most of these Charms come with a built in methodology. However, Sidereal Exalted who want to do things a little differently can generally work out ways to do so. As a matter of personal style, a Sidereal can learn to do most of these Charms in one of three or four fashions best suited to the character. The rest, they can adapt on the fly using stunts. For example, a Sidereal character who needs to dodge an attack without excessive motion can adapt the Absence Charm - that normally helps her get out of the way - to make the attacker miss instead. The Storyteller must, of course, find her player's description of fate's manipulation interesting enough to qualify for a stunt. Depending on the game this may favour heavily visual images or more abstract approaches. Knotting the weave of an enemy's weapon together with the fate-flow of a nearby river, dragging it off course, might work - or the Sidereal might impose her memory of how much being hit with a sword hurts and is bad on the incoming blade"

                        One alternative about what Sidereal Charms are. Possibly an alternative, possibly just a rephrasing of what we see in canon (but let's see where this takes me).

                        Imagine a hero who can pick up any magical weapon. He's not bound to any of them, and can master any he can impose his will and magic on. He can claim and lose any he chooses. His fate is open like a book. This is a lot like how a Solar approaches Charms.

                        Now imagine a hero who is fated to wield a particular blade. Any of the others doesn't... feel right, he can't use them, they rebel from his touch, the path of his fate turns him away from the wrong weapon by a hundred stratagems, until his destined companion - the one he is *meant* to slay kings and nations with - is in his hands again. He's a hero on whom lives heavy the hand of destiny. This is a lot like how a Sidereal approaches Charms.

                        Sidereal Charms are the Charms that they are destined to use, and which are woven into the fabric of their fate as a Sidereal, and the patterns of fate and essence in the world which they can perceive and understand. They are techniques Sidereals are destined to use, which it is within their path, to learn and wield.

                            I think that, if I were writing Sidereals, their charms would be a metaphorical cross between a bureaucratic form that you fill out on paper and a Martial Arts form that you perform with your body. Second, it lets me say that undisciplined tampering with Fate is dangerous and that these particular "Forms" are the safe way to manipulate it... which implies that there are distinctly UNsafe ways to manipulate it that Sidereals, like Rakan Thulio, might be able to access.

                                Personally (and keep in mind I have not kept up with 3rd in the slightest because my group looked at the mammoth of a core book, shrugged, and went a collective "meh", so I'm mostly operating in second edition here), Sidereal charms have always basically struck me as kind of, well, spells. As in, when a Solar learns [Archery Charm whatever], he's just learning to bow gud by pushing a pile of essence into a thing he's already doing awesomely. When a Sidereal is learning Avoidance Kata, he is specifically learning the reproducible spell known as the Avoidance Kata. Sidereal Charms always struck me as very much discrete entities and processes to manipulate external elements - Gnostic spells, sort of. The Sid is not the guy that is so awesome that he bends reality to his whim, he's the guy that has studied where all the buttons of reality are and can play the piano on the universe's keyboard, or the guy who is perfectly acquainted with how the bureaucratic system works and knows which form to fill to get shit done.

                                So yes, a lot more external than most Exalted. But, like, Sidereals always struck me as very much the most "external power" of the Exalts, and I don't see how this takes agency away from them or anything. Shit, they're practically my favorite splat, and this reliance on things outside of themselves may very well be one of the biggest reasons why.

                                        I've always seen Sidereal Charms as "tricks," a series of clever manipulations of Creation, and of its laws, structure, and functions. Sidereals are philosophers, and by choosing to perceive something in a particular way, they can take advantage of Creation's quirks--the shortcuts, hidden features, and Easter eggs built into its warp and woof.

                                        Examples: Poisons each work in a particular way, and diseases progress in specific fashions--but Sidereals can look at both as undesired add-ons to their own destiny, and pull them off, hanging them in orbit around their own to be attached to Someone Else's Destiny, instead.

                                        A doomed or dying Earth elemental has an unfortunate destiny--but Sidereals know how to subvert that fate, if the elemental is willing to attach its resilience to their own for a while.

                                        Cities aren't just spots where lots of buildings and people are, they're bulwarks against the Wild, and are like unto living things themselves, attached to Road Gods and each with its own City Father/Mother--but Sidereals know that each one is a knot defined by its placement, alliances, struggles, secrets, and reckonings, and they know how to slide those knots along the threads that comprise them, changing its placement, and rewriting the other four factors to suit that change.

                                            Does anyone know about the foreign comic called "The Gamer"? Some dude wakes up one day and discovers that his life has become gamified, where he can see people's Levels above their heads and he can earn XP to improve his abilities. It's spawned a number of really boring fanfics where other characters get such powers and just endlessly grind their numbers up.

                                            "This Is The Run" has a different philosophy.

                                            Ruby Rose of RWBY woke up one day to find her personal experience of the world is gamified, but like any game, Remnant seems to have some inherent glitches that she alone can see and exploit.

                                            So the red-cloaked speedster has now set herself on the course to SPEED RUN her own story.

                                            This fanfic is complete with only six chapters, because Ruby succeeds and the gag would quickly overstay its welcome.

                                            Learning how to infinitely copy a jar full of cookies by putting it in her dresser drawer is just the beginning. She also learns how to clip through walls, manipulate encounter rates, and fudge with item drops.

                                            Right next to "Miracles of Ancient Wonder", I think this is the perfect example of what a Sidereal Exalted Ruby Rose would look like.


                                            I see this as being different from a Getimian in that the Sidereal is exploiting qualities which already exist within Creation, whereas Getimians are using their Internal Looms to free-form hack and rewrite the "game" around them.

                                                This is entirely my opinion:

                                                I always viewed the reason that Sidereal Charms are weird, abstract and obtuse is because the Maidens are weird, abstract and obtuse. The Charms of an Exalt represent an Exalt's personal capacities filtered through the lens of their patron's mythos and theme.

                                                If I am an archer and a Solar, then my archery is perfected. My arrows never miss, no matter the distance or circumstance. You could blind me, mutilate my hands, cast me into a hurricane with the crudest and meanest bow, and ask me to strike a target a mile away- and I would do so. Flawlessly. Repeatedly.

                                                You could array an army against me and I would strike them down, for each of my arrows can pierce the hearts of a score of men before stopping, for my skill at arms is beyond divine. Perfection flows from me to my bow, to my arrows! I could shatter the dome of heaven if I so wished!

                                                But if I'm a sidereal, then it's different. My quiver holds not just arrows- but solutions. By dint of my skill, I turn deadly weapon into anything I require. From my quiver can come food, shelter, life and love. In battle my arrows are inescapable, they twist around barriers and come from every direction. Not because of any perfection, but because all possible paths lay before me and I choose them all. If you take quiver, if you strip me of my arrows, I can turn poems and curses into ammunition. Through the medium of the bow, I can touch infinity!

                                                This is because the Sun is perfection through human pursuit. Excellence and light flow from him. The Maidens aren't that- they're obtuse and strange, often unintuitive. Their powers are parables and sutras, abstract lessons drawn from ordinary things. They don't mean to be, anymore than the Sun means to shine, they just are. By simply plucking a beautiful flower from the ground, Saturn transforms an ordinary action into a lesson of the primacy of finality, of the inevitability of death- By arriving somewhere, Mercury reflects the newness of a place as seen through the eyes of a stranger, the beginning of relationships between a person familiar and a person new!

                                                In my mind, Sidereal charms are like they are because the Maidens chose that, they are as they are because of something inherent to the nature of the Maidens.
