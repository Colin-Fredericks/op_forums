    Agree on breadth and character being important. When we have these threads, I do think part of the problem is that people talk about Sidereals in a way that homogenises them.

    With Solars, we'd be talking about how there should be clear and differentiated paths for Castes, and within that, for different characters within a Caste.

    Whereas with Sidereals and Lunars it feels a lot more like so long as there is a Sidereal or Lunar path, then there isn't much sensitivity to context for character or caste.

    Take an example of a Sidereal Caste, the Journeys, just because they're the first in the book -

    "In the First Age, the Chosen of Journeys were charged with maintaining and expanding the complex web of transportation and communication networks that spanned all of Creation. Under their management, every kind of commodity moved quickly and efficiently from one corner of the world to another... the Chosen of Journeys saw to it that this vast web was turned against the world's fallen rulers ... and the Dragon-Blooded out manuevered their former lords at every step." and goes on from there.

    So this stuff is pretty cool right? And increasingly powerful diverse and character sensitive expressions of this kind of theme, the theme of Journeys, should be what a Sidereals of the Journeys Caste should be able to draw on as he becomes stronger, more than him becoming a *generic* powerful Sidereal with a lot of powers that are interchangeable between different Castes and character concepts.

    From this kind of perspective, I can see why magic based on Astrological Colleges might be more appealing than Martial Arts. Martial Arts could make a character into a sort of generic peerless sifu, and seems less naturally to enhance a character as a character and as a character of a particular Caste, with a given role in changing the world in specific ways.

    (Also, isn't Raksi doing the opposite of giving up learning Celestial Sorcery because she can't get Adamant, in the sense of learning it thoroughly while also trying to overcome her limits (in a sort of mad, baby eating sort of way)?)

        You know... what about making Astrology something like meta-Evocations for the 25 constellations? First, you have to learn the fundamentals of the college (the Ability). Then, you can train in the mystic arts of the college (the Charm). Then you can train in something like the destiny of the college, or the nature of the college (Astrology). This could fit in with Sidereal themes a number of ways.

        1) Evocations that change the nature of a skill in some way. Solars are still the best at using the skill. But Sidereals understand the nature of the skill better, so they can apply it in round-about ways. A solar can hit you with a table, or a sword. He can even swing the table like a sword, and might just rip you in half, but he's never going to slit your jugular with that table. The Sidereal could pick up the table, and say, "Through my understanding of the College of Melee, this table is a melee weapon, like a rapier, and so can do rapier-like things," and perfectly pierce you through the heart with it.

        2) Evocations that alter the nature of a charm in some way. Maybe even let Sidereals research or discover 'new' evocations in a College. The constellations are huge, amazingly powerful things, and it would probably be silly to say Sidereals know everything that can be done with them. Lock off Greater Astrology as before, but give them a nice run of possible evocations written up, and let them research how to use their Abilities and Charms a bit more flexibility. I'm thinking something like 'Take an Charm from another ability, apply it to the use of this skill, in this situation.' For example, let's take a theoretical Endurance charm that lets you go without food. You know that charm, and you know an evocation in the College of Medicine that modifies it in a particular way. Something like: "While you have this Evocation active, you cannot use the Endurance charm in its normal manner. You can, however, apply it to one other creature as a Medicine charm."

        3) Let the Sidereals keep giving an astrological blessing, but simply the rules. Make the benefit something resembling a specialty dot, make it easier to give, restrict the number of dots he can hand out, make it for a fixed duration, a hero can only have one astrological blessing at a time, and the effect can be changed until it runs out, but it can be renewed. Something like 'You three heroes get a one-dot specialty in Melee when fighting side-by-side-by-side, for a period of one week.'

        This would allow Sidereals to actually work destiny to some extent: If someone has a Geas that they will only be killed by a rapier, the Sidereal can rework the situation to take advantage of it, even if he doesn't have a rapier. This would also give Sidereals more flexibility in their current charm set by applying them in other places. Honestly, the Sidereal charmset already does this to a great extent. If a Sidereal wants to steal his opponents armor, he uses Larceny. However, Soak is kind of like armor, so the Sidereal says close enough, and steals some Soak. Or, if a Sidereal wants to steal his opponent's knowledge, he can steal his journals. However, thoughts are kind of like a journal, so the Sidereal steals that instead. Finally, this would let the wise sage say 'The prophecy supports this,' or the wise Sifu who teaches the three heroes one particular fancy technique, and it has immediate applicability.

        Do some cost-balancing things (Dots in a college, or just access to the college, plus evocations from the college?) some fine-tuning, and voila, you have a flexible, useful, and in-theme astrology that doesn't require its own subsystem (except in a very small way for #3).

        This idea is VERY unrefined, but I think it is sufficiently simple and elegant enough to fit into whatever Sidereals end up doing in 3rd, and sufficiently easy to houserule into effect for 2.5 games. Doesn't look unbalanced on the surface to me (probably because there are no actual rules there yet). And I think this is very in theme with how Sidereals would do things.

        Last thought... An evocation for Alchemy that would let you turn what would normally be a potion of strength for a human into a potion of super-bouncing for bears. (80s/90s cartoon reference, for those who haven't seen the cartoon in question).
