                THE SAVANT
                About a thousand years ago, the Savant was adopted into a monastery that secretly preserved many magical secrets of the First Age. Quickly mastering these secrets, he would have become one of the leading members of this cloistered society had he not decided to break his vows by giving into the wanderlust that had obsessed him his entire life by leaving to see the forbidden world beyond. It was this act that Exalted him as a Chosen of Mercury, and led him farther than he could ever have dreamed, as he was taken to Heaven to join the Fellowship.

                While he did well at his studies, he excelled at magical artifice, soon establishing a reputation as one of the foremost artifact craftsmen amongst the Viziers, with his work in demand from his fellows and gods alike. He spent his time off from the Bureau amongst the Mountain Folk, learning their ways, from the byzantine politics of the Conclave to the crafting disciplines of the commoners; although he knewthe celestial commands to demand their services, he never used them, which earned him the respect they rarely gave any outsiders. He enjoyed many friends and allies in Yu-Shan, and counted Chejop Kejak himself as one of his truest friends. But he always took every opportunity to visit Creation and dwell among its people and their cultures.

                The Great Contagion and Fair Folk Invasion put an end to this idyllic life. As his fellow Sidereals fled back to the relative safety of Heaven, he was one of the few who urged them to plunge back into danger with what relief they could muster. But he could do little to help a world drowned in disease, death and madness. It was at this time that he lost his original name; some say he sacrificed it to save just one mortal, others claim it was ripped from him by a Raksha Duchess, while others suggest that he lost it due to his inability to keep a Charm-enforced vow to save the goddess who was the love of his life, and whose star he wears as an Artifact to this day. Whatever the truth, from then on, he has only ever been known as the Savant.

                After the Scarlet Empress saved the world and it settled into some semblance of normalcy, the Savant loudly castigated his fellow Chosen and the Gods for their cowardice, breaking off ties with them and especially Kejak. Some say he was exiled for his temerity, while others believe he tendered his resignation from the Bureau and fled before his Exaltation could be "recycled". In any event, he is officially on Detached Duty, has forfeited his Salary and Celestial Manses, and has spent the subsequent centuries traveling Creation and the Realms beyond as a Ronin.

                The Savant has explored every corner of Creation, and has an unending enthusiam for discovering the wonders and horrors it holds. He often travels with company, preferably young heroic mortals who would rarely have such chances to see what their world has to offer (he does ensure that they have low Destiny ratings, as he is mindful not to upset the Bureaus' work too much); many of them return home enriched with experiences, while others find a new place or cause in need of them, and some meet grisly ends. He has also been known to travel with gods, elementals, even the occasional Fair Folk commoner or Third Circle Demon, and sometimes even other Exalts.

                Despite common belief, the Savant has not cut all ties with Yu-Shan. As a ranking Harbinger, he still receives invitations to divine soirees, which he usually ignores, but he often brings his companions to see the Carnival of Meeting (although he personally prefers seasonal parties). He is also often invited to lecture at the Bureau on the methodology of artifact creation to young Sidereals, who are sometimes assigned to travel with him in Creation as part of their training (which usually involves hands-on real-life experience and warnings about Celestial politics that they can't learn from their studies), and sometimes his expertise leads him to be consulted on issues that confound even the highest Gods of Heaven. More irritatingly, he still receives missions from Oversight, high-ranking Gods, or even the Maidens themselves, understanding that his continued sabbatical freedom depends on the plausible deniability his wanderings afford Heaven when they would prefer their doings remain secret...

                For now, the Savant is quite content to travel the world, enjoying its sights, sharing its bounty with a chosen few, and stopping the odd Enemy of Creation. However, he is aware that the winds of fate are blowing and the Third Age is at hand. He has had increasing contact with Solars and other, even rarer, types of Exalted, and is growing aware that he may have to give up meddling in small ways, to once again contribute to the Tapestry of Creation as a whole. Still, he puts off this inevitable accounting, for one more day's journey...

                Notes: The Savant is an elder Chosen of Journeys of about a thousand years and, as such, has a wide range of Abilities and Specialties to reflect his diverse travels and interests, as well as many Sidereal Charms, Excellencies and Colleges. He is a Celestial sorcerer of surpassing skill and power (with Cirrus Skiff as his Control Spell), is very capable at Astrology, and, while he prefers to avoid combat, practices more of the "non-violent" styles, such as Crane, Mantis, the Art of Meditative Discussion and Throne Shadow Style (his mortal companions, who count as Acquaintances, are often Blessed with astrological adjustments to their fates that increase their chances of survival and success, and are claimed by him as Shadow Fingers). He has long since mastered Prismatic Arrangement of Creation Style, and has distilled his unique understanding of Mountain Folk industry into a Sidereal Martial Art he calls Brass Assembly of Clockwork Style, based on the Principle of Creation; he has never shared this with another Vizier, but may be willing to initiate a promising Twilight into its secrets...

                As one of the premier craftsmen in Creation, the Savant naturally has all the wonders his Artifact 5 rating affords, including a suit of Silken Armor (cut in the elegant style of a gentleman adventurer from a society that no longer exists...or, he suggests, does not yet exist), goggles that effectively work as Authochthonian , a Starmetal pocketwatch he designed in Varangia with Evocations that greatly aid his Astrological powers, and a Jade stick, which can vary its length and thickness to become an easily concealable wand, to a walking stick to a Wrackstaff, which serves him as a Favored Weapon; it also has Evocations that aid in the analysis, assembly and disassembly of mundane and magical objects and effects. All of his Artifacts are bound to him by Destiny-Knitting Entanglement.
                Perhaps his most prized treasure is his Traveling Pagoda, which he discovered shortly after parting ways with Heaven. The Savant spent an untold amount of time repairing and restoring it, endlessly modifying it through integrating Artifact subsystems and sorcerous workings, until it is unique even among its rare class of artifact; it now has the cloaking, shielding and teleportation effects (along with their drawbacks) of an Indomitable Conquest Platform, although these are all quite draining. He has also succeeded in awakening its spirit, which can communicate with him wordlessly through the rapport the two have forged in centuries of travel over land and sea together.

                The Savant rarely bothers with crafting Resplendent Destinies (when he does, he makes use of the Gull, the Pillar, the Guardians, and the Treasure Trove the most), and is content to let Arcane Fate distort his doings; he is responsible for many legends of wandering heroes across Creation, and sometimes uses the Resplendency to gift mortals with useful Talismans. Among the Spirit Courts and other beings puissant enough to remember him, he has many Allies, many who owe him Favors, and quite a few enemies.

                                    "DENANDSOR" HAWK

                                    Like many children across Creation, the trouble-making, adventuresome boy called Hawk played at being a Scavenger Lord; unlike most of them, he never grew out of his dream, instead growing into the very model of it, a well-built, rakishly-handsome, rugged explorer in weathered riding leathers, gear and mysterious talismans, daring ancient ruins to bring back lost wonders. Eventurally, he found the Shard Vortex in the Valley of Charred Souls, the big score that would finally bring him fortune and glory...but at what cost? The more Hawk thought about it, the more he could "see" what impact his discovery would have on the world, until, with great reluctance, he put it back where he found it, and destroyed all his notes. That choice, hard as it was, Exalted him as a Chosen of Jupiter.

                                    Hawk's aptitudes landed him on a Convention on Discovery in the Division of Secrets. The planned Destiny of Creation mandates that certain people find certain things at specific times, and it is his job to ensure that things worked out that way. Often this involves using his connections to pull funding from excavations, or inspiring tribesmen to attack and drive out the desecrators. Hawk often secretly buys the survivors a round later, as he does have a great deal of respect for his former confederates, and does regret that aspect of his duty. Other times, Hawk must lure or spur people into the right place to make "accidental" discoveries, at the right time. Many lost writings, artwork, and even the occasional Artifact have rejoined the story of Creation this way, usually causing it to branch off in unexpected ways. For the most part, mortals play out their roles as decreed by the Bureau; even the strongest-willed of them have little chance to alter their fates. But when sorcerers, God-Blooded, Exalts, Demons and their cultists, and stranger beings get involved, Destiny gets warped and complicated, and that's when Hawk rolls up his sleeves and gets to work.

                                    Although he grouses about it, this is the part of his job that he really enjoys the most; getting out from behind his celestial deskful of divine paperwork into the fresh air of Creation to face whatever Essence-Users and the Fateless can throw at him. Bureau wisdom says that the best way to deal with such situations is by carefully arranging matters so that a Sworn Brotherhood or Celestial Circle is properly motivated to take care of the problem for you, but sometimes there's none around when you need them and you don't have time to find one when those skeletons have unearthed the Apocalypse Canon and are shipping it via caravan or ship to their Deathlords' Citadel. Sometimes you just have to jump astride a spell-conjured horse, gallop across the roads or waves, storm the damned thing, wipe out the skeletons/demons/whatever, and put it back yourself!

                                    Of course, Hawk gets called to the carpet all the time for this, and tries not to roll his eyes at the lectures of his Elders about "proper procedure" and keeping a low profile. So long as the job gets done, it's all good, right? Missions like this are part of the reason Hawk gained the nickname "Denandsor" among the Fellowship, although he dislikes talking about that particular mission...

                                    Hawks' job has gotten progressively harder over the last few years, as entire classes of Exalted (some never seen before) have started getting into the Relic Prince business, making his job that much harder. It's enough to make a guy consider joining up with those Gold Faction yahoos that keep slipping their literature under his door...

                                    Notes: Denandsor Hawk is a young Chosen of Secrets, less than a century old. His Backing, Salary and Celestial Manse are all on the low side; his Mentor is Arcius the Wise, the ghost of a First Age scholar who acts as the curator for the Reliquary of Glorious Antiquities, a massive museum in Yu-Shan that Hawk brings some of his findings that are too dangerous or unknown even to Destiny to leave in Creation for safe-keeping and study.

                                    He has already mastered Sidereal Melee and Brawl, and supplements it with the Art of Forceful Declaration. In action, he relies on a Starmetal Bracers and Resistance Charms to keep himself safe. His favorite Artifact weapon is Lasha, a sapient (and sarcastic) metallic serpent he rescued from a First Age site lost in the Wyld; consulting the Loom of Fate revealed that HE was destined to find it, which he found amusing. In addition to biting and strangling his enemies, Hawk can use her as a Direlash, and has become quite adept at Ensnare Gambits.

                                    Hawk is still getting used to adopting various Resplendent Destinies as cover personas, mainly drawing from the Houses of Battles and Secrets. He has become quite skilled at subtly laying Blessings and Curses to help or hinder the discovery of relics.

                                                    HOSHI AI

                                                    There are many in the Division of Serenity who greatly resent the stereotype (true as it may be) of their department being nothing more than a glorified brothel, and the Joybringers as sybarites, so they work very hard to redeem their reputations and the image of the Cerulean Lute of Harmony. And then there are those who work equally hard to live down to all the rumors...Hoshi Ai is one of the latter.

                                                    A midlevel worker in his Division, Ai put in the bare minimum of work he could get away with, spent most of his energy organizing office pools betting on various events in Heaven and Earth, and carousing with as many hot gods and goddesses as he can. This last was the last straw for his superiors, specifically when he was caught in a supply room snorting Celestial Cocaine off of a high-ranking gods' favorite concubines (no one was sure what the Djala was doing there, however).

                                                    This embarrassment was enough to get Ai formally Censured, and the official sentence (handed down by an Elemental Dragon judge who was a close friend of the god in question) was for him to go to Hell. Specifically, Hoshi Ai was assigned to join a Circle of Viziers (who, coincidentally, had also offended the powers that be in various ways) whose thankless duty was to relocate to Malfeas and send regular reports that would likely never be read.

                                                    Hoshi considered running away to some obscure part of Creation instead, but that sounded like too much work, so he joined his new comrades in their unofficial exile as Strangers, where he remains to this day. The incessant noise, weirdly flat green sunlight and bizarre geometries took a bit of getting used to, but he soon adjusted and plunged into new opportunities for decadence with gusto.

                                                    Hoshi pushes his Exalted constitution and Charms to their limits with marathon sessions of mind (and body) bending debauchery. He is a valued client to the many brothels on the Street of Gold Lanterns (which is also a Third Circle Demon) and has sampled the joys of many varieties of demonic courtesans. He has become a connoisseur of Malfean liquers, brandies and other spirits, and is one of the few non-demons to avoid a debilitating addiction to Infernal Crack (which is also a Third Circle Demon). He often pauses on his trips to Hells' gambling parlors to join in impromptu jam sessions with groups of demons loitering on the streets. Hoshi is far more popular in Hell than he ever was in Heaven.

                                                    His Circle-mates fare about the same: Eight-League Yan quickly realized the futility of compiling a working map of Hell, and instead set about seeing if he could catalogue and tame the various types of fauna native to Malfeas; most recently he has learned that Sidereal Familiar Charms DO, in fact, work on such "animals" and hopes to evolve a Peghedu into a Spirit-Beast in time for them to compete in the Thousand-Acre Round, an anything-goes race where one must ride ones' steed around a large orbit of Malfeas' layers, surviving attacks from the inhabitants of the many Fiefdoms one rides through, as well as from ones' fellow racers.

                                                    Blazing Helm hoped to study Malfean methods of making war, from logistics to tactics. Disguising himself as a demon, he joined a underdog Fiefdom in danger of being overrun on three sides by more powerful opponents, fully intending to desert once their cause was finally lost. To his surprise, he has grown an intense bond with the lowly demons he has fought beside, along with great respect for their dedication to die for their territory. He has secretly used his celestial powers to subtly sway things their way so that they have held on longer than they should have. He seriously considers exposing himself to give these demons that he now sees as people an even better fighting chance...

                                                    Violet Cessation got bored and hired herself out as an assassin. Many demon lords and soldalities secretly come to her to rid themselves of rivals, obstacles or anyone else they need dissolved beyond the possibility of reformation. Rumors claim that she has even dispatched a Second Circle Demon on behalf of Serfs! Unfortunately, she recently caught the emotional disease Lypothymie (which is also a...well, you know), which has really put her off her game, as she now spends most of her time listlessly sighing, reminiscing about long-lost times and people that never were.

                                                    Revealing Veil, the de facto leader of the Circle, is the only one who really gives a damn about their mission. She has no intention of spending any more time in Hell than she has to, and so constantly tries to rally the others to do their jobs, which is a losing battle. Still, she dutifully reports to the Warden of the Exiles, establishes a web of contacts throughout demonic society, and keeps her eyes open for anything that Heaven might deem worthy of cutting their mission short. Or at least, hers.

                                                    For his part, Hoshi Ai has recently had an encounter with an odd type of Exalt that he's never heard of before at a "lame-ass" party that he was rescuing with some of the Celestial Coke that he had smuggled from Yu-Shan. Revealing Veil would find this incident very interesting, if only he could clearly recall it from the hazy memories of that night...

                                                            Citrine Warden, aka Duchess of Spice, aka the Merchant's Shield.

                                                            Citrine Warden was born in Thorns in approximately 300 RY, daughter of a proud merchant house that had more debts than success, but wasn't ready to fall in status. Through a series of increasingly improbable events, Citrine became a lightning rod of good and bad fortune, catapulting the family to fantastic wealth before being crushed under a mountain of debt before finding the debt annulled before finding their assets seized before finding a dragonblooded suitor betrothing her into his house before he was mortally wounded by an anathema. Attending to her dying betrothed, wondering what destiny had in store for her next, she saw the road that destiny had led her on, and onward it went. As he looked upon her, he hoarsely whispered, "Anathema", accusing her with his dying breath. The room was bathed in citrine light and she saw the destiny of her merchant family. Bewildered with her new understanding and power, she set about restoring her family's assets, forging aliances, and creating wealth that would last generations. But within a short time the alliance crumbled, and the wealth faded as she was forgotten, and her forceful personality couldn't keep the success coming in. It was at this point that heaven finally caught up with her, explained everything, and with a slight tweaking, showed her her first mission of restoring the family to prominence, without her at its focus. Her affairs settled for the moment, she was brought into heaven, and trained.
                                                            In the centuries since, she has focused on trade, and used her business upbringing and acumen to promote trade relationships throughout the inland sea and beyond. She has a conflicted view of the guild. In Nexus she views the guild as a whole good, where it fosters trade and brings wealth to many who wouldn't have any. In many other strongholds however, she sees it as an evil that hoards wealth and chokes trade. In far flung outposts where it is bringing trade to isolated areas, she encourages and supports their efforts, even as she works to set up rival merchants who can compete.
                                                            She checked in our her family from time to time, less and less as the years went by. It had been 75 years since she had last visited her home when the Deathlord known as the Mask of Winters savaged it. As prominent family, hers was first to have their flesh flensed and their wealth stolen. Only a few family members escaped by luck, their trading ships out of port when the calamity struck. She has traded many favors to have them watched and protected, and may have even crossed a line or two of propriety to ensure it happened. None with hearts have spoken against this, but heaven is sadly lacking in compassion.
                                                            As a Citrine Poxes of Contagion and Crane Style master, she is a noted diplomat and master of the Golden Barque's famously flighty bureaucracy, she is often assigned missions which require a delicate hand in multiple locations, or which involve sorcerous maladies have prevented Destiny from moving as ordained. As such, she has exposure to a great variety of foods and drinks, and is a notable epicurean, making detours for famous chefs and exotic delicacies.
                                                            Since the fall of thorns, she has famously not joined the convention of deathlords, instead redoubling her efforts in protecting her distant relatives and descendants and helping them land on their feet. Talk of revenge earns a stern glare and a riposte, "My vengeance is for the living, not the dead."
                                                            Hooks- her family is not yet out of danger, and any threat to them will bring her running, often with PCs in tow to help. While she doesn't harbor any thoughts of revenge towards the mask of winters, many of the survivors do, and can get themselves in a wide range of trouble. The dying words of her husband still haunt her, and while she is a proud proponent of the bronze faction, the immaculate order does worry her. She can and will turn a caravan around if it means she can try some new food or drink, and as a chosen of Mercury, she can usually get things back on track. But sometimes fate has other plans. Guild merchants shutting down trade is a problem for her, and as the realm tightens down its extortion, she and her allies are struggling to keep trade flowing, what deal will they make to keep the spice flowing?
