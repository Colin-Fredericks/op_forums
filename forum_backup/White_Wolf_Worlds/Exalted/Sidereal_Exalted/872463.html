    Sidereals, Martial Arts, and Violet Bier of Sorrows

    04-23-2016, 01:06 PM
    MARTIAL ARTS
    Peerless Masters of the Martial Arts
    No constellation governs the Martial Arts. All constellations govern the Martial Arts. Enlightenment is found not in Creation, nor Heaven, nor Hell, but by turning within. Strength is controlling others; power is controlling oneself. This is the Essence of Martial Arts.

    The Sidereal Exalted are often considered the peerless masters of martial arts; the motions and emulations of the styles come as naturally to them as their ability to weave fate and destiny. As mentioned in Character Creation (pg. XX), all Sidereals gain the Martial Arts merit for free, and may purchase dots in any Martial Arts ability and any Martial Arts Charms as Favored.

    However, Sidereals do not exhibit the casual mastery that Solars or the recently appeared Abyssals do. While they have a deep relationship with Martial Arts, they lack the raw power needed to automatically make full use of Martial Arts charms.

    Mastery
    As described in the core book (pg. 427), Sidereals can access the Mastery effects of Martial Arts charms through their own esoteric methods. These methods include:
    * When a Sidereal performs the special action action for a Style’s Form Charm, they may use that Style’s charms with the Mastery effect for the remainder of the scene.
    * A Sidereal that has mastered a martial art may craft a Master’s Sutra, describing the essence of the Style. By revealing a prayer strip with that sutra and paying 5m, they may use the mastery effects of that style for the remainder of the scene. These sutras are each unique to the Sidereal, as the nature of enlightenment cannot be copied; revealing them reveals the master’s identity.
    * When a Sidereal enters a Martial Arts form, they may use that Style’s charms with the Mastery effect as long as they remain in the Form.

    Sidereal Martial Arts

    In addition to the traditional forms of Martial Arts practiced in Creation, the Sidereal Exalted practice a collection of secret arts and enlightened techniques, which are collectively known as Sidereal Martial Arts. Until recently, no member of the Exalted Host could learn Sidereal Martial Arts without either being a Sidereal, or being tutored by one- even then, only Solars could match their sifus. It is assumed that Death’s Lawgivers could likewise be trained, but thus far, no Sidereal is known to have taken such a pupil.

    Recently, Rakan Thulio has boasted that the Getiman Exalted outstrip the Chosen of the Maidens in both mastery and enlightenment. That these mysterious Chosen have exhibited secret techniques and enlightened arts comparable to the Sidereals themselves testifies to the impossible truth of this boast. Whether the Getiman Exalted are truly capable of independent mastery of Sidereal Martial Arts, or if they, like the Solars, are simply capable of being taught and have a willing sifu in Rakan Thulio is not known- but it is certain that these mysterious Chosen are capable of matching the Sidereal Exalted’s peerless mastery in one form or another.

    The Violet Bier of Sorrows Style
    An ancient style, with roots dating back to the beginnings of the First Age or before, the Violet Bier of Sorrows Style is often one of the first Styles mastered by young Sidereals. It expresses Saturn’s fundamental truth to Creation, and can readily serve as an introduction to deeper mysteries.
    Violet Bier of Sorrows Weapons: Attacks made with the Violet Bier of Sorrows tend to be heavy, chopping swings and bone-shattering drop-kicks. They can also be used with knives, slashing and chopping swords, staves and seven-section staves.
    Armor: Violet Bier of Sorrows Style is incompatible with armor.

    Secrets of Future Strife
    Cost: -; Mins: Martial Arts 1, Essence 1
    Type: Permanent
    Keywords:: None
    Duration:: Permanent
    Prerequisite Charms: None

    The only certain truth is death; of all the secrets that Jupiter holds, Saturn knows this one best. By purchasing Secrets of Future Strife, a Sidereal gains a bonus die to all rolls to detect danger and Join Battle. This die counts as one added by a charm.

    Flight of Mercury
    Cost: 3m, 1wp; Mins: Martial Arts 2, Essence 1
    Type: Simple
    Keywords:: Mastery
    Duration:: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Secrets of Future Strife

    The only journey that all must walk is that from birth to the grave; of all the journeys that Mercury watches, Saturn knows the destination. Flight of Mercury allows a Sidereal to suddenly explode forward in order to strike at a foe; they advance one range band towards an enemy, and must take either attack or rush. Flight of Mercury can only be used once per scene.
    Mastery: If the martial artist crashes the enemy she attacked or rushed when using this charm, she may use it again against another target.

    Blade of the Battle Maiden
    Cost: 5m; Mins: Martial Arts 3, Essence 1
    Type: Simple
    Keywords:: Terrestrial, Mastery
    Duration:: One scene
    Prerequisite Charms: Secrets of Future Strife

    It is only in death that the struggle ceases; of all the battles that Mars fights, Saturn knows that is one her sister cannot win. Blade of the Battle Maiden enchants the Sidereal’s weapon with this truth. Increase the Overwhelming damage of your attacks by 1 for the remainder of the scene.
    Terrestrial: For a Dragon-Blooded to use this Charm, they must make a sacrifice of pain; increase the cost by 1lhl.
    Mastery: When the martial artist assumes the Violet Bier of Sorrows Form, they may also reflexively activate Blade of the Battle Maiden by spending an additional 1wp.

    Joy in Adversity Stance
    Cost: 3m; Mins: Martial Arts 3, Essence 1
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords:: Withering-only
    Duration:: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Secrets of Future Strife

    It is the temporary and ephemeral nature of love that makes it worth having; of all the joys that Venus knows, Saturn knows they are most keenly felt when lost. Joy in Adversity Stance enhances this understanding; when the martial artist is crashed by an opponent at close range, she may make a reflexive withering attack against him, even if she has already attacked this turn.

    Violet Bier of Sorrows Form
    Cost: 8m; Mins: Martial Arts : The Violet Bier of Sorrows 4, Essence 1
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: Form
    Duration: One Scene
    Prerequisite Charms: Flight of Mercury, Blade of the Battle Maiden, Joy in Adversity Stance

    Meditating on the mysteries of the Gull, the Peacock, the Gauntlet and the Sorcerer, the martial artist embodies the ending of all things and in all things. She gains double 10s on decisive damage rolls. Additionally, her base initiative after resetting from a decisive attack is (Martial Arts) instead of 3.
    Special Activation rules: Whenever the martial artist reaches 15+ initative, she may reflexively activate Violet Bier of Sorrows Form.

    Death-Parrying Stroke
    Cost: 2m + 2m / success; Mins: Martial Arts : The Violet Bier of Sorrows 4, Essence 2
    Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: Withering-only, Terrestrial, Perilous
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Violet Bier of Sorrows Form

    At the last moment, the martial artist sees the path of an attack and knocks it aside with a deft twist of essence. Activating the Death-Parrying Stroke costs 2 motes; she may reduce the damage successes rolled on a withering attack made against her by one success per two motes. Death-Parrying Stroke may be used after damage is rolled.
    Terrestrial: When a Dragon-blooded uses this Charm, they instead reduce the damage dice rolled on a withering attack made against her by one die per two motes, and must be used before damage is rolled.

    Life-Severing Blow
    Cost: 2m / die; Mins: Martial Arts 4, Essence 2
    Type: Supplemental
    Keywords:: Decisive-only, Mastery
    Duration:: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Violet Bier of Sorrows Form

    In the hands of the martial artist is the power of Saturn, to bring an ending to her opponents. Activate Life-Severing Blow when making a decisive attack; if the attack is successful, you may convert up to to Essence damage dice into successes. You must pay for Life-Severing Blow before making your attack.
    Mastery: You may convert up to Martial Arts damage dice into successes instead of Essence.

    Metal Storm
    Cost: 10m, 1wp; Mins: Martial Arts 4, Essence 2
    Type: Simple
    Keywords:: Decisive-only, Terrestrial
    Duration:: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Violet Bier of Sorrows Form

    Come they alone or at the head of a vast host, all eventually yield to Saturn’s blade. The martial artist may make a decisive attack against two separate enemies in close range; she makes one attack roll, and then divides her initiative evenly between them, adding her Essence to the damage of each attack.
    Terrestrial: Dragon-blooded do not add their Essence to the damage of the attacks.

    Conclusion Pursuing Approach
    Cost: 3m; Mins: Martial Arts 5, Essence 3
    Type: Supplemental
    Keywords:: Mastery
    Duration:: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Death-Parrying Stroke, Life-Severing Blow, Metal Storm

    It is only by both accepting and pursuing the end that one may delay the inevitable conclusion. This knowledge heartens the martial artist, as she stokes her fighting spirit with determination to never give up. When an attack enhanced with Conclusion Pursuing Approach strikes and deals damage, she gains 1 willpower.
    Mastery: Enemies dealt damage by Conclusion Pursing Approach also lose 1 willpower.

          Meanwhile, their friend, Sorrow of Ages Lost activates a Sutra containing his epiphany on the wisdoms of the Silver-Voiced Nightingale, and for the rest of the scene gains the Mastery benefits of that style, even if he never assumes the Nightingale Form.

                  Originally posted by Ellis View Post
                  So a Sidereal can use Mastery as long as they're in the Form, or by using an individual sutra? Just trying to clarify, since I don't think I understand what the third way is.
                  Why did you decide to make the sutras individual and not style-specific like they presumably used to be?

                  They can use the Mastery effects of a Style if they are in the style's Form. If you have a style that doesn't synergize well, then this'll do ya.
                  They can use the Mastery effects of a Style for the remainder of the scene, if they have fulfilled the Form's special activation requirement- independently of being in the Form. This gives a way for Sidereals to synergize multiple styles together, without having to have all of them mastered- but requires them to fulfil some esoteric and sometimes downright contradictory requirements. It also gives a way for novices to get Mastery effects before they've gotten to the Form.
                  They can use the Mastery effects of a Style by activating their Sutra for the Style- flashing a prayer strip and burning some motes. They can only do this after mastering the Style, but it gives them an easy way to synergize across multiple styles, without having to go through the pain of multiple special activations.

                  So, three Sidereals; Mike Mercury, Vinny Venus and Justin Jupiter.
                  Mike knows Kiss of the Sun Concentration from Righteous Devil Style, but that's it.
                  Vinny knows Snake Style and Tiger Style, both to the Form.
                  Justin has mastered Snake Style and Tiger Style.

                  Suddenly, Anti-Fate Ninjas!

                  Mike wants to use Kiss of the Sun Concentration to flamethrower some anti-Fate ninjas. Since he's not a Solar or Abyssal, he doesn't get access to the Mastery effect. If he lands a decisive attack after aiming on the previous turn, however, he becomes able to use the Mastery effect for the remainder of the scene, having fulfilled the special activation requirement.

                  Vinny wants to break bad with Tiger and Snake wicked synergies. He considers; if he assumes Snake Form, he can use the Mastery effects for Snake Style. If he assumes Tiger Form, he can use the Mastery effects for Tiger Style. But he really wants both- which he can get by assuming one form and completing the special activation requirements for the other. Snake requires Withering someone above you in initative to being below you; Tiger requires landing a decisive blow which inflicts at least three levels of damage. He decides to enter Tiger Form as a simple action, counting on the Mastery bonuses from Tiger Style to give him full access to Snake before too long.

                  Justin also wants to break bad with Tiger and Snake wicked synergies. Luckily for Justin, he's a master of both styles; as a simple action, he enters Snake Form, and reflexively pays 5m to activate his master's sutra of the Tiger Style. He now has access to the mastery effects of both Snake and Tiger Style.

                  Make sense?

                  Re: Individual Sutras

                  Couple of reasons!
                  First, because the Sutra for Violet Bier of Sorrows is traditionally the Sutra of the Sword, the Scripture of the Expectant Maiden. But I'd eaten that for Integrity.
                  Second, because on top of not wanting to write a new sutra for VBoS (since I'm not as good at zen koans as Jenna), I *really* didn't want to write new sutras for every other martial arts style.
                  Third, because of the concept of signature fighting styles being uniquely identifiable by literal calling cards amuses me.

                                                          I agree with Solar on Blade of the Battle Maiden. I am very aware of the benefits of Overwhelming, but I feel it should be a bit more substantial, as it is a fairly expensive Simple Charm. Compare, for example, a single activation of Increasing Strength Exercise, which gives you an arguably far more useful +1 decisive and withering damage for 3m. Moreover, a hypothetical Terrestrial VBoS master would run a mile before sacrificing a precious health level on so weak an effect.

                                                          If I were writing it I would give it +1 Overwhelming and damage to artifact weapons and raise the Overwhelming rate of mundane weapons (including fists) to their artifact equivalent. This makes it both attractive for weapon fighters and for unarmed masters who fight using multiple styles.
