            I think an unambiguous statement of the Vision of Bronze being no longer viable is not helpful to the credibility of certain characters, or versatility of games.

            Much the same as any statement from developers that Creation may be past saving or that the return of the Solars might actually finish it shouldn't have a definitive statement of what such an end of the world might look like.

            Their practical application for gameplay is largely in setting a mood and informing the motivations of certain characters anyway.

            I think John put it fairly well one time when he articulated the resurgence of interest in the Gold Faction from coming out of Sidereals no longer seeing the diminishing of the world as being worth it.             Although I don't care much for most people's ideas of how to pursue the Gold Vision either.

            It's all looks too much like trying to decode the books for secret insights into the nature of the game world in order to find the one correct solution, rather than a set of contexts in which actors do things.
