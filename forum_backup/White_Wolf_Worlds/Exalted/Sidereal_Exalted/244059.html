    Sidereal Job Fair!

    08-29-2014, 12:37 PM
    As presented in previous editions, Sidereals basically have two options: Work for the Bureau of Destiny for life, or go Ronin. For the new edition, I'd like to see more variety in their choices.

    Starting with the Celestial Bureaucracy, must all Sids only work for the B. of Destiny? Why not open up their options so that they can go to work in the other Bureaus? Chosen of Battles should have the chance to work under E-Naluna's Dept of Warfare, or with the Aerial Legion, and I'd think that Chosen of Journeys would be a natural fit for the B. of Nature. Those are just the obvious ones, there are plenty of gods whose domains would benefit from one of the Maiden's Chosen working with them, either to further their aims in Creation, or just working in any of the innumerable businesses the gods operate in Yu-Shan. For that matter, one can easily imagine Viziers being very valued as enforcers or accountants for the god mobs running the derelict slums of heaven. I just think that it's a shame to detail Heaven's society so much, then to say "but your PCs can only work in this one area".

    Sidereals should also be able to find work in the Underworld or Malfeas, much like Gemstone Ocean Hero. Just walk in and set up shop as a Stranger, providing some valuable service to ghosts/demons, without actually serving the agenda of the Neverborn/Yozis as an Akuma or whatever.

    Lastly, Seers should be able to have more of an impact on Creation. Arcane Fate makes running things there difficult, but it should never be seen as an absolute bar to exercising influence among mankind. Since Sidereals are generally not people who feel comfortable in the spotlight anyway, it really aids their "power behind the throne" methodology. Let's see more Siddies behind mortal families, organizations, social movements, not for Heaven's Plan, but for their own reasons (not to make mindless pawns of the mortals; the leaders probably know all about the "mysterious benefactor" they are partners with).

    That's about all I can come up with. Any other ideas for things for Sidereals to do?

                  There are definitely stories to tell about Sidereals working elsewhere. That said, I find myself raising an eyebrow at the implication that working for the Bureau of Destiny means one is cordoned off from a lot. To do their job and see Destiny carried out, Sidereal stories can have them working with and against potentially anyone in the Celestial Bureaucracy and their fields of interest, which in turn span the whole of Creation and realms beyond. Sidereals can find themselves working closely with gods outside the Bureau of Destiny -- or outside Yu-Shan altogether -- both on projects that concern the Bureau of Destiny and on personally decided initiatives. Not to say there isn't additional space opened or potential value in considering other contexts. But yeah, I feel it's important to recognize that this is an expansion added to an area that's already incredibly robust.

                  Though I'll definitely agree there could be a few more examples of Sidereals highlighting some of these possibilities over the millennia. Expressing more of the vast diversity they can have would be very welcome.

                      Being Ronin just means that you've specifically opted to leave the service of Heaven. Sidereals who opt to find some kind of service in the Underworld, or Malfeas or manipulating mortals in some manner other than according to the designs of Fate... those are all Ronin. Ronin basically defines what you aren't, not what you are.

                      Of course, Sidereals in the service of Destiny should also be pursuing various projects in Creation and other places in order to fulfil those designs. As agents of Yu-Shan go, they're fairly hands-on. Heck, Chejop Kejak alone juggles being head of the Central Convention, membership on all of the special ones, being a special advisor to Nara-O, and managing all of his Bronze Faction concerns with being the secretary to most of the Mouths of Peace, a regular instructor at the Heptagram, and a direct collaborator with the Scarlet Empress and tutor to some of her children.

                      Similarly, the idea that Sidereals never diffuse through the other Bureaus, or that they lack in influence upon the planning of Destiny, seems to underestimate how much of a bureaucracy the Celestial Order is.
