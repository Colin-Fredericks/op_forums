    Citrine Warden, aka Duchess of Spice, aka the Merchant's Shield.

    Citrine Warden was born in Throns in approximately 300 RY, daughter of a proud merchant house that had more debts than success, but wasn't ready to fall in status. Through a series of increasingly improbable events, Citrine became a lightning rod of good and bad fortune, catapulting the family to fantastic wealth before being crushed under a mountain of debt before finding the debt annulled before finding their assets seized before finding a dragonblooded suitor betrothing her into his house before he was mortally wounded by an anathema. Attending to her dying betrothed, wondering what destiny had in store for her next, she saw the road that destiny had led her on, and onward it went. As he looked upon her, he hoarsely whispered, "Anathema", accusing her with his dying breath. The room was bathed in citrine light and she saw the destiny of her merchant family. Bewildered with her new understanding and power, she set about restoring her family's assets, forging aliances, and creating wealth that would last generations. But within a short time the alliance crumbled, and the wealth faded as she was forgotten, and her forceful personality couldn't keep the success coming in. It was at this point that heaven finally caught up with her, explained everything, and with a slight tweaking, showed her her first mission of restoring the family to prominence, without her at its focus. Her affairs settled for the moment, she was brought into heaven, and trained.
    In the centuries since, she has focused on trade, and used her business upbringing and acumen to promote trade relationships throughout the inland sea and beyond. She has a conflicted view of the guild. In Nexus she views the guild as a whole good, where it fosters trade and brings wealth to many who wouldn't have any. In many other strongholds however, she sees it as an evil that hoards wealth and chokes trade. In far flung outposts where it is bringing trade to isolated areas, she encourages and supports their efforts, even as she works to set up rival merchants who can compete.
    She checked in our her family from time to time, less and less as the years went by. It had been 75 years since she had last visited her home when the Deathlord known as the Mask of Winters savaged it. As prominent family, hers was first to have their flesh flensed and their wealth stolen. Only a few family members escaped by luck, their trading ships out of port when the calamity struck. She has traded many favors to have them watched and protected, and may have even crossed a line or two of propriety to ensure it happened. None with hearts have spoken against this, but heaven is sadly lacking in compassion.
    As a Citrine Poxes of Contagion and Crane Style master, she is a noted diplomat and master of the Golden Barque's famously flighty bureaucracy, she is often assigned missions which require a delicate hand in multiple locations, or which involve sorcerous maladies have prevented Destiny from moving as ordained. As such, she has exposure to a great variety of foods and drinks, and is a notable epicurean, making detours for famous chefs and exotic delicacies.
    Since the fall of thorns, she has famously not joined the convention of deathlords, instead redoubling her efforts in protecting her distant relatives and descendants and helping them land on their feet. Talk of revenge earns a stern glare and a riposte, "My vengeance is for the living, not the dead."
    Hooks- her family is not yet out of danger, and any threat to them will bring her running, often with PCs in tow to help. While she doesn't harbor any thoughts of revenge towards the mask of winters, many of the survivors do, and can get themselves in a wide range of trouble. The dying words of her husband still haunt her, and while she is a proud proponent of the bronze faction, the immaculate order does worry her. She can and will turn a caravan around if it means she can try some new food or drink, and as a chosen of Mercury, she can usually get things back on track. But sometimes fate has other plans. Guild merchants shutting down trade is a problem for her, and as the realm tightens down its extortion, she and her allies are struggling to keep trade flowing, what deal will they make to keep the spice flowing?

        Cynis Sazara
        The younger twin brother of Cynis herself, Sazara was born in the opulence of the Realm in ascension. And despite his best efforts, could not keep a friend. His first friend was his sister, fostered away swiftly. His assigned playmates suffered injuries and illnesses that kept them from enjoying the games of youth together.
        Then in primary school everyone wanted to be his friend. After all, his first friend exalted two days later. HIs next friend a week later. In fact, none of his friends would last a season before exalteing as a dragonblood and being whisked away to a more specialized school. It got so bad that the guard had to be brought in when a hundred children and their parents chased him through the corridors begging him to be their friend so they could exalt. Melancholic he steadily became the wet blanket of every event and party he attended. A curmudgeon of an old man attached himself to Sazara upon graduation from primary, too stubborn to take no for an answer. For months the two traveled, as his sister began looking for eligible spouses for him. The curmudgeon hounded his steps, never friendly, but always there. As he visited the grave of his earliest friends, cut down by the plague, he saw the ghost of a child crying , and understood his connection. The old man nodded, "About time kid. Lets get you some friends that'll last." And the old sidereal whisked the young Joybringer to Yu-Shan.
        Where he continued to be a morose wet blanket who liked talking to the dead. As often found at the Violet Bier as the Cerulean Lute, he is perhaps the best necromancer in the bureau of destiny, outside the Green Lady. His certainly is one of the creepiest, keeping a collection of ghosts in a Salon of Siblings made up of some of the Realm's house founders, including Peleps, Jyxall, Ledaal, Cynis, and Iselsi. More secrets of the realm exist in in that room than in the whole of the All-Seeing Eye. Of course, getting those ghosts to spill such a secret is an entirely different matter, and Sazara forfends any thought of harm to his brothers and sisters.
        Sazara watches the impending realm civil war with disappointment, wishing his sister's house could have done better.
        He has mastered Water Dragon style and Prismatic Arrangement of Creation, and is improving his skills in shadow throne style. The majority of his improvement is focused on researching more necromancy.
        Hooks- Any party to the realm civil war would give a favored daughter to have just 15 minutes in the Salon of the Siblings, and perhaps such an offer has been made. Why are the PCs offered to this mysterious old dragonblood they've never heard of and who doesn't show on any family registers to do some bizarre favor for, and why is their mentor making goodbyes?
        While the green lady and the rest of the Oracles are subtly displeased to have so many secrets not locked away, the idea of a dour Joybringer blocking them gives many useful tasks for junior Oracles to poke and prod at the necromancer's abode while he is distracted.
        attempting to befriend Sazara is a trial that none have truly accomplished, even with arcane fate. He seems cursed to solitude; but perhaps you could befriend this font of knowedge?
        Once a decade, the children of the Scarlet Empress gather together, both living and dead, to discuss their mother and recount stories of their youths. This weeklong festival has always been kept extremely quiet, lest she find out, but this is the first time she isn't around. And such a party needs someone to provide security and catering and all the other things that keep such an event going, so what are you doing there?
