    Sidereal birthday party hijinks

    07-19-2015, 02:56 AM
    In the solo game I'm running, a Chosen of Journeys NPC's 14th birthday is soon and will be getting 20-30 Sidereal and spirit friends to attend the party. The party will either be in the Solar's 5-dot manse which is staffed by about 500 1st-circle demons and is constantly creating new rooms, or it will be on an unnaturally tall mountain where an Alchemical scout's outpost has been under siege by a Raksha for about a year.

    I know with so many Sidereals in one place Limit Breaks might happen but what other fun nonsense might occur either in the manse or on the mountain, presumably after the Solar finds out it's occupied and possibly does something about the issue?

      1. The drugs and drink - the various drugs of Heaven are the finest and most potent, sure. They've also got the most exotic. Blow which gives you temporary mutations? Hallucinogens which give you synchronized hallucinations? Clouds of smoke which form varicolored wonderlands in the air, complete with tiny cloud-protagonists acting out epic tales? All of that is a big check.

      2. Pattern Spider Wha? - Sidereals pretty much ignore Resplendent Destinies, but when everyone's drunk and high and having a great time, the guy with Pattern Spider Touch becomes the absolute life of the party. Oh man, nice, where did you get a juggling flying cat? Also, where's Ted?

      3. Dance Dance Revolution - I assume that at least one of the guests will be a Joybringer. Once they flare that anima, everyone in the room is a master dancer. Even better, have the DJ pop out an Easily Accepted Proposition to force synchronized and spontaneous musicals.

      4. The Wyldest Prank - The most useless Larceny Charm is Conning Chaos Technique. It's not fatal, it's expensive and the benefits are minimal. But what it can do is be an hilarious prank when causality starts cutting out around the target.

      5. Where Dreams Come To Life - Speaking of SMA, do any of the guests have Quicksilver Hand of Dreams style? If so, they could potentially be whipping out Raksha-like figments for entertainment purposes. Stupid dream animals? Animated pornographic murals? A thousand dancing teddy bears orbiting near the ceiling? Whatever you'd like!

      6. My Only Regret Is I have Clownitis - Did you know that the SMA about disease allows you to make your own supernatural diseases? Did you know that instead of having dangerous effects, they can just do stupid things like making your ears glow or force you to talk backwards for a bit? Shit, it doesn't matter. Try the wrong drink, kiss the wrong girl and suddenly you can only moonwalk.

      7. Oh My Gods - Read some of those spirit charms, man. Manipulating elements or domains are obviously there, that's easy stuff. But think about the visuals- one water spirit and all the fountains are sporting water sculptures. Clear dance floors in mid air? People frozen in time with silly expressions on their faces? Shapeshifting? It's all there.

      8. Danger - A Yu-Shan party can be as dangerous as the ST wants. You could play it up as a silly episode by filling it with exotic nonsense which vanishes the next day, sure. On the other hand, a more malicious (or interested) Joybringer can magically re-arrange emotions, loves and alignments with a touch. End up with the wrong dance partner and you're at risk of being someone's sex slave forever. Any one of those drugs could actually be a spiritual parasite which tries to replace your mind. Hell, if there's a powerful enough Sidereal there, they could try to replace your soul with friendly handshake. This could be a very, very dangerous social event.

      Good luck!
