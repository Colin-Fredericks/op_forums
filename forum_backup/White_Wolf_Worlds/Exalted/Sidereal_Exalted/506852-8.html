                                                        About the Out of Fate thing, done well, I think it's totally fine for Sidereals to be confounded by uncanny demons and outsiders who are blurry and confusing glimpses in the Sidereal foresight, and that to combat and work around mighty demons, Sidereals have to use old fashioned Sidereal cleverness, cunning and wisdom and their powers over that which is in Fate. So long as it's presented as a challenge they can do, working against this adversity builds their character, and it helps prevent the community feeling like the setting is stale because the Sidereals can predict everything, while not stopping the Sidereals actually be prophets.

                                                        What is actually the problem is the idea that "Lol blind prophets"*, where the Sidereals are sort of like... *unaware* of this factor, and the Bronze Faction are presumed to be sitting back looking pretty satisfied with themselves that they know everything and Creation is, if diminished, safe forevermore (while the Gold Faction are no better, just hyping themselves up for a glorious age of the return of the Solars, in their pocket, while being kind of blase to the threats outside of Fate out the door).

                                                        That the Sidereals cannot predict, so well, that which has no place in Fate should be a basic foundational part of the Sidereal knowledge, and they should be planning around this and mitigating it since Year Zero, like the intelligence service they are. It should be an acknowledged part of the debates that rage between them around the right path to take, and a nagging concern to those who chose the Path of Bronze. No "lol at the blind, blind prophets. so arrogant, yet so, so blind. fortunately the Solars! are! here! to save the day" that's just cheap heat.

                                                        (I'd also say the some Sidereals could maybe sometimes even be trying to exploit the way Out of Fate defies Fate to beat the Great Prophecy and bring some glory or a better life back to the world, although obviously that's not something they do on the regular, and do judiciously, as every time they invite something out of fate into the world, destiny gets a little more destabilised, and this is sort of the opposite of what they are for).

                                                        *well the "blind prophets" sidebar isn't *literally* "lol blind prophets" at least.

                                                            05-20-2015, 01:46 PM
                                                            Originally posted by Ghosthead View Post
                                                            where the Sidereals are sort of like... *unaware* of this factor, and the Bronze Faction are presumed to be sitting back looking pretty satisfied with themselves that they know everything and Creation is, if diminished, safe forevermore (while the Gold Faction are no better, just hyping themselves up for a glorious age of the return of the Solars, in their pocket, while being kind of blase to the threats outside of Fate out the door).
                                                            I think this depiction right here (or my impression that they're depicted this way) is why I hate the Sidereals, or at least the Bronze. They aren't honest about trying to destroy the world or make it into Hell, they're pointing at how horrifically atrocious the world is, how it's closer to apocalypse every single day, how many billions have died hideously for their plans, how many atrocities the Dragon-Blooded and Deathlords commit every week ... patting each other on the back for a Job Well Done. They claim to be saving the world but it's blindingly obvious that they're destroying it instead, and it makes their self-righteousness intolerable. The Gold faction are probably idiots too, but at least they don't like and approve of the horror show Creation has become.

                                                            They'd be harder to hate if both Factions, and the independents, were ALL willing to openly admit that Creation is not surviving well, that humanity is suffering unacceptable harm, that the Great Contagion and Balorian Crusades were bad things and might happen again -- and if they ALL admitted that they need to get their buts in gear to find real solutions. Just because the Bronze believe that Solars and Lunars aren't a viable solution should not mean they're so obnoxiously stupid and sociopathically callous they believe there are no problems happening at all, or that the Dragon-Blooded have never made mistakes or committed atrocities of their own.

                                                            The Gold Faction say "This is a problem, your Bronze Vision is a nightmare, we have a plan to make it better." and the canonical Bronze party line (before 5 years ago) seems to be "No, everything is fine, there are no problems and we should never try to improve anything. This mess is exactly what we wanted." Their response instead should be "No, our Vision was correct, these problems were caused by beings Outside of Fate. We have a better plan to fix these problems, as usual we're understaffed but we're working on it." and their plan should be presented as plausible and potentially sufficient. The independents should have their own suggestions that don't fit either faction's plans, or believe the Maidens have a plan that they need to obey. They Sidereals are cursed to be arrogant and self-righteous, but they should not watch Creation die screaming and respond with apathy or satisfaction. Their default should not be horrible callousness, and no Exalted should default to Chumps To Dumb To Live. The Bronze's plan shouldn't be, with OOC knowledge, unambiguously unviable.

                                                            Basically, the debates should not be "Is the post-Contagion status quo good, yes/no?" It should be "Who is to blame for this mess, and what should we do to fix it?"
