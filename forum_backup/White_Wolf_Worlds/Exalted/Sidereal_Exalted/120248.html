    Sidereal Charms Do Not Work That Way! (And other forum memes)

    04-27-2014, 05:15 AM
    Okay, so, I'm a little tired of certain memes getting brought up by the fandom, so I decided I'd make a thread where we separate what exists in the books from what exists in our own twisted psyches. The one I'd like to address right now is one that has resurfaced thanks to the new Ex3 comic*: Sidereal Charms are meant to work only in weird or indirect ways, and Sidereals work best when they plan.

    Sidereal Charms Work Indirectly/Weirdly

    No they don't- at least, not nearly to the degree that fans say they do. For the most part, Charms from previous editions have functioned exactly how they appear to function, and their uses are immediately obvious and applicable to whichever ability they fall under. Sidereal Melee Charms make you better at hitting people; Sidereal Dodge Charm help you evade harm; Sidereal Socialize Charms help you to manipulate people's Intimacies.

    The only difference between Sidereal Charms and other Exalted Charms is, usually, limitation in scope: Athletics Charms are mostly focused on turning you into the Juggernaut (bitch), Resistance Charms are most effective in turning you into a tank in combat (and less with resisting non-combat hazards like extreme temperatures or weather), and Occult Charms are most effective when dealing with gods and elementals (less so with raksha and demons). There are also some Charms that have effects outside their associated ability (like Defense of Shining Joy from Performance), but they share that property with many Dragon-Blooded Charms (such as the Performance Charms that aid with Stealth, and Lore Charms that are combat-focused).

    This remains true even of Sidereal Martial Arts. The disease effects of Citrine Poxes of Contagion are not all that different from the effects of Cold-Fire Desolation Brand and its upgrades, with similar combat applicability; the Scarlet Patterned Battlefield is simply a mass combat-focused MA; Charcoal March of Spiders is not that distinct from other flurry- and speed-heavy combat styles, with the distinction of the ever notable Pattern Spider Touch; and so on.

    My point is this: let's not pretend that Sidereal Charms are as weird as we like to think they are. Not only is it a false representation of how actual Sidereal Charms work, but it scares away potential readers who might be terrified by claims of oddity that, frankly, don't have much merit.

    Sidereals Work Best When They Plan Ahead

    I think this claim is based on the (largely theoretical) powers of Sidereal Astrology: namely, the ability to predict the future by reading the Loom of Fate, the ability to shape the future with Astrology, and the ability to prepare beforehand by "equipping" appropriate Resplendent Destinies. Of course, this has run into two problems: 1) the predictive ability of any divination power is always largely a matter of ST Fiat, and the mechanics for predictions through Astrology (or the various Lore and Investigation Charms related to fortune telling) are vague at best; and 2) the system for creating Destinies has been borked for quite some time, generally ranging from utterly broken to utterly useless with little in-beetween.

    The result is that, in practice, Sidereals wind up no more prepared than anyone else, since a) their predictive abilities are determined entirely by the ST's willingness to provide useful information and b) the only real "preparations" they could reasonably make are indistinguishable from the scene-long or indefinite Charm commitments that literally every other Essence user in the game has to make. If their mechanics were less broken, this meme might have some merit to it, but as it stands, Sidereals wind up just as surprised as anyone else would be of their relative power levels.

    * Which I quite enjoyed.

              I agree that Sidereal charms aren't quite as strange as internet exaggeration likes to present, but lets not kid ourselves here:

              - If your friend has a gash in their stomach after the last combat, the best ways to heal are via archery and (depending on the source) craft. Medicine can heal, but only defers the wounds to later in the future.

              - If you want to be able to socially deceive people, your go-to charm is currently in Larceny, and ability that isn't generally used for social combat.

              - There is a ride charm which do not require you to be riding anything to function, and has no altered effect if you are riding something.

              - There are charms which function purely as selective social attacks under resistance and survival.

              - There is no awareness charm that gives you a better chance of spotting an enemy hiding in the bushes, aside from letting you know that there might be something to look for nearby.


              Let's not hide this fact from them. If a new player is scared away from playing a Sidereal based on the fact that they sound weird, they will probably play something like a Solar that sounds less weird. If a new player decides to play a Sidereal doctor with a high medicine score because they want to be a healer, or takes a high larceny score because they want to play a thief, they're likely to come away with a bad impression of Exalted.

                  Having played a Sidereal for a while and written a bunch of custom Charms, this is what I have to say:

                  Sideways Charm use is a thing, though it's been blown way out of proportion. This is probably because of the whole "no custom Charms!" thing and that Sidereals for most of their existence in the game have had a more limited toolbox to draw from, encouraging more creative use. Their Charms do what they say they do, and most are very straightforward.

                  Weirdness comes in odd Charm placement in Abilities, though Dragon-Blooded have this too so it's not unique to Sidereals. Still, it's usually worse for Sidereals than DBs, as Sidereals have trees that have extremely few functions directly related to the Ability at hand (Ride, Bureaucracy, Craft), which DBs don't have if I recall correctly. Plus I think there's an expectation for what a Celestial should be able to do which is not there for Terrestrials. So the DBs have an excuse for certain capabilities being absent: they're Terrestrial, they're supposed to be weaker.

                  I'd say in reply to Skeptic Tank that you're only partially right. While there are oddball Charm placement, rarely do you go wrong just picking whatever Ability makes the most sense for the character. You may not end up with the same range of competencies as if you were playing a Solar, and you might be able to do things you wouldn't think you'd be able to, but you won't be bad at the stuff you picked. A player wanting to play a healer will do just fine taking Medicine, and they'll happen to be able to fuck up undead too. A player wanting to be a thief will do just fine taking Larceny, and will happen to be able to do some social stuff and mess with people's dice pools on the side. That's what's so fun about playing Sidereals, to me --- by virtue of the Abilities you decide on, you'll be able to pick up some capabilities that a more straightforward splat like Solars wouldn't get. My no-combat-Abilities Serenity has one of the best Dodge DVs of the group due to Defense of Shining Joy, and that's awesome. Anyway:

                  -Friend gets cut up? Medicine will ease the wounds, remove any infection, and instantly temporarily remove the wound itself. Yeah, if you're extremely good at Archery, or the source of the attack is outside of Creation or sorcery, then Archery/Craft can do better. Medicine is still a great investment for someone who wants to do medical stuff.

                  -Avoiding the Truth is great at deceiving people in the particular way of getting them to believe the opposite of what you said, which is often best used when not trying to deceive but just get people to believe something. To be actually good at pure deception the Fateful Socialize Excellency is probably your best bet. To be generally good at social stuff, the majority of it is in Linguistics, Performance, Presence, and Socialize. Avoiding the Truth, Icy Hand, and Many Missiles Arrow are basically the only oddball social Charms outside the scattering of spirit-related Charms that are across the entire Charmset.

                  -While a lot of Ride Charms are not related to Ride, they can definitely improve a mount (or not, of course; Ride is one of the weirder sets). Yellow Path, while it doesn't require riding, it very well may, and is related to travel, which Ride is generally about. Ride is one of the weirder ones, I'll happily admit.

                  -Social spirit Charms are in Resistance, Survival, Sail, Occult. Spirit effects are one of those motifs that Sidereals have, so you get social spirit Charms scattered everywhere. There are other social Charms in Drive and Stealth, but that's not too weird -- Solars have a social Charm in Stealth, War, Ride, and Sail.

                  -Prior Warning gives you a bonus success for noticing the danger that it told you about, which definitely would be an enemy in the bushes. Expected Pain negates whatever stealth they had when they attack. Supernal Vision lets you know the enemy is there, if you happen to be looking for "people hiding". Sidereal Awareness is very good at finding things, even if it doesn't have any ways to directly enhance senses.

                  Alright, that said, let's take a look at Dragon-Blooded:

                  -Combat defend-other charm in Linguistics.
                  -Attack Charms in Lore.
                  -What is really a Sail or archery-defense Charm in Occult.
                  -Sensory-boosting and movement-enhancement in Stealth.
                  -Soak defense in Thrown.
                  -Put people to sleep, screw with their motes, or literally kill people with Awareness.
                  -A movement effect in Craft.
                  -Strength enhancement in Resistance.
                  -Damage booster in Athletics (though Solars have one too).
                  -Boost soak and health, or make attacks, with Presence.
                  -Instantly form mass combat units with Socialize.
                  -Temporarily seize control of a chosen vessel's mind and body when you die with Bureaucracy.
                  -Lots of sensory effects in Larceny. Really if you want sense-boosting there's more in Larceny than Awareness.
                  -Sickness attack Charm in Medicine (not too far off base, but still an attack booster), along with changing your anima or protecting yourself against disease or toxin (which would normally be Resistance.)
                  -Stealth and combat Charms in Performance.
                  -Medicine effects in Survival.

                  Wow that's a lot of weird placement. Might even be more than Sidereals have.

                  Now that I think about it, I feel that the reason DBs are not felt to be as weird as Sidereals is because it's easier to see and understand elemental associations than it is the thematic associations that define the Sidereal Charmset. Strip away the thematic reasoning, and it looks to me like Dragon-Blooded have more oddball Charm placements than Sidereals by far.

                      Some of their Charms do reward careful thinking about how exactly they can be used, like Easily Accepted Proposition Stance and Avoiding the Truth Technique, some don't as much.

                      The idea that they're the Google interview question style "You're butt naked with a rubber chicken. How do you save the world?" Exalted seems a bit overcooked.

                      It seems to me their magic is of the form where each Ability has a fairly clear theme, and where each their Charms generally have clear uses that almost never contradict the Ability but also have other "Eureka!" uses if you think about them more carefully. Not really "What the heck are all these random Charms that are tenuously connected to this Ability?". (When I say contradict, I mean that Sidereal Charms are almost never of the sort where the Charm actually pushes the character away from the concept of "Ability X" master, but they often do not specifically enhance that character at that Ability.)

                      I get the impression that Sidereal Charms are meant to make the average player feel like they're thinking like a Sidereal, heroes who face limitations in their powers and work with restrictions (like Fate) and overcome them through cleverness, efficiency, brilliant insights and deftness, but they're not like the Advanced Super Puzzle Exalted. Is that like the play experience?

                      Sidereals working best when they plan ahead is supposed to come through via their Charms requiring some forethought and planning to use to their maximum strength, which is a much more elegant way of doing it than putting in a "plans" mechanic.

                                                          Oh yes, one more irritating meme I'd like to address:

                                                          Infernals Are Transhuman

                                                          This is mostly a case of "You Keep Using That Word," since it's about the least useful and most off-putting way of describing how Infernals work without using the phrase "mandatory rape." Yes, Infernals are transformed by their Charms, in ways that other Exalted are not- hell, they Exalt by emerging from a Chrysalis. If the metamorphosis symbolism were any more obvious, Infernals would all come out with butterfly wings and be greeted by Ovid himself.

                                                          The issue is that the word "transhuman" has a lot of real world baggage that comes with it- transhumanism is a real philosophical movement with real adherents and detractors, which has nothing to do with what the Infernals are doing. And unlike the use of "demon" or "god" in the context of Exalted, it's not a deliberate invocation of that real world philosophy, it's just fans being lazy and jumping on a meme because they forget that newbies will have exactly zero context for what they're talking about. So let's just kill this meme dead in the water and figure out a better way to talk about our favorite proto-Primordials, shall we?
