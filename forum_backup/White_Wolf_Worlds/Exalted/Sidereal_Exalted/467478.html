                                  Originally posted by falco1029 View Post
                                  One thing I'm iffy about though is that they might have some limit on evocations? Still, looking forward to learning more, as a lot's unsaid so far.
                                  My read on it from a gameplay perspective is that it might actually work out better for them. The EX3 Team has mentioned keeping an eye on synergy and interactions between different parts of the system a lot more than in the past for the new edition. Through their native Charms and to the end of a unique play space, Sidereals can get at odd capabilities that nobody else really has, while also not possessing some things other splats take for granted. Part of the reasoning for this is that having those together in one Charmset could be unbalancing. The same idea could carry over into Evocations: combining some of the powers Evocations can grant with some of their more unconventional Charms could create some imbalance, meaning they'd either need to lack that Evocation access or lack their more unique capacities. If it came down to that decision, I know I'd take "keep their native unique capacities" every time.

                                  I'd also say that they're only missing out on Evocations if having low capacity with them is framed as a net loss. As Coik notes above, SMA now being in range for an average game probably makes up for it, anyway. I mean, at the Essence ratings that the vast majority of SMAs needed to master them? They were basically an option that Sidereals technically had, but were very unlikely to get to realize. Even aside from the copious amounts of broken all over most SMAs. So they're moving from 2e's "would mostly not really be able to get SMA, for reasons of stats or ST fiat" to EX3's "will be able to get SMA, something in the way of Evocations, and probably a more expansive Charmset." At least at this point, I think they've got nothing but gains.
