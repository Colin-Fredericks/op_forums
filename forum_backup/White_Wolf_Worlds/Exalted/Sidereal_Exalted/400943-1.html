            HUNDRED EYES, Chosen of Serenity

            Among his people, it was said that one was born blind because they had seen too much profound beauty in their past life, so this was Heaven's way of balancing out those scales. Hundred Eyes always meant to ask someone who'd know if that was true, but never got around to it.

            Hundred Eyes grew up in a small Threshold village, where he learned the various tricks those born blind must learn to make it through the day. It surprised no one that the boy had a keen ear, as well as nimble fingers and a strong voice, and so he was instructed in making music through song and instrument, so that he could support himself someday.

            A bright, pleasant sort, the young man was always popular, except with a certain group of teen bullies who resented all the attention he got from the young women of the village. One day they lost their patience and attempted to give him a thrashing. Though blind, he was mildly surprised to discover that he could "see", or perhaps feel, the pattern that their blows made in the world around him; with this foresight, he was easily able to trip and stumble about in such a way that made them take themselves out, in such an embarrassing manner, that they didn't come back.

            Exalting is hard on every young Sidereal, as the Arcane Fate falls over their presence like a shroud, but how much harder was it for the blind? Even his newfound wonder at exploring the web of fate all about him, enabling him to move about much easier than before, was muted by his grief at his lifelong companions forgetting and ignoring him. Fortunately, he was soon found and taken to Yu-Shan, where he would meet the true companions of his life's journey...

            That was 600 years ago. Hundred Eyes soon earned the name which he now bears, and is somewhat famous among his peers. A blind Chosen is rare enough, but for him to have compensated as well as he has, enough to match and even exceed many of his fellows' feats, pleasantly surprised even the normally jaded Fellowship! In the hundreds of years since graduating from his apprenticeship, he has gained a reputation as one of the best (and harshest) instructors of the Viziers, whether for occult studies, sorcery or the martial arts. Despite the legends of his creatively stern lessons and mystifying koans, he is in constant demand, and is especially valued for his ability to train "problem students".

            An exquisitely well-mannered, dignified presence, Hundred Eyes keeps his own feelings and opinions largely to himself, but much can be inferred from the way he has always staunchly avoided the activities of either the Gold or Silver Factions. Similarly, he avoids identification with Nazri's aims, simply doing his job, whether in the Bureau offices or when dispatched to Creation, and spending his private time strolling about the avenues and arcades of Heaven, chatting with Gods, playing gateway with friends, and sampling the divine fruit and drinks from stalls.

            Hundred Eyes still likes to get out among humanity, however, and occasionally can be found in a Resplendent Destiny as an old blind beggar, strumming complex harmonies from his strings or reciting poetic epics for a few coins. It was on such a day that he made the acquaintance of a passing Lawgiver, who paid well to request a very rare ancient song. Was it curiosity--or Fate--that moved Hundred Eyes to subtly follow this one, increasingly certain that he had a role to play in the young Solar's tale?

            Notes:

            Hundred Eyes has a Reputation of "Excellent Teacher" among Sidereals, which is enhanced with World Shaping Artistic Vision. In addition to being one of the few beings to master all four Argument of Virtue Styles, he is also a master of Crane and Throne Shadow Style Martial Arts, both of which he uses extensively in training his students; he has also mastered various general MA Charms such as Secret Lesson Revelation that aid in this.

            Being blind, Hundred Eyes has the Diminished Sense (Blind) Flaw, as well as the Acute Sense (Hearing) Merit; he also enjoys the benefits of the Charm Supernal Awareness as a Permanent Effect. It is possible that some God could grant him eyesight with an Endowment, but he has always refused any suggestion of this. If he really needs to actually see something, he can do so through the eyes of his Familiars, represented by a melodious flock of pretty songbirds, that constantly hover near him, chirping merrily. At the sign of a threat, however, they can become an adamant sword-wielding squad of feathered celestial maidens, all well-schooled in Even Blade Style. They are also excellent singers and dancers, and put on quite a show, especially when enhanced by their master's Anima banner.

            Hundred Eyes teaches self-reliance, and so has few Artifacts, beyond his Silken Robes; one of these few is a very rare Singing Staff, that he normally simply plays as a musical instrument (although he backs up its earth-shaping powers with his Anima when he needs to), but can also use with many of his MA Charms, thanks to Master's Hand Envisioned Anew. He has Resplendent Destinies in The Gull, The Banner, The Guardians and The Treasure Trove.

            (Eh, I may have missed the Friday deadline on this, but I had the idea, then got distracted when writing it down, and wasn't about to wait till next week, so there you are! And, yes, I stole the name, and some of the concept, from the Marco Polo Netflix series; I really tried to come up with a different name, but I just couldn't think of a better one!)

                            This week, we'll be taking a look at Sidereals inhabiting the Shards of the Exalted Dream...primarily the Modern Age, but anything's possible!

                            MADISON SHERMAN, THE GENERAL
                            Madison Sherman was born into a family with a long history of military careers. An army brat growing up on a succession of overseas bases, she learned how to salute before tying her shoes, and could field-strip a rifle better than she could make-up her face. No one was surprised when she enlisted, and her major ambition was to surpass her father's rank of Colonel.

                            She would have succeeded, too, if she hadn't exalted on the field of battle. Arcane Fate prevented her from pursuing her career any further, but her true destiny had found her. Joining with her fellow Viziers, she was instrumental in deciding many large-scale international conflicts over the centuries, always with a favored eye for her beloved Meruvia. Her main regret was never being able to attain the rank of General in the mortal army whose destinies she helped to weave.

                            Recently, after several secret conversations with Chejop Kejak, she has come to resent the malevolent intentions of her Infernal masters and has joined his conspiracy to find and train the resurgent Solars to overthrow them. Rather than hide this attempt completely, they have instead disguised it to enlist Infernal aid, until it is too late for them to do anything about it.

                            The result is Project: Illumination's Gold Force, a highly-trained special mission team which ostensibly exists to further Meruvia's military interests throughout the world. In reality, it is secretly establishing world-wide contacts and covert allies for a series of subtle and overt strikes against the Warlock conspiracy. Already its membership, consisting of Elite Heroic Mortals, God-Blooded, and even Spirits and Exalted of all kinds, grows larger and more effective (so far, they amount to almost a Scale, plus support staff, which counts as Acquaintances 4). Soon, they will publicly debut, and eventually, they will move under General Sherman's direction against their true targets!

                            Notes: Madison is an established Chosen of Battles, with a well-developed suite of combat powers, focused on War. She has rigorously trained and enhanced her troops with her Charms and Astrology, and they are all capable of performing the various Battle Patterns. Madison has also coordinated the different training methods of the Exalts under her to maximum effect, has claimed the ranking officers as Shadow Fingers, and is a near-master of Scarlet-Patterned Battlefield Style Martial Arts.

                            Madison normally appears to her command as General Mospid, a Resplendent Destiny of The Banner. She has others, mostly in the House of Battles, which she mainly uses to walk among her troops and learn their true feelings and opinions. Although she is prepared to lead them into battle herself, she recognizes that her place as a high-ranking officer is behind the lines, and so she works closely with Michael Knight, the Dawn she has chosen to lead Gold Force as field leader. Sgt. Knight is an exceptional combatant and commander, even for the Dawn Caste, and is even able to somehow see through Madison's disguises. His one weakness is that he can't deny the strong feelings he's begun to have for his C.O. She is aware of this, but is unsure of how to deal with it...or even if she wants to.

                                BENNY, THE LAST-MINUTE DRIVER


                                Where he came from, no one knows. They just know he's there when they need him, pulling up in a battered old taxi cab just when the shit hits the fan or when they learn where they need to go but aren't sure how to get there.

                                Benny is a classic stereotype of a cabbie, who'll happily tell you he's been all over and seen it all. He regales his impromptu passengers with tall tales, bad jokes, wry observations and insights that seem superficial, but actually are deep when you ponder them later.

                                Curiously, those who recall enough of their driver to inquire about him afterwards find that their research turns up no sign of the Saffron Cab Co. Benny can turn up in any country, at any time of day or night, and can communicate flawlessly with all tongues, although always with a rough accent that's hard to place. His appearance is as slovenly as his taxi is messy, full of knick-knacks from everywhere and makeshift wards against everything, but both somehow give an impression of solid reliability.

                                Tired of ferrying his Infernal masters about (a duty he still must perform, alas), Benny now lets Destiny steer him to his customers and their destinations. He's confident that Fate is unfolding as it should, if you just trust in it...and occasionally give it the right nudge. That reminds him of a story about this young girl...

                                Notes: Benny is a Harbinger who is coming around to the Gold Faction's view of things. He's seen the mess the Warlocks are making of the world, and believes that something must be done, and he's going to help those who will do it get to where they need to be to do so. He has the full complement of Sidereal Drive Charms, and also knows many Terrestrial and Celestial spells that facilitate travel, although he'll only use those in emergencies. While he prefers to use his trusty cab, he has been known to occasionally show up in some other transport, ranging from horse-drawn coaches to dog-sleds; in these cases, he uses Ride Charms instead.

                                Benny prefers to avoid combat (or drive away from it at breakneck speed, to be more precise), but if pressed, he will show off his mastery of Crystal Chameleon Style Martial Arts. He's usually hidden in a Resplendent Destiny when he picks up his fares, and sometimes passes Astrological Blessings onto them by way of a cheery handshake when he drops them off.

                                Benny's cab is a Artifact car, mostly comprised of Jade (with a tiny Starmetal figurine of the Maiden of Journeys atop the hood), that runs very fast and long with little need for fuel. Instead, it runs on pure quintessence (tho ambrosia is even better), which he gets from his spirit connections. The car is also carries an Elemental Empowerment of Water spell, enabling it to alter its appearance, so that it always seems at least somewhat native to the lands he drives in. His two-way radio is also a prayer transceiver module, that sometimes relates directions to Very Important Customers in a crisp, efficient female voice (Benny has always been evasive about who he takes these directions from; some suspect it is Mercury herself, or a Goddess working directly with the Loom of Fate, such as Asna Firstborn). He rarely takes payment, preferring to instead take some random keepsake from a passenger, and often gives them some parting gift that eventually proves very useful, if not lifesaving.

                                (Benny's cab has the company's motto on the side. I was going to choose one of the final lines from a Journeys sutra, but don't have my books here with me. Any suggestions?)

                                        THE MAN IN GREEN


                                        Agent 33 was always fascinated by the mysterious and the occult, and grew to become a journalist dedicated to discovering the truth of the secret world he could feel all around. He might have had a promising career, but instead he spent his time chasing leads for underground, fringe newspapers and journals. All of this changed the night he secured conclusive proof of the existence of a Lunar of urban legend...then decided that, due to the circumstances, people were better off not knowing about it, and destroyed it. Exalting in that moment, he was soon brought into the fold of the Fivescore Fellowship.

                                        In the many years since, he has gained a reputation for great skill in managing the public's awareness of the world around them, save for one significant incident, where he blew the cover-up of the public assassination of the 35th Empress, allowing the conspiracy to be exposed to the world. As it happened, the resulting loss in faith of the government and increasing social unrest suited his Infernal masters fine, but as he had pledged his name to prevent this from occurring, it was forfeit. Agent 33 has refused assignment of a new name to this day, using his namelessness as a constant reminder of his failure.

                                        Still a relatively young Vizier, Agent 33 still gets much field work dumped on him, most of which consists of covering up supernatural activities to conform to the Infernal narrative of the world. Although he remains strictly professional, showing up on the scene of Lunar sightings, Raksha abductions and Alchemical escapes to talk to witnesses in his well-tailored suit of many shades of green, he must admit that he enjoys leading those who have divined some of the truth into a labyrinth of lies, misdirection, and half-truths.

                                        If there's a sour note in his life, it is his formerly close relationship with his Mentor, Chejop Kejak. He knows the older Seer well enough to know that something troubles him deeply, and that he is keeping it from his former pupil. He suspects he knows what it is, as he can't help but notice odd movements, in both temporal resources and Fate. He isn't yet sure how he will react when he finally discovers what is truly going on...

                                        Notes: Agent 33 is a fairly well-established Oracle of good standing, as seen by his high Salary, Connections, Reputation and Mentor ratings. He has a large support staff of gods and demons who carry out his orders, and do any necessary fighting for him. Still, he is far from helpless, having mastered the Art of Meditative Discussion and Border of Kaleidoscopic Logic Style Martial Arts (the latter up to the Form Charm), which he supplements with Discreet Essence Armor and Gloves of Martial Readiness (green, of course).

                                        The Man in Green uses many of Artifacts, as needed, including Recorders of Everlasting Glory and Dragonfly's Ranging Eyes (which take the form of green-tinted sunglasses), and also uses spells to advance his goals, such as Theft of Memory. He has an extensive collection of gems full of stolen memories of evidence that should never see the light of day. He rarely uses Resplendent Destinies, as the entire point of him is NOT to be remembered, but he does find The Mask sometimes useful; he gets around in eerily-silent, unmarked dark green limos and helicopters.

                                            STARDUST MOONDREAM, and The Serenity Express

                                            For one hundred years, Meruvian culture experienced "The Century of Love", a time of cultural renewal and a rebirth of the arts and pacific values (at least, this is how contemporary pop culture remembers it; there was certainly many serious social problems at that time, many of which still persist). A few well-timed wars, assassinations of popular leaders and exposures of government scandals closed out that century, plunging Meruvian society into a new culture of cynical pragmatism, opportunism and despair...which is exactly what its hidden masters wanted.

                                            While most who actually lived in that time are long since moved on to new incarnations, a few Exalted who were shaped by those days have resolved to never lower their freak flag. Foremost among this is the Joybringer called Stardust Moondream, instantly recognizable with his long beard, garishly colored clothing, and accompanying cloud of hashish smoke.

                                            Stardust wanders aimlessly across Meruvia in a large party bus called The Serenity Express, brightly painted and with lines from various Sutras written all over it. He visits various communes, publishes underground newsletters (the Green Power Civil Rights Movement is a particular passion of his), unites people and groups that Fate wants together, and generally spreads the good word about Peace and Love. The Serenity Express is usually crowded with various strange characters, from Mortals to Spirits to God-Blooded (many of whom are conceived aboard it), all singing, smoking and partying under the pyschedelic azure glow of Stardust's anima banner.

                                            Needless to say, Moondream is NOT happy about how the Infernals have twisted the world, much less how his beloved Century of Love was merely a calculated experiment to *increase* the misery of the masses for the Yozis to feed on by relieving it for a set time only to pile it all back on later. Although he pursues the path of the pacifistic warrior, he realizes that more harsh measures must be taken, and he constantly has his eye open for the heroes able to do so as his bus rides through the land...

                                            Notes: Stardust Moondream is an Elder Joybringer, but also a Ronin who has disassociated himself with his fellows, and so has few resources to offer beyond himself and his books, dreamstones and memory-crystals. That is still considerable, as he is a Sifu of great accomplishment, supplementing his teachings with both Throne Shadow and Crane Styles. His mastery of Orgiastic Fugitive is perhaps a given (the Form is esp useful when driving while baked out of his mind), and he is often seen practicing the languid, flowing katas of Victorious Concession Style atop the Serenity Express. He is fond of the grappling/disarming techniques of Mantis Style and Sapphire Veil of Passions, preferring such non-violent methods of combat, but can teach any Style needed, as well as sorcery.

                                            Stardust uses Sidereal Drive Charms to make sure the Serenity Express is where it needs to be, and gets where its picked up passengers need to go. He is also quite good at Alchemy, and is constantly inventing new drugs that he may recklessly ask visitors to test; if accepted, these often give hallucinatory vision-quests that seem to tap into the Loom of Fate, and may give insight into one's larger journey.

                                            The Serenity Express makes a semi-regular circuit around a country-wide network of communes and other locations of fringe society, keeping Stardusts' Connections alive (he has maxed out his Acquaintances to do so). He travels in a Resplendent Destiny of The Musician, which bears most of his Reputation. Skyflower is his Spirit-Familiar, a pure-white rabbit that can transform into a cute young bunnywoman; she is far more practical than he is, cooly handling many of his business affairs and ruthlessly defending him with her mastery of Swaying Grass Style (which he often provides the music for on his sitar, as well as more direct aid via Silver-Voiced Nightingale).

                                                ANN IVORY, the Drifter

                                                Anne was a quiet child, who disturbed the adults around her with her morbid interests and humor. She likes to imagine that they breathed a small sigh of relief when she ended her stay with them (if they even remember her at all), by running away from their comfortable suburban home as soon as she was old enough to conceivably take care of herself.

                                                She has learned many hard lessons about life on the streets since then, but a combination of her uncanny sense for avoiding trouble and simple common sense kept her well. Now in her late teens, dressed in her distinctive Gypsy-Goth style, Anne drifts easily from place to place, from life to life, following Fate and learning what she can while affecting those she meets as best as she can. Her unusual maturity, unflappable good cheer, and humor help her to pass herself off as older than she is, and she adopts plausible identities with an ease that often surprises her. An exceptional lucky streak (and the fact that few seem to remember her after she's left them) enables her to make enough to live by without having to bother with the complex infrastructure of modern society. She has already met many odd creatures and beings lurking in the shadows on the fringe of society (including at least one Abyssal and Lunar) and has been involved in many strange incidents...

                                                Anne is a voracious reader, and was quite surprised to find that the New Age books she read on channeling one's Inner Chi to alter the world around one actually seem to work. She has also taught herself some basic self-defense, which she seems to have a knack for, as the few times she had to physically defend herself, her improvised moves were far more effective than she expected. Now if only she could understand the meaning behind those strange dreams of other lives...

                                                Notes: Anne Ivory is a neophyte Reckoner, and an unfound Ronin. She has few Backgrounds, excepting some Acquaintances and Familiars (a cat and a raven, which haven't been enhanced into spirits yet). She has just discovered Old Realm, which is a Dead Language in this setting, and is attempting to learn it from books she's found. Since some charms require mastery of that tongue, she can't learn them until she does. Anne doesn't know it yet, but she is well on her way to initiating into Sorcery, requiring only a couple of stations until she makes her Sacrifice for the Terrestrial Circle...although actually learning any spells will be a different challenge all together. Anne has no current ability to work Astrology, but her innate connection to Fate expresses itself with the Lucky Merit. She carries a single Artifact, a Starmetal figure of Saturn's sign on a silver chain; she does not know what it does, or even how she got it, but she has a sense of its importance and never takes it off.

                                                    LORN, THE YOUNG-OLD MASTER


                                                    Exaltation can strike at any time in one's life, although it most typically alights on the young and fit. Sidereals, especially, awaken to the Chosen state they were born to sometime in youth, but occasionally outliers happen. None moreso than with Lorn, who exalted later in life than any other Sidereal.

                                                    Born to a militaristic kingdom in the East, Lorn survived life as a soldier, and when he got too old for that, he settled down to a well-paid position in the military bureaucracy. There, he learned the true power of pen and paperwork, and eventually amassed more actual power than all the generals who led the armies. Lorn finally exalted well into his 60s when he used his influence to subtly alter his nations' war-making, from a nation which his Prince wanted to attack for personal reasons, to another who actually posed a serious threat.

                                                    Lorn had always expected to eventually lose his wits along with his ailing health, so he was surprised to find that HE was the one forgotten by all his subordinates as the Arcane Fate settled over him. When the Viziers came calling (visibly surprised by the old man they were to collect), he bade farewell to his old life, and embraced his new calling.

                                                    Exalting so late in life has given Lorn many advantages: While all Chosen are astonished by their enhanced capacities, few of them truly appreciate having their infirm bodies and minds re-invigorated by the rush of divine Essence like the elderly! Also, Lorn is quick to exploit those who know of the Exalted, but base their impressions on appearances, and therefore assume that he must be far older and more powerful than the "youngsters" alongside him (his actual elders, who are endlessly amused by all this).

                                                    But perhaps his greatest advantage is simply that he has truly lived a full, long mortal life, which gives him certain insights into humanity, Creation and Life that elude even the most ancient of Chosen. His fellows can sense this, and are drawn to him because of it, endlessly fascinated by his straight-forward homilies and tales.

                                                    Despite his skill at working bureaucracies, the ever-pragmatic Lorn has trouble wrapping his head around the esoteric wisdom the Fellowship runs on, and secretly fears that the others look down on him because of this. In fact, it is the complete opposite. Because of this fear, he has driven himself to master combat, frequently volunteers for field duty in dangerous areas, and has saved many of his fellow's lives many times. Because of this and other reasons, Lorn is becoming far more popular in the Fellowship than he has yet realized, has disproportionate pull in the Convention of War, and may well be a powerful voice in debates to come...

                                                    Lorn is a Shieldbearer who has had 5-10 years of experience on the job. A lot of this goes into War (his Supernal Ability), Presence and Bureaucracy, which he has natural aptitudes for, but he has also concentrated on being as useful in combat as he can, and his focus on Melee has made him into one of the best young swordsmen among the Fellowship.

                                                    Lorn has also proven able to extend his mastery of the sword into the martial arts, and has mastered styles that take advantage of the blade. His favorite is Steel Devil, which he uses with a pair of Starmetal Daiklaves, forged from the smelted remains of two Deiphages he captured in Heaven's slums, their hilts joined by a chain of Black Jade. When even their Evocations fail him against powerful foes, he then activates Perfection of the Visionary Warrior, and shows why the Fellowship calls him the Iron Sword Hurricane!

                                                    Lorn rarely ends up on peaceful missions to Creation, but it does occasionally happen. While most of his fellows use Resplendent Destinies to appear as harmless elderly people, he doesn't bother, simply appearing as the genial old man he really seems to be, although he will sometimes use The Spear to appear to be a young man if the need arises. If he has need of a weapon where bringing Daiklaves is inconvenient, then he casts the Spirit Sword spell, and uses his martial arts charms through it.

                                                    Lorn's anima banner is a steady streaming of red light, which sets those it affects on edge, but with the calm alertness of soldiers about to engage the enemy. He has a pet Omen Dog that he has just made into a Familiar, but has yet to alter it with his Charms. His Limit Trigger is becoming aware of the deaths of people who trusted his guidance, to their doom.

                                                    (Lorn is extrapolated from the comedic image of a Sid who exalted late in life, and who was actually the junior of the much younger-looking Viziers around him, much to some mortals' confusion. The "badass swordsman" parts are cannibalized from another NPC idea, a barbarian warrior who becomes a Sid, but just doesn't *get* all this abstract occult weirdness, and makes himself into the best asskicker he can to compensate...which ends up getting him all the respect he thinks he's missing!
