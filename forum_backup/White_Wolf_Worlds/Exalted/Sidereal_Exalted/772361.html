                      If you're the adorable pink haired one in the backdrop wielding the teacup, I think I might have an idea to go with that style ? It takes a little mixing and matching but can be thematically quite fun.

                      Firstly, start with Optimistic Security Practice. Combine that with early Compassion style (Art of Victorious Concession) from Imperfect Lotus and you heroically stand in for your dear friends, softening the force of their cruel intentions.                           Originally posted by Jen View Post
                          Can you create destiny while not having access to the Loom of Fate ? Since my Sidereal is a special snowflake who never interact with Heaven.........
                          There's two things at work here. Creating a capital-D Destiny [as per the background] requires the Loom of Fate. A lower-case d destiny, aka an astrological blessing or curse, is a smaller, shorter-lived enhancement, and only requires contact with the beneficiary when complete; a Destiny lasts until complete, an astrological blessing or curse lasts for as long as the successes dedicated to duration [as opposed to effect and scope] state.
