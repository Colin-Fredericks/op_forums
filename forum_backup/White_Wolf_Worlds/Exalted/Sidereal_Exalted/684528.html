    Hot Button Political Issues for Sidereals

    10-01-2015, 12:23 PM
    (This is a spin off thread from the There are only 100 sidereals...so how are there so many factions? topic.)

    The Great Prophecy is over a thousand years old and the majority of Sidereals were not even alive when it was cast. With the return of the Solars happening only in the last year, there must have been other Political Issues for Sidereals to argue about over the past thousand years. Right now, there is the perception that Sidereal political beliefs are basically "Do you like Solars? Yes or No."

    I would like to start brain storming other Political Issues to for Sidereals to be divided over. There can still be a Gold and a Bronze Faction, but like Political Parties in the US, the Faction evolve with leadership supplying direction on key issues. Of course that doesn't mean that every Sidereal in the faction agrees with Faction leadership. I think having a diverse set of issues would lead to more back room dealings between Factions and less "Kill Sidereals that don't agree with me."

    Here is a start of my list. Feel Free to add your own.

    1. Do you support the Scarlet Empress and the Realm?
    2. Do you believe that all "Lost Eggs" should be recruited by the Realm?
    3. Should other non-Realm groups of Dragonblooded, such as the Seventh Legion, be allowed to exist?
    3. How much should Dragonblooded know about First Age History?
    4. Should Dangerous First Age Artifacts be allowed to Dragonblooded?
    5. Should Sidereals concern themselves with raising the standard of living in Creation?
    6. Should effort be made to maintain failing First Age Infrastructure in the Threshold?

              Hmm. I quibble somewhat with this list, W&A.

              One is going to be a salient issue for a lot of Sidereals, although prior to the return of the Solars it will likely be more a theoretical exercise than anything.

              Two is something that I don't think even the Bronze Faction is going to care about. What is it to the Sidereals if random Outcaste Dragon-Blooded joins up with the Realm or not? I mean... why would they care enough one way or the other to devote resources to it, and even if you don't like the Realm, do you not like it enough to track down random outcastes and try to ensure they don't go to the Realm?

              Three is tricky. Lookshy is actually protected by the Maidens themselves (Mars specifically, I think) from too much meddling. There really aren't significant non-Realm organizations of the Dragon-Blooded besides them. It's like... ooo, the Forest Witches. If they end up on the Sidereals radar it will be for official reasons, not for unofficial faction business.

              Other three is going to be resolutely answered "the official Immaculate version of history of the First Age, the one we wrote, is what they should know." Even if you disagree with that line, how are you going to undermine the massive edifice of seven hundred years of propaganda, supported by both powerful faction elders and the Scarlet Empress.

              Four is going to be something that the Scarlet Empress, who operates the most potent First Age Artifact of them all, has an opinion on. If you're Bronze, you probably give that opinion great weight. If you're not, the answer is likely "none of this stuff can be trusted to the Dragon-Blooded or the Lunars, it should be in our hands."

              Hell, even if you're Bronze, you probably share that opinion, you just don't share it enough to outright steal shit from the Realm without treading lightly.

              Five, Sidereals definitely concern themselves with the standard of living in Creation, but they have so many other things to do that it may just be something they don't gots time for. Theoretically, the other Exalted are meant to worry about things like that.

              Six is going to be dependent on how much that infrastructure ties into their jobs. They probably don't give a shit about rich people in Chiaroscuro having magical streetlights. They probably give a lot of shits about making sure the Lap doesn't catastrophically fail one day and ignite half the dragon lines across the whole direction.

              Really, a lot of these are issues the Sidereals are not going to consider huge priorities prior to the return of the Solars, even people who are heavily into faction stuff. I have trouble seeing a lot of it causing huge divides on its own.

                      I feel like Sidereals should argue (or at least debate and discuss) the meanings of the Scriptures of the Maidens more actively. I mean, these are (presumably) the divine words of their patron goddesses! There should be groups of Journeys who try to figure out what is meant by "I have no use for beginnings" in the Scripture of the Eternal Maiden, and cliques who try to discern the meaning of the incredibly arcane Scripture of Absence.

                      Remember that Sidereals aren't just overworked Heavenly bureaucrat fate ninjas - they're also Chosen by the Maidens, and anything the Maidens say should be treated with reverence, or at least a deep respect. The Maidens aren't just the Sidereals' superiors, they're their goddesses, and I think that gets forgotten a lot because the Sidereals work with other gods and goddesses. I still hold, however, that the Sidereals are probably amongst the more devout of the Exalt types, by simple virtue of proximity - the Maidens are just close enough to impress you with their divine nature and overpowering presence, while remaining distant and enigmatic enough that you can still revere them easily.

                                                      One thing that occurred to me recently is the Bronze Faction has much less reason to invest so heavily in the Realm as it did in past editions. In 1E/2E, the Thousand Dooms gave Kejak and Co. plenty of good reasons to back a ruthless, powerful single actor to control most of Creation. When we lessen those threats, there is much less reason to insist that the Realm is the only horse worth backing.

                                                      So, I can imagine that the Bronze faction debated cultivating other options. Some would want to support Lookshy more surely. Some would want to try spreading the blood of the Dragons wider amongst Creation. Some could more easily take issue with the atrocities of the Realm, and argue for withdrawing support. I can even see some of the Bronzies arguing for attempting contact with the Lunars, and offering to help them build kingdoms on the border of Creation, to keep out the fae.

                                                      When Creation was under constant threat of being destroyed, it made sense for Kejak to humble himself and play along with Scarlet.
