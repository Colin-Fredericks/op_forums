        Ok. So I'm back with a small update today. I'm trying to give full abilities where I can, but I've been jumping around in the writing too much over the last few days to really be able to do that.
        Investigation
        Marvellous Inclusion of Details
        Cost: 3m; Mins: Investigation 3, Essence 1; Type: Supplemental
        Keywords: None
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisite Charms: Auspicious Prospects for (Caste)
        The Sidereal gets +1 non-charm success; maximum (Essence); for every previous case/profile action that the Sidereal has made in trying to solve one case/mystery.

        Efficient Secretary Technique
        Cost: 2m; Mins: Investigation 2, Essence 1; Type: Simple
        Keywords: None
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisite Charms: None
        Call up an emerald spider that retrieves any objective fact or piece of information that is publicly available and known.

        Research Assistant Invocation
        Cost: 5m; Mins: Investigation 3, Essence 2; Type: Simple
        Keywords: None
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisite Charms: Efficient Secretary Technique
        The Sidereal turns a nearby plant into an assistant, who is focused on investigation and research. The new assistant has the stats of a two-dot Retainer and speaks the same language as the Sidereal. The transformation lasts for 5 days, before the assistant turns back into a plant.

        Embracing Life Method
        Cost: 10m, 1wp, 1lhl; Mins: Investigation 5, Essence 3; Type: Simple
        Keywords: Prayer Strip
        Duration: Five days
        Prerequisite Charms: Marvellous Inclusion of Details, Research Assistant Invocation
        The sidereal buries the prayer strip and rolls (Charisma + Investigation). The successes rolled are the number of pieces of information the Sidereal gets about solving a quandary posed when the prayer strip is buried. The Sidereal may end the charm early, for every day less than the duration the roll loses 1 success.
        As you can see this set is missing Auspicious Prospects for (Caste) that is due to me struggling with whether to just bring it straight over with no changes or not. I was considering making it more like Watchman's Infallible Eye for the five astrological houses, but thought that would be too broad for a single charm. Would appreciate some suggestions if anyone has any.

                                    Pho Cart Methodology*
                                    Cost: 5 Motes, 1 WP; Mins: Bureaucracy 3, Essence 2; Type: Simple
                                    Keywords: None
                                    Duration: Seven Days
                                    Prerequisite Charms: None
                                    The Sidereal can set up a small established business in a town. This Business will neither make or lose money. Residents will patron the business as if it was always there. At the end of the charm's duration, the business will vanish and the town's inhabitants will not remember its existence.

                                    Essence 4+: The duration of the business is extended to the end of the current story line.

                                    Repetitive History Assurance
                                    Cost: 3 Motes; Mins: Lore 3, Essence 2; Type: Simple
                                    Keywords: ????
                                    Duration: ???
                                    Prerequisite Charms: ???
                                    Everyone knows history is doomed to repeat itself and clever Sidereals take advantage of this assumption. The Sidereal can alter the fate of a book or document to send a message about current events to another individual. Anyone besides the intended recipient assumes the altered passage has nothing to do with current events.

                                    Discordant String's Echo
                                    Cost: 5m; Mins: Investigation 4, Essence 2; Type: ????
                                    Keywords: None
                                    Duration: ????
                                    Prerequisite Charms: Marvelous Inclusion of Details
                                    The Sidereal can tune into the threads of Fate of a room and relive events that happened in that room within the past week. The Sidereal is unaware of their present surroundings while using this charm.

                                                Glad you like the idea. I have to say it felt right as I wrote it.

                                                I think the costs and requirements for charms are somewhat flexible. Less so for combat charms due to the nature of combat, but other areas generally have less mechanical precision and greater ST input so the exact cost isn't as important.

                                                Anyway here are a few more bits to look at. As always comments and advice are always welcome.
                                                Larceny
                                                Thought Swiping Distraction
                                                Cost: 4m; Mins: Larceny 2, Essence 1; Type: Supplemental
                                                Keywords: Mute
                                                Duration: Instant
                                                Prerequisite Charms: None
                                                Reduce the target's Guile by (Larceny/2, round up) when making a Read Intentions action.

                                                Masque of the Uncanny
                                                Cost: 6m; Mins: Larceny 3, Essence 1; Type: Simple
                                                Keywords: Mute
                                                Duration: Indefinite
                                                Prerequisite Charms: Thought Swiping Distraction
                                                The Sidereal may disguise herself as a fae, ghost or demon without suffering the normal penalties on the disguise roll. The disguise is impervious to mundane senses for (Essence) scenes, but can be pierced by inhuman senses with a roll as normal. Senses heightened to supernatural levels by Charms such as Keen Sight Discipline have a chance to pierce this deception as normal.

                                                Honourable Thief Spirit
                                                Cost: -; Mins: Larceny 3, Essence 1; Type: Permanent
                                                Keywords: None
                                                Duration: Permanent
                                                Prerequisite Charms: None
                                                Criminals who interact with her count as having a minor intimacy of trust or admiration (the players choice) towards the Sidereal.

                                                Sidereal Shell Games
                                                Cost: 8m, 1wp; Mins: Larceny 5, Essence 2; Type: Simple
                                                Keywords: None
                                                Duration: One scene
                                                Prerequisite Charms: Honourable Thief Spirit
                                                Roll (Wits + Larceny) against the target's (Essence) for each threshold success the Sidereal may steal from the target's soak or one of their ability dice pools, up to (Essence) soak/dice. The Sidereal may only steal from dice pools in which they have points in the related college. The target may spend one willpower to reduce the maximum available to steal to (Essence/2, round up). The target's soak/dice pool is reduced by the amount stolen and the Sidereal increases their same trait by the same amount. The decrease of a dice pool does not alter the number of dice that the target may add through charms or other magic. This charm may be used once per scene, unless reset by crashing the target of this charm.

                                                Name Pilfering Practices
                                                Cost: 10m, 1wp; Mins: Larceny 4, Essence 2; Type: Simple
                                                Keywords: Psyche
                                                Duration: Indefinites
                                                Prerequisite Charms: Masque of the Uncanny
                                                The charm allows the Sidereal to use the following gambit, using an attack pool of (Dexterity + Larceny).
                                                Thief of Memory (Difficulty 6): If successful this gambit steals the target's name so that only the Sidereal may think, write or speak it. This means that when the victim leaves someone's presence they must roll (Wits + Integrity) to remember who the victim is, this roll suffers a -3 dice penalty, but is only difficulty one. The effects of the victim's actions remain, but people don't necessarily attribute those actions to the victim. This makes it difficulty for the victim to build long term relationships as memories of who they are fade quickly.
                                                This charm may be used outside of combat by touching the victim and succeeding at a (Manipulation + Larceny) roll against a difficulty of the target's Essence. Essence is only committed to this charm if the gambit is successful.

                                                Dream Confiscation Approach
                                                Cost: 6m, 1wp (+4m); Mins: Larceny 4, Essence 2; Type: Simple
                                                Keywords: Aggravated
                                                Duration: Instant
                                                Prerequisite Charms: Name Pilfering Practice
                                                The charm allows the Sidereal to use the following gambit, using an attack pool of (Dexterity + Larceny).
                                                Touch of the Dreamless (Difficulty 5): If successful this gambit steals the target's ability to gain restful sleep. This means that the victim can no longer recover willpower from sleeping. Trivial opponents suffer from this affliction for the rest of their lives. Non-trivial opponents recover after (Essence) months. If the victim has higher Essence than the Sidereal then this period drops to (Essence) weeks. Using this charm against one of the fair folk causes (Essence) aggravated damage that ignores hardness in addition to the other effects.
                                                This charm may be used outside of combat at a surcharge of 4 motes at which point it only requires that the Sidereal touch the victim and succeed a (Wits + Larceny) roll against a difficulty of the target's Essence.

                                                Conning Chaos Technique
                                                Cost: 10m, 1wp; Mins: Larceny 5, Essence 3; Type: Simple
                                                Keywords: Prayer Strip
                                                Duration: Varies
                                                Prerequisite Charms: Sidereal Shell Games, Dream Confiscation Approach
                                                Roll (Manipulation + Larceny) against difficulty target's Essence, the victim is cursed for a number of months equal to the roll's threshold successes. The curse causes Fair Folk or Wyld creatures to seek him out, possibly bestowing 'favours' on the victim.
                                                The victim gets to make an extended (Intelligence + Lore) roll with an interval of one month. If the victim accrues 6 successes they realise that the prayer strip they saw when being cursed is the cause of the curse. Upon reaching 12 successes the victim is able to figure out the exact location of the prayer strip.
                                                For each month that the victim remains cursed one square mile of the Boardermarches is ceded to Creation. This region is now a true part of creation and suffers no long-term maladies from having been a part of the Wyld.
                                                Linguistics
                                                Favourable Inflection Procedure
                                                Cost: 5m; Mins: Linguistics 2, Essence 1; Type: Reflexive
                                                Keywords: Psyche
                                                Duration: One scene
                                                Prerequisite Charms: None
                                                The Sidereal perfectly mirrors the cadence and inflection of an area giving locals a temporary minor intimacy of friendly familiarity towards her. This does not allow the Sidereal to communicate with people in a language she does not know, merely make people she can communicate with more accepting of her.

                                                Blue Vervain Binding
                                                Cost: 5m, 1xp; Mins: Linguistics 3, Essence 1; Type: Simple
                                                Keywords: None
                                                Duration: Instant
                                                Prerequisite Charms: Favourable Inflection Procedure
                                                The Sidereal recites a minute-long blessing in the Old Tongue. Roll (Intelligence + Linguistics) against a difficulty of 5. Success binds the fates of two beings so that they can understand each other forever after regardless of their respective languages. The extent of the communications is limited only by intelligence of the two creatures. Trying to teach a Tyrant Lizard the finer points of pacifism may be an exercise in futility.

                                                Hot Eyed Snake Whispering
                                                Cost: 4m; Mins: Linguistics 4, Essence 1; Type: Supplemental
                                                Keywords: Written Only
                                                Duration: Instant
                                                Prerequisite Charms: None
                                                The intended recipient of the missive will always be able read and understand this letter. Regardless of if they have 0 dots in Linguistics or no languages in common with the Sidereal. Others attempting to read the text must roll (Intelligence + Linguistics) against a difficulty of the Sidereal's (Essence) in order to interpret it successfully.

                                                Abandoned Words Curse
                                                Cost: 10m; Mins: Linguistics 4, Essence 2; Type: Simple
                                                Keywords: None
                                                Duration: Instant
                                                Prerequisite Charms: Hot Eyed Snake Whispering
                                                Roll (Wits + Linguistics) against the target's (Resolve). Success removes one language from the target, chosen by the Sidereal. The target no longer reads, writes, speaks or understands the chosen language. The victim may re-learn the language with time and effort (usually a minimum of one month to re-learn a native tongue).

                                                Ice and Fire Binding
                                                Cost: 10m; Mins: Linguistics 5, Essence 2; Type: Simple
                                                Keywords: None
                                                Duration: Instant
                                                Prerequisite Charms: Blue Vervain Binding, Abandoned Words Curse
                                                At the end of a ceremony that takes one hour roll (Intelligence + Linguistics) against a difficulty of 4 + intended spirit's (Essence). Success creates a spirit or elemental of fire with a defining intimacy to spread joy, health and pleasure. This intimacy is protected from being weakened for one season plus one month per threshold success on the original roll.

                                                Lover's Oath
                                                Cost: 20m, 1wp; Mins: Linguistics 5, Essence 3; Type: Simple
                                                Keywords: Prayer Strip
                                                Duration: Instant
                                                Prerequisite Charms: Ice and Fire Binding
                                                The two, willing, beneficiaries of this charm roll (Intelligence + Linguistics). If between them they achieve 5 successes then the Lover's Oath comes into effect. The prayer strip turns into a pair of starmetal rings set with a sapphire, which the wearers cannot attempt to remove. Once the rings are in place then each may spend each other's Willpower and Essence as if it where their own. Essence spent counts as Peripheral for the purposes of anima flare. Once per day either may accept the wounds of the other transferring up to (Essence) damaged health levels to themselves from their partner. Health levels transferred are taken from the Incapacitated, then -4 levels, then -2 levels etc. Aggravated damage may not be transferred.
                                                Lore
                                                Systematic Understanding of Everything
                                                Cost: 1m; Mins: Lore 2, Essence 1; Type: Reflexive/Supplemental
                                                Keywords: None
                                                Duration: Instant
                                                Prerequisite Charms: None
                                                Any dice provided by a speciality are converted into successes before the roll is made. This charm may be used with other abilities. If used to enhance a defence then the charm doesn't add to the Sidereal's defense, but causes an attack with 0 threshold successes to miss.

                                                The Methodology of Secrets
                                                Cost: 1m; Mins: Lore 3, Essence 1; Type: Reflexive/Supplemental
                                                Keywords: None
                                                Duration: Instant
                                                Prerequisite Charms: Systematic Understanding of Everything
                                                The Sidereal may choose three specialities to apply to any of her abilities for the duration of the charm. These work as normal specialities and thus may not stack with regular specialities.

                                                Of Truths Best Unspoken
                                                Cost: 5m, 1wp; Mins: Lore 2, Essence 1; Type: Simple
                                                Keywords: None
                                                Duration: Instant
                                                Prerequisite Charms: None
                                                Spend three hours in meditation to make a recall knowledge roll about any topic to learn what its importance will be in the future, from the perspective of the end of the Second Age. Once per story.

                                                Avoiding the Truth Technique
                                                Cost: 5m; Mins: Lore 3, Essence 1; Type: Reflexive
                                                Keywords: None
                                                Duration: Instant
                                                Prerequisite Charms: Of Truths Best Unspoken
                                                The Sidereal may communicate a second message as subtext during another conversation/missive. The secret message may not have content longer than the spoken/written message. Attempts to uncover the secret message are made as an opposed (Intelligence + Lore) roll with the opponent suffering -(Essence) dice.

                                                Of Things Desired and Feared
                                                Cost: 10m; Mins: Lore 3, Essence 2; Type: Reflexive
                                                Keywords: Psyche
                                                Duration: Instant
                                                Prerequisite Charms: None
                                                After spending one month advising or teaching someone the Sidereal may activate this charm to give the beneficiary of their advice/teaching a minor intimacy of fear or desire towards something of the Sidereal's choice. The ST should be fairly liberal in interpreting the intimacy, for example an intimacy of "Bring down the Realm" could be seen as a desire to end the Realm's corrupting influence on the Threshold.

                                                Of the Shape of the World
                                                Cost: 5m, 1wp; Mins: Lore 3, Essence 2; Type: Simple
                                                Keywords: None
                                                Duration: Instant
                                                Prerequisite Charms: Of Things Desired and Feared
                                                After spending one hour in meditation the Sidereal may read about a place, person or object's future. Roll (Intelligence + Lore) threshold successes give the Sidereal one piece of information about the target's future. The difficulty of the roll is based on the area that the Sidereal studies. At difficulty 1 the Sidereal discerns information from the whole of the target's life. Difficulty 3 narrows the length of time down to a specific year. Difficulty 5 narrows it down to a specific month. This charm may only be used once per story for a given target.

                                                Of Horrors Best Unknown
                                                Cost: 10m (+1wp); Mins: Lore 4, Essence 2; Type: Simple
                                                Keywords: None
                                                Duration: Instant
                                                Prerequisite Charms: Of the Shape of the World
                                                Roll (Dexterity + Lore) against a difficulty of the target's (Essence). Success inflicts a curse on the target that makes it more likely that people will succeed against them. The curse reduces the target number of any rolls against the victim by one for one scene. The scene must happen within one year and a day of the curse being placed or it fades away. By spending a surcharge of 1 willpower when using this charm the Sidereal may extend the time period in which the scene can occur to indefinite.

                                                Of Secrets Yet Untold
                                                Cost: 5m; Mins: Lore 4, Essence 2; Type: Supplemental
                                                Keywords: Mute
                                                Duration: Instant
                                                Prerequisite Charms: Avoiding the Truth Technique
                                                Reduce the strength of intimacy required to Persuade a spirit to perform a task/action by one level. This charm may only be used once per story on a given target.

                                                Transcendent Hatchet of Fate
                                                Cost: 16m, 1wp, 1lhl; Mins: Lore 5, Essence 3; Type: Simple
                                                Keywords: Prayer Strip
                                                Duration: Indefinite
                                                Prerequisite Charms: The Methodology of Secrets, Of Horrors Best Unknown, Of Secrets Yet Untold
                                                Roll (Intelligence + Lore) against a difficulty of the target's (Guile + Essence). Success writes the target's worst fear into a prayer strip. Thereafter the Sidereal may read out the contents of the prayer strip to the target. The target understands the Sidereal's words regardless of the language being spoken and loses (Essence/2, round down) Willpower. The fate read out by the Sidereal is now fated to happen and cannot be averted by the victim's actions. The ST should come up with an alternative if the suggested destiny is not possible. Essence committed to this charm is only committed until the Sidereal reads out the contents of the prayer strip. If the Sidereal releases the commitment before reading out it's contents the prayer strip fades into nothing.
