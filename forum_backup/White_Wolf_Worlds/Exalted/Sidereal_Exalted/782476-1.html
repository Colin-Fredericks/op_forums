            So here are the first two completed charm trees. These were some of the easiest ones to do due to a number of them being in the Core pdf. Before that though there is one new keyword to include, that is quite important for Sidereals.
            New Keyword: Prayer Strip
            A Charm with this Keyword has a physical manifestation that, if destroyed ends the charm. If the prayer strip is destroyed before the end of the Charm's duration then the Sidereal is refunded 1 Willpower. A prayer strip has a Defence of (Dexterity + Dodge + Essence/2, round up). It cannot be targeted with withering attacks, has Hardness 10 and 15 [-0] health levels. The prayer strip does not suffer onslaught penalties to its Defense and activated prayer strips are always visible to powers that detect magic at work, even when those powers cannot normally detect the charms of Exalted.
            Archery (The Quiver)
            Generalized Ammunition Technique
            Cost: 1m; Mins: Archery 2, Essence 1; Type: Supplemental
            Keywords: Uniform
            Duration: Instant
            Prerequisite Charms: None
            Fire any small object—a handful or dust, a flame, a rat—as an arrow. If the attack is awarded a stunt, add its level in bonus dice to the attack’s damage, or as success on the Initiative roll of a gambit. May have other effects at the Storyteller’s discretion.

            Any Direction Arrow
            Cost: 3m, 2i; Mins: Archery 3, Essence 1; Type: Supplemental
            Keywords: Dual
            Duration: Instant
            Prerequisite Charms: None
            Sight along the lines of fate to convert any dice from Aiming to successes. Withering attacks also add these successes to their damage roll. Heavy cover is reduced to light; light cover is negated entirely.

            Every Direction Arrow
            Cost: 5m; Mins: Archery 4, Essence 2; Type: Supplemental
            Keywords: Uniform
            Duration: Instant
            Prerequisite Charms: Any Direction Arrow
            The attack becomes undodgeable.

            Opportune Shot
            Cost: 2m, 2i, 1wp; Mins: Archery 2, Essence 1; Type: Reflexive
            Keywords: Clash, Perilous, Withering Only
            Duration: Instant
            Prerequisite Charms: None
            Make a reflexive clash with a withering attack against any attack made from short or medium (but not close) range. Crashing an enemy with Opportune Shot allows the Sidereal to reflexively Aim for another attack against them.

            Five Seasons Approach
            Cost: 4m, 1wp; Mins: Archery 4, Essence 2; Type: Simple
            Keywords: Decisive Only
            Duration: Instant
            Prerequisite Charms: Every Direction Arrow, Opportune Shot
            Make a decisive attack, adding up to (Essence) extra successes on the attack roll as dice of raw damage. The target number of the damage roll is lowered by one. Lower the target number another point at Initiative 12+, and another against a crashed enemy.

            Many Missiles Bow Technique
            Cost: 10m, 1wp, 1lhl; Mins: Archery 5, Essence 3; Type: Simple
            Keywords: Uniform, Prayer Strip
            Duration: One scene
            Prerequisite Charms: Five Seasons Approach
            Archery-based attacks may convert up to (Essence) dice into successes, before rolling. Additionally the Sidereal may reflexively apply transformations to the arrow as it falls. The Sidereal gains one transformation upon purchasing this Charm and may learn additional transformations for 1 experience point each.

            Each arrow transformed in this manner costs 1 mote to shoot. “Arrows” from Generalized Ammunition Technique are not valid targets for the transformation effect, neither are arrows made from the Five Magical Materials. Commonly known transformations include:

            - Glass: Targets must make a (Perception + Awareness) check at difficulty 2. Failure makes the attack a surprise attack.
            - Life: By spending 4i, the Sidereal may heal the target for one level of bashing or lethal damage.
            - Snow: It begins to snow out to Medium range of where the arrow fell. The character must renew this effect with at least one arrow per minute to maintain this effect.
            - Grain: A small patch of ripe wheat, enough to feed one person for a day, sprouts where the arrow lands.
            - Rain of Fire: Creates a fire that extends to Close range of where the arrow landed. The fire is an environmental hazard with Damage/Interval 2L/round and difficulty (Archery). The hazard lasts for (Essence) rounds. The duration and damage of the hazard may be augmented by shooting more arrows into the area. Each arrow adds 2L to the damage of the hazard and (Essence) to the number of rounds remaining. The damage may not be raised above 6L/round.
            - Boulder: By spending 2i; the attack, if successful, may knock the opponent back one range band, or knock them prone.


            Some comments on Archery. A significant amount of the work here was done for me as the charms were written up in the backer pdf. I honestly didn't feel that any of these charms were specifically broad or powerful enough that they should be split down into multiple charms so I just ported them straight over.
            - Every Direction arrow was a bit of a judgement call, I considered setting it up to be a stackable damage enhancer or even a decisive extra attack action. In the end I went for this as it is a relatively simple fit and it evokes there being arrows coming from every angle to hit you. There is also some precedent in the Unobstructed Blow of Brawl, which was an unblockable attack.
            - Many Missiles was a bit interesting too as I wanted prayer strips to be powerful, but not make the Sidereal all powerful by just adding successes. Hopefully the converting dice strikes a bit of a middle ground, but it could easily be too much for a scene-long. My small defense of my decision is that with the excellency a Sidereal can drop the target number far enough then 80% of dice are successes anyway so this doesn't really boost that too much, merely twist the probabilities a little (I hope).

            Athletics
            Burn Life
            Cost: 3m; Mins: Athletics 2, Essence 1; Type: Supplemental
            Keywords: None
            Duration: Instant
            Prerequisite Charms: None
            The Sidereal may add Intimacy non-charm dice to a feat of strength roll. The feat of strength must uphold/protect/advance the Intimacy in some way.

            Hungry Touch
            Cost: -; Mins: Athletics 3, Essence 2; Type: Permanent
            Keywords: None
            Duration: Permanent
            Prerequisite Charms: Burn Life
            When performing a feat of strength to destroy an object the Sidereal reduces the target number of the action to 5.

            Forgotten Earth
            Cost: 2m; Mins: Athletics 2, Essence 1; Type: Supplemental
            Keywords: Perilous
            Duration: Instant
            Prerequisite Charms: None
            When performing a jumping-based movement roll (e.g Rise From Prone action) convert 2 dice to successes before rolling. This charm may be used to enhance other abilities.

            Inexorable Advance
            Cost: 5m,2i; Mins: Athletics 3, Essence 1; Type: Supplemental
            Keywords: None
            Duration: Instant
            Prerequisite Charms: Forgotten Earth, Burn Life
            Negate all wound or mobility penalties on a rush roll, and complete the movement instantly on a success, instead of waiting for the target to move. If the Sidereal attacks a character on the same turn she rushes them with Inexorable Advance, they take -1 Defense.

            Unswerving Juggernaut Principle
            Cost: 5m (+2m, 1wp); Mins: Athletics 2, Essence 2; Type: Supplemental
            Keywords: Perilous
            Duration: One turn
            Prerequisite Charms: None
            Choose an intimacy that can reasonably be said to be upheld/protected/advanced by performing this rush/disengage. Gain +(Intimacy) soak for the turn. If the sidereal attacks a character on the same turn she rushes them with Unswerving Juggernaut Principle then on a successful attack she may knock them back one range band or knock them prone.

            By spending an additional 2 motes and 1 Willpower the Sidereal may extend the duration to one journey, during which she does not need to eat or sleep so long as she continues to run towards her destination. If the Sidereal is forced to stop or significantly deviate from her course, or is crashed, then this charm ends.

            Forward Thinking Technique
            Cost: - (+5m, 1wp); Mins: Athletics 3, Essence 3; Type: Permanent
            Keywords: None
            Duration: Permanent
            Prerequisite Charms: Unswerving Juggernaut Principle
            By spending a surcharge of +5 motes and 1 willpower when activating Unswerving Juggernaut Principle the sidereal gains an extra +(Essence) soak as well as (Essence) hardness. Additionally the Unswerving Juggernaut Principle's duration is extended to (Intimacy) turns.

            Invisible Motion
            Cost: 10m, 1wp, 1lhl; Mins: Athletics 4, Essence 3; Type: Simple
            Keywords: Prayer Strip
            Duration: One scene
            Prerequisite Charms: Inexorable Advance, Forward Thinking Technique
            Each turn, the Sidereal gains a pool of (Athletics) Momentum that may be used to enhance physical actions (explicitly including non-Athletics rolls). Momentum may be used to convert a dice into a success before rolling, or to increase Defense by 1. Momentum may be saved for use on Defense, but Momentum does not carry over from turn to turn.


            Some comments on Athletics.
            - Burn Life I wanted to be something that could stack with excellencies rather than be a replacement for them, which is why it gives non-charm dice. I also thought that the idea of a Sidereal giving up some of his own life in order to protect something cherished or advance towards his goal was quite cool, so I made it intimacy based. I think the wording needs tidying up and clarifying, but I'm not sure how best to do that.
            - Unswerving Juggernaut I wanted to keep fairly similar, but I thought it could have an in combat use as well. So I made it able to enhance a rush/disengage. It doesn't directly make you more likely to succeed, but I think that making yourself better protected whilst doing so is pretty worthy of the charm.
            - Forward Thinking I think would work just as well as an essence 3 upgrade within Unswerving Juggernaut itself. If I get around to expanding on this later on I may do that and make Forward Thinking something else, but for now it is pretty much just a straight conversion of previous editions.
            - For Invisible Motion I decided to stick with the converting dice to successes mechanic, which I think I may use for all prayer strips that work in a similar way, It should allow a Sidereal to boost their combat ability without making them unassailable.
            Anyway that's it for now. Look forward to seeing what people have to say about these

            My attempt at a Sidereal 3e Homebrew

                            I have a draft of a 3e Sidereal set. Here's one thing that bugged me a little bit, writing it: Colleges aren't fun.

                            I mean, Destinies are great fun, don't get me wrong. But Colleges are one more stat that you have to buy, that's fairly expensive, that you only rarely get to roll, and that - at best - is basically duplicating the way you've already done that for your Ability ratings. You could say, "Well, but it's a Solar/Sidereal XP outlet," but Sids are already going to be wanting to pump as much of that as possible into Martial Arts - and that leads to an awkward position where they just don't buy Colleges.

                            I ultimately decided to just let people use Ability scores anywhere they'd normally roll Colleges; we're currently testing that with one of my players. Do you have a design goal for keeping them in your revision?

                                            So here is the next instalment of charms. It's taking me a bit longer than I hoped to get through them, but I'm building up a bit of momentum so things are getting quicker now.

                                            Brawl
                                            Unobstructed Blow
                                            Cost: 5m; Mins: Brawl 2, Essence 1; Type: Supplemental
                                            Keywords: Uniform
                                            Duration: Instant
                                            Prerequisite Charms: None
                                            The attack becomes unblockable.

                                            Tolerant Strife
                                            Cost: 2m, 1wp; Mins: Brawl 3, Essence 1; Type: Supplemental
                                            Keywords: None
                                            Duration: Instant
                                            Prerequisite Charms: None
                                            The attack's dice pool does not suffer from wound, onslaught, flurry, environmental, or being prone penalties.

                                            Horrific Wreath
                                            Cost: 4m; Mins: Brawl 3, Essence 2; Type: Simple
                                            Keywords: Decisive Only, Aggravated
                                            Duration: One scene
                                            Prerequisite Charms: Unobstructed Blow, Tolerant Strife
                                            The Sidereal's unarmed attacks deal Lethal damage. If used to strike at inhabitants of Malfeas or the Underworld then the damage becomes Aggravated.

                                            Crimson Palm Counterstrike
                                            Cost: 5m; Mins: Brawl 4, Essence 2; Type: Reflexive
                                            Keywords: Decisive Only, Counterattack
                                            Duration: Instant
                                            Prerequisite Charms: None
                                            This creates a Brawl counterattack that occurs after the attack result, but before the damage has been rolled. This functions as a decisive attack, but compare the number of successes on the damage roll of the counterattack and the regular attack. The character with the greater total inflicts the difference between those two numbers as damage. The character with the lesser total inflicts no damage.

                                            Orchestration of Conflict
                                            Cost: -; Mins: Brawl 5, Essence 2; Type: Permanent
                                            Keywords: Perilous
                                            Duration: Permanent
                                            Prerequisite Charms: Horrific Wreath, Crimson Palm Counterstrike
                                            In combat the Sidereal may spend motes instead of initiative on combat actions/weapon tags at a 1:1 ratio.

                                            Easily Accepted Proposition Stance
                                            Cost: 12m, 1wp, 1lhl; Mins: Brawl 5, Essence 3; Type: Simple
                                            Keywords: Prayer Strip, Aggravated
                                            Duration: Instant
                                            Prerequisite Charms: Orchestration of Conflict
                                            The charm allows the Sidereal to use the following gambit against the leader of her opponents in a confrontation.
                                            Twist of Fate (Difficulty Varies): The difficulty of this gambit should be determined by the ST based on the absurdity of the circumstances the Sidereal lays out as coming to pass. For example a beleaguered Sidereal may use this charm near to the end of a major battle to say that reinforcements arrive in time to save her and her command. Difficulty 1 might be assigned to a group of 50 beaten and battered soldiers having force marched to the rescue arriving. Whereas a fresh well rested Legion with a close ally as commander arriving could well be Difficulty 9. Or higher. If the gambit is successful then the target has two options. They can either accept this coincidence in which case the circumstances play out as the Sidereal scripted. Or the target chooses to reject the new fate, in which case they take (Gambit's Difficulty + Essence) dice of Aggravated damage that ignores hardness. Use of a charm to reduce/nullify this effect completely blocks the damage, but the circumstance comes into effect as the Sidereal scripted. This charm may only be used once per scene. Also note that invoking higher difficulty gambits with this charm can invite paradox or censure.

                                            Bureaucracy Slick Essence Replenishment
                                            Cost: -; Mins: Bureaucracy 1, Essence 1; Type: Permanent
                                            Keywords: None
                                            Duration: Permanent
                                            Prerequisite Charms: None
                                            Whenever the Sidereal successfully influences a large group with Bureaucracy she gains (Essence) motes. This bonus may only be received once per scene.

                                            Icy Hand
                                            Cost: 4m; Mins: Bureaucracy 2, Essence 1; Type: Simple
                                            Keywords: None
                                            Duration: Instant
                                            Prerequisite Charms: None
                                            A (Charisma + Bureaucracy) Instill action that gives the target bureaucrat a major principle of "Perform my duties diligently". This intimacy lasts for 3 days, at which point it degrades to a minor principle, after 2 further days it disappears entirely. The intimacy created does not need a supporting intimacy.

                                            Terminal Sanction
                                            Cost: 8m,1wp; Mins: Bureaucracy 3, Essence 1; Type: Simple
                                            Keywords: None
                                            Duration: One scene
                                            Prerequisite Charms: Icy Hand
                                            This charm forces a spirit to manifest and materialize before the Sidereal. The spirit is compelled to not dematerialize for the scene. Roll (Charisma + Bureaucracy) against a difficulty of a spirit's (Essence), success means that if the spirit 'dies' during the scene the Sidereal can make one of four choices.
                                            - Destroy the spirit utterly.
                                            - Bind the spirit to service for one task or a year and a day.
                                            - Bind the spirit into a temporary artifact of rating (Essence/2, round up), which lasts for a year and a day.
                                            - Let the spirit reform normally, knowing what the Sidereal could have done.
                                            If used against a demon this charm gives the Sidereal (Essence) non-charm dice on the (Charisma + Bureaucracy) roll.

                                            Underling Invisibility Practice
                                            Cost: 12m, 1wp; Mins: Bureaucracy 4, Essence 2; Type: Simple
                                            Keywords: Mute
                                            Duration: One scene
                                            Prerequisite Charms: None
                                            The Sidereal becomes invisible to everyone who thinks they are her superior (hierarchically, socially etc). Those affected consider themselves unobserved for the purposes of the Sidereal's Read Intentions actions. Additionally against those affected the Sidereal's social influences count as unexpected. The Sidereal rolls (Dexterity + Bureaucracy) against any Awareness rolls to detect her with the observer taking a -(Essence) penalty to their dice pool. If Join Battle is rolled then the Sidereal counts as being in Stealth until they take a violent action.

                                            Paralyzed Mandarin Inflection
                                            Cost: 6m, 1wp; Mins: Bureaucracy 3, Essence 2; Type: Permanent
                                            Keywords: None
                                            Duration: Permanent
                                            Prerequisite Charms: Terminal Sanction
                                            The Sidereal may choose an Intimacy held by the leader of an organisation. This charm causes the Intimacy to spread through the organisation through the next day, week, month, or longer depending on the size of the organisation in question. As it spreads through the ranks the Intimacy reduces in intensity resulting in those at the lowest levels of a large organisation only having a Minor Intimacy, even if the leader has that same Intimacy at Defining.

                                            End Debate
                                            Cost: 10m, 1wp, 1hl; Mins: Bureaucracy 5, Essence 3; Type: Simple
                                            Keywords: Prayer Strip
                                            Duration: Instant
                                            Prerequisite Charms: Paralyzed Mandarin Inflection, Underling Invisibility Practice
                                            Roll (Charisma + Bureaucracy) against the Resolve of everyone in the scene, success drains the will to argue or debate ending the scene. Those affected may spend (Essence/2, round up) willpower to resist it's effects. This charm may only be attempted once per scene.

                                            Craft
                                            Elemental Vision
                                            Cost: -; Mins: Craft 2, Essence 1; Type: Permanent
                                            Keywords: None
                                            Duration: Permanent
                                            Prerequisite Charms: None
                                            There are 5 versions of this charm, one for each of the elements. Different crafts resonate with different elements, so each element requires a different craft ability to buy the apprpriate charm:
                                            Air: calligraphy, jewelery making, glassblowing
                                            Earth: masonry, stone cutting, creating earthworks
                                            Fire: blacksmithing, creating ceramics
                                            Wood: carpentry, weaving, flower arranging
                                            Water: cooking, brewing, leather working
                                            The charm gives a Minor Tie of Respect for the Sidereal in any elementals, dragon-bloods or god of the corresponding element.

                                            Elegant Patterns of Fate
                                            Cost: -; Mins: Craft 1, Essence 1; Type: Permanent
                                            Keywords: None
                                            Duration: Permanent
                                            Prerequisite Charms: None
                                            Once per day roll (Intelligence + Craft[Fate]). Each successes on the roll gains the Sidereal 1sxp.

                                            Excellent Implementation of Objectives
                                            Cost: 4m; Mins: Craft 3, Essence 1; Type: Supplemental
                                            Keywords: None
                                            Duration: Instant
                                            Prerequisite Charms: None
                                            The Sidereal completes Basic and Major projects within minutes to hours.

                                            Destiny-Knitting Entanglement
                                            Cost: 2m; Mins: Craft 4, Essence 1; Type: Reflexive
                                            Keywords: None
                                            Duration: Instant
                                            Prerequisite Charms: Elegant Patterns of Fate
                                            After handling an object for an hour the Sidereal may bind it top her fate. A Sidereal may have up to (Essence) items bound to her in such a way. Thereafter the Sidereal may 2 motes to get a general description of the item's current circumstances. Given knowledge of the situation she may spend 2 motes to describe a reasonable method by which the item can be brought closer to her. Multiple uses of this ability over time allow the Sidereal to bring an item all the way across creation and into her grasp. In combat spending 2 motes and an appropriate stunt allows the Sidereal to call a lost object to her hand. The Sidereal may release the destiny of any object that no longer interests her reflexively.

                                            Mending Warped Designs
                                            Cost: 5m; Mins: Craft 4, Essence 2; Type: Simple
                                            Keywords: None
                                            Duration: Instant
                                            Prerequisite Charms: Excellent Implementation of Objectives
                                            Roll (Dexterity + Craft[Fate]) each success heals on health level that the target lost to a creature or effect of Malfeas, the Underworld or the Wyld. This charm can also be used to counter the direct effects of the Wyld. 3 successes are required to 'cure' every merit point of a Wyld induced mutation. Fifty successes can cleanse a small region of Wyld influence. This charm does not permit extended rolls, but multiple Sidereals can apply it in the same turn, adding their successes together.

                                            Predestined Delivery Shaping
                                            Cost: 7m; Mins: Craft 4, Essence 2; Type: Reflexive
                                            Keywords: None
                                            Duration: Indefinite
                                            Prerequisite Charms: Destiny-Knitting Entanglement
                                            Upon finishing an object that she has crafted herself the Sidereal may activate this charm. Doing so binds the object to the fate of a chosen person or place. As long as the essence remains committed to this charm the object is guided by fate to join with that of the person or place it is bound to. The progress is measured by an extended (Wits + Craft[Fate]) roll, with an interval of 1 month and a goal number based upon the distance the item must travel. The goal number ranges from 1 for across a town to 20 or more to cross Creation. A botch indicates that the item has been destroyed or otherwise cannot proceed any further. The character knows when the object has reached it's intended destination. This charm may be used as a transfer method for Astrological Destinies as an alternative to touching the intended target, carrying the destiny until the intended receives it.

                                            World-Shaping Artistic Vision
                                            Cost: -; Mins: Craft 3, Essence 1; Type: Permanent
