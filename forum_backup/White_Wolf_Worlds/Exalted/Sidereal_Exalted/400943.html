    Sidereal of the Day!

    02-10-2015, 07:13 PM
    In honor of Marsday:

    RED, Chosen of Battles

    Nexus, a metropolis well-known for its many mercenary companies, has even more places to service them. One of these places is a place called "Red's". A tavern specializing in catering to soldiers, Red's has strong drink, diverting music, and a very welcoming atmosphere for those of a military bent. It's especially a great place for those who are new in town, looking for a new outfit to serve with, as the posting board is always full of offers for new commissions.

    Red himself, the owner of the establishment, is almost a living stereotype of the grizzled old soldier, a retired campaigner who is heavily battle-scarred but still wearing his outdated uniform, albeit without any rank or insignia. When not serving drinks or wiping down the bar and glasses, he's telling unbelievable tall tales (some of which are nonetheless true), encouraging the raucous carousing, or simply listening to a shell-shocked soldier pouring his heart out.

    What none of his clientale even suspect is that Red is a Sidereal, a Shieldbearer who once served with great distinction in the Crimson Panoply of Victory on a Convention of Demons for centuries. On one sortie, his life was saved during a demonic incursion by one of the Panoply's soldiers, a lesser Goddess of Warfare, an event that started a torrid love affair that eventually led to them being married by Red's favorite Joybringer. The two fought powerfully side-by-side and loved even more strongly, eventually having a child. Red's hopes for many more were dashed, however, when his wife was slain by an Exalted Akuma.

    In his desolation, his determination to raise their baby girl, all that was left to him of his wife, kept him going. Cashing in some large favors, Red took an extended Sabbatical for her, which he is still technically on. While doing so, he opened Red's, in hopes of continuing contributing to Fate's Plan, even if it's in some much smaller way. After almost two decades there, he is surprised to find out how much he loves these people, with their dreams and their problems, as well as how much he can affect the world's events through subtly steering certain groups of heroes together or apart, or suggesting they take this road or that mission. His charms help him determine such things and he is not above deftly placing blessings and curses on those he feels deserving of them to help them to their destinies.

    "Red's" is not always found in the same spot. It's owner occasionally uses Neighborhood Relocation Scheme to move it about the city districts, either for astrological purposes, to fulfill some plan of his, or just on a whim or for a change of scenery. It is most often found in the area catering to mercenaries' needs just outside of the Barracks in Cinnabar, just inside the city wall in Bastion where newcomers to Nexus can easily find it, or in the Nightside ghetto of Nighthammer, but it could really be anywhere. The city's residents act as though it has always been where ever it is, even if they must cross the entire city to find it.

    So far, the Fivescore Fellowship has been content to leave him to his own devices, which he knows will not last. Eventually, some disaster or other will require his attention, and he will have to leave all this behind. As always, his primary concern is for his daughter, Stellara, who knows nothing of her father's true nature or calling. She's a true soldiers' girl, able to hold her own in a duel, martial arts match or barroom brawl, but her blazing gaze and razor-sharp tongue are enough to keep order in Red's during those times he checks in on Yu-Shan and leaves her in charge. She has her mothers' flame-red hair, and isn't bad-looking for her 19 years, which has made Red a bit overprotective of her. Due to her father laying a Presence-in-Absence charm on her, that replaces her flirtation attempts with his intimidating presence, the poor girl has never even been kissed or asked out, let alone anything more!

    Notes: Red exalted during the Contagion, and has always had more sympathies for the Independent Faction, but at this point could be swayed to either Gold or Bronze depending on events. He has mastered Violet Bier of Sorrows, The Art of Forceful Declaration and Throne Shadow styles, and had almost mastered Scarlet-Patterned Battlefield Style when his wife was killed, which has created an unfortunate association in his mind that prevents him from finishing learning it.

    The persona of "Red" is a Resplendent Destiny of the Spear, and is very close to what he actually looks like. His true name and identity have been offered up to a One Direction Invocation vow to gain revenge for his wife's murder. Stellara, and a few regular customers, are both Acquaintances and Shadow Fingers of his, and bear Astrological adjustments to their Destinies.

    Red has three Familiars: The oldest is a Strix, which he has evolved into a Godly Companion, and who watches out for his interests in Heaven. The next is an Omen Dog, a Spirit Shape Companion, who is largely content to nap by the fire at Red's, but who will act to defend the tavern and Stellara if circumstances call for it. The last is his War Horse, which he has yet to raise to a higher state of being, but is still a powerful animal, and useful for his Ride charms.

    The Old Ball and Chain is a Starmetal Fated Weapon that Red painstakingly crafted from the star that fell when he lost his wife. He speaks to her frequently and, with the aid of Destiny Knitting Entanglement, never lets her far from his sight. He intends to reintroduce her to her killer at the earliest opportunity...

      For Mercuryday:

      EAGLE-EYE SALITH, Chosen of Journeys

      Every child raised on Mount Metagalapa dreams of being selected to become one of the elite Hawkriders who are celebrated by their society, but few are chosen. Salith was one of those few, although her lack of discipline and eccentricities made her family and trainers despair. Sometimes she felt that the only one who truly understood her was the war hawk she grew up alongside, Steelbeak.

      When she reached 17, the age of becoming an official Hawkrider, Salith's wild improvisations and difficultly keeping in formation almost led to her being drummed out of the organization, an unheard of disgrace. Since most figured it was only a matter of time before her natural curiosity and wanderlust made her take off for the Questing, she was quietly encouraged to do so immediately, in hopes that she would have matured over the customary 10 years wandering.

      Sidereal exaltation can be an odd and long-lasting thing, and to this day Salith is still not sure exactly when or why it happened. One day she's seeing the sights, reveling in the sheer freedom of flight, occasionally landing to talk to various peoples in small towns, the next she's being accosted in mid-air by a couple of folks riding small clouds. After some trouble getting Steelbeak past the Celestial Lions, Salith was welcomed to Yu-Shan and began her training for the Fivescore Fellowship.

      It has been a few years since then. Salith never declared for Bronze or Gold, by default remaining Independent by just focusing on doing her Vizierly duties and having fun once those are over. Her exurberant willingness to help fellow Seers of any Faction keeps her from having enemies, however.

      Most of Salith's job involves monitoring aerial trade and communications, a task she is well-suited for. She is welcomed in the crystalline cities of the Cloud People floating high above the earth, and she regularly takes tea with the Council of Winds. She oversees that the development of the Haslanti air fleet is proceeding as it should, without Out-of-Fate interference, and has even visited the People of the Air in Sezakan. She dreams of visiting Vanelith, but knows that she can't do so via Steelbeak. Carrying out her duties has made her an enemy of the Deathknight, Starving Rider of the Dark Wind, and his giant bat steed, Bloodwing, as well as a Ring of winged Raksha hunters of men's dreams.

      Technically, Salith is still on her Questing, and the time for that will soon be up. She knows that, by now, none of her family or friends will remember her in her former home, but she still has increasing feelings of homesickness. She has been asking her mentors if it's worth the pain to put together a Resplendent Destiny that will allow her go home again, only to interact with loved ones who won't know her. She has yet to decide what to do...

      Notes:

      Salith is a young Harbinger, and has focused many of her Charms in Ride and Thrown. Hawkriders are trained to be proficient with javelins, and she expresses this in her favorite weapon, a Starmetal Vajra with lightening-based Evocations called Skybolt. Steelbeak has been upgraded from a Familiar to a Spirit-Shape Companion, enabling him to assume a humanoid form not unlike the hawkmen of Metagalapa; Salith fears enhancing him further, as his resultant heavenly duties would then mandate separation from him.

      Salith has her eye on a Wings of the Raptor cloak owned by one of her fellow Sidereals, and keeps trying to persuade him to part with it by varying means, much to the amusement of the Fellowship. She wants it, not only to experience winged flight under her own power, but also because it will allow her to study Dagger Wind style martial arts under a Dragon King sifu in his dojo atop the clouds of Yu-Shan. Her fellow Seer knows this, and keeps stringing her along mercilessly...

                  BARRISTER ZHENG, Chosen of Secrets

                  Born into a wealthy Patrician family on the Blessed Isle, Zheng was educated for the Imperial Service, where he soon discovered that he had a knack for memorizing and disentangling the byzantine complexities of the Imperial Code of Law. He quickly made a reputation for himself in both the Dynastic and Common Courts, as an advocate who save seemingly last cases from the jaws of persecution, even in the notoriously corrupt and cutthroat Imperial Bureaucracy. If anything, it was all almost too easy for him...

                  Exaltation as a Dragon-Blooded would have been a boon, but as a Sidereal, it destroyed Zheng's career, as his name and accomplishments were all but forgotten. In despair, he followed his Sidereal finders to a new life in Yu-Shan...Where he discovered an even more ancient and complicated law code, maintained by immortal spirits thriving in an even more corrupt bureaucracy! Now, THIS was the challenge he'd been missing all his life!

                  Over the next year and a day, Zheng learned all that was required of him as a new Seer, but threw himself into studying the Laws of Heaven with a passion that was mystifying to his tutors. It all paid off when he persuaded a fellow neophyte Sidereal to allow him to represent her against trumped up charges, a routine harassment from Celestial Lions. When Zheng was done, not only did she not even have to pay the smallest fine, but she got a Salary raise, and a court-ordered public apology from the arresting Lion!

                  Zheng's star only rose from there. Soon, he was in demand from Viziers of all ages and factions, successfully defending them in so dramatic a fashion, that court attendance rose, while frivolous badgering of Sidereals dropped. Needing new cases, he started representing spirits, preferably Elementals and disenfranchised Gods, whose Ambrosial payments nevertheless allowed him to live well above his official Salary level.

                  Life turned into a whirl of success-fueled hedonism, as Zheng accumulated so many favors that he was even able to exempt himself from normal duties of his rank. Instead of serving on Conventions, he attended Celestial Coke parties; instead of sorting out Loom entanglements, he had a different Goddess (or three) every night. All the while, his Elders simply shook their heads and sighed; they had seen (and done, in some cases) this story before, and they knew how it would most likely play out...

                  Zheng's star crashed back down to earth almost literally. Even he could not avoid a mission assigned by Oversight, and even he could not foresee that it would go so horribly wrong. He survived, barely, and although the healers of heaven were able to put the young Oracle's body back together flawlessly, the scars inside linger. He was thrown face-to-face with the fact that his fast-talk and skill at working the system seemed powerless against the sheer, naked brutality of life in Creation, an almost feral existence that mortals must deal with everyday...

                  Zheng's Celestial Manse grows dark and quiet as he kicked all out the revelers and hanger-ons, and takes some time out to recuperate and figure out his next move. IS there some way to apply his social manueverings to advocate for the people of Creation, to better their lives (and his own, of course)? Hmmm...

                  Notes:
                  Barrister Zheng is a Social-focused character, who has placed most of his development in Mental and Social Attributes, and in the Bureaucracy, Lore, Performance and Socialize Abilities, with Specialties in Realm and Celestial Law. He has the Signature Style Merit, which aids his dramatic courtroom Stunts (stirring rhetoric, surprise witnesses, etc). He has plenty of Connections, Spies, Reputation and Followers, although few actual Allies, and many, many Favors.

                  Zheng's Artifacts are ones suited to his work, such as an Audient Brush and an Authoritative Stamp. Outside of an exquisitely tailored set of Silken Robes, he is woefully unprepared for combat. A situation he is now pondering how to rectify...

                      Thank the Loom it's Venusday! RL interrupted this thread last week, so I may as well finish it now...

                      LO CHEN, Chosen of Serenity

                      "Uncle" Lo Chen was an up and coming functionary in an underworld syndicate when he exalted just before the Contagion. On being whisked away to Heaven, he quickly realized that the same methods of how to gain power and keep it applied to the Gods there; only the goods and services were different, and being a Vizier gave him prime access to those.

                      The Contagion was a godsend to Uncle's business, as it resulted in tons of newly destitute deities now in need of his discreet aid. Lo Chen still makes sure to publicly attend all the duties expected of a Seer, including Convention work, but it all takes a backseat to his true, lucrative, passion: Expanding his influence throughout the underworld of heaven itself.

                      Of course, Uncle was not the first Sidereal to have these goals and methods, nor the last, but he has proven to be the most successful, buying out or eliminating his older rivals, while incorporating the younger ones into his organization. Privately, he denounces both Gold and Bronze Factions as idealistic fools, and he only has slightly more respect for the pragmatic Independents. However, Lo Chen happily accepts interviews from all Seers, most of whom hear whispered rumors of his influence and come to him for help in personal matters, which he is glad to provide for favors to be named later, if ever.

                      Gods also come to Uncle for help, whether for aid in swaying the bureaucracy of Yu-Shan or for Astrological Blessings and Curses, which he sells at a premium (his influence is also good for assuring another Sidereal's Astrological petition quickly gets the many co-signatures it needs). Uncle lives well above his actual Salary grade, in the best manses that the Celestial City has to offer. However, he headquarters his business in the Empty Quarters and Secret City of Yu-Shan, where his Sidereal and godly lieutenants sell prayers, mortal slaves, even new domains for desperate gods to represent.

                      Shining Barrator utterly despises Uncle, and lists taking him and his organization down as one of his top missions in life...but even if he were to succeed, it would be unlikely if the Elemental Dragon judge at his trial would do more than give him a slap on the wrist, maybe a century in the more pleasant side of the Archipelago of the Exiles, nowhere near the Gaol of Roaring Thunder, which the Implacable One thinks Uncle richly deserves.

                      Notes: Lo Chen is near the upper limits of Sidereal power, incorporating almost an eon of experience. Much of this is in his Mental and Social traits and charms (esp Larceny, Stealth, Occult, Dodge, Performance, and Socialize), or in his extensive Backgrounds. Although his lackluster work record has only given him Backing 2, he has an effective Salary of 5 (although he is careful not to show this off too much); much of this wealth is scattered in safe houses, as per the 5 pt Cache merit. His many Connections have earned him many Favors of varying levels, although this has earned him the Dark Secret (his criminal organization) and Enemy (Shining Barrator) flaws.

                      Uncle has the maximum number of Familiars available to him, all of which he has advanced to Godly Companions. Most are well-placed in various positions where they can watch out for his interests, the sole exception being Purring Ember, a pampered hearth-cat who sits on his lap most of the time, when she isn't in the form of a dazzlingly-beautiful cat-woman who is also Uncle's best thief. One of his most important Allies is The Red Right, a Bloody Hand of exceptional power and cruelty who serves as his chief leg-breaker and enforcer.

                      Uncle is always impeccably dressed, thanks to his Infinite Resplendence Amulet (5 dot, Starmetal and Moonsilver version), whose larger-on-the-inside pockets hold his many Artifacts. Most of these are formed from spirits he used Terminal Sanction on, as a lesson to others, so they have a limited time he can use them; but there are always more examples to be made. His current favorite Artifacts are a pair of Heaven Thunder Leaves, and he is quite skilled at dancing with them to attract spirits. Of the many hearthstones he has use of, Uncle considers the most valuable one of his least-used ones: a five-dot Home's Hearth stone, for quick escapes.

                      Lo Chen is a true master of Throne Shadow Style, and has innovated many custom charms and combos that he has never taught to anyone else; he counts his top lieutenants as his Shadow-Fingers. He has fully mastered White Veil Style, which he began learning when he belonged to that most secret of societies as a mortal. He now complements this style with the mysteries of the Black Claw. Chen has yet to learn any of the secret arts of his kind, largely due to lack of time and interest; he sometimes ponders what original style he could create. Master's Hand Envisioned Anew allows him to use war fans/fate rings as Form Weapons with all of his martial arts charms.

                      An accomplished Celestial sorcerer, Lo Chen makes good use of such spells as Corrupted Words, Private Plaza of Downcast Eyes, Faithful Ally, Shadow Theft, and Ivory Orchid Pavillion. He is also a past master of Astrology, and has Resplendent Destinies in The Peacock, The Pillar, The Banner, The Mask, The Sorcerer, and the Haywain as well as access to all their Resplendencies. He and his compatriots enjoy many astrological enhancements in their endeavors, both from himself and from other Viziers beholden to him.

                          HEL, Chosen of Endings

                          The North, while having one of the smallest populations in Creation, holds a disproportionate amount of human misery and suffering, from attacks from the walking dead and barbarians, to being preyed upon by mutated monsters and the Winter Folk, as well as the fallen standard of general lifestyle quality in the Second Age. Into this harsh world was born Hel, a young girl who quickly showed a talent for a soothing manner and a healing touch that qualified her for apprenticeship in the famous Hospital of Fella.

                          Her Exaltation came in the form of a group of traders who were set upon by the hungry dead; the survivors were brought to the nearby hospital, where she helped tend to them. Suddenly, Hel realized that she could tell which of the injured would live and which would die, and who was the most efficient use of her time and attention. Despite this, she chose to defy Destiny to save a life she could tell was doomed...and succeeded. Shortly afterwards, the Seers of Heaven came for her.

                          When undergoing the traditional minor hazing of being shown the Loom of Fate for the first time, the vision of Hel's future self did more than wave at her from across the chamber, as often happens. Instead, she came over to her younger self, and whispered something to her. This is unprecedented, and the Chosen of Secrets would give much to know what was said, which Hel has never revealed. Nara-O, who may know, has respected her privacy and blocked any efforts by curious Oracles to look it up...

                          While excelling at her studies, Hel was shocked at the prosperity of Yu-Shan and how quickly most of her Fellowship left Creation behind for its comforts. While understandable, she felt that the Chosen owed direct action to alleviating suffering in Creation, especially since there was an entire Division whose concern was meant to be the health and happiness of humanity, and set about a campaign to reform the Bureaucracy and End its indolence. Unfortunately, as she was so young, help and influence were in short supply for the crusading firebrand. She was largely a lone voice for over a hundred years until the Solars returned...

                          Hel has thrown her lot in with the Gold Faction with great enthusiasm. When not attending to her duties for the Division of Endings and the Convention on Healthcare, she donates her time and efforts to spreading her knowledge to those Solars who are willing to learn and caring for the Cultists who are injured in their service. And destroying zombies; there's ALWAYS time for that.

                          Notes:Hel is still a fairly young Reckoner, but, disgusted by heaven and its denizens, has spent much of her time learning and growing in Creation. She still considers Fella Hospital her true home, and its surgeon-director, Jerva, the immortal God-Blooded daughter of the Northern God of Healing, her Mentor (Jerva is powerful enough to see through her students' Arcane Fate). Jerva knows what she is, as Hel returned to finish out her residency after her initial Vizier training. As a graduation present, Jerva gifted Hel with one of Fellas' true treasures, a Resplendent Satchel of Healing.

                          Hel bears the glowing Essence tattoo of a Fellan physician, and wears a violet, silken-armor version of their distinctive uniform; she is rarely seen without her breathing mask, which aids in Resistance rolls against disease. Her surgical gloves are actually Gloves of Martial Readiness, and she carries a number of Starmetal Needles, which she has Specialties for using as both Thrown and Melee weapons. Hel's Needles can block Essence flows, inducing paralysis in living beings, and can also block Necromantic animating enegies, making them useful against the undead. They are also useful for introducing a wide range of drugs and medicines into the systems of resisting patients (like all Fellan doctors, Hel is an expert thaumaturgist, and always has a number of alchemically brewed potions and pills on hand).

                          Learning Violet Bier of Sorrows Style was mandated for Hel by her trainers; she is not fond of violence, but doesn't mind unleashing it against the hungry dead, esp supplemented with her Medicinal charms. Her preferred fighting art, however, is the Compassion-based Art of Victorious Concession, which she learned from a like-minded Lesser Elemental Dragon of Wood whose life she saved. Hel has been refining her Essence in order to learn Citrine Poxes of Contagion, but has yet to find the time and a good Sifu to instruct her in its mysteries.

                          One of Hel's Allies is the Zenith Glimmering Horizon, the chief physician of the Cult of the Illuminated. Glimmering has learned much of healing and defense from Hel, and the two are fast friends, often travelling the Scavenger Lands in each others' company on Cult business. The two can often be found in Hel's Fellan-sponsered clinic, Blissful Waters, a hotsprings resort renown for its curative waters (Wood Manse, with the Life-Sustaining power); Hel has given Glimmering Horizon its Hearthstone, a Stone of Healer's Flower. A Sobeksis called Bhangi runs and defends the place when they are gone; an Ice and Fire Binding to protect the patrons of the hospice keeps the water elemental's more unsavory nature at bay, so far. Hel also currently enjoys the effects of an Earth and Sky Bargain with a dying Serpent-and-Egg elemental, which is still recuperating inside of her.

                          Hel often walks the world wearing Resplendent Destinies from The Crow, The Rising Smoke and The Sword, and many of her patients bear Blessings that aid in their recovery. She wears her shiny black hair in a long ponytail, which many of her fellows believe is out of vanity; few know that it is almost the length it was well she saw her future-self...

                                      SELEN KRAS, Chosen of Mars

                                      Born to an average family in Lookshy, Selen grew up following the standard path of a young person in such a heavily-militarized society. Life in the Second Field Force suited her so well, that she maintained her commission, becoming full career military, steadily rising through the ranks to become a decorated officer, serving in operations that trained and fought under contract for various states throughout the Scavenger Lands, which enabled her to see many different styles of life apart from Lookshy's. Selen wanted to become an Ashigaru or Ranger for the First Field Force, but her Exaltation occurred when she clearly read the flow of Fate in the midst of battle to determine how to easily win for her side...but also saw the consequences of that victory, and willingly sabotaged it. That sacrifice of her own victory earned her exaltation, although she would not realize this for some time.

                                      Perhaps the Fivescore Fellowship never picked her up because of the Seals placed upon Lookshy by the Maiden of Battles. Selen herself has theories about the Arcane Fate that settled around her, but she mostly believes that she is under a curse of some sort by Tien Yu, the city's goddess, as punishment for throwing a battle. Somewhat bitter about this, and with friends and family no longer recognizing her, Selen easily stole a number of supplies and artifacts from Lookshy's stores and set out to pursue her own destiny.

                                      Since then, Taizei Kras has made a living going into business for herself as a soldier of fortune. She only takes jobs she's comfortable with, largely scouting and extraction missions. She prefers high-risk sorties, as they pay better, but likes pulling them off with a minimum of noise, although she's well prepared to bring it if and when necessary. Her Arcane Fate limits her interactions, but conversely has helped to spread the legend of the Mysterious Merc known only as "THE Taizei", who always comes through for her clients...IF they can find and afford her.

                                      Selen has realized by now that she's an Exalt of some sort, though of a type that she's never heard of before. She marvels at the incredibly vivid dreams she has of being in the middle of enormous battles against creatures and with weapons that she has no name for, but not fighting in them, merely recording details and occasionally intervening in small ways to direct the overall course of the conflict. These dreams have guided her in learning and developing some of her powers, but she instinctively feels that she's missing quite a bit without proper guidance.

                                      One of the most valuable charms she has gained is one that enables her to gain Acquaintances. These are a motley crew of soldiers, many of whom are sympathetic to her goals and methods from some personal loss in their own lives. Most are battle-hardened Heroic Mortals, but there are some stranger ones in her company, including Dragon-Blooded Outcastes, an Anklok Dragon King who serves as the troops Chaplain, a Mountain Folk Warrior who maintains their magitech arsenal, a Raksha Cataphract living the ideal of the ultimate macho war hero, and Spirits of various sorts (including an ancient War Ghost who advises Selen on battle tactics). Selen doesn't always work with all of these people; sometimes she performs missions alone, but usually she tailors her team to the needs of the operation.

                                      The Taizei is beginning to suspect that some of her clients, the ones who are powerful spirits especially, might actually know more of her true nature than they are saying. She has also had a recent confrontation across enemy lines with an Anathema, an encounter that is stirring something within her. She doesn't know what's happening yet, but she intends to deal with it in a straightforward fashion, like a true soldier.

                                      Notes: Selen Kras has Acquaintances 3, but the Special Heroes of her company count as Allies 4. She also has Connections 3 (Mercenaries, Lookshy Rangers), Resources 4 from her many successful missions, which has also gained her mythic persona a Reputation of 3 (though she sometimes has a hard time taking advantage of this). Her highest Ability is War, with Specialties in Guerilla Warfare and Search & Rescue; she also has the Tactical Instincts Merit. She is quite good at Archery, Melee and Brawl, as well as Athletics and Stealth, and is still learning to focus her essence into charms for such.

                                      Among the Arsenal Selen took with her from Lookshy are a Swift Rider, a set of Everyman Armor (usually in the form of a buff jacket), a very small Concussive Essence Cannon, among others. Most of her mundane gear and weapons (including such exotic weaponary as firedust grenades) are of Exceptional/Perfect quality. She also owns a set of Torcs of Unified Action, but only uses them in extraordinary circumstances, preferring to rely on her soldier's highly trained drill and camaraderie, instead.

                                      She has no access to the Loom of Fate and the Resplendent Destinies it grants, but would likely use The Banner and The Spear if she ever does.

                                          LINTHA DEWANTARA UNCLE MALIK, Chosen of Mercury

                                          Malik was born to the undistinguished Dewantara Sept of the Lintha Family. He learned about the Family and its history, goals and methods of prospering just as all the other children like him, and hoped to be the first to bring great honor to his Sept, despite his Half-Blood status. He eagerly rushed across the waves in his watershoes to scale targeted ships as a young man, plundering ships under the black and silver of the dreaded Lintha flag, until the day the Father of his ship dropped dead on the way back to Bluehaven. Impulsively taking the ships' wheel, Malik was suddenly convinced that he could somehow steer the ship safely home through the treacherous sargasso labyrinth surrounding the island...and he was right. It was this feat that awakened his Exaltation as a Harbinger.

                                          Malik was to be sorely disappointed in the reception his feat gained; few recognized it and more seemed to forget who he even was! But many powers are aware of that which is hidden among the Lintha, and those among the Cult of Dukantha knew him for what he was. At the appropriate time, Dukantha himself came forth and met with Malik, praising him and intimated that he couldn't wait until Malik joined Dukantha in the highest blessing the Great Mother could offer, the chance to become her Akuma, when the time was right. Until then, said Dukantha, Malik must not hesitate to use the gifts of the traitor Gods against those who would oppress their people; Dukantha even gave him a gift to hide him from the eyes of Heaven, that they may not seek to steal him away...

                                          Malik had always hoped to become first a Father, then a Grandfather, of the Family, and was a little put out to be promoted to Uncle instead. Still, the role gives him a chance to sail farther and wider than most Lintha, and to shore up the Family's trade and communications with the outside world through managing its far-flung network of spies and underworld connections. While Malik certainly doesn't mind piracy (and still sacks the odd ship and village, just to keep in practice), he has found his new career to be unexpectedly fulfilling.

                                          Uncle Malik has been enjoying his relative freedom so much, in fact, that he's loath to give it up, even to join Dukantha in holy union with the Great Mother. He seeks to put off the Cult of Dukantha as long as possible, by making himself as useful to the Family as possible, but sees no other way out. No one knows better how long the Lintha reach is, and even contemplating leaving the Family (to say nothing of his wives) is madness. Unless one were to go so far as Heaven itself...but would they have one such as himself? Hmm...

                                          Notes: Malik has focused development in Sail, with Specialties in Pursuing Ships and Evading Pursuit, and has most of the Sail, and some Survival, Charms available to Seers. His Lintha Backing also gives him access to the Family's large ratings in Spies, Connections (Underworld) and Resources (Malik usually deals with Lintha Dragon-Blooded sorcerers capable of remembering him). His Dark Secret is his Lintha affiliation, but one of the benefits of Arcane Fate is that that is easily covered up.

                                          As an Uncle of the Lintha, Malik captains a speedy cruiser, the Sea Slicer, which is towed by an Infernal Worm. His crew are all Acquaintances, and he has gained several Allies in Spirits of various sorts along the routes his ship travels. Since few mortals can clearly remember the ship that obviously raided them, its activities have started a legend about a "Phantom Pirate". If any West-gazing Viziers have put the signs together, they have yet to act upon them...

                                          Since Exalting, Malik has discovered that his skill with his exceptional Auhzian has greatly increased. He tries to find more time to practice the unique Lintha martial art based around it; although officially ranked as a First Kur, his skill has actually risen to about the Third. Soon, he may challenge for an even higher rank. He has also mastered Sea-Faring Hero Style up to the Form charm.

                                          Malik has been offered the chance to learn sorcery from the Cult of Dukantha, but has distanced himself from that group, so far. It could well be, however, that his current lifestyle is providing him with the experience of the Stations of Initiation, in which case he will only need to make a significant Sacrifice to unlock the power of Terrestrial Circle Sorcery within him...

                                              SIGRUN, Chosen of Jupiter

                                              In a series of cave-temples in the North dwells the Marmorean Circle, a sisterhood of Outcaste sorceresses fervently devoted to the worship of the Primordial Behemoth Eshemati, former steward of the world's secrets. Their devotion is expressed by their relentless quest to uncover and hoard the secrets of the world, that their master may reclaim its honored role from the Maiden who stole it from him. But that Maiden has many secrets and tricks of her own...

                                              Each of the 13 Marmorean Sisters is permitted to give birth only twice, one of which is to bring a new sister into the Circle. Sigrun was the product of one such birth, born and raised in the ritual traditions of this most secret society. However, she was not to exalt as the Sisters hoped, exalting as an Oracle instead.

                                              The nature of the Sidereal Exalted is one of the secrets known to the Circle, and so Sigrun knew well what she had become, and also how her elders would react to it. Deciding that she'd rather live and explore her new state and the world rather than die for Eshemati, she gathered all the lore and equipment she could, and make a break for it. She has been on the run ever since.

                                              Sigrun now wanders the Northern wilderness, letting her perception of Fate's flows take her where they will. She often appears to small villages and nomadic tribes, lending them aid and advice of varying sorts; she has saved many such groups from attacks by Hushed Ones, Ice Hollows and other such dangers, as well as helped them curry favor with local spirit courts. They can barely remember the strange woman who visited them afterwards, due to Arcane Fate, but her wanderings have inspired many legends of the "Snow Witch", although the tales are sometimes much more fanciful than the truth.

                                              Sigrun has two companions: One is a Marmorean warrior called Asmund, who tracked her down with his fellows, whom he slew when they found her, out of long-suppressed love for her. Like all such servants, he was deafened at birth and knows only the sign language the Sisters use to communicate with them, although Sigrun has been teaching him to read lips. Asmund is one of the most elite warriors of his tribe, and is a master of Falling Blossom Style martial arts, which he uses to shield Sigrun's life with.

                                              Her other boon companion is Frostfang, a Great Terror she befriended on the icy wastes. He is her Familiar and allows her to ride him across the tundra, fights the Restless Dead alongside her, and shelters her from the frozen winds and fog. She wears a hooded cloak woven from fur Frostfang willingly gave to her.

                                              The Snow Witch knows that her former sisters hunt her relentlessly, and that their reach is long. She seldom lingers in large settlements, as she almost always detects one of their servants there (or a servant of the Apheliotropes, or some other hidden conspiracy). She ponders turning herself over to Heaven, although she instinctively dislikes the notion of revealing all that the Circle taught her to their enemies. Then again, perhaps the scales would be balanced if she revealed the secrets of the Ivy Manse to the entire world at large...

                                              Notes: Even before her exaltation, Sigrun was thoroughly trained in Sorcery and Thaumaturgy, and she possesses much knowledge of both that is lost to the world, even if she has not yet learned it all. When she left, she stole many books, scrolls, codices and memory crystals, covering a great many subjects (giving her an effective Library of 5, specializing in Esoteric Subjects), and keeps them all in a Cache Egg she also liberated. When she finds the time, Sigrun studies these, mastering lore, spells and martial arts unknown to many in Creation. Thus, she either knows, or has access to, many of the secrets of Creation, both fair and foul, and the Circle will stop at nothing to regain them (nor would the Division of Secrets, if it knew about this), which gives her the Hunted Flaw.

                                              Sigrun is initiated in Celestial Circle Sorcery, her Sacrifice for this being her loyalty to the Circle. She also has the Alternative Divination merit (Oneiromancy, Sortilege) by which she reads the dreams and fortunes of those she comes across, as well as by the stars. The largely unknown White Veil Style is popular among the Marmoreans, and she has mastered its mysteries; more recently, she has taught herself Crane Style, and is looking for some way to study the secret styles of the Viziers without actually taking one on as a Sifu.

                                                  JARA THE MAGNIFICENT, Chosen of Venus

                                                  The Hundred Kingdoms sees many traveling performers, but few as spectacular as The Flying Menagerie, a collection of brightly painted wagons pulled by the green-maned horses bred by the Haltans. The mistress of the show is Haltan herself; swathed in glittery, gauzy silks, and with a long, green ponytail, Jara the Magnificent hosts the most amazing show in Creation!

                                                  Jara exalted just after her coming-of-age sojourn through the forest. Accepting a foolish dare, she went down to the forest floor for five minutes, intending to come back up, an unofficial rite-of-passage for foolhardy youth. Unfortunately, she was captured by a Faerie Noble and his entourage, who then used her to lure her friends and family down to rescue her, capturing them, as well.

                                                  The Raksha promised to free them all if Jara could entertain him with one hundred tales. She began one, shakily recounting bits of childhood stories, weaving them together with her frightened imaginings...until, suddenly, her story came to her clearly, and flowed much more smoothly. The Fae, the captives, even the forest animals seemed to hang on her every word, and all applauded when she finished. Then, the Noble asked for 99 more, and Jara knew she didn't have them in her.

                                                  But the Lord of Dreams well knew what had happened to his prey, even if she did not, and was loathe to make such quick work of so potentially fine sport, and so he struck a deal with her. They could all feed their hosts' hunger now, or Jara could go out into the world, collecting stories as amazing as the one she told, and bring them back in 10 years time. If she did this, he swore he would release her loved ones. Of course, she noticed that he made no such promise about releasing HER, but she saw that she had little choice, and so swore a binding oath to achieve this. Tearfully, she promised to come back for her friends and family, as she saw them all sealed into a crystalline pods, to marinade in their dreams.

                                                  That was 5 years ago. Jara worked hard to practice and perfect her chosen career, and took her act on the road, where she figured she could gain enough broad experience of different places and peoples that she could create new stories from. Were it not for Arcane Fate, her traveling show would be one of the most memorable and talked about of all those that wander the Hundred Kingdoms; on the plus side, it also means that she can still dazzle audiences without the need for fresh material!

                                                  Like many Haltan entertainers, she also hopes to teach others about the wonders of animals, and to inspire them to take better care of their pets and work-beasts. She supplements her income by providing veterinarian care where needed, as well as by playing up the popular image of the mystical and mysterious Haltan visitor who specializes in fortune-telling, exotic potions and charm-making.

                                                  Jara's primary concern is getting her loved ones back, and her sleep is plagued with nightmares about them, which she suspects are sent by the Fae Lord, but could just be guilt. Occasionally however, she has even more vivid dreams, of a vast, glowing city, full of spirits and such astonishing sights...She hopes that there are opportunities to explore these visions after her task is complete. In the meantime, Jara's hands are full with planning and putting on new shows, moving the Menagerie to new sites, and using all that she sees as inspiration for new and exciting tales. She has begun studying Old Realm, in an attempt to learn what those silken sutra strips drifting through her dreams actually say...

                                                  Notes:
                                                  Jara's quest is fulfilling her initiation into Sorcery, although she does not yet realize this. She has developed her Performance to a high degree, with Specialties in Storytelling, Acrobatics, Dance and Illusion, and is not above enhancing her show with her Anima Banner. She favors Dodge, Stealth and Larceny over fighting, but is also able to use her Survival, Linguistics and Socialize charms to avoid combat. She has begun teaching herself the lessons of Throne Shadow Style, and will likely end up making Shadow-Fingers of her zoo crew in time.

                                                  The Flying Menagerie is composed of all Haltan animals. The horses that pull the wagons are normal, if Fine, creatures, while the rest are Ata and San-Beasts, consisting of tree-pards, forest baboons, emerald monkeys, a colony of hatra, herons, and a large snake she sometimes does an erotic dance with for adult audiences. The Ata-beasts are all equal partners in this enterprise, and the entire troupe splits the take amongst themselves. They often scout ahead and spy on the places they visit, providing Jara with information that she seemingly couldn't know on her own, to enhance her image.

                                                  Jara has made Acquaintances of the entire troupe. Interestingly, she has also made Familiars of a couple of the Ata-beasts (a treepard who is retired from the Haltan Guard and acts as her bodyguard and the python who serves as her dancing partner and Mentor in showmanship), whose animal nature resonates with the charm despite their human-level intelligence; these have not yet become spirits, but it is only a matter of time. Jara also has a number of Allies, mainly consisting of powerful spirits she has befriended or aided who can remember her. Most of the charms she makes are junk for gullible patrons, but she is quite good as the Art of Astrology, and has the Alternative Divination merit (Crystallomancy, Palmistry); her pact with the Fae gives her a 2pt Obligation flaw, as well as the 1pt Nightmares flaw.

                                                      AZIZ, THE JACKAL PROPHET, Chosen of Saturn

                                                      Across the Southern Threshold, where the grasslands give way to desert, between the settled, civilized lands and the sands claimed by the Dune People, roam the Jackal Tribes. Composed of deformed and diseased outcasts from various Southern communities, the Jackel Tribes wander paths across the South, begging for food and supplies from villages (who just want them to move on), while worshiping the spirits of desert and disease who claim ownership over them. The Tribes meet to trade songs and information, warn each other about dangers abroad, and to trade members based on disease.

                                                      Of the few children born among them, many inherit one or more of their parent's diseases, and know no other life; with divine and thaumaturgical blessings, some of these children survive and even thrive. Aziz was one of these, infected with leprosy since before he could walk. It was at one of the plague-fairs, as he observed the ebb and flow of members based on various diseases and disfigurements, that he reached an epiphany about Destiny's design that triggered his Exaltation.

                                                      Had Aziz become any other type of Exalt, his story would likely be one of forging the aimless Jackal Tribes together into a mighty Empire of Malady, ranging across the South. But Seers rarely live out such tales. Instead, in the years that followed, he dedicated himself to steering the random courses of the wandering bands in increasingly elaborate paths that seem to form occult sigils across the lands. It is likely that only elder Exalts, such as Tamuz and Nazri, may notice this actions, or divine their meaning.

                                                      While the Jackals themselves barely remember the mysterious benefactor they term "The Jackal Prophet", the spirit courts they deal with are often powerful enough to see through his Arcane Fate, even if they don't know what precisely they are dealing with. They have witnessed his power over disease and weather, however, so they defer to his wishes, and pass his commandments along to their worshipers, giving Aziz great indirect power over his people.

                                                      Aziz perceives that a Time of Tumult has come, and his people must be ready to take advantage of it if they are to survive. He has increased the congress between the Tribes and the spirits, creating more God-Blooded descended from the disease gods (called Plague Riders) than there have ever been before, and he has also spread various means of awakening Essence throughout the Tribes, and even considers a possible alliance with the Dune People. Whether he has any higher hopes beyond survival, he keeps to himself. Traders and nomads who have had encounters with the newly invigorated Jackal Tribes have learned of his existence, although it has been distorted into a mysterious figure unifying the Tribes for war, called "The Jackal Prince". This amuses Aziz greatly.

                                                      Notes:

                                                      Aziz's Arcane Fate prevents him from directly exerting much Influence in the world; his Connections among the Jackal Tribes are through the spirit courts that they worship. He does have many Allies among various spirits and creatures of the South, many of whom owe him Favors (and a couple of whom he owes Obligations to). He resides in a massive cavern-manse that is home to a Gemlord who Aziz holds in thrall (giving him a hearthstone); those who survive its Deadly Traps assume that the deep, booming voice of the gemlord is the Jackal Prophet himself. This is also the source of Aziz's high Resources and one of his few Vices, the Essence-rich clay Ryku he so loves to smoke from a hookah.

                                                      Aziz quickly learned to use the authority his Medicinal charms gave him over disease to halt the progress of his illnesses, but even though he no longer suffers from them, the damage has been done; he has both the Disfigured and Diminished Attributes flaws, and wears the long red scarf of lepers not as a mask, but as a badge of honor. While most Jackal Tribesmen ride horses and camels, some have proven surprisingly successful at taming Simhata, mostly ridden by their God-Blooded Shaman-leaders, and Aziz always rides one when he must travel.
