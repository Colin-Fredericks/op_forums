    Originally posted by Erinys View Post
    Originally posted by One Vorlon
    Who said they were destroyed? If we look at (real world) history, they were probably carted off to adorn city plazas and the mansions of rich aristocrats. They're tall, cool looking, and they "bring good luck" - they'd be a must-have accessory for any up-and-comer
    Same question. What nutjob thought that conspicuous wealth and privilege was more important than Creation not being devoured by the Wyld? And it was either one nutjob who stole all the monoliths, or a conspiracy of hundreds of nutjobs all doing it, and...
    Who says the looters even knew what the Monoliths were really for? One noble thought the artwork looked cool, so had several shipped home (a la the Elgin Marbles). One local cult, horrified to have an Anathema artifact in their presence, smashed theirs (like ISIS is doing in Syria). A nearby town, in need of fresh stone for a construction project, carved theirs up (think "Italy"). A wannabe sorcerer, convinced the Monoliths contained mystical secrets, carted one off to his workshop . . .

    In the real world, we're actually looking at ancient monoliths (and other ruins) for guidelines on how to mark nuclear waste. You need something that will stand for 10,000 years - without constant maintenance (since you can't guarantee your civilization will be around that long). And one thing we've learned is that - if people can steal or destroy an artifact - they usually will.


    . . .But the Sidereals are on top, and successfully reprogrammed most of human society 180 degrees from what it used to be. They're social engineers on par with the Solars. They can clearly do amazing things! If they want to. . .
    That's part of the problem - the Sidereals aren't on par with the Solars. And they were never meant to be. The Solars were the ones Exalted to lead, to rule. Its why their aura banners are so drastic - they're supposed to be noticed, to be followed. Solars were generals and knight-errants, healers and scholars, diplomats and spies. And they had the charms to back it up.

    Sidereals, on the other hand, were diviners and fortune tellers, advisors and assassins. In their own way, they are just as powerful as the Solars (possibly more so), but they were "built" to hold a supporting role. When the gods created the Exaltations, the assumption was that the Solars would always be around, and (as the Exalts of the king of the gods), always in charge. So there was no point in giving anyone else strong leadership or diplomatic powers.

    So that's a big part of the reason the Sidereals have run into so much difficulty. The Dawn Caste was built to crush problems like the Balorian Crusade. It would have been the Twilight Caste's job to cure the Great Contagion. The Night Caste is supposed to be spying on the Yozi and Malfeans. And the Eclipse Caste could broker a deal between the Gold Faction and the Lunars.

    With the Solars dead, locked away, or in hiding, there's a huge "skill gap" in the Celestial Exalted community. Things are not getting done - and haven't been for centuries. Some of which the Sidereal and Lunar know nothing about. And even if they did, they don't have the power to cover for the missing Solars.

    Yet, can they trust the returning ones?

    From a starting point of the already-corrupt First Age Solar Deliberative with nobody bothering to reform it. . . .

    This isn't something I know about, probably I don't have that book. Can you please summarize what it said about Solar rule? I know the First Age Solars are vaguely supposed to have been worse than the Scarlet Empire, but I don't know of anything concrete that was actually worse. They massacred mortals with exotic weapons and Wyld explosions instead of boring ol' steel and fire, but dead is dead. How is the Scarlet Empire an improvement? They look just as tyrannical to me, but in addition they also can't or won't do jack about the Deathlords and Fae. . .
    What tends to get overlooked is that the Solars weren't merely "crazy' - they were one keg or bad breakup from destroying all of Creation. And their Deliberative wasn't corrupt in the usual sense (as in taking bribes) - any sense of "separation of powers" or "checks and balances" had been completely destroyed. So there was no "legal" means to stop them.

    The Solar castes were meant to complement each other, to form a unified whole, with some support from the other Celestial Exalted. And in the early days, that was true. But once they were in power, with all the wealth and resources of Creation behind them . . . Instead, they scorched the earth, as if five suns had suddenly appeared in the sky.

    As the centuries wore on, the vanity and indifference of the Unconquered Sun mixed with the Great Curse, egging the Solars to ever greater extremes. Dawn Castes were raising armies to settle barroom disputes. Twilights competed to see who could build the most destructive doomsday device. Eclipse members conned demon princes into serving as their footstools for all eternity. In short, at every turn, the Solars were courting massive, Creation-wide destruction.

    And no one cared! Sure, the other Exalted realized there was a problem, but then the Zenith Caste would turn on their leadership and public speaking charms . . .and all those issues would just "go away". A couple days later, the other Exalts would shake it off, and remember that they'd disagreed . . . but by then, it'd be too late.

    That's why the Bronze Faction won out. If the Gold Faction approached the Solars, and couldn't find enough who agreed with them, the Sidereal would never get another chance to fix the problem. The corrupted Solars would be forewarned, and the Sidereal would be (effectively) brainwashed by the Solars again, with Night Caste spies following them ad infinitum . . .

    But if the Bronze Faction could pull off a quick enough coup, kill enough Solars fast enough . . . Well, remember the truism - the best enemy wizard is a dead one. They can't caste a spell on you, if they're dead and buried.

    I assume that's also their reasoning, too, for why the Bronze Faction doesn't embrace the newly returned Solars. When they find out the truth about the Usurpation (and the Solars definitely have the skills to do so), will they forgive the Sidereal? And what if these new Solars end up as destructively insane as the old ones? You can't pull the same trick twice! Better to kill & re-imprison them, and try to find some other way to deal with the Fae and Deathlords.


    Edit: And if the Bronze Faction discovers that the Abyssal and Infernal Exalted are simply "modified" Solars, that will only confirm their bias. The Solars are the root of all their problems, and the only good one is a dead one.


    Edit Two: Oh, and the Scarlet Empire is "better" in the sense that its leaders aren't doing the mystical equivalent of an egg toss . . . with armed nuclear warheads. They're just oppressive and taking bribes. Creation can survive that.
