    ~Sidereal as the Martial Art Exalted~

    04-04-2014, 07:36 AM



    ~The great Anys-Syn find out what happen when you lie too much about being Creation's most peerless Martial Artist. All those bribe to the Boobs Goddess sure backfire very spectacularly~

    So yeah, Sidereal being the the MA Exalted sound extremely stupid in Second Edition, can you imagine the book claiming DB as the Having Sex Exalted (lowly Terrestrial only worthy of being an Exalted of Specialty). They have no native charm that aid them in learning or teaching MA, or charm that evoke the image of Wuxia character, and the ability to use SMA at cheaper cost only come into play at Essence 5 which mean they basically just the same as everyone in the MA apartment at low Essence .


    Not to mention the Martial Arts world in Exalted is so............theme-parked, it's like the author just watch some movies with the Wuxia tag and go on to write SotM or something. I kinda hope that they at least slip some kind of reference from some of Chinese most famous wuxia writer Jin Yong, but no, instead of Thousand Hands Buddha Palm, Divine Gate Thirteen Swords or Nine Yin White Bone Claw we got...well, Scroll of the Monk.

    And thus, I make a wish upon the Maiden that the Sidereal in Third Edition will be a red string of successes instead of failure after failure like the previous one, and someone need to make an information-dumping machine so I can transfer every movie adaption of Jin Yong novels to the writer's heads (I'm serious about Yong being the most successful wuxia writer!).

          Sidereals having a strong Martial Arts theme isn't in itself a bad thing. The problem really came in as time went on and new Charm concepts and refinements were introduced and the whole "lawl closed Charmset" thing was stubbornly adhered to, meaning that Sids' only real options were to make a Martial Art that incorporated them or completely rewrite 80% of their mechanics.

          Or, as it was, both.

          So you ended up in a situation were, as a Sidreal, you were thrice stupid if you didn't favor Martial Arts ( and Occult). First, you were stupid because that was one of the only ways to expand your power set ( I've joked about making an MA focused on punching forms to process them faster in the past). Second, you were an idiot because that was your only access to new mechanics. And third, you were a moron if you didn't take Throne Shadow, because that was the grab bag all your new things ended up in.

                          I don't think Sids' problem lay with lacking advantages when it comes to learning/using martial arts, especially not after Throne Shadow was introduced. I think the real problem was, the way things were set up, martial arts weren't a very advantageous thing in the first place. High buy-in cost, lots of redundancies, problems with utilizing multiple Styles due to different armor/weapon restrictions and the inability to use multiple Forms effectively, and the fact that SMA tended to be broken in an unfun way, created the impression that being master martial artists isn't a desirable thing.
