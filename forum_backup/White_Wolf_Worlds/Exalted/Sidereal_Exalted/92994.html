    Sidereal Taxi Driver

    03-17-2014, 06:10 PM
    So when the current campaign arc I am playing in ends we may start a Modern Shard game. Reading the drive charms I went with my gut and have decided to play a Sidereal Taxi Driver. Journeys seems like the logical caste, but I think there is merit playing a Secrets (who knows all the hidden shortcuts and destinations of the city), or a Endings (because what’s more important to a taxi driver than getting their fare to their final destination).

    I’m envisioning my Character having a radio tuned into dispatch in Yu-Shan, frequenting a diner across from union station full of a usual crowd of “Acquaintances”, and living in the penthouse above the diner (to get to Yu-Shan in our creation you will have to take the elevator to the top floor in very particular buildings, my apartment will be half way between creation and Yu-Shan, I can catch a dragon taxi off the fire escape.)

    What I’m really looking for are any charms or resplendent destiny ideas that you think could compliment this concept in a unique way. I plan on playing a more passive character, focusing on being more of a transporter, lore-master, and even oracle for the party.

          Chosen of Secrets seems like a good choice. There's a good reason cab drivers are informants in cop shows so often. They can see and hear some pretty crazy stuff, depending on where they work.

          A friend of mine knew a cab driver in New York who used to share stock tips based on phone calls he'd overheard driving businessmen around, who retired before 40. Unless a cab driver has a particularly "exotic" scent, or is the type who fancies himself a tour guide or philosopher, it's easy to forget they're there. Even if their passengers keep quiet, they can pick up a lot of info just by watching what sort of people want to go from Point A to Point B.
