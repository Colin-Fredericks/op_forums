                                    Originally posted by Drascin View Post

                                    Having tried to play a support character, this is generally sad but true. Exalted mechanics very much tend towards the selfish, and options to empower others are limited.
                                    Yes, but I feel sidereals have more tools to help them fit this role :
                                    - their anima effect is shared
                                    - Lots of charms, both in 2 and 2.5 ed :
                                    - a climate-changing charm that allows to help your allies with environmental effects
                                    - a ride charm that help find a path for you and your firends
                                    - the thrown charm that lets your ranged allies get a free reroll on their attacks
                                    - A linguistic charm that allows you to share languages with allies
                                    - the "wedding" charm that lets you take hits in place of your mate
                                    - a performance charm to improve stunt dices in a specific context
                                    - Performance also has a powerful willpower regeneration tool.
                                    - a socialize charm that allows you to "craft" a relationship of submisson between two persons (the master being one of your friends)
                                    - dodge-based defend other, and the option to use your dodge charms on their behalf
                                    - the archery pinacle charm thet lets you heal allies or grand them overdrive motes with your arrows
                                    - A melee charm that lets your "students" use your overdrive pool as if it was their own
                                    - Presence in abscence that lets you "hijack" other people's social interaction, possibly to enhace it (on a sidenote to devs : please please, keep this charm as is in 3ed. Thank you)
                                    - Sidereal shell-games or tought-swiping technique. Buff your allies by debuffing the enemy.
                                    - Avoiding the truth technique : pose as a liar to help your allies convince the target.
                                    - the stealth charm that gives arcane-fate to your friends
                                    - 2 awareness charms that help notice ambushes and unexpected attacks for your allies
                                    - integrity allows you to boost your allies' MDV
                                    - Didn't even mention medecine, sail and war since these abilites are strongly tied with working with a team
                                    - A few resplendency charms also allow you to bost or reroll social attacks/defense, boost willpower regeneration, training effects, talisman-creating effects, effects that help succeding valor rolls for people "under your command".

                                    I would also add, but it's not specifically sidereal, though probably easier for them : there is a sidereal hearthstone (rating 3 iirc) that allows to force a reroll on anyone present in the scene (and you chose which result is kept so you can screw with enemy rolls just as efficiently as protecting your allies from unfortunate rolls). It also alows you to substract health levels of damage.

                                    The fool can only learn from a sage, but only the sage can learn from a fool.
