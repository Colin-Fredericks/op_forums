    Can Sidereals just quit the Bureau of Destiny?

    10-12-2020, 12:35 AM
    I'm sorry if this has been brought up before or has an older thread. I was reading the Histories of Betrayal side bar in Fangs at the Gate and noticed the words "resigned from the Bureau of Destiny" (pg 49). I know new addition and new themes. I guess I'm too used to earlier editions ideas. Though this does seem to let Sidereal characters actually act independently of Yu-Shan without filling out paper work for time off or having working vacations. Again sorry if this has been brought up before.

      In earlier editions they were called "Ronin" Sidereals, because they abandoned their duties. They weren't hunted or anything, to my knowledge, but they also didn't have the protection or authority of Heaven. And of course, if you're a Sidereal that's quit the Bureau because you didn't agree with their goals, you might end up crossing them and facing criminal charges down the line.

      I'm not sure, but I wouldn't be surprised if we move to a less exotic term than "ronin" in Ex3.

      Edit: And, of course, corrupt gods and Sidereals might try to charge you anyway even if you've done nothing wrong. But Sidereals are precious and long-lived enough that I'd imagine the Bureau usually offers honey rather than vinegar.
