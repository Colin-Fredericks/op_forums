              Yeah. Unlike Saitama you won't be able to always win in one punch, so the onslaught setup is really good for prolonged battles. Also, Wind and Stones defense, so you can use that Onslaught penalty to increase you defenses too.

              So. From Atlhetics: Monkey Leap Technique, Increasing Strength Exercise and Thunderbolt Attack Prana (lightning speed is also in theme for a Saitama-based character, if you can spare another Charm). From Brawl: Thunderclap Rush Attack, Falling Hammer Strike, Ferocious Jab, Wind and Stones Defense, Heaven Thunder Hammer.

              Observe that this is all Essence 1, though. If you don't want to go for the likes of Adamantine/Orichalcum fists of Battle or the bunch of Charms you need for Super Saya...I mean, Ascendant Battle Visage, you may want to consider taking Resistance as your Supernal, so you can no-sell stuff with Adamant Skin Technique and Aegis of Invincible Might.

                  I don't know this show your talking about, but I assume its all about dropping people with a single punch. Here is a break down of the stuff to grab, as a basic suite of bad ass punching. You should be aware that artifact heavy armor can screw this up due to Hardness.

                  Brawl: I assume your not interested in grapples...
                  -Heaven Thunder Hammer, & Thunderclap Rush Attack are always useful for the offense, for the defense I would suggest Wind & Stones Strike used with Falling Hammer Strike for the Withering Defense but vs. decisive attacks Force Rending Strike is MUCH more likely to work.

                  Athletics charms
                  -Increasing Strength Exercise, Lightning Speed, & Thunderbolt Attack Prana, are your go to charms for closing distance or hitting harder the combination of Heaven Thunder Hammer and Thunderbolt Attack Prana can launch people stupidly far all while closing distance.

                  Example First Round Strike:
                  Starting off at Medium range with 6 initiative you move to short range, then use Thunderbolt Attack Prana and Heaven Thunder Hammer this lets you zip forward again as an attack if you hit you roll 6 dice of damage which is an average of 2.4 successes doubled to 4.8 this lets you knock him a short distance into a nearby tree making him take another 4L health levels and 7B dice of damage.

                  So he on average just took 7 bashing levels, and 4 lethal levels more than likely knocking out an Exalted without even flaring your anima unless you pumped some essence into a Brawl Excellency (Not a bad idea.)

                                  Let's be real here. For for anything approaching a complete Saitama experience you need at least Essence 5, Athletics, Brawl, Dodge, and Resistance 5 and a whole bunch of Charms in each. Probably Integrity too.

                                  That said, for an actual playable, starting character, choosing either the Dodge or Resistance defensive routes work well. Brawl is pretty friendly with other Abilities - Wind and Stones Defense also increases Evasion, for instance, which goes well with Reed in the Wind increasing your Evasion cap. If you go with Supernal Dodge as your defense and use it to accumulate Initiative for a single decisive blow, I'd try to get to at least Flow Like Blood in order to do that faster.

                                  Also, Leaping Dodge Method can help you get close enough to sniper archers to employ Thunderbolt Attack Prana
