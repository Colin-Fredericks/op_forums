        Originally posted by Accelerator View Post
        What's hardness and soak?
        [...]
        Because I think there's some confusion.
        Soak subtracts dice from Withering damage rolls (to a minimum of 1 or the weapon's Overwhelming value).
        Hardness allows you to ignore Decisive Attacks that roll equal-or-less dice of damage (but doesn't subtract from Decisive Damage, it's all or nothing).

        So if you have Hardness 6, an enemy with Initiative 6 or less cannot hope to damage your Health with a Decisive Attack that turn (but they can still Gambit against you to, e.g. set you on fire).

                                            Originally posted by TheCountAlucard View Post
                                            Actually, Arcane Fate affects Terrestrial gods, but not Celestial gods. The god of the field will forget about you, but the Ocean Father won't.
                                            To be complete: Arcane Fate affected all mortals, exalts, and most gods and elementals, excluding: all Sidereal Exalts​ and their familiars and acquaintances, all fellow functionaries of the Bureau of Destiny, all Celestial Gods, and any sundry creatures outside of fate.

                                            Characters attempting to remember a Sidereal's identity after they left their presence, had to make some kind of Wits+Integrity rolls of several successes before one failure. To save time: unimportant characters were assumed to automatically forget, while plot important characters could be assumed to remember unless notable for having low Wit+Integrity.
                                            Originally posted by TheCountAlucard View Post
                                            Also, Green Sun Princes were Outside Fate, so they weren't affected by Arcane Fate either. Whether this carries through into the new edition or not has yet to be determined.
                                            Yeap, although, Abyssals, on the other hand, were not automatically Outside Fate, unless they were in the Underworld, where everything is outside fate (by definition), or were using special charms (of the Avatar keyword). Otherwise, an Abyssal's Dark Fate merely occluded astrological tracking (well, among other related things).

                                            ​Infernals however, had to use a charm to pretend to be affected by Fate.

                                                    When you come to a deadlock, you're basically better off just rolling a contested Dex+Athletics (or whatever's appropriate -- e.g. Stamina+Athletics if swimming, Strength+Athletics if jumping from roof to roof) to see if they catch up/get away.
