    Curing the sick with the power of Schrödinger Catbox (Sidereal Healing Charms)

    11-27-2013, 09:07 AM
    I want to create a Reckoner battle medic, but Mending Warped Designs is a little too weak for my taste and Terminate Illness only work on disease so I copy-pasta from other splats Medicine charm and churn out these charms, probably OP compare to the Solar's Medicine charm since I'm not very crunch-savvy so any feedback is welcome

    Violet-Maiden's Inner Canon
    Cost: 4m (+1wp) ; Mins: Bureaucracy 5, Essence 3; Type: Simple
    Keywords: Combo-OK
    Duration: Indefinite
    Prerequisite Charms: Icy Hands
    Man's schemes are inferior to those made by heaven, and that's the reason that the best laid plans of mice and men often go awry. And it's the Sidereal Exalted's duty to take control of ineffective departments and speeding the bureaucracy wheels. This charm can only be active after a successful medical diagnosis, the Sidereal then write a prayer strip guiding the patient body on how to use their energy efficiently, the strip then dissolve, filling the room with the aroma of lavender and lilac.

    As long as the Sidereal commit essence to this charm, the patient have their natural healing rate is multiplied by her the Sidereal's (Bureaucracy) score. In addition, they also adds the Sidereal's Essence to their Tolerance for any poison currently affecting them. If the poison has no natural Tolerance it acquires one equal to the Sidereal's Essence, with an interval of one day.

    A repurchase at Bureaucracy 5, Essence 4 allows the Sidereal to apply this charm to a group of people with Magnitude equal to her Essence. When use in this way, the Sidereal does need the medical diagnose of everyone she want to affect, though the report does not need to be perform by the Sidereal herself, she merely need to know what is state of her patients are.


    Resurrection Butterfly
    Cost: 3m ; Mins: Medicine 3, Essence 3; Type: Simple
    Keywords: Combo-OK, Crippling, Compulsion, Touch
    Duration: One day
    Prerequisite Charms: Faultless Ceremony
    Recreating the butterfly's dream in its eternal slumber, the Sidereal uses this Charm on an Inactive or consenting character to impose an unnatural Compulsion to sleep for up to one day. If the Sidereal so chooses, she may make the dreams deep and clinging, imposing an internal penalty of (Essence) for any roll the target would make to wake up (for example, a Perception-based roll caused by a loud noise). This Charm automatically ends if the character takes any damage. A sleeping character also immune to any ongoing effect that currently afflicting her, such as diseases, poisons or bleeding are stalled – they are still present, but do not give her any penalties.

    This is not merely a normal sleep however, the patient's inner body become a paradoxically catbox, a dreamland of possibility. The Sidereal can perform surgery on a sleeping victim by simply touching them, such attempt is always have difficulty 1, take only one hour at most and does not inflict any damage on the patient after the operation finish. Furthermore, the Sidereal does not need tools to perform surgery on a sleeping victim but this does require her to continuously touching her patient, and thus to onlooker, it's look like the Sidereal keep holding his sickly husband and weep for hours only to have him "magically" become healthy again.


    Weeping Cherry-Willow Romance
    Cost: 3m ; Mins: Medicine 5, Essence 3; Type: Simple
    Keywords: Combo-OK, Obvious, Shaping
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Resurrection Butterfly
    "Dead bodies are buried under the cherry trees!", the poet sing "You have to believe it. Otherwise, you couldn't possibly explain the beauty of the cherry blossoms". By drawing a portrait or writing a apocalypse prose about the condition of a character currently suffering from a Crippling effect that the Sidereal can perceive within ten miles, the Sidereal ensure that something will happen that can heal the target's injuries.

    Burying the target's condition report under a blooming tree and rolling (Charisma + Performance) against a difficulty of her chosen victim’s Essence. If the roll succeed, then the target's thread in the Loom of Fate is wreathed in strange glittering pollens, this make him unnaturally attractive to spirits, such bewildered entities will always wish to repair the target injuries, what these spirits consider "healing" can be strange or even harmful for the victim however, this can mean the character may end up with a replacement eye that shine with the impossibility of the Wyld or a broken leg being heal by an Uncanny Costume demon that also want to permanently bond with him, this curse last for one month per success rolled. How he respond to such attempt is up to him, but the ST should use this opportunity to fill the story with plot-hooks that make the session more interesting.

    If the target does not wish to come under such a "blessing" by the Sidereal, then he can make (Intelligence + Lore) roll once per month for the character to gain an understanding of the curse. Rolling six successes will tell him that the source of his curse must come from somewhere within ten miles. Rolling twelve success will tell him the precise location that the Sidereal bury his condition report. The tree that act as an anchor for the target's curse always in perfect blooming even in the season of Fire, surrounding by strange will-o-wisp that humming softly in the full moon, if the condition report burying under the tree is burn or destroyed then this charm will immediately end.


    Sweet Matron's Syrup Style
    Cost: - ; Mins: Craft 4, Essence 3; Type: Permanent
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Permanent
    Prerequisite Charms:
    This charm was leaked from Venus's personal wardrobe when her vacation home "accidentally" got hit by Golden Barque of the Heavens, the Maiden of Serenity claim that this is Mercury's revenge again her for making Mercury's wine just a little stronger before the annual Carnival of Meeting and thus make the Saffron Wanderer sleep through the whole Carnival and miss her turn at the Divine Rainbow Race. Learning this charm turn the Sidereal into an cerulean apocalypse in the pantry by providing the following benefit:
    • Any Craft rolls that involve making foods, potions, teapills or decoctions adds (Essence) automatic successes at each interval.
    • Every potions or foods she make will not naturally decay or lose their magical properties, she may leave her Phoenix Soars Nine Heavens in the tundra for nine months and it'll still be fresh and editable when she come back.
    • The Sidereal immediately learns every Degree from the Art of Alchemy, at no additional xp cost, ignoring the necessary Occult ratings. If he already knew any of the Degrees, he is immediately refunded any XP he spent on them. He ignores the Willpower cost of all such rituals, and may ignore the need for specialized tools with an appropriate stunt (he must still provide material components, or obviate them through spending motes). A second purchase of this Charm increases its effects, allowing the Sidereal to reduce the casting time of any such ritual from hours to miscellaneous actions.

      Couple of caveats, first.

      Sidereals cannot, in canon 2nd Edition, create new charms. In order to use these, you would have to literally remove some charms from the charmset, and replace them with these (unless, of course, your ST allows you to ignore that part of canon; their choice). In general, a Sidereal can only create new stuff in one of two ways; firstly, they can create new Martial Arts, and secondly, they can create new Greater Astrology--which is a fun thing, but has quite a bit of setting and character consequence if your ST allows you to use it.

      I, personally, would be perfectly fine with a healing Martial Art; I know a lot of people on the forums wouldn't, but that's just me. If, however, you want to go with the other options, I would recommend changing the Medicine tree, if your ST is okay with that. Using Greater Astrology, unless your ST just pretends there are no consequences, generally means that the world is already sliding into the abyss, and any further damage you can cause by using these is minimal anyway. They're like..weapons of mass destruction, at least they're implied as similar ideals. You really don't want to use them in canon, as cool as they are.

      Violet-Maiden's Inner Canon: This charm is a prayer strip charm, therefore it needs the Prayer Strip keyword. In every case that I can remember, a prayer strip charm is always the last charm in the tree, being the pinnacle, as it were. That taken into consideration, it is also somewhat on the weak side. As comparison, consider One Direction Invocation; it has an indefinite duration, and has some very potent benefits--and penalties (make sure to look at the Scroll of Errata, that is one of those charms that's gotten errata'd, along with almost every other charm from the Sidereal's charmset). Now, copying Body-Mending Meditation is, I suppose, alright, if lackluster. What might be fun is having a charm that manipulates the fate of the wounds themselves, taking them from the person who was hurt and spreading across some volunteers, maybe. Sidereals do weird stuff like that for their charms. Just look at Shield of Destiny, for an example of that kind of weirdness.

      Resurrection Butterfly: This is a pretty cool concept, but definitely needs a new name; implying resurrection in any way is generally to be avoided (additionally, Faultless Ceremony is not a medicine charm; its a performance charm, so can't be a prereq for a medicine charm). The idea is salvageable, though; a charm that lets the sidereal perform surgery on a sleeping subject is pretty cool, and fairly fitting for Sidereals.

      Weeping Cherry Willow Romance: Um...can't generally have two Prayer Strip charms in the same charm tree, which is exactly what this is. Additionally, one should never mix abilities in splat charms; i.e. shouldn't have a Charisma + Performance roll in a medicine charm.Adding in the Crippling healing to Violet-Maiden's Inner Canon, possibly (the redone version, of course) would be better. Additionally, the Sidereals do not do...randomness. They're the static universe of General Relativity, not the randomness of Quantum Mechanics. They would never have a charm which introduces such insanity purposefully. Its more that they would fix the fate of the person so they were never crippled, or another person were crippled in their place.

      Sweet Matron's Syrup Style: I cannot think of a single canon charm that just grants degrees in any thaumaturgy; its why they have a cost to begin with. It seems to be an unworkable idea all around; perhaps it can be replaced with something that, say, allows the Sidereal to manipulate fate to provide himself and some others food for a time, for example, as a survival charm.
