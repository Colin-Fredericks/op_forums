        Originally posted by Nilihum View Post
        Hey, Robert Vance, not sure if this kind of question is in line with what the thread is for, but what was your design philosophy towards writing Craft Charms for the Dragonblooded? It's possibly the least liked system in Ex3 and I was wondering what was your approach, what you wanted to achieve, how you were informed by the Solar Craft Charms, and what your philosophy might be for the other splats looking forward down the line.
        I had plenty of time to take in the fanbase's feedback on Solar Craft Charms, and tried to use to use that constructively. I took a minimalist approach to the number of dice tricks, craft point generators, and slot generators—the more abstract effects that aren't closely coupled to what you're doing in the fiction—there need to be some, to make sure Dragon-Blooded are able to perform at a level that fits how they're depicted in the setting. I also tried to include a lot of flavorful and exciting effects, things that would stand out like Vice Miracle Technique and Dual Magnus Prana do.

                                    Originally posted by Piff View Post
                                    If I have Tiger Warrior Technique and First Knowledge Grace, can I...

                                    Train fae-eaten to become Tiger Warriors?
                                    Yeah, although they're still gonna be dream-eaten, which limits their usefulness as soldiers.

                                    Train blood apes to become Tiger Warriors?
                                    You wouldn't need First Knowledge's Grace to teach blood apes, although their natural traits may be better than the tiger warrior template in many regards..

                                    Train horses to become Tiger Warriors?
                                    There's gotta be some principle of limitation on what First Knowledge's Grace can do, and I'd definitely comfortable drawing the line at not-exceptionally-intelligent animals.

                                    Train skeletons to become Tiger Warriors?
                                    If we're talking "basically zombies," I'd say no. Skeletons with approaching-human levels of intelligence may be a different story.

                                    Train brass men to become Tiger Warriors?
                                    Maybe, although again, I'm not sure that's an upgrade.

                                    Train infants / children to become Tiger Warriors?
                                    You can train them successfully, but you're not going to overcome their innate physical limitations. Don't...don't weaponize babies.
