    It struck me as especially weird since logically the bit with Salem would be vastly more alarming. I can guess why he keeps it a secret, but still, if people had known they might be targeted by human-intelligence enemies who were siding with the Grimm, Cinder's plan in Part 4 might have failed - and assuming Ozpin's goal is to prevent mass panic, it seems completely backwards, since Salem's goal is to cause mass panic and she can release all the information she wants whenever it is most convenient for her and most damaging to Ozpin. Logically he should be revealing everyone to as many people as possible - possibly slowly and carefully, planning it out to limit the panic that results, but he's had thousands of years and his plan seems to be "lie to everyone, hide everything, hide Salem's existence because it would help her if people knew about her, hope Salem cooperates with this plan."

    From a meta reason I assume it's because the viewers already knew about Salem but didn't know about the bird thing, so it was more shocking and the writers wanted to make it the focus. But logically I would expect Yang or Juane to put 2 + 2 together and realize that Pyrrha died, in part, because she didn't have enough information and that Ozpin is therefore directly to blame for what happened. (ie. if she'd known exactly what the Maidens were and were capable of, known who they worked for, etc, things might have gone differently, and Ozpin doesn't really have a justification for keeping all these vital things a secret for so long.)
