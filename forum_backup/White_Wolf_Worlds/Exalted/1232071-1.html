                                                07-05-2018, 01:41 PM
                                                In the fiction where Theion (now Malfeas) created the Unconquered Sun, it stated that Theion was impressed because TUCS was greater then his creator. If Sol Invictis was greater then Theion, he's most assuredly stronger then Malfeas, who like the other Yozi are less then they once were. I would assess that Luna would be fairly close to TUCS in power, with the Maidens only bit behind her. So in this theoretical brawl between the Incarnae and the Yozi, I feel like the Yozi would really dislike the results of such an engagement.

                                                Having said that, I don't see such a battle as feasible at all. The geas that prevents the gods from harming the Primordials/Yozi can't be removed. It was built into then. If it could be removed, they would have done it long ago. Even after the Yozi were imprisoned, all the Exalts combined (Solars with 3rd Circle Sorcery, the Lunars and Sidereals, and close to a million Dragonbloods) still didn't remove the geas. Even with the Yozi imprisoned, there are a lot of good reasons to remove the geas, but they still didn't. So I don't see the gods ever being able to harm a Yozi, and considering their power, I don't see the Yozi directly attacking them unless their lives depended on it (which their lives will never depend on it as the Gods can't harm them). It's a circle of reasoning that means they'll never engage each other directly. This is my own interpretation though, so perhaps others have a different viewpoint.
