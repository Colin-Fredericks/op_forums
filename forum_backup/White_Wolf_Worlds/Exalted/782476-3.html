    Here's the next two abilities. May be a day or two before I get the next few sorted as they (Occult, Performance and Presence) are mostly incomplete at this point.
    Medicine Smooth Transition
    Cost: 2m; Mins: Medicine 2, Essence 1; Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: None
    A living creature, who is willing to die, can be killed instantly and painlessly. The creature's body may not be raised as a zombie, nor may their spirit be raised as a ghost. A body that lies on solid ground when this charm is used sinks into the ground and the body will not be scavenged by animals. If used against a zombie then it collapses instantly and buries itself. Against more unusual corpse creations roll (Charisma + Medicine) against a difficulty of the creator's (Essence) to destroy them.

    Peaceable Conclusion
    Cost: -; Mins: Medicine 3, Essence 1; Type: Permanent
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Permanent
    Prerequisite Charms: Smooth Transition
    If Smooth Transition is used to kill someone who was dying of a disease or poison then the Sidereal learns what that poison was and gains +1 non-charm success when treating that specific disease or poison in the future. By using Smooth Transition on the victims of many diseases the Sidereal may build up a catalogue of diseases and poisons which she finds easier to treat.

    Invocation of the Storm Following Violence
    Cost: - (+3m, 1wp); Mins: Medicine 3, Essence 2; Type: Permanent
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Permanent
    Prerequisite Charms: Smooth Transition
    By paying a surcharge of three motes and one willpower when activating Smooth Transition the Sidereal may affect all those within an area out to short range.
    If this charm is used against a zombie battlegroup the Sidereal rolls (Charisma + Medicine) with +(Essence) non-charm successes against a difficulty of the creator's (Essence). Threshold successes on the roll are inflicted as points of magnitude damage on the battlegroup.

    Deferred Wounds
    Cost: 3m, 1wp; Mins: Medicine 4, Essence 2; Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: Stackable, Decisive Only
    Duration: (Wits + Medicine) hours
    Prerequisite Charms: None
    The Sidereal can push one level of damage (Wits + Medicine) hours into the future. This effect may be stacked up to (Essence) times, but doing so does not extent the duration, instead all the health levels are deferred until the same point as the first activation of this charm. At the end of the charm all the wounds come back as they were when the charm was activated adding on to existing damage as normal.

    Terminate Illness
    Cost: 10m; Mins: Medicine 4, Essence 2; Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Peacable Conclusion
    Roll (Intelligence + Medicine) against a difficulty that varies based on how far advanced the target's symptoms are, success cures the target and allows them to begin to recover. If the victim is suffering from minor symptoms then the difficulty is (Virulence), if the victim is suffering from major symptoms then the difficulty is (Virulence + Mortality) and if the victim is suffering from defining symptoms then the difficulty is (Virulence + [2 * Mortality]).

    Earth and Sky Bargain
    Cost: 10m, 1wp, 1lhl; Mins: Medicine 5, Essence 3; Type: Simple
    Keywords: Prayer Strip
    Duration: Indefinite
    Prerequisite Charms: Invocation of the Storm Following Violence, Deferred Wounds, Terminate Illness
    This charm summons a dying spirit to the Sidereal and offers them a bargain. If they refuse then they die during that scene. If the spirit accepts then their essence is absorbed by the Sidereal who gains +(Essence) soak and an extra [-0] health level. This charm's effects may be passed on to another beneficiary by touching them. When this charm ends the spirit is reborn, slightly changed and no longer on the verge of death.
    Melee
    Meditation on War
    Cost: 3m; Mins: Melee 2, Essence 1; Type: Supplemental
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: None
    The Sidereal may convert up to the lower of (Essence) or (Weapon's Accuracy) dice into successes before the attack pool is rolled.

    Impeding the Flow
    Cost: 3m; Mins: Melee 2, Essence 1; Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: None
    Increase the target number of an attack targeting the Sidereal by one.

    Harmony of Blows
    Cost: 4m, 1wp; Mins: Melee 3, Essence 1; Type: Simple
    Keywords: Decisive Only
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Meditation on War
    Make up to the lower of Dexterity or 3 decisive attacks. Initiative must be split between them, with a minimum of 3 initiative going into each attack. The attacks may be against one or multiple enemies and may be made using any combat ability. The Sidereal's initiative does not reset until all attacks have been made. This charm may be combined with other abilities.

    Smiling at the Damned
    Cost: 3m, 1wp; Mins: Melee 4, Essence 2; Type: Supplemental
    Keywords: Decisive Only, Aggravated
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Harmony of Blows
    After the raw damage of the attack has been compared to hardness the Sidereal halves the damage pool (round up) and makes any damage dealt Aggravated.

    Serenity in Blood
    Cost: 8m, 1wp; Mins: Melee 4, Essence 2; Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Impeding the Flow
    Double the Sidereal's Parry rating, including weapon bonus but ignoring all penalties, against a single attack. This charm also allows the Sidereal to apply their parry rating against unblockable effects. This charm explicitly allows the Sidereal to break their dice cap, but may not be combined with other charms that add to their Parry rating.

    Perfection of the Visionary Warrior
    Cost: 10m, 1wp, 1lhl; Mins: Melee 5, Essence 3; Type: Simple
    Keywords: Prayer Strip
    Duration: One scene
    Prerequisite Charms: Smiling at the Damned, Serenity in Blood
    While this charm is active unexpected attacks do not reduce the Sidereal's defense against them. Additionally she may make a withering counterattack once per turn using her Melee, Brawl, MA, Archery or Thrown dice pools (assuming she has an appropriate weapon readied).

        Occult
        Mark of Exaltation
        Cost: 5m; Mins: Occult 1, Essence 1; Type: Simple
        Keywords: None
        Duration: One scene
        Prerequisite Charms: None
        Spirits within short range of the Sidereal using this charm become visible to everyone, even if dematerialized. This does not make them any easier to interact with physically however. Additionally the Sidereal gains +1 non-charm success on Occult, Presence, Performance, Bureaucracy and Socialize rolls when dealing with spirits or within Yu-Shan.

        Tell Tale Symphony
        Cost: 4m; Mins: Occult 3, Essence 1; Type: Reflexive
        Keywords: None
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisite Charms: Mark of Exaltation
        The Sidereal can sense nearby spirits (even if they are immaterial), permanent magical effects/objects and sorcery. A successful (Intelligence + Occult) roll against difficulty 3 allows the Sidereal to identify details about the enchantment or the rank and job of a detected spirit. Alternatively the Sidereal gains details about the local geomantic environment.

        Incite Decorum
        Cost: 2m; Mins: Occult 2, Essence 1; Type: Simple
        Keywords: None
        Duration: One scene
        Prerequisite Charms: None
        Unless the Sidereal is the one to initiate hostilities then the spirit must pay (Essence/2, round up) Willpower in order to roll Join Battle against the Sidereal.

        Innocuous Maneuver
        Cost: 6m; Mins: Occult 3, Essence 2; Type: Reflexive
        Keywords: Mute
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisite Charms: Tell Tale Symphony, Incite Decorum
        This charm gives the target spirit a positive minor tie towards the Sidereal of the Sidereal's choice.

        Unweaving Method
        Cost: 5m, 1wp; Mins: Occult 4, Essence 2; Type: Simple
        Keywords: Decisive Only, Aggravated
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisite Charms: Innocuous Maneuver
        A decisive attack targeted with (Perception + Occult) that does not reset the Sidereal's initiative. The attack does (Essence) + threshold successes aggravated damage to a single target within short range. If the target is killed they are completely unmade, their soul not reincarnating. This charm does not affect the dead.

        Willing Assumption of Chains
        Cost: 10m, 1wp, 1lhl; Mins: Occult 5, Essence 3; Type: Simple
        Keywords: Prayer Strip
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisite Charms: Unweaving Method
        A god signs the prayer strip and is instantly given a new office end responsibilities as decided by the Sidereal. Prudence suggests that the Sidereal pick suitable candidates for new posts. Likewise a prudent god asks what the duty they will be accepting is. This charm only works if the god signs of their own free will and threatening a god to make them sign is a serious crime in Yu-Shan.

        Terrestrial Circle Sorcery
        Cost: -; Mins: Occult 3, Essence 1; Type: Permanent
        Keywords: None
        Duration: Permanent
        Prerequisite Charms: None
        The Sidereal steps through the First Circle and is forever transformed. Upon learning this Charm, the Exalt gains the ability to learn and cast spells from the Terrestrial Circle of sorcery. In addition, the Sidereal learns one shaping ritual (p. 466) and one Terrestrial spell for free, the spell selected becomes the sorcerer’s “control spell".

        Celestial Circle Sorcery
        Cost: -; Mins: Occult 4, Essence 3; Type: Permanent
        Keywords: None
        Duration: Permanent
        Prerequisite Charms: Terrestrial Circle Sorcery
        The Sidereal ascends to the Second Circle and becomes an avatar bearing aspects of Heaven and Hell in her mind. In all the world, there are only a handful of sorcerers at this level of power. Upon learning this Charm, the Exalt gains the ability to cast spells from the Celestial Circle of sorcery. In addition, the Solar learns one shaping ritual and one Celestial spell for free. The spell selected acts as an additional control.
        Performance
        Compassionate Essence Replenishment
        Cost: -; Mins: Performance 1, Essence 1; Type: Permanent
        Keywords: None
        Duration: Permanent
        Prerequisite Charms: None
        Whenever the Sidereal successfully influences a large group with Performance she gains (Essence) motes. This bonus may only be received once per scene.

        Perfection in Life
        Cost: 7m; Mins: Performance 2, Essence 1; Type: Supplemental
        Keywords: None
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisite Charms: None
        When making a performance-based social influence every person whose Resolve is beaten by the social influence roll gains one temporary willpower. This may take someone above their permanent willpower, but excess willpower disappears at the end of the scene. This chamr may only be used once per scene and individuals may only benefit from the bonus willpower once per day.

        Defense of Shining Joy
        Cost: 5m; Mins: Performance 4, Essence 2; Type: Reflexive
        Keywords: Perilous, Uniform
        Duration: One turn
        Prerequisite Charms: Perfection in Life
        For the duration of the charm the Sidereal only suffers half the effect of onslaught penalties (round down) and gains 1i for every attack that is successfully avoided.

        Song of Spirit Persuasion
        Cost: 5m, 1wp; Mins: Performance 3, Essence 2; Type: Simple
        Keywords: None
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisite Charms: Perfection in Life
        Roll (Manipulation + Performance) against a difficulty of the higher of a spirit's (Resolve) or (Essence). Success on the roll makes the spirit an Ally (as per the Merit) until the end of the story. If treated well the spirit may become a permanent Ally. Spirits of Earth, Water or Air association increase the difficulty of the roll by 2. Usable once per story.

        Faultless Ceremony
        Cost: 4m; Mins: Performance 3, Essence 1; Type: Simple
        Keywords: None
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisite Charms: None
        At the end of a ceremony in which the Sidereal participates roll (Manipulation + Performance) against a difficulty of (8 - resources spent on the ceremony). Success on the roll gives all participants in the ceremony a speciality dice on all of their efforts for a week.

        Heart-Brightening Presentation Style
        Cost: 3m; Mins: Performance 3, Essence 2; Type: Reflexive
        Keywords: None
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisite Charms: None
        The Sidereal may substitute their Bureaucracy, Presence or Socialize with their Performance in any one roll or derived value. This may even be used to roll Performance instead of the associated skill in a charm that requires a specific roll.

        Harmonic Completion
        Cost: 16m, 1wp, 1lhl; Mins: Performance 5, Essence 3; Type: Simple
        Keywords: Prayer Strip, Psyche
        Duration: Five days
        Prerequisite Charms: Defense of Shining Joy, Song of Spirit Persuasion, Faultless Ceremony, Heart-Brightening Presentation Style
        For the duration of the charm the Sidereal becomes blind (as per the flaw). All performance-based social influences inspire love/compassion (Sidereal's choice) in observers on top of their normal effect. The inspire action uses the successes of the social influence roll to determine it's success. Additionally any instill action to create or strengthen an intimacy of love/compassion converts (Essence) dice to successes before rolling.
        Presence
        Heroic Essence Replenishment
        Cost: -; Mins: Presence 1, Essence 1; Type: Permanent
        Keywords: None
        Duration: Permanent
        Prerequisite Charms: None
        Whenever the Sidereal successfully influences a large group with Presence she gains (Essence) motes. This bonus may only be received once per scene.

        Presence in Absence Technique
        Cost: 4m; Mins: Presence 2, Essence 1; Type: Reflexive
        Keywords: Stackable
        Duration: Indefinite
        Prerequisite Charms: None
        This charm may be activated after a successful instill action. The results of the instill action do not come into effect until the Sidereal decides. This means that no-one other than the Sidereal may interact with the 'new/altered' intimacy, not even the target.
        This charm may be stacked up to (Essence) times on a target allowing for multiple changes to be made without the subject being aware until the Sidereal decides. This allows for the Sidereal to hide complete reversals in personality until the time of their choosing by creating a new Defining Intimacy and/or degrading existing intimacies before releasing them onto the target later.

        Impose Nature
        Cost: 3m; Mins: Presence 3, Essence 1; Type: Supplemental
        Keywords: None
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisite Charms: None
        If the Intimacy that is being instilled is one that the Sidereal has (at major or defining) then the instilled intimacy counts as being one level lower for the purposes of requiring supporting intimacies.

        Force Decision
        Cost: 6m; Mins: Presence 4, Essence 2; Type: Supplemental
        Keywords: None
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisite Charms: Impose Nature
        A social influence supplemented with this charm costs an extra willpower to deny/resist.

        Loyalty-Sacrificing Sidestep
        Cost: 5m; Mins: Presence 3, Essence 2; Type: Reflexive
        Keywords: None
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisite Charms: Presence in Absence Technique
        This charm may be activated by the Sidereal after their Resolve has been beaten by a social influence. It allows them to redirect the resolution of the social influence onto someone else present at the scene. The new target of the social influence must have a positive tie towards the Sidereal. In addition to the effects of the social influence one positive tie towards the Sidereal is weakened by one level.

        Vizier's Portentous Advice
        Cost: 15m, 1wp 2xp; Mins: Presence 5, Essence 3; Type: Simple
        Keywords: Prayer Strip
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisite Charms: Force Decision, Loyalty-Sacrificing Sidestep
        This charm can be used to permanently enhance a mortal. The mortal gains a personal Essence pool of five motes, the Exalted Healing Merit and gains +1 dot in an Attribute of the Sidereal's choice. If the mortal has Integrity 3+ then they get a charm called Providential Determination which allows them to reflexively spend five motes of Essence instead of a willpower. A Sidereal may enhance up to (Essence), different, mortals at a time and if any mortal dies then the experience spent on them is refunded.

                    So. I know this is a bad idea as I have yet to finish the base trees for this conversion project. But I couldn't help getting some ideas for things that might be fun to include, or to expand the various trees.

                    Note that many of the names are WIP or placeholder, several were taken from the wiki (because I thought they sounded good) others I have made up myself. Ideas for cool sounding sidereal charm names will always be welcome, and may spark an idea too.

                    Archery
                    Hawk Feathered Arrow- The arrow turns into a hawk allowing the Sidereal to survey their surroundings through the hawks eyes.
                    Splitting the Arrow Shot- If crash an opponent may immediately make a decisive follow-up.
                    Earth Like Air Trick- Allows an attack to be made unexpected with a successful opposed check. The target suffers penalties based on the amount of cover they have from the Sidereal.
                    Cracked Shell Divinations- Soak piercer that improves at high initiative.
                    Bolts of Starlight- Supplements an attack allowing the Sidereal to shoot at medium range without aiming.
                    Arms of the Crimson Panoply- Reflexively make a draw/ready weapon action and for the rest of the scene count as having the Extra Limbs merit. (Have 2 extra hands to hold onto things)
                    Stinging Incitation Strike- If threshold successes are equal to or greater than target's Resolve, they must make a rush action with you as the target next turn. May be combined with other abilities.
                    Volley of Legions- Add (Essence) to raw damage of a withering attack. The shot hits everyone within close range of the target. The Sidereal gains initiative based only on the highest damage dealt.

                    Athletics
                    Sky Touching Toad Technique-                     The air can support the Sidereal for one turn.
                    Strength of Five Prana- The Sidereal may increase her strength by up to (Essence). The combined total may not go above 6.
                    Passing Army Disturbance- May pass through a battlegroup without spending initiative. Battlegroups must roll initiative to successfully Engage her.

                    Brawl
                    Violet Murder Venom-                     Coats the Sidereal's attack in a poison. May be combined with other abilities.
                    Room Emptying Murderer- A fear effect that causes lesser opponents to flee.
                    Numb as the Dead Technique- Activate to make an attack not reduce the turns of control in a grapple.
                    Unrelenting Flesh Method- Increase soak/hardness whilst in a grapple.

                    Craft
                    Inspirational Works Technique-                     May attach a social influence onto a crafted item. The influence fades over time.
                    Feathers of Beautiful Seeming- Increase the Sidereal's Appearance by 1.

                    Dodge
                    Auspicious Reversal-                     Initiative lost when making unsuccessful decisive attacks against the Sidereal increase the Sidereal's initiative.
                    Cooperative Defense Technique- Improves defense if the Sidereal and another are both defending each other.
                    Pursuit of Love- Convert (Intimacy) dice to successes on a rush/disengage roll. The Intimacy must be one of love.

                    Larceny
                    Words Without Words Deception-                     Take no penalties to a social influence based on non-verbal communication to communicate a lie or untruth.
                    Gambler's Den Method- The Sidereal always has a hideout nearby to retreat to.

                    Linguistics
                    Cunning Editor Reading-                     Increase Resolve against written social influences.

                    Lore
                    Sifu in the Dojo-                     When teaching, students are unusually attentive and learn more quickly.

                    Melee
                    Swift Pressed Attack-                     A multi attack that applies onslaught penalty for the attacks upfront from the first attack.
                    Thousand Weapon Training- Reflexively ready/draw a weapon.

                        So after most of a week off for the holidays, here are two more abilities to look at. Not too many more to go now
                        Resistance
                        Ox-Body Technique
                        Cost: -; Mins: Resistance 1, Essence 1; Type: Permanent
                        Keywords: Stackable
                        Duration: Permanent
                        Prerequisite Charms: None
                        The bodies of the Exalted are much more durable than those of mere mortals. To help simulate this, an Exalt may buy extra health levels with this Charm. The purchasing choices are based on the character’s Stamina rating:
                        - At Stamina 1 and 2: One -1 health level.
                        - 3 and 4: One -0 health level.
                        - 5: One -0 and one -1 health level.
                        The Sidereal may purchase Ox-Body Technique (Resistance) times. If she increases her Stamina after purchasing Ox-Body Technique, her health levels automatically change to reflect the new rating.

                        Red Haze
                        Cost: 5m, 1wp; Mins: Resistance 1, Essence 1; Type: Simple
                        Keywords: None
                        Duration: One Scene
                        Prerequisite Charms: Ox Body Technique
                        Gives a single battlegroup of up to Size (Essence), +1 to it's Might when fighting against the Undead, Demons or Fae. The effect can be stacked by multiple Sidereals using this charm on a battlegroup (up to Might 3), but may not be used multiple times on a battlegroup by a single Sidereal.

                        Optimistic Security Practice
                        Cost: 5m; Mins: Resistance 2, Essence 1; Type: Reflexive
                        Keywords: Perilous, Withering Only
                        Duration: One turn
                        Prerequisite Charms: None
                        Gain +(Essence) soak and a defensive pool of (Resistance) Initiative for one round. Any withering damage steals these temporary points before spilling over to damage the Sidereal’s own Initiative. They are not added to decisive attacks nor can they be used for any other purpose besides taking withering damage.

                        Someone Else's Destiny
                        Cost: 3m; Mins: Resistance 2, Essence 1; Type: Reflexive
                        Keywords: Perilous
                        Duration: Indefinite
                        Prerequisite Charms: None
                        Instead of rolling to resist a poison the Sidereal traps it in a loop of fate. The poison is the applied to her next successful decisive attack, which uses up the poison in her system and ends the charm. Using this charm a second time whilst a poison is already trapped reintroduces the first poison into her system. She must now roll to resist the poison as normal.
                        At Essence 3+ this charm gains the Stackable keyword. Allowing the Sidereal to use this charm to hold up to (Essence) poisons in limbo at a time. Activating this charm again allows the Sidereal to chose which poison gets reintroduced into her system.

                        Shield of Destiny
                        Cost: 6m; Mins: Resistance 3, Essence 2; Type: Reflexive
                        Keywords: Perilous, Decisive Only
                        Duration: Indefinite
                        Prerequisite Charms: Optimistic Security Prana, Someone Else's Destiny
                        This charm may be activated after damage has been rolled on a decisive attack. Roll (Resistance + Stamina), for every 2 successes on the roll reduce the damage dealt to the Sidereal by the attack by one health level, up to a maximum of the damage dealt by the attack. The damage removed by this charm is then stored and may be used by the Sidereal to enhance a decisive attack. The damage is a separate pool, so isn't added to the pool to determine whether the attack gets past the target's hardness. The held damage pool ignores hardness and has the same damage type as when it was inflicted on the Sidereal. The Sidereal may have up to (Essence) of these held damage pools at a time, but may only use one to enhance a given decisive attack.

                        Water and Fire Treaty
                        Cost: 5m, 1wp, 1hl; Mins: Resistance 3, Essence 1; Type: Permanent
                        Keywords: Stackable
                        Duration: Indefinite
                        Prerequisite Charms: Ox Body Technique
                        The Sidereal gains +(Resistance) soak and hardness against damage from one elemental source, chosen when the charm is initiated. This charm may be stacked up to (Essence) times, but each use of the charm must chose a different element to protect against.

                        Water and Fire Legion
                        Cost: - (+2m/person); Mins: Resistance 3, Essence 2; Type: Permanent
                        Keywords: None
                        Duration: Permanent
                        Prerequisite Charms: Water and Fire Treaty, Red Haze
                        By paying a surcharge of two motes per person, the Sidereal may include others in her activation of Water and Fire Treaty. The Sidereal may bestow the benefits on to up to (Essence) others.

                        Heartless Maiden Trance
                        Cost: 8m, 1wp; Mins: Resistance 4, Essence 3; Type: Simple
                        Keywords: None
                        Duration: Indefinite
                        Prerequisite Charms: Shield of Destiny
                        The Sidereal halves wound penalties (round down) and may ignore penalties from fatigue, exhaustion and armour. Poisons and diseases pause their effects whilst the charm is active and the Sidereal needs no sleep, food, drink or to breathe. The Sidereal may not be rendered unconscious while this charm is active and does not respire Essence except through charms. When this charm is ended any effects that where paused, ignored or added to the Sidereal during the charm kick in at the point they were at when she activated the charm.

                        Storm's Eye Stance
                        Cost: 6m, 1wp; Mins: Resistance 5, Essence 3; Type: Simple
                        Keywords: Prayer Strip, Dual
                        Duration: One scene
                        Prerequisite Charms: Heartless Maiden Trance, Water and Fire Legion
                        The Sidereal may launch counterattacks against up to (Essence) targets for 2 motes per target. A battlegroup may be targeted up to (Size) times by a single counterattack. The counterattack type is the same as the attack which it is countering and has a base damage equal to that of the attack the Sidereal is countering. Extra successes do not add to the base damage of withering attacks and the Sidereal's initiative is not factored into the damage of decisive attacks. A decisive counterattack created by this charm does not reset the Sidereal's initiative.
                        Ride
                        Ordained Bridle of Mercury
                        Cost: 10m, 1wp; Mins: Ride 2, Essence 1; Type: Simple
                        Keywords: None
                        Duration: Instant
                        Prerequisite Charms: None
                        This charm summons a creature from the area to become the Sidereal's Familiar. The creature summoned is a two-dot Familiar and is usually a riding creature of some kind. By spending a surcharge of +1 willpower the player may choose the species of the new Familiar.

                        Breaking the Wild Mortal
                        Cost: 10m, 1wp; Mins: Ride 3, Essence 1; Type: Simple
                        Keywords: Mute
                        Duration: Instant
                        Prerequisite Charms: Ordained Bridle of Mercury
                        The Sidereal gains a dot in the Acquaintances merit for this area. This increase fades at the end of the story unless the Sidereal makes an effort to maintain the connection.

                        Yellow Path
                        Cost: 2m; Mins: Ride 1, Essence 1; Type: Simple
                        Keywords: None
                        Duration: Instant
                        Prerequisite Charms: None
                        The shortest path to the Sidereal's destination is illuminated by a glimmering yellow light. The path is not necessarily safe, but all obstacles on the way should be surmountable by the Sidereal.
