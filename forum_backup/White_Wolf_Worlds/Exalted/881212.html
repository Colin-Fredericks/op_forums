    SOLARS DEMAND A CONTEXT! (forum game)

    05-03-2016, 04:21 PM
    This is a game where a person writes an out of context quote and the next post gives it a context for the Exalted universe. Now the second post puts up another quote to which a third person will try to place into Creation/Malfeas/the Underworld/Yu Shan/etc and put up yet another quote, and so on so for.

    Example:
    Post A: 'To be or not to be - that is the question'

    Post B: A chosen of serenity deciding if a love affair between a noble and a fishmonger should proceed.
    'IF YOU DESTROY THE BRIDGE, YOU'LL NEVER SEE HER AGAIN!'

    Post C: A Solar is being taunted by the Mask of Winters as the former has only one chance to destroy the Death Lord, by using an evocation to incinerate a bridge with both the Exalt's true love and the undead tyrant. And the bridge was built over Oblivion, because Neverborn!
    'I wanted to change the world too, but I changed nothing'

    Some rules before we start:
    1) Quote can be from anywhere; history, fiction or just an old proverb. Just not from Exalted literature or any game of Exalted.
    2) Do not reveal the source of the quote until some has given it context for Exalted by another poster. You may reveal the source at any later date.
    3) The quote must be applicable to the Exalted universe, no terms or place names that do not fit into Creation or are specific to reality/a particular fictional universe; for example both America and Gotham city cannot be used.
    4) Keep it SFW. No explicit quotes or quotes from material intended for mature audiences. So nothing by E. L. James, the Memoirs of Casanova or the like.

    First quote:
    'A homemade Man? I don't understand?'
