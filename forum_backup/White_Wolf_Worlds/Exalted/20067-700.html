                                        Originally posted by Epitome View Post
                                        So another "winging it" question. How would you handle disengage rolls with creatures of Legendary Size?
                                        If the combatants' exact position on the battlefield isn't relevant, I'd still call for the Legendary Size creature to roll disengage. In that case, failing the disengage would mean, not that it failed to move away from its opponents, but rather that it failed to prevent them from keeping pace with it. When battlefield position does matter (e.g. the smaller fighters are defending a location and don't want to leave it), I think I'd rule that the Legendary Size creature can just auto-succeed on the disengage roll.

                                        Alternatively, another house rule could be that everyone contesting a disengage gets the option, after winning the contest, to choose to either stop the disengaging character or reflexively move with them. For Legendary Size creatures, then, I'd rule that the only option, on winning the contest against one, would be to follow along, and if you chose not to do that, you'd effectively forfeit the contest.
