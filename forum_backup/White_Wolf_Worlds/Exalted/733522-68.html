                                                    My objection is that can learn and did learn are very different things. I went to a major agriculture university! That means I know lots of stuff about plants and animals and growing food, right? Wrong, because that wasn't my major. I could have learned agri stuff, but I didn't learn agri stuff.

                                                    The garden can teach a bunch of things. But as I read the working it doesn't teach everything at once. If it does teach everything at once, then the working needs to be reassessed again.

                                                    And this is why I always start conversations about workings with intent. Because "garden that teaches people" sounds like a means to an end rather than an end in and of itself. And without knowing that desired end, all we can really do is blindly guess what the actual effect of the working is.
                                                    Trying to shave a month off your Solar XP spending? Trying to make an entire backwater village into professional scribes? Eh, no biggie.
                                                    Trying to have a legion of scholar-bodyguards who know everything taught in any school anywhere? Pretty big deal even if you're providing the people.
