    The second manga series (manga, not anime) of Rosario Vampire. No, really. It takes a while for the action to begin, but by the third volume or so you've got a plethora of beautifully drawn fight scenes between various humanoid monsters, all of which slot very easily into Exalted roles- the Akashiya family of vampires, in particular, are exemplars of Abyssal conduct. And the scene where Kurumu cures ghoulification through the Power Of Love (tm) perfectly illustrates how a Chosen of Serenity would Exalt.
    On the musical side of things, "Kings and Queens" and "This Is War" (by 30 Seconds to Mars) always get me in a suitably epic mood.
    Mirror's Edge and Dishonoured are games for the Night Caste in all of us.
    The comic Digger provides excellent inspiration for how the Immaculate Order interacts with spirits and demons.
    Also, has anyone here read this blog? http://tentacledvitriol.wordpress.co...movie-Mondays/

                                            Oh, also, I believe I've mentioned it in other threads, but not here.

                                            Seirei no Moribito is a wonderful example of how Heroic Mortals work, all around - Balsa is my go-to example of what a Heroic Mortal looks like. Pretty much the only Exalt in the entire thing is probably Torogai, but still it probably fits Exalted better than a great many supposedly fitting inspirations. And pretty much any ST can learn of how the author builds the cultures and myths. She's an anthropologist and it rather shows.

                                            That said, it tends to approach moral quandaries from the opposite direction Exalted, being a White Wolf game, usually does.
