    My understanding is that essence manipulation is tied into states of consciousness. You can't trial and error your way into magic, as the results of your experiments are largely dependent on the subjective "enlightenment" you feel at the time. If magic requires some variety of religious experience to function it's much more productive to approach magic with an air of spiritualism. And while you could make a case that this still technically allows for empiricism, it makes perfect sense that Creation's guides to achieving emotional states are going to look more like sutras than psych textbooks.

                                        Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post
                                        Going back to the original premise of this thread, I feel as though there are more interesting ways to articulate massive quantities of Essence than expressing them as a kilomote (in particular terms for elaborate geomantic networks to harness them), and more interesting things to focus on in the expenditure of such Essence than effects on the Loom of Fate.
                                        Metric seems wrong for Exalted anyway. You would have, like, a hogshead of Essence.

                                        This isn't as frivolous a distinction as it appears to be! Classical measurements tend to reflect the origins of counting and measurement as worldly arts, so something might involve "as much essence as X", where X is, I don't know a demesne or a god or a person or whatever. Thinking of the praxis of actual Exalted, I could imagine them measuring Essence in relation to respiration.

                                        There's also been some suggestion in the past that the "flavour" of Essence matters, e.g. that Solar Essence is fundamentally different from Lunar Essence in ways beyond quantity, which seems appropriate and further complicates the picture.

                                        Ultimately, I think the big problems with this perspective come from the linkage between motes-as-game-abstraction and motes-as-setting-construct. I used to be super into the setting/mechanics linkage of Exalted, and I still like it in principle, but that sort of thing can lead to unhappy compromises. It doesn't really matter if Essence is quantized somehow, since lots of things are quantized even when they appear continuous to us. But presenting the quantum of Essence as being huge and relevant to the daily activities of Essence-users raises its visibility and relevance. In real life I can spend my entire life working with water, even doing chem labs, and never have to deal individually with a single molecule of water, but if I could take a cupful of water and determine that it could only be divided into 100 parts before each part became indivisible, it would make "water quanta" relevant in a way that they just aren't, otherwise.

                                        Motes have to be coarse-grained for mechanical reasons, and in most games if a player says they're going to reverse-engineer their power quanta by observing how many times they can use the power, you're free to remind them that it's a mechanical abstraction and that extrapolating it to things it wasn't meant to cover may not "work" in-game. But motes, by linking the setting and mechanics, encourage this kind of extrapolation and the consequences it implies, despite the fact that most of the considerations that go into mote pools and costs are mechanical without much thought to their setting implications.

                                                Originally posted by AnubisXy View Post

                                                Generally speaking, it should be possible to know that Spirit A has a bigger pool of Essence than Spirit B, and that Solar A has a bigger Essence Pool than Solar B. It should even be possible, using the correct charms, to calculate how large someone's Essence pool is, how many motes it costs to fuel a given charm, and therefore how often someone can use a charm.

                                                And let's be clear - you don't even really need the concept of motes to calculate Essence pools.

                                                I mean, I can easily imagine Sally Twilight in the First Age making an Artifact box, which you can channel Essence into.

                                                "Oh, look. I just filled 5 of these boxes with my Essence, and I can't fill a 6th one. That must mean I have 5 boxes worth of Essence. Dawn Caste Dave over there was able to fill 7 boxes worth of Essence."

                                                And, rather than calculating Essence pools by motes (which are an abstraction), they can calculate Essence pools by "boxes" (out of game, each of which house 10 motes of Essence), which are discrete things in the game. So even if Sally doesn't know she has 50 motes, and indeed the word mote doesn't exist, she does know she has 5 boxes of Essence, Dave has 7 boxes of Essence, and she can begin performing calculations.

                                                And if that doesn't work, imagine them making identical artifacts and seeing how many of these one person can attune to before they can't attune to anymore (as their Essence pool is now empty).

                                                I can also imagine similar scenarios where Sally Twilight teaches Dave the same charm she knows, and then the two use the charm the same number of times to calculate Essence. If the charm is a 1 mote cost charm (the smallest amount of Essence you can spend) then they can get a rough count of how many motes (or boxes or artifacts attuned) they each possess.

                                                This doesn't seem like rocket science to and it does feel like the sort of thing that genius Exalted in the First Age, in their quest to understand Exaltation and their own powers might have done. "Even though Sally and Dave are both using the same charm, why can Dave use that charm more than Sally can? We know Charms run off of Essence. So maybe he has more Essence? Let's find out."

                                                Adding more mysteries to the game is fine and all, but this one seems like a mystery for the sake of being a mystery at the risk of ruining my verisimilitude. I just have a lot of difficulty imagining that the Exalted of the First Age didn't engage in comprehensive and detailed examinations of Essence and were never able to come to even a basic understanding of something like an approximation of the size of their own Essence pool.
                                                I know its been several years since this thread started but I've been rereading this thread, and mused on how to understand this. I think I might be able to understand this why this would not be able to work as per Vance's description. I would say that how much mote is used, in so much in that is a thing in universe, would only be able to describe part of what occurs, but not the totality.

                                                Say for example that you had two people with a Essence pool, one smaller then the other and tried to attune several Daiklaves. In your example, obiviously the person, with the larger essence pool would be able to attune more and I would say for many that would be the case. However, if for example, the person with the smaller mote pool, had a stronger metaphysical connection to the concept of swords for example, they would be to attune more, despite that in essence pools wise, this should not be possible and you can't really describe that connection in terms of motes. The equation and mathematical method doesn't account for that factor. Attunement would not be solely dependent on mote pools, you could qualitively meaure it in terms of how strong the metaphysical connection is, but not be able to quantatively describe it in a form of an equation, because how could you do that? Like say, the feelings of love cause me to use X amount of motes in one person, but in another produce an lower or higher amount of motes etc.

                                                So in summary, I would say that measuring things in terms of mote pools would be a thing and that using the Scentific Method is a thing, but only one factor out of many when using Essence, many of which can't really be measured in quantative terms. Like being affected by things such as symbolism, memories, how you were which affects the way Essence is used which you can't really measure in terms of motes or which are highly unique and idiosyncratic to the indidvual and would not have the same result with another person due to their different experiences, beliefs and even soul which would cause differing results .It would be all those things together which combine into the result.

                                                These are obviously things that are not described in the books, because they arent something we can really use OOC, but would probably affect Essence usage in universe.
