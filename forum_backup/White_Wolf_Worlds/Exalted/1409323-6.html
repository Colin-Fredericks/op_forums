            Originally posted by Accelerator View Post
            Talking about Lunars, there *are* gripes there. I think Celestials should be group in all of their own, and that solars shouldn't just order them around.
            I don't think in the context of DotFA's setting the inequality itself is was a problem but rather how it was used.
            Lilith and Ma-Ha-Suchi dominate the discourse when talking about Lunars in this supplement but Ingosh Silverclaws and Tamuz were way more interesting because they highlight the tension between near equals.

            One of the DotFA games I played in had a subplot about Lunars pushing for political reform it was an altered timeline after a second Aftershock War and a lot of the Solar elders who'd stymied their ambitions were now dead so their power grab had a very real chance of success.

            Another had the Lunars and Sidereals masterminding the Usurpation to form the Night Sky Regime which somehow managed to capture the tone of Tron Uprising years before that show aired.

                            Tamuz is not in Fangs at the Gate, my understanding is that he's been given the Schrödinger's Cat treatment. Where he probably won't appear in 3rd edition officially for the sake of keeping the canonical number of First Age Lunars down but if he's still part of your game's lore then nothing breaks. Honestly, a First Age Lunar as the secret patron of the Delzhan whose spent centuries operating under the nose of the Realm might actually work better if it's not documented in a major source book.
