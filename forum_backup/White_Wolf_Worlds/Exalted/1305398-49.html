On the site of their victory, where the Raksha leading the crusade was vanquished, the armies of Creation throw a great feast. And as they drink, and eat, and revel, the great heroes of the day give their speeches.

Instead of a speech celebrating their deeds, the impetuous young woman lays down a challenge.                     “A worthy fight!” She declares, and another cheer goes up.
