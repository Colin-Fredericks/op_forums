    Originally posted by Marros56 View Post
    Actual question time, Can mortals attune to manses
    Per the Merits chapter, there's nothing that stops a mortal from attuning to a demesne or a manse, and I don't see anything horrible coming about as a result of letting it happen. They can't enjoy the increased mote recovery, and rolling Occult to sense Essence use within the demesne/manse would be fine.

    In later books, we may go into more detail on the features and powers of manses, and I wouldn't expect mortals to be able to use all of them.

    or use manse-born Hearthstones?
    Many hearthstones only function if they're slotted into an attuned artifact. This presents a problem to mortals, who can't attune artifacts.

    Many hearthstones have radiating environmental effects. They don't really acquire any kind of attunement at all, so sure, mortals can use them.

    The tricky issue is the stones not covered by those two categories. Stuff like the Mountain-Burden Stone, which you jam into an enemy's artifact to make it really heavy. I'm going to leave those up to individual Storytellers to decide on.
