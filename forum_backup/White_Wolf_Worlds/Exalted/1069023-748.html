                Originally posted by BrilliantRain View Post
                If a character has White Veil Form active and uses Poised Lion Attitude and Thousand Courtesan Ways, how many social influence actions can they make during that turn of combat?
                White Veil Form isn't really relevant; it only mitigates flurry penalties, and you can't flurry two influence rolls together with each other.

                Poised Lion Attitude lets you make a Presence or Socialize-based influence roll reflexively on your turn. Whether that's in addition to an influence roll made using your normal action isn't specified; I'd personally be against it unless there's a good stunt other than "I say these two things really fast."

                Thousand Courtesan Ways lets you tag seduction-based influence onto the back of another social roll. You could use it together with Poised Lion Attitude, although I don't think mid-combat seduction would be super effective.
