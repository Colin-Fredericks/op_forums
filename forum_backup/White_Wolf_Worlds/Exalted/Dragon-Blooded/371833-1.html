Raising Essence that high isn't a particularly good use of a Realm DB's time and effort (read XP), Few are going to have the requisite free time, much less the desire to do so at 101. The most likely candidates for E6 are going to be older DBs who have enough power to take 6 months off to stare at their navels and the political insulation to not get shafted during that time - or monks who have risen in position in the IF to have the freedom to meditate without also being stuck administering a monastery or temple.                                             The best yard stick for essence levels for Terrestrials we have is the Realm since we have the most numbers and fluff relating to it. So you have to figure out what the Realm essence level ratio would be, then chop it down a bit and apply it to the rest of Creation.

                                            The best measurement I have found is the student class sizes in the various secondary schools. This gives us a rough idea of how many students go into each discipline.

                                            The Spiral Academy has 240 students a year.
                                            The Cloister of Wisdom can have up to 125, but rarely has max class levels.
                                            The House of Bells is never specified
                                            The Heptagram only has 75 students at any given time with some of them being graduates who are working on projects.

                                            According to that data alone, it seems that the majority of Terrestrials are becoming "cubical dragons", which when you think about it means that they really don't need to raise their essence above 2 to use most of the charms they would need as a paper pusher.

                                            The Heptagram students require essence 3 to graduate, but they get diminishing returns on going higher in essence score. According to MoEP: Dragon-Blooded one Terrestrial in ten knows a single spell in the Realm even if they never went to the Heptagram, learned additional spells or never considered themselves "sorcerers". This becomes important in that we now know at least 1,0000 or so Terrestrials are essence 3 or higher.

                                            The Cloister of Wisdom also requires Essence 3 to graduate, but with their CMA styles there is a clear benefit to pushing essence higher.

                                            Finally you have the House of Bells, which doesn't require a given essence level to graduate, but mote pool size = life in combat.

                                            But here is the kicker that a lot of people forget about, the two schools which would in theory gain the most from increasing essence score, are also the ones that will have the highest casualty rates. So those students who in theory would go higher in essence never get the chance. This in turn skews the population/essence numbers further.

                                            Now with all that in mind, lets look at the Realm itself. According to that one census report there are about 10,000 Terrestrials in the Realm (all great houses and Lost Eggs accounted for). But here is the kicker, that census took place a few years before the defeat at the hands of the Bull of the North, before the fall of Thorns which had Realm Terrestrial Advisors present, as well several other canon incidents which depict Terrestrials getting wacked left and right.

                                            That means that all those experienced Terrestrials are dead and the numbers have been replenished with low essence, low experience Terrestrials. So the Realm numbers at this point are probably less than 10,000 to say the least. But for the sake of easy math lets call it 10,000 and then assume the numbers will be lower than the math suggests, which in turn means the math for the rest of Creation with be even lower.

                                            Over all I would be comfortable with the following math for the Realm:

                                            Essence 2 Realm Terrestrials: 5,300
                                            Essence 3 Realm Terrestrials: 3,200
                                            Essence 4 Realm Terrestrials: 1,000
                                            Essence 5 Realm Terrestrials: 485
                                            Essence 6 Realm Terrestrials: 15

                                            So to answer the OP, under the best possible conditions, you are looking at 15 terrestrials out of every 10,000 that is elder essence levels. Probably less than that off the Blessed Isle due to higher risks or just lack of knowledge.

                                                        Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post
                                                        (Also, the concept of undiluted Dragon Blooded is terrible)
                                                        Not having it around would be kind of weird in concert with the general Dragon-Blooded themes and, indeed, the themes of the setting as a whole, though, wouldn't it?

                                                        The Dragon-Bloods were explicitly designed to be able to make good their losses (and increase their numbers) via breeding. Their Exaltation is a much less numinous thing than that of the Celestials; it's still transcendent, they're the Princes of the Earth for a good reason, but they're still of the earth, which means they're a sort of bloody, messy, practical kind of Exaltation. Their power flows through their bloodlines. That's kind of the point, right?

                                                        The setting materiel on the Dragon-Blooded really hammers home the idea that power through bloodline and family and breeding is important. Families of Dragon-Blooded arrange powerful marriages and try to avoid getting mortal blood in there because they need to breed more Dragon-Bloods who are more powerful. Dynastic marriage politics are really really big deals. So is the fact that the Dragon-Bloods slept with absolutely everyone (and probably everything) back in the day which is why occasionally their genetic soup mixes together with some random guy in the back of beyond and they pop as a new Prince of the Earth.

                                                        It also ties into the whole "everything in the Second Age is less than what it used to be" theme going on. Dragon-Blooded aren't the immaculately bred killing machines with perfect ability to always give birth to more immaculately bred killing machines anymore. Sometimes even a scion of powerful bloodlines is just... not gonna Exalt. That's great. You can spin wonderful stories out of that!

                                                        If diluting their blood doesn't matter at all, then all that goes right out the window, which I think would make the setting way less interesting than it is. There should, in my opinion, be a whole gamut running from "everyone in my entire family going all the way back to the founding of the Realm is Dragon-Blooded, if there's mortal blood in there anywhere you'll never find it, all my siblings exalted, all my parents and their siblings exalted, I exalted, all my kids exalted. We're awesome. Who the fuck are you, you poorly-bred worm?" down to "okay, sure, I'm the first Exalt in this family of patricians for over 100 years, but I've done well, I worked my ass off in school, I bet I can marry upwards. I'd better marry upwards, for the good of my family. Oh, gods, I hope my kids exalt. They really need to exalt" to "man, fuck you, The Realm, I got lucky and totally scored this sweet-ass exaltation through pure luck. Yeah, I suppose some of my ancestors were Dragon-Bloods, that's how it works, and thank you kindly for making me all badass, but I don't really care about this whole thing. I'm gonna go walk off and take over a petty kingdom and have me a harem." With all points in-between!

                                                        Not having the idea that once there were undiluted Dragon-Bloods, but now there are not, I think takes a lot of interesting stuff away from them.

                                                        I will agree, as all right-thinking people do, that Breeding as it was mechanically implemented in the past is total and complete bullshit. I'm open to the idea of "there's a background you can buy representing how especially awesome your bloodline is, you are gonna get all kinds of neat social bonuses because of it and maybe sometimes Pasiap sends you some kind of weird vision" but I think we all agree that "there's a background that gives you a shit-ton more motes" is a stupid idea.
