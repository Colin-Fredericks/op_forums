                    Re: Realm Child Abuse, Exalted, General DB thoughts

                    I just assumed that almost all Realm Patrician childhoods were essentially the extremes of 1980's American high school movies with more kicking ass, more privilege, and more mental illnesses brought on by a system designed to shape and mold you into a tool of the state while telling you that you are individually important (spiritually). I believe it's more or less IMPOSSIBLE without a lot of bending over backwards and ignoring things to portray a realm DB that doesn't have some kind of mental illness, psychosis, or lingering PTSD from the very environment in which they come from. I don't mean like fishmalk crazy, I mean things more akin to real world mental illnesses like BPD, etc.

                    PC are exceptions and all, but I think it's something to keep in mind. The beneficiaries of such a system, the dragon blooded themselves, are being used and abused too under the aegis of tradition and other things.

                    This might not square exactly with what I said above, but Dragon Bloods are my favorite splat, and the above is one of the reasons- because their plight is still on a certain level very much human, and very familiar.

                    Incentive is not permission or justification.

                                                Originally posted by Seagull View Post
                                                Arent' the Scarlet Empress's sexual partners predominantly her own children/descendants?
                                                {Hmm, this is probably focused on the consorts, official or unofficial, who are Exalted, rather than any random bed mate)

                                                I don't know about predominantly but... some are probably related to her. That distant, meaningless form of relation, where you need to have a genealogy table just to figure out who the common ancestor is, and the genetics are exactly as distinct as they would be from somebody with no observable common ancestor whatsoever.

                                                Although really, the distinction from mortals probably doesn't mean much anyway... the woman is about eight hundred years old, chances are that she has hundreds of thousands of mortal descendants at various strata of society. Dragon Blooded probably inevitably have mortal children (the "probably" comes from a question of whether they have more than one, and were lucky with the first and only), and that sends the line of their lineage off into mortal standards of generation and spreading out.

                                                Originally posted by Seagull
                                                While I'm asking, where do the cofounders of new Houses come from if not the Houses?
                                                About half of those Houses were founded by about the halfway mark of the Realm.

                                                That's time enough for some people from the Shogunate to still be around. Indeed, a lot of institutions from the Realm would need to have been staffed in a different manner prior to that point.

                                                That's also about the time that the term "Scarlet Dynasty" came into use; prior to that, the ruling class of the Realm did not really have the overt characteristic of "enormous extended family of the monarch". Likely, that was about the point when the notion that the Empress could conceivably live forever, and the question of succession had been horrifically complicated by the glut of available candidates.

                                                I would say the Empress is disinclined to have children with lovers from the Houses, for political reasons more than genetic ones. I would also think she's not inclined to do so with Outcastes for similar, combined with not wanting to introduce any wild card elements from their lineage into her pool of candidates to establish new Houses from should the need arise. I would say that what children she has have the fathers come from minor Houses, with such consorts having a lot less fanfare than the prominent figures of Tepet and Nellens and the like.
