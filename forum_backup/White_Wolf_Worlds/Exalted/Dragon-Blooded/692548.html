    Dragonblooded crafting in the third edition

    10-07-2015, 12:21 PM
    I want to apologize in advance if I'm asking this in the wrong place. Rest assured I did my due diligence googling and wasn't able to really find an answer to this question. I would really appreciate anyone shedding some light. Perhaps it's been mentioned in some author q&a or spoiler that I couldn't find.

    In 2e I was always somewhat disappointed by Dragonblooded crafting charms, sure they had some neat effects but beyond the excellency their charms didn't make them much better than the average mortal at artifact creation. Now I know crafting will change a lot in the third edition, which is great. What I'm hopingn to learn is if in the new edition artificer will be a valid design space for terrestrial exalted. Obviously it wouldn't make sense for them to compare to the Solar's in terms of their ability to churn out artifacts-- either from a balance standpoint or from the perspective of the setting, but I'm hoping to learn if they will have charms that enable a sufficiently specialized terrestrial to create mid-tier artifacts (like daiklaives and such) as a matter of course. I've always liked the idea of a grizzled earth aspect serving as an "armorer of heroes" roll, repairing and forging weapons and dispensing sage advice to characters (kind of like totosai if you've seen inu yasha). Sure he might not be able to crank out daiklaives at a clip like a solar can, but perhaps he creates one or two of such remarkable weapons every decade which are prized thoughout the realm.

    Anyway, basically just wondering if that sort of character will be supported by Dragonblooded charms, or if they will more or less be constrained to excelling at mortal crafts and have to mostly rely on their excellency to perform artifact crafting. I can see either scenario working out, and to some degree the second one seems to make more sense in the post apocalyptic landscape of creation in 3e, but I'm still hopeful.

    If anyone knows or can guess at an answer it would be appreciated.

    My apologies for any gross grammatical or spelling errors, typing on my phone and that's no fun for anyone.

              I'm always surprised when people talk about Craft only within it's (Artifact) purview. A Terrestrial Exalted (or any other legendary craftsman) should be able to consistently churn works of art in their field, be it statues, swords, jewelries or any other thing. Artefacts are great and all, but being able to craft a single Daiklave will not help your army when the Tidal Wyld hits, whereas being able to take steel swords and knock the carbon out of it in a few strikes will (because Iron > Wyld). If DBs are specialized in speed-crafting mundane things or repairing/modifying existing equipment I'll be a happy man.

              Don't get me wrong: DBs should be able to create Daiklaves and Powerbows, but since their purview is mass combat and teamwork, I would be really happy to see charms which allow them to craft enough arrows for their army in an hour, using only a tree stump, a handful of rocks and a dull knife.

                                          My thoughts on Dragonblooded crafting, at the mo', are:

                                          1) Ideally, rather than be about artefact level, for one they would have a relatively (genre appropriately) easy time making the sort of idle (but somewhat trival or fragile) sorceries beloved of the decadent sorcerer lords of Melnibone, and the mighty tools of utterly badass elemental samurai. Since those two together are close to what the Dragonblooded Dynasty are like. Then have more of a challenge going outside that.

                                          2) I think the limit where the game effectively goes "OK - what you're trying to do with craft is really a civilizational and cultural scale accomplishment, that is beyond the scope of any reasonable crafting system designed to describe the works of a single magical artisan in a game of drama and kung fu action" should come earlier for Dragonblooded than Solars (and also earlier than for certain demons and other forces divine and strange the DBs can call on... for a price).

                                          At the same time, I'm not interested in the sort of "No way can the united Dragonblooded Host *ever*, repeat *ever*, make one of these world stabilising Jade Obelisks, with any amount of time, resources or circumstances. Here, we've proved it by math with as the mechanics are the physics of the world. You must feel like the Solars are more heroes now, surely?" rhetoric that came up in the 2e fan community about craft. I don't think that sort of overreaching mechanic is very necessary.

                                              Are you asking about crafting for PC or for NPC? The answers are VERY different.

                                              PC are the exception. They do the impossible, they achieve great things, they do what it was thought could not be done.

                                              Therefore, if you have a dragonblooded PC, be they focused on crafting, or swording, they are going to be very very good at what they do. They are going to be the exception. They are going to be the never heard of before grand craftsman that turns sand into sawblades.

                                              If you are asking what the NPC in the setting can do, it's a very different answer. They don't have the time, the skill or the ability to produce on a large scale, you can work that out just by looking at the setting/history.
