    Who will your next Dragon-Blooded PC be?

    04-03-2018, 04:01 PM
    As we start to get a picture of the Dragon-Blooded in 3E and look forward to the mechanics, I wonder what sorts of Dragon-Blooded PCs people are interested in building and playing.

    Because I work in a field that involves a lot of NIMBY issues, I'm more interested in what you actually intend you play rather than things that you think might be interesting or that a buddy might play.

    Has the material released so far inspired you to make a particular terrestrial PC? Do you want to resurrect a 1E/2E PC in this new edition? Are you at all concerned that the fluff or mechanics won't support the kind of exalt that you want to make?

    Who will your next Dragon-Blooded PC be?

                              What began as a joke is slowly becoming reality.

                              Sooo, back when 3e core dropped, I had a look at the Merits, and found all sorts of wonderful NPC-giving merits! Then I got to thinking, what kind of a character would have ALL of them? (Well, Command dropped pretty soon once I realized military commander wasn't the style I wanna go for)

                              Enter "Cynis Cid", the DB pimp stacked with NPC merits. Allowed to remain unmarried due to House Cynis not wanting his bussiness going to the wife's house, he is a traveller through and through. So colourful and exotic is his caravan that they are often mistaken for a traveling circus. He always travels with his posse, which includes his loyal manservant, his bodyguards and of course his ladies of the night. They may be a posse, but to him they are a family and he will do anything to protect that.

                              Haven't fleshed out the character that much more. Debating on should I make him a sorcerer (those Demons and Elementals bring out even more NPCs), or instead just focusing on native DB Brawl Charms. And yeah, in case you couldn't tell, he is to be played for laughs as a sympathetic pimp, not a realistic depiction at all.

                                              Cathak Chiheshi is the youngest and likely to be the last of Cathak Cainan's children. At the House of Bells, she studied logistics and what another place and era would call 'hearts and minds' methods, and spent her Sabbatical in Jiara mastering the art of dance, which she had always loved. The last days of the Empress's reign saw her assigned as an officer of the garrison of the Kingdom of Sieversi, and as the longtime Ledaal governor of that satrapy lives out her last days, Cainan has ordered his daughter to see to it that she, or another member of their house, replaces the woman as Satrap.

                                              Personally, Chiheshi is slimmer and less amazonian than expected of her house, but like most dancers is made entirely out of silk and steel springs.Her skin is fair and her eyes and long flowing hair are the pale gray of wood ash; her aspect marking appears when her hair is struck by a strong breeze, stoking each strand like a hot coal. The same thing happens to hair and eyes alike when her anima flares, while her skin is surrounded by an almost invisible heat haze.

                                              I haven't quite nailed down her combat style, yet - I know that she's a martial artist and I'll have her use whatever lets me get her capoeira on best, but what that'll turn out to be is still undetermined.

                                                      Alexia is the only daughter of a very wealthy Patrician family, who exalted in primary school while being bullied by Dynast scions. Growing up, she had always heard that her family was descended from a Tepet general’s tryst with a mortal woman (Alexia’s grandmother), but being just mortals there was no way to prove the claim. Alexia’s exaltation changed that, and so the family quietly adopted her as an outcaste to avoid a political scandal at the time. Forced to leave her mortal family to join her Exalted one, Alexia was always more than a little conflicted about being shuffled around because of politics that happened generations ago. She was ecstatic to actually meet her grandfather though before she went to Pasiap’s Stair. It was during her time at the secondary school that the Tepet legions were destroyed by the Bull in the North and the perfidious actions of the other Great Houses. Upon graduating, she was given her grandfather’s heirloom daiklave as well as a hastily penned letter from him. It extolled her to redeem the blade’s honor by avenging him against the enemies of the Realm both inside and out. Alexia is conflicted about being treated like the weapon that she wields and the greater duty that her exaltation demands.

                                                      I’m thinking that any socially savvy Dynast who meets Alexia will pretty quickly realize that she is way too familiar with life on the Blessed Isle to be an outcaste. While House Tepet has lost a lot more than political reputation, they certainly don’t want Alexia’s family history revealed as it might damage their already very tenuous attempts to get any marriage offers. While the house elders have forbidden her from seeing her mortal family, she stills sneaks out to visit them occasionally when she has the chance.
