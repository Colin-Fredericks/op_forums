    Dragon-Blooded descended from First Age loyalists?

    04-07-2022, 10:39 AM
    I've started on the Dragon-Blooded books, and a thought suddenly popped up, one that for some reason never occurred to me before: What if, somewhere in Creation, there existed a Dragon-Blooded culture descended from those Terrestrials who fought to defend the Solars during the Usurpation? What if some survived and fled to the edges of the world alongside the Lunars, disgusted by the usurpers? What would they look like? What would they become, unaffected by the lies of the Immaculate Philosophy? What stories would they tell their children, of a paradise lost to treachery and base usurpers, usurpers who justify their rule with lies about the nature of Heaven, the Elemental Dragons and reincarnation? How they and they alone are the only Terrestrials who do not carry traitor's blood in their veins?
    After a thousand years they, like the Lunars, wouldn't be expecting the return of the Solars. They would have their own plans and dreams. But then the leader of the heretics and traitors disappears during Calibration, and people with the mark of the Unconquered Sun on their brow start appearing in unprecedented numbers.
    No doubt many Elders would be disquieted, worried about ancient legends made real. But what would the young and brash think? I wouldn't be surprised if a number of romantics were to think "We will not fail again," grasp their daiklaves and set out into the world to see if these returned Solars are worthy of their assistance, and if so, to aid in killing the Immaculate filth hunting them.

                          In my setting headcanon, during the Shogunate there were Dragon Blooded gens that retained their loyalty to the Solar Exalted. They did so secretly, of course. And there were a couple of instances of that Solar being reborn, their Dragon Blooded loyalists coming to collect them, and then eventually that clan of Dragon Blooded being hunted down and fighting to the death. These handful of instances, in turn, caused the Immaculate Order of the time period to create an inquisition to investigate Dragon Blood gens searching for loyalty to the Solar Exalted.

                          By the time of the Great Contagion pretty much none of the surviving Dragon Blooded gens still retained any loyalty to the Solar Exalted. By that time those had had been loyal had had that loyalty uncovered and they'd been killed, or they had thrown their lot in with one of the reborn Solar Exalted and been destroyed. By the modern time period, there aren't any left. It's possible that some Dragon Blooded families in the Threshold might have some ancient ties, and maybe a Dragon Blooded could find some long-forgotten secret library in their family manse and it would reveal that at one time, their ancestors had been Solar loyalists.

                                  One thing that occurred to me was some gens of Dragonblooded given some task by a First Age Solar (especially as the Sidereals 3rd Ed book makes the Usurpation messy, enough to make it possible a Solar knew something was up, but not what) and that is the focus of this small Outcaste community. Like 'here is my research/neat artifacts/manse/McGuffin/etc., you lot guard it for me until I get back', and the elders decided when 'all of the Solars are dead and the Lunars have all gotten out of dodge', that meant 'guard the McGuffin until our Solar's next incarnation shows up, since htey don't take that long to incarnate normally'. While 1500 years is a lot of time, Dragonblooded folks also live several times longer than baseline humans, so it might be more like 'great-great-grandpa's stories about the US Civil War that are family legends' than 'Visigoths' scale, and having a tangible thing gives a focus especially with Creation having plenty of beings who would love to steal a First Age Solar's stuff in a place other than a fancy trapped tomb built to appease a First Age Solar's ghost by keeping their grave goods safe.

                                  It also makes it personal having an actual historical figure or figures (a Circle, for instance), with the abstract question about the Usurpation being framed around 'the other Exalted that betrayed great-great-granddad's lord were assholes, but we are the last remnants of honor among the Dragonbloods', and maybe largely an abstract question... until the Solars come back, and are looking for answers, and suddenly they might have to hand the McGuffin over to the next incarnation of its owner's Shard.

                                  One could also play with how much actually was known (and how much was known but wrong) because it is essentially family legend and its own sort of religious belief. Maybe the family isn't really sure it was a Solar versus something else from well before the Contagion.

                                      I think your most effective way to do this kind of thing is to have a culture that comes across as though it originates as distinctly against the Solar Purge, and in its modern time has evolved into something that in practical terms is more about having a historical and ideological opposition to the manner of Dragon Blooded rule and conduct found in the Shogunate and its successors. In practical terms I think that means their society functions differently, and it would probably generate the most history and drama if they were close enough to at least one of the other Dragon Blooded powers for there to have been some wars in their time.

                                      For all the talk of the first post being very Second Edition, I feel as though the suggestions of a Shogunate gens that retains loyalty to a surviving Solar to the point of fighting the world for them is also very much arising from an assumption of complete subservience that was more native to that Edition than this one. First Age Dragon Blooded with something like that relationship to a Solar make sense, but I feel as though the Shogunate would have put them in the position of needing to make some very difficult choices rather than just being totally on board with fighting a losing battle down to the last body out of principle.

                                              That was more or less my idea, yeah. There were probably some Gens that had never really hated the Solar Exalted and were blindsided by the Shogunate rebellion. By the time they knew what was happening, it was basically over and too late. Most of those Gens transitioned to the new system because they didn't have much of a choice. It's likely that at least a few made some cursory moves towards supporting the Shogunate, but still secretly retained some level of loyalty to the old Deliberative. Most likely those were Gens that found themselves on the bottom end of the new political system and looked back with rose-tinted glasses at the previous system, or imagined a newer system where they would have more power. In the event that their disloyalty to the Shogunate was discovered, they'd risk being put to the sword.

                                              Keep in mind that there were lots of rebellions and warfare in the Shogunate as various Gens battled and fought one another for power. Some of those rebellious Gens may have ended up with Solar figureheads and/or allies (or possibly Lunar ones too). And I'd expect that there would end up being some sort of Inquisition-type organization devoted to not just hunting down reborn Solar Exalted but also exposing those Gens who didn't display appropriate loyalty to the new Shogunate, and that also didn't accept the historical revisions that were being created and spread (Solars being puppets of the Yozi and such).

                                              Over time any Gens that retained some level of loyalty to the old system ended up becoming something like "crypto-Jews" with families concealing their beliefs in the form of artwork, architecture or stories in order to hide from an Inquisition that was looking to exterminate them. Some of those things might have survived the Contagion and Wyld invasion and can be found in the modern world, though by this point only the greatest of scholars could recognize some of those traditions or displays for what they originally meant.

                                              So by the current era, these end up being little more than a historical quirk, and none of the descendants from those families have any sort of loyalty to Solar Exalted, just some slightly unusual family traditions. Still, it could be part of a Dragon Blooded PC's background, and during the course of a campaign they could uncover the past about their family or something like that, but this would be a sort of one-off event and not something you'd see on any sort of major scale.

                                              *edit* Just to reiterate, these sorts of families would be very much exceptional. The vast majority of Dragon Blooded, even those that had generally been more supportive of the Solar Deliberative would have dropped their loyalty when it became apparent that the Solars were not coming back en masse. After a few decades they would have said, "Well, I guess it really is a new world and a new order and it's time to get in on that." Still, there likely were some Gens, or parts of some Gens, that preferred the old system to the new one for various reasons and would have needed to take steps to hide that fact.
