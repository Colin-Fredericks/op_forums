    Storms and the Dragon-Blooded

    02-21-2014, 01:59 AM
    So, I'm currently sitting at my house at 1:45am in the middle of a pretty grand storm. The worst that I've had this year, at the very least. Plenty of rain being pushed passed faster than my eye can really follow, the wind howling like crazy, lightning flashing every few seconds with zero time between light and sound so I'm figuring its right above my house, all mixed with the slight suspicion that that big tree further up the hill is finally going to be blown over and crash into the house.

    Is this what it's like to be near a pissed off Air Dragon Blood? :.D

    Or any DB really. The anxiety that at any moment that elemental fury is going to be unleashed and terror unleashed upon you and those around you? And then your 'solar' buddy reeaally f***s up and starts a fight. Every blow a crack of thunder right over head...will the collateral smack into you? Flashes of light and essence blind you for moments as you feel those peels of thunder reverberate through your chest? And that god awful sound of their anima, like the wind during a hurricane, that pressure that almost blows you off your feet?

    Then, it all calms down...a standstill. Your Solar friend talking a brief moment of philosophy with him...the eye of the storm. But you feel your friend saying the wrong thing...its building back up... maybe it was the calm, or maybe it just really is now, but its all the more furious for it. A gale, a crash, another blinding flash and for a brief moment, you wonder...

    How the hell do you fight a storm?


    In conclusion, Dragon-Blooded are freaking awesome for evoking such imagery....and Solars are also incredible for being able to wrestle it.

    (And this thread is just something to take my mind off the elements raging above and around my head.)

    [Edit: And for all you Deathlords out there...should I die tonight, whether by being crushed by the house or dropped by a tornado or any other crazy and unfortunate circumstance...or any circumstance at all, mind... I WILL take the deal. I promise.]

                  I'm afraid I disagree with you pretty hard on that.

                  Dragon-Blooded anima banner actively harms the environment that they're in. Anyone who get's too close gets damaged, depending on which stage the Anima Banner is at. Their expressions of Elemental Bolt and Burst, mixed with their flaring anima can definitely be extremely flashy... Lightning bolts that explode, Fire bursting from the ground... And that's not even touching yet the Dragon-Vortex-esque effects of later levels.

                  Specifically, though, my mind was on the Air-Dragon blooded. Wind actively whips around them constantly unless they consciously surpress it, and its stronger for Higher Essence and Breeding Dragon-Bloods. If they get enraged, and their Anima starts flaring to boot, it seems like it really would feel like a storm around you, and I don't think it would be less dramatic for other Dragon-Blooded Aspects either.

                  And I think that's really thematic. They aren't perfectly in tune with their Elemental Nature, as evidenced by a Peripheral Essence pool. So it lets loose and destroys, and in a very obvious way. It's scary. It's awesome. And it makes them seem extremely powerful since they're the controllers of this awesome power.

                  Now of course, I'm not saying that just when they're fighting that their anima makes the sky grow dark, hurricane force winds erupt, lightning flashes, trees grow, boulder fall, volcanoes erupt, etc, etc... I'm just saying it FEELS like it is to those nearby. Of all the Exalts, I'd say that the Dragon-Bloods FEEL more like the atom bombs that all Exalts are. Buuut, I understand if others feel differently...just I hate the mass DB hate, like anything that makes them awesome that doesn't involve teamwork needs to be shot down...and now I'm rambling.
