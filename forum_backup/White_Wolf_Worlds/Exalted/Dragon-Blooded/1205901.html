    (2e) Would it break the setting if DBs could enlighten into CCS...

    04-11-2018, 11:50 PM
    ...but because no organization had ever considered instilling them with that power a priority, no standardized path to that type of enlightenment existed?
    IE, very rare beings like Her Redness and Mnemon initiate into CCS (with the Mantle of Brigid here being an inspiration or implement for that, rather than the source of it), but there is no pair of charms like Tiger and Bear, the Irises path, the Immaculate enlightening charms, or even that thaumaturgy that lets you enlighten into CCS through a number of years of asceticism. Every DB who breaks that barrier has to make their own path, which generally demands it's own sacrifices, before they're prepared to initiate into the step above.

    Because really, the idea that DBs with CCS are possible, but nobody has ever created a standard way to prepare one's mind to receive that enlightenment because nobody ever felt they needed/should have that power is more reasonable to me than "DB martial artists can reach a tier above with difficulty, but FUCK DB sorcerers - their horizons stretch basically no further than mortals". After all, the standardized methods for them to reach CMA all stem back to the Deliberative wanting more effective DB lieutenants and soldiers. Maybe it's just that nobody cared about DB CCS, and Salina was too focused on bringing sorcery to EVERYONE.

                          My premise wasn't that they never tried hard enough, but rather there was never sufficient external support to develop a universal method. The first methods to reach CMA as a DB no matter who you are were developed with the support of a large part of the Deliberative, who were seeking to augment the efficacy of the DBs as warriors. Those same people, or even just some of them, may have considered it not worth it to make DBs celestial circle sorcerers, in such a way that it totally killed the project to develop a universally accessible path to CCS for them.

                          Or rather, 'Discovering the first method which allowed every DB to reach CMA if they got the right tuition canonically required the effort of the most powerful nation in the setting, which could probably work with a number of DB sorcerers larger than the current amount of DBs.' There's no reason to assume creating a universal method for celestial circle sorcerers would be less demanding.

                          Heck, maybe there's a method that was lost to the depths of time that can enlighten some arbitrary subset of DBs, but not all of them - and which was lost, because over time it failed so often that it was considered something no better than normal, or because it failed to meet the needs of the over-ambitious process that sought to create it.
