                            I always think that attaching Essence to age is a fundamental fault in most people's minds.

                            Essence isn't something that just grows as you go, but a measure of enlightenment, of prowess in a very real sense. Essence 6 is kept away from Player Characters to mantain the theme of ancient, incredibly powerful old masters, but that doesn't mean that getting wrinkles on your face equals Essence. You can have Essence 5 15-year-olds (your Okita Soujis), and Essence 3 200-year-olds (your everyone else's).

                            Attempting to tie it to demographics feels even weirder; 'My calculations tell me that we should have 10 Da Vincis and 15 Michelangelos currently!', and then you wonder why we don't have more Cistine Chapels.

                            That gets even more strange when you consider Essence is not really a measure of actual ability in Exalted, and has never been: saying 'an Essence 6 character' accomplishes absolutely nothing. It is the array of Charms and Artifacts and connections that makes a character be actually relevant, and the amount of these entirely depends on how many named, important, earth-shaking characters you can have in mind. That will vary between generations. It absolutely has to! It's just how humans work! Those from generations that just happened to create several gifted figures will look upon a new generation with a certain contempt (even if some are gifted in different ways), and vice-versa, as older characters behold in a new generation so many children with talent such as he had never seen.

                            Your game can have the 'Ten Bells', each an accomplished graduate of the House of Bells, once a Sworn Brotherhood, now sworn enemies, each of them a swordsman who has felled Anathema one-on-one; it can also have the Ledaal Council, comprised all of strange, eerie sages, some of which are more air than man, some of which have demonic shadows, some of which have become one with the stars, as her soul is entwined to a Sidereal lover's. And so on.

                            Conversely, it's entirely possible that other than the examples we have sheets of, the only other Dragon-Blooded in the Realm with Essence higher than 4 disappeared 5 years ago.

                            Talking about the 15 Essence 6 Dragon-Blooded in the Realm tells you nothing, and is innately misleading, in my opinion. Especially when it enters forum parlance with mentions of 'but then all the high-essence Dragon-Blooded in the Realm...' as if one were talking about DBZ power levels, rather than about the three extant who might be an excellent lover, an excellent poet, and another an excellent assassin who sucks at an open fight. There should be only as many high-essence beings as can have names and faces and stories, and never as amorphous numbers or descriptions.

                                        It is not misleading if you don't jump to conclusions.

                                        Knowing that there is only a dozen or so Essence 6 Terrestrials means you treat them as unique treasures, rather than palette swap mook #32b with higher stats to use as part of the most recent Wyld Hunt. Thus each one should be treated like a character, fully flushed out and with their own styles & ways of doing things. In fact if you consider the attrition rates in the combat oriented groups, it would be safe to say that a disproportionate number of the elders will the types who actively stay off the front lines.

                                        So yes you might have a grand master of all five Immaculate Dragon CMA styles, but he will be spending his time either in personal training or training others, rather than running around Creation. So unless the PCs are laying siege to the Cloister of Wisdom it is unlikely they would ever meet such a character regardless how many wyld hunts get called down on them.

                                        Now it is interesting to consider that in the case of Solars and potentially Abyssals, that one can produce very high numbers of Terrestrials with favorable Breeding scores and if given enough infrastructure, they can be trained right up to the edge of Essence 5 before they ever take the field. This in turn creates all sorts of political and social repercussions in the game if the ST plays it right, rather than freaking out about the munchkinish nature of it.

                                        According to the math I used there are only about 500 or so Essence 5 Terrestrials in the Realm and that is divided amongst five different directions with different character builds. Now imagine what happens on a social level if a solar shows up at Lookshy to sign an alliance treaty, but brings 500 (Essence 5 / Breeding 5) Terrestrials along as a body guard, each of which is fully combat trained and a competent sorcerer.

                                        Just by showing up with that kind of numbers, it will send shockwaves through the Realm and Lookshy. The military leaders will have to reconsider what they think they know about battlefield uses of Terrestrials, the priests who claim Terrestrial perfection will be confronted by the fact that Terrestrials who served a Solar lord are on average more powerful and of purer blood than those who follow the immaculate order, etc. etc.

                                        It can be amazing how many potent storyline hooks there are if one considers making power realistically rare and then letting the players flip things around.
