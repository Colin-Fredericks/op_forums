    [2E] On the numbers of Dragon Blooded

    06-02-2016, 06:32 PM
    I used the 2E tag because we don't have much (I think) to go on on DB population dynamics yet. Anyhow - I was talking to my group (5 players) about running a generational DB game in the 300's when the new 3E book comes out. One player proposed the group be a children of two married outcaste DB's forming a Minor Household, and how the PC's try to make the house larger and possibly into a great house over time. Loads of other ideas great ideas too, and lots of room for great stories for me to tell, we all agree to play.

    The reason I'm writing though - is that a couple of my players said they'd play male PC's who assemble a large harem of women and summoned elementals whom they get pregnant, and just through sheer numbers have a massive number of DB Exalted children. I've always been a bit skeptical of this idea actually working out in the world of Exalted.

    (1) The expense alone could be ruinous.
    (2) The (2e) Canon is that DB's in the Dynasty don't really have that many children, the average being 8 per century (for dual exalted couples) It does acknowledge that some DB couples have lots of children, but that is the exception and not the norm
    (3) A Minor House pumping out dozens of DB children a year will eventually get noticed by the Great Houses.
    (4) Such notice will in fact, be lethal. Great Houses don't like competition, and any minor house having even dozens of Children a year will be curb stomped into the ground without the protection of a Great House.
    (5) The Empress herself would likely not tolerate a Minor House upending her balance and control by having say, 2500 new Exalted in a century.
    (6) Even it were allowed, a house expanding so fast would lose cohesion and central control would be impossible. Other houses would poach DB's in marriages, and the time and attention required to build relationships with the parent(s) to have a bond to their house wouldn't be possible with too many children.

    Counterpoints
    (1) The Empire always needs Dynasts and Dragonbloods to do many of the jobs - some 1e books I believe and maybe 2e books imply there are not enough DBs to go around for all of the work needed.
    (2) Assassinations simply over a house having "too many" DB's might compromise the empire and reduce fertility rates to unsustainable levels.

    Is this line of thinking reasonable/unreasonable? What am I missing? Further thoughts?

          The 2e books have terrible, nonsense population dynamics; to make any sense of the numbers at all, you end up in houserule country. One of the following things needs to be true:

          1) Mortality among the Dragon-Blooded is staggeringly high, on the order of constant Black Plague-level population depletion.
          2) Exaltation rate is very, very low even among direct children of Dragon-Blooded - say, 1/10 or 1/100, before breeding gets involved. If one of the parents is mortal, these numbers drop off sharply.

          (I favor #2.)

          I would recommend ignoring anything the book says about summoned elementals shifting these numbers around, because it creates precisely the sort of weird "Why has no one...?" problems you're describing. Your best odds of having Exalted children should come of DB/DB pairings; anything else is a shot in the dark. This has the added benefit of removing the incentives for a lot of behavior that I'd rather not deal with in-game.

                                                          I've reread the 1E and 2E DB books this evening about DB culture and have more thoughts on this. I agree with the thought that that there is social stigma against having too many children is simply of a fact of a, by definition, DB's are an exclusive ruling caste. It's not an exclusive club if everyone gets to join it. There is a social contract of being a DB, as mentioned earlier.

                                                          In both 1E and 2E canon it mentions that the Empress keeps the DB's away from the regular population and insulates them with the patricians. The DB's are considered form of Gods Amongst Men. 250 DB's a year in a population canonically of 10,000 in one house hold (2.5% growth) - if copied - would be an unmanageable increase and violate the long term security of the Empire for the Scarlet Empress. In fact, I think, once considering the financial costs alone - there are a multitude of subtle disincentives for trying to have a large family too quickly. 2e DB Manual of Power mentions:

                                                          The amount of jade a Dynast actually receives from her family is determined by a ludicrously complex formula that takes many factors into consideration. Those factors include age, education, marital status, whether one’s spouse is mortal or Dragon-Blooded, service to the family, service to the legions, service to the Wyld Hunt, service to the Empress, number of children produced, number of children Exalted and the rarity and strategic importance of any particular skills the Dragon-Blood possesses.
                                                          A slow, manageable increase in DB population numbers is probably a good thing for the empire as a whole - it is culturally manageable. If a male DB wants to marry a patrician/dynastic mortal, live in a manse with the correct element and a Dragon's Nest power (base bonus 3+1+2 before breeding)/10 = 60% per child, have one kid a year - and can afford it - would at least be seen as playing by the rules. He would have to make a significant effort into making enough money to pay resources 3 or 4 a year, per child, for tutoring and primary school. Each child that exalts requires a resources 4 or 5 for placement in a secondary school. He likely didn't start out with that manse, and would have to have built it himself, or traded favors etc to get it.

                                                          The amount of economic activity he would have to generate per year would likely be multiple Resource 5's and likely be of great benefit to his House and the Empire's economy as a whole. He would also make enemies, and likely be a target simply because his business holdings are so large that an ambitious or capricious DB might want to eat a piece of his pie. It would take a lot of hard work to manage correctly and successfully. Other DB's would likely be proud of him at some level for managing to pull it off even if they thought poorly of his blood.

                                                          i.e.
                                                          "He obviously likes to rut a lot with those mortal wive's of his, and produces DB's of poor blood, but by the Dragon's, 60 exalted children in a hundred years - and he manages three separate business holdings to pay for it all! I doubt that the man has slept more than 4 hours a day for over a century! I bet he expends most of essence just trying to stay awake!" (laughter ensues) This is assuming he somehow manages to continuously have a legitimate mortal child per year.

                                                          A higher breeding male DB (4 to 6) likely would be of a higher social class and expected to marry a female DB, and it would be exceptional, and likely scandalous circumstances where they marry a mortal.

                                                          I think Lookshy is much the same as the realm in this regard. While they talk about, and give lip service to having more children - really, too many too fast would undermine the cohesive military structure and society. Look at the baby boomers IRL- one of the reasons for the mass cultural rebellion of the 60s and 70s was there were simply too many kids for the adults to control. (Or so my boomer parents tell me.)

                                                          Likely - Outcaste DB's in the threshold have tried this, but can't educate the children well enough, as well - suffer from a much, much higher death rate. If one squints a bit - I think there are arguably setting reasons of the DB population being what it is. Likely, 10,000 DB's or there abouts, is what the Empress and the Sidereals can control with only somewhat frequent peasant rebellions and attempts at regicidal civil war. Canon 2e mentions that the major failing of the Shogunate was near constant civil war and frequent civil strife.

                                                          I think the original writers of 1e and 2e DB books did goof on not calculating through what their numbers actually implied. I think if one alters their head canon just a titch - you can use DB breeding rates in a 1e or 2e game as is, and it makes sense. PC's of course, can upend that goofing around. I'm very, very curious to see what the Dev's pull out of their hat for DB breeding rates, or setting changes, or simply a better explanation (like mine - 10,000 is what the Empress can control) or some combination of all of the above.
