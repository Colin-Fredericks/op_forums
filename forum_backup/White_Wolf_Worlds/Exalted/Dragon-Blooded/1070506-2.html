            And if he's House Iselsi, he's definitely not in favor in the Realm, though his influence in the Threshold is probably significantly greater, given that people widely are in awe of Dragon-Blooded. That said, I would follow most of the recommendations in the thread that you've latched onto, but not be afraid to have him genuinely come round to be loyal to them, if they take it in that direction. As a sorcerer who is not a devout believer of the Immaculate Cult who is also a member of House Iselsi, it is entirely possible that he doesn't exactly like the current power structures of the Realm, and possibly of Creation. He may not have had the idea that "I'm the center of all Creation" as thoroughly beaten into his head as, say, a Mnemon or another, more powerful Great House would've. Those are really some of the bigger roadblocks to truly shifting a Dynast's opinion - if they can demonstrate that working with them is more beneficial to his aims (whether those are selfish, selfless, or House-bound), you could set up for a lot of conflict in him, if that's your style.

            All that I write but don't cite is simply my perspective, colored by my experiences and beliefs. I extrapolate a lot, too, so don't take it too seriously. :P

                                    I've found it helpful for the Realm houses to use a form of "honor currency" - special artifact jade coins that aren't worth a particularly large amount of money, but that can be redeemed no-questions-asked for favors by the house granting them. So, you give one of their heirs back, plus his artifact gear? That's worth a few coins, sure - and there's a banked plot for future use.

                                    They aren't meant to be traded to soulless Anathema abominations, of course - they're meant to be used to ransom stuff back from another House. But... there is a lot of personal pride and institutional credit bound up in honoring them; will they still be respected if a Solar comes to collect? Who knows, but it'll probably be interesting to find out!
