    Originally posted by Nocte ex Mortis View Post
    That said, I've never seen an issue with massive anima banners. I mean, look at what they have for visibility at the highest Peripheral. You can see a Solar's anima banner for miles when they flare, and that's a massive column of light and displays of power. Why not the same for the Terrestrials?
    It's not really a big issue for me, but it's worth pointing out that if a Solar was lit up even as bright as a regular bonfire, you could totally see him from miles. I'm not really sure this should apply to terrestrials, firstly because some of the terrestrial anima banners, particularly water, end up being some rolling fog accompanied by the crushing silence and dark of the ocean depths. The second reason I think it doesn't really fit is that, while DBs are awesome, they aren't as powerful as the celestial exalted. I don't think the anima banners of the terrestrial exalted should be drastically smaller than the celestial ones, but they shouldn't really be so much bigger.

    Lastly you can get a lot of terrestrials together, certainly way easier than getting a bunch of Solars together. and combining massive anima effects that are so different together feels awkward. Like a massive rainstorm from the air/water guys, while brushfires are breaking out and tremors are shocking through the ground and flowers are blooming and vines growing ect. It just seems too much.

    I will say though that Dragon Vortex attack is bad to base it off of because it's so deadly though. However I'm holding on to the idea that an irish superfire wandering around with a little person inside it is still too much.

    Again it's not a big issue, and if people are having fun with it then by all means go nuts with it, I just think it doesn't fit at all.

                            This... Is exactly what I'd like to see more of. Air Aspects will have raging wind storms, Water Aspects torrential downpours, Fire Aspects will cause the temperature to spike to "uncomfortably hot", Earth Aspects cause low-magnitude earthquakes, and Wood Aspects will see weeds growing out of control all over the place. None of these will actually have any mechanical effect barring stunts and thematics... Unless you invest in Dragon-Seared Battlefield or Dragon Vortex Attack, at which point shit goes down.

                            Also, some ideas of how Celestials compare;
                            Solars - Archangels. A totemic Solar Anima makes it blatantly clear that you are dealing with one Chosen by the Highest of Holies; to oppose one is to be Damned, for their Word is Law - to aid them is to be Blessed, for their Will is Divine.
                            Abyssals/Infernals - As with Solars, except Dead-Thing-That-Should-Not-Be and Corrupt-Abomination-From-The-Depths-Of-Hell, respectively.
                            Lunars - The Wild Hunt. No matter whether it is a raging Beast, a Wolf in sheep's clothing, or a Monster in the shadows, a Predator stands before you, a Hunter, and you can only pray you're not its prey. You feel the animal instincts surge to the fore, fight or flight, submit or die.
                            Sidereals - Tricky, since their animas are by far the most subtle. I imagine that it just gives an overwhelming sensation of Destiny, almost like an out-of-body experience. You just know that This, Right Here, is a Key Part of The Big Plan. Or, far worse, Not Part Of The Plan.

                            Aretii: "As one of the players described it, 'After initiation, it was actually a relief seeing who had eyepatches or bandaged hands - you know what they sacrificed, instead of wondering what bits people cut out of their own souls to gain power.'"
