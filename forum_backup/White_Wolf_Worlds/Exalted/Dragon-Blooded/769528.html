    The Elemental Dragons and What Happens When You Kill Them?

    11-19-2015, 05:03 PM
    So, someone/thing decides its a good idea to kill an Elemental Dragon, and oops! They succeed. What on Creation happens next?

    Are the Elemental Dragons tied so much to Creation that Creation being to unravel to a degree? What about the Earth Dragon? Would the Wyld slowly start creeping in more and more? I could see massive earthquakes, tornadoes and hurricanes, volanoes erupting after the appropriate Dragon died but what else? Can a Greater Elemental Dragon take its place? What of the lesser elementals?

    I would generally say that while natural disasters would increase and life for mortal would get even worse, I don't necessarily see Creation unraveling without them. Since the lesser elementals in 2E weren't tied to the Elemental Dragons I would say they wouldn't be effected in an existential sense although I am sure the loss would be deeply felt and all would know regardless of where they were. I would imagine with an appropriate sorcery working or maybe with some loom magick one could tie a Greater Elemental Dragon to the Pole and take its place. What are your thoughts?

              Return of the Scarlet Empress addresses this for 2E.

              Essentially:
              1) The 5EDs are a source, but not the only source, of elemental energy in Creation.
              1a) Their deaths would remove some, but not all, of that energy.
              2) Gaia could create a replacement for one of her dead children.
              2a) Probably by elevating a Dragonblood. But to answer your question, I see a Lesser Dragon getting elevated, but Greater Dragons are insane (probably not a good idea to give them more power).
              3) You don't kill an Elemental Dragon without serious, Sword of Creation, level firepower.
              3a) And if you are using the Realm Defence Grid, you've probably just nuked an elemental pole as well.
              3b) Nuking the elemental pole of Earth will, indeed, cause the Wyld to seep in and Creation to unravel.

              For 3E... I have no idea. Whatever fits the story, I guess.

                          Let's say you kill the Great Dragon of Water.

                          In the moment of her death, you are destroyed by her surging blood. When she falls to the ground, her Essence seeps into the dragon lines, overwhelming them. The Essence of Water surges across Creation, driving up the tide and reviving dead rivers. The immediate environs of her death are annihilated, while the rest of Creation knows a sudden and untimely Season of Water. For three months, rain falls even on the Pole of Fire.

                          The next year, there is no Season of Water, only cold and unseasonal drought. So also the year after, and the year after that (though each drought is less severe than the last). Only four years after the death of Water does the cycle of seasons and elements return to normal, when the fallen Dragon is reincarnated at the heart of the Pole of Water.

                                              CoL, that's the origin of elementals as described in both Games of Divinity and I think the 2E core, although you've misremembered some. (It's also reputedly been dropped entirely from 3E.)

                                              Gaia made her Five Elemental Dragons, then the gods in imitation made five non-draconic Great Elementals to serve them and manage the flows and balance of elemental essence. The Primordials destroyed them in the War to prevent the gods from using them as weapons, and their deaths sent major earthquakes, wildfires, floods, tornados, and [Wood-related disaster I can't remember -- poison floods?] all over Creation. Elementals were born from their scattered Essence. After the War the Five Elemental Dragons designed and organized the Terrestrial Bureaucracy, but they've spent most of their time sleeping in the dragon-lines since then (where they probably have to stay to manage and control the Essence flow).

                                              In my headcanon they have never come out after the Contagion and Balorian Crusade to rebuild the Terrestrial Bureaucracy because ever since then, they've been far too busy desperately trying to hold together Creation's disintegrating dragon lines, and if they step away for even a year the Essence would spiral further out of balance and more of Creation would be ruined.

                                              So if one died, I imagine a lot of "natural" disasters would kill a lot of people, followed by the birth of several new elemental species.
