                    The idea was more complicated than "Dragon Blooded belong to the first god who claims them". It was a bit crudely stated, but the idea was that if a Terrestrial was in Heaven then they had to have the accompaniment of somebody else who had responsibility for what they do. In some ways, that could be advantageous; an offence below what you could be summarily executed for is one that your companion will have to answer for, and a lot of non-judicial harm inflicted on you can be construed as an offence against them.
