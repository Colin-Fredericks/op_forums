                    Originally posted by TheCountAlucard View Post
                    I have my doubts that "mook" is even a term in Creation, considering it was only coined here in the 1930's.
                    I mean, here we're getting back into the subject of how nobody in the setting is actually speaking English, so anything that anybody is presumed to say has to function as a stand-in for something in-character.

                    As for colloquialisms, different people are going to have different standards for what breaks immersion, and there's probably nothing essentially wrong with any given thing.

                    As an in-character insult employed by the Lunar Exalted, something like Moon-blooded, or anything else that might invoke the image of them as interchangeable and expendable minions, is fine by me. I can certainly think of worse things for the Lunars to have called, and done to, many of the Dragon Blooded who reign over Creation.

                    As for the insult registering, I think it's perfectly plausible for it to play off of things like many Terrestrials knowing that many Lunars widely disseminate the narrative that they were the lowest ranked Exalted in a prior age of the world, or how Immaculate mythology still kind of says that they used to be servants under the thumb of the Anathema.

                        I'm entirely against any perspective that portrays the Dragon Blooded as interchangeable antagonists because that really just sees the setting backsliding into some of the worst parts of 2e. 30 Dynasts should be a cataclysm to face at the same time. A non - elder Lunar saying they have the skulls of 30 of them from a single battle should be a boast the same way a 14 year old on Call of Duty saying they have a 3 foot cock is a boast; it makes the person saying it sound like a braggart and a fool.

                        At weakest, Dragon Blooded should be the terrifyingly competent second in commands of the setting. The Odd Job, Boba Fett, or Starscream to a God, Celestial Exalt, or Older Dragon Blood. Fighting one should be the part of the movie where the hero clearly has to put actual effort into victory. The fact that there are ten thousand more where the one you just killed came from shouldn't anonymize them, it should make them terrifying. Absent that, we're back to needing other celestial circles or third circle demons to challenge parties.

                        Exalted is an aesthetic.
