                        As hyped as I am about the DB Kickstarter, I'm usually not very impressed by Onyx Path's stretch goals. Their current system seems to consist of previous edition bundles and t-shirt discounts, neither of which is very compelling. They even stopped selling physical dice add-ons after their MoM Kickstarter so that they could promote their dice app. I guess that some of that stuff is fine, and we should avoid stretch goals that add to the development time (such as for the Ex3 KS). But I kind of wish for more physical rewards, and less things that may appeal to only a handful of people or attempts to get you to purchase more products, like a t-shirt.
