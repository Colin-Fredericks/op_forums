        Since the Realm is supposed to be Chinese or something, so I think the genre Gongdau (宮斗) will be very good for portraying the
        intrigue between the Great Houses.

        The basic plot is that in Chinese imperial harem, concubines and empress will be granted a separate place by the emperor, the ladies then try to eliminate each other to become/remain empress and make sure that their son becomes the next emperor (hence the genre's name, battle         斗 between the hall 宮).

        Korean used the same harem system so they makes novel and Tv shows about this too, though I find them to be so soapy, way too much triangle romance plots between the characters while the murder plots aren't as entertaining.

                    What about Dragon-Blooded Occult makes you think of Link in particular? Does he sniff out jade or hear dematerialized spirits (but only see them if he has a mirror handy)?
                    What makes me think of Occult with Link is exactly what I said: the ocarina of time and the Sheikah Slate (and possibly the Wand of Winds/Storms), but I am umfamiliar with Windwaker) are shoo-ins for sorcerous phylactery artifacts. The Links who use those artifacts use them to initiate into sorcery and cast spells, but as an Exalt, Link would still need to have the Occult rating and the Charms to be a sorceror.

                    I never said he'd have any *other* Dragon-Blooded Occult Charms. He isn't obligated to learn those when he wants to buy up spells instead.


                    Also, why a Wood Aspect? Is it because he wears green?
                    He wears blue in Breath of the Wild, though.

                    But I did mention how Breath of the Wild Link would be getting plenty of mileage out of Survival (finding food, navigating the world, surviving the elements, taming horses), Ride (riding horses and anything else he can mount or snare), and Medicine (making his own health-restoring items and rations).

                    To say nothing of Archery.

                    As I countered to Isator Levi, BotW Link may express plenty of wit and sass despite being canonically silent, but does he seem to be using much of Presence or Socialize to bend people to his will? Or does he get by on being a nice guy who is also the official hero of the world?

                    With his shield, sword, Melee score, archery range, and horse-riding, and all Charms deriving from those, he also doesn't need Dodge Charms so much.


                    So, no. It's not because his default-blue clothes were green in previous games.

                    But even in those games, he does a lot more wilderness-survival, horse-riding, medical self-treatment, and music-playing than he does Presence/Socialize-based social influence. Or even talking.

                    He sails in Windwaker, but with the help of a magical, talking boat.
