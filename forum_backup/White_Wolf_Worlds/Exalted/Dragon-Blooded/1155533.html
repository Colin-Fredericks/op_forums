    School Age Dragon Blooded/Dynasty campaign ideas?

    11-03-2017, 05:08 PM
    I am planing a game set in a Realm Secondary School with un-Exalted Dynasty children. I would like to possibly infuse a Spirderman: Homecoming vibe into it.

    My time has been sorely stretched thine, & I would love some helpful advice & suggestions. Please feel free to pontificate and share at length.

    What type of challenges and intrigues could I breath into the story?

    I am tempted to have all the PC's be children of the Empress herself, to tie them together better and embroil them deeply in the intrigues. I haven't decided on the father(s), yet.

    I also want to emphasize the pressure on the children to Exalt.

    What House would you suggest for their PC's?

    Do you have any suggestions for NPC's?

    Thank you for your help.

              I say just pick the school you want to run. The players can easily be from any house they want. If for some reason inter-house conflict keeps them from working together the instructors can easily force them to work together.

              What you really want is to ensure that NPCs all have differing loyalties. Some place the Realm first, others their house, some faith, some are totally selfish. You'll also want to ensure that the players feel the demands of loyalties and the conflicts that come with it.

              To apply pressure to exalt you simply need to award NPCs that Exalt. Use their exaltation to show favor. An NPC that was once considered a lost cause to family and the school, suddenly Exalting and becoming a favored child should show it well. Add even more if one of the players is from the same house. Take a benefit the player is receiving from the house and give it to the newly Exalted NPC.

              Beyond that a lot depends on a school the players will be attending. The Heptagram really lends itself to some Game of Thrones stuff, but you could change it around to have a more Harry Potter vibe to it. The House of Bells tends to be pretty serious, though it is great for buiding teamwork, but there are lots of older comedies that can be used as inspiration lighten up military training. I'm not so sure that you'll be able to get a Spiderman: Homecoming feel from the Cloister of Wisdom, given the strong religious focus, but you probably could focus more on the Martial Arts aspect and do emphasis the student Sifu relationship. The Spiral Academy probably fits a normal school environment the best, and has the advantage of being in the Imperial City allowing for all sorts of after school shenanigans, that said a school to train kids to be bureaucrats is kinda boring.

                                              Having their mother be the Scarlet Empress, if this fact is widely known, would tend to imply that they're not part of any existing House. Which means that, with her gone, they basically have no support structure. There are enough people loyal to the Empress that their stipend and things they need for school will probably remain intact, but the institutional favoritism in the school is never going to benefit them and will usually work against them.

                                              If the Empress gave her children to various Houses to foster, with the implication that failing to keep the child safe would go poorly for them, the children will fare a little better as the Houses will still be supportive of them to a degree (because the Empress might still come back) but probably not as much as a native member of the house. House V'Neef is probably a good choice here.

                                              Either way, in 3e, a Dragon-Blooded parent can only confer the potential blessing of the dragons on their unborn child about once a decade or so. Which means that, whichever of the players Exalts first, everyone else will tend to assume the others are lost causes. Until they Exalt anyway.
