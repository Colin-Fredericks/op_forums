                                                            Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz View Post
                                                            I'm not talking about the official Wyld Hunt, which has professional spies and Sidereal advisors working for it. I'm talking about the 3 or 4 local Dragonblood who hear of a powerful Exalted beast-lord and think "sounds like an Anathema, it's our job to kill it."

                                                            Of course, that's not to say that it isn't impossible that the masters of the Wyld Hunt might decide an influential or powerful beast-Dragonblood ought to be branded Anathema and slain, as he's probably an agent of those filthy beastman-spawning Lunars.
                                                            I generally agree. What it really comes down to is the local cultures attitudes towards beastmen.

                                                            For the most part in Creation, relations between humans and beastmen tend to be pretty negative. Beastmen are often viewed as pawns of Lunars or Fair Folk and find themselves at war with normal humans, and regions in which beastmen and human live peacefully tend to be extremely rare. While from an out-of-game perspective we know otherwise, many places and people don't even consider beastmen to be human but rather view them as distinctly inhuman monsters.

                                                            If some beastman in Halta Exalted as a Terrestrial people there likely wouldn't care too much or treat him as any different from a normal human who Exalted as a Terrestrial. But in some places a Terrestrial beastman is simply going to be viewed as even more dangerous and monstrous than normal and the local Dragon Blooded will declare it to be Anathema (outside of the Realm/Lookshy that's often a code word for "something we don't like and want to kill,") and go hunt it down because it obviously poses a danger to everyone, especially in regions with a more antagonistic relationship towards beastmen.
