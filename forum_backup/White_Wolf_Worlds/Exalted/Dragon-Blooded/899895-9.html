                    I think that of the 23 Great Houses, most of the ones that we don't see anymore were established around things other than relations to the Empress, and that their elimination has been an ongoing goal of her consolidation of power.

                    ​Look at something like House Jerah; that's been around as early as, what, Realm year forty? Decades before her first child, and when her first husband (who was not called Jerah) was still alive?

                    It looks to me that the early history of the Realm had the Empress (perhaps with some Sidereal guidance) originate the concept of organising Dragon Blooded strictly along family lines as a way of marshalling them to increase their numbers and concentrating them into more manageable packages, so she took a lot of the surviving Exalted (who would likely have been of varying backgrounds and social status in the Shogunate), putting them together and saying "right, you're a family now; you're going to be handed offices and materials as a whole, and here's how marriages are going to work".

                    (My own pet theory is that a lot of these Shogunate elites were also investing into the early government of the Empress, putting forward their own resources to help kickstart civilisation in exchange for a stake in the new power structures and being repaid over time; I think of the stipends of Great Houses as existing within a technical legal basis of repayment of debts, even if the bonds on those debts have been transferred from defunct Houses to newer ones)

                    It was only ever a temporary measure to help get things up and running at the beginning, get some new life into the Exalted devastated by the Contagion, and ensure a spirit of cooperation; uprisings and assassination attempts added urgency to the need to transfer those powers to something conceivably more pliant. Examples like Manosque and Jerah are notably dramatic, but most Great Houses have fallen more quietly, with the Empress seizing on opportunities to cut off their privileges and revenue streams and transfer their assets to new Houses, that were built around direct ceremonial ties to her, sweeping away the remnants of the old regime to establish something whose culture perceived her as more of a fixture.

                    That process may be virtually complete, or may have had a few loose ends hanging, depending on how one assigns Houses that either have no apparent child or consort associated with them, or statements of such are sporadic.
