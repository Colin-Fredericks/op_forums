            Re: communication with transcendent gods, having a more expansive form of awareness is pretty different from communication. Some gods have a larger awareness than humans and feel like talking. Some don't.

            Kulka's seems more like the latter, the UCS is occasionally the former. More of what the UCS would say could be fit into magically enhanced human language, while the Kulka's perceptions, maybe not. Could words really express what it experiences? Or can they only "hint" at them, much like Isator is suggesting is the good strategy to writing a character like Kulka?

            Gods in Exalted are both genuinely transcendent and divine and genuinely petty, at the same time. They're inspired by Dunsany, after all, for all that the tone of Exalted books is more prose, less poetry (and other differences in outlook and focus). They derive from The Gods of Pegana, as much as or more than from the images of Zeus or Apollo as modern people think of them (and of course, the likes of the Kami of Shinto are pretty important as well). And of course, this is in sort of in keeping with contrasts of cholera and glorious demigods fighting for love. You kind of want to keep that up and not collapse it into "They're like us, or they're cosmic idiots whose different perspective is fundamentally a kind of limitation and insanity, rather than a kind of divinity" (although that's always been more a forum thing that a books thing).

                    The weird thing is, if we take some of the later rules written up on the Garduulis into account...it is still sapient enough to follow orders. The write-up on the Daystar was deemed 2.5 canon, and included an explanation that the Garduulis could and would be deployed to recover Aidenweiss should it be stolen from its rightful place. This implies that it is intelligent enough, and sane enough, to follow orders. "Go get the superweapon sword and bring it back."

                    The problem is...the Garduulis is flame incarnate and is miles long. There's going to be collateral damage.

                    According to the 2E Lore...elementals evolve as they grow more powerful. They start off as representations of their element...then they become a quite large dragon...then they become an apocalypse dragon. When these transitions occur, their personalities and behaviors change. The thing to remember, IMO, is that they are not gods, and they do not work like gods. Where gods becomes progressively more powerful and intelligent as they gain Essence, elementals seem to change in fits and starts. Like a god, they start off as something basically unaware of its surroundings. A mere manifestation of its element. They gradually become more intelligent and more powerful as they gain Essence and up to this point, look a lot like the development of a god.

                    Then they hit the Essence necessary for dragonization. They explode upward in size and intelligence, becoming a Lesser Elemental Dragon and leap above the 'second class citizen' status that normal elementals have. Then they sort of plateau for a while...they continue to gain power and the like...until they hit Essence 10. When that happens, the change that overtakes them is just as dramatic. Again, they explode upward in size, becoming something incredibly vast and apocalyptically powerful. And, to the appearance of pretty much everyone else, they go utterly insane.

                    But....the Garduulis sleeps within the sun. The Kukla sleeps beneath the waves, awaiting its appointed time to destroy the world. The Garduulis is obedient to TUS on its instructions to safeguard Aidenweiss. So, they can't be utterly unreasoning monsters. But, again, elementals are not like gods...they don't work the same way gods do, so there is no reason to expect their spiritual growth to work the way a god's does. Here are some options, IMO, that explain their seeming madness.

                    1: Their Mental state changes again, turning them into a true manifestation of their element. Like how an Alchemical with High Clarity becomes machine-like and unrelatable by normal people, a Greater Elemental Dragon becomes element-like and so no longer makes sense to anyone who is not like that element. Their destructive tendencies can be explained pretty well this way. The Elemental Poles spread their influence throughout the world...now you have a miles long ambulatory manifestation of that element...it intends to spread its influence throughout the world, remaking it in the image of its element. They are basically gargantuan terraforming engines...it just sucks to be in the way of their terraforming efforts. However, as they are not completely lost to the rest of the world (just like a high-Clarity Alchemical can still be talked to and interacted with), a sufficiently powerful being, with the right persuasions, can convince them to hold back from their newfound urge to terraform Creation. This is why they sleep...the scraps of relatability left in them were enough to convince them to resist their urge to spread their element, and the easiest way for them to resist it was to hibernate.

                    2: They aren't actually insane. They are completely sane, and totally aware of the ramifications of what happens if they go out and play. Their power is such that their mere presence causes disruption of Essence lines because of the massive elemental flux that they generate, and this results in disasters related to their element. The currently existing GEDs voluntarily entered hibernation, and allowed the misinformation about their madness to be spread in order to prevent further disasters from occurring if more GEDs were born. The maidens prophesied that one day, their unbridled destruction would become necessary....and so they sleep, awaiting that dreadful day when they are to be awakened and must again witness the immense destruction that they cause. The misinformation plot was coined by Luna and enforced by the Maidens, and is persisted as a way to convince LEDs to not attempt to ascend to GED status as a form of damage control to protect Creation. It is possible that the other Incarna voluntarily allowed Jupiter to seal their memories to protect this secret. (Imagine what would happen if there were 50 Kuklas, even if they were all sane.)

                    3: Intentional Sabotage. This one goes out to all the players and STs who like to paint the Incarna as significantly less sympathetic. The Incarna realized that a combined host of Greater Elemental Dragons may be enough to cast them down. After all, they themselves orchestrated the coup against the Primordials, who treated them as second class citizens. Just to be safe, they ensured that a coordinated host of Greater Elemental Dragons could not happen. Perhaps not all of the Incarna are in on it...but at least Jupiter would need to be, and maybe Saturn as well. So, they altered the Fate of elementals such that when they struck Essence 10 and Ascended...their sanity would be terminated. In this case, they are little more than wild dogs that can be aimed at the right thing and unleashed if necessary.
