            Okay, Brawl. When I had heard the name “Become the Hammer”, I actually assumed that it would be a Craft Charm. It’s an interesting combination of Excellency and uncommon dice trick on a damage roll.

            Inescapable Whirlpool Hold refunding Initiative costs for grapples gives me a sense that Water Aspect Brawl is very grappling oriented.

            Heh, I’m expecting a somewhat dense tree, but if these initial Charms are an indication, they’ll mostly be your meat and potatoes types that will not elicit much comment from me.

            Hmm, Blade-Deflecting Palm being based on an enemy’s dice pool reminds me of my old speculation that things such as this create a risk to excessively high dice pools. So apart from its expression of a water theme, it gives me the sense of the Dragon Blooded having their own take on fighting things that are bigger than them. That and how Onslaught eases the burden of having to use the Charm multiple times in a round, even if that comes at the cost of the reduced Defence.

            I’m guessing that Crushing Avalanche Grasp will have some nice synergy with Resistance Charms.

            Ah, I remember the preview of Currents Sweep to Sea. It’s still a neat elemental and tactical effect.

            For the first of the Signatures, there’s Flying Whirlwind Razor, a name that sounds oddly familiar. Its effect of being able to shoot a blade of wind that you have the option to be carried along with sounds enjoyable for the possibilities that it carries. Plus it’s nice to see the occasional elemental blast outside of Elemental Bolt Technique.

            Firmament-Shattering Impact is something like the Dragon Blooded answer to Heaven Thunder Hammer. Possibly not as strong, but apart from the general benefits of flinging people around, it also creates an impact crater that makes it hard for the knocked down opponent to move around when they get up.

            Hmm, Erupting Fury Barrage looks a bit complicated… so you roll a normal decisive attack and get a number of attacks against a single foe equal to your rolled 10s plus 1. The attacks don’t need to be rolled each time, all having the successes of the initial roll. Each time you successfully inflict damage (or just hit? I need to look back at the attack rules), your Initiative is lowered by how many successes you rolled, down to your Essence. When you’ve laid out all attacks, you reset to base as normal, even if you kept missing. Furthermore, successful attacks create a vulnerability in the target meaning decisive attacks cause them to suffer minor explosions. It’s unusual, but I imagine that if applied correctly, it can be unusually powerful.

            Embracing the Violent Flow… ah, of course, one of those cool instances in which a Waterbender would drape water around their shoulders to have tentacles extending from their arms, crossed over with the Octopus Technique, in something reminiscent of that Red Lotus Waterbender from Legend of Korra. Even has the unique benefit of being used with Martial Arts, which I can picture making for some very pretty White Crescent Style.

            (God I’ve got to dig out my Avatar DVDs and rewatch it)

            And Body of Deadly Thorns, to allow Terrestrials their own variation on the dreaded spiky hug.

            Become the Wave is another one that shows a good emphasis on teamwork, and a nice sense that a group of Dragon Blooded can be a bit difficult to pin down.

            Wait… did I misunderstand the mechanics of the Sworn Kinship? When a Dragon Blooded Charm refers to something that keys off of “allies”, is it only referring to Hearth mates? They don’t have a keyword referencing the Hearth, so… I’ll need to check back over some things.

            Anyway, Swift-Striking Tide builds nicely off of the prior Charm by giving one a free attack against somebody that an ally distracted. Yesss, of course I was mistaken in boxing Water Brawl into one niche. There’s a nice combination of grappling, defence and mobility in there.

            Hmm, Waves Swallow the Mountain has to render some opponents awfully vulnerable. I guess that the manner in which Hardness being set to 0 manifests is fairly subtle and metaphysical, unless it’s a simple case of holding somebody so effectively that it becomes easy to hit the vulnerable spots.

            Fist-Spinning Maelstrom offers the alternative take on multiple attacks, ones that hit separate enemies. I believe that multi-attacks for withering are uncommon, and it’s got a clever little elemental manifestation there at the end.

            Drowning Embrace is pretty self-explanatory, although it does remind me that I ought to consider Initiative Crash more often in terms of effects that might capitalise on a vulnerable opponent or end a fight more quickly.

            Hmm, I guess that Hanging Tree Technique does not need to have the Exalt’s limbs actually extend, it just adds some quality of wood to each of them so that they have the strength and firmness to handle one or two targets each. Still, it’s an interesting way to handle the unorthodox idea of grappling multiple enemies at a time, and damaging them therein. It also has to deal with multiple withering attacks.

            And at the top is Smashing Tidal Wave Technique, an enhancement to Currents Sweep to Sea that lets them enhance the water limb they create with their own anima, extending its range, enhancing its power and letting it target opponents that are bigger than usual but distinctly not Legendary Size. That’s an interesting distinction from Solars, even if the overall set has its strengths; it makes me think that Dragon Blooded are a bit more dependent on things like warstriders in the event that a gigantic monster comes rampaging through.

                                Well, since I can’t find a way to enjoy anything at the moment because my brother deeply insulted me, I might as well take this moment to see what they’ve gotten out of Bureaucracy. My big query going in is how Aura will interact with it, considering that it tends to be one of the more passive Abilities.

                                Confluence of Savant Thought bestows the capability of introducing facts using Bureaucracy. That aspect of Lore was a good addition to the game, but it’s a bit unfortunate that Bureaucracy might feel a need to crib it. Finding the Water’s Depths provides a custom read intentions roll to get a sense of what would entice somebody in a bargain action. Testing the Waters is an old Charm, with some new twists; it uses read intentions as the foundation of its effect of determining how somebody will vote in a system now, as well as adding parts such as revealing Intimacies that motivate the intention, and an upgrade that allows one to determine factional lines within a general body.

                                Some other familiar things with delegating authority and speeding along projects; I do like how Dashing Brook Method ties its multiplier to an Attribute reflecting the character’s leadership style, in how it characterizes the Charm as engaging in activity rather than just casting an efficiency spell.

                                Hmm, Unshakeable Mountain Management, an Earth Charm. Boosts the Resolve of members of an organisation against influence to sabotage it or abuse a position. But it only works momentarily, and the Exalt has to be personally present for such attempted influence. I’m on board with the idea that Bureaucracy entails a certain degree of buy-in, that you have to be into those kinds of scenarios playing out as a character that respects their basic principles, but I must admit that I find it a bit of a stretch to think that a Dragon Blood would see somebody acting improperly in one of their organisations, and respond by bolstering people against being taken in by it, rather than intervening directly.

                                Shells-for-Silver Ruse is an interesting crossover with the Water capacity for deceit with the aspect of Bureaucracy focused on perceived economic status or capability.

                                Okay, let’s see if the Signatures have anything a bit more dazzling.

                                Humble Exemplar Attitude lets you provide sizable non-Charm bonus dice to Bureaucracy rolls in service to a project or administrative task that upholds a Defining Principle. It also makes the Principle obvious to everybody participating, and a successful project restores Willpower and automatically instils the Principle (one level lower) into those who could see it. I’d say it’s not half bad; the Terrestrial Exalted’s own take on shaping an organisation to reflect their belief systems, less absolute than that of Solars, but with some benefits in place of that.

                                Graven Stone Edict is a bit closer to being absolute, even if it has the limit that people can follow Defining Intimacies to go against one’s edicts, and the rule created by the Charm needs to be very specific. It’s a nice interplay between institutional order and the Wyld for the rule to help defend people against powers of the Fair Folk.

                                Ah, now Seething Firebreak Technique has an interesting concept to it: galvanizing dissatisfaction within an organisation in order to expose existing opponents (with the added benefit of improving job satisfaction to those who are already on your team).

                                And what can the Water Signature do for its associated Ability? Ah of course, one of those powers for making highly obscure and compelling legal agreements. Mechanically it prevents the character from being aware of certain Intimacies that would oppose a bargain, while it is offset by penalties depending on how severe the results of the clauses would be. If they don’t become aware of the hidden clauses on their own terms, then it twists their mind into assuming that they must have consented, and feeling honour-bound to it. It’s effectively devious.

                                While One Forest, Many Trees… is the first Mute Charm that I’ve seen them have, for one. I was wondering if they would get or need Mute, but if nothing else it would be necessary for Stealth… anyway, it’s a Charm to let a character grant themselves ad hoc Merits representing social contacts. I do like a bit of chicanery with Merit dots such as that, both for the mechanical element (not very obtrusive, and allows one to modify certain factors of a build circumstantially) and for the kind of characterisation that it represents.

                                Jeez, there is a surprising number of Charms past the Signatures…

                                Bestow the Saffron Mantle is another old Charm, but I think in the past it may have just allowed one to grant a character their Bureaucracy dots. For the effects here, while they all look useful as a whole, I’d say the biggest ones are bestowing one’s proxy with an intuitive sense of one’s intentions in the right context, and greatly extending the range to ride senses.
                                Nice to see another Fire Charm in Blazing Hoard of Hesiesh, allowing one to use their relative wealth to enhance social rolls.

                                I imagine that Following the River’s Course plays a major role in the Realm’s financial sector, and the regulation therein. Thrashing Carp Serenade is also returning; I recall that Charm being a form of sabotage, and now it… multiplies the time taken for an organisation to accomplish something, depending on the results of a roll made against the target’s leader.

                                Sea-Changed Secrets enhances attempts at blackmail. Man, a lot of Dragon Blooded Bureaucracy is nasty; no wonder they have such harsh intrigues. It’s an interesting contrast with Solars, with things like striding over an organisation as an exemplar and calling upon old loyalties.

                                A Wood Charm now, with Sprouting Bamboo Cultivation to provide a straightforward but reasonably strong boost to actions that would aid in building an organisation up. The charmingly named Drowning in Negotiation Style returns to allow one to bind people to agreements with the threat of mystically imposed injury.

                                Finally there’s Flowing Authority Insinuation, another Psyche Charm to let one forge credentials or permits to engage in activities, carrying the weight of a Major Intimacy, and the Wood Charm Sowing the Dragon’s Teeth, providing a strong bonus to Strategic Maneuvers that would benefit from some previously established undertaking of logistics. Of all the additional elements, I particularly like how Wood has that theme of drawing people together and collating assets, in contrast to Water slipping through the cracks and seams and tangling people up.

                                                    So yesterday was kind of odd in a manner that meant I was kind of insufficiently motivated to read the book at all. And I’ll admit that at least part of that was a sense of trepidation about reading the Craft Charms.

                                                    But I would feel remiss if I went to sleep without giving the text another look, so I’m ready to get stuck back in.

                                                    I’m curious about any of the limitations that Dragon Blooded will have in comparison to Solars, so it’s interesting that their Excellency is even something that can yield silver craft points for them.
                                                    I’m always going to want the Exalted to have an incentive to use tools, so I appreciate how Stone-Carving Fingers supplements the capacity to work without them with a halfway decent multiplier for using them, even if it’s only for smaller scale and non-magical work with specific materials.

                                                    I think it’s good for Flaw-Finding Examination to build off of the rules for repair and demolition, although I’d need to look back at how the Charm used to work to pin down my exact preference.
                                                    Ah, Shaping Hand Style is an elegant way to extend the materials that Dragon Blooded can work with. It’s also a fun little call back to the elemental Crafts of Second Edition.

                                                    I’d been wondering about Charms that might provide additional sources of crafting points (it appears to me that referring to them as XP has been quietly dropped, unless that shorthand was also in the Solar Charms). With Flawless Facet Realization, it’s more mechanical than I would have expected here (even if Solars also had Charms that provided additional points in methods other than extra objectives to crafting). I wonder if that’s a case for the Ability focus and lower key of Terrestrials, or just that such Charm technology isn’t as viable as I would think.

                                                    Strike the Dragon-Anvil gives an interesting look at a way for a Terrstrial to get at some stronger Craft enhancement, at the cost of the limited craft points.

                                                    And for a pure Fire Charm there’s Passion-Inflaming Artistry. Now, this is interesting; in the past, I’ve expressed a sentiment that I was averse to crafting Charms to lend an inherent emotional quality to what one made, at least for Solars. I saw it as a kind of enchantment, that undermined the relevance of both non-magical crafting and Artifacts. However, the idea of that coming through, not as a Psyche effect, but an inspire action, that implicitly ties the desired emotional tenor to the nature of the actual crafted thing, it feels a bit stronger to me. It’s got me wondering about a few other ways to apply the concept.

                                                    I find Talents-to-Obols Refinement to lend a certain implication to Dragon Blooded crafting; that they might end up with white or gold points that they’re not really in a position to use, and can ensure that they don’t go to waste. Conversely, past Essence 4, they can get a utility out of their Charms to generate silver points as a means of shoring up their gold.

                                                    Ah, I see, Ephemeral Form Composition, which I’ve been seeing repeated glimpses of, is actually their Air aspected Signature. Well, I suppose the idea of projecting images through a sense of worldly patterns still holds true, although I’ve also got another notion of its manifestation… anyway, it seems like a decently powerful crafting effect, at least insofar as it eases some of the burdens of higher level projects, even if they need to find their own ways of getting the numbers. If nothing else, it’s a nice realisation of being able to revise a planning stage.

                                                    Hmm, for that cost in gold points, what might Eternal Omphalos Forge do… provides them with extra terminus to one of the higher crafting projects, a not insignificant benefit (as well as a handy bonus of Willpower if they turn out to have not needed it).

                                                    Conversely, Blazing Dragon-Smith Arete lets them trade in terminus for extra non-Charm dice (plus, strictly speaking, lowering their mote pool by 15 for the duration of the project), and providing an extra source of white points. I wonder if both the lowered terminus and mote pool together might be too hefty a cost even for those non-Charm dice.

                                                    Fortune-from-Flotsam Ingenuity is your standard MacGyver Charm, nicely prevented from overwhelming the system by its craft points cost and limited use, and is finally an instant of bringing a Sworn Kinship into the operation of a Charm (even if it’s not so much combining effects or providing special benefits to a mate as it is their well-being and achievement providing a restoration). I do like the general sense of it being something specially reserved for emergencies, and it kind of enhances my appreciation of Craftsman Needs No Tools. It’s actually an interesting comparison to that, being more limited, but in a manner with some character to it.

                                                    Imago-Hatching Realization is a curious kind of Charm, and possibly among the more flavourful of them, at least insofar as it affects result rather than process. The idea of Craft Charms oriented to particular kinds of products has always come up in regards to redressing deficiencies in the Ability, or speculating on its future, and I’ve often thought it was a bit limited. What’s interesting here is that it’s not about enhancing the crafting of a particular kind of thing, but lends a unique quality to the thing that you make. I especially like the idea that it can make something unique in Artifacts; that’s an area that I have often wondered about ways that different Exalted might have different results, and I like the idea that somebody could pick up a daiklave or the like and find that it has certain tangents in its power, and conclude that it was very likely that a Terrestrial made that.

                                                    Back to the normals, and… does Forge-Hand Prana render Shaping Hand Style a bit of a speed bump? Let me try and compare them… Shaping Hand Style lets you spend 6 motes in order to perform a Fire craft for a basic or major project without tools or workshop, and multiplies the rate of activity by your Essence plus one if you do use tools. Forge-Hand Prana lets you spend 5 mote, 1 Willpower perform a Fire craft basic or major project, or a superior project crafting jade, where you need no tools… ah, but the multiplication is lower. So it frees them up for crafting things that include Artifacts, but the multiplication is lower than the prior Charm. Mind, it’s a case of benefiting the crafting of Artifacts without the need for tools, and I don’t quite like that, least of all for Dragon Blooded. It also partakes of the Dragon Blooded inclination for fighting, by the flaming hands being viable to enhance unarmed fighting (if not the best tools for it).

                                                    And finally Stoking Inspiration’s Forge allows them to gamble the silver points they get from projects on another source of gold points. There are not yet any Charms above Essence 3.

                                                    Now, I’m somebody who’s on board with the crafting system, so I’ll have no discussion on the merits of its basic existence. What’s important to me here is that, in the past, I always found Dragon Blooded to really illustrate the weaknesses of the Craft system. As Ability Exalts, they needed to do something with it, in contrast to Lunars for whom it didn’t need to be as big a priority, Sidereals who were going weird anyway, and Abyssals playing about the same game as Solars were, just limited to killing. Alchemicals had a basis to be kind of as good at it as Solars, and Infernals could more or less get away with never having to address the subject at all (one could kind of assume that they might have Charms to help with it, but the Yozi Charm setup let it not feel like an outright deficiency if they never did). And it didn’t afford much variation and Solars got to soak up the basic good stuff, so the best that Terrestrials really got amounted to… certain specific forms of direct material manipulation and this one strange (and kind of broken, when applied to Artifacts) Charm to break things down into their raw materials, to be placed on top of inferior multipliers.

                                                    Here, I think the Dragon Blooded get something with a bit more punch to it, and remarkably without needing to fall back on things like pulling up earthworks and the like. I think the methods of enhancing crafts interact meaningfully with the system, maintaining their place in the hierarchy while providing a few costly avenues for shooting at the heights of it, and that its got some good ways of letting them go elemental metaphorically. I appreciate how the primary focus remains on the crafting, and I think it’s got some good methods of realising that.

                                                            Okay, two Abilities left. The Dragon Blooded have had some decent defence from the physical, and the Solar Charms went in some interesting directions with Integrity, so I’d like to see how they deal with the social and mystical.

                                                            Granite Curtain of Serenity is another nice example of the elemental take on an Excellency extending it to offsetting some penalties, particularly the Earth quality of mitigating pain or weakness. Follow up with a couple of basic Charms to let them lend a different emotional tone to their Resolve through the elements, in the form of calculating the stat with different Attributes.

                                                            Oath of Ten Thousand Dragons looks a lot more straightforward this time around, but an ongoing bonus to Resolve in defence of a sworn loyalty or duty sounds like a good benefit all on its own. Certainly the kind of thing that will help a Wyld Hunt resist the honeyed words of an Anathema. Heart-Hardening Meditation looks similarly useful.

                                                            Hmm, I wonder how one might play the experience of Inviolate Dragon Spirit. It’s like… overcoming Resolve bolstered by an Intimacy represents an argument that gets around it, but then they’ve got a Charm to… I suppose bolster their commitment to the Tie or Principle, and use it as a basis for resisting a convincing argument. Mind, I sounds like the sort of thing that would make any sufficiently invested Terrestrial difficult to persuade indeed.

                                                            Heh, Unquenchable Battle-Passion, unmoved by pleas for mercy. It’s a nice image, enflaming your own passion to contain the battle against any attempts to dissuade you.

                                                            I need to look back at the relevant Solar Charms to assess Chaos-Warding Prana’s effect on the Wyld, beyond the benefit it can provide to a Sworn Kinship.

                                                            Ohhhh, Ten Thousand Dragons Fight as One, what might this do with an impressive name like that? Errrr forming an ad hoc Charm-bolstered oath of loyalty towards another Terrestrial that one sees in distress. Not bad, not bad.

                                                            And to go along with Integrity steeling one against pain is Wound-Denying Dragon Faith, letting one bolster dedication Charms with being able to ignore wound penalties.

                                                            Now, the Signature Air is one Ascendant Ideal Inspiration, allowing one to bolster dedication to a powerful ideal with a novel method of interfering with attempts to weaken it or make one act against it and make it easier to win other people over to that ideal. The cost of that, apart from the basic Charm cost, is that one needs to actually stand by the ideal, lest breaking with it cause the severe psychic shock of losing all Willpower. I think it’s another nice little example of Dragon Blooded Integrity going in some unique directions, particularly with the element of improved instil actions.

                                                            Aw jeez, if some of the other Integrity Charms made it harder to influence a Terrestrial, Flawless Diamond Heart really takes the cake. Reducing the Willpower costs to as low as zero for maintaining one’s Major and Defining Intimacies, and even being able to reset it by acting in accordance with that Intimacy. I’m just picturing the cocksure Solar who is so sure that they can convince anybody of anything, getting a rude awakening with the shikari who is having none of it.

                                                            Hmm, Immolating Phoenix-Soul Fury has a name with a concerning implication. Ah, good thing that it’s not as literal as its protesting monk name suggests. It’s just a simple matter of being able to respond to somebody trying to mind control you by losing your shit. Mind, from what I’ve heard in the past, many players responded like that regardless of the character they were playing, and without need for special magic; this just has some clearer mechanical process, and a special reward for winning. Plus the image of somebody trying to use a Psyche effect in a relaxed, social situation and running off to fight somebody… or possibly starting an actual war.

                                                            Waves-Swallow-Mountains Persistance allows the Exalt to subtract 1s from an opponent’s influence rolls, with the curious distinctions of the Charm actually getting stronger the more that it’s used, but it can only be used against the same person during a scene. Man, Water can have some weird stuff sometimes.

                                                            And Roots-of-the-World Meditation lets one go into a physically or even mentally heal oneself from a variety of ailments. I appreciate using that as the basis for a sidebar that clarifies precisely what meditation is in mechanical terms.

                                                            Heh, I like the wordplay underlying the Charm name Thicker Than Stone, and the associated boost to Resolve against influence against positive feelings towards other Terrestrials, the family, and subordinates, interestingly enough.

                                                            With all of the Charms based around dedication to an oath, it would be incomplete if one didn’t have something to swear eternal vengeance against somebody who has wronged one. Unflagging Vengeance Meditation sounds rather strong even before I can see the Resistance Charms that it bolsters, particularly with the added soak and Hardness that it provides. Mind, the power in one’s blood is not messing around with holding you to it, if it eats away at your Willpower and then your body.

                                                            And while The Mountain Still Stands is quite short and simple, being able to reap Willpower back from successfully defending one’s Defining Intimacies synergizes nicely with several other Charms to improve Resolve, Intimacies and change social Willpower costs.

                                                            So Dragon Blooded Integrity is a good deal more low key than what the Solars can get… oh damn, that’s also where they had a Charm or two to get spirit Charms that aligned with powerful Intimacies as well, isn’t it? Anyway, it can definitely be seen as weaker on fronts such as that, not quite having the same defence against malefic powers and transformations, and even several of the actual things that manage Intimacies, but it’s not boring, or irrelevant, and it has its own distinct lines of strength running through it. As a possible comparison to Solars, I actually find it to be one of the more interesting ones.
