        I mused on this a while back.

        The God of Fire would probably be a bureaucrat who administrates when fires are supposed to happen (or not happen, as the case may be) as part of Heaven's plan they also double as an ambassador to the courts of prominent Fire Elementals. They may recognise the value of fire and be fascinated by it in ways that make the other gods nervous but it's something other to them, a force to be directed and controlled rather than something innate to them.

        As such the Exalt of such a god would be all about manipulating the element but their lack of an inner flame leaves them crucially lacking a means to will it into existence. Without the correct set-up they would be much weaker than a Fire Aspect.

                                I'm currently playing a DB superhero in an E5 Modern game alongside a group of Sidereals, Lunars and Alchemicals. I think it's fair to say that she's one of the group's best fighters.

                                They have three main weaknesses in high level combat.

                                1) Lack of perfect defences that can block Unblockable/Undodgeable attacks. Without assistance from other DBs using Conflaguraiton of Warding, they only have one, Dragon Vortex Attack's shield function. This makes DVA and Wrath Directing Gesture important Charms for high level combat, but luckily they are great Charms in their own right even without the PD function.

                                2) Lack of accuracy. Unlike most other splats, DBs generally lack means to boost their accuracy temporarily beyond their base dice pools, reduce target DVs or otherwise hit high DV targets with consistency. This can be overcome using certain CMAs but I consider that cheating.

                                3) Reliance on External Penalties for defence. Depending on the form of attack, a DB can have around a -5 external penalty to attacks on top of their DV. This is a solid defence, but it is vulnerable to the few Charms that negate external penalties.

                                Other than that they have a functional combat suite and are great at assisting a group. DB Dodge especially is a lot of fun.
