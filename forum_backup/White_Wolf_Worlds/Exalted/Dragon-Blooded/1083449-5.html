                                                        Concerning Wyld Wandering Forester's Charm, I think that it could be useful in a couple of aspects. First, it allows a DB to introduce a fact using Wits plus Survival, rather than Intelligence plus Lore, which suggests that some of the DB Charms will probably involve similar forms of substitution, allowing DB characters to be potentially very versatile (imagine the utility of a similar Melee Charm that allows a DB to replace Dexterity plus Thrown with Dexterity plus Melee when throwing a weapon that can also function as a melee weapon or a Thrown Charm that allows a DB to replace Dexterity plus Melee with Dexterity plus Thrown when wielding a weapon in melee combat that can also function as a thrown weapon). Second, it allows a DB to introduce a fact about the natural world of Creation without penalty (Lore seems to focus on introducing facts about the manmade world of Creation). Third, I think that an introduced fact would allow a DB to support another roll by reducing the difficulty of another roll (Medicine if the DB introduces a fact about a medicinal herb, Resistance if the DB introduces a fact about a hazard, etc), though we will have to see what other rules are introduced by the DB book before we know for sure.

                                                            So I was re-reading these DB charms (I have an essay on grammar to write, so obviously I'm finding ways to procrastinate), and I noticed this:
                                                            Originally posted by Spirit-Chaining Strike
                                                            While it does not become fully materialized, it is visible to all Dragon-Blooded characters in the scene, and can attack or be attacked by them as though it were material.
                                                            Then of course you have:
                                                            Originally posted by Dragon's Hidden Treasure
                                                            This Charm can also be used to steal objects that other Dragon-Blooded have hidden using it.
                                                            So, essentially, it doesn't matter which DB used the charm, it interacts with all DBs regardless (Dragon's Hidden Treasure of course works best for the DB who activated it, because they know where the item is; but any other DB who knew where it was could get it out).

                                                            I wonder if this is going to be a general thing across the DB charmset, making them very good at co-operating with each other, but no more than anyone else with other Exalt types. But, at the same time, making them more vulnerable to each other if they don't work together (which, of course, in the Dynasty they don't do that well, as it's designed to keep them fighting each other instead of rising up against the Empress).

                                                            This seems like a very elegant solution to "how are DB charms cooperative", much more so than the 2nd ed cooperative charms which generally didn't work that well, or were that interesting.
