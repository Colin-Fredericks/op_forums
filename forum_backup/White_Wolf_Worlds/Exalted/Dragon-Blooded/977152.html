    Dragon Bloods are Daemons?

    09-27-2016, 09:52 AM
    Something that just clicked for me right now which I think is mildly fascinating. Thought I'd share.

    Ok so Yozi's are defeated "Primordials".
    - 3rd Circle Daemons are the highest form of their souls made physical
    - 2nd Circle Daemons are further dilutions of the 3rd
    - 1st Circles are essentially offshoots of the 2nd


    Now consider that Gaia IS a Primordial and remains to be one to this day. And that during the primoridal war she helped the Gods by:
    - Creating 5 Elemental Dragons (i.e.: her "3rd Circle" Daemons)
    - Her Elemental Dragons empowered 40 Males and 160 Females each who were massive breeders (i.e.: her "2nd Circle Daemons")
    - These 1,000 Dragon-Blooded Progenitors bred the first Terrestrial Army for the Primordial War (i.e.: 1st Circle daemons being "offspring" of the 2nd Circles)


    Naturally the name "Daemon" doesn't exactly apply to the Dragon-Blooded, but its an interesting parallel. Could be worth a story-thread to some Storytellers. Or it could be ignored by everyone who reads this :P
