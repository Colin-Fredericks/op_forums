          Scroll of the Monk. Repeated in Scroll of Heroes.

                      Pssh, you guys just don't know all the fun we be having in my games with Face... basically it is as useful as your ST is willing to make it.                                                   Originally posted by L'het'esh View Post
                                                  Thank you all for your help.

                                                  I was actually thinking of making an Islesi musician with high (Performance, of course) Presence, Socialize, and Martial Arts. I am considering Swaying Grass Dance style with a pair of God Kicking Boots while Hearthstone Bracers add to Dodge and the Hearthstone could be a Jewel of Youthful Suppleness. Suggestions? Criticism?
                                                  Swaying Grass Style is a good choice for a Performer/Martial Artist. Remember that you will need an allied musician, preferably one with Performance 5, to make full use out of the capstone. If your party includes a sorcerer, demon harpists are ideal for this.

                                                  For your artifact selection, keep in mind that as per Scroll of Errata, equipment bonuses do not stack. Therefore the -1 Speed for your hearthstone bracers and jade God Kicking Boots are redundant. To remedy this, I suggest using one of the refined jade options from Oadenol's Codex, which do not enhance Speed but instead offer other bonuses. Black Jade is good for Defence, and can ameliorate the boots' poor Defence value if you want to invest in PDV. Red maximizes damage, which is otherwise probably your best option.

                                                  However, also consider fighting with DB Performance Charms. They can be quite strong for ranged attacks (especially against creatures of darkness) and with the right Charm selection they can offer a stunt-upgraded PDV which is immune to external and internal penalties.

                                                  Finally, look into some of the native DB martial arts Charms. The ones in the Charms chapter of MoEP Dragon-Blooded explicitly work in conjunction with any style, so you can use Dragon Claw Elemental Strike to supplement your God Kicking Boot attacks and Blade-Deflecting Palm to boost your PDV.
