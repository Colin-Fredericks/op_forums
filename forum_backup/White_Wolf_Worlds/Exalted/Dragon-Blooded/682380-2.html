                                        Huh. I guess I like my DB's a little more powerful than most people have them. I know they're not supposed to be as strong, but Terrestrials are still Exalted. Indeed the only real mechanical difference is their Charms--a DB with STR 5 Melee 5 and a Solar with STR 5 Melee 5, wielding the same weapon with no Charms, are going to do the same amount of damage. Of course Charms are a massive advantage for the Celestial (especially if they're a Solaroid). But I really like the idea of a crafty/incredibly skilled DB being able to hold their own anyway. They'll have to use every goddamn trick in the book, and will probably collapse in exhaustion at the end (which is why the Realm uses brotherhoods in the Wyld Hunt, to make things easier), but they could take on a Celestial single-handedly. I also like the idea of them using their weaker status as an advantage, making their Celestial opponent go "Hah, this guy's just a Dragon-Blooded, this is gonna be so damn easy--wait what he's actually doing damage, shit, looks like I gotta actually care--oh fuck I'm getting beaten on by a fucking Terrestrial what the hell--". And then the Celestial overextends themselves, angry and hellbent on proving their superior status amongst the Exalted Host--only to fuck up that one critical moment and get a jade daiklaive to the chest. The bigger they are, the harder they fall, after all.

                                        Y'all already know I like my Exalted anime as hell, and few things are more anime than the scrappy underdog beating the much bigger guy. Even the mighty Goliath was slain by the humble David.

                                                    Originally posted by Frostav View Post
                                                    But I really like the idea of a crafty/incredibly skilled DB being able to hold their own anyway. They'll have to use every goddamn trick in the book, [...]
                                                    The problem I'd have with this is...
                                                    ...who says the Solar hasn't read the same book?

                                                    Basically, saying "With enough determination, DBs can beat Solars" sounds to me as if a Solar had a fixed power level which a DB can overcome if he just... spends enough willpower, or something. As if the Solar couldn't do the same. They're both fighting for their lives in this scenario, and once the Solar recognises the DB as actual threat, he can be just as determined to survive.
                                                    Also, I immensely dislike definite outcomes.

                                                    Which is why I imagine [a one on one fight] as a "One in X" chance for a DB to win, with X being a rather large number. (At least 8, I guess.) The difference is, there are (metaphorically) ten thousand dragons, and only three hundred Solar(oid)s. Adding in the (3ed) 400 Lunars makes 700 Celestials (assuming the Sids stay in the background). Assuming the "10 thousand" number is anywhere near the truth, that makes a bit more than 14 DB per Celestial. So, even in consecutive 1-on-1 battles of DB vs. Celestial, the DBs could have an advantage. Add in that their powers work better the larger their groups are, and... well, luckily, they aren't one large group, and you're probably going to have a few on your side after a while.

                                                    But until then, don't go into open warfare. They have reserves. You might be able to kill this one, and the next, and the one after that, but one day, you will slip. You can't kill all of them, and they only have to kill the one of you.


                                                    Of course, once you get to groups, all bets are off, and it starts to depend on group synergy and teamwork.
                                                    And DBs have a strong tendency to be good at teamwork and synergy. Heck, if I remember correctly, they had a charm that allowed them to instantly form an intimacy to a group of allies, allowing even usually mortal enemies to fight together like brothers against a common enemy.
                                                    (Edit: Looked it up. "Unflagging Vengeance Meditation", MoEP: DB, page 144. You instantly form an intimacy towards a goal, and [your Essence] other characters can join you. If anyone abandons the goal prematurely, they suffer [Essence of all characters together] lethal damage.)
