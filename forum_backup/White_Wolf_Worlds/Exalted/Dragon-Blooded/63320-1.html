        Originally posted by Amine Hsu View Post
        While I don't disagree with your overall point it probably isn't a good idea for DBs, this same logic could be used to say Infernals don't need Shintai either.
        This is true in Ex2, where all Infernal charms serve to transform Infernals into Yozis and shintai are just major steps in the process.

        The Ex3 Infernal preview makes it clear that shintai are generally temporary, that they're not 100% compatible with Infernal charms and that they unlock special charms. Presumably, the shintai take over the job of transforming Infernals into cosmic titans without all the baggage of Ex2. Shintai seem to open up new abilities that are not part of Infernal Exaltation by default, in a manner vaguely similar to Lunar shapeshifting.

        Elemental dragon-bodies, however, are the default state of the Dragon-Blooded. The Dragon-Blooded cannot transform into an elemental form because they were born with an elemental body and they spend their lives developing it through their charms and Essence (or rather, they learn charms and increase their Essence by developing their elemental bodies).

        If there is such a thing as an elemental shintai, it's Terrestrial Exaltation.

                        Vance's tweets mention that Unassailable Body of [Element] is still in 3e, so I'm guessing DBs will retain some light transformative effects, but I wouldn't really want them to have a "secret ultimate form". Aesthetically, the Dragon-Blooded equivalent to Deadly Beastman Technique is basically what you get automatically for having high Breeding and going full anima. Despite their elemental associations they're one of the more "human" types of Exalted, so I think it'd be a bit weird if it went farther than that.

                        TCS is where you see the rawest expression of pure elemental themes, and I could see some of this stuff joining Invulnerable Body of Bronze in the TCS roster. Given what we've heard about different sorcerous initiations and splat-specific custom initiation charms, I have my hopes up that Terrestrial Exalted sorcerers will have some way to specialize in spells that reflect their Aspect, which would help make it more than a purely aesthetic choice.
