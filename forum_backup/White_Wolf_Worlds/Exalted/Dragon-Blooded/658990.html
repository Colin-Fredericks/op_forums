    The Dragon Blooded Retinue

    09-10-2015, 02:06 AM
    I've been working on a Wood Aspect fresh from the House of Bells who's eager to make a name for himself the past couple of days. One of the coolest things that I think DB players get to do is spend all those Background dots on stuff - thinking up ways to make your Manse background more than just a magic rock or what exactly are the small fluff bits that make your Jade Short Powerbow more than just another identical Jade Short Powerbow.

    In this case, anything killed by Stalker's Bequest is gonna taste DELICIOUS - which is cool when it's deer or something, but takes a turn for the creepy when you kill some bandits.

    But by far the best Backgrounds to gnaw on are ones like Henchmen and Retainers! Yay NPCs that do stuff for you!

    Anyone got any examples of current or former members of your character's retinues they have always wanted to talk about but no one ever gave them the chance to talk about before this elegant and finely crafted thread opened up?

    When one is accustomed to privilege, equality seems like oppression.

      So, I played a character called Tepet Al'Vir in a long running DB game, savant/general dude who loved airships, built a fleet, now skytyrant of the Realm.

      In his early adventures he and his circle defended Gem from the Autonthonians by defending a pass that had an old temple to the unconquered sun in it, a fully anatomically correct temple of the unconquered sun....

      Anyway scene setting aside there was fighting going on at all corners, queue the most valuable of the retainers, the cook, who dutifully prepared tea and scones and other sweet things for the party. Granted he occasionally ran away in terror and had to be found and dragged back, because you simply cant fight without refreshment.

      That cook was with the character right up until the end, all the way from Gem to Harbourhead, Thorns. Sijian, Nexus, Halta, Wavecrest, fighting the lover and the hislanti in the north, he grew more of a spine eventually.

      Loved that cook.

              Originally posted by hippokrene View Post
              A more general question: What sort of retinue would you expect of a traveling Dragon-Blood?

              For example, what sort of servants would I see for a young sworn brotherhood making their way around the Threshold, a single Dragon-Blooded captain in the army returning home, and an established, well-to-do Dragon-Blooded touring their House's estates?

              That is a difficult question because it depends on the house, the individual wealth of the DB and such.

              As an example again Mr Al'Vir in the early days had a cook and a couple of body guards. At the end never travelled anywhere really without a talon of troops, DB bodyguards and a small force of admin and servants, and the cook....

              But in general:

              Single Dragon-Blooded captain in the army - As little as a squire to help with armour and household tasks and maybe as much as the squire, a clerk for the book keeping, a cook and pleasure servant.

              Young sworn brotherhood making their way around the Threshold - Kinda depends on the method of travel but a couple of toughs, a cook, the caravan master, that type of thing.

              Well-to-do Dragon-Blooded touring their House's estates - Think about the entourage that accompanies high level dignataries.

                  Originally posted by hippokrene View Post
                  A more general question: What sort of retinue would you expect of a traveling Dragon-Blood?
                  A relative unExalted Dynast serving as a squire to soak up what being Exalted entails as a 'special tutoring session' to motivate a reluctant student with a young head in the clouds who turns out to be full of hero worship. This could alternatively be modeled as the 'ward' flaw. (mechanically, they could provide social bonuses to Dynastic interactions)

                  A middle-aged but stout patrician historian assigned to record your Exalted deeds, but also a source of local and global history for those who haven't read all the tomes they have or didn't pay so much attention in classes. (mechanically they could help with Lore rolls)

                  A citizen quartermaster who adequately handles your accounts, mounts, armor and, yes, meals. The quartermaster is constantly worried about money for the consequences of overdrawing promised him by one of your relatives when he was assigned to you, but with no actual power to veto any of your financial decisions. Eventually comes around to seeing money as a tool, not a limit, and will aid your character in acquiring their own funds independent from their House and aid in financial investigations. (mechanically they could substitute for or enhance the resources or contacts backgrounds or aid in specific investigation rolls)

                  A peasant from your family's horse farm's first adventure away from home to learn how the stallions' and mares' training holds up to the demands of an Exalted and their retinue. Generally uneducated and hopeless in combat, the peasant has ridden horses since walking and has an uncanny understanding of the animals. Beyond the usual horsemanship you've learned, this retainer can teach you how to truly understand your mount. (mechanically, they could reduce training times for Ride or Ride specialties and reduce the control rating of your party's mounts)

                  A slave with a talent for eavesdropping that can be easily disguised as a beggar to learn the local rumors or gauge the charity of other NPCs. (mechanically, they could substitute for a single dot of contacts)

                  Of course, you could have either gendered take on the above, and even pair them up since you get several henchmen or retainers. Two historians who love to argue about the finer points of the Seven Tigers Rebellion and the ethics of the deployment of the Imperial Manse, but are the best of friends because nobody else understands why you would even have a stance on such an ancient topic (that they insist is relevant to the current day!)

                                                      One of my favorite ideas that I never got to use was about a traveling Dynast, who was a dandy and a fop, but a pretty decent guy, as Dynasts go. He kept his Retainers well-fed, dressed and paid, and was respectful towards them, if a bit comically high-handed.

                                                      Thing is, danger tends to find the Exalted, whether as bandits, behemoths, cults or assassins, and when it did, he would leap into action...which he sucked at. He would inevitably be quickly knocked out by the attackers, if he didn't trip and knock himself out first. If neither, then he'd be "accidentally" knocked out by his own Followers...

                                                      For they kept a secret from their "lord". In truth, his servants are a Circle of Solar Exalted, who successfully hid their true natures from him, and use him as a key to get all sorts of places they'd have to work to get into normally...as well as to travel in comfort and style! Over time, they've grown quite fond of their "master", actually respect him in some ways, and certainly don't want to see him actually get hurt or killed.

                                                      So, he comes to after all the action is over, groggily awed to hear his servants describe how he lost himself in his battle-madness and utterly destroyed or drove off the enemy, at which he puffs himself up again, and assures them that he will never allow harm to come to his frail but faithful followers!

                                                      I don't know how long this set-up could actually last in-play, but it always gives me a chuckle...
