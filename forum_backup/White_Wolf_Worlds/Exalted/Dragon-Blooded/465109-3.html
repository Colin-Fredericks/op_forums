                                                Originally posted by Murcushio View Post
                                                Hence my qualifier, "all things being equal." It's impossible to get there absent some kind of perfectly constructed arena, of course. Even a white room isn't "equal" because you're taking stunts out of the equation. But you can generally rough out an idea of it, can you not?

                                                It is true that something like "an Elder DB stumbles across a Lunar who Exalted twenty seconds ago" is going to result in that Lunar completely being obliterated, and "Raksi somehow gets loose inside the Spiral Academy dormitories at three in the morning, filled with sleeping teenage untrained Dragon-Blooded" is going to result in her having a colossal DB body count.

                                                But those are obviously edge cases, are they not?

                                                Also, I find it wearying to have to type out a long string of qualifiers and caveats every time I make a statement. "A Lunar should generally eat a Dragon-Blood for breakfast" seems like it ought to automatically carry with it the rhetorical weight of "obviously, there are times when this will not be case" unless clearly specified otherwise. That's what the "generally" is for.
                                                The "generally", unfortunately, makes the statement so vague as to be completely without rhetorical value. If you took any one of the 300 or so Lunars at random, and pitted them against any one of the what, 30,000 DBs at random, sure, it's probably better to bet on the Lunar. But some of those matchups are a lot more likely than others. So, if we account for which characters are actually likely to encounter each other I don't know that it's necessarily clear cut. Or rather, some of those fights will be absurdly one-sided (in either direction) and others will be close, and honestly in aggregate I'm not sure because the terms are pretty nebulous. But then Plot comes along and says that anyone can encounter anyone at any time, so EVERYTHING goes out the window. In the context of a story a young Lunar could run into Memnon, or a bunch of Dynast teenagers might run afoul of Leviathan. Which side the PCs happen to be on probably has more to say about default assumption than your splats do!

                                                In short, there are just too many things under the blanket of "all things". I get the desire to not caveat every single sentence to death, but some minimal amount of context would go a long way to clarifying your thoughts.

                                                Maybe, just maybe, in an established edition like 2.X where the fundamentals of combat are basically a solved problem, it's worthwhile to talk in those sorts of sweeping statements, when "generally" could reasonably be taken to mean something like "both characters are roughly equally invested into the default combat kit on which general consensus has settled for their splat." But I don't know that we have enough information about the way the new rules work to know if it's even strictly true in the broadest sense anymore. Who-would-win-in-a-fight may be an even firmer "it depends" than it used to be, for all we know.
