                        Just a minor addition to what others have said. Enfolded in the Dragon's Wings allows a DB commander to protect his entire legion from the effects on his/her anima and from attacks like dragon vortex. So if a wing of troops is walking towards your anathema, with their commander gained lots of bonus successes for free because of their army at their back while sitting in the middle of a giant flame tornado that moves with his unit and is directed by a special character other DB in the unit, well, then you have something that looks pretty threatening even if your Celestials will triumph. That is just two Dragon Bloods and some mortals, now if you have two sworn brotherhoods you can have five units like that. Also if you are using an honest to god legion their are at least 5 dragons of troops at their disposal which can add tons of successes in mass combat.

                        I know people poo poo mass combat, but if you ignore endurance (which in my experience battles don't last long enough, or your players get bored, before it becomes a factor) and do a little prep it just becomes a wad of free successes for your dragonbloods.

                        Not to mention DBs get a perfect defense when leading a mass combat unit with their upgrade to portentous comet deflecting mode.

                                            Originally posted by Savare View Post
                                            I have some high end lunars and Solars that are about to face the Realms Dragon Blooded legions. How do I make them scary? The Lunars and Solars are kicking around high 30's dice pools on average.
                                            A better question to ask is why would the Terrestrials would agree to fight a battle that is so heavily against them?

                                            To make the Terrestrials a functional threat, stop thinking of them as mooks and start thinking of them as players fighting a superior foe. No starting character solar will willingly stand their ground when a group of deathlords show up.

                                            Step 1: Read Sun Tzu's the Art of War

                                            Step 2: Avoid direct combat with the Celestials at all costs unless you can catch them alone with nearly overwhelming numbers and fire power.

                                            Step 3: Make the Celestials constantly choose between goals. They can attack the Blessed Isle or they can abandon the attack to counter an attack on their supply lines. If they attack the Realm, they may take ground but they will lose resources and allies. If they route the terrestrial preemptive strike, they lose the momentum to attack the Blessed Isle for a while longer, thus giving the Terrestrials a chance to make more plans and set up better defenses.

                                            Step 4: A Terrestrial legion of 5,000 soldiers can hit multiple targets at the same time. Unless the Celestials have spent a great deal of time setting up teleportation infrastructure to get from place to place quickly, there are just not enough Celestials to fight all the battles at once.

                                            Thus the way to win or at least provide a challenge with Terrestrials is to think along the lines of having the Celestials win individual battles but lose the war.

                                            Or if you are dead set on having the Terrestrials be suicidal, remember that Summon Elemental doesn't have a limitation on power level, just summon a half dozen lesser elemental dragons to bolster the strength of the legions.

Ideally, this will happen in a situation where they have a massive strategic and tactical advantage, but perfect hits don't come along every day and eventually you'll have to make some kind of call along the lines of "we know they're down some unspecified number of motes and their allies aren't all with them, this is as good as it will probably ever get, go time is now."
