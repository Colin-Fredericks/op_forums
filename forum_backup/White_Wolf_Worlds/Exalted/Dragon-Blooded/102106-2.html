                                Originally posted by Weimann View Post

                                On a tangent, I personally don't even think that's the biggest problem with the Terrestrial Aspects and their Ability distribution. The fact that your Aspect determines 1) your elemental theme as a whole, 2) your favoured Abilities to the point of putting a surcharge on mote costs when going outside them and 3) your general emotional and psychological direction is at least one too much.
                                Frankly, I actually like the elemental nature/psyche aspect of DBs. It's actually kind of interesting to see precisely how the element expresses itself in a person, and the broadness of scope is sufficient to allow both Peleps Deled and the majority of house Peleps to be Water Aspects, for one example, so it's not really all that restrictive. And determining elemental theme is, well, kind of going to be a given. XD

                                But anyway, as to the topic of the thread--Dragon-Blooded don't just manipulate the elements in people, they manipulate the elements in themselves--change the fires in their heart to match the fires in yours, guide their inner earth around an inner flame, and so forth. There is also, as Weimann and others have said, a distinct non-elemental theme that intersects with that in a way I think is beneficial to them--they're nobility. And that's one of my favorite things about them, by the way, if people try to narrow them down to benders I'll be quite annoyed.

                                            I'd point out that the Blessed Isle and Lookshy are not the only places with Nobility.
