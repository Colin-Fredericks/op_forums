        Originally posted by Aranfan View Post
        If I were trying to make DB Lois Lane, Intrepid Reporter... any goodies for that build in Heirs?
        Originally posted by DrLoveMonkey View Post

        A charm that lets you much more easily profile or read the intentions of a fairies and other Wyld creatures, as well as case the scenes they were involved in, or even just Wyld energy. One that lets you read the intentions and break encryption of other terrestrials better, it also lets you communicate with them across language barriers. One that lets you raise your vision to a point in the sky and see everything from above.

        The bureacracy ones are pretty good. One lets you instantly spot what kind of bureaucratic standing in what kind of bureaucratic organization a character has. Like spotting a member of the Deliberative out in the street, or picking out an important guild factor. One that lets you introduce facts about any kind of bureaucracy, even if you're not familiar with it, as long as you've investigated it for a scene.

        One coming off of the Doctor Who psychic paper charm lets you convince people that you not only belong where you are, but are in a position of authority there.

        A resistance charm that lets you store your armor in an elemental expression also lets you form exceptional clothing out of it, which also gains benefits for the element like water adding to disguises or air adding to stealth.
        I'd add, given it's Lois Lane and she has a black belt in getting into trouble, Heart Conquering Prowess in presence might be good, a balanced fire charm that adds appearance/2 to your attack roll and lets you instill ties. It plays like the trope where the nosey reporter is being intimidated by the mob boss and she puts him on his ass, earning his respect.

        There's a seduction one too in presence, if you're the type of reporter/game to use a honeypot scheme, plus two good socialise ones – one that makes you always look clean/dapper ('how does she manage to look that good in the middle of a war zone?') and lowers the cost of lots of other social charms, and one that enhances any disguise you've put together to sneak into where you shouldn't be ('yes, that is my name tag, I was having a bad hair day').
