        I think a local culture's attitudes towards a beastman will tend to trump more generalized attitudes.

        Hawkmen for example are accepted and welcomed in Halta, but in places that have suffered recent raids from the hawkmen of Mount Metagalapa, they'll probably view a Haltan Hawkmen as an enemy, even if the guy says, "Honestly, I don't know anything about those Metagalapans!"

        The Linnowan are probably going to be fairly hostile towards all beastmen, due in part to their longstanding war with Halta (which has fairly close relations to beastmen) as well as the fact that Linnowan is so close to the Realm which tends to frown on beastmen as well.

        Just because a breed is accepted in one place doesn't necessarily mean that it's accepted everywhere. It's also important to remember that most people in Creation tend to have negative attitudes and stereotypes towards beastmen, viewing them primarily as dangerous monsters rather than fellow humans. So while you may have local level interactions they will still be often colored by, "Well this beatman (or tribe) is okay, but most of the rest of them are really dangerous," and the idea that civilized beastmen tend to be viewed as an exception rather than the rule.

        Places like Nexus or Halta, where beastmen are fully accepted and judged by what they do rather than by what they look like, tend to be comparatively rare in Creation. Even in An-Teng, which has a large colony of snakemen, they tend to be viewed as bizarre and strange and usually keep to themselves.

                                                            Originally posted by The MG View Post
                                                            Being rich won't protect you from the racism of the rich, and being poor won't protect you from the racism of the poor.

                                                            What's different about Nexus is that the locals are going to have a slightly higher tolerance for weirdness than, say, the people of the Imperial City; they won't freak out and think you're a subhuman monster come to eat their children, but they'll still assume that you are a barbarian, or sneaky, or violent, or (stereotype).
                                                            Nexus is the second most cosmopolitan city in Creation (right after Great Forks and even then certain beings like demon-blooded can find more acceptance in Nexus than in Great Forks). Ultimately the discrimination a beastman faces there is not going to be nearly as bad as what he or she would face elsewhere. The biggest factor about Nexus is that, if you have money, regardless of what you look like or where you come from, people will be willing to deal with you and you'll find some level of acceptance.

                                                            A wealthy beastman probably isn't going to find any major metropolitan areas in Creation more willing to accept him and treat him as an equal than Nexus. And a destitute beastman in Nexus isn't really going to be all that much worse off than all of the other poor people starving to death. So while a beastman in Nexus may face discrimination, it's important to keep in mind that the core of that discrimination is based around how much money he has. If he had more money, he would be treated better, and he if had less money, he would be treated worse. In many other parts of Creation, a wealthy beastman would be seen as no different than a poor one - just another monster that poses a danger to humans.

                                                            And it's important to remember that this core idea, that Nexus experiences a type economic discrimination and that it applies equally for everyone in Nexus. Any person with money in Nexus will be treated fairly well, regardless of their race, creed or nationality, and anyone without money is going to be treated like trash. Whether you're Imperial or Lookshayan, whether you're mortal or demonblooded, beastman or Anathema, how you're treated is nearly always going to come down to how much money you have.

                                                            That's not to say that beastmen won't face discrimination in Nexus, just that the discrimination they do face is primarily going to be a bit different from what they would face in other parts of Creation, and will ultimately be determined by their economic standing rather than simply because of the fact that they don't look human. And when they do experience discrimination because of their features, it's going to be rather less than what they would have experienced almost anywhere else in Creation.

                                                            So a beastman in a Nexus bar might find that the service for him is slower than other people... though if he obviously had money to spend, he'd find that his order was brought to him first, before anyone else in the bar. Money is the most important factor and the great equalizer in Nexus and tends to trump anything else. So a wealthy beastman would be accepted and a poor one would be rejected - but that is equally true for regular humans too.
