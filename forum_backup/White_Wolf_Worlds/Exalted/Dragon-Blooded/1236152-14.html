            Originally posted by Angwe View Post
            Well, not defending Fenrir's particular style of getting their message across, but must admit there is a basis to the basic complaint.

            Essence 3 really is a long way off, even if, like us, you condense the progression chart. I too thought that the printed Martial Arts and evocations usually offer a tighter and more focused package of charms that seem much easier to build around than their regular charm trees, especially in the area of combat. I can also see Aura as not being too loved by many players in my regular group for whom Exalted is already at the very edge of their crunch-comfort zone and really don't need yet another thing to track.
            Yeah, depending on the style of campaigns that people are running I can definitely see this as being an issue. There's some really cool stuff in the signature charms that you really should be looking forward to, not treating like level 20 powers in DnD. You know, like something you might get to use for 4 sessions ever or something. I'm not sure if that's necessarily a flaw with the charmset, but the charmset does not very easily support 20 game campaigns. Not without some kind of bonus granted xp or something.

            Especially coming off Solars, who's every power is either interesting or very powerful, or both. It can be very difficult to refocus on the strengths that the DBs have after playing badass Solars and seeing how starkly the DBs lack in those same ways. On the other hand if you wanted your DB to be able to duel a second circle demon like Octavian on her own, then sorry but what you're looking to play is a Solar.

            Some of the charms could definitely use a tweak though. There's a difference between being kind of situational and being next to useless. Extending the range of a class of weapons to (long) when they're almost exclusively long anyway, or adding 5 dice for 5 motes with no other effect when the excellency already does that but better. They're not the whole charmset but they could use a little overhaul.

            Originally posted by Angwe View Post
            Now, as to comparing with Solars, in my opinion it is not something to shy away from. After all, my tables, mine included, really enjoy mixed splat games, and while admittedly it is just an impression based on just reading and not actual in-game experience with the DB, just the Solars, but to me the gap looks a great deal wider than it was before, and further exacerbated by Solars having Supernal abilities. In Ex2.5, if your DB wasn't combat focused, the xp charts were fixed and there was some minimal niche protection agreed upon, it was actually rather fine, while, if you play the DB in a Solar party now, I'm not so sure you feel you are not a total chump and henchman, even with your cultural advantages.
            I feel like the gap is wider in some areas and thinner in others, it will vary overall depending on how min-maxy your group is. First of all, you'll be incomparable to a Solar at chargen in their Supernal, if they wish it. They'll have essence 5 Solar charms and you'll have essence 2 DB charms. On the other hand you'll have access to higher essence evocations, martial arts and natural charms than any non-MA Solar, admittedly with the terrestrial keyword. They'll catch up by like 70xp-80xp but that's a fair bit of time. Your dicecap is still lower but your excellencies are still more efficient, and as long as you're not going up against a Solar in their Supernal I'd say you stand a solid chance. If you're lucky or clever anyway.

            Originally posted by Elfive View Post
            Ok, but how does a dragonblooded who can go all-out compare to a solar who's trying not to flare?
            Pretty favourably. That's kind of where DBs leverage their power too. They get an additional specialty, WAY more merit dots, more bonus points, and cooler anima powers*. As far as I'm concerned non-excellency charms are almost just the bonus on top of your real strength.

            What a "white room" fight between a DB and a Solar looks like is a bloody exhausted Dawn stumbling through the doors of your fortress's main hall after you had your cousin poison his tea, and then he killed his way past all your guards and summoned demons and hired exigent mercenary. At which point you pounce on him with your most elite bodyguards and local mortal immaculate monks to rightly end him.

            I say this is a white room because his merit list says:
            **** Daiklave of killing
            *** Armor of not-getting-killed
            *** Danger Sense
            *** Fast Reflexes

            And yours says:
            *** Daiklave of burning
            *** Fullplate of the unburnt
            **** Backing (House Cathak)
            ***** Command (16th Legion and Attached Support Forces)
            *** Contacts (Nation of Zabarek)
            ***** Influence (Satrap of Zabarek)
            ***** Resources
            *** Ally (Cousin Ylale)
            *** Mighty Thews


            Now imagine you've got a whole sworn kinship of DBs like that. Spies absolutely everywhere, thousands of elite soliders, or tens of thousands of lesser ones, fleets of warships, money and influence that place you in a truly dizzying position of power and authority. Your group might as well be the Emperor, Shogun, High Priest, Champion and Spymaster of your own little Japan. Seriously, if you've all got 20+ story merits like that your idea of a fun sailing trip looks like this.

            Your solar can spend a one mote to reroll all 1s on a melee attack? That's so cute, I can speak one word, unenhanced by essence, and a city burns to the ground. As a DB it's not even hard to get to that point off chargen, and when you do you don't need to worry that the Realm will unite with Lookshy and come fight you out of fear either.

            Then after all that, toss on the ability to hurl a boulder or feel the weight of an assassin on the flagstones behind her.

            Man I have spent way too long on this the night before an exam.

            *Yes they are

                                                            Jumping off of that reference to My Hero Academia, no Dragon-Blood is a mook just as no hero student or professional hero is a mook.

                                                            A mook is one of the faceless minions that Gang Orca brought to the provisional license exam. Characters so unimportant that they all wear indentical masks. They have no individuality, being equipped with the same weapons, and having no apparent unique abilities.

                                                            Compare that to the girl from the rival school whose only known ability is to pull her body parts into herself like a nightmarish turtle without a shell. She has a face, a name, a distinct appearance, a known place of origin, and a unique special ability. It doesn’t seem like much of one, but MHA is frequently showing us that underestimating people is foolish.

                                                            An even better example might be Mirio, a boy who worked hard to make a really difficult, double-edged sword of a Quirk into a game-breaking power. The likes of Bakugo and Todoroki would have easily outclassed him in his first year, but his additional year of experience puts him ahead.
