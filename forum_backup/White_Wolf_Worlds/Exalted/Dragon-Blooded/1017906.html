    2.5e Homebrew Brainstorm - Evolved Dragon-Blooded

    12-15-2016, 10:09 PM
    Hey, guys! I've got to hammer out a hefty bit of homebrew over the next several months. Right now, I'm pretty much at the "spitballing off of the original idea" stage, since I'm just a handful of days out from getting the idea my player wanted to pursue in this campaign. The details of how we get there can be sorted out along the way, and we might even consider the idea of a tragic failure or only partial success, but I feel the need to have suitable mechanics for a success anyway. And hey, maybe it can be fun for me and you folks of the forum.

    I'm here to brainstorm ideas and basic mechanical shapes right now before I touch the actual writing of mechanics, and then when I start solidifying things, I'll come to you guys for the usual critique and reshaping stage as well.

    So this character is a member of the Sanctity of Gaia group, a small and dying remnant branch of Outcaste blood supremacists who wandered away from the fledgling Realm when most of the relatively pure Dragon-Blooded started mingling with mortals in a desperate bit to refresh the Dragon-Blooded numbers, sharply diluting what blood purity the Shogunate had left behind.

    Trying to maintain a group that small is a losing battle by nature, and even the members of the group, self-centered and hysterical by nature, are past the point of deluding themselves into thinking they can make a resurgence. Still, unwilling to break from the elemental blood potency they've vested their entire meaning into, they remain secluded, trying to keep the thoughts from their mind as their numbers enter their final cycles of dwindling.

    All but one. One expert sorcerer has decided to go out into the world and find a way to not only revive the bloodline, but to push the Dragon-Blooded as a whole up past their limits and into a new state of being.

    Her initial benchmark is Celestial Circle Sorcery, but her grand design is something far greater. An evolution.

    ... All that said.

    The way that the player and I are both imagining this going (to varying degrees of consistency with each others' ideas) is that she'll spend the Essence 2-6 segment of the campaign building up resources, special conditions, materials, crafting allies (one of whom will probably be one of the other PCs, because Craft-focused Infernals are good at Artifacts), etc. (and in fact it may be built off of another N/A artifact originating in my campaign's background) towards the ultimate goal of building an N/A artifact which will combine to her Essence and turn the Terrestrial Exaltation into a more powerful and refined form.

    I'm imagining this having a few effects on her descendants going forward. Probably increase Breeding level to one higher than it would normally be on a generational basis, add a couple of forgettable low-Essence Charms along the way. More importantly, it might open up Celestial Circle Sorcery to them, which may be even more massively revolutionary to wherever it hits than the Breeding part.

    But I'm not worried about the generic stuff, unless you guys have some cool ideas or ciritical thoughts about what should be done with that. The part I'm most interested in covering with this homebrew is doing something akin to what the Green Sun Princes do in becoming something resembling Primordials through the Heretical Charm tree. In this case, in keeping with the theme of the Exalted becoming things newer and greater than their creators, I'd imagine these Evolved Dragon-Blooded having the opportunity to aspire towards something resembling the original five Elementals (much how the strongest modern elementals hopped across the origin border to "lesser Elemental Dragon" rather than resembling their own progenitors), but also unique unto themselves.

    I'm hesitant about that idea precisely because of the resemblance, however. Cool as it is, I don't want whatever evolution path I offer to the Dragon-Blooded to tug at the Green Sun Princes' niche. It might be different enough simply on account of the vastly disparate circumstances (the Dragon-Blooded don't have to escape from the metaphysical chains of insane dictatorial Titans, their abilities will be very different, and their redefinition will probably be far less personal) but if the simple act of ascending to a form resembling displaced ancient entities is encroaching too much then I don't want to steal that.

    So with the gist of it all laid out for you, I want to get you guys involved in the brainstorming. What sort of Charm tree (or other non-Charm tree thing, for that matter) might do the idea of Dragon-Blooded evolution justice, and what kinds of Charms or mechanical goals would you want or expect from such a thing?

    Feel free to go weird and metaphysical on me if you have any neat thematic ideas. I love that sort of stuff.

      Well, there's a few ways you could go with this:

      Tree of Life - An 'evolved' DB is simply an older one. Once their age/essence reaches sufficient values they can break through to a Guardian Form, they take on draconic characteristics in emulation of the elemental dragons and attain higher circles of sorcery and/or martial arts. However the advanced charms continue to twist their bodies and advance their transformation while stripping them of things like the ability to breed, mortal perspective, etc.

      Elemental Advancement - Somewhat similar to the above but less angsty, essentially the DBs gain a special set of charms based on their primary elemental aspect and this represents them growing closer to that element and gaining power from that, such as a Wood Aspect becoming a Grove of Magical Trees as well as a person, or a Water Aspect becoming a person who is also a river.

      Overload - Basically they can push themselves farther than a normal DB exaltation would allow, but this damages their bodies and spirits, they achieve higher power at the cost of things like leaking essence so that their banners are always in the first active stage instead of being under wraps, their reincarnation upon death maybe delayed or cancelled, or they may be incapable of manifesting a ghost once they die. Eventually they become beings of almost pure essence held together by willpower.

      Ghost Stitch - Instead of relying on fresh DBs they could go the other way and use the ghosts of past DBs to either power up living DBs or fuse them to members of the bloodline that have yet to exalt in order to try to kickstart and empower the process.

      Devouring - You could try eating/subsuming Elementals into the DB seeking ascension this would likely make them more akin to primordials with the subsumed elementals acting as lower circle souls of the DB.

      Patchwork - Instead of trying to evolve the exaltation lineage as a whole you could develop methods for achieving specific things such as second circle sorcery and then teach others the methods and try to streamline them so it's easier for Future DBs to achieve the same things.

              Well, there has been at least one 'official' thing regarding Dragon-Blooded transcendence. May be worth looking in to.

              Transcendent Gaian Harmony
              Beyond mere modulations of aspect, Transcendent Gaian Harmony exploits the inherent interconnectedness of the Elemental Dragons as part of a greater whole. Upon purchasing this Charm, the Exalt permanently gains the anima power of a chosen aspect and no longer has to pay the surcharge to use Charms of that aspect. Whenever he uses a Charm with different effects per aspect, he may choose which known element to express. Charm clusters purchased separately by element are not affected. This Charm does not actually change the Exalt's aspect, it merely permits incorporation of subsidiary elements.
              Characters can purchase this Charm once per dot of Essence and Medicine they have above 5, allowing a theoretical Essence 9 Dragon-Blood to integrate all five elements. Savants postulate that a fifth purchase might facilitate evolution into an entirely new type of being.

                      This is what I could scavenge. Some of it is probably very overpowered. I allowed it for a player in one of my old 2.5 games because of the power level that had been reached already in that game (some players reached Essence 6). It's just a bunch of new Charms and changes to original Charms to make them more powerful.


                      GREATER DRAGON-BLOODED

                      The Dragon-Blood absorbs the left-over Essence of Infernals' transcension to Devil-Tigers, causing him to undergo a transformation of his own and become a Greater Dragon-Blooded. Unlike normal Dragon-Bloods, he will follow the guidelines below.


                      ATTRIBUTES

                      Greater Dragon-Blooded assign 8 dots to primary Attributes, 6 dots to secondary Attributes and 4 dots to Tertiary Attributes.


                      CHARMS

                      Greater Dragon-Blooded have no elemental surcharge for non-Aspect Charms. They can learn Celestial Martial Arts Styles without initiation and they can learn Celestial Circle Sorcery.

                      The Exalt's dice caps remain the same at (Ability + Specialty). However, the Greater Dragon-Blooded have access to several new Charms and many of his normal Terrestrial Charms are upgraded to Celestial level.


                      ESSENCE

                      A Greater Dragon-Blooded's Personal Essence pool is ([Essence x 2] + Willpower + any gained from Breeding), his Peripheral Essence pool is ([Essence x 5] + Willpower + [the sum of the two highest Virtues] + any gained from Breeding).

                      The Exalt may spend one mote and cause his caste mark or his anima to glow brightly for a scene. The caste mark is not attached to the forehead, but is instead a manifestation of Essence appearing in the air before the forehead. The caste mark is a swirling vortex of air (Air Aspect), a smooth jade stone (Earth Aspect), a rising flame (Fire Aspect), a bubble of water (Water Aspect) or a leaf (Wood Aspect).

                      The Exalt may also spend one mote to sense nearby demesnes of his own aspect up to (Demesne Rating) miles without the need for a roll. If he makes a standard (Perception + Occult) roll, he can add one mile on top of that distance for each success.


                      ANIMA BANNER

                      1m+: Aspect traits are enhanced, the Exalt's caste mark and a faint glow is visible from certain angles. Anyone seeing the Exalt may make a (Perception + Awareness) roll at standard difficulty to notice the glow. May persist for as long as an hour after the Exalt has ceased to burn Essence.

                      4m+: Aspect traits are strong and the Exalt's caste mark and glow shines through anything placed over it. It is impossible to mistake the character for anything but what he is. Stealth Charms fail. May use the Stealth Ability to hide in natural cover at a +2 difficulty.

                      8m+: Surrounded by a coruscant aura bright enough to read by. Stealth is impossible. The Exalt's anima inflicts one dice of lethal damage for every 30 seconds of contact to everything within a number of feet equal to the Exalt's permanent Essence. This damage is sufficient to destroy cloth and soft wood in up to a minute. It can eventually reduce even treated hardwoods to junk (within two to three minutes). Characters who are able to soak lethal damage with Stamina ignores the damage completely.

                      11m+: Engulfed in a brilliant bonfire of Essence that burns from his feet to at least a foot above his head. The Exalt is visible for miles and the light is bright and steady enough to read by out to a spearcast's distance. The anima banner inflicts one die of damage every action in combat, and the range is increased to (Essence) yards. Characters who are able to soak lethal damage with Stamina still suffer a minimum damage of one dice.

                      16m+: The Exalt's anima banner explodes into a totemic display of Essence that stretches (Essence) yards above his head. This totem illustrates the Exalt's aspect and generates a lethal die of damage every tick to all beings and objects within (Essence x 3) yards.


                      FLAW OF INVULNERABILITY

                      Charms carrying this Flaw of Invulnerability may only be used to defend against attacks that may harm one of the Exalt's Intimacies. For example, an Exalt with an Intimacy towards his daughter may use this Charm to defend against attacks against him or her if the daughter is present in the scene and if his defeat would make his daughter vulnerable.

                      When applied to Intimacies that aren't tangible, like the Intimacy to a specific ideology, then this Charm can only be used in defense of an attack by someone whose actions are in direct contradiction to that ideology.

                      The Exalt must declare which Intimacy is being protected when applying this defense, but he is required to be more specific than simply claiming that his own safety is necessary for the protection of various Intimacies. The Storyteller should not allow the player to be vague about how his action is directly linked to the protection of the Intimacy, and he should not allow it to be used by default to protect too broad or general Intimacies. Having an Intimacy towards "humanity" or "Exalted" in general will exclude the Exalt himself from those Intimacies. He cannot simply state that "I am Exalted and therefore I am protected by my own Intimacy."


                      GREATER DRAGON-BLOODED GENERAL CHARMS

                      Note about Elemental Magnitude: A nearby bonfire is Elemental Magnitude 1 while a forest fire/blizzard/thunderstorm is Elemental Magnitude 3. The anima banner is (half Essence), rounded up, in Elemental Magnitude.


                      COSMIC (ABILITY) REINFORCEMENT [TERRESTRIAL (ABILITY) REINFORCEMENT]

                      This Charm functions similar to the original version, but the Greater Dragon-Blood treats all allies targeted by this Charm as if they were fellow officers; they can raise their Abilities above twice their original rating but no higher than the Exalt's own rating. He is no longer forced to pay one mote to invoke the Charm and is only required to pay for the added dice.


                      INFALLIBLE (ABILITY) MASTERY [SURGING (ABILITY) MASTERY]

                      Cost: Varies (1wp); Mins: (Ability) 5, Essence 4; Type: Permanent
                      Keywords: None
                      Duration: Permanent
                      Prerequisite Charms: First (Ability) Excellency or Third (Ability) Excellency

                      Upon activating either the First or Third Excellency for the desired Ability, the Exalt is able to prolong the benefit of that Excellency until his DVs next refresh by adding 1wp to the activation of the Excellency. For example, if the Greater Dragon-Blood spends three motes on the First Excellency, he may add 1wp to that cost and be able to use the added dice for all rolls using that Ability until his DVs refresh.


                      GREATER DRAGON-BLOODED DODGE CHARMS

                      THRESHOLD WARDING STANCE

                      Cost: 1m or 3m; Type: Reflexive (Step 2)
                      Keyword: Combo-OK

                      This Charm may ignore all environmental penalties to one's Dodge DV for one tick for 1m, or all penalties to Dodge DV for 3m.


                      HOPPING FIRECRACKER EVASION

                      Cost: 1m; Type: Reflexive (Step 7)

                      The Dash action used with this Charm is at twice its base movement range.


                      GREATER DRAGON-BLOODED LORE CHARMS

                      ELEMENTAL CONCENTRATION TRANCE

                      Cost: 3m, 1wp


                      ETERNAL MIND MEDITATION

                      Cost:                       1m (+1wp)

                      Just like with the original Charm, the Exalt may recall a hurried conversation from a few years back with one success. Recalling events from early childhood only requires three successes.

                      The Exalt can choose to pay 1 Temporary Willpower in order to have his memory precede his own lifetime. By doing this, one success would allow him to gain a memory from one of his parents (but only from their life before his birth), three successes would allow him to access a memory from up to (Essence + Breeding) generations back (just like with his parents, he can only access their memories from before they passed on their genes and not after), while five successes would allow him to access memories as far back as during the First Age. He must deliberately be aware of and target a particular ancestor in order to access its memories.

                      This Charm comes with an unintentional side effect. As soon as the Greater Dragon-Blood has used this Charm to access the memories of a particular ancestor, he may unconsciously dream about that ancestor whenever he is asleep. This happens only at Storyteller discretion and is nothing the Exalt's player can control.


                      (ASPECT) EMPOWERMENT MEDITATION

                      Cost:                       --; Mins: Lore 4, Essence 3; Type: Permanent
                      Keywords: Elemental, Native
                      Duration: Permanent
                      Prerequisite Charms: Elemental Concentration Stance

                      The Greater Dragon-Blooded may draw upon the might of his progenitors, the Elemental Dragons, evoking their power from the land itself. This Charm works differently depending on the strength of the element surrounding the Exalt.

                      The Exalt may activate his anima power for a reduced cost of -1m per available Elemental Magnitude associated with his aspect. This can reduce the cost to 0m.

                      In addition, the Exalt multiplies the rate at which he naturally recovers motes by a factor equal to the surrounding Elemental Magnitude while resting or meditating in the presence of his element.


                      (ASPECT) SUCCOR METHOD

                      Cost:                       --; Mins: Lore 5, Essence 3; Type: Permanent
                      Keywords: Elemental, Native
                      Duration: Permanent
                      Prerequisite Charms: (Aspect) Empowerment Meditation

                      While (Aspect) Empowerment Meditation allows the Dragon-Blood to replenish his lost Essence, this Charm allows him to restore his own body using the power of the elements to heal his injuries.

                      The Exalt multiplies the time it takes to naturally heal non-magical wounds by a factor equal to his available Elemental Magnitude (minimum 1).


                      (AIR) BOLT

                      Cost:                       1m per 2L; Mins: Lore 2, Essence 2; Type: Simple
                      Keywords: Combo-Basic, Cooperative, Elemental (Air), Obvious
                      Duration: Instant
                      Prerequisite Charms: None

                      The Greater Azure Dragon compresses the winds themselves into an ethereal bolt as small as a human fist. The speed at which the wind travels throughout this bolt is immense, and its lowered temperature is comparable to the wind of the far North.

                      The bolt is targeted with (Dexterity or Perception + Archery or Thrown), whichever is preferred, with an Accuracy bonus equal to the Exalt's Essence and a range of ([Essence + Elemental Magnitude] x 10) yards. The Accuracy of the bolt may also be enhanced with the excellencies appropriate for the roll.

                      The bolt normally inflicts 2L/2 damage, but may be enhanced by +2L for every additional mote spent on the Charm, up to a maximum cost equal to the Exalt's (Essence + Elemental Magnitude).

                      The Air Bolt is near-imperceptible to human eyes, and adds an external penalty of -2 to the target's DVs. Should the attack be successful and inflict at least one level of damage on a target, it will buffet the target, leaving her with a -1 external penalty to her DVs up until her next action. This is a Crippling effect.

                      The cooperative effect works similar to the original Terrestrial Charm, but the Greater Dragon-Blood is always the focus and his Essence is determining the number of motes available for spending.


                      (AIR) BURST

                      Cost:                       (+1wp); Mins: Lore 3, Essence 2; Type: Permanent
                      Keywords: Elemental (Air)
                      Duration: Permanent
                      Prerequisite Charms: (Air) Bolt

                      She who has mastered the Air Bolt may learn to enhance its effects. The Air Burst looks identical to its prerequisite in appearance, but it spreads into a vortex upon impact, affecting everyone in the targeted area.

                      When enhancing Air Bolt with the benefit of Air Burst, everyone within an area of (Essence + Elemental Magnitude) yards around the target will suffer the effects from the attack. This is an environmental hazard.


                      (FIRE) BOLT

                      Cost:                       1m per 2L; Mins: Lore 2, Essence 2; Type: Simple
                      Keywords: Combo-Basic, Cooperative, Elemental (Fire), Obvious
                      Duration: Instant
                      Prerequisite Charms: None

                      The Greater Crimson Dragon channels Fire-aspected Essence into his fist and compresses it into a controlled ball of flame.

                      The bolt is targeted with (Dexterity or Perception + Archery or Thrown), whichever is preferred, with an Accuracy bonus equal to the Exalt's Essence and a range of ([Essence + Elemental Magnitude] x 10) yards. The Accuracy of the bolt may also be enhanced with the excellencies appropriate for the roll.

                      The bolt normally inflicts 2L/2 damage, but may be enhanced by +2L for every additional mote spent on the Charm, up to a maximum cost equal to the Exalt's (Essence + Elemental Magnitude).

                      The attack will ignite flammable materials, even if it doesn't inflict any damage. Should the attack actually manage to land at least one level of damage on a target, it will set the target ablaze for a single action, adding (Elemental Magnitude) additional dice of lethal damage (minimum one dice). This damage can only be resisted with natural soak or hardness.

                      Several invocations of this effect in the same action do not stack the additional fire damage, but instead use the outcome of the highest damage roll from all successful attacks.

                      The cooperative effect works similar to the original Terrestrial Charm, but the Greater Dragon-Blood is always the focus and his Essence is determining the number of motes available for spending.


                      GREATER DRAGON-BLOODED MARTIAL ARTS CHARMS

                      TIGER-AND-BEAR AWARENESS

                      Cost: 5m

                      The Exalt is aware of all beings within a number of yards equal to her (Essence x 3), unless an entity is expending Essence in an effort to conceal itself. Detecting supernatural concealment requires a roll, at which the Exalt gains half her Essence in automatic successes. Though this Charm cannot see dematerialized spirits in range, it can sense their presence.


                      TIGER-AND-BEAR UNITY

                      Cost: --; Type: Permanent
                      Keywords: Enlightening
                      Duration: Permanent
                      Prerequisite Charms: Second Martial Arts Excellency, Tiger-and-Bear Awareness

                      This Charm reduces the cost for the Second Martial Arts Excellency when used to supplement a Martial Arts attack against an opponent. The Exalt pays 1m per success instead of 2m per success.


                      WALKER-AMONG-IRISES PERCEPTION

                      Cost: 4m; Type: Reflexive
                      Keywords: Combo-OK

                      This Charm no longer inflicts an internal penalty upon the Exalt.


                      IRIS-BULB DISCOURSE

                      Cost: 5m.


                      GREATER DRAGON-BLOODED MEDICINE CHARMS

                      INFECTION-BANISHING PRANA

                      The Essence 4+ effect of this Charm is part of the original Charm. In addition, when the Exalt is touching the patient to cure the infection, she will automatically and perfectly diagnose the patient's infection.


                      DREAD INFECTION STRIKE

                      Cost: 1m; Type: Supplemental

                      The Greater Dragon-Blood enchants her weapon with wood-aspected essence. Should the attack deal at least one level of lethal damage, the wound will be infected as a perfect effect. Though the infection itself is not magical in nature, it still affects all targets, though Exalted targets usually see it as a crippling inconvenience rather than a life-threatening ailment, as is the case for mortals.


                      DISEASE-BANISHING TECHNIQUE

                      Cost: 5m

                      When touching a patient using this Charm, the Greater Dragon-Blood will automatically and perfectly diagnose the patient's disease. The disease is expelled instantly. If used in combat, the patient will no longer be crippled for an action.


                      WOUND-CLOSING TOUCH

                      Cost: 1m per 1hl converted, 1wp

                      When touching a patient using this Charm, the Greater Dragon-Blood will automatically and perfectly diagnose the state of the patient's wounds. For every mote spent, she can convert one lethal wound level into a bashing wound level, or remove a bashing wound level completely.


                      GREATER DRAGON-BLOODED MELEE CHARMS

                      CONFLAGRANT SWORD OF PASSION [DRAGON-GRACED WEAPON (FIRE)]

                      Cost: 1m; Mins: Melee 2, Essence 2; Type: Supplemental
                      Keywords: Combo-OK, Elemental (Fire), Leader, Merged (Martial Arts), Obvious
                      Duration: Instant
                      Prerequisite Charms: None

                      The Greater Dragon-Blood has achieved a unity with his weapon that allows him to let it harmonize with the passionate flames of Hesiesh. The flames empowering the Exalt's weapon will ignite flammable materials, even if it doesn't inflict any damage.

                      Should the attack actually manage to land at least one level of damage on a target, it will set the target ablaze for a single action, adding (Essence) additional dice of lethal damage (minimum one dice). This damage can only be resisted with natural soak or hardness.

                      Several invocations of this effect in the same action do not stack the additional fire damage, but instead use the outcome of the highest damage roll from all successful attacks. For example, if a flurry of three attacks all succeed against the target and the Exalt has Essence 3, he will roll three additional dice with each successful attack, assuming the target has a natural lethal soak of 0. The minimum damage is 1 for these damage rolls. Should the outcome of the three rolls be 0, 1 and 2, then the target will not suffer three levels of lethal. She will only suffer the two levels from the highest damage roll.

                      Should the Exalt's weapon be dealing bashing damage, this Charm makes that damage lethal instead. Should the weapon already be dealing lethal damage, it will instead add +2L dice to its raw damage. If a target is trying to parry an attack made with Conflagrant Sword of Passion with natural weapons without any protective magic, the target will suffer damage as with the Fire Aspect anima power. This damage does not stack with that of the anima power.

                      Merged (Martial Arts): This Charm can be applied on natural weapons as well, such as the Exalt's fists. It may be purchased as a Martial Arts Charm instead of as a Melee Charm, but will still be Fire-aspected. When used as a Martial Arts Charm, a more fitting name is Conflagrant Fists of Passion.


                      FLAME SWORD

                      Cost:                       (+2m); Mins: Melee 2, Essence 2; Type: Permanent
                      Keywords: Elemental (Fire), Merged (Martial Arts)
                      Duration: Permanent
                      Prerequisite Charms: Conflagrant Sword of Passion

                      Whenever the Exalt uses Conflagrant Sword of Passion on his unarmed fists, he can activate this Charm for two motes to have the flames shape themselves like a sword.

                      The Exalt uses his Punch attack with the bonuses of the prerequisite Charm, but he may use it with either Martial Arts or Melee. Attacks using Flame Sword add (Essence ÷ 2), rounded up, to Accuracy, Damage and Defense. The bonus cannot exceed the Exalt's dice cap.

                      Though the Flame Sword can be used with Martial Arts, it counts as any kind of mundane one-handed sword if used as a form-type weapon. It cannot be used with Martial Arts styles that are not compatible with swords.


                      FLAME SHIELD

                      Cost:                       (+2m); Mins: Melee 2, Essence 2; Type: Permanent
                      Keywords: Elemental (Fire), Merged (Martial Arts)
                      Duration: Permanent
                      Prerequisite Charms: Flame Sword

                      Whenever the Exalt uses Conflagrant Sword of Passion or Flame Sword, he may supplement his burning weapon by manifesting a shield of flames, assuming he has one hand free to equip it with.

                      This shield has Speed 5, Accuracy +0, Damage +1L, Defense +3, Rate 1, Tags: M, Sh0, though it will also benefit from the effects of Conflagrant Sword of Passion for no additional cost, burning people who come in contact with it.


                      SCORCHING FLAMES OF SAPPHIRE

                      Cost:                       (+4m, 1wp); Mins: Melee 4, Essence 3; Type: Permanent
                      Keywords: Elemental (Fire), Merged (Martial Arts)
                      Duration: Permanent
                      Prerequisite Charms: Conflagrant Sword of Passion

                      Whenever the Exalt has Conflagrant Sword of Passion active, he may invoke the effects of this Charm in order to change its duration to One scene. In addition, the burning flames shrouding the weapon grows hotter and the fire close to its source turns blue. The weapon gains +1 to minimum damage, as well as the Piercing tag. The effects from this Charm will also extend to other upgrades to the prerequisite, such as Flame Sword and Flame Shield.


                      ORDAINED TALON OF HESIESH'S FURY

                      Cost:                       (+3m); Mins: Melee 5, Essence 4; Type: Permanent
                      Keywords: Elemental (Fire), Merged (Martial Arts), Shaping
                      Duration: Permanent
                      Prerequisite Charms: Scorching Flames of Sapphire

                      By invoking Ordained Talon of Hesiesh's Fury when activating Scorching Flames of Sapphire, the fire surrounding the Greater Crimson Dragon's weapon manifest a shape totemic to his being, such as a dragon's head, a phoenix or any form befitting the Exalt.

                      The weapon's Accuracy, Damage and Defense are increased by (Essence ÷ 2), rounded up. If used together with Flame Sword, the benefits are a straight +(Essence), but it is still limited to the Exalt's dice cap.

                      Should the weapon be used to kill someone, the deceased target's body instantly cremates as a Shaping effect.


                      PORTENTOUS COMET DEFLECTING MODE

                      Cost: 8m (+1wp); Type: Reflexive (Step 2)

                      This Charm is now activated in Step 2 of attack resolution and no roll is required to perfectly parry the attack. The Exalt may use the Charm to block magical attacks like normal, but blocking unblockable attacks comes with a 1wp surcharge. This Charm carries with it a Flaw of Invulnerability.


                      CONFLAGRATION OF WARDING

                      This Charm removes the 1wp surcharge when parrying unblockable attacks. The cooperative benefits of this Charm is increased because of the sheer power of the Greater Dragon-Blooded's Portentous Comet Deflecting Mode compared to normal Dragon-Blooded's.
