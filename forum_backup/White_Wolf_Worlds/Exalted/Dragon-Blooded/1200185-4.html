                Ok, chunk two!

                As I read DBWFHW, part 2

                Chapter fiction

                dynast from our sworn kinship is dissatisfied with marriage and life.

                I wondered if they were happy.

                Interesting lead in.

                This explains so much.

                I suppose it’s easier to view your child as a resource when you don’t know them very well. It also lessens the pain if they don’t exalt, I imagine.

                I am glad they have some source of empathy growing up.

                Repeated a sentence.

                Right, because most of them are going five or ten years between children. So ver little chance for siblings.

                What are leftover children?

                Note on orphans and same sex couples. Apparently there are species of animals that produce gay couples too and they adopt orphans as well.

                Will the Realm discuss patrician and peasant life?

                I think it’s worth noting that when someone expects to get something, they start to feel like they already own it. If they don’t receive what they expected, they feel like it’s been taken away. This seems like an important thing to remember going forward about Dynastic life.

                Ok so that’s why those schools, got it.

                Good info on differences in basic education.

                http://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.ph...WickedCultured

                I have a rambunctious nephew which gives me an interesting perspective on just how difficult it must be to get most of these children to behave like that.

                The home schooling sidebar was basically written for Sesus Rafara. Part of the reason she’s mad is because she was home schooled to be the perfect ninja and taught as much as possible to have an outsider’s perspective on the Realm, to the point that they basically turned her into an outcaste, ensured that she would marry poorly, and really just not get any of the benefits that any other DB Dynast gets. In the process, their teaching methods invalidated the standard indoctrination methods they use to promote loyalty to your house without them realizing it.

                I’m glad they left in the exaltation rituals and things that children practice. It’s really only to be expected.

                The paragraph that starts “There are hundreds of primary schools located around the Blessed Isle, plus a few of lesser quality in satrapies with sizable Dynastic populations or cadet houses” feels like it was meant to introduce this section so it’s a little weird to see it all the way down here.

                And now I want a character for whom school was a significant upgrade in terms of lifestyle.

                They haven’t changed the curriculum in hundreds of years? Interesting. Makes sense for their purposes, I suppose.

                Specialization seems logical.

                So they’re basically studying from the moment they wake up until their head hits the pillow at night. That’s intense. Of course the environment is intensely competitive.

                And a note about the Reform schools closes out the Primary schools section. Though, I think Arada turned out ok.

                Good info, good section.

                Next, Secondary schools.

                                Secondary School

                                So, generally speaking, if a child has a preference for a particular school, that’s really only taken into account as much as it’ll make them more likely to excel or their talents align in that direction. But plenty of other reasons might influence their choice of schools.

                                In this society it really is who you know.

                                Mell Expelled: October 14-19, 2002
                                http://narbonic.com/comic/october-14-19-2002/
                                Visit the post for more.


                                Important info about mortals. Good luck to anyone who tries.

                                I wonder if anyone has ever tried to fake an Exaltation?

                                Oooh! Alternatives to the big four. Nice! I’ve been wondering about those.

                                I wonder what someone would have to do to turn one of these “lesser schools” into a rival to the big four. Probably not possible now that the Empress is gone, at least in the short term. You’d need to find a way to get a large chunk of the current crop of DB students to go there, because the networking opportunities are half the reason they go to these schools. Pretty much the only way I think it could work is by convincing the Peleps Matriarch that you’ll provide a better education for all the children of her house for free than they’d get anywhere else, which is a feat even an Eclipse would find daunting, and then leverage that acceptance to convince several other House Matriarchs to “donate” enough money to keep the school going while still “demanding” less than half what the other schools do, while weathering the sabotage from all four of the other schools whose students you’re poaching.

                                Could be a fun game concept.

                                Anyway.

                                Cloister of Wisdom

                                Do not compare it to catholic school.

                                “You have come to me asking that I be your guide along the path of Ti Kwan Leep, but be warned. To learn its ways, you must learn the ways of your own soul. Let us meditate upon this wisdom now.”

                                https://youtu.be/Z8VD4JXUozM


                                ....I joke, but I could actually see that sketch being played out on the first day of classes here with some of the new students wanting to learn how to invert someone’s digestive tract or punch people’s souls off or whatever other nifty move they heard about.

                                See! The book agrees with me!

                                Ok, they have their own informants and ninjas too. It’s only to be expected I suppose.

                                Hmm! That was shorter than I expected, but fairly informative.

                                Heptagram

                                Good point about sorcery.

                                I think it was 60 in previous editions, but a few dozen works. Thinking on it, maybe 77 should be the class size, just to continue with the theme.

                                So, it’s likely that you’ll have one on one time with a tutor and “classes” might be more like you and a lab partner doing hands on research under a sorcerer’s supervision. That sounds pretty nice, honestly.

                                I can’t help but think of the Naming class from the Wise Man’s Fear.

                                I wonder if anyone attends the school for a grounding in occult adjacent studies, like Alchemy, Geomancy, and Artificing without trying to become a Sorcerer?

                                Nice sidebar on sorcery. I wonder how much work it would take to become a popular sorcerer? I’d imagine you’d have to cultivate an air of ...well, whatever the opposite of what people think sorcery is and do it so hard that, if anyone hears that you went to the Heptagram, they assume you flunked out after a year. No demon or elemental bodyguards or other weird stuff. Like at all. It honestly might be hard to have time to yourself without people wondering if you’re brewing curses or something, if they’ve heard.

                                No, what you do is pretend you’re not a sorcerer so hard and so convincingly that, if people find evidence to the contrary, they dismiss it or find an alternative explanation for it.

                                Creating a character who finds sorcery interesting and is innately good at it, but doesn’t want to pay the social cost to master it...and then gets sent to the Heptagram because the House said so, could be interesting.

                                Heck, anyone who wanted to go to a different school than they did could be interesting.

                                Thinking on it, a character who is just gleefully sorcerous could be fun too. The kind of person who rides a cloud everywhere, trades hair with her friends so they can share dreams, and basically just treats sorcery like this matter of fact thing. Would probably get shuffled off somewhere they won’t embarrass their house though. Or maybe a permanent position at the Heptagram.

                                Speaking of, I want a book that goes into the Secondary Schools in detail. Yes, everyone likes the Heptagram, but I have faith that you guys can make the others cool too.

                                Anyway

                                House of Bells

                                Why the House of Bells? I mean, I don’t really think of Bells and Warfare at the same time.

                                “Leave us alone Mel Brooks!”

                                And now I’m imagining one of the villages that’s been assaulted by students so often, everyone living there has just gotten used to it, either heading for storm shelters or just ...walking around the fighting, maybe taking bets. Depends on how humorous you wanna play it. Also how much you wanna play up collateral damage.

                                Glad to hear flunking out is more common than dying. Having your Fang graded together though.... That seems like it could promote “accidents” for those deemed truly hopeless by their peers.

                                Oh wow. Promoting trained officers over those whose parents bribed everyone to get them into that position? What IS this world coming to.

                                So, they’re getting them used to managing on 4 hours sleep while doing the most strenuous tasks they can find. That’s pretty grueling.

                                I wonder just how many criminals ever make it.

                                I like the squad names and things.


                                Spiral Academy

                                So, lets see if they made the boring academy cool.

                                Good points as to its existence.

                                They’re not only teach these students, they’re using the place like a giant economic computer.

                                Naturally they’re profiting off this six ways from Sunday.

                                Good notes on teaching there.

                                Ok, job prospects are useful.

                                Oh? It’s also the spy school. Nifty.

                                Yeah, the secret school you can only attend when you find it is neat.

                                Ok, I like it.

                                And all kinds of secret societies too.

                                A Spiral Academy game could be pretty fun with all kinds of social intrigue and manipulation.


                                Paisap’s Stair

                                This school is hella hardcore. I guess one of the big differences between this place and the House of Bells, is that you can flunk out of the latter, while you can only die out of the former. And the “accidents” I was wondering about before all happen here.

                                I bet House Nellens is cleaning up with these graduates.

                                Sidebar on dropping out. Good to know you can make good. And the whole taking a year off after college thing is a very useful adventuring conceit. It makes sense, sure, but it also serves as a good way to have a group of starter level DBs without any real expectations on them and can end up wherever the hell they need to be for the game to work.
