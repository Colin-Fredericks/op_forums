    Let's hype the Dragon-Blooded and Realm books

    04-04-2017, 05:34 AM
    My questions are as follows:
    • What do you want to see in these books that you haven't really seen before?
    • What do you want to make grand comebacks from previous editions?
    • What don't you want to see in these books?
    • What do you want to discuss or hype about these books?
    Personally I would like to see depicted Gentes symbols just like we've had for the Great Houses. I never read any Dragon-Blood stuff from First Edition but I remember there being a lack of Lookshy related content in Second Edition.

    I want Roseblack to make a comeback. I've always been a Tepet Ejava fanboi waving flags and wearing "Make the Realm great again" caps. Give some Roseblack chapter fiction too.

    I don't want to see elemental surcharges. I don't want a constant reminder of the Dragon-Blooded being either antagonists or weak.

    I'm hyped.

                      My greatest hype is the book itself. The Dragon-Blooded have always been my favorites. I don't know why, they just resonate with me to a far greater degree than any other type of Exalted.

                      I would love to get some ideas about the Dragon-Blooded beyond the Great Houses.
                      - What about the early Dragon-Blooded who came to the Blessed Isle - where they all subsumed into the Great Houses, or are there Lesser Houses running around?
                      - What about families of Dragon-Blooded not beholden to the Realm? There are the gentes in Lookshy, but any Dragon-Blood anywhere would understand the idea and power of building a family.

                      I am very excited about the idea of multi-elemental Charms.
                      I hope that elemental surcharges are gone, but if not, I will likely just house rule them away. I did it in 1st and 2nd edition, and can do so again.
                      Finally, as Ekorren wrote, the reminders of Dragon-Blooded weakness do get a little annoying.

                      I am certainly hyped too. It will be some time yet, but with the Deluxe Edition having literally just been given into my hands, today is a good day.
