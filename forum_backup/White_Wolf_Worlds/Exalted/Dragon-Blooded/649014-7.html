                    Even then, an Exaltation should still carry a certain level of weight, and I don't think it's good if the game's default assumption is that the gods of Yu-Shan will be jeering and sniggering at your character should your ST choose to take the game to Yu-Shan.

                    The Exaltation is the most holy of miracles, and there should be a sense that the gods, who owe their lives and existences to the Exalted, should have some level of respect for the Exalted. And setting it up so that the gods of Yu-Shan consider the Exalted to be goobers until proven otherwise is just not something I can get behind.

                    Even the most baseborn Exalt with no training or courtly manners is still one of the Exalted, one of the highest of beings and lords of existence.

In general, I think that the gods should hold a certain respect and reverence for the Exalted, such that they tend to treat them as equals rather than as lesser beings.

                        for example, the Terrestrial Gods are more likely to have antagonistic relationships with the Dragon-Blooded, while some Celestial Gods are more likely to have antagonistic relationships with the Sidereals. Some gods on the Threshold might be more amenable to the Lunars.

                        some gods are more likely to be snobbish than others and hold Exalts to high standards, particularly ones in power, as they expect great things from the person chosen by the greatest of the gods. while those not in power will be friendly to try and get in their good graces. some might be tired or disgruntled with them for being trouble-makers, some might just wish for them to go away so they can get back to something more peaceful and just not say it.....and thats just for the normal four.

                        But then you have Abyssals, who kind of murder this general attitude with a soulsteel axe to the face. A Solar is that guy returning from the First Age and holds the potential to make everything great again if he plays his cards right. An Abyssal is a guy coming forth to steal away all your worship and replace it with ghosts and the cults worshipping them while killing the entire land around them into darkness.

                        and of course, there is Liminals, who seem to be fighting against Abyssals, so probably respected if known at all.

                        Infernals would just be outright hated because their powers are reminiscent of the Yozis.

                        Exigents are of course made from one of the many smaller gods so....they are probably respected, but is subject to varying circumstances as with all things Exigent.

                        Alchemicals are just plain foreign, but I doubt the gods would like the way they treat gods, as Autochthon organized them differently in Autochthonia.

                        finally, Getimians are made to fight against fate and the Sidereals. this can also be a varying thing, as one hand, they are fighting against fate and the gods could hate them for that, on the other hand they're fighting against Sidereals and could love them for that.

                                                    I have only skimmed the Games of Divinity section but I think it does a pretty good job.

                                                    How I prefer to see it is that Celestial gods look down on the Terrestrial sphere and everything in it, so they do generally consider themselves better than everything from there, even Dragon-Blooded and the Five Elemental Dragons. Terrestrial gods are more likely to fear and resent Dragon-Blooded, and especially Immaculates. After the Great Contagion, many gods in both worlds view the Dragon-Blooded as too weak to defend Creation. But most also respect/fear their power and ability to kill gods Dead, and don't view them as non-people.

                                                    Meanwhile, most gods view Solars with some mix of fear and respect, possibly mixed with hope, awe, and/or terror. Solars have the potential to upset the whole status quo, to return things to the laws of Heaven, or who knows what! They used to rule the world and they might do so again. That's super scary if you like your corruption and really great if you want to restore the law. On the other hand some say their return will destroy the world! You want to be careful about pissing them off. That doesn't mean that most gods are inclined to defer to them purely on principle, not when they rarely defer to each other when they don't have to. If you're careful and good at manipulation, an ignorant Solar or Lunar can become a valuable tool as well as (or maybe instead of) a threat.
