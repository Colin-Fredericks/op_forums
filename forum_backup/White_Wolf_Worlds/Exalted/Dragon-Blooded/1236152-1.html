                    I was just looking through the grapple Charms in Brawl, and it seems like it would be very easy to make an Earth-Aura wrestler that can take advantage of a lot of those synergies in Brawl, and of course lots of Resistance Charms.

                    After looking at this for a while now, the clever placement of the Balanced keyword (especially on Excellencies) really does seem to make it quite easy to get some of these bonuses.

                    In my mind, the manuscript form of the book is a little difficult to flip through, and I think that this difficulty, plus the fact that you have to be a little strategic in your character designs, causes some people to think that the system is somehow flawed, but it looks glorious to me. Maybe look through the Charms before coming up with a final character concept, so you aren’t frustrated by not being able to get all the benefits you think you should, and instead you can let the dynamics of the Charm set guide some of your decisions. But remember, even if you throw Aura to the wind, the Charms all still work just fine.
