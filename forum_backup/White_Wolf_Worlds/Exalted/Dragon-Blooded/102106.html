    Explain the Dragonblooded Design Paradigm to me

    03-30-2014, 03:37 PM
    I've been reflecting lately on a somewhat unsatisfactory experience of playing a DB, and realized that I really don't get what DBs are really about.

    They're a type of Ability exalted, similar to Solars, but their Abilities are tied to their Aspects through what seems to be mostly thought-association rather than caste-role. Sometimes the association makes sense (Air=flying, flying=Throwing. OK!), and sometimes it doesn't (Arrow shafts are made of Wood. Wood=Archery?), and sometimes it seems to be based more on some kind of idea of Humorism (Fire= choleric, choleric = Presence?).

    The Aspect of each Dragonblood is not especially important except for the Anima power and class abilities, because every DB can use charms from the entire DB charmset, including those that are not of their element. For example, a Water-Aspect who wants to get good at using spears will take a lot of Fire charms, because Melee is in the Fire Aspect domain. The vast majority of all DB charms seem to be element-neutral once you get outside the fluff, and the ones that aren't have fairly small differences depending on the user's Aspect.

    No Dragonblood has the ability to actually control the element of their aspect except in fairly superficial ways, or by using very specific charms. Being able to attack people directly using your element requires a Lore charm for some reason. True elemental power is the domain of Sorcerers only. That leaves most DBs with the potential to be sort of generically badass with mostly undeveloped elemental themes.

    I look over everything I just wrote and I don't really see a rhyme or reason to it, beyond possibly a half-assed attempt to pidgeonhole the Dragonbloods into a similar design pattern to the one used for Solars. They certainly are not anything like Benders, despite all those demotivational posters out there. So... what exactly are the Elemental Chosen supposed to be, once you get all the cultural baggage about the Realm and breeding and not being Celestials out of the way?

                      Let me try to address this: (At least how i see this)
                      Air -
                      Linguistics - Air is about language and communication.
                      Lore - Knowledge is part of the element of air, and in fact, if you directly translate from hebrew, all the knowledges, like Philosophy and such are called "Air Sciences".
                      Occult - A continuation of the Lore idea.
                      Stealth - Air can move about unseen, and sometimes unfelt, if it moves slowly enough.
                      Thrown - While Air travels far, it is most damaging in immediate short blasts, like Thrown.

                      Earth -
                      Awareness - The Earth is a great way to sense someone approaching, whether you go up a hill, or put your ear to the ground. A bit shaky, but ok.
                      Craft - People generally like to see their creations stand the test of time, so it's like giving them the permanence of Earth. Also, in ancient times it was rocks, and metals mined from the earth, and clay, who were among the first crafts of our ancestors.
                      Integrity - Again Earth's permanence. It's like talking to a wall when trying to convince them.
                      Resistance - And yet again Earth's permanence, where it's harder to cause you damage.
                      War - Another a bit shaky, but smashing your armies against the city walls is probably a good reason.

                      Fire -
                      Athletics - Not sure, but I'd say it's the fact that Fire can move fast in a tinder field, and consumes all in it's path.
                      Dodge - It's hard to hit something that has no real substance, and that you know can damage you.
                      Melee - The sparks of the clashing of weapons. Shaky, I agree.
                      Presence - Fire, of all the elements, has long captivated the mind and imagination of humans.
                      Socialize - Again, as above. Add in the native americans reference to alcohol as "fire water", and you get the party boosting powers in Socialize.

                      Water -
                      Bureaucracy - It's about the flow of an organization, whether disrupting it, or aiding it.
                      Investigation - Water gets into every nook and crevice, and if you flood a place, you can find hidden things.
                      Larceny - Water in world mythology has a tendency to hide things in it's depths.
                      Martial Arts - Martial Arts are about adapting your Essence to another form, and water is all about adaptation.
                      Sail - Need i even explain? Water, Ships, they go together.

                      Wood -
                      Archery - Bows and arrows have long been made of bows, even until recent times.
                      Medicine - Plants were always a basic source of medicine
                      Performance - Many musical instruments are made of wood. A bit shaky. It might also be the fact that nature, portrayed mostly as forests, has always seemed harmonious to us.
                      Ride - it is with plants, food, that humans first began to domesticate some animals. it is another part of the connection to nature.
                      Survival - Forests, and heavy plants, were great cover, and may seem harmonious, but they can hide predators in them.


                      Everyone has their own opinions on how the different Abilities should correlate to different Elements, but at the end of the day, the idea was that most of their low-level powers are still sort-of human, and only going deeper into the trees, do they really start manifesting their elemental powers more forcefully.

                                      To begin with, I will recognise that your criticism has some merit. The way the Abilities get distributed to the Aspects are not always coherent or obvious. I personally think that's mostly a matter of working with what you got. The way the game is designed, you will have twenty-five Abilities to put into five themed categories (even in 3e, where as mentioned they plan on making "off-Aspect" Charms, they still have to come up with a baseline to work from, a way to group the basic Abilities into neat groups of five), and the result will always be someone's subjective idea of the best compromise.

                                      On a tangent, I personally don't even think that's the biggest problem with the Terrestrial Aspects and their Ability distribution. The fact that your Aspect determines 1) your elemental theme as a whole, 2) your favoured Abilities to the point of putting a surcharge on mote costs when going outside them and 3) your general emotional and psychological direction is at least one too much.

                                      However, you ask about the Terrestrial design paradigm. In this thread, it's so far only been about how the elemental part is handled. That's fine and all, we can talk about that, but if we want to talk about the Terrestrial design paradigm as a whole, it's imperative to remember that elemental themes are not supposed to be their whole idea. Family and warfare are also equally important, which shows up in their Charmsets in different ways. Sometimes perhaps in ways they shouldn't, but many of my favourite Terrestrial Charms borrow from those instead of the elemental theme. Again, not saying you can't complain about the Aspects, but if you feel you're failing to understand the Terrestrial design paradigm, it might be because you're not looking at all of it.

                                          Originally posted by semicasual View Post
                                          So... what exactly are the Elemental Chosen supposed to be, once you get all the cultural baggage about the Realm and breeding and not being Celestials out of the way?
                                          They're the great multitude of philosopher-warriors/scholar-bureaucrats who are fit to serve as the army and administration of the ancient Realm. The elemental stuff primarily adds more mystical flavour to them, as well as a suitable hook into different approaches to being a warrior/bureaucrat that is a bit more interesting than just plain saying it. That and how they all work better if we wrap their themes in mythological motifs; like, you could just have Solars as "warrior/leader/etc." and just have their Charms as out-of-character abstractions, it's just more fun to call Achilles a Dawn Caste and add Appolonian symbolism to his attacks.

                                          The elements really edge quite close to being entirely arbitrary, even if I personally ascribe to them a relevance of representing a complex, interdependent system.

                                          Which... does kind of lead into some of their inspiration points, like any Asian tactical manuals that use the mysticism behind Chinese elements as a reference point for various combat principles, so there's that as well.

                                          Really, in any comparisons to Avatar, the Dragon Blooded are best compared not to the actual feats of Benders, but to their underlying principles and mysticism. Maybe your Earth Aspect will never quite be able to do things that Bumi or Toph do, but they can still live according to ideas of subtlety and carefully chosen moments, and/or by being staid, firm and shiftless; indeed, that's what they're pretty much all doing.

                                          Aspect Abilities are a thing, but they're mostly an idea of what kinds of skillsets your emotional outlook and the physical and mystical manifestations of your element would give you an affinity for. That's what Aspect Abilities mean more than any kind of social role or anything. Like, there's an intellectual edge to all Air Aspects, so they can basically all be good at what Lore and Occult is, but that doesn't mean that all Air Aspects are scholars (although in the overall schema of some Dragon Blooded organisations, there might be a bit of a bias to make all Air Aspects learn the necessary skills to become scholars). There'll still be Air Aspected swordfighters, and they can even develop some (or possibly many) Charms that use the air element or some resonant principles to be good at it, but the emotional outlook is not so assumed to be generally drawn to it or most strongly expressed by it.
