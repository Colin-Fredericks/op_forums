                                                        I was thinking about it and I think I figured out why the Heirloom bonus generally felt underwhelming to me. Granted, I was expecting something like the weapon either only working for scions of a given house or that it would have a unique higher essence evocation that only unlocked for that house or something like that, but "You automatically unlock the first evocation free if you're of the blood-line I like" is actually pretty nice. However, there are enough artifacts that already grant any resonant wielder their first Evocation for free, that it doesn't really feel particularly special for that to generally be the Heirloom bonus.

                                                        The artifacts are quite cool in general, I loved their stories ( "Attempted Bribery" ), but ...I dunno, I'd want a secret technique or something that only unlocked for my House.

                                                        To be fair, Imprimatur's bonus of +1 to max Renown for Nellens and Perdurant Vault's access to a city that's potentially full of house secrets and wonders, both kinda work for me. I guess I kinda feel like Traverse the Secret City should only awaken for members of House Ragara and maybe the Nellens bonus to max renown should be bigger or have an upgrade charm, but both of those feel like unique bonuses that allow members of that house to really get more out of the artifact than anyone else can, literally in Nellen's case and figuratively in Ragara's case.

                                                        (I figure that any Ragara that left something in there for a future wielder would have left records for them and defenses, just in case the shield was lost or stolen. So, anyone who invades the vault without access to the Ragara records should be in for some nasty surprises.)
