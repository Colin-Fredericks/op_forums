    How Good are DB Charms?

    04-10-2020, 08:57 AM
    Hello, I have a question and I think it's going to require a lot of words, so I thought it was better to ask in a new topic instead of bloating the Ask a Question one.

    Also, if I can, I'd like to summon DrLoveMonkey for this, since I read his analysis of both the Lunar and DB splats (yes, I'm a filthy lurker).

    So, in the already mentioned analysis, it turned out that DB sucks majorly against both Lunar and Solar Exalted. That's to be expected, of course, since they have clear weaknesses, especially regarding their Excellencies and their mote pool. Having both less fuel in their tanks and a dice pool maximum inferior to their opponents limit them a lot. Their Charms are also inferior and interact with their Aura in complicated ways. I'm absolutely terrible at remembering what Charms do because dice adders -that are not Excellencies-, dice tricks, and similar things are just white noise for me. The Charms I remember better are the ones that have some strange, esoteric effect, like most Charms that have clear visual effects. But even I can see that Aura is a little clunky at times.

    But how good are their Charms without all those problems? What if the DB had the same -or comparable- Motes of the Solar and Lunar, their Excellency let them have a similar dice pools (in the range of 21, plus whatever for weapons) and the Aura mechanic was a little easier (like, your Aura count as both your Aspect element and the one you used to increase your anima).

    How would DB compare to Solar and Lunars in that case? How good their Charms would be if they had Celestial level mote pool and Excellencies?

      I don't understand the question.

      Solar Charms > Mastery Martial Arts > Lunar Charms > Martial Arts & DB Charms in Aura > Dragon-Blooded Charms out of Aura > Terrestrial Martial Arts

      An optimized DB (RAW) can easily take down an "average" Celestial. An optimized Celestial can easily take down 2(-ish) optimized DBs.

      If DBs had 21 dice caps, easy aura, more motes... they'd be much more powerful. But I still don't understand the question.

      The Essence 5 Solar Medicine capstone is Perfect Celestial Chirurgeon: double 7s on a Medicine roll, 1/story.
      The Essence 5 DB Medicine capstone is Most Beneficent Seed of the Five Dragons: double 7s on a Medicine roll, 1/story for 5m 1ahl but with a reset condition.

      A Solar can start with their capstone Charm. A DB can't. The DB can reset their Charm. A Solar can't. The Solar Charm is FREE. The DB Charm will flare if Peripheral Essence is used, and costs an Aggrevated health level. Solars have a 21 dice cap for 10m (Ave. 16 successes; 10+ successes 95% of the time). DBs have a 17 dice cap with a free non-Charm success for 6m (Ave. 15 successes; 9+ successes 95% of the time) for 6m. A Solar pays +4m for +1 success. Rules As Written.

      These metrics don't change if you give the DB more motes and free Wood Aura. But 21 dice with double-7s and a free success (Ave. 17 successes; 11+ successes 95% of the time) is better than 17 dice with double-7s and a free success.

      Dragon-Blooded are the weakest of the Exalted... but as the Exalted they are still really good.

                  Originally posted by Shapechanger View Post

                  Basically, excluding everything else, how DB charms compare to Celestial Charms?
                  Sorry, still don't understand the question.


                  That's what I wanted to see. As I said, I'm terrible with Charms, but from this, I understand that, given the obvious difference in dices, the DB Charm is pretty comparable to the Solar one, but it costs more.

                  Is that something that's true for all DB Charms, or you just a coincidence?
                  Ehhhh... not all Charms.

                  Highlights

                  Archery
                  Solar: Phantom Arrow Technique (starting Charm), fire your bow for 1m instead of using arrows. Reflexive. 1/scene get bonus attack dice based on an Intimacy at the cost of "spending" that Intimacy. May make one arrow indestructible at Essence 3.
                  DB: Harvest of the Hunter (starting Charm), get guaranteed arrows on a successful ammunition check, with double-8s. Supplemental. 1m.

                  Both Solars and DBs can keep firing their bow once they run out of arrows. Ammunition checks are Reflexive, scavenging is an action. Solars are spending 1m every turn for guaranteed success. DBs are spending 1m every five turns with a chance if failure. Solars get bonus powers tacked onto their Charm. DBs can fully replenish their arrows (which means they can share with their allies). Solars more powerful but costlier, DBs more efficient.

                  Athletics
                  Solars: Increasing Strength Exercise. Simple. 3m, +1 Strength/scene. Stackable upto the lower of (Essence times) or (double Strength). Bonus Decisive damage. May pay with Initiative instead of motes.
                  DB: Strength of Stone Technique. Simple. 5m, +1 Strength/scene. Must be standing on the ground. Bonus Withering damage.

                  The bonus damage is a wash. The DB Charm is more expensive (to the point of raising your Anima), not stackable, and has conditions on its use. Honestly there's not much in it. You are usually standing on the ground. +2m isn't a massive deal. You are really unlikey to be in a position where you can just stand still and charge up Increasing Strength Exercise five times in a row. But the Solar Charm is clearly better.

                  Awareness
                  Solar: Awakening Eye (Essence 1). 5m 1WP. Get a free full Excellency to Join Battle, with extra non-Charm dice, and bonus successes to find hidden enemies.
                  DB: One-With-Earth Embodiment (Essence 3, Signature). 10m 1WP. Swim through earth (including stone) like water.

                  DB Charms do not always work anything like Solar Charms. If you're really good at feeling vibrations, DBs can swim through solid stone and Solars can go first in combat. Solar Charms represent things humans can do dialled up to 11. DB Charms are about manipulating the elements.

                  Brawl
                  Solars: Excellent Brawl (free Charm). Gain upto +10d for 1m each.
                  DB: Become the Hammer (Excellency). Gain upto +6d for 1m each. Parry lethal damage unarmed. Reroll 6s on damage.

                  The Solar Charm has more raw power, but the DB Charm has WAAAAY loads more utility.

                  Bureaucracy
                  Solar: Soul-Snaring Pitch (Essence 3). Make any deal, no matter how bad it is, automatically with anyone who has a lower Willpower than your Essence score or with massive bonuses to the roll if their Willpower is higher.
                  DB: Distraction of the Babbling Brook (Essence 3, Signature). Sneak a hidden clause into a deal, taking a penalty based on how severe it is. 1/story.

                  The Solar charm is stronger, easier to use, and not limited to once per Story. It's much better. But both Solars and DBs can convince you to sell yourself into slavery.

                  Craft
                  Solar: Craftsman Needs No Tools (Essence 1). 6m. Drastically speed up Major and Basic projects for all Crafts, and work without tools.
                  DB: Stone-Carving Fingers AND Shaping Hand Style (Essence 2). 6m. Speed up Major and Basic projects OR work without tools, so long as you either work stone or a Craft you have at 4-dots or more.

                  What takes DBs 2 Charms and Essence 2, Solars do at Essence 1 with 1 Charm. And Solars are more powerful. And Solars are more versatile.

                  Dodge
                  Solar: Shadow Dancer Method. Spend 1m to not lose Initiative on a Disengage. Gain Initiative whenever you Disengage then move back in.
                  DB: Heat-of-Battle Advance. Spend 2m to not lose Initiative on a Disengage AND get extra dice on the attempt, but can only use it if moving towards an enemy, and the Charm is Perilous.

                  The Solar Charm is cheaper, easier to use, and directly helps you win combats. The DB Charm is more powerful, but is more expensive and of more limited use.

                  Hopefully that's illustrative.

                  (There's an Essence 3 Solar Investigation Charm which gives you 3 Charm successes for 6m. Basically giving you a Muted, no discount Excellency. They also have Shadow Over Day, which is "buy a Charm to save 1m under extremely limited circumstances... maybe". So it's not like it's just DBs who have bad Charms.)

                      One other thing you’d want to do is make more DB charms Supplemental instead of Simple. One of a Solar’s really powerful abilities, on top of dice cap, good charms and Supernal, is their ability to combine ten different powerful charms at once, and even on reflexive actions. For example Thunderbolt Attack Prana, it doubles decisive damage. Roaring River Slash also doubles damage, but being a simple charm or can’t be combined with other simple charms or reflexive actions like TAP can. Although TAP also has the restriction that it must be used on a target a range band away.

                      A lot of direct DB charm effects though only end up being weaker when they’re straight power abilities too. For example Fire and Stones Strike if you’re Strength 5 it’s 5 motes for 5 damage, for a DB it’s 3m 1wp for 5 damage using Crimson Fang Bite, similar effect but greater cost. On the other hand something like Flowing God Dragon Stance is a DB charm that turns your body into water for the scene for 15m 1wp letting you slip through cracks in the floor, keyholes, underneath doors, also granting +1 evasion and Essence/2 bonus successes which are non-charm while in water. Solars just don’t get anything like that, because “turn your body into liquid.” Is waaaay outside of Solar power. The closest they get is a 10m 1wp charm that lets them pass immaterially through one locked door or grate, the only benefit is that it works on waterproof portals.

                      So basically DB charms are harder to combo together, usually cost more, although their effects are often pretty close to as good, and in some specific cases even better where their mastery of elements applies. Also some effects that are almost incomparable like lighting a brushfire around you while you rush an enemy, or using awareness charms to destroy an enemy’s motes of essence. Unique effects that are like nothing Solars can do.

                      I would also give some caution against trying to equalize the two outside of very specific games. One of the things that makes Solars cool is the awesome feats they can perform. If a DB could perform those same awesome feats as well as all the cool elemental powers they have the Solars aren’t really left with much. Also Solars are on the unquestioned top dogs for mechanical power and unquestioned bottom dogs for everything else. Playing a Solar is supposed to feel great because you’re so strong, and also confining because you have to hide that power whenever you can, DB games feel great because you can totally cut loose and abuse the fact that most populations believe you to be living saints, while being not so powerful that you can just punch through any problem you come across. In mixed games it could even be an issue “So this Fire aspect guy is just as badass in combat as me, and can flare his anima in public, and get his way with almost anyone by saying ‘doing this for me would give YOU the gratitude of House Cathak.’ So why am I playing a Solar again?”

                                              Shadow Over Day is one of those Charms that don't look like much at a first glance, but then turn out to be ridiculously useful in actual play.

                                              - it allows basically any character to max out their Guile without having to spend peripheral motes or flare their anima
                                              - after an extended social scene, it can trivially leave you with 10+ extra motes in the tank (especially from E2 on)
                                              - at E2, it allows you to counter the effects of a 4m expenditure (which, for Dragon-Blooded, is a full excellency for anybody who is not invested in reading intentions to the max, and for Celestials, often the practical limit of what they can toss around more than once in a scene) for half the cost

                                              Any Solar who cares about keeping their cards close to their chest does themselves a massive disservice by skipping this Charm.
