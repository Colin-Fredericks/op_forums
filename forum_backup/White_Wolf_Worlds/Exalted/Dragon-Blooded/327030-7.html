        Originally posted by wulf View Post
        But I want characters who are totally in control of their own element (the primordial essence of which literally runs in their veins). Are the DBs supposed to be clumsy when it comes to their own Exalted legacy as the inheritors of raw elemental power?

        A lot of the elemental effects I wish Dragon-Blooded had could be approximated with existing Charms, but they all lack the versatility and flexibility that I deeply crave. A Fire-Elemental could start a small fire, cause the fire to leap and ignite separate candles and then dance in the air to put on a show - all with one Charm. A Dragon-Blooded might need two or three Charms to do the same thing. As far as I know, Earth-Aspected DBs do not have a Charm that lets them tunnel through solid earth, but an Earth Elemental could with the appropriate Charm. Does this not seem strange?
        I wouldn't be surprised to see more DB charms for manipulating their element to an extent -- Earth-aspect DBs did get a climbing charm that caused stone and earth to make handholds for them, and it's not unreasonable for them to get a tunnel-making or earthworks-raising charm in Craft.

        However, I think you're misunderstanding what the DBs are. They are not elemental deities or Nobilis-style masters of an elemental domain; they are heroic swords-and-sandals demigods whose powers express themselves through raw elemental essence. A typical Fire Aspect, say, might be a swordsman who burns with unnatural fire when his passions rise and who leaves streaks of flame behind him when he swings his sword full-force; and he might be able to harness this power to draw forth a sword of elemental fire or to fling bolts of flame.

        But the crux of his power's expressions are based around the concept of heroic human talent as realized through fire-aspected Essence, not "master of all things fire"; the DB charmset is not friendly to someone who wants to ignore swordsmanship (or whatever) entirely and just "do fire stuff." And similarly, for the most part, a DB's command over the elements exists at the points where it intersects with their heroic skill, rather than being a sorcery-style unnatural art that they can apply to whatever they desire.

                                                            I think saying that they're "similar to the Solar Exalted" isn't quite on-the-nose. That's just how the Exalted work, as a group. The Solar Exalted are the purest expression of it, but it's more an "Exalted thing" than a "Solar thing". The character of the individual Exalted is not merely aesthetic/cosmetic, but it seldom diverges as far from the baseline as what you seem to want. Not giving its main protagonists Mage-style powers on tap is a deliberate design decision that has subtle interactions with a number of the game's aesthetic and philosophical premises. I'm sorry that you assumed the "elemental Exalted" would rely on generalized elemental manipulation and were let down by the reality, but I don't think you can really blame the game for being misleading, because there's nothing about "the character type with elemental abilities" that implies that.

                                                            There's clearly leeway to mess with this with Exigents, but my inclination would be to go for god-blooded or raksha for what you're after, or to look for it in another RPG. Which isn't meant as a "my way or the highway" statement, it's just that the game is built around a conscious rejection of the specific thing you want, so it's going to be hard to find a satisfactory compromise there.
