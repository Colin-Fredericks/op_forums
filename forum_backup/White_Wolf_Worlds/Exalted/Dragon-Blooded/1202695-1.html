        Cathak Nemis, a Fire Aspect swordswoman who uses the doctrine of House Cathak to temper her relentless bloodlust, forcing herself into the role of "soldier" when among her fellow soldiers, but unleashing her more unhinged, bloodthirsty side when things have broken down and they just need everything even remotely near her dead. Torn between her inherent bloodlust and her desire to be, in her own words, "a warrior, not a murderer". Trained at the House of Bells, where she accidentally killed a classmate during a spar when she went overboard. Will be a fun exploration of the the dynamic between the forced order of The Realm and the intense emotions found in the Dragon Bloods, especially Fire Aspects. Also she's got a cool Red Jade Daiklave with with a bit of Soulsteel mixed in, whose charms will be based around aggression, speed, and relentlessness, with an aspect of it being a small to medium power boost (balance in the works) based on how much blood she's spilled. Fighting style overall is going to be somewhat akin to the Odogaron from MHW, lots of leaps and charges, and just generally an all out offensive.

                        Injira, an Air Aspect Lost Egg of Prasad turned battle-sorcerer. He is, by many metrics, an ideal soldier; taking the coin and seeing the machine of the Realm convinced him it was the greatest force for civilization in the world, and he is fanatically loyal to the Throne; if ordered, he would happily lead a raid against his own homeland to preserve the Empire. He is sometimes known as the Spell-Terrier due to his personal loyalty to Mnemon, who has a daughter who was his tutor in sorcery (and not coincidentally, a Realm patriot herself); it was originally an insult given his small size and love of small canines (he somehow talked the instructors to let him bring his pet domestic fox along as a familiar), but he's embraced the term; he sees himself as a loyal hound meant to serve as an advance force to chase rats out of their dens by shouting his magics into the world.

                        Many metrics. The others are aware his blood brother, who he adores, is a Lunar Anathema with an actual dog totem (my other character idea). One who is still sane, unlike those other Lunars who have been consumed by the beast within, and working very hard to control his instincts towards destruction and domination, but he's still a shapeshifter with a big silver tattoo on his head. He was quite shocked and unnerved when he discovered the mainstream Immaculates think of them as pure monsters incapable of getting on the path back into karma and harmony. He doesn't bring it up, but he quietly pressures his men and fellows to maybe judge people based on their actions as people. He'll assist the shikari, but he runs as an impartial magistrate who judges whether someone should be executed or shipped off to Prasad for purification and a second chance.

                        Which is, naturally, what the Silver Pact wants - they almost planned it.

                                                Well, i think i have a character idea of my own to toy with now. It hinges on 3e's core stuff than anything else and truth be told is more of a build than concept, but it's a start.

                                                Basically, i decided to make a character that goes total nuts with the whole nature/poison theme: Wood Aspect trained in the Immaculate Style of his element, with Sorcery - and Wood Dragon's Claw as control spell - for a bonus. My intention is to see how far i can go in comboing/theming with mixing Wood Dragon Style charms, sorcerous Evocations from Wood Dragon's Claw, his anima and other native charms.

                                                My initial image of him was of a sort of rogue monk, something like an early Cevis Gandharva of the Forest Witches, a sort of youthful Thulsa Doom/Kurtz mix, but somewhere along the way i started thinking of him as a Outcaste and things changed into more group-friendly and interesting direction, a sort of Mowgli/Sun Wukong mix instead:

                                                A rural bumpkin that exalted in the middle of nowhere in the hinterlands of the Blessed Isle (probably, though Threshold in a strongly Immaculate-influenced Satrapy might work too), went into a kind of serious trip, half mystical revelation, half essence fever in action and buried/lost himself amidst the woods from there, wandering, foraging, meditating, hunting & frolicking with dragons only knows what and finding a sort of illumination (sorcerous initiation) along the way and while living of the land for many months, if not a whole year or more, until growing rumors of his many and strange antics lead to the coming of one or more monks, under the mistaken assumption some rogue nature spirit was behind these actions.

                                                The monks, between amused, flabbergasted and shocked at the peculiar "savage enlightened child" took him to the Cloister of Wisdom - coin?choice?what? - where it was fully indoctrinated into proper immaculate ways, though the "proper" part itself (or the ascetism) never meshed well with his temperament, with lots of highs, lows and twists in the form of occasional bouts of rebellion, with and without sorcerous antics resulting from it. But the monks were patient, stern and unyielding, while Wood changes and adapts, so it was that he learned - to disguise well the sybaritic aspects that come all too naturally to his element, if not much else - until the time came for him to be unleashed upon Creation.

                                                ....ok, maybe Kasif/Jack Harkness hybrid - with Sorcery - might be a better description of him in the end.

                                                Anyways, that's the DB PC that i intend to play next.

                                                    Depends entirely on who runs the game, but if the guy who ran the last DB game decides to continue his previous story:

                                                    Mnemon Aliden, the son of the Windtamer and an unknown mother, was given to House Mnemon to raise as part of a deal (which only the GM knows the details of). Aliden is an Air Aspect of slightly above average height, with pale blue skin, short white hair (which is not so much styled as it is windswept) and his iconic anima banner is that of a thundercloud, writhing with energy. His voice, while melodious, has been tamed and focused by years of study at the House of Bells to be almost as much of a weapon as his blue jade daiklave "Stormfang". This blade is in the shape of a lightning bolt's outline, hosts an Elemental Lens, and allows it's wielder to absorb lightning into Essence - which Aliden usually throws out immediately as Elemental Bolts because he likes being a conduit for his element.

                                                    He's never met his father, but as most of his peers don't have a close bond with theirs either it's mostly a matter of curiosity than anything else although he would pay almost any price to know who his mother is. He knows he isn't a child of House Mnemon thanks to a slip from his adopted mother, but he has been at first discouraged, then forbidden, and finally sorcerously bound against pestering any of his house elders about it.

                                                    Aliden has three major problems in his future
                                                    • Firstly, the world doesn't live up to the standards he expects of it. He's old enough to remember how things worked under the Empress, but young enough to still be idealistic, and so is disgusted at the infighting among his elders when the world is going to crap. He doesn't have the wisdom to see that this is a flaw in how the Empress set everything up, and so idolises her.
                                                    • Secondly, he's only 21 and has no idea how to go about fixing things except with a blade or a pretty smile. He's been coasting on natural talent for the last 16 years, so one day he's going to get a rude awakening when he first experiences failure.
                                                    • Lastly, one of his hearthmates is addicted to crack cocaine and another is being slowly driven insane by an artifact cloak (and was a kleptomaniac even before this happened). He's too idealistic to realise his close friends have a problem they can't solve by themselves, and they won't ask for help.
