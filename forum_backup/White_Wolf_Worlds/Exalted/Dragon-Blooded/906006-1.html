    Yeah, I basically see every part of the setting's statements on the Realm DB population as totally bonkers.

    The Empress doesn't want too many DBs for her to control... so she creates a system which incentivizes them to keep as many of such children a secret (and so, outside of her influence) as possible. The Great Houses want to keep their Breeding strong... so they take a bunch of elaborate measures, which end up giving a lower DB birth rate per capita than the far lower-Breeding Lookshy. Most Dynasts never Exalt... which is why you almost never see or hear about unExalted Dynasts, and when you do, they're treated as these terrible pariahs for doing what the majority of people do. Dynasts are infamously hedonistic, with prostitutes and orgies (as well as Neomah, who can magically increase the odds of making DB babies) mentioned constantly... so they practice stellar birth control at all times. Yes, even reckless males on assignments overseas.

    It's bizarre. My advice: ignore everything.

    Well, to be more exact, ignore everything you dislike. The version presented by the game is totally incoherent, so there's no great loss if you throw the nursery out with the swimming pool. Cut anything and everything which doesn't seem cool for the game you want to run, and make things up to cover. My personal headcanon is that DBs have (but may not have had, before the Great Contagion) a low fertility rate amongst themselves, and a VERY low rate with non-DBs. Their children do usually exalt, though - especially if both parents are DBs, and if they have high Essence. This means:
    • Ambitious Dynasts cannot churn out supersoldier armies in secret, because makin' babies that fast is practically impossible.
    • DB-DB couplings really are the best way to keep your bloodline going.
    • UnExalted Dynasts are rare, and a huge blow to their parents.
    • DBs married to mortals have suffered a crippling loss of status, or other extreme fate, rather than this happening constantly due to force of numbers.
    • Most DBs in The Realm had two (and failing that, one) Dragon-Blooded parent, rather than most being born to two unExalted Dynasts (again by force of numbers).

    In other words, it clears up my main objections. But other objections, requiring other solutions, are perfectly reasonable.
