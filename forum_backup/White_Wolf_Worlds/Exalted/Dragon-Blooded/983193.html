    Air Dragon Style (3e conversion) - PEACH

    10-07-2016, 03:01 AM
    While I know the Dragonblooded book is around the corner and the charms in there are likely going to be better than my attempt, a game I'm involved with would rather strongly have the entire style *right now*. So I threw together this quick little conversion after about a day since I have nothing else to do while terribly. I'm hoping its not wildly OP, but so long as it doesn't snap the game in two then I'll be happy with it until DB's arrived for 3e. Overall I am happy with it, especially for something slapped together on 12 hours of sleep in the past 3 days.

    Air Dragon Style

    We all know the fluff, lets just get into it.

    Supplementary Abilities: A high Dodge is useful in this style not only for defending against attacks, but also for disengaging.

    Air Dragon Weapons: This style is compatible with Chakrams and its artifact equivalent, the Infinite Jade Chakram

    Armour: This style may be practiced in light armour.

    Dragonblooded Synergy Bonus: An Air Aspect Dragonblooded who activates Air Dragon Form treats themselves as a Celestial for the purposes of its charms.

    Theme: Being a fast little git and firing off attacks constantly.

    WIND DRAGON SIGHT
    Cost: 3m; Mins: Martial Arts 2 Essence 1; Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: Mastery
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: None

    Attuning to the very air around him, the Immaculate is able grasp information from the very air itself to see not only his surroundings but what the future may bring. So long as the user is surrounded by air (Or some form of gas) they can negate up to (Dexterity) points of penalties from their Evasion rating, with surprise attacks not counting towards this cap. In addition the Immaculate is able to ‘feel’ things around him using the air. Upon using this charm he’s able to sense by touch everything out to medium range, with relevant sensory charms quite possibly boosting his senses further.

    Mastery: The user may activate this charm with an indefinite period in order to sense using the air around them, and the user may activate the charm again to gain access to the Evasion penalty reduction may be done at a cost of 2m. Blind Dragonblooded may also access this mastery benefit as a special exception.

    AIR DRAGON SPEED
    Cost: 4m; Mins: Martial Arts 2 Essence 1; Type: Supplemental
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Air Dragon’s Sight

    The Immaculate of the Air moves as swift as the wind itself. This charm may be used on any movement action, doubling all 9s in it. If used in part of a disengage, waive the initiative cost of the disengage action if all other opponents have lower initiative.

    BREATH SEIZING TECHNIQUE
    Cost: 3m; Mins: Martial Arts 3 Essence 1; Type: Supplemental
    Keywords: Stackable, Withering-Only
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Air Dragon’s Sight

    With the very command of the element of air at their fingertips, the Immaculate is able to tear the very air out of one’s lungs or the spark of liveliness out of their bodies. A withering attack that deals more damage than its target’s Stamina inflicts a cumulative -1 penalty on physical actions for the rest of the scene. If the penalty rises over a character’s Stamina, they begin to suffocate, but only pass out for the rest of the scene, rather than dying, if they run out of air. Each time the victim gains 5+ Initiative from a withering attack, this penalty decreases by one.

    SHROUDING THE BODY AND MIND
    Cost: 4m; Mins: Martial Arts 3 Essence 1; Type: Supplemental
    Keywords: Mute, Terrestrial, Mastery
    Duration: Until movement stops
    Prerequisite Charms: Air Dragon’s Sight

    The Immaculate knows how to emulate the element of wind, being both invisible and swift. This charm can be activated when the user moves (Be it a movement action or a reflexive move triggered by disengage/rush or charms), the Immaculate blurs into the wind to effectively become invisible. Any sight based roll to locate her automatically subtracts two successes and the user suffers no penalties for moving while in Stealth. However this charm automatically ends if the user doesn’t move at least one range brand in a turn.

    Terrestrial: This charm must be reactivated every turn in order to maintain its effects, but for every turn after the first it only costs 2m to maintain.

    Mastery: Ranged attacks suffer a penalty of the Martial Artist’s (Essence) to all ranged attacks so long as she remains invisible.

    AIR DRAGON FORM
    Cost: 9m; Mins: Martial Arts 4 Essence 1; Type: Supplemental
    Keywords: None
    Duration: One Scene
    Prerequisite Charms: Air Dragon Speed, Breath Seizing Technique, Shrouding the Body and Mind

    The Immaculate assumes a stance that is light and flowing like the wind, but at the same time her fists crackle with electricity to show the sudden fury that only the wind is known to bring in an instant. First the Immaculate’s weapons are wreathed in electricity, adding an additional (Essence) damage to all withering attacks. Unarmed attacks may attack out to short range and can be stunted to deal lethal damage while attacks made with Chakrams convert any dice from aiming into automatic successes. Lastly the Immaculate adds +1 to his evasion and +1 automatic success to any movement/balance related action. At Essence +3 these bonuses increase by 1 again to a total of +2.

    Special Activation Rules: Whenever the Martial Artist successfully disengages she is able to throw up Air Dragon Form reflexively.

    TORNADO OFFENSE TECHNIQUE
    Cost: 5m 1WP; Mins: Martial Arts 4 Essence 2; Type: Simple
    Keywords: Withering-Only, Terrestrial
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Air Dragon Form

    Go! Cyflash!

    The Immaculate becomes a whirlwind of terror, raining down destruction and death faster than the eye can see. This charm fires off a withering attack at every single opponent within short range of the Immaculate with a single roll applied to all, but only gains an amount of initiative that any one attack generated. Any extra initiative gained from Initiative crashes are still gained. Against battle groups, this charm ignores any soak bonus a battle group gains from their size. This charm can only be used once per scene, but may be reset if the user manages to disengage/rush (any combination of the two) opponent(s) three times after making a decisive attack.

    Terrestrial: The Dragonblooded only gains an amount of initiative equal to the amount of people successfully damaged by this charm and only one initiative crash bonus (if any were gained).

    This one I'm unsure if it seems too powerful or not. I could make the reset condition much harder, cap the damage somehow/make the damage of the withering attack lower, etc.. I... Think its fine though considering the synerengy it has with Thunderclap Kata.

    WRATHFUL WINDS MANEUVER
    Cost: 6m 3i; Mins: Martial Arts 5 Essence 3; Type: Simple
    Keywords: Terrestrial
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Tornado Offense Technique

    By making a wide clap, shouting a kiai, or holding their chakram up and using it like a bladeless fan, the Immaculate is able to decimate an area with centralized hurricane force winds that can pulp bones and rupture eardrums. Upon using this charm the Immaculate creates an instant environmental hazard that has the traits of (Essence + 1)B damage and (Essence + 1) hazard to resist. Those who are damaged by this sonic assault are subjected to a sharp pain in their ear, causing deafness for (5 - Victim’s Stamina, minimum 1) turns and takes a -2 penalty to all physical actions. This charm can only be used once per scene, but may be reset upon making a decisive attack and building up to +13 initiative.

    Terrestrial: Dragonblooded do not gain the deafness benefit on the attack.

    THUNDERCLAP KATA
    Cost: -; Mins: Martial Arts 5 Essence 3; Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: Terrestrial, Mastery
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Wrathful Winds Maneuver

    The charge time has been improved considerably, why don’t you take a look for yourselves!

    The Elemental Dragon of the Air presides over the storms of the world, those monks who wish to imitate this effect may do so by letting actual lightning shoot out from their fingertips to fry their opponents alive. As the Immaculate chakram bounces from target to target, a lightning bolt flies out to energize the chakram, causing immense pain to those who even so much are grazed by it. This charm may only be activated after Tornado Offense Technique as a reflexive action that costs nothing. The Immaculate gains no initiative from the attack, however everyone who was damaged by Tornado Offense Technique are immediately subjected to a decisive attack with one roll being used against all targets that ignores hardness and has a base damage of the total amount of initiative that was reaped from the target. Targets wearing White Jade armour or are strongly associated with the element of earth retain their hardness as the resulting shocks can be grounded.

    Terrestrial: The amount of damage this charm can do is capped at (Essence or 3, whichever is higher).

    Mastery: Double an amount of 10s on the roll equal to any stamina penalties that the opponent may be suffering (This includes deafness from Wrathful Winds Maneuver)

    CLOUD TREADING METHOD
    Cost: 3m; Mins: Martial Arts 4 Essence 2; Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: Terrestrial
    Duration: One Scene
    Prerequisite Charms: Tornado Offense Technique

    By buoying herself with updrafts of wind the Martial Artist is able to become as light as the air itself. So long as this charm is active, virtually any surface can support the Immaculate’s weight. She can run across paper mache or water without breaking the surface of either and is even able to balance on top of falling leaves, clouds, or small puffs of smoke itself. When climbing such thing balances rolls to remain stable succeed automatically.

    Additionally when the charm is used, several small clouds appear in the area, enough so that the Exalt can move in any one direction they please including upwards. Additional clouds can be spawned reflexively at a rate of 2m per range brand.

    Terrestrial: The Dragonblooded must pay 3m per turn she wishes to keep this charm active.

    LIGHTNING STRIKE STYLE
    Cost: 3m; Mins: Martial Arts 4 Essence 2; Type: Reflexive
    Keywords: Uniform, Terrestrial
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Cloud Treading Method

    Even animals learn that when a storm is overhead one needs to seek shelter immediately lest they be struck by lightning. This charm may be activated on someone who has either not moved a range brand since the user’s last turn and is not in close range or someone who has just suffered initiative crash. Either way the Immaculate is allowed to make an immediate reflexive attack against the selected target. This attack soars to the target with the swiftness of lightning itself, so the attack cannot be dodged. This attack can only be used once per opponent per encounter.

    Terrestrial: Dragonblooded can only use this charm once per round.

    I thought about doing something else with this charm, in that it can just crush armour by negating a large amount of it along with a damage bonus, so I'm not sure which idea I like more. In the end I went with this as I like the tempest of blows better

    AVENGING WIND STRIKE
    Cost: 7m; Mins: Martial Arts 5 Essence 3; Type: Simple
    Keywords: Dual, Terrestrial
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Tornado Offense Technique

    With a simple wave of her hand the Immaculate is now able to channel gale force winds, and those who use Chakram’s will find this charm is superb to prevent foes from closing in on them. Against a target at close range the Immaculate may make a withering attack that doubles up to (Dexterity or Wits) 9s and blows an opponent back two range brands if the damage was greater than their stamina and landing them prone. Decisive attacks double (Dexterity or Wits) 10s and any amount of damage is sufficient to cause the blowback of the opponent. If the opponent is at short range, than the distance they’re sent flying is only one range brand and no doubling occurs.

    Those who have wronged the Immaculate face a far worse fate. If the Immaculate uses this charm against someone who has hit her since her last turn than her withering attacks have double 8s and decisive attacks have double 9s at close range and both capped at (Dexterity or Wits). These numbers increase by 1 if the attack is made from short range.

    Terrestrial: Dragonblooded may only use the decisive version of this charm.

    The original was bonkers strong, even for QC's. Nerfed it a little bit.

    HURRICANE COMBAT METHOD
    Cost: - (+6m 1WP, 3i per turn); Mins: Martial Arts 5 Essence 4; Type: Permanent
    Keywords: Perilous, Terrestrial
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisite Charms: Avenging Wind Strike, Thunderclap Kata

    You called down the thunder, now reap the whirlwind!

    The Immaculate of the Air unleashes the full fury of the storm upon his hapless opponents. This charm enhances Air Dragon form for a 6m 1WP surcharge, where upon activation the Immaculate eyes begin to spark wildly with barely contained power while a miniature hurricane starts around the whisky around her, picking up and thrashing things up to short range that do not require a feat of strength. The user gains the following benefits after activating this charm:
    • Wind Dragon Sight can now ‘feel’ out to long range.
    • She moves with the speed of the wind, Air Dragon Speed is upgraded. Rushes can now be performed from medium range while disengages can be performed from short range.
    • The range of all of her attacks are extended by one range brand.
    • She has harmonized the storm inside of her. The bonus successes the form provides to movement and to evasion no longer count towards dice caps.
    • The electric bursts and sharp gusts can cut through nearly armour. Against crashed opponents Air Dragon Form’s extra damage is now post soak instead of pre-soak.
    • The Immaculate is safely held aloft by the winds blowing around her. She is immune to falling damage.
    • The Immaculate strikes as swiftly as the wind itself. She is allowed to make two actions per turn, but one of those actions must be an attack or gambit. Only one standard Move action is allowed per turn.

    The form however requires an expenditure of 3i per turn to remain functional. If the user cannot pay this or enters initiative crash, the bonus features of this charm immediately end (Air Dragon form by itself remains however). The Immaculate is granted a small window of reprieve in that after making a standard decisive attack the initiative itself is count as ‘spent’ and thus the Immaculate need not pay the cost until the end of her next turn.

    Terrestrial: If the Dragonblooded wish to make a second attack on their turn they must pay 1 HL instead of 3i to maintain the charm, the hurricane tearing their bodies from within.

    I wanted to keep the feel of the original Hurricane Combet Method's "AH HA YOU'RE FUCKED!" feel, so I made a rare Essence 4 capstone with a large ongoing cost in exchange for a ton of offense and speed. Was tempted to make the extra damage add post soak but I feel that'd be too overkill. Attacking twice in a turn is extremely powerful yes, likely putting it up there with Single Point. Unlike single point it has a large drain on your resources, two bad rolls can crash you, but seeing as how this can only be used with Air Dragon and not Thrown/other charms I suppose its alright. Let me know what you think.
