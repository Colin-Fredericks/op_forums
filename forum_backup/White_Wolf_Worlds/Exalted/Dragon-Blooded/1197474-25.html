            Originally posted by Ekorren View Post
            They can also get up to 18 Merits. They are pretty fancy.
            Yes they can, but I wasn't surprised by them getting more Merits then Solars. They've gotten that since 1e, favored abilities though... they've been stuck @ 3 since the beginning, and I love that they are now more open to unique character concepts.

            I'm still on Archery Charms at the moment, and I like most of what I read. The only thing I'm a bit torn on is that if feels like some of the Aspect anima powers aren't really balanced against each other. Some of them are REALLY good (like Earth's and Wood's anima flux) while others are a bit underwhelming (like Water's "pay 3m for a non-Charm success to disengage"). I'm overall glad that the anima powers are tuned down from 2E, but some are still vastly superior choices to others in most situations.
            You'll find that for each ability there is a dominant element, and 1-2 strong supporting elements, and the rest have only a handful of charms. Their entire charm set is designed that way. It allows them to have more powerful effects because of the limitations of Auras, and such.

                                One limitation in dragon blooded charms comes from the aura mechanic. It boosts them, but also shuts off certain combos, because you can't combo an aura charm with a non-balanced aspect charm.

                                To give an example from the preview, Unshakable Mountain Spine would be really good combined with the repurchase version of Grandmother Oak Exertion, but you can't use both at the same time because the former is an earth-aspect aura charm and the latter is a wood aspect charm.
