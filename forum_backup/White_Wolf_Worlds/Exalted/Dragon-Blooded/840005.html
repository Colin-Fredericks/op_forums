    Is Chasing the Dragon still going to be a thing?

    03-01-2016, 01:29 PM
    Pardon me if I'm getting my nomenclature mixed up.

    By "chasing the dragon" I mean that unusual dragonblooded that pursues an Immaculate Martial Art that they are not elementally attuned to. A Fire Terrestrial pursuing Water Dragon style for instance. In 2nd Ed this and some added difficulties involved in it but once mastered gave some minor but cool benefits.

    In My Headcanon: when this was achieved it altered the essence of the Terrestrial making them vary unique cross elemental dragons Like the one mentioned above might be a Steam Dragon/Terrestrial, while a Water Terrestrial that chased the Fire Dragon might become a Boiling Dragon, etc...

    Do we have any information that this will still be a thing and will still be addressed? Or is this something I will likely have to reinsert into the game?

                                  Originally posted by The Unsung Hero View Post

                                  Sometimes I suspect if they had made it a discount instead of a surcharge people would have accepted it openly. Just make the baseline x motes higher by design and give an x mote discount for being properly aspected.
                                  People respond much better to this sort of game design; a perceived reward garners a better response than a perceived penalty, for obvious reasons.

                                  One of the classic examples of this is World of Warcraft. Back when they were designing it initially, in 2003, they were legitimately worried about people spending way to much time obsessively playing it. (This was back when Everquest was still a thing and was only somewhat jokingly known as "Evercrack.") Initially, they came up with the idea of "well, what if you gain XP normally for awhile, but after you've played for a certain amount of time you start earning it more slowly, to incentivize you doing something else for a bit?"

                                  Playtesters hated that.

                                  Then they came up with the idea of "Why don't we do the same thing, but we'll call the current fatigue state 'normal' and the current normal state 'rested.' People will feel like they're getting a bonus when they log on after a good nights sleep and are getting 'extra' XP for awhile because of it."

                                  Playtesters loved it even though it was the exact same mechanic.

                                  Something I'd like to see, in addition to the idea of recasting the out-of-aspect "penalties" as bonuses instead, is for the Ability minimums to be framed as a bonus rather than an obligation. Instead of "If you're playing a Dynast, you get X dots, but you MUST spend Y number of them on this ability spread" it should be "If you're playing a Dynast, you get Y number of dots in these Ability spreads FOR FREE! And you get Z dots to spend on whatever else you want" where Z is actually X-Y. It's the precise same design, but framed in a much better way.

                                  "SEX NOVA is the kind of person who, after being chosen as the divine champion of the god of heroes, decided to call himself SEX NOVA."
