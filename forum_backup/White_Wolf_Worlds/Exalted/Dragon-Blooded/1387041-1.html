            On a slight tangent, what are folks' thoughts for the most bang for your buck in terms for a lightly combat invested chargen DB?

            Came up as part of prep for a potential ease ourselves back into 3e session.

                        Raptorspam is probably the most straightforwardly powerful minimal-investment combat build (you want TCS with Flight of the Brilliant Raptor as your control spell, Threshold Warding Stance, Hopping Firecracker Evasion, annnd... you're pretty much done).

                        You can also abuse Flying Silver Dream, which nets you an independent combatant with better than peak mortal stats and actual Solar Charms. Best bang for your buck here is probably Stoking Bonfire Style, one Ox-Body, then Arise, Moonlight Blade into Winged Argent Guardian into Flickering Lunar Protector.

                        Last but not least, Single Point style is very powerful and engaging to play even with minimal investment and Terrestrial restrictions. You'll want Become the Hammer, one Ox-Body, then the Gathering Light Concentration + Shining Starfall Execution + SP Form triplet, and you're set. You'll never be as killy as someone who also masters Water Dragon Style and double attacks every round for free, but you'll have a set of nontrivial decisions to make in every round of every combat, which is probably more interesting than just destroying everything with Fatal Stroke Flash anyway.

                        Evocations for the demonic tattoos gained from the Pact with Mara sorcerous initiation || Pyre-Kindler (Soulsteel and Red Jade Grimscythe, Artifact 3) || Tenebrous Descent (Stormcaller's Black Jade Reaver Daiklave cousin, Artifact 5)

                            Originally posted by Moss Reynholm View Post
                            A slight preference for water or air I believe (a magistrate/investigator type).
                            Okay so as an investigator type you might want to be ready at all times, and a bit sneaky about things, so you might go thrown. Get Seeking Throw Technique, Invisibly Hidden Chakram Method and Mela's Twin Fangs, also grab Armor Rupturing Fang and two Ox-Bodies if you want to round out the charm selection. Also if you want to grab something like Speech Without Words from Linguistics that will let you roll up to the crime lair with your circle, get pat down for weapons, which will come up empty, and just when the crime lord's conversation seems to be taking a turn for the worst, signal to your crew with Speech Without Words that you're going to ambush them, also thereby entering Air Aura, then pow-pow, hit 'em with double knives right off the hop, and finish him next turn with an Armor Rupturing Fang to cripple his escape.


                            In the same vein as ready at all times, a water brawler might be cool. Get 3 strength, maybe even 4 and sacrifice your dexterity to 1, and take Flotsam-and-Jetsam Approach to use improvised weapons with strength instead of dex, and also take Water Dragon's Coils to use Strength for grapple gambits as well, plus the excellency. Then get either Currents Sweep to Sea and an Ox-Body or two Ox-bodies. It should give you some nice variety to pick up any kind of improvised weapon and use it with a high attack and damage pool, and with the added bonus of being able to grapple people out to Short/Medium range with water tendrils.


                            If you want to go air archery grab Flickering Candle Meditation and Hopping Firecracker Evasion, definitely don't skimp on dex. Hopping Firecracker isn't Air but who cares you're not using Aura anyway. Get the excellency for Dodge, Archery and Sky Calming Draw. Although this works a lot better if your ST remembers that penalties are a thing, especially environmental ones like slick ground, rain, wind, darkness, dust storms, fog, snow, etc. Since the dodge and archery charms do a lot of penalty negation which your enemies won't get. You won't need ox-bodies as much here since as soon as you escape to Short range the rest of your circle will keep most enemies trapped and thus safely away from you. Although I might pick up one later.


                            You could also grab almost any of the Immaculate Dragon styles and you should be pretty good, although maybe avoid Fire Dragon.
