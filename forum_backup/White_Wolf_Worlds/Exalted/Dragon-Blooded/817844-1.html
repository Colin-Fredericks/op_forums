                            Outcastes shouldn't be getting 5 favoured abilities. I'm even kind of dubious about DBs getting modular caste abilities as it is. They're kind of supposed to be really limited and specialized into their aspects.

                            I'm not sure about bringing back the breeding merit to give motes, it was kind of something that people got out of necessity rather than being a cool concept for the character. Maybe make it give a bonus to social rolls with people who treat higher breeding DBs with respect? Maybe it should be subsumed into the reputation merit?

                            The anima-flux is actually a rule in the third edition core. It does mostly what you said except it is caped at 1 die and also any hardness completely negates both effects.

                            I'd say remove Voice of Mastery entirely, effects like this are hard to come by, even in the Solar charmset, this edition.

                            I'd also bring Voices on the Wind down to a bas of Essence X 10 feet, and cap it's magnitude by (Essence/2) so at max essence you can get a thousand foot radius, but otherwise you need to be somewhere remotely closeby.

                            With One Mind is beyond my current ability to balance in third edition, but I'd say it's definitely too powerful as-is.

                            The range on Elemental Bolt Attack can get too high too fast, especially since I don't know if it can be targeted at medium range without aiming. I'd say range equal to (Essence/2)+1 and put in a reminder that attacks beyond short range require an aim action. So it can go short range at essence 1-2, medium at 3-4 and long at 5, with air aspects boosting that a range band each time.

                            Elemental burst should never hit more than one range band, hitting everyone in close range of one-another is great enough. Also half the damage on elemental burst.

                            I'd add a -2 to attacks made against spirits that you're using Spirit Detecting Mirror to see. Which is a pretty generous interpretation of trying to fight someone you can only see in the reflection of your swordblade

                            I'd make the archery disrupting effect of Wind Luring Song as-per my suggestion for air aspect anima, and stipulate that the two effects don't stack

                            Ditch Fivefold Resonance Sense's ability to lock on to artifacts attuned to other users. Yeah they're said to be concealed by any magic concealing them, but it lets you perfectly detect somebody who's not using magic to hide themselves

                            Change Trackless Passage to a simple penalty, maybe equal to (Stealth/2) dice, the effect as is completely cancels with all Solar tracking charms, among also making it impossible for mortal tracking to happen.

                            Dragon Shroud needs to have two effects, one that blurs you, and one that lets you spend motes to get automatic successes on stealth rolls, otherwise it's Infinite Mastery again

                            Whirlwind Shield Form should be as it was in the original, that being adding an (essence/2) dice penalty. This should explicitly not stack with air anima similar effects.

                            Vengeful Gust Counterattack shouldn't be adding non-charm dice, in fact I'm not sure anything in the DB charmset should be adding non-charm dice/successes, and also I kind of thought the counterattack effect was cool, maybe leave it more as it was in 2e? Where if the person successfully parries an attack, then you counter attack with a number of dice equal to the number of successes that they failed to hit you by?

                            Deadly Blades of the Five Dragons, air aspects absolutely need an aim action, maybe make it extend an extra range band?

                            Elemental Armor, Fire Aspects shouldn't increase their anima damage, I think nothing in the DB charmset should actually do that. Maybe just boost the damage of your weapon by 1-2? Also the Water one is way too good, at best it should reduce onslaught penalties by one. The wood one I'd say leave it at withering attacks gain 1 less initiative, the reset to base number of 3 is really important and shouldn't be messed with

                            Sense-Riding Technique should have a time limit of Essence hours or something, and should probably have a more hefty penalty to normal actions on their end of it.

                            Sense-Destroying Method should have a cap, maybe (awareness)? And not be able to take a person's sense completely unless it somehow gives a penalty equal to their whole dicepool

                            Essence Disruption Attack is just straight up too good in any form for third edition

                            Flaw-Finding Examination probably shouldn't be reducing a major repair to one minute. Half the time maybe?

                            Flaw-Finding Exploitation shouldn't affect artifacts.

                            Charms of Unmaking really shouldn't be giving craft experience. That turns them into a massive sxp engine. At best maybe gain 1sxp from unmaking things, if making it would have triggered an objective? As is they're already really powerful effects that can effect artifacts with high enough essence.

                            Unflagging Vengeance Motivation, Unnatural Influence isn't really a thing anymore, maybe he just ignores charms with the Psyche keyword? It also probably shouldn't counter magical fatigue, and definitely shouldn't be adding integrity to his soak.

                            Granite Curtain of Serenity should probably only be instant or action long, even Solars don't really get scenelong buffs like that anymore

                            Inviolate Dragon Spirit should add some bonus to his resolve, maybe equal to the intimacy's resolve bonus that he brought to bear? If it's 10m 1wp it might even be a candidate for non-charm successes.

                            Defense from Anathema Method maybe should be removed, but if it stays in then it's defense bonuses need to be at least halved

                            Ox-Body Technique should take off a -2 health level at stage of it's Stamina progression. Stamina 1-2=(-1) || Stamina 3-4=(-1,-2) || Stamina 5=(-0,-2)

                            Mountain Toppling Method definitely needs to have it's withering damage value pulled back to two or three bonus damage on withering attacks with jade weapons




                            OKAY! Well that took a while, other than all that though I'd say it's good! I like what you did with (Element) Protection Form! Anyway, It must have taken a super long time to write all these out, good work and keep at it! Don't take my criticisms too harshly either, there's just a lot of new stuff in this edition, like the aim action and scenelong dice buffs, that don't seem that important, but are actually super mega important and shouldn't be screwed around with lightly.
