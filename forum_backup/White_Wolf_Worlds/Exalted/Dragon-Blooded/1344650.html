    Dragon-Blooded Archer Advice Wanted

    10-30-2019, 10:06 AM
    I'm trying to build a "combat optimized" (starting character) Dynast who uses archery to destroy her foes.

    My first thought was a Wood Aspect who rode a fearsome beast like a Tyrant Lizard, or Hundred Rivers or a Pestletail, peppering foes with arrows whilst using the mount for speed and close defence. Then I saw that Wood Dragon Form needs a staff or a longbow -- and you can't use a longbow mounted. And the good Survival and Ride Charms seem to be Air aspected... and Air Archery doesn't seem too shabby. (Well Burning Fang Strike needs a Fire Aura.)

    Then I cracked open Arms and thought "Emerald Chevalier is a pretty nice platform for an archer".

    This is for a combat heavy, monster of the week, "And I'll Form the Head" kind of game (we're adding a third player to Dynasty of Dovak who wants a pregen to drop-in, drop-out of the Ganan sessions -- first foe is an army of hobgoblins).

    Any advice on how to build an effective Deeb archer in 3E, sample builds, need combos, common pitfalls would be gratefully received.

    Master cylinder.

                                          Originally posted by JohnDoe244 View Post
                                          But if you're advising Dodge and Athletics Charms... well they work better with Warstriders than mounts...
                                          Well, dodge mostly. Athletics has Soaring Leap which also helps disengage, but if you’re in a group you shouldn’t be contesting rushes TOO often.

                                          If you want to use wood dragon, which is good because it makes you hella versatile, then no mount, but dodge becomes much more important. You also might find yourself just sitting in melee fighting dudes with your powerbow-wrackstaff more often than not, which might not be what you’re going for.

                                          If you’re going pure bow there’s different tactics. Mounts are obviously helpful, flying ones triply so, but they’re also situational. Do you throw a bunch into Ride, possibly neglecting your unmourned movement abilities, and just hope you’re not fighting in too many areas that you can’t bring your mount?

                                          Something else to consider is when you are aiming. A lot of DB archery charms double in effectiveness when you aim, or require aiming. If you’re aiming every other turn you can spend 10m on your attack and still remain mote positive throughout the fight. So you can use a full excellency, Death from Nowhere and Sky Calming Draw for every withering attack, and a full excellency, Sky Calming Draw, Arrow Thorn Technique and Drawing Lightning Style on everyone decisive and never really rub out. It also means attacks from medium/long range are possible, just with lower accuracy. Which is better than it seems, you lose out on 2 accuracy but you also can’t be disengaged into. For an archer the worst thing ever is for somebody to disengage from the melee ball into you, because then you lose 6 accuracy and are stuck in with a melee focused guy. Also remember that decisive attack accuracy isn’t penalized for being at long range.

                                          If you’re an air aspect especially Stealth also gets opened up as an option. If your group lets you scout up ahead and sneak you can start combat while hidden, at Long range and as an exalt that should be easy, maybe set up Vanishing Wind Body, aim twice, and then finally reveal yourself by shooting. Then just keep aiming and/or shooting as they charge towards you, and pull them right into your waiting allies.

                                          On the other hand if you’re a mounted wood aspect I recommend Daring Venture and survival as a focus. Don’t bother much with athletics unless you have spare charms and points, it’s only for when you’re being chased down and also unmounted. It’s kind of an edge case at that point. Also with 3 non-charm autosuccesses to tracking from Hundred Rivers, Stalking Wolf Persuit, and Quarry Revelation Technique you’ll be able to track down just about anything anywhere. Even Solars will have trouble shaking that. Also try to find medium barding for your Wolf, you ignore the mobility penalty with your excellency anyway.

                                              Oh yeah, there’s also range dipping if you want to attack each turn. Turn 1 you’re at Short, you attack and retreat to Medium. Turn 2 you’re at medium, you move to short and attack. Repeat. That way you’re only vulnerable to ranged attacks and being disengaged into about half the time.

                                              A lot of what range you want to sit at, I think, depends on what you’re fighting. Really fast Fair Folk Cataphracts that can easily pop over to you and engage you? That’s probably something you want to fight at medium range, maybe even Long. If you’re facing something with like 9 defence and onslaught penalty negators, that’s probably something you want to risk engaging at Short range.
