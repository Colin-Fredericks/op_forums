                                    We did tons of Dragonblood, and the charms generally work (you just have to ignore what Step the writer thought they were). They work better than Sid charms. Of course, they're kind of weak* in 2.0, but then, we were playing the weak Dragonblood.*
                                    2.5 improves them, but going back and forth to check what's been errata'd is a big headache, and I was pretty much the only one who could remember what had changed most of the time.

                                    If you wanted the power, ​well... that's what Sorcery and TMA were for (I remember some people said MA were supposed to be a bit weaker, ie CMA were weaker than Solar charms, but whoever wrote TMA obviously didn't get that memo!).

                                    *On the other hand, in 2e, they made up with it with free reflexives and dirt-cheap excellencies, which was excellent. Not to mention artefacts. When we first started playing Solars, a Fire-Aspected Dragonblood with Five-Dragon Style was absolutely terrifying. He can attack... and defend! At the same time! And he has first-age power armour and a jade direlance! On one guy! We actually ran away from Dragonblood most of the time. Once the whole party managed to take down one Fire-Aspect.

                                    Finally... I loved playing Dragonblood because the fights were much more enjoyable than Solar fights. No-one needed to paranoia combo, because combos are for Anathema chumps. And no one perfect-spammed because the perfects cost willpower, and had flaws that were actually real flaws. So people actually had a proper combat rather than perfect-spam(x20)+die.
                                    I guess in 2e though, Solars should be able to have fun fights too.

                                        Solars are my favorite splat, because they are a blank canvas. Anyone can become a Solar, they can have any outlook, etc.

                                        Dragon-Blooded are number 2 on my list because they come with so much baked in. I can toss so many levels of family strife, intrigue, and generational ambition on them, and it's gratifying to see it play out. I came to WW as a Vampire player, so I've always enjoyed games that are more than hack 'n slash.

                                        I also really enjoy the experience of my PC somewhat hoodwinked. Most of the Immaculate Doctrine is bullshit, but hey, it tells my PC that he's enlightened and can do no wrong. Sounds like fun!

                                        Finally, there is a certain monster-movie feel to them. Sure, they are tough, but if they run into an Anathema, they could get splatted. So, stick together, and with a lot of skill and a little luck, the PC Dragon-Bloods can triumph over a superior opponent.

                                        One thing I have not had the opportunity to do, and would really enjoy, is playing a Dragon-Blooded Dynast in a group of Celestials. Using my connections and savvy to keep up with people with a great deal more inherent power is my kind of challenge.
