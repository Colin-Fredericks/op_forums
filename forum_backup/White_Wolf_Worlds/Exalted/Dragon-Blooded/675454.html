    Outcaste Dragonblooded Beastmen

    09-23-2015, 02:25 PM
    ...Just a weird thought I suddenly had.
    I mean, it's been said that the Realm and Lookshy both are... well, not aggressively recruiting, but still very much willing to take in outcaste Dragonblooded, because more dragonblooded blood is always good. It also mentions somewhere that these recruited DBs are considered slightly "less pure" than "proper" Dynasts, but still far above any mortal.
    Would that still apply to Beastmen that exalt as Terrestrials?
    Or would the associations between Beastmen and barbarians and Lunar Anathema make that impossible?
    Or is it in-between: Yes, they get accepted into the Realm, but will be considered as "Third-Rate Dragonblooded"?

    Silencing Whisper, Eclipse; Amethyst, Changing Moon;
    Daughter of Charcoal and Ash, Dusk, and her Full Moon sister;
    Broken Crystal, Infernal (Night-, or maybe Twilight-equivalent).

      I don't think the Realm and Lookshy have a monolithic perspective on beastmen. I could see pious Immaculates railing an outcaste beastfolk as a degenerate heresy, and I could see an equally pious Immaculate arguing that the mere fact of his Exaltation is proof that he is no lower than any other Dragon-Blood. The general answer is probably gonna lie somewhere between those two extremes—it's even easier to look down on and discriminate against people of lower social standing when they have patently bestial features, but at the same time, it would be very stupid indeed to let the opportunity to recruit an outcaste slip through your fingers just because they look funny.

              I suspect that the Beastman would be allowed into the Realm, but he would have to take the razor of the coin (most likely the razor), would generally be sneered at and treated rather poorly by the other Dragon Blooded (even other ex-Outcastes) and definitely wouldn't be allowed to marry into a Great House no matter what sort of victories or epic feats he managed to accomplish.. Even House Nellens, which is generally the most open towards recruiting Outcastes would probably view the social issues of openly adding a Beastmen to their House to outweigh any advantages, though maybe they would be willing to add him in under the fiction of recruiting him as of a mercenary. That's likely the absolute best that a beastman Terrestrial could hope to accomplish in the Realm.

              They could probably fair a little better in Lookshy, but I imagine overall a beastman Terrestrial would likely be happier to simply go his own way in the world, or try to join a smaller group (Eos and Oessa, the Forest Witches, Saloy Hins Legion) who would likely be more accepting and welcoming than to try and "fit in" in the Realm or Lookshy.
