                Originally posted by DrLoveMonkey View Post
                I'm really curious though, what battles do you think a DB should find easy or hard? Like I actually want to know, I'm not trying to imply that you're wrong, I just can't discuss it without knowing what you want. As it is right now a Blood Ape is a pretty easy fight for a reasonably optimized chargen DB, I'd consider that pretty exalted. You could also take out a battlegroup of bandits and their leader with ease, which is like fighting 20 men and a mortal hero, that's not bad. Fighting a pair of Blood Apes gets to be a bit harder though, and I wouldn't want to fight 3 without backup, but they are supposed to be the most powerful first circle demons. Fighting 3 Agata or Neomah would be no problem for a single Terrestrial Exalt.

                To me that SEEMS about right, but I'm not sure if that's what you're looking for. Like for it to feel Exalted would it be more like fighting a Blood ape and 20 cultists and the cult leader at once?
                Well, the blood ape thing is misleading because I'd actually buff blood apes a good bit if I could. A blood ape really isn't much stronger than an elite mortal hero, which is not my preference in the slightest. But my problem isn't actually in their pure strength. Their Excellency plus some Ox-Bodies is all they really need to be as strong as I want, in terms of the fights they can win. The problem I have is that so many of their effects are so glaringly miniscule. They feel like all they really are is their Excellency and two or three decent Charms around E2. That most of the Charms on my sheet are speedbumps to deplete my XP so I can't have too many good effects.

                This is why I say I'd only have Evocations or MA. So that I can just write or pluck the effects I want, the proper damage adders and boosters, and grab the social Excellencies, and thus everything on my sheet is a valueable thing with purpose. And not a lie meant to keep me from having cool powers I want to use.

                As it stands, I'm fairly certain if I made an Essence 1 Dragonblooded, I'd beat a size 5 battlegroup of battle ready troops, or Size 3 of Blood Apes. I'd back that same Essence 1 DB against two individually statted blood apes, or 4 individual mortal heroes. The powers that matter most in terms of winning fights are your artifacts and your Excellencies and your Ox-Bodies. Everything else is cool powers, flavor, it's the ability to do fun magic that feels like the strength of a Prince of the Earth, or do cool feats that your Excellency alone can't, or enhance your Excellency in some way.

                The Dragonblooded set has all the power I need it to have. It unfortunately also has a ton of speedbump Charms surrounding the powers I'd actually want to have, which clutter up my sheet and make character designing a mess, because so much of this is stuff I don't want.

                Thus, "fuck it, I'm just making a daiklave and a suit of heavy armor to do the stuff I like".

                And then I get really depressed because Dragonblooded are my favorite Exalted of all and I can't engage with their Charmset in any way but getting angry at it, and then getting sad about how angry I am at it.

                    Okay now I understand a lot more. I can see where you're coming from. In the Solar charmset you've got some charms that go up and down in power too, but the midline is high enough that they're all really powerful and not very situational, like some of the worse DB charms are. Like how Dragon Graced weapon lets you add the Disarming tag to any weapon as roots and vines sprout from it, or how Sky Calming Draw can give you a fast way to snap off a Long range arrow.                     The ones that just give a mechanical buff aren't that crash hot, because DBs with their artifacts and excellencies are kind of already as powerful as they need to be to strike fear into the hearts of just about anything short of a Second Circle Demon. So to stop them from getting too powerful, most of the charms that directly enhance attacks, accuracy especially, are less efficient than the excellency. That definitely sucks. Like Crossfire Flash is basically just (Essence) non-charm dice and if any of those dice end up a 10, add a further success for 5 motes. It's barely half as efficient as an excellency until you get to essence 4-5 and the only benefit is it's non-charm. The only reason you'd use it is if you were in a hard fight and losing 5 motes is worth it when compared to losing a clash, if those 2-3 successes will make the difference.

Almost all of the mechanically mediocre charms are only kind of bad because they rely on (Essence) or sometimes a stat like (Stamina) which gets better as you gain boat loads of xp, obviously. I'm not sure how to fix that though except maybe suggest to your group to just start the game with 150xp and essence 3? Maybe grant 60-90 DXP to even it out?
