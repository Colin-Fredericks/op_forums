                                    Couple things:

                                    1) The Empress's Aspect wasn't set until Return to the Scarlet Empress. I know for a fact that it wasn't "Earth, secretly, until RttSE revealed the truth to the world!" because I've spoken to the person who wrote it. Notably, Grabowski always replied with "Does she even have one anymore?" when asked the Empress's aspect, so for the Earth thing, as with everything in RotSE, take it with a grain of salt.

                                    2) There's a bit in Aspect Book: Air that suggests some bored secret society in the Shogunate aristocracy was engaged in a large-scale game of what's essentially sociopolitical Nomic (see "From a report by Magistrate-Captain Riarda Rikvashta," beginning page 63), and an offshoot of that involved a Dragon-Blooded breeding project. The hints are the Empress is one of the outputs of that. So there's a reason for her unusually strong bloodline.

I don't really know what DB do as a general rule, but I do know it's very important. Plus, who's going to do that paperwork? The Realm bureaucracy is busy enough managing the entire empire, and that assumes that there's some kind of systematic way to measure breeding strength, and you can reliably tell who has/will Exalt, which requires the Houses to be honest about one of their main strengths. Plus Exalting is, to a large extent, random: sure, breeding may may it more likely that someone will Exalt, but it's by no means any kind of systematic certainty. So, a few "strong" breeders could just have a bad statistical run, and some "weak" breeders could have a good run. Plus there's Lost Eggs to consider, and adoption...

                                                    It's really complicated, is the point. They do try to keep track, but they're far from perfect. Hell, they don't even have printing, read the bit on how they handle updates to the law.

                                                        Originally posted by notthepenguins View Post

                                                        I think that the superstition is based off of them having an incomplete understanding of the mechanisms of terrestrial exaltation. They're going "Look at that, with this generation's Dragonbloods almost exclusively married Earth Dragons, and a larger than average number of their kids Exalted, and most of them as Earth Dragons! That obviously means that purity of Aspect is a thing!" While what actually is happening is "Because this generation's dragonbloods married other dragonbloods almost exclusively, the exaltation rate of the next generation increased. Coincidentally, because a vast majority of the involved parents were Earth aspect, that higher proportion passed on to the next generation."

                                                        So, in practice, it looks the same, IF they actually care about purity of aspect. But how did the whole superstition start? I would posit the Sids started it through the immaculate order, and then helped tilt the board in favor of those who did do so for a while, just enough that everyone had just enough doubt in their mind that "Hey, they might actually have a thing going there...." And I could definitely image the Sids starting this superstition, because if the Terrestrials are so focused on breeding with other Terrestrials of their Aspect, then they're actually breeding with other terrestrials.
