    A whole Dragon of troops and statted NPCs for your use

    09-22-2019, 11:26 AM
    This is actually a family of (mostly Air) Outcastes for my game, so it's not exactly the same as a Realm legion; they use Shogunate titles, and there's two shamans, which you probably wouldn't get in a Realm Legion. I haven't included the intimacies, as they're quite specific to my game. But it's extremely easy to adapt for your own games.

    I've only really given them combat stats: I didn't bother writing in the Fire spymaster's larceny and investigation charms, or the Water Aspect's sail and bureaucracy charms, etc, etc. But combat stats are the most hassle generally I find.

    Most importantly, they are designed so that it's really easy to run Aura with them. Lots of balanced charms, a few Aura that match their Aspect. Aura's often a big hassle when running a whole bunch of NPCs fighting your Solars. But when we ran them vs the Lunar PCs, it was pretty easy.

    So I hope people find this useful.

    Kazei Jadis. Air, “the white witch”. Travels in a chariot. Likes giving sweets to children. Katsunaga's wife.
    Shozei Katsunaga, Earth. Fights with a massive tetsubo. Jadis's husband.
    Deputy is Tazei Umi, Air. Jadis's cousin. Fights with her mother's daiklaive, Shining Ice Mirror.
    Shozei – Hatsuno, Air, “Flashing Spear Lioness”. Amazing with her jade spear. Glory-seeker, but also professional. Jadis's cousin.
    Deputy is Tazei Saizo, blind Earth swordsman and master tactician. Yukihiro's lover and Jadis's great-nephew.
    Tazei Nemaja, Water, she wields a magic umbrella and is in charge of the Dragon's boats. Pretends diffidence but actually a vicious monster. Not related to the others.
    Tazei Yukihiro, Fire. In charge of spying operations for the Dragon Jadis's second cousin once removed.
    Tazei Samatsu, Fire. Not related to the others.
    Shugenja Goji, Fire. Seems friendly but actually very manipulative.
    Shugenja Itsuko, Water. Interested in ancient lore and the occult.
