                        06-22-2017, 04:01 PM
                        Signature charms are something I'm really excited for, because they look spectacularly easy to homebrew compared to something like a martial art, or a new charm branch leading up to what you actually want. Ability 5, Essence 3, few or no prerequisites, it can be slightly overtuned, it can have a ton of different effects (players tend to homebrew really complicated charm effects), and that's all fine, it's all fine, whereas with most charms you need to kind of keep all that in mind and balance them all. It also comes at Essence 3, right when you're comfortable with the systems at play and comfortable with your character. I love them!
