So if you can get just into bow-shot range, and set up as many things as you can while the enemy searches for you, and then hopefully break that stealth with a big powerful bowshot, and then harry them with more arrows as they charge you.

        The question I have is "how does this look mechanically"?

        Is such a transformation suitable as an Attunement bonus? Does it push the weapon to 4 dots? 5? What sort of Evocations grow from such a weapon? Does the growth happen Reflexively, is it a Simple Charm, or does it grow and shrink over the course of an hour?

                                Originally posted by JohnDoe244 View Post
                                why can't you use a powerbow mounted and does my table care?
                                I can answer that. Well not why does your table care, but the other thing. From a numbers standpoint archery and thrown weapons are strictly better as you increase in weight. You just get bonus damage and overwhelming and lose nothing. The only thing that balances that is tags. That’s why the heavy ranged weapons have bad tags like Slow, and the light ones have good tags like Mounted or Concealable. Not all weapons are going to be perfectly balanced, but at least that helps.

                                Powerbows standing 2 meters tall makes them awkward to use on horseback, but I don’t think there’s necessarily a big reason you couldn’t make a composite powerbow. It would be strictly better than a short powerbow but I don’t think it would be broken, although combining cavalry with archery is already extremely powerful.
