          In 3rd Edition, the higher ranks of the Immaculate Order, and those considered enlighted enough by the Order to be told, know that Solars and Lunars are Exalted. The idea that they're people possessed by demons who are unsaveable is a simplified version that they tell the masses to avoid confusing them. However, those who know this still believe the Solars and Lunars are Anathema, because "Anathema" doesn't mean "demon who possessed a human and glows gold or silver", it means "a person fundamentally opposed to the Immaculate Order's view of the Perfected Hierarchy, the proper order of the the universe". Solars and Lunars are Anathema by default, but others can be declared Anathema as well, depending on their nature and behavior - some Exigents, for example, are considered Anathema, but others aren't, if they're willing to support the Dragon-Bloods' social order. And Sidereals mostly aren't, though some Sidereals are trying to now tweak the scriptures to say that "beings with star motifs and Fate powers are Anathema if they support known Anathema like Solars", to get the Gold Faction Sidereals.

          Anyway, the Immaculate Order knows that Solars and Lunars are Exalted. Their position is that that doesn't matter - the power of the Sun and Moon are too great, and inevitably drives the holders to madness and destruction, so they still need to be put down.

                          I wrote an in-character spin on this topic, in the context of an NPC giving the Dragon-Blooded PCs a pep talk after becoming shikari:

                          Originally posted by Immaculate Abbot
                          Before the world when all was the formless Wyld, Gaia birthed the Elemental Dragons from shards of her own soul. They, in turn, built Creation by mixing the elements of Air, Wood, Fire, Water, and Earth. Once they completed their labors, Gaia bid the Dragons calm and spread to the corners of the world. And into that calmed world came Gaia's Grandchildren, the Terrestrial Exalted, the Dragon-Blooded.

                          For a time, all was peaceful. But as the years passed the gods of the sun and moon and stars grew jealous of the Dragons' perfection. They consorted with demons, trading their virtue for sorcerous secrets. In a mockery of the Second Breath, they unleashed spirits to possess the innocent among humanity, marking them as Anathema to the Immaculate Order as dangerous to let run free as Wyld Fae or the restless dead. Luckily, the Dragon-Blooded were and are spared the corruption of Anathemetic possession - only a lesser human soul is susceptible.

                          The ancestors of today's Dragon-Blooded pleaded with the gods to cage what they had wrought, but found no way to turn the sun and moon and stars from their path of dark emulation of the Dragon's grace. Instead, these Anathema subjugated the Dragon-Blooded with their strange ways and awesome powers. It was a dark time of tyranny and terror.

                          Then, the Immaculate Dragons graced Creation in Exalted form to lead the fight of liberation. A great war was fought and the Anathema were brought low by the valiant sacrifice of upright soldiers. Their names and deeds are not for us to recall - for it is the Anathema who seek to mark the world with such force that they achieve immortality through fame and legend. The upright soldier puts family before self, nation before ambition, legion before fang, Dragons before gods, and Creation above all. Their names are the first of their many sacrifices for the righteous rule of the Dragon-Blooded.

                          The Shogunate reigned in the Anathema's absence. Glorious Manses were erected. The land was healed from the sorcerous perversions of Cauldronists and demonic influence. The Dragon-Blooded host was fruitful and multiplied in Gaia's image. Art and culture reigned; it was a blessed time. But, it was not to last.

                          A sickness swept across the world that killed nearly everything in its path. No type of creature was spared - even the plants withered and died from the Contagion's touch. Heaven closed its gates in fear. The Dragon-Blooded never abandoned the Blessed Isle, but the defenses at the furthest reaches of the Threshold were stranded without resupply or reinforcements. In that time of weakness, the Fair Folk amassed and breached the reality engines that once ringed Creation. Weakened by sickness, separated by swaths of fresh death, many legions fell to the mad assault. The inner sea was brimming with behemoths, and the nobles that rode them took turns corrupting the sea and lands beyond with whatever whims took their fancy.

                          In that darkest hour, a young lieutenant led her Sworn Kinship into the Imperial Manse to find the trigger of the ancient Anathema weapon known as the Sword of Creation. Though none of her fellows survived the journey, the Scarlet Empress activated that most dangerous of devices, calling down lightning, coaxing forests to explode into splinters, rolling firestorms across the deserts, and summoning great waves to crash upon the shores. The Fae were slaughtered in as great of numbers as the Contagion took from the living. They were routed back beyond the edges of the world.

                          Since that time seven hundred and sixty eight years ago we have prospered under the Scarlet Empress' enlightened rule. We have had peace to cultivate our souls, to seek understanding of the Dragons and their Immaculate visitation, and to climb the Perfected Hierarchy when we perish. The Scarlet Empress is like the Dragons in that she has transcended death so that she might see the Realm rule for all eternity. We faithful Immaculates keep the Five Diligent Practices and study the Five Noble Insights. Your decision to join the Wyld Hunt to ride down Anathema speaks to your strength of will; you will need every scrap of it against the trials to come.

                                          On a tangent, one of the cool things about originally portraying Anathema as believed by Dragon-Blooded to be reincarnating wicked sorcerers who had effectively broken the karmic wheel of reincarnation was how it interacted with Immaculate beliefs; these were originally mortals who'd done the ultimate bad thing and given up their place in the cycle of merit and development for stolen power and stolen eternal life, yet succumbed to the inevitable stresses from too much power for a mortal soul. It also kind of dovetailed a bit more with the mild critique of ideas of cycles of reincarnation as presenting possibilities for structural social inequity. There's a lot of motivation in it for Dragon-Blooded Dynasts who are keen to maintain their social structure. (Bonus for also fitting with Past Lives memories - it's possibly easier for Immaculate following Solars to believe they don't remember an ancient incarnation who made deals with the devil than thinking that they are a demon and none of their ancestors memories feel like that?).

                                          Being possessed by demons is fine of course (whenever edition that idea shifted happened!) just found it interesting to think about above given the recent conversations about Immaculate Order and the Realm etc.

                                                      Originally posted by Posthumanity View Post
                                                      How the Dragon-Blooded think about the Sidereals? Do they revere them?
                                                      Most Dragon-Blooded are in the same position as the rest of Creation - they don't know the Sidereals exist. Again, the upper echelons of the Immaculate Order and Dynastic society in general are better-informed, though they probably still don't know everything about the Sidereals. The original writers of the Immaculate Texts, and Sidereal redactors over the centuries since then, have been careful to drop some hints about "starry-eyed wise people" being a thing that are around, and aren't automatically bad, so that if a Sidereal has to "go loud" during a Wyld Hunt or something, they have something to justify themselves.

                                                      Originally posted by Posthumanity View Post
                                                      I don't think the learned could delude themselves the demons are capable of stealing powers from the Sun and Moon.
                                                      I don't see why not. Remember, nobody in the setting has our omniscient perspective, where we're told the "true" power of the Unconquered Sun, Luna, and other Incarnae. People could believe they're less overwhelmingly-incredible than they are. They certainly don't tend to show off that power, being busy with their "retirement" to the Jade Pleasure Dome, and haven't done much for millennia.

                                                      Also, we actually don't know that it's impossible to steal power from the Sun or Moon. Supernatural beings and Exalts have pulled off thefts of power of that sort over the course of the history of the setting. We don't know if this will carry on into 3e, but in 2e, there was a mighty raksha lord, Laashe, who managed to trick the Unconquered Sun himself, and would have made himself into a power greater than the Sun if Luna hadn't managed to sneak up on him and trick him.

                                                      Originally posted by Posthumanity
                                                      And why they contrive to steal Celestial powers, when could simply bestow their own diabolical gifts to innocent humans?
                                                      The Immaculates are working on the assumption that the demons in question are trying to corrupt people and pull them away from the Perfected Hierarchy - not just the people they provide power to directly, but also others. Providing obviously demonic gifts wouldn't work for that: the average peasant, if she saw someone with horrible claws, fangs, burning with green alien fire, etc., isn't likely to worship or follow them, she's likely to run away. Whereas glowing with the light of the sun or moon, familiar celestial bodies, is familiar, comforting, even. And while the Solars and Lunars exercising their powers can be terrifying, they're also terrifying in a human way (or, sometimes in the Lunars' case, terrifying in a way that's familiar as a common animal). Thus, the Immaculate Philosophy claims, the demons trick more humans into following the Solars and Lunars, pulling them away from the Perfected Hierarchy, and corrupting even more souls.
