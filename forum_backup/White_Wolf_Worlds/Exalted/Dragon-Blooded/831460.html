    ​All aboard the Dragon-Blooded Hype Train!

    02-16-2016, 11:43 PM
    So the Dragon-Blooded book is being drafted up and considering that the core is really good I am excited. As a person who never actually got to play exalted but twice (both solars) in second edition I never got to experience the joys and wonders of the Dragon Blooded. However I always thought that they had a lot of cool things going for them and now that I have a gaming group more interested in exalted games I look forward to the chance to actually get to play one.

    So what do you all think is going to be in the book? What are you hoping to see in the Dragon-Blooded book?

    It is going to be interesting to see the Dragon Blooded book because it is going to give us a clear idea of how the mechanics look from a different angle both from power and theme. Things I am hoping for include: New Spells (pretty much a given), the elemental styles, an idea of how evocations work for other exalts, and additional antagonists as well as more demons and elementals for summoners. Something I would really like is some focus on other Dragon blooded other than the Dynasts and Lookshy. I know they will make up the bulk of the book but I would be delighted for more information on Prasad, forest witches, the blood-bats, and some new groups.

                                      I'd like the Dragon Blooded charms to be somewhat what they alluded to in the kickstarter sample.

                                      House Magic
                                      The point of saying certain Charms come from certain households has appeal to me. It almost feels a bit Legend of the 5 Rings where each of the Clans (7? 9? can't recall right now) each were thematically different but still had plenty of common ground. It also would lend a degree of history to a character which I have found players do like a lot in Exalted - knowing where their character has come from really makes their character pop from 2D cardboard into 3D fleshed out characters.

                                      From that I could see:
                                      - Iselsi playing the role of the "Scorpion"/ League of Shadows with an assortment of Stealth and Awareness techniques
                                      - Sesus playing the role of "Theives guild" with some tasty Larceny tricks
                                      - Tepet playing the roles of the "Lion" in devoting themselves to Warfare and squad tactics
                                      - Cathak being performers, presenters, and master duelists to promote their "Heroes"
                                      - Peleps/ V'Neef being the Sail masters/ Pirates

                                      And so on.


                                      Elemental Effects
                                      Alternatively, seeing how they did Martial Arts with Terrestrial and Celestial levels, I could imagine they could do something similiar here (e.g.: certain Charms gain a boost if you are the Aspect type of that Charm's mechanic - so, as an example, any Dragon can learn Melee Charms to swing a sword at superhuman degrees but Firebloods will be able to add extra damage to their blade as it ignites the air).

                                      This could make for some interesting Charm divides too. Athletics (Jumping) would likely benefit from being Air-Aspected, while Athletics (Strength) may benefit from being Earth Aspected.

                                      I could see even a terrestrial equivilent (e.g.: Dragon Dodge charms are standard, but suffer a minor penalty if the practicioner is Earth or Wood blooded to suggest the etheral effect of Air, Water, and Flame).

                                      Personally I prefer this second path to the first, as it lends similar to the Solars (e.g.: Firebloods and Dawn are Melee Masters, Airbloods and Zenith are Beurocratic savants, Woodbloods and Twilights are prodigal doctors, etc...) but that's just me.



                                      In the end I'll be satisfied if they make sure the Legacy of a Dragonblood to their family (or even lack of one in the case of a Lost Egg) plays a big part in their design. If the Solars are Sun Incarnate, the Abyssals are Death Incarnate, the Infernals are Hell Incarnate, etc... then the Dragons should be Legacy Incarnate. Family and ancestoral history should play a notable role in their theme because they differ from all other Exalts in their creation (aka they are bred; not so much chosen).

Dismissive comments should be equally dismissed as attempts to grab attention. Focus only on contributions that help you grow and develop, and ignore jealous efforts to stunt your creative sparks.

                                                  Well DBs were one of my favourite splats in second edition because they were a lot easier to tone back and not run absolutely roughshod all over everything else in the setting. It was pretty cool to actually have to work together to overcome challenges that weren't battles with yozi. On the flip side though, third edition had been doing a pretty good job of making Solars feel like that, and I think it's going to be really jarring to go to DBs after that.

                                                  Theyre not getting supernal or anything like supernal, obviously, they might not get solar XP, and if it's like second Ed they'll get less attributes and charms with higher XP and BP costs and a slower essence advancement track. Combo that with smaller mote pools, dice caps, less efficient and weaker charms....it might suck.

                                                  I'm glad they don't get supernal, I'm not sure they should have a solar-XP alike because solar XP was to solve a problem that DBs never had. Less attributes and charms, and slower essence progression, okay, but I think maybe it should stop there. Adding extra XP and BP costs onto that feels excessive. Playing a DB is supposed to feel kind of weak and restrictive, it's why every aspect has their role and really strong incentives not to stray from that role, but it can be a bit much.

                                                  Also super doubtful there's going to be new antagonists and demons and spells and stuff, those usually show up in their own books. The Realm setting book will probably have a few though.


                                                  What I'm excited to see are the changes they're making to the houses though. Those sound pretty cool. I'm also mega curious about how they're going to do the dragon styles and if they have the mastery keyword or not. There's really good reasons to have it and also good ones to not.

                                                  Edit: also I'm with kejak, even the players in my group who didn't like Solars before love them now, so I'm betting strongly on DBs being more antagonists than players, and I really want strong teamwork tactics since being the ST lets me essentially craft whole sworn brotherhoods as units.
