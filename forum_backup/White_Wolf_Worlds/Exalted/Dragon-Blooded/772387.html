                              Originally posted by webkilla View Post
                              A minimum requirement for exaltation is that you are - in some way - heroic...

                              but DBs exalt by virtue of their blood, not the fickle and random nature of a heavenly soul-shard.


                              ...so can a non-heroic DB happen? Like, someone with no ambition, who's just a boring nobody? (for NPC purposes...)
                              In the context of Exalted 'heroic' doesn't necessarily mean brave anymore than it means you're a morally upright citizen or even just vaguely safe to be near. It's using heroic in something like the classic Greek sense, where the hero is an impressive or unusual individual but has one or more tragic flaws to their character. They perform mighty deeds and are larger than life, but they aren't necessarily good or courageous. Odysseus is a hero and he's (depending on your POV) either pragmatic or a coward and a violent jerk on top of it.

                              For the purposes of Exaltation, a Dragon Blooded noble who is a cringing coward that prefers to skulk and wheedle and poison his way to greatness is every bit the hero he needs to be. He may not stride forcefully into the audience chamber and hurl the desk of his rival aside with contemptuous might and challenge him to single combat, but the same urge can drive him, the need to crush his foes, see them driven before him and hear the lamentations of their women. He'd just prefer to see it done from a safe distance, ideally by a patsy who has no idea that someone has been pulling the strings.

                              When it comes to Exalted, Yzma is just as good as Conan.

                                                  There's a couple positions I've seen on this.

                                                  On the one hand, there's the position that, no, the Dragon blood flowing in their veisn ensures heroism and passion of sorts, and this is fine because sometimes a person is born great and sometimes they become great (that's life for ya), and really it's cooler if all Dragonblooded are heroes. I think this was the Holden position in arguments on this, although I may be mangling his true position, so don't place too much weight on that.

                                                  On the other, there's the position that it's cooler if the Dragonblooded are in a sense capable of being undeserving, or more charitably, capable of being *only* everymen who may not even become better than they are (yet still struggle in a tough world nonetheless), and that the game is better if you can have characters who are like Regent Fokuf (at the extremes) and even then are still Dragonblooded. I think this was the StephenLs position (mangling, weight, etc.).

                                                  I think they both have their charms.
