        Originally posted by Chausse View Post
        Is mote saving for rolls really worth it for craft ? I can't remember any time it was useful to track motes for crafting rolls
        Probably not for artifacts in most cases, where you're likely to be doing them mostly in downtime, and to have reasonably significant time periods between rolls, to build up craft XP and such. But they can be quite handy for mundane projects, when completing them on an adventure timescale is both more likely and more important. If you want to reinforce a fortress gate before the besiegers assault it, it's nice to have motes left over for the fight afterwards.

        And even artifacts can potentially benefit - if you've banked enough craft xp, there's no technical reason you can't just roll every single crafting roll one after the other, virtually instantaneously. If you're trying to finish the daiklave made from the bone of his corpse that you've just found in his tomb, while actually fighting the First and Forsaken Lion, saving those motes could literally save your life.

                Originally posted by Exthalion View Post

                There is a minimum duration between rolls. Even Solar charms only push it down to weeks.

                Actually, this is not the case! It's definitely unusual compared to most extended rolls, but the craft rules are actually pretty clear - "artifacts require a minimum time spent laboring over their construction before a character can attempt to finish them", but as a one-time thing only. "The roll has no set interval; each time the character pays 10 gold points to attempt to complete the project, she can roll once, and may attempt her next roll once she can pay another 10 gold points to try again." (emphasis mine, all from "Finishing a superior project", Exalted p. 241).
