                    So looking at Sky-Calming Draw there are two distinct effects:

                    Spend a round aiming and ignore environmental penalties to the shot

                    Spend a round aiming and extend the range of a shot by one range band (max long)

                    the first one is basically the first half of There is no Wind which is an Archery 5, Essence 2 power for solars. It doesn't have the same "accuraccy bonus is the same as if she had shot at short range" stuff though so that might be why TinW is so much higher requirements. The Solar version does not require an aim, but nor does it relieve the requirement for an aim past short range.

                    The second one is utterly useless for any weapon except the hand crossbow. The hand crossbow is literally the only thing that qualifies as an archery medium range weapon. You can't apply this to flame pieces because the flame tag explicitly forbids it without a callout. There is no call out for flame weapons. You can't use it on thrown weapons because it's the wrong ability. Literally every other archery weapon has long range and would take an aim action to fire at that range anyway.

                    Then there's air aura: You don't need to take the aim action beforehand. Now it seems that most people are interpreting this as the two effects becoming:

                    ignore environmental penalties to the shot.
                    extend the range of a shot by one range band (max long)

                    The first one is nice because you can make shots at any range regardless of weather. Nice
                    The second one is either really, really good or really, really shit. Either it means you can make medium/or perhaps even long range shots reflexively or it only applies to a single, light weapon in the core and nothing else that we've got stats for.
