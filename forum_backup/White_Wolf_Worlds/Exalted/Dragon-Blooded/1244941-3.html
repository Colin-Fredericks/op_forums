        This is a good thread, and I really liked reading all of y'all's opinions. I have, however, a radical thesis:

        Sol and Luna are the bad guys. The Yozi are the good guys. The Exalted are rearranging deck chairs in the S.S. Titanic which is also parked in Stalingrad.

        Sorcerous Overlord, you daft fool! The Yozi are eviiiiiil! Perhaps, perhaps, but let's look at the facts. The Yozi are a collection of some few dozen eldritch abominations, which are places and concepts made real, that came from the "everything and nothing LSD trip" Chaos. They decided that this was tiresome, and so they made a reality of their own. Then, they made some beings to regulate Creation, and some even lesser beings to furnish them with adoration. Things managed themselves and two especially well made beings protected Creation from things that popped out of the Wyld and even held at bay from rival Yozi who wanted to break the snowglobe. Tired from their labor, they constructed the Games for themselves and everything went on safe and secure.

        The first beings did not like this arrangement, because they had to work and not play. so they wanted to kill their progenitors and steal the games like a bunch of traitorous jerks But the Yozi made them literally incapable of doing that, so they decided they'd toss some god killing super power into the lesser creations, and then arm them against their parent Yozi.

        These lesser beings got various flavors of god killing juice, and they killed a bunch of the Yozi, whose only crime was leaving Creation to perpetually run itself with 0% apocalypse chance. Half got killed, half got trapped in the inside out surgery horror body of their king. The First Beings took the games, the Yozi lead an existence of pure suffering, and the Least Beings moved up sorta into what the First Beings were supposed to do. Then the First Beings got lazy and ignored how bad the God Killers were, and just washed their hands of it.

        Then the weaker flavour god killers killed the stronger ones and locked them in a prison, and around this time it turns out that everyone forgot they were supposed to defend their snowglobe from the snowglobe eating, brick wielding monsters and a lot of Very Bad Globe Smashing happened. Admittedly, some of this snowglobe smashing happened from the Yozi who tried to take their ball and go home, but it was their ball that they made in the first place.

        Now the Weaker God Killer's Queen is missing and the Stronger God Killers are back and they are arguing about which of them is the better of the Lesser Beings. The gods still sit at the games faffing about, and the Snowglobe makers still suffer in eternity, but maybe some of them are trying to fix that.

        So Creation has had 2~5ish small apocalypses (which is a number more than 0), countless mortals have died needlessly (and the system they use to come back to life was broken too), and only fragments of a broken snowglobe remain.

        All because a few upstart gods wanted to Relax and Have Fun.

                                                            The problem with an argument over the Primordial War is that it's impossible to evaluate.

                                                            I mean, quite literally so in 1st and 2nd edition. It's explicit canon that nobody in the setting knows what was lost when She Who Lives In Her Name's sphere-cracking exercise went through. So what could have been obliterated were terrible horrors that made human life utter misery by later standards . . . or great blessings against which even the beneficiaries of the highest wonders of the First Age were living in wretched misery by pre-War standards. It's canon that you can't actually compare conditions before and after the Primordial War, so you can't actually evaluate whether it was better or worse.

                                                            In 3rd edition, on the other hand, we have so little canon at all on anything that happened so long ago, as a matter of developer policy, that it's similarly impossible to compare conditions before and after. (Go ahead and try, giving cites to the actual page numbers in 3rd edition books where you get you data on conditions in the Time Before or First Age.)
