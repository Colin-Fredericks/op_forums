    4 funerals and a DB wedding – How to keep the players involved?

    05-15-2018, 10:45 AM

    How would you keep players involved when their characters do not have much agency, yet?

    But first some background:
    Crashing Wave is an old dragon blooded. Finally, his son is getting married. Now he must throw a party. All kinds of religious ceremonies must be done on auspicious moments, political alliances affirmed, new ones made, gifts must selected. And most importantly: who must be on which guest list for which event?
    Events must meet up to expectations. The common people must also get their own events.

    Lord Crashing Wave has a territory with one colossal capital, a seaport on the Dreaming Sea in the East. (His empire is the size of ancient China with similar geography and population size.) It is wedged between two ocean facing satrapies of the Realm and the Eastern borderlands and high mountains.
    Lord Crashing Wave fought beside the Scarlett Empress when she was young and not the empress. Secretly he considers her to be his equal in status. Pragmatically, he cooperates with the Realm. His son (the groom) is at this moment on a ship returning home after having led a naval campaign in joint-forces with the Empire against forces of the South.

    The bride is a ‘lost-egg’. The romantic story of how they met involves storms, ship wrecking and tropical beaches. She is a political outsider and does not bring influence or resources into the family. She has moved to the capital years ago in preparation of the wedding. She is envied by other DB.

    The ruler follows the teachings of Immaculate Order but is not very strict. He has no patients for the details.

    Lord Crashing Wave life has not been easy. He has buried four of his Exalted sons, one died under unclear circumstance while visiting the Blessed Ile for education, two died together while fighting a Wyld incursion, one simply did not wake up one morning. Two of his Exalted daughters still live. His has outlived his wives.

    He has some serious grudges against the military incompetent free-loading neighboring satrapies. He has contempt for House Isels for rebelling against their grandmother. The idea his own grand children would oppose him disgusts him. On all occasions he has favoured House Ragara over the Guild. He is on amiable footing with House Peleps. Whenever the Realm was occupied with events, Lord Crashing Wave kept his piece of Existence safe.

    In his prime he was a one-man apocalypse. Now, he is in his 8th century and his age starts to bear heavy on him. He has become even more paranoid for the safety of his last remaining son. He desperately needs alliances to ensure the stability of his empire for the next generation.
    The wedding and its preparations are to be the kick-off for a new story with new PC characters.
    To me Exalted is Greek heros who are doomed to fail in an Oriental setting. But the ride to failure should be spectacular and glorious.

    Open questions:
    • How would a DB wedding ceremony led by the Immaculate Order look like?
    • What kind of security measures would there be given unrestricted funding?
    • How to handle guest of different importance: DB, unexalted family of DB, rulers of minor cities, clergy, merchants, commoners?
    • How and what would the offerings to the ancestors be like?
    • Which ‘tools’ are there to cement alliance?
    • How does spending all this money and extra people in the city affect the city and the commoners?
    • What kind of social disasters could happen between the guests?

    The players can choose between Solar and Terrestrial exaltation. Likely, they are one of the multitude of unexalted grand-grand-grand-grand children of Crashing Wave. The odds of exalting are very much against them. They are young, relatively unimportant and do not have control over their own fate yet.
    This leads me to the important question:
    • How would you keep players involved when their characters do not have much agency, yet?

              Fill the situation with conflicts based on the pcs' Intimacies. All that nice detail in your pitch, populate it with npcs - npcs in conflict with each other. Crashing Wave is an interesting character - put some more meat on his bones. Detail the Lost Egg bride, the surviving son, the four dead ones, the most important members of each family, the ministers and monks... not all of them, just enough to create some interesting subplots and social dynamics. Maybe some act as groups - think about what their goals are, their resources, and their means. You don't have to stat anyone for now, and when the players do chargen be open to details and npcs they invent, find places and stories for them, but also, when a player doesn't know who their mentor could be, where they could have been born, who their childhood friends are, suggest some of the npcs you've written about in your prep.

              Even if they start off as mortals they will definitely be able to handle very tough situations, like fighting small armies for a combat-oriented character, or seducing a minor deity for a social one (heroic mortals are often at the apex of mortal competence). So, what the DBs in the service of Crashing Wave don't deem important enough to send actual exalts to solve, there the pcs will certainly come in - they will probably be the most skilled mortals at Crashing Wave's court, or among them.
              • DB wedding ceremony... something like a Chinese traditional wedding? Also considering Crashing Wave's empire is reminiscent of Imperial China.
              • Security measures... unlimited funding? I'd say the Wyld Hunt in case Anathema show up. A lot of funding: preventing assassination, food tasting, soldiers ready to tame rebellions.
              • DB guests will receive marvelous gifts (artifact level perhaps), will be announced, will have an incredible banquet with the rarest meats and spices prepared with sublime sauces, guest palaces - they should be treated like gods. Their families rank probably a little lower - gifts of small wonders, a separate banquet any mortal would still be in awe of, resting in the same palaces of their DB relatives - very very honored. Mortal rulers just a bit less, for instance exceptional quality weapons and jewels, a very very rich banquet, large houses to rest in. Merchants, depends on their status, but could go as high as the treatment for mortal rulers. Mortal clergy, being Immaculates, would only have very spartan accomodations, no real luxury, but a lot of room for training, beautiful meditation halls, simple but clean and comfortable cells to sleep in, simple but nutrient foodstuffs of good quality - mostly vegetables, above all for the highest ranking. Peasants... are peasants even allowed at the marriage? If so I expect they'll be in a separate space, very large but also very simple, with looong tables and a lot of good food - plus whatever the higher ups don't eat, probably no gifts. Also, entertainment - martial displays between powerful DBs and sorcerous entertainment for the DBS and their families, plus theater, music, tourneys of martial arts, fireworks and poetry, and popular versions thereof for the peasants.
              • The offerings to the ancestors would probably be similar to the gifts given to the living - but iirc the Immaculate Faith doesn't really practice ancestral worship... can someone correct me on this? Maybe only the Immaculate monks can offer gifts to the ancestors?
              • Tools to cement an alliance... fostering another clan's children, exchange of valuable gifts, preparing trade routes, building roads from one territory to another, financial help, engagements of course... anything else?
              • The city will be packed. It will be like the Olympics - lots of tourists, lots of money to be made, lots of festive decorations... there will be probably little room to move in the streets - a lot of merchants will converge on the capital to profit from the huge influx of people.
              • Social disasters? Anything you can dream of - challenges and duels between rivals and enemies, illicit relationships, social faux pas involving bad etiquette, poisoned chalices (even with food tasters poison seems to always find a way in - this will be terrible for Crashing Wave, it's his responsibility, and he will dispatch the finest DB healers he can - and then turn all of his resources to uncovering the murderer(s) and kill them)... someone will insult the wife because they're envious of her and she's a "country bumpkin" Lost Egg... people getting drunk and stupid... athletes getting actually wounded... demons breaking their bindings... sky's the limit. But don't push too far, 1) it shouldn't be the worst wedding in the history of the empire, and 2) leave some plot threads for the rest of the campaign!

                  Thanks for the feedback!
                  Wise Old Guru, will steal Maleficent
                  Isator Levi, I intend to have social conventions to be similar to the Realm. But a bit less strict on ancestor worshipping.
                  Roswynn, thanks for the details, some times it is difficult to think big enough for me. Scale is sometimes my problem. Not an ambush by bandits, but an unexpected encounter with a scout party of a clan of hostile horse riders.

                  In retrospective I think Crushing Wave would have been a better name then Crashing Wave. As crushing is more active and things that crash are on the receiving end of the impact.

                  The feedback made me realize that the players should not be too young, too ignorant and too powerless characters. Indeed, they should already have a purpose/duty and connections to the political shakers and movers (and thus motivations) before the wedding starts.

                  New time line: the invasion by the Wyld creatures and malign energies happend 5 years ago. The two exalted sons died and most of the military and the other princes of the earth were slain, mutated or just vanished.

                  This means this part of Creation lost the bulk of its protectors. Now extra-ordinary mortals have to fill the gaps left by the killed DB.
                  A possible mission for the PCs would be that they have to damage control in the more rural areas of the territory where war refugees are, on the brink of famine and after affects of the Wyld run free.

                  I think they could get started as House inspectors, sort of tax collectors & recruits collectors and eyes and ears for one of the Exalted grand children of Crushing Wave. At the same time they can deliver wedding invitations and spy on the invited guests.

                  I could take up the idea of inviting sort of 'Evil' guest. Too powerful to insult, especially now the military suffered a major set back.
                  If the Wyld energies were huge then dragon lines (ley lines) could be slightly shifted, affecting manses and hearthstones and potentially any remaining first age artefact which function on hearthstone.

                  Then it is time to report to their patron and they travel to the city. A city crowed with guests and refugees.

                  That would already take multiple sessions of play.
