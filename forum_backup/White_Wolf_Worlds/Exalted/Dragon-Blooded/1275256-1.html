                                    12-20-2018, 04:03 PM
                                    Have been taking a break from holiday family nonsense to go through this like I did for the Core way back when. First several sections of proofreading follows, but there will be more to come!

                                    Pre-Introduction
                                    1: No space between the colon and the name Adam in the second playtesters group
                                    1: In the legal notice, there is a missing period between “copyright concerned” and “This book uses”
                                    3: In the Table of Contents, Houses Cathak, Peleps, Tepet, and V’neef lack the same Social Standing subheading as the other Houses get (save Iselsi, but… Iselsi.) See Chapter Two proofreading notes for further details.
                                    3: In the Table of Contents, the House subheadings are inconsistent regarding the name of one section. Houses Cathak, Ledaal, Mnemon, Peleps, Ragara, Tepet, and V’neef have a section called “Enemies and Alliances.” Houses Cynis, Sesus, and Iselsi instead have a section called “Allies and Enemies.” House Nellens has “Enemies & Alliances.” This is reflected in the Chapter Two appearances of these sections.
                                    3: In the Table of Contents, the House sobriquets exhibit inconsistency in whether or not auxiliary words are capitalized. House Cynis’s capitalizes “On” and “The,” and House Sesus’s also capitalizes “The,” but Houses Cathak, Ledaal, Mnemon, Nellens, Peleps, Tepet, V’neef, and Iselsi do not. Furthermore, all instances of “That” are capitalized in sobriquets, save for the one appearing in Ledaal’s. This is reflected in the Chapter Two appearances of these sobriquets.

                                    Introduction
                                    11, 12: Small inconsistency in the term Dynast. It is defined as a Dragon-Blood belonging to a Great House, but the definition of primary school uses the term for the unExalted children of Great Houses who attend alongside patricians (and, later in the text, it’s used for un-Exalted members of the Houses as well; cf p. 51)

                                    Chapter One
                                    18: There is a reference to procreative essence; it seems like the book mostly refers to this setting concept as “progenitive potential” instead in later chapters.
                                    24: In the sentence beginning “Lookshy’s gentes believe,” the comma before the word “and” changes the meaning of the sentence: the subclause beginning “restore the Shogunate’s” has as its intended subject “a worthy Shogun,” but by putting the comma before “and” the sentence structure implies the subject to instead be “Lookshy’s gentes”. (Compare the previous sentence, which has similar structure, but in that case both verbs have as their subject “The Seventh Legion”.)
                                    24: The tag “Play a member” is in normal-weight font, while the Scarlet Dynasty’s, Empire of Prasads, and Outcastes’ tags are all in boldface.
                                    28: The tag “Play a member” is in normal-weight font, while the Scarlet Dynasty’s, Empire of Prasads, and Outcastes’ tags are all in boldface.

                                    Chapter Two
                                    35: The House Symbology sidebar renders House V’neef as Vineef.
                                    36: The Social Standing heading here uses a certain font design, but in other Houses’ sections, Social Standing uses the same font design as e.g. House Economies (compare p.39). Probably related to the proofreading note from page 3.
                                    36: “and invited some of the Dynasty’s most notorious gossips and rumormongers were present.” Looks like an editing artifact; either drop “were present” or change “invited” to “ensured”.
                                    37: “The legion, the dragon, the scale: Each of these” has the first word capitalized after a colon, which is not done elsewhere (compare pp 17, 18, 22, 24).
                                    40: “on the activities Dragon-Blooded great and small” missing “of” between “activities” and “Dragon-Blooded”
                                    41: “Of course, House Cynis, watches, reports, and records.” There shouldn’t be a comma after “Cynis”.
                                    52: The first sentence under Enemies & Alliances has the first word capitalized after a colon, which is not done elsewhere (though in this case it introduces an aphorism which is a complete sentence, so that may be a deliberate choice).
                                    55: Peleps lacks a Social Standing section.
                                    66: Tepet lacks a Social Standing section.
                                    69-70: V’neef lacks a Social Standing section.
                                    Last edited by Aretii; 12-20-2018, 04:06 PM.
