    Dragonblooded charms 3e, Or: I'm not sure how this'll work

    01-31-2014, 12:01 PM
    Hello ladies and gentlemen,

    I was rereading the kickstarter preview on 3e Dragonblooded, because I told a friend of mine about Exalted, he seemed very interested in it and particularly in the Dragonblooded (he's a bit A:tLA fan) and he was curious what they'd be like in Third editions.
    Now, when I was reading it through, I started wondering, how would you do Elemental Linguistic Charms, Elemental Resistance Charms or Elemental War Charms, and still make the different elements feel distinct?
    So... does anyone have any answers to that? Any examples maybe? Maybe just some ideas you have for the new Dragonblooded

    Thank you very much,
    WMG

      Well, you can look at the existing examples for inspiration. They only cover one element each, but they tend to have a strong flavour of that element -- Wind-Carried Words is definitely an Air charm, for instance, and not just because of the name. A lot of them are going to be that sort of thing -- DB elemental expressions were never as direct and literal as Avatar's, and while I expect the "bending" style effects to be embellished somewhat for 3e, they're likely to remain a subset of what DBs can do.

              This is admittedly fairly old (pretty sure it predates the Dragonblooded preview), but this was the thinking at some point:

              Originally posted by What We Know Wiki
              Q: Exalted 3rd Dragon-Blooded: can you speak to whether or not players will be able to evoke Avatar: TLA with the system as written? (nicodemus144)

              A: Avatar evokes Dragon-Blooded. Just throwin' that out there. That said, I plan to introduce some more iconic elemental Charms to various abilities, and probably a new series of five (tentatively) DB-only martial arts. (John Mørke)
              In the past, if not on this specific topic, the devs have expressed a disinterest in "symmetry for the sake of symmetry." If some Abilities really aren't giving good grounds for a given element, they might not force Charms in there. If so, this will no doubt be done in such a way that all five elements have plenty of awesome things of their own in their respective toolboxes.

                                                  Off the top of my head, some alternate-aspect Linguistic Charm concepts:

                                                  Fire-aspected: Write a message that evokes unnatural emotions and passions in its readers. Create a meme that goes viral as swiftly as a forest fire. Create a message that consumes itself once read and acted on, burning itself out of a messenger's memory.

                                                  Earth-aspected: Ensure that a meme survives intact, without changing, no matter how it's transmitted. Create an enduring story that will last generations. Write a story that puts anyone who reads it into a coma.

                                                  Water-aspected: Write a message that reads differently to different people. Corrupt an existing message into uselessness or coopt it for yourself. Write a story that's always in the reader's native tongue.

                                                  Wood-aspected: Write a text that learns and grows, letting you argue your points from afar. Create a manual that updates itself as new techniques are discovered and old ones become obselete. Create characters that really come to life.
