    03-27-2015, 08:47 AM

    My local group would like to try an all Dragon Blooded game that participate in the Wyld Hunt. I am a newish ST to the Exalted 2E system and I am wondering what kind of things would you all or suggest I have my players take / have access to so they don't just die in the first real battle against an average solar?

      A full Sworn Brotherhood, appropriately supported, should not die in their first battle against a lone equivalent-XP Solar assuming they're at all intelligent.

      Well, okay. At higher essence levels that becomes a lot less true. But even in 2e, the power arc from Essence 2 to 4 means that 4 to 5 Dragon-Blooded against a single Solar probably favors the Dragon-Blooded.

      Really, the best advice you can give them is highly generic. To the extent that Dragon-Blooded can do so, they need to fill out their combat competencies, grab surprise negators, flurry breakers, a solid 2/7 filter, stuff like that. They won't be able to plug all their holes like a Solar can, so they should try and leverage mote efficiency and play the mote-attrition game. They should take a close look at the synergy charms and at things like Defense-From-Anathema-Method.

      They should also heavily consider backgrounds that let them either have or easily recruit goons. Having goons can be a big deal, especially if the Solar doesn't have goons. Up until they become completely recockulous with their scene-long enhancements, a well-trained mortal has a nontrivial chance of either forcing a Solar to spend Essence or of actually hurting them. Every goon squad that forces a Solar to blow motes is one less mote aimed at your PCs.

      I don't know I'd give them more advice than that, though. The Wyld Hunt is necessarily risky and deadly. Lots of Dragon-Blooded have strapped on the ancestral blade and set sail for the Threshold, only for that ancestral blade to end up on the wall of some Lunar's stronghold somewhere.

      I'd also advise starting them with some newly-Exalted Lunars and Solars before moving to established ones.

                              Honestly the combat system can be pretty well balanced. But there's a few key solar charms that, especially together, make things really hard. I'm talking mostly about the lower essence stuff and things that aren't a million charms into a tree. It might be something to think about to give your players a fair challenge.

                              Offence:
                              Hungry Tiger-just one mote for a potention ass ton of damage.
                              Thunderbolt Attack Prana-bonus success on Attack is meh, doubling post soak damage can be especially bad when combined with...
                              One Weapon Two Bows- deal damage twice for a low low cost.
                              Heaven Thunder Hammer- if you get a good solid hit with this and send them into a nearby wall you'll probably double your damage.
                              Peony Blossom/Invincible Fury of the Dawn- especially combined with IAM these can be deadly.

                              Defence:
                              Fivefold Bulwark Stance- This shuts down a lot of ways to attack the solar, onslaught penalties, coordinated attacks, nothing lowers their DV
                              Element Resisting Prana- Flat out stops environmental effects from mattering
                              Adamant Skin Technique- if something gets through your DV, you can choose to activate this to perfect the damage

                              Both:
                              Infinite Ability Mastery- lets you full excellency every attack and defence for free, one of the few charms on this list that alone is a total game changer
                              Immanent Solar Glory- five purchases of this means an extra 50 motes, which means a huge gas tank to kill everything with

                              Any one of these is pretty fine on their own, but taken all together you can have an opponent with a permanent DV of above 14, dishing out a minimum of 16 damage even against creatures with 600 soak, and is basically a monster.


                              As for the DBs they have this kind of inteesting 5/7 filter because of how their perfect parry goes, but theres a lot more generic things to be getting first. Here's some tips

                              -for DBs Superheavy plate only costs 1.5 artifact dots. Get it.
                              -synergy is God for DBs, between resistance charms, thrown charms and war charms you can boost everyone's soak by an additional...lots
                              -Threshing the Floor Technique auto coordinates attacks AND makes them count as though they're the same flurry, 5FBS is the only thing that can stop it
                              -synergy is God for DBs, With One Mind lets everyone defend for everyone else, and boosts everyone's combat abilities

                              Other than that DB combat trees are retry shallow but pretty good, just focus on your aspects ability and bring LOTS of firedust.

                                          If they're hunting down just your average solar, then you probably don't need to do much. Depending on how many players you have, of course.

                                          If the Solar is not combat focused, then you can take him down with a single Dragon-Blooded.

                                          If the Solar is mildly Combat Focused (3 dots in applicable abilities), then one to three 'average' Dragon-Blooded could be required, and it'll be a tough fight. That's your usual DB having a daiklave, a couple of combat charms, and artifact armor, of course.

                                          If the solar is Combat Focused (4 and 5 dots in applicable abilities, 3+ Dots of Essence), then you're going to need 2-5 Combat Focused Dragon-Blooded to stand a reasonable chance, and even then, its going to be tough.

                                          (To note. This is assuming you are playing with the 2.5 errata. If not, then the free reflexives of the Dragon-Blooded make taking down a Solar infinitely easier.)

                                                      How you approach combat with Dragon-Bloods vs Solars really depends on Essence levels. If the Solar is E2, Dragon-Bloods can leverage their more efficient excellencies; they just need to create defences capable of overcoming the Solar's combos and then slug out the combat until their target lacks the motes to effectively defend himself. Things get harder at higher Essence as the power gap grows. DBs pick up some neat tricks at E4-5, but nothing as good as Solars do.

                                                      Don't pin your hopes on coordinated attacks to press your numerical advantage; Solar defensive Charms can efficiently eliminate DV penalties. Similarly, don't rely on the Immaculate Shaping or Crippling attacks, Solars have easy access to keyword defence Charms that can negate them.

                                                      Offensively, cooperative elemental bolt is an excellent choice, nothing else DBs have access to can rival its accuracy pool. Have a Thrown specialist use Loyal Weapon so he can reuse the group bolt repeatedly during combat.

                                                      A few defensive tips in no particular order:

                                                      1) With One Mind lets the party reflexively Defend Other, invaluable for having specialized defenders guarding more vulnerable allies.
                                                      2) Multiple iterations of (Element) Protection Form can be activated simultaneously, boosting soak to ridiculous levels. It's not efficient and it's not seamless, but if you lack a true perfect defence it might give you the edge you need to survive that Rising Sun Slash doomcombo.
                                                      3) You will want Defence From Anathema Method, and no excuses. No longer will you have to bleed motes to max your DVs. Its prerequisites are also very good for negating Solar influence Charms.
                                                      4) Element Armor Technique in Thrown and Elemental Defence Technique in Dodge both offer persistent External Penalty defences that even the playing field against Solar attack dice pools.
                                                      5) Conflagration of Warding can overcome even perfect attacks provided it is activated by two Dragon-Bloods cooperatively.
                                                      6) When you hit Essence 5, the Shield function of Wrath Directing Gesture is by far the most powerful defence Dragon-Bloods have access to.

                                                          My advice: Remember that NPCs don't need xp, and they don't need the Charms listed in their own splat book! You can make an NPC however you wish. Those Solars or Lunars they'll be opposing? Give them great strengths, but more importantly give them great flaws. Make them people, with blind spots and vices and ragged emotions. Don't be afraid to make custom Charms for these Anathema; don't worry about balance, since the Dragon-Blooded won't be able to get them anyway! Crazy shit with glaring flaws! A Solar that rain down holy fire as long as its daytime or a Lunars that can teleport to any place moonlight strikes. As long as it makes some sense, is interesting and fun and has cool clever ways to get around it (that aren't too hard for Dragon-Blooded to figure out) then go with it!

                                                          Imagine a good, interesting conflict, with many possible ways for your players to come at it, and give your Anathema interesting tools to exaggerate that conflict and bring it to greater levels.

                                                          With all that in mind, there's nothing that the Dragon-Blooded would "need" to take to survive, since there is no "average" NPC Solar. Keep in mind the place Solars and Lunars play in a Dragon-Blooded-centric game, where the Dragon-Blooded are the protagonists and the Anathema are the antagonists (at least at the start).
