    No doubt, some ask, "Why go to so much trouble?"


    I see something very uniquely risky and dangerous in this Charm that I don't see with other Aura-duration Charms. It is the only Aura-duration Charm I can find that makes you more vulnerable in any regard, and there are so many temptingly exciting Fire Signature Charms that can suddenly make all of that vulnerability and sacrifice pointless.

    The Charm seems so demanding, in fact, that I wonder just how much play-testing it received, and I hope that these breakdowns will encourage people to try Raging Fire-Dragon Spirit out and see how it performs.


    I tried making a Fire Aspect brawler, see, who used Raging Fire-Dragon Spirit like Solar Brawlers used Ascendant Battle Visage or Bloodthirsty Sword-Dance Spirit. Something that looked like a Solar Brawler, but on the Terrestrial level.

    IT DOESN'T WORK. Fire Essence just doesn't mix well with Terrestrial Brawl. If your concept for a super-mode doesn't look like Embracing the Violent Flow or Embrace of Deadly Thorns, you need to find another solution.

    Fortunately, someone showed me an alternative possible with Fire Dragon Style, but that still left me with the knowledge that Raging Fire-Dragon Spirit is terribly finicky, and the question of how to build around it so that I wouldn't shoot myself in the foot trying to use it.

                    Originally posted by BrilliantRain View Post

                    That said, I’m not sure that bonus strength dots count for everything, like you suggest.
                    As far as I’m aware the generally accepted rule of thumb is that ability dicepools calculated off strength treat that bonus dot as a dice added by charms, but for all other effects it’s deemed natural. So for example it won’t raise your capped (Strength + Athletics) pool to 18, but if a charm says it adds (Strength) bonus dice of damage, then it will add 6 instead of 5.

                    Numbers are assuming you’ve got max stats and all that.

                                        Vance clarifies:

                                        * rush bonus only applies to Athletics rolls; doesn’t help with mounted rushes

                                        * players can indeed discard bonus die to add automatic successes (or vice versa)

                                        * bonus Strength dot does not count as Strength for (Strength) calculated Charm effects

                                        * Stamina is only reduced to 0 for soak; ISoS still doubles nothing for nothing, but (Stamina) calculated effects like Fire-Elemental Protection Technique are not effected.


                                        These will be reflected in later edits.
