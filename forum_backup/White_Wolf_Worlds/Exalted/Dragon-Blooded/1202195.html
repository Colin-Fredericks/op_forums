    Trying to make a timeline in preparation for my DB game

    04-02-2018, 08:08 AM
    I've been running a Solar game that's taken place between RY 768 and RY 771. The idea is to re-roll DBs but continue in the same game canon. Now I'm trying to figure out how the civil war should have progressed in time for the new campaign. These are my thoughts so far, but I haven't delved much into Threshold and Anathema activities during this time. If anyone notices anything that doesn't make sense or has some cool ideas, I'm very interested in hearing it.


    RY 765: CHAOS
    • On orders from the Scarlet Empress, House Tepet sends their legions to fight the Bull of the North.
    • The Scarlet Empress disappears.
    • As the Realm reacts to the Empress' disappearance, the Tepet legions launch an attack on the Bull of the North and suffer a great defeat. They send messages to other houses to ask for help, but help is refused. After a devastating three month campaign, half a legion survived the war only to find that the other hosues had taken advantage of their defeat to get to their satrapies.
    • In the chaos, the different houses are claiming their own parts of the Realm legions. The remaining Tepet forces stay in Medo, only capable of securing the Red-Piss Legion. Their faraway satrapy Ithen remains independent from the Realm.

    RY 766: ASSETS AND ALLIANCES
    • As an insult masked as respect, the other houses try to calm the infuriated House Tepet by putting Tepet Fokuf (an idiot) on the Scarlet Throne. Knowing that with the remaining Tepet army over in Medo, Tepet Fokus and those close to him are at the sufferance of the Deliberative. The only one thinking they have any real power is Tepet Fokuf himself, while the Deliberative is mocking him behind his back. House Tepet knows that they are being insulted, but they stay quiet for now. Even though Tepet Fokus has no real power, the general populace doesn't understand the mockery and recognizes the Tepet name on the throne.
    • House Ledaal becomes increasingly more concerned about the rumors of Anathema appearing in greater numbers. They speak at the Deliberative of the importance of reclaiming Thorns. Though most houses agree that Thorns is troubling, they deem that the time is not right to launch that sort of campaign. The person who mentioned it was Ragara Banoba. House Ledaal finds this an insult.
    • House Ragara is escalating their efforts to call in favors from the houses who are indebted to them. Some houses, such as Mnemon and Ledaal, are withholding payments to Ragara, recognizing what he's doing as a long-term effort to seize the throne. Ragara Banoba, and other influential members of the house, are beginning to defame Mnemon and Ledaal at various Cynis banquets. Excited at the prospect of war against Ledaal and Mnemon, House Ragara is beginning to pull their strings. They are lenient on House Peleps, instead offering loans to help them finance the Imperial Navy, hoping to be able to call on that favor later. They aren't as lenient on V'neef, undercutting them in the name of securing debt.
    • House Cynis sees Ragara's efforts as troublesome, and their blackmailers try to navigate new business deals with Ragara to keep as much money as possible in their own pockets.

    RY 767: FLEXING AND SCHEMING
    • House Ledaal and House Mnemon come to an agreement to get the Immaculate Order to suggest that if Mnemon takes the throne, the Realm will be strong enough to fight back the Anathema threat. If it would come to blows though, Mnemon would have to promise to come to Ledaal's aid.
    • On a visit to the Immaculate Temple, Mnemon brings up what House Ledaal and her had been whispering about. The Immaculate Order says that they must remain neutral, but she hears from the hierarchs that they will support her should she claim it.
    • Tensions between House Mnemon, Ledaal, and Ragara are increasing, and Cathak Cainan steps in. Knowing that his great army will play a role in being Queenmaker, he makes a joke about how he'd be happy to throw in support for anyone capable of throwing Fokuf's stupid ass off the throne. Because of the strength of House Cathak's legions, he knows that this will interest the other houses and make them eager to bribe him for his support. He can pretend to stay neutral until his victory is secured.
    • House Sesus grows wary after Cathak Cainan's declaration. They increase their espionage efforts to ensure that they know every deal Cainan makes, especially with House Ragara. House Cynis, who is deeply associated with House Sesus, are worried about what Mnemon could do to their assets should she take the throne. They suggest to Sesus to try to play Ragara and Mnemon against each other.
    • V'neef becomes increasingly worried about Mnemon's actions, but she also expected them. She wants the throne for herself but recognizes that she's not in a position where she can make any claims for it. Instead she makes herself open for trade deals to earn favors. She openly speaks fondly about the Scarlet Empress and how she expects her return. She's made Eagle Prefecture a safe haven for the Empress' magistrates. She becomes popular among those who are the most devout to the Scarlet Empress.
    • Knowing that House Cathak is deeply indebted to House Ragara, Banoba assumed Cainan's support and becomes more confident in his rhethorics against Mnemon and Ledaal (which basically becomes rhethorics against Mnemon since she is the obvious claimant). At a banquet, Banoba manages to get Cainan's word that he has his official support should he lay claim to the throne. Cainan says this to get on Ragara's good side considering his massive debt, but he refrains from making any real investments towards Banoba's claim. Banoba makes sure that everyone knows this promise by gladly spreading the rumor on various banquets and in political meetings.
    • Perhaps as a slight to House Tepet, and to get them to be quiet when he eventually gets rid of Fokuf, Ragara Banoba begins to seize Tepet's last remaining satrapies claiming that it's part of their debts to House Ragara. The other houses let it happen. Medo becomes the only satrapy that continues to pay (and house) Tepet and their single legion. Cathak Cainan says, perhaps as an effort to emphasize his neutrality and that his association to Ragara is a mere rumor, that he is willing to aid House Tepet in securing their future shouold they be willing to accept. He argues that the Tepet name is honorable and that they should remain so. House Tepet sees it as a ploy, hate of House Cathak for not aiding them against the Bull of the North. They make no officialy replies.
    • House Tepet recognizes that they need to increase their standing on their own. They send diplomats to Yilali (a homebrew nation for my game bordering Medo) to try to convince them to favor trade deals with House Tepet compared to the other houses. The royals refuse the deal. House Tepet assassinates the royal family. Yilali becomes adamant in their efforts to blockade all Realm trade.
    • Fighting breaks out at the border between Medo and Gwynyfen (a homebrew province part of Yilali). The Red-Piss Legion takes part in the fighting, and manages to recruit and drill locals from Medo to increase their numbers. The conflict is followed by a seize fire after Yilali agrees to make Medo exempt from the trade blockade with the Realm---this greatly benefits House Tepet and allows them to keep Medo from being swallowed up by House Ragara.
    • Recognizing this move, House V'neef reinforced their alliance with House Tepet to be able to benefit from this exemption for their own gain. Tepet uses this opportunity to secure Ithen and open up connections between the two satrapies using V'neef's support.
    • House Iselsi make covert overtures to House Tepet, suggesting that they should help them break the Dynasty as vengeance for how they have been treated by the Great Houses. Tepet is happy with all the aid they can get, and they recognize Iselsi as a solid spy network. The Roseblack is especially interested in Iselsi's Vendetta, dreaming of a new Shogunate under her command. She recognizes that she has no claim, though, but her dream keeps her in close contact with Iselsi.

    RY 768: A SHOW OF MIGHT
    • Mnemon wants the Imperial Navy but has put off trying to convince House Peleps to back her claim for the throne. She knows that they will ask for the Merchant Fleet in return, something she is unwilling to give them. House Peleps has also largely been relying on House Ragara to finance the Imperial Navy, and they have been offered some fine trade deals with House Cynis. Mnemon recognizes House V'neef as a possible naval solution to her decisive claim, but she knows that V'neef herself would never accept her as Empress.
    • Seeing war on the horizon, Mnemon uses a "joint training exercise" as a cover to transfer a portion of her legions to Ledaal's territory. This is made in an effort to truly secure their alliance should she make the decisive move for the throne. She's hoping that Cathak will see the size of the legions and recognize her as the only legitimate contender.
    • House Nellens is worried. They have remained neutral in the politics for years now, but they feel the tides of battle. Ledaal's and Mnemon's alliances make their closeness to LEdaal worrisome, and Ragara is trying to use this by offering partnerships with them. Nellens know that they must recognize Ragara Banoba if they want their support, and that could very well be the slight that turns Mnemon and Ledaal's forces upon their shores. Instead, Nellens decided to reach out for the one house that no one respects---House Tepet, by offering lucrative deals to help secure their position. Since neither Ragara nor Mnemon see Tepet as a direct threat at the moment, this will give Nellens some security while retaining their neutrality.
    • Though having secured Medo, House Tepet's situation is worries as House Sesus have made some moves to seize more of Tepet's satrapies. In order to combat House Sesus' efforts (and despite House Sesus trying to actively sabotage this), House Tepet accepted the deal with House Nellens, knowing full well their intentions, but keeping politics formal and staying out of Mnemon's and Ragara's bids for the throne.
    • House Peleps approached Cathak Cainan on a banquet and whispered that they have been receiving offers from both Mnemon and Ragara and would like to know who Cathak backs. Peleps seems to suggest combining their forces with Cathak if there would ever be a war, but Cathak Cainan fears that it would unite the other houses against them.
    • Even though Cathak Cainan was wise enough to decline the alliance, the fact that their meeting occurred worried House Sesus. Having feared House Peleps for a long time, House Sesus reached out to House V'neef while House Cynis retained their trade deals with House Peleps. Hoping to secure individual alliances with both naval houses, the Sesus-Cynis agreement would hopefully ensure that they could benefit from whomever won the conflict.
    • More and more people starts mentioning V'neef as a solid contender for the throne. This infuriates Mnemon.

    RY 769: THE FIRST CLAIM
    • House Peleps reaches out to both House Ragara and House Mnemon, claiming to be backing either house should they work against V'neef's increasing influence. Ragara Banoba promises House Peleps that they will receive control over the Merchant Fleet should they back his claim to the throne. House Mnemon does not make that promise.
    • House Cathak is being pressured by the Immaculate Order (but really it's Mnemon behind the scenes) to cease their associations with Ragara Banoba. With Cathak Cainan's faith coming into conflict with his indebted honor, he begins to distance himself from House Ragara while listening to other alternatives.
    • Recognizing that if Cathak would side with Mnemon against a Ragara-Peleps alliance, Mnemon would gain a force totalling fifteen legions. This would be her decisive move to take the throne, and it could lead to devastating consequences for all other parties. Ragara Banoba is desperately anchoring Cathak Cainan by suggesting a joint training exercise similar to the one House Mnemon had the year before. Cathak Cainan reluctantly accepts.
    • Cynis Belar invites V'neef to a private party where she whispers that V'neef should be Empress. She suggests a Sesus-Cynis-V'neef alliance where they bribe House Tepet and House Nellens with resources and marriage in order to secure a strong faction. They would recognize V'neef as claimant. This would create a faction wealthy enough to stand against House Ragara, and with a military consisting of fifteen legions---just like a worst possible Mnemon scenario. V'neef expresses interest, but refuses to use any of her own scions to appease House Nellens.
    • The Cathak and Ragara forces join up with the Peleps legions for their joint training exercise. The Realm watches the massive forces, and Mnemon is infuriated. She holds a private meeting with House Peleps, promising them half the Merchant Fleet in exchange for their support. Peleps accepts and uses the distraction of the training exercise to move the Imperial Fleet to blockade the Imperial City. Mnemon and Ledaal move their forces towards the combined Cathak and Ragara forces, the Peleps forces stop following Ragara Banoba's orders, and Mnemon has Tepet Fokuf evicted from the throne and dragged whipped and naked through the streets before being publicly executed. She declares herself Scarlet Empress. The Immaculate Order officially recognizes her claim.
    • Cathak Cainan curses House Mnemon and House Peleps as he's dragged into a fight he does not want. Ignoring Ragara Banoba's orders, he retreats with his forces, drastically decreasing House Ragara's claim. Ragara Banoba flees for now---both houses refuse to recognize Mnemon's claim to the throne. House Tepet openly condemns Mnemon's actions as she publicly executed a Tepet in the Imperial City. House V'neef openly condemns Mnemon's actions as an affront to the Scarlet Empress.

    RY 770: THE CIVIL WAR
    • V'neef, having played on people's loyalty to the Scarlet Empress and their love for V'neef herself, has not openly declared that she wants to become Empress. But as Mnemon takes the throne, more and more people who feel betrayed by Mnemon gravitate towards V'neef.
    • Those who refuse to recognize Mnemon as the new Scarlet Empress refer to her as Amethyst Empress, or Amethyst Usurper.
    • Willing to accept Cynis Belar's earlier offer of an alliance, House Cynis is now officially backing out but hoping for Sesus to keep backing V'neef in their stead. Cynis determine that though they wish to see V'neef on the throne, any such alliances would now be too risky with Mnemon on the throne.
    • House Tepet has been friendly with V'neef for the benefit of their nautical influence, but they still blame Sesus for having actively sabotaged them. When words of a V'neef-Sesus alliance begins to spread, Tepet becomes reluctant to support V'neef and instead starts negotiating with House Ragara.
    • House Cynis begins to stroke Mnemon's back as a way to keep her attention away from them. Mnemon assumes that Cynis just wants to secure their own assets and is lenient on them in these sensitive times.
    • Ragara Banoba and V'neef meet at a Cynis banquet where both parties try to convince the other to join their cause. Both argue that they need to band together against Mnemon, but neither wants to relinquish their claim to the other. While Ragara claims to still have Cathak, V'neef can now boast with Sesus (knowing that they still have Cynis in their back pocket).
    • House Cathak has been guarding Ragara's interests still, but they are being openly defamed by the Immaculate Order. This infuriates Cathak Cainan, who criticizes the Mouth of Peace for the Order's lack of neutrality, and he convinces Ragara to reach out to House Tepet. Eager for further support, Ragara offers financial benefits to help Tepet secure more of their satrapies. House Tepet agrees which fractures their relationship with House Nellens. House Nellens answers by supporting V'neef, being promised scions in marriage as gratitude.
    • House Peleps loses a number of its admirals when Sesus and Iselsi both make a number of assassination attempts independent from each other. All blame is directed at the Sesus-V'neef alliance with results in several naval battles between Peleps and V'neef vessels. House Cynis try to spread false rumors about the Sesus attacks being orchestrated by Ragara Banoba. This fractures Mnemon's leniency for House Cynis and they make the decision to back out and officially join V'neef's alliance.
    • Later at Calibration, the Mouth of Peace is assassinated and the Immaculate Order is without a leader. Ragara Banoba rises up, backed by House Cathak, and House Tepet, claiming that the Immaculate Order was punished for corruption. He takes the title of Emperor of Ivory, arguing that Mnemon and the Immaculate Order decided to facture the Realm and that his self-proclaimed title is a consequence of their treachery. Though Cathak Cainan is a devout worshiper, he has decided to stick with Ragara since the Order began to defame him. People beging to address V'neef as the Emerald Empress, though she hasn't officially adopted the title.
