    How important are auspicious signs among the Dragon Blooded?

    12-07-2020, 10:36 PM
    I'm prepping to run a Dragon-Blooded campaign, and my players and I took a liking to the idea of them being part of a Cadet house that we are planning out for the game. The players are all siblings born at the same time. Basically a triplet group that all exalted. I worked up that their Mother, the house Matriarch, completed the pilgrimage of The Caul and the following pregnancy resulted in these children. I am already aware that the likelihood of all three exalting is next to impossible, but when It's the game we all want to play it doesn't matter how unlikely something is. I'm looking for second opinions on how significant this or other auspicious occurrences might reflect on the Dragon-Blooded as a whole?

      To put it mildly? It's next to impossible for more than one twin to become Exalted, and triplets magnifies that impossibility by a factor of three. If all three do in fact exalt, people aren't going to immediately assume that it's a sign from the dragons or something. They're going to be wondering "...Did the mother just adopt two other children that just so happened to exalt?" because that will be a more believable premise than what you've stated. There's also a chance they could think that something demonic is afoot, but considering that the Immaculate Order says that being Exalted as a Dragon-Blooded is the only "true" way to be Exalted, as it's a sign of spiritual purity, this is quite unlikely, otherwise it would taint the idea of Terrestrial superiority.

                  In regards to how people react, given that there seem to be a lot of different possibilities, I think a good question is "Which reactions would create interesting conflict, that the players are invested in"?

                  That's why I definitely think there should be negative reaction, with people arguing everything from the mundane "she slept with whichever man with very good breeding or who also went on a Caul pilgrimage it would be most politically inconvenient to be the father" to the absurd "she consorted with Anathema to steal Exaltations that rightfully should have went to spiritually pure Dynasts and instead foisted them on her children, who weren't ready for them, as demonstrated by [whatever trouble they've gotten themselves into]".

                  This is not necessarily because they find the triple Exaltation unbelievable, but because other people believe that it is an auspicious sign, meaning that the PCs would be good candidates for positions of responsibility, or at least a sign of good breeding, meaning that the PCs would be good candidates for a marriage above their station, and people competing for the same positions would have reason to come up with dark rumors and other counterarguments.

                  An interesting question is what the PC's mother should think. I feel like "their mother thinks they would be good candidates for an advantageous marriage, but not necessarily for a dangerous mission" is a good setup for classic stories about avoiding parental pressure for a political marriage and sneaking off to go on dangerous adventures, while "their mother thinks that their auspicious birth implies they would be well-suited to, e.g., conquer new territory for the Realm" is good if the players prefer to skip that stuff and go straight to the dangerous adventure. One can adjust as appropriate for your particular game, I hope.

                  Another think to think about is if anything could make this event less unlikely, and whether it would be fun to have any of that in the backstory. I don't know exactly what the mechanics of Caul pilgrimages are supposed to be, but surely if the father also went on a Caul pilgrimage (most likely together...), and they conceived the child on the Caul, it couldn't hurt the chances of something like this happening. A stranger idea is that the father is secretly a Lunar who went on the Caul pilgrimage, and owing to the Caul's origin in love between Luna and Gaia, that combination had a dramatic effect on the children. That one seems like it would be fun to come up at some point in play.
