                  Originally posted by Hark View Post
                  You might get some value out of using everything in Thousand Correct Actions to your advantage by forcing things to be mass combat.
                  Yeah, this. Mass Combat is not a system really anybody wants to use, and making Dragon-Bloods a combat threat at the high-end generally involves armies. It's, well, in their blood.

                  That being said, how many of them are immune to environmental penalties and environmental damage? It doesn't matter how many dice you're rolling for defense, if you stand in the middle of a Dragon-Blood created volcano, you get hit. (Meanwhile Dragon-Bloods can rather trivially immunize themselves and their followers from their own firepower.) Making heavy use of Dragon Vortex Attack and Dragon-Seared Battlefield can go a long way to making a legion of DBs as terrifying as it should be. Getting to shape and direct those via the charm from TCA helps a lot as well, and one of the maneuvers in TCA is getting to turn a Dragon Vortex into a true perfect defense.

                  On a personal level, Surging (Ability) Mastery lets dragon-bloods get close to Celestial dice pools for a while, but they run out of motes and willpower before too long. In that time, however, they can do a lot of damage. TCA charms let you bless all your buddies for a whole scene with all the Dragon-Graced Weapon you know, and if you know all of them, hitting somebody knocks them down, poisons them, delays and penalizes their next action, and does a bunch of damage. It's not quite on the level of Solar "I hit him and he evaporates" but it can get pretty scary.

                  Dragon-Bloods can also have "tanks" at high end, with a combination of taunting ability and eventually the power to link minds to reflexively defend everyone else who is part of the group mind (one of the ultimate powers that Celestials want for their DB allies, since there's nothing on the Celestial end that can replicate it).

                  Celestial Martial Arts (especially Crane Style) helps DBs punch above their weight class as well, Ragara Myrrun knows a bunch of heretical styles in addition to the immaculate ones so if the DBs are pulling out all the stops to stomp on the biggest eminent threat to the Realm, expect more breadth of expertise than your average war party.

                  Finally, Sidereals are pretty good at helping, whether it's through astrological blessings or Throne Shadow Style or being far better informed than anyone else or just straight-up shanking a bitch, so depending on how high-end we're talking it's not at all weird to see them participate. If they've got a good exit strategy you could see high-end Bronzies with Scarlet Patterned Battlefield Style making real trouble when in conjunction with DB armies.

                              Everyone has mentioned taking it to Mass Combat already. That's one of three prongs of the Realm's approach to the threat of a whole mess of high-end Anathema.

                              The second prong? Gear.

                              The Realm is not going to send just a bunch of guys in armor wielding daiklave's against a full cadre of Anathema working together unless it has no other choice. They're going to break out the artifacts. We're talking multiple guys in Warstriders. We're talking Dragon Armor. (It's not just for Lookshy.) We're talking every weird piece of first age weaponry they dredge out of their armories that looks like it might be useful.

                              The third prong? Immaculates.

                              This is what a non-trivial part of the Immaculate Order has spent their whole lives training for. We're talking multiple Immaculate MA masters, who also have Defense-From-Anathema Method. We're talking probably a Grandmaster. (There are a couple more of them than just Myrrun.)

                              Aside from that, if the Realm is marching openly against a bunch of high-end Celestials you guys are probably viewed as an existential threat to the Realm. So plan accordingly. I am going to assume that someone who actually knows what the hell they are doing is calling the shots for the Realm at the highest levels, like Cathak Cainan, Sesus Nagezzar, or the Roseblack. The Realm can transform from a giant inefficient slug to a terrifying engine of extinction very quickly when it has to, and it isn't above doing things like "Okay, we're going to abandon An-Teng, because we need the legions elsewhere. Yes, we're going to abandon ALL of it. We can take it back later." The Realm is going to dig deep. Ask yourself what the Realm could possibly have on tap for just this scenario and then deploy it.

                              A bunch of people have already mentioned the Siddies. I would be real, real hesitant about suddenly busting them out if they haven't been a presence thus far. Would they get involved? Absolutely. But it has to be handled with care. Your players might or might not resent the reality ninjas suddenly busting in out of nowhere to save the Realm's bacon. If Sidereals haven't been a presence in your game so far at all AND your PCs haven't done anything the Gold Faction finds objectionable, I would recommend hand-waving things as "the Bronze Faction did their voodoo, and then the Gold Faction did their hoodoo, and it all cancelled out."

                              A note of warning: be prepared for your PCs to disengage. You probably know them better than we do. But sometimes groups of players look at the business end of a Realm hammer and go "Nope. Nope nope nope nope. Could we win? Yeah, probably. But I'm not wading into THAT unless I absolutely have to. I'm going to fade back into the jungle/forest/steppe/etc. and let them run around exhausting themselves, spending ruinous sums of money maintaining that expeditionary force, and killing every Wyld Hunt they detach from the main force to try and smoke me out. Eventually they'll either fall apart at the seams or go home, and I'll strike THEN."

                              It can happen. Have a Plan B.
