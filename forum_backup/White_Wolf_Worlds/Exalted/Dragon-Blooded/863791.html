                                                      The presentation of Dragons of Another Colour was this weird middle ground between allowing a Dragon-Blooded Necromancer and shamelessly pandering to the notion of Abyssal Dragon-Blooded that resonate with the Elements of the Underworld in a manner that I don’t think really satisfied either side.

                                                      In 3e the Dynastic (or Lookshyan) Necromancer doesn’t really gain from it being a rare genetic anomaly, the number of Dragon-Blooded who have an aptitude for sorcery and specifically choose to look to the Underworld or its denizens for enlightenment is going to be low enough to not require additional restrictions. You have weird and heretical powers much like you’d get if you had secret liaisons with Mara or some creepy Sorcerous Relic that burrowed under your skin when it first came into your possession.

                                                      Meanwhile if you want Dragon-Blooded tainted with the power of death then you can be a little more ambitious than giving them access to necromancy.
