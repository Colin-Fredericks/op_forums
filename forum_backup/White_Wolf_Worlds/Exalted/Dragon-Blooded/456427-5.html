                Originally posted by Silvercat18 View Post
                *snip*
                If an experienced Dragon-Blooded will be killed 100% of the time by a newborn Solar, then why make the Dragon-Blooded playable at all? The Solars are good, and as you say, world-shaking. They are excellence and perfection, but I feel what they are most of all is potential.

                The potential of what a Solar can do is staggering. But that lies in gaining experience, surviving in a world that half the people want you dead, and learning. And that potential never lessens. Even once they've reached mastery of something, there's still so much more they can do.

                That said, this is only slightly less true for the other Exalted. Being Chosen is being gifted with the power to do limitless wonders. Its harder for some to express it, but its still there. Personally, i feel like Exaltation is giving a character the PC Character tag, even if they aren't controlled by a player. It means they're able to do miraculous and outstanding things, above the norm. Every single exalt you come across should feel like this, a potential friend or party member, a lover or an extremely dangerous foe. The feeling you get when you might kill another players character is the feeling you should feel should you choose that you need to kill an Exalt, because you will be trying to destroy something blessed by gods and sacred, whether they're a douche or not.

                So every instance of a Wyld Hunt should be a story as amazing as if both sides were controlled by players. Neither side should be offing the other casually. Newborn Solars aren't going to be laying waste to an entire Wyld Hunt and then strutting off to f*ck b*tch*s, and the Dragon-Blooded aren't likely to off the Solar in a single blow and then snort cocaine off his dead body.

                A Terrestrial vs a Solar, the odds are in the Solar's favor. But it should never be a completely 100% outcome, especially between equally experienced characters.

                                Neither the setting nor the mechanics were written with the expectation that 100% of all Solars shake Creation down to its foundation on day 1 of their Exaltation. For one thing, Solars have been Exalting for thousands of years, and the vast majority of the ones that did so during the times of the Shogunate or the Realm ended up with their heads on spikes, perishing in the holy fires of the Dragon-Blooded, overwhelmed by a monster they unwisely called up, knifed in the back by people they thought were friends, et cetera. If the setting had been written the way Silvercat seems to assume, Creation would not be recognizable because the Realm (or indeed, any of the institutions of Creation) would not have been able to stand up to the might of the dozen or so Solars that could shake up the world from day 1.

                                They have the potential to shake things up, but it's far from automatic. The Exalted, all of them, are mighty, but they have feet of clay.

                                                        It's never been officially stated for what the mechanics at the moment of Exaltation are, and I doubt we'll ever see those.

                                                        In my games (it's come up a few times in games where the players Exalt during the course of the story), for the scene in which he Exalts, the character's Essence is treated as if it were 5, and he can essentially use every Charm for which he meets the Ability/Attribute requirements. This means, for that one scene, an Exalt is one terrifying motherfucker. Then, every scene after that, the character's Essence lowers by 1 until it reaches 2, at which point the character loses all but a handful of Charms and must begin learning Charms and raising Essence as normal.

                                                        This will probably give the character the opportunity to survive or deal with whatever danger he may have been in at the moment of Exaltation.
