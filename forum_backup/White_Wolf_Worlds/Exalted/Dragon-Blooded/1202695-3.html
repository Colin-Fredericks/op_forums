                                                        There's some other builds that entertain me from a theorycrafting perspective, but the character I feel I'd be most likely to try to play is...

                                                        Nellens Kiren is a thoroughly corrupt bureaucrat, and he doesn't even have the decency to be corrupt for the right reasons. Dismissive of his family after his grandmother married him into a patrician family wealthy enough to buy his entire sorry Dynastic household, he's decided that the only way he's ever going to earn the respect he deserves as a Prince of the Earth is to make himself richer than Ragara, one egregious embezzlement at a time.

                                                        In a political game, he could stay in place, swayed for one reason or other to use his power of corruption to support the PCs' faction. In the probably-more-likely scenario where I end up in an action game, he could potentially tweak the wrong nose with some misdeed and end up effectively exiled to the Threshold, forced to mingle with, horror of horrors, barbarians.

                                                        Of course, if he ever realizes that a Tengese peasant doesn't know the difference between a Nellens and a Mnemon, he might change his tune on barbarians right quick.

                                                        Probably a Water Aspect (though Fire with Water-y tendencies is possible), Bureaucracy / Socialize build, minimal "Oh Dragons why am I in a fight?" combat skills.
