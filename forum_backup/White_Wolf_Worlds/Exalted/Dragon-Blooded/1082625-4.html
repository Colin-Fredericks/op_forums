                                                Originally posted by Aya Tari View Post
                                                What is the power of Names in 3e? Could removing her Name from every possible record be a form of protection from Sorcery?
                                                ​I have a personal interest in names not being very significant to that kind of magic, beyond a general targeting mechanism that will go by whatever the person happens to call themselves by. After all, we already know what the setting's general sorcery is like, and it doesn't really incorporate a deal with knowing a person's name giving you a special power over them, and the setting in general doesn't really act as though people hide their names.

                                                ​Mostly it's a case of how I think many instances of the Exalted lend themselves readily to a cultural perspective that your real name is the one you opt to go by at the time. I get the basis of magic through somebody having an essential name, and even like certain properties featuring it, but I would also like some properties in which it has no power and one can go by whatever they want without any implication that it is somehow less real.
