                      Wood seems good for the concept, though Fire also works, as they favour Presence and Socialise.

                      I'd say Wood, but take Socialise as favoured: it has some charms for carousing, like a charm that makes all the alcohol at a party really good.
                      There's some fun charms in Performance (which is Wood) you could use as well. Maybe Medicine... for making sure everyone recovers from the party!

                      But honestly, he sounds like maybe a bit of a dilettante, doing various hobbies because he's just having a good time. So I don't really know what you want to take.

                      You mentioned martial arts: there's a Wood-aspected style called Orgiastic Fugitive style, for drunken master fighting. I find it a little mechanically complicated though. You need to have a good handle on the poison rules.

                          Crunch-wise, I would lean heavily on the high-tech nature of the Age of Nightmares for survival. You CAN have something like Celestial Battle Armor, so go for it. If necessary, justify it as yet another indulgence. You will be thankful for it when the long knives come out.

                          Another portion of the character sheet that is easy to overlook is Backgrounds. You want to have good friends for the end of the world. The kind of people that are likely to live, and have a vested interest in you living. I'd set myself up with enough status in the Deliberative that I had a position safely ensconced in the Bureaucracy. The goal would be high enough up to not do much work, and low enough down that you wouldn't be working directly with any crazed Solars.

                                  Charm recommendations. Pick and choose which abilities you like.

                                  A note on Excellencies: always get the First. For combat abilities, also get the Second and Speciality Focus. Only get Terrestrial Reinforcement if it is a prerequisite or you want your DB to lead mortal groups in this ability (Medicine, Craft, Bureaucracy as well as most combat abilities). Surging Mastery is only good to combo with Extra Action Charms.

                                  Archery:
                                  Primarily useful for group range defence, it also has one of the few DB unblockable/undodgeable attacks in the form of a counter plus a decent Extra Action.
                                  Dragon Graced Arrow (Wood, Water, Fire, Earth)
                                  Dragonfly Finds Mate
                                  Seven Year Swarm Volley
                                  Sparrow Dives at Hawk
                                  Swallows Defend the Nest

                                  Athletics:
                                  Mostly unremarkable. Bellows Pumping Stride can be extended to allies. For a larger investment you could have a flying archer but this is easier done with artifacts in the First Age.
                                  Effortlessly Rising Flame
                                  Falling Star Maneuver
                                  Bellows Pumping Stride

                                  Awareness:
                                  A grab bag of scene-long surprise negation, scrying and shaping/crippling attacks.
                                  All-Encompassing Earth Sense
                                  Sense Riding Technique
                                  Sense Destroying Method
                                  Essence Disruption Attack

                                  Bureaucracy:
                                  Home to some of the most potent DB influence Charms, if you want to make a deal, a DB bureaucrat has you covered.
                                  Finding the Water's Depths
                                  Thoughtful Gift Technique
                                  Distraction of the Babbling Brook
                                  Drowning in Negotiation Style

                                  Craft:
                                  Great for quick-building mundane objects and structures, can also boost others in attacking structures.
                                  Shaping Hand Style
                                  Stone Carving Fingers Form (Wood, Earth, Fire)
                                  Flaw-Finding Examination

                                  Dodge:
                                  DB dodge is pretty solid, with penalty negators, cap-breaking external penalty defences, group defences and counterattacks. The PD is not the best, but worth getting anyway.
                                  Threshold Warding Stance
                                  Hopping Firecracker Evasion
                                  Virtuous Negation Defence
                                  Safety Among Enemies
                                  Elemental Defence Technique (Wood, Air)
                                  Smoldering Karma Strike
                                  Unassailable Body of (Element) Defence (Wood, Fire)

                                  Integrity:
                                  A must have in a Celestial game. Shaping defence, perfect social block and cheap maxed DV and MDV for the scene while in the presence of Celestials.
                                  Inviolate Dragon Spirit
                                  Chaos Warding Prana
                                  Defence from Anathema Method
                                  Oath of Ten Thousand Dragons
                                  Undaunted Dragon Hero

                                  Investigation:
                                  One of the weaker trees. The E5 capstone Dredged Murmurs Hunch is potentially great, but probably best reserved for PCs.
                                  Scent of Crime Method
                                  Falsehood Unearthing Attitude
                                  Revelation of Associates Hunch
                                  Tampering Detection Technique
                                  Bloodhound's Nose Technique

                                  Larceny:
                                  Like the sister-tree Investigation, it's fairly weak. Unlike Investigation, its E5 capstone is amazing and will likely be helpful for a group.
                                  Observer Awareness Method
                                  Ears of the Snowy Owl
                                  Naked Thief Style
                                  Window-In-The-Door Technique
                                  Flowing God-Dragon Stance

                                  Linguistics:
                                  Another must-have. Linguistics is divided into language Charms that aren't very useful and communication Charms which definitely are.
                                  Wind-Carried Words Technique
                                  Voices on the Wind
                                  Poisoned Tongue Technique
                                  Voice of Mastery
                                  With One Mind

                                  Lore:
                                  A grab bag of elemental and mind-expanding powers. At E5, Elemental Bolt Technique is the most accurate attack DBs have. Get The Wind Turns only if your DB has children.
                                  Elemental Concentration Trance
                                  Elemental Empowerment Meditation
                                  Glorious Birthright Font
                                  The Wind Turns
                                  Elemental Bolt Attack
                                  Elemental Burst Technique

                                  Martial Arts:
                                  Not touching this one. What styles are permissible depends heavily on your storyteller. By default Terrestrial Hero is not worth it.

                                  Medicine:
                                  If you lack a Solar physician, a DB is the next best thing.
                                  Infection Banishing Prana
                                  Venom Expulsion Method
                                  Madness Analysing Stare
                                  Disease Banishing Technique
                                  Wound Closing Touch
                                  Purity of Mind Method
                                  Most Beneficent Seed of the Five Dragons
                                  Grievous Wound Alternation Energy
                                  Jade Crucible Method x2

                                  Melee:
                                  Alone, DB Melee is not bad. Its main strength is to enhance others. With other Melee fighters, it is great. Alongside Solar Melee, it produces monsters.
                                  Dragon Graced Weapon (x5)
                                  Refining the Inner Blade
                                  Dragon Blade Beneficence
                                  Portentous Comet-Deflecting Mode
                                  Conflagration of Warding
                                  Threshing Floor Technique
                                  Ringing Anvil Onslaught

                                  Occult:
                                  Terrestrial Sorcery is not all bad and an allied sorcerer could bring in some handy utility spells. Otherwise, DB spirit fighting is not bad.
                                  Terrestrial Circle Sorcery
                                  Elemental Internalization Technique
                                  Spirit-Detecting Mirror
                                  Harmonious Wind-Luring Song
                                  Spirit-Grounding Shout
                                  Spirit-Shredding Attack

                                  Performance:
                                  These vary a lot in utility, but Soul-Stirring Performance Method is probably the strongest DB UMI effect. Several serve double-duty to enhance musical fighting techniques from Three-String Sword Prana and Vibrating Strings Defence.
                                  Soul-Stirring Performance Method
                                  Protective Performance
                                  Talented Improvisation
                                  Invisible Street Performer Method
                                  Vibrating Strings Defence
                                  Tireless String-Plucking Meditation
                                  Three-String Sword Prana

                                  Presence:
                                  A mix of low key social effects, defences and willpower-sapping combat taunts. Not bad, worth at least a dip for the first three.
                                  Auspicious First Meeting
                                  Glowing Coal Radiance
                                  Aura of Invulnerability
                                  Terrifying (Element) Dragon Roar
                                  Unbearable Taunt Technique
                                  Moth to Candle
                                  Warlord's Convocation

                                  Resistance:
                                  Strength of Stones is great for boosting the groups's stats. Mountain Toppling Method is usually only good in conjunction with an extra-action Charm using a jade weapon.
                                  Ox-Body Technique
                                  Strength of Stones Technique
                                  Impervious Skin of Stone Meditation
                                  (Element) Protection Form (Wood, Air)
                                  Mountain Toppling Method

                                  Ride:
                                  Not a lot to offer here unless you want to get into mass combat, and I don't.
                                  Heaven Graced Riding Technique
                                  Ebony Spur Technique
                                  Great Heart Companion
                                  Elemental Halo's Mercy

                                  Sail:
                                  If you reeeeally want a grizzled sea captain go all in and get everything for Waves Like Clouds Voyage. Otherwise skip it.

                                  Socialize:
                                  Generally very weak, but it fits the Cheerful Carouser concept too well to pass up. Have your party Charms.
                                  Sweeten-the-Tap Method
                                  Auspicious Merrymaking Technique
                                  Wary Yellow Dog Attitude
                                  Blood Cries for Blood
                                  Warm-Faced Seduction Style
                                  Smoothing Over the Past Technique
                                  Brother Against Brother Insinuation

                                  Stealth:
                                  Generally pretty good at assisting any group covert action.
                                  Distracting Breeze Meditation
                                  Deadly Mirage Ambush
                                  Trackless Passage Style
                                  Wind Walking Technique
                                  Feeling the Air Technique
                                  Soundless Action Prana
                                  Zone of Silence Stance
                                  Dragon Shroud Technique
                                  Hidden Maelstrom Cloak

                                  Survival:
                                  It's not very good. This one is good for tracking but I would skip the rest.
                                  Quarry Revelation Technique

                                  Thrown:
                                  Good mix of attacks and defences. Elemental Armor is one of the best DB defence Charms. Attacks mix well with Elemental Bolt.
                                  Loyal Weapon
                                  Persistent Hornet Attack
                                  Deadly Blades of the Five Dragons
                                  Whirlwind Shield Form
                                  Vengeful Gust Counterattack
                                  Elemental Armor Technique
                                  Hurricane Hand Gesture

                                  War:
                                  If you use Mass Combat a lot, War is hands down the best DB tree. Even if you don't, Ramparts of Obedient Earth and Dragon Vortex are definitely worth getting.
                                  Enfolded in the Dragon's Wings
                                  Phantom Warrior Horde
                                  Blazing Courageous Swordsman Inspiration
                                  Ramparts of Obedient Earth
                                  Dragon Seared Battlefield
                                  Dragon Vortex Attack
                                  Wrath Directing Gesture (Follow, Sculpt, Shield)

                                  Phew, that ended up a lot longer than I thought.

                                                      Ok, let's take a closer look at it.

                                                      Scent-of-Crime Method - Lets you detect how guilty someone is feeling. This doesn't necessarily provide any helpful information (it doesn't necessarily tell you what they are feeling guilty about and some people feel no guilt) so I am pretty ambivalent about it. However, it is cheap and scene-long, so you can pick guilty-feeling people out of a crowd. It's ok.

                                                      Falsehood Unearthing Attitude - A poor substitute for Judge's Ear, it fails against evasions and half-truths. It is useful for interrogating people, but it can be difficult to use subtly or casually. While you can repeatedly ask the same question ten times, unless your storyteller is telling you that they are lying and spending willpower to avoid it, I'm not sure why you would, and doing that undermines the intended weakness of the Charm.

                                                      Tampering Detection Technique - Examine an object, reveals any tampering throughout the last year. This may provide some use but is generally unimpressive.

                                                      Bloodhound's Nose Technique - An interesting idea that is limited by the practicalities of the game. When invited to give players open-ended clues to the identity of the perpetrator, the storyteller is probably going to give the clues that he intended the party investigator to uncover anyway. Being able to Sherlock-scan a crime scene is nice, though.

                                                      Clear Water Prana - For the princely sum of 5m, 1wp, Clear Water Prana detects concealed objects within Essence yards, except when they are concealed with Celestial Charms or Sorcery. I am struggling to think of a possible use for this Charm. When would you activate it? If you have reason to suspect something is (mundanely) concealed nearby, under what circumstances would a regular Investigation roll not suffice?

                                                      Revelation of Associates Hunch - Aside from being an absolute pain for the Storyteller, this isn't bad for information gathering. However, it does have the ignominious distinction of being the only Charm to have the risible Celestial-No restriction added in errata.

                                                      Homeward Trail Discovery Method - Potentially useable, but I think with Investigation 4+ and knowing a list of closest friends, family and associates, the investigator would be able to narrow down his target's home anyway.

                                                      Dredged Murmurs Hunch - This is a plot level investigation power that puts the Sidereal google spider to shame. I didn't recommend it above because it's something that should really only be in the hands of PCs.

                                                      Overall it's not bad (except for Clear Water Prana) but I feel justified in saying it's one of the weaker trees.

                                                      It can certainly do things other Charms cannot, but Charm purchases have a tight opportunity cost and how much use you will get out of an Investigation investment needs to be compared against investments in other areas. Compared with the breadth of utility you can get out of the Bureaucracy deal making Charms or the Linguistics communication tree, Investigation falls short.

                                                      Dredged Murmurs Hunch is the exception, but you need to buy almost all the other Charms to access it.
