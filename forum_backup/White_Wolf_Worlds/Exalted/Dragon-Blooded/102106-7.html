                    The problem is that it's too pat.

                    Exalted feels too solved.                                         Originally posted by Kyeudo View Post

                                        Exalted's Mandate of Heaven, at least in 2nd edition, is a thing that was specifically given to the Solar Exalted and is not just a marker that the current top dog holds.
                                        Well, then, there's one other thing that could distinguish the Exalted Mandate from the Divine Right of Kings: is it revocable, or can it pass from group to group?

                                        The RL Mandate of Heaven was something that Chinese dynasties gained or lost based on how virtuous they were, supposedly. If multiple disasters struck during your reign, it could be said that you had lost the Mandate because of your misdeeds, and the gods were punishing the nation in general until you shaped up or got usurped. And then if the guy who killed you and took the throne did a better job, the Mandate was now theirs.

                                        The Divine Right, on the other hand, was said to be something integral to kingly bloodlines, and a king is a king no matter what he does. Whether they are virtuous people and whether they rule well or poorly is irrelevant - they still have the Right. Kings can fight Kings, but a bunch of lesser nobles can't rebel, kill the King, and then claim supreme rulership for themselves.

                                        Practical politics didn't follow the ideal, admittedly, but the concepts were pretty solid.

                                        Given the nature of Exaltation (power that cannot be taken away once it is given), I would think that the Solars ruling by Divine Right would be more consistent than Solars ruling by the Mandate of Heaven. It would also help to boost the Solar's status as "returning kings" rather than "really powerful dudes who are possibly more worthy of being kings than the current guys".
