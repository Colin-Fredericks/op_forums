    Originally posted by Moss Reynholm View Post
    1. Earth-focused melee – I've previously figured 'why would you do this when Earth Dragon is right there?', but it looks a bit more of a thing now? Getting an artefact heavy weapon to get the full benefit of Dragon-Scale Shield, pair it with Dragon-Graced Weapon and your preferred heirloom Grand Goremaul/Stone of the Ram's Horn combo and you've got Earth Dragon lite without the merit expenditure and access to the very good melee tree.

    Anyone with more experience got a feel for how good the magma charm is?
    Earth-melee is looking pretty good to me. Wrath-Grounding Guard and Lightning Drinking Blade are really campaign dependent. If you're dealing with rebellions or battling mutant armies in your outcaste run fringe kingdom then it's not likely to show up very often. If you're battling other dragonblooded, sorcerers, gods, demons, elementals, and ghosts on a regular basis it's going to be a lot more helpful.

    Even if it's out of aspect it's probably worth picking up Flame Warden Stance as it gives you a lot more freedom, and you can use the fire aura effect of it until you get into Earth aura.

    Spilling the Mountain's Blood is good, but best against really big opponents. Adding overflow successes to damage, then adding another 40% to the damage, and then causing them to suffer a 6L/round difficulty 5 hazard is pretty punchy. You might want to pair it with (Element) Protection Technique though, since there's a good chance you're going to be brawling in a lava pool by the end of your next turn.

    Originally posted by Moss Reynholm View Post
    2. 5/1/5 Brawler – mucking around with trying to make this, well, not good but fun to play thanks to Flotsam-and-Jetsam Approach (FYI, basically allows you to use strength with improvised weapons for a scene for 1m). End up just spamming Ox-Bodies at the minute, and realising that Water has so many toys it's hard to justify going with any other aspect, even if I'd like to make an Ragara Earth-aspected brutish debt collector work (although honourable mention for Wood and Oaken Thew Exertion).
    Earth Brawl is pretty good. Grab these, bold indicates charms that are neither Earth nor Balanced:


    Flotsam-and-Jetsam Approach
    Become the Hammer
    Water Dragon's Coils
    Inescapable Whirlpool Hold
    Crushing Avalanche Grasp
    Pounding Surf Style
    Stone Fist Strike
    Rolling Boulder Blow
    Firmament Shattering Impact
    Hammering Wave Technique
    Fist Spinning Maelstrom
    At Essence 4 Magma Hell Upheaval


    Floatsam-and-Jetsam can be used reflexively as soon as combat starts, right when you roll join battle so it doesn't mess with your aura. Your withering attacks get enhanced with by the enemy's onslaught penalty and your Essence to your Overwhelming to punch through high soak. Your decisive attacks add (Strength) threshold successes, let you smash an enemy both back and prone literally cratering them and surrounding them with difficult terrain, possibly even throwing them back to Medium or Long range and taking additional fall damage. Your grapples get to use (Strength + Brawl) to hit, add threshold successes to the gambit roll, dissapearing 6s on the gambit roll, and attacks that miss or don't deal damage don't take away control rounds.

    Although Flotsam-and-Jetsam Approach isn't compatible with Stone Fist Strike, so look to use SFS against opponents with soak so high you'll never penetrate it, and smack any other poor bastard with the table leg otherwise.

    You also have the option of holding back from going totemic anima and using Water aura charms, or even doing both and doing things like get into Water Aura to use Fist Spinning Maelstrom to wither everyone around you, and then flare totemic, automatically shifting to Earth aura, and annihilating one enemy with Crater-Making Impact. Later on you can even use Fist Spinning Maelstrom to wither a ton of people and knock them prone, and then use Magma Hell Upheaval to smack all their prone selves with an unblockable magma tidal wave.

    Let me remind you that prone characters have a flat -2 to their Evasion against this unblockable attack.

    There are definitely more toys that a pure water brawler gets to play with, but I'd say Earth still looks good with fun and variation.

        So here's an actual build for Olmar, an immaculate investigator who hunts down heretics and criminals and punctures their armor with his direlance. He either hunts down local criminals or immaculate heretics, but if the case arises that the criminal or heretic in question is a fairy or anathema he won't shy away from it. Secretly though, he wishes he could just master his calligraphy in peace, but he knows his skills and natural talent to violence, combined with the evils of the world force him to put that aside for now.

In combat he's a monster and particularly capable against foes with heavy armor.                                                         Here’s a character build that I’m hoping to use as an antagonist that (a) shows off some of the new Earth melee and (b) is easy enough to run (i.e. doesn’t worry much about aura). Idea is he’s an honourable, unstoppable legion officer who has been called back as the Great Houses shape up for war and essentially earmarked by the house’s powers that be as the enforcer for his geomancer/demonologist prodigy cousin (who’s a drug-addicted loose cannon) who has been tasked with furthering Ragara’s research into the bad stuff. He’s not happy about it – which way is his loyalty going to go?

                                                        Supposed to be relatively balanced (if five Ox-Bodies and Pain Tolerance can be called that, but he’s intended to reoccur so being tough to kill seems to make sense). Min-maxing would involve dropping intelligence and stamina to 1, dropping Impervious Skin of Stone till you’ve upped stamina and ditching some of the fluff dots, buying more favoured abilities and merits instead, taking one of the named Grand Goremauls with free evocations etc etc (if that suits your game).
