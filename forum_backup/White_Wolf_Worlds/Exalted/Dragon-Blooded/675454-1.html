            Originally posted by Amakawa Yuuto View Post
            I really don't think this will change.
            Remember, there's a bit of doublethink going on here: Yes, the Realm knows breeding is a thing, but at the same time, the Immaculate Order preaches that those who exalted as Dragonblooded are blessed by the Dragons and their divine representatives in Creation, Princes of the Earth, while those who didn't exalt as Dragonblooded are mere mortals who must follow the guidance of those with the Blood of Dragons.
            A fine (mortal) child of Mnemon is still lower in the divine hierarchy than that outcaste Dragonblooded, for the sole reason that it didn't exalt. If the dragons wanted it to stand equal or above the outcaste Dragonblooded, they'd have graced it with their blessings.
            There's a huge difference between spiritual elevation and social standing.

            That mortal child of Mnemon will probably be invited to all the big parties and have lots of close friends and contacts among the movers and shakes of the Realm. That Outcast, while people will bow to him, won't be getting any invitations to major events and will largely be looked down on as a barbarian (socially) even by mortals. Until he manages to marry into one of the Houses, he'll always be an outsider in the Realm and many people will look down on him (socially) even if they grudgingly respect his spiritual enlightenment.

            In many situations it would be very apparent that the mortal, with her allies, contacts, family and money would functionally have much more power and influence and thereby receive more respect and be above a Dragon Blooded Outcast with no strong ties or powerful allies in the Realm.

                                            There's also the matter of the Outcastes in the Immaculate Order.

                                            Assuming about half go in​to the Order and half go into the Legions, this implies that the Empress had a deliberate strategy of making it difficult for Outcastes to breed. After all, half of them are banned from doing so. This implies she doesn't want too large a group of Outcastes in the Realm: the Empire may want more Dragonblood, but Scarlet is/was a matriarch, and perhaps wanted to ensure that her family stayed at the top, while those unrelated to her got sent to do the dangerous jobs that require a lot of Dragonblooded, but don't necessarily give them a ton of power or resources.

                                            Of course, now it's 768 years since she founded the thing, she may feel little connection with her great(x10) grand-kids, and they likely feel little familial connection to her, but only to their House. But I imagine this idea has bled into Realm culture: you'd rather marry a member of the dynasty than some foreigner. Does this contradict the Immaculate Philosophy? Yes, but people do things that contradict their own religion all the time.

                                            There's probably something about foreigners vs Realm natives too. If a peasant exalts as a Dragonblood, a few days later the Dynast who owns the land turns up and while the peasants grovel before her announces thats he's finally found the long-lost grand-son of his dead brother, and in his honour she wants to adopt this "lost egg" as her son (of course, he'll never be fully accepted as he's an ill-mannered boor, but the House will grab him nonetheless). Whereas if a Medoan janissary exalts while on campaign for the Realm, no-one's letting him join their House, it's the coin or the razor for him.

                                            (Plus, chances are said Exalted peasant is the great-great-great-great-great-grandson of the Empress, because half the Blessed Isle are. Whereas the Medoan jannisary probably isn't.)

                                            Now, a beastman has all of these issues at the same time. It's a monstrous freak, an abomination against the Immaculate Philosophy by its very nature, but it's also a Dragonblood. Honestly, knowing the prejudice against them (if they don't know already, they'll probably find out quickly), few Beastmen are likely to go to the Realm, they'd rather rule a Beastman tribe. But if they did... I imagine that they'll be told to take the Razor or Coin, and then have an "accident" in training (if you read the fluff, this happens more than you'd think for valuable super-soldiers, because disloyal super-soldiers are a big risk).
