        House Peleps

        Ok, so what did they do to you to make me like you?

        ...This is different.

        Dashing and adventurous. Pure and incorruptible.

        This is very different.

        Not bad though.

        Ah, Abbot and Costello and nautical humor.

        So they’re a combination of the Lion and Unicorn clans then? The ones who went off to exotic foreign lands and are incorruptible and honorable.

        Ah-ha!

        ...oh, no, ok, I’m actually kinda down with having to demonstrate some degree of worthiness for your position.

        So, you have to be lucky and awesome to make it in this house.

        So they’re actually less honorable than they appear it sounds like. I am SHOCKED. SHOCKED I tell you.

        I’m assuming that Peleps Orimu is a pirate hunter and not a judge who is also a pirate, awesome as that might be.

        So, yeah, we’re not having a vote so you get a say. We’re having a vote to prove which of us can manipulate the rest of you the best.

        They’re debating between “try to rule the Realm OR fuck off and go take over the West and rule in an island paradise. Hmmmmm.....”

        If you’re not going to include a timeline in this book, you might wanna insert a reminder somewhere in the text of what the current year is, just to give us a reference for all the dates you keep throwing at us. Maybe in the Lexicon where you talk about the realm year you could say “It is currently RY whatever.”

        Ok, so. Having one House oversee the entire navy on an island nation seems really dangerous. I’m honestly surprised that the Empress let Peleps hold on to that monopoly for as long as she did.

        That said, if the Empress had stayed, I bet she’d have done something like suggest to V’Neef that they trade back some of the merchant marine for one of the navies or something. Unless she was planning to destroy Peleps and divide up the navy between a bunch of other houses.

        I have to say that squeezing your own Satrapies dry because you can always steal more does not sound virtuous.

        All of these methods sound very desperate.

        ....You’re planning on creating an empire that’s true to the Immaculate Order with all sorts of shady dealings?

        So, they’re quietly sending an entire legion west, ready to take it over.

        So, if you do well and you’re a DB woman, you can go fast....and fall hard if you’ve got the least bit of scandal. That sounds like a fun character background. Once the rising star of the Peleps armada, then something happened, and now you’re piloting a tug boat or garbage scow or something else humiliating. But it happens to be the only boat in the area when the other PCs show up and need a boat, and game progresses.

        Yeah, I really wonder what else would have happened to Peleps if the Empress has remained, because allowing one house to become powerful enough to possibly win a fight against most of the other houses seems like something she’d have wanted to avoid.

        This reputation sounds more manufactured than real honestly.

        With all its debt, it seems odd not to explore its relations with Ragara in more depth than just “oh, yeah, it owes Ragara too, just like everyone else.”

        Still a pretty cold blooded house.

        Interesting relationship with Cathak. The knowledge that the two of them could rule the world is interesting. A Cathak-Peleps-Mnemon alliance could force the war hot and make a few other houses, like Ledaal, join them and force the newer and/or disliked houses to band together against them. I really don’t fancy the chances of V’Neef, Tepet, Nellens, Sesus, or Ragara in that situation. Though, I could be wrong.

        That puts the military on one side and the economy and ninjas on the other. Which would be an interesting conflict.

        I want a book about the War in the West now. V’Neef vs. Peleps in a region where neither of them has a complete stranglehold on everything, and there are plenty of non-Realm wild cards, will be interesting.

        Welp. Sarkarn is in a scylla and charybdis situation.

        Really, really want the War in the West now. Wavecrest sounds lots more interesting now than it did when the main thing you had to worry about was being arrested for jaywalking and having the sentence be “toss you into the volcano.”

        Well you know what they say. If our ancestors were alive today, they’d say “why is it so dark in here? Where’s the door?”

        And here we come to one of the big reasons the War hasn’t gone hot yet. Everyone in charge is still half way convinced that the Empress is going to show up tomorrow and do a massive re-org based on how everyone acted during the 5 years she was gone. And most of the Houses will have lots of explaining to do then.

        Aramida... wasn’t she from one of the Aspect books?

        Good luck with that, Zurin. I hope you live through the attempt.

        Interesting house. Seems like its reputation as virtuous and adventurous is about as accurate as the Cynis one of drunken debouching is. In that they’re both true to an extent, but they’re also as much camouflage as anything else.

                                Anyway, Tepet time!

                                I read the preview, but I wanna read this one again.

                                Very nice intro section, very evocative and very good at introducing the central conflict of the house.

                                I love the bits of heroic worldbuilding in their background.

                                This write up really drives home just how much their downfall was orchestrated by the other houses as it was from their military failure. It also serves as a very nice example of what awaits any of the other houses who move too soon, expose the wrong secret, or otherwise show too much weakness.

                                Well, I assume Iselsi will too, but I’m not there yet. While Iselsi was destroyed many more years ago, with the remnants biding their time, rebuilding their power, and waiting for the best time to strike, so they’re an example of what a long lost house might still be able to do.

                                Oh, that’s a good note. “Oh, we’ll grab these Satrapies that Tepet used to administer, the ones where they trained the locals in combat, and we’ll treat them like our own personal banks, because obviously the Tepet broke their spirit for us, so it’ll be fine.” Yeah, keep telling yourself that dude.
