    Originally posted by Deinos View Post
    Hey all.

    Question: I am not sure if I'm going insane, and I am sorry for the squick, but I distinctly recall a reference something like "the Realm is a place where a mother may take her son to bed with her" regarding the dragon blooded. I think this might have been the VERY early, pre-publishing, lore posts from back before nWoD. Did this actually make it into the books, and if so, does anyone know if the 3e Dragon Blooded are intended to be at the same level of squickiness or less?

    I don't remember the exact source, but it's the type of thing that gets spread in rumors about one's political enemies, or among other people's about the Dynasty, but it's not an actual accepted norm. Pretty much like Ancient Rome in that regard. We have the IDEA of these horrible depraved orgies, but the context of their recording means that even if they did happen they were a violation of social taboos, not an actual accepted norm.

    Anything closer than first-cousins is going to be taboo in the Realm. Not that it doesn't ever happen - but it will be viewed scandalously.

    That said, I think marriage between cousins of different households in the same Great House are relatively common.


    CAVEAT: That someone with enough power can freely sexually abuse those they wield power over and get away with it, is unfortunately found all through human society and history, and the Realm isn't going to be any different in that regard.
