It seems that capturing them and using Three Fold Binding of the Heart on them is something that a lot of beings with Celestial Sorcery might be interested in. I imagine that most DBs, once they get some age, learn to be bad asses.

          This is the sort of complex speculative math that gets really variable, really fast. First, we don't exactly have a good grasp on just how many dragon-blooded there even are. One in 10,000 people is a number handed out in places, but it is not clear what the population of Creation is going to be, especially since 3e makes the map a lot bigger and adds considerably more fertile temperate areas (and retcons big chunks of 'the north' back into the temperate zone). Personally I like the 700-800 million range, so that there's 1 celestial exalt for every million people. That yields 70,000-80,000 dragon-blooded (not sure how many Exigents/Getinmians/Liminals yet).

          If we split the difference and go with 75,000 dragon-blooded, then the question is how many are essence 6+, correct? That depends on how you calculate background XP, what proportion of dragon-blooded survive to the relevant age, and what proportion of dragon-blooded start with essence 3 instead of essence 2. Also it additionally depends on the confounding factors of 1. Sorcery and 2. Martial Arts Mastery. Both of those can prolong lifespan considerably, inducing an unusual feedback loop in that the most powerful dragon-blooded are those most likely to live the longest. Ragara Myrrun, if he doesn't explode, isn't staring down mortality for a very long time.

          Honestly, in a 2e context, the answer is 'enough to make the Deathlords think that immediate world conquest isn't happening even with Abyssals and no empress while also intimidating the Lunars that the same thing is true.' Given that, even if only one dragon-blooded in one hundred makes it to essence 6 that would give you quite literally thousands of them at that level and that that would probably supply the Realm and other powers with the necessary elders to hold back the celestial exalts, I think that's a defensible ratio.

              Something like 1/4 to a 1/3 of Dynasts die before making new little Dynasts (mostly from punching above their weight-class while adventuring in sword brotherhoods, with calculated political inhumations filling the number out). So we can ignore them, since this will happen long before Essence 6 becomes a thing. You can probably figure that die-off climbs significantly higher for non-Dynasts, who have to contend with the same threats but less access to the beat-em-up toys of the Realm - though I would also assume Lookshy DBs fall much closer to Dynasts on that scale. Then you have to consider the realities of Realm politics. Raising essence needs a month per dot in meditation and seclusion past E3. This is not a situation that lends itself to maintaining your position in Realm politics, much less improving it (and Lookshy is probably just as cut-throat, if not as blatantly antagonistic). E3 to E4 is a good investment for your House or Gente, because it provides more power and Charm access. But E5 for mid-level schlubs is going to be expensive in terms of time off and effectively create another rival for House/Gente control. So, you average House/Gentes leader is going to work most underlings too hard to be able to increase Essence beyond 4, excepting groomed heirs and other favorites - and that would still be dependent upon not losing political power in the downtime cycle. The IF probably offers more chance for Essence advancement. Realistically, probably only 10% or so are going to ever hit E5. But, those 10% are going to also have the time and age to keep pushing, so I would figure most would hit E6 (though political realities probably push that off until closer to 200 years, rather than at 100 years).

                  IIRC, the books say there are 20,000 DBs. 10,000 (approximately) in the Realm, and 10,000 (approximately) outside it: Lookshy, random outcastes, local ruling dyasties, etc.

                  If we split the difference and go with 75,000 dragon-blooded, then the question is how many are essence 6+, correct? That depends on how you calculate background XP, what proportion of dragon-blooded survive to the relevant age, and what proportion of dragon-blooded start with essence 3 instead of essence 2. Also it additionally depends on the confounding factors of 1. Sorcery and 2. Martial Arts Mastery. Both of those can prolong lifespan considerably, inducing an unusual feedback loop in that the most powerful dragon-blooded are those most likely to live the longest. Ragara Myrrun, if he doesn't explode, isn't staring down mortality for a very long time.
                  All Dragon-blood (well, all Exalts) start with Essence 2. Some have spent their time before you might actually play them, raising their essence, while others have spent it training their abilities or attributes. But if you played a game where you were all 14 year olds who literally exalted yesterday, you'd have Essence 2 (and less abilities, attributes and charms than the character gen rules: those are for people who've finished their schooling, so have probably been exalted for years already).
                  Secondly, neither Martial Arts or Sorcery prolong your lifespan. The things that prolong your lifespan are: money (for anagathics), resistance (ie being healthy), and that charm for extending your lifespan (it's either Resistance 6 or Medicine 6, I forget). And not doing anything dangerous (like, say, constant kung fu duels, or messing around with demons... but saying that, both of those are safer than being a soldier).
                  They are, however, things that encourage you to take the time to meditate and raise your essence, whereas if you're a bureaucrat it's really not all that important.

                  Ballpark figure, I'd say there are 200 DBs with Essence 6 in the Realm (1 out of 50 DBs), and 75% of them are Monks (and therefore, actually very good at personal combat).

                  I should say, however, having played high-essence DBs, I don't think there's a colossal difference between Essence 5 and Essence 6. The power level doesn't increase exponentially. At essence 5, if you're a master of war, you can already nuke battlefields, and you need to be Essence 7 to be better at it. When I was playing a very, very, long-running game, we got over 1000xp, we were all Essence 5, and I (and a few of the others I think) wished I could get to Essence 6. I wasn't thinking "wow, my charms will be massively super-powerful compared to what I have now if I was Essence 6!", I was thinking "I have every attribute and ability I care about at 5, I wish I could buy the ones my character should be best at to 6. so I can differentiate his specialities again".

                  If I was a Deathlord, sure the small number of Essence 6 DBs is scary, but I'm far, far more worried about the thousand or so Essence 4 DBs, because they will make mincemeat out of my 5 Abyssals who've only been Exalted for 5 years.

                  (I should also say, fighting a 150 year old DB is scary, but it's less because he might be Essence 6 and more the fact that he has tons of charms and quite possibly spells and celestial-tier martial arts, not to mention more artefacts than your average Solar circle)
