        Originally posted by The MG View Post
        It was tailor-made to justify Terrestrial rule, control unruly gods, and keep the Anathema in check. The rest is just the culture of the Realm.
        And it justifies that rule by force. Immaculate doctrine doesn't say "Dragon-Bloods should be your rulers, but they will sit quietly on their Blessed Isle until you accept them." It says "The Dragon-Blooded are the rightful rulers of Creation. If you disagree with this, and want to rule yourselves, or not give them your property, they can kill you." It may not literally say "they have the right to kill you," but it doesn't NEED to, in much the same way that laws in Russia that outlaw "gay propaganda," don't need to say "we hate and want to oppress gays."

        It was redesigned to justify all of this, because Kejak cut a deal with Her Redness to use his Immaculate Order to help her assume control of all Creation, and keep the Wyld Hunt going. She gets to run an expansionist empire, and the Immaculate Order will hold her up as the rightful ruler of Creation, and justify her acts.
