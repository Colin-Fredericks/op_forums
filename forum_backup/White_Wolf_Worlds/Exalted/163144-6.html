                    Originally posted by Fata-Ku View Post
                    New Charm: Belittled, Betrayed and Becoming
                    Sidereal Socialize, prereq Cash and Murder Games.

                    This Charm is empowered by a moment in which the Sidereal causes one person to be put above another in a social context. This could be by an insult keenly felt, arrogant commands, humiliation, betrayal, or more. Subtle Seers may find ways to make others belittle each other; if the Sidereal had a hand in it, this Charm may still be used. The result is that the Exalt uses the sudden imbalance of fate to her own benefit, wrapping up the strands and swallowing it like a spider would its meal --- she gains a dot of temporary Appearance that lasts as long as the characters involved in the belittlement are still feeling its effects on their emotional states. The Storyteller is encouraged to have the temporary dot last at least a scene. This Charm is Stackable, but each stacked use must be a new form of belittlement.

                                Fractal Forest Web
                                Lunar Territory Charm (boundary marking meditation as pre-req)

                                The lunar tracks and traces his territory in a web-like pattern, sectionning off parcels of it's area into smaller and smaller pieces with thin lines of essence that only the Lunar can percieve. Whenever a creature enters this web, it is made to believe that it has entered the Bordermarches, Middlemarches or the Deep Wyld, at the Lunar's discretion, as an illusion effect (she acquires illusory mutations and may be targetted by Shaping Combat and Fantasms). As long as the target is within the web, the Lunar has complete control over her perception of the environment, including flora, fauna, weather, distance, the passage of time and percieved position. If the Lunar has any Graces, he may use them for Shaping Combat as long as the illusion holds.

                                This can include making a small empty ice-cave seem like an infinite burning desert in the middle of a sand-storm, populated by teleporting scorpions where a day lasts an eternity and cacti grow in mere seconds.

                                        Seven Hearts Reliquary: Abyssal Integrity Charm, that upgrades Immortal Malevolence Enslavement. It allows the Abyssal to select an alternate "respawn" point by sacrificing six mortals, then cutting out his own heart and placing all six in a soulsteel urn. As long as the urn contains the seven still-beating hearts, he can resurrect either at its location or the Mouth of the Void, and even spirit-killing attacks only delay his return rather than preventing it. It should be noted that the Abyssal has no way of knowing whether the urn is safe without actually going and checking on it.

                                                        Originally posted by The MG View Post
                                                        New Charm: Four Hundred Heartbeats Curse
                                                        Labyrinth Circle Necromancy: All men's days are numbered. The target of this spell knows this more acutely than most. The literal four-hundred beats of this spell--roughly a scene's worth of action--are all she has left from the moment the Necromancer afflicts her, and in her skull the countdown echoes. When her time is up, she immediately [insert mechanic here to be likely-to-certain death for a mortal, but not make this a killswitch against any Exalt without an appropriate counterspell]

                                                        Despite this--and despite the name--the spell has its advantages. The target of the spell may slip into a deathlike state at will, freely aware of her surroundings or in a deep slumber. Her body does not naturally degrade, scavengers will leave her be, and she does not require food or water or air. Most importantly, her heart does not beat. In this way, a victim of this curse may in fact outlive the one who cast it upon them. More than one necromancer has thought to cast a messenger into the future with this spell, but only a couple have thought to use it on themselves--it is an unfortunate but useful discovery by these poor souls that the spell cannot be cast and dispelled by and upon the same Necromancer at once.

                                                            Since the last post didn't give a Charm name, I'll take the last one in the thread untaken: Crack the Setting Sun

                                                            Solar Lore

                                                            The Copper Spider strikes his mind with the Essence of pure thought, cracking it in twain and allowing her to focus her mind on multiple tasks at once. While Essence to this Charm is committed, the Solar may perform two purely mental tasks simultaneously, ignoring any flurry penalties if necessary. She may carefully watch her surroundings while planning for the next day's battle. She may mentally write a speech while drawing up plans for an aqueduct. This Charm is Stackable; each additional activation splits her mind again, allowing an additional task she may focus on without penalty.
