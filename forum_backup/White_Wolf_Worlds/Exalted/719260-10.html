    I'd like some feedback on one I brewed up this afternoon.

    Tyrant's Flame
    The draconic will of the Desert Tyrant burns within the Sorcerer, an inferno of energy that shapes sorcerous motes with it's presence alone.
    Once per story, the Sorcerer may roll (Temporary Willpower+Occult), forcing their will onto Creation to gather sorcerous. For each success, the Tyrant Flame within his heart feeds on the sorcerous motes gathered, channeling the amplified essence and burning it onto the Sorcerer's own flesh which appears as bands of runes, one each per success.
    Once per scene, the Sorcerer may reflexively consume a band of tattoos, instantly receiving a surge of (Essence*2) Sorcery Motes.

            Shaping Ritual

            Having dedicated themselves fully to the practice of their martial art, the practitioner has seen into the mystical truths her style embodies. After a night's sleep the Martial Artist may roll (Willpower+[Martial Arts Style]) while practicing the katas of their art and gain a sorcerous mote for each success.             [Style] Enlightenment

                A spell, an archtype, and a few shaping rituals
                Yes. The inane and pointless HTML for emphasis is extremely important to both the spell and the archetype.
                Explosion!(1wp, 20sm, Celestial Circle Sorcery)
                Keywords: Decisive Only, Perilous, Terrestral
                Duration: Instant

                A Molten Vortex of plasma churning in a sea of sorcerous dust and motes, a spiral galaxy that forms high above the Sorcerer. A promulgation of the Rules of Nature, the burning name of the destruction incarnate, ancient sorcery drawn from the Five Poles of Creation. The Hammer of Eternity descends from it's core, a star born of fire and destruction, the semblance of Sol Invictus in his Glorious Radiance that descends upon the singularity of inevitability: Explosion!

                A miniature sun surges forth from the galaxy of sorcerous essence towards a single point in space within Long range of the Sorcerer's sight, rolling (Occ. + Perc. + Essence) as a decisive attack. The star embodies the pure destructive will of the Sorcerer, with base damage equal to (her Initiative + current temporary Willpower + Essence), and resets her to (base Initiative + Essence) on a successful attack. The star strikes in massive area of effect, incinerating everything in a sphere within Medium range of the point struck and is unparriable with mundane weapons which melt on contact with the star. Battlegroups are especially vulnerable to this spell and suffer -2 their defense value. Each target that takes at least 5 damage to their health track is ignited in an inferno (environmental hazard equal to Lava, p. 230 of Core) that englufs him all terrain within close range of him, and burns until the next day.

                A Sorcerer who knows Explosion! as her control spell may expend her entire periphery essence pool towards the attack, adding 1 die to the raw damage of the spell for every 2m of essence spent.

                Archetype: 8-Ignition Way | Darker than Black and Blacker than Dark: Brilliance that Burns with the Fury of the Crimson Demon Clan– The Path of Explosions

                Shaping Rituals:

                Flames and explosions dance with the Crimson Demon Clan as if they were sisters. As she gives life to and shapes new infernos into the world as she walks the Path of Explosions, her conflagrations breathe new essence into her. Once per round, the Sorcerer may draw up to (Essence) sorcery motes from any bonfire or greater ignition she has caused. These sorcery motes last until sunrise and may only be spent towards casting fire or explosive based spells.

                Those of the Crimson Demon Clan are naturally inclined towards unnecessarily longwinded spell-chants. When the Sorcerer stunts a shape sorcery action to cast a fire or explosion spell, that spell's cost is reduced by (1 + 2*Stunt Rating) sorcery motes until the end of the round.

                The sorcerers who follow the Path of Explosions are forever changed by the power and warmth that flows through them when the shape the essence of creation into a spell, harnessing it for their explosions. Their idea of a proper introduction however, lacks the same sort of impressiveness. Once per story, the Sorcerer may stunt her over the top and theatrical introduction to a unknown group, gathering (3*Stunt Rating) sorcery motes that last until the story ends.

                Sorcerers who adopt the 8-Ignition Way | Darker than Black and Blacker than Dark: Brilliance that Burns with the Fury of the Crimson Demon Clan– The Path of Explosions share the same merits as those who make a Pact with an Ifrit Lord.

                        This is kind of weird, but hey.

                        SURROGATE OF KAL'KAYA
                        Kal'Kaya is a Second Circle Demon with a strange obsession with birth and motherhood. She appears as a teenage girl, many-armed and bearing the swell of pregnancy, her eyes the slot-pupiled gaze of goats and her feet like those of a great landbound predator bird. The sorcerer gained their power through a pact with the Barren Mother; they allowed her to use them as a conduit for the birth of one of her children, and in turn she has granted them sorcerous enlightenment. They gain a supernatural Major Principle toward protecting pregnant women, new mothers, and babies; if this is reduced below Major intensity, they take a -4 penalty to all sorcerous rolls until the Principle resumes Major or higher intensity. They also gain a mundane Minor Tie of some positive nature toward Kal'Kaya, but this has no bearing on their sorcery in particular.

                        Shaping Ritual: Miracle of Birth
                        Kal'Kaya's obsession with procreation allows her surrogates to draw on it for sorcerous power. Once per day, when the sorcerer contributes to a procreative act, they may roll (Charisma + Occult) with (Essence) automatic successes, each success being a sorcerous mote held in a pool whose maximum capacity is 10 sm. The definition of "contributing to a procreative act" is fairly broad; while becoming pregnant or impregnating another person obviously counts, it is also valid to play an important part in the procreation of others---for example, inspiring two lovers to have procreative sex. Serving as a midwife also counts. A sorcerer who is themselves pregnant gets to double up, so to speak, in that they may make this roll when the child is conceived and when the child is born (the tradeoff obviously being the difficulties of pregnancy).
                        Participating in nonprocreative sex gives a reduced benefit; Kal'Kaya appreciates the symbol of the act, but not as much as the real thing. This allows the same roll, but with no automatic successes and 10s do not double on it.
                        The motes thus gained last for one week.

                        Associated Merits
                        Demonchild Familiar (•••): Kal'Kaya sees fit to leave her child with their surrogate parent; the demonchild functions as a three-dot Familiar. They have a Major Tie of love to their surrogate parent and a Defining Tie of love to Kal'Kaya. They share their surrogate parent's Major Principle of protection toward pregnant woman, new mothers, and babies.
                        Infernal Nobility (•): As in the core; First Circle Demons have a Minor Tie of terrified awe, while Second and Third Circle Demons have a positive Minor Tie of respect or something similar.
                        Matchmaker's Eyes (••): When making rolls to see if characters have Ties of romantic and/or erotic love for one another, the surrogate gets their Essence in automatic successes.

                        If this is unbalanced or anything, I'd love to see what people think would make it more balanced. This is really just a weird idea I had driving home, but thought it could be hammered into something interesting. Ideas for other shaping rituals for this Initiation would be cool to hear as well.

                                                        Here's a write-up of the concept I posted earlier. Please let me know what you think. :-3

                                                        __________________________________________________ ______________________

                                                        Pact with an Elder Greenmaw

                                                        As greenmaws age, they become stronger, wiser, and more keenly attuned to the flow of Essence. Some of these elders may be willing to pass on their insights to those who swear to revere and protect nature.

                                                        Shaping Rituals

                                                        The sorcerer borrows power from the profusion of flora around her. Whenever she begins a scene amidst foliage or other plant life in sufficient size or quantity to provide cover or concealment, she gains three sorcerous motes, which last until the end of the scene and can be spent toward any spell she casts.

                                                        The sorcerer's patron offers her power in exchange for her service in protecting and maintaining the life and beauty of the realm. At the end of a project where she cares for plants, the sorcerer may make a prayer roll (Wits + Survival or an appropriate Craft) with a difficulty commensurate to the scale of the project: difficulty 5 for a project on par with maintaining a garden, difficulty 3 for a farm, and difficulty 1 for a forest or similarly large region. Success grants her sorcerous motes equal to (her Essence + extra successes on the roll). These motes last for the duration of the story, and can be spent toward any spell she casts. She cannot pray for power more than once a day, and receiving new motes replaces any granted by past prayers.

                                                        So long as the sorcerer abides by an ascetic lifestyle where she does not consume or use products made from animals or from inorganic materials, she receives (Stamina) sorcerous motes after a night of sleep, plus additional sorcerous mote for every point of deprivation penalties she suffers. These sorcerous motes last until she next sleeps, and can be spent towards any spell she casts. Additionally, once per story, she may spend a scene in meditation to unlock (Essence + Stamina) sorcerous motes which last the duration of the story. Compromising the ascetic lifestyle of this shaping ritual causes the sorcerer to lose all motes granted by it, and denies her its benefits for the remainder of the story.

                                                        Notes: I'm not entirely satisfied with the first or last ritual, as the first (copied from Mara's third) lacks a secondary condition under which the sorcerer can gather sorcerous motes, and the requirements for the last are a bit nebulous. I was trying for something like a vegan lifestyle combined with a DnD druid's oath not to use metal, but I'm not sure if that's appropriate; greenmaws themselves frequently eat animals in the early stages of their lives.

                                                        Merits

                                                        - Brimming with Life (Merit ••••): This Merit functions as the Giant Merit, except that it is not considered Innate. If the sorcerer already has the Giant Merit, she may add (Stamina/2) in non-Charm dice when attempting feats of strength. However, the sorcerer becomes so infused with life that she gains plant-like traits, such as a pine-like aroma, brilliant emerald skin, or flowers in her hair. This imposes an additional -4 penalty to disguise rolls and may cause people to perceive her as a Wyld mutant. In dubious compensation, possessing this Merit allows the sorcerer to purchase additional supernatural Merits that are appropriate to plants, e.g. bark-like skin or a poisoned body.

                                                        - Green Warden (Merit •): Wilderness gods and wood elementals recognize the sorcerer as a protector of their domain, and are treated as having a Minor Tie of respect towards her, or some variation suitable to their individual natures.

                                                        - Woodland Stride (Merit ••): This Merit functions as Tempered by the Elements in jungle and forest terrain. If the sorcerer already has this Merit, she gains an additional (Essence/2) non-Charm dice on Survival rolls in these areas.

                                                            The Art of the Sword

                                                            The Art of the Sword was developed by Demon Hunters and craftsmen in the far North. Using an alloy of feather-steel and powdered Blue Jade, the apprentice spends hours within the cold caves on the slopes of Ascension, forging the blade without flame. Those who fail this test invariably are possessed by their lower soul and become horrible, twisted monsters. For those who pass, they are brought up to Ascenion, where they spend several weeks training in the arts of swordsmanship, and learning the mannerisms of the various enemies of Creation. Finally, before they complete their apprenticeship they are brought beyond the edge of the world, to a floating island. They are left there, to learn the final secret of their Art, meditating on a massive Old Realm character of Blue Jade that rests amidst the island. Within a week, those who's minds are too weak will have flung themselves off the edge of the island, driven insane by revelations they cannot understand; however, this is a rare event as most who come this far have cultivated both mind and body to incredible extremes.

                                                            Most individuals who leave the island have developed Stormwind Rider or Cirrus Skiff as their control spell, but there are occasionally those who instead develop the Infallible Messenger. The Order then divides them between the Dervishes, itnerants, riding their storms to wherever they are needed, the Lords, who often make pacts with various elementals of the air and travel in slow, majestic processions across the North, waging terrible war on their foes, and the Eyes, who use their Messengers to co-ordinate the Order in its ceaseless quest to rid the North of the Undead, vicious demons and fae. Sometimes, they will even fight rogue gods. But one thing they all have in common is a strange serenity.

                                                            Shaping Rituals

                                                            The Swordsman has sealed his Po soul within his sword. It feeds on those slain by the sword, and upon striking a killing blow, the wielder may gain 3+(Wielder's essence + Victim's essence) Sorcerous Motes which last until the next sunrise. However, if the wielder slays more than 5 creatures this way, the Po soul grows violent, and immediately expends all gathered Sorcerous Motes, creating a Bonefire Hazard that lasts one turn and is centered on the Swordsman, reaching out to short range. This Shaping Ritual allows the purchase of the Flying Sword Merit below.

                                                            The Swordsman takes joy in practicing the sword. By performing a mystical sword kata each morning and on every night of the full moon, he attains a deeper understanding of swordsmanship and how it relates to the world around him. While wielding the sword, the sorcerer adds his melee/2(round up) to as many as (Melee) Shape Sorcery Actions per scene. This Ritual is gained for free alongside the previous one.

                                                            The Swordsman has sealed his entire soul inside the sword, becoming an abomination outcast by the Order. By drawing on the vital force of whichever unfortunate person happens to wield such a cursed blade(easily recognisable by the colour of the steel, which becomes pure white), the sorcerer within the sword may gain sorcerous motes equal to the wielders permanent willpower although this reduces the wielders permanent willpower by 1. This Shaping Ritual requires the purchase of the Flying Sword Merit and the Sword-Body Forging Merit, but is gained for free.

                                                            Merits

                                                            Flying Sword(3-dot familiar): The Swordsman seals his Po within the sword, giving it an animating force. The sword may fly and make melee attacks using its wielders Wits+Melee or Int+Melee. However, if the Swordsman fails to roll even a single success, the blade will refuse to obey, and must be picked up again by hand and flung in the air before it will fly again.

                                                            The True Sword(4-dot Merit): The Order teaches its members how to strike with swords in such a manner that they may strike even invisible and intangible creatures. Although they suffer penalties for blindness as normal, those who possess this merit may expend 2 Sorcerous motes to strike immaterial beings with their attacks.

                                                            Sword-Body Forging(5-dot Merit): The Swordsman truly becomes one with his sword, commencing a second forging process, much like his first in the caves below Ascension. As the forging progresses, the Swordsman slowly finds his body absorbed by the blade as his soul comes back together within the blade. At the end of the forging process, the sword becomes a sentient being with the memories of its wielder. However, it also gains a Defining Principle to slay any demon, ghost or fae it comes across. However, it is also immortal unless completely destroyed, slowly regrowing from a core of white diamond. The grip and hilt grow first, followed by the blade.

                                                            Only one Exalt has ever attempted the Sword-Body Forging. The Lunar's Exaltation remained inside its body a few days more, which did not assimilate with the blade. The creature wreaked havoc across the north, driven mad by the division of Soul and Exaltation, before the Order bright the creature down with the help of the Wyld Hunt. However, this is not to say that fusing one's soul with the sword is impossible for the Exalted; such limits after all, can be overcome. However, unless the blade is made entirely of magical materials, it will not contain the Exaltation, and the use of Charms still requires a suitable body.
