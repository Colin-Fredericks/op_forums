                        Changes found:

                        Changes in the fluff :
                        Raksi description of servering human flesh to guests of honor and being called out when she served a child to one of her apprentecies.
                        There is a new fungus forest near Mahalanka.
                        Raksi has a new adherent; Kerrich Storm-Favored - he's a hedonist
                        Some new Lunar Dominions waspfolk in the south
                        Sidebar about Necromancy and Solar Bond added,
                        Sidebar about Giant proprortions added.
                        Sidebar about talking animals and form taking charms added

                        Lunar Chargen. Raising non favored/caste attributes for Lunars with caste are changed from 5bp to 4bp
                        Carapace is changed, hypermobility and imposing features cost less.
                        All Lunars now get 4 specialties at character gen, not 3.

                        No Moons's shadow penumbra now gives enemies a -2 visual penalty to hit them.

                        New Charms:
                        Beast-Form Empowerment (buffs HBR)
                        Silver Collar Binding (buffs Insidious Lunar Transformation)
                        Devourer of Form (buffs Insidious Lunar Transformation)
                        Soul’s Eclipse Unity (better emotional connection to your Solar Mate)

                        Charm changes in no particular order:
                        Finding the Needle's Eye now just gives -1 Defence to the enemy instead of ignoring certain bonuses
                        Striking Mospid Method rerolls higher of 3/Essence failures
                        Relentless Lunar Fury 5m instead of 3m 1 wp
                        Deathless Fury Unleashed changed to E3 and not having to be repurchased
                        Unchained Monster Death-Rage added a second clause
                        Halting the Scarlet Flow added out of combat clause
                        Invincible Beast-Warrior Empowerment 8m 1 wp instead of 15m 1 wp, Perilous Keyword taken off, it also no longer applies to dice added by command actions
                        Soul Beyond Shape is much cheaper.
                        Mountainous Spirit Expression gets bonus soak/damage/lifting
                        Glance-Oration Technique 3m instead of 4m
                        Many-Voiced Mockingbird Attitude (mostly just clarifying) also totemic (Charisma)
                        Angry Rhino Charge less prereqand also no mote cost to gain the initiative and the automatic success is taken out.
                        Ox body two -2 and two -4s at stamina 5 instead of one -1 and one -2 and two -4
                        Striking Mospid Method - rerolling higer of Essence or 3 failed dice , instead of Essence.
                        Secret Sense Intuition: "On a failed roll, the Lunar becomes aware only that someone is hidden nearby" - is removed
                        Flowing Body Evasion changed to once per scene with a reset function.
                        Last Warrior's Unity has a damage nerf in tiny/miniscule forms.
                        Moose soak and hardness nerfed.


                        p.277
                        Rampaging God-Beast Puissance is now simply God-Beast Puissance and costs 4m instead of 5m

                        p.292
                        Insidious Shadow Ruse has been folded into The Spider’s Trapdoor

                        Quicksilver Legend Evolution has gone from 15m, 1wp to 1wp

                            Originally posted by Epitome View Post

                            The Tell exists more for flavor than as an actual restriction for Lunars (because the Lunars are inspired in part by strange witches and other creatures in folklore who would always be recognizable by a distinctive trait).

                            Unless your campaign is 100% about disguises (e.g. you're pretending you're the Scarlet Empress to take control of the Realm), it doesn't really break anything to just houserule it out.
                            Lunars are far from the only shapeshifters in the setting to have that kind of feature, though. Bloody Hands have one hand that's exactly what their name suggests (and which can be only temporarily covered, as the blood eventually seeps out of any glove), Kireeki always has a bunch of tentacles, neomah always look like neomah versions of the subject, and Mara always has deer legs. Just shapeshifter stuff, as you do.

                                        I've just become aware of the fact that the wording for how Raksi welcomes political rivals has been changed to a form that more heavily implies that by the time people are being served on a platter, they're already dead (even if it at least gives the impression of a wider age range).

                                        I've got mixed feelings about this. On the one hand I liked the sense before that serving live babies was something so exaggerated that it made the idea that this was done as a psychological tactic more overt, even playful. On the other, I guess a point that definite murder is involved at least makes it more consistent with things like the Dragon Blooded vivisection.

                                        And I suppose that... it seemed unlikely that anybody who was served a baby would actually go through with it (but that does seem like it would more effectively serve the purpose of throwing them off balance and letting Raksi affect offence), whereas this form seems more accommodating to visitors that would hold their nose, close their eyes and stomach it, because at least they're not the actual killer and it's not as if refusal will bring them back.
