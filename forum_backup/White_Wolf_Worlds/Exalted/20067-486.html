                    [Ex2] When worn beneath clothing or armour, a Perronele can protect the wearer from any attack it is aware. This adds the demon's soak atop the wearer's own; although the perronele takes damage from attacks which penetrate its soak. ​The Perronele's soak is 1L/2B, to 6L/12B using Sheathing the Material Form. (Hardness: 5L/5B).

                    ​Sheathing the Material Form is 1mote per +1L, +B/2, or 5 mote to maximum.
