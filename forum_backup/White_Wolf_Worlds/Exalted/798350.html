    Exigent of the gemstone god

    01-02-2016, 03:30 PM
    So I had this idea today of making an exalt based on the God of gemstones, maybe include coloured light in there too because stained glass. Or maybe add in non-gem but still precious stones.

    Anyway I've been trying to think up appropriate charms for them, certainly some involving hearth stones, but I'm not super mechanically savvy and the forum always surprises me with interesting takes on things.

    I'm thinking maybe a charm that gives resistances to elemental damage/hardship, like an ice storm or a sandstorm, but the cost of the charm is reduced if you have an appropriately aspected hearthstone. Not sure how to do this without making a player devote all his merit and BPs at chargen into getting as many hearthstones as possible though.

      Well, here's a question. Who is the gemstone god in question? While those charms are definitely legitimate (and creative) for a gemstone god's exigent, I've been thinking recently that when I design an Exigent, I am not designing an Exigent of a river god, for instance, but rather an Exigent of Kara-Kim, the Mother Goddess of the River Ammon, which floods and retreats on a regular basis and is associated with birth and motherhood. I wouldn't make an Exigent of a war god, but rather an Exigent of Barsooth Bloody-Ax, the berserker god of the Olar people who was once a god of a certain sort of polar bear before being promoted, for example.

      So I would start by designing a god of gemstones that interests you, then proceed from there, were I you.

      All that I write but don't cite is simply my perspective, colored by my experiences and beliefs. I extrapolate a lot, too, so don't take it too seriously. :P

                      Right context. So really long story short this would be the god of gemstones from the eastern (small e) mountains in the nation that the PCs are in right now. The god was worshiped by the people who lived on, and mostly under, the mountain. Not their patron god, but the gemstones there were the source of their wealth, so while she didn't ravage the place with storms and quakes when angered, she also was kind of the reason anybody cared about their little place. The province actually has a history of leveraging that wealth to the point of trying to split from the kingdom, and has been successful in the past.

                      Either way, currently the biggest and best way to pay tribute to this god is by using the special gems there to make elaborate stained glass sculptures and windows through which light shines into the underground areas where people often live, using either mirrors or other tricks to penetrate deep underground. The entire kingdom is ramping up for war and the mountain province is being called upon to provide all the support they can, which usually means lending their purchased mercenary armies to the war effort. In this case, however, the province feels that they are in great danger of being targeted and pillaged. The gemstone god there likes the people and their tribute, especially since the invading country has a habit of pounding gods into submission. To this end she's exalted one of the miners who she'd grown somewhat infatuated with over the years to be her avatar, both on the field of battle and in the stateroom, to make sure her interests and the interests of her favoured peoples are protected.


                      So considering that, suggestions?

                                      This turned into a three hour project. Here you go.


                                      Dak-Tuliphos the Many Faceted is a series of divine contradictions. Daktuli (as is sometimes used among the non-priests) is displayed as either a squat, muscular man or a pale, narrow shouldered woman, He Or She is considered both exuberant but also hidden and obscured, rich and ostentatious but miserly. Various stories conflict and contradict each other, and all are said to be equally true, for as it is said in the land of Cornath where He Or She is from, "The Facet is Not the Gem".

                                      Dak-Tuliphos (which means "Engraver of Gems" in the old, forgotten tongue of this land) both desires and disdains worship, for He Or She is fickle and capricious but also earnest by turns. However, all forms of belief agree on a few key points: He Or She holds material wealth as the highest good, and His Or Her favor is given most when wealth is gained, lost, traded, found, stolen, transferred, or spent carelessly. Dak-Tuliphos is also always shown to covet the gems, jewels, and precious stones of the ground above metals, pearls, and jade. The Many Faceted One is also a patron of the arts, so long as those arts involve His Or Her precious gems in some way: a chalice of ruby is just as prized as a dramatic performance involving a jewel-emblazoned skull. Those who (claim to) have been spoken to often present a rare and expensive jewel as sign that they are chosen and favored by the Engraver.

                                      Of late, the priest-caste of Carnath (full name, Carnath-What-Lies-Beyond-the-Veil-and-Under-Hills) have become hypocrites, they do not seek to move great amounts of the precious stones as they did in ages passed, they are loathe to give up the gems and seek to hoard, sell, corner and inflate markets. Their offerings are token at best, and they haven't commissioned a new Great Work for Dak-Tuliphos in a generation, content as they are to sit on their laurels and reuse the expensive props of ages past.

                                      However, a change is coming. Stones of late have increasingly been found to bear a unique impurity- unusual color patterns and "flecks" of black Jet and Tourmaline have been found in all deposits, regardless of distance, depth, or material, and the frequency which they appear is increasing rapidly. To date, only the priest-caste and the High Jeweler know of it, with the Chief Gem Cutter and the Stone Cutters Union beginning to grow curious as to why their shipments have become smaller and smaller. Deep in mine 55 (Amethyst), a strong shouldered woman begins her day with pick and shovel in hand. She brushes a hand across her pale brow, and adjusts the straps covering her squat, muscular body. Amber of the Deepening Heart is eager to meet quota early so she can finish her small sculpture in time to make an offering to Dak-Tuliphos on tomorrow's ritual night. She looks at a promising vein and readies herself, focusing her orange-brown eyes in the dim light. It so dim, in fact, the other workers have never noticed that Amber's beautiful eyes have started to have little black flecks in them.
                                      ----------------------------------------------------
                                      Amber of the Deepening Heart
                                      Exigent of Dak-Tuliphos, the Many Faceted.

                                      Stonebreaker, Pickax of the Engraver God
                                      Artifact 3 Pickax, Medium Weapon: A symbol of Daktuli, this odd choice for a weapon is nevertheless effective when needed- In Amber's hands it gains the property to pierce through metal as if it were no more than ordinary stone. While in possession of Stonebreaker, Amber may move ordinary, unworked stone as though it weighed nothing. She can lift, throw or otherwise move worked stone (such as a column) as though it weighed half as much as it does. Gems of any size are weightless to Amber while she is attuned and in possession of Stonebreaker.
                                      Special: On a decisive attack, Stonebreaker ignores Hardness equal to twice Amber's Essence Level.
                                      Special: In Amber's hands, Stonebreaker is terrifyingly effective at its intended purpose of mining. Amber can pulverize stone in front of her at about half the speed she can walk normally.


                                      Intrinsic Charms of the Engraver
                                      Jeweller's Cunning: Amber instantly knows the value of any and all gems, jewels, and works of art primarily composed of jewels. She can also identify all gems and jewels by sight, touch, or even sound (such as a light tap), and recognizes even the best of forgeries. Additionally, Amber has an automatic two successes on rolls to appraise a jewelled item (such as a predominantly gold chalice adorned with rubies), or on Lore checks to recall the placement of jewels on artifacts, artworks, people, etc...

                                      Miner's Insight: Amber can sense the presence of precious stones around her. She can easily find a gem vein withing one mile of her (even one mile straight down) and has a vague sense of how large it is (enough for ten miners working for ten days). If there is no vein within one mile, she instead feels the call of the closest vein to her, but only in a vague sense (i.e. 4-5 days walk Northwest). Amber also can sense cut stones in a similar but limited fashion. She is aware of any gems for sale, in storage, displayed, locked up, or lost within 100 feet of her, provided they are still and not on the body or luggage of a person or beast. Amber can sense these as though they made a music that only she can hear, so she can approximate their size, number, and location with ease. If she wants to identify them based on this feeling, roll Wits+Lore, the difficulty is determined by the storyteller. An easy check would be finding a chest of sapphires behind a fake wall, a hard check would be determining what type single small gem has dropped in a sewer beneath her. Note: the roll is only to sense what kind of gem Amber feels. She does not need to roll to sense them or get a general idea of the volume of gems near her.

                                      Gemcutter's Vindication: A fairly minor ability that Amber has yet to use, any nonmagical gem that Amber or her warpick touches shatters if she wills it. On magical weapons, armor, and other such artifacts of gem, Amber has +2 successes to her sundering attempt to overcome hardness. (Note: This bonus applies only on an artifact made primarily of gems, such as a diamond sword or a Fae dreamship made of carbuncle. An breastplate decorated in emerald is not affect, although Amber could break the gemstones out of pettiness or spite, this doesn't apply the +2 bonus).
                                      Special: At Essence 2 this charm also reproduces the Solar charm Object Strengthening Touch (p. 295), however it only applies to gem-based items.

                                      Diamontaire's Opus: Amber excels at using gems while crafting. Any projects which are predominantly composed of gems (such as a brooch, a jewel encrusted bracelet, a diamond bladed katana) takes only half the regular amount of time to produce and awards additional xp as if Amber had the Intimacy: Use Jewels to Make a Project. Just as with Gemcutter's Vindication, the item must be mainly made of jewels for the bonus to apply. Amber can't add a few diamonds to her saddle-making project to speed it up. However, she can use this ability if her project is to decorate an existing work with gems, or replace cracked gems on a statue, chalice, etc..
                                      Special: So happy is Dak-Tuliphos with the act of creation that Amber recovers one willpower when she finishes a craft with this charm.

                                      Magpie's Covetousness: (Unlocks at Essence 3) Amber has a desire for all gems, as such she can sense and track people by the gems that they wear and carry on their person. She can sense a gem** hidden in someone's boot, luggage, or other such container at close range without rolling, provided she is aware of the person carrying it. No roll is required, this ability functions at close range only and is passively automatic.

                                      If Amber concentrates, she can try to detect the presence of any cut stones around her, with an intuitive knowledge of their size and location. This can reveal the location of people trying to hide from or surprise her. When using this charm, Amber may roll Perception+Awareness every round. At three success, she is able to detect gems all gems in close range- these success are cumulative and do not need to be on the same round. As long as she continues this action she may continuing rolling until she accumulates an additional three success, which increases the range band by one. Amber may take a move action or shout out information while doing this, but making attack rolls, attempting sorcery, or utilizing other charms breaks her concentration and she must start again if she wants to expand her area of effect.

                                      Note: Amber may choose to stop her focus at the beginning of her turn, if she does, she retains the sense of gems in whatever range band she achieved even though she is no longer focusing on this charm; if a gem enters her range band she is aware of it, even if it wasn't there when she stopped manifesting this charm, and if a gem leaves this area she is aware what direction it left. This effect lasts until the end of the scene or until she dismisses it. Amber does not need line of sight use Magpie's Covetousness, it functions just as well in the dark, through walls, or any other nonmagic interference.
                                      5m/round when focusing. 1m/round to sustain.

                                      Special: At Essence 5, Amber's focus has increased to the level where she may use this charm while fighting or manifesting other charms with no penalty.
                                      Special: **Magically obscured gems are not automatically revealed by Magpie's Covetousness. However, Amber can use this charm to gain a +2 dice bonus to her Perception+Occult roll to locate a gem hidden from her by any type of magical effect. Additionally, she gains a basic understanding of how the gem is hidden: Invisibility, illusionary wall, pushed into a secret Wyld Pocket, etc..

                                      Manifold Path of Gems: (Unlocks at Essence 4) So great is Amber's love of gems, jewels, and precious stones that she finds a wellspring of courage in her pursuit of them. Amber may activate this charm to gain a +2 dice bonus on any skill check the directly involves the delivery, purchase, sale, shipment, or other such mercantilism and use of gems. This includes, but is not limited to, using Bureaucracy to allow a caravan of gems to leave a quarantined district, rolling Socialize to haggle over a lazul necklace, Sail to pursue a feeling treasure ship, or rolling Lore to recall the biographies of a cursed gems ill-fated owners (and so on). This bonus stacks Craft rolls made with Diamontaire's Opus.
                                      *Special:                                       On fighting. This bonus does not apply to using a weapon made of gems, but it does apply to trying to disarm someone of a weapon made of gems. It should only be used when Amber (and by extension, Dak-Tuliphos) are using their abilities to acquire or use gems in a monetary or artistic fashion.

                                      Miserly Insistence of Dak-Tuliphos: As the exigent of the greedy and and gem-obsessed Engraver God, Amber has a deep urge to take advantage of the foolish merchants who ill treat the gifts of her Lord. If Amber sees a gem-based item that is priced far lower or far greater than it should be she must attempt action as appropriate- an underpriced item must be bought ("Taken from the Ignorant Swine!") and an overpriced item or product of forgery must be identified as such, loudly, and with insistence that the mistake be corrected. Note that Dak-Tuliphos is no fool, and understands sales, basic market fluctuation, and price gouging. Seeing a Resources Three emerald torque on sale for the cost of a working man's one-month wages (such as if the merchant must quickly flee town before pirates show up) fits, seeing a Diamond Scepter priced for triple it's material worth because it was wielded by Chisofos the Impure King does not.

                                      Note that this ability does not contain a bonus (although Amber can profit on the sale of mispriced items). This is a wholly negative side affect from being the Chosen of the Engraver God. If Amber attempts to resist this effect, such as when doing reconnaissance at a masquerade party) she must pass a Wits+Integrity test to do so, the difficulty to be determined by the Story-teller. An easy test would be to resist throwing some coins at a merchant for a great deal in the middle of a fight, a hard test would be to not declare that the Royal Sapphire Crown of Ammet The Sea Whisper-King is a fake while at an auction. Additionally, so great is Amber's respect for gems and jewels that she can not attempt to steal them, or be willful party to their theft for the purpose of monetary gain without rolling in the same fashion*. Amber gains Limit on a success, on a failure she can't help herself and takes action, however unfortunate.

                                      *Special: Amber may freely attempt to steal a gem or gem-based item of art in retaliation for it being over-priced or unjustly hidden away. Dak-Tuliphos loathes those who squander and hide His Or Her favored treasure. Amber may "steal back" a stolen item with no penalty.Dak-Thuliphos, in His Or Her infinite wisdom, also understands that sometimes an important item, such as a Tourmaline Key to the Dojo-Monastery of Death's Beloved Champions may be partially or wholly made out of gems. Indeed, it thrills Dak-Thuliphos to see such works, so if an important macguffin comes up that would otherwise count under Miserly Insistence, Amber is not only free but encouraged to pursue it. This charm is meant to keep Amber from the disrespect of the monetary value of gems by resorting to petty thievery.

                                      --------
                                      Miscellany
                                      1) Gems and Jewels are considered by Dak-Tuliphos to be diamonds, rubies, emeralds, amethyst, opals, garnets, etc.. Pearls, precious metals, and Jade are not.
                                      2) An item is considered to be a gem or gem based if it is wholly a gem (a crystal cut in the shape of an eye), majorly made of gems (The diamond blade sword of One-Armed Sai Lan). For the sake of non-practical goods, such as art, an item is considered gem based if it's main worth comes from gems used to make it (the Sapphire Cup of Tian the Blessed Sycophant, Kor Diao's Amethyst rimmed glasses), but not if the gems are a minor decoration (the Oricalcum Scepter of Golden Koi Emporer, adorned with rubies)
                                      3) Dak-Tuliphos wishes to see gems become the major currency of the world, and such encourages Amber to facilitate their use as a monetary unit. She does not hoard gems "just to have".
                                      4) Dak-Tuliphos also wishes gems to be more prominent in the Arts, and so Amber will often be drawn to places of culture where she can attempt to cause artistic trends using or glorifying gems and jewels.
                                      5) Amber may commune with Dak-Tuliphos by making a minor craft project depicting His Or Her likeness using gems, however, Dak-Tuliphos will not commune through the same object twice.
                                      6) Whenever practical, Amber will pay in gem stones before resorting to coins or talents of jade. This works for large purchases, she doesn't bother haggling over lunch at a teahouse.
