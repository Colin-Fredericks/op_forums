                            Originally posted by WalkingEye View Post
                            Exegesis of the Distilled form says After completing five legendary project you can do the charms effect. So does this mean once I make/repair 5 legendary things I can use this charm once a story even if I no longer make legendary projects or after each set of 5 legendary projects I can use the charm no more then once a story?

                            If it is the latter How many legendary projects are you expected to be making as a master solar craftsman. There are no where near as many legendary projects around mentioned in the books.
                            This is an interesting question. I always read it like you had to complete 5 legendary projects for each use of the Charm. But I can see where it may be construed differently. Now I don't really know the intent, thanks for confusing me!

                            The Charm is 8 steps deep, on a not particularly stellar branch, and you need a bunch of other Charms to make completing 5 legendary projects possible.

                            If you let the 5 lp deal be one and done, you are allowing that player to get an extra 20xp per story (if he is able to amass the points, which is more or less likely depending on how you all play). If you require 5 lp per use, depending on how many opportunities they are given to complete a legendary, they will only ever get 1-3 uses of the Charm, if that. Remember, its per story, not per session, depending on how often you game an how the ST structures the chronicle, that can be a very long time.

                            As to your second point; Solars are back from a millennium long stint in the slammer. Now is the time when legendary projects will start happening again. How many and how often is really a matter for the table to play out.

                            I say that it wholly depends on how many sessions per story you generally run, how many legendary projects you want people to be striving to complete and how easy you want it to make for this particular player to pull away from the group XP wise.

                                                        Originally posted by Shaddar View Post
                                                        Would it be a good idea to make a charm like adamantine fists that give a normal melee weapon artifact stats? I kind of want to do a char that can cleave the earth with a branch.
                                                        If yes, where would that charm fit?
                                                        The Improvised tag indicates that improvised weapons can be used with either Brawl or Melee, but the Ability descriptions seem to indicate that improvised weapons are more of a Brawl thing, so any Charms involving their use is probably best placed under that Ability.

                                                        Melee already includes the power to make a daiklave out of nothing anyway, so there's not really any need for a Charm that boosts a mundane weapon to artifact status. If you really want to, you can just write an Evocation for Glorious Solar Saber that says something like "if you're already holding a weapon when you activate Glorious Solar Saber, you can treat that weapon as its artifact equivalent for the duration of the Charm. It possesses all the same Evocations as your regular Glorious Solar Saber, just with a different physical profile."

                                                        As for Brawl, you could make a spinoff of Intercepting Fury Strike that lets your improvised weapon count as an artifact just for that clash; I'm not sure how much that should cost, though. Alternatively, you could make a repurchase of Adamantine Fists of Battle that lets you use it with improvised weapons, or at least makes the Charm compatible with Intercepting Fury Strike.

                                                        Side Note: Tearing a crevasse in the earth is a listed Strength 10+ feat, difficulty 20. If you somehow met that requirement and rolled a ridiculous number of successes (likely with Nine Aeons Thew), your ST might let you stunt the feat as cleaving the earth with a branch rather than with your hands.
