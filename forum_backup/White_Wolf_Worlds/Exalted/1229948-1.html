    Well, our games have never gone as bad as some of the previous stories, but a party of 5 solars started investigating a weird temple we found while on our way home from clearing 500 hungry ghosts out of a 1st age ruin... only to discover that we'd walked into the middle of some kind of demon summoning ritual (damn sidereals must have been screwing with fate for the timing to be that perfect)

    20 solar limbs and 10 eyes entered the building. Only 12 limbs and 7 eyes left.
    - The two-sword wielding martial artist was missing a leg AND an arm (because he doesn't know when to stay down)
    - The archer no longer had an arm with which to pull back his string.
    - The weird "I'll take all the strength-boosting athletics charms and not much else" dawn lost an eye
    - And the almost brand new, barely 3 sessions old "tank" martial artist was burst like a bag when he got stamped on by a 30ft tall combat monster of a demon.

    I was the only character to walk out of that building intact, and that's cos I fly around on a cloud using social influence and kai attacks so I never got within range of it. Technically we won, but it certainly didn't feel like it.

            Not in direct combat, simply because the game is lethal enough that GMs are, reasonably, rather queasy about the fact that the difference between "defeat" and "half the party is dead" is kind of razor thin, so a lot of punches are pulled in direct combat scenarios.

            Otherwise, though, I would actually say that not only have I had Exalts who failed, I'm not sure I've ever had a character who unambiguously succeeded. "Win the battle, lose the war" and an ever growing precarious Jenga tower of barely-holding responsibilities you can barely keep from collapsing into a clusterfuck singularity is practically the definitory Exalted experience in my, well, experience.

                        Enough of the Circle has personal history sufficiently antagonistic to the Bull that they're okay with this. (The Dawn is a Tepet)

                        ---

                        This one probably doesn't count. I played a parody character for a one-shot, once, a fumbling meathead named Legendary Brawler Sashimi, whose terrifying anima banner was a glorious, golden, flopping carp. It was so entertaining, we decided to give him a second session. Finding himself surrounded by venomous tree snakes towards the end of the time we had available, he launched into a battle trance, and I meticulously described how everything seemed to slow as he plotted the location and approach of each of his dozens of reptilian assailants. Only, you know, he wasn't that smart? Int 1, Wits 1? So the cinematic slow-mo scene cuts directly to him being covered in venomous snakes. It wasn't so much a battle trance as him kinda spacing out. He died immediately.

                            So, the very first Solar game I ever played is still going. I've been playing it for, oh... 8 years? 9? Once every three-four weeks, with a few hiatuses.

                            We've had our successes: Stopping a number of wars, helping Harborhead chase out its Autocthonian invaders, uniting two warring dynasties, stopping demons rewriting time so they won the Primordial War, etc.
                            But, honestly, as Celestials with hundreds and hundreds of XP (I had 750 by the end of 2nd ed, and we restarted 3rd with 200 +160 Solar xp), it's an extremely incompetent group.

                            We've had plenty of failures and f***-ups.
                            Especially at the beginning of the game (which, as I said, was the first Solar game most of the players had played)

                            We started out on the steppe of Mon-Gul. We came together to investigate an ancient Solar ruin being excavated by the Realm, who'd made an alliance with local warlord Temujin, an enemy of my shaman's tribe.

                            Our Zenith (or Dawn? I can't remember, it was 9 years ago) warlord on his yeddim decided to take on Temujin's army by himself. 1000 archers shot him to death at range before he could get close.
                            My Twilight shaman, Shadow of Kings, challenged Temujin (a god-blood) to single combat. The god-blood beat the crap out of me, and I flared all over the place, revealing myself as a nice juicy Wyld Hunt target.

                            So... we got on my Azure Chariot and fled 1000 miles west.
                            Here we came to Sukhmet, an Egypt-expy country on the northern coast. We were joined by a local Night Caste, and started investigating a lost legion of Clockwork Soldiers left in small groups in different caves across the country.
                            But our Dawn swordsman wandered off from the group and went to a different town (I don't remember why).
                            We saw, miles away, his anima banner shoot up into the sky iconically... then suddenly get cut out.
                            He'd been killed by some mortal guards.

                            As our friend had just died and revealed there were Solars in the area... we got on my Azure Chariot and fled 1000 miles (or whatever) north to the Haslanti League.
                            Here we joined a skyship expedition to the west to find some ancient ruins full of magitek or something.

                            We had various adventures at different islands on the way. We defeated an ancient magitek intelligence, fled a living island that tried to eat everyone, etc, and finally discovered that the weird admiral who didn't care about his crews dying was a Pterok using illusions who just wanted us to take him to a lost city full of slumbering Dragon Kings in the west, who we woke up. We defeated a Realm army attacking them, so that was pretty successful.

                            So, we then decided to fly off to Coral or something in our one remaining airship.
                            We were trundling along when we were attacked by pirates, who used catapults to try and hole our balloon. They made a couple of small holes, and we began sinking. My bound Huraka had been blowing the ship along, and I thought we needed more lift, so I told them to "blow the ship up". So they blew our ship up, and its burning wreckage crashed into the sea.
                            We then spent a month in a boat made of airship wreckage slowly rowing to Coral. (This included a fight with the ghost of a crewmember where we discovered no-one in the party had anti-spirit charms, which was awkward as he chased us around the ship. I managed to trap him in a salt ring eventually.)

                            Tldr;
                            The Zenith challenged an army by himself and was shot to death at range. We ran away.
                            The Dawn died fighting some random guards. We ran away.
                            We discovered we'd been tricked by a mad Pterok who'd got loads of people killed. We just went along with him anyway.
                            During a fight with pirates, I blew up our own airship with poorly-worded commands


                            But wait, there's more! That was just when the first ST was running it.
                            Under our second ST:
                            A Lunar and two Solars were beaten up by mortals. Once a Solar Martial Artist was beaten up by a single cook, who chopped the Solar's arm off with a meat cleaver.
                            The Zenith killed himself because of things he'd done in limit break.
                            A Dragon King outwitted us, summoned a horde of ghosts and zombies to wreck a capital city, and killed our Essence 5 Slayer in a Kaiju vs Kaiju fight.


                            Our most recent classic piece of incompetence is as follows:
                            My Zenith was leading the other PCs across the Plateau of Leng to the Black Monastery to slay the Nephwrack who enslaved my brothers.
                            To save time, we went through the evil forest. I told them not to investigate or get involved with weird stuff. An NPC wondered off in the night and vanished.
                            Of course, they went and investigated. They found some weird trees with doors with weird symbols on. They pulled them open.
                            This caused an eclipse. It stopped when they closed the doors.
                            I told them not to open the doors. They opened them again. There was another eclipse.
                            This made the local giant worm monsters angry. Also, the giant worm monsters ate our crew and airship.
                            We managed to trick an evil sorcerer and took his chariot back home. We discovered that the eclipses had unleashed a horde of weird and evil forbidden monsters from under the ground, who'd killed thousands, including the sick pharoah.

                            I should probably mention that the party's leader is a 16 year old naive boy-prince. His Lunar wife is 17 (they got married because he knocked her up while they were both drunk). My Lunar had Solar Bond 5 to him so went along with whatever he said. No offence to any teens on here, but I think we'd do better with more mature leaders.

                                                    Another defeat occurred to me, though this was really just me, not the whole party.

                                                    So, we were trying to make peace between the Essence 5 Solar Sorceress and her magical kingdom of magical people, and the Essence 5 Warlord who rules (sort of) the Djala.

                                                    We eventually got them to agree to peace. Then we tried to get the Djala to help actually defend that kingdom from the coming Realm invasion, but their Warlord would rather see the mountain people die and then fight the Realm later. We pushed... and he then decided to go back on the peace deal, and invade the mountain kingdom so he could use it as a base to fight the Realm.

                                                    At this point, it's been 4 sessions of negotiation and we've got nowhere. So my Night Caste decided to just go kill the Dawn. (His underlings were more reasonable, they were happy to defend the mountain kingdom in return for food shipments).

                                                    I disguise myself, and sneak through the camp at 4am. I wanted to save my motes for the coming fight, so I just rolled my 10 dice for stealth, and got 5.
                                                    The guard got 7.

                                                    I tried rushing past him. He beat my roll.

                                                    I eventually managed to hide. I tried sneaking past him and the battle group of guards who'd appeared. By this time I've battled him to within two range bands of the Dawn's tent. He rolls 9(!) successes to spot me. They charge after me and we have a big fight, while the Dawn slowly puts his armour on.

                                                    He reaches us by the time I've killed 14 guys worth of battle group... but not this one Djala, who's stopped me disengaging 3 times.

                                                    I hit the Dawn with a 53-dice decisive damage. He uses Adamant Skin and Ruin-Abasing Shrug, and takes 7 damage. I'm now crashed.
                                                    He chops off my hand. My defence is now 2, due to Overwhelming and Wound penalties.
                                                    The random Djala guard bops me on the head and knocks me out.

                                                    I was then stripped and crucified.*

                                                    That random Djala guard has now been promoted to one of the Dawn's top guys.

                                                    *But this isn't a death. I escaped, was put on trial by other Solars, and was found innocent.

                                                    I'd done 7 lethal damage and caused the Dawn to use a lot of his motes in our fight. He was supposed to be dueling a Zenith, naked, in the morning (she challenged him for being so unrighteous) but the other PCs had convinced her not to do it that night because they thought she'd just get herself killed.
                                                    However, he did have the duel with the sorceress he'd arranged for the evening... she hit him with a Death Ray, he took 3 damage and ran away and hid.

                                                    So, we managed to do 10 damage total to a Resistance-supernal Dawn... and he fled, roused his armies, healed all his damage, and the war is just beginning. Thus, I didn't really accomplish anything, and lost a hand. Oh, and he took my level 5 hearthstone. And the sorceress took my warstrider while I was hunting him down.

                                                    (I've got them all back though)
