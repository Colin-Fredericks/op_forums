    The core Abyssal Martial Art ("Abyssal Hero Style") is Dark Messiah Style, whose form weapons are the same as Solar Hero Style. Hungry Ghost Style is a different martial art, also in MoEP: Abyssals, which has the form weapons tiger claws, fighting chains, and razor harnesses. Notably, Hungry Ghost requires you to either be initiated into Necromancy or have a Specialty in Occult or Lore related to ghosts or the underworld.

                            Originally posted by Exthalion View Post
                            Do Jadeborn die of old age, and if so what is their approximate lifespan?
                            They live healthily up to a number of centuries equal to their Essence (so the limit tends to be three hundred for Unenlightened and five hundred for the others), after which they rapidly age and will die within about a decade. The Charm Eternal Jade Rejuvanation can let them reverse some of their own aging, in exchange for being completely immobile during that period (so an Essence 4 Jadeborn who is three hundred years old and spends a century under the effects of the Charm will come out two hundred).
