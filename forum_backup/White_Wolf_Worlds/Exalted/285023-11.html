                There are I think three explicit dungeon crawls in Exalted? They're all pretty bad. The style of the game is just not well suited to something like that -- the game works too hard to encourage off-the-rails solutions to problems, and to motivate players to weigh options other than the "default". It's fine to set something up as good fodder for a dungeon crawl, but you can't get overly detailed about it, or assume the players will do the "right" thing in the "right" way, or you're likely to be caught by surprise. A lot of this is a thematic issue: one of the basic things going on with Exalted is that you play a highly empowered character, and the game tries to make you feel empowered by allowing your actions to have dramatic effects on your surroundings, and by having you make decisions and ensuring that the decisions are meaningful. Dungeon crawls have a lot of friction with these themes.
