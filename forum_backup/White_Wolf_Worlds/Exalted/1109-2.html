                                    I had a very fun Lunar once. A changing moon wolf pack mother. Inspired a little by Holo (yes, Emperor-Ox Expansion), a little by the Last Unicorn, and a little by Spirits (nWoD) and Raksha.

                                    To her, life was an ongoing story that was always better in the telling. She never told the truth when an interesting tale would do. She never allowed herself to regret her actions, no matter what became of them. She never let herself be bound; not physically nor by oaths, promises or titles. And finally she never took straightforward action when cunning and cleverness could be substituted.

                                    Of course, true to her nature, all of these rules were broken at one point or another. Such was her urge for freedom.

                                                            http://ca.news.yahoo.com
                                                            From Yahoo News Canada: Thanks to a large ancient tooth, an Australian university student has filled in an evolutionary gap with a giant carnivorous platypus that became extinct at least 5 million years ago. "A new platypus species, even one that is highly incomplete, is a very important aid in developing understanding about these fascinating mammals," palaeontology student Rebecca Pian said in a statement. Pian was working on a collection of fossil deposits from the Riversleigh World Heritage Area in northern Australia when she discovered the species through a highly distinctive oversized tooth. Based on the size of the tooth, the extinct species was probably about a metre (39 inches) long, nearly three times the 38 cm length of the modern platypus.
