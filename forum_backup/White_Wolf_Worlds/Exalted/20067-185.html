                                    05-10-2016, 11:06 AM
                                    You typically don't roll to lie to someone unless you mean to accomplish something with that roll. If you want to get someone to believe something or feel a certain way or take a certain action, then it's an Instill, Inspire, or Persuade action respectively and would use the appropriate Ability (Presence, Performance, et cetera.) - something not being true doesn't change the general rule in that regard. "Not falling for it" generally involves successfully applying your Resolve, or with the rules for Challenging Facts if a PC can reasonably already know that it's not true.

                                    If your lie serves to cover your true feelings about something, then it's your Guile and the relevant Ability is Socialize. "Not falling for it" in this case involves a successful Read Intentions action.
