                                                        Not sure if this would fall under headcanon or house rules, but I'll post it here.

                                                        Solars, for all their power, are the shortest-lived of the Celestial Exalts, having a maximum lifespan of only 1,500 years, half that of their Lunar comrades. The power of the Unconquered Sun blazing through a mortal frame burns bright, but fast. They can, of course, learn Charms to stave off the inevitable, keeping themselves from old age's grasp for another millenium or two, but even in the First Age, complete immortality eluded them.

                                                        Pursuant to the above, no force other than their own Charms, or the blessing of their own Patron can extend a Celestial Exalt's lifespan. The effect of mortal anagathics, or even sorcery, are subsumed into their natural longevity. Solars can extend their lifespans through the Charms mentioned above, Lunars can reverse-age by hibernation, and Sidereals remain SOL.

                                                        Finally, whenever a Celestial dies of old age (an event that has occurred only a handful of times throughout history), they do not leave a corpse, Solars and Infernals burn up in a blinding flash of gold or green sunlight, respectively, Lunars evaporate into a softly glowing silver mist that (usually) dissipates by the next dawn, and Sidereals dissolve into thousands of motes of starlight that twinkle and fade out. The po soul finds this the most satisfactory tribute imaginable.
