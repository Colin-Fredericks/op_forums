        Iron-Graced Dreamer Form

        Executing a stiff and languid kata, the Martial Artist takes on an otherworldly appearance as her Anima coalesces into flecks of iridescent metal covering her body in elaborate patterns. For the rest of the scene, her body takes on the properties of cold iron, gaining (minor soak/hardness bonus). Her natural attacks gain (accuracy/damage bonus) and can deal Lethal damage if she chooses, but always deal Aggravated damage to the Fair Folk and other denizens of the Wyld. She can also parry lethal and ranged attacks without a stunt. Finally, the unyielding nature of cold iron increases the difficulty of any rolled Shaping effect targeting the her by (Martial Arts/2).

        Iron-Graced Dreamer Form is incompatible with other effects that add the properties of other materials to natural attacks, such as Invulnerable Skin of Bronze, Wood Dragon's Claw, Blade of the Battle Maiden, Deadly Starmetal Offensive, and others. One notable exception is the spell Incomparable Body Arsenal. The ST is the final arbiter of which other effects can stack.

                                            Price-of-Knowledge Sacrifice
                                            Raksha Heart

                                            Expressing infinite magnanimity, the Prince of Chaos forever sets a secret beyond his own reach and within reach of his lessers -- for a price. In using this Charm, the raksha identifies some knowledge he possesses, attaches a price, and extrudes it in the form of a token. This token takes the form of a small ordinary object, with some visible reflection of the knowledge within (an engineering secret may appear as a small gear, while information on someone the raksha adores may appear as a small heart-shaped stone). From then on, the Fair Folk no longer knows this secret and automatically fails any action that depends on knowing it. Even if reacquiring these facts by any means, using them remains elusive; the raksha will forget this knowledge by the end of the scene and actions that depend on knowing it, while they may be attempted during this time, suffer a -4 internal penalty. Actions that do not depend on this knowledge but attempt to incorporate it -- such as threatening someone by name after having forgotten everything about that someone -- suffer only a -2 internal penalty.

                                            Anyone who holds the token instinctively gains some understanding of the knowledge it contains and the raksha's price for learning it. This price may either be a number of dots of Virtue or Willpower, or it may be a particular request for information. If the individual chooses to pay this price, it is deducted immediately as the secret knowledge floods the petitioner's mind. In the case of information, the individual is not mystically robbed of it, but must give it honestly and completely to the best of his or her knowledge; no exchange is possible if the individual simply cannot answer the question. Some Lords of Chaos are fond of exchanging select knowledge for basic identifying information and a deep personal secret or two, for use in later fun.

                                            Should the raksha own the token, he receives payment immediately. Dots of Virtue or Willpower provide the raksha with motes, Willpower, and gossamer, as though feeding with Ravishing the Created Form, and the raksha immediately learns any paid information. If the token has left the Fair Folk's ownership, he receives nothing, but becomes aware that an exchange has taken place, the general nature of the secret knowledge and price, and a general sense of its location. Should it reenter the raksha's possession, prices paid are transferred immediately. If it is destroyed before this happens, the Prince of Chaos learns of its general location, the prices paid and secret knowledge that were lost, and the Fair One becomes able to acquire the secret anew. If the raksha holds the token in his hand, he may draw it back into himself, immediately reacquiring this knowledge, but becomes unable to ever part with it again by means of this Charm.

                                                            Palace of Misconstrued Echoes
                                                            Sidereal Investigation

                                                            Performing a kata, the Sidereal commits the motes for this Charm and traces a vertical line at least as tall as herself, with sufficient space to her sides. Her fingers leave a green trail in the air or on the surface, and large ornate double-doors, adorned with iconography relevant to Jupiter, fade into being on either side. The doors will open only at the Sidereal's touch, though there is no limit to how many may follow. On the other side is a memory, obscured by white light, until the particular memory is chosen. The Sidereal may either select one of her memories or appoint one of her visitors to voluntarily share one. Should the visitor decline, the Sidereal may select another visitor, choose a memory of her own, or end the Charm's effects. The individual providing the memory becomes known as the Charm's subject.

                                                            The Sidereal and all visitors then observe the chosen memory as its subject knew and understood it. They may will a scene to play backwards and forwards as desired. They may walk around the scene and may make rolls to acquire additional information about it, but cannot interact with anyone or anything. None may learn things of which the viewer is unaware, but can surmise things whether or not the viewer understands or understood them. For instance, if an unknown speaker was remembered as hidden behind a curtain, walking around the curtain will not reveal the individual, but those with an ear for voices or accents may find a clue.

                                                            There are additional notes of caution for this Charm. The scene is observed as the subject remembers it, and so there may be discrepancies if there has been tampering with these memories. This includes both supernatural tampering and having been convinced -- or convincing oneself -- of what was actually remembered. Should the subject be convinced of anything else that "must have happened," the scene will adjust to reflect this. What's more, if the memory's subject wishes to obscure or fabricate any components of the memory, be they contexts, details, events, the presence of individuals, or the like, she may deliberately do so. However, all of these discrepancies -- whether from tampering with a memory or deliberate deception -- create flaws and contradictions, which can be detected with successful <insert uses of the Investigation sub-system> opposed by traits (typically Larceny and Manipulation) from the source of the obfuscation.

                                                            If successfully and logically demonstrated, the scene adjusts to reflect the newly understood truth (e.g. "He must have been stabbed from this angle, which means his blood could not possibly have ended up there!" or "With where you were standing, you would never (or must) have been able to identify the culprit's face!"). However, should the subject choose to deliberately withhold information, the scene will reflect only that something is being withheld and its general nature, but not the exact information (e.g. it will be clear the subject knows a particular person's face, but is choosing to obscure this). Unless information is withheld or part of the memory has been magically removed, identifying and addressing all of these flaws can completely reconstruct the memory exactly as the subject observed it.

                                                            When the Sidereal ends this Charm, all individuals still inside the gate find themselves walking out of it as the gate closes and fades away behind them. Individuals may freely enter or exit the gate as they please until the Sidereal ends the Charm. On doing so, the Sidereal may enhance a social influence action that depends on any information the memory reveals, which affects only those who are still in the memory. Any individual may make similarly enhanced social influence actions when the memory changes to reflect a successful use of the Investigation sub-system, to a maximum of that individual's Investigation.

                                                            Once the Sidereal has observed a memory, she may call it back using this Charm as though it was a memory of her own. However, if flaws are identified and rooted out, but the subject is not present to fill in any blanks, these are automatically treated as though information has been withheld. This also means the Sidereal may serve as the subject of the memory for others, and may thus choose to introduce deceptions as normal (which visitors may suss out in the manner described above).
