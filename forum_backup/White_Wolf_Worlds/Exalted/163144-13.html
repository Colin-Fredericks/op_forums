    Originally posted by While My Guitar View Post
    Slay one to Slay a Thousand Tactic.

    Getimian War

    The bonds of Fate weigh heaviest on groups, stemming from followers and coiling around leaders.

    An attack supplemented by this charm exploits these shackles, targeting the leader of a unit, group or organisation.

    Reducing the Raw Damage of her attack by 1 die, the Getimian spreads the Fate of Endings throughout the chains of command, doubling the amount of targets affected by the attack - these new targets need not be within any range, the only requirement is that they are ranked lower than the highest ranking target affected by this charm.

    This charm can be invoked any amount of times, as long as the Getimian has Essence to spend and the raw damage of the attack is above 0.

    20 raw damage could be reduced by 10 dice (for around 3 motes per die + 1wp ) to deal 10 raw damage to 1024 targets.

    Dancing Devil Paracosm
    Solar Performance rquiring Phantom Conjuring Performance

    Through a stirring performance, the solar rouses the imaginations of his audience into taking over their senses.

    As long as the Solar performs, all targets of his performance believe themselves to be in a world described by the Solar - for better or for worse - and those that want to believe can keep the effects of this charm going for themselves.
    The solar overwrites any and potentially all senses with his descriptions, and can make friends and family seem like hellspawn or trees, or simply seem to disappear. He can make himself seem like a specific Emperor or the Silent Wind of Malfeas for example. During the performance, all these changes seem absolutely real to their targets, but once the charm expires are considered to be all part of a particularly compelling show that got everyone too carried away, and not actual events that transpired (unless other efforts are made to that effect).

        Self Dissolving Truth
        Raksha Heart

        The Fair Folk understand that there is sometimes value in not being themselves, and may use this Charm to create deep-cover identities. When using this Charm, the raksha loses all memory of the life he has led and all of a his Intimacies cease to affect them, and are recorded separately. In their place, he acquires a new personal history and new set of appropriate Intimacies equal in number to his Heart Grace, as well as a minor Tie to the idea that he is the false life. A raksha with a Heart Grace of 3 may make this Tie Major, while one with a Heart Grace of 4+ may make this Defining. If this Intimacy is stripped away, the raksha's false life dissipates and the original one returns.

        He may also select (Heart Grace - 1) conditions for the end of the false life. Each condition may either end the false life once it is met, or must be met in addition to the raksha's Tie to its false life. These conditions often involve the erosion or change in context of the false life's Intimacies, sometimes called "pivotal" Intimacies by the Fair Folk.

        At the false life's end, the raksha remembers the original and false lives just as well (and by default knows which is which), while his Intimacies mostly return to what they were. The exceptions include any new Intimacies or contexts to old Intimacies the Creation-born caused the fey to develop during its false life However, the raksha's player and Storyteller may feel the false identity left an impression, particularly if the raksha spent a considerable length of time with it. If they agree, the Fair Folk's present set of Intimacies generally skew toward the original's, but may become a blend of the false life's and the original's, as seems appropriate to them. For instance, if a cataphract with a Major Tie of hatred for a character adopts a false life in which he has a Major or Defining Tie of love instead, he may after a long time return to find that his Tie of hatred has been degraded (perhaps completely) and/or changed its context; he may also or instead find that it has been joined by a Minor, Major, or Defining Tie of love for the character.

        A repurchase of this Charm allows the raksha to use this Charm on behalf of any character whose Heart Grace he owns.

                Next: Mirrored Mores Mansion
                Lunar Manipulation.

                The Chosen of Luna sometimes invite madness into their homes. For what reasons, only the Moonchildren can truly know.

                This Charm alters the minds of any character that sets foot in the Lunar's territory, though this may be limited to (Essence) select areas within the territory, at the Lunar's option. For example, an Essence 3 Lunar could choose to have her entire territory affected by this Charm, or she could choose to only have its effects apply within her home, in the forests bordering her territory, and the area of the sacred spring where she practices Sorcery. Regardless of this choice, this Charm alters the social perceptions and understandings of any character within the boundaries of its effects, causing them to believe the opposite of what they normally would; what was considered polite is now considered rude, and customs of sacred hospitality may exist where none did before. This is an Illusion effect.

                For any character (besides the Lunar herself and other Essence-users) that remains within the boundaries of this Charm's effects for more than a week, this ceases to be an Illusion effect and they believe that this is and has been their cultural practice the entire time, or at least that such beliefs are those of the region. The cost of this Charm must remain committed, however, for this to continue. Once the commitment lapses, the original customs and practices begin to return to public memory, though the Lunar may begin using social attacks to actually alter the beliefs of those subject to this Charm, with a +3 bonus to any relevant rolls - it's very easy to convince someone of something they already believe, after all. This bonus is reduced to +2 after three days, and only +1 after a week, as the original memories begin to return to the populace. After (10 + Lunar's Essence) days, this effect fades entirely, as the memories will have returned completely.

                    Sword and Rose Champion Training
                    Infernal Lore (Szoreny)

                    When a warlock seeks to cultivate a paragon, true devotion is the only proper soil. On activating this Charm, the Green Sun Prince names an ideal for her chosen pupil and plants an appropriate silver seed in his heart with a touch. The silver sands around it will preserve it, but only for (Lore) days, during which time the Infernal may change a Minor Intimacy's context to relate to the named ideal. For instance, a warlock seeking to make a great swordsman might convince the man he can win the town's respect by doing so. If successful, the seed's life is no longer on the clock, and it provides the pupil with <benefit> when he acts toward becoming the ideal in question. However, while the Intimacy is still ostensibly about its original subject, the pupil now cares for it only where it overlaps the ideal. Further, <relevant social influence effect> works to likewise change the context of another Minor Intimacy whenever he enjoys the <benefit>.

                    This proceeds through the pupil's other Minor Intimacies, with the warlock's personal attempts to convert them having identical effects. With a total of three converted (or as many as the pupil possesses, if fewer), work may begin on the Major Intimacies. With the first Major Intimacy, the demon plant in the pupil's heart begins to bud and <the benefit> increases. With a total of two converted (or as many as the pupil possesses, if fewer), the seed may at last begin to seize a Defining Intimacy. With success, the pupil receives <additional benefit>, as the Infernal's efforts yield a blossoming silver rose of roots in the paragon's heart. The pupil becomes an automaton, able to live the ideal and do little else.

                    Should anyone successfully change the context of the pupil's highest-converted Intimacy away from the ideal's -- a task with difficulty increased by 2 -- the demon plant withers and dies, and all effects end, all other affected Intimacies returning to their original contexts.

                            Celestial Martial Arts (Wire-Strung Temple Style?)

                            The Martial Artist takes a precarious stance, seeming still; but every muscle in his body is tense and ready to unleash a multitude of lashings.                                                             Clean Streets, Clean Governments
                                                            Infernal Bureaucracy (Principle of Hierarchy)
                                                            The Warlock understands that all things must know their proper place, and enlightens those without his perspective of the merits. Upon activating this Charm, the Infernal personally or legislatively begins a public work, service, or other campaign to improve well-being for a community by removing something perceived as negative from it, with bonuses toward carrying it out. This may be as large as removing crime from the streets or as minor as an anti-littering campaign. Any who assist or carry it out on his behalf receive a lesser bonus. Those who observe or participate in the work in progress or observe the fruits of these labors begin to develop a Principle that "We must help to make society orderly." Those who know the Infernal is responsible also develop a Principle that "<The Warlock> can create an orderly society."

                                                            The grades of these Principles may be no greater than Minor if the campaign is not one of especially great perceived benefit to an individual (an anti-littering campaign where the streets are not seen as especially filthy), but may be elevated to Major or Defining if the perceived benefit is more significant (working to eliminate bureaucratic corruption, a den of thieves, or a distrusted ethnic minority). No such Intimacies form if one does not perceive them as beneficial. If the campaign is given particular difficulties such that one begins to lose confidence in it, the results of such a campaign are significantly undone, or one no longer sees it as beneficial, the associated Principles fade -- unless they are sustained by another campaign enhanced by a different use of this Charm.
