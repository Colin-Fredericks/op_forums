    Jiara's Fall, House Mnemon in a Bull of the North situation

    08-09-2020, 09:10 PM
    I need some help determining the political fallout of Jiara falling to Solar Anathema. Here's some details:

    -This was a Jiara centered, All-Solar's game in which the player's (one from each caste, all originating from Jiara one way or another) assisted Perfect Soul (Ex3's iconic Zenith) in her rebellion.
    -This game is set in the context of several other games taking place in the same continuity, wherein other players from other parts of the world are either in their own groups or playing single-player (mostly single-player for my sanity).
    -Event's conspired to have a sort-of general war occur, wherin circle's of Solar's, homebrewed Infernal's and Abyssal carve out their own kingdoms. Several of which lend aid to the Jiaran revolution.
    -Volfer (Ex3 iconic Dawn caste) dies at some point during a fighting retreat.
    -At the penultimate battle of the Story my Dawn caste player (now Perfect Soul's senior general) pulls a god damn Cannae on the bulk of Mnemon's forces. I'm talking a total disaster on the scale of The Valley of Shards.
    -During the final battle to take the City of Jiara itself. Mnemon escapes, but summon's Octavian to be the circle's obligatory final boss.

    That's the super truncated summary of the Jiaran Campaign in a nutshell (ask for more details if you want).

    I'm mostly looking for opinions and inferences on how the political situation in the realm changes. The first obvious thing is what I read from the Heirs of the Shogunate kickstarter preview, that Sesus Raenyah's star REALLY starts to rise amongst the more conservative parts of the Dynasty. I'd like to hear other people's takes on how the rest of the Dynasty, the Thousand Scales, the Delibrative, patrician clans, even peasants react to House Mnemon's failure in Jiara.

    Also of great import is how House Mnemon and the woman herself, Mnemon, react. I'd imagine that even with this setback Mnemon would never allow anyone's butt to touch that throne but hers. What actions would she and her house start taking after Jiara's Fall.

          Jiara falling causes Mnemon to lose face, but unless all of her legions are decimated(which they won't be in the default canon because she's got several back on the Isle), she's not near Tepet-level crisis. Tepet's situation was unique in that it fielded all of its legions, which were later scattered due to interference from the rest of the Great Houses coordinated by agents of the Empress that still had momentum after her disappearance. The magnitude of its loss is based on the fact that it formulated its strategy based on reinforcements that never came. Mnemon's endeavor in Jiara appears to be wholly its own endeavor without expectation of aid from the other Houses.

                      So, the general argument against Mnemon here will be, "You lost a war so you suck as a ruler, also now you can't beat us in a fight for the throne, nyah."

                      Mnemon has a strong card to play here, though. Her counterargument is, "I put my legions on the line to protect the Realm and to uphold the Immaculate Philosophy. What are you doing with your legions? How can anyone deserve the throne if they've done less than I in opposing this threat in Jiara? If you're worthy of the throne, prove it."

                      Not only does this narrative framing serve to keep Ledaal firmly in Mnemon's camp, but it places political pressure on the military houses to move against Jiara to prove themselves, providing an easy casus belli for tearing down any rival who marches on the Imperial City without first showing off their bona fides by taking a crack at Jiara. This might not necessarily work, but it's tailor-made to appeal to Cathak Cainan, who remains the Dynasty's kingmaker at this stage of the runup to civil war.

                      I'd go with a period of reprieve as Realm forces withdraw amid months of intense behind-the-scenes politics, followed by a bigger coalition invasion of Jiara and its environs. This might be a Mnemon-Cathak-Peleps-Ledaal force going all-in on thwarting the Anathema, with their (likely inevitable) failure against the amassed PCs leaving a more open field for the remaining candidates. Alternatively, other factions might take turns attacking Jiara and getting knocked out of the running for the throne when they break against the PCs, eventually leaving things clear for something like the Mnemon-Cathak alliance proposed in Heirs to the Shogunate to present an unassailable bid for the throne.

                      Note that the Realm isn't above divide-and-conquer strategies. While the most pious generals and politicians might balk at bargaining with an Abyssal or Infernal prince, there's doubtless some scope to negotiate, especially if there are fault lines between the PC Circles that they can leverage to turn them against one another.

                      (EDIT: There probably will be little to nothing in the way of honest bargains offering to treat known Anathema as legitimate partners. But there's definitely scope for stuff like "you glow green and have a weird Caste Mark, so we have no immediate reason to consider you Anathema" or "we can attack you now, but if instead you work with us to crush Jiara, we'll turn a blind eye to anything you do to that other satrapy controlled by our rivals; you can be their problem instead of ours," or even simply lying outright about a willingness to overlook Anathema.)

                          Originally posted by Lioness View Post
                          Hey, welcome to the forums.
                          As to your question. I think I'd need to know more about these other games to put Mnemon's failure in Jiara into context since multiple PCs acting in different parts of the world can be a considerable alteration to the setting.

                          Thanks for the welcome!

                          Most of what of effected the Jiaran Campaign came from the Scavenger Lands, especially the circles of Solar's, Abyssals, and Infernal's. So let me explain their circumstances:

                          (Warning, incoming Wall of text)

                          -First understand that my Solar games are divided into two categories, Normal Mode and Hard Mode. Normal mode is creation after the Empress disappeared, the All Seeing Eye is losing funding, the Wyld Hunts are spread thin, and the Sidereal's are running ragged. Solar's in Normal mode can expect to find opportunity in this Time of Tumult with relatively low risk. Hard Mode is before the Empress disappeared, the All Seeing Eye is fully funded, plenty of Dragonblooded join the Wyld Hunts and the Sidereal's...are still running ragged but at least they don't have to worry about the Realm potentially collapsing. Hard Mode is when a Solar will find that almost everywhere in the threshold isn't safe from a dedicated Wyld Hunt finding them and killing them. Hard Mode lasts from RY 700 to RY 763, after which Hard Mode automatically turns into Normal Mode.

                          -I have only a few Solar players who survived Hard Mode, none of them made any real waves in Creation until after Hard Mode ended (couldn't afford drawing attention ya know?). One of my Solar PC's (Twilight Caste) have passed herself of as a wandering Sorcerer Crafter, willing to sell her services to the highest bidder. Her goal's involved creating a safe place dedicated to science, lorekeeping, researching the mysteries of creation...and magical martial arts of course. Her game became one of creating a site for a city manse utilizing all the powers of all five elements as well as the Sun, the Moon and the Stars. Only the best for her perfect city (named Lyceum, located equidistant between The Nameless Lair and Great Forks). This long campaign (consisting of several Stories) involved several sorcerous workings, gathering magic materials and several greater hearthstones, slowly taking apart Denandsor to build up her new city, gathering Brass Legionnaires, in one case redirecting multiple minor rivers and creeks to pool into a lake. When she made it to Normal Mode, she was already formidable. Her city state was starting to take form and all she needed were people who wanted to live there. That is when four Normal Mode Solars PC's joined her to make a full circle. Currently she's queen of a constitutional (athenian) democracy and is increasingly becoming known across the Scavenger Lands as a power sorcerer, crafter, and scholar. Concerning the Jiaran Campaign, she contributed at least two legions worth of Brass Legionnaires to the Jiaran circles war effort in the name of solidarity with her fellow Solars.

                          -The most successful group of Abyssals I have rule over Black Chase, a vast Shadowland just north of Sijan. The Black Chase Circle aims to increase their influence in Creation and the Underworld by controlling access to rare underworld goods and workings. They also aim to control access to the dead by sealing Shadowlands and making Black Chase the only major access point to the Underworld in the Scavenger Lands. This is frustrated by the existence of already established underworld powers, not just counting Deathlords either. They've gotten the most help in this task from the Lyceum Circle, the Twilight in particular helping the Daybreak seal Shadowlands. The Daybreak Caste is, if not the leader of the Black Chase Circle, the First among Equals. He's the one designing new underworld amenities for the Dead, he's designed designated zones where the living and the dead can interact safely, he's even created workings and Artifacts that allow qualified ghost merchants to travel for a time in Creation (before needing to renew these workings for a *small* fee).

                          -The Infernal group currently rules a reconstituted City of Beimeni-Ta as well as their conquered territories in Vaneha. Beimeni-Ta appeared near the confluence of the Meander and Maruto rivers, its master Adeimantus had discovered a way to have it appear in creation quicker, causing it to grow at an alarming rate. The Immaculates would've been all over this, but this Infernal Circle was attempting to conquer the Hundred Kingdoms and their legions of war charm enhanced soldiers (and demons, don't forget the demons). They had limited success, but ran into a wall of resistance that was portions of Lookshyan field forces augmented by various forces from Scavenger Land allies. Facing this, the leader of this Infernal Circle hatched an insane plan. During his time in a single-player game he helped the Queen of Lyceum with a significant task, significant enough that he extracted a favor from her. He had the Horizon Caste send a message to her, beseeching help in the form of purging the Wyld from Beimeni-Ta and anchoring it in Creation. The Queen was inclined to pay her debts and assisted the Infernals in researching how to anchor Beimeni-Ta. The Final Act of the Adeimantus Campaign involved crushing Vaneha's forces (augmented by a joint-Lookshyan/Immaculate Order Wyld Hunt) then turning right around to besieging Beimeni-Ta, having the Nadir Caste plant an Artifact made from Broad Cypress's essence trap Beimeni-Ta in Creation, and finally The Queen of Lyceum using Wyld-Shaping Technique to shape/eliminate the Wyld while the Infernal's army fought off hordes of hobgoblins. The confrontation with Adeimantus, by comparison to what transpired before was somewhat anti-climactic (He turned out to be painfully easy to kill in the face of several experienced Infernals and Solars). The Infernal's, now the Beimeni-Ta Circle, have conquered rest of Vaneha, the Penumbra Caste suborning clans willing to be ruled by Beimeni-Ta, the Nadir doing assasin and wetwork to undermine the circles enemies, the Ascendant Caste bringing the spirit of Vaneha into the fold, and the Azimuth Caste slaying the Sword Prince Gensuji. After the siege of Jibei, the Azimuth stole the Imperial Daiklave and proclaimed himself "The Crimson King" (His idea, not mine).

                          -The Great Forks Exigent Circle were mostly born to low class peasants and slaves raised up by gods who wanted easy vassals, as it turned out the Exigent PC's were very independent minded and were prone to socializing among fellow Exigents. The leader of the Exigent Circle was an ex-slave who while loyal to his patron, desired to see his people freed from bondage. The Great Forks revolt was mostly prompted by the Beimeni-Ta Circle, primarily by the Penumbra Caste who was sent on a mission to keep Great Forks from joining a Scavenger Land's alliance against them. He ended up encouraging the Exigent Circle towards revolution with promises that they would live like righteous kings and that they're friends and family would no longer know want. The revolt took the form of a slave revolt as the Penumbra went through slave communities, filling them with indignation at their stations whilst convincing them they had nothing to lose while everything to gain. The Exigent Circles job was to find willing collaborators amongst the other Exigents as well as the gods. The main selling point was that Great Fork's function wouldn't fundamentally change, god's would still get their luxuries provided by a massively expanded Temple of the Reverent Whisper as well as promises of a sorcerous working that would magnify the power of prayers-AFTER the revolt. The Revolt itself was a bloody affair, slaves killing masters, Exigents fighting in the streets, gods either staying out of the way or in the case of war gods and gods of violence joining in for either side. The Revolt took a turn when the Azimuth Caste came bringing an army to help the Exigent Circle take the city, ending with the Three Gods of Great Forks fleeing, the Exigents establishing a ruling council, and slavery being abolished. Currently, the Ruling Council of Great Forks effectively shares power with The Temple of the Reverent Whisper since they effectively monoplized giving prayer to the gods, however the Sorcerous Working that magnifies the benefits of prayer in Great Forks must be renewed yearly by the Beimeni-Ta circle.

                          -The single-player games thus far haven't been *too* consequential to what happen's in Jiara, though in one case several single-player games coalesced into the story of *The Fall of Winters* wherin a mixed group of players (A Zenith Caste, a Day Caste, and a Full Moon Caste) worked towards and succeeded in causing *The Fall* of the Mask of Winters. This mixed group participated in activities such as; false flag attacks against Nexus and Lookshy while leaving planted evidence that implicates the Mask of Winters and his abyssals, gaining the favor of the Queen of Lyceum and using the outskirts of her city as a place to gather refugees from Thorns, and sabotage of the Masks industries within Thorns itself. The mixed group got the favor of the Black Chase Circle and the Lyceum Circle getting their assistance in hindering the Mask and eventually going to war with the Mask. Of note is the fact that the Zenith and the Day Castes are princesses of Thorns but from different branches of the family. The Day Castes' family was murdered in a struggle several years ago before the Mask of Winters came that resulted in her exile and the Kingdom of Thorns coming under more intense Realm control (leading to Thorn's wars against the Scavenger Lands at the behest of the Realm). The Zenith's family were the ones who murdered the Day Castes family, however the Zenith was too young to remember such an event. They did have a confrontation about this (when they finally learned each other's pasts), with the Full Moon Caste trying to mediate between the two of them. The tension remained until the climax of the Siege of Thorns, where the Day and Zenith Caste discovered the remains of the Zeniths family. The Zenith didn't limit break, but she did cry openly about it. The Day Caste comforted her friend as she realized the object of her vengeance had died a long time ago. The Siege of Thorns included the following:
                          • A duel between the Full Moon Caste and Red Iron Rebuke, ending in Rebuke's death when the Zenith and Day Caste came in the nick of time
                          • The Queen of Lyceum purifying the Thorns Shadowland with Benediction of Archgenesis while her circles Dawn Caste savages the dead hordes before they get near her.
                          • The Black Chase Circle Leader giving a chilling monologue to the Mask of Winters, and actually intimidating him with it.
                          *The Fall of Winters* campaign ended with the (ahem) Fall of the Mask of Winters, several of his Abyssal Champions either died or abandoned him, Juggernaut was disassembled in the fighting, collapsing into a heap of fetid rotting flesh. Currently Thorns is recovering, the Zenith taking the mantle of Queen of Thorns and the Day Caste opting to be her Spymaster (claiming to have no patience for political minutia of ruling). The Full Moon rules at her side as Prince Consort.


                          So as it stands there is and Abyssal kingdom in the Black Chase Shadowland, a Solar Kingdom south of Great Forks that has been throwing it's metaphorical weight around with regime change and sorcerous crafts. An Infernal Kingdom hellbent (heh) on conquering the Hundred Kingdoms as well as various city-states across the Maruto River. And now two Solar led kingdoms at the westernmost edge of the Scavenger Lands. That's how the Scavenger Lands stand right now (of course this is all itself a truncated summary of events, if I got into EVERYTHING then I'd be typing here for days)
