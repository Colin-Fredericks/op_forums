                                            I like Fist of the Titans. Especially its doom-combo.

                                            The doom-combo is using Chamber Fist Charge (preferably two purchases of such, so that you only have to do it once) to build up to Titanic Fist Devastation, which can trigger a reflexive Mammoth Slam Quake (which benefits from the Charges spent on TFD), which allows the reflexive activation of Crater-Gouging Blow (which benefits from the Charges spent on TFD). Each of these earth-elemental Evocations chains into each other, and all are built around the hammer's defining mechanic.

                                            It's beautiful, and you could technically do it every other turn (if you're a Solar or Dragon-Blood with the Charge repurchase), but it costs you a minimum of 14m, 1wp, and 3i every time.


                                            Something that bothers me about Fist of the Titans is that it was printed out before a non-elemental, hammer-centric martial art Style.

                                            It's a white jade hammer very much based around earth elementalism, but it has non-earth elemental Evocations that seem like they could have been non-elemental hammer Charms; Unstoppable Hammering Blow, Bone-Crushing Strike, Colossal Whirling Strike...

                                            Bone-Crushing Strike and Colossal Whirling Strike are also notable in being Charms that don't necessarily play into the Fist's unique Charge mechanic or its doom-combo, despite serving as gateways to the capstone of that combo. But as I'll explain, they do serve purposes...


                                            Unstoppable Hammering Blow seems to interact with Titanic Fist Devastation, which is perhaps enough of a smashing attack to benefit from the +1 accuracy, though it doesn't seem to need the Defense penalty waiver. The Evocation can even be gained for free. It definitely fits and helps with the weapon's emphasis on smashing attacks, but so would the kind of hammer Martial Art Style I'd like to see. It does not involve the Charge mechanic at all.


                                            Bone-Crushing Strike can also supplement Titanic Fist Devastation. But it doesn't synergize with the doom-combo. In fact, in some ways, it seems to interfere.

                                            Titanic Fist Devastation cannot trigger Mammoth Slam Quake without causing at least three levels of damage, and Bone-Crushing Strike trades away a level of damage to inflict a crippling penalty. Maybe that health level stills counts, but one of the Evocations should explicitly clarify the matter.

                                            More arguably worse, the crippling penalty doesn't make someone more vulnerable to Mammoth Slam Quake, which seems like a wasted opportunity. The entire weapon is building towards Titanic Fist Devastation and half of the doom-combo hinges on Mammoth Slam Quake succeeding in rendering your victims prone.

                                            Also, since Bone-Crushing Strike requires one Charge to use, it competes with Titanic Fist Devastation for Charges.

                                            On the other hand, the doom-combo is expensive, and using Bone-Crushing Strike against a dueling opponent or the most dangerous of multiple opponents at the beginning of a fight could really help survive Charging up every other turn for the rest of the fight (since it penalizes attack pools) or help keep an opponent from disengaging (since you can't Rush and Aim at the same time). Leaving yourself with an odd number of Charges also points to Avalanche Hammer Attack and Colossal Whirling Strike.

                                            Having 3+ Charges for later also lets you use Bone-Crushing Strike later on your target for TFD, which might not necessarily be the target of the initial Bone-Crushing Strike. Or else that target might have used Anathema devilry to avoid or remove the crippling penalty.

                                            Maybe an upgrade following Titanic Fist Devastation could apply the crippling penalty of Bone-Crushing Strike to the opposed roll of Mammoth Slam Quake.


                                            Colossal Whirling Strike is so much fun that I ask why it should be given to one specific hammer.

                                            The previously mentioned possible upgrade to Bone-Crushing Strike would also serve as an upgrade to this Evocation, since its reset condition requires beating a significant opponent's opposed roll with Mammoth Slam Quake.

                                            However, Colossal Whirling Strike doesn't properly synergize with the doom-combo. Oh, the damage bonus to Titanic Fist Devastation is definitely important, but knocking your target out to medium range removes them from the radius of Mammoth Slam Quake, and thus also prevents them from being a valid target of Crater-Gouging Blow.

                                            On the other hand, using Colossal Whirling Strike can be very useful for buying yourself time to build up Charges.

                                            It doesn't seem worthwhile to combo Bone-Crushing Strike and Colossal Whirling Strike together, though. There's little point to using BCS twice against the same opponent (and would running out of Charge after the first invocation exempt you from paying the mote cost of the second invocation?), and if you're going to be standing around Charging up, your opponent doesn't need to succeed at Rush actions. But they might have those particular Rush Charms that immediately close gaps, and you might decide to tactically retreat instead of Charging up.

                                            EDIT: Colossal Whirling Strike isn't two separate attack rolls; it's mechanically a single attack. You wouldn't invoke or pay for Bone-Crushing Strike twice.


                                            So, all in all, it's a very lovely hammer. Especially if you have someone protecting you while you Charge.

                                            Ah, it's worth mentioning that Chambered Fist Charge very much does count as an Aim action, so while it might seem like a poor man's Strength-Increasing Exercise, it definitely offers a unique advantage towards immediately attacking someone after using it.

                                                EDIT: Got too far ahead of myself.

                                                Robert confirmed that Mammoth Slam Quake requires an addition three Charges to extend its range, separate from the variable Charge cost to activate the Charm and intensify its effect.

                                                Also that the health level sacrificed to Bone-Crushing Strike does not count towards the minimum necessary to trigger a reflexive Mammoth Slam Quake with Titanic Fist Devastation.


                                                One of the most powerful, internal combinations with Fist of the Titans is to spend at least four turns building up eight points of Charge, and then unleash a single attack of Titanic Fist Devastation with at least three charges behind it, but no more than five.

                                                Unstoppable Hammering Blow adds a non-Charm dice to the attack dice-pool.

                                                Solar-exclusive Avalanche Hammer Attack triggers as long as the hammer has at least three points of Charge; it doesn't spend them. The Solar can spend 3m to use Rising Sun Slash without using a full Melee Excellency.

                                                Colossal Whirling Strike adds dice of bonus damage and knocks the target out to medium range.

                                                If you spent four rather than five points of Charge on Titanic Fist Devastation, you can pay for Bone-Crushing Strike. Beware that the health level you trade away for the crippling penalty does not count towards the necessary minimum!

                                                If you inflicted at least three levels of damage, a reflexive Mammoth Slam Quake can ride the variable number of Charges you spent on Titanic Fist Devastation, but now you need to spend another three Charges to extend its range out to medium, catching the target of Colossal Whirling Strike (and anyone else in the radius).

                                                Successfully knocking over any significant opponent in that radius resets Colossal Whirling Strike AND allows you to trigger Crater-Gouging Blow, helping you recover a bit of Initiative after being reset to base.


                                                This is a beautiful, brutal machine of smashing, in the hands of a Resonant master.

                                                You actually use just about EVERY single Evocation within a single round, all against the same target, though it takes a long time to set up. Could be worth it if you have a friend with solid Defend Other capability.

                                                    I’m pretty happy that this read-through is really helping me learn new words since I didn’t know what a knuckle is until now.

                                                    The art here is good, very dynamic. I probably just gonna stop commenting on the art from now on, I feel like I'm complaining too much and it's not really fun at all.

                                                    So, this is Frost-Thorn Knuckles, which is made from Moonsilver instead of Blue Jade? Sooo the material all comes from the North so it’s not that unreasonable, also, in East Asian (or just Chinese?), the Moon is can sometimes be called Supreme Yin (太陰), and Yin is all about coldness so I’m not quite surprised when moon-theme things also has ice in it powerset, I mean, sun-theme artifacts usually involve shooting hot flame as its main schtick, so why can’t moon artifacts shoots moon ice :’D

                                                    Though I’m a little curious if there is anything else in the book that is similar to Frost-Thorn, a quick skim and there is a Green Jade sword with Red Jade theme and Orichalcum sword with Blue Jade theme.

                                                    I think that my preferred way to approach this kind of Artifact is using the Impossible Creatures (IC) method instead of a lego one, so basically IC is some really old game where you can combine animal’s part into chimera i.e Flying Dogotus with Batwing and Wasp Stinger can be a real thing. And the game doesn’t really say “You can’t combine this part with this part”, as opposed to lego where piece X need to be correctly connected to piece A, it’s kinda the same with making Artifact in Exalted, as long as there is some kind of “glue” that binds or connect the theme together, I’m fine with things like Storm Caller and Burning Branch, it opens up some very interesting themes for Evocation, like, a Soulsteel sword being able to create burning black flame since there is sealed fire elemental inside, or a living Green Jade rose being encased inside a block of magic ice allowing the user to call up blood-sucking ice rose from the ground.

Just imagine it, firm and strong muscle harden by the Northern wind for you to bury your face in, the head of an apex predator perfectly designed for kissing, claws of silver ice used exclusively to hold your hands through the festival...To create these dark thoughts swirling in my maiden heart, the writer of this weapon must be a disciple of Satan himself!?
