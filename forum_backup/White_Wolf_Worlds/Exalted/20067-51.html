                                                            It's a traditional division they used because Kindred of the East used it. Kindred, however, was based on a dualistic power scale of yin and yang energies and the po allowed the Kuei-Jin to share some of the Cainites' struggles with the beast without dipping into Christian teachings. The hun and po were integrated into the mechanics and themes of the game, which fits my criteria for a useful division.

                                                            And you can have ghosts without delving into theological issues. The vast majority of ghost stories do not involve theological issues. What the hun and po' division does in Exalted is allow you to answer niche questions that fans on internet forums are likely to ask, which doesn't fit my criteria for a useful division.

                                                            Autochthon gobbling human souls presents the same problems as Autochthon gobbling po souls: less people being born.

                                                            Some ghosts moving on while others linger and make your life difficult is feature of a large number of belief systems, even those that don't recognize a split soul.
