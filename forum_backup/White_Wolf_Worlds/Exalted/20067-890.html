                                        Originally posted by Braininthejar View Post
                                        And as for Paralysing Combustion Imbuement - if you have mastery, and hit a creature of darkness for 3 wound levels, it is totally screwed even if it's a death knight, right? Set on fire until he takes down the stylist, while being forced to keep moving away from him? Or can he spend willpower to stop retreating or something?
                                        He's not forced to move away at all. He can't move away if he's in Close range, and even at Short or longer range he has to disengage to reflexively move away.

                                        Also it's a -1 wound penalty that isn't stackable. If you deal 3 damage with it the penalty is only -1 still, and it has to raise their penalty above their stamina, so if they're Stamina 3, you need to get their penalty up to -4.
