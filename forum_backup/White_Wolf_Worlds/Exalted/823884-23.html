    Through knowledge of a thing, one can begin to understand its nature. Through knowledge of nature, one can transfigure the understanding of a thing. When the exalt knows at least 5 pieces of knowledge revealed via Unbound Knowledge, they may unleash a special gambit attack against foe that warps a quality of their nature. This attack is a shaping effect and is subject to any rules that interact with such.
    • On a successful gambit at difficulty equal to the target’s Essence, the exalt chooses an aspect to alter in their target. For every 2 threshold successes on the gambit roll, the exalt may select an additional effect to inflict upon their foe.
    • Alter the emotional context of a Tie or swap the context (positive/negative) of a Principle
    • Swap the intensities of two revealed Intimacies
    • Swap the rating of a revealed combat ability with that of a revealed non-combat ability
    • Swap Personal/Peripheral essence pools
    • Swap Essence and Willpower ratings. Temporary Willpower above the new permanent rating is reduced by an equal number
    • Other effects may be negotiated with Storyteller. All effects must be a swapping of current states, never created or eliminated ones

    All changes last for the rest of the scene before fading away and reverting to normal.
    Author's notes

                Ok so not quite as epic as the Book of Weaponised History but I'm posting this up anyway. Earth Cracker is a hammer inspired from one of my circle mates in the game I'm taking part in that adds some defence benefits at first then causes minor aggressive until the capstone. Any comments or feed back is welcome as usual

                Earth Cracker (Artifact 3; White Jade Goremaul)

                During the First Age, the mortal White Obelisk exalted as a member of the Dawn Caste for defence during the siege of the palace of Baiya. Her first task was a retaliation attack but before she committed she was inspired to forge a weapon that could allow her and her alone to lead a siege.

                Earth Cracker was the answer. Having bested a greater earth elemental, Multifaceted Terrani, to a duel it agreed to help the young Solar channel her dream. In the depths of his court, they cold forged the hammer with the force of Creations earth itself. Next the head of the hammer was carved and molten orichalcum poured into the recesses of the faces and forged into a collar where the head meets haft.

                When the Usurpation took place Obelisk became furious at the betrayal and assaulted the island where a garrison of traitors was holed up. The weapon and wielder both full of wrath sank the island and killed the betrayers with their death throes.

                The huge head of the weapon has been carved from a single large piece of white jade to depict the image of a ram on either side, with a collar just before the rams head of orichalcum leading to the haft of the weapon. The haft is decorated in old realm runes, tailored to more specific sentences and grammar of earth elementals. The weapon has a hearth stone socket between the ram heads.

                Evocations of Earth Cracker
                Solars and Dragon-Blooded attuning to Earth Cracker feel heavily attuned to the earth around them. They gain their essence in automatic successes to resist knock down or knock back.

                Raise the Stones Bones
                Cost: 3m Mins: Essence 1
                Type: Reflexive or Simple
                Keywords: Withering Only
                Duration: Instant or One Scene
                Prerequisites: None

                The earth and dirt never forget, holding generations of fallen comrades, family and friends. When the wielder of Earth Cracker invokes Raise the Stones Bones, she strikes the ground and forces a large slab of stone to rent the earth and prevent another fallen comrade.

                Raise the Stones Bones creates a protective barrier which provides heavy cover; when used as a Reflexive defend other action, the Evocation adds the wielders (Essence) to their own Parry or Evasion and improving soak by 2 from up to short range before breaking apart at the end of the round.

                At Essence 2+ and cost of 6m, 1wp Raise the Stones Bones can create (Essence x2) heavy cover barriers as a Simple action out to (Essence) range bands that last for the scene, but provide no benefit to defences or soak.

                At the end of a round or scene, depending on how the Evocation was used, the stone pillars drop away into tiny multifaceted pieces of stone, dirt and escaping essence.

                Special Activation Rules
                If used in its Simple format the Raise the Stones Bones can only be used once a scene, unless the wielder of the weapon systematically destroys each pillar, releasing the essence back into the earth.

                Tremor Incanting Method
                Cost: 3m, 2i Mins: Essence 1
                Type: Simple
                Keywords: None
                Duration: Instant
                Prerequisites: None

                As the hammer strikes the ground, the spirits residing within answer the call and bring forth a rapid shaking like which follows an earthquake.
                Tremor Incanting Method creates a knock down effect affecting all who are within short range. All trivial opponents fall prone without contest while battlegroups and non trivial enemies such as
                Exalted, spirits and mortal heroes must pass a Stamina+Athletics difficulty 4.

                At Essence 3+ Tremor Incanting Method draws forth even more of the roiling aggression beneath the soil that causes the ground to shudder, shake and split in the affected area that creates and environmental hazard that causes 3B at difficulty 3 for Essence turns.

                Invoking Terrani’s Fangs
                Cost: 6m Mins: Essence 2
                Type: Simple
                Keywords: Withering Only
                Duration: Instant
                Prerequisites: Raise the Stone Bones, Tremor Incanting Method

                With an immense war cry, the wielder bellows out for Terrani who still resides within the bones of the world to join his essence with the weapons again. As the essence of weapon and spirit mingle, a terrifying fang of stone careens out of the earth ripping the ground as it travels to its target before colliding and crumbling into the stones that formed it.

                Invoking Terrani’s Fangs creates a withering attack that can attack up to long range. At close range the attack roll gains double 7s and knocks enemies prone, short range gains double 8s and the enemy must resist falling prone with a Stamina+Athletics at difficulty 4, medium range double 9s and long range provides no additional benefit beyond attacking at that range.

                As a repurchase, the wielder can spend an additional 2m, Invoking Terrani’s Fangs doesn’t destroy the “fang” afterwards but instead bulwarks it with additional jutting slabs. For the rest of the scene the fang remains as an additional piece of heavy cover that isn’t affected by Raise the Stones Bones limit.

                At Essence 3+ for an additional 1wp the wielder can use the fangs eruption to destroy stone pillars already called forth by Raise the Stone Bones. Adding this provides the wielder with (Essence) non-charm automatic successes on a withering attack roll, as well as converting (Essence) post soak damage dice. This improvement cannot gain any benefit from the previous Essence based improvement.

                Special Activation Rules
                The wielder must have Strength 4+ and a speciality with the weapon or during a siege.

                Rending the Worlds Form
                Cost: 8m, 1wp, 3a Mins: Essence 3
                Type: Simple
                Keywords: Perilous
                Duration: Instant
                Prerequisites: Invoking Gaia’s Fangs

                Plunging the head of the hammer into the ground beneath the wielders feet small rents around the hammer blow span outward, causing orbs of white-grey essence to escape the cracks in the world. As the head is released from the earth, a shockwave ripples along the rented lines and the full power comes into effect.

                Rending the Worlds Form causes an earthquake and rented fissures out to six range band that demolishes buildings, entraps living beings and kills the unwary who are too close.
                Within the first two range bands all mundane structures are destroyed without contest and all living beings suffer damage as if they have fallen from medium height. Trivial opponents suffer the effects without contest and mightier opponents, such as gods and the Exalted roll Stamina+Athletics at difficulty 4.
                At range bands three and four the shock wave causes 30 dice of damage against mundane structures and causes living enemies to suffer damage as if falling from a short range band after failing a Stamina+Athletics difficulty 4.
                At the last two range bands the effects dwindle out, causing 15 dice of damage to mundane structures and causing living enemies to fall prone after a much easier roll at difficulty 2

                Special Activation Rules
                The wielder must have Strength 4+ and a speciality with the weapon or during a siege. Rending the Worlds Form can only be used once per story, its reset condition requiring the wielder to bury the weapon in a cavern anywhere on Creation for three nights and three days with earthy smelling incense burning. During this time the weapon cannot be unearth prematurely or the ritual must begin again.

                    Originally posted by ZeroBadguy View Post
                    I am having trouble coming up with an artifact flame piece for a character I am designing. I would like to supplement rather than improve Righteous Devil Style but I am having trouble even conceptualizing something that won't be unbalanced.
                    Woo flame pieces and RDS!

                    Okay, so, first thing to talk about is what way you want to supplement RDS. What is your character bringing, as a concept, to the Righteous Devil Style? Is your character a cautious justicar, trying to compromise before letting loose the fires of judgment? Then perhaps more social evocations, or gambits aiming to disable rather than kill. Is she a rugged in-your-face vigilante who shoots first and asks questions later, sure of her own righteousness? Then perhaps an altered flame piece that works more like a short-range blunderbuss or shotgun, aiming to punish enemies for getting close. Or maybe he's a surgical one-man strike force, going for accuracy, crippling, and stealth--admittedly hard for a FLAME weapon, but the evocations could bring in some momentary blinding and disorientation while they stay on the move, never giving his target a chance to counter.

                    Basically, if you're looking to supplement, rather than embody, then you need to consider how your character is different from that Unnamed Solar, the original Righteous Devil. What new approach is your character bringing to the table?

                                As for the one-man strike force, you'd want evocations that work toward mixing survivability and pressure.                                         Threw this together on a whim since I couldn't sleep.
                                        I adapted and expanded on the old 2.5 charm, from the Ink Monkeys (Thousand-Arms Prana, to be exact), until I had something fun out of it.
                                        I'm sure someone here will find a use for this because I don't think we'll be using it in this upcoming campaign.

                                        Grand Forge of the Desert Tyrant (Orichalcum Artifact ****):
                                        Attunement: 7m
                                        Prerequisites:
                                        Martial Arts/Archery/Melee/Thrown 5
                                        A Definitive Intimacy towards upholding ideals of Justice and Heroism.

                                        Lore/Fluff:

                                        A Solar knelt in the bloodstained sands, his essence spent, his will gone.
                                        The Wyld ruler who had claimed the far Southern steppes.
                                        With his thirteen greatest knights beside him.
                                        The tattered remains of his grand army stood before the Solar.
                                        Only he remained between them and the edges of Creation.
                                        A fight he could not win.
                                        A battle that was already lost.
                                        A war that had ended the moment it had begun.
                                        Yet the Solar would not accept defeat.
                                        In the falling ashes of his sorrow.
                                        The Solar answered the Desert's roar.
                                        He signed the Tyrant's Pact,
                                        And the Tyrant bestowed upon his soul.
                                        A piece of the Dragon's majestic flames.

                                        In it's inactive state, the Grand Forge of the Tyrant exists as a simple orichalcum pendant with a single, inlaid socket for a hearthstone. Upon placing a Solar, Fire, or Earth hearthstone within the socket, the Solar makes an unbreakable pact with the Tyrant of the Sands. The Elder Dragon bestows upon the Solar a fragment of his eternal fire in exchange for the Solar's service as the Will of the Tyrant's Ideals. The pendant and the hearthstone melt into a river of essence and flow into the Solar's, becoming a mystic code of draconic runes etched onto the Solar's own skin.

                                        His mystic code is the bridge between his essence and the Grand Forge of the Tyrant. A mythical workshop of the a Solar from the first age, he shaped the Forge from the wyld itself, until it was a reflection of his soul. To this day, the Forge exists far away in the Wylds, lost and distant, protected by dragon fire that incinerates all those who would enter its boundary. Five towers, arranged in a pentagram, each powered by three greater hearthstones: one Solar, one of Fire, and one of Earth, hold the Grand Forge stable against the ravaging chaos.
                                        Essence pours from the towers, flowing in streams of golden motes that saturate the atmosphere, wavering in the heat of the flames. The sound of countless heavy hammers falling on anvils fills the air, the sound deafening as the give birth to new arms.



                                        The Grand Forge smiths the raw essence in the atmosphere into new weapons, each crafted to fit the soul of its chosen hero of justice, imbued with the inferno of the Tyrant's Fire, unable to wielded by any other and costing no motes for him to attune to. The weapons it forges are stored Elsewhere: sharpened, polished, and loaded, waiting for their master to will them from their storage to his hands. In addition, the Forge holds extra slots for the Solar, letting him store weapons of his choosing in Elsewhere as well, where the Grand Forge will protect and even repair them.

                                        The Forge is able to create infinite copies artifact weapons, each without a soul and lacking any evocations, but he may only retrieve up to one set at a time from Elsewhere, using Wind War God's Armory to do so (Paired weapons such as war fans, slashing swords, pieces, etc. count as one set), and retrieving a new set of weapons from the forge causes the previous set to fade into essence, disappearing in a golden cloud of motes.

                                        Only upon becoming the Will of the Tyrant may the Solar attune with the pendant and unlock it's evocations. Furthermore, as long as he remains the Will of the Tyrant, the Solar retains all the effects granted by his Hearthstone, and regenerates an additional mote of essence outside of combat.

                                        Evocations of the Grand Forge of the Tyrant:
                                        Passive:
                                        While attuned with the Grand Forge, whenever the Solar draws weapon brought forth from it's flames, he may treat it as if he had the Quickdraw (****) merit for any ability.

                                        Wind War God's Armory
                                        Cost: – (1m); Type: Permanent; Duration: Indefinite
                                        Keywords: Stacking
                                        Requirements: N/A

                                        The Lawgiver may pay one mote to indefinitely store up to (Essence x2) weapons Elsewhere, hidden away in the boundary between nothing, including close- and long-range weapons, in addition to the weapons created and stored by the forge. This passive also expressly permits the storage of shields.
                                        The Solar may reflexively store as many weapons as she wishes Elsewhere, at a rate of one mote per (Essence x 2) weapons.
                                        To retrieve a weapon stored in this manner, or one of the generic artifact weapons produced by the forge, the Solar must concentrate on that weapon and pay an additional 1m mote, reflexively causing the weapon to summon itself from Elsewhere, falling downward into the ground before her, within arms reach for her to immediately draw without penalty.

                                        As long as the Solar maintains an Essence commitment to this Charm, he may sheath weapons in Elsewhere without cost, up to her storage limit. The Solar may release all the motes committed to storing his weapons in Elsewhere as a Simple action, causing them to fall to the ground around the him in a circle ([Essence x 2] x [number of committed motes released]) yards in diameter, in addition to an uncountable assortment of generic artifact weapons.

                                        That Which Defies Heaven– Endless Claws of the Tyrant:
                                        Cost: 7m, 1wp; Type: Reflexive; Duration: Scene
                                        Keywords: Dual
                                        Requirements: Wind War God's Armory

                                        From around the Solar, countless portals to Elsewhere open, held open in a swirling pool of essence motes. From this array of essence, the Grand Forge releases weapon after weapon, firing them in a deadly Rain of Blades. A hail of swords, spears, axes, halberds, every known blade in creation pours forth from the Grand Forge, becoming golden bands of light that tear through foes that dare challenge the Tyrant's ideals.

                                        Once deployed, the Solar may use the Grand Forge as a weapon for his attacks, allowing him to make attacks at up to Medium range, crushing his foe in a withering storm of blades.
                                        The Grand Forge is Heavy Artifact Ranged weapon with the weapon tags Archery (Medium) and Lethal, that explicitly allows it's attacks to be used with Archery and Righteous Devil Form.
                                        When he makes an attack, the countless blades launched by the Grand Forge work together, pincering the enemy and granting the him double 9's on his attack roll.


                                        The Living Archive That Bequeaths New Blades:
                                        Cost: 10m, 1wp; Type: Simple; Duration: Scene
                                        Requirements: That Which Defies Heaven– Striking Claws of the Tyrant

                                        The Grand Forge is a record, the collection of every memory it's Creator had of war.
                                        A sword that he touched, he instinctively understood.
                                        It's memories and evocations were laid bare to him, and he knew the sword as if it were a part of his soul as well.
                                        Thus, the Solar who raised this forge archived a record of every weapon and arm that has existed in creation in his time. Lost within this library are weapons from the First Age so ancient, so carefully forged with evocations of magic so grand that little today could compare. Every artifact today can be said to have descended from some legendary artifact hidden within the Forge's archives, and so the Forge knows every weapon and evocation that could possibly exist.

                                        The Solar opens his mind in meditation, letting the Forge touch his soul and carry his spirit Elsewhere. Within the Grand Forge, the Solar may search the lost archives, and with the aid of the Fires gifted by the Dragon Tyrant, craft from his own Essence a new artifact weapon with a single evocation of his own design, or a single evocation he witness used by another's weapon. From then on, the Forge will produce a copy of the weapon for the Solar to draw from Elsewhere as he wills.
                                        The Lawgiver may re-purchase this evocation up to (Essence*2) times, each time forging a new blade or arm.
                                        The Solar should work with the Storyteller to help fashion new artifact weapons that both approve of.

                                        Sample Artifacts produced by this evocation:

                                        Upon the battlefield, the Solar knelt...
                                        「Avon Sign: Socratic Spring & Romantic Autumn」 (M. White and Black Jade Artifact Straight Swords: ***)
                                        Prerequisite: Essence 1
                                        Lore:

                                        Twin married blades, one black and one white, that were once the personal blades of the Solar who's spirit raised the Forge of Heaven. The Solar may choose to draw either blade or both when he wills Spring and Autumn to him. As once they were an extension of the nameless legendary blacksmith, these swords resonate with desire for justice in the Lawmaker's heart. Spring & Autumn are unique. As they were the Nameless Solar's personal blades, the Forge of Heaven is naturally attuned to them, and is able to create a new copies immediately, without delay.

                                        Evocation:「Nameless Sign: Triple Linked Crane Wings」 (Cost:3m 3i; Type: Supplemental; Duration: Instant):
                                        The Lawmaker is a flurry of blades, in an instant striking three times, pulling three sets of the blade from the Forge of Heaven and cutting down his opponent.
                                        When the Lawmaker successfully strikes a withering attack with Spring or Autumn, he adds (Dex/2, rounded up)+3 to his raw damage, weaving the blade's wife into the attack. If he is using Fire Pieces, both his Fire Pieces are considered unloaded after his attack.
                                        ...Intoxicated in Victory

                                        Alone in graveyard of blades...
                                        「Clockwork Sign: Luna Dial Time Alter Blade」 (S. Moonsilver Artifact Knife: ****)

                                            Hey guys, another artifact I churned out today. Feels like a good one even if its cliched for Soulsteel but it serves it purpose. Comments and critique accepted as always.

                                            Sanguine Bartizan (Soulsteel Lamellar)

                                            Created during the Age of Sorrow by Weeping Raiton Ascending, a Liminal hunted across the North by the Wyld Hunt, who was later slain, the armour hidden within a Solar tomb, due to the Liminals shocking resemblance. Before he was slain he had crafted a piece of armour to help him deliver retribution to those who would ignore the boundaries of life and death.

                                            The lamellar is precise and accurate, each plate a work of art in its own right. On the underside, strips of dire bat hide were riveted into place by small green jade rivets onto the backside of soulsteel plates all etched with the imagery of bats in flight, which were to become the essence channels for the blood. The bands, rather than put horizontally were put diagonally instead, creating a mural within the plates.

                                            The complete suit carries on its bat motifs, with the vambraces of the armour having the visage of the bats carved into it and the greaves carrying the wings in its decoration. The full motif is a sky full of bats flying towards a central moon of extremely polished soulsteel where there is a small inset for a hearthstone. All of the blood channels run around this moon and eventually escape going down the dire bat skirt.

                                            Attunement Bonus:
                                            A Solar, Abyssal or Liminal who attunes to the armour can add the higher of Resistance or Integrity to their dying levels. For an additional 2m the armour improves their healing rate by the wearers Essence in hours. This effect stacks with any improved healing rate charms by removing the additional time after the initial charm effects.

                                            Buoyed by Blood
                                            Cost: - Mins: Essence 1, Stamina 3, Resistance 3
                                            Type: Permanent
                                            Keywords: None
                                            Duration: Permanent
                                            Prerequisites: None
                                            Like a boat in the ocean is raised by the waters, so to is the wielder raised by their own blood. As the blood runs down the channels of the armour, it becomes hardened and protects the wearer from further injury due to their own sacrifice.

                                            Buoyed by Blood provides the wearer with +1 additional soak and hardness, but only while the wearer is suffering from wound penalties. This evocation can be purchased up to Essence/2 times (rounding up) for a maximum of +3 soak/hardness.

                                            Magnified in Agony
                                            Cost: 2m, 1lhl Mins: Essence 2
                                            Type: Simple
                                            Keywords: Perilous
                                            Duration: One Scene
                                            Prerequisites: Buoyed in Blood
                                            As blood runs through the armour down the essence channels, the wearer finds themselves lighter, stronger and more determined in their agony.

                                            For the rest of the scene the armour turns wounds penalties into positives. If the wearer of Sanguine Bartizan is Crashed then Magnified in Agony ends and for the following turn their suffering is two-fold and they suffer from twice the normal wound penalties.

                                            Special Activation Rules: Magnified in Agony cannot be purchased with experience and is automatically unlocked the first time the wearer is reduced to Incapacitated through any source of damage dealt through combat (environmental hazards don’t count).

                                            Might through Suffering
                                            Cost: 1m, 1lhl Mins: Essence 2
                                            Type: Reflexive
                                            Keywords: None
                                            Duration: Instant
                                            Prerequisites: Magnified in Agony
                                            The essence in the wearers blood begins to soak back in, sinking into the leather underneath the plates, and wetting the skin of the wearer giving a renewed vigour in their anguish.

                                            The Evocation provides the wearer with additional strength, giving +1 to strength based actions per wound penalty the wearer is suffering from that count as non-charm dice.

                                            Special Activation Rules: Might through Suffering cannot be purchased through experience and is automatically unlocked the first time the wearer survives for at least the Essence minimum in rounds at -4 wound penalties.

                                            Retribution from Martyrdom
                                            Cost: 6m, 1wp, 2a Mins: Essence 1
                                            Type: Simple
                                            Keywords: Decisive-Only, Perilous
                                            Duration: One Scene
                                            Prerequisites: None
                                            Feeding the lost blood with Essence and some of the wearers anima, it causes it to float out of the essence channels and begin to congeal into the air. The blood can take the form of whichever the wielder chooses as long as it is bladed (scythes, sword blades, chakrams etc) and streamers of black brown essence trail behind them.

                                            Retribution from Martyrdom creates a scene long counterattack that lashes out to kill any who attack at close range, doing a decisive attack with a dice pool of (current wound penalties + Essence) and base damage is equal to (current wound penalties x2)

                                            The Evocation doesn't end when the wearer of Sanguine Bartizan is unconscious, instead it carries on protecting them and deals (wound penalties x3) damage instead, attacking all who come within close range. If the wearer is crashed however, the Evocation ends prematurely and cancels all other active Evocations along with it.

                                            Special Activation Rules: Retribution from Martyrdom cannot be purchased through experience and is automatically unlocked the first time the wearer is plunged into dying levels.

                                                    The talk about Righteous Devil Style inspired me to make another flame piece artifact. I originally designed it for synergy with Righteous Devil style, but it kind of went off into its own direction. They should still work together, though.

                                                    Gravewright (Soulsteel and Starmetal Flametongue Repeater), Artifact ••••
                                                    In the end, there is no escape from the grave. Even the gods meet their occasional doom from wandering heroes or monsters...or even each other. The Gravewright embodies this inevitability, though none know who forged it. As far as anyone knows, Gravewright is as old as death itself and made by it, too.

                                                    Gravewright brings not glory to its wielder, but damnation to her enemies. It emits a terrible gravity in combat, punishing anyone who tries to escape judgment. Many a wielder have been Righteous Devils, but this weapon gives even them pause, for it tears away pieces of the soul from those it slays. Many fear wielding it does something similar to oneself, a fear that is perhaps not so unfounded.

                                                    Gravewright first become widely known when used by Laughing Flames, a Lunar Righteous Devil, who used it to slay one of the directional gods of war long ago. This god of war had been one of tactical retreats and repositioning, powers that became useless in the sights of Gravewright. Few details of the battle are still remembered save that the god of war had, in her last moments, begged the Lunar to stop using Gravewright for her own sake.

                                                    The sights and hammer of Gravewright are made of starmetal, shining in contrast against the two over-under soulsteel barrels. The grip, whatever material it actually is, is wrapped in the unbreakable hide of some forgotten beast, said to be the first life Gravewright ever claimed. Gravewright has a single hearthstone socket beneath the barrel, just in front of the trigger guard.

                                                    Gravewright is a medium archery weapon that can hold two shots, which can be reloaded simultaneously. It attunes for five motes.

                                                    Tags: Lethal, Archery (Short), Flame, Mounted, One-Handed, Special

                                                    Evocations of Gravewright
                                                    By attuning for an additional two motes, withering ranged attacks made with Gravewright at close range deal an additional automatic level of withering damage.

                                                    Gravedigging Humility
                                                    Cost: 1m; Mins: Essence 1; Type: Simple
                                                    Keywords: Decisive-Only
                                                    Duration: Instant
                                                    Prerequisites: None

                                                    With either precision or force, a shot from the Gravewright forces a target to the ground, as if they were ready to enter their own grave. This evocation creates a difficulty 2 gambit. If successful, the target is knocked prone, and any disengage action they may have been taking is ended.


                                                    Inescapable Iron Diligence
                                                    Cost: 3m; Mins: Essence 1; Type: Reflexive
                                                    Keywords: None
                                                    Duration: Instant
                                                    Prerequisites: None

                                                    None may escape from the sights of the Gravewright. When an enemy takes a disengage action (regardless of success), the wielder may activate this evocation to reflexively aim at them. If she was already aiming at them (or aims again before attacking), she gains the initiative the target spent to disengage.


                                                    Fear-the-Reaper Mien
                                                    Cost: 5m, 1wp; Mins: Essence 2; Type: Reflexive
                                                    Keywords: Perilous
                                                    Duration: One Scene
                                                    Prerequisites: Gravedigging Humility, Inescapable Iron Diligence

                                                    It is best for enemies of Gravewright's wielder to meekly accept their fates. While this evocation is active, every enemy within medium range of the wielder must roll to rise from prone (instead of just close range), and the difficulty to do so is raised to 4. It also costs an additional initiative to disengage from the wielder.


                                                    Heel-Biting Plume
                                                    Cost: 7m, 1wp; Mins: Essence 2; Type: Reflexive
                                                    Keywords: Decisive-Only
                                                    Duration: Instant
                                                    Prerequisites: Fear-the-Reaper Mien

                                                    It's not the same as cutting, but an Achilles tendon burned away is still useless. After knocking down a target with an aimed Gravedigging Humility, the wielder may activate this evocation to immediately roll her remaining initiative to deal damage, as with a normal decisive attack. The target is forced to accept a crippling injury that impairs their mobility (generally an injured leg), negating as much of the damage as possible. This does not count against the once per story limit for crippling injuries. Once per fight, with no reset.


                                                    Cloak of Lost Souls
                                                    Cost: 1m or 4m, 1wp; Mins: Essence 3; Type: Reflexive
                                                    Keywords: Decisive-Only
                                                    Duration: Instant
                                                    Prerequisites: Heel-Biting Plume

                                                    Not even death is an escape from the Gravewright. After slaying a target while Fear-the-Reaper Mien is active, the wielder may spend a single mote to rip their hun soul (or equivalent) from their body and bind it to Gravewright. For all intents and purposes, they are permanently slain, similar to Ghost-Eating Technique. Reducing the size of a battle group usually allows at least one soul each time. The souls may optionally become visible when the wielder's anima is at burning or above, a river of silent torment flowing from Gravewright.

                                                    At any time, the wielder may simply choose to release the soul, whereupon it either dissolves or returns to Lethe, as appropriate to its nature. They are also released like this upon the death of the wielder. What Gravewright does with the wielder's own soul is unknown.

                                                    Before a decisive attack roll, the wielder may activate this charm again for four motes and one temporary willpower to fire one of her captive souls, whirling and screaming as it burns into nothingness at last. The shot does not require ammunition, and one die of raw damage is converted into an automatic level of damage, plus one more die converted for each stunt level awarded to the attack (if any). It also has an additional effect according to the nature of the soul used. Some examples are as follows:

                                                    Chilling: The souls of the Abyssal Exalted and other deathly creatures (such as ghosts) chill instead of burn. If hit, for the rest of the scene the target takes a -2 penalty on all actions. They may take a miscellaneous action (which may be flurried) to warm themselves back up to remove the penalty, if they have some means to do so.

                                                    Corrosive: Some souls, along with the essence of demons, seethe with great vitriol, firing chemical lances instead of flame. If hit, the target's withering soak is reduced by 2 for the rest of the scene. This may be undone by a miscellaneous action to wipe themselves off, which may be flurried.

                                                    Holy: The souls of the Solar Exalted along with other holy (more or less) beings burn away the impure. The attack deals aggravated damage to creatures touched by darkness, such as natives of Malfeas or the Underworld.

                                                    Humble: The souls of many mortals burn only dimly, making the attack difficult to see. The target must roll (Wits + Awareness) at difficulty 4, or suffer a -2 penalty to their defense.

                                                    Shifting: The souls of the Lunar Exalted and other shapeshifters lend their protean nature to the shot, giving it a form that molds itself to fit around (or through) any cover less than full, negating its benefits.

                                                    This list is not exhaustive, nor is it required to use these exact effects for souls of the same type (a particularly bitter Solar could yield a corrosive soul, for example). Unique souls give unique effects when fired.
