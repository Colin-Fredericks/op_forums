                                                            Originally posted by Robert Vance View Post
                                                            The destruction of your godly domain is never a great thing. Continuing worship doesn't hurt, but they're going to want a reassignment as god of something else.
                                                            I'm imagining a little corner of Yu-Shan where the unemployed city gods all gather, spending their meager ambrosia allowance from the bureau to hire laborers in order to produce facsimilies of parts of the cities they were from in a sort of mashup of all of them. The younger ones might still believe in re-assignment but the older ones know creation is losing cities, not gaining them. Some of the venerable ones, grown mad with time, scheme and plot to get together to get an Exigence so they could have an agent in creation to support their aims of seeing their cities sprout anew. Problem is, some new cities (and their gods) stand where the old ones used to be...

                                                            Digression aside, my question is this (and requires some explanation):

                                                            In my current game there is a Twilight who loves the idea of "sound". The thrum of essence, she calls it. Her character explores this through music mostly so she is a musician and will go to great lengths to acquire ancient instruments or musical knowledge. A session ago the circle was exploring a valley controlled by a vengeful and corrupted wind god who laid claim over all sound (in his domain area). He did such by enslaving the local air elementals to constantly blow the wind so loudly that none could hear anything but it without yelling. The god's curse was due to his hatred of music and "mortal voices, so shrill in temperment". The Twilight (and circle) calmed the Elementals down through various methods and broke their servitude (and vanquished the complicit one), allowing the peasants in a nearby village to have a festival. With music. Players have the best motivations

                                                            Anyways, the Wind God (Ruu the Thrumming Wind) showed up during the festival while Twilight and circle were plastered. The villagers DID warn them it might happen but due to social loafing everyone assumed everyone else would be on guard duty and chose to get drunk instead. Fantastic. The God realized he was dealing with Exalts he went with a curse over attempted-and-probably-failed-murder. Thanks, Measure the Wind! So I decided the curse would be anytime the Twilight hears music, gradually it becomes drowned out by the sound of howling wind in his mind until he hears it roaring. It gradually dissipates after the music stops (not immediate).


                                                            How would you recommend mechanically representing such a curse? Any ideas for an appropriate method for breaking it besides sorcerous workings/killing the wind god? (I'd think the latter wouldn't necessarily work anyways for making the curse go away). I consider the god levying the curse to be roughly as powerful as Ahlat in terms of raw purview expression. The Twilight has 0 dots of Integrity and does not use sorcery (though the party Dawn does).

                                                            Sorry for the length of question. Looking forward to seeing how you guys handled shedding the terrestrial tag for IMA, I experimented with that in homebrew and am interested to see if I got close to what you guys came up with.
