            On Liminals, I'd also note that the spoilers have not said that the Intimacy a Liminal needs to their "creator" needs to have a context of the emotion that inspired the failed resurrection that was empowered and gave rise to them. Frankenstein has been cited as a Liminal inspiration, and while the Creature in that is pretty clearly a Marrow Aspect, created because of Victor Frankenstein's curiosity and obsession, I would describe the Creature's Intimacy towards him as being much more one of "Anger due to abandonment". And I really doubt that Blood Aspects will all be forced to love or lust for their creators - that would be both creepy, and far too restrictive, cutting off quite a lot of interesting stories. Like, what if a Blood Aspect was created when a woman wanted to resurrect her dead wife, but the new Liminal is asexual, and just wants to be friends with her? Or is obsessed with figuring out her "past life", and only views the woman who tried the resurrection as a link to someone who knew "herself"?

            Point is, I doubt very much they're going to be so restrictive on the Intimacy Liminals need to have to a person.
