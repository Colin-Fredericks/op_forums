                            There would be no new timing. Aura still ignites at the end of your turn, but then once your tick ends, all Charms you use from that point until the end of your next turn count towards your next Aura.

DB vs Solar combat is like, the culmination of an epic story arc that's full of fighting everything that isn't a Solar.                                                 Which is kind of required by the way the setting works, the Realm and Lookshy call a time-out when anathema are spotted nearby to go deal with them together. At that point a full excellency gives you 24 attack dice without a stunt or using Crossfire Flash, and you're clashing it's best attack with 13 dice, and each time you win a clash your excellency becomes cheaper and cheaper, all the way to 0m. Even if you lose that clash it's damage is only 12, minimum 3, and no way to boost it or key special effects off of it, which means your artifact armor will soak it all up and plink 2, maybe 3 initiative off of you.                                                 I think a DB archer might be in some trouble, but otherwise all the other combat styles have ways that let them 1v1 a Lizard. Melee is just the one that's nearly 100% effective. Also bonus points for unleashing a Harnessed Firestorm Assault at somewhere around 35 initiative to almost clear the entire health track in one single action. Then the turn after you can Burning Fury Wreath to take out the last one.
