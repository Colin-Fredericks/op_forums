                                                            You know what time of the year it is?

                                                            "Gonzo's sketch factory RELOADED: HvSf edition 2"

                                                            Time to open the factory once more! The rules are simple: give me a brief description of any of your characters. Some general traits (exalt type, caste, favorite weapon, etc), and in this case, a picture if you already have art of the character.

                                                            The rules are pretty much the same:

                                                            I will choose THREE characters, and make a sketch of them in Heaven's Strife art style. I suggest that you submit characters with interesting traits, as it will be less likely to be chosen if his/her only defining characteristics are hair and robe color. This time let's try to go for broke, so Exigent, Liminal, or even Getimian concepts will get special attention.

                                                            You can see the previous edition's sketches here.

                                                            So Heaven or Hell...

                                                            Let's Rock!
