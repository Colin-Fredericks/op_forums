        I want to expand on why I'm not looking for HDNP content. If I'm going to have to re-work all the numbers and abilities and Intimacies of an antagonist and their entourage to challenge my Celestial Circle, I already own Creatures of the Wyld, Games of Divinity, Roll of Glorious Divinity I and II, literally 11 Celestial/Terrestrial directions books with a monster chapter each, etc. My players are all pretty new to Exalted, and I'm the one whose enthusiasm (and willingness to ST) makes the table happen, so just because Ligier was first published something like 18 years ago, does not dampen his glory in the least when he is introduced to my table (I have not yet done so). Like, who has run out of cool published antagonists for Exalted? I want that group's schedule.

        What I want are a full set of workable, playable, PC-capable Exalts types for 3rd Edition, which are much harder to homebrew (I've tried!). 3rd Ed. Solars are awesome. So are Lunars. So are Dragon-Blooded. But my players want to be Sidereals and Alchemicals and Infernals and Abyssals. They come to the table with a hunger for themes that Solars can't provide. But Exalted can provide for that player's aesthetic! And the corebook is very explicit that these flavors of Exalted exist!

        Basically, I'm ready to buy a half-dozen more books when they come out. Or are kicked or whatever. Just not more antagonists. I'm drowning in them.
