        So I've joined my first ever Exalted game (for Dragon Blooded), and I made this artifact for a bard/archer type Wood Dragon Blooded. I wanted to make something interesting for my first artifact, but that means I have no idea how balanced it might be. So I'm posting it here for feedback!


        Thousand Plucked Strings ●●●●
        This artifact looks like the halfway point between a zither and a guitar, with a curved frame like that of a zither, but the lean handheld design of a guitar. The frame is a mixture of green and black jade carved into perfect miniature plates assembled in such a way to resemble vines emerging from darkness. The strings are made of gutstrings from a river dragon.
        Attunement: 5m; Type: Medium (+12 DMG, OVW 4)
        Accuracy: Close −1; Short +5; Medium +3; Long +1; Extreme −1
        Tags: Lethal, Archery (Long), Exceptional
        Hearthstone slot(s): 2
        Era: The Usurpation

        EVOCATIONS:
        The wielder of Thousand Plucked Strings gains an automatic success on all Performance rolls made using this artifact. In addition, she awakens Heart-Piercing Credenza at no cost as long as her Performance is at least 3.


        Heart-Piercing Cadenza
        Cost:         3m, Mins: Essence 1
        Type: Simple
        Keywords: None
        Duration: One Performance
        Prerequisites: None
        The original owner of Thousand Plucked Strings was an Exalted who grew up in a time of uneasy peace, constantly aware that war may break out any day. She was a gifted musician who loved what she did, but was constantly brought to scenes of conflict to fight or guard as part of her duty as a Dragon-Blooded. When she was able, she enjoyed playing music for anybody around her, be they nobles or commoners. Her songs reflected this, soothing the hearts of the battle-scarred and the innocent alike with the idea that one day the peace would last forever.

        The Exalt’s performance soothes the mind with thoughts of an idyllic life, calming the angry and relaxing the tense. Those who listen to the performance take a -1 penalty to Guile for the scene, representing the lowering of their guard. Listeners with a relevant Major or Defining Intimacy towards the idea of peace take a -2 and -4 penalty respectively instead.

        Rising Overture
        Cost:         2m, Mins: Essence 1
        Type: Supplemental
        Keywords: None
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisites: None

        Unfortunately for the Exalt, that day of eternal peace would not be in her lifetime. When the Usurpation began, she was enlisted and sent to guide a squad of troops near the front lines. As she fought, she grew accustomed to the strum of the bowstring rather than that of her instrument, but couldn’t predict the movements of men quite as easily as she could the beat of a new song. So she derived a new Charm from the foundation of Sky-Calming Strike, a “no frills” version that was cheaper to use repeatedly in battle at the cost of losing the air aspect that helped it fly further and straighter.

        By submerging oneself in the pace of battle, the Exalt can predict the movements of a single focused foe instantly without having to study them. Their range is extended one range band (up to medium) for the turn.

        The Oriflamme, My Conductor
        Cost:         6m, 1w, Mins: Essence 2
        Type: Supplemental
        Keywords: None
        Duration: One Scene
        Prerequisites: Heart-Piercing Cadenza, Rising Overture

        As the war raged on, the Exalt began to lose sight of the day that peace would return. Although she still sought to inspire others, she always found the times where she could do so lessening, while the times to fight and kill continued to increase. Feeling as if she would lose their ability to perform due to her hands growing more accustomed to pulling back a bowstring, she created a Charm that would enable her to continue playing even as she fought by using fingers of Essence. This Charm had an unexpected side-effect - through her subconscious, the music would change by each shot taken by the wielder, adapting itself to the battlefield to create a “perfectly fitting” song. Within earshot of her song, her allies began to find their bodies smoothly moving with the flow of the music to be where they’re needed most, while her enemies found any openings they might exploit pulling them out of tune, causing them to hesitate with each swing.

        Every turn, the Exalt gains one Tempo charge. Within medium range, they can expend a Tempo charge to pull an ally and/or enemy into their music’s beat. Such allies and enemies have +2 and -2 dice respectively to all attack rolls. Thousand Plucked Strings can hold up to ([# allies]+[# enemies])/2 charges total.

        The Corpses, My Audience
        Cost:         2m, 1 Tempo charge, Mins: Essence 2
        Type: Supplemental
        Keywords: None
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisites: The Oriflamme, My Conductor

        Although the Exalt could protect those near to her, defense would not be enough to win a war. As she fought on, she began to grow concerned that she wouldn’t have enough arrows to keep fighting. Even if she could grow new ones from anything that grow, the battlefields were poor places to find plants, and the time she would need to grow them would cost even more lives. So she studied and learned more about her anima, learning to weave her song into the center of it even as the rest flared around her.

        The Oriflamme, My Conductor must be active for this Evocation to be used. The Exalt may pay two motes and expend a Tempo charge to weave Essence and Anima into an arrow. At the glowing or burning level, this arrow deals an additional die of withering damage as the condensed anima breaks free. At bonfire level, the struck enemy is affected as if they’re within the Exalt’s anima flux.

        Inward-Facing Denouement
        Cost:         15m, 1w, 5+ Tempo charges, Mins: Essence 3, Archery 5
        Type: Simple
        Keywords: Decisive-Only
        Duration: Instant
        Prerequisites: The Corpses, My Audience
        Just when it seemed that at last they would win, the enemy general took to the field. A monster of a Solar, he ripped through her allies and friends without swaying to her beat in the slightest. Driven to fury by his actions, she turned her music inwardly to propel her movements beyond what should be possible. When her vision cleared of the red rage that had blinded her, the enemy general knelt in a circle made by the corpses of her slaughtered allies, pinned in place by a thousand arrows. Her arms, meanwhile, hung limply at her side, muscles torn and fingers flayed by the beyond supernatural speed they had moved at without protection. And as her allies ran forward to finish off the general, she could finally rest.

        When Inward-Facing Denouement activates, the Exalt expends all Tempo charges. For each Tempo charge consumed, they may make up to 2 ranged attacks against a single enemy. For the duration of the turn, The Corpses, My Audience is free to use. After the attack ends, take 5 un-soakable levels of damage and count the number of attacks made. If that number is greater than 15, this is lethal damage (unless this would kill the Exalt). Otherwise, it’s bashing damage. Thousand Plucking Strings may not be used again until this damage is fully healed.
        Special Activation Condition: Inward-Facing Denouement can be unlocked at no cost if its wielder loses four or more allies in a single fight to a single enemy.

                        Originally posted by Chakurano View Post

                        Ooh, it's a really good thing this topic came up, then!

                        Basically, the problem is that Sky-Calming Draw does not do the thing you would like to have, and it is actually a cornerstone of the game's balance that no effect does so.

                        Consider: if you have the ability to strike without giving your opponent the chance to retaliate, you have won the fight. Without the assistance of Charms, every character can move one range band per turn as a Reflexive action. Under a paradigm where aiming is not required, ranged attackers could kite indefinitely as long as they start the fight at medium or greater range and have somewhere to retreat back to - they launch an attack, reflexively move one range band back, their opponent moves towards them and can't do anything else, repeat. However, as aiming is incompatible with movement, and rushing is possible from a distance of short or lower, the aim requirement ensures that melee combatant can eventually catch up. (Or rather, that they get a fair chance to do so - with a rush pool of 4 against a disengage pool of 9, there is not much of a chance for the melee combatant to do much.)

                        If you are hell-bent on creating an effect that allows a character to shoot from medium+ range, you should balance the cost by comparing it to effects that allow a character to reflexively complete an aim action - ideally, you want the power to cost Willpower, but other, strict restrictions on its usability are also acceptable. (The Solar Charm Blood Without Balance is a fascinating example, allowing a character to reflexively launch an Archery attack without the aim requirement, but only when she becomes the beneficiary of a successful Distract gambit and enough initiative to act immediately.)

                        With all that said, there is actually a legitimate, non-gamebreaking reason for you to want to ditch the aim requirement! Aim actions cannot be placed into a flurry, and maintaining any of your Charms that have a duration of One performance require you to flurry performance as a miscellaneous action. So, a valid design space for this effect would be something that allows you to reflexively continue a performance while taking an Aim action. (This would not need to be priced as strictly as the ability to aim reflexively - in fact, I'd probably let you have it as a Permanent.)

Knowing his lover was a skilled swords man and more then a little proud Fate's Favor worried that forces outside of his or his lovers control would lead to his death in the coming battle. After all how many great plans fell to ruin because of bad luck? And how many people have been saved by a stroke of good fortune? Fate forged the pair of daiklaves to guard his love from the ravages of luck and to allow him to insure that good luck would not be the saving grace of his enemies.

                            Type: Light (+5 ACC, +10 DMG, +0 DEF, OVW 4)

                            Tags: Melee, Balanced, Lethal

                            Hearth Stone Sockets: 2

                            Era: Usurpation

                            Each is made of red Jade with a handle of ivory inlaid with star metal. Fuun is curved out to strike at those unlucky enough to have made an enemy of the Exalted wielding the pair while Ganbarou is curved in to catch weapons aimed at striking the exalt. They must be wielded as a pair for their powers to work. When wielded together each turn the wielder decided if he is using Fuun or Ganbarou to attack. In addition to granting the normal +2 on clash attacks for using paired weapons if the wielder attacks with Fuun the overwhelming value of the swords are increased by 1, if he attacks with Ganbarou the pair gain a defense rating of 1. Because of their ties to luck and the star metal in the handle Sidereal are resonate with this artifact.

                            Keywords: Withering Only, Dissonant

                            Resonate: The duration is extended to one scene. It ends if an attack is made with Fuun

                            Cost: 5m Mins: Essence 2
                            Type: Supplemental
                            Keywords: Dissonant
                            Duration: Instant
                            Prerequisites: Fortune Favors the Prepared

                                        Taehenshiken (Moonsilver Reaper Daiklave, Artifact ⦁ ⦁ ⦁ ⦁ )

                                        Taehenshiken was one of the stranger creations of the Twilight smith Fuzaki for his friend and circle-mate Desert Tornado. Shortly before the advent of the Usurpation, Tornado had become restless. He had become an unparalleled master of the sword but felt he was not yet as accomplished a warrior as he could be. He commissioned a special weapon from Fuzaki to assist him in learning all the other forms of combat that existed.

                                        A complex piece of magical engineering, Taehenshiken’s default form is that of a Reaper Daiklave, Desert Tornado’s favored weapon. The entire core of the daiklave is made of moonsilver. It has a white jade tsuba (hand guard) and an orichalcum kashira (hilt cap). No matter the form it takes, Taehenshiken always has a silver blade or haft, a golden end cap and a white crossguard. For the unique power of Taehenshiken is to take on the form of any weapon.

                                        After receiving the gift from his friend, Desert Tornado headed off to Chiaroscuro to begin training with a companion there. He was in the City of Glass when the Dragon Blooded struck. Taehenshiken was lost in the ruins where Tornado fell.

                                        Attunement: 5m
                                        Type (default): Medium (+3 ACC, +12 DMG, +1 DEF, OVR 5)
                                        Tags (default): Lethal, Melee, Balanced
                                        Hearthstone Slot(s): 1

                                        Evocations of Taehenshiken

                                        Ever Changing Death
                                        Cost: 5m; Mins: Essence 1
                                        Type: Reflexive
                                        Keywords: Resonant
                                        Duration: One Scene
                                        Prerequisites: None

                                        The essence of combat is being unpredictable to your opponent. Being able to subvert an enemy’s expectations at the appropriate time can give one all the edge they need to bring victory.

                                        With but a thought, the Exalt can shift the form of Taehenshiken into that of any Medium, non-Artifact melee weapon. This shift can be done reflexively once each round on the Exalt’s turn. The weapon still appears to be made of magical materials, but has the size and appearance of the selected weapon. It gains all Tags appropriate to the selected weapon.

                                        At the end of the scene in which this Evocation was used, or when the Exalt’s commitment to it is ended, Taehenshiken reverts to its default form as a Reaper Daiklave.
                                        Special Activation Rules: As long as the wielder is resonant with moonsilver, this Evocation awakens at no cost when she attunes Taehenshiken.

                                        Essence of Change
                                        Cost: – ; Mins: Essence 1
                                        Type: Permanent
                                        Keywords: –
                                        Duration: Permanent
                                        Prerequisites: Ever Changing Death

                                        As the Exalt’s connection with her weapon deepens, she can access additional forms. This Evocation upgrades Ever Changing Death to allow the Exalt to shape Taehenshiken into any non-Artifact melee weapon, of any size. When shifting to a Light or Heavy weapon, the stats of the weapon change appropriately.

                                        Swiftly Changing Demise
                                        Cost: 2m ; Mins: Essence 2
                                        Type: Reflexive
                                        Keywords: Resonant
                                        Duration: Instant
                                        Prerequisites: Ever Changing Death

                                        Being attuned to the flow of battle, the Exalt realizes when a shift is necessary. This Evocation allows the Exalt to shift Taehenshiken into the form of any melee weapon she has access to through Ever Changing Death at a moment’s notice.

                                        Resonant: Reduce the cost of this Evocation to 1 mote.

                                        Distant Death
                                        Cost: – ; Mins: Essence 2
                                        Type: Permanent
                                        Keywords: –
                                        Duration: Permanent
                                        Prerequisites: Ever Changing Death

                                        While Ever Changing Death is active, the Exalt may take a Simple action on her turn to shift Taehenshiken into any non-Artifact ranged weapon. This Simple action takes the place of the reflexive shift the Exalt could have performed based on that Evocation.

                                        The Exalt must still supply any ammunition necessary for the ranged weapon selected (arrows for bows/powerbows, firedust for firewands, etc). If the Exalt selects the form of a thrown ranged weapon, Taehenshiken returns to her after the attack (as if it were an Artifact thrown weapon).

                                        Mastery of Forms
                                        Cost: – (+1wp) ; Mins: Essence 3
                                        Type: Permanent
                                        Keywords: –
                                        Duration: Permanent
                                        Prerequisites: Ever Changing Death

                                        When activating Ever Changing Death, the Exalt may spend a Willpower to add the ability for that Evocation to allow her to shift Taehenshiken into any Artifact weapon of a size category or type that she can access.
