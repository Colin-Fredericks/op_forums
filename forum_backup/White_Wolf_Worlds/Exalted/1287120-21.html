                                                Originally posted by BrilliantRain View Post

                                                So, the Queen of Fangs would have, say, a one or two syllable word that basically translates to "She who is the Queen of Fangs" and might be represented in gameplay by people calling her the Queen of Fangs all the time? Or, if Queen of Fangs is too long, I suppose one could use an English word(s) with an appropriate meaning, such as Feared One or Dread or something.
                                                If I was in a position to do so, my inclination would be to decide on an appropriate language and try to find an approximation of how the term might translate into that, and try to come up with a word that sounds like a compound or evolved form of it.

                                                Something that might capture the feel of how the Roman name Caesar (a word of uncertain etymology even by the time of the most famous Gaius Julius) eventually evolves into the Russian title czar, modified by time and the language conventions of the people adopting it.

                                                In this conception, "Queen of Fangs" would still be the general term for referring to Raksi from a distance, invoking her reputation and what she's been known to do, and referencing her adopted moniker as it translates into their own languages, much as titles for different polities might get revised to be familiar to a given speaker. She gets called that rather than just something more prosaic like the queen of Mahalanka, both because that implies a legitimacy that most competing powers don't want to give to her, and from the general perspective that a Lunar who has actually managed to build up and maintain a sizable sovereign territory in open defiance of the Realm warrants something more distinct and fearsome. I'm imagining that in the minds of most speakers, a comprehensible approximation of Raksi's own fearsome moniker conveys the impression that she's openly displaying herself as an evil monster. Whereas the inhabitants of Mahalanka and its surroundings would refer to her by her formal title, and doing so if ever addressing her (with the possible exception of members of the Silver Pact who can get away with calling her shahan-ya) is a good idea.

                                                It's like how you don't style yourself as Dark Lord if you don't have a particular message to send.

                                                But that's just me. I'm sure that in most cases, the point that people under her authority use the title Queen of Fangs all the time would convey the necessary gravitas.
