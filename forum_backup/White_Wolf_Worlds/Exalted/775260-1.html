                        Originally posted by Zelbinnean View Post
                        Do you think the very meta Butterfly Eyes Charms are at all likely to come back? Like "your opponent is convinced you have leveraged a defense they cannot pierce, thus affecting their actions."
                        I think Exalted already modeled something like this far better with Feral Smile Tactic, and similar Solar/Abyssal Presence Charms.

                        Your opponent thinking you're terrifyingly invincible and dangerous (as per those intimidation Charms) isn't really that much different from your opponent thinking that attacking you will have no effect.

                        The Butterfly Eye Defense Charm ultimately just forced enemies to spend a Willpower to strike you, and that's not a bad effect. But one needs to recognize that such an effect is ultimately what the Charm does, and hammer the meta-gaming into mere flavor along the lines of "I'm super-scary as hell".


                        Alternatively, the Lunar needs to accept a real weakness in return for that fake strength. For example, the Lunar is working so hard to sell the idea that she cannot be harmed that she must use a Full Defense action (even though it may not seem that way to observers) and cannot attack that turn... perhaps she cannot even move, because needing to move would break the illusion that she has no need to evade or retreat.

                        This is similar to my idea of how a Lunar needs to accept real wound penalties in order to sell the idea that she's mortally wounded, even though she's perfectly healthy... or how the Lunar needs to find a way to hide her real wound penalties from her real injuries in order to sell the idea that she's perfectly health instead of really being mortally wounded.

                        Likewise, if the Lunar fakes the idea that her next attack is a Super Ultimate Death-Kill Murder Strike, she needs to accept some sort of vulnerability. Something that system transparency renders into a gamble for the defender -- if the defender calls the Lunar's bluff, she can hurt the Lunar really bad, but she has to accept some consequence in return.



                        If animal imagery is necessary, to me they represent something like...how the nonpoisonous viceroy butterfly looks like the poisonous monarch and therefore things avoid eating it too. Or how some snakes have similar, but not exactly the same, banding as the red-yellow-black coral snake, and have a survival advantage through that?
                        The Lunar Tell is a functional thing now, in the corebook. Functional enough that Lunars could have a disguise Charm as good as the Alchemical one. One that lets a Lunar mimic someone they haven't eaten.

                        Going by your inspiration, a Lunar who assumes the form of someone significantly stronger than himself/the opponent can appear to be as strong as the person he's imitating... so long as someone doesn't spot his Tell, just as someone who suddenly recognizes the difference between the coral snake and king snake knows that the faker isn't so scary.

                            Going back to the ideas of "turn my hunting trophies into gear" and "wearing gear made from an animal lets me treat it as a true form" and "there's a possibility that your spirit shape is merely the first animal form you get and the only one you get automatically, but it isn't mechanically superior to any other animal form, because then you're not pressured to choose a totem based on utility"...


                            I'd like a way to get my beast-weapon and beast-armor from my own Spirit Shape / Totem without having to hunt down and murder the closest mortal equivalent.

                            Not because I'm opposed to killing, but because "killing my kindred spirit" never sat right with me. I am The Wolf already, so why should I kill a wolf to become The Wolf?


                            It might be nice to instead have a Face Yourself ritual, where you fight your own Totem and symbolically overcome yourself, so that you can make your trophy-gear from yourself.

                            This might be related to the final two options I listed in my post on "familiar Charms". The possibility of splitting your human form or spirit shape off to act as a temporary familiar.


                            On a related note, I would also like the option of using a Lunar's Spirit Shape as a war form, rather than a hybrid shape. Like, whatever benefits a Lunar gets in war form aside from opposable thumbs and being able to stand and run like a human being, I'd like to get in a purely animal form.

                            To put it in classic Werewolf: The Apocalypse terms, choosing to use Hispo (near-wolf) rather than Crinos (man-wolf).

                                    Going back to crafting and lair Charms, I want Lunars to have the power to create stuff that inspires pants-wetting terror or tunnel-vision desire in anyone who beholds them.


                                    Gouge my claw marks into someone's door? They decide to leave their land.

                                    Carve an effigy to serve as a border-stone? No one passes through that way.

                                    Create a den? Only the bravest of warriors will dare to enter.

                                    Scar my emissary, or dress him in war paint or sacred priestly garb of my own design? No dude is man enough to fuck with him, at least or especially when he is on my business.

                                    Piss in the woods? The smell terrifies people into avoiding my territory.

                                    You don't want to piss in the woods? Please pay attention, because I first mentioned multiple options that don't involve urination. You could just as easily put up a very fancy Keep Out sign, or leave a sock on the door handle. The concept isn't about urination, but supplementing ANY action that marks an object to warn people that it belongs to you. Pissing has the advantage of being real easy, but as the urine is washed or evaporated away, the Charm's effect dies with it. That's why a Lunar looking for a more permanent border-marker will carve marks into large rocks or stone cliff faces.


                                    In reverse, I can bait traps with pretty objects people see and must have, cook meals that draw men from miles around as the smell carries on the wind, prepare lairs that seem welcoming and homey, or simply leave a careless trail of clothes leading to the bedroom which even the most principled Dragon-Blooded officer can't resist.

                                    Or for giggles, I make a golden ring that people murder each other to own.

                                                Attention-Demanding Presence (Appearance)
                                                Without making any overt action or sound at all, a Lunar shouts with her very being to announce her presence. All characters within a particular radius become aware of the Lunar's exact location, even if their usual senses cannot perceive her. All affected characters are compelled to relocate such that they are capable of watching or listening to her, and then to silently and peacefully pay attention to her until she begins to perform, speak or otherwise communicate. The effect cannot compel characters to place themselves in danger or any particularly awkward positions, and they decide how quickly they wish to relocate. The compulsion effect is broken if the Lunar herself moves with any urgency or initiates combat.

                                                Notes: Lunars walks into a noisy city square, pops this Charm, waits for an audience, then turns the stage over to her Solar partner with his Respect-Commanding Attitude. Or she just launches into her own performance and captivates their attention in some other way.


                                                Observed Prey Instinct (Perception)
                                                As long as this Charm remains active, the Lunar becomes instantly aware whenever anyone is focusing his attention upon her, though she does not automatically discern who he is, where he is, how he is observing her, or why. The Lunar does know exactly how many individual creatures are observing her at any given moment, and which of the creatures she can perceive are among those paying attention to her.

                                                Notes: Definite synergy with Attention-Demanding Presence; you make everyone in the area turn their eyes toward you, and then count up all the observers you can't detect. Of course, for a Lunar trying to fly under the radar, OPI can be used by itself. An upgrade would extend the effect to include non-character observers like cameras and other animate sensors she's triggering. A different upgrade would allow the Lunar the chance to discern where unnoticed observers are in relation to her, or from what vantage point someone is remotely watching her (whether by spell or video camera).

                                                    Hide of the Cunning Hunter
                                                    --- Perfect Symmetry
                                                    --- --- False Invulnerability
                                                    --- --- Fake Endurance
                                                    --- Shaping the Ideal Form / Changing Plumage Mastery
                                                    --- Masking the Brilliant Form
                                                    --- Shifting Penumbra Stance
                                                    --- One of the Herd
                                                    --- Subtle Chameleon Practice
                                                    --- --- Inexplicable Lunar Wonders
                                                    --- One of the Herd
                                                    --- --- Clover Can't Be Found
                                                    --- --- Forgetful Victim Prana


                                                    Hide of the Cunning Hunter (Manipulation)
                                                    Conceal distinguishing marks and cosmetic disfigurements.


                                                    Perfect Symmetry (Manipulation - follows Hide of the Cunning Hunter)
                                                    Conceal crippling disfigurements and obvious wounds. Observers get a chance to see through this disguise every time they witness you performing an action which suffers from a wound penalty or missing body part, but – as per the previewed Abyssal Charm, Incorruptible Beauty – the Exalt can suffer no Appearance penalties that are not the result of Charms or magic that directly affect her Appearance. This Charm must be invoked once per individual wound, with more grievous wounds incurring larger costs.


                                                    False Invulnerability (Manipulation - follows Perfect Symmetry)
                                                    Inflict your attacker (and possibly any witnesses) with horror if you immediately used Perfect Symmetry to make it seem as if the attack which just struck you inflicted no damage at all.


                                                    Fake Endurance (Manipulation - follows Perfect Symmetry)
                                                    You preternaturally conceal any signs of sleep deprivation, exhaustion, hunger, thirst, shortness of breath, or low mote pools.


                                                    Shaping the Ideal Form / Changing Plumage Mastery (Manipulation - follows Hide of the Cunning Hunter)
                                                    Alter cosmetic details, including gender, build, apparent age, voice, and scent.


                                                    Masking the Brilliant Form (Manipulation - follows Hide of the Cunning Hunter)
                                                    Disguise one's Castemark and anima banner to appear as a different Lunar Caste or a member of a completely different Exalted Host.


                                                    Shifting Penumbra Stance (Manipulation - follows Hide of the Cunning Hunter)
                                                    The Lunar ignites her anima to the bonfire level, warping her body and heroic spirit to remove all distinguishing traces. Insufficiently perceptive observers are completely incapable of determining the Lunar's nature or identity, for all that the Lunar cannot even attempt to hide her presence. Her voice and scent also become completely indistinct. However, this Charm does nothing to affect the memories of those who have already observed her features.

                                                    But just because people can't distinguish any of her features any more, that doesn't mean they can't recognize her from moment to moment. If she vanishes from sight only to reappear, witnesses will recognize that she's the same creature. Every time she talks, they know it's the same creature talking. Her scent also remains a valid way to track her, even if the hounds have no idea what they're hunting. Tracking her by her footprints is possible, but they first have to correctly guess that those prints belong to her when they cannot remember the shape of her feet or the weight of her body.


                                                    One of the Herd (Manipulation - follows Hide of the Cunning Hunter)
                                                    A more restrained trick than Shifting Penumbra Stance; so long as the Lunar's anima does not flare, anyone who observes her will be completely incapable of recalling any details about any voices, scents or appearances she displayed while under this Charm's effect. Even if she was the only lizard woman in a crowd of mortal men, witnesses will only remember her words and actions. The effects of this Charm come into play after the scene is over.

                                                    Any witnesses will recall any of the Lunar's previous appearances, voices or scents they observed if they encounter them again in any fashion; this includes even second-hand encounters, such as another character asking if they have met anyone meeting a matching description, or seeing a wanted poster.


                                                    Clover Can't be Found (Manipulation - follows One of the Herd)
                                                    Witnesses forget the Lunar's distinguishing features as soon as they take their eyes off of her. This doesn't work if they can listen to the Lunar's voice at the time. Characters capable of detecting the Lunar's scent also cannot be fooled unless the Lunar manages to banish her scent from the area.


                                                    Forgetful Victim Prana (Manipulation - follows One of the Herd)
                                                    The Lunar makes it more difficult for witnesses to be reminded of her features in any future encounters.


                                                    Subtle Chameleon Practice (Manipulation - follows Hide of the Cunning Hunter)
                                                    By paying a mote surcharge, the Lunar may treat any disguise Charm as though it had the Mute keyword.

                                                        Okay, Manipulation is deception, whether malevolent or benevolent or neither. And Charisma is sincerity.

                                                        I'm recalling what Holden said: If Solar Charms tend to express "I can", Lunar Charms tend to express "I am".

                                                        Thus, Lunar social influence Charms based on Charisma will be all about total honesty. The Lunar cannot use them to deceive, exaggerate, or otherwise convince anyone of anything that he doesn't believe.

                                                        So in return, he can use Charisma Charms to convince people he is definitely absolutely telling the utter truth.


                                                        BEAST-CALMING METHOD
                                                        As detailed in the original post. This Charm lives and dies based on the Lunar's sincerity.


                                                        LORE-SPEAKING METHOD
                                                        No matter how absurd the claim, no matter how rude the delivery, no matter how unsightly the Lunar's grooming or alien his form, the Lunar speaks the truth as best he understands it, and is believed. Yes, your father the king used to be a woman beggar on the street before I helped her. Yes, the girl down the street really is worth falling in love with. Yes, despite appearances, this sword really is worth that bargain. Yes, the task I am persuading you to pursue really isn't as dangerous as you've been led to believe. Yes, despite appearances, I really can and will rip your face off if you take one more step.

                                                        The force of the Lunar's earnestly declared truth is so powerful that those exposed to it more strongly resist social influences which attempt to deceive and confuse them. More than one poor village has been murdered in holocaust to destroy the heresy that the Silver Anathema are not demons.

                                                        The Lunar may use this Charm on himself to better help herself defend against liars who can make coal seem white, though it doesn't help for remembering starry-eyed trouble-makers after they've gone.


                                                        CHARISMATIC LUNAR TRICK
                                                        The stronger the Lunar believes, the stronger his arguments. The Lunar adds a number of dice to his social influence roll, equal to the intensity of his most relevant Intimacy.


                                                        BROTHERHOOD OF LAKE AND RIVER
                                                        As an upgrade to Charismatic Lunar Trick, if the Lunar is reaching the target through an Intimacy of hers which matches his own, or if she is currently in the grips of the same strong mood that holds the Lunar in its sway, she increases any penalty derived from that Intimacy or mood for the purposes of refusing his influence. If the target resists the Lunar's social influence using an Intimacy opposed to the Lunar's own, reduce the bonus that the target would normally gain.


                                                        IT'S A PROMISE
                                                        Whether swearing salvation or revenge, the strength of the Lunar's honesty binds himself to do as he says. He creates in himself a Principle for that task, with an intensity equal to the challenge, and he cannot voluntarily rid himself of that Principle until his work is completed. In the meantime, his beneficiaries derive hope and his opponents derive fear from the sure knowledge that the Lunar will not stop until he has fulfilled his word.


                                                        Without bringing back the ability for Lunars to learn Fair Folk Charms, I still want Lunar Exalted to be able to swear to magically binding oaths like the one above, with various different effects. Blood oaths, if you will, since blood is an important currency for Lunars.

                                                        A blood oath where you swear to protect and provide for someone as a subordinate, while they swear to obey and serve you in return. Just as with the similar bond between you and familiar, this allows your new follower to gift you with their Heart's Blood without you needing to hunt them. They have accepted you as alpha, what more dominance could you possibly hold over them?

                                                        In reverse, an oath sworn between equals could grant you the other party's Heart's Blood if they break the oath before you do, but because you swore the oath sincerely through a Charisma Charm, you are also bound to uphold it and will suffer consequences if you break it first instead.

                                                        With other Charms (Insidious Lunar Transformation), you could not only gain the target's Heart's Blood, but curse them to assume an animal form as punishment for their oathbreaking.
