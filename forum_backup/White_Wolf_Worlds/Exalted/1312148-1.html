        Here's an idea - let's try fusing dots and exp into a single system. If you crunch exp so that each exp is worth about 3 exp, you can have a system that uses exp at chargen without overwhelming players.

        Here's what that looks like for Solars :

        1) First we adjust the trait costs for the system :
        |
        Trait Exp Cost
        Primary or Secondary Attribute new rating
        New Ability 1
        Caste/Favored Ability new rating / 2, round down
        Other Ability new rating / 2, round up
        Specialty 1
        Merits new rating
        Caste/Favored Charm 3
        Other Charm 4
        Spell (Occult is Caste or Favored) 3
        Spell (Occult is not Caste or Favored) 4
        Evocation 4
        Willpower 3
        |
        The result is fairly simple to understand, with no more multiplications and minus one's to juggle with.

        2) With smaller numbers for exp costs, we can now use exp at Chargen while keeping things simple :
        |
        Chargen Available Exp
        Spend in Primary Attributes 28
        Spend in Secondary Attributes 19
        Spend in Tertiary Attributes 14
        Spend in Abilities <= 3 28
        Spend in merits 10
        Bonus exp to spend anywhere 30
        |
        Interestingly Ability allocation remains identical to the default system if you put everything in Caste/Favored.

        3) You can use this system just during chargen and then switch to regular exp for the actual game.
        However If you want to run a whole game with it you just need to adjust Character Advancement accordingly :
        |
        Character Advancement Exp
        Exp awarded per game 2
        Solar Exp awarded per game 2 (1 per bonus)
        Essence 2 20
        Essence 3 50
        Essence 4 80
        Essence 5 120
        Any Exp cost during game
        not covered in Trait costs        		 cost / 3
        round up if >= 0.5,
        round down if < 0.5,
        minimum 1
        |
        Here Players get slightly more buying power per game overall because Charms and many non-Charm upgrades have increased in cost slightly.
        However it still takes the same number of games as before to raise Essence by 1.

                    Originally posted by HighPriest View Post
                    A lot of people are mentioning flat XP. What our group used, back in 2.5 (never got around to house-ruling 3rd much) was very similar in concept - flat BP advancement. It worked well, just giving out BPs (sometimes fractional) instead of XP, and needed less explanation and "mathing" of how each trait would need to cost to work properly since BPs are already a flat "advancement" system with a convenient chart.
                    It's basically the same thing with different starting points.

                    The issues I have with basing things on BP:

                    1) The valuing of traits with BP is significantly different from XP, and I like the general valuing by XP better. For example using BP as your base makes Willpower significantly cheaper.

                    2) You still need to do the "explanation and mathing," stuff. You just need to do it to figure out how much BP to give out instead of how much XP to charge. Esp. since BP are worth more than XP, you also have to figure out an exchange rate for any Charm that costs XP to use.

                    Basically, creating a flat advancement system around BP is simpler upfront, but has more work to do to integrate into the rest of the system. Creating one around XP is more complicated upfront, but works with the rest of the existing systems without change.
