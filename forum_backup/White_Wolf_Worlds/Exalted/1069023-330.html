        Originally posted by glamourweaver View Post
        Does the viability of same-sex marriage recontextualize their marriage at all?
        Not really. Same-sex marriage is recognized, but the Scarlet Dynasty's emphasis on controlling the reproductive autonomy of its Dynasts means that actually getting to marry a same-sex partner you love is no easier than any other love match, and that same-sex couples are stigmatized until they produce heirs via adoption, surrogacy, or sorcery. Kes and Szaya's arrangement is still one that makes sense in setting.

                                Originally posted by WalkingEye View Post
                                Does a warstrider increase the users physical attributes while using it in some way? If so is this increase treated as bonus dice(charm or noncharm?) or a base stat increase?
                                Warstriders have their own Strength rating, which the pilot uses in place of their own.

                                How much is the average health pool of a warstrider and do they interact with the temp health from temp health or soak giving/increasing charms?
                                The typical warstrider health track is -0x5/-1x5/-2x10/-4x10/Incap. Things that give the pilot temporary health levels or soak won't add to the warstrider's health levels or soak.

                                What is the dot rating of the lowest level warstrider?
                                Five.

                                Do any warstriders have evocations that improve the power of the Legendary Size Merit (Like making you need more than 10 dice over soak to deal last bit of imitative)
                                They can.

                                Do warstriders have their own mote reserve?
                                No.

                                Also I know you have mentioned before that gunshoza are not in arms but can we get any basic idea how non exalted use them?
                                Mortals can attune them and awaken their Evocations, but at great cost to their lifespan.

                                            Originally posted by WalkingEye View Post
                                            Since they have their own str track what is the low end of their base str?
                                            Their Strength scales with the physical strength and training of their pilot. Low end is 5.

                                            how many talents of magical materials should it take to make one?
                                            The only place that ever really talks about artifact construction in terms of talents is one line in Wyld-Shaping Technique, which says that a grand daiklave takes about five talents. That's, uh, obviously not terribly helpful. I'd guess it's somewhere on the order of dozens of talents to make an average-size warstrider, maybe as many as a hundred.

                                            Are they first age artifice as mentioned in the core book.
                                            Yes.

                                            Are you looking through a slot in the armor or are there base magic optics. Also Do I have to yell from inside to be hear or is their some sort of sound system in them so I can intimidate my enemies while in the armor?
                                            Baseline is that you're just looking out a slot in the helmet, but some warstriders might have more sophisticated sensory or audio systems.

                                            Since Gunzosha armors use a mortals lifespan to equip and don them. Are they treated as exalted for the base purpose of avoiding some charm effects that just take out normal mortals? Also will non exalted be able to use their evocations by sacrificing months/years of their lives?
                                            I haven't drilled down into the specifics of what their Evocations and costs will look like yet.
