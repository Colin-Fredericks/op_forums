    Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz View Post
    As a Thrown weapon, I'd guess not.

    Does the strength increase from Increasing Strength Exercise improve your strength for charms that give you bonuses based on your Strength? For example, can a brawl-and-athletics essence 5, strength 5 Solar make 11 attacks with Hammer on Iron?
    And does it count as dice from charms, if, for example, you make a strength+athletics roll?

    I assume not, since Power-Suffusing Form (which gives strength extra non-charm dice) says &quot;This Charm supplements a feat of strength, adding (Strength) non-Charm bonus dice to the attempt. This is explicitly compatible with the bonus granted by Increasing Strength Exercise.&quot; Implying that generally it isn't, and a charm that gives (Strength) extra dice wouldn't count the bonus from Increasing Strength Exercise.
    Increasing Strength Exercise does increase your strength for things that check your strength stat.

    The extra dice given by Strength Increasing Exercise count as charm dice, so do not stack with things like the Excellency. It does stack with things that give non-charm dice because they are non-charm dice.
