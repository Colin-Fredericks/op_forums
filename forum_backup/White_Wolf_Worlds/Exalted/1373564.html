    Tiger (Style) Warrior Training Technique

    03-08-2020, 01:02 PM
    So I hope this isn't too self-indulgent, but I thought this would merit its own thread. Maybe I'm wrong & it'll fade into obscurity. It's a tangent from this thread.

    Originally posted by Katamari_Naneke View Post
    Speaking of which, I'm thinking of putting together a custom charm enhancing Tiger Warrior Training Technique to help him do that. Something that allows elite martial artists to be trained, and probably offers some training in complementary abilities. It might be cool to come up with an extra add-on for each style (comparable to Demon-Fighting Principle, Giant-Slaying Tactics etc) that gives some unique benefit, but there are so many styles. Still, maybe I can just come up with ideas that that character might use and then crowdsource the rest.
    Here's my first draft: Efficacious Solar Sifu:
    Cost:     - Mins: War 5, Essence 2
    Type: Permanent
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Permanent
    Prerequisite Charms: Tiger Warrior Training Technique, one Martial Arts form charm

    This charm enhances the Solar's Tiger Warrior Training Technique. It confers the Martial Artist Merit on the Tiger Warriors being trained and allows them to be trained in a Martial Art known by the instructing Solar instead of another combat ability. Tiger Warriors trained to a competent level with this charm can use a dice pool of 5 dice for pools based on their style's complementary abilities. If trained to an elite level, they can use a pool of 9 dice instead.

    Further enhancements become available at Essence 3+ depending on the style being taught:
    False Friend Inception (Ebon Shadow Style) (2xp): Generals commanding units of Ebon Shadow Stylists gain access to an enhanced Ambush stratagem in the Strategic Maneuver roll-off. In addition to the usual effects, a significant number of enemy troops turn out to be Ebon Shadow infiltrators who open the battle by stabbing their “comrades” in the back, provoking an immediate rout check. If this rout check is passed, the enemy commander suffers a +2 difficulty penalty to command actions for the remainder of the battle owing to the resulting confusion and disarray.
    Poisonous Courtier Ideology (Dreaming Pearl Courtesan Style) (2xp): An army of Dreaming Pearl Courtesans is as useful in politics as in war. They can be deployed to embed themselves among high society, courtesans, street performers etc. where they can gather information or simply wait for the right moment to strike. The Courtesan army may even be smuggled in numbers into environments where weapons must ordinarily be left behind or peace-bound.

    ===

    It's late and I don't have the best head for mechanics, so maybe there are glaring balance oversights in this. If people have refinements I'm open to them. But I'm really interested in people's ideas for enhancements that Tiger Warriors might get through other martial arts styles. I didn't really try to develop strictly combat-focused ones like you might get from Tiger Style or Snake Style. How would you even differentiate between a Golden Janissary one and the canon Demon-Fighting Principle? These are questions I'd love to hear other people's ideas about.

    The 5/9 complementary ability pool is based on other pools that regular troops/elite troops get on page 497. Regular troops get 4 dice for Senses and Threaten, but that felt too low to me.

    I think the Ebon Shadow Style one could be really fun in the mirror match, where the generals vie to see whose plants will be in position to strike.

          Crippling Serpent Fangs (Snake Style) (2xp): When a unit of Snake Stylists, following a command action by their sifu-general, attacks and successfully deals damage, any victims damages suffer a -2 penalty on all actions on their next turn. Individual fighters who took only withering damage from the attack may resist this penalty by rolling (Stamina+Resistance) against a difficulty of (unit's Size+sifu-general's Essence).

          Not sure how to balance this one but it's meant to represent the Snake dudes all throwing whatever variant of Crippling Pressure-Point Strike they can as they move in.

          Whirling Reaper Squadron (White Reaper Style) (2xp): A unit of White Reaper Stylists always damages enemy battle groups with double 10s and enjoys +1 Defense against them.

          This...isn't really fair at all, but White Reaper is meant to be comically unfair againstbattle groups, so if you send your army against an army of them you might just deserve this. I dunno, too much?
