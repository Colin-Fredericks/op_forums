                                                            Hmm, the Wanasaan put me in mind of a point about why it might be difficult for many outcaste groups to grow very large; one bad internal dispute (or getting into a nasty fight with an external power), and they'll be driven back to square one.

                                                            ​Now I'm suddenly thinking about certain real life religious organisations that have had very strong ideas about growth through adoption or conversion, some to the point where they actually regard procreation as taboo, and I've got the image of a group of outcastes that is very specific about only growing or replacing members through recruiting other outcastes and actually want their bloodlines to end with them, on the logic that sending the Dragon's blood out into the world where it can randomly and unpredictably create Exalted who'll do who knows what with that power is supremely irresponsible. They hold this view even with regards to their own members husbanding their (still possibly poor) progenitive Essence and matching with one another, on a kind of inversion of the logic in the Realm; to them, it's about planning for the event that their hypothetical children won't Exalt, and will carry a strand of the blood into the mortal population.

                                                            I'm seeing something like a cult that kind of splintered away from the Immaculate Order, which looks upon societies like the Realm and even Lookshy favourably because they actually have the resources to sustain large numbers of dedicated Exalted and marshal any outcastes that fall within their spheres, in contrast to the relative disorder of the Threshold and beyond.

                                                            ​Obviously it's not the kind of thing that could be the central driving conceit of a group of outcastes, and it wouldn't be my first choice for them to be on a kind of crusade against other outcaste societies. Just a detail of their beliefs and practices that would distinguish them from others and has a certain resonance.

                                                            ​Perhaps a kind of itinerant group that goes around charitably performing services as needed and as fits the skills of their members, guided by Immaculate ethics (whether it be fighting off Fair Folk or rogue gods, providing medical services or legal advice, performing or helping out towns and villages with some useful construction like a well or a simple defensive wall), with an eye to recruiting any outcastes that they encounter (or at least convincing them of the merits of their position).

                                                            ​I'm seeing something like them being founded by a Dynast who, after meeting an outcaste in the Blessed Isle and hearing stories about life in the Threshold, decided to go off adventuring, and after a century or two of that developed a very dim view of what Exalted outside of the Realm were like, returned home to train as an Immaculate monk, and then went back out to perform humble good works and preach his beliefs.
