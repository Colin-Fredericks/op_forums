                            Athletics

                            Base excellency adds successes to the roll. Odd.

                            Is the storyteller’s guide going to have an athletics system in it or are we just assuming that “rolling to jump” is a thing?

                            I’m a little confused why Perfect Climbing Attitude is back here. Is it supposed to be Essence 2?

                            I’ve noted that the Signature charms of elements that are not common in that ability tend to lack the Aura keyword. Which means they can be comboed freely, as long as you don’t mind getting kicked out of Aura.

                            Does the swimming booster charm let you breathe underwater, if you don’t already?

                            The Fire aspect runs you down. They are inescapable and trying to flee will likely end with you on fire.

                            The Earth aspect is stronk like bull!

                            The other elements are pretty much just there to help you out in whatever environment is most appropriate to them.


                            Awareness

                            If my character is blind, can I use Feeling the Dragon’s Bones to negate most combat penalties for fighting while blind? Would you consider it reasonable for my character to be able to just have it going indefinitely, provided they never used non-Earth charms? Or would that need an upgrade charm?

                            Would an upgrade to All-Encompassing Earth Sense that let you keep the Air or Water versions up while in the appropriate Aura work? Maybe let the Air one work in non-enclosed spaces and the Water one work in mist/fog/gentle rain too?

                            What effect does Dragon’s Crushing Gaze actually have? Say there’s a stealthy person spying on me, I use this charm, I win the roll and now they lose one success. But...that doesn’t help me, because I won the roll already. Do other people get to reroll? Or are we supposed to remember the rolls everyone else got and they retroactively win if they only missed by one? How does it work?

                            I think Fire’s thing is exploding 10s. Wood’s thing looks like it might be removing penalties. Earth’s thing seems like it’s adding successes. Not sure about air or water yet.

                            Eye of Blazing Truth. Maybe you should skip hiding in cardboard boxes when DBs are looking for you.

                            Ok, so I get that these charms are useful, appropriate, and thematic, but the first three Signature Water charms are only useful if you’re in the water. I feel like Water Aspects are being pigeonholed as Aquaman.

                            Maybe it’ll be better once I get into brawl.

                            That said, Serpent-of-the-Depths Discernment does seem like it’d be fairly handy in any garden with an unexpectedly deep ornamental koi pond. At least until the other DBs twig to that trick anyway.

                            Ok, Sense the Hidden Ember is cool.

                            The earth is watching.
