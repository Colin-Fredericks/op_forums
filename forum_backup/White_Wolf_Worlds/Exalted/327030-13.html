    To chime in on the Anima Flux argument.

    The Anima is not a willful exertion. It's bleed off of essence from a human stuffed full of essence and who was never intended to manipulate essence. The essence that bleeds off is the kind that is infused into the human, this essence which brands their power and what Exalt they are. Solars have Solar essence from the UCS, Lunars have Lunar essence from Luna, the Dragonblooded have the essence of the Elemental Dragons in them. They are not Elemental in nature, they are Dragonblooded. The Elemental Dragons aren't elementals, they're the Elemental Dragons, children of Gaia.

    What does this mean? It means controlling the elements should not be natural to them. Anima is a side effect of the power than infuses them. Should they be able to learn how to control their Anima? Yes, through a martial art as charms are not technically learned, they are already traits you as a character pick for your character. There should never be a manual of charms that one can read in world.

    However there is a place I see for elemental effects and charms. Solars have charms like that of manifesting a sword or armor from their anima. This is not learned, this is a natural expression of their skill. I can see a similar space for elemental effects. Your passion informing your attack so much your drive is inflamed. Your resolve so strong in the face of danger the earth supports your mind and heart, your skills of stealth and subterfuge so great that you are like the wind. These same charms could have far more overt effects when your Anima is in flux. Your attacks actually burst into flames, your mind and heart is actually hardened to stone, your movements and form is actually of wind. This I think is an interesting mechanic that could be implemented.

                                                    Something else that occurred to me about DB vs. Solar approaches to abilities, which I thought I'd dredge this thread up to mention:

                                                    Solars tend to be a bit unique on account of being both fewer in number and lacking a unified philosophy, so it's hard to make an all-encompassing statement about them, but in general, Solar Melee is just about being really good at melee. I mean, yeah, so good at Melee you can do some insane things like forge your soul into a sword, but the unifying statement of it is "really good at Melee."

                                                    The way I see it, DB Melee (and other DB abilities, but I'm using Melee as an example) isn't quite like that. DB Melee is more like a Bushido thing -- it's a sort of elemental-themed enlightenment honed and expressed through your skill with a sword. This is uniquely DB in part because unlike Celestials, there are enough DBs out there for the philosophy behind their charms to become this cultural thing; DB elements have this entire personality and way of life associated with them, which most DBs have drilled into them from birth. Through this, DB mastery of Melee becomes a way for them to hone their spiritual connection with their element.

                                                    I think the Immaculate Faith partially exploits these connections and is partially built on them; I don't think the Immaculate Dragons are (or ever were) real, but they do reflect an actual understanding of the DB Exaltation -- the Sidereals probably started with a decent understanding of it, and with existing concepts to build with (and Sidereals are probably good at the whole look-deep-within-people-and-pull-out-their-hidden-potential thing), and since then the DBs have been refining and honing it based on what works for their spiritual advancement. I don't think the Immaculate Faith is 'real' or that it's uniquely intrinsic to the DB Exaltation or anything; but I think the ability to refine your essence by meditating on a spiritual forebear or mythical figure who embodies your element -- especially through things like martial training or other forms of personal mastery -- might be specifically DB, with the Immaculate Faith being one very successful expression of this (obviously one that has also been co-opted to unrelated political ends.)

                                                    Anyway, that might seem a bit off-topic, but I think it's important to view the DBs as a whole -- this idea of a sort of (exaggerated, fictionalized) "Bushido code" sort of thing where you live your entire way of life according to an ideal and express it through your skills is, I think, very important to the way they understand, improve, and apply their mastery of the elements.
