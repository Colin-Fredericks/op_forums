                    There's also the Amnesia flaw that has 2 levels: forget everything before a significant event, or forget everything every time you go to sleep.

                    Spells or Artifacts could conceivably impose this flaw on someone.

                    Alternatively, I had a Night Caste once that wielded a hatchet that cleaved away memories, which we modeled as a physical attack that goes against MDV and inflicts sorcerously-charged intimacies of something like "[memory] never happened" or "[name] doesn't exist" (keywords: Illusion or Shaping, Crippling), where damage successes instead determine the "potency" of the memory loss (so instead of needing [Conviction] social attacks to erode it, you needed [Damage Successes]).

                    Very useful for when you're accidentally noticed. Throw it and cleave away the guard's memory of having seen you.
