                                    I don't see a general homebrew thread, so I thought I may as well ask for feedback here.

                                    Guarding Star Tactics
                                    Cost __(+4m); Mins: Melee 5, Essence 4
                                    Type: Permanent
                                    Keywords: None
                                    Duration: Permanent
                                    Prerequisite Charms: Heavenly Guardian Defense, Unassailable Guardian Posture

                                    Standing between Creation and the dread forces that wish it undone, the Lawgiver will not be satisfied with merely defending her own life and will save those who follow her from disaster. This charm permanently upgrades Heavenly Guardian Defense; when the Solar uses it's simple version to parry away a source uncountable damage she may pay an additional four motes to completely negate the source. A cloud of raining acid is swept out of the air with a swing of her blade and a mighty torrent of light, the rolling lakes of magma from a volcanic eruption are split in half and soothed, while the billowing clouds of ash are harmlessly dispersed. Any mortals or similar creatures with no way of defending themselves are left unharmed.
                                    An essence 5+ repurchase of this charm allows the solar to negate constant sources of harm, such as the burning flames of the Pole of Fire, or the deathly curse of a Balors Gaze, however in this case the cost of the charm is committed for so long as the Lawgiver wishes to negate the affect, rather than spent.

                                    To expand just a little bit, protecting more people than just himself from a large scale deadly attack was one of the few things that existed in 2e that I felt 'fit' in a way, and could maybe fit into 3e, so I decided to give it a try. It still seems a bit weird to me, in a way kind of to easy to just bat away big calamities but also the sort of thing an ancient powerful hero could do. I'm thinking of either upping the cost to at least a total of 10m 1wp or doing something like making it have some permanent cost so it isn't a thing that can just be done whenever.

                                                        Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post

                                                        As long as you haven't mistreated him, why should he care?
                                                        Apparently it's been a trait of Jacint's since first edition that he can't be in Creation and Malfeas simultaneously. It was originally written in Games of Divinity, and carried over into Roll of Glorious Divinity 2, that keeping Jacint bound in Creation for more than a few days earns you not just his enmity, but his part of the soul heirarchy despising the person as well. So Jacint pretty much views keeping him out of Malfeas for a year and a day as a serious mistreatment.
