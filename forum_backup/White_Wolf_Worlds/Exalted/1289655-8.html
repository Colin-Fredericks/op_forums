                    Ok, I've come up with a possible compromise:

                    While the solar bond shines like the noonday sun for the lunar, it's far more subtle on the solar's end. Rather than immediate recognition, the tie manifests at the end of a scene in which the lunar's nature was revealed to her. This might be by the lunar confronting her directly, or by his bearing or actions sparking dormant past-life memories. Once established it can be eroded away, but will re-establish itself in much the same manner as the lunar's tie does at the end of any scene in which the two interact.

                            *shrug*

                            Guess it's time to retract my Lunars pledge and refuse further pledge contributions on Exalted.

                            This is utterly ridiculous and the fact that the devs seem to be caving to it means that certain people, with very narrow views and worse arguments, are creating changes in the game.

                            The Lunar bond is optional for Lunars. If you don't like the aspects of the Bond, don't take it. I know that I would be furious if I was playing a Solar and a Lunar player decided that my Solar was their object of the bond and the ST told me I now have an Intimacy to this Lunar I can never get rid of.

                            Everyone here is focusing on the Lunar half of the bond. You know, the side with the agency. Has anyone noticed that the bond, from the Solar perspective, is not positive in the least? The Lunar bond allows the Lunar to do a lot of things that the Solar might not want them to do? And what do they have to fight them with? Nothing other than the base rules. The Lunar OTOH gets a LOT of tools that are improved or just do things to a Solar.

                            The reason that the Lunar gets the Intimacy and the Solar doesn't is that the Lunar gets benefits and the Solar doesn't. The Solars cannot 'do' anything with the Bond. This is the clearest representation:

                            Lunar = subject of the bond. They have the agency. They get the improved powers.
                            Solars = object of the bond. They have no agency. They get nothing from the bond that the Lunar does not want to give them.

                            Why are you trying to penalize the Solar even further?

                                                            Ah. I had not actually considered the question of how a Solar might retain an Intimacy.

                                                            Something about it having the same capacity to reassert itself feels mildly off to me, but I can't really form any kind of argument against it.

                                                            By which I mean, I don't think there's any kind of valid argument against it.

                                                            That being said, the point about it coming up when a Lunar's true nature is revealed to the Lunar puts me in mind of a different angle.

                                                            Up until now, I've been thinking of those flashes of past life memory primarily in terms of the first meeting with the Lunar. I've not considered the possibility of them happening later, and with increasing likelihood depending on length of exposure.

                                                            So say... I like the idea that for Lunars, the recognition and connection is immediate and mystical, for Solars it can depend on experience of past life memories, ranging from something persistent and compelling before the Lunar is even met, to something that triggers with a strong wash of emotion when the Lunar is first recognised, down into something that is triggered upon witnessing the Lunar doing something that is evocative of the relationship in a past life, that triggers the associated memory.

                                                            I'm inclined to the idea that most Solars would experience the matter at the latter end of the spectrum.
