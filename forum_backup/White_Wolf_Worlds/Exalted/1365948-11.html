                            In my last Solar game (Modern) the last boss was essentially a superpowered Warstrider that was also infused with the power of the Neverborn then hijacked by the Neverborn. My PC's were not minmaxed but they were still no slouches to combat. It very easily engaged with all of them, and in the end they decided to banish the abomination back to the pit of the Labyrinth than fight it. Mainly because (1) They did not expect the BBEG would be stupid enough to do something to give the Neverborn control of an N/A Warstrider (he did) (2) The PC's were in no way equipped to deal with the literal manifestation of entropy and despair. One PC had to sacrifice themselves (along with a near-game long NPC) to seal the bastard.

                            If it was an Exalt however, I'd make a boss group. The only way to make an Exalt into "Boss mode" is to either (a) give them plot specific and broken powers or increase their soak to disgusting heights. The latter makes the game a slog and makes me wonder if god abandoned me long ago.

                                        I just realized that I think a part of the problem is Soak is a one size fit all solution (is that the correct expression ? I'm not sure). For a simple comparison, take DD : You usually will have some good defenses (for example AC and Fortitude if you are a tank), and you also will have some weaknesses. Considering it's not that hard to also raise your other defenses (for example I've almost never seen any character with less than 4 resolve), and that there are (I think I'm becoming senile because I've written this phrase so many times on this forum) not enough tools at the hands of the ST to suggest using special options (how good are undodgeable and unblockable attacks ? Should I give them often to enemies ? I've also tried to make enemies attack more targetted on abilities like Athletics or Resistance in the form of special attacks from the enemies), I think it makes Soak a too centralized option, where almost all ennemies will attack Soak, because it asks non trivial efforts from the ST to prepare something else, and so Soak becomes too polarized.
