            I would like to point out that the UCS has not always resembled a human, but previously was an orichalcum-scaled Dragon King-like being. His true form on the other hand is a roiling mass of dozens of arms wearing molten orichalcum and obsidian blazing with galaxies with supernatural eyes before being forming. As a compromise between the competing views of "The UCS is always bigger/perfect" and "The UCS has a modestly greco-roman size" I propose that over time the UCS has redefined his conception of the perfect penis in accordance with the ideals of the society that had his favor. Given the existence of Ipithymia in Malfeas, the descriptions of Primordial genitalia in Scroll of Swallowed Darkness and what we know of Theion's attitude from Gunstar Autocthonia I think it's not out of the question that at least one of his Primordial makers sated his lusts on the Sun, and would have expected him to not disappoint.

            In other words, the UCS used to have a truly massive penis. It was a nothing short of a cosmic phenomena, likely akin to a stack of galaxies rotating at high speed like the representation of Gilgamesh's Ea in Fate/Extra CCC, which incidentally has a final boss (Kiara Sessyoin) who shows the kind of genitalia the Sun would have likely been expected to satisfy. Later in the Time of Glory it was likely the bifurcated lizard's penis or an alligator's constantly erect one given his draconic form, although more information is needed on the Dragon Kings' genitalia here. Finally during the time of the Solar Deliberative we can conjecture the Sun reduced himself to a more modest size both to avoid intimidating human partners* familiar with his girth, and frankly because at that point he would have had nothing to prove.

            *Well, except Merela evidently
