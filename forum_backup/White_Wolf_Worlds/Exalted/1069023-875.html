        Originally posted by Sunder the Gold View Post

        What do you mean when you ask Robert about “alive”?

        Robert, Lunars would make their own memories of the stolen memories they experience, right? Like how people normally make memories of their own memories, which is one of the ways that memories change from one’s own retelling.

        So, if you drop the commitment, you lose access to memories you haven’t yet gleaned, but you don’t suddenly forget everything you already experienced.
        Think of it like reading a book. While you've got the book in your hands and are looking through it (have the Charm active), it's really easy to go through and read stuff. Once you put the book away (deactivate the Charm), you might still be able to remember some of what you read (memories you actively accessed on-screen), although your recall isn't going to be as crisp and clear as it would be compared to actually reading the words right off the page, and there may be pages you didn't get to (memories you never actively accessed on-screen) that you can't remember at all.

                                    Originally posted by Robert Vance View Post
                                    I feel like &quot;hit someone really hard so that they go flying back&quot; is a concept that can be implemented in a great variety of ways. The sheer killing power of Heaven Thunder Hammer isn't something I'd make available to others lightly, but there's all sorts of ways you could buff or build off of smashing attacks in a style without running into problems.

                                    That is some ridiculous competition for even the Mastery level of a Martial Arts super-smashing attack Charm.

                                    It’s accessible, stupidly cheap, always available (disarming can’t stop it), and horrendously powerful. As a decisive attack, there’s absolutely no accuracy or raw damage difference between a punch and a hammer blow.
