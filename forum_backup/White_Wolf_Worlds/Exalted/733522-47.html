            Terrestrial 1 or 2

            Blessed self moving tools.

            Tools made from the best mundane materials and craftsmanship, further enhanced by a working that gives the life, movement, and motive power on their own. Examples include axes that cut down trees and stumps with greater strength than any mortal, lathes and grinders far more precise and fast than any mortal powered version. Self stirring cauldrons, spades, and other things serve to increase the rate of work of anybody using such tools, as long as the tools are appropriate.

                The Sighing Maiden (Celestial 3)

                The Sighing Maiden was created during the Shogunate to serve as the companion of the daughter of the Daimyo of Fallen Leaves. She was modeled after the 2nd Circle Demon Mara, meaning that she is a Celestial-level practitioner of supernatural martial arts and a Celestial-level sorceress (she also possesses a number of spiritual and elemental charms). While the Daimyo of Fallen Leaves died over 1,000 years ago, the Sighing Maiden still watches over her descendants, and will act as a mentor for any of her Dragon-Blooded descendants.

                In order to facilitate the protection of the descendants of the Daimyo of Fallen Leaves, the Sighing Maiden has created nine sisters of equal power to her, around one century, and each of them have created their own sisters. In total, fifty-five Sighing Maidens now exist, each with a different human appearance that allows them to blend in with human populations, though they are all based off the original model. Fifty of them mentor and protect the 500 surviving descendants of the Daimyo of Fallen Leaves while five of them act as messengers between the communities protected by the fifty other sisters.

                        Celestial 2

                        Great Stone pillars placed beside the main gate. All who wish to enter the city must walk through them first.

                        As they walk through, the pillars grant understanding of a language, that lasts for a month, and is indefinite as long as the recipient stays within the city itself.

                        The pillars were made to facilitate trade, enabling fast and easy communication and deal making, with the City itself taking a cut from any deal.

                        Another great use of the pillars, is the current influx of refugees. Most would have issues assimilating, lack of communication, strange habits and foreign mannerisms separating them. But with a common lingua franca, it is far easier for them to interact with the ordinary residents.

                                                Originally posted by TheCountAlucard View Post
                                                This is nowhere near the problem some make it out to be.
                                                It's not as dangerous for the locals as right-wing pundits make it out to be (and I think that "refusal to integrate" is often an over-blown excuses to hide disdain for a different colour of skin, which you can tell by seeing how very integrated immigrants are treated) but I teach English to refugees, and many of them feel really isolated because they don't speak good English or understand our customs, so they find it really difficult to make friends, not to mention get a job. Anti-immigrant xenophobes tend to say "they refuse to get jobs!" which is rubbish, because many of them are desperate to get jobs just to get out of the house and meet people (or the government bans them from getting a job), but they find it difficult to get hired because employers prefer to hire people with good English. Not to mention getting a good job, which requires much better English than stacking shelves or driving a taxi. I've taught several Iranians and a Palestinian with medical degrees from their own country, who need to pass the English exam required to be a doctor here. And that exam is hard. Learning English (or any language) is not at all easy (unless your own language is really similar, of course, but you still need to do a lot of work; though that's work that people in the past often did put in instead of learning science or maths).

                                                Similarly, when I lived in China, my lack of Chinese obviously made it impossible for me to do most jobs, or make friends with most people. All my friends were people who happened to be good at English.

                                                            Originally posted by TheCountAlucard View Post
                                                            A statue stands in the Underworld. A person who is hungry may give a point of Willpower to the statue, and in a short burst of varicolored light, food will appear in sufficient quantity to sate them for a day.

                                                            Well, this is a "miracle of supernatural power", which is Celestial Circle. However, that feels like it might be a little too weak for Celestial Circle in general, particularly with the limit you also mentioned (not being able to recover Willpower from sleeping after eating it for too long).

                                                            So, another question - can it feed someone else? Say I, Solar Anne, feel secure in my ability to recover the WP through some other method, and my circle is really hungry. Can I pay 4 WP and produce enough food for each of my other four circlemates? Or my followers? Or some larger group, if I have a way of recovering WP quickly? Or does the food only work for the person who paid the WP?

                                                            If it works for anyone as long as someone has WP to burn, I'd call it Celestial Ambition 1. On the other hand, if it's limited to only feeding the person who paid the WP, I think that pulls it down in power enough that I'd call it Terrestrial 3.
