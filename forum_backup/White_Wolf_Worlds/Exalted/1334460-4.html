    You know something? Screw it.

    After a 6 months of running operations, she should:

    Be able to fight, run, and do other athletics along with the best of mortal men
    Be ridiculously skilled at throwing and shooting.
    Start running an international operation selling art she's making on her own, all under different aliases
    Be able to make artwork which would take years in minutes, each of them a master piece.
    Have seven shadows evasion, and racing hare method
    Suitably good at subterfuge and stealth (capable of entering a hospital undetected, even when the guards are on alert)

    Now, her medicine... she should have custom charms. She probably has an entire lab, probably quite alike to a mad scientists' where she does her work. Bubbling chemicals of indeterminate origin. Bound elementals and demons, doing their work. Computers chugging away, calculating and computing something impossible, on infinite power sources. Numerous tanks of fluid, where strange creatures float in life support. Full-on biological lab with the included autoclaves, defenses, and NBC defense. A man who finds her lab should be frightened and in awe. A geneticist or an engineer who finds her lab should get on his knees and hope that she doesn't get access to real facilities.
