            Originally posted by Braininthejar View Post
            How can an unexalted mortal become a sorcerer, mechanically (I understand this is very rare, and most are just thaumaturgists)
            Ex3 core, p. 470, sidebar 'Mortal Sorcerers and Shaping Rituals'. Basically, a mortal initiates into Terrestrial Circle sorcery by mastering a shaping ritual, represented mechanically by purchasing a five-dot Merit that requires Occult 3. The first spell they learn becomes their control spell.

                                Originally posted by Sorcerous Overlord View Post
                                What can Fair Folk Gossamer be turned into? Is it "just" gold/rubies/wine/turkish delight etc. Or can it be worked into the Magic Metals (jade, moonsilver, etc)?

                                I don't believe it can be turned into the magic materials, no. Note that "gossamer", in past editions, hasn't really described a generic material in any case. It's more a matter of anything made with faerie artifice is generally called "gossamer". It's probably better to think of it in terms of being opposed to "Creation-made" rather than a single material.
