    Originally posted by Eric Minton View Post
    While I don't know exactly what the developers have in mind, a general rule of thumb is that Exaltations are miracles created by gods neither omnipotent nor omniscient, and not devices crafted by engineers on scientific principles. Their natures and qualities, powers and limitations, are functions of narrative and mythic resonance. In short: They are as the storyteller defines them, which is not necessarily how their creators would choose to define them if they were omnipotent, omniscient, perfectly rational actors.
    For the record, even if they were devices crafted by engineers working from scientific principles, they're still constrained by their materials, personal capabilities and personalities, and by the fact that you only get to invoke this thing once, so no testing cycle.

    So, yeah, I could totally buy that a non-human intelligence working with arcane components to a set of priorities based on current needs and predilections, to a set of rules they don't necessarily fully understand would produce something which, after hundreds of years, people would look at and go "What the heck?!? Why did they do that?"

                Five Days Darkness would probably want one, though I'd doubt they would be as Solar-like as he hopes.

                ALL-MIGHTY ORICALCUM CHIEF- One of the more influential gods of Yu-Shan, the egotistical and vain spirit's domain as the god of Cookery allows him huge capital in the hedonistic city of heaven. Never the less, he remains aloft from his peers, his dedication to his art overpowering even his pride. While harsh to those who he feels are not up to snuff, he is fair in his ruthlessness, and will(reluctantly) heap praise on those who prove worthy. While a Exigent would make the perfect errand boy, he has more pressing matters to attend to. For, since the loss of the First Age, the art of Cookery has grown weak in Creation. What is called fine today would be peasent food then, and people then would starve rather than eat what peasents eat in the Broken Age. With the return of the Lawgivers, he now sees his chance. Go forth, he tells his champion, and make a world worthy of the finest meals! Alternativly, he might view hunger as a personal offense, and bid his chosen to rid Creation of such Blasphemy. Likely abilities include Melee, Performance, Craft(Water, if they keep them divided), Socialize, and Resistance.

                TEN THOUSAND BEETLE WHISPERS- The god of Insects is a ...unnerving individual. Many mortals with just enough knowledge assume him to be one of the Forbidden due to his unfortunate tendency to appear as a giant swarm of insects arranged in an almost human form. Never the less, while he is in no way the best conversationalist, he is benevoltent enough, and has a mortal bride with whom he is shockingly faithful. He is also a good friend of the Manse of Ivy, possessing an honorary position for his skill in obtaining the knowledge few others can.
                Exigent possibilities:

                •An important Sidereal fact-finding mission needs a public, Exalted face who knows enough to not be a hinderance while still being both powerful and, if it comes down to it, disposable. Beetle has volunteered his own Essence to provide aid at the behest of a Chosen of Secrets he has befriended.

                • A Loyalist Abyssal has started advertising himself to many towns and villages with a disease that only kills insects. Many are buying his product, eager to be rid of pests, unaware of how badly this could disturb the local ecology. Whispers will need a champion to stop the Herald of Oblivion before any major damages occurs.

                •Someone has captured Whispers's wife and daughter in order to manipulate him in some game of politics. He has responded by Chosing someone with a grudge against the kipnapper and offering them all the pull they need to make him HURT.

                                            Luxana, Goddess of Recorded Knowledge

                                            Luxana, the prim and proper goddess of the written word, works in the Division of Secrets. Every disaster in the Time of Tumult sets back the amount of human knowledge and decreases the level of literacy in Creation, which directly threatens her purview. To prevent this, she has created the first of her Archival Exalted from one of her Scroll Maidens and tasked her with these objectives:
                                            • Find and preserve ancient long-lost stores of learning, from caches of Dragon King crystal records, to the great libraries of the First Age. Special notice is given to the Library of Sperimen, which must be freed from the Queen of Fangs (unless she can be persuaded to allow its general use to those who would seek its knowledge).
                                            • Aid those of scholarly bent (Twilights, No Moons, Scavenger Lords), and oppose those who would destroy the learning of civilization (barbarian Lunars, Fair Folk, Amoth City-Smiter and his minions).
                                            • Encourage the growth of progressive city-states and their libraries and universities, such as in Nexus. Convince other city-states of the benefits of organized learning.
                                            • The Apocalypse Scrolls have been stolen from Heaven! Luxana must act quickly and discreetly to recover them before the Division loses face, and even the Chosen of the Maidens can't be trusted with this mission...
                                            • Entire libraries full of priceless knowledge and writing have been lost over the eons...however, their shadows still linger in the Underworld. The Archival Exalted must dare the Underworld itself to search these shadowy, labyrinthine buildings, many crawling with all manner of monsters, and find a way to bring the plasmic volumes back into Creation.
                                            • The behemoth Halkomelem, spawned by He Who Bleeds the Unknown Word, is a hundred-yard long serpent made of crimson paper covered in golden script. The pages he sheds contains many lost works and secrets known only to the Yozis. The Archival must brave hell to gather enough scale-pages to bind into a new book, containing information vital to the safety of Creation.
                                            • Though it grieves Luxana, there is some knowledge which must never come to light, and some books must be suppressed for the good of all. Chief among these is the Broken-Winged Crane, which the Archival must ever be on the look out for. But many other cursed and forbidden tomes exist, and these must be found and either destroyed or locked away deep within the Forbidding Manse of Ivy or the Malachite Archives in Yu-Shan.

                                            Pictured: The First of the Archival Exalted

                                                        Originally posted by Tiresias View Post
                                                        Siakal, Goddess of Western War, Slaughter and Sharks

                                                        Siakal's purview includes naval warfare, wholesale slaughter and sharks. Potential threats to her purview include:
                                                        - A shark-totem Lunar has set himself up as a god-king in the western archipelagos and demands to be worshiped as the God of Sharks, undermining Siakal's influence over the animals. She requires an Exalted champion of her own to remind the west who commands the ocean's most ruthless predators.
                                                        - A Deathknight in the service of the Silver Prince commands fleets of immortal zombies and specters. This dread fleet swells with victory after victory, and the blood of the dead does not satisfy the thirst of Siakal. The Deathknight must be stopped before bloodless warfare dominates the West.
                                                        - A Solar privateer of the Eclipse Caste has taken up a crusade against piracy, and pirates have become rarer and more timid. Worse, the Solar does not sacrifice her foes to the ocean, but presses them into legitimate service within her own fleet. The Chosen of Siakal must slaughter the Solar and all her followers to return western warfare to its proper state.
