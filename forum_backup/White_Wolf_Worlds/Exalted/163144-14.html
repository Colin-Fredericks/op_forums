        Ill Met by Moonlight
        Celestial Circle Sorcery

        Standing upright with the moon to his back and a mirror to his front, the sorcerer observes the moon looming over the area he wishes to blight.

        This ritual begins when the moon is highest in the sky, and takes roughly an hour to perform, as the Sorcerer intones blessings to the moon and curses to the area.

        The Sorcerer may target specific individuals within that area as long as he can either see them in the reflection or has an Arcane Link to them in the form of tears, blood, or other bodily liquids; saying the name of each and naming the reasons for their suffering.

        Once the spell is cast (with a roll of Charisma + Occult + essence) the light of the moon is a blight upon the land until next Calibration :

        - Dreams depict the deaths of their dreamers through betrayal or suicide. Anyone sleeping in the area counts as having the Nightmares flaw unless they sleep in a pitch-black room into which no outside light can possibly penetrate.

        - Direct contact with moolight causes a persistent rash and similar damage and effects to [some hallucinogenic venom] and applies an additional dose every [hour?] of direct contact. Animals in the area usually go ferile after the first night, as they are just as prone to the nightmares and hallucinations.

        - The area is considered to be 1 level closer to Pure Chaos for the purposes of madness and mutation - this increases to 2 levels if the spell was cast with an exceptional success.

        - [seems appropriate but might be too much] The spell can only be Counterspelled during its initial ritual, afterwards its effects become a permanent part of Creation untill Calibration.

                Infernal Resistance
                Lesser creatures must fear the work of poisoners and assassins, employing tasters (willingly or no) to verify the safety of their foodstuffs. Not so the chosen of Hell. The character gains a bonus equal to his permanent Essence to all rolls to resist imbibed drugs, toxins, or poisons. In addition, he may spend 8 points of Essence to render himself completely immune to a single poison for a scene. However, from now on he suffers something like an addiction, craving the strange, sweet taste of danger. He must consume a number of drugs or poisons with a combined toxicity equal to his Essence every day or suffer a -1 penalty to all mental rolls from the distracting cravings.

                    Solar Investigation.

                    Blazing her Anima, the Solar illuminates an area equal to [her Essence x 10 yards] with a piercing light that pierces obstacles and leaves no shadows (in case of supernatural darkness, supernatural stealth or obstacles made of Magical Materials, this charm adds the Solar's Essence to the roll-off. Unattuned Magical Materials (such as walls of Jade) and Magical Materials the character is attuned to (such as the walls of a manse) offer no resistence.). Everywhere and everything this light hits is considered to be within the Solar's senses for the purposes of all other effects (such as Unsurpassed [Sense] Discipline) including touch and smell. This includes items stored under floor-boards, inside vaults, swallowed by other characters for safe-keeping and simply resting under 20 yards of cooled lava. While this does not pierce closed Portals, if one were to open even for a second, the Solar could get a very detailed idea of its insides.

                    All the shadows that would be cast appear to the solar as arcane scriptures that reveal mood, nature, intent, purpose, history and other such characteristics. Traits (such as Attributes, Abilities and Charms) are not revealed by this charm, though things that would modify or stand out from the target's Pattern with physical manifestations ( such as Graces, Mutations, Prosthetics, Demon-Ink Tattoos and Tattoo Artifacts ) are made Obvious unless magically concealed (in which case roll-off).

                    Activating this charm in tandem with the Night Caste Anima Power yields an invisble light that nevertheless allows the Solar to benefit from the charm's effects.

                            Between Here and Heaven
                            Celestial Circle sorcery

                            This spell creates a floating sanctum (though not a Sanctum) high in the sky, shrouded in clouds and appearing as whatever structure the Sorcerer wishes (from a perfectly flat platform to a floating castle with any amenities the sorcerer wishes to design for it during the preparation of this spell). The sactum's size and structure depend on how much time the Sorcerer is willing to spend preparing, designing and casting this spell. Without any preparation or design, this spell produces a large irregular slab of earth, similar to a forest clearing, that might contain a cave for shelter, a small pond with drinking water and enough wood for a campfire.

                            Additionally, once complete, the Sorcerer can spend [x] motes when within [some distance] to summon a bolt of lightning that would transport himself and anyone touching him (along with their belongings) to the sanctum's entrance.

                            The sanctum's position is fixed by default - it can't be used as a transport to fly from one point in creation to an other. However, it can ascend and descend to a limited degree - it cannot land or go beyond the dome of Creation. The Sorcerer may design some form of propultion for it however (giant sails, wind-mills or wind-oars) or incorporate magical means of transportation during the design process.

                            Lastly, while Magical Materials can be brought to it to reinforce its structure, this spell cannot create Magical Materials, so by default sanctums are made of natural materials such as stone, iron, glass and earth. However, if the Sorcerer has Magical Materials on hand, they can be incorporated into the design, and will position themselves in their assigned places upon the completion of this spell.

                                Sidereal Melee

                                At the edge of Creation, the laws start to break down. Concepts like up, down, front, back, life and death fail to accurately describe the conditions of the Wyld. While it is considered cruel, a Sidereal can apply these conditions to his adversaries.
                                By attacking their opponent's fate through a complex series of strikes, the inside of their opponent's body is treated as being part of the wyld. This attack counts as a regular attack for the purpose of dodging and blocking, though it can't be soaked through natural defenses, armor can still soak it. During this time, anything underneath his skin starts acting unnaturally, moving, twisting, growing, shrinking, changing consistency and changing color are all things that regularly happen. This effect inflicts [Essence] points of unsoakable, undodgeable lethal damage every turn, though it can never kill them, or get them unconscious. In addition, the pain of this technique gives the Sidereal a bonus when gathering information from the victim. The aftereffects of this Charm can be undone by medicine charms, though it is beyond mortal surgery.

                                    Solar War

                                    Against the armies of the Unconquered Sun, none shall stand. This Charm adds Essence dice to all rolls made by any force of the Solar's choice.                                             Terrestrial ... awareness I think.

                                            The terrestrial touches a surface and saturates it with an element, sending forth one of his senses along that surface in a line that glows with elemental color. This line moves forward along the surface at roughly double the exalt's normal movement speed. (and potentially costs motes per unit of distance )

                                            If the character knows more than one version of this charm, [cost] can be paid to activate those effects, endowing the trail with the combined properties of each activated element.

                                            Fire: Sight. The Exalt can see (but not hear or smell) out of any point along the blazing crimson trail. Additionally, the trail can create a blaze of light to illuminate out to [the range of an anima banner].

                                            Water: Taste. The Exalt can pick up tastes (such as spilled blood, food, sweat, salt, poison). Additionally, the trail can move through water in any direction (it can float, and isn't bound to a surface as long as it remains in water). This includes Ice, though not vapour.

                                            Earth: Touch. The exalt can feel the trail and everything with [essence x 10 yards] of it as though it was his own skin. Additionally, the trail can move through solid earth-aspected surfaces such as stone, gravel and earth, but not wood or ice.

                                            Air: Sound. The Exalt can hear everything along the trail as through he was present there himself. Additionally the trail can move through air in any direction like a flying ribbon, no longer bound to a surface.

                                            Wood: Smell. the exalt can pick up and follow smells, pinpointing their location and direction.
                                            (his sence of smell isn't more powerful however, simply more accurate).


                                            The expenditure of [cost, maybe no cost] upon activation or any time during the charm's duration allows the exalt to extend his anima power and effect to the trail (e.g. for a fire aspect, both the glowing and the 4L burning of anyone that touches it)
