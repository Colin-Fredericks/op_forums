    Originally posted by Elfive View Post
    I'm reminded of the episode of Steven Universe where Steven is trying to figure out how to summon his shield. He asks the gems how they summon their weapons, and all three of them give him a different answer.

    I suspect it's a similar situation for Glorious Solar Saber and other "technique" charms. There's a known thing exalts can do, but how they do it varies slightly.
    Not to dismiss your point, but it's also worth noting that future episodes reveal reasons for why the process is different for each of them.

    Gems like Amethyst were made to fight, so her weapon comes instinctually to her without real effort. Gems like Pearl weren't meant to fight at all, and it took her a long time and a lot of effort to figure out how to form a weapon. Garnet is a fusion of a footsoldier Ruby and an aristocractic Sapphire; the gauntlets are Ruby's easy-to-summon weapon, but refined by Sapphire's assistance.

    In Exalted mechanical terms, Pearl needed higher minimums, more XP, and possibly more non-combat Charm prerequisites to arrive at her spear, whereas Amethyst got her whip as a base-level Charm with low minimums and favored costs, and Garnet is using two different sets of Charms from which she made connected advanced Charms.
