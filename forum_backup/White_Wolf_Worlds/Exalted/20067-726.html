    Originally posted by Kelly Pedersen View Post
    I'd say yes, with the caveat that while no one else in the party might know a fight was happening, the victims definitely would (though they could certainly fail to recognize who was attacking them).



    I'd argue that the Poison Outside of Combat sidebar doesn't suggest that the poisoned person doesn't know she's been poisoned, just that she doesn't know who the poisoner is, necessarily. I would pretty much always run it so that someone who had to roll initiative due to poison was aware that something was wrong. I think that's pretty reasonable anyway - a poison fast-acting enough to kill you in seconds is probably going to have pretty noticeable symptoms. And it's better from a story perspective, anyway, I feel. Any character significant enough to roll initiative for upon being poisoned is significant enough to have a chance to react in some way.
    I tend to agree, now that I think about it; it would be entirely unfair to suggest that someone could be losing all their Initiative without knowing something is going wrong. Of course, someone who gets poisoned at a party (and doesn't already know about this White Veil thing) is probably going to jump to entirely the wrong conclusion about the source of his problem. (I expect most people would blame the food or something.)

    I'm not sure what the two victims of Thieves Fall Out would actually perceive, though, other than "we keep getting in each other's way somehow."

    Still, it could definitely be an interesting scene, with freaked-out victims scrambling to try and figure out who their enemy actually is while the assassin tries subtly to get into position for another strike.

            Stealing a question from the Ask the Devs thread here:

            Originally posted by Sith_Happens View Post
            Bumping/rephrasing:

            If your Resources comes from owning a business, does it correspond to your revenue, gross profit, or net profit?
            It's a lot more like net profit than the others, since it indicates how much money you have to spend on things for your character.

            Granted, the text says you're then supposed to spend half of that on your cost-of-living, and the examples of things that are "cost of living" for a merchant (tariffs) or businesswoman (wages of employees) include stuff that would be paid for out of revenue or gross profit. But the consequences of failing to do so are described primarily as social, rather than "you are hunted by the Imperial authorities for tax evasion" or "your employees all quit to work for someone who pays them and your business ceases operating". So I would just write off those as being poor examples. Particularly since if it is thought of as revenues, while your business expenses are "cost of living", it would imply the character's business has a net profit margin in excess of 50%.

                                                    Originally posted by Chausse View Post
                                                    • What is the difficulty for someone to notice there is something off about him ? I've seen there are Subtetly Charms in appearance that makes it so your Tell can only be seen in Glowing anima, at difficulty 7 I guess since it's not precised. What else should I think about when my players (most likely Lunars and Solars) might find clues ?
                                                    You can spot a Lunar's Tell on a Difficulty 7 (Perception+Awareness) roll, with 3 bonus dice if you've noticed it previously, unless it is hidden by Subtle Silver Declaration. Otherwise, the disguise is perfect except by high end charms (such as Eye of the Unconquered Sun). Clues that indicate that the impersonated character is acting oddly, only reveal that the character is acting oddly.
                                                    Originally posted by Chausse View Post
                                                    • Can he actually emulate some Charms that would look like Charms from a Cloud-folk person descendant and it's indiscernable, or does he have to buy Mutations and then use some Charms I haven't seen yet to make them look cloudy and players can notice something ?
                                                    You cannot acquire charms through Sacred Hunt's. You may use the God-body Consumption charm to access the Eclipse charms of a character you permanently kill. [That would be Wind Whistling Trick. if the descendant has that charm.]

                                                    When you take the shape of a human or animal you can gain their Supernatural Merit mutations by paying extra motes equal to the merit's point value. However, Cloud Persons do not have any of these. Still, their descendants might have an unusual trait.
                                                    Originally posted by Chausse View Post
                                                    • Can he hide intimacies or create set of intimacies that prevent others discovering who he really is ? The rules for human shapeshifting do not seem to indicate you can take intimacies from the shape you take ?
                                                    Manipulation's Guile charms will help conceal your imposture.


                                                    Sacred Hunting will not do so by default, but some of the Heart's Blood charms (which allow you to perform a Sacred Hunt without killing the target) do allow you to acquire some of the Sacred Prey's Intimacies.

                                                    If you use the Intelligence Heart's Blood charm Blood Geas Binding to acquire the shape, you may then use Memory-Drinking Alteration to gain one of the oathbreaker's Ties to a person at Minor intensity, and memories associated with it.

                                                    The Perception Heart's Blood charm requires you to discover and adopt one of the prey's Defining Intimacy in order to learn their shape.

                                                            Lunars have a few ways of dealing with anima displays while disguised :

                                                            -Essential Mirror Nature lets you change your anima's appearance to fit whatever Exalt or spirit you are impersonating
                                                            -False Moonrise Phantasm lets you mark a target, and then when your anima flares if the target is within medium range it appears on them instead of you

                                                            For Intimacies, Silver Mask subversion causes you to literally become someone else, forgetting even that you are a shapeshifted Lunar. When activating it, you must set a condition which ends the effect (i.e. when the Satrap dies), because you will no longer remember that you can end the effect yourself unless one of your real major intimacies becomes threatened or the Story ends.
