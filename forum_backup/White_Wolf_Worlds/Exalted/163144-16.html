    Puppets Without Strings Revelation
    Sidereal Presence
    Having carefully mentored her students, the sifu knows they are ready to be weaned of her tutelage. She abandons all connection to someone, rendering them Outside Fate so far as she is concerned; she cannot lay astrology on them, fool them with her Fate-derived illusions, etc. In return, she ensures that she has truly made them in her image, with one last parting gict: the Sidereal's subject gains a secondary magical Motivation which matches the Sidereal's own. This cannot change, even if the Sidereal changes her own; only something capable of breaking the Sidereal's magic can remove it (which excludes the Sidereal, who obviously cannot affect her student any longer).

        Shape of the Soul

        Requires: Wyld Cauldron Technology, Essence 5

        Solars do not simply bring order to chaos - they give it purpose. This charm allows Wyld Shaping Technique successes to used to create customized heroic mortals, at the cost of 4 successess per heroic mortal. The mortal's statline is that of a default heroic mortal, but the solar has complete control of how those stats are distributed. For one additional success the solar can conjure a copy of the original mortal. All mortals created this way appear with the motivation "Obey my maker" In the first age a lot of Solars used this to create legions of quite dimwitted but exceedingly beautiful courtesans, or strong and fiercly loyal bodyguards when not even dragon blooded could be trusted... or test subjects for anything from combat charms to new necromantic spells.

            Tearing the Veil
            Fair Folk Heart

            The raksha may sheathe themselves in beautiful forms of perfection to travel Creation, but the truth -- hungry maws and unformed will -- always lurks beneath. And nearer the surface than most might guess. Though it happens seldom, the Princes of Chaos sometimes allow their true madness to break through a crack in their sublime masks. Though they expose themselves to Creation's deadly pressure, some (short-lived) fey deem the brief power it grants them the only appropriate response to marring such a perfect face.

            Upon receiving at least 1L in damage (this may be self-inflicted as a reflexive action), a Fair Folk with an active but non-Permanent Assumption Charm may reflexively dismiss it, and becomes unable to apply a new one any earlier than the next scene. She immediately receives however many motes she had committed to it. For the rest of the scene, the local area is treated as the Wyld for all purposes, with a strength dependent on the raksha's Heart Grace (2 for Bordermarches, 3 for Middlemarches, 4 for Deep Wyld, and 5+ for Pure Chaos; if used in the Wyld, this Charm cannot reduce the local strength). The new Waypoint acquires some superficial properties reflective of the Assumption Charm (iconography relevant to the particular animal, element, or emotion; small apparent changes to environment, local creatures, etc.), which cannot be altered for the duration. A raksha may also choose to include a number of Fantasies somehow resonant with that Assumption, equal to (Heart - 2), rolled as normal using the raksha's traits.

            At the end of the scene, the area returns to normal, though a few subtle changes may linger, particularly if exposed to more potent raksha. Now without an active Assumption, the raksha begins suffering all associated damage if in Creation, and will no doubt take whatever means necessary to avoid calcifying.

                A New Face in Rippled Water
                Getimian Larceny
                By shattering her opponent's reflection, the Unbound shatters their identity.
                The Peripheral version of this Charm allows the Getimian to make a reflexive disguise attempt on behalf of her target, cursing them with the face of one lost to the world. The Personal variant, meanwhile, simply deprives the victim of self and memory, temporarily removing all but their Defining intimacies.

                Awe-Inspiring World Cauldron
                Infernal Craft
                The warlock stomps his foot, and the earth shatters, and the air, and the boundary between them. Chaos seeps from the cracks.
                This Charm creates a space of almost-Wyld around the user. Within, the Green Sun Prince is considered to have the tools for any Craft roll she performs, allowing him to chisel rocks into statues with his gaze or smelt metal in his cupped hands. Furthermore, he may perform reflexive, Craft-based Intimidate actions against anyone who tries to attack him or enter his cauldron, even if he isnt aware of them.

                        Optical Light Flection Defense
                        Cost: variable; Mins: Strength 4, Essence 3;
                        Type: Reflexive (Step 2)
                        Keywords: Combo-OK, Obvious, Counterattack
                        Duration: Instant
                        Requirements: Light-Etched Interceptor Barrior

                        The champions of the Great Maker reject the offense of their foes. This charm's appearance depends on its prereisite's appearance. If the LEIB is based on external armor, then the OLFD is a white jade and orichalcum circular assembly fitted on the gauntlet, for internal LEIBs the OLFD appears as additional circular patterns seared into the champion's arm.

                        The charm functions by briefly empowering the LEIB on successful parries, pulsing the barrier to thrust the attack back upon the attacker. This costs a number of motes equal to the essence rating of the attacker. This functions as a counterattack, as if the attacker attacked herself, with an identical attack as used on the champion. For close combat attack these cannot be defended with the same weapon being thrust back on the attacker, while ranged attacked reflected back on attackers do not carry this added penalty. This charm cannot be used if the LEIB perfectly parries an attack, as that solidifies the barrier and locks the emiitter nodes, preventing a counter-pulse.

                        Submodules:

                        Enhanced Emitter Stabilizers
                        By strengthening the OLFD's connections and essence circuitry with jade coatings and more refined copper wires, the OLFD can now also function when the LEIB is used to perfectly parry an attack.

                                    Philosophy of a Hated Person
                                    Lunar Manipulation

                                    Luna's Chosen often lead others to more favorable impressions of themselves, but there can be value in inspiring disdain or hatred. Particularly if these negative feelings become tied to ideals and principles the Lunar cannot abide. This Charm supplements a Lunar's attempt to build or strengthen in others a negative Intimacy toward himself (or whomever he appears to be), subtly turning his every look, word, and gesture in an unpalatable direction. If the Lunar is espousing a philosophy or idea and convinces others he holds it, then his targets will find great ease in painting that thought with the same brush as the Lunar, as this Charm supplements an attempt to build or strengthen a negative Intimacy toward it. If the Lunar is wearing the shape of someone who actually holds (or held) a positive Intimacy toward the idea, this borrowed sincerity only magnifies the inspired contempt, reducing the difficulty and strengthening the effect even further.

                                                Mask of Iron Whispers:
                                                Abyssal Presence

                                                The Abyssal's face twists, hardening into a slate grey, emotionless mask, with a voice as cold and distant as Death itself. As long as this Charm is active, the Abyssal's face does not move, he doesn't blink, move his lips or twitch his eyes. As long as the mask is worn, it gives the following effects.

                                                -His cold, distant voice gives people a penalty in gaining information on his Intimacies directly from him.
                                                -The unmoving face and the quiet, inhuman voice uneases even the most brave men, giving a minor intimacy of fear to anyone within earshot of the Abyssal.
                                                -The voice of the mask seems to pierce every other sound, even if it itself is a barely hearable whisper. The voice of the Abyssal is always hearable and understandable and can not be overpowered by louder sound. The voice does not carry any further than his normal voice does.
                                                -His face has hardened into this mask and is considered medium armor for as long as the mask is active. This only applies to his face and does not give him any penalties associated with wearing medium armor.

                                                Maybe a bit obvious, but Abyssals kind of need a creepy face type-Charm.

                                                    Originally posted by While My Guitar View Post
                                                    Truths Written in Scars
                                                    Lunar Perception (or intelligence maybe) - indefinite (committed).

                                                    The Lunar's eyes take on a silvery sheen - a layer of essence shows them the meaning behind markings carved in flesh and wood and stone.

                                                    For the duration of this charm, markings such as scars, bruises, tattoos, claw marks and the like, appear to be exhaling glowing mists that form patterns of meaning, revealing the marking's purpose or reason for being. This often manifests as claw-speak, but is ultimately unique to each Lunar.

                                                    Each success on a Perception (or Intelligence) + [Appropriate ability]* roll reveals pertinent information, such as:
                                                    - what message the marking is supposed to send (e.g. "stay away", "remember your duty", "You have no chance to survive, make your time" etc.)
                                                    - why it is there ("to mark him as a traitor", "to prevent him from walking ever again", "so that no one will ever find him attractive", etc.)
                                                    - how it was made ("By a white-hot blade", "by a Tiger of Larjin's claws", etc.)
                                                    - what context it happened in ("protecting own home", "dueling for honor", "begging for mercy" etc.)
                                                    etc.

                                                    A tattoo can be revealed as being there to remind a warrior of a particular battle, scars can be revealed as coming from defending one's home from invaders, a tiger's missing eye can be revealed as being caused by a particularly callous poacher during a hunt.

                                                    This charm can reveal such information about writing as well, though it doesn't automatically defeat attempts at misdirection - it can reveal the intended meaning of the piece and the context in which it was written, but it doesn't make lies any more obvious than they would usually be. If the writing (or tattoo) is coded, it might appear as devoid of meaning unless the Lunar knows the code. Likewise, this charm isn't affected by most common languages unless one is deliberately being used to obscure the message's meaning, and as such can offer rough translations - wording meant to be specific (such as loop-holes, plays on words, deliberate misspellings and technicalities) does not survive this translation.

                                                    *Appropriate abilities include Craft for tattoos, medicine, survival for scars caused by "natural" means (animals and environments), Martial Arts, Thrown, Archery, War or Melee for markings caused by weapons or intended for troops, Linguistics for writing.

                                                        Infernal (Cecelyne; building off Penitents Like Scattered Grains)

                                                        The Warlock has learned to hear prayers from places of desolation, but it sometimes serves her needs to receive petitions from more vibrant locales. An act of reducing the local vibrancy will do.

                                                        Upon learning this Charm, the Green Sun Prince becomes able to receive prayers from even a lush location, so long as they are accompanied by an act that makes it more a place of desolation.                                                             Raksha Way.

                                                            Allows the Fairfolk to treat any 2 waypoints that are connected by up to [Way + Essence?] waypoints/journeys as being adjacent.
