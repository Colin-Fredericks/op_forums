                    Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post

                    For a start, this feels like a suggestion that the Salinan Working was why the 5 trials worked in the first place, which was not so.

                    I think it's a better approach to see the broad idea of the trials as the template that all initiations into sorcery take. After all, in the prior Editions, there was no one format that they had, so long as they covered the basis of having a teacher, going on a journey, making a sacrifice, whatever the other two were. The Third Edition description of becoming one covers many of the same concepts in its description of the need for a mentor or meditation on a source of power and a need for dedication, effort, and the willingness to abandon something old about yourself.

                    It also depends on what one means by the Salinan Working; are we talking the really vague Second Edition version, or the First version of "spells and sorcerous instruction has been encoded into patterns in nature and the responses of certain spirits, for those who know how to look for them". If the latter, it could still be a thing that exists in the setting, although some of its concepts have been distributed through varied shaping rituals.

                    As for the methods, as Blaque said, the core alone details soul-perfecting elixirs and the Talisman of Ten Thousand Eyes alongside the spirit and Wyld stuff, as well as alluding to the Heptagram, being powered by storms, drawing energies cultivated by a manse or understanding sorcery through song. What Fire Has Wrought expands on the Heptagram stuff and has an initiation based on drowing oneself in a magic freezing pool, coming away with a shard of ice in your heart and an affinity for ghosts. Fangs at the Gate includes the tutelage of Raksi (based on studying ancient texts, hallucinogen enhanced vision quests, and painful trials), being instructed by the ghost of a First Age sorcerer summoned by inexplicable crystal flowers, and occult lore hidden in root and leaf.
                    If the answers you're getting aren't answering your question, let us know what's wrong with those answers so we know what is and isn't working. We cannot help you if we don't know why our answers aren't sufficient.
                    Alternatively, if we're not helpful cut us out of the process and consult the book. I assure you, it has answers to your questions and it probably answers them better than we will since it's got pages of text on the stuff.
