    Kaleidoscopic Memory Auger
    Raksha Cup, 7m 1wp

    Upon Ravishing a target, the Raksha may make a Reflexive Shaping Action, regardless of timing constraints, in which she sets the scene according to one of her target's memories. A proper Stunt in this way robs the target of [its rating] Willpower points and converts them into points of Gossamer which can immediately be spent to activate Assumption or Mutation charms as long as they are congruent with the memory. If the target doesn't have enough remaining willpower, the remainder is converted from her Compassion channels. Any remainder after this is lost.

    Using this power with a Stunt rated at 2 points also allows the Raksha to Reflexively deactivate a single Assumption-type charm as though the Raksha was in the Wyld. A 3-point stunt allows the deactivation of any number of assumptions.

        Blue Roses Under Heaven
        Astrological effect, Performance

        With a song to the Venus, the Sidereal's anima banner takes on an azure brilliance (regardless of his caste), and he temporarily winks out of existence. Over the next scene a garden manifests in the area, whether erupting through the ground or simply shimmering into being.

        As long as he maintains this effect, the Sidereal exists as the garden itself, drawing lovers, artists, and dreamers to himself. He temporarily appears in his Resplendent Destiny to maintain the grounds, but the world and the visitors pay him no heed. While he spends time as a muse, the Exalt resists the efforts those who would investigate him, track him, or seek to do violence upon him or those who visit him.

        The difficulties implicit in existing as architecture, as well as inspiring those who attend him, are taxing duties: The Sidereal has only an hour a week to attend to his own affairs.

                (Writing these for the stopgap system I've been using with my group to fake EX3. Makes me wonder how close my predictions are gonna come. )

                HOPES AND AMBITIONS VITRIFIED (Sidereal Integrity, Reflexive)

                For those who understand that all things must come to an end, they also understand that some things are forever; others can be made so.
                If someone attempts to erode or appeal to one of the Chosen's intimacies with an influence attempt, the Sidereal may instead use this charm to form a crystalline tear "containing" their intimacy, separating it from themselves. The Exalt no longer feels the effects of that intimacy for as long as it is held in this glasslike structure; love turns to apathy, as does hate, he simply has removed that intensity of feeling from himself. If pressed to his temple, the gemstone melts and the intimacy returns to his personality at the same level of intensity it was before using this charm, although if the stone is destroyed the intimacy itself as well as the intense feelings associated with it are gone forever.

                If fashioned into a piece of jewelry and worn by someone other than the Maiden's Chosen, it instills in them the same intimacy contained inside for as long as it is worn.

                CHAMPIONS-AS-CHATTEL PRONOUNCEMENT (Gentimian Presence, Indefinite)

                The Fate-Lost know better than any other how jarring the loss of prestige and status can be, when replaced by the neverwhen.
                The Gentimian may use this charm to make a statement to all present, forcing a Presence+Manipulation Roll that must exceed their target's Resolve; each success adds one year and a day onto the duration. Each person affected by this charm is given a glimpse into a possible fate, for as long as the duration lasts, believe a person of high status, standing or respect selected by the Gentimian to be the opposite; in the minds of those affected, Kings become beggars, generals become cowards, and famed courtesans become common street-whores.

                    Star-Shattering Strike
                    (Infernal, Ophidian)
                    The Infernal shreds Fate with her bare hands, tearing destiny asunder; for a year and a day, the target of the blow is free from all presumptions of himself and of Heaven:
                    * He exists outside of Fate, unable to be predicted or affected by Sidereal astrology.
                    * While his Virtues, Intimacies, and Motivation continue to exist, and he expresses them as he sees fit, they no longer compel him nor defend him. For the duration of this charm, such numinous attributes of his character are prone to fluctuate
                    * The successful damage inflicted by the strike is treated as successes on a Disguise roll; those who knew him before may find his hazy nirvana renders him difficult to recognize.

                                Ten Thousand Discoveries Spirit
                                Lunar Intelligence

                                The Lunar pursues all there is to know with a rare passion and drive, and grows invigorated by the act of finding. The character acquires an overdrive pool of ten motes, which can be filled only through great acts of discovery, such as in research on something particularly obscure, being one of the first in ages to set eyes on a place or thing, or ferreting out a very well-hidden secret. Each such discovery directly from someone else's research adds 2 motes to this pool, while those uncovered personally add 4.

                                In addition to the usual uses for overdrive pools, these may also be spent on actions directly related to making a discovery (be it an Investigation roll, a Survival roll to reach somewhere remote, a Presence roll to intimidate out a secret, or a Stealth roll to spy on a conversation) or overcoming a direct obstacle to such (clearing a doorway to research materials, or Stealth to slip by a guard on the way to a prisoner who knows something).

                                Normally, the character may make no more than one discovery per scene. The exception is when in the Wyld or some other chaotic or dreamlike realm: there, per scene, the character may receive the benefit for a number of discoveries about the place or its inhabitants equal to her Intelligence score. Wise raksha have learned the hard way to give such driven Lunars an especially wide berth.

                                    Bell tolls thirteen

                                    Labyrinth circle necromancy

                                    The necromancer Shapes a sickly green-and-black necromantic circle that hangs in the air as though affixed to an invisible wall. A ritual mace made of bone, salt and chalcedony is then used to strike the circle (the necromancer needn't be the one to perform the strikes) which rings out in deafening tones, sending necrotic waves along the circle's plane for [Essence x 100] yards for one full turn after every strike.

                                    Anything (being, object or structure) that comes into contact with these waves suffers unsoakable damage equal to the striker's Strength + Weapon + Necromancer's Occult rating at least once and thereafter with every wave that it remains in contact. Buildings erode steadily under this effect and do not get the benefits of their natural Hardness.

                                    Anything slain or destroyed by this effect manifests at the adjacent point in the underworld as a grave good, effigy, structure or ghost, as appropriate, belonging to the necromancer, though it is possible to dedicate such destruction to another entity.

                                    On the thirteenth strike or at the crack of dawn (whichever comes sooner), the spell concludes, shattering the mace, releasing one final ear-shattering ring and dealing all cumulated damage to its wielder - this damage is a side-effect, not a cost, and can be readily soaked or redirected through other magic.

                                                        Leaping Lightning Distraction
                                                        Air-aspect Dodge
                                                        Type: Supplemental.

                                                        Lightning courses through the air, transporting the Exalt in a blinding flash of elemental Essence. This charm supplements a Defend or a Defend Other action, allowing the Exalt to move up to their Dash range and use their Dodge DV in place of their Parry DV for the purpose of Defend Other as the flash of light and threat of electrocution dissuade the attacker. For the latter effect to work, the attacker must see the flash of lightning. Normally this charm is too quick to block out by simply shielding ones eyes, but surprise-negating charms or fore-knowledge of this charm's activation may allow a reflexive contest of Wits+Awareness vs. the Exat's Dex+Dodge.

                                                        (set-up for the next one) Clever-Hands: Black wraps made of Chthonic Leather studded with Black Jade. (despite the name, they can be worn on any part of the body around which they can wrap. On the hands they count as smashfists, on the feet they count as god-kicking boots)

                                                        Main effect: Clever-hands offers a pool of 5 successes that can be used to supplement any ability roll as long as the user isn't seen. These successes refill when the bearer successfully completes an action without any witnesses at a rate of 1 success per unsuspecting character within the same room or Essence x 10 yards (whichever is smaller). Extras do not count, and battle-groups count as a single character for this purpose.
                                                        (further Evocations might extend this pool)

                                                        Blink-Step
                                                        Evocation of Clever-Hands
                                                        Cost: ¯\_(ツ)_/¯ enough to curb abuse

                                                        This evocation was discovered (developed?) by a Night-Caste investigator-spy who chose to wrap Clever-Hands around her shins and ankles.

                                                        The world is taken aback by the subtelty of the user's movement, and all blink in unison in an attempt to understand. Using her movement action to activate this effect, the Exalt renders all opponents effectively blind for a single tick, long enough to perform an action - they are completely unable to open their eyes. This time goes unnoticed, and seems to be no longer than a normal blink.
