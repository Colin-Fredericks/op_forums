    Random Name Generator

    09-13-2015, 03:19 PM
    BEHOLD!
    For those cool "word-salad" names that Creationites love so much. Voidstate's name generator has been down for a while, but I found a name generator based on it, which was broken but had the script all laid out. I didn't copy it 100% exactly, I've made some changes and added more vocabulary.

    For Dynast names I made this: https://orteil.dashnet.org/randomgen/?gen=AYsfB34A
    and I also found this: http://tobias.bindslet.dk/names/index.php

    Random Phonetic names: https://orteil.dashnet.org/randomgen/?gen=K4rM3Z41
    Warning: it is not designed to produce familiar-looking European or East Asian names.

    Old Realm names:
    Since Old Realm names and words are a mishmash of so many real-world languages, these generators are based on my own personal statistical interpretation of Old Realm, which I started working out here with help from nitai.
    > Celestial, Terrestrial, and ancient Old Realm: https://orteil.dashnet.org/randomgen/?gen=FaifGb1M
    > Infernal Old Realm: https://orteil.dashnet.org/randomgen/?gen=EcFUJXMw

    __________________________________________________ _________________________________
    Some (ok, a lot) of the results are strange or silly, but what can I do... they're random scripts.

    Please share your comments and criticisms.

    __________________________________________________ _________________________________
    URLS are updated and should be working again. If they fail, make sure they're going to https.

    NEW!
    Artifact description generator: https://orteil.dashnet.org/randomgen/?gen=98pbXTPq
    Tells you what the artifact looks like and what it's made of ... but not what it is or what it does.
    (Obviously this one's unfinished.)

    NEW! 12/2021
    Dragon King name generator: https://orteil.dashnet.org/randomgen/?gen=LwePLTEY
    There are very few canon names in High Holy Speech, so this one is a bit odd, but it does make names (or words) that fit alongside the canon names.
