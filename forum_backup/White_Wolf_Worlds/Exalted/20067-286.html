    Originally posted by Yatogami Odin View Post
    True. Four individual wolves would each receive three automatic successes on their attacks, where a battle group would only attack once. Even with the Size bonus, the danger of that kind of wolf pack is that it's more resilient to damage via the Magnitude track.
    Something to keep in mind is that battlegroups on their own should probably not really be handled as a difficult encounter unless its elite troops or has a might. A Size 1 BG of wolves is only barely more resilient than 2 trivial opponent wolves, and is actually mechanically far less resilient than a single non-trivial wolf. It is fairly possible for a pretty decent mortal (Strength 2, Dex 3, Combat Ability 3) to wipe out a size 1 group of wolves in one round if they fail their rout check and if she spends a WP. If they don't, then it's a matter of 2 rounds. For a mortal against 1 non-trivial wolf, unless they get really lucky on their JB roll, then it's 2 rounds minimum per wolf.

                        The letter of the Lawgivers is absolute, but the letter should never trump the spirit. This Charm serves to upgrade its prerequisite, Spirit Strengthens the Skin.                                                             Originally posted by WalkingEye View Post
                                                            How exactly does Over and Under Method work?
                                                            Ok, so an enemy declares a Decisive attack against your character and you declare that you will Parry. You then declare that you are going to reflexively Clash said attack via Over and Under Method, paying 7m, 1wp. After your (Dex + Melee + everything but Accuracy) roll beats your opponent's (Dex + Brawl/Martial Arts/Melee + everything but Accuracy) roll, you roll (Dex + Melee + everything including Accuracy) vs. your opponent's Defense, then determine your Raw damage for a Withering attack with that roll, roll it, then, after resolving the withering damage, rather than adding the Initiative drained by the Flashing Edge of Dawn to your own Initiative, you add that much Decisive damage, in addition to the extra level gained from the Clash, to the result of the Initiative roll, assuming the target didn't have enough Hardness to absorb the attack, although, and I imagine this is where the confusion arises, I believe that the bonus damage, both from winning the Clash and from Flashing Edge of Dawn, isn't affected by Hardness, and thus is applied directly to their Health Levels. I also believe that the extra point of Initiative gained by landing Flashing Edge of Dawn would not be granted until after the Initiative reset created by the inciting Decisive Attack.
