    I remember once our 40xp Solars+Lunars fought a single Fire Aspect. We ended up fleeing the Wyld Hunt 3 times. (Whereas most games I played, PCs beat the Wyld Hunt a couple of times and then are so far beyond them they don't care).

    But of course, Solars have much weaker charms now, which is the big mitigator.

                                Originally posted by Sunder the Gold View Post
                                I'm not sure what you mean by "Air".
                                The picture gets a lot brighter once you get to Signatures and high-Essence, as you did in your examples. I'm primarily looking at the survivability of starting player characters who have an interest in maintaining Aura, and I'm not limiting myself to Melee defenses in doing so.

                                Here's what I'm looking at. If you're under attack and are trying to defend yourself* or others without interrupting an Aura-duration Charm, at starting Essence...

                                Any Aura can use the Brawl, Dodge, and Melee Excellencies, along with Flame-Borne Interception and Blinding Spark Distraction in Melee, and (Element) Protection Technique in Resistance.

                                Water Aura can additionally use Blade-Deflecting Palm in Brawl, Graceful Flowing Defense in Melee, and Eternal Tide Endurance in Resistance.

                                Fire Aura can additionally use Flickering Candle Meditation, Hopping Firecracker Evasion, and Ember-Amid-Smoke Misdirection in Dodge; Crossfire Flash and Flame Warden Stance from Melee; and Shimmering Heat Mirage from Stealth.

                                Earth Aura can additionally use Impervious Skin of Stone and Unfeeling Earth Meditation in Resistance

                                Wood Aura can additionally use Virtuous Negation Defense in Dodge and Supple Viridian Scales in Resistance

                                Air Aura can additionally use... nothing.

                                A character who wants to defend themselves at starting Essence without breaking Air Aura cannot use any Charm that they couldn't use with any other Aura. Air gains nothing whatsoever special on defense from any Ability at starting Essence. Wood also has very little; Virtuous Negation does nothing to help with attacks against you yourself, and Supple Viridian Scales is only a defense boost if you're wearing Medium+ armor, otherwise it's a utility Charm.

                                Higher Essence does help, especially for Wood, since Wood Melee picks up Aura of Grasping Branches and Root-and-Hand Merging from Ess 3.

                                *I'm defining "defend yourself" here as "you are being attacked and need to not-die." Yes, Air has Stealth, but it's too late to hide if the arrow/daiklave/rock/whatever is already flying at your head.

                                It occurs to me that I could also complain about high-Essence Charms whose use invalidates all their prerequisites. Revolving-Hurricane-Force Defense gives an Essence 5, Resistance-focused character an Air-elemental, Aura-duration persistent defense, but the Aura duration means that of the five Resistance Charms you must have in order to learn Revolving-Hurricane-Force, only (Element) Protection Technique can be used without ending it, rendering the other four dead weight for the duration of Revolving-Hurricane-Force.

                                I don't think any of this is unplayable, mind you. It just makes certain Aura duration Charms feel like their real duration is "until you become the target of a serious attack."
