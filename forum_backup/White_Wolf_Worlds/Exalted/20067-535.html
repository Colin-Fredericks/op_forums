    While I was going through the book Wonders of the Lost age from 2E I noticed some of those artifact armors had components that mirrored the effects of other artifacts of lower ranks, mentioned earlier in the book.
    I understand the cost/rank of an artifact should be case by case but I'm trying think of a general rule of thumb to follow.

    Like how many 1 dot artifacts should be equivalent to a 2 dot artifact? Or to make that more generalized how many abilities from N dot artifacts would it take for an artifact that possesses them all to have a rank of (N+1) dots?

    An example would be if I wanted a character to have boots that increased a character's mobility options. So they make boots that can adhere to surfaces strongly for a climbing bonus (which is equivalent to a 2 dot artifact from 1e called the spider grippers), after a sharp fall by a character using the boots the craftsman went to add a safety feature that makes it so the boots slowed down a character as they fell (equivalent to a 2 dot artifact from 2e called a jump harness). Would that artifact qualify for a 3 dot rating now that has the abilities of two different 2 dot artifacts?

    As stated, 2e was inconsistent at best, 2.5 was sane but eyeballed to the point that I wouldn't try and reverse engineer some sort of scale, and for 3e it is more of a question of scale of effects.

    In Arms of the Chosen, there is a 5 dot armor, that also has artifact razor claws as a gimme evocation, and one of the warstriders wholesale has built in Lightning Ballistas and an implosion bow, but they're unlocked at higher essence.
