    Vosetalii, the Splendid Vessel (Orichalcum Trimarine, Artifact ****) [PEACH]

    06-17-2020, 07:02 PM
    Hi everybody! I made the details on an artifact ship in our campaign, and would love some feedback, as this is the second artifact I have ever made. I wanted to make a swashbucking exploratory-adventure vessel with a full number of excellencies. Would love some honest critique and opinions from you guys - I know it's a huge mess and a long read, but every comment is appreciated!
    Vosetalii, the Splendid Vessel (Orichalcum Trimarine, Artifact ****)
    Vosetalii is an obscure ship, appearing sporadically during all over Creation, and then disappearing for a century, a millennium or an era. The appearance of the ship always heralds adventure, excitement, and deeds of great heroism and daring. The Vosetalii is like a candle in the night, lighting up the darkness for a while, then disappearing again, taking its’ crew of heroes with it as it goes. The origins of the ship are mysterious even to the wisest of sages, chronicles only describing the passage of Vosetalii, but never discussing its’ origins.
    As a ship, Vosetalii is a large three-hulled trimaran, sleek and resplendent in white and golden colors, with six masts spread over its’ decks and a single double-bladed rudder of white wood. A figurehead adorns the high center prow, a golden cherubic face in otherworldly human likeness. Within, the ship is spacious and beams with antediluvian alienness, too sleek and clean for any seafaring vessel made of mundane materials. Orichalcum circuitry adorns the insides of the ship, and strange moonsilver filigrees appear on the decks and the walls at times, revealing the art and science of old in the machinations of the ship. In the heart of the ship lies a man-sized pyramid of black jade, humming softly with energy.


    Attunement: 10m
    Speed: Sails +2, Oars +1, empty cargo holds +1, Essence propulsion engine +4, legendary sails +3, current +1
    Maneuverability: 0
    Hull: 0x2/-1x2/-2x2/-4x2/Incapx2
    Cargo: The Vosetalii can carry a crew of 50 very comfortably in below-deck barracks, and has a 2-ton cargo hold in each of its’ three hulls.
    Era: Eight Directions Era


    Evocations of Vosetalii:
    When attuning to Vosetalii, the Exalt can decide a single goal he wants to attain. While on board the Vosetalii, the ship offers +1 non-charm die to every action working directly towards that goal. The ship also provides an Intimacy-like effect towards advancing that goal, meaning it can be invoked as a major Intimacy in situations resisting social influence against working towards that goal.


    Brotherhood of the Ship:
    Cost: 5m, 1 wp; Mins: Essence 1, Willpower 5
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Permanent
    Prerequisites: None

    Vosetalii is not a vessel for the solitary hero. It is a home, a hearth upon the seas, and welcomes all with open arms. The captain of Vosetalii may allow anyone to use this charm. The Brotherhood of the Ship allows anyone to use and learning of any of Vosetaliis’ evocations that somebody who has used Brotherhood of the Ship has unlocked, as if they were attuned to it.
    In effect this charm, after being learned, allows the Exalted crew to cross-learn and utizile the evocations of Vosetalii freely as long as someone has learned them.


    Unfurl the Pyramid:
    Cost: 1m; Mins: Essence 1, Occult 1;
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisities: None

    By showing the secret mark of Auravash, a crewman of the Vosetalii can open (and close) a stele of black jade inside the middle hull of the ship. Within the pyramid are three hearthstone slots, useable by anyone attuned to the ship. The hearthstones function as if linked to a to a manse, and ignore any dependencies to other manses, hearthstones or artefacts.
    Using Unfurl the Pyramid requires touching the black jade pyramid within the ship.


    Triangular Fermentation:
    Cost: 30m per stone; Mins: Essence 3, Occult 4
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: None
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisities: Unfurl the Pyramid

    By invoking the three hidden treasures of Auravash embedded in syncretic principles of alchemy, Essence forms into Hearthstones in a swift process of thaumaturgical condensation. Every time this charm is used, a hearthstone forms inside the black jade pyramid within the ship. This charm can be used for a total of three times within a story, and the hearthstones cannot be removed from the stele. After a story, the hearthstones vaporize, and the charm must be used again in the next story.
    If there are no empty hearthstone slots within the black stele, Triangular Fermentation cannot be used. The hearthstones created are always the same, and are always formed in the following order:
    1. Stone of Innocent’s Protection
    2. Gem of Fair Winds (fixed to a 90 degree facing of the pyramid, decided by the Exalt)
    3. Stone of Hidden Safety (affecting the ship, or a settlement in the immediate vicinity of the ship)
    Using Triangular Fermentation requires touching the black jade pyramid within the ship.


    Tempest-toss’d Immolation Propulsion
    Cost: 5m, 1 wp, 1 hs; Mins: Essence 3, Occult 3, Sail 2
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: None
    Duration: 1 Scene
    Prerequisities: Unfurl the Pyramid
    By invoking arcane energies to consume a random hearthstone within the black pyramid, the Exalt increases the propulsion and maneuverability of Vosetalii to incredible levels, leaving pursuers and assailants to bite sea-fog.
    For a single encounter, Vosetalii gain a speed of 10 that cannot be modified unless the ship is totally immobilized. Vosetalii also gains Maneuverability of +4. The captain of the ship ignores all penalties to Sail rolls from crossing rough terrain, including non-liquid terrains, lava, and underwater movement.
    Using Tempest-toss’d Immolation Propulsion requires touching the black jade pyramid within the ship.


    Arcana Fueled Drive
    Cost: 1 ssm; Mins: Essence 1, Occult 2, Sail 1
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: None
    Duration: 4 hours
    Prerequisities: None
    By using a sorcerous mote created from a shaping ritual, the Exalt may use thaumathurgical energies to power the formidable Essence propulsion drive of the ship, granting +4 speed to Vosetalii. Using Arcana Fueled Drive requires touching the black jade pyramid within the ship.


    Enriched Thaumathurgical Fuel
    Cost: 1 wp, 1 sm; Mins: Essence 3, Craft (water) 3, Occult 3, Resistance 2
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: Perilous
    Duration: 4 hours
    Prerequisities: Arcana Fueled Drive

    By refining sorcerous motes by perilous internal alchemy, the Exalt may use thaumathurgical energies to power the formidable Essence propulsion drive of the ship, granting +4 speed to Vosetalii. Using Enriched Thaumathurgical Fuel requires touching the black jade pyramid within the ship.
    Does not stack with Arcana Fueled Drive, only provides a means to power it without using sorcery motes that are not provided by a shaping sorcery ritual.


    Critical Sorcerous Mass Core Exhaustion
    Cost: 5m; Mins: Essence 4, Sail 3, Occult 3, War 3
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: Pilot
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisities: Arcana Fueled Drive, Enriched Thaumathurgical Fuel

    By dumping the volatile energies confined with the Essence propulsion drive of the ship, a daring captain may utilize it as a formidable – if somewhat suicidal – weapon system.
    This allows the captain of Vosetalii to make a Broadside Naval Maneuver stratagem action. The action made by this charm inflicts a level of hull damage to Vosetalii and, if successful, three levels of hull damage to the target ship. After taking this action, vividly colored vaporized water and Essence forms a psychedelic fog bank that permits a Concealment Naval Maneuver for participants in the naval combat.
    Critical Sorcerous Mass Exhaustion can also be utilized as a siege weapon:
    Accuracy: Close +5, Short +3, Medium 0, Long -4; Damage/Overwhelming: +20/5; Lethal, Flame.
    This attack deals +10 damage (before rolling, both withering and decisive) to targets within Short distance. The Flame quality applies also to underwater targets. Attacking this way also inflicts a level of hull damage to Vosetalii.
    Using this charm deactivates the Essence propulsion drive of Vosetalii. Using Critical Sorcerous Mass Core Exhaustion requires touching the black jade pyramid within the ship.


    Dynamic Vessel Configuration
    Cost: 5m, 1 wp; Mins: Essence 2, Sail 2, Craft (Wood) 2
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: Perilous
    Duration: Permanent
    Prerequisities: None

    The Vosetalii is a remarkably complex vessel, and a skilled captain or crewman can customize the ship to his liking. The captain of the ship can, at the Storytellers discretion, change the shape and dimensions of the vessel in a cosmetic fashion. This includes the depth and width of the ship, the distance between the hulls (or the number of them), the pitch and number of the masts, the shape of the rudder and the figurehead, and so on. The mass and amount of material of the ship however remain as constant. The captain can choose to pick a number of upgrades up to his Essence rating to the ship from the following list:
    • Hydrodynamic Foils (+1 maneuverability)
    • Optimized Sail Overlapping (+1 speed)
    • Rugged Hull Design (+1 -0 Hull level)
    • Assault Figurehead (+1 non-Charm die to Ram actions, and counts as a ram)
    • Chameleon Deck Patterning (+1 non-Charm die to Concealment actions)
    • Dynamic Rigging (+1 non-charm die to Combat Movement actions on the ship)
    Any upgrade can be taken multiple times. Each use of Dynamic Vessel Configuration lasts until the Charm is used again, and using the Charm takes an hour per upgrade chosen, plus an additional hour for any cosmetic upgrades.
    Using Dynamic Vessel Configuration requires touching the black jade pyramid within the ship.


    Expedited Reconstruction Discipline
    Cost: 2m; Mins: Essence 3, Sail 3, Resilience 2
    Type: Supplemental
    Keywords: Perilous
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisities: Dynamic Vessel Configuration

    By disregarding safety of himself and the ship, the crew of the ship may alter the ship within seconds for spectacular effect. This charm supplements Dynamic Vessel Configuration and allows the changes to be done in a single combat round. Expedited Reconstruction Discipline allows for practical uses for manipulating the dimensions and physical details of the ship – the masts can be used to hit or impale massive enemies, the interiors can be reconfigured to imprison (or engulf) people inside, and so on.
    Using this charm is heavily taxing to the ship, or if he wills it, the captain. After using and resolving the effects from using this charm, roll Wits + Resistance, Difficulty 6. If the roll fails, the ship suffers one level of hull damage for each point the roll was failed by. Instead of Vosetalii suffering a level of hull damage, the character who performed the roll may instead choose to suffer a level of aggravated health damage.


    Under-Depth Approach Build
    Cost: 20m, 2 wp, 1 hs; Mins: Essence 4, Sail 4
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: Pilot, Perilous
    Duration: Permanent
    Prerequisities: Dynamic Vessel Configuration, Expedited Reconstruction Discipline, Arcana Fueled Drive, Unfurl The Pyramid

    By sacrificing a hearthstone from the black pyramid, the captain of the Vosetalii can mold the ship into a form that can submerge underwater. Masts bend low, the rudders form into fins, and the construction of the ship tightens so that not even air can escape from inside. The helm of the ship changes into a strange apparatus that allows for underwater navigation. Activating this charm takes six hours of time, after which time the ship turn in effect into a submarine. Expedited Reconstruction Discipline can be used to shorten this time.
    Only the Essence propulsion drive (and possibly oars) can be used to move the ship underwater. All Sail rolls receive a -3 penalty while operating an undersea vessel, but this penalty can be negated by a Sail specialty in underwater operations. The ship cannot run out of air, and as long as the crew and passengers have provisions, they can survive indefinitely within the confines the ship. After Under-Depth Approach Build has been used, the ship can be used as a submarine and a fully functional above-water vessel indefinitely, unless Dynamic Vessel Configuration is used to transform the vessel into a form that cannot operate underwater.


    Beacon Of Heroes
    Cost: 15m, 1 wp; Mins: Essence 1, Sail 2, Presence 3
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: Psyche
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisities: Brotherhood of the Ship

    Vosetalii is, above all, a vessel that heralds great deeds and high adventure. Wherever it appears, people of all creeds and backgrounds abandon their former lives to see the ship, a decision that often leads to a lifetime on the seas and a fate that will echo in eternity.
    After using Beacon of Heroes, people within (Essence x 10) miles of the ship with a positive Intimacy towards pursuing adventuring, voyaging, exploring and other acts of daring and courage will have unconscious knowledge of the location of the ship, and have a premonition that serving aboard It will provide excitement and possibilities for years to come. This charm only affects mortals and other sentient creatures with unawakened Essence.
    Within the coming days, the captain of Vosetalii can expect a crowd of willing crewmen to come join his crew for modest pay, but the Charm merely provides somewhat subtle encouragement towards this. The people Beacon of Heroes summons may not be experienced sailors, come cheap, or in remote corners of Creation, even exist.
    Using Beacon of Heroes requires touching the black jade pyramid within the ship.


    A Wonder To Behold
    Cost: 25m, 2 wp; Mins: Essence 3, Sail 2, Presence 4
    Type: Supplemental
    Keywords: Psyche
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisites: Brotherhood of the Ship, Beacon of Heroes

    This charm enhances Beacon of Heroes in a fashion that it also affects gods, spirits, Exalts and other beings with awakened Essence. This charm affects beings less powerful (Essence – 2) of the captain of Vosetalii, and like Beacon of Heroes, only piques the interest of such beings towards Vosetalii. Any attempts of recruit, influence or to hire to aid of said beings are not made easier (although knowing their Intimacies now might help).
    A Wonder to Behold also poses a certain amount of danger – not everybody or everything summoned by it is peaceful and co-operative in their nature, and powerful beings may try to overtake the ship by hook or by crook.


    A Ship To Propel Paragons For Terrestial Aeons
    Cost: 10m, 1 WP, 3 HS; Mins: Essence 4, Integrity 3, Performance 1
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: Pilot
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisities: Brotherhood Of The Ship, Triangluar Fermentation, Under-Depth Approach Build, A Wonder To Behold

    Having served its purpose, Vosetalii can be let go of, letting it seek new heroes to serve. The captain of the ship performs a farewell service of some kind, and Vosetalii sinks beneath the waves (or, if stranded on the ground, the soil). The previous owners will be forever parted from the ship, but their lives will be forever enhanced by meeting with the legendary ship.
    By using this charm, all attunements to Vosetalii are broken, the ship disappears from view and proceeds to travel discreetly and unhurriedly to a remote location where it it will not be disturbed. Then, after a long amount of time (decades to Ages), it will resurface again, allowing itself to be found by worthy aspiring heroes (as defined by the Storyteller) who have both need and desire for the Splendid Vessel. The Storyteller or the players will not be able to utilize Vosetalii again in the current Story in any manner.
    After using this charm, everyone attuned to Vosetalii before using this charm gains a permanent +1 to Willpower, and all Experience committed to learning the Evocations to Vosetalii, the Splendid Vessel will be returned to their respective characters.


    Thirteen Reasons Why
    Cost: 10m, 1 WP, 3 HS; Mins: Essence 4, Charisma 4, Integrity 3, Occult 3
    Type: Simple
    Keywords: Pilot
    Duration: Instant
    Prerequisities: Brotherhood Of The Ship, Triangluar Fermentation, Critical Sorcerous Mass Core Exhaustion, A Wonder To Behold

    The Third age of Creation is truly a time of tumult, in which new things are born, and old ones die. By mastering the ship and the existential truth of the void at heart of all things, an Exalt can learn convince Vosetalii, the Splendid Vessel, to explode in a singularity of cataclysmic arcane power.
    By presenting the ship with adaquate reasons why to end it’s existance, after 13 combat rounds have passed, Vosetalii explodes. This blast does:
    30 dice of aggravated damage to every character, structure and vehicle in Short range.
    20 dice of aggravated damage to every character, structure and vehicle in Medium range.
    15 dice of lethal damage to every character, structure and vehicle in Long range.
    10 dice of bashing damage to every character, structure and vehicle in Extreme range.
    By using this charm, all attunements to Vosetalii are broken, and after the 13 round countdown the Splendid Vessel is permanently and irrevocably destroyed. The Storyteller or the players will not be able to utilize Vosetalii again in the current Chronicle in any manner.
    After using this charm, everyone attuned to Vosetalii before using this charm gains a permanent +1 to Willpower, and all Experience committed to learning the Evocations to Vosetalii, the Splendid Vessel will be returned to their respective characters.
    Resonant: The Exalt may decide to detonate the Splendid Vessel at any time during the 13 round countdown.
    Dissonant: At the beginning of every round, roll 5 dice. For each success, remove one round from the countdown to the detonation. 10’s count as two successes.

    Follow your passions, be prepeared to work hard and sacrifice, and, above all, don't let anyone limit your dreams.

          I'm somewhat new to 3E, but I've designed a bunch of artifacts in previous editions. I like the flavor of the artifact, though even for 3E it seems incredibly specific.

          I'd definitely be concerned about Unfurl the Pyramid, though. Dependent hearthstones and linked hearthstones are kind of a big deal. The really powerful ones are supposed to require other stuff to work. Not only does this bypass that, it allows for multiple characters to benefit from said powerful hearthstones. Honestly, the effect itself makes me think of a five dot Artifact, since you're changing how the world works in a pretty serious way. And it should probably be a capstone, at that. Is it really your intention to let someone put a Gem of Incomparable Wellness in here, and pass the benefits around to the whole crew with awakened essence?
