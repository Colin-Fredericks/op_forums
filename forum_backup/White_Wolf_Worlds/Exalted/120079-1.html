            Originally posted by Epimetheus View Post
            So I'm reading the original games of divinity and this is a passage I've read in several places.

            "They made the sidereals, who were wise and mighty seers and weaver of spells but few in number and weaker in battle than the solar and lunar exalted"

            So my question is when did weaver of spells change to martial artists? I mean the implication is that they were more traditional sorcerers compared to other exalts but none the less they were less about fighting and more about mystical might. When did that change and why use Martial arts instead of sorcery?
            The problem comes from the fact that when Exalted 1st Edition was first being written up, it was directly linked to what is now oWoD. In fact the original promotional material for it used the concept that the prehistory of the world as we know it is a lie.

            Each Exalt type was supposed to be the super powered ancestor of each of the core splats of the world of darkness setting. In fact if you look closer at the old books, you can see hold over mechanics from each splat.

            Abyssal = Cainite Vampire
            Dragon-Blooded = Kuei-Jin Vampire
            Lunar = Werewolf
            Solar = Hunter

            And last but not least was the Sidereals as the Mage analog. Why Martial Arts if they were supposed to be the grand magic users of the Exalted setting you ask, because back then the concept of "Do" the magic based martial art of the Akashic Brotherhood was still a central concept.

            So yeah, that's the entire reason Sidereals are the way they are, aside from constantly being written up in such a way that they only make sense as NPCs.

                I think that -- while Astrology should be cool and useful and a viable place to devote your character -- overall, complaining that your Sidereal has to be a wise martial arts sifu in order to advance gets into "stupid dog, why aren't you a cat" territory. If you play a Lunar or a Solar -- I mean, at E2, you do something, and at E5, you do the same thing, but much bigger or grander or more overwhelmingly. That's not meant to be how Sidereals work, I think, and that distinction was a deliberate choice.)
