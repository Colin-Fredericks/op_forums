    2.5 Errata as comments

    02-21-2014, 09:32 AM
    Old post - outdated links. New ones in a new post, once it gets Approved (SO MANY LINKS!)

    Had a friend asking me for these recently, and I realised that thanks to the death of the old forums, and the archive not having everything available, the only up to date version of this was buried several pages deep into an RPG.net page. And since people are still playing 2.5 games, I figure it's still useful to have this available. I've also included at the end the cross-reference comments I made for the core book, which give you the stat prereqs, and a quick (rules-free) description of the effects of every Solar charm that's not in the core book, which book they are in, and show how they extend the core charm trees.

    These were all made using Foxit PDF reader, and thus that's what I have the most experience in doing stuff with them in. In that, go to the comments menu/tab, and look for 'Import comments' or 'Import comments data' (wording appears to be different in different versions). Import the comments into the file, then save the file. Done.

    If you're not using Foxit Reader, then the general approach that seems to work is opening the .fdf file itself, which will then ask what PDF you want to apply it to. Pick the right one, save, done. Alternative, it should also work if you download foxit just for applying the comments, save them all, then open the files in whatever your usual PDF reader is.

                                      Have added the extra errata recently released for Infernals and the Broken Winged Crane to the list; it's a seperate file and slightly more rushed — since I was doing them on a 'clean' PDF, checking they didn't hit the Scroll of Errata comments by eye, they're not quite so nicely lined up.

                                      This errata is done in green stars, so it's distinguishable from the other stuff. I've added the errata to existing charms next to those charms, as I did with the original errata-as-comments, the new charms (that aren't "Replace this charm, even its name) I've added to the charm trees, with arrows distinguishing where they come from (and a dotted arrow to indicate the excellency requirement on one of them).

                                      If there's a particular demand for it, or people's PDF readers are having trouble applying two errata comments files, then I can make a version that combines this with the original errata comments for Infernals. Otherwise, it just seems easier for people to apply both themselves.
