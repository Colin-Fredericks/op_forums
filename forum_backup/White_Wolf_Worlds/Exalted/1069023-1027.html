    Originally posted by Sith_Happens View Post
    How exactly does Steel Storm Descending work? All it says is "To use this Charm, the Solar must win Join Battle and make a decisive attack." Does that decisive attack have to be the first action you take after Joining Battle? Does it have to be on your first turn? How does it interact with Swarm-Culling Instinct?
    It doesn't give you a free attack—it applies to the first attack you make that first round. If you've got Swarm-Culling Instinct, that's the first decisive attack you make with it. If not, you need to use your turn.

    Does Joining Battle mid-combat (via Charm, initiative shift, etc.) count as winning Join Battle (since no one else rolled)?
    No.

    Is Cascade of Cutting Terror meant to create an attack or enhance one?
    I believe it enhances one, although I see why you ask.
