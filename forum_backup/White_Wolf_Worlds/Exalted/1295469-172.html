                Originally posted by NevenkaNieroda View Post
                I actually had a question about Exigents, but it's one that may have a spoilerrific answer that can't be given. I may also be asking it the wrong way, so bear with me:

                We've seen examples of Architects in Revana Quinn, and a little further up the thread there seemed to be an implication that while "Strawmaiden" wasn't an Exalt type per se, it could be extrapolated as one with only a bit of difficulty. We've also heard of the Sovereigns of [REDACTED] now, and then there's a million little pieces of information all over the place about Exigents in general. But the core book mentions corrupted exaltations, which as I understand is being touched on in the Sovereigns of [REDACTED].

                My questions are thus: Are those the only corrupted exaltations? Or could conceivably any Exigent exaltation get weirdly altered/corrupted/polluted/warped?
                Any Exigent empowered by more than one god is potentially subject to such instability.

                Originally posted by NevenkaNieroda View Post
                Also, is it the exaltation itself which gets corrupted? The god giving it at the moment of exaltation? Or is it the exigence (exigency?),
                It's basically a matter of dissonance between the natures of the various gods who've contributed their Essence to the Exigence. It doesn't affect the gods in question, just the Exigent thus empowered.

                Originally posted by NevenkaNieroda View Post
                the "catalyst" as some have been referring to it, like you have a perfectly pure and noble god with the best of intentions but the only catalyst she can get a hold of has been passed around the celestial black market for gods-know-how long, so when she interacts with it it alters the gift of exaltation in new and horrifying ways?
                Because it's a matter of dissonance rather than a matter of corruption vs. purity, an Exigent empowered by two evil and corrupt gods with very similar natures won't be as twisted as an Exigent empowered by two well-intentioned gods with wildly divergent natures.

                                Originally posted by ReshyShira View Post
                                What's the protocol for a mortal dynast who draws their second breath as a solar or lunar, especially one that's esteemed or reputable?
                                Having one's kinfolk become Anathema is scandalous, but not nearly as much as being caught trying to cover it up. So the Dynast's household and Great House will probably try to kill them quietly, rather than risk having the Wyld Hunt spread the new Anathema's name far and wide. (And in many cases, it might feel kinder, more honorable, or more pious to do it oneself, rather than leaving it to uncaring strangers or risking the deaths of bystanders.) If that fails, the next best thing is to publicly disclaim the new Anathema and make a big show of leading the hunt against them, for similar reasons.
