                        Originally posted by BrilliantRain View Post
                        If a Solar wants to use Melee to cut a large piece of scenery in half, is there a specific charm or combination of charms you would recommend for that feat? Would inventing or adapting a charm from another ability be a better idea? Or would just a stunt be ok?
                        Depends on your scenery and what you're trying to do. If it's narratively irrelevant and you're just doing it because it's badass and looks cool, a stunt is fine. If you're trying to cut the mast of an enemy ship from shore with Sandstorm-Wind Attack, or to fell a giant tree so that it crushes a bunch of your enemies, that's probably better handled as a gambit, which could be enhanced with custom Charms.

                                        Originally posted by WalkingEye View Post
                                        So lets say a Solar is alone against many Dragonblood enemies. He is trying to pick them off one at a time without notice. He activates Blinding Battle Feint. His opponents roll Perception+awareness as a response to his charm and the Solar Succeeds over all of them. Do they begin regenerating motes at in combat speed or does that not happen until they begin responding actively to the solars actions?
                                        Once Join Battle is rolled, you're in combat.

                                        If I use Order Affirming Blow on a Lunar with wyld taint are they cured and reset to their natural state(with tattoos of course) or do their tattoos prevent the order Affirming Blow from working or is it their choice if it works or not?
                                        Wyld taint doesn't exist in 3rd Edition. Assuming for the sake of argument that you've got a Lunar with both Wyld-induced physical mutations and Derangements, the tattoos definitely wouldn't block healing mental stuff. What about the physical stuff? We haven't fully pinned down how the tattoos work in Ex3, but I don't think it makes sense for them to block healing and other beneficial effects.

                                        If I use Order Affirming Blow on a Sorcerer who has given themselves a magical enhancement with celestial level sorcery would I be able to remove such affects with Order Affirming Blow or Does Order Affirming Blow only rid the target of Things that they perceive as negative magic?
                                        Sorcerous workings aren't generally the kind of twisting or shaping magic that Order-Affirming Blow undoes, although a particular working that was shaping-based could be undone with it.

                                        [quote[Can the User of Order Affirming Blow Pick and choose what to remove (lets say with a stunt) or is it all or nothing?[/quote]
                                        It hits all of them.

                                        As a storyteller are any of these following powers something you would not allow?

                                        A Lunar has used celestial sorcery to give herself a sixth sense that informs her if eyes are looking upon her. She does not learn the number of eyes looking upon her or its location.

                                        A area Dragon blooded Sorcerer has used terrestrial sorcery to enchant 4 stone totems. When he puts up these stone totems while doing a ritual that takes about 10 minutes he can set up a perimeter that stretches to short range from his central camp. If anyone larger than a rock he places at the center of the totems walks upon the ground in that area he knows. He does not know the location or nature of the intrusion just that it is happening.
                                        They seem largely fine. The first one is a bit lacking in sorcerous flavor, and I think the second might be better done as a spell, but those aren't really damning flaws.
