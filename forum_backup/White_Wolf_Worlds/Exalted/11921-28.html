                        Back on the Dragon Age thing...

                        I thought about running a game in a tweaked Creation. The reason the Solars fell from power is they sought to enter Heaven and usurp control of it from the Gods. For their hubris, they were cast out of Heaven. The stories the Immaculate Faith tell say that the Solars returned from Heaven as twisted, wretched beings who terrorized the world until they were imprisoned deep within the Earth. The Scarlet Empire is, essentially, Orlais; the Scarlet Empress a historical figure who lived long, long ago and is revered as the Savior of Humanity as it was she who led the united armies of Creation against the twisted Solars and their servants. The reason the Empire is in peril now is, instead, because the Divine (who is the Empress' earthly representative) was recently murdered openly and in full view of powerful and influential members of her court.

                        Sidereal Exalted are still Agents of Fate, but they do not live or work in Heaven. Instead they do their work like the Maidens did in pre-history: remotely from Creation.
