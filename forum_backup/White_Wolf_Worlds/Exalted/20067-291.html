                                    Originally posted by Kingdom of One View Post
                                    1) With Thunderbolt Attack Prana, how far do you HAVE to move on a ground attack, and how far CAN you move? "Another range band" sounds like there's no limit so long as you're aware there's an opponent out there, but that seems extraordinary given Leaping Tiger Attack only goes to medium range.

                                    2) Does Terrifying Battle Shriek force a disengage attempt, if the target has no other way to move away from you? Our Tiger Stylist is very excited.

                                    3) Can you clash, magically or not, an unexpected attack and/or an ambush? It's not like those things disable Charms, so it SEEMS LIKE a magical clash should work, but I'm not sure and it's a lot fuzzier when it comes to unexpected attacks versus mundane timing.
                                    1)This thread contains some recent discussion on Thunderbolt Attack Prana. To summarize: Some people allow it to target enemies at medium or further, but in doing so it may step on the toes of several other athletics charms. Reportedly, the backer pdf clearly indicated it could be used on ground targets at short range, no closer, no further. "Another" may in this case be being used to mean "one more" as in the phrase "walk another block and then turn left".

                                    2)Yes, if the target is in close range to an enemy, he must use a disengage action to move away if hiding isn't an option.

                                    3) As BadassOverlord notes, the text of Omniscient Focus Attack indicates that you normally can't clash an attack you are unaware of. Since a clash attack is defined as two characters attacking each other on the same tick, and you can't normally target a character you're not aware of, this is presumably what prevents you from making clash attacks against ambush and surprise attacks.
