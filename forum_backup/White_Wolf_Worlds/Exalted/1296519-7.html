                                            Originally posted by Isator Levi View Post

                                            Thulio acts like Heaven ran interference in a manner that was unjust.
                                            I would care more if Thulio wasn't such a garbage character.

                                            This is a long ass post that I've had issues with for awhile, but basically Rakan Thulio is just a fucking worthless character and his existence in 3E makes the entire game less interesting and less good.

                                            I have never had a game that Rakan Thulio even tangentially appeared in that did not immediately implode, because Rakan Thulio is basically poison to any sort of game he's in due to how much ridiculous bullshit he is, all stacked on top of each other in this giant jenga pile of the worst aspects of 2E Deathlords, 2E Yozi, and 2E Sidereal Elders. Because the second a Getimien shows up, Rakan Thulio has to show up, and the second that Rakan Thulio shows up, the game has to be about him from then on.

                                            Rakan Thulio is toxic to the entire system of Exalted because of the way he works and who he is. He is a single person who we are repeatedly told is a big deal, and whenever there is a chance, they tell us how cool he is and what he's done. Rakan Thulio has an entire splat of Exalts at his beck and call, apparently almost perfectly to the point that one of the Getimen's key plot points is 'getting away from Rakan Thulio'. This is 2E Deathlords and non-fanon Yozi writ-large; a massively powerful being who can do whatever he wants to you and you are brainwashed into listening to him. It means any Getimien has to bring along Rakan Thulio no matter what, because he is their story. And when Rakan Thulio is in a game, he's the big bad guy now, because he's so smart, so intelligent, and so powerful that everyone else is basically just his toady. Even things that should, in theory, be cosmically powerful threats on their own end up feeling like lackys to this guy. He makes everyone else less important, because it turns out he does so much stuff or is implied to do so much stuff that is basically against the 'rules', and the implication is that this makes him cool.

                                            Rakan Thulio is not just a Sidereal, because that would be too 'boring' and not enough of a threat. He's a Sidereal that every major faction hates, but somehow can't deal with. He's an Original Sidereal Primordial War Survivor Sidereal Martial Artist Immortal, a great Hero who Everyone Loved who was visited by Saturn Herself and then who avoided Death through secret Martial Arts Techniques and became the World's Greatest Villain. And the game goes out of it's way to make sure it talks about him and how cool he is whenever it's possible. Every time the Getimiens come up, it's almost always, even just purely on word count, more about Rakan Thulio then the actual Exalt Type. It's like if the Lunars book was more about Luna and Gaia then the Lunars themselves, or an Abyssals book where it focused primarily on the Deathlords (oh wait...). Rakan Thulio gets a page spread in the Core Book. When Lunars talks about the strange Exalts that hate heaven as much as them, it turns out they don't. They instead reiterate about Rakan Thulio, who is so hated because he was an architect of the Usurpation. A third of the 100 word segment about the Getimiens is about Rakan Thulio. He is the worst part of Deathlords and Sidereals, because he feels like a guy whose sheet says 'I know every charm and every martial art, and I know where you are at all times'. What a boring thing to have in a game like this, which has been great so far in presenting that as just not being the case anymore; from Lunar Elders who aren't pathetically moping and doing nothing, to a Realm that is at once both terrifying and wracked with infighting and strife, but that if you aren't careful will team up to rip you to shreds before returning to fighting themselves. And then there's this guy, who feels like a relic of a time when the gameline was written to be about cosmically powered Beyond Exalts and Solars shooting up to Essence 7 to fist fight them while slaying thousands of Dragonblooded in a single sword swing and bouncing the Deathlords off the Elemental Poles to send them hurtling into Malfeas where they stay unable to move for 5 days. Rakan Thulio breaks the rules, but not just a rule in a dramatic way, he breaks all the rules, all together, in this giant pile of mush that stops being interesting because it stops coming across that Rakan Thulio is breaking rules in impressive ways, and more that just the rules don't apply to him and he can do whatever the fuck he wants; he's not an interesting NPC, he's the GM's pet whose the real protagonist, you're just there to watch him succeed, or when you are allowed to beat him there's a dramatic scene where you're supposed to feel very sad and he probably let you win anyway.

                                            The Getimians are a cool idea for a Splat, and people are clearly excited about them, and even I'm kinda excited about them and what they might be in how they are, but the idea that this entire splat type can't be used without this other guy is something that people yelled at in 2E. It's something that led to an entire Fanon that eventually swung back into Canon involving Infernals, where everyone would look at the stuff that told them how much control the Yozis had over them, then just go 'ROCKSTARS FROM HELL, ROCKSTARS FROM HELL, ROCKSTARS FROM HELL' and how 'well really, a Yozi has no power over you and they're basically just mentally disabled people with cosmic power. You can lie to them and say you'll help them then just never go to Malfeas, how can they stop you?' (it was important that you could lie to them, because in comparison, Abyssals could NEVER lie to the Deathlords, they always knew if you were faking), and eventually even the idea that they exalted from failures turned into 'well they could exalt just from not succeeding well enough, the failure isn't very important'. At the end, there were two competing feelings; of Infernals who could casually ignore the Yozi, or Infernals who, when they showed up, immediately demanded that the game turn into a game about The Yozi. And rather then acknowledge this as an issue when you have a splat so utterly defined by a patron like that, the decision was 'what if we just did that again, but this time made it an Exalt?'. And now it feels like details are so scarce on Getimians it's almost impossible to use them very effectively. In comparison, there's been so much information about Rakan Thulio that you can basically put him wherever. The Getimians don't feel like Exalts to me anymore, they feel like Rakan Thulio's Ork Lackies you're expected to cleave through on your way to him. And that's sad, because so many people are excited for them, and I want to like them, but I also have no desire to engage with Thulio.

                                            But it's not even entirely about Rakan Thulio and his presence in the game either, but also about the developers that put him there. Those people who railed against Neph for turning Infernals into an advertisement for his 'new game', who promised they were bringing big changes and were going to change Exalted, who were going to save the gameline, who really 'got it', because they played the game a lot and were just like us, who had swagger and confidence and everything. And then we watched them delay, and delay, and delay. And rant, and rave, and call things unfair, and insult and deride, and mock people. And then they release the first bit of Exalted media for 3E, before even the core, and we're introduced to their own spin on the Deathlords and the Yozi and the Elder Sidereals. He's what we see, and we're informed of just how important he is, through blog posts and forum posts and this book. And his important is further demanded; from the Style specifically made by his ex-lover, from the fact said ex-lover invented Evocations, from his page long spread in the core, from not being able to talk about Getimiens without Rakan Thulio, from a huge stretch goal being about another person embroiled in this cosmic love triangle in Sublime Danger, and HER unique Martial Arts Style Specifically For Her. All this stuff of dubious canonicity now, but it would be actively disingenuous to imply that because these are only 'secondarily-canon' that they do not change the perception of such things; the Scarlet Empress wasn't the Bride of the Ebon Dragon, until she was. These things do matter, regardless of the salve of 'it's not REALLY canon' put on it, and they change how people perceive things, especially when we were hungry for any kind of Exalted information at all. For all that they claimed that they were going to be different, in the end, all it meant was that they took all the overpowered NPCs and introduced an even bigger fish with their own stamp of 'this guy is the coolest and he is here to be cool, so make sure he's cool'. The same guys who now or used to spend time looking over previews of books written by our current developers and go 'oh yeah, I had a way better version of that written out, this version is trash, actually, but you'll never see it now!'

                                            Rakan Thulio's story might be appropriately 'epic' within the context of old legends. He might not be out of the ordinary of being a person spurned by love who is out for revenge against those who did it. He may even be right. I could tell you tons about Rakan Thulio and what he's done and his motivations, more then almost any other NPC (but not a thing about this other group of Exalts other then the one guy written up once in the Sidereal pre-booklet)

                                            But we live in a world where someone spouting those exact same beliefs sometimes picks up a gun and murders a dozen people, screaming about how 'The Government' should give them a girlfriend-slave who cooks for them, cleans for them, and fucks them whenever they want, because it's their rights. And you give me this asshole and you expect me to respect him, to like him, at all? You expect me to want him in the game, to engage with him legitimately? All the while the gameline goes out of it's way to yell 'look at how cool he is! Look at how cool! He's so cool!' about him and desperately present him as something that is just really going to bring the gameline together.

                                            No. I look at Rakan Thulio and I see the stuff that annoyed me about 2E Exalted- that annoyed so many people-, and I see the handiwork of a couple of guys who were never as good, as big, or as talented as they thought they were, and I see someone spouting beliefs that have murdered people in real life, and I see him being presented as something so cool that I should want to engage with him- even with him as a villain- and be happy when I am allowed to win against him.

                                            I will never ever appreciate that. That's why I get hung up with people defend Rakan Thulio, when they go 'His story is just based on Ancient Epics!', and all that other stuff. Because that's not the whole story, it's not the whole reason people dislike him, and it's just a very shallow observation on why a character might be disliked.

                                                            Originally posted by Epimetheus View Post

                                                            See, I know that we're both imposing meanings on the text, but I'm imposing a meaning that actually accounts for the intended purpose of the character and their place in the setting that references long-standing and well known details about what the Sidereal Exalted actually do (many of which, even beyond that one Socialize Charm, have been a basis for complaints about the extent of their power and nature of their agendas for years*), and trying to imagine ways that they all fit together, with maybe a hint of suspension of disbelief to let the insignificant side inconsistencies slide if they're present, and you're imposing the meaning that would make the character terrible and the intent behind it dumb and wrong.
