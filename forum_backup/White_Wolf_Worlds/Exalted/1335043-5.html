                SWIFT LANGUAGE ACQUISITION ACTION
                Linguistics 2, Essence 2
                Cost: 5m
                Minimums: Linguistics excellency
                Duration: 1 month

                The Solar's reach far and wide, and the their domains encompass vast countries. It is, of course, imperative that she learns their language, better for them to share in her glory and wisdom. This encompasses a dramatic action where the solar reads a book, travels alongside those who know the language, or simply meditates on the people who he wishes to communicate with. At the end, he learns the language, speaking in whatever native accent that is local. After one month, the memory of the langauge fades, and he can no longer speak it.*

                *I don't care if it is better than the canon charm. No way is a solar going to be worse than a dragonblooded at rapidly acquiring new langauges.

                Conveying the Ideal word.
                Linguistics 4, Essence 3
                Cost: Permanent
                Prereqs: Poetic expression style

                All langauges have a root. All languages have a similarity. All langugages, from West to South to East to North, each have a similarity due to them all deviating from the root language. The platonic form of all languages, the ideal word. The solar knows this word, and uses it to convey information he needs. Anything he writes or speaks, is instantly understood by anyone who can perceive it, as if it was in their own native language. This ideal word can be copied by scribes and replayed by audio recorders, but in itself it cannot be used by mortals, the intricacies and subtleties of the language too vast for their minds to comprehend.

                                                    Absurd Iron Body Prana
                                                    Essence: 2
                                                    Resistance: 3

                                                    It is not enough that those who attack the lawgivers fail in their affront. They must also be disciplined. After all, those who come too close to the sun burn up. This supplements a resistance roll. When the character is struck by a melee attack and successfully blocks all damage, he may use this charm reflexively to destroy the weapon of the attacker, rolling resistance against the targets essence, with (essence) autosuccesses. The weapon is rendered into useless slag, or if it was a natural weapon, burned into uselessness as a temporary crippling effect.

                                                    At essence 3, a repurchase allows him detonate the weapon with a deadly conflagration, molten shards leaping for the vital organs in a manner not unlike a grenade. At essence 4, this applies to ranged weapons as well.

                                                    Artifact weapons are immune to this effect.
