                                                Originally posted by JohnDoe244 View Post
                                                I think she's saying:
                                                That's partially it, but also-

                                                The Storyteller controls every aspect of the game world including what the players see and to this end they supply what I call "passive competence" where they supply enough information for the players actions to not be frustrating guessing games.

                                                To me the potential "buzzkill" of a James Bond expy hitting on a lesbian and getting turned down isn't infringing on an intrinsic right to fuck every attractive woman but that the Storyteller doesn't attribute them much passive competence in this area, so he has no indication of her sexual orientation until she invokes it to shut him down like he's a pickup artist who hangs around bars in James Bond cosplay. If he's really a master of seduction he should probably notice the lack of interest from her really early on and realise he needs a different strategy rather than bypassing her sexual orientation.

                                                Lastly, if the hypothetical Storyteller doesn't care about the character’s shtick or the possible buzzkill from being turned down, then using Rose-Lipped Seduction Style to brute force the issue is taking a gamble that they won't go onto portray the character as a rapist.
