            Originally posted by Uknown DarkLord View Post
            2E core says that Ghost-Blooded have the touch of death. Does that make them "creatures of death" and thus "creatures of darkness"?
            Ghost-Blooded were explicitly Creatures of Death (MoEP: Abyssals p 123) so certain Abyssal Charms would have special effects on them. However, Creatures of Death were not necessarily Creatures of Darkness.

            Ghost Blooded were not among the default list of Creatures of Darkness, which included, but was not limited to: Yozis, demons, Neverborn, walking dead, ghosts, Deathlords, unshaped Fair Folk, Abyssal Exalted, and Infernal Exalted.

            So the answer is: Yes, and no (unless maybe).

                                Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz View Post

                                So I was wondering if 2nd ed had a different spell I could port. But I think all the terrestrial spells have restrictions that make it unviable. Never mind.
                                Spoke the Wooden Face lets you communicate with anyone in Creation. So long as you have carved your face into the tree and conducted a ritual beforehand. So the restriction is that they have to be at one of your trees.

                                Unity of Dreams allows you to communicate with others in dreams. But they have to be with you first at the initial casting. And then you can only dream communicate for the next essence months. And it is also a Celestial spell.

                                There is the Labyrinth circle spell Dead Man's Voice. Make an artifact that can imitate this spell, and then bam, you possess some dupe who is near the PC's and communicate with them.

                                                Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz View Post
                                                Do any of the Lunar form-gaining charms require you to immediately take that form upon gaining it?
                                                Technically there is the universal Charm Life of the Hummingbird, although you only gain the form temporarily.

                                                None of the permanent Charms require you to take the form upon gaining it, although it might be appropriate to do so when a target breaks the Blood-Binding Geas.

                                                Edit : If you're looking for a Sacred Hunt that does that, here's an idea I had :

                                                Doppleganger's Wager
                                                Cost: —; Mins: Manipulation 3, Essence 1
                                                Type: Permanent
                                                Keywords: None
                                                Duration: Permanent
                                                Prerequisite Charms: None
                                                You may challenge a human to a wager, claiming you could replace them and nobody would be the wiser. If they agree, you immediately take on their form for a week, during which you are to replace the target in their daily activities. The target must have social interactions with people who know them well in their weekly activities to be an applicable target.

                                                If anyone starts suspecting that you are not who you pretend to be (or alternately, that something is wrong with the target), you immediately lose access to the target's shape, revealing you as an impostor. Should you make it through the week, you gain the target's shape permanently.

                                                You may use trickery to make your deception more convincing, however you cannot win the wager by lying to the target that you were successful. Similarly, the target may not sabotage your efforts - if they actively seek to hinder you, you immediately win the wager. If the target is discovered despite their best efforts to remain hidden, you are alerted by a spike of fear and you have until the end of the Scene before you lose access to the shape. If this Hunt fails for any reason, you cannot try it again for the rest of the Story.

                                                If you know Life of the Hummingbird, you may undertake this hunt after killing a human you did not have a sacred hunt towards by drinking their Heart's Blood. In this case, you must convince everyone else that the target is still alive and well by replacing them for a week.
