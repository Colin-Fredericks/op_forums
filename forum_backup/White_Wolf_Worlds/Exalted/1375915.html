    What's the biggest change you've done to Creation in-game?

    03-21-2020, 06:15 AM
    In a previous game, the UCS and Luna vanished, and Autocthon rules Heaven (and now the new ST has had the Maidens vanish and be replaced as well). In addition, Lookshy was destroyed, the Neverborn were put properly to sleep and so the Deathlords are no longer really interacting with Creation much, and it became much harder to travel between Yu-Shan and Creation.

    In my current game, the Solars killed two Elemental Dragons (the Lunar PCs stopped them killing a third), and I just did a 100-year timeskip, during which an Ice Age came and went.


    So it made me curious what others have done. I'm not talking about shards, I'm talking about huge changes to the world that happened during the game.

          In 2E: We killed the Unconquered Sun and Ligier, freed the Ebon Dragon, destroyed the Elemental Pole of Earth and replaced it with a Black Spiral to Malfeas, made Mnemon the new Elemental Dragon of Earth, stole Lytek's cabinet and Lillun, imprisoned the Ebon Dragon in Yu-Shen (alone, the Games of Divinity destroyed, the sky locked in perpetual sunlight, with his only consolation being the nails-on-chalkboard ringing of Sol's deathscream blasting out for all eternity), perverted all the Solar Exaltations to Infernal ones, redeemed (almost) all the Abyssals into new Solars, brought forth my character's soul-world to serve as the new heaven, brokered peace with Autochothonia (where the redeemed Solars converted him into the Gunstar and built a semi-perfect dictatorship of the proletariat), slew a Deathlord, saved the Neverborn from the Void by getting another Deathlord to voluntarily enter Lethe (opening the gates of reincarnation to the slain Primordials as a totally unanticipated side effect), then sent forth each of the new Infernals to the Wyld to forge their own Creations and ascend into a new race of Titans.

          One of the other PCs forged his own 3CD as a flaming green sun. Out in the Wyld, he found a group of Primordial beings ruled over by an impossibly vast mountain. He conquered them and became their new king. They forged a Creation so they could play the Games of Divinity, were astonished when five Celestines governing fate spontaneously popped into existence... well, you get the idea.

          Then things got weird.

          We followed Gaia to the Faraway to learn how to be Primordials, created a rift in the multi-verse and bounced through different dimensions learning that basically every dimension has a "version" of Gaia.

          It ended with Darkseid and Superman wielding He-Man's Power Sword and Mjölnir (respectively) against us in a three way battle for the multi-verse (between us, The Superman Coalition and our future selves (who were our Scion characters)). It was never adequately resolved but after I gave Superman the Investiture of Infernal Glory and the last Deathknight revealed that he had been Darkseid all along it kinda peetered out (I pressume we lost, leading to our reincarnation in Scion).

          We never really did learn about the true nature of Gaia. (But it was heavily implied that the 40k version of Gaia was The Laughing God, not Isha, so take from that what you will.)

          3E: Not much.

              So far in the game I run, my players are a Twilight and No Moon. They are huge nerds who perform sorcerous workings and create artifacts. Working together, they have:
              • Helped a megalomaniacal Solar sorceress destroy Sijan with an army of demons.
              • Murdered that sorceress, creating a new Deathlord in the East.
              • Assisted Lookshy in the creation of brand new Gunzosha suits, supplying the city with some of its first brand-new First Age Artifice in centuries.
              • Repaired one of Lookshy's dilapidated Warstriders, the Cumulonimbus, and restored it to full operational functionality.
              • Forged a perfect crystal of Adamant for the first time in over one thousand years.
              • Invented an alchemical ritual to transform Vitriol back into Theion-To, and then created the first Theion-To the cosmos has seen since the defeat of the Primordials.
              • Combined the aforementioned Adamant and Theion-To to create a N/A Artifact called the Maker's Flame, which is an impossible halo-crown containing the Word of Making (spoken at the birth of Creation) which grants its wearer the Demiurgic powers that the Primordials wielded in the Age of Glories.
              • Built Lookshy a brand-new airship out of huge amounts of brass and demons called the Nebula Caedem.
              • Ripped a beautiful city out of the earth in the North.
              • Murdered Gervesin in a pitched battle.

              As of right now they're only Essence 3, and I imagine soon they'll get on to bigger things like freeing the Primordials or resurrecting Ruvelia or something of that nature. I'm quite excited.

                                                  I've been placing every 3rd edition game I've storytold since release in the same setting and timeline, so it's progressed from RY 768 to RY 779 now. The civil war is over. V'neef is empress. The Realm is fragmented with both Roseblack and one of Cainan's daughters each controlling their own Threshold shogunates, Ragara Banoba having made a pact with Prasad, and Peleps Lai recently tried to break off the Realm as well by launching a failed assassination attempt on Sesus Raenyah before being killed by one of her own admirals who did so to spare the house from causing another civil war. All of the Realm politics have caused ripples all across Creation, not only weakening the central Realm, but emboldening other Dragon-Blooded nations and creating new ones. Since I use the same setting, it's a bit weird to introduce new players unfamiliar with Creation to it - which I've done a few times - since I'm introducing different Creation politics than the canon status quo to them which may confuse them if they actually start reading the books and play with other groups. But alas ...
