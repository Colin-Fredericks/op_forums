                    Ok then. The last path for Flesh is the path of Absorbtion. 19 charms so far, could use a few more.


                    Path of Absorption                     (powers dealing with absorbing body parts and features from other creatures.
                    Essence 1
                    * body graft - basic ability to repair the liminal’s body with other people’s body parts. Takes a minimum of medical skill, but is rather time-consuming.
                    * carrion birds waiting - quicken medical diagnosis to a miscellaneous action.
                    * graft the warrior’s arm - gain ranks in a physical skill (melee, brawl, dodge, archery, thrown, athletics, etc) until the end of adventure, by grafting a body part from a skilled person.
                    Essence 2
                    * graft the dancer’s feet - gain ranks in fine motor or specialised skill ( larceny, craft, perform, sail, survival, etc) until the end of adventure, by grafting a body part from a skilled person.
                    * snake sheds skin - absorb the face of a dead body to create a disguise.
                    * patchwork body reconstruction - (in book) heals himself on a killing blow.
                    * wisdom of carrion - skills obtained by graft powers can be persisted by investing essence.
                    * commanding the ways of the flesh - improve medicine effect on another creature.
                    * tendon-weaving spider - when taking another’s body parts, reduce the operation to one scene. At extra mote cost, remove the need for surgical tools.
                    Essence 3
                    * graft the sage’s tongue - gain intellectual or social skill ( socialise, bureaucracy, lore, occult, investigation, etc) until the end of adventure, by grafting a body part from a skilled person.
                    * the sum of humanity - skills persisted with wisdom of carrion can be bought permanently at XP discount.
                    * fluid flesh prana - improves on tendon-weaving spider; the procedure now takes an action.
                    * twin hearts beating - melds into another person, sharing injuries to keep both alive.
                    * flowing flesh patching - trade health levels for healing an ally.
                    * colossus of flesh - absorbs nearby dead bodies, forming a grotesque giant around the liminal - it rots away at the end of the scene unless extended with will points.
                    Essence 4
                    * cauldron of human flesh - absorb pieces of limbs and organs into a fleshy hump as a reserve for instant repairs, gaining temporary extra health levels that can pay for self-damaging charms, or instantly heal crippling injuries accepted to mitigate damage.
                    * nightmarish chirurgeon - can apply flesh grafts to other people - they don’t get any abilities, but can be quickly healed from life-threatening or debilitating injuries. Non-exalts will be disfigured to various extent.
                    Essence 5
                    * bargain of the grave - nightmarish chirurgeon can commit a mote to the graft, allowing it to be destroyed at will, leaving the patient a hostage to the exalt.
                    * Dark Mother’s mandate - improves colossus of flesh, allowing it to drag in corpses from a larger area and grow to legendary size. With sufficient bodies, it gains multiple health tracks, and needs to be whittled down like a battle group.

                    Do you have any ideas to add here?

                    EDIT: I'm actually a bit concerned with the abiity absorbtion powers. Most exalted characters are spoiled for ability dots, so these won't be useful for that long, and the XP discount for the sum of humanity would have to be massive to even have this part of the charm tree pay for itself.

                        A smattering of ideas, a few of them derived directly from your Path outlines.
                        • Centipede Skin Curse: While disguised, the Liminal can curse someone to take on her own appearance. If she's disguised with Snake Sheds Skin, she can transfer that Charm instead.
                        • Honey of Iron: Wreathing herself in the alluring promise of blood, the Liminal penalizes non-reflexive actions and Perception rolls unless they target her. This Charm only affects the undead, unless the Liminal has Horror Haze active.
                        • Black Teeth Smile: The Liminal can instill positive or sexual Intimacies towards herself in undead targets, and key persuade actions off such Intimacies, even if this would normally constitute unacceptable influence.
                        • The Pallid Promise: Something in the Liminal's graveborn nature calls to the living, allowing her to treat Intimacies of disgust or fear as ones of fascination or attraction instead.
                        • The Heart and the Mind: The Liminal's heart swells in her chest, blossoming into a secondary brain at the center of her being. This sharpens her mental faculties, but also allows her to recover from beheadings and brain damage that would normally be permanently fatal.
                        • Seal of the Eye and the Tongue: By disabling one or more of her senses, the Liminal enhances the ones she has left. Each sense lost bestows a unique benefit, and disabling a missing sense (such as sight for a Liminal who has recently lost her eyes) is free.
                        • Living Coffin Oracle: This charm requires the Liminal to surrender all of her senses with Seal of the Eye and the Tongue, awakening an otherworldly intuition in their stead. She gains all the benefits of the prerequisite with none of the drawbacks, as she simply knows what she cannot perceive.
                        • Groaning in the Earth: The Liminal can sense her creator and the subject of any of her Defining Intimacies from afar, and she can allow them to sense her in turn. This Charm activates automatically and for free when a character is in danger, as long as the Liminal could come to their aid in time.
                        • Sacred Flesh: Implanting or feeding a part of herself to a mortal, the Liminal endows them with a minor supernatural power. Anyone benefitting from Sacred Flesh is a valid target for Groaning in the Earth.
                        • Way of All Flesh: The Liminal disappears into her anima and emerges from the body of a beneficiary of Sacred Flesh. This does no harm to the subject, but consumes the implant and ends the benefits of Sacred Flesh.
                        • Voice-Drinking Solitude: With a subtle inhalation, the Liminal drains spoken social influence of its efficacy. Once per scene, successfully negating an influence attempt allows her to reflexively target the speaker with Steal the Gift of Life.
                        • No Voice to Cry Suffering: Screaming until her voice dies, the Liminal affirms her own existence to negate a Psyche effect or baleful enchantment affecting her. This is a mystical sacrifice as much as a physical one, preventing her from regaining her voice by any means until the end of the current story. She can gain Liminal Experience from her muteness as if it was a Flaw.
                        • Hundred Heartbeats Curse: Condemning the deathless to live, the Liminal makes them as fragile as the mortals beneath their feet. This strips a target of the Exalted Healing Merit and negates any passive immunity to poison or disease, even if it derives from a lack of the requisite biology. Active immunities, such as the Charms of the Exalted, suffer a Willpower surcharge instead.
                        • Embodied Earth Prison Seal: The Liminal seals a defeated spirit in one of her limbs or organs, accepting a crippling injury to deny her victim rebirth. She can safely remove and replace the sacrificed flesh without releasing the spirit, but if it is destroyed, the spirit breaks free. Additionally, the Liminal can meditate to commune with the spirits she carries with her, treating them as tutors for sorcery, martial arts, and other subjects appropriate to the spirits in question.

                                                            Path of passion(powers manipulating emotions of the living and the dead)

                                                            Essence 1
                                                            * tasting the greed - helps to find the target’s price for a barter attempt.
                                                            * rose-cheeked courtesan countenance - supplements a seduction attempt.
                                                            * discern the doomed heart - detect lustful or possessive intimacies in targets.
                                                            Essence 2
                                                            * blood money - creates an illusion of opulence, by making everything the exalt wears seem more attractive.
                                                            * lure of the bloody cup - nearby ghosts and undead are attracted to your location.
                                                            * sweet lie self inflicted - pulls upon the target’s intimacy to make him want to believe you, making a lie more plausible.
                                                            * possessive strength - imbues another person with extra dice to use when acting on lust, jealousy or greed.
                                                            * blood calligraphy - by mixing her blood with the ink, the exalt can apply her appearance to a written social attack, as if she were physically present.
                                                            * the pallid promise: Something in the Liminal's graveborn nature calls to the living, allowing her to treat Intimacies of disgust or fear as ones of fascination or attraction instead.
                                                            * black teeth smile: The Liminal can instill positive or sexual Intimacies towards herself in undead targets, and key persuade actions off such Intimacies, even if this would normally constitute unacceptable influence.
                                                            * honey of iron: Wreathing herself in the alluring promise of blood, the Liminal penalizes non-reflexive actions and Perception rolls unless they target her. This Charm only affects the undead, unless the Liminal has Horror Haze active.
                                                            Essence 3
                                                            * red preached tempts to sin - sweet lie self-inflicted can now affect groups.
                                                            * love’s scarlet nightmare - the next time the target acts on a targeted intimacy, it will be inverted to negative (such as turning love into jealous rage)
                                                            * forbidden fruit - tempts target to crime, causing an intimacy to overcome their inhibitions.
                                                            * words set fire to blood - temporarily strengthen a possessive intimacy, which can incite violence.
                                                            * passion eater - regain a point of will with a successful seduction attempt.
                                                            * essence of life - fill a small blood sacrifice with raw emotion, allowing a ghost to regain a point of will.
                                                            * dance of the carnal lure - fascinate a group of targets for a scene.
                                                            Essence 4
                                                            * heart-gripping poetry - inspire strong emotions through letter.
                                                            * a poem written in blood - upgrade of blood calligraphy, allows the use of appearance-related charms.
                                                            Essence 5
                                                            * third act blood splattered tragedy - words set fire to blood, possessive strength, forbidden fruit, and love’s scarlet nightmare can now be used on groups.
