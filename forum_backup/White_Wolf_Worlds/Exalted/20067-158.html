                                                            Originally posted by Anasurimbor View Post
                                                            Yes. The relevant distinction is a "single enemy" vs. "all enemies in hearing range." Mechanically, a Battle Group is treated as a single enemy. So absent any weirdness as seen with Joint-Wounding Attack, it should just work like any other single target attack.

                                                            (Also, Performance charms ignore the group influence penalty.)
                                                            While the parenthetical is true, Battle Shriek isn't a Performance Charm and it is explicitly unusable against multiple targets under normal circumstances, so that's my rationale for applying the penalty. You can certainly go the other way, but in general I would call for that penalty vs. battle groups any place it would apply to a group of noncombatants.

                                                            I differentiate Shriek from single-target effects like, e.g., a Melee attack in that Performance actions normally can target either a single or multiple targets and this one explicitly can't as an exception, so the inability to use it that way feels weightier in this case. However, as you suggest, the single-target restriction seems to mostly be a balance constraint here related to the combat notion of an "enemy", which makes it reasonable to have it apply to a BG.
