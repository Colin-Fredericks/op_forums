                                    01-07-2018, 11:41 PM
                                    Originally posted by glamourweaver View Post
                                    Am I correct in my impression that unlike Ancient Rome, the Realm has the legal distinction between criminal and civil matters?
                                    Both civil matters (contract disputes, etc.) and criminal law are handled through the same channels—the Imperial courts or the magistracy. I imagine the Realm's legal savants would draw a distinction, but we don't go that deep into it in The Realm.

                                    And under the Empress, (non-lethal) dueling was an acceptable way to resolve civil matters, but not a criminal accusation?
                                    When tempers flare and swords are drawn, I imagine the participants aren't too concerned about the distinction between civil disputes and criminal matters. ^_^

                                    That said, the outcome of a duel isn't going to change the Imperial courts' interests in seeing criminals punished.
