                                            I'm loving the kickstarter previews. The book continues the trend of seeding intriguing story hooks left, right, and centre, which I really appreciate. I love that Dragonbloods can now perform trivial feats of elemental manipulation as an anima ability, and I'm already thinking of ways to incorporate that into stunts (sending a chill breeze racing up someone's spine as you roll to intimidate them, for instance). The chapter fictions are great. Amazing job, guys.

                                            My reading of the latest DB:WFHW preview is that a Charm isn't in-Aspect for the purpose of xp cost simply by virtue of having the matching elemental keyword. So, for instance, a Fire Aspect who didn't choose Archery as Favoured pays full price for Boughs of Burning Autumn. Is this correct?

                                            Have any of the Iselsi elders got plans for what happens after their Vendetta comes to fruition and the other Great Houses lie in ruins? What does the Realm look like in the aftermath of their revenge? Is there a Realm at all? Do they care at this point?

                                            Dragonbloods don't get free excellencies. If I wanted a quick hack to make them more viable as PCs in a mixed game, would changing this so they get bonus excellencies be a good way to do that, or are the additional benefits of those Charms powerful enough that you wouldn't recommend it?

                                            Given that Sidereals are the subtlest of Exalts, it's always struck me as odd that Sidereals didn't get Elsewhere-storage Charms when most other Exalt-types did. Is that likely to change this edition? If not conceptual-elsewhere, do you intend to give Sidereals another cool way to avoid scrutiny for carrying around their panoply?

                                            Does anything break if Wyld Shaping Technique is house-ruled to return wasted xp from a failed WST attempt? Say, at the end of the current story, to avoid players spamming it when they fail? It just seems incredibly punitive compared to other xp-cost Charms and effects which all refund their cost if the investment is destroyed or becomes irrelevant.

                                            Just for fun: I've been re-watching Xena: Warrior Princess (because there wasn't enough ham or cheese in my media diet already), and she's obviously the quintessential Dawn. If you're familiar, what do you think her Supernal Ability is?
