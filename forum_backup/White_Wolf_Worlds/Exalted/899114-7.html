                            I guess I post enough on these forums to merit some kind of post here.

                            I'm ParanoiaCombo, or just PC for short, and I chose this name to remind me that no matter how much I believe gamers overvalue systems in games, there are times when a system is so broken as to inflect the culture of a game so severely it might be worth a 2nd look at. Also because it wasn't taken yet, surprisingly. I've been playing games since 1988, since I was 8, which is a long time I guess. I started playing WW games at the ripe old age of 13 with the VtM 1st edition corebook, and I still remember the feeling that this was "different" from what I was used to.

                            I like to argue about games, primarily, because the stakes are so low. Lately though, I'm beginning to re-evaluate that opinion in light of internet culture and fandom culture in general. As you may have noticed, I have strong opinions about games and game culture in general. I'm kind of an asshole. Sorry about that, genuinely.

                            I wear a lot of hats in life. I am, in no particular order, an ESL teacher, a freelance writer, a balance/play experience consultant for a bunch of game companies usually but not restricted to the Fighting variety, and I do some security gigs on the side for a private company. I am also an occasional crisis counselor and have a hobby of taking in stray humans and helping them out, oftentimes to my own financial detriment, but man.. sometimes you can't let fucked up things slide. Aside from that, my day job is cleaning up the mess from the 2007 housing market crash in Massachusetts, which is a fancy way of saying, I check paperwork and sometimes foreclose on houses. It sucks.

                            I playtested on EX3, and I think it's a good game, but not a game I want to play. I won't get into that here. That's not what this thread is for.

                            Random Facts: My favorite color is sky blue, and my favorite fictional character is Ryu from Street Fighter. I also like to hike, often, because if you quit climbing a mountain halfway, you still have to climb down. I like getting angry PM's from people. People tell me I look like the kind of guy who would stomp you in the ribs behind an Applebees if I caught you hitting your SO on date night. Verbatim.

                            Ok, I think that about it sums it up.

                            Incentive is not permission or justification.
