    I feel that the Neverborn themselves don't have sufficient influence over the Abyssals. The Deathlords are just Oblivion's middle management, but the Neverborn are the Executives. All Abyssals should share in the unliving nightmare of the slain Titans when they slumber, to better appreciate the urgency of their world-murdering mission. This adds their Whispers rating as an External Penalty to their morning Conviction roll to regain Willpower.

    In Creation, the nightmares of the Neverborn grow distant, but this should be no boon. In Creation, Abyssals derive no rest from sleep, accumulating fatigue penalties as though time in repose were replaced by hard labor, and receiving no daily roll for Willpower recovery.

    Every morning, after the Abyssal wakes up, a hungry ghost pops out of the Underworld, kills the nearest mortal and uses their blood to write detailed instructions for the Abyssal.
    (The intent of the thread aside, I'd happily buy that as a charm.)

        Originally posted by Aquillion View Post
        Any Solar who kills an Abyssal experiences a euphoric rush of knowing that they have eliminated something that should not have existed, causing them to learn 10 new charms immediately for free. Beating an Abyssal into incapacitated without killing them earns you one charm for free, so Solars in mixed circles have an incentive to keep Abyssals alive in order to farm them in the long run. However, killing Abyssals is addictive, imposing a cumulative 1 WP cost to resist the opportunity for each Abyssal you've killed previously.
        I don't feel like that's really punitive enough; under that system, one might almost get the impression that having an Abyssal in a mixed circle is a net benefit to the group.

        Instead, I think that the other members of the group, if sleeping at the same time, should get Nightmares as well, and random hungry ghosts coming to menace their loved ones and steal their unattended belongings. (NPCs come and go, but stuff is forever.)

        And if a hungry ghost steals the belongings of a Creation-sider, it should immediately fade away to oblivion with the object in question in tow. If the group lets the Abyssal hang around long enough in Creation, this should also happen to artifacts, manses and nearby geographical features just to drive home the point that the Abyssal is a thing that does not belong.

Now the Infernals take up the spot of the 'corrupted' Solars and the Abyssals are the Broken, The Doomed.
