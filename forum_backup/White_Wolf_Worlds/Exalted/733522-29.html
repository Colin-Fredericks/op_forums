            Originally posted by Wyldwraith View Post
            Creating a being comparable to a Second Circle Demon is Celestial 3, so Celestial Ambition 2?
            Seems reasonable.

            Originally posted by Wyldwraith View Post
            To get your control of the Demon equivalent to the spell, would it have to be Finesse 5?
            That's up to you and your ST; remember, Finesse isn't contingent on how powerful the effect is, it's a function of how much executive power you the player have in regard to how your working manifests. So if your desire is to have it function exactly identically to DotSC, and your ST doesn't veto it, and you choose Finesse 5, then that's what you get. But if your desire is to have a hollow spot in the demon's brain where your surgically-implanted telepathy glands can directly control its body, then it won't function like the spell even at Finesse 5.

            Originally posted by Wyldwraith View Post
            What would choosing Finesse 3 or 1 look like, here?
            That's up to you and your ST. For instance, maybe it only obeys the commands of one who bears a certain magic ring; or perhaps its life is linked to yours, so that if one of you perishes, the other dies by the same means.

            The demon is still going to be a loyal servant if the working succeeds and that's what you the player wanted, no matter what level Finesse you chose.

            Originally posted by Wyldwraith View Post
            For Means: Would you allow Demon of the First Circle as a Complementary Spell?
            Yes.

            Originally posted by Wyldwraith View Post
            Would you allow (for a mortal sorcerer) with Occult 5, a specialty in Demonology, and say an applicable Craft 5 the equivalent of the second Means roll an Exalt could acquire from "Significant Charms related to the Complementary Ability"?
            No.

            Originally posted by Wyldwraith View Post
            When it lists the various Means Alternatives, like a fellow sorcerer, a related exalt, or a group of non-sorcerer occult-aligned individuals, when it says they don't stack I'm not overthinking this..it means pick one, right?
            Right, you can't double-dip from having a sorcerer and an Exalt, or a sorcerer who is an Exalt, et cetera.

            Originally posted by Wyldwraith View Post
            Obviously a mortal trying this would take it out to the maximum time-interval, because they are so completely screwed if this blows up in their face.
            Very true.

            Originally posted by Wyldwraith View Post
            Look about right?
            I'm regrettably AFB at the moment, sorry.

What I would allow is a working to count as a Means for other workings if it was at the same Circle and Ambition (so, for example, a Terrestrial 1 working would serve as a means to summon a 1st Circle demon).
