                                        Do you have any hypotheses on what you would have to do to challenge a party like that?                                         Supernal Integrity Abyssal that social-fu's people into mass murder and suicide while they have that tranquility / immune to damage charm up.

                                        A small child and their puppy. Nothing supernatural about them. Have them try to murder the dawn while wearing a pot for a helmet, using a wooden sword.

                                        Behemoths that need to be killed piece by piece using gambits and lethal attacks.                                         Slow your encounters down and string them out. The Dragon-blooded don't just walk in and seing a goremaul at the dawn. They take it slow, infiltrating and dismantlong the circle's power-base while turning assets against the solars.

                                        Challenge their intimacies and things they hold dear while they have a combat looming.

                                        Don't give them rest. Attack in waves. Numbers *ultimately* win in Exalted.

                                                Originally posted by Zeesun View Post



                                                Do you have any hypotheses on what you would have to do to challenge a party like that? My Circle's Night assassin is starting to get as ludicrous as our Dawn, and our Zenith is getting suspiciously close to Tiger Warrior/War setup with some unpleasant math.
                                                Well, one of my plans was to eventually use the age-old ST trick of mirror NPCs, which is pretty much built-in on Exalted for Solars due to the existance of Abyssals. They were already starting to hear rumors about the advances of a cadre of dark champions expanding the domain of an Underworld warlord into the lands of the living. Basically, I would throw an about equally overpowered circle of Abyssals at them, backed by the resources of a Deathlord.

                                                Before that could materialize, however, their other foes were already realizing that facing them head on was suicidal, and stated targeting their weak points. See, while that circle was godlike in personal combat, they mostly lacked specialized defenses against poison and disease, only one of them was really good at War, none invested in Medicine, and their Social Influence skills were relatively lacking compared to their ass-kicking skills.

                                                Since their coalition of City-States had made some enemies such as rival rulers and a Guild Factor, they had personally pissed off some key figures in an Elemental Court and a big Wyld Hunt was trying to take them down, there were a lot of resourceful foes playing dirty against them. Thus their cities faced plague, poisoned water supplies, cut-off supply lines and commerce, while their more reluctant allies were convinced to betray them at crucial points, multiple armies attacked at different points at once to force them to divide their forces and having relatively bad commanders leading part of their forces.

                                                So, after dealing with multiple attacks, including an ambush from who they thought was an ally, their Essence reserves not full, and they wounded, tired, hungry, some of them sick or poisoned...then the Wyld Hunt threw everything they still had at the Solars. One of the Dawns and the Zenith were killed, and the Night pretty grievously wounded, before retreating with the other two Dawns.

                                                After that the players of the dead characters made less combat-focused ones to give the circle more versatility, and their social and sorcerous capacities will help the new group rebuild a power base faster. Meanwhile those Abyssals are coming, and the remaining forces of the Solars' foes would not be enough to impede them, so the Solars will have to rise to the occasion, and possibly have an uneasy truce with the tattered forces of their foes, to stop the Abyssals from laying waste to the region.
