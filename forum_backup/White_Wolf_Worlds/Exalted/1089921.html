    New Sidereal Homebrew for 3e: Down Came the Spider

    05-08-2017, 04:26 PM
    Greetings!

    While there are already two different Sidereal Homebrews for Third Edition, I personally could not honestly give my recommendation to either. Grifftofer's Sidereals has gone without updates or a sign of life for around a year, while Where Fate Has Led has some tremendous problems with trying to reinvent the wheel, and loses some of the charms, effects and other neat mechanical concepts that made Sidereals come alive for me. So, rather than trying to take the bits I like from both and cobbling together something, I decided to take a few of my NPC Sidereal charms I've made over time and make them usable.

    https://docs.google.com/document/d/1NhOn8TrzeGXr0llm2CZOYcbHpBSGTyXebei79IIjfRY/edit?usp=sharing


    The Current goals are to create a version of Sidereals that fits within the mechanics of Third Edition, while retaining the theme and general charm effects of previous editions. While most charms in first and second edition will be making a reappearance, some new charms will be added to fill in the gaps, boosting the average length of a charm tree from 5-6 charms to 12-15. Rework Paradox into an effect that shows a Sidereal's impact on Creation, and provides a mechanic for the Fate Glitches they are sent to fix. Give Ronin an actual feel to them. Give Craft: Fate an actual purpose outside of planning committees.

    Current Status: Character Creation is finished, every single charm tree has at least 3 essence 1 charms. Should be barebones, but enough for a playtest. In the middle of updating College Information

                              Hey everyone! I have a new potential update I wanted to get feedback upon. Currently I was planning on giving each College two Resplendency powers, but the system felt clunky. So, an Idea I had was to give each caste 3 of their best Resplendency powers as part of their anima banner. Here is the first draft:

                              Anima Banner Powers
                              • For 1 mote, Sidereals may determine the exact time of date through sensing the position of the stars. Incidentally, this also tells the Sidereal how much Paradox is in the area
                              • For 1 mote, Sidereals may display a Caste Mark upon their forehead
                              • The Sidereal may spend 1 mote to learn of any portals to Yu-Shan or Malfeas within long range. She knows not where they are, she only learns of their presence.
                              • For 3 motes, the Sidereal may burn a letter written on fine silk, addressed to another Sidereal. The Letter appears on that Sidereal's skin and buzzes to alert him of its presence


                              Journeys
                              • For 16m, the Sidereal makes the Lesser Sign of Mercury, adding her (Essence) dice to all ally's rolls to rush or Disengage for the rest of the Scene. Every level of anima above Dim reduces the cost of this ability by 5m
                              • Poetic Sacrifice Insurance: For 3m, the Sidereal may ignore penalties by terrain or bad weather, and ignores nonmagical difficult terrain
                              • Field Mouse Rider: The Sidereal may ride any familiar without causing it harm or facing any difficulty of her own. If the Speed bonus of the mount is below (Essence) it instead becomes (Essence)
                              • Good Worker Spirit: For 4m per day, no one will believe the Sidereal is slacking, no matter how little work she does. This is an Illusion effect. The Bureau of Destiny has persistent problems with Sidereals using this on the job.


                              Serenities
                              • For 16m, the Sidereal makes the Lesser Sign of Venus, adding her (Essence) dice to all ally's rolls to instill or inspire positive emotions. Every level of anima above Dim reduces the cost of this ability by 5m
                              • Proper Plumage: The Sidereal is always considered well dressed and fashionable, no matter what her attire actually is. Even the scent of sweat and horse dung will be recieved as a perfume
                              • Handsome Boy Eyes: For 4m, the Sidereal may make seduction attempts with only a glance, needing no supporting intimacy
                              • Comforting Matriarch Embrace: The Sidereal may hold and comfort someone, before putting them to sleep and paying 5m. If she does so, the target will have a rest uninterupted by nightmares and recovers an additional willpower


                              Battles
                              • For 16m, the Sidereal makes the Lesser Sign of Mars, adding her (Essence) to all ally's Soak. Every level of anima above Dim reduces the cost of this ability by 5m
                              • Weak Spine Sense: The Sidereal always known whether anyone has an intimacy of fear towards her, and if so how intense it is
                              • Several Arrows of Reason: After landing an attack against a Creature outside of fate, the Sidereal may pay 4m to ignore all natural soak and (Essence) hardness. If the attack deals even a single point of damage, the Creature is considered inside Fate for the rest of the scene
                              • Wounded Lion Strength: By paying 1wp, the Sidereal may add her wound penalty to the damage of her next decisive attack


                              Secrets
                              • For 16m, the Sidereal makes the Lesser Sign of Jupiter, adding her (Essence) dice to all ally's rolls to Read intentions, and (Essence/2) to their Guile. Every level of anima above Dim reduces the cost of this ability by 5m
                              • Stab the Seer's Eyes: The Sidereal is aware of any astrological reading to detect her, and may pay 3m to change or distort the reading
                              • Talisman Making Touch: For 1wp, the Sidereal may bless a small object and give it to someone in need. The object becomes a Talisman with Resources value equal to the Sidereal's Essence
                              • Provable Location of the Gate: For 20m, 2wp, the Sidereal may prove that the Calibration Gate will appear here and within 5 minutes, it is so. Stepping through the Gate returns her and her allies to Yu-Shan, and it dissapears as soon as all of her friends step through


                              Endings
                              • For 16m, the Sidereal makes the Lesser Sign of Saturn, adding her (Essence) damage to all ally's damage rolls. Every level of anima above Dim reduces the cost of this ability by 5m
                              • Reading Dead Eyes: After eating a Corpse's eye, and spending 2m, the Sidereal learns of the last thing the Corpse saw before it died and whether the body left behind a ghost or hungry ghost
                              • Harvest Ripe Wheat, Falling Feather Hay: The Sidereal instantly knows how corrupt an organization is as soon as she steps within its confines. This tells her nothing about individual members, just the organization as a whole
                              • Tears of the Blade: Once per scene. the Sidereal makes the Sign of the Sword at a mortal and pays 10m, 2wp. The mortal either dies immediately of heart attack, or dies in a horrific fashion of the Sidereal's design within 5 days. In combat, this costs an additional 15 initiative. After using this ability, the area's paradox immediately rises by 2.

                                                          Originally posted by Hermes12 View Post
                                                          Im curious about your choices of abilities and what informed them. For example I would have thought now martial arts are freed up from caste abilities that Melee would be a more obvious choice for Endings over Brawl. The executioners Sword over the pugilists fists? Also there is a lot of overlay between the caste abilities of Secrets and Endings Awareness, Integrity, Occult, Stealth. Why does Endings have Integrity? Thas seems to be about maintaining, resisting change, enduring keeping intimacies. this seems to run counter to the message of Endings?
                                                          The reason behind a few of these choices is due to the abilities in First and Second Edition and the discrepencies between them. In First edition, Endings had Martial Arts, Athletics, Bureaucracy, Medicine and Awareness. In the Transition to Second edition, Endurance stopped being an ability as it was far too close to resistance. They were combined, and given to Journeys who previously had Endurance. Brawl was removed, and its place in Battles was exchanged for War. Athletics was given to Battles to fill in the gap, and Integrity was given to Endings because Endings needed an additional ability. I've given Brawl to Endings because of its link to Martial Arts in this Edition, and kept Integrity in Endings as a reference to its location in Second Edition while shuffling a few things around. I agree that Secrets and Endings as of right now are too close together, so my current proposal is
                                                          • Secrets: Lore, Occult, Investigation, Larceny, Stealth, Socialize, Integrity
                                                          • Endings: Medicine, Brawl, Athletics, Integrity, Awareness, Bureaucracy, Stealth

                                                          This lowers the amount they share to two abilities (Stealth and Integrity) and gives a bit more credibility to Endings Castes as Heaven's assassins. Awareness I had originally given to Secrets because I thought the future sight charms would fit, and Integrity helps you from spilling the beans. With this arrangement, Secrets keeps Integrity and also gains Socialize, an excellent ability for keeping secrets and discerning the secrets of others.
