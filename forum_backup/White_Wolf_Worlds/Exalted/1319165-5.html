                            Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz View Post

                            Huh. Guess I've been houseruling that longer than I thought. Happily retracted.

                            I agree with you on the excellencies, but a lot of charms represent that too. Blazing Solar Bolt is a supernatural power, but stuff like Excellent Strike, Peony Blossom Strike, War Lion Stance, and Hungry Tiger Technique are just ways to make &quot;I'm an amazing swordsman&quot; more mechanically interesting than more dice. But they still just represent being an amazing swordsman.
                            I kind of disagree.

                            All the Solar charms are an extension of skill. Blazing Solar Bolt is the swordsman so skillful he can strike his foes from a distance, unleashing the shining glory of the sun. Excellencies, because they only add dice, don't do anything beyond doing what you do better.

                            Excellent Strike is more like D&D's True Strike. It flat out stops you botching, using magic to strike true and harden your attack against mystical defences. Peony Blossom Strike imbues you with such supernatural potency that you can attack twice as fast as any mortal swordsman could ever achieve. Likewise War Lion Stance reflective nature is much more than a mortal could achieve by flurrying. Hungry Tiger technique, I'll give you, is basically an Excellency for damage, but I still see it as using magic to rend your opponent apart in a way that a mortal just can't replicate.

                            But Excellencies... a Solar swordsman with Dex 2 Melee 2 can use an Excellency to buy 4 more dice. A mortal swordsman can use XP to buy their stats up to 4 to get those dice. Excellencies are far closer to "more of the same but better".

                                    But what can you do with it?

                                    With Dex 5, Athletics 5, a Spec in "jumping" and 10 Excellency dice, you can jump really well. But you can't leap to the top of a building in a single bound. With Dex 1, Athletics 2 and Monkey Leap Technique you CAN leap to the top of that building.

                                    With Excellent Strike, a Solar Swordswoman can never botch. It's not unlikely, it's impossible. With the Excellency, she's just better at sword fighting. And yes, she's better than any mortal can hope to be, but it's a linear progression of skill. It's N+1. She's not doubling her attacks per round, or breaking the turn order, or ignoring magical armor, or pulling a blade from nowhere. She's just doing what the mortal does... only better.

                                    Finally, I'm a 5/5/1 min-maxer all the way, but the 3E corebook makes clear that a Dex 2, Melee 2 character is capable with a sword. I don't write the books, I just play here.

                                    But, again, I don't disagree that Solar charms are dialled up expressions of skill. My point is only that Excellencies are the most basic expression of that, and that my preferred aesthetic is not for Excellencies to be overtly "magical".
