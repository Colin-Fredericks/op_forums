            Sleep First: 0
            Practice Hygiene, THEN Sleep: 4
            Sell On Your Own: 2 (+1 Fangs Only)
            Cut Aunt Dwi In: 2
            Cut Rat In: 0
            Smoke Some Meat For Later: 2
            Do Some Crafting: 1
            Occult Plans: 1

            You’re tired, and the roof tiles in the rain are slippery and dangerous…but you’ve seen what life is like for people who get sick on the streets of Nexus. It’s not pretty. Like Aunt Dwi always says, “hygiene before profit.”

            Carefully, you strip down and hang your clothes on the edge of the eave above you, hoping the pounding rain will wash some of the blood out of them. You stand naked and shivering on the roof, the flimsy layer of warmth your wet rags provided stripped away. You rub the blood off yourself as best you can, and you scrub your fingers through your scraggly black hair, getting it all wet and then clawing along its length in the hopes of removing anything that’s gotten caught in it. Your hair is gonna be a mess tomorrow, you can tell. You consider tying it up with your twine and just chopping it off with your new knife…but then, you remember Aunt Dwi also always says you have gorgeous hair and you feel conflicted. A bad haircut could mess up your chances if you’re trying to do business tomorrow even worse than frizzy tangles…

            You’ll worry about it tomorrow. Now clean, and freezing, you carefully make your way back up to your little warm dry ledge, and take some choice bits of meat from within your bundle along with the fish you caught. You tie some twine around them, and gingerly make your way farther up the roof to hang them over the smokestack. Hopefully the constant flow of hot air and smoke will preserve the meat. At the very least, they’ll keep bugs off it. You pick your way across the slippery roof tiles back to your nest, and wrap yourself in some dry rags next to the warm wall of the foundry. You don’t even really hear the eternal clanging of the hammers and the rumbling of the forges below anymore, but you can feel them vibrate through you as you lie there, and the noise and the rhythm is comforting. Slowly, you drift off to sleep.

            Your dreams are full of distant thunder and flashing teeth and huge scaled forms dancing through the rains high overhead. They terrify you, but instead of running you climb building after building to try and get closer to them. You run so hard you find yourself panting and coughing, struggling to breathe. You reach out towards them, to pull yourself up into the sky alongside them…but you slip, and you are falling, and the world below hurtles up towards you and-

            You’re falling! You wake suddenly, and put out your hands to catch yourself on the eave above. You had started to roll away from the flat section right under the eaves and out onto the sloped roof…but you caught yourself in time. You’re starting to see why Rat gave this spot over to you. You’re a little bigger than you were when you first started sleeping here, and there wasn’t much space to sleep on to start with. Heart still pounding, you stare up at the steel-gray skies and consider spending the Dinar the Emissary gave you to rent a place to live. A whole Dinar would probably be enough to get you one month’s rent…

            It’s early morning, now, and the rain is gone. The streets below are full of thick, soupy white fog. From the rooftop, it’s like looking out over a slowly frothing river with the tops of buildings sticking up from it like islands. It’s beautiful…but your eyes quickly sting and begin to water. Without the rain to pound it into the ground, the choking smog of the Nighthammer District tints the whole scene faintly yellow-brown, and right up here on the rooftop under the stacks spewing it into the sky…well, you’re getting almost as smoked as the meat you tied up last night. You tie a band of fabric around your nose and mouth, like you usually do, and consider your options.

            You don’t really want to cut Rat or Aunt Dwi in on your cut of the River Dragon…but you don’t’ really know how much your stuff is worth, either, and between that and being a kid you’re likely to get either laughed out of the place or just cheated by whatever alchemist you go to. You decide that Dwi is your best bet-she already works with an alchemist who makes medicines for her brothel, and he wants to keep her as a client-but you also decide it would be smart to keep some of the haul for yourself. You decide to bring about half of your haul to sell with Dwi…and once you know what it’s actually worth, you can sell the other half on your own.

            Still hoping to beat anybody else who helped harvest the Dragon on your way down to the markets, you dress quickly, putting on a slightly-less-squalid set of rags from your heap. You grab one of the big fangs and two of the smaller ones you got from the River Dragon, and pull out the eyeball and bundle it up in cloth.

            It’s a quick climb down to street level, and then a short walk to Dwi’s brothel. As usual for this early in the morning, everyone there is asleep-the brothel stays open late and its workers usually don’t rise until the afternoon. You greet the drowsy doorman, who nods sleepily to you. He’s at first reluctant to let you to bother Aunt Dwi…but when you tell him you’ve got a way for her to make some money he grudgingly lets you in. If there’s one thing Aunt Dwi loves, it’s ways to make money.

            She meets you in her office, after keeping you waiting only about five minutes. She has tapestries on the walls depicting scantily clad dancers, and a big desk. The chairs are big and oddly shaped, and they have blue cushions.

            “Good morning,” she says. Her face was painted last night, but this morning it’s smudged and tired. “Ox tells me you have a proposition for me. I assume…it has something to do with the armful of ivory you’re carting about?”

            You know Dwi reasonably well. She’s heavily motivated by profit, but she’s also easily flattered by compliments on her appearance. Dwi also sees herself as a protective motherly figure and likes it when people ask her for help or advice, and is fond of meddling in people’s affairs. How do you want to go about this negotiation?

                                                        “It’s why I’m always telling you to come and work for me! You’d be safer here, and you deserve to be able to be yourself instead of trying to hide under dirty rags. And believe me, hiding isn’t going to get any easier, young lady! You have your father’s eyes, but you look just like your mother did at your age, and if you’re half the looker she was covering yourself in dung isn’t going to stop people noticing you. You’d be safer here at the Flaming Peacock. At least you’d have someone to watch out for you.”

                                                        “Of course not.” Dwi sighs. “Gods, child, our profession is rarely anyone’s first career choice. But the pay is good and once you get decent at it the work is easy and often even pleasant. Green Lily… wanted better things for you than she had for herself, and what mother doesn’t, but the fact is the only reason she lived to know you as well as she did was that working at the Peacock pays well enough to afford healers and herbs to treat the Black Lung…well, so long as you don’t spend it all on drugs and jewelry.”

                                                        “I…I know that, Aunt Dwi.”

                                                        “I know, child…I don’t mean to pressure you. There’s too many boys and girls out there doing this job against their will already. I won’t have it in my house. It’s just...I worry for you. You’re not much more than skin and bones, your hair is a mess, I’m seriously concerned you may wind up sick or worse out there. I can’t keep you here if you don’t work, it wouldn’t be fair to anyone else, and gods know everyone else has troubles they could use help with…but know you have a place to come to, if you need it.”

                                                        You find yourself a little touched. It’s nice to remember there’s someone out there who actually cares about you…even if you suspect it’s mostly out of guilt and lingering fondness for your Mother.

                                                        “Thank you, Aunt Dwi. It’s good to have a safe place to run to…if I ever need to run someplace, I mean.”

                                                        “Well, you always have a safe place here at the Peacock. We all loved Lily…some of us more than others, of course-and a lot of the girls remember taking care of you when you were just a little thing, in the back rooms while she worked. She brought in a lot of business, and she didn’t keep it all to herself, and she was always there to back someone up if they were having a bad day or a bad client. Any daughter of Green Lily is a friend of ours.”

                                                        You find yourself legitimately tearing up just a little bit. It’s not often you hear anything about Mother, and somebody else telling you about how nice she was…it’s bringing back a lot of happy memories you’ve been not-thinking-about-because-being-sad-hurts. You remember the muffled sounds of the Burning Peacock, giggles and grunts and laughter through thin walls, and the little games the girls used to play with you to keep you from making noise and bothering any of the paying customers. You remember the smells of the place-sweat and wine and poppies and smoke. You remember the smells of dried herbs and the muttering of one of Aunt Dwi’s alchemists, when Mother’s cough was getting really bad…you remember tears and shouting and screaming, too, though the reasons for them are mostly hazy now.

                                                        “…thank you,” you tell Aunt Dwi again. You really mean it. Even if you wanted to take her up on her offer-and a big part of you kind of wants to, to have a place to belong-you can’t stay here. Too many memories.

                                                        “It’s no trouble, child. I can always use new help, and my regulars are always delighted to see new faces. Honestly, if you’re really set on continuing to dress like a boy, that wouldn’t necessarily be a problem, some of them might even pay a little extra for-“

                                                        “Aunt Dwi.”

                                                        “Sorry. Sorry. So, I’m guessing no gauzy silk numbers for you, then... That’s most of what I have available, I’m afraid, but I’m always happy to take a couple of the girls shopping. If you don’t want anyone to discover your gender…well, the clothes make the man. What sort of new outfit would you like for me to arrange for you?”

                                                        An Outfit For Business-you want to be able to deal with legitimate merchants, and that means looking clean-cut and classy. Maybe with a nice robe that could cover a snug vest for when your figure starts to develop…

                                                        An Outfit For Winter-You got through the last couple winters by alternately huddling with a bunch of other urchins in an upper section of the Underways, taking Dwi up on her hospitality, and stealing lots of blankets from clotheslines ahead of time. But having a nice cold-weather outfit could possibly save your life this year.

                                                        A Boatman’s Outfit: It’ll require telling Dwi you’re following in your father’s footsteps, and he’s not exactly her favorite person. But having a nice looking outfit that can handle some water without damage or shrinkage could help a lot if you’re serious about starting as Grinning Eel’s apprentice.

                                                        A Craftsman’s Outfit: You’re honestly a pretty good weaver, at this point-you can make your own clothes, though they won’t be fancy. What you really need is a good leather apron with lots of pockets for needles and spools of thread and scissors and suchlike. With the money you get from this River Dragon Teeth deal, you can make any other outfits you need, with cloth and a bit of practice anyway…and hey, being a seamstress? Doesn’t usually require poling around on the canal in bad weather or sleeping with belligerent drunks.

                                                        A Thief’s Outfit: You want something with lots of pockets for various tools, alright…and for hiding your loot. You’re a solid pickpocket, but you’re not great at sneaking, which has led to problems. Something inconspicuous in brown or green so it’s easy to hide, something sturdy that can handle climbing or running, something that a little blood’ll wash out of easy…something with hidden pockets in the lining.

                                                        A Monk’s Outfit: Simple robes and meditating in the rain? Bring it on! Alternately…well, if meditating in the rain doesn’t do it for you after a while, the aspiring monks get to stay in the Monastery overnight, so it could be a good start to robbing the place.

                                                        A Dancer’s Outfit: Dwi actually kind of has a point. Maybe you should go for something you can wear if and when you decide to try using your Wiles instead of your wits. I mean, you haven’t felt “pretty” since before Mother died. Even if you don’t want to work for Dwi, it might be nice to own at least one girly feeling thing.

                                                        A Warrior’s Outfit: If you’re going to be staying on the street, life is going to be dangerous…and apparently more so as you get older and start taking after your Mother. You need protection. Something mobile…but able to turn away a knife or soften the blow of a club. Something to let you deal with the Sluice Gang without worrying about getting beat up. Maybe a buff jacket or a robe with plates sewn in…
