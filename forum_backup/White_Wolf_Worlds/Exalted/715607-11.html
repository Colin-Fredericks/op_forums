                    The impression I have gotten from various commentary is that Solars will have the most bloat because Solars can specialize. They can approach problems with a variety of very specific tools, which gives sometimes too many options but with some practice can make a wide variety of fun character options.

                    I've been led to believe that other splats will have fewer options in some respects not necessarily out of a power differential, but out of a different approach. I forsee Sids having fewer charms with more broad applicability in each. This will likely translate to longer charm descriptions too, but time will tell. I forsee DBs having a similar setup to Solars but with just fewer options. If Solars had 500 charms, DBs might have 300 or 350 maybe. That's not insider knowledge, that's an educated guess from various sources.

                    tl;dr is I think Solars will have the worst of the charm bloat but for narrative reasons, and others will have less of an issue.

                                                            10-30-2015, 09:45 PM
                                                            Performance

                                                            Number of Charms: 37
                                                            By Ability requirement: 0/1/6/12/18
                                                            By Essence requirement: 15/10/8/2/2
                                                            Nonstandard dice tricks: Free full Excellency

                                                            Themes: Boost the actions of others, make other social influence easier, stir emotions, mimic others.

                                                            Performance is, somewhat unusually, broken down into subtrees by type – that is, the Charms you use to dance don’t apply to singing.

                                                            Omni-Applicable:
                                                            1. Bonus success + recurring 1s.
                                                            2. Inflict emotion on a group; all those affected reconsider a past decision in that emotional context.
                                                            3. Recover motes when you influence a group.
                                                            4. Gain motes for rolling 10s or attempting performance, but only with scenelong Charms up.
                                                            5. Non-supplemental Performance Charms can be used with other performance types.
                                                            6. Spend ~25% of your Willpower to make an influence roll more successful and harder to resist.
                                                            7. 1/scene, free full muted Excellency.
                                                            8. 1/day, for the next performance, your Performance Charms cost no motes.
                                                            9. Reset #7 and #8.
                                                            Penultimate Unity of Form (#5) is weird to me – is the conceptual separation of Performance important, or not? The Ability itself says no – there’s no Craft-style division. The Charms say yes – totally separate trees with redundant effects. Penultimate Unity says no, because most of your effects re-fuse, but only post-Essence 3. What gives?

                                                            Utility (not Omni-Applicable?):
                                                            1. People must pay WP to leave or interrupt your performance.
                                                            2. Create illustrative illusions.
                                                            3. A group accepts a false memory. Announcing “Lelouch vi Britannia commands you!” optional.
                                                            4. Vaporize creatures of darkness to long range with an extremely potent Decisive.
                                                            So Memory-Reweaving Discipline (#3) specifies that people act in accord with their new memories. That seems like it bypasses an awful lot of the social system – like, if you land this hit, what can you not do? “Hey, remember when so-and-so tried to kill you and I saved your life?” may take penalties for implausibility, but if it lands, that’s pretty compelling motivation for… well, just about anything.

                                                            Demon-Wracking Shout has prerequisite Charms of “???.” This doesn’t appear to be a mistake – it looks like the intent is you can only learn this Charm if the Storyteller decides it would be thematically appropriate. Which could be kind of cool, but is also pretty inconsistent with how the rest of the set works – I get what they’re going for, but I think I’d rather it just be a Charm.

                                                            Oratory:
                                                            1. Double-9s, on oratory.
                                                            2. Incite a mob to action.
                                                            3. On inspiring a crowd, grant bonus dice to those affected.
                                                            4. Convince another character to attempt social influence now, forgoing all other purposes.
                                                            Typically, Charms that grant bonus dice to mortals seem to have a “non-Charm” rider – I presume because the die cap for a mortal is 0. If that’s accurate – and it’s not clear that it is, as this is more inference than actual rules text – Dogmatic Contagion Discipline (#3) is missing the rider.

                                                            Infectious Zealotry Approach (#4) is kind of a mixed bag. The whole “forgoes all other concerns” thing suggests some interesting uses, but the trigger condition – “I’m thinking about making some social influence, buuuuuuut maybe not now” – seems like a pretty isolated circumstance.

                                                            We come to a half-page art showing exactly what you can do with just a few posed figured copied and pasted fifty times. (Answer: we get a crowd who are all inspired to strike the exact same celebratory pose.)

                                                            Music and Singing:
                                                            1. Double-9s, on music. Hang on, that seems familiar…
                                                            2. Grant or remove dice to attempts to create social influence, depending on whether it fits with the mood of your music.
                                                            3. Grant bonus dice to battle groups.
                                                            4. Grant bonus dice to a social influence.
                                                            5. Appeal to an emotionally-charged Intimacy, creating Storyteller-defined action.
                                                            6. Allies regenerate extra motes while listening to your music.
                                                            7. Either the Solar and an ally are immune to fear, or a larger group (or battle group) gain courage bonuses.
                                                            Okay, I really want to build a character with Mood-Inducing Music (#2), now – just sit in the corner, strumming a sanxian, periodically playing entirely-anachronistic music to mess with what’s going on.

                                                            The primary advantage Plectral Harbinger’s Approach (#4) offers over Mood-Inducing Music seems to be that it offers non-Charm dice rather than Charm dice – is that accurate? (“Plectral?”)

                                                            Heart-Compelling Method (#5) makes it almost the entire Charm before it mentions that it can be used for anything other than melancholy music – and even then, the only mechanical description is for sad tunes. It seems like this could be constructed a little more generally. And for that matter… okay, so the Charm plays off an Intimacy and generates emotion. Isn’t this pretty close to the basic effect of an Instill or Inspire action? What is this doing that you can’t do with a musical stunt? (That’s a legit question – is there a major mechanical effect here I’m overlooking, beyond a vague “The storyteller decides what he does,” i.e., same thing as an Instill action?)

                                                            In general, the music and singing section takes a lot of pages from the D&D bard – lots of “inspire others”-type effects that give a few extra dice to different actions. (“Regen, regen, regenerate the motes!”)

                                                            Soul-Stirring Cantata (#6) says the Wyld Hunt knows what this song sounds like. How the heck does that work?

                                                            Dance:
                                                            1. … gain double-9s while dancing. Um.
                                                            2. Add (Performance/2) to your Defense.
                                                            3. Your dance inspires people to talk about their Ties.
                                                            4. Reduce target’s Resolve or Guile by 2 vs. your next social influence.
                                                            5. Inspire characters to lust after or admire you.
                                                            Battle-Dancer Method (#2) is probably the most efficient Defense booster so far – 1m for a +2 Defense bonus, applicable any time you can stunt your moves as a dance. Compare that to the Charms in Awareness that are a 2m discount to a much more selective roll, under much rarer circumstances. Why does this Charm even bother to say “half Performance, round down” is added? You need Performance 4 just to buy it, and your Performance can’t go over 5. It’s +2! It’s always +2! Just say +2.

                                                            Shining Expression Style (#3) is just kind of ridiculous. It only works if you overcome Resolve – sure, okay, fine. It causes your targets to recall a Tie related to the emotion you’re inspiring – which means you’d better have a pretty good idea of what their Ties are, or you’re fishing blindly. Your targets then reveal their Ties to others – but, according to the Charm, not necessarily to you. So by beating their Resolve, you get someone (you don’t get to pick who) to find out that the target has a particular Tie (you don’t get to pick what). This is a Charm? Why is this a Charm? (“Not even All-Encompassing Sorcerer’s Sight can reveal the power of this Charm” – well, yeah, because AESS doesn’t reveal any Exalted Charms.)

                                                            Winding Sinuous Motion (#5) is a Charm, which must overcome Resolve, whose effect is that your next influence can more easily overcome Resolve. I assume the idea is that your second influence runs counter to Intimacies and would otherwise be harder than the initial roll?

                                                            Husband-Seducing Demon Dance is a better name than Monk-Seducing Demon Dance (#5). I’m just saying.

                                                            Acting:
                                                            1. Gain +1 Guile as long as you stay in character. Characters who fail to pierce your Guile read Intimacies for the character you’re playing.
                                                            2. … Wait, that’s it? That’s the acting tree?
                                                            On the one hand, Master Thespian Style (#Guess) is pretty great. On the other hand… so, uh, how ‘bout that acting tree?

                                                            Voice:
                                                            1. Ventriloquism.
                                                            2. Mimic a voice perfectly. For ten seconds, after an hour of warming up.
                                                            3. Mimic a voice perfectly for a scene.
                                                            4. Mimic an animal.
                                                            Cunning Mimicry Technique (#2) is fun, in very selective circumstances. You can’t have a conversation with it; you can’t have an exchange of orders. You can give a short speech, or walk in and say something and walk out, or cast someone’s voice across the room (if you know an hour in advance that you’re going to need to mimic that one person). After you finish that one sentence, hope you’ve got an hour to practice before you need to reply.

                                                            Splendid Magpie Approach (#4) is noted to “delight and amaze audiences.” Well! That’s certainly worth a Charm purchase – I don’t know how I’d manage that otherwise. Again, this feels rather like something I should be able to do with just Performance 5.

                                                            Sex:
                                                            (Oh, good. White Wolf and sex generally ends well.)
                                                            1. +1 Appearance. Seduce without appealing to an Intimacy. Roll influence once; apply as different influence to all present.
                                                            2. Defining Tie of Lust. (Essence) auto-successes. “World-shaking climax.” … Stay classy, Exalted.
                                                            Thousand Courtesan Ways (#1) is one of the most potent Charms in the Performance set. It would be nice if it was useful in some context other than sexing people up.

                                                            Celestial Bliss Trick (#2), issues of good taste entirely aside, is not a supplemental Charm. Which means, post-Penultimate Unity of Form, you can reroute it through your dance or speeches or etc. And that’s about as far as I’m going with that.

                                                            ***

                                                            Not as impressed by this tree as the last few. Lots of effects that feel a half-step removed from stunts; lots of vague, hand-wavey “Maybe people will feel sad!” stuff. The few powers that stand out pack tremendous amounts of raw power – Memory-Reweaving Discipline, most notably.

                                                            Yes: I… don’t know. MRD feels too good. Thousandfold Courtesan Ways feels the closest to the power level where Charms feel worthwhile but not overwhelming.
                                                            No: THREE DIFFERENT CHARMS to grant double-9s. Three different Charms.
                                                            Just Plain Weird: Acting!
