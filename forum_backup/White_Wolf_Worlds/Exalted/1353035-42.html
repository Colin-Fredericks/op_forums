                Yep I find Exalt Advantages so cool!! I feel they are a perfect way of capturing Exalted and their different vibes and niches. And frankly just having all these powers condensed to one paragraph is interesting seeing, especially for Sidereals.

                For those who don't know, I think this is the current list of Exalt Advantages that was revealed and what they do, though know it's all subject to change.

                Infernals
                Devil-Body. For a scene Infernals may change into a demon form, causing the effects of some of their charms to go differently or have cheaper costs.

                Unwoven Cuadjutor: the Infernal may once per session ask the Dm a question and they must answer.

                Sidereals
                Arcane Fate: after leaving the scene people start to forget about the Sidereal, explaining their actions as the work of passing strangers as they forget all details of the Sidereal. For Exalts this may be contested by a Diff 5 Intergrity roll or if they have an Intimacy to the Sidereal. If so they always remember.

                Weaving Destiny: the Sidereal may weave a Destiny modeled after an archetype, profession, or social roll and give it to another character with a touch. When attempting a task which resonates with that archetype the target may transfer dice for automatic successes up to the Sidereals Essence. A destiny lasts for 5 years up to the Sidereals discretion. They may only be subject to one destiny at a time. A Sidereal may apply a Destiny to themselves and with it they lose access to Arcane Fate, allowing people to remember their fake destiny.

                Solars
                Supremacy of Ability: Solars do not pay the mote cost to use Excellencies

                First Among Equals: Solars always win ties, Solar PCs win against Solar NPCs

                Liminals
                Undying: Liminals are able to get up and can't be incapacitated as long as their brain remains intact.

                Child of Death: may interact with Incorporeal ghosts as if they were corporeal.

                                        Originally posted by The Wizard of Oz View Post
                                        I'm quite curious as to how they'll do shapeshifting in a simple way. It's never been simple... but I think it could be. Each animal could just give 1 or 2 bonuses, rather than a bunch of complex stats. Or there might just be, say, a few possible bonuses (tough hide, flying, being small, being big, water breathing, poison), and you get 1-3 as appropriate.
                                        Scion 2e did an interesting thing with this in the Greek gods' ability to transform people.

                                        Put simply, the cursed target uses their own traits, but gains automatic successes on rolls that capitalize on their new form, while rolls that go against the form's nature suffer drawbacks. This doesn't actually make success less likely, but makes successful rolls more complicated unless you get a lot of extra successes (this mechanic, Complications, is the best part of Storypath).

                                        The rules for personal shapeshifting are marginally more complex, without any drawbacks, but I like the simpler setup. Add special abilities, as you suggest, and it'd be golden.
