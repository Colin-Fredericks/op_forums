                                                        ​
                                                        Originally posted by Robert Vance View Post
                                                        Yes. Its Second Edition incarnation was very good; we've just added a few more Charms.
                                                        How much did Centipede Style need to change?

                                                        Originally, it was explicitly designed to supplement other Styles rather than stand on its own. It seemed to lack a unifying strategy, with none of its three paths (speed, survival, poison) supporting or exploiting the others.

                                                        On Youtube, I looked up bug versus bug fights featuring Centipedes, and learned what kind of terrifying murder-machines they are. Like if Tiger Style had Mantis Style's focus on grappling, and traded its synergy with stealth for Black Claw's venom.


                                                        Originally posted by serGregness View Post
                                                        Hm, it's been a while since I read it, but I remember it being pretty monofocused on the flashy acrobatics when Capoeira has a lot of cool tricky ethos in the malicia parts of it. Here's hoping a bit of that comes out in the new charms!
                                                        I'm hoping that Swaying Grass provides a lot of cool and distinct kicking-based shenanigans that someone can use to change up their tactics in a fight without needing to drop their paired or two-handed weapon. Like, Guts just throws himself into a hand-stand with his sword on the ground and he kicks someone in the face. Didn't expect that, did ya?!

                                                        That flexibility and the Style's association with slave rebellions make it an interesting fit for Fangs at the Gate.


                                                        What book was the 2e version in, by the way? I didn't find it in Scroll of the Monk (which seemed like the most likely candidate I hadn't read. :V).
                                                        Neither Centipede Style nor Swaying Grass Style featured in any printed book. They were released in fan magazines, I believe.
