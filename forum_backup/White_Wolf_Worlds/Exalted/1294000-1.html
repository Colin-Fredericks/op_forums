                Hmmm, I just realized that maybe I should elaborate. So electricity is dangerous, if enough current is available and voltage present to drive it through your body it can do all kinds of bad things. It can bloat your muscles, stop your heart, cook you inside out. It can also cause shorts if improperly installed or nibbled on by rodents, and that can damage electrical equipment, start fires, and do all kinds of things. Fire is another thing that's actually pretty dangerous, even.

                That's why we have electricians who's job is to do that kind of thing. Now, they aren't gods, they can't just make anything safe, or make anything do anything. They're bound by the rules of the electricity that they deal with. You can't just energize the housing of an ungrounded toaster and call it safe because an electrician did it. So try to think of demonologists the same way. No, hopping puppeteers aren't safe, but they're damned good construction workers, if memory serves. Use them appropriately, observe the Occult Safety and Health Administration guildlines, and be constantly vigilant and you should be okay.

                            Like, the system models sorcerous binding as an Intimacy, so apart from the matter of how it can't be changed, it should be treated like an Intimacy; they won't follow it any more maniacally than they'd be inclined to for any other, and other Intimacies of Defining level might be given priority.

                            For hopping puppeteers in particular, the way they were always described in prior Editions made them extremely restless and widespread, and liable to extreme frustration with giving them something too confining to do. Heck, if you went by the deeper demon binding rules of Second Edition, task binding a demon really specifically was liable to cause it to build up Limit very rapidly, and when it Broke, the degree to which the binding restrained it would slip a bit.

                            That being said, I think that trying to be extremely intricate with a task binding is not really in the spirit of things, and being a clever bollocks besides. I actually think that the Intimacy modelling is another thing that would help out there; if it's something way more verbose and multi-faceted than you would actually expect somebody to have as a Principle, you can't bind a demon to it.

                            And again, hopping puppeteers... they're fairly mindless, and those legs just unfurl in every direction, twining around all sorts. They read to me as likely to snare up the little bodies of babies without even being fully conscious of it.
