        Originally posted by The Unsung Hero View Post

        My players have asked the same thing, and I can't find anything in this edition that says they can't. Anyone else come across this?
        Hmm, that exact thing hasn't come up for us either, but I'd say that while the Spending Willpower section says you can spend it for an automatic success "on any roll", the intent is more that you can spend it to boost an action and a damage roll is just resolving consequences, not an action on its own. I mean, would you let your players get stunt dice for a damage roll? If you'd let them stunt it, then they should probably get to spend willpower on it, too. We did have a related issue last session where one of my players wanted to stunt the initiative roll to succeed on a gambit and I used similar reasoning there.


        Have the devs (or anyone else) addressed how Seven Omens Shot interacts with other Archery charms? I'm having a hard time parsing exactly how it's supposed to combo with Accuracy Without Distance and Dust and Ash Sleight. Seven Omens Shot says that AWD can reduce the long aim action by one round, "but may only add a single non-Charm success for each round shortened". The 'for each round' part implies that AWD can shave more than one round off, but as it's not Stackable and Dust and Ash Sleight later in the tree exists solely to shorten the aim action even further suggests that this is a bad reading. The full 'only add a success for each round shortened' part seems to say that if you shorten the aim action more, you add more of the forfeited successes back in, which seems strange.

        I think the intention is that you either take the full long aim and get 3 non-charm successes in two or three rounds (depending on whether you have two purchases of Dust and Ash Sleight), or you can shorten it with AWD and fire Seven Omens Shot in after one or two rounds of aiming (depending, again on DaAS), with only one non-charm success.

        Anyone have a different/better parsing of that?

                                                            Originally posted by Leetsepeak View Post
                                                            If my character uses Summon Elemental to Summon an Essence 3 Elemental and order it to serve me for a year and a day, do I have to use Spirit-Caging Mandala in order to use Spirit-Draining Mudra on them to power my spells like some kind of elemental battery? Or if I do have to use Spirit-Caging Mandala, how does that affect the roles to maintaining the Mandala if the Spirit is not an unwilling captive?
                                                            From the looks of Spirit-Draining Mudra I'd say its kind of an augmentation on Spirit-Caging Mandala i.e. it's a extra set of glyphs or patterns in the cage's design that enable the solar to draw on it's essence. As a result while you can make a case for using Spirit-Caging Mandala without the Mudra after you know the Mudra, attempting to use the Mudra without the Mandala is like trying to draw information out of a memory card without actually plugging it into anything.

                                                            As for the rest I imagine you won't get as much resistance from the elemental since supplementing your essence with it's own is basically the thing's purpose. However, the range band thing is still going to happen and you're still bound by number of rounds you can keep this thing up and running. Considering it's a wits check to keep the Mandala up I'd argue that the elemental being contained is basically in conflict with the Mandala even if the elemental is not actively or consciously trying oppose it. So I mean it'll be easier to bind the elemental when it comes time to use the Mandala and that could be helpful. However, It won't be a case where you could just say spend a week or two summoning elementals every day, binding them for a year and a day, sticking them in the Mandala with the Mudra and then just go around with your new essence batteries for the next year and a day.
