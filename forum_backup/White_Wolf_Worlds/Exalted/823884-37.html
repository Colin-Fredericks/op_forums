                        The Stormblade (Blue Jade Daiklave - Artifact ***)

                        Forged in RY 223 by master bladesmith Manosque Zhennan for his cousin Sharmija, the Stormblade was confiscated during the purge of House Manosque and somehow ended up in the coffers of House Peleps, where it has remained until now. Given as a graduation present to the Lost Egg "Peleps" Kyshatrya, it now serves her will aboard the pirate-hunting vessel Lost Dragon's Justice. The Stormblade itself is a long, curved blade with a basket-hilt crafted to look like a web of lightning around the wielder's hand. The wide blade is graven with runes in Old Realm, exhorting the glory of the storm.

                        Evocations of the Stormblade

                        A Solar or Dragon-Blood who attunes to the Stormblade learns Lightning Strike at no cost.

                        Lightning Strike
                        Cost: 5m; Mins: Essence 1
                        Type: Reflexive
                        Keywords: None
                        Duration: Instant
                        Prerequisites: None

                        Essence lightning arcs along the Stormblade, empowering its strike with the speed and ferocity of striking lightning. The wielder may use this Evocation to take her action (Essence or 3, whichever is greater) ticks higher in the initiative order than her actual initiative would allow. If she uses her turn to make a decisive attack against an enemy who has not yet acted, the damage roll counts the bonus from this Evocation as actual Initiative and receives doubled 10s on its damage roll.

                        Thunderbolt
                        Cost: 6m, 3i; Mins: Essence 2
                        Type: Supplemental
                        Keywords: Dual
                        Duration: Instant
                        Prerequisites: Lightning Strike

                        Hurling a bolt of Essence lightning from the tip of the sword, the wielder can use this Evocation to make an attack at a target at up to Short range. If the target of a decisive attack enhanced by this Evocation is standing in at least ankle-high water, the wielder may pay an additional Willpower to subject everyone else connected to that body of water to a one-time, lightning-based environmental hazard with the traits of a bonfire.

                        Eye of the Storm
                        Cost: 6m; Mins: Essence 2
                        Type: Simple
                        Keywords: None
                        Duration: One scene
                        Prerequisites: Thunderbolt

                        With the Stormblade held high, the wielder calls the fury of the storm down into it like a lightning rod, protecting herself from the deleterious effects of the tempest. While this Evocation is in effect, the wielder suffers no environmental penalties from storm-based effects and takes no damage from storm-based environmental hazards. She also enjoys (Essence) automatic successes to resist the effects of Charms, Evocations and spells that invoke themes of thunder, wind and lightning.

                        Command the Storm
                        Cost: 10m, 1W; Mins: Essence 3
                        Type: Simple
                        Keywords: None
                        Duration: Instant
                        Prerequisites: Eye of the Storm

                        The truest power of the Stormblade is to command storms themselves. By using this Evocation, the wielder can mute or intensify the environmental penalties caused by a storm by one, to a minimum of 0 or a maximum of -3. This can create a storm from nothing or reduce an existing storm to non-existence, dissipating it unnaturally. If activated again in immediate subsequent actions, reduce the cost of the Evocation to 5m, 0W. Storms created by this Evocation tend to last until the end of the scene, though if conditions are right, they may grow and take on a life of their own at the Storyteller's discretion. Finally, while a storm created or enhanced by this Evocation is present in a scene, the wielder may reduce the cost of Thunderbolt to 3m,1i.

                            Armor of the Depths (Black Jade Breastplate - Artifact ***)

                            Forged by the Outcaste Melchior Stag, King of the Black Sea, in RY 654, the Armor of the Depths served him well in his time as a pirate captain. However, in RY 762, he encountered a Peleps military vessel he could neither outfight nor outrun, and he was taken captive. His armor was stripped from him and given to the commander of the vessel, "Peleps" Kyshatrya, who still uses it to this day. The Armor of the Depths is a black jade breastplate, engraved with swirling nautilus patterns, with a high gorget that rises to conceal the lower half of the wearer's face like a mask.

                            Evocations of the Armor of the Depths

                            A Solar or Dragon-Blood who attunes to the Armor of the Depths for two additional motes finds herself able to breathe freely underwater.

                            Tidal Pull
                            Cost: 3m; Mins: Essence 1
                            Type: Reflexive
                            Keywords: None
                            Duration: Instant
                            Prerequisites: None

                            The pull of the tide is inexorable, and so, too, is the pull from this armor. When this Evocation is activated, it creates a momentary pulse of tidal gravity, enough to pull an attacker off-balance. It reverts (wearer's Essence) 10s on the attack roll back to single successes.

                            Tidal Crash
                            Cost: 4m; Mins: Essence 1
                            Type: Reflexive
                            Keywords: None
                            Duration: Instant
                            Prerequisites: Tidal Pull

                            The armor's tidal gravity grows, able to haul an opponent off-balance even when they are not attacking. This Evocation reduces an opponent's Initiative by (wearer's Essence), but only if that would Crash them. The initiative so stolen is not gained by the Armor's wearer, but is lost.

                            Tidal Force
                            Cost: 5m; Mins: Essence 2
                            Type: Reflexive
                            Keywords: None
                            Duration: Instant
                            Prerequisites: Tidal Crash

                            The pull of the armor becomes too great to be borne, separating even the mightiest warriors from their weapons. This Evocation is a Reflexive Disarm gambit.

                                Essence lightning arcs along the Stormblade, empowering its strike. An attack supplemented by this Evocation doubles 10s on its decisive damage roll.
                                I have two problems with this Evocation. First, it is mechanically flavorless. This effect could have literally any other justification. Second, it's somewhat underpowered for its cost - for comparison, Blossom of Inevitable Demise allows you to make an attack up to medium range in addition to doubling 10s for the same cost, while Shining Starfall Execution adds up to 5 damage as well as doubling 10s for 6m.

                                What I would do instead is emphasize the speed and ferocity of striking lightning, allowing the character to act (Essence or 3, whichever is higher) ticks higher in the initiative order than her actual initiative would allow. If she uses her turn to make a decisive attack against an enemy who hasn't yet acted in the turn, the damage roll counts the bonus from this Charm as actual Initiative, and receives double 10s.

                                I think this effect is about on par with Shining Starfall Execution (much harder to get a +5 bonus to damage, but it also allows you to mildly screw with turn order which can be very powerful). I'd peg it as 5m (Flash-Fire Technique is somewhat similar but limited in different ways, and grants up to +2 Initiative, for 4m), but an argument could certainly be made for making it 6m. Playtest it with your group (but be mindful that the difference between a 5m and a 6m effect is massive - you can use the former without preparation even after you've been mote-tapped, while the latter requires a turn to set up under such circumstances).

                                Originally posted by Octopoid View Post
                                Thunderbolt
                                Cost: 6m, 3i; Mins: Essence 2

                                Keywords: Uniform

                                                    So one of my players wanted a 5 dot grand goremaul, and since we're in the West and will eventually be taking on the Silver Prince (hopefully), I decided to make the weapon one of the Bodhisattva's weapons from when he first took control of Skullstone.

                                                    The problem is I'm not sure if it's worthy of the 5 dot status when compared to similar artifacts, so I turn to you guys for an opinion.

                                                    Wail of Despair
                                                    (Soulsteel Grand Goremaul, Artifact 5)

                                                    When he first ascended his throne, the Bodhisattva Anointed by Dark Waters knew he needed a weapon that would strike fear into his enemies and inspire loyalty amongst his followers. He worked for several years on the best way to achieve these goals and eventually settled upon this design.

                                                    He spent years collecting the correct materials and forging the weapon into an instrument of destruction and torture that served him well during his rise to power and creation of the New Order, a religion that keeps the people he ruled in check.

                                                    After his disappearance from Dark Mist he decided to send the weapon out into the world rather than keep it close. He felt that its abilities were of no more use to him personally as he had moved onto a more subtle approach to control. It now passes among his agents as a way to ensure their success in missions.

                                                    The shaft of this weapon is made from a rod of pure Black Jade that is wrapped in the hide of a Behemoth. The head is Soulsteel laced with minor traces of starmetal. Its name is derived from the ear splitting screams that come from its victims souls as they are consumed.

                                                    Evocations of Wail of Despair
                                                    A solar or Abyssal who attunes to Wail of Despair awakens Prison of Penance at no cost. By paying an extra 3 motes when attuning, the wielder may expend a soul stored in Wail of Despair to coat the hammer head so that it can effect immaterial spirits for the scene.

                                                    Prison of Penance
                                                    Cost: 5m; Mins: Essence 1
                                                    Type: Reflexive
                                                    Keywords: Decisive only
                                                    Duration: Instant
                                                    Prerequisite: None

                                                    Upon killing a living being with Wail of Despair, the exalt may invoke this evocation. Ghostly tendrils reach out from the weapon and grab the victims soul as it departs their body. The soul is then dragged screaming into the weapon where it remains to power Wail of Despair.

                                                    The Exalt may store up to Essence x2 souls and each stored soul increases Wail of Despair's base damage by one. If the exalt ever loses commitment to Wail of Despair then all stored souls are lost and pass on to Lethe as normal.

                                                    Exchange of Penance
                                                    Cost: 3m, 1+ souls; Mins: Essence 2
                                                    Type: Reflexive
                                                    Keywords: Decisive only
                                                    Duration: Instant
                                                    Prerequisite: Prison of Penance

                                                    Upon successfully hitting with a decisive attack the exalt may invoke this evocation. He consumes up to his Essence souls stored in Wail of Despair to power its attack with an equal number of automatic successes on the damage roll. If this damage is enough to kill the target then the exalt may activate Prison of Penance at no cost.

                                                    Voice of the Penitent
                                                    Cost: 5m, 1wp; Mins: Essence 3, Lore 3
                                                    Type: Simple
                                                    Keywords: None
                                                    Duration: One Scene or until all questions are answered
                                                    Prerequisite: Prison of Penance

                                                    Calling out to one of the souls trapped inside Wail of Despair, the exalt may offer an exchange of information for freedom. A soul is pulled out of the weapon and as long as they have been trapped for more than a week, will truthfully answer up to Essence questions that the wielder asks. After this, they are free to pass on to Lethe as normal.

                                                    If the soul called has been trapped for less than a week, then it has not yet succumb to the true despair of its prison and will only truthfully answer one question in exchange for its freedom.

                                                    Army of the Broken
                                                    Cost: 10m, 1wp; Mins: Essence 4, Occult 3
                                                    Type: Simple
                                                    Keywords: Perilous
                                                    Duration: Until next sunrise
                                                    Prerequisite: Exchange of Penance

                                                    Drawing upon his stored collection of souls, the exalt calls them to his aid. All of the souls are released and appear as Hungry Ghosts (Exalted 3rd edition page 503) that obey the wielders commands but are destroyed if they go more than 2 range bands from the wielder. These ghosts are unlike others and are not harmed by sunlight but are also unable to dematerialise. Whenever they kill a living creature, its soul is automatically dragged into Wail of Despair unless it has already reached its soul limit.

                                                    Whenever the wielder is hit with a decisive attack while this Evocation is active, the damage is transferred to a random ghost until there are no more available to take the damage.

                                                    Special activation rule: If the exalt kills a non trivial opponent and consumes their soul with Prison of Penance, then this Evocation may be activated Reflexively.

                                                        The Crawling Garment(Green Jade, Moonsilver and Wax Lamellar, 4-dot Artifact)

                                                        The Crawling Garment is a curious construction, consisting of hardened wax plates, infused with Green Jade Dust and bound with Moonsilver. The larger links are forged of amber, each piece containing some long dead insect. The Wax itself is infested with ants, flies, bees, wasps spiders and various other insects, which constantly move through the wax. They constantly repair and restructure the armour in a dozen small ways, spiders reweaving the Moonsilver threads while bees produces wax to be carried by the ants. The flies keep the armour clean, while the wasps and other colourful insects(including the occasional butterfly) give the armour vibrant streaks of colour. A Dragonblood, Lunar or Solar who attunes this armour may have the insects carve his personal seal, the seal of some organisation he supports or indeed anything else onto the front of the armour. The armour automatically fits itself to the wearer and is extremely comfortable besides, inflicting no penalties for extended use.

                                                        Calling the Swarm
                                                        Cost: 2m; Mins: Ess 2
                                                        Type: Simple
                                                        Keywords: None
                                                        Duration: 1 Round
                                                        Prerequisite: None

                                                        With this Evocation, the bearer replenishes the insects within the armour, calling forth any insects in the vicinity. A swarm of insects forms almost immediately, obscuring the bearer from sight and granting him light cover against all attacks for the round. This evocation functions in any area capable of supporting insect life. For the next 3 rounds, due to the insects clinging to the armour, its mobility increases by 1 however, the armour's soak increases by 3 for the same duration. The Evocation may be reactivated reflexively at the end of the 3 rounds, but no earlier.

                                                        Swarm Bursts Forth
                                                        Cost: 3m; Mins: Ess 2
                                                        Type: Reflexive
                                                        Keywords: Perilous
                                                        Duration: Instant
                                                        Prerequisite: Calling the Swarm

                                                        This Evocation allows the wearer to expel a cloud of insects out to short range. Everyone within the cloud benefits from light cover. However, enemies of the bearer find themselves harassed by the insects. While they deal no damage, any foes suffer a -2 penalty to all movement based actions while within the swarm.

                                                        Becoming the Swarm
                                                        Cost: 5m, 1wp; Mins: Ess 3
                                                        Type: Reflexive
                                                        Keywords: None
                                                        Duration: Scene
                                                        Prerequisite: Swarm Bursts Forth

                                                        This evocation may only be used during the 3 rounds after Calling the Swarm has been used. The final evocation of the Crawling Garment transforms the bearer into a sentient swarm of insects. The bearer treats the armoured soak the armour provides as natural soak for all purposes and is no longer treated as wearing armour. However, they also gain the effects of the Hideous Merit as their skin breaks apart into thousands of insects. While in this form, the bearer may fly, fit through any space large enough to fit the constituents of his body(Although it may take some time to fit the entire body through smaller spaces) and may launch hand-to-hand attacks out to short range, stretching his 'arms' to reach that far.

                                                        As a last ditch defense, the bearer may spend 1 Willpower to take a temporary crippling effect, doubling his wound penalties for the rest of the day, to force the swarm to scatter completely. However, until the swarm gathers back together at the end of this evocations duration, the bearer cannot be targeted by any decisive attacks as his form is too scattered to suffer such attacks. However, any attacks he launches may not deal more than the Bearer's Permanent Essence in damage and he may not use any weapons in this form. It is most often used to escape battle safely.

Cultists of Mara also recognise this as a holy relic

                                                        Cost: 3m ; Mins: Ess 2
                                                        Type: Supplemental
                                                        Keywords: Dual
                                                        Duration: Instant
                                                        Prerequisite: None
