        One interesting thing is that the system definitely supports dragonblooded as being the "Best" at thaumaturgy, if for nothing else than their lifespan.

        Only a group long enough lived and wealthy enough to create living libraries of thaumaturges can really excel at it. I'm really seeing the schools for dragonblooded as almost like a retirement home/technical school, which crotchety old thaumaturges who all know dozens upon dozens of skills who are protected as living treasures. Students can learn specific skills from the old masters, who's main job is to teach and preserve the rituals deemed important. However to "earn" a position as a "master" (i'm seeing this kinda like old school oxford) a student has to go out into creation to find a new ritual worth adding to the ranks of the school. A Thaumaturgical skill to make moss grow is fairly useless, a Ritual to change the color of cloth is not.

        thaumaturgy seems really really close to the magic in the "Legend of Nightfall"

        Which is honestly all i need for a good mortal seed game. A group of Young students combing the outer reaches of the empire, looking for someone with a knack that they can bring back and earn their robes, maybe someone exalts? maybe "who knows what happens"?

        It really depends on how long it takes to learn a ritual and what level of personal teaching it takes, one at a time? classroom style?
        Does it take a week, or a month, or a year to learn a ritual?

        Just to make it easier i'll probably steal the guidelines from mage the awakening, a one dot thaumaturgy ritual gets you anything a 1 dot mage could do (pick a one dot rote, or something of equal power) and a two dot ritual gets you a 2 dot mage rote/equivalent. yeah it'll take a little conversion, but it saves me the trouble of making something up whole cloth.

        For example i can totally see the Funerists knowing and keeping a 2 dot ritual that does the same thing as Corpse mask (MtA) or Ghost Summon

                            Originally posted by Lokiyn View Post
                            It really depends on how long it takes to learn a ritual and what level of personal teaching it takes, one at a time? classroom style?
                            Does it take a week, or a month, or a year to learn a ritual?
                            Thaumaturgic rituals are specified as needing to be passed on through one-on-one practical tutoring. A rituallist could possibly mentor several students at once, but with diminished returns.

                            Training Times, on pages 178-9, don't include rituals, but it takes one week per new rating for a (purchased) Merit, and two weeks per Circle for a spell. I'd lean towards the former for rituals (which also follow the dot rating model). Edit: Page 490 discusses the cost and training time for thaumaturgy and it is indeed one week per dot rating; 3xp for a one dot ritual, 5xp for a two dot ritual.

                            This is, of course, for player characters with xp on hand (or a charm like Dark Minder's Observance). NPC may like to take things at a more relaxed pace.

                                I've got a new edition of Unknown Armies, and that means.... new rituals!

                                * You can coax an immaterial spirit into a living human body by driving a metal spike through the parietal bone of their skull while reciting a chant. This is more challenging than it sounds - if you kill the human outright, the ritual fails. Thus, using some kind of drill is recommended.

                                * If you hold someone's head beneath the water until bubbles stop coming to the surface, they do not drown. Instead, they become able to breath in water for a few weeks.

                                * You can make black mirrors that show a person's true face, both in the figurative and mystical sense.

                                * Do not eat or drink anything but water for three days and nights. Lie facedown on the ground for a few minutes, then get up as quickly as you can and spin around counterclockwise. You will have visions of important events transpiring in Yu-Shan. The gods may notice you doing this, and give you unusually good or bad luck for several days afterwards.

                                * Draw a straight line in white on your (or another person's) face, starting where your left earlobe attaches, passing through the left corner of your left eye. Do the same with a red line from right earlobe and right eye. Then, stick a silver needle in the place where the lines meet while concentrating on a memory. You will re-experience the events of that memory with perfect clarity, "refreshing" your knowledge of it as though it had happened less than an hour ago.

                                * Draw a complex mandala pattern, then sit in the middle of it and lick the skin of a psychoactive amphibian. For a few minutes, you gain the ability to "Measure the Wind" (as the charm), and subconsciously use it on everything you can see - which includes all manner of small gods and any invisible, immaterial beings in your presence. You can also spot Dragon Lines, if you happen to be near one.

                                * You can erode someone's Intimacy toward a specific person in their lives with the gift of a carved wooden doll. As long as they keep the doll, that intimacy degrades by one rank each week, until it is all-but-forgotten. Destroying the doll undoes the spell.

                                * You can convert old, misused carriages into ghost traps.

                                * You can make magical amulets with a bunch of nice-smelling ingredients - ambergris, orange peel, cloves - that help prevent people from becoming sick.

                                * You can make a special cake with three ounces of human blood. Supposedly, eating the whole thing will prevent evil spirits from haunting you, but in reality what it does is relieve anxiety - like magical Prozac. Consumption of the blood cake gives the consumer three days of relief from all manner of phobias and background stress.

                                * You know a chant that induces an ASMR response in anyone you speak it to. Some people find it relaxing or pleasurable.

                                    - Borrowed Luck: The thaumaturgists ties coins to a red string and lend it to a child. Creature of darkness and Fair Folk will have trouble noticing the kid, but it becomes easier for them to detect the thaumaturgists.

                                    - False Yin-Yang Eyes: Dip a strawdoll's head into the blood of ink black dog, then nailed it under someone bed. The target will see hallucinations of ghost. Knowing the victim date of birth and tying a locket of their hair into the strawdoll increase the effect.

                                    - Boat of Passage: Put a lotus flower-shaped lantern along with something significant to the dead on a paper boat then release it on a river, the ghost of the decease will feel an strong urge to enter the cycle of reincarnation. The paper boat unnaturally crosses to the other side of river despite the water current. At higher tier, any ghosts who can see the lantern light will feel the same urge.

                                                            - Silence of the Lamb: Putting a white piece of cloth over a baby's face will make it stops crying.

                                                            - Moon-Viewing Flower: As long as the front of your body faces toward the sky and it's night time, you can not sink in water. A higher dot version allows this to work at day time.

                                                            - Fireflies Under the Ocean: Your movement underwater leaves a trail of glowing blue light. Ghosts is attracted to this display of luminescence.

                                                            - Bride of Nine Springs: You don't need to eat, drink or breath, and your body won't product any waste if you stays submerged in a naturally-occurring large or moving body of water (an ocean or river counts, a bathing pool won't), you also immune to any water-based hazard like cold or pressure. If you continuously benefited from this Merit for three days straight then from the fourth day, the ST rolls one dice, failure means you get get transport to one of the Underworld's nine springs.

                                                            - Gnawing on Siren Song: The thaumaturgists can store human teeth in some kind of container (pendant, jar,...etc). She can increase her Resolve against the mental magic of Fair Folk, each points increased causes one tooth in the container to decay.

                                                            - Womb of Drowned-Twin: A person with this Merit can gleam someone thought and emotion, as long as both are in physical contact and is submerged under water. This is a two-way street method, since the other person can do the same thing even if they lack the Merit.
