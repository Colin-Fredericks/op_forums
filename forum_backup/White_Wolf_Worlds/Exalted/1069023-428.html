                                Originally posted by Malfi View Post
                                Now that arms is out (congrats to everyone involved!), some questions:
                                Can small or weird weapons like razor claws, serpent sting staff and daggers reach artifact level 5-n/a? If yes would they require even more strange and rare components than those of noble warstriders?
                                What about light artifact armor like chain shirt or silken armor can it reach artifact level 5- n/a?
                                Yep, light weapons/armor can be 5-dot or N/A just as easily as their medium or heavy counterparts.

                                Can evocations be awakened in artifacts like the golden viper and the chariot of aerial conquest?
                                Any artifact rated at 3 dots or higher can theoretically sustain Evocations, though it may not make sense for some. Horizon Endeavor and the Chariot of Aerial Conquest originally had Evocations, but they were cut for space. Automatons like the Golden Viper or Brass Legionnaires I'm a bit more iffy on, but if your character has a personal bond with them like the one that might exist beyond an Exalt and their daiklave, sure.
